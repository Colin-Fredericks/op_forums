    Clan - Mekhet

    While I think of myself as a likable enough guy, and I make friends easily enough, I have a tendency to get overwhelmed during social events and fade into the background. Plus, I'm fairly obsessed with weird stories and obscure trivia, and the idea of concealing myself with Obfuscate and poking around in hidden or restricted places appeals to me quite a bit. That and I'm kind of an Ancient Egypt nerd.

    Covenant - Ordo Dracul

    It's unlikely that I'd rise terribly high, since I'm fairly squeamish about the kinds of things that hardcore Ordo researchers get up to, but if I were to become a vampire, I imagine that understanding my new condition as completely as possible would quickly become my first priority. Plus it's a good fit for my scholarly tendencies and my bloodline (see below). The Circle of the Crone appeals to me on a purely intellectual level, but I simply don't have the stomach for their particular variety of ritualism. The Lancea et Sanctum has its appeal as well, but being more-or-less and atheist I could never buy into their theology. Then again, if Dr. Caroline Petronius from The Testament of Longinus can be a member of the covenant, maybe I could too.

    Bloodline - Agonistes

    As a history nerd and a student of anthropology, probably the most appealing thing to me about vampires in their connection to history and extinct cultures. Being able to interact with beings who could potentially tell the world so much that has otherwise been forgotten would be amazing, and the libraries and rituals of the Agonistes are resources too amazing for me to pass up.

    Disciplines - Auspex, Dominate, Obfuscate

    I'd probably put one dot in each to start with.

            Clan: Mekhet

            Covenant: Circle of the Crone

            Bloodline: Alucinor

            Disciplines: Auspex, and Crúac primarily likely with a dot of Celerity, Obfuscate, and Majesty as the blood thickens.

            Reasons:
            When it comes to Clans in many ways I am split between the natures of the Daeva and Mekhet, inclined as I am toward both manipulation and subtlety, but I think in the end it is the method that has to be the deciding factor between them. I've always loved secrets and have somethign of an affinity for reading those around me, which is perhaps the core of my social skills, though from the responses I get I gather I'm fun to look at as well. Altering myself based on what I perceive others to desire is a natural inclination for me, and in a way that control over the extent of my presence has led to a certain skill at being unseen when I desire it. Together I think that's probably more than enough justification to tip things toward Shadow over Serpent.

            As for Covenants, I am a pagan, and the way the Circle was described in it's old Covenant book really made it fall into place for me. It is in my opinion the only path which can ever actually lead a vampire to reaching a state of peace with their own natures (excepting perhaps Golconda, but that's another matter entirely). I'm not sure how well I'd fair at learning Crúac via the whole 'torture the wisdom into you' method, but knowing you can recover perfectly from nearly any injury would probably help quite a bit, and if the slightly more cerebral methods implied in BS:S&B are in effect then I'm perfectly content.

            The Alucinor, especially as remade for B&S by Chris Falco are exceptionally attractive to me. The prophetic abilities they possess as well as the pervasive edge their influence gains from dreamweaving are major selling points for me, and I've always found dreams fascinating.

            Given how things have changed in B&S its actually a struggle to decide how to assign things, but I suspect I'd have to start with at least 2 dots of Auspex and perhaps one of Crúac with a focus on divinatory and creation rites. The power to read secrets from a person's every move, and to control the foundations of the world are just too good to pass up. From there it would be pretty natural for me to develop a dot of Obfuscate, though it's unlikely I'd ever go much further, and one of Celerity if only for the sheer utility of it. Majesty would of course require a trainer, but it would make an excellent aid to feeding, and there's nothing quite like having all eyes on you as you move through a rave.

                                    Clan: Mekhet.
                                    Covenant: Unaligned/Circle of the Crone

                                    This would be ideal, for me. I don't have the energy to be a gangrel anymore, nor the interest in attention to be daeva. While I'd love to just avoid notice, at the revelation of a supernatural world I'd want to know more, and coupled with my natural curiousity I'd want to dig up secrets everywhere. Nosferatu is probably the next most likely.

                                    I couldn't stand the court stuff, the politics stuff. I can run it, but I wouldn't want to play it. Outside of grabbing Dominate, my big priority would be keeping my family safe, and getting involved with that would make it really hard to do.
                                    Still, if I absolutely had to, or once the start was up, I'd probably be interested in joining the Circle of the Crone. I'm not a fan of the Church, or politics, or the pursuits of the Ordo Dracul. I suppose I might get the Call to join the Brood at some point in the far future (what's the point of being a monster of the night if you're not going to be an unholy monster of the night), but the Circle of the Crone would be a decent starter for me. Especially since I live in the Triangle, and apparently (according to B&S) they're pretty open minded so I'd be able to find an angle I like to come at it.

                                    Ghoul the wife. Try to resist doing anything with the kid except encouraging them to do well in school (with dominate to help, 'do your homework', though 'go to sleep' would get a lot of use right now). My wife said she'd prefer being a hollow mekhet of the Ordo Dracul (she's more curious about other people than myself, and is a scientist), so by the time the kid was a good way to college we'd find a way to get that done. Probably make my neighborhood a hunting ground, have a quiet presence on the HOA. Justify my blood theft by solving their problems with the area.

                                    Disciplines: Obfuscate, auspex, dominate. Eventually Blood Tenebrous, as I find the Spirit infinitely interesting and vampire-self wouldn't be able to avoid digging into the concept of another world when he found out about it.

                                                        Clan: Ventrue

                                                        I need to control my own path, I really love being free, and being free is about having the means to achieve such freedom no matter the odds. Although I am not good at playing the figurehead or to play the part as the boss people sometimes expect me to be, I always end up being a leader wherever I go.

                                                        I am the person other people come to talk to when ideas are required, when there is a hard talk that must be had, when somebody needs to be convinced of something. I have grown a bit proud of being right all the time, and people rely on my opinions and on my judgement. Even when I am not (de facto) in charge people still expect me to lead and to find a proper way, especially in tough situations. I am the one to take the lead when others are scared, and I revel in going first and beating the unexpected. In a way, I believe that I can achieve more than what most can and the solitude that comes from it due to not having others who I can deem worth it, who can challenge me, who can complete me. That goes hand in hand with both the old noblesse oblige Ventrue and the Aloof Curse Ventrue.

                                                        As I am a teacher, both in profession and speech, it is common for people to look for me for guidance. Especially newcomers and youngsters find a cosy environment around me. Many have deemed me unshakable and honourable, but a few have been able to see my manipulating the odds, and, shamelessly, trying to - and succeeding at - screwing anybody who has wronged me or those who are dear to me. There is always this ongoing debate of whether I am a kind, smiley, sympathetic soul or a manipulative SOB who everybody respects in front of others because they cannot defy simply because it is just hard to see what really affects me whereas, conversely, it seems to be very easy for me to hit people where it hurts when necessary.

                                                        I guess being Ventrue would sit fine with me.

                                                        Covenant: Invictus

                                                        I am reliable, and I tend to know a lot because I know how to approach people and both getting the information I want and making them believe they can count on me (and, usually, I really mean it). I do not wish to take on the mantle of leadership, it happens naturally when it happens, so those who want to wear the crown do not feel more than somewhat threatened by me, and they end up needing my aid. I do not follow commands just because they are orders, but I know how to question and - what is better - questioning somebody else's leadership and orders in a way that will not leave the figurehead exposed... I like whispering in the ears of those who honestly want to wear the crown and occupy the throne while I am the one calling the actual shots and being the one the normal folks look up to, and the one whose guidance the actual leader cannot live without. That grants me the freedom I like so much, and guarantees that I will only be bothered by the things which are more interesting and not so boring.

                                                        Also, I do not believe in utopia. I do believe that organisation is essential. I think that power comes naturally to those who have the means to seize it, and good things will come when somebody is worth it. Crying for equality and justice when life is unfair to begin with is utterly pointless. Adapting and playing the system is the best way to be ultimately free.

                                                        Bloodline: None.

                                                        Disciplines: Auspex, Dominate, Resilience, Celerity.

                                                            My turn.
                                                            CLAN: Mekhet primarily. Possibly Gangrel, Daeva ONLY if I get to be a Septim(See Bloodline)
                                                            REASON: The Shadows are my sort of people. They're quiet, value learning, and aren't the most social. Which, funnily, is why I didn't want the Haunts; I'm bad enough being social as is. The enduring strength of the Gangrel is tempting, and I can often get angry to rather extreme levels. Ventrue are aristocratic in ways that raise my hackles('MERICA!), and I'm far too much a prude to be your average Daeva.

                                                            COVENANT: Ordo Dracul, NO EXCEPTIONS.
                                                            REASON: I cannot stress this enough. The Carthians are well meant, but they target the symptoms rather than the disease. Invictus are what our ancestors threw tea in the harbor to be rid of, and I'm a Christian, so the Circle is right out(not that I'd hate them, though I'd probably have to watch out for Cruac after one "Will you acept Jesus as your lord and Savior?" Too many). Which brings me to the Sanctum. I hate them SO. VERY. MUCH! They are the ultimate Blasphemy, and they are the agents of the very damnation they claim to prevent. The Ordo is, in my opinion, the only sane, moral way to react to being forced into a monster that goes around hurting people to survive. But even then, the Ordo is just the least of all evils.
