    It's possible to coerce a vampire to Embrace someone?

    10-17-2015, 01:55 AM
    If a vampire is caged and chained, and threatened with torture ( both starvation and fire ), it's possible to coerce him to embrace someone?

    If that is possible, could a fugitive / independent ghoul that managed to imprison a vampire force him to "produce" new vampires for him ( he kidnap humans, chain them, bring them to the chained vampire, force him to embrace them, then he feed them with pig or cow blood bought at the local slaughterhouse, then he drains them of vitae when needed ) in order to have a vaste supply of Vitae and not incur in Vinculum?

              I mean...yeah? You might have an argument on whether or not someone can directly mind control you into doing it (and that probably relies on how that mind control interacts with Breaking Points, but I digress), but if you're asking if someone can force a vampire to choose between embracing someone or death (or pain or whatever) then yes, it's absolutely possible. Couple of notes for this specific plan:

              - If you stick with a single "mother" vampire doing all the Embracing, then the Humanity cost will eventually drive him or her to be an unreasoning monster that you -can't- really bargain with. (Also, I'm unsure if Draugr can Embrace intentionally given their lack of Humanity. Hm.)

              - Animal blood is super inefficient, so depending on the needs of the ghoul(s) it can end up causing a noticeable drain. Or it won't! A lot depends on the smarts of the ghoul and what preparations he or she has made. There's probably going to be some sort of paper trail or anomalies that can lead back to her (if only for drama's sake).

              - Sticking with one childer for your blood harvest still puts you in danger of vinculum, and this plan does nothing about Blood Addiction. You can circumvent the first point by disposing of a certain childer after two drinks (if the vinculum will let you at that point, hm.) and cycle in a new one, but that leads directly into...

              - Any ghoul who does this is a fucking psychopath and their Integrity is not long for this world, since this entire plans is an unending cycle of torture and death. I really hope this isn't a PC plan, for your sake.

              - You could probably change the plan somewhat to change up exactly how it plays out: you could start small but use each given childer to create more vampires and turn it into a blood farm, and it's probably more efficient to find a way to supplement your animal blood use with kidnapped human vessels, if only because you're going to be kidnapping more people -anyways- to make more vampires and humans will feed more of these livestock Kindred efficiently. You might also want to invest in multiple locations and even find ways to monetize it, for surely at this level you're going to need conspirators to make it work. Note: this makes the plan way more noticeable and nothing about making the process cleaner and more efficient makes it any less fucking crazy.

                                                  The Vinculum isn't a binary state. Each drink produces a progressive effect. Stealing the Embrace and being a vampire yourself is much more blood-efficient, and so allows you to feed far less conspicuously.
