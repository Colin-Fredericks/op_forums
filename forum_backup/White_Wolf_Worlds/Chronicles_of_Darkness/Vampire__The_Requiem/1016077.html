    When you use Idol during a combat the book states that for someone in order to do something aggressive and not acceptable to you, you need to roll a Resolve+BP-Majesty Dots.
    In order for the character to use this Resolve+BP roll to counter Idol, he needs to spend a point of Willpower? Also does Idol ''breaks'' by Lashing Out? And if u succeed once in a Resolve+Bp roll, on the next turn the character needs to re-roll this?

      There are tons and tons of threads about Idol already, they would be good resources for you.

      I would not allow you to lash out and break Idol because there are several disciplines out there that specifically state when lashing out breaks them as Idol does not include that. Because it is not listed here, I would say that it doesn't break it. Two: it takes a great act of resolve to even attack the vampire after they have done Idol and yes, you have to roll that every turn to attack. Implying if you failed, you just chickened out or changed your mind. I would think you would need a good reason to even want to attack them because of what the flavor text indicates on how you are suppose to feel while affected by this power.

      Now, you can however lash out against Awe and if Awe isn't working, Idol doesn't work. But you would have to have a very very good reason to know they are using awe, because it is subtle, people don't know they have been affected and when it wears off, they don't feel screwed. Same with Idol.

      If you have charmed people and they get moved to enthralled, you would absolutely not be allowed to lash out against the vampire as it takes a will power to lash out against a vampire and if you are enthralled you can't spend will power against the vampire.

      Also you can Awe and Idol in the same turn. I know there were several questions about that in past threads.

                      Yes, lashing out successfully breaks Idol. Make the Resolve + Composure roll -5. If it's successful, you can spend the Willpower to lash out. If you're successful in lashing out you don't have to keep rolling to act.
                      To counter it's activation, use Dread Presence which is reflexive and prevents willpower expenditure or lash out the same turn they activate or start to activate Idol. They'll have to choose between activating their power or losing the predatory aura contest.
