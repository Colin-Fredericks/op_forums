    In a World of Darkness... Murder Rates

    04-28-2018, 01:49 PM
    In a World of Darkness what are the average crime rates? In a world with vampires what are the homicide rates? In the real world - Caracas, Venezuela has held on to its spot as the world's most dangerous city with a murder rate of 130.35 (that's per 100,000 people). Do any roleplaying groups actually figure out the murder rates in their settings?

    I get that ACTUAL numbers don't really matter necessarily, but description does matter. What would a city be like with a murder rate of 1,000 or 5,000 per 100,000 people? How would you describe it? There are a lot of factors involved, but I think it is interesting to think about.

    Just curious what other groups do in this regard?

                                                  Ultimately you need deaths and disappearances to be higher than the real world but low enough that no one questions it. Make it worse, but not so bad that population growth over the last century wouldn't be possible.
                                                  Everyone is saying statistics don't matter, but if you want to make them matter, then that would be my advise--your rates should be low enough that you somehow still got to 7 billion humans by 2001. Keep population growth relatively the same. Less people are perhaps dying of old age, but people aren't having more kids to keep up, cuz that would give off the masquerade if people were deliberately having more kids to compensate for the unknown deaths. Compare to 300 years ago in which people had lots of kids to cope with accidents and diseases, but in the modern day, a lot of people going missing and population fluctuating would stick out.
                                                  Your statistic should not heavily impact population growths. Anyway, hope that is helpful too ;3

                                                      Murder rates is a start, but surely lacking in explaining things. Statistics about missing people and the like needs to come into the calculation as well. But even then you need to consider where the chronicle takes place. Sure there's no problem with the right feel of danger in the night in Honduras or the slums of São Paulo, but for most countires in the EU it's stretching it by plainly saying our world but with supernaturals. Surely it's a premise to the setting but take a look at one splat book and you realize that if you count for half of one splats killings/kidnappings/etc there's just not possible for quite a few countries in the world let alone if you try to stay close to the actual setting with most splats and other things going bump in the night.
                                                      It takes a fair bit of suspension of disbelief to accept that spirits/claimed/vampires/mages/demons/ghost/horrors/werewolves/etc.etc. goes bump in the night.
                                                      Maybe it works better if you are more used to the idea that kidnappings and or murder rates are higher, like the US capital of Washington DC seems like crazy to me if i check the murder rates per capita against my home of Sweden, which is about 1/10 of the rates of DC. And the last few years, gang criminality has gone up and the use of deadly force by them has become way more common today then for a few years back, but still every murder is still extremely newsworthy and get's attention beyond what would be possible of there was ~1500 homicides instead of the 113 we had last year. Our neighbour, Norway has about half of our, Swedens, murder rate, sadly one year, 2011, this number got to about the same rate as Sweden, by one act of terrorism. This massacre tells a lot of how bad a representation statistics can be if you don't know about them, and tells nothing of how safe from being killed you are in Norway for instance if you look at the 2011 statistics instead of any other year.

                                                      So in all this, we at our table start by figuring out the general feel of the place we play in and go from there, accepting that our chronicle will diverge from reality one way or another. For instance the current chronicle is taking place in the city of Catania in Sicily. Italy, a pretty safe place over all but we want to push up the crime and gonzo factor so in our chronicle the city has become a hub of criminal war, forcing the Cosa Nostra to defend itself from foreign crime syndicates trying to get a stronger hold as the main "cover up" for how bad the city is before calculating in supernaturals. The feel I'm pretty sure is way closer to the Mafia wars a few decades ago then it is right now, but that's a feel we're looking for in this particular chronicle.
