      First of all, you can't "sense" the Nosferatu. I'm guessing that you're going with the whole "all Nosferatu feel creepy / ugly" vibe as a dead giveaway. However, other vampires, possibly even ghouls, don't feel creeped out around the Nos by default. Now, that's not saying that really ugly vampires can't be prejudiced against as "Nosferatu," but that's just a kind of fanatsy racism that's reacting to one's appearance, rather than any reliable way of detecting clan. Nothing stops non-Nosferatu from developing creepy vibes as well.

      Meanwhile, detecting clan can be performed via "A Taste of Blood" sidebar of the Kindred Senses section. Bloodhound can pick out details by smelling blood instead of tasting it, and Acute Senses / Distinguished Palate can modify the roll to make it very easy to succeed at. Now, if that means that you have to actively bleed out vitae first, or if you can just sniff another and find out, you'll have to ask your ST, as I'm sure they'll have different ways that they'll have Bloodhound work.

      Lastly, there's the small detail of Blood Sympathy. This is probably your biggest issue, since its fairly easy for Mehket or Ventrue to try and sense a clan tie. If any Ventrue at any time tries to use Sympathy to get a read on your emotions? Its going to be clear to that person you're not a Lord or Lady. It shouldn't be too much of an issue if you're just aiming to be mistaken as a Ven and not actually promoting yourself as one or trying to push any kind of clan bonds.

                                          First, ask the ST.

                                          Auspex, from 2 onward, can detect clan or not. That's a ST's call.

                                          Clan stereotypes are the most probable sources of clan identification on most chronicles, but they're just stereotypes. Even a person who's ugly as hell and gives bad vibes can be just that and be of any, absolutely any clan (Majesty, by the way, is much more to make creepy people into social stars, than to make it with someone who's already good at it), and a Nos can be beautyful, if a little creepy to common mortals. So that's your main asset, you can easily make the Mekhet similar to any other clan.

                                          Blood Simpathy won't work every time, specially if the character isn't linked by blood to other Mekhet in the city except by clan, nor claims close blood relations with the local Ventrue. In those cases, the only clue is repeated failure on the part of a particularly persistent Ventrue, or eventual success of a Mekhet.

                                          On the subject of stereotyping not being completely unreliable, some vampires could just naturally appear other clan without even trying it. A domineering person that obsess for control will be taken for a Ventrue for sure! But such person can be that way simple because she is, she was even before the Embrace. And them be embraced by a Mekhet, making the person a Mekhet herself. So... I thought it obvious, just said because some people I know believe that only a member of the clan would behave in a similar fashion.

                                          My opinion is that this character will probably work pretty well, mainly because I don't see many kindred even caring enough to look further. Maybe even the character herself thinks she's a Ventrue, depending on her story. Depending on the kindred of the city, even if the character tries to live up as a Ventrue it is possible to the others not even caring, or going further to adopt her properly in the clan at a local level, and that's it. She's like a Ventrue, loyal to Ventrue, and no Mekhet sees her as one of them, so what?
