                                    Having read through Blood and Smoke, I enjoy a lot of these bloodline updates, though I feel the Bloodworking translation is not fitting for the Norvegi bloodline, and is more in line with the Amara Havana from Ancient Bloodlines. Norvegi was built for the combat monsters, and the whole idea seems build around the blood spikes, so I feel this rendition, while very cool, is not very appropriate for this bloodline. To be entirely honest though, I don't really see the need for many changes, as all the abilities seem to transfer over very well. The only issue I've seen is that if you keep it the same, the Skewer ability does the same damage as the fifth dot martial art merit "Touch of Death", making having both superfluous, which I felt was weird, since I though that level of martial training should augment brawling abilities in some way. I thought of a few ways it could work out, and these two are my preferred methods.

                                    A: Touch of death gives a +1 bonus to other brawl enhancing abilities, making Protean claws 2L and Skewer 3L. This is a little silly, since the claws do the same amount of damage as touch of death on its own, but it does keep things nice and simple.

                                    B: Touch of death stacks with other brawl enhancing abilities, so Protean claws are 3L and Skewer is 4L. I thought at first this may be a bit overpowered, but considering the cost, I feel it isn't inappropriate, especially since skewer isn't meant to be used casually in the first place, seeing as how bloodworking is a bloodline secret.

                                    Both these abilities provide a simple way to work this merit in with abilities that may show up in other disciplines.

                                    Please excuse my tangent, but I feel that Bloodworking needs no changes, as it works just fine following the same rules, and I felt I should put in my opinion as the suggested update seemed contrary to the point on the bloodline (though almost all of it could be used for the Amara Havana discipline Sakti Pata).

                                        Come up with a Bloodline Bane for the Sangiovanni in Blood & Smoke.

                                        Bloodline Bane: Comfortable Among Corpses

                                        The Sangiovanni have an unhealthy attraction to the dead both as an object of their sorcery and as companions in their Requiem. Whenever a Sangiovanni fails a breaking point, they acquire the Corpse Comfort Condition. They must seek out the bodies of the dead to comfort their troubled souls with a sense of control.

                                        Corpse Comfort

                                        Your character has had their sense of Humanity shaken by an event that’s transpired. They feel the urge to retreat among the things that provide all Sangiovanni with a measure of comfort and control: Corpses. Your character suffers a -2 penalty to all Mental or Social rolls, unless they are in the presence of a corpse. Additionally, all dice pool interactions with mortals are capped by Humanity until the Condition is resolved.
                                        Resolution: Your character spends 10 - Humanity hours in the presence of Shuffling Porters, fall into daysleep among corpses, seek comfort with a corpse.




                                        I left "seeking comfort" deliberately vague to allow for GMs to decide what's appropriate for an individual Sangiovanni. It could be as squicky as their previous iteration suggests, or it could be a bit more benign. Ideally, whatever it is, the obvious presence of dead bodies in this situation should be disquieting. If somebody walks in on a Sangiovanni seeking comfort, they could acquire the Notoriety Condition in regards to that person and others they might tell.
