        I think 'opening a jar,' would be a better example. Honestly I was having fun dwelling on it and I was thinking about a Blood Potency 10 vampire with 8 Resolve and 9 Composure but 1 Strength, who's going to struggle to open a jar in front of this girl they like, but decides to use Ride the Wave to get the strength required to open it. There was also a similar vampire who was at a gym with his friends, who bet him he couldn't press 500 lbs.

        Basically if you can activate Ride the Wave whenever you want, and you can choose the end goal of your Frenzy, you can now choose short term goals that require Strength, Stamina and Dexterity. Once you get over 2 Blood Potency you're just spending 1 Willpower a turn for however many extra dice you get rather than 1 Vitae for 2 from Physical Intensity. Once you're over 3, you can eschew Willpower's +3. Obviously if you really need, you can then spend Vitae in addition to your Riding.

        This is the type of fear that spawns a lot of 'is the rule really like that?' questions.

        EDIT: It's been a while. If you look into it, Riding the Wave is fairly complicated, it reminds me of diablerie mechanically. Actually using it as a Strength or other Physical Attribute substitute would require a lot of Willpower, unless one had a lot of Resolve or Composure. Basically really amping yourself up for something.
