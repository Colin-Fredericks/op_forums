    Gangrel Bloodline: The Cerbat Hunt

    04-23-2015, 02:32 PM
    At the moment, this is mostly Discipline rather than bloodline detailing itself; I've got a bare-bones narrative, but not much in the way of history besides that the founder of the bloodline ate her father in an attempt to drink down his knowledge of firearms, and was transfigured by his blood sorcery, an art for teaching the Beast how to act human; the two elements mixed and let her unleash the Beast through her father's gun by teaching the Beast how to fight like a human.


    Bloodline: The Cerbat Hunt
    Bloodline Weakness: The Even-Handed Curse. The Cannary suffer from the Gangrel clan weakness; the children of dust devils and the masters of the Man’s murderous urge aren’t exactly given to calmly and rationally working out their differences.

    In addition, the bloodline suffers another Curse; the assassin who lost his blood sorcery and his heartsblood to the line’s founder, his daughter and someone else’s childe, vowed on his rifle that the monster who ate his soul, and all that monster’s damned descendants, would forever lose their charnel invincibility, their unfair advantage in every gunfight, and the curse caught and bubbled within his daughter's decrepit, murderous heart. Gunsels take lethal damage from firearms, like mortals: bullets tear through flesh jaggedly, as if they were magical, rather than lodging in useless organs, and they burn - it’s a surprising amount of pain for any new inductee who’s learned to think of a gun in prey’s hands as an inconvenience rather than a murder weapon.
    Disciplines: Resilience, Animalism, Protean, Gunnery

    Gunnery
    The Modern Death (•): The Gunsel can “devote” a firearm so that it deals lethal damage to Kindred.
    Cost: (Size) Vitae + 1 Willpower
    Requirement: The vampire must anoint a firearm of Size 4 or less with her Vitae, which permanently marks it; encrusted blood seeps into the weapon or burns away once the Discipline is complete.
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: Instant
    Duration: Indefinite

    In the All Night Society, a gun - the favorite negotiation tool of murderers since time damn well near immemorial - pales before something as atavistic, as savagely stupid, as claws. Not so for the Gunsel. By infusing her firearm with her Vitae, she can propel bullets not with something as weak as powder, but with the rage of the Beast that’s learned how to fight like the Man.

    Effect: The Gunsel drips an amount of Vitae on the metal of her firearm equal to its Size to activate the Discipline. As the blood sinks in, iron and steel become stained, flecked with red spots; subtly, the curve of the barrel twists, and the trigger curls, until the firearm just slightly resembles the animal of the Gunsel’s Beast - with a barrel that almost looks toothed, a scaled grip, shark-like sights, or what-have-you - as if it had been built by a smith inspired by the Gunsel’s externalized Beast. This process takes about 5 minutes x the gun’s Size.

    Bullets fired from a devoted firearm deal lethal damage to Kindred, inflicting the same feeling (of white-hot light jammed into a little hard ball) as all bullets that strike Gunsels, and are counted as magical weapons; devoted firearms are also more effective for their creators, gaining 9-again on all Firearms rolls. For other people, a devoted firearm locks up, and will not fire unless it is paid in life. Anyone can pay an amount of Vitae equal to the firearm's size or physically bleed on it (for an equivalent amount of lethal damage) to key it to themselves for one scene, allowing them to use it. If this has occurred, the Gunsel can key her devoted firearms back to herself with a touch and a turn of concentration. A Gunsel can devote a number of firearms equal to her Blood Potency at any given time.

    Hematic Caliber (••): The Gunsel can load a firearm with “blood bullets,” sublimated from Vitae.
    Cost: 1 Vitae per clip
    Requirement: The vampire must drip Vitae into the ammunition chambers of the gun, unless she is loading a devoted firearm.
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: Instant or Reflexive

    Ordinary blood has a little iron content; Vitae is blood par excellence. With this Discipline, the Gunsel can sublimate iron-hard clots from her blood to fit a weapon.

    Effect: If the Gunsel is using a normal firearm, she must reload the weapon as normal, with the difference being that she leaks her own blood in lieu of actual ammunition; if she uses a devoted firearm, the blood immediately coalesces inside the chamber, without the need for its user to load it physically. One Vitae, for a normal gun, produces a full clip of characteristically reddish bullets, composed of hardened, inert blood. (For nonstandard ammunition sizes, more Vitae may be required.)

    Blood bullets, if removed from the chamber rather than fired, immediately begin to decay into flakes of dried blood, becoming unusable. This process also occurs once they’ve struck a victim or come to a complete halt, making blood bullets generally superior to the standard for those looking not to be detected; however, Kindred Senses do apply to locating flakes of dried blood from a hemoglobin bullet.

    Blood Will Out (•••): The Gunsel’s Kindred Senses function much more effectively against anyone with a bullet she fired embedded in them.
    Cost: None
    Requirement: The target must have been shot by the vampire, and have an untreated lethal wound caused by said.
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: None
    Duration: Blood Potency in nights

    Effect: Logically, if a gun is the extension of its owner, then so too are the bullets it fires. With this Discipline, the Gunsel can practically feel the flesh she shoots, and attune her senses to victims who haven’t quite been finished off. She multiplies the range for her Kindred Senses by five against people with untreated gunshot wounds, and is treated as having tasted the blood of those she’s shot for the purposes of tracking. If used against Kindred, with a devoted firearm (so the bullets will really penetrate), this Discipline may be invoked to increase the Gunsel’s blood sympathy with the target by one step, to a maximum of Once-Removed, for her Blood Potency in nights.

    Example: Jane of the Unknown, a Gunsel with Blood Will Out, shoots Alan, a hunter, with her devoted pistol; she deals 3 lethal damage, but is briefly occupied by having to fend off and kill Alan’s friends while he escapes. Normally, since she has Blood Potency 4, she can only hear heartbeats at twelve meters; luckily, Alan hasn’t had time to dig out the bullet in his arm. Jane’s range for hearing Alan’s heartbeat increases to 60 meters, and she can smell his blood from 200 meters; since she’s “tasted” it with her bullet, she can add her Blood Potency to rolls to track him by scent. Alan, sure that he’s evaded her, sits down and starts trying to dig out the bullet with a knife, unaware of the bounty hunter coming ever closer…

    Kiss of Cordite (••••): The Gunsel can drain Vitae from bullet wounds at a distance.
    Cost: 1 Willpower
    Requirement: The target must have been shot by the vampire, and have an untreated lethal wound caused by said.
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Firearms + Gunnery vs. Stamina + Supernatural Tolerance
    Action: Instant

    If the vampire’s bullets are her teeth, then her throat can’t be too far away. With this level of mastery of Gunnery, the Gunsel is able to reach out through the fear and the smell of smoke and drag little droplets of Vitae, sublimated to gas, through the air, until all her sight lines are hungry throats.

    Effect: To use this Discipline, the vampire’s target must be within three times her Blood Potency in meters.

    Dramatic Failure: The vampire’s attempt to feed only triggers the blood to heal her target’s wounds. All lethal wounds on her target caused by her firearms attacks within the scene convert to bashing, and become inert for the purposes of Kiss of Cordite.
    Failure: The blood trickles, just for a second, but spills to the ground and becomes inert. The Gunsel cannot use Kiss of Cordite again until she inflicts more lethal wounds on her target.
    Success: The Gunsel drinks deeply through her leaden teeth. She gains an amount of Vitae equal to the lethal damage dealt by the relevant attack, instantly; this also inflicts the Scarred Condition, as if the Gunsel had fed violently on her target (since, essentially, she has).
    Exceptional Success: The Gunsel drains an extra lethal wound’s worth of Vitae, dealing one lethal damage to the target.

    Six-Chambered Beast (•••••): The Gunsel, while frenzied or riding the wave, wields devoted firearms as extensions of herself.
    Cost: -
    Requirement: The vampire must be carrying a devoted firearm.
    Dice Pool: -
    Action: -
    Duration: Frenzy

    A devoted firearm is the power of the Beast, conducted through the medium of the Man - in the hands of the Beast, itself, it becomes an iron talon, an atavistic coil of stored thunder that kills unflinchingly, a culmination of firearm devotion.

    Effect: This Discipline triggers whenever the Gunsel enters anger or hunger frenzy, either involuntarily or through riding the wave, unless she throws her devoted gun away before entering frenzy.

    During frenzy:
    • The vampire uses Gunnery Disciplines instinctively, and does not expend Vitae or Willpower to do so - feeding with Kiss of Cordite in hunger frenzy, or hunting down the injured with Blood Will Out - as long as she uses a devoted firearm.
    • The Beast knows everything about its devoted firearms that the vampire knows, and uses that knowledge instinctively. It can, for example, assemble a devoted ceramic pistol or piece together a devoted shotgun that’s been taken apart, take cover, aim before firing, refrain from wildly firing when using a devoted sniper rifle, etc.; while it is inclined to do whatever is expedient, if the Beast is trying to kill a rapidly fleeing target, it won’t attempt to pursue on foot if it has access to a devoted gun that can hit from a distance. It won’t use non-devoted guns, though - those are just lumps of strangely-shaped metal, not natural weapons.
    • The vampire can temporarily convert firearms to devoted weapons. She reflexively spits 1 Vitae on a firearm, which immediately sinks in and violently deforms it in a second; weapons altered in this way do not count towards the vampire’s maximum number of devoted firearms. Guns altered in this way lose all Structure and fall apart at the end of the scene, losing their devoted status.
    • The vampire gains 8-again on Firearms rolls, and can spend 3 Vitae and 1 Willpower to make such a roll a rote action. The flow of blood and force through the devoted firearm necessary to accomplish this is hard on it; a normal devoted firearm suffers 1 Structure damage and becomes unusable until repaired, while temporary devoted firearms created through Six-Chambered Beast lose all Structure and burst, sloughing apart, without the capacity to contain the Beast flowing within; they become permanently unusable.
    Devotions:
    Depraved Holster (Protean 2, Gunnery 1)
    Cost: None; supplements Unmarked Grave and Predatory Aspect
    Requirement: The firearm involved must have been made devoted.
    Dice Pool: -
    Action: None

    Effect: When the Gunsel slumbers in her Unmarked Grave, the devoted firearms on her person, made mutable by the dreamlike sleep of earth and the thinning of Protean boundaries, can return to the Beast’s own sheaf of weapons. When changing her adaptations in her Unmarked Grave, the Gunsel can consume one of her devoted firearms - as long as it’s on her person - and gain the following adaptation as one of her three:

    Tongue of Fire: The Gangrel may extrude a gun from her flesh. She reflexively draws a devoted firearm, which she earlier consumed, from a rent in her flesh, or lets it bubble out of her veins and patch itself together; adaptations to her senses and hands give her 8-again with this firearm, and she cannot drop it, since it’s fused to her hands in firing position, unless she chooses to let go. This also allows her to empower the gun with her Beast: every point of Vitae she spends increases the gun’s Damage by 1 for one shot.

    The adaptation created by Depraved Holster is added to, and replaces, one of the three adaptations she has available. If she changes her adaptations in her Unmarked Grave, she may remove the Depraved Holster adaptation, but in the process, the Beast fully consumes and digests the weapon - so it’s unwise to use this Devotion on your Touchstone heirloom flintlock.

    This Devotion costs 1 Experience. A variant of this Devotion exists, using Protean 4 instead of 2 and with a cost of 3 Experiences:
    Black Mamba (Protean 4, Gunnery 2)
    Cost: None; supplements Unmarked Grave and Unnatural Aspect
    Requirement: The firearm involved must have been made devoted.
    Dice Pool: -
    Action: None

    Effect: Same as Depraved Holster, but the adaptation created is the following, replacing her Unnatural Aspect adaptation rather than one of her Predatory Aspect adaptations:

    Black Mamba: The gun and the Beast are one, and the Beast and the flesh are one. The Gunsel grows a mobile protuberance underneath her skin, interlaced with the mechanisms of an unpleasantly organic gun and attached to eyes designed to swiftly evaluate threats. The Gunsel can focus to extend the barrel and vision mechanism from any part of her body as a reflexive action, and may fire as an instant action, without using her hands or any part of her body; she takes no penalty for firing at things she can’t see, thanks to the Mamba’s eyes. She may also reload with Hematic Caliber at the same time that she aims.
