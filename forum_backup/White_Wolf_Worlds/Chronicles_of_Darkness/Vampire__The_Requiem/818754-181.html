                    That's up to you and your ST if a Mandragora can function as a retainer. Unless you have other powers to use on a mandragora, it can't uproot itself and walk around if that's what you mean by moving. It moves within the reach of its branches and roots.

                    Typically mandragora would be used to function as a stationary barrier for security or to produce lacrima. A poisonous (to humans) substance that can be a potential substitute for vitae or consumable drink for vampires. Or possibly a component for vampiric alchemical mixtures or potions.

                    No you can't teach or learn disciplines by default from a mandragora. That's ST fiat on whether or not it's possible. That said, there was an Invictus NPC in one setting I played in who used lacrima to bottle and sell discipline powers as one time per drink use.

                                                    I guess that leads me to a new question:

                                                    What would you guys recommend if I want to guard my minions against a Ventrue's Dominate? How can I make sure a Ventrue isn't just going to walk up to my minion and use my own minion against me with Dominate? Countering his Dominate with my own Entombed Command could potentially work, as we discussed above. But what other options would I have? What if I don't have access to Dominate myself? How would a Mekhet protect his minions against Dominate meddling? How would a Nosferatu? A Gangrel?

                                                    Or is Dominate literally so overpowered that there is no way I can protect my minions against it, other than telling all my minions to avoid eye contact? Which in no way is a solid protection, the Ventrue can just brute-force the eye contact with a grapple and hold move, or if the Ventrue also has 1 dot in Majesty he can just use Awe.

                                                    And even if somehow a high-Resolve human with the Indomitable Merit manages to resist a Ventrue's attempt at Mesmerize, what's stopping the Ventrue from simply trying again? As long as the failure is not a dramatic failure, the human does not necessarily realize the Ventrue tried to mesmerize him. So the Ventrue can then just try again, and again, and again, and again, until the ghoul finally realizes something weird is going on. There's no Vitae cost to Mesmerize, so other than Storyteller intervention, there's nothing stopping a Ventrue from just trying over and over again until he succeeds.

                                                        The most straightforward limitation is sheer logistics: the vampire can only give one command at a time, can only prime a single target at a time, can only be in one place at a time, isn't invisible by default, and needs to spend a nontrivial amount of time making their statements.

                                                        Without The Lying Mind, Mesmerize only lets you edit memories of things that happened pretty recently. (With The Lying Mind, you have to engage in fairly obvious blood-based behavior and are spending a minute of description for the crudest of alterations.) Without Obfuscate, the vampire has to give some thought to the problem of witnesses. (With Obfuscate, keeping any involved party that isn't yourself costs precious Vitae.) If your mark is being watched (as they might well be in a detective story), they're going to look and/or sound odd if giving up the information or making the confession is out of sorts for them. (If they aren't being watched, it's possible that's deliberate; they could be a plant or they could be missing vital information or the thing they witnessed wasn't the important part.)

                                                        There's a reason this kind of complex machination generally involves establishing a power base or doing a lot of scattered piecemeal assembly here and there, and that's because these issues exist even with mundane conspiracy-intrigue scenarios. Time is at a premium when you're allergic to daylight, "information is power" is a truism for more than just the Mekhet, and greater degrees of stealth and information control take up resources you could save for other things.

                                                        That being said, yes, this is less of a concern for vampires dealing with purely human systems when allowed to act with relative freedom.
