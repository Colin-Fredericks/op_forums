    Homebrew: Malkavia, Merits and Devotions

    08-17-2015, 07:41 PM
    Trying to update the Malkavia disease into 2nd Edition. Bear with me here because it's a little rough so far.

    Condition: Malkavia

    The character suffers a supernatural affliction of the Blood that causes mental instability or outright insanity. The player and storyteller should work together to determine how Malkavia manifests in that individual and what Conditions may potentially arise from their symptom. When the character encounters sufficient stress (suffering a breaking point, taking lethal damage, when a Kindred lashes out with a Predatory Aura) the character must make a Composure roll. On a failure, the character loses control and their instability manifests. This will often resolve in a secondary Condition being applied, appropriate to the stimulus and the particular symptoms the character encounters. Appropriate conditions include, but are not limited to: Bestial, Broken, Confused, Delusional, Distracted, Frightened, Guilty, Obsession, Shaken, Spooked, Swooning, or Wanton. Any Conditions acquired from Malkavia are resolved normally.

    Beat: The character's irrationality causes them to act inappropriately in a social setting that causes them humiliation, or the character's instability leads them to suffer a breaking point.

    Resolution: The character staves off the affliction (see below)

    System: A mortal that consumes the Vitae of an afflicted Kindred in any quantity makes a Resolve check at the point of consumption or be afflicted with the Malkavia Condition for a number of nights equal to the Blood Potency of the Vampire whose Vitae they consumed. On a dramatic failure, they were afflicted for a number of weeks equal to the Vampire's Blood Potency.

    A Kindred who drinks the blood of a mortal with the Malkavia Condition resists with Resolve + Blood Potency - the number of Vitae they have consumed.. Similarly, they are afflicted for a number of nights equal to the Blood Potency of the Vampire that infected the mortal, however, when they wake each subsequent night, they may roll their Resolve to shake off the affliction. However, each drink makes the affliction more difficult to resist. If they consume blood from an infected mortal before the infection is lapsed, they receive a cumulative penalty for each Vitae consumed to the roll to resist. Rolling a dramatic failure on any roll to resist will result in the affliction being extended a number of weeks equal to the Blood Potency of the original Vampire.

    Example: Cheshire has a Resolve 2 and Blood Potency 3. She consumes two points of Vitae from an afflicted lick and immediately rolls her Resolve + Blood Potency -2 (3 dice) to resist being afflicted. Cheshire rolls three dice with no successes. The Vampire who afflicted the mortal has a Blood Potency of four, so she is afflicted for four nights. She awakens the following night and rolls her Resolve, scoring zero successes. The same evening, she drinks from the same vessel again, this time consuming three blood points, so she now rolls her Resolve + Blood Potency -5 (two from the first drink, three from the second) and is reduced to a Chance Die. The chance die unfortunately comes up as a dramatic failure, and she is now afflicted for four weeks plus three nights.

    Kindred can also afflict each other in two different ways. When an afflicted Kindred uses a Psychic Discipline on another Kindred (Animalism, Auspex, Dominate, Majesty, or Nightmare) and rolls an exceptional success and the resistance roll fails, the Kindred gains the Malkavia condition for the remainder of the scene. If the resistance roll is a dramatic failure and the activation roll is an exceptional success, the condition lasts for the rest of the night. (Spreading the Condition this way can also afflict mortals).

    Note: While Dominate, Majesty, and Nightmare can work this way at any level, Animalism 4 and 5 and Auspex 4 are the only levels of those Disciplines that would specifically target another Kindred. This may not be balanced, per se. Open to suggestions.

    Finally, consuming the Vitae of an afflicted Kindred can spread the infection. This is treated similarly to drinking from a mortal (resist with Resolve + Blood Potency) but the afflicted Kindred must score a number of successes equal to the Blood Potency of the afflicted Vampire to successfully resist. If they initial roll does not achieve the required number of successes, they are afflicted with the Malkavia Condition indefinitely. They can resist with Resolve every subsequent night when they rise. They have a number of nights equal to their Humanity to accumulate successes equal to the Blood Potency of the afflicted Kindred. If they fail to accumulate enough successes over that period, they Malkavia Condition becomes Persistent.

    Example: Beta Cruces consumes the Vitae of an afflicted Kindred in a botched attempt at Diablerie. Unfortunately, not only did the Kindred escape, but he was also afflicted with Malkavia. Beta has a Resolve of 3 and a Blood Potency of 1. The Kindred he drank from was a powerful Elder with a Blood Potency of 6. Beta Immediately rolls his Resolve + Blood Potency of four dice and only gets two successes--he is now afflicted. Beta's Humanity is 5, so he must accumulate 6 successes on his resolve checks within the next five nights or be afflicted permanently. The next night he arises, Beta rolls his Resolve of 3 and gets one success. He is afflicted for the remainder of the night. The next night he manages to roll two successes, halfway to beating the disease, but only three nights left. The next two nights he rolls no successes. He has one night remaining. The last night, he miraculously rolls 3 successes and is no longer afflicted.

    Malkavia may or may not be passed from Sire to Childe of the Sire's Condition is Persistent (storyteller / player discretion). At Storyteller discretion, commiting Diablerie on an afflicted Kindred may result in an automatic Persistent Malkavia Condition.

    MERITS:

    Infectious Aura     ••
    Prerequisite: The Malkavia Condition

    The character's Predatory Aura becomes a vector for the Malkavia Disease. When the character lashes out with his Predatory Aura, any who fail to resist gain the Malkavia Condition as if they had used a Psychic Discipline.

    Maddening Visions •
    Prerequisite: The Malkavia Condition

    The Kindred is able to see the truth in the patterns only she can perceive. The Kindred takes on a Condition appropriate to their Malkavia symptoms for a scene. Once the Condition is resolved, rather than gaining a Beat, they may elect either be granted the Inspired Condition, or gain a +3 bonus to an Investigation, Empathy, or Crafts roll.

    Oracular Insanity •••
    Prerequisite: Maddening Visions

    The Kindred's ability to perceive unique patterns now borders on Precognition. The Kindred takes on a Condition appropriate to their Malkavia symptoms and a particular mystery or problem they are focusing on for an entire night. At the end of the night, the Condition ends, and the Vampire rolls Blood Potency + Wits. For each success, the Storyteller gives the player one clue regarding the subject of their vision.

    Devotions coming next--Malkavia-afflicted Vampires can use their madness in concert with mental Disciplines for some unusual effects.

    Feedback appreciated!

                                          My first thought is to tweak a little the rules on Malkavia itself. Instead of a Condition with too vague effects, make it this way:

                                          The character develops three Malkavia Breaking Points, of the player's choice. They can be as common or rare, as logical or arbitrary as the player wants. To each Breaking Point the player can ascribe one to three Conditions of the player's choice. Even if physical, those Conditions manifest from psychological symptoms, so the ST can revise the Resolution clauses. Yes, your mind can make you blind or paralytic.

                                          I thing this makes both more sense and a clearer line of work to develop each disease.

                                          You should also revise the Merits. Infectious Aura is cool, but useless, because you have no benefit from causing Malkavia on another. Unless the Merit also gives you some benefit from causing the malady or a way to reap benefits from other victims, it is better as a possible vector from the start than a Merit.

                                          Also revise Maddening Visions ASAP. It isn't clear in there if it happens when you acquire the Condition or if it lets you spontaneously acquire it for the scene. In the first case, it is almost useless, since I can hardly see someone choosing the bonuses instead of a sweet Beat unless they already Resolved another Condition in the scene. But in the second case you have a Crazy Beat Machine, with the player taking on symptoms every scene to guarantee Beats coming and coming. Maybe you could change it to every time a character rolls success on a Malkavia Breaking Point roll, the character also receives the Inspired Condition.

                                          For Oracular Insanity, I would make some clearer rules. Looking at a Werewolf Merit that lets you redistribute its dots as Social Merits for the chapter, I had this idea: each time you take a Condition from a Malkavia Breaking Point, choose a 3 dot Merit geared toward get information. You have this Merit until you take another Malkavia Condition, when you can choose another. Even changing Merits, any limit of usages remains in place, so, if you choose Common Sense, as an example, you can only use it once per chapter, no mater how much times you choose it again for new Breaking Points. You still can use it twice by having Common Sense also as a normal Merit. For now, the available Merits I can think of are Common Sense, Aura Reading, Mind of a Madman, Omen Sensitivity, Psychometry, Telepathy (3-dot version, only), Unseen Sense, Distinguished Palate and Dream Visions.

                                          Curiously in this search I thought of a warped version of Cacophony Savvy called Malkavian Madness Network...

                                          Reckless Might could be Vigor 1, Celerity 1, and get rid of the Stamina comparison: using this Devotion always results in a Tilt.
