    Vampirism is a supernatural curse, not a biological condition. Water should be treated as any other non-blood liquid. The reason blood isn't expelled is because it's stored and used up. The vampiric body has no reason to store water and no way to get rid of it outside of purging it or using Blush of Life, like other beverages.

                                                    Vampires aren't susceptible to soul loss in the first place (not explicitly stated, but all soul loss mechanics in VtR core only mentions Integrity, not Humanity) so it would be a matter of how you implement soul loss as a house rule in the first place.
                                                    If it works like it does on humans I see no reason you wouldn't be able to diablerize them. Disciplines, Skills and Blood Potency are present and accounted for. However, if soul loss is implemented the same way it affects mages (slowly losing your magical abilities) you might not be able to gain a Discipline dot, any Blood Experiences or, if it's not possible to diablerize at all at that stage, not even a Skill dot.
