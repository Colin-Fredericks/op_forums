                                            The issue with Vampires vs Garou isn't the massive regeneration, it's Hit and Run. Hit and Run allows the Werewolf to perfectly avoid one attack per turn at the low cost of 1e with no limit. It even works against surprise attacks. The only way I know of to stop it is grappling, and even that isn't clear-cut. For punching through regeneration you'll want to use Brawl (bite + feed), Grapple Style 3, Ride the Wave 1, and Professional Training 5 can do it. Melee with a high damage code can do it. Chokehold works against any werewolves with a Stamina of 5 or less in Garou. Vigor is very helpful at all levels. What none of that can do is bypass Hit and Run.
