                        Originally posted by Captain Shoe View Post
                        How bestial is a vampire in frenzy? It's described as an animal, but also described as knowing how to throw things.

                        Does it know how to balance on a ledge so as to not fall off, to get to its prey faster (if its prey was the frenzy's goal)? Does it know how to use most Disciplines? Does it know how to use firearms? (I'm guessing it would drop any firearms or weapons it's holding and use its claws instead)

                        Also, can one use Physical Intensity while frenzying? Do the benefits stack?

                        Edit: Can one Ride the Wave and change the Frenzy's desire from "kill the target" to something a bit contrarian, like "shoot the target"?
                        For the most part, I say "yes" to all the above. "Beastial" is a very thematic way of describing things, but its not exactly accurate. Beastial and animal-like*, in this case, is a reference to the loss of the ability to reason and think rationally, to plan ahead or care about consequences. I think this particular line from the core book section on frenzy sums up my take on the situation. "End the problem by any means necessary." If shooting a gun or using a Discipline is the best way to acomplish that goal, then yes, it absolutely will use those.

                        Though, it should be noted that some Disciplines and tactics are ill suited for the state of frenzy. Frenzies are "immediate, forceful and destructive abandon." Calm and collected Dominate uses tend to be inappropriate, for instance, as is a carefully constructed Nightmare illusion. Using Obfuscate during a fear frenzy or Majesty for a hunger frenzy wouldn't be amiss, I imagine. Auspex to track prey would likely as well. Protean and the three Physicals are naturally a given. Frenzy is all about losing control over your emotions and lashing out at the world to satisfy a pressing need. If its something that doesn't rely on human ability to plan ahead or avoid violence, then its allowed.



                        * The only reference to animal behavior is the single line that a Kindred is capable of doing the kinds of things an enraged, terrified, or starving animal predator is, and that is actually meant to imply a certain level of cunning, not a restriction on behaviors. Especially given the previous line, "the Beast isn't stupid."
