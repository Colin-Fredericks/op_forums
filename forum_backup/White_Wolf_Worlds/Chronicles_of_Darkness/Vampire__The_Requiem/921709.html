    [2 ed.] Possession

    06-26-2016, 06:07 PM
    Hi all,
    if I'm not wrong in the first edition, possession doesn't apply against other vampires. In second edition it isn't written anything that let you understand this. It simply says that the roll is Intelligence + Intimidation + Dominate – victim’s Resolve. And in the text it says that: "If she attempts to kill herself the victim may roll Resolve + Blood Potency as a reflexive action, on a success he evicts the vampire from his consciousness" so it means that is definetly possible to possess another vampire body. The question is: isn't too eays for an average vampire to possess the body of an ancient vampire. I means., with an average dice pool of 10-12 dice, subtracting 5 for resolve it's quite easy to obtain a success with 5-7 dice.
    Am I missing something?

                                                          I think I'm the only one who made that argument, and I think you may have misunderstood what I was getting at. It's not that Dominate 5 is inherently hard to get (thank goodness 2e got rid of exponentially increasing XP costs), it's that it's far from universally accessible. You have to either play a Ventrue or drink one's Vitae, and then it costs 6-15 experiences for a Ventrue or 16-20 for another vampire, which could be going towards getting you other 5th dot powers (which are also quite effective against elders) or increasing Blood Potency to narrow the gap between yourself and elders. Plus, experience purchases need to be justified in the narrative, and while different groups will have different standards for justifying 5th dot Discipline purchases, any way you slice it, Dominate 5 represents a significant commitment of meta-game resources and narrative investment. In my opinion, players who dedicate that much time and energy into being good at mind controlling people should be very good at mind controlling people.
