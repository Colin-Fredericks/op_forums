    What it Means to Be a Neonate in Your Cities

    08-10-2015, 09:26 AM
    I've an idea for a Domain. Not a fully fleshed out idea yet, but that'll come when/if I run a Chronicle there. Rather than start at the top and work my way down, however, I thought I'd start at the bottom and work my way up. To that end, I have come to the forums to ask for help fleshing this Domain out, but also to start a conversation about what it means to be a neonate in all Domains.

    Basically, I'll post the concept of my Domain, and discuss how I think neonates live there. I'd love it if y'all would offer suggestions, advice, and criticisms, but also submit your own Domains and how neonates spend their Requiems in them.

    So without further ado...

    Ordo Dracul - Executors of the Second Tradition

    The Dragons rule this Domain. They do not waste time enforcing the ancient traditions of the Camarilla, nor do they care for the puerile squabbling of mortal politics or temporal power. However, the Order in this Domain is strangely concerned with one of the three Traditions: "Sire another at the peril of both yourself and your progeny." In this Domain, creation of new Kindred is strictly controlled. Rumors abound as to why the Ordo cares so much about this Tradition, historically the one most ignored. They have allied with one of the other Covenants and authorized them to enforce this law with extreme prejudice.

    Along with this strict adherence to the Second Tradition, the Dragons hoard their Kindred subjects jealously: visiting vampires are not permitted within the Domain without invitation, and Kindred who attempt to leave are hunted down and dragged back. They disappear one way or the other, but rumors of secret underground labs and dungeons flood the local Cacophony. Along with a message frequently attributed to the Ordo: "Only Dragons May Fly". Usually written in Bulgarian or Romanian.

    The Ordo performs frequent, random census-taking. It is rumored that they have a master list of the locations of all of the Havens in the city. If a Kindred doesn't participate in the census, something they love is destroyed within 24 hours. What are the Dragons doing that requires such strict measures of population control?

    Neonates in the Domain

    Nobody feels safe in this domain. The Ordo Dracul does not discuss their business with outsiders, and with their allies they hold a great deal of power in the city. They control the Rack, they have mortal officials in their pockets or under Vinculum, they keep a close eye on the populace. Despite the many freedoms that vampires are allowed, it is rumored that the Dragons perform painful, lethal experiments on the Kindred that they dislike, or who cause too many problems. Many vampires have simply disappeared without an official notice from the city's masters.

    This city-wide fear and paranoia affects the neonates the most. They are already scared and unfamiliar with unlife. They share the night with ancient monsters that see them as potential threats. When the whole city believes that it is facing down the barrel of a gun, extra threats are not tolerated. Even with the harsh punishment for killing another vampire, there are ways to maintain power over a neonate. Blood Bonding is common, as are physical threats of violence. It's even worse for low-ranked members of the Order, who are no more privy to the secrets of the masters of the city than anybody else. The neonates who join the Ordo Dracul do so because they think that they'll be safe and protected as Dragons.

    Occasionally, neonates are delivered to the doorsteps of known coterie hangouts. They have no memory of being Embraced, and do not know who their Sires are. The coterie is told that no harm shall come to the neonate. Naturally, these forced adoptees, colloquially known as either Prodigies or Moles, are treated with intense scrutiny and paranoia, and no small amount of fear.

    To be a neonate in this Domain is to be a test subject, a scapegoat, or a victim. You always have a sense that you're being watched, or hunted, or simply caged.

      Surviving as a neonate in my main domain means accepting oppression.

      Until recently the domain had been a major Carthian strong hold in the region. The Carthians had handled the politics of the domain while a influential Lance contingent addressed the local kindreds' spiritual needs and the Ordo Dracul conducted it's experiments under the protection of secular Carthians who hoped to benefit from "treatments" for the curse. The Circle and Invictus had both been banned. Unfortunately one of the tricks the Carthian/Lance establishment had used to ensure local stability and hold on to power was to encourage the more radical members of their covenants to "crusade in" or "carry the revolution to" neighboring domains.

      After a particularly nasty series of raids an extremely powerful Dragon in a nearby Invictus controlled domain got completely fed up. He used his mastery of the Mystery of the Voivode to build up a powerful force of combat trained ghouls and childer, and recruited mystically powerful allies from a Livian sect (loosely inspired by the Qedeshah from Bloodlines: the Hidden). A force of nearly a dozen mystically potent vampires, backup by over 30 combat trained ghouls descended on the domain and purged it. The leaders of the Carthians and the Lance were assassinated on the first night of the attack, and the more radical and militant members of each covenant were then hunted down over the next several weeks. The Invaders then installed their Dragon leader as "Voivode" and declared themselves the new rulers of the domain.

      Many neonates among the Carthians and the Lance survived the purge, but their patrons among the Elders and Ancilla usually didn't. Local Dragons were completely spared despite the fact that they and the invaders don't really trust each other, so many neonates have taken shelter with the local Ordo. However, this means exchanging the status of "citizen" for that of "slave", which inspires no small amount of resentment.

      Other neonates found that the Livian sect among the invaders consider themselves "mothers to monsters" and are happy to provide protection and support to disenfranchised neonates. This comes with a steep price however, as the "mothers" make extensive use of Majesty based devotions to "guide and counsel" their "adopted children". Life with the Qedeshah is comfortable and safe, but it's a gilded cage built out of emotional co-dependence.

      Some opportunists try to ingratiate themselves to the new regime, but the Voivode demands a full vinculum on all of his direct followers and makes extensive use of Dominate. Those who join with him gain power in the new order, at the expense of their freewill when dealing with that order's leader. A brave few try to go it alone as unaligned, but they know they are under constant scrutiny and that the slightest misstep will lead to them joining their former leaders in final death.

      Those embraced after the regime change have to deal with the fact that the Voivode considers himself a "traditionalist". He was heavily influenced by the Invictus rulers of his former domain, and expects neonates to submit to the authority of their sires. Conversely, sires are considered responsible for the behavior of their progeny until they are formally manumitted. This means new vampires are kept under strict control by those who embrace them.

      Being a neonate means dealing with the fact that much more powerful monsters run your world, and you have to keep them appeased if you want to survive and thrive. You're lucky to avoid notice or to just be treated like an errand boy.

              In my domain (which I've yet to run, waiting until I can get Blood Sorcery) being a neonate means technically being property of an Ancillae or Elder. The city has it so all vampires less than 30 years dead are considered to be property of their sire (or their sire's sire if their sire is still too young), although for most vampires this is seen more as a formality as long as you don't make a fool of yourself. A resourceful neonate can make a name for themselves, and the (theoretical) goal is to be offered a position in a covenant once your 'service' is up.

              The city also doesn't have the standard view of neonates and ancillae. In Berkingham* you're considered a fledgling until 5 years dead, a neonate for the 25 years after that, and then have 70 years of existence as an ancilla.

              *pronounced Barkinghim for those unfamiliar with how weird British place names can be. I wanted a fake county town that sounds real.

              Blue is sarcasm.

              If I suggestion I make contradicts in-setting metaphysics please ignore me, I probably brought in scientific ideas.

                  It's good to be a neonate in post-Katrina New Orleans. The diminished Kindred population led to opportunities for ambitious up-and-comers, and the resulting tense situation accelerated the downfall of the major players as the elders set to openly warring with each other. Now there aren't so many elders left, and a few of the neonates are wondering how high they can rise. Of course, this situation looks equally attractive to elders who are looking to settle in a new domain where they can take control, and the general breakdown of Kindred society has allowed some dangerous elements to take root, so it won't be easy. There's a lot of opportunity for young vampires, but only if they align themselves with a faction. The unaligned are pretty much extinct at this point, since there's nobody protecting them. The Invictus have pretty much been driven from the city as well, around the time Belial's Brood moved in. The other covenants have internal conflicts as well as external ones. Good times.

                  We'll see whether the PCs will be able to put things in order, or at least carve out a stable situation for themselves amid the chaos.

                          Being a neonate in my Requiem city means you are the newest member in a dysfunctional family (read: Clan). Someone decided that you had something that was worth killing you for and resurrecting you as a vampire. Now you are stuck with your older siblings, your mad grandparents and deranged aunts and uncles. You'll be asked to run errants that could potentially put you into unlife threatening situations, you are going to deal with stuff the elders refuse to even acknowledge as a problem, and you are going to accumulate power and enemies in the process. But since you aren't caught up in ages-old arguments, prejuidice, hatred and anger, you are actually able to work together with others of your kind.

                              This is material for my write-up of Metro Manila as a Kindred Domain. It still needs a lot of fleshing out, but I am open to ideas here.

                              The Carthian Movement - The New Regime

                              With the Strix and their servants scoured from the region, the local Carthians are moving to fill the power vacuum left by the Prince's death. Their leader was quickly elected as the domain's first President, and he has spearheaded the Movement's efforts to supplant the old systems of his predecessor.

                              Naturally, such reforms have not gone unchallenged by the domain's historical leaders. The Invictus and the Sanctified still command much influence despite their reduced numbers. And while they have not openly rebelled against the newly ascendant covenant (yet), they are a constant thorn to the Carthians' side.

                              The Carthians of Metro Manila claim diversity as their greatest strength. Many Kindred of the Movement also hold membership in other covenants by virtue of the Right of Return. Hence, it is not uncommon for members to demonstrate knowledge in Cruac or the Mysteries of the Dragon (or even Theban Sorcery or Invictus Oaths). These cosmopolitan practices grant them the tentative support of local Acolytes and Dragons in their work to bring new life into the Black Pearl.

                              Neonates in Metro Manila

                              The Requiem of a neonate in the Black Pearl of the Orient is rife with danger and opportunity. Most younger vampires fall under the Carthians' lead, and those who flock to other covenants (excluding most in the Invictus and the Lancea et Sanctum) still pay token support to the new regime. The Carthians have a vested interest in every neonate, and are well aware of that each is a potential boon (or future threat) to their newfound authority.

                              The cold war between the Carthians and the Invictus/Sanctified alliance means they are eager to bring as much fresh blood into their respective ranks as possible. The Ordo Dracul and the Circle of the Crone, meanwhile, are less proactive in finding recruits. But they will happily cut into such disputes if they find fledglings they believe would belong in their ranks. Hence, unaligned neonates more often than not find themselves drawn into the schemes of covenants seeking their services. And those whose sires belong to a given covenant are often pressured to follow their lead.

                              However, there is no shortage of threats to a fledgling vampire's unlife from other sources. The local Werewolves are perhaps the most notable. They were victims of purges enacted by the Invictus while they were still in power, and they are not blind to how weak their oppressors have become. More than one newly Embraced vampire has met an early Final Death at a werewolf's claws because he was in the wrong place and the wrong time. And those sired by the Gangrel, particularly the Dead Wolves, are frequently marked as prey in a number of their hunts.

                              And then there are Strix. For not all of the Birds of Dis were vanquished from the city. These lingering creatures wait patiently for the right time to strike, and they do not lack in suitable puppets for their plans.

                              To be a neonate in Metro Manila is to find oneself and to take a side. There is opportunity to be found with whichever faction you join, but danger lurks around the corner for those who don't have their wits about them.

                                  Neonates are both a prestigious item and a risk of the highest order. Fledgling kindered are seen as children and the sire is responsible for their actions of his child. It prestigious show of resources to be able to feed one self and a child. Once the sire feels ready to have his child acknowledge as a neonate and is ready to stand on their own two feet, the child is at the mercy of those with status. So most new fledgling stay under control of their sire until deemed worthy bring forth on their acknowledgment day. This puts the child and sire at the most precarious situation, rivals will try to trick, tease, and torment the child into losing control of the beast. It's not unheard of both the sire and child facing final death on acknowledgement day. Once acknowledged by the Prince, child becomes a neonate.

                                  Neonate now need a to earn a name for themselves if they want to be recognized than just the child of so and so (which pretty much faceless). Elders will not even give time to a neonate and find them lacking anything of value. A common saying among neonates is it's better to be unnoticed than noticed by elders. The ancilla see them as most middle management does, tools to gain favors from and use to raise oneself. This give rise to why neonates group up into coretries and pool their resourses for security and advantages.
