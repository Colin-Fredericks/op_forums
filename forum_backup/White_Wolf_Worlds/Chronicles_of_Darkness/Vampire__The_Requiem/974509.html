    How Flexible are kindred powers ?

    09-22-2016, 01:33 PM
    I wanted to know how flexible are kindred powers ?

    When you read them you see each one has a set effect. I wanted to know can you bend some of these effects a bit to do things the original power did not state ?

    Example

    Iron Edict

    Could rather then using it on yourself to make yourself not be scared or even make yourself believe your stronger ?

    like in the anime Rurouni Kenshin where Kenshin was fighting Kurogasa?

      Disciplines are generally not flexible at all.

      If you want them to do something new, that is usually the domain of Devotions.

      Your specific example has been a matter of debate that I'll leave up to other people to comment on.

      Whether you are a valid target for your own Domination powers has come up before. I think the usual consensus is that you can, but I can't speak for certain on that.

          I'm in the "no, you can't dominate yourself" camp. First of all you need eye contact, though that's possible with a mirror.                   Yes and no. Normally deviations from the descriptions are the purview of devotions and usually require multiple disciplines that contribute the desired effect and for the user to possess those disciplines at the respective level beforehand as pre-requisites to learn them. However, there are also single discipline devotions and those allow discipline progression to branch slightly into a different direction and usually have that discipline at the given level as a prerequisite.

                              Well - you could do that, but it does slightly stifle the rule of cool to limit disciplines by not allowing innovative uses. I would say in general yes, for extremely outlandish uses if you want to use it regularly in a certain way (like the self imposition of a condition) a devotion is the way to go. However, for a sudden flash of genius - like a player trying the above in a moment of desperation, or using celerity to check the pistol in a game of Russian roulette with no-one noticing, or obfuscate to hide the taste of your blood in a rival's wine - I would encourage them to be creative, charging them at most a willpower for the improvisation.

                              If you want a regular new power, perhaps a devotion is in order. If you have a cool idea that enhances play directly and doesn't break theme, I try to be a bit more loosey goosey.

                                  I see what you are saying, but another way to look at "innovative uses" is that if they are all Devotions then the innovation effort put in by the vampire that perfects the technique is a type of Capital which then has value due to exclusivity and marketability. Post-Scarcity of Innovation (ie loosey-goosey use of disciplines) spoils the rule of cool for having a high-intrigue investment/reward economy of trade secrets being bartered. People, especially players, like having nifty things no one else have and niche but not overly powerful Devotions make a lot of room for that.

                                  High functioning, reactive, and complex economies and ecosystems in game enrich the experience and immersion. Not to say you are wrong with the bounty you freely give away, just showing the other side of the coin where scarcity of innovation also has value.
