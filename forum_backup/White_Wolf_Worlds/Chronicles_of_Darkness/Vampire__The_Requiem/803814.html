    Belial's Brood and the Strix

    01-08-2016, 12:54 PM
    Hi everybody and happy new year,

    To make a long story short, I started my Chronicle as a VtR one, when Blood and Smoke hit the shelves I was thrilled and decided to go from Requiem to Blood and Smoke but, instead of putting the Strix into the Background, stating that they've always being there, I turned Blood and Smoke into a Chronicle during which, eventually, the Strix will raise again after being gone for centuries and forgotten by most kindred. So here I am, reworking my whole Chronicle, and using a Belial's Brood covey as a nemesis, they are plotting to summon a demon (possibly Belial himself) through a terrible ritual. Eventually they will fail because they were fooled by the Strix: the centuries old book they're using as a blueprint to conduct the ritual is a fake, a clever scheme weaved by the Strix itself a long time ago: the ritual won't summon a demon but open a portal to Dis and allow the Strix to plague our world again.

    I took notes on all this and read the Belial's Brood sourcebook again and it rose some questions:

    1) The Nameless are actually talking about Dis and that didn't catch my attention at first when I read the sourcebook but now I'm thinking "Great ! Right on the money!" but wait, according to the Nameless' myths it's not an underworld place but a very earthly place, a long time ago even before Babylon blossomed. Do you think Blood and Smoke is talking about is the same Dis the Belial's Brood sourcebook is referring to ?
    2) My first idea was: the Forsworn were fooled and will perish at the end of the ritual, being the ultimate sacrifice for the Strix to return. I loved the idea: you're playing with fire and now you're being consumed by it because they are forces that even a badass vampire cannot hope to harness. But, thinking about it, how would the Strix react to the Belial's Brood philosophy ? It seems to me, they are quite similar, it seems to me that Blood and Smoke turned the Belial's Brood into the Strix. What do you think ? And if the Strix and the Forsworn ain't the same concept what would be the difference and how they see each other ?

To be honest, I'm not sure about the Strix: they look great, they have this air of mystery but ... I'd like to scratch the surface ^^ The Belial's sourcebook did that for me.
                                      To me, the Strix are like The Joker. The Joker doesn't have an agenda or a vision, but that doesn't stop him from being a deep and complex villain. At his most basic, he just wants to revel in chaos for its own sake, but there are layers to his character that make him more than just an embodiment of destruction. In my favorite Joker comic, The Killing Joke, he shows that while he doesn't remember exactly how he got the way he is, the one thing he knows for certain is that it was just one bad day that pushed him over the edge. And so, he firmly believes that everyone is just one bad day from becoming just like him.

                                      The Strix, likewise, believe that every vampire is just one bad day from being just like they are. That's why they delight in dragging vampires' Humanity down; they've got something to prove. They want to show the vampires that deep down, they're all just life-sucking monsters inhabiting corpses, and no matter what rules and moralities they try to impose on themselves, they'll never escape the fact that they're just one bad day from ending up just like the Strix.

                                      In my view of the Strix, they would find Belial's Brood entertaining. They embrace their Beasts, they cause lots of chaos and destruction, they're just fun guys to be around. But ultimately, they wouldn't like the Brood any more than they like any other vampires. At the end of the day, their ideals about "dark enlightenment" are still just another way to pretend at some moral high ground that makes them more than just Beasts puppeteering human corpses. Their false morality may be more entertaining than the other vampires, but they'll still need to be shown what they really are sooner or later.
