            It's not a smudge though, at least not necessarily. The Beast just instinctively looks away from any camera it doesn't want to show it's face to.

            Now, what the book doesn't say--but what I personally ruled because it makes sense--is that a vampire can choose to appear on a picture or on footage normally, but only with the vampire's consent.

            This is because I imagine 'The Lost Visage' being a built-in security measure that gives the vampire an easier time upholding the masquerade. It's the beast inside you making sure you don't accidentally break the masquerade because you didn't see there is a security camera pointed at you. But this built-in security measure could quickly become a burden if you have absolute no control over it. This could be intentional and an interesting way of running the game, but I personally find it more interesting to give the vampire some control over his 'lost visage'. I want the vampire to be able to choose to appear normally on a photo or video, accepting the negative consequences this might have in the future. So that's how I run it.

            Another way you could run it of course is to give the beast full control over the lost visage. It's up to it to decide if it wants to be seen on tape or not. Perhaps that could be interesting as well.
