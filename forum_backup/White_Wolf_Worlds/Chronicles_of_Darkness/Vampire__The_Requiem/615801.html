          Personally, I took Theban and just slid a few rituals in it.           Are you looking for 1e or 2e rituals? Because, depending on which, I have different sets for each edition on this matter.

                  I wrote us a crazy-long yet still kinda-bare-bones (no pun intended) sprawling set of rules for Vampire: the Requiem Necromancy that wasn't quite a proper Discipline nor anything like blood sorcery as we know it, but somewhere between a school of magic and a set of Devotions. It incorporated both aspects of ghostly manipulation and the notion of waking cadavers, built into a series of 'Branches' (divided into affecting spirits or flesh) and 'Destinations' within the Branches (dealing with effects upon oneself or on another). I never wound up doing much much beyond the basic schematic of how it worked, and then decided that Kindred - the default vampires in the World of Darkness - weren't the ideal vehicle for using those things.

                  ...So no, I haven't done anything like what you're asking, but now you know that I've done something totally daft and far more complicated than it needed to be. I guess I just wasted your time. Sorry.
