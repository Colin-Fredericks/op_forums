                                                        Originally posted by malonkey1 View Post
                                                        That kind of begs the question. Do White Wolf, Onyx Path and their various games exist in ChroD?
                                                        I like classic World of Darkness' 'Black Dog' with their weird variants. I'd probably not have Vampire the Requiem, but would have vampire role-playing games. Really, I think it would be on a setting by setting basis. Requiem could be the result of a break in the Masquerade.

                                                        It's an interesting change in dynamic between vampires and werewolves. Even disregarding the old stereotypes of cities and wildernesses and cold beauty versus hot passion. Both are predators, creatures of the night, though werewolves aren't confined to it. Werewolves have packs, which aren't entirely free of backstabbing but probably good for teaching a coterie how to rely on, support, and trust one another. Then there's the Requiem. There's no handbook for being dead, vampires have to decide for themselves what to do with their unlives. But werewolves basically have a divine mandate, a primal urge to hunt that lives in every molecule of their being. And they're rewarded for what they do. A vampire might be screwed over by politics, but whether you're vilified or not, the lunes will mark you with that Renown. There's arguably even a difference in their deaths. For vampires it's a source of fear and dread, but for werewolves dying heroically can be a glorious thing.

                                                        I could see vampires digging it.
