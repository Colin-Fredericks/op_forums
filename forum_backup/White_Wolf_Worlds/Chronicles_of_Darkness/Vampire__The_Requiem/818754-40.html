                Size is a weird scale since it seems to be based on some sort of effective volume rather than actual volume or weight. A spear has Size 4, which is the same as people under ~150 cm. The spear being extremely long compensates for lack of volume and weight. The scale is also exponential, so Size 6 covers a larger span than Size 5. For gameplay purposes, though, I'd say humans can't become larger than Size 6, even though some people with gigantism and some people on Rathamus' list probably reaches Size 7.

                                                        11-23-2016, 03:23 AM
                                                        Page 91: "In dramatic instances, severed limbs regenerate or even simply reattach (if she’s able to heal the entire injury in a turn)."
                                                        Since Aggravated damage can't be healed in a turn it stands to reason that Lethal damage is enough to sever limbs. It makes sense since Aggravated damage is only dealt by supernatural sources or extreme damage to the body's structural integrity (i.e. by upgrading damage taken after having taken full Lethal). While the removal of a limb IS a huge thing, it doesn't really affect the body's structural integrity.
                                                        In fact, I'd rule that careful amputation of limbs would only deal Lethal to mortals as well. It's not the loss of biomass that's harmful, it's the wounds inflicted that leads to loss of limbs. Otherwise everyone who has lost limbs would have permanently lost Health levels as well.
