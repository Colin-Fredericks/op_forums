                                          New Orleans by Night
                                          For a detailed history of New Orleans as it appears in the World of Darkness please refer to the City of the Damned book (still available here at DriveThruRPG.com). For my chronicle I’ve assumed that City of the Damned is set in the current year (2018), so I didn’t change the background too much (assume that unless otherwise stated, all the information in city of the Damned is being used unmodified). The one major event missing from City of the Damned is hurricane Katrina (because the book was written before the hurricane happened), so I came up with some ideas to fill in the gap.

                                          Hurricane Katrina
                                          In 2005 hurricane Katrina devastated New Orleans. Fortunately, nearly all the elder Kindred of the city had havens secure enough to protect them from the destruction the hurricane wrought, but many neonates weren’t as lucky. In the couple years following the hurricane, the laws of the city completely broke down. Kindred fighting each other over territory and feeding grounds became a daily occurrence. A few Kindred even began to embrace illegally in order to form their own personal armies. Many Kindred, mostly newer and inexperienced ones, were destroyed, and more than a few were even diablerized. Finally, Prince Vidal was forced to declare martial law. He sent his hounds, led by Caitlin Meadows, into the city with orders to hunt down and destroy any Kindred criminals they could find. In time order was restored and Vidal took back control of his city.

                                          Although Vidal made an attempt to take control of the Treme district and the French Quarter, his resources were spread way too thin. As things began to settle down both the Baron and Savoy were able to take back control of their respective territories, and even expand them slightly.

                                          The stresses of putting his city back together, combined with the fact that he has been awake and active for far too long already, have taken a toll on the Prince’s mind. Despite increasing pressure to put himself into Torpor, Vidal vehemently believes that the city is still in much too vulnerable a state to hand over to a new Prince. If he were to fall into Torpor now the city would inevitably slip back into anarchy and chaos.

                                          Rumours suggest that Katrina caused one or more sleeping elders to awaken from torpor. Since no such elders have made their presence known, most kindred of the city believe such stories are nothing more than stories concocted to scare neonates. In fact the rumours are true; the Choctaw elder known as Sam is one such elder who was awoken by Katrina. It’s entirely possible that other elders were awakened as well, but it’s unlikely any are anywhere as old as Sam.

                                          The Strix
                                          For several months prior to Katrina, Strix appeared in great numbers throughout New Orleans. Strangely, they continue to be spotted every so often even now. It’s not certain whether the Stix simply enjoy being in New Orleans and have decided to stay, or if they remain because they sense an even greater disaster approaching.

                                          House Rules
                                          The materials on this page assume the following house rules are being used:
                                          – Vampires don’t have to feed from other vampires until BP 7.
                                          – Vice and Virtue are used instead of Mask and Dirge.

                                              Map of New Orleans

                                              Apparently I'm not authorized to upload attachments, so for a copy of the map please click here.

                                              Legend

                                              1. Perdido House: A tall black office building. Vidal owns the top half of this building, if not more. He used to only use it for the occasional meeting, but now he pretty much lives here. The security of the building has increased dramatically, especially in the last few years. Now the building is protected by the most advanced security system money can buy, and a veritable army of private security soldiers.

                                              2. City Hall: Courthouses are attached to the eastern end of the building.
                                              Exterior

                                              3. Saenger Theatre: First opened in 1927, the Saengar Theatre suffered severe damage during hurricane Katrina and was forced to close down. In 2011 work began to restore and update the theatre, which opened again two years later in 2013. Saengar Theatre now hosts all manner of live shows, including classics such as Phantom of the Opera and Les Miserables. The Saenger Theatre has always been one of Pearl Chastain’s favorite theatres, so much so that she had a huge hand in getting it restored and reopened.
                                              Exterior
                                              Interior
                                              Website

                                              4. Public Library: Started in 1843 as a small house in the French Quarter and grew into a city-wider service with multiple locations. The location marked is the main library, which opened in 1958.
                                              Exterior
                                              Interior
                                              Website

                                              5. Superdome: This massive stadium not only hosts sporting events, but also events such as live concerts, festivals, and even weddings.
                                              Exterior
                                              Interior
                                              Website

                                              6. St. Louis Cemetery #1: The oldest cemetery in New Orleans, dating all the way back to 1789. This cemetery contains the final resting places of many VIPs from the earliest years of the city’s existence. One such VIP who’s interred here is Marie Laveau, who was often referred to as the Voodoo Queen of New Orleans. Some, however, believe that Marie was able to use her magic to make herself immortal, which means her death was faked and her tomb is actually empty.
                                              Image
                                              Website

                                              7. St. Augustine’s Church: Founded in 1841 by free people of colour, St. Augustine’s Church is the oldest African-American church in the United States. Although primarily Catholic, the church has become well known for adopting the uniquely New Orleans traditions of jazz mass and jazz funerals. Catholic purists, such as Father John and Prince Vidal, aren’t entirely happy with these practices, but so far have mostly left the church alone. At. Augustine’s Church is also the site of the Tomb of the Unknown Slave, a shrine created to honour all the unknown slaves who died in New Orleans.
                                              Exterior
                                              Interior
                                              Tomb of the Unknown Slave
                                              Website

                                              8. City Park: This is the largest park in New Orleans. Like all parks in New Orleans, the City Park is the domain of the Gangrel. The City Park is a prime feeding ground due to it’s popularity among humans, it’s regular hosting of large events, and it’s numerous dark and seclude locations. Even non-Gangrel Kindred have attempted to claim territory inside the park, although the local Gangrel don’t tolerate such intrusions for long.
                                              Image
                                              Website

                                              9. Museum of Art: This museum tends to focus on exhibits that deal with one form of art or another, such as sculpture, painting, and even clothing. Sometimes the museum does host other kinds of exhibits, such as the massively successful Treasure of Tutankhamen exhibit that was shown in 1977.
                                              Exterior
                                              Interior
                                              Floorplan
                                              Website

                                              10. Fairgrounds Race Track: In addition to the regularly held horse races, the Fairgrounds contains a number of gambling machines. Every year, typically in early spring, the reclusive Gangrel elder Nathaniel Blanch takes over the Fairgrounds to put on his Jazz and Heritage Festival.
                                              Image
                                              Fairgrounds Website
                                              Jazz and Heritage Festival Website

                                              11. Tulane/Loyola University: The twin campuses of Tulane and Loyola sit right next to each other, Tulane in the north and Loyola in the south. Tulane University actually began as the University of Louisiana, which opened in 1847. It was renamed Tulane University in 1884. Regardless of it’s name, Tulane University is one of the oldest and most respected educational and research facilities in the country. Loyola is a Jesuit University that first opened it’s doors in 1904. Although not as old as Tulane University, Loyola quickly became a highly respected institute.
                                              Tulane Campus
                                              Loyola Campus
                                              Tulane University Website
                                              Loyola University Website

                                              12. Audubon Zoo: This small zoo sits just to the south of the Audubon Park Golf Course. It’s famous for it’s swamp exhibit, showcasing numerous animals native to southern Louisiana. This is a favourite hunting ground for young neonates who are still able to feed on animal blood and refuse to feed on humans. Although this entire area is technically a park, it is not the domain of of the Gangrel.

                                                  Alexander
                                                  Hound
                                                  (Based on the character from the Requiem demo)
                                                  Alexander is a large, muscular man with pale skin and a bald head he shaves every evening when he wakes up. He typically wears torn jeans and a leather jacket over a wife-beater shirt.
                                                  Alexander was abandoned by his Sire and had to learn about his new nature all on his own. He’s not proud of what he was like in those early years. He took jobs as bouncers and leg breakers, reveling in his violent nature. More than a few times he intentionally pushed himself to Frenzy in order to inflict maximum damage. After getting over hs head by getting into a fight with a man who turned out to be a werewolf, Alexander’s beaten and broken body was found by Father John Marrow. After being nursed back from the brink of Torpor, Father John took Alexander under his wing and taught him the ways of the Lancea Sanctum. Alexander took to the teaching of Longinus almost instantly.
                                                  Alexander is loyal, stubborn, and obedient, all the traits a Prince would want in a Hound. He doesn’t like it when he loses control, so constantly struggles to maintain his temper and control his Beast. When the former Hound, Caitlin Meadows, went AWOL, Alexander jumped at the chance to replace her. He believes that by working for Vidal and Moldanato in the name of the Lancea Sanctum he can atone for past sins and learn how to control the “demon” inside of him.

                                                  Henri
                                                  Member of the Morai and keeper of the Great Library located under Loyola University.
                                                  Henri is a short middle-aged man with salt and pepper hair. He comes off as an absent-minded professor, but his mind is actually exceptionally sharp and many respect his incredible knowledge of a vast number of subjects. Henri uses Obfuscate pretty much all the time, and often forgets he has it active. He has a habit of remaining quiet and unnoticed, then suddenly joining in on a conversation and startling everyone in the room with his sudden appearance.
                                                  In addition to being a respected scholar and professor, Henri is a member of the secretive group known as the Morai. he is the only Morai in New Orleans. in addition to working at the Loyola University as head of the History department, Henri is keeper of a massive library of Morai knowledge and artifacts.                                                   King of the Docks
                                                  No one knows the real name of the man known as King of the Docks. It’s possible even the King himself doesn’t remember. Similarly, no one is entirely certain what he really looks like. The only things people know for certain about him is that he is a man and that he’s fairly short. Otherwise his features are obscured by multiple layers of grimy clothing, which he wears even in the hottest parts of summer, and a thick coating of greasy dirt. He also always wears a huge pair of reflective sunglasses, and a woolen aviator hat with the ear flaps hanging down. To top off his ensemble, the King wears a bent tin drown on his head, on top of the aviator hat.
                                                  The King’s kingdom is made up of a warren of alleys and abandoned warehouses down at the West Riverside docks. His loyal subjects are the vermin and homeless people who also call the docks their home. The King only barely survived hurricane Katrina and has been slowly rebuilding his kingdom ever since. Unfortunately the King is completely unaware that many of his new subjects are in fact shartha. Because of this the King is constantly comes into conflict with werewolves from the Rat Catchers pack.
                                                  Although technically the King is not a recognized member of any covenant, he seems to know a fair amount about the inner workings of the Invictus. Some believe that the King was once a powerful and influential elder within the Invictus until he lost his mind and became the King. Some say his mind was shattered by a rough stint in torpor, others say it was brain damaged caused in such a way that it last even through vampiric healing, but no one really knows what truly happened.

                                                      Other NPCs

                                                      Werewolves
                                                      New Orleans has never had much of a problem with spirits. Mardi Gras can create some troublesome spirits, but there’s never been anything the handful of resident werewolves couldn’t handle. But then the city was hit by hurricane Katrina. The death, loss, and suffering caused by Katrina not only caused a dramatic rise in the number of harmful spirits inhabiting the city, but it also attracted large numbers of shartha, especially beshilu.
                                                      In the years since Katrina, werewolves have flocked to New Orleans. Apart from a handful of individuals and tiny packs, two proper-sized packs have formed in the city: the Sentinels and the Rat Catchers. Even though the werewolf population has exploded in New Orleans, their number still remain relatively small. So far they’ve only just been able to hold their own again the spirits overrunning New Orleans.
                                                      The packs below are comprised of over a dozen members each, including wolf blooded and allied humans. The NPCs described here are just the main werewolf members that the characters are most likely to interact with.

                                                      The Sentinels
                                                      This pack formed just after hurricane Katrina hit New Orleans. It’s mostly comprised of werewolves local to the New Orleans area, but there are several new arrivals. Although they’ve been hunting in and around the city for many decades now, they only recently came together to form a proper pack. It was believed (and rightly so) that working as a proper pack would help the constituent members deal with the massive surge in harmful spirits that appeared in the aftermath of hurricane Katrina.
                                                      The Sentinels have been fighting to restore order in the spirit world in and around New Orleans ever since. Not only do they hunt dangerous spirits, but members of the Sentinels even work with the humans and Kindred of New Orleans to help repair the city and it’s population. Their plan is to not only eliminate all the current dangerous spirits, but also prevent the creation of even more.

                                                      Dimitri
                                                      Pack leader / shaman
                                                      Dimitri is a large, powerfully built man with long dark hair and a bushy beard. He typically wears a loose t-shirt and torn jeans, but spends as much time in wolf form as in human form. In wolf form Dimitri is quite large and is covered in shaggy grey/black fur. Dimitri’s tanned skin is covered in tattoos, some purely decorative, others with specific mystical purposes.
                                                      Russian born, Dimitri and his parents moved to New Orleans when he was only a few years old. Soon after the move, both of his parents died in a car crash. Dimitri didn’t learn he was a werewolf until he went through his first change, during which he attacked his foster family. After the attack, Dimitri fled into the swamps. He wouldn’t find out until much later that his family survived his attack, although in critical condition. After living rough in the swamps for a few months Dimitri found Chickenbones, who took him in and taught him about werewolves and spirits.
                                                      Dimitri is a strong leader who favours knowledge and planning over reckless action, although some say he may be a bit too slow and methodical sometimes. Dimitri likes to consider every angle before committing to a plan. The members of the Sentinels have equal say in a lot of what the pack does, and Dimitri encourages healthy debate among pack members and himself, however he has no trouble putting his foot down and asserting his dominance over the pack when the need arises.
                                                      When hurricane Katrina hit New Orleans, Dimitri was one of the first to leap in and start helping. He pulled together a group of independent and newcomer werewolves into New Orleans’ first proper pack.
                                                      Auspice: Ithaeur
                                                      Tribe: Bone Shadows
                                                      Primary Location: Bywater

                                                      Nicole
                                                      Second in Command
                                                      Nicole is a tall african-american woman with an athletic build and short black hair tied back in cornrows. All of her clothing looks like it was bought from surplus military stores, and she rarely goes anywhere without her custom twin .44 desert eagles. Most of the time Nicole is stern, serious, and humourless, but when hanging out with friends and family she does soften a little and shows that she does indeed know how to have a good time. Nicole is fiercely loyal to those she considers to be close friends and family. Nicole rarely transforms out of human form, instead relying on her pistols and training to see her through a hunt. When she does transform, her wolf form is covered in jet black fur.
                                                      Nicole is an army brat whose entire family has served the military in one way or another. Nicole herself went through military training, both official and taught to her by the rest of her family. However, hurricane Katrina hit before Nicole could properly enlist. Her skills and natural gift for tactical thinking made Nicole an invaluable asset to the hurricane relief efforts. Most of her time was spent fighting off looters and other criminals, as well as the occasional aggressive spirit. Her actions quickly attracted the attention of Dimitri, who asked her not only to join his newly forming pack, but also become his second in command. Nicole was reluctant at first, but soon came to see how hunting with a pack in the post Katrina environment would be preferable, and safer, than doing so alone.
                                                      It didn’t take long for the relationship between Nicole and Dimitri to grow into something more romantic in nature. In the years following Katrina the two didn’t get much time to explore their relationship. Now that things are settling down, however, the two have been spending more time together and have even begun to think about starting a family.
                                                      Auspice: Rahu
                                                      Tribe: Storm Lord
                                                      Primary Location: Bywater

                                                      Martin
                                                      Diplomat
                                                      This lanky man with sandy-blonde hair looks like he’d be more at home on a beach instead of in a boardroom. Even while wearing a suit Martin gives the feeling that he’s dressing casually. Martin rarely transforms out of human form, and gets very uncomfortable when he does. As a wolf Martin is small and lean with golden fur.
                                                      Despite his casual appearance and laid back attitude Martin is a shrewd and intelligent businessman and diplomat. His job is to act as liaison between the pack and the people of New Orleans, human and Kindred alike. Although Dimitri works closely with Martin to come up with plans for the repair and improvement of New Orleans, Martin is given total freedom in how he puts said plans into action. Such plans are often done through Martin’s Ecological and Social protection and improvement company to help lend them legitimacy. It’s usually not a good idea to tell a bunch of human politicians that a plan for social reform came from a pack of werewolves.Dealing with humans, and especially Kindred, can be very difficult at times, especially when they have to be kept in the dark about so many details, like the existence of werewolves and spirits, but Martin has proved himself to be more than up to the task.
                                                      Since he spends nearly all his time dealing with humans and Kindred, Martin doesn’t spend a lot of time with other members of his pack. The only other member he spends a significant amount of time with is Dimitri, and rarely is it in a social capacity. Martin greatly respects Dimitri. They do argue every now and then, usually dealing with what Dimtri wants to happen and what Martin can actually make happen, but for the most part they meet eye-to-eye on their visions of the future of New Orleans.
                                                      Auspice: Elodoth
                                                      Tribe: Iron Masters
                                                      Primary Location: Uptown

                                                      Rebecca
                                                      Technology expert
                                                      Rebecca is a small woman with pale skin and mousy hair. She wears plain and simple clothing that doesn’t attract attention, but she does wear a tremendous amount of jewelry covered in cheap charms and dangly bits. Rebecca rarely transforms out of human form, and intentionally resists transforming as much as she can. When she does transform her wolf form is very small and covered in mousy fur. When she does find herself in wolf form Rebecca will shift back to human form as soon as she is able.
                                                      Rebecca has lived in New Orleans all her life. Originally, she was a Ghost Wolf who wanted nothing to do with her werewolf obligations. She was always more interested in tinkering with electronics and machines than she was in dealing with the spirit world. When Katrina hit, Rebecca witnessed a gang of Kindred and ghouls rampage through her neighborhood. She used her technical know-how to track down the gang’s hideout and rig it with enough explosives to level the entire building. She then took out the survivors all on her own. Rebecca had never really liked the Kindred, and their behaviour during and after Katrina made her feeling towards them much worse. Dimirti learned of Rebecca’s actions pretty quickly and sought her out to recruit her into his newly forming pack. She did so, reluctantly, and Dimitri introduced her to the Iron Masters Tribe, which she has taken with with gusto.
                                                      Rebecca is painfully shy and spends nearly all of her time tinkering with her gadgets. She never goes on hunts alone and when she goes with the rest of the pack she stays in the car and monitors the situation on her laptop and surveillance gear. The only things that can bring Rebecca out of her shell are kindred. Even being in the presence of a vampire is enough to bring Rebecca to the brink of losing control and flying into a rage.
                                                      Auspice: Irraka
                                                      Tribe: Iron Masters
                                                      Primary Location: Mid-City

                                                      The Rat Catchers
                                                      New Orleans has always had a problem with rats and other vermin, which means it’s also always had a problem with beshilu. Before hurricane Katrina hit, a couple Hunters in Darkness were all that were needed to deal with any beshilu that happened to be found in the city. Werewolves flocked to the city from all over America to help out. One such werewolf, Jerome, formed a pack dedicated to a sole purpose: the eradication of all the beshilu living in New Orleans. This pack, called the Rat Catchers, is made up almost entirely of hunters in Darkness. Most members of the pack are also werewolves that moved to the city after hurricane Katrina, with only a few being locals. Some think the Rat Catchers are a little too specialized on a single enemy. They have been known to focus so much on a beshilu hunt that they ignore greater and more immediate threats to the city. Jerome has justified this attitude by claiming that no threat is greater than the beshilu, so why should he waste time on lesser threats that other werewolves could easily take care of.

                                                      Jerome
                                                      Pack leader
                                                      Jerome is a large man with tanned skin and a mop of shaggy blonde hair. As a wolf he is also very large with shaggy light-tan fur. He spends most of his time in human form, but is quick to shift up to Gauru form when fighting beshilu. Not much is known about Jerome from before he came to New Orleans. He’s fond of telling wild stories of travelling the world hunting down the most powerful beshilu he could find, but no one believes his tall tales. All that is known is that Jerome really hates beshilu, and that he’s very good at hunting them down.
                                                      Jerome arrived in New Orleans a few years after hurricane Katrina. He came to New Orleans with a mission: to cleanse the city of it’s beshilu infestation, and make sure everyone sees him as a hero for doing so. He doesn’t much care about restoring New Orleans or trying to make it a better place, all he cares about is “saving the city” from what he considers to be “the greatest threat the world has ever seen”. Fortunately, despite his bravado and glory-seeking, Jerome actually knows what he’s doing. Since the formation of his pack, the Rat Catchers, Jerome has led several successful hunts against beshilu nests. His tactics may be reckless and impulsive, but he gets the job done and he even goes out of his way to make sure all his pack makes come back from a hunt alive. Many werewolves see Jerome and the Rat Catchers as nothing more than children playing at being heroes, and are counting the days until Jerome charges into a situation that gets his entire pack killed.
                                                      Auspice: Cahalith
                                                      Tribe: Hunters in Darkness
                                                      Location: West Riverside

                                                      Dorian
                                                      Second in command
                                                      Dorian is a large, intimidating man with short black hair and piercing black eyes. His face and body are covered in scars which he wears as badges of honour. He wears casual but highly functional clothing, the kind that tears away easily during a transformation, and doesn’t get in the way during a fight. In wolf form Dorian retains the scars, making his course black fur patchy and unkempt.
                                                      Dorian is the patient and thoughtful counterpoint to Jerome’s recklessness. Dorian does his best to reign in Jerome, but it is far from an easy job. The two argue frequently about how the pack and their hunts should be handled, but so far the two haven’t come to blows. Fortunately Jerome has come to greatly respect Dorian as a warrior and a tactician, and sees him as an extremely valuable asset in the war against the beshilu. Dorian has quickly learned how far he can push Jerome and how much he can get away with. As valuable as he is, he knows Jerome wont always tolerate challenges to his leadership, as well intentioned as they may be.
                                                      Before coming to new Orleans Dorian was a soldier and a mercenary. He came to New Orleans just after hurricane Katrina to help with the relief effort. When Jerome arrived and formed the Rat Pack, Dorian decided to stay and help out. He may not particularly like Jerome’s style of leadership, but he can’t argue with the results the man gets.
                                                      Auspice: Elodoth
                                                      Tribe: Hunters in Darkness
                                                      Location: West Riverside

                                                      Alex
                                                      Scout and shartha hunter
                                                      Alex is average in height and build, with light brown hair and eyes. As a wolf he’s small and sleek with chocolate-brown fur. Alex is the kind of person that blends into a crowd and doesn’t attract attention, which suits him perfectly. He’s not a very social person, in fact he purposefully avoids interacting with others. When he is forced to talk to someone he doesn’t say much. Instead, he stares and listens, giving the impression that he’s sizing up the other people in the conversation, looking for weaknesses he can later exploit.
                                                      It was a long time before Alex joined the Rat Catchers. It took a few years and the combined efforts of both Jerome and Dorian. Even now, Alex prefers to live and hunt on his own. He understands the importance of being in a pack, it’s just proving difficult for him break the habit of thinking he can deal with every situation all on his own. Alex acts as a scout and assassin, eliminating specific targets all on his own, or preparing the way for the rest of the pack.
                                                      Auspice: Irraka
                                                      Tribe: Hunters in Darkness
                                                      Location: French Quarter

                                                      Independent

                                                      Medard
                                                      Crazy hermit and prophet
                                                      Medard is an older african-american man with long graying hair. Despite his age, he is still powerfully built, his muscled body covered in a network of battle scars. His clothing is dirty and ragged, looking like it’s been out in the swamp for far too long. A variety of charms, small bones, and beads have been tied into Medard’s hair. Medard keeps the network of scars in wolf form, and still has the decorations tied into his grey fur. Medard spends just as much time in wolf form as he does in human form.
                                                      Medard is well known among the werewolf community as the crazy old hermit who lives in a shack in the swamp, likes to wrestle ‘gators, and gets visions of the future. He’s lived in the swamps outside the New Orleans city limits all his life. He has also possessed the ability to read signs and portents since he was very young. These readings give Medard insight into past, present, and future events. Decades of cryptic visions have shattered Medard’s mind. Most of the time he’s perfectly fine, but without warning he can start suffering from severe hallucinations and delusions. A couple times a year Medard will transform into a wolf and vanish into the swamps for several days, sometimes even a few weeks. He takes this time to lose himself in his wolf form for a time. He will use this time to run, hunt, and commune with a variety of swamp spirits.
                                                      Medard despises the modern world and prefers to live a simple life in the swamp. He refuses to have anything to do with modern technology, believing it’s presence messes with his natural energies. He will refuse to deal with anyone who obviously relies too heavily on technology (like someone who has to check their phone every five minutes). The only time he’s been into the city proper was about a year before Katrina. He left the comfort of his swamp to warn anyone he could about his visions of approaching disaster. unfortunately everyone, even his fellow werewolves, wrote off his apocalyptic prophecies as nothing more than the ranting of a madman. His fellow werewolves take his visions much more seriously now.
                                                      Auspice: Cahalith
                                                      Tribe: Bone Shadows
                                                      Location: The swamps outside New Orleans

                                                      Geists
                                                      For now I’m only providing basic information on geists located in New Orleans, and currently have only written up Rosa Bale to show how she differs from her incarnation in City of the Damned. See that book for a more detailed description on Rosa (just ignore the parts about her being a vampire). Once Geist 2E is released I’ll flesh out Rosa a bit more and add a couple other Geists I have notes for.

                                                      Rosa Bale
                                                      Location: Treme District
                                                      Rosa and her sister Isabel were attacked by the Gangrel pedophile Roger Halliburton. Isabel was killed in the attack, but Rosa made it to the hospital before dying. It was in that moment of her death that she merged with a spirit who calls itself Baron Samedi. Since her brush with death, Rosa has dedicated herself to seeking out justice for the dead, especially those killed by vampires.
                                                      Not long after being connected to Baron Samedi, Rosa discovered that Isabel’s ghost refuses to travel to the Underworld. Isabel haunts her sister, pushing Rosa towards increasingly more violent acts of vengeance, especially against vampires. Isabel sees all vampires as monsters who deserve to be destroyed for all the innocent lives they’ve destroyed.
                                                      Even before becoming a geist, Rosa was a powerful mambo (it’s what attracted the geist in the first place). She sees Baron Cimitier as a threat to New Orleans’ voudon community, and hates how he portrays himself as the Loa that she believes is her Geist. Rosa is smart enough to realize that direct action against the powerful vampire would be suicide, but she takes every opportunity she can to take jabs at the Baron and his followers.

                                                      Mummies
                                                      For now I’m only providing basic information for Meskhenet. Once Mummy 2E is released I’ll flesh her out a bit more. Currently, Meskhanet is the only Mummy NPC located in New Orleans.

                                                      Meskhanet / Sarah
                                                      Location:Mid-City
                                                      Meskhanet, or Sarah as she goes by in the modern world, has dark bronzed skin and jet black hair. She prefers to wear loose fitting dresses and blouses in bright colours. She also likes to wear lots of jewellery. Sarah has a fondness for gummy bears and almost always has a small bag of them in her pocket or stashed away in her purse.
                                                      In 1977 a King Tut exhibit was shown in the Museum in Art. It was wildly popular and ran for more then a year before it was packed up and moved on to the next city. During this time, one of the mummies that was part of the exhibit woke up. Meskhanet emerged into the modern world after thousands of years of sleep. it took her some time to adjust, but soon enough fear gave way to amazement. Meskhanet learned quickly and integrated into modern New Orleans seamlessly. She has taken a number of personas and jobs over the years. Currently she is known as Sarah and works at the Museum of Art as a junior curator.
                                                      Sarah knows she awoke for a reason, but she hasn’t figured out what that reason is. When hurricane Katrina hit, she thought she had woken to help the city through it’s time of crisis, but it’s been over a decade since the hurricane and she’s still awake. something else is coming, and Sarah hopes she’s up to the task of saving the city from whatever disaster lies in it’s future.
