                                                Tone can vary a lot, even within one chronicle.

                                                "The only moral thing for a vampire to do is commit suicide" is something that I hear repeated a lot, and I don't think it's true. A lot of us benefit in some way from bad things in the lives of others. Not usually from actual blood loss, but in very real ways. Often, the benefits aren't even essential the way eating is. I don't think that means that we should kill ourselves, or that anyone involved should feel that their lives are futile. I don't even think that suicide or futility are the most natural exaggerations to put into the game world.

                                                For my home games, I take both a pretty horrific and a pretty optimistic view of the Requiem. There are terrible things in the world. Those things will happen to you, and you may do those things, but you are not inherently one of them. The World of Darkness has room for both hope and tragedy, and which you find depends both on your choices and your perspective.
