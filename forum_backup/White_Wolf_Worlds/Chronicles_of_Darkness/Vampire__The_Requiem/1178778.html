    Bloodlines Through the Eras

    01-24-2018, 10:33 AM
    With Dark Eras 2 coming eventually, Vampire now has quite a few Eras for us to play with (not enough for me, obviously ;D). I’m already running two historical games, a Play-by-post Elizabethan game and my former Rome game that now is in Constantinople going from the Early Middle Ages to the High Middle Ages.

    So I’ve decided to organize all the Bloodlines from the books by the time and location of it’s “birth”. Granted, some are harder to pinpoint, and there’s nothing to say that their origin is just myth. I’ve let players use “future” bloodlines like their it’s actual founder. But I think it’s an useful resource, and at least serves as inspiration for characters and groups for the storyteller to use.

    First I’m going to list, and then do an Era by Era of which bloodlines I think can fit just fine.

    THE ANCIENT ONES

    (These are the ones that are harder to pinpoint)

    - Noctuku (Bloodlines: The Chosen) - Very ancient, some believe neolithic.
    - En (Ancient Bloodlines) - 12 thousand years. Sure.
    - Vedma (Ordo Dracul) - Claim to be older then the clans. Mostly in slavic lands.
    - Children of the Morrigan (Circle of the Crone) - 3000 years. Dwindled around the middle ages.
    - Kuufukuji (Bloodlines: The Legendary) - Ancient Asia.
    - Moroi (Ordo Dracul) - Seem very old. Romanian.
    - Adroanzi (Ancient Bloodlines) - Long before western civilization had knowledge of the African Continent.
    - Galloi (Bloodlines: The Legendary) - Claim to be 8000 years old. But believed to be during the Renaissance. Or even roman times.

    - Usiri (Ancient Bloodlines) - Ancient Egypt.
    - Bak-Ra (Ancient Bloodlines) - Ancient Egypt.
    - Khaibit (Bloodlines the Hidden) - Egypt at the time of Akhenaten.

    - Amara Havana (Ancient Bloodlines) - Ancient India.
    - Mayarap (Ancient Bloodlines) - Ancient Thailand.

    - Annunaku (Invictus) - Mesopotamia, probably. Spread during the Roman Empire.
    - Qedeshah (Bloodlines the Hidden) - Ancient Babylonia.
    - Iltani (Ancient Bloodlines)- Ancient Mesopotamia.

    - Rakshasa (Bloodlines the Hidden) - At least 500 B.C. India.

    ANCIENT GREECE

    - Gorgons (Circle of the Crone) - Before the Camarilla, in greek lands. Waned during the times of Rome. Reappeared again in Constantinople after the fall of the Camarilla, but by the time of the Renaissance, disappeared again. Only to come back by the 19th century.
    - Agonistes (Bloodlines: The Chosen) - Ancient Greece.
    - Mnemosyne (Mekhet) - No one remembers, but they were there in Rome.
    - Hound’s of Actdeon (Gangrel) - They believe they’re ancient greek.
    - Kerberos (Onyx Path Homepage) - Two thousand years ago, in the greek isles.

    ROMAN TIMES

    - Lygos (Nosferatu) - A child of Longinus, in Egypt.
    - Mortifiers of Flesh (Lancea Sanctum) - Origin lost, but at least as old as the Black Abbey (which is 335, supposedly).
    - Osites (Lancea Sanctum) - Origin lost. Supposedly one of the first Sanctified.
    - Alucinor (Bloodlines the Hidden) - Founded during the Roman Empire.
    - Morbus (Core Rules) - Since Ancient Roman Times.
    - Macellarius (Bloodlines: The Legendary) - Ancient Rome.
    - Larvae (Rome) - Ancient Rome. Not known if they still exist, but probably not.
    - Licini (Rome) - Ancient Rome.
    - Malocusians (Invictus) - Nothing about it’s origins. But name of Discipline is based on roman architecture.

    Special Snowflake:

    - Bron (Bloodlines: The Legendary) - Between 50 and 75 AD, or 500 B.C. in Britannia.

    EARLY MIDDLE AGES

    - Baddacelli (Bloodlines: The Chosen) - Italy, after the fall of the Camarilla.
    - Canda Bhanu (Ancient Bloodlines) - India, after the fall of Roman Empire.

    - Anvari (Bloodlines the Hidden) - 4th or 5th Century, in Ancient Baghdad.

    - Taifa (Bloodlines: The Chosen) - Muslim Spain, 720s.

    - Yagnatia (Bloodlines: The Chosen) - 10th century Eastern Europe.

    - Norvegi (Mekhet) - North of Europe, during the Middle Ages.
    - Mystikoi (Ancient Bloodlines) - Constantinople, 976.
    - Spina (Invictus) - Founder Artus Le Jumel also founded the Most Honorable Order of the Thorned Wreath in 9th century France. Dead in 1350.


    HIGH MIDDLE AGES

    - The Mara (Circle of the Crone) - Origins seems celtic, Saxon and germanic. But earlier record is in the 11th century.
    - Order of Sir Martin (Ancient Bloodlines) - 11th Century, in the Holy Land.
    - Kallisti (Invictus) - Origin lost. A 1000 years ago, somewhere along the northernmost edge of Europe or Eastern Europe.
    - Rotgrafen (Bloodlines: The Chosen) - 1000 years ago, Norway.
    - Kinnaree (Ancient Bloodlines) - A Thousand years ago, in Thailand.

    - Nelapsi (Bloodlines the Hidden)- 12th Century, Czechoslovakia.
    - Corajoso (Ancient Bloodlines)- 13th century Portugal.
    - Icarians (Lancea Sanctum) - Founder Icarius, Archbishop of Naples, died in 1388.

    - Children of Judas (Bloodlines: The Legendary) - Maybe Serbia, 13th or 14th century.
    - Gullikan (Bloodlines: The Legendary) - 14th century Constantinople.


    15th CENTURY

    - Asnâm (Circle of the Crone) - Founded by Atiqua Azad, and arab slaver in the late 1400’s.
    - Tismanu (Ordo Dracul) - Wallachia, older then dracula (but not much, and unlike the Moroi seem to be), but only spread after him.
    - Azerkatil (Ordo Dracul) - 15th century Ottoman Empire


    16th CENTURY

    - Gethsemani (Bloodlines the Hidden) - 1522 Florence.
    - Morotrophians (Bloodlines the Hidden) - 16th Century Italy.
    - Sangiovanni (Bloodlines: The Chosen) - Venetia, during the Renaissance.
    - Geheim (Ancient Bloodlines) - 1577, Vienna and Switizerland.


    17th CENTURY

    - Erzsebet (Daeva) - 17th Century Italy.
    - Architects of the Monolith (Bloodlines the Hidden)- 17th Century Paris.
    - Burakumin (Core Rules) - Feudal japan.
    - Sotoha (Invictus) - Founder was embraced at the beginning of 16th century japan. Left for America in the 17th century.
    - Septemi (Ancient Bloodlines) - Ostensible founder embraced during the Hundred Years War, in France. But bloodline might be older.


    18th CENTURY

    - Toreador (Core Rules) - Spain during King Philip V’s reign (1700-1724)
    - The Carnival (Bloodlines: The Legendary) - 18th Century Bohemia.
    - Mellisidae (Bloodlines: The Legendary)- 18th century Britain.
    - Duchagne (Bloodlines: The Chosen) - 1765 France
    - Tianpan Xiao (Bloodlines: The Chosen)- China, height of Qing Dynasty.
    - Apollinaire (Ancient Bloodlines) - Haiti, 1791.
    - Les Gens Libres (Ancient Bloodlines) - Haiti, 1791.

    19th CENTURY

    - Nahualli (Bloodlines the Hidden) - 19th Century Mexico.
    - Oberlochs (Bloodlines the Hidden) - 19th Century, Pennsylvania.
    - Bohagande (Bloodlines the Hidden) - 1850s, Mojave desert.
    - Barjot (Carthians) - 1860’s France.
    - Malkovians (Core Rules) - Between 1864 (Broadmoor Hospital’s first male patients) - 1889 (Malkov’s death)
    - Californai Xiao (Bloodlines: The Chosen) - 19th century California.
    - Sta-Au (Ancient Bloodlines) - 1800s America.
    - Shepperds (Ancient Bloodlines) - 19th century with the Montrose Party.
    - Mabry (Gangrel) - 1800’s Alaska.
    - The Cockscomb Society (Nosferatu) - 19th century England (tied to the Blackwood Abbey).

    - Dragolescu (Ordo Dracul) - Founder arrived in London in 1884, Bloodline seems to have started in 1901.
    - Libitinarius (Ordo Dracul) - Cult began in 1880’s College of London. Bloodline began around 1892-1911.

    20th CENTURY

    - Caporetti (Ancient Bloodlines) - Battle of Caporetto, 1917
    - Brothers of Ypres (Ancient Bloodlines)- Battle of Ypres, 1915.
    - Players (Bloodlines: The Legendary)- 60 to 80 years ago in Hollywood.
    - Bruja (Core Rules) - 1940’s American Southwest.
    - Carnon (Circle of the Crone) - San Francisco in the 1960’s.
    - Deucalion (Carthians) - Last 50 years, New York.
    - Adrestoi (Ventrue) - 1980’s Detroit.
    - Zelani (Carthians) - Hard to pin Lorna Zelani’s embrace. But she woke 10 years ago (from 2006) and began spreading her blood. No source of where, but I guess it’s your game’s city. It sure is mine.

    21st CENTURY

    - Lynx (Invictus) - Months ago as of the Invictus book release, which was in 2005.


    THE DARK ERAS AND THEIR BLOODLINES


    REQUIEM FOR ROME

    Obviously, everything from ROMAN TIMES and before is game. Just take notice of all those Lancea Sanctum bloodlines, that might or might not exist in your game still, depending the year. Also, the Bron would only be relevant in a Britannia setting, instead of the regular Rome one. I think the Greek and Babylonian bloodlines would work fine, as the Noctuku, Children of Morrigan and the Galloi, if you want them.


    THE SOULLESS AND THE DEAD (1346 - 1353)

    Now we’re the in High Middle Ages, up to the 14th century. The setting has two scenarios for Vampires: Constantinople and Florence.

    A lot of those Bloodlines fit with Constantinople, specially (in my opinion): The Mara, kallisti, Rotgrafen (It’s the time of their first incursions), Children of Judas, Taifa, Norvegi. The Gullikan, Mistikoi and Agonistes are very strong in Constantinople. The Gorgons too, but they are starting to fade here. The Bloodlines of Ancient Greece might be strong here as well.

    In Florence, we have the bloodlines that appear around it: Icarians, Baddacelli, as the Roman ones that might still be strong, like the Macellarius. Perhaps those from other parts of Europe, like the Carajoso, Spina and Norvegi.


    REQUIEM FOR REGINA (1590s)

    Not much to add from Florence, except those two centuries :P

    The bloodlines of the 15th century fit very little, but obviously it depends of the stories you’re telling and the Asnam could be fun. The Morothropians can work lovely with Bedlam and the Rotgrafen are active again.


    DARK ERAS 2

    KING ARTHUR’S BRITANNIA

    Now we’re going back to the Early Middle Ages. The Bron seems to fit this setting perfectly. I Imagine the Children of the Morrigan would have a lot of influence in this setting as well. And I can see the Mara being already active in this Era.

    Another thing. With the setting being a crossover with Changeling, what about the Bugman from Immortal Sinners? He seems to be from a lost bloodline, which is very akin to the old Kyasid from Masquerade. Hopefully we see something in this Era.


    FRENCH REVOLUTION


    The Duchagne could be fun during this Era. We see the rise of a lot of cool Bloodlines around Europe, like the Mellisidae and the Architects of the Monolith. The Septemi are active here and their supposed origin is in France. And maybe the Spina are strong around here.

    I’ll refrain from the Islamic Golden Age because I don’t know much about the setting, but I’m sure someone can give us their take.


    So thoughts? I know I made some mistakes, as well. How do you guys fit those bloodlines in your eras?
