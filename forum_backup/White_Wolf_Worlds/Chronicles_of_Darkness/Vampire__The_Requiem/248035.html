    New Orleans Theories: Donovan's Identity

    09-03-2014, 02:34 PM
    Out of courtsey I'm going to warn any New Orleans PCs unfamiliar with the book. This discussion contains several potential SPOILERS to the New Orleans setting. You have been warned...

    Hey everyone, I'm planning on running a pre-hurricane New Orleans chronicle and recently got my hands on the book. I've been reading through the cast thoroughly but there was one thing I couldn't find. In both Donovan's description and Gabriel Hursts' they mention that Sheriff Donovan has a very dirty secret. They discuss how Donovan used to go by the fake name Donovan Gage in both life and death. However he dropped the last name as part of his identity early on as a neonate, leaving only Savoy, Vidal, and Maldonato remembering he ever even went by this false last name. Apparently the sheriff is quite nervous about people digging into his past in case they learn who he really is.

    Now the book never gives us an answer on who our beloved sheriff once was in life (as far as I know, perhaps I missed something). The truth is a mystery that I assume is up to the ST. So what I'm wondering is what identity could be so damning to the Sheriff that he strives so hard to hide it? Afterall he went by this false name even when Savoy embraced him, so clearly it is a mortal identity he has a very vested interest in hiding. But I can't help but wonder what mortal identity could be so bad that it'd have the potential to ruin Donovan. Perhaps he was a ghoul once upon a time? But if that's true then Savoy would likely have known and it seems implied no one at all knows who Donovan actually is.

    Beyond that our only clues seem to be the fact Donovan is not a true believer of the Lancea Sanctum and that he appears to be in his late 20s but was embraced in 1865. Doesn't really narrow things down much at all. Any opinions?
