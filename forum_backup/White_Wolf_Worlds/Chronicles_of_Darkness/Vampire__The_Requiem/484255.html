    New GMC Discipline: Hemotech

    04-18-2015, 12:45 AM
    Foreword: getting a Dramatic Failure on any Hemotech roll not only results in the problems described under each power, but also gives the device the Bad Blood Condition for the rest of the chapter (game session). Of special note is that this also happens when you get an Exceptional Success, but the Condition is permanent in that case.

    Bad Blood [Persistent]: the Vitae used in the repair, improvement or creation of the item carried some kind of anomaly, which was triggered through mishandling or too much power going into the item. The ST has a negative dice pool of [Blood Potency x 3], which he may apply as penalties to the rolls involving the devices. This dice pool is renewed each chapter. He may impose a penalty of up -5 if the device is normal Hemotech or up to -10 if it is a Hemotech automaton. A Failure rolled when the anomaly strikes is turned into a Dramatic Failure, with no Beat. No Resolution for this Condition has ever been found, as far as Kindred know. That does not stop them from trying to find it.

    Insight (•)

    You become more adept with the ways of man made objects.

    Cost: None or 1 Vitae
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: None
    Duration: Always Active or Scene

    Spend the Vitae to add your dots in Hemotech to any dice pool for analyzing, repairing, building and improving mundane technology for one scene. You acquire the Encyclopedic Knowledge Merit for all the necessary Skills for tampering with mundane technology. This Merit is always active. By spending 1 vitae, you may ignore Durability of a technological object up to your dots in Hemotech. Lastly, you may examine a piece of technology for one minute (or one turn by spending 1 Vitae), which gives you the Informed Condition in relation to that object.

    Improvise (••)

    You may substitute necessary materials for the repair of technology with miscellaneous junk and Vitae.

    Requirement: you have used Insight on the object in question.
    Cost: 1 Vitae or more (optional)
    Dice Pool: Dexterity + Crafts + Hemotech
    Action: Extended (Target Number = lost Structure points)
    Duration: Permanent

    Dramatic Failure: the device you were attempting to repair will not be affected by Hemotech for the rest of the scene.

    Failure: the no progress is made.

    Success: progress is made. You may use 1 Vitae to add 2 bonus successes to each Improvise roll, as if you were making the machine a part of yourself spiritually. The repair is permanent

    Exceptional Success: no additional benefit.

    Tinkerer (•••)

    You may alter the properties of technology with Vitae.

    Requirement: you have used Insight on the object in question.
    Cost: 1 Vitae per roll
    Dice Pool: Intelligence + Crafts + Hemotech + Dots in a Haven, if you have one
    Action: Extended (Target Number = 15. Each roll takes one hour)
    Duration: Varies

    Dramatic Failure: the item loses dots of Structure equal to your dots in Hemotech.

    Failure: the item remains unaltered

    Success: you may choose one of the following alterations. The object is immune to Hemotech and other Disciplines or Devotions used by others, unless you allow it. The alteration lasts for a number of days equal to Blood Potency.
    1. The object’s Durability and Equipment/Damage/Armor bonus are increased by 1.
    2. The object can hold a number of Vitae points inside itself equal to your Willpower. You may place or take the Vitae as an instant action, but the amount of points you may transfer is the same as those you can spend per turn. The object may absorb vitae by entering contact with blood, whether through damage or simple submersion. Ghouls may use the stored Vitae in all the ways they normally can.
    3. The object now deals lethal damage against vampires.
    4. The object grants 9 Again on the mundane uses of a single Skill.
    Exceptional Success: choose two alterations.

    Ideaform (••••)

    You may bring machines from dark corners of the realms of pure Idea into a vessel of Vitae.

    Requirement: you have used Insight on the machine you wish to imitate.
    Cost: 3 to 9 Vitae + 1 optional willpower
    Dice Pool: Resolve + Investigation + Hemotech + your Dots in Haven, if you have one
    Action: Extended (Target number = 10. Each roll takes one minute of concentration, or one turn if you spend willpower)
    Duration: Varies

    Dramatic Failure: your attempt at reaching into the realms of Idea has turned against your mind. You gain the Madness Condition, which goes away at the end of the chapter.

    Failure: the Ideaform does not enter its vessel.

    Success: the Vitae is spilled forth from the character’s brow and quickly takes on the shape and function of a mundane machine. It appears as some kind of crystallized blood with the infinite Ideas dancing inside its structure. The required Vitae depends on the general size and complexity of the machine. It remains inside the vessel for a number of hours equal to Blood Potency. At this level of Hemotech, Ideaform can only mimic mundane technology.

    Exceptional Success: the Ideaform is especially durable: it gains extra Durability equal to Blood Potency/2 round up.

    Wonderworker (•••••)

    You may upgrade the previous powers of Hemotech.

    Cost: None or 3 Vitae
    Dice Pool: None or Wits + Science + Hemotech
    Action: Instant
    Duration: Scene

    Dramatic Failure: the Hemotech device becomes an automaton, but goes out of control.

    Failure: the item remains unaffected.

    Success: when Improvising, each point of Vitae now adds 4 bonus successes. You may boost the alterations performed by Tinkerer by spending 2 Vitae per roll, with the following results:
    1. The object’s Durability and Equipment/Damage/Armor bonus are increased by 3.
    2. The object may hold up to Willpower x 3 points of Vitae. You may store a number of Discipline dots which you know in the object equal to successes x 2 (caps at 10 dots). Devotions and Covenant powers cannot be stored. Ghouls and humans may use the stored Vitae and Disciplines, but humans are at risk of becoming ghouls with a blood bond to the creator of the object.
    3. The object deals aggravated damage against vampires.
    4. The object grants 8 Again on mundane uses of a single Skill.
    You may upgrade Ideaform by spending 3 extra Vitae and rolling the aforementioned dice pool. This creates machines that are made out of the same material, but are fully sapient, mobile, obedient and armed. Treat such an automaton as having the same general characteristics of its mundane counterpart, but with the Simplified Attributes of an ephemeral being and some means of locomotion. The automaton is of Rank equal to your Blood Potency/2 round up. You may have a number of such automatons under your command equal to your Blood Potency.

    Exceptional Success: the automaton's Rank is 1 dot higher than your Blood Potency [caps at Rank 5], or gains an extra Numen. You may spend 1 willpower DOT to make Wonderworker alterations last permanently.

    The automatons can be customized, but a generic template looks like this:

    Attributes: [Blood Potency x 5] Dots to be distributed among each Attribute, with the normal maximum determined by Rank

    Size: same as its mundane counterpart

    Corpus: Resistance + Size

    Willpower: Rank + Resistance

    Defense: Finesse + Rank/2 round up

    Initiative Mod: Finesse + Resistance/2 round up

    Speed: highest between Power and Finesse + Rank

    Max Vitae/Per Turn: Rank x 10/Rank x 3

    Influence: Blood 1+

    Manifestation: Twilight Form, Materialize

    Ban: Hemotech automatons follow orders to the letter, and consume 1 Vitae each 24 hours to keep running. Their advantage over ghouls is that they never disobey or feel fear, which might also spell doom for them.

    Bane: sunlight. Fire only deals lethal damage.

    Numina: Powered By Blood for free, Ephemeral Armor, Ephemeral Weapon, Mortal Mask, and extras equal to [Rank x 2] +1

    Powered By Blood: Hemotech automatons use Vitae instead of Essence. They restore Vitae by taking it from Kindred, mortals or other supernaturals through a grapple, receiving 1 Vitae per point of lethal damage dealt. Animals do not supply automatons with Vitae. They may heal their Corpus and improve Physical attributes with Vitae, like Kindred do. However, the feeding of an automaton, while not painful, does not cause the euphoria of the Kiss, so powerful Kindred often delegate the feeding of their automatons to lesser servants.

          I have to agree with Griautis that it looks fine.

          I'm not opposed to the technology angle, but it seems a bit... stretched in places thematically. I think this is most obvious with Wonderworker... which is very much Tinkerer Plus.

          I'd consider the following ideas:

          1) Switch Ideaform and Wonderworker, with Wonderworker being a more general upgrade to Improvise (repair), Tinkerer (improve), and Ideaform (create), as a capstone to boosting the main things you do with developing better versions of objects.

          2) Get rid of the Haven requirement for Tinkerer, since any workshop of sufficient quality should do; but perhaps a general penalty for the Discipline for working outside of a workshop dedicated to Hemotech. That way in their Haven, they're golden, but they can still work their magic in the field if needed.

          3) Having all the 2+ dot powers key off having the Informed Condition for an object rather than having to track individual power uses. Or even consider creating a new Condition instead (maybe something with a bit of a narrative snag to it making the items a bit "cursed" or something).

                              For level one, I'd allow the encyclopedic knowledge to apply for all appropriate sciences when the power is activated on something

                              For improvise, if it were me, I'd rework it- Repairing items is an extended dexterity plus craft plus hemotech roll, the vampire can spend vitae to enhance this roll, each vitae spent this way adds 2 successes. This way, the power is basically the vampire extending their own healing powers to technology, making the technology a part of the vampire's body spiritually, and this would be permanent.

                              Tinkerer- The options really don't seem balanced. As per GMC, a properly forged sword can provide a plus 2 dice, available with three dots of status, resources, allies, or the like. A discipline shouldn't be shown up by a skill. I'd work it like physical intensity, so a minimum of 2 dots increase here, and armor and damage bonuses don't really have limits, so no need to limit them here.

                              A vampire with this might be able to double their blood pool, that's a bit much.

                              Wonderworker seems a little schizophrenic here, I'd make it two separate applications of powers- enhancing the affects of tinkerer, and allowing the vampire to control technology they've had the informed condition with.

                              Ideaform- Well for one thing, I'd make the machine obviously unnatural, since it's a machine of crystalized blood, I'd make it easier to repair with improvise and since it's made from the vampire's blood it ought to be easier to control with wonderworker. I'd give the vampire the benefit of reducing the blood cost by allowing them to incorporate appropriate materials to include- as the blood crystalizes in shape, it forms around the provided materials, making it like a supercharged improvise.

                              I'd also only allow them to recreate machinery they've had the informed condition with. And allowing ideaform to have a synergistic bonus with wonderworker and improvise would make the discipline seem more in tune with the nature of disciplines in blood and smoke- one power with different applications, from lesser to strongest, rather than separate powers connected by theme.

                              So with this it'd the vampire can make vampires as part of their own body, understanding them, healing them, enhancing them, controlling them, and finally recreating them with their own blood
