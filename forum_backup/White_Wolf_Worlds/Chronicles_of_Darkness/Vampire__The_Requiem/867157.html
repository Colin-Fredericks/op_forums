    [2ed] Dominate and Breaking Point

    04-17-2016, 07:34 PM
    Experience a breaking point when following the command, and succeed at the related Resolve + Composure roll.
    Hi all.
    What exactly means:"Experience a breaking point when following the command" in order to resolve the Dominated (or mesmerized) condition? The act that causes the breaking point should be executed or the breking point incurs before the act, awekening the dominated from this condition?

    I've always thought that the breaking point incurs after the act. For example: "Kill that man". The dominated shoots with his gun to the man pointed and after this (killed or not) he take the breaking point and resolve the mesmerized condition. In fact: "Shoot your husband" is an example of suitable command according to the power description.
    But my doubt now is: what in the Dominate mechanics do not allow the victim to commit suicide? Why a command like: "Shoot yourself in the head" or "Jump out the window" (at the tenth floor), shouldn't works?
    There is a sentence in the text or some aspect of the mechanics that I missed?

                                                          Originally posted by Ashenrogue View Post

Like, it can happen, but this shouldn't be the type of thing you come across more than like once a story.
                                                          Integrity breaking points are not tied to the numerical value of your Integrity. What's a breaking point at Integrity 10 is the same as what's a breaking point at Integrity 1. Integrity is not morality, and what I'm talking about is not a moral or immoral action, but rather the personal integrity question of people, which everyone has. I'd wager that the people who would suffer a breaking point in that situation outnumber those who wouldn't.

                                                          Anyway, one thing to keep in mind: A breaking point is just a test. The difference between a soldier who has trained to see combat but hasn't actually been in a violent situation and a civilian who hasn't isn't whether they make the check or not: Both are almost certainly making the check, but the soldier's training might give him the bonus dice that make it easier for him to succeed.

                                                          Just the same, just because you experience a breaking point when you're standing over your wife deciding to murder her (if you're looking for a moment to tie this to, I'd tie it to the declaration of Intent) doesn't mean you don't murder her. It just means it's a very shocking and potentially traumatic thing to do.
