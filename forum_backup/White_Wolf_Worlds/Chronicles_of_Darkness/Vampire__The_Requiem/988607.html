    Has anyone thought up their own lineages?

    10-16-2016, 10:39 PM
    I so wanted to call them bloodlines, but that has another meaning in Requiem.

    What I'm specifically wonder about is if anyone else here has done family trees for their chronicles and characters, showing who Embraced who and detailing what one particular strain of the curse has looked like over the long centuries. I've wanted to do a 'generational' chronicle for ages now (since before Requiem for Rome, but doubly so since that came out) and figured I'd see if anybody else had taken a shot at it before I dove in and explored five centuries of Mekhet shenanigans. I'm toying with whether or not I want to string together the various Dark Eras or not.

    Remi. she/her. game designer.

      I have. Most of my Venture characters belong to my custom Nicomachus bloodline.

      It has no special powers but most experience a similar pattern in life. They are embraced as warrior-poets, possessing strong will, keen intellect and aggressive personalities. During their neonate years, they begin to feel a desire to campaign and conquer; to expand and discover the new. In our saturated world, this does feel harder but most express the beast's wanderlust in some way. Leading a communist party from a local environment to key regional player is a more underground expansion but is sufficient. I prefer being more overt though. After their warring, they oft find themselves in new locals, where they settle down and become local Venture, winning and building, until their childer leave them too.

      'Copyright' claim: You have permission to use them. Just be sure to send me a PM so I can enjoy a sense of pride in your desire to play my creations and so I can add new members and their adventures to the family tree.

                  Last game i played, where the PCs were sort of elders would most certainly count. While the family trees seen wouldn't go too deep - sires & childer mostly, sometimes sires, childer & grandchilder - they sort of spread out in interesting ways. Add to that the fact the older a vampire gets, the more time it had to make other childer (that will have formed ideas/plans/worldviews/connections of their own) and the possible interactions of the elder and/or its varied descendants with the "families" of other kindred, allying, competing, destroying or romancing with them, their sires, childer, rivals & etc and after sometime you end up with a surprisingly loaded minefield of associations.

                  For a PC example, my character, through a process involving more spur-of-the-moment rolls and the improvisational interpretation of them, was defined as having embraced 3 (known) childer in his 120+ years of nightlife, all of them victims of all too human violence "rescued" through the embrace.

                  The first, Melquior, was a bohemian investigative journalist in 1910s Rio de Janeiro with whom Baltazar, himself being a journalist, poet and ex-guerilla fighter dead for 20 years at the time, struck a friendship with, what resulted on his being close by when a group of thugs assaulted them and mortally wounded Melquior, whom Baltazar embraced on impulse. While it started fine, subtle differences of perspective and personal experience - like Baltazar being the sireless product of a strix-esque pact (different critter, different clan) that literally rose from an unmarked grave after being executed and hacked to pieces for opposing the government in a revolt in Santa Catarina - would drive some wedges and lead them in different directions, Melquior becoming a rabid leading Carthian (and nuisance to a number of elders), while his sire become a eccentric though well-established figure of the Ordo Dracul. In fact, due to their stormy relationship and Melquior being himself an ancilla/near-elder already, not all kindred that know of them are aware of the precise family connection, seeing them as either (barely) amicable rivals or brothers.


                  The second, Gaspar, is a fruit of Baltazar's "nomad years" (mid-10s to 50s), when he got tired of petty intrigue and rumour-mongering and left the city for a number of decades to travel across the country and beyond under the guise of a travelling circus freak show (and map obscure dragon's nest and what inhabited them or not, across the continent in the proccess), a runaway boy that ended being sort of adopted into the circus during the 40s. In one of those travels a wandering serial killer crossed their paths and disapeared with the boy, whose buried body in an unmarked grave in the woods would be found by a grieving Baltazar in the edge of frenzy, what lead to a beast-spurred desperate post mortem embrace of the boy, his frenzied return to quasi-life and the two of them would them proceed to track down and give the maniac some the most harrowing and final nights of his life. This incident also lead to the Kanaima, the peculiar devil-cats that made Baltazar, coming to visit their "grandchild", pleased with one that took so quickly well to the heart of predator and monster "unlike the soft talkative one in the big city", alerting Baltazar (a little too late) of one grievous error/omission on his part when he had mostly left Melquior and Rio de Janeiro. Already seeing Baltazar and his wife, the witch Ofelia, as the parents he wanted to stay with, the boy settled them into a macabre family of sorts and they adapted to the new situation with surprisingly little trouble and has become a Dragon, much like dad, in the following decades.


                  The third and final childe, Maria, came to be in the decades after Baltazar & family decided to settle down once more in Rio de Janeiro, a young woman that attracted the attention of Ofelia in the 70s, becoming a friend, apprentice and occasional lover of the witch, who had been seriously considering initiating her, when life decided to intervene and Maria was disappeared with by the military, much like many other university students or young people of the period, for connections (factual or spurious) with the armed resistance movements.
                  Ofelia found out their suspicions through Maria's parents and guilt-tripped/tricked Baltazar in hunting down her whereabouts, to rescue or avenge her. The result was some quick, heated talks with Melquior, a number of younger kindred he had mentored/tutored in the past decades, associates in the Ordo and other covenants disatisfied with how the military's "witch hunts" among intellectual and student circles were messing with the herds and prospective of a number of them, inadvertently sparking a series of quick, fast and very, very, brutally messy attacks against hideouts of the DOPS (the political police of the military junta) as those kindred "rescued" childer to be and/or killed a number of DOPS grunts out of spite, not to mention one or two tales of the group embrace of prisioners who would make their first meals out of their tormentors. Thankfully the political climate, not to mention the interrogators/torturers own penchant for secrecy and for isolated, out-of-the way hideout was probably the main reason the whole thing did not become a masquerade disaster.

                  It also lead to some hefty dissatisfaction with and pressure in the Invictus, that had the strongest ties/investment in the military) and the eventual intervention of the prince of the time, a Crone worshipper, who showed considerable (self-serving) leniency to the dissatisfied/rebelious kindred grievance, in no small part because the tension that had built up and sparked from the situation gave him very convenient justification he desired to take down a peg or two of the Invictus' growing influence for his own sake.

                  Things eventually calmed down and Maria, the childe of Baltazar (though some suspect her real sire is Melquior and Baltazar assumed her because he had a much better relation with prince & sheriff) would be lead by her own sorcerous leanings from life toward the Crone, but her along with a number of people coming from this wave of impassionated, violent embraces of the period, would also form a clique/social circle of sorts beyond their family or covenant relations, a generation of kindred informally known as "crias de chumbo" (childer of lead, a reference to the "years of lead", the harshest period of political persecution in Brasil).

                  At the start i just made some rough basics, to which me, the ST and the rest of the group (the Daeva Carthian PC was also an ally of Melquior, Maria was a pupil of the Ventrue Crone PC) would add or flesh out bits and pieces as the chronicle advanced, like Eric the Daeva assuming as Melquior's "godfather"/mentor as a favor to Baltazar when he decided to go nomad, what leads to some kindred thinking he is Melquior's sire instead, or how in one of his occasional visits through Rio de Janeiro back then sire and childe ended up in opposing sides of an armed revolt in the 30s that caused the fall of a prince, how Isabelle the Ventrue's present nightclub was Baltazar's between the 50-70s, when he changed domains in the city, which would later gain context with the whole "childer of lead" mess - that initially was just about Baltazar & Maria, but then evolved into something else as one or two references to kindred with a bias against the Invictus due to similar experiences settled in and it inspired me to write up something of a "period event" and "mending of bridges" of sorts between Baltazar and Melquior and their fighting on opposing sides of a praxis seizure in the late 30s.

                  And that's just crap relating directly to my character and his childer - and he does have at least one unmentioned grandchilder - things can get pretty convoluted when one tries to map other kindred, their childer, sires and who is still active, in torpor, destroyed or possible agendas. Pretty engrossing and entertaining overall.

                                  Originally posted by Shawarbaaz View Post

                                  Sincerely i don't see much of a problem. A lack of mythology/family trees is corrected by development of them, that much simpler in a setting with greater freedom from expectations about what should be fact, myth or convenient falsehood. No need for canon inconsistencies like Tyler as an ancilla in 1100s england while Critias and Khalid, kindred who actually have the background to justify book appearances, are utterly ignored by Constantinople By Night for example, or flame wars about bits of fiction treated as dogma and even worse flame wars about bits like Mithras being a literal childe of the Ventrue Antediluvian or "just" a very successful diablerist that remade his own history and that of the clan itself from the middle ages onwards, among other stuff.

                                  No presumption of canon/myth/truth means among other things that not only the ST but the players themselves have complete freedom to develop their own chronicle's mythology in tandem and progressively, adding or cutting bits as the narrative offers hooks, hints and scattered bits that are then exploited or tinkered with. It even allows for one to make different takes of a myth or wholy different vampiric backstories from game to game, or even between story arcs.

                                                  I made a powerful Ventrue dynasty in Tokyo named after the god of the moon (Tsukuyomi) with a friend of mine for some unaligned characters we had in mind. They were steeped in all kinds of history and complex traditions, and we wrote up a full family tree of vampires brought into the line from start to modern times. Among their traditions was various 'family gatherings' during events like new Embraces, reaching Ancilla status, and marriage. Sires were expected to be very involved with their childe's activities and essentially take them on as adopted children.

                                                  Marriage was another big thing in the line - for ritual purposes each Ventrue vampire in the line was supposed to pick a mortal and ghoul them, and then join with them as a spouse. It was to reinforce the line's 'familial integrity,' in honor of the line founder lost love, so the line is full of the twisted co-dependent relationships of various kinds of immortal blood addicts. I think we detailed 4 generations with the family tree, back to the 1400s. They were pretty conservative about new embraces, but the line's also full of ancient ghouls and some unacknowledged 'bastard' vampires too.

                                                  They're a bit provincial as vampires go, focusing on controlling specific territories rather than seeking wider influence over the workings of the city and Zaibatsu. We joked that one of the Ancilla in the line is 'The Shogun of the Shopping Mall' since he essentially controlled the owners of every business in the place and made contacts through them, working to keep the place open late hours into the evening, but he and his ghoul bride rarely looked past their current holdings and just focused on securing what they already had. This was the sire of the Neonate who got us started thinking on the entire thing; said Neonate is starting by running a manga cafe from behind the scenes and trying to expand influence over the neighborhood from there.
