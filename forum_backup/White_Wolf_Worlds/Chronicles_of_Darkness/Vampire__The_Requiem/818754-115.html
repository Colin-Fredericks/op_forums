                                            Originally posted by Raktus View Post
                                            So it takes longer to notch an arrow than to reload a gun?
                                            Depends. It takes longer to draw an arrow from a quiver, notch the arrow, pull the drawstring, select your target and fire than it does to pull an already preloaded magazine or cartridge from your pouch, dropping the empty one on the ground, replacing it, chambering the first bullet and firing, yes. But if you're talking about reloading a revolver or magazine cartridge or clip using a speedloader, then guess what? Pulling that arrow out, nocking it, pulling the drawstring, selecting your target and firing will be faster.
