    Yep, a chance die is the result of having a negative pool, it's not the pool itself. If you have a base pool of 3 and a -6 modifier, your pool is -3, which means you would roll a chance die. If you spend willpower, your pool is now 0, so you still roll a chance die. You can't say "a chance die plus three dice is a pool of 3" because a chance die could mean anything from 0 to -99.

        Originally posted by DubiousRuffian View Post
        What is the order of operations when applying environmental modifiers?
        The order for applying modifiers doesn't really matter because chance die is what you roll when your dice pool is zero or less. If your base dice pool is 4 and you have penalties totalling -6, you end up with a dice pool of -2. If you then apply a +2 bonus, you end up with a dice pool of 0. Then you roll a chance die.
        Basically, chance die is never your dice pool. It's what you roll for non-positive dice pools.

                        Is size supposed to impact speed?

                        On p.81 of VtR 2e it says under Step Seven: Determine Advantages

                        Others
                        A vampire’s Defense score is equal to the least of her Wits or Dexterity Attributes, with her Athletics Skill added. Size is 5. Health is equal to your character’s Size + Stamina + Resilience. Speed is Size + Strength + Dexterity.

                                            The impression I get is that instead of adding modifiers (aside from the listed modifiers) the severity of the breaking point should be adjusted by changing which level it's a breaking point on. As such my group has as a thumb rule that any breaking point carried out by someone else but still because of you (i.e. there's a level of distance between you and the event) increases the level of the breaking point by one for you. Since diablerie is always a breaking point, we'd put it at Humanity 1.

                                            Note that having someone carry out a breaking point should be a breaking point in its own right. Probably at around Humanity 5 if it's knowingly, and Humanity 6 if you do it accidentally or realise it after the fact. But don't roll multiple detachment checks for the same event. Just roll for the worst one.
