                        Martial arts is as much mental as muscle memory, Both are a part of it but there is still a large degree of forethought to a fight.                             Have you ever seen ( hopefully not in person) the terrified person keep swinging the baseball bat at the bad guy long after the bad guy has stopped twitching? Nothing but incoherent screams coming from the person while they swing?

                            Or the person who unloads a full clip of bullets into the now unmoving threat while they continue to stand there and pull the trigger?

                            The crazed psych can't get their target who went into the elevator, but takes all their rage out on the person who just got off instead..

                            More real:
                            Has anyone scared you or startled you so badly all you can manage to do is scream or drop to the floor and laugh while you find words.

                            Or you just swing and swing and then realize you just beat down your BFF?

                            When you drop a Brick on your foot and all you can manage to do is swear. You can't even tell someone what happened because the only thing that comes to mind is "fuck " over and over. You can barely nod or shake your head to the onslaught of questions.

                            People go into these "frenzies" all the time in movies or real life. Where they are too angry or too scared to speak or make rational logical choices. They simply react.

                            Not sure why it's so hard to think of a vampire falling into that sort of state...

                            As far as it being that way opening more questions. The answer is simple. How much blood that is spent is a player choice. Vampires are not aware that they have 13 vitae and can only spend 3 a turn or whatever. So spending any quantity on a physical discipline should suffice because your vampire doesn't know if it was 1 or 3 or 5. It was either a little or a lot. So that should answer how much gets spent per turn on physicals.

                            The beast isn't suicidal. Maybe you go all out because you don't care at that moment what happens because all you want is to eat that person. If you were starving you wouldn't waste it...


                            The one thing I think we will all agree on..

                            Do whatever works for your group. Whatever is best for you and your players, go with it. And that's perfectly acceptable. As long as you are having fun playing, that's what matters. If two players disagree, go with whatever the storyteller rules.
