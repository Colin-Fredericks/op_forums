        You can ride the wave when you are provoked to make a frenzy check. Stir the Beast specifically states (now that I've compared the two) that the frenzy that results is an "involuntary frenzy (p.103)"--except you get to specify the target/goal. That last bit makes it functionally identical to riding the wave, however the Merit style will not apply since it is, technically, not riding....

        If your objective is to have a power that enables one to just enter frenzy whenever at cost of 1 Willpower, then simply say "may expend 1 Willpower to provoke an immediate frenzy check in self; the expended Willpower counts as well for activation of Riding the Wave, if desired."

        You could also houserule that the style applies to activation of the Coil as well, if you wish. RAW they do not, however.

        --Khanwulf

            The way I see it; Stir the Beast works like an involuntary frenzy provocation (except it really is voluntary, but mechanically it's involuntary). That means you can attempt to hold off the frenzy and try to control it by spending Willpower and rolling Resolve + Composure (as written on page 105 under 'Riding the Wave').

            Basically, Stir the Beast gives the user some control over when he's going to be provoked into a frenzy. I see no reason why this user then cannot try to grab that frenzy by the reins and turn it into a Riding the Wave just like with any other frenzy provocation. If you read the 2nd to 5th dots from Coil of the Wyrm, it seems highly likely that Stir the Beast is meant to be used this way. Am I wrong?

                Originally posted by Ventrue Life View Post
                Stir the Beast works like an involuntary frenzy provocation (except it really is voluntary, but mechanically it's involuntary).
                It's not a provocation. You go directly to Frenzy, and you can only attempt to Ride the Wave when you otherwise would've attempted to resist Frenzy.

                On the other hand I think it's a common house rule to allow Riding the Wave in combination with Stir the Beast. We do it. Otherwise you get two mutually exclusive benefits from Coil of the Wyrm when the design philosophy for second edition has been for later dots to expand upon or complement the previous dots.

                Edit: Another house rule we have is that we don't award beats for using Stir the Beast unless you use it to redirect a natural Frenzy. Otherwise it's a relatively safe (since you can choose target) WP to Beat converter.
