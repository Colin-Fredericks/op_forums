                    My interpretation is closer to the 2nd one, and I never thought it would be in question. After reading this thread, I can understand why you might lean to the 1st.

                    I've always read Claws of the Unholy as:
                    Merit - you do aggravated damage when you pop claw in frenzy
                    Drawback - you can only ever do aggravated damage when you pop claw in frenzy

                    A frenzied Gangrel will likely pop claw instantly and destroy their enemy if given a chance. For those who say a BP 1 Gangrel can't pop claw right away, then they would spend the two vitae over two turns while pursuing their prey in other ways. If the Gangrel doesn't have the vitae for it, he/she probably won't do it. If Riding the Wave, the player can choose whether to pop claw at all. But if they DO pop claw, they better be prepared to bring someone to final death.

                    That's its own drawback - you are going to Kill whatever you're fighting, and you've permanently lost the ability to claw someone into torpor.

                                        It never even occurred to me that this would be interpreted in any way other than 1. Like, "the character manifests Claws of the Unholy any time she’s frenzied" seems completely unambiguous to me. You're in Frenzy? Then you manifest Claws of the Unholy. The "When wielding claws borne of Unnatural Aspect while in frenzy, this Merit takes effect" part, then, establishes the Vitae cost. Between these two sentences, the natural interpretation to me seems to be that every time a vampire with this Merit enters Frenzy, she must activate Unnatural Aspect for 2 Vitae (spending it over multiple turns if necessary, and presumably falling into Torpor if she doesn't have enough Vitae to pay for it, just like when you don't have enough Vitae to heal all of your injuries during daysleep). When she does this, her claws have a damage modifier of 0A instead of their normal modifier. The only question this interpretation leaves in my mind is what happens if her Unnatural Aspect isn't claws, and my ruling on that is, you would then be unable to access the normal +2L AP2 claws out of Frenzy and be unable to access your wings or whatever in Frenzy.

                                        Upon reading others' interpretations, I can see now how it could be read as 2. Still seems wrong to me, as if you are in Frenzy and don't activate Unnatural Aspect, that would be a time you frenzied and didn't manifest Claws of the Unholy, which seems to be contrary to the drawback to me, but that's just me.
