                            What the pebbles is going on?

                                        40) Contact is lost with a ghoul family in a tiny, isolated southeastern mountain community. Upon investigation, the characters discover that the town's inhabitants are all gone, including the original Gangrel contact, and that a decade's worth of kudzu growth blankets the community. Several shaggy, elusive figures appear to be the only mamillian life remaining, and the only ones who might be able to give the characters a clue as to how a square mile of vegetation could become a single gigantic mandrake colony.

                                        So. Do you try to find the civilians lost in the scary woods, or do you shrug, cut your losses, and open a winery?

                                            41) For whatever reason, a particular lick with a very high humanity has roused the ire of a hateful elder. Without said licks knowledge, this elder has made a game out of finding subtle, second-hand ways to gradually break them down by staging an elaborately-ruled, round-robin game of Break/Corrupt the Cutie and secretly offering other, equally jaded monsters prizes for participating.

                                            Do you warn them, making yourself a target by spoiling the fun? Do you leave yourself out of it so you don't have to watch the carnage? Or do you take the opportunity to make a little social currency by showing that self-righteous jerkstore what unlife is really all about?
