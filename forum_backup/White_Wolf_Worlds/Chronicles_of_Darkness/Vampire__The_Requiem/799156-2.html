                                Read the World's Loom works as such:

                                "The Uratha gains insight from his surroundings, hearing of a coming threat from the growling engines of a traffic jam, reading secrets from amidst the gang tags on the

                                within his Glory Renown in miles. "

                                Things he can learn about:
                                • Threats. (The police chief is planning a raid on the pack; werewolves come seeking a showdown; the tenement is unstable and will soon collapse; the old spirit Thurizag has returned)
                                • The supernatural. (Something preys on human residents in the area; something dwells in the high-rise; something numbs the minds of residents)
                                • Violence. (A riot is brewing; there is a pattern to the murders; a hunt is taking place)
                                • Conflicts. (Gangs are feuding over drug territory; the big corporation is under financial attack, the mayoral election is turning nasty)

                                                        I've only read the first post so forgive me if this is outdated.

                                                        Really, diplomacy is the way to deal with the threat. Unless the town is exceptionally small, a pack of vampires and werewolves should be able to share it. Even then, they both want different things. Vampires need blood, werewolves need Essence. Unless the werewolves are outright man-eaters, or there's no loci, they aren't really likely to be fighting over domains. And if there are no loci, the werewolves would probably be better served by finding some other place to reside. It's worth pointing out that werewolves can make loci, so even if the vampires all happen to lair in the same place, which is also the only locus in town, the werewolves can make a more conveniently accessed locus on their own. Slaughtering a bunch of undead and having them fuel their blood magics in a battle over a locus is likely to taint the locus, or at least spawn rival spirits.

                                                        After things have escalated, the vampires are likely to lose in a straight up fight. So a smart tactic might be to scour the area for monster hunters to point in the general direction of the werewolves, even if it means killing people and framing the werewolves for it. Also, werewolves often have human lives and identities. In the fiction throughout the core book, the werewolves had their lives upturned by a beshilu who inhabited a cop, then sending the rest of the police force after them. A vampire, providing one of them had a nicely placed ghoul or dominate, would be able to do something similar. With human forms wanted by the police, animal control alerted to rabid, wild dogs, the werewolves are likely to have a hard time to operate.

                                                        There's ways around this, of course, the werewolves could just jump into the Hisil or start stealing skins. Alternatively if war were declared, the werewolves would just start a Siskur-Dah, then hunt the vampires down during the day and rip them to shreds. But, like I said, there's no reason for that to happen outside of special circumstances. If they were merciful, they might just stake them, ship them to Abu Dhabi in boxes and never look back.

                                                            Replying to Shadow Knight.

                                                            Let's just assume, that you as a vampire have dominate, and arrange for all of these things to happen. These overlaying threats. Number one, you have to do all of this without the Werewolves noticing. They are entrenched in their territory. Problem is, not only will and entrenched Pack with a dedicated locus be using Read the World's Loom, they will also be using Lore of the Land if at all possible. In addition, since they are set up to fight vampires, they will most likely have enacted wards against anything supernatural from a Kindred. Hey the Mekhet can learn about obscure spirit facets then the Werewolves certainly know about ghouls, and know when they are entering the territory now. With Lore of the Land

                                                            "Success: Each success reveals a single rite, spiritual resonance,
                                                            or other supernatural, widespread effect affecting the
                                                            territory that the Uratha is currently in. If used in the territory
                                                            of the Uratha’s pack, the Facet also reveals the presence of all
                                                            creatures within Purity x 100 yards of his current position, as
                                                            well as other threats such as fires, traps, pitfalls, or explosives.
                                                            Only physically present creatures are detected, so Lore of the
                                                            Land does not reveal spirits, ghosts, or other beings in Twilight.

                                                            Exceptional Success: The Uratha may add his Purity to
                                                            all rolls to navigate and move through the territory he is in
                                                            for the remainder of the scene."

                                                            Then of course you are neglecting spirit allies who will be watching for shit to go down and will inform the pack. Removing a pack from his territory is no easy thing.

                                                            You need to play to a vampires strengths, trying to kill them via combat and traceable servants is not the way. Keeping your hands clean and turning all your resources to ruining their territory and exposing them to the mortal world is.
