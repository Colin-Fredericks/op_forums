    Vicissitude (Modified Protean for Ventrue Bloodline Tzimisce)

    12-22-2015, 02:30 PM
    Hi all this is my first post to the forums. I've been in love with the Tzimisce and Vicissitude since i read about them and have been wanting to make the discipline possible for VtR2e.
    Over the pats few days some friends and I have been discussing Vicissitude and how it could function, i whole heartedly believe it should be a discipline, whereas they vote for devotions or blood sorcery as it has too much overlap with Protean, which is where I got the idea to make it a modified Protean. SO yeah, hope you enjoy, looking forward to feed bacl.


    Vicissitude (Discipline)

    Tzimisce Protean - Vicissitude

    Vicissitude has been a long guarded secret of the Tzimisce bloodline, only allowing themselves to
    learn it, for many centuries it has been a mystery, yet new light would appear to have shined on the
    flesh crafters discipline, a highly specialised version of protean focusing on control rather than
    allowing the beast to burst fourth

    Malleable Visage •
    Tzimisce have long been masters of crafting flesh with ease, this is most prominent when they
    change the way they look, as if becoming an entirely different person. Does any Tzimisce truly
    know their own face ?
    Cost: 1 Vitae + 1 willpower
    Dicepool: Intelligence + medicine + Vicissitude vs stamina

    Dramatic failure: the vampire not only fails but disfigures themselves in a serious manner taking 4
    lethal which can be left to heal over time or healed with further use of vitae
    Failure: nothing happens, no changes are made
    Success: changes are made equal to success
    Exceptional success: changes are made as per success and are flawless, not even a mother
    would think you're not their son.

    Each success affords you a bonus equal to successes for roleplaying as the person you have imitated, up to 5 die.
    Each success determines how well you have managed to imitate your subject, 1 success
    would be sub par and it will be easy for you to be caught out. 4 successes would make it hard for anyone
    to tell the the physical appearance of yourself and the person you are imitating apart.

    Drawback: not all changes are perfect, you can be caught out as a infiltrator if trying to imitate
    someone during a contested roll wits + subterfuge vs perception, unless and exceptional success
    was attained.

    Unnatural Anatomy ••
    Where the Gangrel focus on their beast and taking traits of predators for their own, the Tzimisce
    focus on using their own anatomy for versatile means. A Tzimisce may make their pupils
    dilate beyond normal means turning their eyes black in order to see in the dark, or they may
    even force their bones out of their finger tips to create a lethal weapon, the Tzimisce is only limited
    by what their body will allow them to do.
    When the Tzimisce learns Unnatural Anatomy they may learn 3 Anatomical changes from the list.
    These may only be changed during a full days sleep and at the cost of 1 willpower and 1 vitae per
    change.
    Cost: 1 Vitae
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: Reflexive
    Duration: Scene
    By spending a point of vitae the Tzimisce may manifest three of her chosen anatomical changes
    reflexively during her turn.

    Bone Claws: The Tzmizce forces the bones in his fingertips to push through the skin and form
    bone claws, these claws are harder than bone and cause 1L damage during a brawling
    attack.

    Calcified Sub-Dermis: The Tzmisce produces extra calcium that hardens just beauty their skin
    making them harder to penetrate and damage internal structures, this counts as a 1/1 armour.

    Hyper Senses: the Tzimisce can do what their body allows them, this means that they can force
    their pupils to be extremely large to see in the dark or make their Olfactory Glands become
    enlarged in order to smell better, the Tzimisce may even enlarge their ear drums in order to hear at
    distance. When active treat this as having the Acute Senses (P.109 VtR2e) merit.

    Irresistible Pheromones: Pheromones are one of the most unknown factors in day to day
    relationships, however the Tzimisce have learnt how use their pheromones to their full advantage
    by spreading them through the air and at greater potency, when active gain +2 to all social rolls,
    this can also be stacked with Dominate.

    Bone Plating: Being staked is always a major concern for the kindred, as it induces unwilling
    topor, the Tzimisce however have discovered a way to protect themselves from this threat by
    forming a temporary layer of bone around their hearts during combat or even day sleep. When
    active the heart gains 1/1 armour, this stacks with Calcified Sub-Dermis.

    Environmental Camouflage: the Tzimisce have learned to harness and produce mass amounts
    of melanin within their bodies coupled with the production of chromataphors , affording them to
    instantly change their skin colour to any situation, this is mostly used during stealthy activities
    where every last mm of the skin and hair on the Tzimisce is turned completely black. When active
    gain + 2 to all stealth rolls and subtract your blood potency from all rolls to detect you.

    Adrenal Overload: The adrenaline glands are often associated with the fight or flight response
    within all creatures, the Tzimisce have learnt to harvest this gland and focus in ways other creature
    could not, to such efficiency that even time appears to slow in their eyes as their veins burst with
    power. When active gain +2 to initiative and once per scene after the results of a roll you may
    choose to cancel your action or change course to avoid harm.

    Critical Mass: muscles are the powerhouse for all movement and power, the Tzimisce know this
    so they have learned how to temporarily increase their muscle mass to allow for some devastating
    effects. Whilst active gain +2 to all strength based rolls, due to the increase in mass however take
    -1 to initiative, this can be staked with Adrenal Overload

    Flesh Rending •••
    The Tzimisce are known for being masters over flesh and bone, the way they command it is by all
    means unnatural, however when used in combat it can bring fourth devastating results such as
    ripping chunks of flesh out that only a high caliber bullet could do, or breaking bones as if they
    were chalk, even more devastating, the Tzimisce doesn't even need to be touching their target to
    cause such damage.
    Cost: 2 Vitae
    Dice Pool: Strength or Dex + Medicine + Blood Potency
    System: when used during a brawling attack treat the attack as 1L p/success and apply any
    condition such as wracked as seen fit.
    when used during a ranged attack treat the attack as 1L p/success and treat resolve as +2 meters
    the distance they can attack with this apply any necessary conditions.

    Dramatic Failure: The attack not only backfires and does so to an extreme effect, the Tzimisce
    takes 5 lethal and treats the body part they attacked as unusable for the remainder of the scene.
    When used at range supplement the body part used for the body part targeted.
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: the opponent takes 1L per success during a brawling attack and takes 1L per success
    during a ranged attack.
    Exceptional Success: Vitae is refunded or may spend 1 extra vitae to upgrade every 3L damage
    caused to 1 Aggravated.

    Anatomical Evolution ••••
    Anatomical evolution is one the Tzimisce's ultimate weapons, pushing the body to limits which to
    mortals and some kindred impossible. With these specialised evolutions the Tzimisce become
    formidable opponents that all should fear to challenge. The Tzimisce may don one of the following
    Evolutions.
    Cost: 1 Vitae
    Dice Pool: none
    Action: reflexive
    Duration: scene
    When Unnatural Anatomy has been activated the Kindred may spend 1 vitae to reflexively activate
    his chosen Evolution. To change the evolution a full day sleep and the expenditure of 1 vitae and
    one willpower is necessary.

    Bone Blades: with this evolution the Tzimisce forms blades made of bone on his forearms which
    give +2L modifier on Brawl attacks and act as armour piercing 2, these blades deal lethal to even
    Kindred.

    Gorgon Hair: this evolution causes the hair of the kindred to condense and grow, forming thick
    dreadlock like structures, however vitae also gives the hair life and mobility as if they were sharp
    hardened tentacles. When active add dots in Vicissitude to all grappling rolls, the hair can also be
    used to whip and stab at opponents dealing +0L.

    Bone Armour: bone is a dense material, the femur can take tonnes of pressure before breaking,
    the Tzimisce have realised this and have learnt how to form a bone exoskeleton which acts as a
    3/0 armour with an initiative modifier of -2.

    Juggernaut: when juggernaut is active the vampire becomes an unstoppable force, giving him +3
    to his strength which can thrust past his regular limit and +1 to his size giving him a temporary
    stamina boost too. Whilst active if the vampire sets himself in motion, he cannot be stopped by
    normal means such as wooden or brick walls making him a potent weapon. This also gives a -2 to
    initiative.

    Blackened Blood: Blood is the source of all life but the Tzimisce have learnt how to make it the
    bane of all life and unlife, the bloodline has learnt to turn their blood into a thick black like
    substance that burns like acid and poisons like Cyanide. Whilst active the kindred can spit this
    blood that deals Toxicity damage equal to his dots in Vicissitude. If consumed by another kindred it
    deals immediate lethal equal to half his dots in Vicissitude rounded up.

    True Immortality •••••
    Legends speak of some Tzimisce being immortal absolute. The legends speak of these individuals
    being cut down only to return the next night a new as if they had never been killed, even if the body
    was burnt. What other bloodlines and clans fail to see is that the Tzimisce have found ways to
    cheat death by infusing their essence into safely kept body parts.
    Cost: 5 Vitae + 1 Aggravated
    Dice Pool: intelligence + medicine + Vicissitude
    Action: extended requires 15 successes
    Duration: until used
    When preparing for immortality the Tzimisce must sacrifice a body part such as a hand, eye, rib,
    sets of teeth or whatever is suitable and infuse it with 5 points of vitae, after this is done and
    required amount of successes are reached the Tzimisce can store the body part in a box or jar and
    bury it or put it somewhere for safe keeping. Upon death the Tzimisce will begin to reform from the
    stored body part which takes a full 12 hours during the day. When the Tzimisce awakes, they
    awake with 2 Vitae and a frenzy check must be made, additionally their right most health box is filled with lethal and the remaining filled with aggravated damage, this is to represent the gruelling task of cheating death.

    Drawback: The Tzimisce is not completely immortal, if the body part is found and destroyed, the
    Tzimisce will not regenerate from it and will have met the final death. The Tzimisce will also always have a scar where the body part was sacrificed, to others who notice this, they will know that the Tzimisce has prepared for his own resurrection and actively seek out and destroy what remains of him.

                              Personally... I have two to three critiques. First, I'm not a fan of the overlap between these new abilities, and existing disciplines. Malleable Vissage is effectively repeating an Obtenebration power, as is the camo power. The social bonus power is something Majesty does at low levels. Adrenaline is overlapping with Celerity. Current disciplines, without exception, all do something that no other power can do. Honestly, you could probably put everything else but the 3rd and 5th power as just alternate Protean 2 / 4 abilities that are unique to the bloodline.

                              Second thought is that True Immortality sounds a lot more like a Scale than a discipline power.

                              Third is that this doesn't seem to really follow the format for Disciplines. Disciplines, in 2e, follow a pattern on building up of previous abilities. Take Dominate for example. You hypnotise someoen with your eyes. Then, the second, third, etc powers all build off that initial hypnosis. Same with Majesty - you have an aura that attracts people, then enchants them, then manipulates the enchanted's emtions at a whim. This feels like a disorganized grab bag of abilities.

                              EDIT - 4th thought. Flesh rending needs to include a touch attack of some kind in here as part of the action. And, to be honest, its a bit of a weak attack. Compare to a Fire Axe - 3 automatic successes. Add on 3 dots of vigor as a starting PC, and we're looking at about 7 damage with a single success to hit on average, no willpower, no physical buffing. You won't be getting that even with full power here.
