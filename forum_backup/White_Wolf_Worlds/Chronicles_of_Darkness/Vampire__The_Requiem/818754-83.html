                                                    The penalty roughly corresponds to size of the targeted area. I'd say -4 just like hands. Maybe -3 in the non-human forms. The damage required is dependent on how sturdy the targeted area is. If you're looking to break the jaw I'd say Stamina damage. Jaw bones are massive and you can easily break a hand against it so they're obviously a lot sturdier than hands.

                                                    Just remember that a werewolf heals 1-6 bashing per turn based on Primal Urge, can spend one Essence to heal as many lethal damage, and heals all bashing and lethal damage automatically while in Gauru.

                                                        Originally posted by Cainite View Post
                                                        Scenario: A Vampire-With-a-Strix-Inside is in a fight with some Werewolves and Vampires. It's been around the block enough to know that Werewolf teeth are a real pain against its host body. He would like to disable said teeth.

                                                        There are rules for targeting limbs (for a wrack) and the head itself (for stunning). If one would target the muzzle of a wolf with a sword to cause a "muzzle wrack", would you make it a -2 that needs damage more-than-stamina to disable, a -3 with the same, or -4 to cause an instant wrack just like with targeting a hand to break fingers? So the goal would be to break the teeth/muzzle.
                                                        Targetting a jaw is likely going to be -4, the same as a hand. Targetting teeth specifically will be a -5, same as eye. Of course, werewolves and vampires heal very fast, so its very likely going to end up as healed the next turn. Tilts are very ineffective against vamps and wolves unless done with agg damage.

                                                        Also, a Vampire needs to be Torpid to be open for Strix possession. Would it be possible for a materialized Strix to do enough damage in combat to a future host to make them go down (and not heal enough in 1 turn to get up again), then possess the body on the next turn?
                                                        Sure. It would go something like this -
                                                        Turn 1 - put vampire into torpor.
                                                        Turn 2 - Activate Possess Kindred. Spend willpower, make the roll, spend the turn possessing the body
                                                        Turn 3 - Free to act. Given the wording that it takes "a turn" to possess the body, I'm assuming that means that the possession doesn't fully take for a full round, and thus no reflexive actions (such as Celerity) are possible until the full initiative count goes through.

                                                        Do normal Torpor length rules apply with a Strix possession, or can it use the host body immediately after possessing, even if it was Torpid because of too much lethal damage?
                                                        Torpor rules do not apply to strix possessions. Damage, stake through the heart, you name it. Strix possessed beings don't torpor, they keep going until the body is destroyed through agg damage.
