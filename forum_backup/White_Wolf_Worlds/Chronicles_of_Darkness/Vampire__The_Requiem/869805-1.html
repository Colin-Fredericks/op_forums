    Originally posted by Maina View Post
    We've had this discussion in another thread, though with humans. Your earlier statement kind of contradicts this attitude, too. I don't think you have to drink it all to get all the vitae. Volume of blood in a living thing doesn't really matter in my mind.
    I didn't actually say drink all of their blood. I said drink them to death. Vitae is life, not blood, and the way I see it, if you drink enough of their blood to kill them, you've taken all of their Vitae.

    Originally posted by Maina View Post
    With humans, I don't think drinking all Vitae drains all their blood. It doesn't really make any sense, given you can survive having all your vitae being taken.
    Sure, but if you drink all of a human's vitae, their health track will be full of lethal damage, which is exactly what would happen to an animal under my proposed proportional damage house rule.

    Originally posted by Maina View Post
    With animals, therefore, there are two interpretations that seem to make sense:

    1. It's the same as humans. Which means drinking all their vitae doesn't drink all their blood and they can potentially survive it (though size 1 critters won't give you much time to stabilize them).

    2. It's not the same, and you do have to drink all their blood to get all their vitae. But this means they'll be dead before you finish drinking, and they probably shouldn't survive half-feedings. It makes less sense to me, but it's valid as anything else.
    Like I've said a few times now, I'm pretty sure the intent of the rule is that if you use up the animal, you get its full amount of Vitae. Really, there is no official rule for the animal surviving or for getting less than its full Vitae capacity from it. What I'm saying is that if you want such a thing to be possible, you could make it work like it works on humans by dealing an amount of lethal damage to the animal proportional to the amount of Vitae you drink. Chattanos seemed to try to counter this by linking to a post where he talks a lot about how much blood a pig has and how it would take a long time to drink gallons of blood. I responded that his post was accurate, but really has nothing to do with my proposed house rule, which is concerned with amount of Vitae and amount of Health, not amount of blood.

                                                            Again though, you're probably not completely exsanguinating the animal to get that much vitae. If we compare to feeding from humans,

                                                            Originally posted by page 95
                                                            Kindred can pull a number of Vitae from a mortal equal to the mortal’s unmodified Health dots. Every point of Vitae she takes causes one point of lethal damage.
                                                            That means if you drink all of the Vitae from a human, their health track will be full of lethal damage. That still leaves a number of turns of bleeding out equal to their health dots before the track is full of aggravated damage and they actually die, and you kind of need to have blood to bleed out. Yet, a vampire that continued to drink that blood would not gain Vitae from it, ergo we are forced to conclude that you don't have to drink all of a vessel's blood to get all of its Vitae.

                                                            So again, we're back to "if you take the full amount of Vitae from an animal, fill its health track with lethal damage. If you want a house rule to allow you to take less than the full amount of vitae, deal a proportional amount of lethal damage to it." And the amount of actual blood that needs to be consumed for this to happen is an abstraction.
