    Jiang Shi Unique Discipline, Bloodlines, and Covenant

    09-18-2024, 11:35 AM
    I like the Jiang Shi, but it feel like they are there, they don't even have bloodline or unique discipline, even though they're a full clan that still exist within the modern night, so I'm here to change that, with a new Discipline that replace the Jiang Shi Celerity, 10 Bloodline, and a new Covenant for Vampire of Asia (Mostly South, Southeast, and East Asia).

    Essence
    Jiang Shi need Essence to stay grounded within the land of the living, every time Jiang Shi wake up, he lose a point of Essence, unless he sleep in his grave. Jiang Shi can spend 1 Essence to inflict the (Numb) condition, or 3 Essence to inflict the (Addict) condition to the victim of their feeding.

    Bane: The Ephemeral Curse. Because their presence in
    this world is strangely light, they cannot cause either the
    Swooning or Scarred Conditions by feeding. And when the Jiang Shi have no Essence left, they suffer the (Languid) condition, until they have Essence in their system again.

    Numb
    Suffer no wound penalties for the scene. Additionally, any rolls to detect by touch suffer -5 penalty.
    Possible Sources: Jiang Shi feeding on you
    Resolution: Wait a scene.​

    Tempest God Body
    The wind blow, and death follow, this Discipline represent the Jiang Shi body connection to the wind and storm, but also to life and breath.

    Extract Chi Flow
    The Jiang Shi, can steal more than just the blood out of living being, they can suck out the Essence within the blood. When Jiang Shi is feeding, he get one point of Essence for every 2 Lethal damage he deal, he can store Essence equal to his Resolve dot. If the Jiang Shi is feeding on animal, he gain Essence equal to Vitae he gain from it.

    Great Leap Outward ●
    It is said that the Jiang Shi can leap up into the air with great ease. With the help of the wind, the Jiang Shi can jump and soar like a bird.
    Cost: 1 Essence
    Action: Reflextive
    Dice Pool: None
    Duration: One turn
    Effect: The Jiang Shi can jump 70 feet horizontally and 40 vertically, this distance increase every 35 feet for horizontal, and 20 feet for vertical, per dot in this Discipline, so with Tempest 4, Great Leap Outward can jump up to 140 feet horizontally, and 80 feet vertically. And they take no damage from the fall. While the Jiang Shi is in the air, they fall slowly, and move in the air, which make it look like they're flying, when this Discipline is still active, they fall at a rate of 1 feet per turn, and can move in the air, with speed equal to their Dexterity.

    Stolen Breath ●●
    Cost: None
    Action: Instant
    Dice Pool: Strength + Brawl – Defense
    Duration: None
    Effect: Instead of bitting to suck the life out of mortal, Jiang Shi can choose to steal the breath of it victim, this isn't actual physical breath, but more of the victim animating life force. When feeding this way, the Jiang Shi gain Essence for every point of Lethal damage he deal, but gain no Vitae when feeding like this. If he is feeding from an animal, he gain Essence equal to it size.

    Dust On The Wind ●●●
    Cost: 2 Essence and 1 Vitae
    Action: Instant
    Dice Pool: None
    Duration: 1 Scene.
    Effect: The Jiang Shi becomes insubstantial. The creature seems to turn into a cloud of dust or a noxious fume that quickly dissipates, but such merely masks the shift into Twilight, the insubstantial state shared by various ephemeral being. While in this state, the Jiang Shi may not utilize Vitae or any of his supernatural powers save for the various God Body Discipline and Chi Rite. He may, however, perceive and interact with ephemeral being. While in this state, the character’s Speed becomes Strength + Dexterity + 10. Jiang Shi can steal Essence from ephemeral, when feeding Essence from an ephemeral being, the Jiang Shi don't deal any damage to it, instead he gain one Essence per turn, until the ephemeral leave the grapple, or it run out of Essence. The Jiang Shi can spend a Willpower to instantly teleport to it "grave" (the site where they become a Jiang Shi). The Jiang Shi can go between the frequency of the Twilight, and even go to the Shadow and the Underworld, but that would require another spending of 2 Essence.

    Thunderbolt's Kiss ●●●●
    A Jiang Shi using this power may hold lighting quietly and invisibly in her hand, waiting to be released or may cause it to arc and thunder about her hands and body. Depending
    upon the character's desires, she may appear to throw the
    lightning bolt at her foes, or it may simply jump from her hand (or eyes) and strike them, it have range of 40 yard per dot in Tempest God Body
    Cost: 3 Essence
    Action: Instant
    Dice Pool: Tempest God Body + Occult + Dexterity
    Duration: 1 Turn per Success
    Roll Result
    Dramatic Failure:     The lighting go out of control and strike at the user instead, dealing 3 Lethal damage.
    Failure: Lighting can't manifest.
    Success: The lighting can deal 1 Lethal damage per success, and cause (Stunned) tilt.
    Exceptional Success: Same as regular Success, but cause (Immobilized) tilt instead.

    The Flight of the Dragons ●●●●●
    This most awesome of Tempest God Body powers allows a Jiang Shi to channel Chi through his body and into the world around
    him to affect the local weather patterns. Depending upon the amount of power he can muster, he can even cause terrible storm.
    Cost: 4 Essence and 3 Vitae
    Action: 1 Turn
    Dice Pool: Tempest God Body + Occult + Dexterity
    Duration: Vary
    Roll Result
    Dramatic Failure: The body can't handle the amount of power channeling throughout it and the environment, suffer 3 Aggravated damage.
    Failure: Your power is only enough to have 1 type of environmental tilt, that affect 1 miles radius, and last for one scene.
    Success: You successfully change the environment, you can have 3 type of environmental tilt, that affect 4 miles radius, and last for 1 day per success.
    Exceptional Success: You can have 5 type of environmental tilt, that affect 16 miles radius, and last 1 week per success.

    Chi Art
    The Jiang Shi also have instinctual knowledge of special Chi Art, a form mortal magic that they retain even in unlife, because of their unique Embrace. These Chi Art cost the same experiences as other Ritual Discipline. Library Merit affect Chi Art.

    Cost: one Essence or two Willpower per dot of Art, and each Art have it own special ingredient needed to cast the Rite.

    Dice Pool: Intelligence + Academic + Chi Art.

    Roll Result
    Dramatic Failure: Gain (Wanton) Condition
    Exceptional Success: Can apply (Sated) Condition if and when the ritual succeeds.

    Heart Grave ●
    Target of Success: 2
    The Jiang Shi can relocate it "grave" (the location where it was Embrace) to another location, to do this they bury their heart in the location that they want to be their new grave, and then spill the Vitae of those pure of heart, mind, and soul. The Vitae the Jiang Shi require to spill is equal to 11 - their Humanity. Jiang Shi need their grave because, if they try to wake up outside of their grave, it take 2 Vitae instead of 1.

    Eye of the Invisible ●●
    Target of Success: 3
    The Jiang Shi take out his eye from his socket, suffer 2 Lethal damage, and attune it to the Essence within his body, as he focusing on the type of being he wish to see. Once finished, he put his eye back on, and his sight can now detect trace and reveal the type of creature he focus on, this last until he go to sleep.​

    Second Breath ●●●
    Target of Success: 4
    The Jiang Shi Embrace is different from the other Clan, their Embrace can only be done posthumous. They first need to drain the intended target of the Embrace of all their blood. Then they need to make a grave for them by digging out a hole for them, and spill the Vitae of those pure of heart, mind, and soul onto the target expose heart. The Jiang Shi need to spill a total of 10 Vitae into the target heart, and then bury the body, within the next 90 days, the corpse will rise from the grave and become a Jiang Shi, how long before the Jiang Shi rise depend on how powerful their Willpower is. The Jiang Shi can replace the target heart with the heart of the pure, which will give the Embrace Jiang Shi Blood Potency of 2 instead of 1.

    Enlighten Mind ●●●●
    Target of Success: 5
    The Jiang Shi meditate on his nature, and what is existence thus far represent. At the end of his meditation, he gain a little spark of enlightenment, which clear his mind. The Jiang Shi get +5 to all roll that use Mental skill or attribute, this last for 24 hours, after which he mind is fog by madness and gain the (Madness) condition, and can't use this Art again until he resolve the condition.​

    Mask of the Thousand Faces ●●●●●
    Target of Success: 6
    The Jiang Shi creates a mask and become the form of that mask. The Jiang Shi must kill the person he wish to assume form of, and use their skin as ingredient in making of the mask. The form created by this power typically possesses the same height, build, ethnicity, and gender of the victim, but otherwise the power can alter his appearance significantly. The Jiang Shi may choose to make the form particularly alluring, unassuming, or monsterous, granting a +3 bonus to Persuasion and Socialize (for the alluring), Stealth and Subterfuge (for the unassuming) or Intimidation and Brawl (for the monsterous). This power lasts for a month, though the Jiang Shi can end it as an instant action or extend it for another month with a recasting of this Art.

    Devotion

    Conjuring the Sidamen
    (Tempest God Body ●● Animalism ●)
    A Jiang Shi with this power has the ability to send out a call not a vocal call, but a sort of mental compulsion that summons to him a specific type of animal. The character may specify a desired species (tigers, birds-of-paradise, rhinos) or a more specific subject (Siberian tigers, alpha-male wolves). The animals summoned only come if they are within a two mile radius of the Jiang Shi location when he makes the call and if they are physically able to do so. This power does not grant control over animals, but those summoned by the call generally seem predisposed toward the Jiang Shi, and may fight for him if he appears to be in trouble. The animal that respond can spend Willpower of their own to be able to interact with ephemeral being for one turn for every Willpower spent.​
    Cost: 1 Vitae and 2 Essence
    Dice Pool: Presence + Animal Ken + Tempest God Body
    Action: Instant​
    Duration: 1 Scene
    Roll Result
    Dramatic Failure:     No one answer you call.
    Failure: A single animal show up.
    Success: Half of the animals respond.
    Exceptional Success: All the animals responds, and you gain +3 to Animal Ken when trying to command them.

    This Devotion cost 3 Experience.

    Unleash the Beast Spirit
    (Tempest God Body ●●● Animalism ●●)
    This power creates such a great connection between the
    Jiang Shi and his own Beast, the Beast manifests itself in physical form. The Jiang Shi undergoes changes similar to those experienced by shapeshifters, becoming a sort of half-man, half-animal, with appropriate abilities.
    Cost: 2 Vitae, 2 Essence, and 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: None
    ​Action: 3 Turn
    Duration: 1 Scene
    Effect: The player must choose a "totem animal" which represent his Beast, for his character as soon as the character is able to use this power; the character's totem can never be changed. The Jiang Shi then changes into the form described above, which is similar to the wolf-man form of shapeshifter. The character receives 6 dots that he can use to increase his Attributes, as appropriate, to reflect this form. Additionally, his senses sharpen to reflect his animalistic nature, and at Storyteller's discretion he may gain other abilities based on his new form - for example, a Turtle totem might gain points of armor, a Shark totem improved swimming abilities and sharp teeth, and a Tiger totem claws.

    This Devotion cost 1 Experience.

    The Hampering Smoke of Baigujing
    (Tempest God Body ●● Obfuscate ●)
    The Jiang Shi can cast smoke into the eye, temporary blinding people.
    Cost: 1 Vitae, 2 Essence
    Dice Pool: Dexterity + Occult + Tempest Body
    ​Action: Instant
    Duration: 1 Scene
    Effect: The victim, who must be within 10 yards of the Jiang Shi when the power is used, will gain the (Blind) condition for one turn for every success achieved. And the victim suffer -3 to roll with Composure and Dexterity.

    This Devotion cost 3 Experience.

    Storm Fist
    (Tempest God Body ●●● Obfuscate ●●)
    The hand of the Jiang Shi turn blue and spark with energy.
    Cost: 2 Vitae, 3 Essence
    Dice Pool: None
    ​Action: Instant
    Duration: 1 Scene
    Effect: One of the Jiang Shi hands glow bright blue as if it were made out of lighting. Fleshy object and creatur burst into flame when the hand touch them: consider this touch to be a Inferno fire with the intensity of +3. If they strikes or punches another character with their burning hand, they does their normal damage for that attack, and +3 dice of damage, all of which are considered to be aggravated.​​​ If a creature die of this power, their ash can be use as The Hampering Smoke of Baigujing.

    This Devotion cost 1 Experience.

    Psychic Vampire
    Most Jiang Shi get Embrace by the Second Breath ritual, there are those whose Embrace are from no one. When a Psychic Vampire die, they usually stay dead, but if the Psychic Vampire heart is still intact, their Integrity is above 1, and Ephemera is still in their system, they can potentially come back as a Jiang Shi. To do this they must spend 1 Ephemera and roll Intelligence + Manipulation + Supernatural Resistance, if they get a Success they become a Jiang Shi, they convert all of their remaning Ephemera into Essence and start out with 6 Humanity, and Blood Potency of 1 + Vampiric Potency, but if they fail, they can try again. The attempt they can try is equal to their Willpower, or when their Ephemera run out, they also don't suffer Ephemera bleed when they're dead, their Willpower also determine when they will rise and become a Jiang Shi.​

      Overall, I don't see the need to introduce essence as a secondary resource pool for Jiang Shi - after all, vampires usually drink blood to get to life essence to convert that to Vitae, so Jiang Shi could do the same with breath.

      The discipline itself could be something more akin to a "physical" disciplines, it's passive effect granting you the ability to inflict WP damage to gain Vitae (and then deal damage without having to bite to drain the person), while it's actives could enable the vampire to jump/float, and maybe even "dodge" attacks by becoming insubstantial for a turn (Up to dots of the discipline / scene ),

      This would also maintain them having at least 1 physical like discipline in their loadout

      and then weather control could go into the sorcery itself.

                                                          Some questions and thoughts on this:

                                                          1 - How you explain Essence here, or their need for it, is immaterial. What’s the purpose of this added layer of complexity for the game design?

                                                          2 - For whatever it matters, “typical” Jiang-Shi feed only on qi, not on qi AND blood. And qi isn’t Essence. Essence in CoD is a specific substance linked to the Shadow and its Spirit denizens. Qi is an energy/substance related to breathing and fuels normal life, it has zero to do with spirit.

                                                          3 - As comic as it seems, their rigidity, not their breath-feeding, is their defining feature in both folklore and pop culture. They may be agile, even supernaturally so, but never supple, they’re stiff as dead matter, or even as if in rigor mortis.

                                                          4 - Jiang-shi that don’t “eat” qi, which isn’t uncommon, eat meat. Again, not blood.

                                                          5 - Tying their basic survival to a Discipline is a big problem. Disciplines are optional powers, and ones that anyone can theoretically learn on top of that. If you want to keep them needing a qi-like resource, being able to feed should come in their basic package.

                                                          6 - If you want to keep it as a second resource, get rid of the need, it should only be used to fuel the Discipline that creates it in the first place, or as an optional alternative to uses of Vitae. And if you want to tie it to some stat, tie it to the Discipline level.

                                                          7 - Wolves don’t have “Alphas”. Drop it, that’s an old and very dangerous misconception that’s used to justify a lot of harmful behavior in real life.
