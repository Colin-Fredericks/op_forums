                                        03-07-2023, 08:01 PM
                                        You roll Humanity and get to act for a number of turns equal to your Humanity. During those turns, you're presumably performing actions to get whatever it is done that you needed to wake up for, like fending off an attacker or chasing away an intruder. Count up the total of all your successes across those rolls, the rolls to fend off or to chase away or whatever. If that number exceeds Blood Potency + 5 (not Humanity + 5, more Humanity does not make it harder to stay awake) before you fall asleep again, you get to stay up indefinitely.

                                        Gargoyle's Vigilance doesn't say anything about enhancing your ability to rise from daysleep, so I think you would still need to be in direct danger to rise. The utility of activating it in daysleep is that you know what's going on while you slumber, you're sleeping with your eyes open and can remember what anybody who snuck in did around the place.

                                        The benefits to moving against an intruder apply if you use Gargoyle's Vigilance during the night, or if the intruder threatens you enough to wake you from the daysleep while you have Gargoyle's Vigilance up.

                                            Reasonably certain the "falls asleep without another Humanity roll" means the successes are being counted across the successive unfailed Humanity rolls to stay awake that you're making every [Humanity] turns rather than successes at actions taken during those turns — vampires with low Humanity wake up for longer by sleeping in well-defended Havens they have an affinity for, not by being more capable of ending an intruder quickly.
                                            ​
                                            You roll Humanity(+Haven), get Humanity turns of wakefulness, roll Humanity again after those turns expire to get another Humanity turns of wakefulness, repeat until you fail a roll/accumulate BP+5 successes/don't need to be awake anymore. The vast majority of social vampires having Humanity higher than 2 means most characters have at least a 2-in-3 chance of succeeding each roll outside a Haven, but particularly potent and callous vampires still have more chances to fail before they hit their heightened threshold, so they're incentivized further to prevent that from needing to be done by beefing up their security.

                                                            Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                                                            What's the difference between using Shatter the Shroud - which can end the hiding effect of an obfuscated kindred - and the normal lash out action in case you are able to perceive the obfuscated vampire through Auspex or any other mean? It literally states that if you win the lash out, Obfuscate 3 - as any other obfuscate effect active - shatters.
                                                            The main difference is that Shatter the Shroud works on other supernatural stealth ability that do not have Cloak of Night's particular fragility, and that Vitae is easier to come by than Willpower.

                                                            Winning a Clash of Wills against any of the other Obfuscate powers or supernatural powers of stealth and deception that aren't Obfuscate 3 are subject to the normal stipulation for Clashes of Wills, that being that the losing power isn't cancelled unless only one power could possibly remain — being able to see a hidden character doesn't make everyone else able to see them except in the specific case of winning a Predatory Aura contest against a vampire you can perceive who is using Cloak of Night, and that's subject to the rules and modifiers for lashing out, not Clashes. The Devotion lets you just spend two Vitae after a successful Clash without having to spend Willpower and contend with a resisting character who's hungry or acting on their own Feeding Grounds.

                                                            tl;dr Shatter the Shroud works on things that aren't vampires specifically turned physically invisible and doesn't require you to also spend Willpower on a Predatory Aura contest that you have to win as well.
