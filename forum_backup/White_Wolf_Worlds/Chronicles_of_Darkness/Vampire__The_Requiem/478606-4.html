            Originally posted by saibot View Post
            So here is a question: Is there a particular reason why there are no Theban Sorcery Styles, like the Crúac Styles?

            Now, I realise Theban Sorcery by its nature is much more uniform than Crúac, but I think one could still make interesting distinctions, from quiet, dignified prayer to bombastic spectacle that would make a Pentecostal snake handler proud.
            Mostly I ran out of space. I worked with Filamena when she was writing the Circle of the Crone section, and she asked me if I could do X, Y, and Z. That sounded a lot like what the Styles ended up looking like. That didn't really occur when I was writing the Lance stuff. I also rather like most of the Lance stuff, particularly some of the Theban. It rolled along rather well. So, I went with the momentum when writing.
