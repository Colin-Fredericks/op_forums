        In my experience, Uncanny Perception is functionally almost identical to Aura Perception, it's just more transparent about how it works. I don't know about anyone else, but I could never learn all of those aura colors and what they meant, and I didn't expect my players to memorize them, so we always just had the Auspex user just ask questions anyway. "What emotion is this character feeling most strongly right now?" or "does this character's aura have Diablerie stains?" or what have you. Second Edition just decided to quit beating around the bush and just tell the player to to ask questions and the storyteller to answer in symbolic imagery. Honestly, when I read 2E Auspex, I saw the exact same list of powers, just written in a less obtuse way. The only thing that really changed in a significant way was the first dot, all the other changes were just for ease of use and clarity of mechanical function.

                                                Maybe superficially. Auspex 2 does a lot more than just read emotions. Psychological vulnerabilities, vice/dirge, "who here does this person want to hurt the most"... these things couldn't be done with the old Auspex. And that's just from the sample questions. Going by the power's description you can make a lot of other interesting questions as well. "Is this person lying to me?" for instance. With aura reading you could maybe see someone get nervous, but not everyone gets nervous while telling a lie, and a lot of other people get nervous just by being asked weird questions, even when they're being truthful. It doesn't matter what they feel like now, though.

                                                No, I don't think calling it functionally identical to Aura Perception is fair at all.

                                                Apart from those first two, the other 3 are pretty similar to what we had before, yeah, but that's alright. They didn't change in theme much, but I do think they got better. Also, some Obfuscate changes are pretty neat coupled with Auspex. Familiar Stranger (now much improved) coupled with Lay Open The Mind makes you a perfect look alike while also allowing you to know exactly what the other person is expecting you to say on tricky questions. There's almost no chance of being found out at that point. Sure, you could just brute force dominate your way in a lot of places, but that leaves a trace. (And every power constantly runs into "Well, you could do this with Dominate!" anyway.)

                                                I like how we can go on a year long time skip, and I can hand the ST a list with questions I ask about every important NPC I meet regularly in that year, and get back an entire sheet with all of their masks, dirges, psychological vulnerabilities, greatest aspirations, etc... It's come in handy several times. Dominate could get the same secrets and more, but then the entire domain would likely be at war with me by the end of that year. You always fuck up somewhere, and dominate on that scale...

                                                And for those who say that you can just notice a Mekhet staring at you intently... "Other vampires have no way to know when someone is using Auspex to discover their secrets." Plus it says you use the Beast's senses. It never says this sense has to be vision.
