        So - here is what I am leaning towards....it's a rough outline...

        Use Chronicles of Darkness Character Creation with the following changes:

        1. Concept: decide on concept and select three Aspirations for your character
        2. Achors- Select a virtue and vice
        3. Attributes 5/4/3
        4. Skills 11/7/4
        5. Choose 3 skill specialties
        6. 10 dots of merits
        7. Calculate advantages - use Integrity

        Add Dhampir template

        1. Add both bonus dots from your parent clans favored attributes
        2. BP starts 1 - can spend 5 merit points to increase it by 1. Max BP 5 without diablrie
        3. They are susceptible to Frenzy with a more focus on hunger
        4. 3 dots for disciplines. All physical disciplines are considered in clan.
        5. Vitae regenerates 1 PT a week
        6. Do not have fangs/kiss without spending a Vitae
        7. All mundane damage considered bashing, can spend vitae to heal and all other normal vamperic uses
        8 need a list of special merits like stake combat, poisoned vitae, resistant to bnlood bonds, etc. This is going to be the harder part.
