                      Swarms can only damage those caught inside it’s radius. Instead of standing still like an idiot and allowing yourself to be swarmed, use your movement to move beyond its radius. Any stragglers and hanger ons that are moved beyond the swarm radius immediately shrivel and die, reducing the swarm size accordingly.

Tossing a Solo cup of beer or glass of wine and dropping a match should be more than sufficient to destroy a large chunk of the swarm or kill it. Alcohol is quick burning and likely to be consumed before potentially igniting anything else. Ditto for igniting a napkin or handkerchief and tossing it at the swarm.

                      By the way Swarm Form and Claws of the Unholy are both merits, so no they are not stackable.

                                                  Swarm form is quite powerful but remember it DOES cost 5xp for both the Discipline and the Merit so he could almost get another level of Discipline instead.

                                                  That being said, you as the ST do have one powerful tool at your disposal: It is an animal form, and when a Vampire uses Protean*** pg140 to transform, they take on the statistics of that animal(s). There are suggestions in a couple places for swarm stats but ultimately you as the ST get to stat it out. Strength, Stamina/HP, Speed, and Brawl will be the major ones to tweak the power level. Low Stamina/HP and he's still tough, probably tougher than normal form, but low HP will make him more vulnerable while low Str & Brawl will limit his offensive power and the only way he can raise these is by investing in physical Disciplines. Low speed means maybe he can take on anyone who chooses to try and fight but he doesn't get a vote on when or where the fight occurs, nor can he escape if things go sideways.

                                                  I don't know why Merits wouldn't stack because I don't recall seeing that as a thing anywhere so as for Claws of the Unholy (which requires Protean FOUR), you could build in consequences to such a reckless action as purposely flying into a frenzy. Perhaps the targets takes a bystander, loved one, or coterie member as a human shield or just tosses them in front of himself and the swarm rips them to pieces to get through. Perhaps the target flees out into public and the swarm causes a Masquerade breach. Perhaps the target realizes that he's frenzied and lures the swarm into a hazard that ignores it's resilience to damage such as exposed electrical wires or something.

                                                  Also things that damage Willpower or drain Vitae are unaffected by the resilience of the swam, so that's another avenue of attack.

                                                  If bats are his thing then something as simple as a stout bedsheet can completely ruin his career. Perhaps a loud TV or radio in the room could screw up echolocation or even cause damage to sensitive ears. Lure the swarm into another room and slam the door on the other half of the swarm, and if he continues to follow, then half the creatures will automatically die off (and lose half his HP?) when they get out of range from the rest.

                                                  Also the thing with Gangrel is that eventually they can become the Unstoppable Force and the Immovable Object so you could engineer constant low-grade threats to other things to which he is attached. If he goes the combat wombat route then he can only be in one place at a time and the harder he commits, the less he can protect something else like Haven(s), Touchestone(s), Contacts, Allies, etc. If he's pissed off an Elder then I would expect the Elder to go that route anyway and avoid personal combat.

                                                  Just remember that it costs minimum of 11 xp for Protean 3 + Swarm form, and 15 xp for P4 + Claws of the Unholy so It should be powerful but hopefully there's something here that can stop it from breaking your game.
