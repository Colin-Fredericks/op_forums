        Originally posted by falco1029 View Post

        Remember that it's not that simple to just take the body part/power away from another supernatural. For one, they tend to have societies that frown at their members being dissected and/or killed. For another, you need to know what they are and what they're capable of ahead of time, and then need a way to capture them without them in any of that time realizing you're a threat (or again, society). And it's likely a Humanity breaking point if the individual is in any way human. So it's hardly 'too tempting', you need to be a very specific sort of.... dedicated, to be able to make good use of it.
        That's a terrible justification. Just saying.

        You know of a Kindred who has some kind of interesting Bloodline Power? Like, say, a Khaibit?

        Buy an eye from them; they can grow it back trivially next time they go through Daysleep (especially if you give them the Vitae to do so), you can shove it in your head, and boom, you've got Udjat.

        Heck, there are probably tons of Ordo initiates that would trade a hand (Vigor), some skin (Resilience), an eye (Dominate or Auspex), or a tongue (Majesty) in exchange for some Herd access and some training in the Coils.

        Along those lines, set up a meat market with other members of the Ordo; trade a face from a master of the Coil of the Ascendant (to grab The Warm Face) for a hand from some initiate (Vigor); give yourself the face (to grab 24 hour Blush of Life), graft some Vigor dots onto the master, and give the initiate some training or a leg up; just wash everything down with some Vitae and call it good.

        It's a shortcut; it's not even a particularly difficult shortcut, once you notice that the other guy doesn't need to be dead at the end of it, and that you have a society full of Supernaturals walking around with the ability to trivially regrow limbs. I mean, sure, you're going to have a rocky start, getting Kindred to sell or trade body parts. But once they figure out that, hey, there's a pretty good trade going on there, with minimal risk to themselves...

        I mean, imagine someone came up to you on the street and offered you $1000 dollars for a lock of your hair. With no catch.

        Similar arguments can be made for other Supernaturals (like Werewolves) that can trivially regrow body parts; I mean, offer some of your social clout (or whatever other resources you can muster) to a Werewolf's pack in exchange for whatever non-vital organ you've decided is the root of a Werewolf's regeneration. Or their shapeshifting. Or one of their more "physical" Gifts.

        Or let it be known (discretely) that you're in the market for well-preserved Werewolf parts. Lord only knows its not the first time an Ordo member's done that.

        As for Supernatural Merits being weaker than Auspex... yes and no.

        Yes, Auspex covers most of thenon-combat Supernatural merits... Except for stuff like Omen Sensitivity (which is far broader than any of Auspex's dots), Unseen Senses (Strix) (I'm pretty sure Auspex can't auto-find Strix), City Walking (Auspex doesn't let you teleport between cities! Granted, I don't think any Discipline does), Clear Sighted (again, one Willpower and one Vitae to be immune to two Disciplines for a Scene), or Medium (Ghosts aren't something normal Kindred powers deal with. And Ghosts are useful).

        Of the combat ones, the standouts are Numbing Touch (damage Willpower as part of an Unarmed Attack? Which gives a pretty big penalty to all dice pools for a few turns? Pretty. Darn. Good), Telekinesis (redirect all ranged attacks for a turn by spending one Vitae and one Willpower? Increase "Strength" for 1xp instead of 4xp?), and Psychokinesis (barring cheesy ones like fire or sunlight, stuff like Psychokinesis (Blood) or Psychokinesis (Electricity) are pretty useful.)

        I have decided, after some thought, that I don't really feel happy on these forums. I might decide to come back to post. Who knows - but right now, I'm gone.

        So good bye, good luck, and have a nice day.

                                Thinking of some interesting psychokinesis uses...

                                Metal
                                - Shrink / Warp weapons to the point of unsuitability.
                                - Make guns point and fire, magneto style.
                                - Large scale structural damage.
                                - Building / fortification

                                Blood
                                - Moving and storing blood.
                                - Ripping blood out of wounds for feeding (Not sure if possible???)
                                - One of the more subtle ways to kill someone (spontaneous heart attack)

                                Wood/Plants
                                - Psychic Staking.
                                - Large scale structure building/destroying/shaping.
                                - Grow or destroy plants.

                                Shadow
                                - Increase size to create stuff to hide in / obscure vision.

                                Electricity
                                - Power or turn off electronic devices.


                                Xylokinesis is particularly useful. The rote, physical intensity'd action can stake most people. If Fire is off the table it's probably the best pure Psychic Combat option.
