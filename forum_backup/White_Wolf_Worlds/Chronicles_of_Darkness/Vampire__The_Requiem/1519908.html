    Sibyllam - a rework of Dementation for Requiem

    08-05-2023, 08:44 PM

    I love Malkavians, but I never liked their unique Discipline, Dementation. It sort of makes sense for the Malkavian atitribu, since it basically amounts to making people insane just for the fun of it. But it doesn't capture the spirit of the Malkavian clan as a whole, which is their fractured viewpoint. They see the world differently, which makes others call them "mad," but their madness has an internal logic and can produce truths. Here's my attempt at creating them a unique Discipline in Requiem, somewhat based on the Dementation rework found in the 1e Ventrue book. Please tell me what you think - I believe I'm happy with the "vibe" of each power, I just don't know how well I translated them into game terms!

    Sibyllam

    Fractured Mind •

    The Malkavian’s mind is alien, slippery and untouchable, moving in ways the sane can’t comprehend. Users of Dominate and other mental Disciplines have trouble getting hold of them. Additionally, they can focus their warped insight to see things in a way that others can’t.

    Cost: None, or 1 Vitae

    Dice Pool: None

    The Malkavian adds their Sibyllam rating to all rolls to resist Dominate, Majesty, and other mental Disciplines and compulsions - excluding compulsions resulting from their own derangements. This effect is passive.

    In addition, by spending a point of Vitae, the Malkavian can add their Sibyllam rating to any non-physical perception roll for the duration of the scene. For example, a Wits + Composure roll to detect things in their surroundings, or a Wits + Survival roll to track a beast, wouldn’t be eligible (Malkavians can use Auspex for that), but a Wits + Empathy roll to learn about someone’s state of mind, or a Wits + Occult roll to sense spirits nearby, can be augmented by the Malkavian’s bizarre insights.

    Sympathetic Link ••

    Malkavians see the world in a new way. Others call them “mad,” but they know that their perspective - though erratic, uncomfortable and frightening - is a blessing, because it reveals truth. Many Malkavians want to share that truth with others.

    With this power, a Malkavian can project their heightened emotions - fears, insights, and derangements - to others nearby.

    Cost: None, or 1 Vitae

    Dice Pool: Wits + Empathy + Sibyllam

    Botch: The Malkavian creates a feedback loop with their own mind, boosting their own emotions to unbearable intensity. They instantly roll Willpower to avoid frenzy.

    Success: The Malkavian is able to project their emotion onto the target. This can take several forms:
    • Fear: The target must instantly roll Willpower to avoid Rotschreck, with a target number equal to the number of successes the Malkavian scored.
    • Fury: Similar to the above, but with frenzy instead of Rotschreck.
    • Awe: The target gains the Enthralled Condition.
    • Despair: The target takes a penalty equal to the successes scored, except on Willpower rolls to resist this condition.
    • Elation: The target gains a bonus equal to the successes scored for a number of rolls equal to the Malkavian’s Blood Potency.
    The Malkavian can establish this sympathetic link with multiple people by taking a penalty on the roll equal to the number of targets affected beyond one.

    By spending one Vitae, the Malkavian can use this power reflexively whenever they make a roll due to high emotions - a frenzy roll, a Rotschreck roll, a derangement roll, etc. For every person successfully added to their sympathetic link, the Malkavian gets a +2 to their roll.

    Mad Insight •••

    The power of the Malkavian’s blood tears down preconceptions and assumptions. The mind becomes receptive to extrasensory inputs, and finds the Blood channeling barely incomprehensible signals in the way that dental fillings pick up transmissions. No small part of this telemetry is hallucinatory bullshit - static and nonsense generated by the Malkavian’s cursed blood - but the Malkavian can pick carefully through the sensations to find details that are useful.

    Cost: 1 Vitae, possibly 1 Willpower

    Dice Pool: Wits + Occult + Sibyllam

    Botch: The Malkavian can do little more than scream, weep, and spend a Willpower point to push the visions away.

    Failure: The Malkavian is bombarded with insane, paranoid delusions. They must make an Intelligence + Composure roll to conclude the vision can’t be trusted - otherwise, they will trust the madness.

    Success: The Malkavian has two choices - they can either gain momentary, fleeting insights into things about the world they never understood, or they can attempt to peer beyond the veil of the natural world and gain a supernatural vision.

    The first effect lets the Malkavian suddenly seem like an expert in things they’ve never even attempted - not only do they ignore untrained penalties for all skills, but gain a bonus to them equal to the number of successes rolled. This effect lasts for a scene.

    The second effect is quite open-ended. The storyteller grants the Malkavian a supernatural vision containing valuable information about their surroundings or even the future. This vision could consist of anything - visions of the future, seeing into the spirit world, spontaneously learning some obscure piece of lore, etc. Using this effect costs 1 Willpower as well.

    Exceptional Success: The Malkavian gains a point of Willpower.

    Prank ••••

    Malkavians are infamous for what they term “pranks” - a term that implies something far more harmless than what the victim of a Malkavian prank actually suffers. This power lets the Malkavian plague a victim with a lingering madness that starts as creeping doubts in the world around them and progresses to total lack of stability. Malkavians often “favor” their victims with such a prank if they believe it will teach them some lesson about the world. Often, though, what the Malkavian intended with the prank remains obscure.

    Cost: 1 or more Vitae

    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Subterfuge + Sibyllam vs. Composure + Blood Potency

    System: This is an extended dice roll, the target number of successes being equal to the target’s Willpower. Each roll represents ten minutes of watching and studying the target, projecting a malevolent will. The Malkavian can choose to take a -2 penalty to force the victim to roll every day instead of every two days, or a -4 to make it twelve hours.

    Botch: The Malkavian suffers -2 to Composure for the rest of the night, and is unable to use this power for the rest of the scene.

    Success: Once the target number of successes is reached, the Malkavian spends one Vitae to begin the victim’s suffering. Within a few minutes, the target’s derangement is triggered, if they have one. If they don’t, they develop one when they next wake up.

    Beginning when the victim next wakes, they begin a reflexive extended action to overcome the effects of Prank. They roll Intelligence + Resolve versus a target of the Malkavian’s Manipulation + Subterfuge. The Malkavian can raise this target number by +2 for each additional Vitae she spends, up to a maximum target number of 20. Every roll the victim makes, whether a success or failure, reduces their Composure by one dot. Once that is exhausted, their Resolve is next. This Attribute damage is healed as if it were lethal damage, with one point recovered every two days. Once the victim completes the extended action, the derangement is gone.

    Malkavian Time •••••

    To outsiders, Malkavians have an uncanny knack for showing up in the right place at the right time. Or, in some cases, the wrong place at the right time. Using their mysterious insight, Malkavians adopt a random new routine - it can be as simple as exploring streets of the city they’ve never been down, or as unexpected as doing nothing but learning pottery for several nights - and find invariably that this act has led them one step closer to accomplishing their goals.

    Cost: 2 Willpower

    Dice Pool: Wits + Occult + Sibyllam

    System: The Malkavian’s player selects one of their Aspirations that they’d like to make progress on. The Storyteller applies a rating to that Aspiration from 1 to 3 based on its scope - finding a new haven would be 1, learning a new Discipline would be 2, and overthrowing the Prince would be 3. The Malkavian then begins an extended roll, with a penalty equal to the Aspiration’s rating, and with a target number of three times the Aspiration’s rating. Each roll represents one night of significantly engaging in their new routine. Once the target number is reached, the Malkavian finds that this routine pays off - the Malkavian exploring city streets discovers a perfect new haven, the Malkavian learning pottery finds they’ve unconsciously shaped the face of the person who knows the Prince’s most damaging secrets. This never accomplishes the Aspiration by itself - at the very least, another roll is required. For larger-scope Aspirations, this may represent only a minor step towards its resolution, at the Storyteller’s discretion.

      It's so weird this post didn't show up until more than 12 hours after it was posted. But it's here now so here we go.

      Right off the bat I absolutely disagree with your thread title. You are not "updating dementation" you are creating a brand new discipline that does what you believe the Malkavian clan did. Giving your thread a false title creates different expectations.

      That out of the way, I'm not a huge fan over all. I think you have made it overly strong.

      Let's look at your first level. It has a permanent effect and a spend viate effect and it scales with dots in the discipline, just like the physical disciplines. I can see why you wanted to do that and why you would think it's balanced. It isn't though, the physical disciplines ONLY do that, this discipline does that AND gains powers every level. That is too much.

      Your ssecond dot in this discipline does way, way too much. You can cause vampires to frenzy (an incredibly powerful ability), fear frenzy, resist your frenzy, and give people a variety of conditions. That's almost a full discipline worth of abilities in a single power, and it's only second level.

      I stopped reading here. I think you need to go back to the drawing board, re-examine what you want this discipline to do and how powerful it should be and rework it from the ground up if you still want to keep working on it.

      That said I don't think this discipline really needs to be a thing. In False Gods: Ventrue the Malkovians were updated to 2nd edition and I think that was done very well and it covers your goal of making them be able to see into the truths of the world without being broken. (Also, side note, I LOVE Malkavia the disease and the bonus discipline for the disease, wonderful work there.)

                  Hmm. Could this work as a set of Devotions instead? Some Devotions require dots in just one Discipline to learn, and I think Sybillam can be tweaked to be a set of Auspex Devotions.

                  Fractured Mind grants a psychic defense buffer and a Perception boost. The latter effect especially translates well to Beast’s Hackles (Auspex •), which is about augmenting the Beast’s inherent survival senses.

                  Sympathetic Link projects emotions. The part about projecting a shard of your psyche translates well to Twilight Projection (Auspex •••••).

                  Mad Insight reveals an alternate reality.​ It’s the Oracle’s insight jumping beyond mundane reality and into the hidden worlds, so it works well as an augment to Uncanny Perception (Auspex ••).

                  Prank inflicts a ‘derangement’, which would be a Mental Condition in 2e terms.​ Looks like a much more invasive form of Lay Open the Mind (Auspex ••••) to me.

                  Malkavian Time… aids an Aspiration.​ To match what the rest of the Discipline is about, this could be about ‘seeing’ (read; forcing) a future happening. (It can still be about Aspirations; Aspirations can include what the player wants to happen out-of-character.) This affects the future instead of minds, making it a latetal application of the Discipline. And looking at the other 2e Disciplines, that’s what the 3rd dot power usually does. So, a variant of The Spirit’s Touch (Auspex •••).

                  Actual mechanics would need a bit more thinking, though!
