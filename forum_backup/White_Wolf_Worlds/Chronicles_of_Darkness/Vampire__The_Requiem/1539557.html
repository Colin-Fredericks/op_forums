          It depends. BP 10 doesn't necessarily implies a HUGE personal power. By raw, you gain 1 BP every 50 years, which means that, living a "normal" life, you can reach that power after 450 years. It's a lot of time and surely a lot of higher stats, but not even a Methuselah. More over, BP can be raised by spending XP (AKA being active in your requiem, or by diablerie) this might speed up the BP raise further.

          So, BP10 it's for sure scaring whatever number of attributes/skills and discipline you have as raw power, but it's not everything.

          That being said, I very rarely introduce in my game kindred above BP 7, but as legend, sleeping boogieman, etc. Those almost almighty creature should appear very rarely to not make them ordinary (this is not a superhero game and them would pretty much match a superhero level of power.

          If you ask what they are capable of, this again depend on how ancient they are. 450 years old BP10 vampire is scaring but it's nothing compared to a 1000-2000 years old one that would probably have all discipline at 5 plus very unique and powerful Devotions. Devotions are what make the real difference after a certain point. So I'd say, BP10 Methuselah (or also lower BP Methuselah) are pretty much capable of anything comes into your mind. Just try to keep the mood aligned with an horror game (I hate the potence 5 earthquake from Masquerade V5 in fact).

              Yeah, basically the question is which Devotions they have.

              Any splat at 10 in their Power Stat is potentially god-like. If they reached it organically, you can expect them to be virtually unstoppable monsters that can level a city if push comes to shove, and they are probably influential and legendary the world around.

              But that's just potential. A character that reached this level through Diablerie and spending XP solely on BP isn't really that powerful. They can burn through Vitae for some impressive feats, but after a few rounds they're done.

              Vampires receive two benefits from such a high BP: a lot of Vitae and speed to spend it, and access to Devotions that either benefit from this speed or have high BP as a requirement.

              The first benefit is easy to picture: imagine a vampire able to activate every Discipline they have plus healing any and all damage every round. The second is harder, but a Devotion that requires 20 Vitae to activate, or have BP 10 as prerequisite, should be truly monumental.

                              As far as sheer power goes, such a Neonate wouldn't be all that potent, mainly because they can't use their whole arsenal at once.

                              But that would be a hell of a versatile vampire, which means they're probably equipped to deal with most of their problems and live longer than most of their rivals. At least as long as they keep a low profile.

                              In general it's good to have a mixed approach. Diversifying your power set is more immediately useful, but after a while it has diminishing returns. At the end of the day, you'll need to be able to punch hard in someway if you want to be a serious player (so to speak), and that means high level on your main disciplines at the very least, but also increase BP when you're comfortable with your basic power set.
