                                Generally speaking, I would predict that people who would do this woulf fall in at least one of three camps...
                                A. People who only care aboit the mechanics. In this case branding trans as a positive mechanical effect in their case is the most useful thing you could hope for.
                                B. They are ignorant of the trans experience. In this case. In this case, unless they are unusually receptive to instruction through play, normalizing the experience is hands down the best way to change the minds at the table. Were I an ST, I might try to give juuuuuuust enough tension that they were in the habit of forcing others to accept their decision to be normal, equal, and not something to persecute. That habit will likey, subconciously transfer to their behavior outside of the game.
                                C. They are well aware of the trans experience and as such they should really be allowed to present or ignore that however they damn well please.

                                Personally, again, not the criteria I would use... but hey. That's the pro unity logic.

                                Also, it's probably better to not make assumptions about someone's life or assume that everyone has the same (or even similar) experiences / response / etc. things as you. It almost always does more harm than good, and there are kinder and more constructive ways to present your dissent.

                                Saying that my argument is "speaking like someone [who isn't part of group X]" gives the impression that disagreeing with me (and by extension implicitly agreeing with you) is a necessary part of being X. There are two issues with this.

                                First, it is basically the argument that you are correct because of consensus and solidarity. I can go on for ages why that is a terrifying thing in the best of times, but right now Ill emphasize how it shuts down discussion. It can be summed up as "come at me bro, also my peeps are behind me" (they may or may not be behind you) which also gives a hostile tone to the conversation. You have no arguments to really respond to, except the assertion that for some reason I can't speak on a subject because I'm a terrible person, or otherwise disqualified from being able to voice a valid opinion. Silencing someone or spreading the idea that their voice has no value is... well lets say it invites an unpleasant response.

                                Second, and most importantly, it is exclusionary to a degree I don't think you are going for. One time won't make a difference, but over time that sort of thing being a habit pushes all groups into the more extreme spectrum of their thinking by victimizing their own people. This because you are also implicitly saying that anyone *else* who disagrees with you or agrees with me isn't a part of your group. While this galvenizes the core people who do disagree with you, it pushes others away and makes them less likely to seek fellowship / assistance. If they have a strong sense of identity wrapped up in being a female identifying person presenting as typically male but they also don't have a strong relevant support network this can, over time, be catestrophic. I do emphasize repeated instances over time, such that it becomes part of the percieved culture.

                                So, in short, I am speaking like SoulGambit, a person you do not know with life experiences you are unfamiliar with. While you may have felt justified in presenting your dissent in that manner I respectfully request that you find alternative methods in the future because it has a negative impact on the discussion, on the culture of the forums, and on the broader community we both love.

                                                        Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post



                                                        I just want to point out the irony in the fact that the folks who are arguing in favor of male characters being able to take Undead Menses don't actually want a menstruation power.

                                                        Guys.

                                                        If you want a power that lets you produce blood that grants 8-again on cruac rolls but don't want to have to play as girl or think about icky biological functions women have to deal with, just re-name the Merit "Ritual Bleeder" or something and remove the "must identify as female" requirement.
                                                        To be fair, I see ample thematic ground, in the occult source material Undead Menses channels to represent the mystical power of the feminine archetype and its fertility, to justify the existence of a similar Merit, reserved to male characters (see below about what this entails), that represents the mystical power of the masculine archetype and its fertility. In this context, the closest, most natural masculine equivalent of menstrual blood for the feminine would be sperm. So following this reasoning it seems the male fertility Merit would be available to characters that identify as a male AND are able to produce sperm after activating the blush of life.

                                                        Yes, this would seem to place an additional anatomical hurdle to clear for trans characters to use this merit, but this comes from the fact the universe originally made the mystically relevant female fertility substance blood, while it is sperm for males. This would be where the sex-change applications of Protean or Night Doctor Surgery we previously discussed would come useful. Frankly I find the notion of replacing sperm, or menstrual blood for that matter, with blood supposed to come out from a penis gross enough that I'm not willing to contemplate it for long or bring it at my table if I can avoid it, regardless of the additional hurdles this might impose on certain characters. Then again, if we are willing to allow the 'special fertility substance' to be replaced by Vitae that can be spilled the usual vein-cutting way, I'd be much more willing to allow (and require) the substitution. However, I'm not entirely persuaded it would be mystically appropriate, but then again, it might be fitting for vampires.

                                                        At the moment the creatively challenged me cannot think of a better name for the male version of the Merit than 'Undead Sperm'. I suppose there might well be a good name for the unified version of the Merit that covers both options, even if I cannot think of it atm. 'Ritual Bleeder' might or might not be entirely appropriate depending on the issue I mentioned before. On second thoughts, I might be willing to require blush of life to be active to use any version of this Merit for anyone, female or male, because of its mystical ties with fertility.
