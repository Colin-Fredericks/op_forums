                                          Originally posted by Ur-Than View Post
                                          What kind of event may lead to the - official - wipe out of two Covenants in a City ?
                                          That is very much of a 'whatever you need to have happened in order to work the story you and your players want to tell' question which could obviously have many valid answers. I am currently working up a City where the Lancea et Sanctum and Circle are very weak (but not absent) in the aftermath of them getting into a dispute that turned to blood in the streets when their leaders lost control of the situation. The other Covenants are expanding to fill the vacuum but the Ordo are particularly strong in part because they were the one who stepped in and put an end to the fighting.
