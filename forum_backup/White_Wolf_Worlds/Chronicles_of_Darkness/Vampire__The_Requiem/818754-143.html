                            An individual only provides as much Vitae as their unmodified Health dots. So a human with 7 Health can only provide 7 Vitae before they can't provide anymore. At this point they are at full lethal and will need medical treatment, but there is no rolling over. By RAW, it's the taking of Vitae that deals damage, not the bite itself (unless feeding violently), so you can't even over-feed.

                            A vampire can't kill by feeding non-violently by RAW, though of course I imagine most STs would allow it narratively.

                            Of course, just taking more than Stamina puts peoples' lives at serious risk via the Drained condition, and my players were loathe to take more than that typically. Odd how often people talk about draining people dry.

                                They still need to be stabilised or they'll continue to take damage. Combined with rolls to even keep consciousness a fully fed on mortal has a hard time staying alive without assistance (such as people capable of calling 112/911).
                                Also note that they still contain more blood (total exsanguination is automatically fatal (despite what a certain Scale in SotC thinks) and should be represented by full aggravated damage) meaning the vampire can overfeed and deal more than their victim's Health in damage causing rollover; they simply won't get Vitae from it. If the mortal is already wounded (even if it's just bashing from a relatively light disease) then you're also at risk of overfeeding even if you keep within their Vitae limits. For example, a mortal with 7 Health and 2 bashing gets fed on and the vampire takes all 7 Vitae they can get, the preexisting bashing will roll over to cause two aggravated damage.
                                My recommendation is to never take more than the victim's apparent Size in Vitae, if you care about not leaving corpses around. If you need to feed more then once, spread it out over several hunting rolls (which often leads to taking even less Vitae per victim). For example, if my theoretical BP 5 character has only 3 Vitae and needs 12 to fill up, I'd have her feed on three mortals, taking 4+4+4 Vitae despite two Size 5 people being able to provide at least 12 Vitae (except if they've already been fed on, of course).

It makes no sense. Also, I think the line "the Dragon bleeds a subject of all their blood or Vitae." means the blood must actually be lost instead of just filtered. I don't think an unmodified dialysis machine is capable of removing "the weight of their sin" from the subject's blood before reintroducing it to their body. For some reason the effect of the subject is affected by the vampire's number of dots in Coil of Ziva despite the procedure not mentioning any meaningful interaction between the Dragon and the subject. It's such a shame because it's a really cool Mystery filled with awesome ideas.
