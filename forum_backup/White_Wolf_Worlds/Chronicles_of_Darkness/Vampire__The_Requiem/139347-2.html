    It's useful when you imagine it from the character's perspective: you can get up to more mundane chores, hobbies, and research during the day (reading an interesting book, painting, writing up architectural plans, writing) - and devote every night to being social and partaking in kindred society. You effectively have more time to complete more mundane activities and can devote more time toward social activities. The other benefit is your ability to invite people over during the day; this gives you a much better flexibility in allying with mortals, creating better social networks. It allows you greater flexibility and reliability when sustaining your masquerade.

    You're also capable of wrapping yourself up and paying an enemy a visit during the day.

    It might also surprise enemies who visit your haven during the day to find that you're still perfectly awake and active and able to defend yourself - the fact you can garb yourself and escape your haven if its suddenly firebombed is another positive.

    The reality is Hunters need to strike Vampires during the day to stand a chance against them, because Vampires can absolutely maul them in regard to taking damage, healing damage, using supernatural powers, and buffing themselves up with blood (on top of whatever other defences they have in the form of retainers). Attacking during daylight hours is an absolute must - because even if you overpower a Vampire during night, they're just as capable of running away without any problem (sunlight is needed to stop them from fleeing and allows you to flee if you're about to die, as well). If it's night and things simply aren't going as planned, you're far more capable of running away in the daylight to escape the Vampire. But if it's dark out and you've managed to spur a Vampire to chase you down without any contingency, you're screwed.

            Originally posted by MCN View Post
            Here's the part where I think that a Dragon might not want to Surmount the Daysleep all the time. Boredom

            Sure, you can avoid sleep. Sure, you can walk into sunlight now, in brief stretches, but it still effing HURTS. So, you don't want to do it all the time. No matter your Potency or Humanity, you don't want to be outside for an hour in daylight. You can have every Scale and Coil in the Mystery of the Ascended, and you'll still be taking damage from sunlight exposure once every 10 minutes. Great in a pinch, but not exactly something that will be every day.

            So, as the years stretch on... what do you DO during the daylight hours? Remember, ennui is a theme in Vampire. They get bored as they get old. Sure, its easy to theorycraft all the nice new hobbies you could pick up after all that time, all the books you can read, but that's really not sustainable long term. Cabin fever is still a thing with vampires.

            Which I think makes for a great thematic fit for the Ordo: Be careful what you wish for.
            It looks like an awesome improvement on paper but turns out to be just another form of the curse.

                    Well, the bio-psychological effects of sleep is pretty fascinating. Yes, it does help you lock things in long term memory. It also removes things from memory - in fact, there's some psychologists who believe that dreams are actually are brain tossing away all that unwanted information we picked up over time. That's why suppressed memories come out in dreams - its our brain trying to heal and clean up messes. Our brain builds new connections while asleep, weakens old ones, shifts around our associations, and more. Sleep is pretty dang fascinating from a biological standpoint.


                    That said, vampires are undead, so their brain functions are kinda different. Torpor does help with the memory thing - it makes long term vampires who've frozen into bad habits into someone new and fresh and open to ideas and changes. And daysleep does help with healing, like with humans. So, its entirely possible that sleep will serve a similar function in vampires. That said, vampires don't recover willpower from sleeping, so its not like they wake up refreshed, and constant low level healing from spending vitae can function as the same memory issues. Or if the memory thing can only be acomplished via torpor.
