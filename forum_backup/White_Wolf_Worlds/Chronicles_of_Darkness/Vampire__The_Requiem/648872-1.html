    Igloo

    Alice: "To the igloo?"
    Felix: "To the igloo."

    In Alice's and my second shared haven, we converted a walk-in closet into a normal sized closet hiding a dome shaped concrete bunker. The ceiling was painted with glow in the dark paint to depict the nighttime sky, and there is a comfortable mattress fitted to the space. When we constructed our new residence, we decided to incorporate this kind of sleeping space into the design. This is where we go when we need to discuss things in total privacy. We call it the igloo.

    Inside the igloo, Alice and I lamented the fact that we could not warn the other members of our coterie and family about the coming danger. We resolved to proceed as though everything was normal, and when Broom becomes Prince to oppose his rule, just as we would had we not known the origin of his coming success.


    Tea With Seville

    The next night, Alice and I arrive at Seville's office to see he is hosting an open forum with several carthians from the QZ. He asks if anyone has anything they would like to say.
    Jonathan Poole rises and talks about modern forms of communication, including telephone, email, and the bulletin boards of the nascent internet. Jonathan thinks these were better, more secure ways to communicate than sending messengers with letters than can be intercepted.

    Seville: "Alice, perhaps you would be willing to help me make a point. Jonathan, call Alice a poopy head."
    Jonathan: "What? I don't want to..."
    Seville: "Do it."
    Jonathan: "Alice... you are a poopy head."
    Seville: "Now, try it in a letter."
    Jonathan grabs a sheet of scrap paper and begins to write 'Alice, you are a poopy head.'
    Seville: "No. That's a note. Write a letter. Try this:

    "Madame Alice Fox, member of the Ordo Dracul, Bailiff to Michael Seville, Marshal to Reeve Antony,
    I hope this night finds you well. I have been asked by Alder Michael Seville, priscus of Clan Nosferatu and Carthian Prefect, to call you a 'poopy head.' Please forgive me for this insult. I do not actually think of you in this way.

    I again I hope this letter finds you well.
    Sincerely,
    Jonathan Poole"

    Seville: "And now if you would all please excuse me, I have an appointment."

    Seville leads Alice and I into his private office, where tea is served as promised. A few topics are discussed:


    1. Alice and I are worried about the meeting we just had with the Prince. Seville assures us that any plans the Prince has laid out will take years to mature. This is at once a relief in that we will have more time to prepare for the worst, and an extended sentence under the sword of Damocles.

    2. "What's this I hear about certain kindred not accepting letters unless they are delivered on pretty stationary?" Last chapter, Madeline Ross told Alice and me that due to the security breech at Elysium, certain kindred would no longer be accepting correspondence unless it arrived on watermarked stationary and sealed with a mark unique to the sender.

    Seville: " I was about grab a roll of toilet paper and start a letter writing campaign, but if it's for security..."

    3. Seville: "I have heard the two of you are trying your hands at being a groom." Seville has a candidate for the embrace and wants Alice and I to prepare him for unlife. "I want you to teach him to think like a vampire. I want you to teach him to think like you think, Alice. I want you to teach him to think like you think, Felix."

    First, Alice and I are floored. This is a sign of utmost trust. Second... what if we fuck up? We have enjoyed very much being on Seville's good side. We do not want to find our way to his bad side. Seville assures us that if it doesn't work out, he will merely make the guy into a ghoul and there will be no ill will.

    We tell Seville that we will think about it.

    A few days later one of Coopers ghouls arrived at our haven and delivered two letters, addressed to Alice. Both were written by Jonathan Poole. The first followed very closely the format proposed by Michael Seville. The second was a letter of apology for the first.

    Information from Sherry

    Well, to begin preparing for a dark future, I needed information about the latest batch of newcomers from council territory. As it happens, Sherry (daeva, our houseguest) is from council territory. So, I bring her once again across the bridge under the cloak of Obfuscate 5. We find a place to sit on the roof of the storage facility which overlooks the butcher shop and the Jiffy-Lube. I make her write a few notes about each of the vampires we see as they come and go. Here is what she wrote:

    Ghost: Dangerous. Bad. Not too powerful.
    Molly McMillan: Sam Wagner wants to drink her tears.
    Patrick: Sired in Baltimore. Sire is dead. Sire was in coterie with Mortimer.
    Sam Wagner: Wants to blood bond a bunch of vampires.
    Shaman: Wizard/Banker/Pawn Shop/Drug Dealer/Werewolf Negotiator
    Mark West: Gun Ho Revolutionary. Down with the System.
    Vadic Imamu: Wizard/Blood Magic. Goat Sacrifices.
    Trenton: Weasel. Opportunist.
    Piper Morgan: Feminist.
    Karma: Not happy about the treatment of blacks in Baltimore.
    Sunny: Involved in some kind of conspiracy.
    Marco and Polo: Smarter than they look. Deadlier than they look. Marco once killed a werewolf with his bare hands.
    Nick: Member of the Council. Snake? Poison? Wise? Enemy of my (Sherry's) sire.

    These notes aren't nessecarily accurate or in depth, but Sherry was sired in council territory. She knows more about these vampires than I do. After we returned to my haven, the notes were given to Sidney to burn. Yes, I am paranoid. Thanks for asking.


    Ghouls

    Within our household there are 5 ghouls. One belongs to Alice, and the rest belong to me:

    1. Sidney: Sidney is the first ghoul either of us ever created. Over time I have raised her to a 5 point retainer. Her main duties are to maintain our security both at home and when we are out and about. Though she is a competent fighter, I value her more for her good judgment. She often has the unenviable duty of keeping Mouse out of trouble.

    2. Mouse: Mouse is Alice's ghoul. She is insatiably curious and very easy to overlook. These traits can get her into trouble. However, it also makes her a good little spy. We have often sent her on daytime recon missions.

    3&4. Steve and Eddie: The dayshift. Unlike Sidney and Mouse, Steve and Eddie are not considered attendants. They are not permitted to accompany us to Elysium or to other vampire's havens. They are kept in the dark about the truth of many things. They are the armed guards that patrol the house while Alice and I sleep.

    5. Banjo the Wonderdog: Banjo is our most recently created ghoul. He is a border collie whom I have trained (animalism 1) to work alongside Sidney. Banjo should not be confused with an attack dog. Sure, he's a dog with a bit of resilience and he can bite and stuff, but his primary duty is to identify threats so Sidney can do her job better.

    Believe it or not, the various ghouls of the city have lives of their own that they pursue when they are not fulfilling their duties to their undead masters. In particular those labeled attendants are permitted to be present at Elysium and other social venues. A while ago we gave Mouse and Sidney permission to participate in the reindeer games when their schedules permit.

    So anyway, one night Mouse says to Alice: "Pascal is making a porno." ... and Alice sent her to investigate. Really? Well... whatever. Sometimes Mouse needs something to keep her busy.


    Piper's Problem

    Alice and I returned to the QZ in search of more tickets. After a short stroll we find three vampires sitting at a picnic table near the water:

    Shaman (nosferatu, Carthian)
    Trenton (daeva, carthian)
    Piper Morgan (daeva, Carthian)

    Shaman: "Maybe you should tell Felix about your problem."

    Piper was reluctant to do so, and I wasn't particularly interested in talking with Trenton present. We sent him away and then Piper slid a piece of paper across the table. It read: "Petitio tua negatur." (your request is denied.) Piper knew what the paper said, she had Patrick translate it. She told me that she had made a request to the reeve for permission to create a ghoul. She wanted me to smooth it over for her. Piper said she didn't have a ghoul and she needed one Well, Alice and I had committed to not abusing our position as marshals for personal gain. We felt that would show a lack of integrity. However, I did tell Piper that I would ask the Reeve why her request was denied. So, Alice and I paid a visit to Antony (ventrue, invictus, reeve, my cousin (adopted))

    1. Antony informed us that Piper already had a ghoul. "His name is Charlie and he is one of those goth kids." Furthermore, there were several vampires in the QZ who didn't have even one. "It would be unfair to give Piper two when others have none."

    2. I was a little pissed that Piper led me to believe she did not have a ghoul already and make me look stupid in front of the reeve. We went to confront Piper and after a little cajoling she told us that her ghoul, Charlie had recently killed himself. "He killed himself in the bathroom and left me a stupid note!" Piper had already destroyed the note.

    3. Well, before we went any further we had to confirm that the masquerade is upheld. Piper assured us that Charlie was a transient and unlikely to missed by anyone, and that she had made sure the body will not be found.

    Piper assured us that she was not withholding her blood from the late Charlie, and was in fact feeding him on a weekly basis, well in excess of his needs as a ghoul. However, she wasn't really letting him do anything other than sit at home and deliver her rent checks to the land lord. We tell her she is out of luck and move on.


    Bathtime

    Later that night, we had a meeting with our coterie. For those of you who may not have read the previous chapters, the other two members of Alice's and my coterie are Susan Small (ventrue, dragon, my cousin) and Christina (ventrue, invictus, my cousin.) They are both harpies and spend a lot of time in Elysium providing social cover for us while Alice and I separate the wheat from the chaff in the QZ.

    When the coterie meets to discuss strategy, we generally do it in the bathtub. The locked doors and running water prevent even Mouse from listening in. We aren't actually worried about Mouse going and blabbing our private business. However, vampires who might be reluctant to use dominate/majesty/auspex to get information out of citizens of Baltimore might not give a second thought to plucking such information from a ghoul. Better safe than sorry.

    We discussed three topics:

    1. Elysium: As discussed, Christina's Elysium is not being built at nearly the same speed as other projects in the area. I had recently used Dominate 5 to spend the day at the construction site. I wanted to know what was happening. Basically the workers are arriving a few hours late and leaving a few hours early. They leave early to earn overtime working on other projects. They arrive late because they oversleep after working late on other projects.

    As it turns out, Belinsky isn't actually sabotaging us. He is just doing favors for others; making sure the limited supply of union labor is available to work overtime in Spider's territory, and Dorris Bette's territory, and Darren Soderberg's territory.

    Well... Susan, Alice and I begin discussing who we are going to sabotage in order to free up their share of the labor (Dorris Bettes obviously.) Christina doesn't like this idea. She has built her reputation in the city by being nice and being liked (which is easy to do when you are living under your mother's skirt [Octavia.]) Christina says she will just approach Spider and ask him to delay his projects so we can use his share of the labor pool, and in exchange she will help him financially with his projects at a later date. We let Christina win this one.

    2. Christina's Inheritance: In the aftermath of Octavia's decision to leave the Invictus in favor of the Ordo Dracul, her assets were divided among the other Invictus members of the family. We wanted to know what Christina got so we could help her manage it. She got two main things:

    2a. She was given control over many of Octavia's financial assets. I don't know how much, but I think it's safe to say 'lots.' Christina has asked for the help of our cousin Rosalyn Burnett who is an expert in both security and finance to help her manage her money and determine a budget. Cool beans.

    2b. Christina has also been given domain over one of the city's best feeding grounds. There is a long and winding street which snakes through the territories of many other landholders. Most notably among them: Belinsky (nosferatu, invictus), Septimus (invictus, our uncle), Michael Seville (nosferatu priscus, carthian) and Dorris Bettes (daeva, carthian.) Dorris has been given control over a rather large chunk of Michael Seville's land. The best part of her territory borders Christina's new place in such a fashion where one side of the street is Christina's and the other belongs to Dorris. The street has many nightclubs, restaurants, art galleries, and the like. It is prime feeding territory. The territory includes only the properties that are *on* that street. It does not extend even one block over. What a nightmare it will be to defend.

    I asked Christina who decided what pieces she got. "Why, Septimus of course." Grumble. Septimus has been increasingly pissed off about the actions of many members of the family (mostly me), but nothing has angered him more than Octavia's defection to the Ordo Dracul. This action effectively ended his bid to be the next prince. It is my belief that Septimus is setting Christina up to fail. I think he wants her new territory to be invaded so he can swoop in and take it for himself.

    We cannot allow this to happen. You see Christina is a bit of a wastrel who loves to pawn her work onto others. That is of course irrelevant. She is part of our coterie. Her failures will be our failures.

    In a perfect world, Christina would subinfeudate this land and gain for herself some vassals to defend it. Alice and I made a few recommendations as to who she might choose, but we gave each other a knowing look. We will probably have to intervene. In the meantime, we will assign Aika and Aiko to feed there, and keep their eyes open while they do it. It's a bandaid at best. The good news is that for a little while at least, Octavia's various retainers and influences are still there keeping the land secure. The bad news is that they will have to withdraw sooner rather than later.

    3. Felicity Fox: I had been content to let Felicity decide her own future covenant as long as she chose Invictus or Ordo Dracul. Now that Octavia (one of her main role models) had joined the Order, I felt it was a forgone conclusion that Felicity would join the Order as well, and while Alice and I could not spill the beans about our recent meeting with the Prince, that would not stop us from making preparations. We wanted Felicity out of the kiddie pool.

    So, I asked Susan to be her mentor in the coils of the dragon. If she learned even one, and was tested by the order she could gain citizenship in Baltimore. I chose Susan to be her mentor. Susan has a reputation as being a great teacher of coils and in particular a fast teacher of coils. In fact, she spent only 6 days in the kiddie pool when she was dropped there way back when. She was granted citizenship in Baltimore when she was able to verifiably teach a coil to a new student in 6 days.

    This is all great, but there is a problem: Susan already has two students, Scarlett and Kurt Hoffman. Scarlett (gangrel), Susan chose herself. Kurt, she took on as a student because Michael Seville asked for a boon so she would do it. Susan has said that she would continue to teach Kurt for as long as he would have her as a teacher. It's neither often nor easy to be owed a boon from Michael Seville.

    This problem isn't as simple as Susan taking a break from teaching Scarlett. If for some reason Robin (Scarlett's sister, apprentice to Enrique) were to learn more coils than Scarlett and be promoted above her, there would be serious drama. Well, I told Susan I would find a way to keep them both busy.

    4. The Screening: Earlier this night, Alice decided to stop by the speakeasy in the kiddie pool which she and I helped to open during our residency there. The place functions as an informal Elysium and is currently being run by the coterie of my sister, Felicity Fox. Anyway, Alice went there and decided to spy on a carthian meeting that was happening in the movie theater which adjoined the speakeasy. Alice said she saw something interesting, and using Auspex shared her memory of the event with each of us in turn.

    4a: Alice's memory: So, through Alice's eyes we can see that she is in a movie theatre (the same one where Michael Seville confronted Chibis (mekhet, deceased) a few halloweens ago. There is a TV/VCR on a rolling stand up front playing a tape of cartoons. Alice looks back and forth and we see that two carthians are giving each other some kind of signal. The one closer to the TV puts in a new video tape and hits play.

    On the TV screen are two actors, a black man and a white woman. The woman is on her hands and knees and the man is kneeling behind her as the two have sex. This must be Pascal's porno. As I am watching through Alice's memory I could just about swear that the woman in the film is Patricia Mason (daeva, invictus, member of my cousin Darren's coterie.) Another signal is given and the carthian by the TV pulls a hand held tape recorder from their hoodie and holds in on the TV stand before pressing play. They are trying to do this surreptitiously, but Alice spotted it.

    Moans and crys of "Do it harder!" now accompany the video. The voice is unmistakable. It is Patricia Mason's voice. At this point, Fred Black (nosferatu, invictus, member of my cousin Darren's coterie) comes walking into the movie theater. He stares transfixed at the screen with a look of shock on his face. After a few more minutes of pornography another signal is given. The tape is removed from the player and replaced. Soon the screen is filled with images of amateur claymation. Patricia Mason walks in.

    Patricia (smiling): "Darren, hi! I'm glad you're here. I made a film and I wanted you to see it."
    She looks at the screen showing amateur claymation.
    Patricia: "Oh, have you seen it already? Did you like it?"
    Darren: "You whore!" He slaps Patricia across the face and storms out of the theater.
    Patricia leaves as well.

    And then I remembered that Alice had sent Mouse to investigate Pascal's new... hobby. So, we called for Mouse and once she had joined us in the tub (it's a big tub, but it's getting crowded now) she told us that she had a copy of the tape. So, four vampires and a ghoul march downstairs clad in robes to watch a porno. We rewind the tape and watch from the beginning. Here is what we learned:

    1. The star of this movie is definitely not Patricia Mason, but someone must have gone to a lot of trouble to find someone who could pass for her from a certain angle and in a certain light.

    2. At the beginning of the tape stage hands can been seen setting the scene, applying makeup to actors, and preparing the lighting. One of them we recognize as a ghoul belonging to Pascal Stitcher (mekhet, carthian, member of Dorris Bette's coterie.)

    So, we ask Mouse how she got the tape. Basically, she had a conversation with Pascal's ghoul when they were both at Elysium and he allowed her to make a copy of the tape.


    Damage Control

    So, Alice and I sent Susan and Christina home and we sent Aika and Aiko to see them safely to their destination. Susan tends to travel with heavy security, but Christina does not. Better safe than sorry. Anyway, Alice and I went to visit Patricia Mason as her house. We brought the tape with us. She was completely ignorant of the cruel joke played on her. All she knew was that a member of her coterie had slapped and called her a whore in a room full of carthians.

    We also told her we knew who was behind this attempted character assassination: members of Dorris Bette's coterie. (we later learned that it was Reaper's (daeva) idea and Pascal carried it out without the knowledge of Dorris Bette's.) In any event, Patricia now views Pascal and Reaper as enemies worthy of vengeance of some kind. We tell her that we would be happy to help with whatever she has in mind when the time comes.

    After Patricia's place, we traveled to Elysium. There, we asked Madeline Ross for permission to screen the full video, so as to dispel any nasty rumors from traveling farther than needed. So, we screened the video, in full, to anyone present who was willing to watch. Madame Ross agreed the film was crass, but she did enjoy seeing the schemes of carthians fail.

    Pascal's and Reaper's plan wasn't a complete failure. In fact, it was mostly a success. Sure, we mitigated the character assassination of Patricia Mason, but Patricia Mason has decided to leave the coterie which includes Fred Black and my cousin Darren Soderberg. So the coterie which controls land that borders Dorris Bettes' in the Locust Point Industrial Zone has been broken apart. This may make it easier for Dorris and her coterie to move against Darren's and Fred's territory.

    The ST told us he was surprised we sent Mouse to investigate this lead. If we had not done so, we wouldn't have gotten the tape immediately. There would have been a race against time to get the tape before it was destroyed, and that by the time we could show it to others in the city to defend Patricia's reputation, many would assume we had manufactured the tape ourselves.


    Ordo Dracul Apprentice Arrangements

    So, the next night Alice and I visited Antony (reeve, our boss.) Antony tells us that he is rotating us out of the Quarantine Zone for the time being to give us a little R&R. We will be assigned to watch over the Inner Harbor (the kiddie pool) for awhile, and the trio of Auntie (daeva, invictus), Scarlett (gangrel, dragon), and Robin (gangrel, dragon) will be sent to the Quarantine Zone. This is welcome news. The inner harbor should be a little more mellow. The residents of the Inner Harbor have already been screened for rabies after all; many of them by Alice and me.

    Well, we thanked Antony for this news and told him that it would be advantageous for us if Scarlett and Robin were kept very busy for the next few months. Antony has no problems with that, and that should free up Susan's teaching schedule for Felicity. Yay!

    Felicity isn't the only one we are trying to bring into the Ordo Dracul:

    1. Kyle (mekhet, our partner on the map quests.) has agreed to join the Order and to learn the Coils from me. We have been meeting on a weekly basis off screen for awhile now.

    2. Patrick Brown (nosferatu.) Patrick has agreed to become an Ordo Dracul slave. I didn't offer to teach him because I already had an apprentice and because he and I do not get along well together. We are not enemies, we just rub each other the wrong way. Anyway, he had been a slave for awhile, but to my knowledge had not found a teacher. When I looked into the matter I found that Patrick had been trying to teach the coils to himself without a teacher.

    I felt that this was a dangerous path which could lead to a dangerous chrysalis. So, I spoke to my uncle Quintus (sworn of the dying light.) After all, what is and isn't an OK way to study the coils is certainly his business. After a few months I checked back. There was no change in the situation, so I informed Patrick of the danger of self teaching and asked if he had even tried to find a mentor. He had in fact asked every member of the Order to teach him (except me), but they all refused.

    I told Patrick that I would find him a mentor, or failing that I would teach him. Also around this time, I became aware that Steward Smiles (Patrick's Sire, invictus) was trying to get him to join Invictus. I also learned that he was sired by Steward in no small part because Steward thought he had the correct temperament to become an Invictus Knight.

    I took this information to Enrique (ventrue, sworn of the axe, my cousin.) Enrique is the only axe-sworn in the city. I told him that this could be an opportunity for him to find someone to help shoulder the burden. Enrique told me he would give Patrick another look.


    Sophie's Problem

    So, one night as Alice and I are looking around the Kiddie Pool, we are approched by Sophie (nosferatu, unaligned.) Sophie is the one who long ago gave us a letter of introduction from Michael Seville which read simply "ambitious, but inept." Anyway, Sophie tells us that she had a permission to create a ghoul and she thinks someone stole it. Furthermore, she wants help finding a candidate to make a ghoul. She has heard that vampires can be put to death if their ghouls make mistakes. We ask what kind of qualities she would want in a ghoul and Sophie tells us that she wants an older man who can pose as her father, "...and no weird sex stuff, please."

    So, Alice and I travel across the bay looking for ghouls. After some looking around we discover that Piper Morgan has a new ghoul. The ghoul in question is an older black man wearing a suit and a bowtie. He reminds us of a member of the nation of Islam. When we ask her about her new ghoul, she shows us that she does in fact have permission to make one, and presents us with a piece of paper signed by Michael Seville. Hmm... I wasn't aware that creation of ghouls required written permission. Anyway, Piper tells us that she asked Kade (gangrel, dragon) to find her a candidate. Well, I assume that if Kade was granting a boon he would have done a good job vetting the guy. I thought this guy would probably fit the bill quite well for Sophie, and it was starting to look like Piper had Sophie's permission slip so...

    Felix: "Have you already fed him? How many times?"
    Piper: "I've already fed him like 5 times. He is properly bound."

    Damn... It would have been really nice if we could have just handed this guy over to Sophie. Oh well.

    The long and short of this story is this:
    1. Sophie asked Michael Seville for permission to create a ghoul and it was granted.
    2. Sophie wanted something in writing so there would be no trouble for her. Seville gave her a piece of paper written in latin saying basically that if someone has a question about her making a ghoul they should come talk to him. The paper itself is not a permission slip, transferable or otherwise.
    3. Piper bought the paper from Shaman (nosferatu) who bought it from Getter (deceased) who probably is the one who stole it from Sophie when he was still unliving.
    4. Piper traded in good faith. She believed that she was purchasing a permission to make a ghoul all nice and legal, so we recommend that she not be in any trouble.
    5. Sophie still has permission to create a ghoul. She just needs to find a candidate.


    Information from Seville

    When Alice and I went to Seville to inquire about the permission slip, our conversation wandered as it often does. Tonight, Seville asks what our opinion would be if he were to move into the inner harbor and take a chunk of the Kiddie Pool for himself. We tell him that we would not be in favor of that move. We think that 'contestable territory' is good for the city. It allows the young ones to learn, the angry ones to blow off steam, and the landless ones to travel. Further, once Seville makes such a move most or even all of the other Elders would want to take a piece of that pie for themselves and the kiddie pool would be no more.

    Seville tells us that with Octavia's exit from Invictus, it is all but settled that Peter (mekhet, invictus, Alice's grandsire) would be selected as the Invictus cadidate for next Prince. Because Seville is unwilling to tear the city apart with a civil war, he is content to scoop up as much territory as he can before the transition rather than wrestle for praxis after it.

    Seville: "After all, Baltimore is just as much my city as it is the Prince's city. I have done just as much to shape this domain into what it is today."
    Felix: "Hasn't my Grandsire had a hand in shaping the city as well?"
    Seville: "Your grandsire has shaped his clan, his family, and his covenant certainly, but no... his influence on the city proper has been more modest."

    Seville shared some really interesting information about the founders of the city.
    1. Long ago the Prince was a very ambitious Invictus vampire.
    2. Michael Seville (nosferatu priscus, Carthian) swore he would never rebel against the Prince as long as he was allowed the freedom and the territory to do his carthian social experiments.
    3. Konrad Von Berg (ventrue priscus, Ordo Dracul) swore his allegiance to the Prince as long as the Prince allowed him the freedom to shape his clan and his family as he chose.
    4. The Prince went looking for the right kind of vampire to be the daeva Priscus. Appolonia (daeva priscus, Crone) was chosen because she was content to be mostly apolitical.
    5. The same can be said of the gangrel Priscus. Pilgrim (gangrel priscus, lancea sanctum) was sought out and recruited because he believed it was right for the church to play second fiddle to the invictus politically while the church concerned itself with souls.

    So, even as the Prince has enjoyed absolute monarchy for all this time, Michael Seville has been molding and shaping the city by molding and shaping the hearts and minds of the city's childer as they participate in the trial he created.

    According to Seville, if he is allowed to become Prince the city will look very much the same as it looks now. After all, vampires of every covenant have been sending their childer to run the gauntlet he created before they can be allowed to become 'citizens.'

    By contrast, Seville believes that if Peter has his way (and it won't be easy as he is not powerful enough to rule by fiat) that all immigrant vampires will be destroyed. Those that remain will find themselves living in a 'classical invictus setup: "You know... all the lands divvied up, pretentious titles, boring parties."

    Finally, we asked Seville if he approved of the job we were doing. We had certainly made enemies among the newcomers to the city. But we felt the purpose of the Quarantine Zone was see the newcomers at their worst and check them for rabies. The only way to do that was to put them in a pressure cooker.

    Seville: "If I did not approve of your methods you would be removed as my bailiffs.             Steward hands us a ransom note in the classic style (letters cut out of newspapers and magazines glued to paper.) It tells Steward that they have his childer and that he should bring an unspecified amount of cash to a location across the bay in the Quarantine Zone.

            Before we went off half cocked, we confirmed that Patrick was in fact safely resting at the Chapter House. Enrique had moved him to an area that is not accessible by the unsworn, but I was able to get Bartholomew (daeva, sworn of the mysteries) to confirm Patrick's presence. He was there.

            At this point, I am thinking we have a not fully thought out scheme of some stupid newcomer in the QZ. Well, I have a plan. A long time ago, when Alice and I were still in the kiddie pool we filled paint ball guns with pepper balls and paint balls filled with that purple dye that banks use in dye packs to mark robbers. Well, I still had the barrel of dye in the basement (I wasn't able to get it in smaller portions) and the paintball equipment.

            When we arrived home, Seville's recruit had completed all three puzzles and there was an omelet on the kitchen counter. Cute. I was not distracted by that. Instead I decided to draft the guy into our little gang. He just experienced a metaphorical taste of our existence. Now he could have a real taste.


            The Plan

            Around the time when we acquired Aika and Aiko, I convinced Alice to spend her precious XP to also get obfuscate 5, so that on the (many) occasions when she left me to go do mekhety spy stuff she could bring backup with her in case things went sideways. This is relevant to the plan thusly:

            1. Alice and I would each follow Steward Smiles to the ransom drop point leading a small group of obfuscated fighters. Alice would take Aika and Mouse. I would take Aiko, Sidney, and New Guy (Seville's thrall. He refuses to use his mortal name and he wants us to come up with one for him. We haven't. yet.)

            2. Steward will lure the 'kidnappers' in while Alice and I set up a cross fire.

            3. We will all open fire on the 'kidnappers' marking them with purple bank dye.

            4. We will move in and kick their asses.

            5. Any who manage to escape our ass kicking will be marked. We can find them later and make sure they get the ass kicking they deserve.

            Sounds awesome right? We thought so.


            The Execution

            We travelled across the bay following Steward. The ransom location was not quite a warehouse. It was much larger, and there was a corrugated tin roof to keep the maze of crates and shipping containers dry, but there were not walls, just beams to hold it up. Deep into the yard, we can see lights. Steward heads in that direction and we follow.

            As Steward nears the lights, we see that the ground is covered in sand. No... that's not right. There is a peculiar texture and smell to it. Well... I failed my science roll, and Alice has no dots in science. Fortunately for us, New Guy succeeded on his roll. This is not sand. It is a mix of powdered aluminum and iron oxide which has been spread far and wide over the ground of about one half of the area under the roof. New Guy grabs me by the arm. He looks frightened and points at the ground. I get another science roll and realize we are in deep shit. Aluminum and iron oxide, if ignited are extremely flammable and burn really hot. Fuck.

            Felix: "FIRE TRAP! RUN!!!"

            We run. Alice runs. Steward runs. As we are running, We notice there are men standing on the rooftops of the adjacent buildings. The one closest to Alice's group raises his gun arm which is holding a flare gun. Before he can shoot he is hit by several paintballs and pepper balls. This is enough to throw off his aim and his flare goes wide, falling harmlessly toward the water. So, instead of being surrounded by fire on all sides we are surrounded on only three. Miraculously, no one frenzies and no one dies. Lucky die rolls.

            We are unable to capture any of the assailants, so we withdraw to a safe distance and wait. Across the street from the storage yard there is a car in an alley. Inside that car is Trenton (daeva, carthian.) He sits there looking at his watch. We sit there watching him. After about 90 minutes, he gets out of his car. We follow him back into the storage yard, which by now has burned itself out. When he reaches the edge of what was the field of aluminum and rust he drops his flashlight and runs. We let him.

            So... I'm more than a little pissed. We all leave the area and meet back at Alice's and my haven. Before we do anything else, we call Darren Soderberg. Darren is my cousin and he holds the office of city Censor. He is specialized in responding to potential breeches of the masquerade. I think an arson investigation that could be traced back to a vampire qualifies as such an event.
            Darren will have the contacts and influence to either shut down or misdirect the investigation before it becomes a problem for us.


            Playing a Hunch

            Alice and I are going to find out who just tried to kill us, and we are going to make them suffer. We spend some time looking into sources where one could get large amounts of the chemicals needed to set this up. And as we were looking into all this, we just had a feeling that we should be talking to Terry. Terry the mekhet is a carthian and she spends a lot of her time helping other carthians and neonates get the things they need. For example, a long time ago Alice and I were looking for old arcade games for cheap to put into the speakeasy. Terry introduced us to the owner of a junkyard who collected more than just scrap metal.

            So, we paid a visit to Terry's place and asked her if she had helped anyone get their hands on large quantities of either of the chemicals in question.

            Terry: "Are they going to be in trouble?"
            Alice: "Yes. Lots of it."
            Terry: "He said he was selling it for money."
            Alice: "That's not what they did with it."
            Felix: "Terry, you need to realize that not all carthians are cool and trustworthy like Michael Seville. A lot of them are assholes like whoever tricked you into helping them so they could try to murder us."

            Terry gave us a name: Sam Wagner (nosferatu, carthian)


            Resolution

            We brought this information to the Reeve (Antony.) Then we gathered as much muscle as we could. Our Posse included the following kindred:

            Enrique (ventrue, sworn of the axe)
            Auntie (daeva, marshal, invictus)
            Scarlett (gangrel, dragon, marshal)
            Robin (gangrel, dragon, marshal)
            Antony (ventrue)
            Felix Fox (that's me)
            Alice Fox (everyone's favorite mekhet)
            Kade (gangrel, dragon)

            We also arrived with a mess of ghouls. With this show of force, there was not violence. We took Sam Wagner and all his ghouls to an off grid location owned by Antony. None of the other carthians stood up for him. We also picked up Trenton.

            Both vampires and all their ghouls were separated for interrogation. Here is the outcome:
            1. Wagner maintained his innocence.
            2. He changed his tune when we found that one of his ghouls had welts and purple dye all over his chest. We had succeeded in marking him. Sweet.
            3. Sam then confessed that the 'dark lord' had been whispering to him and made him do it. The dark lord again? wtf!?!
            4. We are pretty sure that Sam was under the effects of a mental discipline of some kind. Probably Nightmare.
            5. Trenton was the patsy. He was hired to pick up the cash nothing more.
            6. The archons arrive and Sam Wagner is put to the final death.
            7. Trenton is given a strike. (the Reeve has a three strikes and you're dead policy for QZ residents.)


            Mercs

            After all this excitement, Alice and I met with Marco and Polo. They have been in the QZ for a few months now and in that time we have known them to be mercenaries. They will take cash money and do jobs. Usually people pay them a few hundred bucks and they get a few hundred bucks worth of effort, but they have never reneged on a contract. We also had new information from Sherry about their capabilities.

            We weren't going to assume that the recent attack was specifically aimed at us (it probably was.) It might have been aimed at the Ordo Dracul. After all, Scarlett and Robin (the other marshals) and Patrick (the nearly killed vampire) are all members of the order. So, Alice and I paid Marco and Polo $10K to watch out for Sunny (daeva, dragon, QZ resident.) We will see what kind of effort they put forth when they are paid less modest sums.

            Book Keeping

            Several vampire residents of the QZ have passed their rabies tests and will be granted permission to move into the inner harbor. They are:

            Piper Morgan (daeva, Carthian)
            Patrick (not Patrick Brown, mekhet, carthian)
            Shaman (nosferatu Carthian)
            Marco and Polo (daeva, carthian) This will make it harder for them to do their job, but I'm not going to lie and say they aren't ready to move on. We have some integrity.


            Thanks again for reading. This brings things up to date. It will be a few weeks before I have anything new to post.

            There Are Four Lights!

                                Alice Fox: Mekhet, Scion of the bloodline of the Prince of Baltimore. Wife of Felix.
                                Felix Fox: Ventrue, Grande-childe of Konrad Von Berg (ventrue Priscus) Husband of Alice.
                                Christina: Felix's cousin. Harpy.
                                Susan Small: Felix's cousin. Great Grand-Childe of Konrad Von Berg. Harpy.

                                Our Plans:

                                We are currently attempting to juggle the responsibilities of being Marshals of the city, while developing our territory in the LPI. There are to be two jewels in the crown of our territory.

                                1. The Foxhole: A cosplay club that will open soon, and be managed by Kat (gangrel, carthian, cosplayer.) It will hopefully become a great feeding ground for us.

                                2. Elysium: We have secured permission from the Master of Elysium to create an official city Elysium within our territory. It is also currently under construction.

                                The Elysium will need staff. Lots of staff. We plan to leverage our coterie's three ventrue dominate powers to make our Elysium into a dollhouse, with lots of dolls to play with. Well, humans who won't be missed aren't too easy to find the numbers we need.

                                You might be thinking that the homeless population would be a good pool from which to draw. However, the over reliance on them for feeding in the Kiddie Pool and Quarantine Zone has fostered a belief amongst many homeless that there is a serial rapist of the homeless in the city. Many of them have banded together for protection.

                                In talking about this, Alice and I have concluded that the Bus Station would be the best source of 'staff.' It brings fresh bodies into the city nightly, many of them transients and runaways. Their disappearances would be relatively easy to cover up. So, we decided to propose to the powers that be to exert their influence to get the bus station moved from its current (unattended) location, to a new location within our coterie's territory. The benefit to us is we can steer likely candidates to staff our Elysium. The benefit to the city is we will keep an eye out at the bus station for new vampire arrivals into the city. Win-win, right?

                                Anyway, Alice and I have spoken on this topic with Septimus (my uncle), Peter (schenescal, Alice's Grandsire), and Michael Seville (elder, nosferatu Priscus) off and on. However, before any kind of agreement could be reached...

                                Alice and I are served with summonses to appear before a court at Elysium. Well... being made to appear at court is not good in the best of circumstances, but it is even worse to be blind-sided. Alice and I do a little asking around in advance so we can form some kind of strategy before appearing in court, but Michael Seville advises us to let it ride.

                                Court

                                So, Alice and I arrive at Elysium at the appointed hour and are led to a large meeting room to find out what is going on. We are seated in a room packed with vampires, most of them Carthian. Pilgrim (gangrel priscus, archbishop) is presiding, and Dorris Bettes (neonate, daeva, carthian) is the plaintiff. Pilgrim calls the meeting to order, and Dorris stands.

                                Dorris: "I have asked you here to discuss the contest over the land in the Locus Point Industrial Area. I believe that the coterie of Alice and Felix Fox are unfairly influencing the outcome of the competition using their family connections. They have already done this to make sure the metro station will be within their territory, and now they doing same with the bus station. I would move that a judgment be rendered preventing the use of outside influence in this competition."

                                Pilgrim waves over an assistant who hurries out of the room. He returns in a few minutes with Septimus and Peter.

                                Peter: "Madame Bettes, I have heard your complaint against Mr. and Mrs. Fox, and I think you are operating under a false assumption. First, the apportionment of territory to neonate coteries is not some kind of gameshow or contest. The placement of the Bus Station, or any other thing in this city will not be decided by competition or committee. It will be decided based on what is best for Baltimore."

                                Peter: "Second, the Bus Station is an entry point into the city which can be used and exploited by our enemies. It must be watched and defended. Felix Fox and Alice Fox have shown that they have not only the necessary skills and resources, but also the nerve and commitment to defend this city against outsiders. You have yet to show these qualities, fledgling."

                                The smug smile worn by Doris at the beginning of the scene is replaced with a look of mortified embarrassment.
