                                                    1. Unless the ghoul is a PC or the NPC recently turned into one, I have them as vitae addicts. It’s Russian Roulette though, eventually they’re going to succumb to vitae addiction.

                                                    2. No they aren’t addicted to blood by default. Nor are they addicted to vitae by default. Whenever they taste vitae (save the Embrace or delineated exceptions) the mechanics for vitae addiction and vinculum come into play.

                                                    3. I run it as detectable via Auspex only if the individual in question currently has the Tainted Condition in effect.

                                                        03-26-2018, 02:11 PM
                                                        3. In first edition the taint of diablerie disappears with time since Auspex used to look at the subject's aura. It's easy to connect the Tainted Condition with how it worked in first edition, but it's not actually how it works in second edition. Now you can directly ask if a person is a diablerist, and whether or not they are a diablerist doesn't change with time. They either have committed it or they haven't.
                                                        Tainted is not supposed to be an indicator for diablerie. It has a different purpose, as stated in its description.

                                                        Edit: But I do understand people who gets confused if they are used to first edition. My group also handles it like in first edition since that's what most of us were used to when we started our current chronicle, and there's no reason to change it during play.
