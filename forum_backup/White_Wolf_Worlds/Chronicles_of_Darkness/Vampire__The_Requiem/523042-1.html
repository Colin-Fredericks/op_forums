    Um. Maybe I'm missing something, but I feel like there's a fairly obvious issue that isn't being addressed. A neonate needs either Animal Ken (fairly high levels for wild animals like boars, in my opinion), animalism, or the ability to grapple and drain a boar or deer (each of which requiring different super-human abilities) to be able to regularly feed from boars and deer. This is not a matter of human-style hunting. If you kill the animal, it's useless. Even with a spear keeping it at bay, hunting injuries to boar are exceedingly common. Since you need vitae to heal, it's a losing proposition.

    Blood that isn't fresh is near-useless, too, if we're talking about 2e. That means you pretty much have to feed from a living animal. That means slaughterhouses aren't really an option like they are in Buffy (and maybe older versions of Vampire; I can't claim familiarity.) At least not to feed an entire city's neonates with.

    So unless you're buying a few kittens a day (a masquerade risk in its own right) or specifically geared for it or going after livestock (another masquerade risk), I don't think feeding from animals is really feasible. It works for some Kindred (those already inclined towards it, like Gangrel), but I can't imagine all Neonates being relegated to it and surviving for more than a couple weeks.

    I also find the idea of restricted feeding interesting, but I don't think animals-only for an entire class of vampire is the way to go for the reasons above. I'd suggest something closer to quotas, but I really have no idea how you could police that. Really, no idea how you could enforce animals-only. You'd probably just get a massive amount of unregulated "poachers."

    Of course, now I'm getting a whole "drug war" or "prohibition" vibe, which may be totally fun to play with if that's where you want to go with it. Neonates selling and trading blood dolls on the vampiric black market. Sneaking bites when and where they think no citizen-ranked vampires are looking. Could be fun.

    Really, I think given human (ha) and vampire nature, you'd get less vying for promotion if it's as locked down as you say. Instead you'd have a thriving blood market. Maybe even run by some of the "citizens" for favors and resources under the radar.
