                    Black Blood

                    if you read Rites of the Dragon and the Ordo Dracul Covenant book more carefully, you'll actually see some subtle but every clear evidence that Dracula is just an ordinary Kindred, embraced like any other Kindred, with a sire just like any other Kindred.

                    Okay, ordinary is not the right word, Dracula is far from ordinary, but that's because of his feats as an extraordinary person. He's special because his accomplishments and feats are special, not because he's inherently unique.

                    Dracula, in Vampire the Requiem, was mostly likely embraced by a brutish (Turkish?) warrior, the one who attacked him with the Ottomans on the night of his betrayal. Based on his abilities in Rites of the Dragon, I'd say Dracula is a Gangrel, but that's just an educated guess, given how often he relies on Protean to get the job done.

                    Dracula also has a Beats just like any other Kindred, and struggles against frenzy, like any other Kindred. Rites of the Dragon is not even ambiguous about that. He constantly describes how he struggles against his inner Beast, especially when he's dealing with those three hunters who invaded his castle (did anyone else think of Castlevania during that chapter or was it just me?).

                    Dracula also comes to stand face to face with his own Beast during a dream at the end of Rites of the Dragon, where his Beast takes the shape of an actual dragon during his dreams.

                    Edit: I get that Dracula being an ordinary Gangrel would raise some questions that need answering, such as why his sire never showed up, and why his brides don't seem to have any blood sympathy with any of the clans. That's fair enough. To answer that question in my own Requiem game set in Victorian London, I decided to convert Clan Tzimisce from Vampire the Masquerade to Vampire the Requiem. I decided that Dracula did not raise as a full Kindred the night of his death. No, he rose as a Revenant instead. As a revenant, he sought out a way to become a clanned vampire. How he did this, I do not want to spoil in case any of my players read this, but suffice to say, Dracula is the first vampire of Clan Tzimisce in my Requiem game.

                    Here is my own homebrew and my customized lore for Clan Tzimisce for my Requiem game: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...it?usp=sharing

                                                        Originally posted by Black Blood View Post

                                                        What I'm aiming for basically porting the entire character from the novel into a potetial CoD game with everything else unchanged pretty much without having him as a Kindred or something like that like your writeup does.

                                                        You're aiming for the impossible, and like someone else said: if you really want a 1 on 1 exact adaptation and don't want Dracula being Kindred, you're going to be forced to make Dracula an entirely different and unique monster, which in turn would ruin the Ordo Dracul or severely cheapen them. It would also make Dracula somewhat irrelevant for a Vampire chronicle. What would be the point of that? What do you gain in return?

                                                        I think if you'd concede on him not being Kindred, and do make him Kindred, you can actually get pretty damned close to what we see in the book.

                                                        To address some of your concerns and how to implement them in CofD:

                                                        Scholomance: The book mentions this place exactly twice, in only 2 sentences total. While the book says Dracula studied there, it doesn't specifically state he became a vampire there. Suffice to say you have a lot of leeway here. You can have a Kindred Scholomance in your game to suit your needs.

                                                        Being able to walk in the sunlight: A Kindred Dracula would be able to do this with the Mystery of the Ascendant.

                                                        Repelled by holy imagery: This is a bane you can pick when you fail a Breaking Point. It's called Repulsion (p. 108 of VTR 2e). Kindred Dracula would have this bane.

                                                        No reflection and not showing up on cameras: 'The Lost Visage' (p. 60 of VTR 2e) says that all vampires lack a reflection. Well, they don't, you just never see the reflection because of the Beast. Dracula's lack of reflection is merely the result of the Lost Visage, something all Kindred have. Not showing up on cameras is also a result of this. Again, something all Kindred deal with.
                                                        If that isn't enough for you. Some Nosferatu actually completely lack a reflection or shadow as part of their curse, and the Hollow Mekhet have an evil spirit twin that has taken over their shadow and reflection, giving it a will of its own.

                                                        Red blazing inferno eyes: All vampires could have this as a result of many things. Frenzy might cause this to happen. Failing to use a Discipline might cause this to happen (a Disicpline always does something). Or it could be the result of hunger, a bane any vampire can get, check 'Face of Hunger' on page 108 of VTR. If that isn't good enough for you, I'd once again point at the Nosferatu, who could easily have this as part of their Clan curse.

                                                        Aging and becoming younger after feeding: This could easily tie into the Face of Hunger bane. There's also a bloodline in 1e that ages, although I forgot which bloodline it was. You could always just come up with your own unique bloodline that does this. Vampire the Requiem has plenty of room for more bloodlines.

                                                        Hairy and clawed hands: Sounds like the Protean Discipline to me. Otherwise this could once again be the result of a Nosferatu Clan bane (I'm starting to see a trend here, maybe Dracula is part of Clan Nosferatu?).

                                                        Cannot cross running water: This never actually happens in the book. Dracula has no problem traveling by ship or crossing bridges in his carriage. If you do insist on giving Dracula this bane. That's fine. It's already in the book, although normally reserved for Strix, it can easily be given to a Kindred as well.

                                                        Invitation: Also an existing bane all Kindred can get. Again, see page 108 of VTR 2e.

                                                        Must sleep in his native soil: Again, see page 108 of VTR 2e.

                                                        No Beast and no Humanity: I think this is nonsense. There are plenty of times where Dracula in Stoker's novel succumbs to bursts of violence and anger, or bursts of strong emotions, which very much reminds me of how frenzy works in VTR 2e. There is also plenty of times the novel points out how bestial Dracula really is. But if you insist that Dracula never frenzies. Well, guess what? There are ways for Kindred to never (or hardly ever) frenzy. the Meditative Mind Merit (p. 46 of CofD) together with a high Resolve + Composure score goes a long way, and Mystery of the Wyrm also allows you to nip a frenzy in the butt by directing your Beast to retreat when you feel a frenzy boil up. Humanity is just a game system to see how close your vampire is to the Beast.

                                                        Sorcery: If you want Dracula to be a sorcerer like in the novel. That's fine. Cruac, Theban Sorcery and Mysteries of the Dragon together cover everything Dracula can do in the novels. Coincidentally, canonically, Dracula knows all 3 of these sorceries in Vampire the Requiem.

                                                        So my conclusion? Making Dracula anything else than Revenant or Kindred would cheapen the character in this setting, it wouldn't do the Requiem setting justice, it wouldn't do Dracula justice, and it wouldn't do the Ordo Dracul justice either.

                                                        My advice? Keep Dracula Kindred. Apply the points I made in this post to your Dracula character (make him a Nosferatu with a unique bloodline and at least a couple of dots in Protean, Animalism and Majesty) and he will be almost identical to the novel Dracula, while also respecting the Vampire the Requiem setting.
