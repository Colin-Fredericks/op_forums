    Reason for a Kindred to engage in philanthropy?

    10-07-2017, 10:07 AM
    Excuse me, but as I understand it, A kindred gains more fore maintaining his humanity by personally helping one highschool drop out study for a GED test. then donating a sum of money to help dozens get into college.

    I understand this is because of the personal human connection, but it brings to mind, is their a reason for a wealthy kindred to engage in philanthropy?

                              Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                              I'd agree with number 2, but again what I said helping one highschool drop out prepare to get their GED will be more effective at maintaining your humanity, then donating money to help dozens of students with college.
                              It might be more effective but it also takes much more time and forces them into a level of closeness they might not want to deal with. You help that kid, they become a target for your enemies. Even a random act of violence can end with your work undone and you frenzying wildly at perceived perpetrators. Maybe it even happens in front of you and there you are with the only thing that can keep them from truly dying, at the cost of your humanity.

                              On the other hand, throw some money at a school and you can enjoy a closeness with the school, even have favorite students you might watch over secretly without actually getting too close. You get influence with the school, without being influenced by it in kind.

                                  Humanity isn't morality. It also isn't really an IC concept; no vampire is going to do something because it "maintains" their "Humanity" stat.

                                  As to the question at hand: Why does anyone do philanthropy?
