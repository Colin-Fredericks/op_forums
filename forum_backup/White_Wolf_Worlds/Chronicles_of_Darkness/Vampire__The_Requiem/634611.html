                                          Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                                          Other question - Did there be set effect on Kindred drinking blood from the Beasts? I would suspect something like hallucination trip of mages and changelings, but in imaginary of Beast's Horror and it's Lair.
                                          First, let me point out that, in 2e, there's no official set disadvantage (because they're always disadvantages). The only requirement in 2e is that you need the merit.

                                          If you absolutely must have a penalty, then you're better off either 1) making the vampire hungry to feed more or establish social dominance (inflict the supernatural hungers that would count as Family Dinner), or 2) inflict an anathema. The thing about supernatural vitae drawbacks in 1e is that they are always the drawback of the supernatural in question. Werewolf blood makes you prone to rages. Mage blood inflicts paradox-bedlam-like madness. Changeling blood makes you unable to tell fantasy from reality. Promethean blood gives you Disquiet. So, if you want to keep that up, I'd go with something that's a "weakness" of Beasts.

                                          I also recommend converting it into the form of a Condition. Make it an incentive.

                                              Originally posted by gflash View Post

                                              I think this price is much too low for the ability to heal a human back to normal from the brink of death.
                                              It might save them, but it's not like it's instant or cheap. Any aggravated damage takes two nights and five vitae. It's not like you can just force feed a human a little blood and they're right as rain in a few minutes or even hours. Lethal is 1:1, and bashing is 2:1. Ghouls can only even hold Vitae = Stamina dots, so the vampire will have to be there feeding them Vitae pretty constantly, and most vampires can only carry between 10 and 20 Vitae, some of which they need for themselves.

                                              Healing a human back from the brink of death is incredibly difficult and costly for a vampire even using Ghouling. If there's aggravated damage involved, you're probably also talking about a stage 3 blood bond very quickly, and I don't interpret those as universally beneficial for the vampire like some people do. It's an obsession, and that can be unpredictable and something a vampire doesn't want.

                                              Stabilizing them is easier, but you can do that with a single mundane dice roll anyway.
