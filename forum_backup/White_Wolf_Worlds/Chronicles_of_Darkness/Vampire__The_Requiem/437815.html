    Baltimore: The Kiddie Pool Saga (Actual Play) Part 7

    03-11-2015, 09:24 PM
    The Kiddie Pool Saga Continues!

    Previous Chapters and Character Portraits can be found here [LINK]


    Back Story

    In part four of the write up (links in my sig), I wrote about how Michael Seville had asked Susan Small to speak with his childe, Kurt Hoffman about the Ordo Dracul. Since then, Kurt has become a slave of the Ordo Dracul and Susan has acted as his mentor in the coils. I once asked Susan for how long she intended to keep teaching Kurt. She responded thusly:

    Susan Small: "I will teach him as much as I can for as long as I can. It's not often that Michel Seville asks for boons that are not already owed to him."

    As part of this agreement, Seville will provide security for Susan when she travels into the Quarantine Zone or Kiddie Pool to teach Kurt.


    More Back Story

    When the Quarantine Zone was being set up, Michael Seville made a back channel deal with the newest member of the Primogen Council (Septimus) to make the newly formed Quarantine Zone the domain of Michael Seville. As Alice and I were hounds at the time, we were quite influential in the creation of the Quarantine Experience.

    1. The storage garages where the new vampires make their havens? Our idea.
    2. The butcher shop that Cooper runs to feed the little ingrates? Our idea.

    Well Michael Seville is a Carthian, so he was quite interested to hear our ideas on vampire government. Anyway, contrary to popular belief, the Quarantine Zone is not contestable land. It is Seville's land, which means that feeding there without permission is poaching. Seville wanted to hear our ideas how to award feeding rights amongst the newcomers. Here is what we came up with:

    3. The tickets. Each night, Cooper will distribute a limited number of tickets which are good for one feeding within the Quarantine Zone. If you don't have a ticket, I guess you're stuck drinking cold blood from Cooper's butcher shop. The color of the tickets changes every few weeks, and the number of tickets released changes from night to night (to avoid overfeeding in the area.)

    This system isn't permanent. In Michael Seville's words, he plans to "shake things up" from time to time.


    Still More Back Story

    When I looked at Eve's ledger and discovered that Theodore had a transferable boon from Aika and Aiko. Eve Mentioned at the time that she knew a secret about Theodore that if we knew it would allow us to "own him." We declined at that time. We have accrued over time lots of little boon debts here and there. We are trying to become creditors as opposed to debtors now.


    Caged Heat

    Alice and I had recently been awarded the lives and freedom of Aika and Aiko (daeva twins, lancea sanctum.) As it turns out, Aika and Aiko didn't feel the same way Alice and I did about the situation. We tracked them to the northeast side of the city, which is now fallow territory but was formerly occupied by "the Boss."

    There is a bus station in that territory with nightly departures. To make a long story short, Alice and I were able to track them down. There was some fighting, and we were able to stake them and take them. They were fed animal blood from Cooper's butcher shop through a funnel to prevent torpor/heal damage/prevent immediate hunger frenzy.

    Anyway, when we pulled the stakes from their hearts they woke up in bird cages which I have described last chapter. We spent about a month doing psychological torture, feeding them our blood, and applying Dominate powers to them. You know, wholesome family activities.

    Out of character, Felix has a Dominate devotion called "the Stepford Devotion." It is similar to Conditioning, which is a prerequisite. However, rather than creating drooling servants with no personal initiative, the Stepford Devotion is used to make permanent changes to the victim's personality. It can be subtle and minor, or enact more drastic changes in the thoughts, beliefs, and behaviors of the victim. Aika is now blood bound to Alice, and Aiko to me. They were both purchased as five dot retainers. So, evil dominate powers, blood bonds, and experience points have transformed Aika and Aiko into loyal retainers who serve House Fox.


    Terry's First Meeting

    I had been wondering about Terry's status in the city. Last chapter, she participated in an action of the Mekhetocracy. This would usually indicate that a Mekhet had become a citizen, but I prefer not to speculate.

    The most recent meeting of the Mekhetocracy took place in the steamy bath downtown where many such meetings are held. As usual, we disrobe and enter the pool, which is heated to a pleasant bathwater temperature. Terry arrives last, sheepishly holding her robe to cover her modesty.

    Kyle looks at Terry and quotes her poem: "So join our ranks and go through the Rite, We always need kindred willing to fight."
    Terry: "Stop that. Don't make fun of me."
    Kyle: "I'm not making fun of you."
    Felix: "Your poem is awesome Terry."
    Alice: "We loved it."

    The Prince enters the room. We do not recognize him by face, but his potent beast is unmistakable. Tonight, he appears as a statue of Apollo. His skin is of white marble and muscled perfection with hair of gold. At that time, I feel the Prince using some kind of Auspex devotion which extended the Prince's power of aura perception to everyone present. The storyteller described it as being soaked in the rain. I looked around the room and watched the auras of all present respond as the Prince quoted the same verse from Terry's poem that Kyle just had. And in that moment, I finally realized what the Mekhetocracy is. It is a cult. It is the cult of the Prince.

    The Prince: "They aren't lying to you Terry. This is your city. Welcome home."

    Terry looked around the room at all of us, dropped her robe, and joined us in the pool. So yeah, I'm pretty sure Terry is a citizen now.


    The Reeve

    After the death of Elliot Rex (nosferatu ancilla carthian sheriff) at the hands of his own childe Elliot Jr (nosferatu neonate carthian, my nemesis? really??) The office of the sheriff remained vacant for months. Eventually, after some influence peddling my cousin Antony (adopted childe of Octavia) was named sheriff. Soon after, three members of the Primogen Council (Appolonia, Pilgrim, and Septimus) voted in favor of greater autonomy for landowners in Baltimore. The Prince did not oppose them. Peter (seneschal, mekhet) responded by doing away with the office of the Sheriff and replacing it with that of Reeve. Reeve in Baltimore is basically the same as sheriff, but only Invictus are eligible to serve.

    Antony continues to serve the city, now as Reeve. His jurisdiction has shrunk considerably, and does not extend beyond the inner harbor (kiddie pool) and Locust Point. The rest of the city would be policed by Bailiffs, who would be appointed by individual landowners. To date, no Bailiffs have been named.

    Meanwhile, the mood in Elysium is that the Reeve is not doing a good job. Citizens do not feel safe traveling to Locust Point or the Inner Harbor. One of the Harpies (Melissa Hopps, crone, childe of the Keeper) has been spreading this view.


    Terry (the Mekhet)

    So, on a typical night Alice and I had just returned from slaking our thirst on the campus of the University of Maryland (dragon territory.) We were heading home to check in when we noticed Terry running down the street, apparently in chase. Terry's service to her landlord (Antony) is to watch for poachers in his territory, and she takes her job seriously.

    Well, Alice and I decide to give chase as well. At first we are chasing Terry, but soon we see what she is chasing and we pull ahead. Terry is chasing a bird; some kind of hawk which happens to come equipped with a vampire's predatory aura. The bird in question was lowering itself for a landing and it appears to be traveling in the direction of Antony's bar.


    Standoff

    Antony's bar is not open to the public. In fact, if you were to walk past, you probably would be unaware there was a bar there at all. To access the bar, one must walk in a side door, down a flight of stairs, and down a hallway before encountering the bar's entrance. When we arrive inside, Sarah (gangrel, quarantine zone resident) is standing in the middle of the bar. The door to Antony's office is closed and some of his ghouls have guns trained on Sarah.

    Felix: "Hello Sarah, what's on your mind?"

    Antony hears Alice's and my arrival, and decides to open his office and join us.

    Sarah: "One of your family members is going to die this night. I overheard Theodore talking to some vampires. He said, 'the ventrue bitch will be at the speakeasy giving her lessons tonight. If you want to get her, tonight might be your only chance.'"

    Alice and I immediately begin running. The road which hugs the inner harbor and leads to the speakeasy is often clogged with slow moving traffic and drunken pedestrians. So, rather than taking our car we run the whole way.


    Bigger Standoff

    Alice and I arrived at the speakeasy (the unofficial elysium of the kiddie pool) to find nothing obviously amiss. We enter through the backdoor and take the stairs up. On the third floor is the small hidden speakeasy which Alice and I had refurbished so long ago. On the second, there is a sound recording studio, and some kind of lounge with tables. There is a small room outside the recording booth.

    In the small room there are four vampires and two ghouls I do not recognize, and Sam Bast (nosferatu, carthian, one of Seville's men.) Sam is blocking the door to the recording booth.

    Vampire: "There are six of us, and one of you. So why don't you just stand aside and live to fight another day. "

    Sam Bast: "I'm Sorry I can't do that."

    I become visible, Alice does not (she does this often.)

    Felix: "I think you have miscalculated. You may have us outnumbered today, and you may even kill us. But this is Baltimore. You will be found, and you will be shown the light of day. You are all dead already, you just don't know it."

    The lead vampire turns and is about to say something, when another vampire who heretofore was cloaked by obfuscate makes himself known. In the corner of the room sits Michael Seville. He uncrosses his legs and folds the newspaper he had been reading.

    Michael Seville: "Well that's enough of that." He took a drag from an imaginary cigarette and flicks it at the vampire leader.


    Terrifying Violence

    The atmosphere in the room becomes very... dreadful. Alice and I block the exit as the invading vampires attempt to flee. We watch as one of them starts screaming that he is on fire and tears chunks from his flesh in an attempt to extinguish himself. As he casts aside chunks of himself and those chunks hit other vampires in the room, they also begin screaming and tearing at their own flesh. Seville has used his elder level dice pools and nightmare powers to wicked effect.

    Even though Alice and I were not targeted by the discipline, we were still operating at -7 dice pools due to the aura of dread in the area. After 3 rounds of holding the door, Alice and I fled downstairs to escape the terror. There, we intercepted the ghoul who was working the door and advised him not to rush upstairs. Soon after, the six vampires came limping down the stairs still pulling flesh from their bodies and flung themselves over the side of the pier into the bay below.


    The Aristocrats!

    Alice and I got our shit together and went back upstairs. The waiting room was covered in blood and viscera. Then, the door to the (sound proof) booth opened and Susan Small and Kurt Hoffman entered the room. We waved. "Hiya Susan " She had difficulty maintaining her composure. Michael Seville was no longer present (or visible), but Sam Bast was.

    At about this time, Antony and Terry arrived (they drove.) We gave them a brief synopsis of the action and it was decided that Alice, Sam, and Kurt would escort Susan back to Konrad Von Berg's estate. Antoni and I would pursue the attackers.

    Well, it didn't take Sherlock Holmes to deduce that perhaps the intruders were heading towards their cars. So, we traveled to the nearby underground carpark. The intruders were there in various states of injury getting ready to enter their car. Antony and I wasted little time in bringing violence to the evil bastards. We violate city law by sending them immediately to torpor, save one for questioning. (the sheriff acted in defense of the city. We are in little danger of prosecution)

    All four of the vampires and two ghouls are put to death by the garden shears of the Archon Helen (mekhet, crone) but not before I ask them wtf they are doing and why. Truth is compelled with dominate, and the lucid one tells us they were there to kidnap the dragon girl and bring her to their boss, a guy named Elliot. We take a time out, and I sketch a portrait of Elliot Jr and show it to the guy. "Yeah, that's him. Except he's bald now." Famous last words.

    We later learned that Spider and Jerome James (both nosferatu) usually would have been present with Sam Bast to guard Susan, but had other obligations that night. Seville was present (obviously), but Sam was unaware. So why was Sam so brave in the face of long odds? As it turns out, Sam is more afraid of Michael Seville than he is of this random gang of vampires.


    Family Meeting

    There was a family meeting scheduled for later in the week, but I considered this revelation reason enough to call for the family to meet immediately. The family gathered and there was some disappointed grumbling that the usual banquet was not present. (Usually my grandsire provides food and wine which is just as nourishing as blood at these gatherings, but it can't be prepared on the spur of the moment.) The following topics were discussed:

    1. I recounted the events of the attack on Susan and informed my family of my belief that Elliot Jr has returned to town. It is agreed that the situation should be dealt with. Until Elliot Jr can be located we have all agreed to increase our personal security.

    2. I ask my Grandsire about his relationship with Michael Seville. It is my current belief that the city government as we know it came about due to the long ago actions of an alliance/coterie of vampires which included my Grandsire, Michael Seville, and the Prince. Konrad is not feeling too talkative tonight. "We are not enemies." Is all I can get out of him.

    3. Octavia speaks more about Seville: "Alder Seville seems to have great territorial ambitions of late. I fear he is preparing a political ambush."

    4. Septimus: "When the Prince chooses to step aside, I plan to climb to that lofty position."
    Felix: "Why?"
    Septimus: "Because Konrad won't do it."
    Felix: "I know that. I mean, why you? Why do you want to sit on the throne? You are not so powerful as to rule independently. Why would you make yourself and our family beholden to the church? Why not consider Peter as the next Prince. He is Invictus, he is older than you, and he has already been serving as Seneschal for a long time. If you press the issue, it may result in a civil war."
    Septimus: "Peter is not that much older than me, and besides, Peter has a weakness: He will do what is best for the city. He will stand aside rather than let it come to civil war."
    Felix: "You think that's a weakness? Interesting. Well, would it be the worst thing if Michael Seville became Prince? There are other elders up and down the east coast who may come here with avarice in their hearts. Only a powerful elder will scare them away. You are not that person, but Seville could be."
    Septimus: "A Carthian? Are you crazy? If Michael Seville became Prince, so many of the priviledges you enjoy would fade away. The best territories which are currently held by Invictus, many by our family, would be chipped away and redistributed until we were pushed to the margins."
    Felix: "But we would survive to grow our power for the next generation rather than invaded by foreign powers."
    Septimus: "This is just typical. When I am acting in the family's interests everyone is so thankful. Thank you Septimus for helping me gain territory. Thank you Septimus for keeping the city free of other Ventrue. But, when I want something for myself it's "Septimus, you shouldn't have joined the Primogen Council. Septimus, your reach exceeds your grasp."
    Alice: "Septimus, you have recently acquired a lofty title, and it is a well deserved one. However, I believe that I have the best solution for the future of the city: Octavia should marry Peter. Peter should succeed the throne and Octavia should rule by his side. Invictus would remain dominant and our two families combined would be formidible."

    You gotta love Alice. She's a showstopper. There isn't much in the way of conversation after that little bomb got dropped.


    After the Meeting

    After the meeting, I asked for a private word with my Aunt and Uncle. In Septimus' office I tell him about a conversation I once had with Michael Seville. I tell him that Seville has a serious dislike and distrust of the church and will not allow the city to become a theocracy. I tell him to think about that as he makes his plans.

    Then, Susan enters the office unannounced dragging Christina by the arm.

    Susan: "Tell them."
    Christina: "I think I was being followed earlier tonight."
    Felix (getting a little angry): "And you didn't think to mention that sooner? Christina... Alice and I cannot be everywhere at once. And while it is true that if you were taken we would risk our lives to save you, it would be much preferable if you would take your safety more seriously so Alice and I can focus on finding and destroying Elliot Jr and anyone else who thinks our family is weak."
    Octavia: "Well, that settles it. Until this situation is resolved you are grounded to my estate unless you are accompanied by a heavy escort of some kind."

    Christina is not happy, but at least I will not have to worry about her for the time being. Susan, I know will be spending more time at home as she does in fact take her security seriously.

    That leaves Felicity, still a resident of the Kiddy Pool, for Alice and me to worry about.


    Diner's Club

    To ensure the safety of Felicity, we took the following steps.
    1. Felicity, Kurt, and Nellie (her coterie) will move their haven to Locust Point near Antony, Alice and me.
    2. The three of them will feed together and be accompanied when they do so. I order Aika and Aiko to accompany them on their dinner trips. I also order Patrick Brown (ordo dracul slave, nosferatu) to do so. It will take them a lot longer to get everyone fed, but six vampires some of whom are combat focused should dissuade any would be attackers.

    With the more vulnerable members of my family out of harm's way, Alice and I are now free to figure out what is going on and bring some justice to the world.


    Questioning Theodore

    After the family meeting, Alice and I mentioned that we were going to go talk to Theodore (nosferatu.) Well, Susan wants to come with. So, the three of us arrive there with some extra ghouls early the next evening at the time when Cooper usually opens the butcher shop. Well, Theodore is not in a very talkative mood tonight.

    So, Susan walks into the butcher shop and asks if she could be the one to distribute the tickets tonight. Then, with a handful of tickets she walks across the parking lot to an old mechanic/oil change place. The locals have pulled a few couches and tables into there and use it as a night time hang out. Susan arrives and says "Anyone who punches Theodore as hard as they can, gets a ticket." Then she throws the tickets in the air making it rain on the hungry neonates.

    Soon, several vampires swarm Theodore each taking their shot at him. Remember, the law states that you can only do so much damage to someone before you are on the wrong side of the law. By spreading the damage amongst several attackers, they are all in the clear, even though Theodore was on the receiving end of a proper beat down. Further, neither Alice, Susan, nor I have laid a finger on him.

    Alice: "We will be back here every night until you talk... and we won't lay a finger on you."

    Theodore started talking. He told us that Jack's crew (the vampire leader from earlier this chapter) used to work for the Dark Lord (Joe, the Daeva whose entire bloodline we had destroyed), but there is a new player in Council territory. A new nosferatu who is getting crews to swear their fealty and work for him.

    Whether or not Theodore talked is irrelevant. He gave information to the group that tried to take Susan Small. Well, we went back to Eve Liebsch. We talked to her once more about her information about Theodore. She agrees to give it over to us in exchange for rights to make haven in our territory, which should be habitable in the not too distant future. She is not asking us to protect her haven or provide, only for the right to make it herself. We agree.


    Slow Motion Masquerade Breach

    Eve tells us that Theodore was involved in a bank heist. He broke into the home of a bank manager and took her and her son hostage. Well the bank robbery didn't go as planned and Theodore killed the woman and her child. Their bodies are in the trunk of a car which was dumped in a lake not too far out of town. Eve knows where the car is, and she knows that the car is registered to the mortal identity of Theodore. It is only a matter of time before the car is found and pulled from the lake.

    Alice and I take this information to Michael Seville. Theodore is never seen again.


    And Speaking of the Masquerade...

    One night, we get a phone call from a mortal contact we have maintained since our days in the kiddie pool. When we met him he was a veterinarian who slept on a couch in his office which was above a pet store. The pet store in question was a frequently used feeding ground by neonates of the kiddy pool, and after being awakened in the night by screaming animals over and over the veterinarian figured out that monsters were real.

    Over time, we learned that the veterinarian was actually trained as a medical doctor, but due to financial issues was unable to get his medical degree and license. Alice and I pulled some strings at the university and the veterinarian became the assistant coroner.

    Anyway, one night we receive a phone call from the assistant coroner. He tells us that he really needs to see us tonight. Alice and I travel to the basement of the county hospital where we are privy to the following tableau:

    1. The coroner's office is in disarray. Some of the tables are knocked over.
    2. There are three dead humans on the floor; two security guards and one doctor.
    3. There is another body on the floor in advanced stages of decomposition.
    4. In the corner is the assistant coroner, also on the floor. He is holding his stomach, blood seeping through his fingers.

    The assistant coroner informs us that the police found a body in an airshaft of a downtown office building. They assumed that the body belonged to a thief who got stuck long ago and died in the airshaft. However, it turned out that the body was that of a torpid vampire. The vampire in question woke from torpor, frenzied, and killed the coroner and two security guards before the assistant coroner was able to draw his pistol and empty it into the vampire's head.

    Fortunately for us, the coroner's office is in a secluded part of the hospital basement. Unfortunately for us, shift change was in less than three hours. We had to act fast.


    The Coverup

    It should come as no surprise that Alice and I took actions to maintain the masquerade. Here is what we did:

    1. First, we moved the rapidly decaying corpse into a morgue drawer in case some hospital staff would arrive early or some such.
    2. The coroner's wound is mortal, but the bleeding is slow. He has an hour or two to live (according to him. I'm not a doctor.)
    3. We contacted Michael Seville and asked for permission to take a mortal from his territory to be made into a ghoul (the county hospital is in his territory.)
    4. We had felicity (my sister) to the hospital and convinced her that the assistant coroner would make an excellent ghoul for her. She had not yet made a ghoul and had somehow gotten it into her head that her first ghoul should be a "butler to run the household of other ghouls." We were able to convince her that an asset in the position of the coroner's office would be an opportunity for her to get boons. Other kindred would, from time to time need a favor from the coroner's office, after all.
    5. The Assistant coroner is made a ghoul and healed.
    6. We got our story straight. A junky broke in looking for narcotics, and when he didn't get what he wanted. He started hacking people to bits with a machete.
    7. We mutilated the corpses with a machete and ransacked some of the medicine cabinets.
    8. We removed the vampire body from the coroner's office and rented a boat to dump it in the bay.

    All this prep work is well and good, but we weren't finished yet. Once the assistant coroner gives his statement to the police there would be a manhunt. This is the sort of high profile crime that will get attention. There will be pressure from on high to solve it. We needed a patsy.

    We were sitting there, debating the ethics of pinning a crime on an innocent man and seeing him sentenced to jail or even put to death. Our first instinct was to find someone who deserved it; someone who perhaps had gotten away with murder previously. Then, Alice had a brilliant Idea.

    Alice: "You know, Habsburg Day is coming up..."


    Habsburg Day

    A few chapters back, a mortal gang robbed an art dealer in downtown Baltimore. In the process of that robbery, they killed the parents of Tigger (former blood doll of Alice and Felix. Current ghoul of Terry the mekhet) and destroyed the Habsburg Lillies (and old and expensive work of art.) The Keeper of Elysium, Madeline Ross (invictus, ancilla) asked Alice and I to look into the matter. At the conclusion of our investigation Alice and I delared a new holiday, Habsburg Day. We told the keeper that every year on Habsburg Day, we would find one of the culprits and do something bad to them. Here is what we did:

    1. We tracked down the gang in question.
    2. We planted a bag full of vials of morphine and ketamine in the trunk of one of their cars.
    3. We also planted the machete which was used to mutilate the corpses of the security guards and coroner in the same man's trunk.
    4. We captured the patsy and used Dominate on him to change his memories and add some compulsions:
    a. He now has memories that make him believe he is in fact the murderer.
    b. He will think it's a great idea to sell the morphine and ketamine to make some extra cash.
    5. We also used Dominate on his girlfriend. According to her, on the night of the murder he drove her home from a party and wanted to come inside for some nookie. She declined, because she saw a machete in the back seat and that made her nervous. (we chose this gang member for this Habsburg Day precisely because he did attend a party that night, and he did drop his girl friend off at home before going home himself and having no alibi for the time of thecrime.)
    6. We contacted one of Octavia's ghouls, a cop. (we helped him with an internal affairs investigation a few chapters back.) We asked the cop to point one of the police departments in the direction of the gang member and send a confidential informant to do a drug buy.

    So, a confidential informant buys drugs from the guy which turn out to be morphine and ketamine in hospital vials. This leads to a search of the guy's home and car which turn up the murder weapon (machete with victims blood on it.) In the course of the investigation, the girlfriend tells the cops about seeing the machete on the night of the murders and being afraid of it. All this, combined with a lack of alibi makes this a slam dunk case.

    Happy Habsburg Day!
