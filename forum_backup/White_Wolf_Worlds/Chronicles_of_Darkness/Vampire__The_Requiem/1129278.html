          Obfuscate 1 only works where people expect to see someone. The details are obscured, so you can walk down a street carrying a machine gun without issue, but if you're alone in someone's apartment they'll see you. True invisibility requires obfuscate 3.

              Obfuscate is more than just invisibility. It's a type of subtle mind control.

              Its basic function compels the person to ignore your presence, unless you do something that overtly draws their attention to yourself, such as combat, or destroying the environment. People will subconsciously walk around you (instead of into you), and they'll block out any normal audio made by those protected under the effects of Obfuscate. Though screaming at the top of your lungs, or setting off a bomb might break the illusion.


              And yes, there are more advanced powers of Obfuscate which can allow the vampire to look like somebody else. So you could be standing in a room in full view of everybody, but they wouldn't see the vampire. They'd see whoever the vampire was trying to impersonate.

              As for using it on other people, I believe it means you can simultaneously affect an entire group. As in, you wave your hands and make 2 or more people vanish from everybody's collective notice.


              Finally, you need to keep in mind that the clans who have a natural affinity for Obfuscate are the Nosferatu and the Mekhet, two clans who have a reputation for secrecy and manipulation. Obfuscate's main purpose is espionage. So hiding yourself inside a drawer and going unnoticed would be extremely useful if you're doing a 'fly on the wall' scenario. You could be standing right next to your enemy, listening to their private conversations and they wouldn't even know about it!

              As for small objects, you could sneak a tool or a weapon past security and give it to a captive prisoner, or you might abscond with evidence vital to an investigation and cause the case to go cold. You could steal a priceless artifact or treasure and walk right out the front door. All kinds of super spy level behavior can be done by hiding small objects on your person.

                                  Originally posted by Narr666 View Post
                                  What happens when I use Obfuscate 1 when and someone comes in: Does Obfuscate fail or will he roll Wits + Composure - Obfuscate. And if he fails that roll, what then?
                                  Obfuscate fails. The thing to remember about Obfuscate 1 is that it works by making people not pay attention to you, because you're "just some guy/gal." It stops working when people start to pay attention to you. If you walk into someone's bedroom looking like "just some guy", you are inherently calling attention to yourself, unless the person was expecting "some guy" to come into their bedroom.

                                  Originally posted by Narr666 View Post
                                  Does he sees "someone" but can´t remember him? Or does he sees someone and recognizes him until he went out of the room and then his memory is clouded?
                                  He sees a random, unimportant person walk into his room. This being a rather unexpected and alarming turn of events, he would likely try to figure out who this random guy is that just walked into his bedroom. At this point, he is paying attention to the vampire, so Obfuscate 1 stops working.

                                  Originally posted by Narr666 View Post
                                  And when he comes into his room, makes his roll and fails, what happens when that Vampire talks with him. Does he talk to someone he does not recognize? What happens if he knows that Vampire he´s speaking to? Would he not recognize him?
                                  Taking the bedroom part out of the example, if a vampire using Obfuscate 1 starts talking to him, that might or might not break Obfuscate, depending on the context of the conversation. If you're in a public place and some random stranger asks you for the time, you don't pay them that much attention. You might answer or tell them you don't know, or ignore them depending on how receptive you are to strangers. But if instead of asking for the time they ask for your social security number, you're probably going to want to figure out who this person is that's asking you for personal information. That would break Obfuscate 1.
