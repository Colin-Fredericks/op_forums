                                            Originally posted by SunlessNick View Post
                                            How similar will the dhampirs be to those from Wicked Dead?
                                            We took some of the core conflict/doom from Wicked Dead, but it's not a supernatural curse anymore -- there are plenty of interpersonal reasons for a vampire's kid to start or get into trouble with other vampires. We've got plenty of hooks detailing how that can unfold, and we deal with violent conflict, social conflict, and mixes of the two. The goal this time is to make dhampir viable player characters, rather than (by default) an antagonist.

                                            I should mention that I'm not developing this one. I'm overseeing it as Chronicles of Darkness lead, but on-the-ground development is in the hands of Vampire's new line dev, Danielle Lauzon.
