    Baltimore: The Kiddie Pool Saga (Actual Play) Part 6

    02-09-2015, 09:31 PM
    The Kiddie Pool Saga Continues!

    Previous Chapters and Character Portraits can be found here [LINK]



    Backstory

    1. At a recent Elysium, Alice and I caught the tail end of a conversation between Earl Gossett (neonate, carthian, gangrel.) and the Peter (youngish elder, mekhet, invictus.) Earl was being somewhat rude to Peter in open Elysium.

    Earl Gossett: "You can't tell me what to do!" I never found out what the conflict was about, only that Earl was mouthing off to Peter.

    2. Eve Liebsch (gangrel, aspiring Invictus, kiddie pooler) had recently asked a favor of Alice and me. She wanted us to store some documents of hers in our haven. As Eve still lives in the trial area, her haven is not officially recognized by the city and therefore not protected by law. Our haven has no such limitations. We agree to store her documents. In payment, Eve hands us a document which appears to be a signed, transferrable boon from Michael Seville.

    I put them in our safe and thought nothing more of it. I didn't even peek. I was after all executing a boon.

    3. The core of Baltimore is surrounded by 4 neighborhoods which are populated to varying degrees by vampires. Most of them are vassals of Baltimore, but not citizens. One such neighborhood lies to the SouthWest of Baltimore, and is populated by Carthians who are lead by a Council.

    Alice and I recently visited what passes for Elysium over there and witnessed some violence nearby. Roy (ventrue) whose territory is north of the council and west of Baltimore arrived with a small posse and had fisticuffs with a coterie of apparent high school aged vampires. Roy's group won the battle and took a prisoner.


    A Request from the Prince

    One fine evening, Alice and I are met near our haven by one of the Mekhet of the city. It is Diana (neonate, crone, childe of the archon Helen.) She informs us that there is Mekhet business to attend, and leads us to the west end of Baltimore. We meet in an elementary school gymnasium in the barrens which separate Baltimore from Council territory.

    We are soon joined by the following Mekhet:

    Kyle (childe of the archon Simon, Ordo Dracul Slave)
    Pascal Stitcher (neonate, carthian, childe of the archon Simon)
    The Archon Simon (lancea sanctum)
    Lucien Du Lac

    Simon looks at Diana and asks why she didn't bring Terry (mekhet, carthian.) They have some words and Diana leaves to get Terry.

    Kyle Speaks: "I have acquired some actionable intel. Later tonight, the coterie of Roy's prisoner will trespass into Roy's territory and attempt to rescue the prisoner."

    Simon: "None of you are required to participate in this operation, but the Prince requests that you intercept the intruders and prevent them from achieving their goals. You are encouraged to be malicious in the carrying out of this mission. If in the course of violence, you accidently destroy one of the offending vampires, your punishment will be light."

    I was a bit shocked that the Prince would issue a request instead of an order, but we readily agreed to participate. About this time, Diana returned with Terry and we filled them in on the situation.

    Kyle told us that we had a few hours before the incursion was likely, and we took that time to visit Kade and Lorraine. Kade has completed his mission for the Ordo Dracul that required him to keep a haven in the East side, and has since moved to the territory of his Sire, Sharon Winter. His haven is fairly close to Roy's territory. In the event that we are caught out near dawn, Kade has agreed to shelter us from the sun.


    The Mission

    Next, we travel to Roy's territory where the prisoner is being kept. From there, we divide into three groups and put eyes on the most likely routes to the prisoner. The rescue team shows up and we beat them up. Badly. The rescue team consists of:

    Mike (daeva)
    Jenny (daeva)
    Missy (daeva)
    Sherry (daeva)

    All four are Carthians, and the girls are all childer of Mike, who is the childer of the Prisoner (I never got his name.) During interrogation, we learn that Jenny and Missy are blood bound to Mike, who is blood bound to his sire. Sherry has somehow avoided the blood bond by convincing Mike that she drank from him while he slept, and is blood bound. Anyway, this is a familiar story. Boy gets embraced and decides to pass the embrace on to some cute girls he knows from high school.

    Before we leave, we wipe their memories of what happened. As far as they are concerned, they came to rescue the prisoner, got the shit kicked out of them by shadowy figures, and woke up in an abandoned building suffering grievous wounds.


    Broken Glass

    I have learned something about Michael Seville (noseratu priscus.) He seems to try his best to keep the boon debt of neonates at manageable levels. However, he will not allow a neonate to become a creditor of his. Well, Alice and I were a bit in debt to Seville, so when we came into possession of the boon from Michael Seville we felt it would be in poor taste to use it to ask for a favor. Instead, we asked to apply it to our debt. Soon after, Seville offered us information which he assured us we would find valuable in exchange for a minor boon. Seville warned us that there would be an attack on our haven some time soon.

    With this information, Alice and I made preparations. First, we switched our day ghouls (steve and eddie) to a night schedule. Second, Alice and I made it a point to dress up and leave our haven only to double back under cloak of obfuscate and watch from a nearby rooftop. About a week later, the following events took place.

    1. We noticed a vampire loitering in an alley across from our haven. The vampire in question is named Angela. She is formerly from Council territory, but has recently joined the kiddie poolers and is attempting to gain citizenship in Baltimore. She is met by a cargo van. They exchange a few words and the cargo van drives to the back of the house.

    2. Miles (daeva, carthian) and Johnathan Poole (daeva, carthian) show up. They have fisticuffs, and Miles wins. Miles then pulls Angela away from our haven. They have words and Angela leaves with Miles.

    3. Meanwhile, in the rear of the house the van has disgorged four armed assailants who are breaking into our home using a hydraulic jack and acetaline torches.

    4. We follow them inside, obfuscated. They walk right into our ambush. There is only 1 survivor.


    Interrogation

    Using magic powers, we interrogate the surviving assailant. He was sent here by "the Dark Lord" to steal some documents. He is a blood slave (not even a ghoul) who is under the impression that the "Dark Lord" will make him immortal one day. Well, unless the Dark Lord is interested in my utility bills, I surmised that it was Eve's documents they were after.

    Further investigation went like so:

    1. Alice is able to pull an image from the mind of the blood slave. The Dark Lord is a vampire whom we have seen before. His name is Joe, and he is a member of the Council who governs the territory to the SW.

    2. Eve felt a little bad about the attack on our haven and told us we could look at the documents. The documents turn out to be a ledger of boons. Not only the boons directly involving Eve, but of any she knows about. Specifically there is some information about a boon Roy owes (ventrue who resides on the west side.

    3. We are warned that Joe has connections and backing within the city by Octavia. We shouldn't rush to action. Further, the members of the council (who number about a dozen) have a mutual defense pact. It would be unwise to go off half cocked.

    So Alice and I resolve to investigate further before forming a slick plan to bring pain upon the house of Joe (the Dark Lord.) We are operating on vampire timescale after all. We need not rush to action.


    Carthian Party

    We have been invited to attend a Carthian party by Terry the mekhet. Dorris Bettes is the one throwing it, but carthians are allowed to invite others to the party. Alice and I were invited by Terry, and Susan Small was invited by Kat (at my request.)

    The party would take place at the hidden speakeasy on the pier that Alice and I refurbished during our time in the kiddie pool. Since we left, the place has changed hands a few times, and in that time has grown. Whereas initially, the "vampire hangout" portion of the building solely included the hidden speakeasy. The building includes a bookstore and a movie theater, and now the book store's upper floor has been taken over as well. I do not know who exactly is responsible for this expansion.

    Anyway, as our group of revelers approached the bookstore, we noticed that Dorris had taken some precautions to keep mortals away tonight. At strategic intersections on approach to the bookstore are groups of intimidating looking groups of people.

    1. Some hoodrats standing next to a car playing loud rap music.
    2. Some "working girls" and their handler.
    3. Some mean looking bums holding a jar of urine.

    To back this up was an aura of dread leaving the place feeling extra forboding. We think Dorris hired a nosferatu to keep the normals away.

    So, we head to the alley entrance where Dorris (daeva, Carthian) and Miles (Daeva, Carthian) are working the door. As we are conversing with them, Kyle (mekhet, childe of Simon) and Simon (Lance, Archon) arrive with about a half dozen ghouls and begin walking into the building (without a ticket.)

    Miles: "Y-you can't do that. You need a ticket."
    Kyle turns to face Miles and says: "We are mekhet, and we are here. to. Take."

    Dorris and Miles back off and the mekhet disappear into the building. Alice and I show our invitations and follow them. On the top floor in the old speakeasy, the Archon is questioning Patrick Brown (nosferatu, ordo dracul slave) while Kyle keeps the other kindred at bay.

    It seems that the Archon is looking for Darren Colville, a known associate of Patrick's. However, Patrick is extremely stubborn and won't tell the Archon how to cross the street, let alone where his friend is.

    Mid interrogation, the archon notices Alice and Me. He points his finger and speaks: "You two. Wait for me on the roof." We comply.


    On the Roof

    We arrive on the roof to find the following kindred:

    Michael Seville (nosferatu priscus, elder)
    Archie (daeva, carthian)
    Earl Gossett (gangrel, carthian, the squirrel poacher from an early chapter)
    Steward Smiles (nosferatu, aspiring invictus)

    Earl Gossett: "He can't do that. Elysium is there. Every kindred has a right to go to Elysium."
    Seville: "You are not being barred from Elysium, only from traveling in the personal territory of the Seneschal. If you want to attend Elysium, you will have to be escorted there by the keeper of Elysium or her designated agent."
    Earl Gossett: "But you could talk to him, or take it to the council and overrule him, right?"
    Seville: "We are not such good friends Earl, that I will oppose the Seneschal on this matter. But, your problems are much bigger than you realize. This is an invictus town, in case you haven't noticed. I think that very soon the other invictus landholders will follow the example of their elder and you will be barred from a lot more territory.
    Earl Gossett: "That's Bullshit!"
    Seville: "Mr. Gossett, you would be amazed at how many problems can be avoided by the application of manners."


    Helicopter Parent

    Steward Smiles then asked to speak to Alice and Me privately. We walked to the other end of the roof and he explained that his childe (Patrick Brown, yes the same one the Archon is questioning) was having a hard time in the kiddie pool. Steward has been following him around using obfuscate.

    Currently, the kiddie pool is inhabited mostly by carthians and aspiring carthians. This is not surprising. As the SW territory gets squeezed by conflict with neighbors and overpopulation, some of them have been choosing to apply for citizenship of Baltimore.

    Anyway, some of the carthians have been beating Patrick up because he is a slave of the Ordo Dracul. They want him to tell them where the chapter house is, switch sides to the Carthians. (Alice and I didn't have a chance to look into this matter this session, but we will. Rest assured.)


    A Message from the Archon

    At this point the Archon arrives on the roof. "Everyone who is not a citizen of the city leave. now."
    Seville (looking a little annoyed): "And what should the citizens of the city do?"
    Archon Simon: "Alder Seville, as always your company is welcomed. However, you may do as you please."
    Seville leaves, proving the point he was trying to make to Earl Gossett a few minutes ago.

    The Archon approaches Alice and Me and says: "Your haven has been attacked. The haven of a mekhet of the city and of the Prince's bloodline. The perpetrators still live. Some delay could be seen as prudent. More delay will not be seen as genius. Take who you need, travel to their territory and enforce collective responsibility."

    Well, Alice and I take this as a go ahead to bypass our normal cautious pace. To make a long story short, a few nights later we gathered a posse of mostly Mekhet and Ventrue and traveled to Council territory. We captured Joe (the dark lord), his grandchilde (mike), and great grandchilder (Jenny, Missy, and Sherry.) Their torpid bodies were brought before the Archon Helen (crone) for final death... with one exception. Sherry was taken prisoner. Why? I'll explain later, but for now I'll say she is part of an evil conspiracy of Alice's and mine.


    The Wicked Tongue of Susan Small

    The main event at the party was supposed to be pot-luck talent show. The participants did not get to choose what talent they would display in advance. Instead, they drew a slip of paper from a hat about a week in advance. While we were waiting for the show to start, we listened in on a conversation Susan Small (ventrue, dragon, neonate, harpy) was having with a few kiddie pool residents.

    Theodore (nosferatu, carthian, kiddie pooler) was complaining about the unfair system the elders had put in place. Miles (carthian, daeva) Angela (carthian, daeva) and Delores (carthian, daeva ) all three kiddie poolers, were listening to Theodore and nodding in agreement. Theodore continues to opine that territories and feeding rights should apportioned according to vampire population and need.

    Well, Susan Small interjects into the conversation. "All this is well and good, but the Elders do have the power and therefore they do make the rules." She continues, "But, if you had the power you could make the rules. Tell me, how far would you be willing to go to get the power of the elders?"

    Theodore: "I would be willing to do whatever it takes."
    Miles, Delores, and Angela all agree with this sentiment.

    Susan: "Whatever it takes, huh? Spider? Spider, will you join us please?"
    Spider (carthian, nosferatu) joins the small group.
    Susan: "Theoretically, if giving a blowjob to Spider would grant you the power of the Elders, would you do it?"
    Theodore: "Do what!?!"
    Susan: "Suck his dick. If performing fellatio on Spider would grant you the powers you seek, would you do it?"
    Theodore sits there staring daggers at Susan as Miles, Delores, and Angela admit somewhat reluctantly that they would in fact give a BJ to Spider to acquire the power of the elders... theoretically.

    Susan: "So, theoretically we have three powerful elders here, but in practice what we have here are two whores and a sexual deviant."

    Jaws drop and Susan walks off with a smug look on her face.


    The Talent Show

    There were only 2 performances of note.

    1. Spider does a magic act in which he saws Auntie (daeva, carthian) in half. However, the box in which Auntie sat awaiting the saw was comically long, making it obvious Spider had no future as a stage magician.

    2. Terry recited a poem. There were some jeers and catcalls when she took the stage. Here is what she read:

    "The Mekhet's Poem", by Terry the Mekhet.

    On highways and byways the car engines sound
    They move in the dark, for Baltimore bound
    The Night will forever be making it plain:
    You have to defend what you want to maintain.

    So join our ranks and go through the Rite
    We always need kindred willing to fight
    With fire in our hearts, and stars in our eyes,
    We'll leave no trespassers under Baltimore skies!

    In the darkest of nights, when trust often dies
    Kindred walk safely under Baltimore skies;
    But some would destroy that in hopes to recall
    The chaos incarnate of all against all.

    Some people are parasites, taking their joy
    In friendships they ruin and hopes they destroy
    While some folks are builders, who hold in our hearts
    A dream that is more than the sum of its parts!

    Trespassers are poison; no trip is too far
    To hound them to death like the vermin they are!
    But they -are- good for one thing — the way that they cry
    When they realise it is their time to die.

    So join our ranks and go thourgh the Rite
    We always need kindred willing to fight
    With fire in our hearts, and stars in our eyes,
    We 'll leave no trespassers under Baltimore skies!

    We'll hunt them like dogs till the last of them dies.

                              The Commissioner's Daughter

                              One of the stops we made while gathering our posse was at the Von Berg Family estate. While there, we were approached by Darren Soderberg (my cousin.) Soberberg is the city Censor. It is his job to proactively seek out and prevent or seal breaches of the masquerade. Usually it's minor stuff that may or may not lead to a real breach. Tonight is different.

                              Darren: "There is a situation I need your assistance with." On the dining room table are photographs, maps, papers etc. "Three girls have recently gone missing. One of them is the Police Commissioner's daughter. They've put their best investigators on this. Until the case is closed, there will be real police work going on. They will be shining their lights into the dark places our kind like to do their business.

                              We are given a list of leads and sent to burn some shoe leather. Since Darren knows we have been operating in and out of Council Territory, we are sent there. Our investigation eventually leads us to a crack house where some cops are "questioning" a dood. With their fists. There are 2 cops in the basement where the suspect is tied up. There are three more in the garage rifling through the junk, and more still sitting in a few cars out front. There are far too many for Alice and I to subdue and dominate.

                              Under cover of obfuscate, we travel to the basement, where Alice peruses the mind of the dood being questioned. His mind is a bit of a mess, but Alice is able to learn that the dood is a blood bound slave of a vampire, and while he does not know where the girls are being kept he does know that his master is involved somehow. He also knows the location of the local Elysium and a couple vampire havens. So far, the blood bond is keeping him from talking, but Alice and I are not confident he will be able to hold out forever.

                              We withdraw and travel to the Council Elysium. Under normal circumstances, Alice would not use her auspex so casually, for legal reasons. All bets are off in this situation, and we are acting with the authority of the Baltimore Censor. So, Alice spends a goodly amount of willpower points to steal the information we seek from the minds of the local vampires.

                              The commissioner's daughter and her friends are being kept in a seriously low class brothel. Alice and I travel there not only to confirm the story, but also to make sure there are no vampires on site presently. After all, we are about to send the cops there. When we arrive, we do see a vampire there. He is negotiating with the brothels owner for some time with one of the girls. He is offered some "new product" but declines. Alice and I wait for him to finish his meal without ever making our presence known.

                              Satisfied that we have found the girls and there are no other vampires on the scene, we call Darren from a payphone. Darren in turn calls in an anonymous tip, and the cops who were questioning the blood slave split up into two more manageable groups.

                              Having rescued the girls and being made heroes, the cops are happy to withdraw and resume their weekend. (it was saturday night.)


                              The Birdcage

                              In the basement of our haven there is a birdcage. It is 2 meters tall, 1 meter in diameter, and is constructed of really thick steel bars. It has sat empty for far too long.

                              Alice and I have been thinking for a while now that we needed some new servants. We are not interested in more ghouls, or even vampires who have sworn their loyalty or fealty to us. No, we want something more permanent. We want owned vampire servants. (Slave is such an ugly word for it.)

                              We had recently been awarded a small patch of land in the Locust Point Industrial Area, and we would need agents to handle our business when we were away doing the cool kickass stuff we do. So, Alice and I put our heads together and made a list of all the vulnerable vampires in the city; the ones who had the least social protection. Then we discussed the list and decided that it would be good to choose servants who would not make the other jealous. In the end, we decided on Aika and Aiko (daeva, neonates, lancea sanctum, twins) That's right, they are twins. Hey, there are worse reasons to destroy someone's life and take their freedom.

                              I will have to build a second birdcage.


                              Stalkers

                              So, Alice and I began stalking Aika and Aiko. To the best of our knowlege, their discipline spread is Celerity 3, Majesty 3 (we could be wrong) and their favorite game is cat and mouse. Throughout the years, we would often find them stalking their own victims. In the kiddie pool, they liked to lurk on rooftops, waiting for a vampire to be walking alone before pouncing on them. Later, after the NE territory of "the Boss" was cleared of vampires, we found them playing the same game with the new game warden there. After they learned of the Princes decree that all territories east of the city and north of Canton would be left fallow, they had moved their operation.

                              We found the twins in the barrens which separate Baltimore from Council Territory. They were perched atop a water tower surveying the land. Alice and I waited patiently, and soon a vampire came walking down the railroad tracks toward Baltimore. The twins followed the vampire, and we followed the twins.

                              The rail road tracks lead directly into the territory of the Lancea Sactum. The twins waited for the vampire to feed (poach) on their territory before pouncing on him. They beat him up and then let him go. Then they chased him again and beat him up again. This is their favorite activity.

                              Alice and I continued to stalk the twins. We arrived in the barrens early in the night, waited for the twins to take up their perch atop the water tower and hunt their prey. I figured it would only be a matter of time before they would either make a mistake and need to be punished or make a mistake and need to be rescued. Either was fine by me.

                              Unexpected Visitors

                              One night, while stalking the twins a vampire came walking down the rail road tracks. (council territory is seriously overpopulated. Even after the Dark Lord and his progeny were destroyed, they still have trouble feeding.)

                              Anyway, Aika and Aiko are preparing to strike when two other people approach. They have predatory auras, but they do not feel like vampire auras... The bigger of the two dudes is currently holding the vampire by the throat and asking him, "Where is your Alpha? Where is the Vampire Alpha of Baltimore." The poor vampire doesn't know. He is left with a concave chest and the two men continue moving toward Baltimore. Meanwhile, Aika and Aiko turned and ran away at the first sign of greater predators.

                              Alice and I choose to follow them under cover of obfuscate. They continue moving east toward the bridge. Just on this side of the bridge is Alice's and my territory. Currently, our territory is unimproved. There are only two things of any importance there at this time:

                              1. A park bench under a tree.
                              2. A large murder of Crows who live in the tree. (I recently bought some animalism and invited the crows to live there. The deal is, they keep their eyes on the place for me, I dominate old men to come there and feed them on a daily basis.

                              So, when the two cross into my territory, I move in closer and realize that I recognize the larger of the two men. I have met him twice.

                              1. A long time ago, I used Dominate to make a meal of him for Terry the mekhet near the movie theatre. She described his blood as being like fire (in a good way.)
                              2. I rescued him from being beaten by Elliot Jr. and his coterie during Pilgrim's golden ticket hunt.

                              So, Alice and I appeared before him (a fair distance off) and asked to speak to him. He remembered me and was willing to talk. He tells me that a young member of his pack has been taken prisoner in the city and is being held captive by vampire magic. I ask him if he will let me look into the matter on his behalf in an effort to avoid violence. He tells me that he will sit on that bench until dawn. If she hasn't been returned to him by then, he will continue looking for her on his own.


                              Lost Dog

                              Well... A few nights ago Alice and I saw the Archon trying to find Darren Colville by questioning Patrick Brown. We headed across the bridge to the Quarantine Zone looking for either or both of them. Here is how it went.

                              1. We spoke to Cooper. He is the almoner and is in this neighborhood pretty much all the time.
                              2. Cooper hasn't seen Darren in a long time, but knows that Patrick Brown has been spending a lot of time with Eve Liebsch lately.
                              3. We went to Eve's apartment, and Patrick was there.
                              4. We are able to get Patrick to speak after a few social rolls and an impassioned plea.
                              5. Darren has recently set himself up in a new haven in Little Venice.

                              Little Venice is a shitty apartment complex on the water just south of Canton. It is in the Quarantine Zone, which also happens to be Michael Seville's territory. About half of the building has been condemned and has no electricity due to water damage from partially sinking into the bay. Alice discovers via auspex that an outsider has recently moved into the area. (most of the residents are chinese. a white guy moving in sticks out a little.) While walking the condemned portion of the building, we notice that one of the apartments has a new steel door installed.

                              To make a long story short, Darren is unaware he is even in trouble. He has met a werewolf girl by coincidence and discovered that her blood is extra good and tasty. He has blood bound her to him and they are playing house together.


                              Uncommon Stupidity

                                                  @Gravitas Man: I'll try to answer your questions in order.

                                                  1. For the most part no. We are not using the 2E rules. We are using some though. I would describe our game as Requiem 1.1.

                                                  We have been ignoring the humanity rules as they are written since as far back as I can remember. We do use humanity as a roleplaying tool, nothing more. For example, Alice and Felix both have humanity 5. Why? Because the players chose it. We do our best to roleplay as vampires with that humanity level.

                                                  Were the humanity rules made better than before with 2E? Yes, absolutely. But still not good enough that I think they would make the game more fun. The main reason is that our group prefers to play long running games where the consequences of one's actions play out socially. As opposed to a game where doing naughty things leads to downward spiral inevitably leading to the PCs becoming unplayable.

                                                  2. Regarding NPC's: Well... first we feel that NPCs are THE most important part of setting up a game. Playing in a game with few NPCs or poorly crafted NPCs in akin to playing in a desert and not very much fun. According to my ST: "the NPCs ARE the game."

                                                  It is important for the PCs to have some sort of emotional connection to as many NPCs as possible. This doesn't mean they have to like and be friends with them. Hating an NPC is just as valuable as loving them.

                                                  Some tips for creating good NPCs include:

                                                  a. A good introduction. It can be easy as an ST to just bring the PCs to a gathering of NPCs, state that the NPCs are clumped into a few groups, and have the PCs just walk up to each group and say "Hi, my name is..." over and over.

                                                  It is better to include in the introduction scene for an NPC some action that tells the players something about that character. So, right away the NPC in question is more than just a cardboard cutout named Bob. It is Bob the vampire whose jealousy and desperation led him to sabotage Jim the vampire. Or something.

                                                  This doesn't mean that you have to do this for every single vampire at the aforementioned gathering. Just realize that just because a given NPC was in the scene, and may even have shook hands and exchanged names with the PCs doesn't necessarily mean they were introduced. Make plans for a proper introduction at some later date.

                                                  b. Good NPCs should have motivations and stuff to do. If the PCs have the impression that whenever they leave the room the NPC ceases to exist until such time as they come back, this is a bad thing. One easy place to start here is to think of the sorts of things PCs have done in previous games you have played in or ran and assign similar motivations to some NPCs.

                                                  c. schrodinger's box: My ST will often have a few general ideas about NPCs but not commit to them until the last moment. Just like schrodinger's cat, you never know the status of the cat till you open the box.

                                                  d. Pictures help. They help both the ST and the Players to imagine the NPC and their place in the world.



                                                  3. Regarding the game plots. I know that my ST does plan out some things in advance of each game. He generally doesn't plan it in such a way where it's like "the PCs will do this or that." It's more like, "X will happen." and from there he thinks about who will care about "X" and what they might do about it. So even if Alice and I don't get involved in "X" it still affects the city and we will probably hear about it sooner or later from the lips of the harpies. If we do choose to get involved, he already knows who is likely to be involved either as ally or adversary.

                                                  The players often plan things in advance of the game as well. We generally will give the ST a heads up during the week if we have something we want to do in the game and how we plan to approach it.

                                                  In addition to the stuff the ST plans and the PCs plan, there is a fair amount of off the cuff stuff that happens. But because my ST has done such a good job of making the city and it's NPCs (he knows who lives where, and what sort of stuff they are likely to be doing) it is easier for him to mesh what we are doing into the fabric of the story of Baltimore.

                                                  4. As for the wyrm's nest, it should come as no surprise that the nest in question had already been discovered and was known to Quintus, and probably other members of the covenant. I am told by the ST (out of character) that there are other nests in the city, but Alice and Felix have yet to find any.

                                                  I haven't pursued the map quest any further. The ST told me that if/when we do, we will learn a lot about the history of the city. However, the next clue is somewhere in Appolonia's garden. Even with the goodwill we think we have with the Circle of the Crone in general and Appolonia in specific, we are afraid of the prospect of digging around in the garden of the Priestess of the Crone. I do intend to pursue the maps in the future, however.
