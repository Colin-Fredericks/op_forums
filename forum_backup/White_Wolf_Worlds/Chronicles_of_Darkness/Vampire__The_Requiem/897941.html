    Idea: Giving Predator's Taint only to the Gangrel

    05-24-2016, 01:17 PM
    I have a idea which I'll bounce this off to see what you think...

    What about only giving Predator's Taint to the Gangrel or one of their bloodlines while the other clans don't have it? Since I thought it would be more fitting for the Gangrel or one their bloodlines due to a clan based on savagery and especially animalism (well especially their WoD incarnations) hence they're more connected to the beast than other clans which therefore it might make more sense. Although I'm not a big fan of Predator's Taint but I think there could be use of it if it's reduced to a single clan or bloodline that only has it to make them distinctive to the others.

    Any thoughts or ideas?
