    Concept: Werewolf hunter

    07-29-2015, 05:33 PM
    So, in about a year or so there's a new game for 2nd Ed. coming out, it's a crossover style game, and I was thinking of making a Gangrel, kind of a keeper of the peace between werewolves and vampires, and if needed, a protector of the vampires.

    Now, to do this, I need to be able to actually stand up to a werewolf. I know they are ridiculous machines of war. It's not likely a vampire can stand toe to toe with them, especially one built for combat, but I am trying to do my best.

    Thus far, stat wise, I was going so start with Str 4 Dex 3 Stam 3, Brawl 4: Specialty Feral Combat, standard faire. Disciplines, I was thinking Protean 3 right off the bat, and first buy is protean 4 + Claws of the unholy. Merit wise, starting off with Riding the Wave fighting style 2 or 3 depending on restricted slots, and blood potency 3. For covenant, I was going Ordo Dracul. Coil of the Wyrm. I've always been fascinated by the beast, and I think that it would be a good roleplaying choice as well for a Gangrel focused on dealing with other beasts.

    So, let's break down the stats.

    Str 4, Brawl 4, Specialty. That's 9 dice, base. BP 3 = 3 blood, +wp = 3 dice, that's 18 dice. In frenzy, you gain your BP to your strength, so that's 21 dice. Weapon is +0, so that's no bonus, but the damage would be aggravated to bypass the werewolf automatic regeneration. The character has little defense, maybe take brawling dodge and we would have like, a 7 defense at most.

    So, do you think this could take out a standard combat focused werewolf?

                              Originally posted by Regentwill View Post
                              So, MCN, what would you suggest? Should I try to throw in some vigor?
                              It depends on what you're trying to do. Just using Protean for the claws is ignoring the first three levels. Protean is awesome at being a general survivor. You can meld into the ground or fly above as a bat, and launch ambushes from that position. Wall climbing, faster speed, flying. Use the whole of the Discipline in your fight, not just one dot.

                              Hell, if we ignore the Claws of the Unholy merit, the actual power of the Protean 4 is when using the Damage manuever in grapples! Otherwise, its just a +0A weapon, offset by both the Gauru's full defense AND reduced by any natural armor they could pick up.

                              Meanwhile, the vast majority of "combat wolves" (ie Rahu) are going to have +Purity dots to their health, have +Purity in automatic successes in any action, +Purity to their Strength (on top of form bonuses), 8-again to all attacks. Rage (Glory) reduces damage, there are ways to get up to +4L claws and 3/3 armor with just merits alone. And that's a starting PC wolf. Your all-out attacking claws (21 dice) are reduced by (Defense+Brawl, lets say 2+4) 6, so 15 dice. And that's assuming the Werewolf hasn't slammed you with the Submissive Condition, effectively leeching your willpower or preventing you from attacking first.

                              That's an average of 5 successes, reduced by 3/3 armor and 1 from Strength (Glory) Gift*, for 1 agg with your huge attack. The Rahu's got, at minimum, 10 health, and that's with Stamina 1. Meanwhile, your defense is down to 2 against his attack, claws that deal +6L automatically, and you can't heal that without using less vitae in your attack. And that's starting Purity 2, Glory 1. We've got a merit that ignores mundane armor. It gets more lopsided as Renown grows.

                              And all that is assuming a Rahu. An Irraka stealth killer using Efficient Killer merit and surprise attacks? Entirely different ballgame. You're off guard, so no Defense. She hits you for her dice pool as all automatic successes. That's going to be at least 10L.


                              With a Protean user, you're going to need to think outside of the box in these kinds of fights. Its going to be hard, since your focus (shapeshifting) is where Werewolves excel. If that's not what you want to do...


                              * Yes, I know that Claws of the Unholy says that its only reduced by Resilience. However, all these armor reductions are from magical sources, making them the Werewolf equivalent of Resilience, since they all stack together. If not, then increase the damage dealt by 2 or 3, which, against a Rahu, is minimal when you're taking around 8 or 9L a turn and your opponent has over 10 health.
                              Having Silver weapons would be awesome for a slug fest, but would be terror for relations, don't you think? The character isn't designed to be a killer, outright. But a protector, a mediator that is able to throw down if push comes to shove.
                              I'm not seeing much room for negotiations with this character here. Bringing silver is a sure sign of aggression, yes, but, on the other hand, I'm fairly sure that they don't have silver-detecting powers either, so a hidden blade should work just as well.

                                      It's not what you want, but I once built a Werewolf had hunter in Masquerade, and the key points were:
