    Advice on tasks and chores for a PC ghoul

    03-02-2018, 01:03 PM
    So I've been playing a CofD chronicle for a while now, and the players have starting making some friends, allies and foes between the different splats etc etc. One of my players has recently become a Ghoul to a young Daeva neonate that is quite childish and outgoing, a party-going drug-and-sex-addict that belongs in the Circle of the Crone. For now She treats the PC "quite well" as her new boyfriend (and she probably sees it that way), giving him an ample blood supply, drugging him before she eats a bit, going to clubs and having a "good time" for the most part. She's started to ask him for a few favors like picking up some new clothes, getting some cocaine and doing some laundry, even ask for help with a fucked up feeding-frenzy gone wrong so he had to clean it up.
    Shes part of a coterie that with only CoC members that focus on carnal desire through sex, drugs & rock n' roll basically.

    He's met the rest and even got a brief interview with the Keeper of Elysium so that he can become a registered ghoul. In this city, which is controlled by a Carthian Senate, ghouls have some decent respect and more then a few priviliges compared to your standard first ed. invictus city. The registred ghouls are considered members of the City and may even buy City and Covenant Status and join a Covenant, though it’s still limited to one, and most laws and rights appliyng to Kindred is also applied to Ghouls. They even have voting rights on the elections of the city.

    But here’s my problem, I have not played a Ghoul chronicle before, well not since the old V:tM days anyway where ghouls usually wasn’t treated any better then gimp-suited slave-furniture. As well as in most chronicles I’ve played in Vampire (both sorts) ghouls haven’t really been very important but I really want to change both my own and my players veiws on ghouls, making them into persons so to speak, givning them character. I’ve got a few ghoul NPCs that stand out with much more focus then before so there I think im gonna be quite happy. But.
    I’ve read both half-damned and the old ghoul-book but it doesn’t really give me much advice on practical matters ghouls can be asked to do on a daily basis. It only talks in abstract terms about service and stuff. I need help to finding out what the regnant could be asking the ghoul on a daily/nightly basis. More appreciated is ’normal’ stuff as in laundry and repairs and such then supernatural things so to speak.

    And last a bit about the PC, he’s damaged goods, super low Integrity, a bit Condition-heavy (usually), muscle-man thug more then brains and a manly-macho-man and works as a bouncer on a respected and hard to get into nightclub. He was once a half-famous boxer but now is an has-been that got suspended. He’s pretty well in most physical aspects and can keep up in social encounters. As well he has a dot in Majesty and two in Vigor by now, and is probably gonna buy the Beast Whisperer merit that allow him to talk down vampires from frenzy.

    So please give me some ideas about what the Vampire can as of her PC ghoul?

      (Disclaimer: Not a Vampire expert)

      Since Ghouls can operate in the day:
      • They can maintain the Haven, in that "yes, someone lives here".
        • Talk to neighbors.
        • Been seen on the property.
        • Be available to answer the door - such as for police, etc.
      • They can conduct business where requiring only contact at night would be suspicious or difficult. Banks, and government facilities, don't hold hours conductive to vampires.
        • Someone has to be filed as the owner, and it is a good idea if they can appear when needed.
        • Majesty is nice and all, but having a valid drivers license and other paperwork is helpful.
      • Since other Kindred are asleep, Ghouls can work without their interference, in case you need spying done, or another vampire wacked (I don't know the rules regarding inter-Kindred violence in that Domain, though).
      Basically, having a Ghoul can provided the necessary cover for the Masquerade. Remembering that in 2E it isn't so much "vampires don't exist" but more "[Person in question] isn't a vampire".

      And besides the kind of daily tasks that only day-walking Ghouls can do? Nearly anything. You said she's a party animal? Doesn't sound like she wants to spend the time on "trivial" things, like cleaning, the above paperwork, etc.

      Just remember that your Ghoul is human too, and needs time for their own chores, as well as food and sleep.

                                  "Being Normal" (ie maintaining a facade of likable banality and normalcy) while secretly housing a vampire offers a vampire many benefits. That isn't going to be the ghoul doing the errand running, maintaining a life which looks normal is a full time job and can easily be ruined by errands that involve anything supernatural. But shifting which ghoul does that if the current ghoul's card is turned over is the three card monte that clever vampires play with their haven (Kansas shuffle, tip if he's smart, he isn't at any of the options presented in the first place)

                                  From what I read of how this ghoul is being used and exposed to danger so far, if a ghoul who manages a haven, it is best as one of the decoy homes. Also that would make sense why he suffered retribution.

                                  As for mundane tasks for a ghoul exposed to moderate to high risk, secure message courier is a great one. Picking up and dropping of messages at drop offs in times and places difficult for vampires to intercept, like in the middle of the day is a public venue with a police presence. Some message may be packages, some letters, others are verbal only where they must meet someone else's pawn and exchange prearranged pass phrases and responses to identify each other. The chance of being followed, being intercepted, manipulated, and all other types of intrigue are possible down this route, make the vampire's higher level of intrigue possible.

                                  You could flip the script and instead have the ghoul try to break into someone else secure line of communication about, intercept messages, follow messagers home so you can report where they are.

                                  Since the vampire is having a issue with the Seer and this pawn is already exposed, the vampire if wise would want to avoid using that pawn against the Seer unless it was needed for the master stroke coup de grace checkmate, most likely lethal or worse for the Seer, perhaps by using the ghoul in such the way the Seer tries to strike again and finds themselves very dead in a trap instead, ei the ghoul will be used to antagonize the Seer in such a way as to be the bait of a trap separate from the ghouls own mechanization (because the ghoul is exposed and you must assume is constantly watched) so likely the nature of those mechanization which are an invisible safety net during the trap will almost certainly be unknown to the ghoul. In fact since the ghoul has been attacked that means almost certainly if anything goes wrong from the Seers point of view they blame the vampire and then predictable try the punish the vampire by striking at the ghoul again, this time more viciously and thus extend themselves so that the Seer could be caught. If the vampire is REALLY wise he won't even be in the safety net trap against the Seer, it'll be unrelated forces he got to move in that direct by manipulate a fourth or fifth removed parties whom themselves are working via proxy twice over. Vampires are very capable of weaving some crazy twisted webs of plots and counter plots..
