        I think wether or not a cure is possible is pretty tangential to the "search for a cure" story. If a player wants to play a vampire who's looking for a cure, they probably don't want to find it very quickly, if at all, because being a vampire is still a central part of that concept. The cure should be found at the end of the chronicle, if it's found at all. But conversely to Nofather, I think "try to not be a vampire" is a perfectly valid answer to the question the setting poses of "you're a vampire, now what?" Seems like a pretty realistic reaction to me, never mind the fact that all the other vampires tell you it's impossible. Do you believe them and give up searching, or do you tell them they just think that because they haven't found a way yet? What lengths will you go to in your quest for mortality?

                    In my view it should be possible, but it should always have a cost. The Beast separating into a Strix-thing mentioned earlier in the thread is a good one, and I might use it if a player goes for a similar route to becoming human, but other ideas I've had or seen are:

                    -One of the key parts of the cure is 100 years of voluntary torpor. Unlike usual the dreams are relatively pleasant, and the vampire awakens on the first day of the 101st year of torpor, completely human. In a world that thinks they don't exist, and that they don't understand. Try opening a bank account, I dare you.
                    -There is a cure for vampirism, but it turns you into something not human. The one I like is the idea of complete synthesis with the Beast turning you into another kind of creature.
                    -You must sacrifice everybody you know and love to whatever ancient horror created kindred. You'll become a human. With Integrity 1, likely some serious mental scars, and a horde of vengeful ghosts. Have fun.
                    -The only cure is to die and be reborn as a human, losing all memories of your current life, as everyone does.
                    -The only way is to become some other sort of supernatural creature. I like the idea of it being a Strix or a Geist (the latter possibly being a sweet deal).

                    Blue is sarcasm.

                    If I suggestion I make contradicts in-setting metaphysics please ignore me, I probably brought in scientific ideas.

                                Originally posted by Nyrufa View Post
                                I don't think a cure should be possible. Vampirism isn't a disease or a virus, it's a mystical state of being. Vampires are undead, their bodily organs are atrophied and their blood no longer flows regularly. It's not like a scientist can whip up some miracle drug that turns them human again. Even if they could, the subject would probably die within seconds due to complete organ failure.

                                Realizing that the embrace is permanent is kind of what makes being a vampire seem like so much of a curse and could explain why choosing to embrace somebody is a strain on their humanity. If becoming a human again were possible or easy, then life as a vampire wouldn't be as big a deal as its portrayed as. Then, of course, there's the fact that now that you're human again, you know too much and could endanger the masquerade.
                                I understand what you are saying, but you seem to be putting a bit to much science into a game about magical undead beings. (lets not even mention Mages). If we look at the game as a whole the other denizens of the WoD have referred to the kindred as a disease. Other diseases of a magical nature have found cures in the game via magic, science or both; i see no reason Umpirism would not be able to be cured. That being said i by no means would allow it to be "easy" and the one seeking a cure would never fully recover or be normal.
