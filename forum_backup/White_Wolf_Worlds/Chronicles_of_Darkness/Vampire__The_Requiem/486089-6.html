    Originally posted by Spencer from The Hills View Post
    I'm going to try Elder Scrolls vampires.

    Arena
    They're badarse spellcasters who wear cloaks. Not enough information

    Daggerfall
    They look and sound weird, which would suggest Nosferatu, but nobody seems to notice that in game. In fact, one of them writes a book about his experiences and describes what it was like meeting a friend from life. He had been buried and everyone thought he was dead. Nobody recognised him, but he was treated much like a human stranger.
    There are nine "tribes". All that distinguishes them is which cut cost spells they get. So I'll list them and make stuff up about them if needs be:

    Vraseth - "Nimbleness" - I think these guy as the types who scuttle around dungeons and trees and move unpredictably like Voldo from Soul Calibur or Jack from Power Stone. Apparently, the younger Queen Dowager thought that one of their castles would be a safe place to keep her private documents. That would fit a Nosferatu archetype nicely. That sort physical ability may otherwise match Gangrel or Daeva.

    Haarvenu - Big flashy fire and ice spells -These abilities wouldn't directly help them hunt, but they may be used to inspire awe and fear from mortal herds. I think of them setting up blood cults in rural western High Rock. Daeva or Ventrue?

    Garlythi - Shield - That's like fortitude so possibly Gangrel or Ventrue.

    Thrafey - Heal - A bit like the above. They're coded as enemies of the Anthosis. Perhaps they're a new tribe with a new ability so the Anthosis abduct them for study when they can. I'm going with Gangrel.

    Lyrezi - Silence and Invisibility - Probably Mekhet, but maybe Nosferatu.

    Khulari - Paralysis - Well certainly not Daeva. Could be Mekhet, Nosferatu or Gangrel.

    Montalion - Free action (cures paralysis) - The ability is worthless for two reasons and I've got nothing.

    Anthosis - They don't get a spell. They get a constant intelligence boost. I'd say Mehket or even Jiang Shi.

    Selenu - Resist fire, cold or shock. Better at withstanding the elements? How about Gangrel?

    Morrowind
    As well as looking and sounding weird, everyone see that they're vampires in the mechanics, which means that you can hardly speak to anyone and everybody shouts at you or pleads for his life. They stay tombs and ruins. That's as Nosferatu as most vampires get in TES. There are three clans:

    Aundae - High Elves with enhanced willpower and magical ability. They also dress much more smartly than the other clans. They hypnotise their prey. Ventrue.
    Berne - Cyrodiils who pride themselves being able to go everywhere unseen. That's quite Mekhet.
    Quarra - Really strong Nords who run at their prey, grab and bite and probably spill a lot of the blood. Other clan think they're wasteful, ugly brutes and they think the other clans are pathetically weak, especially Aundae. Gangrel

    Oblivion
    They pass as mortals and rely on influence over mortal society. That's Invictus. I'd say Ventrue, based on the tone of their manifesto.

    Skyrim
    Largely a rip off of Oblvion's vampires, but they can raise corpses and are more distant from mortal society. Some of them seem quite keen on eating flesh. For Dawnguard, it could be best to go with individuals or small groups.

    Harkon - He says he was a king who sacrifices a thousand souls to gain immortality directly from Molag Bal, the god who invented vampires. And he wants to blot out the sun. He's like one of the Ordo Dracul, but more arrogant and selfish. He doesn't care about mortals even as pawns. Neo-Tzimisce side of Ventrue? Probably could be any, although one thing he isn't is subtle.

    Valerica - She thinks Harkon's plan is stupid because it would turn everyone against them. As an accomplished necromancer, she's spent more centuries than she can count hiding in a dreary pocket realm full of lost souls. Mekhet

    Serana - One thing that annoys fans is how the PC and Serana are friends from when they first meet whether you like it or not. THe PC activate contraption that push a spike though his hand and some blood gets siphoned to her coffin. You're sent by a group of vampire hunters and there's no option to kill her like the others. You just take her back to her castle (which means that the hunters could get to know where the vampires are based). Either she likes the PC or pretends to. She hasn't quite come to terms with her parents' indifference to her as a daughter. And the player doesn't really get resist her. Daeva much?

    The rest of Harkon's brood - Generally the men (as opposed to elves not women) like being able to rip arms off and the elves like magic and show a bit more decorum. Some of them scheme against each other and other find all that boring and/or immature. They stay in their castle away from the mortal world and mostly have their prey delivered to them. They do have a few operatives in the towns and conspire against hunters and other vampires, whom they see as vermin who make too much noise.

    ESO
    I don't know, I haven't got that far yet.

    Wow, that is a lot, and games are an interesting take on the question. Thx.
    I just prefer not lock so much on specific characters, but more on the rough image of Vampires in general.

            I've been thinking over Only Lovers Left Alive:

            Both main characters seem like they'd fit into the Mekhet template, especially Eve; she's quick, and insightful, and loves collecting knowledge—as exemplified by her massive library, packing books instead of clothes, and being able to 'read' them just by a touch. You could also argue Adam as being perhaps Nosferatu in a way, as he's more than a little spooky (especially when visiting the blood bank doctor, or driving away the people in the streets of Morocco with a simple glare and a "You don't have what I need.")

            Eve's sister Eva, meanwhile? Daeva all the way. Impulsive, flighty, charming, but driven by her vices. As for Kit, it's tough to say, although erudite and refined as he is, and clinging as tightly as he does to his one companion (touchstone) Bilal, I'd vote on him being Ventrue.

            . . .

            Meanwhile, as for What We Do in the Shadows (seriously if you haven't seen this, you're missing out):

            Vladislav (the Poker!) with his temper problems and—somewhat unskilled—shapeshifting would probably be Gangrel. Nick would also have to qualify as such if you count shapeshifting and demeanor.

            Petyr is a Nosferatu, no question. The eldest and spookiest of the whole house.

            Viago would have to be Daeva. Foppish and seductive (but a bit, er, fastidious and more than a little clumsy).

            Deacon has to be a Ventrue. Arrogant, doesn't like to be told what to do (seriously, the chore wheel).

            Also, "You will see nothing unusual!" has to count as Dominate.

            Several other minor characters abound across the spectrum of archetypes, too. (Plus there are the zombies, the witches, and the s̶w̶e̶a̶r̶—er, werewolves!)

                If I were to draw parallels between the Warhammer fantasy bloodlines and the ones in NWOD it would probably go something like this. Of course the clans in nwod are fleshed out a bit more than their initial appearance so given more time I would probably change my mind but this is it at a glance.

                Von Carsteins - Ventrue : Lordly vampires that use their powers to dominate men and beasts to serve them. A good example of this is Vlad Von Carstein

                Lahmian - Deva : Manipulative socialites who get by on lies and charm. An somewhat more sympathetic example being Genevieve Dieudonne

                Necrarch - Mekhet/possibly Nosferatu : A bit more skeletal than your average Mekhet but personality wise they are quite similar being mystics with a love for darkness. Neborhest from Warhammer Online is basically the poster boy for these guys.

                Strigoi - Nosferatu: Monstrous, extremely violent and really not funny at all unless getting ripped to pieces by an undead monstrosity and its diseased ghoul servants is your idea of a good time. I can't think of any prominent members of the bloodline but I am sure you get the idea.

                Blood Dragons - Gangrel : Fighters who basically are locked in combat with their inner beast the founder managed to get himself some dragons blood and had somewhat of an ascension. Walach Harkon is a particularly badass member of this bloodline.
