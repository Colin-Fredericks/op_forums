                                                No. It doesn't void it, since acquisition of covenant unique doesn't require you to pledge sole membership to get the relevant merit or magic, just the relevant status rank. And in many cases, that can be ignored. Your "primary covenant" is the one with the most status dots. Obviously if your character's primary covenant is for example, the Circle of the Crone, your character wouldn't be trying to obtain higher status in the Carthian Movement than whatever their Acolyte status is.

                                                Right of Return does not count your Carthian Movement covenant status for purposes of determining the maximum allowable status in other covenants when holding status across multiple covenants is a thing. And it allows you a slightly higher impression than a suspected spy would normally hold.

                                                A 3 Carthian / 3 Invictus character is normally not allowed because you can't have equal or scattered through all of highest covenants.

                                                A 2 Carthian / 4 Circle of the Crone status is sort of the scenario the merit is meant for, a highly influential Acolyte dabbling in the Carthian Movement who has Right of Return is less likely to be stripped of their position than a 3 Circle of the Crone 1 Invictus member.
