          Your clan disciplines are in-clan until you join a bloodline. Once you join a bloodline, anything not in that bloodline's new list counts as out of clan. Yes in theory you could max out the discipline that is going to fall off when inducted into the bloodline at in clan costs but that is really contingent on the ST. If the character is embraced straight into the bloodline it may not make sense. But if the character is required to prove their worth before joining it may be a sort of unspoken requirement before being initiated.
