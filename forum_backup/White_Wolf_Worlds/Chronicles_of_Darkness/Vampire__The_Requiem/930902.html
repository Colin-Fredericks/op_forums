    Baltimore: The Kiddie Pool Saga (Actual Play) Part 9

    07-13-2016, 12:29 PM
    The Kiddie Pool Saga Continues!

    Previous Chapters and Character Portraits can be found here [LINK]


    A Little Back Story

    1. A long time ago, I traded Caroline Key (deava, neonate) whom I owned as a slave to Madeline Ross (daeva, invictus, ancilla, master of Elysium) for the right to open an official city Elysium. We have been working hard to bring this plan to fruition. Christina (ventrue, my cousin) has told us that she will soon be ready to for the grand opening.

    2. Additionally, we have a business arrangement with Kat (gangrel, carthian, neonate) in which Alice and I will provide 50% of the cash for a cosplay nightclub which Kat will run in our territory. Kat gets to do the fun cosplaying that she likes to do, Alice and I get access to what we hope will soon be a good feeding ground. This too, is almost ready to open.

    3. Currently, our coterie is in a contest of wits and politics with several other coteries over who will get the biggest piece of the pie in the Locust Point Industrial area. Our biggest competitors come from a coterie which includes the following vampires:


    Doris Bettes (deava, carthian, neonate)
    Reaper (daeva, carthian, neonate, former hound)
    Pedro (daeva, crone, neonate)
    Pascal Stitcher (mekhet, carthian, neonate)
    Jerome James (nosferatu, carthian, neonate, former hound.

    Doris is the youngest member of the group, as well as one of the most influential carthians in the city. Why? Michael Seville has given the responsibility of divvying up feeding rights within carthian territory, and this territory is vast.

    Reaper had recently hatched a scheme which lead to the breakup of my cousin Darren Soderberg's coterie.


    Gentleman's Agreement

    So, knowing that Reaper is on a mean streak I decided to talk with Doris. We met at the city Elysium (museum) downtown on the Prince's land. Doris and I head to courtyard where we can have a little privacy. I explain to Doris that our coterie will soon be opening an Elysium, as well as a nightclub and that I hope that civilized vampires like us would be able to come to some agreement to prevent interference with these enterprises. Doris replies that she no intentions of messing with these projects whatsoever.

    As we are talking, I notice that Reaper is watching us from a window. I get the idea that he is trying to read our lips and he doesn't seem happy. Well, I didn't think his recent slut shaming campaign was Doris' idea, and when taken with his current demeanor led me to believe there might be a schism within their coterie. So, when Reaper leaves Elysium, Alice and I follow him.

    We wind up having a conversation on an empty metro car where Alice and I explain the same things we explained to Doris, and ask him for the same courtesy.

    Reaper: "Sheesh... You think this land is your God given right, don't you."

    It doesn't sound like we are going to get Reaper's cooperation.


    Bartolomew

    Bartolomew is a member of both Clan Daeva and of the Ordo Dracul. He is of ancilla age and Sworn of the Mysteries. His rank is equal to that of my Uncle Quintus, and when my Grandsire eventually descends into torpor Bartolomew will take the reins of the Order. All that is fine and good, but his claim to fame is that he is Baltimore's Vintner. If you are drinking blood wine in Baltimore, either you are drinking Bartolomew's wine or you are drinking import.

    When the game began, Bartolomew was in torpor. Sleeping across the Chesapeake Bay in what is now the Quarantine Zone. During those earlier chapters, Alice and I freed his childe Mathilda (supplicant of hunger) from an unpleasant blood bond, and defended his haven from would be diablerists/stealers of secrets. Bartolomew showed his gratitude by helping Alice and I to capture the freedom of Aika and Aiko (daeva twins.)

    So, one fine evening Alice and I were in the chapter house and Bartolomew appears and asks us to have a word. His demeanor is warm and welcoming.

    Bartolomew: "I have heard that the two of you are Marshalls for the city and that you are doing good work. I have a small problem and I would like you to look into it. A substantial amount of my wine has gone missing. I believe it has been stolen."

    Alice and I agree to find the culprits and are on our way.


    Neighborhood Watch

    One night we are visiting Antoni (my adopted cousin, reeve of the city, bailiff of Seville.) Among other things, Antoni tells us that there was a utility truck in the vicinity of our haven. He tells us that he checked into it, and that no trucks were scheduled to be in the area by the city utilities. Well this is of some concern to us, but there is a limit to what we can do about it after the fact.

    I decide to visit my across the street neighbors: a married couple and their two high school aged sons. After a conversation with the wife, I convince her of the importance for neighborhood security and she agrees to keep an eye out and a camera ready for strangers in the area. She even goes a step further by revealing that she is active with the Parent Teacher Association and can start a proper neighborhood watch in the area. This might be problematic later, but right now I need eyeballs.


    Indulgence

    A few chapters back I was granted a transferable Indulgence to sire a childe in the city of Baltimore. At the time I was confused by the fact that I was given this award and not Alice. Since then I have realized what should have been immediately obvious. Alice is descended of the Prince. If she was interested in creating a new vampire and had a candidate in mind, she would be granted permission.

    Anyway, one night we are hanging out with the other members of our coterie (Christina, my cousin, and Susan Small, my youngest cousin.) We let slip the news that I am in possession of an Indulgence and that I have no intention of using it anytime soon. After all, I have a boatload of responsibilities.

    I do, however, want it used. Why? To settle once and for all the debate about what to do with foreign Ventrue in Baltimore I want a baby-boom. I know the Prince will allow only so many of each clan to gain citizenship. If I can get my family to sire a bunch of Ventrue and then see them through the trials, then I will no longer need to worry about random Ventrue who arrive in Baltimore messing up our sweet racket. After all, the Prince will not allow an unlimited number of Ventrue to gain citizenship.

    Christina wants it. She says she already has a candidate for the embrace and that if I give her the Indulgence she will embrace her that very night. We ask Christina who this person is, and she tells us that her candidate has been a ghoul of Octavia's for the past 30 years.

    Well this is very interesting. When we ask her why she wants to embrace she talks about how she will have someone who will be with her always. They can work together and play together; and if it some to it the childe can help protect Christina. Basically, she wants a BFF/bodyguard. I'm not so sure this is a great idea. In my opinion, siring is a great responsibility. There are no vampires in House Von Berg who I would describe as sub-par. So, it is the responsibility of the sire to train the childer to be awesome and make the family stronger.

    After Christina leaves, Susan makes the case as to why we should instead give the Indulgence to her. She tells us that she too has a candidate picked out and that if we give her the Indulgence she will embrace her that very night. When we ask who her candidate is, Susan tells us that it is the very same girl who Christina plans to embrace. WTF? It's always something with those two.

    Susan: "Wait! Hear me out. 30 years ago, Christina was not the only one Octavia was vetting for the embrace. In the end, Christina won and got the embrace. The other girl was the runner up and was made into a ghoul by Octavia. Imagine having to assume the role of loyal servant to Octavia while watching Christina get everything that you wanted for 30 years. The ghoul doesn't like Christina, and if she is made vampire by Christina I can see only bad things at the end of that road. The ghoul (I'm sorry I can't remember her name right now) will hate Christina and Christina will be miserable. If I embrace her, Christina will be mad at me, but at least Christina will not be hated by her own childe."

    Wow. Susan really cares about Christina. All this is academic though. In an earlier chapter at one of our famous family dinners, my Grandsire Konrad said "... Octavia will be the next to embrace." And I'm pretty sure he meant it.

    So, to make a long story short: We convinced Octavia that the time was right for her to make a new childe. Octavia will soon embrace the ghoul that both Christina and Susan had planned to embrace. And Bonus: She was already in possession of an Indulgence to do so.


    Nepotism and Politics

    The Order has been growing and it doesn't take a genius to see that it will grow into the shape of a pyramid. Alice and I want to be as near to the top of that pyramid as we can. Furthermore, Alice and I want to be inducted into the Sworn of the Dying Light. Currently there is only one blue dragon in Baltimore: my uncle Quintus. If I am correct and the Order is going to grow into the shape of a pyramid, then it is not a stretch to assume that there are limited seats available. People who have been paying attention to previous chapters would know that Susan Small is something of a wunderkind when it comes to Coils and to Politics. She would likely be a welcome addition to the Dying Light or to the Mysteries. What might be less apparent is that Luis (mekhet, scribe of the curse) has been working for Quintus as his apprentice since the very beginning of the game.

    One scenario I have considered is one in which Susan, Alice and I all take the Blue Oath. Luis is passed over for promotion and becomes bitter. He might even become an enemy. It is also possible that either Alice or I , but not both would be promoted. That's not great either. So, I made a deal with Susan. I would give her the Indulgence to Sire, and she would pursue membership in the Sworn of the Mysteries. This will (hopefully) leave the path clear for Alice and me, while not edging out a longstanding and loyal member of the Order from reaching for glory.


    Break In

    Alice and I are walking down the street in the Inner Harbor one night when we are approached by Miles (daeva, neonate, carthian.) Miles is an immigrant from Council Territory, as well as the leader of a mostly daeva coterie. Miles tells me that he might have gotten himself into some trouble and he is wondering if I might help him. he won't speak on the street, and asks us to join him at his haven. We follow him westward and he leads us out the inner harbor and into the territory owned by Circle of the Crone. It is not too far from the inner harbor, and due to the inexact nature of the maps that are given to residents of the kiddie pool, it is entirely possible that Miles is unaware he is out of bounds.

    Miles's haven is a basement apartment (they are popular with vampires.) He leads us inside and we see the following:

    1. Trenton (daeva, carthian, QZ resident) is lying on the floor, apparently in torpor.
    2. Fishy (daeva, unaligned, QZ resident) has a collar around her neck with a chain leading to a metal loop built into the concrete floor.

    According to Miles, he arrived home one night to find Trenton and Fishy inside his haven uninvited. This lead to fighting, which lead to Trenton's current torpor. Miles thinks the two were there to steal his bloodwine, which he had recently purchased from Patrick (mekhet, QZ resident.)

    Well, this seems to be a rather sticky legal issue:

    1. We are not actually in the kiddie pool right now, therefore I have no legal jurisdiction. My beat includes the kiddie pool and the lands of Michael Seville.
    2. It is in fact a no-no to send vampires into torpor without permission.
    3. Ordinarily, the fact that there were uninvited guests in the haven would be enough invalidate that rule. I don't think there are any judges in Baltimore who want to set a precedent that one must pull their punches when defending their haven.
    4. However, Miles shouldn't be making his haven here in Crone territory. It is possible that his haven is not a legal haven and therefore does not have legal protections.
    5. And then there is the wine, which is likely stolen.

    Sigh... I am le tired. But no time for a nap. Miles seems keen on turning himself in, but I advise him not to. Although this dispute is above my paygrade, I tell him his best bet is to make sure Trenton makes it safely out of his torpor and then tell him to piss off. I also advise him to hold off on drinking the wine, as it may be stolen.

    Before we leave, Miles informs us that there will be a meeting of some kind at the Jiffy Lube turned hangout in the Quarantine Zone, and asks us if we would be willing to attend. I neither accept nor decline at this time, although I am not too keen to go. The QZ is getting more and more dangerous of late.


    H2O

    One fine evening I begin the way I usually do: with a quick shower and breakfast from the pantry. Alice and I are both scholars of hunger in the Ordo Dracul, so we are used to holding back our hunger. However, this night when we feed we find the blood less nourishing than usual. WTF? We are cool kickass dragons. Our knowledge of the coils should not allow this to happen. We will have to look into this.

    But first: Have I mentioned lately how crowded our house is lately? Sleeping under our roof are the following people:

    1. Alice and Felix
    2. Aika and Aiko (deava, property of Alice and Felix)
    3. Sherry (daeva. we are grooming her to make amends to Appolonia for Aika and Aiko)
    4. Michael Seville's potential childer. (we are grooming him for the embrace for Michael Seville)
    This is in addition to our ghouls and a few members of our herd at any given time. Our haven is large (due to the largesse of our patron Octavia - and experience points), but it is not *that* large.

    Anyway, one night we are hanging out at home socializing with some guests when Michael Jr. tries to get my attention. I initially brush him off, but he is insistent. "We have a problem."

    He hands me a glass with a little bit of milk left over after drinking. Floating in the milk are swirls of red and pink. Blood. He thinks he has been poisoned. So, I call my sister Felicity and ask her to bring her ghoul the coroner. They arrive and the coroner gives a physical to Seville Jr and all the other non-vamp residents of our house. He takes blood samples, as well as samples from around the house including food and water.

    A day later he arrives and tells us that our water supply has been poisoned with a very small dose of a chemical (I can't remember it's name) that will lead to a slow decline and eventual death for the humans in our household who drink it. So, we call a plumber. The plumber arrives and inspects everything. Under the house, he finds a gas tank with a regulator has been attached to the main water pipe. So, we pay the plumber and he fixes the problem. We also borrow some cash in order to install a really good reverse osmosis water filtration system. Hopefully we will not have to worry about our water supply again.

    While the workmen are present, I visit my neighbor across the street. She is in her front room with a video camera, making a recording of the plumber, his vehicle, and its license plate. Our game is set in the 80s, so her camera is rather large. I drop by to let her know he is here at my invitation and ask her to delete her recording (with a little dominate.)

    This is a really devious plan. In the short term, our ghouls would not notice that anything was wrong. Their ghoul healing would keep them feeling alright. Our blood dolls are used to feeling under the weather, and probably wouldn't say anything even if they did notice so as not to be kicked out and denied the kiss. Poisoning our ghouls, poisoning our food supply, poisoning us. And what's worse, this isn't the usual half-baked bullshit I'm used to seeing. Whoever thought this up was smart.


    Frame Job

    In light of recent events, Alice and I decide that we will in fact attend the gathering at the Jiffy-Lube. Perhaps it will give us an opportunity to look into Bartolomew's missing wine or, more importantly, look into who has decided to poison our household.

    Because Alice and I have grown rather paranoid, our standard operating procedure is to travel at all times under cloak of obfuscate and survey our destination before deciding how to proceed. We also both have mastered Obfuscate, meaning we can take additional backup wherever we go. So, we travel invisibly accompanied by Aika and Aiko. If there is to be violence this night, we will make the aggressors regret it.

    Anyway, we arrive in the small courtyard which is bordered by the self storage business and includes the butcher shop where Cooper, the almoner, peddles his wares. Also nearby is the Jiffy-Lube, which Miles (daeva, carthian) and his coterie have tried and failed to create an elysium. We appear from a nearby alley and approach Cooper for some conversation.

    Cooper: "Fewer and fewer people are coming here each night, but I can see no evidence of poaching in the area. At least, no more than usual."

    Hmm... Perhaps the wine thieves are living large in this area. As I am pondering this, there is a scream coming from the direction of the self storage. The four of us run in that direction and find the following:

    1. Piper Morgan (daeva, carthian) is standing over the body of her ghoul.
    2. The body of her ghoul is laying on his back, with a knife buried in his chest.

    Piper looks up, sees us and asks: "Why...?" In a previous chapter, I wrote about the suicide of Piper's previous ghoul. While he was not underfed with vitae, he was underfed with respect to the attentions of his regnant. In the wake of that disaster, Piper asked Kade (gangrel, dragon, ancilla) to help her find a good candidate to be a ghoul. Well, he did, and she really likes him. And she is not shy about it.

    Piper is starting to cry when Vadic Imamu (gangrel, crone) steps up.

    Vadic: "I do not think he is dead." Vadic pulls the knife from the chest of Piper's ghoul and the wound begins to close. Piper falls on him, bites her wrist, and shoves it into his mouth. The knife that clatters on the ground is covered in blood. Its handle is wrapped in paracord and the blade is a japanese style tanto point. Both the handle and the blade very thick. In fact, the knife looks very much like the knives Alice and I use to stab people. After a few minutes Piper's ghoul awakens and when asked about his assailant, describes a woman who fits Alice's description including wearing her favorite dress.

    By this time a crowd has gathered. I look back and forth as Piper prepared to point a finger at Alice, and I feel the bloodlust growing inside Alice. Before we pass the point of no return I cow Piper with rather intimidating look and declaration that although the knife looks like the ones we use, it is in fact not so. I know this because I hand crafted our knives and that is not one of them. We are further able to calm her by making the point that we have nothing to gain by killing her ghoul. She agrees to let us help her find the real villain and punish them.


    The Soap Opera Continues

    Ever since the creation of Susan Small less than 10 years ago, her relationship with Christina has had a certain status quo: Susan is in love with Christina, and Christina loves it that Susan is in love with her, but does not reciprocate her feelings.

    In light of the fact that the city is getting more and more dangerous, Octavia (my aunt, Christina's sire) has grounded Christina. Christina is forbidden from leaving the house without some kind of escort that consists of more than just ghouls. So one night, we get a call from Christina asking for help. Christina has a lot of work to do in order to make final preparations for her Elysium. The problem she is having is that usually she travels with Susan and several of their ghouls. However, the last three times she has asked Susan to come out, Susan has been busy. Alice and I agree to talk to Susan. In the meantime, we assign Aika and Aiko to escort Christina on her nightly errands so she can get stuff done.

    When we catch up with Susan she tells us that she has been spending time with Betty (mekhet, neonate, invictus, Alice's sister.) Several chapters back, there was a rather interesting Halloween party. During the party, Susan shared a kiss with Betty, and it has been on her mind. Anyway, Susan and Betty aren't really even dating, just spending time together.

    Susan: "If there is no chance for Christina and I to be together like that, then I am willing to explore this new relationship with Betty and see where it goes."

    I can see Susan looking at me, waiting for me to tell her why she can't go with Betty. But I just don't have it in me. It would be hypocritical of me to deny Susan a shot at the kind of love Alice and I enjoy. Plus, Betty is a cool Mekhet. So, I tell Susan to go for it.

    Susan: "Really? I thought you would try to stop me. Listen... I know you are older than me and your mind powers are greater than mine. I just, really appreciate that you don't hold it over me. Don't use it on me. When I was younger, Quintus (Susan's grandsire, my uncle) used to do things to me, with Dominate... Your house has always been a safe place for me." There is hugging. Susan leaves and Alice speaks.

    Alice: "Are you sure you want to encourage that? I know my sister and I know what she was created to be. She will wrap herself around Susan and won't let go. She won't do it because she is being devious or plotting and scheming. She was chosen and created to go out and create a political marriage with someone. Don't think for a second that it was chance that brought Betty and Susan together on Halloween."
    Felix: "I know. And I'm OK with it. Coteries aren't forever. If Susan can find happiness and further cement the alliance between our two families, then I am happy."
    Alice: "You don't understand. Just as I now spend most of my time with your family, Betty is likely to pull Susan in such a way that she will spend most of her time with mine."
    Felix: "I know. I will miss having Susan here so often, but this might be good for Susan, our families, and the City."


    We did inform Christina of the our conversation with Susan, as we said we would. She looked genuinely upset. Again, she likes having Susan follow her around like a lovesick puppy. It'll be interesting to see what she does.

    Lastly, we arrange an escort for Christina. As the days leading up to the grand opening of her Elysium seem like just the time for someone to make a move against us, we got her some bodyguards. Marco and Polo (daeva) and Sara (gangrel) are each paid a moderate boon to follow Christina around. If nothing happens, great. If something happens, the fee increases to major boon. Better safe than sorry.


    Hide and Seek

    One night Terry (mekhet, carthian) visits our haven. We have a little conversation and I propose a game. I challenge Terry to a game of Hide and Seek in which Mouse (Alice's ghoul) will hide and we both will seek. The first to find Mouse will win. If I win, Terry's ghoul Tigger (a former blood doll of ours) will spend a week with Alice and me in our service. If Terry wins, my ghoul Sidney will go with Terry for the week. Terry is very excited about the prospect and frantically searches the house for Mouse. I take a more methodical (slower) approach and in the end Sidney packs a bag to go live with Terry for a week. I am pleased.

    The other reason Terry was present is that Pilgrim is hosting another of his hunting parties and we are in need of tickets. The golden tickets are a hot commodity. In the end, Terry gives us her ticket in exchange for our promise that we will offer assistance to 6 carthians. You see, Terry spends a lot of her time just helping the lower ranking members of the carthian movement with various things (havens, feedings, etc.) Well, Alice and I could use some good PR with the carthians, so this is probably a win-win.

      Lance Kasson

      Lance Kasson is a Daeva Vampire of Ancilla age and a member of Invictus. He has one childe, Patricia Mason, who in turn has a fledgling, Nellie Stone. Nellie is in a coterie with Felicity Fox (my sister) and Kurt Hoffman (Michael Seville's childe.)

      Lance is also an independent regent whose loyalty is sworn directly to the Prince. His territory is the neighborhood of Otterbein, which borders the inner harbor (aka the Kiddie Pool.) Back in the 19th century, he was part of a coterie which included my sire Sextus and my aunt Octavia. I think Sharon Winter (Kade's sire) was in there too, but don't quote me on that. In past conversation with Octavia, she told me the Lance and Sextus were very close and fond of playing together using the combination of Dominate and Majesty on humans. I once asked Lance what kind of games they liked to play. Lance simply looked at me and said: "Use your imagination."

      So, one night I get a message that Lance Kasson wants my assistance. In the wake of Octavia's departure from the Invictus Darren Soderberg (my cousin) and Septimus (my uncle) have been working even harder than normal to maintain the family's influence in the invictus and the city.

      Anyway, what normally Lance would have asked Darren to do, he asked me to do. He had three people who he had picked up that night. I assume he used them for food and entertainment. Lance wanted me to take care of their memories for the night.

      Lance: "If you do, I'll grant you a trivial boon."

      A trivial boon? WTF!?! My skills are in no way trivial, and furthermore, it will take me most of the night to craft and implant believable memories into the minds of three different people. Nevertheless, I do manage to keep my inner monologue to myself and I agree to his terms. It would serve no purpose to piss Lance off. I and my family need all the allies and goodwill we can get.

      The rest of the night is spend retracing the footsteps of the mortals in question and creating a flowchart of events which I eventually turned into memories and planted into their brains. And let's not pretend that I do not enjoy taking over the free will of others. Find a job you love and never work again.


      Mandy O'Kelly

      Mandy O'Kelly is a neonate and a Daeva, as well as a Circle of the Crone Priestess. Her mastery of Cruac is impressive, but she has access to no other disciplines. We assisted her in her escape from the Kiddie Pool by preventing a ritual she was preparing for from being interrupted by the Lance. She broke a curse that had been levied upon her and impressed Appolonia (daeva priscus) with her impressive magic. She left the kiddie pool before and Alice and me, and ever since the two of us were granted citizenship Mandy has occasionally made overtures of joining our coterie.

      So far, we have declined her offer. I have told her that I do not think it would be fair to invite her to coterie knowing that 4 of the members are family (three cousins and a wife) meaning that if push came to shove, she would likely be the one left to die in a pinch.

      Well, times they are a changing. First, Susan's new relationship with Betty may grow into something that pulls Susan from our coterie. Second, and perhaps more important, we have faced and are likely to continue facing forced that have mystical backing. We will need our own witch to counter their magic. Finally, Mandy O'Kelly is a strategic asset in the city. We cannot let her remain without the protection of a Coterie.

      So, after some discussion with Alice, and then with Susan and Christina, the decision is made to invite Mandy O'Kelly into our Coterie. On the night in question, we selected our favorite blood doll, bathed her, scented her with perfumes, and draped her across the dining room table. She was offered to Mandy as refreshment before we retired to a sitting room to discuss our proposal.

      Mandy: "I am really surprised by your offer. After my overtures were rejected several times, I thought this door was closed to me."

      After some negotiation, she agrees. She will move into our now even more crowded haven so that our numbers will offer protection. She does not have a ghoul, but would like our help getting one for herself. Sure, she could just go out and blood bond one, but Alice's and my Auspex/Dominate combo will streamline the process of finding a suitable candidate quite a bit.


      Felix Fox School for Wayward Girls (and one boy)

      I think my biological clock is ticking. All this talk of siring childer by Christina and Susan is making me want to be a daddy. I think perhaps that is why I keep picking up strays. There is Sheri, the sole survivor of "the Dark Lord's" brood. I told her that she could keep her unlife, but she would have to accept my grooming and tutelage. Alice invades her mind often for any sign of betrayal, so I'm less worried about her than I would otherwise be.

      There is also the mortal I am supposed to be grooming for Michael Seville. The guy expected that his time with us would be an extended torture, but Michael asked Alice and me to make him like us. So, for my part I have been spoiling him rotten. Clothes, cars, money, women. I have been showering him with gifts and getting him used to the idea that humans can be used as tools or entertainment to satisfy whatever itch you need scratched. Why yes, Alice and I do have Humanity 4. Why do you ask? Oh, and we gave him a name. He didn't want to keep his name from his mortal life, and he didn't want to be the one to choose his new name. So, since he arrived here wearing a Metallica t-shirt on his first night, I named him Lars Hetfield. He didn't quite like that, so we went with Lars Bjornson. Shrug. It's a name.

      I also have been teaching him that these rewards are the result of hard work, and that when we are done playing with our toys we must put them away. In this context, I show him how I specifically seek out toys that won't be missed for a few days, and then reinsert them into their lives unaware of the truth.
