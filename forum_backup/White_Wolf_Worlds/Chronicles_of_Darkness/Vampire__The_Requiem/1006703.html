    WICKED DEAD - The Anthropophagi [homebrew]

    11-22-2016, 10:36 AM
    Last week I spend rediscovering Requiem, direct cause: the hunt after this flash film, I remembered from years ago. Took me half a fucking day to find it. But well worth it. Got inspired. Let me know what you think, please. THE ANTHROPOPHAGI


    TRUTHS

    Mythical: The classic historians had heard the hearsay about the Anthropophagi, Herodotus and Pliny the Elder both make mention of that Scythian tribe of monsters whose injust god allows them to feed on the bones of neighbouring peoples. They are the servants of the great wyrm Azi: soldiering in the wars against Mitra, whose covenant sets the bounds of humanity's natural law.
    Documented: This is serious: our lovely old neighbour is a cannibal-cultist. See, that's definetely her face in the picture. She even went by the same name. Just look: it's that same exact weird fucking smile of hers. Don't just assume she has these gross smoker's teeth to hide or else be body-shamed over. Listen to this: 'Annabel Brandt disappeared before she could be arrested by Dutch authorities for sabotaging resistance efforts in her role as a mole in service of the German.' Two policemen died in the attempt to stop her escape. 'Upon entering her residence, investigators uncovered the true horror of miss. Brandt's war-time activities.' She murdered at least ten people. The remains of the victims were found strewn about the basement floor, scattered at the feet of some occult deity. The place was a slaughterhouse. Fucking literally. They found blood and guts in the bathroom, a plate of man-jerky in the kitchen.


    Speculated: There are no official records of who exactly attended dr. Watts' talk. But better believe The Fields Projects Division had a representative there. It's what they do: they aquire some IP and then they make money off of it. Now the Men in Black deploy these highly effective monster hunters, who clean up after themselves like sounders of swine. You do the math: this shit might escalate quickly.
    Known: People from Record Keepers first picked up on it sometime in the early '00s. Initially most thought is was a gag, this leaked recording of a powerpoint presentation given by Canadian bio-molecular engineer dr. Peter Watts. It's a sales pitch, supposedly to a bunch of corporate exec.'s. Dr. Watts talks about resurrecting an extinct sub-species of the homo genus, using retro-viral gene therapies on the junk parts of the genomes of sociopaths. Supposedly, these cousins of ours were quite literally super human. To hunt other hominids they needed to be highly fucking intelligent predators and capable of tearing up an adult human being, limb from limb. The good doctor opined that such potential provides for some interesting commercial applications.


    Deductive: To understand the Machine, one must imagine what it wills, for even gods are forced by their nature to desire some things above others. This one wishes to be all there is to reality, to be that all encompassing, natural principle that rationalizes the world as it is, without recourse to flukes, the paranormal or other otherwordly forces. These creatures, they are geared out for the purpose of out-competing us.
    Inductive: In each case seven individuals commited these acts of horror. All forty-nine of them are dead, their bodies accounted for. Or so report the media. Yet the violence they've instigated continues to spread. And it is uncanny in its familiarity. We hear news of people turning: they hunt, they hide, they feed. They sleep, they shit. They breathe. They live through the day. To date, none of our contacts in the affected domains have responded to our letters. No matter how hard urgency was pressed. So we need to question. It cannot simply be that these targets of terrorism were chosen only to spread the fear of gods and fundamentalism amongst the kine. That cannot be anything but a the cover. The true reason lies deeper beneath the surface.


    RULES
    Like the humans they were, Anthropophagi are creatures of biology: they are alive still. However, there are some disquieting vestiges of the unnatural present in their make-up.
    • They are humans, albeit ones who are beyond good and evil. An Anthropophage determines Breaking Points with reference to Integrity. However, when it comes to restoring Willpower these creatures act according to Masks and a Dirges, instead of Virtues and Vices.
    • They are human vampires with a Blood Potency of 0. Though, for the purpose of Lashing Out an Anthropophage's Blood Potency is equal to the amount of Vitae it has in its system.
    • They are carnivors, who need a little something extra to nourish themselves. Every week an Anthropophage needs to consume at least 1 kilogram of human flesh, ofal, blood or marrow, that isn't converted to Vitae. If one goes a week without, the Anthropophage gains the Hungry condition. If the condition persists for another week or so, Hungry is replaced with the Famished condition. After three weeks without proper sustenance, the Anthropophage replaces Famished for the Starving condition.
    • They can feed on humans in excess of minimum dietary requirements. An Ahropophage's maximum pool of Vitae is equal to its Stamina, to produce 1 Vitae it needs to consume 1 kilogram of human tissue.
    • They can intensify their phsyiological abilities. An Anthropophage can spend 1 Vitae per turn, to either: receive a +2 bonus to a Mental skill for the rest of the scene; to receive a +2 bonus to a Physical attribute for the rest of the turn; or to gain the 9-again rule on a social maneuvering roll.
    • They hibernate. An Anthropophage may opt to resolve the Famished or Starving condition by slipping into a state of torpor, during which the ageing of its body slows down by a factor of 10. If a Starving Anthropophage wakes up after longer than a decade of hibernation, it rises Fanished, if it was Starving, and it rises Hungry, if it was Famished. To get into this energy-saving state, Anthropophagi need to have reserves: for every decade spend hibernating, an Anthropophagi must spend 1 point of Vitae. If it cannot, it awakens. To rise before, an Anthropophage needs to spend 1 Vitae and a point of Willpower.
    • They are nocturnal predators. An Anthropophage is less hindered by the absence of light. Only in pitch darkness do they suffer a -2 penalty to actions requiring visual perception. If one is exposed to a sudden source of bright light, the Antropophage rolls Wits + Composure. Upon failure apply the Blinded Tilt for a turn. A dramatic failure means the Tilt lasts until the end of the scene.
    • Their teeth are horrific, jagged and serrated. If an Anthropophage does not make a considerable effort, people might notice something very blatantly wrong the Anthropophage with a simple succes on a passive Wits + Composure check. When used as weapons their teeth deal +1 lethal damage.
    • They are infectious. Whenever a human is bit by an Anthropophage, the victim rolls Integrity:
      Dramatic failure: Violent seizures take hold of the victim's body, as it becomes infected. Within a number of minutes equal to the character's Resolve + Stamina they turn into an Anthropophage with the Hungry condition. The change is inevitable.
      Failure: The character becomes feverish and succumbs to a coma, during which their body progressively mutates. Losing that arm might still save them. Otherwise the character awakens as an Anthropophage after a number of days equal to their Resolve + Stamina.
      Succes: The character's system fights off the infection.
      Exceptional succes: Not only does the infection take no hold, but the character's sense of humanity is reaffirmed. They take a beat and regain a point of Willpower.
    • They may more eaily grow to the peak of raw human potential. Raising an Attribute costs an Anthropophage 3 Experiences per dot.
    • They may outgrow raw human potential. An Anthropophage may spend Experiences to raise 1 Power, 1 Finesse, and 1 Resistance Attribute to the 6th dot.
    • They aren't Kindred, though Storytellers may allow them to be uplifted. If so, a newly embraced Anthropophage has a Bloodpotency of 3 and a Humanity of 3. They retain their frightening +1l bite and the ability to consume 1 kilogram of human to replenish 1 point of Vitae. Vitae can no longer be spend by an Anthropophage to gain the 9-again rule on social maneuvres or to gain a bonus to the use of Mental skills. As rigor mortis sets in, the cost of Attribute dots is raised back to 4 Experiences. Any 6th dot in an Attribute only comes available to the neonate again if it reaches the required Blood Potency. Though, when embraced, the Anthropophage may choose to retain acces to any 6th dot by spending a dot of Humanity. Even amongst Kindred, the Anthropophagi are considered a corrupted and infectious lot. If they exsanguinate a mortal, roll their Humanity (without the +2 modifiers other Kindred would get). Failure means the victim rises as a Revenant. If one of their ghouls die, roll it's regnant Anhtropophage's Blood Potency +2. With a succes, the ghoul rises as a Revenant. Their bite no longer turns people into Anthropophagi.





    Condition - Hungry
    You have gone several days without any real nourishment, which makes you weak, unfocussed and irritable. As long as the Hungry Condition persists, your Attributes are lowered by 1 dot, to a minimum of 1.
    Possible sources: Fasting, food poisoning
    Resolution: You eat a nice big plate of food.
    Beat: If seeking out food sources causes you to roll for a Breaking Point.


    Condition – Famished
    It has been quite some time since you last ate. Your insides start to protest, your mind wanders, conjuring up the smells of your favourite foodstuffs. Until you resolve the Famished Condition your Attributes are lowered by 3 dots, to a minimum of 1. At each interval you roll Resolve + Stamina. Failure means you fall unconcious for a number of minutes equal to 15 – (Resolve + Stamina). Furthermore, damage you suffer no longer heals naturally.
    Interval: 3 hours
    Possible sources: Eating disorders, parasitic infection
    Resolution: You eat several good meals.
    Beat: Whenever your falling unconcious causes significant hindrance to attaining your own or your group's goals. If seeking out food sources causes you to roll for a Breaking Point.


    Condition - Starving
    It has been weeks, you probably don't even remember when last your stomach was filled. Your body has started to consume itself. All you can think of is food. As long as the Starving Condition endures your Attributes are lowered by 5, to a minimum of 1. You do not heal damage naturally. Any physical action requires a point of Willpower to be spend. And at each interval you roll Resolve + Stamina to stay conscious, dramatic failure means you also suffer a point of lethal damage.
    Interval: 3 hours
    Possible sources: Exceptional cruelty, famine
    Resolution: Medical treatment, over the course of days to slowly but surely increase your food intake.
    Beat: Whenever you suffer a point of damage due to starvation. If seeking out food sources causes you to roll for a Breaking Point.
