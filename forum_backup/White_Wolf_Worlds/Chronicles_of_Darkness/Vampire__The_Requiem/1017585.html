    Requiem and Masquerade

    12-15-2016, 07:24 AM
    What I like about Requiem: great Clans, fascinating Covenants, incredible diversity of individual Kindred in the published Citybooks

    What I don't like: Requiem needs more metaplot, more overarching conspiracies, more sinister elders hiding in the shadows


    Basically, I LOVE the fluff in Requiem, but I don't like the toolbox approach, and I wish it were more like Masquerade in underlying structure.


    P.S. As I always remind people when I post these sorts of posts, I am a girl who loves to cause drama, and I know that writing this will probably push SOMEBODY's buttons. Still, it is really how I feel, and if I ever did run Requiem, this is pretty much how I would do it.


    Colleen

    My name is Colleen. My favorite song is "Wildwood Flower." My ambition is to write the complete history of the White Wolf/Onyx Path universe.

      Well, you're out of luck if you want all those as official, published materials. CofD focuses more on the local and the personal, both geographically and historically. It favors flexibility over stability, and the internal over the external.

      Of course, overarching global conspiracies and uber-elders in the dark are all doable, as long as you work towards building them yourself. Metaplot is still out, because that's by definition something the developers and the writers make.

                                                  I feel like we see this thread every time we see buzz around new books. but anyways. Check out Chicago by Night one of the first source books for masquerade. It doesn't have a world shattering / conquest of conspiracy. It's a very basic elder vs elder from ancient Greece. Between Menele and Helena are the main pushers of the plot. And they play out their wars in Proxy characters. Thrown in with some romance between a few minor characters.

                                                  Which actually makes me think; That a lot of the vision or early intent of the material was a retelling of Greek/Roman gods, but with a dark gothic kick to it. I mean you have ancient and powerful nigh immortal beings dicking over modern 'heros' . I use the Terms heros not in the moral sense but in the larger then life character sense.

                                                  So getting back to the point: Metaplot is what you make it. I personally disliked the later gonzo world spanning my explosion is bigger than your spirit nuke setting shattering nonsense. but the early drama plays are golden material.
