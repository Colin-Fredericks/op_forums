        If "some guy/gal" would not attract attention simply by virtue of being there being "just a guy/gal", Face in the Crowd should work.

        It would work in a nunnery, assuming that you are NOT brandishing pentagrams (mostly for the "brandishing" part, you could have one painted on your face or something I suppose); but not in any situation in which any on the nuns would look at her sisters in the room and identify them all at a glance, because a that point they would go "well here we are for our turn in the kitchen, sister Mary, sister Clara, sister Madeline, sister SomeGal and me WAIT WHAT!?!".

        Another example. If Mr. Iam A. Touchstone and his 2 roommates invite to a party at their house 7 people each, many of which don't know each other, Iam's (un)dead Nosferatu cousin could easily slip inside by activating Face in the Crowd and nobody would notice there are 25 people instead of 24. He's somebody else's friend.

        If Iam invites five close friends for a fairly quiet poker night/ RPG session, on the other hand, his (un)dead Nosferatu cousin can't just stay in the room with them and make them believe he's "some player/gamer". There shouldn't be any "some guy" there, so he can't make them think he's one.

            I actually once ran with something between these examples; a single Obfuscate user carefully negotiating a houseful of disparate family members during Thanksgiving.

            The Obfuscate user was assumed to be just some relative who the victim wasn't very familiar with, unless direct observation or interaction was attempted. In a setting so intimate, getting the attention of a single person was immediately hazardous, but as long as you could skirt the edges of groups, keep moving purposefully long enough to avoid polite conversation, and avoid "look at us here all together, isn't this nice" moments, it was possible to dance from one side of the house to the other.

            I believe I handled it properly, but I'm certainly open to correction. I prefer to stay as RAW as I can with Disciplines.

                                                04-05-2016, 01:44 PM
                                                This is not by any means a complete list, but I would say the following would be drawing attention:

                                                Touching someone. ( like slipping your hand into your boyfriend's or tapping them on the shoulder. )

                                                Speaking directly to someone ( which would break it for that person )

                                                Addressing the crowd (which would break it for everyone )

                                                wielding a weapon(or object) in a threatening manner. ( Walking down the street with the riffle on your back or in your hands, but pointed down would not, but pointing at everyone who walked by, would.)

                                                Hitting someone.

                                                Being in a place where no one else is suppose to be. ( Private residence. The president's office. The bank vault. )

                                                Anything that makes a commotion. ( tripping and falling down the stairs )


                                                Otherwise, I believe most everything else you would be totally ignored.


                                                Remember when you are breaking it for just that one person or the victim everyone else gets :

                                                If he’s doing something that would draw attention, including but not limited to being violent, everyone around him must make a reflexive Wits + Composure roll to notice the commotion, penalized by the vampire’s Obfuscate dots
