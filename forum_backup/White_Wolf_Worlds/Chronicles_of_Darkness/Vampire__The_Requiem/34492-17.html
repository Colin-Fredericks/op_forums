    249) One of the biologicians of the Ordo has spent a century breeding a new type of Kine. Through eugenics and stolen blood magic he has created humans who can condense their blood like the Kindred do vitae. Each mortal holds double their total health boxes of blood. In addition, given plenty of water and enough food, the mortal can replenish his total blood supply in a few hours.
    The dragon who created these Kine only has three of them so far. He’s currently planning a large scale breeding project to make more.

        250) The local Methodist based hunter cell have new clean-cut middle-class members from outside of town who seem exceptionally vampire savvy. They noticed the Lance Sanctum Mekhet that monitors the group to make sure any vampires sloppy enough to gain hunter attention are quarantined for extermination, or disappear into a staked sleep of ages. The Mekhet was disturbed that they seem impenetrable to Auspex and saw him even through his Obfuscate. They left an envelope with a letter to the local Lance Sanctum claiming that they are God's judgement upon the local vampires who have fallen from their roles to frighten the wicked toward righteousness.

        Else where a Carthian gangrel recognizes one of the new hunters as their Lance Sanctum grandsire from a few few cities over, but that can't be can it? This is a living human, without the beast.

        In truth these new hunters are former Lance Sanctum Vampires from a radical sect in another town that have been transformed via Miracle into humans who are immune to vampire power and whose flesh, blood, and bone act as a Banes to all vampires. This is the aftermath of the below story line if not "properly cleaned up" in someone else's town.

        Originally posted by Pale_Crusader View Post
        248) An heretical branch of Lance Sanctum which ascribes to Radical Arminianism that transcend apparent damnation, and is in fact able to enact miracles that cure the Curse. The Orthodox Lance Sanctum wants to burn the heretics at the stake, the circle of the crone are just as deeply offended, and the ordo Dracul want to practice vivisection and the dissection on those performing and receiving the miracles. Frighteningly, for all three factions and perhaps all vampire-kind, those restored to mortality seem to have their flesh, blood, and bone act as a Banes to all vampires and after their transformation are immune to all further vampiric powers. Could this actually be salvation, or is something sinister going on?

            251) A Ventrue of the Ordo Dracul has finally found a way to completely tame the Beast through a custom devotion. It channels the psychic energy of the id and redirects into the surrounding area, leaving the Beast calm.
            Of course, violence has spiked in the city. People are becoming more and more paranoid, rarely leaving their homes. In fact, the Ventrue has a haven in a part of the city where people have begun to turn…feral.

            252) A powerful ghost (or spirit if you prefer) offers a potential solution to a city afflicted by the Strix. The ghost will not only aid the Kindred with identifying the possessed, it will also devour any weakened owl that the Kindred bring to it. Of course everyone sees the parallel with diablerie and many of the wiser vampires worry what creature might be created from this process.

                253) Among the local Circle of the Crone, one of the Fools takes their appointed role a little bit too exuberantly. Styling themselves after the infamous Loki, they have decided to bring Ragnarok (death of the gods) to the rest of the domain. Unfortunately the Circle of the Crone philosophy is no stranger to portraying vampires as a form of deity, which means that this Fool is basically planning to eradicate all kindred in the city, using whatever symbolic representation of fire giants he can get his hands on. It goes without saying that they must be stopped; but how does one locate a powerful warlock with a passion for trickery?

                                                    263) A young but influenceful Dragon has asked an unusual boon of the Prince, to be named Regent of the sun lit city. He offers to keep an eye on the city by day and warn of any dangers he finds in return for hunting rights and the authority to act as he sees best in the absence of any other Kindred authority, something he has been doing anyway.

                                                    264) A group of influenceful Kindred announce that they require the procurement of a Mage, a competent fighter, a thief and a man of the cloth (or suitable substitutes). Please arrange for their investigation free disappearance and all other such matters. Prior to their disappearance they should all meet in a tavern.

                                                    265) A Dragon has discovered a new Scale.
                                                    Invigorating Heartblood.
                                                    Prerequisite Coil: The Vast Dynasty.
                                                    Procedure: The Dragon extracts blood directly from his heart using a pump and his own will. Atempting the prosess courses one Agrivated Damage and each roll costs one Willpower.
                                                    Roll Resolve+Stamina with a target number of 5 but subtract 1 die for each roll after the first, for each success one Vitai is extracted by the pump, when extracted the Vitai lasts one night pre point of Blood Potency of the vampire and losses one point of Blood Potency an hour. The vampire does not have to use all his successes and most stop at 5 Vitai.
                                                    Outcome: Known uses so far include: when injected into the heart of a Revrent roll Blood Potency and uplift as an embrace would on a pass, if fed to a human or ghoul it grants the ghoul state for a number of months equal to Blood Potency with no other costs after which the ghoul may mentain the state as normal, when injected into the heart of a vampire in torpor the vampire will awaken on a success on a roll of combined Blood Potency of the the sample and the torpored vampire, finally the sample is still Vitai but does not course Blood Bond or Addiction and allows anyone who drinks it to learn Disciplines as though they were being trained by the doner for a number of months equal to Blood Potency.
                                                    The possibility of granting loved ones immortality without Vinculum has attracted the intrest of many young Kindred and some Elders have also signalled their intrest in sampling this product.
                                                    (I know this doesn't look impressive but it's very flexible so I don't want to make it too powerful)

                                                        266) Tonight the diablerized corpse of a popular Ancilla and friend of the Prince is found on the stage of an abandoned theater. He is seated in a chair identical to the one favored by the Prince in Elysium and wearing stolen clothes belonging to the Prince. His long dead skin shows its age in fragility. It sloughs off in a shower of pale motes at the slightest hint of a draft to reveal shriveled, dark muscle and sinew. His fangs are broken in half with bright red rose petals filling the gaping mouth. The fragile body is blindfolded and wrapped loosely in barbed wire. The wire is rigged to support the withered right hand and hold up an old fashioned scale. A goblet full of an unidentified Kindred's blood sets on one side balancing a bowl full of old silver coins. The name of a Kindred in the city has been written on the face of each coin and "innocent" or "guilty" is on the back. A letter addressed to one of the player's characters is tied to the other hand.
