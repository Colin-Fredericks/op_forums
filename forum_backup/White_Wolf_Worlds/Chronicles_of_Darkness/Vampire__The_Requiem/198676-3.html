                    Ok, my two cents.

                    You want to let your PCs change a little over time when the players want? That's fair, get 1 Willpower point from them.

                    You want to make your PCs be stuck in the same form forever? That's equaly fair, them just DON'T LET THEM.

                    You want to make it hard, but not to simply forbid? Argue with them, make them roll hard, limit it in every narrative way you can before actualy letting them do it. And when you let, just get 1 Willpower point from them.

                    DON'T ASK FOR DOTS. That's stupid, sorry. Too steep a cost for too little reward, you will just punish your players for having the idea without actualy letting them do the thing.

                    This question really have NOTHING to do with if the vampires can or should have signs of growth and change. That's your opinion, and is a valid one, of course. But to actualy make players pay with XP or similar resources for such isn't a good idea. It serves only to charge too much or what IS a minor effect, doesn't matter how hard it should be in-setting.
