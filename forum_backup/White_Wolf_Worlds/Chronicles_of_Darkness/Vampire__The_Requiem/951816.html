    Seeking counsel for an upcoming game i'll run.

    08-16-2016, 01:31 PM
    Particularly concerning one of my players character.

    So the context first:
    I'm running a vampire the requiem set in Miami in the 80s. Since it will be the first vampire game i run i'm keeping it fairly standard in regards with the covenants. Of note is the fact that vampires are finding out that the networks and hooks they've cultivated in mortal society are being subverted, replaced or removed. Tensions are flying high and there are rumors aplenty on who is doing all that.

    As for the character in question:
    He is mostly defined by three stats.
    1. He has the merit ressources at 5. The money comes from the characters father, but my player left to me to decide who the father is. I've decided that the father is from old family established in miami from a long time and that presently he is an important politician (state senator or something). He is firmly keeping his son at arms length and basically just gave him money instead of taking care of him. Which leads me to point 2.
    2. He has 1 resolve. Which translated in a pretty severe addiction to drugs. Through which the character's sire met him.
    3. He has 5 intelligence but very few points in mental skills. A good way to represent the character is 'wasted potential' (which i love)

    The character was embraced by a gangrel and, like his sire, joined the carthian tho he is not really active.

    So my concerns are threefold:

    1. The player didnt put any point in the safe place merit. He argued that with ressources 5 his father paid a place for him. I explained that since he didnt take the merit he was at risk of someone walking in during the day and that people who knows him knows where he lives and he is okay with that.
    - Should i have him attribute a few of his points of ressources to temporarily gain the safe place merit???

    Also i was wondering, the rest of my players all took the safe place merit but not the haven merit. Is it wrong???

    2. The Sire-Childe relationship is predicated on the fact that the sire keeps the character for access to his money. Really the sire would not have embraced him at all but an overdosed forced his hands.
    To encourage conflict with the sire i'm thinking of having the player temporarily loses access to some or even all of his ressource merit when, for exemple, the sires takes his credit card for a little bit.
    Is that to harsh??? After all the player took that merit to use it and I would be constantly taking it from him (even if only temporarily).

    3. Finally the fact that ultimately the ressources merit is not in the control of the character makes me feel that eventually there will be a conflict where the character loses his 5 dots of ressources (althought he will be free to reallocate the points elsewhere). I'm just unsure on the best way to approach this. I mean it wouldnt happen at the start of the chronicle but there will come a time when either the characters connection with his family is deemed a threat to the mascarade or something can also happen on the mortal family side of thing to.
    So i was wondering if you guys had any idea on what to do with that and also how you dealt with characters losing merits they had while mortal.


    I appreciate the help.

    Completed campaign: Scion 2nd Edition. Les Légendes Currently playing: Being a dad for a 3 year old daughter and a 2 years old son and now a beautiful new baby.

      For the Safe Place / Haven issue, I'd allow a no-point build to still be colored by his high resources. I'd make it a fancy-shmancy mortal run condo who's staff is notorious for looking the other way regarding their elite clients' lifestyles. This translates into even the security guards looking the other way unless the police are actively involved. Maybe layer it so that some residents pay a 'premium' to have security, while some residents do not. If the player wants security mechanics, he can buy the merits, OR - I think the Goods/Services section for Equipment Bonuses lists private security guards. He can spend his resources dots as normal to hire one. I'd charge a two-for-one conversion if he wanted to trade Resource for Safe Place dots each Chapter.

      Re: not taking haven - I've seen this as well. Haven is an easy area to skimp on when you are a starting character, and with Safe Place being a speed-bump/requirement for haven, it can seem double expensive compared to other Merits. I've massaged this issue by adding a little oomph to the Haven Merit in my game. Each Kindred has the right of domain in their personal Haven. They only get this right if they've bough the Merit.

      For looking at taking away resources, just make sure to frame it in terms of opportunities. If the player could use some help and hasn't figured out an angle, the Sire could show up looking for money in exchange. No money, no simple solution. Alternatively, if they are buying the Sire as a Mentor, you tend to have to maintain that connection somehow. Cash can be that connection.

      But yes, I think your instinct that consistently taking away a Merit could be harsh is right. Would you arbitrarily take away combat or style Merits from any physical characters? If not, be aware that you're devaluing Mortal Merits from the others. And in this case, it seems something easily written into a background without a mechanical effect. The Sire rescued the Childe to keep that behind-the-scenes cash flowing, and it does, but behind the scenes. Maybe there was even an agreement that the Childe's family trust (once the rich parents are dead) would be turned over whole-hog to the Sire down the road - lots of possibilities.

      Are you worried the Resources merit is going to cause problem for plots down the road?

                  1. No you shouldn't allow resources to function as a safe place. It devalues the merit for the players who did allocate points for safe place. The resources 5 could be an excuse for him to have the merit safe place but it does not afford him the benefits.

If he decides he wants to keep the merit, he should be taking steps in game to either keep the merit or rebuild his fortune on his own.

                      1. In the same way that it is assumed a character with 0 dots of Resources has enough money to keep themselves fed and their bills paid, but has no disposable income beyond that, it is assumed that a character with 0 dots in Safe Place has a roof over their head, but it isn't secured well enough to penalize any attempts to break in. He might have a basic lock on his door or something, but nothing that will really stop a committed trespasser. And having Safe Place but no Haven is fine. Basically, think of Safe Place as how well protected the place is against intrusion - locks, alarms, traps, etc. and Haven as how well suited the place is for a vampire to sleep in. A vampire's Safe Place with 0 dots of Haven is assumed to have a dark corner somehere they can put a coffin in and sleep through the day in without getting ashed, but hasn't been sun-proofed well enough to give them a bonus to rolls to remain awake during the day, and they haven't properly attuned the space to their predatory nature to gain a bonus to Kindred Senses or Perception rolls to notice danger.

                      2. Rather than take Resources dots away, you could have him find that his alloted once-per-chapter 5 dot Availability purchase has already been spent, or that his effective Resources for this month is 4 because his sire overspent. I don't think that's too unreasonable as long as you don't do it all the time.

                      3. Sanctity of Merits pretty much has you covered there. Let's say he is forced to cut ties with his family in the interest of protecting the Masquerade. He might be afforded an extra dot of Clan, Covenant, or City Status as important vampires are more willing to trust him. Or maybe a powerful Carthian admires his decision and decides to act as his Mentor to help ease him more into vampire society. Maybe he keeps his family at arm's length but doesn't want to completely alienate himself from them, so he checks in on them from time to time; he can no longer lay claim to the family fortune, but he's gained a new Touchstone or two. Or, it could be entirely meta and just give him Parkour or something. I wouldn't go that route with it, though. Actually what I would do is let him choose where he wants the Merit dots reallocated to, and ask him to explain how it happened.
