                                        It is complicated so I might not be explaining it well. It has been the case from the beginning of Requiem that the werewolves in Requiem might or might not be the werewolves in Forsaken. You can stat werewolves using Requiem mechanics and some hand-waiving, which is the default situation unless you play a Requiem/Forsaken game. They are completely compatible so you can indeed play a combined game of Requiem and Forsaken, but that is not the default. They actually don't worry much about cross-over. They don't try to make vampires and werewolves balanced in any way. If you want to have both in your game by using both lines then you can balance them how you want. They leave it to a group that wants to do it. Vampires are balanced. Werewolves are balanced. Everything is balanced within its own line, but not balanced to other lines. It's hard to explain. Forsaken doesn't exist if you play Requiem unless you want it to exist. They write as if it does, not as if it must. They went for an interesting approach. It is a bit different than Classic.
