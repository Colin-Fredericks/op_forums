                                        Originally posted by Maskah View Post

                                        It might not be, but you are stealing a part of ones soul none the less. The reality of the coil is that it is very campy with conflicting themes.

                                        I think it would be much better suited to giving you a roll with a bonus to humanity checks from Diablarie and making it harder to detect that you do that sort of thing, or maybe even be able to get rid of conditions associated with the act.

                                        Locust was evil for sure, but unlike Ziva it had a very clear theme.

                                        Ziva is a cheap cop-out to have your cake and eat it to and needs a serious re-work to have it's theme align better. It absolutely does not follow the kind of theme progression that existing B&S Coils follow.
                                        Actually, it does have a pretty obvious theme progression in my view - one of forcibly clawing one's way back to humanity. The second tier involves spending blood, a symbolic representation of your damnation, to stay closer to humanity. The 4th tier involves forcing others closer to the Beast so you can become closer to the Man. The 5th is a brief return to the Man.

                                        The potential problem is a character abusing the mechanical benefits of the Coil to go on sprees of rampant diablerie (or other particularly villainous acts) while using the 2nd and 4th tiers to stop his humanity from falling significantly. The easiest way to discourage this would be to cap Buttress the Soul at doubling the -base- dice pool (per p.107 of B&S) and make Siphon the Soul a Humanity 2 detachment roll. This would mean Buttress the Soul wouldn't help you if you were committing mass murder or drinking people's souls, but would help you a lot with having selfish thoughts.
