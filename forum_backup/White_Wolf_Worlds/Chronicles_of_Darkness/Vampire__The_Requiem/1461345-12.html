            I don't think bloodlines should be removed, and I definitely don't think they should be unbound from blood, but I do think they should be narrowed down and focused into actual bloodlines that maintain the themes of ancestry and blood and lineage over the eons one would expect from Vampires rather than obscure quirks and weird variants.

            There is a game called Artesia that has a lineage ruleset where you get stats and bonuses from your ancestry and can also, when suitably powerful, add to that lineage/bonus for your own offspring.

            Something like that would be nice, where you get bonuses from your Sire (players could maybe choose a bonus for this much like some splats choose a bonus for their Clan-equivalent) and can, upon reaching a certain BP, enhance or modify their lineage for their own Childer. I don't really think they need all these special mechanics unique to each bloodline like the Kerberoi, neat as these special mechanics may be.

            It should enhance the themes of the game rather than be a prestige class just because all splats need prestige classes for some reason (Legacy, Entitlements, etc, etc, etc.) I feel like a bloodline should make you feel a part of what came before you and like you are contributing to what comes after. Current bloodlines just feel like layering another set of powers which are cool but don't seem to really have a place or reason for existing other than being cool.

            I don't really think Vampire needs this kind of prestige class. A sufficiently old Vampire is suitably unique and terrifying just from the Devotions and allies and resources and powers they create and gather over time and knowledge they've accrued without needing some additional template layered on top.

                    I also think that's a good idea, but I also don't want to lose out on the other 80% of Bloodlines we have today. I think Bloodlines should be split into multiple different systems, kinda like how changelings are changed and individualised through everything between Kiths, Mantles, Entitlements and regular Merits.
                    You can have lineages that comes with certain advantages and disadvantages represented through either Merits or a minor standardised system.
                    You can have sub-factions in Covenants be represented through Merits, like those that already exist for specific positions or as MCI-style Merits.
                    You can have Curses of the Blood be represented through Persistent Conditions, or through other means. They're kinda supposed to be unique and terrifying so no standardised systems would/could work.
                    You can have unique Disciplines unlinked from specific Bloodlines/Clans, similar to Blood Tenebrous or Breath-Drinking, but with inherent drawbacks that comes from learning them. If they're associated with specific groups, then they could be highly guarded secrets and/or have the groups receive an XP discount through their associated Merits.
                    You can have other unique concepts be their own things and not force them into a specific system.

                    And now you could play a vampire of a prestigious lineage who has joined a small faction whose ideologies resonate with her while she tries to hide the curse in her blood from others. Or any other kind of combination you'd want to play.
