                          An interesting Mage-related bloodline might be derived from mage *sight* rather than access to magic. Maybe someone was embraced at what should have been the moment of their awakening - now they're separated from the Supernal, but can still see how its symbols and truths manifest in the world around them. Their bloodline gift manifests as a series of Auspex devotions that give them insight into the truth of things (if they're not Mekhet, Auspex is their fourth Discipline).

                          I'm with Falco, as far as Beast is concerned - Nightmares and Kinship. The alternative I can see might be the capacity to enter people's dreams - the Alucinor as a Nosferatu bloodline rather than a Mekhet one.

                          A Mummy-related bloodline would depend on which aspect of the game you tie it to.
                          • A bloodline could claim to have originated in the Nameless Empire (its gift might be Devotions that extend Dominate to the institutions of civilisation).
                          • Vampires whose Blood is bound up with Sekhem might learn to feed on other expressions of life force: breath, treasured memories, or even heirlooms.
                          • The idea of vampires as a failed attempt to duplicate the Rite of Return is an idea that's been floated from time to time, but that's more likely to be an entire clan than a bloodline. But a bloodline of that clan might have figured out a way to change their blood to become closer to the real thing, with a gift duplicating some of a mummy's Affinities (at the cost of the Judges' attention?).
                          • Or they might be connected to Sekhem in a different way, with an affinity for magical relics - finding them, using them, resisting curses levelled on them.
                          • Or a mummy might have found a way to grant the condition of a Sadikh to a vampire, resulting in a bloodline bound up with their will and cult.
                          • A vampire who was lucky or unlucky enough to collide with a seba might be changed in some way.


                          I'm also with Falco on a Promethean-related one. Maybe the first edition Nosferatu weakness would work for them (as an echo of Disquiet).

                          A Geist-related bloodline, if it doesn't revolve around ghosts, might revolve around the Underworld itself. Their gift could be to open Avernian Gates with the facility of Sineaters, or have an easier time crossing the Rivers. Or more subtly, they could mimic the Underworld's capacity for revealing and showcasing people's worst obsessions.

                                  I've actually had this stewing for a few months in a .txt file on my desktop. There might be some missing stuff that I've simply overlooked due to not working on it for so long. Not my finest work. There's a few plot holes, and it's generally just messy.


                                  Cartographers

                                  Sometime during the late 19th Century it came to the knowledge of the Concilium that a number of local Awakened started to disappear. After a swift investigation and an overzealous strike from the local Sentinels, a number of bodies were recovered. Most of the missing Mages, as well as the unnaturally decomposing remains of several vampires were among the body count, but it also included the perpetrator of the disappearances. It was a Nameless Reaper who, according to the few fragments of the found documentation, tried in some way to introduce the state of vampirism into mages. Luckily the Reaper never succeeded, if such a task is even possible.

                                  Clan: Ventrue

                                  Nickname: Geomancers, Tellurians

                                  Bloodline Bane: The Hubristic Curse.
                                  Anytime the character rolls no successes on a Discipline activation, roll her Humanity. If that roll also fails the initial Failure is downgraded to a Dramatic Failure without awarding a beat.
                                  Most vampires believes this curse is a permutation of the Ventrue's natural arrogance, however any mage that may have had the opportunity to study these blood drinking geomancers often notes an uneasy itch around them for some time after they've been subject to this curse. The similarities to Paradox are there, but it's not as if vampires can be affected to Paradox. After all, it only affects Awakened magic, and even if vampires were subject to Paradox it would affect them en masse instead of just these curious specimens.

                                  Bloodline Disciplines: Animalism, Auspex, Dominate, Resilience

                                  Bloodline Gift: The Cartographers learns Vitae of the Earth upon entering the bloodline. All other Devotions costs 2 Experiences each.

                                  Vitae of the Earth
                                  Whenever the vampire spends her daysleep at a Hallow, she automatically draws upon its mystical energies to awake without having to spend Vitae to do so. Additionally, if the Hallow is connected to a Leyline that is subject to Unholy Geometry (see below) she can draw Vitae directly from the Hallow. This requires a Blood Potency + Composure roll and one hour’s time. Each success gives one Vitae, up to the dot rating of the Hallow (minus one if the vampire also spent daysleep at the Hallow). This can also be used to drain the tass within animals subject to Desecration of Beasts without harming them, but not on any other form of tass.

                                  Desecration of Beasts
                                  (Animalism ••)
                                  In a process non unsimilar from Raise the Familiar the vampire is capable of preparing an animal by feeding it one point of Vitae. For a number of nights equal to the vampire's Blood Potency the animal becomes a vessel for mystical energies. If a Hallow is allowed to produce tass, it does that in the form of the animal's blood. For Kindred (even those who no longer can feed from animals normally) this means the animal contains Vitae up to the animal's Size, on top of the regular nourishment it can bring. However, draining this Vitae still inflicts one point of lethal per Vitae taken.
                                  Mages are also able to gain Mana from the tass bound in the animal, either through the spell Channel Mana, or by consuming the animal's blood as they would other edible tass.

                                  Dowsing the Land
                                  (Auspex ••)
                                  The vampire can, as an Instant action, spend one Vitae in order to clearly perceive ley lines, nodes, and Hallows. She perceives them through all her senses, but the way they express themselves varies wildly depending on the resonance. A ley line of "Wrath" might be a burning river of scarlet fire rushing through the air while a "Subterranean" node might be as a black hole, pulling at the vampire's focus and dampening all other sensory stimuli. As such, all perception based rolls not related to ley lines, nodes, or Hallows are subject to a -2 penalty, as long as she maintains this power in the vicinity of such distractions.
                                  If there are no ley lines, nodes, or Hallows within sensory range, she feels a tugging sensation towards the nearest.
                                  (At the Storyteller's discrection the vampire may also gain access to the benefits relating to the Gross Arcanum connected to a node, as described on page 242 in Mage the Awakening Second Edition. If doing so, the aforementioned distraction penalty does not apply to rolls benefitting from that effect.)

                                  Unholy Geometry
                                  (Dominate ••)
                                  The geomancer is capable of commanding the flow of ley lines by himself. By travelling along the desired path he can move a ley line away from its natural position. This costs one Vitae per mile of ley line he wishes to move. If he instead wishes to move an entire node, the Vitae cost is multiplied by the number of ley lines intersecting at the node, as moving a node this way also pulls at every connected ley line.
                                  Creating a new ley line costs one Willpower point on top of one Vitae per mile, plus one additional Vitae and Willpower for every node created by crossing an existing ley line.
                                  Hallows, being places of Supernal forces, are beyond the capabilities of vampiric Disciplines. However, at the Storyteller's discretion a Hallow may slowly follow its associated node, as long as the node occupies a fitting location. A Hallow on a mountain peak may not move as it is the peak itself that is of importance, but one deep in an old-growth forest might not be tied to the specific spot it currently rests at.

                                  Restoring the Self
                                  (Resilience ••)
                                  As an Instant action the vampire is able to restore himself. Three points of Vitae removes one Mental Condition (without resolving it), or one Personal Tilt.


                                  The Founder

                                  The Cartographer is unlike other Kindred. For him, vampirism is a curse. So what, thinks anyone who cares to listen when he waxes melancholic. Aren't we all cursed? But they don't understand. They don't know what he has lost. They don't know that he has beheld the marvelous splendor of the Supernal!

                                  Whether the Cartographer really were Awakened or just subject to the Apocalypse spell is unknown (or something else altogether) as he has no recollection of his life before the destruction of the Reaper. But that is not to say he's not knowledgable. His introduction to Kindred society was when the Ordo Dracul approached him at a new potential Wyrm's Nest in the middle of a ritual. While the terminology of Oblations and Hallows were foreign to these dragons, they quickly found his knowledge about this particular type of Wyrm's Nest far surpassing anyone at the Academy.

                                  These knowledges were not the only trace of his forgotten past. He has never shown it to anyone, but he has in his possession a handwritten, leatherbound tome. Written inside is a ritual, a Scale in an unknown Mystery of the Dragon. A Scale that promises bloodsoaked immortality and, most importantly, a rebirth into a world of pure truth, where the underlying symbols of the Universe are laid bare. But even this is impeded by the curse of vampirism, for many key components of the Scale are written unintelligible text that defies any attempt of translation. The Cartographer knows that this can only be High Speech, and during the centuries he's never stopped attempting to translate this text, this golden key to his past.

                                  Name: The Cartographer (this is the only name he remembers)
                                  Clan: Ventrue
                                  Covenant: Ordo Dracul
                                  Mask: Scholar
                                  Dirge: Visionary
                                  Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 3, Resolve 3
                                  Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 2, Stamina 3
                                  Social Attributes: Presence 2, Manipulation 3, Composure 4
                                  Mental Skills: Academics 3 (Cartography), Investigation 4, Occult 5 (Leylines, Supernal magic), Science 3
                                  Physical Skills: Athletics 2, Brawl 4, Stealth 2
                                  Social Skills: Animal Ken 2 (Ghouled animals), Empathy 2, Intimidation 1, Subterfuge 2
                                  Merits: Eye for the Strange, Status: Ordo Dracul (4)
                                  Disciplines: Animalism 3, Auspex 5, Coil of the Ascendant 5, Dominate 5, Obfuscate 2, Resilience 4, Vigor 2
                                  Devotions: The Cartographer knows all of his Bloodline Gifts, as well as Shared Sight and Shatter the Shroud
                                  Blood Potency: 5
                                  Health: 12
                                  Willpower: 7
                                  Humanity: 4
                                  Size: 5
                                  Speed: 12
                                  Initiative: 7
                                  Defense: 4
