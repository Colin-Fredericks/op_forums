                          I would imagine Torpor would be a pretty strong incentive.                                                       Originally posted by spaceharrier72 View Post
                                                      ...but thing is though I kinda prefer just playing the simple concept of a undead corpse maintaining their immortality through blood though with no other things shoehorned in.
                                                      Okay, so you like the immortal aspect of vampires. You like the feeding aspect of vampires. You want to replace Frenzy with some other negative aspect that still encourages feeding, but not at the cost of pure player-agency (i.e. loss of control).

                                                      Then yes, I think your previous idea of tying health/immortality to feeding could be a reasonable substitute. Some things you should think about:
                                                      • Frenzy/The Beast is an inherent theme in the game. It's tough to avoid it entirely, and a lot of powers and effects will be taking it into account. Be prepared to do a lot of bookeeping to scrub them from the mechanics. From the Bestial, Wanton, Competitive conditions. From Coil of the Wyrm. From lashing out against Majesty as a defense. There will be a lot of little things you are going to have to decide on the fly when you encounter them.
                                                      • Frenzy/The Beast is something that while not automatically present in every scene is a potential in every scene. It is kind of the Wandering Monster of V:tR. If you aren't deliberately replacing the Beast with something that has the potential of triggering in any given scene, you are making your custom vampire template more powerful, or less disadvantaged, than stock. Account for that.
                                                      Replacing the Beast with some kind of Degeneration means replacing the more overt Predatory themes of the game with something else. In this case, I'd probably suggest Isolation. The vampire should feel the call of immortality and this is what separates them from humans. This should probably come in multiple forms. Examples:
                                                      • Any evening where the kindred does not spend 1 vitae to 'wake' their immortal form, they revert to both the state of the corpse (wounds) and the actual age (Older vampires look more decrepit, etc.) of their true form. Appropriate wound and social penalties would apply to all actions.
                                                      • Ramp up the Social Penalties of low Humanity, or avoid ways for the Kindred to circumvent those penalties. Reframe the perception of them into that alien, not human feeling more than as a wolf among the herd.
                                                      • Recast feeding grounds as less about territorial predators, and more about having one's own space to truly feel alone amongst the crowd.
                                                      • Find a way to simulate a mental condition or theme to replace how frenzy triggers encourage direct, animalistic conflict between Kindred and their opponents / loved ones. Maybe people / other Kindred who see your 'true self' receive bonuses in actions against you, or you receive penalties to actions against them. These penalties can only be removed by feeding from them or some other similar, theme reinforcing manner.

                                                          I don't think frenzy was meant to be a mechanic to force you to drink blood. The rules for frenzy predate the amount of Vitae impacting your frenzy rolls.

                                                          I think the thought was as follows and mind you its only my opinion from the Masquerade 1st edition days (1991):
                                                          1. RPG players play heroes. We need to have a mechanic in there that makes them perform non-heroic acts since these are vampires.
                                                          2. If Humanity is mental/soul hit points (which in some respects it is), players will never do anything that threatens them losing these hit points as it will be suboptimal. We would get a game about vampires where everyone acts like saints, even moreso than Lawful Good characters.

                                                          Now I am not a fan of viewing humanity as mental hit points, losing and gaining them are the major part of the game thematically. That one game where a player bought her Humanity up to 2 was a major wake up call as to how far afield that Daeva has gone. Frenzy was meant to put your Humanity at risk. I think since then, it has taken on other significance within the game.

                                                          At that point in time, there were few vampire stories where the vampires flew off the rails and went crazy so it was not from a literary source (I still don't know many). I mean they got angry and flashed fangs and a lot of them were just straight evil, but nothing to the level of a Frenzy. Raging out is something werewolves do in literature.

                                                          The fact that animating your body, healing damage and using your powers requires Vitae expenditure is what is suppose to force you to drink blood, mostly the first one.

                                                          Thats all separate form not wanting Frenzy in your games. If you remove Frenzy, you don't need to replace it with something else that makes you want to feed. You need to replace it with something that puts your Humanity at risk or not depending on how you feel about the Humanity stat and what the expectations are for it going up and down.
