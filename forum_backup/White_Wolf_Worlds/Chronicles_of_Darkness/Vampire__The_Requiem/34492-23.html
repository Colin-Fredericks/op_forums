                                    360) It's the 4th of July and the US President is on TV. No, not the current one - one of the old ones, like on the currency, old. Turns out, some nitwit Kindred back in the day Embraced them and then tried to keep the whole thing under wraps. Now the former President is back, claiming to have transcended their condition and is pissed at the current state of the nation. This is going to be an interesting election cycle...

                                    A) The Invictus are collectively shitting bricks. Someone let this epic violation of the First Tradition get this far, and there is no way the genie is getting back in the bottle. Or coffin. Coteries are being scrambled to try to vainly contain the situation, figure out who screwed up, and burn the trails to any higher ups.

                                    B) The Lancea Et Sanctum are furious over this blasphemy - a Kindred claiming to have discovered a way of defying God's Curse and purpose for their kind. Their auguries indicate the ex-President is indeed Kindred, even as they walk in the daylight. How is this possible?

                                    C) The Circle of the Crone are nervous, not only for the increased attention, but also for someone so public claiming to have transcended their nature.

                                    D) The Carthian Movement is in conflict - some think the political ramifications are disastrous, while others scent opportunity - what might Carthian Law backed by an entire nation accomplished?

                                    E) The Ordo Dracul are intrigued. This Kindred is able to wield their powers, yet has defied the sun on live camera. Have they uncovered secrets worth copying, or is this a dead-end heretical Coil?

                                    [The President in question isn't really important, though which one it is can add some flavor. One of the Founding Fathers is best for the historical commentary on modern times, while Abraham Lincoln is pop-culturally amusing]
