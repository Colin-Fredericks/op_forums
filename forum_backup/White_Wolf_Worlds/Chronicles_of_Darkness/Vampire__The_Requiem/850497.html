    [2e] Feeding on Werewolves

    03-21-2016, 07:54 AM
    Hey all,

    I'm in the process of arranging a 2e game, and one of the characters I'd like to have running around in the backstory (Probably dead by the time the game starts) is a vampire who feeds on Werewolves. There's some more backstory to it, but the gist is that the vampires drove the werewolves out of their city, but new ones keep popping up. One of the elders of the area makes it his job to hunt down future Werewolves as they approach the first change, and drives them to it by tormenting them subtly. Then he eats them.

    But before I introduce it into the game, I want to make sure it's something that works by the mechanics. Will a Nosferatu with a decent level of Vigor be able to overcome the ludicrous regeneration of even a brand new Gauru-form Werewolf? Is Blood Potency 5 or 6, 3 to 4 points of Vigor and a decent Brawl skill enough to do it? Turns out, not really.

    Rough maths I'm using here is 4 points of Strength, 4 points of Vigor, 4 points of Brawl (I.e. Leaving some room around the top end, especially for BP 6), a +2 Brawl specialty, giving 14 dice (~5 successes), compared to an average Gauru (2 base strength + 3 for Gauru, 2 Brawl) of 7 dice (>2 successes). The Vampire will regularly win, but he'll not manage an exceptional success often. And when he wins he'll do ~5 successes of damage +0L for fangs +4 for Vigor, for 9 levels of damage. But not enough to deal even a single point of Aggravated damage (Average Gauru has 11 health boxes), meaning that next turn the Werewolf heals fully. And he only needs to win the roll once to break free.

    Options I'm considering are Professional Training (Which feels a bit weak. Professional Werewolf hunter? Professional wrestler? Lot of work to justify either, I think), having his fangs silvered (which instantly turns the maths from an endless struggle to a two turn kill), or something clever that I've not yet found. I'd prefer something clever, as I'm not sure that relying on silver gives the character the bad-ass feel that I was going for. Then again, maybe he could hide the fact, which suddenly makes him appear both more bad-ass and (when discovered) makes him appear more cunning.

    Nosferatu isn't set in stone. If another Clan works better, suggest away. It's just where I started, for the monster who scares other monsters.

      Well...if the BP of the vampire is 5 - 6....you can use 5 - 6 vitae at turn. Save 2 or 3 for the physical disciplines, (resilence, Vigor and celerity if him have them) and use the rest to rise strenght (3 vitae in strenght are 6 more dots on the attack pool).
      Furthemore, Nosferatu could use cloack of shadow and deal a surprise attack dealing Killing Blow. With this you don't need to roll your dice pool. Every dice is a success for the attack. So you deal (with your examples) 18 damage plus weapon plus 4 for active vigor.

      EDIT: Remember that the Nosferatu can go invisible every turn of the combat and repeat the same strategy.
      If the vampire is going out of vitae, use a grappling move to drink a little bit. (I suppose that a Vampire used to feed from werewolves have the Unnatural affinity merit in order to gain Vitae drinking their blood).
      Anyway I suppose that a Gangrel with Unholy CLaw (that deal aggravate damage) or a Daeva (both with obfuscate is better), could works better.

      And go with Ride the Wave that adds to your dice pool 5-6 more dice from your BP.

          Also, in grapple, with an exceptional success you use two moves. One is a damage move, one is a feed move; so with 5 sux, that is 5 damage (Damage move), + (Up to BP) damage from blood loss (feed move).

          This assumes a) blood loss harms wolves like mortals, and b) werewolves regenerate their blood every turn. If these are both the case, a grappling vampire has at least a diceroll advantage a lot of the time; adding X from frenzy and up to 2X from blood expenditure, and can refresh this every time he wins with the feed move. Wrestling a Gauru wolf becomes a war of attrition; if the vampire can field the damage, the constant supply of blood in a regenerating gauru can add a lot of dice.

          A gauru will still win if he breaks the grapple and goes for bites; but a grappling vampire, who uses feed move and damage move the same turn with an exceptional success, is a deadly thing - particularly with the never-ending blood-supply of a regenerating werewolf (if this is the case).

          Of course, you could use silver sniper bullets instead...
