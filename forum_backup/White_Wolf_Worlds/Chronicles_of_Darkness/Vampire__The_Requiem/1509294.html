              Carthians, Circle of the Crone, Tenth Choir, and Ume House.

                              Ordo Dracul, because the Mysteries of the Dragon are cool as hell, and because they're the only covenant that has a set of ornate titles with secret meanings to memorize, including the most elaborate and impressive-sounding title of the set, whose secret meaning is "the guy who gave me this title wants you to kill me."

                              I think from a game design standpoint their Therion is a little unwieldy, but I have a soft spot for the Tenth Choir's sheer gall.

                              I like the thoroughness of the consideration put to the Lancea et Sanctum and how different a religion for vampires would look. It doesn't always commit as far as I'd like, but it's a good springboard for some profoundly disturbing monsters.

                              I like the Sun-Walking Knights. When I'm dealing with vampiric conspiracies, I like to lean on them, as an illustration of how temporal power co-opts. The vampires don't own the President, but the President owns several vampires.

                                  Would it be cheating if I said the Camarilla ?

                                  Every wing is just jam-packed with story-potential. A (kind of) functoning unified society of literal predators. What made me love it is the mood of RFR. Everything just feels so dangerous and creepy. Monsters acting as "civilised" or "noble" beings, always at the brink of frenzy, fearing to tear into their political opponents. Fear of it not because it would be a monstrous act but because it would make you lesser in the eyes of your peers.

                                  I even love the notion of the Invictus and Carthians both claiming to be the spiritual heirs of the Camarilla but only because nobody knows the truth of the real thing.

                                  I guess I am gushing more about Requiem for Rome than the Camarilla itself but they are inseperable to me.

                                  I adore everything about RFR. It is what got first me into VTR and everything about it is just kickass to me.

                                      From the core covenants, I really like how similar the Ordo Dracul is to the SCP foundation, especially with their focus on Wyrm's Nests. They're a way to crack into the supernatural horror of the setting; not like the contagion chronicle, but in something like a classic horror story, where you don't see the monster until it's too late.

                                      That being said, I really wish non-core covenants got more love. Sometimes it feels like canon doesn't want to acknowledge the idea of covenants existing outside the main five (looking at you, SotC). Among them, the Brides of Dracula stand out for reveling in vampirism in a way the other covenants don't (save the Circle).
