                                            Hum. If I had to design an alpha predator in the most common sense (a killing machine), en Elder from the bloodline I worked on for a 1ed campaign with my friends would probably do the trick : a Matashunta, Gangrel, with in-bloodline Nightmare, embraced either directly in barbarians Hordes or in medieval Europe, trained with any kind of swords and axes for decades. But what would makes it interesting would then be his flaws : while not stupid, I wouldn't see a character like that able to be very effective in the social arena, despite his centuries of existence. It would makes him a glass-canon : deadly in direct confrontation, but easily fallen through under-handed tactics... except if such a monster has found a "master" to guide and protect him from such threats. I would also play him more like a follower than a leader, because it adds layers of interests both for the players (even when the Vampire would be a NPC, he wouldn't be unidimensional).

                                            Playing him would force the player to act has the bodyguard of another Vampire, with all the consequences that it implies (the Matashunta must obey his master, or else he may very well lose the protection that he has been afforded, something which could probably spell his doom as an important figure and even Final Death).

                                            I don't have the sheet for the B&S,since it isn't released (yet) in French, but I know what background I would give this character.

                                            Jojol's History

                                            Jojol was a simple young man. He fought and killed for his Khan, more than happy with the ride to plunder sedentary settlements and the heat of battle. He was always more a follower than a leader, but when the Bulgarians began to settle and forfeited their nomadic ways, Jojol made his displeasure known. It brought upon him the wrath of his lords and he was disgraced. He spent several years as a drunkard, wandering aimlessly among the Bulgarian villages, until one night he stumbled upon a very beautiful woman, clad in armour, drinking the blood of a poor teenager. When he faced her, terror almost took the better of him, but Jojol attacked the woman nonetheless.

                                            He was overpowered with shameful ease by his foe, who then Embraced him on the spot. Once allowed to enter the world of the Damned, as one of the Warlords, Jojol spent decades working for his sire. He was contend to follow her, executing her enemies and studying under her care so one day he too could become a perfect predator like her. The pair moved from one battlefield to the next, always revealing in the carnages. Alas, in 1545, his sire was felled and he was put into Torpor in Brunswick. He has been awakened in 2000, and from then on has become a bodyguard for a powerful Invictus Elder in exchange of a carte-blanche to "teach" proper manners to the neonates in Brunswick as long as he doesn't kill them or break the Mascarade. While he has always felt the need to follow a "lord", he has recently began to question is own place in the social hierarchy of the Vampire. After all, he too is an Elder.

                                                    Well, I don't have a link for it, sadly, but I guess I can put the whole thin in spoiler here, since it is a 1ed bloodline :


                                                    Matashunta

                                                    “You dare treat me as a barbarian and I'll split your head open, you City scum!”

                                                    History: Contrary to what most Vampires, even young members of this Bloodline, believe the Matashunta Bloodline wasn’t created at the age of Attila. Indeed, the Matashunta aren't only one of the oldest Bloodlines among Vampires, they are also one of the very few which can claim to range from the South-west of France to the north of Beijing since more than 2300 years. Indeed, this offshoot from the Gangrel Clan was created by a Hun warlord, also called Xiongnu by the Chinese before the rose of their first Emperor... Or it is what the Matashunta claim.

                                                    Among the European Warlords, it is said that the creator of the Bloodline was born at the exact same moment than Jesus Christ and made a Vampire when the Christ suffered on Golgotha. However, the Asian Matashunta claims that their creator was made a Vampire when the First Emperor of China crowned himself. Those legends are probably simply a mean to make their creator greater than he was, but telling that to a Matashunta is the better way to get beheaded on the spot, or at least seriously threatened if the Warlord is mostly amiable. Their creator, simply called Matashunta in their tells, is indeed a rumored figure of power and weapon skills beyond what even a Vampire is able, in the legends of the Bloodline. Yet, nothing can be found by Vampire historians to prove that the Bloodline is older than Attila's grandfather, letting between five and two hundred years devoid of history if one believe the Asian or European legend of the Bloodline's foundation.

                                                    Fierce and deadly, the Matashunta have a long history of conquest, for they rode along every invasion of both Europe and China by nomadic hordes, deserting them when they settled in towns. Hailing from an age of constant warfare between the tribes and the nascent states from Asia, the Gangrel who founded this Bloodline concentrated to the terror that horsemen warriors and archers brought to their enemies and learnt the Nightmare Discipline, usually associated with the Nosferatu. Hundreds of tales and legends talk about the great Matashunta among his descendants: how he killed the Wolf-God of Sogdiana, how he had brought the most beautiful goddesses from the Ordos in his bed and hundreds of others variations. Yet, all traces of the Founder are lost around the era of Attila, implying that he either descended into Torpor or was killed.

                                                    It was also at that time that a great divide occurred between the Bloodline's members: in Europe, the Matashunta began to slowly fade to almost insignificant level in the following centuries, except in very limited areas, when barbarians all but forsaken their ancient gods and way of life, until they became nothing more than a loosely connected gathering of Gangrel’s pack with common characteristics. To the contrary, in Asia, the Bloodline remained strong to this day, with members among the entire nomad's people from Siberia, Mongolia and Central Asia, and even great number of them in China. Those Matashunta always protected their ancient traditions, when their brothers and sisters in Europe lost almost all of them thanks to the Danse Macabre and the efforts of both the Invictus and the Lancea Sanctum.

                                                    Yet, one of the very few traditions of the Matashunta was always protected in both Europe and Asia: the Siring. Contrary to most vampires, who chose to create an infant to their own benefits in political schemes and thus scan potential candidates, the Warlords let chance and instinct guide them. Indeed, a Matashunta will only grant Embrace to people who will fight back when they are attacked by them. This practice led to the acceptance of women and non-Asians (or Caucasians in Europe) in the Bloodline since centuries, making them quite progressing in that regard, compared to other clans and Bloodline, especially in Europe. The infant are then strictly trained and destroyed if they appear to be finally unworthy of the Bloodline's legacy.

                                                    One of the key points in the Matashunta neonates is that they are taught to despise those weak enough to succumb to Frenzy of any kind and that the Vampire must always control the Beast, even when he ride the wave. Obviously, said beliefs led to an incredible amount of disgust toward the Draugrs and Larvae, to the point where any true Matashunta will cease all others matters to destroy such creatures if he ever heard of them in his vicinity. Obviously, the Warlords see the Brood of Belial as the worst kind of vampiric degenerates which could exist and will often go to extreme length, even tearing down the Mascarade if need be, to exterminated any and all worshippers of the Devil in their domains.

                                                    Since two decades, the numbers of Warlords in Europe greatly increased for their brothers from Asia sent emissaries to reunite the Bloodline, using the easiest and safest way to travel that the world has ever known, and that, in a strange coincidence, Matashunta's Elders awakened. As of today, there is a lot more of young Gangrel undergoing their training to become true Matashunta in Europe than ever before, and some old Vampires from this Bloodline began to move to the Americas.

                                                    This renewal of the Bloodline is beginning to threaten the Ventrue and Daeva Clans for the Matashunta believe that they are the only ones worthy to commands others, be it the Kine or the Kindred, by the sheer virtue of their might.

                                                    Parent Clan: Gangrel

                                                    Nickname: Warlords (or Warladies)

                                                    Covenant: Hailing from an age of ferocious conquests and where fighting order and monotheism was the usual behavior of the old Warlords, it is no surprise that no Matashunta are found among the Invictus, and only a very few among the fanatics of the Lancea Sanctum, even in Europe, for the Warlords are the pure opposite of the Invictus, creatures of bloody passions and chaos, and not the cold masters of an ordered world.

                                                    A great number of Warlords thus joined the Circle of the Crones since immemorial times, becoming some of its greatest warriors, preventing its destruction in many European towns in ages past. More recently, broods of Matashunta joined with the Carthian Movement, seeking to bring their brethren to an enlighten era; some Warlords can even be found in the Ordo Dracul, espousing its beliefs of transcendence, seeing it as a mean to become an even more formidable warrior, worthy of their Founder's legacy and legends. Yet, most of the Warlords can be found among the Independents, and especially among the Nomads.

                                                    In Asia, Matashunta can be found among any Covenants which exist, contrary to their western kind. However, they are known for always undertaking the more martial roles they can get, for the Asian Matashunta eschew the traps of the usual Kindred politic.

                                                    Appearance : Because of their method of Embrace, the Matashunta do not share any common trait when it comes to appearance, expected the impression of danger and bestiality that they exude, as if the Beast inside of them was let a lot more loose than in any other vampire. Furthermore, no Warlords worthy of this nickname would wander the night without at least a finely crafted knife.
                                                    Still, most of them, especially the women, wore ragged clothes combined with accessories which make them appears as street fighters, vigilantes, or martial artists fanatics.

                                                    Havens : Just like their parent Clan, the European Matashunta often lack a proper haven, preferring to sleep in the earth, trees or even water. Yet, it is not uncommon even for the most hardened Nomad Warlord to have some old empty houses used as an occasional haven, where he can find solace for a time and renew his vows to the Bloodline.
                                                    In Asia, however, the Matashunta living in the steppes always have a yurt while those living in the more urban part of the continent dwell more often than not near the temples and pagoda dedicated to the great warriors of old.

                                                    Background : Just as it is with their appearance, there is no common background among the Matashunta, for they Embrace whoever is brave (or stupid) enough to refuse to flee and surrender before them but muster the will to fight back. Therefore, all kind of individual can be found among the Warlords : ruthless tacticians who are also loving mothers still protecting their mortal families, brutal thugs for whom fighting has become an art in itself and even some rare library rats who were to unused to the situation to be afraid when they should have.

                                                    Character Creation: For the Matashunta, fight and war are everything which should matter, which explain why they often tend to have physical attributes as their primary attributes. Yet, being an imbecile or being devoid of the ability to command lesser beings would be a failure for a Warlord, and thus, Social and Mental attributes are always in a race with each other’s for the secondary place. Some Matashunta are even social warriors more than physical ones, and a rare Warlord will be a pit of knowledge before anything else, even if that kind of Matashunta often have a difficult unlife among their own Bloodline.

                                                    Bloodline Disciplines: Animalism, Protean, Resilience, Nightmare.

                                                    Weakness: The Matashunta suffer from the same weakness than the Gangrels. Furthermore, maybe because of their Founder's own behavior, or possibly because of their tight connection with the Beast thanks to Animalism, Protean and Nightmare Disciplines, the Matashunta suffer a -2 penalty on rolls to resist any kind of Frenzy.

                                                    Organization: In Asia, the Bloodline's members managed to preserve their close connection, despite their participations in the various covenants from the Far East and in fact, an Asian Matashunta is defined before by his Blood than by his Covenant in the minds of both the Bloodline members and other vampires. To the contrary, in Europe the Matashunta are a lot more loosely connected to each other and as a result, a Warlady would be more defined by her Covenants than her Blood, thanks to the long centuries of decline undergone by the Bloodline in the Far West of the Eurasian contient.
                                                    Yet, European Warlords managed to preserve some old traditions and all Matashunta in a city are expected to gather at least twice every year to tall tales of the Bloodline, presentation of new infants and recital of new feats by their fellows.
                                                    A common behavior for all Matashunta is their reverence to the great warriors of ages past, for the Warlords thrive to achieve such fame in their unlife. Therefore, the members of the Bloodline are incredibly knowledgeable when it comes to those warrior’s legacies, to the point when long forgotten heroes are only known only by the Matashunta, their deeds written in their records.

                                                    Concepts: Mercenary, Lancea crusader, bloodthirsty pagan, martial artists, artistic killer, bodyguard.


                                                    Note that the main failings of the Bloodline as a whole is that most of them tend to refuse to work for, or even with others they deem weaker than them. While they are a truly old Bloodline, they aren't that numerous in Europe, and even only certain domains in Central Asia and Siberia will have a whole Brood of Matashunta. in my head, they are far more commonly found among Nomads or as very small pockets of relatives in the rest of the world. Even if I've wrote that they are growing in number in Europe, the way they often tend to live their Requiem coupled with the fact that an unworthy Childe will be far more often killed of by his Sire or another Matashunta mean that you can't have hordes of them swarming. They were also created for a setting where the Belial Brood exists, but I think that they could just as easily hate the Birds of Dis.
