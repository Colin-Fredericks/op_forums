                If you go strictly by the book, werewolves can't be blood bonded, anyway. Only mortals and vampires are subject to Vinculum. Other supernatural creatures can become addicted to Vitae, but getting a raging lupine monster addicted to the blood in your veins is a sure way to lose said blood in a hurry. The point is moot, but even so establishing Vinculum probably doesn't matter for the throes of frenzied battle, and once the vampire is dead ceases to matter entirely.

                Werewolves do not automatically ingest flesh and blood as they bite. This is explicitly laid out in the book on page 100. A werewolf is certainly going to taste the blood on her tongue, at least if she's biting a living creature. Vampires are a different and much stranger case, since they explicitly don't bleed like mortals do. I personally would say the biting werewolf is still going to get some blood and taste its sweetness, but not enough to track by (as is implied by the section under Wolf Senses on page 94). Not enough to count as a drink, either, for purposes of blood addiction, so the vampire would have to force the issue. Those thinking that pushing Vitae down the werewolf's throat will win them the fight will quickly meet disappointment (and Final Death shortly thereafter) when they realize those points are wasted or, at worst, have only made the werewolf more furious in her attack.

                                        Not quite. They only have a chance if they avoid playing into the Werewolf hunting game, and play to their own strengths. Vampire blood is the accumlation of multiple mixed people's blood. Same thing in 1e. This does not imply anything about bite-and-drink-blood issues; this and that are separate, unrelated issues.

                                                    Originally posted by MCN View Post
                                                    Werewolves eat meat. They do not get essence from blood, so swallowing it is completely outside the scope of that paragraph.
                                                    They eat flesh and blood of living things. Werewolves bite all kinds of enemies and aren't stated to explicitly swallow every time. Assuming they do, especially in a worst-case scenario, is just that, an assumption. Vampires are explicitly stated to not bleed in the same way as living creatures, so if we're making assumptions or interpreting the rules, "werewolf biting vampire does not automatically mean werewolf drinks vampire's blood unless the vampire goes out of her way to force it" is certainly valid.
