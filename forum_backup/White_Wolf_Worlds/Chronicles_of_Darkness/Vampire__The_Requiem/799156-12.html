        We've been mostly identifying people through Know Thy Prey.

        There's a few other abilities that let you notice and identify supernaturals, Scent of the Unnatural, one of the Warding ones.

        Know Thy Prey tells you every name they're known by, as well as their Status, Retainer, Allies and Contact merits. After our first experience with vampires we hung out and got as much information on their society as possible, though we neglected their powers and weaknesses, and so we recognize the terms used across the various covenants, and the names of the covenants themselves.

        We've identified a couple hunters through similar methods. But the local hunter compact has a very obvious name so we didn't have to do as much investigation.

        Also, ghouls tend to be identifiable by scent, just being in the same room of them gives a particular odor to the area. Though pinpointing it can be difficult, especially if they have Obfuscation. As a side note, this may just be a regional thing, the vampires are centrally located in certain areas with distinct scents.

                                        Originally posted by MCN View Post
                                        Scent of the Unnatrual and Boundary Ward are the only two abilities that I'm aware of that can actually detect a vampire. Nothing in the book suggests otherwise. If you are assuming otherwise, that's got to be a house call, and not an official rule.
                                        In 1E, werewolves could get a sense of a vampire not being quite right through smell alone. Without blush of life active, they don't give off their natural bodily scents. They smell like old, dried blood and similar unpleasant things. This may still hold true in second edition since that's really just the way heightened senses of smell and hearing work. Yes, the werewolf would have to get close to be sure, but if a vampire doesn't have blush of life active, any werewolf using wolf senses is going to quickly realize that there's something off about the vampire. Scent the Unnatural is better for detecting these things, but the werewolf isn't blind without it.
