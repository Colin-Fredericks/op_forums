                        Originally posted by tsusasi View Post
                        Yeah as in the book literally spells out in English that you DON'T add Majesty or Vigor ratings to your dice rolls for Lashing Out.
                        Yes. The supernatural power. Which Courtoisie isn't, it being a highly social fighting style where the most mechanically unusual element relies on your doing Expression, Intimidation, Socialize, or Subterfuge and/or leveraging your Presence or Manipulation by being sufficiently performative/evocative in the societal theater of A Polite Duel.

                        And that Awe affects Presence rolls, not Manipulation rolls, which is what goading someone into doing something classifies as.
                        How unfortunate, then, that the Style doesn't mention any other form of social engagement, the Condition imposed by the second dot of Majesty doesn't specifically benefit Manipulation rolls, and the VtR2e corebook's general rules chapter's section on Presence doesn't describe that Attribute as "assertiveness, gravitas, and raw appeal" or "a strong bearing that changes moods and minds."

                        And it also classifies Courtoisie as a Fighting Style for using weapons and hand to hand, not a Social merit for Social maneuvering like Etiquette or Fast Talking.
                        It is a Style Merit built for engaging in socially-enforced one-on-one fights with weapons. The first dot is for preventing other characters from cutting in, the third dot is for forcing your opposition to engage with you, and the second dot is for allowing you to use your Social Attributes and Skills offensively and defensively.

                        These effects define and apply within the context of "the duel," which is a narrow enough circumstance that being able to Dodge with Manipulation + Socialize or strike with Presence + Intimidation and get a couple of extra bonus dice as long as you're keeping up an appropriate form of banter while you do so is not an overwhelming bonus to have from something as particular as a clan Discipline coupled with a covenant-specific Style Merit.

                        But continue ignoring what the rules say about Awe use since following it means you have to use 15 dice instead of 20 dice to beat someone to Final Death in a social encounter.
                        What the rules say about Awe use is that it's for talking normally, not for other uses of Majesty, which is a supernatural power whose second dot is rolled with Presence, as are roughly half the Majesty-based Devotions in the core rulebook.

                        It's similarly called out as not boosting Presence for the purposes of Lashing Out, which is an extension of the vampire's supernatural qualities that specifically doesn't get bonus dice from Disciplines unless they individually say otherwise.

                        Neither of these implies that the basic Be Good At Social Stuff power should not apply to a maneuver premised around using the fact that you're Good At Social Stuff to fare better in a formal duel with weapons than you would in an informal punch-up.
