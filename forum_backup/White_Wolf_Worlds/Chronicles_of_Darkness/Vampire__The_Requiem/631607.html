    Circle of the Crone and the Archtypes...

    08-18-2015, 06:10 PM
    One thing I noticed about the Circle of the Crone that it organizes it's members into archtypes which are apparently based on Hecate (Virgin, Maiden, Crone) and the "Three faces of Adam" (Fool, Hero, Prophet) which are strictly gendered based which therefore I have some serious concerns that the Circle of the Crone is problematic because of it since it apparently restricts it's members into gender roles which what if a female member of the Circle didn't want to fill in any of the Virgin, Maiden, Crone roles at all? Is the Circle really that sexist after all which it seems to adapt patriarchal gender roles that can be really rigidly restricting for it's members and what they can do?

                                                  In simple terms, both covenants consider vampires to be ideally asexual beings. The Circle has roles based on the concept of gender, but it would be up to a specific cult to determine an individual's eligibility to take up a role. A female cult member might take a male philosophical role as readily as one of the female roles. The Circle even has marriage, but there are no gender roles in that. Vampires are not supposed to procreate in the mortal sense. They don't raise children. They only take actual gender roles when dealing with mortals. Within the covenant, socially they would be androgynous. Actual gender would only be important for whatever rituals they choose to enact. They are vampires and won't be held down by concepts that apply to mortals.
