                                            12-08-2016, 10:36 PM
                                            First, Wow, The Armory line books have always been good, and this is just another great addition. The Tag system is awesome, and I really like the "merit templates". Also, THANK YOU THANK YOU THANK YOU for putting the quick chart reference in the back. What a timesaver those things are! I do have a couple items that look like they need some adjustment.

                                            The New Merits for Trigger Discipline and Loaded for Bear really should be up in the merits section. My players are never going to be looking for them down on page 143, especially with the quick reference pages for weapons in an appendix. Tilts should also kind of be down with the new Conditions, to try to keep things neat.

                                            One last thing I wanted to bring up is with The Lost Boys and Serum. It looks like this needs to be looked at and fleshed out just a little more. I love the concept (especially overclocking), but the mechanics are a little bit sparse.

                                            We know that Serum, (tox 5) is going to do you some damage (great idea, btw). Fixer Serum (the 'knockoff') is Tox 7 because it's "less pure". Is that the only difference between the two? Are there no other side effects? Does it last just as long as regular Serum?

                                            I would like to see some sort of general rule for people who try to 'ride the wave' with Deprivation. I'm imagining a storyline where some of the implants make their way into students who need a boost in their grades/athletic performance and find out that Deprivation is the 'sweet spot', so operate on half-doses to get the ability to Overclock on-demand 24/7. The students are going to run into the characters of course (they are professors in this chronicle) and basically burn themselves out by staying Deprived for extended periods of time. This lends itself better to some of the merits here than others. Staying Deprived for a long time makes sense when you want to use Augmented Resilience or Augmented Speed because the stat increases are generally going to come out in their favour even with the Deprived Condition knocking down Resistance stats. Even the addition of a cumulative penalty for every period beyond the first that the character stays Deprived might make it work. to balance it out, some of the Deprived costs could be changed from 1L to 2B or something equivalent.


                                            Also, why does the Protocol Fixer chart not line up at least roughly with the needs of the user (As presented in the Protocol merit and using Deprived/Withdrawl rules)? Does Delta just rework your system, then leave you with one dose per month, so you can die in less than a day due to augmentation rejection? For example, here are the dots and dosages between Deprived re-ups, Withdrawl re-ups and the amount supplied by the Fixer merit. By the time you reach 3 Protocol dots, you're lagging pretty significantly behind, even if you wait for full withdrawl to set in.Trying to aim for higher Protocol levels is insanity unless your character is lucky enough to be able to churn the stuff out at home on a constant basis.


                                            Protocol Protocol Protocol Fixer
                                            DOTS Dosages needed per month(Re-up at Deprived) DOTS Dosages needed per month(Re-up at Withdrawl) DOTS Dosages Supplied
                                            1.00 1.00 1.00 0.50 0.00 1.00
                                            2.00 4.00 2.00 2.00 1.00 5.00
                                            3.00 30.00 3.00 15.00 2.00 13.00
                                            4.00 60.00 4.00 30.00 3.00 25.00
                                            5.00 120.00 5.00 60.00 4.00 41.00
                                            5.00 57.00
                                            (NOTE that in the Protocol Fixer column, as with the chart in the book, the merit starts at 0 with one dose supplied by Delta protocol. That will be in addition to your Fixer contact, hence all of the numbers being 'off' by one.)

                                            Using the figures above (converted to monthly on a basis of ~4 weeks, 30 days per month) at Protocol 3, with protocol Fixer 3, you would take about 4L from withdrawl before your next 'delivery'. One dot up seems like way too much, but even dots is not enough and gets to death levels at level 5. Ironically, with the way the delivery scale jumps, level 4 is ok, being in Deprivation about ~25-30% of the time overall.

                                            Again, overall I'm loving the concepts in this book. It's been worth the wait.
