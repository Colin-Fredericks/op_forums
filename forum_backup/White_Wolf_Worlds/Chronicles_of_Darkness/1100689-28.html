                                        well, I must admit that I am yet to back the Kickstarter, for a number of reasons- the first is personal financial problems, which means I am now on a "save money" state. That goes with the fact the minimal amount of money needed to back the project and still get, well, something is much higher- 20$ instead of 1$ (sure, at the old 1$ pledge I would have got only Eras which belong to the gameline I choose to back and at 20$ I get the whole book, but it is still kinda discouraging). The fact that you back the whole book automatically instead of individual lines removes a little bit of the "competitive" factor, which harms the excitement a little. The second, and the more important reason, is that I just can't feel "excited" about this Kickstarter. I try, I really do, but I feel like something is missing. The lack of a preview seems to be an important factor, as the small paragraphs about each Era are nice, but still not enough to catch my interest. I don't know what the plans are for each of the Eras, or what special and weird things make those Eras special other than "Chinese mummies" or "vampires in the french revolution". Also, for some reason, I dislike having crossover eras as the "core concept" of the book. I prefer individual Eras, each being based around a certain gameline, with an option to increase to Crossover through stretch goals or pledges, than less Eras with each including two splats. To me, it makes me feel like the Eras are just less "special". And more than all, I feel like this Kickstarter has just came way too early- I mean, it feels like Dark Eras 1 was just a while ago! There weren't even some teasing about it by writers/developers like it was last time. It all makes it feel a bit blend, to my taste.

                                        Now, I'm not saying I won't end up backing this project- only that for the time being, I don't feel any need to do so. I hope that something would happen and catch my attention, so I'll want to back the Kickstarter- but for now, I just feel, well, bored, and I can't back Kickstarters which can't catch my interest.

                                                            I know for myself, my lack of enthusiasm is down to a couple of reasons.

                                                            The first is that when the first Dark Eras book was on Kickstarter I was just desperate for more Mummy content. Since the Mummy line appeared to be basically finished, new mechanics and concepts were only likely to come through Dark Eras. That made it enormously attractive (also a book full of historical settings is inherently useful when running a game where flashbacks can take you to any time or place on the planet, even if they aren't written specifically for Mummy). I'm not running Mummy anymore though. I still love it to bits, but it also feels a lot more complete than it did pre-Companion and pre-Rio. All the big missing pieces are filled in, save perhaps for a more detailed look at Apotheosis and post-Apotheosis Chronicles. Oh, and this ties more into point three below, but Chinese Mummy isn't interesting to me. It feels like an attempt to win over those people who keep asking for non-Egyptian Mummies in Mummy: The Curse, since China is usually mentioned second only to Inca in those discussions, and that puts me off.

                                                            The second big reason is that I just don't like crossover eras. I'm a purist, I like to focus on one game at a time and explore its themes and complexities while generally leaving crossover to little more than vague hints and Easter Eggs in my games. I am happy that the Mummy crossover is with Hunter, arguably the least intrusive of the other games, but Chinese Mummy doesn't

                                                            Third, there is only one era that really, really grabs me by the lapels and shouts, "I am a perfect fit" the way a lot of the original eras did. And it's for Geist (and not Werewolf, just the Geist half, another issue with the crossover element). And sadly Geist is a game I've by and large decided I will not run unless the second edition is well received, because everything I've ever heard about its mechanics in first edition make them out to be a hot mess.

                                                            I'm not too fussed that Geist, for example, may end up written using first edition text while the second edition is in production. The same happened for Promethean, and I've been using the Dust Bowl as the setting for my Promethean game. There wasn't much in the way of new mechanics, and I wasn't going to use the "canon" content much anyway.

                                                            I want to say I'll back it for the Geist era alone in the hopes Geist one day becomes a game I want to run. But honestly, I may just buy the PDF if that day ever comes instead.
