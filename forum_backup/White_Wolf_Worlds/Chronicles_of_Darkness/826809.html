    Amant Sacre: New Bloodline Attempt

    02-09-2016, 10:39 AM
    Here's a very rough sketch of a bloodline I just started working on. It's based on French Escorts in the 1800s that served the nobility and kept their damned secrets. Basically escorts who were very well trusted because discretion was their brand. Anyway, having trouble with the disciplines. I'd mostly like it to be based on their own vitae and not that of others. So any help or suggestions would be greatly appreciated. Like I said, it's very rough right now. Especially the discipline. The 1st and 5th one are the only ones I've made any real progress with. The Devotion may or may not be too close to Auspex 3 and so probably will have to go.

    Also, I gave them a messed up bane which I really like.

    Amant Sacré
    Parent Clan: Daeva
    Nicknames: Confidants
    Bloodline Disciplines: Auspex, Majesty, Resilience, Sang Sacré

    Reputation:
    Famous as trusted consorts and secret keepers, members of this bloodline often serve as companions to important and influential Kindred. Kindred trade in secrets and plots - for those who spend their entire lives withholding their machinations, nothing is more liberating than being able to trust your secrets with another soul. It is said that unburdening your soul to the Amant Sacre is the most relief some Ancient Kindred ever feel. Most Amant Sacre would suffer Final Death before revealing secrets told to them in confidence. This reputation means those of the Bloodline are greatly valued to those to whom they are close. To have an Amant Sacre as your personal consort or companion is infact considered a symbol of great status in some circles.


    Bloodline Gift: Sacréd Blood

    The blood of the Amant Sacré is sweeter and more inviting than any others, and their Blood Bonds gain additional benefits. They are considered to have the Honey Trap merit. As well, they gain the Beloved (pg 300) and Protected (pg 299) merits toward anyone who has tasted their blood. Additionally they are immune to the effects of their own blood, Amant Sacré do not gain blood bonds or roll for Vitae Addiction when drinking from anyone who has even a single drop of an Amant Sacré’s blood in them. Nor can they gain any sustenance from another of their Bloodline. In fact, the Amant Sacré almost never drink from another of their kind except in the rare cases of Diablerie.


    Bloodline Bane: Cursed Blood

    Though this is not a known fact, the easiest way to learn a secret from the Amant Sacré is to Diablerize them. This means members of this Bloodline often have a target on their backs among those few who know this flaw, especially as they grow more influential and they gather more secrets.

    Further, their blood seems to call for it. Add the Amant Sacré’s dots in Sang Sacré as bonus dice to any Diablerie attempts made upon them, as the long-held secrets of their blood yearn to break free. While still a Breaking Point, committing Diablerie on an Amant Sacré restores all spent Willpower, as the Diablerist devours not just their Soul, but their entire life and memory.

    If a vampire reaches the third stage of Blood Bond with an Amant Sacré who holds a Blood Secret directly involving him, his beast tastes it in the blood and he must immediately roll to resist Frenzy with a goal of Diablerizing the Amant Sacré. Further, Diablerizing an Amant Sacré always results in a permanent Diablerie addiction toward other Amant Sacré. For this reason they rarely keep secrets from their companions, and are quick to defend each other or investigate the murders of those in their bloodline. A Diablerist of one Amant Sacre can quickly become the Diablerist of them all.

    Bloodline Discipline: Sang Sacré
    The Amant Sacre know that blood holds more knowledge and power than most could ever dream of. The discipline of Sang Sacre displays the full mastery of the secrets of one’s blood. However, the discipline is a double edged sword, as every use involves the tasting of vitae and risks creating addiction in the Amant Sacre, or worse, addiction in their victims, who might crave their blood to the point of violence.

    Blood Secret (•)
    The keystone of the Bloodline’s reputation, the Amant Sacre’s blood thickens with its secrets. Users of this discipline may designate an event, memory, or piece of information a ‘Blood Secret. A Blood Secret cannot be revealed by any mundane means, and triggers a Clash of Wills against any Discipline or other Supernatural attempts to reveal or uncover its knowledge.

    The Blood’s Promise (••)
    The promise of the Amant's blood means different things to different people, but an Amant is not whole if she is on her own. Above all the Amant's blood is something she shares with others. Add the Amant's dots in Sang Sacre to her Maximum Vitae. As well, When drinking from the Amant, any Vampire with Blood Potency higher than her dots in Sang Sacre receives two vitae for every one the Amant shares and is immune to the effect of Blood Bonding. This makes the Amant Sacre very attractive companions for elder Kindred who can only feed from their own kind.

    The Blood’s Edict (•••)
    Roll: Presence + Empathy + Sang Sacre vs. Resolve + Blood Potency
    Cost: 2 Vitae
    Anyone who has tasted her vitae cannot speak of her Blood Secrets for their Sang Sacré in weeks, after which speaking of the subject requires the victim to spend a willpower and succeed on a Resolve + Composure roll with the Vampire’s Sang Sacré as a penalty. A dramatic failure probably does something bad. A success removes the Condition which I have not created yet.

    The Blood's Vengeance (••••)
    Cost: 1 Vitae
    Roll: Resolve + Empathy + Sang Sacre vs. Resolve + Blood Potency
    Duration: 1 Scene
    For the Amant Sacre, sharing blood is an act of trust. Their blood is not something to be taken by violence, but given freely. Those who would try to inflict their desire upon an Amant by force soon find themselves in for a rude awakening as the blood boils in their mouth and body. This is the Amant's primary defense against would-be Diablerists, as well as Elders who might come hunting for the Amant for vitae.

    Dramatic Failure: Something bad.
    Failure: Nothing. Sorry you're gonna die.
    Success: For the remainder of the scene, any Kindred who causes lethal damage to the Amant suffers her blood's vengeance.The blood burns like fire in the assailant's mouth and body, dealing 1 point of lethal damage for every point of Vitae that vampire has ingested or ingests for the duration.
    Exceptional Success: Something good.


    Tame the Blood’s Beast (•••••)
    Cost: 1 Vitae
    Duration:
    The Amant Sacre’s blood can tame the Beasts of even the most monstrous Kindred. Any Kindred who tastes the blood of an Amant Sacre with this discipline suffers their dots in Sang Sacre to all Discipline, Lashing Out and Clash of Will rolls against any member of this Bloodline. Further, once per day they may immediately end frenzy or incite frenzy in the vampire (with terms set by the Amant Sacre).


    Bloodline Devotions

    The Blood’s Revelation
    Auspex 1, Sang Sacre 1
    A creature’s life is retained in its blood. This Discipline steals the memories from the subject's mind, ripping them in scattered shards and allowing the user to recall events as they happened to the victim. Though the process is painless for the victim and the victim retains her memory, some victims will experience momentary disorientation if they try to remember any of the memories that have been "stolen."
    The user must have tasted (and swallowed) some of the subject's blood within the last hour. If he has done so, the werewolf is able to meditate on the act of consuming another creature's blood and unlock the secrets of the victim's life. The Uratha can access a store of the subject's memories, which are now within his own mind. It is confusing at first, though the werewolf is always aware that the events "never happened to him," as such. Storytellers should feel free to describe the chaotic nature of the memories that come in a broken stream of information.



    Basic notes I'm trying to hit: Based on the private French escorts of the 1800s who served kings and kept their secrets. Famous for being able to keep secrets, trusted among Kindred. Considered a status symbol to have one as your companion. Often very beloved by their companions due to that sweet sweet vitae. Once you diablerize them you get every memory they ever had, including knowing that diablerie is the best way to get the secrets of others of their kind. Bonuses to diablerizing them. Also permanently become addicted to diablerizing the bloodline if you ever do it. Their blood is great and good at keeping secrets.They always investigate suspicious deaths of their kind and violently hunt down Kindred who diablerize one, because of the permanent addiction. Anyone who does it will definitely hunt down their bloodline forever. Very close knit.
