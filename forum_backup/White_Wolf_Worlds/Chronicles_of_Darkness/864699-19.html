                I know this particular post is kind of dated, but I think it's important...

                Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
                I think you're right. It's not terribly clear in the core rules.
                If you go back to the 1e core rulebook, it becomes clear that you can have modifiers above -5/+5, but not by a single source.
                So you were correct!

                1e Core Rulebook, Page 124:
                As a rule, a single modifier never exceeds five, whether as a bonus or penalty. That means all tools and situational effects are each rated one to five. [...] That is, they’re all cumulative.
                The total max of -5/+5 is an optional rule:

                1e Core Rulebook, Page 124:
                Or your Storyteller may simply consider all the factors at work in terms of a one-to-five range and come up with one total that applies for an effort. He might allow players to calculate some bonuses, but he summarily decrees that weather and character disabilities make an effort “a trying task,” and three dice are removed from a player’s pool.
