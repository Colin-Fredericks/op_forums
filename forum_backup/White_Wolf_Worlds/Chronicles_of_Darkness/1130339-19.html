                                Originally posted by LostLight View Post
                                ammm if I may...

                                a) would there any new playable/antagonist template/splat which is directly connected to the Contagion? Like, could you play a Hurt Locker's Infect which were created by the Contagion?

                                b) we know that the Contagion effects supernatural beings, but how it influence the mortal world? I mean, we know that at least one Faction believes its origin is from human actions (the Cryptocracy's Social Entropy theory), but how do mortal society influenced by those outbreaks?

                                c) are there attempts to create a vaccine against the Contagion, and how successful were they through the years? While I assume that eliminating the Contagion is out of option, are there things which offer a measure of protection from it, or perhaps even allow to heal those infected by it? I expect Cheiron to work on such a thing, for example.

                                d) what is your favorite Faction, and how easy would it be to create new Factions (Sworn/False/Something else? After all, you mentioned that some groups may have their own attitude toward the Contagion which does not align with either the Sworn or False, like Belial's Brood or the Bale Hounds).

                                e) would we see "guest appearances" from old favorite NPCs? Like the Unholy, Zagerus, the Nameless, Theleme, Dr Brine, Blind Man, Unwashed Issac, etc?

                                f) how similar are different outbreaks of the Contagion? I mean, are there always the same symptoms, or that for someone without any knowledge about the subject two outbreaks would look like two very different occult infections? Are there "Contagion strains", with it evolving through time and each creates different symptoms or twist the occult world in different ways?

                                g) If a different occult disaster would strike the planet (like, say, an alien/Void Leviathan invasion or the equivalent of a supernatural earthquake), as in the level of something which would force different monsters to band together in a similar fashion to the Contagion, would the Sworn/False division and methods of operation when dealing the crises would be more or less the same, or are there cases in which a different "Sworn/False" division would form? (so the question is, in fact, how strongly tied is the Sworn/False division to the Contagion itself, and how much it is tied to the group's ideology and world view. I mean, would Naglfar's Army would be considered allies in a case of the awakening of an elder god, with them willing to sacrifice their life while fighting the beast, while some other Sworn group would turn out supporting the beast in an attempt to study/worship it?)

                                That's what I have right now. Feel free to ignore those questions which you would not like to answer right now :P
                                a) That's an interesting question. One of the chapters introduces a Contagion-specific entity (unlike a Contagious vampire or Contagious werewolf). There's no reason an Infected from Hurt Locker couldn't be one such creature, or the alternative, as a mortal (or whatever) infected with Contagion. If that makes sense. As for the pure Contagious as playable? Not immediately.

                                b) Contagion affects the mortal world as well, but mortals (unless they're hunters or otherwise connected to the supernatural) are unlikely to see it as an interdimensional plague so much as "a crippling swathe of depression afflicting Odense," to take one example.

                                c) There are ways to prevent Contagion, and of course, each of the Sworn believe they have the best solution.

                                d) My favourite faction is either the Rosetta Society (on the Sworn side) or Machiavelli Gambit (on the False side). It would be very easy to create others, and something I would be considering if we ever explored Contagion in a Dark Era or through companion books.

                                e) At least two of those characters have made an appearance as of first drafts.

                                f) Contagion's symptoms vary across location, depending on the cause or the carrier. Calling different types of Contagion its different strains would be accurate.

                                g) If a different catastrophe were to occur, falling in with the Sworn or False as you would against Contagion would have a certain logic to it, though some of the factions are more focused on Contagion above all else. Would the factions switch around at all between Sworn and False? In the example where an elder god awakens, the Crucible Initiative (of the False) would likely fall in with the Sworn. The Machiavelli Gambit could swing one way or the other. I imagine Naglfar's Army would throw arms down and become cultists...

                                    Originally posted by Iceblade44 View Post
                                    Thank you very much for the generosity. Now if it's okay.

                                    1) how big of a powerup are Vectors? What can you get with them that you couldn't get with a splats main powers(Disciplines, Arcana, Exploits and Embeds that sort of thing)

                                    2) does the contagion pose a serious threat to all splats, will some be more invested to others, and are those who just see it as an inconvienence.

                                    3) in your opinion what do you think is the biggest difficulty you guys had in writing this with the intent of it being a crossover chronicle.

                                    4) but also. What do you think is the biggest excitement of working on this as a crossover chronicle.

                                    1) They're a solid power-up, but they work best if you're in a group containing other members of the same faction. The advantage you get from them is in many ways a collaborative one.

                                    2) All splats should have cause for concern / reasons to want to use Contagion to their advantage.

                                    3) None of them, really. We have access to all the existing 2e material (including books currently being written).

                                    4) There's undoubtedly going to be a lot of pressure to get this right. I know we won't please everyone, but as long as we please some players and Storytellers and provide some new chronicle ideas, we've done our job.
