    Dungeons & Darkness (English version 1.1)
    Introduction
    Hello, reader. Welcome to Dungeon & Darkness
    If you are here, you really want to play Dungeons & Dragons/Pathfinder as a theme, but you’re probably not happy with the d20 System. Perhaps you always played World of Darkness and d20 System feels too combat heavy for you and not exactly that good for sandbox storytelling. Perhaps you feel like the World of Darkness is more realistic. Perhaps you feel like d20 System is just too complicated for your players and for yourself, and you do prefer a more easy and intuitive system. Or maybe you just want to try this game out for fun’s sake.
    Whatever. I converted the d20 System to the Storyteller System because I really liked the idea and I felt I had the capability to do it. And I knew many people would have liked what I did. Thank you everyone for your support, have a nice campaign, adventure or read!


    Wait, this, this and this is referenced outside those books!!1!!!one!!!
    Everything that was not in these books was probably taken by the World of Darkness: Book of Spirits and Promethean: Pandora's Book. Mainly Numina. If you happen not to have those books, just ignore the Numina involved.
    I know that's a lot of book keeping to start with. But spells are referenced with the page number and they are likely the most part of the book keeping. You couldn't expect me to create all the spells from the start. Contents
    Chapter 1: Character creation explains what you need to create your player character, with its race, class and the new mechanics.
    Chapter 2: Magic and spells defines what you need to know about magic, spells, magic items, special materials and the planes of existence.
    Chapter 3: Monsters is mainly aimed at Storytellers, with lots of examples of monsters, from dragons to devils and much more.
    Appendix 1: Prestige classes describes some of the prestige classes.
    Appendix 2: Guidelines for pre-GMC games describes how to play Dungeons & Darkness without God-Machine Chronicles Rules Update.
    Appendix 3: Conditions lists new Conditions.
    What you need to play? You need a copy of the World of Darkness corebook, and the God Machine Chronicle Rules Update, available at DrivethruRPG.com for free, OR a copy of God Machine Chronicle though note that using the Appendix 2 you might ignore the book almost entirely except for ephemeral creatures rules.
    Werewolf the Forsaken and Mage the Awakening are also optional, but recommended. They might be useful to better understand the magic system and for reference for some spells.
    Wait, this, this and this are referenced outside these books!
    Skill tricks are from World of Darkness: Mirrors (and aren't needed to enjoy the game), World of Darkness: Book of Spirits has some Numina that might be useful. Should you not have access to those books, you might buy them or ignore that specific rule entirely.
    Theme: Magical Adventures Dungeons & Darkness is a game of adventure, great conflict, mysterious places, wonderful and intimidating creatures, and mystic items. A game where good and evil are a real thing. Behind every adventure there is a new mystery, a new story for the bards to tell, or a terrible secret to be buried under the ruins of an old dungeon. You might be adventuring for many reason. You might be in search of adventure to change your boring and grim life, to know yourself, to right the wrongs, to discover the world and grow knowledge, to witness many magical wonders, become rich and powerful, or even because you just got involved and cannot turn back anymore because it will cost you more than you’re willing to give. It’s a life of messy fights, discovery, intrigues, insidious and powerful magics, and cruel monsters. It’s a game where deeds matter, and where you have to forge your own destiny through them.
    Mood: Perpetual Conflict The conflict between Good and Evil, between Law and Chaos, is everywhere. Powerful people have the capability to make their way of thought, to make their desires reality. The clash between powerful people creates conflict, that could be solved with a swift fight, or with a neverending war. Rival arcanists battle each other to determine who’s the best. Political figures debate to rule their nations. Tyrants enforce the law oppressing the populations. Citizens struggle with the bossy and cruel dragons, who want to wrestle the control of cities, villages or even nations. Churches of the many faiths fight to establish their gods’ will. Heaven makes war to the Hell.
    Where will you be when the conflict arises?
    Will you empower the conflict or will you end it?
    Is it even possible to cease all conflicts? Is it right?

      Arcanist
      Hexblade Tradition
      Class skills: Occult, Subterfuge, Weaponry
      • Hex curse
      • • Aura of unluck 1/day
      • • • Hexblade, Arcane restistance
      • • • • Aura of unluck 2/day
      • • • • • Greater hex curse
      Hex curse: At 1st level, The hexblade can bestows a number of hex curse per day up to his Arcanist level. The character rolls Presence + Arete contested by Resolve + Arete of a target within 20 yards, and if the target fails it will recieve a -2 to all Attribute and Skill rolls for a scene or an hour, the shortest. Bestowing a hex curse is a Reflexive action during the hexblade's turn.
      Aura of unluck: At 2nd level once, at 4th twice, the hexblade can surround itself by an aura of unluck, forcing all attack against the hexblade to be rolled twice and take the worst result. This effect lasts 1 turn per hexblade level and can be activated as a Reflexive action during the hexblade's turn.
      Hexblade: At 3rd level and every level after, the character gains one dot in the Hexblade merit.
      Arcane resistance: At 3rd level the hexblade has +1 to contest all spells.
      Greater hex curse: At 5th level the penality of the hex curse becomes -3, and it will last all day.[/spoiler]

      Spellthief Tradition
      Class Skills: Lacerny, Occult, Stealth
      • Sneak Attack 1, Steal spell (1) Trapfinding
      • • Arcane sight, Steal spell (2)
      • • • Spellthief, Steal spell (3)
      • • • • Steal magical effects, Steal spell (4)
      • • • • • Steal spell (5)
      Trapfinding: A spellthief gains the 9 Again rule when finding traps. The spellthief can also disable magical traps.
      Sneak Attack: At 1st level when the spellthief attacks an enemy unable to percieve him, unable to move, caught by surprise, or when attacked from two opposite sides the character adds +1 to the roll.
      Steal spell: When successfully using a sneak attack, the spellthief is immediately aware of all prepared or known spells of the target and can choose to steal a spell of first level and a spell per day. Within one hour from the theft, the spellthief can use that spell, casting it regularily. Meanwhile the spellcaster loses the access to that spell per day for a minute. The SLB for stolen spells is always +1. At every level thereafter, the spellthief can steal spells of one level higher.
      Arcane sight: At 2nd level, the spellthief has detect magic always on, but can choose to turn it off with a Reflexive action. The spellthief can reactivate it with an Istantaneous action. The Potency of the effect is 1.
      Spellthief: At 3rd level, and every level thereafter, the spellthief gains 1 dot in the Spellthief merit.
      Steal magical effects: At 4th level, the spellthief can steal one active magical effect instead of a spell, and the effect is transferred to the spellthief.

      Warlock Tradition
      Class Skills: Occult, Intimidate, Persuasion
      • Detect magic, Eldritch blast, Warlock
      • • Fiendish resilience (bashing)
      • • • Energy resistance
      • • • • Fiendish resilience (lethal)
      • • • • • Imbue item
      Detect magic: At 1st level the warlock can roll Wits + Occult to cast detect magic as a spell-like ability, at will.
      Eldritch blast: The warlock can throw a blast of pure magical energy to cast as a ranged weapon. The warlock must roll Presence + Ranged. The damage is 1L, but becomes one point higher every level.
      Warlock: At 1st level and every level thereafter the warlock gains 1 dot in the Warlock merit.
      Fiendish resilience (bashing): At 2nd level, once per day the warlock can summon its dark powers to heal 1 bashing per Presence turns.
      Energy resistance: Select one element between acid, cold, electricity and fire. You suffer 1 damage less by that element.
      Fiendish resilience (lethal): At 3rd level, you can choose to heal 1 lethal instead of bashing with your Fiendish resilience.
      Imbue item: When creating a magic item, you don't need to cast the exact spell.

      Warmage Tradition
      Class Skills: Intimidate, Occult, Warfare
      • Blast edge, Sudden Widen, Warmage
      • • Advanced learning
      • • • Sudden Empower 1/vg
      • • • • Advanced learning
      • • • • • Sudden Empower 2/vg
      Blast edge: When dealing damage with a spell, the warmage can inflict 1 extra bashing damage. A single spell cannot benefit more than once of the blast edge: ex. a magic missile thrown at more than one people is going to inflict 1 extra bashing only to one target. Same for things like fireball and other area spells.
      Sudden Widen: At first level, once per day, the warmage can use affect a spell like it had the Widen Spell merit for free, but only upgrading its Target or Area factor by one.
      Warmage: At 1st level and every level thereafter the warmage gains 1 dot in the Warmage merit.
      Advanced learning: At 2nd and 4th level, the warmage selects one sorcerer/wizard rote to add to his known spells. The spell must be of the Evokation school.
      Sudden Empower: Once per day at 3rd level, twice at 5th, the Warmage can benefit from the Empower Spell merit for free while casting a spell.

      Wu Jen Tradition
      Class Skills: Academics, Occult, Politics
      • Elemental mastery, Watchful Spirit, Wu Jen, Taboo
      • • Spell secret
      • • • Spell secret
      • • • • Spell secret
      • • • • • Spell secret
      Elemental mastery: At 1st level, Select one of the following elements: earth, fire, metal, water and wood. When casting spells belonging to this elements, you have +1 to the casting roll. At 5th level the bonus becomes +2.
      Watchful spirit: Once per day the wu jen can reroll the Initiative.
      Wu jen: At 1st level and every level thereafter the wu jen gains 1 dot in the Wu Jen merit.
      Spell secret: At 2nd level and every level thereafter the wu jen can select one of its known spell from the spellbook and treat it as always enhanced by one of the following Metamagic merits: Silent Spell, Still Spell (both without expending Willpower), Extended Spell or Widen Spell (only the first spellcasting factor).
      Taboo: To maintain their supernatural power, wu jen must abide by certain taboos that might seem inconsequential to other characters but are vitally important to the wu jen. If a wu jen violates one of her taboos, she cannot cast any more spells that day. A wu jen must choose one taboo per level. Sample taboos include "cannot eat meat", "Cannot own more than she can carry" "Must make daily offerings" "Cannot bathe" "Cannot cut her hair", "Cannot touch a dead body", "Cannot drink alcohol", "Cannot wear a certain color" "Cannot light a fire", "Cannot sit facing in a certain direction".

              New Skill Tricks



              Occult
              Except Mental Healing, all Occult Skill Tricks use tropes a bit out of place for D&D
              Arcane zoology (Story Advancement): The characteris expert when dealing with misterious and magical creatures. When rolling Occult to understand a monster and its powers, double the Occult dots.
              Magic Arts Defense (Extra talent): The character knows a minor ritual that allows it to obtain a minor protection against magic, allowing him to resist against a spell. The ritual needs a calm place and 10 minutes. The character gains +2 to contest the next magical effect on him.
              Magical Aptitude (Extra talent):The character has innate magic power within himself. The character gains the ability to cast a first level rote as a spell-like ability (Wits + Occult) once per day. The spell can be any first level rote, regardless of the class it belongs.

              Ride
              Ride is mostly like Drive. Two Skill Tricks still work for Ride: Lose 'em, Shortcuts.
              Expert Rider (Extra Talent): When trying to put its mount under control (in case it was scared by combat or other things that usually scare mounts), the rider can add double its Ride dots for rolls.
              Mounted rush (Combat): The character is adept at using the mount as a weapon, making it run against opponents. When doing so, he moves the mount against an enemy, and rolls Dexterity + Ride. If it's successful, the rider rolls the successes or the mount's Size, whichever is higher, dealing bashing damage +2. If the mount crashes on an armored or really hard object or creature, it suffers 1 lethal damage and the rider takes -1 to all Ride rolls for the rest of the scene.
              Ride by attack (Combat): The character is expert at hit and run tactics while mounted. While doing so the mount and its rider move up to the Mount's Speed, and the rider can choose to attack one adjacent creature within the path of the mount with a Melee attack, at -2. Long weapons like spears, halberds, naginatas and quarterstaves do not suffer this penality.

              Warfare
              Battlefield Master (Extra Talent): The character can ignore up to 2 points of penality to Warfare rolls caused by hazardous or difficult terrain.
              Quick Skirmish (Time saver): When confronted in quick battles against an army of lower Arete or Rank than the character, and having an army at his disposal the character can expend 1 Willpower point and roll Intelligence + Warfare against against the highest Intelligence and Warfare in the enemy group. The roll is penalized by the difference between the Size of the armies, whether to the attacker or to the defender, the character cannot use this Skill Trick. If the character wins, the leader of the roll must choose whether to take the Beaten Tilt or to deal an inconvenient to the character's army but otherwise be forced to run away safely.
              Impending ambush (Penality): The character is almost always alert for danger, the character gives Warfare as a penality to all Stealth rolls to ambush him or its allies.

                  Priest
                  Favored Soul Tradition
                  Favored souls cast divine spells by means of an innate connection rather than through laborious training and prayer, so their divine connection is natural rather than learned.
                  Class Skills: Academics, Empathy, Occult
                  • Favored Soul
                  • • Deity favored weapon
                  • • • Energy resistance
                  • • • • Wings
                  • • • • • Divine resilience
                  Favored Soul: At 1st level and every level thereafter the favored soul gains 1 dot in the Favored Soul merit.
                  Deity favored weapon: At 2nd level the character's gains a Specialization with the favored weapon of his deity, in the appropriate Skill.
                  Energy resistance: Select one element between acid, cold, electricity and fire. You suffer 1 damage less by that element.
                  Wings: At 4th level, the favored soul gets feathery or batlike wings, allowing it to fly at double its land Speed.
                  Divine resilience: At 5th level, a lawful favored soul gains Armor 1 (except against attacks from chaotic outsiders and chaotic weapons), while a chaotic favored soul gains Armor 1 (except against attacks from lawful outsiders and lawful weapons). A neutral favored soul chooses either.

                  Shugenja Tradition
                  The shugenja is a divine spellcaster who casts spells by attuning himself to the primal energies around him and focusing such energy through his body to produce magical effects.
                  Class Skills: Academics, Investigate, Occult
                  • Shugenja, Elemental focus, Percieve Element
                  • •
                  • • • Elemental magic +1
                  • • • •
                  • • • • • Elemental magic +2
                  Shugenja: At 1st level and every level thereafter the shugenja gains 1 dot in the Shugenja merit.
                  Elemental focus: At 1st level the character chooses one element between Air, Earth, Fire and Water. Opposed element spells (like Air vs Earth or Fire vs Water) are not available to Shugenja.
                  Percieve element: Expending 1 Willpower point, the Shugenja can roll Investigation + Arete. One success reveals the presence, the Size and the location of one of the four elements, within 6 yards. Two successes reveal if the element is natural or a magical effect. Tre successes reveal if it's a creature or an object. Four successes reveal the exact nature of the object/creature (ex. If it's a fire genie or a fire elemental, if the object is gold or stone, if the air is safe or ill). Alternatively, the successes can be used to widen the area of the perception by 6 yards. Living creatures belong to all elements, and they can be revealed with any element.
                  Elemental magic: At 3rd level, spells belonging to the elemental focus of the Shugenja have +1 (+2 at 5th level) to activation rolls.

                  Spirit Shaman Tradition
                  By bargaining with living spirits, the spirit shaman gains power over the natural world and mighty divine magic.
                  Class skills: Medicine,Occult, Survival.
                  • Spirit shaman, Chastise spirits, Spirit sight
                  • • Ward against spirits
                  • • • Spirit Form, Exorcism
                  • • • • Recall spirit
                  • • • • • Favored of the Spirits
                  Spirit Shaman: At 1st level and every level thereafter the spirit shaman gains 1 dot in the Spirit Shaman merit.
                  Chastise spirit: At 1st level, the spirit shaman can damage hostile spirits with Wits + Spirit Shaman level, dealing 1 bashing per success to all spirits within 9 yards from the spirit shaman itself. A spirit shaman can chastise spirit only (Wits) times per day.
                  Detect spirits: At 1st level, the character can roll Wits + Occult to Reflexively find creatures within sight for the rest of the scene, even in the Ethereal Plane. Any penality for anti-detection effects apply.
                  Ward against spirits: At 2rd level, once per day, taking 10 minutes to practice the right ritual, the spirit shaman grants one creature per level the capability to affect spirits like they were manifested, and +1 to their Defenses and contested rolls.
                  Spirit form: At 3rd level, once per day, the spirit shaman can turn incorporeal for up to 1 minute. While in spirit form, the spirit smana can damage both corporeal and incorporeal creatures, damaging with its touch (a 0B weapon). It also loses the Armor derived by physical means (like armor and thick skin)
                  Exorcist: At 3rd level, a spirit shaman gains +2 to all abjure and exorcism rolls.
                  Recall Spirit: At 4th level, once per day, a spirit shaman can resurrect a creature dead within one turn. Necromancy spells, a ruined corpse or any spell that seals away the soul of the target prevents this kind of resurrection.
                  Favored of the Spirits: At 5th level, performing a rite that requires 8 hours, if a spirit shaman would be killed by an attack or spell, the attack or spell is instead nullified, and the protection from the rite discharges. Performing this rite is exhausting, and requires the expenditure of a Willpower dot.

                  edit: What is a spirit?
                  All incorporeal undead, all fey, all elementals, creatures in astral body, aligned outsiders (angels, archons, devils, demons and the like). Elementals and fey are called spirits of nature, ghosts are called as spirits of the dead.

                      NEW CONDITIONS


                      Controller
                      The character has gained insight when using mind-affecting magic. When using enchantment spells, the Duration is enhanced by one Duration Factor like with the Extended Spell merit, without penality.
                      Resolution: The character uses the benefit.
                      Beat: N/A

                      Frayer
                      The character has gained insight when using magic to damage. When using a spell that deals energy damage (fire – cold – electricity – acid), all target suffer 1 more bashing damage.
                      Resolution: The character uses the benefit.
                      Beat: N/A

                      Spellslinger
                      The character has gained insight when casting magic. If the character fails to cast a spell, the character can decide to use this Condition to do gain at least 1 success. If the roll was a chance die, the caster gains a single die instead.
                      Resolution: The mastery of the character guides him, the worst is gone. The Condition is resolved as above.
                      Beat: N/A

                      Seer
                      The character has gained insight when using divination magic. When using spells like divination, augury and similar spells, the character can ask one more question.
                      Resolution: The character uses the benefit.
                      Beat: N/A

                      Veil Master
                      The character has gained insight when using illusion magic. When using illusions, they are counted as Potency +1 when they are to be detected with unveiling magic.
                      Resolution: The character uses the benefit.
                      Beat: N/A

                      Spell havoc
                      The character's magical control is having problem in balancing magical energies, messing with its spells. The spell is no longer under the caster's control. It affects a randomly chosen target (or targets if multiple were factored into the casting) instead of the caster’s declared target(s). The caster himself is a potential random victim. The new target must be of the same type (person, object, location, etc.). For example, if the caster targeted a person, then the pool of random targets includes only people. If the caster is the only viable target present, then he is the target of his own spell unless he was its originally intended target, in which case the spell affects a target of a different kind, such as an object. The new target — including the mage himself, if he is the spell’s new target — can contest or resist the spell if it is normally allowed. In addition, the caster must roll Resolve + Composure. Every spell casted while in this Condition, applies -1 to this roll.With a success, the spell effect is unaltered. With a failure the desired effect is somehow reversed.
                      Resolution: The character must take some time to meditate (Target number 3) to rebalance its psyche or take an Exceptional Success at the Resolve + Composure roll.
                      Beat: When a really important casted spell is reversed because of the Resolve + Composure roll.

                                          Without getting too far off topic, I would like to respond to what you mentioned in the other thread. I think you misunderstood my issue with alignment. Good/Evil, Law/Chaos as tangible things, and characters being rated on where they fall on both scaled is an important part of D&D's identity, I agree. That's why I didn't recommend they replace Virtue and Vice, even though they are the most obvious parallel in the storytelling system. Rather, i think it is very important that both axies be measured, but on a sliding scale rather than a simple positive/neutral/negative for each. Thinking on it, I agree with the suggestion of having two Morality stats, one for Good/Evil and one for Law/Chaos. Additionally, since D&D has a long tradition of alignment shifting based on the character's actions, all you need to do is have "breaking points" that apply in either direction. So killing innocent people will drive you towards Evil, while selfless acts will drive you towards Good. Upholding and enforcing law and order will push you towards Lawful, and breaking laws or disrupting established governing systems will push you towards Chaos. Neutrality can be maintained either by keeping a balance, or simply not getting involved.

                                                  Eidolon (Prestige class)
                                                  Tier 1:                                                   Rank 1 ghost, must have had at least Arete 1 while alive, Resolve 2.
                                                  Phantom Arete: The eidolon uses its Rank instead of Arete to gain class levels, regaining all class levels it had in life.
                                                  Influences and Numina: The eidolon retains all of its Influences and Numina. The character uses Presence + Resolve to uses Influences, and Resolve + Occult to use Numina.
                                                  Wraith: The eidolon uses the flesh rules for Attributes, Skills and Merits even while incorporeal.
                                                  Tier 2: Rank 3 ghost
                                                  Influences and Numina: The eidolon gains bonus 1 Numina.
                                                  Tier 3: Rank 5 ghost
                                                  Numina: The eidolon gains bonus 1 Numina.

                                                  I treated as a prestige class, while I know there are some prestige classes in the Ghostwalk for ghosts, I didn't want to go that deep, as it is an indeed niche part of D&D.
                                                  In any case, both Eidolon and Eidoloncer are done this way. Any class can take this prestige class, and still gain spells, numina and things like that.
