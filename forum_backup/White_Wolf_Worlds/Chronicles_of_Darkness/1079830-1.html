    Father Michael S. O'Neill
    Michael A. O'Neil was born in Brooklyn New York and grew up in the church because that was the only really safe area for a young man growing up. From the moment he herd the bible verse Psalms 22:19 "O my god I trust in you; Let me not be ashamed Let not my enemies triumph over me". When he turned 18 he joined up with the military where the horrors of war and combat only strengthened Father O'Neil's faith in the lord and he became known as a The preacher on his military base. He served two tours in the military before returning to civilian life and joining the church fully. Back in the states it was not long before he was assigned to a church in his old neighborhood. Being back home he saw how much drugs and gang violence took a toll on the area so he set forth in his righteous mission to fix the church and bring hope back to the area. It took then a year to fully repair the church like new with the help of Mother Mary a Nun who served in the military like he did. The old gothic church they were given was designed to with war and from here they spread the word of god to the masses. Bring in more followers then the church had seen in a very long time. Father O'Neil made it into the local news for standing between 4 gang members and the man they were attempting to rob a homeless man. He was able to not only talk the men down through the word of the lord but all 4 men turned their life around the joined the church while in jail. Even though he has helped change his neighborhood for the better in the last few months it has taken a stark turn. Gangs have returned to the area, many young people have started using drugs. Seeing this change he set forth to once again try and help his neighborhood but this time forces of devil attacked him. During a late night session during a black out in the city. Two men entered the church and demanded money from the church. The father went along with it taking to two men to where the money was and handing it over. When one of the men became belligerent with one of nuns. The father tried to stop it only be smacked an inhuman distance away. When the father go up the two men in their anger with nun tore her heart out. Sending her blood across the fathers office. Seeing this horror rather than becoming frozen in it. The father reacted and attacked one of the young men hitting him with enough force that the father could hear the man's skull crack. Knocking him to the floor and making him drop the shotgun he had hidden under his clothing. The second man grew claws and slashed off Father O'Neil cutting his chest deeply. Ignoring the pain the father raised the shotgun and fired directly at the man's heart killing him in a single blow. Right before Michael O'Neil's eyes the man became a shriveled corpse. Knowing they were not normal he turned in time to see the other man running inhumanly fast to the door exiting the church. From this day on Father O'Neil has devoted himself to learning about these creatures and to defend himself, the church and everyone else from these monsters.

At night he invites over other ghosts and shares out his extra essences for information which he sells to various Geist's who come and visit him for information paying him in various ways. He has been doing this racket for years and even holds ghostly poker games on the weekend. Death only made things more interesting for him.

                    Madam Mercurial
                    Second Born to the house of the Silver Needle. Madam Mercurial from a young age showed a great aptitude for anything she tried even magic. The young Madam Mercurial sought approval from both her parents back then and her mother being a hidden agent of the throne saw great potential in Madam Mercurial. Her mother spent years trying to twist her mind to turn her into a good little agent of the throne. Using mental magic on the girl and good old fashion manipulation her mother tried to make Madam Mercurial murder her father and siblings. When the night came to put her mother's murderous plans into motion. Even though everything in her told her she wanted it. It was deeply terrifying seeing her plans go into motion. When she did see her eyes opened back to the fallen world. Where she found that she stopped herself from killing her little brother. When she did her mother sensed the girls awakening and attacked. As she did the woman inside of the mind of Madam Mercurial her Goetic demon manifested itself and defended Madam Mercurial till forces of the Mysterium were able to get to her. During her rescue her mother escaped into the astral, vanishing deep inside. Once saved she saw the pain her father's eyes the betrayal he felt from her harming her family even if it was under the control of her mother's magic. To never fall into another's plans to force her to make a choice she does not want too. Years have passed since she awakened at 10 years old. Now at 17, she is seen as a trusted member of the Mysterium. Even so, she still feels the coldness of her father's gaze. In her normal life, she works as a researcher at her families company known as the Silver corporation. She tries to avoid her brother and older sister out of shame for what she has done in her past. When she is free she spends almost every waking hour of her life honing her magic and her mind to a razor sharp edge. In the Mysterium, she works as a researcher also studying all of the artifacts brought back brought back explores. Her Familiar is a humanoid fox called Umbra
