                    So the problem with Golem, Eidolon (sans Forces conjunctive effect), and most Quickened Corpses (sans Corpse Mask) is that they trigger Dissonance. A Create Life body is the only one you can bring around in normal society. Plus, Eidolon would take up a ridiculous amount of Mana to keep fueled, as we've discussed on this thread before. And zombies are not hygienic, and will likely rot before long without ANOTHER spell to preserve them like Veil of Moments.

                    Bind Spirit can give Goetia Manifestation Conditions, but that requires Spirit 4 + Mind 4. Goetic Summons with Spirit or Death 2 can allow the summoned Goetia to Materialize for the duration. Depending on the Goetia you might be able to get away with Mortal Mask for having it walk around with you, but it's still more prone to tripping Dissonance than the created Sleepwalker. Which just means wasted effort, and possibly the death of your friend.

                    You could also use Astral Grimoire's Lasting Reach effects to create a Dissonance-immune Goetia, although that risks the normal problems of losing a piece of your mind whenever you summon it out. If you scribe it into the Tenemos or Anima Mundi, then you risk those sentient wounds in the Astral whenever you summon it.

                    If you allow the hypothetical Goetia to possess the Create Life body, you have several more issues than just creating a straight Sleepwalker. The Sleepwalker only has one spell to maintain/relinquish, which you could also just offload onto them as a banner-bearer. Whereas the Goetia has at least three (Psychic Genesis, Create Life, Bind Spirit, plus probably Bind Familiar), which they can't carry for you. The Goetia's meat-puppet is also a feral wreck when it isn't being Possessed, which means it can't really be left unattended. You could have a rotating roster of Goetia keeping the body docile, but each one is another two to three spells to maintain or relinquish.

                    A sentient destiny or thinking timeline just sounds like Ananke, which are firmly in the wheelhouse of Archmages. A neat idea, but just a bit too far off the esoteric side of things for a baseline mage.

                                        Originally posted by Cauthon View Post
                                        What's the differentiating point between a combined spells and conjunctive effects? For example, is there anything stopping you from adding a Prime 2 conjunctive effect to a given spell that obfuscates it ála Supernal Veil?
                                        Conjunctional spells are one effect, combined spells are two effect with one spell control slot.

                                        So making Life with conjunctional Mind 5 lets you make one thing (which happens to have a mind and a body). Or Word of Command is one effect (activating a magical object) with optional conjunctional Arcana for different types of magical item.

                                        I think all the conjunctional examples follow this pattern... It is like how the reach effect are still doing the one effect but modified or extended in some way. It is still one spell doing one effect.
