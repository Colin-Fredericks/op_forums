The power sets of endowments would likely have to grow more generic, while the flavour would come from organisation the hunter belongs to...

                    personally, I always see Endowments not as what a conspiracy's Hunter can do, but as what the Hunter's conspiracy can provide- that is, a singular hunter may get a single power equivalent to a specific Endowment or even develop it all alone- but the ability to widespread teach Endowments to many members in a relatively safe and reliable way should be something that only a Conspiracy should do. It means something about the role of Endowments in the setting, for unlike other games where the Kewel Powerz define the character, Endowments define the character's organization, status and connection to other hunters. It reinforce the theme of teamwork and "together we stand", showing that hunters may be weak alone in the dark, but together they can accomplish new feats which are not available to the singular hunter. Plus, Endowments represent the Conspiracy's history, resources and collective knowledge which shape its identity. It is not just a "supernatural merit", but something which ties all the members together and show them they are a part of something bigger. When you shoot a monster with a Mjolnir Cannon, you don't just use a cool piece of tech- you blast the bastard with the might of the United States of America!*

                    At least, that's how I view the role of Endowments in the setting- not as supernatural powers, but as the power of the collective, the highest humanity can reach as a group. This is why I don't think Endowment Access or definition of Compact verse Conspiracy should be divorced in general from Tiers, especially when you look at the organization's Tier less as the "number of members" and more as the "reach and level of influence an organization has". It is not about having hunters who identify themselves as members all across the world- it is about being able to activate members all across the world. The Lucifuge may be small, but the Lady's grip over all 666 members is powerful enough to let her deploy them all over Europe, the US and beyond- something Network Zero may find a lot harder to accomplish.

                    In sort, I'm eagerly waiting for the future Conspiracy/Endowments blog posts to see how Monica would place them in the setting.

                    *yeah, I know that the Lucifuge and the Otodo's Endowments seem to be an exception, but both are exceptions in general- and maybe some thing about them would change in the new edition. Still, I'll argue that while the potential for Castigations is found for every Child of 7th Generation, only the members of the Lucifuge know how to shape them into specific rites which were developed by the Lady (I think it was hinted to be the case in the core book. I'm not sure, need to check it). The same goes for the Otodo.

                                    There's really no circumstance where an Endowment can be stolen. Replicated? Absolutely. But outright stolen with no way of protecting it, recovering it or getting more? Don't think so.

                                        There's really no circumstance where an Endowment can be stolen. Replicated? Absolutely. But outright stolen with no way of protecting it, recovering it or getting more? Don't think so.

                                        the "problem" (if you call it as such, for seeing it as a problem or not is depends on individual storyteller) is that mechanics are meant in order to tell the story better. The way that Endowments are build right now makes you to tell a certain story- a story about teamwork, ideology and shared history. You can't just have the merit, you need to join a Conspiracy to do so, which means you must (at least partially) agree with the group's agenda and point of view on the world. You can't call down Benedictions without joining the Catholic Church, you can't evoke the Goetic Gospels without agreeing the magic should not be destroyed. Making Endowments into something any hunter (or actually, any mortal) could learn freely under the right conditions would harm that story- which could work is you want to play a Supernatural style monster hunter which could use all kinds of cool tricks without real commitment to a bigger organization. While I'm not saying it is not a valid way to play Hunter, I don't think that this is the story the game tries to tell- a setting hack, maybe, but not the default case.

                                        And about stealing Endowments- Conspiracies are monopolies. The powers they perfected through the years are done because they invested time, resources and manpower at discovering and shaping them. Stealing Endowments from a Conspiracy or a rising Compact means stealing their secrets and technique, something which could be done especially when the conspiracy is young and weak. Let's look at the Promethean Brotherhood as an example- for now, they are working on translating the Rite of Hecate and perfecting it. If you steal the ritual and destroy their records, you would greatly harm their ability to use it. While some may have memorized parts of it or have their own copies, if the group is small enough and you are big enough, you could target all of the "big names" in the group and either kill them or simply destroy their libraries. You may even kidnap a few and force what they know about the ritual out of them. You don't even need to kill all the members of the compact- just the important ones. Those who are left beyond may try to reestablish themselves, yet without references to the original rite and with their leaders down, there is little they can do- after all, there is a good chance the ritual would kill them if they'll use it wrongly, and they eventually give up. You, on the other hand, has the secrets of a new Endowment in your hands, which you could use either use for your own advantage or destroy. Doing so to a full grown conspiracy would be a lot harder to do, but in the case of a full scale war it may be done.

                                        In sort, when you steal an Endowment, you steal a part of the Conspiracy's own foundations, and you do it by destroying it and make sure it would not rise again- especially if that someone tries to copy your precious resource you worked years to achieve.
