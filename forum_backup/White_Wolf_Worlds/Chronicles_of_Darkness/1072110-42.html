        I just want to say that, all in all, I personally like most of the groups in the doc. Sure, some of them may need to be expanded a little, and some of the Endowments may need a some kick (I've always felt the Cold Guard's were a bit crowded, even though I liked the rest of the groups writeup), but I haven't found some "deal breaker". For example, one of the things Hunter Conspiracies tends to do is "mass producing" a certain type of Supernatural Merits (which is their Endowment). I see nothing wrong with certain Hunter orgs working with monsters to achieve that goal and fight worse monsters- like how the Crown Jewels work with Nobles in order to fight the Darkness. Notice that I've said "with" and not "for"- because that may be an important factor for the group (not that there aren't groups who work "for" monsters. The Knights of St George work for the Faceless Angels, and I actually find it as cool).

        The only revision I could suggest for the group is to embrace the "mass producing" aspect of Endowment and instead of the hunters personally present their oath to a noble, they present it to some high ranked member of the conspiracy, which in turn was has pledged to another member, which has pledged to another member, etc, etc, until getting to one of a few actual Nobles which support the conspiracy. That serves a number of goals- first, it reinforce the theme of "serving nobility" which the conspiracy has, by creating a some sort of feudal system with the "king" being the Nobles on the top. Second, ti gives much more freedom for the conspiracy, as they pledge their loyalty to the organization itself instead to one of the Nobles. Third, it may allow you to reduce the number of Nobles needed to support the conspiracy, and making it more realistic. Finally, it may put the hunters at odds with Nobles which are not a part of the conspiracy, as the act may not be liked by certain Nobles which would find it as unnatural (and probably a corruption of the Darkness), a way for them to lose their power, or both. Hunters doing things which the monster they hunt can't understand is also a part of Hunter, after all, and the monsters really don't like it when it happens.

        Still, it is just a suggestion- so feel free to ignore it! Remember that not every hunter group is for everyone. It is true for canon, and especially true for homebrew. The most important thing is that you would understand what you group is, and make sure it fulfill its potential.
