    OK, here's the problem. You want to turn the Marduk Society into a hunter conspiracy that has no awful secrets and doesn't force moral compromises on its members, and give it access to hyperpowerful technology better than anything any other World of Darkness faction has (other than high-end supernatural beings.) That is, you're proposing to make Marduk a band of Big Damn Heroes, with the moral authority to persuade most NPCs in the setting to back them up and the power to defeat the few who would object. And you've had them around for thousands of years.

    With that combination of traits, Marduk breaks the nWoD setting. It's a bedrock assumption of the new World of Darkness that the real powers are all, in one way or another, alien to the human mindset. Marduk as you want it to be would be a real power that is not alien in any way.

    So I ask you again. Does Leviathan really need the Marduk Society to continue to the present day?

                                            Had some thoughts on the Wake, Leviathan brainwashing, etc.

                                            The Wake represents the Leviathan's psychic presence: part shadow, part manifestation of their Tempest. Much like a Vampire or Werewolf Predatory Aura, but in the Leviathan's case, they can't turn it off. When it intersects with another psychic presence (such as, say, people's minds), it has a number of effects: For those "lucky" enough to be Atolls (Touchstone-equivalents?), their minds ground out the Tempest and calm the Leviathan's Wake. Most other people whose minds and ideologies conflict with the Leviathan's get their minds worn down by the swirling Tempest, manifesting increasing severities of mental Conditions. They either modify their worldview to match the Leviathans (swimming with the current, and joining the Leviathan's cult), or learn to live with their misery. Some unfortunates, though, break under the strain and become hollowed out (Soul Loss?), only to be filled with the Leviathan's psychic presence - the Beloved. Of course, there is breaking, and then there is breaking - some minds get so warped by the trauma, they come to hate and revile the very mental presence that now fills them, drawing power from it to turn against its source (Ahab's having a Power-Stat mirroring the Leviathan they are obsessed with, like a Changeling's Fetch?).

                                                        Originally posted by Michael Brazier View Post
                                                        OK, here's the problem. You want to turn the Marduk Society into a hunter conspiracy that has no awful secrets and doesn't force moral compromises on its members, and give it access to hyperpowerful technology better than anything any other World of Darkness faction has (other than high-end supernatural beings.) That is, you're proposing to make Marduk a band of Big Damn Heroes, with the moral authority to persuade most NPCs in the setting to back them up and the power to defeat the few who would object. And you've had them around for thousands of years.

                                                        With that combination of traits, Marduk breaks the nWoD setting. It's a bedrock assumption of the new World of Darkness that the real powers are all, in one way or another, alien to the human mindset. Marduk as you want it to be would be a real power that is not alien in any way.

                                                        So I ask you again. Does Leviathan really need the Marduk Society to continue to the present day?
                                                        I think the real problem is the all-powerful role the above Raven write-up grants them, basically a scientific version of the Technocracy.

                                                        But the idea that a small cult of people with their limited resources can soar ahead of the whole world's scientific progress is just unfitting and unrealistic. How?

                                                        The new WoD has been a lot more sober on this subject, thankfully - secret societies aren't secret world governments that control everything, like a conspiracy nut's wet dream. They are mostly busy dealing with their own internal affairs and focusing on simpler, local goals; world events catch them as powerless as everybody else, and they have no choice but to follow the times and hunt for scraps along the way usually either.

                                                        I always enjoyed the MS as a concept, and I liked that they are kinda steampunk-y - if Ahabs are a tribute to "Moby Dick" then Marduk seemed like a salute to "Captain Nemo" and similar Verne-ish adventures. But it has to be kept at that level.

                                                        ---
                                                        I'm totally a nobody here, but let me picture a Marduk Society -I- wouldn't mind putting in a campaign -I- was running, chop it up and take from it what you want:


                                                        What the Marduk society has are certain mystical teachings about the supernatural nature of lightning and electricity.
                                                        - the secrets about Marduk's identity and his powers. (Whether Marduk used a device, godly powers, some kind of magic, some special trick, etc. to defeat Tiamat, that's what the MS got hold of.) Whenever society developed a new use for electricity, they could use this knowledge to improve those technologies. An improved way to bake pottery in 2000 BC, ultra-effective Tesla Coils around 1900. Their scope is narrow, and their researchers must still follow & lag behind the world's scientific progress.
                                                        This is especially true in the modern age where technological advancement has become so rapid that Marduk is decades behind -there certainly isn't a Marduk Cell Phone (though there are quite probably low-tech subtitutes, like ammonite-shaped personal radio transmitter the size of a hand to be placed on one ear that'd have seemed amazingly small and powerful to a person 50 years ago, not so much today.)
                                                        Other Marduk tech is more advanced, but the real draw of these toys is not that they are sci-fi, but that they are avaliable cheaper and more easily than modern top-notch tech, and their effectiveness in Leviathan-hunting.

                                                        The means by these crafting methods become possible is borderline supernatural. It is not simply a new scientific fact that allows them to be built; it's an occult understanding about the supernatural associations of lighting.
                                                        Sharing this method (while it would also be against the Society's goals) would also most likely be flat out impossible unless the world is "awakened" to the existence of the supernatural first; without that, these theories would simply look like strange occultism. A Marduk Society blueprint for a stun-gun would more closely resemble a page from Agrippa's "Occult Philosophy" than a page from modern engineering text.

                                                        This is also why a Marduk agent has to buy them with merits.
                                                        Only the initiated of the inner circle of MS have access to the rituals and creation and re-supply is only possible through these channels.

                                                        While these methods are supernatural though, they do not require a supernatural advantage like Vitae, Ichor, etc. (the advantage of a minor template). If that question ever comes up, rituals would consume Willpower (similar to other minor templates) instead.

                                                        History: (again, pulling this off the top of my head - pick whatever you like from it and discard the rest):

                                                        Marduk was a legendary hero. He may have been a metaphorical figure, a god, a demigod, a spirit-being, a Mage or a Genius (maybe the first or one of the first) or evensomething in-between or something even stranger than these. His battle with Tiamat may have been taken place before the the beginning of the world as we know it, or may have been quite recent and taken place in the real world.
                                                        The truth has been obscured by the (first) Marduk Society who stole and suppressed all accounts they could about him after his death.

                                                        What is known that Marduk faced Tiamat, the embodiment of the sea and the mother of all the monsters in it, and he could "draw forth lightning from the earth" to strike down his hated enemy. Victorious, he then reigned over the city of Babel until his death. Following his death, knowledge about his personal history and his power was seized by the cult that arose around him in his lifetime.
                                                        Yet Marduk was smart enough to foresee this, and real power, real scientific knowledge made its way back to the hands of the common people; what the cult was left with were a small number of select secrets.
                                                        Foremost among these was the secret that the flesh of Leviathans could grant a man immortality. The cult existed for centuries around this one goal, extending their own lifespan beyond its natural capacity. Their other secret they could stole, Marduk's power over lightning became their tool in this, used to seek out the descendants of Tiamat which they then hunted down and devoured.

                                                        Only some time after the 10th century would some leaders of the Society become interested in technological advancments, and sarted harnessing the lightning for other purposes, gaining a technological edge that would make the sciety the stuff of legend and gained them unimaginable power.

                                                        (They have NOT been, however, a contributor to technological development for the rest of the world. Their discoveries haven't been that numberous in comparison, and were kept strictily for themselves.)

                                                        In fact, the 1st industrial revolution left the society completely unaffacted.
                                                        It was during the 2nd revolution in the 19th century that the society would start to develop the devices they are now known for.

                                                        In the meantime however, however, another big change happened. Fate has finally caught up with the society. The Age of Discovery made some Leviathans realise that mankind's advance was unstoppable and they would eventually not be able to hide themselves even in places previously safe. Word of mouth and records about the men secretly hunting them has piled up over the centuries conspired and came down on them hard. A group of Leviathans later to be known as the 7-Sea Alliance have grouped together to end the threat once and for all.
                                                        The followers of Marduk may have had immortal lifespans, but they were not invulnerable, nor were they expecting the enemy to find them in their own homes. Previous Leviathan retaliations have always been solo efforts and settled at sea.
                                                        After a relatively short but intense campaign, the Society as it previously existed - the traditionalists, the priest-wizard, the robed ntediluvians - were wiped out. If any remained, they hid themselves completely from Leviathans and mortals alike.

                                                        Yet this was not the end of the Society, and ironically, this act contributed to the birth of a more radical and violent incarnation.

                                                        Some submarines left behind by the society were discovered by British sailors. These men were primarily motivated by profit and the spreading of British (and Western) imperialism. They cared little for traditions around Marduk's secrets and more about how these could be used for their present-day goals.

                                                        A man named Jacob Arronax, who became their new founder and leader, laid down the new tenets that would guide them (and still do):

                                                        1. Earth belonged to man (and particuarly, the Western man and Western civilization); the monsters that would step in their way would have to be destroyed.

                                                        2. Marduk's secrets and technology should be kept within the group and used to achieve real-world political and financial benefits.

                                                        3. immortality through Leviathan flesh became a secret kept from the common foot soldiers. Only the captains would parttake in immortality should the opportunity arise. (Even like this, the few leaders who achieved immortality would rarely live beyond 200 years - done in by Leviathans or, more often, by their own crew members.

                                                        Unlike the 1st society which viewed the Tribe merely as monsters, the new Society realized that they could be at least partially human if they wanted and more importantly, they could be keepers of important secrets. Maybe a few Leviathans did try to negotiate their way out of captivity by offering maps to treasures or occult secrets, but the Marduk Society now believes that all Leviathans have such know a secret. They are more likely to capture and interrigate Leviathans than outright kill them.

                                                        Let's not mince the world, the new Marduks were little more than a band of pirates. Only at the end of the 20th century did a more intellectual element begin to arise within a society. By and large, the group still follows Arronax's ground rules, but a select few now have started to rediscover their roots (which the annihilation of the 1st society left them largely ignorant of), and re-examine their place in the modern world.
