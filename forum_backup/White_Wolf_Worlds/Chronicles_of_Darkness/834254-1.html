                            Werewolves aren't limited by genetics. Seeing a werewolf can potentially turn you into a werewolf.

                            While I've always been a proponent of 'Do what fits for your story,' Changeling demographics are notoriously disconnected from human demographics. It says this in a few places throughout the books, where large cities like New York have few changelings compared to small towns with dozens. This is likely due to the True Fae, who are not obliged to follow society's rules and flock to large population centers. One of the Gentry can take one life from a village every generation, while entire cities can never be touched by them. It's further reinforced by the fact that not every changeling who is taken makes it back, and not every changeling who makes it back moves back into their own life.
