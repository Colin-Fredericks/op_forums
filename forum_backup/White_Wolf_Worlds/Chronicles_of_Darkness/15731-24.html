    Session CLXIII

    Sara’s Stories

    Sara came down to the upbeat sounds of Natalie singing Kiss the Boys Goodbye. She joined her wives in the music room to listen, sitting on the floor and leaning back against the piano bench. When Natalie finished, Karen teased her about the song’s relevance to their upcoming wedding. Natalie looked to Sara for help and she hopped to her feet, suggesting Alexia’s banana pancakes as a distraction.

    After breakfast, Natalie returned to the music room to work on the cover songs she was preparing for her album: Black Parade for her daughter, Stepping Out for her mother, Paranoid for Karen and Lovesong for Sara. She stayed there until lunch.

    As they finished up lunch, Natalie’s phone buzzed. Before she even got to check it, it buzzed again and again. The first message was from Deanna.

    ‘Clarissa is grounded because of me and it’s not my fault.’

    Natalie scanned the rest and left the room to give Deanna a call. She came back with a bemused expression and gathered the krewe to explain, trying to put it in order despite having heard it middle to first to end. Since Deanna had been really stressed, she had confided in her parents some. She didn’t out any of the supernatural to them, but she did come out as pan herself.

    They were, to put it mildly, pleased as punch. Her dad was less pleased at her dalliances with Natalie, since he’d apparently seen her swear allegiance to Satan on TV. Natalie shook her head in confusion over that. Her mom was OK with Natalie’s paganism since ‘it was a hippie thing’. The strawberry scented woman grimaced.


    “Thankfully she doesn’t care what her parents think. She wants to - ahem - ‘nail me on the sly’. That household is fraught with optimism. Which leads to the actually bad part...”

    When Deanna mentioned that she and Clarissa weren’t involved, Deanna’s parents leapt to the conclusion that Clarissa was also queer. They called Clarissa’s mother, despite Deanna’s protestations, who grounded Clarissa. Not for being queer, but because she hadn’t told her parents that she was, in order for them to vet her suitors. So Clarissa was upset at Deanna and Deanna was heartbroken.

    Natalie listened worriedly while Sara and Alexia talked over what might calm Clarissa’s mother down. Karen smirked.

    “We could come up with a ‘she’s not gay’ musical number.”

    “It might work, since she’s a girl. I’m not sure anyone would be convinced their son wasn’t gay with a musical number.”

    “Be realistic now.”

    Karen grinned at Sara. Natalie shook her head and put a hand on Sara’s shoulder, suggesting they wait until she went to go talk to Deanna and see if she couldn’t get more of the story.

    -------------------------------------------------------------------

    Alexia gave Grin a call, to check on the krewe’s theory that Katie was Li-Hyun’s fetch. He wasn’t aware of any newly arrived Changelings, though he did say there was a group of Changelings, called the Blackbirds, that might know more. He pointed out that it occasionally took some time for a new arrival to trust others like them, but he promised to do his best.

    He also mentioned, when Alexia described what was going on, that a monster had been sighted near the radio station. When she pressed for details, he shrugged.

    “That’s as specific a description as I got: ‘monster’.”

    Alexia went looking for Sara after they said their goodbyes. Sara looked thoughtful and suggested calling the twins, since of the Underwater Story Weavers Club, they were the most likely to be able to object read or glance into the past. Alexia elected to let Sara make the call.

    Tracy answered with a quavery voice. Sara asked her what was wrong.

    “I think I’m starting to agree with my brother’s assessment... I’ve seen Li-Hyun.”

    Sara blinked. Tracy spoke over the pause, saying she wanted to meet, with everyone. Sara agreed, and set to gathering people.

    On the way there, Natalie let everyone know how her visit had gone. Deanna was out of her funk, though it hadn’t been helped by her mom’s mystified reaction to Deanna’s grandmother’s unhappiness at being invited to a coming out party. As far as Clarissa went, they assured Natalie it was just her mother’s temper getting the best of her. Deanna figured it would get sorted out when Clarissa’s father got home.

    When they got to the usual meeting place of the Underwater Story Weaving Club, Tracy was tucked into a chair, her knees under her chin and her arms wrapped around her legs. Timothy was hovering protectively nearby. Sara rushed over and put an arm around her and Tracy leaned into the tattooed woman.

    Once everyone got there, Tracy started talking. Something had changed when she went to seduce Rebecca. The other girl resisted her advances, but since Tracy knew how to push her buttons, Katie’s roommate wasn’t in much of a state to stop her from poking around the missing girl’s computer. KimSori, the name of Katie’s favorite singer, got Tracy in.

    Katie's chats with another student, a Maria Chow, were what had Tracy agreeing with her brother. Li-Hyun had approached Katie and asked her to play certain songs in a certain order on her show. She said no that time and the second time she was asked as well. The third time came with threats.

    The last message Katie had sent was asking Maria to come keep her company. It was Friday night and her roommate had left. When Katie had gone to the bathroom, there had been the usual busy foot traffic. But when she went in, there was only one person there. She had her back to Katie, but looked rather a lot like Li-Hyun. Katie told Maria she had a strong feeling she was in danger, so she didn’t want to go anywhere alone.

    When Tracy left the dorm, she felt a chill herself. As she shivered, her eyes were drawn to a Korean girl standing in a nearby parking lot. The girl was staring at the directly and after a few moment, stepped back into the shadows. Tracy had rushed home, holing up in her room with her gun close at hand until Timothy got home.

    Sara looked worriedly at Alexia and held Tracy close. Once they’d had a moment to process, Sara suggested they leave that line of questioning alone for awhile and look into the monster that had been reported near the radio station. A couple of the students had heard about it as well, and they all agreed.

    When they got to the station, Grin was there. Sara, an arm still around Tracy’s waist, asked him to speak privately. Once they stepped aside, Sara encouraged Tracy to describe her encounter. The Sin-Eater followed it up by asking Grin if anyone in his Court could do something like that.

    “We can make an area more conducive to fear. Would you say she was your greatest fear, in that moment?”

    “Greatest? Not... not really.”

    “Probably not us, then.”

    Grin looked apologetically at Sara, As they walked back to the others, he told her he kenned the area and found nothing.

    Sara asked the twins if they could do anything that might look into the past. Timothy shook his head.

    “My stuff is mostly about minds and a little about dimensions.”

    “And mine is mostly about dimensions and a little about minds.”

    Tracy smiled wanly up at Sara who squeezed her waist. The tattooed woman kissed the spooked woman on the forehead before letting her go to begin her Ceremony. Other than a plethora of late night, behind the bushes liaisons, Sara got two flashes: one of a large, fuzzy winged creature with large yellow eyes and another of Katie walking towards the building at night. After a brief glance at where the creature had been, Sara focused on the image of Katie.

    She was rounding the corner, just passing the bushes when another Korean girl stepped out from behind them. Katie was turning towards her when a long knife blade sprouted from one of the other woman’s fingers and slashed across Katie’s throat in one smooth movement. The woman who Sara presumed was Li-Hyun looked on dispassionately as Katie fell, then walked past her towards the door.

    Sara shuddered and winced away. Natalie was there in a heartbeat with an arm around her shoulder. Sara quietly told her what happened and the strawberry scented woman held her closer. Pulling herself together, Sara took a deep breath and looked to the others. Alexia was looking oddly at a patch of dirt behind the bushes, near where Sara had seen the winged monster stalking around. The tattooed girl bit her lip when she realized it was a shallow grave.

    She swallowed and leaned into Natalie, describing the monster she’d seen. Doc’s brow furrowed. He recognized it as a Mothman, a la Point Pleasant. No one else really knew what to make of it, so Alexia unlocked Industrial Oracle while Deanna called the cops.

    Alexia was able to get images of the Mothman, as well as figure out that it was the first time the Mothman had been there. Asking about Li-Hyun, Alexia got two portraits - the detached emotionless killer, whose hand with a bladed finger turned into a wide shovel to dig the shallow grave and the bubbly radio host, who passed by skipping and whistling.

    When Alexia was done, Sara pulled her aside, watching the grieving students.

    “I know this mystery is important to them but... they should leave it now. It’s too dangerous.”

    “I’ll try to impart that the mystery is solved.”

    Alexia looked sadly at Marie, who was kneeling by the grave, muttering. When they rejoined the others, Alexia spoke seriously, congratulating them on solving the mystery. When she intimated that it was time for the students to back off, Deanna got really upset. Natalie placed a hand on her shoulder to comfort her, but Deanna shrugged her off.

    “We’re supposed to help! I want to help!”

    Tensions rose, with Tracy even volunteering to be bait. Doc stepped forward.

    “Hit me.”

    Deanna stopped and frowned at Doc.

    “Hit me like I was going to stomp you to the ground if I didn’t.”

    She pulled out a pair of brass knuckles and put them on. She swing for Doc, who leaned to the side. He grabbed her arm as it passed and pulled her after it. She tumbled to the ground.

    Natalie helped her up and moved to comfort her again, pointing out she’d had a long day and should take her time. Deanna didn’t push her away this time. Sara’s eyes brightened and she pointed at the pair of them.

    “How do you feel about hosting a radio show?”

    “No one is acting as bait...”

    Sara stared at Doc affrontedly.

    “Of course not! I’m not suggesting Deanna act as bait, I’m suggesting she act as our woman on the inside.”

    Deanna perked up at that and Natalie gave Sara a brilliant smile from over the enthusiastic girl’s shoulder. Sara explained Deanna would just need to make friends with the other DJs to try and get a heads up if anyone made specific requests like Li-Hyun did. And most importantly, to make any requested changes herself.

    The other students perked up a little as well at the idea that there was something to do. Marie looked thoughtful.

    “I thought about having a radio show. But since it’s a school station, and a Catholic school, everything hes to be something that would be approved of by the Catholic Church - you can’t say anything about going down on your girlfriend and stuff.”

    “I think that’s most radio stations...”

    Timothy raised an eyebrow at Marie. Sara chuckled, throwing in her two cents.

    “Also, I’m pretty sure you can’t talk about going down on your boyfriend either.”

    “The bastards!”

    Sara and Timothy shared a grin, giving Doc a little space to apologize to Deanna and compliment her on her weapon of choice. She accepted brightly, leaving him blinking when she pointed out she wasn’t allowed to have a taser since the accident.

    Marie kept looking down at the shallow grave, her shoulders drooped. Sarah stood close, trying to cheer her up. The bean sidhe gently patted her friend on the back as she mourned the fact she hadn’t been able to save Katie. Sarah pointed out that there wasn’t anything Marie could have done differently. A sad look on her face, Alexia watched sympathetically.

    “There will be some you can save - don’t get discouraged.”

    Marie nodded her acknowledgment.

    “That’s giving up.”

    “And we don’t do that!”

    Deanna nodded as well, resolutely.

    Grin came back over from where he’d been talking with the police, to let them know everything was all squared away. He also looked significantly at the krewe when he let them know he’d received a message he’d be checking once he’d finished his report to the police. Alexia smiled her thanks to him and turned to the club.

    “I’ve found - and you might laugh at me - that food helps most anything.”

    -------------------------------------------------------------------

    The krewe and club met up at an Irish restaurant, where Alexia recommended the stew for everyone. Conversation was subdued at first until Marie sighed.

    “Look, Katie wouldn’t want us brooding. And if she would, she’s a bitch.”

    Sara hid a smile, but that did strike up more liveliness in the group. Doc got an agreement from everyone to give him a few drops of blood. As a hedge witch, Marie balked a little, making sure to emphasize to the others that she was only doing so because it was Doc asking, and they only should if they explicitly trusted the asker.

    Timothy spoke across Tracy to Sara, worried about his sister’s safety. He said he’d be escorting her to class, all but the one that conflicted with his. Marie volunteered to help with that one. A worried smile on her face, Sara ran a hand down Tracy’s arm to rest it on her hand, asking if she’d be OK with staying at the Bluffs. The spooked woman nodded as she glanced out the window, looking for threats.

    “Alexia - what’s the name of your bakery again?”

    Deanna looked frustrated as she asked, looking down at her phone. Alexia told her and she sighed as she began typing. When she glanced up and caught everyone’s curious looks, she explained that her mom was planning a coming out party and wanted a cake. When her mom sent her a picture of a lesbian wedding cake, she groaned and put her head on the table.

    “I would be happy to make a cake that you would like. Would you rather me deal with you or with your mom?”

    “I would rather not be outed to my entire extended family... my mom can be a bit effervescent at times.”

    Natalie rubbed Deanna’s back and tried to cheer her up through the rest of dinner. When they left, the krewe made a detour to let Tracy get a few things from her house. At the Bluffs, Tavia greeted them. Sara let her know Tracy would be staying for a bit and Natalie reminded her that Coffee was coming the next day.

    “Do I need to set up a guest room for Coffee or will she be staying with you?”

    “A guest room, I think, just in case?”

    “Oh please.”

    Tavia rolled her eyes playfully at Sara, then sent to set up the rooms. Sara went with her to help while Alexia took Tracy on a tour of the house, focusing on where to find the emergency ice cream and cookies, since she’d already got warnings about the dangers. Tracy gave the Sin-Eater a weak smile.

    “You guys are my favorite people.”

    -------------------------------------------------------------------

    Deanna sighed as she pushed open the door to her house. She slung her backpack into the corner, by the shoe rack, then rolled her eyes when her mom reacted to the noise. She headed back through the house to her room, stopping when her mom poked her head out of the kitchen.

    “Honey? Do you want pink, blue or purple streamers? Oooh, or all three?”

    “I found a shallow grave with a college student in it today.”

    Deanna stomped off upstairs, leaving her mom holding the three sample streamers she’d gotten from Party City. The woman watched her go, a shocked look on her face.

    “I don’t know how to throw a party for that.”

    -------------------------------------------------------------------

    Grin called a little after the krewe got settled in.

    “So, the Wytch King - that’s what we call the Autumn Monarch. It’s with a y, cause he’s that goth.”

    “Just the Wytch, not the Kyng, right?”

    “He’s not that goth.”

    Sara smiled as Grin continued, explaining the plan: the Wytch King was going to gather a Wild Hunt to hold the Hedge near the lodge. The ‘fuzzies’ were going to handle each other, with the Wild Hunt preventing the Hedge-corrupted ones from retreating that way, or possibly attacking them from behind their lines if it started going poorly for the Ivory Claws.

    Once he established that the krewe had the rescue in hand and they set the attack for midnight, he hung up to contact the others. Sara went to find Tracy and explain why they’d be gone, promising she’d be safe at the house. Karen plopped down on the arm of the chair Tracy was sitting in and pointed out she’d be staying behind as well. When Natalie smirked, Karen protested.

    “Hey, I’ve seen horror movies. I know that when I have sex with Tracy, that’s when the monster comes out. Besides, I’ll be thinking about you two in Glenwood and do a half-assed job.”

    “If you’re thinking about Glenwood, she’s doing a half-assed job.”

    -------------------------------------------------------------------

    The krewe spent the next few hours preparing. Doc booked a hotel room for Rawl in the neutral zone. Natalie led a ritual to bring them together, resting most of the power generated in Doc and Sara since they were the two going in. She reserved some in case of emergency.

    As they were getting ready to go, they were interrupted by Tracy’s raised voice.

    “She’s only three years younger than me?”

    They found Tracy and Karen in the TV room. Natalie glared at her wife, shifting uncomfortably as they continued talking about Natalie Lousler.

    “No one is allowed to fuck my daughter who fucked me first.”

    “One: I didn’t agree to that before. And two: you fucked me.”

    “Fine. But it’s a rule from now on.”

    Karen smirked.

    “Is that because she’s your kid, or because she’s a potential romantic rival?”

    With that, Doc, who’d been about to step in, threw up his hands and told them he’d be at the van. Natalie spluttered a bit before turning on her heel and following.

    Tracy looked significantly at Sara’s phone. The tattooed girl brought up a picture, grinning at the appreciative look on Tracy’s face. She didn’t linger long though, giving the pair staying behind a wink as she left to join the others at the van.

    -------------------------------------------------------------------

    As the krewe waited in the parking lot a few miles from the lodge, they heard the engines of a large group of motorcycles. Sara was already projecting and Natalie had her Boneyard up, so Doc stepped into Twilight. He and Sara set off towards the lodge while Alexia and Noriko guarded Natalie.

    Sounds of fighting weren’t far off when they got to Rawl’s room. Doc didn’t leave much time for conversation, simply popping into the material world, setting the dinosaur figure complete with robe on the bedside table, grabbing Rawl and shifting the two of them back into Twilight.

    Rawl took a deep breath, shifting back out of his fox-like wolf form as Doc pulled him back to reality at the van. Sara patted the seat next to her as Doc crawled to the front and started the van. He tossed back a hotel key card before he took off for New Haven.

    The werewolf took the seat Sara suggested and looked at the keycard with a confused look on his face for a moment, then sighed, acknowledging that he hadn’t really thought about where he’d be staying. He smiled wryly at the krewe.

    “So, did you guys have a good Christmas? I didn’t have a good Christmas.”

    Sara smiled sympathetically back and began giving him a backrub. Rawl sighed, more happily this time.

    “This is kinda like Christmas though.”

    -------------------------------------------------------------------

    Karen met them at the door after they’d dropped Rawl off at the hotel. She stepped forward quickly, reassuring herself that both her wives were in one piece. Once she had, she grinned at them, letting them know Tracy had given consent to being taken care of before she began drinking.

    They found Tracy expounding about roofs to Catherine, who was bright red. She stopped when the triad came in, smiling tipsily at them. Natalie smiled back at her.

    “Are you telling stories about the widow walk?”

    “I’ve not done anything there. Yet. But it is on my bucket list.”

    She carefully counted the triad.

    “So’s that.”

                                                Session LCXVI

                                                Sara’s Stories

                                                The krewe picked up Karen and Alexia for the trip to Long Wharf. The group went in singly and in pairs and only Sara was unlucky enough to catch the attention of the sparse few patrolling security guards. Natalie paused, watching her wife play the flirty, flighty teenager to a stoic, uninterested man. She was about to come back her up when Sara flounced off, giving a broad wink to the darkness.

                                                A few minutes later, Sara’s astrally projected form mimed sneaking up on Natalie, while the strawberry scented woman pretended not to notice until Sara kissed her on the back of the neck.

                                                “Hi ghost Sara! Why are you ghost Sara?”

                                                Sara chuckled and explained to Coffee what had happened, in between running ghostly fingers through Karen’s hair. The krewe made their way to the merry go round first, waving to the ghostly operator who smiled and waved back once he saw Doc and Natalie.

                                                As they rode the rides and walked the park, they talked. Coffee led the conversation, asking question after question. How did you meet Chandra? Is Marcie one of your girlfriends too? What do you do when investigating? How would you deal with the hotel in the Shining?

                                                Alexia handled the first question and Natalie the second, and the answer to the third and fourth was unanimously ‘research’. These questions and answers led to more questions, like What’s the Abyss?, a question Sara answered by relating the story of the krewe’s mythos and an explanation of it from the mage’s side as well, as best she knew it. Discussion of their case at the Hidden Hillside Hotel brought up Mr. Ox, who Coffee was of course curious about. They managed to satisfy that curiosity as well.

                                                When they got home, Natalie grabbed Coffee by the hand and dragged her off. Sara chuckled and went off with Doc and Alexia to work on a sketch of the arsonist, as that is what the group had decided to pursue, to give Coffee a first hand idea of how they worked. After two tries, they had a decent sketch and Sara managed to talk Doc into leaving the library research for the next day, so that Coffee could participate if she wanted to.

                                                He agreed with a long suffering sigh and Sara headed gleefully off to the hot tub. Before she got there she found a trail of Natalie and Coffee’s clothes, leading to Coffee’s room. She showed up at the door of the trio’s room holding Coffee’s jacket with a pleading look.

                                                “Not too busy for the hot tub?”

                                                Karen hopped up and smiled at her, taking the jacket and setting it aside.

                                                “Never.”

                                                -------------------------------------

                                                Doc and Noriko hit the streets after Sara left, going on one of their usual patrols but heading straight for the area around the burnt out abandoned building. They slowed down as they neared it, hearing voices from inside.

                                                They moved carefully and quietly up, sticking to the shadows until they caught sight of the immortal Maria pacing and speaking to two other college girls, Queen and Jamie, exhorting them to try and sense something there. Jamie looked frustrated, but Queen spun her a line that even Doc saw through. He stood up and walked forward.

                                                “I don’t know what you’re trying to sense, but those people are long gone.”

                                                The girls were startled but Maria reassured them by pointing out he was involved with the Underwater Story Weaving Club. When he brought out the sketch, Maria deflected but eventually admitted to having been hit on by him at Bar. She assumed he was a townie.

                                                After a moment Doc asked to speak with the immortal alone and after a moment of coyness, she agreed, a flirtatious smile on her face. Once the two students were sent outside, she continued with her flirtation until Noriko emerged from the shadows to warn her off.

                                                Her ardor cooled for the moment, she continued to maintain that Barry always managed to manipulate their dance in ways that never appeared to be of their design. She was not reassured by the Sin-Eater’s insistence that the club was not being used as a weapon against her, pointing out that even if the club dissolved tomorrow, Barry had a collection of powerful students who would look up to him as a mentor. She smiled.

                                                “And if I sound mad, I assure you Barry and I went insane a long time ago.”

                                                Doc made one last stab at forestalling a student-centered cold war, and at calling Noriko to heel, before taking their leave. Crowley was waiting at the van to tell Doc his tail light was out. He also let him know that an old factory on the north end, one that had had a few of his friends squatting in it, had burned down just last January. He thought there might be a connection Doc should know about.

                                                Doc expressed condolences about his friends and offered his hand.

                                                “You should come by the house sometime.”

                                                “That’s a little out of my territory. Besides, the house and I have a gentleman’s agreement.”

                                                -------------------------------------

                                                At breakfast the next day, Doc filled the others in. Noriko’s anger came back as Doc related Maria’s flirtatiousness and she pointed out what made her the most angry was when the immortal kept flirting with Doc in front of her. Natalie grinned at her.

                                                “She didn’t try to pick both of you up? That’s what I would have done.”

                                                “Well she wouldn’t have succeeded?”

                                                “Would I?”

                                                Natalie’s grin turned wicked as Noriko turned many shades of red. Doc continued on with his tale of the previous night, prompting an explanation to Coffee when Natalie called Crowley Mr. Deus Ex Machina. She also was somewhat taken aback by a bar called Bar. Noriko smirked.

                                                “It’s mostly college students, they can’t make it too hard.”

                                                Karen protested, but soon talk turned to plans for the day. Natalie promised to contact Rita and see if they could introduce Coffee to the Changelings. But until then, there was research.

                                                The group decided to go by the Starbucks the arsonist had frequented last autumn first. It was in walking distance of the first arson site and the running theory was that he had frequented it while he was casing the place. That was held up by Natalie’s conversation with the baristas there. She did get a name - James.

                                                Once they got back home, Natalie and Doc went up to the library to begin researching what kinds of supernatural beings could cause the kind of fire that Sara and Alexia had described. The list was short: mages and Changelings were a possibility, but unlikely. More likely was a pyrokinetic or a human Claimed by a fire spirit.

                                                Sara called the police while they research. Posing as a charity worker, she asked where she might find large groups of homeless folks, so that she didn’t need to wander around the bad parts of town. The officer on duty gave her a few addresses, but also warned that the homeless population had scattered since the factory fire, so there were less folks around in general and the usual haunts might be abandoned.

                                                Coffee caught up with Sara as she got off the phone, a question in her eyes. Once they stepped into a side room, she asked it.

                                                “Does everyone who sleeps with Natalie fall for her?”

                                                Sara smiled and laid a hand on her shoulder. Coffee took a deep breath.

                                                “OK. Can you explain your relationship again like I’m two - no then you’ll leave out the sex.”

                                                Sara laughed and did her best, pointing out that it wasn’t all that different from most relationships, except that none of them believed sexual exclusivity was required to prove their love and that love was not diminished by being shared. Coffee sighed, assuring Sara that she didn’t want to cause problems. Sara kissed her on the cheek.

                                                “Just follow your heart. The only way you could cause problems would be to insist she leave us.”

                                                “Following my heart is complicated.”

                                                Sara nodded sympathetically and watched her go to her room to think. When Doc and Natalie came down from the library, she waylaid Natalie on her way to see Coffee, letting her know what they had talked about and that the bubbly Sin-Eater needed some space.

                                                Instead, Sara took Natalie off to the computer, to see if they could find anymore fires that may have been set by the arsonist. They found five. Two were the ones they’d known he set, the abandoned building and factory in the poorer parts of town. One was the bloodbather’s house, so they were able to rule that out.

                                                That left two of the fires were unaccounted for. The first was a scientific facility. Something about it bugged Sara, until she realized the address it gave was the one Eris had gone to when she was trying to find her past. The timing was right to match up with her escape as well.

                                                The second was about six months later and didn’t seem to fit the pattern. It was a house in the nicer part of town, and it was a vacation home, with no one in residence at the time. On one hand, perhaps he had been working up to the kill. On the other, perhaps it was unrelated. However, it was the easier one to look into, so they determined they would, soon.

                                                        “It’s not my fault, it was vespene gas or something.”

Eventually Natalie brought up that Rita had gotten back with her and was free any day after 6. Considering the hour, and that they had two sites to investigate that day, Sara suggested the next day for a dinner meeting.

                                                        “She’s still dating Jasmine’s student. Hasn’t eaten him. Yet.”

                                                        “Excuse me?”

Sara chuckled and took the opportunity to explain a bit more of what they knew of Changelings, how their Keepers changed them in general and Rita in specific. They were still discussing it as they prepared to leave. Karen and Chandra looked to the krewe while Noriko just moved with them.

                                                        Alexia nodded and shrugged at Chandra’s questioning look. Natalie frowned.

                                                        “What’s the homework situation like?”

                                                        “No one’s spouse should ever say that.”

                                                        “If the spouse has homework...”

                                                        In the van, Sara and Natalie looked through the papers they’d retrieved last time they’d gone to the burnt out research facility. They found little other on their firebug than a last name. James Rollin definitely fit their description and was apparently related to a government official, but that was all they could find - everything else was obfuscated with warnings of ‘Classified’.

                                                        They did manage to come across some references to a werewolf that had been held there. Patricia Byerly was the second werewolf they’d captured, the first having escaped some time past. Sara looked closely and managed to find a date, over two years past, that implied Eris was definitely Patricia. She’d had her first change during a domestic abuse incident and her husband wound up very dead. The notes suggested a diagnosis of trauma-induced amnesia, compounded by ‘current living conditions’.

                                                        Sara finished up texting the details to Alice and Deirdre just as the van drove up to the fenced off area of the industrial park north of town that the abandoned facility was in. Alexia hopped out to pick the lock, once they’d given the place a once over for cameras.

                                                        Driving through the opened gate, they found themselves in the big open concrete area surrounding the charred remnants of the facility. Natalie stayed in the van, Boneyarding, with Karen and Chandra to guard her. The others waited for her report - no ghosts, the second floor wall had chunks missing - before heading carefully inside.

                                                        Alexia and Doc took point and found the epicenter of the fire quickly, leading the others to an upstairs room. Sara got to work, pacing the area and opening herself to visions.

                                                        She retched after a moment, and took a moment to gather herself, before she stepped back into the torrents of flashes of torture scenes, medical procedures and screaming. She was distracted by a glimpse of an explosion, but turned aside from that vision when she realized it was contained in a room and the only one killed was a beyond morbidly obese vampire who was in the process of absorbing someone in a uniform.

                                                        She was torn when she caught a glimpse of Eris, but still, purpose in mind, she focused on the vision she had of James. The man was waking up in a hospital bed, an IV in his arm. A young woman walked in, someone Sara had seen before at Murder Ballads. She spoke calmly, letting him know that his dose of had been tampered with and encouraging him to leave quickly. A guard interrupted her. She looked at him calmly and the guard shot himself in the face.

                                                        James stood carefully, building up a fireball between his hands for a moment before thrusting it forwards, the woman egging him on to build it higher and higher. Soon it engulfed the entire corridor. The pair walked forward, the inferno parting before them. At one point a trio of guards tried to stop them, guns out. At a dispassionate look from the woman, one turned to shoot the other two, distracting them until the fire reached them.

                                                        Sara told the others, then went back into a trance. She wanted to know more about Eris. The flashes weren’t as vivid this time, but she knew what she was looking for anyway. The beginning was similar to James’ story, with her waking up slowly and pulling an IV out. The woman wasn’t there to explain why she wasn’t properly sedated, but she was there to coolly watch the mayhem Eris wrought on her way out.

                                                        Sara shook out of her trance to hear Alexia explaining the answers she’d gotten from Industrial Oracle. The woman she’d seen was Miranda Davis. That was a name Sara recognized from the paperwork, a psychic. Alexia had also asked about what had been done to James and after hearing it, figured the same had been done to Miranda. It was a delicate brain surgery, a procedure that burnt out the portions of the brain that were ‘holding them back’. Everyone frowned as the implication sank in.

                                                        “Did they... to Eris...?”

                                                        Sara looked to Alexia. The other woman paused and shook her head. They hadn’t been modifying Eris, just using her as a source of spiritually charged, self regenerating cells. Sara nodded and took a deep breath. Then she frowned again.

                                                        “Eris came right to the papers when we explored here with her. But there’s no way she happened to see them on her way out. She was raging. I wonder if she got some sort of tip.”

                                                        “Or had an implanted suggestion.”

                                                        Doc scowled. Coffee looked between the group quickly.

                                                        “I’m glad there’s no weird supernatural experiments here. If I was a weird supernatural experiment I would totally guard treasure in a place like this but then I’d run out of coffee and that would be bad.”

                                                        That lightened the mood somewhat as they went back to the van and away. Discussion meandered from whether zombie children were more frightening with or without skin to how terrifying Dick Clark was. Doc’s eyes rolled circles around his head as he drove.

                                                        When they got to the final arson site, they found it already rebuilt, presumably courtesy of a large bank account, with a brand spanking new security system installed. Natalie saw the blinking red light through her Boneyard, and Doc noted the wiring. Alexia stretched and activated Industrial Marionette to turn that off quickly, then unlocked the door more mundanely.

                                                        Once in, Sara walked the house, looking into its past. The strongest glimpse she got was an old one, decades old, the flash of light on scissors being used to stab someone to death. The fire was a fainter glimpse, but Sara focused on it to see two men breaking in to steal a painting, then burning the place down to cover their tracks. Nothing supernatural about it. She gave the others a wry smile.

The latest was from a few months before, talking about her vacation in St. Lucia.
