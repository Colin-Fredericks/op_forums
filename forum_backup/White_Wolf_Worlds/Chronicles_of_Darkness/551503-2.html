                        Some cool places in Toronto are ripply's believe it or not aquarium beside the CN Tower between the CN Tower and the Sky Dome. I'd personally add the Skydome and Ripply's to the hedge. Actually because they're so close together, it makes the Skydome look kind of like a set of testicles beside the very phallic CN Tower. Cool thing to note about CN is that while it has a resturant and entertainment facialities, its also transmits signals like over the air TV stations, bet you'd get some interesting channels in the hedge.

                        Younge Street is the longest street in the world (I don't care what the haters say), on it you can find just about anything.

                        On young street you can find a stripclub called the Brass Rail, it was the stripclub used in American Pie: Beta House. It has a rep for being a tourist trap.

                        Toronto used be filled with stripclubs, but there aren't that many anymore, they've given way to Massage Parlours and Escorts, because who doesn't like a happy ending (fairy tale or otherwise). Some of the remaining stripclubs like Fairbanks, and the Trinity Clubs in Missauga (Cafe Alantis, Midway Invader, and one other I think) are extras heavy and may lean towards being brothels at times.

                        Toronto's Entertain district is being ruined by idiots you think its a good place to build condos, and complaints from said idiots have driven some club owners out of business. The last Superclub in TO closed its doors rescently.

                        Toronto sits on one of the Great Lakes (they're practically fresh water seas. Toronto even extends onto islands in Lake Ontario.

                        Former Mayor Ford, best known for his crack piper and buckets of chicken and weird obsession with Toronto's Gravy, really put Toronto on the Map and would make a really good Changeling NPC character in any chronicle.

                        Areas in the wider GTA are York Region, where I live, made up the towns/cities of Richmond Hill, Newmarket, Aurora, Markham, Whitechurch-Stouffville, King, Vaughan, Geogina, East Gwillenbury.

                        My favour parts of this area are Newmarket with Upper Canada Mall, Concord area of Thornhill (which I think is part of Richmond Hill), because it has the Whiskey A-Go-Go stripclub and right beside it Vaughan Spa (adult entertainment) or as I like to call it my happy place. Blondie's Spa is also nearby is by. I also like Wilcox Lake in Oakridges (part of Richmond Hill). Newmarket also has Fairy Lake, how perfect for Changeling, which has trails all over the place with pretty street lights.

                        Scarbough another part of Toronto proper is the inspiration for the movie Wayne's World. I do some business from time to time in Scarbrough.

                        Missauga was governed for ever and a day by Mayor Hazel McCallun aka Hurricane Hazel because she was politically invincible, she'd been Mayor for longer then Missauga has existed in its current form, he'd just retire in the last municiple elections, much beloved, another good potential changeling NPC.

                        Major Parades include the Santa Claus Parade, which gets televised all over the world, both on the internet and to television stations across the globe.

                        Toronto has the largest Carabana Parade outside Trinidad and Tobago I believe, its a huge celebrantion of diversity. Colours, feathers, and all kinds of stuff, its perfect for Changeling.

                        Gay Pride Parade is huge in Toronto, gets tourists from around the world.

                        Slut Walk I think Toronto might be were it originated, not sure, cheaper then a stripclub, but be advised not to bring a sign suggesting you don't believe in rape culture, reporter and libertarian candiate Lauren Southern almost got lynched at the slut walk for doing that.

                        Taste of the Danforth, big foodie thing on the Danforth.

                        NDP leader Tom Mulcair has said Toronto is the most important city in Canada, which is ballsy because Tom Mulcair represents a riding in Montreal, and Toronto and Montreal have rivalry, although Toronto does have a large Habs fans community, mostly Montrealers who got fed up with the xenophobia of the seperatist movement in Quebec so moved to Toronto.

                        Toronto is the capital of Ontario, a Pronvince bigger then most European Countries. We call the legislature Queen's Park.

                        Toronto is also the setting of Lost Girl TV show and Murdoch Mysteries.

                        Bay street is the capital of Canada's finance industry, its like Canada's Wall Street, it would definately be Ivory Court terroritory. The Toronto Stock Exchange, aka the TSX is Canada's largest.

                        GTA, Greater Toronto Area, has a larger population then most provonces within Canada. I think only Ontario, Quebec, and BC have a larger population.

                        Lots of other fun stuff in Toronto. I'll add anything I can think of later.
