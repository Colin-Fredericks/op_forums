                                                Originally posted by nofather View Post
                                                If they do, it's never been mentioned. There's nothing explicitly saying they don't, but the one thing we can be sure of is werewolf ghosts, if they existed, wouldn't go to the Underworld.

                                                While they don't leave spirits behind, they traditionally have ancestor spirits. These are not really a spirit of an individual werewolf so much as the memories of a werewolfs lineage or family and legend.

                                                We've discussed it here.

                                                As for devouring them for Essence, sure. It would justify a Breaking Point towards spirit, it's not a very fleshly thing to do.

                                                I don't believe you'd be able to learn Gifts from a Ghost, it's an inherently spirit-based phenomena, doing so would require a very unnatural ghost. But yes you could drain one of Essence to destroy it pretty easily, there's Lodges dedicated to it, such as the Lodge of Cerberus. A fetish that alters the Barghest Gift or some other fetish or Gift might do it as well.

                                                As for using the Sacred Hunt to do so, the rite does explicitly call for the Essence draining to be used on a spirit. But this is the kind of thing special lodge Sacred Hunt advantages can take care of.
                                                I thought this may be useful to the conversation.
                                                For your given chronicle I'd definitely rule that it was a Ghost, because that puts it outside of the purview of Spirit powers, and anchors to the very things the Players want to loot would be perfect way to keep the players involved.

                                                Remember Werewolves only see in the Spirit frequency of Twilight, not the Ghost, so it'd be a major blind spot that the ghost would be savvy enough to exploit.

                                                The Ghost could even be a Geist if it was the first being interned in the burial site, adding power to it. Imagine a Geist unable to enter the Underworld... That'd be scary.

                                                What your players will likely have to resort to deal with the strangeness caused by a Werewolf Chieftain Ghost:

                                                The Sacred Hunt, the Siskur-Dah, is the most holy of all rites. Many werewolves believe that it draws upon the laws of the hunt that the Wolf-Mother herself personified.

                                                Symbols: The hunt, prey, claws or weapons, blood Sample Rite: Blood Talon ritemasters commonly use a circling dance of participants, with the Urathas’ claws marked with blood or ash. The ritemaster wears a mask representing the intended prey, and what follows is a mock-hunt around the dancing circle as the ritemaster seeks to break free. (Dexterity
                                                + Athletics)
                                                Action: Extended (10 successes; each roll represents 1
                                                minute)
                                                Success: The rite binds hunters and prey into the Siskur- Dah. All werewolves present are cast as the hunters. The ritemaster must indicate the intended prey during the rite, which can be an individual or a group, but they do not need to be identified with precise details or names.
                                                The rite has three effects:
                                                All participating werewolves gain the Siskur-Dah Condition.
                                                If the Uratha bring down a spirit that is the prey of this rite, they may divide its remaining Essence amongst themselves as they see fit; this can be used to destroy a spirit by draining all its Essence and discorporating it, but doing so is usually a breaking point toward Spirit and is seen as disrespectful if done without good reason.
                                                If the Uratha chase down spirit-prey that knows the symbols of a rite or can carve the secret of a Gift, any Uratha hunter present can expend the necessary Experiences to be marked with the Gift or learn the rite from the spirit. If this is done, the hunters do not get any Essence if they then bring the spirit down, and doing so is a breaking point toward Spirit.

                                                SISKUR-DAH (PERSISTENT )

                                                The Bone Shadow Sacred Hunt grants your character the ability to touch and strike ephemeral entities nominated as prey with her natural weapons.

                                                        This is how I would run it, first I'd make the rite:


                                                        Let's call the rite "Restless Hibernation". The werebears did the rite against the Chieftain because of the horrible crimes he did against their people.

                                                        My version would taking the werewolf to be turned into a ghost by ritually entombing them alive with any people or items that would be their Anchors if they were a human becoming a ghost. The werewolf will eventually suffocate, thirst or starve to death. If the werewolf is the first to be interned at the site, they leave behind a Geist but otherwise they leave a rank 2 ghost, with their Anchors being the items and remains of the persons buried with them.

                                                        This is horribly macabre, and not something pulled off easily or lightly. I'd say a 4 dot rite. In this particular case the Werebears didn't seal the tomb to prevent air flow, and there was a drip of water within reach, ensuring a slower and more painful death of starvation. Also beside the Fetishes that the Chieftain owned, all the werewolves, wolf-blooded, and human members of his Pack were included in the burial. The wolf-blooded and humans left ghosts, which the Chieftain as a Geist devoured, with possible exception of whatever ghost I want to use as a plot clue, and that ghost escaped by using the Discorporate Manifestation and reforming at an Anchor that withstand the test of time so the PCs can find it, if that's how I would lead them to the burial site. I also have the Geist devour any spirits in Twilight at its burial site, such as the Hunger Spirits his and his compatriots' deaths would have caused. It isn't even needed for the PCs to be the one to release this being from its burial site, that could have happened decades or hundreds of year prior. It's Anchors could be spread across the world and it could use Discorporate to travel between them.

                                                        In my example I'd have the Chieftain become a rank 3 Geist. I'd stat him like this:

                                                        Twilight Description to those that can see Ghosts: This nine foot tall being looms in what appears to be an emaciated wolf-headed monster covered in matted fur, with hind legs like a bipedal wolf. Its bony hands are massive, and have wicked claws, eyes sunken and yet burn with rage, and its rib-cage a visible arch showing signs of extreme starvation. Werewolves would recognize it as a werewolf in Garu form that looks like its been starved past the point of madness.

                                                        Power: 9
                                                        Finesse: 5
                                                        Resistance: 9
                                                        Essence: 20
                                                        Defense: 9
                                                        Willpower: 10
                                                        Size: 8
                                                        Corpus: 17


                                                        Bane: Werebears (After what they did to him, they become his greatest weakness)
                                                        Ban: Must attempt to kill any Werebear he sees.
                                                        Influence: Werewolves 3
                                                        Manifestations: Claim, Discorporate, Possess, Twilight Form
                                                        Numina: Awe, Drain, Essence Thief-Spirits, Implant Mission, Pathfinder, Stalwart

                                                        This Chieftain wasn't dumb, and neither is the geist he left behind, the corpses of his compatriots are long since dust and Claiming one of the fetishes risks destroying it. Without Image or Manifest, Implant Mission and Possessing are his only methods to contact werewolves without a ghost savvy intermediary. He could possess one of his Fetishes to carve messages, or use his Influence-Werewolves to add the Opened Anchor condition to allow him to Possess one of them. Keeping him an invisible, intangible presence that can Discorporate at will, possess the PCs,use Implant Mission to give visions to a pack's Cahalith, increases the mystery and horror if used correctly.

                                                        Side note: This would be a great Geist for a Silent Threshold Sin-eater.
