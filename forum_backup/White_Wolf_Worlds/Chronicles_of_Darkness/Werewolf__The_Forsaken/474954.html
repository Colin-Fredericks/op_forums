    New Shape-shifter: Tie Lin

    04-09-2015, 06:19 PM
    Hey, holy shit has it been such a long time since I have been able to actually get on here and post something! First, how have all of you been? Second. I have recently rediscovered a shape shifter that I created several years ago and wanted to make my first post in such a long time dedicated to introducing ( or reintroducing) one of my most beloved creations. Without further ado, here we go!
    ___________________________

    Tie Lin (Suhum Hafrutharra)

    He hadn't heeded his packmate's warnings, and now he was paying for it. Midnight-Howl knew of the stories about the strange healers who took residence in LA's Chinatown, but scoffed at them thinking they were just that, stories. So when he went there to deal with some pesky Duguthim alone, he was instead met with a completely unexpected sight: a trio of doctors working together with the Claimed to help treat people's ailments.

    Shocked and greatly confused, Midnight-Howl nonetheless moved to perform his sacred duty of keeping the realms separate, only to be stopped when the three doctors put themselves between him and the Duguthim. Howl ordered the doctors to move aside and let him kill the abominations, but they did not budge. He threatened to end their lives, but they did not budge. He promised them that he would murder their families with his bare hand and parade their corpses before the doctors, but they still did not budge. It was only when he lost patience and attempted to physically strike at them did they make any sort of movement, and even then it was something Midnight-Howl didn't expect.

    The doctors began to rapidly change before his eyes, one after the other, becoming taller.. bulkier.. and covered from head to toe in gold-brown scales. He had never seen such creatures before, they were like giant lumbering pine cones.. if pine cones had scythe like claws and long, broad tails. What happened next was a nightmare, his claws were blunted by their scales, their own claws cut swathes of his flesh away and their tails hacked into him like ax blades. He roared in agony and tried to fight them off, but they tore into him and his vision faded until there was only darkness.

    Howl awoke with a screaming pain in his abdomen and the first sight he beheld once again shocked him. There before him was one of the doctors, now in human form, tending to his wounds. Howls painfully sat up and shakily asked him why he was allowed to live. The doctor chuckled and looked up at the wounded Uratha " An army cannot receive intelligence without messengers, and dead men tell no tales. We will let you go provided that you deliver this message: Tell your wolf friends that they may try to encroach upon our lands, but we will stand in their way, and we will not be moved."

    The Tie Lin have many legends about their origins, and almost all of them involve oppression. Nearly one thousand and five hundred years ago, the elder Iron Scales claim, the Tie Lin came into being, with the spirit world offering aid to a simple farmer. The story is as follows:

    Long ago there was a poor farmer called Huiliang, a man who was as kind and just as his name suggested, and who had a deep respect for for all living creatures. Tragedy befell Huiliang and his family one day as a terrible and wasting sickness overtook his youngest son, threatening to take the boy's life before his first birthday. Huiliang and his wife did what they could for the child, but sadly they lived in a very poor village and the local healer was a corrupt man who demanded outrageous payments for his service, and the poor farmer couldn't possibly afford such prices and support his family at the same time. Desperate and more than a little mad from the possible loss of his child, Huiliang decided to brave the forests near the village in order to find what he knew had great medicinal powers: the scales of a pengguling, or pangolin.

    For days Huiliang searched, dodging predators and other dangers until finally finding one of the shy animals. Saying a quick prayer to the spirits, the man caught the pangolin and raised his knife, prepared to slay the poor beast. But before he could perform the act, the animal looked the farmer in the eye " I know why you've come farmer, and I wish to offer you a deal in exchange for my life." it said in a calm, sagely voice. Suddenly struck with fear, Huiliang dropped his knife and hastily set the armored creature down, who gave a sigh of relief and stood on it's hind legs.

    "What the spirits say about you is true, you are a genuinely good man Huiliang. I have long been watching over your village and all the insufferable corruption within it, and out of all the people, you were the most pure and just among them, even with so many disasters falling upon your family.

    Despite that, I wished to give you one final test to prove yourself worthy of my aid, and you have done so." the pangolin said as the farmer looked upon the creature with a mix of awe and fear before asking if it was a spirit.

    "Yes I am, and to reward your faith and loyalty to the spirits, I will heal your child. I warn you now, this healing will change your son forever, do you still wish to accept my aid?"

    Huiliang was silent for a long moment as he contemplated the spirit's offer. On one hand, he had no idea what it meant when it said that his son would be changed, but on the other hand if he refused his son would die. Huiliang steadied himself and accepted the spirit's aid.

    Huiliang brought the pangolin home with him on the spirit's instructions and carefully extracted one of it's scales. "Now Huiliang, this is my command. On each day that marks your child's birth you must grind one my scales into a powder and mix it into a tea, which your son must drink, and it will cure him. It must be done every year for 16 years however, lest the sickness overtake him once again." the spirit said to Huiliang, who at that time had carefully ground the scale into powder.

    "And once the sixteenth year has come and gone, your son will be fully purged of his sickness. After that, I will make one last request of you."

    Huiliang faithfully followed the commands of the pangolin spirit and each year gave his son the mystically imbued tea, which did indeed halt the disease and gave the boy a robust and healthy constitution, much to the joy of his father and mother. All too soon the 16th year had come and with it everything changed, Huiliang prepared the final cup of tea and gave it to his son, and he drank it as he always had. Once he did though, his body became wracked with immense pain as his bones painfully broke and rearranged themselves, his body grew larger, and scales began grow all over his form. Huiliang looked to the pangolin in terror and asked what was happening, to which it replied

    "This transformation is what I meant by him being changed forever. This world is poisoned, sickened, and your son would of been another victim of the plague that infests these great lands. Now that he has been cured, he has the power to cleanse this world, but he must first be taught how to do so." the pangolin said as the boy gave one final, inhuman cry as his transformation was completed. Where what was once a young man was now a massive bipedal pangolin. Huiliang shrieked and wildly fled as an unnatural fear overtook him and the spirit approached the boy. "Come with me, let me teach you how to heal those who suffer, so that you may share your knowledge and gifts with others." Huiliang's son watched his father run with sadness in his heart and followed the great pangolin into the forest to begin his training.

    The son's name was Tie Lin.

    While the Tie Lin hold their namesake and the unnamed Pangolin spirit in the utmost reverence, the majority of Iron Scales recognize the poor farmer Huiliang as the true father of their race and treat him with a level of love and respect that rivals the Uratha's love for Urfarah and Amahan Iduth, despite the man's fearful abandonment of his child. Because of this, the Tie Lin as a whole tend to be very family oriented and immensely protective of their children, willing to readily fight, kill, and even die to defend the sacred bond that is family in honor of Huiliang's determination to save his son.

    The second figure that they revere the most is the the pangolin spirit that lent its magic to Huiliang in order to heal Tie Lin. While the legends do not give the spirit a name, some Iron Scales have claimed to have been visited by this creature and state that it's name is Pangolin-Sage-of-Purity. While this has never been verified, enough Tie Lin accept this name to make it the unofficial name of the spirit.

    Nickname: Iron Scales

    Appearance and forms: Build wise, there are few characteristics that the Tie Lin have in common, there are as many short and stout Iron Scales as there are tall and lanky ones. Ethnically wise however, is when they truly show a sense of unity. The vast majority of Tie Lin are either Asiatic or black African in origin, though there are a small percent of Arab Tie Lin and an even smaller number who are white Afrikaans. Another common thread among Tie Lin is a typical fondness for loose clothing to help conceal their features, a racial behavioral trait connected to their animal form.

    When they assume their hybrid form the first most obvious change is that the Tie Lin grows bulkier before they increase in height, and the vast amount of this bulk is muscle that will be needed to support additional changes. The next most noticeable changes are the rapid growth of fur, often a grey-brown color but sometimes a sandy brown or outright blonde all over the body and the face and jaw elongate and narrow dramatically, and the eyes shrink with the pupils dilating immensely. A shocking feature about the already freakish head is that the tongue is also changed dramatically, it becomes very thin and worm like... and immensely long, capable of extending almost ten feet away from the head, possibly even longer essence is used to augment it. After this the final three changes take place: the body finally begins to grow almost two feet in height, and the masses of muscle become evenly proportioned at last, and out of the Tie Lin's spine erupts a long, slightly flat and broad tail, which quickly grows fur and is often around six to six and a half feet long. At last as the transformation is near it's zenith the body is covered with a heavy coat of hard scales and the creatures talons erupt from the fingers, completing the change.

    Traits: Strength +2, Dexterity +1, Stamina + 4, Size +2, Perception + 2, Armor 3/3, Claws +2 L, Tail, +1 L, Health + 6.

    Animal form is a slightly larger than average Giant Pangolin, though females typically tend to be smaller species. Coloration are typically normal as per species, though some odd coloration's do exist, seemingly based slightly off of an individual's personality.

    Traits: Strength +1, Dexterity +1, Stamina +2, Size 3(2), Perception +3, Armor 2/1, Claws 2 L, Health -1

    Reproduction: The Tie Lin are capable of breeding with humans and even each other, but the act of sexual procreation isn't enough to to pass on the transformative and restorative abilities of their race to their young. To truly ensure that their race lives on and grows a Tie Lin must perform an prolonged mystical ritual that utilizes their scales and their own bestial Essence. On each of the child's birthday's, starting from the first and ending on the 16th, the Tie Lin parent prepares a special tea where the main ingredient is a ground up scale from the shifter's Animal Form. Once ground it is mixed into the tea and the Tie Lin pays a point of Essence and a willpower point, and then serves it to the child. Once drunk, the Essence inside the child rests within their soul, where it pools year after year until their sixteenth birthday. While normally allowing Essence to sit within a human being would cause them to slowly sicken and die, not to mention unwanted spirit attentions, the magic of the ritual ensures that the child is healthy and safe from negative spiritual influence. In fact, the tea is capable of improving the child's health considerably and keeping the worst of diseases at bay provided that the child keeps imbibing the teach each year. Once the sixteenth birthday is reached and the final cup of tea has been drank the transformation happens immediately, fueled by the vast store of Essence within their souls. Not only that, but once the transformation is complete the newly "born" Tie Lin will find that any sickness he or she suffered from to be completely cured, and if that weren't enough they soon discover that they constantly radiate an aura of healing energy ( Essence) that is capable of rejuvenating the world around them. This ritual must be performed every birthday, if even a single birthday is missed the child can never become a member of the Tie Lin, and the protective magic of the ritual fades, and the child then begins to suffer the effects of having Essence festering within her.

    Auspices: Like the Forsaken, the Tie Lin are "born" into auspices that denote their roles within Tie Lin Society. These auspices, called Elements, are based off the five Chinese elements of: Mu (Wood), Huo (Fire), Tu (Earth), Jin (Metal), and Shui ( Water). In addition to that each element has two sub functions, or Cycles that represent creation ( Generating) and destruction (Overcoming). It is to be noted that Tu and Jin are interesting as they both share the same Generating skill. Cyclic skills all receive a +2 to rolls involving them thanks to the blessings of their elements.

    Mu: The sages and mystics, these Tie Lin have a great understanding of nature and spirits alike. Cyclic skills: Occult/Persuasion.
    Huo: Explorers and Warriors, these Tie Lin are the rarest found yet some of the most treasured. Cyclic skills: Survival/Intimidation.
    Tu: Laborers and Builders, Tie Lin connected to the element of Earth are the firmament of their race. Cyclic skills: Crafts/Athletics.
    Jin: Inventors and weapon-smiths, those Tie Lin aligned with Metal are the tools that are born of Earth's efforts. Cyclic skills: Crafts/Weaponry.
    Shui: Healers and caretakers, Tie Lin of Water are the most commonly encountered and are the most respected. Cyclic skills: Medicine/Empathy

    Another thing that must be noted is that when it comes to these Elements, the Tie Lin are unique from many other shapeshifters in that they are able to actually attune themselves to any of the five Elements, with great difficulty of course. While they are born into a single Element on the moment of their First Change, the Tie Lin are connected to all five of them, and many who learn of this fact often spend many months studying the different Elements and once they are ready they attempt to realign themselves to another Element. To do this, the Tie Lin must first spend an entire day and night in deep contemplation over the Element he intends to attune his bestial soul to. Once he has done so he then spends a willpower dot and rolls his Harmony at a minus 3 penalty. If successful, he realigns his soul with the element he was wanting and gains all the benefits granted by that Element. If they fail, they simply do not change Elements, and if it is a dramatic failure they lose access to their Element blessings for an entire month.

    Rage: Escape Rage.

    Renown: Purity ( in imitation of a cleansing spring.) and Honor ( their intense loyalty love for their family.)

    Inborn Aspects: Shapeshifting, Regeneration, Wellspring


    Inborn Aspect- Wellspring:

    When the Tie Lin undergo their first change the Essence that sat within their souls awakened the potent life force within them and caused it to bubble and well out of them like a spring of pure water. This potent life energy is referred to as Qi by the Tie Lin and exists as an aura that radiates from them, extending around them in a radius of varying distances. This aura of life energy is quite potent, and while it is unable to heal the Tie Lin or others mechanics wise, it does provide a number of benefits. The first is that it adds a + 3 to all medicine rolls made by the Tie Lin unless they are aligned with the Shui Element, in which case the modifier raises to 4. The second benefit is that the Tie Lin and any living being within the radius of their Qi field ( barring hostile subjects) gains a +3 to all stamina rolls to avoid falling unconscious. This aura doesn't only affect the physical world, it even reaches into the Shadow, and that is when the aura's truly interesting properties become evident. While the Tie Lin are unsure exactly how or why, it is often speculated that the Gauntlet that separates the physical and spirit worlds is able to convert the Qi energy that passes through it into Essence, which then coalesces into a single brilliant white orb which then generates and radiates more Essence, transforming it's physical anchor into a variation of a living Locus. As the orb is connected to the Tie Lin, it moves whenever it's host moves and like all loci it causes the shadow to grow thin, making it easy for the two worlds to touch where ever the Iron Scale goes. The locus generates Essence normally, but unlike other loci it's rating does not go up when it reaches a certain amount of essence, instead its rating equates to half of the character's Primal Urge score.

    It should be noted that while yes this personal locus does give the Tie Lin theoretically unlimited Essence, there are two restrictions: The first is that this Aspect doesn't allow the Tie Lin to cross into the Gauntlet, they need the use of a separate power to do so. The second restriction is that while in the spirit world, they can only draw Essence from their personal locus once per day ( they can drink from other Tie Lin loci as often as they please, though it's considered a Harmony 1 sin.). In addition, if this wellspring of Essence is completely drained, the Tie Lin suffers a point of lethal damage every hour for a whole day until the locus regenerates itself.

    Common Aspects: Parallel Gift (Manipulate Earth), Invisible Mark, Near Man Form

    Unique Aspects:

    Parting The Veil(*)
    Cost: one willpower
    Effect: Despite being living loci and having a weakened Gauntlet around them at all times, the Tie Lin do not have an instinctual ability to cross into the spirit world. Luckily the Irons scales had long since learned how to force their way into the Shadow. This Aspect allows the Tie Lin to "step sideways" like the Forsaken and are granted the ability to draw Essence from loci, including their own.

    That Which Rolls Up (**)
    Cost: 1 Essence
    Effect: While in Animal form or Hybrid form the Tie Lin is able to roll up into a ball to protect themselves from attack. By spending an Essence point, the Tie Lin is able to mystically augment their natural armor, granting a 5/5 armor for the Hybrid Form, and a 3/3 armor for the animal form. In addition, attempts to unroll a Tie Lin suffer from a penalty equal to the character's Strength + Brawl.

    Tongue Lash (**)
    Cost: 1 Essence
    Effect: Pangolins have immensely long tongues, they have to in order to capture their preferred prey items ( Ants and Termites). The Tie Lin are no different, possessing tongues up to ten feet long in their hybrid form, and thanks to this Aspect are capable of extending their already massive tongues even further. By spending a point of Essence the Tie Lin is able to extending the length of his already considerable tongue by a number of yards equal to his Primal Urge. In addition, the tongue is quite dextrous and tough, and the Tie Lin is able to use his full dice pools to Strength+Brawl with a + 2 to grappling. If the tongue is ever severed, it can be regenerated with relative ease with a willpower point and three Essence.

    Salve (***)
    Cost: 2 (3) Essence
    Effect: Spending 2 points Essence, the Tie Lin grinds up one of their scales and mixes it with their saliva, creating a restorative paste that when applied to a wound ( whether their own or another creatures) and making a Medicine + Primal roll they are able to heal a number of lethal damage equal to every two successes. by spending an additional Essence the Iron Scale is able to heal Aggravated damage as well.

    Guillotine (****)
    Cost: 2 Essence+ 1 Willpower ( optional)
    Effect: The tail of an Iron Scale is their most powerful weapon, and this Aspect makes it even more deadly. When making a Strength + Brawl attack against a downed opponent while using their tail, the Iron Scale is able to deal aggravated damage by spending two Essence. If they spend a point of willpower in addition to the Essence cost, they can make a called shot for the head to instantly kill their opponent.

    Relationship with the Shadow:

    Due to their status as living loci, it is little surprise that they are often surrounded by spirits at all times. Because of this it's a common belief that the Tie Lin must be constantly weakened from their loci always being drained and they must assuredly be nothing more than slaves to said spirits. In all actuality neither of these assumptions are true, and the Tie Lin are more than willing to disprove them. While Spirits do indeed feed from the loci connected to them, the unique resonance they have ensure that the spirits never take too much, and in fact acts to help change those spirits who do feed from them for the better. Possessing resonances of Tranquility, Balance, and similar concepts has a very calming effect on spirits and they tend to have quite positive dealings with the Tie Lin. Now this isn't always the case, there are plenty of spirits ( such as spirits of violence, murder, oppression, etc) which are likely to regard the Iron Scales with less than peaceful reactions, but for the most part many spirits are more than happy to work with their scaly benefactors. This Resonance doesn't only affect spirits, but the spirit realm as well, and slowly heals the spiritual land scape with in the loci's radius. due to this healing effect upon the land, Tie Lin often flock to places where the Shadow is turbulent in order to fulfill their self imposed duty as "doctors" of the spirit world.

    Relationship with the Uratha:

    When it comes down to it, the Uratha and the Tie Lin have much in common. They both have a deep respect for the spirit world and see themselves as guardians of the Shadow's harmony, not to mention both races being shapeshifting creatures. One would think that these two races could be great allies, one group excising dark elements with their teeth and claws while the other worked their healing magic to rejuvenate and strengthen the positive ones. Sadly this is far from the case,as while the werewolves would more than likely jump at the chance to have access to allies with such great powers of restoration, the Iron Scales are far less receptive. Long ago, the Tie Lin say, the Uratha tried to enslave their race, believing that men who became prey animals should've been treated like prey animals regardless of the magics they possessed. This was to say that the Uratha thought that the Tie Lin were only fit to be exploited and used like a resource, a potent and highly useful resource, but a resource all the same. Naturally the Tie Lin resisted, and after several decades of war and great losses on both sides the Tie Lin gained their freedom and were able to drive the wolves from their lands. It has been many centuries since those wars, but the wounds left by them are still fresh in the Iron Scales minds and has left a deep abiding hatred for the children of Father Wolf within the collective psyche of the race. Now there are very rare exceptions to this and in some places there are alliances between Tie Lin and Uratha, but as stated these arrangements are very rare and more often than not short lived. Rarely does this hatred lead to direct violence between the two races, the Tie Lin are largely not as skilled at combat as their savage cousins, but more through passive aggressive methods. From sabotaging enemy loci to convincing spirits to fight on their behalf, Iron Scales can easily play merry hell with the lives of their Forsaken enemies, and if the wolves do try to fight back and target the Tie Lin's friends and family... well that is when the Tie Lin will strike directly.

    Relationship with Silver:

    Like many shapechangers the Tie Lin suffer aggravated from silver, but unlike most they are unsure exactly why. Despite this general confusion there are several theories, ranging from silver's use in ancient medical practices to being used as a poison to a Tie Lin belief that silver conducts and absorbs Essence. Regardless of why it scorches them, all Tie Lin rightly fear the bite of silver and try to avoid it as best they can.
