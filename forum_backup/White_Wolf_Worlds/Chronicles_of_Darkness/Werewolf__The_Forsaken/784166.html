    Gift of Bone

    12-11-2015, 10:57 AM
    Below is a Gift list I am making for a game I am running this is the very first draft of some of the Gifts Facets and looking for input on what I have and for ideas on the other two Facets. The general theme of this list is the physical side of Bone hence all the combat facets and strengthening, I will be making a spiritual version later that will deal with the mystical aspect of bones and what they mean. But please take a look and feel free to let me know what you think, this is my first shot at writing Gifts for the new edition.

    Bone Gifts

    (Cunning)
    Unknown

    Bone Spikes (Glory)
    The Uratha’s bones grow sharp edges and spike the break through the Uratha’s skin greatly adding him in battle.
    Activate: 1 Essence
    Action: Instant
    Duration: Scene
    Upon activating the spikes erupt from his skin causing 1 lethal and granting a weapon bonus equal to the Uratha’s Glory.

    Bones of Stone (Honor)
    With this Facet the Uratha’s bones become as hard and unbreakable as the earth itself.
    Permanent
    The Uratha’s bones become unbreakable and she gains armor 1/1

    Blades of Bone (Purity)
    The Uratha with this facet may never be without a weapon if she so desires for her very bones can produce blades strong as steel.
    Activate: 2 Essence
    Action: Instant
    Duration: Special
    Dice pool: Strength + Weaponry + Purity
    Dramatic Failure: The Blade grows inward causing 4L in damage.
    Failure: No weapon is created
    Success: The Uratha scores successes and draws forth a bone weapon of her desire allocating her success rolled to the weapons damage, size, and durability. The weapon retains these properties for the scene after which time the essence inside the weapon fades and it becomes just a normal peace of bone. If the Uratha has the Bones of Stone Facet the bone weapon is unbreakable for the same duration.
    Exceptional Success: The Uratha may invest another point of Essence (or maybe a dote of willpower) to make the weapon retain is bonuses permanently

    (Wisdom)
    Unknown

      I am currently writing up an article re Gift design which may prove useful, but initial comments follow. These will be critical because, well, that's how I approach Gift creation:

      - Gifts don't tend to split a single concept between the physical and spiritual. You're probably better off taking your best ideas for physical and your best ideas for spiritual and putting them all in one Gift, rather than scrabbling for filler Facets to round out the 10 Facets you'd need to cover.
      - Bone Spikes is better than Slaughterer in pretty much every way. I would suggest that instead it at least be limited to only adding to damage inflicted in grapples. One-off 1L damage is not a meaningful cost, just flavour, so I wouldn't expect that element to 'buy' it an increased effect for its cost. Possibly it could work if it inflicted 1L on the werewolf every time she uses the damage bonus in a grapple.
      - Bones of Stone is not terribly interesting and has an effect that doesn't scale. Additionally, if it's all about strengthening the bones, I'm not sure why it would give armour against attacks rending at the flesh. Instead I'd suggest it add Honor in armour against attacks that are attempting to apply the Arm Wrack, Leg Wrack or Stunned Conditions, although that's just an idea off the top of my head. Additionally, why is this Honor?
      - Having Blades of Bone feature a dice pool roll seems a bit pointless; I'd just suggest it provide a weapon with bonus equal to Purity for simplicity. That said, bone is... not really associated with strong, resilient weapons in general, tbh, and given a werewolf already has access to natural weapons I'm not sure why the werewolf expression of the Purity of bones would be to produce bone blades.

      - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer

                      Here it is.

                      Cage The Rider (oooo)
                      Cost: 1 Essence to activate + 1 Essence per scene of entrapment
                      Dice Pool: Resolve + Intimidate + Wisdom
                      Action: Instant

                      Many of the spirits that cause the most trouble for Uratha are those that attempt to Ride living creatures. This particularly risky Gift allows the Ithaeur to act as bait to such spirits and then to trap them within herself. Some Ithaeur refer to this Gift as "catching a spirit in my ribcage," while others liken it to swallowing a spirit for a time or caging a spirit within the mind. Most frequently, this Gift is used either to frighten a spirit into complying with the pack's demands, or as a temporary way to catch a spirit until a formal Rite of Binding or other means of disposal can be prepared.


                      Dramatic Failure: The internal "spirit cage" does not form, but the enticing aura still manifests in a way, drawing the attention of nearby spirits without disguising the werewolf's nature. The Ithaeur is likely to suffer from unwanted spirit attention for the remainder of the scene.

                      Failure: The Gift does not work, and spirits regard the Ithaeur as different in no particular way.

                      Success: Until the next moonrise, the Ithaeur exudes a spiritual aura that is particularly enticing to spirits that possess the Living Fetter, Possession or Claim Numina. The werewolf appears to be the ideal host to such a spirit, and the spirit is unlikely to even notice that the Ithaeur is a werewolf at all. Obviously, this Gift is not used lightly; around a nest of spirits with such Numina, it could start a minor war. A spirit that is more than one Rank above the Ithaeur's effective Rank is not affected by this Gift, and most do not notice the lure (though it's possible that one might).

                      ​When a spirit attempts to fetter, claim or possess the Ithaeur via a Numen, the trap activates. The player rolls Intelligence + Occult + Wisdom as a reflexive action to attempt to trap the spirit; the spirit contests the roll by rolling Power. If the roll is a tie or the spirit gets more successes, the Gift effects end, and the spirit breaks free (and may attack or flee, depending on its mental state). If the player gets more successes, though, the spirit is effectively trapped for a scene; the Ithaeur may extend the duration of the imprisonment by spending one Essence per scene. The spirit shares the same space as the Ithaeur, yet cannot break free or manifest itself. The spirit cannot use any Influences or Numina while bound within the Ithaeur. Once per day, at sunrise, the spirit may make a new attempt to break free by rolling Power contested by the Ithaeur's Resolve + Occult. If the spirit breaks free, or is released by the Ithaeur (the Ithaeur may do so at any time as a reflexive action), it wins free of the werewolf's body. If the Ithaeur is in the physical world at this time, the spirit will appear in a state of Twilight. An Ithaeur can have only one spirit bound within her at a time by means of this Gift.

                      Exceptional Success: The trap is particularly cunningly crafted. The player gains two extra dice to the roll made to initially trap the spirit.
                      I was thinking of adapting this as part of my conversion of Witch's Moon into 2E. Though perhaps this could better fit as the Wisdom Facet for your Gift of Bone? On a semi-related note, this was the 1E Gift I used as the basis for the Cage Tell in this thread of mine.

                                      A dice pool of "Presence + Medicine + Honor" works. As does making its duration be a number of turns equal to successes. On the other hand, I got another suggestion, Why not make the immunity to Arm/Leg Wrack be for an Exceptional Success. Either way, here's an updated write-up.

                                      -=-

                                      Bones of the Pack [Honor]
                                      Cost: 5 Essence
                                      Dice Pool: Presence + Medicine + Honor
                                      Action: Instant
                                      Duration: 1 Scene
                                      The Uratha draws strength from the strongest of bonds, the bond of pack. And she weaves that strength into her their very bones to keep them whole.

                                      Dramatic Failure: Not only does the Werewolf fail to activate the Facet, but it rebounds upon her and her packmates as crippling phantom pains. Each member of the pack takes a point of aggravated damage.

                                      Failure: The Facet fails to be invoked.

                                      Success: The Uratha and her packmates each gain Armor rating equal to her Honour Renown.

                                      Exceptional Success: In addition to the Armor granted by the Facet, the pack receives an immunity to the Arm and Leg Wrack Tilts for the remainder of the scene. If any of the members is already suffering from either Tilt, it does not affect them until either the end of the scene or the damage that inflicted it is healed.
