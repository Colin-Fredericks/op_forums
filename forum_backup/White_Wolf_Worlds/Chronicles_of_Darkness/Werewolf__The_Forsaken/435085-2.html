    Okay, so my friends and I started playing our 2ed game in hunting grounds Tokyo. We finished it and want to start another game in the same setting. One of my friends wants his character to be in a samurai lodge. I don't know any in Forsaken but I do know one from Apocalypse. So I present him with the Hakken and he likes it. We try to translated to Forsaken 2ed but the problem was we didn't know how since the description for lodges was pretty vague and not much information. So my question is: are the rules for lodges still the same?

    So far we decided the lodge to be from:
    Eldoth or Storm Lord
    Signature gift: Weather or Evasion
    (1ed) Prerequisite: Honor #? Weaponry (katana)
    I would not assume that the rules for lodges will still be the same.

    - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer

                            I'm curious to see what happens with new Lodges. They've stopped most vampire bloodlines from offering disciplines. But that was never really an issue with Lodges, most of whom didn't have any gifts unique to them, mostly just having a fetish or rite.

                            With all non-Moon gifts being available now to everyone, outside of Auspice-oriented lodges I don't think we'd see more Shadow Gifts from lodges. But I guess it would be possible to have certain Shadow gifts that are just cheaper for Lodge members than 'ordinary' Uratha.

                            I've said before I liked the idea of lodges offering specific sacred prey, and it looks like it's popped up in some of the example ones but isn't going to be the defining factor of a lodge.

                            Whatever it is, I hope it's different from before and I'm sure it'll be interesting.
