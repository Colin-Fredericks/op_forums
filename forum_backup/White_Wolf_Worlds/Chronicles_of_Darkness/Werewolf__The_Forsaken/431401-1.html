    The core rules are future-proofed for the addition of more Moon Gifts for each Auspice in the future.

    Currently each Auspice only has one Moon Gift that it can select, but that may change with future supplements and the system allows for people concocting their own homebrewed Moon Gifts.

    - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
