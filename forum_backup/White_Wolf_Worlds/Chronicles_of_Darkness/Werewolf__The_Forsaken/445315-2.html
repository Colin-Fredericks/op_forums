    Originally posted by Aynie View Post
    is the biological aspects causing me to twitch with the implications? Yeah, but I'm probably not going to ever touch on it in an actual game. I'm not that crazy
    The problem is the only, RAW, biological aspect implication is that werewolves that try to go vegetarian will starve to death; like any other obligate carnivore.

    If you want me to go into a much more thorough biological explanation I can back up with fully scientific analysis, I really can. But it seems pretty pointless. There's a very simple term for what you ingest, but don't digest: excrement. That's what happens to werewolves that eat vegetable matter. It turns into poo without providing nutrients to the bloodstream. It might even make their experience shitting nicer (that's why cats eat grass after all).

    There really isn't any reason for you to "twitch" about "implications" from a purely scientific perspective. Beyond the suspension of disbelief factor of dealing with werewolves here, animals all ingest things they don't/can't digest. It's pretty much a universal animal issue. Sometimes it's a bad thing, most of the time its a neutral thing, and sometimes it's a good thing. Indigestible does not equate to things that make you sick if you ingest them.

    Eh, I would if I wanted to not include physical starvation as a part and parcel of the wolfy condition but I do see where you're coming from.
    Physical starvation is part of the wolfy condition because werewolves are still living creatures that need to eat. The only difference is that PU 1 werewolves can stave off starvation using all the same food as humans, and PU 2+ werewolves have more restrictive diets.

    Unless you're taking out the need to eat completely (which seems like it would set off the biological implication twinge a lot harder...) physical starvation is always going to be a potential problem.

    Originally posted by Incendax View Post
    The humor actually comes from a literal interpretation of Page 93 (RAW) instead of the spirit (RAI). Not from anything being taken out of context. However, I am going to bow out of the discussion because you are taking it in a really ugly direction.
    ....

    1) The literal interpretation of p. 93 is that werewolves can't digest non-meat. Digest means, literally, to break down ingested material into a form that can be absorbed into the body. Not being able to digest something does not literally mean it causes any sort of biological malfunction. As previously posted, animals purposefully ingest things they cannot digest all the time as healthy behavior.

    2) Yes, you are taking it out of context as the book specifies "obligate carnivores" which is not "animals that have severe gastrointestinal distress if they consume anything but meat," but rather, "animals that have to consume meat to obtain their biological nutrients." Yes, you are skipping the next sentence that says, "While she can ingest other foods she cannot gain nourishment from them,..."

    3) The only "dark" place I've taken this, is that humor is subjective. Going around insisting an a incorrect statement on the literal definition of digest, that leads to implications which have no actual backing in the RAW, just to make poop jokes is... not going to work for everyone. That's not dark. That's just basic truth.
