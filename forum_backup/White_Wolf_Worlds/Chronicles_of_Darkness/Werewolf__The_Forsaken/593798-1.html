                                            Here's a test second-rewrite/clarification of Renown into Influence. What do you guys think? (Keep in mind, I'm running a mixed breeds game, not strictly Forsaken, though our pack's favorite allies are Forsaken)

                                            (beta rewrite 2)

                                            Renown (and Respect) is replaced by Influence.

                                            As a Therian progresses in power and understanding, their Influence with the matters of spirit increase.

                                            This acts much like a Spirit's Influence, in that it represents a measure of power. It does not grant the same arbitrary command over the Influenced subject that a Spirit enjoys, but it does both enable and empower a Therian's Gifts.

                                            The five Renowns and five Respects are available as Influences. Like Spirit Influences, others may be defined, with similar guidelines. Not all Therians must have the core five or five.

                                            Influence is displayed as tattooing or branding on the skin. Children of Luna are often marked with silver tattoos which glow in the Umbra. Other traditions may use other options.

                                            Influence also has a social aspect, much like the Renown rules. Spirits and other Spirit-tied characters (including Therians) often compare Influence to determine their relative rank. Influence as a measure of reputation often precedes a character - Spirits whisper and any Therian can readily see silver tattoos in the Umbra.

                                            Lunar silver tattoos (and those of some other traditions) may be empowered by 1 Essence to glow brightly for a Scene. This is bright enough to be faintly visible in the material world. Doing so among the Forsaken is considered a sign of respect, though it inspires much chest-thumping and trash talking. Doing so among other species has varying interpretations.

                                            To gain a Gift, the Therian must have sufficient Influence to obtain it. The linked Influence should be the same as listed, or a sensible alternative. Notice that similar Influences may not include all of the same thing. Purity would not include Gifts for despicable combat tactics, nor Wrath include Gifts for limiting collateral damage, even though both of them are suitable Influences for the gifts of warriors.

                                            When a Gift is obtained, it is forever tied to the particular Influence that enabled it. (extra-special beta-test rule follows) In all cases, any roll to use the Gift includes that Influence in the dice pool.
