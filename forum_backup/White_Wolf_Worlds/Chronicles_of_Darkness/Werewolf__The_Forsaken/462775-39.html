        Originally posted by Heroofthemists View Post
        How would the spilling of vitae on land effected by the facet "Black earth, red hunger" effect the user? Would they still heal a lethal point instead of a bashing?
        Vampires don't bleed Vitae, or at all, except under special circumstances. If one had Blush of Life active and you cut them, they would 'bleed' inert blood and it would work as normal. If you managed to sever limbs with aggravated damage, Vitae would come out with blood as the limb decayed, activating the effect. If you just clawed or bit them with lethal damage, nothing.

        If a vampire just decided to pour out some Vitae, I'd say yes.

        Originally posted by Ur-Than View Post
        I'm sorry if it has already been asked but... what is the link between the Werewolves and the other Changing Breed, at least on a mythological level ?
        Depends on which changing breed you mean. In first edition there wasn't really much of a connection other than being overseen by Luna, patron of change and shapechangers, but there were some exceptions. In second edition you could probably link them to Pangaea, the lost world, but we don't know much about the standard changing breeds. The hosts, the azlu spiders and beshilu rats, are shards of a slain progenitor beast from Pangaea, trying to resurrect them or fulfill its work.

        It's nothing like Werewolf the Apocalypse, if that's why you were asking.

                        Originally posted by Heroofthemists View Post
                        How do multiple opponents work in a grapple situation? Lets say Player A decides to bite and grapple Enemy. Player B also wants to bite and aid Player A in Grapple. I didn't see any rules regarding this, although I may have missed them.
                        If Player B goes first, then Enemy's Defense will be 1 lower when Player A tries to grapple (or Enemy will elect not to use Defense against Player B's attack and will more likely get hit). After the grapple has initiated, there's nothing stopping Player B from attacking Enemy (though unless Player A is Holding Enemy, Enemy still gets their Defense against Player B).

                                Originally posted by sidhehound View Post
                                I have an end game and Totem related question: Primal Urge 10 Uratha, went full spirit and joined a Firstborn pack. Can this now spirit werewolf be taken as a Totem by another werewolf pack? And if so, what would its rank be?
                                This is a suitably unique event to be up to the individual Storyteller rather than something we've done any mapping for on a general mechanical level. In general, the answer given 'this werewolf is now a spirit' is that yes, as a spirit they could be taken by a werewolf pack if that pack could muster enough Totem dots to afford them. Rank would depend hugely on the hows and whys of the werewolf and their ascension. I know in my own games, a predator bodhisattva (ie going past PU10) surpassing their Flesh entirely to become a spirit would be minimum Rank​ 4, more likely Rank 5+, and with additional unique capabilities/aspects of existence on top of that of a usual spirit.
