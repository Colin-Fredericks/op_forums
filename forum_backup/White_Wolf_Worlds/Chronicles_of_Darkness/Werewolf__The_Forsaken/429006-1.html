                                You guys may be overlooking something. You don't have to make the Resolve + Composure check. It's a choice between attacking things or making the check, if you REALLY need to get something else done. Additionally, since the rules are modified for the Rage, you can attack inanimate objects instead of your friends or enemies.

                                Basically, if you've got the time, you can run off into the night, smashing stuff and get to a safe place. I'd play this out narratively if you have 10 minutes to run, allowing players to choose how carefully they go or what they're aiming for.

                                Or, if you're desperate to get something done before you Basu-im, you can roll the checks. Examples being: Getting talked down. Flipping that switch. Shutting off all the trash compactors on the detention level.

                                And, of course, you can blow Willpower and hope for an exceptional if you're desperate.

                                Lastly, Controlled Burn is only weaker if you don't get Fighting Finesse for your bite attack! Urhan for the win!

                                    It's not the power loss I have a problem with. That's fine, that's a natural consequence of suppressing the rage. It's the notion of it pushing you away from gauru I don't like.

                                    See, I view the forms as on a sort of slope, and rage as a landslide that sends you sliding down it. .Gauru is at the bottom, dalu/urshul is a sort of ledge halfway up, and hishu/urhan are at the top. Normally a werewolf entering rage gets sucked down, clinging to the dalu/urshul form for a bit before losing their grip and landing at the base. Controlled Burn I see as providing a sort of handhold at the top, high enough that you can pull yourself out. If you're already in hishu or urhan when you get set off, it works fine. You're stuck at the top because if you let go to drop to dalu or urshul you'll overshoot and end up in gauru and kuruth proper. The problem comes when you enter rage whilst already in dalu/urshul. Then my neat little visualisation goes all wonky because mr werewolf finds themselves launched from halfway down the slope to the top by the landslide. Which doesn't make any sense.
