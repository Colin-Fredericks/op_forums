        We've been having it work on mages, sin-eaters, changelings and demons in human guise, though that's based on first edition sin-eaters and changelings. Vampires, mummies and prometheans don't count because they're essentially dead or inorganic.

        Plus our game involves a lot of vampires so we don't want to make it too easy. Ghouls count for humans, though.

                        Absolutely. They are 'detailed' in a sidebar in Lodges: the Faithful on p18, but they are the children of the Firstborn. Garm, of the Lodge of Garm, for instance, is the son of Fenris.
