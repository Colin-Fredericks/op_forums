    Rotworm
    Reflections of basic concepts of decay, including decomposers, rotworms appear as lethargic green worms with pale white veins, a contracting sphincter acting as a mouth at the 'front.' One can easily see how close they are to becoming hursah, as those nearing the next rank bulge as if over-stuffed and act more energetically. Opportunistic and swarming predators, in the rare occasions they find themselves in the gurihal rotworms often fetter themselves to trash or, preferably, the dead.
    Rank: 1
    Power 2, Finesse 1, Resistance 2, Willpower 3, Essence 10
    Corpus 3, Size 1, Initiative 3, Defense 2, Speed 4
    Influence: Decay
    Ban: Rotworms must move towards any dead or decaying material nearby, not including the supernaturally preserved dead, like vampires Bane: Fire
    Manifestations: Twilight Form, Fetter
    Numina: Entropic Decay, Innocuous

    Graveworm
    Thicker and longer than their hursih predecessors they're often found surrounded by, graveworms are more like snakes, ranging from one to five feet long and as thick around as a human's leg. They tend to look green or brown or black, and are surrounded by a musty stench of rotting flesh. More firmly bound to death, they usually inhabit graveyards or the dens of killers, but will occasionally be seen in the wilderness or near it, feasting on roadkill and other dead animals. In the rare occasions they can, they are known to use their powers through the Gauntlet to turn the dying into the dead.
    Rank: 2
    Power 4, Finesse 2, Resistance 3, Willpower 5, Essence 15
    Corpus 6, Size 3, Initiative 5, Defense 2, Speed 9
    Influence: Decay 1, Death 1
    Ban: Bleeds 1 Essence every hour it's not near something Dead-Resonant as if it was in the guriahl Bane: Any object that has been sterilized
    Manifestations: Twilight Form, Fetter, Reaching
    Numina: Dement, Drain, Entropic Decay, Innocuous, Seek

    Having a hard time figuring out appropriate bans for the ranks. I figure the Graveworm would be a specific kind of 'evolution' from the rotworm, one that has been around dead humans and animals, rather than garbage and other organics, which might become Rustworms or Moldworms.

        Originally posted by The Anubi View Post
        Hello, I was wondering if someone could help me out. I have a game I am running of Requiem set in 1191 AD. The characters are going to be entering in to the city of Bishops Lin where currently there is a feud between the Blood Talons who are of Norwegian descent and still hold the Nordic faith in high esteem and the Iron Masters most of whom are influencing the Christian church. Now I was wondering if I could get help because both would need totems that correspond with their religions.
        I kept meaning to reply to this, mainly because I love me Medieval Christianity and Norse culture. Hope it isn't too late for your game!

        Firstly, I'd say that you shouldn't feel enslaved to the religions for totem ideas. It's not like every American has Jesus as a totem, eh? I've mined some religious ideas below, but also some cultural and regional touchstones (heh), as well. If there are any you're interested in, I can help stat 'em up if you like (or someone else can; we've got some geniuses in this thread!)

        Wall's-Blessed-Strength
        A spirit of stone and protection, or perhaps the spirit of the walls encircling a Christian settlement. It appears as a robed warrior with a battered old sword, chiselled entirely out of stone, with eyes of eternal flames. On the rare times it speaks, its voice booms like a mighty drawbridge heaving into place. Every movement sounds like great stones scraping against one another. While the spirit's might is famous across Norway, it's also its greatest weakness - Wall's-Blessed-Strength can not refuse protection to a Christian traveller, and should the flame at the top of its highest tower ever go out, it will fall into Slumber.

        Black Goat That Howls At Night (Never said the Iron Masters had to be good Christians, right?)
        This spirit is old, and has walked the land for many years. When the invaders came - casting down the old gods, mocking them, literally demonising them - most of the spirits hated them. Not the Black Goat. Nourished on Christian folklore, it grew from a simple goat spirit into a symbol of everything they fear. The pack that serve it hide among the herd, but - like their totem - must embrace the townsfolk's superstitions, risking discovery at every turn. Every Sunday, though, it flees into the wilds, for it cannot abide the sound of church bells.

        Eye-Biter
        A spirit of hidden knowledge, appearing as a flock of ravens that speak as one. Like Odin's messengers, it will whisper many things of past and future to its pack, but always at a cost - it cannot communicate without hurting the listener. Its pack suffers a weaker version of its Ban - they must cause pain, either physical or metaphorical, when they tell a secret. Even so, they're eager to live up to their totem's style; their tongues are wicked barbs, and they foment trouble wherever they go. Whenever they speak, trouble is sure to arise.

            Oooh, more ideas:

            Ravaging Flame
            For decades, this spirit of fiery destruction went a'viking far and wide with a pack of Blood Talons - now, however, it aids them in driving the Christian invaders from their homeland. Its Ban is simple, and one that its pack is only too eager to uphold - each week it must completely destroy something of value by flame or fall to Slumber. Its Bane, however, is more problematic - it cannot abide a human dwelling made completely of stone. It avoids stone buildings altogether; somehow being forced to step onto the floor of one is certain death.

            The-Walker-on-the-Road
            This spirit ceaselessly travels the old Roman Roads of Scandinavia, its two faces partially obscured beneath the hood of a camel-hair monk's robe. Though it never speaks, it will reveal many secrets of the Hisil to its pack. While many others believe the Walker is a spirit of pilgrims and the monastic life, its pack knows it is much older than the Christian holy men that now travel its byways. According to them, it is a spirit of the supernatural itself - the walker-between that treads the road between the gods and flesh. Others might balk at this, if the pack ever spoke of it to outsiders - but they won't. Sworn to permanent silence, the pack's alpha even cut her own tongue from her mouth.

                        The totem I created for a neolithic crossover game I'm in. My Uratha, my wolfblood and an NPC are the contributors. We use the optional Fate system from the Chronicles of Darkness (pg 177) and it’s sort of built with that in mind. The totem was formerly the Wolfblooded familiar of my Uratha, tied to her at birth (perhaps due to her Fate, ‘Martyred’).

                        Last Whisper

                        Last Whisper appears as a large pale-eyed bird whose feathers fall to the floor as he flies, vanishing in tiny puffs of black smoke as they touch the earth. It appears as a large crow or vulture, depending on which of it’s influences (or numina) it has most recently indulged in. If it has indulged in neither influence for more than a week, it appears instead as a hawk as it searches for resonant essence.

                        Rank 2
                        Blessing: Cursed, Striking Looks: Unsettling (or Striking Looks: Touched by Death to those with knowledge of the Supernatural) - Expression, Intimidation
                        Influences: Omens: 1 (Crow), Death: 1 (Vulture)
                        Numina: Omen Trance (Crow), Ghost Eater (Vulture), Seek (Hawk)
                        Power: 3, Finesse 4, Resistance 3
                        Aspiration: To hear a person’s dying words.
                        Ban: Must never interrupt, contradict or interfere with an omen
                        Bane: A strike from anyone who has met their Fate and survived
                        Corpus: 5, Willpower: 6
                        Size: 2, Defense: 3, Speed 17 (Species Factor 10 as per a Raven), Initiative: 7
