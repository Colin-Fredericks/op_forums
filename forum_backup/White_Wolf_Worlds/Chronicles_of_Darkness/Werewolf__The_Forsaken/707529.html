    Seeking a More Mobile Harmony

    10-18-2015, 06:17 PM
    So has anyone else noticed that moving Harmony tends to get really difficult? If you average one success on three dice, then you only tend to occasionally move Harmony unless you go out and just, like, eat your way through a crowd or go live in the Shadow for a while.

    One of the interesting things I noticed was that if you're already away from the middle, it's easier to go to that pole. I think, for some reason, this should have been reversed. If a werewolf is a creature of balance, then being at one end of the spectrum should make it easier to go to the -opposite- end of the spectrum. For instance, a spirit breaking point for someone at Harmony 10 should be a pretty big deal, and the system should make it hard to stay there. But that's not the way it seems to actually work.

    So, has anyone tinkered with Harmony any to try and make it more... well, mobile? If a werewolf's Harmony is supposed to be constantly shifting, then the system should reflect that, I think. As it stands, it tends to favor staying right where you are.

    Who else has seen this? What have you done about it?

                  The possible variant is to make all triggers open to you when you're Harmony 5. But when you move towards Flesh, some triggers toward Spirit close for you. And the more you rise your Harmony toward Flesh, the less triggers toward Spirit can affect you (until the only way for you is to eat wolf or human). The same is true for moving toward Spirit. Then Flesh-triggers cease (until the onlyway for you is to violate the Moon Oath).

                  Also as for dice rolled. The idea is following: When your Harmony is high, it's hard to resist breaking points toward Flesh, and easy toward Spirit. And vice versa. As following:
                  Harmony 9 -- 1 Die to Resist Flesh-Triggers -- 5 Dice to resist Spirit-Triggers
                  Harmony 7-8 -- 2 Dice to Resist Flesh-Triggers -- 4 Dice to resist Spirit-Triggers
                  Harmony 4-6 -- 3 Dice to Resist Flesh-Triggers -- 3 Dice to resist Spirit-Triggers
                  Harmony 2-3 -- 4 Dice to Resist Flesh-Triggers -- 2 Dice to resist Spirit-Triggers
                  Harmony 1 -- 5 Dice to Resist Flesh-Triggers -- 1 Die to resist Spirit-Triggers

                  Then itwill be hard to stick to the middle, but all too easy to fall in either direction.
