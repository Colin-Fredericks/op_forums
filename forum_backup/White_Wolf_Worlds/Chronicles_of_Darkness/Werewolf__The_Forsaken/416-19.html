                            Strange that Tribe is the "What?" while Auspice is the "How?". Tribes being something you chose to join based on ideology and Auspice is something Luna awakened/forced on you to start your Werewolfdom.

                            Hmmm, perhaps Tribes are becoming more of a bloodline thing linking you back to your Ancestor that did the dirty deed. Maybe even turning the Firstborn into the original Uratha that killed Father Wolf, but eventually became full spirits and progenitors of their kind. I noticed fluff appeared to be going that direction in several places and can't say I'm against it. So Tribe becomes "What kind of Werewolf are you?" and Auspice is "How is that expressed in you?".

                            Harmony becoming more about balancing your human life with the wolf spirit tearing across the Shadow could be seen as the ultimate expression of High Harmony. I figure the ultimate expression of low Harmony might be a person that's fallen into being two different being that just so happen to have the same body. The titular Werewolf character in cinema that is a normal guy most of the time and then a bloodthirsty monster with no control over himself the other half of the time.

                            Seeing how Vampires have Status among Covenant, Clan, and City perhaps Werewolves will have status of Tribe, Auspice, and Territory. Reflecting how they affects both the humans and spirits in the lands they claim, and how well-known they are amongst their own kind in reflecting their ideals. Or the clearer course is Uratha, City, and Shadow. How do you balance being part of all 3 groups?

                            No idea, just tossing out what this simple statement is stirring into my head.

                            Sad we won't be getting blog posts(damn Apocalypse gameline), but I can't wait to guesstimate at whatever the writers do share.
