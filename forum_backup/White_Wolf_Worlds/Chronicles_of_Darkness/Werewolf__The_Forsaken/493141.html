    Stupid crazy idea for the day.

    04-26-2015, 10:21 PM
    750 Garou (yes, those guys)are dropped worldwide into the Forsaken's universe. No explanation given. Chronicle starts with the Forsaken going about their daily stuff only to start hearing through the spirit grapevine about weird Uratha screaming about "Gaia" and "Worms" and crap. Quickly followed by a series of obvious werewolf attacks on factories and other industrial plants, all within the Protectorate's territory.

    Or perhaps have it be told from the Garou's side. A pack of soldiers going through their operations only to realize that they are no longer actually fighting their enemy. They are in a strange land where the war that they have been dealing with all their lives in no longer applies. The defining aspect of a character cast away because the enemy that you have been fighting is simply gone. Not vanquished but gone. What kind of existential crisis would erupt from this, and who might exploit it.
