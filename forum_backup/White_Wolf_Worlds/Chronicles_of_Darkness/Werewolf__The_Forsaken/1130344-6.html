Trapdoor of the medium due to lack of gestures, body language, voice pitch and similar signal, as well as this not being my first language, and despite being fairly proficient in it (I like to think so, at least) I still make some very stupid, sometimes funny, sometimes quite serious mistakes. I do not meant to attack anyone, and this is in response to you, but not at you. It is a general statement.

                So, with that out of the way:
                What would us knowing about there not being a developer have changed? Would it have sped up the process of finding someone to take over? I kind of doubt that. I think it wouldn't have done anything positive. There would have probably been less people willing to get into Werewolf, because 'it's been officially abandoned'. Maybe there might have been more threads about the fact that Forsaken is abandoned, about it 'not even having a developer'? Would that have helped? Again, personally, I doubt it.
                I am not denying complaints their space here, or questioning their rightfulness: being left in the dark sucks, especially when it is about something you care about. Especially when it is something you have felt weighing on you for a long time, finally being (sadly) proven right, possibly feeling betrayed. Letting out some steam occasionally is helpful, and if this thread indeed brought the announcement on, great, it served an additional purpose, it provided much needed clarity. But not being able to let go, bringing up the same point again and again, questioning those who, from my point of view, need support now more then ever... Maybe it is just me here, but it feels like losing sight of the big picture. Complaining about the splinter in your finger rather than pull it out. There is no catharsis involved for me here, it is just... dragging down? As good a word as any, I suppose.

                Are there reasons to not make something like this public? From an administrative and marketing standpoint, yes, many.
                Does it suck? Hell yeah it does. Is it okay to address mistakes or things one feels were handled poorly? It is of paramount importance that it is done! But there is a time, and a place for that. And maybe this is my cultural upbringing speaking, but if you address things, address them directly, openly, dont tiptoe around, don't make implications and leave them there. After all, you want to help ensure things work better in the future. At least, that should be your purpose when criticizing. In my opinion ( leave humble out, because it looks fake to me). And once you did that, things should be done. You should not retreat back and repeat the process. Because that is not helpful, nor healthy. For none of the involved.

                All that's left right now is dust off, clean up, and prepare shop for the next day.

                I might be overly optimistic or idealistic here, and if that is so, I apologize.
                And thank you for your understanding.






                P.S.: Also, I hope Stew will still chime in for the line here and there, because I like where he took it.
