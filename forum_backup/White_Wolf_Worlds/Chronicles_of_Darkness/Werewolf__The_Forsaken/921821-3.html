                                                Lupus Dei (Wolf of God) whom demands his followers Sacred Hunt those that do great evil in the name of the God of Jacob and Esau, the one whom begot Jesus Christ (The Judaeo-Christian God whom Islam claims Muhammad was his prophet).

                                                Edit: Also the Creatures of Sin suggestion below is a clear path that would fit the Good vs Evil narrative most people understand about morality. I do like the idea and hit that like button. The reason I don't abandon my suggestion for the Sacred Hunt is that Salvation is a gift of divine grace for all are creatures disposed towards selfishness and evil in a Fallen World. The linked commandment properly translated and put in context (the opposite of semantic game playing with modern definitions done with a scoffing heart) is a theological place where Matthew 5:29, Mark 9:47, with its metaphor of the Church as a body and certain members doing evil or leading others towards evil meets a place where the idea of the Sacred Hunt could find theological footing amid a sea of doctrines of mercy. Evil done in the name of God is a strong force driving up forsaking of the concept of God. Bonus it subverts the Good vs Evil Narrative in exchange for "the failure of authority (moral authority in this case) to police its own is what undermines authority the most" which is trend in western media coverage.

                                                I think both ideas are strong candidates, , maybe a broader category of "Those that war against the Image of God" which would include creatures of sin and those attacking the Image of God by those doing evil in the name of God.
