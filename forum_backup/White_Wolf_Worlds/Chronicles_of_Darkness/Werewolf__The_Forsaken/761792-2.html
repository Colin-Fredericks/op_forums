        Edit: Also non hostile intent, but concerned that's not coming across in text format.

        til;dr - I disagree, look forward to hearing what Stew thinks, and don't want to drag this further into a he said/she said, so won't continue going back and forth on it

        I disagree with your characterisation of my argument (big surprise there), much as I think you're reaching at non-supportive or irrelevant text to support your argument.

        1) Lethal regeneration is natural and an unconscious action. It's always happening at a rate of 1L/15 minutes. Overcharging it by specifically pumping Essence into the regeneration pathway is a conscious choice.

        2) I agree that reflexive actions don't always need to be conscious decisions. I argue that this one does.

        3) Yes, the design decision was to make the werewolf package tougher. That didn't mean leaving everything from 1E and bolting on more. It meant thinking about which parts made the package tougher, and made for a better game. We also removed armour from Gauru and high level Gifts. That didn't make the package weaker, and IMO, neither does this.

        4) We're not talking about sole methods of surviving lethal damage. As stated before, every character has the choice to overdrive regeneration before checking for unconsciousness. If she has already spent all her essence for the turn, or chooses to risk the dice, that's drama. If she keels over unconscious, that's something her pack has to deal with. It's a trivial matter for them to bind her wounds and give her regeneration the 15 minutes it needs, but maybe that's difficult depending on if they're still in the middle of combat. Do they fight on, confident that they'll win before she bleeds out so they can help her, or do they grab her rag-doll form and run? Drama.
        Same with Gauru form. If you're in a scene where you've already taken Gauru and allowed it to lapse, then need it for more combat, that's either your very poor tactical planning, or very good tactical planning from your enemies. Once again, this increases the drama. If the whole pack has done this, you have real problems. Otherwise you have the nuclear option of letting one packmate head into Gauru then Kuruth, and everyone follow her into the carnage.

        If I'm wrong I'm wrong. I think my interpretation is more interesting, but I look forward to hearing what Stew says.

                        It just occurred to me that groups who choose to follow the 'can't spend while unconscious' path could introduce a new merit covering this. Uratha with this merit will overcharge their regeneration whenever they fall unconscious, as long as they have Essence.

                        Drawback... I'd say the unconscious overcharge isn't as fine tuned as conscious control - the character must also spend a point of Essence at the beginning of the next turn to keep her regeneration high. This counts towards how much Essence she can spend in that turn, and may prevent her from taking other options.
