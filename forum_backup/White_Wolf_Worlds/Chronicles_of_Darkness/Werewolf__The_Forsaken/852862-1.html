                                I've wanted an 'organization' system or subsystem for years, I never really thought of it tying into werewolf, outside of cults, until Charlaquin brought it up. So I could see this falling under that but, since that doesn't exist, a pack merit seems like a good way to handle it.

                                Some sort of Pack Renown. The idea of personal renown is one that's been around since, I think, Apocalypse, but there doesn't seem to be as much emphasis on the pack. Obviously the upcoming book is about understanding what it's like to be in the pack, but it seems like there should be something to give your pack a name and a stamp in the world beyond the activities of its individuals, otherwise packs are just a flash in the pan that goes forgotten once the last members have found new ones. Maybe offering a collective bonus in terms of social standing depending on how the pack has aligned itself, or their collective sacred hunts or acts of renown. I'm really not sure how it should be developed mechanically. I keep falling back to the '+# to something' but it would be nice to deviate, especially as it focuses more on the results of the hunt than spurring the pack on to hunt more.

                                A side effect I have thought about is perhaps the renown creates a sort of reflection in the Shadow. An 'advanced' Safe Place in the form of a Place That Isn't. I'm hoping it isn't stepping on the toes of Lair from Beast, I imagine it being more about the victories and triumphs of the pack, spawned from the knowledge of other werewolves and even mortals who have heard of the pack in one form or another but may not be entirely aware of a pack, like if they've set themselves up as a biker gang or business, the place might form in the Hisil in their territory to look like an imagined biker bar or expansive penthouse office.

                                            Pack Status. Like Pack Status: Criminals would be appropriate for a pack of gangbangers.

                                            Also, some kind of pack renown tracker. I suppose that would be an aggregate of the pack's member's renowns, or else simply the highest in each renown among the pack. But I've always wanted some way to represent the influence or reputation of a pack as opposed to the individual members, especially in regards to diplomacy or territory or other pack on pack encounters.

                                            I also once played a game where the GM had pack archetypes that granted bonuses if one had an accepted role in the pack. There was Alpha, Omega, Scout, Diplomat, Soldier, Advisor, etc. They essentially functioned as Pack-based Anchors, I guess. I don't know, maybe that isn't a thing we need.


                                            Mostly what I want, which may or may not be impinging on Pack Tactics, is Pack Fighting Styles.

                                            Sort of like a way the pack fights. Tiered merits that each member can contribute to but everyone benefits from. Maybe only one dot per member, even.

                                            Like a pack that likes to gang their enemies and tear them apart one by one might have a bonus to attacking opponents already in a grapple with one of their members. Or treat a grapple as a Hold, and a Hold as Restrained. That might be too much. Or might gain bonus maneuvers. Or their attacks penalize defense by 2 instead of 1 (is that still a thing in 2e? I have been using it, but I just realized I might not have actually seen it in the books).

                                            Or a pack that fights guerrila warfare style, hit and run, ambush tactics, etc. Might have something that allows them all to attack at once on an ambush. Or a pack that revels in all-out brawls might get a bonus to resolve when outnumbered (equivalent to the difference), or give a penalty to opponent's resolve when they have the numbers.


                                            Edit: I guess I was beaten to the pack renown idea.
