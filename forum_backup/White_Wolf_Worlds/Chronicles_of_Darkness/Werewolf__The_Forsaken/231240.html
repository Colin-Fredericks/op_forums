      Yay, now I can talk a bit about the Pure etc I wrote the Prey section and Idigam rules, although I didn't do the sample idigam write-ups. Writing up the Pure and their favoured prey was fun

      Sadly no space for the Pure Gifts, but I know how I'd do them and have done a rough draft for them (originally in the hope that more word count might magically sprout out of no-where for the core book). I have some ideas for how I'd like to handle the Pure Gifts in future, if Stew lets me keep my claws in them for Pure coverage in a future book, and I enjoyed hinting at some of the ways I'd like to develop their mystical armoury in the Pure write-up.


      - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
