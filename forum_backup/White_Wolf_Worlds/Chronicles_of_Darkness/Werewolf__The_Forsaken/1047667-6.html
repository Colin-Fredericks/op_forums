                        Originally posted by Iry View Post
                        Besides, I personally feel that Werewolf has fewer thematic ties to hunting than Vampire does at this point, and giving everyone Sacred Hunt for free is a small step towards strengthening that theme.
                        Also — and this really can't be stressed enough — a starting character that still has a spiritual Touchstone and hasn't dumped literally all of their Merit dots into Primal Urge takes three months of neglecting the Sacred Hunt before they start suffering the consequences, and the consequences are…

                        Losing a point of Essence per day (which, unlike with vampires, doesn't translate to any extra consequences besides lack of magical fuel until the outermost extremes of the species' feeding restrictions)…

                        Rolling Resolve + Composure with a null modifier to avoid slipping once per day, and…

                        Will begin having a much worse time of things once they (eventually) rise to Harmony 8 (at which point the penalty from higher Harmony rises above the default Touchstone bonus and the spiritual Touchstone detaches), whereupon…

                        They begin to rapidly get less wiggle room for their personal Death Rage trigger (which nevertheless does not reach the extremes of Passive triggers until Harmony 10) to kick off a stretch of gambling Resolve + Composure for an exceptional success (which can be converted to aiming for five turns of lucidity with a one-dot rite that only mandates a single unassisted roll per month) to stop a ten-minute killfrenzy, many of which can be avoided by a Ghost Wolf. Also…

                        They gain an expanded list of breaking points toward Spirit, including several things that Death Rage all but gives them for free with the caveat of needing to find out about them later, some of which also give them Essence.

                        If they still have their physical Touchstone, they can pretty much always jump their way back from Harmony 10, whereupon they only need to get to a locus and succeed once on a roll of three or more dice before they start rolling a counteractive breaking point every day until they either break back down to Harmony 7 and can start rebalancing or (at least implicitly) drop back into the Flesh at Harmony 10, whereupon the pressure to kill and eat people is higher but the primary impetus is still the (theoretically avoidable or at least manageable) personal triggers.

                        It's a lifestyle that's evidently not desirable to a lot of Uratha, but it's clearly playable, particularly given that "increase the bonus your spiritual Touchstone provides" is a fairly cheap Merit, as is "make getting out of soft-Rage easier."
