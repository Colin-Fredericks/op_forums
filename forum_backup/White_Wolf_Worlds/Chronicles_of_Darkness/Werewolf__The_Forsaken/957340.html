    Advice on character build

    08-24-2016, 10:41 PM
    Good evening.

    Recently i got an invitation to join a mix WoD party containing two Geists , one Changeling and 2 Mages. Since almost no one is combat focused i tried to make a "killing machine" but my Gm adviced me for something more "Spirit specialist" like a Bone shadow shaman etc.
    After a careful study on werewolf rites and gifts i came on the conclusion that our "spells" are little flashy magic comparing the Mages spells.... So my question is , is it worthy to make such a character in a mixed party or should i stay in my (almost) no brain killing machine.
    Take note that i will start with 180 exp and the setting is taking place in Gotham city.
    Thanks in advance.

      You can have a little bit of both worlds actually. If you want a "killing machine" but the ST wants you to be "spirit specialist", then I recommend trying either a Rahu Bone Shadow or a Itheaur Blood Talon. You might find something to your liking somewhere between those two combos.

          I would really suggest going Irraka or Rahu Bone Shadow. Even if the party mages aren't spirit specialists, if you go Ithaeur the first few Moon Gifts are going to be quickly outclassed by anyone who takes a dip into Spirit. Moon Gifts tend to be your most powerful ones so it would be better to get ones you would actually use. Full Moon Gifts are very warrior-like, with the first giving you an 8-again on all Brawl and Weaponry rolls for the scene, and the second a permanent extra Health based on your Renown. New Moon's first Gift is great, giving you really powerful ambushing attacks.

          From there the Bone Shadows would give you the 'spirit specialist,' with the Bottle Spirit rite and some large-scale spells like Read the World's Loom. Though with your Experiences you may want to drop a lot in Elemental Gifts, which are really fantastic with high Renown. You can have some mystical might while still having the Rahu or Irraka access to ass-kicking Gifts.
