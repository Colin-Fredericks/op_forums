                  The outright binding, summoning and so forth of spirits was a pretty big pull for me. I know some people don't like the spirit, but I certainly did. I do think they could use some more limitations than dice rolls. Summoning I could see at a penalty if you didn't know the spirit you were targeting, but it makes sense to require more for binding a spirit to a place or object. Obviously creating fetishes was a really cool aspect of it.

                  There were a few rites that really bugged me. One of them was banish human, which you could do to kick someone out of the Shadow, but it only worked on absolutely normal humans, which was the kind of 'so limited it may as well be useless' kind of thing that annoyed me about Werewolf. And as a point to the binding spirit thing, it feels like the writers were trying to be very careful when writing it so as to not make the rituals seem too powerful, resulting in many of them being weak and/or useless. One should be able to bind spirits to more than just places (like people, or objects without necessarily making a fetish).

                  Plus things like rites that offer no mechanical benefit. It know what they mean, and the intent is appreciated, but you only have so much experiences to throw around.

                                                          Using the Storm Lords as an example, there is still a place for the implacable oncoming storm in most stories, and Tribes aren't just their favored prey. Their favored prey sharpens the understanding of them, but it doesn't define them. What still comes first are things like the tribe bans, their renown, and general tribal attitudes.


                                                          So for the Storm Lords, just because the Claimed aren't here doesn't mean the pack doesn't need someone who will never back down, who will always inspire the others to keep standing, and to bring down the powers of the sky upon them. For the Hunters in Darkness, just because the shartha aren't there doesn't mean that a pack doesn't need a sneaky bastard with a mind for purity of purpose and place.

                                                          We've been over this: PREY IS NOT THE END ALL, BE ALL OF THE TRIBES.
