    Is Primal Urge worth getting anymore

    04-08-2015, 11:17 PM
    DaveB said somewhere that Spirit Rank and Primal Urge were not supposed to be D&D-line challenge equalizing stats. I got to thinking after that about what Primal Urge really means now in WtF2E.

    -It lets you store more Essence. Up to Primal Urge 5, that's just one more Essence holding spot per dot of PU, though.
    -It lets you use Essence faster. This is handy for the first couple of tiers, but there's not all that much you'd need to spend Essence on all at once anymore. If you're still at starter Harmony, you might need it to shift reflexively, plus to have the round's healing be lethal damage, plus maybe a Gift or two. Influence powers cost a comparative ton of Essence at one Essence per dot of the power you're using. Even so, I imagine you'd be really hard pressed to need as many as five Essence in a turn ever what with the lack of "spend Essence to add two dice to your strength pool" type mechanics that littered 1E.
    -It makes you better at Clashes of Wills. So does the Renown in question, though PU can go higher than Renowns.
    -It lets you regenerate health faster. But not until PU 4, and then again at PU 6.
    -It lets you sniff out spirits' infuence. But you subtract their Resistence from the roll, so...
    -It makes you generally better at tracking stuff once you hit PU 4.
    -It makes your Lunacy harder to resist, again at PU 4.
    -It makes food harder to digest.
    -It makes your Death Rage last longer. This could be good if you're Raging alongside rival werewolves with lower PU. It will be very bad in almost all other circumstances.
    -It makes you need to hunt more often lest you start to bleed Essence and Break Towards Flesh.

    I feel like there's probably a lot of stuff I forgot (oh! like general resistance to supernatural powers). I guess there are a lot of small benefits to Primal Urge. It felt like there were far more instances of "Skill/Attribute + Primal Urge" in the first edition games and far less now.

    Does anyone else have thoughts on this? Benefits or drawbacks I missed? Reasonable analyses of why, between all of those things, Primal Urge is or is not a good investment over Skills or Renown or Gift Facets or Merits?

      It also adds to Toxin resistance, resisting mind control during Death Rage, a few of the senses get bonuses. Hearing is spectacular in the country, 'When accessing her wolf senses, an Uratha can hear from a distance of one mile (or two kilometers) per dot of Primal Urge.' Your Hishu and Dalu form abilities are enhanced by it, and there's a minor hack offered on page 286. That was more to just add to your list.

      So far I think there's two things I'd change if I could hijack the book on the way to wherever. Skin Thief's human-skin ability is too limited, and Primal Urge ramps up really late. I'd have 4B regeneration at 7 and 8, moving all the healing up one so you had your first 2B at 3 Primal Urge, and move the Basu-Im, Lunacy Penalty and Tracking Bonus down with it. It seems like a really weird change from 1 to 2 Primal Urge where the only real difference is in essence and diet. Though I guess it now means you can regenerate and use gifts, so with mid range Primal Urge you won't be tempted to go into Gauru for a quick full heal. I'm not much of a mechanics guy, obviously you can buy into a new gift or get two new facets or a renown and there's probably some sort of cost efficiency effectiveness concept in the RPG community I'm unaware of but I think that won't always make sense for the character. Which might be a cop out but even in my game I can see a lot of gifts that I'd really like, but wouldn't really fit the character yet.

      Otherwise, you seem to be right, it doesn't look like there's gifts and stuff that work off of Primal Urge but the tracking, Lunacy, senses and other things are good changes. Someone was theorizing on another board the other day about how Pure would be different, and one thing they suggested was that they might have Wolf Gifts that have prerequisites, like Primal Urge for Predator Kings or Low Harmony for Fire-Touched. Forsaken could get them, of course, but they would be considered more standard for the Pure. I don't know what they're going to do with Primal Urge but other than my previous paragraph I don't really have a complaint about it. It doesn't affect every power you have but Renown does and so now Renown is important. I imagine Primal Urge will get more depth and things to take advantage of if we get more books, but it looks like it's more about the core of your werewolf, no gifts or rites or tribal concerns, just the base hunter.

In the case of playing Werewolf, though, my group only meets once a month. That means that 1 Experience = 1 month of real life time in my world. Thus, it is a fairly precious comodity and the ability to put it to good use is imoprtant to me. Given the long gaps between games, there may well be times when we skip ahead and say "everyone did some stuff and got 10xp for it!"                                                       That hack does make it tempting, though...

                                                      Oh! one other main point on the cost/benefit side of things: Renown is pretty much super-powered nowadays. And so long as your Storyteller is cooperative enough to put you in situations where you could do something awesome enough to warrant the new dot, it's easier to come (cheaper) than Primal Urge.
