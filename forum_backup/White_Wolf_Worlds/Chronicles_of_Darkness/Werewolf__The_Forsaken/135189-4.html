                                                    Well, I think there are always some basic Kuruth risks that all Uratha share. It just makes sense they would, so silver and agg sound pretty good.

                                                    I don't feel this Kuruth change is all that bad. But one thing that sticks out to me is the mass Kuruth. Now, I like the idea, but it will be a game killer in online games (games with 30+ players and status quo of some sort). One might say that the game isn't meant to be played that way, but a lot of great WoD memories have come from these online games for a reason. Anyway, having a 30 person brawl with wolf-blooded in the middle may be a fun idea, but it hurts the game severly. I think there should be a way for the others to go into soft-rage before hard-rage, like in normal Kuruth. That way, these larger game killer brawls could be avoided.

                                                    And to note a previous point. It doesn't really feel as satifying to end a siege against Pure in a massive death rage brawl. In those situations, strategy is much more in the forefront.
