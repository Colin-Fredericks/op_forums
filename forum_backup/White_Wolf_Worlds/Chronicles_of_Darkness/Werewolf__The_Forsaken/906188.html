    [Whimsy] Soul Claimed

    06-03-2016, 12:22 AM
    You know what's interesting? An Idigam with Soul Slave and Forge Claimed. Yes, I know that Soul Slaves aren't Spirits - but let's play pretend for a moment.

    Tear someone's soul out, and make it claim their now Soulless body. You now just gave a random mortal a 15 point Essence Pool, Dread Powers, the ability to Reach, and rapid mutation to look like a mirror of their inner selves. Oh, and they're still Soulless. Because you're either a dick or a magical girl villain.

    Or Soul Slave and Forge Flesh-Bound Spirit - now that would be interesting. It'd certainly be a lot tougher than the Rank 2 it normally has - it is immune to all wards and protections vs. spirits and ghosts, and it lacks a Ban or Bane...

    I have decided, after some thought, that I don't really feel happy on these forums. I might decide to come back to post. Who knows - but right now, I'm gone.

                                                          1st- a random soul claimed npc ISN'T likely to be the main villain, rather the idigam would be. Second, you can have your consistency, but mulling over full splat sheets for a random that a pc may just as easily decide to eat because 'lawl im monster'. As for 3rd, that's on you, but the story telling rules, as presented for forsaken rely on some level of improv, which is largely refreshing compared to some of the cut and dry storylines people tend to use.

                                                          As an example of over thinking things, the previous posts are "how would we decide what form they'd take? what would it be based on?" Why would you not design the form first, and then form the back story as a second? For inspiration, one could watch season 2 of penny dreadful for dorian grey and how his painting appears. The big thought process should go into the idigam, but with the cannon fodder? work backwards. Even if you don't get that far it's likely the players aren't going to appreciate the amount of effort one put into 'random buy with barbed teeth', and it frees you up to devote more time to world building.
