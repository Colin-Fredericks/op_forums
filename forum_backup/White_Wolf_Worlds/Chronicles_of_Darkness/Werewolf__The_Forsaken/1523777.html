    Two Homebrew rites

    10-07-2023, 03:01 PM
    Me and my buddies were playing WtF in WW1 for 2 years, the game ended and we moved over to Mage and PF2E, but I homebrew two different rites over the course of this game and wanted to share them here.

    ​​​​​​
    this one was actually my very first homebrewing of anything ever
    Forced Claiming (Pack Rite •••)


    This rite forces a spirit to claim a living creature, creating an abomination even by claimed standards. While the two entities are trapped in one body together, their minds and desires, even their wills to an extent, are separate. However it is still a claimed, just one where both parties have been forced into this interaction.

    Symbols: Binding, Blood, Opening, The Spirit’s Influence(s)
    Sample Rite: Fire-Touched rite masters know the symbolism of Hisil and use their occult knowledge in this rite. By tying the soon to be host creature and carving the symbolism of the spirit they wish to claim the victim into the flesh of the victim they are able to force a spirit to take hold in the flesh. (Intelligence + Occult)
    Action: Extended (15 successes; each roll represent 5 minutes)
    Success: The rite binds a spirit of rank 3 or lower to a living creature, creating a Claimed, this is done without the need for the Controlled Condition and is not resisted by the subject, however the spirit must have the Claim Manifestation. If it does not, the rite fails instead of succeeds and the subject dies, the body no longer being usable for this rite, melting away into a pool of blood, bone, and filth. Because the claiming is forced, the resulting claimed does not tend to act as a homogeneous entity, instead, speaking and acting as if it were two separate entities (assuming the subject was capable of speech). While the claimed still moves towards a shared goal, it takes longer to achieve and is more messy than claimed formed through the appropriate manifestations and conditions. After one lunar month, if the claimed is still alive, these differences fade and the entity operates as a claimed normally. If the spirit and the subject are on different sides of the gauntlet, then the gauntlet strength acts as a penalty towards the roll.


    Samūm’s Blessing (Pack Rite ••)


    This rite can be taught to any wolf-blooded or Uratha by any fire spirit or desert spirit, and most packs in the Middle East have at least one member who can perform this rite. The rite makes participants immune to the effects of high temperature, allowing the hunt to continue even in the hottest places on Earth. Some wolf-blooded blacksmiths also learn this rite to make working with the forge easier.

    Symbols: Desert, Heat, Pain, The Sun
    Sample Rite: A wolf-blooded goes to the outskirts of town, into the desert and undresses, allowing the sun to scorch his skin. Once he feels the sign of heat stroke coming on, he ritualistically cuts into his thigh, allowing the blood to be absorbed by the sand. He does this again and again over the course of thirty minutes before the heat from the sun seems to become tolerable again and the rite is completed. (Stamina + Survival)
    Action: Extended (15 successes; each roll represents 10 minutes)
    Success: Anyone participating in the rite becomes immune to the effects of the Extreme Heat tilt (Chronicles of Darkness 282). This does not extend to magically created environments of extreme heat like in Places That Aren’t created by fire spirits. It also does not shield the participants from being dealt damage by fire or heat attacks, they are only protected from the effects of naturally occurring temperature and climate. They can hunt along the edge of a volcano without feeling the effects of the heat, but if they fall into the lava they will still be burned.
    The effects of the rite lasts for one lunar month.
