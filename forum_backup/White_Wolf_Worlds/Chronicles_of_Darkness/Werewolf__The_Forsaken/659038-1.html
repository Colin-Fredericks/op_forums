Spirits, as ephemeral beings, are creatures of pattern.

Another joy spirit may eat them, the Essence may run out, their Essence may run out, dormancy beckons, making them even easier prey for more powerful and hungrier spirits.             Of course they might decide to stay stationary, and some jump across and ride or claim people, but there are also spirits that travel. The Mockingbird and, arguably, the Colossus from Wolfsbane. The Dreameater, Rafixul, Spearfinger. All seek out new sources of Essence, whether manufactured or taken advantage of.
