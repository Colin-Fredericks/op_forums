                                            After some thinking and looking back over Werewolf, I've started thinking more and more about the Luna / Helios separation. They're opposites - Helios suppresses the Hisil, and sends the essence flowing out of the Hisil into the Material, while Luna reverses it, bringing the Shadow to life, reviving spirits, etc. I actually don't see any mention of Gaia the earth as a spirit at all in 2e. Even the rankings for spirits tops out with Celestial, it seems, from reading the book.

                                            And, I have a little pet theory, from Requiem. The CotC revere the Dark Mother, who is very likely the same as the Beast's Mother. The L+S are all about the angels, (And thus, God-Machine!). VII has rumors of angels, and the Ordo spit in God's face. We have a large Dark Mother v. God duality here in vampire.

                                            Changeling preview of Tokyo from the Beast have hinted that the freehold are calling themselves Moon Princesses. Or something like.

                                            So, I've started a new World of Darkness cosmological theorycraft! On the one hand, champion humans and humanity, we have the God-Machine, the Sun, the Light! On the other, we have the Dark Mother, the Moon Maiden, Luna! Its a nice duality that flows well between the four games we have out for 2e so far. I'm going to run with it!
