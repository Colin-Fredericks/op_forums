                            Technically the only known Rank 8 spirits are Luna and Helios, and there are no known spirits of Rank 9 or higher. The existence of Gaia at Rank 9 is conjecture.

                            This could open up room for hidden Rank 8 continent spirits, if the ST wants, but that depends on how his cosmology works. I use an Earth-centric (and human-centric) cosmology, where Gaia's choir does not consist of the spirits of continents, mountains and oceans, but of Luna, Helios and the other planets. The spirits of continents, mountains and oceans at Rank 6 and races of animals at Rank 7 (and of course the even lower earthen spirits) are part of Gaia, just as the lunes are part of Luna.

                                        As I return to All-Father-Thunder’s Court idea for our game, I will need a bit help in fleshing couple more spirits NPCs.

                                        But first, let’s recall what’s being set on idea to this day:

                                        Originally posted by Papa Bear View Post
                                        I think All-Father-Thunder would have to be pushed up to a Rank 7. He’s going to be the spirit created by all those feelings and thoughts and actions based on an all-powerful, father- like figure from the sky. That means he’s going to have aspects of Odin, Zeus, Ra, YHVH, etc, etc, etc. This figure is all over the place in Europe, which makes it insanely powerful. Major Influences would be things like lightning and thunder, clouds and wind, wisdom and justice, authority and law. This is also the spirit of all those high kings and divinely allowed overlords of the universe. I think it would appear as either a lion or a bull, and it’s kind of hard to tell sometimes because he’s surrounded with rumbling thunder and dark clouds, especially when angry….which is often the reason he appears. When he’s coming in a gentler aspect, there would be a light shining thru the clouds as if from above. He can be the aspect of authority as a punisher and as a protector, but he’s rarely subtle or light-trodding.

                                        The Crash Before The Storm is one of his harbingers. A little storm crow made up of that buzzing in the air before a storm. The wind that brings the smell of ozone to let you know what’s coming. Probably no more than a Greater Gaffling (Rank 2), might make it to Lesser Jaggling while attached to a pack. Influences would be wind and sounds (low thunder in the background), a little bit of premonition based on fore-shadowing and warning signs, speed and flight (since it always has to stay ahead of the storm)….no more than 2 dots in each. Numina – Blast, Chorus, Reaching (for little blasts of air), Wilds Sense (so it knows where All-Father-Thunder is at all times). Higher Finesse and Resistance than Power. This little guy isn’t about power, it’s about warning people about the in-coming power!
                                        So here are my questions to this idea:

                                        1. As All-Father-Thunder is Rank 7 being, he would be outside of regional influence. But local court would be ruled by his Royal Avatar, yes? If so, does Avatar knows about everything what All-Father-Thunder knows, or only his regional domain? What kind of Ban and Bane would be suitable for entity like this?

                                        2. Odin is often depicted in company of two wolves - Geri and Freki - so I assume some alliance with Firstborns is in order? With whom of ancient Wolf Gods? Would powerful spirit wolf be part of Court, like Rank 4 or maybe 5? Maybe even this wolf spirit is from linage of one of Firstborns? Because I don’t think so that Firstborn himself would be working for All-Father-Thunder, even if he is Rank 7 entity.

                                        3. Odin also is surrounded by Valkyries in myths. But are they something that could have spirit analogy in Shadow? If yes, what kind would those spirit be? Death and war humanoids?

                                        4. Any more spirits could be populate this court? Probably ravens, lesser wolves, but also wisdom and war spirits? Most probably wisdom spirits would be ravens and wars ones would be wolves? How many spirits should be in this kind of local court? Any other kinds could also?

                                        5. Last question - were would local court like this gather in Shadow Realm? In some suitable Locus? I was thinking of ancient tree in middle of forest were in time immemorial people would make sacrifices to All-Father-Thunder thinking he is Odin by strangling humans on the branches of tree.

                                            1. Not an avatar so much as a servant. As the lunes are to Luna, not part of her, but things that come from her. It wouldn't know everything All-Father-Thunder knew but it would probably get audience with the higher power and feel orders from it. The spirit wouldn't know everything about anything, though with its servant spies it would probably know a lot of its region, particularly the aspects of the spirits that serve it.

                                            2. Not all wolves are on the Firstborn's side, and if they are it's not necessarily the Forsaken's side of them. Spirits like these would likely be at the sides of your All-Father-Thunder, and occasionally sent to dig into something or assist AFT's servants (like this choir) in their defense, since AFT wouldn't be likely to come themself. There's descendant spirits of the firstborn who are totems for lodges, it wouldn't be hard to consider them that 'level' of power.

                                            3. No matter how anthropomorphic, a spirit will never be human. The human form is just something they wear because it's appropriate for them to do so. All Father Thunderer wouldn't be Odin from the Thor movies, giving words of wisdom, he'd be a force of nature writ large across the skies, sending his message with great booms of thunder and strikes of lightning, and most wouldn't understand the communique.
                                            That out of the way, I'll point you to the Valkyria mot. It's sort of a lodge, and it has valkyries as patron spirits. To quote:
                                            'Several ancestor-spirits serve as this group's totems, usually given the customary names of Brynhildr, Freja, Goll, Gondul, Gudr, Gunn, Herfjoturr, Hildr, Hladgunnr, Hlokk, Hrist, Sigrdrifa, Sigrun and Svafa' (I left out the accents). 'These spirits are often cold and sometimes demand great sacrifices of their daughters' (the mot is female only).

                                            4. Yeah, ravens symbolizing wisdom, wolves symbolizing war is fine, you'll want some weather and elemental spirits old and young. And since the Shadow isn't Disney World, some apathy spirits who get a rush whenever people pass by the holy grounds and dismiss them as a bunch of trees or rocks or whatever.
                                            There'd be a lot of spirits, but it's not like a city where you'd go and see everyone (the river spirit doesn't likely leave the river, but it might send rank 1 spirits in its stead if the choir was doing something important).

                                            5. They tend to be around loci, so they can stay fat and fed. If you went with the tree, you'll want to make it a more dark and sinister-feeling place, with death and sacrifice spirits and maybe even a ghost or two stuck in Twilight, anchored to the tree.
                                            You may want it to seem bigger than it is, everythng echoing the legends of the past where crowds would gather and watch. You'd probably have some things there that don't exactly mesh in the real world (fallen trees looking perfectly healthy in the Shadow, scary bodies hanging from ropes).

                                                Regarding Odin, he has aspects that are a big part of his presentation, and to make him true to the myths they should all be represented. First off, his missing eye. That one is obvious. He lacks one of his eyes, and that follows him no matter what shape he takes. Secondly, his spear. Gungnir, the magical spear that cannot be stopped by anything short of a command from Odin himself. Third, his servants. Freki and Geri are givens, so is Sleipnir, his horse. Most important though are Huginn and Muninn, who are literally parts of him. They are his thoughts and his memories given shape. All of these need to be present to give shape to the god if one is to be close to his original character.

                                                Valkyries are most certainly death-spirits. Basically humanoid. They could fly, and this was either via giant wolves with raven wings, or they had those wings themselves. In fact, there are anthropologists who argue that the modern view of angels came from Valkyries, only, you know, with dyed wings.

                                                The kinds of spirits that populate his court? War, Wisdom, Lies, Strength, and first and foremost, Death.

                                                As to where he would hold court? Well, I'd say a Place That Isn't. One of those places in the shadow without a representation in the Material, a realm created by the All-Father himself, that might connect to Loci of proper essence, where death-spirits in the guise of emaciated wolves stalk and make sure the essence isn't corrupted. If I were to make this I'd have it be a giant hall, with living trees as walls and pillars, and high overhead would be the shadowy shapes of hanged sacrifices.

                                                A bonus: From Odin's throne one could see EVERYTHING, in all the nine worlds, one just had to know what to look for to see it. Could be a fun thing to toss into a campaign, yes?
