    I would point that Norse culture was a large shock to the early Medival Christandom, it was so alien to them.     I can see Orders slowly conquering any land they are neighbor - but they need to have whatever long contact for that to work. HOWEVER protoNorse literally cut off their contacts with the old continent on migration, passing what would be described as Small Ice Age on the North. So I do not see any Awakened contacts between Orders ( that did not converted Germans in 1st century AD ) and local Great Cults of Norse mages.

                And, um, Wyrd. Please be aware that Scandinavia had and continued to have full contact with the rest of the continent and beyond from well before the Roman empire to long afterward. Not directly, in many cases, but passing through the "Germanic" peoples who were, themselves, descended from Scandinavians who moved south when the climate changed.

                They've dug out of [Swedish?] bogs "Roman" equipment with norse names and runes on it that were used in the 2nd century--and the winners tossed them into the bog as an offering. You had mercenaries flowing out of the north, goods flowing back, and the common people couldn't care less if that "Germanic" foederati spoke Frankish or Geatish.

                The Continent was much more unified in trade and flow of ideas than we have imagined in a progressive view of history, and the Dark Ages were dark only because folks weren't writing as much about them that got preserved. Not because they were huddled in shrinking globes of light.

                I'll let others argue how the mechanics of Mage work, except to say that I'm pretty sure things just get harder if you don't have sympathy and tools available--not impossible.

                Now, as to mages forming a group around Hel as a figure? We can assume that "Hel" the bale hound could be either a progenitor for the legends, or may have assumed the identity of Hel for her own purposes (e.g. scaring people stiff). Easy.
