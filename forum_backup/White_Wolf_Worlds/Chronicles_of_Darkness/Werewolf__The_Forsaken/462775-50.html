            Spirit, Ghost and Angel essence is slightly different to each other. Sufficiently so that a numen is required to take it from beings of the wrong type. On the other hand, it's sufficiently similar that ''only'' a numen is needed. They're closer to each other than they are to Vitae or Mana.

            As for how they increase in rank, well since it's generally mentioned to occur when gorging on essence, maybe you could say that if a spirit manages to fill its essence pool then any extra it builds up past that can be traded for attribute dots at some rate of exchange, and if it amasses enough of those that it exceeds the maximum dots for it's current rank it gets promoted to a weak example of the rank above, at which point it begins the process all over again.

                    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                    When and how do spirits increase Rank? I know it involves draining compatible spirits of their Essence and spreading their Influences over our other side of the Gauntlet, but beyond that, it seems kinda nebulous.
                    There's bits of spirit-resonant mechanics spread throughout the gamelines that you can draw inspiration from (Vampire, Mage, and Beast come to mind most directly), but generally speaking Rank Equals Significance. If a spirit has established itself as powerful, old, dangerous, well-connected, or otherwise exemplary on the strong points of its own contextual framework within the spiritual ecology, it's going to be high-Rank. If it can afford to only act directly every once in a while and has numerous and/or powerful subordinates who do most of its day-to-day heavy-lifting, it's probably higher-Rank than a similar spirit that's spread itself thin doing everything on its own.

                                                            Here you go.

                                                            Efficient Killer
                                                            Prerequisites: Purity ••, Brawl •••, Medicine ••, Strength •••, may only be used in Gauru form
                                                            Effect: Your character is a master of immediate, merciful killing. She knows exactly where to clamp down to end a life in one swift blow. When in Gauru form, any time an opponent is completely denied her Defense — for example, if she’s sacrificed it for an all-out attack, during a successful ambush, or when activating certain Merits — you can use Efficient Killer. Sacrifice your Defense for the turn, and you can deal a Killing Blow (see p. 168).
                                                            Drawback: This Merit only works on living targets with discernible weak points. Additionally, this is a common kuruth trigger.

                                                            Killing Blow
                                                            When performing a killing blow, you deal damage equal to your full dice pool plus your weapon modifier. You’ve time enough to line up your attack so it avoids your victim’s armor.
                                                            While people who kill in combat can justify their actions based on the heat of the moment, performing a killing blow is a premeditated attempt to end a human life without the target having a chance to do anything about it. Going through with a killing blow is a breaking point for humans whether the victim survives or not.
