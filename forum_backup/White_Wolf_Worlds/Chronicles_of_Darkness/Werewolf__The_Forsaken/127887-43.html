                            Here we are!

                            First, we have gitsur: "Anthropocentric wolf, anthropocentrism; the werewolf condition in which one is uncomfortable being a wolf, and prefers to work in their human forms". Then we have sa'ath, which a name for Banish the Beast Derangement from Changing Breeds, in which a shapeshifter represses their bestial nature so thoroughly, it is very difficult to shapeshift into it. Then there's the disturbing condition known as umamutag-sahaz, or the The Hunter King Derangement, in which a shapeshifter hates their animal side so much, it manifests as the serial killing and/or abuse of animals. The milder form of this is zatharzemmul, or the the Filthy Brutes! Derangement, in a shapechanger abuses animals as a way of lashing out at themselves or their shapeshifter side. Then there is Gurihal nirih, which means "one who hates, fears or avoids the Shadow; Shadow aversion".

                            Do any of these sound like your guy? If not, I'll work on something. I just realized I haven't finished translating all the Derangements yet in WoD. >.>;

                                                    This also includes a number system. As number systems spirits war for control across the world, the spirits that embody each number fight for their independence, and spirits of concepts like halves and thirds seem near-insane sometimes. The first 29 numbers have managed to hold onto their independence, but anything after 30 falls under the base ten number's influence...in some areas. Sometimes, it's in name only. As for why there are only sixty numbers translated, that's because the concept of sixty numbers (plus the numbers for 100 and 1000) are the only ones that survived an all-out spiritual war, having subdued the existence of other numbers to make them fall under their purview. In order to say anything past 60, you have to phrase it as a math problem — for example, "60 plus 10" instead of saying "70".

                                                    The exceptions to this are the numbers above 1000. The 1000+ spirits are capricious and powerful, known for tricks such as breaking themselves into tiny pieces to escape, reforming elsewhere as a series of swirling motes. Others flood their attackers with hundreds of mathematical equations at a time, shorting out their brains with strings of complex algebra. They refused to be named, as a name gives power and ties them down, and must be summoned or called for with a (poetic) math equation. Asking for a spirit of the number 2000 would be in kenning form, with lines such as, "I beseech the help of the Glorious Thousand-Times-Twice, That Which Is Greater In Double than Thousand, the Hundreds-Mote Lord of Two-Oh-Oh-Oh."

                                                    Then you get to the spirits that represent millions and more. As the brains of Earthly creatures can only conceptualize up to a certain amount of something, spirits that reach a million or more become increasingly bizarre and alien. The big numbers reach near-Celestine levels of power, and as numbers get bigger, it gets harder and harder to tell if some numbers are Celestines. The bigger numbers are also rarer in number of brood, as they absorb any number spirits nearby to retain their immense, numerical power. Asking for their help isn't wise, but if necessary, they will demand hefty sacrifices and the loss of much. They're the bigwigs, after all.
