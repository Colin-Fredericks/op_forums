    Originally posted by Volke View Post
    I'm running a crossover game, and potentially thought in the future that the PCs could have a chapter physically crossing into The Gauntlet as a pass to enter The Shadow. Is this realistic and does this make sense in terms of how the Gauntlet works? Correct me if this interpretation is totally off/ wouldn't make sense in the usage of the Gauntlet. I also know of Loci, but I thought if they couldn't find one this may work. Thanks in advance - Look forward to your opinion
    Technically it's more of a void. It only takes seconds, and there's generally nothing there but sometimes there's exceptions.

    'The seconds of travel in-between are spent hanging in a gray, mist-like void, feeling a claustrophobic pressure all around. Travelers are safe from attack while crossing, and can't move until they emerge on the other side.
    Some Uratha tell tales about encountering bizarre entities inside the Gauntlet - ghostly "spirits," unnatural corpses of no known creature, splashes of blood, and other signs that the barrier is somehow inhabited, always just on the edge of the paralyzed traveler's senses.'

    So putting some vestiges of things or some 'area' inside is fine, but it wouldn't be the normal way to go about things, and likely represent something strange happening to the Gauntlet at that location. Though obviously you can just change the game so the Gauntlet is its own place, in which case you might make it a sort of 'dead' Pangaea.

                                    Personally I'd just convert Primal Urge dots bought into dots of Gnosis if the Wolf-Blooded character with the Primal Instincts Tell ever Awakened. I'd be predisposed to making them a Thyrsus, though I suppose other Paths could potentially fit, but the Primal Urge, as a concept, would suggest to me Gnosis that fits the Thyrsus perspective of the world. Not sure I would do if the character wasn't going to be a Thyrsus though. Probably still have Primal Urge convert to Gnosis. Not very fun if the player sinks all that XP into their Supernatural Resistance Trait only for it to be rendered useless if they become anything other than a Werewolf.

                                    EDIT: To continue on the insanity, I'm pretty sure Unchained Offspring retain access to their Embeds, their Cipher, and their Stigmata if they convert to another being, so you could theoretically have a Wolf-Blooded Offspring without anything rules-breaking going on. I'd argue it would be in bad taste except in fairly specific games, but I can't think of anything it would conflict with.

                                                    Originally posted by Cainite View Post
                                                    Is there a way to drain all their Essence away to prevent them using gifts?
                                                    There are Gifts that would allow this, and Numen. Idigam and strange Tilts in the Shadow, too. I think most splats have an 'energy drain' of some sort.

                                                    Also, what happens to a werewolf that has been bound tightly, let's say with ropes, chains or manacles, and they change into Gauru or wolf form? How strong is the shapeshift when it comes to breaking bonds? I feel most any restraints that are used on a mortal would be useless against most werewolves, if not all of them.
                                                    A werewolf could shift down, granting them much smaller size and allowing them to escape. Shifting up would probably cause some damage. There's a Gift to break free from any restraint.

                                                    Basically what I'm getting at is, I'm trying to keep a werewolf prisoner. When it comes to the Pure, I would assume they might have access to a Fetish or Spirits, or something that drains a werewolf, but what if the captors are a bunch of humans or maybe vampires?
                                                    The Pure would use totems, or bring them into the Shadow or someplace where there's no locus. Humans wouldn't have much luck with a werewolf of any real power, as they are basically walking Essence batteries. But weaker werewolves without many Gifts and with moral problems might just end up stuck. Werewolves can also be forced into a blood bond, though as noted above if you keep a werewolf from shapeshifting, eventually their Harmony is going to shift and they're going to start going into Death Rages at the drop of a hat, and a blood bond might not protect from that. Might take a long time, though.

                                                    Trapping them in a bunker would hold them, but I want a way to have someone torture and interrogate them while in the same room, staring them in the face. Spitting distance. More dramatic that way.
                                                    Find a weak or new werewolf. They might not have the abilities to get themselves out, their pack might not be able to either, or be able to get help, or they might be new enough they don't have one. Of course in that case they might not be able to answer anything.
