        You can read more about it in my other thread about the same situation: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...-of-werewolves

        I don't want this thread to become derailed.

        Speaking of which, since I've been thinking about it:

        The presumably-reconciliatory Pure should start by asking a Lune (I'd say only the Lune of the auspice that they once were) where to start. In my case, it's a former irraka who needs to do the atoning, so he'll need to find an irralunim. My idea thus far is that he'll have to perform a deed in each category of Renown and publicly dedicate them to Luna.

        From then, he'll need to perform various actions proving his willingness and desire to re-auspice:

        1) Renounce his former affiliation publicly (to his old pack, or possibly in a prayer to Rabid Wolf if the pack isn't available). He must show that he's willing to risk their ire.
        2) Sacrifice something important to him (he renounced his inheritance as a child of the Moon Father: thus he must make an offering of appeasement)
        3) Perform an act showing that he understands the inherited duty of Wolf and the Oath of the Moon by leading a Sacred Hunt against an appropriate prey.

        After that point, if he's still on the right path, he'll be rebranded in a ritual involving bottled moonlight during his auspice moon (hence the bottling: irraka are of the New Moon). Probably in the shadow and done by an irralunim.

                        From what I recall off the top of my head, I think they tend to carve off/burn the victim's spiritual brands as part of the attempt to scourge the auspice off them.

                        Of course, the rumours say that this is just to dislodge the actual essence of the Auspice itself from its physical moorings and that, were you able to see an Auspice just by itself, before it rooted itself in flesh, it'd be a sparkling shard of moonlight, almost like a small Lune.

                        Then again, I know this pack that said they found their way into a strange and rather cosmopolitan market in a place-that-is-not, crowded with weird creatures and spirits selling all sorts of impossible things. One of the creatures was selling what it claimed were auspices, little silver talismans of the moon that, according to the merchant, could replace an existing auspice with an entirely new one (albeit in an agonising, flesh-shuddering experience as the new auspice tore out the old one's roots and burrowed in). 'Course, none of the Uratha took that deal so they don't know for sure if it was legit or not...
