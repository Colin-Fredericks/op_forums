                                  Yeah, if you look at the actual mechanics, very high Harmony turns you into David from An American Werewolf in London: someone desperately trying to deny what they are, but when the conditions are right, they turn into a violent monster that goes on a rampage and kills lots of people until he finally calms down and reverts to his human form. It doesn't end well because it doesn't take supernatural senses for other humans to figure out what's happening.

                                  Harmony 5 is where the Uratha are under the most control of their multilayered natures. They are balanced between flesh and spirit, wolf and human. This is the rating where the Uratha have the most control over their various instincts, and even if they do lose control have the most time to remove themselves from a situation before the worst case occurs. That said, being out of balance does have tempting perks as well.

                                  On top of this... the Uratha literally get a bonus to blending in to human society just by taking their human form. They are natural shapeshifters and their human form is a natural state for them. Acting normally human is part of being in a human shape for them (the same thing applies for them as wolves though there's a bit less of "wolf society" to deal with and more making sure humans and supernaturals think you're just a wolf).

                                                          A somewhat more realistic scenario for calling on a roll would be when more subtle aspects of an Uratha's nature might get noticed by someone actively watching them on a regular basis. Their innate regeneration, their increasing need to hunt and eat, popping on wolf senses even in Hishu for the perception bonus, etc. These are not usually difficult to explain away (and Hishu's Wolf In Sheep's Clothing ability makes it nearly impossible for casual passerby notice), but they can lead to an Uratha acting eccentric enough for a long term acquaintance to start questioning what's up with some of these eccentric behaviors (esp. as most of them get more noticeable over time as Primal Urge increases or Harmony sways too far in either direction).

                                                          There's good reasons why Uratha that want to maintain a "normal" human style of living tend to fold jobs into the pack. This enables the core Uratha to have seemingly normal lives, with Wolf-blooded and trusted humans ensuring that there's a layer of distance between the werewolves and more skeptical humans that might ask too many questions about such quirky antics.
