    Shopping Mall Totem

    11-07-2022, 08:44 PM
    Greetings,

    I would like some aid on building a totem.


    This is the spirit of the oldest and one of the largest malls (about 99 000 m² or 1 065 628 ft² in size, and 25% additional for parking lots) in the city.

    The mall is inside the city not the outskirts, the adjacent high income neighborhoods grew around the mall as part of the effect of its construction.

    The place has 4 small casinos insides, a large toy stores, 3 food quarters, 2 pharmacies, a couple of book stores, a large hypermarket, a hotel, an internal park, a small vet office, an office building, 12 bank branches, plus an assortment of high end clothing, beauty and accessories stores (not the most expensive ones but high end), a cinema and minor amenities. The mall does not have everything that the people of the area need (there are other businesses outside of it, mostly specialist stores or restaurants), but it tries to have it all.

    It is not open 24x7 but close to it.

    This is a generational pack, that has been working with this spirit for a while. Iron Masters all of them. They either work at the mall administration or in one of its stores, and I envision them living in the surrounding neighborhoods.

    The pack is amenable to having other creatures, but it would be mainly mortals with minor templates or perhaps mages, everything is too hidden or will predate upon the mall clients.

    The pack claims the mall itself as territory but also the adjacent neighborhoods (not sure how big to make the area, the focus of the pack is the mall itself but it is a good policy to protect the annex area and of course, that is where they live).



    I am looking for aid in the stats of the Totem (about 21 points):
    1. Name. The name of the mall itself.
    2. Rank. A standard Rank 3 creature.
    3. Appereance. It should look as the mortal building itself which would give it an absurd size (and therefore health) but would have to have Speed 0.
    4. Attributes. I was thinking Resistance 9 and Power / Finesse 6 each.
    5. Influences. Banks 1, Cars 1, Cinemas 1, Commerce 1, Restaurants 1, Stores 1.
    6. Manifestations. Twilight Form, Discorporate, Reaching, Shadow Gateway
    7. Numina. It should have exchanged some Numina for a bit more of influence. Emotional Aura, Implant Mission, Rapture are my current candidates for it to keep.
    8. Ban. Must be in the presence of someone shopping every 24 hours, otherwise it becomes morose and lethargic losing it’s defense.
    9. Bane. A human that has been voluntarily poor for a year, like a mendicant monk or an ascetic.
    10. Aspiration. Also lost here, perhaps something related to buy or use the services of the mall daily? In tune with its zero speed. But this could make the pack completely static, no globetrotting for them.
    11. Size. This thing is humongous, I am following this table, but not sure... According to that, my idea of the spirit having the same size as the building this thing would have size 100!
    12. Speed. Zero, it does not move, ever.
    13. Totem Advantages. Residential Area? Pack Dynamics? Professional Training?
    14. Pack Merits. I was thinking of Dedicated Locus, Den, Resources, Safe Place, Staff.


    I appreciate any help making spirit and pack viable.

      A possibly important thing to remember: the Shadow bears less of a direct resemblance to the material world the thicker the Gauntlet is in the area, which means you can afford for this thing to look weirder than a regular shopping mall.

      Spirits' names tend to be descriptive, and here it may be worth determining if this is just the spirit of the building or the spirit of commerce, community, et cetera that's managed to claim dominion over the area of the mall, which may help somewhat with mobility and (assuming this isn't based on an existing real-world place) figuring out what other spirits call it.

      I'd almost suggest a Ban relating to the mall's operating hours — with 21 Totem points, you're looking at a Rank 3 spirit, which tend to have bans that aren't so easy to trigger.

      Aspiration-wise it's worth remembering that this relates to the goals of the spirit and the pack. Why and how did the pack take its services as a totem?

If it's self-aware enough to join a descant and still be considered "the spirit of the shopping mall" then it ought to be secure enough in its place in the world to not be trying to directly embody everything in its domain instead of commanding tenant structure-spirits and visiting vehicle spirits and so on.

                  Think of the mall as a creature made of its structural materials and history and place in the world. Does this thing put out a lure and wait for food to come to it, or does it have subordinates harry its prey into its metaphorical jaws? Is the process of gentrification manifested in its impact on the Shadow of the surrounding area, or has the pack's stewardship bent it away from that kind of unrestrained predation?

                  Have you considered approaching this concept as a lodge or protectorate first, with the mall being the hub/connective tissue between different packs' efforts to steer the development of the city around it?
