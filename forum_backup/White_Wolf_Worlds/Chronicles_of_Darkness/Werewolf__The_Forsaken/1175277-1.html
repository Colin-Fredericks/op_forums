        Okay, system hack off the top of my head...

        Much like Uratha teeth carry the essence of Urfarah for other creatures, werewolf talons are the equaliser for The People. Injuries inflicted by Uratha claws (regardless of form) effectively ‘block’ those health boxes from Gauru’s enhanced regenerating. Rather than marking those boxes with a / or X ,mark them with a C. Uratha in Gauru form regenerate all bashing and lethal damage in their other health boxes every turn, but not boxes marked C. Boxes marked this way don’t otherwise impede the Gauru, but when all boxes are marked C the Uratha falls unconscious. Once unconscious, boxes marked C begin to regenerate as per lethal damage.

        Other Uratha - even those in Kuruth - instinctively recognise their enemy is no threat. Uratha in Death Rage automatically ignore these unconscious foes. Other Uratha feel a strong compulsion to not harm their vulnerable cousins - they suffer a breaking point towards imbalance at a penalty equal to their highest Renown every time they attempt to harm the weakened Uratha.
