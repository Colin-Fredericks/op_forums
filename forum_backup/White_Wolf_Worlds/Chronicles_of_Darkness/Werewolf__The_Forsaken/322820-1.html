    What we know merely implies that the average Iminir would, in absence of a proper frame of reference, mistake a Sin Eater for a Claimed. There's nothing that suggests they are too stubborn to understand the difference given the right information nor that they would enforce the "Wyrmspawn, kill on sight!" policy the Garou generally had for vampires.

    Hunting a prey does not merely mean to kill it immediately as soon as it approaches, it often involves knowing the prey a bit and there's absolutely no kind of compulsion that forces any Uratha of any Tribe to do so.

    I would also say that, even in their usual fanaticism, the CWoD wolves were a bit justified in their attitude against vampires considering the CWoD setting and history and that calling it nazism is a little too much (I won't deny it was unsavory and and closed-minded, especially from a "nice" vampire point of view) but that's another can of worms that does not belong to this discussion.
