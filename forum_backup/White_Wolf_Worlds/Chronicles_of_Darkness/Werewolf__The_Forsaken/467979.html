    Old Clans still Surviving?

    04-03-2015, 07:58 PM
    So its established that vampires rarely travel unless circumstances compel them to do so. The 5 clans that exist as they are are only the most abundant or popular in the west. But the book explicitly states that knowledge of foreign Kindred is scarce. It even points this out when explaining about the Burakumin bloodline. And VII is said to have 6 exclusive clans (The Betrayed) in its organization. Not to mention the supposedly extinct Julii and Traditores

    A lot of the old clans such as the Toreador, Bruja, and Giovanni have been reincarnated as bloodlines, but I don't believe all of them were. Is it possible that some of the original clans still exist out there in the world, undiscovered because of their lack of interaction with the western world?

      While it's likely there's a lot of clans of vampires that are unknown and undiscovered, given the immortal nature of the Kindred, I think the existence of clans from Vampire the Masquerade would only be likely on the tables of those who decided to homebrew it. Because they never existed in new World of Darkness, so it's not a matter of them 'surviving,' any more than the clans from Blade surviving and making a show.

      Though all of the main clans do have aspects of themselves in Requiem. Not merely as bloodlines, either. That's mainly because Requiem takes advantage of a lot of the stereotypes and iconic vampire fiction that Masquerade did. There's only so much vampire myth to go around, after all.
