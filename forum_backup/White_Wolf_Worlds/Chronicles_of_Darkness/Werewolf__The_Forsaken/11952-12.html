He claims to have been able to permanently kill almost a dozen pursuers. Before dying, he hands an amulet to the pack. It's a a large piece of amber with a spider trapped inside.                         237/)Down Among the Dead Men
                        A Hive-Claimed juggernaut prowls the countryside, smashing mortals to death with hammer-like hands of granite and gravedirt. It’s giant form resembles nearly a dozen human corpses melded through as if through a combination of acid and surgery, and it’s hump back is actually a bevy of smashed gravestones. It pounds it’s victims to pulp, then smears the viscera on itself as it howls our wordless devotion to the distant star Vega and the horrid court of Void spirits that cluster around it in the night sky.

                        It is a mad fusion of reanimated human corpses, spirits of gravestones, murder and, most disturbingly of all, things from the Deep Shadow. From the fissures in its body emits a sick green glow, and whispering can be heard everywhere near it.

                        Now, it come to be accompanied by strange bird shapeshifters, corpse-eating things themselves who hear the mad cries from Vega the Hive-Claimed does. This alliance has set the local Shadow to panic. Can the PCs’ pack stop this unholy alliance before it spills out of control?

                        Note:
                        Many thanks to Chris Allen for his awesome Orphean werebirds writeup.

                                239) It's been five consecutive nights now that the crescent moon has hung in the sky. The luminescent sickle imposes its form upon the black firmament with no sign of change on the horizon. Humans notice in the way humans only ever half-notice anything that isn't grabbing them by the labels and screaming, of course. Maybe the astronomers and meteorologists and Wiccans realize something's up, but they're not exactly rushing to the bullhorns about it for whatever reason. Meanwhile the Ithaeur languish in the spiritual bounty flowing into their werewolf-souls, exploiting the windfall of Essence to fuel strange rites, some of which some of them didn't even realize they knew until the lunar anomaly began, though it's now as though the symbols were always encoded into their blood and bones. The lunes, of course, say nothing helpful, while Pure totems grow agitated and divided on the meaning of it all, debating hotly whether this marks a moment of abruption in the Warden Queen's power or some dread new strengthening of antediluvian bonds among sunlight and gravity and reflection. In the gibbous dark something shifts. A drop of blood falls from nowhere onto a shard of mirror. An act is performed, repeated, then reversed. A lake is like a sea, a road a river. A woman who has been weeping nonstop for five hundred years immediately ceases, stands, and exits her geodic prison. Are the times out of joint, or is this a new good that the law is speaking into existence?
