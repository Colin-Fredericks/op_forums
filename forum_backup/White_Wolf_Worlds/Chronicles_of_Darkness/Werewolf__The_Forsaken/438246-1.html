            Originally posted by Seidmadr View Post

            No. It was anathema, back when the scale was a "that way lies disaster"-scale. Now it just points towards more spirit-like behaviour. And the Fire Touched are very spirit-friendly, if their 2e write-up is any indication.



            Yeah. But when it comes to bloody battle, when the time has come to show whose ideology is the strongest, et cetera.. Then this is the ultimate tool.
            Yes, gifts like Savage Rending and Silver Jaws are gone.. but they aren't needed. Every single Werewolf now has the ability to deal Aggravated damage at will. And I don't like that.

            I agree with earlier posters in that while I agree mechanically this is true I'm just not seeing it easily done in a narrative sense. As the ST you've got a whole plethora of reasons why only the occasional werewolf or group would commit this practice. For the Forsaken its a blatant violation of the Oath, which is kind of that set of cultural customs they feel defines their identity. Sure there will be specific circumstances where you have to break it, as with all codes (just killing another werewolf is one, so they're already in violation when trying to kill Pure and they know it). By consuming the flesh of another wolf for any reason they're committing an extreme taboo (for most) on either side. Even the Pure have a vested interest in keeping with Harmony (and their own version of the Oath which I feel probably covers this). Harmony breaks aren't just a mechanical slap on the wrist for these guys. Breaking towards spirit enough means they or their packmates are sliding towards not "more spirit-like behavior" but towards becoming an actual spirit monster trapped in the Hisil with multiple bans, unable to control your own rage and flying towards it at breakneck speed. No matter who you are, Forsaken or Pure, I think this is seen as a serious problem.

            Those issues aside I'm also having a hard time imagining where this is the more convenient damage option for any werewolf. Sure this might be the case if two packs went toe-to-toe with each other using no tactics or other advantages, but if Forsaken and Pure are hunting each other they know it and are acting accordingly, this means pulling in every favor you've got from the nearby human population and Spirit Wilds, choosing a battlefield you prefer if you can, pack silver (if Forsaken), target others instead of the Werewolves themselves. In most engagements werewolves will have enough options that deciding to start consuming your enemy won't be too high on the list.

            While the Forsaken will start packing silver, and one could apply the same argument I did above to its use, it is a lesser breaking point that's easier to deal with, and is also seen by werewolves at large as more a hopefully rare necessary evil. Saying "Ah, screw it" when the chips are down and eating your enemies which also happens to be as addicting as a drug and against your very nature as a descendant of Father Wolf (this I feel would be a major reason why the Pure would frown on the practice, if it goes against their perceived image of Father Wolf they'll repress it sternly, even violently) is rather extreme to me. It is true that I haven't seen it explicitly stated cannibalism is rejected amongst the Pure in 2e, I'd say there's plenty of evidence that its very likely. Though you are the ST, so you could make them have regular barbeque's if you really wanted.
