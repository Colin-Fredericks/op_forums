                    Originally posted by nofather View Post

                    Unless the disguise assisted by retarding the visual signs of aging. Your one hundred and seventy year old werewolf crone could look like a fit thirty-something.
                    According to Blood of the Wolf*, werewolves don't suffer ageing effects until 90-100 years old, and typically look about half their age. As human women (on average) experience menopause around 45-55 years, 90-110 is probably when Uratha would experience menopause.**

                    tl;dr IMO, Delayed menopause works, no menopause makes for poor disguise.***

                    * AFAIK, nothing has been discussed about what carries over from 1st to 2nd ed.
                    ** This is academic only, as I can only see a couple of in game scenarios where it would be relevant.
                    *** If it comes to a discussion, I'd recommend 2nd ed Uratha age at the same rate as the humans around them, as they're meant to be perfectly disguised.**** Besides which, apart from seeding a few ancient NPCs, longevity is almost never a factor in WtF games.
                    **** Except perhaps at PU 10, when the pure spirit Uratha sheds her fleshy shell and becomes something else entirely.

                                Personally - as in, in the unique environment of my comparatively crossover heavy games specifically - I would not have ageless Werewolves.

                                Long lived and healthy certainly. But living forever is rather more of a core conceit for Vampires and Mummies while it is more of an incidental quirk for Werewolves. To become ageless I'd have Werewolves have to find someway of becoming more Spirit, like the Firstborn, more totemic and ascending to a different form rather than being mostly a regular character who happens to have hundreds of XP and PU10 bopping around at 2,312 years of age.

                                                    It's not really touched upon in the core, but I always hold that werewolves age more slowly at higher Primal Urge, to the point of eventually becoming immortal. Especially at Primal Urge 10, where as Satchel pointed out, you're probably more spirit than flesh, and have long ago left humanity behind. And you know what's cool about it? If someone ever runs a high-powered, high-Experiences game with vampires or mummies or whatever, it lets you play an equally ancient werewolf (perhaps awoken from slumber in the Hisil) and get to be a real monster among monsters. So much flavor to be had there.

                                                    Them: "You just awoke. How long have you been slumbering in that cave?"
                                                    You: "The herd still lived in dens of thatch and mud."
                                                    Them: "Holy shit, how are you going to adjust? There's so much that's changed."
                                                    You: "No. One year, ten, a thousand. The hunt remains the same. And I grow hungry. Let me show you how the strongest hunt, pup."
                                                    Them: "What serves as prey to a millennia-old Uratha?"
                                                    You: "Everything."
