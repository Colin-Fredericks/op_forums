                                            Originally posted by TheBadWolf View Post

As I get it: for mages and hunters - completely YES, for vampires, Prometheans and, may be changelings - completely NO. What about sin-eaters, beasts and demons?

                                            Werewolves inflict aggravated damage when they devour flesh for Essence. According to the rules, this is possible on wolves, werewolves and humans. It doesn't say anything about other supernaturals, but some people agree that it should work on any supernatural that still has essentially human flesh. I'd say it doesn't work on vampires, prometheans and mummies. It would work on demons unless they fail to Spoof. I can't say whether it would work on Changelings, as that would depend on how the Mask and Mien interact in second edition.

                                            Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                            Idle spirit ecology question for the room:

                                            At what point does a spirit start to get into politicking (Essence tithes, etc) over direct predation?
                                            According to Werewolf 2nd Edition, p. 180, spirit politics starts at rank 3 for most spirits. Rank 1 and rank 2 spirits can participate, but aren't full participants, being the equivalents of animals and children.

                                                    Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                    I'm aware of the Rank-based tendency and was more curious about the environment or stimuli that prompt the shift from viewing lesser spirits as food to treating them as lackeys.
                                                    I should have expected you to be aware of that. This is a very good question.

                                                    Firstly, I need to note that I think a spirit could still eat its lessers, while trying to engage politically with its superiors. Then, for a spirit to engage in political behavior, it has to be in a situation where political behavior has greater benefit than predatory behavior at least of of the time, and of course it has to realize this, which depends on rank and type of spirit.

                                                    An environment where hunting for food is really dangerous could spur political interaction at an earlier rank, weeding out those who can't manage that. An environment with stronger political benefits than average could do the same. I'm not sure whether environment or stimuli would actually prompt this shift you mentioned, or whether they would just let those who don't make the shift be eaten by those who do, leaving only politically oriented spirits.

                                                    If I have more time, I'll try to think about more circumstances.
