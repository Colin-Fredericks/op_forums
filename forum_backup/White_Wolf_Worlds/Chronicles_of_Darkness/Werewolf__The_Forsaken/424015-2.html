                                        I wholeheartedly agree with this idea: the gods were important and it makes sense that they'd be venerated alongside the Firstborn. I do disagree a little with how you've distributed the gods, so I'll throw out my own version of it, primary god marked in italics.

                                        Blood Talons: Thor, Tyr, Odin
                                        Reasoning: Thor is a god of strength, he is the foremost warrior in Asgard, and all around strong guy. Also a champion slayer of trolls and giants. Tyr, on the other hand (pun intended) is a great commander, and a war leader of the Aesir, and he gives bravery and strength to soldiers - an obvious patron for the more cerebral Talons. And then there is Odin. God of war, death, magic, wisdom and madness. Only the most brave and foolhardy Talons worship him, but none can stand up to their claws and teeth when the Rage take them.

                                        Bone Shadows: Odin, Freyja, Hel, Ran
                                        Reasoning: Odin and Freyja are complimentary gods, both are deities of magic, wisdom and death, and thus they earn veneration from the Hirfathra Hissu. Odin, while being associated with wolves, also is associated with madness, leaving many of the Shadows wary of him. Hel and Ran are also keepers of the dead, and thus worthy of veneration, Ran takes the souls of all those lost at sea, and Hel claims everyone who isn't taken by the others. Hel has a special place in the heart of the Bone Shadows, because they know that she is the final keeper of the dead; even the gods themselves will come to the realm of Hel Half-Dead in the end.

                                        Hunters in Darkness: Ull, Skadi, Nott, Heimdall
                                        Reasoning: Ull is both a great hunter, and a great warrior. Master of the wilds, and master of the foes, the choice to venerate Ull is obvious to the Meninna. Skadi was a jotunn who married in to the family of the Aesir, but she never lost her love for the mountains, she is prayed to by everyone who has to hear out far in winter time, and is thus an obvious choice for the Hunters. Nott is the goddess of the night, and the Hunters in Darkness see a kinship between her and themselves, which gives her a place in their rites. Heimdall, the watchman is also praised, as he is seen as the avatar of their oath to keep the sacred places safe.

                                        Iron Masters: Loki
                                        Reasoning: The Iron Masters keep their rites close to those of the human kin they hide among, and thus don't have special focus on any of the gods, they venerate them all. That said, the Masters' mindset of always cheating when they can has given a lot of them a special liking for the trickster god.

                                        Storm Lords: Thor, Tyr, Odin
                                        Reasoning: Tyr, the one-handed warrior-god is one of the foremost gods in the rites of the children of Winter Wolf. He is a brave and great commander, once the leader of the Aesir, who stepped down in favour of Odin, some say. He sacrificed his hand to chain Fenrir, but did not let that stop him from standing tall and mighty. Odin, likewise a great leader who gave up much, but wasn't weakened by his sacrifice, is worshipped by the Imnir, although with caution. The Lodge of Crows, and Lodge of Winter are said to favour him. Thor, god of strength and thunder seems like a perfect fit for the Lords, and indeed, he is venerated, especially by the members of the Lodge of Thunder,

                                        I am aware that my Iron Master part is very weak, but I couldn't find any way to give any other gods places of importance there. Which bothers me, because I would like to add Frigga and Frey somewhere...

                                        And once again, I don't see any particular spiritual reason for the sacking of churches and monasteries, it's just that they were easy pickings.
