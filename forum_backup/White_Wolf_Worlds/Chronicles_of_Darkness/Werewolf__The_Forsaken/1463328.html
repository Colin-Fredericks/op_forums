                                          Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                                          You know, this was something I've had a question about for a while now. Obviously Werewolf is not beholden to address crossover outside of the times it explcitly comes up, and keeping the merit to humans/wolf-blooded is one of those small touches to keep the Forsaken....forsaken, but was there any additional reasoning behind keeping it to that set?
                                          In universe, it’s about the nature of the pack, the nature of the connection between spirit and Uratha, and the way Luna interacts with her children.

                                          It comes from the nature of the Uratha soul*, and the ‘spaces’ it has for connection to the spiritual side of their nature. Uratha have a space where Auspice connects them to Luna, a space for connection to the Firstborn and tribe, one for their totem etc. For Pure, when they remove their Auspices, we see that they have additional space for their Firstborn to fill in the form of Hunter’s Aspects.

                                          These spaces don’t all just come spontaneously from the First Change. There’s first something to work from, something with the right kind of shape that can be further reformed to deliver the desired outcome. Just like humanity gives the Uratha their basic four limbs, one head, two-eyes, two-ears, hair-covered template, the human soul already has ‘built in’ the potential for transformation into the necessary Uratha-required spiritual shape. When the First Change happens, Luna (through whatever force she has in place) reshapes the body and soul from human to Uratha.

                                          That sets the foundation, and sets the scene for why the merits only apply to WB and humans. WB are already part-way towards reshaping as Uratha. Their bodies and souls are partially transformed in a variety of ways depending on their Tells. It’s relatively small effort for Luna/totem spirits to make the rest of the changes necessary for the WB to gain full access to the connections allowing some Uratha-like advantages. It’s more effort to make those same changes in humans, but still possible, if the spiritual sponsor wants to make the effort. (The relative amount of effort is represented by how may Merit points the characters have to spend to access these advantages.)

                                          Other templates - major or minor - can’t access these advantages because they’ve chosen (or had chosen for them) to go down other paths. Their souls have been twisted away from the shapes spirit creatures can more easily work with, or they’ve filled them with something else. Vampires and mages may look human, but they’re not when it comes to having the foundations of Uratha-potential. Similarly, Ghouls, Sleepwalkers, or whatever other minor templates have gummed up their accessibility as well, spirits can’t latch onto their souls in the right way to give access to the Uratha-like advantages, which results from a game perspective of those characters not being able to purchase the Merits.

                                          *I’m using the term ‘soul’ generically here. I know it has a lot of baggage in CoD but it’s an easy word to use for this explanation.
