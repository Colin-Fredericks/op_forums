                            There is a lot of overlap. At first glance it's easy, vampires and mages for Iron Masters, Prometheans and sin-eaters for Storm Lords, Demons for Bone Shadows, etc

                            But it goes deeper than that and I'm pretty sure it's why they don't do anything like locking them down to one tribal sacred prey. Looking at a mage, you see a person doing magic, and it's easy to think they're just a human with some powers. But they also have this attunement with the Supernal that makes them obsess over mysteries, which might fit a distant sort of Claimed or Ridden. I think you could easily justify having a belief where mages are werewolves who've sacrificed their wolf-soul for power, giving you werewolf hunters.

                            Vampires are human-seeming, of course, so there's the Iron Masters. But they could be controlled by the strix, they're certainly animated by a foreign power, suggesting claiming or the hosts, and thus Storm Lords of Hunters in Darkness. As pointed out by one of the writers there are some legends where vampires and werewolves are the same thing, so you have Blood Talons, and it wouldn't be too hard to see them as spirits manifest, perhaps they're the rogue human spirits, able to survive manifested due to feasting on the blood of humanity.

                            Prometheans are humanoid, they're trying to be human, so Iron Masters. But they're also corpses or objects animated by a foreign spark, which can lend itself to Storm Lords or Hunters in Darkness. Maybe they're the Life Hosts. Maybe they aren't corpses claimed by spirits, but spirits manifesting alongside a corpse, trying to become real like the Scavenger Wolf from Skinchangers, a spirit being that, jealous of werewolves and their ability to straddle both worlds, they're forging a way through influencing demiurges and putting on corpses and mud until they 'fit in,' so Bone Shadows.

                            The overlap is there on purpose.
