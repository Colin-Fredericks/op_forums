                                                Originally posted by nofather View Post

                                                Why does it matter to you what they do in their games?

                                                It's not even that big an advantage until you get to higher Primal Urge, when you would likely be facing mages with higher Gnosis. Besides, mages already have to increase their spell factors to fight werewolves directly, they're much better off using indirect spells that wouldn't have Withstand ratings anyways.
                                                Does it directly affect me? No. I'm just curious as to why. Werewolves are already relatively magic-resistant, and the proposed benefit directly breaks two of the rules that Withstanding was built around: only one trait is used to Withstand, and that trait is never a 1-10 trait. So really, it's confusion. It's a bad rule, that I can't see working as either a player option or antagonist option, and I'd like to hear their thought process. Additionally, I would argue that it is a substantial buff, even at low Primal Urge. Since Withstood directly reduces Potency, and Potency can only be increased by taking penalties, every point of Primal Urge is a effectively reducing the spellcasting pool by 2.

                                                That said, not my game, and I'm sorry if Ephsy felt I was attacking them. I just don't think it's a good rule, and want to know more about how they got there.
