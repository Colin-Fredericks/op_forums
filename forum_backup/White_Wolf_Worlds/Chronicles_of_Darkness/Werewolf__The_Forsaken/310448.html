    Silver, Madness, and Antagonists in Armor

    11-12-2014, 03:37 PM
    I saw this armor one day when reading about a Japanese show from the creator of Power Rangers on Wikipedia. The reasons why I came across it aren't important so much as the idea that suddenly struck me upon seeing it.

    What kind of horror would possess an Uratha to don silver armor? Surely only an insane one might do it. Or maybe was made to do it? I started to ponder. I wanted to formulate a legend and a brutal antagonist.

    ...but I'm not very familiar with Werewolf. My group is waiting for Second Edition before diving in. I've read a few First Edition books and the core, but I wanted to ask the community, are there any hooks already out there, reasons for a Werewolf's soul, mind or body to be trapped in silver armor? Are there any legends already out there for me to start from or should I cloth this whole from something entirely new?

                                      Sorry this took a while GilboD; swamped with work, then family and RL friends and other responsibilities. But, as promised, here's my offering to your idea pool. I hope you like it.


                                      Tank could not believe what was happening to him. The Pure had caught him, broken his body and stolen his Rage, and then hauled him in chains to their hallowed grounds. What awaited him there… was a goddamned rally, almost twenty wolves strong. Had they been there all this time? he numbly wondered as they unceremoniously rolled him atop a flattened slab of stone – an altar.

                                      Suddenly, silence swept across the clearing, and the swarthy skinned                                       Rahu heard footsteps approaching. “Brothers!” cried a man’s voice, melodious yet stentorian, “Sisters! Tonight you stand in the presence of our gods, Lords Hathis, Huzuruth, and Gurim! Tonight you bear witness as they judge this Moon-loved, Forsaken, motherfucker!” the speaker growls, punctuating the last word with a blow to the back of the captive’s head and eliciting a cheer from the gathered Anshega. Tank groaned, thoughts swimming from the pain. Nonetheless he heard as the footsteps moved away from the altar, the speaker pausing for the clamor to die down before he continued.

                                      “Would that he should repent of the Mother and her traitorous ways,” the speech resumed, more solemnly this time. “Would that he should turn around and embrace Father Wolf! But in his heart, he has stricken                                       Urfarah dead, clinging to the love of the Moon-Bitch so; for why should he, unless he believed that Urfarah dead cannot judge him for his betrayal?”

                                      A hush had fallen over the                                       Uratha crowd then, seething with tension. Tank shuddered as he caught a whiff of them, boiling with barely restrained Rage. With hate.

                                      “Alas,” said the speaker softly, “Until the day we bring                                       Taga Dan upon this cursed earth and Father Wolf rejoins us, it falls to his Pure children - us - to punish the Enemy in his stead. Let judgment now then be pronounced by no less than Urfarah’s most faithful Firstborn sons! Apostle Maul, Brother Carver, come forth!”

                                      “What the fuck are you doing to me? The FUCK are you doing to me?!” screamed Tank, growling and trembling furiously as he tried to shake off his iron bonds. Soon there were three                                       Anshega standing around the altar, laying clawed, calloused Dalu hands upon him, chanting in darkly intoned Uremehir:

                                      <Lord Huzuruth, Lord Hathis, and Lord Gurim, we beseech thee to hear our prayer. So long have the Urdaga of this place suckled at the Moon-Bitch’s teat, forgetting our Father and turning their backs to his Truth. So speak we retribution, beginning with this one; we invoke the Rite of Purgation, of fury, fire, and argent pain! May the Forsaken behold him so renewed, and tremble! May their hearts quail with his screams of agony! And then shall the Pure descend, in a glorious blitzkrieg of Urfarah’s justice!>

                                      The three Anshega then threw their heads to the sky and howled, the congregation immediately following suit, their voices melding together into a single, heart-stopping chorus. Suddenly, Tank’s argent spirit brands flared to life, and then there was a white flash of teeth – a grin of pure sadistic glee – and the unsheathing of a single Gauru-sized claw. “Good,” said the speaker, Silent-Scream-the-Lambs, before plunging his claw into the blazing rune on the Forsaken warrior’s forehead.

                                      Tank screamed.

                                      -------------------------

                                      Purge the Impure

                                      An obscure, profane rite that transforms one of the Forsaken into a suicidal, pain-mad berserker, a living weapon forged for the sole purpose of slaying other Uratha. Three Anshega shamans – one for each of the three Pure tribes – must work in concert to perform the rite, and the target must be a werewolf who, at the very least, retains the spirit brands of Renown. Needless to say, this target - victim, really - must be kept immobilized throughout the entire ordeal. The power of the rite afflicts the victim with Silver Wolf’s Agony, twisting his Renown brands into glyphs of blasphemy against Mother Luna which all bleed molten silver. It also fills him with Dire Wolf’s Fury, causing him to change into a grotesque, albeit terrifyingly powerful, mockery of the Gauru. Lastly, the rite gives the victim Rabid Wolf’s Fever, which causes him to burn like a raging inferno from within. This rite, in the rare occasions it is used, serves as the precursor to a pogrom that the Pure are about to unleash.

                                      Mechanically speaking, all the victim’s close combat Brawl attacks inflict aggravated damage, and Uratha who inflict at least 1L upon him with a close combat Brawl attack suffers 1A from a splash of boiling-hot silver. The victim is also in a permanent state of Basu-Im - focused on chasing down and killing other werewolves - while taking on a horribly malformed shape commensurate in power to Primal Form. Lastly, the haze of incredible heat and outward pressure emanating from the victim’s body imposes a -1 penalty to all close combat Brawl and Weaponry attacks made against him. More importantly, the victim explodes upon death or the rising of the sun, whichever comes first, instantly killing him (if he were not dead already) as well as inflicting 4 dice + 4 levels of lethal damage (aggravated for werewolves) to everything within 5 yards.
