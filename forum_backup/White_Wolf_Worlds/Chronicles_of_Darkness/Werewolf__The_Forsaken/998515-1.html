                                As the book reads, your Harmony 8 Players have 30 seconds (ten turns) to snap out of Wasu-Im before they enter Basu-Im (which, at Primal Urge 1 or 2, lasts 10 minutes, and can be ended by and Elodoth, if they have one).While in Wasu-Im, the player can roll Resolve+Composure, each success giving him a turn which he controls himself and doesn't need to smash something to pieces. If he fails, he enters Basu-Im immediately.

                                To avoid Basu-Im entirely, the player needs en Exceptional Success on the Resolve+Composure roll to resist. It can be very hard to achieve, specially since starting pools range from 7 to 9 dice, if you have Willpower to spend on it. However, a little bit of tweaking to the Social Maneuvering system gives some ways to help the character:

                                Assuming his pack has at least an Average Impression with him, they can try to Force his doors and convince him to give it all when resisting Death Rage. Apply 1+Primal Urge as Doors. If the pack gets a success, you can give him a bonus to resit Death Rage (+1 for every success achieved, if they got 5 successes or more, add the Inspired Condition allowing for Exceptional Success on 3 instead of 5). However, if they fail, apply -1 to his resistance rolls, and if it was a Dramatic Fail, his next roll is reduced to a chance die.

                                Edit: Also, you might want to check the Pack Merit: Directed Rage, page 30 of The Pack.

                                    At Harmony 8, your Common triggers are in play and you have to *Witness* your auspice moon in the sky. The character throws on a ball cap and keeps his head down whenever he goes outside. He resists looking up. The Storyteller might throw challenges his way, and his pack should help look out for him.
