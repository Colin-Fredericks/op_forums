    What's up with Werewolves detecting one another?

    03-06-2015, 09:11 AM
    Page 61 of Werewolf states, under Hishu,

    "Most Uratha are most comfortable in human shape. Others see it as just another tool to use on the hunt. While wearing the Hishu skin, a werewolf hides amongst humanity. Efforts to pick her out of the crowd and identify her as somehow supernatural fail, even if those attempts are supernatural in their own right."

    Page 95 of Werewolf states, under Senses --> Smell

    "Werewolves can also identify each other and Wolf-Blooded by smell. Doing so requires a Wits + Primal Urge roll, though this takes an instant action and she has to get in close to the character to be sure. Whether she uses the close press of a nightclub crowd or a tight-knit mob at a riot, she has to invade her suspect’s personal space"

    Page 96 of Werewolf states, under Hishu,

    It's actually what it doesn't state. Despite this being where forms are mechanically defined we don't get a repeat of Hishu's detection immunity. It should really be here if it's a thing.
    '
    Page 98 of Werewolf states, under Hunter Aspect

    "Uratha know each other at a glance. Apex predators smell their own. This crosses all three of the Uratha’s sensory fields; every single sense can identify one Uratha to another, as the man, the wolf, and the spirit all carry a prominent, forceful aura."

    Page 92 of Blood and Smoke states, in the sidebar "Others and the Predatory Aura" state

    "Other denizens of the World of Darkness may have similar auras. Most well-known is the feral mien of the werewolves. When Kindred notice werewolves, they feel physiological responses they
    never otherwise experience, such as goose pimples along their arms, and chills down their backs. They feel the adrenaline surge in the pit of the stomach that humans associate with danger and tense excitement."



    I think some of these entries are in error? I'm not sure how to parse this.

                      Going by each point as a number, this is the best I can figure it all out. It is confusing.

                      1. This is not an automatic detection failure. It is an indication that the detection can fail, see Sheep's Clothing.
                      2. This is for Urhan and Urshul, not Hishu and Dalu, unless the werewolf chooses to use wolf senses.
                      3. Sheep's Clothing
                      4. This seems out of place, but I reconcile it by saying they would be using wolf senses and it is not automatic, see Wolf's Senses.
                      5. Kindred can detect a werewolf if they can beat Sheep's Clothing and would be able to tell the strange human is a werewolf if they know about them.

                      Basically, werewolves are difficult to detect when in human form, but this is less effective when under specific scrutiny. You might not detect anything if you walk onto a crowded train, but if you "sniff" each of the other passengers then you could find your wolf.
