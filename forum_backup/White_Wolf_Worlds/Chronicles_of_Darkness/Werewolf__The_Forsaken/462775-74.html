        Originally posted by magisanctum View Post
        All Uratha see clearly spirits in twilight or it should activate something? Spirits are turning the person to Claimed they can be seen in the twilight?
        Answer:
        P95, 'The werewolf does not need to focus in order to sense spirits in Twilight. Unless the spirit is intentionally trying to hide, Uratha sense spirits in Twilight as well as they would manifested.' A Claimed doesn't have a Twilight presence unless it had a power that gave itself one. Either the spirit is Influencing the target, or it has Possessed or Claimed them, in which case you wouldn't see them. If a Claimed had an ability to go Twilight, I'd suggest you'd see it providing it was a spirit-claimed and not a ghost or something else.

        Since I've never find anything about buying "merit: loci" to begin the game I assume that the locus you start in the territory is purely narrative decided by the players in the territory creation or the gm. For me that was a big problem because its really strange that you are not given any criterion to have a loci to start your game since they are clearly an advantage and needed such as the totem. (Any information about starter locus?)
        Answer:
        P106, 'Dedicated Locus' Merit. Aside from that they are part of the setting and the Storyteller's domain. It's also worth noting that upon gaining a totem the pack is considered Resonant to that totem, and a lot of the things they do are likely to shed Essence to it. Totems don't struggle with Essence the way normal spirits might.

        Ignoring all spirits and narratives results:
        What are the rules for consuming essence in the loci? You can consume their essence until your essence pool is full? Could not find any dicepool for that too.
        Answer:
        Yes, there's no dicepool and you take as much as you need. If you have a way to quickly vent off Essence you can use it to drain loci dry. As far as I can tell they tried to get rid of useless dice rolls like that, since if you didn't get however much you needed, you could just do it again and it's the kind of thing you would most likely not do under pressure like in the middle of a fight.

                                                    Originally posted by The Throne View Post
                                                    On Pack Tactics... each Theme give a +2... or a flat +2 if all the themes are incorporated?
                                                    That's an excellent and interesting question.

                                                    The new Pack Tactics system went through *a lot* of iterations. The earliest didn't look anything like what we ended up with. (They looked more like 1st edition's tactics system.)

                                                    In an earlier iteration of the Pack Tactics system than the one that ended up being published, Tactics had a lot fewer themes (maybe one or two per Tactic), and if you incorporated them you got the +2.

                                                    In a later version, I added more themes per Tactic, and gave +1 for each, but you subtracted the dot rating of the Tactic from the dice roll. The idea being that higher rated Tactics are more difficult to use, but if you can make conditions favorable for the pack you could overcome the penalty and even turn it into a bonus.

                                                    Stew and I went back and forth fine tuning Pack Tactics, so some elements of earlier versions slipped through to publication. (Like the paragraph saying humans and Wolf-Blooded can use Tactics, then later saying they were restricted to Uratha.)

                                                    So, by RAW, the primary actor's dice pool is Attribute + Skill +2 (if you incorporate all the Themes) + Secondary actor successes. (Attribute + Skill for secondary actors.)

                                                    By the errata that I've sent to Stew (that he's said he'll include) the dice pool for the primary actor should be Attribute + Skill - Tactic Dot Rating + 1/Theme + Secondary actor successes. (Attribute + Skill - Tactic Dot rating for secondary actors.)

                                                    Hopefully that's helped clear it up.

                                                    Writer. Developer. World of Darkness | Chronicles of Darkness | The Trinity Continuum
