        'Contagion' is broad term over vastly different regional events that break base reality in area, in some way. They are only put together as it's seem all those different effects are 'spreading' like a disease in various ecologies they start in - going even to mortals and other supernatural beings.

        For now we have those shown as example Contagions:
        • Parallel universe bleeds of beings and places ( Edinburgh, New Zealand )
        • Vampires rising blood addiction and light sensitivity in all around them ( Edinburgh )
        • Suicidal depression sticking to every self-conscious being ( Odense )
        • Ghosts becoming material, permanently ( San Francisco )
        • Corruption that makes you immortal and/or mutated ( Kyoto )
        • And few other I did not read yet...
        My own example idea: Contagion of Being, that slowly erase ephemeral and physical beings from reality.

        Pardon me, but is there some sort of list and/or codex putting together all these 'contagion' scenarios?

                        The Contagion seemed the situation where people who would not normally join together would join together, because there's something awful there. A Contagion that threatens the existence of both parties, requiring them to team up to defeat them. After that, when the Contagion is gone people get to doing their own thing. They may stay in contact with members, but they may just try to forget them as they go back to their hopefully back to normal lives.

                        If the beshilu and Hunters had an agreement to work against something that was screwing both of them over, then they might give each other a pass under certain occasions. Hunters agreeing not to kill the beshilu in a certain area, providing they stay in that area. Beshilu agreeing to avoid the Hunter in Darkness territory. But hosts aren't people, they're rabid monstrosities that only seem to care about three things, themselves, their drive to revive their progenitor, and hating werewolves. The hate represents itself in different ways and most established hosts are not going to go berserk at the sight of a werewolf, when they know there's more cunning ways to deal with the ancient enemy that has been bent on their genocide for thousands of years. If a Contagion affects them both similarly, they may relax enough to work at it, but they may just as likely kill each other to study the remains, or ensure that the other party isn't going to try it first. And if it affects one side and not the other it's hard to imagine either side lifting a finger to do anything to help the others.

                            My players are dwelling deep into Iconoclasm Contagion, to find a permanent cure. Any idea what can it be, from Werewolf: The Forsaken point of gameline?

                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                            Symptoms

                            Outbreak Core: It starts with your secondary traces of being. People start to miss you in conversation. You lose reflection, then your shadow. Then people omit you in life. And, finally, you simply cease to exist.

                            Contagion started with Ephemeral Beings ( spirits, ghosts, goetia, angels, etc. ), worshiped as sacred items and anchored to those images. Spirits and ghosts started to lose Manifestations, then their Essence – then their Corpus, being erased from reality. As the Contagion spread, even non-infected individuals lose they reflections – like the mirrors and water resists to show anyone living in them. ( Non-infected undead like vampires and sin-eaters are still shown. ) And finally whole glory of Empire is forgotten and lost.

                            Stages of Contagion are describing firstly effects on Ephemeral Beings, then after ‘/’ for the humans and physical monsters. Beasts of Dark Mother, Bound Sin-Eaters and Uratha werewolves are in worst situation because of this Plague – they response they were are both Ephemeral and physical beings, at once. On Stage 2 they lose Essence/Plasm/Satiety and their shadow. And on Stage 3 they lose Corpus ( Health in reality ) and lose contact with outside world.

                            Contagion Carrier (Persistent)
                            1. Contagion-Touched – Social penalty -1 to express oneself / Social penalty -1 as people subconsciously omit Touched in conversation.
                            2. Infected (1) – Blocking of 1 Manifestation per day. When loss of last one – next stage. / Loss of reflection in water, mirrors and similar.
                            3. Infected (2) – Loss of 1 extra Essence per day. Cannot use Numina and Influences as ephemeral entity lacks the bodily cohesion to interact with other beings in Twilight. ( From lack of Twilight Form. ) A ghost who suffers this fate drifts through Twilight unable to be seen or touched even by other Twilight ghosts, alone and helpless to affect the world in any way, even only the world of ghosts. Spirit is locked in Twilight and cannot move to Shadow Realm on it’s own. When loss of last one Essence point – next stage. If being has physical representation and at least 3 other material beings worship the representation – Essence lost is stopped for 24 hours. / Loss of shadow on any light source.
                            4. Infected (3) – Loss of 1 Corpus point per day. Uratha are checking entering Basu-Im each night because of Essence hunger. Begotten has 0 Satiety. When loss of Corpus box – final stage. / People do not notice you unless you make physical contact ( transmitting Contagion then ).
                            5. Disappearance – Final outcome, you cease to exist.
                            Consequences of Contagion
                            Ephemeral Beings:
                            • Loss of Manifestations – Slow disappearance of Twilight Form, Reaching, Fetter and Posses or Claim makes ephemerals not possible to affect humans – so no healing, blessings and overwatching of spiritual entities of humanity. More deadly accidents, weaker tools, and first and foremost – no influence of ghosts and spirits on the mortals. People are becoming more and more agnostic in their worships.
                            • Loss of Essence ( and a like ) – Weaker spirits ( and other ephemerals ) are easier prey for their enemies. Predatory spirits are more violent, weakened by Contagion spirits and ghosts looks for the way to jump over to the Flesh World and collect Essence directly. Spiritual landscapes madness. Hungry Werewolves without Essence and Beasts Satiety going down implies rampaging Uratha and Horrors in physical bodies on Flesh World. Mortals become more dull and detached from their emotions.
                            • Ephemerals cease to exist – Like in Loss of Essence, but much more direct. Spirit World and Primordial Dream is turn upside-down with gaps in ecology. Underworld is much more ‘hungry’ as ghosts-slaves of it are disappearing. All the realms are starting to ‘steal’ mortals in place of their native ephemerals, to fill the roosters.
