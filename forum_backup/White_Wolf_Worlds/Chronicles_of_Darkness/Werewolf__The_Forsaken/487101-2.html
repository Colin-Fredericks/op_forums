                    I often thought that Silver bullets would be much more available in the World of Darkness than in our world, simply because, there is an actual use of them. The main law of Demand and Supply in Economics states that, the higher the demand, the more supply there will be. Since Werewolves are one of the more numerous species in the New World of Darkness (about half a million in the first printing), how can there NOT be a market for silver bullets?

                    In the "real world" there are tons of people who have written articles about making silver bullets. You can even buy silver bullet shaped bullion on the market if you are into that kind of thing (it does not fire out of a gun but is a cool investment). If people discuss it on the internet on guns and ammo forums you will see some people joke about a "werewolf problem". However, in the World of Darkness this would not be a joke and a serious concern, so there would most likely be an underground, or even over the counter market for silver bullets. Since Werewolves are in most parts of the world, it would pay to have some silver bullets just in case.

                    Hunters cells, compacts, and conspiracies tend to be internet savvy and well connected. Surely there would be a way to transfer resources among hunters, and some hunter cells would know another hunter cell that happens to have Silver bullets that they can send over. Since casting bullets can be done at home and silver is not too expensive, there must be tons of basement industries devoted to the manufacture and distribution and sale of Silver bullets.

                    What Vampire Prince does not keep a supply of Silver bullets or blades on hand just in case? I really liked the scenario in "Under a Blood Red Moon" which depicted a pretty good conflict between Vampires and Werewolves. Basically, Vampires would supply their ghouls with silver bullets and have them fight werewolves. The same template can be used in the new World of Darkness too. Some vampire in the city would have a ghoul in a basement manufacturing some special bullets "just in case...".

                    Some humans would probably start some industries simply to meet demand. If suddenly strange people are asking about silver bullets that can be shot out of a gun (or shotguns with silver shot), then someone would start making them simply to fill a need. These people don't even need to know that werewolves exist but would only be reacting to a market demand and fill that demand to generate a profit.

                    Of course, the werewolves in the world would probably be trying to shut down such operations, but there is probably no way that they could completely silence the market. If your territory is in Idaho and the local hunters are buying their silver bullets from some guy in Brazil, it would be tricky to shut the supplier down.

                    Then you need to consider a well funded Hunter group like Task Force VALKYRIE. These guys are funded by the US army and so probably have a huge budget to buy all the silver weapons that they need. They are also a task force which probably gets support from other branches of the military. If they need a company of Marines to shoot shiny bullets against hairy insurgents, I am sure that they can arrange it. Sure silver bullets might cost $20.00 each, considering the silver content, but when the US military budget is $600 Billion, it would not take long to make enough to keep the local werewolf problem under control.

                    In addition, Task Force Valkyrie would probably lobby the government to allow the sale of Silver Bullets among ammo shops around the country. After all, why should they do all the hunting? By flooding the market with silver bullets they would allow any aspiring hunter cell the weapons they need to eliminate any local packs. That would keep the werewolf population in check and allow Task Force Valkyrie to focus their efforts on the most dangerous packs.

                    Mages would probably be able to make their own silver through magik so making silver bullets would be a joke to them. Surely some would capitalize on this to corner the market.

                    So yea...I believe that the channels to obtain silver weapons would be available if there was a demand.
