    How do I describe the alien feeling of being in Hisil without especifying any resonant area:



    The loci: You enter the phone booth and pick up the phone the way they taught you. You focus and imagine a call with no return. Your body start to being pulled into the phone and slowly getting stuck and smashed as if rocks were crushing your bones. You need continue and make much effort to pass the Gauntlet during the day. You extend your arms and put your hands starting feeling the phone booth again. You pull your body to the other side falling to the ground inside the same phone booth. You walk outside and everything is a distorted shape of the original buildings. There is no one in the streets, everything is empty.

    Listen this while reading> https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1XOxGeV8QT4
    During the day: Strange, it seems you are pressing your fingers against your ear. You hear a sound pressure, the air seems to cause this effect on you. You feel that your hair looks extremely heavy, it makes you feel your hair is wet. Your clothes(fur) seem to cause you claustrophobia. The air you breathe comes hot on your throat and leaves your mouth dry. You do not feel wind or breeze making you suffocate in the infernal heat of the day. The dripping sweat from your face never seems to stop until you find a cool shade beneath a tree, you sit and let the breeze refresh you, strangely the breeze only stays in that tree.
    You put your mind together and pay attention to the world around you. Everything seems to have the same color despite not having. The sun shafts touches the buildings giving a dark burnt orange tone to everything including yourself. You notice in the sky a strange yellow aurora that covers all where usually is blue. Almost everything remains covered by many dark clouds that keep moving out of the way to the great sun reign in the sky and show its true face.

    A colossal flaming ball appears in the sky. The sun's light is so strong that it is almost impossible to see. You never imagined feeling that kind of intensity even being so far away from the sun. Your eyes and all the renown you earned by lunes burn in flames but that is not enough to hurt you. you insist looking and now you can clearly see how the sun looks on the earth like an giant alien eye that can see everything and everyone. A hypnotizing feeling do not let you look away from the colossal eye, your peripheral vision suddenly fades, you can only see the great flaming eye in the sky looking at you and everything around gets dark.
    You are blind, they told you to never look directly at the sun.

    Listen this while reading> https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cG0y3xxlTJk
    You wake up in the Night Your hair is so light that it seems you are under water, your clothes(fur) comforts you in the cold night. The air you breathe is pure but ice cold, you tremble and slowly you begin to get used to it. You do not feel wind or breeze even when you move. There is no ambient sounds at all, everything is silent except for the sound of your breath and footsteps. Countless stars are in the sky but many clouds cover the stars and moon. The lunar light beams pass through the clouds and light up the sky and the ground giving the buildings their unique tone of color represented by its material version. Sometimes you see lightning crossing from one cloud to another giving the impression they are fighting or travelling between the heavy clouds.
    You touch the water on the ground, the water runs through your fingers almost in slow-motion, you can capture every detail of those little drops enjoying its natural beauty. Raindrops start falling into the pool of water in which you were touching you look up and..


    A DEAFENING THUNDERechoes giving the start for a terrible storm, the water falls violently from the skies, it hits the ground and returns to the sky as if there were no gravity. You run for shelter but the rain is no longer falling from above, the rain comes from aside wetting everything that can touch without giving a chance to hide beneath something.
    You enter the first building you see and expect the rain to stop sitting with your hands on your face. Your experience in the shadows has been terrible already but you still have to complete your training trying to find your way out.

        The city bridge:
        It's a long famous bridge in the city, not for its beauty but by the amount of accidents or suicides that take place in it.
        They removed all the sidewalks on the bridge, only cars can pass today. The only way to cross the bridge is to transit by auto-mobile / taxi / bus

        The Hisil:
        In the spiritual world the bridge is huge and extremely long. The bridge shadows are black as the night sky covering the sea. Beneath the central part of the bridge you see hundreds of dead bodies floating in the water. On its side numerous signs are visible at distance indicating: "Kill yourself, heavy traffic, accidents, no hope" and more thousands of similar signs.
        In the great avenue that connects to the bridge you see cars in extreme speed. Some of the cars are nothing but ghosts of Themselves, insubstantial shadows passing. Others are almost caricatures of Themselves: monstrous SUVs thundering down the street, pursued by predatory Porsche-spirits scattering lazy family sedans out of the way.
        They say many urathas trying to cross on foot this ancient bridge were influenced to commit suicide. Others were run over and killed by cars.
        You can cross the bridge by your own spirit car (totem/pack member) or negotiating with a taxi or bus spirit.

        The Spirits:

        THE BRIDGE has its own ancient spirit. Imagine a giant lying with his belly down to the sea and his back to the sky. His arms and legs are steel beams supporting the weight of his body. The asphalt is his back and his face can appear on either side of the bridge. His face is made of metal and asphalt and when he speaks you can see dead bodies inside.
        In the spiritual world primarily his appearance is not very different from the material world. You can only talk to this spirit when offer great sacrifice or when crossing it on foot without being hit by a car and have resisted the suicidal tendencies.
        Dark melancholic ghostly spirits walks the bridge edges influencing people to come and jump.
        Beneath the bridge the classic spirit of death hooded with his scythe awaits ensuring certain death.

        TAXI spirits are demanding and fast. His appearance is usually a normal taxi but the driver is a man with his body connected to the seat. His hands are stitched to the leather steering wheel.
        Instead of his eyes there is a digital screen in his face with numbers waiting for you to speak one direction. The more the taxi goes more money will increase on his face display. You will have a basic idea of how much you will have to provide in essence. The taxi spirit will not accept any kind of essence that is not from money.
        Some taxi say they charge very cheap for the trip but their intent is to take you to some terrible place which you will never return.

        BUS spirits can charge very cheap but can take any kind of spirit. You can find spirits of all kinds inside a bus even those with negative influences You may be followed by any creature after getting off the bus. Dont forget you are an unusual being called Uratha and you have the marks of luna in your body, that means you are not always welcome in the shadows. If in case you have a bad reputation with some Umia you better watch out for any confusion or you will be banned from that bus along with the provocative spirit.
        Some bus spirits are modern and have a very similar appearance to its material equivalent but much brighter and bigger. Others are old and decayed, cracks are scattered around the rusty metal, windows are broken and when it moves it leaves a trail of smoke behind.
        The decayed bus usually It takes you to the worst parts of the city. The modern(new) ones leads to central parts where it crosses for monuments and historical buildings.
        Never forget that the buses always stop in the place they are used to stop.
        During the morning or night the bus mystically influe all its passengers to sleep. Be aware that you may lost the place you were going to go.

        Inside the bus the driver is always in uniform but has no face and does not speak. You know what he wants and he waits you to pay.
        Before paying there are iron bars blocking the way into the bus. During the day the buses are full of immaterial weak spirits that resemble human beigns. They are everywhere crowding the seats and the bus corridor. During the night the bus is almost empty, specific and intelligent spirits that understand the concept of using a bus can get around more safely in the spiritual city by paying essence too.

                The house:
                You are almost out of gas, gps battery is down, there is no signal on your phone. You are lost in the middle of nowhere.
                You drive a few more miles and find a big old house. All the green vegetation has disappeared, the surroundings of the house and every leaf and twig is gray.
                It's a big country house where an old sympathetic man lives.
                The old man often offer a warm bed and food for people who are lost. He always claims that are trying to fix the phone but it is just a pretext to make anyone stay longer in the house.
                The old man drug their victims with the food offered. His victims are tied to an iron bed in the basement and when they wake up they already lost a llimb or two. Time passes and the wounds are treated and stitched. He tries increasingly to socialize with the victim. First he feeds them with serum, then he makes a soup with the remains of the body parts of the victim.
                After all the psychological and torture the old man likes to see them suffer. He involves their naked bodies without limbs with barbed wire until their bodies no longer withstand the loss of blood. After death he consumes their bodies making a well made food in the oven and feeds the next lost guest.

                The Hisil House:
                All the vegetation is dead. The trees are twisted around the house. On some nights the moon appears behind the house like a giant spotlight making the house shadow extends throughout the yard dark as the nightsky. As you approach the front door you notice that the windows cry blood and wood floor range groans of pain when you step on it. If you are lost in the shadows worse for you because you found hell.
                (I will leave the house interior for interpretation)

                The main spirit:
                He is a twisted corrupted violent spirit of pain and pleasure.
                His body is humanoid made of barbed wire. He has no limbs but he can walk pushing himself with his barbed wire arms/legs cutting the floor and the wall so that it can move.
                He is an extremely violent spirit but he will try to convince you to give your own body to his satisfaction.
