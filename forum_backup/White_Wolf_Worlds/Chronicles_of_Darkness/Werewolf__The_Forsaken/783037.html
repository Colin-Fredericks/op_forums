    Totem Ban and bane ideas needeed

    12-09-2015, 04:41 PM
    Hi
    My players (mixed mage and werewolf group) want to have a totem that will come out either as strong rank 1 or as weak rank 2 (depends on what the last player puts into the totem points).
    Now my problem is that I'm not sure what a good bans/banes could be good (still getting used to 2nd edition bans).


    The group is 1 rahu spirit hunting werewolf and 1 mage.
    The totem is a "shadow wolf". A spirit that is associated with the wolves that hunt in the darkness of night and thus in the "shadows".
    Influences will probably be in the area of Darkness, hunt.

    One idea I had was

    Ban: "Must hunt once a month staying in the shadows"
    Bane: Must be killed by fangs and claws

    Like I said I'm not sure about the bans and banes what could be fitting. Thus would like some feedback there and maybe additional ideas (also how many does such a spirit need each?)
