                                    Well, let me put it this way: do you think I'm a good person? Cause I know a lot of people think I am here.

                                    And for all my bleating about not being a nice person-which is absolutely true, by the way-I know I am far and away from being a bad person. And a lot of that is because I'm invested in being a good person-a good person who's not limited by arbitrary things, but a good person nonetheless. I have a flexible morality, sure, but that still means I have a morality. I'm just willing to constantly reappraise it, and have no problem basing things on the circumstances rather than something stricter. I don't like commandments, I like conscience.(Now wrap your head around the fact that I am an LDS Christian)

                                    Just about anything is not absolutely everything. Lines are needed-even the Iron Masters know this, and there's a reason something like the Idigam are huge ass problems. Are they perpetually needed? Depends on the situation, but for me as an individual there are less than most people think there should be. I want to do things because I care about them, but as a general rule, I care about being a good person. Just not a nice one, and fairly often I have no problem being a very rough person

                                    And while I am inherently something of a narcissist, I do care about other people. Yes, I really like praise and glory, and yes I will totally eat your sausages if I'm hungry or trick you out of your parachute and jump from the building in Betrayal at House On the Hill-but I I will also very likely buy you more sausage later to make it up later, and if you're upset over what I did in the game I will probably apologize for it. The important point, though, is that I don't do either because it's expected of me. If I'm required to, forget it. If it's demanded I do things to make up for them, I will reply with a hearty "Go fuck yourself". But if you let me be myself? I generally fix what I break.(Plus, the way I've decided to get communal fame is through living strangely and writing, that should tell you a few things)

                                    THe big thing though? Big picture. I like the world. I have hope for the world. I think helping it along is great(and a good way to get myself fame too). I don't think the Maeljin promote a picture I'm invested in. I don't think the things I want are wanted badly enough I don't mind them being wrapped so blatantly in sin. The Deceiver might lightly appeal to me, but my lies are mine, not his, and I don't like what they do with it. The Pure aren't much better-their crusade is more understandable, but it's fueled by hate, and while I am deeply familiar with the emotion, I don't like it and would rather not live off of it.

                                    And broadly, I like the struggle the Forsaken have. I mean, yeah, it sucks. It really does. But it's going to a place I like, and it's not terrible actually, in the meanwhile. That's a contradiction. But it all comes down to is that the Forsaken live a life I can get behind, the sort of thing I care about. It's not perfect, but it's not bad.

                                    Plus you say "get something out of it" like I need to sign up with a demon to do so. I don't, thanks much.

                                    Or, simply: I'm just not interested in what the Bale Hounds and the Pure do enough to care what they can give me.

                                    EDIT: Also, for clarity, consider that one of my two favorite Orders from Mage are the Guardians of the Veil. That should explain plenty.

                                    EDIT: Also, there's a difference between proudly and honestly. One thing I try not to do in my life is lie about who I am or excuse it. I'm not always successful, but it's one of those things I care about.

                                                Auspice: Ithaeur
                                                Tribe: Iron Masters

                                                I know myself well enough to understand that even if I have super powers I'd likely spend a good bit of my time reading books. I'd likely continue to try and understand the world around me, especially given how much more of the world I suddenly have access to. I wouldn't necessarily deny my instincts purposefully, but my intense self-scrutiny means I would still think like a human in many ways. The loss of control to Kuruth would horrify me to the core.

                                                There's also the fact that I just plain like people and human culture in general. I'm hardly a shining beacon of what I naively call the Enlightenment, but I try to nurture its embers where I can. I would most likely take up a protective impulse towards the Herd in general, though I can't say I would be above culling it when necessary even if that would be the option I go to last. Curiosity would abound, I'd likely join the Lodge of Scrolls to continue my studies.

                                                Somewhere between Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Jekyll, but a werewolf.
