    How does Regeneration work in 2nd?

    11-09-2015, 06:59 PM
    My group and I are new to 2nd Edition Forsaken and we were trying to puzzle out how exactly Regeneration is suppose to work mechanically during combat rounds, and here are some of the Questions that came up.

    1) Do Werewolves Regenerate on their Turn, or at the end of the Round?

    2) Spending Essence to upgrade Regeneration from Bashing to Lethal can be done Reflexively, does that mean the Werewolf can only do so during their Turn or can a Werewolf decide to spend that Essence at anytime during the Round?

    3) What happens to a Werewolf when their Health Boxes are full of Lethal Damage or Bashing Damage? Do they Fall unconscious? Do they ignore it except for the Wound Penalties and keep on their feet fighting, fleeing, or what have you?

    4) It says that so long as a Werewolf can Regenerate their Right Most Health Box they will not bleed out, we ask if you need to have Essence to spend to upgrade Regeneration to healing Lethal for this to be effective? If a Werewolf with no Essence to spend has his Right Most Health Box filled, are they as likely to bleed out as a normal person?

    5) Can a Werewolf be knocked Unconscious, and if so would being Unconscious prevent a Werewolf from entering Garu form to activate their amazing Regenerative abilities in that form?

If their healing is somehow suppressed they will fall unconscious just like a a mortal though.

If a Werewolf with no Essence to spend has his Right Most Health Box filled, are they as likely to bleed out as a normal person?
      Yes. Mostly. Without essence a werewolf will upgrade one lethal wound to aggravated per minute, while it will take them fifteen minutes to heal their rightmost lethal. This means that if they have more than fifteen health in hishu (Difficult, but not impossible) it will be possible for them to heal and cease bleeding out before they die.

                                                      Originally posted by Iry View Post
                                                      Can reflexive actions such as spending essence to heal lethal actually happen AFTER you have been knocked unconscious?
                                                      By rules as written, I'd say not. WtF 2nd p.169 says 'A werewolf in Gauru form does not suffer wound penalties or need to roll to remain conscious, nor does he bleed out. In other forms, werewolves take damage as humans do, though they have incredible regenerative powers.' The implication is that other forms can fall unconscious.

                                                      Although p.160 says 'a reflexive action is the sort of thing you don't even need to think about doing', I believe that sentence is being colloquial, and you technically do have to be able to devote a modicum of conscious thought to doing an action reflexively. You certainly couldn't justify activating all your reflexive Facets one after the other if you were unconscious, unless they specified they activated that way.

                                                      Note that the Uratha should be able to reflexively trigger her lethal regeneration powers any time before she falls unconscious - such as just before she makes the Stamina roll to see if she does pass out. My ruling would be that if she does that, she regenerates lethal while unconscious. If she chooses to trust the luck of the dice, and see what the Stamina roll brings, if she fails it's too late and she's on the ground only regenerating at the standard rate.

                                                          I believe in 1E you could in fact regenerate while unconscious, like vampires can. That's certainly how I run it, anyway. They regenerate passively while unconscious, and given the description of werewolf healing, I don't see why you couldn't spend Essence to regenerate faster while unconscious. It's not necessarily a conscious decision on the werewolf's part, even if the player is the one choosing to do so. It's the survival instinct kicked up to 11 in response to a grave threat. The intention is that werewolves are terribly hard to put down without silver, powerful magic, or a ton of firepower. Also, page 171 implies if anything that the werewolf can regenerate while unconscious: "The only exception is if a human character has started bleeding out because her Health boxes are full of lethal damage. She can't recover from that without urgent medical attention and emergency surgery. As long as a werewolf can regenerate her rightmost Health box, she won't bleed out." That passage wouldn't make sense if you couldn't spend Essence to regenerate while unconscious from lethal damage.

                                                          You might knock a werewolf out, but it will not last for long. They heal at least 1B every turn, so if you have their Health track full of bashing damage and they fail the Stamina roll, they'll still wake up the next turn regardless. If you knock them down with lethal, they might slump over, seemingly dead and bloodied ... then leap back up and attack before you realize that she's not dead.
