    What if Father Wolf wasn't weakened by the first Pack...

    03-05-2016, 03:21 PM
    ...But instead by breaking the Silver Ladder. The Abyss is a barrier between the Supernal and Phenomenal worlds. The Gauntlet is between the World and the Shadow. The Silver Ladder could have been resting partially in the Border Marches as a sort of sympathetic model of the bridging between both the Supernal and Phenomenal. And Father Wolf as a guardian of borders could have recognized the disruption caused by material beings entering the Supernal and rallied some mages and with their help assaulted the bridge between worlds. In the process he collapsed the Marches into the Gauntlet and tore the Abyss open cutting off the movement between the worlds. But because their was no longer a proper hunting ground between the Shadow and the Flesh he could not adequately perform his duties. So he let himself die so his legacy, the Uratha, could live on.

    I think the only evidence for this is the stories of the Aegis Kai Doru who blame Mages and Werewolves for something. If the Aegis was originally another prefall civilization they could have blamed the Atlanteans and the First pack for the war which damaged their own magics? Any thoughts on this explanation for the World of Darkness? I like it because it makes several of the cosmologies relevant to understanding the world. Just that Werewolves don't know why they had to kill Father Wolf and Mages don't like to admit that they were massive idiots who screwed everything up.

          Consider that the Sundering (presumably) affected the world through all Arcana - it's possible that Father Wolf weakening (and eventual death) occurred long before the event that caused his weakness.

          Perhaps the earliest of what would become the Pure spent their first years/decades/centuries trying to work out how to prevent the magical cataclysm that was still to come, and those who would be Forsaken pushed for the event. For true time-bendy stuff, perhaps the actions of the Pure failed in a way that caused what they were trying to prevent.

          It may not be canon, but I could see some interestingly brain-twisting games come out of it.
