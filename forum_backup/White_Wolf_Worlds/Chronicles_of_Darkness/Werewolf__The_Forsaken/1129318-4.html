                    Is it weird that is easier for me to imagine a radical shift in my mindset caused by being roused to a spiritual metamorphosis into a ruthless hunting and killing machine via arguably arbitrary cosmic forces than being selected by an immortal blood drinker to be initiated into the Night Society?

                    Fear is a major motif in my life, and understanding it and its power is something I've dedicated a large portion of my life to even if I am not someone who wields intimidation tactics in my interpersonal or professional relationships. I am at heart a storyteller and a student of fear, with a technology skill set and native understanding of human propensity for making the world worse off.

                    Imagine a world where some actions had metaphysical consequences in the same way dumping toxic sludge and scum into water reservoirs in the material world would. That's the world Chronicles of Darkness takes place in, from the Hisil savvy point of view. It has an entire invisible ecology being subject to human disruption and contamination, and we know how well humans take care of ecosystems they are aware of, imagine the affect they'd have on one they could easily affect but didn't even knew existed.

                    All of that adds up to being most inclined to be a Cahalith, and choosing/fitting best with the Iron Master Tribe.

                    A Iron Master Cahalith that shares a lot in common in his hunts as serial killers do with theirs is how I 'd imagine the First Change would affect me.
