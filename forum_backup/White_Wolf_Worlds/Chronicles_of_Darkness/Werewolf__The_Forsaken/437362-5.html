    Well, Hellions are rare, and tend to be powerful, so I don't think you'd just bump into one on accident. Either they're causing a ruckus, or you're seeking them out, or both because of the first. Right.

    For controlling a werewolf, as a mage per se, I'd focus less on direct damage and more on environmental. In my (albeit limited in scope. I've run a single Mage chronicle...so not much) personal experience, mage attacks work best when enhancing mundane attacks, ie. super bullets, knife that cuts trucks in half, or by making the environment dangerous. With a limited dice pool, and Uratha either getting pretty decent resistances or having to actually hit them with it in which case they get their defense, you're not going to do enough to even slow them down. I see the Resistant Damage being a cool idea, especially if you could use Matter and make bullets do it. That is, bullets you could fire through a Space Window or from a mile away. Otherwise, you're not going to deal enough to slow them down. Our Pack's basic Rahu has 14 Health levels in Gauru form. No way is a mage going to touch that with a spell.

    However, if you make the street into concrete quicksand, make the air so thick they need to swim through it, give them a friction curse, or sick the dead ghosts of their lost loved ones that they killed in deathrage on them, that might have a stronger effect. I also think using Spirit to give yourself a spirit rank, and then commanding small groups of spirits to deal with the Uratha, who they dislike already, could be effective. A couple small rank 2 spirits can still down an Uratha if they dog pile it. Just need to separate it, get the jump on it, or focus fire.

    I feel like one of the previous posters noted this well: A mage who gets into direct conflict needs to get out. A mage needs preparation, planning, and a side door entrance to be effective, and when dealing with Uratha, will probably take advantage of every single possible beneficial opportunity, as Uratha have something of a reputation.

    Could Mind magic control Wolf Blooded? I feel like using loved ones and non-Uratha pack members would be a major way in for a Mage.

                                                            To the extent white room scenarios are being discussed, it should be noted that one of the greatest advantages that mages possess is the ability to inflict aggravated damage at a distance, while also maintaining sufficient versatility to effect a quick and safe escape from most situations and plan a more appropriate counterattack at their discretion and with proper preparation.

                                                            Nevertheless, any truly wise mage should know better than to engage a rampaging werewolf in melee combat.

                                                            While we are speculating on anti-werewolf combat techniques for mages, I would suggest utilizing simple levitation spells, and at higher levels of Forces, gravity spells. Werewolves may be tough to kill, but there are extremely few circumstances where they can fly. Even a Blood Talon Rahu in basu-im can do little damage if he's floating a few feet in the air. Also, any mage with sufficient levels of Spirit and less than cordial relations with the local werewolf packs should make friends with Helions. Lots and lots of Helions.
