                                Alright, here's what I came up with. I didn't include form modifications, because I couldn't think of them. But the basic information on them was done.



                                Noble Wyrms: There are none still living who remember the origins of these ancient and powerful beings. Carrying the blood of dragons within them, their true kin appear to have left this world long ago. If traces of their progenitors remain on earth, they have not been discovered by archeologists yet.

                                The appointed role of the Noble Wyrms is to be judges over the world. To those who are found worthy, they deliver the blessings of the heavens upon them. To those who are unworthy, they face retribution for their crimes. Over the centuries, these have given rise to stories of seemingly helpless beggars and traveling salesmen asking for shelter or peddling their wares. Those who treat them generously are granted a reward for their kind hearts. Those who abuse them are punished severely for their lack of mercy. In recent memory, the Noble Wyrm's have had to lay down retribution less often than they did in the medieval era. The existence of soup kitchens, charity drives, peace corp. and other good will organizations has provided help to those in need when such needy people would be treated as third class citizens in ages gone by. The Noble Wyrms view this with hope that their work has begun to pay off, and with further guidance from them, it will get better over time.


                                The Noble Wyrms are a collection of breeds from around the world, primarily involving those of Aisan and European folk lore. Among the shifter community, there are tales of lesser reptiles who bow in reverence to the Noble Wyrms, attributing them to the ranks of nobility and perhaps even godhood. Another note of their regalia is the fact that they are prone to vast hordes of wealth and knowledge. Noble Wyrms can have entire rooms of their household dedicated to stock piling volumes of books, or treasured artifacts. These artifacts are usually granted to those who are found worthy of reward, but anyone who attempts to steal them will face the dragon's wrath!


                                Noble Wyrms have incredibly long life spans. If they are allowed to live in peace and maintain their health, they can live for thousands of years. The cause behind this lifespan is probably due to the extremely low fertility rate the Noble Wyms possess, but we will get to that momentarily. Suffice it to say, Noble Wyrms are one of the rarest breeds of shifters on the planet.


                                --------------------------------------------


                                Reproduction: As just mentioned previously, Noble Wyms do not have a very high birth rate. Although not in the way you may be thinking. During their great life span, a Noble Wyrm can have dozens, possibly even hundreds of children depending on their sexual activity. However, in all that time, only about seven offspring will undergo The Change and become Noble Wyms. By the time they finally do produce a 7th Weredragon, they are too old to have anymore children, or merely lose interest in trying.

                                Because of it's rarity to manifest, Noble Wyms are extremely protective of their children who do undergo their First Change. Until the child is strong or smart enough to take care of themselves, their parents will defend them to the death if need be.

                                When the child grows mature enough, they usually leave for new lands in search of potential mates. Probably because half the families within a 100 mile radius are distant relatives of theirs, and that would be incest. It may very well be impossible to tell how many families today carry dragon blood in their lineage.


                                Another thing that should be noted is that Noble Wyms tend to have very specific kinks when it comes to breeding. Some of these are merely eccentric and others are life threatening. The stories of dragons who only take virgins to their bed is one of the most well known. Other stories tell of Noble Wyms who mate during free fall, their bodies plummeting to the ground as they reach climax. The strange or extreme nature of these mating rituals is likely another cause behind their low fertility rate, as finding a willing partner can be a bit of a chore.



                                -----------------------------------


                                First Change: The first change happens differently for Asian and European dragons. In Asia, the First Change is triggered by means of obtaining great wisdom, often following an epiphany of sorts, or perhaps they obtain enlightenment at a young age. The illumination of this knowledge awakens the dragon within their soul who moves to investigate the source of the disturbance. Bringing with it the First Change when the dragon momentarily takes over.


                                For European breeds, the First Change is provoked by intense emotion, similar to other Weres who undergo their first change. But it is mostly focused on witnessing injustice and the culprits being allowed to get away with it one too many times. The Noble Wyrm screams from within, demanding retribution be carried out, and their conscience is only too happy to oblige. The First Change may come in response to vigilante justice, as the dragon fulfills the role that society incompetently refuses.


                                The First Change for Noble Wyrms is unpredictable in when it can occur. Sometimes it's as early as childhood, other times as late as old age. When it does happen, however, the extended lifespan of the Noble Wyrm is activated. Leading to stories of men and women discovering the secret to immortality, and children who possess wisdom far beyond their years. In ancient times, such children were even known to ascend to the throne, becoming child emperors and kings before such systems were abolished in modern society.


                                ----------------------------------

                                Available Breeds:


                                Qilin - The hooved dragons of Chinese mythology. Those who can look upon a Qilin's true form and not go mad are said to become wise scholars, or illustrious rulers beloved by their people. Known as "Kirin" in the regions of Japan, they are sometimes called the Unicorns of China.


                                Celestial - Sky serpents of Asian mythology, who are said to be able to control the weather. They possess the gift of magical flight, and their blessings grant others strength, power, or good luck. Their anger brings about natural disasters upon those who displease them.


                                Draco Splenden - Water dragons attributed to tales of giant sea serpents. They are the most beautiful of the European breeds. Although they can not actually breathe underwater, they do have the capacity to stay submerged for several hours before needing to surface for air!


                                Draco Rex Cristatus - Earth dragons who lack the iconic ability to fly. They are the largest and strongest of the European breeds. During their life times, they acquire great knowledge, and pass that knowledge onto students they deem worthy.


                                Draco Flameus - Fire dragons who's wrath brings destruction upon the lands of Men. They are the most violent of the European breeds, and also the rarest. Possibly because humans kept sending knights to hunt them down!


                                ------------------------------------------

                                Forms: Unlike most breeds, Noble Wyrms possess only two forms. The human form, and a Dire Beast form.


                                Human - The human form of a Noble Wyrm has one of two possible appearances. Either one of great wealth, prestige, and beauty, or one of great poverty, scorn, and hideousness. The later form is used to test the hearts of others and discern if they are worthy of the gifts the dragon can offer. The prior form is used to inspire, advise, or lead society towards a better path.


                                Dire Beast - The true form of the Noble Wyrm is an enormous reptile larger than almost any land animal. They can stand as tall as a house when on all fours, and measure between 20 to 40 feet long from nose to tail. The serpentine dragons such the Celestials and the Draco Splendens are the longest species, while the Qilin are the smallest. When in this form, Draco Flameus gain wings and the ability to fly, while Draco Splendens gain fins and enhanced swimming capabilities. The Dire Beast form is naturally quadrupedal, but the European breeds can stand on their hind legs in exchange for a loss in m                                ovement speed.




                                ---------------------------------------------------------------------


                                Rage: Noble Wyrms are creatures of justice and wisdom, so when they enter a rage state, they do so without remorse or hesitation. When enraged, a Noble Wyrm taps into the pain and suffering of innocents who have yet to be compensated for their losses. They also tap into the remorse and shame of those for who the opportunity for learning has been lost to them, or the act of knowledge being turned to destructive applications. The indignity of a crime gone unpunished, or the shame of clinging to ignorance fuels the Dragon's wrath, and they turn it upon those who are guilty of depriving the world of such. When a Dragon is enraged, they do not stop at the ring leader of the crime, they seek to punish all who had a part in it.

                                In times past when the Draco Flameus were more numerous and society more ignorant, their rage caused them to burn down entire countrysides and gorge themselves upon armies of soldiers until they felt they had purged everyone responsible for the deed, or until a Hunter was sent to slay them. Since these ancient times, Noble Wyrms have saw fit to keep their rage more narrowly focused, but it is no less terrifying than it was thousands of years ago.

                                            Thanks, I was hoping I did good. I couldn't think of tribal names, so I grabbed an idea commonly attributed to western Dragons: Latin!


                                            Qilin Form: The smallest of the Noble Wyrms also has the most exotic looking animal shape. The Qilin is depicted as having the hooves of a horse, the head of a tiger, and draconic scales covering its body. It also has a pair of small antlers regardless of gender.


                                            Celestial Form: The iconic serpents of Asian mythology. Celestial Dragons, as they are popularly known, have a snake-like body, with short legs ending in 3 toed claws. While in this form, a magical organ located in the brain gives them the power to fly, enabling them to soar above the clouds and remain out of sight of the mortals below. These dragons tend to have a pair of blunted antlers and whisker mustaches are common as well.


                                            Splenden Form: The giant sea serpents of European folk lore. Draco Splendens have no arms or legs, rendering it impossible for them to use human tools while in their animal form. They do, however, possess tremendous body strength, able to snap ships (excluding military vessels) in half with their coils. Although this form can slither about on land and breathes air, it has evolved for life in the water and is not as agile when removed from it's preferred environment. This form has fins along it's tail, head, and body which make for better steering and propulsion in the water.


                                            Rex Cristatus Form: The towering lizards of European folk lore. Draco Rex Cristatus are the largest of the non-serpent forms. A giant lizard that can weigh several tons and smash through rock like it was wood. Their scales make them tough as tanks, but they're about as slow as one too. What they gain in raw power, they lose in mobility.


                                            Flameus Form: The iconic flying lizards of European folk lore. Draco Flameus manifest a set of leathery wings that can support them in the skies. But a giant, flying lizard is rather conspicuous in densely populated areas. In order to gain the ability to fly, Flameus are smaller and lighter than Rex Cristatus, and become much more agile than their ground dwelling counter parts.
