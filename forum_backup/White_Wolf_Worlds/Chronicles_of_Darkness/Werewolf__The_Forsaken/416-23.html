    Originally posted by Papa Bear View Post
    B) Father Wolf never had anything about the right to mate.
    Ray is monofocusing on this bit from the corebook's section on Harmony that's never mentioned again thereafter:

    "Once, only Father Wolf had rights and privileges to take mates, privileges he denied to his werewolf children. It’s said that Father Wolf even lorded that right over the People, taunting them with his authority. Denied and insulted, the People claimed the right from their harsh Father and slew him."

    Astute readers will notice that a) this reading has more to do with Freud's notion of the Oedipus complex — the pseudoscience he was blathering about — than the actual myth of Oedipus — the First Pack knew who Urfarah was, no prophecy was involved save for the supposed nature of Father Wolf's ban, and Luna's involvement in the common tale is limited to having the first werewolves sired upon her by Father Wolf and cursing the species after his death, which is hardly analogous to marriage, much less sexual relations — and b) the reading of this as the reason the First Pack slew their father is in contradiction to the story Ray is claiming as monolithic.

    Resident Lore-Hound
