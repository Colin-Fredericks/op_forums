                                            I like what I see in these forms. My thoughts on things about:

                                            Intermediates v. Bullets
                                            As I gather the point of this rule is to make it so much so that the Gauru isn't the go-to way to fight guns and that werewolves fighting werewolves are just as well using claws and such against one-another than bother with uzis. Silver bullets hurt as-is (it sticks to full) and I think that's the main point. It makes it so Dog Soldiers makes more sense to me at least. No amount of machine guns matter unless they're silver rounds or they're tanks.

                                            I also expect the silver claws Facet to be about again still (probably in Blood Talons somewhere). This probably will result in the tools werewolves use to kill one-another.

                                            Gauru Time Limit
                                            With how potent this form is, I am not too against how it has a time limit now. If it's going ot remain short, make it the sort of thing you smash the button to kill the shit out of something and risk that frenzy for. That you can't just hang out in this form is nice too since well, I'm not interested in playing the form up as some harmony of man and wolf, but more a wolf-spirit-man killing machine. This does that well.

                                            WIllpower to Fight
                                            I think I missed this rule, where is it? That being said, I do think if you are not someone subject to Down & Dirty, you probably should be able to fight the werewolf. Its stats alone will probably make that person have to work hard to survive it, especially against a pack.

                                                            FYI guys Stew replied saying healing is based on Primal Urge with the example;

                                                            ’ll get into this when we cover Primal Urge, but at high PU you might regenerate say 4 bashing/turn. For 1 Essence, that becomes 4 lethal/turn.

                                                            Personally really like most of what I see here. I'd like to see Urhan base it's defense on >Wits or Dexterity instead of lesser of. Also I completely disagree that Urhan is pointless. It's the only form that allows for interrupting actions. That's pretty huge. And Speed as a dice pool instead of Dex + Athletics is again, a pretty sizable advantage.

                                                            What I like most here is that they are getting away from attribute bonuses in the forms. While it makes sense in Forsaken, it's always a bit of a pain in the ass to track how much Strength or Health you have at any given moment as you transfer between 2-3 forms every fight and then calculating it all back when the last foe drops. Though I will miss (assuming it's gone) 9-again in Dalu/Urshal and 8-again in Gauru.
