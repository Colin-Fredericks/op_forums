      Sumerian is an enigmatic language for several reasons.

      1) It is the language of the culture that first developed writing. The Sumerians had a number of advancements that conspiracy theorists today will claim came from an otherworldly, higher source.

      2) The culture that spoke Sumerian had gods that were believed to have created humanity as slaves. Not children, not inheritors, slaves. Sound familiar?

      3) It's one of the oldest civilizations of the world, and one of the first to pop up in the Fertile Crescent, the birthplace of modern agriculture.

      That being said, First Tongue is based on Sumerian, but it isn't quite Sumerian. Rather, it is a proto-language based on a semi-"theme park version" of Grimm's Law, where words from Sumerian are combined, swapped out and given new meanings. Furthermore, nobody speaks Sumerian anymore (except if you pick it up as a hobby), so you avoid cultural appropriation (or at least intend to) by using a dead language as your fantasy language. oWoD already had a problem with giving racial groups inherently having mystical powers simply because they were that racial group, and providing a stereotyped "theme park" version of that group.

      That, and finding resources on the Siberian shaman languages can be difficult; these are cultures that are not as well documented or well-known as things like the major ancient civilizations. Believe me, I have tried.
