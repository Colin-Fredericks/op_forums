    Originally posted by Seidmadr View Post
    Because it's boring. It locks down choice. If one wants to be an effective combatant, you have to go Brawl.
    No, it doesn't lock down choice. You can make fetish swords and guns. Nothing stops you from being a silver bullet sniper, or specializing in knife fighting with Dalu. There are plenty of effective choices. And even then, you're confusing what effective means. Effective combatant just means you're competent, not a combat god. And the most effective way of a combatant? Banes and bans. Everyone has a weakness, and specializing in that weakness is the best way to go. Silver, rituals to increase your spirit rank, Claimed exorcisms, fire.

    What I think you mean is "if one wants to be a combat munchkin," which is an entirely different matter. No one is forcing you to buy all these Gifts with the limited amount of XP you earn- there are other areas you can invest in, such as Fighting Styles, Professional Training with a Firearms or Weaponry skill focus, defensive tactics, or being all assassin-backstabby. Or, horror of horrors, spread your experience out among other fields instead of a laser focus on combat ability.

    Here's the thing. Attacking with fang and claws is classic. When most people think "werewolf attacks," they're thinking of using power of the wolf to attack with. Wolves aren't exactly renowned for opposable thumbs and using tools, they're famous for shapeshifting. So, naturally, that style is going to have a lot of attention over the others.

    But you still have access to all the mortal abilities and merits, and there are a large number of merits and Gifts that work with any werewolf form, plus fetishes. There are plenty of viable options.

    I'm just a bit sad that one has absolutely 0 reason to do cool stuff like tear down a lamp post and smash some guy with... because you'll do more damage just ripping him up with claws.
    I suppose I'd have liked it a bit more if that facet had been more like how the Vigor discipline works. Enhancing strength-based attacks in general.
    You can already do both. Plus? Werewolves aren't vampires - why would you want to act like one? Vampires have their areas of expertise, wolves have theirs.
