                    BAT
                    To the Vinca, Bat is a mystery wrapped in an enigma—a divine being, yes, but one who flits through existence without warning. Bat is here, and then gone again. Bat surprises, tricks, causes change, but never stops, always moving on. The Pangaean is a traveller, roaming all the world where its Fleshly kin dwell, dipping into the lives of humans only briefly in pursuit of its desire for transgression and change. The Vinca do not worship him; they fear and respect him.

                    Bat is a great, warping mass of darkness, fur, and eyes—innumerable abstract impressions of a bat pressed into the same spot over one another. Its manifold leathery wings branch through close branes of reality, and its shrill utterances rebound in both directions through time; Bat’s presence is forewarned by this prior echo, and it still speaks and talks even once it has gone from a physical place. In some ways, Bat seems to pale beside the great bulk and monstrous appearances of other Pangaeans, but in truth it can reach to everywhere it can be heard.

                    The Pangaean rarely stays in one place for long. It prefers to be eternally travelling, following the twilight of dusk or dawn. It loves the sun and the moon, and likes to chase their falling light in playful glee. It likes other liminal spaces as well—beaches, shallow caves, the edges where cultivated land meets wilderness. Bat likes crossing those boundaries, and likes others to do so as well. Where it chooses to dally with humans, it is to persuade them to cross such boundaries as well—and not always physical ones. To see a sentient being step across a line that is cultural, social, or mental, is just as pleasing to its alien mind.

                    Bat, Sable and Transgressive
                    Rank 5 Pangaean

                    Power 12 Finesse 15 Resistance 13
                    Willpower 10 Essence 50
                    Initiative 28 Defence 12 Speed 58 (Flying)
                    Size 13 Corpus 26

                    Influences: Bats 5, Transgression 4
                    Numina: Awe, Drain, Emotional Aura, Entropic Decay, Ghost Eater, Regenerate, Speed
                    Arcana: Space 5
                    Ban: Bat must must offer audience and refrain from hostility to anyone who comes to him with an offering of their blood, he she is not bound further if the offer of audience is refused or if the visitor acts against her.
                    Bane: A leash or bag made from the hides of three hundred and thirty three bats.

                    Divine Bleed: The first time in a scene that he suffers Corpus damage, Bat may reflexively use the Awe, Dement, Emotional Aura or Hallucination Numina for free. He may also use the Create power of his Influence (Bats) for free each time he suffers damage, or he may create a Rank 1 bat spirit instead.

                    Echo Presence: In the scene prior and after Bat is present in a location, it can communicate and make use of Shriek Reach to interact with a location. These are ripples through time; the Bat taking the actions is the one that is present in the location, not the contemporary Bat a scene before or after.

                    Guzzle: When dealing damage in a grapple against a target that has blood in their body, Bat must drain the blood out to deal up to an additional 5L and gain 5 Essence in the process. Entities with Vitae also lose up to 5 points of Vitae this way. If Bat kills a target in this way, every character who is a direct blood relation or who has blood sympathy with the target also suffers 1L, regardless of distance.

                    Liminality: Whenever in a liminal location—the band of twilight at dusk or dawn, the boundary between land and sea, etc.—Bat gains access to Forces 5.

                    Lord of Bats: Bat is aware of the health and emotional state (basic information like fear, pain, hunger, etc) of all bats within three hundred and thirty three miles of his location. If there are no bats within that area, he suffers a -3 penalty to all of his Attributes. By touching a dead or incapacitated creature of at least Size 4, Bat can cause a number of newly-created bats equal to the creature’s Stamina to tear their way out of the body and into existence.

                    Monstrous Form: Bat has Armour 13, and may spend 1 Essence to shrug off any Tilt he experiences. These benefits do not apply to attacks from his Bane.

                    Overwhelming Presence: A mortal human who encounters Bat suffers an Integrity breaking point. If this roll is failed, the character gains the Madness persistent condition.

                    Shriek Reach: Bat can directly physically interact with any location that it can currently be heard in, including making physical attacks.

                    Transgressive Sustenance: When a human being commits a transgression against their cultural or social mores or against their own Vice or Virtue in the presence of Bat, Bat regains 1 point of Essence and the human gains the Inspired Condition. If Bat spends 1 Willpower point, he may also initiate a Clash of Wills against the character whereupon, if successful, he may take their Vice or Virtue and consume it, giving him the rote quality on his next use of the Space Arcanum. The lost Vice or Virtue will not return—rather, after a week, the character gains a new, different Vice or Virtue that reflects their recent acts. Bat, meanwhile, can inflict the consumed Vice or Virtue onto another character whom he drinks the blood of within a week, overwriting their existing trait in the process.

                    Unshackled: Any attempt with supernatural powers to immobilize, hold, imprison, or otherwise keep a target in place or prevent them leaving an area made in the presence of Bat immediately instigates a Clash of Wills against the Pangaean. If Bat wins, he may spend a point of Willpower to rip the soul out of the offending character; if he does so, he can bind the soul to the shadow of the character’s intended target. For as long as the soul is pinned to their echo in the world, the target can enter a Clash of Wills against any attempt to hold, imprison, etc them, using Bat’s Rank as a dice bonus to their pool.

                    - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
