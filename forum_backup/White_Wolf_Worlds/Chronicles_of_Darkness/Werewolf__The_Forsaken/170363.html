    [Outline] Revamp of the Surthu Athilal.

    06-15-2014, 11:40 PM
    Surthu Athilal:

    Legends: Mother Sky (midwife) and Father Sun.

    This story is true (aka. Mostly bullshit):

    Long ago, when the world was new. A prideful huntress spoke out, claiming that her ability was above all. That her bow shots found weak points of the hardest of armors, that her blade eased past the toughest of hides. Her reputation spread throughout the land both in the material and the spiritual.
    She bested all of her challengers in contests of wit and cunning. From mortals to even the greater spirits. It was one of these days did a man challenged her to a duel. If she could steal one of the sun's rays within a week, she would win everlasting glory for both her and her decedents. She thought this to be trivial as the sun's rays are always with it when it was shining and took the challenge whole heartily.
    However, the next day, she found the sky was dark with looming storm clouds spread over the horizon, as if a thick layer of cloth had been laid over the sky. Huffing in irritation, she pressed on as surely the clouds had to break at some point.

    Her horse gave out after the third day, fallen from exhaustion. As she walked on, mortal farmers threw insults, The plant choir boycotted her quest and refused her food. The wolves came teeth baring, demanding their moon back and were only held back by their father. Despite all this, she marched on, climbing over mountains and valleys as she continued forward.

    On the last day, She finally came to the highest mountain. Wicked storms blew around it, threatening to skin helpless travelers alive. She continued with the spirits of the storm watching and judging her progress all the while.

    In the twilight hours of her challenge, the spirit of the storm approached her. She used every trick that she had. Threatening them with imprisonment, Gloating them with essence. Even cried out for them to claim her if they would only part the skies.

    The answer was as quick as it was thunderous. The mightiest of them, the Mother Sky herself, want her descendents to feel the rage of the storm within their lives. As quick and relentless as they themselves are. With little option, she agreed and the clouds parted.

    Upon that final hour, she caught the last of the Helios's rays as they feel upon the earth and collapsed upon the snow covered peak. She had won.

    When she awoke, she found herself in the care of the strange man who revealed himself to be none other than the Helios himself. They embraced on the summit of the world. After sometime, they emerged from their shelter to look upon the vast expanse of land. Helios whispered in her ear.

    “Spread your wings and fly mighty huntress. For you and you're children shall inherit the skies and all the high places my rays touch”.

    With that she jumped into the expanse and took on the form of a great eagle. Her sight empowered ten fold. She saw the ants marching on the plain below.

    She saw the father of wolves leading the pack, stalking their prey. She tested her new prowess by giving a quick swipe to the ones that bore their teeth before fleeing upward to laugh at them. She returned to the summit and to the arms of her lover.

    Many months had passed when she started giving birth to the first of her brood. She wondered about the eggs when her lover whispered “Go down to the mass of humanity. Find the mightiest of hunters, the ones of insatiable fire, and bring them into the fold. From my post, I will mark them. From their abilities, they will be known to me”.

    With that, she leap and continued with her quest. From the noonday sun, she took the warriors and the hunters of war. From the dawning sun, she took the artists and the hunters of inspiration. From the dying sun, she took the scholars and the hunters of knowledge. From beyond Helios's sight, she took in the thieves and hunters of men.

    When she was finished, she looked upon herself and found a crone looking back. Her youthful beauty had fled her, her natural ability leaving her. She longingly looked to her lover for guidance. Of which the Sun God replied “As is moral life's destiny to wither and vanish, so is it the fate of my children. However, as it with me to die and be reborn, so too will you, my love. But my children must have their names known to me to have such a gift bestowed to them for it for as mighty as I am, I require a test of one's will to prove that they are worth of being called my descendents.”

    With that, the Huntress instructed her brood in the ways of her and their father. When she died in a middle of a lesson, her brood were distraught and left here there as they decided among themselves what to do with the body. When she came to them the following morning, full of youth and vitality, they were speechless. She spoke out loud the covenant that Helios made with her and by proxy, them.

    Within a year, their instruction was complete. She then turned toward her children and said “Fly now. Have your Father know you by your deeds as I know you by your forms.” and they left her, flying out to the far flung areas of the Shadow to claim their territory and by proxy, their deeds.

    Curse: none, they were simply kicked out of the nest after they aged.

    Drawback to them: Weakness to disease/Lowered lifespan (their age 60 is our 90).

    Breeding: use of procreation is simple. The two mate and produce an egg. The egg is hollow but is used on a human who is then pushed off a high place that activates the change. A male/female mating with a real eagle produces the hollow egg as well while mating with a human simply produces a normal human.

    Usually during birth, the female produces one to three eggs which then she instinctively hoards and starts the process of selecting the right people to revive the “gift”. They aren't above kidnapping.

    Gifts: share most of the same gifts as the Uratha but more of a focus on weather related stuff.

    Auspices: on positions of the sun/Renown

    Noon: Fighter-Purity

    Dusk: Scholar-Wisdom

    Dawn: Artist-Glory

    Night: Thief/Scavenger-Cunning.

    Society: Mostly isolated. The Surthu Athilal are notorious for being most antisocial with the exceptions of mated bonds and possibly groups of three to four (adapting the Harris Hawk social structure here). Usually the sire(s) keep the brooding(s) around until their first year but don't really care if they wander off. After that, they are kicked from the group and expected to find their own way in the world.

    Purpose: Hunting, territory, and Glory-Seeking. As part predatory animal, they feel the same urges that the Uratha do when it comes to territory and the like. However, they do not have the same mandate upon keeping the spirit and material world apart. That's someone else's job. Also, due to their rapid aging, they tend to try to accumulate as much Renown as they can so they can be reborn. This has lead to some interesting... conflicts with the Uratha.

    Animal form: a Golden Eagle. Normal sized.

    War form: Instead of the half human, half animal form, the eagle shifters war form is simply a man sized eagle which is more than able of picking up wolves and dropping them from several feet up. You know that bird on the cover of Blasphemies? That size.

    Human form: uses Primal urge as a penalty from picking out the shifter from the crowd.

    Rage: Eagle shifters don't have the same rage that the Uratha have. Instead of having the build up of Death Rage, they have to resist their rage coming all at once and leaving just as quickly. In this system, use Resolve + Composure vs Primal Urge and have the Rage leave as soon as the first amount of damage is inflicted. If the Uratha's Rage is a fire boiling over, the Surthu Athilal's Rage is a flash of lightning. Blink and you'll miss it.

    Regeneration: not as fast as the Uratha's. Due to their nature, the eagle shifters simply don't have the capability of prolonged battles and the like. Their method of operation is to hit with overwhelming force suddenly and violently.

    Natural weaponry: Eagles in the wild naturally attack their prey by going for the neck and either strangling it, severing the spinal column, or attempting to put a talon through it's brain. As a result, one can expect a lot of agg damage to be dealt by these shifters. Mostly focused on the initial blow.

    Tribes: Unknown. Helios as it currently stands does not give these shifters a lot of purpose (opposed to Luna) and while the Uratha population is low, these shifter tend to be lower and more asocial which gets in the way of the formation of Tribes.

    Harmony: Basically the same except for the part of breeding with humans. Given the reproduction process, this is a non-issue.

    Conflict with the Uratha: Several. the Surthu don't exactly have the best history with the Uratha on several fronts.

    First and fore most, they both hunt in the shadow and often compete over prey items.

    Second is while the Uratha have time to gain Renown, the Surthu tend to go out of their way to get it. They feel the clock ticking.

    Third is they can (and often do) wolf blooded of whom they view as "worthy". This can lead to some interesting situations...

    That's all I got for now.
