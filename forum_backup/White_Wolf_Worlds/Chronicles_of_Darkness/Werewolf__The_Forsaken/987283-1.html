                                                    I can't even describe what I'd do for an entire book on WatP-style shifters. Especially if it threw in some hack ideas/possibilities for adapting the rest of the line to focus on more varieties of shifters, or incorporating them into packs. Speaking of, I've heard that The Pack has info on other supernatural beings as part of a werewolf pack, will there be anything on other shifters there?

                                                    I love lists of stuff, so if you just gave me a long list of profiles and story information and stats of various shifter types, I'd be super happy.

                                                    And while I definitely want WatP style shadow societies of shifters, some info on "flukes" might be nice, but I think that'll probably be handled by Deviant.

                                                    On the topic of stuff for players, the only thing that I want for werewolf almost as much as more shifters would be playable Claimed and more stuff for playing mortal spiritualists.

                                                    Other shifters are great because you don't even have to use them for werewolf, they make great localized miscellaneous weirdness to throw at other splats.                                                         There is something I've been curious about.
