    Alright, I wrote up a rough draft for the Werebunnies. I based the sheet off Neo Tiamat's Water-Horse design. Now granted that I don't have Changing Breeds or War Against the Pure, I don't know about the various Renowns and Gifts that non Uratha might have access to, so I left that blank. I'm also unsure whether or not I made the form buffs balanced or not, so I'd appreciate some feedback or advice on how to make them better if possible. I'm also working on Werevultures as the second of the list.


    NOTE: I had originally intended to write all 7 Beasts of Vice in one sheet. However, I realized the idea I had for them was too diverse to do so, and so I've split each of the 7 Beasts of Vice into their own sections.

    ---------------------------------


    Beasts of Lust (Werebunnies): Before the First Change, Werebunnies are people who displayed an abundance of sexual promiscuity. Whores, players, porn stars, strippers, nymphomaniacs, perverts, all of these and more fit the back story of many Beasts of Lust. Eventually their lustful ways attracted the attention of a Spirit of Lust who initiates the First Change either willingly or forcing it upon them depending upon the mortal's reaction to the situation.

    The First Change for a Werebunny is... mind blowing, to say the least. Their body is wracked with ecstasy as they writhe on the ground, tearing at their clothes. Their mind swims with sexual fantasies, their words escape as moans of euphoria, and their vision blurs with tears of joy. By the time the First Change wears off, the Werebunny is usually left panting naked on the floor, drenched in sweat and their lower regions covered in sexual fluids from their own spontaneous orgasms. When they finally wake up, they remember very little about what happened during that time. All they know is that it was fucking awesome, and they want more!


    Werebunnies are notorious for their sexual urges and are constantly looking for ways to indulge themselves. Every time they do so, it produces a newborn spirit of lust, which their Totem can either devour, absorb, or bind into service in order to increase its own power. Every lover they seduce, every orgasm they achieve, every bastard they conceive strengthens their Totem spirit. As such, those who agreed to the change are not ashamed of their actions, for they know that their powers will grow from the experience. For those who had the change forced upon them, however, they begin to feel like sex slaves. Unable to keep their urges under control for more than a few hours at a time, their Totem's voice constantly demanding them to go out and fulfill their duty, and often they don't have the strength of will to resist such compulsions.


    Unlike the Uratha who disguise their tribes as reclusive societies or cultural heritage living on sacred land, the Beasts of Lust make their Locus in full view of the public. They take up residence in night clubs, brothels, whore houses, anywhere that attracts mortals looking for a good time. Their Totem spirits are often housed somewhere inside these Loci, usually underground where they feed on the sexual energy of the patrons who visit while the Werebunnies pose as workers.

    Hiding in plain sight gives the Werebunnies more security than you might think. Their Loci are often hotspots for public entertainment, and unlike the Pure living on the borders of human civilization, a pack of Forsaken can not march in and begin slaughtering the place without causing all sorts of trouble and backlash in the process. The Werebunnies are not warriors, they are lovers, and while they may possess some degree of fighting skill, it usually pales in comparison to the Uratha who kill supernatural threats on a regular basis. They use their patrons like human shields, knowing the Uratha can not openly challenge them with so many people around to act as witnesses. Lunacy may have its perks, but enough people come up with a similar story to one another, and something is bound to stick.


    As with all Beasts of Vice, the Werebunnies have extended lifespans beyond the normal human capacity. Although they are not immortal, they age at 1/3 the normal rate, physically aging by 1 year for every 3 that goes by. This allows them to retain their youth and beauty for longer periods of time, and thus create more lustful spirits for their Totem to draw power from. An elder Werebunny is rare, but can live to see the early 300's before age finally claims them.

    Simply put, the Werebunnies are one of the more passive aggressive enemies the Uratha must face. They would prefer not to fight at all if they had a choice in the matter, but the fact that they are serving dark spirits and threatening the balance of the world means they have to be dealt with, as Luna's will dictates.



    ----------------------------------------------------------


    Appearance: Unlike many other shapeshifters, the Werebunnies do not appear frightening in any of their forms. They're certainly shocking to come across in certain forms, but their bodies seem to be more alluring than frightening.


    Human: In their human form, the Werebunny appears as a very attractive and enticing human. Even if they were ugly in their original human forms, a Werebunny awakens from the First Change to find they have become incredibly sexy. Their skin is unblemished by acne, warts, skin tags, bruises, or scars, they have lost their excess body weight as they slim down to a more attractive figure. Male Werebunnies are commonly known to take on human forms that are nearly androgynous in their physical appearance, consisting of reduced muscle mass, wider hips, and pout lips. It is a common belief that Werebunny males are gay, or at the very least bisexual due to their appearance in this form.

    Even when they start to show signs of physical age, a Werebunny's human form manages to retain its beauty. They only barely show signs of wrinkles, and while their hair might pale a few shades, it never turns gray or white. Their body retain its curvy sex appeal, and they typically resemble an extremely healthy individual for a man / woman of their age. Such vigorous appearances are practically essential to their survival, as a feeble and decrepit body would make it impossible to satisfy their Primal Urge.


    Animal: In their animal form, the Werebunny looks like typical bunny of various species that differs according to the individual. As with most Beasts of Vice, those who were forced to become a Beast of Lust against their will feel more comfortable in their animal form and resort to it as their default shape when not interacting with others.


    Hybrid: In their hybrid form, the Werebunny takes on the shape of a voluptuous and very attractive anthropomorphic bunny. Those who are not stricken with Lunacy might wonder as to how this form could ever be considered threatening or referred to as a "war form." Their hybrid form is roughly the same size as their human form, unlike other shifters who grow into monstrous sizes. The Werebunny's sexual assets receive an increase in the form of an extra cup size for females, and 3 inches added to their manhood for males. Since their body size remains mostly the same, they can usually hide these features with clothing, but by the time they assume this form, they usually aren't too concerned about modesty anymore. Their leg muscles do become firmer and stronger, however, allowing them to sprint great distances, leap with the ease of Olympic athletes, and deliver one hell of a nasty kick to anyone who tries to give them trouble.


    Near Animal: In their near animal form, the Werebunny takes the form of a freakishly large bunny who is 50% larger than their human form. The bunny's eyes display a keen intelligence on par with that of a human, and it seems to have an understanding of human speech and technology when presented with such, but otherwise looks like a giant bunny.


    Near Human: In their near human form, the Werebunny takes a form that resembles something out of a perverted anime series. They gain the ears, tail, and dexterous paws of their hybrid form, but the rest of their body retains its human appearance. Their leg muscles strengthen, but not the same degree as their hybrid state. Many of these features can be hidden with special clothing, so the near human form is good for increasing one's fighting abilities without going full hybrid in public.


    -----------------------------------------------------------------------


    Reproduction: As with all Beast of Vice, the Werebunnies were born as ordinary humans but were infused with the essence of an animal by a powerful dark spirit. In this case, they were infused with the essence of a bunny by a Spirit of Lust. Werebunnies are also the most sexually active of all the Beasts of Vice and are the most prone to conceiving children who 99% of the time are bastards.

    When a Werebunny produces a child with a human, there is a 1 in 3 chance they will inherit a profound manifestation of their parent's Vice. If they produce a child with another Werebunny, this risk increases to a 60% chance for each child produced. Regardless of who they mate with, the child is born as a human, but may have a higher affinity to the spirit world than most. Children who manifest Lust in such a way are more vulnerable to the Totem's influence and are often targeted sometime around puberty to be granted the First Change.

    Werebunnies who freely accepted the change will often raise their children into the tribe like a sort of family religion. Indoctrinating them from an early age into the Spirit Totem's will. Those who had the change forced upon them might attempt to warn their child or abandon them at an orphanage in a desperate attempt to spare them the same fate.

    Marriage for the Beasts of Lust is an alien concept to them. They serve a spirit of lust, not love, and so most of their sexual encounters are done for the purpose of entertainment as opposed to romantic attachments. It is possible that if they are particularly attracted to someone, they may choose to mate with them repeatedly, but once they start to lose interest, they usually moved on to their next lover.

    Incest is also popular among Werebunnies who freely accepted the First Change. The more experienced family members help the new ones adjust to the powerful urges they're feeling until they learn how to properly control themselves. Then they are deemed ready for human interaction, considering how these families often indoctrinate their children from young ages, this also has the effect of making pedophilia a common trait among such families. Which sickens those who rejected the First Change, and claim that those who accepted it have no sense of decency.


    Because they are born as humans, a Werebunny pack can never be truly destroyed so long as the Totem remains. Even if every living member of the pack is killed, the Totem can simply create some new replacements. The only way to truly stop the Werebunnies from creating more of themselves is to destroy the Totem itself!


    --------------------------------------------------


    Delusion: Euphoria - Those who witness a Werebunny in its hybrid, near human or near animal forms or witness it shapeshifting are not afflicted with madness like typical Lunacy. However, their minds are overcome with a overwhelming sense of euphoric attraction and pleasure as they are drawn to the Werebunny. When the effect wears off, it is nearly impossible to recall events beyond hazy, dream-like flash backs, which makes explaining things to the authorities too obscure to be credible evidence.

    Favored Renowns:

    Favored Gifts:

    Rage: Survival Rage - Werebunnies are not warriors by nature, but as with any member of the animal kingdom, they will fight back in desperation if they are pushed far enough. When a Werebunny is forced into a Rage, they are not lashing out in a desire to tear people to shreds, but rather a desire to escape the situation with their lives. They will most likely try to flee the moment they clear a path to do so, rather than stay behind and finish the job.

    Primal Urge: As the servants of lustful spirits, Werebunnies have extraordinarily high sexual desires. They must slake their lust a number of times a day equal to 1/2 of their Primal Urge with a minimum of once a day. If they fail to satisfy this quota, they will continue to lose 1 Willpower each day which can not be regained until they have satisfied themselves. If their Willpower reaches 0 from such a method, they will immediately fly into a Sexual Rage that lasts until double their appropriate quota has been achieved.


    Form Modifications:

    Human - None

    Near Human - +1 strength (leg related actions), +1 Dexterity, +1 Athletics (Running + Jumping), +2 Perception (sound)

    Hybrid Form - +2 strength (leg related actions), +2 Dexterity, +2 Athletics (Running + Jumping), +3 Speed, +4 Perception (sound)

    Animal Form - +1 strength (leg related actions), +1 Dexterity, +4 Athletics (Running), +2 Athletics (Jumping), +5 Speed, +5 Perception (Sound), Size -3

    Near Animal Form - +2 strength (leg related actions), +2 Dexterity, +3 Athletics (Running), +2 Athletics (Jumping), +4 Perception (Sound)

                                    Beasts of Envy (Werevultures): Before the First Change, Werevultures are people who are stricken with jealousy and desire things that those around them already possess. They often come from the ranks of the poverty stricken, the oppressed and the powerless, scorned lovers, and rival siblings. Their jealousy attracts the attention of a Spirit of Envy who initiates the First Change either willingly or forcing it upon them depending upon the mortal's reaction to the situation.


                                    The First Change for a Werevulture throws them into an uncontrollable fit of jealousy. They look at what others have and long to have it for themselves. During the First Change, it is possible they will become so envious, they are willing to kill people in a jealous rage and take the object of their desire. Whether that be food, possessions, territory, or even a lover! When the First Change wears off, the Werevulture awakens to find themselves surrounded by objects they have always valued but were never able to acquire on their own. Most of these objects are stolen, of course, and the new Werevulture may end up having to hide out until things blow over.


                                    Werevultures are jealous creatures who constantly want what others possess. Like true scavengers, however, they wait for an opportunity to take it. If there is somebody that they lust for, they will secretly wait for a break up to occur (or cause one) and swoop in upon the heart broken lover during a time of emotional vulnerability. If they are hungry for food, they have little restraint in eating the table scraps of those who are better fed than themselves, although Werevultures who had the change forced upon them may be disgusted with their actions. If they have a wealthy family member, they might orchestrate their demise, and then show up with the rest of the family when it's time to collect their inheritance. Werevultures very rarely steal from others publicly. Instead they hide in the shadows and bide their time, waiting for an opportunity or they orchestrate one themselves. They are like the vultures circling high in the sky, waiting for the prey to show a moment of weakness, then they swoop down to scavenge the remains.

                                    Initially serving their Totem spirit is time consuming and difficult. Most Werevultures had nothing when they were human, and so the spirit has to devour, fuse with, or bind envious spirits that the Werevulture personally creates. Over time, however, the Werevulture continues to improve their lot in life until they reach a point where other people become jealous of them instead. This begins producing a larger abundance of envious spirits for their Totem to draw power from. It can take years before a Werevulture is able to stake their claim on a particular Locus, as they do not have the power or the resources to maintain control of it on their own. However, as their powers increase and more Werevultues join their pack, they find that controlling Loci becomes easier. These Loci are usually poverty stricken districts where people are in desperate need and yet often ignored by those who have. However, it is also possible for them to create a Loci in places of abundant wealth and prosperity, mostly if these areas are populated by two faced liars who are constantly fighting with each other over land and possessions.

                                    Werevultures feel an instinctive desire to observe their surroundings from a high altitude. If a Werevulture possessed acrophobia when they were human, they awake from the First Change to find themselves cured of such a weakness. Werevultures commonly patrol the city from the roof tops, gazing down upon the streets and alleyways below and tracking their prey without being detected. Like real birds, they are also incredibly difficult to catch by simply chasing after them. The Werevultures can spot the Werewolves down on the ground and usually escape before they can reach the top of the building. The best chance of capturing them is to wait for one of them to come down, often with the help of setting out some bait to attract their attention. Hopefully the Werevulture is too focused on scavenging to notice the Wolves lurking in the shadows, ready to pounce on the grounded bird.

                                    Werevultures may seem like they aren't much of a threat, being simple scavengers who pick up the scraps left over by society. However their true threat lies in the potential danger of what they can do to society. They need humans to be envious and jealous in order to appease their Totem, and when people are envious and jealous for long periods of time, they often become... unstable. Civility is often discarded in the face of desperation, and if left unchecked, a pack of Werevultures can inadvertently cause rioting in the city streets as the desperate citizens begin demanding for a better change to their lives. They have been oppressed and ignored for too long and now they demand to be heard! It is the possibility of unleashing this anarchy and throwing the district into extreme disharmony that the Werewolves fear, and it is for this reason that they hunt the Beasts of Envy.

                                    Ironically enough, the envious Werevultures possess one special privilege that few others get to share, they have the unbridled freedom that comes with flight! Shapeshifters who are confined to the ground look up with envy at the Werevultures who are free to roam the skies, disconnected from the troubles of the world below. It is a gift that they flaunt, for their Totem is able to feed upon the envy it creates.

                                    As with all Beasts of Vice, Werevultures have an extended lifespan beyond normal human capacity. They age at 1/5th the normal rate, physically aging by 1 year for every 5 that goes by. Their extended lifespans means they are left to suffer in their jealous envy for longer periods of time, providing even greater levels of essence and spirits for their Totem to draw upon. An elder Werevulture can potentially live up to 500 years, but virtually all known cases of elder Werevultures display some kind of mental derangement brought upon by extensive periods of desperation, jealousy, and hopelessness.


                                    -------------------------------------------------------


                                    Appearance:


                                    In their human form, Werevultures often resemble pathetic wretches who have a reason to be jealous of other people. In contrast to the Werebunnies who become more beautiful after the First Change, the Werevultures find they have become uglier. Their skin shows signs of premature aging such as wrinkles, baldness, spots, and lack of muscle mass. It is easier for Werevultures to blend in with society's elderly than it is for them to hang around the young and the beautiful.


                                    In their animal form, the Werevulture takes the shape of a larger than normal bird of prey, specifically the vulture. This form is roughly half the size of their human form and grants them the ability to fly! As befits this form, the Werevulture gains incredible perception in the form of sight and hearing.


                                    In their Hybrid Form, the Werevulture becomes a huge, anthropomorphic vulture standing around 8 feet tall. They have a set of wings growing from their back which although they can not actually fly with, they do have the power to glide. Their hands become like a secondary pair of talons, and their face protrudes into a vicious beak that can easily strip flesh from bone. Their neck elongates, providing them greater flexibility and the power to make lunging bite attacks instead of having to grapple their prey.


                                    In their near human form, the Werevulture becomes even uglier than their human form. Their nose extends in length and becomes hook shaped as their skin becomes wrinkled and clings to their withered body. They stand in a hunched over posture and their fingernails become a set of claws, growing two inches in length and developing a curved shape like the talons of a bird. Their voice takes on a hoarse and raspy tone to it, not unlike those who are trying to imitate the squawking of a bird.

                                    Reproduction: Werevultures rarely have the chance to actually breed with others because they are usually not the object of someone's affection unless they exploit their emotions in some way. On the rare chance that a Werevulture does get the opportunity to produce a biological child, it will be born as a human, although it may exhibit a higher affinity to the spirit world than normal.

                                    If a Werevulture mates with a human, there is a 1 in 3 chance the child will exhibit a profound manifestation of their parent's Vice. This chance increases to 60% for each child if they mate with a fellow Werevulture. As with all Beasts of Vice, these types of progeny are the favored targets of their Totem spirit and will more than likely be selected to join the pack, willingly or not.

                                    It is far more common, however, that Werevultures are single generations. Meaning they have no prior or subsequent connection to Werevultures, because the rarity in which they find willing mates. When a Werevulture attempts to find a mate, they almost never go after someone who is available. Instead, they look for someone who is already taken, because their jealousy compels them to seek out the fortune of others and find some way to take it from them. An open, willing, and eager mate runs the risk of making a Werevulture content, and that is something which their Totem simply can not allow. So they guide the Werevulture's affections towards something better suited to their tastes, often without the Werevulture even knowing about it.

                                    Like all Beasts of Vice, the threat of a Werevulture pack can not be truly eliminated so long as their Totem remains active. Even if every member of the pack is killed, the Totem can simply create some new replacements, so their enemies must destroy the Totem itself to stop their ability to reproduce.


                                    -----------------------------------------------------


                                    Delusion: Jealousy - Those who witness a Werevulture in their near human, near animal, or hybrid forms will suddenly begin to obsess over things which they covet but yet belong to other people. They are so focused on their desires to own these belongings that they ignore what is going on around them and instead may be focused on creating plans, bargains, trades, or heists for how to obtain the objects of their desire. When Jealousy wears off, the subject will come to their senses to realize they have very little memory of what occurred during that time, since they were not paying attention to their surroundings.


                                    Favored Renowns:


                                    Favored Gifts:


                                    Rage: Jealous Rage - Sometimes plans don't work out the way you want them to, or they take too long to get the job done. When a Werevulture feels that they have been denied, oppressed, or suffered too much, they are prone to flying into a murderous envy. During this state, they will attempt to take what they want by force, even if that means they have to kill the current owner to get it. When multiple Werevultures are raging at the same time, it can look like a riot is occurring as they smash windows, loot stores, and beat the crap out of rival lovers.


                                    Primal Urge: Werevultures are scavengers and often the dregs of society. They feed on the scraps left behind by others. As part of their Primal Urge, the Werevultures are constantly seeking to improve their lot in life and rise through the ranks of society. When a Werevulture sees an opportunity to obtain something that they want and does not act upon it, they suffer a -2 on all Physical, Mental, or Social rolls (Story Teller's Choice) for the next 24 hours. This weakness can stack with itself if the Werevulture continues to deny future opportunities, weakening several categories or focusing on a single category to make them guaranteed to fail!


                                    Form Modifications:


                                    Human - None


                                    Near Human - Strength +1, Dexterity +2, Stamina +1, Size +1, Apearance -2, Inflicts lethal damage with talon attacks.


                                    Hybrid Form - Size +1, Health +3, Dexterity +2, Strength +2, Stamina +2, Perception (Sight) +3, inflicts lethal damage with bite and talon attacks.

                                    Glide - The Werevulture in their Hybrid Form is able to glide up to a distance equal to Strength + Dexterity yards


                                    Animal Form - Size -2, Dexterity +1, Speed +2, Perception (Sight) +5, inflicts lethal damage with bite and talon strikes.

                                    Flight - A Werevulture in their animal form is able to fly at a rate of speed = yards per turn.


                                    Near Animal Form - Size +1, Dexterity +2, Strength +1, Stamina +1, Perception (Sight) +5, inflicts lethal damage with bite and talon attacks.

                                            Beasts of Sloth (Wereslugs): Before the first change, Wereslugs are normally people who wouldn't pose much of a threat. They are lazy, unmotivated, uncooperative and have a tendency to do things half-assed. Their lack of effort and enthusiasm attracts the attention of a spirit of Sloth, who initiates the First Change either willingly or forcing it upon them. Usually by force in this particular case!


                                            The First Change for a Wereslug is rather unimpressive. Their mind and body are overcome with an overwhelming sense of lethargy. Their limbs feel too heavy to move, and they can barely keep their eyes open. A Beast of Sloth can awaken from the first change to find they've barely moved from the same spot in the last several hours. And there is also one other trait the Wereslugs awaken to find which more often than not ends up causing them to faint again. After undergoing the First Change, the Wereslug becomes a hermaphrodite, developing the genitals of the opposite gender in addition to the ones they already possessed.

                                            Wereslugs who were male prior to the First Change have a male dominant personality and refer to themselves by the male prefix. Conversely, Wereslugs who were female prior to the First Change have a female dominant personality and refer to themselves by the female prefix. Male Wereslugs do not develop breasts, so it is easy to tell who is "male" and who is "female" among them.



                                            The major threat that a Wereslug poses to the Uratha is not their strategic planning or their combat effectiveness. It is their ability to instil people with exhaustion, doubt, hopelessness. A Wereslug is able to win battles without ever having to lift a finger, draining their opponents of their will to fight back and making them easy prey. A powerful Wereslug can even do this to the Uratha themselves, causing them to neglect their Oaths and fail to uphold their duty to the spirit world! Such laziness creates more spirits of sloth who their Totem can devour, fuse with, or bind into servitude in order to increase its own power!


                                            Out of all the Beasts of Vice, the Wereslugs have the longest extended lifespans and far exceed humans in their longevity. They are not immortal, but they do age at 1/10th the normal rate. For every 10 years that goes by, a Wereslug ages by only 1. Near the end of their lifespan, a Wereslug can approach 1,000 years of age. More often than not, they need this much time in order to accomplish anything worth while for their Totem masters. Wereslugs do not feel particularly enthusiastic to go out and start riling up a pack of wolves. They are more content to indulge opportunities as they come across them, rather than actively seeking them out. As such, it can take years, possibly even decades for a Wereslug to cause any major damage to the area.


                                            Unfortunately, this also makes Wereslugs particularly difficult to track. Most Uratha expect an enemy to be immediately noticeable, either charging in guns blazing or hatching some kind of intricate plan to take over the city. Wereslugs are very mellow and care free, so they pass under the Uratha's radar. However, this is also a manifestation of their Totem's influence. The Uratha do not consider them a major threat (yet), and so they choose to ignore them. A fine example of Sloth, if ever there was one!


                                            At first glance, the Wereslugs may appear to be passive aggressive like the Werebunnies, but this is not the case. Werebunnies are all about having fun, but the job of a Wereslug is to instil people with apathy. Novelists begin to suffer prolonged bouts of writer's block, athletes start to deviate from their training regiments or give up on their diets, police officers become more open to bribery or ignoring protocol in favor of a quicker (though sloppier) method of getting the job done. Construction crews may overlook important safety designs, resulting in unstable and hazardous work environments. Wereslugs have the power to destroy people's lives without having to actually kill them, which makes them more dangerous and hostile than their long eared counterparts.


                                            Because a single Wereslug takes so long to get anything done, it is common for them to exist in large packs. Their Totem feels that if they have more Wereslugs working for them, it will increase their performance rate. The average size of a Wereslug pack measures between 10 to 20 members. Common locations for their Loci include homeless shelters, soup kitchens, back alleyways, and other places where you'll find those who are unemployed beggars who either can't (or won't) get a job. In fact, most of the inhabitants of these places are probably Wereslugs or potential candidates to join the pack.


                                            It should also be noted that Salt is a particularly deadly Bane to Wereslugs. If it makes contact with their skin, it immediately causes 1 Aggravated damage per inch of exposure. If the salt is ingested, it inflicts 1 Aggravated damage per gram that was consumed. Wereslugs must constantly monitor their meals to ensure it has not been seasoned with salt lately.


                                            -------------------------------------


                                            Appearance:


                                            Human: It is a common stereotype that all Wereslugs are obese slobs with poor hygiene in their human forms. While this is certainly the case for many of them, it is not universal. Some Wereslugs are actually quite healthy and fit. Instead their sloth manifests in a lack of attention to detail and favoring the easy path rather than dedicating themselves to hard work and effort. They have the shoot first, ask questions later approach. Wereslugs who live up to the stereotype, however, are surprisingly in tuned with their animal side. They are overweight, slow, have queasy stares, and have poor hygiene as their body gives off foul odors or is permeated by a coating of sweat.


                                            Animal: In their fully animal form, the Wereslug breaks down into a swarm of slugs numbering roughly 10 per rank of spirit power they have achieved (maximum of 50 slugs at Rank 5). The slugs act with one mind, and their attributes are counted for the entire swarm. When all their health boxes are destroyed, the swarm is dead. As long as at least one of the slugs survives, the Wereslug can reform themselves, however.



                                            Hybrid: In their Slug-Man form, the Wereslug's body becomes soft and mushy as their eyes rise out of the sockets and are supported upon two moveable stalks. They grow a massive tail which is roughly twice as large as their body and drags across the ground, reducing their speed as they struggle to pull it. Their teeth recede into their body as their mouth becomes perfectly circular, forming a sphincter shaped hole in their face. Their skin also becomes covered in a thick coating of slime, which they leave a trail of when they move. In other words, they become extremely hideous!


                                            Near Animal: If their Animal Form wasn't already enough to shock people, their Near Animal Form certainly will. The Wereslug takes the shape of an unnaturally large slug roughly 50% larger than their human form. At best, they might be mistaken for some kind of prehistoric missing link. But more than likely they'll inflict normal witnesses with some kind of Lunacy.



                                            Near Human: If a Wereslug wasn't ugly in their human form, they certainly become so in their Near Human form. Their body begins to bloat in size, swelling to obese proportions. Their pupils dialate to freakish sizes, taking up nearly 80% their eye sockets. Their skin also begins to emit a thick, gooey sweat, and their muscles become soft and flabby, rather than the firm tone they might have as a human.


                                            -----------------------------------------------------------

                                            Reproduction: Because of their condition Wereslugs often manifest bisexual tendencies and have even been known to take members of their own gender to be their mates at times. Children who have a Wereslug for a parent have a higher chance of being born with gender deformities than if both parents were humans. Like all Beasts of Vice, their children are born human, but may display a higher affinity to the spirit world than normal.

                                            When a Wereslug mates with a human, there is a 1/3 chance the child will manifest a profound version of the parent's vice. This chance increases to 60% per child if they mate with a fellow Wereslug. Children who display this have a higher chance of being targeted by the Totem to become a Wereslug themselves.

                                            While a Wereslug may be physically capable of breeding, many of them have no interest in doing so. Even if they find someone who doesn't consider them too ugly or lazy and is willing to have a child with them, the Wereslug is often at times not ready for a long term commitment. Raising a child takes work, patience, and a lengthy attention span, something the Wereslugs simply do not possess, and so it ends up becoming more of a chore to them than a blessing when they conceive a child.

                                            Indeed, much like the Werevultures, the majority of Wereslugs are single generations, meaning they were the first in their family to undergo the First Change. All Wereslugs, however, are capable of bearing children, even those who refer to themselves as male, but the male variant has even less interest in doing so than the female variants


                                            ------------------------------------------------------------

                                            Delusion: Disconnection - When someone witnesses a Wereslug in their near human, near animal, or hybrid forms, they are overcome with a sense of disconnection from the situation at hand. Yeah, there's a giant mutant slug in front of them, but they don't particularly care. After all, it's a big world and there are bound to be strange things in it, right? In fact, the witnesses begin to find they don't really care about anything at the moment. They're much more interested in laying down and relaxing, they've had a hard time this month and they haven't been getting enough rest. When the effects of Disconnection wear off, the subject usually finds themselves unable to bring up details about what they saw. Not because they can't remember it, but because they don't feel like it's vital information that needs to be shared. Like most Lunacy rolls, the subject has to make a check to see if they can summon up the willpower to discuss what they witnessed, and even then, they usually share only bits and pieces, rather than the full account.

                                            Favored Gifts:

                                            Favored Renowns:

                                            Rage: Death Rage - Slothful doesn't always mean slow, it can also mean doing things fast and sloppy. When a Wereslug enters into a rage, they attempt to resolve their current problem in the fastest possible manner they have available to them. It's neither pretty nor perfect, but as long as it gets the fucking job done, they don't care.

                                            Primal Urge: Wereslugs are usually easy going and laid back individuals who don't like to exert themselves if they can help it. They grow agitated quickly when they're forced to put forth strenuous effort. As such, if a Wereslug fails to perform the same action 3 times in a row within the same scene, they will automatically fly into a Death Rage as they attempt to force the situation to a speedy conclusion.
