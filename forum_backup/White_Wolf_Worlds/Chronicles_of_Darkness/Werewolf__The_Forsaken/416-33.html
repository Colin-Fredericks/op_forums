                                                Originally posted by Ephsy View Post
                                                Change? sure. Completely divesting the game of stuff like Lunacy, 2 of the five forms, renown, rites? Fuck no.
                                                And if you'd read the thread regarding the changes for -our game-, you'd see where the forms thing was specifically for our chronicle; overall it's assumed many (most?) would want to keep them as innate aspects, and I don't see that as a problem. That's specifically addressing some problems we had in that regard; it's not the expectation that it should be adopted wholesale. Chronicle specific. Having all 5 to me isn't a big thing if they got a differing treatment than currently.

                                                Divesting renown: not as is, not at all. Know how me and the other changed it? We stop using the term renown. What it was is covered by Honorary Rank and Tribal Status. It's a terminology shift, really the only real thing done is not rail-roading gameplay by what was previously required to go up in it, and taking it out as a hard power cap. In mechanical terms? It's just covered by other pre-existing areas. I just don't see much use in the term anymore, it's a redundancy.

                                                Rites? To each their own there. I'm not a fan of a non-nonsensical system that's predicated on "it's anything, and everything that doesn't fit the 1-5 list. Also, stuff" so instead, there are alternate Instinct levels instead of a grab-bag. Seems clean. The focus of what rites are and what they do are tighter; Essence/Shadow manipulation, externalizing the influence of a werewolf on it's spiritual environment where instincts are more the internal. That's not an excision; that's a change of focus and shuffling of what goes where. Also, some rites make more sense as Numina anyhow (see the rite of healing. Healing Numina anyone?), which keeps those aspects -still- accessible.

                                                Lunacy? Some mass human group debuffer? Integrity already covers this in part with some of the Breaking Points it has with humans. The rest is wrapped into the Predatory Aura and treated as something less ridiculously potent to keep humans a legitimate problem, but one that requires -action on the part of the werewolf- to fully exploit, just like with vampires. It's an edge. Want people to take off much more easily? Hit them with the Flight aspect of the Predatory Aura. Want to even more fucking scary? Instincts, Merits, more... I just don't need it overwhelmingly like it was, and it was something my players thought was goofy before. As is the default idea is they travel in packs; that's multiple people capable of whammying multiple others with the aura, those that -don't- do what most humans presented with a situation like that in the wod does: run like hell, rationalize or ignore it.

                                                What you're calling excision is closer to revision, shuffling things around and refocusing. Nothing in what I've tried or my co-ST with our tinkering thus far entirely removes things entirely; even wonky gifts from before are easily kept as Numina of one sort of another.

                                                The real issue isn't that I'm taking stuff -out- of the game, or my co-writer (actually, we're intending to add -more-); it's that it's different enough from the prior iterations that you don't like it due to the level of change present. And as I've said before, brace for impact; the level of differences you've cited as desiring before would barely make for the GMC rules updates and may as well be an errata. I'm expecting heavy changes. In fact, I know heavy changes are coming down the pipe on par with Blood and Smoke at the least.

                                                Prepare to be have your expectations dashed. I wouldn't be surprised if my and my co-st's takes on things don't even go as far, but keep in mind I'm primarily presenting ideas that address problems I've ran into playing or running the game, or ideas that me, my co-st or my players were very interested in. It's going to from that perspective, and it's going to heavily differ if you've been running your games without that sort of experience. In the end you're dealing with an overhaul, not a minor rules update, and no matter how much you stomp your foot on the forums, threadcrap, and pull the equivalent of putting your fingers in your ears going "lalala" because people have differing tastes and ideas on where they want to see the game going is going to avert that. I can agree to disagree. The difference? I'm actually backing up the ideas myself and others have presented as ways to update or adjust the came with -actual constructive content-. Instincts were the tip of the iceberg. The newest draft with other systems is just one portion of a lot more. I don't expect you to like the ideas, I don't expect that level of content from you either (it's a lot of goddamn work), but I don't need the treatment I and others have been getting from you over it when we present something you just don't like, especially when it turns into something ongoing and hostile in tone.

                                                It'd be nice if you did the same instead of constant passive-aggressive jabs at me and others who're trying to present material and ideas, see how they work, learn, revise and otherwise look at the potential of W:tF above and beyond some minor fiddling and stop-gaps. All I have to say on the matter, Ephsy; we have very differing outlooks, obviously, but I'm getting very tired of what you've been doing and I'm not alone in this (so don't you dare right it off as just me taking issue with this as a quick way to dismiss it).

                                                Want to make an impact and change my outlooks or ideas on this? Show -us- what you want to see the game to be like, a consolidated list, some mechanical idea suggestions, some meat, -something- that we can look at collectively (and by we, I mean this forum) and go "Huh, never thought of it that way, that could work better!" or "That's a good idea, lets try that out", or even "That doesn't work for my game, and here's why". As it stands I've seen more pissing on others by you than that, consistently, especially when it's not some echo-chamber with a couple others you see eye-to-eye with. It's been brought up, it has been discussed, and as much as I'd love to continue to contribute on this forum I'm seeing a handful of toxic posters like you making this place very uninviting to do that in. Anything that doesn't directly jive with your personal tastes gets attacked, along with the players who present it though rarely with any meaningful explanation, or constructive criticism. That in of itself isn't fun; it's only worse when there's more people doing it, and there has been for fuck sakes. And when it's not overt insults over it (or mod intervention), it's subtle jabs and pot shots, selective reading and intentionally twisting what people say. You're bringing nothing new to the table.

                                                Until that happens, that is, I see something with some meat to it more than "I don't like what you like and I'm gonna hammer you with it any chance I get", something more than attitude, insults, passive-aggressive jabs, instigation attempts and all the other crap you've been pulling I really don't see further need addressing you..It's going nowhere and it isn't appreciated.
