    To add something else to the suggestion: I feel it was an unfortunate choice to base the dice pool on Resolve + Composure. It creates this strange situation that Werewolves can benefit from having a low Willpower (perhaps unique among the denizens of the World of Darkness), as this makes their Harmony more malleable. In the long run a low Res + Comp score makes it easier to stay around Harmony 6 to 4 through Breaking Points.

    Of course, there are lots of downsides to having a low Res + Comp score, but it still does not feel it is working as intended. I am also having a hard time understanding why it is that dice pool from a narrative and in-universe perspective.

                    @Charlaquin: In your example, having the roll be necessary every time would only make the process take more time, and that only in the breaking towards spirit direction. The person determined to become fleshy by flaunting The Father's Form would, mechanically speaking, have no trouble doing it. Fail a roll? Try again 6 seconds later. The Totemic Empowerment one lasts until sunrise, so that one would be once a day. That said, being a little high on the Flesh side is less severe of a problem than being a little on the low side, so a few extra days might not be a big deal.

                    BigDamnHero brings up a good point, though, that the whole idea of having dice is so the player doesn't automatically get everything they want. My point is that dice rolls are typically there to make that happen. In this case, though, a good roll is a bad thing, which seems a little backwards.
                    Maybe there should always be a roll. But maybe players should declare their intended or hoped-for harmony in advance and rolls towards it should NEED success to break whereas rolls away from it should require success to stay put. Were that the case, then The-Willpower-Pool would still make sense as the dice pool for this roll.
                    saibot commented that, as it stands, Resolve + Composure doesn't make sense for this. If you're rolling that pool to resist a change you're hoping to give in to, then where is this bubble of will coming from? Then it becomes more the Personal-Inertia pool than the actual Willpower pool.

                        Personally, I like the tension caused by the fact that the kind of dice pool you want to have varies with your Harmony. I like that what will help you achieve balance one day, may come back around to bite you when you can no longer stop yourself from falling/rising too far, and successful rolls always giving the preferable result doesn't allow that.

                                        Thing about Detachment rolls is that Resolve and Composure are about a character's mental and emotional focus and stoicism. For mortals, it makes perfect sense for Integrity, as processing the things they've seen requires them to be steely and in control of their emotions, or rapidly descend. Werewolves sorta fit that, in that their "morality" is swingy, and having a solid sense of self will help them steady their course and keep from falling to extremes. Vampires, on the other hand, are set apart from Humanity, and their actual human self is under constant assault by the Curse, by the receding impact of human needs and experience, and that their own growing experience of humanity as a facade colors their experience of everybody else's humanity. So, a high Willpower definitely helps them maintain the facade, but it doesn't help them retain the empathy and connection that Humanity the trait represents.

                                        LFP: Foreign Bodies (Deviant: the Renegades)
