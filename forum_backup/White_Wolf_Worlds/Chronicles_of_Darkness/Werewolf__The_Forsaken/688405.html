    Quick Question: What do Werewolves hunt?

    10-04-2015, 12:27 PM
    Basically, do werewolves hunt 'bad' things or irregular things? Are they ensuring cosmic balance or nibbling on evil? If it is a per pack interpretation, what do the tribes teach?

      They hunt whatever's got the Shadow out of balance. Excess spirits, destabilizing humans, problematic suprnaturals, even other werewolves sometimes.

      Each Tribe hunts a different favored prey:
      • Blood Talons hunt other wolves, usually ones who are making a mess for Forsaken society (read: the Pure)
      • Bone Shadows hunt ephemeral creatures, usually disruptive spirits
      • Hunters in Darkness hunt whatever messes up their turf, especially the Hosts.
      • Iron Masters hunt humans, and are the most at-home in urban areas
      • Storm Lords like to hunt Claimed

          Short answer - EVERYTHING. In 2ED werewolves can even hunt abstract concepts, like "clean streets" of their territory. They even can declare this as Siskur-Dah ( 'Sacred Hunt' ) ritual target. But, most commonly, they hunt spirits, humans and various flesh-spirits hybrids ( like Hosts or Claimed ). It's logical, as Uratha are human-spirits hybrids themselves, so the are most dedicated to humans or spirits side of their nature.

          But in theory - anything can be target of Siskur-Dah.

          Originally posted by Tabanese View Post
          Basically, do werewolves hunt 'bad' things or irregular things?
          Both, depends how you can see them. To the Uratha 'bad' are irregular, as they break normal ecosystem they look for. Uratha can leave, for example, Vice spirits alone in bad part of town. At least till Gluttony, Greed or Lust spirits do not make all residents go to killng rage and by this move the local ecosystem to the unhealthy balance, with new murders and destruction spirits changing the local ecosystem. But as all spirits ( or humans ) are in the end egocentric, Uratha have paws full of work. ( And Chronicles to resolve by this. )


          Originally posted by Tabanese View Post
          Are they ensuring cosmic balance or nibbling on evil?
          More they strive to "cosmic balance" - as hybrid race, Uratha likes things in the middle. See that in 2ED most optimal Harmony is in the center of the scale, at Harmony 5. Enoguth for Flesh and Spirit.

          Still, Malejins - Lords of Vices - are making broad moves to crush anything beside their favorit Sins - so they need to be stop, so some Virtues can blossom. But, in the end, werewolves target "harmony" - of predatory Bodhisattva human-wolf spirits hybrids. But Harmony, none the less.

          Originally posted by Tabanese View Post
          If it is a per pack interpretation, what do the tribes teach?
          It's even per wolf interpretation, but most packs are agreeing on general "spectrum" of what their will Hunt. City packs can hunt mainly humans and spirits, to even the ecosystem. But still one member that is Hunter in Darkness will look over Hosts, they will not invade their territory.

          In 2ED each Tribe has Sacred Prey - the target of their Hunt most worth of their time and dedication. Tribes teach techniques to hunt those beings, so large portion of Uratha join particular Tribe not even from philosophical matters - but practical. If young Rahu lost his beloved wife to the Rat Host, he will surely can join Hunters in Darkness, even if he does not look as the environmentalist or stealthy kind of guy - he just want to know how to fight Rats effectively to take revange, and it's all that is matter to choose this Tribe.

          Sacred Preys for the Tribes of Moon

          Blood Talons - Werewolves - mainly Pure, Bale Hounds or those poor wolf souls that have lost their Harmony on the extremes ( Harmony 10 or 0 werewolves ). But sometimes even other wolves from the Tribes of the Moon.

          Bone Shadows - Spirits - Shadow Realm should be understand and no one knows it better than Bone Shadows, to not make too much fall out from your actions.

          Hunter in Darkness - Hosts - as most disrespecting local territory and almost always making bad things to sacred Gauntlet between Flesh and Spirit.

          Iron Masters - Humans - Thinking Ape is most cunning being and enormously outnumber any other being on Earth. Humans, if they would know, could easily crash any Uratha society. You must be cleverest hunter to hunt inside them and not be caught.

          Storm Lords - Urged and Claimed - unholy merging of both things that should not be whole. Especially, as they look to much like werewolves themselves...

          In our older topic we talk extensively on various subjects of Siskur-Dah and possible targets ( also with devs ) - I advice to read it.

                          There are evil spirits, per sey, they are called Malejins and their lower lot, Malinets. They tend to make Loci in to Wounds and pollute the Hisil.

                          But no - they are not automatic enemies of the werewolves. Tribes of Moon and even Pure fight them as most are to obsessed with their Sins, and by this cross Uratha way of ruling territory. Each pack choose the vision of their "perfect territory", and takes one by one threats to it. Want Order to rule - destroy spirits of anarchy, make humans organisation more prominent, take care of those pesky things coming on "our way". Want Happiness to rule territory? Destroy spirits of sadness, make some activities humans will be joyful around, even invite the Host connected with bliss.

                          It's even why Protectorates are called for in Werewolf 2ED. "Hosts are massively attacking the city? Call the Packs, we need to resolve this, in coordination."

                                          Originally posted by malonkey1 View Post
                                          If it looks like a duck spirit, quacks like a duck spirit, it's probably a duck spirit.

                                          It might not be, but it probably is.

                                          Part of the issue with the Maeljin and wounded spirits is that they do not 'quack like a duck.' They don't act like regular spirits. Even the Banish rite, which pushes spirits out of the real world and into the Hisil, is specifically called out to not push them into the Hisil, but somewhere else.

                                          As explained by a writer, Chris Allen, "So, Wounded spirits aren't 'bad' spirits. They're corrupted. Hatred, anger, death, murder, pain, anguish spirits are all perfectly normal spirits. The creatures that infest the pustules of taint that are Wounds have been infected and changed. A Wounded spirit of happiness would be hurled through a Wound by the Banish rite; an unWounded spirit of hatred would not."

                                          Whether a werewolf decides to hunt them is up to the werewolf. Destruction is part of nature just as creation is, and both can be bad when driven to extremes. Even if things are drastically unbalanced, a werewolf does not have to hunt them. In fact a werewolf might let something like that occur just so they can have a more powerful spirit to hunt in the future.

                                                  The other thing I believe is on the Hunt This, It's Corrupt list: Magath. They're not mentioned much in the 2e core, so I just know what I've picked up here and there - people may be about to correct me on some of this. ;-)

                                                  A Magath is a spirit that's become a strange hybrid by feeding on essence that doesn't suit it. This is quite a conceptual thing - a dog spirit can feed on loyalty spirits and become a loyal dog spirit, or on wrath spirits and become a furious dog spirit. That's fine, both are aspects of Dog, so this isn't a problem. But when a spirit is starved of essence that suits it, it might feed on something that it can't digest properly - like a Happiness spirit stuck in a mouldering wasteland where there aren't any people, where all it can get is Decay essence.

                                                  When this happens, the spirit changes into a weird hybrid spirit. I'm not sure how this manifests (and would welcome someone explaining, I'm curious :-) ) but I'm fairly sure it's something most werewolves see as a breach of the natural order and worth hunting down to destroy.
