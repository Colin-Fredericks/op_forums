                        Originally posted by Ephsy View Post

                        What were the design considerations?
                        Several, but two significant ones very roughly summarised:

                        1) The Endless Furball of Violence - When multiple Uratha entered Death Rage from opposing sides under rules where rager attacked rager, they would then end up in a fight where the constant Gauru regeneration would likely be more than any of them could inflict on their opponent. Combat optimised Uratha might do it but, with raging essentially denying the player the ability to make intelligent use of their capabilities, you ended up with fundamentally boring fights between raging werewolves while everyone waits for the Basu-Im to cease. Who wants to play out 200 combat rounds of Uratha punching each other in the face over and over just in case someone manages to get an edge? This feeds into the second point...

                        2) Death Rage Is Bad - Death Rage is not a go-to fight winner. Doing it to overcome a foe is and should be a massive risk with likely serious consequences. The targeting rules for Basu-Im ensure that the horror remains - you don't get to just flip into it and go 'oh well, it's fine, I'll just be killing the other guys and leaving my pack alone.' When Death Rage spreads, all that werewolf violence goes outwards, not conveniently remains targeted on the people you specifically want dead.

                        - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
