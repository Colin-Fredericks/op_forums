                        Originally posted by nofather View Post
                        I said something about not white rooming it, but here I come with a suggestion.

                        We do have a Play by Post board. And we have access to a Dice roller -> http://rpgroller.com/nwod/
                        It wouldn't be too hard to start up a Werewolf Arena, if someone was particularly interested. We don't know all the rules about werewolf, sure, but the main complaint herein seems to be the combat ability of the forms. It wouldn't be too hard to whip up some humans ranging from lone gangsters to hunter cells and use some existing werewolf write-ups and show your work. You'd probably want to set up an arena, so it wasn't just, well, a white room.
                        White rooms are terrible, yet here I go...

                        Assuming an absurdly average shooter (3's in everything), firing at an improbably average Werewolf (3's in everything, save wits which we'll make 2 for the sake of expediation) who has assumed Gauru form yet against all odds is standing around like a dumbass. Here is roughly how long it'll take to gun down said Gauru with silver bullets (all shooters assumed to be within 20 yards with zero cover of any kind):
                        • Standard Pistol (+1 damage): Gunner has one die per shot, hitting 30% of the time. Thus, for every 10 shots he'll average 6 damage. So ~19 turns to kill the Gauru, ~15 for Dalu or Urshul.
                        • Large SMG (+2 damage, Medium Burst): Gunner now has 3 dice, thanks to the medium burst. 66% chance of hitting for 3 damage, roughly 2 of 3 shots hit. ~11-13 turns for Gauru (don't forget to reload), ~8-9 for Dalu or Urshul.
                        • Hunting Rifle (+4 damage): 1 die again, but way more damage, each shot that hits deals 5 or more. Still ~10 turns for Gauru, between ~5-8 for Dalu and Urshul.
                        • Assault Rifle (+3, Full Auto): 4 dice, and at least 4 damage on a hit of any sort. At a 75% hit rate, it'll kill in 3-4 turns for Gauru, and 2-4 for Dalu and Urshul.
                        Naturally, this requires the white room warning. All the above establishes is approximately how long it'll take to gun down a Werewolf with silver in assorted forms given assorted weapons, while he dances about and makes no effort to attack. It does not account for cunning on either the shooter or Werewolf's part, nor does it factor in potential Willpower usage vs Dalu or Urshul (Willpower cannot be used vs Gauru), Gifts, Merits, or other supernatural influences. This will never, ever occur in play, however a few conclusions can reasonably be drawn.
                        1. Pistols aren't gonna help you. Even loaded with silver and held by an expert, it's still going to take a several turns for a pistol to put down a Werewolf, in Dalu, Urshul or Gauru form. Mind you it's possible, a skilled shooter with good defense vs a not-so-combat-savy Werewolf can probably win the day, even in close combat. Though we'll have to wait for Death Rage rules to see. However, even then they'll want to bring a bigger gun.
                        2. Numbers are your friend. Even the biggest weapon, held by a serious expert won't be killing a Gauru wolf in one turn. Not unless he catches them in Hishu or Urhan form. A group of hunters, all firing in unison is a far better option. The sustained fire from different directions will erode the Werewolf's defense and give each subsequent shooter better damaging odds. This is pretty much the only way a group of hunter will be able to bring down a Werewolf in Gauru form. This or one humongous explosion.
                        3. Ambush is your bigger, better friend. Biggest takeaway, which should be fairly obvious but nevertheless needs to be said, is that a hunters best option is to shoot a Werewolf while she's still a squishy human. Ideally from a safe location she can't cross to in a single turn, with the highest caliber weapon he can find. If the shot is silver, the hunter might just have good odds of putting the wolf down, even if she does hulk out. If it isn't silver...find silver. Gauru is functionally weapon
                        4. Edit: I should note that while this shows a staggering lack of self preservation on the Werewolf's part, the hunter couldn't possibly have a better scenario. In actual play the Werewolf is be guaranteed to be more difficult to kill, barring the introduction of extra shooters, or heavier weaponry. Indeed, I think it'd be safe to say that this above average gunman (3 Dex and 3 Firearms is a pretty good shot), wouldn't be taking down this Werewolf. Not unless he caught it in a lesser form.
                        Final results are that I'm slightly more okay with the firearms defense now than I was when I read it. It still doesn't do shit vs an ambush, which is really how you want to kill a Werewolf in the first place. Even so, I think it remains an unnecessary addition. Uratha have enough solutions to firearms in their arsenal (Gauru is borderline immune, Urhan can preempt, there are Gifts to shutdown guns altogether, and probably some for armor and environmental manipulation) that they don't need to be dodging bullets. At least not all of them.

                        Edit: The numbers without bullet dodging, for comparison.
                        • Standard Pistol (+1 damage): Gunner has six dice per shot, 88% of the time for 1 success, 61% for 2, and 32% for 3. Even so it'll take ~4-6 turns to kill the Werewolf in Gauru, 3-5 in Dalu or Urshul.
                        • Large SMG (+2 damage, Medium Burst): Gunner now has 8 dice, thanks to the medium burst. 94% for 1 success, 77% for 2, 50% for 3, 27% for 4. Average between 3-5 turns for Gauru, 2-4 Dalu and Urshup.
                        • Hunting Rifle (+4 damage): Down to 6, 88% of the time for 1 success, 61% for 2, and 32% for 3. Death is likely in 2-3 turns for Gauru, 1-2 for Dalu and Urshul.
                        • Assault Rifle (+3, Full Auto): 9 dice. 95% for 1, 82% for 2, 59% for 3, 35% for 4. Averages 2-3 turns against Gauru, 1-3 for Dalu and Urshul.
                        If at any point the Werewolf got in close combat, which would be quite easy if the hunter is at the range to shoot unpenalized, defense would again apply and we'd be back to the scenario above.

                                            Originally posted by Nickpire84 View Post
                                            Off hand, can someone tell me where I can find the Down and Dirty rule? I forgot my thumb drive so can't access my PDF's, but could someone tell me what that mechanically means?
                                            Combat boiled down to a single contested action. Both parties roll either a combat dice pool (Strength + Weaponry, Strength + Brawl, Dex + Firearms, Dex + Athletics, etc) or a relevant dice pool for an escape attempt. The losing party takes damage equal to the difference in successes (plus weapon damage) and the winning party achieves what ever their intent was for the combat, which can include killing the losing party.
