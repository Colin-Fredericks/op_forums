        I have run a very sea-focused game as a break from a long-term Werewolf chronicle with my group. I had them build already-experienced werewolves for a plot set around a coastal Cornish village, featuring sunken container ships with strange cargo, eerie dwellers of the deep and spirits of the sea (and the Gull, I love the Gull). Also, a Sea Gift list.

        It was a lot of fun!

        Originally posted by Ephsy View Post

        Such as this fucker:
        The short-term Cornish pack I mentioned above? The final battle of that story arc was against TWO of these buggers on an island off the coast, and they were both buffed up with armour, weapons and further special abilities, and there was also a fishy mage-priest-thing that was multiple Mastery degree in various Arcana. I was heavily riffing off Slaine's Fomori - the Deeps were essentially praetorian guards for the priest.

        Two of the pack died but they took the Deeps and the priest down pretty handily.

        Anyway, I have a general love for things watery, coastal and marine, which will probably come through fairly strongly in the Bristol Hunting Ground in Werewolf 2e, and my Idigam Anthology short story.

        - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
