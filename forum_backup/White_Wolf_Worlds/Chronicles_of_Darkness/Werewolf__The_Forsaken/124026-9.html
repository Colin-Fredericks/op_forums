    Originally posted by PenDragon View Post
    ...here's my full list of Gauru suggestions:
    • Use the Higher of Wits or Dexterity for Defense (Just like natural wolves do)
    • Apply Wits or Dexterity (but not Athletics) as defense vs Firearms (This gives some defense, while keeping bullets at least slightly more effective)
    • Heal all bashing damage and [Stamina + Primal Urge] Lethal Damage at the start of each turn.
    • Claw Damage: +2 (For dishing out nasty conditions)
    • Bite Damage: +4, requires grappling (this gives claws some purpose)
    • When attacking in Gauru count three successes as an exceptional success for the purposes of inflicting Conditions, Tilts, and Grappling maneuvers. (So Gauru can lay on shaken, and arm wrack with ease, and reliably deal damage within the first turn of a grapple)
    • A wolf in Gauru must spend each turn attacking or pursuing his prey. He may prioritize particular prey, even if they are not the closest, but cannot go after non-prey (doing so risks Kuruth). Any non-combat action risks Kuruth. However, if that action is intended to set up for an attack on the subsequent turn, such as stalking around a barn to catch the hunters within by surprise, add +2 dice to the resistance roll.

    I like this a lot - obviously without the full knowledge of Kuruth and other aspects of the book it's hard to fully back any suggestion completely, however these do seem to rectify some of my own concerns presently.

    I've never been a fan of time limits - but I understand why there is one and as Stew is going this route then the +2 resistance roll for setting up an attack seems very plausible.

    I'm not sure if the Bite Damage is slightly too high especially if they were to also follow the suggestion of three successes as exceptional, but I've not played around with those rules myself yet.

    I completely agree with you with the applying Wits or Dex to Defense against Firearms but not Athletics - I think there is definitely a need to lessen the effect of Firearms against Uratha, but whilst a rampaging Garau weaving through gunfire looks great in my head I can't shake the image of the wolf-man doing matrix style bullet-time if they apply full defense. This suggestion really allows to keep the first image and get rid of the second.

    On a slightly different note, but keeping with the same idea - as I commented on the blog I am wondering if there's going to be any specific supernatural merits or gifts that would give additional bonuses to forms, whether they be suggestions from people on the blog (human form getting extra perception/8-9 again rolls for Garou form from Armory reloaded etc) or old favourites such as Partial Change getting a look in.

But the thing that always gets me is that stupid time limit and the limited amount of actions you can take with the Gauru form. It's still the form with "little screen time". What if I want to be in Gauru for the entire Hunt? What if I want to focus on being the ultimate badass in Gauru form? I still can't do that. It's a minor gripe to be sure, but I still would have liked to see those stupid limitations gone. It's still not enough to stop from getting this book though.
