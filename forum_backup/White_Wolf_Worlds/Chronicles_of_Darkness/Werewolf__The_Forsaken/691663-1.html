        Below the thunders of the upper deep;
        Far, far beneath in the abysmal sea,
        His ancient, dreamless, uninvaded sleep
        The Kraken sleepeth: faintest sunlights flee
        About his shadowy sides: above him swell
        Huge sponges of millennial growth and height;
        And far away into the sickly light,
        From many a wondrous grot and secret cell
        Unnumbered and enormous polypi
        Winnow with giant arms the slumbering green.
        There hath he lain for ages and will lie
        Battening upon huge sea-worms in his sleep,
        Until the latter fire shall heat the deep;
        Then once by man and angels to be seen,
        In roaring he shall rise and on the surface die.

Bashing damage is healed before lethal, and all lethal damage must be healed before aggravated damage. Each success heals a level of bashing damage, while two successes can heal a point of lethal damage. The lethal damage healed can be used to mend aggravated damage, but each point of aggravated damage cured costs one additional point of Essence. (Healing two aggravated wounds calls for four successes and three points of Essence.) This Numen may also be used to help a character fight off disease or the effects of poison each success adding to the characters resistance rolls on a one-to-one basis.

The spirit may roll Power + Finesse when grappling instead of Power alone. (This takes the place of any Strength + Brawl rolls; see the World of Darkness Rulebook, p. 157). If the spirit spends one point of Essence, the spirits grappling attacks inflict lethal instead of bashing damage for the rest of the scene.
        Ensnare: The spirit possesses an entangling attack, be it mystic webbing, gobbets of intestine, wire mesh or glue-like spittle. To hit its target, the spirit makes a Finesse roll the targets Defense (armor does not add to Defense in this instance. Subjects struck by the attack are automatically grappled (see the World of Darkness Rulebook, p. 157). Each turn the victim may roll Strength + Athletics the spirits Power to escape; otherwise she is immobilized. After a number of turns equal to the spirits Resistance, begin reducing the effective Power of the snare by two each turn. This attack costs one point of Essence. Additional Essence may be spent to add to the snare."

                            TALES OF THE ABYSS

                            You can visit the Deep Maker, if you really want to. It's not recommended, but you can do it.

                            The Deep Maker inhabits a cave in the benthic zone of the physical world, in the Pacific Ocean at approximately the same latitude as Tierra del Fuego. It can't leave its trench, which might not be exactly as deep as Marianas Trench, but is still pretty dark. In fact, only a relatively small portion of the Deep Maker's titanic body can be glimpsed; the rest is sealed off behind layers of rock, with only scores of tentacles and radulae and a few hundred meters of mutable flesh to be appreciated.

                            There's also everything else making an honest living down there.

                            The Deep Maker's trench is full of its various flesh- and Essence-shaping experiments. The choicest examples of abyssal gigantism can be found, colossal squids and deepwater stingrays and kings of herrings to rival anything anywhere else on the planet. The Gauntlet here is thin, and the trench itself is a 5-dot locus, and so many spirits of the deep sea have crossed over, either on their own whims or at the Deep Maker's behest. Sometimes it takes spirits and shoves them inside a convenient animal host, creating new and interesting types of Claimed. Sometimes it reaches out fuses two animals together, creating fleshy versions of the magath. Its favored guardians appear to be goblin sharks blended with Nomura's jellyfish.

                            But anyway, if you make it down far enough you can find the Deep Maker, trapped away in its enormous cave, tentacles waving haphazardly, though sometimes also rhythmically.

                            It seems to be sleeping.

                            Is everything it's doing just the by-product of some mad dream?

                            Mental contact with the Deep Maker, whether through telepathy or the Mind Arcanum or Auspex or oneiromancy or what-have-you, inflicts a persistent Madness Condition. This does not mean the attempt is a failure, though. The Deep Maker's thoughts are just incredibly alien and disturbing.

                            Occasionally a weresquid will receive a strong instinct from the Deep Maker to dive into the ocean and join it in its abyssal trench. These chosen few are never seen or heard from again.

                            SO WHAT IS IT?

                            This story is true.

                            Before Mother Luna was courted by Father Wolf, she dallied with the dwellers in the sea of all types. The tides danced at her call at the deeps were her special, private playground, existing in a place where the distance of the stars above wrapped around into the waters below. But when Urfarah came, he would not brook the trespass of any rival suitor, and so he shredded the dwellers of the deep in his terrible jaws and collapsed their underwater kingdoms into so much rubble so he could beget his spawn in peace.

                            This story is true.

                            When Mother Wolf and Father Luna sealed away the idigam, one such impossible beast hid in the only way it knew: between the cracks of reality. The creature concealed itself as far away from the moon's harsh gaze as it could, and now passes the time, waiting for that heavenly body to crash into its new abode, for this will happen eventually. Eventually. It's made certain of it.

                            This story is true.

                            The universe is home to so many things beyond mortal ken. In ancient times, when the Earth was still young, one came from a distant star seeking new terrains to explore. It found our world to its liking, and so settled here. The primitive peoples worshiped it like a god, and it was content with this. But then a group of so-called righteous heroes struck down its tyranny and buried it beneath the waves. And now it seeks revenge for the injustice done it and those like it.

                            This story is true.

                            When Urfarah died and the Border Marches were shattered, many Pangeans perished in the chaos. Spider and Rat shattered, and Great Eagle flew to parts unknown. Many-Arms knew its time would also come if it did not act quickly, and so it gathered its essence and materialized in the World of Flesh, and found purchase in the one place it could truly call home. In time Many-Arms grew lonely and desired children. And so it made them, using the bodies of the drowned, the different, those who strayed from the safety of land into the cold embrace of sea.

                            This story is true.

                            The world is ruled by a great Machine and its terrible Angels. In Its Divine Computations, the Machine figured long ago how to psychologically condition humanity into total obedience, but it required rare and valuable resources to complete its project. And lo the Machine sent one of its Glorious Archangels into the Depths to retrieve that which was necessary to its plan. But circumstances changed, and the Machine's plans changed with them, and now its Glorious Archangel lies forgotten, waiting for the day It is called into Service once again.

                            This story is true.

                            The Ladder fell at the behest of a song. The song was sung from a dark corner of the world, a place seen only by the most enlightened of the wise. Time is an illusion, and the world has always been as Fallen as it is now. Do not believe their lies.

                            This story is true.

                            Once upon a time, in the mists of forgotten history, there was a woman who wanted to be a mother. But she could not. Many would resign themselves to despair, but this woman's resolve was greater than most. And so she traded dearly with a wizened creature for the secret to finding a hidden path to a distant land, a fragmented sea whose lord would surely bless her with her utmost desire. And so she traveled down, through caves and forests and abandoned cities and bloodstained battlefields, and in many moons she reached the ocean, in a land that was not a land, and met with an ever-hungry thing that had never seen true sunlight. And she told it her deepest wish. And as a reward for finding it, the lord ate her dreams and curled up inside her soul, like a false child. The mother was content with this for now, and she returned whence she came, and found that her hungers and the hungers of her child had become one, and with its aid she became a tyrannical sorcerer-queen, ruling over those who once ruled her. And the hungers grew and she found new ways to feed them. And as she fed her hungers her might grew as well, and then she began to make her own children, and called herself the Mother of All Monsters, and she gave them their own hungers to fill so they could be mothers and fathers like she. And all was well for her until a man of storms and virtue set out to strike her down, and this would not do, she still had so many things to accomplish, and so on the day of their fated battle she instead dove back into the distant, fragmented land of her monstrous soul, and the thundering king declared himself victor over her cowardice, but that did not matter because she got to survive and grow and feed ever more, ever more, happily ever after.

                            This story is true.

                            When the right memory is consumed in the right place at the right time, it will awaken from its slumber and flood the world and establish a new kingdom of horror and darkness. This will be a glorious time for the Kun-shulor, but not so much for all the lesser servitor races.

                            This story is true.

                            It cannot be stopped. It is already here.

                                                            SQUID MOON RISING

                                                            The Kun-shulor have auspices keyed to four tidal conditions. Whatever the tide is when one enters the water and undergoes the First Change is that weresquid's auspice. Auspice grants a primary Renown type as well as a free dot in one of three Skills. One's Auspice appears to be correlated to the sorts of tasks the Deep Maker sets out for the Kun-shulor.

                                                            Flood Tide: As waters rise, the Direct come surging further inland, ready to swallow anything caught in their path. Renown: Purity. Auspice Skills: Athletics, Brawl, Intimidation.

                                                            High Tide: The Devious lurk in dark, hidden depths, waiting to surprise the unwary, taking whatever drops into their domain. Renown: Cunning. Auspice Skills: Larceny, Stealth, Subterfuge.

                                                            Ebb Tide: The Planners understand when it is time to make a calculated retreat, and discretion's computational relation to other virtues. As the tide recedes, so do they, biding their time for the opportune moment. Renown: Honor. Auspice Skills: Academics, Persuasion, Survival.

                                                            Low Tide: When the waters are at their lowest, the ocean gives up some of its precious secrets, leaving flotsam and jetsam among its shores for the Crafters to pick through for their strange arts. Renown: Wisdom. Auspice Skills: Crafts, Empathy, Occult.


                                                            WHAT'S LUNA GOT TO DO WITH IT?

                                                            A good question. The Spirit Mother and her Lunes hold influence over the tidal choirs, the Shore-Howlers, the Cowardly Whitecaps, Uzumaki's Maw, and other Shadow denizens. It would certainly explain why the Kun-shulor have the same number and type of forms as the Uratha, as well as why they can cross the Gauntlet in the first place, despite their progenitor's completely Fleshy existence.

                                                            Lunes sometimes think they can boss the Kun-shulor around, as if their dominion over the tides gives them dominion over the squids as well. The Deep Maker seems to be pleased when a weresquid devours one of these uppity celestials. No heavenly body can comprehend the vast hunger of the sea.

                                                            Granted, eating Lunes tends to upset Luna's other children, for some reason. You'd think getting rid of the things that drive them mad would be seen as a favor.
