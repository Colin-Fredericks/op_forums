    The Australian setting material problem

    10-18-2022, 10:26 AM
    So obviously this commentary is coming about 7 years after the material I'm gonna be commenting on was released, and the material in question was released a year and a bit before Dark Eras, so there's some alignment problems with the material in canon anyway. Probably this issue can be easily dismissed... but I think it'd be valuable if it weren't - particularly for the writers at Onyx Path.

    I just wanted to say that the WtF2e Australian material is... balls? I think that's the nicest way to put it. It's balls.

    So "The Uratha Dreaming" from the core is... overwriting a diverse set of beliefs of an already marginalised people with a less than flattering view of their beliefs and history. Yes, this is a fictional setting, but it didn't have to be done this way.

    By way of example:
    The Dreaming was a nightmare. The Diharim birthed everything from their blood, bone, and flesh, but have you ever seen a birth?
    It goes on like that for a bit.

    For those unaware, The Dreaming (or Dreamtime) is the term for the collective narratives of creation for Indigenous Australians - not a specific era or period of time per se, but a set of traditions about the land and the people's connection to it, which differs by region and tribes. WtF2e seems to be attempting to syncretise the Aranda tribe's beliefs with werewolf lore, which... makes sense, that's what gets done with all religions in WtF2e. The problem is that indigenous stories already include battles between Dreaming spirits. It already includes massive land-altering events. It didn't need to be painted as universally misconceived by those people and also "the real truth is just super-gory and horrible" (which is very much the continuous impression given), and then also have their beliefs nailed down as a done and in the past thing - which runs directly contrary to the practices of Australian native peoples.

    The thing which really makes it bad though is that Indigenous Australians represent the oldest continuous civilisation on the planet - 50,000 years of continuous habitation, uninterrupted by outsiders until the arrival of the British. Europe may have had grander construction, but the Romans were a baby civilisation compared to Indigenous Australians. Australia has holy sites older than the Sumerian city of Uruk (the mining company Rio Tinto blew up a 46,000 year old still in use holy site two years ago, which... you can imagine how devastating that is). And uh... given that of any set of cultures in the world, Indigenous Australian uratha probably would have the best claim to actual unfiltered knowledge of Pangaea... European settlers taught them about the Firstborn apparently. That is a thing that someone decided to make canon. Not sure whether that was intended to be a "pure untouched native people despoiled by Europeans' conflicts" narrative, or an "ignorant natives being taught civilisation" narrative, or some Frankenstein hybrid of the two... but it's definitely a choice.

    Oh and I feel it needs to be said, songlines aren't the Indigenous internet? They're the paths that creator beings trod across the country, they are the physical manifestation of these native stories. It seems like someone just... saw the word and decided "oh, cool, lines that people sing along, bet that makes uratha life convenient"? It'd be a bit like saying that Mount Olympus was conceptual, so it exists inside all of us - no, Olympus is a real mountain, people have climbed it, it has a national park, you can go there it's just really hard to climb so obviously the gods live atop it. I mean, cool beans... Indigenous werewolves are better coordinated even without the Tribes... but... like, what? I guess in CofD terms songlines are basically leylines if we want to simplify it, but it's just a super weird choice to decide that Australia's big advantage was just having the phone before the phone was invented.

    On the flipside, it's cool that some awareness of native Dreamtime beings was put in there. Even just communicating the names is a big step for sharing some elements of native traditions. That's important.

    But... overall it feels like a very bad miss? Like, the writers, having encountered the existence of a people whose whole faith is that creation is a continuing thing, that they themselves are part of it through their stories and practices, and that those stories are both the history of the land and the shaping of the land which is still ongoing... nobody thought "Hey this might be a good place to have the Temenos play a part of WtF2e" instead? If the Dreamtime is the active participation of the Temenos in the material between the native residents of the land and the land itself, then those beliefs are valid and don't conflict with WtF setting lore. It'd make the Hisil in Australia a very different place as well because goetia would make up a big portion of the spirit courts, and that would be strange and confusing to outsiders. Sorry Business rites (burial and bereavement) also make this more complex, as ancestors may themselves be part of the Hisil - but potentially as goetia who are not to be named that are part of the land rather than ghosts. It could have been very different is what I'm saying, and it's not like there aren't places where the world works differently in CofD (looking at the Underworld issues in southern Africa for example).

    Because the real kick in the nards is, canonically the beliefs of these uratha (and by extension the native people they represent) are wrong. Pangaea didn't end because the Dreamtime spirits asked Luna and Wolf to make it end, but that is apparently what is believed in Australia in CofD. While the Dreamtime spirits may be canonically real... pretty much the entirety of everyone's beliefs about them isn't, and what little "truth" is remembered is an overwriting of those beliefs with what is itself also a misremembered horror story about Pangaea. Which kinda sucks.

    I feel like Onyx Path normally does better than that.

    Also, why you gotta make a whole major Australian city basically uninhabitable? Does someone have a grudge against Australia at OP? The Demon material for Australia's okay but in both Beast and Werewolf you kinda did Australia dirty.
