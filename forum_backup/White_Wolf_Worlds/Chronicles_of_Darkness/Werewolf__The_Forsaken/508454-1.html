    Originally posted by Elfive View Post
    Do we have even a first tongue word for wolf blooded?
    Uragarum. There's also Urdurgarum, which literally means Wolf-Blooded child and could either be used to refer to a child who is wolf-blooded, or the child of a wolf-blooded. Ur-Kahrih is wolf-suckled, and refers to Wolf-Blooded with the Raised by Wolves Merit. Ursumugarum is literally wolf-children-blooded and refers to the child of two werewolves.

    I'm pretty sure all of the above is non-canon, but accurate to de-grimmified Sumerian, so it pretty much checks out. There's also Urazakh-Angir from Night Horrors: Wolfsbane, whose name meant Wolf-Born-Foretold. I assume the Angir part is the "foretold", so if you wanted to include lupine Wolf-Blooded and didn't want the name to sound silly I personally would go with Urazakh, or Wolf-Born.

    Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.
