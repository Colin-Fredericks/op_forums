    Second Edition Character Creation!

    03-05-2015, 04:55 PM
    First of all, to the writers of second edition, you beautiful bastards. Really, thank you.


    So anyways, I'm bored and unable to run a Second Edition game, so I'm going to do some character creation, and share some of my thoughts. Feel free to contribute you own character concepts.

    Conaron Hill Pack
    This pack has barely started, it based around a disused car-garage in the Conaron Hill district of the local city. It only has three Uratha members, one Wolf-Blooded and a few mortals 'in the know'. First the werewolves:

    Richard Keel
    Blood: The Destoyer Bone: Lone Wolf
    Concept: Territorial Defender
    Auspice: Itheaur Tribe: Hunters in Darkness

    Intelligence: 3 Strength: 2 Presence: 1
    Wits: 2 Dexterity: 3 Manipulation: 2
    Resolve: 3 Stamina: 2 Composure: 3

    Crafts: 2, Investigation: 2, Medicine (First Aid): 1, Occult: 3
    Athletics: 2, Brawl (Urhan): 3, Larceny: 1, Stealth (Shadowing): 3, Survival:2
    Intimidation: 2, Streetwise: 2

    Safe Place (shared): 2, Totem (shared): 1, Favoured Form (Urhan): 2, Professional Training (Occultist): 2, Anonymity: 2

    Renown: Purity: 2, Wisdom: 1

    Aspirations
    Kill an interloper (s)
    Build a den (l)
    Hunt a shartha (s)

    Triggers: Passive (Territory), Common (Wound), Specific (Pack)
    Touchstones: The Old Gang, The Lune

    Gifts
    Crescent 1
    Nature (Glory) Black Earth Red Hunger, Ward (Glory) Predator's Claim
    Pack (Wisdom) Pack Awareness

    Rites
    Rite of the Sacred Hunt

    So a nice Hunters template, all th new stuff really helps build a space for the character to inhabit. The Touchstones immediately give me (the ST) some NPCs to have fun with. I'll probably say this time and time again, but I do like the new Gifts. Keel is an occult focussed character, with little social skills and penchant for punishing breaches of territory, he seems to be the driver of the packs vengeance. God help their enemies when he realises more of his Crescent Moon potential.


    Anna Redick
    Blood: The Challenger Bone: Guru
    Concept: Changed Historian
    Auspice: Cahalith Tribe: Bone Shadow

    Intelligence: 2 Strength: 2 Presence: 3
    Wits: 3 Dexterity: 2 Manipulation: 3
    Resolve: 3 Stamina: 2 Composure: 2

    Academics (History): 2, Crafts: 2, Investigation: 2, Occult: 1, Politics: 1
    Athletics: 1, Brawl (Dalu): 2, Survival: 1
    Animal Ken: 1, Empathy: 2, Expression (Guitars): 3, Intimidation 1, Persuasion: 2, Socialise: 2, Streetwise: 1

    Professional Training (Historian): 1, Hearing Whispers: 2, Safe Place (shared): 1, Totem (shared): 1, Allies (Spirit Court): 1, Barfly: 1, Mentor 1: Resources: 1, Striking Looks: 1

    Renown: Glory: 1, Wisdom 1, Cunning 1

    Aspirations
    Play a gig (s)
    Fall into Kuruth (s)
    Join a Lodge (l)

    Touchstones
    The Ex, The Buddy Spirit

    Triggers
    Passive (Moon), Common (Blood), Specific (Pack)

    Gifts
    Gibbous 1
    Death (Glory) Barghest, Knowledge (Wisdom) Sifting the Sands
    Hunting (Cunning) Honed Senses

    Rites
    Messenger
    Banish

    Also fun. More of an intellectual Cahalith powers wise, though she has a good basis in social skills. The thing that strikes out to me now is that, unsurprisingly, the first dot Moon Gifts are just plain useful, not matter what Auspice your character has, they'll find some use out of Auspice 1 dot. Anna strikes me as the waypoint of the pack, more collected. She would work well providing the information for the others to use. Then blast the packs enemies with her War Howl.
    John Dunne

    Blood: The Soldier Bone: Community Organisation
    Concept: Flock Waren
    Auspice: Rahu Tribes: Iron Master

    Intelligence: 2 Strength: 3 Presence: 3
    Wits: 2 Dexterity: 2 Manipulation: 2
    Resolve: 2 Stamina: 3 Composure: 2

    Investigation (Crime): 2, Medicine: 1, Politics (Police): 1
    Athletics: 2, Brawl: 3, Drive: 1, Firearms: 2, Larceny: 1, Survival: 2, Weaponry: 1
    Empathy (Emotions): 1, Intimidation: 1, Persuasion: 2, Streetwise: 2, Subterfuge: 1

    Professional Training (Police): 2, Trained Observer: 1, Safe Place (shared): 1, Totem (shared): 1, Resources: 1, Residential Area: 2, Sounds of the City: 2

    Renown
    Purity: 2, Cunning: 1

    Aspirations
    Leave the Police (s)
    Find the Serial Killer (l)
    Gain Uratha respect (s)

    Touchstones
    The Parents, The Future Self

    Triggers
    Passive (Moon), Common (Pack), Specific (The Other)

    Gifts
    Full Moon 1
    Dominance (Purity): Snarl of the Predator, Knowledge (Purity): Lore of the Land, Strength (Cunning): Unchained

    Rites
    Rite of the Sacred Hunt

    So here's the face of the pack with regards to mortals, even though he's a Rahu, god help them. Looking at the various Gifts Rahu can get, it would be fun playing this ex-copper as he becomes a shredder. Again, the Touchstones are giving the ST two NPCs per PCs. It is rather good. Also, the Community Merit is an awesome little goodness.

    Others

    So the rest of the pack, as you'll note they don't have a locus. This pack instead uses a Wolf-Blooded with that tell. Mortal members are members of the local community, with some specialisations. There's the "I know a guy", there's the "I was a surgeon dammit" and then there's the kid who's cycling around and is always watching.

    The pack creation is really cool. I'm going to look forward to running that at some point. ^_^

    The Totem? Well it's getting late. I'll post that later, with an Idigam.


    So, who are your guys and gals?
