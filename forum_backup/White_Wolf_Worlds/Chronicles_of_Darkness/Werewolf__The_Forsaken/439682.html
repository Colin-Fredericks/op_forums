    What does your totem do when your wolves aren't bothering it?

    03-13-2015, 06:47 AM

    Rosekill is a budding magath. A small little spirit of bullying born in the shadow of the sorority dorm. Every year when rush comes around, Rosekill and its siblings gather in a feeding frenzy to grow on the pain, embarrassment and alienation. And even the little tender bits of glowing hope crushed.

    Rosekill got its jump in growth when one of the pledges hung herself. So now Rosekill's got a taste for suicide, and a mission.

    As far as totems go, Rosekill isn't ideal. But it (she) bullied and wheedled a most unusual pack of one lonely wolf into accepting her.

    I like to think of her lurking around the dorms during the day, encouraging the girls to do terrible things to one another, and themselves. "Tell her she's fat!" "Kiss her boyfriend!" "Make her clean all the toilets!" "Puke up your lunch!" "Cut off your ugly ears!"

    When possible, she jumps into the bodies of the coed's themselves and is happy to spend weeks breeding a culture of cruelty in the flesh.

    Otherwise, I imagine that as a growing spirit in her hierachy, and with the benefit of becoming a magath, she's developing a wider world view, in a spirit sense, and is frequently travelling to seek out the powers that be who can extend her influence.

    So a question - what do you imagine your totems doing all day, when they're not being bothered?

          The usual answer is get more essence.

          But depending on its rank, it might be out and about doing politics. I think the rank where it develops its own identity is 3, offhand, but at that point it may start currying lesser spirits, doling out essence to them so that they will serve it. It might also try finding a place among the courts of higher ranked spirits of similar Resonance, or the location, which will probably stretch beyond the dorm and to the entire university. It might try to find a locus, too, especially one that others may have overlooked, perhaps due to it being in an 'unappetizing' area, like a Wound. If it hasn't gained its own personality yet, it might be used as a tool by higher ranked spirits to further their own ends.

          A big thing to keep in mind, though, is if it's a magath, it will probably be excluded from the spirit politics. I'm not sure to what extent, but it does seem like magath are sort of shunned by 'regular' spirits, which can make it difficult to operate if they won't let it hang out near the bigger loci. However, it also opens up opportunities. If your magath hasn't developed its own personality yet, it can act as more of a mad dog, gathering up other spirits and possibly more influences. If it has, it may start plotting against the existing spirits to sort of assure that its own influences are fed. This might mean, for example, trying to taint the resonance of certain areas, maybe getting rid of certain key spirits at the schools' mental health facility, or if it's stuck to the dorm, trying to get rid of restraint, possibly by driving off the spirit of the sorority, which probably watches over the girls there and keeps things from deviating too far from normal.

          It would probably try and encourage its Uratha to expand its pack, not just with other Uratha but wolf-blooded and humans too, so they could turn smaller incidents of bullying into larger things, or be bullies themselves. Occasionally people die in werewolf hunts, and making those deaths appear to be suicides might be a good way to get some appropriate essence for that kind of thing flowing.

          You posted about the internet spirits. If there was a computer lab nearby it might be a good hunting ground for a spirit of bullying to get some essence and devour some lesser spirits of its kind.

                  I just checked, it's rank 3. The Hursihim, Ensihim and so forth are made up of two ranks each, an -ih and an -ah, each referring to a different rank, which is the tricky part.

                  Rank 0 is the Muthra, who are just dormant.
                  Rank 1 is the Hursih, rank 2 are the Hursah, who are animals and children in the spiritual heirarchy.
                  Then Rank 3 is the Ensih, the 'fully independent spirits, evolved beyond their ties to a single source of Essence. They make up most of the spirits involved with Hisil politics, furthering the agendas of their umia.'

                                          Here is the story of the Raven..
                                          It used to remember some of its kind back in the early days, but they changed into something called "Halaku".
                                          Fihir Crum did not. He instead studied the strange Uratha from a distance. How they move and shift. how they work and how they communicate.
                                          It built a cover as something weak and useful for a pack in need of a totem.
                                          Even though they would never trust him fully, he knew they needed him. Of course they did. And he was happy to oblige.
                                          He just had to be careful to approach them in a way that they would not expect what he is.
                                          He spoke to one of the young wolf blooded. many a time. The Wolf Blooded seemed eager to learn more about spirits. So it asked the young being to let Fihir Crum help the Wolves in the pack.
                                          Every time they don't see, it is secretly spying on the pack. Making sure it has enough dirt on everyone so they dont evict it when the wolves find out what it is.
                                          Fihir Crum does not know all the details. it does not want to remember it all. It wants the lie to continue. Maybe the wolves can hunt down and kill all those pesky Halaku and those spirits that knows?
                                          Every chance it has Fihir Crum steals something. it has to steal something from the pack and does not always want to let go when the theft is found out.
                                          Fihir Crum does not mean to lie to the pack from time to time. It just lies in its nature to do so. Sometimes it is for the best. So it hides and waits, hoping that the pack will protect it while Fihir Crum looks to evolve again..
                                          And so it goes..
