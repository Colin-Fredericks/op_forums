        More terms that were needed. See http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...774#post338774 for why some have three versions:

        Broodmother:
        Sharamahan/Sharama


        Mother of the Swarm:
        Sharingi Amahan/Sharingi Ama
        Sharingi-Amahan/Sharingi-Ama
        Sharingamahan/Sharingama


        Ursufgutha's Justice Spirit:
        Anburag-Namskagla: literally "Heaven-breaking mercilessness"


        Chief Elder:
        Affa Nir
        Affa-Nir
        Affanir


        Lord of the Gauntlet:
        Zathu Nir
        Zathu-Nir
        Zathunir


        Destroyer of Worlds:
        Har Deladu
        Har-Deladu
        Hardeladu


        Big Damn Hero:
        Idiot Version - nusahk tagdu-zi: lit. "Soul who hunts for no head", i.e. one who hunts for danger

        Heroic Version - guth iduth kalekal-zi: lit. "a good moon's hero/renowed soul", i.e. just what you needed from one of Luna's better moods; for Pure it's "guthnam tagdul", which means "a lucky hunter"

        Both - nuthima nugagnamag ul: lit. "thoughtless surprise-making one", the hero that serendipitously saved you all. Replace "ul" with "ur" in the presence of werewolves, or "hith" with spirits, or "lu'u" with humans, etc.


        Slave-Related Terms:
        Slave Trader:
        arath sadu
        arath-sadu
        arathsadu

        Claimer of Slaves:
        arath thekhdu
        arath-thekhdu
        arathekhdu

                            To repeat: they speak a conlang called "the First Tongue" that was developed largely by taking Ancient Sumerian and running the words through a reversal of the processes of Grimm's law. Mother Luna's First Tongue name has been Amahan Iduth since the 1e corebook. To repeat once more: this is not new — the First Tongue has been this way for the entirety of Werewolf: the Forsaken's printed run so far.

                            She is still likely to be referred to with human-language sobriquets like Luna, Tsukuyomi, or the Warden Moon as well for the foreseeable future, because conlangs are tricky business and having a point of common comparison with existing mythology for newcomers to the game, but her First Tongue name is only likely to change in terms of 2e's increased fluidity of gendered-pronoun presentation.
