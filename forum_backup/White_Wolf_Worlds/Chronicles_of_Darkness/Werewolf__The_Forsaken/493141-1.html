                        Uratha in Apocalypse's setting would probably have it a lot easier. They're not as urged on by rage, and they don't have to worry about the idigam or the shartha, they even have potential allies in the other changing breeds and spirits aren't as intent on breaking through to the other side. Take away all those problems and they'd be able to find a clear focus in working against the Wyrm, providing it was necessary, or at least the more baleful of the Wyrm's servants, such as Pentex and the Black Spiral Dancers.

                        As it is Uratha have a hard time with Bale Hounds because they could be anywhere, providing you don't use the Dark Pantheism concept. If all the 'bad' werewolves were in one tribe and you didn't have Pure or Bale Hounds to worry about? Man they would be having it easy.

                        Of course, they're hunters, not warriors for Gaia. So no guarantee they'd fall in line with the paradigm of the rest of the Garou. And the Garou would probably hunt them down as anathema.

                        About the Garou vs Uratha in new World of Darkness' setting, if they did manage to piss off the Pure, which I can't really see happening since they are able to waltz around in 'Father's' form and don't bear the brands of Luna's slavery, the Pure might have the advantage in numbers, since the Pure tend to have larger individual packs. But at that point you get into theoretical logistics, do all the 750 Garou stick together? Are they all in the same area code, or spread across the world in their individual packs, and so forth.
