            The first 11 kennings of 101, including one I translated during the final push for the Viking tier.

            Hafanidu-Sisu: "Battle-Woman's horses" = "Gunnr's horses" = wolves
            nirihdu-namusu-kaf: "the frightful (lit. "fear-making") corpse-sea" = blood
            asde kasinfu imin: "rain of spears" = battle
            kufat: "sole-thorns" = claws
            kurah-namthuf: "rune-caller" (i.e., "calls-to-runes") = wizard, magic-user
            ur-zar-matha: "wolf who runs across the land" = "wolf of the land" = the wind
            bhurninzukh: "the jewel of spreading light" = "the light-jewel" = the sun
            babhar-namsu: "white death" = death by avalanche
            usum-kur: "serpent's slayer" = winter
            suthar-bhirtan: "bloodied swan"/"swan of blood" = raven
            dumu-zarminalthadu: "wind racers"/"those who are always racing the wind" = horses
            kuntha = "whale's way" = the sea

            And a couple of new solo words to add to the dictionary:

            Hafanidu: FT name for the Valkyrie Gunnr
            kuf: foot, paw
            ninzukh: jewel, gemstone, precious stone
            zarminaltha: race (lit. "run-duel")
            dasrithu: Glory, glorification, fame; the renown of Glory
            sar umfisanna: a holy book or text, like the Bible or Torah
            samu: to sell something
            namnuthamnam: to be single, mateless; a single person is simply called "nuthamnam"

            And some canon words:

            Ithama'heme: Aisling Tulane, the insane Cahalith from the spirit wilds in Signs of the Moon
            Usumma: A synonym for Uzuhama, a foul name the Pure have given Mother Luna, and also meaning "Bitch Mother"

                Heu:
                Disufala-Nihhimalan-Tha: "Turns the Right-Star's Way", or "Turning North" i.e. turning towards the North Star (Vikartha); refers both in terms of turning towards the right path and becoming part of the right path

                "People can change. Nothing stays as it is forever. To be able to choose is the essence of power, and there is always, always a choice." = "Asezi duhu faladugu. Nu-nih min-nudaka ina nusirah. Faladugu kal annunhia-muth; sharah faladugu kal nal."


                Perverseness:
                "Duaf nu habalthu, kal nu habalthu." = "Start no challenge, and there will be no challenge."

                OR

                "Duaf nu-nih, kal nu-nih." = "Start nothing, and there'll be nothing."


                nusirah: Forever, eternity
                faladugu: Choice, free weill (lit. "change-ability")
                asezi: A catch-all term for living or currently-existing beings

                                        The next ten kennings, making 21 out of 101 translated:

                                        nisik-gislak: “green clearing” = a shaman’s gathering place
                                        affa-igim: “ancestor’s watch” = a stone circle
                                        ulke-hikaon-lulafuna: “sky’s black cloak” = nightfall
                                        hugunbath-nihunua: “the uncut thread” = life, or a destiny to be fulfilled
                                        ersu-nurada: “bed shame” = dying of sickness or old age
                                        uhamusen-kufulum: “raven’s food” = corpses
                                        gu’urfil: “bane of wood” = fire
                                        uththu-saridufala: “counter of every lunar cycle” = “counter of years” = the moon
                                        umamu-kaf: “sea of beasts” = the earth
                                        thima-anah: “thought’s drink” = poetry

                                        And some Vampire stuff:

                                        Gakal-Sharguzen: Ventrue ("the cunning and rich")
                                        Desasu-Ene: Daeva ("those who lust for pleasure")
                                        Thik-Suthanahag: Gangrel ("chaotic, bloodied savages")
                                        Ziluzuk-Sumukh: Mekhet ("life-stealing shadows")
                                        Haf-Ikhi: Nosferatu ("terrible faces")

                                        Ordo Dracul: Mirskadur-Umfisah ("scribes of the Dragon's child")
                                        Lancea Sanctum: Isib-Fisgah ("priests of the dripping mouths")
                                        Invictus: Mekhar-Igimir ("the silent witness-lords")
                                        Carthians: Shaghu-Uthaskska ("the rising wail of rage")
                                        Circle of the Crone: Muthamahan-Umma ("witches of the bloody mother")

                                        Strix: virusumukh-ninna ("birds of corrupted darkness")
                                        Revenants: muth-aziha ("blood-cursed")
