                                                The problem with the Taliban comparison is that the Fire Touched are fanatics about honesty. Lying about the nature of the Lunes and Taga Dan is just as bad as lying about anything else in their worldview. I would believe of Luna that she enslaves the souls of the Forsaken upon their deaths, turning their renown brands into manacles that force them to obey her will.

                                                Luna is an inconstant, insane being from the outer darkness. She's powerful, yes, but power isn't why you offer your faith and loyalty. Besides, the hypocracy of venerating Luna when you scoff at the idea of venerating spirit gods. She IS a spirit god.

                                                The main thing Luna has going for her in terms of classifying her as worthy to worship is that the Bale Hounds have a huge hate on for her. That would be the one thing to give me pause, since the Maeljin are the most obviously evil things in the setting. Luna might be the lesser of two evils, which would explain why the Forsaken totems signed on with her, but I think there's something to the idea that being pure in your convictions, and keeping any necessary alliances with the lesser of two evils at arms length, and only so long as you're fighting the common enemy of the greater evil.

                                                The Pure establish alliances with the local spirits, but they don't do so blindly. They hate the spiritually damaged resonance of a wound just as much as any Forsaken. It's just the Pure recognize the wisdom in working with the powerful local spirits instead of against them in maintaining a beneficial status quo for all involved.
