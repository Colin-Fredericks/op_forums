                    I don't know if Skinwalkers exist yet (knowing my luck, they probably do), but here's my take on them!



                    Skinwalkers (Weredogs): In ancient times, When Luna was still grieving for the loss of her mate and had not forgiven the Forsaken, some of her children were still arrogant in what their progenitors had done. They saw Luna's wrath as too extreme, and sought ways to undo her punishment that bound them to human forms. It is unknown how long they roamed the world in those ancient times, questioning spirits and shaman alike. Eventually, however, their hubris obtained a result, and they restored their bestial powers.

                    But a spirit as powerful as Luna would not be denied so easily it seemed, and their victory was a shallow one. Their canine forms were genetically inferior to their original state. Smaller, less organized, weaker, their claws were duller and their instincts diminished. These were the first generation of Skinwalkers, or as you might know them today as Weredogs.

                    The Weredogs are pariahs among the Lupines who openly mock and deride them. In the eyes of the Uratha, a Skinwalker is a mutt, a mixed breed who's lineage has been diluted by the trappings of human civilization, such as greed and luxury. A dog is a pet, a domestic animal who can not survive long in the wilds as they grow soft from the affections of humanity. They have forgotten what it means to be a Wolf.

                    Unlike their cousins who seek a balance among the physical and the spiritual, Weredogs are primarily concerned with the well being of humanity. They have become too indoctrinated into human life styles to fully return to the wilderness. So as long as humanity continues to prosper and expand, the Skinwalkers likewise are able to expand their own territory. They pay little heed to the concerns of the wildlife unless it negatively impacts their own well being, which places their reputation among the spirit world in an even worse position than their Lupine cousins.


                    Being socially outcast from typical Werewolf society, the modern day Skinwalkers know relatively little about how the spirit world works. What they have managed to learn was passed down through many generations, or learned the hard way through personal experiences. This is another reason why the Wolves consider them inferior, as they often try to get involved in situations they know little about. They get even more flak when it comes to the Spirits. If the Spirits weren't happy with the Wolves forcing their rules upon everything, they are even less pleased about their bastardized cousins trying the same trick! Skinwalkers who choose to commune with the spirits have to work extra hard to earn their gifts and fetishes as they struggle to prove themselves worthy. Because they are so engrossed in human lifestyle, they typically form alliances with spirits that represent such aspects.


                    Skinwalker society has two distinct sub-groups which are Pedigrees, and Mutts. A Pedigree Skinwalker is a Weredog who's parents and grandparents were both Skinwalkers. A Mutt is either someone who had a human ancestor within the last 2 generations, or someone who became infected, the process behind which shall be explained later. Pedigree Skinwalkers are considered to be higher in rank among their people, while Mutts are considered to be the inferior mixed bloods. It is a common sight to find Mutts acting as servants to Pedigrees when dealing with a Skinwalker pack.


                    --------------------------------------------------------


                    Appearance:

                    Human: The human form of a Skinwalker looks almost completely identical to an ordinary human. There are no outstanding marks or characteristics that would identify them as a shifter. This is both to remind and mock them for how closely they have been corrupted by human influence.

                    Animal: In their animal form, the Skinwalker takes on the shape of a dog who's species is randomly chosen upon undergoing the first change, unless they are the child of a Skinwalker, in which they will become the same species. The available dog species are large and medium sized breeds (Such as Dalmatians and Collies), but not the small breeds (such as Chihuahuas or Shih Tzu).

                    Hybrid: In their hybrid form, the Skinwalker becomes a smaller and weaker mimicry to a Werewolf. They do not stand 9 feet tall and weigh 500 lbs of muscle, but they are certainly stronger than ordinary humans. The average size for the hybrid form is between 6 to 7 feet tall, with a weight close to 250 lbs.

                    Near Human: In this form, the Skinwalker gains a set of claws and their mouth protrudes into a short muzzle, giving them enough room for their sharp, predatory teeth. Their body hair thickens, but they don't grow extra hair where it wasn't already present. Their muscles also bulk up slightly, making them stronger than their normal human forms. Clearly looking at this hairy, muscular thing would alert others to it's unnatural existence.

                    Near Animal: This form is a much larger version of their animal form. In fact, it's roughly the size of a common wolf, making them easily recognizable as an unnatural member of their breed. It is unknown whether the size of this form is a curse, forever reminding them of what they used to be.


                    ----------------------------------------------

                    Reproduction: There are two methods that Skinwalkers are able to reproduce. The first method is through mating, which is considered to be more honorable. Children born from such a union have a 1 in 3 chance to inherit the Skinwalker genes and undergo their first change. The second method is through infection, and it is considered dishonorable, for it depicts the Skinwalkers as rabid dogs. Spreading a disease to those they come into contact with, as opposed to an ancient and noble bloodline.

                    The infection is transmitted with biting, not unlike rabies. Normal humans who receive the bite have a chance to become infected if the wound is not treated properly. If the infection takes hold, they will undergo the first change within one lunar cycle. The time it takes for the infection to set in depends on the individual, but rarely does it require more than a day of neglect to take hold.

                    Only natural born Skinwalkers are considered pure bloods among their people. Those who become infected are considered mixed breeds and regarded as second class citizens by the rest of the Skinwalkers. Even then, only those who's ancestry spans at least 2 generations of Skinwalker couples is a true pure blood. Both their parents and both their grandparents must be Skinwalkers to be officially recognized as pure bloods. Anything less and they are a mixed breed in the eyes of their people.


                    ---------------------

                    Delusion: Lunacy - Skinwalkers may no longer be Werewolves, but they still retain the protection of Lunacy for those who witness them in their hybrid, near animal, or near human forms. Witnesses may interpret what they saw as a large dog, or a hallucination brought upon by the adrenaline rushing through their system.


                    Favored Renowns: Cunning - Having lost their primal instincts, the Skinwalkers had to rely on more inventive ways to survive throughout the ages.


                    Favored Gifts:


                    Cunning - Skinwalkers are not as strong or terrifying as their ancient counterparts. However, what they lack in savagery, they have made up for with their cleverness. Ages of living alongside humanity has imparted them with greater intelligence they can use to outwit foes they can not overpower.


                    City - Skinwalkers are often poorly equipped to survive in the wilderness, and prefer life in the city. As a result, they learned how to draw upon the Gifts it has to offer and further protect their homes.


                    Wolfslayer - When humans learned how to domesticate canines, they often used them as protection against wolves. It is no great surprise, then, that the Skinwalkers learned the secrets to battling against their Uratha cousins who would seek to impede human civilization.



                    Rage: Mad Rage - The corruption that twisted their animal forms also seems to have twisted their rage in the process. When a Skinwalker enters a Rage, they are like a rabid dog. Their teeth bare fiendishly, they emit gutteral snarls and barks, and their saliva begins to foam around the mouth. The Skinwalker is so driven by hatred and anger, they will even turn to acts of self inflicted injury hoping to burn off some of their pent up aggression. A Skinwalker's rage is just as dangerous to their own body as it is to everyone else around them, and therefor is even more terrifying of a concept to them.

                    While in the Mad Rage state, the Skinwalker must roll a Composure save to avoid attacking their own body (tearing at their flesh or slamming against the environment) each round. This only applies if there is nobody else around in which to vent their rage on. The Skinwalker must be able to reach the potential victim as well, meaning if they are behind layers of safety glass, the Skinwalker would still attack themselves if they couldn't smash through.


                    Primal Urge: The Skinwalkers are able to survive because of human civilization and advancements. Because of this, the Skinwalkers feel compelled to aid them in order to facilitate their own survival in the process. Additionally, a Skinwalker can not bring purposeful harm to a human being unless one of the following is met

                    1 - The human is attacking another human ally of the Skinwalker. Much like a pet coming to protect its master from an attacker.

                    2 - The human attacks first and is not considered to be an ally. Standing alliances can be broken if the human continuously abuses the Skinwalker, thus proving that they aren't concerned with the Skinwalker's survival.



                    Form Modifications:


                    Human - None

                    Near Human - Speed +2, Strength +1, Dexterity +1, Health +1

                    Hybrid Form - Size +2, Speed +3, Strength +2, Dexterity +2, Health +2, Perception +1

                    Animal Form - Size -1, Speed +4, Dexterity +1, Strength +1, Perception +3

                    Near Animal Form - Size +1, Speed +3, Strength +1, Dexterity +1, Health +1, Perception +2

                                                    Good News, everyone! I found a website that gave somewhat better instructions on creating new breeds. So I should have better fleshed out versions from now on. I also found out there already exists Wererats.

                                                    ------------------------------------


                                                    Sahuagin (Sa-HWAH-gin): Anyone who knows anything about the Amazon jungles and rainforests agrees that it is an exceptionally dangerous environment. Those who do not have a proper guide are more than likely to end up dead within the first 24 hours of exploration. Few outsiders, however, know the reason behind such dangers. The Sahuagin are the piranha bloods of the jungle, and they are extremely territorial.

                                                    Keeping primarily to the water such as rivers and oasis, the Sahuagin dislike the presence of foreigners who have a long history of chopping down their forests and hunting the beasts for sport. Over the centuries, they have watched as the tribesmen attempted to negotiate with these foreigners about the sanctity of the wildlife, but to no avail. The Sahuagin are tired of negotiations, the time has come for them to act! Foreigners who do not follow a guide's instructions are likely to be snatched up by surprise, their screams muffled as they are dragged to the water and torn to pieces by these vengeful shifters.

                                                    Because they are indigenous to the Amazon, Sahuagin culture is very tribal. They live deep in the jungles, away from the prying eyes of mortals. Like the beasts they resemble, Sahuagin are blood thirsty and savage, natural born hunters and skilled warriors. Traditionally, their weapon of choice is the spear, for its hydrodynamic shape makes it an ideal choice for their amphibious fighting style.

                                                    To protect the Amazon and safeguard the exotic wildlife that resides there, that is the Accord that the Sahuagin pledge themselves to. If the white men from across the sea will not listen to warning, the Sahuagin will make an example of them. That being said, the Sahuagin need to eat too, but when they hunt the animals of the jungle, they do so because they need resources, not because they want bragging rights or trophies.

                                                    ------------------------

                                                    Appearance: Being native to the Amazon, the Sahuagin are typically dark skinned. The hostile environment they live in has also conditioned them with athletic builds, either for chasing down prey, or fleeing a more dangerous hunter. Those who manage to catch a view of their human forms (before they launch into attack mode) might notice that their eyes are unusually large for a human's. Their pupils dialated and glossy like those of a fish. While in human form, they can only survive on land, for they are restricted to breathing air.


                                                    In War Beast form, the Sahuagin resembles the creature from the black lagoon. A monstrous hybrid of man and fish, their mouth is nearly as wide as their face and filled with sharp teeth. Their digits become webbed for easier swimming, and a ridge of fins grows around their face. The warform can survive both on land and underwater thanks to having both a pair of lungs and set of gills, and their rudder-like tail makes it easier to maneuver in the water. While in this form, Sahuagin rarely exceed 140 lbs. But it's not the size that makes them dangerous, it's their mobility.

                                                    In their animal form, the Sahuagin is an abnormally large piranha, weighing an estimate 50 lbs. A school of them working together can strip a man to the bone in seconds. While in this form, the Sahuagin can not breathe air, and loses the ability to use any human instruments because of the fact they are a giant fish. This form is confined to the water, which means it has a limited range of uses. It is primarily used as the patrol form of the Sahuagin, as they swim undetected through the water ways, keeping a look out for trouble.


                                                    -----------------------------


                                                    Reproduction: Intensely xenophobic, the Sahuagin primarily mate with natives of the Amazon, commonly members of other tribes they have formed alliances with. Legends state that children who are born in the water are blessed by the spirits who reside there. By tradition, Sahuagin mothers who are ready to birth will enter into a pool of water that is at least deep enough to reach their hips. The child is baptized in the waters of the jungle the moment they enter the world, before being allowed to take their first breath of air.



                                                    ----------------------------


                                                    Delusion: The Sahuagin do not have a delusional protection. They do not need it, for those who witness them rarely survive the encounter. Those who do manage to escape, however, are not likely to be taken seriously when they recount the story to others. Ever since white men first discovered the rainforest, it has been attributed to ridiculous legends and fairy tales. Survivors are likely to be mocked for having been exposed to some kind of exotic drug, or watching too much Indiana Jones.


                                                    Favored Renowns: Purity - Outsiders bring foolishness and destruction. That is what the Sahuagin have come to realize over the centuries. White men have no respect for their home, the animals, the people, or the spirits who live there until such a time as they are near the brink of extinction. If the Amazon is to be protected, it must be cleansed of outsider presence. Sahuagin do not negotiate, they do not inquire, they do not try to rationalize. They are warriors, and these are foreigners invading their home.

                                                    Rage: Sahuagin live in a place where everything wants to kill you if you aren't careful. Growing up in that kind of environment can really give somebody a violent streak, but when you grow up as half beast, it produces something wholly terrifying. When a Sahuagin enters a rage, they do not qualify as human, they are a denizen of the jungle, a beast who has learned to survive despite the odds, and now it is pissed off!

                                                    Primal Urge: Sahuagin are drawn to the water, and do not enjoy straying too far away from it. Fortunately for them, they live in the rainforest where showers are plentiful. When water falls from the sky, the Sahuagin feel more comfortable traveling abroad. During the monsoon seasons, the Sahuagin tend to become nomadic, traveling great distances across the Amazon that would otherwise be impossible for them.


                                                    -------------------------------------


                                                    Form Modifications:

                                                    Hybrid - Strength +3, Stamina +3, Manipulation -2, Size 6, Health +4, Speed +4, +1 perception rolls.

                                                    Animal - Strength +1, Stamina +2, Manipulation -2, Size 2, Health -2, Speed +6 (0 on Land), +2 Perception rolls.
