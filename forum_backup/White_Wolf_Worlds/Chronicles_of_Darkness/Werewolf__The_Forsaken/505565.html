                              The numbers are supposed to shift in cycles. We're just at an optimum time for Pure antagonism.

                              Essentially once the Forsaken get too numerous, they push the Pure to the far reaches, then turn to in-fighting. That's what the Brethren War was. The Pure then take advantage of this, waiting for the optimum moment to strike and do so, destroying key Forsaken and overturning territories and so forth. They convert more and more until they get all fat and overconfident as the Forsaken slowly rise in numbers and strike back against them when they hear a call for their duty and know the Pure are standing in their way.

                              In times and places of trouble, the Pure numbers dwindle. During periods of complacency, the Pure rule.

                              Right now we're a bit well past the Brethren War, at least in first edition, and the Pure are ascendant, but rumors of idigam are rising, and times of trouble are coming and the Forsaken are or were being called to do their duty. And the Pure are in their way.

                                  While there hasn't been a whole lot of info on the Pure yet in the new edition, what little there has been makes me think that not too much would change for the pure. The Preditor Kings would continue to be obsessed with hunting and killing those who disrespect the hunt. This would probably mean they'd kill a lot more humans who think they're big-shots or vampires who have gotten lazy with easy access to blood. Fire-Touched would still be crazy street-preachers who hunt down Mages and mortal mediums (and maybe Shartha) that disrespect the Shadow. Ivory Claws would continue to track the lineages of the various supernatural taints they know of in the greater human populace, killing anyone that disrespects or defiles their heritage or otherwise gets on the Ivory-Claws' nerves.
                                  While the Forsaken fall under the umbrella of each Pure Tribe's sacred prey, all three spread wider than the Forsaken. Even without the Forsaken, the Pure have plenty to keep their crazy selves occupied.
