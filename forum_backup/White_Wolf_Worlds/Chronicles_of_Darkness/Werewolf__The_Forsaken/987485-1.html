                            My two cents:

                            For the Pure side, just make the characters meet reallity by happenstance. If your point is to guarantee a No Pure pack, without asking this OoC, so just make something happen in the game, not planned by the Pure converters, to show how the Pure are Big B Bad Guys, whyle the Forsaken are the Rude But Cool Guys. It'll probably take care of all your problems, but...

                            If you have a problem with the selling pitch of the duty of a Forsaken, just don't sell it at all. Make the Forsaken explain things to the characters, including the problems that the spirits are going to cause just because they're f*cking alien beings from another world. Taking care of the Shadow isn't about Sacred Duty most of the time, is just sheer necessity. Then let the PCs join the tribes just because it is good to have someone to back you up.

                            You don't have to attach that to the duty at all. Make the NPC Forsaken the kind of people who firmly believe in their religion, but don't require the same from others, not even to join the club. The "duty" will come naturally to your PCs just because they will have to solve problems. Play local and personal until they are convinced that they need to do this shit, not because Duty or boring Prozelytizers command, but because the shit have to be done, or they'll face uncomfortable consequences.

                                                    My take is that, when an established Forsaken character is trying to 'sell' a new werewolf on becoming Forsaken (rather than Ghost Wolf - the 'don't join those maniacs' pitch vs the Pure is fairly easy), they talk about what being Forsaken actually offers as opposed to going it alone. There's not much need to talk about the myths, the legends and all the deep history stuff. The new werewolf has bigger immediate issues - issues that the Forsaken Tribes can give answers and solutions to. 'Well, there was this mighty spirit called Father Wolf who did XYZ, and the Moon herself said ABC' is nonsense stuff for the werewolf who is still in his or her early days of the new existence. It doesn't matter much. What matters is the friendly offer of knowledge and advice about the new state of being, the offer of the Tribal support network, and so on. The rest comes later.
