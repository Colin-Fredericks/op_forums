        Dear Errol,

        Thanks! Mr. Shopping will be really happy to hear that, LJ is quite enamored with her little mystical fortress of knowledge.

        One thing that's been fun about running this campaign in a specific, focused location, rather than the sort of heated, epic journeys of Shopping's games, is seeing how the specific place that LJ maintains affects her spirit friends, who have been a very fun part of the game for me to ST.

        Actually the Shadow has always been one of my biggest gripes with Werewolf, and I've always said that if I ever got around to rewriting it I'd remove the Shadow entirely and focus the game on fleshly matters. One reason I didn't like it because I really just had a hard time liking spirits and thinking of them as beings you can have relationships with, so I tried to take the time with LJ to rectify that. As you can see, some of her closest relationships are with spirits now - Pangur, Git & Gat, Iffa, the Matriarch of the Library, Friend, and these have all been a challenge for me. I wanted them to be believable and relatable, but if you over humanize them, they don't feel like spirits. The middle ground has been the City Spirits, beings of my own invention.

        The Hisil is supposed to lack Human Spirits, so instead there are beings that reflect the activities and societies of the humans in their cities - as you've seen, Chicago's are dun colored, multiple-limbed sort of Gray Alien looking things. In Miami they look like Sahuagin, and in Seattle they almost all cover their faces and heads with hoods of some sort. Git, Gat, and Blue, being these types of spirits, have become some of my favorite NPCs to play.

        Stay tuned. Mr. Shopping and I just completed a certain intense confrontation, which I'll post soon.

You won't find it on a map; it's basically a road, some trailer parks, a diner, and, like, a gas station. Population 200. That fucking place is what you think about when you hear Sweet Home Alabama. It's the kind of dump that Eminem raps about. It's like District 12 with Honey Boo Boo. Missing teeth. Creationism. Dumbing down. Poverty. Pregnant teens.

People there actually talk with rural-ass trailer trash accents. Steer Creek is the quintessential shit hole American town. Even thinking about it pisses me off.

There's a nuclear explosion drawn nearby, detonating in a fireball that's about to gleefully wash over the encampment. There's a scrawl in the margin that says 'fuck Steer Creek' and 'BOOM'. ]

She never finished high school, like 80% of the people there. She's kind, hardworking, and pretty sassy when she wants to be, and, like, as much as I hate to admit it, a really good cook. Momma said that my dad was the only man she ever loved, but obviously the fucker didn't deserve it. You read stories and see stuff on TV about abandoned kids trying to find their birth parents, but like, my dad? He's dead to me. Maybe he is dead. I don't know shit about him and I don't care to.

                                                I was going to try to get out of town the next day, just like, leave the city for a while, maybe go back to Aurora to stay with a friend or maybe even Steer Creek. Anywhere but Chicago.

                                                I couldn’t sleep that night. Just like, lay awake on my bed. Smoked some pot and tried to chill out, but that gun was right next to me. It was late, like 2 AM or something but I was still wide awake, so when I heard the glass dorm security door just freaking shatter, I was up and out into the hall, but Ben Tarn was already on me. He knocked me down and said he’d teach me a lesson. Guy didn’t look human anymore at all - his nose and mouth were fused, his eyes all yellow and fucked up; some hideous mix of man and wolf. He had his knife out, like he was going to carve me up, but when he came in, he fucking -bit- me. Shoulder and neck. He wasn’t expecting the gun though. When Ben came up, my blood all over his jagged, ugly-ass teeth, I started shooting. One went into the ceiling but one hit him in the face, right in one of his eyes. It knocked him back and onto the floor and made him make this horrible howling scream, but it wasn’t enough to kill his ass.

                                                I guess I could’ve kept shooting. Enough bullets to the head probably would’ve put him down, but I didn’t know that yet. I just wanted to get away. I thought maybe he was dead. I ran out into the night, through the streets, screaming, but nobody gave a shit this time. Empty campus, empty streets - and Ben Tarn was right behind me, bleeding from that empty, blown-out eye socket. Then he was a wolf, right at my heels. I realize now that he was chasing me into the woods, keeping just far away for me not to get a good shot, maybe letting me think I’d have a chance to get away. But that’s what Ben wanted all the long. Stupid. Stupid.

                                                I still remember the crunch when the bear trap went around my leg. Ben Tarn had set it, of course. He was their best hunter. I was screaming, on the ground, crying for help, for him to get away from me, leave me alone. Horror movie chick again.

                                                It was real bad. Any pretense of Ben being human was gone when he started stalking up to me and I emptied my revolver into his chest. Didn’t stop him. After that, he just beat the hell out of me. I mean, just took it all out on me. The monster hated me. He broke bones, just…I don’t want to talk about it.

                                                When I woke up, I hurt all over, real bad, but not, like, I should have. Bones that I knew had been shattered weren’t anymore. It was dark again. I heard the voices of my captors, the bastards from the alley. They’d sat me up on the porch of this old farmhouse in the middle of nowhere, Illinois. Creepy cornfields. My ankle was shackled to the railing, and my clothes were bloodied. All five of them were out in the yard in front of me, a big bonfire roaring. Ben was wearing this eyepatch. That made me smile.

                                                Anitra came up to me with a washcloth and a bag of MacDonald’s. Like that was going to make us sisters. She said she was my friend. I started screaming again, knocked that shit away and told them I wanted to go home, they were monsters; started yelling for help. Amal started trying to sell their poison to me again, but I wouldn’t shut up.

                                                They were happy to hurt me. To teach me a lesson again. Anitra did it this time, said it was for my own good. I tried to fight back, but what the fuck can you do with a leg iron against someone who can turn into a man-wolf thing, head and shoulders bigger than you? I could see it in Anitra's eyes when she was kicking me when I was down - she enjoyed it.

                                                I decided to stay quiet then, tears streaming down my face. Just staring straight into the fire. Passive resistance. Amal kept doing his prostheletizing. I don’t remember what else he said, except that they called themselves the Lightning Runners. I just tuned the rest out. He kept asking me questions, like, don’t I want to know what’s happening to me? Don’t I want to know about my purpose and my power? Why my body was healing like this? But I didn’t answer.

                                                Eventually he gave up for the evening after all he got was my stony silence. Each of them tried to talk sense into me that night. All except Ben. Tanner said they weren’t bad people, and this wasn’t easy for them - I laughed at that, which won me a kick in the throat. But that just vindicated me. Anitra, she said she knew punker bitches like me, and we were all posers, and I was just a scared little girl. I’d have to work real hard to ever be good enough to be her sister, and she was losing hope. I spat up into Anitra’s face and told her I’d never be her fucking sister or join their fucking family. Yeah. Broke my nose for that.

                                                She’dve done worse if Jessie hadn’t caught her fist. The older wolf got down real low and looked at me with pity. I tried to plead with her for help but there wasn’t any humanity there. Jessie just said I needed to accept this or I wouldn’t make it. Ben already thought I was dangerous. Some of the ones like me, the ones that were too wild, they just died. Or they had to be put down, and I was starting to look like that. So I needed to make that choice, to choose family or choose death. They’d take care of me. She was sorry that this’d happened but I’d brought it on myself.

                                                I didn’t say shit. Just glared at her defiantly, like there was no way in hell.

                                                That night they partied for hours and got real drunk - probably celebrating the successful hunt. Anitra threw a beer at me; the bottle hit the wall behind and broke. She said I could lap that up if I was thirsty. I overheard Ben talking about how much he hated me. He’d lost face for the knife thing and getting shot up by a teenage girl, but Amal said I was important and I’d come around.

                                                Yeah. I’d come around.

                                                That night I worked on the leg iron. The Lightning Runners had all gone to bed drunk and left me out there alone. The shackle was pretty heavy duty and they’d put it on my ankle real tight. The damage they’d inflicted on me slowly started feeling better. I didn’t know why but it didn’t matter as much as escape. I wasn’t strong enough to break the chain, or the metal railing it was attached to, but the wooden top that ran across? I slowly, painstakingly got the rail free by using the chain to saw through the wood until it was weak enough for me to finally just barely pop it out. Took hours, and my hands were raw, but I did it.

                                                I had no idea where the fuck I was. There were a couple of cars parked out front, but none of them had keys in them. I sure as hell wasn’t going into the house to try and find them, dragging this rattling chain behind me. I was terrified that someone might wake up and find me. If I ran down the road, I might find a car, but Ben Tarn might find me first. The fire was real low - flickers and embers - but still enough for me to get some burning planks out though. I set fire to the porch and threw some up onto the roof. The farmhouse was old wood, so it caught fire fast. Really fast.

                                                Great distraction. I burned the whole fucker down, but they caught me anyway. I didn’t get out of the back roads before the Lightning Runners ran me down, all wolves this time.

                                                Sooo…we finally get to the part I really don’t like talking about. We all have demons, right? Stuff we’ve done or done to us that we try to bury down deep. Or forget. Or say never happened. This one’s mine.

                                                I got caught by the monsters and they really went to town on me for torching their clubhouse. Some of them got burned real bad too. So they showed me their true nature.

                                                Biting me, tearing at me and my clothes. Torn flesh, my blood all over. Tugging me in-between their teeth. It’s all a jumble, really. It felt like it wasn’t really happening to me but happening to someone else. Out-of-body. I thought I was going to die.

                                                When I woke up again, I was down in a hole. It was a big old storm drain - evil as hell, that place, big tunnel on either side, barred off by heavy grates so I couldn’t run. Up top, the only light came from a square grate fifteen feet up. They’d stripped off all my clothes in an attempt to humiliate me and take away my dignity. Anitra was assigned to be my tormentor and had the dubious honor of getting to break me. She’d dump leftovers down the hole from what the pack ate and drank. Anitra taunted me from up above, sometimes dragging a board over it to plunge me into pitch black for what felt like days at a time. The visions got real worse then, when I was alone in the dark. It stank like hell.

                                                Anitra kept asking me if I was ready to be a good girl and come up and join the family, but all she got was me screaming shit up like her like I’d kill them all, just let me go, I’d die first. I didn’t want to be a monster like them.

                                                The torture kept coming, physical and mental. When it rained and the drain got almost full, and I’d be hanging on for dear life, swimming and gasping for breath against the top grate with a couple of inches of air between me and the water. Anitra promised all sorts of shit - a shower, fresh clothes, a toilet, real hot food.

                                                Weeks of this shit. It just got worse when I didn’t break.

                                                You might be like, why didn’t I just give in?

                                                I admit, it was unbelievably traumatizing. I’m still fucked-up over it, but you need to know something about me, okay? I’d fought so hard for my life in Chicago U, to be my own person, to be me and have my own life. I wasn’t going to have anyone take it away and make me a monster. No shit, something was happening to me - all the beatings they’d given me had faded. I could see it on myself. Just some scars from where they’d savaged me as wolves. No way should any of that've gone away in a few days, but it did.

                                                Anitra got frustrated. She started dropping dead rats down instead of food. One night Tanner pissed down the hole. But the worst was when she caught some of my old Steek Creek accent coming through, and she said things to me that still hurt. Her and Tanner both. I can’t believe how much I still fucking hate them.

                                                A couple weeks in, I finally told Anitra I was ready. It was easy to fake the tears, I was so fucked up. But when she put the ladder down to let me up, I went right for her neck and tried to choke the life out of her like a fucking psycho. Mistake, obviously.

                                                When I woke up this time, they had me all snared up in chicken wire down there, the shit digging into my throat and making it almost impossible to breathe. All five of them were up above discussing me when they thought I was out. It sounded like they desperately needed me for something. They called me Ithaeur. Tanner was saying that he didn’t know if it was worth it. Ben was saying shit like I was dangerous, a lot more dangerous than I looked and keeping me would be bad. He didn’t think I’d break anymore. Jessie agreed I was dangerous, but still thought I could be on their side, a real asset. Amal agreed. Anitra was frustrated as hell. She thought I was a stupid bitch and I didn’t know what was good for me. She wanted me dead too. But all that mattered was what Amal thought. He said there just too few of the People to wait for another Ithaeur, and they all knew what was at stake.

                                                I watched the moon change through the grate, and they left me down there for days without food or water. When Jessie finally came down to let me out of the wire, I was too weak to fight. Covered in dried blood and cuts from where the metal had dug into my skin. She brought me a pizza in a box and a big bottle of water but at the same time she put really heavy chains around my ankles, which made it nearly impossible for me to move much.

                                                She didn’t say anything, just gave me this really concerned look at the way I was still glaring at her. Maybe she knew how much I wanted to kill them. Maybe she’d realized her mistake.

                                                But that kindness didn’t last. They only threw down raw meat after that - some of it rotten. Half-eaten animal carcasses. Like they were trying to make me less than human. I didn’t want to die, but that whole thing…it was a living nightmare, man, a hell I wouldn’t wish on anyone but them.

                                                It didn't matter how hungry I was. I was crying, and I wanted to eat so badly, but I didn't.

                                                Ben Tarn dropped this deer skull down one night when the moon was waning, going down from half to crescent. Most of the meat was gone, sockets all empty, big broken antlers. I thought I’d finally lost my mind when it started talking to me.

                                                The skull said it was my friend, and it was going to help me get free. It’d help me get free and then kill the wolves that did this to me.

                                                They probably thought I’d snapped when I started talking to it. It told me things, about what was really happening to me, and how when the crescent moon was full, it’d be time. All I had to do was let him in. I believed him too. I don’t know why, but Friend’s never lied to me. He told me that I’d get to live my life. I’d get it back if I just let him in when it was time. I could use my power however I wanted, because it was mine. I didn’t have to be afraid. I felt warmth when I held the skull, like a blanket around me. I felt concern. I felt love, as alien as it was.

                                                [There’s a sketch of the stag skull in the margin, eyes like coals burning in the sockets. Some words, mostly illegible are in the margin - ‘I love Friend’ and ‘I owe Friend my life’ are about all you can make out.]

                                                It didn’t matter what they did to me after that. I just laughed. It freaked the hell out of Anitra. I just told them they’d all be dead soon.

                                                When the crescent moon was full that final night, I started hearing this otherworldly song. Angelic, demonic, a shimmering woman’s aria from far away. It overwhelmed my senses, and the moon shone down on me and ripped through me like

                                                [Words trail off.]

                                                I saw Friend in that moment, like for what he really was - this giant shape, this wendigo embodiment of terror, this whispering dark angel that wore the face of the dead stag. I opened my arms and let him in, and I screamed my soul out

                                                [Illegible.]

                                                He was in me and the power was like being a god, like nothing I ever felt before or ever since. I wasn’t me anymore when I tore out of that fucking storm drain. I was this unstoppable thing, this killing machine. All I saw was red heat and blood. With Friend inside me, I made good on my promise to the Lightning Runners. We tore them apart. None of them had a chance. I saw fear in their eyes, heard their terror, and it just made Friend stronger. There was a lot of blood, and a lot of screaming. Some of them were able to become wolf-gods themselves, but it didn’t matter. Friend did things through me - he made their claws and jaws do nothing. All their Shadow tricks failed. Some of them begged. It didn’t matter. He tore their spirits out of their bodies.

                                                When it was done, I was naked and drenched in their blood and gore. Head to toe. Their body parts were all over the fucking woods. Trees smashed. I was gasping for breath but unhurt. Everything looked different - sharper, brighter. My senses were a thousand times what they’d used to be. My body felt stronger, healthier.

                                                I’d changed, but I was free. Friend was there, his presence over me, and all I could do was cry and thank him over and over again. He said he’d come for me when I was ready, and then he was gone.

                                                I dragged what was left of the Lightning Runners and threw them down the storm drain where they belonged. I’m not an evil person but I’d never been so satisfied as to see the glassy fear in their dead fucking eyes as they disappeared down into the dark.

                                                Okay. Wow. Never thought I’d write that down.

                                                So…life goes on, right? They had a big shack nearby that was probably one of their safehouses. Took the best shower of my life, still shaking with adrenaline, and saw something else. My hair was now a shock of pure white, my brows black…my eyes this intense, incredible violet. Friend being inside me during that first change, it’d left its mark on my soul.

                                                I liked it.

                                                I took some of Anitra’s clothes and eventually made it back to civilization and the University of Chicago. It was still summer vacation and nobody had really missed me. I just said I’d been visiting family. My new look? I owned it. The break in? Some tweaker maniac running through campus with a gun. Police were still looking for him.

                                                Friend came to me a few nights later. He showed me how to cross over into the Hisil, the spirit world. He took me to his secret place in the dark forest and taught me everything, all without the Forsaken lies. He showed me the power in my spirit side and in my blood, and gave me gifts that no Uratha would ever have.

                                                So how’d I become the White-Haired Witch? White hair’s self-explanatory, but coming to rule the University’s spiritside’s not at all what you might think.

                                                It just…kind of happened.

                                                Friend told me all the Father Wolf bullshit, about what the Forsaken thought their duty was.

                                                Yeah right. The spirits came to me for help and I helped them out. I didn't treat them like the Forsaken did, like they were prey. I let spirits do their thing, as long as no one back in the real world got hurt. That was a big deal to them. No one to control them, or police them. They saw me less like a predator and more like a friend.

                                                Spirits...they're like animals or children, like...they’re pure and naïve, y'know? They just need someone to help guide them, and I just…kind of took that role. I became the University’s de-facto guardian. Some said I was its queen, which made me uncomfortable at first, but when this court came into being just for me, who was I to refuse, especially if it helped me keep things safe for everyone? If there was one thing I could do, it would be to make sure none of my classmates ever had to go through what I did, so I made sure all the Uratha, all the things in the darkness knew that they couldn’t come to UC and fuck around.

                                                Oh yeah, one last thing. Pangur.

                                                I have the moon brands on my arm and up my shoulder but I’m no moon-slave. I’m no Forsaken. I’ve got no love for the Lunes either, or that crazy bitch Luna. But they took an interest in me anyway. Maybe because of my bond with Friend, the Terror King. Or who knows why. But they sent someone to watch after me not long after I left Friend’s dark forest.

                                                Pangur started out as this little ping-pong sized ball of silver light that came down on my window sill and hid in my backpack, manifesting himself in the Flesh. I tried to get it to fuck off but he stuck around no matter what I did. Eventually I tried communicating, but this glimmerling? All it could do was make these ding-ding chimey noises. Shiver when it was mad or scared. I let it hang around, watch TV with me. Y’know. Like you do in your sophomore year.

                                                Over time, the glimmerling changed too. Like an infant, he started talking - hilariously, mimicking me. Laughing like a baby. Then he started making faces on the surface of his orb. One day, we were watching cute YouTube cats and Internet memes and he turned into this glowing, silvery kitten. Cutest fucking thing. Suddenly I had a kitty.

                                                He grew up with me as I raised him on a healthy diet of TV and Internet. Now I’m a senior and he’s like, I don’t know, a young adolescent or something. My best friend in the whole world. I'm still not Forsaken, but that doesn't really matter to him.

                                                So that’s it. Werewolf memoir complete. I’m doing good now, like well-adjusted, doing my whole walking between the two worlds thing while keeping up my 4.0 GPA. Valedictorian track, bitches. Go Maroons! Woo.
