                      I usually rule it as it awarding +1 per dot in the influence. I think the influence chart in one of the versions said that it grants a bonus equal to rank. Lore of the Forsaken also has a section on artificial spirits near the end, and it describes how much essence to spend to get what particular favors out of spirits of various ranks - which makes a fairly handy supplement to the Influences chart. I've also looked at Mage practices for some parallels as to what influences can do, especially for Manipulation [although I know this is technically incorrect, as Imperial Mysteries says it takes 6 dots in Influence to have the free form of a Mage arcana. but I don't see a good reason for that in many cases.]
