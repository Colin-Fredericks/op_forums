            Leaving Pentex aside for a moment, I'd propose that the Wyrm is an ancient, ancient spirit-thing, not unlike the Idigam, that can corrupt spirits. Wyrm-taint is literal corrupted essence.                                                 It's always irked me that theory was presented as "The truth" in C&C. Cheiron in my mind began back in Ancient Greece where a mortal found a way to codify Pyros to the point where they can slap bits of supernaturals on to mortals and started calling a few friends . Though the ages they prefect the art until you reach the point where you are effectively have "mortals" bashing open vampire and werewolf eldars/True Fae to keep themselves operational and keep their professional curiosity entertained. Fast forward past the Roman Empire, the Middle Ages, the Renaissance (a Major point on Cheiron's history but I don't have time to explain) to the age of empires where Cheiron starts to get more abrasive when it comes getting what it wants. It's soon to be members were majority shareholders in the East India Company which became fabulously wealthy as it basically owned India and several markets by proxy. Following the success of that venture (viewed as a successful distraction by most of the group), Cheiron reinvested back into the medical field with it's most notable accomplishment is giving Louis Pasteur the resources necessary to create the rabies vaccine for mortals (Cheiron figured out it werewolves were immune to most diseases and got a tissue sample for Louis to study).

                                                From there it was the civil war (something they didn't have a major part in but were there to get samples), to it's official incorporation in 1859. The great war hit them hard in the pocketbook however was international trade was cut off as Europe killed it's young. They had enough assets in the US and various other places to continue on though. However, Cheiron's greatest screw up was the unintentional release of a virus which would be latter be known as Spanish flu by one of it's Hunters who dropped the jar on a troop ship (this is the precursor to the extinction virus that Cheiron holds in the freezer somewhere). Aside from accidentally killing 3% to 5% of the world's population, They road the highs of the 20s, survived the 30s, and through government contracts, continued through the 40s. It's in the 50s that they began to gain public momentum. That continues to snowball into present day where they have a size able hold (if not outright monopoly) on several markets in the medical field via it's patents.

                                                Of course that is simplifying my head cannon a bit when it comes to them and I've left out several projects which blew up in their faces (sometimes literally) but simply put, I've always had the idea of Cheiron of being a bit more interesting that just "We're aliens who fucked up our own planet".
