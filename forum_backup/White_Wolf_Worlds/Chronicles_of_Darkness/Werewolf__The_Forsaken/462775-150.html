        Establishing a new Touchstone is a breaking point in the opposite direction. Losing a pack Touchstone is a breaking point for everyone in the pack towards the opposite of the lost Touchstone.

        Breaking points towards Spirit: Often experiences in Basu-Im. An animal dying while Beast Riding it or machine being destroyed while Iron Slaving it. Dramatic Failure on Crimson Spasm. Destroying a spirit via discorporation in Siskur-Dah. Being a host for Totemic Empowerment.

        Breaking points towards Flesh: Not going on Siskur-Dah for a time depending on your Primal Urge. Using Self-Control Merit. Shifting to another form to end Father's Form. Using Hard Leverage while Forcing Doors. Using a packmate as a human shield (I'm assuming this is Flesh but the book doesn't say). Teaching mysteries of a Lodge to someone who is not an adherent.

                    Originally posted by Aeveth View Post
                    What are the limits on influences per level? I kind of find it a bit confusing to understand, in either spirits or werewolves, specially on werewolves, the Elemental gift might be rather straighforward but the Wellspring rite opens up a whole lot of things to try up.

                    I got to say I'm confused about the whole thing, from how you use the effects, the duration and the bonus they offer at each level.
                    They haven't been expanded on very well (most of it is done by fans on this forum, who have read the books that give out a little detail and compiled the information), but the 'limits' per level should be clear. In addition to adding the assorted Conditions (such as Open), Strengthen lets you strengthen a single sphere of influence (the fire burns hotter, but doesn't move of its own and you can't create a fire from nothing). Manipulate lets you make changes within the sphere of influence that make sense (the fire spreads to the pile of oily rags nearby, or someone's pants), Control lets you make clearly strange changes (the fire leaps onto someone's face), Create lets you create something from nothing (a fire appears), Mass Create lets you create many somethings from nothing (flames pop up everywhere and on everything). The duration is on the Influence Durations chart, on p186 in the Werewolf book, usually near the Influences in other books. Most people seem to go with successes = the bonus amount, or a success giving you whatever your dots of Influence are, so a Rank 4 spirit of Fire might be able to use Strengthen to give your torch a +4 bonus to damage with Strengthen.

                    Werewolves use Presence + Wits when using Wellspring Influences. It's different from the Elemental Gift as it's not attached to any Renown, so you generally have a smaller dicepool.

                    Some people use a toned down (and Supernally detached) version of Mage's spellcasting Practices to determine capabilities of Influences.

                    Influences are meant to be fairly open ended, with their being a potentially infinite amount of different ones. So having rules for Strengthening everything from aardvark to zymurgy is clearly unreasonable, but I'm sure people would enjoy a more clarified rule system for it. Until then it's more 'soft' mechanics.

                                    I'm away from my books at the moment, but I believe in Werewolf 2E and the Chronicles of Darkness core rulebook the Influence chart specifies that Strengthen adds +Rank to something. I believe one of the examples used is healing a dog with Influence (Dogs). Conceivably, if there's a trait you could add +Rank to that falls under the purview of the Influence, Strengthen can temporarily boost or lastingly heal it.

                                    The Arcana Practices, as mentioned above, are really good guidelines, it's just that the Arcana are much, much broader than most Influences. Also, it's important to remember that all Spirits can passively sense Resonance up to a mile away. The Seek Numen boosts that even more. An example the book gives is that if you're hiding from a Stealth spirit it will be able to sense you by virtue of you using stealth. Keep in mind what your characters "are" (as well as where they are) and what they're feeling or doing. That will really impact what spirits are able to figure out about them.
