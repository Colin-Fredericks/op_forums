                                                            P32, 'Uratha are not immune to being Claimed, and the greatest fear of any Iminir Rahu is becoming one of the Su’ur.' Being supernatural and aware of spirits they are, of course, more able to resist than the standard mortal.

                                                            In first edition I believe the rules kept them and other supernaturals from being Claimed, but it was brought up as a story hook at least once. In second edition there is no such restriction, any supernatural may be Claimed, or the other types of things like what idigam and strix do.

                                                            The idigam isn't technically Claiming the werewolf with Forge Empty Wolf. It's ripping the werewolf's soul from its body and replacing it with a spirit. A spirit Claiming a werewolf, or anything else, instead sort of merges with and subsumes the existing soul. Though an idigam can likely also do that, taking a spirit or human or other supernatural soul and shoving it into a werewolf without removing said werewolf's soul. Likely one has even gathered the souls of werewolves of four other auspices and tribes and shoved them into the body of a werewolf with the fifth.
