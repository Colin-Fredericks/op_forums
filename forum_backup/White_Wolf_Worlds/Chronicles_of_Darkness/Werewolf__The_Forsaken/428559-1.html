                            Here's some ideas beyond fire, in addition to the universal spirit-related uses. Mass create I didn't bother with since, you know, it's just more of create or more permanent creation.

                            Water:
                            Strengthen: Provide a penalty to someone swimming as the water 'thickens' and becomes hard to move through. Make it so water you have 'lasts longer' before you need to check for deprivation. Make it so a smaller amount of water can be used to douse a fire. Enhance the strength of existing waves or movement of water to a larger degree. Purify drinkable water.
                            Manipulate: Provide a much bigger penalty to swimming, perhaps making it contested as the water more actively moves against someone. Cause rain nearby to fall 'around' something to keep it dry. Dry something off that's wet by displacing the water. Stop up a sink or other minor water source. Enhance the strength of existing waves or movement of water to a larger degree.
                            Control: Change water vapor to liquid, liquid to ice, or visa versa (could be used to short circuit something on a humid day, easily). Attempt to actively drown someone by forcing water into their lungs (whether they're swimming or you just have another source onhand) which would likely be akin to a grapple. Drag someone through water when they're swimming. Actively part a rainstorm around a small area, keeping the area dry. Keep a boat from sinking, or stop up some other high pressure water source. Cause a violent wave in a lake or ocean.
                            Create: All sorts of things can be done with this, as you're creating water from nowhere. You can likely drown someone without an existing water source, or cause no small amount of damage by using water to create short circuits or get people electrocuted inside a powered building.

                            Air:
                            Strengthen: Breathe in thin air with no penalty. Hold your breath longer (I'd add a minute/turn per success). Cause the wind to blow faster (maybe adding or subtracting dice to whatever bonus/penalty, like archery or flying a plane). Thicken fog to make perception penalties even worse. Lighten a fog by strengthening the 'clear' air in the area. Purify air to make it more breathable.
                            Manipulate: Create a light wind (great for scattering papers and causing minor chaos indoors). Do CPR without touching someone. Change the direction the wind is blowing. Create an area of 'thin' air, likely causing some penalties to chases or other in depth athletics. Clear fog away entirely (the fog oriented things probably also fall under water, btw).
                            Control: Create a much stronger gust, likely capable of knocking people down or causing huge problems to flying creatures/vehicles. Create a small cyclone. Cause the air to follow you, forming a bubble underwater or in other similar conditions. Cause a vacuum to suffocate someone, snuff fires out, and similar. Perform acts akin to clumsy telekinesis using much more concentrated movements of air. Breathe without needing to expand or contract your lungs (likely worth some stealth bonuses, help you appear dead).
                            Create: Again, all sorts of things. Create air in a vacuum or when already underwater. Do more finesse-oriented telekinesis, maybe even fly, depending on your ST. Create more dangerous cyclones or other bits of locally oriented weather.

                            Earth (I'm assuming Metal is its own influence if not in raw ore form):
                            Strengthen: Bonuses to stone weaponry and similar, more damage with thrown rocks or the like. Harden the ground, dirt, stone, or natural barriers, making it hard to break them. Make quicksand less dangerous. More easily cut diamonds (by hardening a diamond based tool). Intensify an earthquake locally. Make a gemstone appear to be worth more.
                            Manipulate: Trip people up on land by making it shift and move a bit. Remove your own penalties to move across rough terrain. Shape stonework more easily. Create miniature landslides, or large ones where it's already fairly hazardous and loose. Bend/warp tools made with natural stone or the like. Walk safely across quicksand or across mud without getting stuck. Cause a minor tremor.
                            Control: Turn stones from one kind to another (easy Resources from turning rocks to diamonds comes to mind). Shape a stone (perfectly 'cut' diamonds, for instance) completely with no other effort. Cause a foundation to shift enough to collapse a building. Create small earthquakes. Create weapons from raw materials in an instant. Create a larger landslide from nothing. Trap people by making the earth or stone jut out and grab them, or form around them. Create little 'natural grenades' by making rocks explode upon impact.
                            Create: Again, a lot, but a few examples that come to mind. Bury someone alive out of nowhere. Fill a room, trapping everyone inside. Create a much more noticeable earthquake (locally). Create gemstones and other valuables, or natural weapons out of nowhere.


                            And of course, when you use the facet that expands the radius of these, your options grow even more.

                                The Gift of the Elementals is a challenging one because it's comparatively freeform next to a lot of other supernatural powers that are available. Exactly how you might attack someone with Influence (Fire), for example, depends on your level of mastery of it and the context. Someone already on fire? Use Strengthen to increase the damage they're taking. Want to lash someone with flame from a torch or whatnot? Use the Control dice pool as an attack pool, with damage dealt based on the flame intensity as normal. No fire around? Just snap your fingers and Create fire, using the same style of attack but no need for a source.
