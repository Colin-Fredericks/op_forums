                                Hello,

                                just a few thoughts:

                                1. The sunlight issue
                                In my opinion, you should'nt make this easy for the vampire. Sunlight is the primary ban for vampires, and it should always stay a problem. However, for low-blood potency high-humanity vampires, it is not that much of an issue. Additionally, the according frenzy trigger is that the vampire get hurt by sunlight, which a young vampire can avoid easily in many situations. Cruac should offer some tricks to make unlife at day easier in the future, especially with reagards to waking up and maybe enduring sunlight for a short amount of time on very high levels.

                                Sun in Hisil does not have to be a problem in my opinion. It is the sun that burns the vampire, not some strange spiritual reflection of it. Certainly, from the viewpoint of the werewolves, Helios is the sun, but it should be the viewpoint of the vampire that counts here. Not sure if there are any rules, but I would let sunlight damage a vampire only in the "real" world.

                                2. Crossing the gauntlet
                                Here are a few ideas:
                                - Give a pack member or the pack totem the ability to transport the vampire to the other side
                                - An artifact: There is an item that allows for crossing the gauntlet like a werewolf. But where does it come from? Maybe there are hidden costs?
                                - The witch: A powerful member of the circle can grant our vampire the ability to cross the gauntlet for some time by means of a powerful spell (Yes, according to the book that isn't possible, but I never liked that rule. ). But what is her price? She surely has her own agenda and reasons...

                                3. Speaking to spirits
                                You should grant her the ability to speak with spirits early on. If she knows Cruac, it could be possible with a low level spell to speak their language.

                                4. Werewolf forms
                                I see no reason why a vampire should have the ability to shapeshift like a werewolf. In combat, a vampire can keep up with her fighting disciplines. Faster running and hightened senses can also be granted by disciplines and merits. In my opinion, a vampire should be a vampire and not an undead werewolf copy.
