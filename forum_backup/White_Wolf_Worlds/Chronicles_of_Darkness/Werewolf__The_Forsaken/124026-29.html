            That is pretty well in the same boat, though unlike the werewolf claw thing, it is a wide ranging capability, able to apply to everything, whereas the supernatural damage currently only applies to possible werewolf specific antagonists, and vampires. Its basically a targeted 'vampire killer' capability the way it was originally developed. With the current iteration, its still vampire killer, but has the drawback of being a bite, and I don't particularly like that its one or the other, when personally I thought both teeth and claws should have been Gauru only in regards to vampires. I still don't know why it couldn't just have been "Hurts spirits".

            Mind you werewolves do have other capabilities that vampires do not, just as vampires do. Vampires aren't going to be attacking from the Shadow, nor will they be able to follow. Vampires and werewolves both have a form of predatory aura (renown flaring's hunt capability that werewolves have), in the case of Werewolf, this is far more powerful. They don't have to be able to innately target vampires to be optimized for their own game. Fire and sunlight are much more common than silver, and spirit negotiations are very unlikely to happen with vampires.

            So much wanting to push a "Can kill vampires" capability, when there's a wide range of advantages they have that can be leveraged against vampires and other groups. Tailoring capabilities specifically to combat another splat is a bad trend to start in any case, especially when there's unlikely anything short of maybe Promethean and maybe Mummy and those are big maybes, if they get updates. Most likely the majority of splats will require some Arcana/Transmutation/etc to do the same.

            Also, I think the "Blood Bond affects other supernaturals" ruling came from this argument, and as a potential drawback for hurting vampires with the bite. It wasn't even given an official ruling until this and its basically clone in the Vampire forum came up with such contentious arguing.

            Nearly everything has a healing capability, if werewolf claws/bites took double time/resource trait to heal, that would be better than something that really applies to just one other splat.

            Also, in the blog there was no mention of them losing the Armor. Also liked your earlier suggestion of an essence per scene to make them supernatural weapons in regard to vampires/spirits/whatever. A cost would have been preferable to scrapping claws altogether.
            But by your own reasoning one line shouldn't say a damn thing about how something they do specifically affects another line. By that logic Vampire shouldn't say a damn thing about what does and doesn't affect werewolves/mages/whatever. That should be left up to the ST. Do you agree with that? Do you agree that werewolves shouldn't explicitly be subject to the bloodbond? If you don't, you're being a bit of a hypocrite. If you're fine with that you're saying it's fine for vampire to call out werewolf and explicitly say that one of their features (for better or for worse) messes with them when you so strongly fight against the reverse.
