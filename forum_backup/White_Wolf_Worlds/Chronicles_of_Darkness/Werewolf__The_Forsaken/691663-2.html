        GIFT OF INK

        Cunning: The weresquid can shoot a burst of ink into someone's eyes, temporarily blinding them. Spend 1 Essence and roll Dexterity + Athletics + Cunning - Defense. On a success, she inflicts the Blinded Tilt.

        Honor: The weresquid can create a cloud of ink on land the same way he can in the water. This costs 1 Essence and creates an Environmental Tilt (perhaps something like the Darkness Lair Trait from Beast: The Primordial).

        Purity: Add the weresquid's Purity rating to the duration of his ink cloud. Additionally, for 1 Essence, the Kun-shulor can designate a number of individuals up to his Purity rating exempt from the obfuscating effects of his ink; they can see through it as easily as if it were water.

        Wisdom: With a touch, the weresquid can alter the shape of ink, including printed media. She spends 1 Essence and rolls Wits + Expression + Wisdom. On a success, the ink warps to her desire, lasting either 24 hours (for printed media and other lasting forms of ink), until the ink dissipates (such as a cloud in the water), or until the weresquid wills it otherwise (on an exceptional success). She might alter the text on a flyer, reshape a tattoo into a different design, keep an ink cloud centered on one individual, etc.

            GIFT OF THE DEPTHS

            (NB: Unlike other Squid Gifts, the Gift of the Depths may be acquired by other werethings as a Shadow Gift. Finding the bathypelagic spirits that can carve the Facets and convincing them to do so is another matter entirely.)

            Cunning: The weresquid becomes bioluminescent. In any form, he may emit a soft glow from his skin for no cost. The light is about as bright as a candle. In darkness, he gains one other trick: He may cause the patterns of his bioluminescence to shift deceptively and attractively. Choose a target, spend 1 Essence and roll Manipulation + Expression + Cunning - Composure. On a success, the target gains the Compulsion Condition to draw toward the strange lights.

            Honor: The weresquid no long has to worry about drowning. In every form she can breathe both water and air. Environmental penalties in water are also reduced for her by her Honor: entangling kelp, strong currents, low visibility, arctic waters, etc.

            Purity: Abyssal gigantism is a phenomenon by which animals that inhabit the lower depths of the ocean tend to grow much larger than their shallower counterparts, such as the deepwater stingray, the king of herrings, the Japanese spider crab, or the colossal squid. Anyway, a weresquid with this Facet may, as a reflexive action, spend a point of Essence to increase her Size by 1 for the rest of the scene. She may keep spending Essence one-for-one to continue growing, gaining up to her Purity rating in Size this way.

            Wisdom: By reflexively spending a point of Essence, the weresquid gains a type of blindsense for the rest of the scene. He may detect things through the way they disturb the air, water, and earth around them. This works best against things that are moving; to detect the presence of immobile objects or creatures, the weresquid must succeed on a Perception roll.

                    11-14-2015, 06:01 PM
                    Patrick opened the door to his apartment, tired from a day’s work, but also satisfied, and for a moment he did not hear the music softly wafting from an anterior room. The lights in the hall and kitchen were switched off, as was his bedroom left; the only illumination came from the soft evening glow of a few west-facing windows. Patrick closed his eyes and savored the small differences between near-darkness and darkness, or epipelagic and aphotic as he had spent the day describing to rapt schoolchildren, or creeping dread and mortal terror as the many-limbed thing that had years ago eaten his soul liked to evoke.

                    It was when his eyes were closed that his ears first detected a few faint notes, an acoustic guitar’s chord, accompanied by a tenor voice whose words were still inaudible. Even then for a moment Patrick thought that the music was wafting in from a neighboring residence, but a new glimmer of realization traced the sound to the living room at the end of the hallway.

                    Patrick felt himself tense, his nerves on high alert. The remnants of human instinct prepared his body for either fight or flight, hormonal encoders that no ethereal kraken could fully purge. The music continued; it was a vaguely-familiar melody, a song Patrick was sure he hadn’t listened to in some time.
                    The living room was also dark, save for the faint gray of twilight. As Patrick crept to the threshold, the music’s sonic outline became clearer. He recognized the lyrics now, partly from their new clarity, partly from a distant memory.

                    A moment later he also distinguished the outline of a human figure sitting on an Ottoman in the far corner of the room. It shifted as Patrick stood on the threshold. His eyes had no need to adjust to the darkness, and so he also made out the crude paper mask affixed to the intruder’s face. It had almond-shaped holes cut into it, but otherwise was featureless. The music was coming from a neighboring speaker connected to an old cd player.

                    “This is my favorite song on the mix cd you gave me,” the figure suddenly said, its—his—voice somewhat hoarse.

                    Patrick had a moment of realization. “Tim?” he asked, his voice somewhat nervous. This was a person he had believed he would never see again. It had been years since they had last spoken. Patrick’s mouth suddenly felt and tasted as if he had just swallowed a large gulp of seawater as a faint buzzing coursed through the back of his head. This was new. The sensation cut off the “Fuck you, this isn’t your story” he was preparing to lob. His ex-lover was no Hero, but instead Kin.

                    This, of course, still did not answer the question of why Tim was sitting in Patrick’s living room, in the dark, wearing a mask, and playing the mix cd Patrick had gifted him during their relationship.

                    In the tense moment of silence between the two, the song ended. It was replaced by a ballad comparing amorous feelings to fermented juniper berries.
                    Tim stood, slowly, like a wad of paper uncrumpling. His mask shifted.

                    “Mother wants me to eat your brain,” he rasped, as the first tentacles began to snake from beneath the mask, “but I’d much rather eat your heart, you see.”
                    Patrick took a step back—out of surprise, he told himself, rather than actual fear—as he evaluated the situation. Tim was Kin, but was he. . . “Mother”? He didn’t seem to possess a Horror, so what could he. . .

                    The sudden migraine cut off his train of thought. It felt like his neurons had been dipped in acid. Patrick grasped his head and fell to his knees as Tim approached. Patrick managed to look up; at some point Tim had removed the flimsy paper mask from his face. His head resembled something one would find in a deep-sea trench, or in a horror film about aliens. Maybe both. Despite the unfortunate and uncomfortable situation Patrick was in, the kraken in his soul felt a sympathy for the kind of fear this new form Tim had taken on might inspire in tremulous land-dwellers. A high-pitched grinding sensation in his occipital lobe kept Patrick from forming a coherent sentence.

                    Tim was now standing directly in front of, and above, Patrick. The modern-casual style of his jeans and flannel shirt contrasted with the rubbery appearance of his flesh, the mass of tentacles surrounding his mouth, his enlarged eyes. To Patrick’s surprise, Tim’s first action was to run a hand across the side of Patrick’s head, brushing a lock of hair over his right ear. The look of what remained human in his face made Patrick think of longing and sadness.

                    It is true that Tim had not taken the break-up very well.

                    For a moment, Patrick thought of Ahmed. Ahmed was busy with his own things that evening, investigating a puzzling mystery with a cadre of necromancers. Patrick hoped that he was having a much better time than he was, then wished Ahmed was there with him, then idly wondered what Tim would think of Ahmed.

                    At that moment the sun had set just enough more to plunge the twilit room into total darkness.

                    Dark like the bottom of the sea, Patrick thought. Unbeknownst to him, Tim had thought this too, but that was of no importance right then.

                    Patrick closed his eyes, bowed his head, and as he felt the brush of a clammy, boneless appendage on his brow, he summoned an ocean.

                    Three moments later, two very large cephalopods were entangled in the lightless depths of a sea without a surface, while the last echoes of a soft melody faded.

                            Kinship Nightmare: Garbled Nonsense (Weresquid)

                            There’s this awful ringing in your head. You can’t keep anything straight. Thoughts slip out of your head before you can fully think them. Maybe you should be getting more sleep, or stronger coffee.

                            Dice Pool: Intelligence + Satiety - Resolve

                            Normal Effect: All mental actions suffer a -3 penalty for the duration of the Nightmare.

                            High Satiety: The target’s thoughts are drowned in static and staccato, besieged by bizarre imagery. Whenever the target fails a mental roll, he takes a Madness Condition that’s resolved when the Nightmare ends.

                            Satiety Expenditure: For the duration of the Nightmare, all information-gathering powers are also scrambled. Auspex, Omen Sensitivity, Telepathy, Clairvoyance, Mimir’s Wisdom, and similar abilities cannot be activated; attempting to just gives the victim a headache and tinnitus.

                            Exceptional Success: The Nightmare lasts for 24 hours.

                                            FINDING MEANING IN THE ABSURD

                                            After getting over the initial reaction that they are now a kid who is also a squid, a Kun-shulor has to figure out their relationship with the Deep Maker. Obeying the Maker's implanted instincts feels not unpleasant, and comes with perks, but will sooner or later put you into an awkward moral or social (or physical) position. Disobeying means you're more of your own free squid, but also comes with Harmony gain, distancing you from your spirit half, and moreover, the instincts get louder and louder as pressure builds. . .

                                            Figuring out one's existence as a cephalopodic shapeshifter revolves around two axes: The first is what doing all these things for the Deep Maker is meant to accomplish. The second is whether or not that's a good thing.

                                            A common belief among the Kun-shulor is that the Deep Maker is currently asleep, or trapped, or in a near-death state, and each task completed for it gives it a bit more strength, that fulfilling these instincts delivers to its underwater abode a jolt of occult Essence. Eventually, with enough deep instincts obeyed, the Maker will free itself, and do. . . something. Maybe it will return to the Shadow (or wherever it came from). Maybe it will destroy the world, or remake it in its own image, become a new god for a new age, with the weresquids as its favored servitor angels.

                                            Other Kun-shulor think that the Deep Maker will never break free of its oneiric prison, and so it has set up the weresquids as its abovewater agents, playing a game of cosmic chess with other powerful entities. Every strange command it sends from the depths is to accomplish some purpose mortal minds can hardly fathom, singular moves that drive forward the occult physics of the universe. Perhaps what the weresquids do is even necessary to maintain the world as it is now, and without the larceny, gaslighting, and brain-eating, things would fall apart completely.

                                            Others still, especially those who have gone to visit the Deep Maker in person, think that there is no higher purpose to all of this, that the Deep Maker has gone mad from its imprisonment, or was always a lunatic, a blind idiot god from the beginning of time, that its choosing of the weresquids is pure chance, their missions nothing but the nonsensical whims of a dead dreamer. That the Kun-shulor only derive spiritual power from obeying the Deep Maker because it has carved its Essence into their souls.

                                            Their are stranger beliefs still among the squids. No tribal customs or traditions bind them together, so everyone has to find their own way, from what they can piece together on their own and from what others of their kind (as well as other denizens of the World of Darkness) can tell them. No one's gone to war over what they think of the Deep Maker, but conflicts of interest do arise when you are still quite attached to humanity while the neighboring shoal has no qualms about turning your hometown into the next Innsmouth.

                                                THE FORMS

                                                Hishu
                                                Terrestrial
                                                Perception +1
                                                Sheep's Clothing

                                                Fa'ra
                                                Dexterity +1, Stamina +1
                                                Terrestrial
                                                Consume Brain (3 rounds)
                                                Perception +2
                                                Mild Lunacy
                                                Defense vs Firearms
                                                Psychic Shock: A weresquid in this form can temporarily override the neural receptors in his prey's brain, leaving him exposed. The weresquid must make eye contact and roll Intelligence + Primal Urge, contested by the prey's Resolve + Primal Urge. If the weresquid wins, the prey receives the Stunned Tilt for one round.


                                                Kirul
                                                Strength +2, Dexterity +3, Stamina +1
                                                Amphibious
                                                Ink
                                                Consume Brain (1 round)
                                                Tentacles/Beak (+2L)
                                                Boneless
                                                Size +2
                                                Perception +3
                                                Full Lunacy
                                                Hunger (as the Gauru-form's Rage, but with the Feeding Frenzy)
                                                Defense vs Firearms
                                                Enhanced Regeneration
                                                Primal Madness: The weresquid's prey can only apply their Dexterity or Wits to Defense. Anyone the weresquid in Kirul successfully attacks or grapples gains the Insensate Tilt.


                                                Kafu'ul
                                                Strength +2, Dexterity +2, Stamina +2
                                                Aquatic
                                                Ink
                                                Tentacles/Beak (+1L)
                                                Boneless
                                                Size +1
                                                Perception +3
                                                Moderate Lunacy
                                                Defense vs Firearms
                                                Big Hugs: When a weresquid in Kafu'ul succeeds at a grapple, she may perform two grapple moves at once. If she chooses Hold or Immobilize, she may still perform other grapple moves. She may inflict the Arm Wrack, Leg Wrack, or Knocked Down Tilt as a grapple move.


                                                Adalku
                                                Dexterity +2, Stamina +1
                                                Aquatic
                                                Ink
                                                Tentacles/Beak (+0L)
                                                Boneless
                                                Size -1
                                                Perception +4
                                                Entangled: Once per round, the weresquid may spend 1 Essence to make a grapple move as a reflexive action against someone either the weresquid is already grappling, or who is within tentacle's length distance.

                                                        ". . . a weresquid must be in either fa'ra or kirul to consume a person's brain (straight from the head anyway). First, he must establish a grapple. Then he must perform a special new grapple move called Consume Brain. In fa'ra, the weresquid must use this move three times in a row to fully extract the brain, while as the monstrous kirul, he needs only a single turn of insertion. When performing the Consume Brain move, the weresquid wraps his buccal tentacles around the victim's head and digs into the back of the skull. Every turn that the weresquid uses the Consume Brain move, he inflicts his net successes in lethal damage as he opens up his victim's head. This does mean that certain beings with powerful enough regeneration can stall or even completely deter a Kun-shulor's efforts. A human that somehow survives a brain-eating attempt will require immediate hospitalization and intensive care, followed by innumerate psychotherapy sessions."
