            Originally posted by Newb95 View Post

            Does silver work even in hishu form?
            I’ve missed a lot of this thread due to sleeping (time zones!) but on aquick skim didn’t see that anyone had directly addressed this.

            Silver works in any form. Unlike Garou (Werewolf the Apocalypse), Uratha don’t have a breed form that isn’t vulnerable to silver. Uratha are werewolves in all their forms, all of which are as natural and comfortable as the others.

            Their auspice provides some protection, however. The silver has to be 80%+ pure, and it gas to be a weapon. They can touch silver cutlery or jewelry without damage. It may not be comfortable but it’s not damaging. The Pure werewolves don’t have this protection (having removed their auspices) - silver always burns them.
