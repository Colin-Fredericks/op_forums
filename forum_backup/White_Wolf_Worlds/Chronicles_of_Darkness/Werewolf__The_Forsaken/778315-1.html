                I don't think there's going to be a winning argument in, "A Ghost Wolf's life experiences should be worth more mechanically." I can easily make a character concept that could be made with less dots than creation allows, or would require more dots than the creation spread allows. Ghost Wolves don't have some universal experience that Tribal Uratha don't. The struggles they deal with are not inherently worth more than the struggles another werewolf deals with joining a Tribe.

                There's also a significant issue with the idea that Ghost Wolves should have a mechanical advantage other werewolves do not. A Ghost Wolf can always join a Tribe, gaining what they didn't get in creation. Unless whatever they would have is lost in the process, they'd actually end up better off than characters just made in a Tribe in first place.

                So, what sort of bonus should they get? If we give them 3XP, and say they get the perks of joining a Tribe for free (including a Renown dot), they're 3 XP up unless they lost 3XP in buying now-favored Gifts. Etc.

                What would be an appropriate advantage that would get lost if they join a Tribe? How does it make sense in-character?
