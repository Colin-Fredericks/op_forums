                            Originally posted by Laraqua View Post
                            Curious about other people's perspectives. Take one small city of 1.6 million people. Only small isolated wounds here and there. Nothing massively nasty in the spirit world. Let's say, though, that another supernatural group managed to cull a number of the fractious werewolves which were always in small numbers in a city which had already had large tracts of gauntlet unpatrolled. What would you have happen to the city? And how noticeable would it be? Just curious in terms of how important other GMs make the werewolves to the health of their areas.
                            My Chronicle is set in a New York that hasn't seen a permanent pack of wolves for ten years. I cheated and wiped the Hisil too. But I only did that after I considered the ramifications of not having a single pack in NYC for that long.

                            Spirit Resonance
                            First any human city will generate a metric fuck ton of Essence. And most of it will come from the most emotionally charged events and actions in the city. In Nwod terms this is certainly murders, rapes, riots, and extreme violent accidents. Not much good happens among Men anymore. What rare celebrations exist are fleeting.
                            This means the most abundant essence other than that of raw ‘city’ resonance is violence, hate, and pain. Meaning that most conceptual choirs are ruled by it. Wolf pack naturally compete with this resonance. They hunt it down balance out their own turf. No one likes sleeping on a blood soaked mattress made of knives. Least of all wolves.
                            SO without packs it only made sense the city would fall apart through the feed-back loop of Hisil to Material to Hisil. I couldn’t let that be. But that is least of the city’s many dooms.

                            Ridden
                            Next come the Ridden. Spirits that reach out (perhaps because the city’s Hisil resonance is choked on blood) to find a host to generate their nourishment. Without wolves, the only other Supernatural aware and capable enough to stop Ridden are Mages. So it would make sense that with 80% of the policing force missing the Ridden would grow powerful enough to form pseudo Choirs of their own. A dark image indeed. Dark enough I let them stick around. Don’t tell my players.

                            Hosts
                            Ridden may be bad, but the Host take the cake. The Beshilu would eat the city inside out. They largely lack the foresight to manage the human population, and if they did have that foresight it would mean the worst has come true. The Beshilu are organized under a proper Rat King.
                            The only hope the city would have ironically is an infestation of Azlu. The Spider Hosts would separate the Hisil far enough from the City to allow both to survive longer apart then they would together. Though the separation would mean a host of other issues.

                            Other SuperNats
                            Vamps would hardly understand why the city would be crumbling around them. I’m not up to date on Vamp. But I can only see the Ordu being able stave off the inevitable.
                            For Mages, the need for Spirit Shamans would mean the Thyrsus Path would have a butt-load of power in the city. I would expect the Stone Book to seize power, likely through the Arrow.
                            Hunters might figure out some of the problem but their lack of spiritual weight would hamstring their efforts.
                            Changelings and the rest would just be boned.

                            TL;DR:
                            I opted to just remove the City’s representation altogether and explain it through plot. My wolves threw off the dearth of ‘city’ when they established their turf boundary with the Hunting Ground Rite. Their turf is ‘growing’ back quickly while also standing as a juxtaposition to the rest of the empty city.
                            It beat having to imagine all the horrible calamities the city would otherwise be subject too. Taking back such a broken City didn’t seem appropriate for new-born wolves fresh form their change.

                                Resurrecting an old thread, I know... but I felt the need to point out that this premise exists in canon.

                                One of the many weird (and tbh, slightly objectionable) decisions that OP made with the suggested Australian setting materials was exactly this premise. Beast: The Primordial pp257-258 puts forward a version of the city of Adelaide (a city of between 1.3 and 1.4 million people) that has - apparently barring a trucker werewolf who passes through periodically - exactly one supernatural residing in it who seems to murder every other supernatural who comes there, and that this has been the case for about 150 years. Has it been the same supernatural the whole time? Who knows. What is that supernatural exactly? Who knows.

                                Bear in mind, this is the fifth most populated city on the continent and a state capital. It'd be like if Phoenix, Arizona was canonically written to only have one supernatural NPC in it.

                                What are the consequences of there canonically being no werewolves in Adelaide? Well... it's just Adelaide. So... I guess the consequences are that there are two vertically stacked metallic spheres at a shopping mall known as "the mall balls" for some reason?

                                The Yozis are right (and you should suffer and obey for all eternity).
