              I have set various games in the Australia Outback; I create games with an emphasis on the sheer isolation of the Australia Outback, some pack claim territory larger then countries (it’s actual control on the other hand is very low), and most packs of Uratha claim territory far larger than the rest of the world. Pack is far more important than tribe, it’s not that they don’t they don’t find tribal identification important, it’s that Outback Uratha are clannish and frankly a little xenophobic. The most defining characteristic, however, is that the isolation allows the Uratha to be far more open about themselves, they've no need to hide from the herd, it largely doesn't exist in the outback. Without any need to hide, the conflict between Pure vs Forsaken is far volatile then elsewhere.

              Ignore everything you read about Australia, and Indigenous Australians, in the Lodge of the Firestick. It's depiction of Indigenous Australians is not only inaccurate, it's also incredibly offensive. I believe the author mistaken what is an incredibly offensive ethnic slur; it’s like calling someone the N-word, for what they thought was a generic term for white people. Sadly, White Wolf has never been very successful at depicting Australia in print.

              edit: I come off far more harsh then I intend to be, okay, they're not even close to accurate and came off as a little offensive; but at least they're not ignoring us, like we don't even exist or only deserving a couple of sentences. We're a continent, we exist, would it kill ya to put more Australian stuff in the book?
