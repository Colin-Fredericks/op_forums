    I honestly see no reason not to include the Weaver, Wyld, and Wyrm As-Is in the new world of darkness, and just have them as slightly more distant forces than they were in oWoD. The Wyrm represents Entropy and (because of a snafu in the nature of reality) Corruption. But instead of falling into the trap of making every "bad" spirit wyrm-tainted, only have the direct influence of the Wyrm show up with the truly corrupt and fucked-up spirits. The wyrm can operate on a much more macro level, such that most people (and even most werewolves) never really encounter its direct servitors, and once you start to you know you're in really, really deep.

    Basically, I see no reason it's strictly necessary to downgrade the Wyrm from "underpinning of the universe" into "something Father Wolf hunted" or "just some really big spirit". It works fine as it was.

            The more I think about it, The more I start to realize that the Wyrm has the deck very stacked against it in "Default" nWoD.

            Most entites in W:tF will oppose it to the point perhaps uniting the Forsaken, Pure, and maybe even some of the Moon-Banished (even some of the Idigam have agendas which would be disrupted if the world was killed off) along with very powerful spirits.

            The Exarchs and Archmagi would look down to see that the world they depend on is getting thrashed and while the Exarchs are dicks about most things, destroying elements of their oppression would get their attention. Same with the Pentacle.

            Hunter compacts and conspiracies from not only Cheiron but the Lucifuge (assuming the Wyrm is an infernal demon), The Union, Network Zero and more would be very unhappy with a "monster production line" of Pentex.

            Don't know about the Deathlords(going off Raven's vision of them being overworked trying to get everyone to pass on, they may take interest in the world not ending purely to keep their workload manageable and not having the dead walk the earth) but the Judges are like the Exarchs in respect, they want to keep mummy's cults operational and more importantly, keep sending them crap in Duat. Again, something that would be highly inconvenienced with the world ending.

            The Empyrean which was hinted at in WoD: Inferno.

            Elements of the Inferno who don't want the world killed off because they love it (stated outright in the book).

            And then you have the God-Machine who's sole purpose seems to be built on preventing the world from ending and backing it up from saved points every so often when it does (Seattle for instance).

            The wild cards would be the Fae (can't see Arcadia burning from the Wyrm), The Principle (mostly because it does it's own thing because it's the Principle), and maybe the Aeons.

            The Abyss of course would be very happy with the Wyrm for ending the world and thus it's suffering but that's about it.

            This is not to say that the Wyrm can't blow up the default nWoD, it's just gonna have a very, very hard time trying to get pass entities who either benefit from the status quo or who's sole job is preserving it in doing so.

                I've had the idea in my head for a little while now that Pentex and the subsidiaries are a deal between some clever Bale Hounds and some humans looking for power. Union cells protesting labor practices, Keepers of the Source protesting the environmental record, the Barrett Commission investigating some trading deals, etc.
