                That one is far harder than the Vampire version, if only because I don't know as much (by far) about the Forsaken as I do about the Kindred (which is sad, considering that if prefer werewolves over vampires in the folklore). Still, there is some things that I'm sure of.

                First thing first, I wouldn't be a Pure. Just their name displease me. I hate peoples who think that they are "better" or "purer" than others for reasons of blood, ancestry, creed or faith. SO, obviously, out with them.

                Now, the Forsaken... it's a little hard, because I don't dig too much the whole "sin of the father" angle they have. In the same time, they have things to back up their stories, even their hated enemies believes that they have indeed sinned in the past... so I could either be a Forsaken or a Ghost Wolf leaning toward them. So, I guess it would be something like that.

                Auspice : Cahalith

                I like to tell stories. I love to learn about history, legends and all. Obviously, Luna would see it fitting to make me pursue my passion in her service (or she could be fickle and says "Hey, you've got some times when you let your anger get the best of you ! Why don't you become a Rahu ?)). But she wouldn't be like that, right ? Right ?
                I'm not much of a fighter, nor a fit addict, but I'm not too out of shape for my age, so I pretty much thinks I would be an adventurous Cahalith. I would probably write down the history of my pack and our discoveries in the form of fantasy novels so as to prevent humans from learning about us by accident.

                Tribe : Bone Shadows

                I'm a lore nut, and so are they. A perfect match ! Plus, I tend to believe in the "mundane" supernatural (gypsy cruses, amulets, spirits, etc.), so I think I could join them pretty easily, even while maintaining my belief that the world must be made better socially and environnementaly.

                Usual life : As a Werewolf, I would probably help my pack to claim and then protect our territory... if I can get some privacy at times. I really need some moments of solitude to read and contemplate and think and create. I would also seek to forge beneficial pacts with minor spirits, learning the First Tongue and trying to convince humans to better the place my pack and I would call our territory. For my tribe, I would try to discover ancient knowledge, first locals and then globals.

                As an elder Werewolf : I would be trying my best to pass down informations and knowledge to the younger Uratha, while lamenting their lack of care for such history earned at the risk of our pelts by my packmates and I.

                                            Skimming the 2ed Core, I find myself, surprisingly, drawn to the Elodoth, purely by the fluff of it. Irraka or Rahu work, too, but Elodoth definitly fits me the well, probably the most (close second would be Irraka).
                                            So let's got with Elodoth.
                                            Of the tribes, the Bone Shadows seem like something I might end up in, as the spirit of Exploration and Knowledge-gathering it entails speaks to me, albeit I might join the Hunters in Darkness purely to be part of the Lodge of the Hook Hand.
                                            However, let us for now assume a Bone Shadow Elodoth as I am now, and an Hook Hand Hunter in Darkness Irraka if I could chose.

                                            So, what kind of Uratha would I be?
                                            Unsurprisingly, I would be the guy trying to learn stuff about this new world, learn how to deal with spirits and how to keep them in check; I'd try to charter new waters, and try to keep peace and unity both within my pack, and with other packs.
                                            Does not mean I would stop thinking about how I could take you down should you become a threat, or that I have a problem with taking the other side in a confrontation, even if that side is not exactly my own beliefs - I quite often take a contrary position in discussion simply because I enjoy debating, something that drives some people nuts =) My secondary role would be to mingle, get information. I usually establish connections to other people quite quickly, and if you throw me into a room of a people, chances are I will come out with having made a few friendly acquaintances - even if it was a meeting of e.g. football (for the americans: soccer) enthusiasts (something I have zero interest in). I can muster some interest for anything, if necessary, at the very least for the passion people have for something. Those connections aren't very deep though - I'd probably be the connection burner type, despite lacking the necessary charisma you'd need for that imo.
                                            I've always been a fan of fighting, and fighting styles, especially barehanded and old-school weapons like sword, staffs and axes, and practised, tried out and dabbled in some myself. Seeing how that would become more important in my new lifestyle, I would definitly do a lot more fighting and physical training.
                                            I do not know if I'd keep my current path in life - I feel I would definitly want to stay/go back to University, and enroll in something like History or Computer Science, to acquire some additional skills that could come in handy. Alternatively, I might go back to the Military, to brush up other skills like Survival, Firearms and Expression.

                                            Don't know enough about elders yet, but I assume I would be the guy that others would come to ask for help in settling a dispute, or a fair judgement. I'd also consider it my responsibility to take cubs in and give them some much needed assistance, by directing them to the right mindset, helping them ask the right questions. Afte all, giving answers is never as good a lesson as figuring it out for yourself.I wouldn't necessarily infringe on the Spirit laws and stuff, since many of that is Ithaeur territory, but there are many things cubs need to learn. A diplomat, and a teacher, basically.

                                            As Irraka, this would only chance insofar that eliminating foes would rise up in priority, so I would focus more on becoming stealthy and deadly. Doesn't mean I cannot help people talk it out, but my means would be different. Also, as an Irraka Elder, I would be a dangerous Assassine, but I'd still try and make sure young cubs know how to step and not make tons of noise, how to strike so that there won't be a reply, and relay my experiences in general.

                                            At least, that is my current impression, haven't finished the book (not by a long shot) yet, so... take this with a grain of salt.

                                            But yeah. Elodoth seems pretty accurate.

                                                Giving this a try (keep in mind there are many things i don't know about forsaken so there could be some misinterpretation on my part)

                                                The most important thing that will go toward my werewolf identity would be my auspice. Which would be much more important to me than my tribe or even my pack.

                                                So first the auspice:
                                                I'm big into wisdom as a virtue but i think that for my auspice i would be an Irraka. In general I prefer not being noticed and I actually need to make a specific effort to make noise when i move so that i dont startle people. Also (and that could just be in my head) i have the feeling that i can tell where peoples blind spots are and i feel more comfortable there. The more i think about it the more that i feel irraka is the choice for me.

                                                Next the pack:
                                                Although i, myself, likes cunning and wisdom, i actually think i'd prefer to be in a pack that values honor and wisdom.

                                                Last the tribe:
                                                No tribe really sticks out to me sadly. I could come back to the fact i value wisdom highly and join the bone shadows and i'm certainly into the investigative aspect of the hunt, so that could work. But i'm not really interested in hunting spirits above others (i find the host more interesting). Also i have this thing where i really dont like showing weakness in front of others (which i think is a storm lord thing?).
                                                I have to say, unless there is some social pressure from packmates or other werewolf into joining a tribe, i likely either wont bother at all or try to learn about all the tribe and be a marginal member of each. (is it like vampire where you can have status into more than one covenant, well tribes in this intance???).

                                                Last last, the lodge:
                                                I don't know which lodges there are but if there is one about learning secrets, forbidden knowledges (or really any knowledges, the forbidden ones are just generally more fun) and history then i'd join that lodge.

                                                So in the end, the most important thing for me is the auspice. To be chosen and given a role that suits me and that i'm expected to follow would go a long way toward making me feel ... calmer about myself, with a better sense of purpose i guess. Ugh, i'm having trouble expressing this well sorry.
                                                Anyway the short of it is that my auspice would do more toward defining who i am than my tribe would.
