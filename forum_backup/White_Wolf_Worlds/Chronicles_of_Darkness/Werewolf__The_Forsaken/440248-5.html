            I have a Changing Breeds 2.0 that I'm working on, but it's very different both mechanically and flavourwise. I changed it from "You're a therian who wants to have sex with things" to "You're a rakshasa of an ancient curse who has to control their monstrous vice, follow the hierarchy of an ancient dynasty, and go on a path of self-improvement and survive punishment for crimes that were committed long before you.". Gave it the new name of Rakshasa: the Kingdom (featuring the extinction chronicles). It was partially inspired by the previously mentioned Old One Chronicles, but in the end it became very different.

            I should probably make a thread for it actually since it's basically a fan-gameline. I've gotten down the whole introduction, all the mechanics of chapter 1, and I think I was 3/4ths the way through the character creation chapter, and put down a framework for what the antagonists will be, how the various gift/discipline/arcana equivalents work, etc.
