                This example from the beast preview might help.

                Example: A werewolf, Kyle, and a Beast, Anika, have come into conflict. Kyle is attempting to
                use Feet of Mist (the Gift of Cunning Evasion) to escape Anika. However, Anika has the Needs
                Must Atavism, which hones her senses to preternatural levels and allows her to track damn near
                anything, thus forcing the two characters enter a Clash of Wills. Kyle has three dots of Cunning
                Renown and a Primal Urge of four, so his player will roll seven dice. Anika has five dots of Lair,
                and her highest Resistance Attribute is her Composure of four dots, for a total of nine dice. Since
                Anika has expended Satiety to invoke the most powerful effect of her Atavism, which allows her
                to track a target indefinitely, her player adds four additional dice to her pool, for a total of
                thirteen dice. Neither character can spend Willpower on the roll because Kyle isn’t on the scene,
                and Anika has no way of knowing Kyle has masked his trail with spirit magic. Both players roll;
                Kyle’s player gets three successes, while Anika’s rolls an impressive five. Anika’s Predatory
                Awareness thus wins out over Kyle’s Feet of Mist, and Anika can track Kyle normally. To all
                other characters, Kyle’s trail remains imperceptible.

                                I really don't understand why people keep claiming that Beast is so overpowered. To my eye, they look like one of the weaker splats when it comes to acomplishing things, to be honest. Specifically in the fields of Combat, Investigation, Reasearch, Social Maneuvering, Chases, Crafting, hunting, socio-political-organization-fu, etc. Sure, they're better than mortals, but I generally put my money on other supernaturals in any of the above fields.

                                Anyways, on subject. To answer the opening poster's question, my rule of thumb is that a passive ability generally cannot beat an activated power that you purchased with XP. In short:
                                Active supernatural power > passive supernatural power > active innate ability > passive innate ability

                                So, a mage's passive Mage Sight won't work at noticing a Gift used to hide. No contest at all. You'd need some activated ability, and even then, Clash of Wills. So, in the case of an Uratha versus Kindred? Actively using some kind of sensory Gift to notice a vampire under Obfuscate. A werewolf using Impossible Spoor that was actively tracking the vampire? Clash of Wills as normal. A werewolf trying to track a vampire without such a Gift? Nope, auto failure. A werewolf with a Gift that gives passive Perception bonuses and not actively searching for something? No. Likewise, even if a werewolf is covered in blood, I would require Auspex to detect the werewolf if they're using a hiding Gift, despite smelling blood being an supernatural ability of supernaturally heightened Kindred senses.

                                                My appologies to the thread.

                                                Originally posted by Amravanti View Post

                                                I was joking mostly, but I don't know how you think Beasts are not amazing at all of those.
                                                Simple really. All the powers you mention have very significant restrictions on their usage. Lets say we have a vamp, wolf, mage, 'ling, and beast all living together for some reason. Someone murders their neighbor.

                                                The vampire has access to Auspex and a couple rituals that allow tracking. The mage can do almost any of it depending on the Arcana. The werewolf can sniff out the killer immediately. The changeling, I imagine, can arrange a goblin vow.

                                                A Beast? Their tracking abilities rely on either 1) resonating with their Hunger, or 2) marking a target. And, even in the case of the Hunger, Needs Must doesn't track the killer specifically, only general instances that can feed one's Hunger in the immediate fascility - if the killer is long gone, there's no option. Epic Direction Sense is the only ability that's worth anything here, and pretty much everyone can do that too. Beast are the most worthless supernaturals at tracking out of the five examples because of these hard restrictions.

                                                Alien Allure gives lots of dice bonus. But given Social Maneuvering rules, you don't care about large dice pools outside of Forcing Doors/Hard Leverage. You care about Impressions and auto-opening Doors. Vampires bypass the need for doors entirely with Majesty/Dominate, and the vinculum gives Perfect impression. Mages have plenty of abilities, I'm sure. Same with Changelings; hells, feeding on emotions alone can be an advantage, since the target is weaker at resisting you now. Werewolves have some pretty potent abilities with Cunning and Purity Dominate. Beasts are pretty only good at Hard Leverage/Forcing Doors, and kinda sucky at the rest. Even the Obey Me! Nightmare is kinda weak since a mortal just obeys out of fear, and will split if given the option - its only an Integrity check, and you really can't force someone to make other Integrity checks with it - good chance your victim will run away first than obey you than get a worse breaking point.

                                                Wings of the Raptor only increases your speed for a few turns, and at a high cost; Urhan can actually chase down WotR like it was nothing using the current system. Changelings are the ultimate in evading pursuit by Hedge jumping. Mages have the option to teleport, as do the Khaibit.

                                                I've done white room comparisons between a combat Giant, a Rahu, and a Daeva. Generally speaking, the Rahu and Daeva came out ahead in terms of raw power. That's not even using the one insta-death merit Uratha get either. Demons I didn't even bother going all out - they decimate the opposition with Demon Form abilities and embeds that laugh off damage, not even Going Loud. There are embeds that paralize and kill people just with a look. In this case, its primarily because Vamp, Wolf, and Demon all come with innate abilities that are applicable for combat - Beasts have no innate combat abilities, only Atavisms or spell-like Nightmares.

                                                Mirmir's Wisdom is not better than simple divination rituals you can find in Vampire or Werewolf due to a very important restiction no one else has - it can't identify people. Auspex and Werewolf Gifts can easily do all the rest of Mirmir's normal effects. Low Satiety is primarily a social effect (granting Leverage), and again only related to one's Hunger. Satiety expendature gives you access to a library. Most supernaturals just have an actual library. Best investigative tool? I'm seriously wondering how its not in competition for the worst. Its mostly reproducing mundane talents or something a Mehket could easily pull off. I don't even want to think how much Knoweldge Gifts and Arcana put it to shame.

                                                For every Atavism or Nightmare, pretty much every game has a better and more effective alternative that's not burdened with the very specific identification problem that Beast suffers. As far as I can honestly tell, people are overreacting to the high power level of 2e as a whole, and mentally comparing Beasts to 1e games. The only advantage Beasts have over any other game is being extremely XP efficent. So, they grow in power faster than anyone else, but, as starting characters? They're by far the weakest and worst at a wide variety of things. Futhermore, the XP efficency is really only good at making combat monsters, of which I didn't include in my list.


                                                And most of these restrictions are by design. Beasts are meant to have trouble at Social Maneuvering outside of Forcing Doors/Hard Leverage. Social Maneuvering is how you make Kin. And, for a Beast, Kin are both power and survival. Beasts don't play nicely. They take. They're offensive. They appeal to the worst in all of us, the worst in the supernaturals. And that's how they make enemies through crossover actions. When it comes to tracking or investigation, their powers are hyper focused on whatever appeals to their Hungers. Anything outside the purview of their Hunger gets little to no love, so we're stuck with a hyper focus on filling out your Hunger in some way. Anyone else can do good with tracking abilities - Beasts have a hard time doing so. Beasts have less organization than even Werewolves, and no abilities that manipulate social merits like every other splat; this is deliberate, because they don't care about humans.

                                                Even with a specialized, made-to-order-Beast, there are just too many holes in their abilities to make them what I consider good at these things.
