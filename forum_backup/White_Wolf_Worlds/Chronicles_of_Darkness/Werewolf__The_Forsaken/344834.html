    Alternative Shifter Origins?

    12-19-2014, 12:22 PM
    So while the WoD setting is pretty awesome, I've never been a fan of the idea that you have to be born as a Lycanthrope, as opposed to being able to become it. I was curious if there were any alternative methods, and if so, what they might include.

    A long time ago, I used to play a game called Shadowbane which had Werewolves, Werebears, and Wererats as obtainable skills. The premise was similar in that Lycanthropes were people who carried the spirits of beasts within them, but instead of it being a type of lineage, it was a skill that anyone with the proper dedication could learn. You dedicate yourself to the animal spirit and commune with it, eventually learning to take on its form. and it grew more powerful with additional training. This means that like WoD, it wasn't a virus that was passed on through infections.


    Personally, I like this version as it suggests that if someone is willing to take up the burden, they too can walk the border between physical and spiritual, helping to keep the world in balance.
