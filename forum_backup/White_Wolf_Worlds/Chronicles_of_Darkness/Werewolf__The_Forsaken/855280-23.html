                                            REFLECTION
                                            Reflection is a strange Pangaean, a shining divinity that is simultaneously an empty, echoing thing. It flits between the glistening raindrops, cavorts across the surfaces of still forest pools, and grimaces from the brightness of flame reflecting from human-polished metal. It hunts for profound truths so that it can reflect those truths for a little while, mimicking all manner of things from animals to rocks to concepts to other Pangaeans before eventually the hollowness at its core drives it further on in search of satisfaction. Sometimes, where it can, it reaches across to the other worlds to drive the creatures of Flesh or Spirit into mad carnivalesque endeavours of creating mirrored surfaces – digging pits to fill them with water, polishing surfaces to a sheen, bending and twisting ephemera into mirrors that catch spirits of light and flay them apart into captive images. These diversions never keep its attention any longer than pretending to be someone else does.

                                            The Wise fear Reflection, because it has shown a proclivity for infiltrating their own ranks on a whim in order to reflect whatever truths they might have managed to hoard. A circle of Wise gather in a village to discuss their discoveries and share their dreams, only to discover afterwards that it has eaten their souls and now slithers through the Border Marches in mimicry of their faces. The Uratha watch guardedly for Reflection, knowing it often comes with the rain or on those days where the light streams down through the clouds in a way that seems to render the world in stark clarity. The Uratha do not fear the Pangaean so much, for it is a source of power to them as much as a threat – a powerful prey indeed, but one that resonates closely with their nature.

                                            Reflection’s true appearance tends towards semi-translucence, its flesh shivering with ripples and brief impressions of abstract shapes. Long, draping curtains of presence dangle from its turgid form, trailing around it and tapering down to their bright, splinter-sharp tips. Pulses of mirror-bright reflectiveness wash across its twisted limbs and the wide, pupil-less eyes that sit upon the palms of its many hands. Raindrops bubble up from its flesh and wash down onto the ground around it. This bizarre form is rarely seen, however, for Reflection often prefers to cloak itself in the form of something else – an illusion that the sheer, reality-warping nature of its presence renders a truth.




                                            Reflection, Bright and Empty

                                            Rank 5 Pangaean

                                            Power 15 Finesse 14 Resistance 12

                                            Willpower 10 Essence 50

                                            Initiative 26 Defence 14 Speed 26 Size 13 Corpus 25

                                            Influences: Reflections 5, Water 3

                                            Numina: Aggressive Meme, Awe, Dement, Drain, Entropic Decay, Hallucination, Regenerate, Speed

                                            Arcana: Forces 1, Prime 4

                                            Ban: Reflection must offer audience and refrain from hostility to anyone who comes to it wearing a mask, although it is not bound further if the offer of audience is refused or if the visitor acts against it.

                                            Bane: A weapon wetted in water taken from seven hundred and seventy seven natural pools that have since dried up entirely.

                                            Bright Hunt: If declared the target of a hunt by a pack of Uratha, Reflection may choose to gain 50 Essence in return for complying with the hunt in the form of fleeing prey. Choosing to comply constrains Reflection to forms such as prey, natural objects or phenomena; it cannot take the form of a predator or attempt to turn the hunt around against the Uratha. It may also only use the swiftness and means of escape of the forms it copies or its own Speed, and cannot use Infinite Light to evade the hunters. Should the hunters fail to catch the Pangaean within seven hundred and seventy seven minutes, the hunt immediately fails and ends; should the hunters catch the Pangaean, the first Uratha to seize Reflection in their jaws becomes able to use the Reflection of Truth ability themselves for the next seven hunred and seventy seven days. Only a single Uratha can bear this ability at any one time; Reflection cannot choose to comply with the Bright Hunt again until that time has passed.

                                            Divine Bleed:The first time in a scene that it suffers Corpus damage, Reflection may reflexively use the Awe, Dement, Entropic Decay or Hallucination Numina for free. It may also create a Rank 1 spirit of light or darkness for free each time it suffers damage.

                                            Emptiness Within: Whenever a character succeeds at a roll based on Wits, Expression or Subterfuge while in the presence of Reflection, the Pangaean gains 1 point of Essence. If Reflection spends 1 point of Willpower, it may also initiate a Reflexive Clash of Wills against the target, with success allowing it to steal their reflection; they will quite literally, no longer present a reflection in reflective surfaces. Furthermore, their normal failures at Wits, Expression and Subterfuge rolls are aggravated into dramatic failures. While it possesses a victim’s reflection, the Pangaean can use Reflection of Truth to copy their appearance even while not in their presence; after seven hundred and seventy days, the reflection fades away and rips the victim’s soul free from their body in the process.

                                            Infinite Light: Reflection can perceive through every reflective surface within seven hundred and seventy seven miles, including raindrops, the surfaces of bodies of water, copper, and obsidian. For 5 Essence, Reflection can emerge out of any such reflective surface within that range as an Instant action.

                                            Monstrous Form: Reflection has Armour 12, and may spend 1 Essence to shrug off any Tilt it experiences. These benefits do not apply to attacks from its Banes.

                                            Overwhelming Presence: A mortal human who encounters Reflection suffers an Integrity breaking point. If this roll is failed, the character gains the Madness persistent condition.

                                            Reflection Of Truth: As an Instant action, Reflection can copy the appearance of anything in its presence at the cost of 1 point of Essence. The copy extends to voice and other elements of the mimicked target; if Reflection copied a bird, it could sing the bird’s song, fly through the air, and even alight on a branch without snapping it under the Pangaean’s actually huge bulk. Reflection does not actually change shape, merely appearing to be the thing it copies; however, although this is an illusion, it is also rendered true by Reflection’s power. As a result, supernatural powers that would detect illusions or lies will return the answer that it is, in fact, the thing it is pretending to be. This is not a mind-affecting power; someone who witnesses Reflection copy a target knows what they have seen.

                                            Symbolic Mirror: Whenever Reflection gains a Condition (or has one inflicted on it) or is the target of a supernatural effect, it can spend 5 Essence to copy the Condition or effect onto any number of additional targets as it chooses that are within seven hundred and seventy yards of its current location. After doing so, Reflection ceases to exist at its current point in time and space; instead, within seven hundred and seventy days, it will turn out to be one of the targets that the effect was copied onto, and indeed retroactively was that target all along, emerging from the chrysalis of the copied form in a shining moment of divine revelation.

                                            - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
