                            During the first turn of a chase it's likely to be a roll-off to set the edge, using best pools from both sides. This is because while urshul or urhan are better at running, an uratha in death rage isn't going to care about how it goes through things to get at its target--but it will do so cunningly and expeditiously, and probably in a manner appropriate to auspice.

                            Thanks to gauru stamina, the pursued will have at least 7 successes (5 base + 2 opponent cannot tire) to evade, probably more thanks to senses, and the rules indicate that this number may fluctuate during the chase. Further, both parties should be attempting to seize the edge during each turn.

                            But really, step back a moment: if you're looking to run from a pack of ravening, death-raging werewolves, regardless of the flee-ee's form or nature, the first question the ST should answer is "do I want the merciless death-predators to just end this character?" If the answer is anything beyond "meh, sure" then the chase scene is there for dramatic tension and to see if you care what the dice tell you.

                            I put it this way because once everyone's in death rage except a runner, either you're at a planned script point, or there has been a grave miscalculation and as ST you're deciding how to salvage the situation/campaign. If all the PCs are raging, that's another matter and you can resolve the question of "does my NPC live" with a few dice rolls behind a screen and a fade to black.

                            --Khanwulf

                            PS. It is unfortunate that the game line all about chasing down prey does not hook into the GMC chase rules--that would make things simpler.

                            PPS. It is also a trivial matter to scrape a basu-im werewolf off your tail. Run past anything else that's meat (or, I assume, a meat-substitute in the Hisil). The impulse is to kill and destroy, not to hunt a specific prey. If you're in the wilderness or someplace where you won't come across other targets, then sure, you can lead around your kuruth-ing pals until their timers finish and they black out. Otherwise, succeeding in the chase may be represented as tripping the pizza boy as you run past....
