Just like humans start with no particular skill, but can choose wich ones to focus on and that changes them (becoming a doctor, or policeman, skill and abilities define humans and what they are) or are also shaped by the circumstances of its surroundings (if you live in a place dominated by gangs you have more chances to end up in one, and get a related set of skills).

                                      It seems more like Galactus. They are the surviving spirits of a previous universe. Or perhaps just the concept of 'possibility' that has been narrowed down by reality.
                                      They are the bastard shards of the Wyrd, Weaver, and Wyrm. When the old world finally collapsed under it's own weight, it's citizens were cast in the void beyond. Oblivion stood triumphant before devouring it's self, leaving all that couldn't be consumed to drift in the Abyssal blackness of the void. And drift they did. For time beyond time, eon beyond eon. Bits of their substance fading away. Strange complexities take shape. Morphing, changing, wanting, lusting. They are spat out worlds and summoned by lessor creatures. They forget themselves and in doing so, forget their nature.

                                      Until they are eventually spat out from this one. Either from the arcane mechanisms of a system who's reach is beyond space and time or perhaps the insane babbling of a half lucid god-spirit. They are thrown here. Stumbling into the light like deformed newborns. Old missions and directives echo dimly through their minds but oh so much has been forgotten and so much is not even relevant to the world they now inhabit. Now they twist without knowing, breaking while shaping.
