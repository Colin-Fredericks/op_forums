    Idigam Chronicle - Crossover Issues

    06-08-2014, 06:46 PM
    So I run today mine 5 PCs mages cabal in to fight with relative the same level 5 NPC Forsaken pack. Werewolf were using new forms damage ( in Gauru +1 from Claws ) and good fight dicepools ( 7-11 dices ), but mages just tear apart 2 NPCs, and only one of the have serious Bashing damage. Only one. Wolves also weren't using Gifts, as I still didn't get to them in my knowledge of Werewolf to make them suitable.

    The Mage PCs ware using Stop Gap rules. The only differences was I didn't use the new Gauru's "Regenerate all" rules on regeneration, only those in Forsaken corebook. PCs were having one 4th dot Arcanum, but the resolution should me a lot more bloodier for Awakaned then.

    Effect was like this - only one mage get a 4 bashes, when cabal destroyed almost all Uratha in fight. Mage's new Shields with 4/1 rules on 4th dot of Arcanum made Awakened unstoppable tanks, even better then werewolves.

    From my little playtest I would advise to give Uratha's claws and jaw attacks something of supernatural armor piercing - damage level is good, but Mages ability to just make "puff the damage", only need to it is just stay still.
