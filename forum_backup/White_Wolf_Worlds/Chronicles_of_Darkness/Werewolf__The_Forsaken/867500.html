                  Then they hunt for might and bravery?

                  While they do seem interested in the really big hunts, any prey could really fit that. You could go the Apocalypse way and have it be about corruption, but it seems like they were really disparaging of the weak or cowardly. I admit that having them as sacred prey can seem like they're only interested in targeting the weak, but that's not what sacred prey is about. Hunting the weak or cowardly is a way of culling them out so only the strong survive to hunt whatever mighty things they want to hunt. While also holding to the darker aspects of the Get.

                  Lets not forget that a sacred prey doesn't have to be a certain kind of creature. As evidenced by the Thousand Steel Teeth, and obviously the Pure, it can be about how they go about things. The lodge hunts those who use the roads, while the Pure have wide ranging sacred prey.
