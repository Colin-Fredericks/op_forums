        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
        One of the general modifiers for rites is &quot;All participants are wearing or showing symbols that indicate their shared purpose.&quot; Is this per-rite or per-ritual-group? (i.e. Is this to encourage painting the entire set of ritual participants with the symbolism of the rite or is it to encourage packs to develop consistent ritual iconography of their own?)
        It's a slightly tricksy answer, but I'd suggest that the symbolism is per rite, but nothing prevents a group from using the same dress/symbolism for multiple rites.

        To break this down further, a pack could have one style of dress they use as affirmation for all their rites, or they may modify the clothing/symbols based on the type of rite and its intended purpose. The modifier allows for both and doesn't punish either style of play.
