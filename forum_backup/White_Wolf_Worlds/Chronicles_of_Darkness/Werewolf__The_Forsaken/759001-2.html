                Since we're coming up with rumors and story seeds now... Some werewolves believe that children of werewolves conceived while the parent(s) were in the father's form have a tendency to go through the first change unusually early. Related to that first rumor is the claim that the parents of these young uratha are then contacted by a lodge that runs a series of schools hidden around the world for these prepubescent werewolves. Three schools like this are indeed known to exist, though the lodges that run them are "officially" separate entities.

                One of the schools is housed in the ghost town of Bayou Goula in Iberville parish LA and is run by a consortium of lodges based in southern culture, including the Lodge of the Hook hand and an Iron Master lodge called the lodge of the Briar Patch that follows the famous rabbit himself. The lodges have the help of a group of vampires who were educators in life (supplied by Augusto Vidal of New Orleans in exchange for a condition that the werewolf students kill Catholics who don't follow lent and collect their blood, in order to scare the mortals back to the church and feed the vampire faculty.) The banes of the vampires; tend to include a fear of frogs and a need to count things, both of which have crossed into the mortal folklore about werewolves in the region as a result of a combination of lunacy, the telephone game and good old fashioned vampire mind control.

                Another is nestled in a mountain somewhere in Japan, founded by a wolf blooded widow and her two uratha children at the behest of her werewolf lover's spirit. The Iron Master daughter focuses on teaching her students to navigate human society and her younger Hunter in Darkness brother, teaches his students how to survive in the wilderness The lodge that runs this school of the school is divided between the two factions with the mother doing her best to keep things civil amid the sibling rivalry.

                The third and oldest is a religious school hidden in the Liviona region of Latvia and Estonia that teaches a combination of Uratha animism and Christan theology, focusing it's lessons on combating the Bale Hounds and their masters and allies. The alumni of these schools tend to be experts in their fields and often reach a high primal urge at a young age, though that may be because of their head start in life as a werewolf.

                (and yes, the 2nd school is a reference to wolf children (which inspired this whole train of thought, along with me thinking that mages shouldn't hog all the hidden magic school fun. Also, werewolf puppies in school uniforms would be cute as hell). The religious school in Livonia is based on the Hound of God legends and the one in the bayou is based on the Roogarou legends of Louisiana, which tend to get mixed up with vampire folklore thus why Vidal is involved.

                anyone else got some rumors about the fathers form and how it might affect werewolf society?
