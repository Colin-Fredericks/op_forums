                                                The idea is that a storm is one of the most intensive things to endure by yourself. TO be fair, a blizzard is one of the harshest storms a person can live through(not disparaging monsoons and hurricanes, but still), and therefore one of the strongest images to enact and incorporate into your culture(plus Skolis himself). Ice itself also lends to the Storm Lords mentality-it can be hard, it can be sharp, it can be soft, ever present, and dismissable, it can help spread light(hope and encouragement) to others or it can block it out, and of course, it's most insisidous and enduring fact is that all other facts aside, the cold will slowly grip you, and the longer that game goes on, the worse most things do against it-regardless of how that ice manifests. Plus, as a minor nod of Storm Lords to the Iron Masters as shared philosophies go, ice can always change into water and take on new things. It's pervasive in it's effects.

                                                That said, I grew up in the desert. Power was a brown out, a sheet of lightning dancing across the flats, a sun that refused to look away and dared clouds to cross it's gaze(few did). All of those are quite evocative of the Storm Lords, because they endure the storm.

                                                A Blizzard may be the most potent of images to the Lords, going all the way to the heat death of the universe as the ultimately conquest of cold, but storms of any kind serve the point.
