                                I personally would pay to keep him from it. Claire certainly, but I don't feel you and Tatterdemalion's crusade against all forms of Lunacy to be in the interest of the game. I think SOME measure of memory loss is necessary. In the real world we didn't have evidence because there was none. In the World of Darkness, there was. There were even experts on these various things. Likely the Malleus Maleficarum was an active group that likely did the investigations back then... and it was beneficial for the church to have people believe.

                                You had people who travelled and sold trinkets to prevent those fears. The 'educated' were exceptionally rare, and likely officials sent to investigate would, like in our world, bring back reports as stated. There are documents when literacy was even rare, documenting cases of werewolves. Even after the Printing Press there were newspapers that had mentions of vampires being staked. Even the educated believed during much of history. Its only been in about the last 200 years that people have really backed away from folkloric beliefs.

                                Again I feel Lunacy as-is is too much, but I feel some memory loss or other protection is necessary for suspension of disbelief in regards to why people don't believe in werewolves. Prometheans only display what they are in rare occasions, and on top of that are extremely rare themselves. Changelings have a powerful magic that protects their true forms from even intense medical examinations. They may be 'odd' but they're hardly supernatural. Their entire society is heavily influenced by hiding as well.

                                Despite what others think, the vinculum is a heavy handed and easily applied capability that basically mimics "conditioning" dominate power in some ways. It makes an obedient servant. Then there's the protections against being identified (which may have undergone some changes to make it less overtly supernatural). Vampires also have the ability to basically turn their bodies on as well.

                                Lunacy needs toned down, but getting rid of it entirely and saying the world is as it is today, but werewolves are real and people throughout time have probably seen them often (especially in the case of death rage which I'm sure was far more common in past eras as well) but nobody really believes is silly at best, and bordering on Absurd.
