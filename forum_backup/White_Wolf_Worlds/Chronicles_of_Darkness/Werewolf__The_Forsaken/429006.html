    Explain 2ed Death Rage to me please?

    03-05-2015, 02:06 PM
    The text on Kuruth and Wasu-Im and Basu-Im seems a bit confusing, so lets see if I got the steps right.

    If an Uratha encounters a Trigger (passive, common, specific) then she's going into Wasu-Im, the Soft Rage. She suffers a modified version of Rage for her Harmony Level Chart time in minutes or seconds. While in this Rage she has to attack things/cause a ruckus in Dalu or or Urshul forms or go into Basu-Im the Hard Rage that involves being Gauru for Primal Urge Level Chart time and starts mauling all the things forever because Rage.

    However, while in Wasu-Im she can roll Resolve+Composure to not have to attack things/cause a ruckus for a turn per success, and only through an exceptional success on this roll can she end Wasu-Im. Failing it causes her to go into Basu-Im.

    She has her Harmony Level Chart time in minutes or seconds to accomplish this exceptional success or she goes into Basu-Im, or can do so if willing anytime.

    ....That seems rather rough to require an exceptional success and to draw out rolling for such a long period of time which is part of why I'm confused but lets continue....

    Presuming the time limit is up, she goes into Basu-Im, and her packmates, if around, go into as well unless they roll Resolve+Composure needing one success per character in Basu-Im and needing to re-roll every time someone falls to Basu-Im.


    Is this story true?


    If so, Wasu-Im seems like a very difficult ticking time-bomb to avoid exploding into Basu-Im. Or is there a way to work out the Rage that I'm missing?

                  It's definitely a ticking time bomb. But there's a few things you can get up to with some investment and preparation;

                  One is just trying to get to a safe venting area. The Exit Strategy facet might help here, but you can also prepare your own areas - reinforced warehouses with stuff to shred, areas of wilderness you know how to get to quickly, maybe a part of the undercity where you just don't care about anything you'd encounter.

                  There's also three tricks to pull you out of Wasu-Im. An Elodoth can do it once a session automatically, and anyone with the Inspiration facet Still Small Voice can do it with a roll - take a few swings at doing it with a roll if you can pass your own roll(s) to keep some control. Then if you've got the Chain Rage rite up, just passing the self-control roll twice (not even consecutively) can pull you back out.

                  Chain Rage, though, means that at the end of the month you need to get someplace secure and go into Wasu-Im without its help, and there's a chance that you could be screwed if the rite's foci are stolen or broken.

                  So Death Rage is most brutal when:
                  • Your pack is unprepared for it because of youth, ignorance, or lack of resources/effort.
                  • Your Harmony is getting too far out of balance.
                  • You're surrounded by a number of triggers.
                  • There's too much fighting going on for your pack to safely work on helping you.
                  • You're alone.
