                                    One of the complaints I've heard about Lodges is that people don't like them because they don't offer new gift sets. The specific comparisons I heard were usually to Mage's legacies and Vampire's bloodlines (not all of which offered their own disciplines, of course). Obviously overloading on gifts can be seen as a problem, but they're just...kinda fun. And with the limitations (auspice only gifts are auspice only, I think, as opposed to things anyone not of that auspice can learn but at a higher cost) the more options the better.

                                    Plus considering how cool the ones we've seen are other more outre gifts would just be mindblowing, I'm sure.

                                            I'd be very wary of Lodges being a major soruce of new Gifts - among other things, I'd rather pitch widely available Shadow Gifts rather than really specialised, really restricted ones. I can see a few Lodges having unusual Gift access but I think it's better if Lodges generally give other special capabiltiies.

                                            I mean, back in 1e, I wrote up a regional Lodge of the Stannary for a Cornwall game I ran, and created a Stannary Gift for them, but it's basically a 'Gift of Metal' and, were I to write it again, I'd just do it as a generally available Shadow Gift of Metal; the bits of the Gift that were more to do with underground stuff I'd split out into a separate Gift of Caverns or something.
