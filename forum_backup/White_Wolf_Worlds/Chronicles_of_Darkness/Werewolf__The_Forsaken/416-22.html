            Along with what has be said above I would like to see something more about the importance of Packs. We know it's a Harmony sin to be a lone wolf but I want to see more into the value/build of a pack.

            Alter the duration of the change. I don't want the way it was in Apocalypse but the fact it last all of a minute or two..That's not really threatening. An hour or two or if it's their auspice moon out then it last as long as the moon is up.
            Yar. Pack tactics, Merits and abilities based off of pack hunting/activity (maybe some Gifts have pack synergy), and fun, detailed hunting rules. Hunting should be something you look forward to doing in game because it tells a story in howls and heartbeats, mud and bone and snapping fangs. Werewolves communicate on the hunt. Each kind of prey is different, offering its own challenges. Hunting shouldn't be just another extended action. It should be its own mini-game, rewarding in its own right.

            Re: Gauru form duration

            As I've mentioned here before, I see two ways to go about it. Either the form is limited and dangerous in proportion to that limitation, or it's not so hindered but not as powerful, either. I'm all for it remaining of very limited duration, but being a complete killing machine while it lasts. I would be okay with it not being a nuclear option and lasting longer (for a scene, for instance), but I think a brief, bloody eruption of Rage fits the game better.

                                Make it more of a game about werewolves, that I think is really the most important point and both Apocalypse AND Forsaken failed to be one so far in many ways.

                                I personally think that in many ways, books like skinchangers did more to be about the idea of a werewolf then Werewolf itself did.

                                Vampire managed to do archetypes of vampire legends and vampire fiction, and blood and smoke included the kind of vampires that were left out.
                                Werwolf needs that, to feel more connected with the different ideas people have about werewolves.

                                Make the kind of film werewolf, the out of control, the killing machine a type of antagonist, or something you could become, but focus on the way were animals work around the world, of the connection with magic, curses, divine punishment, being descended from a "god" of sorts...

                                All those could have been very colourful backgrounds instead of the rather bland tribes and mostly functionary auspices.

                                Make the game strongly about being part human and part wolf and that means actual wolves.
                                I often found the way wolves are represented in both gamelines to be rather lacking, it felt more like generic monster and weird spirit...

                                The shadow, the animism that does fit with werewolves quite well, but I think the spirit world often felt more weird and freaky than, well, something natural, which it should be (there is a lot of horror in that, when you use it!).
                                I think many eastern media just do that kind of thing better given that belief in spirits, minor gods and similar is still very much a part of such cultures.
                                A bit less of Geiger and body horror and some more Asian themed horror elements of spirits and possession.
                                And since there is that weird problem of spirits both all hating you and being the one source of power, why not just ditch the hatred and focus on the fact that spirits are DIFFERENT, alien with very peculiar interests.
                                Cultures are full of malevolent spirits or just strange ones, keep that duality.
                                Really, a spirit world more based on the Japanese concept of Yokai, Ayakashi and similar would be a perfect fit that can be very scary without all of it hating you.

                                In addition, use the human element more!

                                The werewolf as the other, the one who is different, who might be feared, persecuted, unable to really be a part of society, there is a lot of potential in that which is way too often killed of with lunacy and similar which make human opponents less dangerous.
                                There has been such a rich history of "werewolves", like witches, being prosecuted, do something with the fear of being turned upon.
                                Even if you go berserk and kill loads of people, they might come back.
                                And next time they might kill you.
                                I found that werewolf tries too much to make all the horror you face come from spirits and all the horror you embody from being a violent monster.
                                But you really SHOULD be drawn between what you grew up with as a human, your instincts as a wolf and what living in a world full of dangerous spirits and other enemies demands of you.
                                I hated harmony because it just catapults you away from human values and ignores the interesting conflict of the dual nature and instead ingraining upon you a code of behaviour that feels neither really like wolf nor human nor anything.

                                You should really get to care about how difficult it is to retain some of what it means to be human.

                                Another thing to explore more would be the ties between humans and wolves.
                                There is just so much to be done with that, to explain why werewolves are the most common werecreatures, to play with the theme of the early most history of how men domesticated wolves, how they share so many similarities in their social life...

                                Make the rage more of an understandable emotional reaction, werewolf often feels like it just slaps a "choleric" sticker on everyone.
                                You can built rage so nicely as blind panic, as lashing out, as just a mechanism to cope when things become too much, make it more of an instinct, stress how it is necessary to keep you fighting, and not just everyone being a berserker with bad temper.

                                And discard the canonical sanctity of the father wolf story from the core book.
                                Having it presented as the exact and true account of how werewolves came to be just kills the mystery and kills the suspense.
                                Vampires get so many different interesting origin stories, divine curses, ancient goddesses and monsters, werewolves?
                                A furry Oedipus myth...
                                No, even Blasphemies did not fix that issue quite well...

                                I also think the big world wide groupings are not too great.
                                Werewolves operate on a pack basis, they do not even have the sept like in Apocalypse, tribes do hardly make much sense at all.
                                Reimagining tribes as a sort of "origin" which could make use of there into being one single origin of all werewolves might work better and many of the big global inter werewolf conflicts could just as well be dropped, or be left open for storytellers to use or discard.

                                Discarding the taboo of werewolves not being able to mate would also work nicely, it could strengthen the idea of packs as families, quite like a wolfpack where young wolves go out and form their own.
                                That way, one could also bring back the idea of Metis and Lupus, just, please, with a bit more interesting implementation, with more of an emphasis on inclination towards one or the other half, wolf or man or inbetween.

                                All in all, don't waste the opportunity to make it a game that feels more thematic and easier to work in existing story concepts ike it was done with Changeling for example.
