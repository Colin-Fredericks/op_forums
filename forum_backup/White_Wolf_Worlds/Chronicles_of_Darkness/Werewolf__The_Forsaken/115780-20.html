                                        Now I see Crimson Spasm, and I think it is AWESOME (hey, spasm and awesome kind of rhymes!). Not just because of what it mechanically does, but because, to me, it is a statement: "Take heart, Ursufgutha Storm-Born, for Little Timmy at your tabletop will no longer be tempted to abuse mechanical vagaries just to get the Gauru's phenomenal might in Hishu!" I was just asking for a verification on whether or not this was the intent, and maybe a hint on what Partial Change does now, or whether or not it even continues to exist.

                                        I did some light reading of the new Vampire Disciplines in Blood and Smoke a couple days ago, saw what they did to Feral Claws, and it got me wondering. Just to make it clear, I would NOT be sorry to see Partial Change go, or at the least cleaned up.

                                        What I would be sorry about, however, is being unable to make jokes anymore about growing the Gauru brain inside a Hishu skull.
