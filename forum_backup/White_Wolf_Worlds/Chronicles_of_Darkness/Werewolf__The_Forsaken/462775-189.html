                    They are highly tied to a specific realm (or two, if you also consider the Astral), uses Essence instead of Mana, and doesn't share any similarities to actually confirmed Supernal beings that have been cast down: the Bound.

                    The two points for their Supernal origins are their conceptual nature (which is very likely tied to their spiritual nature rather than them being Supernal symbols) and their ability to use the Arcana. The latter doesn't have an explanation that I'm aware of, but it only implies a connection rather than origin. As mentioned, the Awakened also aren't Supernal entities just because they happen to have access to the Arcana.

                                            Originally posted by Helur View Post
                                            also, can this gift be cumulative ?
                                            What can stop an uratha to strike at the liver and makes it bleed and then strike to another organ?
                                            The text is not explicit about this, but effects that can be applied additively usually use the word "cumulative" or "stackable" somewhere. I personally wouldn't allow this Facet to be stacked multiple times on the same target, but this is a judgment call.

                                            Narratively, you can strike the liver and cause the target to suffer supernatural bleeding. Now the target is suffering a condition of supernatural bleeding. You could strike another organ with the Facet but the target is already suffering the Facet's condition of supernatural bleeding. You already did the thing the Facet does.
