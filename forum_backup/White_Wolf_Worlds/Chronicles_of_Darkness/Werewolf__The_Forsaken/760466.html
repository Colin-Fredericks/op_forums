                  I started a thread like this some time ago, but it was specifically about combinations of spirit types. I'm guessing that more savvy love spirits would have the Mortal Mask Numen to not freak people out. Personally, one of the clearest mental images I have of a love spirit using that Numen is a smiling, welcoming, serene granny, all knitted sweaters and home made cookies.

                  Also, just because it is a love spirit does not mean it will be friendly. It could be the possessive love of mentally unstable stalkers, for example.

                  Another interesting question: what does a madness spirit look like? There are many types of madness, of course, which could lead to many appearances.
