    Using the Firstborn

    04-28-2014, 03:45 PM
    Setting hack: the Firstborn are not some distant deities hunting in the depths of the Shadow who the average werewolf never gets to see, whose entire existence is a matter of faith.

    The Firstborn show up every now and then. They occasionally make their will know to the Uratha. All nine of them.

    If you're a PC, interesting things happen around you, and you're bound to see your tribal totem one day.

    They're still wolf gods, and they don't care for deus ex machina. They won't do your job or interfere in your hunts in any way. They won't save your life when the Pure are at your throats. You don't get to summon them and beg for their help like lesser spirits. The Firstborn children of Father Wolf choose when to make themselves known.

    The Firstborn are not nice or compassionate or human. They're spirits, and as thus, bound by their intrinsic nature. They do not know or care about human concerns or Harmony. Outside of their individual characteristics, they know only the hunt.

                                              Funnily enough, this is quite similar to something we do in my game, where the Firstborn are a bit more involved.

                                              What are Tribes? Who are the Firstborn and what do they do?

                                              Tribes are the chosen groupings of the Werewolves -- and they look very little like human tribes, or like the Covenants, Courts, and Orders of the other supernatural nations. Werewolf tribes are essentially philosophies, ideological prisms through which one looks at the world. They have no offices, no hierarchy, no organization. What they do have are the Firstborn.

                                              The Firstborn were other children of Father Wolf, fathered on lesser wolf-spirits. Each one is an Incarnae of significant power, and they are the patrons of the Uratha. In prehistoric times, each was somehow bested by the werewolves, and consented to serve as mentors and guides for their nephews and nieces. This is a role that they continue to fulfill to the present day.

                                              Each of the Firstborn acts as a spirit-guide and personal mentor to every werewolf in their tribe -- these are spirits powerful enough to divide their attention a thousand-ways at any given moment. They usually appear in dreams or in visions, as whispers in the back of the head or vague intuitions. Different werewolves get differing amounts of personal attention.

                                              They're primarily concerned with the personal growth and harmony of each werewolf. The Firstborn try to make you a better werewolf -- or a better example of their idea of a werewolf (which differs quite a bit from Tribe to Tribe). They give advice, pose challenges, or ask questions, and generally act in whatever fashion they think will get the best results in terms of a werewolf's spiritual growth.

                                              They are not leaders. The Firstborn have a kind of blind-spot, they only notice very small and very big things. In their role as personal spirit-guides, they notice the werewolf, and only the werewolf. They're concerned with spiritual growth, not acting as clue-dispensers, and seem to notice things only as they pertain to the werewolf's internal growth. In their role as Incarnae, they only notice events on a truly massive scale -- they notice WWII, and they might notice the Blitz if someone pointed it out to them, but anything smaller is under their radar. Plus they're spirits of immense antiquity, so they're a bit... peculiar.

                                              That said, the Firstborn do act as repositories of lore and tradition for their Tribes. They won't tell you where to find the Azlu, but they might suggest a Deed Name, or the proper way to memorialize one's ancestors. They also operate as central switchboards, so to speak, helping werewolves of the same Tribe find one another. They do not (usually) teach Gifts themselves, but can direct the werewolf towards teachers. Finally, they require each werewolf to follow a Tribal Ban, in order to maintain the Firstborn's favor.

                                              There are eight Firstborn.
                                              • Fenris-Ur, the Destroyer Wolf, patron of the Blood Talons
                                              • Kamduis-Ur, the Death Wolf, patron of the Bone Shadows
                                              • Sagrim-Ur, the Red Wolf, patron of the Iron Masters
                                              • Hikaon-Ur, the Black Wolf, patron of the Hunters in Darkness
                                              • Skolis-Ur, the Winter Wolf, patron of the Storm Lords
                                              • Gurim-Ur, the Burning Wolf, patron of the Fire-Touched
                                              • Huzuruth-Ur, the Dire Wolf, patron of the Predator Kings
                                              • Hathis-Ur, the Silver Wolf, patron of the Ivory Claws
                                              • A very few legends and rumors claim that there is also Viruhk-Ur, the Soulless Wolf, patron of the Bale Hounds -- though this may be just speculation and Bale Hound propaganda.
