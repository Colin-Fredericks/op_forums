    My neighborhood probably wouldn't last long in terms of territory, it would have to be expanded, but I guess alone it would be tougher to spread out if there was neighbors. Still, I'd want to range further for sacred hunts, there hasn't been many murders here throughout history and vicious animal attacks would gain attention. The spirits in my area would have to be tamed as well. I have no idea what it would be like as an elder werewolf. I always had a dream for my werewolf character where they sought out every Maeljin to seek enlightenment in all of the sins, sort of become a 'shining example' of each aspect a Maeljin represents, in the end becoming a type of dark champion for them among werewolves.                                                     Auspice I couldn't tell.
                                                    I submit myself to the ever-wise and ever-mad moon for that judgment. I can easily see aspects of all five auspices in myself, so none of them would be a bad fit, and I'd accept our celestial patron's decision. I'm withdrawn enough as a person to fit as an Irraka, but have enough anger-issues to be a Rahu. I'm creative enough that Cahalith would fit, but I'm also superstitious and spiritual enough that Ithaeur wouldn't be bad either. And Elodoth, standing there in the middle, being a little of everything, and on the balance would therefore also fit.

                                                    Tribe is easier, but not easy. I am quite certain I'd join either the Iron Masters or the Bone Shadows. Both have a core of curiosity and intellectualism that attracts me. I believe in ghosts, and I'm quite certain I've seen them a few times, and I am very curious about this part of the universe, so that is a pull of the Bone Shadows... But on the other hand, I'm more than a little bit a coward, and leaving humanity behind is a scary thought, so I might be drawn to the Iron Masters, just for the familiarity of mankind.

                                                    In the end, I'm quite certain I'd end up as a hybrid case, fitting both tribes. Likely joining one tribe, and a lodge tightly knit to the other. Such as Bone Shadows and Lodge of Scrolls, or Iron Master and Lodge of Death.

                                                    I know I'd learn the nature gifts early, for the Pack Kin and Beast Ride facets. I like animals, and I would enjoy getting closer with them, my pet cats in particular - provided they survive my First Change of course. When I find the limits of that, a search for Predator gifts, properly communicate with them will likely be a goal for me.
