          Hmm... I would argue that ancestral spirits are a werewolf thing, unless you wanted to dive into the Underworld or get a Mage involved. Abraham Lincoln wouldn't have a spirit, but probably wouldn't have a ghost either- he'd likely have the Temenos being discussed in Astral Realms called a Walker; which if I'm understanding correctly is an amalgamation of hundreds of people's perception of a famous person. I think Mage even mentions some famous people being possessed by their own Walkers during their lifetime. A spirit of the Lincoln Memorial would make sense, since it's a place, not a person. I'd also suggest that spirits of relationships like brotherhood, sisterhood, fathers, and mothers exist. Those identities are not just human-specific. They have also been seen to apply to great apes, cetaceans, and elephants. And even if they didn't count enough to make the emotion, or if it was too human, werewolves feel those emotions too.
