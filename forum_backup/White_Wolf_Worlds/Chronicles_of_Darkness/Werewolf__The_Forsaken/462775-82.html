                                                    Originally posted by Grimbli View Post
                                                    How serious is breaking the Oath of the Moon by consuming flesh for essence? It says the Pack and Totem get pissed, but no concrete answers as far as I can tell.

                                                    And if eating flesh is off the table, else does a WW regain Essence if they won't have access to a Hallow?
                                                    It's not so serious that it's entirely off the table. Generally if there's a specific rule about something, it's something that has a tendency to happen, if not to you, then somewhere.

                                                    It is taboo, and some wolves are going to get up in arms about it, while some may shrug it off as a matter of necessity. But just like the other Oaths it's going to depend on your world and characters within it to decide how serious it's taken. There's an entire tribe focused on breaking, 'The People Do Not Murder The People,' while most wolves will agree that it's a line that can be crossed in times of 'great need.' And most packs aren't entirely going to stick to honoring the high and respecting the low. From a Storyteller's standpoint, these rules exist to be tested, if not outright broken. They're all strings you can pull.

                                                    The easiest way to get Essence is from a locus. They have an abundant amount, usually, and as much can be taken as one wants. Performing the Sacred Hunt against other spirits is a way to do it too, but at that point you're risking danger and you might not get much. From there you can get drips and drops from breaking open fetishes, seeing your Auspice moon, and the occasional Rite like Harness the Cycle.
