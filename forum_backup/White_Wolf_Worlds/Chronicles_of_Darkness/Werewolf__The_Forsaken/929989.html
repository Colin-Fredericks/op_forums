    Lodge of Wires (2e)

    07-11-2016, 06:09 PM
    Techwolf/hackwolf is one of my favourite character concepts. Probably why I love Iron Masters so much. With all the Ghost in the Shell stuff going on with the Technology gift I thought to create a character who was a member of the Lodge of Wires (Tribes of the Moon pg 138). This character is for a supernatural Taskforce crossover type game (Vampire, Mage, Werewolf and Beast), so I sort of wanted to create a Ghost in the Shell/Serial Experiments Lain type feel with it (though it already had most of that going on to begin with so the lore is mostly unchanged). I have a tech spirit retainer (specifically communication networks), and totemic empowerment. Drones to Iron Slave, etc.

    This is what I got so far, let me know what y'all think. I am not quite sure on the Lodge Sorcery influences being what they are, and I'm debating whether the lodge should even have Lodge Stronghold. Lorehouse might suffice, though I like the idea of a hackerwolf who got in over her head being able to lay low at some tech fortress for protection until the heat dies down and she can construct a new cover. Obviously the lodge lorehouse is a website of some kind.

    Also, a friend has implied that he thinks the Sacred Hunt effect is incredibly strong, so I wanted feedback on that. Initially its effect was tracking people by the frequencies of their devices (as with Taste of Blood), but that seemed silly since most devices have GPS' as well. Next it had an effect of being impossible to pick up on surveillance, but again that seemed unnecessary for a lodge that probably doesn't spend too much of it's time outdoors. So I ended up with this, which I like, but am looking for feedback on.

    While I don't necessarily feel it has to change, the ban seems pretty easy to maintain, especially with the lodge fetish (which is a direct port from 1e) so I might look to bump that ban up a bit and am very open to suggestions.

    Also, still working on Amplified Frequency and refining the idea.

    And lastly I'm looking to revamp the gift of Information, which was pretty useless in 1e but is still a strong concept to me. If anyone has ideas on that or has already homebrewed a version I'd love to hear/look/borrow them.
    Lodge of Wires

    Bonds


    Blessing: The Lodge of Wires erases all traces of the lodge member from the world's computers. Lodge members also achieve exceptional success on Computer and Science rolls on 3 successes instead of 5.

    Aspiration: Develop new ways to fuse technology and spirit magic

    Ban: Must always have access to the internet or other communication network.
    Sacred Hunt


    While on Sacred Hunt, The Lodge of Wires grants your character the ability to use Gifts that require visual sight, even when viewing a location, object or person through lenses or other video technology. When viewing through surveillance equipment, the lodge member may project their wolf or spirit senses as though present at the location, this ability even extends to recorded video.
    Tools


    The Lodge of Wires has access to the Lodge Connections, Lodge Lorehouse, Lodge Sorcery and Lodge Stronghold merits, and may choose Sorcery Influences from Internet, Surveillance and Knowledge. Lodge Strongholds exist in many of tech hubs and burgeoning tech hubs in the United States, such as San Jose (California), Dallas (Texas), Seattle (Washington), New York (New York) as well as globally in Bangalore (India), Geneva (Switzerland), Beijing (China) and Guangdong (China).

    Lodge Fetish: Digital Eye (•• or •••)

    Most Wireheads create or inherit an Eyepiece shortly after becoming full members of the lodge. The werewolf must bind a spirit servant of the Digital Eye into a cutting-edge smartphone with runes of the future etched into the case. All versions of the Eyepiece can call any phone or access the Internet from anywhere. A lack of phone signal is no problem, neither is the Gauntlet. Each Eyepiece also monitors news sources, flagging news stories that might be of interest to its owner. More importantly, the Eyepiece is the only way that a Wirehead can access the secure computer networks of the Lodge of Wires. The three-dot version of this fetish gives a werewolf a measure of the Digital Eye’s true power. He can use his Eyepiece to see through any surveillance camera within a mile. If he selects a specific target, he can seamlessly track that target using any camera in range

    Lodge Rite: Amplified Frequency (•)

    Amplified Frequency amplifies the range of a wirehead's spiritual frequency to emanate not just from her, but from her packmates as well - greatly extending the range and effectiveness of her gifts.
    Symbols: Amplification, networks, power, technology
    Sample Rite:
    Cost: 1 (network resonant) Essence per packmate involved.
    Action: Extended (10 successes; each roll represents 15 minutes)
    Duration: 3 Months
    Success: Allows the lodgemember to use packmates as well as themselves for calculation of the range of any gift or fetish.

    (Example, Pack Awareness = sense packmates within 1 mile, amplifies to sense packmates within 1 mile of Franky or any packmate with Franky’s amplified essence.)



    I reused most of the 1e stuff, but some of it had to change with the new direction of the lodge.

                      The original write-up has the lodge Totem as The Digital Eye, but it doesn't really give a lot of info. I don't want to post much directly from the book, but this is pretty much all it says:

                      "The Lodge of Wires pays lip service to Red Wolf, but members save their reverence for the Digital Eye, a vastly powerful spirit of the future. Only a few Wireheads know that their patron is also the Lord of Surveillance, and the lodge his chosen warriors." and "Once her training is complete, the recruit petitions the Digital Eye. If she’s deemed incompatible, she never hears from the lodge again — and if she goes looking, powerful spirit magics obstruct her path. If the Lord of Surveillance believes that she will advance his agenda, he erases all traces of her from the world’s computers, welcoming her to the lodge."

                      It's able to erase info from the world's computers, as well as prevent access to whatever web presence the lodge has. This to me suggests a powerful spirit, Rank 4-5. More likely 5. Powerful enough to represent the Big Brother type surveillance and have it's sphere of influence spread wide.

                      It also sounds to me like a spirit of Surveillance masquerading as a spirit of the Future, or a spirit of Surveillance that manifests with a Futuristic bent. I'm imagining a controlling, obsessive, potentially paranoid/conspiratorial/secretive spirit that is using the Uratha it binds to the lodge to ensure that it will be the strongest tech spirit when our dystopian future finally arrives. There will be tech spirits aplenty, and a huge power boom, so if it is to survive it needs to be prepared.

                      Looked at generously, it might want to create a future where it can manage the growth of tech spirits, because of their immense influence on the direction of humanity. It might be nurturing or just monitoring their growth, so that it can react appropriately. It might even go so far as to use its influence and Uratha to prevent particularly dangerous or malevolent tech spirits from taking hold of humanity's imagination. To guide it in the right direction.

                      The flipside of this, of course is that it might be creating a future where it can reap the rewards of the resonant essence that Humanity creates. It might be gently urging it in that direction, and taking down all comers to the throne. Afterall, it was sitting on the throne long before the palace was built around it. There's a lot of really juicy stuff there as far as plot hooks.

                      To quote the book "Tomorrow’s Son (leader of the lodge) believes in the Panopticon, a particular future where everyone is tracked and watched at all times. Despite all he’s tried, most lodge members don’t think his particular future is the best option. There’s plenty of room for conflicting opinions, especially from characters who have a different view of the future. Other groups and lodges study the future in the short and medium terms, but the Lodge of Wires is the scary bastard thinking two steps beyond the cutting edge. Nobody likes the Wireheads, but for now they’re a necessary evil."

                      So I was thinking Influences in Knowledge, Internet, and Surveillance. Maybe Computers instead of Knowledge or Internet. Mostly because I don't really know how Influence: Future would work, or if that's even possible.

                                              Current changes in my head involve dropping Lodge Stronghold as I've been debating, and creating an associated mystery cult.

                                              Something like:

                                              The Panopticon

                                              The Panopticon is a hacker group whose mandate involves promoting the advance of technological advancement. The Lodge of Wires has had it's claws in the group since it's inception, and members of the Lodge of Wires may purchase the Mystery Cult Initiation or Mystery Cult Influence merit in regard to The Panopticon, and in fact many Wireheads were members of The Panopticon before being brought into the fold.

                                              (•) New recruits are tasked primarily with DDoS attacks and doxxing politicians or influential personalities who oppose technological progress. There is no hand holding in the Panopticon, as members are immediately thrown into the deep end and are expected to drown or learn to swim, and quick. They gain the Hacking specialty in Computers, if the initiate already has the specialty (and most do before joining) they gain the Area of Expertise merit instead.
                                              (••) Members who stick around this long have learned the value of patience. One mistake, one sloppy hack or miscalculation could bring the whole house of cards down. They gain the Patientmerit.
                                              (•••) While still not trusted with the Panopticon’s vast stores of information, members at this level now have access to the group’s secure channels, and at this stage are likely to have contact info or other ways of reaching a few members of the group for advice or collaboration. They gain the Hobbyist Clique (Computers) merit.
                                              (••••) At this stage members gain access to large stores of collected data Library (•••) merit with a focus on Computers. The Lodge of Wires keeps close watch of The Panopticon’s ranks, at this stage if the member is Uratha or Wolfblooded, he is most likely approached for initiation into the Lodge.

                                              (•••••) Only the most trusted and influential members of The Panopticon, or members of the Lodge of Wires, have access to this level. The Lodge of Wires is willing to let a portion of their true knowledge and data slip to humans. Members now gain access to the Lodge Lorehouse (•••) merit, even if they are not members of the Lodge. Though stores of knowledge that involve the Uratha, Spirits or other supernatural phenomena are still off limits to humans.

                                              Or, Humans with this level of dedication to the cause must be supported where necessary, Members gain access to the Lodge Connections                                               (•••) merit, even if they are not members of the Lodge. Though resources that involve Uratha, Spirits, or other supernatural phenomena are still off limits to humans.


                                              Very rough draft at this point. Literally just wrote it right now, but critique is welcome.
