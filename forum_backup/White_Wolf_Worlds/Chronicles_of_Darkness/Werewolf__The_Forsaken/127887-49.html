                                    Deionscribe, Inquisitor, Amravanti, The young man in the cafe :

                                    liz: crumbs, scraps, pieces
                                    Urliz/Ur-Liz/Ur Liz: Scrap Hounds
                                    a'iltudu-besdul: praying mantis/a mantis (lit. "praying ones who raise their arms to strike")
                                    Namubesdulu: Mantis Hosts (lit. "death prayer hosts")
                                    Iziduksium-Igi: Eye of Providence (lit. "Flaming Shield Eye")
                                    suguludaha: a fad or toy that people get worked up over, with the potential to cause chaos if it's low in stock, such as a hot toy during Christmastime on a Black Friday; something that is popular and desirable, but ultimately might be bad or not worth it in a long run (lit. "treasure treat")

                                    skush: to cover with liquid; to envelop; to douse; to pour over; to coat
                                    kunnu: to be unworthy, weak, worthless; to not be strong; to be of omega-ish qualities in a pack
                                    Kunnumuth-Skushdu-Gimak-Zardu-Munu: He Who Runs Through the Halls of the Dead and Bathes In the Blood of the Unworthy (lit. "unworthy blood-bathing tomb [halls of the dead] running [male] one") — short forms could include Zardul ("runner"), Muth-Skushdul ("blood-bather"), and Gimak-Ina-Zardul ("one who runs through the tomb [halls of the dead]")

                                    izidukul: a torch
                                    izifuful: a candle (lit. "fire twig")
                                    Izifufulag-Nazaru: Candle Keeper (lit. "guardian of the candles")
                                    zidu-cahha: a movie, a motion picture (lit. "living story")
                                    zidu-cahhabh: a movie screen, a television screen, or any other screen that's showing media of any kind (lit. "living story window")
                                    Izul-Cahha-Anna-Zidu-Cahhabh: Stolen Screentime (lit. "Steals the Story From the Screen")

                                    Hirfathra-Dunfathul: Bone-Breaker
                                    ezthum: cannon fodder, expendable forces
                                    u'il: carrier, runner; one who bears or carries something
                                    zila'u-u'il: "bearer of one's life spark"
                                    thegh: to forage; to pick out; to collect; to search through
                                    theghul: a forager; one who forages
                                    unil: a worker, a laborer
                                    Hafal Agalu/Hafal-Agalu/Hafagalu: He Who Feeds On War (lit. "war-eater")

                                    @Kirby Jerusalem: They have been done! You can find them here: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets...gid=1890564968

                                    @ Everybody else: I've sometimes disappeared for a while before and come back with translations. I'm not gone yet!
