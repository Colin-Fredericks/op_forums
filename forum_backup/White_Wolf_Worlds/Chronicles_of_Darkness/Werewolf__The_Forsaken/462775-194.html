                    Out of all the given examples of influences in the Ephemeral Influence source book, Influence Water was very, very, VERY underwhelmingly.                             Water just feels like it isn't given a lot of options. You can use air influence to increase your speed by adding your rank and at the high end its multiplied by your rank. You can make usable tools out of earth or cause an earthquake out of nowhere. In contrast, water lets you swim better and lets you not dehydrate as fast if you drink 'magic' water made by your influence. In comparison its just so lacking I wonder why anyone would take Heart of Water. The fourth dot example, downpour, can only gives a tilt effect over a building meanwhile the fourth dot of earthquake can effect an entire neighborhood. And that flood tilt at dot 3 is dependent on if their is a flood already in progress meanwhile, again, Earth lets you cause an earthquake out of nowhere. Maybe if you could use strengthen to bust open a waterpipe in a house or street or make a fire hydrant fail but would that even be possible? You can't turn the water to ice and use icicles as weapons because that would be Influence Ice according to the book. Can't use steam because again that would be influence steam and I'm not sure about using the moisture in the air or taking water out of plant life. There's so many interesting things water can do that's locked behind the reasoning that if it isn't pure water in its liquid form, then you have no influence over it. Meanwhile those with the air influence are walking around in complete vacuum. Where are the tsunamis, the redirected rivers, the heavy storms that floods an entire neighborhood? Hell, you should be able to create your own geysers at strong enough influence but given the power levels of the examples given I just don't know if that's even remotely possible.

                                    Originally posted by cybirddude View Post
                                    If a werewolf is in a polyamorous relationship, can all of their lovers count as one touchstone?
                                    As someone in a polyamorous relationship, I would probably handle the entirety of the polycule itself as probably a Flesh Touchstone, as it would presumably centre not only meaningful interactions with individual human members but facilitate meaningful participation in a human system. Plus, I'd count the polycule a viable Touchstone because it can potentially be threatened in various forms - threat of violence from the outside, threat of destabilization from the inside - and would essentially require the uratha's involvement to protect and resolve it. Granted, I think the limiting factor would be that I'd only count meaningful interaction with those whom the uratha is directly partnering with.
