    Forsaken and the Ainu

    08-24-2016, 01:27 PM
    Someone reminded me of this today, and I was surprised nobody made a thread about it on launch: how does everyone feel about the Tokyo setting stating that most of Japan's werewolves are of Ainu descent? CofD has been pretty adamant from day one about distancing itself from saying various marginalized ethnicities are magical like WoD was so prone to, and this feels like a step backwards to me (to say nothing of how limiting it is narratively).

    Does this leave a bad taste in anyone else's mouth, or just me?
