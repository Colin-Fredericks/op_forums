    I appreciate that you're coming at this from a specific angle... but you're not the only person in the conversation.

    It's also really disingenuous to keep citing a single phrase from p. 93 completely out of context. It only has humorous implications if you ignore the rest of the sentence around it, ignore the sentences after it, and ignore what the word digest actually means in a biological context.

    Saying something can only digest meat doesn't mean it cannot ingest grains, and process grains through the digestive tract. It means that thing cannot convert grains into a material that can be absorbed through its digestive organs to be processed into nutrients for the body. The whole big reason people stress things like having more fiber and whole grains in your diet is actually because of the stuff you can't digest. Those indigestible parts of grains actually aid normal human digestion and excretion. For a normal person it's the people that don't eat indigestible food that have "knee-slapping" implications from their diets; not the other way around.

    It's also worth remembering that some people just aren't going to find, "eating this makes you violently ill!" funny; especially if you're twisting the wording of the text so hard just to make a poop joke.

    -----------

    Originally posted by Aynie View Post
    For my own sanity I'm probably just going to houserule werewolves having to eat in regards to their restrictions to gain/keep Essence and other foods won't supply that spiritual nourishment but biologically, they'll be fine if they decide to stay vegetarian for a day.
    You realize that you basically don't need this to be a house rule?

    A werewolf can go Stamina + Resolve days (basic WOD Deprivation rules) before they'd suffer any ill-effects from not consuming enough of their current feeding restriction before biological starvation would set in. If they go vegetarian for a few days is isn't going to do anything by the rules already besides mess with their Essence; which you aren't intending to house-rule away as it is. The Essence tax side of it is so much harsher than the biological starvation side of it, that worrying about the biological starvation side is practically pointless.

    Werewolves are predators. The game rules reinforce that. The game rules aren't so harsh as you seem to be reading them, so most PCs really never have to worry about this. This is only really going to be an issue if you're purposefully playing a character that's struggling against their predator nature; at which point the systems punishing them is sort of the point.
