            Found the rules for Swarm Tilt in DtD corebook...

            Originally posted by DtD, p. 179
            SWARM (ENVIRONMENTAL)
            Animals of Size 1 are generally best represented by swarms, flocks and other groupings of the animals. Swarms are measured by their radius in yards. A swarm inflicts one point of bashing damage to anyone within its radius. A swarm can inflict more damage by condensing. Every time the swarm condenses to cover half of its full area, it inflicts one additional point of damage per turn. Therefore, a swarm of eight yards in radius inflicts two bashing damage per turn if it constricts down to a four-yard radius, three bashing if it halves that again to a two-yard radius, and four bashing damage per turn it if condensed itself down to a one-yard radius. Though condensing doesn’t usually happen all that often in nature (save in the case of creatures such as killer bees), it is an easy enough thing for a being with supernatural powers to command them to do so.
            Armor is effective against a swarm only if it covers one’s full body, but even then it provides only half its rating. In addition, targets are distracted by the swarm, suffering –2 dice on all rolls while they are within the radius, even if they’re not specifically attacked.
            Causing the Tilt: The Swarm Exploit can cause this Tilt, as can similar powers from non-demonic sources. The Tilt can occur naturally if someone disturbs a nest of bees.
            Ending the Tilt: The swarm cannot be attacked with fists, clubs, swords or guns. Only area-affect attacks such as a torch affect it. Each point of damage inflicted by a flame or other applicable attack halves the swarm’s size. Once the swarm is reduced below a one-yard radius, either all insects are dead or the few remaining disperse.
            My Comment on Swarm Tilt with Hosts: To relate them more to Ephemeral Beings rules, I would simply make that Swarm’s Size is it’s radius - and that ‘condensing’ of Swarm is lowering their Size ( and thus Health ), but adds damage bonus. I think it simply could work as Manifestation for Hosts, instead of Twilight Form.

                So here is my first proposition for Beshilu Host...

                Rats Swarm
                <Scratching sounds in the walls>

                Background: Shartha are spreading by spiritual shards infecting living animals that are descended from their Pangean progenitor – Plague King. In the first phase of Beshilu intrusion, shards are spreading in rats nest population, leading some of vermins in it to sustain shards until full grown up in rats bodies. When mature, individual shards are separating from nest and biting humans to spread the Host. Till then, they are all living in rats swarms that gnaw the Gauntlet – and humans – in underground and dark places. All seems to follow the single mind, governed by the shard in their midst.

                Description: Few dozens of filthy rats, doing actions like from one mind – until meet other Beshilu shard, then two groups fights for control over colony. Or they evolve further, when finding suitable human body they can Claim.

                Storytelling Hints: Rat Swarm is looking for places to hide and spread. Once finding perfect place for rats, it multiplies and growing next Bashilu shards in it’s midst. All the time they are gnawing the Gauntlet, letting lose next spirits to split in to world of Flesh. When colony is spread enough and shards are mature, Swarm starts to seek human host – or abduct some to bite into them. It’s most often around Size 8-10 of the Swarm.

                Notice: Rats Swarm is using traits as Ephemeral Being, but is totally physical ( well, physical rats that are spiritual infected ). Instead of Rank it has Cohesion, working more or less the same for it as Rank for Spirits. Rats Swarm is, obviously, under Swarm as Manifestation instead of Twilight Form and follows rules for Swarms Tilt in it. It’s Size is Swarm radius and he can halve it for extra damage. Even if normal attacks of Swarm are only Bashing damage, each of them is spreading Moderate Poisoned Tilt on it’s victims from Venomous Ichor – adding each turn 1 Bashing damage if not resisted – but as Uratha have inner resistance to Toxins ( they omit number of points equal to their Primal Urge ), it’s not very helping rats here.

                Cohesion: 1
                Attributes: Power 3, Finesse 2, Resistance 4
                Willpower: 6
                Essence: 10
                Initiative: 6
                Defense: 3
                Speed: 12
                Size: 5
                Corpus: 9
                Manifestations: Discorporate, Possess, Swarm
                Dread Powers: Beastmaster (Rats), Gnaw Gauntlet, Venomous Ichor

If successful, the devourer gains one Experience per dot of Cohesion/Primal Urge. They also gain a new Dread Power or an Attribute dot.

                                        So, I mucked around with those rules a little more, especially with an eye for the text and mechanics in Werewolf: The Forsaken Second Edition pg. 202-205, and seemed to find a couple of numerical patterns that stand out and allow for a slightly more elegant rewrite. This might be seeing something in nothing, but it works alright for me. wyrdhamster Cohesion + Resistance is, generally, a decent amount of Health, if only because conventional assault against a Swarm is useless, as per the Swarm Tilt rules. Area of Effect damage behaves normally instead of halving the Swarm, because a Swarm of shartha is very different than a Swarm of natural insects or rats. Because of this, unless your Werewolf is one with Gifts that enable easy access to Area of Effect damage (hello Gift of the Elements, favoured Gift of the Hunters in Darkness), pawing at a Host swarm is effectively useless, and you'll just get yourself bogged down.

                                        Anyways, here's my rewrite.

                                        *****

                                        Shattered Gods- The Hosts

                                        The Hosts are the most dangerous prey that any werewolf can hunt. These blasphemous fragments of ancients gods- beings that survived even Father Wolf’s rage -are caught in an eternal effort to forge their progenitors anew from their spiritual shards. They are a foe that can hide, coiled in the carcasses of humanity, and at the same time they are terrible abominations of immense size and strength. They twist the Gauntlet with ease, befouling the two worlds. Just killing a shartha is rarely enough to truly finish it off.

                                        Essence: The base substance of ephemera is the lifeblood of the Hosts, dictating the limits of their power. A Host shard starts with an Essence of 1, which is also the total number of points of Essence they can hold.
                                        · Attributes: A Host in its natural shape possesses the three simplified Attributes Power, Finesse, and Resistance. They possess a number of Attribute dots equal to (Essence + 3), and they infest a victim at the same rate as the Claimed.
                                        · Verminous Swarm: The Hosts slowly accumulate around themselves swarms of vessels for the essence of their Pangaean progenitor, presenting as a Swarm (as per the Swarm Tilt) with a radius equal to Essence. In this shape they possess Health equal to Resistance + Essence, though they can divide themselves into multiple, smaller, Swarms if necessary.
                                        · Dread Powers: When first infested, a Host only possesses the Discorporation and Gnaw Gauntlet (Beshilu) or Gauntlet Webs (Azlu) Dread Powers. They gain another Dread Power for every three points of Maximum Essence they possess.
                                        · Regaining Essence: The Host may gather Essence from Loci, or by devouring the flesh of humans, werewolves, and other shartha. When devouring for Essence they inflict Aggravated damage, but only regain a single point of Essence, regardless of how much damage they would naturally inflict.

                                        Cohesion: A Supernatural Tolerance Trait representing the shartha’s accumulation of Essence, slowly building into something that is like the dead god from whose corpse they crawled forth. Use the Primal Urge chart on Werewolf: The Forsaken Second Edition pg. 93. When the Host’s maximum Essence increases past the equivalent maximum Essence for that level of Primal Urge, their Cohesion increases.

                                        Willpower: The Hosts possess neither Virtue nor Vice, instead regaining Willpower only through rest, and through the consumption of Essence. Like Spirits, they regain a point of Willpower for every three points of Essence they regain.

                                        Devouring: When the Host kills another shard, or a werewolf, they may take the time devouring them utterly, adding their spiritual potency to their own. If they fully consume another Host shard or werewolf (as per Regaining Essence above) then they add the victim’s Primal Urge (or Essence) to their own Essence.

                                        *****

                                        So, a few things that this does which I consider rather nifty. At high Cohesion/Essence it allows Swarms to get positively enormous instead of just reasonably big. If the Red Minister, as written, was to be killed in their hybrid form, they'd transform into a Swarm with a radius of 20 Yards, or 60-ish feet (not withstanding any reduction in radius due to damage taken that killed the hybrid). And, of course, the Swarm could divide itself up in order to cover the retreat of a fraction of the original Swarm. Risky, but potentially highly rewarding if it allows the Host to survive a werewolf assault. Another thing I noticed is that, following these rules, Cohesion starts out building rather slowly, explodes very quickly in the mid-range, and then slows way down once it hits Cohesion 8. The transformation into something resembling a reformed Pangaean god takes time. Of course, if the Host doesn't mind cannibalizing its own kind, it can build up power a lot more quickly than they would if they went after, say, werewolves exclusively.

                                        I also did away with Manifestations for the shartha because I realized that the mechanics of inducing a Host infection should really be narrative in scope and really don't need to be written up except for as a short-hand of how long it takes before a fairly regular person warp into a monstrosity. It's up to you whether certain Dread Powers are repressed in Swarm Form and only arise as their host body mutates, or whether they are "on" all the time. Use judgement as necessary. Personally, depending on the Dread Power, I'd have them arise one at a time for every three Attribute dots the target gains, barring the ones they get for free which are always present. Gives the nice, slow, Body Horror slide from freshly dead human to sack of skin filled with rats/spiders, to gigantic, monstrous hybrid.
