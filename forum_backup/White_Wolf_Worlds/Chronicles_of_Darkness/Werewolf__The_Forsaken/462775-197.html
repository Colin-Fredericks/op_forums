                                07-19-2023, 11:04 PM
                                Originally posted by ForsakenProspects View Post
                                The core books says that using the Unfetter manifestation only temporarily disconnects a Spirit from it's host and that if its not back within range of its fetter by scene's end, it goes dormant. Is there anyway for a Spirit to voluntarily end the Fetter permanently without going dormant or having to kill the host?
                                Corebook also says a spirit can voluntarily end the Fettered Condition by using the Unfetter Manifestation, which I think it is reasonable to interpret as a different but identically-priced application to the "temporarily suppresses the Condition" option described on the Manifestation table.

                                Essentially, if you have Unfetter, you can either totally end the Fettered Condition (and need to spend the Essence in order to re-apply it later) or you can temporarily suppress it for the scene and regain its benefits at the end as long as you're back in range in time.

                                Answering your question more literally, getting rid of the supporting Resonant or Open Conditions ends the Fettered Condition as normal for Manifestations in general.
