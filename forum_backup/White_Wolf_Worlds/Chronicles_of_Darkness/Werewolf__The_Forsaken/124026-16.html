                                                    This isn't a good solution to the current issue in any way. Agg in general seems to be becoming much rarer as a damage type in general and I feel it's extremely out of place to add it here. Besides, as has been stated, no one has a problem with Gauru dealing lethal to vampires anyways. This still doesn't solve the actual problem, which is the unclear wording used in the damage downgrade section of vampire. The fact that they state that most non-supernatural weapons, heavily implying fully supernatural weapons are exempt to this rule, are downgraded. That is what is causing the problem. This is a crossover issue that deals with the fact that by it's wording, all (or at least most) explicitly supernatural powers that deal lethal bypass the alteration. People complained and MachineIV gave his own ruling of it that could be taken two ways. The first interpretation kept it mostly the same, while the second requires any effect that would bypass it, regardless of which line said effect came from, to have to explicitly call out vampires in order to have it working as intended. We've seen the kind of keyboard crusades people waged this time when another line specifically mentions something bypasses it, so to have every line be forced to specifically call it out time and time again will cause no end to the bellyaching.

                                                    As for werewolves bypassing an ability that vampires have to spend xp on, that is false. We're talking about nothing but innate abilities here. And a vampire is better off taking this than a funds manager, because they have the ability to heal it off with relatively little effort. So again, we aren't even arguing over the war form, it is fine as is (if not a little too intensive in the healing department).
