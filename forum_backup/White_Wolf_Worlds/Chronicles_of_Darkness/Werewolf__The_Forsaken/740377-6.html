                            In-progress conversion of the Scheherezade Gifts, Drawing the Curtain and Magnum Opus, for Crooked Moon. Any suggestions for reskinning them are welcome.

                            -=-

                            Drawing The Curtain (oooo)
                            Cost: 3 Essence
                            Dice Pool: Manipulation + Expression + Glory vs. the subject's Composure + Primal Urge
                            Action: Instant
                            Duration: One roll per success
                            A wise storyteller knows that when an enemy must be dealt with, the best story is a tragedy. Cahalith with this Facet can tell a tale of woe so terrible that the subject of the tale is marked by fate for an awful end.

                            Dramatic Failure: Not only does the story not stir fate to turn on the listener, but the listener is aware that the Cahalith was toying with his destiny.

                            Failure: The Cahalith finishes her story, but nothing happens.

                            Success: The story influences the listener in a dark way. The next time the listener's player (or Storyteller) chooses to spend Willpower to add dice to a roll, tragedy strikes. The Cahalith's player may declare that the roll is automatically considered a dramatic failure.

                            Exceptional Success: As a success, but the prey receives the Demoralized Condition after suffering from the effects of the Facet for the first time.


                            Magnum Opus (ooooo)
                            Cost: 3 Essence
                            Dice Pool: Presence + Expression + Glory
                            Action: Instant
                            Duration: 1 roll Per Success
                            Stories serve a number of functions for a pack: To remind them of past glories, educate, or inspire to future greatness. The inspirational potential of the story is demonstrated at its full potential with this Facet. The storyteller weaves an epic tale of how spectacular their successes will be in the near future, and the listeners are so inspired they are able to perform acts with success beyond their wildest dreams. The Cahalith can use this Facet to affect several listeners equal to her Glory + Primal Urge.

                            Dramatic Failure: The story's effect is counter to the intention. The next time the listener spends Willpower to enhance a roll, no dice are added to the roll, and the listener still loses the point.

                            Failure: The Facet has no effect.

                            Success: The story rouses greatness in its listener. The next time the listener spends Willpower to enhance an action, instead of adding three dice to the roll, add three successes to the total rolled.

                            Exceptional Success: The next time the listener spends a point of Willpower to enhance an action, that action is automatically an exceptional success.

                                Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post
                                Gift of Savagery
                                At its heart, a predator is a savage creature. And the Uratha are no exception. Those who seek to master the Gift of Savagery take this primal truth, and shape it into another tool in their hunts. Among the Pure, Predator Kings are masters at controlling their feral instincts and manipulating such savagery in others. Those with this Gift become more capable killers, and even more lethal foes in a hunt. Similarly, Blood Talons who have had the Gift marked upon their bodies are counted among the most vicious of their tribe. And more than once have they felled prey who made the mistake of underestimating their ferocity.


                                -=-

                                Unshackled Rage [Honor]
                                Cost: 1 Essence per target
                                Dice Pool: Manipulation + Expression + Honor vs. Composure + Primal Urge
                                Action: Contested; resistance is considered reflexive
                                With this Facet, the Predator King has mastered his own savagery and anger to the point where he can fiercely project it onto others, making them experience the depths of his potential for viciousness. More than that, he can focus his anger so powerfully that it can steal the rational thoughts of humans and other werewolves, causing humans to act like savage animals, and pushing Uratha close to Kuruth.

                                Dramatic Failure: The attempted projection of the character's killing urges backfires, and he must test for Death Rage.

                                Failure: The Gift has no effect.

                                Success: The minds of the targets are clouded in animalist fury, making people act on their most savage instincts. Each individual targeted suffers the following effects depending on their nature.

                                Werewolves must roll for Death Rage, and treat their effective Harmony as one step lower for the purpose of resisting Death Rage stimuli for the scene.

                                Humans become violently angry with the closest person to them (including the werewolf if appropriate). They suffer the effects of the Berserk Condition for the remainder of the scene.

                                Exceptional Success: As with a success, though a werewolf affected by the Facet falls into Wasu-Im on the spot. Furthermore, the effects now last a number of scenes equal to the werewolf's Honor Renown.
                                -=-

                                I was thinking of using this Gift in a game I'm in, but I realized this facet has some major overlap with the Cunning Facet of the Gift of Rage. Very nearly identical.

                                I was thinking of replacing it with some kind of gift that gives you your Honor in turns after your last health box fills with aggravated damage, sort of a last ditch effort that seems very Predator King, even if it's not terribly useful for a player character it could also allow for some cool RP moments.

                                Anyway, I just figured I'd mention it.
