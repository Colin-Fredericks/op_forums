    Werewolf Antagonist: The Broodmother

    06-13-2014, 05:40 AM
    I was thinking of starting a Werewolf game to test PenDragon's GMC werewolf fix. I wanted to structure the campaign around a central threat, something dangerous, and something that would give some more experienced Uratha a run for their money, and be strange enough to surprise the players. So I decided to look through the various antagonist books I have, and came upon the Mother from Predators. It sparked an idea, one of werewolves versus the Zerg ala Starcraft and the idea brought a devious smile to my face. So I am restructuring the Mother in the image of the Tyranid, and the Zerg. So here's my notes on it.

    The Broodmother

    According to one all-but-forgotten tale, when Luna descended to the earth, her handmaidens walked with her. They were the ones to draw away the rejected suitors so that Luna could be alone with Father Wolf, and when her time there was done, they ascended back to the sky with her. All but one. One of her handmaidens decided to tarry, taking her leisure with Luna’s rejected suitors and enjoying the delights of the flesh. She was still there when the Gauntlet rose, and became flesh forevermore.

    At least, that is what the people whisper, when the name, [First Tongue Name], comes up. The truth may be far stranger.

    From places unknown did the Broodmother come from, and it came to assimilate everything to be part of its' Brood.

    The Broodmother is a grotesque creature that stands taller than an Uratha in Gauru form. It's body is covered in a layer of thick hide, made partly of chitin and knotted muscles. Behind it is a mockery of a womb, a long, nearly transparent, sack that glows in a sickly light, dark shadows seem to flit below the skin; larvae and it's children. It's large body is supported by six, strong, bladed limbs.

    (Ugh, not so good at fluff writing right now -.-)

    Alright, so in my game, the truth behind the Broodmother is that it's part of biological weapon designed by the Ancients (the one's who created humanity) and when the Hisil and material were closer. In simplest terms, the Broodmother takes Essence and genetic materials and births monsters, until they wipe out the everything on the planet. However, it was corrupted and the Brood seeks to make the perfect creature, the pinnacle of evolution. That's why it wants Uratha, to assimilate their shapechanging into the Swarm.

    Anyways, my idea for the campaign is for a corporation of humans to dig it up with the intent to exploit it. They'll be the first antagonists, what it'll start with. However, they unleash the Broodmother which begins to multiply. They attack, capture and assimilate Uratha. It'll involve protecting their territory, convincing other packs to help, and even humans learning about it. There'll be repercussions in the Hisil, my idea is the psychic influence of the Brood causes spirits to become more likely to go Magath, devour other spirits in a grotesque mockery of the Brood's own drive to eat, and assimilate everything to become the perfect creature.

    • Spawn: The Brood mother can spawn three larvae as a
    standard action for a point of Willpower,alternatively, the
    Broodmother can spawn one Broodling, The newborn
    broodling takes one turn to get its bearings,and then
    can begin attacking its Mother’s assailants or otherwise
    carrying out its progenitor’s will. Newborn spawn are of
    course the weakest of the brood, and should be given
    appropriate statistics.
    • Empathic: The Broodmother communicates by receiving
    and broadcasting emotions. It can effectively sense the
    emotions of anyone within 100 yards, and can “read” a
    specific person’s emotions in more detail as easily as a person
    observes another person’s clothing. It may attempt to
    broadcast its own emotions as a means of communication.
    Actively sifting through these emotions to understand the
    gist of what the Mother is attempting to “say” requires a
    Composure + Empathy roll. Success establishes some measure
    of context (“It wants us to leave”), and exceptional
    success provides greater context (“It recognizes us as old
    enemies but doesn’t want to fight us yet”).
    • Brood Control: The Mother is in constant telepathic
    communication with all of its spawn that remain
    within 200 yards. Unlike communication with outsiders,
    the Mother and its spawn instinctively understand one
    another, and can communicate as effectively as if they
    were using language. The psychic presence of the Broodmother
    can control the Swarm, giving them purpose and intelligence.
    Any spawn under the Broodmother's psychic control has
    its Intelligence, Resolve and Composure traits increased by 2.
    • Infection: The Broodmother can infect a living target with
    Brood parasites. Spend 3 Essence to releases a cloud of
    eggs which latch onto the either through breath or burrowing
    into their skin. When infected, the parasites rewrite the genetic
    material of the target, and pump them full of the Mother's vile
    Essence, turning it into a Brood, and allowing it to be controlled
    by the Broodmother.

    • Regeneration: The Mother regains either three
    wounds of lethal damage or four wounds of bashing
    damage each turn as a reflexive action. It will regenerate
    lethal damage first, regenerating bashing only when no
    more lethal damage is left on its health track. Aggravated
    damage is healed at the rate of two levels per day.


    On the Infected, I'd probably base it off the system for Claiming a victim.

    Anyways, any idea for story seeds of Brood, or any ways you could see this refined, and expanded on?

                      Brood Infested Human

                      When the infestation reaches critical stages it starts to twist the flesh and bone of the infected, strengthening muscles, bones and evolving weaponry to make one an effective killing machine. The will of the person is subsumed, and their memories stripped from them and transferred to the Hivemind of the Brood. All that is left is a feral killing machine, it's will inexorably tied to the will of the Broodmother.
                      Description: The figure was once a man, or a woman, but now it's nothing but a shambling horror. It's eyes are twin pinpricks of crimson, like burning coals. It's skin its leathery with misshapen, hulking muscles. It's jaw is split in twain, you can see fangs where teeth once where, and a tube that looks as if it's a writhing centipede, runs down it's neck, and disappears into it's chest. It makes a strange, constant growling sound. The only thing it can say is, "For the Swarm..." in a hollow, buzzing voice. The infested's hands are morphed into a mockery of the human hand, it's fingers are lengthened to to extreme lengths the skin and nails replaced by a dull grey chitin sharper than a knife.

                      Brood Drone
                      The Brood Drone is one of the most basic genetic templates within the larvae's code; it is the beginning of the Hive and one of the most protected of the Brood. Found almost exclusively within the Hive the Drone has the ability to produce and spread the nest jelly that forms the basis of the Hive. The nest jelly is an organic, wax like substance that breaks down nutrients from the soil, and creatures cocooned within it, and transfer it, through veins within the Jelly itself, to the genetic pools, and Hive structure-organs. The Drone's most important ability is it's ability to morph into the organ-structures of the Hive. One that will eventually store genetic memory, and produce larvae and the other horrors of the Swarm.
                      Description: The Drone is an oversized, and matured larvae in many ways. It's body is a long, and sinuous with thick chitin. It has short legs keeping it's body low to the ground. It's tail ends with a nasty stinger that drips with a vile, green liquid. It's head is characterized by multiple eyes dotting it's 'face', and an insect like mandible that drips a thick, viscous material. IIt has two arms that end in fine, clawed manipulators allowing it to shape and structure the jelly.

                      Brood Warrior

                      The staple, and most basic warrior template of the Brood uses the DNA of various terrestrial predators from wolves, great cats, et al and improved upon by the Brood's engineering. It's a fearless and efficient killing machine, using stealth, ambush, strength and tactics to overpower foes. Usually the second of the Brood to be hatched by a nascent Hive, the Warriors are adept at killing, or subduing living biomass to bring back to the Hive to help it flourish.
