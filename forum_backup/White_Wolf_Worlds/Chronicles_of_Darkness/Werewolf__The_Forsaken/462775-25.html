        I was running a goof-off game with a friend of mine to get used to a variety of WoD games and I ran into a bizarre situation. Once again, this was a one-on-one game where we were both more or less messing around, so he was using a god-like spirit to attack the material realm using a royal avatar and I quickly made a pack of werewolves to go fight it, and they managed to kill the royal avatar.

        But it made me wonder some things that I've not found a rule for in the various books I've collected. The question is regarding discorporated, but not destroyed spirits.

        When they reappear after "gaining" 1 essence for each healthbox, does a spirit have the same essence they had when they discorporated, like hibernation? If it has 3 essence left and 10 corpus, it takes 10 days to reform and has 3 essence when it reforms, making it vulnerable to destruction? Is that how it works?

        My second question is, when a spirit reforms after being discorporated from sheer trauma, does it reappear at full health or does it still have some degree of damage?

        Example, if I've got a Ensir rank 3 with 10 corpus and 20 essence, and it takes 10 aggravated from a bane, it will discorporate but not die and discorporate with 10 essence left... When it reappears at a resonant location, what's its health and essence like?

        My last question is how this also applies to a royal avatar. I think, I remember, that gods can't appear in the material world at all so they'd have to use a royal avatar (not that it WOULD go beserk or that its reasonable for an ST to expect werewolves to outright go to war with a god...) but, if you defeat the royal avatar, what then? How does a god make a new avatar, and how long does it take? Could it make one immediately, or does it only have the one avatar and has to wait for that one to re-corporate over time like any other spirit below rank 6? Is it difficult for a god to make a royal avatar if it is destroyed instead of merely discorporated?

        I don't remember seeing any rules on this sort of thing (and probably for a very good reason, this was a ridiculous scenario), but I am curious.

                Keep in mind, this has to happen around sources that give the spirit appropriate essence. If there aren't any source available, the spirit will stick to slumber indefinitely. Then there's the fact that other spirits of the same inclinations would already be on the hunt for such sources. Discorporation isn't a pleasant, or safe experience.

                @Royal Avatar: A spirit capable of creating a Royal Avatar of himself likely has the ability as a numina or manifestation. If it wants to make another, it uses it again. No, Royal Avatars wouldn't normally reform like normal spirits, unless the sponsor of it has some compulsion to invest more on it. And while the likes of spirits such as Firstborns have essence enough to make several avatars, I doubt it'd allow them to drive more than one at the same time.

                            Originally posted by Scarlet Witch View Post
                            A discorporated spirit cannot be hunted though can it?
                            Discorporation treats the spirit as if it had 'died' by losing all its Corpus to lethal injury. On page 186, it explains that spirits that lose all their Corpus like that return to a safe place and turns Dormant, which page 184 states puts them into a Twilight state of hibernation.

                            Providing you can find where they've run off to and effect Twilight you should be able to destroy them. Other spirits of similar umia can awaken them or feast upon them.
