I would think it would make more sense for some be born in Hishu form to human parents and some be born in Urhan form to wolf parents. It would make sense for some to be born in the hybrid forms too, if there were anything in the natural world that resembled those forms to serve as parents. I like to imagine that before the Sundering, some of them might have been born in hybrid forms to Pangean patents. Maybe the first pack was born in Gauru; after all, it's said that was the Father's form.

Lots of splats are more inhuman than not, and in my opinion, werewolves are among those splats.

The character can be just as relatable as any werewolf, even if their situation is not. My second response is, isn't that the point of roleplaying? Expanding our experiences by empathizing with characters who have different experiences and different worldviews than we do? I may not know exactly how it feels to be the only pup in my litter with the self-awareness and critical thinking skills of a human, but I can empathize with the feeling of not fitting in because my mind works differently than others' minds, and I can extrapolate from that feeling to portray a character with sincerity. My third response is, I don't think Urhanu need to be a core feature in Forsaken. I fully understand that they're a departure from the default setting. But they're a departure I think is cool, so I'm interested in discussing it. As Wyrdhampster said, if you don't like it, no one is going to make you use it in your games.

                                                    Originally posted by Almarck View Post
                                                    Question: How about approaching the Spirit-Flesh hybrid angle from the other way? A wolf or hunting spirit that gains corporeal form, developing from a relatively simple spirit into a complex quasicorporeal entity?                                                     As I mentioned earlier, I think “spirit-born” might work as the explanation for a WtF counterpart to the Metis: not of man, nor of wolf. (What does “Ulle'u” mean, anyway? We might need to rename it in light of this reinterpretation.) I wouldn't want to replace the wolf-born with the spirit-born; but I could see having both.

                                                    In fact, how about this: in WtA, the Homid and Lupus Breeds were presented as polar opposites, with the Metis existing in a middle ground between them; but in the context of WtF, I could see the Urhanu and the Ulle'u being “off-center”, with the Urhanu having a special affinity for Father Wolf and the Ulle'u having a similar affinity with Mother Luna. Meanwhile, the Hishu would occupy the “middle ground”, having a natural affinity for balance between the flesh and the spirit. Some possible manifestations of this arrangement: the Ulle'u might have some sort of edge when it comes to Shadow Gifts, while the Urhanu get a similar edge with respect to Wolf Gifts; the Ulle'u might get some sort of bias toward Breaking toward Spirit while the Urhanu get a bias toward Breaking toward Flesh. (The Ulle'u “deformities” thing can go, as far as I'm concerned; if it's kept, it should be representative of what sort of spirit you were before your encounter with Mother Luna.)

                                                    The Pure Tribes probably have “Breed” preferences, with the Fire-Touched tending to recruit from Ulle'u, the Predator Kings recruiting from the Urhanu, and the Ivory Claws favoring the Hishu.
