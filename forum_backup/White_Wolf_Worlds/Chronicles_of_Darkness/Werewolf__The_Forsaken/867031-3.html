                                        Originally posted by BinAly View Post
                                        I've always maintained that Spirit Totems impose Bans that feed them Essence, instead of Bans that are similar to theirs. An Arson Spirit will require his followers to burn something of value once a month, while Smoke Drinker requires his followers to never sleep at the same place twice, probably because the act of moving away gives him Essence to feed.
                                        Plausible. I don't think the spirits decide what Ban to impose on a Lodge or Pack, though - I think it naturally emerges from their nature.

                                        At that point, the question gets very muddy; the same underlying thing (the nature of the spirit in question) informs its Ban, the Ban it gives to werewolves tied to it, and what it can feed on. It'd have to be a damn strange spirit for those three things to all inherit something completely different from its core nature.

                                        It's the nature of an Arson spirit to burn things, so it does that, feeds on other people doing that, and imposes that requirement on its werewolves; it's the nature of the Smoke Drinker to never stop moving, so it never stops moving, its werewolves never stop moving, and it feeds on that.

                                        I think "can the spirit feed on this?" is a good question to ask when inventing a Ban, but if I have a really strong idea that doesn't directly feed the spirit but does suit it in other ways I wouldn't worry about using it.
