    USB Needs Help with Chronicle Planning! (Pleeease?)

    08-27-2014, 10:06 PM
    Hi everyone, USB here. I am currently in the process of drafting a solo Chronicle to coincide with the release of Werewolf 2e, and I need help ironing out some of the ideas I have had so far. While I am sure that I could eventually figure it out on my own, there is definitely something to be said for the sheer efficiency and sometimes effectiveness of crowd-sourcing, and I am sure that, on your part, helping will not eat up such a significant amount of time. So, without further ado!

    Synopsis

    Ciara Hudson is a 16-year-old high school sophomore who lives in Verdant Pines, a (fictional) college town somewhere in the Northwest. She enjoys art, music, gymnastics, trips to the local woods, and hanging out with her best friend; normal teenage girl stuff. Recently, however, she has been in the grip of some terrifying dreams, where something, or someone, is stalking her through the corridors of their house. Somehow in these dreams, she is always 8 years old again, and the house is far bigger than it seemed even back then. A chilling dread - inexplicable fear - and the rattling of chains heralds the thing's approach. Always, she has to try and hide from it. Always, she has to run. Always, she is jerked awake right before it catches her, or she catches a glimpse of it.

    She has been fortunate so far, but she keeps having the dreams, and they are starting to bleed into her life. Just what is this dream-stalker of hers, and what does it want?

    ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Now, obviously this is hardly the entire Chronicle; maybe a session or 2, but it is going to be part of the Prelude leading up to the First Change; a crucial stage, especially given that my player has never tried Werewolf before. I already have an idea for the story behind this story, which I will detail below; what I need help with is making the explanation work.

    Here it goes:


    The Story Behind the Story

    The life of Uratha is a living hell: this has been Mark Hudson's belief and greatest fear ever since the night of his First Change, when he lost his girlfriend to Rage beyond his control. Even though he was found by a Forsaken pack, who were decent folk by most standards; even after they taught him what it means to be Uratha, the legends, the powers, the Oath, he still struggled against living with it. Perhaps it was the endless Hunt, which felt more like a lifetime of war. Perhaps it was the internecine conflicts; Forsaken against Forsaken, vying for territory, resources, dominance, even before you took the Pure into account. Perhaps it was the outbursts of Rage, which, although unusual in occurrence, were still considered an inevitability, and only reinforced the idea in Mark's mind that the condition was a curse, a cruel, horrifying trick. Whatever the reason, he found the conviction - or cowardice - to turn his back on the pack, turn his back on it all.

    Unfortunately, the pack was faced in battle by a mighty spirit almost as soon as he left, and it slew all but one of them. This sole survivor, the fallen pack's Ithaeur, was left maddened with physical pain and grief, and utterly broken. Lashing out in Rage, he called to the Spirit Wilds, demanding retribution on the one who had abandoned and left them to die. A spirit answered the call, and bound by the power of Ritual and Ban, was let loose to hunt.

    Meanwhile, Mark Hudson had found a life of his own in Verdant Pines. He made a living as groundskeeper on the college campus, and had a wife and 2 daughters. He had completely forsaken the Wolf, utterly devoted to living his life as a highly disciplined, responsible, and even upstanding member of the Herd. He had forgotten his war and his enemies, and the spirit, Grievance Paid in Blood, sought to exploit this weakness.

    It started with Ciara, who was 8 years old then, latching onto her and probing her mind for secrets it could use against her father. When it could glean no further information, it Urged her, using her body as its vehicle for further intelligence gathering. Ciara's juvenile mind interpreted this interaction with the spirit of vengeance as nightmares, hallucinations, and later on, when Grievance Paid in Blood finally decided to strike, a night terror:

    The thing was huge and covered with fur - a bear? - but somewhat resembled a man. It padded through her house, crept up the stairs, and made its way into her parents' bedroom. Her parents! Ciara was terrified, but at the same time knew that she had to do something. She followed the thing upstairs and peered in through the door, and she saw that the thing was on top of her mom, all razor-sharp claws and terrible teeth. She hurried downstairs to the kitchen, grabbing a knife from the rack, hoping that she would not be too late. The thing's back was turned as she opened the door, her heart pounding at her throat as she pitter-pattered as silently as she could manage on the carpeted floor, her trembling hand almost releasing the knife. But she held on, raised it high, and screamed as she plunged it down...

    Alerted by the scream, Mark was, thankfully, able to avoid getting stabbed and swiftly disarm Ciara and restrain her. Her maddened wailing and thrashing clued him in to the spirit's influence behind it, and, pausing only to order his wife out of the room and lock the door, immediately got to Banishing the spirit. The Rite took a tremendous effort, as he had denied his Wolf side for so long, and it drained his last reserve of Essence. Still, Grievance Paid in Blood was hurled back to the Spirit Wilds, and Ciara was left not only free, but mercifully without any recollection of what had happened to her.

    For a time, Mark had to be what he feared becoming again, only for the sake of his family; they could not risk another incident like that. Thus, as the Wolf, he Hunted. He tore Gifts of Warding and Safeguarding, as well as oaths of asylum and servitude from the throats of his Spirit-prey. He used every trick he knew to sow a spiritually empowered hedge of protection around his household. And thus, for a time, they were safe.

    But as the old Blood Talon adage goes, wards eventually fail, bindings can be broken, and the banished will return. 8 years have passed, 8 years of peace, and Ciara is starting to have nightmares once more...

    ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Does the Story Behind the Story provide a sufficient explanation of present events as described in the Synopsis? Does the chain of events make sense?

      Hmmm... it appears my premise for Mark is rather shaky, based on my re-reading of the previewed Kuruth and Harmony mechanics. The one I was remembering whilst making him was the Harmony revision, how a very high Harmony makes a Werewolf too flesh and, specifically, too human, and thus not only makes shapeshifting difficult but sidestepping into the Hisil impossible. I was fine with this; I was very enthralled with the idea of a reverse-Zi'ir (instead of very low Harmony, very unstable in mind and in body, he'd have too high Harmony and be too stable), and that was what Mark was supposed to represent. Unfortunately, after re-reading the Kuruth revision, it seems that he'd still be going mad every so often, in fact more often due to his very high Harmony, which I can understand; Uratha are meant to stay balanced between wolf and human.

      Ugh, stymied now... Anyone have any ideas to make this work, barring waiting for the finalized mechanics to come out (well, it IS practically September now, so yay!)?
