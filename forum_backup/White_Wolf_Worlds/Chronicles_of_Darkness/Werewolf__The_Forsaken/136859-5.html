                                                I actually had a weredragons thing I made back when we were doing a 1e site. I mean, I know there is a weredragons on this list, but this is... something of a different beast. I will probably convert it to 2e if they make a 2e version of watP - though I could probably figure out a method to do it anyways even if they don't. *considers* Anyways. It's a little edited from it's original form because the old one didn't work in all chronicles but... well, here it is.


                                                The Mirska

                                                Mythology

                                                In the beginning, the world was covered in darkness. Nothing but thick, black primordial shadow - a shadow that was vivid and alive, consuming the fabric of all that was. In this darkness the earth was nothing but dirt and wind, a cold and bleak desert hell which bore no heat and no vision. In this darkness existed only alien creatures that had existed since the beginning of time, born from the darkness and seeking only to dominate and rule over it - perhaps to even overcome it. Some spirits remember this reality as Pangaea, or the World~That~Was~Whole, but the Dragons only know it as the World~That~Was.

                                                Within the darkness was born the stars - and among those stars rose to power Seven Great beings, but only one of them important to this story: Helios, the Sun and the Ruler of the World by light. Helios saw the earth and the Darkness, and saw the creatures within it - monstrosities and terrors and horrifying visions that knew nothing but eternal destruction. Like all before him, he sought dominion over this world, but unlike many he struck with a fierce rage and an unbearable strength.

                                                He banished the Darkness itself from his presence. From him was birthed his Army, who even now fight within the Deep Shadow eternally. Unyielding and unforgiving, he cast a deathly gaze to the earth. But though his wrath was terrible, his Dominion was just: Beneath his gaze, new life was allowed to prosper. Life that was not destructive to the Whole. He sought to encase the world in his light, and destroy all those who fell beneath him. And when this era came, is when the Dragon's speak of Pangaea's Birth as the World~That~Was~Whole.

                                                But those condemned to the Darkness were not so satisfied. They saw the Sun's Dominion, and wanted it for themselves. They saw the Sun's cruelty to them, and wanted to destroy them. They plotted amongst themselves, great and terrible beings that the mind could not fathom. They argued and quarrelled, but eventually agreed that Helios must be destroyed, and that they must work together to destroy them. They agreed between them a champion that world represent the Darkness, and bestowed upon that champion powers that no being could ever have. And within that champion, they bestilled within them all the things best in the dark.

                                                That champion's name was Zirnitra - A Dragon God of Sorcery. Zirnitra ensorcelled itself into the form of a female being, who stood at the edge of Helios' Domain. There, she fought against the entities of the Darkness, to prove to the world her ferocity, her cunning, and her power. As Helios' Watched this being, he began to fall for her - a warrior as he was, and a champion at that. He came to her, and they consummated their love. And within her embrace, Zirnitra took it's true form, and swallowed the Sun whole.

                                                And the world was encased within Darkness, with a new ruler of Helios' Domain. And it could never be argued, by man or beast, that it was the Dragon who had defeated the Sun, and who swallowed it whole. Because of his spot within the Dragon's belly, the beast was bestowed with Helios' fire, which granted it a weapon against those who would challenge it's domain. And under it's rule, life wilted.

                                                It would come a time that Zirnitra would then seek to build her army. She started simply, with a set of eggs born of her from the consummation with the sun. She kept the eggs hot with her breath, and close as a mother would.

                                                The eggs hatched, and when they hatched, their minds were opened by the light of their father, with the memories of both mother and father. They saw their parent's cruelty, but knew of the result on either path - on one, life and on another, destruction. And no sooner had the eggs hatched than they set upon their mother, tearing her limb from limb and devouring her piece by piece.

                                                As as they devoured her, they set free the Sun. As he looked down, he saw what had become of them, and they looked to him as they looked to their mother, and spoke to their father upon the destroyed corpse of their mother.

                                                "Look," They cried to their father, high in the sky, "And know this, Sun God. For though you walk this world now, your path is one of life. We accept this path, and we choose it. However..." The Eldest crushed a bone beneath his foot, and snarled, "The Darkness is as much a part of the world as the light. Should you enshroud it fully, you would be no different than that of our mother, and you would destroy this world in your fire. If you walk this path, if you seek to destroy this world, Father, then we will grant you no mercy. Beneath our teeth and fangs, we will rend you as we have our mother, if it need be that we take the place of the sun itself."

                                                And at this outrage, the Sun roared - in Anger, but also in Respect, for he saw within them the light of himself, and the darkness of their mother. He agreed to keep his light to only one half of the world, and to share it with another, but in return the Dragon's world serve him - serve to ensure that never again did the world become encased in the darkness of the World~That~Was.


                                                World-View

                                                The Dragons are real. They've been there, ever since the beginning and a bit before then - in a land before time and in a world that the world does not remember. But the Mirska remember - they remember everything. Born with the genetic code of all individuals before them, they have samples of the memories of past lives few creatures are privy to, and it's this unique ability that allowed them to know of their mothers crimes and make an appropriate judgement.

                                                The Dragons task themselves not with the protection of man or monster, but of the protection of what they call 'the balance'. They recognize two primordial forces - light and darkness, and seek to ensure that neither gain the upper hand over the other, because to do such would destroy the world and allow the advent of the Things~Before. The reality is, this creed is left very loose because though the dragons remember, these memories are kept in bits and pieces, making little sense the further back it returns.

                                                Humanity still remembers the dragons in the height of their prime. They remember a group of Wise Gods, the source of all magic and the teachers of man. The remember beasts which ruled the wilds, and who taught man all it knew of the secrets of the other world. They remember a dying breed, which was both beautiful, glorious, and terrifying. They taught humanity much -- far too much.

                                                The Dragons are responsible for many of the occultic secrets that the world is aware of today or so they claim. How to hide from the creatures of the dark and how to use the light to it's advantage. It was their words that rose the Sun to such predominance, and their power which was given to humanity to defend themselves. But humanity was a creature that feared all that was more powerful in it, and the dragons were arrogant.

                                                Their fall arose around the period of time that many individuals call the dark ages - and it would have ended there, had it not been for the choice actions of a few brave Dragons, who sacrificed themselves for the rest of their kind. Driven and hunted through the machinations of the creatures of the Dark, the Dragons numbered as few as a hundred before they actually realized they had lost the earth and failed in their duties. And it was during this time that a small clutch of dragons made a sacrifice, offering their lives to a powerful incantation that would forever afflict the world - the extinction of their reptilian forms, for the similar extinction of the true memory of their existence, for a time until the dragons were needed.

                                                The power worked, afflicting the Dragons with much smaller, more innocuous forms that would never be seen as dangerous by the mortals - but in exchange, they fell into rumor, hidden creatures hiding within their burrowed holes, waiting until the time would come where they returned. As the power afflicted them, they too began to forget - to forget what they were and their power. Individuals who held the blessing of their mother and father became not the beasts of old, but men and women described as mad, sun-touched or otherwise outcasted.

                                                And then, in the early 1940s, something began to change. A great wave enveloped the world as a creature of violence and corruption stepped out from it's own realm and into the Shadow. The results were so horrific that they still haunt the world to this day. With this creature stepping forward, the chains of the spell cast so long ago upon the Dragons began to falter and break . The souls of the dragons which nestled in the hearts of men of mad dreams began to rise, and they began to remember the past - both that of those who had suffered during their slumber, and that of before, of the histories and stories they knew and their rise to power.

                                                By the middle of the decade, the Dragons had fully roused and made their efforts to return the creature from whence it came. When the dust finally cleared, the creature was gone and the Dragons made aware of a world that was vastly different from the one which they slept in - but the full picture they held before the slumbering had not returned.They remembered shattered bits and pieces of thousands if not millions of individual lifetimes, and seeking to understand the ones which existed during the slumbering has driven many mad. With the creature gone, many are cursed with the memory of a purpose, but not the recollection of how they fulfilled that purpose. Some question that the Slumbering ought to have recalled them after the event, and wonder if the creature which stepped into the Hisil during that time was merely a herald of something else.

                                                Something worse.


                                                Systems

                                                Inborn Aspects: Shapeshifting, Regeneration, Breath-Attack

                                                Custom Aspect: Breath Attack - Children of the Dragon who swallowed the sun, the Mirska still hold a shard of him within their chest. Because of this, the Mirska all benefit from the ability to breath fire. The Mirska spends an essence point, and rolls Dexterity + Athletics + Primal Urge, Penalized by Armor and Defense as appropriate. The Range is equal to a number of yards equal to 20 x Primal Urge. Any target successfully hit with this attack suffers lethal damage each turn equal to the Mirska's primal urge rating until the fire is put out. A Dragon's Breath is not a normal fire - it comes directly from Helios. As a Result, any creature who would be harmed by sunlight or fire is especially vulnerable to this, carrying the qualities of both.

                                                Weakness: Unlike most shifters, the Dragons are not actually allergic to the silver metal, and can use it and wield it without issue. That said, they are actually weak to another precious metal - gold. Helios remembers his time in Zirnitra's belly, and though the dragons are seen as his servants, that fear still echoes within him - as such, gold, which holds a strong metaphysical connection to him, seems to resonate with that spiritual fear and serves as a weapon against the Mirska. It is perhaps odd then that the dragons hold such a love of gold, to the point that many wealthy or powerful dragons will adorn themselves with the metal or hoarde it within caves- but then again, avoiding it seems as though it would clue people in, doesn't it?

                                                Reproduction: The Dragons, like their lizard forms, seem to have a great difficulty reproducing - at least, reproducing weredragons. While the dragons themselves bear no mortal inclination against mating, it will almost always uniformly result in a Dragon Blooded. If one were to go by their lizard cousins, it would be assumed that birthing an actual Were-Dragon could only be performed at a specific time, every two years - and only in the natural wilds rather than in the mortal society, where the spiritual connection is thinnest.

                                                Still, the dragons exist despite these harsh restrictions - during the Slumber, Blooded and Slumbering Dragons alike felt the natural inclination to perform this ritual, where they would begin to migrate towards climates what fit appropriately to the dragons needs and lie together in estatic splendor. Many then would attribute this act as a loss of senses, a madness creeping in, but even then those who concieved true dragons were rare until the Awakening, as in order to become a dragon you not only had to follow this ritual, but had to concieve within nature - under the light of the sun.

                                                There are two types of weredragons - The most common type today are those who are born a Mirska, notably called "Pure Bloods". These shifters are the ones who followed the above method to being born. When a Weredragon is born, they are born with a caul over their head - a thick layer of scaled, fleshy skin that is easily peeled off to reveal the child beneath. From birth, the child is capable of shifting into it's infant animal form, though much like walking it needs to learn these things in time -- and during the Slumber, shifting was all but impossible.

                                                The other type are notable more rare though it is believed it will gain more commonality as the Dragons return. The process is through a ritual, crafted of Dragon Alchemy, in which a Dragon, after consuming the potion, consumates with another shifter to create a Dragon's Egg. The Dragon's Egg is a spiritual phenomena that is solely accessable by the Mirska. The egg may be implanted within the heart of a Blooded mortal - generally of a type associated with the one consummated with. The process is not gentle and very risky - while the blooded is alive, the dragon must tear out the mortal's heart cleanly, with their own claws, and replace the heart with the egg before the mortal dies. If successful, the egg begins to beat in place of the heart, and will hatch within a month. The process will evoke a phenomena similar to the first change - traumatic, bloody, and often related to the auspice in which the dragon will be created.

                                                It should be noted that, while a target does not need to be willing to undergo this process, that it is both traumatic and painful and the target is under no compulsion to assist the Dragon that turned them. In fact, arrogance may blind a dragon so that he believes his target will take this blessing well, only to find the new dragon seeking every avenue to destroy their spiritual parent.

                                                Human: The mortal form of a dragon typically tends to build muscle in a lean, athletic way rather than a bulky stocky way. They typically have an almost golden texture to their skin, and many might (after realizing their nature) describe their features as serpentine or reptilian. Perhaps most notably of all, however, is that all Dragons in their mortal form bear a birthmark, a tell of sorts which is a symbol of their status.
                                                Modifiers: +2 to perception rolls. Attempts to identify them as supernatural beings suffer the standard -2 penalty.

                                                Near-Man: Taking on the dragonic form while maintaining a sense of what it is to be human, this form is typically used as a sort of battle form for the dragon, especially for a trained or militant one. Their arms develop gauntlet like scales, as do their legs - their skin takes on a soft reptilian pallor, and their eyes always turn a shade golden. If the Dragon has the Wings or Additional Limbs Aspect (for a tail) these features can optionally be accessed in this form, giving the dragon an almost demon-like appearance.
                                                Modifiers: +2 to Strength, + 1 to Stamina, +1 Size, +2 Health, +2 Speed, +2 Perception. Bite for 0L or Claw for 2L. All Strength rolls, including attacks, gain the 9 again benefit. Use the higher of Dexterity or Wits to calculate Defense.

                                                War-Form: Combining the form of the Sungazer lizard and human, the Mirska grows a full two to three feet taller and puts on roughly two hundred pounds of weight. The legs and body become scaled and jagged, and the head takes on a lizard's face. The eyes glow with a golden light, and the inside of the Mirska's mouth glows with the threat of fire to erupt. If the Dragon has the Wings or Additional Limbs Aspect (for a tail), these aspects are added to this form.
                                                Modifiers: +4 Strength, +2 Stamina, +2 Size, +4 Health, +4 Speed, +3 Perception, Bite for 3L or Claw for 2L, bulletproof armor equal to half their Primal Urge score. All Strength-based rolls, including attacks, gain the 8-again quality. Use the higher of Dexterity or Wits to calculate Defense.

                                                Dire-Beast: The often forgotten form of the original dragon, this creature seems to be a much larger version of the animal form in which the Dragons are born to. Standing on four legs at the size of the average man, and weighing between two to three hundred pounds, it's hard to believe this reptile can fly if it has wings - never the less, if they hold the aspect the option is readily available to them. In this form, the creature's eyes take on a golden hue, and their chest seems to glow with a golden light, especially when they are about to breath fire upon the enemy.
                                                Modifiers: Strength +2, Dexterity +2, Stamina +2, +1 Size, +3 Health, +7 Speed, +2 Perception, Bite for 2L or Claw for 1L. All Strength-based rolls, including attacks, gain the 9-again quality. Use the higher of Dexterity or Wits to calculate Defense.

                                                Animal-Form: Tiny and adorable, the Dragons claim that this was not their original form - merely the end result of a sacrificed ritual designed to protect them all from death's gaze. By default, a Dragon's animal form mimicks that of the armadillo girdled lizard. A character who takes on other aspects such as camouflage may appear as a chameleon, or one with wings may take on the form of the Draco Volans - but it always seems to take on a small lizards form.
                                                Modifiers: Dexterity +2, Stamina +1, Size 1, Health -3, Speed +5, Perception +2, Bite for 0L. Use the higher of Dexterity or Wits to Calculate Defense.


                                                Auspice: Like werewolves, the Mirska benefit from Auspices of their own, which denotes their spiritual position within the Draconic Society. This Auspice is typically decided by the time of day in which the Mirska was born - or in the case of the Turned, the time of day of their first change. Because it is not possible to birth a dragon outside of the light of Helios, there are no (known) dragons who do not have an auspice or have a night related auspice... or at least, that is what the Dragon's believe. Ancient memories suggest another truth of darker things which corrupt dragons within the womb, allowing for them to be born in the night... but such things are hearsay and rumor, nothing more.

                                                The Farseers- Born at Dawn, the Farseers are the innovators of the future. It's said that it is the Farseers who crafted Dragon Alchemy, but whether or not that is true stands to debate, as Mnesis does not actually seem to provide the answer of the dragon's nature. Born with the ability to see the upcoming day, the Farseers benefit from prophetic dreams: Once per story, the player may ask the Storyteller for a dream of prophecy, providing some clue about the challenges facing the Farseer. The Farseer must sleep for at least four hours in order to dream of the future. The dream is always veiled in symbolism that the character must interpret. In addition, the Farseer automatically gains one die to any Occult rolls made to interpret omens or to solve occult riddles. Associated with the Dawn Descant.

                                                The Burning Ones - Born of the Rising Sun, the Burning Ones are the Warrior Dragons. Known for their Ferocity, the Burning Ones will tear asunder any who stand in their way or threaten what they call theirs. They are blessed with the Burning Talon's Benefit: The burning ones claws burn hot, to the point that they may add their Primal Urge Penalty to any attack roll. Furthermore, this additional damage is treated as heat damage (if bashing) or fire damage (if lethal), afflicting supernaturals who would call these things their banes respectively. Associated with the Searings Descant.

                                                The Sun Priests - Born when the sun is at it's highest peak. The Chosen Sorcerers of the Dragon's, the Sun Priests dedicate their time to the study of the supernatural, the hidden and the known. They benefit from the Ancient Foe's memory - any time engaging with a supernatural phenomena, the Sun Priests may roll their Mnesis rating to gather 'information' about the entity relevant to them. Associated with the Firelings Descant.

                                                The Warm Ones - Born during the Setting sun, when the sun begins to cool and gentle against earths creatures. Known as Healers, for their part, the Warm Ones are blessed with the Healer's Eye: With a reflexive Wits + Composure roll, the Warm One can identify the wounds and sicknesses which ail an individual. This typically provides the Warm One with a bonus equal to the successes rolled on all rolls to 'heal' an afflicted individual. Associated with the Warmlings Descant.

                                                The Lore Keepers - Born at Dusk, the Lore Keepers, as their name implies, are tasked with the memory and history of the Mirska - they bear a unique and powerful connection to the memories that the Dragons gather, to such an extent that they can tap into the memories at will. The Lore Keepers may buy Mnesis at half cost. Further, once per scene they may choose to roll their Mnesis rating to tap into their ancient memory. Each success may be used to increase a skill of their choice, as the Lore Keeper taps into past lives and makes them their own. Associated with the Dusk Descant.

                                                The Hidden Ones - Born on a day so cloudy that it hides even the Sun, the Hidden Ones are those dragons whom have been blessed with the ability to hide even in the light. They benefit from the Chameleon's Curse - Any rolls to notice these individuals are penalized by the Dragon's Primal Urge Penalty. Oddly enough, they are associated with the Illuminator's Descant.

                                                The Crowned Ones - Born on a day which the Sun is Eclipsed, these Dragons are beyond the concept of rare - they are practically unheard of. Still, memories remember the Crowned Ones with a fervent fondness as the Princes of the Dragons, those whose ability granted them the Lordly Tongue, Which allows both communication and command to lesser entities, typically lesser animals and entities.


                                                Rage: Ferocious and full of Avarice, the Dragon that loses himself to rage is a terrifying thing to behold. When the rage strikes, it fills the Mirska with memories of the Slumber - of all the Slumbering Dragons who were called mad for their dreams, their memories of distant times, and all the persecution and destruction reigned on the Dragon's brethren. He loses himself to an unstoppable killing frenzy, not able to tell the difference between friend or foe. Once all has been destroyed in his path, the Mirska is haunted by the memories and images which filled it for the next twenty four hours - they have difficulty telling which memories are theirs or their predecessors, and may develop the fear that they will be hunted again and tortured for their 'madness'. (Paranoia).


                                                ~New Merit: Mnesis (1-5)~

                                                Note: Based on Ancient Echoes from banishers.

                                                Prerequisites: Mirska or Mirska Blooded 4

                                                Effect: The Mirska retain the genetic memory of those who came before them - like past lives, they remember the lives of their mother and father, and their great grand father - on and on until the beginning of time. These memories are fractured, broken, and maddening - but some try to make sense of it. This merit reflects the memories a Dragon can make sense of. Each dot in this merit also adds to any rolls related to remembering an event, or to any roll which can be supplimented by the Mirska's ancient memory.

                                                (1) Deja vu The character has clear memories of experiences a in a past life up to 200 years ago, i.e., something that happened during the Victorian era (19th century) or later (20th century). The character may receive simply a feeling ("I feel as if I've walked down this street before when it was cobblestones instead of asphalt.") or a more profound "memory." ("I followed a man into this library and through this hallway once before -- long ago. There was a locked door and then the restricted books section.").

                                                (2) Memory's Thin Walls The character's memory or seeming memory extends back as far as 400 years (the 17th century). The more recent the memory, the more vivid its details are to the dragon. ("I remember watching fascinated as a child while some of my uncles shoved heavy crates of tea into the waters of the harbor.") or ("I remember the stars were in the same position in the sky when a man darted across my path fleeing something indescribable and unspeakable -- much like that!")

                                                (3) Memory's Open Door The character can remember something (or something similar) having occurred as far back as the 13th century. ("I've seen the signs before in this very house -- a plague house!") Dragons can sometimes experience premonitions based on past events at this level of the Merit. ("The earth shook here once before, and I feel it growing restless again.")

                                                (4) Stepping Backward The character's memory can extend as far back as the beginning of the Common Era (1st century) and the amount of detail remembered may seem to place the character back in time, though this is certainly not an instance of time travel. ("We hunted even then, when Man was still building their mud huts and their straw roofs, we hoarded all that was valuable, secrets lost and hidden.)

                                                (5) Echoes of the World~That~Came~Before: At the highest level of this Merit, the character seems to remember aspects of Pangaea itself. Dragons learn ancient secrets lost to the throes of humanity, of relics and disasters seeking to come and return. An ancient beast comes, and they remember it, and where it was born. Dragons of this level sometimes recieve insights into the ways of ancient and lost spirits. They might experience this level with hallucinatory-like intensity or as a waking nightare; the memories of that time are the memories of symbolism rather than the literal thoughts and perceptions Dragons are accustomed to now, which makes gathering information from them difficult and frustrating.


                                                ~New Aspect: Dragon Alchemy~

                                                The blood of a dragon is a potent mystical source. The blood of a slain dragon is depicted as either beneficent or as poisonous in medieval legend and literary fiction. In German legend, dragon blood has the power to render invincible skin or armor bathed in it, as is the case with Siegfried's skin or Ortnit's armor. In the Slavic myth, the Earth refuses it as it is so vile that Mother Earth wishes not to have it within her womb, and it remains above ground for all eternity. The blood of the dragon in Beowulf has acidic qualities, allowing it to seep through iron. Heinrich von Winkelried dies after the blood of the dragon slain by him accidentally drips on him.

                                                And as always, there is always a hint of truth to the story. The Dragons have learned, throughout the years, to tap into the divinity within her blood and use it to their advantage. Through the study of their blood in relationship to alchemy they managed to create a set of mystical practices in their own form of ritual. Dragon Alchemy is an Aspect bought at new dots x7. Each dot purchased grants one Alchemical Ritual of the equivalent level to the Dragon, and allows them to purchase future rituals at a cost of ritual dots x 2. Each concoction created through the use of Dragon Alchemy requires a list of materials to meld into the dragons blood, and of couse, the blood cost itself. In game terms, each potion requires the dragon to take a number of lethal equal to the level of the ritual created.

                                                As a curious note, it is actually possible for Dragon Blooded to learn this art - unfortunately the thinness of the Dragon's Blood coursing through the Dragon Blooded makes the cost significantly higher for the blooded, to the point that those with weak blood would die before being able to use the most potent of these rituals. If a Dragon Blooded's blood is used for this, the cost is a number of lethal equal to (7 - Blooded Merit) per ritual dot.

                                                Salamander's Oil - Despite all of their strengths, Mirska do not actually have any protection against fire - Helios is a sun god, and while his light creates fire, it is not an actual fire god. Alchemists however, say to hell with that, and spill their blood with various chemicals designed as a deterrent of fire, and bath in the resulting oil. As long as an individual is covered in this oil, they are not harmed by fire or heat. If the fire is supernatural in origin, this power grants armor against it equal to the Dragon's Primal Urge.

                                                Blood Vial - This Chemical is actually fairly base. The Dragon spills their blood, flowing with essence, into a crystal vial which holds the magic within it. The Dragon adds spiritual preservatives, and then corks the vial. When the Dragon - or who ever holds the vial - needs the essence, they simply drink it and add the essence contained into their pool. A Blood Vial can hold a number of essence equal to the Dragon's Primal Urge.
