    Would a city / murder spirit be a magath?

    12-28-2015, 11:10 AM
    A friend of mine is about to run his first werewolf game, set in Glasgow. The city is meant to be the murder capital of Europe during the game and so the leader of the city spirits in Glasgow is Nanfarthir'dar (the city that kills), a city / murder spirit who is either rank 5 or 6. So my question is the one from the title, would a spirit of murder and cities count as a magath?

          Probably not. City-fathers as a spirit type are very unusual creatures (not surprisingly once you get to the sort of power, rank and complexity that these spirits have). They frequently include traits that are related to the area that they rule, without it turning them into Magath even when they're strange combinations. Take a look at Shadows over the UK for some examples of just how strange some of these spirits can be. Also as a City spirit, it thematically fits that it would be natural for the spirit to devour anything that has a link to its city, so it should be able to devour murder essence as much as it likes without ever mutating and becoming a Magath.

          That said when it comes to your friend's setting, it's their Chronicle and the idea of a mutated Magath city-father ruling over the spirit courts is an amazingly terrifying concept so if they want to do it... I'd just say go for it. The Spirit world is more art than science and if they say this one spirit worked out as a Magath, then that's perfectly fine.
