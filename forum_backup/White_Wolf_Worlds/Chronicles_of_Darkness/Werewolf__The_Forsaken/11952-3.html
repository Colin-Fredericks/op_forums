                                99) A number of hunter cells banded together and, in a daring (not to mention astonishingly successful) raid, destroyed the entire ruling court of the city's vampires. Naturally, the leeches have descended into a mad, backstabbing power grab. One of the major figures in this struggle approaches your pack and makes an offer: help him eliminate his rivals, and he will use his influence over mortal affairs to gift you ownership of an office building, all to yourselves--an office building that also happens to house a powerful locus. He'll even help secure the area around it, once you've added it to your territory.

                                It sounds too good to pass up--almost to good to be true. Wait, just how does this leech know so much about werewolves and what they would want? If you pass up the offer, he'll take it to another pack, maybe one of your enemies...

                                        101: There's a new power player in the area your pack patrols. Spirits of technology spiked in number for a few days, but now deadlier magaths of technology and other spirits keep appearing. These are too many to pin down on natural spirit life cycles. It's like someone is purposefully creating magath out of high technology and other spirits.

                                        102: The water isn't a natural place for the People to patrol, but stories from the grapevine are picking up. A story about local "hunters" facing down fishmen could've been brushed off as the kill-happy idiots shooting at something they didn't understand, but then you heard about a group of witches that vanished from the beach after researching "something strange". Hunters you can write off easy. Actual witches? That's a different story. Somethings coming out of the seas.

                                        103: Most teenagers, on seeing war-forms, flee in terror and start babbling about sasquatch. Only now a younger pack is running to yours, screaming about a bunch of warriors as young as them using magic to force them away from their territory. They say their attackers have taken up the territory, and that there's been a strange upswing; spirits of hope and love.
