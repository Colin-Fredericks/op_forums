    Wyoming Chronicle Fluff

    01-26-2016, 01:33 AM
    Hey all,

    Just finished writing up this bit of fluff in preparation for a chronicle I'll be starting soon and I thought I'd share with you good folks, It's a rough summary of my previous chronicle which took place within the same setting.



    This Story Is True..



    Wyoming didn't used to be as fucked up as it is today. We still had all the usual problems that the People have to deal with the world over: shartha, spirit ridden, hunters and weirder shit alike; but we didn't have to worry so much about the Pure tribes. Now don’t get me wrong our erstwhile brothers hated our guts and would have gladly seen us all dead. It was a passive level of loathing though, they didn’t hunt us back then and we stayed the hell away from them. It was a tacit understanding of non aggression. And it was too damn good to last.

    So a little while back there is this new pack of young hot blooded forsaken and they call themselves the Grey Wolves. Big pack, I can't remember quite how big, all different tribes and all faces of the moon. They set up shop in Douglas, the biggest little town between Casper and Cheyenne and start laying down roots. Douglas had two other packs who claimed parts of the town and the surrounding area, they weren’t as big but they were seasoned packs who had their shit down. The Grey Wolves had to make do with the scraps of what was left.

    So we have a large group of Urdur roaming around a new territory looking for something to cut their teeth on and make a name for themselves. Now they could have found anything out there to hunt, a nest of Shartha, some troublesome duguthim or just a stubborn ass bison spirit that still thinks it owns the place. Instead they found themselves a Wound, Yep that's right a great big sucking wound slap bang in the middle of their new home.

    A newbie pack should have maybe started looking elsewhere for a territory to lay claim to. Hell, I know plenty of packs who aren't so new at all who've seriously weighed their options. The Grey Wolves had big brass ones. No matter what happened afterwards or what anyone says that can't be taken away from them, they had enormous kahunas.

    The Grey Wolves dedicated themselves to trying to cleanse that wound. Time and time again they risked their lives journeying as far into it as they could to drive off and destroy the corrupted spirits within. After the Grey Wolves decided that the direct approach wasn't working out they started being smart about it. They looked to see how the wound affected the Hisil around it and in turn how the Hisil affected the skin world and vice versa.

    They found out that as they were trying to cleanse the wound from the shadow side someone else was helping it to fester from the physical world. It didn't take them long, all things considered, to track down who was responsible. What they found was something no young pack of Uratha should have to face alone one of the worst of the worst; Asha Gadar a Bale Hound.

    There is no telling how long he had been in Douglas spreading his poison until both worlds began to sicken. A Bale Hound was an enemy far beyond anything the Grey Wolves had seen before but they sacked up and hunted the bastard down. They tracked him into the recesses of that wound, brought him to bay on his own ground and tore his throat out. Luna alone knows how they did it without getting half of their pack slaughtered but they did it. The victory howls carried the news all over Douglas and the cahalunim spread it even further.

    Yep three cheers to those guys, no I'm being entirely sincere. They had just killed one of the Asha Gadar, has your pack? No then shut up. Anyway so after well earned kudos and pats on the back all around the Grey Wolves felt like hot shit, wouldn't you? Sure they still had that wound to take care of but they figured with the Bale Hound outta the way a few more trips inside to clean house would do the trick, idiots.


    With their troubles inside of the territory taken care of (or so they believed) they turned their eyes outwards to expansion. They started challenging their neighbours (losing consistently to one pack and wiping the floor with the other) and exploring the unclaimed lands around. Of course you can only go so far in Wyoming before the land stops being unclaimed and starts being someone else's territory. They found pack markers. Anshega fucking pack markers. The Grey Wolves were drunk on victory, full of piss and vinegar. They desecrated the pack markers, pissed all over the boundary then went back home with their tails held high.

    Fast forwards about a week, our Grey Wolves have calmed down a bit and almost forgotten about the mother of all fuck ups that they had made. That's when the chickens come home to roost. The Pure turn up right on the doorstep; claws out, teeth bared, ready for blood, howling a challenge. They wouldn't have come after a forsaken pack ordinarily but they couldn't ignore an insult like that. It would have made them look too weak, if you think we’re bad for chastising weakness you have no idea what the Pure tribes are like.

    So the Grey Wolves and this pack of pure throwdown just outside of Douglas, same way that any two forsaken packs would have over that sort of thing. It goes badly, there are a lot of different stories going around about who's to blame but no way of knowing for sure, the point is it stopped being a throwdown and turned into a slaughter. The Grey Wolves killed a full half of that pure pack before the rest fled for their lives. There are a good number of us who think that whilst regrettable a foe slain in the heat of the moment in a fair challenge is no shame. What happened next was fucking murder.

    The Grey Wolves paused just long enough to hide the bodies of the pure they’d killed, heal their superficial wounds and then they took off after the defeated pure. They caught up to them before the pure had gotten halfway back to their own territory, then they ended them. There was no resistance, no fight in them left, just one old grey haired woman and two pups not a year out of their first change. Yep that's how you go from being the legends who killed a Bale Hound to a disgrace to the oath.

    The Grey Wolves went back to Douglas with the blood of the people still wet on their claws. It didn’t take long for karma to come and kick their asses this time, karma taking the form of one pissed off totem spirit. The totem of the Grey Wolves was, i’ve heard from a reputable source, a wolf spirit. Nobody knows what ban the spirit placed upon its pack but whatever it was the Grey Wolves but they broke it with that little killing spree. Their totem ripped the pack a new one then left them. Thats hard man, i'm not saying they didn't deserve it after what they did an’ all but for a pack to lose its totem that's a heavy loss. The Grey Wolves didn’t survive it, they argued, they blamed each other and they broke apart. The remnants formed two new packs, split the old territory in half and tried to start over fresh.

    Meanwhile all over the state word had began to spread of the massacre of that pack of pure. From Cheyenne to Rock Springs, from the Devil’s Tower to Yellowstone the news traveled until it found its way into the ears of particularly nasty bastard. The particular kin-slaying and traitorous bastard in question being the Izidakh called Burning Truth.

    You don’t recognise the name? I can tell you’re not one of those Cold Bastards. I’ll tell you something if you ever really wanna get under the skin of a Storm Lord, and I mean any Storm Lord in the state; you walk up to them all nice and you mention that name. Now they’ll all try and act like they don’t know who you’re on about but they know. you can tell by the narrowing of the eyes and the clenching of their jaws that they know exactly who you’re on about. He used to be one of them you see? Before he tore away the mark of the moon and swore himself to Rabid wolf he was known as Howl of the East Wind and there was no Cahalith of the Iminir more glorious.

    Storm Lords don’t talk about Howl of the East Wind or why he defected. They’re all far too proud to admit that one of their rare elite would throw it all away, especially one so renowned as he was. If you ask the other tribes however and they all have stories of Burning Truth. He rose quickly amongst the Anshega until he was an alpha of his own pack then made it his mission to convert any Urdaga he could and butcher those he couldn't. Burning Truth hated the unspoken truce that kept pure and forsaken from each other's throats. When he heard of what the Grey Wolves had done he saw the chance to end it.

    Burning Truth he tracked down the spirits that had witnessed the massacre and bound them to the bones of the dead Anshega as fetishes that recreated the horrific scene upon command. Burning Truth traveled all over Wyoming and whipped up the fury of the Pure with those and his rhetoric of hate until they were all baying for blood. They hit first in Casper, with the Ivory Claws almost running the Iron Masters outta town with the shock and ferocity of the attack, but soon it was happening everywhere. I um, I lost three packmates back when the hunts first started, it was bad.

    If Burning Truth had gotten his way it would have been a lot worse.


    That Izidakh fucker didn't just want to see us and the Anshega hunting each other, he wanted us at war. You pups have never seen what war looks like for the people, neither have I truth be told but i've heard what it's like from some old timers who got to see war between the Uratha first hand. Yeah the Blood Talons like to harp on about their All War macho bullshit but the real thing means hell on both worlds for our kind. Luckily for us bringing it about is easier said than done.

    We Uratha are a wilful bunch, and joining any number of us to together means we need something to help bind us. So just like a pack, lodge or tribe a war needs a spiritual patron; a totem. Burning Truth had almost everything he needed except a totem powerful enough to bind all the Pure of Wyoming together for his war to begin. He set his pack to gathering what would be needed for a rite to summon such a spirit.

    The activity of the Anshega had obviously caused quite the stir and some of us Urdaga had started looking into what the hell to do about it. Enter Ira Doomkeeper, an elder Bone Shadow who likely taught every ritemaster you’ll ever meet everything they knew. Doomkeeper had a crazy amount of pull in the shadow, something to do with a lifetime of keeping strictly to the oath and fulfilling his tribal ban with a certain amount of vindictive vengefulness. He knew what the Izidakh were gonna attempt almost before they did.

    This is another one of those parts where there is no way to say for certain exactly what happened. We know what happened leading up to this part and what happened afterwards but there aren't many friendly living witnesses to this bit in the middle, so what i'm gonna do is tell you the coolest version i've heard and hope it's not too full of bullshit.

    So picture this: you’ve got Burning Truth and his entire pack working on this rite to summon up their prospective war totem. There’s a massive bonfire burning, heaps of smouldering gathra, ritualised combat, a chorus of howls as the wolf-blooded join in with specially crafted drums. Burning Truth is leading the invocation as the ritemaster finishes applying the correct sigils on the circle. The rite is completed as this Dihir begins to take form before them all.

    Burning Truth is halfway through his formal greeting to the spirit when the ritemaster produces some old ass wooden box outta nowhere and binds the Pure tribe’s would be totem into a fetish right in front of their faces. Of course it isn't the pure ritemaster but Doomkeeper, he used a gift to steal the ritemaster’s skin and impersonate him for the rite. The Anshega are all set to make an example of him when a whole host of Doomkeeper’s spirit allies jump the lot of them and in the middle of it all he slips away clutching his trophy.

    Sounds cool huh? too bad it’s like all speculation. Where we’re going next solid ground, fact checked and everything, Doomkeeper arrives at Douglas torn half to shreds. Why Douglas? well it was close, he knew the packs there respected him and would take him in and he knew that the Pure would be coming to recover the spirit within that fetish and he’d need help to keep it out of the hands of Burning Truth, people were gonna die over it. The Grey Wolves had been the ones to start it, to Doomkeeper it seemed fitting what was left of them should help finish it.

    The packs of Douglas take him in and Doomkeeper tells them all what the score is. To their credit they don't even bat an eyelid when he tells them they should die before the pure are allowed to get their hands on this box. They don't start panicing or gnashing their teeth they just get right down to work trying to turn that small town into a death trap for the Anshega. I told you man massive kahunas. By the end of the week Doomkeeper is almost back a one hundred percent and the four packs are just waiting for the hammer to fall. It’s the first night of the waxing gibbous moon when the pure arrive.

    They encircled Douglas dozens of them, and they howled out to the Urdaga; we’re here, no way out, we’re waiting for you. They shut down all the loci and stalked both sides of the gauntlet, a cage of teeth and claws. One of the packs who used to belong to the Grey Wolves tried to face them out there in the wild, half of them didn't make it back. After that they started to tighten the noose. Attacks night and day, they drew blood then slipped away, one by one the forsaken of Douglas started to fall. Burning Truth offered them the ultimatum, if they handed over the fetish and Doomkeeper, the rest of them could limp away and live to die another day. If not he’d slaughter them before the end of the full moon.

    I guess at some point they realized that they could stand there defiantly and fight until their last breaths; They could take as many of them out as they were able, it would be glorious as all hell but it would ultimately futile. The Anshega would take their losses, release the spirit from its prison, bind themselves to it and begin a mass slaughter of the people. The packs of Douglas couldn't win by holding their ground and fighting, that road led only to their deaths and the deaths of other Uratha all over the state. So they chose the only other avenue, storm the lines of the pure and break the hell outta there.

    When the full moon was at its highest and brightest they went all out, balls to the walls, charge of the light brigade style. They attacked in all different directions with all the ferocity they could muster. Almost all of them died at the blockade around the town, either at the hands of the Pure or the legion of spirits they had enlisted. A few of them, a scant handful managed to punch through and made it out the otherside. One of them, the last surviving member of the Grey Wolves, holding the sealed Dihir.

    The outrage of the Pure when they realized that their prize had slipped their grasp was exceptional. Burning Truth’s ambition of setting Wyoming ablaze were dead. If those poor bastards hadn't already died fighting the Anshega, they would have wished they had.

    They did unspeakable things to the bodies of those dead Uratha.

    The Hisil trembled with the after effects, then it split open.

    It went back to the wound. It was still there and what none of the packs of Douglas had ever found was the locus at its center. The Bale Hound must have known, he must have had something terrible planned for it. The spirits of the wound had been routinely cleared from the area by first the Grey Wolves and then the packs they split into. All that time weeks and months the locus had been generating its poisonous essence; with nothing feeding from it the wound grew. When the pure preformed their horrific acts of spite and hatred they laid down the straw that broke the camel's back. The locus bloomed with power and something terrible, not from the flesh or the shadow but from somewhere else, reached through and exerted its sway on the town.

    The Anshega ran the fuck outta there. Burning Truth’s horde splintered back into their packs and went their separate ways. Douglas was lost to the people and the Urdaga who once called it home are dead or scattered. Ira Doomkeeper hasn’t been heard from since and is presumed dead, though unusually nobody has tried claiming renown by being the one to slain him. The fragile truce that once kept Pure and Forsaken from each other's throats is gone.

    But hey at least we’re not at war right?

    So yeah I guess things are a bit shittier that they used to be, but then we had it a bit sweeter back then than most places. It’s still Wyoming though, that hasn't changed and the blood of the wolf runs strong. There is good territory here to claim and protect. On good nights, which is most, Mother Moon smiles down and watches us with pride. Best is the prey; strong prey to test us in the hunt and the wolf must hunt.

    Song of 0’s and 1’s, Iron Master Cahalith
