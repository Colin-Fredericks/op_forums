        Wrt Demon. I see two ways to go...

        Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 3, Resolve 2, Strength 2, Dexterity 3, Stamina 1, Presence 2, Manipulation 3, Composure 3
        Skills: Medicine [Just Bruised] 5, Science 1, Occult 1, Brawl [Grapple] 5, Athletics [Spitting] 5, Intimidation [Glory and Terror] 5
        Merits: Terrible Form 4, Advanced Form 1, Professional Training [Thug, add Athletics as an Asset Skill] 5
        Embeds: Just Bruised, Victory at Any Price, Shift Consequences, Strike First
        Exploits:
        Modifications: Sonic Acuity, Inhuman Strength, Vicegrip
        Technologies: Mantle of Fire, Glory and Terror, Acid Spit
        Propulsion: Plasma Drive, Urban Fluidity
        Processes: Insect Swarm, Wound Healing, Extra Mechanical Limbs

        Traits: Speed 10, Initiative +6, Health Levels 6, Defense 8 (+2 vs Single Target, +3 if two hands free)
        Brawl Attack: +0L (+0A in Grapple), 10 Dice, 9-Again
        Spit Acid Attack: +0A, 9 Dice, 9-Again
        Grapple Checks: 18 Dice, 9-Again

        OR

        Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 3, Resolve 2, Strength 1, Dexterity 3, Stamina 2, Presence 2, Manipulation 3, Composure 3
        Skills: Medicine [Just Bruised] 5, Science 1, Ocult 1, Firearms [Rivet Gun] 5, Athletics 5, Intimidation [Glory and Terror] 4
        Merits: Terrible Form 4, Advanced Form 1, Whatever You Want 5
        Embeds: Just Bruised, Victory at Any Price, Merciless Gunman
        Exploits: Hellfire
        Modifications: Sonic Acuity, Inhuman Reflexes, Fast Combat, Rivet Arms
        Technologies: Blind Sense, Glory and Terror
        Propulsion: Plasma Drive, Urban Fluidity
        Processes: Insect Swarm, Wound Healing, Magnesium Flare

        Traits: Speed 10, Initiative +8, Health Levels 7, Defense 8 (+2 vs Single Target)
        Rivet Gun: +3L (+3A with Hellfire), 11 Dice

        With Build one, you go first and trigger Glory and Terror, rendering a non-zero number of Werewolves Insensate and thus unable to contribute to combat. Your goal is to isolate a target. Then you grapple them and murder them with you 18 dice grapple pool and aggravated bite and Mantle of Fire. Victory at Any Cost ensures that your grapple /will/ land. Insect Swarm, Wound Healing and Urban Fluidity are your oh shit buttons.

        Build two is similar. Trigger Insensate vs as many people as possible to isolate a target. Trigger Hellfire and Merciless Gunman on your weapon as you are able. Use Called Shot [Head] and use Victory at Any Price to make sure you land the hit, stunlocking the target. Like the other build, Insect Swarm, Wound Healing and Urban Fluidity are your oh shit buttons.

                                Originally posted by Xenorath View Post
                                One thing to keep in mind if your a pack of werewolves going after a mage is you can now have mortals in your pack. If you manage to learn about Paradox and have some mortals in the know you can just bring them along with you to make casting spells more difficult.
                                Don't forget that regular mortals, unlike wolf-blooded packmates, are not immune or resistant to Lunacy (or an werewolf in Kuruth). As explicitly stated in the new corebook, human pack members do not generally accompany their werewolf packmates on a hunt. Using such human packmates as paradox magnets would essentially be treating them as little more than expendable cannon fodder, besides the other dangers of excessive paradox effects that could be just as dangerous to werewolves as to the mages, particularly in light of some of the new material and paradox rules that will be included in Mage 2e posted by Dave B.

                                Although white room battles scenarios are fun and amusing, I tend to more often look at relations between major templates as a more cooperative effort. Imagine a group of werewolves who have befriended or allied themselves with local mages (or other powerful supernaturals), and the overwhelming mutual benefit for both groups. If such a group of werewolves were then engage in combat with other more belligerent mages, the best strategy would be to bring you own mages to such an encounter. Even one mage who can proficiently counterspell or identify opposing mage targets would be of incalculable value. A smart Alpha uses all resources at his disposal. A few dots in Allies or Contacts is often worth far more than high power stats, combat merits and characters designed for little else besides violence..
