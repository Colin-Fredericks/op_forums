    01-09-2018, 09:32 AM
    Show your pack Totems - that you created yourself or with your players. At least write Totem concept, Bane and Ban - but full stats will be better. Also good place for hammering your new Totems. Here are mine! ( I will add stats of them later. )

    Eagle of Justice - Eagle spirit from the place of Hólmganga - honorable and justice duels in Viking Era Normans - that now serves for the pack that looks for justice.
    Ban: Do not let injustice prevail
    Bane: ???

    Crow of Ash and Blood - Crow spirit of Viking raids - connected with Death and Bloodshed. He is one of Ember Carrion spirits, ( See Dark Eras, p. 161 )
    Ban: ???
    Bane: ???

    ‘Munin’ / Crow of Memory - Spirit of lore and knowledge, protecting sacred Temple of Uppsala and it's treasures. He passes often as sacred crow send by Odin, from Norse myths.

    Rank: 3 - Ensih
    Power: 6, Finesse: 8 , Resistance: 8
    Corpus: 11
    Willpower: 10
    Essence: 20
    Initiative:
    Defense:
    Speed: 21
    Size: 3
    Influences: Crows 1, Knowledge 2
    Aspiration: Protect the Temple in Uppsala or add more knowledge to the hoard in Temple
    Manifestation: Twilight Form, Discorporate, Shadow Gateway, Image, Fetter.
    Numina: Implant Mission, Innocuous, Seek, Sign.
    ​

    Ban: Can’t refuse a knowledge or memory
    Bane: A flagstaff from burned Raven Banner


    Notice: As you can see, I would appreciate some Ban & Bane help on ours.
