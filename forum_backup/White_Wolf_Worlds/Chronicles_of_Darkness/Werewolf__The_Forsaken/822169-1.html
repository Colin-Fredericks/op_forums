                            The new werewolf characters still get's Tribal Renown. I would imply that with new Prey focus of the Tribes - each new member need to Hunt on it's Sacred Prey and in the process, get the Renown.

                            That means that Intiation Rites for Tribes are:
                            • Blood Talons - Hunting other Uratha and getting Glory at the end.
                            • Bone Shadows - Hunting ephemeral being and getting Wisdom at the end.
                            • Hunters in Darkness - Hunting Host and getting Purity at the end.
                            • Iron Masters - Hunting human ( or even group of them ) inside mortal population and getting Cunning at the end.
                            • Storm Lords - Hunting the Urged and getting Honor at the end.
