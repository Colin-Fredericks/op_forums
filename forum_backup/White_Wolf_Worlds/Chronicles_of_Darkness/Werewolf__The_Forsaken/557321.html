                                                  There's a lot of weirdness in the Hisil that the books don't really cover, or even really get into much. The virus spirits are an interesting example, even other spirits don't know where they belong, and consider them aberrations. Just like in real life, we don't even know if they're alive or not.

                                                  Ephemera seems to be just a catch-all term for, 'I don't know what it is, but it isn't made of matter.'

                                                  But yeah, you're either standing on Gaea, or a layer of dormant spirits of dirt and soil and earth on top of another layer of dormant spirits, or as someone pointed out above, a location spirit. It's probably worth noting that higher-ranked spirits tend to have their own secret realm in the Hisil, a place-that-isn't, as opposed to just being in the locale of the Hisil's version of their source. This is how you can have powerful spirits of very common things, like Destruction, that aren't in all places where destruction is occurring. Instead, it has its own lair someplace and its court and members of its court have their own hierarchy and lesser spirits all the way down. Though, it seems, in the current world one of the major spirits of Destruction is in Basra. So while you might be standing on those lesser spirits, you're not necessarily standing on Gaea.

                                                          While like everything, there should be different options available, the question is something worth asking.

                                                          A werewolf or human who is respectful of spirits might want to know if they're walking on spirits as they travel the Hisil. The answer to the question helps the Storyteller maintain the integrity of their setting.
