            Originally posted by Claire Redfield View Post
            A later section also refers to werewolves as "Native Predators" of Pangaea. This also makes sense: as half-flesh, half-spirit creatures, a realm that blends both seems perfect for werewolves. Distinct from their progenitor god, different from the Hosts, werewolves seem better adapted for the duality. Perhaps their grounding in mortal flesh gives them that edge, or allowed them to adapt to a changing world. And these make for interesting theories and possibilities. What makes werewolves different? Why did they survive?
            After reading this, I came up with a possible answer to this question: I'm wondering if we can toss in an anthropological element to help explain why werewolves are distinct among Pangaeans, and to help keep werewolves at least tangentially close to humanity. I guess the way it would work in my head is that in some point in prehistory, Wolf noticed either a tribe of early humans who had domesticated wolves to assist them on the hunt. Wolf, maybe impressed with these humans working with his lesser kin, or maybe following the advice of her lover Moon who saw the potential these humans could add to Wolf's pack, worked together to basically adopt, modify, and guide the evolution of these humans into what we know as Werewolves.

            The initial humans probably loved this idea, since when THE Predator-God asks you to be his child, you say YES. I guess this could mean that the Wolf-Blooded actually came first, and the firstborn Werewolves were the first to go through their first change and thus fully embodied the three parts of themselves: the intelligence of Human, the theme and role of Wolf, and the renown of Moon. After that change, they transition from weird followers to the native hunters of Pangaea.

            But yea, this is why I think Werewolves are distinct from the other hosts, including the Adarusharu Wolf-Hosts: conceptually (which is what actually matters to spirits and Pangaeans) Werewolves are indeed the children of Wolf and Moon. In an evolutionary sense though, they're actually an offshoot of humanity and it's our species that now carries this legacy in that we provide the core symbol-creature that makes werewolves work.

            In any case, I also want to express my thanks to all the writers involved for making such a kick-ass section! Thank you so much!
