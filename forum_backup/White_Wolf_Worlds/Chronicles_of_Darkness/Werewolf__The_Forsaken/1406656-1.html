                                08-09-2020, 04:48 PM
                                Originally posted by DubiousRuffian View Post
                                So I've finished the book. My biggest criticism is that the Cult rules weren't entirely included. We're y'all unable to include them due to wc restrictions, or because it made use of a kind of proprietary system?
                                Long story short, transferring the full Krewe System over from Geist would have meant an immense amount of copying and pasting without alteration, and I just wasn't comfortable with that, or, I would think, allowed to do that. I also wasn't inclined to try to rewrite the whole thing when the Geist book already says it just fine (the Krewe system is just really good). If this were an official book, I definitely would've included a complete system, but that's the limit of things like STV. But, I also really wanted to include something like the organization system for cults, so this was the awkward compromise. This is why it's only about three pages; I didn't want it to take up a huge amount of space for something you need the Geist core to run.

                                As for the Inconnu, I'm not entirely satisfied with the way the final version comes off, so I have a little errata to clarify things/adjust when I update the file for the final version.

                                                            Originally posted by Alpharn View Post
                                                            The Mnemosyne preview example seems to be lacking Favored Attributes? Is that intended?
                                                            Long story short, Favored Attributes for bloodlines got cut from the final version of Spilled Blood.
