                        Some relevant excerpts:
                        The hierarchical nature of ephemeral beings also plays a part — Rank isn't a social convention for them but a funda-mental part of their nature. Ephemeral entities of 2 Ranks or more higher than an opponent of the same type (a Rank 5 spirit attacking a Rank 3 spirit, for example) count as their opponent's bane when using unarmed attacks, claws, or teeth.
                        God-Machine Chronicle page 222
                        and
                        Some supernatural creatures that are closely related to a form of ephemeral being have “honorary” Rank in the appropriate otherworldly hierarchy; Sin-Eaters are all Bound to a ghost, for example, and werewolves are treated with respect by spirits according to their Renown.

                        Technicalities count, in this case, but only against the ephemeral entity. A werewolf who “outranks” a minor spirit will deal devastating wounds to it with his claws, but a high-Rank spirit can't burn that werewolf by touching him. There are other ways to assert dominance; high-Rank entities are quite capable of showing the half-fleshed who's boss.
                        “Honorary Rank”, God-Machine Chronicle page 221
                        Because werewolves with at least 8 total Renown start with an Honorary Rank of 3, they automatically count as a Bane to Rank 1 spirits, in addition to whatever the spirit’s actual Bane is. When they reach Honorary Rank 4 (16 Renown), they automatically count as a Bane to Rank 2 spirits. Werewolves with Honorary Rank 5 (24 Renown) count as banes for Rank 3 spirits. Per the new Ephemeral Beings rules, werewolves interact with sufficiently low-ranking spirits as if they were solid; and such spirits are harmed just by being touched by the werewolf. This alone goes a long way to helping werewolves deal with spirits; and it incentivizes werewolves to invest in Renown.

                        Personally, I’d like to see Idigam Chronicle accelerate the relationship between total Renown and Honorary Rank to something like, say, a base Honorary Rank of 2, plus one for every five full dots of Renown. That would let endgame werewolves get up to Honorary Rank 7, just shy of Mother Luna, and would allow such werewolves to tear through even Rank 5 spirits without any special preparations.

                        Or maybe Honorary Rank should vary by the type of Renown (e.g., 2 + Honor or 2 + Wisdom), and every spirit should resonate with one type of Renown: so when confronting a Glory spirit that’s Rank 3, an Uratha with Glory ••• or more would be able to tear into it. Short form: if your Renown that resonates with the spirit in question equals or exceeds its Rank, you count as a Bane for it. Since every werewolf has a few points of Renown to start, there would always be some kind of spirit that’s vulnerable to it.

                                                    "Is forsaken the game I want it to be?" is an interesting question to me. I didnt play apocolypse and media/stories of being a werewolf are few and far between. That said I had no expectations of what a game would be from a werewolves perspective beyond "you're a werewolf." When I began reading the core book the mythology made sense to me. An ancient paradise ruled over by gods and constantly under siege by otherworldy entities was interesting but what was more interesting was it had that theme of "nothing lasts forever".

                                                    As more books have been released Ive collected most of them and been decently happy. Forsaken has some great writing, good ideas, and plenty of room for the Storyteller to adapt the world to their universe. I can see why some people dont like the original mythology surrounding Forsaken society but blasphemies basicly gave free reign to write new creation myths. I found that thoughtful and interesting. Yes more attention is payed toward Father wolf and pangea but I think thats more likely because its the creation myth thats believed the most. I look at it like living in Iran and expecting Christian or Hindu philosophies to get the same treatment as Islamic philosophies. It seems unreasonable

                                                    Territories was IMO the most needed book. The territory is supposed to be a living, breathing thing and that book made it so. Territory became a character in itself that the forsaken desperately wanted to take care of similar to the Spirit binding most packs together.

                                                    Lore of the forsaken and Predators expanded on spirits, spirit life, courts, and the shadow itself.

                                                    Blood of the wolf expanded on what it was to be a werewolf and wolfblooded.

                                                    The Rage (which contains the only representation of the blood talons I like), tribes of the moon, and Blood of the wolf all expanded on tribal and auspice affiliation.

                                                    Everything that I think needed expansion and more explanation has been taken care of and theres even been more expansion on things that I would have never thought of (Things that should not have been for example). This brings me back to the original question. "Is forsaken the game I want it to be?". Yes, for the most part. Coming to the core book with the simplest expectations really helped me to get into the story that is the werewolf venue. The various books have addressed my questions and even encouraged a huge amount of creative freedom which IMO shows a very mature line thats ready to give the reigns to the people playing it while simultaneously giving enough narrative to tell its own story.

                                                    Now is there room to improve? Sure. I would like to see more info on the bale hounds. I would like to see alternative and yet more horrific ways to earn gifts besides sucking up to or forcing spirits to teach them. I would like to see more lodges. I would like to see rules for making werewolf safe houses (similar to mages Sanctum and Sigil book). Are those things needed? Probably not, it would just be nice to have those additions.
