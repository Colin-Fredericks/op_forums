                                Well, I already tackled me being a vampire, let's see what I would be like as a werewolf.

                                Auspice: I've always been the strange outsider type with a close-knit friend group, so it would be Ithaeur with a leaning towards Honor for me. In fact, I'm so used to being on the periphery I didn't notice I was popular at my high school (which was an artsy school full of misfits and geeks) until the big end-of-year graduation party. A friend of mine later mentioned that I was probably the most popular guy in the grade, to which my response was a dumbfounded "Why didn't you tell me?! I could have used the confidence boost!" Either way, personally I know... way too much about obscure and esoteric subjects for most people's comfort. Like to "why on earth do you even know that" levels. I also have a near-eidetic memory, so all that occult information is locked up in my head. I actually took a class on the Anthropology of Magic where one of the assignments was to design a myth and paired ritual from scratch and that came pretty easily to me, so rites would be easy enough to grasp.

                                Tribe: If I joined a Tribe, it would probably be the Bone Shadows or the Hunters in Darkness. Bone Shadows is the obvious choice, doubling down on my fascination with the esoteric and occult, as well as a means of handling the crazy, animistic world I am now expected to be apex predator of. However, personality-wise, talent-wise, reflex-wise, I would probably make a good Hunter in Darkness. See, I have the uncanny ability to just... memorize an entire location in my head. Like a map in three dimensions. I recently went on a vacation to Quebec City, and even though it's been two years since I've been there I still remembered, down to the street and the shortcuts to get there, where everything I wanted to find was. It's actually rather spooky, but it meant I was the family's living GPS for the trip. Knowing and keeping my territory wouldn't even be a chore, it would become reflex. Find the raised nails and hammer them down.

                                On the Hunt: A locally-minded werewolf, I'd keep to my territory. There's a decent stretch of forest called the Spirit Woods right next to where I live now, so if I had the opportunity to stay exactly where I am that's where I'd lair. There's a surprisingly dense deer population so if I needed to kill something that wouldn't be a problem. Human-me has a problem with lasting physical harm, werewolf-me wouldn't. The only problem with this place is that there's a pretty dense human population, so there's a very high chance I'd kill a person eventually, and that means trouble. I'd have to move at some point. My pack is relatively small, a tight-knit circle of friends. I tend to the Shadow, I tend to the pack, and for the most part I probably wouldn't care all that much about other things. Stuff outside my area of interest, stuff outside the sphere of influence of my packmates, is not my problem. You cross a packmate of mine though? Probably a fatal mistake, or at the least a very painful one. Spirits coming out of the woodwork to harass, haunt, and possess. The natural world turning against you. It might not be feasible to go after a transgressor now, but I am patient, oh, am I ever patient, and I don't forget things. Ever.

                                Alpha Predator: If I manage to survive to become an elderly werewolf, then I would probably hole myself up in the centre of my territory. I'm at the centre of a web of relationships with friends, packmates, their relatives, and the local Shadow. I travel, but never far, and never for long. There are no mates in my life, no children. I get all the emotional intimacy I need from my close friends. I'm a wolf grown fat and sleek in their den, wisely leading my pack in hunt after hunt, or at least advising from off to the side. Leadership positions just aren't my thing, except in the case of a teacher. That is what I am, that's my calling, it's what I'm passionate about. I'm an educator, first and foremost, so I'm the guy who fosters young Hunters in Darkness in order to show them the ropes. But just because I seem fairly sedate and unexciting doesn't mean that I'm not still an Uratha. You cross me, and I can forgive it, but you cross my pack? Cross my friends? You will need to made an example. By this point I'm Dihar of my own Spirit court, master of the natural world, Flesh and Shadow, capable of bending the elements to my will. My howl forces your spiritual allies to abandon you, my Gifts mean you will not see the devastation I will wreak until it is upon you, in my own territory, even the Dihim cannot stand against me. I cultivate Loci like gardens, I shape Resonance with little more than will and occult know-how, I have an armoury of fetishes at my disposal, and when I walk over the shattered ephemera of your spiritual allies I'll have a few more trinkets to loan out to the pups.
