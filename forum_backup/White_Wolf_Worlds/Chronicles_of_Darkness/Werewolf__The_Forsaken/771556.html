    Fenris-Ur Goals in Basra, Iraq

    11-22-2015, 12:53 PM
    Heads up for my players if you guys read these forums, reading this thread may ruin my game for you if I go through with a hunt that leads to Basra!




    Now that that is out of the way. I was reading the write up for Basra, Iraq​ and I throughout the whole thing I was under the impression that Fenris-Ur's purpose in Iraq is to awaken Dana-Ur and fill the part of itself that is missing. But since Fenris-Ur doesn't know what is missing (Dana-Ur) it is acting on instinct, and probably doesn't even actually have the plans for the Ghost Wolves, Fenris-Ur just knows it needs them.

    Now assuming that Dana-Ur is also a first born, that makes it a known First Born without a tribe, and there is a convenient population of Ghost Wolves in Basra that can form a Tribe, if they locate Dana-Ur.

    This got me thinking if the Ghost Wolves find Dana-Ur what would Creator Wolf's "trial" be? and what would the resulting tribe be like? So far all my only ideas is to make the preferred prey either Maeljin, or the Shadow itself. In particular things like Shoal's (unless someone can argue why they are a good), Wounds, Barrens, or other such "corruptions" in the shadow, and maybe even corruptions of the "flesh" (like heavily polluted region).

    I have no ideas what their culture, oath, or anything else would be like. So I was hoping we could get some ideas together! (Maybe even come up with a better idea for Preferred Prey. Since Preferred Prey don't have to be things, the Pure write ups in the Core gives us conceptual things like: those that dishonour or disrespect the shadow, the "tainted", and those that refuse to honor the hunt.)

    ​What we have got so far:

    Favored Prey: ​"Those who've lingered to go long", "that which has lingered too long", or "Those who are obsolete"
    Renown: Purity or Cunning
    Gifts: ​Inspiration, Warding, or Shaping

                      Also I just Ctrl+F'ed Creator Wolf and found this passage in the Blood Talons write up

                      Originally posted by Werewolf the Forsaken - Second Edition, page 34
                      ​This story is true. In the time before, when Father Wolf led his children in the hunt, Fenris-Ur had a twin Danu-Ur, she was called, the Creator Wolf. She alone could calm her brother's rages and convince him to quit the field. He alone could drive off the chattering spirits that swarmed around her and threatened to drive her mad. They were in balance then, but the Father's death tore them asunder. Now the Destroyer rages without end, and the Creator gibbers rapidly and spreads her madness like a plague.


                      So those "Construction projects" and Chimeric Creatures could be constructs from Creator Wolf, or the Spirits that are driving her mad. So at least we have the challenge for the Ghost Wolves. To drive those spirits off, and bring peace to her "Place that is not".
