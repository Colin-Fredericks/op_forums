    Okay, so if I take Living Weapon and maxed out it for Gauru claws they will have bypass 2 Armor of Colossus being, they will do +3 Damage - so it's will 'bypass' Armor 5 on hit, is it? Or will Living Weapon ***** let you automaticly bypass even Armor 10+ in Colossus beings?
    If you're talking about 10+ Resistance, then we are talking about Rank 4 / 5 monsters. The game is designed to make you go hunt down banes and bans for that powerful of a spirit.

    Lesser spirits? Well, you can try to outrank the spirit and use your claws as a bane, or just overpower the rather reasonable amount of armor. Or, don't forget Purity Rage gift. It think that's the one that gives extra damage equal to your Purity. You can easily get upwards of 6+ damage and lots of armor piercing. Or have your pack grapple the spirit, then use Killing Blow to end the battle with lots of auto-successes. Don't forget your local Storm Lord / Cahalith to also bump up your power a bit.

    There are seriously a ton of ways to boost damage, armor piercing, and more. And that's just for a stereotypical Rahu. Other auspieces will be using other tactics.

                                Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                                Okay, so to compile most of ways Uratha can put down high Armor enemies:
                                1. Outrank lesser beings, so your claws become Bane - you need only 8+ Renown dots for Rank 1 beings, 13+ Renown dots for Rank 2, and 19+ Renown dots for Rank 3.
                                There's a gift for raising your effective Rank by 2 while on your Territory. So, with this Gift, you easily can handle rank 1-2 beings at char gen, and rank 3 beings after a bit of time playing. There may be other ways to raise your effective rank, but I can't recall others.

                                The only drawback here is that you have to be on your territory for it to count, but that perfectly fits with the themes of Werewolf in the first place, so that's kinda the point.

                                2. Living Weapon Merit, which can give you Armor Piercing 2 - and in Gauru +3 Damage from claws.
                                3. Gift's of Shaping Glory Facet gives Armor Piercing equal to Glory.
                                4. Gift's of Rage Purity Facet gives extra Damage equal to Purity.
                                5. Possibly Killing Blow attack on spirits.
                                6. Cahalith or Storm Lords boosting powers.
                                And don't forget Gift of Strength Purity facet. In general, a Blood Talon Rahu with decent levels of Glory and Purity is going to have easy access to a number of abilities that very significantly increase their damage. Favored Form and some other werewolf fighting merits, I'm sure, could help too. There are probably other things out there, but I can't think of any off the top of my head.
