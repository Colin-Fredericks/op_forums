                                                        Re: the confusion about tribes and why starting level characters would join one of them - when considering the tribes, it is important to take into account the basic situation of a newly changed werewolf and of the setting at a larger scale.


                                                        You've changed and you've probably killed someone. You already know what it's like to be hunted - strange shadowy beings stalking you, trying to kill you. Spirits and rats infesting your home and trying to get close to the people you care about. You've probably already fought for your life once or twice - and won, obviously, but it doesn't take much smarts to figure out that the next time could well be different.


                                                        Your skin doesn't act the way it used to, and neither do your emotions, senses and appetites, even. You can't help looking at other people and, sometimes, thinking "food". You feel claustrophobic in your human skin, and when previously you felt like hitting somebody who pissed you off, now it's a challenge not to tear his throat out.

                                                        This is where the tribes come in. This is what the tribes have to offer:


                                                        As a werewolf, you'll have to fight for your life; probably on a regular basis. The Blood Talons show you how. They know how to protect yourself and those close to you; Fenris-Ur will teach you how to be an effective warrior. They also give you ways how to deal with the burning rage affecting all werewolves. The Blood Talons are the ones you approach when you don't want to wake up in the blood of your husband one day, and when you want to survive the next time the Predator Kings show up from nowhere and start biting huge chunks of flesh out of your flesh.


                                                        This scary, hostile world of predator and prey you are now a part of? The Bone Shadows best understand how it works and how to make sense of it. They explain spirits. They tell you which places in the Shadow to avoid and where you'll be safe. Death Wolf provides you with the means of fighting your enemies with ways other than using your claws. And they also show you how to make sense of the death and pain and grief that always seems to follow the Forsaken around. They provide meaning in a world that doesn't appear to have any, at the first glance.


                                                        There's a wolf in your heart, your bones and your flesh, and it'll never go away. The Hunters in Darkness know that wolf better than any other tribe. They know how to make sense of the rage, the new senses, instincts, demands of the wolf-heart. They also teach you how to hunt effectively, and how to survive when you're prey. Things like where to best hide, how to best track your prey, how to kill prey in as little time as possible are natural to the children of the Black Wolf.


                                                        Werewolves are not human. The human skin doesn't change their basic nature; but despite the wolf-heart and spirit-soul there's still a human mind. After the traumatic experience of the First Change, which more often than not, ends with lots of blood, the Iron Masters are the ones who help a young werewolf to reintegrate into the human culture and world and to reconcile wolf-heart with human-mind. They're the ones who know how a wolf hides and hunts among sheep. Red Wolf knows the spirits that hunt in the cities and close to humanity, and his children will teach you how to hunt with cunning, how to adapt to situations that cannot otherwise be dealt with.


                                                        The Storm Lords don't pick you up when you're weak. They won't comfort you in your time of need. Winter Wolf does not care about your weakness and failings. The Storm Lords cannot soothe any pain you feel from the First Change or from being what you are. The Winter Wolf will instead be here in your moments of strength and endurance. His children show you how to be your best, strongest self - how to stare this cold, dark, hostile world that wants you dead at every turn, in the face, and how to make it back down with an alpha's growl. They know how to fight. They know the spirit world, the worlds of wolf and men. All of these things are part of their responsibility.


                                                        ... still three tribes left to cover!


                                                        You've been beaten and bruised by this world. There's nothing that feels right. Friends? Jobs? Relationships? Family? Nothing has ever worked out for you. The world of men has let you down and failed you more often than you can count.

                                                        No more. Something's got to give.

                                                        Your old life is gone. You might have killed those you love during your first change. You're a monster and a hunter now. There's no way back.
                                                        It's not your fault. It's THEM. This world isn't how it should be.
                                                        There is hope. You've got the oldest, nastiest, toughest of all Wolf Gods on your side, giving you strength, rage, purpose and a mission - rebuild this world in your - in his - image. Make them all pay what they did to you. It's what you should do. It's what's natural to do.


                                                        The First Change isn't the end of your old life, it's you reaching your destiny and becoming a true member of your family. It's when you realize your potential and the universe gives you what you're owed.
                                                        The Ivory Claws teach you how people are not born equal. There are those whose blood and ancestry matters more. You're one of the lucky ones. You're special - the purest of the Pure. You're better than other people. It's quite simply genetics.
                                                        This world belongs to its true rulers. The Chosen Ones, those selected by Silver Wolf to run this world, must take command.
                                                        Go hunt those who would refuse to obey.


                                                        Spirits stalk and try to kill you. The Hosts are at your heels, only waiting to take you down once you're weak. Werewolves hunt you as the competition you are.
                                                        It's good, so very good, to have friends in this world. People who look out for you. Spirits who let you live as long as you abase yourself before them instead of attempting, vainly, to keep them in check. The Fire-Touched have more to offer than just protection from the spirits, strength in numbers and sympathetic ears for all your troubles and worries. They also have faith, insight and the certainty that their cause is the right one. And they're always, without exception, honest to you - Rabid Wolf sees lying as one of the worst of sins.
                                                        Only a lunatic would refuse to take their offer and join them.
