                                      Possibilities for Strengthening the Influence of Blood, depending on the particular nature of the spirit in question:
                                      - Increasing the rate of bleeding. A target of Strengthening bleeds out at twice the usual speed.
                                      - Slowing the rate of bleeding by making the blood perform its natural role (clotting etc) faster. A target of Strengthening stops bleeding out immediately.
                                      - Increasing the evidence of blood. Bloodstains become great gory splatters, a trickle of droplets becomes a stream of the stuff, it comes oozing out of containers and vials and blood packs, all sticky and thick.
                                      - Increasing the benefit that certain haemophages gain when drinking a victim's blood.
                                      - Increasing the health of a subject as their blood seems to flow faster, carry more oxygen and nutrients and fill them with life; skin gets ruddier, blood vessels pump with greater pressure. The subject gets a dot of Fast Reflexes and Iron Stamina but it's probably not healthy to be subjected to this for long periods at a time.

                                      - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer

                                                  With Influence Blood 3, this spirit might control bloodflow in unnatural ways, such as causing the failure of a specific organ by temporarily redirecting the blood inside into some sort of fleshy, transparent sav with no function. Taking blood from the brain might cause the Insensate Tilt, while messing around with the kidneys might result in a toxic build up represented by the Poisoned Tilt. This would allow for several other effects, so you could balance it by limiting how many types of Tilts the spirit can inflict to its dots in Influence Blood. Regardless, I'd say that the spirit shouldn't spend these Tilt slots on Arm Wrack, Leg Wrack, Blinded or Stunned, since those Tilts can be inflicted with the spirit's melee attacks (and the Bleeding Tilt might be worsened or stopped with 1 dot in the Influence, as mentioned before). Since ephemeral beings that Materialize don't really have internal organs, I'd say this Influence does not work on them. On the other hand, a snake-like spirit with Influence Plague 3 might be more restricted in what it can do, but it could affect other ephemeral entities because its plagues are more metaphysical than chemical. That way, Influence Blood is not better in every aspect to other Influences that affect the body. The Power + Finesse roll should definitely be contested by Stamina + Supernatural Tolerance, with dots in the Hardy and Iron Stamina Merits applying as bonus dice if you want. The biology here doesn't have to be 100% realistic, of course.
