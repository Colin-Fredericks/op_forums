    Void Spirits

    08-01-2025, 07:49 PM
    Doing a short chronicle (first werewolf, not first in chronicles though) as a pre-amble to a bigger one later.

    It's going to be about the Gauntlet falling in Australia after a Void Spirit crashes down there. A cult of Beshilu happen upon it, and form a bizarre fusion. They change rapidly and spread throughout the continent quickly, and by the time any Forsaken realize the problem it's too late, the Gauntlet falls.

    I'm hoping for more inspiration for Void spirits, and how they might mutate and change a Shartha, I've read the bit in Shunned by the Moon, but was wanting for more examples of anyone has any.

              Something that's probably worth thinking about is that, while obviously coming up with new effects is directly encouraged, the list of example Spirit Blight effects is kind of tilted in the opposite direction from what the Beshilu do to the Gauntlet; taking the description in the "Hunting the Gauntlet" sidebar and the modifications void spirits make to void reivers as a starting point, leaning into the emptiness and alien inhospitability angles is probably the right way to go, since hunger and disease are motifs that sort of race each other to the end of their bearers' lives without the functional immortality common to the Shadow.

              You might give the mutant Beshilu Dread Powers with effects similar to the Facets of the Gift of Hunger, render the infected Shards semi-contagious, or take some cues from Shunned's mechanics for Wounds (or the corebook's Essence Shaping powers from the idigam) to apply to loci and other areas affected by the Hosts' gnawing. It may be worth addressing, if this isn't simply a common train of thought being hijacked by the void, how the rats think the Gauntlet as a whole is in the way of their reunion — The Sundered World says that Rat once carved out a warren of tunnels in Pangaea and filled them with beautiful things, so perhaps there's something stuck in the Gauntlet somewhere (probably somewhere thick, if you'd like to lean into the impact on major population centers) that can only be reached by tearing it all down?

                      Wrote this for myself as a retainer of thoughts and ideas, helps me get a mood. It was SUPPOSED to be from the perspective of a Beshilu minister figure, but uh, apparently I have a hard time actually writing in a more maddened fanatic method! So I ended up altering the perspective to be a bit more omniscient.


                      The firmament of night was darker and blacker, it was inky and beautiful, the stars shown brightly in the Outback. Luna’s face was hidden. Her senses blind.

                      That was when it landed, a spirit from beyond even her. It came unto their nest, and it spoke in garbled speech, pain wrenching, disgusting, but familiar speech. They knew not what it said, but understood it nonetheless.

                      And so, they partook its flesh, a communion of the void, of the greater beyond, and it instilled a great, furious, empty hunger upon on nest.

                      And so, they spread. They gnawed at the walls between with greater fervor than ever before. They spread more, they gnawed more. They spread and gnawed, the wolves could scarce believe it as it was happening, as it all fell crashing and crumbling and collapsing down. The Uratha couldn’t prevent us, it was far, far too late. The one from beyond promises them that their eternal hunger to once again be the great king of plagues to be satisfied. To overwhelm the scions of Urfarah, their great nests re-united.

                      The Gauntlet of Australia fell. And so they wait. In hiding, for command and purpose. Changing ever dimmer, ever emptier, ever hungrier. In absent abandon.
