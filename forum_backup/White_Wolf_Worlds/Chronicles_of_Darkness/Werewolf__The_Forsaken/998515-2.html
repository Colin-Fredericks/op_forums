            It's entirely possible I'm wrong - it's been years now since we discussed and tested these rules. One of the design principles is to eliminate pointless or undramatic rolls. IMO, rolling Resolve+Composure just to see whether you can roll it again is a pointless roll.

            And this (as I believe) is why it's a modified form of Gauru's Rage - the same roll can give multiple turns of control, rather than a single turn-by-turn scenario.

            Another interpretation of the writing is that in Wasu-Im you can either (a) Attack, or (b) Rolle R+C to do something else. If you choose b, you can either do the one other action for one turn, or you can roll R+C again to see if you can continue to do something else for multiple turns. There's not much to gain by not going for multiple turns of control - you can always choose to fall into Basu-Im at any time. Essentially, you're just doubling up on rolls, which IMO is a less fun use of the rules.

I would just say the turn you spend taking the "get breathing room" action doesn't trigger a reflexive Gauru Rage roll, or if you want to be a stickler for wording, at least count it as one of your turns of lucidity.

                            Originally posted by BinAly View Post

The point is you make that lucidity roll to buy yourself turns to get as far away as possible from anyone and anything you care about. Or failing that, to at least get into a position where you'll do more damage to your enemies than your allies. It's not Ook, you've got 200 tries to get an exceptional succcess", it's "Ok, you've got three turns to try to position yourself, do you want to run away or try to get in the middle of the action?" and for the chance of an exceptional success to be a lucky bonus. The 10 minutes probably won't all get played out in combat turns, at a certain point it becomes "and after a few minutes of struggle, you finally succumb to full death rage. Fortunately since you were able to get a good distance into the woods, it's probably safe to assume you didn't cause too much collateral damage."

                                Kuruth Trigger Reaction
                                Action: Instant

                                Dramatic Failure: Instantly enter Basu-Im, going straight for the source of the trigger. Even after Basu-Im ends, you suffer from the Atavism condition towards any sources of the same trigger.
