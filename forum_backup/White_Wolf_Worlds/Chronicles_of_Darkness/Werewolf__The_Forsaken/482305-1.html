    I'm fine with Swaggering, I like the mechanics and I think it makes sense as a Condition the Rahu would place on their prey. Besides, I thought Submissive and Resigned were to conceptually similar and stepped on each other's toes anyway.

    But I don't like "Dominant" as the name of the Hunter's Aspect that causes Swaggering. It makes sense that a monstrous presence would make you feel resigned to your fate. It makes sense that an isolating presence would make you feel alone and helpless. It makes sense that a mystifying presence would leave you in fear of the strange and mysterious. It makes sense that a deceptively blissful presence would leave you ignorant of the fact that you're being hunted. It makes sense that a dominant presence would make you feel submissive, but not so much that it would make you overconfident or swaggering.

    I'd rather the Hunter's Aspect be called Challenging or something similar. Instead of trying to dominate the prey, the Rahu calls it out. It's Hunter's Aspect says "come at me without any of your fancy tools. Let's go, just me and you." And the prey can't help but accept the challenge. Or, it can, but it costs Willpower and an extended Resolve + Composure roll.

    Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.
