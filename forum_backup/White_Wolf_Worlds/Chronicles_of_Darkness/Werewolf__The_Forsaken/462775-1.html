                                                    I'm not sure what you mean by passive. Since Spirits use the simplified traits, I would base their Doors on their Resistance. Probably the lower of Resistance or Rank. It does take a long time to maneuver anyone at a neutral impression (and it's impossible at hostile) unless you force doors. In my experience, most of the problems people have with the social maneuvering system is that they don't force doors nearly enough. I'm guessing that's because it's presented alongside hard leverage, so people assume forcing doors always means using coercion. But you actually don't need hard leverage to force doors (though it helps) and it doesn't need to involve threatening the mark. Forcing Doors is just your option for single-roll social actions. Also, telling a spirit "do as I say or I'll call a Siskur Dah on you" is a VERY Uratha thing to do, and I'd count that threat as pretty solid Hard Leverage, depending on the pack's strength relative to the spirit and their reputation among the local spirit courts.
