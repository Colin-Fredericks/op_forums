    49 Maeljin servant demons..That is a lot of actions for one ST to plan! O.o


    I think I am just playing W:tF games with a different scale of combat than others here.

                                                            Generally speaking, an ephemeral entity is only truly destroyed if they lose all of their Essence in addition to their Corpus. There's a rite that lets werewolves devour the essence of a defeated spirit that is the target of a sacred hunt. It's considered disrespectful to drain a spirits Essence completely with the rite, but it is possible. It's impractical for killing multiple spirits at once though. It's also usually a harmony breaking point towards spirit. If a werewolf is using it to systematically kill off a lot of spirits they would probably end up trapped in the Shadow. There's a merit that also damages Essence, but it only depletes 1 or 2 Essence per hit as part of an attack. It's enough to force a Spirit to be careful, but it's unlikely to kill a spirit unless they are reckless. Ephemeral entities do lose Essence when they take Aggravated damage though, so manifesting in the presence of someone holding their bane is very risky for them. For similar reasons, Spirits (But not ghosts or angels, etc) have to be very wary of angering Werewolves that have a high enough honorary rank to inflict aggravated damage against them.

                                                            We don't know whether Death Rage still has a flight response. I suspect it does, but we don't really know. For that matter, we don't know if a werewolf that has already used up their war form for the scene can still shift to Gauru during Wasu-im and voluntarily enter a Death Rage. We only know that they will shift to Gauru during Basu-im. I'm not convinced that a PU 2/Purity 2 Rahu is going to be able to kill a spirit every round, but this discussion is getting a little off track from the thread topic as it is.
