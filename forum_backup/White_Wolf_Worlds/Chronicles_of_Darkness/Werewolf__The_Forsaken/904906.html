    So Which Tribe is Right?

    06-01-2016, 09:18 AM
    Each Tribe in Werewolf lists arguments for why its favoured prey is the most dangerous of all. And all of the make fairly good cases. So which one do you think is right? (Discounting the idigam for now).

    Humans have intelligence, technology, versatility, and social systems at their disposal. But careful werewolves can have those things as well, if they're not too proud. Nothing can match humans for numbers, though, and numbers are an advantage that shouldn't be underestimated. From a werewolf perspective, humans are also nearly unmatched at creating secondary problems.

    As werewolves, the Pure can have those advantages too, and turn them against the Blood Talons. Additonally, they can bring anything to take fight that Forsaken can - though the reverse is also largely true, and the Pure are ideologically predictable.

    Spirits are variable, hugely variable, though each one isn't especially versatile. They can have enormous power, but their weaknesses - when you can find them - are usually fairly easy to make use of (at least if you're a Bone Shadow). But where humans and werewolves have known weaknesses, those of spirits can be anything, and you're starting from, if not square one, probably square two, with each one.

    Hosts can vary a lot in power. But they're very hard to get rid of - more than any other threat, they're long term. And if anything beats humans for spawning secondary problems, it's the Hosts.

    The Ridden, especially the Claimed, are an unpredictable mix of humans and spirits - with both the advantages and disadvantages of each, though muted in both cases. And their mentalities take in both natures too, which makes them the hardest to get a handle on.


    So... by a small margin I'd say the Hunters in Darkness and Storm Lords have the first and second most dangerous, though I'm not sure what order I'd put them in.


    You?

      I'd probably say hosts are the most dangerous of the five tribal sacred prey. They combine aspects of Claimed with their usurping of humans, and werewolves with their being able to affect both sides.

      But most of the spirit examples we're given are more passive threats, with the only really high rank spirit acting in the world being one of the tribal totems. And aside from the Pure, other werewolves aren't made out to be very aggressive either, seemingly wrapped up in their own thing or acting more on a one-on-one skirmishes. Claimed are implied to be uncommon and don't seem to multiply the way hosts do, or often do the damage, though I can see why the Storm Lords would feel they were more of a moral corruption, since they are examples of weakness in mankind.

      When the hosts get out of control they seem to leave entire cities dead in their wake.

              I think its the a tie beteween human and spirit. These two are so closely interrelated that I don't think its possible to separate them. Humans beget negative spirits, which influence more bad things in people. Claimed are a mix of the two that have the worst aspects of both human and spirit, but weaker in scope. You can't even pull the 'eat them for aggravated damage' thing on most of the hosts, and they have no banes or bans. They're not just the most dangerous antagonist, they're the ones werewolves are least equipped to deal with, not counting the out of context idigam threat.

                  Certainly in a one-on-one scenario or situation where your pack is the target any of the options can be a nasty threat. But hosts represent a more large-scale threat. The fiction throughout the second edition corebook has a pack dealing with beshilu. Thousands of people die, most of the pack is dead and their lives are up-ended, when victory is finally achieved over a a period of years it's because the alpha has sworn to serve a spirit for twenty years to win it.

                      Bone Shadows. The Bone Shadows are right, spirits are the most dangerous prey.

                      A great deal of what makes werewolves dangerous prey is power borrowed from spirits - a werewolf is a deadly combatant, but what kind of foe would he be without his Renown, Rites, or Gifts?

                      Humans are only damgerous because of the impact they have on the Shadow ecosystem - one human is no threat at all, but humanity on the whole twists spirits into strange beasts and feeds them tainted Essence.

                      The Hosts are dangerous because they manipulate the barrier between flesh and spirit - Shartha are pests, difficult to eradicate, but the real danger they pose is the spirits their gnawing lets in, or the allies their weaving keeps out.

                      Claimed are dangerous not only to fight, but in how they transgress the nature of spirit and flesh - what is a Claimed but a spirit that has taken control of its host to blasphemous levels?

                      It all comes back to spirits. They may not be the most dangerous prey on their own, but they are what makes the other prey as dangerous as it is.

                      Also, don't forget that while Idigam are strange spirits that don't follow the normal rules, at the end of the day, they are spirits.
