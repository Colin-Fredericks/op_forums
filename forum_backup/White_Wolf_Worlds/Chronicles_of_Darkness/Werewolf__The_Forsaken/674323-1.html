    Originally posted by Elfive View Post
    What do you think would be a good ratio of primal urge to other stuff if you start with 30 bonus XP?
    I was just thinking of that.

    Lets just write down the Experience Costs first and get that out of the way. Attributes cost 4, Skills 2, Skill Specialties 1, Merits & Rites 1 per dot, Affinity Gifts 3, Non-Affinity Gifts 5, additional Facets 2, Wolf Gift Facets 1, Renown 3, Primal Urge 5.

    Just rounding it out, a fairly active Uratha who isn't focusing in one way or another. Lets say 1 Attribute and new Affinity or Non Gift every 2 Primal Urge, 1 Skill every Primal Urge, 2 Renown every Primal Urge, 1 Facet and 1 Wolf Gift Facet every Primal Urge. With, maybe, 3 Experiences left over per Primal Urge to be spread out among Rites, Merits and Skill Specialties?

    That's a lot of gifts, maybe take out the 1 Facet per Primal Urge and have it rely on Renown and new Gift openings.
