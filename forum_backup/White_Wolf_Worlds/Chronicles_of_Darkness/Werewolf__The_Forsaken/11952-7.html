    161) One of your wealthy and influential wolf-blooded Pack member has met the man of her dreams! He's articulate, brilliant, confident, competent, kind, and an expert in everything she cares about. She met him on an exclusive dating site which guarantees results, and in her head and heart she's already planning the wedding. She was feeling deeply lonely and depressed without stable romantic options because of her being a member of the pack and her high pressure job. She is asking how she should introduce the PCs to her potential future husband, and it is obvious she is in love.

    163) While tracking down Ideal Inc. by following the bloody trail their Ideal Mates leave (the first drop of blood having come from your pack), you eventually find ones that have "stabilized," so to speak. They show no emotional instability and suffer no violent fits of rage, but instead show little quirks and odd behaviors. Turns out that they're all Ridden by spirits antithetical to the Resonances created by the Ideal Mates, as a means of protecting their own feeding grounds. Furthermore, it appears that possessing these clones is much more easier for spirits in general, according to the Ridden clones. While the choice of leaving the Ridden be or hunting them down is an obvious issue, there's one more problem. The Shadow is yet unaware of this advantage, given the lack of Ridden clones. Ideal Inc. also seems oblivious, given the lack of spiritual defenses on their "products." With every Ideal Mate on the loose, a mass outbreak of Ridden and Claimed becomes closer and closer, and they're not going to be so docile as the already Ridden ones.

        164) A god-like Dihar (Rank 6) that appears as a giant serpent with bloodily severed wings, which reeks of deception and secret knowledge, dwells in a Place-That-Isn't connected to a major political buildings within easy travel of the pack's territory, and has sent an envoy to the Pack. It is requesting that the Pack's Ithaeur bind its Master into a Fetish at its Master's behest and is offering unlimited access to the Place-That-Isn't in exchange. The Place-That-Isn't has passages leading to many arcane, occult, and political locations around the world, and contains hints of some secret society clandestinely controlling Everything.

        Originally posted by Pale_Crusader View Post
        Fetish Flaw Condition
        Breached
        The fetish does not perfectly contain the entity within, there are cracks in its prison that it can exploit. A bit of its Influence and Manifestations insidiously sips out during its use.

        Effect: The user gains the Resonant Condition appropriate to the Entity bound within the fetish when they use it and don't already have that Condition. The condition lasts for a day. If they do have that Resonant Condition it becomes Open for a scene, and if already Open the user gains the Persistent Urged Condition which redefines the Urge each time they use it. After having earning 15 - (the contained Entity's Rank) worth of Beats from that Condition they gain the Possessed Condition for a scene each day afterward. This continues to happen unless the body of the victim is destroyed (it'll continue to happen to corpses after death), the Possessed Condition gets ended via Abjuration, exorcisms and forced contact with banes or bans while it is happening, or the victim is Claimed. If the victim goes a year being subjected to Possessed Condition each day the victim gains the Claimed Condition caused by the entity within and the Fetish disintegrates as it escapes, and everyone affected by this fetish flaw are freed from cycles of gaining the Possessed Condition.
        Cause: Dramatic Failure in Fetish Creation; Apparently "succeeding" Fetish Creation on Entities greater and 5 Ranks;
        Beats: Are awarded for the conditions this causes.
        Unbeknownst to the pack the Dihar has discovered the above loophole that would allow it to Claim a body in the Material world without diminishing its strength. As far as the supposed secret society, it may be a Mystery Cult Initiation with Seers of the Throne and other powerful beings forming a cosmos spanning conspiracy... or maybe its a ploy... possibly both.

                                169) Mysterious, semi-tangible creatures have begun to appear, simply manifesting wherever humanity has created the conditions for them to manifest, whatever those actually may be. Solid and visible to a few, but completely unnoticed to most of the herd. These beings, called Serpents by the mortals aware of them, but often resembling lizards due to the highly variable number of limbs, resemble living organisms, yet defy many basic laws of nature or physics. Possessing no digestive, nervous, reproductive, circulatory or skeletal systems, the exact mechanics of how they work are left a mystery. What is clear, is that the Serpents live exclusively in urban areas, predominantly manifesting near or inside especially large buildings, favoring the tallest structures in the area. Though some breeds favor underground tunnels.

                                Some Serpents have hides made of leather, skin, or scale, some have flesh of wrought iron, plastic, or paper. Many are completely hollow inside, others are filled with rare gases, motor oil, human organ tissue, or other, seemingly meaningless substances. While exclusively found on the flesh side of the gauntlet, their existence and behaviors are oddly spirit like, each 'breed' devoted to a single act, chewing wires, clogging gas tanks, inducing stutters, luring people to deserted areas to mangling them beyond recognition... They are mostly disinterested in humans, outside of how they might factor into their compulsions, or whatever bans or banes the things may have, if they have any at all.

                                Where did these so-called 'Serpents' come from, why have these creatures begun to infest the herd's cities, and how can the pack deal with the effects they may have on their territory?

Not sure if I'll ever turn my notes into genuine home-brew, though.))

                                                    173) New Pangaea
                                                    Something stirs inside the Gauntlet. Uratha and Spirits Reaching through it report a sense of movement and purpose. Belishu have been observed being repelled by forces emerging from where they gnaw, and Alzu are being evicted from their nests. Oracles and seers (cryptically) tell one thing - Pangaeans are returning/reforming.
                                                    A) Fragments of the ancient ones are being reborn. Turtle rises once again. Bull's Heart beats once more in its chest. Echos of howls presage even the return of Wolf. But are these the same beings they were before, or new gods filling old roles?
                                                    B) But these Pangaeans are not the old gods of elements, beasts, and cycles. They are new ones formed from Humanity's impact upon the world. How will the Uratha take Wolf being replaced by some aspect of the Herd?
                                                    C) These Pangaeans are not as they were, however. They embody alien concepts and clashing natures. Some say that Idigam have usurped the role of the old gods.
                                                    D) The Pangaeans are reforming, but the process is not complete. Powerful representatives of their old roles are competing to be the new embodiment of Fox, Spider, and Reflection. Which Uratha will win the title and become Wolf Reborn?

                                                    [If you want to downgrade the cosmic implications, you could instead re-cast this as a local restoration of the Border Marches, with the Gauntlet remaining (for the moment) as it is everywhere else.]

                                                            175) A wolf-blooded, or human packmate is in trouble. Their company was doing things they weren't supposed to be doing and he found out. Now he's on a hit list. But it's not a thug that's going to come after him, it's a spirit. Can you protect your packmate? Can you do it while also finding out how this company can send spirits out to kill and whatever else they were doing?

                                                            It can be made a ghost or other entity. Even a host. If ephemeral, spice it up so that it will only attempt to kill when the target is alone, requiring them to either use the target as bait, or babysit them.

                                                            176) The trap worked, you trapped the host in and the fire went off and now they're surely dead. Except not quite. In the chaos, a hungry spirit of fire and death decided it's had it with the increased safety measures throughout town, and went and claimed one of those fleeing beshilu. His arson crusade was cut short, however, as he was eaten by another beshilu. A beshilu who's just gained Influence over Fire that will only increase in power and use it gets bigger. And since it has Warp Shard it can also start creating new ones like it.

                                                            Feel free to change the host or spirit. Perhaps electrical azlu that craft a great web through the powerlines across the territory? Or just use other powers, one of my razilu has Beast's Dragonfire.

                                                            177) That Ivory Claw pack has been a monument in town. Built around a family, between their wealth and connections they maintain a strong control over even the territories of Forsaken wolves. Yet aside from some 'punishments' for acting against them, and the disappearance of an entire pack that was trying to get other Forsaken to go after them, they seem content to let the Forsaken go about their business. But now a member of your pack receives a text or phone call. They claim to be one of the daughters of the Pack's patriarch. And she also claims that the Change will be coming over them soon and the family is going to ensure Luna's brands never appear on her. But she doesn't want any part of it, and asks your pack for help for her escape so that she can join one of the Forsaken tribes and be properly awakened to Luna.

                                                            It might be a trap! Perhaps it's not, and it wasn't even she that contacted you, but a Lune or emissary from your tribal totem. Meaning you would have to take her unwillingly from a ritual she wants to do.

                                                            178) You had to watch this guy. The lunes and spirits all agreed, he was going to have his First Change. And the Pure have been on a recruiting run lately so you needed to get to him before they did. And it begins, in suitably violent manner during an attempted carjacking. Criminals torn to bits. Better get your elodoth on that. But something weird happens when it wears off. In hishu form, he climbs off the ground, dusts himself off, looks around in a fearful manner, gets back into his car and drives off. Leaving his werewolf body behind. It also awakens, shifting from gauru to dalu. Somehow, his hishu form is out there on its own.

                                                            Maybe he doesn't even have dalu, and the guy (or girl, all these can flip around) can't go to human or near-human. What's the human side doing? Is it aware what happened? How do you tell the werewolf that his life has changed and, apparently, ignored him and kept going.

                                                            179) Babysitting again. He's supposed to have his First Change tonight and, fortunately, it looks like he's working late at his office in the middle of nowhere. Wait till it happens, try to minimize the damage without becoming a target, wait till he passes out, then explain things to him when he wakes up. Easy peasy you thought, until he kept raging after the first ten minutes. And now, the guy's been raging for 8 hours now and it doesn't look like he's going to stop. His office building is wrecked and it looks like he's going to make his way to more populated areas. To make matters worse, the sun is rising, work is starting soon and people will be coming back to the office. And he's still raging.

                                                            180) A firefighter went through their First Change. He's a good guy, takes the Oath of the Moon seriously but refuses to join a tribe. He explains that, as he was raging, surrounded by fire, he saw and connected with something more vast than him. And after finding out about Uratha culture, he's decided it must be one of the firstborn, a fiery wolf that burns whatever it touches who is only rumored of in some of the most obscure of legends. Eventually, he comes to your pack, because he wants your help finding it and releasing it from its imprisonment or awakening it from its slumber.

                                                            For a potential twist, maybe the legends also suggest that this firstborn would be staunchly on the Pure side.
