    I'm trying to get my head around how to bring all the players together to form a pack. And it's just not clicking...yet. It maybe from the years upon years of d20 indoctrination that has skewed my GM brain... so help, please?

    What are some ways you have done or are doing to bring your game together and get the ball rolling?

          There's another book out there for World of Darkness called Mysterious Places, it's full of all kinds of plot hooks to drag characters into the same situation from different places and angles. Get that, if you can, and try a few, or dream up a few ideas of your own about how to bring all the characters together. After that, you can initiate them into the Forsaken world as a group with yourself as some NPC mentors and such, and that will help get them on their feet.

          Another option is to give them a rundown of "Forsaken 101" after you know the material well yourself. It's a good time to have everyone make their characters, too. Let them know what you know and that you don't know it all yet, but here's what you've got. Then you all learn it together.

          Or, as Matt has above, the quick-start method. If the pre-made adventures suit you and your group, look at the SAS stuff on DriveThruRPG for Forsaken.

                          Yeah most of the stuff in the Core is considered to be general knowledge to Werewolves (more or less). The idea being that during their initiation period where they're introduced to a tribe and learn about their mythology, they're told some basic information about what they are. Granted this can seem to be a problem if you're playing a mortal-to-werewolf game, but then you can take care of it by having there be a learning period so after they get hooked up with a mentor pack or whatever they're told the basics.

                          As for how the pack can get together, they could be the only ones native to a certain area (which then becomes their territory), they could be given an area by whichever pack or packs watches over a territory, they could have some sort of spiritual connection to it that links them together (perhaps when they went through their first change they earned the friendship of a totem spirit who lives in a specific spot), maybe they were all at a werewolf moot and, having no territory, were sent to some place that needs tending. Or maybe they all met in a bar and, realizing they were on the same side, decided to team up.

                                                  My longest W:tF chronicle began with the pack members all being the junior members of a larger pack of veteran Uratha. The first session started with them sitting in a pub, watching the tv news report with horror as it panned across the flaming wreckage that had been the pack's main holding. A large Pure pack had attacked, and the elders had sent the fresh newbies out of harms way to keep them safe, then got wiped out as they defended their turf (well, all but one, but he went into hiding due to, among other things, being a Bale Hound).

                                                  The Pure then retreated, having finished their mission (who they were and why they had performed the attack was one of the long-running elements in the chronicle), leaving the fledgeling werewolves to then try to pick up the pieces that their dead mentors had left behind.

                                                  - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
