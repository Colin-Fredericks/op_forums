    Werewolf 2e and the internet

    03-07-2015, 08:47 PM
    Reading through 2e and just finished up the tribes. While I was reading about the Iron Masters I got to thinking - how would this technology-oriented tribe view the internet? Might they be able to reconsider their territory as an online forum or mmorpg instead of a physical place? Maybe some might actually stake out and protect server locations hosting websites they feel connected to? Or maybe they just white knight on their own special forums.

    I apologize if anything later in the book gets around to discussing it. Interested in hearing any other ideas how Iron Masters (or others) might view the web as space to be claimed and protected.

                          Originally posted by Satchel View Post

                          "Location Spirits" sidebar, page 266.

                          Thanks, that is an interesting idea. (To summarize for anyone unable or too lazy to go look it up: location spirits can combine with nearby, potentially dissimilar location spirits to form a 'larger' location spirit. The same being said to happen with spirits relating to virtual locations.)

                          ​
                          Otherwise, in response to Aiden: I agree they'd definitely use technologies for hunting. That seems to be the emphasis for Iron Masters. But I can imagine a high humanity basement dweller, seething in Dalu form, frustrated that he can't use his werewolf abilities to dominate in LoL or CoD. (or whatever the latest popular version is). Or I could envision an ex-IT Ilatheur who goes out of his way to block or banish malware spirits passing through his server. Or maybe, on a darker note, an Iron Master who will seek you out, no matter where you exist in the world, if he perceives you invading or violating his online territory.

                          Werewolves were all once just normal folk, and many many normal folk now take to the internet like a second home. I think that instinct would be hard to give up, even after the change.

                          But again, I'm happy and interested to hear other thoughts on it.

                                                  Yeah. Originally the lodge tried to harness the spirit of the Internet, but after they were almost completely wiped out for their efforts, they rebuilt and went about studying it instead. The totem is Digital Eye. Most are unaware that Eye is the Lord of Surveillance. Like ArcaneArts said it's in Tribes of the Moon, on page 137.

                                                  I'm always surprised Werewolf hasn't expanded more on that. I know Werewolf the Apocalypse had more emphasis on the Internet with the 'Cyberrealm,' but I don't know much about it. There's a Claimed who first thought she was talking to an AI in the Book of Spirits, called Cray. The book also talks about the significant presence of computers in the Shadow on page 74, and a sort of invisible 'connection between every computer reflection in the Shadow' with spirits taking advantage of the various kinds of addicts.
