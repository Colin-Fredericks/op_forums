                                    Presenting my take on Bone Gnawers as urban information wolves.

                                    Bone Gnawers
                                    Children of Rat, Scavengers, Punks

                                    “Whatever works.” It’s motto for the wolf Children of Rat. One of oldest ( some say THE oldest ) of Lodges is about survival. But survival in world after Sundering, where Hunters Paradise is no more. Many say that future Bone Gnawers foreseen rise of humanity and prepared for years to come before final blow on Urfarah put the end to Pangea. Others say that Mother Rat was best informed as she get all about Wolf’s weakness from Plague King, as his consort, even when she betrayed him later. But it’s all myths and legends now. And now is what Bone Gnawers are all about.

                                    Scavengers were always with humans, on the low and outskirts, with wretched and downrotten. Eating scraps from Man’s table, convincing to be only common dogs, they have best place to listen what humans say when no one sees. Bone Gnawers are always where humanity pretends it does not have problems with it’s own society. And it’s always have.

                                    Even if commonly disdain for their society circles and Totem, Punks are known as best information gatherers in area with human populations - the larger, the better. Like the rats under auspice of their Mother, they feel best in crowd. They are also then less seen, with their somehow odd and disturbing to rest of Uratha quirks.

                                    Even if Lodge is open to all Tribes members, not many outside of Iron Masters join them. Children of Red Wolf makes perfect Children of Rat, as both groups motto’s are almost the same. Few really devoted urban shamans from Bone Shadows join it, reconciling “bone” parts of groups names in looking over deaths and dead in cities. Very dedicated Hunters in Darkness, fighting Beshilu, enter Lodge to understand their enemy better. Surprisingly, many Ghost Wolves become Bone Gnawers, understanding living outside of main society. But it’s okay - Rat will take each that accept her.

                                    Totem: Rat
                                    Mother Rat is patron of Gnawers. She is spirit of filth, crowds and doing what’s necessary - but also knowledge and information. Most Uratha despise her as taking her for Plague King of Beshilu Host. Truth is those two entities where couple eons ago, preparing rats to take the world. But when Urfarah hunt the Plague King and his kind, she give up King for the lives of her children. And Wolf let them live, as he was hunting King for breaking his rules in Pangea. From then on, Rat do everything whats need to be done to survive. However, Plague King never forgave her and still is hunting her and spiritual kin. This means that Beshilu actively tries to kill any Rat spirits they can find in nearby. Kill - or maybe do something much worse...

                                    Bonds
                                    Blessing: The Lodge member starts with Perfect Impression on every Social Maneuvering from Urban Choir spirits and people in lower strata of society. When spending Willpower to add dices on Survival roll, he gains the rote quality on that roll.
                                    Aspiration: To gather information on local population.
                                    Ban: A Lodge member cannot let downrotten to suffer.
                                    The Sacred Hunt
                                    The Lodge Sacred Hunt grants your character the ability to track prey even while she is in ugliest part of town; she suffers no penalties to the tracking attempt for sight or smell in urban centers.

                                    Many of Bone Gnawers Lodge members have those Conditions.

                                    Homeless (Persistent)
                                    Character does not have his own home and probably live on the street. She cannot also found new one. If character do not sleep in some kind of shelter, she does not get Willpower from resting for that night. Forced starvation or dehydration can lead her to Deprived Condition or even Sick Tilt. Long time without shower makes other people to be off putted by her presence - every Social Maneuvering with character without this Condition starts on one level lower Impression than normal. Character also cannot buy dots in Resources Merit.
                                    Possible Sources: Life tragedy, like fire in previous house or loss of family. Lost of wealth or job.
                                    Resolution: Getting steady income enough to sustain living in normal accommodation for at least month. Help from close ones, letting to support character.
                                    Beat: Character makes other people to leave his presence. Character get’s free food, clothes or money on them from other.

                                    Outsider (Persistent)
                                    This character looks and acts counter to the main society. Depending on location or history era, this character can be from untouchable caste Dalit in India or Burakumin in Japan, gay, hippie, punk, goth or any radical counterculture of the time. Character have -1 to -3 penalty on social rolls with others depending on target conservative worldview. Mainstream target would have -1 and ultraconservative would have -3 penalty. Character also add one more Door for each penalty point to the target’s pool.
                                    Possible Sources: Skin color or sexuality. Loss of status in society. Teenage rebellion.
                                    Beat: Whenever characters status leads to conflict with authorities or community.
                                    Resolution: Character integrates with mainstream. She changes her act and look to be with norm or even is lifted to proper society of location and era.

                                                        Silver Fangs
                                                        Nobles, Houses of Sun and Moon, Boyars
                                                        “Noblesse oblige.” Being noble is obligation - to country, to people - and first and foremost - to family. It’s most crucial thing to Silver Fangs, Lodge dedicated to ‘proper’ ruling of Uratha - by them and their kin. Heirs to great heroes and leaders, they pass they greatness till this day.

Under guidance of Falcon, one group finally take control and initiated all chieftains and rulers to come to cult of their patron. Under their secret Totem, Silver Fangs remade human tribes in to kingdoms, and those in to empires. They spread all over the world, always making sure “their own” are real force in power. There were only one problem - ancestors.

                                                        Original Fangs were coming from the lines started even in Pangea. Descendants of what would be once Ivory Claws Tribe under Silver Wolf, were meticulous to point to their great forefathers. One side say only original predecessors were worthy of veneration and no new line could join their fold. Other group said there is place for new idols and their own posterity in Lodge. Two sides split, one joining Ivory Claws and making sure no new blood will pollute their kin.                                                             Got idea for the most disliked old Tribe that I hope will make interesting Lodge now.

                                                            Children of Gaia
                                                            Children of Unicorn, Harmonious, Purists
                                                            Gaia is all around us. In streams and rivers, in mountains and trees. In animals and humans. And especially in Shadow. To be true, Earth’s Shadow is Gaia. And just like Uratha are part of Shadow, they are part of Gaia. We are part of Gaia. Gaia is part of us. We are part of Harmony.

                                                            But there are places that should not be. Places twisted and corrupted. They are those that turn around from our purpose. Those that lost their way and gone astray. And those that turn from Gaia, need to be dealt with. We should save our brothers and sisters, if we can. Restore them to true way. And lead them to final resting place, if we cannot.

                                                            Children of Gaia seek to help Uratha in maintaining Harmony. They seek to improve those that goes too much in ways of Flesh or Spirit. To clean the material and Hisil worlds, return them to balance. They clean Wounds and heal Uratha hearts. And they truly despise Bale Hounds and their treacheries.

                                                            Lodge is open to member from all Tribes of Moon, but Blood Talons rarely join it, seeing them as to ‘soft’. Hunters in Darkness are probably the most numerous in pursuit of Purity, but Bone Shadows trying to understand relations of Harmony to Shadow are second after them. More farseeing Storm Lords enter Lodge to show rest of Uratha better way to their victories.

                                                            Totem: Unicorn
                                                            Unicorn if one of purest of spirits, compassionate in peace but also ferocious in war. He leads his children to make Shadow clean of unnatural tendencies and be true to themselves and ways of their People. Finally, to strike those that spoil it for all around.

                                                            Bonds
                                                            Blessing: The Lodge member can easier maintain his Harmony. He get’s constant +1 bonus on any Breaking Point roll, irrelevant if to Flesh or to Spirit.
                                                            Aspiration: To purify Shadow and Material Realm.
                                                            Ban: A Lodge member must destroy any corruption of Shadow.

                                                            The Sacred Hunt
                                                            The Lodge Sacred Hunt grants your character the ability to sense corruption in things. He gets supernatural sense on Wounds related phenomena, even when they are disguised. He also can feel unbalanced werewolves – those of Harmony 10-9 and 1-2.
