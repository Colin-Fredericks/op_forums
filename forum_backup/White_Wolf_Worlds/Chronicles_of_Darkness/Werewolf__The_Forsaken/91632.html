              I'm not quite sure what the God Machine has to do with it, but okay.

              Lyrics is right though about how in terms of historically meaningful settings, werewolves don't have a lot of formative transitional periods the way some of the other courts do-to cover when their culture developed would almost be in the realm of "pretty much spin it out of whole cloth". That leaves periods evocative of Werewolf ala New Wave, Noir, and Victorian, and while there are plenty of settings that can fit there, it's another thing to find one that's juuuuust right.

              Plus, you know, currently Werewolf gets less love from the audience than others.
