                                                Originally posted by Tabanese View Post
                                                Question: Do/Must Lunes have a specific Ban and Bane? If not, recommendation?
                                                Yes, all spirits have them. In first edition they were sort of all compiled into bans and are now sort of divided amongst bans, referring to behaviors they have to follow or avoid, and banes, more of a physical thing that harms them. Here's a compilation of Lune bans from the Predators book, p50 to 53.

                                                'Cahalunim are vulnerable to silence, and lose much of their power in an area without ambient sound. Elunim can be weakened by light so bright that it casts no shadow, or darkness so complete that there is no light. Most Glimmerings are bound under a ban not to appear on moonless nights. Others are bound only to appear under a certain phase of the moon. Some Irralunim are bound to silence. Others cannot abide the presence of Helions or affect the physical world during daylight hours. The Ithalunim are commonly bound against freely giving information. Many Ralunim are incapable of lies. Others are bound to accept any challenge made to them.'

                                                There are others, of course.
