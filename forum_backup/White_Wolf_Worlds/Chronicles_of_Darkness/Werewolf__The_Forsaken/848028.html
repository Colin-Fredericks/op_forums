    [Mystery Cult] PENTECH: Worldwide Leaders in Natural Technology

    03-16-2016, 03:06 AM
    PENTech: Worldwide Leaders in Natural Technology

    Our task must be to free ourselves from this prison by widening our circle of compassion to embrace all living creatures and the whole of nature in its beauty.
    —Albert Einstein


    Hello, and welcome to our presentation. We are excited to have you here and eager to share our vision of a future without disease, hunger, or even the ravages of age. Here at PENTech we value all life, and seek the betterment of all creatures by perfecting the gifts nature has given us. It's right in our name: Planetary Engineering by Natural Technology. What is "natural technology," you ask? You'll love the answer! You see, nature is itself a sort of technology, engineered through environmental and biological means. At PENTech, we take this one step further, further enhancing the evolution of living processes by directly interacting with the fundamental components of life. I know, that's a mouthful, right? Between you and I, the backroom bigwigs like to use their big words, but basically, what we do is modify things in a naturalistic fashion. We don't add or subtract, we simply enhance genes already present, or promote growth with our all-natural ReFertilizer™ in areas where vegetation has diminished. What we here at PENTech do is embrace the philosophy that Earth is everything, and nature provides all the tools we need to achieve a better life. Our GAIA program, founded in 1865 by J. Lassater, seeks to ultimately create a paradise on Earth for all living creatures. We can't achieve this through the smogs of industry, but rather through the emissions of a healthy biosphere.
    PENTech is always searching for the best and brightest. Of course, in prospective sponsors and employees we desire intelligence and scientific knowledge, but more important than that is integrity. PENTech stands for something larger than just one person, or even one generation. Our mission requires dedication, optimism, and above all, vision. It doesn't matter what garden you first sprouted from, race, creed, nation, or wealth, what we here at PENTech want most is the imagination and adaptability to help us plant a new future. You must be able to envision a world without sickness, genetic defects, and conflicts born of scarcity. The Earth will provide everything we need; we just need to know how to ask it. This way, please.

    This is the Greenhouse. It's sort of our think tank, where we germinate the seeds of an Edenic future. Our Gardeners—excuse the in-joke—cultivate the greatest ideas and send them to our developmental department, where our scientists design and implement new natural technologies. This is where many of our members remain throughout their careers with PENTech. Board members and our scientists regularly visit the Greenhouse to interact with newer members, fertilize new ideas, if you will. Ah, sorry. Just a bit of a dirty joke there. Ahem. Please don't tell my superiors. I might end up in deep fertilizer myself! *laughs* Please follow me.

    Out here we have the real gardens, where we grow fully tested enhanced crops for our think tank. What better to help promote intellectual and scientific growth than brain food specifically engineered to provide the greatest nutrition? As you can see across the field there, we have our facilities, all constructed completely out of our proprietary SolShield™ technology. Every piece of glass is in fact a solar panel! When light reflects through the outer panels, even the inner windows and halls collect small amounts of its energy. Biofuel powers all the advanced machinery within, each 100% constructed of recycled materials. At PENTech we take our mission very seriously. The GAIA program is not a mere job, but a mission, a privilege, and it is something we fully embrace. Everything PENTech does or uses betters the state of the world today with this goal in mind.

    Finally, I wanted to talk to you a little about what the GAIA program is, and discuss our benefits, for those of you interested in joining the team. Gaia is a figure in mythology, of course, but her status as a creator goddess of Earth befit Mr. Lasseter's vision of a better world. He called his idea "Global Advancement of Interwoven Adaptations," or GAIA. Today, "GAIA" stands for "Green-Augmented Interdigitation Advancement." I know, more big words, but basically what it means is that we want to employ natural technologies to transform the entire world, which is all connected. You've seen it, of course, with smart phones and international commerce, but also with less fortunate things like diseases that cross international lines or, even worse, war. The GAIA program will end any need for war based on resources, and we believe our message will eventually reach the hearts of those whose roots have been poisoned by theological and teleological toxins. This is why we need people like you: people who are sharp, boundlessly enthusiastic for the future, and willing to dedicate themselves to the cause.

    Ah, I mentioned benefits earlier! Well, let's take a look at the basic roles most of our members will play, whether at the seedling level or at the top of GAIA program's future. PENTech offers unmatched benefits, including health care only we can dream of. Can you imagine living to 175? Well, one of our oldest members just passed his 175th birthday. Of course you won't hear that on the news. Contemporaries like Magad—I'm sorry? Oh, yes, the refreshments are for everyone. As I said, our rivals might argue that only their pharmaceuticals can extend human life, but we managed it with only natural technology. We don't share that knowledge with the world yet because few would believe us, just as you didn't, and fewer still would understand that this is only a small step along the path. When the GAIA program is complete, age will become irrelevant. Our lifetimes will be those of the Earth itself, eternal and infinitely adaptable. Please forgive me if I wax poetic, but I've seen the results firsthand. The person you see before you is the picture of health, right? Once, I was drug-addled and disease-ridden, destined for an early grave. But I had a vision, a glimpse of what the world might be like if not corrupted by weaknesses like the ones killing me. It's how I came in contact with PENTech and joined them. Today I am not only healthier than I've ever been, but I'm completely clean and impervious to disease.

    Gaia may be mythological, but the GAIA program is very real. It changed my life, and it can change yours, too.

    • Growers are the ones who plant the seeds. This is where you start, and in many ways is the root of our organization. Growers seek out new ideas and innovations that allow us to overcome a disease, a parasite, a logistical issue with crop growth, or even a new medical procedure. Not every seed bears fruit, of course, but what is important that we do not ever stop planting the seeds. They are our future. With every new idea that germinates and sprouts, the world comes one step closer to salvation through natural technology.

    Members gain the Holistic Awareness Merit, thanks to immersion and training with natural technology.

    •• Gardeners are one of our most important roles. It is the Gardener's job to take any promising seeds and help grow them into something we can develop in the long-term. This requires constant testing and theorizing, as well as intimate working relationships with our Growers. True to their name, Gardeners cultivate ideas as well as workers, helping continue this natural cycle of growth. As a Gardener, you are also privileged enough to receive fully 100% of your dietary needs through PENTech, ensuring you are as healthy as the ideas you help shape.

    Members gain one dot of the Hardy Merit.

    ••• Green Engineers are the men and women who develop, refine, and implement ideas that grow out of the lower levels. It is their job to test every conceivable modification on a wide variety of flora and fauna, humans included. These are the people who put thought and theory into action. Between us, I like to call them "Greengineers," but don't tell them that!

    Members gain one dot of Science. This may take them over their normal Skill maximum of 5.

    •••• GAIA Engineers take everything one step further. Theirs is perhaps the most vital role, because they are out there, implementing our natural technology, securing funding, and transforming parts of the world little by little. These are the people who actually bring all our hard work to fruition. And believe me, it works. You won't find any public deals and trades with PENTech, no, because the companies who share our vision realize that many of their competitors do not. We receive funding from our partners and in exchange we give them the tools they need to join the GAIA program. This, ladies and gentleman, is where the future is born.

    Members gain the Inspiring Merit.

    ••••• Board Members may conjure images of stuffy old men smoking cigars around a long table. Nothing could be further from the truth! Many of our board members are young and vital, and all of them benefit from the latest advances in GAIA-based natural technology. The board is a highly dynamic and interactive group, working with, not over, the people of PENTech. Their goals are your goals. Their families are your families. Their future is your future. Unlike most major corporations, the PENTech board do not hoard their profits. Each year they funnel millions, the majority of their earnings, into environmental causes and fostering new working relationships. Welcome to the new world of progress.

    Members gain one dot in each of their Physical Attributes. This can take them over their normal Attribute maximum of 5.

    GAIA isn't just a philosophy, or a way of life. "GAIA is Life Itself™."

    Paradise. Eden. Natural technology. Available only through PENTech.

    Secret Truths
    Is PENTech truly the philanthropic organization it claims to be? Why, of course it is! Many of our members, such as yourself, ask that question when they realize some of the lies this world has fed them. It's only natural, and here we are all about natural. In fact, we recruited you because you know how to ask questions, to see outside the obvious to a greener future. Our works have opened your eyes, but I'm afraid there are other things out there that want to blind you again.

    Those things you saw attacking the Garden? Werewolves. Yes, that's right. Werewolves. All too real, I'm afraid. I know your mind must be grasping for answers. The truth is this: these creatures are very old and very unnatural. Mr. Lasseter first discovered them the year before he created GAIA, and he did so partially in response to the existence of such aberrations. These creatures destroy our world, tear through the fabric between mortal and spirit. Yes, indeed: a spirit world. What's that? Oh, I know this is hard to believe, and your mind is conditioned to be skeptical. But you know it, don't you? Somehow you've always felt this natural part of you missing, like the world was sick. That's what GAIA is: not just a scientific and medical process, but a way of mending the broken world. Every Garden we grow, every malady we destroy, it helps bring things back into their natural alignment. The world was once whole, but creatures like those terrible beasts ruined it and poisoned our ancestors.

    They are not the only ones. Oh no. We have been studying them for a long time. In fact, more than once these evil creatures have nearly destroyed our movement. But GAIA is stronger than werewolves, or the undead, and so is our devotion to GAIA. Rather than despairing or falling victim to unnatural predation, we developed ways to fight back. Our works make us healthier, stronger, able to see these things that hide from the half-blind public in the smog of the modern world. We have salves for any wound they inflict, cures for any poison. They try to tear us down so that they can go on breeding their unclean race and killing at their whim. For every root they tear out of the soil, or every tree they topple in their madness, ten more seeds are planted. We'll outlast them, don't you worry. We will persevere, because GAIA guides us to become what these monsters can never be: perfect.

    Nor are we alone in our fight. There are many spirits of the natural world, who do not fight in the name of some slavering wolf-god or to satisfy debased cravings for human flesh. They exist simply to be part of the natural cycle. Like the plants of this world, they grow, and like the animals they feed, but they do not die of age. They have been stricken into imbalance the same as we, but while we can bleed, and we can change, they aren't capable of true change. That's why they need us, and we need them. To be whole again. Many of our members enjoy the benefits of a spirit companion, and you will, too. In fact, there is a rather crafty spider spirit that has taken a liking to you. If I remember correctly, you are quite the established entomologist-slash-arachnologist, aren't you? It's a calling, and it's something they feel, too. The spirit of the spider has guided you for some time, and inspired you to many of your breakthroughs. In time, it may ask you to join more completely with it, to regain that missing piece of you. And when that happens, our people can help cement that relationship for you. We have the experts and the facilities where you can put your greatest gifts to use. Maybe one day you will be inspired to join the fight against the Forsaken—oh, those are the cursed ones who call themselves werewolves. The spirits detest them as much as you and I do, for the same reasons: they want to sink their fangs into the throat of our paradise. But they can't.

    The Forsaken will never destroy our cause. GAIA is with us. She will guide us to our true future. GAIA is Life Itself™.

    Additional Rules
    Most members have some interaction with spirits on almost a daily basis. Few below the Green Engineer level know it, however. Most Growers and Gardeners simply generate Essence for legions of hungry spirits who congregate in the Greenhouses or other conceptually relevant places. Some, especially Gardeners, have spirits fettered to them, which enhance their contributions of the GAIA program. Many Green Engineers deliberately cultivate fetters, because the spirits help them achieve astounding breakthroughs. GAIA Engineers all have spirits fettered to them at a minimum, and the spirits often have attendant lesser spirits that aid the PENTech member's individual cause. A significant portion of GAIA Engineers are, in fact, Claimed, though all can hide their supernatural aspects—something they attest to the naturalness of their union. All board members are Claimed, and again most can hide their spirit-born traits, or use stand-ins when they must engage with mundane folk. The Claimed of PENTech are ageless and crafty, most inexperienced in combat but capable of interacting with the natural world more fluidly than even werewolves. Nearly all PENTech Claimed boost their Physical Attributes with the spirit's trait dots.

    When combat against threats to GAIA is necessary, PENTech employs its special division: PENTEX Teams. "Paranormal Entity Neutralization, Targeting, and Excision" Teams. These are the warriors of GAIA. Claimed by dangerous spirits of nature and concepts of progress, bearing savage weapons of natural technology, they take the fight to the werewolves ... and any other enemies of PENTech's agenda. In battle they can transform into monstrous forms blending human features with those of nature spirits: plants, animals, elementals, and so on. These transformations seem unnatural, but to PENTEX, they are completely natural. After all, nature is not always pretty. Evolution is a bloody, messy affair. Theirs isn't the majestic metamorphosis of the butterfly, unlike the other Claimed of PENTech, but instead the raw emergence of the raptor from its egg. Highly trained in tactics and combat, capable of deploying anywhere in the world with specialized adaptations for any environment, PENTEX Teams are the world's foremost soldiers in a war against the supernatural.

    New Dread Power: Monstrous Transformation (Claimed Only, •, ••, or •••)
    This Dread Power, almost universal among PENTech Claimed, essentially gives the hybrid being two forms. In the human form (or animal form, in some cases), the character displays no overt supernatural powers. For each Physical Attribute enhanced by the possession, only one additional dot is available while in human form. This can take an Attribute beyond its normal maximum, but members are exceedingly careful not to use superhuman levels of ability in public. As an instant action, the Claimed may change into its monstrous form, suddenly manifesting its inhuman traits. The fear and dismay caused by such a startling transformation causes all lesser opponents to use Down and Dirty Combat, if they choose to fight rather than flee. This costs 1 Essence. With two dots, this ability is a reflexive action that costs 1 Essence, and with three dots it may be used reflexively without cost.

    Even More Secret Truths
    Is PENTech really benevolent? Does it have paradise in mind, or is it a corrupt organization poisoning the world by its actions? It's hard to say. The spirits that inhabit all levels of the corporation are crafty, alien, and greedy, but the PENTech leaders and visionaries are equally cagey. Do they work at the behest of some ancient, forgotten god? Do they have any connection with the Umaraha'e, the mysterious Children of the Worm? And what about their efforts to explore the Wounds and subjugate the dread spirits found within? For that matter, are the spirits of PENTech themselves tainted, perhaps magath born of nature and technology and human concepts?

    The answer may be "yes" to any or all of these. Umarah, if it exists, works on levels so far removed from mortals that no one entity, corporate or otherwise, could hope to encompass its will. Further, the Children of the Worm believe their god to be trapped, bound away from the world, but awakening soon and to terrible wrath. Their goal is to provide the proper resonance worldwide for Umarah to manifest and regain its power. Perhaps without willingly knowing this, PENTech is furthering that goal. Who knows what arcane principles are needed to create resonance for a being so vast and alien? If it is a bringer of balance, perhaps it seeks balance through destruction and cosmic digestion, and so PENTech operates on one end of the spectrum, while at the other the Umaraha'e corrupt the soils that PENTech tills.

    Werewolves and others who seek the answers to these mysteries find secrets as dangerous and ancient as nature itself. Nothing can prepare them for a war with such entities.
