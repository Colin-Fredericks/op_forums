    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
    Are there any means for spirits and other ephemeral beings to deal aggravated damage? Would there be anything wrong with homebrewing a Numen that lets them spend additional Essence + 1 willpower point to deal aggravated damage with Blasts and a melee weapon Numen? Should this power have a Rank prerequisite?
    I can't think of any ways to do it by default. The reason *not* to do it is the Bane system which is such a significant element of the ephemeral beings system. A power that gives ephemeral beings a shortcut round such a key part of their interactions with each other is not a great idea; on a personal note, I also find 'deals Aggravated damage' powers to *mostly* be interminably dull.

    - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer

        Originally posted by Acrozatarim View Post

        I can't think of any ways to do it by default. The reason *not* to do it is the Bane system which is such a significant element of the ephemeral beings system. A power that gives ephemeral beings a shortcut round such a key part of their interactions with each other is not a great idea; on a personal note, I also find 'deals Aggravated damage' powers to *mostly* be interminably dull.
        Agreed, not to mention the rule which says ephemeral beings automatically deal aggravated damage to others of their kind which are 2 or more Ranks below them.

        What I wonder is if Rank 6+ entities can mimic the Practice Of Unraveling that mages use to deal aggravated damage. Imperial Mysteries says these godlike beings can mimic any of the Common Practices of any Arcanum, as long as the effect fits with the being's purview. I assume this property does not transfer to Royal Avatars, since they are "only" Rank 5.

        That being said, if a Numen that only upgrades the damage type of other Numina is uncreative, we could try to include the Bane system somehow. How about a Numen that lets spirits and other ephemerals absorb the Essence of an instance of an enemy's Bane? That would upgrade each point of lethal damage into aggravated per success on the Power + Finesse roll (caps at Rank x 2 and improves only one future attack). That is seen as subverting the hierarchy of spirits, and has a chance of turning the offender into a magath with a second purview based on the Bane it absorbed Essence from. So, a spirit would need to have ready access to said Bane and use this bad Numen repeatedly, thus increasing the chances that it will be caught and/or become a magath. A group of rebel spirits with this Numen could pose a threat to spirits of greater Rank, which adds a layer of suspense to spirit politics. Does this sound like a good idea?

                        Considering the vast majority of ephemeral entities are only going to be having relevant combat with players, you're looking for a way to throw aggravated damage at player characters and potentially ending their lives because of one extra success or an exploding 10.

                        Spirits don't even have to be high rank to easily push past the gauru health boxes and start chipping away at them with aggravated damage, and that's just doing lethal damage and relying on it wrapping around. Having them just dole out aggravated damage without using a bane like silver is going to lead to very short games and no one risking attacking spirits.

                                                    What would people think about a merit that allowed you to activate a Gift Facet for free? There would be a limit, probably it couldn't be more than 1 Essence. On one hand, Gifts seem to work a very clear way, you pour your Essence through them and out pops the effect, plus at least to me, the game has always been about the cost of things. Like, a really active wolf throwing around Gifts is going to in turn require more Essence, gained primarily through either draining loci or eating things.
