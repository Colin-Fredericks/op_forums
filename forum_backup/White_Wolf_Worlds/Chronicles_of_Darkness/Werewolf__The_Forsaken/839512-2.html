            Don't forget emotions and concepts. Were-schadenfruede, Were-music, etc.

            But joking aside, what other concepts make sense? Father Wolf was a spirit (or Pangaean) of the Hunt, who also happened to identify as a wolf. Had kids with a Celestine, which survive to this day. A similar concept would need to jump through similar hoops. Hosts are similar, but different. Most spiritual representations would be Claimed. You might do a few lineages of Claimed, but they wouldn't have the varied hooks and drives of the Uratha. Urfarah provides the wolf link and the Hunt aspects. Luna allows for the shifting. History accounts for their silver bane, Tribes, duty, etc.

            Anything remotely equivalent will need a fair amount of backstory to be anything other than a one-off worth little note.

            Though I do remember some not-quite Raven-host in one book?

            And because criticism without alternative ideas rubs some people the wrong way:

            A long time ago, there was the [Pact/Pantheon/Guardians/Whatever], a small choir/group of spirits that wanted to see and enjoy the world of flesh. Or something. At first conflicting with Urfarah, they eventually struck a deal to abide by certain rules when travelling beyond the Border Marches (alternatively, they tricked the great hunter and snuck past). Keeping their influence small, they found compatible humans to "ride" with, each member taking a turn to enjoy the world with the human, and offering the human power in return. After the Gauntlet arose and cut them off from their homes in the Shadow, the [Whatever] developed a plan for their survival - they would merge with their human allies, with each human containing a fragment from one of the [Whatever], which they would pass on to their progeny, when circumstances were auspicious. These bloodlines have continued down to today, with members occasionally awakening the dormant shards of the [Whatever] inside them.

            Maps to Werewolves with:
            Alternate forms -> whichever spirit-member is riding shotgun at the time
            Potential powers -> reclaimed spirit Numina, Gifts just like Uratha, or Dread Powers like Claimed (which they closely resemble)
            History allows for their existence.
            Need for subtly (through stealth or pact) explains why they aren't common and can be dropped in without prior notice.
            They do need some other reason for existence, though. Maybe to "experience the world"? Bon Vivant to the Werewolf's Hunter? Or maintain the "Sacred Places" (Loci)? Or Teach Humanity (a mirror of Beast's justification)?

            Whatever, I came up with it in a few minutes. Can easily state the spirit members where a group of animals, or related concepts, with one being the animal of choice.
