                                                            My first impressions of Second Edition:

                                                            I have a deep love for Werewolf. Werewolf the Apocalypse 2 edition was one of my first books for Roleplaying. Then came revised edition, and New world of darkness with the Forsaken. tIt has been a long and fantastic ride.
                                                            each time Werewolf changes, I find the game opens new sides to tell stories. It has a long evolution and it is an evolution Im happy to have told some stories in.

                                                            Man, im getting older!
                                                            Before we get all emotional, lets get to impressions!

                                                            The Good:
                                                            * The written content is top-notch. Some errors to be fixed before physical book-release, no biggie.
                                                            * The material offers a solid update to the GMC system rues changes.
                                                            * I like that the material focuses on Uratha. Pure were meant as antagonists and I dont need a book to run them as NPC's. As for playing them? well 1st edition did not have playable Pure from the get go either.
                                                            * New Gift system looks great, but I need to test it in-game to give verdict.
                                                            * Tribe and Auspice update was simple, well put together. Easy to use.
                                                            * Pack structure! I knew this was coming, but this changes the ball-game for me. Again: i must play-test this.
                                                            * Harmony
                                                            * Wolf blooded! This was maybe one of the most anticipated parts of the new book for my sake. It was all I wanted. it felt like it was short, but at the same time it took full use of its ages to be as informative and useful as possible. Well done! We are playing with wolf-blooded now and some of the tells are similar to what we are playing with already. they will be converted to the book standard as we play onwards.

                                                            The Bad:
                                                            * The Artwork was sadly very uneven.
                                                            I liked the wolf shown in all its dread as a full page color (Brian Le Blanc I believe).
                                                            This was perhaps the picture that struck me the most.
                                                            I also liked the Tribe picture of the female Imnir. Dreads and Gauru cant go wrong!
                                                            Some pictures were from the old edition. I know that some artists did not make it in with new art this time around.
                                                            Hopefully some of them can deliver new work for the physical book release on Drive-thru?
                                                            The auspice artwork was not to my tastes. The pictures had some composition-issues. One of them reminds me of a very good picture in 1 edition (Uratha vs Azlu). this can be issues I have with style and how I feel, but the new picture is too close to the one in 1 edition (white background, single battle between Azlu and Uratha).
                                                            Some of the full-pagers looked more like Shadowrun than Werewolf and felt rushed, or like there was a bit of uncertainty on what would fit the layout.

                                                            * Spirit Flowchart is back! I'm so sorry, but I really don't like this thing. Keep it simple!

                                                            * The Idigam are still an impossible foe to relate to.
                                                            Even though the Idigam are fascinating, they are not really the meat of this book.
                                                            I think the material worked well to build on the excellent Wolfsbane material, and build "the big bad" mood.
                                                            And that is kind of it: Idigam will always be that one shot enemy you put in the background.
                                                            The Shartha, The Pure, The Bale Hounds, hunters, spirits and humans would all be more interesting to explore in-depth for their drives and motivations.
                                                            You will far more often face these foes than the Idigam. Especially in a brutal and savage game that invites you to investigate the insides of raging beasts and what makes them tick.
                                                            I know that WtF 2nd ed was envisioned not as full book but as a chronicle book for the Idigam. As the process went, I'm glad this turned into a full blown 2nd edition.

                                                            Sum up:
                                                            In general from what I have read and skimmed over, this book hits the ball out of the park for me.
                                                            The written material is strong and discuss the Uratha re-imagined for a new edition and makes me hungry to build on my running WtF game. Players have more than enought to keep them occupied, as their roles are evolved with onset of GMC game-rules. this is good as it gives both players and Storyteller the time to mesh their game in a way that everyone wants to play.
                                                            Storytelling-parts of the book gives me ideas and inspiration for future stories and games.
                                                            I feel like I will be playing a lot of Werewolf in the future and Im happy that the book is refueling my interest in one of 1st editions finest games. 1st edition had some flaws (among them Unihar and the lack of enpowered wolfblooded). It is good to see the game opening up and showing more of the insides. More guts.. Actually the game is now far more brutal. the focus on Kuruth and the fury inside make uratha a ticking bomb. The game wants the players to let loose the beast. unleash chaos. The Werewolves are hungry.
                                                            This is a game that wants players to know the headaches of consequences and how it is to play very unstable beings in a world where they are also the apex predator.

                                                            The pack structure is less about the Uratha now. Wolfblooded come in and take up some space. This lets the players play different games. Wolfblooded or Uratha. both have implications for what you do in the pack. I also like the focus on letting humans support the pack more directly than before. Someone has been watching a lot of Sons of Anarchy!

                                                            Some portons (like the Idigam) have less to give for a story than more mature topics that come from Uratha interaction with humans and spirits. This is nitpicking from my side.

                                                            I think the artwork is suffering from a time-crunch here and there. There are parts that glimmer, but sadly this is not the best drawn wolves I've seen.

                                                            The book is worth a buy if you are till on the fence. If you have wanted to upgrade your Werewolf game to new edition, this is a good one folks!
