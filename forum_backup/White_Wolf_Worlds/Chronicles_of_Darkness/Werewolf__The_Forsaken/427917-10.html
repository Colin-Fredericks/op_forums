            p.88 “The Wilds (Spiritual Touchstone) — The wilderness calls to your character. Those places where humans dare to tread”
            - Should probably be “dare not tread” or “fear to tread” as a think the current wording contradicts the intended meaning.


            p.126 “Scent the Unnatural will not detect Uratha or the use of Gifts”
            The ‘o’ in or isn’t there. Or it is, but the font colour is white and so it can’t be seen in text. At least in my copy.

            p.134-135 - This isn’t so much errata necessarily as something I worry might be a bit problematic with the Warding Gift as written. The Warding Gift facets for Cunning and for Honor seem like they should be switched. The Honor facet bypasses the need for larceny rolls in lockpicking doors, and seems more like a gift that say, an Irraka or other sneaky type would want to trap a quarry or get into places they otherwise couldn’t. Especially as there's a good chance many Irraka would never get Honor renown, it seems odd to supplement a non infiltration type Uratha with the means to basically have 100% lockpick power as far as doors are concerned and might overshadow a sneaky type’s utility/purpose in the pack. Whereas the Cunning facet Maze Ward seems more likely to be used for protection as much as escape (especially since it doesn’t affect packmates), and definitely seems more in line with a powerful protector/warden than a trickster/sneaky type. I realize I’m working with some assumptions but I just thought i’d mention it in case the powers that be agreed.
