                                        Hmm. The system in the linked thread is essentially a formalization of 'the numbers go up' - which is a valid approach, but I'm not sure it arms a Storyteller with much more ability than they already have when it comes to making an antagonist's numbers go up as it is.

                                        The specifics of increase in power due to consumption of Shards is not the kind of thing I could really see myself actually tracking as a Storyteller - I'm not going to be mulling over a stat block and trying to calculate precisely how many Shards have been eaten, after all. My approach would generally be much broader; for example, considering a possible Beshilu antagonist, I'd look at the general level of threat and power I want it to provide, and build to meet those needs and the aesthetics and tone I want the antagonist to bring across.

                                        - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
