        As written, Poisoned and Sick get around the resistance to toxins and disease. The resistance applies to Toxicity rolls, which neither of those use. Against supernatural powers/Dread Powers, that doesn't bug me. Against mundane sources I'd probably allow a toxin/disease resistance roll on the turn they get hit with something, and use those Tilts for the remainder of combat if they fail the roll.

        The Poison/Toxin chart hasn't been overridden, so I'd say it still applies. The errata to 1e's original roll has been included in the 2e material so far. I'm not seeing a big reason to adjust it. Though I prefer the WOD: Armory expansion of it.
