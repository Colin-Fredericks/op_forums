                                    More translations for the New Year! Still behind, though — if it's not here, I'm still working on it.

                                    Nyrufa:
                                    Four options for "the pack goes to war tonight" if phrased poetically:

                                    "Tonight we bloody our fangs!": Khilaske shakurdum kuk suthadu!
                                    "Tonight we tear throat!": Khilaske shakurdum burdu keli!
                                    "Tonight the Moon turns red!": Khilaske Iduth suthadu!
                                    "Tonight is a night of rattling howls!": Khilaske kal kuluskirkhil!

                                    "The pack goes to war tonight.": "Urdum ina hafaldu khilase."

                                    "You are beneath my notice.": "Nuku kal igim thirikdu ina ku." (lit. "You fall beneath my gaze.")

                                    "You cannot stop my vengeance now!": "Nuku nuhalithakase ku nihhina-kakana!" (lit. "You cannot stop now my call for justice!")

                                    Dirt Scratchers: Sagardur-Karam ("dirt-child birds")


                                    FallenEco:
                                    Cursed Dance of the Rulers: Azithanir Elum ("the song and dance/the play of the cursed kings")

                                    Mekhathakkan

                                                While I'm working on a couple more translations, here are some new words featured in recent translations that I've yet to elaborate on:

                                                bhar: To forge something, or to fire something in a kiln
                                                bhur: Brightness, glow, spreading light
                                                bhurudh: Deep; to have depth
                                                dumuskir: A whisper or the act of whispering (lit. "breezy-voice")
                                                gi'endhu: To step; to move
                                                igim: To gaze; to witness; to view
                                                min: Twin, dual, double; sameness or a copy
                                                nihhina-kakana: Revenge, vengeance (lit. "a cry for justice")
                                                sharingi: A swarm; a horde; an undulating mass
                                                shaku: We (possessive form, "ours", is "shakag")
                                                shanuku: They (possessive form, "theirs", is "shanukag")
                                                zalag: Shine, brightness, glow; concentrated light, often said of intense light or sunlight — more universal than and directly related to the more lunar-oriented zalah

                                                    Here are some astrological terms for you guys to flavour your games with. I'm still working on other translations, this is just to work out a few translating muscles that have been stagnating in my brain — I wanted to poetically challenge myself:

                                                    Aries:

                                                    - The Ram (English Name): Ulkudhughal (lit. "The Sky Ram")
                                                    - The Labourer (Babylonian Name): Ulke-Lukunka (lit. "The Hireling of the Heavens"), or Anagarrath ("The Slave of Heaven") to the Pure


                                                    Taurus

                                                    - The Bull (English and Babylonian Name): Malanabis (lit. "The Bull Made of Stars")


                                                    Gemini

                                                    - The Twins (English Name): Mindur-Ina-Malan (lit. "The Twin Children In the Stars")
                                                    - The Great Shepherd and Twins (Babylonian Name): Ur-Ina-Eren (lit. "The Wolf Amongst the Herd")


                                                    Cancer:

                                                    - The Crab (English Name): Kashumfin (lit. "The Great Crab")
                                                    - The Tortoise (Babylonian Name): Kanifuna (lit. "The Great Tortoise")


                                                    Leo

                                                    - The Lion (English Name): Nirsua (lit. "The Cat-King")
                                                    - The Great Dog (Babylonian Name): Iurhir-Ina-Usala Nuth (lit. "The Sleeping Dog")


                                                    Virgo:

                                                    - The Virgin (English Name): Guthirim (lit. "Purest Maiden")
                                                    - The Furrow (Babylonian Name): Amahadum (lit. "Mother's Den")


                                                    Libra:

                                                    - The Scales (English and Babylonian Name): Uthanga-Ladul (lit. "Helios's Balancer" [the scales were sacred to the Sumerian sun god Shamash])
                                                    - The Claws of the Scorpion (Purely Babylonian Name): Suhu-Lalazagsag-Umfin (lit. "Scorpion's [Pain-Tails'] Claws")


                                                    Scorpio:

                                                    - The Scorpion (English and Sumerian Name): Suhu-Lalazagsi (lit. "Pain-Tail")


                                                    Sagittarius:

                                                    - The Archer (English Name): Sharmalanag-Ti (lit. "Arrow of the Many Stars")
                                                    - The Chief Ancestor/The God Nergal (Sumerian/Babylonian Name): Urfarahag-Kuunag (lit. "The Shadow Cast by Urfarah")


                                                    Capricorn:

                                                    - The Goat-Fish (English/Babylonian Name): Isibmas (lit. "The Sacrificial Priest-Goat" [Capricorn was used to mark the beginning of the winter solstice before the stars drifted])


                                                    Aquarius:

                                                    - The Water-Bearer (English Name): Kakilludu (lit. "Bringer of Water")
                                                    - The God Ea (Babylonian Name): Namsillu-Nir (lit. "Lord of Watery Deaths" [Aquarius was negatively associated with dangerous flooding, and the FT name is...less flattering than the human one])


                                                    Pisces:

                                                    - The Fishes (English Name): Minbu (lit. "The Twin Fish")
                                                    - The Stag/The Great Swallow/The Tails (Babylonian Names [Pisces as we know it was originally part of a bunch of other constellations]: Shul (lit. "The One of Many" [referring to how Pisces has changed so much in its definition; to the werewolves, Pisces appears as a mercurial, almost Magath-like spirit])

                                                            Okay, so I had some oooooold translations that never got done, and some stuff that has to be redefined now that spirits have dev-made names. If someone could contact Seraph Kitty on here and tell them their translation is done, that would be grand:

                                                            Redefined:
                                                            athalim: A group of spirits in service to a totem or a group of shapeshifters or mages — though they may not be the group's patrons, they have some sort of loyalty or ties to the group, and do things for them



                                                            For Seraph Kitty:
                                                            ram: udhughal (if not castrated)
                                                            sheep: ulakhar (for ewes, lambs and castrated sheep)
                                                            steadfast, loyal, devoted: zidh
                                                            harthakhdul: world-walker, world-walking one; nomad, worldly traveller — "Gurihathakhdul" refers more to someone who's extensively travelled the physical world
                                                            Hisithakhdul: one who has extensively travelled the Shadow, or roams it nomadically
                                                            mi'imuth: Biracial, bicultural — a mix of two different parentages from things such as different ethnicities or clans
                                                            Hisimuth: "Shadow-blooded", i.e. someone with spiritual heritage
                                                            uzuhith: A spirit in a physical, flesh-like form, such as a lust spirit taking the form of a handsome bachelor; plural is "uzuhithim"



                                                            More Elaborated Terms:
                                                            lukanka: hireling, servant, labourer
                                                            mas: a goat; a sacrificial animal
                                                            suhu-lalazagsi: scorpion (lit. "pain-tail")
                                                            kuunag: a shadow, in reference to a shadow cast by an object or a being; this word inherently carries a definite article's specification of meaning, so something like ur kuunag means "the wolf's shadow", instead of ur ki'unag, which means more along the lines of "shadow from a wolf" or "wolfish shadow"
