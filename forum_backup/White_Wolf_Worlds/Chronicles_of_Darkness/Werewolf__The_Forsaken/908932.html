              Okay, I for staters how much 2e knowledge do you have? Have you checked out some of the NPCs in the 2e book (in the back sections)?

              Also, if you are looking for a physical confrontation be wary. Werewolves got a huge boost from 1e, Gauru form in particular is a terror, even a group of physically inclined Vampires would struggle against a newly changed Werewolf in war form. So if you were picturing a down and dirty fight to the death sort of battle, you might want to reconsider.

              As for Clash of Wills, that's a Storyteller call, but an easy one to make. If two magical powers conflict, such as a Gift that detects enemies and Obfuscate, that's a Clash of Wills. If two characters attempt to dominate the same subject and give them conflicting commands, that's a Clash of Wills. For the most part these situations are pretty obvious when they arise, and the handing is very straight forward. If magic conflicts with magic, Clash of Wills.

                                  I just wrote this up real quick. You said 10-20 so I just went with 15. As has been said, attempts to deter or alter a werewolf's behaviour during Kuruth suffer twice their Primal Urge as a penalty. This is in addition to their normal resistance roll. Garou in general is difficult to deal with. Enemies lose their skill bonus to defense and the Werewolf regenerates all damage per turn (as well as +5 health and +3 stealth, and +2 weapon modifier and initiating grapples after dealing damage). I avoided a bunch of spirit stuff because Vampires just don't really have any ways to handle that. Like, any at all. So your group probably isn't prepared for it. I mostly just went with renown because powers are fun, but might have over did it. I also didn't bother finishing the totem because I got lazy and it ideally shouldn't actually show up except perhaps when using Totemic Empowerment.

                                  I went with a more Urban scout/infiltrator type than the traditional sneaky wolf. The idea is that the scout can use his gifts to change his gender, his face/body or even appear as a vampire. He can unlock any doors and appear as anyone, essentially. He can absorb information directly from any store of knowledge, from a library to a computer, can learn the names and social merits of anyone he comes across. So he is the infiltrator and information gatherer. He is also difficult to pin down, due to Hit and Run, Exit Strategy and his Warding gifts.

                                  The Warrior is pretty straightforward. He fights and kills. He's pretty strong so it's unwise to take him on directly. In Garou form he's likely unkillable by any of the group, so indirect tactics and baits are better options. He can increase his strength and damage, reduce damage, and double his speed to chase down anything he comes across. He can tear through most structures with his claws. In Death Rage he is The Juggernaut.

                                  The Elder can use Reflected Facets to double up on Berserker's Might if necessary, can improve the entire pack's initiative to the highest roller's, can predict in vague terms impending doom, as well as various other insight gifts to plan or outmaneuver enemies.                                   If he succeeds at resisting any Mental Disciplines, the entire pack also succeeds at it.He is also always aware of his packmate's general location and emotional state, and can speak to them telepathically. They are really dangerous to fight as a group, partially because of him. He's also no slouch at fighting, especially in Garou form.

                                  The trick is to separate them somehow. If they lose any one member, they become woefully unprepared for an assault of some kind. Using human allies, the police or other political or social influence is likely to be pretty effective as well. Dominating a member on his own might also work. They don't really have a lot of defenses for obfuscate either. I may have made them too effective, I don't know.

                                  If you're rushed for time, I'd focus on understanding Death Rage, the 5 forms and their uses, and the gifts and rites the antagonists use. Again, I avoided spirit magic largely because it's just hard to deal with.

                                  Also, make sure to look at the Ivory Claw Sacred Hunt because it's relevant and probably useful for tracking down vampires
