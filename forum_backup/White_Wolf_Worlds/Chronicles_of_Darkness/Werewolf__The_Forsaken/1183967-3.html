                                                Originally posted by Gryphon's Feather View Post
                                                Centipedes are considered a symbol of impurity in some cultures.
                                                Yup. There’s at least one Korean folk tale/ legend where a gigantic demon-centipede takes the role of the dragon who terrorizes a village and demands virgin maiden sacrifices. One of the village girls finds a talking toad, feeding and taking care of it. When the girl is picked as the next sacrifice, the toad stands up to the centipede, locking itself and the monster in the latter’s cave, and engages in a battle where they try to out-poison each other. When dawn comes and the villagers visit the cave, they find that both have perished, and they make a statue of the toad to remember him by.

                                                Another piece of lore states that centipede poison is a bane to dragons, because it melts their bones. Which is ironic, because centipedes are one of the types of animals that can ascend to dragon-hood via enlightenment.
