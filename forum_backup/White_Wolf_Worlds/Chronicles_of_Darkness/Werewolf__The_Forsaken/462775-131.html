                    I'm not sure where I had read it (or if I had read it), but I have a recollection that each level of Primal Urge would lessen Toxicity levels by 1. Beer and wine are considered level 3 and "rubbing alcohol" (which I assume stands for pure 100% alcohol) is level 5.

                    So Primal Urge 5 werewolf could drink pure alcohol without ever getting drunk.

                    If anyone remembers if there was a rule about this, please let us know.

                    In my game, my players still wanted a way to get drunk so I had the idea that mixing worse poisons with the alcohol would prevent the werewolf body from clearing the alcohol before it deals with the worse poison. That way you could at least for a little while still get the "benefits" of alcohol even as a higher PU werewolf. They were sitting at a table with Blood Talons that were putting stuff like wolfsbane or snake venom into their own drinks.

                    I think most snake venom is not actually that dangerous to drink, but I liked the visual of it.

                            Cainite A piece of unsolicited trivia. "Rubbing alcohol" can refer to either isopropyl alcohol, which is not fit for human consumption, or ethanol with toxic compounds purposefully mixed in. I assume pure ethanol would have a toxicity of 3 or 4.

                            Funnily enough, when it comes to disinfection, the optimum concentration of ethanol is about 70%, not more. Higher concentrations cause pathogen proteins to denature so hard they create a barrier against further penetration of the disinfectant.
