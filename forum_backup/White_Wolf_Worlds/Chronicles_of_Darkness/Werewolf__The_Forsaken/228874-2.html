                        Healing all bashing and Primal Urge lethal actually wouldn't make much of a difference in your example. The Gauru would still heal the 13 bashing and 1 lethal per round. I don't think any of the game lines are balanced around the assumption the players are in plate or riot gear. If the Storyteller gives the player magic full plate and situations where they can use it, then they shouldn't be surprised that it is hard to hurt them during some of those situations.

                        Armor and full regeneration are strong, but there are plenty of ways to still make the fight interesting. Presumably the demons have Numina or Influences that can tilt the odds in there favor. If not, why even bother playing things out? Dozens of identical spirits that have no special abilities seems like the kind of fodder that Down&Dirty combat is made for, especially given that the designers specifically mention Down&Dirty fighting against equal or lower ranked spirits in the Gauru rules preview. Let's assume though that we really want to play out a fight involving perfectly generic low-ranked Maeljin foot soldiers and we want the demons to win, force a retreat, or at least expend some of the Gauru's resources before the main fight. The Gauru is probably only rolling around 11 to 13 dice without spending Essence or Willpower. Even a Rank 1 Spirits can reach defense 8 if they buff their attributes. The Gauru probably eats one in round one. After that, the Maeljin can just dodge until war form runs out, spending willpower on the contested dodge roll. Why bog down the game doing 15-49 attack rolls a turn that you don't think matter when you can just declare they're dodging, let the player whiff for 8 attacks and then they're back to Dalu or Ushul for the rest of the fight? If the Rahu is soloing the combat like the example suggests it would only take a few minutes of table time to run out the clock. Once they're back down to normal werewolf regeneration, I think the player would feel differently about all of the nickel and dime damage coming their way.

                        Death Rage duration is based on Primal Urge. It starts at around 10 minutes and reaches 12 hours at Primal Urge 10. After that, the Uratha "collapses" in Hishu form. If a low Primal Urge werewolf is dropped into enemy territory and then Death Rages, there's a good chance that at least one enemy managed to run or hide long enough to kill the kamikaze berserker once the rage ends.
