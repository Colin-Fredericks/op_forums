                            Originally posted by Ipergigio View Post
                            Hi guys!
                            Having a Lune as totem is really so bad? And It affects the game mechanics, or the potential madness is just narrative stuff?
                            The pack's totem has a deep spiritual connection with a pack - to the point that pack members gain its ban. Totems will also influence the pack as much as any other member - either directly by stating their demands, or indirectly using their influences. For this reason packs need to be careful in their choice of totem, and taking a spirit of madness as a totem is just a bad idea.

                            The book doesn't give any mechanics for how exactly a Lune totem drives packs mad but it isn't hard to imagine. The totem's ban and aspiration would change with the phases of the Moon, spiritually pulling the pack this way and that. Uratha vulnerable to frenzying at the sight of their Auspice Moon might find themselves frenzying all the time, even if they keep their harmony in check. And the Lune is certainly going to extend its maddening influence over the mortals and wolf-blooded in the pack, perhaps poking and prodding them to see if it can cause them to change into full children of Luna.

                            If you were going to run a game where the Pack's totem is a Lune it might be possible, but to do it justice I think you'd need to be very liberal with the madness condition. All the players should find their characters repeatedly afflicted by madness due to the Lune's changing whims.
