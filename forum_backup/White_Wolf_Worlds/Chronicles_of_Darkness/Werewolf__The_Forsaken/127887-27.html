    sag: Front, forward, forward-facing
    sag thakkan: Front door, front gate
    u'asgu: stairs, staircase
    sag-u'asgu: front stoop
    afad: a small shelter such as an open tent; a porch or gazebo
    hirmak: a furnace or kiln; an oven
    umak: to blow, to puff
    saganumak: a furnace for heating; a heater (lit. "blows heat")
    assudunfath: a breaker or fuse (lit. "power-breaker")
    guzluh: a box, a container
    assudunfath guzluh: fuse box
    zummurigsu: a gable; a joining part of a roof
    maar garum: a place where wagons or other vehicles are kept (more broad than ramamaar garum)
    maar thakkan: the garage door (lit. "gate to the wagons"); a more car-oriented term is ramamaar thakkan
    luzukeshla: a skeleton key; an automatic door opener such as a garage door opener
    zurum: waste, litter; pee, poop, droppings
    zurukurum: a garbage can, a dustbin
    unuhzar lalarak: a shed in a garden or yard, usually used to keep tools and yard equipment in
    anfahirfathra: a girder; steel beams (lit. "skeleton of iron/metal")
    nisigarum: greenhouse
    zummur ahlalu: the gutters, the raincatchers, the piping around the sides of a roof
    gindu: hallway, corridor
    eshla: a key
    bakar: a hall or public place like one; a gathering place

    "Anna'u": The spirit version of "guth ni", which means "cool", "rad", or "awesome". It literally means "I gift you with my approval". "Guth ni" is more werewolf/predatory shapeshifter phrasing.

                I don't think I've translated the names of all the derangements from 1e oWoD. I think I'll do that now; please let me know, guys, if any of these give the wrong message or seem politically incorrect.

                tabh: a repetitive behaviour; a compulsion; a tic
                isu: to be urged, to be compelled; to be obsessed, an obsession
                Yagekentabh: The Abyssal Compulsion derangement, in which one is compelled into learning more about the Abyss and its secrets
                hirihdin-estha: The Animalistic Dependency derangement from Vampire, in which one is dependent on Animalism or seeing animals nearby to use it on (lit. "animal-bound")
                (Anxiety has already been translated)
                skir-izu: Aphasia, the loss of the ability to speak or understand speech (lit. "stolen of speech")
                negargar: to circle around; to avoid; the Avoidance Derangement, in which one avoids a place, person or thing due to a past trauma or something similar
                sa'ath: to trap or cage an animal; to snare an animal or one's prey; the Banish the Beast Derangement from Changing Breeds, in which a shapeshifter represses their bestial nature so thoroughly, it is very difficult to shapeshift into it
                hirihdinirih: zoophobia; the Beast Fears derangement
                muthtabh: the Blood Fascination Derangement, in which one is compelled or drawn towards images of blood or blood itself
                sa'ath-nirih: the Cage Shock Derangement, in which a shapeshifter begins losing control if indoors or otherwise confined (lit. "trap fear")
                sakhskir: an emotion, feeling (lit. "heart-speech")
                sakhskir-tuf: the Cataplexy Derangement, in which one collapses (though remains conscious) after laughter or a strong emotion (lit. "struck by emotion")
                agzu: to be dangerous, to be a threat; a danger, a threat
                agzigitabh: the Compulsive-Aggressive Disorder Derangement, in which everyone is seen as a threat
                garrmal: the Decadence Derangement, in which a character loses their sense of excess when it comes to extreme decorum, triggered by a victory in the social arena or a humiliating defeat
                thimasafru: Delusional, hallucinating; delusions, hallucinations; to delude oneself (lit. "lying [to the sufferer] thoughts or visions because of tricked senses")
                isuthimasafru: a delusional obsession, such as those found in certain mental health disorders; the Delusional Obsession derangement
                luldudam: the Delusional Witness derangement, in which one justifies (or sometimes hallucinates) something when witnessing/having witnessed supernatural phenomena in order to fit it into their belief system (lit. "lying to believe")
                gidumm: to bury, to roll up, to put away
                thima-gidumm: The Repression Derangement; to repress, to compartmentalize

                More to come.

                        More Derangements, and a few other words that are not Derangements, but things I forgot to add earlier.

                        afu: old age; elderly; an old thing
                        afutabh: the Degenerative Fixation Derangement, in one fixates negatively on signs of aging; for Ghouls, it's a fear of aging once the blood bond wears off, which can lead to begging and harassing one's master for more
                        maran: Depression; the Depression Derangement (lit. "of greatest sadness")
                        dafa: Erythema, a redness or rash caused by infection, disease, etc. such as the "bullseye mark" of Lyme's disease; for vampires spending blood to look alive around others, it's zisu (lit. "life-skin")
                        aselulu: a habitat; an ecosystem
                        estha-kiliteg: Demiromantic
                        estha-ene: Demisexual (lit. "finds pleasure in those they feel a bond to")
                        farsekhae: Of undecided or "curious" gender orientation
                        farsekh-ene: Of undecided or "curious" romantic orientation
                        farsekh-kiliteg: Of undecided or "curious" sexual orientation
                        garin: a temple; a church; a place of worship
                        agmamu: the Feral Antics Derangement, in which a shapeshifter acts like a wild animal with little human behaviour or speech (lit. "beast-acting")
                        sa'ath-uth: the Feral Frenzy Derangement, in which one goes feral or into a rage-like state if they feel confined and until they "escape" (lit. "rage of the trap")
                        guzbhu: a fetish of the sexual kind
                        namguzbhu: the Fetishism Derangement
                        emmu: to abuse; abuse
                        emmul: an abuser; one who abuses
                        hirihdin emmu: animal abuse
                        zatharzemmul: the Filthy Brutes! Derangement, in a shapechanger abuses animals as a way of lashing out at themselves or their shapeshifter side
                        ta'af: a fixation; to fixate; to focus on
                        mata'af: to focus greatly; hyperfocus
                        lilta'af: to focus weakly; underfocus
                        ta'haf: the Fixation Derangement (lit. "fixates badly"); an unhealthy fixation
                        farsesu: a psychological fugue; the Fugue Derangement (lit. "fog of the self/body-fog")
                        shaeme: glossolalia; the Glossolalia Derangement
                        dunfathaf: the Goetic Fracture Derangement, in which your personal demon becomes personified and tries to manipulate you into succumbing to it (lit. "broken bad piece")
                        nirthima: delusions of grandeur; the Grandiose Delusion Derangement
                        kulu'ugigurr: to be speciesist towards humankind; the Hate of Man Derangement

                            Correcting some stuff already translated, and more Derangements.

                            - Ameni now means "a reason; an answer; an answered question"
                            - Mannu now means "a qualified person; those who are fit for a certain job; those who know what to do in a certain situation"
                            - Mu (to pilot, to ride, etc.) is now mu'u, as it turns out just plain mu means "to name, to address; a name or address".
                            - Mas (goat, sacrifical animal) is now ma'as, due to it being a homonym with the word for dreams, mas

                            And now, words.

                            mag: to be sick, to be ill; a more neutral word than gurim, and used by the Pure and spirits in general to refer to illness or madness
                            tag-sahaz: A serial killing spree; the act of serial killing
                            umamutag-sahaz: The Hunter King Derangement, in which a shapeshifter hates their animal side so much, it manifests as the serial killing and/or abuse of animals
                            zeh: to leak; to spill
                            zehvisktha: The Hypnagogic Hallucination Derangement; hypnagogic hallucination, in which one hallucinates while coming out of or going into the sleep phase. Also known as zehmas. (Lit. "leaking dreams")
                            affah: the past; past times
                            affah kusku: The Identity Erasure Derangement, in which a ghoul or vampire is compelled to destroy all evidence of their past, human life
                            haflal-sakhskir: An inferiority complex; the Inferiority Complex Derangement (lit. "feelings about being little-bad [inferior]")
                            haflal: weak; inferior
                            thalabu: to be sleepless; to have recurring trouble getting to sleep; insomnia; the Insomnia Derangement
                            ihlif: to be awake; to wake up; to awaken
                            zarru: a state of being in which a supernatural that has hibernated for a long time (such as a vampire) wakes up and mistakes people, places or events for things from the past; the Intermetamorphosis Derangement
                            dhuthmu: to argue against; to defy; to disobey
                            nirdhuthmu: the Irrational Defiance Derangement, in which one is compelled to disobey their superiors
                            nufulum: irrationality, irrational, without logic; the Irrational Derangement
                            naafutusakh: to lose compassion, to lose care for someone, to lose empathy; the Loss of Compassion Derangement
                            liltabh: the Magical Ideation Derangement, in which a vampire is compelled to seek some sort of greater intelligence or power in the surrounding environment

                                So I decided to write up some lyrics to the song "Sacrifice" from Vikings. There are no official lyrics for this song, the singer (Sharon Lyons) said that she picked random Celtic-sounding/Latin-sounding syllables. Especially with Norse Werewolves on the near horizon, I thought it would be appropriate to create an Uratha version of this.


                                Uremehir:

                                Magh da su-ah eh;
                                Si bun-gala,
                                Eh Matires.
                                Si-ah, si-la;
                                Si bun-kala,
                                Muh de.
                                Su-sulea,
                                Ase-endesh,
                                Matu eh, mati ya
                                Ezem de.

                                Magh da du-ah eh;
                                Si bun-ghala,
                                Eh Matires.
                                Si-lal, si-la;
                                Si bun-kala,
                                Si mus-tala,
                                Muh de.
                                Ase-endesh,
                                Matu eh, mati ya
                                Ezem de.


                                English:

                                It is now time to watch as the body is burnt;
                                Remember the labour of the bellows,
                                And keep watch for the Burning Ship of Our Lady.
                                It is now time to remember, and to remember to keep vigilant;
                                Remember the bellows as they must be,
                                And pour out [an offering or libation] from the cups.
                                Body of tar,
                                The priests make you a shrine now;
                                Watch over this land, [for] five burning ships
                                Carry you through this celebration [in your honour].

                                It is now a time to watch and act;
                                Remember to protect the bellows,
                                And keep watch for the Burning Ship of Our Lady.
                                Remember the little [things], and remember to keep vigilant;
                                Remember the bellows as they must be,
                                Remember the holy cry to battle,
                                And pour out [an offering or libation] from the cups.
                                Body of tar,
                                The priests make you a shrine now;
                                Watch over this land, [for] five burning ships
                                Carry you through this celebration [in your honour].
