                      There's two things to note about Binding of Locks:
                      - Remember that rites draw on spiritual power, they aren't just for using against spirits. Spirits might have ways round locked doors but lots of other prey don't.
                      - Ethan Skemp had this excellent notion about using rites to change the environs into horror-movie style situations. The series of rites that Binding of Locks was part of are an exploration down that path of thinking. That said, and as some may have noticed from the Gift Facets that Stew revealed on the blog a few weeks back, I've actually gone and put a lot of that theme into certain Gifts now. You can expect that the core Rites list will probably not go down that particular path. Can't say anything for sure at this stage, of course - first draft and all that!

                      - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
