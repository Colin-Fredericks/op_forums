                          A strix is not a spirit. It is not made of ephemera, nor does it use spirit mechanics. The rules for creating a totem wouldn't work. A strix can not become a totem any more than a vampire could.

                          An angel is close enough to a spirit, but it lacks the ability to agree to the terms. If an angel agreed to be a totem then it would fall, turn into a demon. The God Machine would have to enter into the pact for an angel, essentially. This would make the pack agents of the GM. I think you want an Exile instead.

                                      A totem provides direction and identity to a pack. It isn't a matter of whether or not the God Machine can order an angel to become a totem. The point is you have a pack taking orders from the God Machine now. With an Exile, at least you wouldn't have werewolves doing the bidding of the God Machine.

                                      You could create a Dread Power that provides whatever bonuses you wanted a totem to give, but the strix would still not be a totem. Strix don't have bans therefore the pack can't take up their "totem's" ban. It just won't work and the better question is why bother?

                                      You could literally create a spirit that looks like a strix, acts like a strix and has abilities similar to a strix (it is the same for an angel for that matter), so why make things harder on yourself by forcing something that wasn't meant to be a totem to try to take the place of a totem? Make life easy and just create a spirit by taking what you like from an angel, a strix, a ghost, or whatever. Maybe the spirit ate one of those others to take up some of the same traits and abilities. It is easier to start with a spirit than force something that is not a spirit into a cookie cutter for a spirit.
