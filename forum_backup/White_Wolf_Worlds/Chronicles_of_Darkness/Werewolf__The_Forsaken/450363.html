    Mad wolves

    03-20-2015, 09:30 AM
    I was looking at the Gifts of Inspiration, and thought "Lunatic Inspiration might be a handy one to put on a pack member as they'd be Inspired every day and at the end they'd get some message from Luna. I wonder how the Madness Condition works..."

    The Madness Condition works off of Integrity, being as it otherwise usually comes when things fail breaking points. The ST gets to take away 10 - Integrity dice from you once per chapter.
    If a Werewolf has the Madness Condition (say, by having that Gift Facet used on her), do you just apply a 10 - Harmony modifier? That seems a little odd as 5 is supposed to be the sweet spot for werewolves. I guess it maybe makes a little sense as 10 Harmony represents someone who has a persistently repressed sense of the supernatural. But then again, they're really messed up people that will kill their neighborhood over tiny infractions like being there when the full moon rises...

    Thoughts?

                      You could do:

                      Harmony 5 = Integrity 10.
                      Harmony 4/6 = Integrity 9.
                      Harmony 3/7 = Integrity 8.
                      Harmony 2/8 = Integrity 7.
                      Harmony 1/9 = Integrity 6.
                      Harmony 0/10 = Integrity 5.

                      Or lower it by 2 each instead of 1. Or some other way. I would honestly just use it straight and say that the closer you are to the Spirit, the more prone to are to losses in human logic (which is what Madness is, sort of). And Harmony 5 may be a sweet spot for wolves, but this could just be one of the downsides of being balanced: less resistance to things that attack Integrity than someone to cleaves to the Flesh.
