                                It's been a while since I've posted on this thread, but it seems to have moved on, which is good for the discussion on what we would like to see!

                                Regarding the "PC status" for Claimed/Bale Hounds/Pure? Please leave it out (by default) of the core Idigams Chronicles. That way we get extra words on concentrating the (frankly) brilliant game line back into a single core book. If at the end there's space for that sort of stuff, then yeah that'll be good. Although writing this has mad me realise that what I mean to say about PC status would be that enough mechanics to allow the GM to build interesting antagonists with a tool-set to give them 'dread powers'. More word count would be able to add more to the mechanics and information. I feel that a word-set that includes rules and advice on running those guys as full PCs can be in another book. I'm sure the Idigam Chronicles is going to sell, and that we'll have more werewolf books in the future. There's space in that future to hit on stuff like this, that isn't that relevant to a core book that is putting all the gameline's development over the years back in to book.

                                Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post
                                I should note that Claimed was written the way it is in GMC to set up Idigam Chronicle using Claimed as bad guys. GMC has how spirits Claim victims, but we didn't have room for any Dread Powers. So we left them for future books to figure out.
                                IIRC you wrote the spirit rules for GMC. Does this mean you'll be the one behind this sort of thing for Idigam? If so, that would be grand.

                                Concerning Tatterdemalion King's points, first a caveat; I have no interest in your game, in not going to play in it, and it isn't (from what I've seen) the Forsaken that gt me into the line in the first place. So I'm not making coments on that game (I would have no right to, you haven't asked me for feedback :P ). So the following comments touch on how I feel to Werewolf the Forsaken.




                                Originally posted by Tatterdemalion King View Post
                                Divesting renown: not as is, not at all. Know how me and the other changed it? We stop using the term renown. What it was is covered by Honorary Rank and Tribal Status. It's a terminology shift, really the only real thing done is not rail-roading gameplay by what was previously required to go up in it, and taking it out as a hard power cap. In mechanical terms? It's just covered by other pre-existing areas. I just don't see much use in the term anymore, it's a redundancy.
                                The trick with Renown is, it is quite obviously magical Status. I'm not even going to try to defend that, because it's obvious!

                                That said, Renown should stay. Werewolves in nWoD should have their status as a magical power because it is *entirely thematic* that a group of Tribal beings have some sort of status built from spiritual power. I think the problem with renown at the moment is that it might not be clear enough, or obviously thematic enough. The fact that it could very easily be houseruled to be used as status should be used in conjunction with it's supernatural advantages. This is a stat that belongs in Werewolf, regardless whether this was in Apocolypse (which I couldn't care less about, and never impacted by initial buy in to Forsaken).



                                Originally posted by Tatterdemalion King View Post
                                Rites? To each their own there. I'm not a fan of a non-nonsensical system that's predicated on "it's anything, and everything that doesn't fit the 1-5 list. Also, stuff" so instead, there are alternate Instinct levels instead of a grab-bag. Seems clean. The focus of what rites are and what they do are tighter; Essence/Shadow manipulation, externalizing the influence of a werewolf on it's spiritual environment where instincts are more the internal. That's not an excision; that's a change of focus and shuffling of what goes where. Also, some rites make more sense as Numina anyhow (see the rite of healing. Healing Numina anyone?), which keeps those aspects -still- accessible.
                                It does someties feel that way, doesn't it? Which is a shame. Look at how Geist does ceremonies (if you haven't, do - Geist is a lovely line, tat like werewolf, needs some TLC), they're thematic as hell. I always liked the rituals that were incredibly cultural, wether it was the funeral rite or the rite of the last stand (or whatever it's called, my bookshelves aaaall the way across the room). Rites rewritten fluff wise (mechanically wise, they're okay as far as I'm concerned, they just need to be added to the new extended actions paradigm, a la blood sorcery in Blood and Smoke.

                                The way that the supernatrual powers (specifically Gifts and Rites) are thematic (notmechanically), and how this is written, could benefit a lot (IMO, remember) from looking how Geist did it. For all it faults, Geist feelslike the game it's selling. Some bits of Forsaken are a bit old, and not the tightest thematically. That said, there are awesomely thematic stuff out there, just look at the Tribes and Auspices books.


                                Originally posted by Tatterdemalion King View Post
                                Lunacy? Some mass human group debuffer? Integrity already covers this in part with some of the Breaking Points it has with humans. The rest is wrapped into the Predatory Aura and treated as something less ridiculously potent to keep humans a legitimate problem, but one that requires -action on the part of the werewolf- to fully exploit, just like with vampires. It's an edge. Want people to take off much more easily? Hit them with the Flight aspect of the Predatory Aura. Want to even more fucking scary? Instincts, Merits, more... I just don't need it overwhelmingly like it was, and it was something my players thought was goofy before. As is the default idea is they travel in packs; that's multiple people capable of whammying multiple others with the aura, those that -don't- do what most humans presented with a situation like that in the wod does: run like hell, rationalize or ignore it.
                                I agree that the way Lunacy works should be updated now that we've seen how well the new predatory aura works, and how conditions get into the game.

                                But then again, I would be very surprised if the forum users aren't preaching to the choir when they wink-wink-nudge-nudge this to the developers.


                                Originally posted by Tatterdemalion King View Post
                                The real issue isn't that I'm taking stuff -out- of the game, or my co-writer (actually, we're intending to add -more-); it's that it's different enough from the prior iterations that you don't like it due to the level of change present. And as I've said before, brace for impact; the level of differences you've cited as desiring before would barely make for the GMC rules updates and may as well be an errata. I'm expecting heavy changes. In fact, I know heavy changes are coming down the pipe on par with Blood and Smoke at the least.
                                The thing is, I don't think Blood and Smoke changed much. My opinion is that it's still very much Requiem. Hell, the only affect it has had on the Requiem game I'm in right now is that the mechanics are far more fun to play around with. I would be very surprised (and, I have to admit disappointed, which may heavily affect my opinion) if Forsaken changed as much as you seem to expect. Then again, I'm sure we are both heavily biased towards what we want out of the game - I just think you want a game that's like Forsaken, but not the mainstream Forsaken that the line represents.

                                Different opinions, but I'm hating on you - we can disagree amiably of course.





                                I just wished we would cut ALL the passive aggressive from everyone. It doesn't help, it massively derails the thread and won't (and never has) added to the discussion. It just kills the thread for a week or so, until someone comes back and goes, "um guys? I had this thought about what I would like...." and it starts over again. It's a tad frustrating to have to scroll though (which I want to, I love Forsaken.

                                *Looks back at the post*. Blimely, that the largest post I've ever done O.o
