                Personally, I don't incorporate arguments from 2004 about changes made between owod and nwod, I just use what's in the modern books. So Defense is "moving out of the way while trying to hit someone else at the same time" per the core WOD book. Weaponry Dodge, Brawling Dodge, and later Athletics Dodge altered the Dodge mechanic. The GMC Defense trait is identical to nWOD's Athletics Dodge: You add your Athletics score to your Defense, which was the lower of Wits/Dexterity, and subtract that from the incoming attack roll, and this meant a greater ability to *move out of the way*, nothing about being tougher.

                Since we're moving into yet another set of rules with GMC, I really think we should move any arguments and bases of rules into at least nWOD and throwing out anything from oWOD in these arguments. It's more than enough to keep 1 version back, which goes back 10 years, rather than everyone being expected to know internet arguments/explanations from 10+ years ago.

                        Truthfully I'm not really sold on the defense against guns thing either, just for that reason. Personally I'd prefer them to gain armor against everything but silver weaponry, representing the wall of hyper dense muscle you'd now have to get through in order to really harm them. I would gladly accept using the higher of wits and dex in order to simulate animal like reflexes, but I don't really care much applying defense against bullets. I get what they're going for but I can't help but wonder if there's a better way to go about it. I'll think on it a bit more.

                        Though slightly off topic I do want to point out that I feel that caving into pressure and changing the claws from being supernatural to mundane was nothing but a detriment to the werewolf line as a whole. This is more of a thematic complaint than a mechanical one, because the damage type wasn't really mechanically important anyways, but I feel it is extremely awkward for only a portion of a werewolves natural weaponry to be considered supernatural. If you're going to give, at least give in a way that maintains internal consistency and have neither teeth nor claws be supernatural. While I still feel that thematically the line would be best off with both claws and teeth being considered supernatural weaponry, if you can't do that then don't set it in some weird half state, just let it be one way or the other.
