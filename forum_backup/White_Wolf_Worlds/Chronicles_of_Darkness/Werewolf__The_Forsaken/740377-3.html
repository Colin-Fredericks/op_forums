                            Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post
                            If anyone has critique - especially for the last Facet - I would deeply appreciate it.
                            My initial impression is that the Gift name doesn't really match the content. It doesn't feel very... dream-related, symbolically or practically.

                            Waking Visions (o)
                            Excessively vague. The dice bonus is nice, albeit limited, but gaining 'insights' could either be incredibly powerful or utterly useless depending on what insights *are*.​ Needs more definition as to what info etc it can convey on threats and weaknesses, and ideally needs to avoid crossover with existing Facets on that front.

                            Lover's Voice (oo)
                            Mimicking a voice seems a pretty minor power, doesn't feel like something for a 'Dreaming Moon' list and doesn't do a *lot* towards making the Cahalith a better hunter; I mean, it does a bit, but it's very underwhelming.

                            [B]Hunter's Dream (ooo)
                            Again, doesn't feel like it matches 'Dreaming Moon'. Otherwise not too bad power-wise but I'd need to double-check Steadfast and Inspired. Also sometimes you *want* to suffer breaking points on the hunt since they're bringing you closer to balance rather than further away.

                            Aria of the Soul (oooo)
                            I am really really wary of any power that is basically 'full Willpower refill', which is what this is. It takes time and requires the target to pass some trivial rolls to stay conscious (unless the werewolf is an utter weakling and even then doesn't want to shift to, say, Urshul for the extra Stamina), but that's not enough of a drawback for the capability to directly shunt Essence into Willpower as needed. It suspends Conditions but not all Conditions are necessarily internal and mental and, more importantly, some Conditions are flat-out beneficial and you don't *want* them relieved for a month.

                            Lunatic Pandora (ooooo)
                            Thematically this might work if the other powers really gave the Dreaming feel to the Dreaming Moon list, but they don't so this doesn't really convey the nightmares side of things as it has no foundation to rest on. Why can the Facet only be used once per scene? Doesn't it overlap too much with Soul Shriek and Thousand Throat Howl?

                                Here is a brief summary of what the Gibbous Gifts already cover:

                                Rank 1: Inspire to Violence, Freeze from Fear (Increased Violence for Allies and Initiative Penalties for Enemies)

                                Rank 2: Superb Warleader, Superb Intimidation (Increased Capacity to Incite Violence and Increased Capacity to Incite Fear)

                                Rank 3: Dream Hunter, Maddening Visage (Weakening Willpower from Sleeping Prey and Weakening Prey's Ability to Maintain Sanity (all during the Siskur-Dah))

                                Rank 4: Monstrous Opponent, Lingering Madness (Initiative and Emotional Penalties to Prey and Powerful Mental Conditions, Fugue and Amnesia)

                                Rank 5: Ultimate Trapper, Soul Eater (Diminishes Prey's Capacity to Escape and Removes the Soul, damning the prey to certain madness)
                                This might help measuring which powers overlap. Also, I believe we should mostly depart from violence oriented powers, since there are many. To be unique, the Dream Gift seems to need more subtle facets.

                                                Here's my revision of the first Dreaming Moon Facet.

                                                -=-

                                                Waking Visions (o)
                                                Cost: None (or 1 Essence)
                                                Duration: Permanent
                                                Those who first Changed under the Dreaming Moon are frequently struck with vivid dreams. By developing this Facet, the Cahalith becomes more attuned to her subconscious, receiving brief visions which warn her of impending danger. She may add his Glory Renown to all perception rolls, and to rolls made to detect ambushes and traps. If he chooses to spend Essence and roll the Facet's dice pool, the werewolf can extend her foresight, allowing her to divine impending events within a period of [Glory x4] hours.

                                                Dice Pool: Wits + Empathy + Glory

                                                Dramatic Failure: The player asks a question as though she had rolled a success; the Storyteller should either give false information, or present a warped version of the truth.

                                                Failure: The Cahalith's visions are too cryptic to decipher. She does not gain any information, though he may try again at a cumulative -1 penalty (to a maximum of -5).

                                                Success: The player can ask one question from the Storyteller. The Storyteller’s answer should include the imagery conveyed by the visions.

                                                Exceptional Success: As a success, but the Cahalith automatically receives a vision relevant to the events he is experiencing. The Storyteller must reveal something relevant to the story, which the player can use to pose his question.

                                                -=-

                                                Alternatively, I was thinking of making the Facet allow for the player to ask a number of questions equal to her Glory Renown. And maybe extend the temporal range of the power to within a day, or even a week or (lunar) month? Thoughts?

                                                            How about this?

                                                            -=-

                                                            Lapse of Faith [Cunning]
                                                            Cost:                                                             1 Essence
                                                            Dice Pool: Presence + Expression + Cunning vs. Resolve + Primal Urge
                                                            Action: Reflexive and Contested
                                                            Duration: Scene
                                                            The werewolf can force a spiritual compulsion that hinders her prey's attempts at fighting back against him. It can potentially even force him to turn on those he's fighting alongside.

                                                            Dramatic Failure: The Facet's energies rebound on the werewolf. He cannot attack anyone without a successful Resolve + Composure roll for the remainder of the scene.

                                                            Failure: The Facet has no effect.

                                                            Success: For the duration of the Facet, the victim is compelled to not attack the werewolf or any of his allies. He suffers the Demoralized Condition.

                                                            Exceptional Success: The victim now feels compelled to aid the werewolf, even to the point of attacking his family, friends or pack. For the duration of the Facet, he now gains the Cowed Condition, as well as the Obsession Condition towards her.
