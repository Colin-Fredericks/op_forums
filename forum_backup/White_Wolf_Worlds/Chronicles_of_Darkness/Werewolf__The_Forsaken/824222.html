          It also had pictures.



          Though I think they were representative of more powerful lunes, and it doesn't seem to depict glimmerings and other non-Auspice ones. The book itself is more informative, as it describes multiple different forms they could take. Helions, too, come in a variety of types, with Sun Scholars appearing 'as radiant torches, candles, and even incandescent bulbs,' but also 'birds with impossibly bright plumage or the visage of a cloaked hermit bearing a lantern from which emanates a blindingly bright glow.' While searings 'often take the form of blazing, animated weapons, burning eternally with a blinding intensity.' And other powerful and bright imagery.

          I think other pictures are scattered throughout the books.

                  I wish I could take the credit.

                  Depending on your interest, it could be a good get. The Predators book has descriptions and stat-blocks for 61 different spirits, including the lunes and helions, as well as building blocks for the primary hosts, azlu and beshilu, with write ups for various types of them, as well as write-ups for one each of the locust, crow and snake hosts. And the chapter on urged and claimed really has a lot of information about those, and descriptions and stat-blocks for pre-made urged, ridden and claimed. It's a pretty good resource for Werewolf, especially since you can get it cheap on Amazon but obviously if all you want is lune descriptions it's an easy choice to avoid.

                  The reason I mention is because while spirits have changed how they interact with the different worlds in the shift to second edition, their stat blocks are pretty much interchangeable.
