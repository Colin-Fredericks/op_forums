    Today's game, while a day late due to toddler issues yesterday, was very good.

    The pack have roughed up drug dealers, been given some 'helpful' information by an Amazigh Ivory Claw (who really disapproves of a criminal organisation they are clashing with because Magrebhi and white French mix in its ranks! Gasp!), encountered a poor bastard Ghost Wolf trying to nab Essence from their Locus who is infested with Ukusgualu, met an actual Ukusgualu and probably killed it, and ended up with the Ghost Wolf strapped to a medical table in a criminal back street surgery, slicing him open to yank the writhing lampreys out from his guts. The Ghost Wolf, who has wildly imbalanced Harmony, managed to pass double figures of Death Rage checks despite having a Willpower dice pool of 4! They also did their first proper Sacred Hunt rite (targeting the Ghost Wolf) and used the new tracking rules to great effect (when trying to track the Ghost Wolf down to his riverside lair).

    End result of all that included a pretty massive pile of Beats - 29 in the pot from this session. Everyone's now earned 3 Experiences.

    From this, I learned that the Sacred Hunt don't necessarily need a death at the end of the hunt...

    This opens up very interesting possibilities in my head right now. Not sure why I was thinking that...

                                    Originally posted by Aiden View Post
                                    Yes, speaking of blending in: How have you handled the fact that Urhan are wolves, and wolves are not generally a sight you see in a city? Do people take notice of this, or...? How's that working out for you?
                                    So far, they've been running with having a Hishu or human pack-member 'owner' with the Urhan 'dogs'. There are probably, what, 100 wolves in France total so 'obviously' the Urhan aren't actually wolves but just wolf-looking hounds. When they've had the Urhan split up while tracking, they've probably just been hoping that they'll get seen as feral dogs keeping to the back alleys etc.

                                    If they keep doing that, roving around in Urhan without an 'owner' with them, it may cause them problems. Lots of reports feral/ownerless dogs may get animal control paying attention - or even hapless animal charities who want to take the poor dogs into a shelter It also makes it easier for other forces to detect the pack's activity, whether Uratha or other supernatural beings that are paying attention. Finally, should things go badly tits up and the area end up getting terrorised by the pack's antics and/or by the prey they're battling, the appearance of the wolves will likely feed into the general level of tension and fear amongst the regular population, which will further feed the fear Essence bleeding into the Hisil and may well give rise to wolf-like fear spirits.

                                            In prepping for my Detroit game, I've been reading up on coyotes. In the northeast U.S. and Canada, there has been a rise in "coywolves", which are crossbreeds of coyotes, wolves, and dogs. They are larger than coyotes, hunt in packs, and are more aggressive and can take down larger prey. There was recently a news story about a woman with a farm near Detroit that raised horses for the police, who swears that a pack of large coyotes took down one of her animals. Given that my game is focused on one of the large urban prairies just east of Downtown Detroit, I intend to use this should the pack wander in Urhan. The coywolf thing is breaking news (a few recent articles on the web have brought it to public attention), so it would make sense for the media to latch onto any sighting to declare that coywolves are now hunting Detroit. Animal control response would give the Iron Masters things to deal with.

                                                We had our fourth session last night; a slightly slower Beat-count and broadly less action apart from a sudden fight with a spirit right at the very end.
