            We are the Forsaken, for we refused the flesh of our flesh. We are Forsaken, for we did not come to our Father's aid. When the world was new, when spirit and flesh were the same Great Wolf and his pack hunted. Born of Grandfather Sky and Grandmother Earth, the First Pack could not be out done, when they hunted together. Great Wolf, first born of the First Pack, knew no equal even among the other 8 of his pack. His might was such that even together now two members of the pack could out-hunt him, nor could they overpower him. The world was wild and savage, and the pack never lacked prey to hunt.

            Centuries passed, and the First Pack hunted across them. In those years of savage grace the Pack met the many spirits-in-flesh, but none more beautiful than our Mother Moon. Great Wolf courted her and sang love songs to her, but he was not the only one who desired her. Rabid Wolf (then called Spirit Wolf), Silver Wolf and Dire Wolf each sought to earn her affections in their own way. Spirit Wolf showed Moon many of the things he had learned from other spirits, working magics both strange and terrific. Yet, for all his wonders, she spurned Spirit Wolf. Silver Wolf attempted to seduce Moon with his perfect coat and ideal features. No matter how smooth his words or beautiful his coat, Moon still rejected him. Dire Wolf sung passionate songs and brought Moon the hides of his most daring kills. Despite the lavish gifts and beautiful songs, Moon denied Dire Wolf for her heart belonged to another.

            Each Wolf sang their sorrows in the dark places of the savage world, and each heard the other. They hurt and it only grew worse when they heard that Great Wolf's love song to Moon. They had been spurned for the Alpha, and their love for Moon became hate for Great Wolf. It was decided that they would lure Great Wolf to the darkest place they could find and destroy their rival. Spirit Wolf, in a righteous fury, called upon every trick and talent the spirits had showed him and would lead Great Wolf astray. Silver Wolf with simmering hate would betray Great Wolf when he needed it most. Dire wolf, his heart cold, would wait and in the darkest place, he would kill Great Wolf.

            While Great Wolf was with Moon, Rabid Wolf's spirit tricks drew them away. Silver Wolf broke in to Great Wolf's dalliance, and showed him to the dark path. Once they were in the deepest shadows, Dire Wolf and Silver Wolf attacked. However, Great Wolf was stronger than any two of the Pack, but he was not untouchable. He howled, begging for help from the Pack, but they were too far away to hear. Rabid Wolf lead the Pack further and further out, his rage twisting his features. In the Dark, those spirits whom Great Wolf had hunted but could not kill crawled from the shadows and joined in the killing.

            Not bad for a rough draft of an idea.
