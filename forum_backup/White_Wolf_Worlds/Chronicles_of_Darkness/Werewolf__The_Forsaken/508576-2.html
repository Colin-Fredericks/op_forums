I think if I had never experienced that, the main themes of Promethean wouldn't connect that strongly with me.
                    While I agree that pregnancy gone wrong is a bit more devastating then mere social ostracisation, I still think those themes might resonate all the more strongly with the people who can actually relate those experiences with real life experiences.

                    I dont think any book of the WoD ever explicitly stated who you're suposed to root for and fighting against a society that abhors your chosen lifestyle might easily prove for a very entertaining game. This is true in all gamelines. If I suddenly Awakened to a world of magic and god-like supernatural powers, I dont think I would give two shits, when some aged man in a robe came up and told me to stick to the path of Wisdom and obey some obscure set of rules set in latin or ancient greek.
