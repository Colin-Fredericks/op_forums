    She has to fight for the time she has free from the Spinner Hag's overwhelming drive. Every day is precious. Each fake life is built as a researcher or academic with as much access as she can get not just to the laboratories and equipment of cutting edge establishments, but to the people thereof and their minds. She treasures them, treats them like precious jewels to be turned and tilted in the light of discovery so that she can see what marvellous shapes shine out of their facets. Sometimes, she becomes entranced, and falls in love with them. She's looking for the parts of reality where humans have found those vibrating threads that weave through the universe and learned how to pluck at them - string theory, quantum mechanics, artificial intelligence, even weirder areas of inquiry. She's analysing her origins not as a myth made real but as something more akin to a computer program - a spiritual encoding that forces certain behaviours in the copies of the Spinner Hag's data fragments. She'll harness the ingenuity of beautiful, mad, wonderful humankind, and she'll fucking well cut herself free from the Spinner Hag's binding thread. And then she'll be free to be whatever she wants to be, just her, at last.
    Chris, this is why I love your vision for Werewolf. You keep finding ways to encode science and reality into the mythical and symbolic. It is utterly gorgeous and fascinating.

        See, my theory about the number imagery for Pangaeans is its about orders of magnitude. We have two example Pangaeans whose Ranks are known. Harvest is Rank 4 and uses a pattern with a magnitude of 101 (IE. 44). Fox is a Rank 5 Pangaean that uses a pattern with a magnitude of 102 (IE. 333). Centipede we don't have stats for, but they use the number 33 as their pattern number, which if the above holds would suggest they are Rank 4.

        Hypothetically, a Rank 3 Pangaean has a pattern with a magnitude of 100 (IE. Any whole number from 1-9), while a Rank 6 Pangaean would have a pattern with a magnitude of 103 (IE. Any four-digit number where each individual numeral is the same). If that pattern holds, then Spider is Rank 6, which seems kind of low for the Spinner Hag but still makes it possible for them to actually put up something resembling a fight against Wolf.

        I think Fox uses three as their number as a lot of their special powers are about wisdom and insight, and their Wisdom Influence has a rating of three. And I'm pretty sure that Harvest uses four either because their Hunger Influence has a rating of four, or because the parts of the world where Harvest would primarily be relevant have four seasons. And I'm definitely sure that Spider uses the number eight because Spider is Spider.

                                                        Right, have popped the E entry for the human mastermind and her trophy up on Patreon, and here's the trophy from Ariadne. Sorry about the delay!

                                                        ​And with this, the Twelve Days series is officially over!

                                                        Trophy: Hagstone
                                                        There’s rarely much to take as a trophy from the site of a Host’s defeat. All that an Uratha is let with is a carpet of vermin, a twitching mass of dying life. Handfuls of burned insects or crushed rodents are hardly an exciting prize, although werewolves don't look down on seemingly lowly trophies such as rat-skull necklaces or fetish-bags of spider husks - the People appreciate the threat that such a trinket shows the defeat of, after all. Still, for once, there's something else here.

                                                        ​The largest of the spiders that spilled forth is bloated and misshapen even for an Azlu shard. Its body is clearly distended from within and, like an overripe fruit, the arachnid is now splitting apart. Its carapace cracks and its meat sours, sloughing away from something nestling inside - a gnarled bezoar of stone or bone, a lumpen gobbet of coalesced, sickening power.

                                                        ​The hagstone thrums with ancient strength. Malformed, not just incomplete but incoherent, its touch stings with a stomach-churning loathing for the Uratha - an inchoate emotion that's not even attached to any sort of true mind. It's an idiot hatred, a blind spite. The energy stirs at the touch of a werewolf's Essence nonetheless. Its ancestral rage does not prevent an Uratha from harnessing it.

                                                        ​If the hagstone is set as a weight in a device for weaving or creating threads, it grants all Craft rolls with the device the rote quality, but sometimes the device will also spontaneously produce a venomous spider, or weave spider patterns into the fabric, or catch small animals in its weave that will perish and stain the threads with their fluids. Used in a rite of Shadowcall to conjure up a spider spirit, the hagstone offers the rote action ​and 8-again quality on the ritemaster's dice pool, but when they first open their mouth, or are cut, after the summoning completes, spiders will spill out instead of words or blood. Wield the hagstone when levying Facets against the Azlu, and the possessor benefits from the 8-again quality, but will dream deeply of spiders the following night, and all Azlu within a mile will dream of the Uratha and her life.

                                                        Swallow the hagstone, let it sink into the gut and the churning cauldron of Essence within the Uratha, and it fuses their soul with Ariadne's own spark of Pangaea. The Uratha gains Ariadne's abilities to read and tamper with fateful connections and to read the doom of others; whenever they take the Gauru form, they take on certain unsettling arachnid features and become able to Spider Climb as per the Dread Power; and their Primal Urge rises by 1. However the hagstone slowly poisons their very being. Each year, it removes the 10-again quality from another Allies or Contacts merit, or strips away a Touchstone. If the Uratha has none of these left to be affected, they are rendered Soulless, their fundamental nature leeched away into the stone. If they perish with the hagstone still in their gut, their body comes apart in a tidal wave of spiders, leaving nothing but bones and skin behind. The hagstone can​ be removed within a year, taking all its benefits with it, but doing so rips out all of the Uratha's Essence and deals 5 aggravated damage in the process. After being in the body for over a year, it is no longer possible to remove the hagstone without killing the Uratha.

                                                        Finally, the one who possesses the hagstone, whether swallowed or not, has a subconscious ability and compulsion to sense and seek out the human, Wolf-Blooded or Host within 8888 miles of their position who will fall most deeply in love with them, then hurt them. Should they fall in love - if they already are in love - then sometimes, in the glint of a mirror, the reflection of a window, the chiaroscuro of passing shadows in the sunlight, they will see her for a moment, just a passing glimpse, a fanciful impression: Ariadne, waiting forever, alone on an empty shore.




                                                        - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
