        No, first you figure out how much you're gonna spend in the boons, then figure out the ban, and take off totem points assuming you upgrade it, then pay the boons with the discount of a ban upgrade and finally add the rest of the dots into the totem's traits.

        So, if you have 25 points, 11 go towards the ban. But you upgrade it from rank 3 to five. That diminishes the cost by five point, the new ban (if you had upgraded it to four it would have been four points, etc). So, the boons would cost 6 points. After that you have 19 points to put on the totem.
