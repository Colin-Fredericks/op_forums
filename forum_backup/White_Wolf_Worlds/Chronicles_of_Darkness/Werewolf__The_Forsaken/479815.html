    Tell: Shape-Shifted

    04-13-2015, 11:28 PM
    Okay, there are a few things unclear with this one. Given as it grants a form which may not work the same for wolf-blooded as it does for true Uratha. Now, they can cause Lunacy, which has caused some confusion given the form in question, but I just figured that was to explain how mortals might react to actually seeing them shift, or regenerate damage, so it was pretty common sense to me.

    The bits which aren't clear though are as follows;

    1) Smell: So the Urhan form gets all of the benefits that it normally would. But would this include the ability to sense other Uratha by smell, just with Wits since they don't have Primal Urge to back the roll?

    2) Belongings: Given their lack of harmony, is it safe to say that shape-shifted wolf-blooded do not have their clothing and immediate possessions subsume into their body when they shift?

    3) First Tongue: Urhan can manage a few simple words in FT. Does this mean shape-shifted blooded can benefit from this when in Urhan?

      Well for 3, I believe what that means is that, due to the way their throat and tongue works, they can manage a few simple words in the First Tongue. Not that Urhan magically gifts anyone who takes that form with the ability to speak in the First Tongue.

      And for 1, the ability to scent out other Uratha is an ability of Uratha, not the Urhan form.

      Lastly for 2, I think technically they'd have the equivalent of Harmony 7, where you start as a werewolf. It's appreciating that you have a spirit side but leaning much closer to the flesh. But having them lose their belongings when they change is more of a rule of cool thing.

          Originally posted by RichE View Post
          1) Smell: So the Urhan form gets all of the benefits that it normally would. But would this include the ability to sense other Uratha by smell, just with Wits since they don't have Primal Urge to back the roll?
          Well, The Urhan form's default senses are wolf senses, though without Primal Urge, a Shape-Shifted Wolf-Blooded would miss out on some, but not all of the benefits. I'd say they get the normal +4 to perception-based rolls, don't suffer penalties to said rolls due to sensory depravation. They can recognize other characters by scent, but has to rely on the usual Wits + Composure and can't substitute Primal Urge, only rolls Wits (instead of Wits + Primal Urge) to recognize werewolves and other wolf-blooded by scent. They don't get any Primal Urge based bonus to their hearing range, but they still ignore penalties to perception rolls to listen based on distance, and can hear frequencies outside the human audible range. They'd still see in fewer colors but halve penalties for seeing in the dark and ignore penalties to vision due to rapid movement. And they should still be able to track prey by the taste of their blood. That's how I would run it, anyway. Nofather's interpretation is also valid, given that the enhanced senses are not technically a benefit of Urhan form, but a benefit of the werewolf template, whith the Shape-Shifted Wolf-Blooded wouldn't actually have. I think this is kind of an ST discretion thing, but I favor allowing the benefits of wolf senses (minus those that rely on Primal Urge) because a wolf using wolf senses is more versimillar for me than a wolf using human senses.

          Originally posted by RichE View Post
          2) Belongings: Given their lack of harmony, is it safe to say that shape-shifted wolf-blooded do not have their clothing and immediate possessions subsume into their body when they shift?
          That's definitely how I'd run it. Nofather's interpretation could also work here, though in that case I would say you should treat their Harmony as 9 because that's what characters who First Change in-game start at (reference: Humans who go through the First Change replace Integrity with Harmony, and start with nine dots regardless of their Integrity." pg 104)

          Originally posted by RichE View Post
          3) First Tongue: Urhan can manage a few simple words in FT. Does this mean shape-shifted blooded can benefit from this when in Urhan?
          They are capable of saying a few words in First Tongue, but without the Tongues Tell, they don't know the language. Much like my mouth is capable of producing the appropriate sounds to say a few words in Sumerian, but I can't because I don't know Sumerian.

                              @night owl: Yeah, it is Urshul. The thing is, that got changed between when the book was first put out as an advanced release and now. Originally it was accidentally written as Urhan. Many people called it out as errata given the whole Lunacy aspect of it and Urhan not causing Lunacy.
                              It says in the text that they regenerate while in the form, so I guess they do.

                              As to belongings: the thing about starting at Harmony 7 (where they'd come with you) is to indicate that you've made some progress towards balance since your first change. It says that somewhere, though I don't remember where. As such, someone before their first change would still be more like Harmony 10. This Tell feels more like a Harmony 10 thing than anything, so I would probably say that while its not spelled out, belongings not coming along is closer to RAW. That said, I'm for rule of cooling this one.

                                  Originally posted by night owl View Post
                                  3 questions:
                                  (1)
                                  Isn't this tell about urshul instead of urhan?
                                  Yes. In the advance release version of the PDF, it said Urhan. This was determined to be unintentional, and got errataed to the correct Urshul form in the official release.

                                  Originally posted by night owl View Post
                                  (2)
                                  Would it work to substitute urshul with urhan or ould it break some kind of gaming balance?
                                  The developers are pretty much all very up-front about the fact that they subscribe to the "balance is an illusion" camp of game design. If you would like to substitute Urshul for Urhan in your games, you're more than welcome to. You probably want to make sure to inform your players of this change up front, and discuss how this change might or might not impact your own game. I can't see this causing any issues, but every group is different, so just talk about it with your group and find your own balance.
