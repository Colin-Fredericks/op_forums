                                            01-01-2019, 03:19 PM
                                            In character, Bale Hounds are seen as a more insidious threat, as you never really know if one is lurking nearby. And Bale Hounds are adept at sowing seeds of mistrust and paranoia.

                                            Plus, idigam are written as still largely unknown by the wider Uratha community. Some packs/protectorates have encountered them, most still don’t know precisely what they faced, and no one is really comparing notes at this stage. Plus Uratha deal with enough weird shit that they don’t necessarily know that an idigam wasn’t just a really tough, strange spirit. Or something else.

                                            Out of character, personally I consider a well-run idigam to be a huge threat, with layers of minions and rabbit holes that the Uratha run down and destroy, only to later discover that there’s more to do. IMO, no idigam should be a ‘monster of the week’ (though its minions could be). They should be chronicle-spanning villains who are only defeated with cunning and careful planning or dramatic sacrifice in the closing stages of the chronicle.

                                            Bale Hounds can similarly be a chronicle-wide threat, but in a different way to idigam. How can I put this... idigam can cover much of the same themes Bale Hounds can as threats, while Bale Hounds can’t cover everything an idigam can.

                                            But that’s just IMO.
