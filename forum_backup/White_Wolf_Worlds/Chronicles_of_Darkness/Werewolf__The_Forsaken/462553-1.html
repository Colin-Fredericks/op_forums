            I just took for granted that you only got to shape shift once per turn, so the force shift was what you got instead of what you wanted, unless you roll with it.Lets try an example:

            Say I'm a Harmony 3 werewolf chill'in in the bar minding my own beeswax, in Hishu of course, when a scmuck walks up to me and just pulls a knife! Now, because I have low harmony, my instincts are driving me toward Garou, 'cuz that's for fight'in, but I don't want to hit them with full lunacy, so I just allow my crappy harmony shape shifting to take me to Dalu, and I take a swing at him. Next turn, my body still wants full Garou, I disagree given the situation, so I spend a point of essence to prevent myself from shifting, because my body is attempting to make shape shifts, and I'm consciously fighting it, I can't make any other shape shifting this turn either. I could use my movement to try to get away from the assailant, but given that he's a wacko with a knife, he's probably pretty determined, without some other help, I've gotta go full Garou and put him in the dirt. Afterwards, there's no more threat, and I probably want to lay low, which desires Hishu, I decide to roll with it and go back to full human and see if I can't blend in with the crowd to avoid the cops.

                                The whole context of the "shifts one form towards an appropriate form each turn" bit is resisting the change. The person can spend Essence each scene to keep in one form for longer, unless [enter paragraph in question].
                                My point here is that it seems like "one form towards" is the extent of the resistance the low-Harmony Uratha is able to put up, if they want to resist the change at all. In the "Run! be a wolf! / Yes! be a wolf!" scenario, they're not trying to resist the change and they can get right to it.
                                If their instincts go "Run! Be a wolf!" and their brain is more like "But this box I'm holding is important!!!!" then they've got to shift, but maybe they can spend the next turn in Dalu and put the box down. Then on the next turn it can be Urhan.

                                I said this bit about the forms before, but it seems to have been overlooked or something. As much as the five forms make a neat line on the character sheet, there's not actually an order to them. Most-like-a-regular-human to most-like-a-normal-wolf they go in the Hishu, Dalu, Gauru, Urshul, Urhan line-up. Biggest to smallest they're Gauru, Urshul, Dalu, Hishu, Urhan. Highest Speed stat to lowest they're Urshul, Urhan, Gauru, Dalu, Hishu. Most obviously out of place in a bar to least they go Gauru, Urshul, Urhan, Dalu, Hishu.
                                Where you're shifting from and where you're shifting to both matter. So does your willingness to get there and your ability to convince the ST that the form you do take is "towards" what you should probably be doing.

                                That all said, if your Harmony is 3 or lower, what you should really be doing is eating a cheeseburger to get your Harmony back on straight.
