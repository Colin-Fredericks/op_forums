                        Something interesting in the sidebar on p137 of VtR2:

                        Mixed Signals.
                        Obfuscate tricks the victim's mind directly, removing traces of the vampire from the sensory information reaching her brain, rather than fooling her senses directly. Obfuscate affects smell and taste as much as sight or hearing - potentially useful if you've angered a pack of werewolves, or a vampire with shard Kindred senses.

                        Cloak of Night probably has useful information for this conversation on p138:

                        The vampire can be tracked by indirect clues, as mentioned above. Tracking is an extended action, with the observer rolling Wits + Resolve - Obfuscate. A vampire observer can apply the Blood Potency bonus from Kindred senses. Each roll takes one turn, and the observer must roll more successes than the vampire's Wits + Stealth + Obfuscate. Teamwork may be applied.

                        I'd interpret that in a Werewolf context by allowing the use of Forsaken's tracking system with the vampire's Obfuscate as a penalty. Gifts that enhance tracking could be used as normal. Primal Urge tracking bonuses and enhanced wolf senses would also apply, IMO.
