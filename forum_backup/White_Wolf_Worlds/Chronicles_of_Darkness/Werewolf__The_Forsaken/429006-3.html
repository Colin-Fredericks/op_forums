    And why, exactly, would another form not suffice for such a tactic? Urshul is tailored for such things, in fact (at least it used to be).
    Because while you're a monster, you can be a responsible monster.

    Dalu and Urshul are great picks for combat focused forms where you keep your rationality about you. And of course, you have the few precious free turns available to you in Gauru.

    ...But you're not perfect.

    You don't need to be IN Gauru to be triggered into Death Rage. You can lose control.

    If you're intending on placing yourself into a stressful situation where your control may be tested (say for instance, combat), then why not be the responsible monster you should be and select your battleground carefully so that when you do go into Death Rage, you don't damage things you care about?

    In such a place, you and your packmates can cut loose. To stop resisting that call of the Hard Rage and just give into it. After all, you've prepared your territory in advance and whatever you're fighting... a Pack of raging werewolves will put it down hard. Why fear and avoid what you are when, with some foresight and planning, you can revel in the fundamental meaning of what you were born to do?

    Honor your territory. Build yourself an abattoir for the purpose of blood hitting the ground and walls. And above all else, warn the Wolf-Blooded and humans of your pack that when the howls of the Hunt fill the moonlit night, to stay away from the killing grounds.
