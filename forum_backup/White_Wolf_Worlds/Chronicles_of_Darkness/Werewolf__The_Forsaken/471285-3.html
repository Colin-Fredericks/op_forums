    Originally posted by nofather View Post
    An existing one could be triggered to erupt by movements of the earth, just like you could create a flood by using earth to destroy a dam. But I don't think you could use earth to just create a new volcano from scratch, no.

    I understand what you're saying, but as I see it spirits aren't based off of that kind of logic, you know? A greed spirit isn't necessarily going to have any control over money, but greed could be used to get money. Fire influence could be used to expand a fire, and make a lot of smoke, but it couldn't control the smoke.
    But money isn't greed and smoke isn't fire. Ice is literally cold water (or, water ice is, anyway) and magma is literally hot rock. I just don't think temperature change would remove a substance from a spirit's sphere of influence. It also gets a little hairy to start excluding certain abilities from an influence on the grounds that it's covered by a different influence. Like, can Influence: cars not be used to make a car go really fast because that's the purview of Influence: speed? Can it not be used to turn the car on because that's the purview of Influence: machines? Can it not change the car's shape because that's the purview of Influence: metal? Like, the subjectivity of Influences makes overlap inherently unavoidable. I think as long as each influence has a unique combination of things it can do, it doesn't matter if some of those things can also be done by other influences.

                                                I'd like to throw my hat back in here.
                                                Given that Strengthen doesn't add Durability (as I had mistakenly thought based on an early reading), it makes sense that it can't be used to make things that are typically non-solid things solid. I think this much has been established.

                                                Manipulate can be used to change the temperature of a thing. Charlaquin (and possibly others, but his/her assertions are the only ones I remember at the moment) has been saying this for a while and I don't recall seeing anyone dispute that general claim. As such, if something can be frozen through a change in temperature, Manipulate can freeze it.

                                                As to the question of overlaps in Influence, I would point to the difference between an Umia and Ilthum, given that those are basic spirit distinctions. Umia take the form of broad categories of things (Water, Air, Information, Mamals, Emotions, etc.) whereas Ilthum are the sub-groups of spirits within those that cling to more specific phenomena (Rain, Wind, Books, Wolves, Fear, etc.) The thing is, Ilthum are not a natural spirit division - they pick those and latch onto them to speed their growth. As such, something like Ice would be a sub-set of Water. Maybe it's power could leak out to beyond the bounds of Water when faced with frozen things that are not water, but when dealing with water, anything Influence: Ice could do, Influence: Water could do.
