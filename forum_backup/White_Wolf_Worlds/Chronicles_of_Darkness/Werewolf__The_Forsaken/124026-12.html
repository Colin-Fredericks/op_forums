                                                    On top of that, it is worth noting that the GMC era updates greatly increased Defense relative to attack dice pools (not including firearms) and made armor much more potent.

                                                    Even if you did have Whirlwind Strike from the pre-GMC rules in the new rules, it would be incredibly stupid to use. The brokeness of the pre-GMC multiple action Merits was strongly linked to the disparity in access to large offensive pools to large Defense totals. In the pre-GMC rules have 4-6 dice after Defense even on a Brawl attack was very easy; so when multiplied you could easily dish out more damage than a target could hope to take. In the GMC rules have an attack pool of 3+ after Defense represents a large imbalance between offensive investment vs. defensive investment (aka the defender is way weaker than the attacker; though again not counting guns, though in the new form rules Gauru applies full Defense against guns anyway). Having 1-2 dice per attack and then running into Armor on top of that, while sacrificing your Defense is far from the reliable KO route of the old rules.
