That's not what Lunacy is, though. Think about it like a horror movie. When the werewolf is right there in front of the main characters, none of them try to deny what they're seeing. They don't really have time to try to rationalize it, all they can do is run, hide, or fight. It's not until later, when they're not face to face with an eight foot killing machine that they start to say things like "I don't know what I saw" or "It had to have been a bear, right?" And that's when they start making stupid decisions like "we've got to find whatever did this and put it down!" or seeing weird shit, or getting jumpy about little things. That's what Lunacy is. Lunacy is the fear of the wolf at the door, not the wolf inside your house. It comes from knowing there's a big bad wolf out there somewhere that wants to eat you up, not from staring into the jaws of death.
