    Not enough Werewolves [Game Idea]

    05-08-2015, 03:47 PM
    I'm planning to ST a game in which Uratha are running thin - especially in the player's home town - so they have to make-do with enrolling extraordinary Wolf-Blooded into the hunt. Basically it would be a 2 Uratha 3 Wolf-Blooded game. Now the power-difference between Uratha and WB is a large gap and I don't want to simply drop 20 XP on my WB players to try and balance things so I need another way.

    Here are the ideas I had, but I wanted your oppinions on what would work and what would cause important problems.

    -Allow the WB with a corresponding tell the ability to cast Wolf Rites
    -Allow Supernatural Merits to the WB
    -Give free Auspice and Tribe merits to the WB
    -Allow buying Shadow Gifts to the WB after a succesful hunt

    I don't want to give them all of these options (because then why not simply make them werewolves) but some oppinions or alternative ideas would be greatly appreciated

                                          I don't think there's anything wrong with free xp. 10 xp would be roughly equivalent to the free renown/gifts that Uratha start with.

                                          I'd offer them a couple extra tells. Including maybe some custom made ones if they feel limited by the options.

                                          I think the free Auspice/Tribe Wolf Blood merits make a lot of sense as well.

                                          Then maybe access to one or two gifts. With either no renown modifier (to represent their limited capability), or the renown modifier being equal to the number of facets they have in said gift (to demonstrate their mastery of the gift). I'd probably go with two gifts max, as they would have a limited capacity for spirit gifts being carved into them. Maybe represent them as tattoos. They would have to really work to acquire those gifts. Gifts would all cost 1-2 willpower to use and might be modified in other ways to make sense for the wolf blooded.
