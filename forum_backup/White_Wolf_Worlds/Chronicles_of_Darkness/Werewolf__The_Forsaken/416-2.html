    Something I wasn't a big fan of recently myself was the Rituals stat. After Hunter and Geist, it seems like Rituals as a Merit type would just work better, so essentially there's no sacrifice of a Gift at character creation as its purchased from the Merit pool, as the then increasing Rites as Merit (Rituals) would just be new dots * 2 across the board, rather than new dots * 5 for the stat; then rite dots * 2 for each additional Rite within the current rating.

    Regarding any dynamic Rite creation, I'd like it to be less "flexible magic system" and more tied in with the development of the Pack's Totem. Possibly the option for a pack to forge (and teach) a new Rite when their totem increases in Rank. Allowing the deeds of the Pack over the course of a chronicle actually "game-mechanically" shape Uratha culture as a whole. It would seem more in line with the animistic themes of the game as opposed to Blood Sorcery/Gutter Magic mechanics (which are appropriate to their respective games themes in their own right). While not having an "improvised" feel, it does add an extra "cash in on your investment" to Totem development.
