    Ok. Secret isn't perhaps the right word here, but it's insular. The nature of the Firstborn, and everything in Uratha culture is generally explained after the First Change, provided you didn't grow up in a pack that explained things (and that's probably rare). Hell, a Werewolf pack in 9th century Scandinavia needn't be that deviant in their rites and rituals, compared to their neighbours. They are a bit more accurate in their blóts to the Alfar, is all.

    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
    It maybe the reasons why the Totems of Packs are enraging the Uratha on raids and fighting with Church.
    I think that conceptual war is too high and impersonal for totems on the pack level. More likely is that the werewolves have bound a wolf-spirit to them. And I'll explain what that has to do with things: Wolves in this era were seen as ravagers and destroyers, creatures of danger and destruction. Odin was a wolf-god, but all other mythical wolves were unambiguously bad. Wolves were bad news. Thus, a wolf-spirit in that era and place would've been a more destructive spirit than the more "natural" ones we see today, since humans devote less thought to wolves than they did then. Therefore, a pack with a wolf-spirit as totem would want to destroy things like churches, at least if they want to keep their totem happy.

    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
    Also, thing with Fenris is odd one - if he is Firstborn, he's name should be secret, by your way of thinking. Why it is not?

    Maybe other question will answer ours - how the thing with Firstborn worship was solved in Mesopotamia setting? Did werewolves have their own secret pantheon, and did not called on local Wolf and Moon spirits? Did they not change the names of Firstborns for local pantheon representatives?
    Fenrir is, indeed, the odd one here. The question is if it was the Uratha that introduced him, or if it was the people of Scandinavia who found him on their own. Considering their view on the danger of wolves, it's possible that the choir of wolves in Scandinavia was mostly dominated by Fenris-Ur's offspring, and shamans picked up on the great destroyer-wolf who was out there, and a giant danger to all.

    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
