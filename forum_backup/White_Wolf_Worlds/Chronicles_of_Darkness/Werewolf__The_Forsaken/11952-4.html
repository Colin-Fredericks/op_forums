                112) The Fire-Touched have always been fanatical zealots, but their latest alpha has gone straight off the deep end. She offers the local Forsaken an ultimatum, either join her tribe and be purified, or she and her fanatics will go begin massacring humans in the streets. They have until the next full moon to agree to her terms, which is thankfully a month away. Its a race against time to locate the Fire-Touched lodge and stamp out this rampage before it starts!


                113) The PC's are all newly changed werewolves and don't know much about their responsibilities. The bad part is before they came along, the Forsaken and Pure were deadlocked in power against each other, and a brand new pack could be just what they need to tip the scales in their favor. At first both tribes seem to be friendly enough in offering them membership, but when the players start taking too long to decide, things turn ugly. Their latest recruitment ploy was pretty straight forward "If we can't have you, we'll make sure they can't either!"

                        115) For well over 20 years now, the city has had problems with pollution and illegal dumping and it's gotten so bad it's starting to produce a haze in the air. Unnaturally black clouds hang over the city, obscuring the sunlight and respiratory illnesses are becoming more common and the city is strangely reluctant to clean up its act. As the resident pack of Iron Masters, the PC's are shocked when the look up to see the cloud of smog has a face, and it's leering down upon them. This cloud isn't a cloud, it's a colossal spirit of poison, and it's growing powerful enough to manifest itself in the physical world!
