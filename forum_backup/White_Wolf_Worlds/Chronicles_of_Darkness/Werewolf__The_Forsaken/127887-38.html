                                    And now, for various terms in War Against the Pure's shapeshifter section that don't have an FT translations, starting with the werebulls. And some other stuff.

                                    Werebulls (Baal-Hadad, Gudthabak)

                                    ghudam: the "blade" or leader of a werebull herd
                                    uthzif: a "sun-mark", or the solar spirit brands the werebulls have carved/burnt/etc. into their flesh
                                    Ghulthu'u: the hybrid form of the Baal-Hadad
                                    Abisu: the animal form of the Baal-Hadad
                                    Mabhos: the near-beast form of the Baal-Hadad
                                    Ahaskudh: the near-human form of the Baal-Hadad
                                    Dhigir-Mir: "Rage of the Gods", the werebulls' name for Death Rage
                                    anbis: a bull-changer who becomes a white cow, and considered more sacred or lucky than others (lit. "heavenly cow")
                                    Nihhina-Sagrik Alal: The Rite of Molech, or the Rite of True Sacrifice
                                    Malgu: An alternate name for Helios used by the Baal-Hadad
                                    Zahigi-Dhigir: "Unawakened Gods", the bull-blooded that have the potential to become Baal-Hadad
                                    Anizi: "holy fires", or the Helions of Helios that visit the Baal-Hadad (and are believed to actually be Helios's avatars)
                                    subhur: the mortal "herd" that a werebull or werebulls rule over
                                    ammar: "calves" or "calf", children or descendants of a werebull, who usually act as liaisons or have some sort of important position when it comes to the werebull herd
                                    subhurhia: the offerings a werebull's herd are supposed to make to their bull-headed overlords
                                    absasa: personal idols the Baal-Hadad make themselves that grant them special boons and bonuses
                                    Anizalath: a "holy fire" that a Baal-Hadad has taken as a totem
                                    Malgubes-igi: said of a werebull that has the Sun-Flecked Eyes aspect, it literally means "sun-blessed eyes" and refers to how the sun grants them better sight
                                    zulalumag-zif: "the punishment's mark", a Castigating Brand put upon one by a werebull
                                    halzugenak: the title or deed name of a werebull

                                    Other Stuff:

                                    yaan: perfect knowledge or heavenly wisdom; knowledge that is inherently true about the fundamental reality of the universe
                                    dhigir: a catch-all term for deities, gods or goddesses
                                    abhos: like abis, this refers to cows and cattle, but can also refer to more ancient members of the cow family, and extinct cow breeds
                                    burhia: a catch-all term for sacrifices or offerings
                                    zulalum: a punishment; a sentence

                                                        Added a few more Baal-Hadad terms, and now, time for the werecats to get a mini-lexicon.

                                                        Werecats (The Colony, Suthanu-sua)

                                                        Zuarri: the goddess Bast/Bastet, patroness and "mother" of all werecat kind
                                                        Maeresh Zuarri: "Great Lady Bastet", a respectful title for Bastet
                                                        Amahan Zuarri: Another name for Bastet, meaning "Mother Bastet"
                                                        Zi'irulag-Kadah: "The Great Hunt of the Sinful", the driving mission and entire purpose for the Colony's existence
                                                        Zi'irani: the prey of the Suthanu-sua, sinful and inharmonious individuals
                                                        Nuserdha: "The Guiltless", or those who are mistakenly slain by the werecats in their missions
                                                        Kuth-Kurah: "The Call", in which a werecat gains a nagging urge to convert another into one of the Colony; coloquially known as "going into heat"
                                                        Suammethu: "The Fever", or the spiritual disease that is meant to turn one into one of the Colony; this is fatal if a werecat doesn't intervene and bring the victim into the fold, and is coloquially known as "Cat Scratch Fever"
                                                        umfi'ira: the foul, pungent oil extruded from a werecat's claws that is used to "infect" another with the Colony curse
                                                        Suaigi: the near-human form of the Suthanu-sua
                                                        Usiira: the hybrid form of the Suthanu-sua
                                                        Tagaraah: the near-beast form of the Suthanu-sua
                                                        Ehsua: the animal form of the Suthanu-sua
                                                        Zifara: "soul-colour", or the "auspice" of a werecat as determined by their eyes
                                                        Suthzibigi: a Bloodeyes werecat
                                                        Afuahigi: a Goldeyes werecat
                                                        Thiranigi: a Leafeyes werecat
                                                        Thuhhima: The megalomaniac Madness Rage of the werecats
                                                        Namserdha: "The Guilt" — when capitalized, it refers to the madness werecats suffer if they do not kill a sinful person within a certain time period
                                                        Karana: "The Burden" — the trials and tribulations of the werecat curse, and the heavy burden it lays on the chosen werecat, sometimes driving the werecat completely insane; a word used to sum up a werecat's existence
                                                        Zulalumul: "Punishers", werecats who turn others into one of the Colony because they were sinful offenders as mortals
                                                        Numalasul: "The Careless", werecats who choose a new Colony curse victim completely at random
                                                        Suaziha: "Cat-Cursed", those who were picked to become werecats because of a vengeful werecat, or completely at random
                                                        Sua-Namtar: "Cat-Chosen", those who were deliberately picked to become werecats after a period of long observation and thought
                                                        yamak: the various beliefs and superstitions of the Suthanu-sua
                                                        ziramak: things believed to be sinful or taboo, and thus fodder for punishment or retribution, by the werecats
                                                        suamu: the deed name of a werecat
                                                        dumizu: the act of a werecat stealing a child's life force to gain essence; considered the ziramak of all ziramak by the Colony
                                                        kuk-keli: a "neck-biter"; said of werecats who have the Spinebite aspect
                                                        murudu: a "sprayer"; said of werecats who have the Malodor aspect
                                                        thulmalas: a "graceful faller"; said of werecats who have the Righting Reflex aspect


                                                        And derived terms:
                                                        ummethu: fever, sickness, illness
                                                        i'ira: a scented oil or residue
                                                        serdha: crime, offense; to be guilty of something
                                                        namserdha: guilt, guiltful feelings
                                                        muru: mist; drizzle; spray

                                                            More g-homonyms! (And a couple of derived terms.)

                                                            hu'u: a corded net
                                                            ghu: the entirety, sum or total of a deal, a lot, or some other legal, financial or business-related thing
                                                            kuu: fibrous plants that have not yet been retted (soaked) for use
                                                            khu: the bank of something, like a river or gully
                                                            khu'u: the pulse in the neck; the arterial pulse
                                                            khumusen: birds that nest on the sides of hills or riverbanks, like certain types of swallows
                                                            huu: the voice or cry of a certain animal; an animal call or the mimicry of one
                                                            ghu'u: to square something in mathematics
                                                            kalabu: to cover with; to slip into; to slip through
                                                            hu'e: to roar; to call out in invocation; to call out or challenge a god or spirit; used as an interjection in the vein of, "I call to you, O ____!"
                                                            khuf: to purify oneself through means of a ritual bath; rites that involve bathing or dunking someone in water, such as baptism
                                                            hubh: to stand up when one is called for; to stand at attention
                                                            ghubh: to be assigned to a task; an assignment
                                                            zeru: dislike; to have a dislike or distaste for something or someone; to have disgust for
                                                            skir dhe: to address someone; to call out to someone
                                                            zerru: to ignore; to neglect
                                                            kuluhuu-skir: to bray; to bawl; to bellow or shout in some sort of distress, such as pain, sadness or fear
                                                            haranu: to pile up something, like throwing sacks of flour into a pile; to sort into piles
                                                            shurru: to lean; to bend forwards; to bow
                                                            ghith: to drag; dragging; to pull along
                                                            keli ghith: a werewolf phrasing meaning "to rob" or "to take unlawfully" (it literally means "drag by the neck", referring to a scavenger dragging away stolen prey)

                                                            shaghu-skir: to lift the head to howl; a howling session carried out by a wolf pack or werewolf pack

                                                            gubhi: a marsh bird, like an egret
                                                            khud: to pounce on; to jump on to attack
                                                            gud: an ox; a bull
