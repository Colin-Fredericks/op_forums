                        Originally posted by Tabanese View Post
                        Can someone explain the Resonance -> Essence -> Spirit cycle to me? I've been reading the material and I'd like a concise explanation from someone else to hang mine off, to see if I've got it right.
                        • Resonance is spiritual flavour. Everything in the Flesh (material world; not the Shadow) is spiritually Resonant (which means everything has the appropriate Resonant Conditions).
                          • A fire has a fire Resonance (and the Resonant Condition for fire).
                        • Objects and locations generates Essence that share the same resonance as its source.
                          • A fire generates Essence that has a fire resonance (aka, fire Essence).
                        • Spirits also match resonances (though we generally refer it to their nature or their Influences, just to make it a little less confusing), and they feed on Essence that has compatible resonance.
                          • A spirit of a fire nature (aka, a fire spirit) feeds on fire Essence.
                        • Essence that is not eaten or otherwise removed may coalesce into motes (Rank 0 spirits) that naturally grows if they are near appropriate Resonances.
                          • A fire generates fire Essence that coalesce into a mote. If the fire continues to burn for a long time or is repeatedly reignited, the mote may become a sentient spirit with time.

                        Technically this is not a cycle. Essence generates naturally and spirits doesn't have to do anything, but spirits are hungry and competitive so if they can they will try to maximise the amount of Essence of the appropriate type that generates. The fire spirit would use its Influence: Fire to start new fires so that more fire Essence is generated.

                        (Stronger Resonances are supposed to grant more Essence, but this is not reflected in the game rules except when it comes to Loci. This means a fire spirit should want to increase the size and intensity of a preexisting fire because that would cause the fire to generate more Essence. The problem is that the roll for gaining Essence is based on the spirit's own traits so in practice it's larger spirits that pull more Essence from Resonances rather than the intensity of the Resonances generating varying amounts of Essence. I recommend just skipping that rule and just grant an amount of Essence appropriate for the situation if you want to track the Essence pool of a spirit.)

                        Also note that Resonances can be a lot more abstract than "object A has Resonance A". A fire will Resonate fire but also Resonate light and heat, as well as more abstract concepts like destruction (if it's a destructive fire).

                                                    Originally posted by Tabanese View Post
                                                    How come wolves can only tap loci for essence if this is the case?
                                                    While werewolves are part spirit and part flesh, they are wholly neither. They don't need Essence to survive except at high ranks of Primal Urge, and they get it differently. So they do not have an innate mechanism for devouring Essence the way spirits do (outside of eating people and other werewolves, which is verboten). In their own world, Pangaea (detailed in Dark Eras, but now gone) every act of the hunt was effectively a sacred hunt, allowing them to sate themselves of the needs of their primal urge. As the world is gone, they are forced to use the Sacred Hunt rite as a medium.

                                                        Elements of the physical world generally create resonance(s) around them. A river might have resonances of life, travel, and water, among others. These resonances leak through the Gauntlet, making zones that spirits can harvest for Essence. Humans are particularly good at generating all kinds of complicated resonances, especially emotionally-charged ones.

                                                        When a spirit is on the flesh side of the Gauntlet, they’re tapping into the Resonance directly. It’s not being filtered through the Gauntlet to pool for others to take, the spirit can take it from the source. As an imperfect metaphor, it can be like the difference between drinking a beer poured straight from the tap versus one that you’ve purchased in a can. Normal spirit feeding isn’t going to exhaust a resonance, but spirits are greedy and their Influences often align with the resonances they feed from so they use them to inflame the resonance and give themselves more to gorge on. Unchecked, this often leads to burning out the source of the resonance.

                                                        When resonance pools on an object or creature and no spirits come to sup at it the accumulation can turn that object/creature into a locus.

                                                        Werewolves can’t access Essence as easily as spirits because they’re creatures of half flesh and spirit. They have a bunch of advantages from that but also a few disadvantages such as less ready access to Essence and needing to Reach at a locus.
