                                                        Originally posted by Story Letter View Post
                                                        Only that a vampire in frenzy cannot make sophisticated attacks like targetting specific body areas.
                                                        A vampire in frenzy isn't stupid. It's animalistic and monomaniacal, but not mindless. As the book says: "the Beast isn’t stupid." If the Beast believes staking is the best course of action to achieve its goal, then it will do so.

                                                        Edit: I'm personally not surprised that a vampire beat a werewolf at those power levels. Werewolves start out hard with the regeneration, but vampires can become ridiculously dangerous with Vigor/Resilience, a giant bonus to all physical rolls in Frenzy, as well as being able to gain enormous dice pools with Physical Intensity. I play in a similarly high end game of VtR, and if you don't have 40+ dice on an attack then you're not even trying.
