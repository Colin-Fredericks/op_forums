            Assuming Gifts themselves remained in their current fashion? Primal Urge (as it always was a measure of spiritual potency regardless, and it'd likely be used to determine effective Rank without the Renown system anyhow) or like many splats, ones rating in the Gift set if one needed a quick and dirty.

            I'd honestly treat Rank as equal to half the Werewolf's Primal Urge rounded up (yes, this would mean as a brand new werewolf you'd get far less respect and may get spiritual harassment for being low man on the totem pole), or Half your Primal Urge rounded up +1 if you want to present the situation as less dire for new werewolves. To reflect the Pure's advantage in that arena, I'd give them a +1 bonus to that, or just give them access to a Pure merit that bolsters their honorary rank in social dealings (not on gift dice pools).

            You could be generous and keep it just a straight Primal Urge, or you could use honorary spirit Rank for it. Keep in mind that, while costly for initial merit points, you could start as a Primal Urge 3 with a starting werewolf (meaning by this notion, you'd start as effective Rank 2, having 2 dice to each Gift on top of an attribute + skill, or 3 dice if using straight Primal-Urge). Given the costs and limitations of the old Renown set and GMC experience, I don't feel it'd be a big problem given how costly it was as is to get high Renown-based bonuses. As is, I'm not all that keen on keeping Gifts in their current incarnation and common means of garnering; they're one of the biggest messes W:tF has at the moment. Powers gained from spirits? Sure. Gift lists with renown requirements? Not so much.

                So regarding the Lunacy with conditions point - a "Prey's Mind" condition is added onto the mortal?
                __________________________________________________ __________________________________

                Condition: "Prey's Mind" (it's a terrible name, but I can't think of anything else without coffee - I'm tempted to just leave the name Lunacy, but I like the prey response theme)

                If a mortal suffers a breaking point witnessing a werewolf's obvious supernatural ability (for example shapeshaping and obvious Gift displays) the mortal gains the condition "Prey's Mind".

                Whilst under this condition the mortal must get herself from the source of werewolf power in the quickest way. This includes attacking and destroying obstacles in her way, but never the source of "Prey's Mind". She acts only on instinct and is incapable of complex thought.

                Resolution: Once the mortal cannot percieve or believe that she is pursued or in the presence of the origin of the condition, the condition ends. Immediately roll willpower penalised by the werewolf who caused this condition's Primal Urge. A dramatic failure indicates no memory from the cause of the condition to the end, and she suffers -2 to composure tests for a month, a failure indicates only half memories of a frightful beast and piss-your-pants terror. Success indicates she remembers most details of the incident, but unless has cause otherwise to, fills in the gap by assuming she was attacked by a large animal or some other coincidence. Exceptional success indicates that she remembers all of it, even if she doesn't necessarily believe it, the werewolf who caused this may have issues within the future.

                Beat: No idea. Any thoughts?




                Notes: Should stuff like sidestepping be included as triggers?

                    The "Primal Aura" should be more proactive.
