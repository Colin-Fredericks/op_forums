          Supplement addition, but I'll point out that the Aztec skinchangers referenced in the Skinchangers book are very related to the original Werewolf concept, specifically being created by the Incarna Ka'an Balam, the Jaguar God who ruled the Middle Kingdom at Father Wolf's allowance. While I find it unlikely they will stick completely to the concept from the Skinchangers book, just based on that sort of theme it's easy to incorporate many Skinchangers into the Werewolf mythos. For instance Blasphemies has the Balam Colop, the Tribe of the Jaguar, who are modern enemies of the Uratha and something that would fit under Skinchangers..

                          Demon doesn't have a first edition, no.

                          I should have been more specific. Demon and Vampire use 2e rules. Because the 2e books are out for them. Werewolf straddles the fence, using 2e rules for human stuff and tilts and conditions but nothing werewolf specific. Everything else is working off of first edition stuff.

                          But yes, when it comes down to mortal stuff it's not that hard to convert 1e to 2e rules. Things like Changeling will be harder, since the seeming and kith setup is different, as are how contracts work. But we don't really know how they'll work and Dark Eras will probably be out before Changeling 2e is.

                          Fortunately the book is mostly setting.
