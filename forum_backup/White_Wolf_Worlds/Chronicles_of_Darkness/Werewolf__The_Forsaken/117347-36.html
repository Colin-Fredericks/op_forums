                                                            Originally posted by eberg View Post

I know it was mentioned that it is not necessary for a spirit to be of a negative concept to be Wound-tainted, that a tainted spirit of Joy is just as possible as a tainted spirit of Murder.                                                             Wounds are written post-Inferno, so while the text about them in Idigam never includes the words "Lower Depths," it does describe how Wounds split open to reveal gateways to somewhere other than the Shadow, that the maeljin aren't spirits, and that spirits feeding in Wounds get corrupted by the energies of wherever-it-is the Wound links to.

                                                            The only line Stew cut was one raising the possibility of Wounded Uratha, as Bale Hounds are too big a kettle of fish to raise in one line if we're not then going to devote any time to them.
