                                            Someone else can probably explain it better than myself, but if memory serves, the Bale Hounds are werewolves who basically decide that they're going to throw in their lot with extremely nasty spirits called Maeljin who are representative of the seven deadly sins. This corrupts them both inside and out and their preferred tactics are to pretend to be werewolves of other tribes, infiltrate packs and then tempt them into the sin they joined up with. Hoping to corrupt their targets and make them fall as they had. They also spend some of their spare time creating and strengthening Wounds. Wounds being areas in the Shadow which have become infected with corruption and evil that then begin influencing the area around them to fall to similar corruption. For example, a wound of wrath caused by a murder will create nasty murder spirits that will likely use their influence on the material world to cause more murders, spreading the infamy of murders in the region and making the wound larger and more powerful.
