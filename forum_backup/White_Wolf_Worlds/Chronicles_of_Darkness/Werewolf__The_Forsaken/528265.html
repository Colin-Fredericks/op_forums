    Werewolf Investigating

    05-25-2015, 08:23 PM
    I'm trying to write an opening adventure, and my mind is blanking.

    The pack is going to start by investigating a clinic that disappeared, then reappeared, sans all patients and medical staff in a small-to-mid size midwestern town.

    This is my first time running WtF, I'm not quite up on the information gathering/problem-solving tools that a pack of uratha have at their disposal (group composition, if it helps: Hunter in Darkness Edolath, Irraka Bone Shadow, Rahu Iron Master and a Storm Lord Irrakka, all vanilla beginning level characters with no frills or extra xp)

    My general outline is that something dragged the building into the Hisil, possibly using a corrupted ritual, grabbed the building inhabitants, and then left. My choice of culprit is probably going to be an Alzu.

    In general, I'm wondering how they might case the scene, (hopefully without alerting the police), and what sorts of investigative strategies they might try to track down what's going on, what sort of clues they should find.

    Any ideas?

      From meatspace, you could have the werewolves hear whispers from witnesses. Nothing that would be believed by police, most likely, but might grab a werewolf's attention. Alternatively you could have had a spirit wander into the clinic while it was in the Hisil, but not leave in time, forcing it into reality or Twilight. From there it might be trying to find a way back or taking advantage of the opportunity. They might not even be alone.

      From the Hisil there might be a bit of an uproar as spirits were displaced or suddenly found their sources of Essence materialized in front of them. Perhaps the Azlu didn't manage to keep all the spirits away, leaving one person who managed to hide the whole time who is traumatized by what they saw, or simply dead. Or maybe they managed to escape the clinic while it was in the Shadow, and are currently summoning a host of fear spirits as they run away from the fear hosts appearing behind them.

      There's a lot of gifts they could use, to tell things that have happened in the recent past to objects or places, or any dead that might be around. You should probably check the gifts your players have for that, specifically Death, Knowledge and Insight gifts, though not limited to those.

      They have enhanced senses and are able to track via blood, so if one of the missing patients had their blood drawn, a werewolf will be able to know which direction they're in through that. There's also the aforementioned questioning of witnesses, both human and spirit. Even security cameras might be checked out, showing an Azlu chasing people through the building or simply being dragged away, wrapped in webbing. Their tracking is really excellent, and even without blood someone's bound to have left a scent.

                  I'd say there are various gifts that could help. Pretty much all the Insight Facets could lead to clues about the culprits. (Echo Dream, Read the World's Loom and maybe even Scent the Supernatural if the Hosts are still around or there is a lingering effect of the ritual). Both the Bone Shadow Irraka and the Elodoth may have access to one or more of those gifts.

                  Other than that, basic canvassing. Maybe a spooked neighbour or a drunkard saw something. Something with too many legs, or a swarm of spiders joining together to look like a person. Maybe they overheard something. Nobody else would believe it, but to those who know what to listen for it might stand out.

                  They could use actual research for similar occurrences. If any of them have any occultist contacts, for example.

                  If any of them had a specialty in Hosts (or maybe just the Hunter in Darkness anyway by virtue of Hosts being their chosen prey) I would probably allow for an Occult roll just to see if it seems familiar to him/her. That's a bit easy, though.

                  Of course on the Shadow side, Azlu tend to leave the Gauntlet a lot thicker than they found it, so that could tip off anyone who decides to look through the Gauntlet. Anyone with spirit contacts could also find information that way.
