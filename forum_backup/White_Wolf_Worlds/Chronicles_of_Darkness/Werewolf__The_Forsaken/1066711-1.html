                The pack in our chronicle were all 'orphans', new wolves with no one else to turn to so they supported each other.
                ​One had the effect of amnesia to represent that she was literally orphaned at such a young age she didn't know who her family was. Her parentage fueled the first arc of the chronicle.
                Another had family, at least up to the moment the Ivory Claws wiped them out. I don't think I have to explain how this one was played out.
                ​The third still had a family, an extensive one made up of wolves and wolf blooded. She left them because she refused to accept the plans they had for her. The family was okay with existing in the background at first, after all they were confident she would come home eventually. Even after they realized she was never coming home they were okay with lending much needed assistance, in exchange for the child of a father of their choosing (this was 1ed).

                                Made a pack for a vampire crossover. Half crazed ghost wolf Holly-Runs-With-Rats and a coterie of Nosferatu who basically became a sewer patrol due to the cities Beshilu threat. The treated themselves as doctors cutting the tumors from the city's body. "Tumors need precision tools" Johhny One Nut said "but while most doctors use scalpels this tumor is much bigger and has legs, a tail, and a pipe wrench. Nurse we need to employ a larger tool" Johhny then revved a chainsaw and threw himself into the coming onslaught. With celerity, vigor, and resilience he fought off the small horde. Holly on the other hand was dressed in a ww2 nurse outfit using her a fetish weapon to electrify the Beshilu. A weaponized shock treatment machine with a surge spirit inside it. The ending of that game was pure insanity and I loved it.

                                        I think about 2-3 NPC pack I need for my Sweden Uppsala modern setting. I was going by using two Tribes per pack - to have variety of Uratha in region - but I seem to have problem with hammering in-game working concepts. My PCs pack sounds to be connected to gangs and criminal underworld in profile. I was thinking about those combinations:

                                        Variant 1
                                        • Pack 1 - Blood Talon + Bone Shadows - 'Traditionalist' - I do not know on what base pack here. Thought about maybe police force? But Bone Shadows do not seems to jive with idea.
                                        • Pack 2 - Iron Masters + Storm Lords - 'Neo-pagans' - Pack connected to local univeristy and all 'neo-pagan' movement in it.
                                        Variant 2
                                        • Pack 3 - Blood Talon + Iron Masters - Police force pack, watching over humans and local Uratha. Works as 'good cop, bad cop'. Can see interesting interactions if PCs will be connected to criminals here.
                                        • Pack 4 - Bones Shadows + Storm Lords - Neo-pagans from university, very serious about Nordic religion revival, and looking over Ridden and spirits.

                                        Writing this, it sounds to me that Variant 2 is general better packs spread and ideas...
