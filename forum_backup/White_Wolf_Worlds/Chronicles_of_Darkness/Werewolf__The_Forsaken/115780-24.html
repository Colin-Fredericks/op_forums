                                                        Originally posted by Monghani View Post
                                                        Despite being the one that suggested the Strength and Rage gift teasers I wonder how the social side of things fall for Uratha now. I figure the Gift of Dominance is the go-to thing, and wonder if the 1e restriction of only working on mortals, wolf-blooded, and werewolves is still there.
                                                        I remember mentioning this quite a while back; the Gift of Dominance no longer has its restriction on targets, although one of the Facets is essentially restricted because it alters the effect of Lunacy (which won't affect werewolves, other supernatural beings etc).

                                                        The old Gift of Dominance was largely 'give an order that must be obeyed' with increasing effect as the dots rose. The new one doesn't work really work like that - if nothing else, it's five individual powers rather than the old one's effective 3 powers. More than that, though, I looked towards making several of the Dominance Facets interface with the social manouever system, where the Vampire powers tend to do an end-run round them.

                                                        - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
