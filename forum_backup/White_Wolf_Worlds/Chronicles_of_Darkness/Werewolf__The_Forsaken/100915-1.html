                            You know, one thing I just realized, that I kind of love, is that the Blood Talons clearly hunt werewolves. Not only does it flat out say that, it clarifies by saying they mostly hunt the Pure, but not just them.

                            All the other tribes are werewolves.

                            Why I like this is that it both feels like a fulfillment and subversion of the classic warrior class thing. On the one hand, throughout history, leaders of armies have usurped the conventional ruler and used their might to subjugate a populace, and with an advantage against werewolves, the Blood Talons, who were the classic fighter/warrior tribe before, can now do this, and it's a subtle threat in any interaction with them-declaring yourself a Blood Talon comes with the unsaid promise of "I can fuck you up if I want to". On the other hand, werewolves are not the average citizen populace, nor is any alpha like most emperors or kings of humanity-each and everyone of them has the unstated promise of "You can fuck me up, but I can and will make you take the mileage for it".

                            It's kinda cool.

                                    I'd say hold off on that assessment. Human cultures are informed by the elements of their environment,including what they hunt-contrast native Americas with their buffaloes and bobcats and India with their elephants and tigers. The contrast that with African tribes with their elephants and Siberian tribes with their tigers. What we hunt shapes our culture as much as the way our rivers, our fields and deserts, and the rest of our environments do.

                                    It doesn't take much how the Blood Talons we knew became the way they are by focusing on werewolves, nor Iron Masters with Humanity or the Bone Shadows with Spirits. Their chosen prey reflects on them pretty easily. The culture, I assume, is still there, it's just now more sharply defined by a root idea-an idea that, by the way, is radically different from human sources(or at least more alien). As an example, I don't think many cultures have kept hunting as such a central position to their culture as the Uratha have.

                                        Things to note in new world vs old:

                                        Apocalypse features Tribes based on cultural identities and ideologies. Spirits operate much differently in Apocalypse (lore wise not mechanics)

                                        Forsaken features Tribes that appear to be grouped more on how their spirit responds. They are creatures of spirit and should be driven to certain things like most spirits (Idigam aside).

                                        I always liked that Forsakens tribes were ideological over cultural. That way we don't have a whole pack of say... almost nazi werewolves in the first edition.

                                        And oi! Blood Talons damn well deserve to take it to the pure! They train their bodies from the beggining to be weapons. They are (or were) the combined ideas of the Wendigo and Fenrir without the racial bias. Not to say they aren't capable of differing activities, but Tribes of the Moon makes it pretty clear they're a "screw this shit, we're hitting it hard first" Tribe, and what else pushes the limits of physical combat and wits besides another uratha?

                                        The Tribes all fit very well when ya think about it. The Storm Lords hunting other uratha as their alpha status? Nein, I'd say it makes more sense to lead by example. And so we have the "Alpha" tribe performing Urfahra's duties in the physical, while the Bone Shadows perform a similar hunt in the Hisil. The Storm Lords are still in the position to lead, and with the urged and claimed priorities, probably the best at dealing with incursions in the territory from such threats. They're the ones with the eyes on the prize as it were. Blood Talons are just out to kick ass and take renown.

                                        Edit: I previously thought of the Iron folk as being something similar to Glass walkers, and wanted nothing to do with them. This new motif of theirs makes them sound almost sexy as player characters.

                                                    It seems more like the situation where the ST should tailor the antagonists to the PCs. If you're a group made up mostly of Blood Talons the enemy should primarily be other werewolves, but that doesn't mean you don't deal with spirits/spirit-claimed/shartha/humanity/whatnot. Its just that by slapping your Tribe down on the sheet it says to the ST this is what my character favors for their hunting.

                                                    I figure it'll work similarly to how Agendas work in Demon. You gain Beats for dealing with your Tribal Prey, and resolving it nets you neat bonuses to finish the deal. Think of it more as an incentive for favoring something rather than a detriment.

                                                    Besides, I doubt your powers stop working because the enemy isn't the one you normally like to chew on.
