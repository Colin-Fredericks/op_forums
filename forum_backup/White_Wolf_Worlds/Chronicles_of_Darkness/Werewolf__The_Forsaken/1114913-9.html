        Originally posted by Ever Professional View Post
        I think we should also apply the various covenant advantages in this discussion as well, at least to make it a bit more even.

        The Invictus and Carthians have some of the best social merits hands down, and it can be debated which one beats the other in that regard. So when you deal with vampires, it's not just vampires you'll be dealing with. You'll be dealing with every bit of influence they have.

        Then we have the Ordo Dracul, who are basically vampire scientists. They'be unlocked certain aspects of the Kindred condition: some are absolute terrors on the battlefield, others are stronger against fire and sunlight, some more able to control mortal society, and some have super natural merits not available to kindred. The Ordo also typically get more involved in mortal occult societies as well, so they might have some resources against spirits.

        Then the Circle of the Crone can some entities from another realm, cause storms, mess with ghosts to an extent, and can create havens where everything alive fights their enemies.

        Then we have the Lancea et Sanctum who can pull of miracles to empower kindred warriors, help kindred find safe havens, and even blot out the sun.

        So I don't think this battle should lean incredibly far towards werewolves unless we're pulling out things like Influence Vitae.
        Things like temporal power would matter if their antagonists functioned within the same spheres of influences. We've got casual examples of werewolves leaving their human lives behind to the exclusive aim of getting rid of an infestation in their city, and to the lengths of spiritual bargains they would go in order to hunt their quarry to virtual extinction. Which Haven would be safe if a pack went to such lenght as to firebomb them during daylight from the relative comfort of the Shadow with the relevant elemental facets? Vampires have too many weaknesses that are easily exploitable by the apex predators of Pangaea.
