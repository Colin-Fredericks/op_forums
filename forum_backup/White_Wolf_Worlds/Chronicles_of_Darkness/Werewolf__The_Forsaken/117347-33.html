                        I hope that Lodges are different this time around.

                        A player of mine loved lodges for the fact that they brought some specific bonds and connections into the game. I think he was right, but many of the lodges presented were just plain crazy.
                        Others were so dull you found little to no reason why a pack of diverse PC's should care to connect with a bunch of Uratha who had gotten it wrong.

                        I always felt the options given were narrow-minded and far too dense to build a chronicle around.
                        Lodges were a bit like "the very special club for very special wolves" or simply: a frathouse.
                        And sometimes a very boring one.

                        They were groups that had a common connection but that ultimately fail to create interest in their views or their causes for PC's.
                        Mainly because Lodges were so specific or nit-picky on who they allowed that many players felt they came of as elitist and game-stopping.
                        It was like the worlds tiniest political party without any pull for votes because they lack a popular agenda.

                        Why would anyone join that? To be with the cool kids? Lodges were a serious flaw in my opinion. Sometimes an interesting flaw, but most of the times it felt like an attempt to make another layer of fraternization that was not needed.

                        The best lodges were few and far between, but those that went more towards mystical societies with interesting views on origins and the hisil I found to be more interesting. The mystery society with a focus on hisil or the cult that is slowly going nuts from the inside are good lodges that would be fun to use in a game. Lodges with norse myths, greek myths or similar we-have seen-this-done-500-times-already are not so interesting.

                        But then the requirements were just ludicrus, and any group of PC's would go: "Oh, I'm not a Storm-Lord" followed by a sad face. The ones who did play Storm-Lords did not even care about the lodges. Why would they? They were awesome without them!

                        Also something I would like to see: Lodges more akin to Covenants. 4 or 5 of those.Large-scale loose fit structures with a clear interest in you and your packs beliefs. Maybe with different views on creation myths (Father moon and Mother wolf anyone?), on the World of Darkness in general and on how to tackle the different challenges of a larger scale. Would be fun for something bigger than pack-scale games.It is always more fun with inclusive groups who want your players than a group that actually goes out f its way to make it hard for the ST to introduce them. Seriously. That sucks.

                        Also: A how to make your own lodge chart would be totally cool. :-)
                        (Just don't make it into something similar to that dry and boring spirit-flowchart in GMC)

                            As many have expressed already, a little consistency would be nice with Lodges in the future as they've tended to be a bit all over the place in terms of theme, prerequisites, and benefits. I'm not advocating for a complete departure from that paradigm, but bringing them a little closer together would go a long way to resolve some of the issues they present. I'd also like to see a little more from the Pillar Lodges, I really dig the idea that they form major branches of the tribes themselves and therefore they should be a bit more prominent as a result.

                            Smaller, more niche, or prestige lodges I'm somewhat on the fence about. They have their place, but since you're only allowed to be in a single Lodge at a time it feels weird. Why would my character who has been a member of the Lodge of Thunder for decades renounce his alliance with them so he can join the Lodge of Scars? I can come up with a few in-context reasons, but the short answer is that they probably wouldn't. It almost feels to me like there should be a second category of Lodges or something like Lodges to fulfill this, but I can understand the need to keep things relatively simple. I'm not sure there's a simple answer to that.
