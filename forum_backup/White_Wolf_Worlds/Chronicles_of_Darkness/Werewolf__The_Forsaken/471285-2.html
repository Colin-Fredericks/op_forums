                                            Originally posted by nofather View Post
                                            Yes, but Influence is a specific kind of magic that plays up the natural properties of an existing concept, emotion, animal or plant or element or anything. Fire doesn't really have a property of being hard or solid to begin with, so why would Strengthening Fire make a solid fire? You get the same dilemma with air.                                             Well fire isn't matter, so it's not the best analogy. But yeah, Strengthen shouldn't be able to make non-solid Mater viscous enough to support any significant amount of weight, let alone give it a positive Durability rating. Strengthen just enhances an Influence's natural properties, it doesn't make it do things it couldn't naturally do. Likewise, Manipulate alters an Influence within the constraints of its natural properties. It just happens that the conditions on earth are such that water locally has very versatile natural properties. But any changes that go beyond those constraints (such as making it effectly solid without freezing it) would require Control.

                                            Although, this conversation has lead me to the conclusion that freezing water probably would require Manipulate, not Strengthen. So there's definitely value in this kind of exploration of the possibilities of Influences.
