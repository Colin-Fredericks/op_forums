                                    After some quick testing I will tentatively rescind some of my concern about the bullet dodging properties, but I'm still generally concerned about Gauru's immense durability.

                                    The two test scenarios, included a classic encounter between a completely fresh (i.e. 0xp) Werewolf and four middling human hunters with a bit of silver. And a not so classic encounter between the same Werewolf and a tricked out Vampire soldier without any silver. Both can be viewed here:

                                    Werewolf vs Plucky Country Folk
                                    Werewolf vs Vampire Super Soldier

                                    The big find, well confirmation really, is that without silver or another source of aggravated damage there odds of killing Gauru in single combat are next to zero. Even in large groups attackers must be extremely well coordinated or their attacks will be healed away like they're nothing. The claims that a Rahu can take on a tank (as in the armored siege weapon) are not BS, it might not even need Gifts...

                                    At the same time I found the damage from a Werewolf's claws and bite a little lack luster. Claws were offset by heavy clothing, which seems a little weak.

                                    Suggestions:
                                    • Use the Higher of Wits or Dexterity for defense in Gauru, Urshul and Urhan.
                                    • Increase claw and bite damage in Gauru to +2 and +4 respectively (being grappled by Gauru should be horrifying).
                                    • Reduce Gauru Lethal healing to a high, but manageable number. Stamina (including the +2 from Gauru) might do, that'd have had the test Werewolf recovering 5 lethal per action, enough to largely undo the effects of the four hunters, but not totally outpace the combat-monstrosity vampire.
                                    • Allow controlled rage a little more flexibility with its options. Attacking each turn is fine, but being able to choose who to attack would make the form a lot scarier.
                                    Edit: A little bit more testing has revealed Stamina + Primal Urge, makes a werewolf ample durable. That leaves our sample wolf recovering 6 per turn, half her health, and enough to make the Plucky Country Folk scenario play out nearly unchanged. While the Super Soldier scenario ends in 5-6 turns as opposed to the previous ~15.
