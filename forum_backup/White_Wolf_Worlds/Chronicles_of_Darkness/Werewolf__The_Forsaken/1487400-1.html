    22)Soulless Wolf, like all Firstborn of Father Wolf, has It’s own Place-That-Isn’t, and this realm can only be found by going to the sites of humankind’s deepest mines and furthest underground exploration. By chanting and bloodletting, self-flagellation and ritual torture, one will eventually find a crack in the deep earth....leading down. Down...to it.

    Fitting down this crack takes supernatural aid, as it is only a few inches wide. Unearthly sounds can be heard if one listens at this crack long enough: mad laughter, soft weeping, someone screaming for help, the sound of gunshots and beatings and even worse. All coming from below, ever downwards.

    Should one somehow manage to follow this crack in the Earth downwards, one would enter an upside world, with infinite inky oblivion below and torturous Labyrinthian paths above. Everything is wrong, in a way that’s hard to define in any meaningful sense. From below bellows forth forlorn wolf-howls full of absolute despair.

    There are no inhabitants of the maze. It just goes on and on, never repeating itself twice. Many make it no further and slowly starve to death.

    Bizarre markings etch the wall, but they make no sense. No etchings in the First Tongue, but in a language nonetheless; it’s just a language one must be mad and evil and depraved to have any hope of figuring out. Once deciphered, these messages lead down routes that are otherwise impossible to find or follow, til one reaches a deeper level altogether.

    As Above, So Below. To go down, one must go up. It is a contradiction, but it is nonetheless true. Crazed human cultists wander this hidden layer of the maze, their eyes gnawed out and their tongues removed. Freakish gargoyle-like spirits wander to and fro, and drag victims partially into the walls, where they suffocate to death. Hearths full of fire that burns but does not consume may be found here or there. Telephones connecting to somewhere dot the walls, connected to mysterious cable that one can’t follow. Pick up a phone, and you’ll hear something so terrible you’ll ram pencils into your eardrums to make it stop.

    Shadows move and slither, angles are wrong, proportions are off, contradictory architecture everywhere. Passages don’t lead the same way twice.

    Eventually, it finds all intruders. Soulless Wolf stalks the ceiling, its body turned inside out in grotesque parody of normal physiology. It will hunt you, but it won’t harm you more than it must. It’s herding you, you see, to an even deeper level. Those that go there never return.

            24)Even machine-spirits have Places-That-Aren’t, it seems. By entering the Highhopfer building at exactly 11:11 pm, using elevator three and going to the following floors in the following order (10, 1, 1, 2, 22, 11), one finds oneself in a computer lab more advanced than anything the pack has ever seen before.

            Glowing computer screens calculate the orbits of every asteroid in the solar system, another computer keeps constant tabs of the exact numbers of pigeons in the city, while yet another records every game of Backgammon it can, by whatever means it can. This data is archived, then sent...somewhere.

            And then vaguely insectile machine-spirits unfold from the walls, whirring, flesh-tearing blades, armored chassises and magnetic rail guns, ready to destroy the intruders.

                25)Some Places-That-Aren’t seem completely outside cosmology as werewolves understand it, and the Little Labyrinth is one such place.

                A glass labyrinth just large enough for a werewolf in wolf-shape to crawl through, made of glass with a strong, refreshing breeze running through from some unknowable distant place, the labyrinth is in the sky above an infinite plain of reeds and flooded farmland. The glass is unbreakable, and escaping to the sea of marshy reeds Below is impossible. Above, the sky is perpetually at night, with Luna absent entirely.

                Uratha who look at these stars long enough can invoke prophecies in themselves as Cahalith can.

                Below them, the fields are empty of spirits, though, occasionally, light will shine down from the infinite starry abyss, depositing a single man of Egyptian ethnicity, nude but otherwise healthy. He can’t see the Uratha or the Little Labyrinth, and seems content to stare at the stars in wonder.

                Uratha here have a great, immense feeling this place is not for them. Most feel compelled to try to leave, though the strong willed can resist it.

                Getting to the Little Labyrinth happens when a Uratha shifts into a wolf and enters a cardboard ‘maze’ built by a young child in Egypt. The doorways in can’t be forced open, and the Little Labyrinth eventually vomits an intruding werewolf back out.

                26)Easily enterable, the Place-That-Isn’t under Three Mile Island is a biotoxin dumpnest, full of irradiated monuments of cancer-ridden tissue, great pulsating zits the size of large dogs growing from the ground, and the constant sound of wheezing and labored breathing.

                Radiation-spirits abound, but take the form of hideously mutated zombies. They can only regain Essence through the consumption of sentient beings, be they spirit, mortal or otherwise. Surprisingly, these spirits are not Wounded, and prey upon Maeltinets and Bale Hounds as fervently as they do Pure and Forsaken.

                            29)A strange Claimed ‘immortal’ (‘purified’ in some unspecified manner) has tamed a colony of Termite Hosts and built herself quite a Place-That-Isn’t in Yosemite Park. A cathedral of tunnels lined with fossilized bones, this nexus of Stone bodies could sustain thousands of people with its fungal crops—-assuming the mega-Claimed in control of the whole thing cared to do so.

                            30)Norse werewolves claim to have found Jotunheim. A Place-That-Isn’t bound behind ancient seals, past the stone archway that blocks the path is a vast frozen forest where giants roam. Some are spirits, to be clear, but others are shuddering Hive-Claimed behemoths and creatures of primeval fear drawn from deepest human prehistory, while still others appear spun from dreams.

                                31)Not all Places-That-Aren’t are in the Hisil—-sometimes, a pinched-off zone physical reality occurs, and some unknowable force seals it away.

                                Such is the case of the Land of the Maggot Titans. Appearing for all intents and purposes as a sealed-off alternate timeline, this version of Earth is dominated by vast chthonic sea beasts, who rule over the land as part of a ‘wicked tribe’. Divided into squabbling factions, these fish-monsters proclaim themselves living gods, and exist in a sealed off timeline stretching from 1929 to 1950, when the alternate world ends in fire and cleansing light.
