    Starting a new game - any suggested house rules or commonly overlooked actual rules?

    02-04-2016, 03:43 PM
    So I've gone through the 2nd ed core book about twice and I'm having some players over to write up characters this weekend. I'm super excited about the game, but I haven't played any nWoD system games before (I've only run oWoD in the past). Do you guys have any suggestions for starting to GM my first Werewolf game?

    Specifically, I'm looking for house rules you've found helpful, actual rules I might have overlooked, or just suggestions about what to expect from the system (like, how combat flows, etc)?

      It's easy to overlook the contagiousness of death rage, but the big thing I've seen a lot of people misunderstand has been the choice of personalized suites of triggers. Basically you pick one group, like the Blood, the Other, or Moon triggers on p103, and they apply to your character. This way different members of the pack can have different triggers. Though there are a few triggers at the end of the list applicable to all werewolves.

      Another thing is that Sacred Prey is not 'only prey.' No one is forced to only hunt their sacred prey. Also, with the opening up of rites, only one member of a pack really needs to have the Sacred Hunt rite.

          I would recommend against any sort of proactive house ruling until you have a better feel for the system: the system as printed was designed to work for most groups, most of the time; house rules are designed to work for one group, and one group only.

          I would, however, recommend for establishing "table rules": expectations for the players. My table rules are:

          1) Players must create characters that will participate in the story: this is a CofD game, not a mundane life simulator.
          2) Player versus Player social rolls are only allowed if both players agree to the roll and the "defending" character is genuinely on the fence about an action.
          3) Combat PvP is a no.

          Rule 3 applies only to deadly force, if two character want to spar and use dice to determine the winner, that's fine.

                  I actually disagree about no PvP, even when it comes to combat. that said I have a regular group that I know enjoys that kind of play and that I trust to handle it maturely. If I was running for a new or unknown group I would not want to allow PvP.

                  I like to give everyone an extra Shadow Gift at character creation. Doesn't have a huge impact but it makes for a slightly higher power game out of the gate. I also use the extra Auspices from Signs of the Moon (except for Stellar Eclipse, because I didn't want to convert the Moon Gift and "stellar eclipses" aren't a thing anyway).

                  I would recommend running the game by RAW your first time and adjusting with house rules to suit your taste once you've got a good feel for the baseline.

                          This isn't a house rule, but just one piece of advice I find essential to Chronicles of Darkness. Make sure to apply modifiers to dice rolls frequently (when appropriate). As a storyteller you're often too distracted a lot of the time so I make it a habit of every time someone rolls the dice asking myself whether there should be a modifier. The system expects it in a lot of places, there's a lot of abilities that interact with modifiers that never get used if you don't. Most dice rolls should have at least a +1 if there's something making it easier or a -1 if there's something making it more difficult.

                          Don't be afraid to use conditions frequently. They're hard to get used to but they're one of the main sources of XP in the new system, use them wherever you can.

                          Werewolf gifts can be quite powerful right out of the gate. It's not like the old game where you worked your way through weak abilities to get to strong abilities. They're all amazingly powerful and useful, you just get more dice and a more varied range as you get more powerful.

                                                      I have a bit of a problem with Kuruth triggers in a sense that some of them are a lot more broad than the other. "Seeing someone attack a Pack member" is a lot more common than interacting with Wound spirits. Hell, maybe for some narrative reasons you don't want to have Wounds in your chronicle and the trigger never will apply. Similar thing with Blood trigger, it seems that between tasting and swallowing blood you leave choice to the player (unless you design some convoluted scene where villains force-feed human blood to the PC) while other PC can be triggered by Death Rage on some fancy party when a hapless vampire tries to seduce/dominate him with no choice left to him.

                                                      There's no easy and perfect answer how to solve that, but I personally would every lunar month (or other miscellaneous circumstance like maybe meeting a Lune corresponding to the Auspice) roll or make player choose new Kuruth trigger, also among new ones made on forum.

                                                      Or maybe just choose 2 and alternate them depending on the Harmony - "The Other" trigger could apply to higher harmony werewolves and "Territory" to lower.

                                                      Also look on the new Blood & Bone archetypes for more character customization.
