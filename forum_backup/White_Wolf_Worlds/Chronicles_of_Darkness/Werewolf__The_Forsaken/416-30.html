        As some mentioned housreules (and big controversial changes), why not keep the tradition from the chromnicler guide and make another supplement about how to really alter werewolves that encorporates the new rules?

        Werewolves without much control and no connection to the shadows, with fewer forms, lesser werewolves that are more like Skinchangers...

        As wererwolf has a distinct flavour, even one little chapter about how to make it a different game would likely be nice and maybe a good way to appease people who want more radical changes.

        I think this and the Fera discussion showed that we just want quite a few different things when it comes to all that goes BEYOND better mechanics and a general clean up of the rules.

        In general, I think werewolf tried too much to sell one rather specific image instead of a broader gallery.
        It is really good if writers are passionate about what they think is a good game, but ultimately, it is the storyteller and the players who bring it to life and they all have their own idea about what makes a good werewolf story.

        I think trying to make the game so that it opens up to as many of those ideas as possible while still offering lots of interesting storyhooks is the most important.

        last tangent about other shifters: I quite disagree with Thorbes suggestion about having them be sired as a werewolf mockery by the Idigam or similar.
        Just let them be their culturally distinct thing, don't make them mere satellites orbiting the Forsaken.
        There are so many legends or fictional ideas about shapeshifters that work well with the Forsaken setting about spirits and the shadow, all the stories featuring animal gods or bloodlines can work wonderfully to make werewolf like part spirit shifters.
        I think the only thing one might have to worry about is the fact that by now, a good deal of other lines have claimed certain shapeshifting myths (Selkies and similar by Changeling for example), so I would not try to hard to work shifters in whose mythological origin better suits another line (Though a reference to them could help).
