            20 Beats in total were earned. We're using the Group Beats optional rule.

            Started with 4 Beats base, one per player as the 'participation' Beat. Then I threw in 5 additional Beats at the end for general story progress etc (it wasn't necessarily going to be 5 specifically but we were at 15 at that point so it felt nice to end the first session with everyone getting an Experience). Of the remaining 11, I think... 4 or 5 were tagged from Aspirations? The rest were a mixture of Conditions resolving (all gained by exceptional successes) and bonus Beats based off stuff happening in game.

                        02-22-2015, 09:08 AM
                        Our second session went well! Weird entities, strange phone calls (including from the data idigam!), sorting the pack name out and ritually claiming their territory. The Bone Shadow is carefully keeping track of every slight that a spirit gives him for later retribution, while still dealing with them for the time being. The Storm Lord is carefully making a list of Things To Do in sorting their territory out. The Blood Talon is providing the blunt voice of practicality, as only a person with Intelligence 1 can do The Wolf-Blooded is planning on how to get the equipment he needs to start making bronze arrowheads, because that will please the totem.

                        The totem's just being weird, including scaring the fuck out of one of the human pack members who was going to take out some rubbish that included (gasp) reusable metal in it. Sacrelige!

                        So they've made their mark on the banlieue, to the point where their ritual claiming of the territory involved spraying over the gang graffiti tags with their own and even roughing up some of the drug dealers etc they met. This caused a retaliatory graffiti attack on the pack's home, the junkyard. This in turn caused them to go and find the gang members responsible, resulting in them breaking into the drug den, resulting in one gang member trying to do a runner out of a window and onto the rooftop, resulting in the Wolf-Blooded and his Parkour merit going after them, resulting in a rooftop chase that didn't get very far before the gang member slipped and nearly fell off the roof, saved only from a messy death by a lucky roll from the Wolf-Blooded.

                        Interestingly, the Wolf-Blooded also identified Hunter marks amongst the graffiti sprayed on the junkyard, but he doesn't recognise what organisation they're from. He also has absolutely abysmal social skills and hence utterly fluffed his attempt to poke his Hunter contacts for info. Back when he worked for the Office, there were research teams to do that sort of thing for him. Not any more :P

                        Another pack's Elodoth also spent some time laying into him for being a weak link in the pack and untrustworthy, then spent time laying into the rest of the pack for the fact they'd let her chew out one of their pack members without leaping to his defence.

                        Anyway, fun times. Ancient dust-breathing women who claim to be part of something called 'The Chancel', a phone call from a being calling itself The Operator that quite happily gave them one free answer to any question in celebration of their pack's formation, and a belligerent road-car-spider-thing spirit that gave the Ithaeur all sorts of info about the local spirit politics in return for that tasty, tasty Essence. And now it knows there's a locus on the pack's turf, and is going to go tell that to its 'boss', the spirit noble of pollution and decay that's a middle-manager in an even bigger spirit of urban decay's court.

                        At some point I'll introduce the Ghost Wolf who's infected with Lamprey Hosts...

                                                            Today's game, while a day late due to toddler issues yesterday, was very good.

                                                            The pack have roughed up drug dealers, been given some 'helpful' information by an Amazigh Ivory Claw (who really disapproves of a criminal organisation they are clashing with because Magrebhi and white French mix in its ranks! Gasp!), encountered a poor bastard Ghost Wolf trying to nab Essence from their Locus who is infested with Ukusgualu, met an actual Ukusgualu and probably killed it, and ended up with the Ghost Wolf strapped to a medical table in a criminal back street surgery, slicing him open to yank the writhing lampreys out from his guts. The Ghost Wolf, who has wildly imbalanced Harmony, managed to pass double figures of Death Rage checks despite having a Willpower dice pool of 4! They also did their first proper Sacred Hunt rite (targeting the Ghost Wolf) and used the new tracking rules to great effect (when trying to track the Ghost Wolf down to his riverside lair).

                                                            End result of all that included a pretty massive pile of Beats - 29 in the pot from this session. Everyone's now earned 3 Experiences.
