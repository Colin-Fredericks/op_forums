                                        WelshBoi

                                        ​Verges don't seem to exist in Werewolf in second edition. Instead it's a Mage thing, with Supernal and Abyssal verges.

                                        Arcanist has it pretty on the dot. It's all in the Chronicles rules update, which you can get for free here. But they need the right Condition to get things Open, basically giving them some air to breathe on the other side once they cross. They start with something Resonant on the other side, and they either Strengthen it or something else occurs so that it's Open. At that point they can Reach across. To actually cross they need either Gauntlet Breach or Shadow Gateway. Then when they do cross they're in Twilight, which is a state of existence and not a place, and from there they can Materialize, giving themselves a physical form, or Fetter themselves to a nice source of Essence.

                                        A locus allows you to Reach without needing the Reach Manifestation, so basically any spirit could act across the Gauntlet around a locus.

                                        @Arcanist

                                        While loci were limited, in the default setting, to places or at least parts of places, such as a certain painting in a high-rise lobby, there was an optional rule to allow them to be more mobile objects and even people, ghosts and fetishes. There was even theoretical people who were walking Wounds or Shoals. This is all detailed in Lore of the Forsaken. Second edition seems to have said, 'Screw it, let's have fun,' with a lot of the default setting. Yes, it does mean that the Hisil can get screwed by someone or something moving, and that's part of the fun of the game, things are always happening. You've set up your rows of dominos, there wouldn't be much of a game if there wasn't a chance for them to be knocked over. If you really want to get rid of it, you can just not have any of them in your story, but by the rules it's still part of the 'default' setting.
