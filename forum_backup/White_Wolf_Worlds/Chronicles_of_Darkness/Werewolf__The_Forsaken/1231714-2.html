    Got back from holidays on Thursday, and of course one of the first things I did after getting off the plane was making sure everyone was ready for a game over the weekend. And with that we have...

    Session 9

    With their duties discharged and Christmas upon them, each of the Harbingers spent their Christmas Eve busying themselves in their own way. Regan wanted to do something nice for her aunt, so she spent the day baking. Orla had a present in mind for her new pack, so she sealed herself away in her room with a bucket of dead rats, a coat hanger, and an imprisoned wind spirit. Aaron had a morning mass to give, which he delivered in a half-hearted fashion. And Aed, well, Aed mostly just followed Aaron around all day, not having anything better to do with his time.

    The last hunt's events were weighing on Aaron, so he resolved after mass to confront the Whispers. Aed went with him against his wishes, but the Cahalith's presence proved worthwhile when they reached the Whispers' territory and were accosted by members of the constabulary. The two wolves had been lurking in the alley where Aed had previously contacted the Whispers, waiting for the other pack to show up, when they were spotted. The suspicious behaviour coupled with Aaron's obviously Catholic attire drew attention, but as the conversation went on the two Harbingers became increasingly aware that two of the officers they were speaking to were conspicuously keeping to the elongated shadows of the nearby buildings. Indeed, Blackstaff's spiritual afflictions had not been healed by the dispersal of the Suthilu, rather to Aed's disappointment.

    Aed kept the attention of the officers largely by talking about himself until Jamie, the young wait who appeared to lead the Whispers, showed up. Since they'd claimed they were only in the area to meet friends, the arrival of those friends convinced the constables that everything was in order... although Aaron did notice that the two who kept out of the sunlight didn't retreat very far. They went back into the alley with Jamie and found the wolf called Greyson lurking there, but no sign of the larger man, Dorian, Aed had met before.

    Throwing himself fully into the Cahalith role, Aed relayed the story of their hunt in Blackstaff. He left nothing out, beginning with tracking Ciara to the creature called We-Who-Eat-In-Darkness, and all the way to his single-handed triumph over the Bat Hosts. Jamie's attention was fixed from the moment he mentioned the magath, and when he finished she made him go over every detail of that encounter. Looking somewhat ashamed, she mentioned that the creature he described had once been the Whispers' Totem. Someone or something had convinced the spirit of gluttony that it should turn its appetites towards essence of a darker, shadowy nature, leading to its corruption. When the Whispers realised what had happened they excised the totem from the pack, but hadn't been able to locate it since. They thanked the Harbingers for alerting them to the Suthilu, but it was clear from the orders Jamie gave Greyson that the Whispers intended to go straight after We-Who-Eat-In-Darkness as soon as they could.

    At this point Aaron interrupted, warning the Whispers that intrusions into the Harbingers' territory wouldn't be tolerated any longer. Jamie looked at him flatly and declared that she rather thought he should be happy that another pack had chosen to take on the Pure problem on the Harbingers' behalf. She left without offering any particular commitment to keeping clear of Andersonstown, Greyson racing ahead of her.

    The two Harbingers returned to their territory. When Aaron reached St Huberts and sorted through his mail for the day, he found a plain envelope with a short message inside. "9pm, 26/12/81, 28 Longwood Avenue. The Hunters make their final run." While an opportunity to hunt with his fellow Hunters in Darkness was something he was looking forward to, Longwood Avenue lay in the territory of the Balelight Howl. He knew their leader, Mercy, personally as she'd been present for his own initiation into the Hunters, and he knew the Howl as a pack with no fewer than three Hunters as part of their membership. But the territory they claimed ran through Shankill, practically the birthplace of the UVF.

    That was all still two days away, however. Aed spent his afternoon trying to track down a Christmas party to crash, before finally giving up and going to Regan's place with a bottle of wine. Regan and her Auntie Steph were there, deep in a conversation about Regan's father and why he hadn't taken Regan in when her mother died. Steph had been trying to delicately explain that, with a wife and several daughters already, it hadn't really been possible for him to accept Regan. Regan had been halfway through asking Steph how she could contact her father when there was a heavy knock on the door. And with that Regan found herself having to explain that her new involvement with the church had led to her making friends with, among others, a B-grade movie star.

    Once the initial surprise had worn off, Aed had introduced himself to Steph, and the wine had been broken open, Regan found herself noting with some dismay that Aed and Steph were really begin to hit it off. She ducked off to her room and grabbed a mirror to invoke her Messenger Rite and contact Aaron, begging him to come over as a chaperone, and then started contacting anyone else she could think of on short notice by more mundane means. She spoke on the phone briefly with Jo Austin, asking her and her father to come over for a surprise Christmas dinner, and to bring anyone else they knew with no other plans.

    Before long, the small household had become quite cramped. The Austins, hearing that Aed was there, had brought their copies of Diamond Man 2 and their VCR and had the movie playing in the living room. Aed was alternating between telling stories about his movie career and acting as an impromptu bar tender, while Orla eventually tracked her packmates down and started awkwardly flirting with a couple of the miners while their wives were distracted. The wives, for their matter, were deep in conversation with Father Aaron as they asked for advice on how to deal with the sudden loss of income to their households following on from the mine closure. Regan kept trying to minimise how much one on one time Aed and Steph spent together, but then Jo wanted to thank her for organising this party and taking her father's mind off losing his job. By the time Regan extricated herself from that conversation she found an intoxicated Steph complaining to Aed about how Regan's own father considered himself so superior, how he'd always looked down his nose at their family. Rather than interrupt, Regan just lurked where she could listen in until the subject changed back to Aed.

    The party began to wrap up a little bit before midnight. One by one the humans began to trickle out of the household and returning to their beds. Aed lingered, but when it became clear that Aaron wouldn't let him be the last one to leave he reluctantly walked Orla back to his home. Aaron excused himself and went out on patrol, while Regan helped Steph to bed.

    Christmas day dawned with Steph swearing at the mess she had to clean up, and conscripting Regan to help her. Orla presented Aed with her gift, a bizarre parody of a wind chime made of wire and dried rat, with the Wisp inside compelled to clean away the scents of those who come near. Aed accepted it, frowning, and had a good long talk with her about how they had a responsibility to keep the denizens of the Shadow on their side as much as possible. Still, the Fetish would come in useful. Aed figured planting it somewhere out of the way and unconnected to the members of the pack would give them the ability to foil would-be trackers by erasing their trails, but he had no clear ideas yet of what a good location would be.

    Aaron was busy with his congregation, Regan with her one family member and chores, but Orla and Aed still had some time to kill and they were curious to see what the Shadow would be like on this day. They pushed their way through Hope's Locus, passing through the looking glass and into the ruins of burning Belfast. And there they found war. The cacophony of battle was everywhere, hulking, furred beasts grappled with ephemeral creatures of wind and air on every side. At a quick glance Aed could already spot several skirmishes being fought on his street, while larger spirits could be glimpsed moving among the ash in the near distance. The two of them slipped away rather than get caught up in the maelstrom, escaping back across the Gauntlet as fast as they could.

    On Christmas night, each of the wolves spotted lights flash on the slopes of the Old Man. Aaron, out on patrol, Orla and Aed, drinking on the porch as Orla smoked, and Regan staring out of her bedroom window, each saw a pulse of silver light on the mountain. It came again, a few seconds later. And then a third flash, red and raw and burning, leaving ugly afterimages in their eyes. Each of them set off, with Regan slipping out of the house quietly to avoid waking Steph. Aaron was the first to arrive and quickly located the source of the lights. The ground was churned while tangled, overgrown hedges looked blackened and burned. And there was blood, a great deal of it, splattered across the ground. The rest of the Harbingers arrived within minutes, shifting back to Hishu as they examined the scene. They were all pretty sure that the Whispers and Cleareyes had fought, and after a brief discussion they agreed that Orla and Aaron should taste some of the blood. Aaron gained a sense of a prey heading south at a steady trot, while Orla's impression was of someone moving north and fast. The Whispers lacked permission to be on Black Mountain, and they'd been warned, so as one the pack shifted into Urhan and raced north.

    Unfortunately, Orla was the slowest of the group and the one with a bead on their target. Racing ahead, Aed and Aaron tried to pick up the trail on their own, but doing so slowed them down and they all gathered again on the edge of town without having caught their prey red-handed. Aaron was spitting furious and tried to push forward into Blackstaff to run down the Whispers, but Aed tackled him in wolf form and kept him pinned until he agreed to wait for the others to arrive. At last, with the pack assembled, they decided that this conflict in their territory couldn't continue. They agreed to send Orla and Regan after the Whispers to ask them to meet with the Cleareyes, with the Harbingers as mediators, while Aed and Aaron went south to deliver the same message to the Fire-Touched.

    Of course, technically, the Cleareyes hadn't broken any law or promise. Aed mollified Aaron as they set off by pointing out that, should the Whispers accept, they would regret putting themselves at the mercy of the Harbingers.

        Session 10

        In the last few hours of Christmas night, the Harbingers ventured beyond their borders in the north and south. Regan, Orla, and Hope followed the trail of blood to a groundskeeper's shack on the grounds of a local school. While they were still at a safe distance, Regan invoked both her Cloak of Fear and Totemic Empowerment Rites to garb herself in hissing shadows and give herself the ability to manipulate the target's weaknesses. She approached, moving quietly, and saw a large, injured man rummaging around in a trunk. She walked out and greeted him, scaring the ever-living crap out of him, and turned real waspish at his panicked questions. After seeing how angry he was getting at her snarky answers she finally introduced herself, and they were able to have a proper conversation. The injured wolf was Dorian, and he gave her a quick rundown on what had happened. The three Whispers had been roving south, intending to raid the borders of the Cleareyes' territory again, when they ran into a Pure who burned like fire on the slopes of Black Mountain. They fought, but the Pure did something awful to Greyson that caused his flesh to bubble and boil and sent him screaming worse than anything Dorian had ever heard before, then subdued Jamie and dragged her off while Dorian ran. Regan asked Dorian to come with her, telling him that they intended to bring the Pure in for a meeting to try and end the war peacefully, and she convinced him only thanks to the New Moon's shadowy gift allowing her to slip inside his guard.

        Down south, Aaron and Aed had crossed into Cleareyes territory. They crossed through the fields beyond Belfast in Urhan form, finally shifting back to Dalu to stalk through a small village only a short distance outside the city. The blood they were following led them unerringly through the village, to the start of a narrow dirt road winding away from town and into a forested thicket. It was at the start of that trail that they spotted a man waiting in the lee of a building, small spirits of hedge and nature clambering across his body and poking and prodding him towards the two Uratha. The man introduced himself as Peadar, a Wolf-Blooded of the Cleareyes pack, and after they said they needed to speak to Eaven he agreed to lead them to her. They followed the dirt road on foot for a good half hour before the thicket parted and they saw the Cleareyes' compound, four large buildings facing out onto a central square, a rust-coloured monolith at its heart. Peadar led them straight to the largest building, and up close the Harbingers noted that while the buildings looked new, the foundations at their base were very old.

        Aed entered first, and found himself in a room with four other Uratha. Eaven was reclining on the lounge, holding a china doll to her chest and her head in the lap of another young woman with a discoloured patch of skin running down her throat. This new woman was cleaning Eaven's many wounds carefully, even keeping them from scabbing over in order to rise out the injuries. The two others, a man and a woman, both got to their feet as he entered. The man was of indeterminate age, emaciated, flannel clothing hanging like loose sails from his frame, his face covered by a thick beard. The woman looked to be in her fifties, her clothes homespun and adorned in iron medallions, and she was already shifting to Dalu in response to the intrusion. Aed's senses were keen enough to pick up Eaven saying, "No sunlight. No food either, until-" but all conversation ceased as he went in.

        After a few moments of staring down the room, Eaven said, "I don't recall giving permission for you to return the favour. What brings you two into my home?"

        Aed explained that they'd grown tired of their territory being used as a battleground, and that he hoped to end the war. At that, Eaven grinned. The war was over, she told them. She'd met the Whispers and broken them. One, Jamie, was in her keeping, one had been disposed of, and the third had run like a whipped cur. No need for negotiations, there was no one left to fight.

        Unfortunately, Aed really didn't like the idea of the Cleareyes keeping one of the Whispers. He insisted that Eaven attend the negotiations, and that she bring Jamie with her. Eaven was both a little incredulous and beginning to get angry. She had absolutely no reason to talk things over with the last Whisper, if he even had the courage to show up. She certainly had no reason to risk giving up her prize. She warned Aed that the Harbingers would find themselves quite isolated if their fellow Forsaken found out that they'd been dealing with the Cleareyes, and told him not to press this any further. Aed told her that he was confident he'd be able to talk the other packs around and that he wasn't scared, which finally prompted her to get off the couch.

        At around this point, Aed noticed the eyes of the china doll were looking at him. They were eyes he recognised, the compound, refracted glare of an angry wasp. Eaven noticed him looking and mentioned that while she'd been hunting the Whispers, this spirit had come to her seeking protection. It had promised to serve her in a fetish in return for being taken away and kept safe, and she had used it to help her deal with the Whispers. Aaron snarled at the doll, remembering that they'd ordered Mazdarix to hop across the Gauntlet rather than stay in the Flesh, but Aed turned his attention back to the confrontation with Eaven. He offered to represent her fairly at the negotiation with the Whispers, make sure that there was no retaliation, and allow her to remain in her compound so long as she allowed Jamie to come with him. The audaciousness of such a one-sided deal took Eaven off-guard and she wavered between laughing and ordering him to get out, ultimately settling on the latter.

        The Harbingers reconvened back on the slopes of Black Mountain with Dorian in tow. They worked out where they stood, and decided that if they wanted to go up against the Cleareyes and get Jamie back then they had a few things they needed to do. The Fetish made from Mazdarix was clearly something they had to get out of the Pure's hands, so Regan would need to steal it away before any confrontation. They would then try and mount a distraction while Regan rescued Jamie. But first, Aed wanted to see if they could track down Greyson. If they could get another Blackstaff wolf back on his feet it would probably help steel the spine of the very shaken Dorian and help to balance out the Cleareyes' numbers. They asked Dorian to identify which patch of blood-sodden ground was where Greyson had been hit, and then used that to allow Regan to track his trail.

        They moved quickly, the pack spreading out as they pushed to reach Greyson as fast as they could in case he was clinging to life and in need of medical attention. Regan raced far ahead of the others and reached the source of the trail. Greyson lay slumped against a rotten trunk, his body ravaged in sores and gaping wounds. He was in Urshul, his massive body covered in crushed fragments of mushrooms and splintered wood. Regan approached cautiously, listening to his liquid, laboured breathing. She watched as a small, juvenile wasp crawled out from a wound just beneath his ear and flexed its wings. She crouched, baring her teeth and ready to attack the small creature, when Greyson lurched to his feet. The sores across his body moved and shifted, larvae and carapace-covered bodies shifting within his flesh. He lunged at Regan, tearing a terrible wound open across her side and forcing her into Garou to survive. As her flesh knit back together, she grappled with Greyson and tried to pin him. Stronger than him in her larger form, she tried to call on Hope's powers to strengthen his mind and allow him to overthrow the influence of the creatures possessing him... but instead she got the sense that his mind was far away and too distant for her influences to effect.

        Luckily, the others reached her before the Death Rage began to overwhelm her. Aed pinned Greyson beneath him and rendered him immobile, giving Regan the time to conduct a basic first aid examination. She tried pulling some of the larvae from his wounds, crushing them as they emerged. Dorian squatted down to watch, and they could clearly see that as Greyson's body tried to knit itself back together it was repairing itself with wasp larvae already in the regenerated flesh. Whatever curse Eaven had unleashed on him with Mazdarix's help, it would require a Rite to repair. Even though he was a Bone Shadow, Dorian couldn't think of any local spirits who might know such a Rite.

        By this point, the sun was starting to creep up over the horizon. Regan needed to be home soon, before Steph was up and about. They realised that the only time Greyson showed hostility towards them was when they tried to harm the wasps, and otherwise he seemed "content" to lie on the ground and allow the larvae to eat away at him from the inside. With Regan having tasted his blood, they decided that they would break for the day, work out a solution, and track him down again once they had some idea of how to help him.

        Dorian returned to Blackstaff, saying he had a shotgun with silver rounds that he would make ready. The Harbingers returned to their respective homes. Regan was approaching her front door when she was accosted by a man stinking of sweat and stale alcohol. His hands pawed desperately at her jacket as he begged her to please, please, please undo what she had done. Regan was baffled and backed up, bracing herself against the door, and she demanded to know who he was. For the briefest moment they made eye contact and she saw his left eye, with a milky white pupil and an iris the colour of blood. Her essence revolted at the sight, feeling like a full body vomit, and the man ducked his head again to stare at the floor, ashamed. He muttered that his name was Arthur Cosgrave, he was an officer with the Ulster Royal Constabulary, and the last thing he remembered before the world went mad was standing guard at a crime scene and making eye contact with her. He begged her again to fix whatever it was that she'd done to him.

        Regan remembered using her Gifts to inflict Lunacy at a glance, filling the constable's flesh with essence so that Hope could possess him and let the pack easily access the scene at Ciara's home after their initial investigation. She realised that in the throws of Lunacy, Arthur had come to Luna's attention. She had adopted him as Wolf-Blooded. And with that Regan realised that she had no idea how to help.

                                        Session 11

                                        As the desperate, newly Wolf-Blooded constable grasped and pleaded at Regan to take back what she'd done, she carefully located and stole the handgun he had tucked into the back of his jeans. Better safe than sorry. Feeling a bit more comfortable now, she explained what she could at short notice to Arthur. He was Wolf-Blooded, something she didn't believe there was any cure to. When he asked if that's what she was too, she laughed. The earnestness of her next words cut short Arthur's attempt to reject what she was saying, but she explained that she was a werewolf and that there were others like her. Then, realising that she'd been about to enter her home with her face still daubed in blood and clay from the rituals she'd been performing, she asked Arthur for his handkerchief. At that he noticed the fact his gun was missing and went very pale and frantic. He didn't notice that Regan had pocketed it, and instead asked if they could meet again to talk about this more. He'd lost something very important and needed to try and find out where he'd dropped it. Regan agreed easily, told him to go to St Hubert's when he was ready and ask for Father Aaron, and let him go. She crept inside, made sure her aunt still slept, and washed her face.

                                        A few hours later, the pack convened at St Hubert’s in order to discuss strategy for taking on the Cleareyes. Aed laid out a range of options, but made sure the pack understood their priorities. They needed to get Mazdarix away from Eaven and rescue Jamie. Aed had a few ideas on how to achieve these objectives, ranging from the comparatively bloodless to an attempt to slaughter the entire Pure pack. He started to talk about how they need to find out a cure for Greyson first, to up their numbers, and that their best bet from there would be to take the Cleareyes unaware. Aaron objected to this. He demanded that the pack make sure the Pure would know they were coming, and know that it was as a response to profaning Black Mountain with their conflict. They needed to know their doom approached, and why.

                                        Aed suggested a few ideas for leading the Pure into traps, but ultimately everyone agreed that they needed to find a cure for Greyson first. They debated a few different beings they could ask for help in learning a purification rite, and settled on the Old Man. They entered the Shadow together and found it a wasteland, the discorporating corpses of spirits everywhere and the streets silent and bereft. Aed mentioned the battle he and Orla had seen taking place yesterday and figured that this must be the aftermath.

                                        The group continued on to Black Mountain, where they found the entrance to the Old Man’s throne room closed. Hope figured that the entry must only open when the Old Man held court, and that they needed to find another way. Aed suggested tracking down the Other, the only powerful spirit he could think of, but given the peace and healing they needed Aaron broached the subject of the nearby Glade he’d been carefully tending in his free time.

                                        The Glade took the form of a small garden, reflecting a stretch of wild growth in the real world just beyond the edge of Andersonstown’s streets. Aaron had spent the past few months nurturing its growth, and within its rows the group found the first spirits they’d seen since entering the Hisil. Aed sought to identify the strongest spirit within, a Hursah who took the form of a gauzy shroud wrapped around the scars of an old tree spirit in the midst of the garden. The Hursah, introducing herself as Silkenspun, listened to Aed’s description of Greyson’s affliction and sadly pronounced that she had not the knowledge to help. At the offer of gathra she told the pack that the only spirit of the right temperament and age that she could think of was the being known as Cairnbreath. But… Silkenspun warned them that Cairnbreath had been sealed beneath the earth long ago by the Wolf In Golden Chains, and surely would hunger fiercely. There would certainly be safer options than that.

                                        Aed declared that they didn’t have time to seek out safer alternatives. A good gift for a hungry death spirit would surely be another, weaker death spirit, and Belfast was full of those.

                                        While all this had been going on, Orla and Aaron were on the verge of coming to blows. Orla had been picking flowers and braiding grass from the gardens, and Aaron saw her ruining his good work. They shouted and yelled at one another over it, and Aaron was still fuming as he led the group from the Glade. In his distraction, he failed to notice the greater threat. The violence spirits who followed Aed around, the gremlin-like beings he called his Entourage, had followed their champion into the Glade. While Aed was able to shoo them out, the sight of violence literally manifesting in the place he’d sworn to preserve from that shook Aaron to his core. He could feel the Flesh begin to pull on him all the stronger.

                                        Unbalanced, frustrated, and angry, Aaron slammed into Orla from behind once they were clear of the Glade. She whirled on him, not really understanding why they were fighting but determined to defend herself. She struck him once, twice, but her blows were light compared to the rage driving Aaron. His second blow almost sent her to her knees, but with a supreme effort she was able to rise. The sudden strike to his temple staggered Aaron, a second blow to his stomach doubled him over, and a boot to his knee sent him crashing to the ground. He tried to lift himself but his reserves were spent. Six days without sleep, his body fuelled only by his connection to Hope, his constant exertion in warding his territory since then, all came crashing down and he found his arms unwilling to lift him off the ground.

                                        Aed ordered the two of them to cool off, and Aaron stomped back to his glade. He tried to repair some of the damage done by the visit, but he couldn’t feel the connection he normally did amidst the peace and quiet of the garden. His Harmony had pulled him ever further from this precious place, and his usual connection was clouded.

                                        The rest of the group sought out the site of the Weeping Tree, remembering that it had been born from the sorrow at a massacre. The street was like a sodden marsh, even the asphalt yielding to their feet like a swamp’s surface and filling with brackish, salty water. He'd been quizzing Regan a lot about Eaven's abilities, and had stocked up on protective gear. He had several lengths of chains, a fire blanket, and a fire extinguisher ready for when they went toe to toe with her. He also wants to track down Scarred Beast Burning again, so that Orla can forge a Fetish weapon to help them in the fight.

                                                        The pack declares a hunt against Scarred Beast Burning, and Orla uses Impossible Spoor to trace the spirit. Aaron insists on coming, shifting to Urhan so that his other senses can better compensate for his lost sight. They trace the rogue spirit to a warren it had carved to itself in a hill on the far side of Black Mountain. There, Aed confronts the beast. It declared that it wasn't scared of the pack any longer, that the Court of Bloody Communion had driven back the Union from Black Mountain and it now had the backing of its fellows. Aed didn't bat an eyelid. He'd just treated with a god of death, this little mongrel wasn't going to be a challenge. He declared that Scarred Beast Burning's only hope of staying this side of the Gauntlet was to help the Harbingers, beginning by serving them as a Fetish. It looked for a moment like the spirit might resist him, but then its will broke and it agreed. Orla bound the spirit to a single bullet, creating a shapeshifting bullet wish gnashing teeth for Regan to use in her gun.

                                                        With the preparations almost complete, Regan used her Message Rite to summon Dorian to meet with them at Aed's home. Aed asked Lizzie to give them some space, and the Harbingers performed the Siskur-Dah against the Cleareyes while they waited. Aed then retreated to his bedroom, sensing that several members of the Cleareyes were currently asleep. He intended to hunt them through their dreams.

                                                        Dorian arrived, and the others took him down to see the healing Greyson. Barring the immense wound in his chest, the dire wolf appeared to be largely recovered from his harrowing experience. The only problem was that he was still unresponsive. Dorian mentioned that Greyson had the capacity to send his mind elsewhere, to possess wild creatures and ride them instead, and that he could be doing so in order to avoid the pain afflicting his real body. They just needed to find a way to tell Greyson it was safe to return. Regan suggested her Message Rite, and hunched over her little hand mirror as she tried to bridge a connection to Greyson's mind.

                                                        The Rite worked. Greyson stirred to life, breathing out a shuddering thank you in the First Tongue. At around this point, a shaken-looking Aed entered the room. He was pleased to see Greyson up and talking, but had some good news and bad news to share. He'd been hunting in the dreams of the Cleareyes, and discovered a few things. First he encountered the emaciated man he and Aaron had seen when they visited the Cleareyes. That man was sleeping in one of the buildings in the compound, the smallest one right next to the rust-coloured stone monolith that sat at the centre of the Pure compound. His dreamscape had been barren and, strangely, entirely defenceless. The only thing in the room with him was a trapdoor, which Aed was confident would be where the Pure were keeping Jamie.

                                                        The bad news, however, was that he'd then entered the dreams of an unknown Pure. She'd taken the form of a withered, ancient crone. He'd decided to force her awake, stalking her and driving her to exhaustion in her dreams and making sleep itself foreign to her. It had been a struggle, as if the material of her dreams resisted him in a way he wasn't prepared for, but it had worked. He'd then slipped into the dreamscape of the the third Cleareyes, a second Pure he didn't recognise. And it was at this point he put two and two together. It was known that there were six Cleareyes, but two hadn't been seen in some time. He'd seen four of them when he and Aaron visited the compound, and now he'd seen another two. With a sinking feeling he'd used the final sleeping Cleareyes' dreams to scry out her surroundings. He'd seen something resembling an infirmary, another one of the buildings in the Pure compound. Two of the beds were occupied, one by the sleeping girl whose dreams he was in, the other by the ancient crone he'd just forced awake. The other Cleareyes were gathered around the bedside of the old woman, and though words were difficult to hear precisely through the fog of dreams he got the impression of repeated variations on the theme of, "It's a miracle!"

                                                        So, basically, they now had five Cleareyes to deal with instead of four. Aaron, somewhat bitterly, sniped that Aed could hardly accuse him of recklessness now. The two packs tried to work out a plan for getting Jamie back from there. They could just try to steal her away from the Cleareyes, but that would no doubt just invite retaliation. Aed asked Greyson what the Whispers knew about the Cleareyes' totem. The Whispers hadn't ever encountered the totem during their raids of Cleareyes territory, but they did know that the Cleareyes compound surrounded a Locus, the rust-coloured monolith, that shared a resonance of earth and violence.

                                                        The problem now was that they were outnumbered. Greyson was still healing from his experience with Mazdarix, and Aaron was in no shape to fight. Everyone promptly ignored Aaron's insistence that he still intended to join them. They were now up against five Pure.

                                                        Finally, Aed turned to the Whispers and asked them why they were fighting the Cleareyes in the first place. The story, as it transpired, was that the Blackstaff wolves had no Locus in their territory. After they lost their Totem, their own essence reserves ended up largely depleted. They couldn't take a new totem, no spirit would accept them until they dealt with the spirit that had become We-Who-Eat-In-Darkness and in doing so sever their old ties. But without essence there was little they could do as their territory spiralled more and more out of control. The closest locus that they knew of at the time (and here Dorian cast an eye towards the mirror in Aed's living room, the locus where Hope made her home) was on Cleareyes turf, so they began raiding Cleareyes territory for essence. When pressed, they denied being responsible for two of the Cleareyes being put into enchanted sleep.

                                                        After hearing the story, Aed announced that he had a solution. He'd tried to resolve things with Eaven amicably, but he'd lacked anything she wanted as much as a new convert. Now, possibly, he had something. The Whispers agreed to a non-violent solution so long as they got Jamie back and the Harbingers helped deal with We-Who-Eat-In-Darkness.

                                                        Aed had Regan send another Message to Eaven, asking her to meet the Whispers and Harbingers at their old meeting spot. "And give my regards to sleeping beauty."

                                                        The message certainly piqued her interest. Eaven showed up shortly after the other two packs arrived. She came alone, and stood on her side of the grove with her feet rooted in her territory. After a momentary diversion caused by Eaven's shock at the state of Aaron's face, Aed convinced her that he was responsible for waking up her sleeping packmate, and that he was willing to wake the other one so long as she agreed to turn over Jamie. Eaven agreed, hashing out some additional terms. The Whispers were to return to Blackstaff. If they ever set foot in Andersonstown, or on Cleareyes turf, then the Harbingers and Cleareyes would work together to destroy them. And Aed would wake up the sixth Cleareyes before they handed over Jamie.

                                                        Bearing in mind Eaven's Honour, her reputation of having never told a lie, the Harbingers agreed to the terms. He dropped himself into a supernaturally induced sleep, and entered the dreams of the sleeping girl once again. Just as with the old woman, he had to struggle to impose his will on the dreamscape of the sleeping girl, but he had an advantage over the force that opposed him. His affinity for dreams, Auspice-given and Luna-blessed, allowed him to navigate the dreamscape as if it were his own. He drove and harried the avatar of the girl until she, too, was forced back to consciousness.

                                                        Eaven had Aaron and Aed accompany her back to the compound to retrieve Jamie. To Aed's surprise, Jamie turned out to not be in the building with the trapdoor, but in the fourth building he had yet to see inside of. There, they found a battered Jamie sitting in a room bereft of floorboards. The bare earth had been carved with savage, angular runes, and watching over her was a horrifying man-shaped being of raw, pink flesh. Eaven called the creature Num-Num, and he appeared to be immediately taken with Aed. He couldn't speak properly, just making happy gurgling noises at the sight of the newcomers, before swapping to a pained whine when Eaven released Jamie. Aaron pieces together enough of what was going on to realise they were in the presence of a duguthim, and accused Eaven of being a monster. She got defensive. The creature had been irrevocably fused with its host when she found it, and it was better that it be locked away here than to have a Torture Claimed wandering around loose.

                                                        They left Num-Num waving sadly at the departing Aed, and escorted Jamie back to the border. It was later that night that each of them felt Luna's approval for their actions. For treating with their enemies and finding a diplomatic solution between three packs on the brink of war, each of them received new brands of Honour.
