    Death Rage interpretations for the 5 Forsaken Tribes

    12-16-2025, 12:07 AM
    I'm working on a handful of merits for the Forsaken Tribe to try and give them some variety like the Pure tribes have and had the idea to give each of them a 5 dot Primal Urge 5 merit that revolves around the idea of them having a special kind of rage. An example of this is:

    Fenriduth (•••••)


    Prerequisites: Blood Talon, Primal Urge 5


    Effect: Fenriduth, the “Destroyer’s Rage” is a unique form of the Death Rage that exists within some of the most primal of Fenris-Ur’s children, allowing for revelry rather than slavery to the rage. When your character falls to Basu-Im,she may choose to expend a point of willpower to fall into Fenriduth. When in Fenriduth, she gains a +1 bonus to her strength and size for every turn spent in Basu-Im, up to an amount of strength and size each gained equal to her Primal Urge, and can spend a point of essence at the start of her turn to heal a single point of aggravated damage.



    Drawback:In any story where she has fallen to Fenriduth, the uratha suffers a -2 penalty to any dice pools made to resist falling to Wasu-Im. She will prioritize other enemies over her packmates while in Basu-Im as normal, but will crave the never ending rage of Fenriduth and must spend a point of willpower to fall to the Destroyer’s Rage any chance she has if she has already done so this story. If the Uratha has no willpower to spend, she will instead suffer a point of aggravated damage to activate this merit as she pushes herself beyond her limits.


    While writing up the other merits, of which I've completed 3 others, I ran into a bit of an issue regarding Hikaon-Ur and the Hunters in Darkness/Meninna. I don't interpret the Hunters in Darkness as never using Gauru, but it feels more in line with Hikaon-Ur (the merits are meant to connect to the patron and apply most strongly to their tribe's prey) to boost her children's abilities to plan. I played around with the idea of giving them a sort of control in Basu-Im, but that feels like it clashes slightly with what Basu-Im is and also doesn't very thematically Meninna/Hikaon-Ur.

    Would it be better to make them the odd wolf out and give them a merit related to instead boosting their Urshul/Urhan form with qualities of Gauru while giving them a drawback involving Kuruth somehow, or should I just give them an uncannily sane version of Kuruth with a condition based drawback that inflicts them a passive primal edge?

              Glad you like it, I have the others (excluding hunters, obviously) written up as drafts right now. I'll put them below so people can get an idea of what's going on with them. The thing about them that makes me a little nervous is how they're all fairly similar in write up, but I did like the idea of most of the tribes having a Basu-Im based effect when they let their firstborn run rampant. Hunters are probably going to get some level of invisibility or perhaps shadow walking.

              Endenuth (•••••)

              Prerequisites: Storm Lord, Primal Urge 5


              Effect: Endenuth the “Winter Rage”, is a unique form of the Death Rage that exists within some of Skolis-Ur’s most primal children, granting them the winter’s biting cold. When your character falls to Basu-Im, she may choose to expend a point of willpower to fall into Endenuth. While in Endenuth, she radiates the deepest chills of winter within a number of yards equal to her Primal Urge, gains +1 Stamina per turn spent in Basu-Im, up to an amount of Stamina gained equal to her Primal Urge, and gains armor 2/2. Prey within the chills of winter suffer -3 to their defense, speed, and initiative.


              Drawback: For the rest of the story in which the Uratha has fallen to Endenuth, the Uratha suffers a -1 penalty to actions involving Resolve, Stamina, or Composure as she can feel the disappointment of Skolis-Ur, causing her to second guess herself. This penalty can be mitigated for a scene by spending a point of willpower.

              Mudhhaluth (•••••)

              Prerequisites: Iron Master, Primal Urge 5


              Effect: Mudhhaluth the “Creative Rage”, is a unique form of the Death Rage that exists within some of Sagrim-Ur’s most primal children, taking advantage of Red Wolf’s deals with foundational spirits relating to humanity's innovation. When your character falls to Basu-Im, she may choose to expend a point of willpower to fall into Mudhhaluth. When in Mudhhaluth, any items more advanced than stone-age level “technology” within a number of yards equal to her Primal Urge cease to function in any way that would impede the hunt. This may be represented by devices suffering complications and breaking in ways that prevent it from functioning or simple failure, such as a gun’s trigger locking up or a car’s spark plug becoming defective. While in the Creative Rage, her flesh also coats itself in manmade metal or electricity, causing any target who attacks her to suffer a point of lethal damage, even spirits.


              Drawback: For the rest of a story in which the Uratha has fallen to Mudhhaluth, technology spirits grow angry at the blatant abuse of their sacred dealings. All rolls by the Uratha, or her packmates, if the action obviously benefits her, to use technology suffer a -2 penalty. A Manifested spirit or one also in the hisil who can see the Uratha may expend a point of essence to turn a failure on any such roll into a dramatic failure.

              I’ruth (•••••)


              Prerequisites: Bone Shadow, Primal Urge 5


              Effect: I’ruth the “Manifest Rage”, is a unique form of the Death Rage that exists within some of Kamduis-Ur’s most primal children, granting them Kamduis-Ur’s superiority over all spirits. When your character falls to Basu-Im, she may choose to expend a point of willpower to fall into I’ruth. When in I’ruth, her honorary spirit rank increases by 2, allowing it to rise above 5. She does not have to expend essence to target spirits below her honorary rank with gifts and gains a bonus to rolls to resist the numina of spirits equal to the difference in rank if the Uratha’s is higher.


              Drawback: For the rest of a story in which the Uratha has fallen to I’ruth, spirits within her honorary rank in miles gain a +2 bonus to tracking or finding her as the spiritual power of Kamduis-Ur continues leaking from her. Spirits will also register the Uratha as being more attuned to the spirit, meaning that her being within their territory could be seen as a threat that needs to be taken care of.

                          This last merit leaves me easily the most unsure of its quality, which is saying something, considering my opinions of the others. I tried to take notes from Lore of the Forsaken and apply them to the benefits of the Primal Hunting Grounds in such a way that don't make the Hunter in Darkness the best fighter, but certainly can make them the best hunter among the tribes. I don't know if this ranks too strong compared to the other merits and I have some doubts about the drawback, but this is the initial draft.

                          Ninnuth (•••••)

                          Prerequisites: Hunter in Darkness, Primal Urge 5


                          Effect: Ninnuth the “Hunter’s Rage” is unique compared to the various rages granted by the firstborn, as it does not actually rely on the burning rage of Kuruth or Basu-Im. Instead, as an instant action, the Uratha may expend 3 essence to summon up the primal hunting grounds of Hikaon-Ur, causing both the flesh and shadow within a radius equal to the Uratha’s primal urge to bend to her will as it once did to Hikaon-Ur’s. These hunting grounds remain until the next sunrise or she leaves the hunting grounds themselves. Spirits, Uratha, and other denizens of the Hisil can immediately tell something is wrong while within these hunting grounds, but do not innately know who or what the cause is. While within her primal hunting grounds, the Uratha’s rage grows cold and calculated, offering her the following benefits.
                          • She gains a +3 bonus to Investigation, Larceny, Stealth, Survival, and Subterfuge rolls and willpower spent on these rolls provide a +4 bonus instead of their usual.
                          • Her speed increases by 3 and her footsteps do not make noise.
                          • Her dice pools made to chase prey cannot be reduced as a result of mundane obstacles, including restraints, and supernatural obstacles cause a Clash of Wills that the Uratha makes with the Rote quality.
                          • She may reflexively spend a point of essence when she moves to teleport to any shadow large enough to hold her that she knows exists within her primal hunting grounds, even if she cannot see it at the time. Doing so is an immediate breaking point towards spirit.


                          Drawback: Immediately upon leaving her primal hunting grounds or her primal hunting grounds expiring, the innate focus tamping down the primal emotions of the Uratha vanish, causing her to fall into Basu-Im for twice the period dictated by her Primal Urge. In addition, the summoning of these primal grounds on any territory besides her own is considered a grave sin, almost ensuring mobs or packs of angry denizens demanding gathra or payment for these transgressions.


                          I have 2 other merits for each tribe that are more miscellaneous in what they accomplish, but I don't know if it's appropriate to post in this same thread focusing specifically on these existing 5. As such, I'll leave this here for people to offer opinions and post more on the merits as I think on them for an upcoming chronicle.
