                    When I started this thread, I basically had two questions: "How many triggers does a werewolf have" and "how do you pick them".
                    Most things cited (namely Chris' post of new triggers) and the general consensus both seem to point to the answer to the first question being "one at each tier".
                    My reading of Chris' post is that he is all for mixing and matching from different lists, but despite his being one of the writers, this was an unofficial stance.

                    I came into this thinking that mixing and matching was madness, but the most compelling thing I've heard on the matter was someone pointing out that its a supernatural and personal thing anyway. It doesn't need to follow our earth logic.
                    On top of that, the game is built to have exceptions to almost every rule, and the space for these exceptions exists to make the stories you tell interesting. While it makes sense to pick one list and run with it so that your character has one highly thematic death rage trigger, I can't honestly think of a reason why actually new triggers couldn't apply as your Uratha gets more and more out of whack. If you want to, as Aiden put it, risk raging more often by not having your specific and common triggers subsumed as your harmony erodes, that's your perogative.

                    So I guess the answer to my question of "how many triggers" is "one of each is more than enough".

                    Anyone else, specifically devs or people involved in play tests, want to chime in about the intention of experience of running with the current trigger system?
