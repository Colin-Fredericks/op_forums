    04-12-2014, 08:47 AM
    Hail, hail, the gang's all here!

    So, this week we get a sneak preview on the subject of Packs, and it's everything I wanted and more! You want violent street gangs? You got it! You want quiet conspiracies? You got it! You want a whole freaking church bowing down your totem? YOU GOT IT

    YOU GET A PACK AND YOU GET A PACK!!



    Ahem.

    But yes, humans (knowingly or unknowingly), wolf bloods and werewolves alike can get in on the action, which has some really cool implications for different moods between chronicles.

                                      Of course, the big crossover question is "can other supernaturals join packs" :P
                                      It goes into the actual nature of wolf packs, in my opinion. Actual wolf packs are families, with siblings, aunts and uncles and mum and dad. And so, now with Uratha having the ability to breed with other uratha, the packs can function like that. Since some kids are ot born uratha, nor wolf-blood, they now can be as much part of the pack as the rest.

                                      That, and with that freedom to have non-uratha, it means packs can manifest in interesting ways. Maybe a pack of Iron Masters is like an Anarchist or Greenpeace group that attacks companies. While a Hunters in the Darkness pack could easily include a coven of Wiccans or other pagan groups. We could easily also have a group of business men.

                                      But the main role is still family, because otherwise the human/wolf-blooded would be allies or contacts, not packmates.

                                              So, what do I think of new packs? Well, to be fair, the basic premise of this was presented last week, so it's not a new concept. But that said...

                                              FUCK YEAH, WEREWOLVES ARE NOW OCCULTIC FIGURES! Werewolves, more so than any other supernatural template(save Promethean), have missed out on the sense of being the center of mystical attention, as keystones of a cult, normally because of that entire "Herd Must Not Know" thing. This has steadily started becoming a real downer since with the release of Mummy, human involvement in monstrous affairs has been a growing matter, even if 'Cult' isn't a Blood and Smoke merit directly, But now it's changed-the Uratha can play as anything from the wolf-headed demigods they were in Bau to fellows in a mass of sharp toothed individuals. I said it last week, and I say it again-I love cult/gangs, and I particularly love the earlier mentioned affair of werewolves holding 'mass' in a church, paying chiminage to the totem.(It doesn't hurt that I caught up on Gunnerkrigg Court and found Coyote's true form very Forsaken)

                                              I'm having a thought towards Kyoko Sakura from Madoka Magica, but that's neither here nor there.

                                              We didn't talk about the mechanics of packs, but I'm assuming that totem's will be cleaned up from a proto-krewe mechanic to a post-krewe refinement(yes, talking about GtSE here), which will be much appreciated-I never actually liked the base totem rules. My only question is if humans in a pack receive benefits-I imagine they will, but confirmation is a nice thing.

                                              WEREWOLVES HAVE TOUCHSTONES! WEREWOLVES HAVE TOUCHSTONES! FUCK YES!

                                              I really like the Touchstone mechanic, both as a mechanic and as a necessary character narrative element. I really despise the idea that a character is alone and associates with no one and in particular has no ties to their own lives. Touchstones were a smart way of counteracting that train of thought and make WoD games more a game of relationships through mechanical benefits. I was hoping they'd be a thing from Vampire on, and it looks like they are. The fact that werewolves can have them both ways is also really neat.

                                              Ugh, I had more to say but I got lost in a Legend of Zelda convo. Oh well. Anyways, very much approve of this post.

                                                  Originally posted by falco1029 View Post

                                                  If humans, can, I imagine supernaturals theoretically could, but it'd be exceedingly rare. If they DO know it's a supernatural, they'd likely be very suspicious about bringing one out of their own group and into theirs, and if they DON"T, when the totem inevitably mentions 'hey that guy can do magic/isn't alive/etc' they'd probably not be let in because of not being truthful, and maybe treated as a spy.

                                                  That being said, I see it as something that should likely be mechanically allowable, just not common by any stretch. But that's just my guess/what i'd go with if it was up to me.
                                                  I think some supernaturals have more of a precedent for joining a Pack then others and these supernaturals could theoretically work within a Pack.

                                                  Sure beings like Vampires, Changelings, Mummys and Demons who have little direct interaction with Spirits and Spirit phenomena are not likely to join a Pack. As they know far less then Werewolves about Spirits and the Shadow and likely don't have the tools needed to directly hunt such beings.

                                                  I can see a place for a Spirit Mage (Thyrsus likely) in such a group, as while his own knowledge of Spirits and the Shadow will be different from that of the Werewolves, he can bring an alternate point of view to affairs that might be useful to the Pack. Likewise, he has abilities that allow him to contribute to the hunt in some fashion.

                                                  The same applies to the Ulgan lineage of Prometheans could work in a similar way. Particularly one following the refinement of Silver (ironically) that's allowing him to mimic Spirit and Werewolf abilities. The ST would likely have to rule that the Totem bond did something to blunt Disquiet to really make it work, but its not impossible.

                                                  Hunter groups that deal with the possessed and Spirits but don't count Werewolves in their target list could also work. At current I don't think any of the Compacts or Conspiracies quite fit. Ultimately though a tier 1 cell of Hunters could easily be part of the more "extended" pack of humans, as they see the Wolves as a more efficient way to guard against Spirit related stuff.

                                                  Finally, we have Sin-Eaters, who obviously have little to do with Spirits and the Shadow. However, they pretty much mirror the Wolves for dealing with the dead. So I can see a mutual agreement situation coming along of the Sin-Eaters being part of an extended pack (fitting the same role as the humans for the most part) and the Wolves being sorta extended Krewe. They cross-reference stuff with one another to figure out if its Underworld or Shadow related and then they have a wider support network to deal with this stuff in the area.

                                                  That's just my thoughts on the subject. I find trying to add all the Shadow/Spirit stuff to Changeling and Mummy to be problematic in that it makes the setting a little over-complex for me. Vampire's themes are, in my opinion, the most "mundane" of any of the games (including Hunter) and I think adding "other realms" to that equation only degrades that feeling and the wider themes of Vampire. Demon has one power that interacts with the Shadow and frankly the God-Machine and the whole Cover idea present in Demon does not really play well with Werewolf I think. Bad enough to be dealing with Spirit all the time, adding Hunter Angels to the mix is just a bridge to far for me.

                                                  So I guess I agree with Falco in the end. Some other Supernaturals work better then others. The occurrence should not be common at all and like all crossover it should either be used to highlight and interesting point of convergence between the games or should be used to purely for world building.

                                                  (Weirdly I've always felt Promethean crosses over very well with Werewolf, but that's just a mental quirk of mine.)

                                                  "There is no God in the Badlands." - Sunny
