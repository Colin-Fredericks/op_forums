        Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
        See, this I think gets at the heart of it very well. I think that's a fantastic example of what Forsaken is all about, and as you point out, no werewolves. Every time I've asked or seen someone ask this question, the answers include a variety of great examples of books, movies, tv shows, etc. that really encapsulate the essence of Forsaken. Very few, if any, of those examples actually contain werewolves. The pop culture image of what werewolves are like, and the themes that are focused on in Forsaken are just different things. That's ok, there's nothing wrong with a unique take on a folkloric creature (and truthfully, the take in Forsaken is in a lot of ways closer to original werewolf lore than the pop culture image of one is). But it does make it difficult for some people to recognize them as that creature if they're only familiar with the pop culture version. Fortunately, it sounds like the new Wolf Blooded are giving us that iconic movie werewolf that many people expect, and the new Harmony is giving us a way for a recently first changed Uratha to go through a process of learning to control her nature by embracing what she is instead of fighting it. Exactly the tools that I feel Forsaken needs to bridge the gap that a lot of newcomers have.
        Whenever someone brings out the "not like the pop culture version", I just like to point out what the public perception of mages and changelings and wraiths and demons and mummies and Sin-Eaters (WTF are those?) would be.

        The games can never possibly accommodate everybody's perception of what a monster should be, so they have to nail it down and start somewhere.

        And, if you ask me, I'd rather they didn't go too far away from some of the much older legends about the creatures in question. After all, if they kept to just the modern ideas, the vampires would sparkle and the werewolves would have to be shirtless 90% of time time.

                            The only real similarities between Harry Dresden and the other Mage examples to Mage are they do magic. With Fables is that they live among humankind.

                            Werewolf does werewolf things. You turn into a bestial human, a monstrous wolfman, a hulking wolf, or a regular wolf, all pretty much staples of iconic werewolf media. You have the chance to do it while emotionally amped up, as well, falling into Kuruth and slaughtering whatever you might find. Also, after the First Change, if you try and resist what you are, you're going to regret it.

                            What more is there to the majority of werewolf fiction?

                            Spread it to others.
                            Sex a lot (could still happen, it's just not emphasized in the books).
                            Can cure yourself by killing the dude who gave it to you.
                            And blackout shapeshifting, where you don't know what happened.

                            The problem with the comparisons to other media is that people pick and choose what they want. And it's not a matter of the game not reflecting fiction in general, as much as not accurately matching one specific piece of fiction that they prefer.

                                            I still think the best example I've seen of a group that felt like a "pack" in a show that really played the "us and them" theme up to 11 is The Walking Dead.

                                            Honestly, to core group of survivors that the show follows (despite its membership changing a fair bit due to the "anyone can die" trope being in effect) really strike me as a group of very different people pulled together out of a need to survive and face the dangers of the world they live in and the duty they have to the ones they love. Some of these people are not at all alike or even have the same values when the series starts, but they bond due to necessity and the extreme situation they find themselves in.

                                            The guy that you might have found to be a someone who needs anger management classes and a wider education on the idea of racial and sexual equality is the same guy that save your ass a few dozen times in the last week. He might have found you to be an over-educated nabby-pamby academic with no real world experiences, but you've pulled his ass out of the fire and backed him up when it counts. Pretty soon everyone comes to except everyone and a new "family" is formed.
