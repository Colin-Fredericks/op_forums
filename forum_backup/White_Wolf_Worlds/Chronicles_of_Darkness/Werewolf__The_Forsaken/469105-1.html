    Originally posted by nofather View Post
    The comparison between Luna and the God Machine wasn't originally made about their morality or lack thereof, it was about their omnipresence and willingness to act.
    On that much we agree, certainly.


    Originally posted by nofather View Post
    Also, while I'll grant that I haven't read much about the God Machine itself, Luna isn't exactly hands off. She has the Oath of the Moon, and legions of Uratha to follow them and do her bidding. The Pure seem more analogous to the Demons in this situation, except instead of Hell you have Paradise.
    Sorry, but again this seems too much of a stretch to me. The analogy falls short when you consider that Mother Moon doesn't wage war against the Pure, not per default at least. She also doesn't send the lunes to attack the Pure except in rare occasions, like the rite of the final moonrise. The Pure, on the other hand, explicitly make war against the forsaken, kidnap and torture lunes when they think they can get away with it, do the same to humans and wolf-blooded as just another tactic of their war, and they become subservient to their spirits totem while accusing the Forsaken of being willing slaves to Luna.

    Trying to bring sympathy to the Pure goes only so far before starting to fall apart at the seams. Individually, the Pure may be looked at with a sympathetic eye, but collectively? They are assholes.

    We can respectfully agree to disagree however, YMMV after all.

            Originally posted by Zooroos View Post
            I've just read the rite Satchel mentioned, but the part relevant to this conversation is the one which describes how performing the rite attracts the Lunes, who will try to attack the rite master.
            If performed by a werewolf with an auspice, notably. Within moments.

            However, I still think that trying to come with a parallelism between Mother Moon and the God-Machine is misleading, in the same sense as using the Apocalypse mindset in Forsaken is misleading too.
            I have played exactly nothing of Apocalypse and read only marginally more of it. For contrast, I've comprehensively read all of Forsaken 1e along with Promethean 1e and Geist 1e within the last three years and am at least passingly familiar with concepts in every other published nWoD 1e gameline. I didn't come up with this comparison on a lark.

            Against it, the Demons are clearly the underdogs and the oppressed minority, chased and forcibly reprogrammed when captured.
            Demons cannot be remade into angels, are only given priority attention when they become a problem or necessary to a plan, and are occasionally stuck taking orders from loyal and/or mad angels in the very occurrences which most directly discourage them from banding together into large organized groups.

            I'm aware that demons aren't werewolves. My point of comparison was "the Pure and the Unchained have both disconnected themselves from a unifying supernatural category whose overseer can exploit their association with it as a matter of policy."

            Against her, the Pure stands as bigoted and hateful, and their reasoning leaks from many holes, trying to paint themselves as justified in their hatred towards the Forsaken.
            There are demons who want to destroy reality without the excuse of being bound to beings that embody literal millennia of monomaniacal hatred. Culture is a distinct step from metaphysical alteration here.

            But clearly, their opposition is many shades darker and brutal, which works exactly as intended for the game.
            As highlighted, I'm not sure what you think this has to do with anything about their capabilities and the behavior of their opposition.
