                    Originally posted by Iry View Post
                    I firmly stand by the opinion that making Sacred Hunt an innate ability of Werewolves adds character choices, encourages a little more roleplay, makes the Sacred Hunt a little more personal, and slightly adds to the theme "The Wolf Must Hunt".
                    It also makes it not a rite. A major part of the point of it being a rite and doing what it does is that it helps explain the substantial differences between the Forsaken and what many people in the modern Western world think of when the word "werewolf" comes up by providing a pervasive ritual element of Uratha society that allows them to better manage their time and Harmony.

                    Making this feature innate does not make creating a character who somehow can't use it equivalent to starting with Social Merits. It makes it equivalent to me having to cobble together a Persistent Condition for one of my players because he took three dots in Socialize and wants to play a fish-out-of-water who regularly commits faux-pas in everyday mortal society — and that specific character at least has the excuse of being expressly conceived as woefully unintelligent, which is not a brush I imagine you want to tar every would-be Ghost Wolf with.
