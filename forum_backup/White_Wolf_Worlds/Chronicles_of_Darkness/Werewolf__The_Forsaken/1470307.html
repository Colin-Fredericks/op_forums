    I am running a campaign where a group of humans is hunting an Irraka serial killer, I won't go over the whole story so far but basically, my main antagonist had a streak of bad luck and ended with 7 points of aggravated damage while in Garou form, at that point he shifted to Urshul and successfully escaped now, what happens if he shift back into hishu? the book says that the temporary health levels granted by the various forms are lost when shifting back to a different form, so I wanted to know if there was some mechanic that I missed that wouldn't force my Irraka Michael Myers to remain in dalu for 4 days unti it heals a point of aggravated.

      Originally posted by Werewolf: The Forsaken, p. 172
      Temporary Health Dots
      Some characters, werewolves included, can acquire extra Health dots that make them more robust for a temporary period of time. Add bonus Health dots to the right side of your character’s Health chart, with a corresponding box to record wounds.

      What happens when the effect wears off? The extra Health dots go away, but wounds in them do not. Any wounds from his “lost” Health dots now upgrade the least severe wounds that he already has, from left to right.

      A werewolf in Dalu form has had the shit kicked out of him, and has nine of his 10 Health boxes filled with lethal damage. When he’s forced to shift into Hishu, his Health returns to 8 dots, all full of lethal wounds. The remaining one point of lethal damage upgrades the left-most point of lethal damage to aggravated damage.
      That's the official rule, but you can always fudge it for NPCs. Maybe they have a special Gift that lets them avoid that damage when they shift into other forms.

          If shifting down means all your boxes are filled with agg you die. This can mean you are stuck in Dalu and Urshul form for several days, but remember that no form is "more natural" than others for Werewolves so it's less inconvenient that you might think. Dalu and Urshul are not easy forms to stay unnoticed in, but a Werewolf with only 1 health level not filled with agg should not be out and about, they should be resting in the den. Remember that until the agg heals those are huge open wounds on the Werewolf's body, so not only are they painful and inconvenient, but any prey will be able to see (or smell) that you are injured and might take advantage of that.

          There isn't any way to speed the healing of agg damage in the core book (which I think is by design). There are really only three options to speed your character returning to the field:
          • A character with Memento Mori from the Gift of Death can reduce your agg by one point by taking it on themselves. They can do this repeatedly though they will have to pay the facet's activation cost (1 essence) each time
          • If your character has Clot from the Gift of Blood (which can be found in Night Horrors: Shunned By The Moon) you can double your natural regeneration rates, meaning you will heal two agg every four days
          • Medical care can greatly speed your character's recovery. Any damage beyond bashing requires a hospital or a similar facility with equipment, medicine and staff to provide round-the-clock intensive care and unfortunately witnessing a Werewolf's regeneration up close causes lunacy in normal humans. However if your pack can secure one, having control of a hospital with a special ward crewed by Wolf-Blooded doctors and nurses this can be invaluable, since full time medical care can downgrade one point of aggravated damage to lethal every hour (however it requires 10 successes per point of agg with an interval of one hour per roll, so it's more likely to take 2-3 hours to downgrade each point)

                      This should be in the Simple Questions thread, but since were here.

                      I think this guy will probably seek medical attention or hide in a hole for a long time. This amount of Agg pretty much means someone needing IC urgently. In theory he can even die of those wounds if not treated and cared for.

                      So you have three options:

                      1 - He hides and tries to heal by himself. He'll be severely weakened for weeks, but going for a theme of tough survival it shouldn't mean anything on rules, just take the time to heal. Even without penalties, damage means ongoing recovery, so he should consider himself in recovery until clean of damage. You may also increase the time for recovery due to lack of proper treatment, it isn't any damage, but Aggravated damage, so any further penalty can be applied without remorse.

                      Going that route, do not simply skip time. Consider first the possibility of your PCs tracking him down and see what they want to do about it.

                      2 - He seeks treatment. Secrecy can easily be thrown off the window when one's life nis o the hinge, he's probably suffering from one of the greatest injuries of his life. Wounded and scared. Then you play by the logical consequences of it, he may attract attention from other Uratha, he will probably cause Lunacy on hospital staff time and again, but in general he'll try to get needed attention. If his face isn't publicly known, there's no reason for him to avoid hospitals at this point unless he goes for the first option due to unreasonable fears.

                      As in option 1, your PCs may have the opportunity to find out where he is, but the logistics in dealing with it change completely. While he is obviously weird and all, once he's in a hospital the PCs have an easier time locating him, but a far harder one reaching to him, since he's now under care and protection.

                      3 - Just kill him. Make a time skip to tell the characters that as days become weeks and weeks become months the killings don't start again. Either the creature escaped to a far place, ceased its activities or died of the grievous wounds.

                              Think of it that way: if he got Aggravated, it means the amount of damage completely overwhelmed his regeneration. So that means one of two things (or both): either regen went into short-circuit and isn't working properly, or what he's under is far beyond what a human should have as injury. He may very well have his shredded kidneys dangling from a gaping wound right know.

                              Damage isn't just an injury. It is an injury still too fresh. It still bleeds and whatnot. That's why a paper cut is a Bashing damage and heals in minutes even if the actual cut skin may take days to completely close. This isn't about the time for the injury to completely heal, but for the injury to stop being mechanically relevant. They're not just there, but are actively hindering his survivability (as he have less Health Boxes to risk, at the least).

                              And his injuries will be mechanically relevant for a month.

                              So killing him would be fair game. Not killing him would, too, and in that case I think the Tilt is a good idea. That lead is still there and the wounds are still bleeding when he moves too much. But if you want to go full psycho (and risk a PC's life), go ahead and make the attack without any penalty.

                              It is less about strict rules and more about exploring the best option for your story, given that all options are explainable by circumstances even if not supported by rules directly.
