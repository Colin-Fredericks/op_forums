    Originally posted by Lyrics Of War View Post
    Did Bunyip by chance work on the Kerasi?
    I don't want to derail a Forsaken thread too much, but quickly - yes. I wrote the stretch goals for Fallen Breeds, Mockery Breeds, First Teams, Yeren Gifts and a bunch of new Products.

    Originally posted by Lyrics Of War View Post
    Off topic question, but did the new world of darkness take on things influence the new direction of the spirals or did you guys feel that direct rapist monsters was a touch over done?
    I was only involved in the stretch goals (not the pre-Kickstarter draft or outline), so can't answer that with any authority. What I would say is Stew (and writers) want to make W20 as open to player choice as possible, and giving Wyrm followers more purpose than 'rape monsters' is a good thing.

    If you (or anyone else) have more Apocalypse questions, feel free to make a thread over it that forum. I'll be as open as I can.

    Originally posted by Lyrics Of War View Post
    Edit addition: does your work on apocalypse influence your work on idigam and vice versa or are they just so separate that it doesn't really matter?
    Interesting question. I keep them both very separate - they're different games with very different themes, protagonists and antagonists. I tend to get 'tunnel vision' when writing one or the other, and have to flip a mental switch to move back across to the other one. (Much to the amusement/irritation of my players who have had to deal with me forgetting nWoD rules while trying to write BotW stretch goals and playtest Idigam.)

    It would be impossible to say there's no influence at all, though. I am only one writer, and would suggest my writing will inevitably have some subconscious crossover. Particularly in mechanical bits and pieces.

    Tell you what, when IC comes out, if you want I'll point out my bits and pieces and you can compare with similar in CB, BoTW and Umbra.

    Writer. Developer. World of Darkness | Chronicles of Darkness | The Trinity Continuum
