        Some thoughts about how to divide the potential tribes of the "lesser Firstborn" among the factions of the People-

        Soulless Wolf- as the patron of the Bale Hounds, he stands on his own. If he works behind the scenes to train an "exorcist tribe", however, I think he could fall to the Forsaken's alley, even if on its furthermost edge, with them having infernals as their "sacred prey", and serves as the totem of a "tribe of exorcists"

        Watcher Wolf- seems to be more of a Pure material than a Forsaken one, with her having "those who dishonor the law" as her sacred prey, and with her serving as the totem of a "tribe of secret police"

        Growling Wolf- still kinda struggling with him. I tend to align him with the Pure, but I still tries to think what kind of prey would the "tribe of diplomats" would hunt. Maybe "those who dishonor society"? Or "those who dishonor resources"? Sure, it would make them a bit nicer than the rest of the Pure, but it makes sense for them to take a more neutral position in relation to the Forsaken, at least on the outside.

        Ravening Wolf- with her even the dead as her sacred prey, it feels like she has a solid place among the Forsaken.

        Eater of Names- I think she could work nicely as a Forsaken totem, with occultists as her prey, but I feel like there has to be something a bit more.. insidious about her. Her tribe would be censures of some kind, I assume, who hide/destroy forbidden lore and heretic rites, while utilizing them to their own benefit. Some kind of "church warlocks", I think. Perhaps they would end up on a neutral ground, together with Soulless and Growling Wolves.

        Sky Hunter- while at first I thought about him as a Forsaken totem, I think he could work better as a member of the Pure, having "those who dishonor the boundaries". I could see his acting as a totem for a tribe of gatekeepers and stormwardens of some kind.

        Wolf Who Whispers- after giving it some thought, I think she would work the best as the "abyssal Firstborn", which would make her either as Forsaken or as a neutral tribe totem. For the tribe itself, I think they would be a "tribe of philosophers", who search to find the truth in the Abyss.

        Opalescent- I'm still pretty sure that's the old name for Silver Wolf, before something bad happened to his twin.

        Incandescent- as Apollo Wolf, we know that his prey is "those who dishonor the temples of the sun", which is kinda Pure-y. As the Temple choose a neutral stand, it makes sense for the tribe version to stay neutral, similarly to the other "Ghost Wolf Tribes", or join the Pure.

        Sea Wolf- as the hunter of the horrors of the sea (or perhaps Sea? She was a terror during Pangaea, after all. It makes sense to have a Firstborn dedicated to hunt her spawn), it feels to me that she would work better as a Forsaken tribe. I assume her tribe would be the "tribe of explorers" detailed in the Rage, and we could tie her to the Drowned from Shadows of UK (with them being a part of her prey).

        Glitter Wolf- I think he could work as a Pure totem, hunting for "those who dishonor the relics of the past". I assume he would have a "tribe of temple guardians" in his service.

        Dreaming Wolf- with her hunting for dreams and nightmares, I think she would work well as a part of the Forsaken, acting as a totem for a "tribe of visionaries". Could be tied to the Dreaming Lodge, as suggested.

        Wearer of Skins- with focus around other shapeshifters (or "Luna's bastards"), I could see him working as a Forsaken totem. On the other hand, perhaps taking a neutral stance would work better for his "tribe of survivors".

        Briar Wolf- as a Firstborn hunting for the fey and Arcadia, I think she would definitely be Forsaken. Her tribe would probably be a "tribe of bards and storytellers", I think.

        Night Wolf- the God Machine dedicated Firstborn (I'm assuming that the Field of Dogs would be either absorbed or destroyed by the Eaters of the Dead), he would hunt for false idols and angels of the Machine. Would work nicely as the patron of the "tribe of priests" presented in the Rage. I tend to put them on the Pure side (as "those who disrespect the faith"), but as they already have their own priests I think they would be better as a Forsaken tribe.

        So, to sum things up (and by including existing tribes), we would potentially have 10 Forsaken Tribes, 6 Pure Tribes and 6 Ghost Tribes (all is subject to be changed, of course). I assume, of course, that the "Ghost Tribes" would have much less numbers than the Forsaken/Pure. Next step- to start thinking about cool names for the tribes (of course, if someone has a cool concept for more Firstborns feel free to pitch in, or comment about the Forsaken/Pure/Ghost division).

                    So some thoughts about the Tribes themselves-

                    Ghost Tribes
                    Soulless Wolf (following the "inside tribe" option)- Howlers of Hell, tribe of exorcists and hunters of demons. Renown: Cunning. Gifts: Warding, Knowledge, Dominance (potentially replacing one of the with a an homebrew Gift of Wounds)

                    Growling Wolf- Speakers of the Vine, tribe of diplomats and hunters of those who dishonor their people. Primary Renown: Purity. Secondary Renown: Cunning and Wisdom. Gifts: Insight, Inspiration, Dominance, Nature. Skills: Politics, Empathy, Persuasion.

                    Eater of Names- Devoured Letters, tribe of censures and hunters of occultists. Renown: Wisdom. Gifts: Knowledge, Insight, Warding (potentially replacing one of the options with an homebrew Gift of Oblivion)

                    Wolf Who Whispers- Void Binders, tribe of philosophers and hunters of the Abyss. Renown: Wisdom. Gifts: Insight, Warding, Shaping

                    Sun Wolf- Temple of Apollo, tribe of wardens and hunters of those who dishonor the temples. Primary Renown: Purity. Secondary Renown: Wisdom, Glory. Gifts: Elemental, Inspiration, Dominance, Warding (potentially replacing one of the options with an homebrew Gift of Radiance). Skills: Occult, Socialize, Medicine

                    Scavenger Wolf- Skinner Beasts, tribe of survivors and hunters of shapeshifters. Renown: Cunning. Gifts: Nature, Shaping, Strength

                    Briar Wolf- Fey Dogs, tribe of bards and hunters of goblins. Renown: Glory. Gifts: Rage, Shaping, Insight (potentially replacing one of the options with an homebrew Gift of Song).

                    Roaming Wolf- Dust Walkers, tribe of nomads and hunters of the created. Renown: Wisdom. Gifts: Elemental, Stealth, Knowledge

                    Blood Wolf- Avenging Brethren, tribe of zealots and hunters of those who disrespect the hunters. Renown: Purity. Gifts: Rage, Warding, Larceny

                    Pure Tribes
                    Watcher Wolf- Tower Hounds, tribe of spies and hunters of those who dishonor the law. Primary Renown: Honor. Secondary Renown: Purity and Cunning. Gifts: Warding, Stealth, Evasion, Insight. Skills: Larceny, Investigation, Subterfuge

                    Sky Hunter- Horizon Watchers, tribe of gatekeepers and hunters of those who dishonor the boundaries. Primary Renown: Glory. Secondary Renown: Honor and Cunning. Gifts: Weather, Warding, Strength. Skills: Survival, Athletics, Intimidation

                    Glitter Wolf- Collectors of the Lost, tribe of archivists and hunters of those who dishonor the past. Primary Renown: Wisdom. Secondary Renown: Cunning, Glory. Gifts: Warding, Shaping, Knowledge, Stealth. Skills: Occult, Investigation, Academics.

                    Forsaken Tribes
                    Ravening Wolf- Eaters of the Dead, tribe of cleaners and hunters of the undead. Renown: Purity. Gifts: Death, Strength, Warding

                    Sea Wolf- Wave Riders, tribe of mariners and hunters of the drowned. Renown: Glory. Gifts: Weather, Elemental, Strength (potentially replacing one of the options with an homebrew Gift of Seas)

                    Dreaming Wolf- Sleeping Seers, tribe of visionaries and hunters of goetia. Renown: Wisdom. Gifts: Inspiration, Shaping, Evasion (potentially replacing one of the options with an homebrew Gift of Dreams)

                    Night Wolf- Savage Priests, tribe of clerics and hunters of angels. Renown: Glory. Gifts: Dominance, Strength, Technology

                    Burrow Wolf- Lantern Bearers, tribe of redeemers and hunters of the underfolk. Renown: Purity. Gifts: Rage, Stealth, Warding



                    Well, those are the pitches I have for now. I'll think about bans and Sacred Hunts later.

                        Some thoughts about origin stories for three of our Firstborn:

                        Soulless Wolf- Pangaea has died with Father Wolf's last howl. Silent Wolf, an outside to his family, recognized it- but his job was not over yet. In fact, it has only just began- for without the Great Predator to tend the Shadow, corruption has took hold, and its roots reached deep. Hisil was wounded, over and over again, and dark spirits which until than were held in bay has feasted upon the chaos and terror and grew in power. Recognizing that there was no way for him to remove the corruption which has started to fester, Silent Wolf came before the Lord of the Wound and pledge allegiance. If you can't beat them, join them- that's what he said. Gleeful for the chance to corrupt such a mighty spirit, the Maeljin accepted the former demon hunter and taught him the ways of vice, granting him the name of Soulless Wolf and a betrayer of his own family. At least, that's how it seemed- for hidden from all, Soulless Wolf hid a single shard of his heart, so it would remain pure and true to its cause- the only way he could infiltrate the heart of the darkness and not be devoured. Since than, Soulless Wolf has served as the Eyes of the Maeljin and patron of the Bale Hounds- but he kept at least one eye open for those who were willing to learn the paths of Hell without being corrupted, and fight the demons with their own tools. Before those, he manifested as his true, untainted self- giving them gifts and secrets unknown by all, and one command. Stay silent, until the time to strike would come. Soon, Hell would taste its own medicine.

                        Growling Wolf- one of the youngest of the Firstborn, Growling Wolf has never had much interest in the hunt, always being one more interested in talking than fighting (his mother's fault, if Red Wolf were to be asked). As such, none of his older siblings took him seriously, leaving him alone to speak with the cults of man and deal with the politics of the Shadow. Only Creation Wolf had a soft spot for him, and even as she was consumed by plague Growling Wolf stuck with her, uncaring for the changes which passed upon the world. Having little to no interest in the Uratha society, he stayed mostly away from the dispute of the Tribes, serving merely as a patron to the small Lodge of Mania (which he found to be hilarious)- but every once in ten years, he and Rabid Wolf met, discussing spirituality and philosophy, and the opinions of man and spirit. For ages the meetings were kept intact- until in the last one, as he came to meet his sister, he discovered she was not alone- his brothers, Dire Wolf and Silver Wolf, has accompanied her to the meeting. Scolding their sibling, they told him that they waited long enough, and it was the time for him to "grow up" and "stop shaming their father's memory". Seeing no other choice, Growling Wolf accepted the ultimatum (for he, like any other sane being, did not wanted to be on Dire Wolf's bad side, for he probably was light-years from "sane"). Revealing himself before those Pure who ripped their Auspice yet chose no tribe, Growling Wolf provided a worthy challenge for those whom he deemed worthy- to hunt with no claw or fang, no tooth or nail. Those who succeeded have became the first of his Tribe, hunters of word and letter. Yet, even in his situation Growling Wolf wasn't able to fully commit himself to the struggle- making him and his followers at the outskirts of the Pure society, and serving as a bridge between the two, conflicting sides.

                        Night Wolf- a child of faith, Night Wolf has always searched for a meaning higher of his own, something which was above the wild and untamed chaos of the Shadow. He roamed the Bordered Marches of Pangaea, searched heaven and earth in an attempt to find something more, something higher and greater. Thinking it was just a phase, his siblings left him for his own hunts- for as long as he didn't disturb anyone, what kind of damage such a hopeless search would have? And yet, during one, moonless night, Night Wolf did the impossible- he found something, a god of metal and steam, a machine which held purpose for any being upon the face of earth. Fascinated by divine machinery, Night Wolf felt humility and bowed before the idol, pledging loyalty knowing that his hunt was over once and forever. The machine has accepted the offer- and than, his nightmare has only started. Trapped by its mechanical clutches, the machine has swallowed Night Wolf whole- and with the fall of Pangaea his siblings has barely even noticed, and those who did believed he died with the Gauntlet's collapse. Yet, Night Wolf lived still- his body and heart being violated by the divine creation, transformed and probed and tormented again and again, until the day his god decided he was ready to serve him. Giving him the name of Lycaon, he served as a totem to a cannibal lodge which served to power the idol through human sacrifice, growing in power with each and every day. Bound by machinery, Lycaon was perfectly faith to the machine and served its mission- until the day another Firstborn thought to be long dead challenged his control, and rose against his faithful with a lodge of his own. Long as raging was the battle between the two godlings, until the faithful of Ravening Wolf has found the heart of Lycaon bound by foreign machinery. Shattering the prison which contained his soul, Lycaon was freed from the bindings and transformation of his god, and returned to be Night Wolf once more. On that day, he made a new pledge to himself, and forced those of his Lodge who survived to take it with him- serve no false god, and hunt the cursed idol who claimed to be divine. Finally, Night Wolf found the meaning he was searching for.
