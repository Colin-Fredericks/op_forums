        Originally posted by Voytek View Post
        I mean every tribe looks "evil" when you describe them in broad enough strokes.
        I feel like this misses the forest for the trees.

        Broadly speaking, the franchise's use of monsters as a lens for humanity tends to mean that the individual character types are kind of default sort of grey as that entire moral black and white morality scale goes, with individual players deciding how to brighten up or darken down on their deicisons, which often sets a tone for the conflict.

        As a result of that grey nature to of everything, larger organizations of the monsters tend to conglomerate around those grey concepts, mixtures of positives that are also paired with negatives that reflect the thematic concerns and common answers to those concerns in a way that are generally morally and ethically conflicting (nevermind the larger tendency that while these organization tends to give monsters a form of community where they can be themselves, they also tend to increase the incentives to dive into further monstrosity)-conflicting enough that they make for evocative characters and set pieces for certain kinds of stories, but also arguably justified and even beneficient enough in their way. They look evil in the broad stokes because they are Monstrous Organizations and Monsters are Some Kind of Strife for Humanity to a Human Perspective(and they often have a right to say such, Hunter's aren't devoid of justifable monstrosity)

        The Antagonist Organizations, by contrast (in all but....one and a half and a half cases) are there as something of a crystal clear message of what is meant to be Too Far down the monstrous scale, where it stops being a matter of your circumstances and a clear case of being an asshole. They are a set black to contrast all that grey up above against. You may be a monster, you may be Some Kind of Strife, and the way you deal with that may be Some Kind of Questionable, but these guys are the Line In The Sand, the point where you go from kinda iffy to Absolute Fucktard. Let's not sugarcoat in, outside of the odd exceptions, these organizations of monsters tend to reflect some of the clearest evils we see in the real world, unabashedly. Individuals may be in them for what are much more conflicted reasons, but the organizations themselves are supposed to be a clear evil.

        This is kind of why I feel like "Decent Anshega" and "Good Seers of the Throne" miss the point. Yes, we want characters to be believable and going for a sympathetic approach where in we approach the motivations of the antagonists rationally is a way of doing that, but it misses both the point that these aren't organizations that appeal to people with a mind towards communal decency and personal moral and ethical standing, and often they don't appeal to rational argumentation.

        The Pure are werewolves motivated by hatred of the world and what they got, they hearken back to a myth that doesn't reflect the realities of either the present or the days those myths took place in because it gives them a feeling of power and place. They are motivated by a sense that civilization has castrated them, that their racial superiority and purity is being defiled by interbreeding, that their religions justify their violence against an unworthy and unenlightened mass. These things will never make logical or rational sense, and certainly defy any sense of actual moral or ethical consideration, but because their feelings on this feeds their sense of identity and capacity for action, they will dive head first into and cling to it. At best, you might be able to have something like civil conversation with them-hell, the Fire-touched love it when you buy into that -but at the end of the day, these are people who would gladly support action to kill you because it makes them feel good and right and in a place they belong that is them and theirs if they weren't werewolves for anything any reasonable and rational person would clearly see as wrong-and since they are werewolves, they don't just have to support it, they can and will take action to those ends.

        That the Forsaken also kill humans and willfully shape territories in ways that is demonstrably monstrous does not make the Pure an equal form of evil to them. They clearly embody ideologies and practices that are far more reprehensible than the murky waters the Forsaken exist and act in.

        The only decent Pure is an Uratha who stops being a Pure. Anything else is just killing you with a smile.
