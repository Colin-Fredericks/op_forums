            This right here. You have justice spirits (for whatever you want 'justice spirits' to be). They're likely weak and need protection from the various 'crime spirits' (who'd love to feast on justice), but they exist somewhere important to the club - where the humans meet and talk about bringing justice to the neighbourhood.

            Originally posted by magisanctum View Post
            Trying to imagine the reflection of this is extremely difficult. I've read a lot of nwod lines and even comparing with Awakening that has plenty of little rules to be aware is not so hard to build campaigns and understand causes and consequences. Still Werewolf is the most difficult game to GM because is abstract.
            One way of doing it is to picture the physical world territory. If possible, sketch out a rough map - it doesn't need to be too detailed, just include some of the major locations within the neighbourhood, such as the pack's den, the place where the humans meet to talk about justice, the crime boss's place, the drug dealing areas etc. (Maybe even the police station.)

            Around these key points, draw a circle reflecting how strong an influence each point has on the Hisil. Don't worry if they overlap.The justice club is likely to be very small, the pack maybe bigger, drug hot spots may not be circles, they may be oblongs along certain roads, the kingpin's circle may overlap several others.

            Now, within these circles are the spirit ecologies. The justice spirits stay close to the human clubhouse. Spirits of addiction, pain, greed and death stick close to the drug dens. Spirits of wealth, greed and power are close to the kingpin. Where the circles overlap, the different spirits mingle and prey on each other. The justice spirits will probably be consumed as fast as they can spawn unless the Uratha help. The zone around the den is probably a somewhat safe haven as those spirits allowed by the pack and the totem linger for long.

            Take your zones, imagine what you think they look like in the physical world (and I'm sure you can find a bunch of images from movies and TV to help here), then stick a twisted 'Silent Hill' filter over them. As one example, for drug-heavy streets, think The Wire. Roughly humanoid substance spirits hang out on corners, using their Numen and Influences to draw prospective 'customers' in, offering them the fix they need. Sometimes they'll just take a little piece of Essence for the trade, but they'll snatch up the smaller spirits and consume them whole. Spirits of withdrawal pain and addiction sidle up to the pusher spirits, their natures simultaneously needing to approach but wanting to stay away and survive. Spirits of greed and power cruise the addiction places, demanding tithes of Essence to take to bigger power spirits closer to the (human) kingpin. If they don't get what they want, they take the pusher spirits instead to be consumed - this opens up a vacancy in the drug court.

                    The Hisil is an ecosystem, I've had Ithaeur characters use the Science and Animal Ken skills to better discern how to influence it. The thing you have to do is first deal with the general aura of disregard around the neighborhood, squash the bystander effect, unite the decent folk under righteousness, make visible manifestations, and eventually you'll get a martyr to ignite the fires of change.

                    It'll get messy afterwards. People is going to start taking justice by their own hand, they'll be persecuted, and then you'll have to get more subtle, influencing the outcome of the trials that come. Eventually it'll be too much trouble to pursue profit in that neighborhood.

                    A spirit of outrage sounds amazing for such an endeavor.
