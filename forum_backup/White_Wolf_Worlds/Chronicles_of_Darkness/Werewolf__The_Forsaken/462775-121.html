        Originally posted by Blakemikizuki View Post
        Ok so I'm having a dispute over the teeth and claw abilities of the wolf forms.
        Some friend's of mine are saying that it can do the bite attack and as the same instant action begin a grapple.
        I'm saying the rules say you can either do damage or grapple. Not both at the same time.
        Which is right?
        Rules below
        Teeth and Claws: Claw attacks deal +1 lethal damage. Bites deal +2 lethal damage and do not require a grapple. Bite attacks can establish grapples as well as cause damage.
        ​I'll be honest - it can be read both ways, and I'm not entirely sure myself of the intended reading. I lean towards reading it as the bite attack can be used for grapples and for inflicting damage, but not both simultaneously, as the Chronicles of Darkness system tends to veer away hard from anything that tampers with the action economy by stacking actions or offering multiple actions in one go. I wouldn't lay claim to my reading being a one true interpretation, however.

        - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer

                                        Okay, I've got a handful of werewolf questions:

                                        1) Can Uratha carry spirits across the gauntlet while reaching? I assume this would probably at least require Harmony below 9.

                                        2) In Apocalypse (old world of darkness) there was a ritual to change one's auspice. Has there been an equivalent in Forsaken?

                                        3) A weird one: how much clothing damage should a high-harmony werewolf expect when shifting into Dalu? Are we talking straining of seams, or hulking out?

                                        4) A bit more subjective: would you allow a wolf-blooded with the Anger Issues Tell to voluntarily "trigger" themselves with an instant action and a willpower point (a bit like a high-harmony Uratha shifting)?

                                        5) How would you deal with another supernatural power trying to interfere with Kuruth? For example, a mage using Mind to calm down someone currently in Wasu-Im. A Clash of Wills? Versus the Uratha's Renown+Primal Urge, maybe? What if the raging individual is wolf-blooded (no renown, effective primal urge of 1)?

                                        6) The Shape Shifted Tell has the wolf-blooded going Urhsul during the full moon. Classic werewolf lore would suggest they would enter Kuruth, but the Tell doesn't specify this. Bug or feature?

                                        7) How many Tells do you reckon it would take before a wolf-blooded would register as Uratha to supernatural senses? And is this before or after the Storyteller has a fit and tells you to just play an actual werewolf already?

                                        The longer I study science the more I am convinced that it is functionally indistinguishable from what our ancestors would refer to as sorcery. And I would know, being both scientist and sorcerer.

                                            2. Not to change Auspice, as far as I know. There is, however, a ritual for tearing out a werewolf's Auspice that is used by the Pure.

A Wolf-Blooded would use the same dice pool, which would start at chance die before any bonuses such as from Willpower. (There is one Tell that does increase a Wolf-Blooded's Primal Urge that would help in this situation.)

                                            7. I don't think Wolf-Blooded are supposed to have more than two Tells considering that the Tell Merit is for developing a "second tell".

                                                    Originally posted by Scriptorian View Post
                                                    1) Can Uratha carry spirits across the gauntlet while reaching? I assume this would probably at least require Harmony below 9.
                                                    I don't believe it's been answered in second edition. If it is possible, the spirit would shift into Twilight Form when it crossed over, and therefore be generally out of reach.

                                                    2) In Apocalypse (old world of darkness) there was a ritual to change one's auspice. Has there been an equivalent in Forsaken?
                                                    No, like Tessie said, you can remove your Auspice, and potentially gain it back, but that's mostly it, and half of it is storyteller fiat.

                                                    3) A weird one: how much clothing damage should a high-harmony werewolf expect when shifting into Dalu? Are we talking straining of seams, or hulking out?
                                                    A lot would depend on the clothes, eh? Straining, maybe bursting some seams. Definitely not hulking out level.

                                                    4) A bit more subjective: would you allow a wolf-blooded with the Anger Issues Tell to voluntarily "trigger" themselves with an instant action and a willpower point (a bit like a high-harmony Uratha shifting)?
                                                    They would have to have a good reason for it, some sort of Merit that allows them to achieve a meditative state or a worthy memory to fall back on. That's just my personal ST decision, though, I could see others allowing it or not.

                                                    5) How would you deal with another supernatural power trying to interfere with Kuruth? For example, a mage using Mind to calm down someone currently in Wasu-Im. A Clash of Wills? Versus the Uratha's Renown+Primal Urge, maybe? What if the raging individual is wolf-blooded (no renown, effective primal urge of 1)?
                                                    For Basu-Im the answer's on page 103. Clash of Wills is generally reserved for two vying powers, so it doesn't seem to be warranted unless someone was forcing Kuruth on the werewolf at the same time someone else was trying to nullify it (two elodoth using their abilities on the same werewolf, for instance). It would just be a resistance roll, likely based on Resolve+Primal Urge, though it would depend on the ability used.

                                                    6) The Shape Shifted Tell has the wolf-blooded going Urhsul during the full moon. Classic werewolf lore would suggest they would enter Kuruth, but the Tell doesn't specify this. Bug or feature?
                                                    Feature. Urshul doesn't go Kuruth, anyways, that's Gauru. You could easily tack on a variant rule that had the wolf be uncontrollably raging or doing things based on the character's subconscious desires, or controlled by the ST.

                                                    7) How many Tells do you reckon it would take before a wolf-blooded would register as Uratha to supernatural senses? And is this before or after the Storyteller has a fit and tells you to just play an actual werewolf already?
                                                    If you mean a werewolf's supernatural senses, never, though you could have a Tell that gives false positives. Going outward it would depend on whatever ability was being used to sense the supernatural and how knowledgeable the user is about werewolves or wolf-blooded.
