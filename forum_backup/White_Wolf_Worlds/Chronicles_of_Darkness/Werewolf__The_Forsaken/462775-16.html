    Originally posted by Bunyip View Post
    Sorry, they don't. Perception is a *Reflexive* Wits + Composure roll, Tracking is either an *Instant* Wits + Skill roll or *Extended/Contested* Wits + Skill roll. They're not the same, they have some overlapping modifiers, but their Gifts and Facets are separate.
    As mentioned before, tracking with Survival is massively reliant upon the information you gain from your senses. It simply makes no sense for your extraordinary capabilities of perception to fail the moment you attempt to track someone. This is not just limited to Honed Senses, but is inclusive of things like Trained Observer as well.
    Originally posted by Bunyip View Post
    Werewolves are incredible trackers, but they're not meant to be able to combine incompatible Facets to achieve Exceptional Success from rolling just one success on a roll.
    Works fine for everybody I know, so it is far from incompatible.
    It also informs the setting in really interesting ways, so I heartily recommend anyone to keep running it this way.

        Originally posted by Bunyip View Post

        Do you mean uncontested Tracking (rather than resisted)? I assume so.

        Form-based perception bonuses specifically *do not* apply to uncontested Tracking. This is because uncontested tracking is more abstract and assumes the Uratha switches forms as needed to blend in across however long it takes to track the prey. They add their Primal Urge bonus to this roll.

        Contested tracking more closely follows the Uratha's form changes - the werewolf might use Urhan in back alleys for one roll, but more prudently take Hishu for the next roll on a crowded street.

                                                    Originally posted by Amravanti View Post
                                                    Can werewolves see spirits in Twilight? It says they can sense them, but that's not quite a clear description of how it works.
                                                    I'd take, 'Uratha sense spirits in Twilight as well as they would manifested.' To mean that they can see them, smell them, hear them, and so forth.

                                                    How does one deal with a spirit in twilight if you can't interact with them?
                                                    Very carefully. Unless you have a bane, or the rite that kicks things out of Twilight. And a note to point out that werewolves count as banes to spirits more than two ranks below them. And a nice Death gift that lets you affect ghosts and death-oriented things in Twilight.

                                                    And why would a spirit ever materialize? Why do spirits even want to be in the Flesh anyway? What do they gain?
                                                    Lack of competition. Bountiful essence. Are you a fire spirit? Just light a match. Set a car on fire. Drive it into a fireworks factory. It mostly revolves around essence and it being easier to get, which is their whole raison de vivre.
