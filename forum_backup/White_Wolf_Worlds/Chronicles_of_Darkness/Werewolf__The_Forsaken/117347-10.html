    If it helps, I have a house rule I made up to help with grazing attacks. You only add a number of automatic damage equal to the dice pool used, at most. So, if you're down to 1-2 dice, you only add up to 1-2 of the weapon's damage rating. On a chance die, you don't add any automatic damage at all.
