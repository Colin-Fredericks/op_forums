                    Hmm... lots of proper nouns and technical terms...

                    Some of these have already been addressed:

                    Ninurum is the term used for the realm of the True Fae, literally meaning "an area surrounded by hedges"

                    Kathir is the Hedge, literally the "Great Thorn Bush Forest"

                    Angii-Thilikh is the God-Machine

                    Angzermusim refers to the angels of the God-Machine

                    besdurzi seems like an appropriate enough equivalent for "offspring," as used in Demon; it can literally mean offspring or descendants, but can also refer to an offshoot of some "parent" phenomenon.

                    Sikla will probably want to take a crack at the rest himself, but I can't resist coming up with my own interpretations:

                    First of all, based on the morphology we've seen so far and the derivation of the names for other groups related to the God Machine, Angzer seems to be a term (or at least an element in compounds) meaning "of or related to the God-Machine." Based on that, I would propose the following:

                    Angzerhufar: A Demon, as in a fallen angel of the God-Machine (roughly "adversary of the God-Machine")

                    Angii-er: Aether (something like "God's tears")

                    Angzera'ur: Infrastructure ("limb of the God-Machine")

                    Lul-Ikhi: Cover (literally "false face")

                    Not sure what to do with the other Demon ones at the moment, but here are a few ideas for the Changeling ones:

                    Falahazi: Changeling (literally "changed soul")

                    At-Nuun: One of the True Fae (roughly "brier-prince")

                    Dirum: Court

                    Seh: Contract (I found one reference to this word meaning "agreement." Sumerian uses the word for "agreement" to refer to contracts, so I figure First Tongue should too)
