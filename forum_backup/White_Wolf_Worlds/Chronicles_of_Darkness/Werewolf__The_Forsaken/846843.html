    The Siskur-Dah of the Izidakh

    03-13-2016, 09:24 PM
    the Fire-Touched Sacred Hunt grants your character increased influence over creatures of the Hisil. Spirits gain a bonus equal to the Uratha’s effective spirit Rank whenever they follow the werewolf’s direct instructions, or when they target the prey in combat or with their powers.
    The scouting mission had gone well and truly south, thought Jenny Whisper-Paw as she raced through the dense pinewood that the Fire-Touched claimed as their own. She had led the four other members of her pack into the enemy territory at the behest of the local protectorate, who had assured her that it would be a simple in and out job. Simple her ass. Now there were only three of them, Ricky was barely keeping pace while pissing blood from his throat and Helen was freaking the hell out enough for all of them put together including their two now deceased packmates. Jenny was trying to keep her cool but there was no denying how dire their situation was. Daring briefly to glance to each side she could make out the hulking urshul forms of their pursuers loping after them on each flank. Two on the left, Two on the right. No choice but to race ahead, if they could clear the forest they would find the road where Colin, Ricky's wolf blooded cousin was waiting with the car pretending to have engine trouble. Colin was a born getaway driver, his reckless expertise had saved their asses before they just had to get to him. A few more hundred yards, Jenny quickly let out a couple of sharp calls that Colin would know mean to start the car, almost there. That was when the ground fell away from beneath her paws and she tumbled down followed shortly by Ricky and Helen.

    They had ran directly into a pit trap, chased by the Pure who Jenny realized hadn't been trying to catch them but just herd them into this hole. It was a good twelve feet down with a bottom of wet sucking mud. Jenny tried to right herself and realized with a wince of pain that she snapped something in her right forelimb during the fall. Helen was trying to scramble up the side of the pit shifting out of her wolf shape to her human skin for better grip but the soft earth made for poor handholds and came away under her hands. Ricky lay very still, the blood seeping from his throat mixing with the sludge. Jenny nuzzled her injured friend, silently willing him to not give up and stand again, while she forced the last of her essence to accelerate her regeneration. Jenny shivered, grateful for the numbing cold of essence-healing as her bones snapped and slid back into place and muscles and tendons re-knit themselves. Helen made it to the lip of the pit when a snarl announced the arrival of the fire-touched. Helen hesitated, uncertain what to do and a moment later the choice was removed from her and she found herself at the bottom of the pit with a mangled hand courtesy of the jaws of one of the Anshega. Jenny and Helen looked at each other for a moment and the time between racing heartbeats seemed like an eternity.

    "Rise; Come forth and kill!" Spoke a cruel voice in clear and commanding First Tongue. Helen's fear turned to confusion at the instruction of the Fire-Touched until the earthen Ensih made itself known as it emerged from the ground. The spirit raised what might have been an inscrutable craggy face to top of the pit and the Pure who hovered there like carrion birds with malice written in their eyes. The one who had spoken before repeated his demand, then the one next to him repeated it then another. The command became a chant and the scent of wet earth intensified as the spirit influenced the area to accept its manifested form. Helen began hyperventilating in panic. Jenny stood on her hind legs which thickened and shifted as she went to Dalu, better suited to fighting in the close confines of the pit. She hoped that they could defeat this thing without resorting to the war form, if they where going to somehow survive the Anshega they'd need it later. Jenny closed her eyes and calmed herself and then tried to rally Helen.

    "We can do this Helen, you and me. This isn't how we go out. Lets teach these motherfuckers about the Forsaken." but Helen was intractable, eyes wide and fixed on the spirit as it materialized between the two of them. The Ensih moved towards Helen with then inevitability of a landslide. Wounded, broken and terrified Helen merely threw her arms in front of her face as the spirit crushed her beneath its bulk with a sickening squish and a final sounding crack as the earth spirit shifted atop her. Jenny lost herself, as rage and sorrow welled up inside alloying into something that pushed her forward and drover her claws at the Ensih with all the force she could muster lacerating emphermal flesh. The spirit turned as if noticing her for the first time and turned slowly and inexorably. The Anshega continued their hate filled tirade.

    "Crush her"

    "Kill the bitch"

    "Bury the insolent pup"

    The spirit rose up to full height dwarfing even Jenny in Dalu and threw itself at her....

    ... But how hard does it hit?

    So the situation is this we have a werewolf who is the prey of a pack of pure under the fire touched Siskur-Dah condition. Rather than combating the Urdaga themselves the Pure decide to take advantage of the Siskur-Dar condition and have one of their spirit allies do the dirty work for them. The spirit has four pure of varying honorary spirit Ranks telling them to kill this forsaken, lets say for argument they are Ranks 3, 2, 2 and 1. should the spirit concced to the wishes of the pure what bonus should the Spirit get? +3 as its the highest or +8 as each pure grants its own rank as a bonus?

                                      Originally posted by Bunyip View Post

                                      I appreciate the fluff, it gave me the chance to illustrate a few points. I'd suggest that most spirits are going to follow the letter of commands rather than the spirit.
                                      Yeah, that's how I run spirits as well. In fact, I have a story about doing just that:

                                      Years ago, I was in the Camarilla (now Mind's Eye Society). I was on the ST staff for a convention, and a friend of mine decided that he wanted his character to go find a "time spirit." I eventually let him find a spirit representing the clock in front of a bank (I just rolled with it, because I was curious what he wanted). So he rolls up on this spirit, makes nice (it helped that his character was a powerful and respected Storm Lord Ithaeur), and then asks, "May I speak with you a minute?"

                                      Without missing a beat, I (playing the spirit) say, "You have one minute. Go."

                                      He stared at me for a second and shook his head, chuckling, because he should have known better. I then let him think for however long it took for my phone to click over to the next minute (so as to time him), and then start the conversation. I don't actually remember what he was looking for, but I do recall that he managed to complete the conversation within the time limit.
