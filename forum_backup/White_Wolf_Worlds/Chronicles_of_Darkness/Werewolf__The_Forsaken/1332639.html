    [Pangaean] Many-Arms, Irised and Grasping

    08-26-2019, 08:18 AM
    Many-Arms may not be the mightiest of Pangaeans, but it is certainly a clever one, and oh-so curious as well, probing the seas of three worlds for answers to the innumerable questions it asks. It even undergoes forays onto dry land from time to time in search of new riddles to solve.

    The Pangaean appears as a spiraling, iridescent mass of nigh-infinite tentacles surrounding a central indistinct, consistently altering shape, as well as two enormous and shining eyes whose ink-black pupils are sometimes perfect circles, and other times wavy sinusoids. Many-Arms can also take on the form of any kind of cephalopod, a human, or a strange hybrid between the two, in addition to its inexhaustible repertoire of deceptive mimicries. Many only know the Pangaean as a tangle of enormous, grasping appendages bursting forth from one portal or another.

    Many-Arms remembers a time in the distant, hazy past when it was quite a different being. Its hunger was of a more prosaic sort, and the Pangaean and its brood ruled the oceans of prehistory, until the day a mighty new creature named Strong-Jaws swam from the depths and took an enormous, painful bite out of it. Out of desperation, Many-Arms blew a cloud of darkness over this terrifying newcomer’s eyes and quickly jetted away.

    Grievously injured, Many-Arms drifted through the Border Marches, certain of its impending death, when it came across a strange thing that had fallen from the sky. Out of hunger for Essence, Many-Arms seized the foreign being and devoured it, healing the worst of its injuries in the process. And once it was all gone, down in the Pangaean’s gullet, Many-Arms stopped to ponder: I wonder what that could have been?

    Ever since, Many-Arms has been nearly bursting with questions about the wider universe, obsessively exploring every nook and cranny for strange truths, and it’s developed quite a few tricks along the way. It knows how to change its appearance to trick humans, spirits, and Uratha alike, and can fit into even the smallest of places. Once, it squeezed its way through a tiny hole in reality and found itself in an ocean of human tears. It’s been back there several times, savoring the pearls of memory it sometimes comes across floating in that abyss. Ever since, Death Wolf has visited Many-Arms on many an occasion, with quite a few questions of her own for the Pangaean.

    Of course, boundless (and often tactless) curiosity has landed Many-Arms in hot water on more than one occasion. There was the time that it longed to learn more about the Warden Moon, and so extended one of its tentacles high into the air in order to find out what the spirit felt like. Urfarah, enraged at what he perceived as a challenge to his nocturnal lover, quickly put a stop to that, biting off the offending appendage. It grew back, of course, but, for once in its godly existence, Many-Arms learned to favor caution over hunger. Beyond that, the Pangaean has expressed much curiosity toward the children of Wolf and Moon. It marvels at the prowess of their shapeshifting (though obviously no match for its own), and from time to time has chosen to walk or hunt alongside the Uratha. Sometimes it even attempts to bestow favor upon those whom it deems particularly clever, carving oceantide Renown brands into werewolf flesh with its denticles. Such rewards rarely go appreciated, however, and often serve to draw the ire of the lunar choirs.

    Many-Arms is not very well-known to the Vinca; it’s much more communicative with an island civilization to the south of the People’s lands, and has appeared to coastal-dwelling humans the world over, posing all manner of arcane questions and mind-bending riddles to those willing to listen. Sometimes, contemplation of these mysteries proves quite enlightening—a select few ancient temples on India’s coastline contain hidden rooms adorned with bizarre mandalas in which the knowledgeable may recognize the Pangaean’s shifting mien.

    As with the other lords of the Border Marches, Many-Arms has made itself scarce in modern days. Some believe it died during the Sundering, or it fell victim to predatory Wise who coveted the secrets and arcane power nestled in the wily Pangaean’s heart. Others believe that a creature of such cunning and trickery must have found a way to escape the collapse of the Border Marches and those who wish it ill, and that somewhere in some world or another, Many-Arms stalks in hidden forms, still searching for knowledge of all kinds, answers to questions no mortal has ever thought to ask.

    Many-Arms, Irised and Grasping

    Rank 4 Pangaean

    Power 12 Finesse 15 Resistance 10
    Willpower 10 Essence 25
    Initiative 27 Defense 12 Speed 35
    Size 10 Corpus 20

    Arcana: Mind 4
    Influences: Cephalopods 4, Obsession 2
    Numina: Awe, Dement, Ghost Eater, Hallucination, Regenerate
    Ban: Many-Arms must stop and consider any question, mystery, or riddle that it has never encountered before, and must make an effort to try and figure out the answer for itself.
    Bane: A net made with 88 megalodon teeth.

    Divine Bleed: The first time in a scene that it suffers Corpus damage, Many-Arms may reflexively use the Awe, Dement, or Hallucination Numina for free. It may also use the Create power of its Influence (Cephalopods) for free each time it suffers damage, or it may create a Rank 1 cephalopod spirit instead.

    Essence Meddling: Many-Arms can carve Renown brands of Cunning or Wisdom into an Uratha’s spirit-flesh. To use this feature, the Pangaean must spend 12 Essence and touch a werewolf it deems worthy of the bestowment. The Uratha gains a dot in either Cunning or Wisdom, as well as one of the following as a Persistent Condition: Addled, Amnesia, Fugue, Obsession, or Madness. These brands glow with a shimmering cerulean light while in the Shadow or when flared. Uratha who have more dots of Renown gained this way rather than from Lunes find their non-Hishu forms begin to take on aspects of cephalopods, such as changing skin colors, rubbery bodies, tentacles, or the ability to breathe water instead of air. Whether the criteria for creating these brands stem from arcane laws of the Shadow or are merely based on the Pangaean’s personal preferences and whims remains a mystery. Occasionally, Many-Arms will attempt to carve Renown into mortals who please it or who exploit its Ban; the results are about what one would expect.

    Intruder: Many-Arms can pass through any opening, no matter how small, and can always escape from any restraint, except for its Bane. By spending 4 points of Essence, it can activate a dormant portal of any kind, and by spending 8 Essence, it can create a new Synese that leads to anywhere in the Temenos, or the Oneiros of anyone suffering from a Condition that the Pangaean has caused. The Iris remains open until Many-Arms stops paying attention to it.

    Kraken: Against creatures of lesser Rank, Many-Arms automatically wins any contested grapple rolls, and is treated as having rolled 8 successes. Against beings of equal or higher Rank, Many-Arms enjoys a +8 to its roll. Attacks with its tentacles have a damage rating of 8, Armor Piercing 8, and ignore 8 points of Durability.

    Lord of Squids: Many-Arms is aware of the health and emotional state of all cephalopods within 88 miles of its current location. If there are no cephalopods within that area, it suffers a -3 to its Attributes. By touching a dead or incapacitated creature of at least Size 4, Many-Arms can cause a number of cephalopods equal to the creature’s Stamina to tear their way out of the body and into existence.

    Master of Disguise: Many-Arms can, as a reflexive action, alter its size, shape, and coloration to resemble any sort of creature or object it desires, between Sizes 1 and 12. The Pangaean could appear as a snake, a coral reef, a slice of New York-style pizza, or even a werewolf, and is capable of perfectly copying the features of any being it has observed. It requires a Perception roll with a -8 penalty to detect that something is off with the subject in question. This is not a “true” transformation, and Many-Arms keeps all of its own traits regardless of its current shape, although while disguised, the Pangean may choose to suppress its Overwhelming Presence ability.

    Monstrous Form: Many-Arms has Armor 10, and may spend 1 Essence to shrug off any Tilt it suffers. These benefits to not apply to attacks from its Banes.

    Murkblur: As a reflexive action, Many-Arms can spend 3 Essence and envelop an area of up to 88 cubic yards in a thick, murky darkness that occludes and confuses, imposing a -8 penalty to all actions. The ink covers all phases of Twilight as well, and interferes with natural and supernatural senses of any kind. Many-Arms may instead with this ability release a cloud of luminescent blue orbs; any who see these lights must succeed on a Resolve + Composure roll with a -8 penalty or feel compelled to approach them.

    Overwhelming Presence: A mortal human who encounters Many-Arms suffers an Integrity breaking point. If this roll is failed, the character gains the Madness Persistent Condition.

    Slippery Mind: Whenever a supernatural power attempts to read or probe Many-Arms’s thoughts, it may choose to automatically impose either the Obsession or Madness Condition on the intruder. Mundane attempts to logically follow the Pangaean’s thought processes and patterns automatically inflict the Confused Condition. Creatures suffering any of these Conditions are also considered Open to spirits of curiosity, madness, and cephalopods.

    Thought Devourer: By touching a sapient being with its tentacles, Many-Arms can draw out a single thought, memory, or emotion from the target, transforming it into a Rank 1 spirit. This resolves any connected Conditions or Tilts, but may potentially impose others, such as Addled or Confused. Many-Arms may choose to immediately devour the creation, gaining 10 Essence as well as any knowledge the spirit contained, though the Pangaean may also allow it to wander freely.

    So, here's a Pangaean I'd been working on for a while now, inspired by Chris Allen's posts on the subject, Danna Staaf's excellent book Squid Empire, a pseudoscientific theory about the origin of octopuses, and a few other things as well. Many-Arms is technically a little more powerful than a Rank 4 entity should be, but that's because it's led an interesting life, and, fittingly enough, not everything is quite as it may seem. Can YOU solve the mystery of Many-Arms?
