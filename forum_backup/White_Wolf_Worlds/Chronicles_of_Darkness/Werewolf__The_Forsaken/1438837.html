    How do you discourage players from gaming Harmony using junk food?

    03-09-2021, 07:30 PM
    Overall I really like how 2E Harmony is something you have to keep in balance, but it seems very vulnerable to some very silly exploits. Like, oops, I just ate an orphan for essence and broke toward Spirit. Better pop open a bag of Cheetos so I can break back to Flesh again. I've dubbed this the "Bag of Cheetos" problem in my group and I'm curious how other groups handle it.

          Originally posted by AsaTJ View Post
          Like, oops, I just ate an orphan for essence and broke toward Spirit. Better pop open a bag of Cheetos so I can break back to Flesh again.
          • As nofather points out, unless you're Harmony 3 or below, that's not even giving you a chance to rebalance, and even when it does it's only back up to the point where you have a single Ban.
          • Unless you're Primal Urge 1 you are deriving no benefit from eating processed food that isn't meat (and even processed food that is meat, at 4 and above), and otherwise you're only getting to make a roll that a character with average Attributes and no Touchstone bonus will generally succeed at three times out of four.
          • Processed food literally does not grow on trees and if your character is at the level of Harmony where they can drop into the Shadow from the Flesh without a locus but not vice-versa then sources of it may be hard to come by.
          • The same category of breaking points toward flesh that eating processed food occupies includes staying out of the Shadow for one day, which likewise only gets you out of the bottom of the scale on a successful roll.
          • Breaking towards the opposite half of the scale is exactly as easy for any given character as it is for them to fall into Hard Rage, Touchstone bonus notwithstanding, and the contributing Attributes directly reduce the effectiveness of mind-altering powers used against the character. A werewolf character that can easily rebalance is a volatile one.

          Unless you're uselessly dedicating camera time to characters gorging on useless food to dig out of the hole they've put themselves in, this is a comparative non-issue.

                          I’ve yet to actually play Werewolf, so this is to be taken with a boulder of salt, but my reading of the Harmony system is that in order to stay at either extreme of Harmony, you, the player, have to make a concerted effort to do so. It seems devised that, all else being equal, the breaking points will tend to send you back into the 4-7 range eventually. As already brought up, simply staying out of the Shadow for a day can bring you back to Harmony 4. On the flip side, you can lose high Harmony simply by avoiding your pack. So yes, eating Cheetos will help ground in in humanity if you are way too close to spirit. But so will other pretty mundane things. I get the impression that the intent is less “eat Cheetos to regain Harmony” and more “if you are intentionally roleplaying a low Harmony character, avoid Cheetos.” This is reflected in-universe, at least in extreme cases, as Shunned by the Moon makes it clear that most Zi’ir are the result of deliberately neglecting one’s Harmony.

                                  Originally posted by AsaTJ View Post
                                  The "atonement" (eat a cheeseburger) doesn't have the same narrative weight as the "crime" (eating a human being).
                                  It's not meant to. Harmony is, to a degree, self-balancing. When more constant deeds push you further and further towards the spirit, you reach a crisis point where your imbalance becomes extremely unhealthy. Even mild nods towards the flesh are then able to pull you back from the brink, though they may be increasingly difficult to perform in the condition you've driven yourself toward. When the flesh is starving, even a small meal is better than nothing at all. The same applies to cleaving too closely to the flesh. When your Harmony enters crisis, you're not trapped in that crisis. More lenient triggers become available to pull you back.

                                  2e Harmony isn't a slippery slope where the farther down you fall the harder it is to climb back up. Crises of imbalance happen to really unwise werewolves, ideologically extreme werewolves, or werewolves in extreme situations. Harmony is more like hygeine: a spiritual health regime you need to moderate and mind and keep up on. Don't forget to hunt. Neither isolate yourself in the Hisil nor forget it to concentrate on the ways of humanity and flesh. The pressure of Harmony is the pressure to stay liminal.

                                          Harmony seems to be something that's constantly shifting, especially at the extremes. A Uratha who finds himself falling too far toward spirit would be wise to go invest in his human side, whether that's going out to the movies, snacking on junk food, wasting time on video games, or whatever keeps him feeling human.

                                          As said earlier, carrying around a bag of cheetos wouldn't do it, a wolf far in spirit needs to go engage anything at all to remind him that he's partially human.

                                          This isn't like a Vampire's Humanity or a Mage's Wisdom that spirals slowly down and requires concerted effort to raise. Harmony is just a day-to-day part of a Uratha's life that goes up and down like moods or habits. Keeping Harmony in balance is like reminding yourself to stay hydrated. Nothing is "OP" when it comes to moving it in either direction.
