    Forsaken without the Alpha theory

    05-21-2015, 06:19 PM
    Forsaken packs seems to be based on the premise that a single alpha wins a dominance contest to become alpha and rule the pack. Based on more recent studies, that seems to be an inaccurate depiction of a wolf pack. http://io9.com/why-everything-you-kn...rong-502754629 Instead a wolf pack seems to just be a family with pups eventually breaking off and forming new packs. Is there a way to instead apply this pack mentality to forsaken and would it change anything essential to the pack? Possibly a pack operating more with two older veteran wolves and adopted pack members who eventually break off on their own. Or maybe having werewolf packs tied strongly to familial bloodlines.

                                                          The only time that the Alpha thing comes up, in my opinion, is when we start talking about 1) the Dominance Gifts, and 2) Spirit Rank. It feels like the spirit hierarchy in the Hisil is now entirely based on the Alpha-theory situation, so, as the werewolves are necessarily part of it, they can't get away from it when dealing with spirits.

                                                          With Dominance Gifts, its only Honor and Purity aspects. You do what I say, because I'm Dominant to you (though, lets be fair, we can have PCs that have the gifts but don't use them on pack). And its not so much that you are dominant to another, as you try to force that Dominance onto someone else, designated "prey."

                                                          Neither of the above are forced to apply to inter-pack relations, but absolutely with pack-v-everyone else. And its very easy to go both ways.

                                                          EDIT - oh, a third and fourth way. Dalu's Badass Motherfucker trait - use your presence to force someone to give another up. Another I'm-the-dominant-one-here thing. Again, still no requirement to actually use it on another wolf. The Rahu can inflict Submissive on prey, but again, no promise of using it on fellow wolves. If anything, that last one is discouraged from use.
