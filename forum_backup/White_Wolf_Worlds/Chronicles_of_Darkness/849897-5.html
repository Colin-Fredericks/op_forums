                        And now, here are more angelic form abilities.. after half a day of web troubles XD

                        Note, the scaling of utility and power does vary wildly a bit, though I did do my best to make them not too crazy. Constructive feedback is greatly appreciated ! Also, some abilities are noted to be incompatible with the Fall and thus are unavailable for demons to possess, and others are noted on how they change to fit the demonic form. Otherwise, they function much the same way for demons as well as angels.
                        __________
                        Imperial Raiment ( Angelic Modification)
                        There are times where the Word must be heard by mortals, and in order to impress it upon those who are to receive it, sometimes Messengers are augmented with an appearance that matches the authority they represent, as well as offering a small amount of physical protection from attack.

                        Appearance: The angel's form is gilded and encrusted with various gemstones, all which glow as if illuminated from within with thousands of tiny stars. A crown of phantasmal fire adorns the angel's head and a beautiful mosaic of images and iconography adorn it's body- all designed to awe and induce reverence for the angel and it's purpose.

                        Mechanics: This Modification enhances the natural gravitas of it's bearer, subverting the minds and softening the hearts of those mortals who gaze upon it. Mortal characters who engage in social interactions with the angel suffer a penalty to resist it's maneuvers equal to half the angel's Finesse score rounded down. Supernatural targets find the penalty slightly lessened, being only equal to the angel's Rank. Imperial Raiment also offers greater defenses against attack, adding a + 1 to it's armor rating without inciting an Initiative penalty.

                        Influence Enhancer ( Angelic Technology)
                        Angels are called to perform a great many tasks over the course of their existence in the Service in the Machine, and the very Influences that every angel possess are among their greatest instruments. As such, those angels whose Missions revolve around manipulating anything from physical objects to living creatures to emotions and concepts are granted hardware needed to give these powers an extra boost.

                        Appearance: This ability does not normally manifest as a physical aspect on the angel's form, being present more as indelible aura that alerts those capable of perceiving it that the angel is graced with an air of increased proficiency, along with an unshakable feeling of absolute purpose surrounding the angel.

                        Mechanics: by spending a point of Essence to activate it; Influence Enhancer allows the angel who possesses this Technology to utilize their Influences as if they were one dot higher than their current level to a maximum of 5, for the remainder of the scene. This Technology has the added benefit of adding a + 2 to all rolls involving the angel's Influences. Should the user of this Technology Fall, they also lose access to this ability, instead being replaced with a very similar Technology called Exploit Enhancer.

                        Aspect Shears ( Angelic Modification/Technology)
                        A hundred thousand Projects require living sacrifices-human or otherwise-to function, and the vast majority of Psychopomps ( and occasionally Destroyers) are responsible for providing these sacrificial subjects. Some sacrifices are more particular, however, requiring incredibly specific materials and ingredients: The right hand of a thief who broke into a hundred homes before being caught or the heart of a truly righteous mother for example. For these sacrifices, angels are sometimes equipped with the means of shearing these pieces away from their victims- with said pieces miraculously remaining alive and unspoiled until they are ready for their purpose.

                        Appearance: One of the angel's hands is replaced with a highly articulate pair of blades that greatly resemble scissors or garden shears, though the blades themselves are unnaturally long and sharp- easily being a foot or more in length, These shears are often engraved with arcane runes that no mortal ( or demon) can decipher, and typically glow slightly with a wan ruddy or yellow light in dark places.

                        Function: These shears are meant for one thing- dismembering living beings without killing either the victim or the pieces the angel intends to cut away. The angel pays a point of essence and rolls for a standard melee attack with it's Power+ Finesse -The victims Defense + Stamina ( armor bonuses apply where applicable). On a successful attack instead of dealing damage, the shears instead painlessly cut away the desired limb or body part away, inflicting appropriate tilts such as Arm Wrack, Leg Wrack, Blind, and so on. There are exceptions, decapitation or removing the heart may leave the head or heart themselves alive, but the rest of the body dies without these vital pieces. Interestingly, and perhaps horrifyingly, this Modification can affect demonic form abilities just as easily as it can the flesh of more mundane creatures- stripping these powers away from the owners while leaving the abilities fully intact and operational in their shorn state. Like with mortals, demons are affected by the adverse tilts from losing limbs ( and potentially death), but also lose access to the corresponding abilities.

                        However hope is not lost for the victims of these Shears, as long as these body parts are recovered before they are sacrificed, they can be put back in place with very little effort- the rightful owner's bodies accept these parts back as if they were never cut away in the first place, reattaching with no additional aid. Only an extended Dexterity + Medicine roll is required, with three successes needed to successfully restore the severed parts. As before, Unfortunately, once removed a head or heart cannot be replaced- their bodies are sadly dead and useless. It is possible that through Exploits or other magic the victim can be restored, albeit the particulars of such a feat are at the Storyteller's discretion.

                        Note: This ability can survive the transition of an Angels Fall, where the Modification/Technology functions much the same, though the demon in question can graft stolen limbs to his own body or demonic form, gaining the traits or powers associated with whatever parts are integrated into the enterprising Unchained- though unfortunately the demon cannot strip away angelic powers with this ability, it has lost the potency to do so. ( Also I as unsure on what to classify it. It's a bit too strong to be either a Modification or Technology, but at the same time I don't see it as a Process.. also for those who are eagle eyed, this ability is inspired by the Collector from the Dark Eras book)

                        Optimized Impulse Drives ( Angelic Propulsion)
                        While many angels possess numina designed for rapid translocation, the Machine realizes that sometimes it is more advantageous to install it's agents with hardware to allow for rapid movement that does not require large expenditures of occult energy. Thus some angels possess internal and external machinery to allow for immense acceleration while remaining almost undetectable to enemies capable of sensing essence emissions.

                        Appearance: Additional pistons and gears are built into the Angel's limbs, glowing with slight luminescence from within.

                        Mechanics: with an expenditure of willpower, the angel activates its impulse drives, rolling Finesse + Rank and adding the successes to it's speed trait. These drives carry the added benefit of adding this speed modifier to activation rolls for numina that allow for supernatural speed or transportation- the extra hardware providing a boost to it's internal systems for the transit. Strangely, this propulsion does not function should the angel in question utilize Incepts such as Delivery or Motion- It must either choose to utilize this ability or the Incepts. Attempting to utilize both to augment each other overtaxes the angels' systems, dealing a number of points of lethal damage equal to the angel's Finesse as it experiences a destructive energy surge through it's internal structure.

                        Note: This ability can be retained through the Fall, though naturally it does not interact with Numina or Incepts. As such, it is capable of being used to boost Exploits and Embeds with little difficulty- the strain on the demon is much lessened.

                        Celestial Halo ( Angelic Process)
                        The God-Machine, despite not being omniscient, is near omnipotent in it's mastery of universal forces. Being Its servants, it is only natural a number of them are bequeathed with a greater fraction of this dominance from time to time. Demons who know of this Process are often stricken with fear when they encounter an angelic sibling who possesses it; for they know these slaves have been granted freedom to make use of their greatest of powers for the advancement of their Missions- powers that blessed with a larger portion of their God's awesome might behind them.

                        Appearance: A great, fiery halo of divine energy encircles or rests behind the angel's head. It's appearance varies widely from a simple lambent band of light to a intricately engraved blue-white gear made of lightning to a wheel of Dharma composed of golden flames, to anything in between.

                        Mechanics: The Celestial Halo grants incredible power to those angels outfitted with it- and represent a grave danger to those who would interfere with their Missions. This Process doubles the efficiency of an angels' Incepts primary effects: Units' effect increases to twice the user's Rank + 2 for example. This Process also is embedded with programming imperatives that grant the user more operational freedom concerning its Incepts and their use, enabling the angel to utilize these root-codes with freedom a number of times equal to it's Rank+ 1 without violating it's Mission parameters- as long as the use of said Incepts do not directly endanger the Mission or cause it's failure ( i.e. destroying what the angel was assigned to protect, killing an excess number of mortals, etc). Naturally, should an angel who possesses this Process somehow Fall, this ability is lost along with its Incept privileges.

                        Familiar-Drone ( Angelic Process)
                        Some angels require additional eyes and hands to aid them with their Missions, and rather than assign multiple angels to a single assignment, the God-Machine instead equips it's servant with a semi-autonomous sub-unit that is capable of performing less strenuous or otherwise lower priority tasks while the angel tackles more pressing matters- though the drone does require support and upkeep from it's angelic “platform”.

                        Appearance: The bio-mechanical drone linked to the angel can possess a wide variety of forms: a iron feathered raven, an iridescent dragonfly with airfoils made of fiberglass and aluminum, or something as artificial as a rotor drone. The only things these familiars have in common are that they all possess the ability to fly, and all are anchored to/generated from some sort of docking structure built into the angel's body when not in use.

                        Mechanics: The familiar drone uses the same ephemeral rules as angels, though with some alterations. These drones are treated as Rank 1 angels and possess four dots in each of their attributes; though their angel platform may choose to allocate dot's from their own Attributes to the drone, to a maximum of three dots per attribute in order to increase the servitors capabilities. In addition, a familiar-drone possesses three numina at it's disposal; two of it's user's choosing along with the Innocuous numen, as well as possessing a single dot in any Influence it's user chooses for it to have-provided the angel also has access to those Influences. Being ephemeral constructs like angels, the drone must make use of Manifestation conditions in order to effectively interact with the world around them, thus they are equipped with the Twilight Form and Materialization Manifestations. Due to their relative weakness and not being true angels, the drone cannot possess or utilize Incepts, though they can benefit from the effects of them should their platform deign to use one to empower them.

                        These drones are size 1 and possess an Essence pool of 10 when fully fueled, and burn through 1 point of essence per day of being separated from their controller, though they might burn through their stores faster if pressed to defend themselves or utilize numina that require essence expenditure. Despite being semi-autonomous and possessing a surprising level of creativity when interpreting tasks given to them, the drones are virtually mindless when not performing functions assigned to them by their platform, and thus do not have willpower pools of their own, though like with Attribute allocation, the angel may imbue it's drone with it's own willpower points, up to a maximum of three. As the drones share a constant link to their controller, the angel is aware of it's drone at all times, and is able to send commands to the drone reflexively at no cost, or take direct control of the drone at any time with an expenditure of a willpower.

                        An angel may only have one familiar-drone active at a time, though if the pre existing drone is destroyed, the angel may generate a new one at the cost of five essence. Attribute allocation, willpower investment, and essence transference can only be performed while the drone is docked with the angel, and is conducted on a 1:1 ratio per turn- the drone's systems are simply not advanced enough to handle power transfers of greater intensity.

                        Note: Like others, this ability can be retained through the Fall, though the drone becomes a fully material construct and loses it's rank ( which is replaced by Primium), manifestations, utilizes embeds instead of numina, and is sustained through Aether. Otherwise the ability is unchanged.
