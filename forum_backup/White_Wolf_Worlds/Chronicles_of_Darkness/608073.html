    Deviant: The ??? – You woke up…different. Someone changed you; on a slab, an operating table, an altar. By luck or by fate, you escaped. Hunted by the ones who made you, they hold all the cards.                           A theory on how it differs from Changeling:

                          The fact it seems the Deviant-makers are human (and in all likelihood the Seer equivalent of Deviants), that's a pretty big difference. Seeing as how a character is more defined as how you escaped in 2E rather than what was done, that's a newly-made niche to fit in. And changelings aren't as revenge-centric.

                          I can also guess there's a vastly different ghoul version for Deviants-I'm guessing people with whatever supernatural ability allows them to experiment on unlucky sods, but don't view other people as acceptable costs for whatever goals they were planning on. People called something like Illuminated (both as a pun on Illuminati, ye olde bogeyman of conspiracy theory and a reference to the political power they can acquire and leverage for their purposes, and because they've been enlightened to just how vast reality is and how to harness at least some of it for magic that may as well be science-and often is. If this sounds like mages, that's probably a good thing-emphasizes their actions are a very human horror).

                          I also theorize there's going to be a nice sidebar on how the procedures that make Deviants aren't something exclusive to [INSERT HIDDEN HUMAN FACTIONS HERE]. If you want a crossover, there's the Ordo Dracul, corrupt mystagogues and Thrall-making Seers, hypocritical changelings looking for soldiers against the Huntsmen, and Centimani right there. And of course, I think and hope Deviants can get on the mad science gig too; the temptation to sink to your enemy's level is always a huge threat in revenge stories, no?
