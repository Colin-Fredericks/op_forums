                    Aye, I concur in that Mummy's odd power mechanics definitely come off as a feature, not a bug. You don't have to be playing long to recognise the patterns. Powers that include Sekhem are often extremely strong... for a little while. Those that include several additional stats to roll in addition to Sekhem (Secrets Ripped from Skies Tier 2, for example), are clearly designed to peak at high Sekhem and decline, but remain strong even at low Sekhem. Powers without Sekhem included in their pool (Tier 1 Words of Dead Glory, for example) are evidently designed to be equally strong irrespective of the user's current Sekhem trait. Powers that are just Sekhem, or Sekhem+one other trait, are meant to be heavily influenced in strength by the Descent and become dramatically weaker within a few scenes of the first session (see Power of Re Tier 3).

                    There's a very clear intent behind the lack of uniformity in Mummy's powers, and I am more than happy to forfeit a sense of cohesiveness for the excellent ways in which these mechanics integrate with the game on a thematic level.

                        Fair enough.

                        As I said, I personally find the mechanics clunky, unintuitive and messy, but that's just me. I like uniformity and consistency in my systems and I find coherency and simplicity aesthetically pleasing. However, as I said before, that's just my personal taste, so its not something I expect everyone to agree on.

                        Though I have said it before in other threads I'll repeat it here: I've been living and breathing God-Machine, Strix and Demon for the last 9-12 months. Not to mention following the open dev for Werewolf, Mage and Promethean. So I am just much more involved with the 2nd Ed system now.

                        While mechanically its very similar obviously, I feel that the 2nd Ed games are much more streamlined in their content then the 1st Ed versions were. Looking at the very storyteller friendly system for Embeds in Demon for example were every power is two stat (attribute + skill) and most of them that invoke a resistance roll (which is left in the hands of the ST, not as a hard rule) are penalized rather then resisted. Very simple to learn and very friendly to not bogging down play looking up unique rolls for each power.

                        Basically, I'm following Mummy because I feel the core idea about identity and Memory is awesome. The writing is good, the setting, worldbuilding and general "fluff" text is great. Do I think I'm ever going to run it as an actual game ever again while its in its 1st Ed iteration? Hell no. I'm happy to bask in the setting until the 2nd Ed comes out in 2-3 years and streamlines the whole thing nicely (hopefully).

                        Not to mention hopefully give us a more inclusive Core book. Since Mummy got short changed in page count. The Vampire 2nd Ed (which Werewolf is supposedly about the same size as) is about 320 pages. Thats a full 50 more then Mummy. So hopefully the extra space in Mummy 2nd Ed will let us get the rules for Vestiges (from DC), Relic Architecture and the Deceived (obviously in a cut down form, though second ed might simply their system as well) in the core. If not just a little more space for Relics in general (and the Shuankshen maybe) and actual example Nomes for various cities of interest to the Arisen around the world.

                        Anyway, so this post was not completely off topic:

                        The rules for Relic Architecture are supposed to be in Sothis I believe. Just remembered that.

                        DC has a few examples of Relic Architecture, but it mentions the full rules for creating Relic Architecture will be in a later Mummy book. I presume this is Sothis. Will be nice to have the rules, as it will let people playing Tef-Aahbi finally do some more active Geomancy stuff that's not just flavour text.

                        Also on Sorcerers:

                        I feel we have a lot of ways of modeling these guys without even stepping out of the Mummy line. Giving them a Pillars pool and some Utterance abilities is just the most obvious. Affinities, Seba and Pillar powers for the Fasid/Sadikh could all represent this stuff as well.

                        As I said before if you include a couple of non-Mummy books: Second Sight (core WoD 1st Ed), Immortals (core WoD 1st Ed), Reliquary (core WoD 1st Ed) and Witch Hunters (HtV) you get a lot more options too. When I ran Mummy I used a fan-hack for a Second Sight Tradition and some elements of the "Eternal" type of "lesser" Immortal from Immortals. If I ran it again I'd likely use Witch Hunters system for Gutter Magic.

                        Oh, something I do hope is included in Sothis Ascends:

                        Bane Relics

                        We've had one so far in Guildhalls and it was awesome. I kinda like the idea of Ammut being a bit more active in supporting the Shaunkshen and these fit the bill awesomely. That being said, I'd also like some more Bane Affinities too. A pipe dream would be some Bane Utterances, but that's not likely to happen I suspect.

                        Basically more "Bane" stuff in Sothis and Rio will make me pretty happy really. We know from Deceived that Ammut can't really interact with the world directly anymore, but having her as a terrible eldrich presence and patron of the Shaunkshen works nicely in the setting I feel.

                        In a similar line of thought, example Duat entities of a lesser magnitude then the Judges, Ammut and Shezmu would also be kinda nice to have. As I kinda like the idea of Sorcerers conjuring up Slaughterers and other lesser demons.

                        Sothis seems like the only place these things could go now really. As they don't really fit in a setting book like Rio or a prop book like Avarice.
