                        Yeah. I figure the Malleus showed up in the very early 17th century, when the Netherlands and Japan established trade relations. The Dutch helped to quell the Japanese Christian uprisings, which I figure was a matter of ideological purity for the Malleus. The Malleus doesn't maintain a strong presence in Edo in the period, but enough of one that others pay attention.

                        It's taking place shortly after the isolationism laws took place. We're aiming for the 1640s. And yeah, I went with Edo because I didn't really want to make it about the Battle of Sekigahara. I'm not too interested in war stuff, but the aftermath is really great for Hunter stories. Basically, this is in the shift to the Tokugawa Shogunate, when there's a huge push to purify all the outside influence out of Japan. I figure that's got to be a metaphor for Hunter if there ever was one, right?

                                                I really enjoyed the writeup. It presented an interesting view of the 7th Generation in places before Christianity took root. It also seems that the Rampant are, apparently, not among the 12 races the changeling send ambassadors to, which can narrow the list a bit. Another surprising feature is that they hunt Beasts down- since that according to Beast, the Begotten have some sort of an alliance with the Rampant against the Heroes- I assume the Dawn of Heroes and the Guilds were created in a latter time?

                                                Also, I'm not sure, but the way they see the MM may indicate that their Endowments don't work in Japan of that Era. Sure, I assume I read too much into the text, but if this is true it may have some interesting implications about the nature of their powers. The other groups also seems kinda interesting- the Ama San seem to be like a mariner, sea monster hunting version of the Union, and for some reason they make me think about epic fisherman who fights to fish a Japanese dragon out of the sea :P. I personally don't find the Gimu really attractive, but this is because samurais aren't really my shtick- but I think they present a very interesting mirror to the TF:V of the time and the place. The Bijin interest me because male escorts who hunt monsters is just awesome, and while I've made a similar group once I'm interesting in seen how those guys may end up. Finally, the Hototogisu- I think that among the different Japanese groups, they are the greatest power to reckon with. I wonder how tied they are to the tradition on the Onmyodo, since I've made a group of onmyoji hunters with a twist once and I wonder if I'll have to revise them...

                                                Anyway, I eagerly wait for the full Dark Era!
