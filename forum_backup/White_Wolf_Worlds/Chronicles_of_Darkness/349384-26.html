    Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
    I'd say they're closer to spirits than Goetia. The former can invade our realm, Possess and Claim more easily, while the latter usually can't Materialize on the rare occasions when they do invade. That being said, the Chaos Gods are definitely horrible and corrupting enough to count as Abyssals, whether they manifest in the Shadow or Astral.
    It is worth noting that it is not, in fact, easy for the Ruinous Powers to invade our realm. The Materium and Immaterium are seperate and only the strongest manifestations of the Warp can pass through and survive without extenuating circumstances. Things like the Webway, the Eye of Terror, and, of course, legions upon legions of mortal cultists are required for the servants of the Chaos Gods to make their presence felt in this plane of reality.

    The Chaos Gods themselves don't seem to be willing or able to venture into our realm at all, in fact, preferring instead to act through agents.

                                    I honestly think you can do many characters as different games, so with that in mind...

                                    Korra from Legend of Korra as an Ithaeur Blood Talon

                                    I mean she patrols the Spirit and Physical world and decides how separate they should be representing both. Has rage issues, Tries constantly to become balanced and Spiritually whole. Even though spirits are a bit hostile toward her they look to her to settle disputes and she obviously has an honorary spirit rank. It's discovered the balance between worlds was created by the choice of a spiritual ancestor. Blood Talon because most of her enemies are other benders and she's aggressive. And to top it all off, sometimes loses control in physical distress than if more destructive could be a form of Karuth.

                                    Mako- An Irraka Storm Lord for both the lightning and he tries to play tough like things don't bother him, but also he tries to think things through and questions things. He tries to be stealthy and likes to plan ahead.

                                    Bolin- A Cahalith Iron Master Much more relaxed than the others, but between being a mover star, his general enthusiasm, and his belief in others Cahalith makes the most sense. Iron Master because he spends more time with people than the others, and has a deeper connection to place than the other two when Kovira came to town.

                                    Asami- Wolfblood with the Sagrim Ur merit and A Wolf's Meat Tell. The first is obvious she loves machines and is good with her hands. The second because she is always wearing gloves or other layers and it seemed appropriate if she's bringing silver to the fight.
