        ... And time for a new set of "All Killer, No Filler" Slashers!

        The Apex
        Modi Bjornson is the type of person who has triumphed at everything he tried his hand at. Raised in an upper-middle class home in his native Iceland, he excelled in academics and sports, and showed a particular skill at power-lifting. Graduating from college with high marks, he traveled the world, voraciously seeking new opportunities, and trying his hand at many different careers. Over his travels, he saw the weaknesses of humanity all across the world, and developed a philosophy of personal fulfillment and accomplishment that allows it’s practitioner to reach the “Apex” of human potential. With the publication of his book “The Apex Principle”, he has created a small empire, with online classes, speaking engagements, and a line of dietary supplements. His success has made him famous (especially among those who decry the loss of “traditional” masculinity), wealthy, and has attracted a throng of loyal devotees to his philosophy.
        Modi also harbors the dark secret that he enjoys culling the weak from humanity. He uses his resources to find targets (typically drug addicts, homeless people, and the similarly under-privileged), and takes them to a remote wilderness area to hunt them down over the course of several days. If they survive, they will have proven their worth and would be rewarded with a large sum of cash. So far he has never had to pay.
        Modi is a handsome, heavily muscled man in his early forties. He has dark hair (stylishly cut), a full beard, and piercing blue eyes. He has distinctive tattoos with a dragon motif on his back and arms. He is well dressed in fashionable clothing that is cut to show off his impressive physique. He is a fanatic about fitness and diet, shunning any intoxicants, eating a carefully balanced diet, and is voluntarily celibate (at least until he can find an equally superior woman to be his mate).

        Undertaking: Puppeteer; Virtue: Edifying; Vice: Ambitious
        Aspiration: Raise up a superior human race
        Tell: Modi believes in culling the “weak” and inferior”; most of his victims come from marginalized and under-privileged communities, but he has occasionally hunted those he has deemed “unworthy” of their wealth and privilege.
        Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 3, Resolve 3
        Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 3, Stamina 4
        Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 3, Composure 3
        Skills: Academics 3 (Business), Athletics 3 (Weight Lifting), Brawl 3 (Overpower), Crafts 2, Computers 2, Drive 1, Empathy 2, Firearms 3 (Rifle), Intimidate 3, Investigation 3, Medicine 1(Nutrition), Persuasion 4 (Sales), Science 1, Socialize 2 (Social Media), Subterfuge 3, Survival 3, Weaponry 2
        Merits: Contacts 3 (Investors, Men’s Rights, Publishers), Cover-Up, Fame 1, Inspiring, Language (English, German, Japanese, Russian), Professional Training 3(Entrepreneur; Academics, Persuasion, Socialize), Resources 5, Safe Place 3 (Palatial Estate), Staff 2 (Devotees), Striking Looks 2 (Handsome), Style: Martial Arts 3, Style: Strength Performance 2
        Integrity: 0, Potency: 2, Willpower: 8
        Size: 5, Initiative: 6, Defense: 6
        Speed: 12
        Health: 9
        Weapons/Attacks
        Unarmed [+0B melee; 8 dice]
        Heavy Pistol [+2L ranged; -2 Initiative; 6 dice]
        Rifle [+4L ranged; -5 Initiative; 7 dice]
        Knife [+0L melee;-1 Initiative; 7 dice]

        Talent: Compelling Frailty: Falter
        Dread Powers: Hypnotic Gaze, Supernatural Merit (Biokinesis 2)

        Compelling: A Puppeteer can profile a victim much like a Virtuoso, but additionally benefits from the 8-again quality on any contested roll against a profiled target. They also impose a penalty equal to the scourge’s Intelligence on victims’ rolls to notice traps or react to ambushes set for them in their nest.

        Falter: Whenever a Puppeteer fails a Social roll, it becomes a dramatic failure.

        Hypnotic Gaze: The scourge’s gaze can charm and beguile. When meeting the target’s gaze, he can spend 1 Willpower and roll Presence + Persuasion contested by the target’s Composure. If successful, the scourge counts as having a perfect impression against the target for Social maneuvers until the end of the scene.

        Style: Strength Performance: Modi has specifically trained in lifting, pushing, and pulling enormous weights.
        • Strength Tricks (•): Modi combines trained strength with a practical knowledge of physics to perform impressive, if minor, feats of strength. He can rip phone books in half, bend rebar, and smash bricks with his bare hands. He benefits from a +2 bonus to Expression and Intimidation rolls where he can demonstrate his talents, and an additional +1 to other non-combat feats of Strength.
        • Lifting (••): Modi can perform incredible feats of raw strength. When attempting a feat of strength that requires a Strength + Stamina roll, he gains the Rote benefit — re-roll failed dice. This does not aid running, jumping, or combat rolls, but does aid in attempts to damage relatively immobile, inanimate objects. In that case he gains the Rote benefit on a combat dice pool no larger than his Strength + Stamina.


        Nest: Palatial Estate
        A large home sits on acres of carefully manicured lawns and topiary, and has all the trappings of luxury. The property is surrounded by high walls and fences, and is protected by a professional security service that responds quickly to intruders or any other unusual disturbances.
        Safe Place Features: Escape Hatch, Home Security, Privacy

        Privacy (Feature): The Safe Place is built with an eye towards discretion. The windows tint or drop a shade with the push of a button, the walls are sound-proofed, and even waste disposal is managed to prevent unwanted parties from picking through refuse looking for clues. In addition to penalties imposed by Home Security, anyone outside of the Safe Place that attempt to observe or investigate events or persons inside the Safe Place suffer an additional -2 penalty.


        Nest: Hunting Grounds
        The Hunting Grounds are a remote forest miles from anything resembling civilization. The area is filled with a variety of natural hazards and dangerous wildlife. During the winter months, these hazards are compounded by freezing temperatures and sudden storms that will kill anyone unprepared for the harsh conditions and can challenge even seasoned and well-equipped survivalists.
        Tilts: Blizzard (Winter), Extreme Cold (Winter), Maze, Stinging (Summer), Undergrowth
        Traps: Pit Trap

        Maze (Environmental): The terrain twists and turns without readily available landmarks, disorienting and confusing affected characters. Characters attempting to trace their steps or find a way out of the area must succeed at an Intelligence + Composure roll with a 2 dice penalty.

        Pit Trap (Trap): A deep pit lined with sharped stakes and concealed beneath a tarp and piles of leaves. trap comprised of large rocks and lumber hand suspended from the dense canopy of trees. Once the trap is triggered, the victims suffers a point of lethal damage from the fall and the spikes, or one point of aggravated damage on a dramatic failure. Escaping a pit trap requires a Strength + Athletics – 2 roll to climb or jump out.
        Trigger: False Floor. A successful Wits + Composure roll with a -2 penalty avoids the trap.

        Stinging (Environmental): Mild toxins in undergrowth or stinging insects can irritate the skin and eyes of victims, impeding their efforts. The stinging or itching isn’t enough to cause injury, but imposes a –1 dice “wound penalty” on all rolls as though the affected character were in the third-to-last box of Health.

        Undergrowth (Environmental): Dense undergrowth provides cover and entangles characters moving through it. Characters attempting to move through the undergrowth must succeed at a Strength + Athletics roll penalized by the
        undergrowth’s Durability rating (typically 1-2 for roots, shrubs, and other vegetation).​

                The Badge Collector
                Deidre “Dee Cee” Carter had a difficult upbringing in the inner city. Raised by a drug-addict mother with a father that spent most of his time in and out of prison, she had to grow up fast. After fatally shooting her mother's boyfriend when he tried to force himself on her, she ran away from home and did not look back. Falling in with a crew of small-time drug dealers, she quickly gained a reputation for being a cold-blooded enforcer. As the gang grew in size and power, they gained the attention of the authorities, who planned to shut them down for good. A police raid took a violent turned that killed her entire gang and burned their hideout to the ground. Though injured, Dee Cee escaped the massacre. With the loss of the only people she ever cared about, she swore bloody revenge on anyone wearing a badge. She isn’t stupid, preferring to attack by ambush, and she doesn’t care a single damn for civilians caught in the crossfire. The local police know they are being stalked by a cop-murdering serial killer, and task force is being assemble to hunt her down. She expects to die fighting, and wants to take as many cops as possible with her.
                Dee Cee is African-American woman in her mid twenties, but the haunted look in her dark eyes makes her seem older. She wears her hair in braids and dresses in dark, functional clothing. She is in exceptional physical condition, as she has few interests beyond exercise and teaching herself the skills necessary to aid her vendetta. She lives comfortably off the hidden stashes of cash left behind by her gang and occasionally accepts payment as a hit woman.


                Undertaking: Avenger; Virtue: Loyal; Vice: Focused
                Aspiration: Kill every mf’er wearing a badge.
                Tell: Dee Cee collects badges; and claims the badges of her victims as trophies.
                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 3, Resolve 3
                Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 5 Stamina 4
                Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 2, Composure 3
                Skills: Athletics 2, Brawl 3 (Boxing), Crafts 2, Drive 2, Firearms 3 (Pistols, Shotguns), Intimidation 3, Investigation 2, Larceny 3, Science 2, Streetwise 3 (Gangs), Stealth 3 (Ambush), Subterfuge 2, Weaponry 3
                Merits: Allies 3 (Gangs), Danger Sense, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Professional Training 3 (Criminal), Resources 2, Style: Firefight 1, Style: Street Fighting 3, Trained Observer 1
                Integrity: 0, Willpower: 6
                Size: 5, Initiative: 8, Armor: 1/3 (Kevlar Vest), Defense: 6
                Speed: 13
                Health: 9
                Weapons/Attacks
                Unarmed [+0B melee, 7 dice]
                Machete [+2L melee; -2 Initiative; 6 dice]
                Light Pistol [+1L ranged; 9 dice]
                Shotgun [+3L ranged; -4 Initiative; 9 dice, 9-again]

                Talent: Working the Room; Frailty: Nothing but the Mission

                Working the Room: Avengers might try to kill their victims one by one, but sooner or later they wind up with a roomful of people that require their attention. The rules for Defense vs. multiple opponents do not apply to Avengers. No matter how many times in a turn an Avenger is attacked, apply his full Defense to all incoming attacks.

                Nothing but the Mission: Most slashers are obsessive, but Avengers take it to a whole new level. The Avenger has the Obsession Condition in regards to pursuing their chosen targets. When faced with the choice between pursuing a target and taking some other course of action, the Storyteller’s must roll Resolve + Composure. If this roll succeeds, the Avenger can choose to let the target go (for now). If the roll fails, the Avenger remains focused on his mission and ignores the other situation.​

                    The Bakemono
                    Death has been stalking the streets of Tokyo for nearly five years, with no signs of stopping. The victims are varied; men and women, wealthy and poor, police and criminals. All have been found dead and decapitated. Only occasionally is the victim’s head recovered. The press and authorities have dubbed the killer “The Bakemono”, which roughly translates to “Monster”. The Bakemono has been confronted by authorities once, and was described as a man in dark clothing, with a sword and a demonic mask. During the altercation, the killer wounded several officers and despite being shot several times, the escaped by crashing through a window (several stories up) and vanished into the night. So far the Bakemono has claimed ten victims, with seemingly no connection between them, and it is only a matter of time before he strikes again.
                    Some of the urban legends that have grown around the Bakemono state that he was an emotionally disturbed youth from a wealthy home that was committed to an asylum who later escaped. Others paint him as a member of a doomsday cult ushering in the apocalypse. Still others claim he is a betrayed assassin returned from the dead to seek revenge upon those responsible. The truth remains unknown. A police task force has been assembled to track down the killer and put an end to his rampage, but have made little progress.
                    The Bakemono is presumably a Japanese man of average height and athletic physique. He dresses in dark clothing that allows for motion and physical activity, and wears a mask with a demonic motif. He carries a sword as his main killing tool, but has engaged in a firefight with several police offices and shows proficiency with firearms. He has been baffling to investigate, leaving little evidence of who he might be, and what his true motives are.


                    Undertaking: Mask; Virtue: Driven; Vice: Excessive
                    Aspiration: To bring death to those that have earned it.
                    Tell: The Bakemono decapitates each of his victims. In addition, unknown to the authorities, the Bakemono knows each of his victims personally, and has good reason (by Slasher standards) to see them all dead.
                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 4, Resolve 4
                    Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 5, Stamina 3
                    Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 2, Composure 3
                    Skills: Academics 2, Athletics 4 (Climbing), Brawl 4, Firearms 3 (Pistols), Intimidation 4, Larceny 3 (Intrusion), Occult 2, Stealth 5 (Escape), Streetwise 2, Survival 3, Weaponry 5 (Blades)
                    Merits: Anonymity 3, Atavism, Cover Tracks, Danger Sense, Defensive Combat (Weaponry), Fleet of Foot 2, Lucky Charm (Mask), Seizing the Edge, Style: Armed Defense 3, Style: Parkour 3, Weaponry Obsession (Sword)
                    Integrity: 0, Potency 3, Willpower: 10
                    Size: 5, Initiative: 8, Defense: 10
                    Speed: 16
                    Health: 8
                    Weapons/Attacks
                    Unarmed [+0B melee, 8 dice]
                    Light Pistol [+1L ranged; 9 dice]
                    Sword [+3L melee;-2 Initiative; 10 dice]
                    Talent: Killing Machine Frailty: Only the Kill
                    Dread Powers: Glitch, Shadow Attraction, Surprise Entrance

                    Killing Machine: Like a Brute, a Mask does not suffer wound penalties and passes rolls to remain conscious due to damage or pain. Additionally, the Mask does not need to sleep or eat, and only suffers a single point of damage from any attack regardless of the damage or number of successes. This applies to mundane and supernatural forms of attack; only environmental damage such as fire, electrocution, or falling bricks applies damage normally.

                    Only the Kill: A Mask has lost the ability to communicate. They are illiterate and are limited to simple gestures or grunts. To understand what’s been said to them, they must succeed at an Intelligence + Composure roll.

                    Surprise Entrance: No matter how secure its victims think they are, by spending 1 Willpower the scourge may suddenly appear in the scene. Any character witnessing this entrance must succeed on a reflexive Resolve + Composure roll or gain the Shaken Condition. If the characters have actively taken appropriate measures to keep the Slasher out (barring and locking the door, smashing all the mirrors they can find, etc.) it will take them one full turn to break through, giving the characters time to react. If not, it’s just there as a reflexive action and can take one more action before anyone can react.​

                        Blank
                        Every year, untold numbers of people disappear without a trace. And many of these disappearances can be attributed to a figure known as “Blank”. The first cases were documented in the late seventies, when three students (two male, one female) vanished from the campus of their north-eastern university over a long holiday weekend. The students were all popular and well-liked, with sterling reputations among their family and friends. Over the next few weeks, their ID’s and driver’s licenses began appear in the mail, all with the photo of the victim marked out with a dark pen. Despite extensive time and resources dedicated to the search, the students were never found. Similar abductions across the east coast and south of the United States persisted into the early nineties before stopping abruptly.
                        Recently, a well-known and popular internet personality (known online as “TravelKat94”) vanished in a similar fashion; her defaced driver’s license arriving in the mail days after her disappearance. Authorities believe that an imitator has adopted the signature taunt of Blank, but others believe that after thirty years, the original Blank has returned.
                        No one knows who Blank really is. The only element tying Blank’s victims together is that all of them were well-liked, “decent” people, and their abductions inflicted deep and lasting emotional wounds on everyone in their immediate social circles. The only surviving victim of an abduction speaks of a figure in a dark coat, wearing an eye-less black mask, and speaking in a voice that never rose above a whisper. While her testimony was considered suspect at the time due to the vagueness of her descriptions and the gaps in her memory, she is the only known person to have encountered Blank and lived. While Blank was in all likelihood was once a mortal criminal who delighted in pain they caused, decades of rumors and legend have forged them into something ….else.


                        Undertaking: Legend; Virtue: Patient; Vice: Cruel
                        Aspiration: To cause suffering through social networks by abducting and murdering beloved individuals.
                        Tell: Sending the victim’s defaced ID card to their family, friends, or local authorities.
                        Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 4, Resolve 5
                        Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 3, Stamina 4
                        Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 3, Composure 3
                        Skills: Brawl 4 (Restrain), Crafts 2, Drive 2, Firearms 2, Intimidation 2, Investigation 3 (Police Procedure), Larceny 3 (Breaking and Entering), Medicine 2, Occult 1 (Urban Legends), Science 2, Stealth 4 (Avoid Surveillance, Silence), Streetwise 3 (Body Disposal), Subterfuge 3, Weaponry 2
                        Merits: Anonymity 3, Choke Hold, Cover-Up, Cover Tracks, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Professional Training 4 (Criminal; Investigation, Larceny, Streetwise), Resources 3, Sleight of Hand, Untouchable
                        Integrity: 0, Potency 4, Willpower: 12
                        Size: 5, Initiative: 6, Defense: 7
                        Speed: 12
                        Health: 9
                        Weapons/Attacks
                        Unarmed [+0B melee, 8 dice]
                        Heavy Pistol [+2L ranged; -2 Initiative; 5 dice]
                        Sap [+0L melee; -1 Initiative; 6 dice; Stun]
                        Talent: Living Legend Frailty: Narrative Chains (Can only be harmed by the anonymous and forgotten)
                        Dread Powers: Blackout, Camera Shy, Reality Stutter

                        Living Legend: Once per scene, when someone fulfills one of the tenets of the Legend’s “rules,” the player can roll the slasher’s chosen Attribute + Occult. Successes on this roll may be applied on one of the following: restoring spent Willpower, healing damage (one success for a bashing wound, two for lethal, three for aggravated), or applying the bonuses to rolls in pursuit of their goal. After this has been activated, successes that haven’t been spent are discarded. They may not be banked to use in a following scene.

                        Narrative Chains: The Legend gains a unique and specific frailty enforced by the nature of the tales surrounding them. Additionally, characters investigating the scourge’s Tell gain an exceptional success on three successes rather than five.

                        Camera Shy: The creature only ever appear on camera as a blurred silhouette of their actual shape and mortals have a hard time remembering exact detail about it. Rolls to accurately describe or recall such events are made at a –3 modifier.

                        Reality Stutter: The creature’s presence sets reality stuttering and convulsing, allowing it to flicker from place to place. By reflexively spending 1 Willpower when it moves, the creature can translocate itself to any location it can see up to its Speed in meters away, seeming to observers to just spasm in and out of reality as it goes. Doing so adds +2 to its Defense for the turn.​

                            The Botanist
                            Genevieve Gallo grew up in a home on the outskirts of town, on a plot of land inherited from her immigrant grandparents. She was an only child, and witnessed her parent’s deteriorating relationship; their traditional values would not condone a divorce. From her father, she gained a lifelong love of growing plants, and spent much of her youth in the family greenhouse, and eventually left home to study botany. She was often lonely and made every attempt to find companionship, but ended up drifting in and out of entanglements that were emotionally (and often physically) abusive. With the death of her father she moved back home to care for her ailing mother. Her mother was a difficult woman, and her infirmity only made her behavior worse. After an intense argument, a lifetime of frustrations boiled over and she stabbed her mother to death with a spade. After mulching her mother’s corpse and using it to fertilize her plants, Genevieve experienced a great sense of calm. Since that day, she makes a point to keep a stock of her “special” mulch on hand. Her garden has never been greener.
                            Genevieve is a thin Mediterranean woman in her early forties, with dark hair and brown eyes. She is plain-faced and has a mousy demeanor. Her over-sized glasses and old-fashioned mode of dress makes her seem older than she is. Despite her slender frame, she knows how to leverage her strength and is capable of surprising feats of brute force. Over the years, she has become reclusive and is out of practice with many social queues and expectations. She rarely makes eye contact and often mumbles when she speaks. If discussing topics related to plants, botany or gardening, she becomes animated and speaks with confidence, authority, and humor.


                            Undertaking: Undesirable; Virtue: Patient; Vice: Violence
                            Aspiration: To make her garden grow.
                            Tell: Genevieve limits her depredations to trespassers and those she can lure to her property (typically gardening enthusiasts and day laborers). She mulches or composts the corpses and uses them to feed her plants.
                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 5, Resolve 4
                            Physical Attributes: Strength 2, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3
                            Social Attributes: Presence 2, Manipulation 3, Composure 3
                            Skills: Academics 2, Athletics 2 (Manual Labor), Crafts 3, Drive 1,Empathy 2, Firearms 2, Investigation 2 (Research), Medicine 1, Persuasion 2 (Everything’s Fine), Science 3 (Botany), Stealth 3 (Ambush), Subterfuge 2 (Lies), Survival 2, Weaponry 3 (Gardening Tools)
                            Merits: Area of Expertise (Botany), Atavism, Cover-Up, Face in the Crowd, Hands of a Killer, Holistic Awareness, Language (Italian), Professional Training 3 (Botanist; Investigation, Science, Survival), Safe Place 3 (Green House), Resources 2, Sympathetic
                            Integrity: 0, Willpower: 7
                            Size: 5, Initiative: 6, Defense: 7
                            Speed: 10
                            Health: 8
                            Weapons/Attacks
                            Unarmed [+0B melee, 8 dice]
                            Gardening Tools [+0L melee; +2 Initiative; 7 dice; Improvised]
                            Light Revolver [+1L ranged; 5 dice]
                            Shovel [+1L melee;-3 Initiative; 6 dice; Knockdown]

                            Talent: Natural Compass; Frailty: Rejection

                            Natural Compass: An Undesirable has a near-perfect sense of direction, and gains a +3 bonus to Perception, Athletics, Investigation, and Survival rolls when present in their nest.

                            Rejection: The Undesirable suffers a −4 penalty on Social rolls that do not involve slashers and other monsters.


                            Nest: The Greenhouse
                            The Greenhouse is built on a large plot of land, purposely overgrown to provide privacy and dissuade trespassers. The greenhouse itself is large and well-maintained, and nearly every planting box is teeming with life. Warm light beams from the windows during the day and the air inside is uncomfortably humid. Any variety of garden vegetables, medicinal herbs, and other interesting horticulture can be found by those that know what to look for. The Greenhouse has an adjoining shed filled with well-maintained tools, including a high-end mulcher. After all, if there is no body, there is no crime.
                            Safe Place Features: Arsenal, Concealed, Innocent Facade

Any attempts to Investigate or use abilities to detect criminal, supernatural or otherwise unusual activity suffer a penalty equal to dots purchased in the Merit.

                                The Champion
                                David Carpenter was fascinated by death at an early age, and his youth was marked by incidents of animal cruelty and violence against his peers. As he matured, he demonstrated a keen mind and natural athletic talent, but his anti social and violent tendencies always undermined his potential to live a normal life. By his mid twenties, he had committed several murders, and was looking forward to committing more. David see’s himself as a “grim reaper”, and will study his victims his days, if not weeks, before deciding to make a kill. He chooses his victims on whim and instinct, preferring those who might present a challenge. He is almost disappointed if they die easily, and truly relishes when they fight back.                                                 The Drifter
                                                Michael “Coyote” Nakai was drawn to the freedom of the open road at the age of sixteen, and has never looked back. Over the course of the following decades, he has crossed the country several times. And along the way he has left a trail of death. Coyote kills when the mood strikes. Sometimes, it’s in anger, sometimes it’s to steal valuables, other times it’s for no reason at all. There is no consistency to his methods, and he can be brutally creative when the urge to kill strikes. After a kill, he moves on to the next town; waiting several years before returning to that location (if he ever returns at all). Coyote never stays in one place for long, never more than a few months. He travels by land, using whatever conveyance is convenient, typically by bus or passenger train, but he has covered countless miles just by walking. He hates air travel and avoids it whenever possible. His erratic methods and wide-ranging movement patterns have kept the authorities from tying his crimes together.
                                                Coyote is a Native-American man in his late-forties; his weathered skin and long, graying hair make him seem older. He is of average height and of wiry build. He sets people at ease with an air of effortless cool and is also an entertaining storyteller and harmonica player. He dresses in sturdy clothing suited to life on the road, a well-traveled denim jacket covered in buttons, and a wide-brimmed leather hat. Everything he owns is carried in an old duffel bag, and keeps few things that cannot be replaced easily. He supports himself by working odd jobs, petty larceny, and robbing his victims. Coyote rarely thinks about friends or family anymore, and considers himself as a wolf among hares.
