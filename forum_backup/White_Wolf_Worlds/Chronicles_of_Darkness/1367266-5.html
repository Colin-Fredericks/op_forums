    In most cases, a Storyteller character doesn’t need to gain Conditions, since the Storyteller doesn’t earn Beats.                 I feel as though I would be remiss to not bring up an important thing about vampiric Willpower recovery:

                The Inspired Condition — the Condition specifically recommended as a go-to pick for "an exceptional success on a roll allows you to take a Condition" — gives you a point of Willpower in addition to reducing the number of successes required to gain an exceptional success on a roll it's resolved with.

                The Predatory Aura applies one of three Conditions — the expressions of the Bestial Triad — that reduces the number of successes required to gain an exceptional success on a roll against a target.

                The Predatory Aura costs Willpower to fight (if you're a mortal or a vampire with lower Blood Potency), but lashing out itself doesn't cost anything to do.

                (Less consistently: exceptional success on frenzy resistance still refunds your spent Willpower +1, and the roll to ride the wave does not suggest that it works any differently in that regard.)
