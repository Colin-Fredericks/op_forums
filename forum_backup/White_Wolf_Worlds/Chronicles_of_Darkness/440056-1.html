            Passive triggers are supposed to be rare and unavoidable. Your auspice moon is up. It doesn't matter if you're in a submarine underneath the ocean, the moon is up and you're going to death rage. Because you're very much unbalanced.

            Specific triggers are because you are mostly keeping it together. Something very specific has to happen in order to make you death rage, and providing no one plans on using it against you, it's something you can prepare for and avoid. You can stop hanging around those vampires for a while.

            Common is something you can avoid as well, but it's going to inconvenience you. There's no way to predict whether you'll witness a supernatural creature doing something unless you avoid all supernaturals.

            You should probably be putting your problems with the system in the errata thread, so they'll know what they should clean up. It sounds like having it overlap is fine, where you're triggered by specifics and commons at certain harmony, and all of them at the extremes. But it's supposed to be a personal thing and up to the ST to take advantage of it or not, no matter how uncommon or rare you think something is, the Storyteller is the final arbiter of that, and they could just as easily make Luna hang in the sky of the Shadow under the same auspice every night for a month.

                    I think the reason for mixing and matching triggers is that the passive ones can render the common and specific obsolete. If you go Moon Moon Moon then while the passive is active you'll rage whenever the moon is in the sky, which means that the more specific ones will never happen because they both require your moon to be in the sky, and you're already raging.
