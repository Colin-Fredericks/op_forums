    Should I kill PCs?

    01-04-2015, 04:20 PM
    My chronicle have a near-final fight with Bale Hounds. Character have almost all Health boxes filled with Aggravated wounds, Essence depleted, enemies still standing. And player still do not want to retreat from the fight.

    It is probably the suicide fight, PCs will probably not win. On general, in RPG, I make PCs very tired, used almost all resources, having extra scars or even lost a limb. But PCs, in my games, live. At least till player say he want his character to die.

    But the Werewolf is very violent game about warriors fighting impossible battles. Some of PCs should die, from time to time, I reason from the sound of writing in corebook. And this fight looks like it could be last for PCs.

    On the other hand, it's a bit pre-mature ending to chronicle - I still want PCs to explore a Wound I put as end game stuff, and go down in to Hell from it. There, as I planned, some character can sacrifice themsvles to seal the damn thing and maybe one or two PCs could return to normal world.

    I once saved the one PC on today's game session - he would be dead if I'm not "miscalculated somethings". But saving players every time they made half-stupid choice is also bad, in my POV. If they fight noble, but unwinnable combat, should not they lose?

    But on the third hand - each death should be meaningful, yes? So maybe PC will die at the hand of their enemies, but at least rest of the pack can fulfill the mission?

    I'm very torn on what to do in game...

                          My general view is that GMs ought to do all they can to keep up the Threat of Death (since this keeps the game tense), while pulling the trigger as little as they can possibly get away with (since that ends any dramatic or story arc that the character had, and ends all the investment in them). So I'm usually in your camp regarding PC death.

                          That said, if the players are knowingly going for a Glorious Last Stand, well then, I'd feel cheated if I'd opted for a heroic sacrifice of my character and then some Deus Ex Machina came along. It can actually be kind of liberating, when all involved know that survival is not an option, since then you can make the deaths as memorable as possible.

                          So my advice here is to be sure that the players are aware that this is going to lead to their (likely) deaths -- just ask them in OOC "you guys know you're probably all going to die now, right?" -- and if they still want to forge ahead, then fair enough.

                                  I'd have to agree with most people, particularly NeoTiamat. Combat has no tension if there's no chance it could end badly: of course, badly doesn't always mean fatal. But in this context, seems pretty obvious that it does. I'd be very clear with the players that the fight they're choosing is near-certainly going to kill them, and if they choose to keep going, then let it unfold (rolling openly if you're concerned they could get angry).

                                  Of course, there's a potential deeper problem. I don't know your group, so I can't comment on their specific case, but if a group is throwing themselves into a suicidal option, it might indicate that they're bored or otherwise not enjoying the game and want it to end, even if so with a TPK. In which case, the outcome really isn't important, but rather the underlying situation. Maybe your ST-ing isn't to a style they enjoy, or maybe there's just something about this game that isn't grabbing them. Like I said, I don't know the group, so I may be entirely wrong here. Just seems a potential aspect.

                                  As always in RPing, the key is trust and communication between players and ST.
