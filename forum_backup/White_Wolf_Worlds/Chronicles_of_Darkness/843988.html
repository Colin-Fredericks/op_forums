              I don't really see much difference between 1e Auspex and 2e Auspex, to be honest. The biggest difference is the first dot. In 1e it enhancing the vampire's mundane senses. In 2e, every vampire has partially enhanced senses as part of the template, and there's a one dot Merit anyone can take that further enhances them, pretty much replicating 1e Auspex 1. The new Auspex 1 works as a low-level extrasensory impression of the surroundings ("reading the room", so to speak). Every other level is pretty much the same power, just with cleaner execution.

              Level 2 Auspex, you scrutinize a character with your extrasensory perception and learn things about them like their mood, supernatural template, and if they're a diablerist. In 1e, this was done through a color code that you either had to memorize or keep a chart on hand. In 2e this is done by just directly asking questions out of character and having them answered. My 1e group always used the question and answer method because we didn't like the chart. If you liked the chart, you could just give colors as answers in 2e.

              Level 3 Auspex lets you touch an object and receive extrasensory information about it. In 1e, it was mostly left up to the ST how this actually worked. In 2e, you use questions so you know you're getting the information you're actually looking for. Again, my 1e group had been using the 2e method long before 2e came out. If you liked the ST fiat based visions of 1e, you don't really need any rules to incorporate them into 2e Auspex.

              Auspex 4 lets you communicate telepathically. Frankly, I don't see much difference between the 1e and 2e versions at all.

              Auspex 5 lets you do something like astral projection, but not actually astral, since that word has a different specific meaning within the context of CofD. In 1e it was an invisible projection of your body that went about in Twilight. In 2e, it's your senses that leave your body. There is functionally very little difference here, just a slightly different way of describing the idea of "your consciousness becomes separate from your body and goes around spying on folks on its own."

                                                          Personally, I would recommend staying away from the Celerity effect. One of the pretty awesome thing about 2e is the complete lack of overlap between different Disciplines. Even the swiss-knife-Protean abilities lack overlap with the abilities of anything else. Either way, with all due respect Charlaquin, I don't really feel that you are adding in anything worthy of a level 5 effect - at best, we're looking at a level 2 effect here at best.

                                                          If we're looking for a replacement for level 5 that's less about projecting your mind outside your body (especially when there are cruac scrying effects that do similar), I would do something that's similar to Lord of the Land and Oubliette, but with all lower level abilities at will across a large area. I would also include a wide scale use of the old devotion Talk of the Town. For those of you unfamiliar with this awesome devotion from Invite Only (awesome yet underused book), it lets you instantly zero in on any conversation OR thoughts that are about you. You can literally hear it anytime someone starts thinking about you. Even the surface thoughts of other vampires, without using a willpower. Ability to stretch your senses anywhere in your territory mixed with the ability to pick up anytime someone is plotting against you? That's sheer gold.
