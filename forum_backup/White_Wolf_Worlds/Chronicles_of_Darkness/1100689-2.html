    Some more from Rich:

    Originally posted by Rich
    To Ian’s point: we’re planning on a Dark Eras 2 Companion in order to allow continued Chapter Stretch Goals ala DE1. As for a Stretch Goal for an entire product, it’s been something I’m considering but adding something like that will a) take the place of some new Stretch Goals for adding new Eras, and b) require a velocity of pledging that we normally don’t see in order to justify the Stretch Goal amount required to cover the cost of creation of said book that we normally don’t see in non-Deluxe KSs.
    What we try and do with Stretch Goal projects is to add them to a KS so that the KS “extra” funds will pay for the creation of something we normally wouldn’t create because of the chance we won’t make enough on it to cover the cost of creating it. Using KSs to fund fiction books, for example. Or at least the SG amount should be a big boost to covering costs. But like I said, I am considering a couple of ideas, and also keeping a close eye on the individual DE1 chapters we’ve been publishing on DTRPG to see what the interest is there.

                    Originally posted by nofather View Post
                    If I recall correctly, they didn't plan on Dark Eras being such a success and that so many eras would be achieved through stretch goals. The Kickstarter was, originally, just for the Dark Eras book, but then that book exploded to an unfeasible 900 pages, and so had to be divided into two books, Dark Eras and the Dark Eras Companion. Some people were upset that they didn't get the Companion for free, instead getting a coupon to get it from DriveThruRPG at a discount. So Rich seems to be taking that into account this time.
                    We never actually intended to give away a physical copy of the DE Companion, anymore than we give away physical copies of other KS Companion books. It's pretty much a free PDF version, and then sometimes a coupon for a discounted PoD version. But, I was later than I should have been to stop adding Eras to the main book, and realized it was uncomfortably big and so some more of the Stretch Goal Eras were taken from DE itself, and put into the Companion near or after the end of the KS. That burned a few people, but most understood the necessity. This time around, we are able to learn from that and will have a pre-planned maximum number of Eras in the main book and will state clearly that we are overflowing into the Companion when that Era is up as a Stretch Goal. Hope that clarifies that part of what we're doing with DE2. Thanks!

                                                            We have new details people!

                                                            Originally posted by Rich, via Kickstarter
                                                            We're going to start with 6 Dark Eras that have the denizens of two game lines each in them, so all your favorite Chronicles of Darkness game lines will be represented.
                                                            From there, we'll be giving you the chance to vote on which Dark Eras make it into the book as Stretch Goals, and sometimes we're planning on switching things up, like presenting you with an Era but you vote on the game line(s) that goes with it. Or starting with us giving you the game line(s), but you vote on an Era. Things like that.
                                                            We have 6 slots for added Dark Eras and once we hit those, and essentially double the size of the book, we'll be going right into the creation of a Dark Eras 2 Companion book with room for a bunch more Dark Eras.
                                                            In an effort to avoid some of the congestion and roadblocks that slowed down the creation of the original Dark Eras book, we're starting this Kickstarter before we have anything written. Our three, count 'em three, developers, Monica Valentinelli, Matthew Dawkins, and Meghan Fitzgerald, have writers ready to start on the initial six Eras as soon as we finish the KS, and more lined up to take on any additional Eras we unlock.
                                                            That way, any added bits to any of the chapters don't have to be shoe-horned in, but can be planned for and organically fit into the writing that will happen after the KS dust settles. After the original Dark Eras KS, we really think this method will result in a much faster writing and art direction cycle.
                                                            Hope to see ya there!
                                                            Any guesses on the pairs and initial eras?

                                                            EDIT: So there are ten gamelines, but eleven if you include Deviant. Are we going to see an era with the Remade? Who's going to get the extra crossover? Beast maybe, since it is crossover friendly and missed the first Kickstarter and only made it in due to popular demand in its own Kickstarter? Or - could we see one of the Eras be a crossover with something like Innocents or Inferno?
