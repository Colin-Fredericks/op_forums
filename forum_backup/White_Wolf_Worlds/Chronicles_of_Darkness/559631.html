    On Ex-Privateers (Character Questions)

    06-18-2015, 03:01 PM
    So. Privateers. They're villains - but they range from unrepentant evildoers gladly selling out their comrades to the people who made them what they are to terrified people who, unlike the Changelings they live amongst, never actually escaped from their abusers. Still, unless I'm wrong, the response is pretty universally to destroy them; it's understandable, of course (the most sympathetic of Privateers is still an immense threat to a community of paranoid victims), if not always the best option if people were able to take more time to address the causes and find out more, but the point remains.

    Still. The treatment of active Privateers isn't the primary point here; an active Privateer is almost certainly an NPC (or if not the game has already diverged from the norms), and I don't have any characters who are one, so it's not particularly relevant to me right now. What I'm wondering about is someone who was a Privateer, but has since stopped being one - specifically, in the case of the character in question, she was Privateer who was trained up to be a specialist in dangerous retrieval, or removal, if the escapee was too tricky to bring back.

    She was, naturally enough, good at it, with the help of her gear (mechanically, my ST let me make a 5-dot token that did something like two Agg base, scaled off of Dex+Weaponry, and added Mantle; she then proceeded to max out all three, for a truly ridiculous amount of damage - on top of which she had another 5-dot token that made her almost immune to ranged attacks, forcing people to come at her on her own terms); she took well to her training, and had a Keeper dedicated to making sure that she was capable.

    Then she finally decided she couldn't do this anymore - she saw what happened to one of those less fortunate her Keeper had taken (she was treated surprisingly well, for a Changeling, and her frequent stays in the mortal world were pleasant too), and it wasn't pretty -, which she took, fleeing into the Hedge with the girl, who she'd rescued on her way out. Since that time, they've settled down as a complicated thing that's sealed with ties of both Wyrd and emotion, lovers, considered by the Wyrd to be relations, and sworn on three different things to never act against each other. Furthermore, given that she still has her tokens, her Contracts, and her skills, she's, despite being Winter, (though in fairness, her lover is Summer, and the oath they took incorporates some of their courts into each other) one of the best defenders the Freehold has had in a long, long, time, and is in fact completely dedicated to this now.

    But. Her past remains, despite that, and (regardless of the long and somewhat rambling set-up), the question is actually pretty simple; what would the likely reactions to finding out that, while she's definitely changed, and that can be proven by her actions and any magical means you care to specify, she used to be a Privateer? The usual 'kill them now'? Or would the reaction likely be more nuanced, especially as that was entirely before her Durance was over?

    Edit: It's possible this is better described as a Loyalist; point's the same, though - she's got a past with a capital 'P' and I'm curious what the most likely reactions to it catching up with her are.

          I'm inclined to agree with 2ptTakrill. I suspect there's just too much diversity in the personal reactions she'd get to be clear on a general, majority reaction. Aside from usually feeling pretty strongly about it.

          That it was part of her Durance may be a help for her. A huge number of the Lost did things they aren't proud of, or even hate themselves over, during their Durance. A non-trivial number within that group did those horrible things to fellow Changelings. There's a huge spectrum of reactions to her even within that cross-section. Some may be inclined toward understanding, even toward leniency or forgiveness. Some may feel Privateering is a whole other class of atrocity. Some may still feel if she was free enough to leave the Hedge, her actions were all on her; some will conclude her defense work "makes up" for this (at least enough), some very much won't. Some may understand on some level, but still want her out of their lives and likely out of the Freehold. Some may sympathize completely, but fear the suspicion that there are dirty secrets in their own pasts, and thus rail against her all the harder. Some may stick up for her, especially given her track record, and may even respect her all the more for turning from that life. Some may feel betrayed, even if fully understanding why she kept mum; they may stick up for her anyway, on principle.

          Yeah, it's just really hard to say. The only certainty is that things are liable to be... eventful for her, her love, and the rest of the Freehold. Many connections she's made may well have their contexts tweaked, snapped, or reforged.

                  Personally, I think that most changelings would react badly to a Privateer. I remember fiction where a 'ling forced to help create fetches were scorned and hated. Loyalists are warped by their Keepers come for changelings, but they're still abuse victims.

                  Privateers are basically the worst of the lot. They're changelings that escaped from a Keeper, but choose to keep selling people back. An escaped Loyalist eventually realizes he's being mentally messed with, that he's not doing the right thing. A Privateer willing and knowingly chose to engage in human slavery rings. That's not the kind of thing we forget or forgive, let alone in human society. We still punish and imprison involvement in that.

                  All changelings have been mindwarped by Keepers at one point, and they can sympathize with how former Loyalists can come to their senses and break off, wanting to start over. A Privateer has already broken away, and chooses to engage in slavery and abuse.
