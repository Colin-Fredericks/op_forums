                                            Hmm. Here's what I have so far for my Cold Guard salvage efforts. I'm still working on the fluff and refining the mechanics. Feedback is appreciated.
                                            The Cold Guard

                                            Background
                                            They depended on Nobles, but were "betrayed" (possibly they were merely left unprotected when the Princesses defending them fell). *They survived as best they could, fighting back as fiercely, when they noticed that the Darkness often fought against itself. And wasn't exactly picky about who it conferred power to.* [<- This part is optional] They reached the grim decision to use the Darkness against itself, making a vow not to let it turn them from their purpose. Through group effort, codified ritual, rigorous training, and grim determination, they've managed to utilize the powers of the Dark without being corrupted from their goals (up to a point).

                                            The Enemy
                                            They oppose the monsters of the Darkness on base principle, turning its powers against itself. The oppose other monsters both for their threat to humanity, and for their tendency to create Nests incidentally through their depredations. They oppose Princesses due to prior experience and philosophy - throughout history, the "Nobles" have either tried to kill them on sight, been self-absorbed tyrants, or committed hypocrisy by furthering the Darkness. The newer ones have argued for forsaking one's sins, rather than drawing strength from utilizing them, all while they try to make humanity dependent upon themselves once more.

                                            Hunters
                                            [The Cold Guard only takes those who've done something to gain Shadows or have proven the necessary resolve and willingness to fight the Darkness with itself. They offer purpose, if not redemption.] [Hunters are encouraged to form bonds, if not too close ones, with their fellows. They need all the emotional and moral support they can get.]

                                            Philosophy
                                            Fight fire with fire. Keep to your purpose, despite the costs. Don't hide from your inner darkness - use it.

                                            Endowment: Cold Resolve (• to •••••)
                                            Prerequisite: Status (Cold Guard) 1+
                                            Effect: The Guardsman has faced their sins and taken part of the Darkness into their own heart. Only through chilly resolve and determination, do they hold its corruption at bay. They can acquire a number of Penumbrae or Darkness-based Dread Powers equal to their dots in this Endowment plus their Resolve without affecting their Integrity or being treated as a creature of Darkness.
                                            Drawback: The corruption of the Darkness can't be held at bay forever, nor invoked lightly. Each Penumbrae or Dread Power acquired gives the Hunter a permanent dot of Shadows. Each use often gives the Guardsman a temporary point of Shadows, which fade at a rate of one per day (or once per Scene, if the Guardsman fulfills their Virtue that Scene). If their Shadows (permanent and temporary) exceeds their dots in this merit and Resolve, they either take 1 Lethal damage or suffer a Breaking Point each day, and each additional time they gain a dot of Shadows.

                                            Penumbrae

                                            Channel Spite
                                            Dice Pool: Resolve + Athletics - Defense
                                            Action: Instant
                                            Cost: 1 Willpower + gain 1 Temporary Shadow
                                            Effect: The Guardsman taps into their hunger for violence and revenge, directing a bolt of shadow at the monster. Treat this as a ranged attack with a Damage Rating equal to the number of damaged Health boxes the Guardsman has.

                                            Devouring Dark
                                            Dice Pool: Composure + Occult
                                            Action: Instant
                                            Cost: 1 Willpower + gain 1 Temporary Shadow
                                            Effect: The Darkness consumes everything, even itself. The Hunter turns this hungry darkness upon a supernatural source, draining it of power. This triggers a Clash of Wills against the supernatural effect with the Hunter using the dice pool above - if they win, the supernatural effect is instantly cancelled.

                                            Veil of Corruption
                                            Dice Pool: Stamina + Subterfuge - number of targets
                                            Action: Extended, 10 minutes per roll
                                            Cost: 1 Willpower + gain 1 Temporary Shadow
                                            Effect: The Guardsman chants a litany of hatred, wrapping themselves and others in a shroud of Darkness. This veil will disguise the recipients as creatures of evil and Darkness, protecting them from the Curses of Nests, passive aggression from the less intelligent Lurkers, and even slight protection from the deadly effects of the Dark World. The effect lasts for a number of Scenes or Hours equal to the target number the Hunter sets when beginning the ritual.
                                            Drawback: The targets count as Creatures of Darkness for supernatural effects, and acquire their Bane of Jade as well, for the duration of the effect.

                                            Cloak of Shadows
                                            Dice Pool: Resolve + Occult
                                            Action: Instant
                                            Cost: 1 Willpower + gain 1 Temporary Shadow
                                            Effect: By accepting the Darkness in their heart, the Hunter can hide in it from hostile supernatural powers. For the rest of the Scene, they may treat their Shadows as a Supernatural Tolerance trait for the purposes of resisting effects.
                                            Drawback: The Hunter counts as a creature of Darkness for supernatural effects, and acquire their Bane of Jade as well, for the duration of the effect.

                                            Bleak Oubliette
                                            Dice Pool: Manipulation + Persuasion
                                            Action: Extended, 1 roll per hour
                                            Cost: 1 Willpower per roll + gain 1 Temporary Shadow per use
                                            Effect: Through ritual invocation of a Nest’s sins while maintaining their own detachment, the Hunter constructs a honeyed prison out of the tainted place. After achieving a number of successes equal to the Nest’s Corruption plus its Size, the entire place is affected - it begins attracting all creatures of Darkness within a number of miles equal to its Size, and once they arrive, they must succeed at a Resolve + Composure - Corruption roll to leave its confines. The effect lasts for a number of days equal to the Nest’s Hold track, though if a blood sacrifice (Lethal damage equal to the Hold track) is used, it lasts for 1 year per Hold box instead. [Or tie it directly to clearing the Hold Track instead?]
                                            Note: Attempts to purify the Nest directly inhibit this power’s functioning. Each Hold box that is checked off lowers the remaining duration by 1 interval.

                                            Feast of Sins
                                            Dice Pool: Wits + Socialize - Resolve (optional)
                                            Action: Instant or Scene
                                            Cost: 1 Willpower
                                            Effect: By engaging in ceremonial confession and judgement, the Hunter is able to transfer Shadows from themselves and others. Temporary Shadows can be exchanged between willing targets, up to the number of successes rolled. If used offensively, this power is resisted by Resolve, and instead allows the Hunter to temporarily remove up to successes dots of Shadows (minimum 1) from creatures of Darkness, gaining them as temporary Shadows. The dots of Shadows return to the creature of Darkness at a rate of one per Scene (regardless of whether the Hunter has shed them or not).
                                            Special: A Scene-long ritual version also exists, that lets the Guardsman permanently move dots of Shadows from willing targets, but the recipient gains those dots as permanent Shadows as well.

                                            Ebon Fury
                                            Action: Reflexive
                                            Cost: 1 Willpower + gain 1 Temporary Shadow
                                            Effect: By drowning themselves in their own hatred and anger, the Hunter is able to bolster themselves with the Darkness. For each dot of Shadows they possess, they can add a plus one bonus to any physical attribute. This can even exceed human limits, by an amount equal to their dots in Cold Resolve (so, up to 6 at one dot, and up to 10 at five dots). They can maintain this state for a number of turns equal to their Composure.

                                            Vile Curse
                                            Dice Pool: Presence + Intimidation vs Resolve + Potency
                                            Action: Instant and Contested
                                            Cost: 1 Willpower + gain 1 Temporary Shadow
                                            Effect: By spewing a litany of invective and malice, the Guardsman cripples their target with an infection born of the Darkness inside them. For each dot of Shadows they possess, they can inflict one curse (as per a Nest) upon a target, which fades at the end of the Scene.
                                            Special: If they destroy the Foci of a Nest prior to using this ability (taking 1 Instant action), the power affects everyone in the same area as the Size of the Nest. In this case, each potential target that rolls less successes than the Hunter is affected.
                                            Special: By willingly sacrificing a portion of themselves, the Guardsman can even turn the curses of the Darkness upon its own minions. The Hunter may take 1 Lethal damage and make a Breaking Point roll (as if acting on their Vice), and in exchange the curse will even affect creatures of Darkness.

                                            Feed on Madness
                                            Dice Pool: Intelligence + Empathy - Shadows
                                            Roll: Reflexive
                                            Cost: gain 1 Temporary Shadow
                                            Effect: As the Darkness grows stronger at the decay of sanity, so too can the Hunter syphon energy from madness. Whenever a Breaking Point (or equivalent, at the Storyteller's option) is inflicted, the Hunter gains either 1 point of Willpower (as if satisfying their Vice), or downgrades 1 box of Lethal damage to Bashing. If Integrity (or equivalent) was lost this way, they may instead regain 1 point of Willpower (without limitation per Scene), or completely heal 1 box of Lethal damage.

Each Penumbrae or Dread Power acquired gives the Hunter a permanent dot of Shadows. Each use often gives the Guardsman a temporary point of Shadows, which fade at a rate of one per day (or once per Scene, if the Guardsman fulfills their Virtue that Scene). If their Shadows (permanent and temporary) exceeds their dots in this merit and Resolve, they either take 1 Lethal damage or suffer a Breaking Point each day, and each additional time they gain a dot of Shadows.
                                                            This is a cool idea, and the powers are nicely thematic too. But that drawback ... if I read that correctly, once Shadows > Resolve + Endowment dots, it never goes back down below the limit. The hunter gains one temporary Shadows dot each day for being over the limit, then loses that dot from the natural fading, leaving him where he was - and he takes damage or an Integrity hit as well. Only fulfilling Virtue gets him partly out of the hole. Is that intentional?

                                                            If so, abilities like Channel Spite and Ebon Fury are a mug's game. You have a strict limit on the number of times you can use Penumbrae before you lock yourself into the damage-or-breaking-point loop, so each use had better give a major advantage. A scene-long buff or debuff may be worth what you're paying; a single ranged attack, or a few turns of boosted strength, aren't. A Penumbra should be designed on the assumption that it'll see use maybe twice in a session.

                                                            An alternative, if that's not what you had in mind, is that going over the limit just imposes the Darkened Condition, with all that that implies. It's a lot harder to get out of being Darkened, but the Condition doesn't kill you by inches as this drawback would.
