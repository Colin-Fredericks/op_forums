                                              08-02-2017, 06:15 PM
                                              I'll try to arrange some more direct spoilers in the future, but I thought I'd note that this book's design process came up in a CtL thread:

                                              Originally posted by Rose Bailey View Post
                                              Danielle and I sat down at Gen Con last year and had a long talk about Half-Damned. We'd looked at dhampir in Wicked Dead, and we loved the conflicts they set up for vampires. But when you're looking at player characters (or even just a part of the mythos that we mention more than once), being a literal curse seemed too one-note. And Danielle's insight was that if you have dhampir in the first place, and you have Kindred trying to be parents (or abandoning their kids) when the Requiem is usually one long solo... well, you've got plenty of conflict right there.

                                              So you'll see in Half-Damned that although there are definitely some unique supernatural angles, and while the conflicts from Wicked Dead are still there, the Vampire team looked at them much more from the perspective of "okay, what would actually happen if occasionally a vampire becomes a mom? what are all the ways that could go wrong? and most importantly: why would they do it anyway?"

                                              Cavaliers of Mars Creator
                                              Retired CofD Lead

                                                      Good. I never thought Wicked Dead dhampir were useful outside of an antagonist and they didn't really need a curse to serve that role, although it certainly made them more generally useful as antagonists. If someone is playing a vampire that has a kid then I think they deserve full support in an attempt to be a parent because there is so much that can go wrong.                                                       Of all things that could be spoiled, I want to know the role of a dhampir. Normally, I prefer mechanics and feel "meh" over fluff spoilers, but I think the aspect of that interests me most of all the subject matter of the book is how a vampire deals with having a child and not old life leftovers either. It has to be shocking.
