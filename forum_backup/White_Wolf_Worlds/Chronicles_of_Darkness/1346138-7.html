                                                    I really like how Utterances are learned. The fact that it occurs around other activity is brilliant. A cool dice roll for inspiration followed by an adventure then another dice roll and more adventure. Very very cool.                                                     I also like Keywords. I know they existed before but they are such a good way to organize rules.
