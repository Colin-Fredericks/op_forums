                                    I see Glamour as the reality-supporting Essence of certain types of possible ways the world could be if it were constructed differently, i.e. in a more Arcadian manner. It does not exist in the Fallen World in our timeline, but Changelings' property of being pulled out of the boundaries of our Time/Fate give them access to the possible-but-not supernatural structure/energy of the events that happen in our world. In other words, they're drawing Essence from disparate timelines, except that in those places where Fate is Wyrd, time doesn't come in lines, and its natural energy is free to flow according to Glamour-moving events in our world. From our world's point of view, though, things that happen are completely separate from things that could have been but aren't, so the causal flow is one-way: Our world affects otherworldly Glamour, but it doesn't provide feedback to our Fate the way the Shadow does until the Fae intrude in a more deliberate and local manner. Therefore, notwithstanding Legacies or Archmastery, even if a (post-Fall?) Mage could manipulate and even create Glamour, I don't think they could ever perceive the flow that Changelings normally harvest from, and they learn nothing of its nature from Awakening to the Watchtower-centered aspect of Arcadia that shines on our timeline.

                                    Except when it doesn't work like that, and it shouldn't, but maybe it could, but probably not, but what does "probably not" even mean?

                                                Originally posted by ErikModi View Post
                                                Vitae is quite simply the life force of human beings, concentrated by the vampire's own undead body into a more potent form. Think of it like "life extract." A vampire doesn't need the red corpuscles and platelets and plasma. They just need the force of life. The mystical energy that gives Vitae (and human blood, for that matter) its unique characteristics is the same energy that becomes Essence when it fuels the Shadow, but in this case is filtered through the biological processes of a living organism and that strange magic of "being alive," creating this mystical, distilled extract of life. When vampires consume blood and turn it into Vitae, they filter and modify it yet again, creating a new energetic substance.
                                                Though, Kindred are able to transmute Vitae into Essence (via the second-tier ability of Blood Tenebrous) as well as transmute Essence into Vitae (directly from a ghost's anchors (via the Ghost Eater merit in Book of the Dead) or indirectly through ectoplasm (via the second-tier ability of Essentiaphagia)), as well as derive extra Vitae (5:2 ratio) from Uratha blood (which ostensibly has a greater Essence-to-volume ratio than mortal blood) & experience hallucinogenic "trips" from Mage or Changling blood, and gain additional abilities from consuming certain Essence-based materials (e.g. consuming another Kindred's soul via Diablerie or consuming a ghost via the fifth-tier ability of Essentiaphagia) - all of which would seem to imply some substantial link between Vitae and Essence.

                                                Speaking of which, how many (if any) of the feeding-from-other-template effects will be carried over to B&S?
                                                The Mortal
                                                Feeding from a mortal is pretty much a given considering this is how vampires survive.

                                                Effects: In most cases mortals do not resist the ecstasy of the bite. Therefore they go into a state of shock feeling bliss. Depending on the level of success for the feeding vampire, a mortal may have no memory of what happened.

                                                The Werewolf
                                                Effects: The Werewolf's experience is identical to that of a mundane mortal.

                                                Result: The Vampire learns that the ferocious blood of Werewolves is over two times as nourishing but distills some of their aggression into the imbibing Vampire leading to a greater risk of frenzying for a number of nights equal to health boxes 'drained' from the Werewolf.

                                                The Vampire
                                                Effects: Kindred do not succumb to the kiss since they know what is going on. They can fight back if they so inclined. Feeding until the subject dies is called diablerie.

                                                The Mage
                                                Effects: Mages are just as tasty and filling as any other mortal. They also react the same way to being nommed. Although they are not all that high on the menu since their blood is rumored to be quite hallucinogenic to kindred. Whether they see the reality only known to mages or if it truly is a hallucination is not known, but most vampires are not that eager to find out. Those that do risk suffer a derangement per night for each vitae drained.

                                                The Changeling
                                                Effects: Changlings are the same as mortals when it comes to experiencing the kiss. However, as a vampire drinks from a Changling they begin to become affected by the Changling's particular influence - Seeming, Kith, Wyrd, Glamour, and Clarify all play a roll in the 'hallucinogenic trip' the vampire experiences but the system is the same.

                                                For the rest of the night the vampire is at a -1 penalty to Perception for each point of Changling blood in their system and that day the vampire will actually dream, vividly. The next night the penalty is no more as the effect is 'slept off' during the day-dreams.

                                                The Changling's blood tastes very good and contains strong emotions which the vampire might very well enjoy, however the delusions become stronger in intensity as the feeding continues which will likely cause the vampire to break off before killing the victim. This can be expressed as a self control roll if they wish to continue to drink from the Changling for more traits worth than the vampire has points in Resolve. This roll is also penalized by the modifier for having the glamoured blood in their system.

                                                The Promethean
                                                Effects: Now the Prometheans are immune to the overwhelming nature of the kiss. With the fact that the life blood, Azoth, is only half as nourishing as mortal blood AND does not taste very good. Most vampires wouldn't even bother viewing them as a "Grade D" but edible food source.

                                                The Sin-Eater
                                                Effects: There doesn't seem to be any difference between a Mortal and a Sin-Eater, as far as their blood is concerned. However, many of the Caul Manifestations have a dramatic effect on the body of the Sin-Eater, and thus could render his blood unpalatable. While not as low as Prometheans on the menu, they're probably almost as risky a food source as Mages.
