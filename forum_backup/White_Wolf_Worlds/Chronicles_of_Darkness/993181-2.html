                            Here are a new set, hope you like em'.
                            Little Glass Weavers
                            Little Glass Weavers are small, common spirits that gather around electronic devices. They are largely unobtrusive, and are mostly concerned with keeping electronics working properly. The spirits take the form of spiders, seemingly sculpted from glass and copper and roughly the size of a house cat.
                            Rank 1[Spirit]
                            Power 2, Finesse 4, Resistance 2, Willpower 6, Essence 10
                            Corpus 4, Size 2, Initiative 6, Defense 4, Speed 12
                            Influence: Technology 1
                            Ban: Cannot use Influence or Numina on non-electronic devices; Bane: Wood
                            Manifestations: Fetter, Twilight Form
                            Numina: Innocuous, Left Handed Spanner, Sign
                            Throttlewind
                            “Throttlewind” is an air spirit that gathers it essence when a living creature asphyxiates. It lurks around those who suffer from respiratory ailments like asthma or emphysema, but has been known to urge individuals to strangle or smother others (or themselves if no other targets are available). The spirit appears as a sinister looking cloud of dingy yellow vapor.
                            Rank 2[Spirit]
                            Power 6, Finesse 4, Resistance 4, Willpower 8, Essence 15
                            Corpus 8, Size 4, Initiative 8, Defense 4, Speed 14
                            Influence: Air 2
                            Ban: Cannot use Influence or Numina on creatures that do not breathe air; Bane: A weapon made of Tin (a metal associated with Air)
                            Manifestations: Discorporate, Fetter, Possess, Twilight Form
                            Numina: Drain, Host Jump, Regenerate
                            The Wendigo
                            The Wendigo is an old spirit, associated with cannibalism, famine, and the icy depths of winter. The entity is an insatiable flesh- eater, claiming its victims so it may better prey upon the living, then abandoning them once they have outlived their usefulness. The Wendigo appears as a towering human corpse; emaciated from hunger and frostbitten. Its lips have been torn off, revealing its long yellow teeth. A dull glow emanates from its eyes and its nails are long and sharp.
                            Rank 4[Spirit]
                            Power 12, Finesse 10, Resistance 10, Willpower 10, Essence 25
                            Corpus 17, Size 7, Initiative 20, Defense 10, Speed 29
                            Influence: Cold 3, Hunger 4
                            Ban: Can only manifest on cold (below freezing) winter nights; Bane: Boiling Fat
                            Manifestations: Claim, Fetter, Gauntlet Breach, Posses, Twilight Form
                            Numina: Awe, Blast (Icy Wind), Host Jump, Regenerate, Speed

                                    Re: Spirit Weapons and Armor
                                    I have a couple modified Numina I use for this. Please note that spirits are already frightfully powerful when compared to most denizens of the CofD, so these Numina I mostly often employ to help a rank 1 or 2 spirits punch a little harder and survive a little more without moving them up a full rank.

                                    Armored: A spirit with this Numina is difficult to injure, and are often manifest impressively armored forms. The Spirit spends 1 Essence point to gain Armor equalto its rank untilhe end of scene. This armor does not apply against the Spirits Bane.

                                    Nightmare Weaponry: The entity spends 1 Essence point to manifest claws, fangs, horns, suckers, or other appropriate natural weaponry. The modification acts as a 1L weapon.
                                    (This is adjusted from the power of the same name from Beast the Primordial. Pg.236)

                                    Re: Spirit Doors
                                    I debated this in my games, but ultimately decided to use Resistance. While trying to negotiate against a entity with a potential to start the interaction at +10 Doors can seem daunting, It stresses that entities as single-minded as spirits would be difficult to sway from their aims, and makes it more important for the players to offer Gathra and to actually negotiate with the entity.

                                    Re: Coyote
                                    I would consider Coyote to be in the Rank 6+ range, and would most likely be represented by his spirit brood or by lesser avatars of himself.

                                    Below is an example. I went with a aspect that is a more benevolent interpretation (this could easily be shifted to reflect some of the more sinister depictions of Coyote)
                                    Gold Eyes Laughing
                                    “Gold Eyes Laughing” is a powerful trickster spirit, who, if asked, claims to be the mythological Coyote himself. While this is likely a lie, few can deny the staggering might the entity possesses. Gold Eyes Laughing reflects the aspect of the trickster that is an agent of change, upsetting routines and breaking patterns. He is benevolent enough that the outcome of his meddling is usually positive, but even he cannot always predict how things will turn out. He is also renowned for his sense of humor, and is filled with a millennia worth of jokes, riddles, and tall tales. Gold Eyes can take many forms, but prefers that of a common western coyote, and anthropomorphic coyote-man, or a handsome Native American man with a mustache and twinkling gold eyes.
                                    Rank 5[Spirit]
                                    Power 13, Finesse 15, Resistance 12, Willpower 10, Essence 50
                                    Corpus 17, Size 5, Initiative 27, Defense 12, Speed 33
                                    Influence: Deception 4, Humor 4
                                    Ban: Gold Eyes Laughing cannot resist an opportunity for (typically) benign mischief; Bane: A stone headed spear; adorned in buffalo hide and eagle feather and stained with rattle snake venom.
                                    Manifestations: Discorporate, Gauntlet Breach, Image, Materialize, Reaching, Twilight Form
                                    Numina: Aggressive Meme, Essence Thief, Hallucination, Innocuous, Mortal Mask, Perfect Lie*, Regenerate, Speed

                                    Numina – Perfect Lie: When this Numen is used successfully, its victim will believe anything it says, no matter how unlikely, as long as it isn’t utter fantasy or contradicted by anything the target knows to be true. The Numen costs one Essence to activate and is contested by Composure + Supernatural Tolerance. If the entity succeeds, the victim believes the lie for the scene.

                                            Re: Claimed

                                            With Claimed, I run a fairly straight read of the Claimed Condition (distribute power, finesse and resistance to their respective categories, full access to Influence, etc). For Supernatural Tolerance I use the Rank and for determining Dread Powers I give a number of powers equal to the Rank+1 (if using a system where the powers are ranked by dots,give 1+ (Rank x2)). For using its powers, the Claimed uses Essence (or Willpower it it wishes).

                                            Re: Bans/Banes

                                            These are probably the hardest part of any spirit. My general rule is the same as the book advises - the more powerful the entity, the more obscure/rarefied/situational the bane or ban. I try to come up with things that speak to the nature of the entity. I like banes that are contrary to the nature of the entity (such as a hatred spirit harmed by symbols of love or a water spirit harmed by fire). For Bans, I use them to define the personality of the entity. I like using the Must/Must Not schema, but I also get alot of use from Bans that restrict where, how, or upon who the entity can use its powers.

                                                        Here are a pair of new Ghosts. As always, feedback is welcome!

                                                        The Drowned Woman
                                                        She remembers drowning. It happened so long ago that she has forgotten her name or exactly how she ended up on that ship. But she remembers the storm; months too early in the season and with rain like blades and waves like the fists of an angry god. She remembers the crack of the vessel being torn in half and the cold of the water filling her lungs. She haunted that stretch of coast for decades –tied to a locket given to her by someone she loved (perhaps a husband or fiancé). But eventually that was worn away by time and tide as well. In life she was a willful woman, and she has continued on in the underworld, becoming a traveler and guide well versed in the many rivers, lakes, and seas that exist within the underworlds winding caverns.
                                                        The Drowned Woman appears as a slender woman with bright red hair that stands out against her deathly pale skin and has eyes the color of seawater. Her age is indeterminate, seeming middle aged or youthful by turns. She is dressed in a simple white gown and is perpetually drenched in water as if she just emerged from the waves. She has a lovely voice and sings sea shanties with gusto and skill.

                                                        Rank 3[Geist]
                                                        Power 8 Finesse 8, Resistance 6, Willpower 10, Essence 20
                                                        Corpus 11, Size 5, Initiative 14, Defense 8, Speed 21
                                                        Virtue Kind, Vice Headstrong
                                                        Aspiration: To help those that travel by waterway
                                                        Remembrance: Expression
                                                        Innate Key: Key of Deep Waters
                                                        Ban: Cannot board a vessel without offering payment to the Captain Bane: Tokens of Romantic Affection
                                                        Manifestation: Discorporate, Image, Materialize, Twilight Form
                                                        Numina: Banshee Wail*, Emotional Aura (Loss), Pathfinder, Seek, Telekinesis
                                                        Numina – Banshee Wail: The entity creates an earsplitting scream that deafens anyone nearby. Anyone within 20 yards of the entity must succeed on a Resolve + Stamina roll – the number of successes gained or suffer the Stunned and Deafened Tilt. The Numen costs one Essence to activate.

                                                        The Lost Ronin
                                                        The Lost Ronin lived during the waning days of Tokugawa era Japan, and was a Ronin Samurai known for his love of drinking and gambling, and for his entitled attitude towards the peasantry (or anyone else he considered beneath him). After a particularly ugly incident at a gambling hall, he was ambushed by a group of assailants while too drunk to defend himself, as was murdered. His corpse was buried deep in a field and the murderers never spoke of the incident again. His angry spirit lingers on, enraged that he was slain by peasants while drunk, and denied a death befitting a Samurai. Quenching his thirst in the Rivers of Meido have made the Ronin strong, and he longs for re-entry to the world of the living, for only there can he earn a true, worthy, death.
                                                        The Lost Ronin has forgotten many details about his mortal life and his appearance has taken on an indistinct quality – a straw hat that obscures his face and a tattered dark blue kimono are the most striking elements, all other details are a water-color blur.

                                                        Rank 3[Geist]
                                                        Power 8 Finesse 8, Resistance 8, Willpower 10, Essence 20
                                                        Corpus 13, Size 5, Initiative 16, Defense 8, Speed 21

                                                        Aspiration: to be put to rest in a manner befitting a samurai

                                                        Innate Key: Key of Blood

The modification acts as a +1L weapon. By spending an additional point of essence, this weapon gains the “9-Again” quality.
