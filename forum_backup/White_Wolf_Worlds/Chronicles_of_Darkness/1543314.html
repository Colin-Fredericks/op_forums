    Otodo Conspiracy 2e update [Homebrew]

    10-07-2024, 10:13 PM

    I wrote an update version of the Otodo Endowment, looking for feedback. They all use Integrity as dice pool to roll.

    As well if you have Lore or system update to make them fit more an contemporary feel.

    My head canon was after WWII the Otodo broke off out of the Island of japan traversing the world finding family members and other Conspiracy members in other demon and ogres like entities from different cultures like Djin, Jolten and others

    Thank you

    Seitokuken Hunter the Vigil 2e

    Hannya

    Your character intuits the flow of souls and spirits. She sees immaterial spirits and demons as if they were materialized. The Otodo demonic heritage giving his sight of the ephemeral in Twilight seeing demons and spirits.
    The hunter can also peer into the soul of a Target, mortal or otherwise seeing the true form of their soul.

    Dice Pool: None or Integrity + Seitokuken vs. Resolve/Resistance + DreadPower or Clash of Wills*

    Cost: none

    Action: Instant

    Duration: One scene

    The character can view the twilight and the ephemeral being that cross into twilight naturally.
    Additionally the Otodo can focus their sight and perceive the soul and aura by spending a willpower.

    Roll Results

    Whenever a character she can perceive suffers a breaking point, your character sees a flash across his aura. When she perceives a character without Integrity (usually meaning a supernatural creature) for the first time

    Success: The Otodo sees the integrity of the target or when she perceives a character without Integrity knows the character is not natural and pierces any illusion.

    Additionally the player may ask any number of yes/no questions about the subject’s aura over the course of the scene equal to the number of success over their resolve
    Exceptional Success: As with a success, but the hunter can identify details of the monster’s nature, letting her know it if she has come across it before.

    Failure: The target reveal nothing to her

    Dramatic Failure: The hunter doesn’t just miss her mark, she looses the sight of twilight and ephemeral being for the rest of the scene

    Drawback: The Flames of hell burn in the eyes of the Otodo giving him a tell of his demonic lineage. Their eyes glow Yellow or Red, which can attract unwanted attention and add -2 to stealth rolls.

    *If the monster has a way of hiding his nature or appearance supernaturally, both players must make a roll. The Otodo must achieve more successes than the monster to pierce the illusion.

    Jabaku

    This gift enables the Otodo to control the weak of will, as well as demons and Spirits he knows the name of.

    Dice Pool: Integrity vs. Resolve/Resistance
    Cost: 1 Willpower

    Action: Instant

    It requires that the two characters perceive one another and the Otodo must make a command.

    It can be used on any demon or spirit the Otodo can see or knows by name.
    It works on any human characters the Otodo can see.

    Roll Results

    Success: Command with no more than two steps in it: “Go to the church and bring me back the priest” or “attack him, but do not kill him.” The target acts immediately to enact the command given, though it chooses its route or how to go about the action. If the hunter wants to give it more actions to take, she must use this Endowment again. The command lasts no longer than a scene
    Exceptional Success: The victim suffers the Leverage Condition as the imprint of the Otodo’s will unto his target.

    The hunter may give an additional command to the demon or add an additional step to a complex command.

    Failure: The victims resist the Otodo and ignores her commands.

    Dramatic Failure: The target listens to the order and carries out the exact opposite action, wreaking chaos and havoc.

    *If the command violates the victim’s Aspirations or would cause harm to himself, he may spend a point of Willpower to ignore the command. Nothing stops the Otodo from attempting the command again.

    ** Humans with Thrall’s condition cannot resit Jabaku

    Kaibutsu

    Your character can let loose her demonic heritage, and let it shape her form. She grows in size, and becomes an utter monstrosity with blue or red skin, massive muscles, fangs, and horns.

    Cost: 1 Willpower

    Dice Pool: None

    Action: Instant

    Duration: One scene

    Divide out your character’s Seitokuken dots to her Physical Attributes, however you wish.

    This can take her above five dots, but no higher than eight in a given Attribute.

    The Otodo gains:

    2/1 armor, but loses any worn armor as the growth shreds anything she’s wearing.

    +2 Size, which increases her Health.

    Massive horns and fangs can be used as damage rating 2 lethal weapons.

    Immunity to Fire

    While transformed, you may spend Willpower to reflexively heal one point of lethal damage or two points of bashing damage.

    Additionally, human onlookers must roll Resolve + Composure to avoid running in fear, requiring successes equal to your character’s Seitokuken dots.

    Characters who run will only barely remember the scene, rationalizing it as an animal attack or human brutality. Even characters who manage to remain on the scene have spotty, muddled memories and cannot connect the Otodo with the monster they saw without extensive evidence afterwards.

    Kenshi

    This gift allows your Otodo to craft a ban for a demon or spirit. Your character must see the monster, know its name, or otherwise have a scrap of its form to use Kenshi.

    Dice Pool: Integrity

    Cost: None or 1 Willpower for Instant Action

    Action: Extended. Each roll represents 30 minutes of ritual crafting and the ritualist must achieve 10 successes to create ban

    Duration: One week

    Generally, the Otodo craft bans related to specific talismans and symbols they create. Your character’s Seitokuken dots act as the monster’s spirit Rank for the purpose of determining the ban level.

    Kigo

    With this gift, your character marks a victim with a burning emblem. Most Otodo have a signature kanji they use for this gift. The emblem sears into the victim. Even once the damage is healed, any Otodo or character able to see auras will see the mark.

    Dice Pool: Integrity vs Resolve/Resistance

    Cost: 1 Willpower
    Duration: indefinitely, A victim can self-inflict aggravated damage equal to your character’s Seitokuken in order to remove the brand.

    adds +1 damage to the attack

    A victim may only be subject to a single Kigo mark from a single Otodo

    Touch the victim (or pass through an ephemeral victim); this touch can be part of a physical attack.

    Against vampires and other undead monsters, it causes aggravated damage instead.

    Even a blinded Otodo clearly sees these marks like a burning white beacon. This means your character never suffers penalties for obscured vision or blindness when engaging a marked enemy, and can see it from upwards of a mile away.

    This adds your character’s Seitokuken dots as a bonus to any rolls to find or pursue the victim.

    Shinigami Buki

    With this gift, the Otodo can literally devour souls. This is typically used to exorcise possessing spirits, or to destroy demons. However, it can be used against human targets to devastating effect.
    The Otodo must touch her victim throughout the process; against a human this usually means restraining him, but against a demon, it generally means trapping it with the Kenshi gift or relying on allies.

    Dice Pool: Integrity vs. Resolve/Resistance
    Cost: 1 willpower

    Action: Reflexive

    Roll Results

    Success: The hunter devours part of the victims soul, the number of success over the resolve replenishing his own Willpower Pool, the victim suffers from the Guilty Condition*
    Exceptional Success: The victim suffers the Broken Condition instead of Guilty.**

    Failure: The Otodo brings Attempts to devour the soul, but his victim’s soul latches on.

    Dramatic Failure: The Otodo fails to eat the soul of his victim, the horror of his action causing backlash. He gains the Guilty Condition.

    *Against spirits, demons, and other otherworldly creatures, this also causes a point of lethal

    **A mortal losing half his total Willpower to this power gains the Soulless Condition. If he loses all Willpower, he gains the Thrall Condition.

    Shonetsu Jigoku

    This gift allows the Otodo to bring forth the hell within her veins, and wield it as a weapon. It oozes out of her pores like lava, and catches fire upon contact with the air around her. She can safely let her body burn with lambent flame, coat a weapon with her fiery blood, or splash the flames across her enemies.

    Cost: 1 willpower
    Dice Pool: Integrity

    Action: Instant

    The hunter summons Shonetsu Jigoku (Burning Hell) fires

    Each success allows for one attack with the Shonetsu Jigoku fires.

    The fire has a damage rating of Seitokuken -1.

    Used on a weapon, it replaces the weapon’s damage.

    It can be thrown with a Dexterity + Athletics roll.

      I don't understand what the purpose of this is. Seitokuten was already written with the core 2e rules system in mind. Hunter Second Edition hadn't been written yet, but the things which it changed aren't relevant to the Seitokuten powers as written.

      This is just big chunks of Seitokuten copied and pasted, sometimes paraphrased (in ways that are occasionally unclear or seem to misunderstand the original rules or use the system text incorrectly?), plus exceptional success/dramatic failure results and some quirky buffs and nerfs. Running down what I've sifted out...
      • Hannya has to be activated for a scene and has an added drawback
      • Jabaku now works on all humans (!!!), but has some fiddly restrictions on how complex the commands can be
      • Kaibutsu makes you immune to fire
      • Kenshi requires a target 10 successes flat instead of the subject's Willpower and no longer works on soulless humans
      • Shinigami Buki requires a contested roll, but can be used reflexively in an instant, and it makes the victim feel Guilty (???)
      • Shonetsu Jigoku is unchanged
      This is fixing what wasn't broken. Is there a reason you're unsatisfied with just using the Seitokuten rules as written in the book? Because I'm pretty sure they work fine with Hunter 2e.

          I don’t think anything is wrong with them, I mostly wanted to rewrite so the text format is similar the ones in the 2e book, for fun and to adapt the conspiracy to a modern setting. It’s mostly for fun, also running a chronicle with one in it. And also to follow the gameplay uniformity like roll results, 10D instead of will to craft ritual(but now I think i understand the point of it being willpower). And yes some rewriting of the rules is my paraphrasing to my understanding because I interpreted then in a certain way and want to make sure it make sense.
          I’m not looking to fix something that isn’t broken, we are using the rule as is now, I just feel some power could use a broader edge case to use.

          my taught behind Hannya I wanted to give agency behind the use of the power to the player. Letting the player decide when he want to observe a soul not when a npc rolls a integrity roll. Which as a ST I don’t really roll. I roll to see how a npc will react, if they will breakdown, freak out or lash out, but integrity rolls are mostly reserved for players. I gave a drawback because having a passive of seeing twilight and and active power to scrutinize someone is powerful so to balance it out. And the flavour is cool

          I wanted to give a effect if my player wants to try to use it on a human that are not hust soulless ir thralls. I understand the combination with Shinigami Buki and wanted to keep that synchronicity. I changed it to must know the human name as well. and wanted to give levels of complexity to the commands for rule uniformity with the lucifuge and council of bones similar endowments that command or compel creatures

          kabutsu should be unchanged it’s strong enough, I taught I removed that

          I put 10 success on the ritual on Kenshi to make it more in line with the other ritual rules in the games. Most extended rolls have a target of 10 (if I’m not mistaken) as for working on soulless humans i missed that part, also how would that work gameplay wise?

          original Shinigami Buki is also a reflexive action as ling as you have contact and spend willpower reflexively
          I wanted to give a condition if the person didn’t loose more then half their willpower and just a few points (I think I could use that soul shock condition from one if the mage legacies where the soul is moved but not damaged) still requires touch, one contested roll to devour soul seems more streamlined also not sure about the filling the willpower back if the user, kinda strong but also the concept is cool. My taught is that the contested roll would balance it out, but this might just created a feed activate power feed loop idk

          anyway thank you for the feedback, this is for fun and to just make the powers a little wider in situations to use. And the og rules are great they are the ones we use. But Dark eras 1 and Hunter 2e have a long time appart in release.
