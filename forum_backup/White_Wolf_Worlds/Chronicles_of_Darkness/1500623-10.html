The Lasting Condition can be applied to to it with +2 Reach, generally if there's no way to revert a material. And from what it sounds like, this is pertinent to an Aspiration/Obsession, which means the answer should probably be "Yes, you can create it" with just a little above the average effort.

                                Which brings me around to the question....why do you want to create lasting Perfected Blood?
                                I'm pretty sure Perfecting via the spell doesn't qualify for that "+2 Reach" rule on p127, given that Dispellation in any form would be a viable method of reverting the material. Granted, I take that stance because 2e's rules read to me as if Lasting should almost never occur as a direct result of magic. Incidentally occur, sure, like fire spreading even once the fireball has been hucked, but directly creating a lasting Perfected item via the spell seems like it goes against the spirit.

                                As to why... mostly a thought exercise? I know you can't cast Hone the Perfected Form, and then pass the Perfected object through Twilight manually to make the effect Lasting*. And since blood, skin, and other organics need the spell cast on them immediately to be Perfected, manually refining them isn't an option. I like figuring out circuitous routes to create Lasting effects that would be impossible with direct spellcasting.

                                There's also a part of me that really wants to know what would happen if you created an entire body piecemeal out of Perfected organics. According to SoS they're still alive, so long as they're Perfected, and Life sure as heck would be a good tool for fusing the pieces together into a Frankinstienien creation.

                                *At least, the consensus when I asked the forums about it was that it isn't viable. Obviously, final ruling is table-to-table.

                                                    Or however you want to say it. Awakening is very much a game of how you say a thing as much as it is what you're saying.

                                                    The other argument of substantiation is working backwards from the text-Perfected Blood has a narrow window of time to be made, so there must be something metaphysically pertinent to how it's main application readily carries the assumption of Lasting. Perfected materials are sufficiently rare enough, with the text making a particular point of noting when spell durations end, that there must be something about their metaphysical makeup or positioning that makes that the case. Those facts are there, they're just text. Everything else is just details to be played with.
