                                            Well, once again, I was talking about individual campaigns.                                             Yeah, I'm really curious to see how they handle this, but I trust the team to find a clean way to make it work. I see two potential ways to make this work.

                                            One way would be to make conspiracies BIG. Say, you have an arm of American or Russian intelligence that breaks their superhuman research into a bunch of sub-departments and all uses an elite wing of the military to track down escaped subjects. Or you have something like the Cheiron Corporation that specializes in splicing together a wide variety of monster parts onto their experiments.

                                            The other alternative I see is to have these conspiracies using contractors. Say most of them are research groups that don't have their own military/security arm, so they tend to utilize mercenary groups and bounty hunters that specialize in hunting deviants. Stack on opportunists and branches of the military that are focused on containment and neutralization of all mad science gone amok, and you have unified threats for the players to band against.
