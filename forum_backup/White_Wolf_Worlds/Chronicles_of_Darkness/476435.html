    A wholesale kidnapping: how?

    04-10-2015, 08:14 PM
    One of the Gentry decides that he needs a large number of teenage girls to populate and decorate some portion of his realm, so he decides to kidnap a bunch of them all at once. One big swoop. As getting them is part of the fun, he decides to maximize the challenge by kidnapping them while they're all naked. So, how might he go about doing this? Any girls he considers insufficiently pretty will be reshaped in Arcadia, probably painfully, so he doesn't have to try to filter outer girls on basis of looks.
