    I also got around to revising the honeysoul, finally. Let me know what you think.

    Honeysoul

    Look, I'm no happier about this than you are, but you're wounded, and if we're going to have any chance of getting out of here alive, you're going to need to get over it and start eating.

    Some Keepers fancy themselves to be gourmets, partaking of exotic fare and hosting great banquets featuring esoteric recipes. It was the sad lot of the honeysouls to provide the substance for these bizarre repasts. Each night, the honeysoul was devoured by her Keeper or his favored guests and servants, and each morning she was born anew from her bones. Even when a honeysoul has escaped this vicious cycle, it is not uncommon to see her weep in sympathy over her own meals.

    Beast: She is a timid rabbit girl, with a bite taken out of one long chocolate ear.
    Darkling: Green and red gummy flesh mingle together, covering a visible skeleton of hard black bones.
    Elemental: His features are lovingly detailed with icing upon his gingerbread flesh.
    Fairest: Her skin is the color of honey, and it is slightly sticky to the touch and sweet to the taste.
    Ogre: He is a great boar-like creature with slightly burned yet succulent flesh, a tantalizing aroma filling the air around him.

    Blessing: Each night when the honeysoul sleeps, she heals all bashing and lethal damage she has suffered, or downgrades a single level of aggravated damage to lethal. Additionally, her flesh and blood is mystically potent and especially nourishing. A honeysoul will never suffer any negative effects due to starvation. Other beings may also benefit from her nourishing flesh by devouring it, regaining a point of Willpower, a point of Glamour, or recovering a single level of bashing or lethal damage for each point of lethal damage inflicted upon the honeysoul. This blessing can only be used a number of times per day equal to the honeysoul's Wyrd.
    Here's an alternative take on the Blessing.

    -=-

    Blessing: The Honeysoul's flesh mends quickly, halving the healing time for all levels of damage. She also naturally heals bashing and lethal damage equal to half her Wyrd (rounded up), and never suffers any negative effects due to starvation. Furthermore, her flesh and blood is mystically potent and especially nourishing, and any being who devours it may regain a point of Willpower or Glamour, or recover a single level of bashing or lethal damage for each point of lethal damage inflicted upon the Honeysoul.

            Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post
            White Oak Dragon
            I am wondering if it's possible to just axe the Defense-lowering aspect of the Venator Blessing, seeing as it could be something that could be better covered with Contracts and Merits (like Killer Instinct and its advanced version for the latter, found in p. 117 of Beast: The Primordial). Furthermore, I imagine that a Venator's methods for handling Hedgebeast would be shaped more by the nature of his Durance instead of an innate quirk to his Kith. One guy could be a skilled ambusher and trapper, while another would exploit openings and weaknesses in a direct confrontation.

            On a semi-related note, here's a slight revision to the Blessing if we go down this line of thought.

            -=-

            Blessing: A Venator is skilled at hunting, slaying, and taming wild hobgoblins of the Hedge. He receives bonus dice equal to half his Wyrd, rounded up, on all Animal Ken, Investigation, and Survival rolls related to bestial hobgoblins. If the Venator successfully renders one completely helpless without killing it, he may spend a point of Glamour to make it into a temporary Fae Mount or Hedgebeast Companion as per the Merits for a number of days equal to his Wyrd. This can be extended by spending additional points of Glamour for each additional day that he wishes to maintain the bond. The Venator may only have a number of hobgoblins bonded to him this way equal to the lower of his Presence or Wyrd, though permanent instances of the Merits do not count against this total. He also cannot apply this Blessing to the more humanoid hobs.
            The venatores traditionally hunted and killed animals in an arena for spectators, as well as performing tricks with trained ones. I feel that they should have some benefit in combat against them as part of their kith blessing to reflect that, rather than making them just trackers and tamers and saying that any ability to actually deal with them violently stems from the rest of the character's build. It always drove me nuts in first edition that some kiths seemed to need additional points to be spent in order to make them do something they should have had a talent for, such as needing to take Contracts of Elements (Fire) if you wanted a fire elemental to be able to start fires. I think, perhaps, I've been going through too much trouble with it, though. There's an easier way to make them good at all of them. What about this:

            Blessing: A venator is skilled at hunting, slaying, and taming the wild hobgoblins of the Hedge. The venator gains the Interdisciplinary Specialty (Bestial Hobgoblins) Merit for free, and the venator gains an exceptional success on three successes rather than five on any roll that benefits from it. Additionally, if the venator successfully renders one completely helpless without killing it, he may spend a point of Glamour to make it into a temporary Fae Mount or Hedgebeast Companion as per the Merits for a number of days equal to his Wyrd. This can be extended by spending additional points of Glamour for each additional day that he wishes to maintain the bond. The venator may only have a number of hobgoblins bonded to him in this way equal to the lower of his Presence or Wyrd, although permanent instances of the Merits do not count against this total. He also cannot apply this blessing to the more humanoid hobs.

                            Update to the Windwalker kith.

                            -+-

                            Windwalker
                            "I'm a leaf on the wind. Watch me soar".

                            Vassals to Lords and Ladies of Arcadia's skies, the Windwalkers were tasked with directing the wind for various tasks. These could be benign like the sculpting clouds and dispersing storms, or as destructive as sweeping away armies with a hurricane. However, a Windwalker's affinity for the winds can quicken him to action once an opportunity for escape is found.

                            • Darkling: A disheveled figure wearing a black, featureless cowl and a frayed noose around his neck, who was forcibly taught how to dance in the wind.
                            • Elemental: Formed of thick clouds and mist that darken and lighten according to its mood, this being is a blur of motion with the winds at its beck and call.
                            • Fairest: Lithe and graceful, this sylph's clothing is fashioned out of thin cloud vapor that never seems to disperse unless she wants it to.
                            • Ogre: A body comprised of dense, black clouds with sparking eyes and the strength of a storm coursing through their limbs.

                            Blessing: Windwalkers use the higher of Wits or Dexterity for Defense, and may spend Glamour to apply it against Firearms attacks on a 1-to-1 ratio. They also gain the rote quality on all Initiative rolls, and take no physical penalties from the Heavy Winds Tilt and similar effects. If they spend Glamour or Willpower, they can use the three-dot version of Psychokinesis (Air).

                                    Stoutfellow


                                    "Big things come in small packages"


                                    In Arcadia nothing is ever quite as it seems, as the True Fae have a love for creating mind bending illusions and distorting reality. Changelings who became Stoutfellow were compressed into a fraction of their former size, yet retained all of their physical ability. What resulted was a being who could compete with those several times their size. The Gentry were said to find great amusement in seeing their performance, until even they made the mistake of underestimating their creation.


                                    Beast: An armored carapace and twitching antennae reveal this Changeling's previous life as an insect.

                                    Ogre: Nearly as broad as he is tall and with a beard hanging down to his stomach, this man resembles the dwarf of Norse mythology.

                                    Darkling: Scrawny and timid from years of public humiliation, they found a strength within themselves they never knew they had.


                                    Blessing: When it comes to the Stoutfellow, size isn't everything. They automatically gain the Small-Framed merit at character creation without suffering the reduced health box for their smaller size. Additionally, the Stoutfellow may spend a point of Glamour to add half their Wyrd (rounded up) to their Strength for one action. They may continue to do this as many times in a scene as they have Glamour to spend.

                                            Logician By Deionscribe
                                            I have already gone through all possible courses of action. Believe me when I say that this is the best one.

                                            Some changelings were shaped to be the living beings of pure reason and thought. These Lost served as calculated advisors to their faerie master, while others were given tasks of great magnitude and order. Their heightened thought processes, however, led them to the solution to their plight, and thus made their escape possible.

                                            • Beast: An animal well-known for its intellect, like a dolphin or an octopus.
                                            • Darkling: A cold-hearted demon whose pupiless eyes gleam with inhuman intellect.
                                            • Elemental: Shifting fractals of light that cluster together in the shape of a man.
                                            • Wizened:A human computer, with circuit lines and fiber wires running throughout his body, and a crystalline processor for a brain.

                                            Blessing: A Logician gains a dot of Intelligence, which can take him above the usual limitations imposed by his Wyrd. He also does not suffer any penalties to any rolls which use that Attribute, and he may spend a point of Glamour to halve their target number of successes if they are performed as Extended Actions.
                                            I think that making them immune to Intelligence penalties might be a bit too potent, as that might give them an inherent immunity to some powers that might attack the mind. What about making them immune to the untrained penalty for Mental Skill rolls and giving them the ability to spend Glamour to gain the Encyclopedic Knowledge Merit for a Mental Skill for the scene?                                                             Wavemaster
                                                            "Do what you want with them. Drown them if you like. I certainly would".

                                                            Anyone who survived a flood knows very well how destructive water can be. Changelings who were remade into Wavemasters spent their durance commanding raging rivers and turbulent seas. More than once, they have used this power to drown any who dared intrude on their Keepers' realms. And they have just as often slain fellow captives who tried to swim for freedom. Those Wavemasters who do escape did so after willing the waters of their prison to hold the Gentry at bay.

                                                            • Beast: An anthropomorphic shark with sharp teeth and webbed hands and feet.
                                                            • Elemental: A being sculpted wholly from living coral.
                                                            • Fairest: A sea witch with blue skin and silvery hair, with a pair of gills at the sides of her neck.

                                                            Blessing: A Wavemaster can breathe underwater, and adds her Wyrd to her Initiative so long as more than half her body is submerged. If she spends Glamour or Willpower, she may use the three-dot version of the Psychokinesis Merit to affect water. All rolls to use the Merit gain the 8-again quality if performed at night.
