                I'd love to do something for Vampire in India. Not sure exactly when, but I think I'd want to aim at a period of major decline for a ruling power. Have all sorts of fun stuff running around; rakshasa, vetala, etc.

                ​Also, a bit of a non-standard one: Book of Highways and Kingdoms Vampire, ie 11th Century north and central Africa. I'd love to see it done in an almost travelogue style touching on a series of vampire polities and cultures, from regular Kindred to weirder stuff like areas where the Adze of Ghanan folklore are a dominant subspecies. Have an Al-Bakri-esque figure writing about the various areas from their distant viewpoint, then with a description of what it's really like from a local point of view, rules for key elements, etc. You could get an amazing overview of how the Kindred had adapted in some really interesting cultures and nations of the time.

                For Vampire & Werewolf, I'd want to do a Mongol expansion era; either the reign of Genghis, or a more specific area a few generations down the line like early Yuan China. Werewolf is obvious, but the reason for Vampire is because, well... think about it. Pre-expansion, what does it mean to be a vampire in the Land of the Eternal Blue Sky? What sort of vampire societies and oddities would form in those circumstances? And then, following their expansion, how do the Kindred societies carried into contact with the existing vampiric populations of subjugated areas cope? Do they adapt and become rulers, or do they become ruled in turn? How are they forced to change to survive, and what changes do they force on the Kindred in the lands conquered and ruled by the Mongols?

                ​Another Vampire/Werewolf crossover I'd want is Beast of Gevaudan France. There's already some groundwork laid in the Werewolf 2e corebook about Gevaudan, and it'd be an intriguing scenario to have Uratha and French vampires all caught up scheming, allying, fighting and backstabbing one another.

                - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer

                ​Like my work? Feel like helping me stay supplied with tea? Check out my Patreon

                                                Ok, so a few crossover pitches

                                                Hunter/Mummy - Book of Judges (Israelite Shoftim defend their land from dark gods and their cults, while those cults are fleeing Akhenaten back in Egypt)

                                                Mage/Changeling - Golden Age of Islam (1001 Nights!)

                                                Hunter/Demon - Cathar France (Gnostic Compact struggles against the Demiurge, while the Templar Knights of St George hunt those who would subvert natural law and corrupt the faithful)

                                                Promethean/Demon - Mansa Musa Pilgrimage (the pilgrimage of course works great for Prometheans, and the new infrastructure linking the Mali Empire to the greater Muslim world in the east has great DtD potential; the hook is conflicting signs from Angels and Qashmallim, leaning into the mystery of the God Machine and the Principle)

                                                Mage/Geist - Golden Age of Piracy (would also work to swap either out for Demon, but Geist was the possible era last time, and Mage has a hook story hook in this era as the Silver Ladder and the Seers of the Throne allied against the Nassau Nameless - my idea is that this could create a non-aggression relationship that underlies a Libertine alliance with dissastisfied Diamond Mages almost a century later in the French Revolution)

                                                Mage/Beast - French Revolution (I know the original proposed era would have been Mage/Mummy, but as we're getting Mage/Mummy with the Mutapa Empire, I thought this might work better as something different. The outbreak of the Nameless War, paired with rotating Apexes struggling to define the Paris Hive. It's literally called "the Terror" for a reason)

                                                Vampire/Hunter - Pre-Mutiny India (chance to update and substantially expand on the caste Bloodlines of India, with the thuggee hook for Hunters that was offered as one of the possible eras last time)

                                                Werewolf/Geist - Great War (the Horsemen themselves stalk the earth as war, famine, influenza rage; soldiers touch death and are reborn in the trenches while the Uratha stalk new more and more horrific spirits of slaughter and industrialized devastation)

                                                Vampire/Beast - Jazz Age (intrigue and beauty of the Harlem Renaissance set against the era of the greatest post-Civil War racial violence in American History, with a touch of Lovecraft)

                                                Check out my expansion to the Realm of Brass and Shadow

                                                    To borrow your format, then:

                                                    Werewolf - Meiji Japan (the Forsaken are bound to old traditions as foreign modernity intrudes; will they die off with the turning of the age like the samurai and the Japanese wolf [which poetically goes extinct around this time] or kill the old Japan like they once killed Father Wolf when he grew weak?)

                                                    Hunter/Changeling, possibly Deviant - 1960s America (New Age cults and Slashers [who may well become 'Mansons' by the end of the decade] keep the dubiously-moral organizations of the time hunting while people return from psychedelic jungles to a rapidly-changing homeland that's on the brink of an unpleasant psychic awakening; an excuse to make Hotel Mascaron canon)

                                                    Changeling/Beast - Mythic Greece (the Begotten are self-appointed Furies who believe the gods have sent them to punish hubris but have their credibility threatened by the Lost who are heirs to the divine punishments of Arachne and Medusa)

                                                    Vampire/Demon - 1930s Shanghai (the Pearl of the Orient sees her neon-drenched streets occupied by Imperial Japanese and her back alleys flood with White Russian mobsters, local crime bosses, Nazi spies, and wealthy fools from the West looking for an exotic good time; espionage and murder are the name of the game, but there's older and greater things here than the newest mortal nations)
