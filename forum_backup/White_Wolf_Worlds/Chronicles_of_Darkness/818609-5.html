                        Some thoughts on Wonders and Costs:
                        • Requires Bound Mania: Old system.
                          • Pros:
                            • Already built out.
                            • Makes the Wonders tied to the Genius who created it. It is theirs.
                            • Creates cost/value decisions - More Wonders, but less fluid Mania, or fewer Wonders, but more Mania to work with.
                          • Cons:
                            • Dramatically limits the number of Wonders a Genius can possess.
                            • Restricts Mania economy.
                            • Makes Wonders either owned or Orphaned, no in between.
                          • Hacks:
                            • Inspiration or Axioms can grant "freebies" on the Bound Mania costs - going over your "allotment" still requires Bound Mania, but this allows for more Wonders per Genius.
                            • Standby or Hibernation mode - Mania is only bound to Wonders that are actively being used, with the rest just "resting" in storage or on a shelf somewhere. Bound Wonders are the Genius's, but anyone else can pickup and Bind a Wonder just sitting around.
                        • Requires XP: Proposed system
                          • Pros:
                            • Maps to newer "Magic item" creation a bit better (though removing the middle-man of Willpower Dots and converting it to straight XP).
                            • Opens up theoretically more Wonders in play.
                            • May allow for more nuanced costing structures.
                            • Opens up Mania for active abilities.
                          • Cons
                            • New system - needs to be designed and balanced.
                            • Makes Wonders a bit less tied to the Genius that created them.
                            • XP Sink - Geniuses would find more XP going towards individual Wonders, rather than self-improvement.
                            • XP Economy confusion - Is the XP refunded if the Genius looses a Wonder? Under what conditions? If the Genius wishes to cannibalize a Wonder - can that XP be spent on non-Wonders? Does this restrict Wonder creation to XP-level?
                          • Hacks:
                            • Wondrous XP - Separate XP track solely used for Wonder creation. Normal XP can be spent in addition (optional?), but this offers a separate mechanism for the Wonder creation economy.
                            • Hybrid - Mania binding might still exist, giving either bonuses, or reducing/obviating the XP costs for those Genii without the XP to spare at the moment.
                        • Requires Minimum Resources Merit: Old System
                          • Pros:
                            • Already built out.
                            • Restricts the heights and extremes of mad science - all those toys have to come from somewhere.
                            • Adds story hooks - Why would a Genius rob a bank? Because funding is hard.
                          • Cons:
                            • Restricts upper Wonder creation to the Wealthy.
                            • Adds a hidden "requirement" stat to Wonder creation.
                            • Budgets suck up enjoyment for those not interested in accounting (in which case, it might be moved to Pros?).
                          • Hacks:
                            • Make it a Roll - Having enough Resources for your Wonder is nearly assured to work, but those short on funds and material can always cut corners and use sub-standard parts. Failure might lead to simple Wonder creation failure, or add more Faults, but there is always the chance the impoverished Genius can succeed. Having more money than necessary might allow for bonuses or Fault reduction, even (so billionaire Genii can still rub it in their peers' faces).
                            • Allow for substitute Merits - though subject to justification and ST permission, allow for Merits like Allies, or Fame, or even Barfly and Striking Looks to substitute for Resource dots. You'd be trading on said Merits rather than directly funding the creation yourself.
                        Sidebar: But Where's The Science?
                        Much has been stated how Genius operates more as Mad Engineering (with the focus on Wonder creation) rather than any actual science. Some potential solutions to that could be:
                        • Wondrous/Inspired XP: Conducting insane research, uncovering enigmas in the world, and reverse engineering Wonders might give special XP, encouraging actual research.
                        • Wondrous Plans - The new Equipment creation system allows for Plans now - and who would be better at making crazy, yet astonishingly successful Plans that the Inspired? The more they know (about anything, really, considering their inherent cross-specialization), the better and more durable their Plans are.
                        • Wondrous Facts/Knowledge - Uncover something previous unknown to the Genius? Get a Fact. These could be used in Plan creation, cashed in for Beats, be used to "pay" for Wonder costs (XP or Bound Mania), or some new thing analogous to Clues in the Investigation System.

                                            So, in a flash, I was blessed with a collection of jumbled, disjointed thoughts which I will attempt to push through Jabir.

                                            The first point was that the horrific tone of the game is very well represented by the songs of the Clockwork Quartet, that mad drive to accomplish tasks entirely divorced from sense but still seeming perfectly logical within the head of those attempting to accomplish them - devising a complicated contraption to kill a random bystander as an act of revenge on a former employer, turning your wife into a wasted wretch on a thousand different life support machines that only keep her alive through the fact that she's technically not dead, burning through all your money, your resources, your worldly possessions, because you need a microscope that can see just a little closer...
                                            That's the tone that fits into the Chronicles of Darkness proper, but that's not the tone that's projected by the mechanics - the mechanics project a wacky, hammy science adventure that people seem to enjoy.
                                            The question is, how do we resolve these clashing tones, and perhaps it could be done through simply changing the rules according to the tone you wish to play ala Paranoia's Zap/Classic/Straight options. More work, certainly, but the changes don't have to be terribly drastic and can, indeed, be slotted neatly into an appendix explaining the small alterations that must be made to fit a certain tone


                                            Now that that odd little rant is out of the way, I can move on to what I actually wanted to discuss here, namely that the Genius powerset is, paradoxically, both too broad and too narrow, that Mania being used to create and power Wonders is an important construct of the setting (and that removing it would make Mania useless) and that Mad Science is about more than just building cool things.
                                            All of these problems, I propose, can be solved through a single measure - we can add an additional sort of power that a Genius can possess which we can call... uh... Courses? Fountains? Alignments? Realignments?
                                            The name doesn't really matter. What matters is that these Alignments (I'll just call them that for now, it's simpler) represent the Genius tapping into Inspiration to alter his mindset to better accomplish a particular sort of task, and take the place of the more generic powers of other splats.
                                            Let's throw create and examine an incredibly crude prototype for the purpose of examining this idea.
                                            We'll say that "The Workings of the Human Machine" is a Wits+Inspiration roll to manifest, although other Alignments might use either Intelligence or Resolve, and sets the Genius on the path of thinking of the human body as a machine. It costs 1 Mania a turn/scene to maintain (whichever is more relevant) and, in addition to giving the "Anatomy" interdisciplinary specialty for the duration of its effect if the Genius does not already possess it, adds Inspiration to the dicepool of any Crafts or Medicine rolls while active, as well as any attack roll that seeks to cause a specific physical impairment (for example, a leg rack), with some sort of additional bonus on an exceptional success.
                                            There are a couple of catches, however.
                                            One is that this ability requires continued Mania expenditure to maintain, and the other is that it can't simply be turned off - the Genius must make a roll of, say, the amount of Mania spent to maintain any currently active Alignment + Inspiration, - Obligation?
                                            On an exceptional success, the Genius becomes caught in the Alignment mindset, losing a point of Obligation, gain a negative mental condition appropriate to the Alignment and cannot manifest the Alignment again until this condition is resolved.
                                            On a success, the Genius must make an Obligation check and gains a condition unique to the Alignment (for example, this one might provide a malus to empathy rolls?). They can attempt to manifest this Alignment again before the condition is resolved, but gain a cumulative -1 to activate and +1 to deactivate for as many times as this effect is rolled before the condition is resolved.
                                            On a failure, the Genius successfully reverts to a more stable mindframe
                                            On a dramatic failure, the Genius is refunded a portion of the mania spent and gains a +1 to activate and -1 to deactivate next time this Alignment is used.

                                            There is also the possibility of using these "Alignments" as prerequisites for building certain kinds of Wonder, or Wonders that provide certain effects, or at least providing bonuses to manufacturing those sort of Wonder.


                                            EDIT: I think this might be what Inspiration feels like.
