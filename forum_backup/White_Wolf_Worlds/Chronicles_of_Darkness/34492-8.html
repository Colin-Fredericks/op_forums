            110) A mortal, or perhaps a ghoul, is found spying on a member of the Council. Normally this wouldn't have been much of an issue, whoever sent the spy would be punished (if kindred) or killed (if anyone else really) and people would forget.             The spy is now kept in a cell, also known as a bomb shelter, and the prince wants Someone to figure out what the hell the thing is, and if it might be possible to fix his sheriff.

                111) The city was growing, the mortals were quite staunchly against mass-surveillance, the werewolves had finally been wiped out. Life was looking up, big time, for the kindred of the city. The CEO was even such a charmer and diplomat the Carthians gave her their support. Then, one day, the characters wake up not only with one secret each from another kindred in the city, where the bodies are buried so to speak, but also a piece of occult secrets, formulae, methods, signs.
                A lot of people worried, wondered what it might be. A Carthian tried his and managed to step right through a wall. Suddenly everyone tried theirs and they all did something incredible. Then the madness came.
                Everyone knows things, everyone has some impossible new power and, over night, three elders declared themselves the rulers of the domain... run for cover or try to figure out what is going on?

                        113) At first it seemed like a normal breach of the Masquerade, some sadistic fuck getting caught on camera feeding on three men, literally tearing them apart while doing so. It became wildly popular on youtube, spread for a while, the Chairman made some decisions and efforts, had some special effects experts claim responsibility. All done. Except the worrying thing is; the creature in the video can't be kindred. It was firmly on the video, it could split it's hands and they had sharp bits, it grew wings and flew away into the sunlight.
                        Now a blood hunt is usually a pretty straightforward solution to things, but how do you hunt something you've never even heard of before? Something that seems to be immune to the weaknesses of kindred?

                                115
                                You're a bit of an occultist, so you understand the danger of Strix possession, what can happen. Your coterie embarks with you on a quest for more knowledge and defense, having seen first-hand what Strix possession can do (perhaps to a friendly NPC, perhaps to an official in the city).
                                While searching, the expected happens: you get possessed. All of you. In one terrible hurricane of feathers and unlight.

                                You awake two weeks later, by your watch, and expect to see dead loved ones, an eviscerated touchstone, maybe the sheriff banging down the door demanding to know why you (and your coterie, who are coming-to nearby) ashed everyone.
                                Instead, you see the back of a piece of paper, which you proceed to tear off your face (it was stapled).
                                It's written in a sloppy hand, vaguely reminiscent of your own. It just reads: "WE ARE HAVING A SCAVENGER HUNT."

                                The group of you leave the apartment, only to find the list of ten items on the outside door, along with another note (nailed, of course, which is classy):
                                "PS: WELCOME TO DETROIT."
                                Shit.

                                                            The Prince calls a meeting. Everyone rolls in. It's the kind of meeting where no one gets to bicker, and when they start, stern glares from a hundred and one beasts silence them immediately.

                                                            The wolves in the woods and the Kindred of the city, they don't get along so great here. It was territory war, for a long time, and now it's a city divided in half, incursions to the other side constant and covert - the Kindred need blood, and the wolves need to do whatever the fuck they do.

                                                            The Prince got a very clear and verifiable message, though, form their royalty: "Go crazy," it said, "the city is yours."
                                                            What made them finally end the feud? Are they moving? Why would they abandon territory?

                                                            Most of your Kindred exalt in being able to hunt without fear of reprisal, curfews, or feeding from something that only looks human; a few voices dissent, though, and say this must be a trap. It's a set-up; they want us to do something for them, or whatever scared them off? Maybe they think it and us, we'll wipe the board with each other.
