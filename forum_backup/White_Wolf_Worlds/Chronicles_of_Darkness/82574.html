    Victorian Geist

    03-02-2014, 06:25 AM
    So I'm loosely rolling the idea around of running a game set in Victorian London, probably in the 1880s-1890s. I don't have too much trouble coming up with central plots, plus I foresee the group largely making their own stories, which I can facilitate without too much trouble.

    What I'm falling down on is sources on what it would be like to live there. Obviously it'd vary hugely on class and area. I can find stats, history, maps and so on easily enough (although if anyone has any really good sources on those it'd be nice). What I can't find is something that'd help me understand the day to day existence, struggles, victories of people in the area at the time.

    Anyone got any sources for that?

                  I recommend the Granada Television version of Sherlock Holmes, with Jeremy Brett. It is often on instant watch on Netflix. I am not positive if it is right at the moment but it often is. It's a damn good series, set in London (usually) very entertaining. Most of the "movies" are less good, being overly padded out (exception: Hound of the Baskervilles and Sign of Four, which are good) but the Adventures of Sherlock Holmes TV series is solid, though the last season suffers from Jeremy's ill health. They are very entertaining to watch, and have been some of my favorites my entire life.

                  Comme Il Fault is a supplement for Castle Falkenstein written to introduce people to the victorian era. While it is written in CF's odd fiction system where someone from the present is supposedly sending you stuff from that setting, and it is set in CF's setting, it nonetheless has some very useful information. If you can find it sitting around inexpensively pick it up. I always use it for any Victorian set game, not just CF.

                  Vampire (Masquerade) and Changeling (Lost) have Victorian set books, though they focus more on the game and how it is different for the setting than the setting itself. Still, if you have any of them sitting around they are good, and if you are into either setting they may be good for a game of that type someday.

                  ETA: The Sherlock Holms are not on instant watch right now, but will be in the future I am sure. Here is the series, and the two good full length movies The Sign of Four, The Hound of the BaskervillesEven if you don't use Netflix this gives you the series covers to look for.

                      The corebook for the Unhallowed Metropolis game contains a fairly large amount of setting information on Victorian London as well. A lot of it has then been expanded to meet the game's 2105 setting, but as a rule the setting hasn't advanced all that much socially and a lot of what you read should be applicable. The history section also shows the roots of the game's setting, and in doing so provides a pretty good background on Victorian England in general.

                          Daily Life in Victorian England (1993) by Sally Mitchell seems good, read through a bit of it and skimmed the rest on Google Books (books.google.com). ISBN: 0313294674
                          Also, more recently Inside the Victorian Home: A Portrait of Domestic Life in Victorian England (2003) by Judith Flanders seems like more recent information closer to what would be useful for a game setting. ISBN: 0393052095
                          Whether or not these books are able to be purchased or even found... well, I can't do all the work. :-)
