    Z-splat books

    11-19-2016, 10:28 PM
    This is no biggie. No huge topic in any way... But... I have thought a bit about this and come to realize I would very much like to see Z-splat books for the updated CofD game lines. I would most certainly buy a book full of only Bloodlines, Lodges, or Legacies. The stories behind them are interesting to read, and the more examples one gets, the easier it is to design stuff on ones own, I think.

    Would it be possible for such books to be made for 2e?
    This isn't exactly a petition for it, I'm just curious as to if it could get made.

                                  I think the desire for them not to just be in books like The Pack or Dark Eras, is that if you just want some neat z-splats, they get locked behind books with lots of other stuff. The big books of them (esp. since few of those are referenced in other books, making them extremely optional stuff) had an appeal to people in that way.

                                  A different approach are things like some of the later Night Horrors books: be a big book of them, but have clusters of linked groups that can be used as part of a bigger story rather than just a bunch of loosely thematically related ones, and even include groups from other game lines (like a new Hunter group that's linked in with the cluster) to expand things out.

                                  When executed well you have both a collection of functionally stand-alone z-splats, as well as something more engaging to do with the book.

                                      I personally prefer the "book of many things including a few Z-splats" approach over the "book of many Z-splats" approach. The latter is fine if you just want a ton of character options, but I prefer the former as it tends to be better rounded and have something for everyone. It also seems to be the preferred model at the moment, not just for CofD, but for the RPG industry as a whole, if we look at D&D as the standard. The "big book of splats" model is a little 90s/00s.

                                                          I've advocated for it in the past as well. Release individual or small batches of z-splat pdfs, and have a compilation option (probably cheaper than buying each individually) for people that are going to be more collect-them-all minded, and that's enough material to justify PoD.
