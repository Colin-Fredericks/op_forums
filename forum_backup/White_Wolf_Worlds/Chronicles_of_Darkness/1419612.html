    Looking for dark secrets.

    10-22-2020, 01:55 PM
    Hey all, coming to the hive mind for some ideas.

    I'm going to be running a "one shot" (it'll be 4-6 sessions, but that'll be it) in the next month or so, with pre-gen characters. I have a large number of players, so I'm thinking that I'm going to split them into two coteries forced to work together to overcome a situation.

    That said, part of the character packet I'll give each player is going to be a dark secret of some sort. While I have ideas on what some of them could be, I thought that the folks here might come up with some really good ones I hadn't considered.

    The characters are going to run the breadth of the clans and covenants, and will even include one Holy Engineer (depending on how much I'd have to convert to 2e; in any event, that character will also have that new bloodline I can't think of the name of right now). Here's the kicker: each character's dark secret will also be known by another player character. I want there to be opportunity for bribery, blackmail, and so on.

    So with all that said, anyone have any ideas for some good secrets a vampire would want to keep hidden -- or would be blackmailed, etc, over?

                  The character...
                  • ...killed (accidentally or not) the Touchstone of a major local player. Or maybe even of another player character.
                  • ...belongs to a mysterious and condemned Bloodline. The character themselves may not even be aware. Alternatively, the character is Twice-cursed (Spilled Blood, p. 102).
                  • ...is a Solace addict (p. 73) and actually has a reliable supplier.
                  • ...was once nearly killed by hunters, but was spared by selling out other vampires. Many of those have in fact been destroyed.
                  • ...has developed a heretical Theban Miracle or Coil of the Dragon.

                              Ok, let me try to think of a few ones:

                              - You have blackouts from time to time - when you awaken from them you usually find yourself with your hands covered in blood in some unknown room covered in strange bloody magic scrawlings. It's always the same room and you can't find it after you leave. Until you black out again, that is.
                              - You have recently concluded you cannot embrace for some reason. All your past (secret) attempts so far have resulted in some sort of bestial sort of hungry corpse kind of undead. You are still tracking down at least one of your...rogue childer.
                              - Every winter solstice you deliver a "tithe" of two bronze coins in a certain mausoleum in the old city's cemetery, the one with owls in the wrought iron gates around a hedge maze, as instructed to by your sire before its disappearance. You know you're not the only one to do so, but are not really certain of who they are. You also know that you're warned when the time is coming. By whom, you don't know.
                              - Your current persona is a facade hiding something much older and alien. In fact you strongly suspect you're also your sire, strange as it may sound. Unfortunately you don't remember how to return to what you were. Or why you changed identities in the first place.
                              - You can commit diablerie on ghouls, no idea whatsoever why. It's not the same as doing so to other kindred, but still good.
                              - You are haunted by the dead victims of botched feedings, your or others. You have more than once made deals with those that discovered you, just so the damn shades would stop their pestering.
