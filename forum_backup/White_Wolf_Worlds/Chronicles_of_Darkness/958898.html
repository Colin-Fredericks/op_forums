    Celestial: The Reclamation

    08-27-2016, 05:56 PM
    “Celestial: The Reclamation
    You were a god once--a Celestial. You were an Olympian sitting on your golden throne high atop your mountain, maintaining order in the world. You were a Deva sitting in your shining palace in the Heavens, passing judgment and bringing balance to the cycle of life. You were a Grigori flying high in the skies, teaching and guiding humanity towards a brighter future on behalf of Heaven. The world was once bright and full of possibilities--and then you fucked it all up.

    Your selfish and petty actions led to the creation of a child which overthrew the gods from their mountain; you're wasteful and negligent focus caused you to burn through your merit and you fell from your shining palaces in the heavens; you became distracted, eventually shirking your duties and bringing forth the great flood which nearly destroyed the world and forever banned you from your place in Heaven. You were a god once--immortal, all-powerful, and all but invincible…but now you walk in the modern nights a mere shadow of your former self. This is a World of Darkness, and it's YOUR FAULT. You move across the planet now, hunted by creatures that once feared and worshipped you, trying desperately to pick up the pieces of your former glory all in the quest of that one seemingly impossible goal…redemption.

    Celestial, a game of sorrow and lost majesty, where fallen gods seek reclamation: a way to right their wrongs and put the world the way it once was.”

    A fan made game for the Chronicles of Darkness.

    An idea I first posted in the fan-made splat display thread by ZealousChristian24, and due to the very positive response I got for it, I decided to throw it out there…

    Looking for opinions, ideas, and things fans of the CoD want in a game about fallen god-like beings (because this is first and foremost a game for fans of the CoD), and others who would like to help develop this project and see if it is actually worth developing…

    Inspirational material includes Literature like Neil Gaiman’s American Gods; video games like From Dust and Dark Souls; and films like The Green Mile.

      X-Splat--
      Regnum
      The origins of a Celestial, and the domain from which they draw power, they are as follows:
      Madriax: The Celestials of the Heavens, the All Seeing. Human legend recalls them as Indra, Thoth, Lao Zi, and Raziel. Even now as they walk the modern nights as shadows of what they once were, their preternatural powers of awareness are unmatched. Their symbol is the Eye of Providence.
      Caosga: The Celestials of Earth, the All Mighty. Mythology recalls them as Poseidon, Amurru, Thor, and the Thunderbird. Boasting power over the elements, even in their weakened state few can hope to match them for raw power. Their symbol is the occult symbol of the Earth and Power
      Cicle: The Celestials of the Mysteries, The Travelers of the Planes. Mortal religion recalls them as Loki, Anansi, Hermes, and Coyote. Tricksters and travelers of multiple dimensions, even now in the darker modern nights they possess the uncanny ability to travel almost anywhere they please. Their symbol is the Traveler’s Seal.
      Malpirgi: The Celestials of Life, the Creators. Ancient tradition remembers them as Amun, Saule, Aphrodite, and Inanna. Despite not being what they once were, many would be jealous for their powers over the affairs of mortals as well as their strange demiurgic abilities. Their symbol is a burning heart within the Star of Creation.
      Teloah: The Celestials of Death, the Destroyers. Human folklore might paint them as Azrael, Hades, the Baron Samedi, and Anubis. Not just lords over the dead and annihilators, but also hailed as protectors and guardians against malevolent forces—even in their now weakened state, most wouldn’t be able to challenge them in their chosen domains. Their symbol is a skull sat atop the Symbol of the Underworld.

      Y-Splat--
      Creed
      The faction a Celestial chooses for itself; groups who ban together under common ideals in how to reclaim their destinies based on their own personal beliefs and their fuzzy memories of their past lives:
      Titanes: Other wise known as the Elder Gods
      The Grigorian Choir: Otherwise known as the Watchers
      The Fallen Devas: Otherwise known as Divinities
      The Defiled Shrine: Otherwise known as Totems,
      The Descended Masters: Otherwise known as the New Gods
      Outer Gods: Otherwise known as Horrors

                          Glad you do! Here's part of the Lexicon I've been building up:
                          Lexicon (work in progress)

                          Bodhi: The level of understanding a Celestial possesses about themselves, their nature, their past sins, and how best to go about reclaiming their destiny; the Celestial Integrity trait.

                          Choshek: A corruptive and infectious darkness born of the chaos caused by the misdeeds of the Celestials in ages past fermented in the energies of the Welkin—capable of infecting places; objects; and even people, it can nullify Celestial powers and cause tremendous damage not only to their Vessels, but their Manifestations as well.

                          Cintamani: Strange crystalline stones, also known as ‘Baetylus,’ with strange and unpredictable properties which materialize in the Welkin from unused potential energy and fall to the mortal plane.

                          Constellation: A group of two or more Celestials working towards common ends.

                          Creed: One of six factions Celestials join in response to the broken world and how to go about reclaiming their destiny.

                          Designs: Powers which Celestials enact through their Raptures to subtly influence creatures and world events, the influence of which can grow more far-reaching and profound by tapping into the Welkin.

                          Imperium: The measure of a Celestial’s power—as well as how far and intimately it may influence the world around it; the Celestial power level and supernatural tolerance trait.

                          Keres: A collective term for the former servants and enforcers, and now sworn enemies, of the Celestials who are prone to using Choshek to their advantage.

                          Manifestation: The ‘true form’ of a Celestial, and the embodiment of their power that maintains its integrity by remaining attached to a mortal Vessel, and can only be made manifest in the mundane world under very specific circumstances.

                          Nectar: The foodstuffs of Celestials; Odum energy harvested from mortal concepts and ideals in action.

                          Odum: Otherwise known as the Odic force, or the élan vital—the raw energy through which Celestials enact the powers—the Celestial energy trait.

                          Outer Darkness: The realm just outside the edge of the Welkin; the Great Void

                          Phenomena: The impressive otherworldly abilities unique to Celestials, which can be enhanced by tapping into the Welkin, and come in two categories—Revelations and Designs.

                          Rapture: The gigantic ‘shadow’ cast by a Celestial’s Manifestation which influences events and behavior of all things that fall under it; through this ‘shadow’ Celestials may enact Designs.

                          Regnum: One of five origins determining what “type” of higher being a Celestial is and the types of powers they lean towards.

                          Revelations: The ‘up close and personal’ powers in a Celestial’s arsenal, limited in scope and duration (affecting only the immediate environment), but blatantly supernatural and spectacular to behold. Can be truly earth shaking when tapped into the power of the Welkin.

                          Sanctuary: A safe haven for a Celestial as well as those in her Constellation.

                          Vessel: A recently deceased mortal body that a Celestial has attached its Manifestation to in order to maintain itself, as well as interact with the mortal world.

                          Welkin: The original abode of the Celestials; a higher plane of existence that connected all the realms in the World of Darkness together and ensured their vitality and potential in ages long past, but now lies in ruins.

                                              Well, glad you like it, just for you then:

                                              Y-splat Expansion/Explanation:

                                              Celestials memories of their past are fuzzy and non-substantial at best. What was once a mighty entity dwelling in dimensions of existence beyond human comprehension now forced to squeeze its consciousness into the meat of a puny mortal frame just to remain coherent. Very few things of its past squeeze through the consciousness of the Celestial's vessel...but a few things go through as feelings, images, and visions with highly metaphorical imagery...what few pieces a Celestial can gather of what it once was is used as its foundation into how to right its past wrongs and reclaim its destiny as a higher being.

                                              These foundations are what has served as the basis of the five Creeds which Celestials choose to become a part of, each one drawing from certain mortal religions and philosophies (for every religion ultimately revolves around the suffering of the world and how to mitigate it, and as far as Celestials are concerned, mortal religion is based on THEM). Each Creed is governed by a specific overseer which coordinates the Celestials of their own Pantheon worldwide, each revolves around a specific theme, and each draws Nectar from specific human concepts--
                                              • Titanes: Otherwise known as the Elder Gods, these beings find resonance with the ancient giants, the elemental spirits, and thundering lords of heaven from many old religions. These Celestials believe their primary failing was a misuse of Power--Whether they took it for granted and became complacent enough to be overthrown, or they abused it to the point that those beneath them rose up, or they simply grew overconfident and were overcome is up for debate, but all of them agree that to reclaim their former existence they must amass the power they once had.
                                                • Their theme is Personal Greatness
                                                • The Celestial governing the Creed is known as the Tyrant
                                                • They draw their philosophy from such religious beliefs as Ancient Greek, Babylonian, and even more modern philosophers like Nietzsche
                                                • The Concepts they harvest Nectar from are such things as Competition, Strength, and Reaching for the Impossible
                                              • The Grigorian Choir: Otherwise known as the Watchers, these beings find resonance with the fallen angels, beings of light, and djinns of Abrahamic myth. These Celestials believe their primary failing had been in succumbing to corruption, and in turn, letting the world that was supposed to be their ward fall to corruption as well. To reclaim the world that once was, these Celestials believe that they must restore the good that was drained from it many eons ago.
                                                • Their theme is Virtue
                                                • The Celestial in charge is known as the Metatron
                                                • They draw their philosophies from mainly Monotheistic faiths, including lesser known books and mystic off-branches such as the Kabbalah
                                                • The Concepts they receive Nectar from are such things as Charity, Forgiveness, and Healing
                                              • The Fallen Devas: Otherwise known as Divinities, these Celestials find themselves most like the devas, demons, and spiritual beings of Eastern religions. They believe their primary failing had been in losing touch with their inner self, losing balance and thus throwing the world itself into disorder. To reclaim their place in the heavens, they believe that they must restore balance to the world and the creatures within it.
                                                • Their theme is Order
                                                • The Celestial who oversees their activities is known as the Bodhisattva
                                                • They draw their philosophies from a melting pot of eastern philosophies such as Hinduism, Buddhism, Taoism, etc.
                                                • The Concepts they draw Nectar from are such things as Karma, Detachment, and Self-Control
                                              • The Defiled Shrine: Otherwise known as Totems, These Celestials find the most in common with Animistic philosophies from around the world. They believe their primary failing had been in letting too much control fall over Nature, and thus the spirit of the world grew sick. To reclaim the fallen world, they believe they must remove the illusion of control brought on by modern times, and let Nature rule as it once did.
                                                • Their theme is Freedom
                                                • Though they tend to avoid titles, the Celestial hailed first (especially when dealing with other Celestial groups) is referred to as the Alpha
                                                • They draw their philosophies from such things a s Shintoism, various Native American beliefs, and shamanic practices
                                                • The Concepts they procure Nectar from are such things as Growth, Tradition, and Intuition
                                              • The Descended Masters: Otherwise known as the New Gods, these Celestials don't really seem to fit anywhere, and seek to reinvent themselves in a new light. These odd beings believe that whatever they once were had failed due to an inability to evolve or change with the times. To heal the world, they feel they must become something entirely new to the world of spirituality.
                                                • Their theme is Progress
                                                • The group who govern the Creed are known as the Wise Ones
                                                • They draw their philosophies from New Age sources; a pick and mix of whatever they believe works
                                                • The Concepts they gather Nectar from are such things as Advancement, Artistic Expression, and Originality
                                              • Outer Gods: Otherwise known as Horrors, these Celestials have for whatever reason, given up on reclaiming their original place and now seek to destroy the world. Their reasons vary, but the most popular one is that the world is just too broken...too dark to be worth saving. A new, better world must be made, and to do that, the World of Darkness must be smashed to atoms.
                                                • Their theme is Death
                                                • Their is no formal leadership among the outer gods, though they may form small groups dedicated to destroying the world in a specific way
                                                • Their philosophy is very personal, but if one had to choose, they draw many of their ideas from Doomsday cults from around the globe
                                                • The Concepts they gain Nectar from are such things as Blood Sacrifice, Enslavement, and Genocide
                                              Haven't quite figured what the benefits of each Creed would be yet...
