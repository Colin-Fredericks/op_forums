                                  Good Legacies, I'm not sure of, especially since that is fairly narrow and specific, but hey, there's no reason your character can't form their own! Tamers of Honey, anyone?

                                  As for a gun that fires bees, Wonderful Machine explicitly has the ability to graft abilities onto living things, or vice versa, with Life 3. Doesn't seem like a stretch at all to make a bee-shotgun with that.

                                  Sweat glands into beeswax-producing glands seems like nothing more than Transform Life (Life 3).

                                  Giant bees. Well, that sounds like (essentially) a new species all together, so Create Life, which is Life 5, with Mind 5 if you want to make them sapient. That size isn't too unreasonable in regards to flight (word of warning: not a biologist or physicist); a quick online search shows that there are the kori bustard which are about 16kg, so flight should be doable; maybe mix in Forces 4 to help them out on the flight problem. The only way (that I can think of) to make them not subject to Paradox would be via a Legacy Attainment, so more evidence for a custom Legacy.

                                  Now Bees!.exe hmmm... Well, I guess that could be as simple as a Making spell at sympathetic range (the sympathy forged by whatever online interaction you have with the target), so Life 5 with Space 2. Though if the bees are just the vector for damage and then just sublimate away, that is Life 3 or 4 at sympathetic range with Space 2. If you wanted a literal download that when opened, summoned a bunch of bees, that sounds like one of the aforementioned spells hung with Hung Spell (so Time 2) that has a conditional duration (so Fate 2) set to expire when the file opens. Maybe with Forces 2 in order to transfer the "file" as Forces seems to have what little computer-y stuff there is. Honestly, Forces 2 might replace Space 2 as the vector of transmission at this point.
