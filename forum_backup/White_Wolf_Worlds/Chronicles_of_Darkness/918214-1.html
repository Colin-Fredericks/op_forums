                        I like [b]Gargoyles, Ben10, 98's Godzilla (it went a lot better than that trashy movie) and Batman Animated Series. Really inspirational for a lot of games in the CoD style.

                        Courage, The Cowardly Dog strikes me as being in the CoD setting, if in a Shard with talking animals. And that computer is certainly a cryptid of some kind.

                        MiB is a classic for the modern Conspiracy, and the first story that I know (and remember now) were these Men In Black are actually the good guys. It was one of the greater inspirations for the Revised-era Technocracy and some conspiracies in CoD.

                        Roswell Conspiracies seemed pretty good, but it didn't went for too long here were I live, so I didn't saw more than 3 episodes. And later forget to look for more, maybe I'll do it later.

                        Scooby Doo Mystery Incorporated is a lot of material to use, but actually any Scooby Doo series can be used for a lot of inspiration. Even being a parody of the Gothic genre, they're one of the most complete examples of it, and also of the best. There is bound to be a reason for Scooby Doo being the most successful cartoon in history.

                        I remember a lot of Jackie Chan Adventures, but it is actually far more food for Pulp Action than Horror, though it go fairly well with Mage. The same can be said of Xiaolin Showdown.

                        Both The Real Ghostbusters and Extreme Ghostbusters are very good, indeed. By the way, the episode where they face Great Cthulhu on Real Ghostbusters does have a continuation on Extreme Ghostbusters.

                        Good to remember Juniper Lee, and I kinda liked My Life As A Teenage Robot, and certainly loved to watch Powerpuff Girls. Also Danny Phantom did a fairly good job. But Kim Possible wasn't my game.

                        And The Secret Saturdays, honestly, it strikes me as a really bad copy of another classic: Jonny Quest. It was to the Pulp genre almost what Scooby Doo was to the Gothic. Gravity Falls I going to watch later tonight.

                        You certainly can draw a lot for Sin-Eater from Beetlejuice, but also a lot of inspiration can be taken from The Grim Adventures of Billy And Mandy.

                            An odd suggestion (less ChroD related, but still excellent):

                            Who here has seen Wakfu? If not, watch it - it's definite inspiration for Exalted/Scion in feel, though I feel Sadlygrove's relationship with Rubilax has some excellent stuff to steal (I'm surprised "wielders of demonically-infused relics, both as jailers and warriors" isn't a Hunter conspiracy). This is not about Wakfu¹.

                            No, this is about the mini-spinoff for kids, Dofus: The Treasures of Kerubim Crespin. The series is about a grandfather telling his grandson about the adventures of his youth - because how do you think his item shop got stocked? Over the course of the series, you learn that, while the events he's sharing are true, they aren't exactly what happened.

                            =---=

                            For a more pertinent ones - I suggest Megas XLR for a light-hearted chronicle, Paranorman as stupidly good Sin Eater inspiration, and I can't wait for the Zombillenium movie to be out. Also Xiaolin Showdown is... just watch it. That's my next Hunter chronicle (well, most of the characters are Hunters. Wuya is, I would guess, an Earthbound True Fae).

                            Seriously, though, Paranorman has one of the best "help a ghost move on" scenes I've seen.

                            ¹ I will talk about how Wakfu is one of the best works of Western animation of the last 10 years on request, though.
