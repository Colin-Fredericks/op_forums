              Well, Vigil Hunters are designed to be the protagonists of their own game, not the antagonist of the other game-lines. Unless you're running a game where actively dismantling a Compact or Conspiracy is important to the plot (Cells are too small to really make it worth it), interfacing with the specific Hunters is still going to be the focus of what's going on.

              Your average vampire that's being targeted by Network Zero is unlikely to really see them as an organized threat that needs to be dismantled (and if they do, they might not want to get rid of them if they can be pointed at other supernaturals instead). A bunch of humans that find out the vampires are real and try to warn the herd about them? That's why Princes organize enough power to create a Masquerade so the majority of people react with disbelief while vampires can use mind-control powers to discredit those with evidence, and stealth powers to get rid of the evidence. It's not something where you're going to get into the whole mechanical system because vampires default to, "we'll be alive in 100 years, these idiots will be dead in a few decades," when considering what to do with the latest group of humans trying to make a fuss.

                                      Originally posted by Cross-a-way View Post
                                      Would Deivents Consparicy rules need to be Modified for them to work as Hunters in other games?
                                      I think the best way to sum up what everyone is trying to say is:

                                      Deviant Conspiracy rules model conspiracies. This is not a size or type of organization, it's an activity/theme.

                                      And that's what runs afoul of what you're describing, because Hunting is another activity and theme.

                                      The same group of people can do both, hunt and conspire, but the rules will take care of the later.

                                      When the point of those hunters in the game is to investigate the characters and meet them at times face to face, sometimes leveraging their resources and connections as individuals with the sole purpose of finishing off the monsters, Conspiracy rules won't help you no matter what organization the hunters form, even an actual Conspiracy.

                                      But if you want to run a conspiracy heavy game a lá Deviant, where you need to model the organization as a whole and pit its entirety against the PCs, then sure. But what type of game is this?

                                      Deviant assumes Conspiracies keep track of them and want to get them so badly they'll never be left alone. The premise is there even a single Chronicle will see the downfall of more than one Conspiracy and that they matter as a whole rather than just as sources of characters to use. And Deviants are particular targets as they have no actual society to shield them like most others do.

                                      You can run a similar game with any splat with few adjustments, but that's not about hunters. You can also run a conspiracy game where the players have their own Conspiracy, that could be interesting. You have to keep in mind also that other splats usually have whole societies not to Conspiracies, but to their whole Web. If a Conspiracy starts to bring institutional weight to the fight, they will stir their supernatural cousins that can usually turn the game against them quite effectively.
