    Classic WoD Traditions and Technocracy converted into 22 Mystery Cult Initiations

    12-03-2017, 01:38 AM
    I wanted a way to have the flavor of the Classic Mage: the Ascension Technocracy vs the Tradition within Chronicles of Darkness without discarding all the wonderful setting for Chronicles of Darkness, and since neither the Technocracy nor the formalization of Traditions predate the middle ages it seems natural to me that they can be made into Mystery Cults that Mages opt into.

    For the Seers of the Throne/Technocracy to make the most sense please see my post with the 6 Crowns Attainments here. Also in the version I presume Seers part of the Technocracy are much more united by a common vision of the Exarchs' collective Wills, and with that collaboration the Ten Exarchs representing the 10 Arcanum each would have a Ministry.

    Originally posted by Pale_Crusader View Post
    Chancellor - Exarch of Matter
    The Chancellor (Mammon) grants the Crown of Wealth. The character adds her Matter dots to their Resources for purposes of applying soft leverage, making purchases and availability. When someone accepts soft leverage from the character, the character regains a Mana.
    Nemesis - Exarch of Spirit
    The Nemesis grants the Crown of Veils. The character benefits that spirits must subtract the character's Spirit dots from any roll concerning the character, from perceiving him, recalling anything about him, or attempting to affect him in anyway. The character also has no naturally occurring Resonant conditions and may reflexively invoke a Clash of Wills on attempts to establish such a Condition.
    Prophet - Exarch of Time
    The Prophet grants the Crown of Greatness. The character adds his Time dots to their Destiny, which may exceed the standard limits of that merit. When regaining Willpower from the Destiny's Doom also regain a Mana.
    Psychopomp - Exarch of Death
    The Psychopomp grants the Crown of Woe. When someone is attempting to open the character's Doors using one or more Merits, the Seer may reduce each of her opponent’s social Merits by her Death dots, denying the use of any techniques “lost” by instinctively ignoring them with a death hardened heart.
    Raptor - Exarch of Life
    The Raptor grants the Crown of Carnality. The character gains an additional Vice, and regains one point of Mana every time she gains Willpower through either Vice. Also, she may apply Soft Leverage through preternaturally potent sexual favors in Social maneuvering, counting her Life dots and Striking Looks Merits as if they were Resources for that purpose.
    Ruin - Exarch of Fate
    The Ruin grants the Crown of Banality. The character benefits that everyone must subtract the character's Fate dots from any roll to learn anything about him that isn't solely mundane and banal. Supernatural attempts to make the character stand out or seem special may be reflexively resisted by a Clash of Wills. In particular the seer's active supernatural effects don't alert Peripheral Mage Sight.
    Also I have used non-standard Familars by applying that condition to God-Machine Angels (In particular the Technocratic Union and Sons of Ether) from Chronicles of Darkness which is outside context for normal the vanilla Orders of Mages within Mage: The Awakening 2ed, thus giving reason why memberships within these Mystery Cult Initiations Mage Merit would out compete Mages without said membership.


    Council of Nine Mystic Traditions
    Prerequisite: Pentacle Order, Mage of any Path, Must belong to one of the nine Mystery Cult Intiation noted as a Mystic Tradition.
    Purpose: To promote Awakening of worthy sleepers, and provide resources to support the pursuit of Ascension by worthy Mages.
    Relic: Council Table of Nine, an Artifact regular nonagon table with nine wedges representing the nine traditions including the insignia of each Tradition. It materialized during the first Grand Convocation, a Supernal blessing on the founding of the Council of Nine Mystic Traditions. (It is rumored the first Grand Convocation was unknown archmages' bids at Ascension, and the existence of these mystery cults and their related Artifacts are the remains of those Ascension in the Fallen World.)
    Doctrine: Humanity's potential is atrophying in the coma of quiescence, and eventual everything that makes humanity worthwhile will die if humanity as a whole does not Awaken. The Technocratic Union attempts keep humanity asleep amid a world full of supernatural terrors and wornders is like a mother using a pillow to smother a baby's wailing for its own safety, dangerously misguided at best, monstrous selfishness at worst. Humanity must be free to Awaken and the most noble act one can do is progress an individual, a group, or even humanity as the whole. Given the inevitable and catastrophic consequences of humanity's quiescence, no risk is too great or sacrifice too demanding if there is even the slightest chance of success. Awakening of humanity and Ascension of the individual at any price are the goals, and to fail is the end of the world and everyone/thing in it.

    Apprentice: A one-dot Cabal Theme (The entirety of the Pentacle mages whom are members of the same Consilium and this Mystery Cult are considered part of the same Themed Cabal, and given Tarot Deck themed Shadow Names based on their role in the Council of Nine Mystical Traditions portion of the Consilium, includes inverted reading for a total of 156 positions.)
    Initiate: A one-dot Allies (Members of the Council of Nine Mystic Traditions)
    Disciple: A two-dot Familiar ("Avatar" Which is a personal Geotia of the Mage's idealized magical identity)
    Adept: A three-dot Shadow Name (Reflecting previously granted Shadow Name from their role in the Themed Cabal)
    Master: Add two-dot to Familiar and it now counts a one-dot Mentor (The three Skills are the same as Order Rote Specialties)

    Akashic Brotherhood (Mystic Tradition)
    Prerequisite: Adamantine Arrow Order, Any Path Mage, which doesn't belong to any of the other eight Mystic Traditions or Hollow One Mystery Cult Initiation
    Purpose: Hone mind, body, and spirit towards perfection of Awakening and Ascesion through discipline, practiced excellence in all that they do, and training that develops unity of mind, body, and spirit.
    Relic: Akashic Record (An Supernal Artifact that creates a portal that allows for bodily travel to anywhere in the Temenos that the reader is aware of, thus granting access to all of human history and knowledge.)
    Doctrine:
    Originally posted by White Wolf Wikia
    They believe that mind, body and spirit are all part of the larger whole of the person, just like at the same time that person is part of the whole of the universe. In other words, conflict, is an illusion, and the same can be said of identity and dispute, so when mind and body harmonize, the soul follow them. By honing their bodies, these mages make a temple for the mind that ultimately brings the comprehension of the spirit. The Brotherhood uses simple tools — exercise, meditation, practice and study — to refine the simple man into a brother of knowledge.
    Specialty - Brawl (Pacifism)
    A one-dot Status (Adamantine Arrow)
    A two-dot Adamantine Hand
    A three-dot Astral Adept (Obligation Eastern Meditation)
    A three-dot Trained Observer


    Celestial Choir (Mystic Tradition)
    Prerequisite: Any Pentacle Order, Obrimos Path Mage, which doesn't belong to any of the other eight Mystic Traditions or Hollow One Mystery Cult Initiation
    Purpose: To promote the unifying harmony in the sacred aspects of monothestic religion and faith which Awakens the human spirit while exposing both secular and religious faiths which promote Sleep for the shams fostering the Lie they are. Also to seek peity, righteousness, and occassionally martyrdom unto Ascension.
    Relic: Tree of Life (Within the Garden of Eden, that which mankind was expelled from so that we may not eat of it)
    Doctrine:
    Originally posted by White Wolf Wikia
    The Celestial Chorus is a Tradition of mages united by their efforts to touch the Divine as well as their belief in the One and Prime from which all things originate.
    Closely tied to the God of Abrahamic faiths (but its origins and beliefs date back to Ancient Egypt monotheistic god Aten), the One represents the source of all Creation and all Creation is believed to be a part of the One. Humans in particular carry shards of the divine within them, though it is known by many names: the divine flame, the soul, the Avatar. It is this connection which the Celestial Chorus believes enables them to clearly hear the One's song and to shape Creation. While their faith is essentially monotheistic, it is accepted that the Divine has many facets and can be expressed through any number of names, religions, and creeds.
    Members of many faiths and denominations (including a variety of soft polytheists) come together in worship and service of the One, accepting that each person has their own path to the One. Of course, this ideal remains a work in progress and the Singers' history has been one of divisions and compromises, holy wars and martyrs, sacrifice and rejuvenation.
    A one-dot Mana Sensitivity
    A one-dot Status (Ecclesiastical)
    A two-dot Safe Place (Always a church building where the Mage serves as clergy)
    A three-dot Sanctum (The previous church building begins to count as a Sanctum for the Mage)
    A three-dot Hallow (The previous church building spontaniously becomes a Hallow, with Obligations of Piously Singing Worship Songs)

    Cult of Ecstasy (Mystic Tradition)
    Prerequisite: Any Pentacle Order, Arcanthus Path Mage, which doesn't belong to any of the other eight Mystic Traditions or Hollow One Mystery Cult Initiation
    Purpose: Expand human consciousness by transcending the limitations common experience to Awaken those Asleep and ultimately reach Ascension.
    Relic: Artifact: Eternal Lotus (Which produces petals when plucked indefinitely, petals which if eaten sends the eater into their own Oneiros, to partially awaken in absolutel pleasure of the Insensate Tilt after their journey.)
    Doctrine:
    Originally posted by White Wolf Wikia
    The Cult of Ecstasy (also known as Sahajiya, a term derived from the Sanskrit word Sahaja which literally means spontaneous or natural) is a Tradition of mages and visionary seers who transcend boundaries and limitations through sacred experience.

    To achieve that the Cult use things like drugs, music and sex, but they're just means to an end. The objective is to achieve a mental state that transcends the physical world, a trance that allows the mage to see beyond his normal experience and go outside all the lines that keep most people and mages penned in. Cultists take the word ecstasy in its original meaning: a sudden, intense rush of feeling that catapults the subject into an altered state. It doesn't have to be a pleasant feeling, but given a choice, most Cultists definitely prefer it that way. The Cult of Ecstasy breaks down barriers and self-imposed limitations to give people a rush of eye-opening experience.
    Specialty - Socialize (Hedonism)
    A one-dot Fame (The hip, fun, and "In" Crowd)
    A two-dot Between the Ticks
    A three-dot Destiny (Doom: The End of the party)
    A two-dot Potent Resonance and one-dot Potent Nimbus (if Gnosis 3+)/ two-dot Potent Nimbus and one-dot Dream (if Gnosis <3)

    Dreamspeakers (Mystic Tradition)
    Prerequisite: Any Pentacle Order, Thyrsus Path Mage, which doesn't belong to any of the other eight Mystic Traditions or Hollow One Mystery Cult Initiation
    Purpose: Act as a bridge, gaurdian, and guide between the material and the spiritual to protect humanity from forces they do not understand and pass on the knowledge and pactices to teach greater spiritual awareness, for the next generation to Awaken, and should you reach the end of your life-long spirit quest, Ascension.
    Relic: Artifact: Spirit Drums, Changes shape to match the animistic beliefs of the holder and produces an Essence Fountain effect when beat as an Instant Effect with the Resonance of the DreamSpeaker's Choice. The Drum and the person beating it also become the Bane of any Spirit that dares touches either of them.
    Doctrine:
    Originally posted by White Wolf Wikia
    It is one of the most diverse Traditions, with those representing the ancient cultures of African, Native American, Inuit, and Aboriginal Australian societies standing alongside practitioners of Shinto, independent spiritual savants, and descendants of other forgotten tribes and civilizations. Though originally shoehorned into a single group by the other, Eurocentric Traditions, the disparate cultures within the Dreamspeakers have found common ground in their respect for and dedication to the balance between physical and spiritual reality.

    As the Gauntlet has grown thicker and the Spirit World more dangerous, the shamans' duties have become increasingly harder. But whereas other mages tend to overlook matters of spirit and Sleepers forget them altogether, the Dreamspeakers were born to walk the middle round, to see and hear what others do not, and fill roles still very much needed in the modern world.
    Specialty - Occult (Vision Quests)
    A one-dot Dream
    A two-dot Familiar (A wisdom Spirit that is both a personal totem and spirit guide which is symbolically appropriate for you)
    A three-dot True Friend (Your wisdom Spirit Familiar)
    A three-dot Mentor (Also your wisdom Spirit Familiar, as it gains power to assist you, Choose three Skills of the Mentor to reflect how the Familiar helps you)

    Euthanatos (Mystic Tradition)
    Prerequisite: Guardian of the Veil Order, Any Path Mage, which doesn't belong to any of the other eight Mystic Traditions or Hollow One Mystery Cult Initiation
    Purpose: Redeem or bring death to those that are dangerous to the World and damage humanity by their continued existence, especially those whom should not be Mages and are Awakened or about to Awaken.
    Relic: Artifact: Euthanatos Scarf, the Thuggee silk scarf used as the Masque of the first Euthanatos, after which all future mundane costuming portion of the Euthanatos Masques are modeled.Long enough to conceal the face while still using it as a garrot.
    Doctrine:
    Originally posted by White Wolf Wikia
    The Euthanatoi are a Tradition of mages intimately devoted to the forces of death, destiny, and karma in the world. They represent a collection of thanatotic cultists, necromancers, priests of fate, assassins, and healers.

    Euthanatos mages embrace the role of death in the world as that which cleanses and makes way for future growth. Most believe in the reincarnation of souls, meaning death in one life is not to be feared and in fact may be crucial to one's spiritual development. Even those who do not share this belief recognize a continuous cycle of death and rebirth throughout life, and accept that at times death may be necessary to end suffering.

    Most controversially, many Euthanatos see it as their duty to push this cycle forward, removing sources of disease, corruption, and misery from the world in order to quicken the turn of the Wheel of Ages. This means judging when a person's moral degradation has grown too harmful to themselves or others to be allowed to continue, and when it is appropriate to deliver the Good Death. Euthanatos are not cavalier about this responsibility, and are painfully aware of the risk of Jhor, but they know their work is necessary and that they are destined for it.
    Specialty - Empathy (Judge Moral Character)
    A one-dot Status (Guardians of the Veil)
    A two-dot Masque (Either as a secondary Masque or as the first two-dots of the primary Euthanatos Masque)
    A three-dot Masque (Switch the Euthanatos Masque to primary if you don't already have a 5 dot Masque and raise Euthanatos Masque to 5 dots, if you already have a Masque at 5 dots instead gain three-dot Occultation)
    A three-dot Occultation (if doesn't already have) or an three-dot Euthanatos Mentor whom will do them the favor of killing them if they become corrupted and/or otherwise need to die (If they already have Occultation)

    Please note this Tradition shares a Masque and thus while within that Masque they share a Signature Nimbus, Shadowname, and Alternate Identity which has the humble status of an entry level Guardian of the Veil, thus making it very difficult to mystically single out a single Euthanatos from others how have, do and will don the Masque.

    Order of Hermes (Mystic Tradition)
    Prerequisite: Silver Ladder Order, Any Path Mage, which doesn't belong to any of the other eight Mystic Traditions or Hollow One Mystery Cult Initiation
    Purpose: To maintain great magical traditions that demand respect for raw mastery of cosmic forces to make Sleepers dream of Awakening, and to provide the sturdy backbone of a magical society to it can find its way unto Ascension.
    Relic: Artifact: Solomon's Great Seal, created by the wizard king himself to command magical forces lesser Mages can't hope to comprehend.
    Doctrine:
    Originally posted by White Wolf Wikia
    Hermetic philosophy is complex and many-layered. At the heart, the Hermetics profess the drive to perfection. This drive manifests through trials, tests, self-discovery, and the rejoining of fragmented patterns like disparate languages or mathematical conundrums. Ideally, each individual has a Word, a divine imperative that drives the figure's revelations. By exploring the boundaries of that Word and all of its meanings, the individual rises to his inner nature, then beyond. Each step in the process is a challenge that requires a leap of perception but also opens the way to the next path. Eventually, the human passes far enough to become something cosmically divine.
    A one-dot Status (Silver Ladder)
    A one-dot Status (Consilium)
    A two-dot Lex Magica
    A three-dot Mentor (Resource, Occult, Politics)
    A three-dot Infamous Mentor

    Purpose: To employ and study True Science for the pursuit of Ascension in the light of a dynamic non-objective reality worldview, thus eschewing banal false science presumptions of the Lie, and instead applying a flexible scientific method and engineering principles to the choatic wonder of the Supernatural, occasionally inspiring hope and Awakening in bystandards.
    Relic: Imperial weight unit standards set made of crystallized Luminiferous Aether incribed with the Sons of Ether insignia.
    Doctrine:
    Originally posted by White Wolf Wikia
    Exotic theories of orgone fields, hypercombustion chambers and etheric transmission matrices enable the Etherites to create even more weird and wonderful devices. Their Science almost always has a baroque feel, as if it leapt from the pages of pulp fiction or Victorian sci-fi — death rays, robotic servants built of brass and powered by clockwork and fantastic space or aquatic vehicles spring to mind when others discuss the Sons of Ether.
    The Sons of Ether see the science of the Technocracy with disdain, abhorring its static view on the nature of things. Scientific practices are not predictable mechanisms that can be recombined in a near endless rate, but a dynamic and chaotic process. As the Kitab Al-Alacir teaches: Everything is true. Anything is possible.

    Verbena (Mystic Tradition)
    Prerequisite: Mysterium Order, Any Path Mage, which doesn't belong to any of the other eight Mystic Traditions or Hollow One Mystery Cult Initiation
    Purpose: Keep the old ways of magic alive in the collective sleeper consciousness
    Relic: Mask of the Inner Divinity, which changes to reflect the God or Goddess the Verbena is representing in a particular ceremony, with a variety of other known abilities
    Doctrine:
    Originally posted by White Wolf Wikia
    The Verbena is a Tradition of mages... ...dedicated to preserving the ancient crafts and wisdom passed down over the ages by witches and warlocks, druids and druidesses, shamans, mystics, and priests and priestesses of the Old Gods.
    Though modern Verbena have some similarities to Wiccans, they are inheritors of far older legacies from disparate cultures originating around the world. Regardless of their many differing faiths and customs, the Verbena share many principles in common. This includes a deep respect for the Earth and the natural order, a holistic view of people and the world as intrinsically intertwined, and the belief that power and understanding can be found by embracing life in all its passions and pains.
    Of all the Traditions, the Verbena are perhaps most challenged by the changes brought to the world by advancing technology and expanding civilizations. Each member must find a way to apply the lessons and practices of the old ways to the modern world, or be seen by both mages and mortals as merely a fringe practitioner of a dying craft.
    Specialty - Occult (Visceral Magic)
    A one-dot Status (Mysterium)
    A two-dot Egregore
    A three-dot Mystery Cult Influence (Any worship cult of pre-Christian faiths, but Non-Technocratic Union, Non-Convention, Non-Council of Nine Mystic Traditions, and Non-Mystic Tradition)
    A three-dot Egregore

    Virtual Adepts (Mystic Tradition)
    Prerequisite: Free Council Order, Any Path Mage, which doesn't belong to any of the other eight Mystic Traditions or Hollow One Mystery Cult Initiation
    Purpose: Free humanity to escape the Lie through enabling creativity to craft new Truths within their own personal worlds made possible and increasingly widely available by the advance of technology. To use these escapes methods as ways to lead people on a virtual hero's journey towards Amakening and eventually Ascension.
    Relic: The Virtual Reality Hub, an Artifact Server hidden on the Darkweb which allows those accessing it with VR gear to travel to Astral Realms which represent the thoughts of Computers, as if the internet was the Temenos and individual computers are those machine's Oneiros.
    Doctrine:
    Originally posted by White Wolf Wikia
    The Virtual Adept paradigm is one of the more difficult concepts to grasp, as many... ...rationales involve theoretical physics and complex mathematics, but also because it involves looking at reality in a manner that is foreign to most. Central to their magic is the idea of Information as a metaphysical component of existence. There is Information describing all objects, people, places, and ideas; changing the Information changes reality. According to the VAs view of the universe, the Information for all things throughout space and time exist in the Correspondence Point. Magic is then performed by lowering their consciousness into Virtual Space to perceive that Information, and then changing it through force of will. Things are not so simple, however. The VAs know the cosmos is an incredibly complex system involving laws and mathematics that surpass the ability of the mortal mind to comprehend. One cannot use brute force to alter reality any more than they can start a car engine by kicking it. Changing Information in such a way that magic will be performed requires understanding how that data will be processed and complex calculations to determine what exact alterations are needed.
    A one-dot Safe Place (A computer room and/or "Virtual Reality" Refuge)
    A one-dot Grimoire (Digital, using Computers as the Skill attuned to Free Council symbolism, of any two Rotes you want)
    A two-dot Teche' (Computer Networking)
    A three-dot Library (Computers)
    A zero-dot Sanctum and three-dot Demesne (This Demesne allow the Virtual Adept to travel to
    the Astral Realms, and counts physical object media upon which the Digital Grimoire gained as the Demesne's soul stone of the Adept's Path, counting the Adept as its source without loss of Gnosis Potential.)


    Hollow Ones (Non-Tradition, Non-Convention)
    Prerequisite: Any Pentacle Order, Mastigos Path Mage, which doesn't belong to any of the other eight Mystic Traditions Mystery Cult Initiation
    Purpose: To lean deep without hesitation into the dark strangeness of the world and unblinking lead those willing to follow you towards Awakening and Ascension by connecting together all the True weirdness which seem contradictory.
    Relic: Artifact: Magic 8 Ball which is able to cryptically answer almost any question in one to three High Speech glyphs, the darker and stranger the topic the more accurate and clear are its answers.
    Doctrine:
    Originally posted by White Wolf Wikia
    Although disparate and lacking one cohesive Paradigm, the Hollowers are largely dedicated to the ideal of Romance. They are careful to stress that this is not the "grocery store novel, picture of Fabio on the cover" type of romance, but the story of gothic horror, tragic romance and mysterious whimsy. Hollow Ones are likely to invoke their magic using "watered down" versions of classic spells, or rote spells borrowed from other Traditions. For instance, while a Hermetic mage might use his properly dedicated Pentacle of Mercury in a ritual, the Hollower might simply draw a star on a quarter with eyeliner and use that.
    Hollow Ones refuse to believe in Ascension, or that Ascension can only be achieved when the Council of Nine casts away their prejudices and form a truly united powergroup. As the Order of Reason sought to bring all concepts under its heel, most Hollow Ones decided long ago that every member should learn magic on an individual base.
    Specialty - Streetwise (Weirdness and Strange Places)
    A one-dot Striking Looks
    A Occult Skill dot
    A three-dot Shadow Name
    A three-dot Mystery Cult Influence (Non-Technocratic Union, Non-Convention, Non-Council of Nine Mystic Traditions, and Non-Mystic Tradition only)

    Technocratic Union
    Prerequisite: Seer of the Throne, Mage of any Path, Must belong to a Technocrat Convention Mystery Cult Initiation
    Purpose: The mission statement of "One World, One Truth, One Order" to guide the Seers of the Throne which join this Mystery Cult unites them in a way few other agendas could. They seek to keep humanity comfortably and safely Asleep, while expanding in power and securing the world against supernatural beings, especially Pentacle Mages.
    Relic: The Order of Reason Charter
    Doctrine: Sleepers are by definition meant to slumber, living and dying without ever having the notion that they are not experiencing reality. Seers are those whom have awoken and understand that it is the will of beings far greater than themselves that humanity stays in a state of quiescence and seek to serve those beings. However, there are terrible threats to humanity, sanity, and even reality itself within this prison, from the Abyss, and even from the Supernal realms. The supernatural threaten the fabric of the world and mortal society, the Pentacle mages can pontificate all day about the glories of Atlantis and how horrible the oppression by the Exarchs is but a world where individual mages walked around with the destructive power of entire modern nation's arsonals is a terrifyingly unsafe place for the majority of Sleepers, as it was in the days of Atlantis. Though the world is a Lie and a prison, it is a much safer and sane place, and continues to progress in safety as the Exarchs' control strengthens. Technology is a gift from the Exarchs to provide comfort in the prison and as a tool to keep people imprisoned both as tools for the jailers and as distrctions from awakening. The advancement of technology and science towards the Exarchs' ends can be considered the calcification of the Lie, solidifying the soft areas that might lead to burdenless inquiry into the nature of reality, thus in essence walling off potential avenues towards the Truth. The Truth is dangerous, painful, and unpredictable, and it is in humanity's best interest to believe in safe, comfortable and understandable Lies instead. Understanding all this is essential to understanding the Technocratic Union, why powerful and meek Seers of the Throne representing all the Exarchs come together under the banner of humanist utilitarianism dressed in a costume of scientific dogma and technogical promises to in the same breath valiantly protect humanity from the supernatural and oppressively crush any hope it has to Awaken. No other faction of Seers of the Throne are as militant in their war to convert or execute Pentacle Mages for this reason, commonly calling any none Seer of the Throne supernaturally empowered being a Reality Deviant with the same disgust an exterminator might talk of vermin. Seers of the Throne whom choose not to join this Mystery Cult Initiation tend to avoid angering those who have as those within the Mystery Cult are often favored by the Exarchs, and seem plugged into some powerful unseen mover shaping the world. Angels clandestinely transmitting and recieving over modern digital telecommunication networks link those with any real authority within the organization and act as a level of security which is hard to beat.

    Initiate Technocrat: Specialty - Politics (Redtape)
    Apprentice Technocrat: A one-dot Prelacy

    Master Technocrat: A three-dot Prelacy
    Grandmaster Technocrat: Mystery Cult Influence (Any Convention Mystery Cult except the one you belong to)
