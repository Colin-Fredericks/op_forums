    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
    Characters inside the Astral Realms look like how they imagine themselves to look. If a Demon used Rip The Gates to enter the Astral, does he end up looking like his Demonic Form, or does the Cover stay strong?
    I'd say that's a manifestation of the dream form most characters assume when they enter the Astral Realms. It's fluid in appearance because it's not their physical body, but a psychic projection of their identity. If a demon were to enter the Astral Realms in dream form, such as with the assistance of a mage, I'd say his Cover would remain intact, but fluid appearance would take off from there – so a demon with Cover as a mercenary would appear in the Astral the same way the mercenary would if he weren't a demon. Rip the Gates simplifies this further: it opens a physical gate into the Astral Realms. You're just walking in with your normal body, so you look the same you would anywhere else, complete with ability to switch Covers on the fly.

    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
    As for what they can do in the Astral, I'd say they follow the same rules Mages do. In other words, they don't gain the ability to transform their surroundings with their thoughts. They will have to use Embeds, Exploits and so on, like Mages have to use their spells. Unlike Mages, who don't suffer Paradox inside the Astral, Demons still suffer Compromises.
    Yup, sounds about right to me.

                    New question. The Nanobot Composition Process allows you to create objects and simple machines from his body. You can't change your total Size, but could a Demon transform things other than his hands and other extremities? Could he transform his head, or even his entire torso into a machine? If paired with the Huge Size Modification, could the Demon transform into a small vehicle like a motorcycle? If so, should this require a roll and/other Aether? What exactly would count as simple for a techno-arcane monster? A "mere" Modification or Technology can do impressive things, and the most advanced electronics on Earth are basic compared to a lot of Infrastructure. The limitation that I'm sure of is that Nanobot Composition can only create weapons or potentially harmful objects with a Damage Bonus of +0L. If the player wants an effective weapon for the Demonic Form, he can pick Blade Hand, Rivet Arm, Eliminator Cannon, Cavernous Maw and so on.

                    I also think that the ability to ignore all damage with 1 Aether per ignored attack kind of makes other Demonic Form powers meant for protection look less desirable. Sure, Nanobot Composition can't protect you from flamethrowers, explosives or just a big enough club. Since being made of nanobots is like being a swarm, I'd say that spending 1 Aether allows you to treat parts of your body as if they belonged to a Swarm. More specifically, Swarms as house-ruled by Claire Redfield:

                    Originally posted by Claire Redfield View Post

                    Fighting The Swarm

                    Swarms are difficult to harm. After rolling attacks against the swarm as normal, factoring in armor and other modifiers, the swarm takes one point of damage of the appropriate type, or two points with an exceptional success. Damage suffered reduces its Health. Fire, explosions, chemical sprays and other area effects cause normal damage.
                    Therefore, combining Nanobot Composition with Armored Plates, Tough As Stone and Aegis Protocol would be an effective protection all-around, rather than a bit redundant. You'll still burn through Aether quickly if a lot of enemies are attacking you in a single turn, but Demons always roll Primum to generate a few points of Aether when they run out.

                                                    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                                                    Can a Demon's Total Control and Liar's Tongue be short-circuited by the mind control powers of other splats, like Auspex and the Mind Arcanum?
                                                    Liar's Tongue is specifically there to address powers like Auspex and the Mind Arcanum – it's trippy and unintuitive because that explains how it can get around those powers. (It certainly doesn't do anything when you're dealing with mortal humans without any Supernatural Merits, after all.) It's telling you what happens if a vampire uses Auspex to intuit if the demon believes what he's saying: even when the vampire successfully "reads" the demon's mind, the demon's mind is reflexively fluid and returns whatever answer the demon wants.

                                                    Total Control and Liar's Tongue only address microexpressions, body language, and the demon's ability to cheat at what counts as internal honesty, though. They're anti-sensory bulwarks. They usually do nothing against powers like Dominate and Majesty, or the Telepathic Control spell, though those powers still have to contend with the Unchained's often unusually high Supernatural Tolerance.

                                                    You run into grey areas when powers like these are used to provoke responses relating to these things. If a vampire were to use Dominate to command a demon to tell her the truth behind a given subject, I'd probably say the demon is compelled to answer and give the vampire "the truth," but the Liar's Tongue still permits the demon to decide on the fly what "the truth" is at the moment (meaning the command becomes pretty useless). If a mage were to cast Emotional Urging on the demon, to evoke, say, a feeling of pity and sympathy, a successful roll would mean the feeling is evoked, but Total Control still permits the demon to decide whether to let that feeling show outwardly. It could be used to nudge the demon's decision making internally, but not to make the demon outwardly crack if he doesn't want to.

                                                    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                                                    Also, I'd like some second opinions on my question about clothes and Demonic Form above. In any case, Demons could make clothes that adapt to their Demonic Forms as minor Gadgets, like the outfits of the Fantastic Four.
                                                    I tend to assume that the demonic form is unclad (unless you make a point to get a separate wardrobe for it to wear), on the idea that if you switch between Covers from being Janet in marketing to being Mean Lou who works at the auto shop, you're not going to still be wearing Janet's pantsuit. It's one of those things that doesn't have huge bearing, though, so if a player wanted their cool coat to come with from their human form to demonic form, I'd probably just say sure, you can do that.
