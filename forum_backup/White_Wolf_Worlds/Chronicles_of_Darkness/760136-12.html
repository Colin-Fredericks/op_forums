                        Shade
                        Mysteries are webs: A perfect snare and hiding spot, easy to get tangled, but can be unraveled
                        A spider lying in wait above their invisible webs, a venomous scorpion that hides under stones and sands, a praying mantis that blends against trees hidden in plain sight, the assassin who holds his knife against his victims neck, threatening murder to gain a valuable piece of information….
                        A Shade is meant to be a sneak, a master of subterfuge, stealth, and conspiracy. His task is simple, to learn of the hidden plots, the threats to the realm brewing underneath the notice of others, dispatching them himself if he needs to. A Shade’s Colony resembles something like a network of spies and thieves, individual Drones spying upon one another as if paranoid about the threat of covert operatives...
                        Though it should be no surprise that many Shades are loyal to their Courts, many Kings feel threatened about letting an individual who could secretly end their lives and not face repercussions so close to their highest Circles. Still, they serve a vital function, even if their actions escape the bounds of honor or border on breaking Law.
                        Above all else, Shades value pragmatism


                        Noble Form Benefits



                        Favored Principalities
                        Bows - ranged
                        Censers - smokescreen, gasses, incense
                        Chalices - poisons, potions
                        Cloaks - flight, sneaking, gravity
                        Veils - shadows, illusions, lying

The only risk then is taking the focus too much out of the "normal" world, to where Monarchs become insular little extra-dimensional fiefdoms.
                                                        That's the fear I had for the splat. It feels like it's becoming too disconnected with the normal world. What drives Monarchs to be with normal people? What benefits do Colonies provide in the 'real' world and what can one do in the 'real' world to benefit the Colony? What connects Monarchs to humanity or even to the other splats? I think that we'll have to really play up the fact that this metamorphosis is not consensual for most people and while it gives them great power, it's power that they are forced to exercise by the schemes and plots of the Kingmakers and the more powerful Monarchs. The more powerful Monarchs could get away with this disconnection but it should still be a problem. Touchstones will definitely be quite important, I think, if we want to maintain the human element.

                                                        The less powerful Monarchs (i.e. the newbie players) would probably have to deal with heavy taxes and required service to the higher-ups to drive the war machine against the Geodics and even other Courts. I would imagine that a lot of the inter-monarch conflict is going to be political though, mostly about gaining influence so that you have more leeway to do the things that you want to do.
