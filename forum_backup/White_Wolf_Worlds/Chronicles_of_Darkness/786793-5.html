    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    I think it's a bit unfair to exclude a middle here. I mean, my suggestion (which obviously is an expression of what I'd like to see, and wyrmhamster seems pretty keen on), isn't really adding another widget to HtV. It's leveraging an already HtV exclusive system (Tactics) with a general 2e mechanic (Conditions), to help shape a different style/focus of game-play. So it seems a bit much to treat this as wanting all hunters to start to have multiple page Merit lists of borderline or outright supernatural bonuses ala the spoiled Hurt Locker minor templates.
    Hunters, in reality, are Lesser Template ( with Conspiracy guys being Minor ). They are on the same footing like spoiled today The Plain - mostly it's "special techniques", maybe element or two supernatural. And this is totally okay - they still are human and "mortal".

    I just think that mechanics should be putted more emphasis that Vigil is not "we are mortals and shoot to monsters" and that being hunter is kinda special state, where you are partially free from the Quiescence/Lunacy/etc. New version of Code should take care of this - other way we risk of truly unstable hunters all the time from confrontation with Unknown. Also, nicely could it be made as first step in to Slashdom...

    I would leave Practical EXP and Tactics all along - I just pointing they are kinda "special special". Tactics your cell need to learn "hard way", and need to practice them - so they natural stuff of "late Tier 1, beginning Tier 2 & 3" gameplay. There is problem for starting and continuing of Tier 1 play here, as I see in our own Tier 1 Hunter chronicle. But I found solution! Making "Plans" as Building Equipment ( ones again, from CoD corebook ) - they sound as "not tested Tactics" and should be totally used as this - I plan to talk over with our Hunter ST on next session about Plans. Your cell want to set fire nicely some vampire, but never used this particular Tactic? Let them made "Plan" on this, and use this bonus on first try. Later just let them practice it in downtime and buy Tactics.

    I would also add Conditions to mark that "hunt" is a life for the hunters. Many posters say that difference between normal mortals and hunters is in narrative - well, Conditions are mechanics for putting narratives pieces in them, so let's use them as such. Mark that our hunters are special compered to mortal investigators by drive, experience and a bit screw on Integrity. And give us some nice chained Condition there, that will outline much more the hunt itself, compared to mortal investigators "take shotguns and point in to heads".

    Or because hunters are only one that have free XP to buy it, as they do not have other things to buy.
