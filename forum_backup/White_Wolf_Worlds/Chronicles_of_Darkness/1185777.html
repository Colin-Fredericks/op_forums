    Crossover Concept: Vigilante Society

    02-13-2018, 08:10 PM
    Inspired from the Superheroes thread, this is for a band of CofD natives that have joined forces (or occasionally team up) to fight crime! Mortal and supernatural.

    List any characters here (at least description, backstory, and major characteristics, through semi-full write-ups are encouraged), sub-plots, and (eventually) even villains and hostile organizations.

    Feel free to go as serious or as campy as you need. Remember that while code-names are nice for ease of reference, certain splats don't need civilian alter-egos.

      I currently play a Promethean that might fit the bill: Tammuz on the Path of Ferrum (2 of 3 roles fulfilled, one Vitality Alembic calcified), tries to be a Hero, going by the given hero-name of Breaker (albeit his Created name Mark 5 would be just as good a cover name). Concept is Hero-with-an-F-in-Good, as not really understanding concepts like Intention, indirect threat, payback, mitigating circumstances etc. does not make for a good heroic character. Neither does Merciless Torment help, and he half-ended a gang by breaking the people literally apart. Nonetheless, he's trying and learning, and being disciplined, diligent and hard-working, he'll get there. Major characteristic are self-sufficiency (Acid Stomach, Sleepless, Weatherproof, Direction Sense) and physical prowess: thanks to Motus, Charites and Hygeius as well as Parkour, he is agile, strong and dextereous. Thanks to all of Vitality, he is very tanky and powerful, with quite some Willpower to fall back on. Even without charging Alembics or using Brute Force, due to the persistant effects he is a tough opponent easily matching up with several assailants. The Transmutations and Merits, especially together with Motus' persistent effect, create a 'superhero' that is constantly on the move, incredibly fast, barely ever stops, needs no rest, has a good grasp of the area, can easily cover a lot of distance not matter the terrain, lay on a lot of hurt, and doesn't stop coming even when you manage to hurt him. Sadly, he isn't all that smart, so people tricking him into doing their dirty work is not unheard of. If he weren't so stubborn, it'd take place a lot more often. He is currently on a mission (from the principle, as he thinks) to 'redeem' a former Centimanus who's now his crime-fighting partner. And boy does Stannum make for effective crime fighting. Currently taking the fight to a corporation of Alchemists that kidnapped Prometheans, among them one of his friends, to perform evil experiments on them.
      When out as his alter ego, he wears a cheap costume: camu-pants, black shirt, makeshift mask (usually black) made from a pullover, debris, a trash-bag... (if he's prepared, it's black cloth)
      Usually, wears jeans and some shirt or maybe a cheap old jacket he found somewhere.

      Rereading this, I have an image of a Rorschach/Batman/Wolverine hybrid. Bit off the mark.
      Character is a PbP char on this forum, in the Muddy Waters Chronicle.

              Edit to apologize for such a long reply! This is my first time posting here after lurking for a couple of years. I swear I didn't mean to make my comment so long! I did try to answer the OP's questions as fully as possible. ^_^''

              Disclaimer because this is a crossover that involves WoD and CofD: I'm in a one player crossover chronicle with my SO as the ST. He combines splats from both WoD and CofD (1e and 2e), and I have 3 main PC's who I play in that chronicle. Each PC lives in NOLA, however, their stories and experiences are very different from each other and they never cross paths.

              Backstory: I will talk about my kindred PC and her coterie, which is a very mixed group, and nearly 50/50 between kindred and non-kindred. They are known as the St. Louis Armstrong Park Coterie because the kindred in it were from a group embrace that was an Ordo experiment done at the park (Danse de la Morte module is where the ST gets the start of his story), and abandoned shortly thereafter. Each member of the group is either an outcast, has been abandoned, or is running from some past. The primogen and the prince picked up the coterie to basically both raise and use them. Now, they continue to work for the primogen primarily, however, they also "fight crime" and dig up and expose whatever they deem morally wrong or "off" as they come across these things in the city. The coterie, especially my PC, has a penchant for sticking their nose where it doesn't belong, which typically means they busy themselves with the seedy underbelly of the city. They also protect the French Quarter and their Elysium within it, given to them by the primogen to keep the coterie busy. In the French Quarter, they use Network Zero to tap into city cameras and watch the streets. When they are alerted someone is breaking the masquerade (blatantly), someone is feeding there, or some sort of crime is happening the group heads out to solve the issue. Chronicle is on-going and each arc of my PC's story features a different focus, a different supposed "BBEG" or major issue that must be resolved. As a player in this and not the ST, I cannot give complete plot descriptions (nor do I view it within my purview). However, I'm able to provide backstory, and I can provide a breakdown of my PC and her NPC coterie.

              Why I think this fits: Coterie is mixed, and they view themselves as heroes/guardians/defenders to the community even if sometimes they take their vigilante justice too far (innocent causalities, fatalities, etc.). While the coterie may appear like a hodge podge, they are very close knit with good dynamics and strong loyalty to each other.

              Coterie:
              Lark Fields (my PC), khaibit, unaligned, virtue is justice, vice is wrath. She enjoys collecting people and creating contacts, and she specializes in working with hunters. Lark rarely involves herself in kindred affairs unless she sees the coterie can profit from it or is directed to do so by the primogen members. She does own the Elysium which doubles as a business, the strip club Lark's Nest. The Elysium is unique in that it is a safe haven for every splat, not just vampires, and that it functions as a safe haven 24/7. The coterie lives together in the Elysium. Lark is extremely whimsical and wears her heart of her sleeve. She is typically the one to scout the city for their next assignment or investigation, and is the defacto leader of the coterie.

              Becky Adler, gangrel, unaligned, unaligned, virtue is hope, vice is envy. Becky is the second in command in the coterie. If Lark is the official owner of the Elysium, Becky is the official manager. Becky is smart, but can still act like the typical teenager that she is every once in a while. Even as a gangrel, she attempts to hold up the facade of being lady-like. She can still be seen, on the occasions she grows out claws with protean, to still be as dainty as she ever was as a southern belle. Becky specializes in occult knowledge. She has great connections through her upper class family, especially her father who works i a subdivision/company of Cheiron.

              Ebony, daeva, unaligned. Not as much is known about her given she keeps to herself a lot. She was once a faculty member of University of New Orleans before her embrace. As mentioned, she is quiet, but this is due to her derangement she received from diablerizing her own sire in her frenzy. Ebony now spends her freetime adding books to her book collections, and knives to her weapons collection (which is an obsession for her). She apprentices under her stand-in sire, a tzimisce.

              Jack McCandless, khaibit, unaligned, virtue is justice, vice is pride. Jack is typically laid back, like most of the guys in the coterie, but gets his thrills from collecting firearms (legal and illegal in nature) and vehicles (legal and illegal in nature). He is your stereotypical redneck from the boonies (he is literally from the middle of the swamps), and can come off as gruff. However, he has great connections within the PD, and knows the city well due to his previous job as a courier (of legal and illegal goods).

              Louis Maddox, ventrue, unaligned, virtue is temperance, vice is sloth. Once part of the IT staff at the same university Ebony worked at, he now uses his skills to the coterie's benefit. He specializes in tech and computers and works closely with the coterie's Network Zero hunters to help monitor the French Quarter. If the coterie is on a mission that involves infiltrating, he is responsible for hacking into the security and camera systems as well as being the "command center." Louis is the most skittish towards violence in the coterie. Despite being a ventrue, he does not find dominating and controlling others to be pleasant nor necessary. Louis is head of the IT team for the Elysium, alongside a Network Zero hunter.

              Naomi Cortez, promethean, ulgan, virtue temperance, vice unknown. Naomi was an experiment found abandoned in a dilapidated and empty store in Storyville and picked up by the original coterie members. They do their best to help her along her path to understanding herself and humanity. Since she is "new" to life, she comes off as very naive and very childish. While the coterie typically involves themselves in looking after the living aspect of the community, Naomi finds herself, as an ulgan, looking after the ephemeral side of the community alongside coterie-mate Sophie. She has a good handle on firearms despite how new she is to life which may be due to the body's old muscle memory. Her disquiet is lessened due to the fact that the Coterie employs a few other different types of prometheans at the Elysium. Also, for story purposes, the ST nerfed disquiet.

              Sophie Le'Fluer, sin-eater, the stricken, real name unknown, from France, former Cheiron hunter, independent hunter, virtue is charity, vice is medical curiosity. While she lacks empathy, she feels guilt over the things she had done as a Cheiron scientist and the part she played in the NOLA Syndrome (Danse de la Morte). Sohpie now uses her skills to aid others, even aiding the coterie into infiltrating the local Cheiron labs to rescue children who were being used as test subjects. While she comes off as a wee bit unhinged, she still tries to do good for the community, both the living and the dead. She specializes in giving medical aid to the coterie as well as being able to dual-wield firearms to provide backup (which can be horrifying when she can disembody her limbs with her keys and manifestation).

              Quincy Jones, real name unknown, from Wisconsin (supposedly), active Union hunter, Lark's main ghoul (ghouled before the imbuing which allows the condition to remain), imbued hunter (defender with the first few edges), virtue is justice, vice is wrath. He was saved by the coterie during the chaos of the NOLA Syndrome and with the fall of the Union (along with most compacts and conspiracies in NOLA), he gets absorbed into the coterie. like the rest of the guys in the coterie, he has a chill demeanor. Like fellow hunter Sophie, he lacks empathy and often takes a more pragmatic approach to difficult situations. He specializes in having connections and contacts with the blue collared folks of NOLA, and for being able to build weapons on the fly. Quincy also lends his architecture and building expertise when the coterie has to infiltrate buildings. Despite being an imbued hunter, he melds well with the group viewing the group as trying to do their best for the community they live in. He is head of the Elysium's maintenance/utility team.

              Kurak, beast, anakim, legend is vicious, life is patience. Possibly the chillest member of the coterie, and ironically (because he is physically imposing), the most intimidated by social situations. He was added to the coterie while they were vacationing in Las Vegas. He was seeking asylum with the Yakuza there from his own community. He had accidentally killed a few kids when he failed to keep his horror in check. instead of him paying off the Yakuza to take him in, Lark stepped forward (who was making ties with their leader) and offered him asylum with her coterie back in NOLA. He is now a full member of the coterie and is head of security at the Elysium. He specializes in hunting, chasing, and capturing targets of the coterie as well as working alongside Quincy and Louis to ensure security at the Elysium.

                                                      I know this is mainly for heroes, but many classical enemies are mob bosses and the like, people who draw their power partly from influencing others: Kingpin and the Hand, Joker and his crew, Pinguin and the mob, even the likes of Bane and Ras-al-ghul rely on mooks. And what is a Hero without a Villain?
                                                      For my Anakim Predator Mobster, I drew inspiration from such illustrious sources as Collossus, the T-1000, the Kingpin and Fullmetal Alchemist (mostly for the cheap joke of having a character named Alphonse with a Metal Golem Horror). Physically, I moddelled him after the great Ron Perlman in his role as Clay Morrow. What I think is often more true of Villians than heroes is the fact that heroes have two identities, while Villians quite often have just the one: they may have a (ridiculous) moniker, but they don't change their appearance by using a mask to do bad deeds. This is most often true for organized crime lords.

                                                      Alphonse "Iron Al" Moran is an up-and-coming Mobster, been with the Irish Mob for ages as muscle, and when you do the dirty jobs and stay alive to reach his age, you're good at what you're doing. But after his awakening, he wants more, and is building up his reputation to take over the mob, then, the underworld, and after that... who knows, maybe the whole city?
                                                      To achieve this, being an Anakim with traits like Giant, Looming Presence, Iron Skin+Iron Skin Advanced, Unbreakable and Relentless Hunter doesn't hurt. Throw in some Riot Gear, and there is little in the way of normal firepower that can stop this guy. Nightmares likes You're on Fire! (re-fluffed Bugs Everywhere!) and Your Rage Consumes you certainly help. The Anakim Birthright is always fun for a very unexpected ambush.
                                                      However, so far, while climbing the ranks, this gangster has shown little to none guile - everything he does is achieved by brute strength. Indeed, when it comes to Manipulation, he is sup-bar, leaving him with unnecessary enemies and at a loss when a door cannot be pushed in by force.
                                                      It's only a matter of time when he'll attract Heroes to his city...
                                                      While he may have some redeeming qualities (Family-oriented and caring for his subordinates, honourable in so far that he keeps his word and has a code, etc.) he is still a criminal, unnecessarily violent brute, who hurts others simply for his own gain and for fun, and is willing to destroy any semblance of order and balance as long as he or his Family benefit from it.
