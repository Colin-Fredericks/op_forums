        79) [Demon & Princess] The Story Hook exists in multiple phases, each representing the plot moved further along in its operation, or an optional complication.

        • The callous workings of the God-Machine have inadvertently strengthened the Darkness in the area, leading to an abnormally high number of Tainted areas, and swarms of Darkened and Darkspawn. These creatures risked contamination of other Projects' Outputs, and so had to be contained. It determined the best way to do this would be to hook up Princesses into Infrastructure that would use their Inner Light to suppress the Tainted connections to the Dark World, and drive out the Darkened. The Ring must work with local Nakamas to protect them from the Machine's agents (and earn their trust enough to do so).

        •• When the Ring uncovers this piece of Infrastructure, they learn that even if they kill the entrapped Princesses, another piece of Infrastructure is set up to entrap their souls instead of letting them reincarnate (the backup Infrastructure isn't as efficient, so keeping the captive Nobles alive is still desirable). They'll need to co-ordinate with a Nakama to hit both sites simultaneously, or risk the hit on one alerting the other.

        ••• Servants of the Darkness have learned of this Soul-Entrapment device, and seek to steal it to fortify and repair the imprisoning powers of the Dreamworld.

        ••• Another Ring has contacted a different Nakama, but sources indicate they are going to betray them and instead Suborn the Infrastructure to their own purposes (Or, this could be the PC Ring's goal). Not only is this interference, but if the information leaks, it could threaten the already tenuous ties of trust.

        ••• Stormwracked were contacted by agent of the God-Machine, and offered assistance against the forces of the Darkness in exchange for selling out Unchained to be captured. They've already led one Ring to capture and recycling, and if/when word spreads, it could bring down what fragile alliance exist between Princess and Demon.

        •••• The Infrastructure lies in ruins and the souls of the entrapped Nobles released to be reincarnated. But that leaves the Darkness completely unopposed, and swarms of Darkspawn, Mnemosyn, Cataphractoi, and Darkened Cryptids to plague the area. They need to be put in check the hard way.

        Malkydel: "And the Machine dictated; let there be adequate illumination."
        Yossarian: "And lo, it was optimal."

            80) [Demon & Promethean 2E] A new Alchemist corporation is boast of having found a way to "stabilize" Pyros, leading to workings that require no additional input (or risk madness), though they are not as potent as more "traditional" methods. A bidding and corporate espionage war has started of large organizations trying to steal this upstart's secrets. They don't know that the reason given is BS - their "concoctions" are actually Show of Power Gadgets based on true Distillations. Why the controlling Demons are playing this game and what they hope to gain is a mystery. As is what would happen when you pour actual Pyros into these knockoffs.

                (Geist and Changeling) A new designer drug is making its run through the city club scene. A powerful hallucinogen and stimulant it guarantees a good night, unless the images you see makes your heart stop or you become so stimulated you forget how to feel pain, or your dehydrate. A local Krewe begins searching for the source of this drug because the spirits it creates are unusually disruptive and powerful. Their path leads them to motley that is selling a goblin fruit found in the hedge, which they dry and cut into powder and sell as a drug. Are these Changelings alone in this or is the whole freehold involved? Can other motley's help them take down this dangerous ring before more people die?

                    82. (Any and Vampire): There's a Dragon coming to town, and this Dragon collects an unusual hoard. The Hydra, her real name long since forgotten, is a bogeyman among bogeymen, a rogue member of the Ordo Dracul who developed the Coil of Zirnitra to mastery- some say she even created it. After developing the power to Graft Unholy Flesh, she became obsessed with "collecting" powers from other supernatural beings. The lure that brings her to town is the presence of a rare monster that she's been searching for, such as a Demon, a Promethean, or an Azlu Crone. The Hydra will stop at nothing to win her bounty, and her arsenal of stolen powers makes her a terrifying foe, especially as she's adept at using Obfuscate and Protean to hide the many extra limbs and appendages that sprout from her body.

                                        86- Essence of the Wolf [Promethean/Werewolf]: There's been a uproar amongst the local Uratha. For most, the death by disection of a homeless person might not ping too many radars, but the fact that this individual reeked of Essence was something the Aspirants of the Lodge of the Shield thought worth looking into. Unfortunately, they were forced to put it on hold when the first Werewolf died. Since then, two others have been picked off, all killed and then mutilated in the same ritualistic way. Already, the Rahu and Elodoth sharpen their claws as they wonder when this shadow killer will next strike.

                                        Meanwhile, a Throng of Prometheans watch worriedly over recent events. An Ulgan they had just met has been killed in a way that implies more than mere malice, and now a number of unconnected individuals has been murdered, their bodies devoid of the organs and fluids associated with the classical humors. Besides the fact that Disquiet risks making them subjects #1, a stranger appears to be stalking them, and it appears they could very well be the next victims.

                                        In truth, the blame for the murders lie at the feet of an Alchemist, one grown twisted and jealous after watching his brother undergo the First Change while he remained mortal. He believes he can use Vitriol mixed with the humors of a Werewolf will allow him to force his way into the People; so far he has managed to force himself to manifest a Tell, and now he hopes to expand even further. Will the Forsaken and the Created be able to put aside the things that keep them apart before someone else dies?

                                                        88. Changeling and Promethean: A True Fae who fancies himself an artist of Fetches has found out about the Created, and he's getting a bit jealous. Not only can these beings do things that not even his "masterpieces" can, but they can apparently be made by mere mortals! The Sculptor of Leaves is obviously quite curious, and he's looking for any leads on how to incorporate Pyros into his creations. Making things more dangerous, a Promethean who wishes to become a True Fae has fallen under his sway in the search of their New Dawn, and is providing the Sculptor with knowledge and resources...and test subjects.

                                                        89. Promethean and Mage: The Transgression: An Abyssal Disassociation has formed that seems to have its sights set on wiping out the Created. The rapid growth of this entity is suspicious, and it turns out that the Redeemers, a cult of Prometheans that believe that their own kind are abominations, have been "feeding" it and helping it grow. But any Mages who investigate might wonder how they gained so much knowledge about the Abyss- do they have a benefactor within the Mage community?

                                                            90 (Promethean/Mage): She didn't exist a week ago; now, she's standing on a downtown street corner speaking gibberish gospel to mostly-indifferent crowds walking by. The rare few that listen have their eyes ripped open to the symbols of the Supernal, their minds cracking in the face of truths only an Awakened soul can comprehend. Where did she come from? How is she doing this? Why is her skin covered in tattoos that look so much like High Speech?

                                                            91 (Vampire/Demon): When a neonate goes missing, few care to make note of it; they assume her nerve failed her and she faced the sun, or that her indiscretions led to an early Final Death at the hands of hunters or rivals. It's when she shows up on security camera footage two weeks later, killing a gas station clerk with new syringe-fingers, that the undead start to ask questions. Other young Kindred have gone missing as well, and now no one can find the leader of the local Crone cult...

                                                            92 (Beast/Changeling/Mage): A new indie film has just hit local theaters after receiving high praise at a small film festival, and seems to be a triumph of low-budget horror that uses strikingly hand-painted sets and minimal dialogue to convey its moods and themes. Critics and aficionados are riveted by the distinct, surreal visuals... riveted enough that the dreams of the entire city are taking on a sinister, cinematic quality. The images of the film are bleeding into the minds of people who have never seen or even heard of the film, and some have claimed to see its nameless stars in places where the membrane between worlds grows thin. And now the director seems to have vanished, and nobody seems to recall their name, or indeed even what they looked like.
