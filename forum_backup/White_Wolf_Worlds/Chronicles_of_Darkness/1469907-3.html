                        I think the problem here is just that you're seeing this rule as having to be used consistently, and this isn't that kind of rule.

                        Killing Blow, better explained, isn't for dealing damage to a defenseless target every time this comes into play, but when counting the damage matters for the story.

                        This may come into play in two situations: when time matters and when you want to give a character a chance. Are the cops or enemy reinforcements coming? Do you need to leave soon for any reason? You have time to aim your attack, but not all the time in the world? If the ST decides the pressure of time is enough to cause uncertainty, then a Killing Blow rule let you calculate how much harm you cause in a given time frame.

                        And for the ST that executed a PC? In my book, unless the player wanted the scene that way, this was a shitty job. Most often than not the player does not comes out happy at loosing a character without a chance, while number crunching, by suspending the ultimate answer for a time, actually rises drama and makes it feel fair, which is important with PCs.

                        So here is my answer. When do I use Killing Blows? When I think haste may make for a sloppy job, or when I want to give a character a chance to be the 10% that survives a headshot. That and, of course, when the target is supernatural and the calculation naturally matters.

                        Now, you are full of assumptions that I already talked about here. The rule isn't about guns, and guns aren't magic. You keep saying that survivors of head shots are exceedingly rare, but 10% isn't anything to dismiss so easily. Weapons of every kind are made to kill, and they are all extremely effective. Guns are easier, but they aren't less or more effective at killing. You totally can do a messy job with one, and for this you're comparing apples and oranges when you're insisting in comparing general knife attacks with head shots.

                        Your sample character can cause 8 damage on a Killing Blow. 1 Dex + 1 Gun + 3 Aiming + 3 Willpower. Why shouldn't they do all that? This is a completely untrained person with extremely poor coordination. The act of deciding to execute by itself demonstrates that spending Willpower is an easy call for me. And Aiming? Without taking aim they are taking all of 3 seconds to do the job. I think any untrained person trying to use a gun in 3 seconds should indeed get to fail. Or take several shots.

                        Oh, a shot to the head isn't less lethal because the gun is in untrained hands. It's that untrained hands are more likely to not hit the head to begin with. Untrained people do this frequently even at point blank, there are suicide attempts where the recoil plus panic was enough to save a life. By the way, if someone that inapt is trying to execute someone else, they're probably in panic.

                        People here, at least some of them, have a distinct perception of what is realistic than you. You think that making someone hostage in a fight is gamey. I think that risking your life in a fight is stupid and most will do anything they can to reduce that risk. You think guns are so powerful they shouldn't ever fail to kill in an execution, but melee weapons could. I disagree. This is life, not everything you see as unrealistic necessarily is.
