    Originally posted by MachineIV View Post

    Yes. That's the kind of quest I'd love to do a supplement about.

    I'd buy that supplement sight unseen. I would love to see some exploration of whether or not a Changeling can switch Seeming. I'd be even more interested in the possibility of a character outgrowing Seeming entirely. The Seemings represent different coping mechanisms for dealing with truama, sure, but they seem to be largely maladaptive/unhealthy coping mechanisms. I feel like the higher one's Clarity gets, the less one will rely on their Seeming. They'll have less need to fall back on the Blessing to gain Clarity and be able to successfully cope with (and succeed on rolls for) breaking points caused by their Curse. It would be cool if, when one reached high Clarity, there was a difficult (probably practically mythical - like Golconda) way to achieve a transcendence that represented dealing with their past and their nature in an entirely healthy way.
