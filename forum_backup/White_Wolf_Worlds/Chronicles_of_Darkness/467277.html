    Strix Dread Power: Create Revenant

    04-03-2015, 09:23 AM
    Something I made up for a Strix in a New Wave Requiem game that's nearing completion. Lemme know what y'all think:

    Create Revenant
    Minimum Shadow Potency 4
    Create Revenant targets a corpse that the Strix wishes to turn into a Revenant, and involves possessing the corpse as part of the ritual. The fresher the corpse, the better. Corpses older than a month cannot be used.
    Cost: 1 Willpower, 1 Vitae (special)
    Dice Pool: Power + Shadow Potency
    Action: Extended (5 or 10 successes), each roll takes 1 night and requires the expenditure of another Vitae
    The Strix imbues a corpse with the power of the Beast, and uses Vitae to animate its cold limbs. If the corpse is less than a week old, only 5 successes are required; older than that requires more Vitae and time (10 successes). If the corpse has taken any amount of Aggravated damage, it's treated as over a week old. The Strix spends the Willpower at the beginning of the process, and Vitae is spent at the beginning of each night. After all necessary successes are obtained and all Vitae is spent, the corpse becomes a Revenant, with all of the benefits implied.
    Dramatic Failure: In addition to obtaining no successes, the corpse effectively "ages" a week. This could double the successes required or make the corpse unusable. EDIT: Removed "All Vitae and Willpower is lost." I figured that the aging, expended Vitae, and no successes was bad enough. Plus I didn't want to imply that the Strix had to start over.
    Failure: No successes are gained that night; the Vitae is still lost.
    Success: Successes rolled are added to the ongoing process.
    Exceptional Success: The Strix does such a good job that night that the resultant Revenant is better than it was in life: add a single Attribute point to any of the Revenant's Attributes.

                          So page 94, in the paragraph above "Anomalies", says that "being responsible for a revenant's creation is a breaking point for a Kindred with Humanity 2 or more."

                          Which made me curious about something: How would you handle a breaking point for something a character wasn't responsible for, but believed they were?

                          In this case, being framed for creating a revenant. Say they fail the breaking point roll, but later find out that they were framed: Do you give the character a chance to make it back up with a roll somehow, or do you just let it go and say, "Spend XP to get it back"?

                          I think I'm inclined to go with the latter. Seems more in-theme for Vampire, plus sometimes finding out that you didn't do something that you've been stressed about doesn't always help.

                          But if you did allow for "making up" Breaking Points due to mistakes, how would you handle it?
