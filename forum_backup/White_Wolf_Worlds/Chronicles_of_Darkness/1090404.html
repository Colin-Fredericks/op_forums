    Humanity check? Input apreciated

    05-09-2017, 02:55 PM
    The story (background is important I think): The player M is a humanity 8 vampire (60+ human years, 25 vampire ones) sympathetic and likable guy. He has a friend L (also player character, humanity 5 these days) since human days and they have simultaneously been sired as Vampire. They have spent a lot of time together, despite changing views, generally considered good friends. A Human that had a favor to collect from M reached out to him to ask him to set up a meeting between L and the human. M obliged and setup this meeting at which point the Human asked L to make him a Vampire. L lied to the human saying he would do it, took him outside of town and killed him. The human has risen as a ghost entity capable of possessing L and having him walk into the sunlight (shortly, 3 lethal dmg), the Anchor of this entity has come into the hands of M and after talking to it and learning of the what L has done he has decided to give it back to L. M is aware of the dangers this poses to L.

    the question: Does this invoke a humanity check for player M and if so at which rank?

    I am ST-ing the game and am inclined to waive the humanity check here, because of the circumstances. M has offered to help L find another solution for the vampire request and didn't condone the murder or knew of it beforehand. Also there is no way for M to stop the ghost entity from pursuing his goal

    Anyways, different viewpoints and opinions appreciated.

                  Keep in mind, Humanity Detatchment in 2nd Edition (B&S) is about losing touch with the human experience, not about adherence to a behavioral code. Breaking Points are triggered not by sins, but by experiences that force the character to face their inhumanity - "why don't I feel remorse for killing this person?" or "how did I survive that?" or "it's been so long since I talked to a human, I forgot how sensitive they can be." etc. I don't really see anything about M putting the ghost in L's vicinity would cause M to lose his grip on what it means to be human, so I don't see any need for a Detachment roll.

                                      Without knowing what happened to L, there would be no way to determine what level a breaking point this situation would be. I assume everyone here is human. Potentially setting up a friend of at least 25 years to be killed even though they didn't actually do anything to me sounds like something I would never do, but maybe I'm not very judgmental.

Injury could be 5, 6, or 7 depending on the severity and no injury maybe puts it at 8. M's behavior sounds antisocial to me. That balances against the impersonal nature of whatever the ghost does to L. I would wait to see what happens to L then do a humanity check. If nothing else, M did unintentionally help someone become ghosted. I would sum up the entire scenario into one check. The downside to Humanity 8 is lots of things are breaking points.
