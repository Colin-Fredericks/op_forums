                                                            Seriously, how the hell do you organize deviants? With so many different origins and possibilities I just can't come with an effective way. The only thing they share is that they have been changed against their will, that's a very broad term. If the developers want to go with the 5 types, five organizations structure then my suggestion is that their type depends on which organization changed them, and faction on their motivation. Tell me what you think, of course, this is mere speculation done for pure fun, not because I have any idea of what they will do.
                                                            Those changed by corporations reflect greed, those changed by governments reflect a desire for dominance, those changed by cults reflect a desire for knowledge, those changed by aliens (both extraterrestrial and extradimensional) reflect alien concepts, and finally, those change by accidents reflect the randomness of the universe.
                                                            Then we have ideologies. We have the ones motivated by vengeance, who want to destroy their creators no matter what; there are those obsessed with finding a cure; Those who want to hide and start new lives; those who want to rescue others like them and expose the organizations; and those who embrace their change and seek more power. They are not mutually exclusive; it all depends on which one the deviant chooses to focus on.
                                                            I think I discussed this in this forum in the past, but I don’t remember.
