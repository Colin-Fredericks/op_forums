                    I actually really like the idea of an "After-Action Report" Tactic. So yeah, everyone has rolled their Breaking Points and no one quite remembers the encounter the way it actually happened. Then you all sit down and discuss what you experienced, and where there are inconsistencies, you know magic must have been involved. You might be able to pice together something close to the actual events, even if it's not 100% accurate.

                    I'd say on a Dramatic Failure you piece together something very different from what actually happened but believe it to be accurate. On a failure you just can't work out anything that makes sense, on a success you get a general idea of what really happened, and on an exceptional success you get a few specific details (maybe expressed by allowing you to ask questions of the Storyteller).

                    Such a Tactic would fit really well into Thorbes' idea of a game where the ST just describes what the characters remember, instead of what *really* happened. Which, in my honest opinion, is exactly what you'd want out of an Sleeper hunters vs. Awakened Mage(s) chronicle. If you're remembering things exactly as they actually happened, either the antagonist isn't really an Awakened or you're not really sleepers. I'd much rather see in-game workarounds to the problem, like the suggested Tactic, than change the way Supernal magic affects Sleepers for the sake of the chronicle.

                                I'm not sure how useful the tactic would actually be. I mean, the tactic only works if it's possible to rationalise the magic. And that would mean you don't need to reconstruct what happened, you know what happened, you've merely altered how it happened. To give an example, if a mage throws a fireball, the cell rationalises it as the mage firing a gun. Through differences in story they work out that it's magic... ok, but they presumably needed to suspect it was magic already and they already know what basically happened: the mage tried to kill them.
                                That's just my thoughts on it, maybe others have a better idea how it would work.

                                More broadly, I'm not so sure that the Lie is really the biggest problem. Sleepers still notice uncanny things happening around the mage (You lock a mage up, he appears outside, somehow; You shoot a mage, they're fine the next day etc.), and magi have lots of occult paraphenalia around which makes them look all the more like wizards. The fact you can't actually see them throw a bolt of lightning is not really that big a barrier. They're still creepy occultists who meet in secret groups and run conspiracies. You don't need to see the Nemean summon a spirit to find out he's the head of a criminal outfit and meets with other secretive groups at regular intervals (including at least two other major criminal groups).

                                                            Originally posted by Joker View Post

                                                            Though there could easily be Sleeper Hunters that roll 10+ dice on their Breaking Point rolls and have become pretty powerful via regular interaction with Mages. Basically unknowingly using Beats from supernal Magic induced breaking points as their high speed highway to becoming really dangerous and powerful quickly.
                                                            You're right. If we suppose you can use something like that system from 1ed Hunter where you can fiddle with the hierarchy of "sins", but for Integrity, we can easily roll that many dice.

                                                            Let's imagine a Hunter that has Resolve 3, Composure 3 and Integrity 7. He also has the Favored Weapon Merit, and is carrying said weapon. He has Meditative Mind 4, and meditated on that day. He kills a Slasher that is trying to murder a hostage. He went inside the location of the hostages with a plan in mind, so it is premeditated murder (-5 penalty). The Hunter has the Pragmatic Virtue. One of the hostages is a member of his Cell and a close friend. If we choose to ignore the -5 penalty by saying he did it for the Vigil and we spend a willpower point, this is what we roll:

                                                            Resolve + Composure + Integrity + Favored Weapon + Fulfilling Virtue + Protecting Loved One + Willpower Point + Meditative Mind = 3 + 3 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 2 + 3 = 17 dice! And don't forget 10 Again.

                                                            Granted, this is a very specific scenario created with bonuses in mind, but if Sleeper Hunters are going up against Mages, they better get all the bonuses they can to keep their Integrity in place. A Beat and a Willpower every now and then also help. The ST should keep Breaking Point rolls caused by witnessing Supernal magic to a minimum, methinks. The Sleeper Curse has that name for a reason, however, and so Sleeper Hunters should not be immune to such rolls.

                                                            That After Action Report Tactic doesn't sound so bad, now that Charlaquin described it with more detail. Perhaps getting an Exceptional Success on said Tactic lets you re-roll a failed Breaking Point and keep the best result of the two rolls.

                                                            In any case, worsening Paradox with their observation of Supernal magic should still be a risk. There could be a Tactic that increases the chance of Paradox hitting the Mage instead of the Hunters or bystanders. We already have Tactics that do borderline supernatural stuff. For example, Bait And Switch provokes a werewolf into a frenzy but makes him attack his own pack. Deprogramming lets you remove mind control. Effigy lets you trap Spirits within inanimate objects, and may work on Geister for the purposes of permanently killing Sin-Eaters, if the ST says so.

                                                            So, it's not like going up against Mages instead of Witches always results in a downward spiral of Integrity loss and insanity.
