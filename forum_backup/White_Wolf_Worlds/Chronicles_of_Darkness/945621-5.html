                                    If you want to make "people are dying" a bad thing that should be fought, then you have a very easy lever to pull:
                                    Make "humanity is destined to die" an Exarch safety mechanism baked into the Lie in order to keep humanity in check and to make sure that no matter how Mages struggle, they will never get enough time to undermine Exarch plots. This way you make it a tragic situation where you are enslaved by death, and only way of defying and rebelling is to commit to Left Handed Paths. It's a seed for interesting stories that aren't black and white.
                                    And if a singular Mage attains immortality, you might literally send him an Ochema of Psychopomp who will tell them to keep the secret for themselves, or else.

                                    EDIT
                                    This way you don't have to celebrate mortality, but still have to deal with the idea that alternatives to it might be worse than good. Pick your poison, choose between evils, etc.
