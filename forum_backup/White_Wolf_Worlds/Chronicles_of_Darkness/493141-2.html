        Been thinking about on how Gaia works in nWoD. (with it's tradition of inverting oWoD concepts on it's head) brings me to the following.

        1) Gaia is Old.
        2) Gaia is Uncaring.
        3) Gaia is Rageful
        4) Gaia, at times, is Cruel.

        Gaia is old is the statement is that the planet' history is so expansive that we are simply a blink in the eye. The dinos were just 4 mins away. A mote in God's eye.

        Gaia is uncaring simply is the statement that nature, as a whole, doesn't give a shit. Gaia has suffered 5 Mass Extinction events (including one where 90% of Life died) as long as life has been around. The idea that humanity's activity's in causing species to die off would get her to care is absurd. Survival of the fittest and all must be tested.

        Gaia is rageful means that the sometimes, the planet feels the need to wipe something off the map. It's the psycho hobo who smiles at you while waving a bloody knife out in the Wal-Mart parking lot at 3 am. No real meaning or rhyme. Just a plain "Fuck this place."

        Gaia, lastly, is sometimes cruel. Doesn't need to be said how cruel nature can be at times.

        So in the end, I have a spirit who is basically an insane old testament god who doesn't follow her own rules and like her sister Luna, is very likely to cut you if you say the wrong thing.

        That being said, Gaia has a lot to offer the Garou and can certain solidify their hold in keeping a active presence in nWoD. They just have to prove it first.

        Which may end up breaking them as Gaia keeps ordering them to fight against things simply to prove their worth to exist.

        So what's the thought on this?

                You lot ain't conprehending garou power in play. Let's go with attack examples. To strike is usually diff 6 or so, so you use dex+brawl for claw attack (say an average of 3 dex + 3 brawl) you've got 6 dice to strike. You need 1 success to hit, and any extras ADD to the damage roll.

                Damage is then whatever stated per attack so let's use Crinos claw ( pretty sure it's str+2 aggravated) so you've got as an average 3 strength. So 5 dice, plus possibly an additional 5 dice, plus re rolling 10's. With a conservative 3 str you could be doing 10+ damage dice. and at char creation you can easily get 5 dots of strength so... Cwod is a lot more liberal with the initial points.

                As for defense roll you apply stamina as soak so you roll stamina to negate any successes on the opposing side, and apply damage as usual. Garou heal 1 bashing/lethal per turn, not just bashing.

                Multiple actions work on rage points (scale of 1-10) and you can only use half as many rounded up as you have permanent rage stat (there's permanent rage and temporary). Temporary could get you to 10, but you could only use 2 per turn if you had 3 rage as your perm. With 2 rage though it is an additional 2 turns which is a good deal of damage dealt, with no dice handicap for multiple actions (I.rle. Dice splitting). My wife's black spiral is deadly efficient in combat, especially merged with the unicorn spirit like she is. Any uratha is going to have an insanely hard time dealing with that level of power, kuruth or not.

                They also have frenzy, which works like kuruth, but gives you the opportunity to heal any/all damage based on your rage roll.

                I'm all for uratha beating garou in a lot of ways, but pack on pack it just ain't gonna happen. Uratha are hunters and garou are essentially the planets jihadists against damn near anything. In their timeline they almost exterminated all other changing breeds in a rather brutal genocide. If doing a cross over I highly suggest sticking with the gameplay system you know and not trying to mix the two.

                Red sticks would still destroy em all though.

                            I am going with indifference now. I've been constructing the idea of how the Garou would act. As it currently stands. I have them being dropped all over the world and then converging on the place that was oWoD's final battleground.

                            Running theory was that the Wyrm/Oblivion won in the final battle and in the height of it's victory, the universe terminated. Everything broke down. The Garou had themselves swimming in the Abyss (oWoD's Abyss and nWod's Abyss are the same for the sake of the game) until being vomited up onto the grounds of nWoD.

                            As such, with their limited numbers, they are simply making the first and last sept of the Garou nation on the area of the final battleground (going to be left unstated for the STs to place around but assume the area between New Mexico and Denver).

                            The Tribes are without totems but are still kinda considered tribes. In the same manner that a group of people are considered a club even if they don't have a clubhouse. I have have yet to work that out the mechanics but the politics comes as follows:
                            The Children of Gaia would turn into a quasi religious organization focused on getting in contact with Gaia or waking her from slumber.

                            The Black Furies would be very influential on the leadership of the Sept mostly due to them being able to use breeding and protection of the young as leverage.

                            The Bone Gnawers would spend their time mostly outside the sept other than using it as a occasional safe haven and dropping off information about threats to it.

                            The Fianna would be busy being obsessed with documenting all of the Garou's oral history while the members who still carry it still exist.

                            The Get of Fenris would be nursing their wounds after getting their asses kicked via their first swing at spirits and more focused on material enemies such as Uratha and humans that threaten the nation.

                            The Glass Walkers would expand into massive projects that may or may not get them kicked out the Garou nation. One of which is to take a page out of Magadon's playbook and use vaccines to create Kinfolk who will later turn into Garou.

                            The Red Talons would be welcome at first but have their welcome start to wear thin after a while (mostly by bringing Law Enforcement attention onto the Nation in it's very weak state.) May or may not run off with the Predator Kings.

                            The Shadow Lords would emerge as the top "tribe" given the Sliver Fangs emerged in a weakened state than they and have made the convincing argument that if the Garou nation is going to survive, extensive changes to favor brains over brawn must be made.

                            The Silent Striders would become start to be quite adapt at exploring the Shadow and most likely (along with the Gets, although they learned from a more painful manner) learn how spirits work and how the Shadow operates as a whole, quickly becoming an analog for the Uratha Bone Shadows.

                            The Sliver Fangs are struggling to keep relevant while most of their members suffer from Harano not from Gaia's suffering but rather from the fact that they have completely lost their history, lineage, and kinfolk supporting them. May or may not make a comeback.

                            The Stargazers.. I don't really how they would adapt other than perhaps working with the Children of Gaia mostly to find another "caern" (Massive locus with positive essence). Other tribes would view them with distrust as they stories of them pulling out when the Nation needed them most persist.

                            The Uktena and the Wendigo would merge due to the lack of enough members in both tribes and carve out a spot in the nation as effective assassins of the Nation while still trying to forge a connection to the native people around the area.
                            In this, I would farther condense these groups down into themes:

                            Explorers/Shadow Meddlers: Children of Gaia, The Bone Gnawers, The Silent Striders, and The Stargazers. Most likely for the Uratha to see bumbling around the Shadow and causing trouble.

                            Politics/Sept home: Fianna, Get of Fenris, Shadow Lords, Black Furies, and Sliver Fangs. Basically focusing on the politics of the Sept.

                            Big issues: Glass Walkers, Red Talons. Issues of those tribes that may or may not get them exiled.

                            Wildcards: Uktena and the Wendigo.

                            Edit: as much as the translation guide does help, it's still rather hard to take groups which are mostly based on location/certain culture and transfer them into purpose-driven groups.
