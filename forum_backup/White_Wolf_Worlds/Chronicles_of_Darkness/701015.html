    Resources as a Condition

    10-14-2015, 12:43 PM
    While trying to figure out exactly how the Resources Merit and the Cash equipment interacted, I was struck by how similar Cash is to a Condition, so I whipped up a couple quick changes that I'll probably be using in future games. Hopefully, some of you will find this useful or interesting!


    Resources (Merit *-*****)
    Effect:     At the beginning of each Chapter, the character takes the Cash Condition with rating equal to her Resources dots if she didn't already have it with an equal or greater rating.
    At any time a character may reduce their effective Resources by 1 for a month to take the Cash Condition with rating equal to their Resources plus 1. This replaces a Cash Condition granted by the Merit if the character already had one.

    Cash Condition
    Money is burning a hole in your pocket, begging to be spent. This Condition is rated between 1 and 5.
    Effect: When you flash your cash, you gain a bonus equal to its rating minus 2 to appropriate social actions. Additionally, you can buy as many items with Availability at least 2 lower than this Condition's rating as you want.
    Resolution: You may resolve this Condition to spend the money, which can provide a bonus to a single social action equal to the Condition's rating or buy an item with Availability equal or less than the rating. Alternately, you can shuffle your cash into your Resources, providing the Resource Merit with dots equal to the Condition's rating minus 1 for a month.
    For example, a character with Cash 4 can buy as many Availability 1 or 2 items as they want, but must Resolve the Condition to buy an item with Availability 3 or 4.


    The Wording would look so much nicer if WoD used variables like MtG, lol.

    I think this does a good job of bringing Resources forward, and showing inexperienced players what money can do in the system. Only two real differences from the existing system:

    First, spending your Cash gives you a Beat. Probably the biggest change, and I don't dislike it. It's easy enough to say that the Cash Condition doesn't grant Beats.

    I also reduced the Resources you can get from Cash, because just by the numbers you can get up to Resources 5 from Resources 1 by buying Cash then using it as Resources. This evens it out.

      Well, It's not a bad hack, I'd say. It would probably make using Resources feel a bit more narratively relevant. I think I might use it. The issue, of course, is what happens if you raise Resources during a chapter.

      The neat part about using the Cash Condition, I think, is that it would provide a clear mechanical effect for a sudden, one-time influx of dosh. So, if you just pulled an Italian Job or won the lottery, you would have the Condition, but not have to spend Expriences on actual Resources dots.

      One change I would make is to make the Condition "stacked" instead of giving it a rating. It works basically the same, but I feel it would make the Condition a little easier to visualize, effectively "spending" one of the Cash conditions to get stuff, more like how money works.

                                      I believe the intention is that when you reshuffle you don't get your hand AND another hand on top of that.

                                      So, Resources 3 becomes Resources 2/Cash 4, and when you add Cash 4 to Resources 2 you get Resources 3 back. It's a reciprocal condition. R3 <=> R2+C4.

                                      Case 1: Your character gets Equipment: Cash 4. If you liquidize your Cash 4 Assets, you can have Resources 3 for one month, at the end of which you're back to having Resources 0.
                                      Case 2: Your character has Resources 3. Your Character decides to buy Equipment: Cash 4. Now your character has Resources 2 and Cash 4 until the end of the month.
                                      Case 3: Your character has Resources 2 and Cash 4. Your character decides to buy Resources 3 by combining Resources 2 + Cash 4, but it lasts for just one month, at the end of which it goes back to normal resources level (in this case Resources 2).

                                      The way the OP phrased it, when combining Cases 1 through 3 you get:

                                      Your character has Resources 3. The character buys Cash 4 and gets Resources 2. At the end of the month the character will be back to Resources 3. Should the character wish to buy Resources 3 it must recombine Cash 4 into the Resources 2, or wait a full-month.

                                      Also remember, Resources isn't linear in progression, it's closer to exponential. Resources 2 + Resources 2 will never give you Resources 4, it'll give you, at the most, Resources 3.
                                      Resources 0 is just barely enough for paying rent, bills, food and having $10 in your pocket.
                                      Resources 1 adds up to paid bills, rent, better food and about $50-150 at the end of the month. (price of a Kevlar vest rounding the $100)
                                      Resources 2 will raise that to about $170-250 (price range of a good enough rapier)
                                      Resources 3 to around $500-750;
                                      Resources 4 is about $1500 or more;
                                      Resources 5 could go for $5000/month spending money.
