                                            The character is the best in the world at something. With the three dot version of this merit one of their Skills gains an additional dot, increasing it to 6. The five dot version does the same, but applies to an Attribute instead. The bonus dot comes along with the Merit, it needn’t be purchased separately. The Prodigy Merit may be bought more than once, but Storytellers are strongly encouraged to restrict this only to skills and attributes in which a character has been proven to be unprecedentedly adroit.
