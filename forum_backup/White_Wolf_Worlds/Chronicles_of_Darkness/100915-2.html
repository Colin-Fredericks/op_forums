                        We're right back to "Proper tool for the job" and "the right Tribe/Auspice/Renown mix" all over again until we see this clarified, something that was a severe pain in the ass previously. The game is building your character for you in a soft (or hard) way.

                        It's a step away from the "we need a rogue, a bard, a wizard, a warrior, and a paladin" rut Auspices were saddled with since their inception, but narrowing down a Tribe and presenting a very particular specialization and focus as the Big Thing tm is... well, it's just digging another rut in that area instead.

                        Unless there's a further fundamental shift to the notion of Tribes (and how one joins one), I can't get behind this yet. I should comfortably be able to make a Hunter in Darkness who tangles with other Werewolves and doesn't feel like he's getting shortchanged for doing it and not being a Blood Talon. You know, like when you played a Tribe member who's Renown wasn't "optomized" and "encouraged" to be made a particular way not to get stiffed previously.

                        This half-spoiler isn't exactly helping that; we're just getting a bunch of player assumptions without the content to back it on both ends. That's not too helpful. Hopefully we'll see more to do this so we can make a better assessment one way or the other.
