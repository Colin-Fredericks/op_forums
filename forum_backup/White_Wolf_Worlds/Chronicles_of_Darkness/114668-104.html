                    Originally posted by Shaddar View Post
                    If i use Life 5 to create life, that life has children, and then the spell expires/ is dispelled. What happens to the children?? Are they real?
                    Yes, they are a natural side-effect of the spell and thus persist after the spell ends. It's no different than if you use Life 5 to create something and it kills someone, or creates a tool, or poops in the woods, the consequences don't go away just because the spell ends.
