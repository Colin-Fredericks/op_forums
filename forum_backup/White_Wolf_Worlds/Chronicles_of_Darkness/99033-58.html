            Lorelei’s Diary

            And just when I thought I had come across a simple, easily-solved problem at long last, the forces of Fate have conspired to follow it up with something that is both complex and massively embarrassing for me personally. Oh well, I guess one can’t have it all.

            Maria and I climbed out of bed for the usual Thursday morning therapy-college shuffle, leaving Gloria grumbling sleepily and pulling the covers tightly back around herself. Yes, even in Southern California, it can get brisk on January mornings. We each gave her a lingering kiss goodbye and promised that we’d see her at Sex Breakfast, then headed downstairs.

            Calamity was already drinking, if the smell of her morning coffee was any indication. I can’t say that I really blame her; figuring out how to explain her recent traumas to a therapist can’t have been easy. Although I suppose if anyone is prepared to deal with “I was psychologically tortured by a powerful, inscrutable ancient creature this week,” it’s the Freehold’s psychiatrists…

            So Becky and I climbed into the ‘vette and motored off to UCLA. Not much of import happened while we were on campus… a few idle flirts here and there, and I threw one of my lab partners a wink when his hand “accidentally” brushed up against my ass in Biology. I thought I might be able to parlay that into a quick liaison after class let out, but he only seemed interested in stammering and not meeting my eyes after that, so nothing came of it.

            Which was probably for the best, as I found that I’d missed a call from John while I was matriculating. I hit the speed-dial to call him back as I walked out of the building (Becky falling into step beside me as inconspicuously as possible). He answered on the third ring, and I asked what I could do for him.

            “Things have escalated on the sea monster front,” he explained, and I felt my heart jump into my throat. “Nobody’s been seriously injured yet, but we need to step up our timetable. We’d still like to lure the sea serpent back into the Hedge if possible; it seems more confused by its situation than destructive… but something else has come through. It’s some sort of giant crab creature, and it’s behaving a lot more aggressively than its friend.”

            “How do you want to handle this?” I asked, all business.

            “I’ve got two boats standing by… Rowan and Veronica are taking Rowan’s Sea Spray out, and I’m sending a group out on my own Garmonbozia. Rowan can take two other people with her. My ship will hold about a dozen, but we should probably send less to avoid people tripping over each other. Jolie will be in the water leading the serpent away while Ginny holds the crab’s attention. Louis Guo and Ji Wen Ja will be on the Garmonbozia assisting the latter, and I’ve asked Soon-Hee Kwon to lend us her monster-hunting expertise as well. I’d like you in the air if possible, scouting the area, applying healing contracts where necessary, and ferrying people back to the boats if things turn ugly. That okay with you?”

            I nodded, even though he couldn’t see the gesture. “I’ll be happy to help however I can, John. You want me to bring the rest of the motley in on this?”

            “Anyone you think can help is welcome to come along,” he said, “but remember… we need to keep the group relatively small. And I don’t want word of this getting out to the Freehold in general until we’re done… the last thing we need is to start a panic, or to attract a crowd of sight-seers who might get hurt or get in the way.”

            “I hear you,” I said. “I’ll keep the circle small. You want me to call you when we’re on our way, or just meet your team at the docks?”

            “Either is fine,” he said. “We don’t have a specific time set for the expedition, but I don’t want to delay it too long. A few hours at most.”

            “Got it,” I said. “Becky and I should be at the Dream House within the hour; we’ll head out to the dock as soon as I’ve explained the situation to the others.”

            “Thanks, Lori,” he said, “I knew I could count on you.” Damn, that man really knows how to play on my daddy issues. The vote of confidence and approval from a strong male authority figure bolstered me all the way home.

            I dialed Calamity next and asked her to make sure the motley was ready for a meeting when I got home. “Sorry,” I said, “I know everybody was looking forward to Sex Breakfast, but we’re going to have something to discuss and it’s time-sensitive.”

            “No problem,” she said, “any hints as to what this is about?”

            “Let’s just say… ‘we’re going to need a bigger boat,’” I quipped, certain she’d pick up the hint. I’ve always wanted to say that!

            I got back just in time to kiss Gloria goodbye and join the others in the kitchen for our meeting. I outlined the problem and the basics of the plan, and then opened things up for discussion. Calamity was predictably excited by the prospect of getting to fight a giant sea monster. The presence of the crab-thing (I called it Sebastian; D continuously referred to it as “Ebirah,” a reference which I got but refused to acknowledge) even made her sanguine about our plan to deal with the serpent non-violently. Jacob offered his assistance as well; he’s a very strong swimmer. D of course asserted immediately that if I was a part of this mission, she’d be there regardless of what anyone said about it. Becky was oath-bound to protect me, so she joined the team as well. Marguerite offered to swim up to the crab invisibly and weaken it with her contracts, and I think we all saw the value in that.

            I turned toward Luna. “We’d love to have you along,” I said fervently. “Your swoop-and-attack tactics could be very useful. But I know that water isn’t your element, and most of the time you’ll probably be stuck with me on scouting and retrieval duty.”

            She nodded. “It needs to be done,” she said simply, and that settled the matter.

            I turned toward Missy and Jane. “Don’t take this the wrong way,” I said, “but I think you two should stay behind on this one. Your wings aren’t strong enough to let you really fly yet, and they get really heavy when they’re wet.” They nodded their understanding. Missy looked relieved; Jane looked a little disappointed. Julia will be staying behind with the two of them, naturally… I love her to death, but she’s not built for missions like this.

            I also told people to keep the nature of the mission quiet until it was over, and caught Calamity throwing me a sheepish look. When I questioned her about this privately, she admitted that she had told Trinh what was going on and suggested that she might want to cook up some giant crab meat at the inevitable victory party. I wasn’t sure whether to laugh or roll my eyes at that, but Calamity assured me that Trinh would keep things under her hat until everything had been taken care of.

            As we drove the question of SCUBA gear came up. Renting a few sets was mentioned, and I pointed out that you have to be certified in its use before they’ll let you rent a tank. None of us know how to use SCUBA (and I don’t see that changing for me any time soon, thank you very much), so its legal procurement seemed out of the question. Calamity chimed in that John could probably acquire some gear for us, but I put my foot down.

            “You have to be trained to use that stuff for a very good reason,” I explained. “If you don’t know how to use the regulator, you could get too much oxygen, or too little, and either of those circumstances would make you light-headed… which is something we can’t afford in a combat against giant hedge beasts.”

            “It also affects the way you move underwater,” Maria added. “Even if you’re a very good swimmer, having that much weight strapped to your back is very awkward until you’re used to it. I… know how the oxygen tanks work, but I’m not actually certified. I could probably get everyone set up, but if anything happened while we were underwater and couldn’t communicate effectively…”

            “Best not to mess with it, then,” Calamity said with a sigh. “I guess we’ll all be protecting the boats anyway…”

            I did call John to let him know we were on the way, and while I had him on the phone I asked about the possibility of SCUBA gear. He couldn’t get his hands on any quickly enough to be of any use to us, but did tell me that Tamara kept a wetsuit, tank, and diving gear on the Garmonbozia, and that we’d be welcome to use it if it would fit any of us. As Tamara is about 5’2” and very slender, it seemed unlikely, but I passed the info along to the others. D figured she might at least be able to make use of the tank and breathing apparatus, even if the suit and flippers wouldn’t fit her.

            When we got to the docks, it turned out that we had other options. The others were already gathered and preparing the boats for departure. Jolie told us that she could give someone the ability to breathe underwater for the better part of an hour… but the ability had strings attached.

            “I’ll have to kiss whoever needs this power,” she said. No problems there, right? But there was more. “It only works on one person at a time, and it makes the subject highly… suggestible when it comes to my wishes. Basically, if I use this on you, you’re going to feel compelled to do anything I order you to do, so you’re going to have to trust me.” Calamity decided that she trusted Jolie enough for that, and that was that.

            Spoiler alert… things went pretty much according to plan. Jacob and Calamity joined Rowan and Veronica (who drily informed us that Neon had threatened to kill her if anything happened to Rowan) on the Sea Spray while the rest of us piled into the Garmonbozia and headed out to sea. I gave D a passionate kiss, admonished her to be careful underwater, to protect my courtier, and to kick the cloaca of anything that tried to interfere with either of those goals. She favored me with her trademarked cocky grin, tightened the straps on her tank, and fell backwards into the brine, following Ginny and Jolie. I sighed, stretched, and launched myself into the air with a strong beat of my wings.

            I was wearing light clothing, since I didn’t want anything heavier to weigh me down if it got wet, so it was a little cold up there, but the glorious California sunshine was bright, and soon I was sweeping through the air in wide gyres, scanning the horizon for our quarry. It didn’t take me long to find them… Cecil was keeping his distance, eyeing the boats nervously with his head just above the surface, while Sebastian was a massive shadow sidling along just beneath the waves. I pointed them out using my Bluetooth thingummy, just in case any of the others hadn’t seen them already, and the fight was joined.

            Marguerite was as good as her word, slipping on to the crab’s back before it was aware of her and draining some of its vitality with her touch… which is probably why the rest of the fight went as well as it did. She had used a contract to give herself fins, which gave her the speed to slip in and out of the fray quickly. Marguerite is nothing if not versatile.

            Ginny got the first attack off, and it was obvious that the crab wasn’t expecting a frontal assault. It lashed out at Ginny, grasping her in one of its massive pincers. Calamity and Jacob joined the fray, the former having obtained Jolie’s magic kiss while the latter was content to hold his breath the old-fashioned way. Maria and Jolie made a bee-line for Cecil while the others kept the crab busy.

            Calamity and Jacob focused on the beast’s claw first, hoping to free Ginny before it could kill her, and they were successful. Ginny darted up to the surface looking seriously miserable and gave me a pre-arranged hand-signal indicating that she could use some healing magic. I swooped down and activated the contract, and she looked a lot better instantly. Based on the amount of energy I poured into her and the extent of the bruises I saw after the fight was through, I’d guess she had suffered at least a few broken ribs.

            Louis Guo provided cover fire from the top of the Garmonbozia, with Becky standing right behind him with her claws out the entire time. I don’t know if he actually affected the outcome of the fight, but I’m glad he was there all the same. I know Becky was keeping an eye on him on my behalf – she gets antsy whenever anyone has a sniper rifle in my general vicinity – but I don’t think Louis means me any harm, and I doubt he would have chosen such a public forum even if he did.

            Jacob, Calamity and Ginny finished Sebastian off pretty quickly after that, but that didn’t end our day’s watery adventures. Jolie came on the Bluetooth (she can speak underwater, and Lowjack had provided her with a waterproof communicator) and warned Calamity to stay close to the surface, as Maria had spotted another threat and engaged it. Jolie wanted the option of giving Maria the ability to breathe water just in case her tank was ruptured in the fight or something. I nervously inquired as to the nature of the fracas, but Jolie reassured me that Maria had it well in hand. There was some sort of giant anglerfish down there, but my honey made short work of it between her massive strength and her leechfinger abilities.

            The fight – and the resulting blood in the water – had attracted more mundane threats as well. I spotted a large shark (I’m pretty sure it was a great white) homing in on my friends’ location and communicated this to them quickly. Calamity clicked her tongue to indicate that she had received the message and… well, she punched the shark in the face as it approached. Jacob finished it off. From above, it looked like the poor fish just sort of exploded into a cloud of blood and gore in the water. I feel a little bad for it, but those things are exceptionally dangerous and single-minded, and I don’t think my friends had any other good options.

            Once Maria had taken care of the anglerfish thing, Jolie was able to escort Cecil through the hedge gate with no further problems (I hope he was grateful for our efforts). Maria then set a couple of small explosive charges around the entrance to the underwater cave. Once the two of them were clear, we detonated the charges, which will hopefully bring this whole matter to a close once and for all.

            I alit back on the upper deck of the Garmonbozia once everyone had returned. D shrugged out of her SCUBA tank and suffered through a long hug and kiss. She and Ginny had secured the crab’s carcass to the back of the ship so that we could tow it in to shore and carve it up for the inevitable victory party. Eww.

            As we headed back for the docks, I made a few phone calls. First, I called John to let him know that it was done and to ask him to get a truck to the docks so that we could transport the umpteen thousand pounds of crab meat back to the Dream House. He promised to send a large truck and several people who wouldn’t ask questions to butcher our kill. I also invited him to the victory party Calamity was planning. He said he’d check with Tamara, but would likely attend.

            I called Dark Kimberly next. “Sorry honey,” I said breathlessly, “I know it’s short notice, but we’re going to close the Dream House for a private function tonight. If you could, please contact all of our mortal employees and let them know that they have the night off. Then send an email out to the Freehold and our close allies to let them know that the sea monster threat has been eliminated, and that we’re holding a victory party at the Dream House from 7:00 until whenever.”

            “Got it,” she said, sounding chipper. “Do we need to make any other arrangements?”

            Calamity had already called Trinh to ask her about cooking stuff, and Lowjack had promised to provide the necessary spices in bulk, so I told Kimberly that would suffice.

            Finally, I called Dad. Sure enough, whatever weird hotline he has to the Man Upstairs had been tingling throughout the encounter, though he told me that it had been fairly mild in this instance. I filled him in on the general details of the fight and my part in it, and told him that he would be welcome at the victory party (I knew that Elena was going to be there and that Stasia was out of town, and I didn’t want the former to be the only mortal in attendance). He agreed to put in an appearance. Me and my big mouth.

            We arrived back at the Dream House, and by the time we’d all showered and gotten cleaned up, both Trinh and the massive truckload full of Hedge Crab Meat had arrived. John was there too, and he made himself useful in the kitchen, though he was careful to cook only according to Trinh’s directions… he was clearly the sous-chef in that arrangement. Once again I was vaguely nauseated at the thought of eating a Hedge Beast… and once again I found it to be utterly delicious, though in this case that might have more to do with Trinh’s cooking than the intrinsic qualities of the meat itself.

            We had a lot of people at the Dream House last night. Most of the Freehold showed up to celebrate with us, and the food went over really well. I did get up on stage once to perform a single number… I couldn’t really generate any glamour from it, since the vast majority of our guests were changelings, but it’s expected of me whenever the Freehold gathers in a situation like that. I had Marguerite throw a blue-green spot on me, and the projector thingy threw up subtle bubble effects against the backdrop as I crooned my way through “Kiss the Girl” from The Little Mermaid. It seemed appropriate.

            So, let’s see… highlights (and low points) of the evening. Well, first of all, it didn’t take Marie-Hélène long to fixate on Missy and Jane. She swooped in and turned on the charm, and they both seemed to be reacting favorably to her advances. I’d resolved to keep my nose out of it for the time being when some sort of commotion started up in their general vicinity, and I saw Marie-Hélène menacing a bristling Carla. I made my way over to them at once, of course.

            “Do we have a problem, ladies?” I asked, my voice firm and level.

            “She’s stalking them,” Marie-Hélène growled, inclining her head toward Missy and Jane. I felt a shudder go up my spine involuntarily as I considered the implications.

            “I was not!” Carla shot back.

            “You were going to follow Missy into the bathroom,” Marie-Hélène said, her voice rising.

            “I… had to go?” Carla returned without much conviction.

            “Okay, ladies, cool your jets,” I said, placing myself between them. “Carla, I’d like to speak with you for a moment. Marie-Hélène, I’ll be right back. Okay?”

            Carla followed me to another corner of the room, looking sheepish. “What was that all about?” I asked.

            “They just… I thought they were interesting. They look… tasty.” She seemed to realize what she’d said, and backpedaled with her hands up in front of her. “Metaphorically! I wasn’t going to eat them! I wasn’t going to hurt them, I promise…”

            I sighed. “Look, I know instinct is a powerful thing,” I said in a soothing voice. “It just… might be best if you kept your distance for now. Those two haven’t been back from Arcadia for very long, and they’re still a little skittish. And they’re very important to me. I don’t want to see anything happen to them…”

            She nodded. “Okay, I understand,” she said. “I’ll back off. And… sorry if I spooked them. This is a great party. Thanks for inviting me…”

            “Carla,” I said, taking her shoulder in a gentle grip, “you’re always welcome here. I’m not mad, I promise. I know how powerful those instincts can be.”

            “Thanks,” she said softly.

            I headed back to Marie-Hélène’s table and spoke with her briefly as well. I wasn’t flexing my muscles or anything; just making sure she understood that my two best friends were still relatively newly liberated, and that they still had a few triggers. The Belgian rocker promised to handle them with care, and admitted that Carla might have been set off by Missy, who responded to her first sight of the catgirl with a frightened shriek.

            At different points in the evening, both Trinh (who I believe ended up spending the evening with Rosalita) and Julia expressed to me how adorable they thought the romantic interplay between John Lowjack and Captain Jack was. Julia was particularly vehement in her declaration that “we have to set those two up.” Despite her attempts to appear otherwise, she’s definitely the most romantic member of our motley. My little Gothic Yenta.

            Brightstar approached me as I crossed the floor, as ebullient as always. “Sorry I haven’t been around much, babe,” she said after an enthusiastic opening kiss. “I know you’ve been busy with your Queen gig, and I’ve been spending time with my boyfriend, but I don’t want you to think that I don’t want to spend time with you…”

            She glanced over her shoulder at the table I’d been heading toward, and caught sight of Joan and Andromeda. “You’ve got plans tonight,” she said, not crestfallen but resigned.

            I nodded. “I made plans with those two earlier this week,” I explained.

            She smiled brightly. “Well… let’s make plans for us now,” she said. Nothing brings her down. “What’s the next non-weekend night you’ll be free?”

            I thought about it. “You could join us for movie night on Monday,” I said after a moment.

            She shook her head. “Thing is… it’s been a while since I’ve seen one of your shows…”

            “Tuesday night it is,” I promised. “I’ll add it to my calendar and save the date. And honey… thanks for being such a good sport about this. I know it’s not always easy…”

            She waved it off. “I knew what I was getting into,” she said simply. We shared another few passionate kisses, and then she took her leave.

            There’s one more conversation I need to detail before I move on to my evening with Joan and Andromeda. At some point I glanced at the balcony and saw my father sharing a table with Tanner. This set off alarm bells in my head like nothing else, and I made my way up to them as quickly as I could do so without being rude to the other guests.

            Dad gave me an enormously amused grin as I approached. “Miss Tanner and I have been speaking, Lorelei,” he said, and I knew that he knew.

            “Someone told me that he was a really good listener, and might be able to help me,” Tanner muttered under her breath. Then she looked at me, eyes wide. “In my defense, I didn’t know he was your father…”

            I smiled and took her hand in mine. “It’s okay, Tanner,” I said, “you haven’t done anything wrong. And if Dad can help you, then… I’m glad you told him. It’s just… massively embarrassing for me…”

            “Yes, I imagine it would be,” Dad interjected. He didn’t seem angry or upset, so I’m not entirely sure how he felt about learning that his only daughter is still one of the biggest sluts in town. Maybe he was just holding it together and remaining calm for Tanner’s benefit. I know we’re going to have to revisit the topic the next time we’re alone, but… at that moment I couldn’t get away from him fast enough.

            “I’ll leave you two to your talk,” I said quickly. “I’ll… I’ll see you later, Dad.”

            “Yes,” he replied. I looked into his eyes, and I could tell that the old bastard knew how uncomfortable I was and that he was enjoying it on some level. I guess some things never change.

            So Joan and Andromeda were treated to the sight of me blushing crimson and stammering my way through our greetings, which is something not a lot of people have seen lately. They managed to pry the story out of me eventually; Joan was sympathetic and Andromeda darkly amused.

            And this entry has already gone on too long. As I prepared to leave with my paramours, I stopped by the door to make sure my wife was okay. She shrugged noncommittally when I asked what she had planned for the night, and then indicated that she was probably going to amuse herself by throwing heavy metal objects at T.J.

            “Save tomorrow night for me?” I asked in a whisper. “And maybe Marguerite and Julia?”

            “Sounds perfect,” she said, “if we can keep Missy and Jane out of their room… you’ve picked up on the fact that Missy really likes Julia, right?”

            I nodded. “I think it’s a good thing, as long as Jane’s okay with it. Honestly, my fondest dream is that someday the six of us will settle down together somewhere. I know we’re not there yet, but…”

            “Someday, Jules,” Maria said with a fond smile. “Maybe someday.”

            So that was on my mind as I took my leave and drove across town to Joan and Andromeda’s place. The night started out playful, and I had to endure a lot of ribbing from Andromeda… but things turned tender quickly enough. I love those two. I really do. But as I scribble in this diary by the light of a soothing scented candle and glance at them on their big double bed across the room, I kind of want my own family. I can’t wait for tonight.

            Lorelei de la Rocha


            Jacob’s Journal

            Today was definitely not an ordinary day. It was Thursday, so Calamity, the others and I all got ready for therapy. I think the therapy is doing good things for the two newest members of our motley. They seem much happier and have plans for a future here. It is a long way from being a flight risk back to Arcadia. The only difference on the therapy front was Calamity decided to “Irish” up her coffee before going to meet her therapist. Good thing I was the one driving. I got the impression that she was finally going to tell her doctor about what happened with Spidertoes and wanted the extra “fortification” that alcohol provides. Anyway, we all went to therapy and talked to our doctors about whatever was on our minds. I didn’t have much to talk to Sullivan about. Things had been fairly quiet lately. No emergencies or crises for us to deal with. The whole thing with the videoed ritual seems to have fallen by the wayside. I thought that we were going to go looking in the underground tunnels, but I haven’t heard anything new about that lately. Maybe I’ll talk to Stasia about it when she gets back to town.

            So, the interesting thing that happened today was not something I would have even guessed about. Lori got a call from Lowjack while she was in L.A. and he had some news about the sea monster that had been spotted in the bay. So, she called a motley meeting and discussed what was going on. The sea serpent, or Cecil as Lori calls it, has not been causing any trouble but it seems that something might have followed it through the gate from Arcadia. A giant crab. Yup, that’s what I wrote. A giant crab. Lori has named this one Sebastian after the crab in the Little Mermaid. So, after discussing things at the Dream House for a bit we met up with the group that Lowjack had put together on short notice. I didn’t pay close attention to everyone that was there, but there were a good number of Freehold members present. Jolie and Ginny Green were going to go into the water and try and lure Cecil back through the gate. Calamity and I went on Rowan’s boat with De la Rocha, Insincere Marguerite and the two water aspected among us. Jolie offered to give one of us the ability to breathe underwater for 45 minutes to an hour if we trusted her.                                                         I found Calamity in her room with Becky (who glared a few daggers at me as I knocked and entered). “Look,” I said with a sigh, “I’m sorry, okay. I shouldn’t have mentioned Spidertoes when your own encounter with her was so fresh. I wasn’t thinking.”

But it was still thoughtless of me, and I need you to know that I’m sorry about that. You’re my motley-mate, my sister, and my friend, and I love you. I didn’t mean to make things difficult for you.”

                                                        “Okay,” she said after a pause. “Thank you.” And that was that.

                                                        The show last night went well enough for a Tuesday. I chose my solo with Brightstar in mind, since we were planning to spend the evening together. It was a dance number from the 90s called “Unbelievable” by a group called EMF, and it was… vigorous. I think the audience liked it, though. Marguerite, Calamity, Missy and Jane all pulled out songs they’d done before. I think they’re doing familiar numbers so they can save their A-game for our big ensemble performance this weekend. “Feel Good Inc,” by Gorillaz. It’s gonna rock.

                                                        The redhead brigade was present and accounted for… by which I mean Jolie, Desiree, and Veronica (I’m sure lots of people see her as a redhead). I stopped by their table early in the evening to chat and found out a little more about what had happened to upset Jolie the night before. Apparently she went out to dinner with Felicity. Our neighbor believed that Jolie was attempting to reconnect on a romantic level, and when she found out that Jolie wasn’t interested (“I mean I would have slept with her,” she hedged) she became upset. Both parties said a few things they now regret, and chardonnay was thrown in faces. I tried to offer what comfort I could, but Desiree already had that well in and. Veronica mostly just looked uncomfortable.

                                                        I stopped by the bar briefly to say hello to Halley and Molly – who I presume is still keeping an eye on Veronica – but since Tamara wasn’t there last night I didn’t linger too long. Brightstar had been patiently waiting for me to finish my rounds, and I didn’t want to delay our joyous reunion any longer.

Though I make the trip into L.A. at least twice a week these days, that’s during the morning for classes, and I rarely have the time or opportunity to see her then. Our dates and trysts have to be carefully scheduled in advance (due, I know, mostly to my own full social calendar), but she never complains.

                                                        Each of my girls is unique in their own way, and I get something different out of my relationships with each of them. In Brightstar’s case, I think it’s her boundless energy and optimism. I’ve hardly ever seen her down, and even on those rare occasions when she does get depressed, she still does her all to make sure that nobody else is affected by her ennui. Just being in her presence is enough to lift my spirits, and her energy is infectious.

                                                        She gave me a brilliant smile as I approached, and I knew that I was in for a good night. We talked animatedly while the theater emptied, and I’m sure we sounded for all the world like a couple of high school cheerleaders gossiping and giggling in the cafeteria. It was fun feeling like I was allowed to cut loose and laugh like that in public. And I knew that Brightstar wouldn’t judge me or think less of me for my behavior.

It’s obvious that she’s uncomfortable discussing it; the few times we’ve even obliquely approached the subject she’s gotten tense and nervous. She’s known my name for months – at least since my wedding, if she was paying attention – but she never calls me anything other than Lori. Regardless, unless and until she chooses to share her original name with me, she’ll continue to be my Brightstar.

                                                        Nope, didn’t sleep at all last night, but I expected that. My lover kept me busy and occupied until the sun was just beginning to rise, by which point it was time for me to get showered and ready for my online courses. I let Brightstar drift off to sleep and covered her with a sheet (nice for some, I suppose), and then headed to the media room to work.

Unless something drastic changes before May, I’m probably going to ace my French Lit class. Civics doesn’t come quite as naturally to me, but it’s no problem as long as I keep up with the reading and pay close attention to the professor’s recorded lectures. Organic Chem and Bio are a snap thanks to my acquisition of the Chirurgeon kith; I just need to make sure I attend all of the lab sessions, turn in all of the assignments, and double-check the work of some of my less diligent lab partners.

                                                        Calculus is going to be my Achilles heel this semester. I’m passing it – I’m pretty sure I have a mid-level B average at the moment – but that’s only because I’m working hard at it; I have no particular aptitude for the work. Ideally I’d like to pull my grade up by the time the final rolls around. Not so much because I want straight As – the Dean’s List would be a nice feather in the cap, but I don’t need that sort of acclaim to validate what I’m doing – but because I’d like a safety net just in case I completely choke when it comes to the final exam (and that’s a real concern… my confidence is not at its highest when it comes to math). The larger problem is that I don’t know if I can pull my grade higher than a B… I feel like I’m working my delectable ass off as it is. I don’t know if I can devote any more time to studying advanced mathematics without sacrificing in some other area.

                                                        Well, all I can do is my best.

                                                        Sex Breakfast was largely uneventful this morning, and the afternoon is shaping up to be the same. So I’m studying Calculus. Because I’m not going to waste this second chance the world has given me. That’s right. I’m spending a rare free afternoon – four days before my birthday, for crying out loud – studying fucking Calculus. Back in the 70s, Dad never would have believed it was possible. But then again… a lot of what’s going on in my life today didn’t seem possible back then.

                                                        Lorelei de la Rocha

                                                            Lorelei’s Diary

                                                            I suppose it’s only natural that I start this new chapter of my life by starting a new diary. That’s right, today is Monday, March 21st, and as of last night I am officially the sitting monarch of the Lady by the Sea Freehold. Mistress Lorelei Grace de la Rocha, First of Her Name, et cetera. Look on my works, ye mighty, and despair.

                                                            So I guess that was pretty abrupt. I know it’s been a while since I last wrote anything, but I ran out of space in the old book just before my birthday and… well, you know how it is. I didn’t want to buy another journal in case someone gave me one for my birthday, and then they didn’t, and I’ve been so busy with school and meetings and running the Dream House and what-have-you that I didn’t really get around to buying one until a few days ago. And like I said, well… it’s been a busy few days.

                                                            So my birthday (calendar-wise my fifty-sixth, by my driver’s license my twenty-third, and by actual passage of time from my point of view somewhere around my twenty-first) came and went without too much hoopla this time around. Oh, my friends and family threw me a party of course, and my nearest and dearest were all in attendance, but it didn’t have the scale or grandeur of last year’s affair… and that’s fine with me, as it’s just what I wanted in any case. It’s hard to get people to come to large-scale social events on Valentine’s Day anyway.

                                                            I thought things were going to be even calmer than they turned out to be when Calamity brought forth my birthday cake… a normal-sized cake this time, without anywhere near enough room for anyone to jump out of it. Imagine my surprise when, as I approached with the knife, Desiree leaped from a concealed chamber within, having miniaturized herself with Contracts! Nearly gave me a cardiac episode, let me tell you! Calamity was quick to mollify me with the real cake… double-chocolate with cream cheese icing. Yum!

                                                            I got quite a few nice gifts – mostly clothing – but honestly the best gift was having so many people there confirming that they care about me. Aside from the motley, all of my regular lovers were there – Oleander, Brightstar, Joan & Andromeda and the like – though some of them had to leave a little early so that they could be with their other significant others. I didn’t mind; D, Marguerite, Julia, Missy and Jane made sure that I had enough to occupy my attention for the remainder of the evening.

                                                            And speaking of the six of us… things quieted down in the Freehold over the next couple of weeks, and we were actually able to take that vacation Marguerite proposed! And what’s even better, we didn’t have to drag one of my clavigers along for security! Becky agreed that as long as I stayed with D, I’d be as safe as I could be, and she sort of swore my wife in as a “temporary claviger” for that week only. So it really was just the six of us.

                                                            It was a wonderful vacation, and I made sure Marguerite knew exactly how thankful I was to her for having the idea in the first place and making the arrangements. A whole week for the six of us to just hang out, enjoy each other’s company, and get to know each other (or in some cases, reacquaint ourselves with each other). And yes, we did make love with some degree of frequency and in many combinations… but that wasn’t all we did by any means.

                                                            I wish I could say the rest of my time has been spent in such pleasant pursuits. The Dream House continues to turn a tidy profit day by day; the novelty of our little cabaret might have worn off in the area, but we can still pack in the crowds thanks to an abundance of top-notch regular acts. Each of the motley has debuted a few new numbers, but by and large we’ve been taking things sort of slowly and focusing on other pursuits.

                                                            Like college. My classes proceed apace, and I think I’ve managed to drag my Calculus grade up to an A-, but I’m not going to rest on my laurels. We’re about halfway through the semester now, and I have to keep reminding myself that I’ll be done with Calculus for good in another two months. My other classes are actually kind of fun… French Lit is giving me the chance to read a few things I’d always meant to read but never got around to, while the science classes have proven to be a great help already to my healing abilities.

                                                            And then there have been the meetings. Oh yes, I’ve been having a lot of meetings with different folks over the past few weeks, all ostensibly to prepare me for the burden of the monarchy. After I scheduled the third meeting, I voluntarily called a fourth meeting – this one with Jolie – just to prepare me for the others!

                                                            Okay, so my first meeting wasn’t so bad. Kimberly and I got together about a month ago to begin planning for the transfer of power itself. Jolie used to hold that ceremony at Glamour Springs, and she offered to let us continue that tradition… but in the end I thought it might be better to start my reign in my own place of power, to let people know symbolically that I wasn’t simply taking direction from my predecessor. So we arranged to close the Dream House on the 20th for a private function. Kimberly was good enough to arrange the details like the catering, the band, and my dress. More on that in due course.

                                                            I met privately with Jolie before meeting the other monarchs for my debriefings. Both Augustine and Lowjack wanted to go over a few details vital to the defense of the Freehold, and each had told me that I was free to bring advisors to those meetings, but cautioned me to keep the circles small. I thought that Jolie would be able to give me a few pointers in that area (or at least tell me who she tended to trust with such information). Her advice was helpful if not particularly surprising… as her chief aide-de-cap, Dark Kimberly was privy to the details of the military defense of the Freehold, and as the Spring Court’s chief espionage agent, Davie tended to sit in on her meetings with the Freehold’s spymaster.

                                                            I did bring Kimberly along to my meeting with Viscount Augustine, though there really isn’t much to tell about that. Augustine laid out what the Summer Court is doing to patrol both the local Hedge and the mortal lands in and around the Freehold, and asked me if there were any changes I’d like to make while I was sitting the throne. I gave him a watery smile before I replied.

                                                            “I’m no tactician, Viscount,” I said softly, “and you and your court have ably protected the Freehold for years. I trust your judgment on these matters. Please continue your usual routines, and keep me informed if the situation changes.” He agreed to do so, and that was that. Before we said our goodbyes, he reiterated his opinion that it would be nice to have another monarch with good Christian values in the Freehold, and I thanked him for the sentiment.

                                                            The meeting with Lowjack was more complicated. I did invite Davie to sit in with us, but I also invited a few other members of the motley… namely Jacob, Luna, Calamity, and Marguerite. Julia wasn’t interested in attending, D was afraid she’d react badly if she heard something from John she didn’t like, and… I love Missy and Jane dearly, but they’re still very new to the Freehold, and I didn’t think it was appropriate to bring them in on our darkest secrets just yet.

                                                            Before the meeting I spoke privately with those of the motley who would be attending. “You might hear some things… things I’ve been keeping from you,” I began solemnly. “I want you all to know that I trust you with my life, but there’s more at stake here than just us. I’m not going to ask you to make any pledges or anything like that, but I want you to understand how very important it is that nothing we discuss leaves that room.” They all agreed (some more readily than others).

                                                            Lowjack took in my entourage with a tight frown, but didn’t complain that I was inviting the rest of the motley into the loop. I think he may have edited what he was saying so that particularly sensitive information wasn’t discussed, though. When I asked about the war effort, for example, he told me that nothing had really changed and there was no reason to update my previous debriefing.

                                                            He did want to talk about Veronica, though. He hasn’t been able to learn anything concrete about her background, but has been able to discredit just about everything she’s volunteered about her past. He put forth a variety of theories, including the possibility that she’s associated with some sort of official espionage agency. Apparently Midwestern accents are particularly easy to emulate for spies, and it’s possible that her old hand wounds could be the result of torture… though I personally doubt the latter. I’ve seen the look in her eye whenever her hands become the center of attention, and I have no doubt that she got those wounds in some way related to her Durance.

                                                            I don’t know what to do about this situation. I like Veronica, and even John admits that he doesn’t think she means the Freehold any direct harm. It seems likely that she’s concealing her identity as a means to hide from some outside force, whether Arcadian or mortal… but I can’t afford to take that chance. After thinking it over, I sighed, pinched the bridge of my nose, and told John to continue active surveillance on Veronica for the time being, and to keep me posted with weekly updates.

                                                            We also spoke briefly about Tanner, but John wasn’t so worried about her. She’s an enigma to be sure… her own memories and various clues derived from her looks and her attitude would seem to indicate that she was Taken in the 80s or 90s, but that doesn’t jive with what we’ve learned about her Fetch’s death… unless the dead woman at that motel in L.A. wasn’t Tanner’s Fetch. I put forward the possibility that it might have been her daughter, though – without descending into crudity – my own examinations of her body don’t seem to indicate that she’s given birth. Her Fetch’s daughter, perhaps? I understand that it’s rare for fetches to procreate, but not unheard of.

                                                            Tanner doesn’t seem like a threat to me, and John agrees. We’re going to continue digging into her mysterious past… but mostly because she wants to uncover it. So that’s something I don’t necessarily have to worry about for the moment, which is, I’ll admit, a bit of a relief.

                                                            I made a decision after John left. Before the meeting I’d resigned myself to the possibility that the rest of the motley might learn some of the Freehold’s best-kept secrets, and that they’d know that I’d been keeping things from them. Then, John didn’t actually cover any of our top-secret war plans in his debriefing. As I felt all of that weight landing solely on my shoulders once again, I realized that I didn’t actually have to bear it alone. I’m the Queen… and one of the reasons I accepted that mantle was because I knew I had a good support network built up around me.

                                                            So yes, I told Jacob, Calamity, Luna, and Marguerite about the Four Horsemen, and the projects they’re undertaking on our behalf. People took the information in stride, though it was obvious that everyone was affected by the news in one way or another. I almost broke down when I talked about Oleander’s plan to deliver her toxin personally, but I managed to hold it together (mostly). Jacob and Calamity still don’t like Anton (I’m glad in retrospect that Maria wasn’t in on this meeting), but they’ve both agreed not to do anything which might distract him while he’s working on an alternate delivery mechanism with Oleander. They were all so reassuring, trying to convince me that it won’t be necessary for me to give the order for Oleander to…

                                                            You know what, I don’t want to talk about this anymore.

                                                            So… the inauguration. I’ll get the single dark cloud out of the way first: Calamity wasn’t there. She actually approached me a few days before the ceremony itself and told me that she wouldn’t be there. And why did one of my best friends opt to skip one of the most important days of my life? Because she was convinced she wouldn’t be able to summon up the modicum of self-control it would take to politely avoid Madame Spidertoes for a couple of hours.

                                                            I tried to gently reason with her on this, to point out that sooner or later she’s going to have to interact with Spidertoes, but she was having none of it. She was utterly convinced that if she and the Madame were both present, she’d do something to spoil my day. While I am privately convinced that she’s just running from her problems and using her respect for my position as a shield to hide her fear, I know Calamity well enough to never bring that opinion up in her presence.

                                                            So instead of attending the inauguration, she opted to hit the town with Stasia… who was incidentally one of the very few mortals I had invited to the inauguration. With her gone, the non-changeling attendance was down to my Dad and Elena. As far as changelings went, almost every notable member of the Freehold was in attendance, as well as a couple of notable outsiders like the Unseelie Court motley. The big surprise came when Jimmy Squarefoot and Peg Powler walked through the front door! I might have squealed just a little as I rushed across the floor to give them both big hugs. But if so, I’m sure I did it in a regal, dignified fashion.

                                                            I kept things classy for the party and the inauguration itself. The band played light jazz mostly, and there was good food, copious wine, and sedate dancing. I did take up the microphone once or twice over the course of the evening… my constituents expected it, and when have I ever shied away from the spotlight in any case? I didn’t channel a lot of glamour into my performances, though; there didn’t seem to be much point since there were so few mortals present. I just used a contract to enhance my beauty a bit, because… well, I suppose because I’m pretty vain when you get right down to it, and I wanted to look my best for the ceremony.

                                                            There’s not a whole lot to tell about the inauguration, actually, as I was too nervous to engage in any truly meaningful conversations for most of the evening. I said hello to everyone, of course… even Darke, though that conversation was pretty tense and uncomfortable. And in case you’re curious, no… Dr. Bright didn’t make the ceremony, so I still haven’t seen the two of them together. At this point I think they’re doing it on purpose.

                                                            At one point I noticed that Dad – who had been wandering around pretty wide-eyed for most of the evening – talking intently with Jimmy. I didn’t want to interrupt their conversation, but he seemed to be trying to bolster my ogre friend’s courage in regards to his relationship with Peg. Good for him! Those two are obviously perfect for each other, regardless of Jimmy’s low self-esteem. Here’s hoping that this signifies better times ahead for two of my three favorite Brits (the third being Ace, wherever he is).

                                                            The main event of the evening was the passing of the torch from Winter to Spring… which is to say from John Lowjack to me. I’d had Kimberly work up a new hedge-spun dress for me over the previous few weeks, and though it wasn’t as elaborate as the one I wore at my coronation, I think it fit well with the theme of the evening. It was made of green, growing vines which twisted sinuously about my form in a way that represented both growth and desire.

                                                            D stood with me on our little stage as my Consort while I gave a short speech, thanking the Freehold for their faith in my abilities and giving my promise that I would do everything within my power to ensure their safety and to serve their best interests. I acknowledged my lack of experience – which I know is a big concern for some of my people – but tried to spin it in a positive light, reminding everyone that Spring is a time of new growth and renewal. As I said this, and the mantle of leadership settled upon me, my hedge-spun dress began to blossom, changing over the course of a few minutes from a dappled green to a riot of seasonal color. It got the reaction I’d hoped for… which is to say people gasped and applauded.

                                                            Things began to wind down after that, and our guests drifted out slowly over the next hour or so. Some members of the Freehold (having enjoyed Spring Court functions on several previous occasions) lingered after the event, expecting things to devolve into debauchery. I knew that these expectations existed, and fully intended to honor them, but I made sure that nothing untoward happened until after my Dad left the party.

                                                            The orgy that ensued wasn’t as big as the one after my coronation, but it was still big enough for me to lose myself in it for a while. It was nice to allow myself to be carried away by sensation; to let the pressures of the monarchy drain from me temporarily. I know I haven’t even had to do anything as reigning Queen yet, but the pressure is there nevertheless. Everyone expects great things from me, and… I’m just not sure I’m that girl. Not yet.

                                                            Lorelei de la Rocha
