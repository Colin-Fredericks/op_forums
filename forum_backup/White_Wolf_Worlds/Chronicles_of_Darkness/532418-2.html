        #NotEveryPilgramage

        The qashmallim and Principle are the 'black box mysteries' of Promethean, an area where weirdness prevails, in much the same way that hob/goblins in Changeling is this big ol' bag of weirdness. Each game has their known places and quantities, and then they have the "there be dragons" area.

        The qashmallim and Principle are tied to the forces at work in the Pilgramage-they have to be, because Promethean is a tight, thematically focused game. But the reasons why this sea of divine fire and it's occasionally acting drops of change get involved are not the end-all, be-all of the pilgramage. It's entirely possible something else is going on that just happens to be related to Prometheans chasing the New Dawn.

        My point, more or less, is that I don't think the way it was presented in the blog means that Qashmallim are more of a thing. They are clarified, put to purpose, but that purpose and the effects of their missions are strange and alien, made worse because they have this central uniting feature that might not actually be central at all. Qashmallim exist to cast shadows on the otherwise 'simple' world of the Pilgramage, to raise questions without answers, doubts without satiation, and fear from your characters hopes.

                I used hobgoblins a lot too, that's not quite my point. My point with the hobgoblins and the entire goblin thing is, well....not to be derogatory, I just need to say it like this, but have you seen hobgoblins?

                Of course you have, but what I mean by that is that in the general sense of changelings' narrative, hobgoblins don't fit nicely. You can't classify them the way you can Huntsmen, Strangers, and changelings, the whole motley bunch defy sense and sanity in any categorical way. I can generally place them as a primarily Going Home problem as well as a way to round out Leaving Arcadia problems, but beyond that, they are wild, unruly, and create this big space of weirdness where Changeling kind of challenges Changeling. It's all strangeness and murk with all things goblins.

                Coming back to Promethean, the qashmallim fit the same sort of box. Everything else fits into the box of Promethean's narrative 'neatly', but the qashmallim come in muddle the entire thing-the flow is broken by their presence and interference. Hell, as much as they egg on the pilgramage, their presence is one of the biggest factors to make a Promethean question whether or not chasing the New Dawn is a good thing for them or not. Before, it was a personal escap,e now it serves a purpose you're not privy too, if nothing else, and that's ignoring the nature of what they do with or to Prometheans on the individual basis.

                The difference between the hob/goblin and the qashmallim is that the weirdness has more place in the common Changeling game than it does in the Promethean game. Hob/goblin(s) just don't upset the apple cart in the same way the qashmallim do. I will always find a reason to slip that into changeling. I might go an entire game of Promethean without ever having a qashmallim.

                    Originally posted by Vithrir View Post
                    It's really simple, actually: Prometheans can't stay the way they are because Qashmallim are a bunch of total dicks who have made it the purpose of their very existence to endlessly troll Prometheans until they aren't Prometheans anymore. It's not actually about becoming human -- that the Prometheans are no longer trolled once they become human is simply a side-effect of the fact that humans aren't as fun to troll as Prometheans are -- it's because Qashmallim are easily amused, and despite apparently being super important embodiments of some important principle or another, they literally have nothing better to do than to make Prometheans miserable for their own amusement.
                    As others have pointed out, that ain't necessarily so. Sure, you can play it that way if you so choose; but by default, qashmallim simply aren't around that much.

                    I also find it interesting that at least two posters have characterized the Promethean condition in terms of outside entities harassing the Woobie “for no good reason” or “because they're dicks”, implying that there's nothing wrong with the Promethean condition itself. Forget about Torment, where the Humours that animate the Created surge out of control and drive it to destructive behaviors; forget about the fact that Wasteland is less about the land rejecting the Promethean and more about the Promethean's presence poisoning the land; and never mind that even Disquiet isn't so much about people fearing the Other as it is about Azothic radiation poisoning peoples' minds. Promethean isn't a game about misunderstood protagonists overcoming bigotry and coming to realize that they're fine as they are; it's a game about flawed protagonists coming to understand that they're flawed and fixing the flaws.

                    And with that in mind, qashmallim typically don't guide and harass Prometheans out of spite or malice; they do so to remind the Promethean that it is flawed, and to motivate it to get back to the work of correcting its flaws. I think of it more like them being agents of an absentee parent (the Principle) who provide the tough love that the Promethean needs to eventually achieve its potential.

                                        I've always seen them as story catalysts, rather than a focal point. Their mystery isn't meant to be penetrable. They serve better as signposts than they do anything else, and their Deus Ex Machina role is best kept to the barest of minimums.

                                        Here's the thing though: they are bloody awesome. Beings made of fire and inspiration, with divinity their fuel and bringing messages and prophecies from on high. Just one is enough to ratchet up a chronicle from a sedate contemplation to a Revelations-style apocalypse as you see fit, and you don't even have to contrive some sort of context, like you might for an idigam to, say, take every first-born son or turn the seas to blood.

                                        That sort of power isn't the right tool for every job, but used wisely, they show just how monumental and incredible the Pilgrimage truly is. Prometheans were born out of attempts to play God, and the Principle seems to hold that just playing counts. The Qashmallim confirm in their attentions that the Prometheans have divine value and potential, and are capable of literal miracles.

                                            So a thread on a "transhuman" (the quote marks are deliberate for reasons I'm about to elaborate on) hack just got closed, but what it did do was crystalise my thoughts on why none of these attempts have worked for me. Which is that they never actually evoke transhumanism, just a hatred of humanity.

                                            Ever heard the line, "Don't knock stone age medicine; it's the reason we survived long enough to have a bronze age?"

                                            It's been observed by various posters that you have be human before you can be transhuman, but mostly in the context of defending the Pilgrimmage as is. But it applies another way, that to be transhuman, it has to be something humans can achieve. None of the so called transhuman hacks for Promethean incorporate that - they're all ways for Prometheans to become things that are more powerful than humans, or lose all their weaknesses while keeping all their powers. In other words, just ways for the powerful to become more powerful.

                                            In the real world, if transhumanity ever comes to be, it'll be because of humanity, just like with the aforementioned medicine. So it's not enough to base a transhuman hack on "humans suck" - it has to be something human will and endeavour can attain - and that's something that fits way better with the humanism of Promethean.

                                            So here's a rough go at one that does that, which I'm going to call a Heraklean:

                                                BASELINE:

                                                A Heraklean is a human who has awakened a seed of Azoth inside them. For the moment, this is an Azoth of 0 - it exists, but it doesn't do a whole lot. Its radiance only extends to the same room, and the Heraklean cannot store Pyros inside her body - instead, she must use an external Alembic (we can assume a PC has made one of these). [I've not thought about how to render an Alembic mechanically beyond this yet]

                                                Pyros can be gathered through the same means as for a Promethean, though it's the Alembic that must be in the right place at the right time.

                                                Herakleans have Humours and Hopes. Humour works like vice, and if the Heraklean starts picking up Promethean weaknesses, characterises them the way Lineage does; it represents a major and unchanging aspect of the character's personality. Hope represents a framework for the character's aspirations, and what drives her to exceed her humanity; mechanically, it works like virtue. [Humours can be anything that would make sense for an alchemical or quasi-alchemical Humour; Hopes, I guess, would be similar to virtues and vices?]

                                                Herakleans still have Integrity.

                                                They can form Alchemical Pacts, though at this stage, there's little point.


                                                REFINEMENTS:

                                                These are the eight of the same ten as the Promethean ones, though they have different emphases, because the character has to learn different things. [Really, they should have a different name than Refinement]

                                                Each Refinement is the study of self and/or world. Aurum is of ties to humanity, Cuprum of your own mind and dreams, Ferrum of your body, Mercurius of the Fire you hope to draw further into yourself, Stannum of your impulses and instincts, Aes of your ties to other seekers on this path, Argentum of the supernatural world, Cobalus of recognising your imperfections, Phosphorum of the nature of change, and Plumbum of the physical world. [Plumbum is deliberately different: "the human condition" is covered by the others, and there ought to be a Refinement for the physical world; maybe it should also have a different name] [Also, I'm decidedly not happy with Phosphorum]

                                                Unlike Prometheans, A Heraklean's Refinements have dot values, 1-10: these are the equivalents of Pilgrimmage levels (yes, a Heraklean is on ten pilgrimmages at once, potentially - no one said this would be easy).

                                                Rather than a cost-break on Transmutations, a Heraklean can only learn those of the Refinements she has dots in.

                                                Milestones show up along the way, and provide Vitriol as normal. These can be used to buy dots in whichever Refinements are appropriate to the lesson learned in the milestone. [This is based on first edition milestones, which seem more appropriate for Herakleans than second edition ones]


                                                WEDDINGS:

                                                The baseline aspects of the Promethean condition are paired up, one strength and one weakness. My current thinking here is:

                                                Azoth goes to 1, and can increase further, allowing the Heraklean to hold Pyros inside + Wasteland. [These are the apices - is the plural of apex apices or apexes? - of the inner Fire]

                                                Transhuman potential (ie attribute buffing) + Torment. [Both represent your energy surging out of you and pushing you]

                                                Healed by electricity + Hurt by fire. [Conductivity for good or ill]

                                                Superlative Endurance/Constitution (and adding immortality) + Pandorans wanna eat ya! [As your body's substance is perfected, it becomes appetising to those who long for Azoth]

                                                Ability to perform a Generative Act + Disquiet. [Your inner Fire wants to spread to other kindling, and wears on the psyches of those around you]

                                                Each of these pairs is also associated with a pair of Refinements [though I haven't formulated thoughts on that yet]. When you feel sufficiently advanced in one of these (you don't get to add them), you can attempt a Wedding roll, similar to a New Dawn roll: on an exceptional success you get the benefit without the weakness, on a regular success you get both, on a failure you get nothing, and on a dramatic failure you get only the weakness; however it turns out, you lose all dots in that Refinement (though you can start again if you want).


                                                PROMETHEANS:

                                                Prometheans can do this as well, but they must finish a regular Pilgrimmage first: to transcend humanity, you must first attain it. When a Promethean reaches the New Dawn, rather than becoming human, she can choose to remain as she is and seek the Heraklean Weddings. This means revisiting the Refinements in light of her new insight and condition, but some of the work is done (she can distribute her old Pilgrimmage dots among any Refinements she spent time on).

                                                A Promethean on this path replaces Pilgrimmage with Integrity. Unlike a Heraklean, she can learn any sort of Transmutation, though - being less volatile - she no longer gets cost breaks. She also gets a Humour and Hope.

                                                When she's ready for a Wedding, an exceptional success eliminates the paired weakness, while a regular success or failure has no effect. A dramatic failure doesn't eliminate any of her power however - it destroys her hard-won soul, reinstating all Promethean weaknesses and returning her to the beginning of her Pilgrimmage.



                                                ADDENDUM 1; LESS BOOKKEEPING:

                                                Rather than tracking dots for all ten Refinements, you're on one and one only until you roll for your Wedding or abandon it for another (losing all your progress either way). In this case, I would link your Hope with one Refinement, in which you would keep your dots even if you spend time on another path.


                                                ADDENDUM 2; 8/10:

                                                As I said, I'm not too happy with my idea for Phosphorum. And Argentum seems less relevant here than it does for Prometheans. So maybe drop those two, and Herakleans only have eight. In this case, Ability to perform a Generative Act and Pandorans wanna eat ya! would go into the baseline, while Superlative Endurance/Constitution would now be paired with Disquiet (your body no longer perfoms quite the same give and take with the world - the absence can imbalance the psyches of people near you).
