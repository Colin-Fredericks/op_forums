                    Presumably, the same way it did before. Gravity is still there, doing the same thing. Mage treats forces (and Forces) more like liquids you can gather in a bucket and pour on people than particles in motion or molecular interactions. Otherwise, the Zoom In spell would create enormous black rings around the eyes of the Mage using it as it funnels light into a single point like a telescope, and an Invisibility spell would also make you blind.

                    Think symbolically, not physically. An area under a Ban is basically the same place it was before; cut-and-pasted into a blank white void. Lighting conditions, gravity, all that jazz, should theoretically change, but since it's symbolically the same place, I'd treat it as no different.

                                                    1. Do you mean lowering the target number? If so, no. Hell no. Hell no not in a million years no. Mummy is the only gameline that has messed with target numbers and that was a pretty bad idea then.
