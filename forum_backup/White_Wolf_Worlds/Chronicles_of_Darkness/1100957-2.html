                    Future's Blues
                    "Let's see how that works out, hmm?"
                    Background: People who visit fortune-tellers already have doubt in their hearts, a nagging feeling that something would go wrong. While some people become further frustrated when someone tells them that, yes, you are in for a rough time, others find the confirmed doom strangely relieving in that they are no longer uncertain of their future. Future's Blues feeds on the latter emotion, whispering disaster to inspire grim resolution.
                    Description: The hursah usually appears as an instinct mass of blue-grey mist, foretelling misfortune to those caught within it and encouraging them to overcome it at the same time. When called upon or provoked, it congeals into a vaguely humanoid shape with two blue will-o-wisps for eyes. It speaks calmly in a low voice, but when agitated it becomes considerably more talkative, high-pitched and mixes in several jargon, making it rather hard to understand clearly.
                    Storytelling Hints: Future's Blues is content with foretelling future crises so that people may gather the courage to face it head-on. It usually Fetters itself to a fortune-teller and whispers what it has foreseen, gathering Essence from the shock its host creates. The local Uratha and other players in the know leave the spirit be, since that is all what it does. Recently, however, the spirit has started to ponder the implications of creating misfortune and suffering, instead of merely foretelling them. It has not yet taken such actions, but its powers are growing and so does its hunger for Essence.
                    Rank: 2
                    Attributes: Power 2, Finesse 4, Resistance 3
                    Corpus: 8
                    Willpower: 7
                    Essence: 15
                    Initiative: 7
                    Defense: 2
                    Speed: 11 (species factor 5)
                    Size: 5
                    Influences: Misfortune •, Determination •
                    Manifestations: Twilight Form, Discorporate, Fetter
                    Numina: Omen Trance, Innocuous, Seek, Sign
                    Ban: Future's Blues cannot use its Influences, Manifestations, or Numina on subjects under supernatural fortune or misfortune.
                    Bane: Anyone or anything that has been enhanced with supernatural luck.

                        Tideteller
                        "Pfft, what you mean with "too many fishmen"? Don't be silly"
                        Background: Born from the awe, wonder and insignificance the sight of the ocean provokes to human minds, Tideteller is a odd little spirit that feeds on stories and ideas. It spends most of its existence underwater, lurking beneath the waves and listening to what others have to say, entertaining itself with their tales. Sure as the tides go, it will eventually follow his nature and emerge in order to share its own stories. Tideteller embodies inspiration coming from the sea, but stories have to be told in order for it to be meaningful. During these periods ashore, Tideteller talks with everyone willing to listen. Its tales are strange, a little bit too self-absorbed and they feature sea monsters and abyssal horrors so frequently there's an overall lack of variety, but the spirit tells them anyways. Tideteller is desperate for a public but, even when it finds one, its nature don't allow it to feel like he has done a good job, regardless if this time was the case or not. Soon, it will feel the call of the ocean and return to it, as if each of its adventure on the main land make it feel out of place. Following once again the turning of the tides, the spirit will plunge back into the sea, starting the cycle all over again.
                        Description: Tideteller looks like a nasty hybrid between a man and an oceanic creature. It has two big, black, glassy eyes, sharp teeth, gills on his neck, elonged limbs and its skin resembles that of a fish instead of that of a man. It often carries a drenched book of some kind, claiming that's the one it is reading right now. It's a blatant lie: the book is too damaged for anyone to read it and, if asked to talk about it, Tideteller will just made up another lie, creating a little plot on the spot. The spirit seems an anxious one, as it spends each moment ashore skittering and breathing with difficulty. It's evident it struggles to remain out of the water and that it is deathly afraid those it speaks with won't like it or its stories.
                        Storytelling Hints: Tideteller is the spirit you want to go meet if you want to waste some time, but it is does not mean bad. It merely wants to hear more stories and share the one it creates, hoping they' ll mean to others what they mean to him. Still, it can be a useful spirit, since all it does is to listen to what's going on. With little effort, perhaps by giving it attention, criticism and a tiny bit of adulation, Tideteller will share what it knows. Just don't debate with it over the awesomeness of tentacled monsters: that's not a topic it is willing to let go.
                        Rank: 2
                        Attributes: Power 3, Finesse 4, Resistance 2
                        Corpus: 7
                        Willpower: 6
                        Essence: 15
                        Initiative: 7
                        Defense: 3
                        Speed: (species factor 5)
                        Size: 5
                        Influences: Ocean •, Inspiration •
                        Manifestations: Twilight Form, Discorporate, Image, Fetter
                        Numina: Awe, Dement, Innocuous, Seek, Sign
                        Ban: Tideteller loses its Defense and cannot use its Numina or Influences on anyone who disagrees with him over the value of stories about the ocean and/or monsters with piscine features.
                        Bane: A written criticism about one of its stories. Extremely brief ones, like a "Meh, I don't like it", do count already

                            Gentle-Pusher
                            "Listen, I’m very sorry for what happened to you, but it’s time to move on, don’t you think?"

                            Background: It is often hard to stand up for oneself and confronting one’s problems head on without coming across as a insensitive, misguided, opinionated and a bunch of other negative words. We can become afraid to say what we really feel for fearing what others think. Out of this emotional struggle between politeness and confrontation arises a conflict of Resonances which creates Magaths like Gentle-Pusher.

                            Description: Gentle-Pusher has two sides. When attempting to be spread politeness, it appears as a deer with a third eye on its forehead, hooves and horns like crystals and fur like glowing tentacles. Its voice is calm and measured, if rather verbose. When trying to make someone confront their problems, it appears as a bull with no eyes, a massive under jaw with protruding fangs, and hooves and horns made of iron instead of crystals. Its voice turns low and serious, and it becomes a lot less verbose.

                            Storytelling Hints: Gentle-Pusher is always seeking people who are having trouble coping with their problems, and offers advice on how to solve them. However, it has a somewhat narrow idea of the right way to approach most conflicts, and will insist that its targets handle their crises in a calm and civilized manner even under duress. On the bright side, Gentle-Pusher is a lot more empathic than most spirits, and will willingly listen to someone’s problems regardless of who they are. While it can warn others of danger and even empower them toward victory, it still has the single-mindedness of other spirits, and will pester and manipulate people who might not be ready to face their struggles just yet, or have legitimate reasons to not be polite.

                            Rank: 2
                            Attributes: Power 3, Finesse 5, Resistance 4
                            Corpus: 10
                            Willpower: 6
                            Max Essence/Per Turn: 20/6
                            Initiative: 5
                            Defense: 4
                            Speed: 22 (Species Factor 15)
                            Size: 6
                            Influences: Politeness ••, Confrontation ••
                            Manifestations: Twilight Form, Discorporate, Materialize
                            Numina: Implant Mission, Omen Trance, Mortal Mask, Occult Sight*, Sign, Price Of Power x 1

                            Ban 1: Cannot use violence to solve a conflict unless someone else uses it first.

                            Ban 2: Cannot use Numina or Influences on anyone who has not apologized or worked toward an Aspiration in the past 24 hours.

                            Bane: A weapon coated in the blood or tears of someone who lost Integrity for following its advice.

                            *Occult Sight: The entity is sensitive to supernatural phenomena. The entity notices the active use of supernatural abilities within the range of its senses and may make a Finesse roll to approximate its distance and direction. In addition, by spending a point of Essence, it can see through powers of concealment, illusion and deceit with a Clash of Wills using its Finesse + Rank as its die pool. Lastly, it may mimic the Aura Reading Supernatural Merit using Finesse + Rank, but without drawback.
