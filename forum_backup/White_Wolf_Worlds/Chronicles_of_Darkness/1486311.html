    Updating "Immortal: The Gathering" for CofD.

    07-12-2022, 06:53 PM
    So sometime ago someone on the boards was trying to updated this fan splat for "Highlander"-style immortales in what was then the NWOD: https://web.archive.org/web/20140313...es_Revised.pdf

    And here is the thread for those who are curious: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...tal-in-the-gmc

    That thread seems to have been abandoned which is a shame as I always like the OWOD fan-splats for Immortals and the one for NWOD has one of my favorite machanics for immoral healing.

    For those unaware, there are a few differences between Immortals in the Movies and the TV series, which some of the older fan-works tried to blend in. For example:
    Film
    1. Do not experience ‘First Death’. Connor Macleod never actually died even when initially stabbed by the Kurgan “and last night you were all but a corpse” were the words uttered by one of his clansmen. At some point in their lives the immortality gene ‘kicks in’, and with it certain properties.

    2. Do not suffer temporary death o any kind. No matter what kind of damage Connor suffered he automatically got up straight afterwards – getting shot by Nazi he awoke immediately telling the young Rebecca to be quiet; and stabbing himself in the heart to show Brenda his immortality.

    3. Heal instantly from any wound, including severed limbs. It is never stated in the film they do not, plus over several thousand years of sword fights you’ll lose a limb or two.

    4. Are capable of absorbing huge amounts energy, this is shown in the film where Connor is sent to hilltop to absorb lightning strikes. The roof sign had fallen and electrified the water while both immortals continued to fight and were seen to absorb the electricity.

    5. Slightly stronger than normal humans. For instance when Connor was in the police station, he was surrounded, one officer grabbed his arm. Martial ability aside, Connor just raised his arm to shake the officer off which resulted in the police officer letting go and tumbling across the room.

    6. Have a connection to every living thing, man or animal.


    Highlander Immortals TV Series

    1. First Death. In order for immortality to trigger they must experience a violent death and this can be at any age although usually events conspire to make the new immortal young.

    2. Immortals can sense Pre-Immortals, Duncan ad Richie.

    3. Heal from any wound except severed limbs. Xavier St Cloud was forced to attach a hook.

    4. Should they suffer massive trauma, or fatal damage for a normal human they will suffer temporary death; during this time their body goes into dormancy healing the damage.

    5. Cannot absorb energy like the film version and are susceptible to temporary death by electrocution.

    The NWOD FanSplat for immortals had something that acted as both a balance and a way to move between these extremes. See the healing rules on p. 40 for details.
    Basicaly an immortal here heals 1B of damage every minute and 1L every hour and 1A every day (well it doesn't say that, but it is a reasonable assumption on my part). When in the presence of another immortal (like say for a duel) or when using a point of Edge (their name for the supernatural fuel here), the immortal heals 1B every turn, 1L every minute, and 1A every hour. If that isn't enough, the immortal can spend 1 Edge to remove any single wound no matter the level.

    Again I find this to be a nice compromise between how healing was portrayed in the original film and the TV series.

    Other things I liked:

    On p. 21 we get a description of the Power Stat, Kenning, and I must say, the fluff on it is well done.

    However, this was made for the original NWOD, and now since we are in CofD, I think some things need an upgrade. For example, alot of the Immortal's "tricks" in the original seem to only work on other immortals. These were based on how Immortals in the franchise could sense each other, but only each other, and influence them. For this upgrade, we could have more like the original film and have these tricks work on other living creatures (though that means Vamps and Emphermals are immune).

    Any other ideas?

          I would say it would be a good idea to look at World of Darkness: Immortals for a reference on how to codify mechanically and to cross reference when fine tuning balance.

          In this case specifically, one major thing is missing is the price. Immortality must always have a price. The Blood Bathers, for example, need to completely bathe in blood from time to time, with more complex and inflexible conditions for the ritual granting more supernatural advantages. Body Thieves can only stay in the same body for so long before starting to lose their grip on it, some rely on mystical baubles to jump hosts, they activate all their abilities with Willpower. The Purified are able to reassemble themselves after death and get spirit powers, but can be eaten by the denizens of the Shadow like anyone else (I think they might be the closest thing to what you want). There are more but I think you get the idea.

          The Highlanders don't seem to suffer a downside to their abilities. Their regeneration is second only to werewolves, what resource fuels it ? What bane can shut it down ? They return from death, what can kill them for good ? I would recommend decapitation, translated into taking more lethal or aggravated damage on a called head shot than their Stamina rating. Now snipers will make them nervous.

          There is no need for the Buzz, unless you want them to hunt each other. Then a reward could be 2 XP that can only be used to buy dots in skill the defeated Highlander had. Buzz radius could be Wits x 10 meters.

          Lastly, I would recommend making the resource that fuels their active powers be something that they have to go out of their way to replenish, something that could get them in trouble or make them cross paths with other supernaturals.
