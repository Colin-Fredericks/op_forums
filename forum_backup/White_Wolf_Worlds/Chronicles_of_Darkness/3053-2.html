                                                            On the first two, I’m hoping that the Promethean Dark Era will be far enough back in time to justify both — at least in part. If it’s set prior to the 19th century, it will predate both electricity as a technology and Frankenstein: the former would all but require an overhaul of the Electrification Transmutations, and the latter would require addressing the lack of the Wretched Lineage.

                                                            More generally though, I agree about having something of a split between Lineage and Humour. Drawing a loose analogy to Vampire, I like to think of Humours as being like Clans: broad, general archetypes of the different sorts of Prometheans that exist; whereas Lineages should be a bit more like Bloodlines: specialized cases of the general archetypes that the Humours represent. The main difference would be that Lineages would be mandatory (like Changeling’s Kiths) rather than optional (like Vampire’s Bloodlines). Put another way, Humours would have a timeless quality to them — you should get the sense that the five Humours have been around as long as Prometheans have existed, and may in fact reflect innate qualities of the Divine Fire itself — whereas Lineages are something with distinct lifespans: you can point to the first Frankenstein monster, and if the last Promethean in a Lineage dies before creating progeny, the Lineage itself dies. The last Frankenstein won’t be the last Choleric; but the next Choleric will be created using a different generative act and will manifest the Choleric Humour in a different way than the Frankensteins did. (Unless someone rediscovers Frankenstein’s notes and replicates his feat closely enough to resurrect the Frankenstein Lineage.)
