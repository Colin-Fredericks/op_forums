    One stolen slave and 3 stolen tokens

    09-23-2019, 10:11 PM
    I'm looking for a little help with my tokens. For a short synopsis of what has happened in the style of Overly Sarcastic Productions.
    So! yeah this happened in a game I'm in we started off with it being innocents and then fast forward 15 years Iron Side.
    So as a Child Jason was abused by his mother and found himself going into the local haunted/abandoned house, after having dreams about it for months. He and 5 others were all tributes sacrificed to a powerful Gentry who goes by Soldat for short. Full name is in russian and I don't speak that but it roughly translates to Winter Soldier. Anyway...as a kid he's grabbed, made into a "werewolf knight" and over time given others made into wolves and knights/warriors. Eventually he's even trusted enough to go into the house to help recruit to fill his army.

    Meanwhile back iron side his sister and mother turn out to be Wolf Blooded, from the blood line of the Hunters in Dark and catch on right away the Fetch isn't the real him. The mother disgards it, and the sister treats it like a step brother. Bit of fast forward and 6 years later sister is a Full Moon Aspect werewolf and got knocked up. Totem forbids harming mothers or the unborn (fertility spirit - 65 point totem...yes I have discovered this thing is basically Aphrodite now) and the child is adopted by her mother, who raises it as if they were sisters. Hiding from the baby the fact that it's sister is in reality her mother. Child is born with a Wolf Blooded gift that lets her see and be attacked by spirits. The Spirits don't like her and attack her constantly.

    Fast forward 9 years, The Gentry cultists thinks she's another abused kid and slate her to be a Halloween sacrifice for a good winter. Her real mother (ie big sis) manages to slip in by disguising herself as a pet dog while in wolf form. Once big brother shows up to lead her away to a life of adventure serving under her brother. Well he get's as far as "Hello" when the girls dog turns into a 6 foot tall amazon and proceeds to beat his knock off werewolf ass until he's somewhere between out cold and bloody pulp. She realizes who she just kicked the crap out of him, hog ties him, throws him over her shoulder like a sack of spuds and hauls him out of the house. With all his gear.

    Sadly Briar Springs has no Changeling courts. What it does have is a werewolf pack liberal enough to accept the two changelings to ever escape.

    SO! That's where we are now. Now I figure I have 3 stolen tokens on me.
    The First is the
    Lord's Mantle. Stolen Token 1
    This blue silk cape has intricate designs embroidered into it with silver threads made with moonlight and wolf howls. It embodies the rank and status of the Lord Knight, Protector of the boarders. It carries with it the power of command and authority. When the wearer gives an order he may bind his followers to obey by spending 1 glamour each, in doing so they gain a bonus 3 dice to any action that contributes to completing his orders for the day.
    Drawback: When this token activates the keeper becomes aware the changeling is still alive and how many subordinates are under them.

    Swift Protectors Carapace Stolen Token 2
    This plate armor is forged from Titanium armor from Jetfighters, kevlar from the most courageous spetznaz armor, and the sheer neigh legendary can do of an enraged Russian. The winter soldier himself forged this armor for Jason.
    The armor is a superior specimen of its kind. When the user feeds the armor enough glamor the wearer can move with unexpected and unprecedented speed! Using the better of dex or wits to figure their defense, as well as ignoring all defense and speed penalties from the armor. Tapping into the Spirit and speed of the Jet fighter in doing so. They may freely apply their defense to melee and ranged attacks with equal ease, reacting to the threat of the gun with unnatural swiftness.
    Armor value 5/3, Strength 3, Defense -2, Speed -2
    Drawback: In additionally to alerting huntsmen in the Tri City area, the wearer must come to grips with the fact their body was not built to move like that, taking bashing damage equal to 3 times the number of rounds the effect was active minus stamina.

    Moon's Wrathful Crescent Stolen Token 3
    This scythe was custom made for Jason. It's double edged blade and perfectly balanced half work well. What's more with an infusion of Faerie power its blade can cut with a force to make the old Father Wolf laugh in excitement, and mother Luna to tremble in predatory ecstasy. The blade was forged for Luna's own sighs, a discarded story of Father Wolf, alloys found deep in the heart of Arcadian wars, and not seen since the age of mages. The blade may scythe though rabble, and force weaker beings to bow. By spending a glamour the wielder may attack all foes in 2 yards/meters with a special attack of Strength+Weaponry+wyrd -stamina + supernatural tolerance.
    Normal: Do lethal damage as per a normal attack.
    Exceptional Success: Apply the arm wrack or leg wrack tilt, in addition to the damage as normal. If the damage meets or exceeds the target's stamina, the tilt is caused by the loss of the limb.
    Failure:Nothing, but the enemies may make snide remarks about feeling a breeze or being fanned.
    Dramatic Failure: Apply the roll vs. the wielder.
    Drawback: Every huntsman in the Tristate area knows your name, face, and location, and is gunning for you. Additionally after the end of any scene where the power of this weapon is used the wielder takes the swooning condition aimed at the nearest female

          Just offering some two cents here on my thought regarding these, hopefully it's helpful.

          All of these are incredibly powerful. I understand that stolen tokens are -incredibly- powerful, but that doesn't mean cheap (you do pay 1 less for them because of the side effect of automatically applying the paranoid / hunted condition on top of their drawback). I think that all the effects that effectively let huntsmen and keepers know about you seem redundant, so will try to offer other solutions for those drawbacks.

          I will offer no catches for the moment, but note that they seem to be missing. (Can probably think of some later)

          Lord's Mantle

          Lords Mantle is, as written, effectively the Fairest Blessing - with the only difference I can see being the cost being cheaper and duration being longer. If you wanted it to be 1-2 dots, I would make it 1 roll - not all rolls per scene or day. If you went with 'all rolls to achieve your roll for the duration of the scene' I would move it to 3 - but that would only be if you could use it on one individual for its duration. If it's stack-able and can be used against multiple individuals for the scene, I would move to 4 - and if it's able to effect 1 individual for an entire day... 5.

          For the drawback, I might instead do something like: The authority that the mantle offers is powerful and intoxicating. While using the power, the user feels as though they hold actual authority over the target of this token - whether they do or not. They gain the Delusion Condition, which persists for a single scene (or, in the case of it lasting a day, for 'as long as this power is active').

          Swift Protector's Caparace

          The Armor is - again - incredibly powerful. It's kind of like Fae Cunning, only it doesn't offer something if your dodging. Assuming it's plate, it seems like it would be 3 dots even without the token effect (just from the ratings that it provides, though your speed reduction would be 1 in that case). With that effect I couldn't tell you what rating I'd put it at. Probably 5, maybe higher not sure.

          For the drawback, ignoring the part about alerting the huntsman (as I already noted my thoughts on that), it seems... too drastic. Token's last for a scene, not periodically - and most scenes last more than anyone is going to have the health to survive, much less after combat. I mean you are hanging out with werewolves, so combat scenes might not last long but - still, IMO, too drastic.

          I was going to note not to use stamina to reduce the amount (as a higher stamina can -already- endure more without needing to reduce it) but honestly... I'd           remove the possibility of automatic death from it's use, maybe wrack the target with pain for a period for a solid week, applying a -2 to all physical rolls while he works through that (that isn't reduced by merits and cannot be healed by magic)

          Moons Wrathful Crescent

          So with the Scythe, I feel the need to clarify - for the scene, or is this a glamour -per- attack? I'm going to assume it's a glamour per attack - in which case, I'd actually change that, while also removing the special attack. Instead, give it a bonus to attack and treat the attacks like autofire - +3 to hit, can attack multiple targets rolling separately for each as long as they are all in range, -1 penalty to the roll for each additional target.

          For the Drawback... The scythe must take a life, of human quality or higher. Once activated, it -will- have it's due, and if the wielder doesn't have it in them, then it's fine with the wielder's life.

                  From what I understand and how I read the passage, a Stolen Token already seems to alert it's owner that it was used. When you use it, it applies the paranoid condition - or the hunted condition if you already have the paranoid condition. Two uses of one of these tokens automatically means you are being hunted and don't know who to trust. That occurs before the drawback - which means that a drawback such as 'the keeper knows you are alive' is a bit redundant, as is 'all the huntmen are gunning for you'.

                  I'd keep it thematically appropriate to each power. Regarding the Lord's Mantle, the reason you'd have a delusion is exactly -because- they don't have to listen to you. The reason I suggested that drawback is because the idea is that the token fools you into thinking that they have to listen to you - or at the very least, they'd be far more successful following your orders than doing whatever they want to do.

                  ...As an aside, I'm not sure which facet of Dominance you are referring to. None of them seem to do what it is you are having Lord's mantle does. Admittedly I usually would suggest not using the werewolf book to make changeling powers and items - but I do understand your trying to find a way to make this Lost competitive in a werewolf game.

                  As for the Carapace - I'm not sure which kith blessing that you would have, but - per the book - it wouldn't matter. No amount of magic or trickery can avoid the drawback, so if the drawback involves wracking pain your regeneration wouldn't matter. If it's damage itself?

                  Well, none of the books have an example of a drawback inflicting damage after it's used - but personally, I'd say the damage would have to heal in the most natural way possible. No regeneration. No magical heal powers. The wounds were part of the price paid to activate the power, and if it is to heal, it should heal in the most natural way possible.

                  Regarding the Crescent, I still recommend you make it as it's default of a scene. Seriously, more than likely if your needing to use the weapon you'll want to use it more than once in a single combat and having it require being activated every attack means that more than likely, using it will end with you both Paranoid and Hunted.

                  As far as a time sink, it's no more of a time sink than if you got a machine gun and did the same thing. The reason for the recommendation to use those rules is because the option exists for people to dodge your attack, which means that against those individuals you'd have your full dice pool - and because really, attacking a larger sum of people would be more difficult than attacking a single person.

                  Ultimately though, it's up to you and your crew what you consider to fit well with the rest of the group and it's game flow though. I think that's about as much as I can offer to assist with your request. Hopefully this helps, and if not well then um... well I got nothing :P
