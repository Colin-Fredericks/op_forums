    Wooo, quite a list, and some great ideas. Let me parse them one-by-one.
    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
    [*]In addition to the Major Brand or Tell, each Stigmatic has a way of generating Aether as part of the template, similarly to the Potent Blood Merit (Flowers Of Hell page 130). However, the method varies a lot, and isn't limited to hurtful secretions, but it is always detrimental in some way. For example, they might temporarily or permanently remove Attribute and Skill dots like a Mage Scouring their own Pattern, or inflict Conditions and Tilts on themselves at will. The amount of Aether is proportional to the loss, but no Stigmatic can generate more than 5 Aether per chapter. Wise Demons do not abuse their Stigmatic allies, and offer rewards for services rendered.[*]Greater use of Cryptids as spying and Aether-hunting tools. All Cryptids now have the Aether Hive Adaptation. Should they have the [Fuelstat] Eater Adaptation, they convert it into Aether. They still need to spend this resource to remain alive, but many are willing to share in exchange for protection or anything else their strange minds can desire.
    I'll pass on these. Part of the aim with Stigmatics and Cryptids (and I can say this confidently, I wrote them) was to hit the infinite and alien aspects of the God-Machine head on. Cryptids can be literally anything, one can never assume with a Cryptid. Stigmatics less so, but they are still wildly varied. That said, I will add a note about using them for this purpose if you (the storyteller) are looking for other G-M related avenues to Aether.
    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
    [*]Demons can dismantle Gadgets to absorb the Aether stored inside, but they cannot learn the installed Embed, Exploit or Demonic Form advantage if they do this. They must touch the Gadget, spend 1 willpower point and roll Wits + Science or Occult + Primum as an instant action. They gain 1 Aether automatically, in addition to Successes. This requires a Compromise roll, following Pendragon's house rules (or Aetheric Resonance, if you still want to use that in this situation). If a Demon dismantles a Gadget they created, the Willpower dot is not returned.
    This is a good idea. I'll add this (wrote Aetheric Resonane as well, so that'll see use).
    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
    [*]Allow the Essence Drain Technology to work on all supernaturals, and change its name to just Energy Drain. Ephemeral beings use their [Resistance/2 round up] as their Resolve to Resist the Demon's Wits + Occult + Primum roll.
    I think I'll do a variant of this. Energy Drain takes Essence first, beings without Essence merely get shredded as the Demon unravels them for Aether.
    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
    [*]The Extispicy Exploit can now be used to absorb Aether, like a mortal being sacrificed to turn their Integrity into Essence for an Angel. The Aether gained is equal to Successes, capped by the mortal's [Integrity/2 round up].         Another idea I just had was to create a Merit that lets Demons temporarily downgrade but not remove Glitches, since they'll have a lot more of those under your house rules. Let me try. I chose that name because Glitches are also known as Bugs and I couldn't resist that pun. Change the name if you want, no problem. Do you think this makes Glitches trivial? Should I add more prerequisites?

        Bug Spray (• to •••••)

        Prerequisites: Intelligence 3, Composure 3, Occult 3

        Effects: Spend Aether equal to the number of Glitches you want to downgrade + 1 willpower point and roll Intelligence + Composure.as an instant action. The Aether expenditure is capped by your dots in this Merit. On a Success, they are downgraded one step for the scene (Catastrophic -> Major -> Minor). On an Exceptional Success, they are downgraded two steps. You may also sacrifice a willpower dot to make it last the whole chapter, but you can only do this once per story. You may decrease your effective Primum for the rest of the chapter to lower the threshold of an Exceptional Success. For each dot removed, one less Success is required, down to a minimum of one. Your Attributes, Skills, Fuelstat, Demonic Form advantages and Merits with a Primum prerequisite are lowered or removed accordingly.

                            I got board. Here's two new Arts for you



                            Haberdashery

                            Why is a hat the most common symbol of authority? A crown, a mitre, a feathered headdress. On every continent headgear is a potent symbol of authority, belonging, or expertise. The prime Yantra for all Haberdashers. The Silver Ladder believe that somewhere in the Supernal the symbol of authority adorns his head and all Earthly authority follows; but despite their claims the Ladder did not invent Haberdashery. The Art arose independently from many mundane hatmaking guilds and trade associates around the world. Many of these were absorbed whole by the Ladder; the rest are dismissive referred to as mere hedge magicians for their lack of occult philosophy and general focus on using magic for purely mundane ends. Though the Ladder is always welcoming if a Haberdasher decides that magic is more interesting than winning the New York Fashion show.

                            The term Milliner or Millinery is occasionally used for female Haberdasher, any Haberdasher spell using a women's hat, or a Haberdasher currently using a feminine occult role, or the term is not used at all. It depends where in the world you are.

                            Resonant Path: Oribamus

                            Primary Arcana: Prime, along with Mind

                            Yantra: Making a hat by hand is the only possible Yantra available to Haberdashers. It is not a particularly potent Art.

                            Rotes: Haberdasher rotes can do one of two things. Either they can cast an effect upon the wearer, or they can cast a Mind effect upon everyone who sees the wearer. A builder's hard hat could bestow Skill in construction or convince everyone on the building site that the Haberdasher is on the construction team. Within this limit any effect that's symbolically appropriate for the chosen hat is possible, the hard hat could also grant mage armour, convince anyone the Haberdasher talks too that they're currently employing builders, or physically transform the Haberdasher into someone who looks like a builder.

                            Attainments

                            Hat Making: Haberdashery is not a particularly potent art, but what they do they do well. A Haberdasher must make a hat to cast a spell, the effect begins when the hat is first worn and the hat falls apart at the end. In addition even the most novice Haberdasher can make Imbued Items and add their Crafts skill to all Imbuing rolls. Naturally they can only imbue headgear.

                            Pointy Hat: When the Haberdashers say that headgear is a nearly universal icon, they're not kidding. Even wizards are in on the act. Haberdashers can create Grimoires in the form of any hat associated with Mages, and they can do so in partnership with another Mage who actually knows the desired Rotes. The more thematically appropriate the hat is for the spells within the better. Anyone wearing the hat gains the full benefits, which has the advantage of leaving the wearers hand's free for casting. The Grimoire may be Imbued with other effects.

                            Hats for Kings: A Mind effect to convince everyone you're the role implied by your headgear is a popular Haberdashery effect. At this level it becomes an Attainment and can be done with any hat rather than just those hand made by Haberdashers for the purpose.

                            Crowns for commoners: The interesting thing about hats, is that a top hat is just a potent symbol of the Victorian Era as Queen Victoria's crown. Hat's are as much about belonging as they are about authority or expertise. Belonging to a time, a place, a society. By donning an ordinary hat popular with the general public, the Haberdasher becomes temporally mortal. No magic short of Archmastery can detect their supernatural nature, all sympathetic links to their Shadow Name fail, and any Supernal magic or attainments currently affecting them are suspended. Haberdashers use this to hide from both the Abyss and the God Machine, as well as magical rivals, but if cornered they can regain their magic as easily as removing their hat.


                            Dumbledore's Army


                            If the Supernal is a reflection of humanity, does it not stand to reason that humanity suddenly falling in love with a story of magic and wonder would cast a bright reflection upon the Supernal? Dumbledore's Army insist that it does, and this art was not discovered by occult experimentation but intentionally created by Traditionalists who were sure it must exist just waiting for practitioners.

                            They were right, and Dumbledore's Army has become a popular art due to it's flexibility and it's self affirming philosophies, and has been attracting an unusual number of young Mage's who's mystery play leads them to the DA's door. They've even built a school to attract them, currently it's small enough that the head (and only) teacher is the Astral Walker of Albus Dumbledoor himself.


                            Resonant Path: Oribamus

                            Primary Arcana: Forces, though DA is spread almost evenly among all Arcana, with a noticeable weakness in Fate, Spirit, and Death.

                            Yantra: Wands, potions, pseudo-Latin incarnations, odd devices, robes, school uniforms, textbooks, the British school system, symbolically adopting occult roles based on Harry Potter characters. The Art's most potent Yantra is a Harry, a Ron and a Hermione acting in concert.

                            Rotes: Due to the poorly defined rules and logic underlying magic in Harry Potter Dumbledore's Army are capable of almost every effect and have easy access to Yantras; however they have trouble building up truly impressive Reaches or large dicepools. Their rotes tend to be quick, easy, and produce relatively small effects.


                            Attainments

                            The Wand Choose The Wizard: Dumbledore's Army gain a Wand as a dedicated focus tool for free. A mentor does something simple like buy a set of cheap Harry Potter merchandise wands and asks his apprentice to choose the one which feels right. Inevitably it will turn out to be an authentic occult item made from wood and supernal regents. Members of the DA must use a Wand as a Yantra in every spell they cast, though some radicals use a staff. Using or creating imbued items is an exception.

                            Sorting Hat: The philosophical core of DA is that your choices define you more than accidents of birth or Awakening; and that this in turn puts the responsibility on you to choose Griffindore not Slytherin (Mages being Mages, most are in fact Ravenclaw). A Mage in with this Attainment gains the Virtuous Merit for free and chooses the Virtue; in addition if they fulfil their new Virtue they gain +2 to the next Degeneration check they make this Session.

                            Choosing a Slytherin like Virtue such as Ambition would be heavily frowned upon by the Art, though choosing one unconnected to the philosophy of Harry Potter would be even worse. This philosophy also means DA tend to strongly identify with their Shadow persona over their mundane one – though it is easier to ask your Mortal parents to call you Hermione than Thoth.

                            Letter by Owl: Members of Dumbledore's Army may temporarily take a Sleeper outside of normalcy. The Sleeper's contacts consider it perfectly normal that their friend or family member will go away for a while (unless they roll well to resist of course), and the Sleeper themself becomes a Sleepwalker for the duration. The effect cannot last longer than one school term, as defined by the British government and can never be used on the same person twice.

                            Inspiring a Generation: At their apex a member of DA treats any place where a large group of Sleepers are focusing on the Harry Potter stories or related merchandise as a Demanse.

The charachter who inspired the art had a lot of voodoo trappings. Bezalel Kabbalism really should use Hebrew rather than Atlantian Runes - and it's practitioners should believe that Hebrew is a higher language than Atlantian.
