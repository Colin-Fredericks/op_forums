                                Currently looking for suggestions for the new opening fiction.

                                I'm thinking of something like "The Mortal's Perspective". We actually start with a Genius nearing the completion of their latest masterplan, In Medias Res style. The narrator, who is a Beholden, manages to summon the willpower to overcome the toxic relationship and become an Edison. This allows him to no longer depend on the worldview of his master, steal his designs for Wonders and actually make them profitable.

                                The Edison becomes famous while the Genius is exposed for the criminal mastermind he is...however, the Edison is not satisfied, the allure of the Wonders is too great, and he begins investigating their inner workings, thus triggering his own Breakthrough into a Genius. He promises himself to do better than his master, to create a true Utopia, but at this point he has been confirmed to be an unreliable narrator. We then cut back to the new Genius when he was still mortal, saying something like "it's always the little people who have the biggest dreams".

                                                        Originally posted by ajf115 View Post
                                                        Perhaps a little off-topic, but would Gravity Falls (the town, from the cartoon of the same name) have a place in Genius? I don't really know if Bardos are sticking around, but it seems like a pretty textbook one in my opinion.
                                                        Bardos, as far as I've been writing things, do indeed still exist. They're less directly tied to Inspiration in many cases, but Bardos are still definitely there as places outside what human knowledge normally knows, often with laws of reality that differe from our own subtly or obviously. As an aside, Bardo is now a blanket term that includes "Inspired Bardos," which are Bardos explicitly centered around Genii, Inspiration, and Impetus, and are what the term was used for in 1E. Any alternate plane or dimension is now a "bardo." Underworld, Arcadia, Duat, all Bardos of varying levels of unfriendliness.

                                                        And, strictly speaking, the Inspired Bardos would be considered Lower Depths by Mages that feed upon Impetus, lacking reliable sources of their own.

                                                        I'm not terribly familiar with the show, but from what I can tell, Gravity Falls itself would not be a Bardo, although certainly a nexus of Inspired phenomena, possibly a powerful Generator, but Bill Cipher's dimension could be a Bardo.

                                                        Other examples that could make good Bardos:

                                                        Mirrodin: A world that once symbolized a perfect paradise of artificial life, it has since become infected and dominated by a more modern conception of technology as a force of dehumanization from the rival Bardo of Phyrexia.

                                                        The Room WITH A MOOSE: A Wondrous Bardo (that is, a Bardo custom-built as a Wonder) built by a particularly mad Mane with a bone to pick against a bus full of children. It contains only a particularly vicious moose.

                                                        The DC Universe: Another Wondrous Bardo, created as an experiment by one Arch-Genius known as Doctor Manhattan, to resemble a more idealized version of his own world. It denizens are slowly becoming increasingly aware of their creator, with possibly disastrous consequences.

                                                        Shattered Seattle: The result of temporal machinations by some enigmatic "God-Machine," Seattle is host to a number of looping parallel timelines. Genii without appropriate Apokalypsi wonders will become rapidly disoriented, not to mention the host of clockwork demons prowling the timelines.

                                                        The Upside-Down: A dimension parallel to our own, with its own unholy interpretation of life. It is unknown if intelligent life can be found in this toxic, slime-coated bardo, but God help us all if there is, as the wildlife is more than bad enough.
