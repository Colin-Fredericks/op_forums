        Originally posted by tasti man LH View Post

        Either way, if nuWW had that much distaste for CofD, they wouldn't have bothered to keep it around.

        They would have shut it down and just had OPP go all-hands-on-deck with WoD5e. And even then, all we know for sure that OPP is doing is *just* Chicago by Night V5.
        That's not how these politics work.

        1) It's still a cash revenue with a solid base. PW may not like CofD and not want to draw any more attention then they have to, but no one says no free cash(and yes, this isn't free cash, but it's still cash flow).

        2) They already have enough trouble dealing with the lashback of the old guard fanbase in response to the things they are trying out right now-pissing off even more of a fanbase and creating even more negative word-of-mouth by canning the CofD would really have hurt their business model, which they can't afford to do right now.

        3) Also, CofD represents a loose alliance with the old guard of White Wolf's crew, in some ways, since OPP was born out of Richard catching the falling pieces from CCP and CofD has been a rather prominent feature of OPP's productions since it's inception.

        Like, as is, no, they don't like ChroD.* You are talking about people who clearly feel that it contributed to the fall of White Wolf and RPGs in general(whether or not they have a point is a subject all together). It definitely does not represent the glory days of gaming they so desperately want to try and revive.There's no point denying that.

        But it's current operations are beneficial in a wide variety, both in establishing legitimacy, maintaining a ready-and-curious word-of-mouth base for promotion, and keeping a decent cash flow coming in as it can, so long as it doesn't ultimately detract from what they're doing with World of Darkness. Give it time and possible success, and they might feel they can satisfactorily toss it off, but for now that's a dumb decision in practical terms.

        People can not like things and still keep them because they make sense for the current mode of operation. It happens a lot.

        *Which isn't to say they don't like individual lines within the entire franchise, because that would be silly.
