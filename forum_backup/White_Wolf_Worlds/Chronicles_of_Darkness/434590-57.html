    Draft 4

    Self-Serving Idol of Glory (Ancestral Channel)
    The Leviathan is no mere creature of flesh and bone, but a living god capable of empowering icons made in their honor.

    The Leviathan's Divine nature empowers and curses objects in their vicinity.
    Depth 1: Birthright of Sanctity

    Depth 2: Items that the Leviathan has made partake of their divine nature. They gain 7 Dedication points to assign to the item, but only can access their current Depth in benefits, and only while they hold the item. The Leviathan can have up to Sheol in personally crafted items dedicated in this way, though they can destroy previous creations to make way for new ones.

    Depth 3: The Leviathan can now blight a type of object with curses, reducing its effectiveness in their presence. They declare a category of item (such as medical tools, chain armor, swords, pistols, etc.) for each dot of Sheol anathema to them, and assign a pool of 7 Blight points to effects, though they can only utilize up to their current Depth in effects. The Leviathan can suppress this effect for the Scene for 1 Willpower. They can rededicate target items and blight effects after successfully meditating after 24 hours.

    Depth 4: Blessings now flow through onto the Leviathan's followers. Objects in the current Scene that have been crafted by their Cult and dedicated to Leviathan (such as by having one of their names or depictions inscribed upon it or being used in a Ritual) can gain Dedications as well, though they all possess the same bonuses, as applicable. The Leviathan can change which Dedications are in effect in the same Scene by spending 1 Willpower point.

    Depth 5: Wrath falls upon whatever mortal artifice would dare impede a living god. Blighted items that strike or impede the Leviathan automatically take Sheol points of Structure damage (bypassing Durability). Supernatural objects can still be dealt damage, but do not have their Durability ignored.

    Depth 6: [Placeholder: The Leviathan's power can reach across their Wake, enabling dedicated items to act as if they were the Leviathans own hands. Powers that rely upon the Leviathan touching a target can apply if they are touching the chosen item. This does not give a Leviathan any ablility to see the item in question, or know the conditions around it - they require other means of communication or Awareness Vestiges for that.]

    Depth 7: A Leviathan in Apotheosis can expunge the presence of blasphemous object from their very presence. All Blighted objects within the Leviathan's Wake are treated as if they were a character in an Extreme Environment with a rating equal the the Leviathan's Presence, and their Durability treated as Stamina. The effect lasts until the item leaves the Leviathan's Wake, or the Leviathan changes Depth.
    Dedication Cost Effect
    1 +1 Equipment Bonus (max. +5 total)
    1 +1 Weapon Damage (max. 5 total)
    1 +1 Durability
    2 +1 General or Ballistic Armor
    2 9-Again on Skill rolls (or upgrade to 8-Again)
    3 Exceptional Success on 3 Successes
    Blight Cost Effect
    1 -1 Equipment Bonus (min. 0 total)
    1 -1 Weapon Damage (min. 0 total)
    1 -1 Durability (min. 0)
    2 -1 General or Ballistic Armor
    2 Lose 10-Again (or downgrade 9-Again)
    3 Failures become Dramatic Failures
    Adaptations:
    Divine Receptacle (••): The Leviathan has access to an additional 1-dot Dedication - the ability to store Ichor inside the item. Items can only store Ichor up to their Durability safely.
    Channel the Divine (••••, Requires Divine Receptacle): The Leviathan has access to an additional variable cost Dedication - the item can replicate the ability of a Channel the Leviathan knows. The cost in Dedication points is equal to the Depth of the Channel ability. Any Ichor costs must be paid for from either the bearer, or the item's own reserve (through Divine Receptacle). Sheol is treated as 1 in the case where the bearer does not possess a Sheol Attribute (such as a favored cultist being given such an item).
    Primordial Creator (•••): The Leviathan gains 3 additional Dedication points and can access their Depth+1 at Depth 2, Depth+2 at Depth 4, and Depth+3 at Depth 6.
    Accursed Idol (•••): The Leviathan gains 3 additional Blight points and can access their Depth+1 at Depth 3, Depth+2 at Depth 5, and Depth+3 at Depth 7.



    I'm also thinking about a "The Leviathan knows the location of all dedicated item inside their Wake" effect, but that seems lackluster alone. Also a "restore/reinforce dedicated items" power. Not sure that is thematic.

                                                        Next submission, Descendant Awareness:

                                                        Weaving the Loom of Fate (Descendant Channel)
                                                        Description pending

                                                        Depth 1: Birthright of Awareness
                                                        Depth 2: By spending 1 Ichor, the Leviathan can glimpse the threads of Fate. They state a desired goal, qualities of a person they seek, or generalities for an item. They know which direction and rough distance to a person or source that either fits their criteria, or could lead them to it (though they don't quite know how, yet). Upon finding the subject, they also get a sense that it is who or what they seek.
                                                        Depth 3:                                                         The Leviathan's sense are now open to the omens of strange fates around them. They can see supernaturally altered destinies, or even just particularly potent ones. The Leviathan also effectively has access to the Omen Sensitivity merit, except without the drawback.
                                                        Depth 4: No longer merely restricted to merely seeing Fate's threads, the Leviathan can now pull them to bend events to their will. As an Instant action spending 2 Ichor and rolling Wits + Occult + Sheol - Resolve + Sheol, the Leviathan can give modify their own or another's dice pool. Each success adds or subtracts 2 dice from the pool, or adjusts the threshold for Exceptional Success by 1 in either direction.
                                                        Depth 5: Upon using the Depth 2 ability on a subject, they can intuit their weaknesses, figuring out the target's Ban, Bane, who or where they can hurt they target the most, or what they fear (The Leviathan might not be able to grasp why the target fears the action, but they know just what to do or say).
                                                        Depth 6: When using the Depth 4 ability, you can also force the target to re-roll once per usage, as events conspire to steal or grant victory as you choose.
                                                        Depth 7: By spending 4 Ichor, the Leviathan can gaze into the weaving of Fate and spin their own destiny. The Leviathan creates a Prophecy Condition, tied to a goal or fate they set. Unlike most Conditions, this isn't tied to a character or object, but is rather available to any it concerns.
                                                        Sidebar: You Can't Fight Fate (Unless You Do)
                                                        If the Storyteller and Players want a bit more activity and urgency, they can give the Prophecy a bit more mechanical weight. Treat the stated goal as an Extended Action with a target number of (chart). Each Session, the Prophecy itself rolls the Leviathan's Sheol as a Dice Pool, as Fate itself works to ensure its designs come to pass. Significant actions toward fulfilling the Prophecy act as supporting actions in a Teamwork roll. Actions that impede the Prophecy can generate a Dice Pool to make it a contested roll.

                                                        Adaptations:
                                                        Just As Planned (•••): Once per Chapter, by spending 2 Ichor, you can decree that an action actually forwarded one of your ongoing Prophecy Conditions. The owner of the action still gets a Beat for advancing the Prophecy.
                                                        Reading the Omens (••): At Depth 3 and below, the Leviathan can spend another point of Ichor to active their effective Omen Sensitivity.
