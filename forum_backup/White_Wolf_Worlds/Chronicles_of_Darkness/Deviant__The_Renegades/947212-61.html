                                                    Changeling I think lacks the Body Horror that a lot of kidnapped-by-aliens fiction has. In Changeling, the horror is the trauma of the kidnapping itself, the helplessness of being there as they operate on you. In Deviant, the horror is being back on earth, and feeling your heart beat on the wrong side of your chest, and feeling something move under your chest, and screaming "what have they done to me!" Changeling also uses the Durance as a sort of opening-to-a-world-of-fancy, where sure you were kidnapped by aliens, but your best friend was a candle that burned as Shahrazade told her 1001 stories, and your boyfriend was a billy-goat crossing a troll-bridge. You may have been kidnapped by aliens, but once you enter the Changeling society, it becomes clear that you're all being forced to play sick cosplay-as-torture by the fickle gods of Faerie.
                                                    To me, that weakens the usefulness of Changeling to tell certain types of alien-abduction stories. It doesn't make much sense to me for the abduction victims to return with the kind of Contracts that Changelings get. Deviant now, Deviant's body-horror themes fit better here, at least in my opinion.

                                                    But I know that this is a long and hashed-over argument on these forums.

                                                            Originally posted by Gwyn chan 'r Gwyll View Post
                                                            Changeling I think lacks the Body Horror that a lot of kidnapped-by-aliens fiction has.

                                                            Perhaps the body horror is not physically present (to normal mortals), but it is still very much there.

                                                            Have you ever heard of body dysmorphic disorder? It's a psychological condition in which a person rejects pieces of their own body because they are under the delusion that they are somehow foreign or aggressive. In the most extreme cases, victims have attempted to amputate or surgically modify their bodies just to ease their condition.

                                                            Now, you take somebody like a Changeling, who can see through the mask and realize they're physiology isn't what it's supposed to be and starts freaking out over it. From a mortal's point of view, they're going to see an ordinary human screaming about how their own body is some sort of abomination, or has even turned against them.

                                                            That can lead to some extremely disturbing stories if handled correctly.
