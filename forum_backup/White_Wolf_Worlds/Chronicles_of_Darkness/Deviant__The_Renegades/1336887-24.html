    Originally posted by The Throne View Post
    Can i ask another two? Thanks for the previous!

    1) When my accidental imbued get a new scar from instability... and want to entangle it with a new variation... at what magnitue the variation start? At par with the magnitude of the Scar? At 1?
    The Variation starts at whatever Magnitude you want it at, up to the Magnitude of the entangled Scar.
    2) If my pc had a Scar of Magnitude 3 and an entangled Variation of Magnitude 2... how much px must pay for get a new Variation of Magnitude 2? 8 Px?
    You don't pay Experiences to add permanent Magnitude to an existing Variation. The cost is simply that you have to have a high enough Magnitude on the entangled Scar to support the new Magnitude of Variation.

                        As regards Batman, I had a thought for a Deviant chronicle.

                        Arkham Asylum is a place where people go to get better. And many of them do. They employ accredited, ethical doctors and psychologists who actually care about their patients and help them get better.

                        The Conspiracy is mostly concentrated in the High Security Ward for the Criminally Insane, as few care about the people who end up there and all the security means its very easy to hide things from outsiders, but they have their hooks into other staff members. Once they’re done gathering data, they let loose their Devoted hunter to gather up the escapees and bring them “home.”

                        After all, he does a hell of a good job, for all that he dresses like a giant Bat.
