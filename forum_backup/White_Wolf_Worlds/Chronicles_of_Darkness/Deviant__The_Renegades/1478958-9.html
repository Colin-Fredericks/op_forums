    Originally posted by BronzeDog View Post
    I'm revisiting a character concept: Ray was partially Spirit-Claimed by a void spirit. He's been physically transformed, but most of the time he's invisible, or as he describes it, "technically not completely existing." Right now, he's got Relentless/Suppressible Camouflage 3 entangled with Power Failure (Main Scar) and Concentration (Secondary Scar for Suppressible). I'm thinking of applying the Disfiguring Scar Deviation, so when his Camouflage fails, or he reveals himself, it's Overt. Relentless Variation specifies +1 Magnitude if persistence would cause problems, specifically mentioning Camo 3+ as an example. Disfiguring Scar gives +1 Magnitude to the scar it's attached to, and I'd say it'd make both PF and Concentration Overt.

    How do I work out the final magnitudes of Camouflage, Power Failure, and Concentration? If you were the Storyteller, would you allow this?
    The entire construction is mechanically illegal in the first place because Power Failure is not a Persistent Scar. Which Persistent Scar you replace Power Failure with in turn affects whether it's a valid subject for Disfiguring Scar, and as the secondary Scar Concentration is specifically the same Magnitude as the Persistent Scar.

            Giving some more thought on pseudo-Claimed as Coactive Deviants, and currently focused on appropriate Scars.
            • Bane is pretty easily adapted to represent the spirit's Bane.
            • Subliminal Conditioning seems like it could work as a Ban at lower levels, though it gets more specific to controlling at higher levels.
            • Dependency could be on Essence, where the skill roll represents finding a Locus or appropriately resonant location (Wits+Occult) or making an area resonant.
            • Philia from the Devoted Companion might represent the spirit's overriding drive to harvest essence through the compulsion.
            • Alternate Persona could represent an incomplete union, where the spirit and human half each have their own agenda.
            • Conspicuous Appearance would be appropriate for sufficiently monstrous transformations as a result of the partial claiming.
            • Phobia could represent a Ban that requires the spirit to flee certain situations.

                                    Originally posted by BronzeDog View Post
                                    So, new version to be checked:
                                    Variation: Camouflage 3 (Relentless/Suppressible +0?)
                                    Primary Scar: Dependency 2, (Disfiguring Scar for +1 and to make it Overt)
                                    Secondary Scar: Concentration 3 (made Overt by Disfiguring Scar)
                                    You may be able to get away with a base Magnitude of 1 on Dependency through Relentless, as initially surmised, though how you intend to make it obvious to observers when Ray's missed a dose then becomes a necessary narrative exercise.

                                    Important follow-up question re: intention: what makes Ray re-appearing Overt? Because as a Toggled effect Camouflage normally can be ended before the end of the scene to no especial consequence for conspiracy surveillance rolls, so it's obviously not just "a person has appeared where previously there was not a visible one."​

                                    Originally posted by cybirddude View Post
                                    2: Can symbiote manticores only be used by humans and deviants, or can other supernaturals, such as vampires, werewolves, changelings, etc. also use them.
                                    The Manticore Companion Merit or conspiracy icons are the primary means of modeling symbionts. The former has a prerequisite of "Deviant" and the latter is the product of a conspiracy of Baselines and Deviants, the latter of whom the Symbiont section says can use symbionts if they haven't physically diverged too much (from the "human host" model indicated as the target user of a symbiont in the second sentence of the section).

                                    Vampires are undead, werewolves are excessively alive, changelings may well have nonsense anatomy under the Mask, and the fact that long-term symbiont use can trigger a Divergence (i.e. crack your soul open) suggests that in the circumstance where this hookup has managed to happen it will create a unique result best determined by the needs of the story or more depressingly trigger analogous circumstances like becoming one of the Enraptured/Broken Souls/Rampant/Petrificati/etc.

                                    They are notionally valid subjects for the Sacred Flesh Variation, but that's at a remove from "integrate a magical animal into your human biology for first-and-a-half-hand access to superpowers."

                                                            There is no need to mess around with examples of what Stubborn Resolve helps with, because Stubborn Resolve's applicability requires that you are acting in support of the drives of a Touchstone and trying to avoid being persuaded or compelled away from acting on that drive.
