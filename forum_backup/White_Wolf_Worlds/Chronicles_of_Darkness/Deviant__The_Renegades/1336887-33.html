            A freak supernatural accident involving a convergence of ley lines, an alignment of the planets, an unusual combination of chemicals, and a lightning strike, killed and changed a family of merchants traveling across the old west.                             RE: Hyper-Competence: How about making it Tiered, but making Mag 2 half Scar Power? That caps the total bonus at +4 (including Specialty, and usually +2 or +3), but still makes the Mag 3 effect feel like a superpower - which is important to me, since otherwise it is narratively boring compared to every other Mag 3 Variation in the game. Doubling the Specialty bonus at Mag 1 if you have a relevant Specialty could also give it a bit more oomph at that level, but that might be an unnecessary complication.

                            Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post

                            As I understand it, the point of the Relentless Variation Scar is to change a Toggled Variation into a Perpetual Variation: that is, there would be no point in taking the Relentless Variation Scar if the only entangled Variations are Perpetual. Likewise, there would be no point in applying the Suppressible Deviation if the only entangled Variations are Toggled. So you'd take Astral Travel with Relentless Variation (to change it from Toggled to Perpetual), then apply the Suppressible Deviation by way of Tabula Rasa to give you a way to temporarily shut it off.
                            In fact, since Relentless has the Toggled keyword, you can't entangle it with Perpetual Variations - and for this exact reason.

                            Originally posted by lnodiv View Post
                            The inability to teleport targets into 'immediate danger' with Translocation is really hampering my Nightcrawler build.

                            Any chance for a higher Magnitude Deviation that removes that restriction, maybe at the cost of needing to teleport alongside the target (ref. teleporting into the air with someone and then trying to teleport away before hitting the ground)?
                            Teleportation can pretty easily take over a game, so I was being somewhat conservative with it. In truth, I didn't add "teleport other" to the mix until very late in the process, and not without a bit of trepidation. Conspiracies are less of a threat if you can teleport 100 miles away from their capture squads, and being able to teleport the Linchpin 10 stories up can be too easy a way to get rid of them. As to your solution, if you can teleport back or have a way to survive a 10-story fall (such as Flight), it can still be a fairly assuredly lethal solution to all your problem. So, I'm not likely to expand the options, although groups are always welcome to adjust the game to suit their play style.

                            Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                            Going over Involuntary Scars again. There's a particular hole in Indelible Mark/Involuntary Stimulus that I think can be plugged by making Rare and Common into Standard Deviations and/or making Unpredictable exclusive with Rare and Common in the same way that Uncontrollable is.

                            (The hole, essentially, is that by taking an Involuntary Stimulus that is Common and/or Unpredictable, you can take a Continuous Indelible Mark and still come out with a Magnitude 4+ Scar whose only effects are "the Variation uses the Involuntary activation method," "exposure to a common stimulus (and/or an unknown stimulus) may cause the Variation to activate on its own, which grants Beats when it causes trouble," and "you have constant access to a source of Beats in the form of a Persistent Scar that often has no other systems attached to it." Entangled with a Subtle Variation like Aquatic or Superhuman Attribute or Predator's Cunning, this has… functionally zero teeth despite the clear intent of the Deviation's wording being "one of the Scars has to do more than offer Beats for this Deviation to apply." It's tricky.)

                            Relatedly, do secondary Scars count as separate Scars for the purposes of Beats-per-Scar-per-scene?
                            Making Unpredicatable with Common/Rare makes sense to me.

                            In the interest of not making it possible to accumulate several Beats in each scene, I'd say it should be limited to one Scar-based Beat per scene, regardless of how many Scars come into play. I'll spell this out in the text.

                            Originally posted by Deinos View Post
                            Does anyone know secondary scars have to match the physical/mental/social category of the primary?
                            They don't currently, but the more I think about the question, the more I think that, thematically, they probably should. It's the whole "Scars turn your natural strengths (Attributes) into weakness" thing I've talked about before. I know that won't a universally popular decision.

                            Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                            Another oddity that's probably been caught in the working draft: The example of the Consequences of Instability in Preview 2 alludes to Involuntary Scars worsening at a different threshold than the other two categories ("this means that only his Scars linked to an Involuntary Variation increase by a Magnitude of 1"). Old version of the mechanics poking through?
                            Good catch. Fixing in the working draft.

                            Originally posted by espritdecalmar View Post
                            I want to make sure I'm parsing the Shapeshifter Deviation of Animal Transformation correctly: A Deviant with Shapeshifter gains a nearly unlimited repertoire of animal shapes within the parameters of Variation Magnitude, and it is not the case that they must choose one appropriate form for each tier (ie, "I can turn into a housecat, a pelagic thresher shark, and a swarm of Japanese hornets"), correct?

                            Also, entangling Shapeshifter Animal Transformation with Fluctuating Variation must make for some very chaotic scenes.
                            Correct on the first point.

                            Regarding the second, Fluctuating Variation probably shouldn't work on Discrete Variations. It's not too much trouble when entangled with Animal Transformation (or, say, Carapace), but it's nonsensical with several of the others. Immunity? Specialized Sense? A blanket prohibition might be necessary, but I'll see if I can avoid throwing out the baby with the bath water, because there are certainly some exceptions that are awesome, but I also have to watch out that any fix doesn't make Wild Variation incredibly easy to abuse.

                            Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
                            Personally, I think Eric got the Cephalists and the Mutants mixed up: this one feels more like the “realms of the mind” stuff that I'd expect from Cephalists, whereas the one we got for the Cephalists feels more like the sort of “your body is different” thing that I'd expect from Mutants.

                            But that's just a nitpick.
                            Eh. Dividing up the stuff I wanted to include in the Companion into Clade-themed chunks was more of a marketing device. I was originally toying with making one Form and one set of mechanical options "for" each Clade, but it was really artificial, so my draft outline organizes it more like you'd expect (all Forms in one chapter, for example), rather than having Clades as chapter names or some such. It made for a nice way to present the contents for the Kickstarter, though, so that's how I wrote the pitches for the five pieces.

                            Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                            On that subject, hey kids, there's finally a game that easily supports freedom from death by aging.

                            Not that it'll help you much when you implode.
                            Achieving immortality (sans Instability death) is easy. Getting away from the conspiracies of the Web of Pain long enough to enjoy your extra years is all but impossible.

                            Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                            I'm fairly certain your alternate personas can't be Touchstones, though.
