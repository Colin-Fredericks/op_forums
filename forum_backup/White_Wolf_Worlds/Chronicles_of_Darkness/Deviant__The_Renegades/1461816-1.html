        Oh damn, I thought the Changeling one was rough... Wolfram & Hart and the Yeerks would both make potentially very compelling Deviant antagonists if handled properly, for very different reasons and through very different lenses. Buuut... as much as I utterly adore Animorphs as a series overall and would love to see it manifest in the ChroD setting, unless the interpretation were very loose indeed relative to the origin, the Yeerks would be yet another sciencey-adjacent faction in a gameline already laden with them (although it would certainly stand out in its own way). On the other hand, Wolfram & Hart would much more readily bring a directly occult angle that official Deviant material is currently a touch short on, and seems like it could quite easily brush up against the Inferno or Maeljin specifically, and either would be a lot of fun.

        Whichever one of the two doesn't win out here might wind up getting homebrewed for my own use, but I think I'm gonna vote B.
