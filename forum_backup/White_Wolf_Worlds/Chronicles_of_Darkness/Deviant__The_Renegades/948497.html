    Deviant: Inspirational charachters in other media

    08-11-2016, 07:10 AM
    Who would you like to be able to recreate in Deviant.

    I'd quite like to play the charachters from Sense8. Power wise they'd be easy, all they need is telepathic communication and the ability to jump into each other's bodies.

    I'd also like to play Penny from the really obscure game The Strangers (it's a mod for the game Freedom Force). She's ageless and can transmute matter into different forms - but finds elements easier than compounds. If she transmutes herself it's always harmless so she could become solid metal for toughness or turn into gas for intangibility. She also has a symbiotic and loyal ephemeral being that lives in her body, which she can send out on errands. It's extremely fast and can transmute matter like she can, but it's causes atomic instability if it gets too far from her body.

    Chaos' Conspiracy:
    Is actually the science superhero who created her. In his civilian identity he was working on a cure for Penny's rare form of cancer, but after too much time fighting evil he mixed up the medicine with a vial of demon blood and injected it directly into Penny's cancer. The result caused it to transform into a loyal symbiotic ephemeral being. This saved Penny's life from cancer, granted her top-tier super powers, and possibly agelessness.

    Naturally Penny's was ecstatic but her creator is convinced he's ruined her life and is trying to cure her. Unlike the superhero verse of The Strangers, in Deviant he'd have a good reason to want to cure Penny.

                                                          Originally posted by Vent0 View Post

                                                          Wasn't Megaman(Rockman) a custom built Android, though?
                                                          Yes and no.

                                                          Yes, he's an android.

                                                          No, he wasn't built for superheroics, he was altered for superheroics.

                                                          He was built to be a lab assistant. When the other robots were corrupted, he volunteered to be changed into megaman.

                                                          At least, if one is talking about classic megaman. Other iterations have included such beings as a boy possessed by an electromagnetic alien, a dead boy being converted into a virtual lifeform, or a child merged with a nanotech symbiote.

                                                          Androids was treated as a mere origin, worthy of a 1-5 group of merits, but so was being born rich, poor, or being bridge and tunnel in Bleeding Edge, the cyberpunk supplement.

                                                          Promethean treated three law compliant robots as a thing in the nwod, even without the divine fire, in fact especially without the divine fire, because it said that any such programming restrictions went away when a machine becomes a promethean.

                                                          And "in vitro" is considered a valid origin for Deviants, so some deviants were never normal to begin with.

                                                          Now certainly a ST can veto an android deviant in their games, but such would not contradict the setting, the rules, or the basic premise of the game.
