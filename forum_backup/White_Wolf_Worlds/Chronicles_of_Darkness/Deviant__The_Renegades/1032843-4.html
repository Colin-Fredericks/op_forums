                                        Originally posted by Robert D View Post
                                        Understood May I ask what your favorite part of the playtest was (a clever move by the players, a fun twist in your storytelling, that sort of thing)?
                                        I wasn't at Mid-Winter, but I ran a couple one-shots in late December, and my winner for clever use of a Variation probably goes to my second playtest group:

                                        The Mutant with Translocation could only teleport to places she could either see (difficult, given the security of the area they wanted to access) or where she had left significant traces of her DNA (some hair, blood, etc.). So, she got her hands on some gum, chewed it up good to make sure it had enough of her DNA in it, and then stuck it to the undersides of food carts, the shoes of guards, and other objects that were bound for the secure area. The Cephalist with Body-Snatcher facilitated further this by taking over a guard's body and using it to place wads of the Mutant's chewed gum anywhere she might need to access in the secure area, so she could go pretty much wherever she wanted, pocket small items in each location, and then teleport out without needing to deal with the scrutiny an exiting guard would.

                                        Although the first playtest group was the one who carefully combined the Mutant's Shadow Selves (for a distraction), the Chimeric's Bioluminescence (to blind the guards), and the Invasive's Sensor Array (to ignore the blinding light) to storm the communications tower. Any resistance the guards had left in them after that was no match for the Coactive nun with Gigantic, Natural Weapon, and Supernatural Attribute (Strength).

                                        (The usual caveats apply: We just entered second drafts. Quite a number of things might still change between now and the time the game is available for public consumption. Management is not responsible for spontaneous mutation, brain damage, or death resulting from exposure to Deviant spoilers. Etc.)

                                                            Originally posted by Primordial newcomer View Post
                                                            similar to the embeds and exploits it sounds like. however I do think it is more possible that it will be less omniscient detection and more like what's physically witnessed (whether in person or caught on video)
                                                            With Embeds vs. Exploits, it was more a matter of power level. Embeds didn't have as much oomph, but they let you fly under the radar. Exploits, meanwhile, were terribly noisy, but boy could you crack some skulls with those things.

                                                            Overt vs. Subtle is more about how likely your Variation or Scar is to attract the attention of eyewitnesses to you specifically and to leave tongues wagging in your wake. If your Variation can be passed off as luck, or that somehow you're just really, really good at a Skill (which no one nearby realizes you had no training in) or it just makes everyone think you're an Important Person in their organization, that's Subtle. If you're 10 feet tall or have ram's horns or shoot fire from your hands or take flight over a crowded square, that's Overt. Being 10 feet tall is pretty useful (Overt), but being able to puppet someone's body for a few hours is also useful (Subtle).

                                                            Scars are similar. Are you a retired elementary school teacher who can now think of nothing but committing murder all day? That's Subtle. Do you manifest stigmata whenever you peer into the past? That's Overt. Mechanically, a little narrative blood loss (or even a level of bashing or lethal damage) isn't as much of a big deal as entering a delicate negotiation knowing that if anyone so much as makes veiled threat in your direction, you probably won't be able to resist bashing their head against a wall until it breaks open like a rotten cantaloupe.

                                                            Make sense?
