            Originally posted by Primordial newcomer View Post
            If I may ask, what's the story behind making the Dedicated Form? Genuinely shocked and interested it's a Form and not some kinda thing you'd see in a storytellers guide
            There were three directions from which I arrived at Dedicated. First, the core includes numerous references to failed Divergences, including implying that some people survive but wind up with Scars and no Variations. It made sense to address those directly. Second, people would occasionally ask me why there was no "half-splat" in Deviant. After all, Werewolf has its Wolf-Blooded, Vampire its ghouls, and Mage its Sleepwalkers. It seemed to me that there was an appetite for an answer to that question beyond the flip "a half-Deviant is just a mortal with a Persistent Condition." The more I thought about it, the more it seemed like these failed Remade were just as much victims of the Web of Pain as the Broken and had every reason to want revenge; they just lacked the full set of tools that Deviants had. Third, I've always had this vision in my head of what Broken were like before conspiracies had ubiquitous means of surveillance, and part of that was the idea that some people went on quests to encounter supernatural shit that would transform them.

            The Dedicated were meant to address all that. They're an advanced Form like those in The Clades Companion. Depending on the options you choose, they can range from, yes, blue book mortals with Scars (or other pieces of the Deviant template) to Deviants with a couple pieces of the template excised. If you want to play a Dedicated in a cohort of full Renegades and explore the horror and tragedy of the Divergence without all the kewl powerz, you can do that. If you want to play an all-Dedicated chronicle - whether the arc is "failed experiments trying to get revenge anyway" or "Born not yet come into their full strength but going to get there in a story or two" (literally a game I'm in right now) - you can do that, too.

            These aren't automatically antagonists, and while I think the Form is only going to appeal to certain kinds of players, it didn't seem like it would be a waste of 1,000 or so words to give them a way to do it. It's not like everyone wants to play a Transmissible, Symbiote, Self-Made, or Amalgam, either. So, even though they make great STCs, they go in the Player's Guide because they are characters I can envision players choosing to play, either in Dedicated-only chronicles or mixed ones.

            Onyx Path Freelancer and former Deviant line developer.

                    I'm definitely looking forward to these. I've personally been interested in what happens when you've been subjected to the Web of Pain, but not (yet?) to the point of cracking your soul open and replacing Integrity with Conviction and Loyalty. In particular, when I posted my Mad Science thread a while back (link in my sig),I posited the idea that the inventors would come face to face with “weird science” phenomena that would take a toll on their Integrity, but in so doing leave them with the ability to invent. This was conceived before Deviant was even announced, and riffed off of Hunter instead; but Deviant is easily the better fit, and from what I'm hearing about it, the Dedicated Form goes a long way toward enabling the existing Deviant system to handle it.

                    Frankly, my anticipated Mad Science supplement keeps on shrinking as “official” supplements slowly eat away at what it needs to cover; all that's really left is a way to adjust the Investigation rules to represent research into Fortean phenomena, and what new forms of weird science they can open up. And that's fine with me; if everything ends up handled by “official” books, so much the better.

                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
