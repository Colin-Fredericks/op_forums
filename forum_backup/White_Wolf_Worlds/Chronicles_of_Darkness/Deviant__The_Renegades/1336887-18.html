    One of the things that gets me most excited about a new game is how easily I can generate character ideas I really want to play. I have been brimming with them for Deviant. I actually wrote up a few mini stories in the style of the Origins. They may be useful in showing my potential players the variety of characters they can create. They are included below in case anyone is interested.

    Do not call up that which you cannot put down.

    That is the first rule of summoning. He was a brilliant student of sorcery. Brilliant enough to know which rules he could bend to the breaking point, or so he thought. But he called up a demon too powerful for him to control. It slew his master and tried to possess him, but he had one more brilliant trick up his sleeve. He did not use his magic to try and repel it. Instead he let it in, and used his magic to bind it.

    He is not much of a sorcerer any more, as most of his magic is used keeping the binding intact. However, the seal is imperfect and things leak through. Sometimes it is power he can draw upon for strength. Sometimes it is voices, whispering into his thoughts.

    Epimorph / Volunteer
    Coactive / Infused
    Symbiote


    She had always been normal. Exceedingly so. Average height, average build. Several friends but none close. A few romances but none serious. No great struggles but no great ambitions. She did have an interest in medicine and had a good career as a nurse.

    Everything changed the day she was diagnosed with a brain tumor. It was aggressive, malignant, and too far along for standard medicine to help. Her doctor told her about an experimental treatment that may be her only hope. Having no other options, she agreed.

    The experimental brain surgery was a success, the surgeon told her, but he had other news. While operating they had been surprised at how otherwise *normal* her brain was. No *potential* whatsoever. They really wanted her to be the control subject in their more advanced experiments. No, they were not asking. Consider it the price of saving her life.

    What followed was months of torturous experiments and repeated brain surgeries. She wasn't sure who was more surprised when the *control* subject was the first to awaken to telekinetic powers, but she took advantage of the surprise first. One destructive and bloody breakout later, the formerly normal nurse is now a ticking time bomb desperate to control her powers, or at least channel them against the ones trying to reclaim her.

    Exomorph / Unwilling
    Cephalist / Psychic


    Do you know what happens when a pregnant woman is turned by a vampire? Too early, and she has a miscarriage. Too late, and labor is induced immediately. Somewhere in the middle? The new vampire can carry the pregnancy to term, and that is when things get interesting.

    They are called dhampirs, half-vampires, daywalkers. They have many of the vampire's powers but few of their weaknesses. Many creatures of the night consider them a threat, or an abomination that must be put down.

    He doesn't consider himself a vampire hunter. He really just wants to be left alone. But as long as they keep coming after him he will defend himself, and sometimes take the fight back to them.

    He wonders if he will ever find the one who turned his mother. His second father, in a way. That is one he would enjoy hunting.

    Genotypal / Born
    Mutant / Grotesque

    (Blade homage, obviously. I wasn't sure if Mutant or Chimeric (Bat) was more appropriate, but the Unpredictable Adaptation seemed useful for the grab bag of powers vampires sometimes display.)


    The woman was her mother twice over. Once as the loving mom who raised her daughter with kindness and care, even after her father died. Once as the doctor who used forbidden techniques to bring her back after her unfortunate accident.

    Much of her body used to belong to someone else. At least a dozen different people, she thinks, if she is keeping track of the strange memories correctly. At least she can still identify which memories are hers and which are not. She assumes so anyway. Could she even tell if she was wrong?

    Why is everyone after her and her mother? Sure, she is technically back from the dead, and she heals injuries quickly, but the seams between the parts never heal. The stitching will never be able to come out. It chafes. Horribly.

    Pathological / Accident
    Invasive / Cyborg
    Amalgam

    (If things had gone differently, this could have been a Promethean. At least this way she has her memory and her soul, however messed up they are.)

                                                - Yes. Hybrid Form (which I'll probably end up renaming again, because it foolishly uses two existing game terms that are unrelated to it) is the opposite, reflecting a rapid transformation into something obviously inhuman.

                                                - It should allow Controlled and Involuntary. Not sure why those aren't provided as examples, but possibly it was Persistent-Only in a previous draft.

                                                - Relentless Variation is a Scar, but it's a weird one in that it has a mandatory secondary Scar (either Controlled of Persistent). It started its life as a Deviation, but I found that it was easier to explain its effects, this way. It is indeed the way you get persistently invisible characters.

                                                Magnitude 3 is required to interact with immaterial beings, including Lash. Within those limits, non-Baselines can fall prey to this Variation.
