                                                            Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post
                                                            Reading the spoilers one thing I want to know is how possible will it be to do something like The Invisible Man. Someone who's just got one narrowly defined power.

                                                            The Invisible Man becomes less and less human as time goes on, so that part's easy, but could there be alternatives to him getting more powerful or additional powers that occurs as his humanity degrades.

                                                            On the subject of the Invisible Man I'm interested in how a Deviant without a Conspiracy will play, somebody who tinkered with forbidden science in his own garage and became a Deviant. (Mind you, concerned mortal friends and family could make a very unique and interesting &quot;Conspiracy&quot;, if likely a very low powered one).

                                                            And on the subject of unique conspiracies, I'm hoping one of the supplements covers non-human Conspiracies. The Ordo Dracul for example.


                                                            And one other question I had. Is it possible to go full Doctor Manhatten? That is, achieve ultimate power at the cost of ultimate inhumanity but not die. Perhaps by becoming a Spirit or something weirder.
                                                            All of those are excellent questions, and I'm very curious to see how they play out.

                                                            About selfmade Deviants, there are plenty of ways you can do:

                                                            - You could just say that they don't have a conspiracy after them...but this is a party game, so they get drawn in to fighting the conspiracy the other PCs are fighting. This could be useful if you want PCs from different origins without having to juggle four different conspiracies.
                                                            - Maybe they changed themselves, but they did so using the resources of a conspiracy. Maybe they stole technology or research from the conspiracy and used it, maybe they were a member of the conspiracy and performed unauthorized experiments on themself, etc.
                                                            - Alternately, you could have a conspiracy that opposes them. A good example of this would be the Proletheans from Orphan Black: a group of Luddite fanatics who consider the Deviants to be abominations.

                                                            And about IM, I think Dave's spoilers imply that it's easy to make a Deviant with a narrowly defined range of powers? Though I'd prefer to keep them a bit more fluid and flexible to differentiate them from Mortals+ characters.
