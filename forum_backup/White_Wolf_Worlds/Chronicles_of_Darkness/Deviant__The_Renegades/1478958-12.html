        Originally posted by RAW_brewer_devoted View Post
        If no tools exist, what is the consensus on how they should be randomized? Scars or variations first? How should variations be generated to ensure that half or more of its variations are from universal/clade-it-belongs-to? At what point do you add the scar-free variation from its origin?
        The way Divergences work mean there isn't really a practical way to randomize its results without involving some sort of large language model to identify when e.g. Pyrokinesis is not an appropriate Variation to arise from an experimental cryogenics procedure. As the sidebar on the preceding page says, the point is more for there to be inherent uncertainty in dabbling with the Divergence — as a player-facing tool, Launch Devoted Line dedicates an exceptional success effect to guaranteeing some of the results are the specific things you were trying for to emphasize that uncertainty, but the outcome otherwise is as much in the Storyteller's hands as whether Recruit Devoted turns out a deep-cover spy or a press-ganged Renegade or a far-gone Feral.

        As far as generalizable notes, the following Universal Variations tend to be broadly applicable: Brachiation, Carapace, Enhanced Speed, Hyper-Competence, Immunity, Lash, Specialized Sense, and Superhuman (Attribute). Flashier and more esoteric fare will likely depend on the method of Divergence, which in turn will probably suggest an appropriate Clade and to a lesser extent the probable Origin. Following the standard character creation order of picking Variations and then picking Scars to counterbalance them is probably the best bet unless you have a Scar in mind from the jump.

        The fact that Projects are attached to Nodes responsible for ensuring they come to fruition and Launch Devoted Line specifically requires an appropriate Node for the task should lend some ideas about how the conspiracy is attempting to produce a Divergence just as surely as its Principles and Anchors would inform its reasons for doing so and/or what might complicate it. Like most supernatural beings with character full sheets, the Remade aren't really narratively suited to serving throwaway roles for which a true randomizer would be appropriate.

                                                As Shadowcat says, despite the exceptions for entangling Scars and Deviations, a Lash is not a Directed Variation and so cannot be Channeled — ranged Lashes can have drawbacks akin to those the system uses with other forms of ranged targeted effect, but the thing that the Variation "Lash" does is give you a new form of attack rather than serve as the attack itself.

                                                More stylistically, when the game disallows nesting Secondary Scars, it seems self-evident that recursive nested Variations are beyond the bounds of reason to permit and the system does not presently contain generic rules for stacking explosions having any effect on their blast radius.

                                                        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                        Some Node-based questions after reading the Devoted Companion again:

                                                        Does a Privileges-type Organizational Icon functioning "almost exactly like a Privilege" include its inability to be moved to another Node?
                                                        That wasn't my intent, but the entry implies it (the danger of using the space-saving "works the same as X" language, I suppose). If you want to allow Privilege Icons to be moved to another Node, I'd say it's the table's call to make.

                                                        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                        Is an "abandoned Node" just a Node formerly belonging to a conspiracy that has been destroyed, and/or can a Node that has been used in an Expel- or Reform-type purge be assimilated with a Complexity 1 Project as well?
                                                        The language here is a bit vague (sorry about that). The narrative goal is for a conspiracy to be able to find resources another conspiracy has abandoned or lost and assimilate them into itself. This can be a Node that has been purged or one destroyed due to the loss of its Linchpin. In some cases, it would even be appropriate to allow a conspiracy to assimilate one that was blown up, burned down, buried under rubble, sent to the bottom of the sea, etc., as the inspirational media often includes examples of an organization finding something powerful and dangerous in the ruins of a defunct organization. It shouldn't be used as a way to cheat by purging a Node to clear a bunch of damage to Association and then immediately spending a single Effort to assimilate the Node again a chapter later.

                                                        The upside is that this kind of Project is cheap. The man downside is that the conspiracy has no control over the nature of the Node it is assimilating; beggars can't be choosers (unless the conspiracy takes out a rival's Linchpin and then quickly assimilates the Node she led).
