            Originally posted by The MG View Post
            I am so very curious about how Relentless Variation interacts with a Controlled Scar, but I think I'll just have to wait until the manuscript drops tomorrow.

            It's going to be dope.
            Probably 12-ish hours before all will be revealed (barring James having a bout of insomnia), but the short version is the entangled Variation is "on" unless you pay its "activation cost" to temporarily suppress it. If the secondary Scar is Tribulation, for example, you spend Willpower to stop being gigantic instead of spending a point of Willpower to become gigantic. Instead of Power Failure being the secret weakness your enemies roll out to strip you of your powers, it's the thing you use to keep your always-on power from getting out of hand (Relentless Variation was originally pitched to me as "the Cyclops Scar", though it has expanded significantly since then).

                                This preview is everything I'd hoped it would be. Some thoughts, which I'll probably edit in more of as I go:

                                1) Miniaturization should probably function in a way that makes it compatible with Small-Framed(decreasing size by x, much like Giant does in reverse, instead of the first dot 'setting' the character to Size 4, for example).

                                2) Looking at Hybrid form made me reflect further on scar entanglement scaling. As long as I want exactly two Magnitude 4 abilities for a Magnitude 5 Scar, this works fine. But what if I want 1 Mag 4 ability and a selection of lower magnitude ones? As written, the rules don't support that.

                                3) Seeing a few instances of auras that roll to affect each target individually. This seems like it has the potential to bog down play.
