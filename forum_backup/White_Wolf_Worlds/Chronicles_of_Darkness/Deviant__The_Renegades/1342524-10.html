            Yes, this again. I just keep thinking of ways to tweak and rewrite it. Maybe, one day, I'll be finished.

            ...But it's more likely that we get something official that renders my work happily obsolete.

            Homunculus (Mutant Variation; • to •••••)
            Overt
            Discrete, Perpetual
            Part of the Remade’s power manifests through – or as – a separate entity.
            This Variation may be purchased any number of times, and each time it grants the Deviant control over a different entity.
            Though not Directed, Homunculus can be entangled with Directed-only Scars and Deviations that grant the Storyteller control over entangled Variations. In this case, they allow the Storyteller to determine what the entity does for a turn. Additionally, the entity’s rolls count as Scar Finesse rolls for the purposes of the Symbiote Form.
            The homunculus works as follows:
            • It uses one of two dice pools for all actions. Any action reflecting its nature (including activating or directing Variations) uses the larger pool, and all other actions use the smaller pool. These pools are 5/2 at Magnitude •, 7/3 at Magnitude ••, 9/4 at Magnitude •••, 11/5 at Magnitude ••••, and 13/6 at Magnitude •••••.
            • Any statistics or effects based on Attributes (such as Initiative, Defense, or resisted rolls) use the smaller pool instead, though Scar Attributes are capped at five.
            • Its Size can be anywhere between 3 and 7, and its Speed modifier is +5.
            • The entity possesses Variations, assigned as if entangled with a Scar of a Magnitude equal to its smaller pool - 1.
            • If the homunculus takes or heals Instability for any reason, treat it as points of damage of equivalent severity (lethal for minor, aggravated for medium or major). This damage can’t be healed by Variations.
            • The entity lacks a Willpower track, but accepting a point of lethal damage grants it the benefits of spending Willpower. This damage can’t be healed by Variations.

            If the homunculus dies, it returns to life at the beginning of the next story, or at the beginning of the next chapter if the Deviant accepts a medium Instability.

            Deviations
            • Malformed (-1-3 Magnitude): The homunculus suffers an unentangled Persistent Scar with a Magnitude based on the value of this Deviation (•• at +1, ••• or •••• at +2, and ••••• at +3).
            • Calcified (+1 Magnitude): One or more of the homunculus’ Toggled Variations become Perpetual.
            • Derived (+1-3 Magnitude): The entity develops additional Variations, assigned as if entangled with a Scar of a Magnitude based on the value of this Deviation (• or •• at +1, ••• or •••• at +2, and ••••• at +3). Part or all of this effective Magnitude can instead be added to the entity’s smaller pool for the purpose of entangling Variations.

            The Coactive’s shadow doesn’t follow her movements, instead acting on its own strange hungers (Persistent Drawback [Conspicuous Appearance]; Controlled; Dexterity; Deviation: Rebellious Variation).

            The Invasive “donated” a number of his limbs and organs to an autonomous combat drone, but it surprised his Progenitor by obeying him instead of its programming (Missing Limb; Persistent).

            Where others see something tall and crooked and skinless, the Mutant only sees his daughter (Hallucinations; Persistent).

            Design Notes
            It got a lot wordier this time around, which wasn’t the intention, but I think the improved clarity (the previous version called on Magnitude for stats, which becomes ambiguous once Deviations come into play) and power boost are worth it. Working out how exactly the Derived Variation should work took quite some time, and I’m still uncertain about it, but it at least fits my arbitrary limit of “only two Mag 5s per entity.”
            The biggest change here is that I abandoned my previous attempts at making the entity more autonomous, instead offloading them onto extant effects (such as Rebellious Variation).

                And now: Deviations. Deviations and Lash effects, most of them dreamt up over the course of a long walk under the unseasonably warm and sunny spectre of global warming.

                Variations

                Lash
                New Effects
                • Extended (Melee Lash Only): The Lash has a range of 15 yards/meters. It deals with concealment and cover like a ranged attack. An Extended Lash can take the Reach effect, increasing its range to 30 yards/meters; an Extended Deadly Ichor can trigger off ranged attacks made from within range, as long as they inflict the requisite damage.
                • Savage (Bruising/Cutting Lash Only): When attacking, increase the Lash’s damage rating by up to +3. Each point of increase penalizes the attack roll by -2, in addition to any other penalties.
                • Shield (Melee Lash Only): The Lash provides concealment against ranged attacks equal to either half Scar Power or half Magnitude (round up). It has an Initiative penalty one point lower than its concealment modifier.
                • Wrecking: When damaging an object or attacking through cover, subtract the higher of Magnitude or Scar Power from the object’s Durability.

                New Deviations
                • Singular Power (-2 Magnitude): As Channel, but the secondary Variation cannot be activated or Directed except through the Lash. Exclusive with Auxiliary Power and Channel.
                • Indiscriminate (+0 Magnitude): Requires Circle Strike or Full Auto. The Remade must attack all valid targets in range with her long bursts, including allies, but the Lash gains two additional non-Deviation effects.
                • Auxiliary Power (+1-3 Magnitude): As Channel, but the Remade doesn’t need to possess the secondary Variation. Instead, she is considered to have it at a Magnitude based on the level of this Deviation (• or •• at +1, ••• or •••• at +2, and ••••• at +3) for the purpose of channeling it through the Lash. Exclusive with Channel and Singular Power.

                Body Snatcher
                New Deviation
                • Disembodied (+1 Magnitude): The Remade disappears while he rides a target’s senses, and reappears within short range of the target when the possession ends. This Deviation is incompatible with the Body Swap option.

                Deadly Ichor
                New Effect
                • Warding (Gentle Lash Only): Successes on the attack roll cancel out the triggering attack’s successes on a two-for-one basis; this can’t reduce the damage below what is needed to trigger Deadly Ichor.

                Sacred Flesh
                New Deviations
                • Tainted (-1 Magnitude): Choose a Controlled Scar with a Magnitude equal to the Magnitude of the secondary Variation (minimum Magnitude ••). Activating the secondary Variation requires paying the Controlled Scar’s cost, though the user doesn’t need to make a Scar Finesse roll. When combined with Remote Control (see below), the Deviant pays the cost for forced activations in place of the target.
                • Remote Control (+1 Magnitude): Once per scene, the Remade can forcibly activate or deactivate the secondary Variation for a target in range as a Directed effect. If the secondary Variation is a Boon, she can bestow and activate it with the same Scar Finesse roll.

                Scars

                Amnesia
                New Deviation
                • Confabulation (+1 Magnitude): Replace the Amnesia Condition with False Memories (see below).
                False Memories (Persistent; Vampire: the Requiem Second Edition, p. 303)
                The way you remember things doesn’t match up with how they happened. You might remember a son who didn’t exist, your alcoholic father abusing you despite being raised an orphan, or never getting married. You believe your memories to be true no matter what; even conclusive proof has a hard time getting through to you. Being faced with proof that your memory is fake is a breaking point for Baselines; Remade suffer a minor Instability instead.
                Resolution: Face proof that your memory is false and succeed at the breaking point (Baselines); shed the Amnesia Scar (Deviants).
                Beat: Your character trusts someone or takes a risky action based on his faked memories alone.
                New Standard Deviations

                Access Control
                Scar Type:                 Controlled, Persistent
                Keywords: Toggled-Only (Persistent Scar Only)
                +1 Magnitude: Entangled Variations require authorization to activate. One of up to (Scar Resistance) secondary actors must enter a password, utter a spell, or otherwise grant their permission before the Deviant can unleash her powers. This takes an action, but can be done remotely if the secondary actor has a line of communication to the Deviant.
                • High Security (+1 Magnitude): Secondary actors must touch the Deviant to unlock the entangled Variations, or only a single individual (represented by a Touchstone or a Social Merit such as Allies or Retainer) has clearance to do so. If both apply, entangled Variations gain the Reflexive keyword.
                Sidebar: Lost Forever
                Usually, only conspiracies can grant the requisite clearance to unlock Variations with Access Control. Depending on the exact method (implanted RFID chip, occult ritual, etc.), Renegades may be able to transfer clearance to their allies, though never to themselves, and never to more individuals than her Scar Resistance allows.
                If all characters with clearance are killed (or all access keys are destroyed) with no way to replace them, liquidate the affected Variations as per the Sanctity of Merits and remove the Access Control Deviation, replacing it if caused by Instability.
                Extrinsic Variation
                Scar Type:                 Any
                +0-2 Magnitude: One or more entangled Variations are separate from the Deviant's body, taking the form of items of Size 3 or less. The character can't activate extrinsic Variations unless she uses the representative item (wielding a weapon, wearing armour or a piece of jewelry, etc.), and they deactivate immediately if she removes or loses it.
                Extrinsic Variations have a Durability of half Scar Resistance (round up), but no Structure. Instead, each point of damage taken subtracts one dot of effective Magnitude, which can be restored with a Build Equipment action with a built-in penalty of (6 - Scar Resistance). A Variation can't be destroyed beyond repair without the player's permission; the Sanctity of Merits applies.
                The value of this Deviation is +0 if applied to an Involuntary Scar, +1 if applied to a Controlled Scar, and +2 if applied to a Persistent Scar.

                Rooted
                Scar Type: Any
                +1 Magnitude: Entangled Variations require the Deviant to stay in place, whether due to drawing power from the earth beneath her feet, needing steady footing to resist their extreme recoil, or physically rooting her in place. Whatever the reason, the Remade can’t Dodge or move under her own power on the same turn as she uses or benefits from an entangled Variation, and loses access to the Variation for a turn if she Dodges or moves, and while under the effects of the Knocked Down Tilt. An interrupted Variation reactivates reflexively, without the Deviant having to repeat any roll, cost, or once-per-scene/chapter limitation.
                An Involuntary Variation with this Deviation that activates involuntarily prevents the Remade from moving or Dodging until she deactivates it.


                Design Notes
                Extended/Savage/Wrecking: I feel like these are self-explanatory, but what I can say is that I really didn't intend to do this much with Lash.

                Shield: For Lashes that include, well, some kind of shield, whether it's an actual sword-and-board of cosmic energy or some kind of fuckhuge bone-plated monster arm. It doesn't behave exactly a the shields in Hurt Locker, since it doesn't provide bonus Defense and can attack and protect at the same time, and unlike other non-Deviation effects, it allows you to choose the lower or the higher of Magnitude and Scar Power. This is to give you a choice between more protection and more Initiative.

                Auxiliary Power and Singular Power: I originally planned to do this as a Standard Deviation, but it felt silly making one that only relates to a single Variation. As to why there are two Deviations doing basically the same thing, I wanted to both give players some options (maybe you want an additional Scar, maybe you don't) and leave room for the hypothetical ST to only permit the version they prefer.

                Indiscriminate: The odd cost of this Deviation is due to it not quite feeling worth a -1 to me. Instead, it's equivalent to taking a -1 and a dot of the core Variation, without leaving room for additional Deviations.

                Disembodied: For all your horrible worm-person needs.

                Warding: Originally, this was just Dodging baked into a counter, but that opened a whole can of worms relating to Defense and action economy, so now it behaves as a weaker Dodge that stacks with Defense (and, in fact, Dodging), but doesn't eat your action and ignores penalties from multiple opponents. Naturally, it can only negate damage from attacks that could validly trigger your Deadly Ichor (melee attacks for a melee DI, for example).

                Tainted and Remote Control: These are pretty self-explanatory, but for some reason, I can only picture Deviants with Remote Control being just... unreasonably smug.

                Confabulation: This is only here because I happened to leaf through the Vampire corebook. Aside from the mechanical drawback of False Memories over Amnesia, misremembering your past will have natural story consequences.

                Access Control: This is just No Guns Life in Deviation form, and probably best idea among the bunch. It can't modify Involuntary Scars because you can pull off the same effect natively with Involuntary Stimulus.

                Extrinsic Variation: With the changes to Maintenance in the backer PDF, the previous version no longer made sense. In addition to that QoL fix, I decided to broaden it a little. Persistent/Perpetual Extrinsic Variation needs further scrutiny, I think, since it compares very favourably to Suppressible Variation, but I need other eyes on it to determine how to fix that.

                Rooted: I'm the least certain about this Deviation out of all the ones presented here. It's cool, but is it really a worth a +1, and if so, is it actually legible enough to use in actual play?

                        In which I mine other gamelines for mechanics.

                        Specifically, Arrogance and Instant Gratification are derived from the Competitive and Wanton Conditions from VtR, and are intended as sort of parallels to Bestial Mind, while Taboo builds on the Ban Condition from WtF. In that particular case, I only intended to take a look for some inspiration, but the extant mechanics were kind of perfect for what I wanted to do, so here we are.

                        Of these three, Instant Gratification is the least polished, so if you have any critique (on any of my homebrew, really, but particularly IG) I'm all ears.

                        Arrogance (Persistent Scar; • to •••••)
                        Keywords: Mental, Social; Subtle
                        The Deviant’s reach exceeds her grasp. For some Remade, this Scar is a direct result of the heady rush of their newfound power, while others come to it as a coping mechanism in the aftermath of the Divergence. Either way, their delusions of grandeur lead them into trouble and taint their interactions with lessers.
                        At Magnitude •, the Remade’s player can accept a Beat to automatically dramatically fail a social interaction without rolling, setting the other party off with conceit or near-instinctive putdowns.
                        At Magnitude ••, as Magnitude •, and the character can’t let others get the better of her. Any time she’s in direct competition with another character, she suffers a -2 penalty on any rolls where she doesn’t spend Willpower, including contested and extended rolls. Additionally, attempts to goad or coerce her into competition achieve exceptional success on three successes instead of five.
                        At Magnitude •••, as Magnitude ••, and whenever another character defeats, outdoes, or humiliates the Remade, she’s compelled to settle the score. If she fails to defeat or antagonize at least one such character before the end of the chapter, she takes a minor Instability. This doesn’t stack with Instability taken from lack of interaction with Touchstones.
                        At Magnitude ••••, as Magnitude •••, and choose one:
                        • Glory: Furthering a Conviction Touchstone (or settling the score as per the Magnitude ••• effect) now requires the Deviant to be seen, leave a calling card, or otherwise make sure that the victim knows it was her. Additionally, she doesn’t heal Instability from killing a Conviction Touchstone unless she first holds him in her power to gloat or otherwise displays her superiority over him.
                        • Pride: Once per chapter, when the Remade is defeated, outdone, or humiliated, she must accept a medium Instability or name the offender as a Conviction Touchstone. If she has no free slots (and the offender isn’t already a Loyalty Touchstone), she instead takes a minor Instability. This replaces the normal Magnitude ••• effect.

                        At Magnitude •••••, as Magnitude •••• but both effects apply.

                        Instant Gratification (Persistent Scar; • to •••••)
                        Keywords: Mental, Social; Subtle
                        Overindulgence has become a balm for the character’s broken soul, but she doesn’t know when enough is enough. For many Deviants, this Scar represents very human hedonism: drugs, sex, food, drink. Others, burdened with stranger urges (often via Scars like Dependency or Addictive Variation), take the edge off with orgies of human meat and flagrant displays of power. Either way, the issue is often less the need itself, and more the Remade’s inability to say no to it.
                        At Magnitude •, the Remade’s player can accept a Beat for the character to allow her vice to harm or debilitate her, whether by leaving her incapacitated by a hangover or blinding her to a threat.
                        At Magnitude ••, as Magnitude •, and Composure or Resolve rolls to resist temptation suffer a -2 penalty. As well, characters offering or tempting the Deviant with indulgence achieve exceptional success on three successes instead of five, whether using supernatural powers or mundane social influence.
                        At Magnitude •••, as Magnitude ••, and choose one:
                        • Irresistible: When rolling to resist temptation, downgrade the result one step (exceptional success to success, success to failure, failure to dramatic failure) unless the Remade spends a point of Willpower. The expenditure does not add to the roll.
                        • Self-Destructive: Indulgence inflicts one of either the Fatigued or Intoxicated Conditions.

                        At Magnitude ••••, as Magnitude •••, but the Deviant suffers both effects.

                        Deviations
                        • Association (+1 Magnitude): The Remade is a bad influence on those around her. Allies, including Loyalty Touchstones and members of her cohort, suffer the effects of this Scar in the Deviant’s presence. Instant Gratification needs to be at least Magnitude •• to take this Deviation.

                        Taboo (Persistent Scar; • to •••••)
                        Keywords: Mental, Social; Subtle; Repeatable
                        The Remade’s behaviour is restricted by a forbiddance or compulsion, whether due to some occult need, brainwashing, or simply a code of conduct wound too tightly around her identity.
                        At Magnitude •, take a Beat when the Deviant’s adherence to the taboo causes her or her cohort a setback.
                        At Magnitude ••, as Magnitude •, and the Remade suffers a -3 penalty to all rolls made as part of an action that violates the taboo. If breaking the taboo would not involve any rolls, she instead suffers a -2 penalty to all dice pools until the end of the chapter or until she corrects the breach (such as by admitting to a lie). These penalties don’t affect Faltering rolls.
                        At Magnitude •••, as Magnitude ••, and choose one:
                        • Dilemma: Faltering rolls made in the course of upholding or violating the taboo inflict an additional minor Instability.
                        • Forbiddance: The penalties from the Magnitude •• effect increase to -5 and -3, respectively.
                        • Penance: Violating the taboo increases the Magnitude of another Scar by 1 (chosen or selected at random by the Storyteller) until the end of the chapter or until the Deviant corrects the breach. This does not allow her to develop a new Variation or increase the Magnitude of a Variation.

                        At Magnitude ••••, as Magnitude •••, but choose two options
                        At Magnitude •••••, as Magnitude •••, but the Deviant suffers all three effects.

                        Deviations
                        • Narrow (-1 Magnitude): The Remade’s taboo is particularly narrow or otherwise unlikely to come up in the chronicle.
                        • Complex (+1 Magnitude): Instead of a single taboo, the Deviant labours under a number of interrelated restrictions.

                            A while back I recast an Invasive Variation for Chimerics, so I figured I'd return the favor for the cyborgs. This is also an attempt to recreate an effect from Mutants & Masterminds.

                            Flux Array (Invasive Variation, •• - •••••)

                            Subtle

                            Discrete, Toggled

                            The Deviant possesses a Variation that can function as different Variations.

                            Choose a number of secondary Variations in the same way as you would for Monstrous Transformation. Chosen secondary Variations cannot stipulate that can only be entangled with Persistent Scars (such as certain aspects of Anomalous Biology). When Flux Array is activated, choose one of these secondary Variations to activate. Only one secondary Variation may be active at a time, but the Deviant may switch between secondary Variations with an instant action. Effects that directly target Variations only target the active secondary Variation.

                            Although Subtle by default, secondary Variations may be Overt, and incur the consequences for such while active.

                            The Coactive embodies the gods of both life and death, whose entwined presences sometimes overwhelm her will (Alternate Persona; Persistent; Resolve; Secondary Variations: Healer, Lash).

                            The Invasive's atomic reconfiguration engine allows him to alter the size, shape, and density of his body in multiple ways, though the long-term consequences for this process are still unknown (Unstable Variation; Controlled; Wits; Secondary Variations: Animal Transformation, Carapace, Gigantic, Miniaturization).

                            With enough focus, the Mutant can force his ever-changing DNA to generate useful adaptations (Concentration; Controlled; Wits; Secondary Variations: Aquatic, Enhanced Speed, Flight).

                            --

                            And here's a Deviation for Sacred Flesh I thought of while on a walk:

                            Addictive (+1 Magnitude): Anyone who benefits from the secondary Variation must succeed on a Scar Resistance roll at the end of the scene or gain the Addicted Condition for the Variation. Social rolls the Deviant makes against addicted persons when using Sacred Flesh as leverage achieve an exceptional success with three successes instead of five.

                                                            Externalized (Form)

                                                            The Remade’s Divergence is intrinsically tied to an outside source (called a “shell”), such as a suit of power armor that he wears, a vat-grown body into which she projects her consciousness, a robotic puppet they command from a central HQ, or so on.

                                                            When not connected to their shell, the Remade does not possess any of their starting Variations or Scars, though all other aspects of the Deviant template (Conviction/Loyalty, Acclimation, Instability, etc) still apply. Persistent Scars still affect the shell (when applicable) even while it is offline.

                                                            Connecting to the shell requires five minutes of activity (five turns in action scenes), and a roll determined at character creation, composed either of Attribute + Attribute + Acclimation, or Attribute + Skill + Acclimation.

                                                            Roll Results

                                                            Success: The Remade connects with the shell without a hitch, and has immediate access to all Variations (and entangled Scars).

                                                            Exceptional Success: The Deviant’s connection goes particularly smoothly, healing a minor Instability in the process.

                                                            Failure: The Deviant has trouble connecting to the shell. He can either try again after five more minutes, or suffer a minor Instability to force the connection immediately.

                                                            Dramatic Failure: Something goes terribly wrong with the connection. The Broken immediately suffers a medium Instability.

                                                            The shell shares the same Skills, as well as Mental and Social Attributes as the Deviant. The player may rearrange Physical Attribute dots for the shell at character creation, if they so choose. Physical Merit dots can also be rearranged. Mechanical or otherwise inorganic shells possess Structure instead of Health, as well as Durability equal to half Acclimation (rounded up). However, they do not heal naturally and so require occasional repairs. Inorganic shells cannot take Scars that slow or inhibit natural healing. Biological shells possess Health and heal at the same rate as Baselines (unless a Variation or Scar alters this). If the shell is destroyed, the Remade immediately suffers a major Instability. If the Deviant is physically present inside the shell at the time of destruction, he suffers a point of lethal damage, otherwise, he loses a point of Willpower. A destroyed shell can be made functional again with an extended action of an appropriate dice pool, requiring successes equal to the total Magnitude of Variations the shell possesses. Add the Deviant’s Acclimation to the total number of rolls that can be made if he is the one performing the repairs.

                                                            When the Deviant gains new Scars from Instability, the player chooses whether the Scar affects the Remade, the shell, or both. Any new Variations entangled with this Scar go to the same.
