                Originally posted by cybirddude View Post
                Does a Deviant's Progenitor have to be human, or can another supernatural produce a Deviant? Some examples of the latter would be a werewolf trying to force a First Change, a Mage trying to force an awakening, or a vampire bloodline whose embrace turns people into Deviants rather than more vampires.
                The Divergence is specifically produced by forms of power that don't have a guiding magical force behind them like Luna or the God-Machine or a vampire; different splats tend to have their own variations on "your soul cracked open and stuff's gone wrong with it" (e.g. the Enraptured, the Rampant, the Broken Souls) and, like Promethean 1e points out for demiurges, better-understood types of monster tend to have more readily available ways to make other monsters than the unreliable and poorly-understood process of engineering a Deviant who will survive the Divergence and not go Feral from failure to form connections. Thematically, the game is built around Baselines exploiting magic (or science, or spiritualism) for power at the expense of other people's humanity.

                                Originally posted by cybirddude View Post
                                Also, while we are on the subject of disabilities, what scars could represent artificial limbs (realistic ones not sci fi ones)?
                                If you're asking for Scars relating to missing or limited limbs and senses, that's Missing Limb and Sensory Deprivation, respectively. If you're asking about Scars that can represent having obvious artificial limbs that serve as an identifying feature, Conspicuous Appearance at low Magnitudes can fill that role. If you're asking about just having artificial limbs, that's not a Scar unless it's intrinsic enough to the Deviant's condition to hang Variations off of by stemming from the Instability of their soul.

                                                04-30-2023, 10:03 PM
                                                Originally posted by Maina View Post
                                                Is there a rule in the book that explains what happens if a Scar and a Variation are in conflict?
                                                There's the "Choosing Scars" sidebar on p173 as far as advice goes, and the general precedent seems to be that Scars take precedent in situations where that wouldn't completely negate the Variation's whole effect (i.e. if you've got Anomalous Biology [Tireless] it doesn't make sense to allow the character to get Scar Magnitude from Dependency [Fatigued]).

                                                The specific case is Frozen Heart (-3 untrained penalty for social skills) and Omnicompetent (no untrained penalty).
                                                I'd default to Frozen Heart applying the penalty here, because Omnicompetence negating the untrained penalty still applies to other categories and once you're past Magnitude 1 the Variation can circumvent the penalty by just temporarily having dots in the Skill you're using. You could also, if preferred, say that Omnicompetence 1 applies since Frozen Heart 2 still has another effect even without untrained penalties, but that's to taste.
