    101 Deviant Orgs

    09-19-2016, 07:17 PM
    So Deviant is coming sometime semi-soon, I think we should get some ideas for some Deviant Orgs out there. Silly, spooky, cool, what-have-you, sky's the limit.

    1) The Archon Conglomerate: Founded by Seer Magi, the company was taken over by their Proximus slave-apprentices, who have been attempting to manufacture Awakened Souls. They promised you unimaginable wealth, in exchange for a few drug treatments. Now you can see everything.

    2) Last Dynasty International: The old man paid you to steal some files about something called an "amkhat". You were pretty upset to learn what exactly that was, and how sharp its teeth are. They were pretty upset, too, but as long as you steal for them, they say they'll make sure the poison doesn't spread any further.

    3) The Church of the Dawn: All your life, you worshiped it, along with your family in friends, in a compound in Nevada. Then it chose you as its priest. It showed you its glorious countenance, but all you saw was a demon born of fire and dread light, burning its profane scriptures into your skin in ancient holy tongues.

    4) The Department of Postcognition: According to the government, you died in the Gulf. According to the CIA, you're an analyst. According to you, well, you don't know what you are. All you know is that you can remember things that never happened to you. Every time you remember, the memories stay a little longer. They said you'd only work the job for five years and then get a cushy severance package. Then you remembered the last guy's last day.

    5) The Intersect Program: It started when your jackass former friend from Stanford sent you an email. Now you have the sum total of the CIA and NSA's intelligence hidden in the folds of your brain. Life at the Buy-More will never be the same.

                      10) The Emerald Circle: You were working on something great. An alchemical cure for cancer, Parkinson's, dementia, any genetic disease in the world. The Circle found you, and promised you that they would help-and they did. At the cost of sapient beings condemned to endless misery because you needed their Vitriol. Even if you didn't die a little inside you cut open a Promethean, you knew attempting to help the world on the backs of a hundred or so homunculi was never going to work. So, you decided, you needed a way to sublimate your own life experiences into Vitriol-sacrificing the self for the good of the whole, instead of the completely blameless other. Good news-you now have an exciting enough life to the point where you don't lose much more of your actual self when you spit up the clear, miraculous acid. Bad news is, that's because you forgot something-the people who are willing to dissect sapient beings for resources are more than cold enough to ignore that fact that their subject and source used to be their buddy. At least it isn't Amalgam.

                      11) Cult of Elar'dhi: What the hell were you thinking? Oh that's right-you're codependent, so when your lesbian crush (who was hetero, for extra pathetic points) killed herself, you fell apart so badly that when the Violinist told you of a song that could contact the dead, you were so desperate to hear her again you joined that fucking nutcase support group. Oh, they taught you the song all right-they also taught you the fact that it was also very much alive, a blind idiot dissonance that wanted (to the extent a disincarnate entity made of cosmic echos wants anything) to disrupt the song that holds the living and the dead apart. And you, oh so noble martyr, possessed the exact disorder needed to become the Herald of the Last Echo. The rite worked-you now have a voice in your mind made up of thousands of other voices, all telling you to destroy things and return all that makes sound into a harmony of discordance. And, uh, the fact that the Violinist and all of his gang are emo omnicidal psychos who deeply resent the fact that you still look and think human. That's not fun.

                                  14) Weapon Chi: They said they wanted to help you understand your powers, control them, maybe give you a semblance of a normal life. Thankfully, their doctor had more scruples than the people in charge. Your control chip "failed", you ran, and you've been running ever since.

                                  15) The Order of the Quivering Palm: You learned at the Monastery of the Sun Lotus, at your Master's feet for ten years, when your whole order was slain by the Quivering Palms, who sought an ancient forbidden technique that only the Master knew. You escaped with the last scroll of the technique. The Palms are deadly and driven, and your only hope may be the secrets of the scroll. But is the technique even worth protecting?

                                  16) The Ordo Dracul: You had a normal enough life, working as a geneticist, until you had a run-in with the last person you'd expect: Your great-great-great-great-grandpa. He promised you access to something that would assure a Nobel Prize, the blood of a vampire. You agreed, and he gave you his blood, both to study and to feed on. He thought he could keep you dependent, but you were smarter than that. You perfected a treatment that freed you from the need to feed, and even granted you a measure of "granpappy's" powers, provided you kept to a very particular cocktail of intravenous amino acids, synthetic proteins, and reverse transcriptase inhibitors. When you decided to keep this information to yourself, your granpappy was, to say the least, displeased, and his friends weren't too happy, either.

                                  17) Tristan Cybernetics: You were just walking by when somebody crashed a semi into the side of an office building. You were caught in the rubble, but somehow you managed to dig yourself free. It wasn't until you got an X-ray that you realized that...something...metal had bonded itself to your skeleton, something that didn't like the idea of being removed, even by its original owners.

                                          20: The Cheiron Group: Internship your ass! Whatever they put into you, they didn't stop putting it in. Surgery after surgery, graft after graft, you can barely remember what your old body looks like some days. They threw around phrases like "interstatial acquisition", "hemophiliac augment", words that sounded medical but turned out to be anything but. Afraid and unsure of what to do, you managed to escape one night from the labs you and others like you were being held in. You know that if they find you, you're as good as dissected. Is there anyone that can help at this point?

                                          21: VASCU: Your mind, you know they did something to your mind. Your brain was normal, you know that. You remember that they talked to you, said you could become someone good. They put you in a chair, gave you injections, but your mind is hazy in-between then and now. Something changed, your whole body changed. You don't feel like you know you used to feel, right? Why does your body feel one way and your brain another? You look like other people, but inside your head there's a screaming thing trying to claw it's way out. And you can see it changing your body more and more each day.

                                          22: Les Mysteries: You trusted the spirits, thought they were something good for you and your loved ones. They convinced you to find others like you, other people who had the same gifts. Then it happened, one night the spirits wanted more than you were willing to give. They took your body, turned it into a shrine to self. By the time your allies found you the spirit's work was irreversible. Somehow you survived, when everyone around you said that you should by all rights be dead. Is there any going back?

                                          23: The Lucifuge: Not all human, not all demon. You were born with cursed blood, but soon it became a cursed body. Your "family" tried to help you learn control, to become something more than just another tool of Satan's works. It didn't take. One night you realized that you weren't able to shift your body back to being fully human, that whatever your demon-parent was, they had left an indelible mark not only on your soul but your flesh. It's going to rip you apart one day, and no one can tell you any way to stop it.

                                                          29) Bugster Genome Project

                                                          It started with the headaches. And then the fevers. And then the passing out. And then the hallucinations: what you could best describe as little yellow men jumping out of your skin. When you were taken the hospital, the doctors had no idea what you had. They know you had a viral infection, yes, but none they had seen before. Then the men in suits and lab coats came. They tell you that you're Patient Zero of a brand new virus. And that if something isn't done NOW, there will be an epidemic the likes of not seen in centuries. Luckily for you, they have some new experimental medical technology that they believe will be just the thing that'll pick you back up. Make you as good as new...

                                                          ...except not really. While some of your symptoms have resided some are still there. Namely the headaches and the hallucinations. And your body has been feeling stranger too in general: lighter, but weight-ier, if that made any sense. Not helping were the regular rotation of new and different people each day during your stay, and how you were constantly referred to as "the Subject". Having enough of it, and deciding that you felt well enough anyways, you ended up leaving...and knew something was off when you were met by men with guns as you left.

                                                          You managed to escape with your life, now on the run from the Genome Project that would like nothing more then their subject and their experimental medical equipment back. Of course you have bigger things to worry about: those headaches still bug you from time to time. And your hallucinations seem to be acting up again: since you seem to see the tiny yellow men crawling on the skin on OTHER people since you left.
