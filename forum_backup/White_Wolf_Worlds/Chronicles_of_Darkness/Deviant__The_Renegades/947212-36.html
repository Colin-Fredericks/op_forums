    Well that's just great. I think I'm addicted to this now... SO MANY POSSIBILITIES!


    ------------------------------------


    1. A perverted geneticist in Japan really wanted to create his own cat girl. Maybe he should have tried using house cats, instead of tigers!


    2. A wannabe witch dabbles in a grimoire she can barely translate and ends up casting the wrong spell. Now, her soul has merged with an ethereal being and the two struggle for control of her body.


    3. Puberty is never pretty, your body starts going through all these changes and it's really confusing at first. But nothing in your sex ed class mentioned anything about mutating into an entirely different species! Turns out, somebody in your recent ancestry was an alien shapeshifter, and you inherited their genes. Now, men in black suits are trying to capture you for research.


    4. A crippled soldier offers himself for testing a new form of experimental prosthetics. Upon waking up from the surgery, he finds that his new implants include military grade hardware and is told his body is now property of the government! They're pretty sure that this is a violation of their basic human rights, however, so when the government assigns them to their first mission, they go AWOL.


    5. Psionic abilities run strong in your family; they've been cultivating them for centuries. And now after countless generations of carefully selected breeding, they've produced you. One of the most talented psychics in the family's collective history. It wouldn't be so bad, if your family wasn't obsessed with power. They don't see you as their relative, they see you as an asset to be used and exploited. You couldn't stomach it anymore, so you ran away from home. That's when your parents put out a reward for your 'safe' return!

                                    The Singularity: While testing a prototype neural interface technology, a catastrophic power surge results in the beta testers' minds being separated from their bodies. Now they're trapped in cyberspace, battling Angels and Demons from the inside out!

                                    50% Off Deal: In an amazing stroke of luck, a demon botched on setting the proper terms for a soul pact and ended up with only half the mortal's soul.
