                    The premise of Deviant is that the powers of the transformed come with metaphysical damage to the soul that messes with their ability to maintain normal relationships and supernaturally reinforces various issues (at best giving them drawbacks to any powers that manifest at the same time).

                                I intend to get around to monteparnas' proposal a bit later. But first, since this is the aa forum:
                                Princesses among the Deviants
                                Princesses, known among themselves as the Hopeful, are individuals whose intense passions and empathy have caused them to Blossom, unlocking magical powers fueled by the Light. They fight a desperate, largely unseen war against the All-Consuming Darkness—an omnipresent metaphysical force that feeds on human fear, spawning monsters and corrupting souls. Divided into the Radiant and Twilight Courts, the Hopeful must balance their mundane lives with their supernatural duties, donning magical Regalia to battle the creatures of the dark.
                                Threat Level: 4

                                Themes
                                Princess: The Hopeful explores what it means to hold onto empathy and hope in a world ruled by fear and isolation. Like Renegades, the Hopeful are transformed individuals who must carefully navigate their mundane lives alongside their supernatural reality, relying heavily on Touchstones to keep their volatile souls intact. The tragic slide of a Deviant into End Stage Instability perfectly mirrors a Princess whose Belief drops to zero, transforming her into a horrific, irredeemable monster known as a Dethroned.

                                Allies
                                Radiant Princesses are naturally empathetic and driven to help victims of abuse, exploitation, and despair. They make staunch allies for Renegades, often viewing the Broken as wounded souls in need of healing and protection from the conspiracies that abused them. A cohort might find a Princess to be a tireless (if occasionally naive) defender against their mutual enemies.

                                Enemies
                                A cohort whose pursuit of vengeance causes massive collateral damage, or who preys on innocents to survive, will quickly draw the ire of Radiant Guardians. Meanwhile, Twilight Nemeses might target Deviants whose chaotic powers or monstrous Scars trigger their Victim Profiles, viewing the Broken as tainted aberrations of the Darkness that must be violently purged.

                                Web of Pain
                                The Twilight Courts operate seamlessly as conspiracies within the Web of Pain. The Court of Tears runs authoritarian, bureaucratic Enclaves to tax hope and resources from the populace; the Court of Storms fields ruthless paramilitary cults to annihilate any perceived taint of the Darkness; and the Court of Mirrors builds narcissistic mystery cults around their True Heirs. They readily create, recruit, or exploit the Remade to serve as foot soldiers, lab rats, or weapons, seeing them as expendable tools in their cosmic wars.

                                Greater Form: Princess
                                The creature is one of the Hopeful, a magical being driven by intense passions. Anchored entirely by extremes of love and hate, she exists in a volatile spiritual state, locked into either the Radiant or Twilight Courts.
                                • Supernatural Potency (Inner Light): Dictates maximum Attribute ratings, caps Wisp capacity, and serves as Supernatural Tolerance. Sensitivity Drawback: Witnessing suffering, cruelty, or Pure Darkness triggers a mental or physical attack. The Princess must roll Inner Light; failure generates Shadows (Instability), which erode her magic and well-being.
                                • Fuel Trait (Wisps): Motes of positive emotion and magical energy. Maximum capacity equals Inner Light. Spend 1 Wisp to reflexively fuel Defiance (Holy Shield) to downgrade or negate incoming damage. She may spend unlimited Wisps per turn. Regain Wisps via: Call of Duty (meaningfully furthering Calling duties), Inner Strength (spending Willpower in immediate danger, or reflexively converting unspent Willpower), or Circle of Light (socializing with friends and family).
                                • Integrity Trait (Belief): Starting at 7 (or 8 for a Onceborn), Belief is a three-tiered track measuring moral conviction: Paragon (10-8), Idealist (7-4), and Pragmatist (3-1). Faltering against Touchstones triggers a Belief Compromise, risking Belief damage, Shadows, and negative Conditions. Failing to further a Touchstone in a chapter inflicts a minor Instability.
                                • Virtue and Vice (Drive and Dread): Due to her inherently empathetic and driven nature, a Princess retains a Baseline's Virtue and Vice, often reframed as Drive and Dread. These supplement her Loyalty and Conviction rather than replacing them. Indulging Vice/Dread restores 1 Willpower and heals a minor Instability. Meaningfully upholding Virtue/Drive despite danger restores all Willpower and heals a minor or medium Instability.
                                • End Stage (The Dethroned): If Belief reaches 0, Inner Light gutters out into a cold, silent absence. The Princess reverts to her mundane identity, loses all powers, and sheds one Willpower dot per hour as her soul disintegrates. Reaching 0 Willpower triggers a final, horrific transformation into an irredeemable monster of the Darkness known as the Dethroned.


                                The Courts
                                Princesses must align with the Radiant or Twilight Courts, functioning as Guardians or Nemeses. In this state of spiritual equilibrium, she regains one extra Willpower per day (even without restful sleep), ignores automatic minor Instabilities at story start, and automatically heals one minor Instability at chapter start.

                                Radiant Guardians
                                Function in a state of Catharsis; Loyalty outweighs Conviction.
                                • Touchstones: Begins with more Loyalty than Conviction.
                                • The Fall: If Conviction ever equals or exceeds active Loyalty, she loses Catharsis and Falters on her Loyalty Touchstones. She spirals toward becoming Feral (The Dethroned) unless she seeks refuge in a Twilight conspiracy.


                                Twilight Nemeses
                                Function in a state of Fury; Conviction outweighs Loyalty.
                                • Touchstones: Begins with more Conviction than Loyalty. Purging targets heals her soul; showing mercy inflicts Instability.
                                • The Redemption: If active Loyalty ever equals or exceeds Conviction, she loses Fury and Falters on her Conviction Touchstones. As toxic hatreds break, new bonds of Loyalty catch her, allowing a safe transition back to Radiant Catharsis.


                                Lesser Forms

                                Lesser Form: Calling
                                Defines her approach to fighting the Darkness. With the Charms Form, grants an affinity for three Charm families. With the Transformation Lesser Form, she gains a free Transformed Attribute dot. By itself, the Form grants Sacred Code if Radiant or Victim Profile if Twilight.
                                • Champion: +1 Strength or Resolve. Affinities: Bless, Fight, Perfect.
                                  • Radiant Code: Defend the weak, shun violence for personal gain, never back down.
                                  • Victim Profile (Tears): Operates outside Alhambra's law, hoards hope/resources, actively resists Tithe collection.
                                  • Victim Profile (Storms): Operates outside Twilight authority, bears Darkness taint, harmed an innocent.
                                  • Victim Profile (Mirrors): Rival for leadership, criticized the Princess's combat prowess, refuses to submit to her "protection."
                                • Grace: +1 Presence or Composure. Affinities: Bless, Connect, Govern.
                                  • Radiant Code: Take responsibility, uplift others, never magically manipulate.
                                  • Victim Profile (Tears): Mundane authority figure, resisted the Princess, refuses emotional tribute to Alhambra.
                                  • Victim Profile (Storms): Preaches peace or compromise, associated with a target of the Princess's wrath, obstructs the crusade.
                                  • Victim Profile (Mirrors): Social influencer, ignored the Princess, refuses to acknowledge her as the True Heir.
                                • Mender: +1 Intelligence or Dexterity. Affinities: Perfect, Restore, Shape.
                                  • Radiant Code: Provide requested aid, mitigate suffering, never harm a patient.
                                  • Victim Profile (Tears): Member of a rival Court, wastes magical resources, threatens Alhambra's infrastructure.
                                  • Victim Profile (Storms): Cultist, corrupted physiology, inflicted structural damage.
                                  • Victim Profile (Mirrors): Offers aid to others instead of the Princess, refused the Princess's "healing", shows independence.
                                • Seeker: +1 Intelligence or Wits. Affinities: Appear, Govern, Learn.
                                  • Radiant Code: Seek truth, never cover up wrongdoing, accept no unsolvable mystery.
                                  • Victim Profile (Tears): Conceals forbidden truths, rebel cell member, lies to the Princess.
                                  • Victim Profile (Storms): Hides creatures of Darkness, uses stealth/deception to avoid the crusade, bears a Dark taint.
                                  • Victim Profile (Mirrors): Uncovered the Princess's flaws, refuses to believe the Princess's narrative, spreads "lies" about the True Heir.
                                • Troubadour: +1 Presence or Dexterity. Affinities: Appear, Inspire, Shape.
                                  • Radiant Code: Awaken audiences, eschew ego-driven art, never cease creating.
                                  • Victim Profile (Tears): Inspires hope in the Rebellious Provinces, refuses to surrender Wisps to Alhambra, openly joyful.
                                  • Victim Profile (Storms): Creates art depicting peace/mercy, distracts the Sworn Guard, associated with the Darkness.
                                  • Victim Profile (Mirrors): Mocked the Princess, rival agent, steals the spotlight.


                                Lesser Form: Charms
                                This Form grants access to four Charms (Scar-free Magnitude 2 Variations). If the character possesses the Calling Lesser Form, her four free Charms must be chosen from among her Calling's Affinities, and improving them or purchasing new Affinity Charms costs only 2 Experiences per Magnitude. Without a Calling, her free Charms can be chosen without restriction, but all Charms cost the standard 3 Experiences per Magnitude to improve or purchase.
                                • Appear (Illusions & Stealth): Camouflage, Face Thief, Holographic Projection, Shadow Selves.
                                • Bless (Enhancements & Luck): Blessing, Hyper-Competence, Superhuman Attribute.
                                • Connect (Social bonds & Manipulation): Fate-Bind, Pheromones, Psycho-Onomastics, Telepathy.
                                • Fight (Combat prowess): Carapace, Lash, Kiai (Buster/Deadly Ichor).
                                • Govern (Supernatural control & Wards): Fate's Agent, Occult Singularity, Otherworldly Connection, Soul Seer.
                                • Inspire (Crowd control & Emotion): Delude, Emotion Amplification, Vocalization.
                                • Learn (Senses & Knowledge): Clairvoyance, Internet Acuity, Precognition, Shadows of the Past, Specialized Sense.
                                • Perfect (Movement & Self-defense): Aquatic, Brachiation, Enhanced Speed, Environmental Adaptation, Flight, Immunity.
                                • Restore (Healing & Purging): Absorb Instability, Healer, Rapid Healing.
                                • Shape (Elements & Matter): Aerokinesis, Conjuration, Cryokinesis, Electrokinesis, Hydrokinesis, Matter Control, Matter Emulation, Mechanical Transformation, Plant Control, Pyrokinesis, Terrakinesis.


                                Lesser Form: Transformation & Secret Identity
                                The Princess possesses a Phylactery, a physical representation of her Belief.
                                • The Transformation: Instant action once per scene (or reflexive with 1 Wisp/exceptional success). Grants Transformed Traits and Regalia. Bypasses the risk to her Secret Identity when using Charms.
                                • Transformed Traits: Adds Transformed dots to Baseline Attributes/Skills, recalculating Advantages. Transformed Health damage doesn't wrap to mundane Health; Transformed Willpower can only be spent while Transformed. Transformed Traits cost one Experience less than normal. If the character lacks the Greater Form's Inner Light to cap these traits, her maximum Transformed dots across all traits cannot exceed the Magnitude of her Transformation Variation.
                                • Regalia (Preparation & Catalyst): Magical gear modeled as Variations entangled with the Preparation Scar and Catalyst Deviation. Because Regalia is personalized, it functions as a +2 or +3 Magnitude Catalyst. Catalysts manifest upon Transformation, and can be dismissed and resummoned at will.
                                • Secret Identity: Grants the Secret Identity Merit free. Enemies cannot magically link her personas. Using an Overt Charm or a Subtle Charm (Magnitude 3+) untransformed suppresses this Merit for the chapter.


                                Lesser Form: Invocations
                                Once per scene, reflexively apply an Invocation to add its dots to a Charm's activation pool. Applying an Invocation requires the Princess to meet a specific philosophical condition.
                                • The Radiant Queens:
                                  • Acqua (Currents/Diamonds): Requires clear and careful thought.
                                  • Aria (Spades): Requires joy, freedom, or unconventional methods.
                                  • Fuoco (Swords): Requires love and unbridled passion.
                                  • Legno (Flowers/Clubs): Requires harmony, resilience, and finding balance.
                                  • Terra (Jewels/Hearts): Requires building relationships, trust, and society.
                                • The Twilight Queens (Conspiracies): Because the Twilight Queens operate as vast conspiracies within the Web of Pain, learning their Invocations also grants (or requires) the Devoted Form (Advanced).
                                  • Lacrima (Tears): Requires sacrificing others to preserve what is left (Devoted to Alhambra).
                                  • Tempesta (Storms): Requires caustic hatred and merciless destruction of the Darkness (Devoted to the Sworn Guard).
                                  • Specchio (Mirrors): Requires vanity and exalting oneself above all others (Devoted to the Cult of the True Heir).
                                • Philosophies as Touchstones: Each Invocation acts as a Touchstone; upholding its ideals restores Willpower and heals Shadows. Violating its Ban triggers a Belief Compromise. Without the Transformation Form, Invocation Touchstones take up regular Touchstone slots. If she does possess the Transformation Form, Invocation Touchstones are considered part of the Transformed state and are only accessible while Transformed.


                                Lesser Form: Crawlspace
                                Physically enter the Dreamlands through a localized threshold (like a closet) via an extended Inner Light + Empathy + Shadows roll.

                                The Light-Touched

                                New Form Variant: Dedicated (Light-Touched)
                                Used by Beacons and Sworn. The character remains a Baseline and does not Diverge through Instability.
                                • The Blossoming Trigger: The character only Diverges into a full Princess narratively: by facing a severe crisis, taking personal responsibility, and finding the inner strength to act.
                                • Crushed Hope: Accumulating too much Instability (dropping to 0 Integrity) crushes their spirit. They lose the Dedicated Form and all magical benefits, reverting to a normal, traumatized Baseline.


                                THE BEACONS (Threat 1-2)
                                Everyday humans with a tiny, unconscious spark of the Light.
                                • Base Form: Dedicated (Light-Touched Variant).
                                • Adaptation (The Spark of Hope): Once per chapter, when the Beacon spends Willpower in immediate danger or fulfills her Virtue/Drive, she may reset one per-scene/chapter ability.
                                • Drawback (Sensitivity): Rolls Integrity to resist accumulating Shadows when witnessing cruelty.
                                • The Quiet Echo: Allies in her immediate presence gain +1 to breaking point rolls caused by fear or despair.
                                • Lesser Form: Charms (The Spark): Possesses exactly one Charm (Magnitude 1 or 2 Variation) locked into a single, highly specific application. Activates once per scene.


                                THE SWORN (Threat 2-3)
                                Mortals formally initiated into the Light to serve the Nobility.
                                • Base Forms: Dedicated (Light-Touched Variant). Pledging to an authoritarian or highly organized faction grants the Devoted Form.
                                • Adaptation (Oathbound Magic): Cannot easily fuel his own magic. A Princess may spend her Wisps or use her Adaptation to reset the Sworn's per-scene/chapter features (requires 1 minute of physical contact to confirm his oath). Bequests may have independent reset conditions.
                                • Drawback (Sensitivity): Rolls Integrity to resist Shadows.
                                • Lesser Form: Invocations: Gains 1 free dot in his Queen's Invocation, acting as a Touchstone (restores Willpower/risks Integrity). Can be reflexively applied to Charms.
                                • Lesser Form: Charms (Bequests): Gains 2-4 Charms. Every Charm must use the Preparation (Catalyst) Scar, representing physical magical items. Losing the Bequest removes access to the Charm. Activates once per scene.


                                THE SHIKIGAMI (Threat 2-3)
                                Dreamlands spirits bound to physical vessels to mentor Princesses.
                                • Base Forms (The Vessel): Outsider (Root: Dreamlander), suffering the Awkward trait. Combined with either Automaton (toys/dolls) or Uplift (animals), maintaining Size 1-3 and the physical limitations/Scars of their shell.
                                • Greater Form: Shikigami (Familiar Spirit)
                                  • Supernatural Potency (Reflected Light): Magical limits (Max Charm rating, Transformed traits) are capped by the highest Inner Light among all bonded Princesses.
                                  • Adaptation (Symbiotic Familiar): Once per scene, when a bonded Princess gains a Beat from an Aspiration/Dream, the Shikigami may reset an ability. A Princess may also spend Wisps/use her Adaptation to reset the Shikigami's abilities via 1 minute of physical contact.
                                  • Integrity & Sensitivity: Uses Belief (starts at 8). Suffers Sensitivity with a base pool of 1 die.
                                  • Anchors: One Anchor is focused on the Nakama (an ideal to embody or pitfall to avoid); the second is a personal quirk tied to their vessel.
                                  • Lesser Form: The Bond (Magical Relay): Turns the bonded Nakama into a Dedicated Commonalty for the purposes of targeting Charms.
                                • Lesser Form: Shikigami Transformation (Variant): Activates once per scene to gain Transformed Attributes/Skills. Differences: No Phylactery (suffers Stunned Tilt on a dramatic failure instead of losing an item); can purchase Transformed Size dots (1 Merit dot per Size) to scale its tiny vessel up to Size 8.
                                • Lesser Form: Shikigami Charms (Variant): Activates each Charm once per scene. Differences: Max Charm rating is capped by Reflected Light. A bonded Princess can channel her own Skills and Charms through the familiar by spending Wisps/using her Adaptation to reset the Shikigami's ability for the roll.
                                • Lesser Form: Crawlspace Guide (Variant): Automatically finds physical thresholds into the Dreamlands. Adds a flat +3 bonus as a secondary actor on teamwork actions when aiding a Princess using her Crawlspace Lesser Form.

                                    Mystery Cult Initiation: Embassies and Protectorates
                                    Specialized diplomatic or authoritarian cults that alter the character's magic, modeled via the 1-to-5 dot Mystery Cult Initiation Merit.

                                        DETHRONED
                                        Dethroned are former Princesses who despaired of their calling, lost all their Touchstones, and went completely Feral. Having shed their mortal forms and lost their Belief, they have succumbed permanently to End Stage Instability. They are twisted and empowered by a blasphemous transformation that fills the void in their souls with pure Taint. They are horrific, corrupted reflections of the Hopeful who project their unending grief and bitterness onto the world around them, reshaping reality into twisted reflections of their own nightmares.

                                        Threat Level: 6

                                        Themes
                                        The Dethroned represent the themes of hopelessness, tragedy, and the destructive cycle of abuse. They are the Hopeful equivalent of a Feral Deviant in End Stage—a creature utterly devoid of the Loyalty and Conviction Touchstones required to anchor a shattered soul. Because they lack these bonds, they have no way to heal their Instability. Their Scars and Variations run rampant, mutating them beyond all recognition into unrecognizable caricatures of their former selves. Rather than being driven by the focused vengeance of a Renegade or the obedience of a Devoted, the Dethroned act entirely on terror, agony, and instinct. Their existence is a constant, surreal loop of their own failures.

                                        Allies
                                        Dethroned have no true friends, only thralls. Any Dark being that enters their presence has its will twisted to become a willing supporter of the Dethroned's despair, making even intelligent monsters extremely wary of them. The most degenerate cults of the Darkness may worship them as fallen gods, but the Dethroned view them only as props in their eternal tragedies.

                                        Enemies
                                        Radiant Princesses hunt the Dethroned in hopes of defeating their physical forms so they can bear the terrible burden of redeeming their souls. Normal Renegades and Devoted despise them for their ability to violently subjugate the minds of others and warp victims into mindless extensions of their own twisted psyches.

                                        Web of Pain
                                        Conspiracies treat Dethroned the same way they treat any powerful Feral: as apocalyptic errors that must be euthanized, scrubbed from records, and destroyed, or as disposable throw-away weapons of mass destruction to unleash upon their rivals. Occasionally, a conspiracy's cruel experiments on a captive Princess will inadvertently strip away her final Touchstone, triggering her horrific transformation into a Feral Dethroned and unleashing a localized apocalypse upon the conspirators' own facility.

                                        Greater Form: Dethroned
                                        The creature is a fallen Noble. She has a Supernatural Potency trait called Inner Darkness, Anchors in the form of horrific Obsessions, and a Fuel trait called Clouds (or Shadows). This Greater Form completely replaces the Princess Greater Form and grants access to two exclusive Lesser Forms: Chains and Hexes.
                                        • Supernatural Potency (Inner Darkness): When a Princess falls, her Inner Light inverts into Inner Darkness. This trait acts as her Supernatural Tolerance, dictates the maximum number of Clouds she can hold, and limits the amount of fuel she can spend in a single turn.
                                        • Anchors (Zero Touchstones & The Fall): A Dethroned is Feral; she possesses zero Touchstones. Instead, she is anchored entirely by the circumstances of her fall. She interprets all current events as a recapitulation of her personal tragedy, using her powers to forcefully assign roles to her victims so they can reenact her failures.
                                        • Fuel Trait: Clouds (Shadows): Where the Hopeful use Wisps, the Dethroned fuel their dark magic with Clouds (also known as Shadows)—fragments of misery and despair. A Dethroned regains Clouds in the following ways:
                                          • Endless Angst: Merely by existing in a state of eternal torment, she regains 1 Cloud each night at midnight.
                                          • Sadism: The Dethroned replaces a Princess's Sensitivity with Sadism. Whenever she witnesses or causes the suffering of others, it confirms her dark choices; she rolls her Inner Darkness and regains 1 Cloud for each success.
                                          • Misery Gluttony: If she possesses the Misery Glutton Hex, she regains Willpower when a victim loses their last Willpower point, and fully restores all Clouds and Willpower if a victim commits suicide in her presence.


                                        Lesser Form: Chains
                                        Chains represent the Dethroned's innate authority over the Dark World and its creatures.
                                        • Fortress of Regrets: The Dethroned projects a Lament (a personal Nightscape) into the Dark World that continuously broadcasts the agonizing allegories of her fall.
                                        • Companions in Misery: She forcefully overwrites the loyalties of Dark beings and mortals, inflicting the Dethroned Follower Condition to make them subservient actors in her tragedy.
                                        • Spawn Darkchild: She can spend Willpower to spontaneously generate bespoke Darkspawn from the substance of the Dark World to serve as foot soldiers.


                                        Lesser Form: Hexes
                                        Hexes are corrupted black magics formed of solid misery that replace the Charms she wielded in life.
                                        • Destructive Magic: Hexes allow the Dethroned to project devastating energy (Blast), infect victims with supernatural despair (Infectious Madness), or bind her own Shadows into mortals to corrupt them (Kiss of a Princess).
                                        • Monstrous Mutations: Hexes also warp her physical form into an uncontrollable End Stage monstrosity, granting her the ability to swell to gigantic proportions (Monstrous Size), dissolve into a swarm of horrors (Swarming Horror), or rapidly heal her injuries by oozing void from her wounds (Regeneration).

                                        For a normal Deviant, going Feral and reaching End Stage Instability is an inescapable, incurable condition. The Dethroned are the sole exception to this cosmic rule. After a Dethroned's physical form is defeated, a Radiant Princess can reach out to its soul through her Sensitivity. By voluntarily taking on the Dethroned's accumulated Shadows into her own soul, the Princess can ultimately redeem the fallen Noble, returning her to the Light and the cycle of reincarnation.
