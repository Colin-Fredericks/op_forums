    Loved the work, first of all it's excellent as basis. We can get a lot out of this simple 2 merits structure. Worst case scenario a game of Magical Girls start with a free dot in each, so it's an easy and low power setup to get running the baseline.

    Considerations:

    Transformation brings more than common equipment to the game: it smuggles it. You can get those items anywhere, past any inspection, it can't be tracked and you're given a "burn ID" of sorts that use it in the open then drop out of sight taking the offending material with it.

    My question is just if the benefits and equip dots are balanced for this. The answer may be yes.

    A more pressing matter: can a Transformation include other Merit-based magic objects? The various splats also introduce various Merits to represent objects produced or modified by the supernatural that may fit, actually pretty well, the genre.

    But adding the smuggling benefits to those items may, or may not, be too much. Tentative take: Transformation also unlocks access to any other Merit that gives access to equipment of any kind. Merits acquired like that become part of the Transformation load out rather than existing independently.

    Subsuming found items into a Transformation may be possible, but as default the items are developments of the Transformation from the start.

    What this adds: Magical Girls can access item merits across splats. Not only does it increase tools to customize their equipment (a genre staple), also facilitate a frequently overlooked means of integrating splats or crossing over key capabilities.

    Mortal MGs in particular get a solid avenue to invest XP in powerful effects without loosing characterization as mortals. They get more out of being Magical Girls instead, as more magic.

    Note: Identity Shield may be only Presence + Supernatural Tolerance, making the acquisition of Conviction even more relevant and MGs with low pools have reason to not try their luck. If it is meant to be too hard, it may be Presence + Subterfuge + Supernatural Tolerance always. Both work for me and confirm Superman as a Magical Girl.

    Shared Grace and Cosmic Essence could be mortal only. Alternatively, they could be cheaper to splats but cost splat-specific fuel to make happen. Also just occurred to me how close Transformation can become of a Lantern Ring or a Utility Belt, and I think those are good to lean on for possible new Merits.

    Hardest for me is to evaluate the Mascot for now, but I think it is a bit too powerful and anyway needs some key conceptual revisions.

    1. Mascots need physicality. Even when they're truly abnormal beings, they're also estranged from their reality/people to remain with their charges. I think Ephemeral rules are not the best to capture them.

    2. Power imbalance is also a staple: even the most powerful mascots are rarely able to help with the main action. Their expertise is support to the main magic itself and the basics on the villains, otherwise have mundane abilities and civilian skill levels at them.

    3. We must be careful to not make a shared Mascot too powerful, especially not to shadow the splat-specific equivalents like a Totem. The result should also preferably equally support groups with multiple or single Mascots.

    I'm really weary of Esoteric Conduit. It doesn't just makes for a weird description of what's going on, but can severely impact the regular themes of the original lines as each splat has it expressed above all in their farming methods.

    Friendship is magic, but should this magic be strong enough that a Vampire never feeds or a Demon never needs venture into Infrastructure to refuel?

    On Pure Spark I'd switch Unyielding Resolve and The Blindfold of Hope. Witnessing magic without going bananas is the earliest must have, while immunity to auras from the get go is a bit too much. A Magical Girl still in the stage where exposure scares simply doesn't has this Merit yet.

    Spark of Conviction maybe should be 5th dot? Also Empowered Aegis should be something it brings with the stat, already.

    Didn't analyze the Style Merits yet, just noticed they look a lot like effective splat powers since they combine variety of powers with use of a supernatural fuel. Maybe use Willpower instead and then make something to convert Spark into Willpower?

        My inspiration for Spark of Conviction was the Conviction “power stat” from World of Darkness: Mirrors (pp.32–33). I should probably hew closer to that, with Willpower as the fuel instead of introducing a new “Sparks” fuel. In summary:

        Conviction is rated 1–10. It gives you a Resistance rating of roughly half that (0 at Conviction 1 up to 6 at Conviction 10) and lets you spend multiple Willpower/turn (from 2/turn at Conviction 4 up to 4/turn at Conviction 10). And once per chapter/session, you can do one of the following: (Concentration) ignore environmental penalties up to your Conviction rating; (Drive) add your Conviction to one Attribute for one roll; (Endurance) ignore wound penalties for a number of turns equal to your Conviction; (Perception) spend a Willpower point to “see” monsters for what they are.

        (The Conviction section is immediately followed by an Expanded Willpower section that presents other ways that the Storyteller might allow Willpower to be spent; I'd review that section to see which if any of those should be made available via Conviction.)

        I'd also add an increased “recovery rate” for Willpower: since you're empowered to burn through your Willpower more rapidly, you ought also to be able to restore it more easily. Instead of being able to fulfill a Virtue once per session and a Vice once per scene, perhaps allow both of these to be fulfilled a number of times equal to how many Willpower you can spend per turn? So once you hit Conviction 4, you can indulge your Vice twice per scene or fulfill your Virtue twice per chapter; at Conviction 7, that becomes three times each; and at Conviction 10, it's four times each…

            I think that's a case where the genre actually offers the simplest solutions rather than trying to put the known staples of the setting in.

            Mascots in the genre are technical advisors. They understand the transformation, powers, and what the MG has to deal with as a MG. All else are possible additions, not the other way around, the baseline Mascot is a talking pet for real with the expected needs and capacities. At most it is an impossible but not exceptional entity that has to hide in exchange for a few perks like wings or functional hands. Otherwise stat as a cat or bunny.

            What a baseline Mascot Merit should then offer is the ability of this pet to help with the technical side. What the Mascot does that matters?
            1. They locate the Compact and the user. They have to bring one to the other by mundane means, though.
            2. They know how this thing works, and how the main enemies work (even if that just means knowing a crash course on each splat). Their advice is highly specialized, with the pros and cons of being so.
            3. They do maintenance. Magical Girls are not immune to the literal grit of reality, while magic does a lot to help they also have the Mascots doing menial but important tasks that are not tangential: a genre of feminine empowerment means one that recognizes forms of care that are frequently made invisible.
            4. This maintenance includes mundane tasks. It isn't uncommon for Mascots to help with daily chores, mail, self care routines and other tasks a very helpful roommate would. They do what they can for their charge to save the world even if it isn't glamorous.
            5. 3 and 4 are particularly important when the standard Magical Girl is 12-14yo. The Mascots have a big role in helping teens overprotected by the law and subject to curfew to get into situations they shouldn't. That's doubly relevant while they're not transformed and still have to walk around (but even if the are, they're still just small girls in costume and they know it).
            6. As above, they fiddle with the magic, locate possible power ups, and generally help with or enable at all fine tuning the transformation.
            7. If there are other Mascots around, they know how to contact each other, be it an alternative communication channel for PCs or a means to get news from other parts of the world.

            Progressive Bond Abilities are sure needed and must be linked to the Mascot, but otherwise I'd say the Mascot is very consistently a mundane help in the genre, they do what a small animal can do unless specifics. One big advantage a Mascot can carry in CofD that derives from the above is they may have working knowledge of local supernatural societies, so a Mascot has the ability to work as messenger and liaison with supernatural powers the MG can't meet in person.

            For those specifics, the Mascot should be a valid expression of other Merits. Allies, Mentor, even Resources, who knows, can be made into things your particular Mascot can do. Familiar isn't a bad choice, by the way.

            I'd add they are also fucking hard to kill, though not hard to harm. They frequently accept tasks that are too dangerous to their frail bodies with no hesitation, despite evidence that a task that seemed almost suicidal was indeed almost suicidal, and since we have little if any of them going KIA in the genre I think it's safe to say they at least expect to survive almost anything, if not in a state they're thrilled to experience.

                Maybe some tweaking of Conviction is in order to better represent the Magical Girl genre. What I'm thinking right now is to scrap the Pure Spark Merit entirely; instead, mortal Magical Girls gain the ability to develop a Willpower-enhancing power stat inspired by the aforementioned Conviction but tailored more to the MG genre. Where Conviction is written in terms of confronting and overcoming the supernatural (very masculine energy), this trait should be tailored more to, hmm…

                Thinking aloud here: in HtR, there are three Virtues: Mercy, Vision, and Zeal. I'm not suggesting that we use these; only that they provide me with a framework for looking at the problem. Zeal is full of masculine energy; it's where you get your Avengers, Defenders, and Judges. Mercy is where I'd put the feminine energy, with the Innocents, Martyrs, and Redeemers. And the Visionaries would be neutral. Likewise, Deviant has Conviction and Loyalty with its Conviction having a lot in common with HtR's Zeal Creeds, whereas Loyalty leans now toward the Mercy side of things. In my attempt at setting up an adaptation of Princess the Hopeful in Deviant terms, the Radiant Princesses ended up as Guardians (“Renegades” with more Loyalty than Conviction) while the Twilight Princesses ended up as Nemeses (“Devoted” with more Conviction than Loyalty).

Also emotional sincerity as source of strength. (There's also room to explore toxic femininity; but that's more a matter for the oni-like adult women you requested as the villains.

                                            Beta draft of an Idealism power stat for an otherwise-mortal Magical Girl:

                                            (I stuck with the multiple Virtue refreshes because it's not about spamming the same Virtue multiple times in a single scene; it's about calling upon the Virtue multiple times in the same chapter. As well, I believe that there's a Merit somewhere that lets you take multiple Virtues.)

                                            Idealism

                                            Cost: 5 Experiences per dot (Standard Power Stat cost)
                                            Prerequisites: Mortal Only (No supernatural templates).
                                            Nature: Idealism is an expression of pure human empathy, moral courage, and earnest belief.

                                            Passive Capabilities
                                            • Expanded Willpower Engine: The maximum number of Willpower points the character can spend on a single action or resistance roll scales with her Idealism rating row limit (1 WP/turn at 1–3, 2 WP/turn at 4–6, 3 WP/turn at 7–9, and 4 WP/turn at 10). Each Willpower spent grants its standard +3 dice bonus or +2 resistance bonus; but they can't stack.
                                            • Resistance & Clash Modifier: The character adds her full Idealism rating (+1 to +10) to all resistance dice pools and Clash of Wills checks against supernatural powers targeting her mind, emotional state, or Willpower.
                                            • Integrity Anchor: The character gains complete immunity to Integrity breaking points caused strictly by witnessing supernatural horrors, monsters, alien Infrastructure, or eldritch anomalies. Her everyday human identity remains grounded. (Standard breaking points still apply if she personally commits moral wrongs, betrays loved ones, or violates her own code).
                                            • Empathetic Resonance: Add half Idealism (rounded down) to all Empathy rolls and Social Maneuvering checks whenever her primary goal is to genuinely understand, comfort, or build trust with someone, rather than deceive or coerce them.

                                            Active Feats of Idealism

                                            By spending 1 Willpower, the character can execute one of the following feats once per chapter:

                                            1. Empathy & Insight
                                            • Concept: Looking past a target’s frightening exterior, angry façade, or monstrous form to perceive the vulnerable person beneath.
                                            • Mechanism: Spend 1 Willpower as an instant action. For the remainder of the scene, the character intuitively perceives the target’s core emotional state, their Virtue, Vice, or an Aspiration/Touchstone tied to what they are trying to protect or mourn.
                                            • System Effect: When using this insight to reach out, build trust, or de-escalate conflict, add half Idealism (rounded down) to all Empathy, Persuasion, or Clash of Wills rolls made to reach them.
                                            2. De-escalation & Common Ground
                                            • Concept: An earnest, unarmored gesture of peace that creates a temporary pause in supernatural rage, opening space for human dialogue.
                                            • Dice Pool: Presence + Empathy + half Idealism (rounded down) vs. Composure + Supernatural Tolerance
                                            • Cost: 1 Willpower
                                            • System Effect: On a success, the character temporarily soothes an enemy’s active frenzy, Death Rage, Disquiet, or predatory hostility for a number of turns equal to her Idealism rating.
                                            • Social Impact: Making this genuine attempt to find common ground opens 1 Door immediately on a target who would normally refuse to negotiate, or waives the standard wait time between Social Maneuvering Impression rolls for the scene.
                                            3. Protection & Reassurance
                                            • Concept: Stepping up to offer direct, personal support ("I'm right here with you; you don't have to face this alone").
                                            • Activation: Spend 1 Willpower when offering direct help or standing firm to protect others. Choose one scope upon activation:
                                              • Stage-Wide Anchor: All allies on stage add half Idealism (rounded down) to their Resolve + Composure rolls to resist fear, horror, or mind-altering powers for the rest of the scene.
                                              • Targeted Shield (One Person): One specific ally or civilian nearby adds Composure + half Idealism (rounded down) to their resistance rolls against fear, horror, or emotional manipulation, and may immediately make a new resistance roll to break free from an active mental Tilt (such as Shaken, Terrified, or Thrall).

                                            Tactical Exposure

                                            To break through emotional walls or shield others, an Idealist can choose to deliberately lay down her emotional defenses and undergo an Integrity Breaking Point:
                                            • Vicarious Exposure (Absorbing Trauma): When a nearby ally, civilian, or enemy faces an Integrity, Clarity, or Humanity Breaking Point, the Idealist can step in emotionally as a reflexive action. The original target suffers no loss. The Idealist takes the Breaking Point on her own sheet instead (rolling Resolve + Composure + Idealism modifier). Because this is an act of profound self-sacrifice, taking on the Breaking Point instantly resets the once per chapter active use of her Idealism.
                                            • Supercharged Olive Branch: When activating The Olive Branch, she can accept an Integrity Breaking Point. If she does, the target cannot roll Supernatural Tolerance to resist—their violent rage immediately stalls for turns equal to her Idealism rating.
                                            • Absorbing Trauma via Aegis: When using Aegis of Devotion on a single target, she can take an Integrity Breaking Point to instantly cure and remove a severe mental Condition or Tilt (like Broken, Despair, Shaken, or Thrall) from that target.

                                            The Virtue Engine: Feat Resets & Deferred Refills

                                            Fulfilling her Virtue serves a dual purpose:
                                            • Guaranteed Active Feat Reset: Whenever she fulfills her Virtue (or takes on a Vicarious Breaking Point / resolves an encounter peacefully), she instantly resets her once-per-chapter active feat use (Heart's Mirror, The Olive Branch, or Aegis of Devotion). This reset occurs regardless of whether she refills Willpower.
                                            • Flexible / Deferred Willpower Refills: Fulfilling her Virtue grants the opportunity to completely refill her spent Willpower pool (up to her session cap based on Idealism). However, she is not forced to take the refill immediately—she can bank that recovery opportunity and trigger the full Willpower refill later in the session when her pool is depleted.

                                            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                                    I ended up taking some inspiration from World of Darkness: Inferno, specifically the way that the Possessed in that game are given dot ratings for their Vices, which in turn serve as a sort of power stat. This flips that on its head, and removes the possession aspect, giving us a mechanism for turning a Virtue into a power stat.

                                                    Try this:
                                                    Spark of Virtue

                                                    Spark of Virtue (•••••)
                                                    Type: Supernatural / Mental Merit
                                                    Prerequisites: Mortal Only (No supernatural templates).
                                                    Cost: 5 Merit Points / 5 Experiences
                                                    Effect: The character’s unyielding alignment with her chosen Virtue unlocks Virtue as a primary Power Stat at rating 1. Additional dots in Virtue are purchased independently as a Power Stat at a cost of 5 Experiences per dot (up to a maximum rating of 10).

                                                    The Virtue Power Stat

                                                    Cost: 5 Experiences per dot (Standard Power Stat cost)
                                                    Prerequisites: Spark of Virtue Gateway Merit (•••••), Mortal Only (No supernatural templates).
                                                    Nature: Virtue represents the reality-anchoring force of a character's core moral principle. It functions identically whether the character is in civilian form or transformed.

                                                    Virtue Progression & Scaling Table
                                                    Virtue Rating Max Willpower / Turn Virtue Recoveries / Session Resistance & Clash Modifier Alignment & Anchor Bonus (Half Virtue, Up)
                                                    1 1 Willpower / turn 1 time / session +1 +1 die
                                                    2 1 Willpower / turn 1 time / session +2 +1 die
                                                    3 1 Willpower / turn 1 time / session +3 +2 dice
                                                    4 2 Willpower / turn 2 times / session +4 +2 dice
                                                    5 2 Willpower / turn 2 times / session +5 +3 dice
                                                    6 2 Willpower / turn 2 times / session +6 +3 dice
                                                    7 3 Willpower / turn 3 times / session +7 +4 dice
                                                    8 3 Willpower / turn 3 times / session +8 +4 dice
                                                    9 3 Willpower / turn 3 times / session +9 +5 dice
                                                    10 4 Willpower / turn 4 times / session +10 +5 dice

                                                    Passive Capabilities (Universal)
                                                    • Expanded Willpower Engine: The maximum number of Willpower points the character can spend in a single turn scales with her Virtue tier (1 WP/turn at 1–3, 2 WP/turn at 4–6, 3 WP/turn at 7–9, and 4 WP/turn at 10).
                                                      Expenditure Rule: Multiple Willpower points spent in the same turn cannot be stacked on the same roll or effect. However, a character may spend multiple Willpower points in a turn for different purposes—for example, spending 1 WP for +3 dice on an action roll, and 1 WP to gain a +2 resistance bonus or activate a Merit ability.
                                                    • Resistance & Clash Modifier: The character adds her full Virtue rating (+1 to +10) to all resistance dice pools and Clash of Wills checks against supernatural powers targeting her mind, emotional state, body, or Willpower.
                                                    • Integrity Anchor: When making an Integrity Breaking Point roll caused by witnessing supernatural horrors, eldritch phenomena, or events that represent a direct affront to her Virtue, the character adds a bonus equal to half her Virtue rating, rounded up (+1 at ratings 1–2, up to +5 at ratings 9–10). On a success, her emotional response manifests as righteous indignation or moral outrage rather than shock, terror, or guilt.

                                                    Innate Active Feature (Universal)

                                                    Virtuous Alignment
                                                    • Concept: When taking an action that directly aligns with and expresses her specific Virtue, the character can tap her moral core to enhance her effort.
                                                    • Mechanism: Once per scene, when making a roll for an action taken in strict accordance with her Virtue, the character may spend 1 Willpower to add half her Virtue rating (rounded up) as a bonus to that roll.
                                                    • Virtue Reset: Whenever the character fulfills her Virtue under conditions of personal risk or self-sacrifice, this once-per-scene active use resets immediately.

                                                    The Virtue Engine: Resets, Refill Limits, & The Refill Gamble
                                                    • Guaranteed Feat Reset: Fulfilling her Virtue under risk or self-sacrifice always resets her once-per-scene Virtuous Alignment active use.
                                                    • Refill Rate Limits: Fulfilling her Virtue offers the opportunity to completely refill her spent Willpower pool. This recovery is subject to two strict limits:
                                                      • Session Limit: A character can only recover Willpower through her Virtue a maximum number of times per session based on her Virtue rating (1 time at ratings 1–3, up to 4 times at rating 10).
                                                      • Scene Limit: A Virtue cannot refill Willpower more than once in a single scene, regardless of how many times the Virtue is fulfilled during that scene.
                                                    • The Refill Gamble: If the character fulfills her Virtue while her Willpower pool is only slightly depleted, she may choose not to refill her Willpower. Doing so does not consume one of her session's capped Virtue Refills. She retains that Refill slot and resets her active feat, gambling that she will fulfill her Virtue again in a later scene when her pool is low and she can gain maximum benefit from the recovery.


                                                    Vicarious Exposure (••)

                                                    Prerequisites: Spark of Virtue (•••••), An Other-Directed Virtue.

                                                    An other-directed Virtue is a moral anchor whose fundamental definition requires caring for, protecting, serving, comforting, or redeeming others (such as Compassion, Justice, Mercy, Protection, Selflessness, or Devotion), as opposed to a self-directed Virtue focused on individual discipline, personal mastery, or internal standards (such as Pride, Fortitude, or Purity). Because an other-directed character's moral identity is intrinsically tied to the well-being of the people around her, her conviction allows her to deliberately step into harm's way—putting her own emotional safety on the line to absorb trauma and shield others from psychological collapse.

                                                    By choosing to undergo a Tactical Exposure—deliberately accepting an Integrity Breaking Point on her own sheet—she unlocks the following capabilities:
                                                    • Vicarious Exposure (Absorbing Trauma): When a nearby ally, civilian, or vulnerable character within line of sight is forced to make an Integrity, Clarity, or moral Breaking Point roll, the character may declare a Tactical Exposure as a reflexive action. The original target suffers no loss and makes no roll; the character takes the Breaking Point on her own sheet instead, rolling Resolve + Composure + Integrity Anchor Bonus. Because taking on someone else's suffering is an act of profound self-sacrifice, absorbing a Breaking Point instantly triggers a Virtue Reset of her Virtuous Alignment active use.
                                                    • De-escalating Exposure: As an instant action when confronting an enemy locked in an active state of supernatural rage (such as Vampiric frenzy, Werewolf Death Rage, Promethean Disquiet, or predatory hostility), the character may completely drop her emotional defenses and offer an unarmored gesture of peace. By accepting a Tactical Exposure, she rolls Presence + Empathy + (half Virtue rating, rounded up) to reach them. Because of her complete, undefended vulnerability, the target cannot roll Supernatural Tolerance to resist or subtract from her roll. On a success, the target's active rage or hostility is suppressed for a number of turns equal to her Virtue rating, creating a critical opening for dialogue.
                                                    • Absorbing Mental Conditions: When an ally or civilian within line of sight is afflicted by a severe mental Condition or Tilt (such as Broken, Despair, Shaken, or Thrall), the character may accept a Tactical Exposure as an instant action. The target's mental Condition or Tilt is instantly cured and removed. If the character succeeds on her Breaking Point roll, her conviction processes the emotional shock cleanly; if she fails, she takes on a temporary mental Condition herself.
                                                    In theory, there's should also be a follow-up Merit for self-directed Virtues; but I'm not sure that exploring that side of things is consistent with the mission statement. That is, the themes of magical girls are such that I could easily see requiring all magical girls to take other-directed Virtues. It's all about the relationships, after all.

                                                            Revising the follow-up Merits:

                                                            Supplemental Virtue Merits

                                                            Vicarious Exposure (••)

                                                            Prerequisites: Spark of Virtue (•••••), An Other-Directed Virtue.
                                                            Active Use Limit: Activating a standard capability below consumes the character's once-per-session active Virtue use. Capabilities designated as a Tactical Exposure involve immediate self-sacrifice and do not consume the once-per-session active slot (and may be used even if the slot is already spent).
                                                            Universal Constraint (Communication Requirement): None of the capabilities granted by this Merit can affect another character unless there is an active channel of communication between the character and the target (direct speech/vocal reassurance, eye contact, physical touch, or an active telepathic/empathic link). The target must be aware of the character's presence and gesture.

                                                            Capabilities
                                                            • Empathetic Aegis (Proactive Protection): [Consumes Once-Per-Session Active Slot]
                                                              As a reflexive action when an ally within line of sight is targeted by a fear effect, horror, or mind-altering power, the character may spend 1 Willpower and call out to them, lock eyes, or reach for them. For the remainder of the scene, all allies with whom she maintains active communication add the character's Integrity Anchor bonus (+half Virtue rating, rounded up) to their resistance rolls and Breaking Point rolls against supernatural horror or fear.
                                                            • Vicarious Exposure (Absorbing Trauma): [Tactical Exposure — Does Not Consume Active Slot]
                                                              When a nearby ally or civilian is forced to make an Integrity, Clarity, or moral Breaking Point roll, the character may declare a Tactical Exposure as a reflexive action, calling out their name, grabbing their hand, or locking eyes ("Give it to me, look at me!"). The original target suffers no loss and makes no roll; the character takes the Breaking Point on her own sheet instead, rolling Resolve + Composure + Integrity Anchor Bonus.
                                                            • De-escalating Touch (Soothing Rage): [Consumes Once-Per-Session Active Slot]
                                                              As an instant action when confronting an enemy locked in active supernatural rage (such as Vampiric frenzy, Werewolf Death Rage, or Disquiet), the character steps into their line of sight, lowers her weapons, and directly speaks to or reaches for them. She spends 1 Willpower and rolls Presence + Empathy + (half Virtue rating, rounded up) vs. Composure + Supernatural Tolerance to soothe them for turns equal to her Virtue rating.
                                                              • Unarmored Supercharge [Tactical Exposure — Does Not Consume Active Slot]: If she voluntarily accepts a Tactical Exposure (taking an immediate Breaking Point) during this appeal, the target cannot roll Supernatural Tolerance to resist.
                                                            • Soothing Burden (Condition Removal): The character must make physical contact, lock eyes, or speak directly to the target ("Breathe, I'm right here").
                                                              • Minor Relief [Consumes Once-Per-Session Active Slot]: Spend 1 Willpower as an instant action to clear a temporary mental Tilt (Shaken, Terrified, Spooked) from the target or allow them to immediately re-roll a failed resistance against an active mental Condition.
                                                              • Trauma Transfer [Tactical Exposure — Does Not Consume Active Slot]: Accept a Tactical Exposure (taking an immediate Breaking Point) as an instant action to permanently cure and remove a severe mental Condition (Broken, Despair, Thrall) from the target, drawing the shock onto herself.
                                                            Crucible of Conviction (••)

                                                            Prerequisites: Spark of Virtue (•••••), A Self-Directed Virtue.
                                                            Active Use Limit: Activating a standard capability below consumes the character's once-per-session active Virtue use. Capabilities designated as a Tactical Exposure involve immediate self-sacrifice and do not consume the once-per-session active slot (and may be used even if the slot is already spent).

                                                            Capabilities
                                                            • Unshakeable Sovereignty (Resisting Control): [Consumes Once-Per-Session Active Slot]
                                                              As a reflexive action when targeted by a supernatural power that would force her to act against her core nature, rewrite her memories, exert direct mind control, or corrupt her soul or flesh, the character may spend 1 Willpower. Doing so grants her complete immunity to supernatural mind-control, memory modification, or horror-induced Breaking Points for the remainder of the scene (or grants an automatic success on a Clash of Wills against direct soul or bodily alteration).
                                                            • Crucible of Endurance (Overcoming Shock): [Consumes Once-Per-Session Active Slot]
                                                              As a reflexive action when afflicted by severe temporary physical or mental Tilts (such as Stunned, Knocked Down, Shaken, or Blind) or heavy wound penalties, the character may spend 1 Willpower. Doing so instantly clears all temporary physical and mental Tilts and allows her to completely ignore all wound penalties and fatigue for a number of turns equal to her Virtue rating.
                                                            • Purifying Exposure (Moral Triumph): [Tactical Exposure — Conditional Reset]
                                                              When the character chooses to suffer severe personal loss, physical injury, or social ruin specifically to refuse a moral compromise or uphold her personal code, any Integrity roll forced by that ordeal gains her Integrity Anchor bonus (+half Virtue rating, rounded up). This action is a Tactical Exposure and does not require an available active slot. If she succeeds on the Breaking Point roll, her emotional response converts the trial into righteous moral pride, granting her a +2 bonus on all Resolve and Composure rolls for the remainder of the scene and instantly resetting her once-per-session active Virtue slot if it was spent.

                                                            Eye of Revelation (••)

                                                            Prerequisites: Spark of Virtue (•••••), A Virtue centered on Truth, Discernment, Wisdom, or Honesty.
                                                            Active Use Limit: Activating a standard capability below consumes the character's once-per-session active Virtue use. Capabilities designated as a Tactical Exposure involve immediate self-sacrifice and do not consume the once-per-session active slot (and may be used even if the slot is already spent).
                                                            Nature: This Merit can be purchased alongside either Vicarious Exposure or Crucible of Conviction, provided the character's Virtue embodies an element of truth-seeking, wisdom, or unmasking deception.

                                                            Capabilities
                                                            • Piercing the Veil (Unmasking Illusions): [Consumes Once-Per-Session Active Slot]
                                                              As a reflexive action when encountering a supernatural illusion, glamour, mind-altering concealment, memory rewrite, or shapechanged entity (such as a Changeling's Mask, a Vampire's Obfuscate, or a Demon's Cover), the character may spend 1 Willpower. For the remainder of the scene, she automatically sees through all magical disguises and illusions within line of sight, perceiving the true nature of hidden entities and reality.
                                                            • Uncompromising Truth (Forcing Disclosure): [Consumes Once-Per-Session Active Slot]
                                                              When conducting an interrogation or Social Maneuvering action against a target who is actively lying, withholding vital secrets, or operating under supernatural compulsion, the character may spend 1 Willpower and speak a direct, unvarnished truth. The target cannot use supernatural abilities to conceal their intentions or lie to her for the remainder of the scene; any attempt to speak a deliberate falsehood forces the target to spend 1 Willpower or remain silent.
                                                            • Exposing the Horror (Unveiling Concealment): [Tactical Exposure — Conditional Reset]
                                                              When the character deliberately forces a dangerous, hidden truth into the open—such as unveiling a monster hiding in human society, exposing a conspiracy's Infrastructure, or revealing a terrible secret to save others—she may accept a Tactical Exposure (taking an immediate Integrity Breaking Point) as an instant action. Doing so strips supernatural concealment, stealth, and Cover from all entities in the scene for turns equal to her Virtue rating. If she succeeds on the Breaking Point roll, her once-per-session active Virtue slot instantly resets if it was spent.
