                    I had been thinking of making the “roll Scar Finesse instead of getting one per chapter” thing as a Merit, because I'm well aware of how powerful that can be. But I rather like the idea of maybe making it a Deviation for Body Modification and/or Matter Emulation instead, or even as a Standard Deviation: with this Deviation, you can use the Variation to emulate another variation on a successful Scar Finesse roll. (The Storyteller is free to apply this Deviation to any other Variation where he thinks it's suitable; but that's strictly at his discretion.)

                    But instead of limiting it to Magnitude 1 or 2, maybe the emulated Variation can be of any Magnitude; but you must entangle it with a secondary Controlled Scar of its own? Let's say, for instance, that Matter Emulation doesn't let me turn into a liquid and flow through a pipe or between iron bars; I'd emulate that using Out of Phase 3 (Body) or Out of Phase 4; but that's not possible if I'm restricted to Magnitude 1 or 2. So instead, I go ahead and emulate the Magnitude 3 or 4 Variation, but use a Controlled Scar of sufficient Magnitude to do so.

                    Hmm… As a +1 Deviation, the improvised Variation needs to be entangled with a Controlled Scar with more Magnitude than it has; as a +2 Deviation, it can be entangled with a Scar of equal Magnitude. And this is on top of whatever Scars are entangled with the Variation you're using to do the emulating. Or something like that.

                    Frankly, I'd much prefer an “any Magnitude is available; but it must be entangled with a sufficiently severe Controlled Scar” approach to “you get it at Magnitude 1 or 2” when dealing with improvised Variations: sometimes the effect that you're trying to produce simply isn't something that a Magnitude 1 Variation can represent. For instance, I don't know if it's possible to use Matter Emulation as written to liquify your body enough to squeeze through an iron-mesh filter; but if it isn't, I'd want to be able to use this Deviation in order to emulate Out of Phase (Body), which is Magnitude 3, or even Out of Phase with Magnitude 4. The former might be doable by applying a Standard Deviation to reduce its effective Magnitude to 2 (such as something that prevents you from passing through watertight barriers); but that becomes a major hassle and an unnecessary complication which potentially prevents me from doing something that makes perfect sense within the in-game setting; so I'd rather just call it a case of emulating a Magnitude 3 Variation and requiring the Broken character to use a Magnitude 4 Scar to activate it.

                    And this should apply to granting an ally a temporary use of a Variation, too: the person using the Technique decides on a suitable Variation to grant, which can be of any Magnitude; it just needs to be conceptually consistent with the Variation that he's using to do the emulation. But he has to use a Controlled Scar of greater Magnitude than the granted Variation in order to do it, though Mastery lets him do it with a Controlled Scar equal to the Magnitude of the granted Variation.

                    What do you think?

                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                    I've added it to my list to noodle with in errata. Speaking of errata, I've made some adjustments to Noblesse Oblige in response to comments here and elsewhere. Is it an improvement?

                                    Noblesse Oblige (• to •••••)
                                    Keywords: Mental, Social; Subtle
                                    The Broken suffers a supernatural compulsion to fulfill the desires of strangers.
                                    At Magnitude •, the Deviant’s player may accept a Beat whenever she performs a random act of kindness for a stranger that results in a complication for her or her cohort.
                                    At Magnitude ••, as Magnitude •, and up to once per chapter the Storyteller may choose a character the Remade is compelled to help, which will not be a Conviction Touchstone. The Deviant must ensure that the target fulfills a Vice, Virtue, Aspiration, or esoteric drive (such as furthering a Touchstone). The Remade knows which person is her target but doesn’t automatically receive knowledge of his desires; she needs to learn that information on her own. Unless the target accomplishes his goal before the end of the next chapter, the Deviant suffers one of the consequences below at the beginning of the following chapter, chosen at the time she develops this Scar:
                                    • Health: The Remade suffers a point of lethal damage that cannot be healed by supernatural effects.
                                    • Power: The Deviant cannot activate the entangled Variations until she fulfills the obligation. If it is Persistent and Perpetual, it ceases to function until then.
                                    • Stability: The Broken suffers a minor Instability.
                                    • Will: The Deviant loses a point of Willpower.
                                    If the Storyteller chooses a new target, it frees the Remade from any existing obligation (and potential consequences for failing to uphold it).
                                    At Magnitude •••, as Magnitude ••, but the Broken has fewer avenues by which to satisfy her compulsion. Choose two of the following when the Deviant develops this Scar, and helping the target fulfill these no longer satisfies the conditions of this Scar: Esoteric, Short-Term Aspiration, Long-Term Aspiration, Vice, or Virtue.
                                    At Magnitude ••••, as Magnitude •••, but only one type of desire satisfies the conditions of this Scar.
                                    Deviations
                                    Except for Compelled, Kind, and Cruel, all the below Deviations are only allowed if the base effect is Magnitude •• or higher.
                                    • Guided (–1 Magnitude): The Deviant automatically learns one of the target’s relevant desires that can be used to satisfy this Scar.
                                    • Aware (–2 Magnitude): Exclusive with Guided, the Remade automatically learns all the target’s relevant desires that can be used to satisfy this Scar.
                                    • Compelled (+0 Magnitude): Rather than the choice of target being wholly at the Storyteller’s whim, it must be someone matching certain criteria, one who takes a certain action, or a combination of the two. For example, the Broken might be compelled to bring joy to children, perform a service for the first person to pay her fee, or grant the last wishes of the dying. The Deviant’s player decides when choosing this effect whether targets can never include Conviction Touchstones (or other enemies), Loyalty Touchstones, cohort members, or other close allies (even if they meet the criteria) OR whether it can include any of these as long as they meet the criteria. The Deviant’s obligation to a target persists until a new viable target presents himself, with each satisfied desire of the current target replaced with a compulsion to satisfy another until a new target is acquired.
                                    • Kind (-1 Magnitude): As Compelled and exclusive with it and Cruel, except targets never include Conviction Touchstones or other enemies, but they can include Loyalty Touchstones, cohort members, or close allies — as long as they meet the criteria.
                                    • Cruel (+1 Magnitude): As Compelled and exclusive with it and Kind, except targets never include Loyalty Touchstones, cohort members, or close allies, but they can include Conviction Touchstones or other enemies — as long as they meet the criteria.
                                    • Costly (+1 Magnitude): The price of failure to satisfy this Scar is higher — aggravated damage for Health, medium Instability for Stability, and three Willpower for Will. If the Remade’s price is Power, it shuts down additional Variations equal to (6 – Scar Resistance)..
                                    • Multitudinous (+1 Magnitude): Choose a second price of failing to satisfy this Scar (Health, Power, Stability, or Will). The Deviant suffers both.
                                    • Crushing (+2 Magnitude): As Multitudinous and exclusive with it, but the transformed suffers three prices of failing to satisfy the Scar.
                                    • Unyielding (+1 Magnitude): Continued failure to satisfy this Scar causes the Remade to wither away. Any damage, Instability, or Willpower lost to this Scar cannot be healed/replenished until the Broken successfully satisfies the Scar. If the price is Power, fulfilling her obligation does not allow the Deviant to activate the entangled Variation until the beginning of the next chapter.

                                            I've made a minor edit to my working version to account for that corner case. As for Compelled, perhaps:

                                            • Compelled (+0 Magnitude): Rather than the choice of target being wholly at the Storyteller’s whim, it must be someone matching certain criteria, one who takes a certain action, or a combination of the two. For example, the Broken might be compelled to bring joy to children, perform a service for the first person to pay her fee, or grant the last wishes of the dying. The Deviant’s player decides when choosing this effect whether targets can never include Conviction Touchstones (or other enemies), Loyalty Touchstones, cohort members, or other close allies (even if they meet the criteria) OR whether it can include any of these as long as they meet the criteria. The Deviant’s obligation to a target persists until the Remade satisfies the target’s desire or a new viable target presents himself. No more than one such favor can be compelled of the Broken per chapter, nor does changing targets delay the consequences of failing to uphold the obligation.


                                            I want the option to have a person who knows your True Name (or whatever) forcing you do their bidding chapter after chapter, but I don't want that control to be as direct as Subliminal Conditioning and I don't want a player to be able to delay the obligation indefinitely by finding new targets who meet the criteria.
