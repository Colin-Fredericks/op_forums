    Deviant: The Renegades, when?!

    02-27-2018, 12:00 AM
    When I get the newsletter, the first thing I look at is Deviant: The Renegades status.... again .... since forever.

    I pretty much have all the books since White wolf and have started replacing them with digital copies so I'm pretty invested.

    I remember the excitement I felt when something new came out, Werewolf when I had Vampire, Geist, Beast the Primordial.

    When I look at the current production schedule I can't help but be disappointed by all the rehashing of all the same old stuff. Vampires in the dark ages? OK, but several books about it?

    I'm sure it's all good, but if you could shunt at least half your resources to previously announced (years ago) product it would be appreciated and I can't help feeling a good business move as well. I can't be the only one.

    Thank You for Your time.

                                                      I'm also waiting for Deviant, and boy did I wait for Beast, but to sum up what has been pointed out numerous times:
                                                      - these things take (a lot of!) time
                                                      - new lines take longer, because you cannot build on pre-existing themes/concepts/mechanics
                                                      - other products are just as eagerly awaited by other people (go and ask how many people are dying to get the Beast- Player's Guide, and then compare with Signs of Sorcery. Then ask Geist-fans how much they care about those when they're eagerly awaiting their 2nd Ed upgrade)
                                                      - that isn't a problem though, because what the Mage team does doesn't have a bearing on the Deviant team's work (and even the people working on one Vampire book might be independent from the development of other Vampire books)
                                                      - many people don't care about new gamelines, instead prefering more books on existing gamelines (just go to werewolf and read the claims of 'W:tF has been abandoned' because there hasn't been a 2ed Book in a while, then ask yourself what they would prefer: Deviant or new WtF books?)
                                                      - Yes, you have a right to express your opinion. So do others. None of this makes anyone 'right'.

                                                      One more thing, though: it may be due to my reading of your answers, but I get a vibe of 'I am entitled to that gameline, because it has been promised'. If this is not at all what you are feeling, I apologize full-heartedly in advance. However, since this is a personal pet peeve of mine: None of us have a 'right' to new books or gamelines. OPP is still a business, which means to keep producing, they have to make a winning somewhere, otherwise they'd have to stop producing completely, which would suck immensely. Even if they would decide to slow down the development of Deviant to save on resources in favour of other gamelines, it'd be their call to make. We could be unhappy with it, yeah, but there wouldn't be any sense in complaining about it, and it would be preferable to the alternative.
                                                      Bottom line: Try to be patient, because otherwise, you're just going to make waiting harder on yourself.
