                                10-03-2019, 11:03 AM
                                Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                Speaking of which, a few questions about the Scar-free dot, some of which are just confirmatory:
                                1. Can the Scar-free dot raise a Variation's Magnitude above its attached Scar's Magnitude? (Example of character creation suggests yes.)
                                2. Can the Scar-free dot create an exception to the rule for entangling multiple Variations with a single Scar such that a Scar of Magnitude N is supporting a Variation of Magnitude N (with the dot) and a Variation of Magnitude N-1, or an N-1 and two N-2s, and so on down the line? (e.g. can I have Tabula Rasa 4 attached to Boneless 4 and Camouflage 3 as long as Boneless used the bonus dot from Origin?)
                                3. Can the Scar-free dot be reassigned to an appropriate other Variation or used to further increase its initial Variation's Magnitude if its entangled Scar increases in Magnitude permanently, or can the free dot from Origin be "lost" in this manner?
                                1) Yes. Essentially, the Variation counts as Magnitude 3 for the purposes of entanglement but has the mechanical effects of Magnitude 4.

                                2) Yes.

                                3) I'm having trouble seeing a situation where this might happen. If you have, say, Superhuman Strength 5 entangled with Lying Eyes 4, and Instability causes you to raise Lying Eyes to 5, you now have the capacity to entangle a second Scar of up to Magnitude 4 with Lying Eyes. If you have Superhuman Strength 4 and Lying Eyes 3, and Instability causes you to raise Lying Eyes to 4, you can entangle another Scar with Lying Eyes or increase the Magnitude of Superhuman Strength to 5.
