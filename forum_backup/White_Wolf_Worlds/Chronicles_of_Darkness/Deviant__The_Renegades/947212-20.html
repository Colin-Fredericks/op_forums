                    It depends how you define scifi: there's always going to be that edge to it, because even when Variations don't look like physical implants, they're still experiments. Taking something supernatural and trying to apply it to a person, with terrible results.

                    Clades are divergence-agnostic. Is an animal/human hybrid, Dr Moreau style, the result of genetic tampering, surgical alteration, a skinwalking ritual gone wrong, a cult worshipping an animal dirty choosing a child as their holy leader, slivers of cursed poached ivory forced into a poacher's flesh as a punishment... It doesn't matter.

                    Even cyborgs. Is someone a cyborg because they had experimental implants, or because a machine-worshipping cult did a ritual on them causing metal parts to grow out of them?

                    There's a lot of breadth here.

                                                Ok I had an interesting idea based on what DaveB said about rituals. Ever seen Ju-on? Played Fatal Fram II: Crimson Butterfly?

                                                Especially with the latter, imagine a the main antagonist actually as a Devient rather than a ghost/spiritual entity. The Towns people call on the connection between twins and focus it all (through a weird effect in the locale: ley lines, spirit hunting grounds, opening the hedge, ambient/old Infrastructure, /shrug) into the twins. This, over the course of generations, infuses that latent psychic power (or something) into the last one remaining creating creating a deviant who wreaks havok on the town in vengeance.

                                                ... actually, looking at the Fatal Frame 'Antagonist' wiki, pretty much all of them were created due to some ritual or failed ritual to appease some realm/deity or as a way to collect energy from others. I know that they are all Ghost stories but it doesn't take much to swing it into the creation of a Deviant.
