        Originally posted by Eric Zawadzki View Post
        A few more questions, and then I should back off for a bit, but I promise to reveal more details soon.

        There aren't Clade-specific Scars.

        We haven't had a chance to playtest, yet, so I'm going to demure on specific Magnitude Variation effects, for now. I'd love to believe we're going to get the balance of everything perfect before playtesters see it, but I know better. Something always breaks, and it's almost never what you expect.

        Symbiote is a Form, which is a Z-splat similar to kiths. There will be a few in the core and likely more in later supplements. Forms are non-exclusive. You can actually have multiple Forms on the same character, so if you want to be your own Progenitor with a contagious, self-aware Divergence (requiring three different Forms), that's possible.

        Anyway, I'm glad to see people so excited about Deviant. I'm pretty excited about it myself, in case you haven't noticed! :-)
        One final question! A complex one if you don't mind. So. I'm planning to storytell my first deviant game pitting the players against against another Deviant. One of the Elect,a guy who became a Deviant by choice,and his power allows him absorb the Variations of Deviants he kills. The classic PowerStealer villain. I'd love to know,is VariationStealing at all possible?
