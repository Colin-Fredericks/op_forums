                                I'd lean toward either keeping it Discrete and allowing multiple purchases to enable different bonuses for the same Skill, or making it Tiered, changing the rote effect to a per-scene ability and letting the various Clade Adaptations balance that out, and maybe extend the ladder so that there's an 8-Again and/or 9-Again in the mix somewhere.

                                Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                                I guess my question is more about how, with Indelible Mark, you can get a level 1 scar and a level 2 scar and have them entangling a level 4 Variation or three level 2 Variations, and I don’t really quite get how that is equally as bad as a level 4 scar?

                                Hmmm....

                                Ok, so. I have three level 2 variations. Normally I would need three level 2 scars. I could get Involuntary Stimulus on one, so it can trigger if, say, I sense silver nearby. And I could have Bane 2 so I get hurt if I get touched by silver all the time. And another Persistent scar for the third one.

                                Given those circumstances, that means I have two powers that are effectively always on/available, and one where I have to work a bit to activate it, unless I’m in the presence of my trigger.

                                Alternately, I can use Indelible Mark. Which means I just have Involuntary Stimulus 2 and Bane 1(Continuous). So, while I’m down one Scar and Bane is now “silver causes a nasty rash” instead of hurting me, it also means that all three of my powers require a roll to trigger. Alternately, if I’m in the presence of silver, all three of my Variations go off

                                So...I guess, I can see it working. But it’s not obvious.
                                A couple of big things:

                                1) Involuntary Scars above Magnitude 1 mean that your Variations run on the highest level of Multiple Attribute Dependency, since they engage with at least Scar Finesse and Scar Resistance even if they don't calculate anything based on Scar Power. This further means that you have more to worry about from any degree of Instability over your Acclimation score, unless you have the Stabilizer Merit, in which case you're depending on keeping up with treatments or keeping hold of a particular item or what have you — in other words, you either deal with more from Instability or you have a separate weak point to manage on top of your Loyalty Touchstones.

                                2) The main problems with bundling multiple Variations into one Scar are that a) you have a more severe drawback that applies to fewer powers with no extra strength to compensate for them, and b) medium and major Instabilities have fewer channels to put additional dots of Scar Magnitude into, which means — surprise — you have to keep up with Instability more if you have fewer Scars and more Variations.

                                Despite this, Involuntary Variations and their attached Deviations are comparatively cheap for the cost! That's the trade you make for having powers not entirely within your control, which I feel is to be expected for a class of power-at-a-price with the word "Involuntary" in the name.

                                                    Originally posted by The MG View Post
                                                    Would the new Suppressible still allow you to convert toggled Variations into persistent ones? The ability to, say, have Tabula Rasa when you rouse yourself from your unending Astral Journey has been one of the most satisfying discoveries I've made whhile playing around with chargen. I'd be disappointed to see it go.
                                                    As I understand it, the point of the Relentless Variation Scar is to change a Toggled Variation into a Perpetual Variation: that is, there would be no point in taking the Relentless Variation Scar if the only entangled Variations are Perpetual. Likewise, there would be no point in applying the Suppressible Deviation if the only entangled Variations are Toggled. So you'd take Astral Travel with Relentless Variation (to change it from Toggled to Perpetual), then apply the Suppressible Deviation by way of Tabula Rasa to give you a way to temporarily shut it off.

                                                    I'm still not getting the reasoning why it was changed from a Deviation to a Scar in the first place; it seems to me that a “Relentless” Deviation for a Persistent Scar that changes all entangled Toggled Variations into Perpetual Variations would be simpler than a “Relentless Variation” Scar that requires you to supplement it with another Persistent Scar.

                                                    That would be my suggestion: two independent Deviations for Persistent Scars; one that changes the entangled Toggled Variations to Perpetual Variations (“Relentless”), and another that lets you suppress any entangled Perpetual Variations (“Suppressible”). I could see the first being a +1 Deviation, and the second a -1 Deviation. They would not be mutually exclusive.
