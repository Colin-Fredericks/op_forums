    1: A carnivore deviant who can only consume meat
    2: An herbivore deviant who can only consume plant matter

Again, this would be kind of a jerk move for the Storyteller to deliberately pull more than once or twice, especially since it's sapping resources the player invested in having.

            Originally posted by cybirddude View Post
            Questions about animal transformation:

            1: Can deviants transform into prehistoric animals like dinosaurs?
            There's nothing saying you can't if your ST allows it. You'd still be limited by the Size restrictions, however, which would narrow possible species dramatically.

            Originally posted by cybirddude View Post
            Questions about animal transformation:

            2: If the deviant transforms into a poisonous/venomous animal/swarm like scorpions, snakes, certain species of spiders, etc, can the deviant poison people?
            ​
            ​
            The rule for the Variation says you are able to attack in any way the animal can. If the animal is venomous, it basically has an attack that can inflict the Poisoned Tilt, possibly Severe depending which animal.


            Originally posted by cybirddude View Post
            Questions about animal transformation:

            3: Can a deviant reproduce with normal animals of a species its shapeshifted into? If yes, can the resultant offspring be manticores?
            ​​

            The book does not address this and would be up to your ST to decide what happens. I would highly advise checking everyone's boundaries before bringing this subject to the table, though.

            Originally posted by cybirddude View Post
            Questions about animal transformation:

            4: Can a deviants communicate with animals of the same species its transformed into?
            ​​

            Up to the ST, though wouldn't be unreasonable. Voice of the Wild Merit and Pheromones Variation ensure that there wouldn't be a communication barrier ever, however.

            Originally posted by cybirddude View Post
            Questions about animal transformation:

            5: Do deviants gain the same weaknesses/limitations of the animals it becomes while transformed? Some examples would be, insect deviants become harmed by bug spray, dog deviants being sickened by chocolate, or fish deviants not being able to breath outside of water.
            ​

            You gain the same physical stats including Size and Health of the animal you transform into. The book doesn't address whether you take on the animal's weaknesses despite specifying other things, so you probably don't (outside the things that you would by mechanics). Keep in mind that Humans can also be harmed by bug spray, they just aren't typically around enough quantities of it to seriously affect them, whereas ants are several magnitudes smaller in addition to being so different biologically. You could probably still treat bug spray as a poison, but might be able to get away with chocolate. The fish out of water will probably have a bad time for the few moments until the Deviant remembers to just switch out of animal form.

                                                    Deviations have a “cost”, in that they adjust the balance between the Variation and the entangled Scar: a +1 Deviation on a Variation, for example, requires you to entangle the Variation with a Scar one level higher than it would normally need to be entangled with. And yes, they're optional: they exist to refine what the Variation does, letting a single Variation cover a wider range of concepts than might be at first supposed.
