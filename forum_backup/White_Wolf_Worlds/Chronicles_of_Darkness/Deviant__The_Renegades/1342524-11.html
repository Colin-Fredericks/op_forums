    One thing I've considered a few times, but never implemented, is removing Variations from the Homunculus altogether, in favour of a more Lash-like system of unique properties. Or making cheaper, less potent alternatives for certain, less sexy Variation effects, such as Carapace's armor.

        Burrowing (Universal Variation, • - •••••)

        Subtle

        Perpetual, Tiered

        The Deviant can dig through solid ground.

        At Magnitude 1, the Deviant can bore through material with a Durability no greater than half Scar Power (rounded up), at half her maximum Speed. This effect provides no special protection against any hazards the Broken might encounter while tunneling through the earth, and unwise or excessive use of this Variation could risk destabilizing an area. In addition to tunneling, this Variation can be used to bore holes through sufficiently flimsy walls.

        At Magnitude 2, choose one:
        • Drill: The Remade can bore through material with a Durability up to Scar Power at half Speed, or material up to half Scar Power at full Speed.
        • Habitat: The Remade reduces damage and penalties from underground hazards by half Scar Power, and gains a general sense of what lies on the other side of a spot through which she is about to burrow (such as a cave, water, magma, a harder substance, etc), as well as whether tunneling through a given space risks collapse.
        At Magnitude 3, the Deviant gains both Magnitude 2 effects.

        Deviations

        Adamant (+1 Magnitude):         Add the Magnitude of Burrowing to the Remade’s Scar Power when determining the level of Durability through which she can dig.

        Without a Trace (+1 Magnitude): Any holes the Deviant makes with this Variation seal behind her one minute later (or one turn in action scenes).

        The Chimeric possesses the muscles, talons, and posture of a fossorial mustelid (Conspicuous Appearance; Persistent; Stamina).

        The Coactive’s ectoplasmic aura can shovel through dirt and stone with proper focus (Concentration; Controlled).

        The Invasive’s hands were replaced with powerful diamond-tipped drills that can bore through steel, at the cost of turning every attempt at manual dexterity into a tragicomic farce (Missing Limb; Persistent).

            Another one because I'm a grubby little level 1 goblin with 5 hit points who can only obsess about one thing at a time.

            Inland Empire (Cephalist Variation, • - •••••)

            Subtle

            Perpetual, Tiered

            The Deviant is capable of making uncanny leaps of logic.

            Unlike other information-gathering Variations such as Telepathy or Shadows of the Past, the answers Inland Empire provides are sometimes enigmatic, sometimes not the whole truth, sometimes of only oblique relevance to the investigation at hand. Just because you know you can take someone in a fight or that they’re struggling with a drug problem doesn’t mean you should act on that information—not right now, anyway. Save it for later.

            Depending on the specific form this Variation takes, answers to the Deviant’s questions might be couched in symbolism, narrative, or a biased viewpoint.

            At Magnitude 1, the Deviant may ask up to their Scar Power in questions from the Common Sense Merit each chapter. This allocation does not have to be used all at once, and the player may spread their questions out among multiple scenes.

            At Magnitude 2, once per scene, the Remade may ask a number of following questions about a subject equal to Scar Power:
            • What about the subject is not as it appears?
            • Is the subject a part of the Web of Pain?
            • Is taking X course of action with the subject a bad idea?
            • What is most interesting about the subject?
            • Why is the subject acting the way they are?
            The Storyteller answers questions posed truthfully but succinctly, sticking tightly to the scope of what is asked. A given question can be asked multiple times if there’s more information to be had. Valid subjects for interrogation include people, objects, Manticores, or anything else within short range of the Remade. Once per chapter, if relevant to an investigation, the Deviant may use this effect either to remove the Incomplete tag from a Clue, or to gain a new Clue with a single Element. The Storyteller should signal to the player when this is applicable. When making a mundane roll following through on an answer provided by this Variation, three successes counts as an exceptional success.

            Although not Directed, the Magnitude 2 and higher effects of this Variation count as such for any other effects (such as Unruly Variation or Channel), though nota bene that no Scar Finesse roll is involved in the employment of Inland Empire, and so certain effects may not be appropriate.

            At Magnitude 3, choose one:
            • Esprit de Corps: Add the following questions to the list the Deviant may ask with this Variation:
              • What status does the subject hold?
              • Who wants control over the subject?
              • What is a member of my cohort/conspiracy up to right now that relates to the subject?
              • Who do I know that has a relationship with the subject?
              • Can the subject serve as a trusted ally?
            • Half-Light: Add the following questions to the list the Deviant may ask with this Variation:
              • How could the subject do me harm?
              • How do I make the subject do what I want?
              • What is the subject afraid of?
              • What is the subject’s weak point?
              • What is the subject’s strength (mental, physical, or social) compared to mine?
            • Shivers: Add the following questions to the list the Deviant may ask with this Variation:
              • What is the subject’s connection to this place?
              • How has this place changed since the last time I was here?
              • Where is the subject going next?
              • Where has the subject just been?
              • What is special about this place?
            At Magnitude 4, choose a second option from the Magnitude 3 list.

            At Magnitude 5, the Deviant can ask any question from any of the Magnitude 3 lists.

            The voice in the Cephalist’s head provides a direct line from the world around him to the deepest reaches of his dreams, even if it has a habit of telling him things he doesn’t want to hear, such as why he can’t remember his own past (Amnesia; Persistent; Deviation: Unruly Variation).

            The Chimeric’s instincts are highly attuned to not just physical threats, but also the weird and uncanny, though such a sensitivity leads to poor impulse control and viewing every confrontation as an existential threat (Bestial Mind; Persistent).

            The grimoire inscribed on the Invasive’s skin knows many things, but accessing its wisdom risks madness (Persistent Drawback [Glitch]; Controlled; Wits).

            --

            An Unruly Empire

            When entangled with Unruly Variation, the Storyteller is encouraged to provide unsolicited information that, although technically true, might be misleading or cause the Deviant to take an unwise course of action. Whenever the Remade gets into trouble from following a Scar-induced suggestion from this Variation, the player takes a Beat.

                                            Anathema (Coactive Variation; •• to •••••)
                                            Subtle
                                            Directed, Tiered, Toggled
                                            The Deviant can curse a target in range, inflicting an unentangled Persistent Scar of a Magnitude equal to Magnitude of Anathema. Determine the Scar and all of its properties (such as the exact weakness of a Bane, and any Deviations) when the Remade develops Anathema.
                                            The following special rules apply:
                                            • If the victim is a Deviant, the curse doesn’t count against his number of unentangled Scars, doesn’t worsen with rising Instability, and can’t be used to entangle new Variations.
                                            • If the curse matches one of the victim’s extant, non-Repeatable Scars, he only suffers the Scar with higher Magnitude.
                                            • The inflicted Scar can’t be Addictive Variation or Maintenance, and can’t include any Standard Deviation that only modifies Variations (such as Target Restriction).
                                            • The victim can’t gain beats from the Scar’s Magnitude • effect.

                                            The inflicted Scar lingers for a scene, but the Remade can spend a point of Willpower to extend it to the beginning of the next chapter (potentially cursing the victim for several consecutive chapters).
                                            The Broken may purchase Anathema any number of times, each purchase allowing her to inflict a different Scar.

                                            Deviations
                                            • Cluster (+1-4 Magnitude): Anathema inflicts an additional Scar for each Magnitude assigned to this Deviation. Additional Scars are of the same Magnitude as the first one, and the Deviant inflicts all Scars with each use.
                                            • Flexibility (+1 Magnitude): The Deviant can alter the inflicted Scar’s properties each time she lays a curse.
                                            • Sympathy (+1 Magnitude): Once per chapter, the Deviant can curse a target from anywhere in the world. This requires a sympathetic link to the target (a DNA sample, a vital personal possession, etc.) and imposes a -4 penalty to the Scar Finesse roll.

                                            The Cephalist can suppress the emotions of others (Tribulation; Controlled; Wits).

                                            The Chimeric’s bite transforms people into monsters like himself (Conspicuous Appearance; Persistent; Deviation: Target Restriction; connected to a melee Lash with the Channel effect).

                                            The Coactive can tug on the razor strands of fate to guide misfortune to her enemies (Perilous Variation; Controlled; Dexterity).

                                            Design Notes
                                            A significant tightening of the previous Anathema, removing the Geas and Forbiddance options (which I’m considering spinning off into their own Variation). Sympathy became a bit more accessible, in return for being a Deviation now, and the new Cluster Deviation allows for more specific curses (such as turning someone into a frenzied wolfman with Conspicuous Appearance and Murderous Urge).

                                                A Deviation for Phobia:

                                                Diminishing (+1 - +2 Magnitude): The Remade's Variations are more difficult to use when confronted with his fears. If Phobia is at Magnitude 1 or 2, the Deviant must succeed on a Scar Resistance roll before he can activate or use an entangled Variation while in a scene with his Phobia. At Phobia Magnitude 3 or higher, the Deviant can't activate or use entangled Variations at all while his Phobia is present. For +2 Magnitude, this Deviation instead affects all of the Remade's Variations. This does not deactivate Perpetual Variations. Diminishing cannot be attached to a Phobia acting as a Secondary Scar.

                                                        I seem particularly drawn to Deviations. Maybe because they allow for such a wide range of niche characterization. Maybe because I keep coming up with Deviant concepts that don't quite line up with published material, but I can see how to make them fit. Maybe I'm just a natural-born quibbler. Anyway, I was trying to conceptualize a Scar to represent epileptic seizures, but then realized: Why reinvent the cow when there's so many in the field? So here's a Deviation for Glitch:

                                                        Triggered (+1 Magnitude): The Remade's Glitch is set off by external stimuli. Choose an uncommon but still probable condition, such as "seeing flashing lights," "taking lethal damage," or "exposed to the Extreme Cold Tilt." In addition to spending Willpower, exposure to this stimulus also activates the Scar. The base effect of the Scar must be Magnitude 2 or higher in order to take this Deviation.
