                Originally posted by Minty View Post
                Do Clades have anything to do with the size of your Stability track or how you mitigate Instability and/or Scars? And is there a limit to how many new Scars you can pick up to fend off Instability? Or rather, I think there would have to be - is this also based on Acclimation, or perhaps your Conviction/Loyalty?
                It was said before that Scars don't balance Variations on a 1-to-1 basis, though El Centinela's stat block in the Chilean chapter of the Contagion Chronicle made it look like besides the free Origin Magnitude there was a balance on the numbers for Variations and Scars. Do the tags on Variations such as "Toggled" and "Subtle" have any impact on what Scars you are allowed to balance them with, or do the Scars say what they can balance against?
                - No. Sort of. Yes.

                - Yes. There are rules concerning how many "un-entangled Scars" you can have, for the sake of gaming table sanity. I want to see them in playtests before I talk about them more than that, though. Acclimation and Loyalty/Conviction don't currently figure into those mechanics, however.

                - It is possible to create a character with a 1-to-1 balance of Variations and Scars, just not a requirement. More to come on this topic, eventually.

                - Keywords provide some clarity around when a Variation is active or inactive, and the activation method (Controlled, Involuntary, or Persistent) can make these keywords more or less relevant. For example, Toggled only applies to Variations entangled with Persistent Scars, meaning that they're something the Deviant "toggles on," as opposed to Perpetual, which means that if it's attached to a Persistent Scar, it is always active (since Toggled agelessness would be meaningless, while Overt Variations are a huge problem if they're always active). It is possible to fiddle with these keywords within the system, as it currently stands, such that you can have a Toggled version of Gigantic (hulking out only when you want to) or a Perpetual one (this is just how big you are, now, with all the attention that comes with being, say, Size 10). But if you've attached Gigantic to a Controlled Scar (making it a Controlled Variation), Toggled is irrelevant to you, since the very definition of a Controlled Variation is that you have to activate it before it benefits/hoses you.

                Onyx Path Freelancer and former Deviant line developer.
