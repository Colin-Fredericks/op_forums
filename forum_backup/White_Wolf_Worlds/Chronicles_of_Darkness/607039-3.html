    So I'm working on a supernatural power set that works with mechs direcrly. Pending balancing and revision is the below. Not sure about it, but it's the bare minimum of what I want to do. It's a bit inspired by Eva.


    Embodied in the Machine
    Walkers though they are often built emulating the human form are still no more than fancy vehicles and machines. They retain this property no matter who or what controls them. Atleast that's how things should be.
    Some denizens of the dark possess a strange talent that transcends mere controlling or piloting walkers through technopathy. Some learn of a unique talent to directly merge their consciousness with their piloted mech.
    Such pilots become able to pilot their mech without the need of even traditional controls, the machine obeys their will perfectly. A mage that is missing both arms and legs doesn't need them to pilot the mech. Certain supernatural powers, dependant on the type of supernatural being, can directly be used on the mech as if the Machine was a true extention of the supernatural's body, such as the werewolf's strength gifts or vampire vigor and celerity.

    Additionally, all attempts to subvert the machine away from the pilots desires or outright destroy it (such as by Unmake) either through supernatural powers or mundane hacking reduce the effectiveness of such attempts by the pilots power stat. If said means are already penalized by the pilots power trait, the power trait applies again.

    Unfortunately this synchronization comes with a drawback. Whatever damage the walker takes is also infinite to the pilot. Any structure damage inflicts lethal damage directly on the pilot as through the pilot himself was struck by the attack himself. Any tilts inflicted as a result of damage onto the Walker are also inflicted on the pilot. Including limb loss. The psychic connection creates violent damage feedback onto the pilots body.

    Mechs: Because even the Chronicles of Darkness needs robot fights.
    DarkFrame: Crossover setting that puts Chronicles of Darkness in the far future that is Warframe.
    Monarch: The Endless You are an alien ruler, charged with maintaining a people who you shape to suit your needs.

                            Sorry if this has already been covered, but I just thought I'd share a few ideas on "racial splats" in regards to Mecha...

                            Vampires: As a race focused on stealth and subterfuge over flash and violence, vampires don't have much use for mecha. That said, between their political ties and their ancient lineages, it's not uncommon for vampires to have "appropriated" technological mecha, or various forms of relic mecha - golems and the like. They also produce two kinds of mecha of their own; necrocrafts, and blightents.

                            Necrocrafts are the most monstrous of all mecha in the world of darkness. These horrific golems are crafted only by bloodlines who have found a way to extend the control of the Blood over dead flesh and bone, necromancer-vampires such as the Burakumin and the Sangiovanni. A necrocraft is a mecha-golem of bones and carrion sewn or magically molded together, and fueled by vitae. Whilst some necrocrafts are fashioned that they anchor themselves into the dead flesh of a vampire "pilot" and use them as simultaneously fuel and pilot, most are designed to respond only to the dark disciplines of their creators, ensuring their monopoly.

                            Blightents are the rarest of all mecha. They are an incredibly rare form of mandragora - ghouled plants nourished with vampire blood and shaped into strange, wooden golem-armors. A vampire enters such a construct and is impaled by synchro-thorns, his vitae coursing through the veins of this unliving plant and making them of one mind and body. Although certainly bizarre to look at, blightents are deceptively swift and agile, controlled by the very thoughts of their pilot and translating their every thought into action.


                            Mages: The fashioning of war-golems and battle-armor is one of the lost arts of the mages, and those few who can still do so in the modern nights are a treasured rarity. Each of the Pentacle Orders keeps its magical mecha carefully guarded, and they all have them, although they are stereotypically most numerous amongst the Diamond Arrow and the Free Council. Most Mage mecha are clearly mystical in nature, amazing constructs of Forces and Matter, always highly individualized and treated. Such mecha are hidden away from humans at all costs, for fear of Quiescence unraveling these wondrous relics and reducing them to nothing.


                            Werewolves: The children of Mother Luna and Father Wolf have the least need for mecha of almost any race, imbued as they are with the blessings of pack and power. Those few that they have are literally Relics - enchanted statues or armor augmented by bound Spirits.


                            Changelings: Ironically, the hidden folk are the most likely of all the creatures of the night to have mecha at their disposal, by virtue of various dreamspun and hedgewoven constructs. These vary widely, from clearly magical statues of animated elemental matter to bizarre magiteknological constructs.


                            Prometheans: It's rare indeed to see a Promethean controlling a mecha. They are more likely to seek transmutations that let them assimilate flesh and/or metal into themselves, transforming into hulking gargantuas that can match mecha instead.


                            Sin-Eaters: Mecha created by sin-eaters are typically heaved up from the Underworld, strange Twilight constructs of ghostly machinery liberated from the Dead Dominions. Other sin-eater mecha are "technological golems"; they look like robots, but their powers come from the ghosts that have been convinced or coerced to possess and manipulate the machinery.


                            Mummies: The Arisen occasionally produce mecha in the form of Relics, enchanted armor and golems left over from the days of Irem.


                            Demons: As a demon might be forced to admit, they are basically killer magical robots. What do they need mecha for?

                                                Let's add some special materials, shall we?

                                                Contracting and Mechs: Trying to use a Soulsteel mech without the benefits of the Brotherhood's contract is... ill-advised. In addition to the normal problems with using a tool uncontracted, you also suffer 1L whenever a point of Fuel is spent.

                                                Stygium: A mech made of Stygium grants the pilot the benefits of a Stygium Icon; however, the pilot must spend Fuel instead of Willpower, allowing the sensors to "sniff out" any nearby Ghosts in Twilight.
                                                Khimertut: A pilot contracted to a mech made of Khimertut improves the Handling by two, In addition, they may spend one Fuel as an Instant action to remove one Tilt the mech is suffering from, psychoreactive metal reforming to compensate for lost struts and broken wiring.
                                                Krovstal: A Krovstal mech readily drinks in the blood of its pilot - the pilot may choose to reflexively take 1L to have the mech regain one point of Fuel.
                                                Zexonyte: A mech incorporating Zexonyte parts gains +2/+0 Armor against magic, as defined by Zexonyte armor.
                                                Bleshkota: A mech with Bleshkota plating increases its Durability by one against unarmed attacks.
                                                P-Lichina: A mech incorporating P-Lichina serves as a P-Lichina Icon. Much like Stygium, however, the pilot spends Fuel instead of Willpower.
