        So I have been futzing around with these "keychain" thingies for fun, and I think I've cooked up a winner. I call him "Mr. Murder", and his deal is framing people. Here's a full keychain for him. I'd love to hear what you all think.


        Core Manifestation: Trace Evidence
        Keys: Stillness Boneyard
        Dice Pool: Power + Subterfuge
        Action: Instant
        Duration: Scene
        The Sin-Eater thinks of someone they have met, even if it was in passing. For the remainder of the scene, their Geist leaves trace evidence behind as if it were that person. Fingerprints are left on objects, as they glide across the floor they leave behind footprints of shoes the victim owns, and bitemarks will match the victim’s dental records. Physical evidence, such as hair follicles, clothing fibers or blood splatter, is outside of this power’s reach.

        Link #1: It’s Always Someone You Know
        Keys: Phantasmal Oracle
        Trigger: When the Geist attacks someone who knows the victim...
        ...the defending person sees who the Geist is impersonating attacking them. The victim picks up the gun to shoot them, the victim throws the lamp at their head, the victim chases them with the chainsaw. The details are all provided by the defender’s mind so the Geist appears as a perfect facsimile, down to even the smell of their breath, the sound of their voice, and feel of their hands.

        Link #2: I Did It
        Keys: Phantasmal Shroud
        Trigger: If the victim directly observes the Geist attacking someone else…
        ...they experience the scene as the Geist does, riding its senses. When they think back and remember the incident they clearly recall doing all the things the Geist did, even if it would have been impossible for them. While affected by this power, the victim stands catatonic and at the mercy of their surroundings. Mortals have no defense against this power, but Supernaturals can contest the activation roll with a Composure + Supernatural Tolerance roll.

        Link #3: Did I Do It?
        Keys: Phantasmal Curse
        Trigger: If the victim is currently asleep...
        ...they experience the Geist’s actions in the first person as in I Did It, but as a vivid dream they clearly remember upon waking. Should anything the Geist does trigger a Breaking Point for the character, they are unable to recover Willpower from that night’s rest due to the fitful sleep. In the following [Psyche] weeks should they encounter anything related to that Breaking Point -- such as the victim or police investigating the incident -- they experience further Breaking Points and are afflicted with the Nightmares Condition.

        Link #4: Apparent Suicide
        Keys: Cold Wind Rage
        Trigger: If the victim encounters objects marked by Trace Evidence...
        …the object attacks them and aims for a lethal spot, such as the heart or jugular. For the next [Successes on activation roll] weeks if they come within [Psyche] yards of the object, it flies at the victim with deadly intent. In mechanical terms, a Melee weapon gets one all-out attack against the victim as though it were wielded by the Geist, with no penalty for a called shot; a Firearms weapon goes off as though it were wielded by the Geist with a full Aim bonus, also without penalty for a called shot. As this is usually an unexpected attack, Defense is usually not applied and the results are often deadly. What’s worse, because their prints are already on the implement of their demise, local law enforcement often decaler them open and shut cases.

        Simulacre: An Alternative Morality Stat for Prometheans

                    Well if you're taking contributions. There was a pretty cool architecture Geist in the Thresholds (I admit it, the Threshold write ups only mostly useless).

                    Note that this power should be given to a character with something that causes an "insatiable hunger for Plasm"; or swap the Plasm gain with Willpower and give them an insatiable hunger for that instead.



                    Core Power: Haunted House
                    Keys: Phantasmal Boneyard
                    Dice Pool: Presence + Intimidation
                    Action: Instant (maybe extended?)
                    Duration: Scene

                    The Sin-Eater creates a ectoplasmic version of whatever building they're in, it looks like the same building after a decade of years or neglect - with a lot more creepy doors everywhere. Anyone within the building takes a penalty equal to the Sin-Eater's dots in Psyche to all Composure and navigation rolls as the Manifestation preys upon their mind. Resistance is also penalised when the Storyteller deems that it is being used in a Composure like manner.

                    Link 1: You've arrived.
                    Keys: Phantasmal Marionette
                    Trigger: Someone makes a dramatic failure or composure failures in a row while trying to navigate
                    ... They arrive at a door and feel a strong compulsion to enter. Resting requires a Composure + Supernatural Advantage roll, with the usual penalties from Haunted House. If they enter the room they are confronted with one of their fears. Every turn they roll Resolve + Supernatural Advantage vs the Sin-Eaters Presence + Intimidation. When they accumulate successes equal to the Sin-Eaters Psyche + Boneyard they escape; but every roll where the Sin-Eater scores more successes they loose a point of Willpower and the Sin-Eater gains a point of Plasm. If they run out of willpower before escaping they are thrown from the room, semi-comatose and highly vulnerable to both natural and supernatural manipulation.

                    Link 2: Who Enter Here.
                    Keys: Stigmatic Marionette
                    Trigger: A ghost attempts to use a supernatural ability to travel within the affected area; this includes teleporting to an anchor or even floating through a a wall.
                    ... Roll Resistance vs the Sin-Eaters Presence + Psyche. If the Ghost looses they find themselves in a room filled with mementoes of their life that are destroyed one by one. This duplicates the effects of You've Arrived; except that the Ghost looses Essence instead of Willpower. If they have no Essence they loose Corpus instead. The roll to resist being trapped is penalsied by the Sin-Eater's Psyche, the roll to escape is not.

                    Link 3: You'll never leave.
                    Keys: Industrial Boneyard
                    Trigger: Someone attempts to physically destroy or lockpick a door, window, or wall leading to outside.
                    ... The roll is penalised by the Sin-Eater's Presence.

                    Link 4: Master of the House.
                    Keys: Stigmatic Pit
                    Trigger: Someone attacks the Sin-Eater or targets them with hostile magic.
                    Roll the number of successes scored on whatever action was targeting the Sin-Eater. For each success a Ghost, selected randomly from Ghosts destroyed by Who Enter Here, appear fully Manifested and attack whoever triggered this Link. If the Ghosts slay their target they'll drift around aimlessly until someone else triggers Master of the House. For simplicity treat all Ghosts as a Rank 1 entity with Power 3, Finease 2, Resistance 3 and the Blast Numinea. It is not known what happens if a Sin-Eater would attempt to rescue one of these Ghosts.

                        Originally posted by Leetsepeak View Post
                        Yeah, I think that one sounds absolutely awesome.

                        Y'know, the only problem is the intimidating notion of being entirely original with these powers. The ones you guys have made are cool enough to crib themselves.
                        I can see that, but there is a trick: you don't have to be entirely original.

                        Mage Rotes can be re-purposed pretty painlessly, Disciplines and Contracts are right up that alley as well. Embeds and Exploits too, those are some of the best. And don't restrict yourself to WoD, you can steal from other RP engines, Exalted in particular has some really great power concepts in a similar structure. Comics, novels, television, raid the creative fruits of others unabashedly. The system itself is largely inspired by the power structures found in Anime and Manga (notably Hunter x Hunter and Jojo's Bizzare Advanture).

                        And don't feel caged by Keys either. They are there to provide a thematic element, not a thematic wall. Want something ragey but lack the Passion key? Pyre-Flame seems to fit just as well. Need it bloody but have no Stigmata? Change it to salt water and use that Tear-Stained Key. Just a cursory glance through the sample Manifestations will show how rigidly I stick to effects. (i.e. not at all)

                        Of course all this is well and good, but I suspect Mechanics are the big stumbling point. There I can empathize, getting numbers right can be daunting. So here are a few guidelines:
                        • Keep it short. If your having trouble describing the full effects of single Manifestation, it might be because you're trying to do too much with it. Before I write a new power I find it helpful to summarize it, ideally with as few words as possible. If you can't do it in a sentence, the power might need to be broken up into a series of Links. Same applies once you've written it, first drafts are almost always too wordy. Often I find it most helpful just to scrap the first draft entirely and rewrite it ground up now that I've had the opportunity to organize my thoughts.
                        • Keep it simple. Does the power have seven different calculations, damage, duration all tracked in real world numbers? Then trash it, that's terrible. The fewer things you need to track, the better. I try to follow the rule of two, two modifiers. So that could mean damage and distance, armor and duration, duration and iteration, you get the drift. The don't be afraid to use words like "near" or "within a few scenes". Those are often better descriptors in play than "feet" or "hours".
                        • Keep it awesome. Game balance is an illusion, don't obsess over it. I follow the rule of cool. If all abilities are awesome and desirable then it doesn't really matter if they are all mathematically equal. Circumstance and player ability are more likely to define a Manifestation's utility than the numbers, cost, and dice-pool. As a storyteller you can always tweak the odds, numbers behind the scene or revise the power later if it's a little off mark. And remember, the right links can turn an apparently underpowered Manifestation isn't a nightmarish beast of a superpower.
                        Above all, use your best judgement. When that fails, use someone else's judgement. The OP forum is pretty supportive, and I'm sure your players would gladly weigh in on what they think is and isn't going to work out in play.

                                    So; Sin-Eaters are on the whole a religious group, and Krewes are often the preisthoods for small (and rarely not so small) death cults. After all, religion helps people make sense of the world, and what is more mysterious and frighting than death?

                                    The general feeling I want to invoke is "exhausting, but worth it." This isn't just religion. It's a party.Some of this stuff duplicates abilities common to all Sin-Eaters, but that would still be useful; mostly Krewe Benefits. But it could be extremely useful for a Sin-Eater in the right situation, for example a catholic priest who for political or personal reasons cannot form his own myths and rituals, but has a Geist who can supercharge the Church's rites. The rest is unique, and thus worth buying.

                                    PenDragon, once again I call upon your ability to actually name powers


                                    Core Power: Call to Prayer
                                    Keys: Passion Boneyard
                                    Dice Pool: Presence + Expression
                                    Action: Extended
                                    Duration: Scene

                                    All mortals within the Boneyard who participate in a group ritual, shouting slogans, praying, waving religious imagrey, etc, gain the Inspired Condition. The Sin-Eater may need to make a mundane Presence + Expression roll to get things started, but the Boneyard's effects quickly make this self-sustaining.



                                    Link 1: Day of the Dead
                                    Keys: Stygian Pit
                                    Trigger: When the crowd (at least 50 people or 10 Sin-Eaters) is inside a Cenote and made aware of it's significance.
                                    ... The Cenote's Fluidity and Residue jumps up to five dots and remain that way for a month.

                                    Link 2: Guidance of the Ancestors
                                    Keys: Stigmata Pit
                                    Trigger: The crowd expects a specific named ghost to appear.
                                    ... If enough of the crowd expect the ghost to appear, it is summoned from the Underworld, fully Manifested and reinforced with the benefits of Plasmic Infusion. It takes 10 people to call a Ghost from the Upper Reaches, and each River doubles the required numbers. No amount of devotees will call up a Ghost that has Passed On or been destroyed. At the end of the scene the Ghost is sent back to wherever it came from, unless something happened during the scene to prevent that, such as the Bind Anchor Ceremony.

                                    Link 3: Heal my Scabs Lord
                                    Keys: Primeval Caul
                                    Trigger: Someone's prayer for healing attracts sufficient attention.
                                    ... Everyone who's attention has been caught spends 1 Willpower, and the ailments heal. As a rule of thumb; a point of lethal damage or any inconveniencing temporary sickness takes 10 people. A chronic dehabiliting ailment takes 20 people. A crippling disease or a point of Aggravated damage takes 30 people. A potentially fatal disease takes 40, and a guaranteed fatal disease takes 50.

                                    Link 4: Virgin Protect Me
                                    Keys: Stigmatic Marionette
                                    Trigger: Someone confesses to thirty people, the majority must not turn away.
                                    ... A random Ghost is called from the nearby Upper Reaches of the Underworld and anchored to that person for a month. So long as they loyally guard their charge they are rewarded with their continuing presence outside the Underworld. For simplicity treat this as a rank two Ghost with Power 3, Finesse 4, Resistance 3 and three of the following Numina: Awe, Dement, Hallucination, Left-Handed Spanner, Sign, Telekinesis.

                                    Fun fact. This was based on Santa Muerte's patronage of prostitutes and alternative sexualities. Confess you're gay (probably for the nth time if you're in this congregation) and gain a spectral guardian.
