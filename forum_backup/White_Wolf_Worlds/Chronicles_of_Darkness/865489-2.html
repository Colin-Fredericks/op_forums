        Foreclosed, but not Forgotten
        This particular case arose as a series on anonymous calls leading to newly constructed night club, each detailing a mysterious transaction or supernatural murder. After a random health inspection threatened to close the place down, their anonymous source contacted the agency again with a new job - find a respectable new owner of the club, who could keep business flowing without involving themselves in petty turf wars and disputes. Why? Because the club itself needed it - it was a Lares, and needs tenants to fulfill it's purpose. The IE Detective Agency had proven themselves knowledgeable of the supernatural movers and shakers, criminals, and dealers in the area, and more importantly, who wasn't on that list. Playing matchmaker was hardly a standard job, but having an entire building on your side was a hard benefit to argue against. Especially one that could report on supernatural comings and goings (as long as the owner and the club remained neutral).

            and now for a profile on the culprit bonus points if anyone can spot the reference to a very not CoD-ish franchise.

            Daniel Hubert aka GG Ziegesar - (Beast, Ugalla, Predator)

            Daniel was born into a middle class Manhattan family, barely affording to stay in the decent parts of the city but never truly wanting for anything. He managed to get into a prestigious upper East side private school on a scholarship. He was a good student and surprisingly popular among the student body. He made some good friends among the city's young elites. Of course he didn't do this on luck alone as Daniel was a Beast, he preyed ruthlessly upon his classmates, exposing their every secret to the world through a pseudonymous blog. He tore down every socialite that dared look down upon him until this ambitious young upstart looked preferable as an ally to many an old money legacy. By the time he graduated he no longer had to do his own digging as the socialites were all too eager to throw each other to the horrors of GG Ziegesar.

            In time Daniel grew bored of tearing down mortals. He sought a new challenge and turned his attention to his own family. The other creatures that lurk in the dark and feed in secret. He stumbled his way into their inner circles and began taking their secrets for his own. He knew a blog wouldn't work this time, too many eyes are on the internet these days so he tried an older approach. Hiding his stolen secrets in a work of fiction and taunting his victims with the absurdity of their own lives. Until IE's detectives track him down at least.

            Daniel's lair is an ever shifting labyrinth of lockers and class rooms hanging over a nearly bottomless cliff. His horror, which uses his pen name, appears as a blonde teenage girl but upon closer inspection her hair is a plume of golden feathers and her fingers end in bloodstained talons. She represents the fear of the gossip, a relentless probing of one's social life for any misdeeds that will be exposed for all the world to see when found.

                            Vera
                            Princess, Troubadour, Tempesta
                            Gang leader

                            Who are we? We're what this city needs. We're the janitors, the garbagemen, the sanitation engineers for the sickness in the system. We're going to scrub this city clean, scrape every last bit of filth out of here, and when we're done? This city is gonna gleam!

                            Any city has gangs, groups of toughs that band together to keep their own turf safe. Sometimes, two smaller ones join together, find what they can in common, take over more territory. Five years ago, a number of smaller gangs on the south side of town joined together, and started calling themselves the Flints. Their leader, a woman known only as Vera, has organized them into a force of violent vigilantes.

                            Vera is a Princess of Storms, but little is known about her past. Presumably, she's from the city, as it's the place she's fighting to save. Her gangs tend towards arson as a means of getting rid of problems, and there have been a couple pimps and drug dealers who have turned up as charred corpses. She runs a protection racket for her area of the city, although there's rumors that if you fail to pay up, nothing happens.

                            Vera is semi-antagonistic to the Agency: she is a dangerous criminal, and is responsible for a lot of damage and violence. There's been a few cases where her gang was responsible, and a few more where it just looked like they were. However, she's worked with the Agency in the past, usually to clear up a frame. Most recently, she used them to find out where a kid named Mance was hiding. The Agency thought Vera wanted to help him, thought he might be someone from her past, a brother or cousin. A few days later, Mance's body turned up in the morgue, a bullet in his head. Vera killed him, on the grounds that he was a Princess of Tears, but kept that fact secret until GG Ziegesar's book revealed it. She's one of the groups that wants that author found and ended.

                                            Oh, shoot! Someone beat me to a member of rogues gallery! Well, gotta compensate for that! Please welcome the notorious vampire murderer, Albrecht von Spinneburg

                                            Albrecht von Spinneburg (Rogue Ghoul with Ashwood Abbey associations, former Hunter)

                                            It all started back in the day, when Albrecht was but a humble member of the hunting lodge that made itself part of Ashwood Abbey, back in the Golden Age as he referrs to (Noone's sure when exactly, but most agree that it was somewhere before WWII). The bunch that was meeting there was all the most spoiled, sadistic hedonists that the compact could offer, and Albrecht was one of them. He took his pleasure in heinous mind games with his prey, a quality that prevailed in him to today. He did not mind more traditional ways of dealing with the prey though, was it rape, gruesome murder, most sadistic of torture imaginable or the all time classic "foxhunt", with the victim of the week taking the role of the fox. Somewhere along the way though, he grew old. The age spared him most of it's bite, leaving him still somewhat handsome, but that didsn't mean the aging has stopped, and the slow march towards death. And death, as one knew, would prevent him from having fun. This simply couldn't be accepted. So Albrecht, on the next club meeting talked eye to eye with the vampire they were holding that day. In exchange for freedom and a month long headstart to finish his matters before getting the hell outta Dodge, the vampire shared him the information that all vampires knew so well: That one could achieve immortality by drinking the blood of one of the Kindred in a specific way. Albrecht was offered to achieve such immprtality and accepted. The vampire thought it would make the foolish hunter into his eternal servant. Albrecht, however, already knew of all the vampire told him, as well as of the adverse effects of such practice. Not even an hour after the end of the process of making Albrecht into a ghoul, the vampire was killed by the Abbey. From then on, Albrecht started to take the means to uphold his immortality.
                                            He continues this practice to this day. He kidnaps vampires from the streets, nightclubs, meeting places, whether personally or by his henchman, keeping them in a specialy prepared basement and draining them from just enough blood before killing them in a gruesome manner. However he stepped out of the compact a long ago, focusing only on upkeeping himself alive and maybe manipulating politics between the supernaturals. No harsh feelings though, and the current local cell of Ashwood Abbey and him keep mutual relationship on a friendly level, helping each other out from time to time.
                                            Not once and not twice he found himself under attack by enraged vampires who wanted to stop his practices and tried to besiege him at his manor. Each and every time however, they broke their thief and many of them were captured and kept as further blood sources after getting lost in the fortress secured better than Fort Knox itself and designed to be more fear-inspiring than the lair of not one Beast...
                                            Legaly, nothing could ever be proven to him. He's way too wealthy and influential.

                                            His feelings on others:
                                            Illuminated Eye: Nasty little flies, but as long as they do not bother me I'll restrain from turning their miserable lives upside down and downside up.
                                            GG Ziegesar: I admire his work, truly, even if I cannot even aspire to my literary equivalent. With the way he presented me as, I'd give Hannibal Lecter himself a run for his money. *chuckle*
                                            Vera: How does the youth say it these days? "Come at me, bro?"

                                                Tik-Tik, Tik-Tik
                                                A recent string of abductions has kept some of the IE Agency's members occupied. The only thing they have in common was that people who were near the sites of the disappearances kept hearing the flapping of wings and the chirping of some strange bird. Fortunately, they get a lead when a certain Gangrel manages to foil the latest kidnapping, and he recognizes the sounds as belonging to the familiar of a particular type of vampire.

                                                The Past Catches Up
                                                It's a new morning at the IE Agency when Aldo (literally) comes stumbling into the office. He's clearly fresh from a fight, and in a lot of trouble if his need for a place to lay low is anything to go by. Matters escalate when a Lord from the Invictus based in the Philippines visits later that evening with his coterie of bodyguards. He's looking for the Freedman, who has already killed a couple of his men. And he knows that he has acquaintances with members of the group. The elder offers an ultimatum, hand Aldo over by the end of the week, or he'll use his resources to force them to.
