    Cross-line play with Minor Templates

    06-25-2016, 09:17 AM
    Hey all,

    I've had a game idea rattling around for the last week or so, and it coalesced this morning into a pitch I gave my players:

    Chronicles of Darkness police procedural. You're a cross-section of the local police force, who end up encountering the supernatural and getting thrown together. You also all have a secret - Maybe you're a dirty cop, maybe you're addicted to something etc. Your job will be to deal with the supernatural, while keeping your secret safe.

    What I've not mentioned is that the secret is that they're all different flavours of supernatural. Maybe they've got Biokinesis, maybe they're a Stigmatic, or maybe they're a Wolf-Blooded. I'm aiming to introduce some group conflict (In a pleasant way - Everyone works really well together, so this should provide some tension without actually causing problems).

    As best as I can tell, most of the minor templates really are quite minor. Wolf-Blooded buys you a 3-merit Tell (And I'll strongly encourage the ones that play well outside of the Pack), Sleepwalker seems easy, Stigmatic seems straight-forward (It's basically the generic supernatural merits, plus a bit of God-Machine flavour)

    The ones that concern me are Ghouls - They seem relatively powerful. They're a full-on Supernatural force, rather than just Human+.

    I'm also not sure whether Hunters can be counted as a minor template or not, if they're not too well connected to the bigger groups. I'll need to go through their book and check them out, especially in the context of 2nd ed and Mortal Remains.

    I'm not familiar with the other minor templates. I know there's a preview for Fae Touched, but I'm not sure how much that's going to match the final version. Are the children of Demons a minor template, or are they just Demons with less connection to the God-Machine?

    Thoughts? Anyone managed something like this?

                                          Fae Touched are more powerful then ghouls for couple of reasons.

                                          1 they can harvest their own glamour via emotions, even without Changeling support, Ghoul need to be given vitae from a vampire.

                                          2 Touch Fae have access to all kinds of contracts, admittedly they need a Changeling to train them,aside from their 2 free dots. Ghouls I believe can't take Coils of the Dragon, use any disciplines that require bleeding out vitae.

                                          3 With a merit they can gain a mask/mien which they can drop to bewitch people and get autosuccesses on contracts.

                                          4. They can also get the Seeming effects of their Seeming on contracts.

                                          5. They can do Sealings and join Changeling Courts for extra bonuses.

                                          I can build an Elemental Fae Touched character with 12xp that has both Armour of the Phantom Glory contract with elemental effect, and Stealing the Solid Reflection contract with elemental effect.

                                          So the Fae Touched character can drop their mask, turn into fire, making her invulnerible to physical attacks, and allowing her to deal lethal fire damage, then pull a copy of herself out of a nearby reflection, and attack any equal xp ghoul and kill it with ease. Actually that would likely be enough to kill many vampires.

                                          In the unlikely event that things go heywire, that Fae Touched can just go into twilight and be invisible, or pull the reflection of a car out and drive away.

Plus, your build doesn't work in the spoiled rules as written: Stealing the Solid Reflection caps traits at the user's Wyrd, which is 0, meaning your copy appears...then falls over dead. Even if it did work, it would take five rounds to get all that off (1 Glamour/round, because Wyrd 0, and fae-touched can't use catches). Maybe she can survive 2-3 rounds before Phantom Glory kicks in, maybe not, but it's not exactly the bulldoze that you imply. (Don't drive in reflected cars, kids: the driver's door will be on the right side, and your gas and break will be switched!)


                                              To answer OP's question, ghouls do get more neat power than most microtemplates. However, they also don't tend to have a lot of Vitae to play with, meaning that they have a lot of options, but not a lot of fuel. If you think the ghouls are too cool, limit their Vitae intake.
