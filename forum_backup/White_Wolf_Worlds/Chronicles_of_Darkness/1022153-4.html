            Tough to say what scares me the most. I mean, as a rule, not a fan of a lot of things in the Chronicles world, lot of things I wouldn't want to come across. Hoard of spiders swarming allover me, devouring me into a convienent flesh suit? Yep. Being forced against my will to do horrible things to other people? Sure. The shattering of my deeply rooted connection to the illusions that allow me to sleep in peace, thus rendering me subject to the overload of primal, naked fear that would surely break me down into nothing more than a mere rabbit? You betcha.

            But honestly, the thing that scares me most are things that I'm also realistically going to come across, which is funny, because my initial reaction to it is actually anger rather than fear: short sighted, self-unaware assholes who think they have everything figured out and deserve power because of course they do, who have no fear of the world or of self, blinded by their own fucking ego and the delusions they've been feeding themselves to rise over their own pettiness, frailty, and fear. And while my response is anger, it stems from the fear that assholes like that are going to hurt people and never give a shit because they are wrapped up in their own self-affirming prattle. It scares me because I know way too many people like this, and there's no real changing their point of view until they confront their weakness and fear-and unfortunately, in our world, so many of them will never actually connect to the things that will scare the fucking shit out of them.

            Fear and doubt are healthy, fuckers. Embrace it before the world cracks your ego like an egg.

                                        Originally posted by Omegaphallic View Post
                                        The purviews capacity to leverage IS internet forumites, Omegaphallic is an internet forumite, so he is directly in the preview of internet forumites.
                                        He is in the purview of Internet Forumites to influence, and if there was a Contract with Internet Forumites (what effect does that even have on your mien?) and he was a valid target for its use then that would matter, but you don't get to declare people your slaves with Goblin Vow any more than you get to do so by just spending Experiences to buy the Retainer Merit. A deal to get a particular person's help or loyalty via a Goblin Vow works by enhancing your ability to convince that person to help you and be loyal to you — it's not an exchange of favor for Retainer.

                                        And nothing in the merit says subgroupings are not purviews of the Wyrd. You could have Elementary Teachers, Paid Assassins, Door to Door Salesmen, nothing in the merit prevents it.
                                        The Merit operates as a more diffused basis of supernatural oathbinding than those aspects of the Wyrd that allow a changeling to internalize a Contract, the given examples of valid purviews map with moderately high fidelity to those of its 1e basis, and its 1e basis saw fit to divide its explicit list of examples into categories that quite conspicuously lacks peoples and professions among its subheadings. The Wyrd is not a human force and you have regular Social Merits to accomplish feats of social engineering through status and networking.

                                        It's fae magic — the stuff lets you accomplish feats by talking to the abstract, the inanimate, and the inhuman, and the things those aspects of the Wyrd can do on your behalf are bound by what they are. Nothing about the storybook-logic capabilities of the abstract phenomenon of Internet Forumites (or Internet Forums, for that matter) lets you instantaneously subordinate a person to your will and turn them into a steadfast ally and pillar of moral support, and expecting it to work that way just on the basis of doesn't-say is only a few steps removed from the shenanigans of taking Meat as an element for Control Elements. These powers do not exist in a vacuum.
