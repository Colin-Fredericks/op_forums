            Originally posted by Paradim View Post
            Are mortals considered members of a krewe, too?
            They and ghosts make up the majority of the membership by virtue of how geists and the Bound work. Sin-Eaters are distinguished by having innate access to a number of mid- to high-dot Ceremonies, including Krewe Binding; since the Esotery of a krewe caps its membership's dots in MCI, this means that for most of the early life of a krewe, the Bound are going to be the ones to call upon for inducting new members, bestowing the mystical mantles of its philosophy, channeling the dead, or laying spirits to rest, barring individual members who've learned those Ceremonies beyond the provisions of their Initiation, but every initiated member of a Necropolitan krewe, for example, knows how to connect a call to the nearest phone to a person whose name they know no matter where they are.
