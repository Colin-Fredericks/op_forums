                The thing is, those are only the 1 dot equivilents for 2e (as far as we know), but what about the higher dots? Because at 3 dots or more, Gravity is God.

                An example is the Adept spell: Forces 4 - Transform Energy: Convert one form of energy into another form.
                The description suggests sound to light or fire to electricity, but consider what happens when you convert electrical energy to gravitational energy.

                You see, there's this curious thing known as the Coulomb Force which describes the force between charged particles. Unlike charges attract and like charges repel.
                Now, if you were to convert that Coulomb Force into Gravitational Force, positive ions would no longer repel each other. They can now only attract each other through Gravity.
                On its own though, Gravity is the weakest of the 4 fundamental forces (for example a small fridge magnet can defeat the Earth's Gravity).
                Now, what happens when you give Gravity the strength of the Coulomb Force? What happens when that SuperGravity isn't resisted by the Electrical repulsion?

                What you have there folks is Core Collapse, or more commonly known as a Hypernova.

                And this isn't even Master rank (no energy was created or destroyed, only converted from one form to another).
