                                        Originally posted by Darinas View Post
                                        RP question, how much can Geists communicate with their Sin-Eaters? Can they talk and exchange dialogues? Or is it limited to the Geist doing signs or mimics? Or does it vary on the Geist? That always was a source of confusion for me.
                                        Unbound geists roll a chance die on all social rolls outside the context of frightening or intimidating (and apply the same limitation to social rolls made against them), per the Broken quality.

                                        The descriptions of the levels of Relationship seem to indicate that even at a Coercive level both halves of a Sin-Eater can understand each other's immediate desires, with higher levels of Synergy affording a stronger understanding of each other's emotions and goals.

                                        Your geist can't sit down and have a frank conversation with you, but it can be cryptic and unsettling and terrifying in helpful ways just fine, and the Tyrannous lean into that being the only language it clearly understands without being more powerfully aligned. Most of its contribution is going to be crisis points and shared gut feelings as you help solve the mystery of its Remembrance and wrap up your unfinished business.
