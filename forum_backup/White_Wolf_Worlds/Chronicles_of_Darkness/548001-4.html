                                            Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post
                                            Thematically the idea that the Fury's "patron" would ever let practical concerns get in the way of justice but ignoring the G-M because it's just a mindless machine, that makes sense. (Though I still think there's a lot of milage in Fury vs G-M games)
                                            Furies may believe their "boss" ignores the Machine because the latter's mindless, demons may believe it's because the latter is too big or impractical to take on, and angels inasmuch as they believe anything believe it is the boss. Other supernaturals who've heard enough about the subject to bother with an opinion usually go with whichever of these they hear first; mages of course have many more theories than that, because mages are theory-junkies (a leading one is that the Furies' boss and the Machine are both forces of order, and while they can recognise chaos as an enemy, they can't imagine a different order, and so are blind to one another).
