              Primarily I'm interested in understanding if there is already something out there for this, independently or in one of the game lines. I may be dreaming that I read something on the subject here before.

              That said, uplifting an animal wouldn't be too hard if we were to define a set of merits that accomplishes the step-up in intelligence and capabilities (speaking, say). Animals, for example, have mental stats but the don't work on quite the same scale. Certain magics (Mind, Life, other sorcerous modifications) could do this easily. I'm not really thinking "add opposable thumbs" as that crosses the line into hybrid in my book.

              I got on this track of thinking by conceiving an scenario for a PC who might be able to turn his soul/spirit into a raven to escape what would otherwise be certain death. And then, probably would not be able to turn back. (Long story, so just roll with this.)

              --Khanwulf
