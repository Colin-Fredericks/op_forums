        Time to check in with the Golden Eagle Crew!

        I need to head this one off with some explanation to follow the journals a bit, as a lot focuses on Ghosts, being this covers the play. So the Uratha did the Siskur da ritual to allow the whole Conspiracy to interact with ghosts physically. The thing that makes this complicated is only a few of them can see or hear ghosts; Wolfram, being a Sin-Eater, can, and can use his Keystone (a flaming sword) under the influence of the Ritual to affect ghosts as well. Dacian can through the Darkling Contract of Shade and Spirit (but is not skilled at barehanded fighting). Marek can because of Bone Shadow Gifts. However, Alla, Tatiana (by far the best barehanded fighter), Dearbháil, and Lizette are essentially blind and deaf to them. I believe Wolfram may plan to get some Ceremonies to deal with this problem for next time. Dana, not being part of the Conspiracy, did not participate in the Ritual.

        So the play on Walpurgis Night 1922. Begin!

        Session XXVII

        Marek’s Thoughts

        30 April 1922

        Today was the day of the play. We started things off by calling down a Sacred Hunt on the play and our intentions to make sure that no rituals are completed. We included most everyone in the pack, so they would have the ability to see ghosts. Much like the last ritual I conducted, Lizette represented the ritual within the play and I snuck up on her and ran my claws along her throat. I did not carry my rifle this time since my gift does not allow me to shoot ghosts, only bare handed or melee attacks will work. That was fine by me. I’ve been practicing and have gotten better since the last time we called Siskur da.

        We arrived at the play early so we could position ourselves. Dacian immediately went up into the rafters above the stage and hid. Dearbhail, Fenric, and Dana all went to the audience area. Tatiana stationed herself in the orchestra pit. I went backstage and hid among the shadows. Wolfram and Lizette arrived shortly after the play started. Wolfram went to the audience while Lizette snuck backstage and made her way up to the catwalk. Shortly after the play started I noticed that Greta was around. I could not see her, but I could smell her. I let Alla , who was waiting for her scene to come up, know that Greta was hiding backstage. Alla tried to get Greta’s attention to no effect.

        Between the first and second acts I noticed that aura that accompanies Blackstaff’s gift when he wants to spy on an area come up. It had a slightly different feel this time, but it was the same gift. I climbed up to the catwalk to let Lizette know what was going on. I tried to find Dacian, but he was too well hidden. I just hoped that he had heard me talking to Lizette. Around that time I also spotted Greta and pointed her out to our slight mechanic. I figured all of this was important for the others to know, so I made my way to the audience seating discreetly and told Wolfram what was going on. My message passed along, I made my way back to the backstage area.

        During the second act I noticed two ghosts walking towards the stage from the back. It was a mother/daughter pair. They looked emaciated, like they’d died of starvation or possibly exposure. I thought that they might have been refugees. They spoke Greek. I stepped in their path and tried to make myself understood though we apparently did not share a language. I tried different languages until I hit on one that they had some familiarity with, Russian. I think I managed to get the word “stop” across to them. Then suddenly the aura of the power surrounding us changed and I was suddenly struck blind, deaf and mute. It was rather disconcerting to say the least. The only reason I didn’t become more upset was because of my wolf senses. I could still smell and that gave me an idea of where everything and everyone was. I indicated my eyes, ears and throat and made a slashing motion to tell the others that I was blind, deaf and dumb. I do not know if they saw me or not, but I think they did. That I will admit is an experience that I would rather not repeat. It reminded me a little too much of when a grenade went off too close to my trench. The sound was deafening and for a while all I could hear was ringing in my ears. I could not hear it when I tried to talk, nor when someone tried to talk to me. My squad and I were brought back to the medical tent for a while until our hearing returned. I was never so happy to hear a bird’s call until that day.

        As soon as I was struck by that power the ghosts made a dash for the stage. I managed to catch the mother ghost and Wolfram, who’d come backstage, grabbed the little girl. He tried to tell them to stop, and that he cannot let them continue. Then just as suddenly I was able to hear and see again. Maybe Blackstaff realized that the power wasn’t restricting me the way he’d hoped it would and decided to try something different. I will admit that it was good to be able to see with my eyes again. My sense of smell gave me a good idea of what was around me, but it doesn’t take the place of my eyes.

        Then, before we could do anything, we were all pulled through the floor of the stage. The ghosts were pulled with us. We were not alone. The other man there asked us what we were doing, so we told him. He said that he was not a friend and then seemed to disappear. It wasn’t until later that I realized that he was likely one of the vampires charged with keeping any powers from being used on the play or the actors. I guess he assumed that we were all a threat and pulled us beneath the stage. While we were down there Wolfram continued to talk to the mother and they eventually came to an accord. They would stop trying to influence the play and Wolfram would do something for them. After that was settled we found the stairs and climbed back up to the backstage area. We were greeted by the sight of two armed ghosts wearing bone armor and carrying maces. We figured that they were part of Blackstaff’s guards. The mother and daughter ghost went back below the stage to stay out of danger.

        The fight with the guards didn’t go too badly. It was especially unpleasant for Dacian, but he doesn’t remember what happened. That’s for the best. It started out with me grabbing one of the guards and Wolfrman materializing a sword and going after the other one. He wounded it, if it can be said that ghosts can be injured. After a relatively short time the one that I was holding got away and Greta went after him. She used one of her powers and grappled the ghost that I lost my grip on. The power was a little disturbing to watch. Suddenly she resembled a somewhat bloated corpse. Her skin was blue-ish and distended. I think even the humans saw that. They started to react and Tatiana got them out of the backstage area to safety. We continued the fight in private.

        One of the ghosts then decided that possessing one of us would be his best bet and went after Dacian. Upon possessing him, Dacian’s body deformed. His ribs came out and made claws for his hands. His spine lengthened and his skull was pushed upward. It looked rather painful to me. Tatiana went ahead and grabbed Dacian to ensure that the ghost could not harm anyone on or off stage. While she was grappling that one I attacked the one that Greta was holding. I shifted to Dalu form and attacked him with my claws. I managed a couple of hits on the ghost before it finally faded. It is not permanently dead, but it will be awhile before it is possible for him to come back to the waking world. Meanwhile I believe that Alla was trying to use one of her Vampire gifts to convince the ghost to leave Dacian’s body. I believe she was successful, as the ghost fled our friend and his body returned to normal leaving wounds as it did so. As it fled I tried to attack it, but did not manage to take it down or restrain it.

        Dearbhail came backstage at the intermission and we caught her up on all that had happened. She immediately healed a confused Dacian. While she was doing that Wolfram, Tatiana and I discussed going after Blackstaff before he got away. We had felt his area-wide power come down shortly after the last ghost fled. The problem we ran into was the area that he could affect with that gift is awfully large and there were only three of us. And of those three only the two of us could track by scent. In the end we decided that Tatiana, Greta and I would track Blackstaff. We headed out in a South by Southeast pattern. We caught a faint scent of him, but it ended. We assume that he got into a car and fled that way.

        We were told, while we searched for Blackstaff, that Alla did a very good job with her altered lines in the play. The audience didn’t know that anything had been changed. I believe that Connie and Agrippa did and Agrippa at least was not happy with it. From what he heard from Dearbhail those two plus Fenric disappeared from the audience for a while and reappeared at the end. My guess is that the same guy that pulled us through the floor of the backstage did something similar to Agrippa and her two companions. Perhaps they were trying to influence the play and were removed to keep their powers from working? Fenric was likely just caught up in the wrong place at the wrong time. He was asleep when he was returned to his seat. I would have been angry if he’d been returned in worse shape than when he “left,” but he was fine.

        After the play we discussed Wolfram’s wedding. It was decided that Marlena will be the maid of honor, and Heinrich will be the best man. Dacian and I will be the groomsman, and Dearbhail and Lizette will be the bridesmaids. Tatiana will be the ring-bearer. I believe that something was also discussed about Lizette building a “flower cannon” to replace the need for a flower girl. That should be interesting to say the least.

        I will admit that I was honored to be asked to be a groomsman. I missed my brother’s wedding two years ago when I was dealing with the situation with the orphans. Sometimes I feel like I have let my brother down because I was not there for him, but I’m not sure what else I could have done. Those children needed to be freed from those monsters and I was hired to do so. I was also in a position to help them after the Deuderman’s had been dealt with. Speaking of which, I received a letter from my brother a little while ago letting me know that Denisa and Erik were able to adopt both Kvĕta and Dominik. They are both growing well and seem to be happy with their new home. I am glad that I was able to help them find them a safe and caring place to live. I have been told that both children insist on calling me “Uncle Marek.” I guess I made an impression. As for Desnisa and Erik, I only met them the one time, but they seemed like good people. Andel has confirmed this with his letter I am glad to say.

        That said, I am looking forward to Wolfram and Christine’s wedding. They will finally be allowed to act like the mated pair that they are, though I’m sure they would find my choice of words uncouth. I am happy for them though. They make a good couple and will make good parents. I am looking forward to it all.

        Lizette’s Plans

        30th April 1922

        We prevented the play from becoming a disaster. It soon proved out of my league.

        First, I escorted Wolfram to see Prince Etienne and negotiate having Vampires make sure nothing vampiric and supernatural happened at the play. This lead to the Prince sending out his goon squad of Nosferatu Vampires who thoroughly intimidated all the vampires there, well except Alla who decided they were cool. They did overstep their bounds a little with Wolfram and Marek, but that did not prove too dangerous.

        The real problem was the ghosts. Marek, Dacian, and Wolfram were the only ones on our team who could see ghosts, and while we could all, say, punch them, Wolfram and Dacian are not known for their fisticuffs. I suppose one could say we should have foreseen the problem, but I am not sure what we could have done about it. Greta’s arrival helped, but she is a slip of a girl and not a skilled brawler either.

        I need to invent some sort of ghost goggles. That would be useful. I don’t even know where I would begin to work on that, though. It will take a lot of thought. Perhaps, some sort of box trap I can store ghosts in. Possibly to release on my enemies…

        Mayhem! Er, where was I…

        Dacian took position in the catwalks and rafters above stage with me, and quickly vanished from my view. Marek came and checked on us near the end of act one, and pointed out that Greta was there as well. Soon after act two began a pair of ghosts, mother and daughter I am told, tried to get in. Marek and Dacian intercepted, but after a short conversation I could not really understand, they tried to bolt. Marek grabbed the mother, while Wolfram got the little girl, coming from the audience to back stage just in time. That was when they were grabbed by the Nosferatu and pulled beneath the stage somehow, and Dacian, Marek and Tatiana went below with Greta.

        This was an error, as ghosts got in I could not see. Armored ones, apparently. When they got up there was a fierce brawl, and while initially Marek and grabbed one, they escaped. Greta managed to hold onto one Wolfram tore apart, but the other briefly possessed Dacian, turning him into an awful monster. That I could see. Fortunately Alla, who arrived at that point, turned her Vampiric charms toward influencing that ghost to leave Dacian. He fled, since Tatiana terrified him and Wolfram had destroyed his counterpart.

She has gone to get her clothes, a few items, and pick out a bottle of Champaign and perhaps port from the cellar. I’ve got two bottles of Cognac here.

It won’t be like Wolfram and Christina’s, though.

                    Session XXVIII

                    Marek’s Thoughts

I brought back dolls for the two girls from France and a Polish pocket knife for Dominik. I made sure that he knew how to be safe around a blade and showed him how to whittle with spare wood.                                             Duisburg
                                            25th of October

                                            The office was familiar. The chair Wolfram sat in as he looked out the large window that dominated one wall was as old as he could remember. The furnishings were equally unchanged from his childhood. He looked away from the view of the factory below to the ancient desk of strong dark wood and to his brother sitting behind it scowling down at the papers before him before removing his glasses to pinch the bridge of his nose and sigh. Heinrich had not claimed this space so much as it had claimed him.

                                            “I wasn't expecting it to be so quiet today,” Wolfram said with a glance at the mostly still and darkened floor out the room's sole window.

                                            “The farmers can hardly afford seed and stock,” Heinrich replied as he leaned back, “Let alone new tractors.                                                             Session XXXI

                                                            Marek's Thoughts

                                                            26 October, 1922

                                                            Well, today proved to be an interesting day. It started with Lizette working on a new weapon. Something akin to a crossbow, but instead of firing bolts it will fire the equivalent of saw blades. It is of course an interesting device. I’m not sure how practical it will be to use, but it will definitely provide the ability to do massive damage to enemy combatants. Especially if she perfects her idea of setting it up so it lights the saw blades on fire upon release. I’m not sure what it is with Lizette and fire, but the two are inexorably linked.

                                                            During this conversation we found out that Fenric does not know how to fire a gun. Well, Lizette offered to teach him how to shoot using her Thompson. I argued that it might be better to start with something smaller, like a pistol, before he works up to a machine gun. Lizette argued that a machine gun was more effective since you didn’t really have to aim it, you just point it in the general direction of the enemy and fire. While that may be true, a machine gun is something that you should have to work your way up to using. Getting used to the recoil on a pistol will help, not to mention he should be taught gun safety before any shooting actually happens. Tatiana was also against the idea of starting out with a machine gun, but didn’t stick around long to argue the point. I don’t think she wanted to get in the middle of all of that.

                                                            Wolfram came outside around that point and after hearing what we were discussing, took my side about showing Fenric how to fire a pistol first. Wolfram brought up the gun safety idea as well, which was a good thing. I think Lizette got a little frustrated with us. She espoused the idea of starting with the gun that doesn’t take fine aim to work effectively. I can see that not needing to aim would be a good thing, but from my perspective it is important to know exactly how guns work and how to fire one responsibly. Guns were created with one purpose in mind, and that is to kill. They kill animals and humans with great efficiency. They should not be thought of as toys or seen like a simple tool. They are far too dangerous for that mindset. Not that I think Lizette sees guns as toys, but she is rather more blasé about their use than I am. Granted I am a sniper by trade, so I do specialize in hitting individual targets with accuracy and am very careful about who and what I hit.
                                                            Eventually Lizette realized that we weren’t going to have Fenric start his firearms training with a Thompson, and she lent him her pistol. I let Wolfram go through the safety issues first and we’ll start on actual target practice later.

                                                            So, while we were doing all that Dearbhail was researching the different Cabaret’s in town to see which one(s) we should go to. She also spent some time shopping for German style clothing so she doesn’t stand out as being from Paris when we go out later. Thankfully men’s cloths look much the same no matter where you go, so I just wore my usual button up shirt and slacks. They are neutral colors and don’t stand out all that much.

                                                            After Dearbhail came back with her information and the sun has sufficiently set we decided to go out to one or two of these Cabaret’s. The first one that we went to was called “The Hedge Maze.” Needless to say it was run by people like my fae companions. It was also the place to be if you want to meet the Spring Court leader Prince Sascha. So we went to this club. It was fine as far as clubs go. My fae friends were greeted by a woman named Oneida, who is the bodyguard of Prince Sascha. She would not let the others meet with her Prince until they had taken a vow that they would not do him any harm. Apparently a day or two ago there was an attempt on his life by one of the Bound, as they called him. I would have called the man one of the Claimed. He was like Wolfram, a “Sin-Eater.” He came to the club and pulled an oriental style sword out of this chest, like Wolfram can do, and proceeded to attack Sascha. Oneida was not overly forthcoming on the details, but apparently the Claimed man was driven off and the fae Prince saved. He made it out with no visible injuries. Or if he was hurt they used their fae magicks to heal him, like I have seen Dearbhail do in the past. It is a very useful ability.

                                                            Once Dearbhail heard that it was a Claimed that attacked the Prince she left and went back with Wolfram in tow. I think she wanted his perspective on the events of that night, or so he could confirm whether or not it was a Claimed man. They did not know to which group of Claimed the man belonged to, but Oneida is under the impression that Skadi had something to do with it. I am not sure if Skadi would actually try and eliminate a fellow monarch or not. She has been “culling” possible Summer Courtiers that are particularly angry about the political state of affairs in Germany, so she might turn her attentions on Sascha, but I don’t really know her well enough to make an informed guess about that.

                                                            After that we went to the club called “Saints and Sinners.” The owner of the club is a wolf-blooded man named Herr Holbien. In his employ he has a Claimed woman named Sabine. Wolfram talked to her a couple of times over the course of the night. Apparently she is rather protective of the other dancers and will defend them if needs be. She also has an interest in bladed weapons and was carrying a couple on her when they met. Her act also included knife throwing. I do not know if she has a knife throwing act on stage to explain why she carries blades around with her, or if it was a happy coincidence. Either way, it worked out well for her. Sabine also said that members of The Watch frequent the club in order to cause trouble. There are also a few “foreign” neighborhoods that they go to on a regular basis to also cause similar problems. It does not take much to imagine what kinds of trouble they get involved in when they go to the club or those neighborhoods.

                                                            In the course of our visit we saw the headliner for this club. It is a woman named Della. She sang a rather haunting song about killing anyone in the army remotely connected to the death of her lover. This reminded me of the situation with Thérèse. She was rather adamant about killing the one responsible for the death of her lover. I don’t think she went through as many people to find the one that did it, but the similarity was there all the same.

                                                            So, Dacian used a power to evaluate whether or not Della was supernatural or not. Apparently she is. We do not know what kind of supernatural being she is though. She is not a fae, we would have all seen that. She is not Uratha, Tatiana and I would have known that. And she is not dead, so she is not a Claimed or a Vampire. She may be one of those creatures like Iris, or she could be a magic user. We don’t know for sure. I do not know if this will be a mystery that we will be able to unravel or not. I will make sure to keep an eye out for her though, to be on the safe side.

                                                            Overall it was an interesting experience. We learned more about what The Watch is up to, and about another supernatural being in town. We found out that a Claimed tried to kill the Spring Court monarch, and that at least some people believe that Skadi was involved in that incident. We also saw several “interesting” acts at the clubs. There was knife throwing and cross-dressing and a lot of groping for breasts. I think the others enjoyed that bit more than I did, but I will admit that it was entertaining to watch. Not just the acts but people’s reactions to the acts. Some people openly enjoyed them. Others were more discrete in showing their interest, and still others probably shouldn’t have come at all given their reactions to the acts.

                                                            We left the clubs pretty late and headed back to the house. When we got back we saw Greta leaving just as we were arriving. She seemed rather pale to me and I was about to inquire into her health, but then I remembered that Marissa was at the house as well. Those two together is the likely cause for Greta’s wan appearance.

                                                            Greta did have some information for us though. She said that we could find the Communists at a place called the “Gold Sparrow.” She also said that Willie had left us a message, that she may have “accidentally” steamed open when she was making a pot of coffee. The message simply said that the Black Company was descending upon the railyard to look for clues to what happened there. It was good to know, but not something that we could actively do anything about then. It was late and if we showed up it would be obvious that we had something to do with the events that took place. It will be known soon enough that we are in town since we delivered that picture of Tatiana to Ilsa, but it may not be known what exactly we did that day. I can only hope that our involvement will go undiscovered. If it is revealed then Willie will be in trouble, and we will have enemies on two fronts looking for our blood.

                                                            We are after all strangers in a potentially hostile place. Alla’s affiliations make her more of a target from her own kind, as do me and Tatiana’s. We are surrounded by the Pure, and as soon as they know that we are here, life will become difficult for us. Alla is thankfully protected by the Circle of the Crone, but Tatiana and I have no such protection. Not that my fiery companion would ever ask for protection. She is far too interested in fighting her own battles to want someone else to do it for her. I just happen to think that we would be greatly outnumbered if things go badly for us.

                                                            Because it is pleasant to be drunk and lie still on a comfortable bed.

                                                            Yes, that place with the smoke and the many humans and many human noises was a cabaret, foolish volchonok.

                                                            I did not drink so much at the first one because our pack was separated and those away had made a promise that tied their hands.

                                                            I did not think the fairy was a threat, no, but they were asked to make such a promise because of an attack by a Sin-Eater who pulled a sword from his chest, like our Wolfram can do.

                                                            Because this Prince Sasha is a Jew and has sex with men and owns a club. He says. The other fairy, the pretty one, thinks it was Skadi.

                                                            I do not know, noisy volchonok, that is for fairies to find out. I do not think Skadi works with Sin-Eaters though.

                                                            Our Sin-Eater is very upset, yes. He does not like this Watch and what they do to his land.

I do not think the Pure would kill someone because they slept with the same sex or because they were a Jew.                                                             Lizette’s Plans

                                                            27th October, Morning

                                                            Yesterday was wild. We went to two Cabarets and met cute charismatic guys and beautiful, deadly women. Tis almost enough to make me like Germany. Then Greta showed to remind me I don’t.

                                                            I began the morning working on a buzzaw bow, which I saw in a dream. After working on bows for Dacian, I got the idea of a crossbow base that fired round, serrated blades. It took longer to design than I expected, though building went quick. I even built an upgrade to light the saw when fired if it was covered with accelerant. I could saw arms or legs right off with this thing, making it a near perfect non-lethal weapon.

                                                            Mayhem!

                                                            However, some members of my group are just suspicious of progress, and bound to old-fashioned ideas about how to learn to use weapons. Even otherwise rather dashing members.

                                                            Anyway, the problem began when I was trying out using one buzzaw bow in each hand. It was a bit much, so since the bow was sized for me not, say, Dacian, I offered Fenric the other one. I then discovered Fenric did not know how to shoot! Well, I saw a golden opportunity.

                                                            See, in the 19th Century, people had to rely on target practice from even the start of their shooting career, be it hunting, skeet shooting or dummies. However, we were in the 20th Century now, and machine guns are far more effective. Also, easier to learn to use as a starter weapon. After all, one need only aim in the general direction of foes and spray the area to achieve maximum effectiveness. Advanced skills at targeting and hitting a single target with a single shot are useful, but less immediately important to the new shootist. After all, which is easier, aiming in the vague direction of a target or aiming for a weak point? You could give schoolchildren machineguns and they would be able to mow down a number of foes without training.

                                                            However, many members of the Motley are mired in archaic practices. For example Marek, a sniper himself, seemed to feel that aiming for a target, the entire thing he has advanced skill in, is easier than simply spraying a few dozen bullets in the air. I cannot imagine how he came to this conclusion. It is utterly bizarre. Tatiana also argued and then quickly left, but she had seemed leery ever since I was firing flaming buzzsaws, so she probably feared my wrath. Not that I would shoot a Motley mate with a flaming buzzsaw, whether their limbs would grow back or not.

                                                            The real disappointment was Wolfram, and I imagine this is one of the few times I will find myself writing that phrase. He also strongly felt that introducing Fenric to the modern firearm via a machinegun (particularly one so handy as the Thompson) was a bad idea. Yes, he too felt shooting one bullet at one target was somehow an easier start for Fenric than spraying an entire area with bullets. I cannot imagine what he is thinking.

                                                            I figure in the future we will have weapons which can turn an area dark as nightfall by filling the area so full of bullets. Then people won’t be teaching archaic technology like single shot pistols. No, we will be firing hundreds, possibly thousands in the air from a single weapon, possibly from a flying artillery platform.

                                                            Mayhem!

                                                            Anyway, we eventually finished that up. I loaned Fenric my Star. Seemed the kindest thing to do, since his education was going the slowest way possible, and to show my magnanimity.

                                                            That evening, we planned our evening out. Dearbháil had looked into the Cabarets and found the most promising, including the residence of the Spring King, Prince Sascha. As the sun went down and Alla became more animated, we set out.

                                                            I neglected to tell her I tried to improve her Ghoul’s education. I am sure she would have appreciated it. Anyway.

                                                            We wound up visiting two Cabarets. The first, The Hedge Maze, was a club run by Prince Sascha, the Spring King. Going in we met Oneida, a Fairest Shadowsoul who was the new Claviger, and she was intent on keeping us away from Prince Sascha until we had made an Oath not to harm him. He had been attacked by a Sin-Eater she presumed was sent by Queen Skadi. Dacian, Dearbháil, Alla and I went back. He offered us drinks, and flirted heavily with Dearbháil and Dacian. Alla and I were apparently less his type. He downplayed the threat to himself, but my Witch’s Intuition told me he was very afraid. Oneida was worried too, but it was more in line with her visible behavior. Dearbháil went and got Wolfram when she heard the Fog Man had used a similar power to his, much to Oneida’s irritation. I am not sure if Prince Sascha got anything useful from Wolfram, but he was added to Dearbháil and Dacian on his flirt list. In the end, the five of us had alcohol brought to us and three of us had the option to “walk him home” for safety, but few answers.

                                                            Prince Sascha is Jewish, Bisexual and influential in the club scene. It is small wonder Die Wacht dislike him. They may not even know he is faerie. He is also a Romancer and Dancer. I found him a fop, but a harmless one. Oneida was pretty, but so tired and driven to protect her king it was hard to learn much about her.

                                                            Next we visited Saints and Sinners, a much larger club with a religious motif. I was not real crazy about that. It is run by a Herr Holbein, who is apparently not a Werewolf but kin to them. We never really got to talk to him, it was busy, and Tatiana and Marek did not seem to really have a consensus for how to deal with him anyway. Saints and Sinners was possibly twice as big as The Hedge Maze,, and more than twice as busy.

                                                            Soon after arriving we met a Sin-Eater named Sabine who was rather suspicious of Wolfram. Listening to her conversations, it was clear she was a dancer, and took a protective view of the other dancers. Watching her, she wore tells that she was Sapphic, suggesting she was a member of Die Freundinnen though I don’t think she ever confirmed that. She was a pleasure to see, dancing, walking, or catching and hurling knives, as her rather unusual show demonstrated. Her skill with knives was quite impressively displayed in her act, and I am pretty sure everyone, male or female, was impressed, and a little bit in love. Apparently she died of suffocation. I don’t even want to think about the implications of that…

                                                            Beyond that, the acts were entertaining. There was one with a “guy” (clearly a woman, and not even a butch one) and “his” wife, where she got her head stuck and he tried to get her out, accidentally undressing her at one point and taking advantage of the situation a bit. The next act had a “guy” and girl where the “guy” had her look at the sun with binoculars, which had her blinded and feeling all the way around and, shockingly, groping several women’s breasts. It was rather lowbrow and juvenile, and I had seen the latter interpreted in Paris, but reasonably entertaining.

                                                            The act that really caught my attention, though, was a chanteuse named Della with a smokey voice and bedroom eyes, who sauntered onstage and sang a tale of revenge that began as a sad song but was a long, lurid ballad of seduction and murder, with her pulling the hearts out of men responsible for her lover’s death in the war. Despite their bloody ends, one could almost envy them as the bourbon-voiced siren sang of how she seduced them to their ends. I found myself utterly distracted by her performance, not even thinking about my steel hardening process. I felt like I was coming out of a trance when she sashayed off stage, and the others agreed she was supernatural, if not what her deal was. I am sure finding out will be intriguing.

                                                            Dacian and Dearbháil did not care to “guard” Prince Sascha, and of course Wolfram didn’t, so we went back to the house. We met a slightly disheveled and very pale Greta Hess leaving. I suppose Apparently she and Marissa Sangiovanni had made up. Intensely, from the look of it. Anyway, her obvious sexual exploits were not what she brought up, but that Willie had contacted Wolfram. She had read the letter of course, making a half assed excuse. Willie was warning us that Schwarze Reichswehr was looking heavily into what happened. Wolfram had warned him he might be looked into. Wolfram elected to not chastise Greta for breaking into our house, reading our mail and sleeping with our housemate, but to let her go staggering back to her car.

                                                            By that point, I was tired, with beautiful singers and dancers roaming in my head and not imagining what gross stuff that child had been up to. Then I slept, and dreamed of a tank that could grow airplane wings and fly around shooting rockets.

                                                            Mayhem!

                                                            Now tis my Birthday. We will see if anyone remembers.

                                                            Wolfram’s Stories

                                                            Berlin
                                                            26th of October

                                                            Wolfram lifted a glass of wine from the tidy desk before him and sipped it as he held the phone to his ear and waited. The study around him was well ordered but it was all new and lacked the charm and familiarity that only time spent living in a space could give it. A fire crackled, freshly stoked and flickering brightly as it cast dancing shadows about the otherwise dark room. From behind him, Wolfram could feel the heat of the Firebrand Templar standing vigilant and ready for battle. More than ready, eager. Conflict was what kindled and fed his Geist's fiery spirit after all and there was no shortage of conflict brewing in the city around them.

                                                            But before his thoughts could follow that trail further his wife's voice came to his ear and his lips curled up at the corners automatically in response. “Sorry to keep you waiting darling, I was resting and it takes so long for me to do anything these days.”

                                                            He closed his eyes and let the smile claim more of his lips. “I wish this business had not called me away from your side, Christina.”

                                                            “It is important. You're needed there.”

                                                            “You are important,” he countered, “But yes, there is much that needs to be done here. So very much.”

                                                            “Is it not going well? Is everyone alright?”

                                                            “Don't fret my love. Everyone is fine. Things are going as well as could be hoped. Our initial efforts proved successful and we're considering how best to proceed.”

                                                            “You sound troubled dearheart. What's the matter?”

                                                            “Nothing...” Wolfram stopped and shook his head slightly. “We are dealing with the Watch very cautiously, I promise.”

                                                            “All of you?” There was a hint of concern and doubt in her voice.

                                                            “All of us love,” Wolfram assured her, “Even Tatiana and Lizette. We spent the evening meeting with one of the fay monarchs at his cabaret and investigating another larger and more prominent one where I met a member of one of the other Krewes here. We are being careful and not rushing into anything.”

                                                            “I've heard some things about those cabarets...”

                                                            “It was all a bit risque to be honest. But the others seemed to enjoy it.”

                                                            “The others you say.”

                                                            “The performers were talented but my heart has found it's home with you my love.” There was a pause as Wolfram took a slow breath. “Besides which, there is so much that needs to be done. Too much to be distracted.”

                                                            “So, is it really as bad as we've heard?”

                                                            Wolfram's lips drew into a thin line as he considered his answer. “Worse,” he admitted honestly, “Or perhaps it is simply that it has to be seen to truly be understood.” His voice trailed off and there was a long silence before he shook his head and spoke again. “But please, let us speak of something brighter. Is everything ready for tomorrow night?”

                                                            “It will be. The servants have set up tables in the west green and I have enlisted several men from the village to bring lumber and tomorrow they'll be setting up the bonfire. I do believe they are convinced that I am mad to insist on such a large one but one of the advantages of my condition is that I am allowed such flights of whimsy without question.” As she continued to speak of the other details involving the food and drink and music Wolfram listened to Christina’s voice and felt himself relaxing.

                                                            “I love you so much.”

                                                            Christina’s ongoing recount halted mid-sentence. “I love you too. Are you sure you're all right?”

                                                            “I will be,” he answered softly, “I just miss you. It's good to hear your voice.”

                                                            “Yes it is. I worry about you. I know the Eagle won't let you or Lizette come to permanent harm but still...” Her voice trailed off.

                                                            “I will always return to you my love. Always.”

                                                            End Session XXXI

Sabine would be one of the Silent; she is “played by” Dita Von Teese while Price Sascha is “played by” Alan Cumming. I don’t use many celebrities, but happened to with those two.

                                                            So much tension. But I also tried to play up the sort of desperate frivolity this time. Less of the “times are tough” sort of talk, though their was some, more beautiful women, handsome men, and music. I wanted the Cabarets to feel unreal, and they did.
