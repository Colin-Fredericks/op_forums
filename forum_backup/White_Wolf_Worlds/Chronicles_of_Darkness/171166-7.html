            Currently working on a retro-style re-imagining of the New World of Darkness/Chronicles of Darkness, using the 1e nWoD rules.

            It eschews the modern Gothic aesthetic and instead pursues a nostalgic retro aesthetic heavily inspired by the pop culture and consumerism of the 1980's and 1990's. It's even set in Miami Beach in the year 199X for that extra touch.

            Essentially, this new chronicle setting is like "World of Darkness meets Vaporwave aesthetics" or "Vice City with Fangs" and it incorporates Vampire, Hunter, Changeling, and Princess.

            Werewolf, Mage, Promethean and Geist would be canon but not playable while Demon and Beast are explicitly excised from the setting and declared non-canon.
