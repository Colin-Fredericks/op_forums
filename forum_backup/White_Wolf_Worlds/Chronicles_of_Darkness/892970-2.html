        So right now we have:

        Crown of Obligation
        The character gains an additional Vice, and regains one point of Mana every time she gains Willpower through either Vice. Also, she reduces opponent’s Doors in Social maneuvering by her Mind dots

        Crown of Vision
        When using the Sympathetic Range Attainment, the character counts as having a weak sympathetic connection to any subject she has no connection for. She reduces the effects of Occultation and similar powers by her Space dots
        _

        Crown of Doctrine
        The character adds her Prime dots to her Gnosis to determine her Mana pool (every effective dot above 10 grants 10 extra Mana), and may heal resistant damage by Pattern restoration

        Crown of Fury (Revised)
        Any physical attack, the character makes ignores Armor or successes from a Dodge equal to her dots in Forces. In addition, she may raise or change her Mage Armor without spending Mana.
        _

        Ministry of the Nemesis (Crown of Boundaries)
        The Crown of Boundaries increases the local Gauntlet rating by 1; additionally, the character's Spirit spells ignore penalties from the Gauntlet or other similar sources equal to her dots in Spirit.

        Ministry of the Raptor (Crown of Predation)
        The character may ignore surrender and does not suffer the Beaten Down tilt; conversely, the Beaten Down and Surrender rules are still in force for her opponents even if she are trying to kill them. Additionally, she adds her Life dots as dice to any rolls for Down and Dirty combat.
        _

        Crown of [Fate]
        description

        Crown of Agency
        The character may ignore the effects of Status Merits or Destiny dice used directly against her, up to her Time dots. The Seer may also ignore one point of Withstand when using the Temporal Sympathy Attainment.
        _

        Crown of [Death]
        description

        Crown of Scarcity
        The character disregards the equipment bonus of items and uses their Resources cost instead. She may also have a number of dedicated magical tools equal to her dots in Matter. (I changed this slightly to be symmetrical with the others)
        _

        So, we lack the Psychopomp and the Ruin's Crowns.

                    From the old forums, Words from the Brookshaw on the Prophet.

                    Originally posted by David Brookshaw
                    The Prophet, remember, is all about the bogus but pervasive idea of the "Great Man" view of history - He tells Her followers that only select individuals have any agency to change the world, while the mass of people are doomed to follow along behind the pedagogue of the current flavour. Her followers both try to become "Great Men" and identify and neutralise the same among their enemies - they're the sorts to kill the leader of a rebellion, identify the guru who's attracting Pentacle mages to their banner and do away with or subvert them.



                    The Crown Prelacy for the Prophet would be about assuming that mantle of leadership and destiny, the Sword Prelacy is about identifying and countering it in others and the Temple Prelacy creates areas of "gravity" that such individuals are drawn to; a Seer of the Prophet would find people flocking to their banner while they set up base in a Temple to that Exarch, while other Seers use Time Prelacy Temple Stones to set traps for Great Men they disapprove of.

                                    Here's some ideas for Crown Attainments on my end. One is a version of amechra's Crown of Boundaries. While two (the Crowns of Denial and Corruption) are a 2E Conversion of 1E Homebrew from King of the Hill.

                                    The Nemesis grants the Crown of Boundaries. When conducting social maneuvering with spirits, the Seer raises his first impression with them by half his Spirit dots, rounded up. Furthermore, he may choose to raise or lower the Gauntlet Strength of his surroundings by half his Spirit dots, rounded up.

                                    The Psychopomp grants the Crown of Denial. The Seer is considered to be an anchor for any and all ghosts within sensory range, and can choose to inflict a penalty to their Manifestations and Numina equal to her Death dots. When interacting with a ghost, she automatically what conditions must be fulfilled for him to resolve his anchors.

                                    The Prophet grants the Crown of Judgement. The Seer adds his Time dots to the Withstand ratings of his Temporal Sympathy. Conversely, he halves the Withstand ratings of those targeted by his spells through Temporal Sympathy.

                                    The Ruin grants the Crown of Corruption. When carrying out actions which carry out the Exarch's Mystery Commands, the Seer's enemies suffer a penalty equal to his Fate dots on all attempts to oppose him. Furthermore, when using the Conditional Duration Attainment, double the levels of Duration given by a condition.
