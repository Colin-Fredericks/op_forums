                                I'd either have it be a very important person, at least important regarding the locus' resonance, or someone utterly unassuming who just happens to have been born at the right time with the right stuff or lived the right life and happens to be full of Essence for no easily identifiable reason.

                                The former would probably be urged on and off by spirits that matched its resonance. They would gather around the locus and protect them zealously and try their best to keep it motivated and doing whatever it is to amplify the locus aspect of the person. For more rare or dangerous resonances this could be a wrecking ball to the spirit. A walking Wound would basically spawn demons everywhere and have them constantly around it, protecting the locus like the dark forces protected Damien Thorn.

                                An unassuming sort seems like it would be more openly manipulated and taken advantage of. If for whatever reason they aren't doing something that creates the Resonance, they just 'are' then spirits and other forces would do what they could to take advantage of it. With the 'important person' more aware spirits might guess that by Claiming they would lose access to the locus, as it would alter whatever they're doing, and the merging with the soul might damage whatever connection they had. With a random locus-person what they do doesn't matter. You could throw them in the trunk of your car and drive them wherever you go, as long as you kept them healthy. They would probably be either hostages or specially cared for people in Bale and Ghost Wolf cults, they might be strong-armed or otherwise forced into being a part of a Forsaken or Pure territory. I imagine they would probably be targets of other supernaturals, perhaps beshilu, since a locus is a thin point between worlds, it would probably give them a leg-up on their wrecking the Gauntlet.

                                                            Originally posted by Ephsy View Post

                                                            TL;DR version?
                                                            Another tribe, the Drevlians, assassinated her husband and wanted her to marry their prince so they could take over the tribal federation.

                                                            Olga welcomed the delegates from that tribe and buried them alive.

                                                            She then sent a message back to the rest of the Drevlians saying she accepted the proposal. The prince and his retainers thus turned up. She invited them into a bath-house, then shut it and set it on fire.

                                                            She then invited a load of the Drevlians to a funeral feast to mourn her dead 'husband'. They got very drunk, and she managed to have 5000 of them slaughtered.

                                                            She *then* went to the Drevlians' city and besieged it. She offered them what seemed to be very merciful peace terms - her price was three sparrows and three pigeons from each household. They happily sent out cages and boxes full of the birds. She then had her soldiers attach slow-burning matches to the legs of the birds and let them all loose. They predictably all flew straight home to the city. The result was almost the entire place catching fire simultaneously, so there was no way to meaningfully firefight. The city burned and the survivors fled.

                                                            She then captured them and killed some, enslaved others and left the last portion alive to pay her tribute.

                                                            Olga did not do vengeance half-heartedly.

                                                            - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
