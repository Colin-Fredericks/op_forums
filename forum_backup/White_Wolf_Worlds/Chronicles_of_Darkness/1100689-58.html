    Originally posted by White Oak Dragon View Post

    I have no other suggestions to offer until they reveal the lines for the stretch goals, but of those that you've put forth here, Pirate Hunter, Western Front Promethean, or the Mummy options would be the ones that I'd find most interesting.
    So here are some expansions on those. I haven't written up the Arthurian one yet since we don't know the games, but suffice to say, Fate Arcana/Pacts/Pledgecrafting would be central to it. Bonus points for being a late Roman era, since the Romans always took their oaths super seriously.

    French Revolution (VtR/MtC/DtD)
    In this era, immortal, ancient, and inhuman beings stalk the streets driven by all too human conflict. Vampires and Mummies are both ancient, relying on old, conservative power structures... until they start to crumble. How do these undead immortals relate to each other? What is the relationship between the forces that sustain them, Sekhem and Vitae? In the upheaval of the Revolution, both Demons and the God-Machine take advantage of the chaos to alter and twist Infrastructure, but these alterations are not taken kindly by the geometrically-inclined Arisen. The God-Machine may be an ancient and unknowable horror, but so are the Judges and the Mummies themselves as they strike out from their fortress in Louvre palace. It is up to the Unchained whether they should trust this enemy of their enemy... or whether this is just more competition. Does the Aether that Infrastructure generates have any relation to the Lifeweb of the Mummies? Can Infrastructure influence the channeling of Heka? Do Demons stand to gain anything from Relic Architecture and the Tombs of Mummies?

    Great War (WtF/PtC/GtSE)
    Promethean walk the land and new lineage is born out of the gas attacks, starting at Ypres, but they are not the only Created to create Wastelands in no man's lands. The Ulgan across France, Belgium, and Germany can sense the turmoil that the spirits are in because of the war and urge every throng they cross to come to the aid of the Werewolves desperately trying to keep them in check, even if that means crossing into the Shadow. Meanwhile, Prometheans well on their pilgrimage feel stirrings from the Underworld, and feel compelled to journey physically to the Rivers of Death to investigate, even as Sin-Eaters do the same. As the Created journey farther afield into the supernatural realms, they find the Shadow and Underworld roiling in unexpected ways, and must learn how to interact with ghosts, spirits, and cthonians... and how their disquiet impacts each one.

    Seven Wonders (PtC/CtL/MtC)
    As Promethens, Mummies, and Changelings are all drawn towards the greatest monuments of their times, they all ask themselves how did they get here? Mummies say that it was Fate, somehow intoning it so solemnly, you know that the first letter is a capital. Changelings say that it was the Wyrd, and the stories-oathes bound within. Prometheans say it was the Principle, and that their Pilgrimage demands it. How do the Wyrd, the Principle, and Fate relate to each other? Are they reflections of one another viewed from different lenses, or are they separate forces working at cross-purposes? Does the power of Azoth relate at all to life-giving Sekhem? Could a mummy eat away at the Divine Fire, or will they be burned from within? And is there some fundamental connection between the Glamour that Changelings harvest and the Pillars that keep Mummies standing?

    1001 Nights (VtR/MtA/BtP)
    It is a time of fables, stories, and high adventure! It is also a time of science, discovery, and exploration! It is inevitable that the two would collide. In this age, Ibn Batuta travels to far off lands, bringing back stories of horrors and wonders from the edge of ten Muslim world. Ibn Sina, the great scientist in the East or West prior to the Renaissance, compiled the medical knowledge of China, India, Persia, and Greece using poetic verse. Vampires, Mages, and Beast all hunger for different things; blood, knowledge, and fear; but those goals overlap and interlock. Thyrusus and Moros Mages collaborate with Vampire scientists to study the properties Vitae and understand the Kindred's undead condition. Obrimos and Acanthus enjoy the Islamic Neoplatonic revitalization, applying the idea of ideal forms to stories, and working with Beasts to track the spread and changes of the 1001 Nights through the Astral Realms. From the Nizari Ismaili State the Mastigos Hassan-I-Sabah and Nizari Quhistani, watch as their people are slandered as drug addicts and hedonists, when really they are farmers and philosophers. With the Fatimids, Ayuubids, and Mongols pressing in on them, and Kindred infiltrating the Syrian stronghold Masayaf, they have better things to worry about than setting the record straight, allowing the Assassin Legends to take root.

    Pirates (MtA/HtV/GtSE)
    Pirates! What's worse than pirates? Monster pirates. The British started this whole mess with their privateers. Our Spanish compact will crush any pirate or privateer they meet on the high seas root out the heretics among them. The Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb commanded us to rid the Indian Ocean of this plague of thieves before he died. If the pirates traffic with ghostly jinn to avoid death, we will hunt them down again! Pirate witches come north from New York to hide their treasures and ghastly rites in Nova Scotia. We will see them tarred and feathered, hanging from the gallows! All of these self righteous Compacts are fools! Why do they think we built a Pirate Nation in Madagascar? Because society is corrupt with monsters. Our Conspiracy of monster hunting keeps us free. Look at the these other who say they hold the Vigil? The Council of Bones and the Lucifuge? The Ageis Kai Doru and the Mallius Malficarum? The Acheron Group? They all pervert themselves by using magic to hunt monsters. Witches can see the realms from which they draw their magic, and so turn it against them. On Madagascar, we made no such mistake.

    Right now I'm leading towards Mage 1001 Nights, but as always, I am open to suggestions, and may change my mind. Also, if other expansion backers are lurking here, you are welcome, nay encouraged, to steal what I've written for your own pitches.

                        Earlier you suggested Demon for Authur era, I'm curious as to why, it would be an unexpected choice?
                        I'd imagine it would have something to do with the stories of Merlin being half-demon. Or else examining the complicated situation that Christianity is in immediately after the Romans leave, what with the Pelagian heresy, and what Demons think about a doctrine that says everyone is capable of making the choice between good and evil, and whether they see any reflection how they live. Plus strange Cryptids like long-necked leopards with a stomach full of baying dogs (or maybe it just projects that noise from speakers on its stomach as a form of camouflage, or as a lure for those lost in the woods, or as a way of mimicking the local wildlife). And the strange gadget that the king's sword is sheathed in, one that seems to have temporal control over injuries and resets battles until the wielder finds a way to win. And that one piece of Infrastructure that connects the fertility of the surrounding area with the physical well-being of a specific Stigmatic, and how a recent battle injury threatens to lead to the starvation of thousands.

                        Admittedly, most of those examples would work just as well in Changeling: Merlin as potentially a child of the Gentry or an enterprising Changeling himself, Changelings turning to Christianity to try to understand their suffering (and just look at poems like The Dream of the Rood for an interesting British conception of Christ-as-warrior and active participant in his crucifixion), the Questing Beast as a Hedge-beast, Excalibur and its scabbard as artifacts from Arcadia, and the Fisher King as having screwed up a certain Goblin Contract horribly, requiring the PCs to find a loophole to mend the land or else some way of mending his uncurable wound.

                                Arthurian Britannia (MtA/CtL/DtD)
                                Merlin always said that Oaths were important. Many say that he was a fae poet. Others claim he was a mighty sorcerer. Some even whisper he was the son of the Devil himself. Whatever the truth may be, the Romans agreed that Oaths are important, but now Rome is leaving the shores, leaving us to the Angles and the Saxons. Faeries and Demons lurk in the dark alleys and forests, weaving pledges and pacts while buying and selling fates in Goblin Markets. Mages cultivate Proximi dynasties, while Demons seek aid from their Offspring. Neither realize that these Children of the Round Table are actually one and the same, and they have become very adept at pulling the strings of their erstwhile minders. How are the Offspring able to hide from the Mage's Supernal Sight? And what do they see when they look at Demons or Changelings? The Fetches that the Gentry have left behind are forced to looks at Demons and ask: are these monsters more or less human than I am? And can any of these creatures contend with the inexorable spread of the God-Machine and it's angels as they work to build their own personal Camelot?
