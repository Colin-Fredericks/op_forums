    Fury: The Scourge

    06-10-2015, 01:24 AM
    So, I've had a few people over the years ask, "What about the World of Darkness games that get pitched but never happen? What are they like?"

    Over the years, there have been tons of them. But usually, they just kinda get canned, or repurposed and pitched again, or folded into another game's conceptual space.

    This is a pitch for a game I made a couple of years ago, called Fury: The Scourge. As it stands, it's not a fully playable game. But since I get asked sometimes, I figured, "hey, why not?"

    Anyway, here's some thoughts on it, and a doc link.
