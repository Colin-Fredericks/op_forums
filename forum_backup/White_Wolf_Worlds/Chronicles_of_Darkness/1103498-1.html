            Originally posted by milo v3 View Post
            I am surprised you chose Persephone rather than Underworld Gods in general.
            yeah, it may look surprising at first glance- but I gave it a lot of thought on the subject, actually. At first I was about to choose Ereshkigal, but the more I've started to think about what a ghost truly is, the more I understood that a simple Underworld divinity is not going to work- ghosts are not the natural residents of the Underworld. They are created in the material plane and fall to the Great Bellow when Death claims them as its own. Ghosts are beings of regret, of what might have been if only they weren't killed- or if only they weren't "kidnapped" by Death. They want to go out of the Underworld, but they always must return to it. They are birthed by death, but they cling to life- and that vision of a desperate spirit clinging to life while with a foot in its grave immediately made me think about Persephone.

            In sort, the reason I've chose Persephone as the "patron of ghosts" is because she is not the standard underworld divinity. Other Underworld gods would be mentioned, of course, but they'll have to wait for Geist's post.
