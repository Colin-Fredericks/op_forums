                                                            Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
                                                            This is definitely interesting, and probably the first critique of Demon not being suitable for a &quot;traditional&quot; angels vs. demons game I've read that I can actually understand and agree with. The God-Machine has secret cults of followers, but it's not a genuine religion followed by ordinary people worldwide. Demons are not the amoral opposition to the God-Machine's dogmatic view of morality. I can see how the focus on secrecy and espionage over religion and faith could take the Demon out of Demon the Descent for some people. And unlike the technological aesthetics, that's something you can't easily remove from Descent without changing the game on a fundamental level. And it's something I hadn't even considered because it's such a given to me that any heaven vs. hell conflict in the Chronicles of Darkness would be a secret war between factions that are definitively not the Judeo-Christian versions of heaven and hell. The secrecy is what makes it a CofD game, to me.
                                                            And I actually like the fact that DtD is like this, as it makes the game playable for me.

                                                            It's also the reason why I consider Inferno to be a better “hook” for translating elements of DtF into the ChroD, despite being as just as different from DtF as DtD is: Inferno actually does concern itself with matters of human morality as a central theme. So if that's what you want out of a game about demons — for them to be involved in the sorts of concerns that religions are actually about — then Inferno is a much better pick than Descent is.
