    My group wants to make a Floating...

    01-31-2018, 01:37 AM
    Well, they are nearing adept and master levels for the first time and are exploring whats possible. Problem is I am not really sure myself whats possible.

    The setting is post apoc with zombies and other weird stuff like hunters funded by shadow governments and episode of the week esque monsters that are coming out of the woodwork. The bottom line here is tho, they want to find something big to make as a permanent but mobile base/sanctum/terminus.

    First idea was a cruise ship, would this fit into a small neighborhood as stated in the factor table?

    Next would be a legit island manipulated by matter and life. Could it really fly and float free somehow? Could you steer it like a ship?

    I guess I am asking for ideas, pros and cons, etc.

    If we must go with one example for questioning, a cruise ship seems to be their popular vote. Perhaps a portion of the big ship cleared out for soil and greens to mimic an island.

    I hope I haven't been to vague, figured its easier to ask for whats needed than provide everything they have available.

          Something floating is fine until a group of Sleepers look at it passing by overhead and then it falls out of the sky.

          Or if it's something that isn't obviously magical (a blimp/zeppelin whose fuel is provided by Forces for example) that's fine. Until a mage finds it, dispels it, and it falls out of the sky.

          If a mage doesn't find it until a shadow government uses one of their missiles to shoot an unrecognized aircraft, and it falls out of the sky.

          The cruise ship is probably the safer thing, unless you cloak the floating Sanctum as suggested above. Aside from it being fairly easy to maintain a small ecosystem on a cruise ship using good old nature, Life, Time, and Forces can easily make it big and sustainable regardless of time and weather conditions. Need food? Accelerate the growth of your plants from months to hours. Need a rainstorm? Control Weather, or use Life to ignore it entirely!

              Problem with a flying base is that I think it would exceed the Size 30 limit of the Advanced Scale table.
              And I don't think that the 'area of effect' can instead be made into a mobile thing for the purpose of affecting a target instead of using Size.

              Regardless, if you can push the scale higher than 30 (size 60 is a 'small apartment building') then that should function nicely.
              Create a gas-bladder full of sufficiently high buoyancy gases to keep you alight, mess with fundamental forces, remove its relationship with the concept of 'down'.
              There are plenty of ways to make an island float.

              If you do it complex enough, Dissonance shouldn't be a problem as the Withstand will be 5 or greater.
              There does still exist the problem of Quiescence and its effects upon the Wisdom of whoever cast the spell to keep it afloat.
              There's also the problem of dispelling. Without some 'fallen' source of lift, it's far too easy for someone to just dispel your spell, as the dispellation doesn't require the same spell factors as the casting spell.

              Meanwhile if it was maintained by some 'fallen', albeit mystically enabled source of lift, such as gigantic amounts of accumulated or enhanced helium/hydrogen. Then they'd either have to see the 'lift' mechanism to dispel the enhancement, or use some other incredibly large-scale effect such as a gravity manipulation effect to bring it down.

              So Pros: Flying bases are always a fun idea
              Cons: Too easy to just dispel, and catastrophic if it is.

                  Travel to the deepest darkest corner of the shadow. Find a behemoth sized flying whale spirit. Beat it into submission and drag it into the real world. Build your sky castle on top of it! Spirits don't give a damn about dissonance!
