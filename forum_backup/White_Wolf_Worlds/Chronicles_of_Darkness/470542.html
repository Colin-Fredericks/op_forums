                                              The Ka is a concept from ancient Egyptian religion. It is the counterpart to the Ba, and together they are the dual aspects that make up a person. In the broadest strokes, the Ba is the body and the Ka is the soul.

                                              The idea of the Hollow Mekhet is that their Ka and Ba were separated, leaving the Mekhet herself as an empty body, and the Ka as a disembodied spirit. Neither can exist without the other, so if either dies the other dies with it, but the Ka despises its Ba because it sees the Ba as having got the better end of the deal - the Ba can interact with the world physically, and live a more-or-less normal life, while the Ka is left as intangible, invisible, as a shadow with nothing casting it. This animosity causes the Ka to torment the Ba for revenge, but ultimately unless it becomes suicidal, it will protect the Ba if its life is threatened.

                                              This parallels perfectly with the Strix's feelings toward the Kindred, so I agree that it makes more sense to run the Ka as a modified Strix than as a modified ghost. If you want to tie in the idea of Strix as the Beast, maybe Hollow Mekhet don't frenzy? What if they make Frenzy checks normally, but when they fail, instead of going into Frenzy themselves, the Strix does? It would make the prospect a bit more appealing, as the original mechanic was almost all-downside (you are completely unable to communicate over the phone or any recorded media, and are constantly at risk of breaching the masquerade if anyone notices you don't have a shadow, reflection, or echo, and in exchange you get to be haunted by an evil spirit that looks just like you and hates your guts? What's not to love!) and gives the ST a good guideline for when to have the Ka's activities cause problems for the character.

                                                          Not quite the same, but in my games Red Jack (from Mythologies) is a particularly powerful and cunning wandering Ka. His "Ars Speculorum" powers compose a particular ritual that subsumes the ka of the individual taught into Jack, making that person into an additional anchor for him (a secret price in the deal most kindred making such deals are not aware of). Thanks to this Red Jack is considerably more mobile than most such beings, having managed to accrue dozens, possibly hundreds of anchors across the Earth. And while this might make him maybe the only one of its kind possibly capable of outliving its original "other half", Jack still feels an uncertainty bordering on paranoia about the subject, to the point of imprisoning the original hollow Mekhet in torpor in a "safe place" of his creation and choice, the Glass Mansion (Reliquary, pg. 60-63), the source of legends about Jack's "realm of mirrors" and another source of mobility for the mysterious being.
