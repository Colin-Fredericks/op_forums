            I can see appeal in changing Spirit to be less about spirits (as in, ephemeral individuals which require their own subsystem) and more about spiritual qualities and factors that can be manipulated like gravity or liquid's shape. When I proposed playing a Thyrsus to my players, the consensus seemed to be "Life is cool, but Spirit? With all these weird rules and things I need to pick up and read and understand and that are going to be literally something I'm only going to ever interact with? Too much effort and too big headache."
            Similar reason why our Moroses are Matter focused and don't like exploring Ghost subsystem, and low level Death doesn't offer much without "yay Twilight! yay Ghosts!".
            Imagine a world where Mind Arcanum still operates on Mind...but instead of working on it "directly", you have to constantly deal with goetias and astral creatures of various scale and purviews and their own subset of rules. The horror.
