                        03-01-2015, 08:54 AM
                        Originally posted by Dreaminggod View Post
                        So while we're waiting for Werewolf 2nd to be released for the juicy, juicy mechanics. Care to indulge (some?) of us at how these guys would interact with Vampires. I've always like to see the intersection of two predators hunting the same food source. However, this one has the juicy question of 'So, infected vampires, eh? Those gotta be pretty."
                        Oh wow, yes. Well, for starters I now want to do a Jiang Shi write-up-style of a group of vampires that are Lamprey Hosts gravitating towards the communal vampire state that the various Clans share.

                        Setting that aside, though, Christ, Ukusgualu and vampires interacting would be interesting. Obviously a fair amount depends on the relative balance of strength between the Lampreys and the Kindred in an area, but I think we can take certain things as a given. An example of how things might play out:

                        You're a vampire. You've been reborn into undeath, a walking cadaver sustained by the blood and the Beast. You've become part of the All-Night Society, the community of vampires in your city, and are enmeshed in their power plays, their politics and their mysteries. One day, someone starts 'poaching' on your feeding grounds. People are being fed off, and it's both taking from your meal and threatening problems from investigations - the vampires, whoever they are, are a mix of clean and messy feeders. Some are leaving little more than pinpricks on their victims, but others leave messy throat scars - if they even feed from the throat rather than another body part. Sometimes the wounds are becoming properly infected. The victims seem to be delirious afterwards, at least, and have little memory of what happened to them, but their poor health and the sickness that plagues them is screwing with your herd. You pull some strings; whoever this is hasn't cleared their depredations with the high-ups, and none of the Covenants are willing to take the blame. If you're lucky, maybe you have access to some allies with lore and you do some research. You wonder whether a rogue bloodline has taken your turf as a playground. A few worrying names start to bubble up amongst those who learn of what's going on - some wonder if the Morbus are at work.

                        You corner one of the intruders in a back-alley where they've lured a hapless victim to. In you swagger, all ready to show them who's boss, and it all goes wrong immediately. They aren't normal Kindred; your Beast flinches back from the revulsive sense of other you get from the man-shaped horror. It opens its mouth and there are far too many fangs in there. Still, the thing isn't particularly strong, and you and your allies tear it apart. It erupts in a tidal wave of squirming eel-things that go writhing away into the shadows, leaving just a hollowed out pile of wet skin and bloody bones. Someone gets bitten by one of them in the chaos. It's disgusting, but one thing is clear - these Kindred are something completely unlike any of the Clans or Bloodlines you've heard of.

                        Things get worse. More of the Lampreys start appearing and feeding. They stage an ambush; a vampire you know is grabbed and never seen again. The south half of what is supposed to be your territory is becoming a no-go zone because it's near the river, and everyone is terrified that's where the infection has started - some sort of blood-drinking lampreys in the water. If you go there, the smell of blood is always in your face, maddening and tantalising. Sometimes you can see it just dripping out of no-where in corners, here and there. Your ghoul, hungry on a bad day, tried some of the blood. Now she's gone rogue and started some sort of bizarre cult amongst the riverside humans. Now she is leading them in trying to kidnap vampires she knows of so that the cult can feed from them. She's tried targeting you, but you've moved your haven to somewhere high, somewhere dry, somewhere with a lot of padlocks and where there are no vents or drains that the lampreys can get through.

                        The rest of the city can't ignore the Lamprey Bloodline any more. They've gone from a bizarre and horrifying curiosity to a real threat, because other Kindred's territories butt onto the river too. They regularly attack vampires they encounter now, but they aren't killing them - Kindred are drunk from and then set free. High-rankers in the Covenants are forced to do something by the clamouring lower rankers. Organised attacks are launched; it's time to go into the docklands and clear these things out. Ghouls are to go in during the day; the Kindred will then move in at night.

                        When nightfall comes, you get to hear the garbled reports from the surviving ghouls. Most of the Lampreys can walk under the sun and need not sleep. They may prefer the night, but they are by no means bound to it. Attacks on Lamprey 'nests' have had mixed success, with heavy casualties on both sides; a blood-drunk ghoul is a good match for a bag of skin and bones stuffed with lampreys, but some of the monsters are showing new and disturbing capabilities, and their bodies have started to warp and mutate further. Some ghouls just disappeared. Others never came back but left jumbled messages talking about the blood between worlds, and that they must swim the divide.

                        The Kindred taking part in the attack go in. It's as bad as you fear, and the blood, the blood everywhere. The river water seems to stink of it - a human would barely notice it but your Beast growls with ravenous hunger. Pressed and on the back foot, the Lampreys fight hard; they're no match for angry vampires, but it's not just them that you're facing now. The lampreys themselves come squirming out of pipes, out of vents, out of drains, biting into panicking Kindred who tear them off and stamp the things to jelly underfoot. Some start to scream and claw at their own skin as bloody pock-marks appear, shouting about unseen leeches clamping onto them that they cannot get off. One Kindred sends himself to Final Death in a frenzy of self-mutilation. You feel something clamp onto your back, feel an oozing wound, but there's nothing there. More of the Kindred start to frenzy as some otherworldly presence drinks deep of their Vitae.

                        Then the Lamprey elders appear. Slick-skinned worm-men, their bodies androgynous, their faces writhing circles of teeth and hunger, and they're fast. These things are a match for the Kindred who still stand, their spindly limbs possessing immense strength. Some spray blinding gouts of ichorous blood from their mouths; others literally fall apart into pools of bloody water, only to reform elsewhere. Something even bigger wallows beneath the warehouse floor; a massive lamprey-thing that smashes its way upwards, the progenitor of these horrors perhaps. Its immense bulk alone is enough to flatten one hapless lick.

                        Carnage and blood and lampreys everywhere. Even these elders erupt into writhing eels when they're killed. And despite the horrors, you are winning. Whatever twisted power of the blood these things possess, you have your own strengths and your own elders. Powerful old Kindred feel threatened enough by what is happening to join the fray.

                        You win. You win.

                        The ghouls set several buildings on fire as the Kindred retreat, and the news that morning is rife with stories of gangland warfare and arson.

                        And then it gets worse.

                        You've won, but it'll take time to rebuild; the blood-mad humans and your rogue ghoul are still out there, plus any survivors of the Lamprey Bloodline. You get together with other Kindred of your level of society who share riverside territory, and start to plan a systematic method of clearing up - the high-ups have thrown the responsibility down on the shoulders of you Ancillae. Half-way through the meeting, one of the Kindred starts to cough and gag. He vomits. Amidst the blood on the table writhes a lamprey.

                        You all stare.

                        He's one of those who was bitten in the struggle. Paranoia explodes around that table and quickly spreads further on the medium of frantic phonecalls. Suddenly everyone who was bitten is a suspect, but no-one's quite sure of what. Are they infected somehow? Disease carriers? What happens next?

                        The knives come out. High-ups have bitten vampires isolated where possible. Some escape the round-up; some conceal the fact they were bitten; some are high-ups, and who is going to try and incarcerate one of the elders? The Ordo are eager to get their hands on subjects to test and observe.

                        The next night, you meet another Ancillae in your territory near the waterfront. He shouldn't be hear. You challenge him angrily, and he opens his mouth, and it is full of lampreys.
                        * * *

                        In the end, a frail accord is brought about. Only some of the infected Kindred are turned into Lampreys over the following days, and no-one can understand the whys and wherefores of who ends up hollowed out and who doesn't. Once a bloody purge is done, the Lampreys surprise everyone by making contact through a ghoul of some sort. The water-vampires lay out the obvious; this struggle has been pricy for both sides, but the Kindred have evidently failed to defeat them, and further attacks will simply cost more Kindred with the same result. Equally, the Lampreys are apparently only interested in the riverside territories. They apologise - apologise! - for the violence and attacks, calling them a 'misunderstanding', but now that they understand the Kindred they feel there is no need for further struggle. If the Kindred wisely cede the riverside territories to the Lampreys, the Lampreys will co-exist in peace, and will even send representatives to the Prince's court in deference to Kindred authority to shoulder the responsibility for what happens along the river.

                        With bile in their throats, the Kindred agree. No-one is sure how the Lampreys infect Kindred after a bite, but one of the elders who was bitten has disappeared and there are once again numerous men and women packed full of Lampreys on the waterfront Rack, as if the whole battle never happened. The vampires will need time to understand these things if any further clash will be anything other than futile. Too many Kindred have met Final Death already, and there's no guarantee the remaining vampires can win a fight against an enemy they don't seem able to actually kill permanently. Over the enraged objections of those who have riverside territories, the accord is signed.

                        The Kindred loathe them still, of course, even as they start to weave the Lampreys into their schemes. Everyone looks on the two pallid representatives to the Prince's court with disgust, watches in horror at the shivering and writhing beneath their skin, but they still talk to them. The Lampreys, it seems, have their own internal struggles, their own agendas and factions. They seem to want to eat each other far more than they want to kill Kindred. Things settle down over time; theoretically everyone's supposed to be figuring out the weaknesses of the Lampreys but the Horrible New becomes the Status Quo. The Ordo Dracul are rumoured to have captured Lampreys and dissected them behind closed doors. The Crone are rumoured to have cosied up to the fish-vampires, and there are even scurrilous whispers that some have voluntarily become infested with them in bloody old rites that the Circle guards jealously. A few angry Kindred who have lost their territories try to move againt the Lampreys; they're sometimes seen later, mad-eyed and blood-hungry, their bodies bulging with squirming parasites, and soon burn themselves out in ravenous frenzy. At least one Kindred, bitten in a scuffle by a Lamprey over a particular bit of territory border, decides to walk into the sunrise rather than... whatever it is that happens next to the bitten.

                        No-one talks about the blood-cults around the river, or the way everything seems to taste and smell of blood to the Kindred. As long as the Lampreys keep control of it, as long as they keep everything nice and orderly, Kindred would rather not have to think about it. And that's what the Lampreys do, for a while.

                        Then, years later, a new Lamprey representative turns up at the court. It has a petition for new territory, away from the river, in an area of the city that used to be a marsh. There are strong hints that if the petition is refused, the Lampreys will take it by force. And now there are new reports of vampire feeding grounds near the Lampreys' existing turf; that same bloody touch to the very air itself has spread. Blood is starting to trickle out of no-where, and things wallow in the sewers. The Lampreys are testing the will of the Kindred, seeing how far they can push them for more concessions.

                        No-one is sure what happens next, not even the Lampreys.
                        * * *

                        And then there's you.

                        You spent the night in the bath, your flesh carefully sliced open, blood everywhere, as you pulled the writhing worm-fish from your flesh. Mad with the pain and the gore, you made sure every last lamprey was torn apart before you smashed the bath itself to pieces and spent 'til morning screaming til your throat was raw and bloody.

                        You've drunk the blood that pools where the Lampreys lair. You've walked like a shadow amongst them and, while the parasites know someone is there the Disciplines of your blood have kept you just a hair's breadth ahead of them so far. You've heard the whispers that the humans unwillingly mutter amongst themselves when they studiously ignore a drop of blood on a windowpane, and the incomprehending chants of cultists clustered round a pyramid of wet brass and bone in a basement.

                        You've started to see the deeper well of blood everywhere; the shore of red that washes up against reality, with lampreys sporting and squirming in its shallows. You've seen people with lampreys clustered across their skin, drinking deep, even though they can't see the parasites themselves because the horrors are half-submerged in the sea of blood. You've heard the crashing roar of the waves, and beneath that something else, the heartbeat of something vast and great that wants to reach out, to speak to you.

                        You have a plan. If you can catch one of the elders, the ghastly man-lamprey hybrids, when it is weak or vulnerable or utterly unwary, and snare it - if rather than 'killing' it and letting it turn into a thousand lampreys, you can drink down its blood, all of it, to the last drop, and truly diablerise it...

                        ... Then perhaps you too can step onto that shore, and swim in the ocean of blood. Perhaps from the secrets in the Vitae of the lamprey, you can find the gateway to that paradise-nightmare, and forever sport amidst the crimson waves and rejoice and laugh and kill. And perhaps you can hear the whispers of what lies beneath, the vast and ancient heartbeat of that ocean.

                        First, you must hunt, and kill, and drink.

                        - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
