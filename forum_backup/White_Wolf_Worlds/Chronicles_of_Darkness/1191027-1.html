                            Not responding to anyone in particular and not defending the actions of the player or the PC, more trying to explain a bit of the circumstance.

                            Some of the reasons the fight started (short on why it escalated is in the above post) was because the Seer, knowing that the PC was a sleepwalker but not yet what kind and wanted to expose him and embarrass him in front of the other guests and clients of the club in where the PC is a host/bouncer. The Seer showed up before they opened, demanded that they opened for him and his entourage, bribing them, ordered a lot of expensive drinks and spoiled them (vaskade) down the drain https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=Vaska , threw shit around, groped the waitresses and coworkers to the PC, over-all insulted people and showered bystanders with champagne among and bribed the police to leave them alone among other things. So in short, not really knowing in-charachter how Vigor yet worked (really new ghoul, and a regnant not really that informative on the whole vampire-business things yet) and lot's of 10-again got what would be just a fuck you punch in the back into almost killing the "poor" seer.

                            As for the relations of the city's vamps and mages and such, as above again.
                            Let's start, the domain in ruled by a carthian senate, that makes the laws and appoint the prince for a five year mandate to uphold the law and secure the food supply, e.g. seeing that vamps are allowed their feeding grounds and such, the people vote on most appointments of other officers for the domain and the ghouls are emancipated and giving votes in the elections. In that regard the ghouls are not only considered property but as a lower class of citizens and people in their own right among their fellow kindred citizens. However, if a crime happens by the hand of a ghoul, the regnant is considered a co-conspirator of the crime, but only if the regnant was informed of what happened (although unbeknownst to the PCs and the players) .

                            The mages in the domain has reconstructed a new Consilium in the last decades after a long war between the mages and forsaken of the area, both splats was almost extinct in the area before the archmaster in the city intervened and put up a large protection spell around the city from the werewolves, creating a barrier in the shadow that can't be crossed and any werewolf that changes inside or enters the shadow is in all simplicity killed by the spell. This has created a new sub-ecosystem of the local shadow almost fully encapsulated from the rest which is the single one mystery of the new conslium to explore, in where they now have laws to be really careful around the shadow and the now gone archmasters protective spells. Given the last decades of almost open warfare and after with the spirits of the city living in their own ecosystem, there is a lot, a lot, of occult phenomena in the city so the guardians are really keen on trying to keep the lid on the occult, in that the sought out allies in the city, lowering their weapons and making an unholy alliance with the seer pylon and local kindred to keep the normies as unknowing of the darkness as could be.
                            The mages though consider themselves the ultimate authority among inferiors and have excessive use of closed tribunals for their legislative system, passing the leadership around every few years to avoid tyranny.
                            Most of the alliance is about the "nocturnal states" to help each others enforce public knowledge and opinion away from the occult and breeches of the veil/masquerade and to stay out of each others business. Although a fair bit of information-trading and schemes and plots roll over to each other on an individual basis, most they stay out of sight from each other. There are ambassadors that try to mediate and come to understandings in conflict interests between the parts.
