    Best Twists

    05-17-2016, 11:25 PM
    Okay, so we all know what a twist is, sometimes its done well (Fight Club), sometimes its done horribly (The Village). What I'd love to hear is the best twists you've either had inflicted on you in a game, or the best ones you've managed to inflict on your players. Now, to get the ball rolling, I'll mention the best one I've inflicted on my players recently.

    So, we're playing Mage the Awakening and Mage C is the champ of this tale. So, Mage C is in a police station and attempts to cast a spell to gain access to a police computer. However, despite there only being a chance die for the paradox, it ends up with several successes and he cops a lot of damage soaking it into his pattern, so much so, that he starts bleeding and collapses. At this point, the police within the station notice him and call an ambulance. The ambulance arrives and Mage C is backed into the back and he is soon heading for the hospital. Not wanting to have to produce fake ID, he decides to use some spells to get himself out of the ambulance. Low on mana, he does some improvised casting to try and escape the ambulance and with two paradox dice, I manage to get eight successes. Seeing that this will kill him if he takes it into his pattern, Mage C decides to let the paradox resolve. There's a bizarre sound as unreality rips into the world and an Abyssal quickly appears within the ambulance. At this point, the ambulance EMT becomes possessed, eyes filling with blackness and his mouth drooling blood and oily mess as the abyssal possessing the EMT thanks Mage C for releasing it. It then pats his cheek and disappears.

    Over the next few months tracking down this stubbornly persistent abyssal becomes a central plot to the chronicle as it begins committing ritualistic murders to fuel its ability to stay in this world. Multiple times players confront it, but each time they end up retreating, typically after an NPC or two of note go down. Then, after four or five months of RL playtime, they finally discover the truth when they visit the Onieros of the player who originally paradoxed in an attempt to review his memories of its original summoning. All along, the abyssal has been hiding in the Onieros of Mage C and possessing him to carry out its plan.

    At this point, the players all go "No way!". However, over the next twenty minutes of discussion, they realise Mage C's player has never been in the same scene as the Abyssal. The Abyssal did always seem to know what they were going to do, but not if Mage C wasn't involved. After their "holy shit" discussion, I was congratulated on a damn good game. Was a really happy moment that none of them saw it coming OOC

    Anyway, that's the best twist I've pulled off recently, but I'd love to hear some of yours.

          This one is really good. I was running a crossover chronicle in cyberpunk future of Tokyo. Changeling, werewolf and mage were fighting against an evil corporation EgzoGen. Corporation was trying to obtain a very powerful artifact of unknown purpose called Vector Omega. Heroes put tremendous effort in capturing the Artifact and forging a fake one to cheat EgzoGen. Mage working for Mysterium, decided to hide Vector Omega in superstrong “Vault III” protected with hundred spells, wards and many guardian mages. Vault III has always been used to store potentially dangerous artifacts from all over the world. Everything seemed ok so far…
          Heroes then learned that EgozGen already have an artifact called Vector Alpha, what surprised them. But still they didn’t know true purpose of either of Vectors besides that they’re both powerful. Finally a werewolf managed to sneak into EgzoGen and find a privet chamber of a fate mage working for corporation. Seeing ropes hanging all over the place connecting people, things and places – he finally realized the terrible truth… Vector Alpha and Vector Omega were designed by Egzogen itself to create a specetime wormhole between them! And the portal has just opened.
          You can imagine the face of Mysterium Mage realizing that in this very moment someone has direct access to the most forbidden place of the Order, and to hundreds of dangerous artifacts
          I didn’t know if mage will use Vault III, but I knew this is one of his favourite stuff and I hoped he will.

                  Oh man, things like this are absolute crack to me.

                  I love Kristoff's twist, very appropriate and also one of those things where it's almost like you can see the signs of the twist way before they happen but the signs only now make sense. Just gold to me.

                  No for mine, I have a lot of them so I'll try and keep them short and separate the stories that the twists were in with three asterisks.

                  ***
                  (massive crossover but mostly mage)
                  In the very first game I ran: Circle of 5, the player characters where humans who were chosen to become the the avatars and holders of five pieces of the supreme supernal entity that rules all of the supernal realm. He abducted their souls as children to the Supernal, trained them in their mission and then sent them back to their bodies in the fallen realm with his own soul split in 5 pieces (one piece (each embodying two arcana) for each) conjoined to theirs. The mission: to eradicate the approaching Abyss in the Fallen Realm which is soon to flood through and destroy all.

                  The problem is: They don't remember anything other than what they are (avatars of the entity) and that they have a mission to eradicate the Abyss from the Fallen Realm. However they get dreams every so often that act as clues to how to do the task. It also gives them more power (crap tons of spells as rotes) as the entities memories and knowledge bleeds through to them.

                  Eventually they start gathering individuals from other lines that are connected some how and want to help. They also eventually learn that there's an artifact (that they know are called pillars) that are associated with one of the arcana (so 10 in total). The avatar that has that aracana's soul is drawn to that artifact and has a very strong urge to associate that artifact with one of their companions that they've gathered. When they do this, they learn every spell in that arcana as rote and jump in power. The problem is, the action to associate the companion with the artifact also seems to sacrifice some part of the companion for the power they receive.

                  So the twist was that the goal was to pour all of their arcana into the associated "pillar" there by strengthening the pillar with the companion finding new purpose as it's "Guardian". Sacrificing the Guardian to draw power from the Pillar essentually crumbled it and corrupted the mind of the avatar causing them to turn against the others after both their pillars crumbled. I did let the players that did the action to sacrifice the first pillar that they get to now be somewhat antagonistic and have a strong draw to grab more power, but the surprise (and the point where it "clicked") for the players that hadn't gone off the deep end was very satisfying and lept the story into the final phase.
                  ***

                  (Demon)
                  So this occurred over two stories but i'll only address the events that contributed to the twist.

                  One of the PCs, Moeshe, used to be a part of a group of very old, near Exiled Rank 5 Angels before falling. One of the four: Vesuvius has basically always hated Moeshe. So when Moeshe becomes hunted, the Destroyer angel Vesuvius took up the job. Somewhat long story short (it's a humorous and thrilling tale but not essential to the twist), Vesuvius destroys Moeshe.

                  Understandably, most in the Ring are filled with HATE for Vesuvius and upon inspection of the death site they find Moeshe's body without the head and the mechanical remains of what looks like the evidence of the falling of an angel. likely Vesuvius.

                  Fast Forward almost a year and the ring has been through a lot. They've continued they're on and off war with a swarm of cryptid bugs that are only getting smarter and the ring leader is making leaps and bounds towards its goal to be more and more human (one of aspirations was to find a fellow student that will go on investigations with her which she does after meeting a student who runs the school paper at an apartment complex that she was investigating) and one of the demon's in the ring has secured a safer cover with which to raise his child that he became a Demon for and has obsessively protected since.

                  The Ring start to (separately) get wind of the following information:
                  - There is a fallen Fire demon that is hunting them out personally (it's easy to guess who.
                  - He's taken one of moeshe's old (friendly) angel friends.
                  - There is a Demon that has created it's own infrastructure somewhere.

                  Half the ring go to the the probable nest of the insects, half of that squad stay to contain the insects the other half follow a tunnel made by them to what looks to be a hollowed out apartment with a figure with molten veins in the middle of the room with wires hooked to it connecting it to the metallic head of Moeshe. They hear Vesuvius's voice apparently communicating with the bugs. Using a power to link their minds, one of the players finds that it is hooked up to the memories of Moeshe's head with information about the ring streaming into its head. So they destroy it and take both heads back to the meeting point.

                  It's about this time that the Ring Leader's human friend comes out as a newly fallen Demon who, now knowing that the girl she met is a Demon want nothing to do with her as she just wants to hide. But the Ring's reckless actions continue to prevent her from finding a safer avenue to hide and stay safe then to go the same way. She is understandably pissed that she's along for the ride and the ring keep her on menial tasks cause she's basically useless. And the ring leader has gone home to try becoming totally human for a night (I forgot the power). She's met with a bunch of the insects that almost immolate her to death (with her vowing to never make herself that vulnerable again).

                  So they meet back at the meeting point with the heads being revealed. Moeshe 2.0 (long story), with horror reveals that that is not Vesuvius but his Sister that was taken by Vesuvius (mentioned earlier) and was likely being used as a way to convert moeshe's head data to useful memories to learn about the ring with. Realizing that the first target will likely be the ring leader they rush to her house stuffing the useless demon in a bolthole with the daughter of one of the members. They do save the ring leader and go back to the meeting spot. during the discussion the daughter fizzles out of existence making everyone (especially the parent) panic) when she comes back the ring leader uses her mind meld power on the daughter only to find Vesuvius who attacks them and escapes (with the useless demon fizzling out of existence.)

                  with horror and anger the Ring realize what has happened. The "Useless" demon was found at the same apartment that they later found the makeshift infrastructure. They also realize that they put the useless demon in a bolt hole alone with on of the PC's daughter and that she has been gone since their decision to put them both in the bolt hole.
                  ***

                  Part of what made the Demon twist so good, was because it was due to a decision that the characters made recently, like a twist of a knife.

                  tl;dr twists:
                  - Mage: The characters were sacrificing the pillars for power when they should have been sacrificing their power for the pillars.

                  - Demon: (3 twists)
                  - The "demon" they killed was the used up Angel husk of a former friend.
                  - The antagonist was the new friend (obtained through fulfillment of an aspiration)
                  - The daughter was also the antagonist due to PC actions.


                  Frequent Story Teller for the Circle of Five gaming group.
