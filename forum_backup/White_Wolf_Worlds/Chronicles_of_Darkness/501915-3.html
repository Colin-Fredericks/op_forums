    Originally posted by Griautis View Post
    You're looking at this from strictly mechanical perspective. Ethical reasons? How is it ethical to be attacking the individual right up front?
    Feeding doesn't necessitate attacking your food. There's violent and non-violent feeding in VtR for a reason. Taking blood from someone doesn't necessitate harm, either. A vampire can sip one or two Vitae worth of blood without any real adverse side-effects. Chronic feeding from the same human may have deleterious long-term side-effects, but you can have a little fling with a human and nibble and then leave it at that with no measurable physical harm done. It's like if you cut yourself with a knife while cooking. You can lose a lot of blood, but you'll probably only need stitches, not a blood transfusion. And the vampire takes care of the stitches for you.

    Vampires are parasites, but I'm pretty convinced you can feed ethically, particularly with a willing herd, which implies informed consent. I don't really think you can ethically steal from a blood bank, and finding a way to do it legit would be far more effort than it's worth nutritionally to a vampire.

    Of course, just because a vampire can feed ethically doesn't mean most can or will, but this little tangent started with the morality of drinking cold blood, so that's what I'm presenting my views on.
