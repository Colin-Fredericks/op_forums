      They come from a dark place that nobody ever seems to return from, steal life and corrupt other things into things that steal life, and are seemingly attracted to things going badly wrong, all of which make them fairly easily associated with other probable incursions from the Lower Depths, like the servants of the Maeljin (which 1e's book on the Inferno, the original primary Lower Depth of the setting, identifies as close enough to its sort of demons for that book's purposes) or the Cthonic Gods ("the Underworld is provably the worst-damaged of the Fallen Worlds" is a thing Dave Brookshaw has observed) or the Judges of Duat (which drain the manifest energy of magical relics through their proxies and were only too ready to feed a tenth of the world to an oblivion-goddess).

      Wherever they came from originally, modern Strix are native to the material plane and possibly have many different origins blended into their pedigree through diablerie and magical mitosis.

          Mage gets its a bit into the metaphysics of the Lower Depths, but it’s obviously not relevant from a VTR perspective. General rule of thumb is any lower depth realm lacks some aspect of reality, and the beings from that realm hunger for it. Strix seem to hunger for life. If you’re familiar with the platonic cosmology of Mage, the Lower Depths are thought of as being as far beneath the Phenomenal World as the Phenomenal World is beneath the Supernal, defined by the absences of certain world defining truths.

                  This is an interesting connection that I'd never noticed before.

                  Plato mentions shadows and poetry as among the least real things. So we're talking about things that are not ontologically grounded -- things whose existence depends on other things (think of reflections, illusions, or other epiphenomena). The Strix are made of smoke and shadow, which is exactly the kind of transient byproduct that Plato would have identified as being "least real".

                  EDIT: technically smoke has its own existence, but whatever, technicalities are for nerds and philosophy majors.

                          Rose's book Hijack lists one possible origin that was used by the devs, but it's CofD, there's No True Canon.

                          The Strix might always have existed, long before humanity did, but humans were the ones who first pushed the shadows back by taming fire and building houses with thresholds and running water, so they hate humanity in particular.

                          According to Ovid (a Roman poet and dubiously-accurate mythographer), they might have once been humans, who tried to achieve immortality through magic. If that's the case, in the CofD-verse, their attempt succeeded at a horrific cost, leaving them with no trace of their former humanity except a lingering hatred for what they've lost and can never have again.

                          They might be ephemerals from another world, who ended up non-ephemeral in our world (cf Hedge Ghosts) and found that they can use human life-force like Essence to keep themselves here.

                          They might be exactly the same thing as the Beast is, just with nothing holding it back: the remains of draugar whose souls survived the destruction of their bodies.

                              Originally posted by Nightsyko View Post
                              Interesting a high level game in the world of Dis could be fun to run.
                              Here's an idea:

                              The local Ordo Dracul trapped a bunch of Strix in jars, wired them up together, and connected them to a full length obsidian mirror in a specially chosen Wyrm's Nest. They perform a ritual which harness the Strix to turn the mirror into a gateway to Dis. They send ghouls, humans, neonates, animals, robots, and anyone and anything they can through to explore. The subjects are mounted with cameras and tethered with a cable that can be used to drag them back and sends telemetry. Subjects usually don't get very far before being killed or possessed by Strix, but that's fine; the experiment keeps burning through the Strix in the jars and replacements are always welcome.

                              The Prince doesn't know what they are up to, but they dispose of problematic kindred and kine for him and in exchange he doesn't ask where they are going. However they are running out of subjects faster than he supplies them, and have started abducting subjects that are the least likely to be noticed. What happens if they accidentally take someone with powerful connections?

                              Despite the Ordo Dracul's best efforts to shield the project, other Strix have started to sense something happening; more and more Strix are being spotted in the neighborhood where the Wyrm's Nest is located. They aren't attacking yet, just searching. What will happen if they find the gateway?
