                                Reptilian Conspiracies -
                                Structures of Power
                                If an Oroboros defines their approach by their Method, and their belief by their philosophy, how do they define their interaction with the world at large? Often, Oroboroi who have come into their own will end up joining Reptilian Conspiracies, large networks of Oroboroi which work to influence the world on a global scale. Of these, five are truly major players on the world stage. In chronological order of their formation, they are:

                                The Dominion
                                The oldest conspiracy in history, the Dominion traces its structure back to the Dragons themselves. Their goal is simple: to impose order upon humanity as the Dragons once did. The Dominion is known for being cold, calculating, and ruthless in its goal to control the world governments, and its leaders often see all other ends as futile: a world without the Dominion is a world in chaos. Individual Dominionists range from bright eyed young drakes looking to make a positive impact in the world, to corrupt power-mongers looking to lord over humanity with an iron talon...

                                The Collapsers
                                Ever since the final days of the Dominion, the Collapsers have been a thorn in the side of anyone who wants to control large groups of people. Despite not having the structure for any real historical records, they claim to trace their ideological roots back to the first Oroboroi who caused the collapse. The Collapsers are a network of libertines, minarchists, and outright anarchists who form cells of rebellion, and work tirelessly prevent hegemonies like the Dominion from forming again, no matter who’s in charge. In the eyes of a Collapser, it is better to struggle in chaos than to live under the tyranny of another’s will...

                                The Naga
                                Founded by an oroboros named Kadru in the chaos after the collapse, the Naga are as mystical and mysterious as they are ancient. The exact goals and workings of their leadership are shrouded in mystery, but their stated goal is to return the harmony between the Dreamtide and the Waking which was lost as the Dominion fell apart. They believe that, if they can bring peace to the World of Darkness, that they can end the cycle of violence which plagues it on all levels. How they go about this is a matter of debate however, as the machinations are often complex and magical, and sometimes involve violence without much explanation other than “it was what the cycle needed.” Naga leaders tend to be mystics, sorcerers, and spiritualists, but will welcome any being who wishes to bring peace to the cycle, and even count non-Oroboroi among their ranks...

                                The Order of St George
                                The Order of St. George is unique in many ways. For one, the most famous deed of their titular founder, a Roman Legionary of the pre-catholic era, who became an Oroboroi after slaying a mad plaguebearing dragon at the behest of his remorseful peer. This founding act has echoed throughout their history; the Order is a conspiracy of draconic dragonslayers, dedicated to protecting the world from the excesses of their kin, in particular Religious institutions who would be perverted into mockeries by literal Serpents of Eden. While they are the least numerous conspiracy, they more than make up for their low numbers in both zeal and their connections to the human world; the ideal Oroboroi of St. George is a general who leads from the front, eager to suffer and die for the sake of her men and her cause, accepting any personal loss to see that justice is done. Their membership ranges from young warriors filled with idealistic fervor to guilt-ridden ancients who have seen and done too much to do anything but atone. They will save the world, or die trying-and there is few more unpredictable things than a would-be hero with godlike powers...

                                The Delvers
                                It’s hard to say when the group now known as the Delvers first formed, as they have records of groups meeting to discuss science and philosophy dating back to ancient Greece, and a structure dating back to record-keepers in the fall of the Roman Empire, but the group didn’t rise to prominence until the mass-discovery of the ancient records of the Dragons during the Renaissance. Soon after that though, the Delver’s discoveries lead them to become a player on par with the other 4 conspiracies, and today they are an occult information network which spans the globe. The Delvers have been known to consort with many otherworldly beings, including Fae, Mages, Spirits, and especially the Deep Ones, and believe that only through knowledge can they achieve their many goals. They have a sort of hierarchy system, in which new initiates must prove themselves to be taught the secrets which the higher members know, and considering who their leaders are talking to...

                                                    Some sort of sleeping pill that gives you vivid dreams and loosens your supernatural blocks so you can dreamwalk, I'd guess... That sort of detail isn't important.

                                                    When developing a new group, or any sort of content for one of these, you need to have multiple things in mind that you think over.

                                                    On top of anything else, you need to be thinking of it from a meta perspective: how is the Storyteller going to use this? What power level and kind of threat should you make these guys? Do they have a special mechanic? Do they have a special plot meaning? Why would the players find this group interesting to interact with? World building isn't just a creative outlet for the writer. It's a tool for the Storyteller, which gives them things to flesh out the game world with and use on the player without having to develop a setting from scratch. Just to be clear, these guys don't sound like they'd make a good universal villain to me.

                                                    Before you write about their activities, you have to figure out their motivations in detail, then move to their resources and internal structure/culture. Those are the most important details, because everything else about their operations is going to be supported by that.

                                                    What EXACTLY are they interested in and why? What do they think they will find and what lengths are they willing to go through for it? What underlying motivations do they have and why do they have these? Why would a real person be part of this group? Give them some logical explanations so you have some ideas to base their actions on before you start writing the other stuff, then think of what fits.

                                                    Finally, you need to also think about how they fit into the rest of the book. Pretty much everything in there so far connects with everything else. We have a set of underlying references to things like Nietsche and David Icke that give the book a consistent theme and concept. How does this fit?

                                                    Start with that, not a series of tropes and disconnected ideas. That's how you write a solid part of the setting.

                                                        So gathered my thoughts and retooled a skeleton framework on turning the philosophies into "motivational" archetypes.

                                                        The four that I have worked on are Sweat, Ash, Bone, and Blood. They exist as the follow:

                                                        Sweat - Now known as the Unconquered. They have the short term goals known as Breakthroughs with their long term goal called the Magnum Opus.

                                                        Bone - Now known as Avatars. They have the short term goals known as Revelations with their long term goal called The Trial.

                                                        Ash - Now known as the Catalysts. They have two categories of short term goals: Those being crucibles and forges. They have the long term goal known as Synthesis (Synthesis is not earned until both Crucibles and Forges have been completed)

                                                        Blood - Now known as the Immutable (Working title, feel free to suggest another). They have the short term goal of Pledges (working title.) with the long term goal being Final Testament (working title).

                                                        Completion of short term goals give you a point of breath per dot of furnace.

                                                        Completion of long term goals fills your breath track and gives you a dot of furnace free of charge (Because you already spent entire chronicle to get it)

                                                        "But what do dragons do?" answer.

                                                        Unconquered: To bring my desires forth. To make the covert vulgar. To the imagined into the real.
                                                        Catalysts: To destroy a corrupt system and replace it with a better one.
                                                        Immutable: To live the truth in all things.
                                                        Bone: to be the God we are born to be.

                                                        Passive abilities gained upon joining. No point of breath needed to activate. They can only be activated once every 30 days. Possibility OP. Do I care? No. Reason? They're fucking dragons. Let's get started.

                                                        Catalysts: Auto successes on one roll equal to furnace when damaging a person, place, or thing, that is the subject of his Crucible and/or attempting to renew a person place or thing the target of his Forge.

                                                        Avatars: Remove damage (Any) on a one to one basis on any supernatural being within line of sight per dot of furnace or complete wipe the damage track clean of mortals with in her line of sight up her dots in furnace. (example: three mortals get turn apart by angry tribbles and suffer Agg. Dragon has three dots of furnace, activates ability, they are good as new).

                                                        Unconquered: Gain extra willpower dots equal to furnace. They function as normal and the will power dots can be spent per usual. Will power dots committed in this fashion return at the end of 30 days. No exp needed.



                                                        The following is a work in progress:
                                                        Immutable: each active Pledge gives the dragon two extra attributes. They may have as many pledges up to their dots in furnace. Pledges completed count as short term goals.

                                                        Draconic pledges akin to the Adamantine Arrow Oaths (AA book. Page 62-66). Meaning they work off the spirit of the oath rather than the letter and as such, focuses on metaphorical intent rather than literal action. For one of the Immutable to state that they will be with someone in their darkest hour does not mean hiding out in their closet, disabling their light and locking them inside for an hour as a Changeling pledge may fulfill but rather sending physical, emotional, and/or financial support when they are most in need.

                                                        Also such pledges can not be forced. Such coercion when the soul is not with the words fails to activate the ability. Both parties must pledge of their own free will with the full intent of fulfilling the pledge.

                                                        Any attempt to ensnare the Immutable through unwilling and unwitting pacts or pledges through supernatural abilities fail outright. Old hearts beat within them and will not be conquered so easily.
