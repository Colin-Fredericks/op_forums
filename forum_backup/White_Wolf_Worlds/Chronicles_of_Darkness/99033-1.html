    Ace, Summer Court Beast Coldscale and Venombite

    Imagine if you will, having a fairly happy life. The sun is warm all the time, the sand around you is beautiful white and shines like a thousand stars. There is a lake you can swim in which is never cold, and it is a blue so pure it could only be made of angel's tears. There is a large flat rock you can hide under or bask on top of. It is a good and peaceful life. The food is plentiful, never hard to catch, and remarkably delicious. Out past the warm sand are so many other animals. Each one is happy in its own special little place. Everyone sings together to truly show happiness. All animals has a really good and happy existence. There is nothing to fear, nothing to want, every single need is provided for. Never forget that you life is very, very good and you should always be grateful. Be grateful of the caretaker, Mister Menagerie.

    Mr. M gives you your food. Mr. M gives you safety. Mr. M gives you everything will ever possibly need. Mr. M provides. Mr. M is a little different than the other animals you know. He is large and has a strange way of moving. He does have legs, not that you would honestly understand the notion, but where the other animals walk on four legs; he only walks on two. You always think he would be better off gliding on his belly like you do, but he seems to get around just fine. You see him putter around each animal's special area dropping off food, changing water, and fixing bedding. He spends time with every creature making sure they are happy and well. He can talk to each animal in their own voice. He chirps to birds, squeaks to rats, barks to dogs, croaks to frogs. He sings to you in your ophidian tongue and it is beautiful. He is your best friend and savior. He says he will never hurt you, and you believe him because he has never been anything but kind and caring to you and your friends. Life would be scary and harsh without Mr. M. There would be no food, no water, no warmth. You would be cold and alone in a huge land you can't begin to comprehend. It is so much better with Mr. M. It is so much better with Mr. M. You never want to make him angry by trying to leave. You've never seen him angry. Mr. M. is always smiling and laughing and talking to the animals. Why would you even think of breaking his heart and risk disappointing such a kindly old soul? It is madness to even think of disobeying Mr. M. Best not to think on such thoughts. Disobedience has no place in Mr. M's beautiful, magical world. Happiness is better than truth right? There Couldn't be very dark secrets lurking just outside your view, right? Could there?

    Imagine if you will, a day like any other happy, warm day. You have eaten your food and you took a nice swim in your lake. You are just settling in to bask on your rock when you hear a loud crash. You hear metal clattering and glasses shattering on the floor. The sounds are coming from a room far behind where the animals are kept. Your interest is piqued not so much by the bang on the door but by the odd wail that follows it. The wail raises into a pained scream and the door crashes open. You raise up on your rock as best you can and you see another two leg walking creature fall out onto the ground. You only catch a flash of pink before the thing rolls out of your sight. You hear it yelling strangely. The beast is too big to be a snake like yourself, but it strikes you as familiar in a way you cannot place. You hear it scramble and cry on the ground beneath your home. You can tell it is scared and hurt. You stretch closer towards the sound and the creature pops up looking you in the eyes. It is a strange looking thing. This yellow haired soft thing before you. One eye is green and slitted like your own and the other is round and blue. It reminds you of Mr. M's eyes to be fair. On the proper eye side of her, yes she is female, face is a bloom of green and red scales around her eye that spread all down her cheek and neck. She is bleeding where the scales meet her soft pink flesh. She is shaking and mewling like a trapped mouse. She is looks to be about to talk to you but stops when Mr. M. walks into the room.

    Mr. M. looks around until he sees the strange female. He makes a tutting sound and runs over to her. His usually clean white apron has blood stains all down the front. He looks annoyed, which you know is never a good thing. He runs over to the strange girl and tries to grab her but she jumps out of the way. She bumps into the table beneath you and knocks you home onto the floor. Everything you know about being safe is shattered as you fall. Sand flies everywhere. Your lake spills. Everything goes black as you are buried. You can hear a struggle and yelling from Mr. M. and the girl. You manage to poke your head out of the sand and see the female being dragged by her feet back into the dark room. She seems to look directly at you again and she screams for you to help. You find it very odd that you understand her words, and also that you feel the urge to do so. You slide out of your destroyed home and quickly make for the dark room.

    You see the tall metal table where the girl is being strapped down by Mr. M. He is whispering in in her ear and she is screaming. You are torn between wanting To help this strange creature and not wanting to make Mr. M angry. Something inside your mind seems to rip apart. You have to help this woman, her screams make you remember something you were forced to forget. You are not a snake. You are a human being. You now realize Mr. M is trying to turn the girl into an animal. You cannot let this happen. Against every instinct you have to just run away, you coil tightly and launch yourself at Mr. M's leg. You sink your fangs deep into his flesh and flood his blood with your deadly venom. Mr. M grabs his leg and screams. He looks down at you with a mix of anger and disapointment. He tuts again then kicks you across the room into the wall with so much force your vision goes grey.
    You see him walk back to the girl but as he reaches for her a pained grimace fills his face and he collapses knocking over the operating table. A few minutes later you see the girl shake her self out of her restraints. She stands up and walks to you but your head spins and everything goes black.

    Imagine if you will, one day waking up from a dream so real you well and truly believed you were a snake kept as a pet in an alien creature's twisted idea of a pet shop. You had been stuck there ever since you were kidnapped 10 years ago. You were in the states with your father meeting up with a friend for a boxing exhibition. That is over now of course. You walked down an alley and ended up a snake in a strange world not remotely like the one you know. You were replaced. You have another life now since your old one was ruined. Oh, you aren't a snake anymore obviously but you definitely look like one to others of your new kind. The saddest part of this whole thought experiment is that you don't really remember how you got out of the strange place. You vaguely remember being carried by the girl, Alyssa her name was, but you can't place what happened. You can assume you changed back to a man shape when you got free of the hedge but Mr. M kicking you definitely caused some sort of brain trauma. Maybe you could meet her again in the real word. You think you would like that.

    Now imagine if you will, that the story I am telling is not in fact just a story. It is a cautionary tale. It is a nightmare. You can't trust anything. There are things in the world that defy definition. You will never be truly safe. All you can do is go on with your life. You have to make the best of every day.


    Imagine if you will, that you are me and everything I have said is completely true. Welcome to my life, have a good day...

    Court Environs Information

    The Seasonal Courts your group are attached to are based in NW LA, around Malibu and Santa Monica. They are not the most powerful in the LA area in theory, but may be close to the largest. The number of Faerie in the general area are not known for sure, but there are a lot, and more passing through every day.

    On the other side of downtown LA there are another group of Seasonal Courts, older than yours. These tend to be based in Hollywood, and have pride in that. However, most of the faerie leading them are crazy, or so you have heard. You really only have secondary information about them.

    LA proper is kinda a no man’s land. Once under control of the distant seasonal courts, the battles with a few Gentry who have come through decimated much of the changeling populace, and lead to a decline in their influence. Some Changelings with both your Courts and theirs are in LA, usually on the sides nearer the court proper. The Day and Night Courts have set up shop in LA, and spend their time pissing each other off. This has increased saner fae’s avoidance of LA, as often passing through gets you involved in their wars. Either you may be manipulated into helping one side, or you may be spying for the other side as well as general changeling “are you a loyalist” paranoia. A number of Faerie want to drive them out, but then “LA has always been a hellhole.”

    Chinatown has a small number of Directional Court Faerie. They tend to distrust those of the Seasonal Courts (because they are closer to the crazy ones in Hollywood) and the Day and Night Courts. However, passing through is safe, generally, if you don’t make trouble, and there are a surprising number of non Asian members, usually people who wanted to distance themselves from the Hollywood Seasonal Court. They are probably the most together court besides yours, but are easily the smallest in LA County.

    The Hollywood Seasonal Courts ruled LA until sometime in the late 60s, when a horrific Gentry called the Grand Maw entered the city. The Grand Maw, who looked like a little old lady who could make her mouth enormous, ate other Gentry for power, and was hunted by them, possibly driven into the world. She set about eating Changelings to generate power to go back and battle those who drove her from the Hedge, and demonstrated a frightening ability to swallow Changelings whole and absorb their Glamour. The Hollywood Faerie fought the Grand Maw, but lost more and more members. Finally, they managed to beat her on a smoggy day during a race riot.

    Claims continue to be whispered the Hollywood Autumn Court captured, not killed her, as a living weapon to use against the Gentry and feed her Changelings to keep her satiated, but this is uncertain. Either way, the hold on the city was perilous and the Seasonal Courts organized after the Grand Maw’s defeat. Then in the early 80s a second Gentry arrived in LA, in the form of a Dragon, looking for Grand Maw. This Gentry was slain by the Hollywood Courts at significant losses, and it was soon after that the Day and Night Courts, once operating out of view, openly began running changeling affairs in LA.

    The Hollywood Court’s losses have been your local Court’s gains, as the local Freehold has grown in power and prestige. The Day and Night Court spend all their time stifling each other, so they have not tightened their grip on anything, and your courts have spread a little into NW LA, though mainly it controls Santa Monica and Malibu, and to a growing degree Venice Beach.

    None of these Courts are actively hostile to each other, well, except the Day and Night to each other and even then they are not killing each other. There is a lot of distrust, though. And the dates are vague because that is how you heard them…this happened “in the 60s,” that in “the early 80s” is all you have really been told.

    You know little about the local Courts with whom you are not affiliated, but have heard about the Monarchs

    Spring: Princess Jolie, Fairest, based in Glamour Springs Natural Spa in Malibu
    Summer: Viscount Augustine, Ogre, based in the Old Methodist Mission Church outside Santa Monica between it and Malibu.
    Autumn: Queen Charlotte, Beast, based in the Point Lookout Lighthouse outside Santa Monica
    Winter: Master Darke, Elemental, based in Santa Monica Community Rehabilitation Hospital rehab center in Santa Monica.

    The Court locations ironically go north to south, with Glamour Springs being the northernmost and the SMCRH being southernmost.

        April 27th

        So, today’s the day we looked like a total doofus. Found a cool house, but man, we looked like a doofus. It was not my day, but Calamity still likes me at least. She at least knows I can do better - I don’t even wanna think about what this’d be like if this were the first day at a new job where nobody knew me. I blame the nightmares, you know? I mean, in the end I got enough sleep, but that damn dream was back.

I didn’t bother going over to the House to early, since Calamity was all set to crash. I got there a bit before she work up, something like 11 or so, and started making breakfast, French Toast this time. That’s alot easier to cook in a kitchen, let me tell you. When it was done, I called up for Calamity - all the others were already downstairs. Ace was discussing piranhas very loudly. That would be a super cool act, right, but I have no idea where we’d get them *or* a cow. Insincere Marguerite was dancing on the stage, practicing moves, or maybe making them up just then. She kept popping in the kitchen and grabbing a piece of toast then going back out. She said she didn’t need any powdered sugar or anything cause it would get her fat. Seems a shame though, French toast without powdered sugar and lemon on it. Lori was dressed super nice, like she was going to some kind of wedding or something. I guess it was Sunday though, so I think maybe it was just church? She looked very nice and was wearing glasses. I wonder if she usually wears contacts, or if the glasses were a disguise, like for Superman! But if it’s a disguise, who is she trying to fool? Is she a reporter too? Do reporters go to church? They were all very pretty, well, except Austin, cause he was a dude, but he was handsome, I guess? He was a Satyr, and was mostly there cause he could do whatever. He did have a fancy cane, which was pretty cool. I think he’s going to be singing a duet, or something. I was mostly just there for watching.

I thought at first that Marie-Helene was French too, but Insincere Marguerite said she was Belgian and that Belgian was better than Canadian at least. Insincere Marguerite is strange sometimes. She was really good at playing the guitar - Marie-Helene, that is. She played all kinds of songs, lots of rocking ones too. She might have been my favorite performer. I’m gonna keep having to do this, but if you forgot her, I’m sorry. She did this whole singing and dancing thing, and was wearing an awesome suit. She had a whip too, which was a little weird, but the chair worked for me, gotta say.

But after a bit I picked it back up, and got into the groove with it. I was doing not so bad by the time I got all done. Well, really, by the time Ace can charging out yelling about a Hedge gate. Well, I do some forge work first, cause I was a little worked up, you know? Jacob kept me company after a bit. I was making knives, and he asked what kind. They were just normal stabbing kinda knives, but he started telling me about throwing knives. I’d never worked with them too much, but maybe I should. I mean, I could make some magic-y ones for when I wasn’t able to get up close, you know? Can’t really do that for guns.

        After showers we kinda chilled for a bit before Ace decided to go to a biker bar and see if he could find a fight. Calamity decided to go along with, well, give him a ride really, so I decided to follow, not creepily or anything, just to make sure she was alright. Not that she couldn’t handle herself, but you know. I just get nervous when she does that. They were good though, and didn’t actually start a fight, especially after one started without them trying. Ace left after a bit, I think he mentioned something about a soup kitchen, so I went over to sit with Calamity. There was a guy that had been giving her a look all night, so I went and put the fear of god into him. It always feels weird, playing like I’m a jealous boyfriend and everything, but I’m pretty ok at scaring douchebags like him, and that’s good Glamour for Calamity.

        Calamity asked me to keep her out of trouble after that, so I offered to take her on back to the House. Then I thought about it for a second, and offered to match her in some video games. I kicked her butt at the Sonic racing game of course... and then she kicked mine in Dead or Alive. Borderlands is always my favorite though - it’s nice to get to work together sometimes. Video games have come a long way since I got taken. That was probably the best thing about being gone 20 years.

        She ended up crashing here, since we stayed up playing so late. I’ll drive both us in tomorrow at... whenever we wake up, I guess.


        Lorelei’s Diary

        I’m not sure quite what to think about everything that happened yesterday. Some of it was empowering, some of it was intriguing, some of it was terrifying, and some of it was just exhilarating. But I suppose I should put it all down in writing so that I can sort it out later.

        The alarm got me out of bed obscenely early this morning, as per usual for a Sunday lately, and I managed to stagger into the shower and stand there like a zombie until the hot water woke me up. I put on one of my more sedate ensembles, pulled my hair back, put on those old reading glasses I bought in that thrift store, and walked downstairs. By the time I got to the kitchen, I was chipper enough to say hi to Ace and Jacob, grab a cup of coffee, and head out to church.

        I kept the top up on the ‘vette today, of course. It wouldn’t do to muss my coiffure before I even got to the church… even if it was only a ponytail.

        The service was lovely. The choir sang a few traditional hymns and one more modern Christian piece. No solos for me this week, but I’ve got one coming up next weekend. The sermon was all about tolerance and forgiveness. A lot of dad’s sermons seem to run along those lines these days, which is a nice change from the fire and brimstone I grew up with.

        I got back to the house about 12:30, and everyone was up and eating a late brunch of French toast. I gratefully nibbled at a slice when Gawain offered it, but I spent most of my time trying to convince Ace that setting up an act where a cow gets eaten by piranhas would be both impractical and… well, icky. I think he came around after a while. I’m all for showcasing avant-garde acts at the Dream House, but there have to be limits. And cows are expensive, dammit.

        After a while, we started getting ready to receive some of the local changelings. We’d put the word out that we were looking for Lost who would be interested in performing at the Dream House on opening night, and we had more than a few nibbles. I shook my hair out of my ponytail (okay, I admit it… I enjoyed the looks I got from the others when I did that), ran upstairs, and threw on a pair of tight jeans and a t-shirt, and hurried back downstairs to meet some of our new neighbors.

        I was kind of surprised that I actually knew one of the five changelings who showed up to strut their stuff. Austin was the only male among our guests, and also the only one who didn’t bother to audition. “I can do pretty much anything,” he said with his typical modesty. “Just tell me what you need and what I should wear and I’m up for it.” I don’t know what we’re going to have him do as of yet, but I thought a male-female duet would probably work well if we could find the right song.

        There were two other Spring Courtiers in the group, though I hadn’t yet met either of them. Chalcedony was a thin, pale woman with white-blonde hair. She’s a dancer, and I could tell after a few minutes of watching her that she really knows her stuff. We didn’t get much of a chance to talk, but she seemed nice enough in an aloof, artistic sort of way.

        Marie-Hélène Beaumont is a singer and guitarist. I liked her instantly. She’s very in-your-face, and that’s reflected in her music. Most of the others just referred to her style as “rock,” though it was a lot harder and more visceral than anything we called rock music before I was Taken… the genre must have taken more than a few cues from punk and metal along the way. We thanked her for her audition, and I privately told her that I’d be happy to back her up on vocals if she wanted me to.

        The other two who showed up were from the Autumn Court. Ginny Green was an adorable singer with the biggest eyes I’ve ever seen.                 Insincere Marguerite’s Whispers

                Today has been interesting but deeply frustrating to me.

                I got the idea from a dream to choreograph a dance number based around the song “It’s a Sin” by the Pet Shop Boys. I am aware that burlesque houses get a lot of men, and men like the implication of homosexual acts between women (fair, I enjoy watching men kiss each other more than any other combination of sexes) so a variation on it with a female singer and dancers seems like a sure thing. And the women who come would probably like it. So I grabbed some lost bread that Gawain prepared (French Toast he called it) and worked on it until interviews. A few puppets watched.

                We spoke to five Fae who were prospective entertainers…from the Spring Court were Austin, a Beast along with Chalcedony and Marie-Hélène, both Fairest. From Autumn we had Annika, a Darkling and Ginny, a Ogre Nixie. Annika was arrogant in a way that weakened the knees and took the best aspects from both sexes in a Dietrich-inspired show, combining singing and dancing. Marie-Hélène was a rock singer and musician, Ginny was a singer, Chalcedony a dancer. Austin noted he could do anything. It gives us some good variety to add. Ace mentioned magic and I may see about some prestidigitation, but the idea of that many people looking at me makes me queasy.

                When they left I returned to focusing on the choreography, until Ace found me to announce he found a “Hedge Hole” and was gathering people to explore it. Calamity used her Contract with Dream to get a feel for the local Hedge, and found there was a Hollow nearby, and a “large predator.” As it would turn out, the “large predator” was a Harvestman who had taken up residence in what looked like an echo of The Dream House in the Hedge. I am no expert on these things, but such does not happen often…this had to be deliberately created. But why? Calamity mentioned some suicides in the house…apparently happened a while ago but as far as we know no connection to the Hedge. Strange. There must be more to this house than we know.

                We killed the Harvestman, but it dropped webbing on us that incapacitated Lorelei and inconvenienced Gawain. I weakened it with my contract with Darkness, and Calamity, Ace, and Jacob killed it. That was not the interesting part. We found some very nice clothes, but Calamity and Gawain wanted the outfits I liked, though Calamity discussed sharing. That also was not the interesting part.

                Our rooms each had some reflection of us. Or a clue to our nature. Mine had restraints, the sort that in my mind’s eye I could see Lorelei struggling in like, well, when she was caught in the web. Which is bad. Bad bad bad. But the other’s…well…it was juicy. Calamity’s had innocent blood on the floor. I knew because it smelled innocent, but that seemed to be a Darkling thing. Lorelei’s was littered with broken hearts, Jacob’s had bloody knives, Ace’s a cage, and the forge room was strewn with decapitated heads. Not good decorating.

                I wonder so much. I know the story of Gawain and the Green Knight, and wonder if Gawain was the Green Knight for awhile and became Gawain somehow? Maybe earned it? If so he clearly killed a number of people. Ace’s room seemed more to keep him in than others out. Is he more innocent than we? Lorelei’s was no doubt because she is a total flirt, and not much we did not know there, but I wonder about Jacob’s and Calamity’s. Calamity noted, without much remorse but without reveling in it, that she “pushed Timmy down the well” which is so totally Calamity. The others asked her many forward questions about the innocent blood, but apparently got tired of interrogating before asking me about the restraints. My stomach sinks thinking about them doing so.

                But what of Jacob? I like Jacob; he is cute. But…the bloody knives bother me. He seems friendly, innocent and a little unsure. Could it be an act? I am beginning to wonder. And if it is, being involved with him is potentially dangerous if I decided to go that way. I should…proceed carefully. He could be an abominable little snowman.

                So we came back, and Calamity was flirting with Lorelei when we returned, talking about washing her wings. I knew Lorelei was a flirt, but I was not expecting her to jump into the shower with Calamity. I don’t like the way that made me feel. I felt jealous that Lorelei, who literally flirted with all of us, wound up having sex with Calamity. I did not have reason to. It was possessive and bad. I am still an unhappy little grub. I think Lorelei may be a bad thing for my psyche. Not that I am angry with her or Calamity, mind. Such would be decidedly unfair.

                Still my shower seemed to take forever and they were still going, so I sat down to write this. I will go work on the choreography again, I think. Bah.

I heard Ace, Calamity, and Gawain talking about going to a biker bar to get some glamour, but apparently Lorelei also wanted to go out to a bar or something, and Jacob knew of one friendly to the Kind called “Lowjack’s” and Lorelei came and invited me. I noticed her glance at me, and noticed her pale skin and statuesque physique and how it reminded me of my Dearest, and before I realized what I was doing I had struck up a conversation with her. We began discussing it, and complimenting each other, so Flores suggested I go over there and she could do so tonight. It is spring after all, a time of such passions, and the night is a time to act upon ones impulses. The rest of the night passed delightfully. Julia proved a most pleasing lover.

                In the morning, Julia made breakfast, and we decided to go ahead and have her do some sketches after I called the house. She never commented on my name, choosing to call me various pet names. While working she was quite serious and intent but her demeanor the rest of the time was…different. She seemed nervous, giddy. Even a little awkward. She said she was not ready for a relationship with a woman, but added (a little too) quickly that that may change in the future, and she did not want everything to end here. I made it clear I was not yet ready for a committed relationship either, with anyone, but I also did not want to simply walk away and forget, so we decided we may meet again, possibly for sketching and possibly for “sketching.” She dropped me off at the House with some signed prints of her work, and watched me go until I was out of sight.

                I can’t believe I did that. I have not shared a bed with anyone since Arcadia. And never with anyone I did not own. It was unspeakably weird to be the one waking in someone else’s bed. And…Julia’s lips said she was not interested in a relationship, but her eyes told me her lips were lying. She did say I was the most intriguing woman she had ever met, and my…invasive passions did not trouble her but were apparently pleasing. She seemed rather attached to me in the morning.

                And she is talented and I really like her smell, and I liked her smelling like a mix of her and I this morning. She is amused if anything when I smell her hair. She apparently can even sing, and implied she may for me…she sings for only special people apparently. But the urge to take her, to keep her is…strong. Very strong. Also, I know I partially did that because of Lorelei, and that is unfortunate. But I feel no shame for it. Shame is what the Day Court wants. And I would have been intrigued by Julia anyway. I think. If I wasn’t it would be foolish of me.

                And she reminds me of the Dearest, and that is bad, she should stand on her own. And it is bad to think about the Dearest she is…precious to me. Perhaps someone needs to save Miss Oldacre from me. Before we both find ourselves in my dark place, literally and figuratively.

                I will put up this art on my wall, and think about it. I have options, no need to ignore one because of a tumble. Besides, there is a show to prepare!

                End Session II

Lorelei failed the Athletics roll, and failed the chance die to ignore the poison on the web as per Autumn Nightmares. Gawain passed easily, activating his Metalflesh blessing.

                I gave Gawain the initiative penalty for his sword, which, in retrospect, I should not have, but it did not matter. Calamity did an insane 9 levels of damage in one strike with her knife fighting, and the Harvestman only had 10 levels. Marguerite used Paralyzing Shudder to weaken it.                 I decided any Darklings or Ogres would be able to smell the “innocent blood” in Calamity’s room, though the Motley has none of the latter.

Marguerite got some bonuses for her slightly unusual looks (and one level of Striking Looks) and accent, as well as a die from Flores being encouraging (trying to get her reclusive friend laid) but had a penalty, as Julia does not self-identify as being into women though she is somewhat bisexual. Insincere Marguerite is nowhere near the social juggernaut Lorelei is but is sort of on the second tier with Ace, and managed to seduce her fellow Darkling with Willpower use. No such rolls were needed with Lorelei and Calamity.

                    Spring Court Handout

                    The Spring Court Monarch is Jolie, who goes by the title Princess rather than King or Queen. Jolie is an attractive redheaded woman, who runs a Spa in Malibu called Glamour Springs. It has some natural and artificial hot springs, and has special areas for special members (Changelings) and VIPs (Spring Court or high Affinity) as well as Jolie’s personal areas.

                    Rumors persist around the Jolie and the Spa, however, that new Changelings are sometimes lead there and never return, sold to the Gentry or consumed by Jolie. However, these rumors seem without any merit and almost without source. No one knows of any Changelings who ever disappeared there. No one has seen foundlings there who were never seen again, and Jolie does not make any particular attempt to have foundlings brought there unless they have an interest in the Spring Court (though she won’t turn them away).

                    However there is an almost to the almost without source. Two girls did vanish there in 2011. Abby James and Christine Williams, 16 and 17, used false Ids and their parent’s credit cards to get in, and were never seen again. The employee’s say they left after staying awhile, but that was the last place the cards were used. A search by law enforcement revealed nothing, though some family and supporters still believed they met a dark end in Glamour Springs.

                    Jolie is an outgoing and pleasing woman, who is very kind to those who are polite. She keeps numerous lovers and does sometimes have affairs with newcomers. These affairs mean that the Spring Court has some supporters in other courts including, it is rumored, the Autumn Queen. She is quite shrewd, never vapid, and maintains control well, though she is lassiz-faire with her constituents. The Malibu Dream House was her idea. Jolie is a Fairest Muse/Waterborn.

But as he points out, he will wear anything and ride anything that looks good. He is more often the first one met by newcomers, and tries to make anyone feel at home. I’d made it about halfway through the 1980s when I discovered a band called Night Ranger. I knew intellectually that the band was probably old news to the rest of the motley, but I couldn’t help myself… I just had to share my feelings. As I probably should have expected, the power ballad strains of “Sister Christian” didn’t really make much of a splash with my friends. What I didn’t expect was the news that Night Ranger never really made much of a splash at all after that… at least not in America.

I told them all when I’d been taken, and confessed how frustrating it was to have missed out on three and a half decades of music, and then asked them to each recommend at least one band and one song from post-1978. The answers were, as expected, a mixed bag… but I found something to like in almost everything they suggested.

I got a lot of suggestions. Not all of them are entirely to my taste, but I think it’s a good baseline. I was the most accomplished non-Gentry thief in all of Arcadia. How could I not steal a turtle? It is a walking house that eats lettuce!”

Ace’s Keeper was a genial being who treated his charges with gentle affection unless he was defied. The creature I refer to as The Stranger never showed anything like emotion while I was in his care.

I think we got lucky with that… they all seem to know their stuff well enough to work backup even on short notice. Of course, the other local Changelings were there to rehearse as well. At one point, Annika asked me if I wanted her to dance backup in one of my numbers… but the way she asked sort of put me off. It was almost like she thought she was doing me a favor, or offering me a treat. I told her that wouldn’t be necessary, and went over to talk to Austin.

I confessed my ignorance of recent music, and he suggested a few numbers which might work, and played them for me on his phone. In the end we decided to perform “La Plage de Saint Tropez,” a fun little French/English tropical number that should have the crowd feeling good.

Apparently there used to be another club in the local freehold, a place where changelings could go to showcase their talents and soak up the glamour. This place was run by a Lost who called himself Golden Swan. He passed away in the late nineties, and Annika thought he might have left his belongings in the care of the Spring Court.

Austin promised to look into it for me, and told me a bit about Golden Swan himself as well. First, he apparently had a partner who moved to San Francisco; he might be able help us find some of Golden Swan’s records. So we might have to arrange a road trip in the near future.

He suffered from something called AIDS, or “Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome.” I looked it up after talking with Austin, and found out more than I bargained for. It was apparently around and spreading before I was Taken, though scientists didn’t really classify it until a few years later. It’s a sexually-transmitted disease that attacks the body’s immune system to the point that any minor infection or contagion will eventually prove fatal. There’s some sort of goblin-magic from the hedge that can cure it… but nobody in the freehold had access to that magic back then. Ever since Golden Swan passed, the Spring Court has made certain that someone in the freehold can cast those spells.

Ace tried on the wispy cloud outfit, but we only caught a glimpse of him as he hurried through the stage area muttering that it “wasn’t going to work.” We even managed to get Calamity in the “Wicked Queen” outfit, with the lightning bolt bodice, and she looked amazing. When he saw her showing off her stuff, Gawain put on the midnight-black tux, and we got the two of them to dance to a stately Renaissance-era ditty. It made me smile; those two are good together.

I had a lot of prepping to do to get ready for the show (and I admit I didn’t relish the idea of going back there just yet), so I demurred. I understand that they succeeded in making the mirror house a bit more secure, so the next time we need to go back there we shouldn’t have to worry about anything unsavory having taken up residence.

I couldn’t resist teasing her gently about her uptight attitude, so I told her that I hoped she’d join us for the show and let her hair down a little. I’m… honestly not sure if she even noticed I was teasing her. She was so focused on warning Ace that two of our performers were new in town. Apparently the Summer Court feels that Ginny and Chalcedony might be loyalists, and wanted us to keep a close eye on them to ensure that they didn’t try anything. I get the concern; we were planning to have an almost unprecedented number of the Lost gathered in one place… but I can’t help but wonder if she issued a similar caution about me when I wasn’t listening.

Everybody – and I do mean everybody – was there. Including the motley and the other performers, there were about forty Changelings gathered in the Dream House. All four monarchs were there, of course, as well as representatives of the Day, Night, and Dusk Courts.

Princess Jolie gave me an approving nod and a piercing stare that made me go all weak in the knees when I approached her. I also met the lords of Summer (Viscount Augustine), Autumn (Queen Charlotte), and Winter (Master Darke). They’re… different. Queen Charlotte was very kind and complimentary, but throughout our conversation, I got the feeling that she was sizing me up in some way. Viscount Augustine was more direct, if a bit stiff and formal for my taste. Master Darke was… creepy. Okay, really creepy. Like, you know, that guy who stares at you from across the bar all night but never says anything, and then you go and talk to him because you figure he’s just shy, and he has this wheezy voice and all he wants to talk about is his pet tarantula and how he lives in his mother’s basement. Sort of creepy like that, but different. Not like stalker-creepy; more just… weird.

I got a lot of appreciative glances, a few flirtatious overtones, and several outright ogles… and I loved every second of it. I channeled a little glamour into my appearance just before we opened the doors, and I guess it paid off, because I ended up being the belle of the ball. Men, women, changelings, mundanes… everyone seemed to want to get to know me, and I lapped up the attention.

Becky Wan and her clique sat together gossiping as usual. This time I did the hostess thing and joined them for a bit; they seem like nice enough girls once you get them talking. I noticed a stately lady in the company of the Autumn Queen who called herself Madame Spidertoes… I didn’t ask why. And Monica and de la Rocha waved me over to their table for a chat as well, which was fun.

Calamity insisted on doing this New-Age “power circle” thing where we all joined hands for encouragement; I think she might have worked another short-term pledge into it. Once that was done, I pulled on my first costume (a flashy, spangly dress with a high hemline), made sure my backup was ready, and listened to Ace welcome people to our humble establishment. If I’d had any lingering doubts about Ace’s sense of showmanship, they would have been obliterated in those first few minutes. He had the crowd eating out of his hand in no time. They laughed at every joke and applauded appreciatively in all the right places. He’s a natural.

I started slowly, singing the intro like an old-timey blues singer. Then the curtain parted, the music started, and my backup and I launched into a more traditional version of Labelle’s “Lady Marmalade.” Once again, I channeled glamour into the performance, making it as smoky and seductive as I could, and the crowd really seemed to respond to it (if I can judge by the number of catcalls and wolf-whistles I heard).

Marie-Hélène shredded on that guitar, adding her own style to the Stones’ “Paint It Black” and performing a couple of songs I didn’t know (but quite enjoyed). Ginny Green did a few old-fashioned torch songs, again by bands I didn’t know. I got the impression that they were (relatively) recent, but done in a much older style. I asked her about it after the show, and she told me that most of her songs were by a band with the unlikely name of “Squirrel Nut Zippers.”

She commanded the crowd in a way I’d never seen before, pulling people out of the audience and dancing with them in ways that defied expectations. She owned that audience last night, and no one could seem to resist her charms. She did this one dance where she was sort of half in drag, and it actually looked like a couple dancing together. I have no idea how she pulled it off.

Jacob kept the bar running smoothly the entire time, and no one ever lacked for refreshment. Gawain kept near the front door (which had closed at 8:00 sharp) so that he could keep a careful eye on the crowd, while Calamity posted herself nearer the stage to handle any patrons who got too drunk, rowdy, or grabby. Marguerite had her hands full the whole time managing the lights, sound, and curtain.

I chose a song by Tori Amos (recommended by Jacob as one of his sister’s favorite artists) called “Leather.” Appropriately enough, I wore the leather outfit with the fishnet stockings for that number. There was a woman I didn’t recognize sitting at one of the vanities, brushing her platinum blonde hair. I approached her and asked for her name, and she turned to look at me, and… her head… it just sort of… lolled off to one side. Her neck was too long, and rubbery, like it had been badly broken at some point. After that, the strange woman just sort of faded away.

But we were in the middle of a production, for crying out loud, and someone had to show an iota of professionalism. I got them all to quiet down, and it wasn’t long before Calamity showed up to see what the commotion was all about. Luckily, Marie-Hélène was wailing on stage at that moment, so most of the crowd hadn’t heard the girls’ screams. I told Calamity what had happened, and she promised to get someone from the Autumn Court to look into it. I wonder if this has anything to do with the deaths she hinted at the other day… the ones that happened in this house?

Ace introduced our final set by doing a pantomime dance… while wearing the hedge-spun cloud outfit! It went over like a house on fire, and I don’t think anyone in that audience will ever forget his antics… even without his proposed cow-and-piranha act.

Of course, Gawain later explained to us that Captain Jack was his “nemesis,” whatever that means, and that the two of them had worked that little scene out in advance, just to get people talking about them.

I was only wearing the hedge-spun dress at the time… but I admit that it had been hiked up to a dangerous degree before the door to my bedroom closed behind us.

He seemed surprised that I was calling, and I felt a little bad about disturbing him after such a long night, but he accepted my request with good grace. He could have sent one of the kitchen staff up, but he came himself instead. Two bowls of chocolate ice cream. Yum!

I don’t know if chocolate would stain a dress that’s actually made of red wine, but I wasn’t anxious to find out, so I pulled it over my head and answered the door in just my panties. I don’t think Jacob minded much.

                        We talked for a bit in the afterglow. I remember her running her fingers gently through my feathers, a fascinated look on her face. Yeah, the bad girls always love the wings for some reason. They always want someone who looks like an angel but fucks like a demon, and I guess I fit that description well enough for most of them. Anyway, we were doing the gentle post-coital stuff when she started in on the standard not-looking-for-a-serious-relationship talk. I swear, it’s enough to make me wonder if anyone in this county is looking for love!

                        I rolled over and faced her with a smile. “De la Rocha,” I said softly. “I like you. I like hanging out with you. I like having sex with you. But I don’t want to marry you, or even go steady right now. Okay?”

                        (Yes, by the way, apparently two girls can get married to each other these days… at least in some states and countries, including California. They can even have a traditional church wedding, depending on the church and the denomination. You could have knocked me over with a feather when I first heard about that. I’m still a long way from even thinking about getting married right now… but it’s nice to know that my options are a bit broader these days than they used to be).

                        She leaned in and kissed me. “Okay,” she said with a sigh. “You want me to head out now, so you can get some sleep?”

                        I shook my head. “You can stay here if you want to, I don’t mind. I am going to have to get some shuteye soon, though, so no more shenanigans.”

                        She nodded and settled her head onto one of my pillows as I turned off my lamp. “Ready when you are,” she said.

                        And then we fell asleep for about eight hours. Actually, de la Rocha is still asleep on my bed across the room, even though it’s nearly noon. I’ve been writing in this diary for about half an hour, stealing glances at her, fascinated by the way the sunlight streaming through my window accentuates the purple highlights in her black hair. This girl is something special. We’re lovers. We’re friends. We may never be more to each other… but I think we’ll always have that much, at least.

                        Lorelei Grace

I am not sure. How do you tell if a suit of armor is gay? Same way as anyone else I suppose.

                                                            Maybe I should just ask who is a LGBT in the group. I would not want to make anyone uncomfortable though.

                                                            Annika, Julia, and Gunnar all came over today. All Darklings. Annika came to negotiate a coming out of retirement (did opening night not do that?) and begin training Calamity as an understudy (she could do a lot worse). Julia came to do some sketching and painting of the stage and such, and to see me. I introduced her to everyone, including Gawain when he returned.

                                                            Of course, Julia mostly kept sketching my eyes. She did describe Annika as a recluse, which surprised me somewhat. Apparently she took the death of Golden Swan very very badly. Julia came long after his time, but heard of Annika and her self-imposed isolation.

                                                            Gunnar came to look for the ghost. He did not find her. He did talk to us about how to deal with her, but it involves channeling faith as much as anything, and I am as terrible a Pagan as I was Catholic. I guess I am still a terrible Catholic, just a more terrible Catholic being also Pagan.

                                                            Gunnar did pop up in the Catwalks while I was up there with Julia. I am sure it looked shady. I was just showing her my puppets.

                                                            Mostly.

                                                            With Gunnar gone, we are going to have a late dinner. We have been together a week as of two this afternoon. Speeding toward Walpurgis like a freight train and relaxing after it did not seem like a week, but that is good, right?

                                                            I have revised my opinion of Gawain little. I still like him, despite my misgivings over his Court. I still think he and Calamity are a good match, though possibly a doomed one. He appears to be a good guy. A little clueless, though I am given to understand that is not uncommon with Elementals, as evidenced with my conversation with Flores. I am glad he is with us; I fear some might take advantage of his helpfulness and generosity. Maybe he will even move in with us at some point. I don’t see why he hasn’t; I do not smell bad. Not at all.

                                                            Lorelei is nice, and I still find her attractive. To say she is full of herself would be unfair, but I will say she is acutely aware of her attractiveness in a way that is slightly off-putting. Maybe it is my feelings about Day Courtiers, but I feel she is in opposition with me when she isn’t, and that is a shame. She was flirty at first but has grown considerably less so. Have I been cold? Perhaps. Has my newness worn off? Perhaps. Is it because I did not melt into her arms? Perhaps. Regardless, I hold no ill will toward Lorelei, and hope our friendship improves. I’d hate to think the mystique put her off.

                                                            Ace I also have become less wary of, much like Gawain. He is certainly bombastic, but not annoyingly so…while he represents light to my darkness he is not my enemy. I apparently robbed the pet store he was in, which still amuses me. Ace seems to busy himself with his own projects a lot, and I know him least well. He seems the least sane of our number, or perhaps that is the notorious Beast tendency to be direct. Still, madness begets creativity, just sometimes it is an awesome routine on stage, sometimes it is a fixation with cows and piranha. Still, I like him well enough. He does seem quite fearless, which will be handy when we eventually face threats.

                                                            I still really like Calamity. She along with Jacob I feel kinship toward, and this has grown into a fair amount of trust. Maybe partially because I think that if Calamity did not want me to be here, I wouldn’t. Maybe. But she is a Darkling, she understands the need to be part of the shadows, she understands our changed souls. And while I am sure she feels closer to Gawain then I, there seems an unspoken bond of respect between us. Honestly, I like that.
                                                            She is free to make it spoken and tell me how wonderful I am though. Calamity may not be as fierce as Gawain or Ace, but she is no less dangerous. Which is good.

                                                            Jacob is an intriguing mystery, which of course makes me somewhat interested in him. On the other hand, I do not like being cold. I am skinny; I can’t trap heat. Jacob likes rooftops much like I do, and seems eager to get to know us thus also I. He is quiet but not unfriendly. Still, the meeting on the roof two nights ago keeps coming back to mind. He won’t let me in, so I don’t know what is going on, and it just makes me what to know more. Which may be deliberate. If anyone in the group is dangerous to I, it is Jacob. But I don’t think he is. Maybe.

                                                            Well off to dinner, this Motley and I.

                                                            End Session IV

                                                            Mostly socializing this time. Beltane was one of the two big “NPC dumps” in which we introduce a whole bunch of NPCs so the players have context for who they are, the other was the previous session opening night. Really now with the opening night done I am letting the players into the drivers seat, but I will still be dropping them some hooks.
