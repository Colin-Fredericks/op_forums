    Idigam Chronicles Weekly Discussion: Shapeshifting

    05-02-2014, 06:01 PM
    The changing forms for the sons and daughters of Luna
    The Changing Man – Onyx Path Publishing
    http://theonyxpath.com/the-changing-man/


    My current thoughts,

    Hishu: No real qualms about the form, yet Uratha are constantly portrayed as having supernatural senses even in their most mundane form, I do think at least a +1 to perception rolls or the ability to track by scent (at a penalty of course) would be a decent course of action. Not too powerful, but maybe enough to turn the tide over a simple mundane

    Dalu: I have no problems with this form as is, especially if the Hishu changes are implemented. Would merely be an enhanced for of Hishu

    Gauru: This form I both love the changes and am disappointed a bit. First of all the heals all bashing and lethal damage per turn definitely makes the form useful especially against hard hitting opponents. What I am sort of disappointed on is the always uses down and dirty combat rules against lesser foes. My reasoning is it is some of those encounters that take you by surprise. A group of low ranking level 1 spirits can actually take down a Gauru werewolf if they use the correct numen. Additionally, humans can band together and even a rampaging Gauru if known is no match for 2 or 3 people with a silver slug each if they get lucky. Lunacy itself should be the equalizer, not a blanket mechanic such as a condition.

    Urshul: I almost think this form is a bit too strong since losing a defense for the rest of the scene is massive. My suggestion is have the damage act more like beats such as arm wrack. If the damage is healed, they gain the defense back. This way, a sufficiently powerful werewolf or vampire can keep going. Even a Gauru werewolf attacked by a few Urshul werewolves would find it odd that his defense does not come back when all of the damage dealt has been healed.


    Urhan: I like the pre-emp action in combat for 1 essence, but I would make it contested if the opponent had an ability such as Celerity, or used Urhan form of their own. Against mundane abilities, makes perfect sense.

                  Oh Goodie! Crunch. I shall break down my thoughts into three categories:

                  Things That Are Great:
                  • Love that all five forms have clear applications in and around the hunt.
                  • Love that Gauru regenerates ALL Bashing and Lethal (though I have some reservations about all lethal).
                  • Love that Gauru uses Down & Dirty combat inherently against extras (though I do feel allowances should be made with certain spirits, even if their rank is lower).
                  • Love that Dalu can part crowds and intimidate (though I might suggest Wits as a defense in a chase)
                  Things That I'm Not So Hot On:
                  • The Hishu crowd penalty doesn't make a whole lot of sense to me. Perhaps that's something to be justified with fluff.
                  • "Can't Spend Willpower to Attack a Gauru Werewolf" really bothers me for some reason. It feels almost like it should be the other way around, that a Werewolf should be horrifying to aggress. But that'd be a bit much, perhaps lesser returns on Willpower expenditure or something to convey that fighting a massive wolfman is mentally exhausting.
                  • Limited Gauru duration. Restricting Gauru duration in this manner feels so...artificial, but it's nothing terrible.
                  • "Urshul defense penalties last the whole scene" this makes very little sense, a character who has time to back off and recover shouldn't continue to suffer a penalty from onslaught. Maybe instead have Urshul double onslaught penalty, regardless of damaging success. That might better enforce the forms position as 'prey harrier'.
                  Things That I Absolutely Oppose:
                  • "Apply full Defense against firearms attacks" <- That. Not for some goofy crossover reason, not for combat balance, but because it means a wolf with middling to high defense doesn't have to fear a silver bullet. And that's just not right.
                  Beyond that last one, which is a humongous deal breaker. This is all just stellar.

                                                  I'll be the dissenting voice this week, though I like most of the changes. As a WtA fan, the five forms are possibly the straightest copy from the previous game (with changes admittedly).

                                                  The time limit to Gauru is still there, which is rather bleh. It seems Urshul will still be a go to battle form.

                                                  But mainly, Urhan as a form is still a bit strange to me. I understand it is for long distance running, but the form is a bit lackluster in Forsaken, as the Uratha don't really have the same connection to the wild wolves that justified the lupus form in Apocalypse. This means its a normal looking wolf-form that limited to where it can run due it being a wolf, is basically used solely for long running because other forms can hunt well, and in general feels like an artifact from WtA.

                                                  Adding to it, the other Forsaken forms have a purpose beyond the Hunt. Hishu is dealing with humans, Dalu is for rituals/controlled fights with weapons, Gauru is for brutal beatdown, Urshul is for gauru-style combat without the Death Rage. Urhan.. doesn't. I guess if it could appear domesticated with a gift/ability, to spy on the prey.

                                                  Though, if you've had/used urhan well, great. Just from personal experience I feel the form could be dropped or modified.
