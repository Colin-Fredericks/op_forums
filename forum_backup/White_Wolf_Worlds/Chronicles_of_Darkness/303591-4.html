                I agree with others that this is a very dense book, and is going to take awhile to unravel, but so far, it's incredible. Well done to the authors!

                So many questions:

                1) I think the only purely mechanical question I have concerns the Memory 8 sin for Deceived. It mentions that vigorously pursuing your temakh's goals is a sin against Memory. However, later in that same section, I believe it mentions that vigorously pursuing your temakh's goals can grant you a reset on your Descent with no Memory penalty. How are these reconciled?

                2) I lied; one more mechanical question. It's noted that magic requiring knowledge of a target's True Name doesn't work well (or at all) on mummies, whose True Names are kept by the Judges or the temakhs. How does this reconcile with something like the Echoes of the Rattled Chains Affinity, which suggests that it can target mummies to determine information about them, despite requiring knowledge of their True Names?

                3) I can't wait to see what seba of ranks 4 and 5 can do. Speaking of which, the temakh descriptions discuss various avenues to seba that each temakh apparently favors (such as inspiration, insight, etc.) Do you think these influence the kind of seba that a given Deceived can find and bind, or are they more along the lines of descriptions of where and how each temakh's Deceived is likely to find artistic seba, without limiting what such seba actually can do?

                4) Are the illustrations in each temakh section a sample of a Deceived who belongs to that temakh, or somehow a representation of the temakh themselves?
