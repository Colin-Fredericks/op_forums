                                Changeling
                                The first of the True Fae began as a tribe of early humans who came to Faerie, making a pact with the Wyrd for immortality and power at the cost of their humanity.

                                Demon
                                The God-Machine is the engine that produces and maintains the Mortal World. It's Infrastructure is ultimately the infrastructure of the physical world itself and the God-Machine has influence across the entire physical universe, though its power is somewhat less notable in the various Otherworlds wherein other entities such as the Chthonic Gods and the Wyrd serve a similar role.

                                Geist​
                                A ghost who Passes On and a Sin-Eater who achieves Catharsis rejoin the rest of their soul in Duat, which typically don't undergo judgement until after this transpires.

                                Mage
                                The Time Before would be borderline incomprehensible to a modern human, even a Mage. It operated according to very different physics and metaphysics, such as the spirit of a thing being part of the the thing itself. There was no such thing as an individual 'soul' back then, nor was there any clear distinction between the Anima Mundi, Oneiros and Temenos.
                                The Watchtowers are purely a post-fall phenomenon. The Oracles weren't Mages of the Time Before, they were probably the first Mages of the Fallen World to successfully Ascend in defiance of the Exarchs. Mages were a lot rarer in those iterations of the Fallen World that existed before their Ascension and were basically pathless, which among other things meant that they had no way to summon Supernal Beings and their Arcana were all Common.

                                Mummy
                                Duat is where the soul (or, at least, what Mages call the 'soul') of a person goes after death and the Shades of Duat are actual souls. The souls that successfully pass judgement Ascend to the Supernal, while those that fail are stripped of their memories by Shezmu and reincarnated. Souls devoured by Ammut are destroyed.

                                Promethean
                                The Seer was the first human being and all humans can ultimately trace their lineage back to Prometheans who achieved the New Dawn. Most of the earliest Prometheans were Extempores.

                                Vampire
                                The heart of VII is the Akhud, but the organisation accepts many vampires outside of that clan. It also operates according to a very different ideology and structure than that which is presented in the First Edition VII book, with Shaddad in particular not really being a thing.
                                The Pijavica were to the Gangrel what the Hollow Mekhet are to the 'baseline' Mekhet.
                                The Traditores were the Ventrue. The Ventrue have no closer tie to the Julii than any other Clan-... They just both happen to have Dominate.

                                Werewolf​
                                Uratha are something akin to Hosts of Urfarah, but sanctified by Luna and tied to humans, rather than wolves. The 'soul-shard' of Urfarah that lies within an Uratha 'reincarnates' after death into a new Uratha of the same initial Auspice undergoing their First Change, though there is often a period wherein the soul-shard is 'discarnate'. This means that there is a maximum number of Uratha that can exist at any point in time. Sometimes these soul-shards attach to wolves instead and become Adarusharu.


                                Raksi plays Peek-a-boo for keeps. ~ nalak42
