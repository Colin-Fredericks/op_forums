                                Okay, so Integrity and its equivalent traits for each splat set a precedent of rules that PCs are supposed to follow. I don't think this thread will move unless we settle for a relative value of Übermensch.

                                We can hypothesize ways for PCs to abandon the need for Integrity traits, but the canon methods for that (e.g. changelings becoming True Fae at Wyrd 10 and Clarity 0) distance them from our world, be it physically and/or mentally. Aren't Übermenschen supposed to embrace this world as it is and not run away from its challenges?

                                Now that I think about it, since Legacy Attainments don't risk Wisdom loss in 2ED, and the Mastigos are all about confronting your flaws and self-determination, a Legacy associated with that Path might be the closest we can get to an Over-Man in the strictest sense.

                                    You all have a limited grasp on the superman concept of neitzsche. EVERYONE can attain or strive to attain the superman. The first thing one must understand is the 3 stages before one can even begin to transform into the superman. First is the camel, the beast of burden performing everyones will. Next you become the lion, after you transcend into your own power and slaughter the dragon with golden scales known only as "thou shalt" (Nietzsche wasn't a fan of christianity, rather referring to priests as the preachers of death in the book "thus spoke zutharusta"). Only after you have slain "thou shalt" can you become a child again, as you are now able to look at the world with new eyes and question everything on your own. Once you rise above that, through a superior set of morals and living can you become the superman. You become the superman through suffering and hard work. One should never take the easy path in life as that only produces the last man.

                                    By this token Lycoan from forsaken's hunting grounds may fit. He has transcended the simple morality of the forsaken society and has quite literally risen to god status.
