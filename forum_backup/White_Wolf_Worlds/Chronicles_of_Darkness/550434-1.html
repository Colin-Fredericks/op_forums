                                            Originally posted by Lian View Post


                                            You seem to the only person interpretting all of night in this game as a shadow. RAW does not say all of Night is single shadow. That's you.


                                            I'm going to preface this with stating I don't think this power should encompass the entire night side of the planet.

                                            BUT: "RAW does not say all of night is a single shadow. That's you." is REALLY shitty reasoning. As a matter of face, RAW not speaking as to whether night is a shadow means that night is a shadow because a lack of statement to the contrary means it defaults to how reality works. The way reality works is that standing outside at night puts you in the shadow of Earth. You can make all sorts of OTHER arguments -- night isn't shadow in Beast because Beast and it's powers work off of thematic words and thematically night is Night not Shadow or whatever. That's fine. But that argument is in addition to and in spite of the fact that nighttime is the Earth's shadow. Stating this isn't the case because RAW never says it is does some incredibly wonky stuff with every other game, since now RAW has to explicitly confirm scientific facts for those facts to be present in a game. For instance, note that vehicles don't have an "average/maximum range" in their statlines, which means they can drive forever now.
