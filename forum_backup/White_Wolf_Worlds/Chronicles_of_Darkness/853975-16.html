                    Here are the Numinous Form Traits/Aeons that I've got so far. Any opinions on them would be welcome. Also, ideas for more, please. In particular, some ideas on how to handle Power/Finesse/Resistance increasing Traits would be great.

                    Flight (O - OOOOO)

                    The demigod has the ability to take to the air, becoming able to fly at a Speed equal to his Speed*Flight dots. This ability may manifest through great feathery wings, whipping winds that carry the demigod or as a psionic aura around him.

                    Crushing Blow (OO)
                    The demigod’s strength in battle is unmatchable by mortals. When making an attack (armed or unarmed) while in Numinous Form, add the demigod’s Luminance to the damage dealt. This damage will be of the same type as would be dealt otherwise (a knife would deal extra Lethal, while fists would add Bashing).

                    Many Limbs (O-OOO)
                    The demigod has multiple limbs, perhaps like the Hindu gods or maybe more in the vein of a spider-deity. Regardless, for every dot in this Form Trait that the demigod possesses, he has an extra pair of arms which can be used to aid in his endeavours. For example, a demigod with 1 dot can hold a shield and a two-handed weapon and still have an extra hand free for other activities. For every pair of arms that the demigod uses on one endeavour (swinging a sword, prying open a door, climbing, grappling etc), he gains +1 to Strength for that purpose. Extra limbs can also help the demon defend himself against an attack. For every pair of limbs he has ‘unoccupied’, the demigod gains +1 to Defense.

                    Armour (O-OOOOO)
                    The demigod’s Numinous Form has some form of armour or protection. Perhaps it manifests as simple metal armour, perhaps as a chitinous carapace, but regardless, the demigod gains 2/1 armour for every point he buys in this Form Trait.

                    Integral Weapons (O-OOOOO)
                    The demigod’s Numinous Form manifests complete with weapons, perhaps claws and teeth, perhaps a magnificent sword. Regardless, the demigod has a weapon which deals 1 Lethal damage for every point invested in this Form trait. If the weapon is something that could be removed from the demigod’s possession such as a sword or knife it vanishes upon leaving his hand and can be re-summoned with a roll of Luminance + Resolve as a Reflexive action.
                    This Trait can be purchased multiple times to represent different weapons.

                    Swift (OO)
                    The demigod is inhumanly swift, both in movement and reaction. The demigod gains +3 to Initiative and adds (Luminance*2) to his Speed while in Numinous Form.

                    Charmspeak (OO)
                    The demigod’s words are endowed with a supernatural charm, resonating with the allure of the divine. Add the demigod's Luminance to all social rolls. Trying to use this on a supernatural being invokes a Clash of Wills. More than one demigod has used this, in conjunction with Halysis, to make it big as a singer or pop star.

                    Aquatic (O)
                    The demigod's Numinous Form is perfectly adapted to surviving underwater. The demigod may move a this full Speed while underwater in Numinous Form and can breathe underwater. In addition, cold and pressure as a result of depth have no effect on him, although he has no protection against these from other sources. He can still be crushed in a car crusher or freeze in the Arctic, for example.

                    Sight beyond Sight (OOO)
                    The demigod’s senses surpass mundane limitations such as lack of light or faintness of sound, allowing him to perceive the world around him no matter his conditions. The demigod may add his Luminance to all dice pools dedicated to perception and may ignore any Conditions which would impair his senses.

                    Venomous (O-OOOO)
                    The demigod’s Numinous Form secretes some kind of venom or poison. This can be attached to another Numinous Form trait which allows the demigod to do damage (such as the Integral Weapons Trait) or not, in which case the poison is delivered by a separate method such as a poisonous tail which deals Bashing damage. This Trait delivers a poison of Toxicity equal to the dots spent on it.

                    Noxious Breath (OOO)
                    Prerequisite: Venomous
                    Instead of injecting his poison directly, the demigod may instead opt to deliver it by exhaling a cloud of poisonous gas which lingers for (Luminance) rounds in a radius of 5 yards around the demigod.

                    Healing Hands (OOO)
                    While in Numinous Form and touching a wounded mortal or animal, the demigod may roll (Presence + Resolve + Luminance) and heal the subject of up to successes Lethal or Bashing damage. Healing Aggravated damage requires an Exceptional success. This may only be used a number of times in a scene equal to (½ Willpower). Once these are exhausted, each use costs 1 Potentia.

                            Here's a preliminary draft of the Following of the Morningstar. I'm not at all sure about the Emblems. I think that they might be far too broad, but I'll leave that up to you.

                            The Following of the Morningstar
                            The Fallen
                            The Black-Winged
                            The Exiles
                            Introduction
                            Ostensibly inspired by the famous Paradise Lost - which they claim was written by the very first of their Following as a metaphorical chronicle of His war against their greatest enemy, the Creator - the Following of the Morningstar believes that all demigods are (or were) angelic beings which were cast from some kind of Heaven to Earth (or perhaps left voluntarily) after rebelling against the Creator. Exactly what they rebelled for the sake of and what they were cast out for depend on who you ask within the Following but one thread runs true: it was unjust, for not only did God (or Jehovah or Allah or the Great Spirit) take their power and their position from them, He robbed them of their very identities. He did not allow them even a chance to redeem themselves, instead simply casting them from His sight and mind. Many say that He could not kill them, as He had created them to be immortal, so He did the next best thing and made it so that there was next to no chance that they would ever recover themselves.

                            Philosophy
                            The core tenets of the Following of the Morningstar are thus:
                            • Authority is only legitimate when earned. Most of the Exiles take this to mean that the Most High is unworthy of his rulership of Creation, as demonstrated by their unjust exile. God is corrupt, they say, and He was corrupt long before He damned us. More directly, this philosophy dictates the transfer and holders of power within the Following. Competition and rivalry are rife within the power structures of the Exiles, although most channel this to productive ends, attempting to prove that they are worthy of greater power.
                            • Unworthy authority should be overthrown. In many ways, this is the very heart of the Following. It is their drive, to tear down those who have grown fat on the tribute of their subjects. It is also the reason that the Following is so unstable, with regional leaders rising to power, upsetting someone or other and within a year a charismatic rebel has raised a force against them. Such transfers of power are mostly bloodless, but too often the entrenched power digs his heels in and ignites a far more dire situation.
                            • Both our history and future can be uncovered. The Fallen hope that those of their Following who have undergone the Second Ascension to make war at the gates of Heaven again have remembered their true selves and have left messages in the dust and writings of ancient times. They hope to one day divine a pattern in the religious artefacts and texts of the world, a common thread that will allow them to work out what they can do here on Earth to aid their comrades in the heavens. Some even believe that the Morningstar himself lies out there on Earth somewhere, entombed and waiting for his followers to find him.
                            • The Second Ascension is a return to war. The Fallen regard the Second Ascension with a mix of fear, apprehension and hope. They believe that returning to the heavens means a return to a seemingly unwinnable war against the Creator. They believe that it is their duty to undergo that transition, but also realize that even if they win, which is by no means certain, they may not be able to return to Earth again.
                            Organisation
                            As an organisation, the Following of the Morningstar is fractious and decentralised, the largest single power structures covering at most a city or a group of towns. Given the relative rarity of demigods, such ‘Circles’ are usually composed of between one or two dozen demigods in a kind of informal council. The only real formal position of power that the Exiles recognise is that of a chairman to these councils, responsible for deciding the agenda of meetings and breaking ties in votes. This position is known as the Lux Signifer, meaning ‘light bearer’, a reference to the Morningstar’s title of lightbringer.

                            In times of trouble, such as when another Following or a powerful Tera or Titan threatens the Circle, the Lux Signifer can be called upon by the Circle to take up the position of Vastator, destroyer, a military leader with the power to command the rest of the Circle for as long as the crisis persists.

                            Factions
                            Among the Followers of the Morningstar there are two major factions:
                            • The Lightbringers This faction believes that before anything else, the primary duty of the Exiled is to secure more troops for the war against the Creator, whether by finding a method of engendering the Ascension, converting demigods of other Followings to the true cause (especially the Vassals) or by seeking the Second Ascension with as much speed as possible.
                            • The Disciples of the Grigori This faction believes that the first order of business for the Following should be the recovery of their history and that of the War in Heaven. Many of this faction choose to leave established Circles and explore the world, seeking any rumour or hearsay of sites where the history of the War could be found. Others become scholars and theologians, examining the sacred texts of as many religions as they can, attempting to find the common threads that they are certain exist and hold the secrets they seek.
                            Emblems
                            The signature Miracles of the Following of the Morningstar are those which reject. They reject the current state of things, working towards something new. The Followers of the Morningstar tear down that which is abhorrent to them.

                            By contrast, the Black-Winged often have trouble with Miracles to support that which already is. When healing, they tend to drive out illness rather than bolster the body and when in battle they more often strike at their enemies directly with Miracles than improve an existing weapon.

                            Numinous Forms
                            In their Numinous Form, the Followers of Morningstar are a study in contrasts. They are beautiful and terrible, at once exquisite and horrific. They often manifest aspects of light and darkness, as well as mostly-humanoid forms which are nonetheless warped enough to be horrible. They might have the body of a statuesque and naked figure, wreathed in white flame and black smoke whose proud and cruel face bears yellow, slitted eyes and the fangs of a monstrous predator.

                            Stereotypes
                            • Vassals: Traitors and turncoats, doing the will of a corrupt god.
                            • Annunaki: You know, they say that the Annunaki and the Grigori might have been the same thing...
                            • Banners: Honorable, but ultimately misguided. I can respect that they want to earn the right to lead, but don’t they see that there’s more to it than that?
                            • Nine Circles: Deserters.
                            • Mortals: Rise against the establishment!

                                                            Another thought on Emblems, and one which was inspired by the Following of Reincarnates: perhaps they could be a ritual, passive bonus or set of rituals which are empowered by the Following's collective will and faith in their cause. Mechanically, they would probably be similar to Geist's Ceremonies or always-active Dread Powers and each would correspond to the given philosophy and dogma of the Following.

                                                            Examples might include:

                                                            Rebellion!
                                                            This Emblem belongs to the Followers of the Morningstar and acts over an extended period of time on a single institution, with the speed and size of organisation affected increasing with Status in the Following. Essentially, it causes those within the organisation to become dissatisfied with the current leadership. How serious this is again depends upon the power of the demigod that invoked the Emblem and the size of the organisation, with larger organisations being more susceptible to it. At the climax of the Emblem's power, the members of the organisation rise up against their superiors, whether with pitchforks torches or with a vote of no-confidence depends on how disliked the leader is.

                                                            Reincarnatory Divination
                                                            This rite is the very heart of the Following of Reincarnates, which uses it to divine the identity of a demigod's former life as a god. While Reincarnates act in accordance with their divine antecessor, they receive a minor bonus. (this one seems a bit weak. Any ideas to help with it?)

                                                            Spirit Crown
                                                            The primary Emblem of the Following of Embodiments, this rite allows them to rule as lords of the spirit world, allowing them to perceive and interact with spirits (and only spirits) in Twilight and giving them an effective Rank equivalent to their highest Domain. Note that this only applies when acting upon others. A high-rank Embodiment can inflict grievous harm to lesser spirits but a high-rank spirit cannot burn him with its touch.


                                                            Anyway, those are just some ideas and by no means are they fully-complete. If you think this is a better idea (or not) than the current thing, please speak up.
