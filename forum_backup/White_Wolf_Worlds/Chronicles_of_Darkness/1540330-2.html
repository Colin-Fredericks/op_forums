                            Originally posted by Yumlum View Post
                            I know that the book said the Population is as much as your story need but what is the actual population number in your game?
                            Earlier, I used to try to fit such numbers as well. But gradually, I realized that in my world, the populations would not be evenly distributed. I do not have numbers for all supernaturals, but for example for vampires, I came up with the following relation:

                            vampires in the city = city population/(10000+17.82*sqrt(city population))

                            where I would assume cities under 30k people would have 0 vampires. Averaged over all cities this leads to cca 1 in 35,000 in EU and 1 in 20,000 in the USA.

                            The reasoning is following: vampires tend to live in the cities. If the cities are large, the Prince holding dominion over the city will probably want to regulate the vampire populations so that the problems do not grow over his head. But even a small city of, say 30,000 people might sustain a few vampires who are reasonably secretive. I would assume they would use the freedom of not being under a strict control of a prince in a large city.

                            It also has a practical reason: I often play in Prague (1 million people), and I want to have non-trivial population in the city for the game to be interesting. On the other hand, if I would keep this ratio and play in large cities of 20 million people, there would be so many characters that my head would explode just thinking about the factions and interactions.
