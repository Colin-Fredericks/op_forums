        As far as I know, Mrs. Robinson has never appeared in fiction, per se, but she is confirmed as the maker of the Mixtape that appears in the Daeva clanbook, which is interesting in its own right as an introduction to kindred society from the perspective of a vampire in the Mission. She's given a short description in Blood and Smoke as well, in the section on the Mission.

        Count Dracula (not the Count, but rather a Gangrel who claims to be his heir) pops up in a few different places, including the Gangrel clanbook and the story Noblesse Oblige in the Strix Chronicle Anthology.

        Frances Black is the in-universe compiler of the documents making up the Mekhet clanbook, and features in many of the fiction pieces therein. She is also mentioned in some of the other clanbooks, but I don't think she makes an appearance.

        I honestly have no idea where Jenny Shakes comes from... it's possible she's a new character who will appear in some future book...

        The Mogul appears as an interviewee in the Ventrue clanbook. He introduces himself as Cyrus, but that seems to be just one of his many aliases (possibly his "business name").


        Incidentally, I really want to make an actual prop out of that mixtape, but there's no way I could pull off an appropriate voice. :P
