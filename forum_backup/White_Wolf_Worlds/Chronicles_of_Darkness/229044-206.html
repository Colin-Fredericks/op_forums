                                    In an attempt to make things a little less problematic, here is how I'm going to pitch it to my group:

                                    The World of Darkness is home to terrible things, most of them wrought by mortal hands, but what mortals do leaves lasting impressions on dreams. Urban legends drift into truth, nightmares turn real, and life imitates fiction, becomes legend, and the circle continues. Beasts and Heroes are both victims, trapped in their own cyclic narrative; they were each once mortals, and they were each exposed to the supernatural. For the Beasts, what they learned and found tormented them until they became like it. For Heroes, what they found terrified them that they have broken themselves trying to stay away from it - and destroy that which has become it. To them, the Beasts have switched sides. To the Beasts, however, in becoming the legend they have prevented something far worse, and in feeding, they protect other mortals from falling into the same terrible revelations that turned them into what they are today.

                                    so you have Beasts as people who saw the darkness and in order to halt the spread, became it, and try to hide it because they know the situation (supernatural stuff hiding itself) is not permanent, but that no one is prepared for it, and Heroes as people who saw the darkness for what it was and recoiled from it so violently that they want to destroy everything in it, but they can't. They come up against Beasts because they are dressed like the enemy and because Heroes are loose cannons: they will use their supernatural abilities publicly and dangerously - in their attempts to save lives, they will ruin lives instead.

                                    Beasts try to track down supernatural things and either help them be less destructive/hide them/prepare nearby mortals to face it.

                                            If there's space for both Astral Entities and Angels, then sure; include them both. But if you have to choose between them, please favor the Astral Entities over the Angels.

                                            In particular, it's worth noting that Demons are the one crossover that Beast deliberately doesn't cater to; and if you are doing a crossover featuring demons and/or the God-Machine, then Demon and/or God-Machine Chronicle will supply the necessary rules for Angels. Conversely, Astral Entities are likely to show up even in a Beast chronicle that doesn't feature any crossovers: if your Lair is anything at all like a mage's Oneiros, it's likely to have creatures in it; and those creatures would be Astral Entities by definition. As such, I would argue that their inclusion in the game is even more important than the inclusion of ghosts and spirits.

                                            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
