    The thing about Magic's color pie is that it's so archetypal that any sufficiently complex character will have aspects of all five colors. For example, look at vampire. The overall concept is based in black, with maybe some red in the form of Frenzy, but each Clan is a color. Daeva are passionate and physical, as red as they come. Gangrel are driven by instinct and connected with their animal natures, which is very much green. Mekhet are all about knowledge and secrets, they're definitely blue. Nosferatu are all about fear which is a big theme in black. And Ventrue are a perfect example of the negative side of white.

                                For me, Unchained are pretty clearly Red/Blue as a species. They want the ability to choose above all else (even Integrators, who want a better way of serving God than what they were given), and to act according to their hearts. They do so via subterfuge, planning, and the acquisition of knowledge. Inquisitors are heavy on the Blue, Saboteurs on the Red, Integrators have a tinge of White in their beliefs, and how much White and Black a Tempter has varies from Builder to Builder. Not one of them has a single leaf of Green, though-natural instinct, simply accepting the nature of things, and naivety is something an Unchained who survives finds utterly inexplicable.

                                            Genius: I'd say Blue/Red with Blue being Obligation and Red being madness.
