    It's been awhile since I've added any new form abilities to this thread, rl matters and the werewolf stuff demanding the majority of my attention.. but I do have two new ones for those of you who enjoy this thread Enjoy, and as always let me know what you think! Once again, there is another Modifcation that I sort of think is too powerful for a Modification.. yet at the same time it does what a Modification is supposed to do- amplify and exaggerate physical capabilities.

    ____

    Reaper Life-Scythes ( Angelic Technology.)
    Sometimes the God-Machine requires living things to die in order to further a project; it is a simple, objective fact. This is achieved any number of ways; a family of four dying in a terrible premeditated car accident, a former angel is torn apart by packs of man-maggot things with steel edged mouthparts, or a loyal servant is merely cut into over and over again for hours- a long and agonizing death that requires an equal amount of the hapless mortal's life force and misery to power the appropriate Occult Matrix. Destroyers and Psychopomps armed with this form ability are terrifying harvesters who rip away the soul-sparks of those they kill- a remnant of that energy being left behind to lend vitality to the reapers responsible.

    Appearance: The Reaper Life-Scythes come in many forms ranging from techno-organic Praying Mantis-like forelimbs to traditional rune-engraved scythe blades replacing the wielder's hands entirely to diseased, bony sickles extending from one's forearms.

    Mechanics: The Scythes are a 3L weapon with a -1 penalty to Initiative that are capable of inflicting lethal damage upon supernatural entities. These blades can also be partially retracted somewhat- becoming 2L weapons, losing their Initiative penalty in the process. In addition, the Scythes are designed to rip away the life energy of those they've wounded; every two points of damage inflicted by the Scythes the angel regains one point of corpus lost to lethal damage - though the Scythes cannot repair aggravated damage in this fashion.

    note: Demons can retain this form ability, and it is one of the very few that possesses even greater functionality than they did while still in direct connection with the God-Machine. No longer used to supply occult energies to the Machine, a Demon can make full use of the life harvesting powers of her scythes, regaining health on a 1:1 ratio with the damage she inflicts.


    Psionic Amplifier Nodes ( Angelic Modification)
    The brain is an incredible organ, it's potential is vast and it's complexity beautiful and mind numbing. Angels are an order of being higher than humans, so it stands to reason that their cognitive potential is even greater - shackled and constrained as it may be by the God-Machine. Sometimes though, the great Machine has use of such intellect and other more... physical benefits thereof. As such, Analysts are the most common angels to receive these enhancements in the sake of missions that require both tremendous processing power and, very unusual for Analysts, a means of interacting with their targets without making physical contact.

    Appearance: The Angel has several external brains along its sides while it's own brain is exposed, though all of these neural nodes are protected by nearly unbreakable membranes of psychic force, shielding them from the elements of the outside world.

    Mechanics: This Modification grants the angel a staggering +5 to rolls related to mental tasks, and grants him enough cognitive focus to perform two actions in a single turn, so long as they are separate actions ( such as performing a non combat action and a combat action). In addition, this grants the ability of telekinesis that functions much like the Telekinesis supernatural Merit, albeit with a +2 modifier regarding it's use.

    Note: Demons can retain this ability, though it's modifier for Intelligence rolls is reduced by one, and it loses the ability to perform two actions at once. It keeps it's telekinetic properties however.

                                ____________

                                Weapon Integration System (Angelic Modification)
                                Many angels are built or grown with a variety of " natural" weapons; some more esoteric than others. Sometimes, however, the situation at hand calls for a more mundane approach when it comes to the art of killing, or at times requires an unwitting pawn's handgun in order to implicate them in a murder for a specific Occult Matrix to fully actualize. Those angels with this ability are able to incorporate mortal weaponry into their biomechanical forms, utilizing these devices as fluidly as they can their own angelic abilities.

                                Appearance: The angel has several ports along it's body. When incorporating a mortal weapon a host of biomechanical nerves and wires emerge from a port and forcibly invade the material structure of the weapon as they anchor it inside the port, shifting it to a bio-ephemeral state for the duration of the merger.

                                Mechanics: The user of this ability is capable of merging a non-supernatural weapon into their angelic form by rolling Finesse + Resistance and spending a number of points of Essence equal to the object's size. Once merged said weapon essentially functions as a form ability and the user can operate the weapon with a +3 bonus due to this more intimate connection, though the angel in question can only possess a number of these "form abilities" equal to half their Rank ( so one if Exampiel is Rank 1, two if Rank 4 and so on.). The weapons can remain merged with the user's body for a number of days equal to Rank before their biomechanical form rejects the foreign objects, ejecting them back into the world as if they were never part of the angel's form to begin with,- with an expenditure of a point of Essence the angel may retain the object for an additional day.however( though some rumors exist that particularly powerful angels leave a piece of themselves within these weapons should they be bonded to said angel for long enough- creating supernaturally enhanced tools.. but this has never been verified.). Lastly the angel may remove the weapon from their form at any time with a Finesse + Resistance roll and a point of Essence.

                                Note: This ability can be retained by demons, though they can only integrate two weapons into their Demonic Forms, and may only retain these "form abilities" for a number of days equal to Premium, with the rule of spending Essence to extend the duration being replaced with Aether.

                                                    Thought on something that is more useful to a Demon, but an Angel still might find a use for it.

                                                    Dominion Processor (Angelic Process)
                                                    Angels already are granted control over various aspects of reality related to their mission. There are situations, however, where a broader range of control is warranted. In these cases, the God-Machine equips Its Angels with additional reality manipulation engines, above and beyond what they normally have access to.
                                                    Appearance: The Angel has an obvious external processor that flickers with some representation of the force it manipulates when it is used.
                                                    Mechanics: The Angel gain access to another Influence with a rating equal to their Rank without having to trade in Numina or Manifestations. It must be in a single Influence, and cannot be split between 2 or more. It is otherwise treated as any other Influence they possess.
                                                    Note: Demons may retain this ability, fueling it with Aether as opposed to raw Essence, but it takes them time to restore it to full functionality. The Demon only has access to an effective Influence rating of half their Primum (round up). Additionally, using the Influence provokes a Compromise roll (though it gets a bonus if the Demon is using a lower Influence effect than what they are capable of - +1 per "level" lower than their maximum). Whenever a Demon increases their Primum, they can change what the Influence is for, if desired.
