                You want help with running an evil game? That's fine. Its trivially easy to not apply Integrity towards evil actions if you're a bad person. Integrity is designed to measure the shock and trauma you recieve, and how much dealing with the supernatural will change you. If you're playing characters with zero qualms about tormenting others, then simply don't roll Integrity. The section on Integrity again and again stresses that you should take individual personalities into account. Or use any other suggestions in this thread.

                The problem is that you are displaying a marked misunderstanding of not only how Integrity works, but sadism, psychopathy or in-group mentalities. Reduced empathy for others, true or not, has nothing to do with Integrity. Nothing. Integrity is not Morality. I repeat. Integrity does not measure morality. It measures how well you deal with supernatural trauma and revelations as well as how far you are willing to change in order to survive/cope with interacting with the supernatural. The anti-social behaviors you want to function have no relationship to how Integrity works. The two are completely separate issues. And that's not even touching on the fact that having psychopathic, sadistic, or sociopathic tendacies does not equate to anti-social behavior, like you are saying it does.


                Integrity measures how much of a toll your sense of self and mental stability has suffered.
                Humanity cares about empathy and relating to humans. But there's nothing wrong with playing low Humanity vampires - they can function in vampire society, and live hiding in human society well enough with just a single dot your choice of the right disciplines.
                Harmony doesn't care about empathy towards others. In fact, it FOSTERS the in-group, out-of-group mentality you want through seeing everyone not-pack as prey.
                Wisdom cares about control over magic, not empathy or treating others nicely. Its been discussed how cold, calculated killers actually would have high Wisdom, since they're so precise.
                A Promethean's pilgrimage cares about understanding humans and becoming mentally balanced, which can include a lack of empathy and a desire to inflict pain. Humanity isn't always nice.
                Clarity cares about avoiding the trauma you suffered before, and reinforcing the sense of self.
                Cover only cares about acting like the role you adopt. If you are playing the part of an anti-social killer, then your Cover goes up.
                Satiety doesn't care about what actions you take. Its your supernatural fuel.


                Only one of the 2e Integrity-equivalents cares about the evil behaviors you want in your game. The others simply don't measure it at all. All the games, in fact, allow "evil" in one manner or another as playable entites.

                                            As a high-functioning psychopath, I can provide some personal insight on the issue. One of the best ways to approximate my own psychological makeup in CoD terms is to ignore and discard any and all Breaking Points that are related to harming others or being exposed to their suffering, especially if the subject can reasonably assume it was necessary or really, really convenient for one's welfare, to protect the persons and things one cares for (psychopaths can form love and friendship bonds, even if they are shallower and more conditional than for other people), or to advance one's vision of the greater good (psychopaths can have ideals).

                                            At most, if the subject has a well-developed, not-empathy-based sense of responsibility or a perfectionism streak, you can have a BP if harm to others would result from one's gross incompetence or sloppiness. Or it might be a result of violating one's ideals in other ways: e.g. if I care a lot about science or art, I may feel bad harming an important scientist or artist or letting such a person come to harm by my inaction, even if I do not usually give a damn about strangers.

                                            There is simply no conceivable circumstance I can realistically picture myself suffering a PTSD-like backlash from harming others or witnessing their suffering, up to and including killing someone in cold blood, if I am genuinely persuaded it was somehow necessary or inevitable. Let psychopath characters replace BP related to harming others or witnessing their suffering with others related to harming the self, betraying their self-image, and other issues based on the rest of their personality. E.g. for someone like me, a case could certainly be made for dramatic failures or violation of my own ideals being a BP.
