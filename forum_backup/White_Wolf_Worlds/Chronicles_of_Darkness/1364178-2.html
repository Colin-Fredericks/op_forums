                Originally posted by Tzauri View Post
                Here's two other possibilities I can see for sane hero.

                First, moderation and principal. Much like a Hunter, a Hero could cleave to an idea. Their Hero senses tell them when someone has suffered by a beast's hand. They try to cause more good than harm. They don't unduly risk others. They're not glory hounds by nature. And perhaps, even, they allow a Beast who is trying to minimize the harm they inflict to live, perhaps even seeing them as a person. The one that just ripped an arm off that high school student? Yeah, he's ending up six feet under.

                Second, the Hero as a judge. A Hero possibly who tries to convince Beasts to moderate their actions. To be as human. And they strike if they refuse their advances. A Hero could even be an ally to a Beast who's been trying to moderate their actions. Perhaps a Hero even visits the Beast's own depredations upon them if they insist, but the Hero isn't quite ready to swing a sword... unless, of course, the Anakim needs to pushed ahead of them. Perhaps even a system for asserting their authority over a beast to get gifts. Or at least, perhaps, the Hero can recover Integrity for free that way, as the idea they can peacefully get that.

                Or, as one more idea for a system change? A Hero who works peacefully can recover Integrity over 10, and perhaps gain powers the higher it goes. Heroes that slay Beasts are like Heracles or Odysseus, strong but fatally flawed. Heroes of Integrity are different. They may be a little more than human - they might have had to slay an unredeemable Beast. But they more than make up for it with their Wisdom. They're more than able to see past fear. Nightmares have no purchase on them. A Hero of Integrity is David facing Goliath - throwing warning shots before their faith or wisdom delivers a killing blow. They tame dragons, rather than slay them.

                In short, you have Beasts that cling to their Life and Beasts that revel in their Legend. Why not have Heroes who act the same way?
                This post has me thinking along a different line: namely, Heroes and their like as the ChroD counterpart to Hunter: the Reckoning. The point of similarity is that both are people who have become supernaturally aware of monsters, and have to decide how they'll respond to that.

                Heroes are like the Zealous: they fight the monsters: avengers, defenders, and judges. Oracles are like the Visionary, seeking to understand the monsters. Carrying the parallel further, maybe there's another type, akin to HtR's Merciful (innocents, martyrs, and redeemers) who see their role as minimizing the damage that Beasts do.

                Hmm…

                Or perhaps a more correct analogy is that Heroes are Waywards, Oracles are Visionaries, and the third time would be Hermits. Those three Creeds were defined as the ones who were most directly influenced by Vision, a supernatural awareness of the monsters. If Heroes are Waywards (relatively speaking), then some of the more sane mindsets for, well, not exactly Heroes but others of their kind might be analogous to Avengers, Defenders, and Judges.
