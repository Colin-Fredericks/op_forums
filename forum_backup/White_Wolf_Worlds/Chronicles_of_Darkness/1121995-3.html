    I don't see how I would appeal to any clan, but I'm superficially a bit closer to Mekhet, so I'll go with that.

    If we were talking about covenants working in mortal interests, I'd pick the Carthians, no runner up. I don't know if I would extend the same standards to vampires though. If I last more than a few months though, I expect that I'd start to justify my own continued existence in which I'd be inclined to do the same for other vampires and that's when I'd decide that we need government.

    The Lancea et Sanctum could be tempting if they lead with the history, relics and occult stuff with some miracles to demonstrate their significance, especially if they're something like the Westminister Creed; even when I was religious, I became disillusioned with the ritualistic trappings that I commonly perceived within my own sect. I might feed using the internet a lot of the time in which case there would probably be a psychotic Spear somewhere thinking "Splendid! We can groom him on the sly into a Craigslist mass murderer to pick off degenerates like him as an example so that the mortals keep it in their pants outside marriage!". But then where I live, they're probably propping up Invictus more than doing their own job.

    I'd like to chat to acolytes and try to learn about their ways and ideas, but I wouldn't accept an invitation to join their ceremonies or spy on them unless I was pushed by own covenant. But then, if my own covenant wanted me out, they could say "You're friendly with the Crones. Get closer to them and tell us what they're up to,". Then the Circle would pretend they believed that I was interested in joining them and sacrifice me*. Maybe if I just make my reasons for not being trustworthy to the Circle open and clear from the start, that wouldn't happen.

    It would take a good sales pitch get me into join the Ordo Dracul, which isn't their style. If I was embraced into it, I think I'd be less inclined to leave it than most covenants though.

    I'd only be in Invictus for convenience, but they're good at making themselves the convenient option (as a member or a tolerated outsider pleb).

            Clan: Mekhet or possibly Ventrue
            Reason: I tend to be the quiet kind of person, usually. I like to watch others, observe them, analyse things. I love learning stuff. That whole part of Mekhet just feels right to me. On the other hand, I'm the kind of person that people listen to, for whatever reason. I'm not the most charismatic person in the sense that I have a forceful personality, but I guess I'm mostly just very reasonable, and I'm very pragmatic. I don't really want to be a leader of any kind, but I'm the type of person who'll grab it fast as hell if I don't think there's anyone qualified for it, because I can't stand leaders who're doing a bad job of it (assuming it's a leadership position that I'd be qualified for). I also love to debate and argue, and have recently discovered that I like talking in front of people (going to a Toastmaster's club and everything). I think those are qualities that might make a Ventrue take interest.

            Covenenat: Ordo Dracul of the Carthian Movement
            Reason: I love politics, new ideas, I'm very progressive. The Carthian Movement feels like a good fit. I also like science, and the idea of studying what it means to be a Vampire and to transcend undeath is also extremely fascinating.
