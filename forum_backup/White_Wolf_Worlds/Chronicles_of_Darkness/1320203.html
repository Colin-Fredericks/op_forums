      1 - ‘Bear Growl’ Pack

      This is cave bear primitives cult run by Predator Kings under patronage of cave bear Totem. Think of them as Wendols from 13th Warrior movie or Eaters of Dead book. Pack is stealing human children and raise them ‘as bears’ in the caves that are heart of it’s territory.

      Pack Aspirations:

      Short-Term – Raid human settlements
      Medium-Term – Clean area of Forsaken werewolves
      Long-Term – Restore the cave bears to being - May try breeding of brown bears or possessing by their Totem, for example

      Pack Touchstones:

      Physical – ‘Den’ of pack, previous cave bears cave
      Spiritual – Skull of the Totems original ( see below )

      Pack Complications:

      Stone Age Culture – Pack members are living like humans before any technology beside stone and bones tools.

      Hunting Nature: Wolf

      Pack Merits:
      Den •••• – ‘Bear’s Den’, cave system
      Directed Rage ••••
      Magnanimous Totem •••
      Territorial Advantage •••

      Secondary Packmembers

      ( Standard are 5/4/3 pools, but I changed them because of this packs profile. )

      Physical: 6
      Social: 4
      Mental: 2

      Totem: Paw in Darkness

      <Echo of roar of the raged bear in cave>
      Totem Type: Hunting Bear Spirit ( Cave Bear )
      Totem Rank: 4
      Background: Paw is prehistoric being, first created when Neanderthals killed local cave bears colony. By death of biggest male, the spirit awoken, taking it’s form. It’s survived and roused in power when first humans took the bones and relicts from its physical bodies, starting to worship it as ancient primordial god. In a year or two, first local Uratha that later become Predator Kings found the spirit – and sign the bloody compact. Werewolves will bring the spiritual hunting offerings to Paw, and it will grant ‘the might of 10 bears’ for them. It’s the same now from the millennia. Pure kidnap humans and Wolf-Blooded to run primitive cult in caves nearby that’s worship generate Essence for the Paw to survive all this years. Spirit lord also guide and watch over local brown bears population, like his own grandchildren.

      Description: Paw physically is great cave bear male – looking like weighting over 500 kg - only shrouded in darkness ( like permanent shadow ) and dirty in blood of it’s victims.

      Storytelling Hints: Paw understand that time of his kind is over. He is using Pure and humans as Essence generators, but in secret he loathes them. However, he is not stupid and knows that without constant spring of worship, he would perish quickly. He then transfer his inner rage on the civilized settlements of humans he destroys with his Uratha adopted children.

      Aspirations:
      To resurrect glory of bear-kind
      Destroy human civilization

      Rank: 4
      Attributes: Power 12, Finesse 8, Resistance 10
      Willpower: 10
      Essence: 25
      Initiative: 18
      Defense: 8
      Speed: 25
      Size: 10
      Corpus: 20
      Influences: Bear ••••, Hunting •••, Darkness ••
      Manifestations: Gauntlet Breach, Image, Materialize, Posses, Twilight Form
      Numina: Awe, Blast (Claw marks on victim ), Host Jump, Might*, Pathfinder, Regenerate, Shadow Form**
      Ban: Cannot rest in man-made den ( Paw is nocturnal creature of darkness - and Helios make Shadow Realm more dormant in day - so rest means in the light of day hours. Thanks nofather for that idea! )
      Bane: Stone weapons of the Neatherdals that killed it’s physical original

      New Numina

      Might
      Spirit can blessed itself – or other being it is touching – with supernatural strength. After activating power with touch, next roll based on Strength or Power of the target use this trait multiplied by the Rank of spirit. Each blessing costs 3 Essence and takes one action of the spirit if made on other being. If spirit is Totem for the pack, it can bless pack members from the other side.

      Shadow Form
      Spirit can take form of living shadow, indistinguishable from normal lights effect. It became two dimensional and moves on any surface just like shadow could. Change cost 1 Essence and is instant. Change back to more physical form cost the same.

      Uratha

      Alpha
      Seer
      Tracker

      Wolf-Blooded

                  2 - ‘VIP Experiences Inc.’ Pack


                  This is a now human cult, a Storm Lord pack that was wiped out by conflict with the Pure. Lacking Uratha guidance, the totem is driving the human and wolf-blooded members toward a maddening behaviour. The Mystery Cult ended up attracting a possessor Lust spirit; the original Pack rescued them as part of the process of bringing the Totem to heel.


                  Pack Aspirations:
                  Short-Term – Indulge desires, specially those of a sexual nature.
                  Medium-Term – Expand the ranks of the pack.
                  Long-Term – None, lack of purpose is the worst part of losing the Uratha. Zia has a vague hint of what it wants but nothing it has shared with the cult.

                  Pack Touchstones:
                  Physical – A sex-club in a discrete neighborhood. Here the cult activities take place.
                  Spiritual – A lock of braided hair, the main symbol used to bind Zia in service to the original pack.

                  Pack Complications:
                  No goals – Pack members are living day to day, nearly like junkies, focused on the next orgy or encounter.
                  Old habits – The current Pack leader is the former assistant of the founder of the Mystery Cult. She has grown the cult repeating the things her master did (enhanced by the Totem powers) but these activities will soon attract notice.

                  Hunting Nature:
                  Human

                  Pack Merits:
                  Magnanimous Totem 4


                  Totem: Zia, Overwhelming Passion

                  Totem Type: Lust Spirit
                  Totem Rank: 3
                  Background: Zia was born of the indulgence of the elite, but it became quite damaging to the humans in the sex-club that it used to inhabit. Brought to heel by the Alpha of the original pack, it survived the pack's destruction by possessing a human host.

                  Description: Zia is a pink-reddish translucent bulge of flesh contorted in vaguely suggestive shapes, but can produce male or female genitalia at will.

                  Storytelling Hints: Zia wants to use the cult as a recruiting ground for its pack, the more the cult grows (and it is a very attractive proposition to join it), the more Zia's power rises. Zia misses the spiritual component that is lacking in the current configuration of the pack, but finds the Uratha too violent for its tastes. To fulfill that need, it is trying to attract supernatural-touched humans as part of the cult. However, the current human leader of the cult has different expectations of the members, some friction will arise from this.

                  Aspirations: Satisfy sexual urges.

                  Totem Points: 21

                  Rank: 3
                  Attributes: Power 8, Finesse 8, Resistance 5
                  Willpower: 10
                  Essence: 20
                  Initiative: 13
                  Defense: 8
                  Speed: 21
                  Size: 5
                  Corpus: 10
                  Influences: Lust 3
                  Manifestations: Twilight Form, Gauntlet Breach, Possess, Claim
                  Numina: Aggressive Meme, Drain, Emotional Aura, Host Jump, Implant Mission, Rapture
                  Ban: Zia must be in the presence of someone indulging in lust every 24 hours, otherwise it becomes morose and lethargic losing it’s defense.
                  Bane: A human that has been voluntarily celibate for a year.
                  Totem Advantage: Dexterity +1, Stamina +1, Merit: Striking Looks 2



                  Mystery Cult Initiation (VIP Experiences Inc.)

                  Entry to the cult has stringent criteria set by the current leader Marina, so there are few cult initiates and even fewer actual pack members among them. The cult offers to connect you with the "In" crowd; but the higher one rises, the more it demands dedication to Zia's aims (at this moment frequent sexual encounters to be feed, and searching for humans with supernatural abilities, which has been unsuccessful due to the lack of mystical talent in the cult).

                  Cultists: Former sex worker, vicarious assistant, hopeful starlet, jaded celebrity, aspiring model.

                  Rating Rank Description (Shares benefits of the Status Merit)

                      3 - ‘K4b4l’ Pack

                      An experiment run between the Iron Masters and the Free Council, a hybrid of cabal and pack, more of the latter than the former though.

                      Pack Aspirations:
                      Short-Term – Strengthen the totem.
                      Medium-Term – Grow in resources by absorbing the Packs and Cabals of Iron Masters and Free Councillors.
                      Long-Term – Transform into a Lodge / Create a Legacy.

                      Pack Touchstones:
                      Physical – A reformed warehouse, fitted with comfortable living quarters and quite secure.
                      Spiritual – The server that is KPX’s near permanent fetter.

                      Pack Complications:
                      Something to prove – Can this union work? Especially with limited membership and some traditionalist of each splat opposed to dealing with "others".
                      Different objectives – Iron Masters and Free Councillors have divergent expectations for the experiment, for the werewolves this is a pack that feeds of the mages resonance and resources, for the mages this is an experiment on inter-species cooperation and the potentials of techno-spirituality.

                      Hunting Nature:
                      Human

                      Pack Merits:
                      Den 5
                      Safe Place 5


                      Totem: KPX


                      Totem Type: Magath
                      Totem Rank: 1
                      Background: KPX is a completely artificial spirit, in the sense that it did not appear naturally, but it was created by forcefully mixing diverse resonances associated to computing and finance to create a subservient being.

                      Description: A silvered sphere with flashing currency symbols on its surface.

                      Storytelling Hints: Mages and Werewolves have different sets of prospects of how this could work, KPX itself is not strong enough to have opinions beyond what its nature demands. Some members think the pack could also transform into a bridge for diverse technology related creatures (things like Unfleshed Prometheans or Demons could fit easily here).

                      Aspirations: Make money through IT.

                      Totem Points: 5

                                          4 - ‘Palisades Park’ Pack

                                          This is a generational pack, that has been working with the totem for over 40 years, Iron Masters all of them. They either work at the mall administration, security, or in one of its stores; all Werewolves and some Wolf-Blooded live in the surrounding area. The pack claims the mall itself as territory but also the adjacent high income neighborhood that grew around it.

                                          Pack Aspirations:
                                          Short-Term – Increase the area security.
                                          Medium-Term – Relocate the pack members closer to the mall.
                                          Long-Term – Cater to every need that the people of the area could have (Hotel, Hospital, and Gym are in the roadmap).

                                          Pack Touchstones:
                                          Physical – The mall (including its fenced parking lot) itself.
                                          Spiritual – The receipt of first sale made in the mall.

                                          Pack Complications:
                                          Security issues – Someone entered the mall and killed a pizza parlor dispatcher in a murder-suicide. This rattled customers and businesses both, but how to improve the perception of security without being an obstacle to operations?
                                          Funding – While quite a prosperous Pack, the expansion plans are very expensive and will tie assets for a while. Shady characters are offering cheap loans but the pack is looking for alternate options.
                                          New homes – Most, but not all, pack members live within walking distance of the mall; the Pack wants to buy an old residential building in the area to offer cheap housing to their members, but one of the residents is a supernatural that does not want to sell, can he be convinced? Must he be dealt with violence? And how did this guy came to live in their territory without them knowing?

                                          Hunting Nature:
                                          Human

                                          Pack Merits:
                                          Dedicated Locus 5
                                          Den 5
                                          Resources 5
                                          Safe Place 5
                                          Staff 5


                                          Totem: Palisades, Prosperity Through Commerce


                                          Totem Type: Multiplex Spirit
                                          Totem Rank: 3
                                          Background: This is the spirit of the oldest and one of the largest malls in the city. The mall is not in the outskirts, the adjacent high income neighborhoods grew around the mall as part of the effect of its construction.

                                          Description: A perpetually smiling attractive female made of white plaster dressed in professional attire mounted on (and merged with) a Segway, they have a blinking earpiece, the mall logo instead of irises and every so often they incite the interlocutor to acquire wares or services from the mall.

                                          Storytelling Hints: The pack has carefully cultivated the influences of the spirit, they want it to embody all the region qualities they like, but not too much of anything. "We want you to be important but not important enough to overshadow us". So far it has worked because the totem was not self aware enough to aspire more, but the growth in Rank gave a commensurate change in mindset, the Totem aims for higher office and the Borough area spirit seems a very tempting target.

                                          Aspirations: The mall must grow.

                                          Totem Points: 21



                                          Rank: 3
                                          Attributes: Power 6, Finesse 9, Resistance 6
                                          Willpower: 10
                                          Essence: 20
                                          Initiative: 15
                                          Defense: 6
                                          Speed: 20
                                          Size: 5
                                          Corpus: 11
                                          Influences: Commerce 5
                                          Manifestations: Twilight Form, Discorporate, Reaching, Shadow Gateway
                                          Numina: Drain, Innocuous, Pathfinder, Seek; 2 Numina exchanged for Influence dots

This includes, but is not limited to, physical objects like old-fashioned advertising materials (e.g., vintage billboards or brochures), obsolete technology (like old cash registers or outdated mall directories), or even digital artifacts representing past commercial trends.​

                                                  Not a full pack concept, but a pretty nifty totem idea I wanted to share. This may or may not have sprung from a particularly weird dream I recently had.

                                                  The Flesh Cradle

                                                  Instead of worn tile, there’s a thick layer of seemingly human skin veiling the interior surfaces of the hot tub. It gently pulsates as if breathing through some unseen orifice. Undefinable muscles begin undulating beneath the surface. The flesh swells and parts, tendrils extended as if for an embrace. Enter, it beckons. Be made whole. Never be alone and broken again.

                                                  Concept: Rejuvenation through visceral rebirth.

                                                  Aspiration: Render whole all flesh

                                                  Description: The Flesh Cradle has long feasted on feelings of healing, life, renewal, and the dysphoria of a body changed, reconstructed, forever altered. It acts as a sort of reverse scavenger, trailing those who leave broken, mutilated flesh in their wake, so it can offer its embrace to those surviving violent encounters. Thus it’s no surprise that it may willingly offer itself as Totem to Uratha packs. In addition to an ever fresh supply of potential “children”, the Cradle is fascinated by the mercurial flesh of Luna’s children. The pack members in turn receive the Cradle’s prodigious healing gifts, allowing them to conserve Essence and recover from injuries even their inhuman metabolisms cannot otherwise easily mend. Experiencing the rapture of the embrace is just a fringe benefit.

                                                  When the Cradle embraces an organism, its corpus merges with the subject’s flesh, filling in wounds, purging toxins, stimulating regrowth. It claims these subject’s as its own, joined together like mother and child. The Cradle prefers to fully envelope its subjects, but with sufficient cajoling or offerings, it will be willing to merely embrace the part of the subject that needs reconstruction. However, it always extends the offer for the subject to remain, to become truly one with the Fleshy Cradle.

                                                  The experience of being released from the embrace can also be rather unsettling. The subject must “peel” themselves free from what was, temporarily, an extension of their own flesh. This takes no small amount of constitution. Subjects requiring extensive reconstruction or replacement body parts will not emerge quite the same as before; the spirit is versed in what the human body is supposed to look like and how it functions, but is a bit hazy on individual variation. Those released after an extended embrace are essentially born anew when they are finally expelled. If they ever emerge at all; those who approached the Cradle in violence are not afforded that privilege.

                                                  The Cradle does not speak, not with words, but in a more intimate and visceral manner. While in physical contact, a creature perceives the Cradle’s intent as a trickle of sensation, a rush of hormones, speaking directly to primal reflex rather than higher consciousness. A successful Empathy+Stamina roll may be required to interpret the nuances of these communications.

                                                  Totem Points: 15
                                                  Rank: 3
                                                  Attributes: Power 5, Finesse 5, Resistance 5
                                                  Willpower: 10
                                                  Essence: 15
                                                  Initiative: 10
                                                  Defense: 5
                                                  Speed: 1
                                                  Size: 10
                                                  Corpus: 15
                                                  Influences: Flesh 3
                                                  Manifestations: Gauntlet Breach, Materialize, Reaching, Shadow Gate
                                                  Numina: Fleshy Embrace, Rapture, Regenerate
                                                  Ban: If the Cradle has not embraced at least one creature in the last week, it cannot regain Essence until it does so. Pack members must spend a number of hours in its embrace per week equal to their dots in the Totem merit. Other pack members can make up for another member’s lapse for a week before the Ban is considered violated.
                                                  Bane: Temperatures and substances cold enough to damage flesh on contact.
                                                  Totem Advantage: Merit: Biokinesis (Chronicles of Darkness pg 57) at five dots. For Uratha, increase the amount of bashing damage healed per round by 1, instead of doubling it. Normal healing times for lethal and aggravate damage are still halved.

                                                  New Numina - Fleshy Embrace (R): The Flesh Cradle may embrace any organic creature within its walls. Against an unwilling subject, the Flesh Cradle must make a grappling maneuver following a successful grapple attempt. The creature is then immobilized within the spirit’s corpus. Breaking free (whether the embrace was voluntary or not) requires 3 successes on an extended Strength+Resolve roll (roll once per round), potentially contested by the Cradle’s Power+Resistance.

                                                  Once embraced, a bleeding creature stops losing health. The embrace provides the equivalent of intensive medical care, rolling the Cradle’s Power+Finesse in place of Intelligence+Medicine, and with no limit on the number of wounds it can downgrade per day. Additionally, embraced creatures double their natural healing rate, and can regenerate body parts or otherwise fully recover from physical injuries and Tilts that would normally be permanent.

                                                  The Cradle pays one less Essence to use Influences and Numina on those it embraces, and can embrace up to four size 5 creatures at a time.

                                                  Embraced creatures lose their Defense against all attacks.

                                                  Mortals suffer a breaking point with each embrace. It is a breaking point towards spirit for Uratha with a Harmony of 8 or higher.

                                                  When used with the Reaching Manifestation, only a portion of the Cradle’s corpus slips through the gauntlet, forming a sort of fleshy cocoon around the subject.

                                                  The longer I study science the more I am convinced that it is functionally indistinguishable from what our ancestors would refer to as sorcery. And I would know, being both scientist and sorcerer.
