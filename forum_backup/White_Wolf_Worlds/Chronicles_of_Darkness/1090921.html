                              Originally posted by totalgit View Post
                              Im surprised how many people ignore purviews.
                              Super constructive input. Good contribution.

                              I'm surprised how many people only look at purviews with a single, rigid definition in mind. Decay, absence, enervation, absence, and endings are all in the purview of death.

                              • Unraveling decay can be interpreted as healing. (with decay as 'the process of declining in quality, power, or vigor')
                              • Unraveling the ending (of one's life), the same.
                              • Unraveling enervation (a feeling of being drained of energy or vitality; fatigue) would necessarily have the opposite effect (giving them a feeling of being full of energy or vitality, and unfatigued).

                              All of these are reasonable interpretations, they don't become less so because 'Healing' is under Life.
                              You're allowed to do the same things with different Arcana. The book is pretty explicit about that, and if Purview meant that 'No other Arcana can touch this', that would be necessarily false.

                              Note that just because these are reasonable interpretations, it doesn't mean that they have to be allowed at any given table. Hence most everyone in this thread saying it depends on your table.
