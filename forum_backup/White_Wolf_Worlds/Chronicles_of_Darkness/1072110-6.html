    @The Kings Raven,

    Good point. Sinister in retrospect is definitly not the right words to describe the Light, but I am very pleased that it does possess some level of amorality. Just like with Beast (one of my favorite gamelines) I'm truly glad that it's the player character's active choice to do good, and how. Even if it is supernaturally motivated, it still allows the level of agency that I've found very few other games have that the Chronicles possess in spades.

    This line of thinking is all towards a common purpose though: working out potential Tactics that Hunters may utilize in their conflicts with either the Nobles or the Darkness. And in that vein I have two ideas solidifying right now.

    ~ Knight of Mirror's Tactic: Otherwise known as Empath Preservation Tactics or the "Don Kehote Tactic", this technique is the improved upon methods that led to the disasterous attempt to rid one of the Hopeful of their self-destructive delusions about a "Purpose on Earth." While the first time it was used led to the revelation about what a Noble becomes when their delusions progress to psychosis, it has since been modified by [insert name of anti child soldier compact] in order to at least ground the Noble's delusions about saving the world with the very real danger and trauma that fighting the supernaturals bring. This tactic is risky, and difficult because in order for it to succeed the Hunters will need to discover a Noble's true identity, and confront their family or circle of friends with this revelation before confronting the Noble themselves. If successful, then the Noble's Empathic powers will turn against her as their desire to end the suffering of others will conflict with their family and friend's desire for their loved one to not be in danger any more or risk their lives for their sake. While this doesn't necessarily take away a Noble's powers, if successful it will weaken them enought that they won't be able to endanger themselves for a good deal of time. And that just might be what the Hunter needs in order to either capture the Noble themselves, or target and eliminate the supernatural threat that the Noble themselves were going to confront.

    ~ Beacon of Hope Tactic: Often mockingly referred to as the "Bat Signal" this tactic solely involves the Hunters putting out a noticeable coded message to an unidenrified Noble in the area for assistance. This is primarily used when the Hunter Cell realizes they're out matched by the supernatural force they fight against and are either unable to contact the Noble for a more formal request for assistance, the Cell is too prideful or prejudice to ask so instead they rely upon plausibly deniable assistance from an unknown Noble, or (most dastardly) when the Cell wants to set a trap for the Noble themselves. Success results in a Noble of the ST's choosing arriving at either a critical moment of a combat encounter, or for the Hunter's trap. The danger, of course, is even when the call is genuine and sincere, there is no guarentee which Noble shows up, or even if they will take the Hunter's side of the fight.
