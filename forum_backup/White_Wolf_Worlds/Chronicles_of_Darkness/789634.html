              The Storm Lord in question is, in brief, very driven and controlling. A nightmare that forces the target to stay indoors for fear of what the sky might do to them sounds right. I was thinking:

              Normal: The target feels that a storm is coming that will devastate the area. The target tries to hide and find shelter; -2 to all rolls not related to getting away and finding a spot to take shelter and hide in.

              High Satiety: The storm is almost on you and the target can hear the thunder in her ears. A feeling of panic overwhelms the target. Any roll not related to getting away, and finding a spot to take shelter in and hide requires a reflexive Resolve + Composure roll. As well, the victim cannot focus on anything but the thunder ringing in her ears; the Deafened tilt is also applied for the remainder of the scene or the Nightmare ends.

              Satiety expenditure: The desire to hide and take shelter from the storm overwhelms the target. Any turn in which the Resolve + Composure roll is failed when trying to do something other than hide and take shelter the target doesn't apply Defense. The Beast may spend an additional Satiety to reduce all Perception rolls to a chance die.

              Exceptional success: The fear of the upcoming storm renders the target unable to recall much of what happened. While general details such as three people approached will be remembered the target will be unable to provide specifics about the people. The target may also add in minor details that don't represent what actually happened making the target further unreliable as a witness.

              Activation roll: Presence + Satiety vs Composure + Supernatural Tolerance

              How does that sound? Any suggestions?
