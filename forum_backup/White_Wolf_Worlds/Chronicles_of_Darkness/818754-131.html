        Question about Psychic Vampires from the Hurt Locker book (page 101 - 103):

        It says:

        "By and large, Psychic Vampirism works on most things in the Chronicles of Darkness. However, a psychic vampire cannot steal the life energy of an actual vampire. She can steal Willpower if her Psychic Vampirism Merit is strong enough. Psychic vampires cannot steal the life of ghosts or spirits, unless they have the Soul Eater Merit. Using psychic vampirism on supernatural creatures is slightly tougher; subtract their Supernatural Potency trait (Blood Potency, Gnosis, Primal Urge, etc.) from the roll to activate the power."

        The first dot of this Merit says:

        Amateur (•):
        With this basic level, your character can cause bashing damage. She causes one bashing damage per success on her roll. Every two points of bashing damage gives her one Ephemera (this can be accumulated over multiple turns). She can use one Ephemera to heal a point of bashing damage, or two Ephemera to heal one lethal damage.


        The other dots basically just built on to that, but my question is this:

        Can a Psychic Vampire still use his/her powers to damage Kindred? Or is that tied to the stealing life energy, meaning that damaging the Kindred doesn't work either?

                                                            Originally posted by Raktus View Post
                                                            Question about Dhampir...
                                                            Can they buy Covenant merits if they have status?
                                                            From reading p.113 and 123 of VtR 2nd I don't see why not. The hurdle is that dot of Covenant status. Once achieved, many more doors open. That said, the Covenants are likely to be wary of a Dhampir acting among them in anything but the most deferential and circumspect manner.

                                                            Similarly, a Ghoul might acquire Covenant status, but will still be a Ghoul and second-class in virtually every respect.

                                                            The status Merit reflects establishing your personal value to the group in a way that is sufficiently widespread and unchallenged such that you are considered a reliable and trusted asset/member. How you get to that point is the crux of the story.

                                                            --Khanwulf
