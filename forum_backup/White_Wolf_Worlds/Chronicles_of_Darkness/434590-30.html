                    There's already the stereotypes under Schools to give you some ideas.

                    I imagine that Leviathans won't be popular with any supernatural group. Even if this particular Leviathan is keeping to itself and not causing trouble, a lot of people are going to feel nervous having something caperable of the kind of destruction Leviathans can cause nearby. Mages and the Circle of the Crone are probably the biggest exception, since Leviathans can be a great mystery to study, but even then - why not poke the sleeping dragon who doesn't live near you? Meanwhile the Circle may actually want the Leviathan to start causing mass destruction and chaos since, you know, they're the Circle. Someone really should write a Bloodline built for Circle-Leviathan crossover. (not me though, I don't know enough Vampire)

                    For Beast? I wouldn't say that Beast is the closest game to Leviathan (that would definitely be Genius); but they both use themes of family and lizard brain instincts; and they use both in incompatible ways which makes crossover harder. I think the most interesting crossover you could do is to make Leviathan's into the Beasts' Beast. Beasts go around telling humans to learn lessons from their primordial instincts and encourage other supernaturals to embrace their monstrous nature. Leviathans tell Beasts "I'm what you get when you take that too far".

                            On the topic of other fansplats, I might have inadvertently created a fansplat the has a unique... clash with Leviathan on a thematic level.

                            The idea for Monarch was that they were created as a result of experimentation from an alien race to act as rulers for a colonization attempt, or atleast, what seems like it.
                            -They are relatively new having only showed up in the past 70 years, contrasting against the timeless and ancient existance of Leviathans
                            -They are the beacons of civilization, order and authority imposed against wild evolution, contrasted against the Levithans being anticiviliation.
                            -They possess great psychic powers able to command nests or small cities of creatures, but not men without first developing these powers and even then it's limited. Leviathans just passively brainwash bystanders.
                            -Monarchs congregate into a massive feudal system or rules and regulations. Leviathans are more solitary.
                            -The creators of the Monarchs still exist, creating only new Monarchs, sending them down to Earth, but otherwise, not being involved. Leviathans have their Progenitors long gone.


                            They both possess psychic powers, reality warping abilities, the idea of being monsters that birth new horrors, and even rulership, but they're so different otherwise.

                            Mechs: Because even the Chronicles of Darkness needs robot fights.
                            DarkFrame: Crossover setting that puts Chronicles of Darkness in the far future that is Warframe.
                            Monarch: The Endless You are an alien ruler, charged with maintaining a people who you shape to suit your needs.

                                                I had some ideas for channels/adaptations based on the Endbringers from Worm (for those who haven't read, think 2 Typhons and an Opheon all at the Sheol 20+ range)

                                                Behemoth: Behemoth's lightning attacks and pyrokinesis don't really fit thematically with the themes of Leviathan. Other than that, there are a few things that could be adapted. For these purposes, the most interesting thing from the Behemoth fight was the description of how the fires in the area were even worse than they should have been due to his power.

                                                - Sun Poisoned Bile: Probably a high Sheol adaptation to Swallowing the Sun. Enhance the darkness created with the depth 4 of Swallowing the Sun to do damage to those inside it (Benny's kill aura), cause the Primal Darkness of depth 7 to make existing environmental hazards worse (force people to check more often, increase severity, things like that)
                                                - Call The Earth's Fire: High Sheol adaptation to the channel that lets you create earthquakes. Lets you create persistent environmental hazards involving magma.

                                                Leviathan: Arc 8 (Extermination) of Worm should be required reading for anyone wanting to run/play a high Sheol Leviathan focusing on Elements. In fact, here is a link: https://parahumans.wordpress.com/cat...rmination/8-1/ . Of Worm!Leviathan's abilities, the only one that probably isn't already being planned is his water clone, which totally needs to be an adaptation to whichever channel ends up letting the Leviathan control water.

                                                - The Levies Shall Break: Adaptation for the channel that lets you create storms/waves (I suspect the 2 1e channels that do that will be combined, maybe also incorporating the earthquake one), allows you to summon waves on land so long as your wake touches a body of water.
                                                - The Sea Shall Know Its Master's Face: Adaptation to the channel that lets you control water. Lets you duplicate your attacks/soak extra damage so long as there is water around.

                                                The Simurgh: An example of an Awareness/Sanctity focused Opheon. Most of her schtick could be accomplished by combining Lambent Eyes of Judgement, The Key and the Gate, Call of the Depths and Besieging the Tower of Will.

                                                - Herald of the Worst of all Worlds: Very high Sheol adaptation for Call of the Depths, requires Lambent Eyes of Judgement, The Key and the Gate and Besieging the Tower of Will. Allows you to declare a long term agenda when you use the depth 7 version of Call of the Depths. People affected by your Call of the Depths will be altered, a la Besieging the Tower of Will, to support that agenda. The Leviathan gains a number of points based on the roll and the number of people affected that can later be traded in to add bonuses to rolls supporting that agenda (mainly thinking cult rolls) or penalties to rolls opposing that agenda. These point can also be spent to retroactively introduce elements like hidden or unwitting collaborators as appropriate.

                                                All: The Endbringers keep most of their physical form in other realities.

                                                - Shielding the Source: Adaptation to whichever channel lets you trade size for durability. Places a cap on the damage you take from a single attack (maybe in a round at depth 7?)
