    Originally posted by Griautis View Post

    That is false. "For the rest of the scene, he suffers no penalties to Social rolls from his actions or appearance ". Your clan bane is not your actions or appearance, it's a supernatural force, which reduces your humanity by two in terms of humanity penalties and downgrades rolls.

    Hence, Majesty helps to fail less and offset the penalties (since you're likely to get - from humanity), but it does not outright remove the bane. Just helps fight it.
    Majesty effectively negates the Humanity penalties; the Humanity scale describes the penalties in terms of appearing sick and deathly, and as you quoted, someone using Majesty suffers no penalties from appearance. The Nosferatu bane only makes Humanity penalties worse. Its still a Humanity-based penalty, and thus appearance based, though.

    Though, I am incorrect in two regards. Lower Humanity also makes it harder to make Empathy checks, which is not covered by Majesty. And I forgot the Presence/Manipulation failures to dramatic failures part - I only remembered the Humanity as two dots lower bit. Granted, most Nosferatu I've seen played have a high enough Presence and Manipulation score for Nightmare to work, so its rare for them to fail those rolls in the first place; I can't think of any time that came up.
