                Because in a game with Creative Thaumaturgy that expects you to design your own spell effects, there needs to be solid and consistent guidelines or nobody has a clue what they're doing.

                If they work differently, the exception should probably be explained.

                Otherwise, you end up with players not knowing what to expect because there are inconsistent and contradictory standards and the Storytellers doing way more work than they should have to because there is no solid guideline in place.

                The Practices and Arcana Purviews go a long way towards this, as do the guidelines in the Creative Thaumaturgy section, but what qualifies as Lasting is all over the place and a source of problems in just about every community I've looked into. This thread is a prime example. I'm hoping there's a solid clarification upcoming.
