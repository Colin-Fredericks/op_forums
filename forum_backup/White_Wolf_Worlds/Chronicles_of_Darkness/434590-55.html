                Draft 3

                Self-Serving Idol of Glory (Ancestral Channel)
                The Leviathan is no mere creature of flesh and bone, but a living god capable of empowering icons made in their honor.

                The Leviathan's Divine nature empowers and curses objects in their vicinity.
                Depth 1: Birthright of Sanctity

                Depth 2: Items that the Leviathan has made partake of their divine nature. They gain 7 Dedication points to assign to the item, but only can access their current Depth in benefits, and only while they hold the item. The Leviathan can have up to Sheol in personally crafted items dedicated in this way, though they can destroy previous creations to make way for new ones.

                Depth 3: The Leviathan can now blight a type of object with curses, reducing its effectiveness in their presence. They declare a category of item (such as medical tools, chain armor, swords, pistols, etc.) for each dot of Sheol anathema to them, and assign a pool of 7 Blight points to effects, though they can only utilize up to their current Depth in effects. The Leviathan can suppress this effect for the Scene for 1 Willpower. They can rededicate target items and blight effects after successfully meditating after 24 hours.

                Depth 4: What the Leviathan has made with their own hands (or tentacles) is as much them as their own flesh. Dedicated items in the Leviathan's possession are treated as the Leviathan for the purposes of Size increases, and any effect that would manipulate the item is subject to a Clash of Wills.

                Depth 5: Wrath falls upon whatever mortal artifice would dare impede a living god. Blighted items that strike or impede the Leviathan automatically take Sheol points of Structure damage (bypassing Durability). Supernatural objects can still be dealt damage, but do not have their Durability ignored.

                Depth 6: Blessings now flow through onto the Leviathan's followers. Objects crafted by their Cult and dedicated to Leviathan, such as by having one of their names or depictions inscribed upon it or being used in a Ritual, can gain up to 7 Dedications as well, though the amount a user can access is limited further by their Knowledge of the Leviathan.

                Depth 7: A Leviathan in Apotheosis can expunge the presence of blasphemous object from their very presence. All Blighted objects within the Leviathan's Wake are treated as if they were a character in an Extreme Environment with a rating equal the the Leviathan's Presence, and their Durability treated as Stamina. The effect lasts until the item leaves the Leviathan's Wake, or the Leviathan changes Depth.
                Dedication Cost Effect
                1 +1 Equipment Bonus (max. +5 total)
                1 +1 Weapon Damage (max. 5 total)
                1 +1 Durability
                2 +1 General or Ballistic Armor
                2 9-Again on Skill rolls (or upgrade to 8-Again)
                3 Exceptional Success on 3 Successes
                Blight Cost Effect
                1 -1 Equipment Bonus (min. 0 total)
                1 -1 Weapon Damage (min. 0 total)
                1 -1 Durability (min. 0)
                2 -1 General or Ballistic Armor
                2 Lose 10-Again (or downgrade 9-Again)
                3 Failures become Dramatic Failures
                Adaptations:
                Divine Receptacle (••): The Leviathan has access to an additional 1-dot Dedication - the ability to store Ichor inside the item. Items can only store Ichor up to their Durability safely.
                Channel the Divine (••••, Requires Divine Receptacle): The Leviathan has access to an additional variable cost Dedication - the item can replicate the ability of a Channel the Leviathan knows. The cost in Dedication points is equal to the Depth of the Channel ability. Any Ichor costs must be paid for from either the bearer, or the item's own reserve (through Divine Receptacle). Sheol is treated as 1 in the case where the bearer does not possess a Sheol Attribute (such as a favored cultist being given such an item).
                Primordial Creator (•••): The Leviathan gains 3 additional Dedication points and can access their Depth+1 at Depth 2, Depth+2 at Depth 4, and Depth+3 at Depth 6.
                Accursed Idol (•••): The Leviathan gains 3 additional Blight points and can access their Depth+1 at Depth 3, Depth+2 at Depth 5, and Depth+3 at Depth 7.

                    Originally posted by ajf115 View Post
                    Awareness

                    Well, we've only got one Descendant Channel left to do here, and it seems to me that about the only thing we're missing in Awareness is some kind of prophetic ability. Now, prophecy is always tricky to do in an RPG, so perhaps we could work with that to make something a little unique as prophetic powers go. What if, instead of having the Leviathan be a 'future-seer', they are a speaker of self-fulfilling prophecies - part prediction and part memetic instrument of will. In essence what I'm proposing is that the Leviathan's prophecy, by virtue of being spoken, increases the odds of a specific future happening partly by giving dice to those furthering it and partly by serving as a statement to the Storyteller of what the player wants, allowing them to work that into the story or present obstacles to it as necessary. The 'weight' a Leviathan can put behind their future increases with increasing Depth.
                    I think there was a Divination Channel in 1E, or proposed earlier.

As for the other, what about a Channel that allows a degree of control over Resonance and Essence within the Wake? Leviathans predate most divisions, including presumably that between the flesh and the spirit. We could crib some powers from the Idigam's Essence-Shaping abilities. My only issue with that is that the power might be a too powerful to be a passive one, and it might be better as a Descendant Channel, which doesn't really help. Perhaps some kind of natural EMP/tech-disruption aura?
