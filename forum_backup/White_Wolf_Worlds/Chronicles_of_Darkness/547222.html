    Why not obvious Atavisms?

    06-09-2015, 01:02 PM
    As I preper to include the mythical horror of Beast in to mine games, I have one question - Why Atavisms are not visible to anyone? The are to be seen only to at least minor supernatural templates. I do not get idea beside this concept - can someone from Beast's developers team answer to me on this?

    It's wierd, as the Children in RAW do not need to conceal they existance to humanity - contrary toMasquerade, "The Herd Must Not Know" or Percept of Secrecy, too mention only the Big Three of WoD settings. It's even stranger when you concider that Beasts want to be other supernaturals family - so they would want to play by the others rules, i.e. not using their powers ostensibly on humans, or their vampires, werewolves or changeling cousins will talk about it with them. And taking in account the effects of powers are visible - dragon's fire is burining ground, Raptor's claws are slashing objects, Makra's tentacles are suffocating people in the room - why some physical, obvious changes are not shown in Atavisms of Begotten?

    Like the Ugallu's hands become real birds claws for those few second. Or Beast with Dragon's Soul just spits fire from scaled head on his victims for one turn. Why Soul cannot be shown in slight why to everyone watching, and is visible only to those with supernatural powers?

    It's seems very illogical and counter-intuitive in setting.

      Well, one thing is that the whole masquerade system is generally reinforced by magic, not laws. As the WoD games went on and on, you'll notice there's less and less "laws" over the matter, and more supernatural consequences. Vampires have the mind-bluring Kiss and the mirror effects. Werewolves have Lunacy. Mages avoid it because of 'dox. Prommies to disquiet, changelings to attracting the Others, Mummies to do their cult-brainwashing thing, GtSE to not annoy their geister. In Demon, its to avoid Compromise.

      Secrecy is literally part of their nature. So, tied into that, invisible Atavism might be part of it. You can't go walking down Main Street looking like a dragon. Werewolves cover their shapeshifting with Lunacy. Demons with compromises. And so on. Obvious shapeshifting has its own secrecy laws - in this case, its "no one sees it."

      Another part of it might be the nature of the Astral - you'll notice that in 1e at least, goetics couldn't manifest physically without a Mind 4/Spirit 4 patterning effect to turn them from astral being to spirit. In the Summoner's book, all the goetic summons could never materialize. They can only use their Numina and Influences from Twilight. I'm assuming that's carrying forwards into 2e. The Astral can affect the physical, but it cannot directly manifest physically.

          It's not a societal or even a magical law, but a secret world of monsters is still a theme of the larger World of Darkness.

          That aside, being creatures of nightmare, there's something wholly disquieting about the being grabbed by a man-just a man- and you can feel the suckers of a giant tentacle breaking your bones. Even more disturbing, there are those marks after the assaullt-but his hand is always a hand. What the hell is going on here?

          A woman leaps an impossible distance. You don't know how she did it. It's impossible. You used a cable, she has nothing. You heard the beating of mighty wings as she landed, but there's nothing there to make the sound. As she glares with a vicious grin, you have to ask yourself when you'll wake up.

          We're a species reliant on our senses, particularly sight. The most discordant thing you can do, then, the one that blends dreams and reality, is to make the signals contradict each other. You smell the brine in the Desert. You feel the claws on a model's hand. You can taste battery acid on top of the mountain, and there's never anything there.

          So it makes perfect sense.

              They're entirely visible depending on the effect. We've been over this. If you spend satiety for Infestation, then people are going to bloody notice you turned into a swarm of bugs or what have you. If you use Dragonfire people are going to notice you are breathing fire. If you spend satiety with Limb From Limb, everyone can glimpse the soul looming over you. With Low Satiety, Unbreakable visibly makes your skin seem more leathery, or scaley or whatever.

              It doesn't matter that ordinary people can't see the Soul when these powers are called on with Satiety use. They risk the masquerade as much as anything else that destructive or obvious. The reason why Atavisms usally have no obvious, physical changes to the Beast's mostly human body is because they're not physical characteristics it acquires. They're the aspects of the Begotten's Soul seeping into the physical world through their flesh.

              Anyone with supernatural sensitivity can perceive the Soul when satiety is used to power an atavism, because the Beast is drawing so much of its power to them. It does not, however, take that to notice what it causes.

              I don't get why you think this is counterintuitive. Also see the explanations above.

                                      It seems like Wyrdhamster is under the impression that Atavisms are invisible by choice, as opposed to just because that is how they work. The Beasts have no formal rule of secrecy but their powers are, for the most part, inherently pretty low profile. For instance, blasting someone with a Nightmare mainly just looks (from the outside) like they've suddenly flipped out for no reason.

                                      I've been explaining it like this: a Beast's human component is like a dimensional intrusion point for a much larger entity. You see the 'normal' person, but when the whole of the being exerts it's power other aspects of it are acting on the world. A vanilla mortal sees nothing change because of the simple fact that nothing HAS changed. An Atavism that allows you to attack with your claws doesn't cause you to grow claws because the claws are already there and always have been, you're simply allowing/forcing them to influence the world around your human focal point. It also explains why low Satiety causes a lot of the physical Atavisms to have more pronounced visible effects on your body, because the hunger is focusing more of your own reality down into the person shape, distorting it with the metaphysical equivalent of surface tension.

                                      I agree with ArcaneArts, in that I love the way this works and I think it fits perfectly with the themes of the game. It makes Beasts that much more frightening to some poor mortal who meets one. A vampire, or a werewolf, you can understand. You might reject what you're seeing, you'll be horrified that there's this clear monster that shouldn't exist coming at you, but at least some dude turning into a giant wolf monster and then ripping a door apart with their claws makes mechanical sense to your eyes.


                                      Imagine a 10 year old girl with adorable pigtails looking up at you with cold confidence and being completely unable to shake the visceral certainty that she's 60 feet tall. Imagine that same little girl pounding through a 2 foot thick vault door as if were made of rice paper, barely bothering to break her stride as she stalks after you, leaving size 44 footprints cracked into your flooring and making the walls of the hallway groan and crack at the passage of a massive body she doesn't have.

                                      Imagine trying to shake down the local health inspector, trying to beat him down when he doesn't budge, and seeing the smug sneer on his face as your bat clangs off his scrawny frame with the same effect it would have had on a tank.


                                      No fangs, no strange pallor, no fur, nothing. Just normal. Just anything but normal.

                                      Sisyphus has it easy...
