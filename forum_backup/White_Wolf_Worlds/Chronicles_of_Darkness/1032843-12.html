                    I think I'll leave the Kin question to a Beast developer.

                    As to the definition of Devoted, however, I wanted to clarify that Devoted are Deviants who serve a conspiracy, not necessarily the one that created them. Some conspiracies have Devoted who rise high in their ranks. Others relegate Remade to supporting roles. Most actively recruit Broken, partially because it's a whole lot easier than initiating new Divergences. We'll cover Devoted in much more detail in a later supplement, but that's sort of the executive summary.

                                        Originally posted by Primordial newcomer View Post
                                        so chimerics are the social splat? able to to go espionage with other supernaturals? and mutants are sorta the ones filled with surprises.
                                        "Social splat" might be too strong a word (Remade really are *highly* variable, even within the same Clade), but you know the trope of the hunters hunting the monster that is hunting them back (raptors, Ghost in the Darkness, that one early X-Files episode with the invisible creature in the woods)? Tracking a Hybrid with a lot of Chimeric Variations is a bit like that. They could turn up anywhere. You might walk right past them and not realize it, or think you have them pinned, and then they turn a corner and are just gone, leaving you alone with an alley cat.

                                        Mutants are indeed full of surprises. You can study one for months, monitoring her via a network of tracking devices, but when you try to capture her, she will still pull some new trick out of nowhere. If Chimerics are known for slipping the proverbial net, Mutants are known for tearing free of it.
