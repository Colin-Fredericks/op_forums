    I want to run a Beast - but do not know what to do

    03-09-2018, 12:17 AM
    After reading Beast Player's Guide I finally feel the gameline is complete (-ish) and playable. I have urge to run at least a story with it. But I do not have any concrete ideas to explore - or rather I got too much for them, thanks to other fans - from Begotten of the Past topic. For example, I now run Werewolf chronicle in Viking times - as I always had soft spot for Raiders from the North. I even run short lived game on the Norse mythology before even Dark Eras was mentioned to have Viking setting. So I'm rather one of those 'prepared and dedicated' Storytellers.

    And then we go to Beast and I have - 'Well. I really want to run it. But I'm blank on even main chronicle plot'. I just want to run an game, without having any plot idea. It's soooo weird for me.

    So maybe you would help? Pointing to some interesting stuff you can do with Beast as game. Marking proposed Dark Eras for Beast that are in Begotten of the Past topic is all hands on. I was thinking about doing this Post WW2 Japan with Kaiju game - but I think it could quickly become some weird version of 'Neon Genesis Evangelion with monsters psychic sex'.

    What I'm sure is inclusion of Minorities subplots in game - like in this topic - but it's now universal to any game of Beast, thanks to BPG book.

              I mean, "weird version of Neon Genesis Evangelion" sounds like a sales pitch to me, but you do you. :-)

              That said, a couple ideas that have come to me just from reading the Player's Guide:

              --Two high-Lair Beasts, a Nemesis and an Enabler, start a power struggle similar to a superhero vs. supervillain fight. The Enabler keeps masterminding ever-grander transgressions and the Nemesis becomes more and more desperate to stop them, even if they're also using the Enabler's sloppy seconds to feed. The player brood could be related to either of them, or just bystanders trying to keep the conflict from drawing too much Heroic (not to mention mortal) attention.

              --The Lair-based radio station mentioned in the "Connected Lair" Merit write-up. Just...who would run a radio station out of the Primordial Dream? Would it be audible like normal, or only in dreams? What would you even broadcast? You could make a chronicle out of a) the player brood starts up Radio Free Nightmare because why not, or b) the player brood are trying to trace the broadcast to its source, and have to get through various obstacles of increasing complexity along the way.

              --For a one-shot or the first few sessions of a long chronicle, I think the Night of Revels Rite would make a good intro to the game and let all the players feel out what their Beasts can do.

                  I have a particular fondness for subversions of familiar stories & tropes. Especially where the Beasts and Heroes are actually outsiders and interlopers to these stories.

                  One I've always wanted to run is a power struggle story between one supernatural splat where the Brood gets caught up in the turmoil without a full understanding of what's going on and why. The first choice I'd go for personally is a Praxis battle between the Vampire Prince of the city and a would be replacement. The Brood would start as humans who undergo the Devouring during the first few sessions and have a pretty good Begotten mentor who tries to help them adjust to their new condition. Then, one day he simply vanishes without a trace and the Brood is left virtually alone in a city that keeps seeing a rise in violent crime with police who seem uncannily red-taped into doing nothing as strange events begin to occure all around the city. Ultimately the Brood could learn that their mentor was Kin to a Vampire who saw too good of an opportunity with their nightmarish cousin and Blood-Bound them to their service. Or, the Brood could just bounce and try to get out of dodge with what little knowledge they have. Either way, a group of Heroes (ideally mirrors to the characters themselves) would begin tracking and stalking the Brood, not understanding the situation themselves (or possibly being directly manipulated) and believing that the Beasts are the cause of the upheaval. Either way, the war will happen. It's up to the players to decide which role they'll play in the conflict, and what the consiquences of those choices would be.

                          A very simple plot idea: The Apex makes life hard on every Begotten - not because he's actively out to do something, but because the Lair Trait he imposes is Burning. Even if it's just Bonfires cropping up all over the place, it is hard to not be able to move freely in your Lair. So one way to deal with the Problem would be to topple the Apex (with all the problems that entails). Another would be to help the Apex (who turns out to be a genuine nice guy) to figure out how to instead impose a different Lair Trait like Overgrowth on the Hive.

                          Another: No matter in what form, spreading Fear has consequences, and by attracting Spirits feeding on these events to the city, the Brood managed to draw a new Pack's ire. Now they're the target of their hunt. How will they be able to convince their Cousins not to slay them? Will the Werewolves accept penance in the form of the Brood helping with a Siskur-Dah on a more pressing target? Do they even care, or is their need to prove themselves to their totem more important to them? What role will the "Tourist" Hero play who always seems to just turn up whenever these two groups meet? Will they find a way to cause less spiritual Havoc when Feeding?

                          A third: The local Beasts are a small, but tightly-knit community, because for some reason or other, they are under constant attack: Kindred try to enslave them, Hunters seem to pop up and find them at the most inconvenient times, and while not openly hostile, Changelings run for their lives whenever they meet. Being cut of from their extended Family like this hurts, and it's obvious someone or something actively caused this. What will they do when they find out these are the works of a Demon trying to 'isolate an irregular variable', and a Hero with far too many 'friends' seeking to destroy them?

                          A fourth: A powerful Beast has died. Their remaining cult, however, chose someone of the Player's Brood to fill this hole - and they're not asking. While it would certainly give the group additional resources, they expect the new Beast to take up the mantle of their old leader, including their goals, behaviour, Feeding... How will they deal with this unwanted attention?

                          A fifth and last one: During a climactic battle with a Hero in her heart, a Beast nearly died, and grasped for straws. Something went awire. As a result, her Horror was split into dozens of Horrorspawn, who feed relentlessly in the Primordial Dream. While the Beast is alive, she's near powerless (No Atavisms, no Lair Traits), and the abundance of Feeding causes way too many ripples.
                          The Beast approaches the Brood to ask them for help: subdue the Horrorspawn, collect them and bring them to her Lair, so she can try to regain her Horror. Easier said than done, and certainly not without its dangers, but most importantly, there is no guarantee it'll work at all..

                          If you want more ideas, I'd like to direct you to this thread.

                                              Here's a simple idea:- Your have your group of Beasts take out a local chapter of the Ashwood Abbey. If you decide to continue it, you could explore how the Abbey attempts to retaliate, and the conflict drawing the attention of other supernaturals...

                                              ​Another:- The Apex has disappeared, and as a new one (unidentified) takes up the mantle an unsettling and strange shift occurs in the Hive, a trait unlike any other has appeared, and it's causing disturbances not just in Lairs, but in the region's Primordial Dream, inflicting terrible nightmares and making Hero's senses go haywire. What is the cause? Who, or what is the new Apex?

                                              And here's one last idea I had that could work for a story in Japan:- People are disappearing seemingly at random, vanishing without a trace; the only thing the victims have in common is that they come from big families with lots of relatives. What's more, the families claim that they see the victim ever night in their dreams: trapped, terrified, and desperate to come home. Is this the work of a Beast, the Fae, or something else?
