                                    Originally posted by Maitrecorbo View Post
                                    I'm curious about something. Keep in my i've only skimmed the book so far. But how possible is it to play a centimanus? I could see a prometheans character disgusted with humanity and deciding to become a crime boss or hit man or enforcer. Could you still play your character in the transmutation of flux i think its called?? I see it like wanting to play a low humanity vampire. Maybe not part of the majority but definitaly doable.
                                    A couple things. First, while it is possible to play a Centimanus, it is unsustainable. Flux will eventually eat you alive. This isn't a reason not to do it, but it is a consideration. Myself, I've loved playing characters on a tragic downward spiral. Also, the thing to remember about Centimanus is that they can always return to the Pilgrimage. Any Promethean can shift to Lead, which can lead to a really interesting redemption arc. Second, there is nothing to say a Promethean wants to be a nice human. The dark sides of humanity like crime, war, and such are parts of humanity. It is entirely possible to play the sort of character you describe and still be on the Pilgrimage. Plenty of the Roles either explicitly or potentially delve into sin, weakness, or evil. In fact, I'd argue that doing it this way fits the character better than becoming Centimanus. It is hard to be a crime boss when you've abandoned rationality, civilization, and even the humanoid form to the chaos of Flux.

                                            Yeah, the question you need to ask is what concept you want for the character. Playing a Centimanus is perfectly doable no matter what version you're playing, but first you have to consider what about the concept relates to that. A good way to work it out is to consider your character's goals. What do they want? Do they just want money/power? Are they working towards a Pilgrimage, and if so how do they see it and what goal do they envision at the end? For example, if your character is part of a criminal organization, what's their relationship with it? Do they see it as a means to end or a surrogate family? If it's the latter, I could see a Promethean wanting to become human so that they could truly be a part of it, and not just ultimately a tool that the organization uses.

                                            However, I don't think Centimani are that difficult to play- if the only option for Prometheans who didn't want to become human was so unstable and unsympathetic it was comparable to a draugr, then you'd have to either change it or open up the Refinements.
