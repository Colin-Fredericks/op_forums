    08-07-2016, 09:28 PM

    Oh, we are soo doing DarkEras II.

    Kickstarter in 2017, book in 2018 or late 2017.
    So with a second Dark Eras book officially confirmed for two years from now, I figured I'd do the only sensible thing and make a thread for it. Is there anything in particular you're clamoring for, whether specific eras or more general ideas? Are there crossover settings you want to see? What historical settings have you dreamed up on your own that you'd like to see done?

      Having seen what we've gotten so far I think, personally, that the eras do best when they're at a differing or defining point for the supernatural they're focusing on to act as a linchpin for the era.

      With that in mind, without a specific era yet, I'd like to see the first of the Brethren Wars, for Werewolf. Also detailing of the Auytthaya Kingdom, mentioned in the Pack. It was supposed to be a place of great difference, but a specific time period could focus on their conquest and expansion or the Burmese Wars and their eventual fall. In addition the colonization of Australia, which according to the core book brought the Pure and Forsaken tribes into contact with the previously independent Dreaming Lodge

      As always, I like Golden Age of Piracy and Vietnam War Werewolf and expansion into the New World Vampire, preferably with a focus on South and Central America.

              1) Ancient Sumer: Werewolf/Hunter.
              The Sumer chaper of Forsaken Chronicler's Guide was TOO SHORT. That setting has a great potential for werewolf, so I'd love to see it expanded. And in 2nd Edition, given the Sundering was about 2000 years before Sumer, the chapter could be the origin of the Werewolf Tribes.
              And for Hunters, it could be a great opportunity to see the Nibiru, the first Hunter conspirancy of known history.

              2) Roman Empire: Vampire/Mage.
              For vampire, it could be an updating of Requiem for Rome to 2nd Edition.
              For Mage, it's the last one of the big three without a roman setting, and given than the origin of the darshanas it's been just a few centuries ago, it could be the moment in which the darshanas became orders, at least in the western world.

              3) Spanish Civil War: Geist. It was the prelude of the WWII, and the first time there were massive plane bombings over civilian objectives in cities. Also, the repression (in both sides of the conflict, although specially in the Franco's side) during and after was enormous, causing hundreds of thousands of fatalities. Spain has over 200.000 corpses unidentified in mass graves of the postwar repression. So I think the death themes and mood are perfect for Geist.

              4) Mummy: I've got two options.
              a) Akhenaton Egypt: As an historian I'm really fascinated by Akhenaton reign, so I'd love to know more about him in the CoD. But I understand that there's been too Egyptian themed Eras for Mummy (3 from Sothis Ascends, and 2 from Dark Eras and Companion).
              b) Andine Mummies. How Iremite mummies arrived to the Andes (eve before the Incan Empire) and created there their own cults. But please, don't write than mummies are the creators of the andine cultures or the teachers of their most advanced knowledge.

              5) Ancient India: Whatever line!!!

                  I'd scribbled together something for a Seven Years' War setting in Vampire, specifically focused around the areas that would become Canada and acting as a prologue to the Montreal setting in the 2e corebook. It painted the Invictus and Lance as respectively backing the British mercantile interests and French Catholics, explored some of the weirdness surrounding the Native vampires and their interactions with the Strix, had a lost Wendigo clan/bloodline, and was supposed to offer hints towards the identity of the Nameless. It might be fun to toss Hunter onto the pile in order to show what the Acheron Trade Company (the precursor to the Cheiron Group) was like, along with a weaker Malleus Maleficarum and perhaps an Iroquois Conspiracy as well.

                  Though currently stalled, my All Along The Watchtowers is a 1960s American setting for Mage, though again it could work well with some Hunter thrown in (especially to incorporate some of the Witch Finders groups) focused on the symbolic upheaval of the time and the painful awakening of the country following the Vietnam War and so much racial conflict. I wanted to let the conflict between the Pentacle and Iron Pyramid to take center stage and heat up, as well as provide a 'sequel' to Mage Noir.

                  This one's a lot less thought out, but an Ancient Greek setting that lets Beasts act more openly as fantastic monsters and agents of divine will (like the Furies), also acting as a Changeling or possibly even Deviant crossover era where the focus is more on being cursed (like Medusa or Arachne) by the gods. I'd like to see the Changeling/Deviants victims of possibly undue or overwhelming punishment butt heads with the sacred monsters, along with having an excuse to rampage around as Scylla or the Hydra. (Someone else had proposed a Bronze Age Collapse setting for Beast, which I love but know nothing about).

                  Other than that, more crossover settings, more settings throughout Asia (I'd pay for a Victorian Hong Kong, 1930s Shanghai, or Russian Civil War anything) and South America (I need that Andrean Mummy idea), try to do Rome for another game or two to complete the set, and more lost or historical splats like old Clans/Bloodlines/Lodges/Legacies/etc.
