    Speaking of, have two [EDIT]now-complete Principalities, brought to you by the girl from WLSD. (I love the feathery psychic velociraptors, btw.)

    Crowns
    A Diplomat knows that more flies are caught with rotten meat then with waved sticks. Crowns gives the people what they want, and in exchange, the Monarch profits.

    Mandates
    [1] Inclination
    Sometimes, a good impression is all it takes to get things done. A Monarch can improve someone's perspective, though it may still fall later if she abuses it.
    Cost: 1 Psi
    Action: Instant
    Duration: Indefinite
    Effect: Improve the target's impression rating by one. This power can only be used once on a given target per story. This power does not prevent impression levels from being lost.

    [2] Declaration
    By his presence alone, a Monarch can shape a room's mood to match his own.
    Cost: 1 Psi (free in Noble Form)
    Effect: Based on the Monarch's current mood, anyone in the scene is inclined to match that mood, though strong emotion may snap them out of it. Any who roughly match the mood of the Monarch gain a +1 to skills or actions in favor of the mood (intimidation and attacks for anger, persuasion and social maneuvering for calm or happiness, empathy and resistance for sadness, etc), and a -1 to all other actions.

    [3] Motivation
    Mental reassurances and adjustment of pheromones can be all it takes to get someone to want to do something.
    Cost: 2 Psi
    Action: Extended (5 minutes of conversation per roll), Resisted
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Authority - Resolve
    Duration: Success weeks or until fulfilled
    Dramatic Failure: The target becomes contrary. The target gains an aspiration opposite the intended one that lasts for a week or until resolved.
    Failure: The target's motives are unchanged. The Monarch cannot try again in the scene.
    Success: When the Monarch reaches successes equal to the target's Willpower + Supernatural Tolerance, she picks a short-term aspiration for her target that would not cause a breaking point and is not mutually exclusive with any other aspiration he has. The target gains that aspiration in addition to any others he has. Fulfilling this aspiration grants the target a full Willpower refresh, in addition to any other benefits. The aspiration fades after weeks equal to successes, at which point it fades if not fulfilled.
    Exceptional Success: When reaching five more successes, the Monarch may pick an aspiration that may cause a breaking point for the target.

    [4] Cessation
    When negotiations have become heated, when an assassin makes a move, when violence will only make things worse, a Monarch can calm those around him.
    Cost: 2 Psi
    Action: Instant, Resisted
    Dice Pool: Composure + Authority - highest Composure
    Duration: Scene
    Dramatic Failure: Tempers flare, and everyone becomes more emotional. Violence becomes more likely, and any attackers will prioritize the Monarch as a target.
    Failure: No one is calmed. The Monarch may try again at a cumulative -1 penalty.
    Success: Everyone in the scene calms down. No one may attempt to attack or directly physically harm anyone else in the scene. All are inclined to behave calmly and rationally. Entering the scene causes the effect; leaving the scene ends the effect.
    Exceptional Success: As success, and all in the scene become more inclined towards the Monarch, giving him +1 to Empathy, Persuasion, and Socialize rolls for the scene.

    [5] Inspiration
    What's better than convincing one person to see your way? Convincing all of them!
    Cost: +2 Psi (Free in Noble Form)
    Effect: A Monarch can use Motivation on every person in the room. Individuals are convinced as she accrues more successes then their Willpower + Supernatural Tolerance. It is possible for some, but not all, in a room to be convinced. However, she can only work a room this way once per scene, regardless of how successful she is.

    Colony Aspect Ideas
    Suborning other colonies
    Creating ambient emotional effects
    Increased colony size/control

    Union Ideas
    Chalices: Happy juice (or sad juice, or angry juice, generally emotional juice)
    Cloaks: The Peter Pan effect (if you're happy, you can fly!)
    Quills: Subliminal message
    Scepters: Command target; target justifies actions
    Shields: Emotional protection


    Scepters
    A Monarch is authority incarnate. Scepters allows an Endless to weild her nobility as a weapon, using force of personality to get her way.

    Mandates
    [1] Vex
    Pain is a powerful teacher. A Monarch can cause someone to suffer by simply thinking about it.
    Cost: None
    Action: Instant, Resisted
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Authority - target Resolve
    Duration: Scene
    Dramatic Failure: The target feels fine. The Monarch suffers from the headache. He takes one bashing, and suffers from a -2 penalty on all mental rolls and on all extended actions.
    Failure:
    Success: The target suffers from a headache. She takes one bashing damages, and suffers from a penalty equal to successes on all mental rolls and on all extended actions. The Monarch cannot effect more than one person with Vex at a time, but he may choose to end the effect to try to Vex another.
    Exceptional Success: The penalty caps at -5, but the Monarch may choose to Vex another without lifting the affects of the person he's currently Vexing.

    [2] Kneel
    With the power of her will, a Monarch can command someone to commit a simple act.
    Cost: 1 Psi
    Action: Instant, Contested
    Dice Pool: Presence + Authority vs. Resolve + Authority
    Duration: Successes turns
    Dramatic Failure: The target remains unbowed, and will resist all uses of this power for the next week.
    Failure: The target remains unbowed. The Monarch can try again at a cumulative -1 penalty.
    Success: The target obeys a single command for a number of rounds equal to successes. This command cannot take longer than a round to say, and must be straightforward ("Kneel," "Drop the gun," "Walk away"). If this action would cause a breaking point, the target gets a +2 to his resistance roll. The target can spend one willpower to choose to do nothing for a turn.
    Exceptional Success: The Monarch can issue a new command, so long as there are still turns remaining.

    [3] Cower
    Sometimes, it's easier for a Monarch to cause his enemies to fall into a trembling heap, and use them as stepping stones.
    Cost: 1 Psi
    Action: Instant, Contested
    Dice Pool: Presence + Authority vs Composure + Authority
    Duration: Scene
    Dramatic Failure: The target does not turn to jelly, which is honestly kind of scary. The Monarch is intimidated by the target, and takes a -2 to all actions taken against her.
    Failure: The target does not turn to jelly. The Monarch can try again at a cumulative -1.
    Success: The target suffers from the Insensate Tilt for the scene, or until they suffer any damage.
    Exceptional Success: Additionally, the target remembers nothing that happened while under the effect of this power.

    [4] Obey
    With greater practice, a Monarch can command more complicated acts from one she commands.
    Cost: +1 Psi (free in Noble Form)
    Effect: Commands given by Kneel can be more complicated and involved. For commands that do not cause a breaking point, the effects of Kneel last for a scene per success, with exceptional lasting for up to a week or until completed. A Monarch cannot change the command of Kneel when using Obey

    [5] Delay
    At his mastery of Scepters, a Monarch can create a sleeper agent, ready to act at the opportune moment.
    Cost: +1 Psi
    Effect: Commands given by Kneel can be delayed in their execution. The Monarch can choose a time or set of circumstances when the command will take effect. One target cannot have more delayed commands than the lower of her Resolve or Composure. The command fades after successes months if the parameters are not met.

    Colony Aspect Ideas
    Mind control brain slugs (a la Yeerks, Goa'uld, etc)
    Mind blast
    Memetic attacks

    Union Ideas
    Cloaks: Telekinesis
    Crowns: Command target; target justifies actions
    Hammers: Mind-control device
    Quills: Memory edits
    Records: Delay commands
    Shields: Mental protections
    Staves: Command from afar

Spheres was one of the easiest things to work on, because of the ideas that were already present and the flexibility of putting them with powers.

                                                Crucibles

                                                A crucible is a tool of transformation and of refinement. A ruler should be as a crucible to the unworked ore of his lands and fiefdoms, ever refining them into more perfect forms.

                                                Mandates

                                                (1) Internal Alchemy

                                                The first step in both chemistry and alchemy is understanding, and this Mandate transforms the Monarch's body into a laboratory capable of analysing any substance without harm.
                                                Cost: 1 Psi (Free in Noble Form)
                                                Duration: 1 scene (permanent in Noble Form)
                                                Effect: The Monarch becomes capable of digesting any substance and will suffer no ill effects. This means that he can eat rocks, wood, rotten meat or even metal and suffer no ill effects. He may even thrive on such fare, although it becomes no more appetising. Furthermore, the Monarch may reflexively roll Intelligence + Science (with no penalty for lacking dots in the latter) in order to identify the properties and chemical make-up of an ingested substance. A single success is enough to successfully analyse the material, although supernatural compounds may take more (this includes most substances created through the Principality of Chalices).

                                                (2) Transmutation of Structure

                                                The laboratory of the Monarch's body is now capable of not only analysis but also of transmutation, reconfiguring molecules in order to attain the desired effect. The Monarch can use this Mandate in order to transmute a mass of material weighing no more than the Monarch's Authority in lbs (in earth gravity) to another state of that same material (water to ice, iron to molten iron etc) or into another substance which is made of the same elements (water to hydrogen and oxygen, coal to graphite). The Monarch must be touching the material he intends to transmute. Extremely rare or unique materials such as diamond or radioactive metals may only be transmuted at the Storyteller's discretion. This Mandate cannot be used to alter the physical shape of an object, that is the domain of the Principality of Hammers.
                                                In Noble Form, the Monarch may elect to ingest the substance to be transmuting it, passing it through a specialised series of organs before regurgitating it or expelling it via a specialised orifice. Doing so eliminates the Psi cost.
                                                (Note: this need not be an option if your group is not all that OK with biosquick).
                                                Cost: 1 Psi or free in Noble Form
                                                Dice Pool: Manipulation + Science +Crucibles
                                                Action: Extended, 3 turns per roll
                                                Effect:
                                                Dramatic Failure: The transmutation backfires, transmuting a portion of the Monarch's body randomly and inflicting (Crucibles) in Lethal damage. The Monarch loses all successes accumulated so far.
                                                Failure: No successes are accrued towards completing the transmutation.
                                                Success: Successes are accrued towards completing the transmutation. The Storyteller sets the target number of successes. Usually, making simple, natural transmutations such as water to ice take 3 successes, more complex ones such as water to oxygen and hydrogen take 5 successes and complex molecular realignments such as changing plastics to oil or petrol take 8.
                                                Exceptional Success: The Monarch so masterfully manipulates the molecular structure of the matter that any Psi spent is recovered.

                                                (3) Workshop

                                                The monarch can exert his psychic abilities to create an area in which he can easily call upon the strange physics of the Void in order to utilise Mandates of the Principality of Crucibles outside his own body, as well as easing his transmutations. This area endures for a reasonable time and is usually about the size of a small apartment.
                                                Cost: 3 Psi
                                                Dice Pool: Intelligence + Science + Crucibles
                                                Action: Extended, 10 minutes per roll
                                                Effect: The Monarch attunes an area of approximately the size of a small apartment to the physics of the Void, allowing him to more easily call upon them in the delicate manners necessary for the application of the Principality of Crucibles. A Monarch may only have one Workshop at a time.
                                                The number of successes needed to in order to successfully create the Workshop depends upon the location in which it is being created.
                                                Location Number of Required Successes
                                                Within a Province of the Monarch in the Beneath 5
                                                Within the Beneath but outside of the Monarch's Province 10
                                                Within the Monarch's Province in the phenomenal world. 15
                                                Outside the Monarch's Province in the phenomenal world. 20
                                                While within this area, the Monarch gains a bonus to all rolls involving Science, including Mandates of the Principality of Crucibles, equal to the Monarch's rating in Crucibles, as well as being able to waive Psi costs for using Mandates of this Principality.
                                                If the Monarch at any time rolls an Exceptional Success, he may add the 9-again rule to any Science rolls within the Workshop. if he rolls a second Exceptional Success, he may add 8-again. If he rolls a third, he may add the Rote quality to any Science rolls. Only only one bonus from Exceptional Success may be applied to a given roll.
                                                If the Monarch rolls a Dramatic Failure, he loses all successes accrued so far and the location instead becomes anathema to the powers of the Void, causing a penalty to any attempt to use Principalities suffer a penalty equal to the Monarch's Authority. This effect endures for the same amount of time as a successful Workshop would.
                                                The Workshop will endure for a number of days equal to (Authority*3).



                                                (4) Transmutation of Substance

                                                . The Monarch can now not only manipulate molecular structure but also the positions of subatomic particles, allowing the Monarch to transmute a substance into a completely different one, so long as the input and output have the same mass. The mass of the material transmuted can be no greater than the Monarch's Authority in lbs. The Monarch must be touching the material he intends to transmute. Extremely rare or unique materials such as diamond or radioactive metals may only be transmuted at the Storyteller's discretion. This Mandate cannot be used to alter the physical shape of an object, that is the domain of the Principality of Hammers. The Storyteller sets the target number of successes.
                                                In Noble Form, the Monarch may elect to ingest the substance to be transmuting it, passing it through a specialised series of organs before regurgitating it or expelling it via a specialised orifice. Doing so reduces the Psi cost to 1
                                                Cost: 2 Psi or 1 Psi in Noble Form
                                                Dice Pool: Manipulation + Science + Crucibles
                                                Action: Extended, 3 turns per roll
                                                Effect:
                                                Dramatic Failure: The transmutation backfires, transmuting a portion of the Monarch's body randomly and inflicting (Crucibles) in Lethal damage. The Monarch loses all successes accumulated so far.
                                                Failure: No successes are accrued towards completing the transmutation.
                                                Success: Successes are accrued towards completing the transmutation. The Storyteller sets the target number of successes. Making transmutations within the same 'category' of material, such one ferrous or noble metal to another usually require 3 successes, making transmutations across categories take 5 and drastic changes such as granite to plutonium require at least 8, if not more.
                                                Exceptional Success: The Monarch so masterfully manipulates the molecular structure of the matter that any Psi spent is recovered.

                                                (5) Mass Transmutation

                                                By exerting more of his psionic strength, the Monarch becomes able to transmute materials of far greater mass, as well as more swiftly, although this is quite draining.
                                                Cost: +3 Psi
                                                Effect: By paying the extra Psi when using either Transmutation of Structure or Transmutation of Substance, the Monarch becomes able to transmute materials of a mass equal to (Authority*10). He must still be able to touch the material. He may no longer opt to ingest it. This can be used to attack a structure, ignoring Durability and inflicting Damage to Structure equal to the highest individual roll (e.g., if a Monarch rolls three times and achieves 3, 1 and 2 successes respectively before completing the transmutation, it deals 3 damage to Structure).
                                                If the Monarch chooses to, they can also make the Action to use the aforementioned Mandates an Instant one, although this means that the Monarch only has 1 roll with which to gather the required Successes.
                                                The fact that the transmutation is now Instant means that it can be used as an attack. Before rolling to see if the Mandate succeeds, the Monarch chooses whether to inflict (successes*2) Bashing, (successes) Lethal or (successes/2) Aggravated damage.
