                            Originally posted by Acrozatarim View Post
                            On Butchery:

                            Assume that the line 'For the duration of the Facet and as long as the Rahu is engaging more than one enemy' now reads 'For the duration of the Facet and as long as the Rahu is engaging more than one enemy in melee'

                            If there are five guys, but four are hanging back, you don't get the Butchery Tilt-infliction on your one opponent who has dared step up to engage you in a rousing competition of fisticuffs. Same if the others are shooting at you.

                            I'll poke Stew about sorting that for the published version.
                            Well in that case my only real quibble is the weird interaction where multiple opponents may be preferable to a single one.

                            To provide an example: A frequent recurring PC in my game, a Vampire Duelist to be specific, has a prodigious standing defense of 14 (I assume Uratha can achieve something similar with the proper gifts). Another character, a Rahu with a slightly less developed combat suite has about 11 dice on his average attack. In his case, even with Willpower, he basically cannot hit the Vampire (sure he could attack all-out, but that'll end poorly). However, with Butchery we run into a strange interaction. 1 vs 1 this Werewolf has slim odds of winning, but if the Vampire brings a friend... now suddenly when the Vampire attacks his sword arm breaks, his eyes are gouged, his knees buckle, etc.

                            It's a fringe case, but not so far on the fringe as to be impossible (it would have come up in my games, that's for sure). The proper ST response is, IMO, to say "sorry, you can't hurt him normally, being a berserk cyclone of carnage isn't going to help". Still, feels weird.
