            Originally posted by WHW View Post
            Assume you want to stat up a Competent FBI Agent PC using normal mortal chargen and no Experience. What kind of Attributes, Merits, skill buys and Persistent Conditions would fit the build?
            Attributes: Rounded. Dexterity and Wits 3 are important since so many Merits have those as prerequisites, but otherwise I'd just say don't leave any Attribute with a single dot.

            Skills: At least one dot in Academics, Computer, Medicine, Athletics, Brawl, Drive, Stealth, Intimidation and Persuasion. If you have points over you could add some more into these. They are probably common Skills, but not something every agent needs to focus on.
            Skills that all agents should have more than one dot in are Investigation, Firearms and Empathy.

            Merits: Quick Draw (handguns) and Professional Training (Investigaiton, Firearms and Empathy as Asset Skills). Maybe Language (Spanish) depending on what state the agent's in the most.
            A few dots spread over a few fighting styles such as Firefight, Grappling, Marksmanship, Martial Arts and Police Tactics depending on the agent's training and focus. There might be other fitting styles in Hurt Locker.
            Allies, Contacts, and Resources are Merits that should be granted by the job so it's up for the ST to say if you need to buy them or if you character gets them from backstory.

                It's going to depend on what kind of agent they are. A hostage rescue agent is going to have a vastly different set of skills than an intelligence analyst, and both from a forensic examiner.

                The FBI hires a lot of people with a wide range of skillsets, which is the point.

                At the least a special agent would have no 1s in Physical or Mental Attributes, a decent amount of Academics, at least 1 in Drive, Firearms, Weaponry.

                Tales from the 13th Precinct has 'minimum criteria' for things like cadets at police academy and detectives. If you're going for more of the media-driven idea of an FBI agent you might go with their detective minimums.
