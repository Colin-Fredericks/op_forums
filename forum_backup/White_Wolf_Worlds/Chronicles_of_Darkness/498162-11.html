                                                            Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post

                                                            However to me the problem is that the current write up is:

                                                            "a shared identity between survivors who mostly suffered similar abuses and cope with their trauma in similar ways"

                                                            When it should be

                                                            "a shared identity between survivors who each suffered different abuses but cope with their trauma in similar ways"
                                                            The latter is a freehold. The latter is changeling community in general.

                                                            The former is the little group of guys who go to the local Sonic after the meetings, share some drinks and hot dogs, and talk about the particulars no one else seems to gets.

                                                            EDIT: This stands true of even first edition: THe Fairest commiserated with each other because they suffered pleasures unfathomable to the others and had to rise above the sensation. Ogres could drink together when they couldn't for any other reason because of their involvement with violence. Darklings would give each other the nod of recognition for knowing that each other had to fumble through the dark paths.

                                                            It's just clearer now just what exactly those shared experiences are. They share not only choices, but places where they got hurt the most, and commiserate how these likenesses led them to where they felt they had to go.

                                                            I'm not a hundred percent sold on the story structure built in, BUT! The narrative provided is interesting enough with plenty of hooks to challenge, reinterpret, deconstruct, subvert, and such that I'm willing to play around with it for a bit before I go in and rewrite things.
