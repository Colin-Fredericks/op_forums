                            Originally posted by OsuNeko View Post
                            I wont to know why at medium and long distances penalty so small, it is considered that they are not, and at greater distances become almost impossible regardless of the circumstances?
                            Just to keep it simple and easy to manage. At close range you take no penalty, at medium you take -1, and at long you take -2; it's easy to remember, easy to use, and gets the idea across that shots at a greater distance are harder to make accurately. Beyond a weapon's long range, it's a chance die, because a gun can only shoot a bullet as far as it can shoot it. Beyond that range, you'd be lucky to hit anything you were aiming for.

                            The ranges themselves aren't the most realistic, nor is the difficulty curve, but it works well enough to use without slowing down play.

                            Originally posted by OsuNeko View Post
                            I have home rule, where I use "realistic" model with a quadratic dispersion. But it seems to use home rule without understanding, why the authors (which has more than 20 years digest this system) left in this form, are misdeal.
                            Quadratic dispersion sounds like way more math than I want to have to do every time a character makes an attack with a firearm. Even if you put it in a table for reference, I'd still have to look it up, which slows down play. Conventional wisdom in RPG design says ease of use is more important than realism.

                            That all said, I do prefer the way Range Bands work in the Hurt Locker preview more than the default. Instead of different weapons having different close/medium/long ranges, range bands are the same for any weapon. The penalty for hitting a target at 100 yards is the same no matter what gun you're using unless it has a scope. The only thing the weapon itself affects is its maximum range - the point after which you always use a chance die, because the gun can't fire a bullet further than it can fire a bullet.
