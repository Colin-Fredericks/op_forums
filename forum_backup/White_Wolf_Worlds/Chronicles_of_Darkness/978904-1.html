                                                I guess there's a few things here.

                                                1. Automatic successes (even if only applied after a successful roll) are much stronger than just adding dice.

                                                2. You could stack +dice with +successes.

                                                3. Adding Stamina would be the equivalent of a one-off hit buff, since one hit would strip the health right back off. The Stamina also doesn't add auto-successes when added to a pool.

                                                4. The teleport spell would be Withstood, and need sympathy, which would increase the Withstand rating

                                                On the other hand, there is already a spell (Grave Misfortune) that increases damage if the target damage, to one particular subject. It's limited to that target, but also applies to ANY damage dealt.

                                                So...I forgot about Grave Misfortune previously. My bad. I'd probably be ok with it as a Fate spell, maybe a Death spell, or a Matter spell, or something else with a creative approach.
