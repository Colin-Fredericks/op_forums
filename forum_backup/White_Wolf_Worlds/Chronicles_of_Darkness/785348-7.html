                            Originally posted by lnodiv View Post

                            That is a good question. I'd also like to know (genuinely, I'm not sure how most of this works) how long a company can ignore abuse of their IP without losing various rights to it. I imagine it's something of a balancing act between the two points, for something like the subnet. Did Paradox pursue this too aggressively as the new owners, or is this relatively par for the course in order to protect their new property?
                            You don't lose your copyright if you don't enforce it. As a copyright owner, you're free to decide whether to go after people infringing on your copyrights or not. If you don't enforce your copyright, then infringers might get bolder with what they do about it. But legally, you can still take them to court if you decide to do so.

                            It's explicitly trademarks where not actively defending your trademark can lead you to legally losing the trademark. Trademark and copyright are two very different types of intellectual property and protected very differently under the law.
