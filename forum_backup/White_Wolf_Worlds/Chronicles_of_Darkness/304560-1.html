                                                    Page 63, left column - "I had known this slit corpse would become the instrument of my betrayal, perhaps I would have turned away from this particular shade of red..." feels like it should read "Had I known".

                                                    Page 69, right column - "The build shantytowns, ships of fools and anarchic cults..."

                                                    Page 69, right column - "men and women brought up under new, made regimes"

                                                    Page 69, right column - "Better to defile mystic vessels so that their enemy's dupes send a measure of poison with every one the sacrifice to the powers beyond."

                                                    Page 70, left column - "He, who had been a slave the Keeper abused for pleasure had been bound to his lord through the Rite of Return. It was an insult from his betrayers." The main problem, I think, is the use of "his" to refer to Awa in the first sentence and, I think, the Keeper in the second. The first sentence is also worded rather clumsily.

                                                    Page 70, right column - "They made the lords of Irem to conquer humanity, break it with civilized ways, and send its dead to attend training them in A'aru." The word "them" should just be removed from this sentence.

                                                    Page 70, right column - "They were responsible for Awa's suffering, and the plight of all mortals, who've been thrown into the service kings, priests and presidents." Missing the word "of" in there.

                                                    Page 72, left column, "Impure Syllable" description - "If the corrupted vessel gets sacrificed to Duat, this counts as a use of the vessel, but inflicts a -3 dice penalty to the Descent check." Given the higher cost (a permanent dot of Willpower) for letting it go this far, and that feeding poison to the Judges seems a bit more impressive than merely tainting their servants, this should be a +3 bonus rather than a -3 penalty, surely? Descent checks get worse when more dice are rolled, rather than fewer.

                                                    I'm also not entirely sure how this one is meant to work in general, because I was under the impression most vessels are sent to Duat when the Mummy returns to henet, implying that they have hit Sekhem 0 anyway.
