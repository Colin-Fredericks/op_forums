                                            Originally posted by Crowley View Post
                                            im still reading up on VtR (recently ordered second ed through drivethroughrpg), as i read it im working on my setting game. i got a question about old vampires, im currently working on a vampire from the north american civil war (who happends to be a sheriff). but as i read the book i wonder more about the rules on elder vampires, how potent would this character's blood be. and if being older he must decide to go to torpor every once on a while or can he lie down in peace for a bit? to explain to me please how being an old vampire works
                                            Blood potency is at least 1 + 1 more per each 50 years active. So your vampire could have as much as blood potency 4 (say embraced in 1865, +1 BP in 1915, +2 in 1965, and +3 in 2015). Blood potency can be increased further with experience (5 experiences per bp) and diablerie.

                                            Torpor is not required when your blood potency reaches 6. However, it may be desirable if you have no way to feed. There are several ways to feed outlined in the core rules (the ability to feed on other supernaturals, an Invictus merit that allows a vampire to accept tithes of blood from Kindred in their territory, and an Ordo Dracul scale that enables vampires to feed on specific mortals as if the vampire's blood potency were lower)

                                            Beyond that I'm not sure what you're asking when you ask "how being an old vampire works."

                                            Here's a thread discussing how BP 6+ elders might deal with feeding requirements: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...lood-potency-6
