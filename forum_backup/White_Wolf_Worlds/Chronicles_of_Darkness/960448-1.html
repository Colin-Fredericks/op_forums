    I don't know about a single use of a power, but yeah, I'd argue any repeated and clever use of an appropriate discipline could drive someone to suicide (or snapping and getting themselves killed). Nightmare, being all about exploiting the fears would easily drive someone nuts since no medication would help and suicide would be an easy way out. Dominating someone into doing stuff they don't remember (like antagonizing their loved ones or dominating the loved ones to do it) or messing with their memories could have the same outcome. Of course, the hard part is getting in touch with them regularly enough.

    Another really fucked up way to do this would be drugs. I say you can't dominate someone into doing something they realize could kill them, but dominate them (or push them with majesty, jesus, a drug dealer/pusher with majesty would be a nightmare) into putting needles into themselves, then dominate/majesty them into "come on, I'm augmenting the dose this time, I want a bigger rush, you with me ?". Then, just don't call an ambulance when they OD. It's not exactly suicide, but the end result is pretty much the same.

I'd make the Children of Judas unable to embrace *except* post mortums on suicides as their weakness, plus another small thing that makes them dependant on making people sad... maybe they lose the first (10-Humanity) Vitae they take from victims unless they are depressed, suffering from a persostent condition resulting from a breaking point, or engaged in self harm in the past 24hrs.

            Make disciplines Auspex, Despond, Resilience, Vigor. Upon joining the bloodline all Majesty dots convert to Despond dots. The character's dots in Celerity are traded for Resilience, and visa versa.

            As for Despond...

            Despond 1
            Aura. Can lash out to defend. People can't spend willpower on actions, but can on defenses. Failures become dramatic failures unless they spend a willpower. People take a penalty on all breaking point rolls equal to Despond dots. People in the aura gain Depression as a vice, which triggers if they inflict lethal damage

            Despond 2
            Overwhelm the target with sadness. They will cry and audibly lament about their hidden sorrows, which are magnified and brought to bare. The target gains the Insensate tilt, though they may engage in self harm unimpeded. Inflicting lethal damage on themselves ends the tilt. So long as the tilt lasts, if they do not spend willpower to do something else or engage in self harm, then they must talk about their greatest sources of sadness. The Child of Judas may guide the conversation to a specific sadness with a Manipulation + Empathy roll. The target will feel glad to have had someone to listen to them, and their impression of the vampire improves by one step. However, this doesn't cause the victim to trust the vampire, and they may regret their sharing the information and seek to contain it.

            Despond 3
            Give the target a Condition. Target becomes absolutely convinced that everyone hates them because they terrible and have done somethiing wrong and its all their fault. Friends arent just busy, they dont want to be near you because you are annoying. All rolls to interact with people socially are reduced to a chance die unless you spend willpower to enhance the diceroll. Empathy rolls are hampered even then, the willpower only allowing the roll instead of adding dice. The victim also may not recover willpower except through Despond. Whenever the victim's beliefs are validated or they engage in self destructive acts (doesnt have to be physical) they gain a point of willpower and a beat. Condition can be shed by suffering a dramatic failure on a breaking point. Otherwise it lasts for one night, or a number of days equal to the vampire's blood potency if Despond 1 is active.

            Despond 4
            Sap the target's energy. They suffer a condition that gives a -2 penalty to all rolls and they can not heal through any means. Any activity time the target makes a non-reflexive roll they take one bashing damage from the stress.

            Despond 5
            Target takes lethal damage, which manafests as sogns of self harm. The target gains the addicted condition towards taking damage.

                            It was lightly touched on in this thread, and bringing it back might constitute as hijacking, but that does pose another question.

                            "Experience a breaking point as part of a vampire’s command." <- that is how you resolve the condition. How can you "experience a breaking point" without actually doing anything? Say shooting myself would be a breaking point, but how could I know that I have hit that breaking point without actually pulling the trigger?

                            It would be like "getting high" as a breaking point, but unless I actually physically smoke the whatever I havent hit that breaking point. I could sit there with it in my hand, even put it to my mouth, and not experience the breaking point of "getting high" because I didnt actually do it.

                            Or even if you take a glass plate. I think about dropping it and I know it will shatter, but it isnt actually going to get broken until someone drops it. Until the action of letting go has been done, the plate wont get break, and in some cases, the plate doesn't even break when it hits the ground.

                            If you are afraid of heights to the point of it being a phobia, you probably werent on the roof to begin with, but how can you experience a breaking point from.. the POSSIBILITY of jumping? The breaking point would happen when you are falling, when you had already jumped.

                            Dominate condition is better worded "Experience a breaking point when following the command," So when you lift the gun to shoot yourself, but the time you "experience the breaking point from following the command" It would be too late. Your condition would be resolved, but you would also be dead/dying. Simply holding a gun shouldnt be a breaking point. Pulling the trigger should be.
