      • The Transhuman Conglomerate: A megacorporation ruled by the cybernetically-enhanced technocratic elite, while maintaining control over their slave labor populace by promising them the chance for promotion. Strong Seer and GM-Cult presence.
      • The Imperium of Adam: A theocratic planet-state ruled over by a thousand-year-old god-king and his semi-divine children (who are all secretly various levels of sire or ghoul).
      • The Commonwealth: An anarchist utopia spanning a space that includes Old Earth, the Commonwealth maintains order through extreme social pressure, as well as the hidden remainder of Task Force Valkyrie, acting as a shadow government manipulating the people for their own protection and governing without their knowledge.
      • The Redeemed Empire: After interstellar war wiped out nearly all of humanity, it was up to the Prometheans to rebuild human society. Now the Created are publically known and welcome within the Redeemed worlds, but are kept under extreme regulation in how and when they can come into contact with humans, as well as being kept to "Reservation Worlds" for 9/10ths of the Galactic Standard Year.
      • The Great Thorn Protectorate: An empire ruled as a royal court, the Thorn Protectorate contains within it a massive freehold, ruled by courts of the twelve constellations of western astrology. The leader of the freehold if eternally bound as the Knight Protector of the Thorn Protectorate's royal family, and to get to the Changelings, they must get to them through the Thorn Court.
      • New Kemet: An empire that styles itself as the new Egyptian Kingdom, the royal family of New Kemet is also the center of an ancient Mummy's cult. While it has been centuries since Kemet has ever needed their protector, the time of Sothis's ascent draws near.
      • The United Peoples of Earth and Alpha Centauri: After the Unification War of 2096, the world was left grasping for leadership, with a single charismatic leader now only known by his title of Administrator organized the world's peoples and turned us to the stars. He graciously stepped down and retired to seclusion after twenty years, his successor leading with the same skill and wisdom, until he, too, stepped down twenty years later. In truth, only the Administrator's body steps down, and his spirit has been ruling through the bodies of his apparent successors for over a millennium. He (or occasionally she, Admin isn't too picky about it anymore) been ruling over the the UPEAC with genuine benevolence, for as little comfort as this brings his victims, or those few who oppose his continued rule.

          The Sodality Of The Great And Bountiful Human Spirit: Less of an empire and more a roving church with enough power to rival a nation, the Sodality is a massive fleet of various sanctuary-vessels with a mysterious, almost impossible to detect form of FTL that allows them to slip in and out of sectors unannounced and unexpected. Despite their mysterious nature, they are fairly open with their nature-they are a multi-world confederation of various faiths from the great to the small, accepted to the arcane, most of whom may disagree on the specifics, but all agree on the fundamental nobility of the human condition and must shepherd it to its perfect state of perfect compassion and complete mastery of emotions. With mass drivers to remove wolves at the door, if needed-they are notorious for appearing long enough to reduce planetary capitals and military bases to rubble with incredible precision, or intervening on one side of a war or another without explaining their motives.

          Naturally, they are not quite who they say they are-but they are what they say they are. They just neglect to mention the Council of Doctrines that direct them is an extended Brood of Beasts and their Sanctified kin who view this whole business as an extension of the mandate to teach humanity.

                  Confederates States of New Pangea. A multisystem government formed from the alliance of a number of small planetary governments and active celestial bodies for mutual protection and security. Unlike many other governments, the territory that the Confederacy occupies is not fully or even largely within Real-Space, specifically, large chunks of the Spirit World overlap with the Void space, effectively creating a massive Verge area that spans hundreds of light years called the Expanse. Most planets and stars in the Expanse are rather self aware and involved in the material universe and during the PreConfederacy period were vying for control over the whole Sector and were in that habit of forming massive heirarchies. The human colonists of this era found themselves in a rather lucrative yet dangerous relationship of being both resources and enemies to verifiable gods.

                  The Confederacy came into being as unknown and unforseen alien invaders came and devoured the actual souls of whole world's using living starships.

                  It was not until Adrian Shepherd of Eden Prime, a werewolf, lead a desperate offence against the first wave of invaders and devastated the invaders as the devoured the heart of Star. It cost him his life, but in that daring act of defiance, the Confederacy was born, in honor of the long forgotten world where there were truly no limits between flesh and spirit.

                  The Confederacy is best described as a military republic. It's governing bodies are divided in a number of ways. Generally speaking, spiritual leaders are given positions as permanent ministers over specific fields in their local colonies, while humans and their derived supernaturals are given executive powrr, largely because most spirits are not suited to being heads of state. The high ranking spirit entities don't mind, as long as their needs are met and deals fullfilled.

                  The military part of military republic comes in in the sense that you need to have atleast some military or government serviceto vote, and a requirement to be atleast be given a medal of service to hold public office.

                  The Confederacy is noted for its extremely high werewolf population. Most are not sure of why, but many speculate that the conditions of the Expanse are most favorable to reawaken the strange heritage of their blood. Other werecreatures are common sights well.Werewolves are not given a choice on whether to join in the military or not; they are required to serve 4 years: though some slip between the cracks. It is estimated that 30% of the entire armed forces is a were creature of some kind, 80% of which is werewolf.


                  Though Luna herself is a distant memory, Auspices are granted to werewolves upon the start of their training, depending on what patron has chosen them.

                  Most mundane humans require powered armor just to compete, and some of the best operators find themselves being selected into becoming mech pilots before they see field training.


                  All Spaceships of sufficient size are bonded with powerful spirits, replacing the needs of artifical intelligences ships outside the Expanse often possess. These powerful vessels form the back bone of the Confederate fleets.

                  There is no shortage of enemies the forces of the Confederacy face, because the Bugs always seem to come back.

                  Just because there's a lack of werewolf or spirit centric governments. And yes, I managed to combine Starship Troopers, some of Mass Effect, and werewolves and Pangea all into one entry.... it was not intentional.

                                              For the human/hunter empires, I thought I take ideas (liberally) from Tech Infantry, Mind's Eye Theater's Space: The Infinite Edge, and EVE Online (fitting since it was CCP Games that attempted to make a WoD mmo.)
                                              • A Federation uniting colonies founded during the Second Exodus, when sleeper ships fled a ruined Earth and Sol System. With them they took Task Force: VALKYRIE to investigate supernatural crime.
                                              • A league of Megacorporations, large and powerful to exist as sovereign entities outside of the federation's control. One of the massive worlds controlling conglomerates include the Cheiron Group as a major subsidiary.
                                              • The semi-feudalistic Empire founded by colonies settled by the generation ships of the First Exodus. The Inquisitors of the Malleus Maleficarum watch over the flock of the Empire's church, as the noblemen of the Knights of St. George defend it from supernatural threats.
                                              • The revolutionary Republic founded by imperial colonies that broke away from the empire, seeking liberty and freedom. Various compacts and conspiracies protect these more-or-less unified collection of systems from the paranormal.
                                              • Transhuman species created by First and Second Exodite colonists, seeking to either to adapt to less hospitable environments or augment themselves genetically or cybernetically.
                                              • There can also be clans of nomads and a kingdom of powerful nobles that seceded from the Empire.
