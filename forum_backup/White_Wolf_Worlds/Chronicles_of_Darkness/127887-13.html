                                Translated Words:

                                halan: The relatives of other, non-werewolf shapeshifters, who (depending on species) may carry the genetic potential to pass on their relatives' changing abilities. May also be known as "halagarum".

                                gihurah: a ward, a charge; a person or persons under protection

                                zifiurhir: African Wild Dogs, also known as African Painted Dogs

                                uthanna/uthfil: Gold in the sacred sense (such a sacred artifact of gold) and gold in the destructive sense (such as a golden bullet that could affect gold-sensitive shifters, like the Corax of oWoD).

                                amaduthanna: "Mother Moon's silver", or the moonsilver of oWoD, which does twice the damage normal sil'ver does and is only obtainable by going on a special quest for the Moon. May also be known as "amaduthfil" if the moonsilver obtained is used for harmful or destructive purposes.

                                gumuthanna: "Burning gold", or the sungold of oWoD, which does twice the damage gold does to shifters sensitive to it. Obtainable only by doing certain quests for the Sun, and may also be known as "gumuthfil" if used to do harm or cause negativity.

                                shulor: octopus, squid, cephalopod

                                kuluhunirih: something that causes great fear, a.k.a. "shiver-making fear"; dread, foreboding

                                yeh’ren: wrong, twisted, a mockery or unsettling false image; the Uncanny Valley effect

                                kun: whale, cetacean

                                ket: shellfish; many also apply to any ocean-bearing creatures with a shell

                                kirul: sea monster; spirits (which, by themselves, are also known as kirulath) or mythical/monstrous beings of the deep ocean, some of which resemble Cthulhu or other Lovecraftian beings

                                su’ba: a shepherd, a herdsman

                                Asadah: An incredibly blasphemous, horrific term to Anshega and Urdaga alike, it refers to the disgusting and terrible acts the Bale Hounds perform in service to the Maeljin. It is not a term used by either Pure, Forsaken or Ghost Wolves/half wolves, but by the Bale Hounds themselves.

                                mumarum-keli: “Rumble-throat”, the FT name for a capercaillie

                                mumarum: To rumble

                                For Wild-Eyes:
                                Dread Wolf:
                                Urniriha’akdu (“wolf that commands fear itself”)

                                He Who Is Empty of Fear:
                                Nirih Siduk
                                Nirih-Siduk
                                Nirisiduk

                                The Shepherd:
                                Lu’u Su’ba
                                Lu’u-Su’ba
                                Lu’usu’ba

                                OR

                                Eren Su’ba
                                Eren-Su’ba
                                Erensu’ba

                                        in'im: a word, a saying, a phrase (compare "namthuf", which refers more to the written word, rather than the spoken)
                                        namthumuth: ink (lit. "the blood of writing")
                                        namthumuth'na: inkstone
                                        umfisah: scholar, scribe
                                        namfisamthuf: calligraphy, font work (lit. "the scribal/scholarly arts")
                                        namthumuzif: a tattoo (lit. "ink-flesh marking")
                                        namthumusu: a full-body tattoo (lit. "ink-bodied")
                                        sar: in addition to meaning "each", it also can mean "to write"
                                        safarah: brush, writing tool (lit. "writing's father")
                                        sarama: page, writing surface (lit. "writing's mother")
                                        dabh: a flat surface; to flatten
                                        dar: to crush, to split, to grind; to kill slowly
                                        saranis: paper (lit. "writing leaf")

                                        Litheon:

                                        "As I mark the paper with my brush, I will mark the shadow with my will.": Asku kusafarah zifdu sarama, asku kunamm zifdu Hisil.


                                        Perverseness:

                                        "Don't start none, DON'T BE NONE!": Nuku nuduaf, NUKU NUKAL! (lit. "You don't start anything, you won't be anything.")
