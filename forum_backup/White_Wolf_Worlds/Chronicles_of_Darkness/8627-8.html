                Modifying these a little bit, looking for some feedback.

                Forced March (Celerity 2, Resilience 2)

                Cost: 2 Vitae
                Dice Pool: None
                Action: Reflexive
                Duration: One Scene (One hour)
                This devotion serves as a popular means of overland transportation for Kindred who have learned to toughen their bodies to withstand the dizzying power of Celerity enough to aid their movement for hours on end. The vampire can reflexively spend Vitae to increase her running speed, multiplying her speed by [Celerity + 1] for one hour. While this version of Celerity is invoked, the Kindred cannot use any of Celerity's other active functions without disabling the Forced March, requiring it to be reactivated.
                This Devotion Costs 1 Experience to Learn

                Vaulting (Vigor 2)

                Cost: 1 Vitae
                Dice Pool: None
                Action: Reflexive
                Duration: One Scene (One hour)

                This easily formulated Devotion allows the Kindred to spend 1 Vitae reflexively to multiply the amount of feet jumped by a Successful Strength + Athletics roll by her Vigor dots plus two. This allows the Vampire to jump even greater distances than the enhanced strength of Vigor typically allows. Obviously this power might be a Masquerade breach for anyone who witnesses the unnatural capability of the Kindred.

                Example: Diana is a Daeva who wants to make a vertical standing jump up on top of a semitruck, which is 12 feet tall. She rolls 4 successes on her Strength + Athletics + Vigor roll, allowing her to jump 4 feet up in the air. She spends 1 Vitae reflexively to activate Vaulting, which given her Vigor of 3, increases her jump height from 4 feet to 20 feet in the air. She deftly lands on top of the truck.
                This Devotion Costs 1 Experience to Learn

                Changes made include lowering the Experience cost of Forced March to reflect that it's a minor trick that those Kindred who invest in Resilience and Celerity can rather easily figure out.

                Vaulting I thought might be more economic if it lasts for a scene. Really makes for some cool jumping Vampires. Do you guys think it should go back to being for one turn instead, or do you prefer it like this? I want to suggest it to one of the players in our group as a cool investment, worried the per turn cost might make it unattractive.

                                        I don't know if this exactly counts as homebrew so much as a glorified list, but I've had a few people request the link to the original thread I posted it in, and I've had good feedback on it. I've added some stuff as well.

                                        Yossarian's Ad Hoc Requiem for Rome Conversion:

                                        Senex

                                        Advantage: Carthian Law ("Corpus Juris")
                                        Merits: As with Invictus in B&S

                                        Cult of Augurs

                                        Advantage: Veneficia, but use the rules for Cruac in Blood and Smoke, albeit with Veneficia's dice pool
                                        Merits: As with Circle of the Crone in B&S

                                        Lancea et Sanctum

                                        Advantage: Theban Sorcery
                                        Merits: As with Lancea et Sanctum in B&S; optionally, it would make sense for them to also have a version of their advantage in the Pit of the Nameless scenario from B&S, to reflect their tight knit community:

                                        Originally posted by Blood and Smoke
                                        ...Lancea et Sanctum members receive Retainer, Herd, and Haven dots equal to their Lancea et Sanctum Status. This reflects the church’s collective pool of resources that they share amongst one another.
                                        Edit: If you took that option, it might be better to change out Retainer for Contacts. Give them more of that "early Christians on the run from the Romans" feel.

                                        Legio Mortuum

                                        Advantage: Blood Oaths
                                        Merits: For each dot of Status, Legio vampires gain a dot in Professional Training (Soldier) or in a Fighting Style. Additionally, they may purchase the Invested Merit, but with Allies replacing Herd.

                                        Peregrine Collegia

                                        Advantage: All Carthian and Invictus Merits, except Blood Oaths and Carthian Law.

                                        Julii

                                        Options:

                                        1. Use the Ventrue Bane in B&S.

                                        2. Use the Julii Bane as written (it does fill the prerequisite of scaling with Humanity).

                                        3. Use PenDragon's custom Daeva Bane:

                                        Originally posted by PenDragon
                                        The Degenerate Curse
                                        Daeva are prone to whims, flights of fancy and acts of impulse. The further a Daeva’s Humanity descends the more they find themselves compelled to act on their Dirge. A Daeva should act on this impulse at least [8-Humanity] times per chapter. This means a Humanity 7 Daeva is only slightly more degenerate than a normal vampire, while at lower Humanity they find themselves indulging at every opportunity. Daeva Draugr lose their mask entirely, and must act on their Dirge at every conceivable opportunity.
                                        4. Use this:

                                        Originally posted by Yossarian
                                        Clan Bane (The Debased Curse): Julii are ruled by base desires. A Founder retains her mortal Vice: she cannot regain Willpower from it, but her Beast urges her to act on it all the same. Whenever an opportunity arises to act on this Vice, roll the vampire’s Humanity. On failure, she gives in to the impulse and gains the Debauched Condition. While Debauched, she suffers a cumulative –1 penalty (up to –5) to further Humanity rolls to resist her Vice. Resolution comes when the Julii finally indulges herself, or has a meaningful connection with her Touchstone.
                                        Last edited by Yossarian; 05-19-2014, 01:57 AM.

                                                Oh yeah! Lemme dig that up...

                                                The Decadent Curse: Julii are prone to excess. It may be that the Embrace amplifies the desires and entitlement the noble-born Founders experienced as mortals, or, as some only whisper, it may be that the debauchery of the Striges has tainted them through the unholy covenant that led to their creation.
                                                System: Whenever a Julii wants to stop feeding, she must roll her Humanity not to become lost in the pleasure of the Kiss. On a failure, the Julii gains the Indulgent Condition:
                                                Indulgent

                                                Your character wants to extend the pleasure of feeding as much as she can. She must drink at least double the Vitae she previously consumed before gaining this Condition, even if that would force her to exceed the limits of her blood pool. Excess Vitae are spent or vomited up (some monstrous vampires revel in this act).
                                                This is not Wassail. The character is in control of herself despite the compulsion, being able to change vessels from moment to moment if she so desires, just as long as she drinks at least 1 Vitae for roughly each turn until the Condition is resolved.
                                                Failure to do so (whether there is no more blood available or because she was forced to stop feeding by an outside force) may prompt an anger Frenzy check.
                                                Possible Sources: Julii clan bane.
                                                Resolution: Drink double the amount of Vitae as you did before gaining the Condition or enter Frenzy.
                                                Beat: Suffer a Detachment roll due to the lengths you've gone to indulge yourself.
                                                There were a couple of versions of that. I can't remember who came up with what...I THINK Thorbes was behind that revision.
