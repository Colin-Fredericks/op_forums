                                                        Which is why this question is so difficult to answer in the CoD world. It completely tears down what most people in the real world believe a soul is supposed to be and replaces it with its own obscure version.


                                                        In literally every other form of media I've ever studied / come across, a soul is supposed to be the very essence of your being. Your body is just a container, the soul is what forms your personality, your ambitions, everything that makes you a person. Removing the soul has one of two consequences. Either A) your body collapses dead on the floor, or B) your body continues to function, but as an emotionless shell devoid of personality.

                                                        Replacing a missing soul with a new one does not restore the body's original personality. It replaces it with that of the new soul. In other words, the transferred soul possesses the new body and claims it for their own, or seeks to return to its original body if at all possible.


                                                        But in CoD, your soul is more like... I don't know, some kind of metaphysical battery system? It keeps the subject charged with motivation, but it's not the actual source of their motivation. A person's thoughts, actions and personality seem to be directly linked to either their physical body, or some other metaphysical aspect that nobody has mentioned here, yet.


                                                        It's all very confusing and seemingly impossible to give a satisfactory answer to the nature of what makes a person real or false.
