    Taking more damage when your track is full of bashing or lethal

    04-16-2015, 02:20 AM
    I'm not really sure I understand how to track the damage... on the damage track... especially if it's already full, in the particular case of vampires. I've just read this other thread (here: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...al-death/page2) and it helped clear things up... a bit.
    1. If, as a Vampire, your track is full of bashing (say a pool of 7, looking like: / / / / / / / ), you don't fall unconscious/incapacitated; you stay "awake" and suffer wound penalties (-3) to all relevant dice pools. True?
    2. If you suffer one more point of bashing, you swap the leftmost point of bashing on the already full damage track and convert it into a point of Lethal. Looking like: X / / / / / / ?
    3. If you suffer a point of Lethal instead, you do the same thing as above, meaning that contrary to a misconception (and I quote: «mistakenly thinking that damage that gets pushed off the right end of the Health track wraps back around instead of being ignored» see above link), if your track is full of bashing already, additional points of Bashing or Lethal both equate to the same result. Looking like: X / / / / / / ?
    4. If, for some reason, your track is full of Lethal damage, and you need to keep track the amount of added damage on top (be it because you got the Eternal Frenzy Coil, Rites of the Impaled merit, or even if someone is hitting your torpid corpse to get rid of you permanently), what does adding one point of Bashing do? Does it convert a point of Lethal to Aggravated or not?
    5. Does adding 1 bashing damage equate to adding 1 Lethal OR 1 Aggravated at this point, in terms of end results? All resulting in: * X X X X X X ?
    6. Also, should I assume that, akin to #3, any added Aggravated damage on the left doesn't make the rightmost Lethal "wrap back around" ?
    7. If I'm not mistaken, one could punch a Vampire to True Death, albeit slowly. True?


    To summarize:

    / / / / / / / (7 Bashing, full, Vampire is still conscious, has wound penalties of -3)

    Add 1 Bashing:
    X / / / / / /

    Add 1 Lethal:
    X X / / / / /

    Add 6 Lethal:
    * X X X X X X (Vampire should be unconscious and in torpor by now, unless merits, coils, etc.)

    Add 1 Lethal:
    * * X X X X X

    Add 1 Bashing:
    * * * X X X X

    Add 1 Aggravated:
    * * * * X X X

    (the above three doing the same amount of damage, whatever the type)

    Add 3 whatever damage:
    * * * * * * * (True Death, dramatic ripping of the sheet, or similar ritual)

    Is this true?

    If so, Rites of the Impale's second dot and Eternal Frenzy are a lot less useful than it would seem (the first is in a playtest document for Secrets of the Covenants, Ordo Dracul the second is in the Core rulebook):

    Beyond the Pale (••): Your character does not suffer torpor when her last Health box is filled
    with lethal damage, unless she also suffers aggravated damage equal to her Resolve.
    While he is in frenzy the Dragon will not fall into torpor even if all his Health levels are filled with lethal damage.
    The first means you stay out of Torpor until you take [Resolve] boxes of any damage (since they will all get converted into Aggravated once on the Track) before entering forced torpor.

    The second means you get to avoid falling into torpor once your track is full of Lethal, with only Final Death to stop you, which includes damage from all three types equally.



    Thanks in advance for any explanation/answer !

      You got it right. When your Health is full of one particular type of Damage, any additional damage becomes the next highest type of Damage. This accumulates until all your Health boxes are full of Agg, in which case your character is dead.
