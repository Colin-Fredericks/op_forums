            Multiple order status questions:
            1. What would it mean for a starting character to have high Order Status?

            2. Do you need to know Rotes to actually advance in the Mysterium?
              I am envisioning a Mage that is focused on spending his experience on growing in understanding (Gnosis) and capabilities (Arcana), but not much focus on formulaic efforts (Rotes)
            3. Can you get to the high status levels of Silver Ladder on Personal (not Allies, Contacts, Resources just Gnosis + Arcana) power only?
            4. Do you need to have/be part of Cryptopolies to get to upper levels of status with Silver Ladder?
            5. Which Order is most likely to have members dedicated to the Order financial matters almost exclusively?
              I am thinking Silver Ladder and Free Council, but how does the Mysterium finance itself? By selling to other orders? How does the Adamantine Arrow get money? Guardians of the Veil?
            6. Which Order is most likely to have members exclusively dedicated to the members security and well-being?
              A mix of human resources AND security office for the Order members. Could include the financial part: guaranteeing income for all members, that their living quarters are at least safe, and that they can be reached in case of need (whether their own needs or the order needing their skills).
            Thanks,
            Important note before I go into my opinions - the Orders aren't necessarily homogeneous monoliths. The Ladder in Japan is different from the Ladder in Brazil, for example, and while you can generalize the practices every one is going to be a little different. Further, the size and composition of a given Consilium is going to have a MASSIVE impact on how things are done. The thirty-some Consilium of DC is going to look very different to the five-man Consilium of middle-of-nowhere Alaska. Cooperation is also important - a Consilium whose FC is on the outs with everyone else (London, for example) probably doesn't have access to TELM.


            1. Free Council has a good chance of it happening - as I recall, force of personality and charisma are the deciding factors in electing Chairmen or being recognized as a Syndic. For the rest...

            Silver Ladder might give higher status to a former Cryptopoly/Mystery Cult member. As I recall, 1e's Order book explains how the SL handle low-level initiation for non-Awakened.

            The Arrow decides by strength, so if you challenged someone and won, you could "keep what you kill", as it were. Better hope you're popular though, because otherwise you're likely to be challenged yourself soon enough.

            The Mysterium is probably pretty Mystery-dependent, in addition to factoring in how much you've contributed to the Athenaeum.

            Guardians are pretty regimented, I imagine their promotion system hinges on how successful you are at your assigned duties.

            and for all of them, having dirty laundry on others might lead to a rapid promotion. Or assassination.

            2. I doubt it. It's a good resource to have when trying to make yourself appealing as a teacher, but scholarly work is more likely to be an important consideration for them.

            3. Potentially. The Ladder has a political bent, and showing you can further their aims without resorting to magic is one of the initiatory trials IIRC. You'll get less respect than someone with a higher Gnosis in the same rank would expect, but you'd still have clout.

            4. No, but like becoming a Factotum, it's a good feather in your cap to make you look good to the bosses. Also provides you with influence in the Fallen World and any new Awakened that come from the Cryptopoly.

            5. Most Actual Plays I've seen will mention off-handedly one or two uber-rich Mages who fund the rest of the Consilium. From the Broken Diamond it was Shore, and in The Man Comes Around there was a stockbroker for TELM.

            It's also not difficult for a Mage to be wealthy -although it's hard to be a Life doctor unless you get good forged credentials or were already certified, or take the time to go back to school- and honestly quite a few minor concerns can be handled with Magic. Need something build, or maintenance done? Matter Mage gets some practice in. Can't afford enough food this month? Use Mend or Knit to stretch your larders. A Time Mage can play the stock markets like nobody's buisness, and a Mind Mage can skill-monkey their way into just about any profession.

            It's also going to depend on how unified the Consilium is. In one where everyone gets along, the Mysterium can probably just ask the Hierarch and Council for a blank check to build a new Athenaeum. In one where they don't get along, every order probably has their own revenue streams and occasionally Clash over them.

            Also worth remembering that anyone might join any order. You could easily have an AA doctor, or a SL gangster. It's really going to depend on the local landscape.

            6. Entirely subjective to the location and disposition of the local Mages. The cult-ier Orders are probably more likely to have compounds though.

                            Originally posted by lbeaumanior View Post
                            What would it mean for a starting character to have high Order Status?
                            Something's up, for which the individual character's situation should probably provide an explanation given that, as you observe peripherally, even mage societies that don't talk about Gold Laws still tend to favor recognizing mages for their mastery of the Arcana. As with putting a disproportionately high starting rating in other Traits, players should be expected to have an answer for where that comes from if it seems to produce weird results in a vacuum.

                            Do you need to know Rotes to actually advance in the Mysterium?
                            Not on paper, but transmissible knowledge is certainly a useful currency to spend in the Order that has its own named economy of favors.

                            Can you get to the high status levels of Silver Ladder on Personal (not Allies, Contacts, Resources just Gnosis + Arcana) power only?
                            If you can get the Caucus to recognize you in that regard, sure.
                            Which Order is most likely to have members dedicated to the Order financial matters almost exclusively?
                            One of the many advantages of 2e being more explicit about the Orders being more than their Awakened membership is that this is a non-issue unless someone's pursuing a Mystery related to it. The Orders run cults, make connections, dip their toes into organized crime, and so on — if they need to secure funds, they have people for that. Sleepers and Sleepwalkers don't have a pressing metaphysical focus on personal mystical R&D to keep them away from the distractions of cooking the books, drumming up funds, and making sure the budget is balanced.
                            Which Order is most likely to have members exclusively dedicated to the members[/B][B] security and well-being?
                            If this a question assuming that the Orders work like open institutions, I would point out that you are talking about global conspiracies that mostly work with fronts and other obfuscatory measures, and that as the most generally knowledgeable groups about the Awakened condition they are going to know better than to put their constituents' day-to-day living situations in frequent contact with active wizards. You were ostensibly trained by a mentor who was likely to be held responsible if things turned out badly for you, and "graduating" to the point of being trusted to handle yourself generally means the local Caucus knows you can handle yourself (or at least keep in touch with your peers enough to get help if something goes wrong).

                            Just as the Consilium is a court of adjudication rather than a governing body, belong to a Caucus is not active military service or an extended monastic retreat or an undergraduate program. If you are dealing with work for the Order that entails maintaining a secure site, someone is going to be responsible for that site, and the particulars will vary based on the work, the site, the Order, and all manner of other conditions.
