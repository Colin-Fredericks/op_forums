                Now, Promethean in of itself can be made to work within the Wild West.

                Their Pilgrimage in of itself being the Created heading out on the frontier to see and be among settlers moving West. From seeing ordinary people trying to make a new life and home in a new location. Humans struggling to make that new home running. About how one person can make all the difference (to go with "lone gunslinger" fiction). And of course, observing and meditating the worst of human qualities like greed when it comes to bands of outlaws, land/oil/cattle barons, and of course...the issues of white settlers vs Native Americans.

                So Promethean can work in a Wild West story...just that it's not synonymous with Westworld's story.
