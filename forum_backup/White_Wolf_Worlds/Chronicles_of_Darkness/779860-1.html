                                    On the first paragraph. When it comes to stories concerning Sirens and Mermaids and them being depicted as "Spirits, Demons etc", that's pretty much cultural variation rather than gameline differences. For example, Vampires were once categorized as Demons in folklore, yet they bear enough differences to make their own gamelines. As for "Sirens have abit of the same things as other gamelines do", the things you've listed were purely in cosmetics. Gangrel vampires appear like werewolves, Leechfinger Changelings could be mistaken for vampires etc.

                                    And when it comes to that certain concepts not existing before until the WoD put them that way, I must note that they actually did exist before then, and the WoD only conceptualized them afterwards. Mage Towers are basically the concept of the witch stories of them following paths of magic or the like (Left Hand Path (not the supplement) was once a term for evil magicdoers). As for Werewolves, there have been stories of Werewolves being men channeling/being possessed by wolf spirits, dealing with familiars or even being commanded to be into a wolf form for their sins. As for Mummies, Egyptians had a thing called a Cartouche. They're basically a hieroglyph depicting a person's name that was put with them after death, and if the person's Cartouche was destroyed, it was believed that the person would then die forever in the afterlife, aka cessitation of existence. Even though they weren't in the "one way" the (n)WoD puts them, they still existed as concepts for them before then.

                                    As for the Time and Water Aspects, there's only one glaring flaw I can see. The logic I get is "everything changes and nothing remains the same". By that logic, that would mean that Sirens wouldn't/shouldn't be able to Time travel at all because they can't return to how things were before. I can see what/where you mean to go with the Time motif, but in my opinion it would fit better in the sense of "History". It would keep the Time motif more in the sense of that you can change the future rather than changing the past. If I may go a step further, the "Deluge = Astral Realm" idea (sorry for not giving this idea up) fits with this, because the Astral Realm (mostly in Tenemos and Anima Mundi) contains the history of all of humanity and nature within it. You can't change the past, but you can see how it was and make a better future to where it won't happen again. Perhaps that's even the flaw of the Abyssal Sirens, that they didn't read their history, and as such are doomed to repeat it (aka the Deluge, there's been more than one story of a primordial flood wiping out life before it began again ). Perhaps as a final note on Time, Genius and Mage both cover it (especially Genius), so the idea of Time Travelling might not be good with Sirens. I do agree though that there should be more to Sirens than just "Ecowarriors" because then it would be kind of like Werewolf the Apocalypse, minus the "Technology is evullll" part.

                                    As for why Sirens should be fighting pollution, there's a very good reason why that the History motif covers actually. Let's go with the "Nothing remains the same" stance, because this is the best arguement for why they would fight against pollution. Nature's very nature (English at it's finest ladies and gents ) is to adapt and survive. Pollution and radiation change nature to adapt to this life, and become the new way nature forms. Sure, many things may die, but Nature doesn't. It simply changes to something else, even if how it adapts is very negative to the people living there. There's nothing worked out for Siren's paradise, although on part of the thread calls it "Babylon", which by now I'm very sure that Leviathan has as their "Paradise". We should probably take a name of another paradise, preferably separate from Atlantis since most nWoD games can easily be crossed over as easily as they can be played alone.

                                    As for Mage arcana fittingness, there doesn't have to be a gameline for every Arcana. Sometimes things in the depths should remain in the depths...although the Mage shouldn't whine about not being warned when his eyes are gouged out by whatever was in the Depths. (Just to note, that was meant to be a joke, not anything else. D: Ack sorry)
