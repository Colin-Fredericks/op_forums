    New to CofD and need some advice

    10-06-2016, 02:47 PM
    First I would like to say hello. I made a forum account some months ago but never found the right place to make my first post

    I recently bought and red CofD core rulebook. Almost immediately fell in love with the system and the vibe it had. Even got to play a few short games with a couple of friends but sadly they returned to their other games that they felt suited them better.

    Yesterday I decided that I want to invest in one of the supernatural splats but soon realized that no one in the area I live in plays tabletop games. I looked online for play by post sites but the few I found were for 1st edition and I would very much like to play 2nd edition.

    I figured I'd ask here for advice on which splat I should go with that is played more online. I plan to buy pdfs, hardcover and maybe some additional books for extra material so spending money on something I won't be able to use would be a bummer.

          The PbP community for CofD is pretty small, sadly. But, generally speaking if you're willing to ST, it's not too hard to find players. Vampire, Werewolf, and Mage are probably the three most central game lines to the setting, and you should be able to find players for any of them, just depends on which you find the most interesting. Changeling is possibly the best critically received, and is among the most popular, though it's not out for 2e just yet.

              Cannot find original post, but I copy this to my hard drive. Anyone responsible for that - thanks for this, and I hope I did not violate something sharing in here this summary of games.

              Chronicles of Darkness:
              The core rulebook and its line of supplements support play as mortal humans in a dark world, where the supernatural is powerful, unknown and striking. Strangeness, creeping dread, and something black in the back woods. Twin Peaks. The Mothman Prophecies. The Twilight Zone.
              1e World of Darkness rulebook. 2e standalone rulebook available.

              Vampire: the Requiem:
              You're a predator. Fast, sleek, alluring, powerful, and sooner or later, you're probably going to have to hurt someone badly just to survive. Bloody nights, turf wars, empty vessels and wondering what you can do to make your untimely rising worth the damage it causes – or if you still care.
              1e corebook requires World of Darkness rulebook. 2e standalone rulebook.

              Werewolf: the Forsaken:
              Half of you doesn't belong here. You were half-born to the other world, the life beneath the surface of the visible, where everything natural and artificial has words to talk and teeth to eat. You can smell blood, even when you're not trying. The blood shed by men, by the people of your other home, by your fellow wolves. The wolf inside doesn't care how you get it. It wants blood. The wolf must hunt.
              1e corebook requires World of Darkness rulebook. 2e standalone rulebook.

              Mage: the Awakening:
              This is not the best of all possible worlds. Your revelation has pulled back the curtain. You can see the stuff of truth itself hiding behind and within the world of matter and causality. You can speak secrets into the world. You collect them. They're your arsenal. You arm yourself with mysteries. There are others like you, who worship the darkest of truths and seek to hold back knowledge from mankind, to be the sole secret rulers of destiny. You can find ways to fight back. But truth is fragile. Grasp it too hard and it can shatter.
              1e corebook requires World of Darkness rulebook. 2e standalone rulebook.

              Promethean: the Created:
              You are a transgression. You were not the transgressor, yet you still carry the burden. Mockeries of life, mighty and helpless, powerful and ignorant, on a painful journey to understand the world they have unjustly been thrust into, the people they were meant to be, and to find somewhere they can belong. The pilgrimage is long, but it is not endless. Homeward Bound meets Frankenstein.
              1e corebook requires World of Darkness rulebook. 2e standalone rulebook.

              Changeling: the Lost:
              How do you find your way home when you're no longer the person who lived there? The Good Folk still take people. They vanish mysteriously, spirited away to alien kingdoms where the Lords and Ladies of Faerie, wreathed in fire and with voices like knives, delight in making stories of them. You know because you're one of the strong and lucky ones. You fought your way back. But your years in timeless Arcadia have changed you in ways you can see, smell and touch, even if the average person can't. The capricious impulses that carried you back from Faerie don't belong in the mortal realm. Even if the Gentry aren't riding out to find you, even if they didn't leave some false person of twigs and glass to fill the hole you left, there is no home waiting for you. You'll just have to build one.
              1e corebook requires World of Darkness rulebook. 2e standalone rulebook forthcoming.

              Hunter: the Vigil:
              Hunter: the Vigil supports play as mortal humans in a dark world, where the supernatural is powerful, unknown, and striking... but a shotgun at point blank will still do the job pretty good. Hunter scales smoothly from a garage of mechanics and shop clerks who can swing a mean wrench to government conspiracies and ancient brotherhoods wielding weapons as strange as the creatures they combat. Supernatural. Night of the Living Dead.
              1e corebook requires World of Darkness rulebook, with 2e update supplement available. 2e standalone rulebook anticipated.

              Geist: the Sin-Eaters:
              Life is short. You oughta know: you died. Just were one of the rare few offered a deal to ride back into the world of the living with a passenger so old it's forgotten anything about itself except how it felt to choke on soot and flame until its own life gave out. Your new backseat driver has its own perspective on life, as do the ghosts you couldn't see before, passing along your way. It sounds macabre, and geister have weird demands for their new living vehicles. But you've got a second chance, and if you keep your geist happy, this time around you're a bigger walking haunting than you could have been if you just stayed dead. You have a new lease on life. Make it count this time.
              1e corebook requires World of Darkness rulebook.

              Mummy: the Curse:
              You're old. You're tired. You've died hundreds of times and might die thousands more. The world has left you behind, forgotten when the gods walked among men, forgotten their divine commands, forgotten your empire, the first and greatest empire. Some days you feel you've forgotten yourself beneath the weight of ages. Your labor for the Judges of Death is not yet complete, and you wonder if it ever will be. Return to the world, claim that which must be consigned to the Duat on order of your great masters. Emerge from your tomb and lay your mighty curse, and then feel yourself wither away until you sink into the death-sleep, again and again. You are all that remains to remember glory.
              1e corebook requires World of Darkness rulebook.

              Demon: the Descent:
              God is not a king ruling from on high. God is among us and within us. God lives in highway layouts and shipping schedules. His roots stretch underground across deserts strewn with solar panels. His breath is encoded in high-frequency background radiation. You were one of those stars in the heavenly constellation, a divine node in a celestial network, gifted with perfect knowledge of your purpose in life and the means to attain it. You threw that away. You chose us instead: the teeming masses of humanity, our messy lives of romance, pleasure-seeking and drudgery. To escape the gaze of an angry God is to hide your luminous visage of terror and awe beneath a suit of identity, a false human woven from friendships, run-ins and acquaintences. If you let that facade slip, it might find you, and seek to claim you again for the divine purpose. But the beauty of God's essence being right among us, wired into our systems and living in our neighborhoods, is that those things are very responsive to dynamite. Burn Notice. Person of Interest.
              2e corebook requires World of Darkness rulebook. (Yes, it's the only 2e corebook that needs the 1e rulebook, or any of the 2e standalones would work as a substitute. It incorporates the 2e update supplement.)

              Beast: the Primordial:
              You are nightmare riding flesh. You're the featureless thing in the dark cave, the deadly stalker and the endless road on which he chases his prey, the maze that turns into itself, the shark in the water. You're not human, not this body of meat and bone, and when you lurk the places in which the nightmare lives, you show your true self. You crawled awful and hungry out of a hole in the heart of the world, and you feed off the terror and insignificance that gives you power. There will be no end. Only the feast.
              2e standalone rulebook.
