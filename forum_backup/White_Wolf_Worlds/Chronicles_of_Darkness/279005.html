    Is Halloween a special day in your chronicle?

    10-12-2014, 04:36 PM
    In the long-running Changeling game I'm in, we just did our Halloween story.* In it, the characters were surprised by an envoy from the Hedge, who want our artist character to stop painting gateways into places and times, lest they fall into the hands of the artist's former Keeper. In exchange, they offered our characters the information to rescue the minor hedge god who helped the artist escape.

    That wasn't the big thing, though. The big thing is that my character's Keeper has been a complete mystery, because my character only remembers her many attempts to escape, not what she was escaping from. Through the artist's paintings, we found out that my character actually has/will go in and out of the Keeper's pocket of Arcadia many times in her life, and that the Keeper experiences them in a different order from my character. Perhaps the worst revelation is that sometime in the future, my character will go with the Keeper on purpose.

    Do you do anything special for Halloween? What's happened in your games?


    (* We don't sync with the real time of year, it just happened by coincidence that October in our game matched up with real October.)

                                          Wow it must have been two years ago now.

                                          Our motley was at odds with the main freehold in the area.

                                          The King of the freehold was a cold loyalist, who no one believed was a loyalist. He had it out for one of our motley on a personal level (their Keeper was playing a game), and invited us to what would clearly be a trap. However, his messenger said we could bring along people if we didn't feel safe going on our own.

                                          So we brought everyone. We organized a huge Halloween party, got a couple bands, got invites out all over the local college and high schools and social media, someone even arranged a deal with the goblin market to sort of stretch into the party grounds. We got caught up in sticking it to the leader of the freehold, so we didn't really get to enjoy much of the party ourselves. It was over a year ago, but the highlights were a powerful spring court exile getting up on stage to perform, all sorts of hedge-drugs, this mysterious tattoo artist from the goblin market having a stall and doing his business (he took flesh for payment and pigment), dodging a hunter cell who was just barely aware of the existence of other worlds but didn't realize we were changelings, some time travel when we dodged the folks the King sent to catch us at the party and managed to get to his freehold just as he was sending them, and finding out the King had an army of skeletons. We also had t-shirts made.

                                          Later we found out about three dozen people went missing, most taken by the King. And we had gained the attention of a nasty pack of True Fae.

                                                      For my general games ( and tomorrow session ) I made nice in-setting idea…


                                                      Arcadia's Laws of Halloween
                                                      Probably made by first Autumn Royality with Faeire itself, those precepts are protecting both humans and changlings from being taken by Wild Hunt, that is especially often on All Hallows Eve. On this time of thee year, the veil between Earth and Arcadia is very thin and Gentry looking for they Wild Hunts, mainly for new humans slaves to be changed in to the Other. But if you are respecting those Laws, you will be safe - only most humans just don't remember why those Laws were laid, and treat them as harmless play. They also isn't just simple to broken - most of the time you need to word that you are all intentions on breaking them.

                                                      Arcadia's Laws of Halloween:
                                                      1. Don't be alone.
                                                      2. Disguise.
                                                      3. Don't break traditions.

                                                      First Law's meaning is simple - strength in numbers. You are safer in larger crowd. Also, True Fae are sensing weakness, particularly in that night, and being alone is viewed as such. There are tale even that sometimes being only in small group of people is not enough and is treated as violation of the First Law. So better take the invites and go to those big parties and balls – Autumn Court is making one each Halloween for your safety. Or if you need to spend time with humans, just go to large event – more crowded, the better. The problem is if you reject the invitation.

                                                      Second Law’s meaning is more obscure – it’s connected to the way True Fae perceive reality. Gentry always see the world past the Mask, so they see changlings Mein all the time. But when humans disguise in costumes as ghouls and goblins, the Others can’ see the difference. As most Wild Hunts are made to get new slaves, so mortals are most seized trophy. If it is not the case, still is better to mix with crowd of disguised mortals that looks like Losts army, than to be the one alone, don’t you think?

                                                      Third Law’s meaning is connected to the magic of the holiday. Beside the First and Second Law, this day has come to many smaller traditions that reproduce the behavior of Gentry – giving sweets to thee strangers, doing pranks and mischiefs, even calling down the dead. All this makes you look as one of Loyalist to the True Fae, one of their own. And so they will leave you be it.

                                                      Rules: When character is clearly points out with the word ( or sometimes even very strong deed is enough) breaking of one Law, Others will become interested in him. Depending on the power and nature of the Gentry - and the protective magic contained in this Freehold - it can be from one to five offenses. Usually, however, there is principle of the three offenses - where a third evokes the Wild Hunt on character. Every breaking of Laws, offender gets a signal from the Others that did offense - even if she does not understand the message. Offences can be of the same kind or different types


                                                      Example: Connor is jock, leader of local high school football team. He was planning to go on to this big party with his girlfriend and pals on Halloween, because he get invited, but they fight earlier that day and he said to her he don’t go. On the return to home Connor see the weird shadow of giant dog or wolf, going like from nowhere. He just assumes he was overseeing something.
                                                      When return to his home, his mother asking him if will disguise himself. Even if Connor has costume ready, he says he would not dress up. He sits on the couch and watch scary movies. In one time on this creepy werewolf flick, he hear load howling. His mother in the kitchen says she didn’t hear anything. Little scared, he plays on his console.
                                                      Later this night the Trick or Treaters come to his house. Being a bit angry on the holiday itself, he runes the kids from his porch. He then hear few howlings at once. He returns to home, to take the trash for his mother. When he is outside, back of the house, he see three hounds made of shadows that want to eat him alive! He throws a bag and run, in the woods, where the Arcadian Huntsman is waiting for him to be…

                                                      I hope it’s cool addition to the games and nice little story generator. I can’t wait for tomorrow’s game session, as my players will play guys hating Halloween. I suspect so much fun with this idea… ​
