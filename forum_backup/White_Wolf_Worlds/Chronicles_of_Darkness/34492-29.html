        434.Parasite
        The city of [Fill-In-The-Blank] has a picture-perfect Domain: an elder prince, relatively non-contentious primogen and Elysians that are truly respected.

        What no one knows, of course, is that the prince was killed by hunters, and something...else...wears his skin. Something old and hungry and clever dragged itself out of the wet earth, and it has a dire agenda.

        It is not a vampire. It is not some other form of supernatural from any of the other splats. It is something unique, a one-of-a-kind abomination that feeds on vampires. It can wear the skins of its victims, and the longer it is awake, the greater its hunger grows.

        It has made contact with Belial's Brood. It's plans for the city's Kindred is a blood-soaked, cannibal apocalypse..,

            435.The Great Bear
            A Native American Nosferatu elder, warped and twisted to appear as s gargantuan tardigrade, has emerged from a torpor of over a thousand years. Rendered weak by his long torpor, this Great Bear is lost in a world that makes no sense, where everything he knew as sacrosanct is upended into ruination.

            Unable to find a Touchstone, confused and terrified, the Great Bear's Humanity slowly slips away to nothingness.

            But this is not the end of him. His sentience does not fade. Belial's Brood finds him, and now he hunts the despoilers of everything he has ever known!
