    Speaking of Impetus and Clockstoppers, I had a though about both very recently thanks to some lovely videos I've recently viewed regarding actual science!

    An interesting angle to look at how Impetus is shaping up is very similar to the concept of Dark Energy, the theoretic forces that set the universe into motion and will inevitable lead to the heat death of the universe itself. In this take the Genii are able to channel that Dark Energy, the Prima Impetus, in order to accelerate physical and metaphysical phenomena into new shapes and forms that would otherwise be impossible with humanity's current level of technology. The Clockstoppers by contrast in this theory would be the unknowing avatars for the counterpart to Dark Energy: Dark Matter, the matter that binds the known universe together into something even capable of sustaining form and structure, let alone the potential for sentient life. Rather than an anti-Impetus force they would be more akin to a counter-Impetus force that act as Stasis' agents to negate strong outputs of Impetus in order to prevent reality from being irrevocably changed by the Genii's vision. But then again that's just some sci-fi musing I had. More than anything I'm just amazed that Impetus ended up sharing such similarities with an actual theoretical energy.
    That is definitely how a Genius with an astrophysicist bent might explain Impetus. It wasn't on my mind at the time, but it's neat. I might use that at some point.

                            Glad the Sci-Fi musings were enjoyed.

                            I have a couple more ideas based around the whole Dark Matter & Dark Energy thought, use them as you will or not at all if it please you.

                            • Impetus & Stasis as energy sources for Genii: In this scenario the Inspired have two power sources instead of one. Impetus functions as normal without any changes, Stasis on the other hand functions as a stabilizing energy to Impetus' creative/destructive energy. Wonders then would be created with a certain number of Links, the more one has the more advanced it is. Linking Impetus to a Wonder does exactly what was talked about earlier. It powers the Wonder and allows it unleash more power at the cost of potential Havoc. Linking Stasis to a Wonder however still powers it, but at the cost of loosing some of the more potent functions of the Wonder at the cost of eliminating the chances for Havoc and adding greater longevity and durability to the Wonder. Also, in this system Impetus would be far more readily come by since Stasis is rarer and potentially more dangerous to acquire since harvesting it is more likely to attract entities that are devoted to keeping the status quo. With this system Stasis would be needed to build the most advance Wonders, and balancing which Links are filled with which energy could potentially make for an excellent puzzle for player characters.

                            • Stasis and Banisher Equivalencies: In this scenario we introduce the Genii Clockstoppers. Or, more accurately, Genii who's vision is filled with all the possibilities for everything to go wrong. Instead of seeing Utopia they see 1984 or Mad Max potential in the labors of the other Inspired, so they instead work to prevent such futures from ever happening by working against their fellows. Like Banishers many might join together to form their own Stasis Foundations, some more terrifying still might become Unmada. But the two things they all have in common is a deep fear for what the future may bring with Genii behind the wheel and the ability to create Clockstoppers in the same way Genii create Beholden.

                            And that's all I've got for now. As mentioned these are just personal ideas I've had based around the work done so far. Great work is being done here, can't wait to see more.

                                                            Yeah, I'm not opposed. Making Larvae about a more personal severance from humanity. Larvae being used in a Wonder, or being used as an easy path to a Thesis. For example, it's a lot easier to study the effects of soul loss if you rip the soul out of somebody, rather than waiting for somebody to lose their soul.

                                                            As for Theses, I wasn't sure where to go with them, or if we should even keep them. The idea was that in order to learn a new Field, gain Inspiration, or gain a rank in a non-favored Axiom, you had to acquire the Thesis Persistent Condition from studying a phenomenon relating to the Field, Axiom, or your Paradigm, and then once you earned 4 Inspired Beats (1 Inspired Experience), you would then resolve the Condition and be able to gain the Field/Axiom Dot/Inspiration Dot. Using a Larva under this paradigm* would basically be equivalent to taking moral shortcuts, letting you learn a lot quickly, but at a terrible moral cost, immediately Resolving it and giving you the Inspired Experience on the spot, similarly to Diablerie being an easy way to get Experiences for Blood Potency at the cost of messing up what remains of their soul.

                                                            Off-the-cuff, rough idea for the Thesis Condition:

                                                            Thesis (Persistent)
                                                            In order to advance in their work, a Genius needs to perform studies to understand their subjects. This takes the form of a Thesis, a collection of observations and experiments that are intended to lay a subject bare to the Genius's Inspired knowledge.
                                                            Acquiring the Condition: A Genius can choose to acquire this condition upon encountering a new Inspired or otherwise Paranormal phenomenon, keying it to a relevant Field or Axiom, or keying it to their Paradigm (and thus Inspiration). What qualifies as "new" is vague, but there has to be a real qulaitative difference from anything they've yet studied. For example, a Genius could study a Mane capable of manipulating Orgone energy to get this Condition, and later study a different Mane that accesses Gestalt consciousness to get a different version of this Condition, but they can't try and study an Orgone Mane, and then another Orgone Mane. A Genius can have up to three versions of this Condition, one for a Field, one for a non-favored Axiom, and one for their Paradigm.
                                                            Beat: Each time the Genius further studies an Inspired or paranormal phenomenon relating to the subject of this Condition, they gain an Inspired Beat.
                                                            Resolution: This Condition Resolves when the Genius has gained 4 Inspired Beats from it. Upon Resolving the Condition, the Genius can now gain the Field, Axiom Dot, or Inspiration Dot they were working toward. The Genius can also use a Larva to immediately Resolve the Condition, gaining an Inspired Experience in the process.
                                                            Ending the Condition: A Genius can voluntarily end this Condition without Resolving it, effectively giving up on the avenue of study it represents.

                                                            Example Thesis: "A Dissertation on the Speciation of Non-Material Entities"
                                                            Doctor Astral has noted that, despite being the most important part of human reality, astral beings are emphatically the least common form of non-material beings in the universe. Seeing as that is the case, he's decided to study how the various ephemerals differ in order to better understand and control the Gestalt. This is keyed to Doctor Astral's Paradigm of Gestalt consciousness, and resolving it will allow him to increase his Inspiration.
                                                            Beat: Gain an Inspired Beat each time you learn the properties of a new ephemeral being, or find a new interaction between differing ephemera. Thus far, he has gained 2 such Beats, and is working on his third.
                                                            • Thus far, Doctor Astral has been able to study a being that called itself "Begotten" which appears to be some kind of human fear dredged up from the Gestalt and placed in a human body. Studying how its Lair connects the Astral to the physical world was quite edifying.
                                                            • Doctor Astral has also been able to analyze a ghost, and determined the symbolic significance of Anchors as they relate to the ghost's experiences, and has determined the ghost to be a psychic imprint of its former self upon those Anchors.
                                                            • Presently, Doctor Astral has been attempting to determine how Spirits are able to cross the Gauntlet, and what separates their animistic spiritual Gestalt from the human Gestalt, but has not yet made any breakthroughs.

                                                            Resolution: Upon gaining his fourth Inspired Beat from this Condition, it will Resolve. Doctor Astral's player, Roger, has also determined that he could make a Larva if he were to purposely cause his Touchstone, a kindly old neighbor named Lucille, to become possessed or Claimed in order to study its effects.


                                                            *Pun totally intended
