                                                  I'm actually a big fan of the subtle Arcana. The gross Arcana are more directly powerful but I just love how much more creative you can be with the subtle Arcana of each pairing.

                                                  I find that with the gross Arcana you solve problems by casting a spell directly and rolling Gnosis + Arcanum. Whereas with the subtle Arcana you're more likely to open up some options with your spellcasting, but then you use mundane skills and attributes in order to make the most of those options. I find the latter way of doing things more fun most of the time.

                                                  So here's my favourite subtle Arcana in order of preference.

                                                  Mind, I love for the Astral Realms. I'm not a big fan of most of the mind spells, they're too ethically dubious for the real world me to want to use them. However exploring the Astral would be amazing and Mind is the best Arcanum to interact with the Astral.

                                                  Fate, I love for just how dependent it is on mundane action. IMO there's no Arcanum that's more likely to lead to your mage actually going out, doing things and having fun adventures.

                                                  Prime, I love for how subtle it is. There's almost no interaction with the fallen world through Prime, however it lets you deal a lot more with Supernal reality. You're more of a mage than any other mage if you master Prime.

                                                  Death, I very secretly love for the forbidden secrets of working with the soul. While I wouldn't want to be a Reaper and steal other people's souls, I'd love to be a Moros and research them in order to find out secrets that no other mages has dared to learn (and reapers are too busy exploiting souls for selfish and practical purposes to ever really understand them). Shhh, don't tell the consillium.

                                                  Spirit... I don't much care for. Sorry to break the pattern. It's a great Arcanum and I've got nothing against its design mechanically, but the part of me that would like playing a spirit mage would much rather play a Werewolf. I tend to think of Spirit much more in Werewolf terms than I do in Mage terms.

                                                      And I'm here to always buck the trend. Always.

                                                      For me, it's Forces. I love it dearly not because it has a huge chunk of magical powers most people think of when they think of wizards (hurling fireballs, lightning bolts, flying, telekinesis, etc.) but rather because all those powers are the least imaginative uses of Forces. Manipulate light, sound, and pressure to create near-perfect illusions (yeah, you can argue that Mind does it a lot better, and you're right, but that's the thing, Mind is better, so other mages would suspect you're using Mind rather than Forces until they take an Active look), manipulate radio waves to control communications, create motherfuckin' black holes, turn a space vessel into pure light and move across the galaxies while magnifying the speed of light itself (Space magic won't help you go places no one has gone before), just to name a few.
