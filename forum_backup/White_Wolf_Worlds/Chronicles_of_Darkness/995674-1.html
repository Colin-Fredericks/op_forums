                    It seems pretty unlikely that no nuclear weapons would get used. They have a utility beyond the mass murder of civilians, especially when one country has a vast proportion of the worlds military power. If there's an American fleet steaming towards you, using nuclear weapons against it is probably your only option; and are the Americans really going to retaliate for your strike against a purely military target with a strike on your homeland? Hopefully not.

                    Plus, I gather much of the NATO doctrine for world war 3 was based around using nuclear weapons in a defensive manner. As the Warsaw Pact troops overran your position you'd pull back and detonate nuclear weapons in an attempt to stall them.

                    The relevance for here is that while warfare isn't all that great for the Shadow, I'd guess nuclear weapons probably punch wounds into it very easily.

                    Imagine that, a huge number of massive wounds out in the middle of the ocean where no one can see them.
