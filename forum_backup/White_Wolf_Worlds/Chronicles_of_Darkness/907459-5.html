    LokiRavenSpeak
    I get where you're coming from Raven. And in some ways I can agree with you. I find Mage frustrating to the extreme because I found character creation too limiting while giving no direction of play, much like you feel with many of the Lines. I find Atlantis/Hermetic Magic/and ancient Mystery dull and uninspiring personally, but admit they can be intriguing for others.

    But, here's the hard part, the books are rather casual and some STs are okay with that and some aren't. I know WW best so your comment on Uratha/ Spirits I can answer and hopefully give you some insight that this has more to do with intuition than facts.

    How does a Spirit react to a spirit with Uratha agents?
    It matters. Are they the same Ilthium or even Brood and the Spirit is using the Uratha to keep them inline? Rebellious most likely, but depends on that spirit's power and how much that group of Uratha have helped produce their essence in the past. If the spirit was forced and intimidated? It makes them look weak and very likely the uratha will be dealing with someone else who took control soon. If the Spirit is weaker but is bullying a tougher Spirit with Uratha aid? Probably view above their station and seen as cowardly, like someone might think of a fink to the police. This of course ignores if the Uratha agents are not Pure who are respected by the Spirits more, especially Fire Touched who serve the Shadow and may be viewed as just a clever play unless personal treachery between the two spirits are at play.


    Is there a difference between a spirit part of the pack or owing favours?
    Yes Absolutely. It's the difference between Family and someone you bribed. A totem especially defines a Pack, makes it a family. Who it is has to be adopted by the pack and incorporated into who they are, and the spirit gives part of itself and ability to give the Pack power. How much the pack invests in their totem and its goals determines how much the spirit can give itself in return. The bond is near religious, making the ban something the spirit literally cannot do and thus part of the Pack as well. It is the most beneficial relationship many Uratha have with their spirit halves. Even normal spirit packmates are treated the same as other packmates; family, closer even than blood. Favours are favours, I did something for you so you agree you owe me, or because you did this I owe you, no sincere relationship need apply.


    How much does a court affect the other side?
    That's complex and interwoven. Drug spirits are born because someone used drugs, maybe because depression spirits fostered that action or not, but once they exist they will influence the world around them using their, well influences. They try to get as much essence to stay awake and get more to increase their power or to defeat their rivals. Each spirit has a bent who encourages that influence and the reaction is based on power. If a weak spirit is taken out not much difference in the human world unless that spirit was closely attached to the individual that birthed it, likely the human will have a decrease of that influence IF the catalyst that birthed it in the first place didn't exist. If The Coup of drug spirits have a drug spirit representing the exhilaration of a stimulant be replaced by a drug spirit representing obsession and the push of addiction, you better believe that will change in the real world. None of the users are new or young, perhaps the addiction spirits pushed it in that direction. Also realize spirits are very much local creatures so can't adapt quickly to changes come from outside their influence. A city evolves and change as the people inside it, but the new suburbs are ruins in the Hisil because out of nowhere old nature spirits were deprived of essence. The new people are creating spirits in a vacuum without strong ties until history allows the spirits to grow. Never mind the gauntlet can show how easily each place can be affected with it's thickness. The old cemetery? Each political coup has a huge effect, the new modern hospital? The spirits are just trying to figure out what the place is barely intelligent enough to have any politics rather than bare survival.


    Is Court a Pyramid structure? is it a council?
    Yes, or both or neither. They order how suits them best. Sometimes there is a top dog, but greed and money spirits might do it like a board meeting with essence treated like stocks and there being a Chairman but the board able to overturn if they can agree. Of course they grumble about paying "taxes" to a more powerful Spirit which could be a River, the spirit of the City, a bigger greed Spirit (Think Wall Street), or none at all. They view the Uratha as police to be bribed, or as nosy federal agents taking away their right to their own work. Another is a group of powerful Alley Cat spirits, they don't agree on anything and don't make decisions as a group. Their politics are more petty and personal, Yowling Queen stole essence from Alley Mange and he wants to get her back. They hate the dog spirits though and enjoy rats spirits they feed on, making their own enemies as a group even if not united. Sometimes a large emotion of deed/tragedy on the private scale produces one large spirit who fosters the others who are born after it. Because of one woman's malice she goes on a large serial killer spree. The spirit born of her malice (it's source) would lord supreme over the lesser Murder and Fear spirits that were produced from that Malice because that's the source. It's so subjective and takes into account history...


    What a lord spirit could ask a uratha to do? Is simply provoking a spike of fear any valuable to a higher member of a court? Or its barely a blip in his radar and not worth his notice?
    Well depends on the Spirit and what the favour being asked, ask a large favour from a powerful Spirit and you could owe years of your life helping the spirit whenever it asks. You ask a small favour and something else will react. A spike of fear would be a small favour, much more likely the large Spirit of fear would ask them to get rid of a human, or group of them, inspiring Courage or deal with a bunch of spirits forming that are encouraging apathy as the residents get used to fear. Maybe even take out it's second so its lessers know (or believe) the spirit has The People to call on if they ever resist. Lots of things.


    Why do they fight for territory? How if the humans move more the shadows than the spirit can then why would they want a territory thats not favors them? Why spirit fight each other when they essence is incompatible if eating each other makes them abominations?
    The spirits don't often vie for territory so much as influence. A spirit of Joy doesn't want the West side, it want to spread joy so it can have more joy to consume and more power. It does NOT become Magath if it consumes things natural to it. An owl spirit can consume rat spirits all day and might represent the hunting aspect more as it does so. It does what owls do. Magaths form when the source of what they are disappear and the spirit out of desperation eats essence completely unaligned to what it is. All the owls in the area are killed off by say a plague, but the owl spirit survives by eating a bear spirit and continues doing so. Owls don't hunt bears, have different natures and ideals, having both changes what it is and drives it mad, because the opposite pulls. Magaths are a terrible abomination to Spirits. Spread of territory doesn't do that. Owl spirits might fight a bear if the Bear spirit was worried they were too many and might overtake his position as head spirit in the area, but in this case they wouldn't consume each other's essence, just use each other's influence against the other or bring in other Choir allies. Unless the spirit of an actual location, area and land isn't the issue so much as spreading control. No data spirit wants to take over the back yard, but would love to have more information, electricity and power to give it more influence.


    TLDR: I wrote all that up so you can understand how I got all of this from reading the book and how you seem to want cut and dry answers from something that cannot be summed up neatly. They explained this in the book, but I don't think it is how you want. And I get that. Honestly for me Mage is often that way for me too. There's nothing wrong with it, and being frustrated with it I think just means you need/desire a different mode of play or themes.
