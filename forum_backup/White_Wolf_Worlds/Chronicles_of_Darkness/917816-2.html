                                        I already dislike being out in the hot sun, I love nighttime, disease and sickness frighten me, I've always wanted to shake off the limitations of my mortal body, growing old and dying is scary. There are reasons why I would consider such a thing.

                                        Feasting on the blood of the living carries the risk of killing innocent lives, this Beast inside me has its own desires that I may not be able to control, which could harm my loved ones, sunlight is now deadly to me, there are those that might seek my destruction for simply being who I am and, unlike reasons such as sexuality, skin color, or gender, this actually carries a legitimate reason; I'm a walking, talking parasite and potential monster. Lastly, unless I spread the curse to my friends and loved ones, they will all eventually die (unless I ghoul them consistently, but that would be a perversion of our real bonds).

                                        Fire can kill me in either case, so I won't count it.

                                        Fact is, the idea is heavily tempting, but my life would change in certain ways that I would probably never be able to change back. Bye-bye family, goodbye old life, assume a new identity, meet these new people (that might have it in for me as I could just be a pawn or something else insane like that). There are many things to consider. Don't let the kiddies that just want the superpowers ruin it for you. If someone told me that I'm not the old me, but a "Thing-copy" of the old me, I'd dismiss it as insanity or shrug and say, "Oh well." As for if it were the case, then current me that's not a vampire wouldn't give a damn because I just died. Now if ghosts are real, well, I just might become one and want revenge, but that's a slightly different topic.
