    Originally posted by Rater202 View Post
    Just because it doen'st have the trappings of magic doesn't mean it is not magic, and just because one doen'st know that it's magic or how it works done'st necessarily mean it can't be done.(The Thaumatech Specialty might well just be "You know how the magic works.")

    I'm reasonably certain that making potions and the like in the modern day looks an awful lot more like making soup or cooking up some kind of drug than it does a witch sitting over a cauldron.
    Except as I already pointed out, specialty thaumatech isn't necessary. Proto- thaumatech implants can be extracted and implanted using the exact same techniques that every single surgeon learned in medical school and residency.

    And in Urban Legends, inventing a supernatural drug requires occult knowledge, thaumatech does not. Obviously because one requires occult knowledge, and the other doesn't.

    Claiming that it's magic but cheiron agents are ignorant of it doesn't fly because Cheiron agents can invent their own thaumatech, according to the hunter core book the proper roll is either medicine, science, or crafts.

    So there are cheiron agents with enough practical knowledge to extract and implant thaumatech, there are cheiron agents with enough theoretical knowledge to invent new thaumatech, some of those agents are the same people.

    No occult knowledge necessary, no occult items necessary, no occult rituals necessary, on the practical or theoretical level.

    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
    This. For instance, it would be perfectly appropriate for Cheiron to harvest a mage's eyes in order to give one of its agents a “mage sight”-like Endowment. Really though, discussions of what Thaumatechnology can or can’t do really belong on the Hunter forum.
    Actually it wouldn't be, because witches, barring those who changed themselves with magic, are 100% biologically normal according to witchfinders. No biological differences, no thaumatech, which is why there isn't any thaumatech taken from mages in any hunter book. And since Cheiron is probably going to be one of the usual suspects for Deviant creation, I'd say it is appropriate.

    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
    I'm also curious as to what was meant when we were told that the Deviant template is “unstable”.
    Well it might be more than that but there is the "blowout" function when a deviant needs to turn the dial to 11. Blowout as in tire blowout (which can cause a car wreck or being stranded), as in well blowout (which causes ecological disaster or a fire), it probably is hardly safe for the deviant or anyone around them.

            Originally posted by blackheartz View Post
            Dave posted this on the comments section of the mid-week Meeting Notes:

            "Deviant is about people turned into supernatural beings by human actors – their transformation is “messy” compared to other beings, and they don’t end up with an entirely stable “template”. Most are the result of human conspiracies, cults, mad science, and other groups. Even those whose change was self-inflicted are sought as assets. PC Deviants are the ones who’ve escaped the clutches of their conspiracy – for now – and have a thirst for revenge.
            Deviant is Sense8, Orphan Black, Fringe, The Fly, Hulk, Guyver, Tetsuo The Iron Man, Akira, Hollow Man, Jekyll, V for Vendetta, Videodrome, Existenz, Scanners, Dark Angel, several versions of Beauty and the Beast, John Doe, Kyle XY… One thing I quickly realized after Rose and Rich handed me the blurb you’ve seen in the catalog and asked me to make a game out of it is how much inspirational media there is for it, which we’ve not tapped so far.
            Many (but, crucially, not all) powers will be physically obvious.
            Counter to the trend to increase “power level” over recent games, because the bulk of their foes are normal (albeit organized) people, they will trend to being the street level. The potential for Akira or Dr Manhatten-style blowouts will be there, but most Deviants will be the lowest-powered full game protagonists bar Hunters."
            I WANT THIS GAME

            SO MUCH

            ...as long as I can play Cameron Vale, I'll be a happy gamer. (Long, long ago, I wrote a fan supplement for the fan oWoD game "Scanners: the Madness." In my defense, I didn't have a life at the time.) I particularly like that the "up to eleven" option will be there along with the largely street-level stuff. IMO, gonzo building explosions from overclocked pyrokinesis are as much in genre as hiding from the PMC-of-the-day in back alleys.

            Ooo, speaking of PMCs, would Metal Gear Rising be a valid near-future Deviant adventure?

            Heroes die. Hope is a phoenix.

                                                I read "unstable" as "failing implants". Going with the body horror idea, what if your "improvements" require . . . "upkeep". For example, your "improved" heart starts to fail, and you need a "new" one . . . Or if you want to boost your powers. Are you prepared to cut into your own body, and start taking pieces out?

                                                This also strikes me as the kind of game that needs a "Sanity" stat, to really make it pop. Slashed across the arm, the Deviant gets a good look at the insides. . . and there's nothing human left, just tubes and wires. Shot, the bullet is pushed out of the wound, along with what looks like a bunch of insect larvae, wriggling and writhing on the floor. You throw up, and wormlike things crawl out of the mess, and back into your mouth, even as the remaining vomit eats through the floor . . .

                                                Or going back to the idea of needing a new heart. Sure, you can live without one for several hours (thanks to another "improvement") . . . but what of your sanity? Sawing open your chest, and stitching a new one in place would be an incredibly grisly prospect . . .

                                                Just how much can you take, before you go completely batshit insane?
