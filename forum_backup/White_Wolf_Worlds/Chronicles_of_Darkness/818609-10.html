                                    More thoughts:

                                    Havoc

                                    So when an un-Inspired mortal directly interacts with or analyzes a Wonder, it provokes Havoc. I'm thinking a Wonder only Fails, Explodes, suffers/causes an Accident, or goes Orphan on Dramatic Failure, Failure applies a Fault, Success does nothing (but applies a -1 Penalty to the next roll, for the same Scene), and Exceptional Success is nothing (and maybe prevents the next roll). Modifiers: -1 per Fault (so Failures increase the risk of Dramatic Failure). -1 Per applicable skill the mortal has (Science for most Wonders, though biological ones might be Medicine, etc.). +1 or -1 per Rank? [Either more advanced Wonder are more robust, or more fragile]. Base Pool: Obligation (or replacement name), or based on it (5 dice at the highest levels, 1 die at lowest)? Or make that a feature of Bound Wonders, and track Stable Wonders differently (such as a flat 4 dice as a base, similar to the bonus for permanent effects in a Clash of Wills)? Unmada Fields might offer a dice bonus scaled to Inspiration (a high Inspiration and Obligation Genius - rare as they are - rarely has their Wonders suffer from Havoc)?

                                    Interstellar and Time Travel

                                    Have super-luminal and temporal transit risk/cause a "Bardo incident", a dimensional shift that throws the travelers into the alternate universe of a Bardo. Outsiders are treated as Manes inside such alien realities (and natives from such realities are treated as Manes in the "real" world)? This can explain the various "Mane-like" behavior and effects from Time Travellers and "Aliens". It can also serve as justification for stories of why the Genii visitors are different/exceptional, and they solve some problem that was plaguing the natives for some time (they literally have an edge... sometimes). Havoc in such cases might be "alien sickness" or "temporal ejection" or spawning "time/space wraith to deal with the violators" (a Genius version of Abyssal Manifestations).

                                    Specialist Wonders

                                    I'm thinking "specialized" Wonders (guns that only harm Ghosts, fields that only block one kind of creature, scanners that only detect a certain form of radiation) should just have a dice bonus, maybe +1 (or higher, if justified). They are kind of like the equivalent of Skill Specialties, after all.
