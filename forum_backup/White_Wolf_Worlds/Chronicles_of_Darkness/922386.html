    What are you playing?

    06-27-2016, 11:00 PM
    It's been a while since I've seen one of these.

    The Chronicles of Darkness are many and diverse. So what are you playing? How are you playing it? What's been your favorite moment so far?

    I'll start. I'm currently involved in 2 games, soon to be 3.

    The first is a Mortals to crossover supernaturals game that I've been running since February 2015. Started as a group of mortals with seemingly no ties meeting as bystanders in a massive accident that they discovered was caused by gang style warfare between changelings and vampires. They have since gained their own supernatural powers and templates (A mage, a couple Sin Eaters, a Changeling, and a couple of custom templates), and they have learned of their common ties to a couple of truly evil entities. A bit of lighter in tone than most CoD games, but it's a lot of fun to dial up the Darkness every now and then. The highlight so far has been a jaunt through the dreams of a psychotic abyss cultist to try and find the cult's current location. I'm running online, via roll20 and skype, and it's been a lot of fun.

    Second (and soon to be third) is Mind's Eye Society LARP. I'm playing in the Changeling venue, as a Winter Court Fireheart Elemental, who's recently become the Monarch of Winter in our Freehold, which has been my favorite moment so far. The Winter Court in our area had its king and queen, and the third leader, our jack, was uncovered to be an actor of a true Fae who had been manipulating the freehold for years. The actor was killed and we restructured the freehold pledge so that this could never again happen.

    The upcoming game is another MES game, Accord. I'm super excited for this one. There's been absolutely bananas stuff going on in the national plot, and it centers on our domain! I'm coming in with a Sin Eater (Silent Necromancer), and I can't wait.

    Who's next? It's sharing time!

                          So what are you playing? How are you playing it? What's been your favorite moment so far?

                          Werewolf the Forsaken. Playing a Bale Hound in concept, without any special mechanics for it other than a personal totem, though he recently lost that and now have to seek a new one. Mechanically he's an Elodoth Iron Master. Our pack are non-natives to the area and upon arriving found themselves and practically no other werewolves. There's a pretty nasty situation going on with an entity we can't really identify that's keeping us from being able to expand for any worthwhile reason.

                          After dealing with a vampire elder and leaving their domain in a level of chaos that has reached media-worthy attentions, the National Guard has been called in, though that wasn't really in our territory and we're safely away from that mess. He was the one in charge of keeping up the Masquerade and with his disappearance the fight over the power vacuum got vicious. We levied proof of the takedown, since the vampire has been an enemy of them, to make a hasty alliance with a protectorate made up of Pure, Forsaken and Ghost Wolves that's acting as the last bastion of werewolves in a city where they're being forced to either end of the Harmony scale by various forces. We're sort of doing a hiatus while characters get used to the new setting and we spend Experiences. My character got a new identity.

                          My favorite moment? There was a few. The first time he just flat out ate a person, and really every time thereafter has been a great thrill. Another time he was helping out with a 'side quest' for the Cahalith and ended up saving them and their spirit buddy from a near death with Command Artifice. And the vampire hunt, later on. It was very hard to pull off, but we did it perfectly. No alarums raised, we managed to obscure his divinations, tackled his ghouls before they could call for help, narrowly avoided so many silver bullets. The only trouble was when I did the staking, which took three rounds to pull off and gave some less obvious minions time to activate. Then we dragged him away and let the vampires freak out.

                          He had an NPC girlfriend, briefly, too. It was interesting, I think a very good depiction of how an evil werewolf would 'date' a human.

                              I've been running a crossover game for about a year now. The PCs are private detectives working out of the French Quarter that are hired to investigate the Kindred Prince's murder. However their bosses are a Mastigos and a Ugallu Tyrant who are using them as pawns to shake up the New Orleans underworld so they could install the Tyrant as the city's new Apex. The tone started off with a brutal and introspective tone similar to True Detective or Bryan Fuller's Hannibal, but as we've gone on and more characters have been introduced it's become more like a urban horror/noir tinged A Song of Ice and Fire. It has a heavy focus on the politics of the different supernaturals (which we call The Masked) and the PCs are now all separated from each other and working for opposing factions as mercenaries.

                              None of my players have read any of the books outside the core and whatever splat their playing, so I'm having a blast introducing things like the True Fae, Belial's Brood, and the Begotten to them for the first time.

                              My favorite moment was when on a mission to rescue some children from Arcadia, the PCs were caught by the Fae that was keeping the children imprisoned. Taking advantage of the Fae's love of games, one of my players negotiated with her to let the children go free if he and the PCs play any game she chose. That game ended up being The Wild Hunt. The PCs were the prey and had to navigate the Hedge while being pursued by a hunting party of enslaved Changelings, Huntsmen and Hobgoblins. They survived but after escaping the Hedge they were so traumatized and betrayed by both what they saw and what they had learned about each other that they decided to go their own separate ways.
