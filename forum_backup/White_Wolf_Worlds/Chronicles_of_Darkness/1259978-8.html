                    I'm debating writing up a couple supplements, but I'm not sure where to start, so I'd like a little input from the community. If people could let me know what they'd like to see, or springboard off of these concepts with their own ideas, that would be fantastic. I'll list my concepts below:
                    • Blue Book: Something about mortals with Supernatural Merits and/or access to Rites or Ceremonies; potentially building things like Medium into a Merit-based micro template. Expect a lot about folklore, magical practices in different cultures around the world (including Dark Eras content), Mystery Cults (including my Merit-based Organization rules), and how mortals with Supernatural Merits can "punch up" in games with a mixed cast. I'm debating sprucing up my Pacts rules, playing with the Fate optional rule, and using the Favours mechanic from Requiem for Regina to make Social Merits a little more fluid and dynamic. Possibly adding some inter-Merit synergy as well.
                    • Vampire: A conversion of Threnodies from Blood Sorcery: Sacraments and Blasphemies to Second Edition. Possibly add some other tweaks to how things like Blood Sorcery, Oaths, and Laws work. Bringing the Strix more in line with the standard Ephemeral Being rules. Possibly some Dark Eras content Rose Bailey's BITE ME: How to Write Vampire has me inspired and I'm percolating on how to play with the concept of the First Tradition, different attitudes towards and methods of feeding, how vampires relate to others, and different ways to Climb the Ladder or Spin the Web. Also, stuff for the Half-Damned, and possibly something about designing Clans.
                    • Werewolf: Something akin to a Night Horrors book. Lots of Spirits, lots of territories; things about how I see the Hisil's ecology. Possibly stuff set in the Sundered World, or dealing with remnants of the Border Marches, that's seriously caught my imagination. Acrozatarim's particular brand of body horror, animism, and Spirit-empowered witchcraft is a current I can definitely swim in, so I'm also pondering writing a ton of Rites and playing with Influences a bit. Possibly making something for Rank 6+ Spirits (and Ephemeral Beings in general) inspired by Imperial Mysteries, the rules for Things That Should Not Be from Second Sight, and some of the unofficial Rank 6+ Pangaeans.
                    • Mage: There is an absolutely absurd amount of Dark Eras stuff frothing in my brain here, especially when it comes to concepts like Great Cults, Darshanas, and how the Mutapa Mages were presented in Princes of the Conquered Land. More cross-splat weirdness inspired by the aforesaid Dark Era, overhauls of Artifacts, Imbued Items, and Supernal Summoning (possibly Supernal Beings in general). Reworking Mage Sight to slot into the Investigation rules, and the Duel Arcane more properly into either Social Maneuvering or Chases. A whole lot of stuff on how I see the Supernal World (and why it isn't really separate or distant from the Fallen World), how to portray Mages in ways that fit folklore and traditional practices more closely than pop culture, emphasizing the drift Mages experience from human to something Other, and how to work with Mages so they don't completely overwhelm other magic users in the setting.
                    Last edited by Arcanist; 01-06-2019, 12:40 PM.
