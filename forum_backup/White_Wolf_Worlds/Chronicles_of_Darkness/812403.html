    Options to expand Kith-based abilities?

    01-20-2016, 02:03 AM
    Just wondering, but will there be options in the form of specific powers or merits that expand upon the abilities of a particular kith? I noticed in the original Changeling the Lost that despite there being a theme of you turning more other as your gained Wyrd, there was not a whole lot of changes actually associated with it. Maybe I missed something when I went to dig into it, but high Wyrd supposedly brings about more changes in changelings, but it's not really reflected too well. It's at maybe 7+ Wyrd that changes become noticeable, and even then it's just a larger number associated with the existing ability (Eg. +1 becomes a +3). Part of me thinks it'd be neat if there were Wyrd-related and Kith-related merits tied to expressing the changeling's ongoing transformation into the other.

          In Equinox Road, the rules for Wyrd Evolution were presented for Wyrd 7+ changelings, as well as a number of powerful merits available only to high Wyrd characters. In Winter Masques, an optional rule allowed changelings to take on new Kiths at Wyrd 4 and 7, I think. These are all still considered canon until otherwise contradicted in 2E texts.

          Wyrd stuff will be explored, and possibly some with Kiths, though given the plans around Kiths and the fact that they number about a hundred already, we're hesitant to make Kith-specific merits like they existed in the previous supplements.

          But yeah, this is stuff we're looking at.

                          Well, on that one, the market works against you-one of the big things that was heavily liked by fans in first edition was the wide variety of options when it came to Kiths. It's been shown to be profitable.

                          I understand the quality over quantity approach, but Kiths just aren't quite the right subject base to take that approach. They've been given more punch in Second Edition, but the design has never been in favor of treating them as a tunnel to explore, more like a pocket. It's a significant nail in the persona of a character, but as it's one forced by the Fae, it's not one the invites exploration in terms of the themes of rising above what the Fae did to you.

                          Like I said, none of that means it won't be explored-I think there's some interesting room for exploration. But between design structure of kiths, the implications of that thematically and in-character, and the popularity of variety, it's not something that can be the major directional focus of Kiths.

                              I can imagine some Changelings might enjoy the idea of strengthening their kith powers to use against their Keepers or to get something more beneficial out of their hard experience. But for the most part, I think it'd be more common for Changelings to expand on their seeming, since that's how they escaped, and with relatively few seemings, it's easier to devote sufficient time and pages to that enhancement.

                              I think it's also worthwhile to have a lot of merits that could be extensions of a kith or seeming. You're a Runnerswift? Take Fleet of Foot, Greyhound, etc to emphasize just how crazy fast you are on top of the kith blessing.

                              One angle I'm taking with Ziggy Zapf: Gameplayer kith who went Beast by becoming a glitch 'mon to escape, and becomes a different sort of bodybuilder. Biokinesis supernatural merit represents his ability to alter his stats. (Spend willpower, transfer dots from one physical stat to another for an hour.) In-character, he's modifying his "code" to do it. Out of character, he's just using a merit from a different part of the ST game system.

                                                  I have an idea. How about borrowing the Advanced and Epic Merits from Beast, and expanding them? Mortals and supernaturals could have the former, but the latter all had the Beast template as a prerequisite, IIRC. The latter's mythological feel is great for changelings with high Wyrd. I personally see no reason to limit Epic Merits to Beasts, though some might not fit thematically with certain splats. I'd house rule that Beasts, being the older brothers and sisters of monsterkind, can have Epic Merits at character generation. Other splats can have them, but first they have to reach Powerstat 6+. Let's take a look at a Merit that does something similar: Wyrdskill (Equinox Road page 15). Starting at Wyrd 6, every increase in Wyrd gives you a free Specialty in a single Skill. However, buying Specialties and Skill dots within the same category (Physical, Mental or Social) costs more XP. There could a system for Wyrd Evolution like the one in Equinox Road, which lets changelings with Wyrd 6+ gain a maxed out instance of one Epic Merit for free at each increase in Wyrd. I agree that it makes more sense to expand your Seeming abilities, since Seemings are all about choosing your own path in life. As BronzeDog said, you can expand on Kiths with normal and Advanced Merits.

                                                  EDIT: I'd be very, very interested in the possibility of changelings with Wyrd 10 becoming something other than True Fae. I'd also like it if turning into a True Fae was a choice, rather than an automatic downward spiral to Clarity 0. You were taken away, transformed in more ways than one to fit the whims of a monster, escaped by the skin of your teeth and started writing your own story. Would you be going back to square one if you became one of the Others, or are you only climbing higher toward something much bigger?
