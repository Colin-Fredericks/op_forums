    I don't think that Inferno's universal nature is a problem. My feeling from the book was always that demons are not meant to be a "thing of their own", but a symbol for corruption. They are "just" evil spirits, mad ghosts and dreams who got out of control. The Whisperer is the way Hell continues to corrupt others, feeding upon them to expand itself. Inferno can't create anything, but must feeds on the rest of the World of Darkness in order to exist.

    The point is that not every dark spirit of evil ghost is a demon. Many are just what they are. A demon is what happens when Inferno touch them, lures them and devours them. Hell is a parasitic entity which needs to feed upon morality to survive. A "spirit of Lust" could be a regular entity of the Shadow until one day it hears a whisper- and that whisper grants it a bit more freedom. It still acts according to its nature, but for a change, it really enjoys it. It becomes aware, gaining freedom- and freedom allows it to fall. That is, from my point of view, the point of Hell in WoD. It is a realm of free will- and its consequences. As long as a spirit, a ghost or a geotia does something "evil" without knowing it is evil, they are immune to its effect, but when they do the same thing while knowing- that's when Inferno may corrupt them.

    This is the point of Hell- it doesn't want to destroy us. It loves us. Really, it loves us so much it can each us up! It is filled with passion and hate and rage and care. It can just eat us up! It loves the fact that we are free, and that we can act against the rules we ourselves enforced. While it spread evil, it isn't about just evil- but about transgression. It is a separate, made realm sure- but a very human one. Inferno understands mankind, or else its Whisperers would have done pretty bad job. It is a realm made out of our own evil, out of our own choices. At first it may have been nothing more then a collection of Whisperers- but after feeding upon human morality for ages it became a "real" place- as far as a Lower Depth can be called "real". Its denizens know what they are doing- we are not toys or pets seen from some twisted point of view of beautiful madness. We are like cattle for them- they know what they are doing. This is the realm of human evil, not alien evil.

    At least, that's how I see it.

There is zero reason that a spirit whose central nature is a human emotion and that he resonates and feeds off of this emotion (in this case Lust) is by necessity inhuman in its outlook in any way that the demon equivalent may be considered human its outlook.
