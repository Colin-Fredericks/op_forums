    Mage: The Ascension Traditions as Nameless Orders?

    06-26-2022, 09:19 PM
    So, I am running a V5 game where I made mention of the the Order of Hermes being behind the SI's destruction of the Tremere Chantry in Vienna. But I want to use the overall setting of Awakening for this particular V5 game I am running. How would groups like the OOH, the Verbena, or the Sahajiya look as Nameless Orders under the rubric of the Free Council, which supposedly has as large a membership equal to the 4 Diamond orders or the Seers? Has anyone done any work in this direction?

      For clarity, by "under the rubric of the Free Council" do you mean "as part of the Free Council"? Because if they're part of the Free Council then they're no longer Nameless Orders — that was part of the point of the Council of Free Assemblies becoming a (loosely) unified Sect of their own. A Nameless Order is mechanically modeled with most of the same mechanics as Mystery Cult Initiation — Awakening's version of the Tremere are organized as a Nameless Order that facilitates some of the modifications to their template.

      It's also worth noting that the Orders being given equally-long writeups doesn't translate to their having equivalent headcount; all the Seers put together number about two thirds of the combined membership of the three Pentacle Orders that aren't the Guardians (smallest major Order in the setting) and the Council (largest major Order in the setting), which nonetheless may translate to equivalent numbers between the Followers of the Lie and their sworn enemies since the line avoids giving hard numbers.

      I'm unfortunately not terribly familiar with Ascension's particulars, but the Mage Translation Guide has some material for translating splats between the two settings that may be useful for your purposes, bearing in mind that it was written back when Awakening was in its late first edition.

          To add on to Satchel's point, don't mistake "if they're under the umbrella of the Free Council then they're not technically Nameless Orders anymore" for "they've lost their distinct identities and dissolved into the Libertine identity." The Councillors are a lot less unified in their culture and precepts than the Diamond Orders are, and while they now benefit from sharing rote knowledge and resources amongst each other rather than each internal school having its own distinct Nameless Order Merit benefits, their Order Tool of Techné models a wide breadth of methodologies that still leaves plenty of room for iconoclastic Hermetic Councillors to differ in practices from priests of a pre-Christian craft of blood and life.

          If you want to give them more distinct mechanical identities than that, they would easily make for thematic fodder for Legacies. You can see influences from various Ascension groups in some of Awakening's Legacies already, like the Perfected Adepts drawing on some similar vibes from the Akashic Brotherhood, and the Dreamspeakers resembling... well, I think you can figure that one out pretty quickly.

              If you're willing to set your current approach aside, you can consider implementing Ascension's Traditions as Legacies. Unlike in Ascension, each Order has members who subscribe to varying symbolisms. Not only Free Councillors can model themselves after Verbena - all other Orders can too, just with different goals in mind. Free Councillors will simply be more invested in experiencing Sleeper culture and its non-Supernal aspects, like living together with mundane neo-pagans. This is where Legacies come in. They're "Prestige Classes" that result from Mages internalizing an aspect of Magic to such a degree it becomes a part of their Gnosis. They also form little communities of adherents who can come from multiple Orders - united by common purpose and methodology. As an example, Hermetic aesthetics in Awakening have been relegated to Thrice-Great. They study hermetic symbolism to interact with Spirits of planetary courts. Their membership overlaps mostly with Silver Ladder, who are Mages most likely to be human supremacists, but they can coordinate support from the rest of the Pentacle.
