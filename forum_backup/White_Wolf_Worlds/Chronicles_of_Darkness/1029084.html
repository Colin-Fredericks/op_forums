    Cross over / clash of wills question.

    01-09-2017, 11:30 AM
    Help with scenario:
    A werewolf has used "Knotted Path" to trap a mage he's stalking in a large wooded park. The mage has Death 4, Life 3, Matter 4. Is there anything said mage could do that would directly oppose the effect / provoke a clash of wills with those Arcana, so he can leave the park? Or does he just need to prepare for a fight or hide? With Fate or Space or Spirit I can easily see it done, but I can't find it with the three arcana at hand.
