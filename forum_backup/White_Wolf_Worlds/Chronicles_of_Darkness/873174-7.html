                Team Application:

                Position: Fluff and Fiction Writer, Focusing On Antagonists

                Sample Writing: In-Character Philosophy of Ash Overview

                The Philosophy of Ash-Destruction

                {Warlord Is Another Term For Hero}

                (Nicknames: Generals, Renewers, Despoilers)

                First; let me welcome you to the world of the winged. Second; took you long enough. My troops were really starting to lobby for me to just kill that bastard myself and give you the Heart. But, you stuck through it, with only a little help from me,

                Oh don't act so surprised. Surely you didn't think it wasn't a little unlikely that you discovered that old bastard was a dragon-and by eating his heart, you could become one-by a random pdf search, did you? Don't bother answering-I can tell by that look in your eye you're relieved that you finally get the full big picture. That's good, even if you don't join the practical chapter of our little clubhouses. But I think you will,because there's a reason you chose to eat the Heart and not rid the world of its baleful legacy forever. Before you ask, I'm not a mind reader-just very good at reading potential enemies and hesitant allies, which is to say I'm good at reading people who actually interact with me. Useful quality when knowing your opponent is the difference between a victory and a devastating rout.

                But I digress. Back to you-I read your blog, which is how you came to my attention. You were always unafraid to admit there were things in the world that weren't right, and you knew there were more things to wrong than just racism, class divide, or greed. But you didn't fall into the trap of assuming that everything bad was a single force-you're more of a conspiracy realist than a theorist, knowing that the hidden masters of the world are just a fractious, fallible, and occasionally noble as their subjects. Probably a little nastier on average, but no more so than politicians. Which is why, when you found a hidden master, obvious signs of magic, soft power and all, you didn't rant about it on that paranoid con man's show, or plaster his true nature across the media in all caps, or even make a note on your public blog-you followed him, studied him, tried to understand what made him tick. Even when you realized he was a far stranger being than any humanoid lizard from outer space, you studied further-you realized it was more a question of who he was, not what he was. And what you found horrified you. Paying off police, inciting riots, threatening politicians-it wasn't even the conspiracy, it was what the conspiracy was meant to do. Hurt people-sure, he thought it was about criminals, but you guessed he told criminals apart from the normal law-abiding populace by skin color. Barbarians, after all, are part of a different culture, which means they do actually look different-of course, we by definition are all barbarians. None of us speak Greek.

                But you didn't want just to stop him. You knew that if someone could be that petty, and awful, and cold, then other secret masters could be worse. More ambitious perhaps, but perhaps even worse, even more small-minded. And more importantly, you knew that hidden masters don't run everything-prejudice just happened for no good reason. You wanted to change things, but changing things without power is futile at best, uncontrollable at worst. And so you tore your predecessor down, broke his world to pieces-and you used his remains to draw up the blueprints to a better one.

                Well, the bad news is, there is a hidden master-just one that was born of racism rather than the other way around. A spirit, a parasite grown fat on unthinking privilege and the rage that comes from forcing others to confront their own benefits from hidden evil. almost a god of inbred purity, now. The good news is, you were right on all other counts-and dragons were the embodiments of the biggest enemy of God in the West.

                So I come to you with an offer of apprenticeship, one Devil to another-wanna make a Hell of Heaven?


                Qualifications: I'm the main and only writer behind the GURPS Monsters thread, and spent a significant portion of my life on a setting called Darkness of the Endless Space, which seemed to be popular before I got burnt out on it. I'm still fairly new as a writer, but I seem to be fairly popular and liked-I'm very good at writing antagonists and passable at short fiction in established settings, I've found.

                Interests: Again, antagonists and world-building, with some fiction if need be. Mostly fluff, but I can help brainstorm some more abstract Conditions and Tilts.

                        Position- writer, both fluff and crunch

                        Sample Writing- Musing about the Deep Ones

                        At the lowest depth of the Dreamtide, they lurk quietly. They live in cities of broken ideas and alien concepts, shaped from the madness of the pit called Pandemonium. There, they made thousands of new religions, laws and societies as they enslave the lower denizens of the nightmare realm- social structures both elegant and alien to the mind of both Man and Dragon. Their knowledge of the Dreamtide and its shaping are greater than almost any other race, allowing them to shape the forms of the mind as if it were clay. Some of them are willing to teach their secrets to those who come from the higher levels of the Dreamtide, shaping their dream-flesh as they wish if they are willing to pay the price, which changed from one monster to another- but is always alien and twisted. While the journey to the depths of the dream is dangerous, some among the Orobori are willing to go great lengths in order to achieve greater power. However, those touched by the Deep Ones are never the same as they used too- their touch gives freedom of form, yet it leave the mark of the pit upon them. Each time they twist the form, a part of their very beings is carried back to the wake world with the subject- and as the dragon takes her true form and rise in power, the resonance echos back to the pit, strengthening the primordial madness. Through the Orobori's awe, the Deep One's fear leaks, creeping into the minds of loyal subjects and shows them the greatness of Pandemonium. Slowly, the corruption spreads, and madness grows around the dragon. The more the Orobori seek the pit's freedom, the greater is the fear she spreads- until eventually, she becomes the embodiment of that madness, becoming from a divine king into a demonic tyrant.

                        It is not to say one should stay away from the pit- after all, even the greatest among kings were touched by madness. But when you do, do it wisely- for all know that once the king lose his divine right to role, his subjects are allowed to claim his head. And Heart.

For specific information, you could look at my signature.

                        Interests- Dragon was my favorite fan-made game, and I was really sad to see it die out. Although I'm not sure I would have enough time to help as regular, I'll be happy to help as much as I can with whatever you need.

                                I’m in your attic, haunting your stuff.

                                I read what you have so far. Your setting elements are great and consistent. However, some of the mechanics of the second edition don’t fit the feel of your reboot. I have a few suggestions you may take or leave as you please.



                                First, I’ve drawn new dragon images for the five methods.
                                Link to Google Folder
                                https://drive.google.com/drive/folde...UZHVUhSc1hkTWs
                                Please use these instead of those pictures from the first edition. When a player turns to the method section they’ll want to see a cool image, and the clumsy art of my past does not make a good impression (Ironically you used an image I intended to replace the Slayer method image with in the Conviction section).
                                As a good rule of thumb, if it has cursive initials instead of the initial grin it’s from when I was a teenager and it shouldn’t be used. It’s too painful to look at! ^_^



                                Second, I feel ablutions will need to be simplified to better correspond to the thematic elements of your new chronicles. Here are my recommendations: if you use these, do so as a replacer for the current ablutions instead of an addition. You said you were working on them in the shadows, though, so all of this might be redundant or contradicted already.

                                Anyway, here’s my pass on the ablutions.

                                ======

                                Weaponry
                                Deadly weaponry built into the dragon’s anatomy.
                                Every two points of Weaponry manifest across the board increases the damage of the spawned weapons by 1.
                                Tell: The weapon grows out of the dragon’s flesh.
                                Weaponry: Damage (Common)
                                Increases the raw damage of the weapon by one per point manifest.
                                Additionally gives the dragon the option to upgrade the damage type associated with the attack: brawl can become lethal and weaponry can become aggravated.

                                Weaponry: Cleave (Slayer)
                                Increases the number of targets a single attack can strike by the points manifest. For attacks against multiple targets, use the highest armor rating and lowest defense rating and make one roll.

                                Weaponry: Pierce (Finder)
                                Allows the weapon to ignore an amount of armor equal to the points manifest.
                                Ignores both worn and biological armor. Worn armor is shredded by the weapon’s attack, reducing its rating by one per successful attack until it is destroyed. Supernatural gear is only temporarily damaged by this type of attack.

                                Weaponry: Trauma (Trickster)
                                Causes the attack to inflict the Trauma condition at a rating equal to the points manifest. Trauma doesn’t stack from similar sources; only the highest Trauma from Ablutions counts.
                                ___
                                Trauma <X> [Condition]
                                Severe internal injury makes every action taxingly painful. The afflicted may only function at half physical capacity (halve physical skills and attributes for the purpose of forming dice pools, halve speed) or risk acquiring the Agonizing Pain tilt for the remainder of the scene. Trauma may only inflict this tilt once per scene.
                                Trauma resolves when all wounds have been cleared from the afflicted. Resolving Trauma gives a beat.

                                Agonizing Pain <X> [Tilt]
                                Physical trauma is strained by overexertion and floors the afflicted with pain. The afflicted must suffer an amount equal to the triggering Trauma condition: this suffering can take the form of a penalty to all actions for the rest of the scene or the form of aggravated damage immediately. This is a sliding option: for instance a Trauma 4 trigger of the Agonizing Pain tilt could inflict 3 aggravated wounds and a -1 penalty at the afflicted’s discretion. Choosing to take the aggravated damage grants one beat per wound suffered.
                                ___
                                Weaponry: Grapple (Challenger)
                                The weapon may be used to initiate a grapple. Rolls to grapple and perform maneuvers receive +1 for each point manifest, and the dragon may attach a damage maneuver onto any other maneuver as a free action. The weapon cannot be removed from the struggle with the disarm maneuver but can be controlled by the grappled victim if they gain control of the struggle.

                                Weaponry: Reliable (Inheritor)
                                For each point manifest you may recycle one failed die back into the combat pool.
                                Physique
                                Draconic ablution which enhances physical attributes.
                                Every two points of Physique manifest across the board increases the dragon’s size by 1, with all associated benefits of such growth.
                                Health gained from these ablutions and the change in size is Heroic; track it on the bottom health bar and mark wounds there first. These wounds are ERASED when those health levels disappear from disbanding the ablution.
                                Tell: Increase in size and muscularity
                                Physique: Strength (Slayer)
                                Increases Strength by one per point manifest.
                                Also multiplies throwing distance by its manifest rating and multiplies effective lifting strength by its manifest rating.

                                Physique: Dexterity (Trickster)
                                Increases Dexterity by one per point manifest.
                                Additionally, the dragon may move through spaces as if her size is one rating smaller per point manifest, to a minimum size of 1.

                                Physique: Stamina (Finder)
                                Increases Stamina by one per point manifest.
                                Additionally, the dragon may multiply her effective stamina rating by its manifest rating for the purpose of holding her breath.

                                Physique: Armor (Inheritor)
                                Increases Armor by +1/+1 per point manifest. This armor is considered biological and is counted in addition to any worn armor.

                                Physique: Regeneration (Challenger)
                                When the dragon would recover 1 point of health, she instead recovers a number of points of health equal to this ablution’s manifest rating. This includes health regained through natural healing, health from reforging with breath, and health recovered by medical attention.
                                Stature
                                Draconic ablution which enhances social impact.
                                Every two points of Stature manifest across the board render the dragon’s features more striking and memorable, increasing social rolls by one.
                                Tell: Increasingly sharp and statuesque facial structure
                                Stature: Presence (Inheritor)
                                Increases Presence by one per point manifest.
                                Additionally, all parties will respect the dragon as if she held status in their organization equal to the points manifest in this Ablution.

                                Stature: Manipulation (Trickster)
                                Increases Manipulation by one per point manifest.
                                When rolling to open doors while this ablution is manifest, the roll applies to a number of doors equal to the points manifest (instead of just one). This is true if attempting to persuade a single target (punching through many doors simultaneously) or when working with a group (simultaneously opening single doors of multiple targets in a single roll).

                                Stature: Composure (Finder)
                                Increases Composure by one per point manifest.
                                Additionally, when opponents are trying to persuade the dragon they must overcome an additional number of doors equal to the points manifest in this ablution.

                                Stature: Predator Glare (Slayer)
                                The dragon may stare down a target, forcing them to do nothing but flee for a number of turns equal to the points manifest in this ablution. The first such stare in a scene is free, but subsequent stares require a point of Willpower.
                                The target of a Predator Glare loses an amount of Willpower equal to the points manifest in this ablution. If that reduction would render them completely depleted, they will flee without regard to their own safety, potentially leaping through windows out of skyscrapers to escape.
                                Mortals have no defense against this ability, but supernatural beings initiate a clash of wills: Presence + Heartstring against Composure + Resistance.

                                Stature: Word of the Crown (Challenger)
                                The dragon may issue a command (for 1 Breath) with a number of steps equal to the points manifest in this ablution. Someone who hears the command must obey.
                                Mortals have no defense against this ability, but may roll Resolve – Stature: Word of the Crown if the command places them in immediate danger or at risk of a breaking point. Supernatural beings initiate a clash of wills: Manipulation + Heartstring against Composure + Resistance. Additionally, like mortals they get an opportunity to break free of the command (if subjugated) when placed in immediate danger or at risk of a breaking point.
                                Only one person must follow the orders for each command; the dragon may decide WHO exactly is subject to the power by spending 1 Willpower; otherwise the storyteller decides. The mortal performing the steps of the command receives a bonus to all rolls related to that command, equal to the points manifest in this ablution.
                                Acuity
                                Draconic ablution which enhances mental capacity.
                                Every two points of Acuity manifest across the board increases the dragon’s perception by one.
                                Tell: Increasingly complex and numerous sigils form across the body, colored in the same tone the dragon’s scales will eventually take.
                                Acuity: Intelligence (Finder)
                                Increases Intelligence by one per point manifest.
                                Additionally, each scene the dragon may receive a number of storyteller hints (or clues towards a discovery) equal to the points manifest.

                                Acuity: Wits (Slayer)
                                Increases Wits by one per point manifest.
                                Additionally, Acuity: Wits is added to the dragon’s defense, even if she has sacrificed it this turn.

                                Acuity: Resolve (Challenger)
                                Increases Composure by one per point manifest.
                                Additionally, when Willpower is spent or lost, roll a number of chance dice equal to the points manifest in this ablution. If any show 10, the Willpower is recovered.

                                Acuity: Keen Senses (Trickster)
                                Grants +1 to Perception rolls per point manifest.
                                Additionally, the dragon may make a perception roll to detect extrasensory phenomenon, such as electrical energy in the air, infrared and ultraviolet light, dreamstuff, and subsonic and hypersonic vibration. This ability also allows them to detect supernatural power, potentially revealing other dragons or supernatural beings.

                                Acuity: Eye of Value (Inheritor)
                                Once per scene, the dragon may conduct research rolls on an object in 3 second intervals rather than the usual extended action periods. The number of such accelerated rolls possible each scene is equal to the points manifest in Acuity: Eye of Value, but successes accumulated may be applied later to traditional duration extended action rolls.
                                Respiration
                                Draconic ablution which unleashes concentrated Dreamtide energy from the Heart.
                                Breath weapons have a 10 yard cone by default (working like explosives, but with the firearms skill), but every two points of Respiration manifest doubles that range.
                                10 à 20 à 20 à 40 à 40 à 80 à 80 à 160 (“Close” ranges)
                                A point of Willpower may be spent to exclude specific targets from the effects of a Respiration.
                                Tell: Increasingly bright and warm glow in the chest over the Heart, eventually resembling a patch of lava.
                                Respiration: Inferno (Common)
                                By spending one point of breath, the dragon lets loose a blast of flames, which inflicts aggravated damage as fire equal to the points manifest, and applies the “On Fire” tilt.

                                Respiration: Acid (Common)
                                By spending one point of breath, the dragon expels a surge of corrosive acid, which dissolves an amount of structure on impacted objects equal to the points manifest, regardless of the durability. Supernatural objects are not permanently destroyed by this attack but if it reduces their structure to 0 they lose one dot of their rating.
                                The acid destroys clothes as well but will not inflict harm on living things.

                                Respiration: Blizzard (Slayer)
                                By spending one point of breath, the dragon emits a wave of freezing cold, which pierces an amount of armor equal to the points manifest, inflicts lethal damage, and applies the “Hypothermia” tilt.

                                Respiration: Hypnotic Dust (Trickster)
                                By spending one point of breath, the dragon exhumes a cloud of hypnotic particles. Anyone who inhales these particles suffers the Hypnotic Dust condition, at a rating equal to the points manifest in this ablution.
                                Subsequent exhalations clog the air, causing a choking fit and bashing damage to all who inhale the dust.
                                ___
                                Hypnotic Dust <X> [Condition]
                                Dreamstuff-charged particles induce the mind of the afflicted to open the Ora and embrace sleep. If the rating of this condition exceeds the afflicted’s stamina he becomes listless, lowers himself to the ground, and sleeps immediately. Otherwise each turn spent in the dust increases the rating of this condition by one unless the afflicted holds his breath.
                                Supernatural beings add their Resistance to their Stamina for the purpose of determining if and when the embrace of sleep occurs.
                                This sleep is normal and triggers the normal benefit of sleep, but the afflicted cannot be roused until the scene ends or they suffer physical harm.
                                The condition is resolved when the subject is removed from the dust or when the scene ends, and rewards a beat unless the afflicted was friendly to the dragon.
                                ___
                                Respiration: Inspiring Light (Inheritor)
                                By spending one point of breath, the dragon exhumes a burst of warm light. Anyone touched by this light receives a pool of bonus dice equal to the points manifest in this ablution. These dice can be spent as the targets please for the remainder of the scene. Unspent dice are lost at scene’s end.
                                Respiration: X-Ray (Finder)
                                By spending one point of breath, the dragon exhumes a beam of radiation which allows her to see though objects. For the rest of the scene, the dragon receives a bonus to perception rolls equal to the points manifest in this ablution. Additionally, all objects held by targets in the area of the beam are revealed to everyone who saw the ray; searching them for weapons or hidden materials automatically succeeds.
                                Finally, if the dragon is researching for a discovery, she immediately unveils a number of clues equal to the points manifest in this ablution.

                                Respiration: Tempest Roar (Challenger)
                                By spending one point of breath, the dragon roars with terrible fury. For the rest of the scene, anyone caught in this roar suffers the “Cower before God” condition at a level equal to the points manifest in this ablution.
                                ___
                                Cower before God <X> [Condition]
                                The terrifying power of the Tempest Roar leaves the afflicted shaking in their boots. Any action they would take which could go against the roaring Oroboros suffers a penalty equal to the rating of this condition.
                                The afflicted may voluntarily suffer a dramatic failure against the Oroboros to end the condition. Otherwise, it ends at the end of the scene. This condition awards a beat when resolved.
                                ___
                                Anatomy
                                Draconic ablution which modifies the body for metamorphic advantage.
                                Every two points of Anatomy manifest increases the species speed factors by one.
                                Tell: Larger and larger patches of scales, feathers, or fur across the body, with a general drift towards an animalistic appearance.
                                Anatomy: Chameleon (Common)
                                The dragon may blend with her surroundings, allowing her to attempt to move undetected without cover, effectively becoming invisible. A point of breath is required to hide equipment and clothes in this same manner.
                                Anyone attempting to perceive the dragon suffers a penalty to such attempt equal to the points manifest in this ablution, plus the dragon’s Heartstring. Using infrared goggles or an equivalent will halve this penalty.
                                For one point of Willpower, Anatomy: Chameleon negates an amount of supernatural-sourced perception equal to its points manifest (That is, Anatomy: Chameleon 3 can block 3-dot supernatural powers which perceive hidden things).
                                Anatomy: Scale of Fortune (Common)
                                By inflicting one lethal wound to rip off scales, the dragon may gain a temporary Resources rating equal to the points manifest in this Ablution. Once removed, the scales take the form of gold coins; generally they are plain but for one point of Willpower they can be made to look like officially minted currency.

                                Anatomy: Toxic Blood (Common)
                                By spending one point of breath, the dragon’s blood becomes dangerously toxic. When the dragon suffers lethal damage, she bleeds this terrible substance on everyone within melee reach. Targets contacted by the blood suffer the Corrosion Spray tilt, at a rating equal to the points manifest in this ablution.
                                ___
                                Corrosion Spray <X> [Tilt]
                                The afflicted suffers a number of bashing wounds equal to the rating of this tilt. They will continue to suffer this damage each turn until they remove the clothing covered by the toxic blood or rinse it away with pure water.
                                ___
                                Anatomy: Burrow (Common)
                                By spending one point of Willpower, the dragon can dig through a number of feet of solid matter equal to the points manifest in this ablution. During the turn of the dig, the dragon’s defense is increased by the distance travelled. Soft and loose materials like soil triple the distance the dragon can dig in a single use of Burrow.
                                [This ablution could be perceived as a weaker version of Flight, and may need to be pruned. It also diminishes the impact of Physique: Dexterity which is used for bypassing security systems]
                                Anatomy: Preemptive Focus (Common)
                                The dragon is able to take aim on activities in which the concept should not normally be possible. For instance, the dragon may aim a repair on an automobile to gain a bonus to her rolls when she begins. The number of time intervals which can be spent aiming with preemptive focus and likewise the maximum bonus attainable is equal to the points manifest in this ablution.
                                Aiming for a task takes the same amount of time as a single interval of work on the task. For instance, 30 minute repair intervals requires 30 minutes of preemptive focus to gain a +1 aiming bonus, while a one-turn baseball bat swing takes one turn of preemptive focus to gain a +1 aiming bonus.
                                The bonuses gained from aiming with preemptive focus persist as long as the dragon remains intent on her task. So as long as she is repairing the vehicle, every roll of the extended action receives the aim bonus. Likewise as long as she is wielding the bat in combat the aim bonus applies to all attacks made with the bat (instead of just the first). Firearms can be aimed with preemptive focus as well; aim bonuses persist across multiple turns of shots when used with this ablution.
                                [This ablution’s mechanic is fairly complex to describe; the trait may need to be pruned or redirected into an imposition]
                                Excellence
                                Draconic ablution which taps into otherworldly power.
                                Every two points of Excellence manifest increases Willpower by one.
                                Excellence: Skill of the Gods (Common)
                                For a point of breath, 3s, 2s, and 1s on a roll may be treated as 8s, 9s, and 10s (respectively). Additionally, you may reroll a number of dice from the pool equal to the points manifest in this ablution.
                                If the breath is not paid before the dice are rolled, it may be paid by also suffering a single aggravated wound.
                                Excellence: Flight (Common)
                                For a point of breath this scene the dragon may take to the air and fly.
                                Flight speed is (Stamina + Strength + Species Flight Factor) x Flight Ablution manifest. Dragons have a Species Flight Factor of 18. Birds have a Species Flight Factor of 54 to 84.
                                Flight’s manifest points are added to defense while airborne. Additionally, while airborne dodge actions are twice as effective and also count against ranged and firearm attacks.

                                Excellence: Impassioned Effort (Common)
                                The dragon’s Willpower expenditures receive a bonus equal to the points manifest. For instance, using a Willpower ability with Excellence: Impassioned Effort 4 gives the corresponding roll a +4. Spending Willpower to boost a regular action with Excellence: Impassioned Effort 3 gives +6 instead of +3. Using Willpower to raise resistance with Excellence: Impassioned Effort 5 grants +7 instead of +2 to the resistance attribute.

                                Excellence: Speed of Information (Common)
                                For one point of Willpower per roll, the dragon may divide the duration of an extended action’s interval by the points manifest in this ablution +1. For instance, a 30 minute repair interval subjected to Excellence: Speed of Information 4 would instead take 6 minutes per roll.
                                [This ablution might need to be cut, since it conflicts with Acuity: Eye of Value. It has a wider range of application but is less accelerated.]

                                Excellence: Fangs of the Beast (Reimagining)
                                For one point of Willpower, the dragon may utilize another supernatural being’s means of gathering energy to generate breath. The amount of breath the dragon can generate is equal to the points manifest in this ablution. For instance, a dragon might drink vitae from a human, but would only be able to accumulate up to 4 breath in that manner while manifesting Excellence: Fangs of the Beast 4.
                                The type of supernatural the dragon can mimic with this ablution depends on which one triggered the ablution’s acquisition with the reimagining. That is, a dragon can’t draw energy like a changeling if she’s hanging out with werewolves.

                                Excellence: Barrier (Reimagining)
                                For one point of breath per scene, the dragon may generate a forcefield around herself. This field stops objects from approaching her person and functions as full cover with a durability rating equal to the points manifest. As it is a field of energy it has no structure and any damage it suffers to durability is repaired at the start of the turn.
                                The field acts as cover only against objects to which the dragon is perceived invulnerable.
                                ======

                                I’ve tried to keep the spirit of the ablutions you chose to keep as well as facilitate your new Reimagining mechanic: instead of removing ablutions the storyteller can shift dots into another ablution of that category, or into the man-forged category.
                                I also tried to combine some of the traits which had overlap, cleaving to the clearly dominant power.

                                The categories each contain at least five Ablutions, which can be purchased separately. I’ve made an effort to put them into methods I thought would be appropriate. I’ve also doubled down on the power of this stuff; playing a dragon with these Ablutions should feel awesome, which is appropriate considering the drawbacks worked into the narrative portion of the game.

                                Tells are intended to follow the rule you established in your draft—that is every point manifest in a particular ablution requires a size <x> item to conceal it. Since ablutions are in categories, stacking the same type of ablution can result in traits very difficult to conceal. For example, getting Physique 12 from Physique: Stamina 5, Physique: Armor 3, and Physique: Strength 4 would require a size 12 object to hide the supernatural features (that is, it would require hiding behind a small family vehicle). Also, by putting the tells by category they can be rendered more general in their approach, giving the player more freedom to make the dragon look the way they want. When each separate ablution has a unique tell, eventually you run out of things to display on the body (as you saw in the second edition with the completely random and unrelated tells!).

                                Ablutions need to be complex enough to warrant their experience cost, but simple enough to be tallied quickly when used. I’ve tried to attain that balance with this pass here, and I hope that my efforts were successful. Again, certain ablutions have been eliminated because of overlap (Mesmerizing Gaze was just a weaker form of the Word of the Crown; Spines was an ineffective overlap of Corrosive Blood; Sing oh Muse and Master and Student were better implementations of Skill of the Gods; there may have been others but those were the big ones). What’s left should be diverse enough to be interesting but condensed enough so as to not overwhelm prospective players. I couldn’t come up with much for the reimagining ablutions, but perhaps that’s a good thing. Reimagined ablutions might work better as conditions instead of actual ablutions the dragon can turn on and off.
                                Still, you said you were analyzing the mechanical side of that idea and I’m sure you have some good notes on where you’re going.

                                I haven’t had a chance to pass the Deep One ablutions with scrutiny yet, but from my first read they should fit into these categories fairly easily. At worst they can have their own category, but you get what I’m going for, right?



                                Third is Divinity. This mechanic was originally implemented to give dragons a general ‘monster’ vibe if their tells didn’t do it for them. That being said, the way it’s written is too restrictive for the feel I believe you’re aiming at in your revival. I’ve been toying with some ideas and I think I’ve got something that could work.

                                ======
                                Divinity

                                For every ten points manifest in an ablution, you attain your next degree of divinity. Divinity increases your size by its current rating and doubles the effectiveness of an ablution category’s bonus. You should pick which categories are affiliated with which divinity levels the first time you attain them, and mark those categories on your sheet.

                                Example
                                Divinity 1 Weaponry (Your biological weapon does 1 additional damage per point of Weaponry Ablutions manifest)
                                Divinity 2 Weaponry (Your biological weapon does 2 additional damage per point of Weaponry Ablutions manifest)
                                Divinity 3 Physique (Size increased by 1 for every point of Physique Ablutions manifest)
                                Divinity 4 Physique (Size increased by 2 for every point of Physique Ablutions manifest)
                                Divinity 5 Physique (Size increased by 4 for every point of Physique Ablutions manifest) [Yep, Kaiju it up here]
                                Divinity 6 Respiration [Dreamtide only] (Range of breath attacks doubled for every point of Respiration Ablutions manifest)
                                Divinity 7 Respiration [Dreamtide only] (Range of breath attacks quadrupled for every point of Respiration Ablutions manifest)
                                Divinity 8 Anatomy [Dreamtide only] (Species speed factor increased by 1 for every point of Anatomy Ablutions manifest) [At such a big size, the low speed is a detriment]
                                Divinity 9 Anatomy [Dreamtide only] (Species speed factor increased by 2 for every point of Anatomy Ablutions manifest) [Gotta speed up]
                                Divinity 10 Anatomy [Dreamtide only] (Species speed factor increased by 4 for every point of Anatomy Ablutions manifest) [to be the Jormungandr world destroyer of your dreams!]

                                I know multiplicative mechanics fly out of reasonable scope relatively quickly, but we agreed that doubling down on the power was the right idea. Also, this kind of prowess might be needed in the depths of the Dreamtide, the only place attaining the last levels of divinity is possible.

                                ======



                                Fourth, I was looking at your three new philosophies and I think they should have names related to bodily humours (in line with the others).

                                ======

                                Instead of Materialism, I think Sinew would work well (as it emphasizes the connections between various body parts), or possibly Claw (the hand which distributes). Wisdom could be Nerves (taking the brain literally) or Anima (taking the brain as a house for the soul). Nihilism could be Bile (representing their reprehensible view of the universe and their pursuit of wretched [heh, wretch] activities) or Flesh (representing the hedonism which sometimes accompanies nihilism). Alternatively, Greenhorn might work as the “Folly of Flesh” (instead of a Philosophy) since their connection to the Oroboros condition is as fleeting as the skin flaking off the body’s surface.

                                Naming the social splats after humours has several functions: first it relates them to an obsolete ideology of medicine, drawing parallel to their own obsolete nature in the world, and second it enhances the body horror, emphasizing gruesome terminology consistently throughout the book.
                                ======



                                Finally, the Purviews. If the first edition and its [Power] Philosophies taught us anything, it is that specific mechanical ordering does not create engaging gameplay. Impositions need to have a clear and easily understood function which can be blended with others in the purview. Some that are in your test draft are not achieving this, such as Ruin’s abilities which are leaning too heavily on mechanics. Perhaps the impositions should instead take a more narrative approach to power instead of tweaking mechanics.
                                Still, it sounds like you’re on top of that. I had planned to do a pass on Purviews to give you, but I’ll wait since you’ve said they’re in flux.


                                Great work so far!
