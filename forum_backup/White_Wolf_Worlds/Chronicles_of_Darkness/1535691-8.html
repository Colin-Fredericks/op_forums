            You're right that I need to clarify that, as the pseudo-conspiracy is intended to largely work backwards from the usual multi-conspiracy situation. Instead of generating Effort that slowly advances its Projects, the You-Hating Club gets to direct it into a single conspiracy action. It doesn't really have long-term designs beyond that.

            Hunted usually represents just one person, so if you neutralize that person, the threat goes away. Even if the Condition refreshes itself because of a Scar, it should still feel like just one enemy out to get you at any given time. With Evil Twin, I wanted to give the impression that a whole-ass conspiracy is out there with the sole intent of making your life a living hell.

                    Originally posted by MrParaduo View Post
                    Q: how does Anomalous Biology affect a homunculus? Do they, by default, require food, air, (etc) if they don't have it?
                    By default a Homunculus has no Health, disappears if it takes any damage, has set dicepools for things inside and outside its areas of expertise, has a limited understanding of the world around it and no volition of its own, and has its presence work like a Toggled Variation, meaning it doesn't last long enough to worry about food or sleep, doesn't have the luxury of unconsciousness or bleeding out or wound penalties or being poisoned, and doesn't have much of a mind to read. Many of the possible skins for a homunculus are things that don't need to worry about breathing or exposure, particularly given that even if it's "killed" you can summon it again next chapter with the same qualities.

                    You could certainly construct a homunculus for which Anomalous Biology's various low-level passive effects are relevant, but the default systems are very much set up such that a homunculus is a fantastical channel for some or all of your Variations rather than a living creature that needs to breathe and eat.

                                                A Night Horrors book is on my list. It's far enough out that I don't even have an outline for it, but my initial conception involves a lot of conspiracy write-ups (from Standing 1 to 10, although it will be weighted towards Standing 1-6 conspiracies) with Icons, STCs, Nodes, and interconnections between them - most of which can be easily transported piecemeal into an existing chronicle. Basically, it would be, if not an entire Web of Pain, at least a starting point for building an entire Web of Pain.

                                                Just as oodles of Variations and Scars serve as examples that make it easier to design new Variations and Scars, I think having oodles of conspiracies, Nodes, Icons, and Devoted will go a long way to making it easy to design new conspiracies (and their parts). My only concern with this approach is that I might end up shortchanging Ferals and rival Renegades in the process.
