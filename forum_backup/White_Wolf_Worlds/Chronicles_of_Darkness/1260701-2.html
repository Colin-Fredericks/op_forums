                    Death 3

                    Practice: Perfecting
                    Primary Factor: Potency
                    Withstand: Composure
                    Suggested Rote Skills: Socialize, Empathy, Academics

                    People are social creatures, sometimes coming together like parts of an organism to form a cohesive social group by sharing common ground with each other. Like all organisms it is alive in a sense, and this spell brings that closer to an end. This spell targets the individuals that form part of a social group, removing Doors equal to Potency when attempting to break up the social group. If the spell removes all of the groups Doors, it’s no need for any social roll, the spell simple decays the groups reasons to be with each other for the Duration. This spell is only effective against social groups, that have some common characteristics and some sense of unity with each other. For instance, it’s quite effective to force people to leave a rock concert but not to disperse a crowd of people on the street with no interaction between them.


                    + 1 Reach: gain the 8-again quality for the social roll action to Force Doors against the group for the sole purpose of dispersing it.
