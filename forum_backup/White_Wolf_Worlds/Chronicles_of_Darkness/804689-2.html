        Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post

        It is kind of odd that the writeup specifically points out that the Ivory Claws consider all Uratha betrayers to their lineage, but doesn't say that the Fire Touched consider all Uratha disrespectful towards spirits, or that the Predator Kings consider them disrespectful towards the hunt.
        I don't think it's odd at all. I think it says more about those Uratha who consider themselves leaders of the Pure, rather than those they consider followers.

        Many Fire Touched consider Forsaken to be disrespectful of spirits, but this isn't a blanket tribal position. You'd probably find many Fire Touched quietly believe the Bone Shadows to be very clinically balanced in their respect towards spirits, for one.

        Similarly, the Predator Kings don't consider all Forsaken disrespectful of the hunt. It's hard to maintain this against Urdaga who enact the Siskur-Dah and pursue their sacred duties through to the end. Some may even have grudging respect for the Hunters in Darkness, for example.

        As I see them, the Pure aren't monolithic hate-mongers without any variation. They have nuance, and regional differences, same as any tribe. Even the Ivory Claws have these individuals, but they have to keep these views very quiet, or risk the wrath of their tribe-mates.

                                    Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
                                    That last paragraph is the reason I think it's odd that it's specifically called out that the Ivory Claws feel that way, as though the statement could be applied to all Ivory Claws unilaterally. I'll buy that it's more about the stance their leaders take, but then the implication is definitely that the Firetouched and the Predator Kings would be more likely to have peace with the Uratha, if only because their leaders are less prejudiced against them.
                                    I think peace might be too strong of a word.

                                    Even if they aren't going out there and slaughtering the Forsaken, the sacred hunts and ideological differences of both sides are likely to cause some butting of heads.

                                    The Fire-Touched seem like they're still interested in tearing the barrier between worlds down, letting the spirit leak into the world. When spirits do cross over, are the Forsaken going to let those spirits run amok near their territory, riding and urging folks and gorging on Essence? Alternatively, are the Fire-Touched going to let it slide when the Forsaken hunt spirits they think are causing trouble? They don't seem to have the same outlook on what is 'wrong.'

                                    And the Predator Kings are called out as hunting, among others, 'Humans who don't hunt at least once per season.' Which gives them a rather large portion of the human population to choose from for their hunts, especially in more urban areas, where they're justified in their hunts by 'culling an overgrown population.'

                                    While they don't seem as rabidly antagonistic as whole as they appeared in first edition, there's bound to be a lot of flaring of hostilities when one side does something the other doesn't approve of. Plus there would be the constant disrespect of Luna. I wouldn't be too surprised to see protectorates with an uneasy balance of power, but true peace seems unlikely. At least that's my guess based on what we've seen.

                                    Of course, I don't expect it to be too long until we see players asking for more information to help build their inter-faction packs.
