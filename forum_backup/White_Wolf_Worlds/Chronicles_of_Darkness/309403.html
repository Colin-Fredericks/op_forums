    Athanors as Physical Objects [2e Speculation]

    11-11-2014, 04:06 PM
    The blog post on the Basic Refinements is up! Check it out. This post confirms to me something that I was wondering about since last week when it was hinted at: Athanors are physical things.

    It was always a bit vague as to what exactly they were in 1e, with the only description of them I can recall is that they have to be built and that they are inside a Promethean (whether that was a physical or metaphysical inside, no idea). But now it seems that Athanors can be "encountered", and apparently they contain or impart "memories and teachings" of a Pilgrimage.

    My guess is they're what's left behind from a Promethean when they achieve the New Dawn, similar to how when you refine coal you get coke; sure it's refuse but damn is it still useful (and boy can it ever burn). As to what that means in game... perhaps it provides a sort of template to be human based off what kind of person the Redeemed became. And from there, you could extrapolate mechanical benefits like the old Athanors -- if they are even still being used as the Z-axis.

    I think this ties some fun things together as far as the humors and such go. Maybe in a Promethean, the heart is where their emotions are stored or IS their emotions.
