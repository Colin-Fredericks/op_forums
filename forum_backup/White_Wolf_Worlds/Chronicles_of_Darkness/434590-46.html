                    Awesome

                    For Attributes I'd go for 10 dots. In theory a Leviathan can go beyond that, but regular character sheets don't go beyond 5 dots when the maximum is ten. For Leviathan 10 dots for a maximum of 17 should have a similar balance. (Leviathans can't boost Skills past 10).

                    The following traits are just renames:

                    Power stat: Sheol
                    Energy Stat: Ichor
                    X-Splat: Strain
                    Y-Splat: School
                    Z-Splat: Evolution
                    Virtue: Upwelling
                    Vice: Undertow

                    Integrity Trait: Leviathans have an unusual integrity trait called Tranquility. It has 12 boxes not 10 dots (ideally they'd be vertical), and each box can be empty, occupied by the divine nature, or occupied by the bestial nature. The divine nature always occupies the top box and grows downwards. The bestial nature always occupies the bottom box and grows upwards. Both the divine and bestial nature cannot grow beyond seven boxes total so the middle two boxes can potentially belong to either.

                    Willpower: This would need a second set of dots in brackets (like Princess has)

                    Size could be split into 6 different numbers; but that might not be worth the effort.
                    The Ripple Effect should be included with Size, it's just a space to write a number.
                    For Health I'd suggest having space to write numbers rather than boxes. Since Size is health Leviathans can get enormous health quite easily.


                    For supernatural powers Leviathans have 4:

                    Atavisms: Which can be lesser, greater, or ancient. The theoretical maximum is to have 2/3/2 atavisms and each Atavism is linked to an Attribute. You can have multiple Atavisms for a single attribute.

                    Channels/Birthright. These are simply a you have them or you don't.

                    Adaptations: These have a dot rating and are always associated with a Channel.

                    Codified Rituals: In a word, Mage Rotes.


                    The Leviathan sheet will definitely need space for a Cult. The Cult should have the following traits:

                    Name
                    Structure (one of eight options)
                    Fervour rated 1 - 7
                    Piety rated 1 - 7
                    Zeal rated 1 - 5
                    Space for the Cult's Merits



                    For the general look, well I'm not sure if this is practical, but I imagined a sort of dishevelled academia look. As though this were one of H.P. Lovecraft's half mad investigator's research notes. The colour scheme is a watery blue/green - like the opening picture in https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...WQcjg5Qw/edit#

                    Let me know if you have any more questions?

                                Example settings are a ton of work and not fully necessary, so I don't plan to do one.

                                1E had an unfinished San Diego and Tijuana as the sample setting. There were some good ideas there, with a theme of neither city being able to exist without the other but also having conflicting fear and envy of the land across the border.

                                If I was writing one I'd probably choose one of the following:
                                • Somalia at the hight of piracy.
                                • Iraq/Levant at ISIL's peak.
                                • New Orleans just after Hurricane Katrina; but not the real New Orleans, the New Orleans from hyperbolic and hysterical news reporting. Full of roving armed gangs, looters, and cannibalism. It would basically be a zombie apocalypse without (ok, maybe with) the zombies. Especially the part where the real danger is other humans or conflict within in your own band of survivors.
