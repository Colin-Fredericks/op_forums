    Originally posted by amechra View Post

    Weapon modifiers actually are a bonus to your successes already, so that stays the same.

    Wonderful, I guessed right.

    So a battle under this system would look like this:

    Sisyphus and Lysandra decide to fight, for some arbitrary reason:

    Sisyphus is a Blood Potency 3 vampire with Vigor 3, Resilience 4 and Celerity 3 because he is made of fite, while Lysandra is a Gnosis 1 mage with Forces 3, Prime 1, Life 2.

    Lysandra is also a Prelate of Fury using your version of that Prelacy.

    Lysandra has three health levels and Sisyphus has seven due to Resilience.

    They both roll Initiative: Lysandra gets 4, and Sisyphus gets 9.

    Sisyphus rolls to attack, using a dice pool of 9, reduced to 5 by Lysandra's Defense, he gets 1 success, which is increased to 3 by his Makhaira, dealing a single point of damage to Lysandra, bringing her down to two health levels.

    Lysandra chooses to spend her turn on activating her Forces Armor.

    Sisyphus attacks her again, rolling a total of 3 successes, reduced to 0 by Lysandra's Mage Armor.

    Lysandra starts casting a Forces spell to burn him to cinders with a touch. She uses only the Yantra of her Prelacy for a +2 bonus, hoping to finish this quickly.

    She gains 2 successes, increased by 3 due to her Potency, gaining her 5 successes.

    Sisyphus chooses to accept the damage and takes two levels of aggravated.

    And so on.

    "There is a remedy for everything but death, a hope for everything but wickedness, and everything will lapse except righteousness."

                        Alright, so let me map how this works in my brain,

                        Turn 1
                        So a detective with size 5 and Stamina 2 is ambushed by a mob gunman in the hallway outside of an office he was about to break into. The gunman fires, and with no defense or armor, the detective is hit.

                        With 7 points he can only take the Maimed Condition and the Incapacitated Condition. Being has he doesn't want to be Incapacitated, he takes the Maimed Condition. All his actions suffer a -2 penalty, including initiative. In addition his speed is halved.

                        The Detective, on his turn draws and shoots the gunman, spending a willpower and aiming for the gunman's hand; hoping for an Arm Wrack tilt(-6 penalty total; with the -2 for the Maimed Condition plus the -4 aiming penalty). Remarkably, he hits, forcing the gunman to drop his gun.With Stamina + Size of 8 the gunman can and does take the Bruised Condition in addition to the Arm Wrack tilt. The gunman takes a -1 penalty to initiative and all instant actions. As well he takes a -2 penalties that involve manual dexterity with his off hand(like using a gun). Most importantly, the gunman drops his gun.

                        Turn 2
                        The gunman, now disarmed but not out draws a knife with his off hand and charges the detective. Not having enough actions to attack he ends his turn in melee range of the detective, knife drawn.

                        The detective, not wanting to shoot in melee combat, decides to improvise; using his heavy revolver as a improvised weapon he slams the weapon into the head of the gunman the moment he gets in arms reach. The gunman has defense 5, the ST rules the heavy revolver is close enough to a sap to use it as a general guideline and uses its stat block. With strength 3 and weaponry 3(-1 penalty for improvised weapon), he spends a willpower and an all out attack to make sure he gets over the gunman's defense(made worse as the Maimed Condition applies a -2 penalty to this roll). With 3 dice he lands 1 success. Slamming the weapon into the head of the gunman.

                        With his only Bruised Condition used, the gunman is forced to take the Maimed Condition. Really should of brought some armor.

                        Turn 3
                        Recap
                        The Detective has:
                        • Maimed Condition; -2 penalty to all rolls and initiative and halved speed.
                        The Gunman has:
                        • Bruised Condition; -1 penalty to initiative and all instant actions.
                        • Arm Wrack; -2 penalty to rolls requiring manual dexterity in your off hand.
                        • Maimed Condition; -2 penalty to all rolls and initiative and halved speed.
                        The Gunman, with little other choice, tries to stab the detective in the gut. His normal dice pool is Strength 2 + weaponry(Knife specialty) 3 = 6 dice. With his three conditions, he applies a -5 penalty to his attack. He spends a willpower to raise his dice pool up to 4. He gains 1 success.

                        The Detective, with no defense or armor takes the Incapacitated Condition. It levy's no penalty, but every turn he must roll stamina or fall unconscious.

                        The detective rolls, spending his last willpower to add 3 dice to his stamina roll. he gains a success so he doesn't get knocked out this turn. its now or never. He attacks. Unfortunately, he knows the gunman has 5 defense, his attack pool is 5, and he has a -2 penalty thanks to the Maimed Condition. his attack is a chance die, but he rolls it anyway. He fails his roll, the gunman easily deflecting his blow.

                        Turn 4 and so on...
                        The gunman attacks, this time at a detective with his full 5 defense. he rolls a chance die.

                        The Detective rolls to stay up, if he wins he attacks, he can take any penalty he wants on the roll; as long as the gunman doesn't drop his defenses the attack will be a chance die. If he ever fails his stamina roll he goes down.

                        Does this sound about right?
