    I Need Feedback on a New Face for the Sabbat

    07-29-2015, 09:14 PM
    I started playing the World of Darkness games with Vampire in college in the 90s, so I admit to having some love for the Old Canon as well as the New. There is a lot to be said for bits of all of it. Recently, my weekly group asked me to run a WoD game. The group is a mix of experienced and inexperience gamers; both with respect to gaming in general and the WoD specifically. I know I want to do a game that pays homage to the original while bringing in the wonderful elements of the Requiem.

    Long story short, I am starting the PCs as Unbound in New York under the rule of the re-imagined Sabbat. From there, they will be drawn into an effort by a coalition of other Covenants –who have chosen to call themselves the Camarilla in homage to the ancient body- who seek to dethrone the Sabbat and claim the city for themselves. Once that plays out, nature will follow suit as the Camarilla collapses under the weight of infighting and self interest. All in all, it should leave the PCs with ample opportunity to impact the face of the city.
    But before I can get there, I need to re-imagine the Sabbat. What follows is my first draft of just that. I would love to see constructive feedback. Thanks for reading!

    The Sabbat of New York
    The Sanguineous Catechism’s first chapter states unequivocally the theological position of the Sanctified; the Original Sin of Adam and Eve taints every man, woman and child and leaves them incapable of being inherently “good”; the stain of that sin condemns them to separation from God; this world is the physical form of that separation, a prison for the Sons of Adam and the Daughters of Eve. Humanity’s only hope lies in the Grace of God present in the Blood of Christ, shed by the spear of Longinus.

    Prior to their Embrace, the vampire is no more or less eternally Damned than any other human soul. However, the Embrace, the blood of the Kindred- wholly isolates the new vampire from God’s Grace; the Blood of Caine is wholly incompatible with the Blood of Christ. Not because of any weakness in God’s ability to provide Grace to the repentant, but because of the nature of God’s plan; created by God, the Kindred have been set among humanity as wolves in order that Christ’s Sheep might better learn the nature of their dependence on Grace.

    To the Kindred of the Sabbat, a faction of the Lancea et Sanctum, the role of Kindred as tormentors of humanity is paramount to their Requiems. Comprised of some of the most zealous of the followers of Longinus, the members of the Sabbat are devoted to the doctrinal split known as the Heresy of Milan. Named for the Council of Milan, at which the heresy was first debated by the Sanctified, at its heart, the heresy teaches that God, who is always faithful, would never forget His love for any soul and that even the Embrace is not without its opportunity for Grace. Further, the Kindred are not the Damned of God but rather the very Elect of God; that they have been chosen to serve the highest of purposes and that those who serve well can earn their redemption, a place in Paradise. The Kindred of the Sabbat believe that God offers salvation to the Kindred through the zeal with which they fill their roles as wolves among the sheep.

    While the Sabbat rejects the central authority of the Sanctified leadership, they recognize the wisdom of Longinus’ teaching and organize into independent, local bodies called Consistories that rarely govern more than a single domain and operate independently of each other. The Consistory of New York, the largest such body in the world, is comprises of five Assemblages; each of which governs one of the city’s five boroughs and is traditionally comprised of no fewer than seven members (seven being the Judaic number of perfection) or more than twelve (twelve being the number of the priesthood). A sixth body, the Plurality, is lead by a Teaching Elder who is served by six Ruling Elders. Together these seven Kindred represent the leadership of the covenant in the city.

    The Consistory of New York is careful to prevent any other covenant, including the mainline Lancea et Sanctum, from becoming either to numerous or to entrenched within the city. To this end, the Plurality routinely organizes “Revivals” among the Assemblages with each Assemblage providing a designated “evangelist”. Together with two of the Ruling Elders, the evangelists crusade through the city to thin the ranks of competing covenants and the Unbound alike. Those who find themselves confronted by a Sabbat Revival are given two choices, convert or die.

    Assemblages of the Consistory of New York

    The Plurality
    The senior leadership of the Sabbat in New York, the Plurality is ostensibly the unbiased arbiter of disputes between the Assemblages. In truth, the Teaching Elder, a Ventrue Elder who claims to be the famous minister Cotton Mather, is a master manipulator who plays the five Assemblages against one another to ensure that his power remains unchallenged. The six Ruling Elders serve Mather, five of them acting as advisors –and secret police- among the Assemblages with the sixth being Mather’s personal assistant.

    For reasons as yet not fully understood by any of the city’s Kindred, Mather has recently launched a crusade against the werewolves of the city. The Plurality has ordered the wholesale embrace of expendable soldiers whose blood he has been shedding wantonly in the pursuit of an agenda that can only be defined as the purge of werewolves from the domain of the Consistory.

    The Broken Seal
    Comprised almost entirely of Ventrue stock, the Broken Seal controls Manhattan. Their leadership has openly challenged Cotton Mather’s decisions regarding the war with the werewolves and is rumored to be seeking to displace Mather for the good of the Consistory and the preservation of the secrecy which best serves their role as the monsters in the darkness. If they could be certain that another Ventrue would secure the position of Teaching Elder, they would have long ago backed the Repentant Few in their challenge of Mather’s leadership. Their position is further complicated by the discovery of a Kindred faction in Manhattan of which the Broken Seal was aware prior to the start of the Conflict; a growing presence in Chinatown.

    The First Trumpet
    Also known as Rate-7, or r8-7, an allusion to Revelations 8:7 from which they drew their Assemblage’s name, the First Trumpet governs Brooklyn and has endured the brunt of the bloodshed in Mather’s Crusade. Looking to the verse of their inspiration, the Sabbat of the First Trumpet have reveled in the destruction, seeing the destruction of the werewolves as in line with the destruction of the green places alluded to in the biblical passage. The war has taken a toll on the Assemblage, however, attrition has left them with the thinnest blood and youngest leadership.

    The Repentant Few
    The Repentant Few governs Queens and is the most independent of the five Assemblages, often finding reasons to delay compliance with a request from the Plurality and on at least one occasion allowing a Ruling Elder to become “lost” for several weeks while in their care. They have contributed the least to Mather’s War, proclaiming it as a waste of Kindred unlives that in no way serves God’s purpose for them. They maintain that it is a violation of the Sabbat’s deeply held theology in that it in no way serves to bring humanity closer to God; a position that has begun to spread through the Consistory.

    The Tenth Crown
    Controlling the Bronx, the Sabbat of the Tenth Crown have gone to great lengths to ensure instability and conflict on the streets of that borough; few areas of the city are more dangerous than the Bronx. Murder, rape, assault; the violence native to the domain of the Tenth Crown serves to attract the worst of humanity and provide the Assemblage with the greatest opportunity to serve God’s purpose. Mather’s War has afforded the Assemblage with yet another source of fuel for the violence they have cultivated. Or at least that’s the official story. In truth, the Assemblage is comprised entirely of Kindred of Belial’s Brood. The Infernalists infiltrated the Assemblage decades ago and since that time have converted or killed off every member of it. The violence of the Bronx serves some purpose, but to be certain it is not the purpose of the Sabbat.

    Milites Interfecti Agnus
    This order of militant novices serves as the shock troops of the Consistory of New York both within the city and outside its borders. Comprised only of female Kindred, the Assemblage controls Staten Island, which it polices militantly. It is said that when God needs a killing, He sends an angel. When the Consistory needs a killing, they send the Soldiers of the Slaughter Lamb. As the warriors of the Consistory, it chafes at the Soldiers that they have not been allowed a more direct role in the conflict with the werewolves. They see the position of the Repentant Few as cowardly; openly criticizing the other Assemblage afraid to face the werewolves, preferring to challenge only sheep.

                                  OK, I took a second stab at the Sabbat as a stand alone covenant; stepped back and added in some history to boot. Decided to build it on the Cappadocians and Assamites. As before, I'd love your feedback!

                                  The Sabbat of New York
                                  The Council of Diospolis
                                  In December of 415, the church fathers of Christianity gathered at Diospolis, present day Laodicea in Turkey. On their agenda was the trial of Pelagius, a British-born ascetic moralist well known throughout ancient Rome. The theologian stood accused of heresy for his position regarding predestination and the free will of humanity; maintaining that humanity did not need divine aid in the performance of good works. Further, citing Deuteronomy 24:16 in support of his position, Pelagius denied that humanity was “wounded by Adam’s sin”; teaching that humanity was able to satisfy the Law of God in their own strength.

                                  In the course of the council, Pelagius and the doctrine that bore his name were examined and confronted by the bishop of Cæsarea. From its shadows, Kindred of the Lancea et Sanctum watched the proceedings; themselves deeply divided over the nature of free will and the growing popularity of the Cappadocian Doctrine and its author, Japheth of Derinkuyu, among the Sanctified. Deeply influenced by the writings of the theologian Caelestius, a student of Pelagius, Japheth’s teachings relied on the concept of Original Sin in conjunction with Pelagius’ thoughts on Free Will. By the end of the Council of Diospolis, Pelagius had recanted his theology and denounced Caelestius and, unknowingly, Japheth as well.

                                  Undeterred by Pelagius’ recanting, on returning to Derinkuyu in Cappadocia, Japheth codified his doctrine and gathered a core of believers, twelve in number. Together with his devoted acolytes, he spent the long nights teaching from the Testament of Longinus and the letters of Caelestius. He taught that the Original Sin of Adam and Eve taints every man, woman and child and leaves them incapable of being inherently “good” and that the stain of that sin condemns humanity to separation from God. Further, this world is the physical form of that separation, a prison for the Sons of Adam and the Daughters of Eve. It’s at this point that Japheth’s teachings diverge from those of the Lancea Sanctum. As Caelestius taught that humanity was challenged to grow into the image of Christ through good works done in their own strength; Japheth taught the Kindred to could find salvation in through works; the works they were called to perform, the works of wolves among sheep. Each act of terror, each torment, each moment of wanton bloodletting was a step closer to Paradise for the Kindred performing them.

                                  The Founding of a Covenant
                                  With the coming of the Passover in 416, Japheth called his disciples to a ritual deep beneath Derinkuyu. There they found the bishop of Diospolis stripped, bound and suspended from the ceiling. When his disciples were seated, Japheth rose, took a silver chalice to the bishop and opened the churchman’s femoral artery, collecting his blood in the chalice. Then, he mixed his own along with a measure of that of each of his disciples and all drank. As they did, Japheth repeated the tenants of his doctrine, point by point declaring first of the new covenant’s laws and traditions. In this way both the Blood Feast and the Sabbat were formed.

                                  Over the next year Japheth and his disciples worked to convert the remainder of the Cappadocian Kindred to their covenant. They tolerated no other covenants to function within their domain; giving all who they encountered the choice between faith and fire. Once the Sabbat had an established power base they expanded outwardly into the rest of the Anatolian Peninsula, establishing Assemblages in the cities they came to and seeking out Lancea et Sanctum parishes, which they sought to disrupt or destroy.

                                  As the covenant expanded west, they reached the small market town of Pazarköy, where they encountered a secretive clan of Kindred who hotly resisted their advanced. Their first interactions were violent and resulted in the death of several Sabbat. It was only when Japheth journeyed to Pazarköy and asked to speak with the leader of the sect that common ground could be found and the bloodshed ended. Led to an ancient fortress south of the market, Japheth learned of the Assamites and they learned of the Sabbat and the Covenant’s view of the Requiem.

                                  Since that time the Cappadocians have functioned as the scholars and theologians of the Sabbat cells with the Assamites functioning as the warriors, assassins, advisors and mystics. It is rare to see members of either clan within any other covenant.
