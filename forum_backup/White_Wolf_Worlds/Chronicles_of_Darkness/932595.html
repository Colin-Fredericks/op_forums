                                              Originally posted by Mr.F.I.X. View Post
                                              What more could you be wanting? As someone else said, what you are asking for sounds like flowers of hell. Or do you just want more for the sake of more?
                                              Night Horrors: Demon is the one that's bandied about the the most often. (Hell, it's the one I bandy around the most) The STG gave a complete tool box for assembling angles and other threats, but most story tellers won't turn their nose up at pre-made antagonists, especially give the sheer amount of imagination that's been put into the Night Horrors books. Just about every line has a Night horrors book, and a few have a dedicated antagonist source book (Autumn Nightmares for changeling, a bunch from Mage and Hunter)

                                              Cryptids: Another great idea that gets minimal coverage beyond the tool box. Cryptids give the GMC a different feel than the standard CoD, less Supernatural, more X-files. Additionally, crypto-flora has only barely been touched on, despite hints that there are enough different species to fill a botanical garden.

                                              There were plenty, plenty more suggested in a earlier thread, but I think those two are the most likely candidates as they already have precedent in other lines and they avoid being more of the same.
