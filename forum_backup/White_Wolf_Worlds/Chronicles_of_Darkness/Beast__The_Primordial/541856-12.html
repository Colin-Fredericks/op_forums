    Anakim Ravager: The boom of a thunder clap, the shockwave of an explosion, the violent shaking of an earthquake. They are the Beast's offspring, its lovers, its siblings, maybe even its parents. Most terrors in this world merely cause destruction, but this particular Beast is destruction. It is the unstoppable force of nature that defies mankind's attempts to leash it. Its fellow Giants revere it as one of their great grandsires, an ancient progenitor of their kind. It is perhaps one of the few Beasts in the world old enough to remember The Dark Mother's face.


    The Beast's soul is a monumental creature of the elements. A colossus of earth and wood with thunder clouds for hair and volcanic magma for blood. Its very footsteps crack the earth's crust beneath it's weight and its heavy breathing can dismantle the feeble constructions of man. It sees mankind as mere ants with a shocking level of accuracy. It can flatten their towns and villages in the blink of an eye, but it would only be a matter of days before the pitiful humans started to rebuild what was lost. That's okay, it just means he gets to do it all over again!

            I did, however, just now come up with a terror for him: The Fear of Failure



            -----------------------------------------------------------


            Eshmaki Ravager: War is hell, or at least that is what sound minded individuals who have actually taken part in it will tell you. They lost friends and loved ones, watched as men, women and even children degenerated into savages just to survive another day while their cities were ransacked between two clashing armies. This Beast embodies the fear of conflict, and preys upon the tension that comes before all hell breaks loose. Centuries ago, it served as the adviser to kings and tyrants, dripping poisoned whispers into their ear as they manipulated them into skirmishes with foreigners. In the modern world, it serves as a weapons manufacturer, supplying tools between foreign powers that would result in mutual destruction, should they come to blows. It's most recent achievement was instigating the Cold War between America and Russia by supplying both countries with nuclear arms. Woe be to mortals when it's next scheme gets off the ground!


            The first thing Heroes will notice when they see the Beast's true form is that they may actually be able to kill it. But in exchange, they will be sacrificing their own lives in the process. The Beast is covered in natural weaponry. Poisonous mandibles grow from its mouth while needle sharp spines protrude from its chitinous exoskeleton. Serrated talons are attached to its arms and legs while a dagger length stinger caps its bulbous tail. The Beast is not afraid to take damage in a fight, because it knows that it's opponent will injure itself in the process. The Beast may die, it knows this is always a possibility; but if it's going to die, it's going to take you down with it!

                    Makara Tyrant: Not all trappings are physical in nature, as this Beast has come to understand. When it comes to being a Leviathan, his eyes are like the sea, easy to get lost in. His voice is like the waves, washing over listeners with a siren's allure. In public, he has a menagerie of girls from his school following him around and hanging on his every word! Occasionally, one of the Ex-boyfriends will show up as a Hero trying to win their girl back through violence and brutishness. It never fails to bring a smile to his face when he exposes them as jealous, possessive individuals in serious need of anger management.

                    The Beast's horror is a strange entity. It has the head and torso of an Asian male possessed of bishounen levels of beauty and speaks in a calm, seductive voice. But from the waist down, it is comprised of a writhing mass of tentacles that it slithers around on like snakes. The technical term for his species is "Roper," but he has been called a few less pleasant names in the past by Heroes.


                    -------------------------------------------------------


                    Eshmaki Nemeses: Some people have trouble coming out of their shell. They mostly keep to themselves, rarely speaking up and just trying to get through the day without incident. Unfortunately these people are often the ones who get pushed around the most. Which is exactly what this Beast is hoping for! They are the one who eventually snaps and exacts retribution for being singled out as a victim. It tries to starve its soul when possible, but sometimes that's just too hard. When it goes hungry for too long, the Beast gets irritable; and should its abusers manage to run into them on those days, it usually doesn't end well for them!

                    The Beast's horror is an anthropomorphic snapping tortoise. Its mouth is a sharpened beak while its limbs end with reptilian claws. It's shell is lined with spiked ridges around the sides and top, giving it a more ornery appearance than other tortoises. It's usually not aggressive and tries to go about its business, but mess around in its territory or agitate it too long and it starts hitting back!

                        Eshmaki Ravager: A fear that heroes and villains are all too familiar with is the possibility of a good intention going horribly wrong. That somewhere down the line, you lose sight of what you're trying to achieve and become a corrupted mockery of what you once were. This Beast knows that fear because they have lived it, they thought that humans and monsters could co-exist peacefully with one another; only to be met with violence and misunderstanding. Now it encourages others to stray from their path, to give into temptations and watches with glee as their morals and ethics crumble in the face of inconvenience. It lives by the motto that in this world: It's kill or be killed!

                        The Beast's horror is a tragic creature. It possesses the wings and halo of an angel with snow white hair and the body of an anthropomorphic goat dressed in purple robes. The wings are tattered and frayed while the halo is cracked and chipped. their body is wrapped in a network of thorny briers and although it speaks with the gleefulness of a psychopath, its dark eyes reveal the pained sorrow that it carries within itself.

                                Eshmaki Predator: They say that those who fight monsters should watch themselves so as to not become what they fight. Many Hunters know this all too well, but one of them didn't listen. The Traitor, as it is commonly known among the Begotten, was a Hunter who rejected all that made Man vulnerable so as to become perfect at its calling. And it got what it wished for, to the expense of all that which was dear to it. Its eager patriotism faded as went from nation to nation, seeking the monsters that were corrupting the halls of power from within. Its chivalry and camaraderie atrophied, as it started to see genders as just disguises to take and allies as tools. Resources and safe houses were used and discarded quickly so as to not become liabilities. Its hobbies and friends became distant memories that no longer entered its dreams. Its faith in God became less than nothing.

                                As more and more of its anchors to humanity were abandoned, the Thing in its nightmares grew closer. When the Thing came for the Traitor, they embraced each other with more purpose than any parent, child or lover. The Traitor used its new powers to study monsters up close, finding out every single weakness they thought they could hide. While Heroes concerned themselves with glory and petty grievances, it made sure to destroy any supernatural community it saw as dangerous from the roots. Common mortal criminals were dispatched just as ruthlessly. Vampire Elders and Slashers, an Archmage here and a True Fae there, it all blurred together. No calling cards were left, no melodrama before or after each beautifully heartless murder. Some were glad, others went mad with hatred, and quite a few just carried on. Their reactions soon became indistinct as well.

                                The Traitor knew very well it would never last like this. It searched during decades for a way to make it impossible for others to remember its appearance, only its actions. It found an unspeakable rite inscribed with a part of the Dark Mother's true name, and carried it out beneath the eclipsed moon. The Hunters who used to work with it only recall terrifying glimpses of cautionary nightmares. "Do not follow my steps", the nightmares would say. "I cannot go back, enjoy the company of what you fight for while you still can". The Traitor's profiles in the data banks of Conspiracies and the Libraries of monsters have little else to say about it.

                                The Thing that gave the Traitor all it could hope for has grown strong. So very strong.

                                The Traitor has no one to call Kin among Man or Monster. No place on Earth or any realm to call Home. It has become lonely. So very lonely.

                                It keeps on killing. No one is safe from it. The world has not changed much since it started, but the lessons it teaches were never forgotten. Man must keep Monster at bay to grow, and Monster must contain Man lest it destroy us all with its endless appetites and ambitions. The truth is that the Traitor is becoming obsolete. They are used to the Darkness.

                                The Traitor wants to find an ending. Maybe it can abandon the bloodshed long enough to settle down somewhere and make others' lives a bit happier before it too fades away. Perhaps it will find where the Dark Mother sleeps, kill her and destroy the world's need for fear. It might finally give up and take down all it can along with itself.

                                While that doesn't happen, you better watch how long you stare into the Abyss. The Traitor is the one inside looking back at you.

                                Bye.

                                                        Namtaru Ravager: They call him The Doctor, but any mortal institution would have stripped his medical license from him centuries ago if they knew what sort of malpractice he indulges in. He does not cure sickness, he spreads it! From deep within his lair, he concocts vile toxins and deadly pathogens before releasing them upon the unsuspecting public. He lives for the chaos brought upon by social collapse, and one of the best ways to collapse society is with a highly contagious disease entering the city!


                                                        The Beast's horror is a putrid thing. A giant rat that walks upright like a man and clothes itself in tattered, filth covered rags. Its fur is mangy and unkempt, it's skin is covered in blisters and pustules, foam drips from the corners of its mouth. Physical contact with the creature causes paint to peel, metal to rust and infections to set, while it's fetid breath can knock all but the strongest constitutions out cold! Anyone who engages in unprotected sex with the Beast is guaranteed to contract something that will take more than lotion to get rid of.


                                                        The Doctor's lair is a subterranean laboratory filled with canisters and pipes that contain brightly colored liquids and gases. Bubbling vats of poison and hissing steam vents fill visitors with dread if they attempt to wander without an escort, fearing that one wrong step will cause them to be exposed to whatever sick experiments The Doctor is working on now. If he gets his claws on any Heroes wandering the area, he always takes perverse glee in throwing them into one of the churning vats and watching them spend the last few moments of their life trying to climb back out before they sink to the bottom.
