    Advice on my first Beast

    08-27-2016, 11:29 PM
    Just want an opinion on my first beast and would appreciate the feedback. The premises for this beast is an Eshmaki Collector who loves to take from people what they covet most, and also through doing so teaches a person appreciation for what they have.

    Here are stats I also got a bonus 11xp from previous char death it is a crossover
    The theme is a naga-esque lurker that hunts its prey stealthy only to strike viciously like a snake, its lair follows a kind of marsh and heavy rain feel. With the lair traits Downpour, Suffocating, Flooding as starts.

    Lair 3 dots

    Mental- Intel 2, Wits 3, Resolve 2
    Physical- Strength 2, Dex 3, Stamina 3
    Social- Pres 2, Manip 1, Comp 3

    Skills

    Mental Skills- Academics 1, Invest 2, Occult 3, Medicine 2
    Physical Skills- Athletics 3, Brawl 3, Larceny 1, Stealth 3, Survival 1
    Social Skills- Empathy 1, Intimidation 2, Persuasion 1

    Merits

    Fighting Finesse(Dexterity)
    Iron Skin 2 dots
    Killer Instinct 2 dots
    Indomitable
    Skill Specialty(Brawling)

    Atavism

    Relentless Hunter [Eshmaki]
    Heart of the Ocean [Makara]
    From the Shadows [Eshmaki]
    Limb From Limb [Eshmaki]

    Nightmares

    You Cannot Run
    Behold, My True Form!


    Any advice for on how to improve concept, or overall tips on improving said character would be helpful also please keep in mind this is my first beast.

      Slight thing, you may want to specify more on what your Beast collects. Mostly for when you're at higher satiety and if you're in a mixed game you'll be able to feed more often from Family Dinners, but at that point you'll need something more specific than just a person's most treasured possession.(Though you can specify it down from a very specific type of person as easily as a specific type of object. The main part is that it is supposed to become harder to find the food to feed your Horror as it gets more picky and well fed.)

      You'll need to adjust your skill specialty. It needs to be something your character does with Brawling not just brawling. Like is your character an unarmed brawler to simulate just rising from the water and lashing at the target, a grappler who immobilizes it's prey then does what it wants, or are you going with the cunning snake thing and specializing in improvised weapons?

                  Ooh loved ones are fun. However if we look at the Mahabarata for so information on Nagas it says nagas were :
                  "the snakes were of virulent poison, great prowess and excess of strength, and ever bent on biting other creatures"

                  Actually I like this idea. How lethal are you planning to go with this? Like is your Beast planning to often leave survivors (generally not trying to kill anyone, but if the crap hits the fan) or is your Beast more than willing to end someone when they go to feed? Cause if you're going lethal then the idea collecting a person's loved one's can be something like ambushing the target hitting them with Basilisk's Touch (I would advise that over limb from limb actually) Then taking a picture of the deceased and the one mourner or an important object from both of them.
                  In a nonlethal iteration I'm fond of claiming the targets as a hostage and exchanging them for blackmail material or things of great personal significance in exchange for the hostage. This is assuming you don't just go with the idea of say taking the person down walking out with their wallet and phone and IDs.

                  In these case your horror's scale would be driving you to more difficult targets higher up in a specific field. So like your low feeding is anyone maybe you start having to target people holding greater power in the community as it goes higher, and eventually you'll need to hit the mayor/prince/whatever criminal runs the town from the shadows.

                  Thoughts, comments, other ideas? Can you give me some more information on how you want to run this Beast? Any idea what the others in the group are? It doesn't hurt if your Beast started hanging with them because you got along and favored the same hunting grounds.

                                  Okay, might I recommend a crypt or a mausoleum as a chamber in the lair where the deceased members of your Beast's collection are stored and functions as the eventual resting place for those who've ceased being able to escape. (Nagas do have some relation to the underworld, plus crypt in the swamp sounds kind of creepy.)

                                  Still preferring Basilisk's Touch to Limb from Limb as a starting atavism. It functions off touch, so grappling will work well with it and it compliments venomous swamp monster.

                                  I kind of lean towards You Must Obey over Behold the True Form since your character is a studious one the idea of being able to pry the information from people or make them follow despite their own will seems useful. Plus I will admit a fondness for the thematic idea of robbing a person of their will being done by a being in some versions of the legends known for being a keeper of the powers of consciousness. Granted you don't have to, but I would recommend picking it up later on as it'll be a great tool in your arsenal and probably be of a lot of help to your mage friends too.
