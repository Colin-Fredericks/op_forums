    How do heroes work when they encounter other supernatural creatures? For example, an Anathema of garlic that a hero place on a vampire,with what rating should we regard that? and how do the other gifts work, like Champion's Endurance? do they work or are the heroes just normal humans with no abilities whatsoever?

      Generally, heroes are just normal humans against other supernaturals (though they are a bit tougher thanks to their default Hard to Kill ability), though their ability to attract followers gives them another edge against supernaturals regardless of their nature. The Kinslayer Gift allows a Hero to use many of their powers (though not placing Anathema) on any creatures that have the Family Ties Condition to a Beast, but it only works on such individuals.

                                      As I noted in my first post on the thread (and that was over 1.5 years ago... it's OK to let old threads be), there's a few things that Heroes have over normal humans:

                                      1) The Hard to Kill trait applies even if they're up against other supernaturals. It's not a huge deal, but it's something.

                                      2) If they're aware of other supernatural threats, they aren't going to shirk away like normal humans and can stand up and apply all their skills at hunting Beasts to hunting anything else.

                                      3) They can gain the ability to amass followers. Those followers aren't particularly special, but numbers means a lot and can cause number supernaturals their own issues.

                                      4) There is a Hero power that lets them apply more of their powers to other supernaturals, but it's restricted to those that have the Family Ties Condition towards a Beast (which are going to be almost any supernatural that's close friends with one; the benefits to the Begotten and their allies are too big and they don't inherently know Heroes can abuse that bond).

                                      As the thread covered, it gets very individualized very quickly. Some Heroes are going to die easily if they go up against anything else because they're not prepared, and some can even the playing field significantly.
