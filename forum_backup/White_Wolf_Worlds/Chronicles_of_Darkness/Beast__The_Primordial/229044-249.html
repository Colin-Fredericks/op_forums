                                    Re: the idea of the God Machine as the outer edge of Weird (or not) in the WoD, I think the intent isn't that Demons and their parent/designer/Clockwork Dickhead Machine are unique in being unfamiliar to the Beasts, just that they are a definitive Other in comparison to the other critters who have their own games.

                                    It makes sense if you think about it. All the other major supernatural splats are a human base with a template of strangeness stamped onto them, whether by infection or fate or external meddling. The Demons are a malfunctioning thing that was never human, hiding behind a ghillie suit of person-bits they've woven together. A fair number of the other splats are natives of, or have a strong connection to, another realm of some kind while Demons and their ilk are explicitly native to the material world. In some respects they are the least alien thing in the WoD because they are as native as something can get.

                                    The Beasts and Demons potentially have a number of things to offer one another, but the Demons are paranoid by necessity and Beasts are so used to having a relatively effortless connection with their cousins that someone who doesn't read as familiar stands out as more of a monster than any werebeast or bloodsucker will. Simple fear and caution will make them wary of one another, and they will inevitably clash over resources and misunderstood intent, but that doesn't mean it can't be overcome.

                                        I don't think it's about humanity, although at one point I did, because unfleshed prometheans and even strix apparently count as kin.

                                        I have two theories.

                                        One- Demons are kin, but kinship breaks their cover, so they innately deny the kinship as a defense mechanism (and demons do resist being sensed through kinship). Cover is life for a demon. Of course unless a demon finds their hell there is no way to prove that one way or another. Basically, the unchained have a firewall and a cookie erasing feature which is why kinship never gets that far.

                                        Secondly- Kinship is based on fear, beasts are nightmares made flesh, they tie in to the fear that monsters generate. How many people have nightmares about reality hacking quantum supercomputer former energy constructs? Not a lot of people. Even when the unchained let the mask slip, Joe Q Public is going to attribute the supernatural to the powers of Inferno.

                                        Of course unless demons and the unchained get outed, one can't test that theory either.
