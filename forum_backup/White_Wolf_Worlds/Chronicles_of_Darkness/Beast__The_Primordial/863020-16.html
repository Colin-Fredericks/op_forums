    I actually have a mass of chaotic thoughts in my mind I'm trying to disentangle that deals with how Nightmare Mom might manifest and relate to the "Mother of Monsters" myths. I know Royal Avatars are a thing but there's a side of me that insists treating her "just" as a huge spirit with no unusual rules is not the right way to go. I think to have more interesting ideas for that, a series of reasons to justify them and why I believe that should be the case, but I'm not yet able to organize them in a coherent, readable shape for the life of me

    I think she should be more...physical? Not sure, that's not exactly the right word nor conveys what I'm trying to say. Mostly because I'm not even sure myself. So frustrating
