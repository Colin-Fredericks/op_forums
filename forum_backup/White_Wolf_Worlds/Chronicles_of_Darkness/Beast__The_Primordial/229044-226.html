                I'll grab what I wrote on the kickstarter and give it some editing so it sounds better.

                I just said this on the Onyx Path homepage, but I wanted to say it here since I'll hopefully reach some more folks in this medium, but I'm not a big fan of the tonal shift in the rewritten introduction. Like no doubt a few have said, the Devouring versus Homecoming seems a lot weaker an introduction to the supernatural setting of Beast from an in character perspective.

                A Homecoming gives an innate feeling of alienation for characters, you were always different and now that you embrace your monstrous soul, you finally realise why. You always were alien, you were a monster all along, you just didn't know it and didn't know what made you different.

                With the Devouring, you're offered a choice to become a monster and accept a duty to show humanity the true horrors lurking in the world. So, you basically are playing Lancea et Sanctum: the Guides.

                                                        I've only read a bit of the last 8 or so pages. So please take my next statement in the spirit of co-operation and an attempt to understand.

                                                        It appears that some members of our community are conflating the changes with other concepts which may or may not be effected.
                                                        Beasts requiring another Beast to gain the template - Not relevant to name change.
                                                        Beasts having to decide to become Begotten - Not related to name change.
                                                        Heroes being connected to the Primordial Dream - has nothing to do with name change.
                                                        Becoming Begotten is a sacrifice - Is implied by name change
                                                        Pod People - Not a result of the name change but tightly connected, I grant you.
                                                        Begotten being mini-bodhisattvas of the Autumn Court - Not related to the name change.

                                                        What the name change, from Homecoming to Devouring does is change the tone of the transformation. Before it was implied to be a relief of sorts...now it appears to at least some to be a form of suicide.

                                                        I am not pleased by all the changes but I will not blame a label for ruining a concept.

                                                        Take for example this:
                                                        Originally posted by Beast revised Introduction - Devouring
                                                        Then one day, a Beast finds her. How the Beast knew about her dream, she has no idea, at least not at first. The Beast demonstrates its power and its nature, and she sees the Horror beneath...and recognizes it. Maybe she’s never seen that particular monster, but she’s seen its brothers and sisters in her dreams. Some people recoil in horror at coming face to face with the thing in the dark, but some feel a rush of gratitude. The nightmares were terrifying, yes, but they brought catharsis and good lessons.

                                                        The Beast explains that the dreamer feels the knowledge of the Primordial Dream more deeply than most, and that she is therefore equipped to become one of the Children. If she chooses to join the family, though, she gives up her soul. She will become inhuman, seemingly a person but possessed of a Horror. She will never dream again. Instead, she will become the nightmare that teaches the harsh lessons. She will become the Primordial incarnate.
                                                        Most of the new concepts can be used with Homecoming with only minor tweaking;

                                                        The Beast explains that the dreamer feels the knowledge of the Primordial Dream more deeply than most, and that she is therefore one of the Children. If she chooses to join to come Home, she releases her Soul. She will become inhuman, seemingly a person but possessed of a horror within. She will never dream again. Instead, she will become the nightmare that teaches the harsh lessons. She will become the Primordial incarnate.

                                                        Not exactly world shattering, but the tone changes dramatically...and only one paragraph was altered. So please, try to remember that Devouring is not the other changes; it is a shift in tone from the reconciliatory to a darker, less apologetic hue.

                                                        I have faith that dispite whatever changes they make, the authors will produce something amazing that I am able to (and probably will) enjoy. I know nothing I can do will alter the content. I know this is the same for almost all of us. With that in mind; can we please agree that is ok to disagree and everyone is allowed to have their own interpretations & share them here?
                                                        No rants, no rubbing opinions or facts in each other's faces. Just polite-ish discourse?
