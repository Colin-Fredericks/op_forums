                                            I'm somewhat perplexed by two things.

                                            One, that people conflate Vampire feeding to Beast feeding.
                                            Two, that the common position seems to be that these actions are approximately equivalent.

                                            I posit that they are not, not even slightly.

                                            See, Vampire assumes one of two primary feeding methods:

                                            First and foremost that you will convince your "victim" to consent. Herds, blood dolls, ghouls, old-fashioned seduction, whatever. The Kiss is pleasurable and has no more adverse side-effects than donating blood (usually less, if the vampire is careful). It is not harmful by default. As with most things in life, the dose makes the poison, and a little Kiss does no harm at all. A vampire's food can be willing and left unharmed by the experience. You can't condemn willing vampire bites any more than you can condemn alcohol or a tattoo. It's the same level of "harm."

                                            Second, and admittedly worse, is feeding from the unaware (mostly Mekhet and Nosferatu do this). Still, though, if the vampire takes care not to over-feed, they are never even aware that something happened and experience no negative effects from the bite.

                                            Now, that's not to say that vampire bites are never bad. Done aggressively, or without restraint, or in a frenzy, there are victims being made. It can and does happen. In fact, it almost certainly will happen at least once for every vampire. And, I suppose similarly to Beasts, taking this path is going to lead to people out to kill you. Hunters. Friends of the victim. Police. Whatever.

                                            The difference is the default for Beast is explicitly harmful, and they take specific powers and effort to not harm.

                                            The default for vampires is explicitly mutually (if not equally) beneficial or neutral, and it takes specific effort or lack of good judgment to harm.

                                            (I'm sure someone will bring up lethal damage from feeding. 1: If it was consented to, it's still no worse than anything else people can consent to that causes physical damage, like alcohol or even extreme sports. 2: Given that the Kiss leaves no physical marks, one has to assume the damage is just a crude mechanical representation of blood loss and the time it takes the body to replace it, not actual physical injury.)

                                            I think the line of thinking that Beasts feeding are no worse than Vampires is a bit misleading or ill-informed.
