    Beast: The City of No Illusions (Actual Play)

    08-11-2015, 10:59 AM
    I’m about to start a short Beast chronicle using the Kickstarter rules preview. A couple of my players were very excited and anxious to do it, so I couldn’t wait for the rules to be officially released.
    We met to create characters on the 1st, but a couple of the players have been preparing them for much longer than that! It’s been kind of a fragmented process, since not everyone made it to the creation session and it started late so we had to end early. Long story short, it’s totally possible that all the PCs won’t know each other to begin with, which is something I’m actually ok with! I do regret the lack of group-setting discussion…
    • Tricia’s character is Lyudmilla (Eshmaki Nemesis). She’s a 19 year old runaway in a Puerto Rican girl gang. Her parents are undocumented immigrants from eastern Europe, but she’s very much an American girl. Her Horror is a huge black dog with a skull head. She generally feeds by punishing people who abuse their power. Her prophetic nightmares let her serve as her gang’s bruja, but since being Devoured she’s been struggling with whether or not she should stay with them.
    • Josie will be playing Shiloh (Makara Nemesis). She was Devoured in 2005 when she was 15, so she’s been a Beast for her entire adulthood. Her parents always pushed her to succeed at everything she did, which led her to a good college on the coast and a great career overseas as a high-powered administrative assistant. She used her position to find and punish people for their entitlement, but her disconnection to the broader monstrous community has recently left her feeling unfulfilled. She’s moved back home to try and reconnect with the life she left behind. Her horror is a cecaelia (octopus mermaid) with an anglerfish’s lure.
    • Chucky’s character is Cale Belisario (Eshmaki Predator). An up and comer in the local organized crime scene, his branch of the family was framed for disloyalty and wiped out. He survived and wants revenge against those who framed him—he doesn’t blame the bosses who actually gave the order. He really believes in the family and his highest aspiration is to put the house back in order. During the day-to-day he’s a grafter, a thief, and a monster that teaches honor among thieves.
    • Andrew’s character is Dave Jackson (Eshmaki Collector). He’s a very talented but hard on his luck artist. His own landscape paintings (based on what he saw in his disturbing dreams) sell poorly. He’s also a capable forger, but the paintings he replaces go into his collection, which is a bad way to meet the rent. Oh, also he’s a thrill-seeker, and spends what little cash he has going hiking, climbing, and sky-diving, trying to reproduce the thrill of flying that he used to have in his dreams. His Horror is a golden, fire-breathing dragon.
    • Melissa plays ??? (Makara Nemesis). She’s a skilled veterinarian who cares deeply about animals and punishes people who abuse them. Alongside her medical work, she’s also political mover and fund-raiser. Her Horror is a classically beautiful Greek siren.
    I wanted to talk about what city it would be in, but I’d been previously thinking about using Buffalo, NY, so when we were running short on time I pitched that and people seemed fine with it (Tricia previously lived in Buffalo so she was particularly tickled to play in it).
    I’ve still got some work to do with the characters; Melissa needs a name, Chucky needs a solid Horror, a couple still need more fleshing out, and most of them need connections to each other and to NPCs. This coming week I’ll make a post about my plans for the first session and the rest of the chronicle.

          Planning and random thoughts! Players: please don't open spoiler tags!

          Buffalo is a city of disparate pieces living in relative harmony. Walking down a block can practically transport you from one world to another. Safe, rich streets butt up against gang territory and adjacent neighborhoods have totally different, starkly defined cultures.

          Buffalo is also a city struggling to reclaim its golden years, redefining itself, polishing its iron and glass face, but unable to completely rid itself of rust. Gangs, human and monstrous, vie for territory and fifteen thousand abandoned buildings provide ample cover for things that prefer to not be seen.

          People in Buffalo are used to taking the good with the bad. It’s hard not to when you can drive down the street and see both laid out in front of you. They understand what kind of a place they live in, and any who can’t cope with that dual nature move away before too long. But a Beast’s sight pierces further, and sees the terrors behind the day-to-day. For Beasts, Buffalo is truly a City of No Illusions.


          Both Lyudmila and Shiloh were devoured by the same Beast (inspired by the Ludiaeg from the October Daye series). I’m not sure if she’s the Apex yet. I want her to be powerful, possibly Incarnate, but reclusive and less interested in other monsters than in Beasts.

          Looking at the characters, there are some pretty clear trends. Nemesis, nemesis, nemesis. Eshmaki, Eshmaki, Eshmaki. Mobster, gangster, criminal. I think one of the themes is going to be crime and punishment. What makes something a crime? What gives someone the right to punish?


          So let’s think about the supernatural scene in Buffalo. Tricia wants Lyudmila to associate with Werewolves, several players thought it made sense for them to have Vampiric contacts, and Melissa was particularly interested in Changelings. Nobody seemed interested in Mages, and while I love them, I’m relieved to be able to mostly exclude them. There was also some interest in Sin-Eaters and Prometheans. I may also end up including Princesses, since my previous game was a year-and-a-half Princess chronicle.


          My gut instinct is to represent the rich vs poor aspect of Buffalo as Vampires vs Werewolves. But honestly, there are Vampires on both sides of the fence (Werewolves, too, but it’s less of a trend). So let’s focus on having two factions of Vampires. Invictus with ties to mortal organized crime is definitely one. The obvious opposition would be Carthians, but I think the Circle would work better due to its potential ties to the Dark Mother. The other three covenants exist in Buffalo, but don’t have as strong a presence.

          Werewolves fit in well with the street gangs. We want one near Lyudmila, so probably a neighboring gang. I could see a more religiously-minded pack being aligned with the Circle (though probably looked down on by the other packs). I’ll need to take another look at my map of Buffalo neighborhoods while I think about this. [Dave’s bar fight might be made to relate to Werewolves somehow]

          Changelings, huh. Well, first, I want to use some of the new ideas about Court creation, even if we don’t’ quite have all the rules for it to make sense. The different Courts that spring to mind are Light (Buffalo was one of the first cities with electricity, and it’s called the City of Light), Water (the river and falls are pretty important), Iron (the industrial revolution was a big deal), and Rust. I think Rust is a relatively new Court that started forming in the 70s and had been gaining strength as the others, especially Iron, declines. Not a particularly subtle metaphor but that’s OK. Light and Water are strongly allied. Light is loosely supportive of Iron. Rust isn’t allied with anyone, but isn’t enemies with Light and Water. It makes sense elementally for Rust and Water to be associated, but it wouldn’t look good, so perhaps it’s an underhanded alliance.


          I think I’ve decided that the Apex of the area is an Oneiromancer from the Court of Rust. At the behest of their king, they’ve spread a lot of subtle influence directly into Buffalo’s dreamscape, and the Primordial Dream reflects that. They’re the one who framed Cale’s family as part of an attempt to destabilize the mob, because the mob is on the opposite side of the “economic revival” spectrum.

                          Here here! Can't wait to see how this Chronicle will develop.

                          May I suggest also implementing elements of the other game lines core themes to reflect the city as well? Some example might be:

                          Who we are tonight (Requiem 2E): The future for most of the Begotten is dark and unknowable so how do they chose to be one a nightly/daily basis? How do the people of Buffalo chose to live in an environment as such?

                          The Wolf Must Hunt (Forsaken 2E): The Begotten must hunt as well, how do they reconcile their existence? What meaning do they bring to their condition? What of the other predators of Buffalo. After all the Devouring might have felt frighteningly like a Homecoming to some of the Begotten.

                          Beautiful Lies, Bittersweet Truths (Lost 2E): The Primordial Dream and the World of Darkness are but a distant trickle of water or a faulty lightbulb away at certain places. How do the Begotten distinguish between their regular lives and pursuit of furthering their Legend. What lies do they see all around them? What lies don't they see, do they not see it conveniently? What is the grim truth about life in Buffalo for both mortal and supernatural beings?

                          Hope that helps. XD

                              So it's been absolutely forever, but the chronicle is slowly moving forward. We've only had two sessions so far, and we'll be having another on the 10th. I'm still working on the session summary, but here's some of what I've got done for now.



                              Meetings and Introductions: Chapter One

                              An average weekend. Saturday, August 15th, 2015.

                              Dave Jackson is in his studio, working on a new painting. He’s trying, really trying, to discard years of intuition and experience. Rent is coming due, and he needs to sell something. But an hour later, when he steps back and squints, he sees the signs: a cloud that looks like a skull, rivers of viscera stretching across a fleshy plain. It’s perfect, he can’t imagine how to fix it. No sane person will buy it. People sleeping near these things get awful nightmares. Dave sighs. He needs a drink.

                              Cale Belisario, Mafioso extraordinaire, is on the hunt. He’s prowling through wretched hives of scum and villainy, looking for his prey of choice. He doesn’t hunt criminals, he’s another step up the food chain. He hunts thieves who steal from thieves. The wendigo that only eats cannibals.

                              Lyudmila the Young Queen is dining with her fellow royalty. Cheap wings and cheaper beer. She left her family name and it’s foreign blood behind. She’s 100% American hellhound and she runs with a gang of furies. The only all-female gang in Buffalo and their miserable slice of East side. Their leader, Maria Ortez, is a transplant from NYC, here to start a new franchise of Young Queens.


                              Three Beasts walk into a bar.

                              Well, not all at once. Lyudmila was here first, The Queen City is her gang’s place. They all but own it, and the staff is glad for the business and security, even if it means occasionally catering to the whims of young, dangerous women. Cale came in around sundown and chose a seat where he could watch the rest of the patrons. Finally, Dave enters.

                              The Eshmaki notice each other, tasting heritage through the smoke. They tamp down their reactions, and Dave continues to the bar to get a drink.

                              The bartender has it ready before he sits down, shaking his head with amusement. Dave smiles at him and sips his screwdriver. He’s been coming here for a week or two now and the bartender is finally getting the hang of using mixers. It’s kind of a dive, and he doesn’t really know anyone here, but he likes the atmosphere. The boisterous attitude of the girls on the balcony (Dave is vaguely aware that they must be some kind of gang) is contagious, and the other people here have a familiar, down-on-their-luck air about them. They’re his kind of people, all things considered.

                              The door opens. Four guys walk in. Young, tough looking men. The atmosphere changes immediately. They hold themselves in formation, like soldiers in enemy territory. Their body language is overbearing and they practically sneer at the patrons. They don’t belong. The Beasts get a whiff of them, two are human, the other two have a feral scent, like watered-down Werewolf They walk toward the back, toward the stairs up to the balcony.

                              Dave stands up as the thugs pass him. “Excuse me,” he says politely.

                              One of the humans puts a hand on his shoulder and shoves him back onto his stool. “Shut up and sit down.”

                              “I was just wondering what you’re doing here.”

                              “Who the fuck are you, asshole? Stay out of it and finish your drink.”

                              Upstairs, the Young Queens have noticed the change in mood below. They all look down and see the guys walking toward them. None of them seem to have any gang colors or signs. Lyudmila identifies two of them as Wolf-blooded, mostly humans who have some of the powers of Werewolves and often have a role in their packs. Maria narrows her eyes, then stomps toward the stairs, with Lyudmila the rest of the Queens behind her. The two groups meet at the bottom of the stairs.

                              Cale pays Satiety and activates From the Shadows, turning invisible, and sneaks up behind the group. Gang war is absolutely a violation of honor among thieves. He spends Satiety and hits one of the wolf-blooded with You Cannot Run. The ganger pales, aware that the whole bars attention is on his group. He shrinks back, then forces his way past his allies, running for the door. To everyone else, it looked like he ran away as soon as he saw Maria. The leader shakes his head, then turns back to Maria. He probably shouldn't have been able to do that, given the target couldn't see him...

                              “Who the fuck are you and what the fuck do you want?” She growls.

                              “This is your warning, bitches. Clear out. This isn’t your territory anymore.”

                              “And what if we don’t?” Lyudmila retorts.

                              The thug cracks his knuckles. “Well, I wasn’t looking forward to hurting a bunch of little girls,” he shrugs, “but I figured I might have to.”

                              “Fuck that!” With a flash, Maria pulls her knife and slashes at the wolf-blood, scoring a deep cut across his chest. He snarls and starts to draw, when Lyudmila raises her arms and begins chanting in ululating Ukrainian. To most of those present, it may as well be Tongues. She looks deep in the leader’s eyes and uses All Your Teeth Are Falling Out. The wolf-blooded catches a glimpse of her Horror, a canine skull suddenly imposed on her face.

                              She rolled an exceptional success, but after some checking of the rules I found out that she can’t add this as a Chamber because it isn’t Foggy or, uh, Disruptive. :’(

                              He flinches back, eyes wide and trembling. “What the fuck?” He mumbles, feeling his teeth shift in his mouth. He reaches up and catches a glimpse of his withered hand. “What the fuck!? The fuck did you do to me!?”

                              Lyudmila and the rest of the girls grin at him, sharp and predatory.

                              He staggers back, pointing and shouting. “This is your only warning, get it? We’re just the messengers!” He gasps, wheezing, then runs for the door. The two humans exchange a terrified look, then follow.

                              Cale, still invisible, sneaks behind the bar, grabs a bottle of high-proof whiskey, and jogs out after them. Dave, also decides to follow. Lyudmila asks Maria if she can be excused.
                              “Shit, I dunno why you’d wanna leave after that performance! But, sure, go ahead.”

                              As Lyudmila walks out after Dave, Maria announces that drinks are on her.

                                  Meetings and Introductions, Chapter One (cont)

                                  Shiloh is playing prodigal daughter. She’s gotten an apartment downtown, a car, even a job. But now it’s time to stop putting off the whole reason she came back. First stop on her journey of self-discovery is her Elder Sister, who she sometimes has trouble not thinking of as her Sire, despite the vast cultural difference. The Makara drives down to the waterfront, to the apartment building that Carrie lives in.

                                  The building isn’t abandoned. It’s clearly being taken care of, if not well. It still has heat and electricity. Just… no one lives there anymore. No one but Carrie.

                                  She’s on the third floor. Shiloh walks up the stairs, feeling conspicuous in the low-rent building. It’s been a long time since she’s spent any time in the gutter. She rings the doorbell and waits, anxiously. Normally, she’s cool and collected, but this is outside her day-to-day. Which is kind of the point.

                                  Carrie answers the door. She’s a short, older woman, somewhere between middle and old age. She’s a sharp contrast to Shiloh’s youth and glamour.

                                  “Shiloh,” she says, apparently nonplussed, “I never expected you to visit. Come in.”

                                  Shiloh does, the pull of the invitation as irresistible as the pull of Carrie’s whirlpool Horror.

                                  The two Beasts make some small talk, but the conversation quickly comes around to why Shiloh is back in town. Shiloh tells her elder sister that she’s looking to reconnect with her monstrous heritage. Carrie recalls that Shiloh used to run with a brood of younger vampires, and recommends trying to find them.

                                  They’d called themselves the Young Bloods. Three kindred—Carlos, Veronica, and Gloria—and Shiloh. Four monsters from different walks of life, none of them quite adults. They were barely a coterie and no one took them seriously. The vamps had all been turned too young or barely old enough, and only had a couple years of nightlife on them. Shiloh fit in perfectly. Then she left them behind when she went to college, and barely thought of them when she chased her career overseas. Carlos and Gloria were Gangrel and Veronica was a Mehket.

                                  “Are they even still alive?” It was almost a miracle they’d survived long enough for her to leave.

                                  Carrie shrugs. “Maybe, maybe not. Good for you either way.”


                                  Goal in mind, the Makara heads back to her apartment to change, then walks downtown to the rack. The best way to find her friends, she reasons, is to ask around.

                                  Despite its run-down Eastside, Buffalo has a thriving nightlife downtown, especially on the weekend. It’s bigger, brighter, and louder than Shiloh remembers, but she’s not sure if this is a sign of growth and revitalization, or the convulsions of a dying city. She moves through the crowds with grace and precision, turning heads as she goes.

                                  Sure enough, after checking out a couple clubs, she finds a pretty large group of vampires. She approaches and introduces herself, making it clear that she’s not kine. They introduce themselves as Tyesha, Bruce, Verde, Venom, and Alta. They’re friendly, if slightly suspicious (thanks, Thicker than Water!), and out for a night of unwinding, not feeding. They only start looking predatory when Shiloh asks them about the Young Bloods. Tyesha and Alta are Daeva, Verde and Venom are Gangrel, and Bruce is a Nosferatu.

                                  “Maybe we know something, maybe not. Get a drink, sit a while, I’m sure we can work something out.”

                                  Shiloh reluctantly does so. She chills with them for a while, making sure to casually touch each one and learn something about them with Mimir’s Wisdom. Another mistake, because she wasn’t at low satiety… After about an hour or so, she gets Tyesha alone and whispers the vampire’s secret. Tyesha’s face clenches.

                                  “What’s your intention?” She demands.

                                  Shiloh remains cool. “Same as when I walked in. You tell me what I want to know, no one hears about this.”

                                  Tyesha grimaces. “Fine,” she spits, “here’s the deal: your friends are dead. Carlos and Veronica got ripped apart by werewolves years ago. The other one skipped town. Happy?”

                                  She’s telling the truth. Shiloh frowns. “Not really, no.”

                                  “Too bad, bitch. Now get the fuck out of here and don’t come back. I see you around here again, I’ll drain you dry. Got it?”

                                  Shiloh holds up her hands in sarcastic surrender and does as she’s told.

                                  Mechanically, I wanted to put Leveraged on Shiloh in exchange for the info. She then put Leveraged on Tyesha with Mimir’s Wisdom, which canceled it out. Wore out her good impression by being so blunt, though.
