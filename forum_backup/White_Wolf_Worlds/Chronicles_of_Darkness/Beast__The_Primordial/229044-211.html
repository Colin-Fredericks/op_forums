                                                You know, one thing I'm having trouble nailing down with Beast is the whole society connection thing. Vampires are about politics, night life, and relationships. Werewolf deals a lot with the transgender community. Mage has some privileged upper class vibes to it. Changeling and trauma / abuse. So... what's the Beast's vibe?


                                                Human sadists? Put aside the word Hunger for a moment. We're talking about satisfying a psychological urge to cause others pain and distress. They get a voyeuristic thrill from watching others hunt. Are we looking at a splat based around bullies? And, if we are, are we looking at Heroes as the Columbine-type kids, who snapped and shot up the place in revenge?
                                                But neither effect is automatic anymore, just like the Rampage. Also, I'm pretty sure that one of the story goals in the SAS was to prevent the mass murder rather than being accessory to it. Again, you mistake mechanical advantage for narrative license. Any decent storyteller would deliver consequences for that sort of behavior, which is where that argument, like those of MADD and the like, fall flat.

                                                Like I said on this past Monday Meetings post, there needs to be a discussion of addiction, obsession, and the interplay between underworld subcultures when talking about Beast. That's what they're about. If Vampires are the Mob, then Beasts are the Drug-addled fences, fixers, and leg-breakers that truck with them for their next fix while arranging meet-ups with other, more or less law-abiding groups.
