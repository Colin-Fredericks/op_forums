    Adding on, the BPG includes an epic-level Obcasus Rite to bind a Horror with a new human host after the original Beast has undergone the Retreat or the Merger, resulting in a new Beast with the same Horror as the old (and occasionally "dreams, intuitions, and occasional recollections of the life of" the prior Beast).

    Pages 234-235 of the corebook, when discussing the Retreat, also notes that "death is not necessarily the end. It may be an end, but the Horror is not so easily set aside. It can persist, living on in the Primordial Dream."

    So TYoN didn't pull this out of nowhere, but it's not framed as something that's particularly common or likely.

            They're metaphysically descended from the universal idea of fear. There's a kind of baseline like-attracts-like affinity at play between them and other monsters in a setting where witnessing the supernatural causes most humans to recoil in their souls. A better analogue would be how in 2e a vampire's Beast imparts upon them a sort of intrinsic understanding of how to place themselves to avoid being clearly reflected in mirrors or captured in media. The Begotten get on the same wavelength as other monsters in the absence of complicating social factors.

                                                    Originally posted by Warpwind View Post

What if he opens a burrow from temenos into underworld. does his body come through? doe she manifest as a spirit?
                                                    I was going to answer point by point, but they're all dependent on other answers so it's difficult to do.

                                                    It all depends on how the Beast enters their Lair. If only in Dream Form (implied by them meditating, though I'm not sure that's something Beasts can do innately) then the body remains wherever it's left regardless of what you do with your Dream Form. When the Dream Form dies, you get Soul Shocked. (It can cause a drop to 0 WP, but no one automatically loses all their WP due to dying in their Dream Form.)
                                                    Once the Beast leaves the Lair the Horror remains behind. The Horror never leaves the Lair and cannot be forced to leave it except through certain Inheritances or when it (without being merged) hunts because it's hungry.

                                                    If you bodily enter the Lair you just bring your body with you wherever you go. The downside being that you die when killed.

                                                    Edit: The only thing I'm not sure of is whether you can leave the Astral while in your Dream Form. Without a power that maintains a mental projection into non-dream realms you might not be able to enter them. Not sure whether you're just not capable of going through portals or if you simply just wake up instead (possibly with Soul-Shocked if your projection is destroyed in the process).
