    "You play the monster" isn't subversive in WoD; it's sort of the whole point. Maybe the crossover-friendliness is, in light of previous design, but even that has groundwork laid, in Supernatural Tolerance or whatever it's called these days.

    For better or worse, I think all the Beauty and the Beast references say more about what we'll see than CftBL. Actually, what I'm picturing is the demonic underworld from Buffy and Angel. Beasts are the one-offs, and the extras you see in Lorne's bar, the special monsters that don't pose a threat until you see what they really are and really do. They hang with the vampires and werewolves, because that's who the hunters will go after first, these known quantities.

    I'd like to hear Matt's pitch, though. We've got the lonely, cursed beasts down with Vampire and Promethean, primordial monsters with Werewolf, and mythic, cyclopean potential in Changeling and Mage. What do Beasts bring to the table?

                                Wow what is going on in this thread?

                                The only argument against the game you could make is that, by the very nature of making more game-lines, some of the games will have some blurred or overlapping themes. They did from day one, though, between a Vampire's Beast and Werewolf's bestial raging, or Hunters hunting and Werewolves Hunting. I don't think it's going to be much of an issue here because we don't really know much about the game's themes yet. The Vampires-as-cousins analogy was made in a similar vein that an Idigam Chronicle writer/developer made about how Vampires and Werewolves both hunt but for different reasons and with different prey.

                                We know more about the mechanics than any in-depth themes. Heroes are called, Hunger morality where extremes are good and fluctuate in a single game session, PC groups are broods and might see other supernaturals as brood-mates, something about a Lair (which is what Promethean's safe place merit is called oh no!).

                                Let's just be excited about the game-line and not worry until we know there's something to be worried about
