    Christmas Bonus: Klaus & Krampus

    12-25-2017, 11:44 AM
    This was an idea I had yesterday and I had to fit it between work, family and obligations, so it might not be top notch and need some refining, but I really wanted to get it out in time for today. Forgive me if it needs some polishing, but time was not on my side. It's my little, silly way to wish happy holidays to the forums. An extra, outside all my plans and open threads. Since there are plenty of gamelines I'm able to do passable work with but I made writing enough Beast material to annoy everyone my resolution for the New Year, it seemed appropriate to do it this way.

    It also is not meant to be taken that seriously, in case you were wondering. Lemme know if you like all this weird stuff and, again, happy holidays!

    New Hunger: Holiday Spirit

    Beasts with Hunger for Holiday Spirit, also knowns as Festives, are an odd and small group of Begotten whose Horror craves for remembering others how to behave during the days of celebration. Whether a Festive channels the spirits of the cold days nears the Winter Solstice, the chaotic upheaval of Carnival or the optimistic celebrations for a New Year, it does not matter. Christmas, Yule, Lunar New Year, Saturnalia...holidays have been a part of human culture since the times of old. A Beast with Hunger for Holiday Spirit act as a reminder about what those celebrations are about and punishes those who forget in order to make them learn their lesson and change their behavior.

    A Festive with low Satiety can feed on small demonstrations of festive spirit, like little heartfelt traditions, actions and decoration, but one whose Satiety is higher needs more complex plans and reprimand the most egregious defilers of the holiday spirit they relate to.


    Klaus and Krampus (as Incarnates)

    Two figures arrive into town as days become shorter and the nights grow colder.

    One is a big, heavy-built man with an impressive beard. His strong appearance commands respect and awe, but it’s evident his heart is kind and generous. Anyone would feel safe in his company.

    The other one is equally tall, but he’s gaunt, with an hawkish nose, a short black goatee and sharp features. He’s dressed impeccably and moves with grace, but he’s also intimidating. People look the other way when he passes and nobody would dare to cross his gaze.

    They’re that but also something else. A giant made of ice and wood, coming from an immense snowy mountain and the endless forest that surrounds it. A horned demon with cloven hooves, a monstrous goat-like skull for a face and a fiery breath that reeks of coal and terror, inhabiting the deep, dark caves underground.

    They’ve been at this for centuries, friends and partners, even if they disagree on certain points of their duty. Whether it was a choice of theirs to take on this mission or they were appointed to it by someone else, none can say, but the two remain Legends under all aspects.

    Atavisms

    -Frozen Heart

    Frozen Heart is a modified version of Heart Of The Ocean. Frozen Heart represents an affinity to ice and snow, culminating in the slow, silent and inexorable destruction caused by the neverending cold. It works the same as Heart Of The Ocean unless specified.

    Normal Effect: The Atavism grants immunity to cold and to all sorts of penalties caused by snow and ice. While surrounded by snow or within a blizzard, the character add its Lair rating as a bonus to all physical actions. It can also run through the snow effortlessly and burrow through thick ice as if it was water and move at double its normal speed. While the Atavism grants immunity to cold, the character still needs to breathe while swimming in icy waters


    -Ice Under The Skin

    Ice Under The Skin is a modified version of Infestation. Instead of a swarm of ravenous creatures, the skin of the character hides a unforgiving blizzard. It works the same as Infestation unless specified and helps get through chimneys, fireplaces and small windows

    Low Satiety: The Beast takes bashing damage from all sorts of mundane attacks except fire-based ones. The damage caused by the gusts of frozen winds and ice inflict bashing damage rather than lethal

    Satiety Expenditure: the character becomes a sentient blizzard. If condensed to attack a single character, it inflicts bashing damage each turn equal to its Lair. Those enveloped by the Beast also suffer from the Blizzard, Extreme Cold and Heavy Winds Tilt. As the Beast moves, it leaves behind a layer of ice that lasts for a number of hours equal to its Lair and causes the Ice Tilt.


    -Coal Furnace

    Coal Furnace is a modified version of Dragonfire. The breath of the character is not a turrent of fire but rather a burning cloud of black choking smoke and coal embers. It works the same as Dragonfire unless specified

    Normal Effect: Coal Furnace is still a vicious attack than can burn people alive, but its flames are more subdue and tame, if not less vicious. Without Satiety expenditure, Coal Furnace ignites flammable materials only on an exceptional success. A successful attack applies the Blinded Condition and moderate Poisoned Condition for a number of turns equal to the Beast’s Lair to targets. Wearing eye and nose/mouth protection protection protects from gaining these Conditions respectively.

    Satiety Expenditure: Coal Furnace ignites flammable material regardless of successes, but ignores no Durability. The damage of Coal Furnace is Lethal instead of Aggravate. It still applies the Blinded Condition, but those hit by it also gain the grave version of the Poisoned Condition. Protections don’t help this time: the might of the Beast cannot be stopped by such simple measures.
