                The dream starts the same way every time: You're driving somewhere down the interstate. It's evening; the sun's going down. You notice that you're lower on fuel than you thought, so you pull off at the next exit to a gas station.

                The station itself is quiet. You're the only one around, save for a single figure you can't make out on the fringes. They're far enough away and it's dark enough now to be just a silhouette. Sometimes they're lit ever so slightly by the cherry speck of a cigarette, or the electric glow of a phone. A shiver runs down your spine, but it's nothing. Just a person. You notice a sign on the fuel pump: CARD READER BROKEN. PAY INSIDE.

                You go into the attached convenience store, with its soul-killing fluorescent lighting and rows of artery-clogging snack foods. You hand the tired-looking clerk some money and go back out to fill your car and get back on the highway before you lose too much time out here.

                In the middle of pumping, you casually notice the figure from earlier is gone. Must have walked off somewhere. Maybe they went to the bathroom or just finished their smoke break.

                A cold wind blows, causing you to shiver again.

                Or maybe. . .

                Suddenly, you feel a sharp pain in your leg, where your calf connects with your foot. Suddenly, you can't stand anymore. You start to fall, gripping onto the pump for feeble support. At the bottom of your descent, you see the black-gloved hand holding the dull-metal knife retreat back under your car. You scream, but no one can hear you--not out here in the sticks, not at this time of night.

                You've fallen victim to the Eshmaki Predator who haunts these parts of the dreamlands. Maybe next time you'll listen to your mother's admonishments about driving alone at night. Maybe next time you won't dismiss the urban legends as needless fearmongering.

As beings who were created by and answer only to God Himself, they have the power to eradicate whole empires and reap countless lives without effort or mortals having any say in the matter. As if that wasn't enough, they wouldn't look out of place among a gathering of Great Old Ones:

                        Their whole body, their backs, their hands, their wings and the wheels were full of eyes all around, the wheels belonging to all four of them. The wheels were called in my hearing, the whirling wheels. And each one had four faces. The first face was the face of a cherub, the second face was the face of a man, the third the face of a lion, and the fourth the face of an eagle. - Book Of Ezekiel 10:12-14
                        This makes me imagine Beasts who believe they are divine agents on a mission that lasts a human lifetime, or who wear divine trappings for deception and amusement.

                                            I have three characters floating around my head

                                            The first one's Horror is a colossal snake, an Ouroboros Anakim that crawls in a desert littered with ruins and abandoned temples. It embodies the notion that nothing ever lasts, that even the greatest empires are doomed to become dust and that all the works of man will eventually be forgotten. The character itself works as an history teacher in a small school, which helps to get the lesson across but is rarely enough to sate his Hunger for destabilizing Ruin. He is torn between the fundamental truth at the core of his nature and the desire to keep the place safe and peaceful

                                            The second is a Tyrant Eshmaki, his Horror a majestic deer with the skin made of darkness and antlers covered in fire, like some sort burning bush. He's a sheriff who sees his town as a glade he has the duty to protect. As the guardian and king of the place, he keeps an eye over all the inhabitants, supernatural or not, somehow managing to exert his control from the shadow. Nobody is exactly afraid of him, it's not like he gave them reason to, but they all know he's the one who has the final word over all the issues in town.

                                            Still a bit unsure about the last one. I'm thinking about a Namtaru Nemesis with a terrifying scarecrow as Horror. She punishes those who dare to cross bounduaries, but I'm still working on how to translate this to a character

                                                    A Beast who was a former astronaut and who underwent the Devouring while onboard the ISS. His Horror is an alien thing, golden eyes in an amorphous oily body which can seep around barriers and which can shape itself into tendrils and spikes on which to impale its prey. Its Lair is the same space station on which he underwent the Devouring, save that it is abandoned, save for the thing. When Heroes come to try and kill it, it need only open an airlock to fling them into the endless blackness of space.
                                                    Now retired, the Beast feeds its Hunger for Prey by isolation, making its prey so alone and helpless that it might as well be in the void before swooping in and delivering the final blow, a mark upon his prey to forever remind them of how empty life is without others. He targets those who think themselves an island, able to stand on their own. He tries to teach that an empty life is no life at all.


                                                    For imagery, have a look at the form of Hermaeus Mora from the Elder Scrolls series. At least for the whole 'oil and eyes' thing. For Atavisms I'd take Infestation and Limb from Limb. I'm not sure about Nightmares. Perhaps Behold! My True Form, except that instead of showing the victim a visage that's so horrifying to look at it physically hurts the, instead it shows them the truth of their utter insignificance in comparison to the vastness of the universe.

                                                        I was reading about the Hannibal TV series by Bryan Fuller, and that really sold me on the idea that the eponymous serial killer could be a Beast just as much as a Slasher. And I kept thinking about Beasts with vibes of medical horror. Soon a concept came to me.

                                                        A Namtaru who chooses to be a street surgeon despite graduating with great honor from an admired institution, much to the disgust and shame of his family and colleagues. He simply doesn't care, because he believes true knowledge and breakthroughs are only possible when one disregards useless rules. He does not reject the Hippocratic Oath outright, for that is equally foolish. For the most part, he follows his own code, despite caring little for philosophical discourse.

                                                        Besides cold hard cash, he also demands payment in the form of new things to study, the things no one else wants to be near, yet alone touch. All he asks for is assistance in entering the dark corners where marvels are waiting to be dissected (i.e. help with using Skeleton Key). He has no real home besides his Lair, where he has built a surprisingly normal house amidst all the preserved organs and anatomical diagrams of critters that shouldn't live.

                                                        He is a Collector, but not because he kidnaps people and experiments on them (he finds that method laughably theatrical). The shock is caused by his pragmatism and disturbingly accurate empathy. People near him are often revealed to be sick in more than just the flesh, which he uses as leverage. His lesson, when he bothers to teach one, is that there is something ugly inside everyone, and that pretending it's not there will only make it fester.

                                                        Despite all this macabre curiosity and clinical detachment, he still has a desire to be seen as a source of safety and warmth. To help with this, his Lair has a Chamber dedicated to unorthodox cuisine, which he calls "medicine's more extroverted sister". There you will find things you never imagined could be edible, yet alone delicious. Good luck with getting past the unappetizing exteriors, however. Any mortal or supernatural who behaves is welcome.

                                                        He probably has Kinship with Changelings and Prometheans. Atavisms would include Mimir's Wisdom, Unbreakable, Limb From Limb, Relentless Hunter and Needs Must. Nightmares would include You Must Obey and We Know All Your Secrets. I don't know what his Horror will be like at the moment.

                                                        Does this sound like a viable character? Is there anything you thought was vague or confusing? What do you think is worth fleshing out (pun intended)?
