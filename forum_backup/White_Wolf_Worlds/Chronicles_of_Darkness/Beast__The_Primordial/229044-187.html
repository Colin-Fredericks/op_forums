                                        I'm not going to read 188 pages to see if what I'm going to say has already been said, so i'll sum it up.

                                        Firstly, all the authors of Beast are individuals that I've immensely enjoyed reading in the past.

                                        Beast will be the first OPP product I don't back and, in the future, won't allow at game sessions.

                                        I'll ignore the the victim-abuse/victim-blaming criticism, which I think is valid, but it doesn't bother me. I think art should explore the dark corners of human behavior, though I perfectly understand how people are disturbed by it. I don't think the authors condone victim blaming or anything like that, and I'm perfectly fine roleplaying an evil SOB that spreads hate and pain...but not when the system itself is not self-aware enough to call a spade a spade and say "ya, you're evil". Gamers like to talk about 'ludonarrative dissonance', mostly in attempt to sound like they understand literary theory, but I think in this case, it's the best description I can find in terms of the contradictory messages of the author, the Beast's actions, and the mechanics.

                                        I don't think the late addition of Integrity makes the book's tone about Heroes justified either, as both a Beasts' actions can lower this, AND Integrity can be low by things such as PTSD and abuse. It says a lot when people sympathize and would rather play the Heroes than the Begotten.

                                        I don't like how Beasts fit into the rest of the world, and basically says "all your vampire, werewolf, etc..that rich history of separate philosophies, creation myths, and politics are really bullshit..you're all under the dark mother, except Demons". Oh, and all you guys get along great with Beasts.

                                        I really really don't like how they fit in with Changelings, since a Changeling would take one look at a Beast's action and say "no way, i've see this shit before" and run or rip the Begotten apart. I won't even get into some of the other specifics, like a Begotten's ability to shape the Hedge and their willingness to participate in the Hunt.

                                        Beasts seem like they have all the advantage of every other system, with none of the drawbacks.

                                        I usually really enjoy the fiction in the books...but the fiction in Beast is...bad.

                                        Torturing a guy to death for not tipping a waitress?
                                        Being an assistant Principle that likes to torture little kids that joke around in class?
                                        Poisoning a little boy for stealing candy, then suffocating him with a plastic bag, then turning his house into a torture den? Seriously...he's trick or treating...he's probably like 11 or 12.

                                        I think even some of the old school evils like the baali would even be like "uh, chill dude".
