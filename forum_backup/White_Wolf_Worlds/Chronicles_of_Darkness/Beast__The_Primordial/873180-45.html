                    This is as close to an answer as I've gotten as well — the contested roll, the Willpower cost, and the fact that forcing your way through takes success and multiple turns combined with a Pathway normally staying open for not much longer than that, combined with those timers being on neighboring pages seems like supporting evidence, at least — but the example of someone trying to leave the external location while the Pathway is open lacks the details to indicate whether its outcome is deliberate or not; the phrasing of Hold the Door doesn't refer to that section save through the use of the word "guest" (and vice versa) and the guest-based mechanics talk about their subjects being unable to enter the Lair rather than encountering the same resistance as an intruder would.
