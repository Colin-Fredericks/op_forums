                                                        Given that the Kindred, Uratha, and Awakened were always shown as possible frenemies in almost every game line in the WoD, I feel that it would be a wasted opportunity if there was an X-splat dedicated to them, given that Beasts seem to focus on the metaphysical fears. Mages would be the most vulnerable here because they are still Human. And the Kindred would also be affected similarly because of their slim ties to said Humanity.

                                                        That's why I thought the Strix was a Beast type focused on the Kindred of the World of Darkness. They hit many of the prequisites stated in the podcast, despite the "Human" aspect of it. And their main point of satisfying themselves is to cause chaos among the Kindred and to completely destroy the Masquerade, causing a global recognition of vampire existence. As for the Hero portion, it wasn't stated that the Hero HAS to be Human. In Blood and Smoke, the Khaibit would fit the role of Hero, as they are best equipped to handle the Strix.

                                                        I know that it's too early to tell the actual synopsis, but again it would be a missed opportunity to not include the supernatural community as possible allies/victims of Beasts.

                                                        Hopefully this will be answered in the upcoming spoiler blog.
