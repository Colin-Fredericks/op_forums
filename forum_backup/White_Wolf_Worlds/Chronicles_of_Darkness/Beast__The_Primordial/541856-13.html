                                                Do not call up that which you cannot put down.

                                                Said it before and I'll say it again: taking the stance of attempting to deliberately impart a lesson makes it easier to manage what people do take away from your activities. Killing people in the course of feeding has its own consequences that can be significantly harder to handle discreetly.

                                                Being a living repository of warning signs means you have more of a choice in your life than the guy whose Hunger so defines him that he's only not gone Rampant as a formality. There are lots of ways to react to increasing evidence that you inhabit a world where the myriad gods of hunger overtly control a significant portion of your peers, and deciding that you're going to be the Bai Ze instead of the Nemean Lion is a course of action that's easier to live with in both senses of the word.
