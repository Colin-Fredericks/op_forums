    Can someone help me with the Eshmaki. They confuse me.

    Okay, so they're established as Nightmares of Darkness (although they were once Nightmares of Destruction, which is baffling why they would be changed to Darkness but whatever), they're the Slendermen and Creepers (the Minecraft ones included, I suppose). Okay, got it. Let's take a look at their Atavisms: From the Shadows, makes sense. Limb from Limb, alright. Dragonfire...

    Wat? Dragonfire? As in breathing fire on people? Um, how is that fitting for the whole Darkness theme? Fire was the first thing humans used for light, and that's symbolically linked to creatures in darkness? Shouldn't Dragonfire be an Anakim Atavism? Oh, "a curl of smoke or a dreadful gleam in her eyes is often her victim’s only warning" (taken from Dargonfire's description).

    ...Weak.

    Let's take a look at the fourth one: Relentless Hunter. "Not all Eshmaki strike from the shadows" (from the description). What in the-! I thought Eshmaki were creatures of darkness, aren't they all supposed to strike from the shadows? Isn't that their thing? They're nicknamed the Lurkers for crying out loud! What's worse is that they don't have Shadowed Soul, which allows a Beast to meld into shadows. Y'know, their thing. But actually no, that's for Namtaru, Nightmares of Revulsion, because that's makes much more sense apparently! Oh, "Medusa dwelled in darkness. Not by choice, no, but to hide from her own hideousness. No mirror was allowed within her Lair, and no friendly soul came to visit her. Even the darkness betrayed her in time. It permeated her flesh and filled her eyes, opening itself to her, until there was no more refuge in the dark. Gnashing her teeth, she shut her eyes and lay in wait, listening to the silence of her Lair. She belonged to the darkness and it to her." (from Shadowed Soul's description).

    ...Weak.

    So I ask again, can somebody please explain this to me? I sure can't.

                                                Can a beast effectively immunise itself against anathemas by hiding a weapon which has been used for the weaponbound anathema in the lair?

                                                After all the rules state that a beast can only suffer from one anathema at a time, any further attempts to place one fail and instead reveal the current one. For most anathemas that is just as dangerous as placing a new one, but the weaponbound anathema is completely harmless for the beast unless the hero has the weapon in question. If it is hidden beyond his reach - for instance in a lair - he cannot exploit the anathema. Even if he has the open gate gift allowing him to enter the lair he has first to deal with the (potentially lethal) environmental tilts there.
