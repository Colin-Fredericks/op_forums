                    So, I am going to go a bit Meta on this, but I hope it helps with this discussion a bit.

                    Kinship seems to be tied to the the Primordial dream tapping into the Astral Reflections that correspond to the different splats. The Astral Realms is a conceptual space, where the collective ideas of humanity and the concepts of the world find representation. Everything from realms representing the whole of humanity, from different religions, political ideas, philosophies, fears, dreams, etc. exist in the Temenos, and the Anima Mundi is reflection of the concepts of the world itself. And while more personal, each onieros contains different reflections and symbolic representations of that person.

                    My theory is that when a Beast is calling on Kinship powers, they are connecting the Primordial Dream to Realms representative of the other splats and entities, channel concepts related to those supernaturals through the lens of the Primordial Dream. If I was a mage, I would describe it as akin to how mages use magic, but utilizing Astral Symbolism rather than Supernal Symbols, with the Primordial Dream forging those connections by utilizing sympathy between the Supernatural that the Beast had developed a kinship bond with and the respective Astral concepts and/or realm (while the Beast hearing this would roll his eyes and wonder why mages overthink and try to explain everything). This is why nightmares aren't just "replicate a power" but draws on concepts associated with the splats in question Thus, it is going to not quite follow the same rules, or even be the same energies, as they aren't tapping into the original source of those things but the astral representation instead. Whats more, they can only draw on the concepts that resonate with the Primordial Dream, meaning that their connections and applications will always be through the lens of being tied to primordial fear.

                    So for the concept of the Nightmare that replicates the effects of the Abyss:
                    This nightmare is drawing on the Astral concepts of the Abyss as it is perceived by mages and then projecting this astral concept. The vision of the abyss and the effects aren't actually drawing on the Abyssal spark or abyss, but the ideas of these things and what they represent to mages, and using these astral concepts to create an effect that duplicates these concepts more broadly. Thus, you have a force that creates something that embodies the traits of havoc but effecting all of the supernatural, not just the supernal magics, because it isn't actually the abyss but the astral concept of the Abyss.

                    I also look at this as to why Demons don't really have kinship: they lack any real Astral Reflections. They destroy a human soul and merge with the metaphysical void, and the soul is what connects most to the Astral. Furthermore, discussion have made the God Machine and its products as metaphysical manifestations of human concepts of technology, logistics and infrastructure that developed an alien intelligence, akin to the astral entities, but existing in the material realm. Thus, it lacks a true astral representation, as do the demons since they are derived from it. While they can invade the Astral, they don't have a conceptual space reflecting them and their mythos that can be tapped by the beasts connection to them, as they themselves are that conceptual space.

                    And Beasts don't really look at it this way at all, as their own ideas of the Astral beyond the primoridal dream is limited to whatever exposure they get following a mage or chamber into it.

                    Though, this is is all just conjecture and I could be completely wrong. Beast makes me seek connections based on other game lines, and I will admit that I do turn to mage since the Astral is their thing (The Beasts may exist in it, but the Mages can go anywhere they want in it).

                    TlNR- My theory is that the thing tapped into is the Astral Concept of the Abyss, which the Beast draws on after being with a mage whose nature associates him with said concept and allows the beast to call on it through their kinship.
