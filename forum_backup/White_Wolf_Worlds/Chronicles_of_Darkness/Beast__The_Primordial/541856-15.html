                Anakim Tyrant: To say that he casts a long shadow over the city would be an understatement. Before his devouring, he felt like he never had any control over his life, always following orders and being powerless to follow through on his own decisions. But once he stood tall, he saw just how small and insignificant those who oppressed him truly were. On that day, he decided that not only was he going to be in control of his own life, he was going to take control of everyone else's life to make up for the humiliation he endured!

                Now, he rules the city, nothing gets approved in it without his say so. His shadow looms over the heads of the common rabble, watching their every move and taking swift action to silence any dissension in the ranks. He laughs at the notion of being called "mayor" behind closed doors. King would be a more appropriate title, and he's not relinquishing the crown as long as he continues to draw breath!

                            Namtaru Nemesis: The absolute strongest adversary to overcome is yourself. This Gorgon is the manifestation of the self, bringing with it doubt, fear and weakness. From a casual observation, the Beast appears to be an identical copy of its victim, but a closer inspection will reveal the truth. The monster's body is composed of reflective glass and polished metal, like an anthropomorphic mirror reflecting the image of those it preys upon. It studies its victim, learning to move and act as they do. When it feels confident that it can replicate their behavior, that is when it attacks. Infiltrating their dreams and claiming to be their subconscious emotions. Envy, hatred, lust, pride, greed, the Namtaru is the guilty conscience that tears you up inside.


                            The monster's lair is a house of mirrors, each one of which is a window into the soul. A monitor through which to view the most humiliating, painful, and terrifying moments of a person's life. The monster herds its prey through the mirrors, forcing them to watch one scene after another, as their spirits are crushed beneath fear and despair. But the Namtaru must take great care not to go too far with their sadism.

                            A Hero capable of forgiving themselves is a terrifying opponent, indeed.

                                        Makara Tyrant (from my Savannah setting): Normally the potential to become a beast doesn't run in human bloodlines, her father's is one of the exceptions. Supposedly he and his many half-siblings were born devoured, the spawn of an ancient incarnate who reached a state beyond that. The ancient sea serpent's spawn became the apex incarnates of their own hives, all of them linked into their father's primordial empire

                                        She on the other hand was devoured after birth, by her father of course, and her horror looks much like his, a loch ness monster type creature that swims through the swamps and rivers of the primordal dream in her father's domain. Her tyrant nature mostly takes the form of being the smartest kid in the room, the one who knows how her peers' schemes will blow up in their faces before they even begin and enjoys asserting her mental superiority over the other local children, and making her owns plans that use them as pawns. That being said she isn't above playing the "spoiled princess of the apex" form of tyrant either. Her main kinships are with some local changeling kids with aquatic kiths and with a boy who.... she actually doesn't know what he is, to tell the truth, except that he is both incredibly dumb, absurdly lucky and somehow never stays dead while never remembering his deaths from their misadventures.

                                        in human form, she is a biracial 9 year old girl, capable of passing for white if she takes care not to let her Gullah accent slip and uses enough hair products. When she isn't wearing her school uniform, she usually wears the sort of clothes little rich girls usually wear, with subtle accessories drawn from her Gullah heritage.

                                        Her lair takes the form of a sunken amusement park in brackish water, not unlike if an amusement park in Orlando sank back into the swamp, with chambers made from the various places she's mentally dominated and terrified the other children of Savannah, though the latest chamber comes from the Disney Cruise her father took her on over the summer as a promised reward for straight As last year in school, the ship's counterpart in her lair a capsized wreck on the marsh floor.

                                        Quotes:
                                        (Upon seeing the results of her idiot kin's latest failed scheme) "I don't even... Look, just... explain to me what you were trying to do when this happened."

                                        (intimidation) "Do you want me to tell daddy what you're doing in his turf? because he will be much harder on you than I would if you let me in on this."

                                        (impressed) "I hate to admit it, but the idiot has a point."

                                        (Bratty) "But Daddy, you said I could land the death blow on that hero!"

                                            Honestly, despite my assorted misgivings and gripes with Beast seeing people's character ideas has kept me interested. Plus, I really want to play Ingrid some day, she's one of my favorite characters that I've created. Plus she has something a personal connection my own fears in a way that most monsters don't- I heard about the mothman and Indrid Cold when I was pretty young, and the story (and the drawings of the mothman I saw online) freaked me out in a way that not much else ever has. For a significant period in my life, my main fears were the mothman, Indrid Cold, and dementors coming out of the toilet.

                                            Also, I retooled a Leviathan character I had into a Beast and made her part of a cross-splat Mummy cult I was working on. She's a Makara Tyrant and the owner of a small New Jersey-based and heavily mafia-connected construction company. Basically, if the Cloverfield monster had more arms and took fashion tips from The Situation. I also came up with a nasty Kinship Nightmare for her that came from the casino-owning business magnate Mummy she worked for- it's called Everyone Has A Price, and it forces the target to think of things- including themself- as commodities.

                                                Anakim Tyrant: Where his friend shows her prey their own failings, he shows them how far they are from what they want. He preys upon those who desire to be perfectly beautiful or attractive. He shows them that there is always someone more perfect, more attractive, that even supermodel-looks won't stop the company that you model for Photoshoppping you to be better.

                                                Entering his Lair, even Heroes are overawed by the utter, impossible beauty of the rainforest that greets them, of the exquisite placement of the impossibly green leaves, of the droplets of water which fall like diamonds from every frond and leaf. Even the storms that sometimes roll in overhead are beautiful, tempestuous cathedrals in the sky. The entire place has a crystalline, unearthly allure to it, as if it were a living, growing artwork.

                                                He calls his Horror the Peacock Serpent and it takes the form of an enormous, feathered serpent. Every inch of its plumage is spotless and gleaming, iridescent in countless shades of red, blue and green. Its eyes are like yellow gems in its face and even its deadly, venomous fangs look beautiful and shapely. When he feeds, he brings his prey into his Lair, usually making it seem as if it is a dream. In serpent-form, he is almost pleasant and genial at first, if prideful, offering to guide the bewildered mortal around his domain. Over time, though, as its prey becomes more and more overawed with its surroundings and the Serpent itself, its words become crueller, more cutting. It begins by commenting on the mortal in relation to its glorious self, and how they must be envious and it's so sorry it can't help. Then it moves on, letting the prey consider their own place. Eventually, when they sit down and despair over themselves, it simply devolves into a tirade of insults and deriding comments as the Serpent coils ever-tighter around them.

                                                Those who can push past this and gather the will to leave (and live) are guided to the exit and wake up, unharmed and, perhaps, made wiser. Those who cannot are either devoured whole by the Serpent or remain within its Lair. A few have survived, feeding upon the fruits and beasts within the Lair and have created almost a 'cult' of sorts, venerating the Serpent as the most beautiful thing in the universe, slowly forgetting their original lives within the mind-bending beauty of the Lair.
