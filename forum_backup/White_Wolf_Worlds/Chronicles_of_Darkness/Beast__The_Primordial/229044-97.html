                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                            Character creation training exercise: What Families would work with now supernatural creatures from the World of Darkness? In the other words - what fears ( and Families of Children) reflect vampires, werewolves, mages, Prometheans, changelings, sin-eaters etc.?


                            I agree that vamps and wolves would be raptors, since their attacks can come straight out of nowhere and where you least expect it. "He's an apex predator, so he never looks up." Well until something humanity didn't expect blind sides them!


                            Mage would be Anakim, obviously. A being who has all of reality as their plaything can definitely make you feel powerless. I would also suggest that Changeling would fit the same role, especially after what they experienced at the hand of the True Fae. You're taken to a world that's completely alien to you and every time you venture back there you barely escape with your life, sanity, freedom or all three!


                            Demons and Prometheans would be Eshmaki. The Prometheans being soulless constructs seeking to become more human, while Demons lose their sense of purpose and are constantly being hunted by an angry god and his angels!


                            Mummies would be Leviathans, given their origin and the fact they wake up with demi-god powers for a few years before going back into hibernation.


                            I have no idea what Sin Eaters would be, as they don't seem to fit the roles presented.



                            From what I've read on Beast, it sounds like the characters you play have human bodies, but the souls of monsters. That's kind of disappointing, but at the same time it makes sense. Awfully hard to pretend knowledge of the supernatural is kept secret when you have a 50 foot tall Anakim caught on the 11 o'clock news!

                                Originally posted by Bloodspeaker View Post


                                We do indeed.
                                To be honest i half expect and fear that beasts will go the route of werewolf regarding their warforms, with a variety of different/unique characteristics.
                                But i fervently hope that this will not be the case. It would be a disservice and a missed opportunity for wod in general, to pass up on big, scary monsters (and even bigger, gigantic ones in the endgame) as warforms and "alter-egos" for the PCs regardless of their origin, nature or other characteristics.

                                Plus it will have the effect of further distinguishing and reinforcing the Beats position as the common, scary family that links all supers together and fills that last horror niche that begs to be filled.
                                At least that's the impression i get by reading the title and the scant info we have.
                                I guess we will see in due time.



                                I wouldn't mind the Beasts having the ability to change between human and monster form. You might think the Werewolf form is pretty small, until you remember that it is 9 feet tall and weighs 400 lbs. Then, of course, you have the Gargantuan Kith who can add up to 10 levels on their Size. If a Grizzly bear is registered as a Size 7, and humans are a Size 5, imagine what a Size 15 Ogre would look like.


                                It's not impossible for us to have massive characters in WoD, it's just that only a small group of them have size alteration powers. That being said, I'm sure that the Anakim who's entire premise is descended from giant creatures will have some ability to alter their size. Perhaps not to monolythic proportions, but large enough that you feel like a power house.
