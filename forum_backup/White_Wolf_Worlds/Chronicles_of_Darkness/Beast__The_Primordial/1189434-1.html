                                        Where the Wendigo Walks (Rupert's Land, 1670-1870)
                                        Canada may have confederated to the east, but the forests and plains of the west remain wild. Dominated by the fur trade of the Hudson's Bay Company, British Imperial power continues to creep its way along the trade route seven as the fur traders and Metis seek to keep themselves free from the Crown's oversite. This is a feeding ground for Beasts of all kinds, be that European, First Nations, or Metis, as the wilderness of the far North is fraught with danger even for the most experienced of hunters. That danger is starting to take a new form that makes even the Begotten tremble, however. The Wendigo stalks the lands and if it is a Beast, it is certainly the most powerful Apex seen in generations, but some Begotten wonder if it isn't the avatar of the Dark Mother herself.

                                        Chronicles Seeds:
                                        • Wendigo hunts – First Nations tales speak of ancient, always hungry monster called Wendigo. Many Begotten assumes it must be local Apex of immerse power. They are right. They only do not know that his's Hunger stretch outside mortals, for the Family itself! PCs are hunted by the Wendigo that pointed them as his delicate course. Only the Hungry Owl – as he is sometimes called - like's his meals to have real taste. And they are best when they got some real life experience, with Lair much more powerful than just after Devour.
                                        • Always hungry – Newly discovered Rupert's Land is harsh realm. Winters are extremely difficult - many times food is short on long, cold nights. And in those times, humans need to fill their bellies. When mortals look over one another as dish to be served – they often are inspired by Begotten. The so called 'Hungers Cults' are known phenomena in those times. Sometimes Children of Dark Mother are leading humans to do what's need to be done. Other times it's Beasts that served as next meal by mortals. PCs are discovering one of those Cults in settlement where they stay. Will they found new human supporters? Or will they be hunted by the mortals or their own kind?
                                        • Family matters – With colonists, come to the America totally new mix of Beasts and their Horrors. Not all of them recognize one another as Family. Understanding that all Beasts are Children of the same Dark Mother maybe difficult. PCs are thrown between two communities of Begotten, that cannot understand one another, like European and First Nations Beasts. It's on PCs shoulders to to communicate between two sides – or the war will ensure.

                                            Seeds for next Era...
                                            Tyrants of the Rising Sun (Occupied Japan, 1945-1952)
                                            In August 1945, the world and the Primordial Dream was changed forever. The atomic bombs dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki, and the world was introduced to a brand new fear that would dominate the rest of the century: the fear of nuclear annihilation. That weapons technology had reached a point where tens of thousands of people could die in an instant. The people of Japan know this better then anything else. Especially since the radiation still lingers on in the sites where the bombs dropped. This is further exacerbated when American forces occupied Japan for half a decade, increasing anxiety and lethargy on losing in a major world war. The combination of new and old fears would create...an unusual effect on the Horrors created in this time. Years later the Japanese Begotten still wasn't sure what it was, but the Horrors all grew to enormous size, almost as tall as buildings. Whatever they were, a new kind of Beast was created in the embers of the dying Empire of the Rising Sun: kaiju. From oversized insects, to mutated lizards and dinosaurs, to sea dragons that crawl on land, Japan is about to face a new kind of fear.
                                            Chronicles Seeds:
                                            • Daikaijū–Atomic blasts in Hiroshima and Nagasakiborn new Fears in humanity – fear of nuclear annihilation. Theories of mutated, giant monsters capture Japanese imagination and dreams. In coming weeks there is strange phenomena on Begotten – all newly Devoured have Looming Presence Atavisms from their great new Horrors, regardless of their Family. Begotten are given this powers on top of normal two Atavisms they come from their transformation. Those new Beasts have also intrinsic pull to be Apex in their local communities, to overthrown previous ones. ( I think about special Condition here. ) Coming Begotten are compelled to join their own Broods and in groups fight for supremacy on Primordial Dream of Japan - and in the end fight inside the Brood to shown the last, most powerful Apex, once and for all.

                                            New Merit: Kaiju (****)

                                            Prerequisite: Looming Presence Atavism

                                            Horror of this Begotten is extremely imposing. The effective Size bonus by using Looming Presence is twice normal – so Normal Effect gives Beast Size bonus of Lair x 2 and Satiety Expenditure makes Size bonus of Lair x 4. Ture Apex of Kaiju kind can rise up to effective Size 45 and more!
                                            • ​Enemy Within – American forces are occupying previous Empire of the Rising Sun. Many traditional Japanese – and Japan's Beasts – are feeling betrayed or that they will lost their way with U.S. Military. Some islands Begotten are using their powers to solve the problems of their human kin, not other Children of Dark Mother. There are attacks on humans – more gruesome than normal Hunger feeding – and some Edo-styled markings, praising 'original' Japan's culture. There is resurgence in classical Japan's myths Horrors and there is rise of 'pure' feeding of Hungers. Will PCs uncover orthodox faction in Begotten society? Will they stop them, when their Lesson Teaching will become massacring American civilians?
                                            • Manga –Manga as art style has roots in Ukiyo-e, traditional style of Edo period. These pieces were heavily influenced by American comics and animation – mainly Disney works – and Manga evolved by intermingling of those two directions. Creation of this mix has interesting – and somehow terrifying - effect on Primordial Dream. It created being similar to Horror but less imposing on first sight. Avatar of Manga idea is hunting for Begotten – both Japan and American - and when finding one, she joins second Horror for looking of new 'art'. Those Manga-Spawn are highly unstable, with weird and unstable Hungers.
