                            Dusksage: Do you guys think that the Beasts will have some kind of Mask like ability that hides their monstrous form from the rest of humanity or if they're human in appearance but have a predator's aura like the Kindred and Uratha from 2nd Edition Requiem and Forsaken?
                            For now, I'm going to guess that they look human most of the time, but have a Predaura/Disquiet/Sybaris-alike mechanic. Their souls were described earlier on as big monstrous things that ravage the local dreamscape - whatever dreamscape means in this context - and they might be able to pull aspects of it into themselves if they need to monster out.
                            Also do you think that Heroes will be able to see/detect a Beast's true form/aura or if they're having something more similar to Disquiet where the Heroes don't have to know exactly what the Beast is but they have a clear instinct that it's the source of their troubles?
                            I suspect the latter, because it has a wider range of potential plotlines. But I'm betting a Hero will know a Lair when he sees it.
                            StSword: It might, they revealed that the fear of being eaten is one of the splat types in beast.
                            I think darkness is another, but that might stated more generally of the type of thing that a splat might be. I do suspect, right now, that each Family will be, or think itself to be, more closely related to a particular other splat or two than others. Like the splat about being eaten is most closely related to werewolves (and maybe vampires too), and if there's a darkness one, it might be related to vampires and/or sineaters - meanwhile changelings and/or Prometheans might be related to a splat about the fear of being mutilated or disfigured.

                                                hmf...considering how monstrous humanity can become...and all while wearing a pretty face.
                                                just dropping a quote that feels relevant:

                                                ...Man did not evolve naturally, as modern science teaches, either accidentally or as part of a divine plan. He is a freak of his own making. All of his actions — not only his cruelty to his own kind and his ruthless exploitation of the animal world, but even his great intellectual and artistic achievements — are the actions of a monster.
                                                and here's what Nietzsc-, I mean Zarathustra has to say on this matter:

                                                "Man is evil"—so said to me for consolation, all the wisest ones. Ah, if only it be still true today! For the evil is man's best force. "Man must become better and eviler"—so do I teach. The evilest is necessary for the Superman's best.
                                                It may have been well for the preacher of the petty people to suffer and be burdened by men's sin. I, however, rejoice in great sin as my great consolation.—
                                                Such things, however, are not said for long ears. Every word, also, is not suited for every mouth. These are fine far-away things: at them sheep's claws shall not grasp!
                                                There are reasons why supernaturals maintain their their own "masquerades". Humanity has not only advantages in numbers, is it as capable of monstrous acts as any supernatural, if not more. Slashers are the ultimate example of "man's best force" manifested.

                                                That's why I would love if it was emphasized that while Beasts are monsters Heroes could be equally if not more monstrous as well. Look at myths, heroic figures like Herakles fought monsters, but were capable of monstrous savagery as well.

                                                “I am absolute, I am perfect, I am supreme. I shall be eternal. My tragedy, is that there is no other fate for me. My powerlessness was that I couldn’t subjugate my journey to the gods, while dreaming of rebirth at the end of distant time, like other pharaohs.” Ramesses II, Fate/Prototype: Argent Fragments.
