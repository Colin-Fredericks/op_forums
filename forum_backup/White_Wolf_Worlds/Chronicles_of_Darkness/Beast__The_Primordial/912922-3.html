                            I'm thinking for the concept of the character that as a child (and up until his becoming) that he had constant nightmares about finding a book that was missing. He'd run from crooked shelf to shelf looking among the ashes of burnt parchment remains as the dread grew stronger. In truth what he felt was that the Beast in him had been damaged horribly in a previous incarnation and many of the books he held dear were lost. However, the Horror knew that not all that is destroyed is gone. Sometimes things slip between the cracks, falling into other realms. That the underworld holds many a lost library to time.

                            The Horror takes the shape of a giant owl, twice as tall as a man. Thin in proportion with exaggerated beak and talons but nothing can detract from it's enormous, wide yellow eyes, unblinking and unwavering. It's coloring brown and black in patterns that almost appear like a lost script. Omen knows that the pursuit of truth is not just the most enviable quest, it is the only one. As such those who would fabricate bring us all low and must pay a terrible price for their crimes.

                            The heart of Omen's lair is a great tree, forming a maze of many intertwining root systems. Each root has wrought into it shelves holding books, parchments, carvings and ancient forgotten languages. Or many did, before the hunter came. Now the frigid heights of the tree contain burnt limbs and ashen shelves. The ravens that carried the books and scrolls to their shelves now sound their mourning in cacophony and pick through delicate ash heaps, looking for the scraps that remain. While the books may be gone, the memories of what was read remains, it is enough until copies can be recovered. Already in a several blind forays into the underworld has a destroyed library been found, taking up a vacant chamber in the lair. However without a guide the underworld is a treacherous place. Clearly, more study is required.

                            Samuel Albertine was always an odd child. Born to a family with means and high expectations, he was noteworthy for a lack of interest in the people around him. However the dusty library his father kept was full of worlds he could almost touch, and somehow were already familiar to him. The round rimless glasses that sat low on his nose were constantly reflecting in the dim light. In an effort to force the young child into a semblance of social interaction, Samuel's parents forced him to take up a sport of some kind. Rather than doing something brutish and competitive, he decided track and gymnastics were a sport of form and technique. His coach used to say that he spent more time with his feet off the ground than on it.

                            Getting into the professional world was another matter. Samuel felt constant dread that he didn't know ENOUGH to be good at what he did, did he really belong where he was? Could he teach what he knew to others? Was he SURE he was good enough at what he knew? After he discovered the true nature of what he was those feelings calmed. The answers were waiting, and like anything else in his life it was just a matter of finding the right book.

                                    Aaand here's the character sheet: https://www.dropbox.com/s/8q4y6zxneo...Elias.pdf?dl=0 Asset skills are Drive, Firearms and Survival.
                                    Also a question to the Storyteller, regarding nightmares and determinig satiety: Would Cheiron Group Hunters be elligible for kinship, with all their taumatechnology implantations and whatnot?
                                    Aaand here's his backstory and fluff.

                                    Elias' story started off as many before him and many after him. Expanding upon his childhood fascination with airplanes, he chased the career of a pilot. Of course, shortly after he entered the adult life, the country got involved in another sensless war for oil, and, due to a combination of idealistic idiocy and thrill-seeking he decided to become a fighter pilot. After a couple years duing which he completed all the required trainings, passed the required exams and whatnot, he became an active pilot in service of the US Air Force, and was promply sent somewhere where he could be useful, namely Iraq.
                                    There, Elias realized two things. First is that he wasn't quite fighting the good fight for the truth, justice and democracy, which wasn't that much of a surprise. Second was that all the death and destruction he was dealing from up high, every missle he fired, every life he took, every smoldering wreckage he left behind on a successfull mission, it filled him with some weird feeling. Feeling of accomplishment, of joy, of satisfaction. He figured out that he liked what he was doing, all the destruction and ruin. At one of his missions, however, a lucky MANPAD missle put this to stop. His jet got hit. Elias promptly lost control over the handicapped vehicle and crashed. He crawled out of the wreckage, heavily wounded, and promptly tried to somehow bring himself to relative working order, before losing conciousness.
                                    While unconcious, he was visited by a dream. The same one that haunted him regularily from the very beginning of his career. He was standing there, in the middle of a scorched desert, earth marked with cracks and craters, with a couple weak looking cases of growth a generous person might describe as plants, stretching as far as eye can see and then some. On the horizon, a visage of some sort of city, or a village. In the sky, an indiscernible winged silhouette, flying around, the slow, quiet swooshes of it's wings repearing regularily, still drumming in the back of the consciousness. And when Elias decided to make way to the city, the offensive began. The silhouette dived down quickly, firing a couple balls of fire at Elias, all of them missing their mark, but still hitting the earth around him with thunderous force, enough to knock him down. Nevertheless, he continued his walk, and the firing repeated itself a couple times, fountains of earth, rocks and gravel firing from the ground at Elias, bruising and cutting him, but not injuring much. Persistent, he arrvied at the city. A disorganized mass of clay cottages and houses, small or big box-like creations, stacked with one another, narrow streets making ways between them. But he knew that they wouldn't shelter him. The blasts of fire wrecked every house he entered, or tried to, large pieces of clay flying around, hitting him more often than not. Finally, after a couple eternities of such situation, his injuries now exactly replicating those he suffered in the crash, he managed to get himself to a place where he could no longer run from. Cornered at the roof of the tallest building, he expected one last blast to finish him off. But instead, the enormous draconic creature in the sky descended and stood before him on the roof and looked down on him curiously. Elias started to laugh. And then the beast ate him.
                                    He awoke in an infirmary, apparently connected to all sorts of medical apparatus and shortly after a surgery. From the information he managed to gather, he was spotted and recovered by a nearby patrol, shortly after falling unconcious. They managed to secure him just enough that he could be transported to a place where he could hopefully be taken better care of. And, miraculously, his life was indeed saved. What wasn't saved, however, was his humanity. Beacause from that moment on, Elias was a Beast.
                                    Shortly after he managed to recover, due to the fact that in the crash he managed to lose an eye(Said eye is now cyclically being regenerated by a certain Thyrsus mage, in exchange for various favours.), Elias was discharged from the military and moved to Chicago, where he got involved in the local supernatural community.

                                    How does his situation look at the current point in time?
                                    Currently Elias has managed to deeply lodge himself in position as the leader of a Hunter Cell including members of the Union and the Cheiron Group. The members of the cell think it's just a usual hunter cell... Well, as usual as a Cheiron/Union mixed cell can be, although amongst some more aware parts of the city's Hunter community, especially the Lucifuge, they have a reputation of notoriously commiting so called "Van Helsing Hate Crimes". None of them however know of the fact that the cell is a cancer cell, and it's better for it to stay this way.
                                    The truth about the cell, known in the supernatural community, is that they serve as a mercenary hit squad, directed by Elias at whatever target his client wants taken care of, in exchange for various items or services, ranging from a "dinner invitation" to various items of power. About the only group that hasn't in the passing years used their services even once is the organized Demon agencies. Elias also deals other than assassinations, including using his current job as a security advisor for a couple of companies to leave a couple holes in their systems and then selling information about them to willing buyers.

                                    The horror that places itself where Elias' soul used to be manifests itself as a sizable wyvern, of a deep red, almost black coloration, surprisingly reminescent of the armadillo grilled lizard fused with an unidentifiable carnosaurus, with a short, rather barrel-shaped body, massive muscular pillar-like legs similiar to those of modern day ostriches, a thick heavy tail, helping it keep balance , and an elongated head adorned with many curious horns and spikes, forming a crest in the back, as well as some pyramid-shaped smaller spikes more towards the front and a pair of whiskers similiar to those of a catfish. Most of it's body is covered in plate-like scales, forming more spikes along it's body, but being surprisingly soft to touch, offering barely any protection from attacks. In adition, spikes running along it's spine can open itself up to reveal a sail crest, for reasons unknown. The creature utilizes it's enormous wings in a manner similiar to pterosaurs, using them as help when walking.

                                    The Lair is divided into two chambers. The first one, that also serves as it's heart is the one described in the third paragraph. The second one is based the military base Elias resided in before coming back to the States from Iraq. A great area covered by excersise squares, landing pads, parking areas and whatnot, with a couple hangars, barrracks, garages and other buildings scattered around the place. All around, air sirens are ringing out, signalizing an aerial attack incoming. People around (If the good Storyteller accepts the idea of dream extras in the lair, if not, scratch that, the base is empty) are running frantically, trying to escape or mount some sort of defence, but the gates are locked down and the fences seem to be stretched out miles high upwards. Looking upwards, the wyvern's silhouette is painted upon the sky, roaring of it's wings bearly hearable... And when the visitor to the lair realizes that, the wyvern begins it's bombardment.


                                    Jeesus, that came out waaay too long. Does anyone here have anything against what was written down above?
                                    EDIT: Alongside the changes in the character sheet, a starting satiety breakdown:

                                    Who are your kin? Elias has, to a degree, bonded with the Cheiron Group employees in his Cell. It's not much of the traditional Beast-other supernaturals Big Brother-Little Brother relationship, but more of a "Father to His Men" kind of thing. They are his squad, and while it may not seem like much, for Elias it's kind of a big deal.
                                    Does your belly fill easily? No. Elias' feedings are to a degree associated with the hits and hunts his cell undertakes, which are oftentimes irregular, happening in larger intervals and usually taking up a lot of planning.
                                    Are you a social predator? Nope. Elias generally doesn't hold that big of an opinion on other children. The whole "Lessons" thing they have going on reminds him a lot of the Middle East.
                                    How frequently do you show your true self? Not often, really. He has to keep a low profile to not gain the KOS status amongst his men.
                                    How drawn to your Lair are you? Barely. Elias and the Wyvern are as much conflicted as it is possible for them to be, really.
