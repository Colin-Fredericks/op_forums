                                    I'm curious, anyone think up any ideas they want to see if they can work into Kinships? I've got a couple for Prometheans.

                                    •You Are A Object(Golem/Unfleshed, whichever is appropriate in 2e): Makes the target subject of a form of the lineage's Disquiet, causing others to treat them like things, and attempt to "own" them at all costs.

                                    •Do You Hear The Walls Thinning?(Ulgan): A targeted version of the Ulgan Wasteland, damaging the Gauntlet in a local area.

                                    •You Are The Outsider(General): Makes the target the subject of a general Disquiet-like effect, making everyone hate and distrust them.

                                    •You Can't Drink Lightning?(General): Protects the user from electric damage, and can even heal them at a cost.

                                    •You Can't Control Yourself(Any Lineage): Less of a Nightmare in and of itself and more of a vague template, the idea is that it forces the target to experence the Torment of a particular Lineage. For example, the one that inflicts the Torment that belonged to the Tammuz in first edition might be called "You Aren't Going To Take That, Are You?", while the Galateid version might be "Tell Me I'm Pretty!"

                                        Originally posted by ZealousChristian24 View Post
                                        I'm curious, anyone think up any ideas they want to see if they can work into Kinships?


                                        We Need a Donor (Vampire): The subject is placed under the delusion that they are severely ill and require a blood transfusion to stay healthy. Doctors may refuse to give such treatment to a "healthy" patient, driving them to further desperation.



                                        Fever Dream (Changeling): The subject is assaulted by wild, incoherent hallucinations that drives them into a disoriented state. Can possibly inflict the subject with a temporary Derangement!



                                        How did That Get Here? (Mage): The user has the ability to conceal an object or objects small enough to fit in their hands from the senses of others. Unless a thorough inspection is performed or the inspector knows what they're looking for, their minds will be tricked into ignoring the object(s) during a search. The objects can not be concealed while they are in use, however.



                                        Naked in Public (Werewolf): The victim feels as if they are wearing little or even no protection and this carries over into battle as well. Naked in Public allows their attackers to bypass the armor rating of the victim. The amount of armor that can be ignored depends upon the successes rolled. Only mundane armor (i.e clothing) is affected by this Nightmare.
