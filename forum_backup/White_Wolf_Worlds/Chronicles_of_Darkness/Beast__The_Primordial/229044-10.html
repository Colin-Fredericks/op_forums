            From the info presented in this thread, I have a thought on how the game might be considered subversive. All the other games focus on humanity with a theme related to humanity; holding on to it, acquiring it, reacquiring it, etc. The games may tend towards players losing humanity, but there is always a struggle. This game sounds more like the players might not start out human. If they do then they must be geared towards discarding humanity. There are elements to most games with characters that lose humanity but they are designed to be "bad guys." Perhaps in this case, the characters discard humanity to replace it with bestial aspects. They try to become monsters, but not the insane, evil creatures in the other games. These guys are more like sharks, large cats, wolves and all the other animals that people are irrationally afraid of. A shark doesn't bite you because it has malice. It bites you because it is hungry, and on instinct, when you came in only to start thrashing in his territory. The shark isn't evil. The victim of the shark bite might not be evil. The mob that starts killing all the local sharks so they can "feel safe" are the evil ones. The Heroes are antagonists. They aren't Hunters, but hunters. People looking for trophies, basically. A Beast could be evil, or dangerous, or simply trying to hide from the most dangerous predator in the real world, humans. Humans aren't the strongest or fiercest, but they are still the most dangerous. No other creature will purposely destroy it's own home permanently just to win a challenge. Humans aren't prey, they are the monsters. A Beast is just trying to survive in a world filled with monsters. They take the place humans have in all the other books. Sounds subversive to me. Just a thought.
