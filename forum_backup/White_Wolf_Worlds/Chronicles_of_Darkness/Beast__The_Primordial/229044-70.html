                            Some other things include:
                            According to Developer DaveB:
                            •"Unless Matt changes it from how I did the crossover rules for Prometheans, they're immune to Disquiet."

                            According to Developer Rose Bailey: •"Some things individual Beasts do, from my second draft of the splats chapter:
                            - Protect the good kids in a juvenile facility from the horrors that walk the halls at night.
                            - Steal back an inheritance piece by piece, leaving deadly things in their place.
                            - Guard the pool that grants wishes, and profit from the secrets it whispers.
                            - Watch over a shore-bound lover from afar.
                            - Keep the thing in the hospital basement at bay."

                            According to Developer Blackhat_Matt:
                            •"Heroes always seem to discover weaknesses for Beasts. Almost like the put the weakness on the Beast.

                            (Said weaknesses are called Anathema, and the rules for placing them play into the Satiety rules.)"

                            •"Neither. You can get to your Lair any place that resonates with it. If your Lair is, like my wife's character, an ocean with a black-sand beach, good luck getting there from, like, Ohio in February ("THERE IS NO WATER IN THIS AIR!"), but then "Flooding" isn't the only Lair trait she has to work with.

                            Plus, y'know, you could always find a swimming pool or something. "

                            •"Yes. Beasts have two basic power types, Nightmares and Atavisms. Nightmares are highly customizable.

                            They work just fine on people in the waking world. They work better if your Satiety is high (as opposed to Atavisms, which are usually more effective when it's low)."

                            •In regards to Heroes or other people getting into your Lair: "2) Yes, and that's dangerous. If your Lair dies, you die with it.

                            Well, most of you."
