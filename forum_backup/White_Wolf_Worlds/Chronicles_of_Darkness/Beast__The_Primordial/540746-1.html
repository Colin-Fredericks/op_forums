Noone wants to have something like intestinal worms inside them, for example... So the name Infestation, though it's an Atavism, not a Nightmare and is not actually about that but more becoming a human sized swarm of insects... I think that would make a powerful custom Nightmare... I suppose it would fall under Bugs Everywhere! but it's not about them crawling over and stinging and being visible...                         I don't think I have nightmares - at least nothing I could use for Beast.

                        Oh, I've had falling dreams (which end in dissatisfaction; that was the jolt at the bottom?), I had one dream drenched in dread (a robot was suffering an existential crisis. I wasn't in it), and there was one case where a spider crawled on my face while I was asleep and I dreamt a squirrel-thing came through my window and tried to suffocate me with its tail. All of them were one-offs.

                        One of my only persistent "sets" of dreams are the ones where I'm in love. They're usually really sappy and mundane; cuddling up on the couch, meeting up with friends, having quiet talks where I realize that yes, they do indeed love me. I had one a few nights ago where I was on a vast, ornate train and I was just starting to flirt with the girl in a nearby cabin (maybe mine? It's a bit vague) - it felt like that feeling where you're just starting to get a crush on someone, that quietly happy swelling feeling. And then I wake up, and the realization that none of that is real hits me. The loneliness cuts like a knife.

                        It depends - sometimes the realization is a gentle let-down, and sometimes I'm shaken to the core. Most of the time the girl in question looks nothing like anyone I know; heck, the people in those dreams tend not to be anyone I recognize. The one time one of those dreams included people I know involved one of my friends speaking to me on the side of the highway where his house should have been.

                        He told me love would only result in me dying alone in the gutter. I believed him for days after waking up; the words in that ditch were utterly convincing, like they were right.

                        If anyone can make a Beast character around that, I'd be happy to see them.
                        • • •

                        The only other persistent theme in my dreams seems to be vast towers and places much larger than they should be. This is my happy theme, and tends to show up everywhere from beaches to pirate ships to city-centers to streets where smiling children are selling cake that makes you evil (that was a bizarrely entertaining dream.)

                        That does sound like a Lair theme to me, actually. Hmm...

                        I have decided, after some thought, that I don't really feel happy on these forums. I might decide to come back to post. Who knows - but right now, I'm gone.

                        So good bye, good luck, and have a nice day.

                                                    The last nightmare, the last dream I can remember, from when I was a kid. It was like a B-rated adventure horror movie.

                                                    Originally I showed up in a jeep to an ancient ghost town (seemingly a mix of Pueblo style and Wild West buildings) hidden deeep inside the mouth of a gigantic cave in the shape of a Dragon's head. From here I was attacked by hordes of zombies and forced towards the back of the cavern, gore being spilled from the people who were with me. This leads to a large downward spiraling stairs made of natural stone, leading into the bowels of the earth, and lit only from below by what turns out to be sweltering pools of magma. and on the other side is an enormous portcullis made of solid wood. I still don't know why, but I suddenly thought it was a good idea to open it and as it pulls upwards, rows upon rows of demons (or what I think were demons) start to march out of the darkness within, that darkness moving outwards swirling with the light from the glowing magma. And deep within the darkness, are two glowing eyes...

                                                    I didn't really wake up there but I did start dreaming lucidly, so it wasn't really the dream anymore. Usually I don't even remember having dreams, the fact that I remember this one so well is rather amazing.

                                                    Edit: I'd say that if I were to put a Family to my nightmare it would probably be Anakim, Eshmaki, or Makara.
