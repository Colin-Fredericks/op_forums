                                                Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                                                Idly fun question: What do you think are the most likely Path(s) each Family would appear as when using Family Resemblance to pass as mages?
                                                Slightly less boring answer: Mastigos at high Satiety, Thyrsus at low Satiety.


                                                To check my latest understanding of a particular set of Lair mechanics that I've been sporadically grappling with over the years:
                                                • Hold the Door operates under the assumption that the space you've used to open a Primordial Pathway is "an entrance to your Lair," and is the only means by which visitors to your Lair may receive the protections afforded to a guest (aside from Be My Guest, which works ).
                                                • Separate from this, anyone attempting to leave the while a Primordial Pathway is open finds themselves in the Beast's Lair, moving through one of their Burrows to another Chamber (presumably regardless of what the normal route to access that Burrow is within the open Chamber).
                                                • This isn't usually a huge problem because (besides the usual exercises of common sense that mean most Beasts aren't going to have unintended visitors slipping into their souls on a frequent basis) every Beast has the capacity to sustain at least two Chambers whose collapse does not end their lives, a single free pass through the front door doesn't make any other transition less difficult, Chambers are the Horror's favored hunting grounds (and the Horror is immune to its own Lair Traits and doesn't have to struggle to move between Chambers), and it's textual that most Beasts structure their Lairs in such a way that the Heart is the most distant Chamber from any entrances.
                                                • Be My Guest grants a character the ability to open Primordial Pathways as though they were the Beast whose permission they received, rolling Wits + Resolve along with any modifiers as normal.

                                                Does that sound about right?
