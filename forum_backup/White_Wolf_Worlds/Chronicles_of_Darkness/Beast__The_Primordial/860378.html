    The Hero my players deserve

    04-06-2016, 12:43 PM
    I needed to share this with all of you. It's of vital importance. I was explaining to my wife (who is one of my players) about the narrative relationship between Beasts and Heroes (whom I wish they had called Champions). After a moment of digestion she has that sudden "Aha!" moment come across her face, and before I can continue on to the next point about the book she goes "So they're just like Gaston!" The math added up in my head far too quickly. Hunts Beasts, obsessed with himself, thinks the story is about him and convinces an entire hoard of idiots to follow him into certain death.

    Thanks to this realization, I've decided that all of my Heroes will give their epic monologues in the form of choreographed song and dance numbers.

                  Gaston's Choreography
                  Legends and tales have been told through songs for generations, but the worthy Hero can create songs in order to tell legends worthy of them.
                  A Hero can activate Gaston's Choreography by spending 1 willpower in presence of a Beast suffering from an Anathema. As long as he sings and dances about the Beast's monstrous nature and/or his own heroic ways, he benefits from his Presence as bonus dice to any actions performed. Directly attacking the Beast breaks the effect of Gaston's Choreography. For example, leading the monster in a trap would benefit from it, but as soon as the hero tries to land the killing blow the effect would dissipate.

                  Glorious Useless-Post Prana
                  Cost: 2m; Mins: Linguistics 1, Essence 1 Type: Simple
                  Keywords: Mute
                  Duration: Instant
                  Prerequisite Charms: Ten-Characters Methodology
