                        Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                        I'd wager that most people that feel Homecoming > Devouring, wouldn't feel that way if they never saw the earlier version of the game.
                        From a raw nomenclature perspective, at least, I can see an argument to be made for "Homecoming" being more appropriate to the outcome the line arrived at — I've pointed out before in a couple places that the original Kickstarter draft was a lot more oriented toward Beasts as self-contained and self-concerned monsters of Hunger than the supernatural fixtures of the darker world that Kinship ties onto — and there's no denying that "Devouring" sounds more like a thing that was done to you than a thing you happened upon in the course of living your life.

                        The mess of connotations Homecoming was tangled up with, though, has me doubtful that its proponents aren't just talking about the cathartic resonance of the concept in isolation from its larger context, because "I am what I am and I can't and won't change" becomes a lot more overtly anti-aspirational when blown up into "You are basically doomed to inhabit a cycle of violence wherein you and the people you associate with are increasingly likely to be serial killers and their accomplices."
