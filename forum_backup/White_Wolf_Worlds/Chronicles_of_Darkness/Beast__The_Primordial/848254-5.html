                    Crap, the forum ate my rather long post. Let's see if I can make it shorter this time.

                    More information on how different Beasts view feeding and teaching lessons would be nice. It wouldn't have to be anything big, but if it were to be more heavily expanded on something similar to Agendas from Demon would be welcome. On a social level it would be nice to have a section on what it's like for a Beast attempting to be a part of vampire society and what it's like to be a member of a pack of werewolves and other stuff like that. Similar to this, I'd also like information on Beasts interacting with stranger stuff like spirits and really rare monsters that aren't usually playable.

                    Expanding on Kinship and Lairs, I'd like more information on how Kinship changes a Lair. The section in the book that talked about how having Kinship with a Sin-Eater can sometimes create an Avernian Gate was really cool to me and I'd like more information on it and similar things. A merit that could represent having such a thing in your Lair would be cool, but isn't necessary if we could get enough information about it. I'd also like information on what it's like and what benefits and drawbacks can be gained by letting ghosts, spirits, and other monsters live in your Lair as guardians and friends. A merit that would allow ghosts to treat you as an anchor or something similar could be pretty cool for helping facilitate this sort of thing.

                    As far as merits go, I'd like information on how to use Retainer to represent ephemeral beings and other monsters or a merit that could help represent something like that. I would also absolutely love merits that would make your Horror stronger in some way, be it by adding more points to split between Power, Finesse, Resistance, and Size, or something else entirely. Speaking of Horrors, I'm kind of interested in how increasing its size is useful seeing as how size doesn't add to health. It kind of makes having a huge horror a liability because every point you put into size could be put into something else instead. Merits that could alter your Lair in some way would be really nice too, especially if it was something like that idea for an Avernian Gate merit I mentioned earlier.

                    I'd also like to know what other worlds like the Shadow, Hedge, and Astral are like for Beasts along with more information on the Primordial Dream, but I can understand if that's more of a Storyteller's Guide thing.

                    Regardless, I'm really looking forward to the Player's Guide and the Storyteller's Guide when that finally starts getting written.

                                                        Reading through the thread, I see several ideas I'd like to see expanded on, either here or in the Storyteller's Guide. Off-hand, I'd like to see material on:

                                                        * The Care and Feeding of Princesses - From Hansel & Gretel to high fantasy epics, many Beasts seem to have an interest in keeping a long-term "guest". After all, what self-respecting dragon doesn't have their own princess? Sure, in the modern day they might have to settle for an heiress, govt. official's daughter, or movie star, but its the principle that counts!

                                                        Exploring the phenomena could be interesting. Sure, it could be an outgrowth of the "Lessons" idea (what better way to teach a snobby rich kid to appreciate their wealth and servants, than by making them scrub the floors themselves), but are there other reasons? Are they de facto familiars? Are they a status symbol? Do Beasts in a Hive compete to have the most famous/exotic/cool "princess"?

                                                        And what happens when a mage, interested in learning more about Beasts, volunteers for the position?

                                                        * Public Awareness - Given that Beasts need humanity to react to them, in order to feed, it makes sense that they'd have some sort of "Reputation" or "Public Awareness" score. At what point do rumors start to spread? Or conspiracy forums or "news of the weird" articles start focusing on a Beast's hunts? When does my character get their own underground comic, and what does it take to get your own police task force hunting you?

                                                        * Social Impact - Similar to "Public Awareness", what effects does a Beast have on the society around them? If a Nemesis (with a Hunger for Punishment) decides to go all Batman on the local criminals, how fast do the rumors spread? At what point do they start to make a noticeable impact on the crime levels? What tactics will the local mob (and vampires) start taking to preserve their turf?

                                                        The other Hungers would presumably have their own impacts - a Beast into Ruin, and a flair of explosives, will have a very noticeable one . But what about the Collector who specializes in rare art/books/artifacts? At some point, (human) collectors (and probably mages) would start getting interested in their collection. . .

                                                        * Expanded Info on Lairs - Personally, I'd like a "Scaling" or "Giant Size" Lair trait, to help "big" monsters come across as suitably impressive. That way your Anakim could throw a prisoner in a giant bird cage, while the tiny Hero hides in a mouse hole, looking for a moment to strike.

                                                        I'm also not clear on whether Beasts can add chambers with supernatural properties. For example, can a Beast visiting Tokyo annex an "ansho" (mana draining zone)? What about the Fountain of Youth (or Well of Madness)? If a PC stumbles across the "Library of Skulls", where the severed heads of the dead must answer any question truthfully, can add they add it to their Lair? And if so, do the original properties transfer properly, or are they twisted by the Lair?

                                                        Some sample Lairs and unique chambers might be nice, too. Inspiration for new players, on how to make their own Lairs suitably freaky.

                                                        * However, one thing I'm not interested in (at least for the Player's Guide) is information on playable Heroes. Unless things have changed, the corebook was pretty clear that Beasts don't really run into the more stable ones - only the crazies that have gotten obsessed with hunting them. I'm not 100% clear on whether the average Beast even knows that Heroes come in non-"murderous wacko" flavors. At some point, a Hero spinoff supplement might make sense, but in the Player's Guide I'd prefer the focus to be on Heroes as antagonists ("101 Ways to Hero-Proof Your Lair ).


                                                        Edit: Oh, and good luck, Matt! You came up with a cool game line, and I can't wait to see what comes next.
