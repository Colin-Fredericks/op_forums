So what would happen to bring them together. It doesn't have to be a friendly relationship, but one lasting longer than a single operation. Any ideas from you guys?

                  Okay, so.

                  Why would a Beast and a Demon work together?

                  In the entire vein of Beast's crossover elements and it's many implications and questions, Demon is perhaps one of the more interesting one simply because it was the one time Beast gave a hard "NO" on the subject. As I brought up in another response, there's a lot of reasons for this, although I would mainly argue that it comes down to the fact that, on a thematic level, the God-Machine embodies everything that mankind has done to defeat Beasts in the most meaningful sense. Civilization diminishes the primordial fears, and the God-Machine has it's radio waves all up in all corners of civilization-in fact, it wouldn't be unfair to say that the fear of the God-Machine is the fear of it eliminating fear and desire, the two things that drive Beasts.

                  But if you're paying a modicum of attention, you'll notice I said God-Machine, not Demon, and while on a cosmological level the two are tied together, on a thematic level, the greatest irony is that demons and Beasts actually fill somewhat similar niches, just facing different directions, with Beast's reducing to the past and demons subverting towards the future. And that already creates a unique precedence for some settings, where in the two work together against the God-machine for their own reasons. Is that the only way? No, and a little digging brings up a few points that ensure that Beasts and Demons can avoid the catastrophe of cats and dogs, but even do it in the long term.

                  Yeah. Beasts and Demons can be friends for the long term. We're going crazy here.

                  So, what's the first way this can happen? Well...

                  1) The Beast doesn't push the search key.

                  Admittedly, this one is unlikely to happen because a big part of Beast's instincts and incentives relate to seeking out and drawing from the presence and experiences of other monsters. So, because it's a violation of a Beast's general gaming direction, we won't dwell here-but it's worth noting that the thing that usually sets Beasts and demons at each other's throats is a Beast using Family Resemblance on the demon and winning to some degree. If a Beast can avoid doing that and dropping Shaken or Leveraged on one of them, and instead they come to a knowledge of one another's nature the long way around, this could easily set the stage for an easier relationship. Long and tedious, sure, but that's how most people work.

                  2) For the Cute Murderpuppy We Call Fenris-Ur

                  The most common reason for them to start working together is because they have ties to the same person, and in fact that can lead to a long relationship that increases a likelihood of them coming together to share totally radical fistbumps. And it's not even all that unusual, particularly since they do have motives to seek out the same kind of persons. Beasts inherently follow their own pack instinct in seeking out other monsters, but a demon does have reason to seek out the other monsters as well and establish good working relationships of some kind, since other monsters don't tip the angels off as easily. That's a huge advantage for any demon, and it's one they would totally try and take advantage of. This kind of seeking often leads to the same places, and it also quickly reveals the value of the Beast to the demon, because a demon will doubtless see how the Beast makes everyone else more effective. Meanwhile, a Best is likely to hang out with and work with a demon for no other reason than their family members do so as well. Naturally, this involves a lot of time working things out, but sheer repetition and reliability means that their propinquity is likely to increase one way or the other.

                  3) Rage Against the Teachers, The Parents, and, oh yeah, The Machine

                  Neither Beasts nor demons have an instinctive liking for systems in the world. Demons might be able to take advantage of the systems for the purposes of hiding, but almost all of them recognize it as the imposition, if not subjugation, of malefic forces from on high. Beasts hearken back to the primal days without all this order, nevermind how the current system favors Heroes trying to one-up Beasts in brutal and violent ways. Really, it wouldn't be surprising to find Beasts and demons protesting the same Trump rally, or participating in the same riot. Their reasons are different, but things like anarchy, freedom of expression, the chasing of taboos-all this counter-cultural stuff is bound to attract them, and where there's common complaints, there's common cause which leads to allegiances, and allegiances can lead to camaraderie.

                  4) Across this Beautiful World

                  Beasts and Demons are unique in that they both have the greatest ability to travel between all the worlds. Now, granted, Demons only have that in the form of a God-machine alerting Exploit, so it's not intrinsic to them, but they are the only one to have a tool that lets them go to any other plane in existence-and as world walkers go, the company is a little lonely, before even talking about all the problems where it pays to have someone at your back without having the same tools as you. On this elite but broad road through worlds, demons and Beasts are likely to be one another's only consistent companions, and while hanging with your own is fine, in the end, the road has it's own demands, and travellers never know who they'll end up with. What were once begrudging souls caught in the same ravine can easily turn into a caravan of friendship, dreams, and bloody, bloody murder.

                  And that's just a few things. The key thing to remember is that the deck is stacked against them, but it's not prohibiting. If monster's personalities click, well, just like with people, they'll find reasons to work with one another anyways. And in the end, they might just end up being the best damned partners they ever had, going back in time to save each other because, dammit, they love each other.

                  Yeah, no, that was pretty damn weak.

                              The long answer would take an essay I don't much care to write at this point, so the long and short of it is that both thematically and by the implied cosmology of Demon, all things God-Machine and such basically are not a part of the world that Beast's can interact with. These decisions help to cement the feel and themes of each one respectively. One way or another, angels and demons are sacred, profane, and always apart.

                                  Also, the Tempters would definitely see the potential in hiring Beasts as diplomatic allies when interacting with other supernaturals. Besides Thicker Than Water, Family Resemblance and Mother's Kiss, the Tempters could make use all the Social Merits that the more outgoing Beasts accumulate in exchange for lending/selling their own Social Merits gained through Pacts and such. If the Tempter in question is lucky, he can even hire a Beast with Find A Vein.

                                  Like Arc said, the Beast can use Skeleton Key and the Demon can use Rip The Gates to explore the four main Invisible Realms together, and likely more.

                                  Tempters would do this for the sake of finding new business partners and resources to turn into products. The big attraction here are the Goblin Markets, but there's a lot more. For example: hiring spirits as spies/hidden security and convincing Kerberoi to guard your Pact documents. If the Demon has Show Of Power, all the different supernatural powers he has seen firsthand can be turned into a Gadget through that Exploit, essentially letting him sell bootleg Tokens and such. The Beast partner can share in the profits, or just enjoy the exploration and Feeding for their own sake.

                                  Inquisitors would do this for the sake of gathering intel and keeping an eye on possible threats before they can cross into the baseline realm.

                                  Saboteurs could have more pragmatic partnerships along the lines of "I take care of this Hero, you help me bring down this Infrastructure". As the Saboteur and Beast prove their skills and reliability to each other, they can become fellow warriors of one type or another. Plus, the right Lairs make for incredible training rooms, obstacle courses, firing ranges and so on.

                                  At the moment, I don't know how Integrators could benefit from having a Beast around. Sorry.
