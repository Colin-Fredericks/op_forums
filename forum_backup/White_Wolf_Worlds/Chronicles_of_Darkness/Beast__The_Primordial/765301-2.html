    Originally posted by Hexmeister Vic View Post
    could you explain to me how accepting and guiding others through their impulses is less healthy and affirming than telling them to stop.
    That is certainly one way you can approach it. Maybe it's even an admirable way for a moral, enlightened character to approach it. But that isn't the default. All the actual rules say are that if you as a Beast accompany a supernatural being as it hunts, feeds, or both, you can gain Satiety by watching them. Could be a vampire stalking his prey in a ballroom, could be a werewolf pursuing an intruder in her territory, whatever, so long as the supernatural being in question is actively tracking prey and/or taking sustenance from a hunt. The supernatural being doesn't have to know you're there, but the hunt must be real a real act of predation (it can't be a vampire feeding on their own minion who is willing to be "hunted", for example), and you must be physically present while observing it. The fact that it genuinely has to be an act of predation is going to make it much harder (but not impossible) for an enlightened and moral Beast to benefit from it, because in doing so they are subverting what the mechanics of the ability actually suggest, which is something altogether darker. And while a Beast may not have to be an enabler of the darker impulses of the various supernatural beings of the World of Darkness, being such an enabler is probably the easiest way to benefit from this ability.
