    Tactics matter, because no one is going to go in and just blindly swing a punch, and because of this, you'll excuse me if I don't quite buy that only an Eshmaki or Ugallu can challenge them.

    I mean, mind you, your PC's are powerful enough you should consider ending the campaign and going onto something else, but if we must challenge them, then the assumptions are wrong.

    1st problem! I saw no mention about what else a Beast is bringing to the game, in terms of allies. Considering that Beasts are crossover characters and that one of the two universal toolkits that every Beast is basically grabbing allies in the other corners of the world and empowering that, that seems like a phenomenally stupid thing to do. What good is Obfuscate and Celerity against spirits of stealth who can simply see you by virtue of trying to hide? How useful is all that armor and defense from Protean, Resilience, and Celerity if a Fae or Strix can just declare you're vulnerable and you can't do shit about it? In an entire franchise where no one stands alone, a Beast absolutely does not stand alone.

    2nd problem! So your players have impressive dice pools and applied modifiers.

    Why are you trying to power through them then? Don't set them on fire, set the building on fire and drop lair traits that trap them on the place. They can stand against a truck, but can they stop an entire ocean? THey can't, so get creative and sweep them to the bottom of the ocean-it's not that hard for a Beast create that sort of flow. There are plenty of options in the Chronicles world that just happen, and a Beast is one of the best character types to go, hunt down these options, and bring them to bear. Lair allows them to dictate the flow and direction of the environment in a way that makes werewolves drool and make even elder vampires sweat.

    Like, with just the basic power options of a Beast and some creative thinking, you can easily panic any of your players. Nevermind some of the really creative uses of Birthrights, Atavisms, and Nightmares.

    If you really wanna take someone "underpowered" and challenge someone absurdly powerful, stop and sincerely think about how you'd go about it as a player character. Good players are creative and can overcome any challenge with a good enough plan. On top of that, players with characters that big (like yours) tend to overtly rely on just their powers, so it's not that hard to start spiralling around them.

    Be smart, stack the deck with your resources, hit them hard and without mercy. The only additional principle you need as a GM is to remember to give them a chance.

    And, seriously, if you can't imagine a challenge for your PC's, it's time to end the game. Sometimes, you just have to accept you've arrived.

                                                    Paradim A glass, perhaps, but not all glass in the area, which would not only swing at different frequencies, but also be vastly different in how much energy it takes to break them: just hitting the frequency is by no means enough. You need something like 105 Dezibel just to break a wine glass. Simple tumbler would be vastly more sturdy. A window far beyond that. If car glass breaks, that is approximately 163 db. 140 db is the point of definite long-term damage for all frequencies, and the threshold of pain, and causing your throat and vocal chords to resonate. At 145 db, vision is affected, at 150 joints are affected and swallowing become hard, 165 is similar to the internal pressure of a jet turbine, at around 170 db it is about 1 pound of force per square inch. 180 db finally would equal the force of 1 pound of TNT exploding at 15 ft distance. 190 is where eardrums rupture, 200 db is enough to cause death from the sonic shockwave alone.

                                                    With such a voice, you could definetly do more than inflict a simple tilt. You could cause mini-explosions.

                                                    Which is why I think we're not meant to take this so physically acurate.

                                                    If you do, you should probably assume 165 db, which is enough to shatter most glass, and the other effects (percieved cooling of the air, balloons exploding, flashlights dimming and brightening according to frequency, and being about as loud as a Boeing 747 at take off in general) aren't overwhelming.
                                                    Also, keep in mind that the dB is an exponential scale, adding 10 to the value about equals doubling the sound's force.

                                                            Compare.

                                                            Social Vampire
                                                            Disciplines: Dominate 3 or Majesty 3
                                                            Merits: Honestly doesn't matter. Allies, Contacts, Resources, Status to taste.

                                                            Compare:
                                                            Dominate Vamp can get someone to perform simple tasks with a aingle roll and alter memories within a scene. With more effort, they can implant commands.

                                                            Majesty makes people want to do the Kindred Favors and allows the Kindred to swing their emotions all over the place.

                                                            Beasts can get a target to perform any task, even long term ones in a single roll *and* get them to be semi-complicent. At low Saity they give the Charned Condition in addition to their normal effects. You Must Obey is the stopgap for the immediate control, although it has more holes than Dominate. Tabula Rasa can match or exceed Dominate's memory wiping powers, bit not altering.

                                                            Overall, the Beast seems like a very inbetween the two Kindred Builds. It isn't going to be as reliable as either of the two Kindred Builds at their thing, but it can do a bit of everything. Kindred are going to have more merits to throw at support structures. A Beast *can* match it, but in that case they want to really focus on improving their dicepool so they can reliably Force Doors.

                                                            Overall, I would be happy with any of these Social Characters on my team. Remember that a Beast, in addition to performing these duties, also
                                                            - Can open portals to places
                                                            - Autodetect the supernatural
                                                            - Empower other splat's abilities
                                                            - Drop their Lair on people which, as discussed, lets them Win Combat agaonst most things.

                                                            Investigation / Tracking Vampire
                                                            Disciplines: Auspex 3
                                                            Merits: Acute Senses 1, Bloodhound 2, Cacaphony Savvy 3, Distinguished Pallet 1, Dream Visions 1, ??? 2

                                                            Comparison:
                                                            Kindred can learn a lot about their surroundings and people's weaknesses using Auspex. Auspex 3 scans a location. All useful. They gain some serious bonuses tracking and identifying things by blood. Cacaphony Savvy keeps the Kindred in the know about the city's going ons. If you can swing Where the Bodies Are Buried you can get even more information. Dream Visions optional.

                                                            Compare Beast. Beast can retroactively study anything they have ever witnessed and keep track of the exact location of anything they have ever witnessed. In terms of Investigation, Mimir Wisdom's special ability to gain Mental Skills means the Beast has whatever skills they need (at 4+, matters for the Investigation subgame) and access to Advanced Library. They also get Postcognition, meaning they can just solve mysteries essentially at will.

                                                            I don'r think there is a comparison here. Beast makes Mekhet look bad.

                                                            Infiltration Vampire
                                                            Disciplines: Obfuscate 3
                                                            Merits: Barfly, Close Quarters Fighting 4
                                                            Note: Obfuscate 1 is an infiltration add on to anyone.

                                                            Comparison:
                                                            The Vampire can, for free, go unnoticed. They can hide other objects or even other people. If they spend blood, they can go completely invisible, requiring an extended action to see through. Best of all? Breaking Invisibility for one person doesn't break it for other people!

                                                            The Beast gets significant bonuses to amd extra excuses to use Stealth. They can become completely invisible by spending a Saity, and the invisibility only drops dor a turn when they attack. They alsp gain the ability to perfectly go incorporeal in shadows, see through shadows, and shadow teleport. You Are Not Alone, possibly with Fist of Nightmares and/or Fear is Contagious is the stealth sledgehammer, but you need to target individuals.

                                                            Honestly? Advantage Vampires, here. Obfuscate just offers such an overwhelming stealth kit, and the innate ability to attack while remaining stealthed to everyone else and the power to stealth other people is just hard to compete with. Beast is slightly better at getting in to places.

                                                            Combat Vampire
                                                            Disciplines: Protean 3 -or- Celerity 1 + Vigor 2 or Fortitude 2
                                                            Merits: Swarm Form 2 (if Protean), Combat Merits to taste (Grappling Style!)

                                                            Comparison
                                                            ... yeah. Beast wins. This isn't a meaningful contest. Lair Traits by itself wrecks anything a vampire can bring to the table. Swarm Form is almost as survivable as Unbreakable Beasts. Almost.
