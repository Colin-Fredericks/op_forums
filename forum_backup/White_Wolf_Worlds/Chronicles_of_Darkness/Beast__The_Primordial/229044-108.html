                                                            Beasts apparently have to deal with a little thing called Panic.
                                                            Which starts with a P.
                                                            Which rhymes with D.
                                                            And D stands for Disquiet (or Lunacy's twin, Delusion).

                                                            I imagine that a prior to a Hero arising, there will be moments where mortal NPCs will get an inkling that something's not right. Probably at mid to high Satiety, this Panic will be mostly individual, and most of the time, the victim will forget, rationalize, or go a bit crazy. Rarely, they'll fully and clearly remember what they saw, start putting 2 and 2 together, and eventually, although not satisfying the Hunger exacerbates the process, that NPC goes Hero and starts stalking the Beast. There may even be a vector and compounding mechanic, but I can only speculate.
