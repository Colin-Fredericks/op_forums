    Over at the Beast spoiler thread on RPG.net, BHM revealed that Lairs can lead to otherworldly places. So, here's my prediction for how Beast deals with turning into a giant monster (because let's face it, being able to turn into a giant monster would be awesome): In your Lair, have fun. If you evoke the fear of being dragged underwater and drowned, in your Lair you can actually be a kraken in the depths, with your Lair providing a suitable environment for that. If a Hero (or anyone else) chooses to fight you in your Lair, they have to fight an actual dragon, or kraken, or whatever. Presumably, Heroes are drawn to your Lair to confront you and have powers of their own that let them put up a credible fight. Also, apparently you can rampage around in people's collective dreams as a true monster, although what good this does you is dubious given that this apparently is how Heroes are made.

    Outside of your Lair you can call on powers that are reminiscent of your monstrous form, but you can't actually become a dragon in the middle of Times Square.

    Just my prediction. We'll see what happens.

                So, in relation to Beasts shapechanging, Matt has (cryptically) stated that they've done splats with alternate forms a lot (see, e.g., werewolves), and that they're doing something "a little different" with Beasts. No idea what that means, but it doesn't seem to bode well for my previous theory.

                However, Matt also revealed that Beasts aren't prevented from remaining in their human lives, although they still have to sate their Hunger. This brings me back to the idea I had before that Frances Dolarhyde could be a source of inspiration for a Beast. He's undeniably a person struggling with something monstrous inside him, the Great Red Dragon that has a Hunger for love and change that must be sated, and Dolarhyde (at least in Manhunter) arguably demonstrates some measure of low-level powers. It's not a perfect 1:1 matchup with the little we know about Beasts (i.e., we never see a Great Red Dragon rampaging through the dreams of people in St. Louis), but I just get that vibe off of what Matt is saying.
