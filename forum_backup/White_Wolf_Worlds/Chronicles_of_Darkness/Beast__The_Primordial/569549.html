              This power is written in a confusing manner now that you point it out. Here's how it reads to me, after looking it back and forth for a moment.

              Automatic success:
              A monster who is not under concealment and who is fully kin (vampires, werewolves, other beasts) is detected by Family Resemblance without making a roll.

              Roll without clash:
              A human who is partially but not fully kin (mages, psychics), or a demon (the one type of monster that is not kin), can be detected by a successful activation roll for Family Resemblance. This is the Wits + Occult + Lair - (target's Composure) roll given (also - target's Integrity trait if a partially kin human has not used any powers in the same scene).

              In the case of the demon, even if the beast succeeds on this roll, the demon may respond by rolling Cover to "spoof" Family Resemblance. If, and only if, the Family Resemblance roll succeeds and the Spoof roll fails, a Clash of Wills of raw Lair versus Primum ensues as per the Rage and the Machines sidebar, but this Clash has nothing to do with whether the demon is detected, only the hostile Conditions that result.

              Clash alone:
              A monster who is fully kin, but under concealment, is successfully targeted by Family Resemblance without the need for the above activation roll, but a Clash of Wills ensues which the beast must win to detect the monster. If the beast loses the Clash of Wills, nothing happens.

              1e rules for Changeling did not have the concept of the Clash of Wills, and would probably have had the Family Resemblance activation roll (Wits + Occult + Lair) penalized by 3 dice and contested by the changeling's Resolve + Wyrd, as per Rites of Spring. It seems a natural move to fold this function of the Mask into 2e's Clash of Wills rules until Lost 2e comes out with official rules for this case, as they already resemble the structure of the Clash anyway. If you were to do it this 2e way, then the Clash of Wills would be the beast's Wits + Lair versus the changeling's Resolve + Wyrd. Up to you whether the -3 penalty would still apply or not. I'd lean towards no — the -3 would apply to activation rolls to detect the changeling, but Family Resemblance's activation roll automatically succeeds in this case, jumping straight to the Clash.

              Roll with clash:
              A human who is partial kin and under concealment prompts a Family Resemblance activation roll. Success for the beast still proceeds to a Clash of Wills, with the beast's Wits + Lair pitted against the human's Appropriate Trait + Supernatural Tolerance. The Appropriate Trait in this case would be the most relevant trait for the concealment power, resorting to the closest fitting Attribute if no clear power trait is in use. For example, a mage under an occluding Mind spell might add her dots in the Mind Arcanum, while a psychic using a Supernatural Merit that uses Composure in its activation pool would probably just roll Composure, as he would not typically have a Supernatural Tolerance trait like the mage would.

              Demons under active concealment also fall under this category, for the following confusing sequence as I understand it: Roll Family Resemblance. On a success, roll Clash of Wills, Wits + Lair versus the demon's appropriate Attribute (probably Manipulation or Wits) + Primum. If the beast wins, the demon's presence is detected, but he gets to roll Spoof to block detecting his nature. If the spoof fails, then proceed to a second Clash of Wills (Lair versus Primum) to determine the resulting Condition from the beast identifying the demon as such.

              At this point in action resolution, I pass out from traumatic Exalted adjudication flashbacks, and drift into darkness.

                  As an aside, the Beast book's section on Clash of Wills (hidden curiously in the Storytelling chapter for some reason) suggests that Beasts simply add their highest Resistance Attribute to a Clash of Wills. If you favor that assertion, then just replace the Wits in (Wits + Lair) above with the respective Resistance Attribute. I chose Wits according to Demon's Clash of Wills guidelines, which suggest, in the absence of a power trait like Disciplines or Contracts, whatever Attribute is relevant to the power in question. Family Resemblance's activation roll uses Wits, so I plugged Wits in. It ultimately doesn't matter much which one you go with, so long as it's an Attribute + Lair and it makes sense to your group.
