            Originally posted by Aspel View Post
            Satchel The entire point of teaching a Lesson is that you're ostensibly doing it for the victim's benefit.
            The point of "teaching a lesson" as a disconnected phrase divorced from context is, yes.

            Unfortunately, like many things in the Chronicles of Darkness, being divorced from context renders the concept useless for understanding how it fits into the game.

            The "teaching a lesson" bit is an attempted modification to an immutable facet of Begotten existence such that it more fully reads "using feeding your Hunger — which you have to do at some point because the alternative is dying — as an opportunity to impress upon people basic lessons in the same way that a child who puts their hand on a hot stove quickly learns not to put their hand on a hot stove."

            You're teaching them something.
            As an alternative to murdering them and leaving their body in a place where it will never be found.

            I mean, nevermind that the argument is sort of silly. The Horror goes on a rampage if you have low Satiety, but it's unlikely to actually do harm unless you're a big bad powerful beastie with lots of Power and Lair. Especially when you get to Ravenous, where it's basically impossible to harm anyone, since it's a Chance die no matter how strong your Lair is.
            The Horror is a recurring nightmare by inclination and getting to Ravenous is better known as the point where your lack of activity has led to a direct threat to your life and a recurring signal beacon for several more.

            Preemptive damage control isn't self deception. Arguing that you're doing something to benefit someone when it benefits you and harms them is.
            "Trying to lower the net harm done to a person by imparting experience that will ideally make them safer in the long run" is not "doing something to benefit someone" and nobody has argued that it was in anything more than the broadest abstract of "some Beasts believe the purpose given to them by the Dark Mother is to convey the wisdom of the Primordial Dream to people who would otherwise only learn it when it was too late."

                                                Originally posted by Aspel View Post

                                                That's why it's dissonant. That's the only story you can tell. As written, a Beast is not able to feed "safely". It's not like Vampire where the parasitism metaphor can be stretched to the point that you do very little harm and feed safely from your willing blood dolls. You have to do harm. And since you need to get Potential Satiety over your current Satiety, that often means doing a lot of harm. That you do harm and justify it with "this is for your own good" is why a lot of people see Beast as an abuse metaphor. And it would be nice if you could move beyond that. Consider for a moment "shock":
                                                You don't have to cause harm. A tyrant can literally feed by just being visibly and clearly better than other people in their job. Or are you arguing that it's harmful to be competently at your job? A collector can own a pawn shop or actually be a collector and buy stuff from people. A ravager can be a white hacker and destroy someone's sense of security by demonstrating how easily their security can be bypassed.
                                                Originally posted by Aspel View Post
                                                Now, let's put aside how reasonable a conclusion it is whether or not "lock your doors" constitutes a lesson, as if all Beasts were members of DeadRed Cell, testing out security weaknesses by staging fake terrorist attacks. Shock is a moment of profound realization. Why does this have to come at the result of harm? Why does only negative emotion strengthen the Primordial Dream? Yes, yes, it's the Darkbad Shadowlands of the Temenos, but that's part of the problem. It's kind of arbitrary. Why shouldn't a Beast be able to work with their Horror when they change their outlook and work towards actually doing good in the world? If the Beast can potentially learn positive, helpful traits (even if they really only benefit those they have Kinship with), why can't they alter their Beast to not feed from misery? Maybe an example would be in order.

                                                You're a Beast. You buy into the notion of teaching Lessons. You could try to teach a lesson against assault by assaulting someone. It's big, bad, harmful, gorges the Beast, and says "see how you like when it's done to you". That's more or less what the book encourages you to do. But let's be honest, in the real world if people having bad things done to them ever actually taught them a lesson, we wouldn't live in such a crappy world that isn't a WorldChronicle of Darkness. More often than not, the lesson people get taught is "no one cares about you" or even "this is normal, so it will be okay when you do it". There's a reason abused kids grow up to be abusers. It's because their parents taught through example that abuse is an acceptable response.
                                                But, what if instead of beating someone up or raping them or stealing their shit or abusing your authority over them, you instead make them empathize? What if instead of tormenting them you make them feel for someone else, instead of for themselves. What if you do something like this ad to cause shock? What about making someone realize the gays are just like them? The book would have you teach a bully a Lesson the old fashioned way, bullying them right back and making them to afraid to retaliate, but why can't you feed from the shock of showing them that the people they torment are just like them? I mean, I know the reason; "teach a Lesson" was thrown in at the last minute to appease people because Beast's first draft was received negatively. It's not a bad idea, but by necessity as a last minute addition, it's pretty much tacked on.

                                                Hopefully in a Player's Guide or STG, we will see ways to feed that don't have to directly involve harming people. Even if it was harder to feed this way, it would at least be something working towards that theme of teaching a lesson.
                                                I think I've figured out the issue here. Let me clarify this one more time. Your Horror doesn't give a crap what you do so long as it gets fed. You want to use that towards a greater good, fine it's with you every step of the way as your faithful hunting dog. Maybe you have to rein it in because it wants to do more than you do, but so long as it gets some jerky strips at the end of it the Horror is cool with what you want to do. No the book isn't saying "your lesson has to be done as horribly as possible." You want to stop a bully fine just go in their and no sell everything they try to do. Then you make it clear that you could destroy them, but you don't think that would be right. You give them a warning that they may want to reconsider doing this again lest they come against someone less merciful. Maybe they learn the lesson you were aiming for and lay off the bullying, maybe they miss the point and the conclusion they come to is not to screw with you again. The lessons a Beast tries to teach are decided by the Beast and are fully capable of changing to match the character's desires and situation. The lesson does not have to be some epic life changing statement. It is not Epic Motivation.

                                                As for non-harmful methods of feeding. It's called family dinner pretty sure it's been mentioned before. Seriously we have a thread where a guy mentions feeding off a changeling getting glamour by pranking the Beast.
