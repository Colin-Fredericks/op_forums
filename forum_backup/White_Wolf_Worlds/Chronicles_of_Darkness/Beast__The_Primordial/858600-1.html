                I went through a few lists of fears in search of something new. It's harder than I expected.                                 Originally posted by espritdecalmar View Post
                                I think there may be space for a Hunger for Pleasure or Excess, wanton hedonist Beasts who inspire fear through reckless consumption. Think Frank Booth from Blue Velvet, or the Macellarius Bloodline from Vampire: The Requiem.

                                The Hunger for Pleasure is one that I would really like to see. If it doesn't make it into the Player's Guide, I might just have to homebrew it. While I can see some overlap with some of the others that GibberingEloquence mentioned, I can also see some overlap between the existing Hungers, as well. The key difference that I'd see is that seeking excess might not be directly harmful by default, nor would it necessarily mean taking something from someone else. Although I suppose that, to be fair, I've never really seen how taking a new lover for one's Hoard necessarily constitutes a loss on the side of said lover.

                                I would like to see a Hunger for Secrets as well. I see this as different from the Hoard in that a secret doesn't need to be taken from someone else necessarily. I'd imagine that the power of a secret, and thus how satisfying it would be to the Horror, would depend both on the obscurity of the knowledge gained and its potential effect should it be revealed. This would mean that learning that a gardener has created a new bloom for the next flower show would be a pretty thin meal even though few people would know about it, but learning that the local small town pastor has"exclusive" relationships with several different members of his congregation on the sly could be potentially quite rewarding, even without the threat of revealing it.

                                I could see the Hunger for Change as well that others have mentioned working well.

                                                So, a couple things that I've kicked around my brainpan recently:

                                                Nightmares of the Other – These would include notions from spirit possession (think The Exorcist) to extreme parasitism (it was the thought of a sentient 'vanishing twin' level of parasite that inspired this, actually). Examples include terrors such as the 'cinematic version' of schizophrenia/ split personality (not the REAL condition, though), a malevolent malignancy growing inside like a thinking and hungry cancer, something akin to gaining (or BECOMING) the parasitic male angler fish, and even the idea of existing only as a puppet dancing on another's strings. I can see it having a really nice synergy with the characters from Changeling: the Lost as it includes a loss of agency, but it more has a vibe – to me, at least – of that feeling of being or having a stranger in one's own skin, offering interesting resonance to Promethean: the Created and also with the Unchained of Demon: the Descent, though the latter are far removed from the Primordial Dream... or are they? Note that, yeah, these nightmares COULD be covered by a Namtaru to some degree, but I believe that this is distinct enough to make it a family of its own. That's beyond the fact that these Horrors give me the willies like few other things can!

                                                Nightmares of Loss – These are the fears of atrophy, of the powerlessness that comes from forgetting or being forgotten, the terror we feel at the notion of part of what's always been 'myself' is gone forever. Different from the Makara who take ALL of you, this family chooses bite-sized portions that are vital to one's self-image. That loss of voice when needed, the certainty that your job has been eliminated in the new merger, even your dread of your daughter's wedding because you'll never be her daddy again, all of these are Horrors waiting in the Primordial Dream...

                                                Edit: Both of the above are expressions of the fear of extinction, which while it covers death, also includes those notions of what it is that makes each of us an individual. Extinction as expressed by the Makara is, frankly, the most extreme of this form of fear. The 'nightmares' above COULD be expressed by a Makara Horror, but I like the idea of a more granular approach. Alternately, you might wanna look into the introduction of a 'Z axis' to the game (a la changeling kiths, vampire bloodlines, etc.)? I dunno... I just love this game so much that I can't get it out of my head. Fear is actually THAT important to me, it turns out. Yeah, and THAT is why I'm playing these games.
