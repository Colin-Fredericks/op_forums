                                        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                        The subtitle that in practice means "give people hope
                                        What is hope if not the possibility that tomorrow is better than today?

                                        What is a better tomorrow if not one that is one step further from mankind's savage and primordial past?


                                        The Courts of Hearts and Diamonds would agree with the implied answer to that rhetorical question. Spades would neither agree nor disagree since they don't think in these sorts of terms. Swords would deny that there is such thing as an "inner ape", they think there is an inner angel instead. Clubs would disagree with the idea of abandoning the "inner ape" and instead they'd wish to befriend it. (Though friends don't trigger the flight or fight response).

                                        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                        They need to ensure somebody misses a step or loses their phone or gashes their hand. Dropping into the primeval flight-reflex for a moment is not the pervasive awfulness of a Taint.
                                        Aside from the fact that I cannot see a charachter who's actions are on the level of Bart Simpson as a viable monster in a horror story. There's three different Invocations that would be strongly opposed to a beast "just making you stumble".

                                        Terra believes in society and social contracts. And there are a lot of highly regarded social contracts regarding education and therapy. You might call it "just a gash" but corporal punishment has been considered unacceptable behaviour for teachers for years. To a follower of Terra it's not "just a gash", it's defiling something sacred. (Of course, nothing actually stops a Beast getting qualifications and rigorously following all the professional ethics guidelines but I imagine it's fairly rare.)


                                        Acqua isn't entirely focused around education, but it does hold education to be one of the fundamental forces of good and claims all Princesses have a duty to educate themselves and others. Princesses who believe in Acqua who see a Beast's lessons would consider the quality of those lessons to be of paramount impotence. Imagine someone with the equivalent of PhDs in educational theory, psychology and developmental psychology (if an Acqua Princess doesn't have superhuman intelligence she'll know people who does) reviewing a Beast's intended lesson and their method to teach it. If a Beast insists on tasty lessons over good lessons, or worse, is teaching lessons the Princess considers wrong that's a huge deal to believers in Acqua. And most practitioners of Acqua would consider teaching people to use the fight or flight reflex (as opposed to calm rational thought) to be wrong.


                                        To Fuoco if something hurts someone, it is wrong. Even if it's just a gash in the hand, the wilful attempt to harm someone goes against everything Fuoco believes in. Even if it's not serious enough for the Princess to think she needs to intervene it will still affect her view of the Beast. Believers in Fuoco would also be extreemly suspicious of anyone claiming they don't have a choice other than to harm others and assume it's either immorality or ignorance rather than a genuine lack of options. (and thanks to family dinner Beasts have plenty of options).

                                        Basically your interpretation of the subtitle is a vast oversimplification. Saying Princesses give people hope and clean up festering pits of despair is like saying vampires drink blood. Yes they do. But nobody plays Vampire as nothing but back to back feeding scenes.

                                        You cannot talk about Princess without pointing out that their morality trait is Belief. Every psychologically healthy Princess has deeply held moral convictions and principals. If you want to examine Princesses views on the Begotten, you have to look at those Philosophies and what they say about Beasts and Beasts' own philosophical convictions.

                                        You can't just point out that the Darkness is worse and leave it at that. It doesn't need to be as bad as the Darkness for Princesses to care. There is an entire Z-Splat of Princesses who specialise in white collar crime.


                                        And obviously Princesses aren't going to attack anyone they have a philosophical disagreement with on sight. Left and right wing princesses can be friends and two different followers of Acqua can teach contradictory lessons about the unsettled questions of the day without loosing respect for each other so long as they're honest about what's known and what's speculative.

                                        What I am objecting to is the idea that Princesses would write of "just as gash" as no big deal.


                                        (The Invocations are far from the whole story about Princess' beliefs, but when generalising about the entire splat - they're close enough.)

                                            For the record I'm somewhere in the middle but closer to the latter. Stealing your phone just isn't a viable source of horror IMO. (I have on occasion misplaced my phone. It was shocking, it was not horrific and did not teach me any lessons).
                                            The thing to me is that it's not just stealing your phone. There's a story in Page 62 of the core rulebook where the Beast starts by trying to steal someone's watch and ends by killing them. To me part of the horror isn't just that Horror drives the Beast to do these petty acts (although there is some horror in that the Beast is driven to do these things to sate their Horror) but that you can't tell a Beast with Low Satiety, "Help me against the bad guy and feed your Horror" if you're not ready for them to possibly go from zero to murder in six seconds.

                                            I've been interested for a while how Princesses and Beasts have these weird connections to each other (Connections to dreams, connections to human emotions, the dual identity aspects, claims to seek to instill virtues in humanity) etc. But I personally think the two are almost incompatible with each other. Short-term, their first interaction would most likely be the Princess finding the Beast in the middle of feeding, which would very likely lead to conflict. Long-term, I'm having trouble seeing many relationships besides "Beast sees Princess as Kin in principle but in practice is resentful at the Princess's attempts to restrain them."

                                            I know I speak from reading and not experience, but I'm realizing that playing the Beast around other supernaturals can very easily become an analogy to playing the Shadow from Wraith: the Oblivion, and from what I read, that was very hard to play well.

                                            Please correct me if I am wrong on any of this.
