    Slenderman for a horror?

    09-10-2015, 01:45 AM
    In Beast the horrors are often described as, well, beasts. There's dragons, kraken, giant birds of prey and the like, but what about a more modern take on it? Like the title suggests, I'm talking about stuff like Slenderman, but where would you draw the line? Would you let someone's horror be Golden Freddy from FNAF? A Dalek from Doctor Who? A radioactive specter born from dropping the atomic bombs that ended WWII?

    What would you say you're favorite "modern" horror is?

    Personally, I've always liked the idea of Spring Heeled Jack 'cuz I' my weird like that.

              You know how in the game where you have to collect the 8 pages while Slendy stalks you, one of the pages says "always watches, no eyes"? And how Slendy is apparently capable of being anywhere without you noticing he's watching you? That may suggest Ugallu, but a big part of what makes him scary is how little we know about him and how he could be right behind you. Besides, it isn't like people are scared of Slendy because he knows how to expose your secrets or what have you. I'd say he is an Eshmaki. Several theories exist that try to explain why he kidnaps people, often children. One of them says he is a faerie who punishes bad children by scaring them half to death, and only allows them to go back home if they admit what they did. That would suggest Hunger For Punishment. However, I find it more likely that his habit of stalking, tormenting, kidnapping and supposedly killing people is caused by Hunger For Prey. He might have Kinship with True Fae, allowing him to develop Nightmares for locating and stalking people more effectively, in an Ugallu-like way. His Lair is probably something like this. I already have some stats for him written up, I'll add the Beast traits later and post them in this thread.

              I like to think that, if Slender Man is not a Beast's Soul/Horror, he is a True Fae or a very mysterious Unchained Demon.

                      When doing presentation about Beast game for mine RPG club, I added more modern takes on Horrors to every Family, beside mythical monsters. So here is mine short list:
                      • Anakim - Godzilla - Giant atomic created lizard you cannot run from and can stomp all your life with one blow.
                      • Eshmaki - Slenderman and Monster Under a Bad - Things you know that ARE THERE, SOMEWHERE <Ominous Music>.
                      • Makara - Cthulhu - You cannot go better with alien tentacle horror from the depths of ocean.
                      • Namtaru - Freddy Kruger - The demonic "ghost" of serial killer, so foul, his face is causing disgust in everyone.
                      • Ugallu - Internet Horror - Web spidery thing that knows EVERYTHING about you, and can use it all the time your dirtiest secrets.
