    Inheritance Hacks (or getting the Suicide out)

    03-17-2016, 11:22 AM
    If you don't want to get sidetracked you can leave the editorializing out. Please don't attribute thoughts or motives to others. Post edited by moderator.

    Long story short, since there's nothing in the Kickstarter description that makes suicide seem like it's a necessary feature of the game I've come up with some hacks for suicide free (or at least suicide not baked in) alternatives to Inheritances. Without further ado:

    Death of a Beast
    A Beast’s Horror is a scion of the Mother of All Darkness. As such Beasts are not easily killed. And even when they die, some portion of their essence continues on.
    A Beast cannot really die unless the Heart of its Lair is destroyed while its merged with its Horror.
    When a Beast dies in the mortal world of the Chronicles of Darkness, its soul, the Horror, undergoes a transformation into one of the Unfettered--ephemeral nightmares that slumber within abandoned Chambers in the Hive and stalk mortals through the Primordial Dream.
    If a Beast’s Lair is destroyed while the Beast is not merged with its Horror (a rare occurrence), its Horror dies. When this happens, the Beast’s Lair trait immediately becomes zero, he loses all of his Lair Traits, and immediately gains the Soulless (Persistent) condition. While under the effects of the Soulless Condition, the Beast neither gains nor loses Satiety. He cannot employ Atavisms or Nightmares and are for all intents and purposes is a human. This condition persists until the Soulless Beast passes away (from natural or unnatural causes), he gains a new soul, or he is Claimed by an Unfettered or some other ephemeral entity that looks for such empty vessels.
    If a Soulless Beast gains a new soul it slowly transforms into human, replacing it’s Satiety with an equal amount of Integrity and losing all Atavisms and Nightmares in the process but gaining Unseen Sense (Beasts).

    The Beast Unfettered

    Becoming Unfettered is not a matter of retreat. All Horrors can withstand the loss of their human bodies (i.e., ignore the Initiating the Retreat section). They are, after all, the children of things ancient beyond description. The Unfettered otherwise operate as described in B:tP except as follows.
    Lair:Unfettered retain the Lairs that their Horror’s dwelled in while their Beast’s were alive. These forlorn places drift to the bottom-most part of the Hive where they lay forgotten at the ends of unused Primordial Pathways. Each of the Lair’s Chamber still has the Lair Traits that it had while the Beast was alive but, the Unfettered cannot exert any control over them or employ them in any conscious manner. This is not a matter of statistic recorded on a description of the Unfettered but rather describes the den in which the Unfettered slumbers away its meals.
    Slumber: Like Horrors, Unfettered will slumber once they’ve consumed enough Essence to fill their reserve. They always return to their Lair to sleep. As more ephemeral version of its former self, an Unfettered’s Size remains the same as its Size when it was a Horror.




    Second Chances
    Unlike other Soulless mortals, Soulless Beasts maintain tenuous connections to both the Temenos and the Primordial Dream (possibly untrue, more information possibly coming with Mage: the Awakening?). These connections take the form of crumbled and ruined Oneiroses and overgrown, seemingly abandoned Chambers. Horrors instinctively avoid such places but Unfettered and other, darker entities, are attracted to them.
    The vast majority of those few Beasts that find themselves Soulless do not remain so for very long. The Soulless are magnets for ephemeral entities that desire access to the living world of the Chronicles of Darkness. Of particular significance is that Soulless Beasts gain the Resonant Condition as soon as an Unfettered enters their corresponding Chamber in the Primordial Dream.
    At that point the Unfettered may use its Influences to Strengthen the Chamber eventually bringing about the Open Condition. From there the Unfettered will Possess the Soulless Beast. If the no one notices a pattern of repeated Possession occurs until the relationship progresses to Controlled. Once a Soulless Beast has been Controlled by an Unfettered, the Unfettered can Merge with it.

    The Beast Rampant
    The effects of an Unfettered Merging with a Soulless Beast work exactly as the Merger is described in B:tP except as noted below. For all intents and purposes the Merger works in a similar manner to the process of Claiming.
    Merger Effects: The Rampant may select one of the following three options for each Rank possessed by the Unfettered doing the Merging.
    • Armor: As described in B:tP.
    • Body Warp: The Rampant may select a Dread Power. See CofD and W:tF. This rule replaces those found in B:tP.
    • Increased Attribute: As described in B:tP.
    • Increased Awareness: All Rampant gain this Merger effect for free.
    Rampant also retain access to any Atavisms or Nightmares that they had in life but lose any Numina that the Unfettered Merging with them possessed. They have an effective Lair rating of 0.
    Rampant otherwise operate exactly as described in B:tP. Many Rampant eke out short, brutal lives that frequently end on the tip of a Hero’s spear. Occasionally other Beasts find Rampants and grant them succor within the confines of their Lairs allowing both of them to realize several mutual benefits. There are also persistent but unsubstantiated rumors that some few Beasts have found a way to raise Rampant up from their semi-human bestial nature back to full Beasthood, splitting the Merged being back into Beast and Horror again.


    Some Rampant Specific Merits
    Guardian (2) -- Requires: True Friend, Rampant only. Effects: Your character has been befriended by a Beast who looks after him He gains a limited form of Primordial Pathways that allows him to enter and leave her guardian’s Lair at will. His guardian also provides for his upkeep by providing prey thereby reducing the rate at which he loses Satiety to 1 every 3 days.

    Pet (2) --Effects: Your character has befriended one of the Rampant and looks after her. This merit operates exactly like True Friend except that the Rampant gains a limited form of the Primordial Pathways ability which allows her to enter and leave the Beast’s Lair at will. Drawbacks: Because Rampants can no longer really function in human society your character has taken on the responsibility for limiting her friend’s impact. Not only must you feed your own Horror, you must also provide prey for your friend.





    Inheritance
    Beasts and Heroes are both interlinked through the monomyths that produced them. Their conflicts with one another are foreordained by destiny itself. If it goes on long enough Beasts either die, destroyed along with their Lairs by the Heroes that hunt them or they subvert the myth and turn it on its ear.
    Subversion is accomplished in one of two ways: either the Beast embraces the wisdom she has been dealing out for so long and becomes one of the Sagacious or she throws wisdom to the wind and succeeds in becoming everything Heroes fear her to be, an Incarnate.

    The Beast Incarnate (The Lesser Subversion)

    Becoming Incarnate works exactly as described in B:tP with the following exceptions.

    The Beast’s life isn’t really subsumed inasmuch it evolves into the post-human state of a living nightmare.
    The Beast must become the Apex of his region and build a Myth for himself as normal (see B:tP). But, not only must the Beast completely dominate a Hero in opposition to the monomyth’s cycle, he must slay the Hero. And not just any Hero will do. The Beast must dominate and slay a Hero (or a Band of Heroes) who has (or have) slain at least 8 Beasts. Only after all three tasks have been accomplished can the Beast claim the prize of evolving into an Incarnate.



    The Beast Sagacious (The Greater Subversion)

    Humans aren’t the only beings capable of learning lessons from a Beast’s nightmare feeding. Introspective Beasts slowly learn about their hungers, their legends, and their relationship with Heroes. Similar to the path to becoming an Incarnate, the path to joining the ranks of the Sagacious requires that a Beast accomplish three difficult tasks.

    Heal the Hero: The Beast must engage with a Hero who has slain 8 or more Beasts and through repeated lessons, impart wisdom that slowly heals the Hero’s soul. This is often accomplished by showing the Hero the harm she is doing to others around her as they suffer for her righteousness and obsessions.
    In game terms, this is accomplished by forcing the Hero through a number of Breaking Points designed to increase his Integrity, thereby healing his soul. The Beast’s player and the storyteller should agree to a list of reasonable breaking points that make this possible. Some examples include: Saw the Beast save a mortal, Saw the Beast save a mortal while under Anathema, Saw the Beast save a mortal that the Hero’s own actions put in danger, Saved from certain death by the Beast (-2), etc.
    Subvert the Hero: The Beast must befriend a Hero whose Integrity is 7 or greater. Like Spawn Legend, the goalposts for this achievement are more subjective than objective. The player and the storyteller should agree on a number of quests (around three to five sounds about right) where the Beast overtly aids the Hero in conquering threats to humanity. These may be other Beasts but Rampants, and especially Unfettered, could also easily be acceptable menaces. The Beast must purchase the True Friend merit with any experiences earned through this process.
    Conquer the Terror:Finally, the Beast and the Hero he has befriended must overcome a dire threat to the Primordial Dream. These usually take the form of Incarnates but a particularly brutal Apex (whose Lair is 8 or more) or an Unfettered whose Rank exceeds 7 (i.e., Rank 8+) would also suffice. The duo don't need to kill the terror, it is sufficient to contain it indefinitely, but, they do need to work together in order to accomplish this goal.
    Success: The Wisdom
    Once these tasks have been accomplished both Beast and Hero emerge as something new that shakes off the clothing of myths and stories altogether. The become a paired being--The Beast Sagacious and The Paragon. Rather than a framework of reinforcing myths, their dual existence is maintained by a syllabary of lessons to be taught to both the human minds drifting in and out of the Primordial Dream and those things that stalk them.
    The Sagacious gain a number of abilities that are the same or similar to those gain by Incarnates. These abilities include:
    • Adopt the Beast-Shape: Exactly as described for Incarnates in B:tP.
    • Ethereal Voyager: As long as she is in the presence of her Paragon, the pair may enter the Astral and voyage to any realm within it. (This includes the Primordial Dream and the Supernal Realms.)
    • Intertwined Lives: If either the Sagacious or the Paragon should die, the other dies too.
    • Legend/Life: Both the Beast and the Hero lose their Legend and Life and gain a shared combined trait called Wisdom. Both can regain Willpower through this trait exactly in the same way as they did through their Life traits.
    • Master of Magic: The Sagacious gains five dots in a single Arcana of his choice. The Sagacious also selects a number Spells equal in number to the number of Nightmares he knows (see M:tA; use Dread Powers and Numena instead if the troupe doesn’t want to crossover with Mage: the Awakening).
    • No More Heroes: The Beast Sagacious has a completely different interaction with Heroes than the Beast Incarnate. Having achieved a form that no longer needs to feed on the suffering of others, Sagacious do not cause Heroes to awaken and Heroes are not drawn to them. As such Heroes cannot use their powers on Sagacious (or Paragons); however, as the duo very much appears to be a complete perversion of the natural order of things from a Hero’s perspective, Heroes have been known to hunt them.
    • No More Lair: The Beast’s Horror merges with her, precipitating a complete collapse of her Lair. She loses all invulnerabilities and Lair Traits associated with it. She uses her Paragon’s supernatural tolerance trait--Anointment--in place of her Lair trait.
    • Satiety: Unlike Incarnates, Sagacious no longer need to feed at all. They replace their Satiety trait with an energy pool called Prudence. The size of this pool is 80 and both the Sagacious and her Paragon may draw upon it each turn. They may spend Prudence anytime they would spend Willpower or Satiety (or Mana) to employ an ability that calls for it. No more than 8 Prudence can be spent in any given turn. Anytime either the Sagacious or the Paragon could gain Willpower they may choose to gain Prudence instead. They also lose all of their Nightmares.
    • Soul Sickness: The Beast Sagacious does not suffer Breaking Points; however, its Paragon can and does suffer Breaking Points for any decisions that either of them make that would normally cause an Integrity Breaking Point. Because the Paragon has an Integrity trait, it is possible for the duo to fall from grace. For the Sagacious’ part, this is experienced as a physical sickening. The Sagacious suffers one Aggravated wound each time its Paragon loses a point of Integrity. Should the Paragon’s Integrity fall to zero, the Sagacious dies. In this case, the Paragon (or whatever they have become) lives on.
    The Paragon (The Hero Ennobled)
    Heroes that learn the lessons taught by Beasts on the Sagacious path find themselves uplifted into something more than human but less than divine. This transfiguration forges the Hero into an extension of the Beast’s will to teach lessons. This expresses itself through several abilities that the Paragon gains.
    • Anathema: Anathema provide a powerful incentivising tool for the Sagacious to use in place of Nightmares when teaching lessons. As such a Paragon may place an Anathema on any entity, mortal or supernatural. However, because their existence is so focused on teaching moments, the Paragon suffers a Breaking Point anytime he places an Anathema for a reason other than teaching a lesson.
    • Anointment: The Paragon gains a supernatural tolerance trait called Anointment. It’s value is equal to the average of number of Beasts the Paragon had slain as a Hero and the Lair rating of the Beast who uplifted her.
    • Heroic Tracking & Stalking: Since the purpose of their new lives is to teach lessons, the Paragon’s ability to track and stalk subtly changes so that he can track and stalk any entity whose Integrity (or Integrity analogue, e.g., Humanity, Cover, Harmony, Satiety, etc.) is 3 or less. The Sagacious and their Paragons ignore entities with higher Integrities (or Integrity analogues), even if they’re causing more harm. Ennoblement dulls the Paragon’s senses to the “wrongs” of the world.
    • Integrity: As a metaphysically conjoined being, the Paragon must carry the burden of both of their actions. In addition to suffering through Integrity Breaking Points anytime his own actions would call for it, the Paragon must endure an Integrity Breaking Point anytime his Sagacious partner’s actions would call for it.
    • Intertwined Lives: If either the Sagacious or the Paragon should die, the other dies too.
    • Legend/Life: Both the Beast and the Hero lose their Legend and Life and gain a shared combined trait called Wisdom. Both can regain Willpower through this trait exactly in the same way as they did through their Life traits.
    • Prudence: The Paragon’s Sagacious partner maintains a pool of spiritual energy called Prudence. The Paragon may draw upon this pool anytime they would be called upon to spend Willpower. Similarly, the Paragon may choose to replenish one Prudence to the pool anytime he could gain one Willpower for himself.
    • Soul Shock: Should the Paragon’s Integrity ever fall to zero, her Sagacious partner dies. The resulting shock transforms the Paragon again, this time into a maniacal Slasher (see H:tV; the Paragon can gain the Soulless (Persistent) condition instead if the troupe doesn’t want to crossover with Hunter: the Vigil).
    Once they have joined together and emerged as new dualistic being the Beast Sagacious and his Paragon spend their time giving lessons to those whose souls are near to destruction. These lessons aren’t limited to mortals and the tools that the pair can employ are much broader than the subtle but often clumsy application of fear alone. They can inflict pain, praise, and employ any number of other techniques in pursuit of their task. Should the pupil prove intransigent enough, they can even employ the final lesson.
    Like Incarnates, a Sagacious’ existence is hardly secure. She is completely dependent upon her Paragon to bear the weight of both of their souls and can quickly die if it turns out that the Hero she chose ultimately wasn’t equal to the task.




    Feedback on these hacks is always welcome.
