                *typing this in my snazzy new Beast t-shirt*

                Oh boy, so much I'd like to see:
                - New Atavisms and Nightmares, a mix of kinship or non-kinship for the latest. New Lair Traits, Merits, etc... you name it
                - New powers and mechanics for Heroes. The current selection is a little bit thin.
                - New advice and possibly rules for exploring the astral realms outside of one's Lair. Possibly denizens of those realms as somebody suggested on these forums. In general more development on the "dreamspace"
                - Some kind of creatures/factions which are related to Beasts but neither beasts nor heroes. Something to populate the ecology, as phrased above.
                - Some advice on running a purely Beast campaign, contrasted with a more kinship based one. Not just one or the other, but seeing both options would be nice.
                - More examples, rules and context on the inheritances.
                - More mythology, culture, factions etc - even if historical - for Beasts.
                - A sub-template perhaps (what can I say I quite like these)
                - An in depth discussion of the five hunger groups and how they shape the psychology of Beasts. Perhaps some hunger-specific mechanics (merits, or nightmares to contrast with the Families)

                Aaaand that's all I can think of for now. Great to see the other answers in this thread too.

                                                03-17-2016, 04:47 PM
                                                One thing that would be helpful in the player's guide is a short section or table that summarizes general Beast abilities. It's hard enough to describe Beast as a concept to new players (even CofD players) but an appendix or something that keeps that information readily available would be very helpful. For example, traversing the Primordial Pathways, Mother's Kiss, Family Resemblence, Family Dinner, imposing lair traits, anything that all beasts can DO in short summary on one page would be great to have around (sort of like an expanded screen I guess). Just the name, the roll, the cost (if any), and a one sentence summary.

                                                I definitely agree with what's been posted so far. The most important aspect of the Player's Guide should be helping players have a deeper understanding of what it means to be a beast. I want more information on what different beasts go through in their daily lives, how they interact with each other, how they interact with their mortal families. Most of this is covered in the core but a greater amount of detail will be valuable to players who want that extra layer of depth. Perhaps exploring further the concept of a beast being torn between their mortal family and their brood. One of the highlights of beast is that dichotomy of mortal life and monster life.

                                                Also, I love what ArcaneArts said about good and evil. Many players have a very black and white concept of being a monster. Let's explore the grey area! Beasts are the stuff of nightmares and fear but that doesn't make them innately evil. A section on this concept could help players understand that it isn't so simple as 'you're a spooky villain'.

                                                            Things I'd like to see:
                                                            • A list of Horror inspirations, both historical and modern, and possible Family concepts for them.
                                                            • Minions, both in the waking world and in the Lair. If the Beast brings a Werewolf into his Gloomy Forest Chamber, would the Werewolf be able to interact in any kind of meaningful way with the pack of wolves that provide that sweet music of the night, or are they just wolf shaped sound effect generators?
                                                            • More on Lairs and Burrows, especially some "In character" perspectives on what Chambers look and feel like to Beasts and non-Beasts alike. Also some expansion of how Beasts can modify a Chamber's appearance and/or nature, either when they add the Chamber or as they gain more Lair traits.
                                                            • More Atavisms and Kinship Nightmares.
                                                            • A section on how Beasts perceive and interact with the world around them, given the advantages they can gain from Environmental Immunity. An example would be an Eshmaki that has Darkness as a Lair trait, so she can see in the dark. Does she even notice when the lights go out suddenly, or would she just keep on reading her book?
                                                            • Examples of Satiety potentials, especially things that a High-Satiety Beast would find acceptable to his Horror.
                                                            • A "Day in the Life" section for a Brood. How they would hunt together (or apart), how they would interact with their Kin, and how they would team up to deal with a Hero entering a Broodmember's Lair; would all be interesting concepts to explore.
