    Unexpected Beast Inspiration: Persona 5

    09-13-2016, 05:30 PM
    So Persona 5 is finally out in Japan, and a glance at some of the early spoilers confirms something I've been thinking since it was revealed: it's totally a Beast game! (If you want to go into it blind when it hits the states, look away now!)

    The basic premise is that the player characters are a group of high school students who discover their Personas, creatures made up of their inner thoughts and desires who draw their forms from mythology and fiction, and use their newfound powers to hunt down criminals, abusers, and other loathsome people by entering their subconscious minds and 'stealing' the cruelty out of their hearts. The Persona series has always leaned on some interesting psychology, but the newest entry focusing so much on trying to redeem and reform terrible folks by entering their minds/souls and teaching them Lessons... I can't be the only one seeing this, can I?

    Remi. she/her. game designer.

              The fact that the Persona 5 team comes from a delinquent school and masquerade as master thieves gives them more ambiguous morals than the past Persona users, the criminality tying into the villainy of Beast quite nicely.

              I'm also glad to see the dungeons of Persona 5 (heists into people's hearts and minds) expanding from the Shadow created dungeons of the Midnight Channel in Persona 4, a similar concept. Beasts having this connection to the primordial pathways into dreams/the collected unconscious is one of my favorite things about the game.
