                                    He's a psychopath. That's what the psychiatrist said when he was 12, before she was paid a truly magnificent son by his parents to keep it quiet. She just wrote 'genius' on the psych evaluation, and that was true as well. He's just thought of the whole thing like a cosmic joke. Make him a genius so that any intellectual entertainment is short-lived and then a psychopath so that emotions don't offer any interest either. He entered the corporate sphere, just as his parents had hoped, but not for their sake. He just did it hoping that the Board might make his life a bit harder, provide him something to play against. That lasted all of a month, before he'd bankrupted each and every one of them and replaced them with people who actually did their jobs. Then he went for the top spot. Becoming CEO consumed half a year - the longest he'd ever had fun for - and then that was it. Then he went on to working on other companies, hoping that they'd put up a challenge. Some did, but the ones he could go for lasted only so long. He seemed to be cursed to while away his life in utter boredom.

                                    Until It came.

                                    That night, when the thing he'd always seen in his dreams, the giant-king, finally ate him up and became him. Finally, he had something to do. Where before he had simply been empty, now he was hungry . And hunger could be sated. He'd take the utmost pleasure in doing so.

                                    Anakim Tyrant, obviously, probably with Looming Presence and Mimir's Wisdom for Atavisms and You Must Obey and a reskinned You Can't Take It With You for Nightmares, the latter reflecting the fact that the victim knows that no matter what they do, the monster has the money and power to counter it, so they don't even try.

                                                He had always been a man of God, and he sought to lead his flock by example. His lifestyle was frugal, he acted with honesty in all affairs and all were welcome in his church. Despite all of this, he suffered from recurring nightmares where his church would be overtaken by an all-seeing demonic figure. His former followers would sign their names on a book the demon was carrying in exchange for having the chains around them broken.

                                                At first he was unable to interpret the meaning of this nightmare, and brushed it off. But the people who followed him diminished in number as the years passed, some under mysterious circumstances. When he went to investigate what had happened to them, he discovered the once faithful had turned to witchcraft and displayed terrifying reality-altering powers. Unable to convince them to abandon this heresy, he began researching what aspect of the occult attracted his lost lambs. Each night he'd stay awake in libraries and online, until he found out that his city was a nexus of occult phenomena. Fearing that this was caused by the dwindling number of faithful, he took it upon himself to eliminate these mysteries and bring back people to the righteous path.

                                                However, try as he might, it was impossible to fight back witchcraft with the tools of common men. He decided to fight fire with fire and started a Mystery Cult with the intent of gathering Relics in the hopes of banishing the curse from his city. What happened instead was that his cult attracted the attention of a Beast who worked with the witches. The Beast invited the priest to an occult gathering, promising to show him the truth if priest would hand over his relics and cultists to the witches. After much deliberation, the priest accepted, and the Beast returned the favor by Devouring him. That night, the priest dreamt of the demon again, but recognized that it was no servant of the Devil, but a part of his own being that represented his urge to control other people.

                                                Accepting that this monster was him, he merged with it, becoming an Ugallu Tyrant. His talent for leadership was noticed by a Mastigos who belonged to a group of witches called the Silver Ladder. They offered him the chance to prove himself so he might join their ranks and keep protecting the members of his flock, which he managed to do. Nowadays, his view of religion has been both expanded and confused, and he struggles with maintaining his honor while feeding his Hunger. Despite this, he is determined to teach a lesson to whoever falls into Hubris: you lead by example, not force.

                                                Atavisms: Mimir’s Wisdom, Needs Must, Eye Of Heaven.

                                                Nightmares: We Know All Your Secrets, You Must Obey

                                                    Anakim Tyrant: Speed. That's all that mattered, once upon a time. He was a racer, but not good enough to get into the big leagues. He made do, though, with the thrill of speed from backstreet racing, pushing his second-hand Mercades as fast as it could go and revelling in the acceleration. That lasted until the accident. A misjudged turn, a foot just a hair too far down on the accelerator and he was waking up in hospital with an amputated leg and a cocktail of chemicals bringing him in and out of consciousness.
                                                    He lay there in the hospital bed, half alive, and wondered. What would he do now? When he slept, though, he dreamt of driving and of the huge beast in the rear view mirror, a vaguely feline outline with exhaust smoke curling from its jaws and white LED headlights for eyes. He dreamt of driving as fast as he could and yet it always caught him, ending the dream with a steel claw tearing his little vehicle in two.
                                                    Eventually, though, after he'd ben discharged with a carbon fibre leg and gotten hold of a car he could drive, he tired of running. That night, he turned the car around and charged the beast, thinking that if he was going to go out again, he'd make it hurt this time. Instead, he saw the joy in its eyes, that he had finally faced it and this time when it devoured him, he looked out through its eyes and roared in the voice of a living engine.
                                                    Now it's not speed he cares for, but beating others, showing them that even though he's down a leg, he's still better than them.

                                                    Atavisms: Looming Presence, Inescapable, Unbreakable, Relentless Hunter
                                                    Nightmares: You Must Flee, You Cannot Run

                                                        Inspired by the eternal 'the homeless won't be missed and are prey for the monsters of the night' trope.

                                                        Ugallu Predator: He's been on the streets for as long as he can remember, and he's seen everything that hides in the shadows. He's spoken with vampires, informed mages, pointed werewolves on their way and hidden from monsters that chase half-men through the night. He's dealt with spirits and counselled the ghosts of less fortunate vagrants. And that was all before the bat-monster ate up his soul and made a nest in the remnants.
                                                        Now he's got the strength to deal on an equal footing with the other denizens of the night and he makes use of it. He protects the rest of the guys on the streets, as best he can. He can't keep them all safe, but he can teach them to keep themselves from harm. By day he tells them what he wishes he knew when he was younger, the stuff he had to learn the hard way. How to ward of a ghost and what names to drop when a fella with pale skin and too-sharp teeth comes wandering around.
                                                        By night, though, he notes the ones who don't believe him and gives them an introduction to the shadow world. He waits til they're asleep, then he opens up his lair, a crumbling, gothic city, and brings them in. He watches them wake up to a neverending moonlit night and burning eyes watching them from the rooftops. He watches them run from him until they can run no more until, when they collapse from exhaustion, he gives them a bite to remember him by and dumps them out in the waking world, scared but hopefully wiser.
