                            I've got the basic outline of my character down.

                            They're either a Collector or a Tyrant, possibly both depending on how you spin it. Whatever I end up deciding on, I've decided that this Beast hungers for vulnerabilities and deeply personal secrets- either to exploit them later (Tyrant) or simply for the delicious knowledge of a secret only they know in addition to that of another (Collector). This Beast uses the secrets and vulnerabilities for the other- enjoying the delicious Satiety and using them as leverage in equal measure.

                            For their day job, they either run an occult bookshop and act as a sort of information broker to the supernatural communities of Chicago, as well as a reasonable supplier of potential Ban or Bane materials. They also will accept secrets or lore instead of money as payment, so they tend to know everyone's personal Bans, Banes, Frailties, etc. Alternatively they are a clinical therapist- keeping the Horror reasonably well fed and guiding their now rather alien psychology with the ethics of a therapist.

                            The Horror is probably going to be a dragon of some kind, but I'm thinking of maybe going for something a little more eldritch- not quite sure yet. Family is either Eshmaki (Sudden betrayal through secret vulnerabilities) or Ugallu (You can't hide anything from them).

                                        Since we seem to be expanding on ideas:

                                        •A Anakim (though I admit he might edge over into Ugallu a little) Nemesis who works as a police detective and whose Horror manifests as a Talos-style giant with red and blue searchlights for eyes, representing the inevitability of law. He may be Wolfblooded, and either way he has some strong connections with the Lodge of the Shield. I'm thinking he's going to be making positive inroads with Kin or supernatural groups focused on upholding the law... though I imagine he struggles with how far is too far with going outside the law to enforce it.

                                        •A Namtaru Nemesis that serves as a preacher and whose Horror takes the shape of an oversized preying mantis with vaguely humanesque features, who is meant to represent the fear of being the reason for one's own failures. He seeks to scare the wicked into the arms of God, which would put him on good terms with the Lancea Sanctum if he didn't despise everything they stand for. Not only do they speak what he sees as heresy, they claim that vampires are innately Dammed, which clashes with the core of his philosophy, in which he believed that anyone can strive to be good, and that any supernatural strangeness is just another issue to be overcome. Sees himself as a very "tough love" sort of teacher. Likely going to develop (contentious) links to other Kin interested in the immortal souls of themselves and mortals.

                                        •A Makara Collector as encapsulated here, a cunning sea serpent who takes a keen interest in the sorceries of his Kin and has some serious envy issues. The sister would come into play later, I imagine; mostly I'd start by having him develop inroads with other supernaturals to feed his hunger for objects of ocult power and lore and to live though the spells of others. I imagined he'd run a bookstore or something; if I go with this one, Hexmeister Vic and Arcanist, all of them might have to do lunch.

                                                    Originally posted by Arcanist View Post
                                                    Only question is, what should I do for my starting Atavisms? There's just too many great ones, and more or less all of them fit for a dragon.
                                                    Hmm, depending on whether you're going to be an Eshmaki or Ugallu will influence that decision I imagine, since you have to take one of their starting. I suppose you could choose thematically based on how you'll use the information (whether you'll hide it until you surprise your target with it, or whether you'll rain it down on them) or you could decide which Atavisms you absolutely need to start the game, and then see if one of them is Ugallu or Eshmaki.

                                                    I know a Dragon can be an Eshmaki, but I feel like thematically that's not usually the case. I mean, with Eshmaki I feel like you aren't supposed to see them until the moment of the kill, legends about Dragons usually show them in all of their terrifying glory first. Unless they're wingless, I can see them being pretty well defined as Ugallu. That is however highly motivated by personal opinion, I know that a Dragon could realistically fit into any of the families.
