    Why to kill the victim?

    01-08-2016, 03:44 PM
    As we know, Beasts are feeding their Horrors by the fear their victims feel. So it would be logical that Beast would want to leave their victims alive so they could tell stories about Horror and with this - rising the paranoia in general population on this particular Horror. The more people are afraid of Beast, the more likely it will become local Apex.

    So why does much of corebook fiction insinuate that Beasts eat people? Eaten human cannot be afraid, don't he. What Beasts get from killing their victims instead of letting them free?
