    [Beast] Why I don't want them in crossovers

    07-05-2015, 09:52 AM
    People are saying that Beasts are really crossover friendly thanks to their innate powers and thematics, but I honestly see nothing of it and I believe Beasts would ruin any game in which players are mostly something else such as werewolves or Vampires.

    Power level
    First thing, the PL of Beast is off the charts, with free, scene-long perfect-effects at every corner. And then there are Vampires with the capacity to run faster for a turn by spending a point of Vitae.

    Theme
    Beast's themes are twofold, as far as I know: "Monsters are what they do not what they are", and Hunger. Both of which can be twisted into what's needed for any other gameline, but then again in a Mage game I don't see a Beast fitting in real well. At best it'll clash thematically, at worst their whole theme goes to trash and it's a Mage game with a Beast in it.

    The Beast Mythos
    Even if many many people have stated that the Beast mythology is no more true or false than any other, they have so many proofs to back their claim that it basically breaks any other gameline's mythos. It's even worse than playing with mages.

    Mother's Kiss
    I could also call this power 'Spotlight Stealer' because I can't see a situation in which a player being helped by Mother's Kiss won't get the message that the Beast player believes his powers are worthless without some help.
    "I don't think 4 dice is enough, here have a few more" or "I fear that your obfuscate has too many flaws, take some dice to enhance it" will not be received well for many players.

    Thicker Than Water
    The 'best-friends forever' power which states that Beasts are always on good terms with everyone because reasons. This power only proves a little more that the Beast Mythos is the one and only truth rather than their own vision. Had it been that Beasts always see other monsters with a Good first impression, it would have made a lot more sense.

    Family Dinner
    So...this power seems to exist to make sure that Beasts never go below 7 in a Vampire game, and always stay at 2 in a Mage game. Also, it removes the whole Hunger theme from them since the Horror that could only feed by harvesting people's fingers is now fine and sated after watching a Kindred take a bite.

    Skeleton Key
    A power which allow Beasts to almost instantly open gateways to any other world as far as they are aware that such gateways exist. So say a pack of werewolves want to bring the Beast and a few wolf-blooded and maybe a Vampire with them to hunt in the Hisil, the Ritemaster telling them that it will take some time since he has to open the gateway to all of them and that they'll have to take part in a rite that will last an hour, maybe more. And then the Beast just goes "Dude don't, I got this!" and opens the gate. Thanks, Beast, for ruining the scene and grabbing the spotlight again.

    Family Ressemblance
    This power allows the Beast to remove from the GM's toolbox any kind of paranoia. That's it. Vampire game with a Beast in? No Strixes. Werewolf game? This guy's a Claimed still undercover. Mage game? These two looking at us in the shadow are also mages, not some goons. Changeling? This guy reading the journal is a Huntsman trying to get info before striking. Wanna know what's even worst? Thicker Than Water then allows him to walk up to the antagonist and have coffee with him.

    Final Disclaimer
    I don't hate these powers, I think they fit thematically with Beast and allow for awesome Beast-only games, but as of now, Beast suffers the same problem that Mage has: They have the answer to every problem and their Mythos explains everything.
