                                                        Originally posted by HelmsDerp View Post
                                                        Beasts don't start out the strongest at melee combat, but they scale faster than almost anyone else. Except, oddly, in the case of Armor. Beasts have better out the gate armor than almost anything other than Metalflesh, but it doesn't scale with Lair. They have crazy potent poison in Basilisk's touch, too, though Toxicity mechanics make it vastly more potent applied out of combat than in.
                                                        True. The scaling was something I meant to mention, but I think I forgot. Point for point, Gifts and Disciplines are at least equal, but better. The XP efficency means that you can invest more in things, faster.

                                                        As for Social, Forcing Doors only leads to problems if you fail, and that's unlikely to happen with someone stacking Alien Allure, Striking Looks, and Siren's Treacherous Song.
                                                        There's a lot more context behind this than I went into with that initial post. Social maneuvering with supernaturals is how Beasts develop Kinship. Sure, against mortals, its easy enough to just Force Doors. You generally don't care about long term relationships with these people. However? Arcane had the right of it when he described Besats as the whole Mistress of Monsters. There are long term implications of using Forced Doors here on your minions. Sorry, I mean Kin. Sabotage can come in the form of merely disgruntled whispers that build up. Or, remember that at High Satiety, you actually can't add Lair to resist supernatural powers. So, if someone interrupts your Forcing Doors with a dominance play of their own? Both vampires and werewolves are predators used to such dominance play, and are known to respond in kind. And you can't tell me that a good portion of other supernaturals won't drool at taking over as the hub of this little gathering?

                                                        Also, there's the fact that Forcing Doors tends to require coersion of some kind. Even if its only the implicit "I am your boss, you must obey me!" kind. How many people are comfortable using coersion as their modus operandus? It requires a special kind of mind to happily rely on it.

                                                        And, interestingly, its actually is ineffective in the long run to not spend the time using Social Maneuvering. Low Satiety Alien Allure can give Persistant Charm Condition if you don't use Forcing Doors. Which means not only do you have Kin less likely to be subtly undermining you, but they're actually happily including you in their hunts, which means more Satiety. Meanwhile, if you Force Doors to make a Kin, word is likely going to spread through supernatural society, which actively hurts you.

                                                        And, yeah, Heroes are still an issue. When you use that much supernatural charm to Force Doors, it can cause a trigger. So can feeding your Hunger. Any time you are obvious with your abilities (ie being blunt) there's a chance that you could attract a Hero. Heroes are the reason that Beasts generally don't live out in the open and need to stay hidden. It won't happen every time, but keep on rolling the dice and you will eventually hit snake eyes.

                                                        So, yeah. Forcing Doors isn't a cure-all for Beasts. In a pinch, its fine. But it has long term implications. No one exists in a vaccuum.
