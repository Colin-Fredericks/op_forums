                                    And as I pointed out earlier, Beasts draw their abilities from a part of the Astral Realms, a place where concepts and beliefs take tangible form or manifest as actualities. Metaphors exist as real things, ideas take flesh and symbols embody what they represent. The Astral is flexible and adjusts as ideas and beliefs change, meaning that Realms shift in both prominence and what they manifest as changes occur within the world and Humanity's views of things. And these realms and entities are capable of interacting with other Realms, including the material, and have their powers and influences manifest in those worlds. What's more, we know that in 1e at least that the splat that spent the most time in the Astral, mages, that their powers changed to conform to the Astral: space magic worked differently, for example, to conform to humanities collective view of distance (this might change in 2e). What's more, we know astral entities associated with Arcana, the Abyss and Mages as a whole could influence mage powers and physical abilities in both the Astral and other Realms (Looking at you Acamoths and Aeons), at least within the purview of what they represented.

                                    The Primordial Dream is a substrate of the Astral Realms, and seems to sit as part of the area between the Tenemos and Anima Mundi (maybe). This likely means that it represents more base elements of human thoughts and emotions due to its positions on the border of the collective unconciousness of Humanity and the World's Soul, which matches it's role as the Realm of Humanity's base fears. Everything in that Realm is a symbolic reflection of these fears, as well as everything associated with these fears. For example, the Horrors are fears and related concepts made manifest as individual monsters. It also means that the line between symbolism and actualities are blurred. Thus the idea of Kinship and the metaphor of the Dark Mother as the link between Kin may be simply that in any other Realm: an idea, a metaphor to explain the WoD and what the Primoral Dream and it's denizens are. But the Astral gives these concepts substance, and thus in the Primordial Dream, the idea of Kinship and the Dark Mothers Family defines how the Realm functions, as well as how any Astral entity of the Realm would interact with things.

                                    So in other words: the Beasts are actually Kin and everyone is a descendent or draws from the Dark Mother when operating by the rules of the Primordial Dream . It doesn't mean Mages actually use her power or Werewolves are truly descendent from one of her wayward offspring. What it means is that when the Primordial Dream and the entities from it interact with the other splats as according to how their realm defines such interactions, and their realm defines most splats as Kin.

                                    Essentially, the Primordial Dream contains it's own self-contained cosmology of the WoD by which it operates. For all intents and purposes, this cosmology is true for the realm and it's denizens, as it is made manifest as an actuality within this Astral Realm. It could be that the Realm was once different, and was shaped by the collective interactions and beliefs of the Beasts to now embody Kinship and the Dark Mother as a means to explain themselves and the different splats, or the Realm always manifested this idea. Regardless of the orogin, all of the Realms influences and powers stem from this cosmology and the Realms nature as the collective embodiment of base fears, and this in turn governs it's interactions with everything both with and beyond itself. Thus, a Beast is going to see the Vampires as Kin and have powers that bond off of that because the source of its powers and the realm in which it operates defines Vampires as such. It doesn't over-write or even need to match up with any other splats cosmology, as it doesn't mean that either is "wrong" when there is an incinsistency: it is just the difference between the Primordial Dream's representation of what happened and the actual event. It is also why their powers based on other splats are more thematically tied to the splat rather than just straight duplications: they are drawing on and making manifest the concepts tied to that splat and shaped through the lens of the Primordial Dream. It also means that they can't use Supernal magic, the Abyss, Numina, Keys, etc. but can reflect what those things represent.

                                    As for the GM and Demons? I am not sure. It could be that the GM and it's workings fall outside the Primordial Dream because their lore says it does, and thus this theory is reinforced by its manifestations as an actuality in the PD. Maybe there is a disconnect between the GM and the Astral such that it is incapable of a true representation within it. Maybe the GM is an alien physical force from outside of our reality and thus in opposition with the Astral, which is a abstract reflection of it. Maybe it is because all the other splats at least influence the Astral in some way (without physically invading it) via having some form of (proto-)soul (or reflect one, such as Ghosts and Spirits), while demons and GM can only interact by invading it. It could be that the GM and it's entities are so interwoven within the material reality that their presence doesn't register as anything supernatural, and this is just part of everyday mundane fears. Maybe it is an Astral realm manifest in our world, reflecting technology and Humanity's interactions with it, and thus it exists parrallel but opposite the Primordial Dream. Or maybe...the GM has so much mystery surrounding it, I can only guess. But no matter why, if the Primordial Dream doesn't recognize them as Kin, then it makes sense for this to manifest as a lack of ability to tap kinship abilities. Depending on the "why" you choose to fill in, maybe this could change or maybe it is a fixed feature. I actually don't mind this lack of specifics though as it means I can add it in as a ST.

                                    Edit: Oh, and I do wonder if the denizens of the Lower Depths, Supernal, Abyss, Outer Realms (the Cthullu space in Summoners ) can interact with Beasts as Kin. We only know that Demons can't be kin, but most of the focus of who could be was on the splats and common non-PC supernaturals (ghosts and spirits). While I would assume Astral entities are similar to ghosts/spirits, a part of me wonders how you could tie beings that represent the defining aspects of reality/anti-reality (supernal or abyssal entities, respectively) or fractured/incomplete aspects of reality (lower depths) as Kin when Demons aren't due to their association with the GM.
