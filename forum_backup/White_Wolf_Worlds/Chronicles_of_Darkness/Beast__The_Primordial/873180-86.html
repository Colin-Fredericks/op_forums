                                            Originally posted by Darinas View Post
                                            So quick question: the books seem to suggest it's possible for Beasts to enter people's dreams directly from their Lair (presumably through the Tenemos/Primordial Dream) and feed on them in their dreams (similar to how the Horror does it of its own accord when you're starving/ravenous). How does that work exactly, though? I mean... I suppose they just open a Primordial Pathway to areas of the Astral, but how do Beasts find Oneiros (or whatever they call the area for people's dream) ? Do they just wander into the Astral until they find an Oneiros by accident, or do they have some way to directly get to an Oneiros if they know what they're looking for?
                                            The Player's Guide goes into more detail, but the process essentially runs:

                                            1. Be in your Lair.
                                            2. Leave one of your Chambers without using a Burrow or Primordial Pathway, placing you in the larger dreamscape of the Primordial Dream, which mostly mirrors the material world (don't try this from your Heart or more esoteric Chambers, you will have further to travel).
                                            3. The dreamscape is not a populated space; move towards places where more people should be (based on where you currently are) to cross the Horizon called the Day and enter the Bright Dream/Temenos.
                                            4. Navigate between realms in the Bright Dream (largely mechanically identical to the rules for navigating the Temenos in Astral Realms), bridging your way conceptually toward a specific person to reach their Oneiros.
                                            5. (Optional) Open a Primordial Pathway back to your Lair (possible in any part of the Bright Dream if one of your Lair Traits matches its Theme, which the Begotten always know) to deliberately target an individual for your Horror to feed from.
