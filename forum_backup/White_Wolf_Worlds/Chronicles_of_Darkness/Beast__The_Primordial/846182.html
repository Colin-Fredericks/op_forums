    Sandbox enhancing hacks for Beast

    03-12-2016, 01:47 PM
    Thought I'd share some hacks that I've been developing for my troupe.

    A new (un)Family of Beasts.
    Y’tomim: Nightmares Astray
    Her first thought is to wait. Surely someone will realize that she’s been left behind. But long minutes crawl by and loneliness sets in. Maybe, she reasons, she can find her own way home. It is only then that she realizes that she isn’t alone. There’s something there in the dark with her. She can’t see it. It has no form. But it is there nonetheless. Her breath comes raggedly as the need to get away crashes down around her and she runs. How did this place become so maze-like she asks herself as she flies around corner after corner. In the end she gasps in relief thinking that she’s evaded whatever it was only to feel its formless limbs envelop her and drag her kicking and screaming into the darkness around it.
    You’ve always found the overbearing confidence and arrogance of others insufferable. You constantly danced to their tune like a marionette. Now the shoe is on the other foot.
    You’ve awoken to a power that you never knew you had within you. You don’t know where it comes from but you’re glad you have it. Now they dance to your music.
    You’ve become a cold reminder in the dark recesses of their minds that agency cannot come at the expense of the agency of others. Other people’s dreams are not mere stepping stones to achieving your own.

    Lives
    She has a non-descript house nestled deep in a wooded lot. The shadows grow long and visitors easily lose their way. Sometimes children whisper about the old house and say it’s haunted but mostly people forget that it’s even there. Only in the dark of the night do they remember why they shouldn’t go near such places.
    He works in a small cubicle where everyone sees him come and go day after day without really realizing who he is or what he does. His appearance is well kept but nondescript. He lives in a small basement apartment in the middle of a run-down neighborhood but no one ever bothers him because no one ever really remembers that he’s there. For the most part he’s fine with this lot in life but occasionally he’ll see something that makes him want to intervene and set things to rights.
    He rolls his eyes when the senior partners brag about their achievements. Their eyes pass right over him like he was just a piece of furniture. They take his empty presence for granted--just another fixture of their day-to-day existence. Every now and again he takes them down a peg from the comfortable setting of their own dreaming minds.
    She’s always been a wallflower. A long history of being talked over and ignored has taught her that no one values her input. Even when she adds a valuable point to the conversation people just breeze on past it until someone else brings it up. It doesn’t really bother her though. She knows that she’ll catch up with them soon enough and then they’ll know what it’s like to be trodden down.

    Stories:
    Y’tomim (singular Yatom) or Orphans are unlike other Beasts in that they don’t represent stories that anyone remembers. Long forgotten, they are more closely related to the multitude of formless things that humanity has yet to encounter and does not know. No human tongue has voiced such tales for if they had, the formless would have been lent a shape and be orphaned no more.
    Instead Orphans fill the void of represented by humanity’s collective fear of the unknown. It’s fear of change. As such every Y’tomim’s story is merely a beginning. It is a chrysalis for a thing yet to be.
    How the story develops and turns out varies according to the individuals who have roles in it.

    The Horror:
    The nightmares of Y’tomim are those of the unknown. They are cloaked in darkness and the dreamer’s antagonist is often formless, invisible, or so deep in shadow that it’s true nature cannot be made out.
    Being lost in a claustrophobic environment like an endless maze of tunnels or caves or a never-ending expanse of twisted and overgrown woodland are common nightmare-scapes. The Orphan’s Horror typically lets the dreamer proceed at their own pace, allowing their self-confidence to lead them deeper and deeper into the labyrinth that sprawls around them.
    As time goes on the dreamer slowly begins to feel the Horror’s presence and they struggle to escape only to discover that they have been outmaneuvered, outthought, or outfought by the Y’tomim’s canny Horror.

    Lair:
    Orphans create Lairs that constantly twist in upon themselves. Their landscapes are designed to confound and confuse their prey, leaving their faith in the abilities broken and shattered.
    Suggested Lair Traits: Cramped, Disruption, Downpour, Echoing, Engulfing, Fog, Maze, Poor Light, Sealed Exits, Undergrowth, Viscous

    Birthright:
    The Y’tomim have seemingly been disowned by the Dark Mother. Unlike other Beasts they can employ their Kinship abilities on their brethren and sistren. Both parties can realize the full range of benefits that this normally allows.
    Because their Horrors are so nebulous in form, Y’tomim can realize additional benefits from teaching lessons to mortals. Anytime an Orphan teaches a mortal a valuable lesson about their limitations while feeding, she gains one additional Satiety beyond those she would normally gain from the feeding.
    Nickname: Orphans
    Atavisms: None. Y’tomim begin play with only one Atavism.
    Adoption: An Orphan may be uplifted into one of the known families by finding another Beast who is willing to adopt her. Both parties must join their Lairs together. Thereafter they hold a simple ritual wherein each of them slice open the palms of their hands, share blood, and sacrifice one dot of Willpower.
    Thereafter, the newly adopted Beast replaces her Birthright with her adoptive parent’s Birthright and gains one of her parent’s In-Family Atavism that is known to her parent.


    And some cross-over helpful/sandbox expanding merits:

    Castellan (1~5 dots) -- Effect: You have a supernatural ally that has taken up residence in one of your Lair’s Chambers. At only one dot, this being is more mortal than supernatural (e.g., it is a Ghoul, Stigmatic, Wolf-Blooded, etc.). At two or more dots the entity is a true supernatural being (e.g., it is a Vampire, Demon, Werewolf, etc.). The Supernatural should be built with 5 additional experiences for each dot of Castellan beyond 2 (i.e., at Castellan 4, the supernatural has 10 experiences). This merit works in all ways like the Retainer merit. At three dots and above, it also works as the Mentor merit at an equivalent rating of Castellan dots - 2. Whatever kind of entity the Castellan is, it gains a limited form of Primordial Pathways which it can use to come and go from its Chamber and is in all ways, your invited guest.
    Drawbacks: For the purposes of calculating Satiety loss due to Time, your Lair trait counts as one higher for every two dots (round up) of this merit.

    Haunted (1~5 dots) -- Effect: Your Horror has permitted one or more ephemeral beings to take up residence in one of the Lair’s Chambers. This merit acts as either Retainer or Staff (chosen when it is purchased). In the first case, the ephemeral being’s rank is equal to the dots in this merit. In the second case the dots in this merit represent a like number of Rank 1 ephemeral beings. They cannot leave the Chamber in which they reside. They may be Angels, Ghosts, Spirits, or other ephemeral beings.

    Spawn (1~5 dots)-- Effect: Your Horror has spawned a multitude of tiny Horrors. Dots of this merit act as dots of Allies or Staff interchangeably. Among other things these Rank 1 ephemeral beings can come and go from the Lair at their leisure and may be used to gather information regarding the surrounding Hive and Primordial Dream. They will also savagely attack any intruders that enter the Lair.
    Drawbacks: You lose additional Satiety equal to the dots of this merit once per month. This loss can be spread across the month.

    Steward (1~5 dots)-- Effect: You have a mortal servant that you trust to look after affairs within your Lair. This mortal is your invited guest and has taken up residence in your Lair. Dots in this merit act exactly like dots in Retainer except that the mortal also gains a limited form of Primordial Pathways which they can use to come and go from the Lair.

                              One more hack I originally meant to post with the others. The exception that makes the rule as it were:
                              Techgnostic Horror (1 dot) -- Effect: Your Horror is inexplicably more closely linked to the God-Machine than to the Dark Mother. No one knows why this is but you count only Demons and Angels as being Descended from the Dark Mother and Kinship Does Not Apply to Vampires, Werewolves, Prometheans, Changelings, Mummies, etc.
                              Drawbacks: This reversal of the natural order doesn’t sit well with other Beasts who have a Hostile impression when meeting a character with this merit for the first time.
                              This merit may only be purchased at character creation.
