    Family and Hunger

    Are you sure you want to lay it out this way? A brief intro to both Family and Hunger at the very beginning, and that's it? With no transition in between Family and Hunger?

    Also, the italicized intro paragraph for each Family (visually) blends right into the description of the Family. I like the intros . . .but they needs something more to set them off from the general text

    Anakim
    p. 26

    When she freed the terrors of the world from their secret hiding place and held back hope from the world,
    Shouldn’t that be "secret hiding places" (plural)? And where does holding back hope come from? Seems like a mangled mix of Pandora's Box, and the richest man in the world (whom Raven stole the sun & stars from)

    Not sure about the choice of mythical giant families. Three out of the four are European . . . but no mention of the Hekatonkheires (Hundred Handed Ones), arguably the greatest giants in mythology? Briareus - all by his lonesome self - smacked down the entire Greek pantheon, when the Olympians dared challenge Zeus

    P. 27
    The dreamer is left with no doubt that might makes right, and that the Giant has all the right in the world.
    Shouldn’t that be "the Giant has all the might in the world"?

    Hungers

    p. 36
    Last line references "Soul"
    An Eshmaki Tyrant’s Soul hunts from the shadows, its form never fully seen; the glimpses that his victims catch reveal
    p.37
    Again, a reference to "Soul":

    An Ugallu Tyrant’s Soul often appears impressive in his own right,
