    The short answer is no. It does not reach into other worlds. It's something that affects the material and the Primordial Dream and going into those places counts as "leave the area", even if after a month within the Maelstrom there are probably some minor reflections of its effects in the Shadow.

    Mages, provided they travel deep enough in the Temenos to reach the Primordial Dream, can fight it but it's not of great use. The best they can do is to delay its growth (let's say, if you slain the fake Horror that day does not count for the total of the twenty-six days and the Maelstrom has to go through that specific combination of Horror and Hunger one more time) but its astral form it's too strong and reforms the following day. It usually is not worth the effort.

                                            I'd like to add something here.. or atleast place the idea out there for people.... An addition to the Heroes journey in Beast (Esspecially since it doesn't seem to have been touched upon in the Corebook) Heros Regnans or Heros Ascendant.

                                            Regnans

                                            "Heros will always be remembered,
                                            But you know legends never Die"
                                            Emperors New Clothes, Panic at the Disco.

                                            Heroes are trouble. They are dangerous, they hunt and kill for their own amusement and hungers, yet they cannot look past what they have become. For all they wish to be the center or the story they are just asmuch a pawn in the narrative. They cannot look past their own gain or needs, this is there role and wants. But what happens when a Hero becomes an Apex?

                                            Sometimes a Hero becomes so feared and strong in a hive that they simply cannot be stopped, they begin hunting and slaying the Begotten in there own lairs, making themselves have the largest impact upon the Dream in the local area, they become Monsters of their own making, slaugtering the Children and shaping themselves into a Horror of Horrors. They Change, they lose all pretext of humanity and become something else.

                                            Heros Regnans or Heros Ascendant without the flowery words are Monsterous Heroes that have became what they hunt but are still so wrapped up in their own Tale that they do not understand they are asbad or in many cases Worse then what they hunt. They are the Terrifying Vigilante whose mear mention makes people blanch and hide, they are mob boss who doesn't just kill you for your betrayal, no they make EVERYONE suffer. They kill all your family, friends and associates, leaving you alone, a Pariah amongst society, only then will they finish you.

                                            (Will add more as i write it)

                                            Light and Dark are two Sides of a Coin... Humanity is What Happens when it Lands on it's Edge.

                                                        Other topics:
                                                        • Dreamtides - less... cataclysmic intrusions (compared to Maelstroms) of the Dream upon the waking World. Similar to when a Beast overlays their Lair upon an area, but larger and not directed by an intelligence.
                                                          • Smaller manifestations could be confused with hauntings, or faerie circles (from when someone wanders in, but doesn't get out before the effect fades).
                                                          • Need a mysterious Dark Scary Forest that only appears on some nights? The Dream intrudes. Better escape before the convergence ends, and you are trapped in the Dream.
                                                        • Mythic Items - The Primordial Dream made manifest in (or through) an object of repute. Dreaded Weapons, Cursed Artifacts, etc. The Legend of the thing gives it power, and warps the narrative/destiny of those around it.
                                                        • [Atavisms?] - Flora and Fauna touched by the Primordial Dream, and perhaps have undergone a lesser Devouring of their own. Mutated, material counterparts to Night Terrors. Not the manifest Fear-of-Bears, but a real bear touched/invested by Fear.
                                                          • [Might be redundant with Night Terror!Claimed]
                                                          • [If not redundant, maybe stick an Atavism or few on a creature/plant, give it a Legend, optional Anathema, call it good? Getting chased by a bear with Relentless Hunter or a rat with Looming Presence is pretty scary. Even a tree with Stormwracked(?) has some potential.]
                                                        • Formidolosii - People afflicted by a connection to the Primordial Dream. They become vectors for Nightmares, spreading them either deliberately or unwillingly (when suffering their own Breaking Points, or when a Condition triggers from letting the fear build up). Not Heroes, not Beasts, but something else. Maybe they are a "release valve" for pent up fears stuck in the Dream. Might make a decent Minor Template.
                                                        • Odd Kinships - Strange locations that Beasts can form Kinships to. Maybe the ability to form Kinships with the Mythic Items above for extra perks. Maybe adding a Z-splat (tentative name Colleges, as a nod to the "teaching culture") for Beasts to form Kinship (and Kinship Nightmares) to philosophies and modes of thought.
                                                        • Catharsi - Lesser beings, similar to Night Terrors (but probably from the Bright Dream) that represent the emotional and stress relief that Fear can provide. Probably few in number, unfortunately.
