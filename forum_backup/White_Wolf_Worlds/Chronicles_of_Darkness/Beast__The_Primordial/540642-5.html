    Aaand here I am, noticing that there are general Hunter Kinship's, but nothing specific. No Cheiron, no Lucifuge, no VASCU in the pool. Time to fix it, isn't it?

    You're Just an Item: (Cheiron Group Hunter, though I guess it could be repurposed for a Loyalist Changeling or a certain type of Deviant)
    You've made the deal, you cashed in your boons, but now it's time for them to cash in theirs. They gave a clause. You won't do anything they don't want you to with what they gave you. And you'll do with it whatever they want you to. There is but a single problem. What they gave you, is you yourself. Your life. They own you. For them, you're nothing more than a mere tool like that screwdriver you're just holding. And if you won't serve, they'll break you, or make you serve by force. You wouldn't want it, right? Good. Now go, do as you're told.
    Nature of the fear: The fear that you're not belonging to yourself, that you don't have free will, are basically someone's slave and have to obey their every whim.
    Normal effect: When using the nightmare, choose a character, PC or NPC. The victim now belives it's entire life is owned by that character, and under threat of terrible consequences has to do as told. The victim gains a -2 penalty to acting against the chosen character until the end of the scene.
    High Satiety: The Victim subconsciously belives that his "enslavement" is fundamentally right and that his "master's" orders are the proper thing to do. Any action undertaken against the "master" removes the -2 penalty, but inspires the Guilty Condition on the victim until the end of the scene.
    Satiety Expenditure: The victim gains the Leveraged condition in regards to the chosen character. In addition, if the High Satiety effect of the Nightmare is active, the -2 penalty persists on all actions.
    Dice Pool: Wits+Satiety vs Resolve+Supernatural Tolerance

    You Won't Repeat Their Mistakes: (Lucifuge Hunter)
    They screwed up, didn't they? Fathers, mothers, all those who fancy themselves as older and wiser, they've all been wrong all the time. They did the wrong thing. But you're not like them, are you? You'll do the right thing, and the right thing is certainly not what they say.
    Nature of the fear: Following in the footsteps of people who are or were morally wrong.
    Normal Effect: The victim has to spend a Willpower point to act in accordance with anyone in position of authority over him, beliving their decisions to be fundamentally wrong.
    High Satiety: Any rolls made while acting in accordance with anyone in position of authority over the victim suffers from a -2 penalty.
    Satiety Expenditure: Any roll made while acting in accordance with anyone in position of authrotiy over the victim suffers from a -3 penalty. This effect stacks with the High Satiety Effect.
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Satiety vs Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance

                    Continuing with conspiracy Kinship Nightmares:
                    They're Onto You: (VASCU Kinship. Could be repurposed for Changeling, Deviant or a Magistos Mage)
                    They know it. They saw what you did, they hear what you're thinking, they know every your move, they're coming, and you can only run... No, scratch that. Even if you run, they find you. You can't do jack about them, only wait for them to come.
                    Nature of the fear: Being stalked and chased by something that know more about you than you yourself and can see and predict your every move.
                    Normal Effect: The victim believes that some unknown beings are constantly spying on it trying to punish it for some unspecified atrocities. The victim gains the Paranoid condition.
                    High Satiety: The victim's fear of being surveiled and captured leads it to panic at the very thought of whatever migh the beings do to him unless he somehow escapes their grasp. The victim suffers a -2 penalty to all actions not involving running from the ones he believes to be after her.
                    Satiety Expenditure: The victim thinks that the atrocity he's being chased for actually happens and is able to recall it in great detail. In addition, he belives that those surveiling him have all the right to do so for said crime. He gains the Guilty condition
                    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Satiety vs Composure + Supernatural Tolerance

                    You're Impure: (Malleus Maleficarum Kinship.)
                    You've sinned. You've sinned greatly, and as any other sinner before you, you will go to Hell. And you know what awaits those who end up in Hell? That's right. Suffering. You do not want to suffer, do you? But there is still a chance for your soul. Go, my child, and do good in the name of the lord.
                    Nature of the Fear: Fear of being punished for one's misdeeds.
                    Normal Effect: The victim believes that he's commited misdeeds that he will be punished for, in Hell, by Karma, appropriately to beliefs, and that the only way to avert that is by comitting good deeds. Any time the victim is presented with an opportunity to fulfill his virtue during the scene he must roll a reflexive Resolve + Composure check to resist doing so.
                    High Satiety: The victim now can no longer regain willpower from fulfilling his vice. Instead, he loses willpower by doing so.
                    Satiety Expenditure: The victim can't regain willpower by any other means than fulfilling his or her virtue or vice (Or only virtue, if the High Satiety effect applies.). In addition, fulfilling the virtue now only provides one point of Willpower.
                    Dice Pool: Wits+Satiety vs Resolve+Supernatural Tolerance

                    Both are pretty straightforwards and flavorwise uncreative, but I still consider them somehow interesting.
                    Lemme know what other Conspiracies would you like to see next.

                                    I've written an ivory claw kinship for a game I'm currently playing

                                    You're Not Good Enough (Ivory Claw Kinship)
                                    You're worthless, you know it. They can all tell, you might as well just give up and stop trying. No matter how hard you try you're just going to fail.

                                    Dice Pool: wits + satiety vs resolve + supernatural tolerance
                                    Normal Effect: The target suffers the broken condition.
                                    High Satiety Effect: 8-again on social rolls made against the target
                                    Satiety Expenditure Effect: The broken condition becomes persistent
                                    Exceptional success: The target suffers a -1 to all actions taken (-2 if the high effect is also used)

                                    I've also toyed with the idea of making the high satiety effect cause a -4 to all actions within a specific category (like the Everything You Do Is Worthless normal effect).

                                        Unhallowed Ground (SinEater w/ Boneyard) - (Modeled after You Are Infected)
                                        "Hey, did you hear that? What do you mean no? This place is creepy man, I swear. I have to get out of here."
                                        Normal: The Beast infuses an area with a nightmare for one scene. The area must be contiguous and have some distinct designation ex The clearing at the center of forest street, the hotel lobby, Turner Graveyard. The next individual to enter the area who is not the Beast or a member of her Brood triggers the nightmare to be rolled at -2. Any decisions for the nightmare must be made at the time it is infused in the area rather than when it is rolled. The Beast does not know who triggered the Nightmare but does know when it was triggered.
                                        High Satiety: As above but the Beast takes a -4 roll to invoke a nightmare and suffuses a location with it's essence. Anyone who enters the location suffers its effects while they remain there.
                                        Satiety Expenditure: The combined Nightmare lasts for a full day.
                                        Exceptional Success: The Beast receives an instinctual impression of the target. While they do not specifically see him, they could describe standout features such as male, tall, scarred. If the Beast encounters the individual they can roll Intelligence + Resolve + 2 to identify them as someone who was effected by their nightmare.

                                            Tainted Waters (Obrimos Mage or Ordo Dracul Vampire)
                                            "I knew the resonance of that Hallow was strong, but nothing like this... There's this buzzing in my ear now, it's almost like I can hear whispers. I think they're getting louder,.."
                                            Normal: The Beast uses the Hallow, Node, Wyrm's Next or other Font of magical energy to infect the last individual to pull energy from that location with a nightmare. The Beast rolls at -2 in order to affect them.
                                            High Satiety: The Beast infuses a Font of magical energy with a nightmare. The Beast rolls for the nightmare at -4 and the next time any entity pulls energy from that location they immediately suffer its effects.
                                            Satiety Expenditure: For each Satiety spent the Beast can infect an additional target that takes from the source of energy.
                                            Exceptional Success: As per activated Nightmare.

                                                Your Life Is Mine (Leechfinger Changeling)

                                                Dice Pool: Presence + Satiety – Stamina

                                                Normal: The attack inflicts one point of lethal damage per success. Victims who die from this Nightmare’s effects are found drained of something vital: blood, breath, water, or even their youth.

                                                High Satiety: The attack drains points of supernatural resources (Vitae, Essence, Mana, etc) or Willpower for targets who do not have such resources, equal to the Beast’s Lair dots.

                                                Satiety Expenditure: For the duration of the scene, each point of damage caused by this Nightmare will heal one point of bashing damage suffered by the Beast or downgrade one point of her lethal damage to bashing damage.

                                                Exceptional Success: A successful attack counts as fulfilling the Beast’s Hunger (based Satiety potential of 3)
