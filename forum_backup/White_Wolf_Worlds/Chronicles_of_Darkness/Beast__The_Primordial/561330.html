          The long of it is, click that Kickstarter link. You can read the entirety of the first draft of the book there for free. The game is undergoing revisions due to feedback; check the updates section for the latest on that.

          The short of it is, you're Grendel and Smaug with a dash of Freddy Krueger. There's a little bit of primal dread in your makeup, a dark little hollow in the soul of the world, and it wants, and it needs. It feels good to hoard, or to punish, or to lay waste. It brings clarity. Bringing meaning with it, though, that part's up to you.

          There was also Demon: the Descent, if you missed that; not sure when you fell back. That one's about gnostic liars collecting false lives to conceal themselves from the harsh celestial light.

                              Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                              Remind me to ask the guys behind financial logistics how the Prestige Edition cost less than that. I'm genuinely interested in how that worked out.
                              Printing books in bulk, with a set number of copies to be produced determined ahead of time, is cheaper than printing and binding individual books at irregular intervals in response to individual orders, I would assume is the largest factor. Especially when you don't own the printers and compare the cost of a single print run versus having on-going availability because you don't know when customers are going to want a book and you don't have a warehouse to store pre-printed books in ahead of time.

                              Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post

You get a fantastic deal on these nWoD Kickstarters. Not only do you get a nicer-produced book for less, but you get the electronic copy earlier than anybody else, and you end up getting all the stretch goal PDF material on top of that.
