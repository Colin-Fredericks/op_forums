    I’m taking inspiration for this Plot-Hook from the Horrorspawn idea proposed by Mad-Maudlin.

    72) A local brood of surprisingly imaginative Beasts have founded a tournament around the region's Horrorspawn fights entitled as ‘The Terror Games!’ The seven of them are offering an unknown ‘Ultimate Prize’ to any who can both win the tournament and best them. Not only that, but, if the news on the supernatural grapevine is to be believed, the Brood has somehow managed to link the position of Apex and the tournament’s championship title ‘Lord/Lady of Fear’ together. The Brood has a chance to sign up, but only one of the Begotten is able to win the chanpionship.

    Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
    Adds a handicap. Scaring people is easy when you have the ability to directly inflict fear. Plus it offer scenes where the the scary Beast turns into a bumbling caricature without the powers to back their implied threat.

    Though you could let them have some powers, provided you really enforce the &quot;no harm&quot; constraint.
    There’s also a possible in-universe reason why the Brood can’t access their Atavisms. Such things are essentially the Begotten channelling a portion of their true visages into their human forms. Ergo, they can’t channel such into their bodies since they’re already in their true forms.

    Edit: Decided to change up my plot hook slightly.

        73)
        Begotten and Unchained don't work together, usually. They do have much to give to each other, however, and occasionally, strike up a partnership.
        One such pair has been working for decades. The Ugallu tyrant has been Mayor and Apex for almost two decades now, and with his unlikely partner, they've completely redesigned the place. They were intent on changing the Infrastructure, and acquiring and integrating key Chambers as well as Obcasus Rites through prodigious use. Now, they have almost reached their goal and enlist the Brood to help them to reap their ultimate rewards: the entrance to the Primordial Dream is to be ripped open, turning the whole area into a gateway to the Mother's Land. All they need to do is performing human sacrifices of powerful people in different places over a short period. The leaders remind them that the rewards would be immense, but should they not agree, they'd find someone else. A pack of Pure, a Kindred Coterie, another Brood - the list of possible help is long enough.
        But will this truly achieve the leader's dreams of rising up to Incarnate and creating their own Hell, save from the G-M for good? How will the inhabitants (both mundane and supernatural) react to living in a Nightmare version of their city, with some among them suddenly appearing to be horrific monsters? How will the rest of the world react when they learn of this abrupt change?
        How will the Dark Mother react? Can the Brood stop this madness - do they even want to?

                Came up with the first idea after watching Bambi 2 and doing a bit of thinking on it, during which my thoughts somehow went into B:tP territory.

                75) Terror-Fawn! While out on a Summer-long group Hike through a particularly large forest/wood, the players come upon something absolutely shocking; a young fawn (or some other woodland critter) who registers as Kin! To be more specific, the little one’s a Beast! What does the Brood do with this apparent anomaly? How will the rest of the Begotten react to this discovery?? What does it mean if ‘mere’ animals can become Devoured by Horrors??? And just HOW did this even happen in the first place????

                76) The Kindgdom of Horror; A Brood has just gone ‘Dark Lord’ and conquered a small country. They’ve started to invite both fellow Begotten and various Kin along to become citizens in this new ‘Kingdom of Horror’.

                Originally posted by Kvedulf View Post
                10) The Corrupting Touch

                Something strange is happening to the local area's Begotten, or more precisely, to the targets of their feeding. Some people who are fed on by Beasts are changing, their bodies warping to reflect the Horror of the Beast that fed on them. A Namtaru whose Horror manifests as a massive horned rat might cause their greasy black or brown body hair to grow over their victim's bodies, faces becoming elongated and muscles twitchy. An Anakim whose Horror takes the form of a shambling mass of blood-soaked metal shards finds that his last meal has gone into hiding as weird metallic growths manifest under the skin.

                Making a bad situation worse, those who are warped by a Beast's Horror gain the ability to sense the presence of the Begotten, with a particularly strong connection to the monster with whom their condition originates. Beasts in your city are now gaining collections of weird hangers-on. Obsessed cultists, children trying to please their cruel creator gods, hating and loving the Begotten all at once. What will you do when even your gentle feedings may leave scars?
                77) A new religion has recently sprung up. Usually this wouldn’t mean anything to the local Begotten, until a week ago, when a Beast investigating the whole shebang came back reporting something that no-one expected; this Religion worships the Dark Mother Herself, glorifying her offspring as Demigods! What do the players do with this new information?

                Originally posted by malonkey1 View Post
                28) Some magician has figured out how to bind Horrors to their service, capturing them in stainless steel balls, and now everybody's doing it. What the hell are you gonna do about it?
                28-A) A couple of these so called ‘trainers’ have started performing knock-off Pokemón-Style fights with their captured Horrors, competing over bragging rights and bets.

                Originally posted by Nyrufa View Post
                58) Ahh, Real Monsters: The local Apex has decided to open an academy that caters exclusively to the Begotten somewhere within the Hive. Most of the students are fresh from their devouring and have little experience with what it means to be a literal monster. The academy is here to help provide them with a formal education on such matters and grades them based on their scare tactics, as well as the difficulty of the prey they choose to go after.

                As new Beasts themselves, the players have found themselves inducted into the academy. Unfortunately, they quickly find out that no matter where you go, school is still school...
                Reminds me of Monsters University. Was that part of your inspiration?

                Originally posted by Kvedulf View Post
                7) You Meddling Kids!
                The aftermath of some of your more... creative feedings has always drawn people - gawkers ready to build your legend or baffled cops, the occassional wild-eyed Hero. Now, however, your trail has been picked up by a group of amateur monster hunters. You'd classify these annoyingly persistent sleuths as Heroes except for two things. First, they haven't tried to kill you, and second, they are totally convinced that you are a fake. Even repeated use of Nightmares hasn't seemed to deter the investigators. Even if they're not actively malevolent, their prying might lead a Hero to you or to your Family in the city's supernatural underworld.
                Oooh, BOY, those kids’re gonna need a fresh set of underwear when they realise that the Beast is for real.

                    78) Federal Begotten of Investigation: The moment Agent Langston saw you, you saw him - and the Stalker in Streets mirrored the exact look of disgust as his human body did. Langston's first experience with the supernatural was before his Devouring, you see; he was one of the Vanguard Serial Crime Unit's top profilers due to his ability to understand slashers intuitively even without their pet psionic projects. When VASCU was forced to go private, he stayed with the Feds, where away from his ability to constantly focus on looking outward at the dark things he hunted, he turned inward towards his dreams, where the Stalker finally caught up to his soul and revealed his natural talent was due to having more in common with the supernatural murderers than his fellow agents. He's never quite forgiven himself for it, and while he's channeled it into a very productive end of at least attempting to rehabilitate slashers (and taking what food he can when it's a lost cause), he's absolutely certain he, and anyone like him, is a form of psychic Undertaking - and given how he saw you around an actual murdered corpse, his mind has leapt to the worst possible conclusion. You have a wayward brother who started off with a lot more experience in running people down after you than you did when you were Devoured, and he's kept in touch with his friends.

                                        78) The Primordial Dream experiences something that is being called a "dreamquake". The bonds between Chambers in Lairs become damaged, among other things.
                                        While people are cleaning up from this unexpected event, you are approached by a Begotten from a Family you don't recognize & have never heard of before.
                                        And then, it comes out that something momentous happened before the dreamquake. An Unchained managed to make contact with the the Dark Mother & beseeched her for a safe place & to be part of the family. The Dark Mother decided to show kindness on this kinda pathetic creature (because it was).
                                        That moment caused the dreamquake, created the Automata Family.
                                        Now everyone is asking has this happened before? How does this effect our relations with Unchained as a whole? What are the capabilities of the Automata Family? Where do we go from here?

                                        • The Unchained still do not register as kin, that is unchanged. This is no different even if you are part of the Automata Family.
                                        • The dreamquake happened because Unchained have no kinship with Begotten, & the Dark Mother had to do something extra to make this work.
                                        • The Automata Family have Birthrights, Atavisms, & stuff connected to espionage, demonism, & technology.
