                        Some more LoL Champs as Begotten:

                        Cho'Gath: The Champion is an Anakim Predator Horror, bringing with it such Atavsisms as Siren's Treacherous Song, Vengeful Earth, Limb from Limb, Ravenous Maw, Looming Presence to represent his AoE Q and W as well as his Ultimate.

                        Maokai: A Talassii Nemesis Horror with Monster from the Deep, Caught in the Web, Relentless Hunter, Vengeful Earth and Looming Presence.

                        Janna: A Makara Nemesis with Alien Allure and Storm-Lashed as well as some Obcasus Rites

                        Annie: Anakim Tyrant with Tibbers as her Horror, Dragonbreath to represent her abilities, and 3-Dot Horrorspawn for her ultimate.

                        Vladimir: is a Beast, the Horror some vaguely-humanoid shaped pool of blood. Inguma Predator with Infestation (Pool of Blood), Vengeful Earth-refluffed to Bloodlust (AoE attacks + Ranged)

                        Illaoi: Rare aquatic Anakim Tyrant with Smashing Currents and Monster from the Deep. Ouroborous is her Horror.

                        Zyra: Eshmaki Predator with Caught in the Web, several 1 and 2 dot Horrorspawn and Vengeful Earth(refluffed to Malice of the Forest)

                        Malphite: Anakim Nemesis with Titanic Blow, Unbreakable and Vengeful Earth.

                        Urgot (as Crabgot preferrably): Namtaru Predator with Cyclopean Strength, Crushing Coils, Caught in the Web, Ravenous Maw

                        Dr. Mundo: Anakim Ravager. Relentless Hunter, Looming Presence, Cyclopean Strength, and a changed Dragonbreath that throws out the range to instead deal AoE Flame Damage of varying strength.

                        Do you agree? What would you change?
