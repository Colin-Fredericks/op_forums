                                            List of things I Like
                                            -------------------------------
                                            Opening Pitch
                                            *The Main thing I like is the sort of discussion what fear does, how fear acts as a teacher and crucible for development. THere's some good clarity and sense of place here-it reminds me of a good bit from Dresden Files in Ghost Story. But not all monsters are-well, I'll save that for the torn section.

                                            Overview
                                            *You know, it just occurred to me that Beasts were kind of 'justified victims' as well from a certain point of view in the old read. This avoids that. I'm not sold on the seperate soul taking the place of yours, but I think having the full claiming of one's Soul in context removes a troublesome element.

                                            The Devouring
                                            *Oh god, some of those dreams are visceral. Good story fodder there. You experience the nightmares of others, and you just know what it means, and you try to do something about it, but you aren't equipped yet to do it. I like that as a tie into desire to improve humanity through fear, questioned as that might be.

                                            Heroes
                                            *Aaaand that's the right angle. It fit well before, but now it's smoother, like there were bubbles underneath the surface I wasn't aware of that got popped.

                                            List of things I don't like
                                            -----------------------------------
                                            Opening Pitch
                                            *What's with this "Being devoured/chosen by the Beast/your Big Brother Or Sister" stuff? You have an expansive Soul that draws from the soul of the world and of humanity as a whole, you can have wisdom from your Homecoming without divorcing your Soul from yourself. Plus you can parody the Chosen Ones narrative a little, and somewhat mirror Heroes more(parallels are great!). I mean, it does tie into the family frame, but still.

                                            Overview
                                            *Too much humanity, not enough monstrosity(?). You wanna sell me on Beasts bringing the Family home and become better teachers-by-fear, sure, but let's keep the ugliness of the monstrous family in view.

                                            Hunger
                                            *Okay, confusion. The Horrors call on the Beast to teach humanity through fear, and then says it doesn't care and will eat anything wholly, regardless of if that thing survives to get wisdom or not. Pick a party line, Horrors-are you teachers or eaters?

                                            List of things I'm torn on
                                            -----------------------------------
                                            Opening Pitch
                                            *On the one hand, I liked the entitlement of Beasts. It felt real and risky to me-it rang true that a Beast has a sense of....not quite entitlement, but freedom to claim things for themselves now. It also really fed into their need to defy the cycle. On the other hand, the monstrous mentor thing is really cool to me too, and it's one of my preferred archetypes, but I have a harder time buying into the need to deviate from the cycle. I dunno, I like the 'altruism', but I also like the brutal liberation. Hmm.

                                            THings that I just want to comment on without assigning a qualifier on.
                                            --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                            The Horror
                                            *I could dig the idea of Horrors as a sort of incubator for the Beast's Soul. It expands the personal mythology of Beasts a bit, going from Beasts, to Unfettered, Rampant, Incarnates to the Horrors to the Dark Mother.

                                            Overall Impression
                                            ------------------------------
                                            The first version thrilled me. This one does...less so, but at the same time it is a wiser approach. It isn't as sharp edged, but it is still interesting. Color me cautiously optimistic about the changes.
