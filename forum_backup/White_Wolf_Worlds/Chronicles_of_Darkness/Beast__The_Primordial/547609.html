    Beast: Makara

    06-09-2015, 06:48 PM
    As much as I love me some Lovecraftian Dagon/Deep One/Kraken sea monster awesomeness, I'm not quite feeling where Makara can fit outside of a watery themed character. I see the Giants as those who feared powerlessness, the Lurkers are those who feared hunters in their dark dreams who do what their title says, Namtaru is the fear of revulsion or being revolting to others, of creepy-crawlies etc and Ugallu is the fear of exposure (literal and secrets of theirs being exposed)...

    I'm not sure where the Makara fit in outside of a fear of drowning... I thought about the drowning perhaps being someone fearing drowning in problems thrown their way, though I thought the Giants fear of power being taken covered that. I know the fear of water is a legitimate thing, though I'm struggling to link such a Beast who had this nightmare into a character concept.

    I love the Ugallu and I can think of concepts for all the Beast Families, though all I can think of for a Makara is someone who feared being swallowed up and drowned by their life, though that could easily be a Giant, personified into a Eshmaki or others.

    Long story short, I'm not really grokking the Makara compared to the others... Can anyone tell me how they may have their recurring Nightmare before Homecoming and not have it linked to literal water phobia?

                                      Makara can also be about other things.

                                      Being pulled away from the familiar by strong currents until you are too far out to swim home. Being trapped in a claustrophobic space (you are buried alive! You are in a van that just went off a bridge and is now sitting on the bottom of the river and slowly filling with water! You are crawling through a cave but it just keeps getting smaller and smaller and oh god you're stuck!) also fit into their wheelhouse.

                                      It's not always about the water. It's about what the water represents: being out of your element. Water is scary because you can't breathe and everything else swims faster than you and you can't hear very well and it requires constant exertion simply to stay where you are. Similarly hostile environments (outer space, frozen tundras, deserts, deep caves, etc) can induce terrors that could inspire unusual Makara. It's not the fear of powerlessness or helplessness, it's the fear of finding yourself in an alien world whose workings you don't understand and which is indifferent or actively hostile to your well being. Hell, you could play a Makara that embodies the nightmare of mean-girls style high school social bullshit who preys on freshmen and it would fit.

                                      So I'm making God-Kicking Boot, an Exalted webcomic, now. Updates on Sundays. Full-color, mediocre but slowly improving art. It's a thing.
