    Anakim Tyrant + Collector: The wisdom he he wants to share is simple: raw supernatural power doesn't make you a giant among men. It's the capacity to get back up after taking a beating and learn from your mistakes that separates you from the common folk. Case in point, he works as a martial arts instructor for anyone who is willing to suffer through his demanding but rewarding methods. He watches his pupils closely for signs of both physical and psychological weakness, and hones them into warriors. Not a single one of his pupils have been used to mop the floor when faced with an opponent who relies on supernatural tricks to impose their will on others.

    But that is not enough to satisfy his dual Hunger. He holds a secret tournament inside his arena Lair. Only supernaturals and mortals in the know are allowed to enter. Those who make it to the finals get to fight the instructor himself in his Horror form, a towering multi-armed ogre with a single piercing eye that knows your next attack before you do. Those who lose must give an object they worked extremely hard to obtain, though he prefers supernatural weapons that some think are enough to beat him. Those who win are allowed to pick anything from his hoard and have their faces carved on the walls of fame inside his Lair. All who participate later enjoy a feast cooked by the instructor himself, so they can restore their energy and share what they have learned. He uses this feast as an opportunity to bring people together and create new Family Ties. After all, all warriors are his Kin.

    Atavisms: Looming Presence, Cyclopean Strength, Relentless Hunter, Needs Must, Unbreakable

    Nightmares: Behold My True Form, You Cannot Kill It, Your Tools Betray You

        Makara Nemesis: She lives in the woods. Some say she is a secretly a witch, and they're not too far off. Choosing to make her home there wasn't a whim. Hungry and alien shades lurk just beyond the sight of mortals, and the barrier that separates their realm from ours is weaker in the places that are farther away from civilization. She acts as a diplomat and law enforcer for both realms. To mortals, she is a guardian of esoteric laws and verges of spiritual activity, always on the lookout for those who would disrespect or desecrate them. To the spirits, she is a rogue element who knows their names and weaknesses, and is more than willing to put her knowledge to use in banishing them should their predation go too far.

        The lesson she wants to teach is that people have become too prone to dismissing evidence of the unseen on their lives. She wants people to know that the gods and fiends are all too real, and teach them the difference between superstition and the true laws of the other side. This duty she imposed upon herself attracted the attention of the local Uratha, who have come to see her as a useful if strange ally.

        When not protecting the balance between the realms, she makes a modest living by selling fruit, wood carvings and remedies she growns in her own garden. She does take in visitors, and likes it when people listen to her retellings of folklore that most have forgotten. Just be careful when you ask her about the Fetishes. She does sell them, but God help you if you misuse them.

        Her Horror is a humanoid tree covered in lurid carvings of powerful spirits. Occult items of bone, leather and stone dangle from its many limbs, each one a Fetish given to her by werewolves whose hunts she assisted. The bark is pitch-black, save for the eyes, which glows like twin moons that emit a ghastly mist.

        Atavisms: Witchwood, Monster From The Deep, Unbreakable, Looming Presence, Eye Of Heaven

                    Ugallu Ravager: Susan used to be normal. Just another small-town girl with lower-than average grades looking for a way in the world. She dreamed, though. In her dreams, she ran across an endless plain, grass as far as the eye could see, with a firestorm chasing at her heels. Then, one night on the way back from remedial classes, she saw Adam, a transfer from someplace no-one had heard of getting beaten on by a bunch of the steroid-using 'jocks'. Except that didn't last long. Adam was usually a nice, quiet kid, but this time he'd had enough. Eyes burning, he grabbed one of the jocks and sent him flying, before lightning peeled away his skin and revealed the sewn-together horror beneath. Two of the jocks spent the next few moths in a body-cast and another never made it back home. He didn't notice her, though, and she went to sleep that night with her mind whirling.
                    When the flames came, though, this time she didn't run. She faced them and raged with fury to match them. The two blazes joined and merged, blazing all the higher for their union, and when she awoke again it was with of fire and storm in her soul.
                    The next day she went to the camper where Adam was staying. She talked to him and he told her about what he was, a stitched-together man made from corpses and lightning, with a soul that burned too hot. So she showed him the fire in her soul, and within the week they were gone together, on their way to the next town over with a pocket full of cash.

                    Atavisms: Dragonfire, Storm-Lashed, Untouchable
                    Nightmares: Run Away, You Deserve This

                    Lair: Extreme Heat, Burning

                    Susan Brand's Lair has two Chambers, besides the Heart. The Heart of her Lair is the same burning plain that she once ran across, while the other two Chambers are the farm of a farmer who Adam once worked for and who tried to kill him once the Disquiet set in, while the other is her old house, which she occasionally uses to return home.

                    Horror: Susan's Horror is a formless, blazing mass of fire and the gale-winds it kicks up, almost indistinguishable from the fires of the Lair itself. Sometimes faces of ravenous beasts or claws stretch out of the conflagration. Ultimately, her Horror is more a force than it is a creature, and it is more than happy to blaze its way out of her soul and into the world.

                                    ​Anakim Tyrant

                                    He sits across the desk from you. He's leaning back in his seat, one foot resting on the other knee, fingers steepled, his expression relaxed and mildly curious. "Tell me why you think you deserve to keep your job." Some people sit in horror of this modern-day king, stammering out limp excuses, trying to appeal to the better nature that doesn't exist. These people are fired on the spot, dismissed with a mocking, "Have a nice day." Some others manage to work up the courage to respond, pointing to their accomplishment, fighting for the right to keep getting paychecks. These people get to keep their jobs- for now. Sooner or later, the company will have to make tough decisions, and he'll be the one sitting in judgement of the unlucky. And they'll have to prove themselves again- or suffer the consequences.

                                    He teaches people that they have to be their own heroes. Nobody is coming to save you, and power cannot be appealed to, only defied.

                                    His horror is a hulking ogre, cunning and cruel, holding court over a massively-scaled castle made of skeletons, filled with doors only he is strong enough to open, and traps that only he is clever enough to avoid.

                                        This was inspired by the fairies from Jonathan Strange and Mr. Norrel, as well as their realm of Lost Hope.

                                        Makara Nemesis: His Horror takes the form of an ethereal fae lord and his Lair is the ruins of an ancient mansion, every flat surface bedecked with candles whose flames burn in impossible colours while faerie dancers twirl and waltz across the cracked floors and through the mirrors which bedeck the place, dancing between reality and reflections with no distinction made. Invaders to the Lair are swept into the dance, passed from one dancer to another in an endless waltz. Trying to strike out at one of them yields nothing, as no matter what is done they placidly twirl away from the assault.
                                        In the real world, he's a lawyer who gets brought in when contracts and deals are transgressed against. He's the one that makes sure that those who break their word never do so again, no matter their reasons or motivations for doing so. It might be childish, but he's always believed that a promise given is a promise true, and such covenants should be kept. He holds himself to the same standards, after all, so he can't be called a hypocrite. When he gets really hungry, though, he'll take one of the oath-breakers into one of his mirrors, and he'll leave them there, trapped in a reflection-realm until they agree to whatever he asks them to, in order to get out. Then he watches them, and if they break their word, he takes them again, this time into his Lair, and they join the dance, never to leave.

                                        Atavisms: Alien Allure, Through the Looking Glass*

                                        Nightmares: You Cannot Run, You Made a Deal**

                                        Lair: Currents, Mirages

                                        *
                                        Through the Looking Glass (Ugallu)

                                        Reflections are everywhere in the modern world. Windows, video screens, puddles after rainfall, everywhere. People spend years looking into mirrors, making sure that their face is just right, that they are ‘beautiful’. People trust mirrors, because when you look into one, you know that what looks back at you is just a reflection.
                                        Except when it’s not. When you look into a mirror and something other looks back. When all of those reflections are the thing’s eyes and every mirror is a door that it can step through. When that happens, where can you hide?

                                        Dice Pool: None

                                        Action: None

                                        Normal Effect: The Beast can step into any reflective surface which is large enough to fit him, entering a world which such Beasts call Mirrorspace. Mirrorspace itself is completely black and empty, the only points of light being the Beast himself and other reflective surfaces, which appear as ‘windows’ looking out into the real world. These ‘windows’ are in the same position as they would be in real life (a puddle is on the floor, a bathroom mirror hangs suspended in the air at head height) and show what they would reflect in the real world (the mirror would show a bathroom, the puddle the sky). While the Beast is looking out of a mirror, his reflection appears in its real-world counterpart.
                                        The Beast can only enter and exit Mirrorspace through the same reflection that he used to enter it and always knows the direction and distance to that reflection. Each Beast enters their own Mirrorspace and never encounters another Beast within it. Items can be brought into Mirrorspace so long as the beast is holding them. Items can be left in Mirrorspace but only last a number of days equal to the Beast’s (Lair + Composure) before vanishing. It is unknown where these items go. The exception to this is within the Lair, where items can remain within a mirror for as long as desired by the Beast.
                                        Beasts can only exit Mirrorspace via the same reflection through which they entered and should that reflection be destroyed the Beast is immediately ejected from Mirrorspace and is subject to the Stunned tilt. A Beast inside Mirrorspace can smash one of the 'windows', destroying its real-life counterpart.

                                        Low Satiety: As the Beast's hunger grows, he becomes able to access aspects of the Mirrorspace that he otherwise could not. In this case, he gains the ability to exit Mirrorspace through any reflection big enough to fit him. He still has to travel to the mirror physically but is no longer ejected if the point of entry is destroyed.

                                        Satiety Expenditure: With the expenditure of a dot of Satiety, the Beast can bring another with them into Mirrorspace, leaving them trapped there until the Beast retrieves them. Mortals in Mirrorspace do not need food, drink or sleep and do not age. However, after a number of years equal to the Beast's Lair at the time when the victim was trapped, the victim is ejected from Mirrorspace into a random world. They could end up in the mortal world, Arcadia, the Shadow, the Hedge or any other world.


                                        **

                                        You Made a Deal (Changeling or True Fae Kinship)

                                        You made that bargain, and now it hangs around your neck like a chain. You know what will happen if you try to break your word. You know how you'll suffer.

                                        Dicepool: Manipulation + Satiety vs Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance

                                        Normal Satiety: This Nightmare can only be activated immediately after the intended victim has made a promise, deal or vow, although it need not be with the Beast himself. When activating this Nightmare, activate another simultaneously. The second Nightmare will hang over the victim's head like the proverbial Sword of Damocles for (Lair + Satiety) days, taking effect if the victim attempts to go against the letter of his word. The Nightmare will only take effect the first time the victim tries to transgress against the bargain.

                                        High Satiety: The Beast is automatically made aware when the victim tries to break his word.

                                        Satiety Expenditure: For every dot of Satiety spent, the Nightmare 'attached' to this one gains another 'charge', meaning that if three dots are expended, the Nightmare will take effect the first, second, third and fourth times that the victim attempts to break his word.

                                        Exceptional Success: On an Exceptional Success, the victim must roll Resolve + Composure to attempt to transgress against the bargain, as he gains a bone-deep certainty that if he does so, he will suffer some horrific retribution.

                                            Makara Predator: "Dude, you've got to relax, just go with the flow..." it's a motto that he's all too familiar with preaching to others. For a vicious, blood thirsty monster, he's shockingly laid back and calm; preferring to take life as it comes and not get worked up about the details. Ironically, this Leviathan works as a lifeguard, soaking up the sun's rays amidst white sandy beaches and cool, rippling waves. Life is good for the care free monster of the deep, though not so much for his feeding victims.

                                            He is the unseen terror of the deep. The one you don't even notice until it's right beside you brushing against one of your extremities. His horror takes the form of an enormous jellyfish, it's translucent body nearly invisible beneath the water as it drifts amidst the currents. It's toxin filled tentacles lazily reaching out for a victim they can ensnare before the feeding commences. He doesn't particularly care who he feeds on, just as long as they're satisfying. Perhaps it is because of this lax attitude that he's been known to spawn the occasional Heroes from nearly any background.

                                                    Makara Tyrant: Turns out old nightmares can learn new tricks. It's actually really embarrassing for him. All the other Beasts are things from ancient mythology, monsters that have been hunting forever, werewolves and simurghs and gashadokuros.

                                                    He's definitely sure he's never seen another Horror that has a copyright on it.

                                                    But, this is where our young Leviathan stands. His soul is an aboleth, straight from the pages of the Monster Manual. It has a certain number hit points and can cast a so many spells per day and gives you a nice amount of experience points if you kill it. Maybe that's why he seems to draw all the really nerdy Heroes to him, the ones who don't think of themselves as Beowulf or Odysseus but as Drizzt Do'Urden or Harry Potter.

                                                    But he'll show them. They'll have to find him first, get past the little cult he's been gradually building in a sleepy coastal town, uncover layer after layer of deception and rumor, venture down into the perilous sea caves, fight his ichthyous Kin, and dive, deep, deeper than their lungs can bear, and find, what, exactly?

                                                    Oh, he'll show them.
