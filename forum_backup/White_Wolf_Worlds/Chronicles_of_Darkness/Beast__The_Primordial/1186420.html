    Favourite Satiety Conditions

    02-15-2018, 05:40 PM
    the absolute high's and low's are blatantly debilitating...

    if your almost starving your horror wildly lashes out at the world.

    If your Horror's in slumbber you lose all your power's though can take a lul from feeding (Must be handy when tqaking a sabbatical from feeding because of a dangerous Hero)


    The less extreme versions arent quite as debilitating... each giving a boon and bane.

    Sated is middle ground is dangerous if you fear Heroe's placing Anathema on you, but otherwise it's good with no special bane or boon.

Not wanting to risk to drop to Ravenous, so you don't accidently attack your newborn child in their dreams. Plenty of IC reasons that make it an interesting option, imo. Not ideal, but preferable for a given amount of time.
