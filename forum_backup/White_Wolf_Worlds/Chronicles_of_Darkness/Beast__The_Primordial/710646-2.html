            10-29-2015, 01:31 AM
            p.54
            THE VIEW FROM WITHOUT:
            JULIA PHOENIX, GALATEID
            . . .The woman turned and exhaled fire. And I saw — I promise, I saw — the dragon, superimposed on her, mighty and terrible . . .
            This may be nitpicky, but isn't the expression usually "I swear", not "I promise"?

            P.59

            In the Micah, Ogre Earthbones sidebar:

            But you know what? I asked her, "you never take them? Never take them away."
            The punctuation doesn’t make sense in this line. Either you have a stray question mark after "what", or you're missing quote marks around "But you know what?"

            They too are dream-walkers steeped in legend, and Seemings and Families share some similarities.
            You might need commas around "too". You can argue the point, but generally that's how that phrase is set up.

            p.60

            Possible typo in the legend of Tiamat. Shouldn't it read, "Marduk, jealous son"? And not "Marduk, jealous sun)?

            P.61

            That whisper is the reason a Beast gets up in the morning, or carries on when her brood is slain and an ax-happy Hero prepares his coup de grâce.
            Got a typo here - "Axe" has an "e" in it

            A sated Beast takes the pain that threw her victim’s life into a spiral of self-hatred and sorrow and says I can do better. She tortures the world until it feeds her again.
            Another judgment call. Should there be a comma before "I can do better" (and maybe quote marks around it, too)?

            P. 63

            Gossamer’s Hunger is a brilliant spectrum. . . .

            The people love him. For decades, Vikram’s fellow citizens . . .

            Luka’s a Predator. . .

            They say that internal affairs is for slimeballs. . .

            He has always needed to burn. As a kid, Levin . .
            Up until this point, all the other character blurbs are italicized. I can understand a hesitancy to do so here, since there are so many . . . but doing so really sets them off from the general text (in a good way). Maybe use the alternating spot color blocks (like the other lists) along with italics?

            Also - this is the second place where non-standard pronouns are used for an ambiguously gendered character. "Zie" and "hir", and so on. And, frankly, I had to check multiple sources to verify that you weren't just making them up, because the first dictionaries I pulled didn't have them. Are you sure its a good idea to use such niche terms?

            p.64

            Should the lesson of "Attention" actually be "Pay attention"?

            P.65-66

            As with the "Starving" list, both "Home" and "Family Secrets" have lengthy lists of character blurbs, with no italics or spot color blocks to distinguish them

            p. 66

            Changelings live and breathe fate, just as the Norn does.
            "The Norn" (three Norse goddesses who govern fate) are plural, so it should be "do" not "does"

            p.67

            She is born in blood again and again.
            Another judgment call, but normally you'd find a comma after "blood"

            p. 68

            They Hero longs to enter a Beast’s Lair
            Think you've got another typo - shouldn't it be "The Hero", not "They Hero"?
            Last edited by One Vorlon; 10-29-2015, 01:34 AM. Reason: Missed the Hero typo
