              So, I just did the second proof for Beast (for context, "did the second proof" means "went through the pdf and put in page numbers to replace all the p. XXs"). At this point, the pdf goes to CCP for approvals, and then it'll get finished and, if the patterns holds, sent to Kickstarter backers. They'll have a chance to look through it and see if they catch any mistakes, which then get corrected, and then the whole thing gets put up for sale on PDF and POD.

              When will that happen? My best guess would be sometime in November, but I really can't say for sure.
