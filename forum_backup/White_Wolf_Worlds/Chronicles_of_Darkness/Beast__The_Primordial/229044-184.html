                                        Well, I haven't begun phase two quite yet. I can drop some perspective.

                                        It is worth noting that I have had two times where I had to sit long and hard with the game and figure out if it was justifiable. The first was upon initially reading Hunger, and my conclusion was that, for me, manipulating and side stepping the Hunger was a big part of the game-finding a balance between accepting the Hunger and not being ruled by it.

                                        The second was actually Shadowflame and one his arguments. There was a point when I suddenly thought I didn't have a leg to stand on, around the but* on paragraph 4 here. I just hit that word and I didn't quite know where to go. And that's with having a lot of the book a lot of time before y'all got it. Truth be told, I still think my defense was a little weak around that point, but I also think it's valid given some of the way Mage works(I don't exactly know if the principles I applied from Mage can directly apply to the Astral, but commonalities and such).

                                        My point is that I can really strongly see what people are getting at. It's a dangerous balance Beast goes for, and I'm willing to concede it doesn't toe it perfectly. But I know the people involved were not going for that sort of thing, and in the end my critical reading doesn't end that way. But it's tough for Beast, particularly given the story form it's emulating(How many heroes do we have who only do something after something tragic occurs to them, Spiderman/Batman/Oh, screw it, most of the American Superheroes, and an even larger portion of Western protagonists in general).

                                        I suppose what I'm saying is that it's not ridiculous, but it's also not intended. Beast is supposed to read a different way, and it's tough to make it read that way. My biggest problem, at this moment, is really not that people have the argument, but that people aren't quite reading this in good faith, as it were. It's like they can't they see another side to it**, and that always annoys me (See my fury over "DEMON IS ABOUT ROBOTS!" arguments)

                                        *No, not the ogre's butt, but thanks for asking, Quest For Camelot.

                                        **I'm avoiding the suggestion that some people are deliberately not looking at it, because that's a dick move, but I would be foolish if I didn't mention the possibility as well, so here it is.

                                        Kelly R.S. Steele, Freelance Writer(Feel free to call me Kelly, Arcane, or Arc)
                                        The world is not beautiful, therefore it is.-Keiichi Sigsawa, Kino's Journey
