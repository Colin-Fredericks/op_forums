    Their Lair is a glimmering glass tower rising over a blasted plain/desert. The approach leads one through a cramped, twisting labyrinth of tunnels that also double as dungeons.         Ugallu Predator: The vampires know him all too well, for he counts them among his closest of kin. Nocturnal hunters that awaken with the setting sun, emerging to feed on the blood of their prey. He is not so different from his undead cousins, although perhaps a bit less subtle about it.

        His horror is a chiropteran monstrosity. A man sized bat that walks on two legs, using its needle sharp fangs to pierce its victim's flesh and sip at the blood that pools from their wound. With its attack successfully carried out, it spreads its wings to take to the sky, leaving no footprints with which to track its whereabouts.

        It's lair is like something out of a bad horror film. The moonless night leaves travelers in nearly pitch black darkness. A forest surrounds them in all direction, but the lack of foliage growing from the branches indicate it might be mid autumn, or perhaps winter seasons. There is only one noticeable building in the entire forest, a dilapidated church standing upon a hill. Unfortunately for those who attempt to seek shelter within, the Beast makes its nest inside the bell tower, and they're heading straight for it!

            Originally posted by ajf115 View Post
            Ugallu Ravager: He calls his Horror the Gremlin. Doesn't sound very intimidating does it? Nevertheless, those with secrets to hide fear both him and it. Unlike the Horrors of his boodmates, his is not some kind of dragon or giant of the kind that terrified neolithic cavemen and Sumerian villagers. His is a new fear. That of the ghost in the machine, of your helpers becoming your foes and the devices you trust your secrets to betraying you.

            In the world of flesh he's a hacker, one of the best. He can crack systems in minutes that others spend days chipping away at. He's in high demand from dozens of factions in the city, from the mafia to the police even to supernatural clades and covenants. Others would be long-gone, sacrificed to keep the secret. He's made sure that he knows his employer's secrets though. He keeps dozens of copies of them. Some on hard drives, some tucked away in the dark corners of the web. He uses them as a combination dead-man's switch and Sword of Damocles. They're his insurance, because if they off him he won't be there to stop the programs posting all of those juicy, juicy secrets for the world to see. He gorges on the fear that he causes, the fear of everything coming crashing down around you.

            And all it would take would be the press of a button.
            I just read the playtest version yesterday, and I was thinking up a gremlin-inspired Ugallu Ravager of my own, though she does like handing out Ruin as a form of Punishment. Might as well do some good with her powers.

            ---

            He looked up into the piercing light, trying to spot his assailant. White was all he saw. The ground, the sky. The omnipresent light burning his eyes denied him even the cover of shade. Something was lurking in the empty sky, but he couldn't look up for more than a glimpse, and at most he'd catch a form leaping between... what? The tops of buildings? Trees? White on white was all he saw, and everywhere he reached, he could only feel an endless expanse of polished white stone, without even a horizon to mark where it transitioned to sky.

            "I'm here to teach you a lesson. 'What are you?' CEO?" She grinned at the fearful figure from her perch, "Not anymore. Poisoning the locals' drinking water wasn't a wise move. Saving money isn't any good if the world knows you'd kill their precious children to buy another yacht. That's why I told them."

            He blubbered the usual insincere promises of atonement. This was not something to be forgiven.

            "Nobility? There are no peasants here to serve you, and none of your friends from that club to pat you on the back for being born in the right family. What is a lord, or a count, or a duke if no one follows them?"

            He finally discovered a pillar and began orbiting it by touch, trying to hide in its shadow. She leaped to the top of the short piece of cover, finally providing the darkness he was looking for. It would be no hiding place. He recoiled and ran, staggering in the void, flailing for something to hide behind.

            "A business man? You were born into wealth! The only reason you're rich is because you were handed everything. You may have made calls and badgered people into doing your will, but you never made anything with your own hands. All you ever did with the companies you owned was strip away expenses, including the ones that made a profit in the long run."

            "You can't do this!" He finally protested.

            "Why not? Are you going to sue me? I don't see your army of lawyers. I don't see the judges you bought or the crooked cops you paid to look the other way. Even they don't want anything to do with you, anymore." But they'll get their turn, she thought. "Let's try this from another angle. What do you have? You don't have friends like those lawyers to protect you. No advisors to tell you what to do for your own good or steer you away from your own stupidity. No kind strangers, since everyone knows what you did. Since they froze your accounts, you don't have any money. Those off shore accounts they don't know about? They're mine, now."

            She rushed towards him, bounding between feet and knuckles, and started tearing at his expensive clothes, "No million dollar power suit to awe me into submission, Iron Man. No weapons. No body guards. I've stripped that all away from you. Without the accounts, these cards are just meaningless plastic. Your pocket change won't buy any mercy from me. Without all those illusions of power and wealth, you're just a weak. naked. monkey. All I see is a frail collection of skin and bones... Let's see what, if anything, is left after I strip you of that. My guess? Underneath it all, you're nothing. Look around. Nothing is my field of expertise."

                                        I came up with this character during my Homecoming story I posted up here a while ago. But didn't really flesh out what his hunger was.


                                        Makara Collector: Mysteries both ancient and occult are the forte of this abhorrent creature. Though it is no mage, it has its own breed of dark magics that were never intended to be wielded by mortal hands. At times, the mages have even come to seek its counsel on their own research, an honor that it takes great pride in, despite its attempts to maintain a stoic front about it. Its lair is a city of stone carved into unnatural architecture. The city itself is completely submerged inside cloudy, green water that obscures the vision of anything beyond your immediate surroundings. The water allows for movement along both the X and Y axis, which is required in order to navigate the stone architecture. Clearly this city was not built with idea of humans maintaining residence, but something more alien and complex.


                                        Humans don't have a name for his Horror yet, it is to obscene for it to have been classified as an identifiable species. It's skin is pale green and clammy with sea slime. It walks upon four legs with webbed feet that end in needle sharp claws. An elongated, flexible torso grows up from the center where the four legs join together. Atop the torso is a bulbous head shaped like that of an octopus with solid black eyes and a clacking beak for a mouth. Along the length of the torso is a swarming mass of tentacles so dense that it frequently obscures the body from view. Possessing so many limbs allows it to multitask on a level that no natural human could replicate. Reading dozens of books at a time, concocting multiple scientific formulas, or performing complex rituals that are beyond the limits of human anatomy.

                                                    Eshmaki Ravager: Change is something that everybody fears, even if they don't admit it. Normalcy makes people feel comfortable, and when you introduce something new to the equation, they reflexively become agitated and paranoid. This Beast feeds on that fear, visiting nice, orderly environments and introducing something new, something that did not exist before. And when his victims realize something is out of place, they get scared. Maybe not enough to cause a panic, but enough to keep him satisfied.

                                                    His horror is a chimera, an amalgamation of different species that should not exist together. But unlike the classical Greek interpretation, where the animals are simply merged onto the same body, his parts blend seamlessly together. To an untrained eye, it becomes difficult to tell where one species ends and the other begins, as it exists in a perfectly hybridized state. He appears to have the genetic makeup of a tiger, a gorilla, a crocodile, and a bull.


                                                    His lair is as equally confusing as his horror. An outdoor habitat which blends together environments that should not exist together in the real world. To those not experienced in traversing it, the unnatural landscape becomes disorienting to navigate, much less survive in. How do you know what plants are safe to eat when the plants are taken from over a dozen ecosystems? You think you can hear a pack of wolves howling along the sea bed floor at night. And is that a school of sharks moving through the clouds?
