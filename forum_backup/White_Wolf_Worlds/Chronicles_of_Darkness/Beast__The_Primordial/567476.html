    Beast Ready Made hang out monster

    06-25-2015, 02:55 AM
    Devs are asking us what kind of monster Kin of Beasts we would like to see added in the Read Made Characters.

    The whole list is interesting and I would gladly here what fans think about what monster should join the example Brood made by devs.

    I'm most interested in seeing a mage, as by corebook Awakened are Distant Relatives to Begotten. But on the other hand, sorcerers are only other race that get their existence and powers from Astral Space and it's so intrinsic to them. Also, concept Wisdom is very integral to both of species - mages try to retain their Sofia, beasts are teaching humanity hard lessons of Primordial Wisdom.

    Here is poll where you can vote for them.
