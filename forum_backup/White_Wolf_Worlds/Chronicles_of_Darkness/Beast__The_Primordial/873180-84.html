                                Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post
                                1) Is there a way to make a nice adjacent Tempter? That hunger seems rather hard to make a positive spin out of. I know part of the point of Beast is Monstrousness nature, but getting someone to cheat on their spouse seems rather horrible for a mid tier meal.
                                Enablers who're trying to play out their needs in a positive fashion have the uncomfortable requirement of intimately knowing their victims' boundaries. Setting aside how fucked up that can get, that's a time investment that you might not have.
                                2) Would a Horror that represents the fear of insanity be valid? Something like a Mooneborn Changeling?
                                This would be that "high-concept" thing again — figuring out what your Horror "represents" is the process of Legend-building in a nutshell. You can steer your reputation toward "'madness' is bad news" easily enough, but I would advise against trying to play Lovecraft Lite with the Begotten when the irrationality of the world is so much more the Insatiable's cup of ichor.
                                3) Is it possible to try to intentionally send the Horror into people’s nightmares for it to feed, in the event of it’s hungers either being too impractical at that point in time or simply being against the human parts morality?
                                3.5) Is it practical to do so?
                                Short of getting extremely lucky or having a very particular geographical distance you can arrange for while you're Starving, not really. If your Hunger is impractical it's usually because you're fed beyond the threshold of mere satiation.

                                                Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post
                                                4) Would being with a loyal group of followers count as being within the Minions or Thralls lair traits for Lair Resonance?
                                                I feel with Thralls and Minions the trick there is at what points it stops being a swarm. I reckon, just as an initial judgement, it would depend on the disposition of the group. Minions would need to be small subservient beings, so thinking animals and the like, but it'd be tricky to impose. Same for Thrall, but I think that might be a bit easier to Impose, as the Thrall condition does work.

                                                My own general rule of thumb is this: Don't rely on Thrall or Minions for resonance purposes, the general parameters are very difficult to conceive. If you need something easier to Impose or Resonate with, your best bet is the likes of Poor Light, Jagged etc, as these can be fairly easy to forcibly recreate.
