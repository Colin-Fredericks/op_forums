    Deionscribe I have to admit, I like Dark Walker a lot! Especially considering those vagrant and smuggler character ideas I had... I totally don't get how no one realized something like that was missing.


    Wanted to try something different. Did it work?

    Hivemind (ooo-ooooo)

    Prerequisites: Kinship with Melissidae + Infestation.

    Some Kinships run deeper than others. And some are more scary and threatening than others. A swarm Horror and a Melissid make a horrible couple - the Beast is a Hive unto itself, something the Melissid admires (and he'd prove an interesting and soo useful addition to her own), while she is similar to him in ways even other Beasts are not, and offers ways to quiet the voices and silence their hunger...
    Effect: Hivemind gives the Beast and the Melissid a number of abilities:
    Once Kinship is established with Hivemind, the Melissid and the Begotten are constantly in Swarm Communion - they are connected by a Hive Mind and can share impressions and thought when closer than 100 yards to each other, and count as one target regarding mental abilities like Dominate, Majesty or Nightmares. In those cases, the higher available resistance trait applies. Furthermore, the Beast counts as another Melissid for purposes of her Clan Weakness (cannot be seperated from hive/other Melissids for more than an hour or get increasing penalties for frenzy checks and mental dice pools.) Teamwork between the two enjoys a +2 bonus due to the uncanny synergy they share.

    At oooo, after the Beast ingests one point of Vitae of her Blood and she consumes some of the Beast's, the Melissid can ride alongside the Beast (even over large distances) and see what he sees as if she possessed him for 24 hours. This allows her to use her mental Disciplines through the Begotten.

    With the ooooo version, ingesting the Melissid's Vitae allows the Beast to extend the duration of his Swarm form (Infestation Satiety Expenditure) by one scene per five dots consumed. Sunrise renders that Vitae powerless, but other than that, he could store it for up to 12 hours before making use of it.

                        Is it just me, or are Beasts terribly unsuited to teaching lessons?                                     Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
                                    Is it just me, or are Beasts terribly unsuited to teaching lessons?
                                    The lessons Beasts' culture exhorts them to instill are refinements on the nature of their Hungers that attempts to mitigate the damage they inflict, not literal teaching curricula.

                                    "Fire is hot; do not touch it barehanded or linger long in its company."

                                    "You can replace your possessions, but not your life; drop the relic and flee."

                                    "This house is haunted; do not wake what you cannot put to rest."

                                    "Midhampton's Prince of Broken Glass stalks the night and kills anybody he meets alone at a bus stop; stick together after dark."

                                    These are the sort of lessons the Begotten offer, and the common benefit they provide is that survivors live to spread the word in a manner that influences them personally or society in general, which is a fine alternative to killing every victim and risking the more dangerous vulnerability of the slumber-based cooldown cycle.

                                            Astral Armory (ooooo)

                                            Prerequisites: Beast, .Mage Kinship

                                            There are dreams of fears and monsters, yes. However, there are other dreams, of tools and symbols, gods and archetypes. Kinship with the Astrally inclined mage gives Beast understanding how to interact and manipulate such things.
                                            Effect:
                                            The beast can purchase/quest for the unique dreams of tools and weapons and temporarily drag them to it's layer. Spending a satiety point the beast can manifest such a thing in material world like an Atavism (mortals can't see it).


                                            Astral Familiar (ooo)

                                            Prerequisites: Beast, Mage kinship

                                            Mages of ten use strange and unusual creatures as a familiars. A benevolent sorcerer may grant his friend one of these (Mind 3). Due to Beast's nature, only astral beings can be used this way.
                                            Effect: Beast gains one rank 2 Astral being as a familiar.


                                            Burrows (o+)

                                            Prerequisites: Beast.

                                            Beast can lay ways to the specific Astral realms, like Mars, Polis, etc.
                                            Effect: Choose one destination, for each merit dot, in Astral realms you can reach instantly from the Layer. .


                                            Court of Nightmares (o-ooooo)

                                            Prerequisites: Beast, Spirit Kinship.

                                            Mostly the layers are empty save for the horror itself and any pests generated by the layer. However, some Beasts tend to enjoy company and fill their homes with various beings. Sometimes, the various beings may wonder into the layer on accident and be stuck/settle there. (an example from beast core book would be the vampire friend living in the layer. it didn't go well, don't invite vampires).
                                            Effect: Layer is populated by a number of beings equal to the Combined Rank=2 xMerit dots (e.i level 3 merit can support 2 3rd rank or 6 1st rank beings). The beings generally live in the layer and only leave it when accompanied by the Horror. Otherwise the beings can be anything that could exist in Astral, be it abducted humans, Goetia, ghosts, etc.) Companions has to be either quested for In-Game or payed for 1exp each rank.


                                            Astral Numina (ooo)

                                            Prerequisites: Beast. At least one dot in Allies/Retainers [Astral Beings]

                                            Human mind opens interesting new ways to the Horror.
                                            Effect: Hi.
                                            Buy one numina from the ones used by your friend.



                                            March of Hundred Demons (ooo)

                                            Prerequisites: Beast. Allies/Retainers (or something such) 5

                                            Gathering a great host, the Beast leads a parade of horrors and monsters through the Dreams of mortals and realms of Astral. The march is a grand affair, driving Heroes (and some mortals) completely nuts and spreading nightmares.
                                            Effect: Each participant completely fills energy reserve and looses all but 1 willpower.



                                            QUESTION!: are Astral beings still unable to materialize?
