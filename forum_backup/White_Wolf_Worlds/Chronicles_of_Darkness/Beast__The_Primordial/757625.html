                      Originally posted by Windrave View Post
                      I noticed there was a distinct lack of items that beast can craft that are specific to their splat... did I miss something or is that intentional? (can they use the gear of other supers?)
                      No items. Its not the only game with the lack of items (vampire doesn't either) so its probably intentional. Its simply not thematic for them to have magic items, so it probably didn't come up. Heroes using magic items to slay monsters is thematic (and they kinda do get the magic item, but in the form of hero-powers). Monsters using magic items to slay heroes isn't. There's no reason for there to be magic tiems in BtP.

                      It makes sense for mages to enchant items. Prometheans and demons are built around alchemical/technological aspects, plus the whole holy themes in those games, so it makes sense. Fae use enchanted items in stories all the time. Geist kinda has enchanted items, but their Memento are generally glorified equipment bonuses instead of something like a relic with its own powers. Relics in HtV invoke Warehouse 13 style games. Mummy tombs and relics is a given.

                      For the most part, magic items make sense in most of those games. In Beast, there's no story or legend about primordial monsters using magic items. Their power is the power of the elements, of the fear and chaos. Magic items tend to be seen as the symbol of civilization and humanity. Its just not thematically appropriate here.

                      Also.. I cant recall what the beast aura/blood effects was supposed to be for crossover purposes.
                      Depends on what you mean. Vampires have their Predator's Aura, werewolves their Aspects, and magi their Nimbus. Meanwhile, demons have no aura effect, I haven't heard anything about Prometheans or Changelings getting an aura either. Its possible the big three are the only ones getting an aura that can be used as a weapon.

                      If you mean the effect of vampires drinking Beast blood, and what auras look like? Neither exist in 2e. Auras don't use visual cues like 1e did, and vampires don't suffer from drinking supernatural blood anymore (barring ST calls, of course).

                              A beast would have no problem finding magic items, if they were so inclined- relics, awakened items, ghost fetishes, cursed items, trinkets.

                              If I were to add item crafting, my first response would be to give them access to a new lair trait, or perhaps a merit -cornerstones.

                              Cornerstones manifest as items that can be removed from the beast's lair, they are essentially an additional manifestation of the beast's lair. The beast always knows where it is by just thinking about it.

                              In the beast's possession, it acts as an imposed lair trait.

                              In the possession of someone else, a beast can use it as a conduit to travel to it, like a lair, but only if the item is activated in some way- a ring turned, a sword unsheathed or fed blood, a cup drunk from, etc.

                              Having it in someone else's possession is a two edged sword though, anyone with the cornerstone can use it enter the beast's lair.

                              Additionally, cornerstones are capable of manifesting lair traits- a sword might glow with blinding light, or burn with the burning trait.
