                "Connected to a Hive" means "in the same general geographical location." My Lair can have Chambers that are connected to different Hives, and a Hive is (of course) made up of lots of different Chambers belonging to different Lairs, plus some unclaimed ones.

                Not every Beast has Chambers in more than one Hive, which is why your solution to the Apex Trait problem doesn't always work. If all my Chambers are located in St. Louis, and the Apex of St. Louis is a dick, I can't just collapse my whole Lair and leave town -- or rather, that's a nuclear option/last resort.

                Instead, I can isolate my Lair from the rest of the Hive. This cuts the Apex off from my Lair, so it can't impose an Apex Trait on me, but it also fences in my Horror. If I fall to Ravenous, the Horror can't range as far from my physical location to feed itself, which makes this situation extra fraught (as it's more likely to target people I like, more likely to repeatedly feed on the same person, and generally more likely to attract a Hero.) Still, isolating my Lair can buy me time to either unseat the troublesome Apex or put my RL affairs in order so I can move to a new city -- then I can gradually collapse the old, St. Louis chambers as I claim new ones in my new Hive.

                If I have just one Chamber in St. Louis and the rest are in another Hive, I might decide that the Chamber isn't worth putting up with the Apex Trait and just collapse it, or I might decide I want to hold onto it as a last resort (a backdoor/emergency exit, maybe). I admit I'm not sure if a Beast is allowed to isolate just the one Chamber from its Hive without isolating the whole Lair from all Hives it might be connected to -- my intuition is yes but I don't have time for a close re-read of the RAW.
