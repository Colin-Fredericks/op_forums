    1. I didn't say that the X-splats will be solely based on the other supernaturals. I'm just saying that it would be a "missed opportunity" for the developers to NOT do something special for at least the Kindred, Uratha, and Awakened of the World of Darkness because those three splats are just as numerous as humans (in some cases). If you want to add in the other splats, even though their membership is sorely diminished compared to the main three, then do as you will.
