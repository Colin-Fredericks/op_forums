                From the Outer Darkness [Makara]
                Dice Pool: Strength + Intimidation vs. Stamina + Supernatural Advantage (Normal Effect)
                Action: Instant
                Many Leviathans favor Lairs that either crush trespassers under extreme pressure, or cause them to suffocate from a sudden lack of air to breathe. Such Lairs evoke landscapes ranging from the lightless depths of the ocean floor to even the cold, unforgiving void that is Space. However it manifests, Makara who possess such realms often develop this Atavism as a means to further capitalize on their inhospitable nature.

                Normal Effect: Anyone within a radius of [Lair x5] feet from the Beast are sent crashing to the ground by a wave of intense pressure. They suffer the Knocked Down Tilt, and take lethal damage equal to half the Beast's Lair dots (rounded up).

                Low Satiety: The pressure inflicted by the Atavism becomes so powerful that those laid low by it can barely move an inch. They suffer the Immobilized Tilt, and suffer a penalty equal to the Beast's Lair dots to all Strength + Athletic rolls made to break free.

                Satiety Expenditure: The influence of the Beast's Lair seeps out of the Primordial Dream in a more concentrated manifestation. Unless they somehow escape, anyone trapped within the area will rapidly die from either the heightened pressure or asphyxiation. Spending a point of Satiety inflicts the grave version of the Suffocate Tilt.

                        Here's one I've been tossing around:

                        Long of Limb (Eshmaki)

                        The crushing tentacles of the kraken. The skeleton lord who builds upon himself with the remains of his fallen troops. The demon-clown with the impossibly-long, razor-sharp tongue. Horrors from every nightmare are maestros of grabbing things (or people) that should be out of their grasp. You can never escape the monster's hideous touch.

                        Action: Reflexive

                        Normal Effect: The Beast's reach is increased by ten yards. He can pick up and manipulate nearby objects, as well as make Brawl rolls against foes who think themselves out of harm's way.

                        Low Satiety: The Beast's limbs grow even longer when he is hungry, and deadlier too. In addition to the Normal Effect, the Beast's reach increases by an extra (2 x Lair) yards. Furthermore, when the Beast makes a Brawl roll against someone from across some kind of threshold (such as if the Beast is underwater and grabs someone on land, or lunges out of the shadows into the light), he may immediately perform a grapple move against his victim as if he had already established a grapple. The aboleth drags its prey into the deeps, while the demon-clown just wants to latch on from afar.

                        Satiety Expenditure: The Beast unleashes the true horror (and Horror) of the inescapable truth of his hunger. Increase his reach by another ten yards. When he makes Brawl rolls at a distance, those who cannot see the Horror can only use Wits or Dexterity for Defense. Finally, add the Beast's Lair to all grapple rolls, which are considered an Exceptional Success on three successes instead of five.

                                            OK, I've had a go at writing From the Outer Darkness and I think it works, but the Satiety Expenditure might be too strong. If I could bet some feedback that would be great.

                                            From the Outer Darkness (Makara)

                                            People don’t like to think about space, not really. Oh, they talk about the planets and the stars and how they all orbit each other and how pretty they look, but they never dwell too long on what lies in between. The cold void that is as close to empty as really exists. The human mind cannot comprehend that place. It was not made to handle the sizes involved. Not meant to process the concept of so vast an emptiness.
                                            Except what if it’s not empty? What if something is there, lairing in the voids between stars? What kind of creature could exist there, writhing in the un-light of dead stars?
                                            You know.
                                            You are that creature.
                                            Action: Reflexive, Instant (Satiety Expenditure and slowing breathing or heartbeat.)
                                            Dice Pool:

                                            Normal: the Beast is a creature of the places between worlds, of the dark void where noting moves but it and the solar winds, feeble as they are. The need for air is a foreign to him and the time between meals or beats of his heart is measured in millennia. The Beast may go without food, water or air for a number of years equal to his Stamina before he begins to feel the effects of starvation, thirst or suffocation. The Beast is also immune to the effects of a vacuum beyond suffocation, such as boiling blood.
                                            In addition the Beast may, with an Instant action, slow down his heartbeat enormously, effectively placing venoms and poisons into stasis and allowing them to degrade into their component chemicals. This could also be used to fake death, as the Beast can also stop breathing without any ill effects.

                                            Low Satiety: As the Beast’s hunger grows and the desires of his Horror begin to bleed through into his consciousness, he begins to see the warp and weft of reality and the flaws therein. As the alien mathematics that the Horror performs on instinct filter into his mind, he becomes aware of the places in reality that don’t quite fit and learns how to take advantage of the little gaps and flaws in the world that go unnoticed by almost all.
                                            The Beast is aware of all distortions in space within an area equal to (Lair*10) yards including God-Machine Infrastructure, Avernian Gates, active spells of the Space Arcana, Trods and a Demon’s Bolthole, although he only knows of its existence and location, not its nature.
                                            In addition, the Beast’s Lair is added to his Speed and he may ignore any barrier which is less than the Beast’s Lair feet thick as he steps through the cracks in reality.

                                            Satiety Expenditure: With an exertion of effort the Beast pulls a hypergeometrical equation from the mind of his Horror, one that allows him to not only take advantage of a flaw in space-time, but to alter it to lead where he wants.
                                            The Beast can effectively ‘teleport’ to any location that he has been before or knows the precise coordinates of. These coordinates must be accurate to within at least a metre (so looking up the latitude and longitude of Fort Knox won’t do you any good). This ability cannot transport a Beast to another realm such as the Hedge or the Shadow. It can operate within those realms though.
