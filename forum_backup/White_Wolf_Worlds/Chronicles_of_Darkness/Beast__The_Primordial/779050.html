    Dragonfire Mechanics (Wait, it's Reflexive?)

    12-03-2015, 12:49 PM
    So, uh, is dragonfire supposed to be reflexive? Because the ability to breathe fire as a reflexive action seems... good. I mean, it makes sense that other 'offensive' abilities like Basilisk's Touch or Limb from Limb would be Reflexive given that their mechanical effect is to enhance something that requires an instant action (an attack in hand to hand), but this seems like it might be a bit much. Is this working as intended?

                                                      Originally posted by Hexmeister Vic View Post
                                                      Activating the Power is, BLAMMing someone with it is not. It still uses an attack action, as written in the systems section.
                                                      This line of reasoning works very well for Atavisms such as Gorgon's Touch, which actually specifically states that, "applying the poison requires a touch attack, but activating the Atavism itself is reflexive."

                                                      Step 1: Reflexively make your touch poisonous.
                                                      Step 2: touch someone.

                                                      Both steps are explicitly spelled out in the rules text of the Atavism in question. In the case of Dragonfire, however, there is no step between activating the power and blamming someone with it. Nowhere is there any suggestion that you must reflexively charge your breath in order to then breathe fire. You just breathe fire. The Dragonfire Atavism *is* the attack. Which is reflexive. And counts as a ranged attack with a thrown weapon, and requires a roll of Dex + Athletics - opponent's defense. Yes, all other instances of the ranged attack action require instant actions. This one, because of the way it is written and because it is defined as a reflexive action, does not.

                                                      There are other Atavisms that follow the same pattern as Dragonfire. All of the others are Instant. Compare Dragonfire to Storm-Lashed, for example. Storm-Lashed is Instant, as it should be. It grants you access to a whole bunch of special attack modes and other useful actions, ranging from 'fun things to do with sand and the eyes of your enemies' to 'summon supernaturally violent storm from which you can call down lightning bolts to murder your enemies.' Aside from the passive immunity to weather effects that the Atavism provides, all of the various applications of it are instant actions. As they should be. As dragonfire should be.

                                                      I am not arguing that this is a good thing. I think this is silly and should be fixed -- either before the game goes to print or in its first errata. Yes, granted, no sane GM would ever allow you to do it the way that the rules actually say you can do it. But that doesn't change the fact that the rules are written that way.
