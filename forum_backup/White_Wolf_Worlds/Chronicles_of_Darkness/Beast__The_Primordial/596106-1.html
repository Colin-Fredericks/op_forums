                Only thing I wasn't happy about with Beast was that they didn't have a way to transform into full on monsters in the physical world. When I think of Beast that way, it reminds me of Supernatural or Lost Girl, in which the monsters and mythical creatures look like ordinary humans. The "dragons" in Supernatural were just guys who kidnapped virgins and could burn you with a touch. That's not very appealing to me, it sounds more like a monster flick with a low special effects budget.

                If I could, I would have included a general Atavism dedicated to manifesting the physical traits of the family you're descended from, incurring attribute changes and skill performances that vary by family. At its highest stage of mastery, you fully transform into your monster self!

                                                            Satiety Expenditure: By spending a dot of Satiety, the Beast surrenders to the hive within, and his entire body disintegrates into a mass of insects or spiders, leaving his clothing and worldly possessions behind. The Beast can also condense to attack a single target, inflicting lethal damage each turn equal to his Lair dots.
