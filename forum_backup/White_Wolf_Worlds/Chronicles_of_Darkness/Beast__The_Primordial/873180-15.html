                        Originally posted by Master Aquatosic View Post
                        Do new Beasts instinctively know what Nightmares and Atavisms they have? Or at least instinctively use them at first when they are threatened or hungry for fear?
                        Well atavisms are actually physical. In the case of most Beasts, and probably the majority of atavisms, you'd know your atavisms by just knowing what your Horror is. "I'm going to assume I have rending claws." "Why?" "Well my Horror has freaking surgical scalpels embeded into the pads of it's fingers. So call it a hunch."

                        Your starting nightmares I'd say are sort of representative of your own understanding of fear. So a Beast who was always worried about not being in control might start with "You must Obey" because they understand the terror of not being in control and they can use their connection to the dream to spread it to others they can connect with. Not necessarily instinctive, but I'd say one needs a solid grasp of why that is scary, so they would have kind of an understanding about what they can use based on feeling capable of teaching or reminding others of that fear.

                                        Originally posted by Master Aquatosic View Post

                                        How does that work with an Atavism like Mimir's Wisdom, though? And where do Atavisms bought after Character Creation come from?
                                        You would know things. You might realize that your memory has suddenly improved and that seeing through deception is easy for you now, occasionally you know things you have no possible way of knowing just because you wondered it. If you're low satiety you suddenly know something horrible about anyone you bump into.

                                        Mind you this would also depend on how it's played. Afterall it would be pretty easy to guess that your insights into people lying to you are in some way derived from your Horror if someone lying to you is met with the phantasmal voice of your Horror in your mind whispering, "It lies. You know why it lies. It thinks no one witnessed it, but we know. We always know."

                                        As for where later ones come from. That would depend on how you're playing it again. For example your Horror and you are now feeding much better and have a more significant impact on the world around it which feeds back into the Primordial Dream. Consequently the Horror is affected by it's own legend and how it reverberates in the Primordial Dream and so as your Horror becomes more powerful it evolves in new ways. Suddenly those claws are dripping with some kind of toxin that paralyzes the victims because the nightmares of that thing include people being so scared they couldn't move.

                                        Or maybe you go a different way. Your Horror is this abomination of a spider monster, massive, chitin that looks like it was forged to be used on a particularly durable tank rather than a living creature, it's fangs drip venom so potent they dissolve the very rocks they drip on, it has no eyes yet it can wrap a gnat in webbing from easily over 200 feet and with such precision that not a single strand of webbing is away from the minimal amount of area to wrap the insect, and it doesn't stick to the walls of it's chamber so much as it punctures the rock with it's legs to climb. So you've got that thing and you're not exactly close to it because that thing has been haunting your nightmares since you were 3 and the knowledge that it might actually be what you have in place of a human soul unnerves you a little so you want to keep your distance as much as possible. As a consequence you only started with Needs Must because this thing is always looking for food and since it's part of you then you are now acutely aware of when you walk past lunch. Of course that venom is always dripping from it's maw, so you have Basilisk's Touch as well owing to your ability to draw on the excess venom and distribute it as needed. You feed when you have to then you get into a few bad scuffles, you get screwed up royally after a a fight with a werewolf (who knew those were a thing) you and it are closer owing to it and you being very much in agreement about not wanting to die. The new closeness allows you to draw on the things monsterous chitin to protect yourself from harm as you gain the Unbreakable atavism. You know that sort of thing.


                                        Also to your question on the bridge troll that's called extortion or purchase. You aren't giving anything up and the person is giving it up because they have to in order to advance. At the very least I'd say it would be allowable for lower satiety feeds.
