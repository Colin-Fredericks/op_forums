                                                Originally posted by nalak42 View Post
                                                (Though I'm 99% sure the part where you have to spend satiety for the big effect will still cost satiety.)
                                                What makes you think that? You said it yourself: the effects of Atavisms are about drawing part of the Soul from the Primordial Dream into the Beast's physical body.

                                                They're natural abilities or traits of the monster the Begotten truly is. The normal effect is simply from the portion of the Soul's power that seeps from the Lair and into the Beast's flesh at all times. When the Soul is hungry, it becomes restless and driven. Its power in the Primordial Dream increases, and more bleeds out, sharpening the Atavisms of the Beast in the physical world.

                                                The final effect pulls the majority of strength of the Atavism into the physical world, in a normally very drastic and unsubtle way. The sheer potency involved is evident in how anyone with appropriate sensitivity can perceive the form of the Soul when a Beast activates it, and is why it costs Satiety to do so in the first place.

                                                But in the Lair, the Soul is completely present. The Beast doesn't need to be hungry or to expend their metaphysical fuel to manifest Atavisms. They're already there in their full capacity, as part of the Soul.

                                                    Originally posted by Orphan81 View Post
                                                    Get rid of all the Strawman "They're all horrible people, really!" stuff, and keep the fact they're people who are broken and have been beaten down. People who have been abused, and then by some chance run across a Beast. Finally they decide to stand up for themselves and their fellow humans when faced with this monstrous creature who exists only to create more misery around it..
                                                    And what about all of the people who are naturally at Integrity 4 because they're extremely sociopathic or psychopathic?

                                                    What about the ones who degenerated that low because they committed murders?

                                                    What about all of the bullies or abusers? you know, the people who are flat out called as being likely to become heroes? I mean, Things like Abuse and Supernatural Encounters are unlikely to bring integrity that low-then the abuser is more likely to become a hero than the abusee.

                                                    What about the assholes who sit around blaming everybody but themselves for their problems? The other people called out as being likely to become heroes?

                                                    What about the fact that integrity 4 is Slasher Territory, and as soon as you hit "serial killer" levels of integrity you don't get to play the victim card as an excuse anymore-you get to play the "mad dog who needs to be put down before he hurts someone" card.

                                                    And Heroes do not come from "a chance encounter with a beast" They come from 1: A Beast not feeding long enough for their soul to go rampaging, having the misfortune for their soul, and being unlucky enough to fuck it up, or 2: A Beast fucking up one Nightmare and having the poor luck to do it to a person who is deeply mentally unstable.

                                                    Considering that the Nightmares caused by soul ravaging are, for everybody but a hero, harmless, that means that a hero is not a victim of the Beast, they're a victim of a accident.

                                                    And an other thing: As has been pointed out, their are Beasts who thrive on causing fear, seriously hurt people. And there are the beasts who generate the nightmares that sometimes turn potential serial killers into Heroes.

                                                    THESE ARE NOT THE SAME BEASTS

                                                    The Beasts who deserve to be killed are not the ones who make the hero making nightmares. They are not the ones with heroes coming to kill them(at least at first).

                                                    And heroes can and will kill anyone they perceive as being on the Beast's side.

                                                    So, you have a deeply unstable person, most likely a bully, abuser, sociopath, psychopath, or extreme narcissist, with Serial Killer levels of Integrity, who after having a bad dream, decided to go hunt down a monster. This "monster" is real, but does not deserve death. The person hunting it will kill anyone who gets in their way.

                                                    sounds a fucking lot like a Slasher.

                                                    I think people are getting hung up on the ":but you can lose integrity from abuse/supernatural" and not stopping to think how unlikely it is to get that low from anything but natural degeneration or deliberately doing things that lower integrity.
