                            Originally posted by Hawthorn View Post

                            For me I always try to consider resources when it comes to the God Machine. The largest resource on planet Earth for its purposes is mankind (at current at least). As such it want's a monopoly on its control of mankind. I can imagine this bringing the GM into conflict with say the Exarch's, Maeljin or the True Fae, who want to either usurp control of mankind or predate on them to a unsustainable level.
                            Given the GM's interactions with humanity, I would say that out of all the parties, the GM cares about having control over mankind the least. Exarchs need humanity because what is tyranny if you don't have slaves? Maeljin needs Sin of some sort and humanity seems to be the only ones that can offer that. True Fae rival the God Machine in lack of caring but that's more about plundering what's there over long term planning.

                            GM, as a whole, doesn't seem to care all that much. Sure, having humans operate things like the useful tools that they are is great but they are still tools. If something else comes along or the GM comes up with a project in which requires humanity to be "wiped off the workbench", it has no problems with doing so. The GM has been here longer than humanity has and can work without them.

                            Only reason why the GM hasn't pulled the plug on humanity in my mind is that it's a lynchpin for enough projects that are still being created or maintained to justify it's continued existence.
