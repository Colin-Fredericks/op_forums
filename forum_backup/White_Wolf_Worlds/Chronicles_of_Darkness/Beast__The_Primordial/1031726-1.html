                            Originally posted by K9ine View Post
                            Lovecraft? So were they human like beast, or were they never human, and if so why do they take on human form?
                            At first glance they're almost indistinguishable from Beasts, but that's the Lovecraft angle coming through.

                            They have human forms like Beasts, and were once humans themselves before being consumed by their Moment, their equivelent of a Horror. Unlike the Begotten though, the Insatiable are parasitic and can only manifest in individuals who already have connections to the Primordial Dream but have not become Beasts or Heroes. And, also unlike Beast they have absolutely no vestige of their former humanity after their creation. If a Beast must define their life by having to live with their Hunger, then the Insatiable must define their life as their hunger.

                            The reason they must take human forms is because for reasons lost to time the Primordial Dream rejects then, and they can only gain Satiaety by physically killing creatures and feeding off their fear (mostly of the time.) They have no Lair of their own, an must create shallow Dens in the waking world, but can hyjack the Lair of a Beast so long as they remain alive in order to steal their might an powers (Lair rating and Atavisms.)

                            Their unknown rejection from the Primordial Dream also means they interact with it in strange ways. When they feed, unlike Beasts, the fears of humanity don't get filtered back into the Primordial Dream but this also means they cause virtually no ripples through their predations for Heroes and Beasts to pick up from the Primordial Dream. They do however cause quite the waves in the waking world by disrupting human's connection to the Primordial Dream by an effect called Schism. It's not to different from a Promethean's Disquiet except that it can be greatly benificial to the Insatiable at the cost of alerting canny observer's to it's presence. It also subsides once the Insatiable moves on as the Primordial Dream repairs itself, but the longer they stay and feed in one place the worse it gets.
