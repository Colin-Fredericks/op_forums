    Beast Player's Guide?

    11-26-2017, 08:07 PM
    So i looked around online for a projected release date, but i haven't found anything definitive. Is this thing coming anytime before the next ice age? I check every single day for over a year now, eagerly waiting for this book, but every day I am sad and disappointed. Is this book just on the backburner permanently because it's Beast and nobody cares about Beast? And yes, i saw the image for one of the new Hungers last month, but this book has been in the works for a loooong time and i'm wondering what's up. Usually this far into development, we start seeing more about a book's impending release, and Beast desperately needs more content and stuff to fill in some blanks mechanics and setting-wise.

      It actually just moved into layout in the last couple of weeks, which means the pdf is being assembled. It was stuck in art direction for quite awhile.                       This is actually a reason why I'm glad we don't see too much of the sausages being made. CtL 2e is suffering a lot from David Hill being very open in his development process getting people very excited for it, then he and Onyx Path parted ways less then amicably, and now the actual CtL 2e went in a different (though not bad) direction from what Hill was going to do, so there's a damper on excitement because people liked Hill's work even if Rose and Megann's work is looking pretty good if different.

                      I think paying to see development progress would be really bad, because lots of things happen in development. Good ideas don't work out. People leave projects. Etc. Paying to watch would give a lot of people the idea that they have a say in that process.
