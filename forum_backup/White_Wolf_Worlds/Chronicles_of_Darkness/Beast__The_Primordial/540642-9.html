                            This needs balancing, any ideas?

                            This isn't real!Changeling/Mastigos Kinship)
                            Are you sure this world is real?
                            Roll: Intelligence + Satiety vs. Composure + Supernatural Tolerance
                            Effect: The Victim suffers the Delusion condition, becoming convinced that the world he inhabits is just a simulation/illusion/dream etc., and a nagging feeling that he needs to get out. ST decides what method the Victim feels is the way to freedom from the following list (advance as needed):
                            Escape the area, jump from a high building, harm yourself (Either 1B or 1L), experience an intense sensation for a strong enough 'kick' (e.g.falling, pain, or water), destroy a certain object, convince someone else of the 'Truth', make someone go to a place, perform a certain action at a certain place (e.g. open and close every drawer twice) etc.
                            The Victim can resist obviously dangerous activities like 'jump from a high building' with an uncontested Resolve+Composure roll + Bonus depending on the threat level (kill yourself would yield +5, harm yourself +1). If they fail, they will take actions into the necessary direction. If they don't fail, they will search for a way to do this safely, e.g. get a parachute, have a friend close-by to resuscitate them etc.
                            After having performed the action, the Nightmare ends.
                            High Satiety effect: The Beast chooses what the Victim believes in, and how they believe to be able to escape. In addition, the victim's social rolls are penalized by -3, because to their mind, they do not deal with real people, and treat them accordingly. Futhermore, having performed the action does not end the Nightmare, and the Victim becomes convinced they did something wrong and try again. This could mean escape a larger Area (Building-> Block-> City), experience a stronger sensation (higher, faster), harming themselves again etc.
                            Satiety Expenditure: Dreams have Projections, The Matrix has Agents. Whatever the means, the Victim is convinced that people trying to stop them from leaving are not real, and thus has no qualms in resorting to disproportionate violence. Upon the end of the Nightmare, Breaking Points are rolled as normal. Furthermore, the Victim feels that time is running out: the closer they get to the Nightmare ending, the more desperate and frantic they get to achieve their goal.
                            Exceptional Success: Doing anything that does not advance the goal suffers a -2 Penalty.


                            Need some help with the next one, too
                            This is what you really are (Common)
                            Sometimes, the scariest things are not something in the corner of your eye, or what can be done to you by others. Sometimes, acknowledging yourself, realizing your weaknesses and being unable to deny your flaws is what breaks people. Let's lend them a helping hand, shall we?
                            Roll: Intelligence+ Satiety vs. Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance
                            Effect: The Victim is drawn towards his flaws and feels drained acting against them. Acting according to their Vice yields double the reward, whereas acting according to their Virtue costs one Willpower.
                            High Satiety effect: The Victim acts compulsively whenever faced with a situation where they could sate their Vice. Roll Resolve+Composure-Beast's Lair to determine whether they manage to not give in. This stacks with the Normal Effect.
                            Satiety Expenditure: Anyone an idea for this?
                            Exceptional Success: Same here.

                                In a game I'm helping run (discord-type 'chat' game), the local Begotten in the city have been 'welcomed' into the Vampiric Court, and as a special little bonus, if they get Covenant status, instead of getting vampiric powers, they count as automatically having Family Ties to Vampires of that Covenant. So, I made one for each of the primary Covenants so there's something for players to start with:

                                Your boss brings me my lunch (Invictus Vampire)

                                Don't you know him? He's the one that's always in -those- meetings, isn't he? What does he want with you? What could he possibly need? And what's going to happen to you if you refuse him?
                                Dice Pool: Manipulation + Satiety vs Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance
                                Normal: The victim becomes convinced that the Beast is his superior, either in a named group or organization the Beast knows they have status in, or just in a general uneasy feeling that the Beast has some sway over him. Mechanically, the Beast is treated as having 1 more dot in (mundane) status than the victim in the applicable group for the sake of social benefits and perceived authority, or else the victim simply takes the Leveraged condition applying to the Beast, resolving it normally.
                                High Satiety: The effect of the Beast's apparent authority becomes more direct. In addition to the above, the victim has one fewer door in social maneuvering attempts from the Beast, and will never drop below an Average impression.
                                Satiety Expenditure: By spending Satiety, the Beast may duplicate Supernatural status (and equivalents, like Mantle and Renown), even if the victim knows full well the Beast couldn't have it. For instance, when dealing with a Changeling in the Ash Court, he gains the social benefit of having a dot more of Mantle than the victim (but no other bonus from it).
                                Exceptional Success: The Beast is treated as having 2 more dots than the victim, or else also applies the Guilty condition (as the victim gains the nagging feeling that they've already earned the Beast's ire).


                                Don't abuse your power (Carthian Vampire)

                                They're at the door. They know who you are, and they don't care. They're going to get in, they're going to find you, and they're going to stop you from abusing your power any longer. The only way to stop it is to give it up altogether.
                                Dice Pool: Wits + Satiety vs Composure + Supernatural Tolerance
                                Normal: The victim becomes convinced that he's stood on his title or position for too long, and it's going to bite him in the ass. Any social rolls he makes, whether his own or resisting another's actions, take a -2 penalty if he has higher Status than the other character in the same organization (lacking status altogether doesn't count), and he loses the normal dice bonus from the status as well. This lasts for a full 24 hours.
                                High Satiety: The victim actually effectively loses his status in a specified group for the duration, as he becomes unwilling to pull any strings, go to any meetings, or otherwise make his presence known among the organization.
                                Satiety Expenditure: The victim will levy a favor or other use of his Status for the Beast, becoming convinced that doing so will keep others off his back. This must be something typically possible for his status level, not something that would risk the status long term.
                                Exceptional Success: The effect lasts for a week.


                                What's with this place? (Ordo Dracul Vampire)

                                There's something about this place. What, what was that? It doesn't feel right. It's almost like there's something here. Something wrong. Something… unnatural.
                                Dice Pool: Intelligence + Satiety vs Composure + Supernatural Tolerance
                                Normal: The victim becomes extra-sensitive to the resonance of supernatural locations. Whenever present within a Haunted locale, spiritual nexus, or other “Wyrm's Nest” (as the Ordo Vampires call it), the victim takes the Spooked condition, immediately becoming aware that something's wrong with the place. If present at the time, the Beast becomes inherently aware that the victim has sensed something.
                                High Satiety: The victim takes on the Obsessed condition relating to the location for the duration, gaining the usual benefits for looking into or studying it. It cannot be resolved until after the Nightmare's duration ends.
                                Satiety Expenditure: When sensing the victim's Spooked condition, the Beast may spend a Satiety to gain a flash of empathic intuition regarding the nature of the fear; this effectively provides insight into the nature of the Nest. A Haunt might reveal a flash of the ghost out of the corner of the victim's eye, or the feeling of the chill of the grave, whereas a Mage's Hallow might cause a sensation of inferiority and wrongness about themselves.
                                Exceptional Success: The power lasts for a full 24 hours.


                                You are Unworthy (Lancea et Sanctum Vampire)

                                He's watching you. He knows you're unworthy. You need to prove yourself. You have to show Him that you can reflect His glory.
                                Dice Pool: Manipulation + Satiety vs Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance
                                Normal: The victim becomes filled with a feeling of inadequacy, and they feel as if the Beast is the only one who can help redeem them. For the duration, they suffer a -2 penalty to all actions, UNLESS they're either assisting the Beast in some way, or doing something they know the Beast wants, in which case the penalty instead becomes a +2 bonus.
                                High Satiety: In teamwork actions with the Beast as the leader, the victim's efforts grant the Beast the 8-again quality on their own roll, so long as they've rolled at least a single success.
                                Satiety Expenditure: By spending a Satiety, the victim gains the 8-again benefit on any rolls meant to protect the Beast from some form of harm or hostility, lest their one potential method of redemption be lost.
                                Exceptional Success: The penalty/bonus becomes 3 dice.


                                You're a Monster Too (Circle of the Crone Vampire)

                                What's that working through your blood? It feels primal. Powerful. Monstrous. But… good. Why does it feel so good? What can you possibly do but unleash it?
                                Dice Pool: None, see below
                                Action: Reflexive
                                Normal: The Beast takes a -2 penalty to invoke another Nightmare on the target (who can choose not to resist), but instead of it taking effect, the victim becomes capable of unleashing the Nightmare for the Beast, on a target of their own choosing. Doing so requires them to suffer 1L damage, which manifests as a deep laceration on their body as the 'magic' escapes them. The effect works the same as if the Beast themself had utilized it with the same number of successes originally rolled.
                                High Satiety: The victim unleashes the Nightmare's High Satiety effect.
                                Satiety Expenditure: The victim unleashes the Nightmare's Satiety Expenditure effect, and suffers no damage to do so.
                                Exceptional Success: As per the invoked Nightmare.

                                Tried to expand things a little to let some act as 'buffs' of sorts as the book hinted is possible (but didn't provide any examples of, if I recall).



                                On another note, I've been pondering a Changeling Kinship Nightmare that at Normal Satiety gives you glimpses of their dreams as they see images of you in their sleep (which grants some minor bonuses), at High Satiety gives you direct ingress, and with Satiety Expenditure counts their dreams as part of your Lair for that night, for all intents and purposes. Something I may toy with.
                                Also opens the possibility of then permanently creating a Chamber out of a Dream, though that might be arguably impossible since dreams fade away each morning and all, and might not be in the same place metaphysically speaking otherwise. Still, it could make for an interesting merit or just special system. I suppose I'm still hoping that the player's guide will cover more ways to directly interact with dreams.

                                                    What Satchel said. I quoted the core book in the thread in question in response.


                                                    You cannot look down (Common)
                                                    Man+Satiety vs. Composure+Supernatural Tolerance
                                                    Falling a certain distance kills you, which is why the Body got good at warning you that certain heights are dangerous.
                                                    Normal Effect: Everything that is further down than a floor registers as a fall that would result in death to the Body and Triggers the appropriate Response: the victim suffers from the Acrophobic Condition.

                                                    High Satiety: The victim suffers vertigo as even shallow differences in depth grow before their eyes. The normal effect applies even to the height difference in staircase steps. Futhermore, even the most simple falls (e.g. from a chair) deal at least 1 Bashing damage due to the nightmare (in Addition to whatever the fall would have), regardless of mitigating abilities.

                                                    Satiety Expenditure: Your mind makes it real. When confronted with a height difference that causes fear, every Action other than getting away is penalized by -3 as avoiding potential death is at the victim's mind's Forefront. Upgrade any falling damage (including the 1B of this nightmare when applicable) to 1L.

                                                    Exceptional Success: In Addition, the victim suffers from the Sick tilt every time they have to look down at least 1 floor, for 6-Victim's Resolve turns.

                                                    Acrophobic is basically Agoraphobic, replacing wide open spaces and masses of People with heights.

                                                    You cannot scream (Common)
                                                    Int+Satiety vs. Composure + Supernatural Tolerance
                                                    Everyone knows it: the horrible Moment you want to say something, but can't. Losing the ability to talk is a common horror Scenario in dreams for a reason: we are social animals that have a Need to communicate. Losing the main means of doing so is akin to Mutilation, a deep-rooted fear.
                                                    Normal: Words won't come out as needed or wanted, getting stuck in the victim's throat or staying at the tip of their tongue. Stuttering becomes commonplace. Actions that require speech are penalized by -2.

                                                    High Satiety: Talking becomes incredibly hard, as the victim needs to fight down the Silence. The victim needs to expend one WP to roll for Actions that require speech (e.g. Persuasion). This is in Addition to the normal effect.

                                                    SatExp: The victim loses the ability to talk or produce Sound of any Kind - they suffer the Mute Condition. Roll Resolve+Composure to determine whether this causes a panic attack.

                                                    Exceptional success: Increase the Penalty to -4


                                                    You are Soul-Bound! (Dunno, Acanthus Kinship? Mastigos, maybe?)
                                                    Manipulation+Satiety vs Composure+Supernatural Tolerance
                                                    When it gets hurt, you feel the pain.
                                                    Normal: Half the Damage dealt to the beast, rounded up is also dealt the Victim of the Nightmare, so long as the Victim can see them.

                                                    High Satiety: The Victim recieves any damage the Beast would normally recieve, and the beast takes only half damage instead..

                                                    Satiety Expenditure: The Beast may Chose to bond someone else to the Victim in their place.

                                                    Exceptional Success: for the next [Lair] turns, all damage the Beast (or Placeholder) would recieve is transferred completely to the victim.
