                Conjoined Horror: (ooo to ooooo)

                You did not go through your devouring alone, you have one or more identical siblings who all fell victim to the same Horror simultaneously. Now, your souls are merged as one, taking the form of a great, multi-headed Beast.

                Effects: Your character is part of a set of identical siblings who are all in sync with one another. Not only do they look identical, but they think and act identically, as well. Mechanically, this gives the player access to multiple copies of the same character, allowing them to exist in multiple locations in the physical world at the same time. While the siblings share each other's skills and attributes, they do not automatically share knowledge, unless they have another merit / ability which enables them to do so.

                This merit can only be taken at character creation, and if one of the siblings should happen to die, they permanently lose the benefits of their respective rank in this merit.

                The 3rd dot version of this merit results in identical twins, the fourth dot produces identical triplets, and the fifth dot results in identical quadruplets.

                    Dark Denizens (•-•••••)
                    Prerequisites: Beast
                    Your Lair is not limited to illusory habitants. You have developed an ecosystem capable of sustaining a number of Night Terrors.
                    Effect: For every dot in this Merit, each Chamber in your Lair has 3 dots worth of Rank for Night Terrors. You may choose to have fewer instances with higher Rank, or more instances with lower Rank. Since they are most comfortable in a Chamber that suits their Hunger, they will usually stay there. However, you may force all your Night Terrors to go into the same Chamber by spending 1 Satiety. This lasts for one scene and can be done a number of times per Story equal to [highest between your rating in this Merit and Lair].
                    Drawback: Even the most loyal of Night Terrors will turn against their masters if not properly fed. They require a number of Satiety points every week equal to [dot rating in this Merit] to remain satisfied. This can either be given by the Beast through an act similar to Mother's Kiss, or come from anyone who happens to find their way into the Lair. Should they not receive this, they will escape the Lair like a Horror going Rampant and Feed on people in the material world.

                        Gigantic Weapons (o-ooooo; Style)
                        Prerequisites: Strength >5 OR Cyclopean Strength
                        Others might use swords and hammers, but your ability and strength are heightened to a degree that you can successfuly use weapons other People wouldn't even be able to lift. The weapon has to have markings of classic weaponry - oversized Buster Swords, gigantic Shaolin Spears, proper Clubs etc. qualify, car-doors (or cars) and other improvised weaponry does not.
                        o When using an overdimensioned weapon, you gain Reach.
                        oo Substracts the Targets Stamina by the weapons size/2 rounded up to determine effects like knocked down/stunned....
                        ooo It's not the edge that kills, but the pure force of the impact: sacrifice your defense to ignore (Dots in this style/2 rounded up) Armor for your next attack
                        oooo Making use of the huge size of your weapon in wide Arcs, you can sacrifice your defence for a turn to attack several opponents, as Long as they are within Reach
                        ooooo You can make an All-Out-Attack and Combine it with any of the manouveurs in this style.

                        Alternatively for oo: When attacking another armed fighter, disarm then upon dealing dealing damage higher than their Weaponry. (Especially nice for some dark eras?)

                                            Working on "Family Fighting Styles" (just for fun), based on the Families' main characteristics and Birthrights. I'd like some feedback on the general concepts, this is all very early and basically off the top of my head. But since I love Martial Arts and Beast, I'd like to combine the two (maybe for a Dark Era game. Both Three Kingdoms of Darkness and Fallen Blossoms come to mind as near-ideal settings). Many of these ideas seem stronger than normal styles, which is why I think there'd be higher/additional requirements (certain advanced merits, for example).

                                            Imo, Makara should feature a grappling fighting style - fits with the themes of taking someone out of their depths, chokeholds resemble the Birthright, and punching and being underwater don't mesh well, which is a main association with the Family. So a style adding special maneuvers like joint locks and choke holds, maybe allow them to grapple multiple opponents at once.

                                            Ugallu seem the Ueshiba kind to me: relaxed, hard to hit, don't strike often but always hard, not necessarily faster but always in the right spot to intercept/evade/counterattack, as if they knew you'd try that. After all, their fluff is all about seeing through people, so let's cram this up to Sharingan-levels here =P Stuff like keeping defense against multiple opponents, applying defense against firearms/projectile weapons they are aware of, debuff-inflicting strikes to the head/eyes to neutralize a target...

                                            Namtaru are disgusting, but to me, their most striking physical aspect is outlasting the opponent: in-Family Atavisms like Infestation and Unbreakable make it harder to be killed, Shadowed Soul helps avoid being attacked altogether and Basilisk's Touch is not the weapon of someone that tries to one-hit-K.O. someone. So maybe a Bonus to Defense, Going-all-out granting temporary armor, and debuff-inflicting attacks. I thought what might be an appropriate five-dot skills is the following: After taking a Weaponry hit, the Beast may op to take Bonus damage and a Debuff themselves (like Arm-wreck) to represent a shocking display of defiance and disarm the opponent (block a knife by going full-anime and grapping the hand after the blade has pierced the hand or use a headbutt to the shaft to blow a hammer strike away). In addition to being disarmed, the opponent might have to roll resolve+composure to not freak out, take a Tilt like "Sick" or a penalty to attacking the beast due to sheer freak-out.

                                            Eshmaki are definitly the ambush-type. Skills like additional damage on surprise attacks, spending WP to inflict a condition with a surprise attack, establishing a grapple in a surprise attack allows to follow up immediately with a grapple action as if rolling an exceptional success, spending WP to give a surprise attack the rote quality or similar all seem like a prime choice.

                                            Anakim are all about making you feel helpless. Very Going-All-Out based fighting style, Spending WP to subtract successes from a successfull attack and reduce the opponents defense pool for the rest of the fight/inflict arm wreck or similar conditions/ cause negative penalties. Spending WP to gain 8-again/have an attack apply to multiple opponents/turn Fists into +1Lethal weapons using a Strength+Brawl dicepool.


                                            Please feel free to comment, critizise, explain and point out what I missed/your point of view. All help welcome =)


                                            Edit:
                                            Another idea for Ugallu: ignoring enemy defense for a WP.
                                            For Anakim: a bonus on grapple-ending actions (e.g. Grapple action Push or Throw)

                                                Recently had this idea for a merit for Heroes, thought I'd post it here:

                                                ​Slayer's Insight (OO)
                                                ​Prerequisites: Hero, Must have slain 2 or more Beasts of select Family/Hunger
                                                A Hero's Legend becomes better at imposing itself on certain kinds of Beasts, at the expense of weakening against others. To the Hero though, it is simply that their experience fighting Beasts of a certain kind has allowed them to spot shared weakness or flaws in their enemy.

                                                ​Effect: Upon taking this merit, select a Beast Family or Hunger, when attempting to inflict Anathema on a Beast of that category, the Hero's rolls gain the 9-again quality.
                                                Drawback: When trying to inflict Anathema on Beasts outside that category, the Hero's rolls lose the 10-again quality


                                                ​Note, I'm very foggy on mechanical details, so if someone thinks this should be altered, let me know.

                                                ​==Edit==

                                                ​I changed it from a Three dot to Two dot at the suggestion of Wormwood.
