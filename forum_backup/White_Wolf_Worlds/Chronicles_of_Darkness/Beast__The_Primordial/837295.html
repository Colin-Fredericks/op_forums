    You're Adopted: A Homecoming Story

    02-25-2016, 08:07 PM
    Been thinking about writing this for a couple days and finally worked up the nerve to do it. It's a possible idea I had for a homecoming / devouring origin story. Looking at it now, it turned out a lot shorter than I was expected it to. But hopefully you guys might like it!



    -----------------------------------------------------



    It was supposed to be a moon lit night, but the sky was heavily clouded at this hour, making everything quite dark. Two figures stood outside a large, black iron gate which lead up to an old, three story manor built out of cobble stone. A clear sign that this place had been around for at least a couple centuries, if not longer.


    "You actually live here?" said one of the figures in disbelief, clearly shocked that their companion would pick some place that, at the moment, look like the opening sequence to Tales from the Crypt.


    "Of course not. This is just where me and the others gather to conduct business." the other figure said, opening the gate with a rusty squeak that caused the first one to cringe. "Which, by the way, they're not very happy about this."


    "I thought you said they like meeting new members?" the first figure asked as they slipped through and began making their way up the path towards the manor. The air had become moist and if there were any light sources, it would have revealed a fog to be rolling in.


    "You're not one of us... yet." the second figure said, "And there's a lot of things that can go wrong. That's what they're worried about." they added, their footsteps being disturbingly loud in the otherwise quite night as they approached the door.


    "But you said you'd help me out." the first figure said as the door was pried open with another loud creak and they stepped inside. Their companion nodded in the darkness before giving a sharp clap of their hands.


    The immediate room they had stepped into was suddenly illuminated by wall sconces that lit their candles as if by magic. The sconces were attached to stone pillars arranged in a circle around the center of the room. The second figure stepped between them and was finally illuminated in the light.

    They were a young man, possibly in their early 20's. Tan skin and black hair with bright green eyes. He was a squat man who stood around 5'5 in height, but was pudgy in frame, wearing black jeans, shoes and a hoody. The back of the hoody had a pale green octopus emblazoned upon it, and now that he was in the light, he pulled the hood over his head and turned to face his companion.


    "If we weren't old friends, I'd be running my ass off right now. This is some black magic shit, right here." The other figure said as they slowly approached the circle of pillars and stepped into the light.


    "It's because we're old friends that I'm even doing this for you." the hooded man said, "Now do you remember what we discussed the other day?" he asked as his companion was finally illuminated.


    The second figure was another male who looked to be around the same age as the first. He had white skin with light brown hair and brown eyes. He was a lanky fellow, but was still taller than his companion, measuring 6'2 in height. He was wearing a pair of beige cargo pants with a bright red T-shirt and white velcro shoes.


    "My fear of weakness..." the lanky man said in a tone that indicated shame or regret. They were both standing in the center of the circle now. The tension was so thick that the crackling of the candle flames could be heard, even though that should have been much too faint to notice.


    "A dead end position at a dead end job in a dead end community." the dark clad man said, "You want to escape, but you don't have the motivation... Don't have the strength to go through with it." he gave him a psychological evaluation while his partner just kept staring at the floor.


    "That ends tonight." the dark clad man said firmly, as his partner lifted his gaze to look him in the face. Or at least what wasn't obscured by the shadow of his hoody. "You won't be weak or cowardly anymore."


    The lanky man took a couple deep breathes to steady his mind before looking at his partner and giving a sharp nod. "Let's get this over with." he said apprehensively.

    The dark clad man motioned for him to lay on the floor, which he did. After which, the dark clad man took hold of his partner's head, placing his finger tips around his cranium and gave him a rather unexpected command. He told him to go to sleep!

    Despite the uncomfortable scenario of laying on the floor with another man grabbing his head, he managed to drift off quite easily. Almost artificially so, if he tried to think about it. Soon, however, he began to dream as images and sounds began to form in his lazy mind.


    "Eric, are you there?" The man called out, feeling a bit silly for thinking his friend would be able to converse with him at this moment. So he jumped in surprise when he got a response.


    "I'm here, Alex." an echoing voice spoke from all around him, "though it would be too dangerous to show myself right now." his partner's voice was strangely alien, as if it were speaking to him underwater. "Don't worry, I promise you'll meet the real me later. Right now, we need to have a talk with your greatest nightmare."


    Alex, the lanky man who's dream this was, looked in surprise as the dream around him began to shift and distort. Somebody else was taking control of the dream space, forcefully drawing him towards a location it deemed appropriate!


    "Are you doing that!?" Alex demanded, feeling very spooked at this point as images raced by him like a high speed camera.


    "Be quite and let me concentrate." Eric's voice answered curtly as the dream began to slow down "Ah, here we go... " he added. They were in front of a cave at the foot of a mountain that loomed so high as to pierce the clouds.


    Alex stared into the darkness curiously, wondering why this place was so special. And that's when a bright red eye began to glow from within. An eye that was nearly the size of Alex's entire body! When the second eye appeared, a growl so powerful it vibrated the ground beneath his feet could be heard.


    "NOPE!" Alex shouted as he turned and ran. The thing inside the cave roared so loudly, Alex thought he could feel his teeth shaking before the sounds of thunder boomed around him. It wasn't long before he realized the thunder was the creature's footsteps. It had begun to chase him!


    He didn't dare look behind him, somehow knowing that the moment he did, the creature would catch him. All he could do was run and desperately try to wake up, but he couldn't. Whatever Eric was doing, it was compelling him to remain asleep, even though he was terrified at this point.


    "Eric, wake me up!" Alex shouted, the thundering steps getting louder and more frequent. "You son of a bitch!"


    "You have to face your fear, Alex." Eric's voice said to him matter-of-factly. "I can't hand hold you through this. You have to turn around and face what you've been running from." and with that, his voice faded from the dream. Alex was furious, Eric was just going to let him suffer like this.


    "How am I-" Alex trailed off before clenching his fists and swearing before he skidded to a stop. There was no place left to run now, he was standing at the edge of a cliff, a swift vertical drop into a black abyss.


    Alex didn't know how he knew it, but he recognized the cliff to be a metaphor. If he went back to his normal life, this is how it would be, a dead end with no place to go but down. If he wanted to escape, wanted to take control of his life, he had to turn around. And so, with a great deal of willpower, he turned to face his fear, and his jaw dropped in surprise.

    Standing just five feet away from him was Alex, himself. An exact copy right down to the clothes. It just stood there, staring at him, not making a sound. Once again, the message was clear; all this time, Alex had been running from himself. He took a careful step towards his duplicate, which made no move of its own. Just continued standing there in silence as he took another, then another until he was inches away.


    "Are you... me?" Alex asked, already knowing the answer. The duplicate said nothing, but grinned widely as it knew Alex realized the truth. In the blink of an eye, the duplicate had transformed, becoming a gargantuan figure with rippling muscles, thick body hair, sharp fangs and glowing red eyes. It snatched Alex up with two fingers and plopped him into its mouth, swallowing him whole!


    And then Alex finally awoke, launching himself into a sit up without even having to bend his legs. He was so disoriented, he only half noticed it when somebody used a party blower right next to his face. He turned to look at the face of a teenage girl, holding the party blower clasped between her teeth and smiling at him.


    "Congratulations, you're adopted!" she laughed, before rising to her feet as she had been bending over at the time. Eric stepped in front of Alex with a satisfied grin as his hands were tucked into the pockets of his hoody.


    "Welcome to the family, Alex." Eric said, "Let me introduce you to our siblings..."

      Chapter 2: Meet the Relatives

      ------------------------------------------------------



      Now that Alex was awake and had picked himself off the floor, he was ready for an introduction to the so called family that he had just been adopted into. He could see other shadowy figures standing beyond the pillars, but at the moment, a girl had stepped in to congratulate him.

      The girl was a good deal younger than either Alex or Eric, looking to be in her mid to early teens. She appeared to be a fan of the goth style as indicated by her pasty white skin, pitch black hair, and black makeup. She was also dressed in a black hoody with a matching skirt, striped thigh high socks, fingerless gloves and buckled up boots. She casually tossed the party blower away, no longer finding it amusing. As she turned and walked away, Alex noticed that the back of her hoody displayed the image of a dark purple bird on the back of it. He wasn't exactly a bird watcher, but if he had to guess, he'd say it was the image of a raven.



      "That playful girl is Veronica, our youngest sibling." Eric said as the goth girl was now leaning against one of the pillars with her arms crossed and one foot pressing against the stone. Soon, another figure emerged from the shadows.



      Once again, Alex saw that they were wearing hooded garments and began to realize this was going to be a running theme. But unlike Alex and Veronica who dressed mostly normal, this new one did not appreciate being noticeable.

      They stood with a hunched posture wearing a full length robe with the hood drawn over their head to obscure them almost completely in shadow. Only a pair of withered hands emerged from the sleeves, one grasping hold of a cane to steady themselves. As they hobbled into the circle, Alex noticed that upon the back of the robes was the image of a turtle floating peacefully inside a pond.



      "And this sweet, old lady, is Granny Turtle." Eric introduced as the feeble old woman moved her head underneath the robe, giving the impression that she was looking upwards. She moved one of her withered hands and began pinching Alex's cheek, making him wince.



      "You're a big boy, aren't you?" she said in a cheerful, though shaky tone, "Don't worry, Granny Turtle will make you extra treats to fill you up." she laughed before releasing Alex and hobbling towards another of the pillars to lean her weary body against it.



      The third and final figure didn't seem to enjoy stepping out of the shadows. Eric had to beckon him with a wave of his hand, promising him that it was alright. With a sigh of frustration, they finally stepped into the candle light.



      "And this is Mathew, the middle child." Eric introduced as Alex looked him over. If he thought Veronica was gothic, she had nothing on Mathew.



      Mathew looked to be in his late teens, only a few years behind Eric and Alex. His skin was practically albino white and his jet black hair was long enough to reach his collar bone. Like the others, he wore a black hooded garment, and also wore a pair of studded leather pants, steel toed boots and biker gloves which had steel plates on the knuckles. He had piercings in both ears, his nose and his right eyebrow and Alex could swear he was wearing black mascara around his blue eyes.



      "He doesn't like being out of the shadows." Eric teased, to which Mathew promptly told him to fuck off before turning around and making his way over towards one of the pillars as quickly as he could.



      Alex had expected Mathew's hoody to show another depiction of an animal, but instead it showed a rotted, claw tipped hand reaching out from a set of busted floor boards with two yellow eyes shining in the darkness behind it.



      "And then there's me, Eric." his long time friend finally said, as if he needed an introduction. "I'm the head of this family, which is why when I told them I'd be adding you to it, they didn't put up much resistance." he explained.



      "Hey, he's a giant, isn't he? That means we've got the full set!" Veronica exclaimed cheerfully. Alex noticed she was practically giddy at this point, like she might fly away if she didn't reel it in.



      "Full set?" Alex repeated, eliciting a laugh from Granny Turtle as she placed both hands on her cane and rapped it on the floor twice. Alex didn't know what the gesture meant, but clearly something had both Veronica and the grandmother excited.



      "Well you've met our meat suits, now it's time you saw who we really are!" Eric declared before clapping his hands sharply. The candles lighting the room were snuffed out by a stale wind as the floor inside the circle began to glow with a pale blue light.


      Alex watched in amazement at what was happening as he and the others began to sink through the floor. It was unbelievable, Eric really did know magic; or at least something close to it!



      "This is gonna be awesome!" Veronica squealed as all five of them were swallowed completely by the glowing floor which soon after dimmed and faded, leaving the room in total darkness. Meanwhile the five people were emerging into another room by rising out of the floor.



      The first thing that Alex noticed as his head poke through the portal was that the room seemed very familiar to the one they had just left, and yet it was far more attractive. The room looked like it belonged to some kind of Victorian era castle with gothic chandeliers hanging overhead, lit by candles that gave off a sickly green light.

      The walls of the room were also painted with pale, acid green coloration except for at the bottom which seemed to be made of fresh cobblestone, as did the floor. The floor was decorated with purple carpeting with gold embroidery while pictures of nightmarish figures lined the walls.



      "This is our lair." Eric said as they finished emerging, "Invites only, private property." he added with a chuckle that indicated he was only half joking.



      Aside from the room change, Alex immediately began to noticed something else. Namely the fact that he was feeling very claustrophobic. His head pressed against the ceiling in such a way he had to crane his neck downward, chin pressed against his chest even as he was sitting on the ground. Looking over his body, he saw the thick body hair covering his massive hands and arms as he struggled to adjust his posture, feeling his back ache against the strain.



      "Whoops, looks like big brother may be a little too big!" Veronica's voice chirped out. "We need to change the scale for him, Eric!" she said.



      "Shit, you're right. He's no good like that." Eric's voice agreed, but this time it had the same alien, watery sound to it that it had from Alex's dream. Before he could question it, however, he felt the pressure around him decrease as the walls and ceiling expanded to accommodate the new occupant.



      Prior to the change, the room was already impressive in size, as befits a castle. But now, the room seemed monolithic in scale. When Alex moved to stand up, he measured almost 30 feet tall and nearly 10 feet wide from shoulder to shoulder. At this posture, he was able to notice that the thick body hair covering him was more like a coat of fur than actual hair. The only parts of him that weren't covered were his palms, the bottom of his feet, his ears, nose, lips and eyes. He also noticed that he was completely naked, but thankfully for him, the fur he had was thick enough to hide anything he might wish to otherwise cover up.



      "I look like a damn Sasquatch!" Alex declared, his booming voice reverberating off the walls louder than he had intended it to. But as he looked towards the others, he noticed that much like him, they were no longer human. Some even looked more freakish than Alex had become!



      Veronica still possessed the head and torso of her human form, but her arms and legs had been replaced with those of a bird, having been scaled to proportion with her human body. Her upper body was naked, so as to not impede the use of her wings, but her lower body continued to wear the skirt, which Alex noticed she also had a set of tail feathers resting over the waist band.

      Veronica flapped her wings and took to the air, perching on Alex's shoulder and holding one of the wings up in front of Alex's eyes so he could see the razor's edge of her feathers.



      "I'm a Harpy." she told him, "I'm much more dangerous than I look." she warned before she started to preen her wings, which at this distance looked like she was licking an arm full of daggers!



      In another circumstance, Alex may have been focused on a bare chested bird girl perching on his shoulder, but he wanted to see what happened to the others and twisted himself to get a better look at them.

      His sight turned to Granny Turtle who had literally become a turtle, or at least something close to it. She was a green skinned humanoid with webbed digits and a sharp pointed beak. A fattened turtle shell obscured her entire torso while a metal cap rested upon her cranium. Like Alex, she too was completely naked, but her shell served to cover up anything that she might need clothing for.



      "Granny's a Kappa." Veronica explained, "She'll rip your soul out your ass... I've seen her do it!" she added with a giggle.



      "Don't go giving your brother the wrong impression of Granny, child!" the old turtle said in a warning tone, moving one eye to look at Veronica out of the corner of her vision.



      "Sorry, Granny!" Veronica quickly apologized, momentarily losing the childish humor she had displayed so far.


      Next Alex turned to face Mathew, hoping he was still visible and then immediately wished he wasn't! Mathew was more than freakish, he was appalling. Alex was almost offended to look upon him!


      Mathew's skin was light faded purple with sickly green splotches all over it. His skin was withered like rotting flesh. Boney claws protruded from the tips of his fingers and toes while his glowing yellow eyes drew attention to his blackened teeth within his blood caked mouth, a pair of long fangs visible when he opened it wide enough. Mathew was also the first member of the family to wear decent clothing, as his outfit was mostly the same from his human form, although now covered in greasy filth.


      Alex looked away to see the final member of the family's true forms. Eric, who was by far the most alien of them all, even more unexpected than Mathew had been.


      There in front of Alex, stood a creature who walked on four legs, with a human torso emerging from the center of them. The human torso had no arms, only a huge bundle of tentacles so massive, Alex couldn't even count all of them before he lost his place and had to start over. Eric's head was bulbous and his eyes were solid black like those of a squid. His entire body writhed and squished as he steadied himself on those four webbed feet.


      "Are you a Great Old One?" Alex questioned in a dead serious tone of voice. He didn't know what Eric was capable of at this point, nor was he entirely sure he knew what he had just signed up for.



      "I suppose that depends your point of view." Eric replied in his watery voice. "Technically, I'm not much older than you are." he stated before seeming to turn around and beckon him to follow.



      "We'll give you a tour of the place and then help you create a chamber for yourself." he offered, "It's time you make yourself at home!" and with that, Veronica took flight again, landing on the floor as she, Mathew and Granny Turtle began to follow Eric out of the room. Not wanting to be left behind, Alex proceeded to go with them, trying not to make too much noise with his massive feet.

          Chapter 3: The Lair


          ----------------------------------------


          As Alex was lead through the family's lair, it was clear that Eric held the most sway over this place. The dimensions of the castle seemed to stretch and contort to his whim, expanding themselves to fit their new arrival as Alex was larger than previously accounted for. The sickly pale green of the candle light was the major source of illumination as they passed through the halls with yet more portraits of hideous creatures lining the walls. Alex took a wild guess and assumed they were either distant relatives he had yet to meet.


          "You see, Alex," his tentacled friend spoke up as they rounded a corner to come to a thick iron door, "All monsters have a lair where we can be ourselves. Some of us live on our own, but others like to acquire roommates." he paused as Veronica approached the door and nimbly pushed it open with one of her taloned feet. There was an intense flash of light that momentarily blinded Alex.


          As his vision began to clear, they were no longer standing in the dimly lit hallway, but now they were outside, standing on the peak of a mountain with a very shear drop! Winds howled around them as the ground crumbled and gave loose at random intervals.


          "Welcome to my room!" the harpy said, taking flight and circling over head. Alex watched as she soared through the air and came to perch atop a stone outcrop with a giant bird nest balancing on top of it. With a couple flaps of her wings, she landed softly within it, giving a playful kaw before folding her wings to her side.


          "The lair is more than just a home, " Eric continued, "It is a piece of our soul, custom tailored to serve our needs. We take security extremely serious here." the tone in his voice sounded almost threatening to Alex, as if to say that they would not tolerate him putting the rest of the family in jeopardy.


          "In the ancient tongue, Veronica is an Ugallu, a terror of the skies and a relative to Dragons. As you can see, her chamber is modeled after steep mountain sides and unstable cliffs. Designed to prey on acrophobia for those who don't like being stranded in high places." Eric mused.


          The ground suddenly decided it couldn't support Alex's weight and started to crumble away, nearly plummeting the Sasquatch to his death, but there was another bright flash and he found himself tumbling onto the cobble stones of the hallway they had previously been in. Veronica was gently closing the door behind them as they continued on their tour.


          "This feels like the longest dream I've ever had." Alex said, his deep voice still sounding foreign to him. "I keep thinking I'm going to wake up in bed with the sun beaming in my face as I'm late for work. How is any of this even possible?" they were coming upon another door, this time Granny Turtle approaching it.


          "The old legends are real, dearie." she said, "In fact, you're one of them now. One of the big, bad monsters that used to gobble up misbehaved children." She began cackling as she pried open the iron door with strength that she should not have possessed. Once again there was a flash and the scenery changed.


          Instead of the castle halls, they now stood at the edge of a clear lake. All but Granny stayed out of the waters, while the kappa proceeded to wade through them and sink beneath the lake. Her shell gently breaching the surface was the only indication she was there, as her monstrous features were concealed deeper beneath.


          "The old tongue calls her kind Eshmaki, a terror of destruction and relative to Lurkers." Eric began to explain once more, "They like to ambush their prey when they least expect it. I wouldn't recommend swimming in Granny's pond without her permission."


          As the turtle woman vanished completely beneath the water, a flash of light had them all back in the dim hallways just as she was closing the door behind them. Alex was thankful that this time nothing had tried to kill him before he was ejected from the room. He turned to face Mathew, knowing he would be next if the pattern continued to hold true. The festering ghoul of a boy making him squint in disgust.

          Sure enough, they would come to yet another iron door, but this one was slightly different. It had chains and padlocks stretched across in order to bar easy access. Mathew approached the door and touched it as the locks popped themselves open with ease and the chains slid out of place to allow the door to creak open, a flash of light indicating another change in scenery.


          "In the old tongue," Eric began, "Mathew is a Namtaru, a terror of revulsion. He's closely related to the Gorgon, as you can tell by his unpleasant disposition." he explained, once again prompting his younger brother to curse at him.


          The scenery of this room was the most disturbing thing Alex had seen all night. It was a gore pit with mangled bodies scattered around them. He didn't see any candles, but somehow the room was illuminated with bright red lights. Naked corpses of men and women were either stacked into piles, impaled on spikes, hanging from the ceiling or nailed to the wall, their blood painting the walls, floors and ceiling in a grotesque display.


          "Don't worry, they're just part of the scenery." Veronica said as she poked one of the corpses with a sharp feathered wing, "Mathew didn't actually kill all these people." she laughed. But even knowing that Mathew wasn't some sort of mass murderer, the illusion was still very real to Alex's perceptions.


          Alex watched as Mathew approached one of the bodies and sliced its stomach open with a claw with the same ease as if he were opening a zipper. Just as its organs began to spill out of the corpse, a flash of light returned them all to the hallway, the locks and chains fastening themselves back into place to bar access once more.


          "And now we come to my chamber." Eric said, almost sounding excited to show off his personal room to his old friend. Personally, Alex would have been happy with anything that wasn't House of a Thousand Corpses, but then he looked at Eric and realized it was probably going to be even worse.



          Unlike the other siblings, Eric's door was more grandiose. A heavy set of double doors carved out of stone waited for them at the end of the long hallway. The doors were carved with the image of an octopus which really didn't seem to resemble Eric at all. He was definitely more alien than sea creature. As Eric approached the doors and forced them open with the same ease as if they were weightless, a flash of light signaled yet another change in scenery.


          "In the old tongue, I am Makara, a terror of the depths and relative to the Kraken." Eric's voice sounded as the light was taking longer to fade than the others. When it did, Alex was presented with a wondrous sight. They were floating over a sunken city made out of stone which was now coated in a thick layer of deep sea algae.

          Alex could just make out carvings in the stone depicting ancient stories and alien beings, but the algae was obscuring most of the details. As Alex gazed around, he had just enough time to spot a large stone altar in the middle of the city's ruins. Unlike the rest of the stonework, it was in pristine condition, to the point where it may have even been brand new. The carved octopus on the sides embedded with black jewels in the eyes, as if seeming to glare at Alex. But as his sight fell upon it, the flash of light came once more and they were ejected from the chamber.

          "And at last, we come to you." Eric said calmly, the stone doors shutting behind him as the rest of the family members turned to look at Alex, making him interested in what was going on.

          "In the old tongue, you are Anakim, a terror of powerlessness and a relative to Giants." Eric told him, "You've seen our private chambers, now it's time we built one for you." the others began to close in around the hairy behemoth as he watched in fascination.

          Alex felt that it was rather silly for him to be scared of the monsters circling around him, especially since he was one of them now and was easily 5 or 6 times their size. The nervousness he felt at this moment was fear of the unknown. All of this was completely new for Alex, he had never experienced anything like this before. So a bit of nervous curiosity was to be expected, given the circumstances.

          "Your chamber will be a reflection of your inner self. It will compliment your inner horror. Don't try to think about it, just feel within yourself, your soul will do the rest." Eric said. There was a light that seemed to be building around them, but Alex couldn't tell its source.

          Alex closed his eyes as he tried to calm himself down, trying to feel his inner horror, like Eric had told him. Even through closed eyes, the light was becoming more intense, forming him to squint his eye lids tighter together as he waited for something to happen. Much to his relief, he did feel something emerging from within him, a power he had never felt before.

          The light around them became nearly blinding as Alex inhaled a deep breath with his mighty lungs and exhaled slowly. The light faded and Alex opened his eyes to reveal they were standing in front of the cave where Alex had first encountered his inner beast. But the scenery was a little different. This time, they were surrounded by tall trees, in what looked to be some kind of forest, or woodland area. The sound of chirping birds could be heard in the distance, which managed to grab Veronica's attention.


          "Really playing on the whole Big Foot motif." Mathew said, his voice more guttural than Alex had expected. Alex hadn't planned on creating a forest, and yet somehow it felt right. Like he belonged in the middle of the wilderness.


          "This feels... natural." Alex said, "A place to kick back and enjoy the scenery. No work, no bills, no responsibilities!" he could get used to this place. He turned to face the cave which was at the foot of the mountain and realized that if he climbed it, he would have been able to see the entire forest from that vantage point.


          And so, with a great heave of his body, he leaped onto the side of the mountain and began to climb...

              Epilogue:


              -------------------------------------------


              It has been three weeks since Alex was adopted into Eric's Brood, and he had been adjusting quickly to this new life. He had spent the first week sprucing up his chamber in the community Lair they all shared. Eventually he learned how to make it seem more primal and savage, a place where those who lived a pampered life could not survive unless they learned to fend for themselves. By the time he was done, he had changed the peaceful forest, into a savage jungle, and he was the apex predator of his environment.


              The second week was spent teaching him how to hunt. Alex would discover that the Begotten (what he had become) needed to feed on the fears of others to sustain themselves. He sampled a variety of different fears, but the one that satisfied his hunger the most was Tyranny. All his life, he believed that if he just did what he was told and behaved himself, everything would work out for him in the end. Only in the last couple of weeks did he realize how utterly wrong he was. He was sick and tired of being the powerless, following peoples orders just because it was easier to obey. From now on, he was going to live as he pleased, and anybody who had a problem with it would have to beat him into submission!


              The third week with the family, Alex spent associating with his new relatives. He began to explore the lair more and even became desensitized about the whole modesty concept. Out of the entire brood, only Mathew wore a full set of clothing in his true form. Veronica seemed happy to remain topless, while Alex, Eric and Granny Turtle were completely nude. Although Granny had her shell, Alex had his thick fur, and Eric... well he didn't look like he could wear clothes if he wanted to.


              He also got to know his family in more depth than just their initial mannerisms. Eric was the leader of the Brood because he seemed to be the smartest among them. When he spoke, the rest of the family listened to his words. But while Eric may have been the leader, Alex saw signs that he wasn't strictly the most powerful. Veronica and Mathew seemed very dedicated to remain on Granny Turtle's good side. Any sign that they were aggravating her, and they quickly changed their attitudes. Alex didn't know what had them so scared of the little, old lady, but he decided it was best not to get a first hand experience.


              He decided to ask them once, but the only reply he got out of Veronica was "she'll slap the crap out of you!" Alex would later do some online research and discover that Kappa were supposed to be expert sumo wrestlers...


              Veronica and Mathew were polar opposites in their personality. The Harpy was fun loving, energetic and outgoing, while the Ghoul (as Alex had no other way to describe him) was dark, brooding and a social recluse. The only time he regularly showed himself was during family meetings, or when he went out for a hunt. Otherwise, he would lock himself in his room for hours and it took a great deal of effort to coax him out.


              Mathew was not entirely comfortable being addressed as a "ghoul" the first time Alex called him it. When asked why, he said that the vampires kind of ruined it for him, which caused Alex to quirk a brow in confusion. But unfortunately that was as talkative as Mathew got, before he decided to drop the subject.


              So now that Alex was starting his fourth week, the Brood had once again gathered themselves at the creepy-as-fuck house for another important meeting. This time, Alex would not be escorted by Eric. He had come here enough times to be able to let himself in if he needed to. As he rapped his knuckles on the door, he opened it to reveal the others standing inside the circle of pillars dressed in their black outfits, the ones that bore the symbols of their true forms on their backsides. Slowly making his way to join them, Alex watched as Eric gestured with his hand and Granny Turtle reached under her robes.


              "It's been almost a month since your homecoming." Eric said, attracting the attention of his long time friend. "And you have spent this time learning our ways. You have added your chamber to our lair, you are able to feed yourself, and you have spent time bonding with your new family." with another gesture, Granny Turtle pulled out whatever she had been holding to reveal a black hoody.


              "As leader of this Brood, I present you with this gift, and fully accept you as one of us. Congratulations, Alex." Eric finished as Alex took the coat from Granny. Veronica predictably began to clap and cheer, while Mathew just gave a grunt of acceptance.


              Alex noticed that the hoody was made out of black leather and had some kind of wool or fleece lining on the inside to keep him warm. The coat would also have been heavy to a normal human of his size, but part of becoming a giant had endowed him with supernatural strength, even in his human form. As he turned the coat around to look at it's back, he saw his own symbol emblazoned on the back. It showed the image of a brown furred Sasquatch hoisting a boulder over its head as it snarled in rage. He grinned as he admired the image before slipping the coat on and pulling the hood over his head.


              "Glad to be here... brother!" he said proudly.

              The End
