    [Fix PEACH] Everything you do is worthless Nightmare

    07-11-2017, 07:07 AM
    This Nightmare is really cool on paper, but when used, it's lackluster. Giving "7 successes for Exceptional Success" doesn't convey the idea that what you do is worthless, and it's the basic effect of the Nightmare. It only gives the idea that excellence will be more difficult, but you usually don't need an Exceptional to do something.

    Here's my fix.

    Normal: The Beast chooses a type of action. This can be a trait category (Mental, Physical, Social), a particular field (combat, infiltration, research, etc.), or any other reasonably
    broad category. Within that category of action, the victim’s roll is penalyzed by -2.
    High Satiety: The results of all of the victim’s actions in the chosen category are listless, barely adequate, and uninspired. When the number of successes on a roll matters (for example, in combat or in an extended action), any successful roll is treated as though it scored only a single success. A rolled exceptional success counts as two successes. The victim may reflexively spend 1 Willpower to negate this effect for a single action.
    Satiety Expenditure: As long as the Nightmare is active, the Beast may spend 1 Satiety to reduce the victim to a chance die on a single roll.
    Exceptional Success: In addition, the victim’s threshold for exceptional success on the action to 7 successes.

    What do you think about this?

          Gotta disagree with The Kings Raven here: I've always felt the same about the normal effect, and think penalizing an action by 2 is far from "why bother saving her, she's going to die in the end anyway". I much prefer your version of Everything you do is Worthless!, Jesuncolo.
          +2 is something you could easily end up with for having the right tools/equipment, which would be negated with this, highlighting incompetence. Basically "I have an excellent forge, and still my products are as good as what my neighbour churns out with his outdated, inefficient and broken stuff. I suck as a blacksmith -.-" or "I have superior ingredients and tools, and my dishes taste about as good as instant-noodles. I am a failure as a chef -.-"
          The High Satiety effect has not been changed if I see that correctly (can't check right now). And is still not a "why bother" case imo: you can still save her, it'll just be harder/take longer and there is a higher chance of screwing up the longer the task is.
