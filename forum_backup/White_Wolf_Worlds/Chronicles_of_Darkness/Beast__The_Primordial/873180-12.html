        Originally posted by Taidragon View Post

        There are quite a few differences actually; for a start, as Beasts don't suffer from Iron unless it's an anathema placed on them by a Hero (and even them its only a weakness to particular iron, rather than iron as a whole), they arent Fae like the Gentry and Changelings are. They also don't have a Mask that hides their true selves - they have their human self, which they always had, and their inner Horror, which isn't hidden away so much as on another plane of reality. Same with their fuel; Changelings and, as far as I know, Fae in general can simply draw from emotional energy from a person or that which is in the air itself (provided there's a lot of people around), and though they may have particular tastes that mitigate how much they get, any Fae can get Glamour from any emotion. Beasts, meanwhile, need to get Satiety from terror specifically, and it has to have an impact on others. Further, the more sated a Beast's Horror is, the harder it is to actually get more satiety, whereas Fae aren't limited in this regard in the slightest.

        Regarding the "changing others" aspect, True Fae capture and change a person's very nature, down to their soul, whereas Beasts can either open up the change for someone willing (or potentially unwilling), or it comes naturally to them, with the Horror coming and outright replacing their soul. That's pretty significant, because it's either with consent (if perhaps not knowledge of what one is getting into), or it's just a natural thing like puberty - either way, it needs to have the potential there in order to happen at all. For True Fae...well, let's just say there are a lot of rape analogies that don't take a lot of effort to make when it comes to them.

        Even how they affect dreams is different. A Beast is, well, a thrashing beast inside of a dream. They don't have fine control a Fae does - even the most rudimentary Fae creature can tear a dream apart and put it together into a new configuration, whereas a Beast can only tear it apart (or, at best, make it nightmarish). Beasts are specifically living nightmares, whereas the Gentry have access to all dreaming. A Beast's Horror is near never clever, whereas a True Fae can run circles around geniuses...if with the danger of dizzying themselves, but that's besides the point.

        Really, though True Fae and Beasts have a lot similarities, they're more skin deep than anything else. They just happen to be terrifying creatures wh affect the local dreamscape.
        Quick correction, a horror can be intelligent. It's just really unlikely, but Mimir's wisdom does exist for a reason and it is mentioned that sometimes things like the anakim are terrifying because they appear to have figured you out and you can't think them. (Not the exact words, but it is mentioned briefly in the book.)

                                Originally posted by K9ine View Post
                                Now I'm really confused... So, lets say a antagonistic beast wanted to intrude on your lair. What would happen?
                                It might stalk you until you open a primordial pathway to your lair and try to sneak in while it's open or it might just visciously attack you until you make a primordial path for the "merge with horror" buff and charge into another chamber of your lair. Once in your lair it follows the intruder rules from page 98. The antagonistic beast has to deal with your lair traits, and moving between the chambers takes a point of willpower and an intelligence + Resolve roll contested by your Horror’s Power + Resistance. But if you never leave the door open for the other beast, then they will never get in.
