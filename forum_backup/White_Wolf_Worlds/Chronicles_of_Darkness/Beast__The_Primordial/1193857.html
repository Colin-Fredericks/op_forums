    Skin Deep Modification Ideas

    03-10-2018, 05:44 AM
    So BPG is out, and one of my favourite additions is the Skin Deep Atavism.
    Being able to go physical horror is just too good to pass on, and the versitality of it is intriguing.

    That said, we are presented 7 different possible modifications, and I thought this is the right spot to see if we cannot come up with more =)
    We already have
    - a fall-slowing modification
    - literal eyes in the back of the head (+2 on sight-based rolls)
    - improved grabbing for both grappling and climbing via prehensile limbs
    - tough hide downgrading 2L into 2B each turn
    - speedbuff of Lair+2
    - growing claws, unarmed attacks dealing 1L (+1 to climbing)
    - bite attacks don't need a grapple and deal Lethal damage

    What I came up with (although not sure regarding Balance with some):
    Growing an enlarged or elongated head to support a more powerful brain, +1 dice on Investigation and Science rolls.
    Growing antennas to pick up "vibes", +2 on Empathy and the ability to roll for characters out of sight (e.g. around the corner).
    Temporarily grow larger, gaining 1 HP.
    Growing suckers/spiderhair on the inside of his hands, negating negative modifiers for surfaces or lack of tools when climbing.
    Having eyes turn an unnatural colour (completely black, red, Sith-yellow, eye-stalks coming out etc.) to gain a bonus on Intimidate.
    Growing pins/needles/thorns etc. on the skin, so that each unarmed attack received returns 1 Bashing damage.
    Flesh turns oozy, attacks are more likely to not deal any damage at all. +2 Bonus to Defense.
    Arms grow longer, giving the character the ability to interact with things from 2 yards away.
    Fingers are replaced by tiny hands, whose fingers in turn are replaced by tinier hands. Situations that call for extremely delicate motor control enjoy 9-again.
    Retractable limbs: Character can pull his limbs inside to a certain degree, allowing him to fit into spaces he'd usually couldn't. -1 to size for purposes of deciding whether the character fits or not.
    Camo Skin: +2 on Stealth

    What ideas can you come up with? Let's pool! =D

          • Eyes become larger, with slit or triangular pupils and a shine or faint glow. +2 to sight-based checks in low light or darkness.
          • Skin becomes coated in slime or mucus; attacker takes -2 on any attempt to grapple, Beast gains +2 on a roll to Break Free.
          • Saliva becomes a poison with Toxicity equal to Lair/2 rounded down. Applies Poisoned (Grave) if used in conjunction with a bite attack, or Poisoned (Moderate) if spit.
          • Blood or saliva becomes acidic, dealing 1L damage.
          • All joints become hypermobile, giving the Beast +2 to a grapple role to Break Free and allowing her to squeeze through any opening that will accomodate her head (though if this means e.g. crawling through a pipe, she moves at half her usual Speed).
