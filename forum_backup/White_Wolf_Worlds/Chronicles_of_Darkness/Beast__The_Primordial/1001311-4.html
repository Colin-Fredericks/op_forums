        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
        I am counting the Chamber and its seed as one location for the purposes of "being there" and the likelihood of accomplishing that research without ever encountering the material area is absurdly low in light of how physical navigation is less constrained than the Pathways.
        Fair enough on the first point. I was really only questioning the absoluteness of your previous statements; here you say that it is absurdly low but not impossible, and that's fine by me.

        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
        The fact that it is a random location and declaring a specific degree of connection to your Legend that is not intrinsic to the physical nature of the location is at odds with the idea that you are simply angling for any location analogous to one of your Chambers.

        The point of Chamber resonance is to encourage players to use the things that formed their Chambers as the template for building up a pattern that they can exploit to move across their extended hunting grounds easily. It's a territory-expansion mechanism.

        A character isn't going to blindly reach through the Dream to get to a place they've never been via a hazy connection they have no established awareness of; they're pushing through to a [Chamber type] in [realm] on the basis of there being any [Chamber type] in [realm] at all, and a connection based solely on [Chamber type] is descriptive similarity regardless of whether something went down to form a Chamber in a place they pulled out of a hat through means they have no foreknowledge of.
        I wasn't assuming that the character was declaring a specific degree or anything like that. Only that the players (including the Storyteller) might decide that such a location is what is found and that increases the likelyhood of the successful random jump. This has the benefit of allowing the Storyteller to lead the character where they want them to go with an in-universe justification and also provides the player with a new known location that they can then use in the future. Purely an out of character narrative device that I wanted to justify within the metaphysics of the setting. I think we are on the same page here.

                    Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                    You haven't located a weak spot though. You've located the spot you happen to want to punch though. When you just spread out and probe for a random location you're going to get places with lair traits and good resonances.
                    And good resonances by default key off of things that are connected to your personal Legend and your place in it, which, again, doesn't mesh with a random push the way narrowing your search to "someplace burning" or "somewhere swarming with bugs" does.

                    So even if you add the location penalty, it should never be -5. That would mean that given the choice of any possible location your horror automatically gravitates to the least suitable one it can use at all, which is patently absurd.
                    Because, again, the least suitable one it can use at all is the one you are consciously directing the power of your Lair toward on account of that being the only degree of connection to a random location that you can rely upon.

                    Secondhand Chambers have an explicit shelf life beyond the immediate formation chance and get incorporated through your essentially consuming the story of what went down in a given location, but Beasts don't have an inherent bond with clandestine killer psyche-fractures the way they do, say, slashers — treating the hazy in-absentia echoing of a breaking point roll you were once present for the same as such a repetition where you're actually in the area means that you have to assume the Begotten have a sense for not just the seeds of Chambers but the near-miss opportunities for Chambers to have formed, and that's a degree of awareness that would merit at least as much attention as Family Resemblance just for the sheer quantity of data such a sense would make available.

                    So if the resonance penalty persists on a random jump — and I feel I've made my case for it doing so unless you actually have an argument for why random jumps being more likely to succeed than a jump to a known location with a weak connection is a desirable mechanical and thematic outcome — then it uses the only degree of similarity you can rely upon, because narrative and spiritual similarity call for your presence or at least your knowledge on the basis that Beasts do not have a natural sense for events that replicate the roots of their Chambers that would logically be discernible just by trying to open Pathways from random analogues. You had to be there.
