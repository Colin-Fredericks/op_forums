                                                I put Zuko as a Namtaru, because pretty much everything about his character development fits it perfectly. In addition to having a prominent facial scar, he's looked down upon and ostracized by his own country and both his father and sister have tried to murder him at least once. It also took a while before any of the heroes accepted him into their group, and even longer before they were willing to trust him.

                                                As for Aang being a Beast, I could see him as a Nemesis. The Fire Nation directed large portions of their military assets specifically to hunt down and kill him, because they knew he was a threat to their global conquest. And most people throughout the series treated the Avatar with either reverent faith, or abject fear. Once he was forced into the Avatar state (i.e Incarnate form), most people decided to run in the opposite direction, except for Azula, who was psychotic enough to shoot a demigod in the back.
