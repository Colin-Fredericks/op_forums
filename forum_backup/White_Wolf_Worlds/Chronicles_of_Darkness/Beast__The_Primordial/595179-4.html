                                I guess I'll resurrect this thread. I'm sure there's probably a more recent version, but if so, I overlooked it.

                                I'd like to to recommend "The Devil is a Part-Timer!", which is a series of novels, two mangas, and an anime series. The basic premise is that Devil King Satan escapes to our world after being defeated by the Hero Emilia, only to find himself stuck in human form. His new plan for world domination involves gaining a part-time job at a fast food restaurant and becoming a model employee. This is complicated by the fact that the Hero has followed him, and now stalks him so that she can catch him in an evil act and destroy him. I think this show nicely illustrates the relationship that could exist between a somewhat benevolent Tyrant and a Hero with a conscience.

                                        I'd like to recommend both the graphic novel "I Kill Giants" and the book/movie "A Monster Calls". The latter was already mentioned in this thread, but now I can agree how much pertinent it is.

                                        They both rely quite a bit on the twist at their end (though it's not hard to spot them) so I won't spoil it, but they're both works about kids dealings with the bad side of life and getting through them by coming to terms with it through the harsh lessons and truth carried over by monsters. It's not clear how much is true and how much is immagination at the end, but that's not the point.

                                        What matters is that they're both good and, for what involves us, nice examples of Anakim doing their thing, making people deal with their hopelessness and learn lessons about it.

                                        There are many parallels between the two, so I think I can mention them together.

                                                The manga Majin Tantei Nougami Neuro​ is great inspiration of how to be a Beast and help people without necessarily having a "just" motive. The title character solves murder mysteries, not to help people, but because he literally feeds off the murderous will of a pre-meditated murderer. Heck, the extent Neuro's dedication to his hunger lines up perfectly with a Beast's; at one point it's revealed that his hunger is so strong, he's effectively chosen eating over breathing.

                                                Many of Neuro's powers also resemble nightmares and atavisms, either breaking his opponents brains or revealing the more inhuman features of his body, allowing him to uncover details his opponents have carefully hidden.

                                                ​Neuro would definitely an Ugallu (given how he leaves killers feeling naked and vulnerable) and Ravager (He shows no interest in actually punishing criminals, only in solving the puzzles their crimes make).


                                                ​Meanwhile, the main villain of the series, Sicks, is a perfect example of an Insatiable. Just look at the things the man does, I won't spoil it for anyone who wants to enjoy the series for themselves.
                                                ​WARNING, the anime ended before the manga finished, so the endings are very different.

                                                ==Edit==

                                                ​With the BPG released, I want to add a little to this post:
                                                ​Neuro might also count as a hunger for Whispers (since he reveals a criminals secrets and methods)
                                                ​Also, Yako would definitely be a Herald, especially after what happened during the Kamigiri incident.
