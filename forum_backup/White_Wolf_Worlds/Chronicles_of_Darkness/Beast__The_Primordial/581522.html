                                  Originally posted by Xenesis View Post
                                  Isn't that what the "Piercing" attribute should be specifically for, though?
                                  No, piercing is for things that are part of a broader category that would normally be stopped by armor (such as bullets), but are specially designed to bypass a portion of that armor (e.g. armor piercing bullets). A giant ball of fire (magical or non), bolt of lightning (likewise, magical or non), or a direct assault on the mystical energy holding a person together (probably not non-magical) doesn't "pierce" or "penetrate" armor in the sense that it is more capable of punching through it, it bypasses it because the armor is not designed to protect against such an assault at all.
