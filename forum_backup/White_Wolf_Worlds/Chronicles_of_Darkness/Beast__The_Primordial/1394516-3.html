                        Also, to attempt to explain things that Satchel and Arcane have already laid out in language that I hope is easier to follow: In Chronicles of Darkness there are planes of existence where abstract concepts have tangible manifestations. Ideas exist as things you can interact with, if you have the means to get to them in the Astral.

                        Beasts are, in a very literal sense, various ideas of what A Monster Is/Does/Looks Like. They are the collective nightmares of humanity made real.

                        And because they have a direct and personal connection to the abstract, bedrock concepts of what supernatural monster is to humanity, anything else that humanity defines as supernatural and monstrous is by definition conceptually related or at least adjacent.

                        Put another way, they are capital M Monsters. On a sliding scale of looking at things and putting them into boxes of definitely a monster, maybe a monster, probably not a monster not only are they in the definitely box, they are the definitely box. They are the archetype and the ideal, and the benchmark against others are judged.

                        In idea space this translates to everything that's labeled little m monster being related.

                        Does that help clarify any?
