        True, these factors are among the factual constraints and drawbacks I acknowledge about the True Fae condition. As a matter of fact, coming back closer to our original topic, I see reason to argue the Incarnate Inheritance is a more desirable way of supernatural trascendence than True Faehood, because the former seems more self-directed than the latter, even if the former seems theoretically less vulnerable to destruction. Roughly speaking, I'd rate several 'advanced' WoD supernatural conditions with immortality and substantial supernatural power this way, in order of preferability: Ascension/Archmage > Incarnate Inheritance > Purified/Reborn/Eternal with psychic powers > True Fae > vampire elder > other kinds of Immortals. I know too little about Mummies and Demons to make a reasoned comparison, and I'm not sure if Sin-Eaters ever get a way of their own to get any kind of equivalent condition. So far, I'm honestly challenged to think of any real drawbacks to the uber-mage or uber-beast conditions that aren't based in an appeal to the inherent value of morality, humanism, or empathy with baseline humanity.

        That being said, I disagree that the True Fae are superior simply for raw power and immortality- barring things like Titles, I think it would be better to be a Wyrd 10 Changeling than a True Fae- at least you'd still have your free will.
        A fair point, but then again, my whole argument about this was that True Fae may be seen as preferable to baseline humanity if that gets to be the choice. As far as I can tell, the Gentry are not entirely devoid of free will.
