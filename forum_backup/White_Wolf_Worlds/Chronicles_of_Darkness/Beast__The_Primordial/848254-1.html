                                                        Inheritance isn't an endgame, it's a way out. A Beast could reach an "endgame" by expanding its family and rising above the needs of Hunger. A Beast can grow in power and simply endure what life throws at it, or it can take a way out. That escape is, for all meaningful intents and purposes, false- you let the Monomyth break you and define you, either by descending into the depths of the Primordial Dream or by forcing your Horror into the world. The Unfettered and the Rampart purge their humanity and go "Fine- you say I'm a monster, that I'm not a person? You're right. Let's see how you can handle it"

                                                        The Incarnate does something different. The world tells them they're a monster doomed to die for the benefit of the Hero, and they say "Alright- I'm a monster. Let's see just how terrible a monster I can be.'

                                                        Heroes are not what they seem to be either- for all but those with low Integrity, they genuinely are altruistic and benevolent, seeking out sources of fear and learning what they can from the experience. Low Integrity Heroes buy into the Legend, they can't help but think it's all about them, that the myth exists for their benefit and glory- they see the Legend as an end, and not a means to it. They refuse to learn.

                                                            Because it's not about evil. It's about being a monster.

                                                            Because, unlike Becoming Nightmare, the Inheritances are much more emotionally complex for any given Beast. To Thine Own Self Be True is challenged by the Inheritances, which ask questions about just what is the self of a Beast.

                                                            Because, sometimes, the villain isn't the villain.

                                                            Because, sometimes, even if the villain is the villain, it's a better choice than giving up who you are.

                                                            Because, sometimes, your choices are inevitable, and your destiny is never certain.

                                                            Because, sometimes, a tragedy is the sort of story you want to live, because the alternatives as emptier still.

                                                            Because all the love and kindness in the world doesn't do a damn thing if a person isn't willing to move and change themselves.

                                                            Because the Inheritances aren't the only endgames you have.

                                                            In short, because Beast is more complicated than most people seem to get. Cough.
