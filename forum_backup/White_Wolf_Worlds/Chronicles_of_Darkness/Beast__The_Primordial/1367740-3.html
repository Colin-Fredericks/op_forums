                Replicating something like the 9/11 attack is ridiculously overkill for an example High Satiety event for Hunger for Ruin. In general, you shouldn't be focusing on such extremes for anything you do to feed your hungers. It's just not necessary and actually detrimental towards focusing the game on the local community. As a more reasonable example of a High Satiety Ruin feeding, I'd accept a Beast breaking the stained glass windows of the local prominent church. High profile for the community, attacks something that a lot of the people feel a strong connection to. It would certainly be the talk of the town and cause a reaction from people.

                Regarding Satiety Conditions... A factor that really impresses me with their design is how they really catch Beasts coming and going. None of them are really comfortable states to be in. Not even Slumbering.

                When you're Ravenous, you're out of control with your Hunger, having to take any minor opportunities you see even though they don't help you beyond giving you the will to keep pushing forward just a bit longer, while you're slowly starving to death.

                When you're Starving, you're constantly jonesing for your next fix. You're restless and on edge, encouraging you to be reckless with your behavior. This is sustainable, but an uncomfortable state to be in.

                When you're Sated... You're actually doing pretty decent. Unfortunately, while it's doable to reach this state, it's also rather dangerous for you, opening you up to Anathema from Heroes.

                When you're Gorged, you're now feeling lethargic. Not only are you more suggestible from supernatural effects, but you're more apt to fail tasks because it's just so difficult for you to keep yourself active and engaged with whats going on around you.

                When you're Slumbering... while you're now feeling functionally human... you're also contending with depression on top of losing all the kinds of advantages that help keep you safe from supernatural threats and Heroes.

                And this is pretty deliberate. A Beast looking to minimize the harm they do can try to stay around the Sated area, either focusing on doing many small feedings to bump from Starving back to Sated to balance themselves out, or doing more meaningful, more damaging feedings from a single target each time that lets you last longer between feedings, effectively balancing from Gorged and Sated.

                Now, there is another path to take, but one that requires being more active in the local supernatural community. Taking advantage of Family Dinner and participating in the hunts of other supernatural entities can be a useful way to extend how often your own feedings have to occur. This is also deliberate.

                ....I gotta admit though... I'm really puzzled as to how the monstrosity of the other splats keep getting downplayed whenever the monstrosity of Beasts gets discussed. Like vampires assaulting people for their blood isn't assault, like werewolves chasing and hunting someone down isn't a terrifying experience for them, like leeching off of other people's personal grief isn't an invasion of privacy or creepy as hell. My guess is that it's a factor of a feeling of separation through metaphor, but it's been a consideration in my mind that what these monsters do is always... well, monstrous. I think taking away the human aspects of the behavior that the monsters symbolize takes away from the visceral nature of the base acts.

                Part of what really fascinates me with the Chronicles of Darkness games is there is a real discussion going on about the nature of monstrosity and what can be done with it. Do you use your monstrous nature for your own purposes? Do you try to do some good with it? Do you limit your predation to feel like you're being moral with them?
