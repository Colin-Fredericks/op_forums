                                                            1) Inside the lair is where the traits come from in an overlay, so you can't really turn them off.

                                                            2) Well Apex traits tend to be kind of faint and not necessarily noticed unless one is looking for them. So like Aquastosic says it might be something like if you climb the trees or end up in a clearing you can barely make out a city in the distant or maybe in quiet moments you can hear the noise of traffic, but can't figure out a way to reach them.

                                                            3) I'm guessing you're talking about a Beast imposing the maze trait on an area as opposed to how it would appear if you picked a fight with someone in a house of mirrors or a corn maze. The way I see it the overlay drags dream logic into the world. So it could easily go with either angle of the cube films. Either the person just finds it really easy to get lost for some reason, or when they aren't looking everything shifts around causing them to open a door and arrive at a room that was two doors a left fork in the hall and up a flight of stairs. It would really depend more on the lair than anything else.

                                                            4)Trait immunity primarily works on tilts. As far as a fire goes, unless you open the lair, you would be blinded by smoke not because the smoke stings your eyes but in the same way a shrub blinds a person because it is directly blocking your vision. Sealed exit wouldn't apply unless you pulled an overlay, so if someone locked you in a bank vault you are actually stuck in the vault. That said one the overlay is in place then whatever method your Horror is able to use to bypass sealed exits applies and that the only exit is sealed would no longer prove a threat.
