                                    Originally posted by Aiden View Post
                                    I wonder how many different ways you can make Dragons with the rules here ...?

                                    Or rather, what Atavisms would everyone think were best suited to making a dragon?
                                    Dragonfire and Wings of the Raptor are both classics, of course. Basilisk's Touch is good for the poison-breathers. Relentless Hunter and Unbreakable are good for the invulnerable, while Limb From Limb, Titanic Blow, and Cyclopean Strength provide the raw muscle. Heart Of The Ocean and Monster From The Deep are good for the "sea serpent" type. Looming Presence adds both size and social prowess, Alien Allure and Siren's Treacherous Song make you good at manipulating people, Mimir's Wisdom makes you a cunning dragon...

                                    Okay, to be blunt, there isn't a single Atavism that I think wouldn't work for a dragon. Except maybe Infestation. Even then, it might work for a dragon made out of a lot of tiny dragons, or one whose scales are actually hundreds or scarabs.
