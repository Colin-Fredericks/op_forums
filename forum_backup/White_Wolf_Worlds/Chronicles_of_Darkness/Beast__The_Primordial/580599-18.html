        Feedback is appreciated.

        Lord Of The Corals [Makara]

        Normal Satiety: The Beast may alter the shape, Durability, Size and function of both inert matter and base organisms. This requires a Dexterity + Crafts extended roll, with each roll requiring one turn. The target must be within touching range. When shaping inert matter, each Success can add or subtract one Durability, Equipment Bonus, Size unit or a Skill which benefits from the Equipment Bonus. The total amount of Successes that can be distributed in one scene is equal to Lair x 3. When shaping base organisms, roll Dexterity + Medicine instead, and treat the base organisms as having all the properties of their mundane counterparts. Complex tools and mechanisms are not possible with this level of Satiety.

        Low Satiety: The Beast can not only transform inert matter and base organisms with greater efficiency and potency, but also transform one into the other. The targets may be from a distance of Lair x 5 yards away, and each Success transforms [Lair + 1] units of Size. Complex tools and mechanisms may now be created and dismantled. Median organisms may be created as well.

        Satiety Expenditure: The Beast may now cause inert matter and organisms to shift into each other and lash out violently all around him, creating a deadly zone where the ground erupts in poisonous tentacles and the the enemies’ weapons become swarms of locusts. As an instant action, everything within a Lair x 20 yard radius inflicst a Lair/2 round up penalty to all Physical and Perception rolls for the scene if the Satiety is Normal (full Lair penalty if it’s Low). Everytime a failure is suffered for these rolls within this zone, it is downgraded into a dramatic failure.

                    I wanted to tackle one of the themes that are present in the book, but were not done as an Atavism as far as I know yet, namely the supporting nature Beasts can take. I feel like that could take some additional fleshing out. I thought about making these Merits, but something made me think Nah, Atavisms would be interesting because they would be vastly different from the rest. So, here is an attempt to expand on Mother's Kiss. Please give me feedback on what needs to be changed.



                    All Beast's have access to the Mother's Kiss. Some can do more than that, though: they can nurture their Cousins like Grendel's motherdid with her children, emulating the Dark Mother herself. They push them further, egg them on, and those working with them say they are always just at the right spot, doing just the right thing to help them on, making them real teamplayers.

                    Setting the Table [Common]


                    Roll: Normal Satiety Stamina + Satiety - Supernatural Tolerance; Low Satiety Stamina + Satiety; Satiety Expenditure: Stamina + Lair

                    Normal Satiety: Rather than needing Family Ties, the Beast can apply Mother's Kiss to any willing Supernatural. Furthermore, when supporting a Supernatural in a team effort, the Begotten knows just where to be, how to move, to make them work perfectly in sync: reduce the threshold for exceptional success to 3. If Family Ties are established with the Beast and both within 15 yards of each other, the Target adds 1/2 Lair rounded up to their Defense.

                    Low Satiety: The Horror is eager, impatient, and instead of helping gently, if forces its power on the target, pushing them to succeed so it may feed faster. Ignore Supernatural Tolerance when applying Mother's Kiss, and trigger its exceptional success at 3 dice. Also, the Beast does not have to pay Willpower to use Mother's Kiss, as the Horror steps in to hurry this up. However, since the Horror is not careful in providing its help, the Target pays a price for the help in suffering 1/2 the rolled results in Bashing Damage as strength surges into them violently. The target needs to be willing to recieve this help.
                    In addition, when making use of Family Dinner, the Beast heals Lethal damage by the number of Satiety she gains. If she actively participated in the hunt e.g. via the use of Mother's Kiss, she regains 2 Willpower.

                    Satiety Expenditure: The Beast does not shed blood to support her chosen family, but something much more precious: her Satiety. Every success scored may be allocated by the Target as an Attribute Point for the rest of the Scene. This may take them over their usual limit.
                    For the Rest of the Scene, team efforts with the Beast in it enjoy a +2 Bonus in addition to the Normal Effect.



                    This next one is meant to expand on Family Dinner... but taking it in a different direction


                    Riling up the Pack [Common]
                    Some Beasts see their Cousins and Siblings as too meek, feeling they need to stand up and take what is theirs, even if they need to push them for it.
                    Historically, these Beasts were feared not for their own strength, but for the ferociousness they caused in others. They are walking sparks of chaos, destroying the balance.


                    Roll: none for Normal/Low; Presence + Occult + Lair vs. Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance

                    Normal Satiety: As long as the Beast follows a Supernatural they have no Family Ties with, they gain a +3 Bonus on all actions meant to hide their presence. In addition, if a victim is being hunted in a way that would qualify for Family Dinner within Lair*10 Yards, the Beast is instinctively aware of it, even if she cannot know who hunts whom.

                    Low Satiety: The Horror's hunger is palpable. Other supernaturals within 20*Lair Yards feel it, mistaking it for their own. This can lead to confusion if they just ate, and with the right skills they may be able to realize their sensations are being tampered with, but they cannot trace it's source without a supernatural skill, which causes a Clash of Wills. Only a hunt will satisfy this sensation of hunger, posing a problem for those used to more... refined ways of feeding.

                    Satiety Expenditure: Upon spending Satiety, the Beast can deny nourishment: the blood is dead, the plasm dissipates, the Mana corrupted.
                    The Beast needs to be able to see the Target directly for this to work.


                    Aaaand, finally, Primordial Pathways

                    No Barriers hold Me [Common]
                    Some Beasts are intrinsically familiar with the bounderies of these worlds, able to enter and leave seemingly as they please. They could disappear there today, and reappear under your bed tomorrow...


                    Roll: Wits+Composure+Lair; Occult + Lair vs. Supernatural Tolerance; Occult + Lair vs. Supernatural Tolerance

                    Normal Satiety: The Beast may attempt to locate wether there is a supernatural gateway within Lair*5 Yards. Using a Gateway to open to the Primordial Dream does not cost Willpower. Under the Bed can now be used to leave the Primordial Dream at the target's location.

                    Low Satiety: With the Horror growing hungry, the pathways become easier to access. The Beast can force the Door to simply stay open for [Lair] days. Additionally, it can pull the target of Under the Bed into the Primordial Dream.

                    Satiety Expenditure: When the Horror attempts to open a Gateway, it is fuelled by Willpower. When it is fuelled by Satiety, it doesn't just open the Gateway, it tears down the fabric of it or slams it shut so hard it stays stuck. Unless tended to, the Gateway stays that way.


                    Eh, the latter will be heavily reworked within the week, I think.

                                            Collaboration with ArchonAres

                                            Thousand Eyes
                                            Most monsters have eyes. A certain few, however, have a lot of eyes, placed all over their body, like Argus Panoptes, the greek giant. Others have fewer, but special eyes. They all have one thing in common: they See.

                                            Normal Effect: You can see in all directions at once, and are immune to being blinded.

                                            Low Satiety: Your vision cannot be impeded by physical objects. Effectively, you can spend an instant action to see selectively through materials in the way.

                                            Satiety Expenditure: Pluck an eyeball from your body, and place it somewhere. You may spend satiety, and see remotely through that eye for a scene. The eyeball will last 1 week or until used before drying up. You can place up to [1+Lair] eyeballs like this.


                                            Manyskin
                                            Some Monsters do not come directly at you. They send mundane animals first. You get the feeling that the birds are watching you, that they follow you - and you are right. For they do the Monster's bidding. And they will soon descend on you.This is done by Horrors that prefer indirect action, e.g. the living tree gathering the whispers, the crones in the forest collecting secrets to assert their dominance over the people by striking unexpectedly where it hurts the most, or the leshy that coldly watches on as wolves tear you apart for defiling his territory.

                                            Action: Instant
                                            Dice Pool: Pres+Lair vs Resolve + ST
                                            Dram Fail: The animal becomes immediately hostile to you and attacks or flees if it can.
                                            Fail: The action fails.
                                            Success: Activates as below.
                                            Exceptional Success: The animal gains the Bonded Condition

                                            Normal Effect: Take control of target animal you can see. You can move the animal, and see from it's senses. Your body however, is motionless while you focus on the creature, and you are unaware of what happens around you. The animal will not perform tasks it percieves as dangerous. You may end this effect at any time. You can control animals of up to Lair+1 Size with this atavism, or a number of animals who's total size equals Lair+1.

                                            Low Satiety: The hungry horror doesn't ask animals, it tells. Your horror may force an animal to perform any action, dangerous or not. You are also more aware of your own body during this, though still unable to move without ending the connection.

                                            Sat Expenditure: Your size limit doubles for one scene. The animal(s) may be used as the origin point for your Lair traits. If controlling a single animal, it gains access to 1 nightmare of your choosing. The Beast is able to move without ending the connection.

                                                Hibernation

                                                Some monsters are known for their hibernation cycles, emerging periodically to feast upon humanity before vanishing again for years at a time. These restful periods allow them to conserve their energy and drop off the radar of many Heroes who would see them destroyed.

                                                Normal Effect: The Beast can enter a deep slumber in order to conserve resources and mask its trail. As long as they remain asleep in this fashion, their length of time between satiety depletion is doubled its usual rate. In addition, the Beast's does not impose a subconscious effect on the Primordial Dream, effectively masking them from the instinctive awareness of Heroes.

                                                Low Satiety: Desperate to conserve as much energy as possible, the Beast shuts down all non essential bodily functions. The length of time between satiety depletion is now multiplied by their dots in Lair.

                                                Satiety Expenditure: By spending a point of satiety, the Beast can project their sense of sight and sound outside of their body in order to observe the physical world while they sleep. Such an ability has been instrumental for many such Beasts, who wish to keep themselves updated on what is happening in the world while their bodies rest.

                                                    Heart of the Land (Makara):

                                                    Like the ancient precursors of the ancient dinosaurs, some leviathans crawled out of their watery homes and adapted to survive on dry land. There, they discovered a new kind of depth in which to call home, the depths of the wild!

                                                    Mechanically speaking, Heart of the Land is a remodeled version of Heart of the Ocean. Instead of water, the Beast is able to travel through soil at double their usual speed, rapidly burrowing through the earth as though it were water. Like many subterranean creatures, the Beast always seems to have a fresh pocket of air while underground, ensuring that they do not suffocate from being buried alive. Their burrowing also deposits loose soil, which quickly fills in the tunnels behind them and hides the evidence of their activities from prying investigators.

                                                    When experiencing the effects of low Satiety, the Beast enters a state of communion with the land itself. This communion is born of instinct, rather than spiritualism, but the effects are the same as those for Heart of the Ocean. The Beast gains a preternatural awareness of nearby prey, being able to sense their location and movements through subtle messages in the environment. This awareness does not rely purely on the sense of sight and, as such, it is not fooled by tricks that might evade the naked eye. However, this awareness is not omniscient; the Beast doesn't know the location of everybody in the area. Rather, their awareness is laser focused on those who would make obvious targets for feeding their Satiety.

                                                    Instead of watery brine corroding the environment, spending a point of satiety causes the environment to succumb to the ravages of nature. Patches of dirt seem to manifest out of nowhere and quickly give way to creeping plant life as vines and mosses spread across the area. Like a blade of grass breaking through concrete, the plants and soil gradually break down the surrounding area, the effects of which continue to persist even after the Beast leaves the area.
