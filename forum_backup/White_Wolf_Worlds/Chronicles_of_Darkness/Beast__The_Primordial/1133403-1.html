                Why would Princesses and Demons fight? Why wouldn't they fight? Beats spread fear, Princesses fight fear. Beasts encourage their kin to be more monsterous, Princesses encourage monsters to be more humane. Beasts teach lessons drawn from the primordial, Princesses teach lessons drawn from civilisation. A Begotten and a Noble are like two politicians from rival political parties, they disagree on most things, and the things they agree on are even more reason to fight.

                In battle Beasts should favour Nightmares, a Princess' magic is emotional in nature and so Nightmares that attack her emotional stability or self-confidence can be devistating. In a physical bout Princesses usually focus on the simple but scarily effective tactic of manifesting a magical sword or wand of fireballs while pumping up their attributes and skills to superhuman levels. Unless a Beast has some way to land a blow on an opponent with a base Defence of ten (which even applies to guns) or more he'll need some kind of edge. Lair Traits like Cramped or Darkness can even the odds.


                Genisues are mad. When a Beast encourages a vampire or a werewolf to indulge his monsterous nature he knows what he's going to get. But who knows what will happen when a Genius abandons self-restraint and gives into his Inspiration? A Beast may be perfectly comfortable encouraging a bit of medical research on orphans only to realise that they've pushed their kin to brew appocolyptic bio-weapons in the basement and now somebody has to take them down quickly. For a Genius' part there's infinite reasons to oppose a Beast once you stop limiting yourself to sane justifications. What does family and friendship matter when your equations prove a Beast is a net harm to humanity? An encouragement to delve deeper implies disagreement with your current actions, and when you alone are smart enough to see the truth the only people who could disagree with you are lesser minds driven to jealousy and spite by your brilliance. Or perhaps the Genius just wants to cut the horror open and document what he finds for science.

                Geniuses embody everything a Beast is not. Their powers come from science and modinanity, however warped. Their insanity derives not from the primordial lizard brain, but from glitches in the brains highest functions: logic, pattern matching, creativity. Consiquentually a Genius can be a devistating threat to a Beast, they are perhaps the only Splat who turn a Lair from an advantage into a weakness. A Genius who builds a nuclear bomb in his basement cannot actually use it without making enemies of everyone, including more powerful geniuses who're quite willing to hop into the time machine and erase his crimes from history. But a detonating that bomb inside a Lair won't attract much attention. An experienced Genius with money and a few weeks of lab time to prepare appropriate tools is an almost unbeatable foe (unless their own insanity trips them up).

                A Beast's best bet then, is to catch their opponent outside their 50 foot attack robot. Without their gadgets a Genius is just a very smart yet crazy human. Chances are the Beast could beat them in a fist fight even without Atavisms. Just about any Avavism or Lair Trait could be useful, what matters is if the Genius has prepared for it and built a specific device to counter it; not what the power does.


                Leviathans are demigods. Nobody wins by unfurling their claws, flairing their crest, and picking a fight with Cthulhu. Even a 0 exp Leviathan could have Claws that do seven aggravated damage and a bullet proof armoured hide. At their apex a Leviathan could unleash thousands of zerglings, flood entire cities, and push over skyscrapers. Leviathans aren't outright immune to mental attacks like Nightmares, but they're so hard to affect that they might as well be.

                Why then would a Beast pick a fight with something so powerful? Perhaps the Beast wants to be the apex, ordinary monsters cannot hope to make a bigger impression than a mile wide psychic broadcast screaming "fear and worship me!" into peoples souls. Perhaps the Beast has found there is a limit to their tolerence for a family's monsterousness. A Ravager who fed by throwing a town's local politics into chaos may still feel revulsion at someone forcing the entire population of the town to slaughter each other.

                But more likely is that the Beast dosn't want to fight. Leviathans claim descent from Tiamat, Beasts from the Dark Mother. Is there a connection there? Perhaps, but it is not wise to suggest the possibility to a short tempered religious fanatic. And when Leviathans aren't short tempered and lacking in impulse control, and sometimes even when they are, they're sadistic masterminds who might see a Beast as a useful pawn. A Beast who sees a secluded lake not far from town as a good spot for a Chamber might wake up one day and discover it's advantages were equally appealing to a Leviathan. Worst of all, a Leviathans protection from anything that affects their mind or emotions means that a Beast cannot rely on making a good first impression so simply going to visit the new kin in town is a risk.

                If the fight starts the best strategy for a Beast would be to catch the Leviathan in human form and kill it before it can transform. Failing that simply get out of town and wait for the Leviathans sheer metaphysical size to get it into trouble. Even for a Leviathan, there is always a bigger fish.

I don't see a need for a CoW, as you can let both apply, and both do exactly what they are meant to do (the mage still benefits by having more defense than if he hadn't used the spell, the Beast still benefits from having to fight against lower defense than he would have to otherwise).
