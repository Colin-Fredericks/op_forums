                                    Originally posted by Krycanelo View Post


                                    &quot; she whipped up an angry mob to go after her targets. Through empty rhetoric and carefully coordinated campaigns of doxing and harassment, she terrorized players, game developers, and journalists in the industry who don’t share her trolls’ views.&quot;

                                    I interpreted it as that these man children existed on the internet before but were relatively benign and uncoordinated, but she was the one who masterminded GG since it says she did it, not her trolls. My problem is that it goes against the Insatiable's theme of subversion. It's not that she subverted and corrupted a legitimate campaign about ethics in games journalism, it's that her trolls, under her control, created it. Then again, this could all feed into the idea that Snyper is on the cusp of becoming something different, an evolved form of an insatiable born from the internet transcending humanity to the point where it is equal to a force of nature.
                                    I came away with a different interpretation.

                                    "Recently, Null Snyper discovered the adolescent man-children that exist where gamers and men's rights activists overlap. She felt nothing but contempt for them, but she decided to try flexing her Internet muscles. Instead of driving people to suicide through direct online interaction, she whipped up an angry mob to go after her targets. Through empty rhetoric and carefully coordinated campaigns of doxing and harassment, she terrorized players, game developers, and journalists in the industry who don't share her trolls' views."

                                    There's no specific mention of GG. Only reference to common circles. It points out that these people already existed, and all she did was fan the flames and provide coordination to very specific campaigns against particular people. So this suggests to me that she's subverting an already existing group to direct them against targets of her choosing.

                                    There is no mention of a legitimate campaign or the creation of a movement, whether legitimate or illegitimate. The "coordinated campaigns" is specifically in reference to efforts to dox and harass particular victims.

                                    That's why I come away interpreting there being a movement similar to GG that Null Snyper noticed and took advantage of to cause trouble. So I don't think she's particularly responsible for it, though she's used it to her own advantage. I like that interpretation, because humans can be pretty awful on our own and I don't think we need to have an actual monster be involved to explain how something like GG happened.
