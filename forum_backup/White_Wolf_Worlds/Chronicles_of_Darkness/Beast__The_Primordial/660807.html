          Nah, Joker isn't a Hero. He's probably a Namtaru Ravager. Joker has no motivation or plans beyond "kill everyone and everything."

          Batman believes that in its heart, mankind is good and those who turn to evil do so because events in their lives have drove them to it. He doesn't kill people because he honestly believes they can be redeemed through proper effort and treatment.

          Joker, on the other hand, believes that mankind is naturally evil and all it takes is one bad event to set the gears in motion.

          Batman and Joker don't kill each other because they are polar opposites. Batman wants to prove his ideals by successfully reforming the Joker, and conversely the Joker wants to prove his ideals by driving Batman over the edge. As long as one of them is alive, it gives the other something to continue fighting for. If either of them killed the other, it would prove they could not be corrupted / rehabilitated and thus their ideals would be flawed or even completely wrong.

          Batman and Joker need each other. In a way, they're kind of like broodmates who have a very intense rivalry with one another. They're frenemies.

                                          As Nyrufa mentioned, Joker is probably a Ravager, though he could also have Tyrant as his second Hunger, seeing as how he's known as "The Clown Prince Of Crime".

                                          As for his Family, you could go multiple ways. Namtaru does fit. It is said many people fear clowns because they look like dead and deformed people to our subconscious, with their pale skins and bloated noses. Namtaru!Joker's Horror might look like a psychedelic zombie straight out of a Nurgle army, and he painted his human form to vaguely resemble his Horror.

                                          However, you could say that he is a Makara. In this case, the "Depths" do not refer to the sea nor the earth, but to the mind, and the dark things found within. The Makara are said to often possesses hidden or sunken knowledge, as do some madmen who might be called visionaries from the right angle. The water in his Chamber based on the chemical plant where he used to work isn't water at all, but a mix of Lair Traits that mimics the effects of the Joker Venom. When people feel like they are "drowning" due to the effect of his Birthright, that could be the Joker Venom following him around like a noxious gas. He might have used Alien Allure to make Harley his sidekick, or he might have even Devoured her. His Poker Cards are custom made to be coated with the poison created by the Basilisk's Touch Atavism. Despite all of this, I still think Joker focuses on Family Dinners with the other Batman villains and very special feedings (see Barbara's fate in "The Killing Joke") to keep his Satiety high and improve his Nightmares. That gives him an edge when trying to drive people mad. His Horror in this case would look different. The strands of his hair become green tentacles, his skin becomes pale like that of an eel, his eyes glow like the lure of an angler fish, and his smile turns very, very shark-like, all dripping with blood. I like this version better.
