                                              While a bit of a stretch in points, Overlord could serve with some ideas. Specifically, Sebas Tian, who has a Hunger for Punishment if the latest episode is anything to go by. While he is indeed going out of his way to teach (harsh) lessons, accepting that they might kill the learner, those seem to be disconnected from his Hunger, so not applicable.
                                              His Atavisms would be Cyclopean Strength, Limb from Limb and Eye of Heaven, maybe Lightning Strike from what we've seen so far. I'd say he's probably a benevolent Anakim. His Horror is most likely his true form (he's some sort of Draconid, apparently). If his battle-aura is anything to go by, his Lair-traits would be ice-based. Pure WMG but could be Overgrowth and Extreme Cold.


                                              Edit:
                                              Naberal Gamma could be an Inguma Predator, her Horror a shapeshifting Doppelganger. Her Atavisms are Enemy Within, Alien Mindset, Lightning Strike and Storm-Lashed, possibly also Wings of the Raptor (to represent Fly).

                                              Ainz is a Mage for sure, don't see a way to represent him as a Beast. Still, he'd be the Apex of Ainz Ool Gown.
