        Originally posted by MagicSwordsman View Post
        I'm making a spidery Ugallu, and was disappointed by the lack of a "climbing" Atavism, so here's my take on this:

        Web-Spinner [Ugallu]
        Normal: The Beast can climb any surface, no matter how slick, at their normal Speed. They can even climb upside-down like this!
        Low Satiety: The Beast can climb as though they weigh practically nothing. Even a thin thread will bear their weight. Furthermore, the Beast is immune to all damage from falling as long as it wasn’t a terminal velocity fall.
        Satiety Expenditure: The Beast produces sticky thread from their fingertips or mouth. Each point of Satiety spent creates up to 50 feet of thread. The thread has the following properties:
        • Durability and Structure equal to your Stamina + Lair and will support 100 lbs per point of Structure.
        • May be thrown as an aerodynamic object that sticks to people and objects. Removing the thread is a Strength roll.
        • The Beast may use it as part of a grapple to use the Restrain move without first using the Hold move.
        • It dissolves suddenly and completely after 24 hours, back into the ephemera of the Primordial Dream.

        I went for a different approach with the name. Without further ado, here's my take on an Atavism that uses webs, in general.

        -=-

        Web-Spinner [Ugallu]
        Nothing leaves a creature so exposed and powerless like being snared in a spider's webs. From Anansi and Areo-Enap to La Princesse and Shelob, spiders have been the focus of fears, stories and mythologies throughout the centuries. Beasts who claim kinship with arachnids are endowed with the ability to weave their threads for various purposes.

        Normal Effect: The Beast rolls Dexterity + Crafts + Lair to produce strands of webbing with Durability, Size, and Structure equal to half his Lair dots (rounded up). Successes in the roll can be allocated among those three properties, leading to various uses with the proper craftsmanship. For large, thick or elaborate webs, the weaving roll may be an extended action.

        If the Beast is trying to weave another character into that web while she works, that character can make a contested Strength + Athletics roll against her efforts. If he's agile instead of strong, he could use Dexterity in place of Strength. The trapped victim gets two attempts to pull free before the webbing dries. If the werespider gets more successes than her prey, that creature is bound in place and suffers the Immobilized Tilt until he can break free.

        Low Satiety: Hunger pushes the Beast to weave with greater fervor, allowing her to create masterpieces capable of felling all but the fiercest (or luckiest) of prey. The roll for the Atavism now benefits from the 8-Again Rule.

        Satiety Expenditure: By spending a point of Satiety, the Beast spins together the threads of her web with the speed and skill of Arachne, herself. The roll for the Atavism now benefits from the rote quality, and the webbing itself allocates half the Beast's Lair dots (rounded up) between its Durability, Size, and Structure.
        Last edited by Deionscribe; 01-25-2016, 08:46 PM. Reason: Slightly tweaked the Satiety Expenditure.

            I'd like to request an Atavism for my horror based on the Ravagore from Hordes. The ability is called Eyeless sight.
            What it does in its home setting is remove the benefits of stealth, concielment, hiding in a forrest, or hiding with the aid of a smoke or dust cloud. Let's call that the guide for Low satiaty

            What it represents is being able to perceive the world through other, and sometimes unusual senses. Examples being echo location, heat sense, being able to feel the slight tremors in the ground as a person walks, or sense the supernatural energy inherent in many beings, and likely some mix of these.

            I'd be happy to see what experienced person can come up with.

                    Salamander Claws [Eshmaki]
                    Dice pool: None
                    Action: Reflexive
                    Normal: Fire burns in your soul, and with it into your claws, and fangs.
                    When making a brawl based attack, or a ranged attack granted by another
                    atavism like dragon fire or Storm lashed, if that attack successfully hits the
                    targets hit are set on fire. They may resist with a dexterity + composure
                    - lair roll. This fire lasts for a number of turns equal to the beasts
                    stamina less successes rolled. This does Lair Lethal damage a turn, until
                    put out, or it expires.
                    Low Satiety: The hunger of the beast cause the flames it's claws leave to
                    burn hotter and longer than they would otherwise. Extend the base duration
                    of the fire by Lair. Increase damage by 1.
                    Satiety Expenditure: With extra power behind it the beasts flames burn white
                    hot, causing visible fires to wreath their hands and feet as they strike burning
                    what they touch with tremendous effect. The weapon modifier of the initial attack
                    is increased by 1, and the damage of the burning effect is increased by 2 instead of 1.
                    Corrosive Touch [Namatru]
                    Dice Pool: None
                    Action: Reflexive
                    Normal: Your body is that of a great toxic menace secreting horrible chemicals
                    that eat away at what ever they touch like hungry insects leaving chemical burns
                    though is the least of what you can do though. When active any brawl based
                    melee attack, or an attack generated by another atavism leaves behind a viscus
                    corrosive agent that eats into flesh, bone, and steel with equal ease. The corrosive
                    compound is treated as a poison with a toxicity of Stamina that deals damage every
                    turn until it has been fully resisted once with two successes. Objects are not so lucky
                    Every turn they take 1 structure damage that bypasses durability.
                    Low Satiety: Your deadly toxic touch damages armor, warping even the finest ceramic
                    plates, and fine steel. Every turn until It expires the armor value of the target's ballistic
                    armor decreases by 1, until it reaches 0. On the following turn, and every 2 turns after
                    reduce general armor by one. Against objects it reduces Durability by 1 on the beasts
                    next turn and every 2 turns after that.
                    Satiety Expenditure: With the power of the beast behind it no force can stop this toxic
                    ​ touch. Increase toxicity by lair, damage becomes aggravated.

                                    Originally posted by MatthiasThalmann View Post
                                    Sons of Wayland

                                    While many beasts are creatures of destruction some are just as skilled at creating things: An Ugallu may forge deadly weapons cutting down enemies who thought to be protected by an armour, while an Anakim build mighty castles - or in the modern age skyscrapers - to show his power and leave onlooker in awe. Many of these beasts think that Wayland was one of them who became an apex after taking revenge on Nidung.

                                    Dice Pool: N/A

                                    Action: Extended

                                    Normal effect: Relying on the supernatural skills of its horror the beast becomes an excellent craftsman and has maximum (5) dots in the crafts skill. However, the items are not supernatural just extraordinary well crafted. The beast is also capable to recognise forgeries or inferior items at first glance. Once per chapter the beast may create an item that is enhanced with supernatural strength: A knife might impossibly sharp or a pillar might bear load that should according to the laws of physics crush it. Items created this way have their natural proprieties enhanced but do not gain new capabilities.

                                    Low satiety: The horror lurks just below the surface and all the beast works are imbued with its power. All items the beasts create are better than the law of physics allow them to be. However, strength is not the only thing leaking into the beasts products: the hunger the beast feels infects its creations as well. Jewels created by a collector try to get lost to make their owners feel the loss, weapons created by a hunter thirst for blood and cut and injure everyone careless enough, chandeliers made by a ravager fall onto the table destroying the cutlery and meal... To the beast those side effects are not necessarily bad: If it can witness the effects of his creations it can regain 1 dot of satiety.

                                    Satiety Expenditure: Weaving part of the primordial dream into its creations the beast is capable to create outright supernatural items mimicking the effects of other atavisms (e.g. the coat Wayland used to escape would be equivalent to Wings of the Raptor). If the beast has Family ties with another monster who can create magical items the beast may create counterfeits of those. However, such creations do not last for long in the real world and the supernatural effects evaporate after a number of hours equal to the beast lair.

                                    What do you think?
                                    Well, let's see:

                                    Normal Satiety: Rather than setting a flat bonus, how about Beasts find it easier to make exceptional items? Like, Exceptional Success at Build Equipment actions with three successes instead of five, but only for actual, physical items? Plans, organizations, and repositories are to ephemeral and/or decentralized to be covered by this. Vehicles, however, would be a special case; fitting with the new rules for vehicle modification, this Atavism would increase the number of mods a vehicle can support from (the Beast's Craft x 2) to (Craft x 3), or (Craft x 4) if they have the Automotive Genius Merit.

                                    Low Satiety: I like the idea of items with desires (dare I say, Hungers) of their own. Rather than motion, though, it might be a better fit at this level if the item were to inflict a Condition or Tilt on a target, like in the stories where people become obsessed with enchanted rings, or become berserkers due to magic swords, or magic hammers that can send even the strongest warrior tumbling to the ground. I think this might be best represented by having the Condition/Tilt capable of being applied automatically against a target once per scene, giving player characters a Beat in exchange for the auto-application.

                                    Satiety Expenditure: This is the realm of magic carpets and helms of invisibility. The imbuing of the effect is permanent, and the Beast must either know the effect herself (5 Satiety), share a Lair with a Beast who does (6 Satiety), or have Kinship Ties with a creature with the ability in question (7 Satiety).

                                    That about does it. Any thoughts?
