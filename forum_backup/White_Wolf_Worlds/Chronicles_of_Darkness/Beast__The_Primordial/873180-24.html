                        How does the Mage even know he has been Marked? The write-up gives no indication the target is somehow aware of it. Routinely checking his Sympathy Connections? If he didn't do that beforehand, this seems a bit like a IC Knowledge/OOC knowledge seperation failure.

                        Regarding Clash of Wills: The Book clearly states
                        Originally posted by BtP p 222
                        "Beasts, lacking both dot-ranked powers and secondary Traits driving their powers, roll Lair + their highest Resistance Attribute."
                        , emphasize mine. As such, since Arcana are dot-ranked powers, it is pretty clear that they instead use their highest resistance attribute for clash of wills. Anything that deviates from that is a house rule and should be made clear as such, imo. Otherwise, people are not on the same page, which harms the game.

                        Regarding withstanding a Mage Spell:
                        Originally posted by MtAw p 127
                        "Living (or undead) subjects usually Withstand spells with Stamina (for physical transformations or afflictions), Resolve (for attempted mind control or other mental effects), or Composure (for emotional manipulation or effects that target sanity, perception, or the soul)."
                        This is what you do outside a Clash of Will, usually. No Lair specifically.

                        Also, regarding opacity:
                        Originally posted by MtAw p 94
                        Non-Supernal Phenomena Are More Opaque: A mage can Scrutinize the site of a Wasteland created by a Promethean and learn a great deal, but the Opacity rating should be higher (roughly 1.5 times higher, as a suggestion) than a similar effect created by Awakened magic.
                        Opacity of 3 seems appropriate to me, seeing how Scrying is Space 2. Even then, to track it down to the Beast would take more than just Scrutiny. He learns
                        Whether the Mystery is the result of Awakened magic.
                        • If so, what Arcana were involved and the Signature Nimbus of the caster (unless it was cast using a Rote).
                        • Roughly how old the Mystery is (hours, days, months,years, centuries).
                        • Optionally, what Practice created the Mystery (if a non-Supernal Mystery, then what Practice the Mystery most closely resembles).
                        and with Deep Knowledge
                        If Awakened magic cast using a Rote, or the effects of an Attainment, the Signature Nimbus of the mage involved.
                        • For Awakened spells, the spell factors involved (including the remaining duration), whether the spell caused a Paradox (and if so whether that Paradox was released or contained), and whether the spell has been relinquished.
                        • Identification of a phenomenon as being linked to one the mage has Scrutinized before.
                        • If not Awakened magic, the power level of the creator relative to the mage’s Gnosis (if applicable).
                        • Whether the Mystery is related to the Supernal Realms or Abyss.
                        • How the Arcanum used relates to the Mystery — for example, using Death Sight to scrutinize a vampire’s ghoul will reveal Deep Information relating to the undead blood in the subject’s system, and any powers the ghoul has gained from it. Using Fate Sight to assess a changeling’s powers will reveal that they are formed by mystical bargains.
                        It certainly would not automatically "lay out bare" all your secrets to the mage whenever you use anything. Could you give some more information with how the Mage does that? Not to mention that Scrutinizing yourself is probably a bit harder than an object or other person, and that it pours magic into the area, meaning anyone able to sense magic notices - not always (what am I saying, not USUALLY) a good thing.

                        Why do you Mark you teammates, though? Or are you opposing each other in this Chronicle?
