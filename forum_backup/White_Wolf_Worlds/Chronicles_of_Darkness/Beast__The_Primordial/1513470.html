    Like a Whirlwind Inside of My Head-What Do You Want from Nightmares?

    05-31-2023, 06:54 PM
    I wouldn't regard this a high priority subject in things to do with Beast, but on the other hand, enough people have mentioned not being drawn into Nightmares as a mechanic that I think it makes for a worthwhile bit of dicussion. Most of the issues sounds like it suffers from the issue of Beast on the whole, where in the value and purpose of the powerset isn't clearly communicated, with a side of just not being as "sexy"(/appealing) as Atavisms, but what do you like about the powerset, where do you feel it falls short, and what might you like to see done with them in the future?

    (A lot of my ideas stem from baking meta-Nightmares into the system without going all the way to make it "Beast does Subtle Arcana spellcasting system", so a little short there.)

      The way I see it, Nightmares as is (minus the Kinship ones?) are more connected to the intensive psychology metaphors (and ultimately the extensive social commentaries) facet of Beast. Which, frankly put, is the part most people would gloss over. Meanwhile the Atavisms put to front the “Rawr, I’m the mythic monster!” facet. Which is the fun and sexy part.

      I’d reconcile this by making Nightmares the ‘advanced play’ powers, tied to the Hungers as splats. The model here being Vampire’s ‘covenant Disciplines’ such as the Coils and Theban Sorcery.

      Easier said than done, as usual, but it’s what I’d start from.

                      Having the danged things fit into a metaphysical/cosmological model re: What Is Happening Here feels like it would go a fair ways toward making them pop; Atavisms are well-enough-explained as aspects of your Horror manifesting through your shared selfhood, Lair Traits and Primordial Pathways leverage sympathy to your location to bring the Lair you carry with you to the outside world, Obcasus Rites are deep religion, Kinship Merits are developed through affinity with your Family Ties, but Nightmares are individually-targeted powers that are learned and developed and rely on touch or eye contact and spoken word to somehow communicate a Primordial fear in such a way that the Dark Dream can manifest real-enough psychosomatic effects, and the fact that the Horror is a creature of fear from the soul of the world seems to be doing a lot of the heavy lifting there considering larval Horrors develop Nightmares, too.

                      Basically I think Nightmares presently feel a little disconnected from the rest of the Begotten powerset in terms of provided explanation for how they work, despite notionally serving as a notable reason to engage with Kinship and being the most direct variety of power one would expect from the psychic hosts/human faces of nightmare creatures. Knowing how the process compares to e.g. ideological attacks in the Temenos or the emotional effects imposed by a scene in the Oneiros and how/why a Beast can learn Nightmares from a given source would go a long way, especially (as with many other things) now that the way the Primordial Dream works has been defined more than it was in the corebook.

                              Honestly, what Nicolas said.

                              I always found Nightmares a bit weird, because if I want focus on them for a character, I want to make a creepy Mind Flayer or something cerebral as the Horror. Otherwise it feels like I’d just kinda ignore it, which obviously isn’t how it should feel. I think something like what Hermit suggested is a good idea. Nightmares need to be tied to something about a character, otherwise they feel very much like something tacked on rather than an integral part of your character.

                              I also really feel like it gets especially unclear of what a given Nightmare is supposed to represent. Like, can We know all your Secrets feel like telepathy? Is it just someone found your diary? Feels simultaneously too broad to make something kinda like it, and too narrow if that makes sense.

                              I also feel like there should be more…utility uses of Nightmares. But that’s probably more of a personal preference. I really want a hive mind/telepathy style nightmare for instance.

                              Some of the more meta-magic nightmare stuff feels like it could be merits or Rites rather than a separate nightmares. Like, those abilities are cool, but they don’t feel like the same kind of thing as a regular Nightmare.

                              I feel like it should tie into Obacus Rites somehow? I don’t know, it feels like a stepping stone, like the most primal and basic usage of one’s power is a Avatism, and next up is Nightmares, and finally Obacus Rites is the most advanced usage.

                              I do like their current systemic opposition to Atavisms though. That both makes sense, and informs a lot about the unstable balancing act that is Satiety.

                              I also like the basic levels of infliction. Eyes are the windows of the soul, and a touch is terribly intimate. It also feels like a good base level, while leaving room for more specialized techniques.​

                              I guess what I want with them is something slightly less rigid. Like the effects are not set in stone, but more like suggestions of what you could do with it. A Nightmare of old age might make you feel frailer, have all your teeth fall out, or look like your are practically a corpse already. You know, like before. But it can also make one feel like the world has left them behind, and confused at technology. Instead of Satiety picking the effect, the Beats does, and instead Satiety determines the potency I don’t know, maybe too close to the aforementioned subtle arcana spellcasting, Beast edition.

                              To whomever reads this, I hope you have a good day/night. May you be Happy.

                              So, I made some Mage Legacies here, with some help. They vary in quality, but I hope you take a look at them. Every one contains pieces of me, for better or worse.

                                  Thinking on this a bit... what I basically would love to see is a Beast twist on Changeling's Oneiromancy rules. Nightmares themselves could be maintained as a concept as something that helps differentiate the two splats interacting with the same dream-manipulating subsystem. For example, without an appropriate Nightmare tagged on your target, Beasts could have much greater difficulty with Paradigm Shifts in a target's dreams, but Nightmares allow them to "pre-load" Shifts into a targets dream far more easily than the Lost can as more generalists in the activity.

                                  I feel like A Nightmare on Elm Street should be a benchmark here. If learning how to get good use out of Nightmares let you be Freddy, it would (at least as a pop culture reference point) add a lot more "sexy" to them compared to how they currently work. While Freddy himself might be more of an Inheritance of a Begotten and Horror bonding together to live on the fear of nightmares, the whole concept there also plays a lot into the building of one's own Legend. Freddy doesn't just invade dreams and do Subtle Shifts until he's fed enough to be powerful enough to toss around Paradigm Shifts on a whim, he makes sure to get his targets talking about specific dream-images, so other targets are primed to dream of them too.

                                      Kinship Nightmares

                                      On a more serious note, I'm mostly drawing a blank on this matter. My obseration is minor, all things considered, but here it is: The way Common and Kinship Nightmares are currently divided seems arbitrary and unnecessary. Is there really a reason why a Beast can't receive You Are Lost upon their Devouring? It's a Changeling Kinship Nightmare, but reflects a universal fear. Conversely, couldn't Behold, My True Form! be learned from a Werewolf by an otherwise shy Horror? True, some additional mechanic could be introduced that plays off the source of a known Nightmare (innate, Kinship, other), but as things stand the mechanical sorting only limits player's options.​

                                          Agreed: I'd prefer if Kinship Nightmares weren't tied to special Nightmares you can only unlock this way. I think separating the two would both broaden the possibilities for starting character concepts, and make Kinship more organic: sure, you know that there's power and insight in each new nightly sibling you get... but you're not filling a Pokedex.

                                          Personally, I would be to root Nightmares in the 2e design elements we've taken to designate as aura powers.

                                          Perceiving a monster, interacting with it, may dredge out something in you, something that twists your mind, encroaches on your body, and crackles your veneer of civilized rationality. That's true of Nightmares. That's also true of most Auras of other splats.

                                          The very fact that we as a fandom can understand aura powers as a loose collection is a view that is typically Beast-like in universe. It's a wildly variegated set of abilities. But for Beasts, who are convinced that all monsters are one extended Family, it makes sense to take all those abilities based on perception and/or monstrous unmasking, and just count them all as the same gift from the Dark Mother to her children.

                                          We know Beasts with High-Satiety have an increased Passing Resemblance to other splats: we also know that Beasts with High-Satiety are better at conjuring Nightmares. Kinship Nightmares echoing the auras of other monsters would feel quite natural and elegant, in that context.

                                          And lastly, that also explains why a Kinship with Demons doesn't come naturally to Beasts, at least in the regard of Nightmares. Standing out in a monstrous way is the very last thing a Demon wants. They try to Spoof the world and mortals every chance they get, and only Go Loud when absolutely desperate. Their "aura powers", if one strains to describe them as such, are predicated on opacity, normalcy, and distress: the opposite paradigm to what Beasts want to relate to in their Kin.

                                                      The younger me who first read Beast would have been ecstatic over Nightmares-as-subtle-Arcana. Current me grudgingly admits it probably shouldn’t go that far. But more flexibility would be welcome. The current mechanics leave many Nightmares feeling very situational and game-y.

                                                      I also agree that having Kinship should let you accrue bonus xp to learning appropriately themed Nightmares, but as it currently stands, there’s not much of a reason to lock effects behind Kinship. The couple times I’ve had Beast characters, I’ve just politely ignored the division anyway.

                                                      I recommend the Magnus Archive as having several good examples of very Nightmare-esque powers. I liked the concept of Nightmares before, but that series gave me a new appreciation for just how creepy and potent imposing supernatural manifestations of fear onto someone could be.

                                                      The longer I study science the more I am convinced that it is functionally indistinguishable from what our ancestors would refer to as sorcery. And I would know, being both scientist and sorcerer.
