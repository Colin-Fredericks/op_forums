    Hunger with "changes".

    10-03-2023, 11:39 PM
    This is something I'm testing and I wanted a second opinion, as beast hungers can be very uncomfortable I thought of these variants that might be more tolerable.

    Tyrants: Basically you can satisfy your hunger by exercising your authority or superiority. In the first case, the target needs to obey your command, but it needs to be something that she normally would not do willingly or does not like to do, whether due to laziness or pride. Superiority is highly explanatory, you would just have to prove that it is better in something whether physical or intellectual, however there are two important points in this case:

    1. The victim in question must recognize defeat, so if he thinks the beast won by cheating, she won't receive satisfaction from the act;

    2. The victim must be defeated at something that they value, even if they are not good at it.

    In the case of tyrants, the Beast feeds on the obedience or humiliation of the victim. Exercising your superiority over other beasts or other supernatural beings provides more satiety.

    Collectors: Collectors are treasure hunters, who satisfy their hunger by collecting precious things, however "precious things" have a very relative value depending on the family and the characteristics of each individual, Makaras tend to collect antiques, Ugallus collect compromising photos or evidence of crime, Anakim usually collect trophies from defeated opponents and so on. Detail, they only need to collect the object in question to satisfy themselves, what they do with it is at their discretion, however if the object already belongs to another person it cannot be given or purchased, it must be taken or at least obtained per bet, in some cases trade is a option.

    Predators: They are satisfied with the hunt, to satisfy their horrors they must track, ambush and capture/kill the prey, how much they are satisfied varies depending on how much the prey has been injured and how strong it is, other supernaturals usually grant a lot more satiety. They usually become hunters or if they live in cities, the healthiest option is to become hunters of offenders, like bounty hunters for example.

    Nemesis: Also known as bogeymen, they satisfy themselves by punishing bad children or bad adults, however they need to have committed a given crime or infraction to deserve the punishment, the beast must be sure that the given individual committed the infraction and the offender must know why he is being punished, otherwise he will not be able to be satisfied. Punishment can range from a scare to extreme damage to the body. The greater the crime, the greater the satisfaction.

    Ravager: Those who bring ruin, they are the simplest who are satisfied with destruction, but not just any destruction, the target needs to have some value either for the individual or for a community, if it is trash that no one cares about it won't have any effect. How much the destruction will satisfy the beast depends on the number and how much it will affect other people. In this case the beast is a change agent and it feeds from the victins emontions, be positive or negative.

    Along with this I would also put humanity points to balance and have a cost in case some beast goes overboard. In general terms it is very similar, in fact almost the same, I just put it in such a way that the Beasts need to sustain habits rather than feed on fears and traumas, although these would also be options.

      First... Beasts are monsters. What they do isn't supposed to be "comfortable" otherwise they wouldn't really be monsters. I don't see what the benefit of changing from "feeding from fear," to "feeding from habitual behaviors that result in fear and trauma," is supposed to accomplish. The only thing it can really do is let Beasts off easy. For example Collectors can just take whatever and it doesn't matter if their target cares about it. I can make a Namtaru that collects used chewing gum (which most people would find a revolting habit to have), but I don't need to worry about the person I'm taking the gum from, so I can just find a trash can near a school or something that I know kids that chew gum use a lot and loot it when nobody else is around.

      Second, adding more to track isn't a good thing. Beasts not having a "morality" track that says how good or bad they're being is a purposeful part of the game. Adding some sort of Humanity analog is both clunky design, and detracts from what's good about the game: Beasts decide who they are. Anything that creates objective right or wrong ways for Beasts to act is missing the point. Every Beast should be living in a state of tension where being pushed too hard could derail them from who they're trying to be; unless they've just given up and decided to be evil for the sake of evil and don't care anymore (which means you can pass them an Inheritance and move that character to NPC status if necessary). Beasts that go overboard already have to deal with either Satiety 0 or Satiety 10, which are nasty states to get stuck in.

              Beasts in the RAW can generally get by just fine with a combination of low level feeding, and kinship to a supernatural type that's easy to make use of Family Dinner. The example low Satiety feedings are generally very easy to accomplish without making much of a fuss; you don't even really want to get a lot of Satiety out of them anyway, it's more to feed frequently enough for your Lair rating. Family Dinner can get you 1-3 Satiety as your main source of it just by watching another supernatural hunt and feed.

              There isn't a need to "hack" here to keep it to "lesser" evils. The tools are already in the game. A character that hungers for prey can make sure to spend their Satiety down to 3 before a weekly/twice weekly (for as powerful as most PCs are going to get with their Lairs) trip to hunt animals. Then bring your catch back to small church your changeling buddy helps run free meals for the homeless out of and you give your meat to. Soak up 2 Satiety from watching the changeling Harvest Glamour from the gratitude of those in need getting help making it through the week. So you're regularly be sitting around 5-6 Satiety to do actual major scenes and you can then get that back down to 3 to rinse and repeat.

              Obviously a serious story is going to upset such routines in some fashion to add some challenge to the PC's life, but the bar for "lesser evil" in the books is so low any hack trying to lower it seems pointless.
