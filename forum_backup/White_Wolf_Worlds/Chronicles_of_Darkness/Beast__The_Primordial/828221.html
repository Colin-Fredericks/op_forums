    Gathering ideas for a Dark Era, could use some some discussion

    02-11-2016, 10:59 AM
    Hello everyone, as the title suggests, I need some help. There's a Beast Dark Era I've been thinking about since I read the game and, even if I won't start writing it nowhere in the near future, I want to put down the basic structure so I can keep it in mind and elaborate on it as time goes on. Writing a Dark Era is no easy task (especially writing a good one) and while I'm not sure to be up to it, I'm certain that having some discussion about it with you will be quite helpful.

    The Dark Era I'm talking about is the Congo Free State one I've been annoying people about for ages. Essentially "Beast meets Heart of Darkness". It will touch the whole period of King Leopold's rule, but the main focus will be the phase where rubber became the greatest objective of the colonization. A moment where, to be blunt, an already horrible situation took a turn for the worse.

    What I'm asking for is criticism, ideas, suggestions, requests, plot hooks for Beast and other gamelines or really any sort of comment you might have.

    The preliminary theme and mood are the following:

    Theme: "Savagery Hidden Within"
    No matter who you were or the place you came from, living in King Leopold's Congo was an experience nobody was prepared about. Natives, agents of the colonial companies, missionaries, explorers and reporters...all agreed that the moment you became part of the nightmarish picture created to satisfy the demands of the colonial machine was as if entering another world. Given free reign and the excuse to behave anyway they want as long as they obtain results, with almost no judgment whatsoever, respectable gentlemen turn into monsters and brothers betray brothers. It's all more primal here, the norms of society abandoned in favor of impulses and will of survival. The only true rules are those made by the strong and the deeper you go along the river, the easier becomes to you distance yourself from civilization. Empathy is a virtue and, sometimes, a luxury, that's dangerously easy to loose along the way. The dissonance between boasted dignity and manifest savagery is ever-present. The fact that much of it is spawned by the very same dictates of the so-called civil society only makes it more jarring. Ultimately, every single person is left struggling with her darker instincts, having to fight the impulse to embrace the beast that dwells inside.

    Mood: "Waking Nightmare"
    In Congo, one has to wonder where reality ends and where the Primordial Dream seeps in. The sheer magnitude of atrocities and horrors one can witness and that are carried on as if it's the norm is insane. Much of the population seems trapped into a narrative they unconsciously became part of and the few who notice either cannot do much or eventually fall for it as well.

    Then there's some snippets, ideas and miscellanea that I came up with to characterize the Era:
    - No easy answers. There's no such thing as "bad guys VS good guys". It's all very, very grey. Absolute morality is tricky here, everyone's alone with himself and the only elements that speak about their valor are their actions. By default, you are alone in the darkness and you can only hope to find support in your...
    - ...Family. Which, then again, might not be easy. You don't choose it after all and in this era of clashing cultures its members come from all the sides. Native Beasts and Beasts from abroad (the same goes for all supernaturals of course) often have a hard time finding a common ground and they are torn between their human culture and Beastly nature. With the factions fragmented even within themselves, learning to accept your family is complicated.
    - Lots of Heroes, many of them of the bad kind. The cultural context makes it far too easy to embark on the kind of heroic crusade Beasts are afraid of. Good Heroes still exist, but a damaged individual that becomes a Hero is still a damaged individual. And there's plenty of those around here. It almost seems as the Primordial Dream is close to the waking world in King Leopold's Congo and such proximity has its consequences.
    - African Supernaturals. Faeries, vampires, spirits and everything else. Geist Kreves based on the cult of the ancestors, wereleopards and Hunter Compacts appropriate to the settings. You get the idea. Plenty of material to work with here.
    - Mokele-mbembe, as in the Beast behind the legend, is the Apex. He was on the verge of becoming an Incarnate but then came the Belgian domination and everything it brought along. Previously a lonely and powerful Beast, Mokele delayed his ascension in order to deal with his Family members, both local and foreign. Even if he's not eager too admit it, the events of the Era made him look back at his mortal identity and allegiances, something he thought to be ready to leave behind.

    EDIT: Working on the Theme. Leestsepeak expressed some legitimate and worrisome doubts about it so the I'm changing the "first draft" above
