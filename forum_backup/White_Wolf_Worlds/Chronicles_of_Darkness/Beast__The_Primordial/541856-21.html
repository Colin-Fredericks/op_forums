    Some believe there is no punishment greater than the one we inflict upon ourselves when we feel like we deserve retribution. This Beast would like very much to test that theory, and so they have refined their feeding habits to accommodate it. They don't physically harm their victim, at least not directly, but what they do resort to is sending them on a nightmare powered guilt trip, the likes of which can drive a man to martyrdom.

    Their horror is the scrawny, helpless kid who always got bullied by others because they felt he was too weak to defend himself. He's the captain of a boat, or a train, or a plane, or sometimes it's just a car. Whatever it is, he'll be your driver for the night, while you, the unlucky passenger, are strapped tightly into your seat with no perceivable way to undo your bindings. He limps into view covered in wounds and bandages as he informs you that he is here to ferry you to the afterlife. That's when the lights dim, he retreats from sight, and the ride starts moving.

    His lair is reminiscent to Willy Wanka's tunnel from the original movie. Those who take the ride through it are assaulted on all sides by exaggerated recreations of the consequences for their actions. The people they've hurt are screaming in blinding agony and wailing in despair like damned souls who can not escape their torment. A slow motion replay of the Beast's victim is shown violently assaulting them with a sadistic glee only matched by demons etched across their face. Visions of medical treatments come next, but they're way beyond anything the victim might have actually done. Disgusting bruises that turn the flesh dark purple or decayed brown, and shattered bones that protrude through the skin. The Beast's victim hasn't simply attacked their victim, they've brutalized them, all to make themselves feel powerful (this particular lesson doesn't ingratiate himself with Tyrants). The next series of visions are an endless bombardment of obscenities and humiliating scenarios. Every insult they've ever thrown at others, or prank they've ever pulled is now directed at them a thousand times over without pause. Most victims end up screaming to get off by the time this starts happening. And just as the victim's psyche has reached its limit, a glow begins to form over the horizon, with the vehicle getting closer as is gets brighter. The victim has to strain their vision at first, but then their eyes go wide and they start thrashing as the image becomes clear. The glow is produced by an endless sea of flames, and they're drifting straight for it!

    The ride usually ends before the flames start licking the victim's skin and with the Beast having suitably gorged itself upon their mind numbing terror. His family has repeatedly encouraged him to go on a stricter diet, but he can't help himself. These people never showed him an ounce of mercy when he was human. Now that he's a monster, he feels no inclination to do so, either. The people he punishes have only themselves to blame!

    Horror: the Ferryman

    Family: Namtaru

    Hunger: Punishment

    Kinship: Mummies

    Lesson: Caution - Some day, it might be you who's begging for mercy.

        Ugallu Ravager: She was voted in scarcely a month ago, and since then she's been on a crusade, not to push her own agenda through, but to uncover all the dirty laundry of her 'peers', exposing it to the public eye and watching their careers crash and burn. To her colleagues, she's a threat, a monster which they can only hope doesn't turn its gaze on them until they've had time to clear away the things they don't want dragged into the light. To the public she's an avenging angel, a paragon upholding the right and the just and punishing the wicked. The fact that she's survived more than one assassination attempt and disproven a number of false claims that she is just as corrupt as those she tears down hasn't hurt her reputation either.

        In truth, she's both: a monster and an angel.

        In her childhood, her family, friends and neighbours suffered under the uncaring boot of far-distant corporation owners and politicians, just another blot on the urban landscape, another set of miscreants and layabouts without jobs, as if that was a choice they made. In school, she pushed herself harder and further than her peers, desperate to rise above where she was born and to whom, to stand on a level with their 'lawful' oppressors and cast them down, to help the people like her. In dreams, she found herself under the vast and pitiless regard of the creature she called the Watcher and - later, after she learned Latin - the Grigori, a thing of a thousand wings and a hundred thousand judgemental eyes, each scourging her nightly with their contempt for her weaknesses. With each step she made towards her goal, though, another pair of wings unfurled, until eventually, on the night after she won her campaign, the last pulled back and she saw the one who stood beneath the wings, the face of that seraph - her own.

        Since that day, she has continued her crusade, feeding on the destruction of the careers and lives of the leeches who think themselves kings. She only wishes that she could pretend that it was the justice and the punishment that set her heart beating faster, not the looks of despair and horror on their faces as they realise that all they have built is for nought.

        Lair Traits: Exposed, Blazing Light

        Atavisms: Eye of Heaven, Mimir's Wisdom, Wings of the Raptor, Dragonfire (manifests as a scourging, burning radiance)

        Nightmares: You Deserve This, Everything You Do Is Worthless

        Lesson: What you do can ruin you (she doesn't really focus on teaching this as much as she does on actually bringing that ruin)

            I wrote this character idea up waaaaay back on Page 7.

            Beast has been more fleshed out and refined since then, and now that I've gotten my hands on the official book, I've decided to revise my previous version. So here it goes!

            ----------------------------------------

            Bottom feeders, scum suckers, ass kissers, parasites, these are words society often attributes to people who prefer to stick close to power, without assuming any responsibility. They praise and venerate those above them in hopes of being shown nepotism when they need a favor done. Timothy Blight knows these kinds of people all to well, for he used to be one before his devouring. Now, he wishes to show these boot lickers just how disgusting they truly are.


            Timothy Blight's horror is a horrifying cross between a tape worm and a centipede that has been grown to nightmarish proportions. Its mouth is shaped like sphincter, and opens to reveal multiple rows of needle sharp teeth. It skitters across the walls, floor and ceiling on countless spindly legs, while it's long antennae dangle around in the air, sensing for prey as it lacks proper eyes to see with. When it detects one nearby, it detaches from the wall with a blood curdling screech before charging in for the attack.

            His lair is not so much a place, as it is a thing. A living creature of unimaginable size, and Timothy resides inside its guts. More specifically its digestive track, where he feeds on the partially digested meals that find their way down there. Partially digested meals, like the victims who's dreams he torments. He's not the only parasite to be found here, either. Timothy has laid thousands of eggs throughout the creature's intestines, which have hatched into miniature versions of himself, and threaten to attack those who come within reach. Their best chance at survival here is to feed on the host's left overs. But that's something they should be comfortable with, already. After all, they've been feeding on society's hard work for years; so why get squeamish now?

            Family: Namtaru

            Hunger: Prey

            Atavisms: Basilisk's Touch, Infestation, Shadowed Soul, Heart of the Ocean

            Nightmares: Bugs Everywhere, You Must Obey, Everything You Do Is Worthless

            Lair Traits: Stench, Flooded, Slick, Corrosive, Swarm

            Lesson: Don't manipulate others to support your livelihood.

                The Frosts: while all Begotten see themselves as family on some historical level, some of the children are family in a more literal sense. The Frosts are one such example, a Brood who are related to one another by blood. It consists of five members including the father (Anthony Frost), the mother (Heather Frost), and their three lovely children (Michael, Winter, and Lily).

                Interesting of note is that despite all being related to each other in the human world, their Horrors actually trace their lineages to different family origins. Each member of the Frosts are a different kind of Beast, with a different kind of Hunger. And while this gives them a significant advantage when it comes to fending off Heroes, it also makes family dinners more problematic when everybody in the family wants something different.

                The collective Lair shared by the Frosts resembles a frozen mountain range surrounded by arctic tundras spanning in all directions. Each member of the family claims a different part of the mountain as their lair, and those attempting to scale it will progressively encounter the entire family by the time they reach the top. The family's locations are as follows.

                Anthony - Outer Base

                Lily - Inner Base

                Michael - Outer Surface

                Heather - Inner Surface

                Winter - Mountain Peak

                -------------------------------------------

                Anthony Frost, Makara Nemesis: The patriarch of the family and founder of the Brood, Anthony's career as a S.W.A.T officer enables him to dispense uncompromising justice upon the heads of those who abuse society. He deeply cares about his family and will go any lengths to ensure their safety. Unfortunately, this love is hard to appreciate at times, when it forces him to commit heinous acts for the greater good. The most recent example being when he forced the Devouring upon Lily, after she exhibited no potential connection to the Primordial Dream on her own. The Frost children seem to have inherited their Horrors from Anthony, as demonstrated by their winter-themed motif.

                Looking astoundingly healthy for a man in his late 40's, Anthony law enforcement training has combined well with his Begotten capabilities. He stands 6'2 in human form, weighing 217 lbs. His hair is kept short above the ears and still retains its healthy chestnut coloration from his youth, instead of fading like other men his age are known to do. His deep blue eyes have a piercing gaze that speaks of having witnessed the worst the world has to offer, and yet he's still prepared to keep fighting.

                Anthony's horror is a sea dragon, with a serpentine body that moves gracefully through the water and burrows caverns through the ice with its webbed claws. Its lair is an arctic landscape where platforms of slick ice drift upon frigid waters, threatening to plunge the unwary into instant hypothermia should they lose their balance. He is also the oldest and most powerful Beast in the family, although Michael's raw strength does make him a decent competitor.

                Aspirations: Reconcile with Lily (long term), Take down a criminal operation (short term)

                Hunger: Punishment

                Lair 4: Extreme Cold, Slick, Flooded, Unstable

                Atavisms: Heart of the Ocean, Monster of the Deep, Limb from Limb, Storm Lashed

                Nightmares: Fear is Contageous, You Deserve This, Everything You Do is Worthless, You Cannot Run

                ----------------------------------------------------------------


                Heather Frost: Namtaru Predator: Time has not been as kind to Heather as it was for her husband. Before meeting Anthony, Heather was a vicious serial killer who gorged herself by mutilating her victims into shredded meat. It was with his help that she learned to control her appetite and they eventually fell in love. Today, Heather displays a much more subdued attitude and plays the role of doting housewife in front of the neighbors. But behind this motherly facade lurks the mind of a predator. Even her own family have learned by now that they are better off not knowing the secret ingredient to her family recipes.

                In her human form, Heather sports bright green eyes and medium cut, cherry blond hair. Being only a few years younger than Anthony, age has siphoned off much of the sex appeal she once had in her younger days. Standing at 5'7 tall and weighing 156 lbs, Heather's skin has begun to show the signs of wrinkling and her hair is not as vibrantly healthy as it used to be. She also dresses a couple decades out of date, as if she is trying to emulate the stereotypical housewife, but doesn't know how.

                In her Horror form, Heather is a gargantuan ice worm who's body is protected by an armored caripace as she burrows her way through ice and stone that composes the family's mountain. When her mouth opens, it reveals a ring shaped maw filled with several rows of needle sharp teeth ready to swallow her victims whole. Heather dwells inside the upper regions of the mountain, hunting down anyone who attempts to seek shelter from the weather by hiding inside one of the many cave systems she's burrowed out over the years.

                Aspirations: Have another child with Anthony (long term), Get Lily to laugh (short term)

                Hunger: Prey

                Lair 3: Poor Lights, Maze, Jagged

                Atavisms: Unbreakable, Monster From The Deep, Shadowed Soul

                Nightmares: Behold, My True Form, You Are Not Alone, You Will Never Rest

                ----------------------------------------------------------------


                Michael Frost, Anakim Tyrant: A regular party animal, Michael is the eldest of the Frost children and the one who's closest in power to that of his parents. Michael has moved out of his parents' house in order to attend college, but the Lair shared by his Brood allows him to quickly pop over and visit them whenever he wants. Michael revels in his heritage and flaunts his power whenever he sees an opportunity to get away with it. Of course, being a giant means that Michael has plenty of opportunities to get away with it!

                At 18 years of age, Michael's human form has all the obnoxious rowdiness of a college fratboy. Which is fitting, considering that he actually is one. He's a big boy, standing just shy of 7 feet tall and weighing 240 lbs of athletic muscle. Michael takes after his father, having inherited the chestnut hair and blue eyes that Anthony possesses. However, he's also inherited Heather's drive to make the rest of the family happy and gets quite protective around his younger sisters.

                In the Primordial Dream, he takes the form of a great yeti, with icy blue skin, thick white fur, glowing red eyes and sharpened teeth. His lair spans the surface of the mountain which his Brood calls home. The strong winds and unstable terrain makes travel extremely dangerous for those not accustomed to mountain climbing, and yet Michael seems to be a natural at maneuvering through the environment. Much to the chagrin of those attempting to flee his pursuit.

                Aspirations: Graduate College (long term), Find a girlfriend (short term)

                Hunger: Power

                Lair 3: Extreme Cold, Unstable, Crosswinds

                Atavisms: Cyclopean Strength, Titanic Blow, Storm-Lashed

                Nightmares: You Will Never Rest, All Your Teeth Are Falling Out

                ---------------------------------------------------------------


                Winter Frost, Ugallu Collector: The middle child of the Frost family, Winter broke the mold of traditional sibling roles by becoming one of the most popular girls in town, as opposed to being over shadowed and neglected by the attention given to her older brother and little sister. Unfortunately, her popularity comes at the expense of being a shameless flirt who manipulates both males and females into doing what she wants. Indeed, Winter's hoard is a veritable harem of admirers who will gladly do whatever is in their power to please her, so long as her demands are not too extreme. Winter is especially attracted to those who play hard to get, and her Hunger is stirred by the thought of adding them to her collection.

                At 16 years old, Winter has developed nicely as a voluptuous young girl. The kind that turns the heads of the sports team's star player and leaves the cheerleaders seething with jealousy. And she flaunts this sex appeal to the furthest extent that is legally tolerable in public. She stands 5'5 and weighs 130 lbs. Wearing skin tight, revealing outfits and bright, colorful fashions designed to lure in her prey. She's inherited Heather's cherry blond hair and bright green eyes, but she has Anthony's tenacity for getting what she wants.

                Winter's horror is an ice dragon with majestic white scales across her primary body. Her underside (and membrane on her wings) are a crystal blue hue, making her appear stunningly beautiful for a creature out of mankind's nightmares. Her lair is an ice cave that has been carved into the peak of her family's mountain. Sub zero temperatures combined with a lack of fresh air outside her cave leaves Winter's captives few options for survival beyond remaining trapped inside with her. And even then, their survival depends entirely upon whether or not they continue to please their mistress.

                Aspirations: Seduce a celebrity (long term), acquire a new lover (short term)

                Hunger: Collector

                Lair 2: Blizzard, Extreme Cold, Thin Air

                Atavisms: Wings of the Raptor, Alien Allure, Storm-Lashed

                Nightmares: You Must Obey, Flying and Falling

                ----------------------------------------------------------

                Lily Frost, Eshmaki Ravager: it's official, every household has to have the one family member who makes everything more difficult than it needs to be. The youngest member of the Frost family, Lily did not exhibit any natural affinity to the Primordial Dream and would probably have gone through life as an ordinary human. But Anthony, fearing the worst possible outcome, decided to force the Devouring upon her, turning Lily into a monster without her knowledge or consent. Lily did not appreciate the well intended gesture, and has emotionally withdrawn herself from the world. Now, she is the most quiet and reserved member of the family, having focused most of her energy on self contemplation until she can determine where she stands on her current situation.

                At 12 years old, Lily's human form is a shy and soft spoken girl as far as outward appearances go. Inwardly, she struggles to come to terms with what's been done to her, leaving her mind a jumbled mess of raw emotion that strains to lash out. Due to her youth, she stands 4'6 tall and weighs 92 lbs. She has the chestnut colored hair of Anthony, and the bright green eyes of Heather, but prefers to dress in plain, muted outfits so as to draw as little attention to herself as possible. So far, her hunger for destruction can be waved off as a rebellious phase she's going through; but that excuse won't work forever, once she's older.

                Lily's horror is a sabertooth cat; large and savage, with fangs as long as a man's hand. Her lair is a network of cave systems that span beneath the foot of the mountain shared by her family. There are many exits to these caves, but they all deposit travelers into the frozen wastes that the rest of her Brood calls home. Trespassers must choose which is the better option; being torn apart by Lily, or risk freezing to death on the surface. And should her victims prove lucky enough to escape her pursuit within the mountain, there's still the chance they'll encounter her mother on the upper levels of the caves!

                Aspirations: Come to terms with her new life (long term), Relieve some pent up aggression (short term).

                Hunger: Ruin

                Lair 1: Jagged, Darkness

                Atavisms: From the Darkness, Limb from Limb

                Nightmares: Behold, My True Form, You're Not Alone

But to mainstream society, he's unheard of; not even a whisper upon the lips of others.

                                                        Carl, the Goblin King: A bitter and cynical man, Carl's childhood was filled with empty promises and broken dreams. All throughout his youth, people told him dreams came true and that he could be anything he wanted. So when reality kicked in, Carl was crushed and retreated into his own fantasy realm as a way of coping with the disappointment and anger he felt towards the world. On the day of his 21st birthday, however, Carl's dreams really did come true; but not the kind that anybody wanted!

                                                        Almost immediately following the events of his Devouring, Carl became ravenous to learn as much about the supernatural as he could. He began devoting all his time and energy into tracking down occult knowledge, magical trinkets and artifacts of power, which for a normal person would probably have turned up bunk. But as a member of the supernatural community, Carl achieved a surprising level of success in his hunts. Eventually, he discovered a way to contact the True Fae, and that is where Carl's legend truly begins, for he realized that Arcadia held nearly limitless magical potential, and he wanted it for himself. Carl is now the head of a human trafficking ring that caters to the denizens of Arcadia. Those who are kidnapped by his organization are sold off to the highest bidder in exchange for magical items and secrets. Changelings can no longer bring themselves to utter Carl's name without spitting in disgust, so they simply call him the Goblin King!

                                                        It probably comes as no surprise that Carl has his sights set on Incarnation, at which point he plans to take up a more permanent residence near Arcadia. In fact, he's already figured out a way to solidify his Legend as the Goblin King; he plans to sell an entire band of Heroes to the True Fae as slaves. It's a dangerous plan that borders on the realm of suicidal, but it will be so deliciously exquisite if he can pull it off.

                                                        Carl's Lair is a vast, torch lit goblin market held deep underground where slaves are bartered for with magic. Black iron cages built into the walls, floors and celing house a variety of mortals waiting to be sold off to the highest bidder. There are dozens of such captives, but most of them are simply illusions generated to inspire fear in unwanted visitors. Only prisoners who actually get sold off are real, and they only comprise maybe a tenth of the Goblin King's monthly stock. Those who aren't locked in cages are paraded through the streets in chains, their hands, feet and necks shackled together as whips are cracked against their exposed flesh. The market is shielded by several hundred feet worth of rock in any direction, which prevents electronic devices from functioning properly due to poor signal coverage and magnetic interference. As with any slave market, the atmosphere is constantly noisy, and filled with the sounds of excited bidding, rattling chains, and wailing captives.


                                                        Family: Namtaru

                                                        Hunger: Power

                                                        Horror: A foul and ugly goblin, Carl's horror stands six feet tall and is morbidly obese at 462 lbs. His ears are gnarled and floppy like a pig's, while his nose is bulbous and his skin is a mottled orange. His yellow eyes are too large for human sockets and a protruding overbite reveals his sharp teeth to onlookers. He dresses himself in wolf pelts, with an assortment of accessories made from human bones. The most prominent of which is the belt made of skulls he wears around his waist.

                                                        Life: Honest - If Carl has any redeeming qualities, it's that he prides himself on being a fair and honest businessman. Treat him with respect, and he will do the same to you, provided you're not currently up for market. Carl has even developed a multitude of powers dedicated to appraising others and deciphering truth from falsehoods.

                                                        Legend: Relentless - Carl's hunger for power is all consuming, and once he finds something he wants, he won't just give it up without a fight. Of course, this also means that he will go to nearly any lengths to satisfy the terms of an agreement, regardless of who gets hurt along the way.

                                                        Aspirations: Enslave a band of Heroes (Long Term), Capture a red haired virgin (short term)


                                                        Lair: 8 (Disruption, Cramped, Mirages, Stinging, Poor Light, Echoing)

                                                        Nightmares: You Must Obey, Everything You Do Is Worthless, You Can't Wake Up, You Cannot Run

                                                        Atavisms: Eye of Heaven, Mimir's Wisdom, Needs Must, Unbreakable, Shadowed Soul, Looming Presence

                                                            Two little ideas that cropped up due to reading Lovecraft again...

                                                            Francis Thurston: A likeable professor, knowledgeable on anthropology and the occult as well as a lover of art, who takes care of his chosen students. He can be harsh when it comes to work, and working with him leaves people feeling drained and exhausted, but it also often ends up their best work yet. He considers it in human's best interest to stay away from vampires and werewolves and the like, and will scare them off if possible. On the other hand, he can be a cruel opponent that gives people searching too strongly for the supernatural exactly what they were looking for, to show them that they cannot handle that at all, and feeds by breaking their world, even if that means driving them quite literally insane. The lesson he teaches is a simple one: "Ignorance is bliss. Don't look too hard at the darkness."
                                                            Professor Thurston used to run with a small Krewe and gets along quite well with the local Kindred due to the amount of cover-up work he does, and likes to go diving in his free time.
                                                            Unlike many other Beasts, he likes to stay fed and relies more on his Nightmares.

                                                            Family: Makara

                                                            Hunger: Prey

                                                            Horror: Cthulhu - an gigantic abomination of man, dragon and kraken. With three sets of eyes, a greenish gelatinous body and flabby claws it is a mind-numbing monster that simply should not be. The Chambers are the island with alien geometry and the city of R'lyeh itself.

                                                            Life: Parental- Thurston deeply cares for his protégés, and goes to quite some length to make sure they stay safe.

                                                            Legend: Vicious- Thurston doesn't believe in half measures. When he gets serious, he leaves lifes (and minds) in shambles.

                                                            Lair: Mirages, Slick, Unstable, Exposed / Darkness, Flooded, Diseased

                                                            Nightmares: Behold, My True Form!, You Can't Wake Up, Flying and Falling, They are all around you, You can't take it with you

                                                            Atavisms: Mimir's Wisdom, Heart of the Ocean, Relentless Hunter, Looming Presence, Cyclopean Strength, Eye of Heaven


                                                            Richard Upton Pickman:
                                                            The Pickmans were a rich and respected family. Along came Richard, and things got complicated: diagnosed early on with a variety of derangements, he had always been sort of a creepy child. His devouring made everything worse. His family was helpless, and on the verge of giving up, when they met a therapist claiming to have a solution: While he most likely would never be able to take over the family business, Richard might be able to channel his... 'urges' in a creative manner. To his surprise, Richard found he liked that idea very much, and ever since found his very own niche. A crazy artist creating absurd, partly abstruse and always shocking pieces, Pickman is a sort of a fringe celebrity. However, unbeknown to most, each of his paintings are based on real situations: some Pure hunting humans, a Mummy punishing a would-be thief, kindred dueling each other, a Hero slaying a broodmate, the same Hero being taken down in revenge - he seeks out places and scenes where someone loses their most important posession: their live. From each scenery, he takes something to turn into his paintings (which are not on sale, just showcased). This could be actual blood for a painting, a brush or braiding made from a werewolf's hair, kindred teeth turned into tiny figurines, Promethean hide for binding a book, the strange symbols he saw in a mages book making up the edging, human bones crumbled and used as colouring, jewlery with inlaid soulstones etc.
                                                            Of course, if there is no murder or similar loss he can feed upon, he simply has to make his own food.
                                                            He recently started a new project: "Facebook".

                                                            Family: Namtaru

                                                            Hunger: Collector

                                                            Horror: A humanoid with a freakishly large, canine head and long limbs with horrifying claws, the Ghoul is a rotten creature that manages to disgust with its sight alone, let alone its smell of rotten meat and feces. It has no fur, so you can see it is practically blueish-green skin and bones, and has weird spiky growths on its spine. It would seem a dangerous animal if not for its eyes, betraying an abundance of cunning and intelligence and turning it into a far more scary monstrosity.

                                                            Life: Cautious - Pickman is a right bastard, one who knows how to stay out of trouble, and he won't hesitate doing anything necessary to stay safe.

                                                            Legend: Watchful - He is there, watching you. He sees what is happening to you, he might even be responsible for it, and if you are very unlucky, the last thing you'll see is his face.

                                                            Lair: Poor Light, Extreme Cold, Rotting / Stench, Toxic, Decayed

                                                            Nightmares: Run Away, Fear is Contagious, Your Rage consumes you, You are Lost, Tabula Rasa, They are all around you

                                                            Atavisms: Needs Must, Limb from Limb, Shadowed Soul, Basilisk's Touch, From the Shadows
