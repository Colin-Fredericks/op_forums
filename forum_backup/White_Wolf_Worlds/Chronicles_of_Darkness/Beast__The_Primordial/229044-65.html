    Originally posted by JTroy View Post
    Netflix has Nightbreed: the Directors Cut currently, so I just watched it. The movie itself is a classic, centering mostly around a place called Midian, the place where the Monsters go, and Boone, a guy who thinks he committed a whole bunch of murders and thus belongs there. Midian itself feels like it would be an amazing shared Lair. The monsters all all varied and individual, but also a part of a larger family of monsters that all trace their lineages back to legends of precursor races("the Tribes of the Moon") that over time have been wiped out. Led by a deranged psychiatrist/masked lunatic killer, Midian is eventually discovered by the worst monsters of all: redneck cops who think they are heroes.

    I feel like I am going to be drawing a lot of inspiration from that movie when I play Beast. It totally should have gotten the sequel that the end was so obviously setting up for.
    Man I am so behind...I didn't know that they finally released the extended version.

    The original storyline is inspiring for Beast. As opposed to the movie that was released, which was just sad. The movie was originally 145 minutes long, which was cut to 102 minutes. The extended version is I think nearly 160 minutes. I really need to see that version!
