                        I think the minions/thralls should definitely be considered to be "physically" present. One feature of imposing Lair traits is that the magic is in the summoning, not material effect. Imposing Downpour makes real water fall from the sky; Blazing Light makes real sunlight shine in the area. (it explicitly says that you can hurt vampires this way, which would only happen if the light truly is sunlight)

                        Carrying that over to Minions and Thralls, the magic is giving them purchase in the real world temporarily; you're not creating false bodies for them or anything, they're now there just as much as they are in the Lair. So I would say that there's no mechanical difference between an imposed minion/thrall and one in the Lair.

                                                            Originally posted by Tessie View Post
                                                            Are there any limitations on who can become the Apex of the area? Can ephemerals, extremely rad humans (with or without supernatural qualities) and/or non-kin such as demons become the Apex?
                                                            The only requirement is "inspires the most fear in the area," and the core book mentions that an independent ghoul is the Apex of Tokyo, so minor templates are fair game. But I'm not sure a 100% baseline mortal would inspire the kind of fear necessary to penetrate the Primordial Dream. Horrible things happen to people all the time, even things that cause Integrity loss, but it takes a supernatural twist to create a Chamber. I'd say the same limit applies to the Apex.

                                                            The problem with a Demon being Apex isn't so much that they're not Kin, but that the Apex has to personally inspire a lot of fear. Since Demons operate undercover (and under Cover) and indirectly, it's pretty unlikely they'd be the focus of enough fear to gain Apex status. Again, the core book gives the example of a powerful elder vampire who mostly operates through minions and catspaws as a character unlikely to become Apex; Demons generally fit a similar description.

                                                            But then again, these two points together could be a plot hook -- a seemingly powerless mortal (or weirder, a group of mortals) has gained Apex status, but when the brood investigates they realize it's actually a Demon.
