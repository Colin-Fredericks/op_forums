                                                            There were basically two successive conflicts. The North won the first (the Civil War). The South won the second (the insurrection against Reconstruction). History as usual was written by the victors - in this case the victors of said insurrection. Hence the half-century of the Dunning School dominating Civil War and Reconstruction historography. "It wasn't about slavery for the Confederacy! It was about State's Rights!" "The evil Radical Republicans oppressed the ex-Confederates!" The North historically went along with this because it served the cause of national (white) unity. It's only been since the Civil Rights movement that historians have seriously reexamined the history surrounding Reconstruction, and how its primary function (which it ended up failing at thanks to too many concessions to the ex-Confederates) was to assure the rights of freed slaves.
