                Family: Makara - It's odd, because although I love swimming I've always had dreams of drowning. A few years back I went scuba diving and about, oh, 10 metres or so beneath the surface my mouthpiece came out. I had to swim for the surface and for quite a while I kept dreaming about that. So yeah. Also, I often feel like I have no control over my own life, like I'm just drifting on the waves of expectations and such. You do school, then uni, then you get a proper job, you don't break the rules. That university's good, that course is good etc etc. That seems to em to resonate somewhat with the lack of control that the Sirens represent. The whole 'sea of humanity' theme is a thing as well. There are just so many people in the world, a number I can't even begin to put into terms that I can comprehend. 9 billion humans. What can I do that one of them can't do better?

                Nightmares:

                You Must Obey - for the aforementioned feeling of being caught up in a current of conformity and expectations. I think that this Nightmare is one I would have something of an affinity for.

                You Are Meat and Everything You Do Is Worthless - For the aforementioned 'seas of humanity' in which we drown.

                You Cannot Run and You Deserve This - to reflect the character of the Horror, as explained below.

                Atavisms:

                Alien Allure, Limb From Limb, Monster from the Deep, Looming Presence, Titanic Blow, Heart of the Ocean

                Lair: A placid, sunlit sea, dotted with islands more like stacks of rock. Between the islands, the sea falls away into the depths, and beneath and between them is the labyrinth of channels and tunnels in which the Horror makes its lair, studded with bright sea life, shining treasures and the polished bones of older would-be heroes. The reflected sunlight that fills even the deepest caverns plays tricks on the eyes, making them see things that aren't there or masking what is. A few passageways come up above the water level inside the various islets, and it's here where the majority of the true Chambers are, albeit still knee-deep in salt water.

                Lair Traits: Wondrous, Blazing Light, Currents, Maze, Suffocating, Mirages, Flooded

                Hunger: Power - I have a 'thing' where I'm really placid for a long time, and I tend to be quite a cool guy in general. then someone will say something or do something that just offends me - often on the other side of the world, like these 'white power' rallies - and I'll go off like a firework. I think that I would likely sate my Hunger with a kind of orchestrated vengeance against the people that offend me like that. It'd almost certainly be disproportionate to what they did to me personally, but damn if it wouldn't be satisfying to actually be able to do something about something like that, even if only on a local, ultimately inconsequential scale. Who knows? Maybe the legend will spread?

                Horror: Something vast and serpentine, beautiful and terrible. A sea-serpent with an alien grace to it, with scales that shine like jewels and teeth that glint like diamonds even as they turn your blood to ruby droplets on its hide. A thing of irresistible power and magnificence. Something unique, singular. A monster on the verge of godhood, but too evil to cross the line.

                    Now I am not going to lie, Beast is my absolute least favorite game line ( with Mage being a close second) of CofD. I always felt that Beast fell frustratingly short of what it was promising as a gameline, and I have just never been able to get myself to enjoy it due to knowing what it was SUPPOSED to be, and seeing what it instead became. As a result this will be rather short in comparison to what I would normally do, though I promise that aside from Mage, this will be the only one that is short and sweet.

                    Family: Makara

                    My greatest fear is the Ocean, specifically drowning in it. This wasn't always the case, and there was a time where I was avidly fond of the ocean and everything in it.. unfortunately those days are long, long gone. While this is veering into Real Talk more than I like to admit, I had a traumatic experience regarding a very deep body of water a good five- six years ago where I came perilously close to drowning to death, and it still deeply affects me even now to the point where I am terrified of swimming unless I can firmly touch the bottom of whatever I'm swimming in with both feet. If I were to become one of the Makara, I would probably immerse myself in the existence. If I can't get away from it, I will show everything what I felt for years and years..

                    Now as for what sort of beastie my Nightmare self would be, my mind drifts to the Rokea splatbook in Old World, specifically the Black Shark Rite, which forcibly and permanently melds ten or more Rokea into a nigh unstoppable, whale sized shark monster. What would be me, such a form reflecting the voracious abyss of the Ocean and how it would greedily suck me under and to my demise in all my worst dreams involving it.

                    Hunger: Power

                    This is rather cut and dry( haha), I would hunger to inflict the stark terror of knowing how easily the depths could snuff out a person, and how there is practically nothing that can be done to prevent it accept delaying the inevitable. Thrash about, kick and flail wildly in the depths, all you will do is exhaust yourself and waste precious breath before finally.. painfully having to inhale.

                    .... And there you have it! That is what i would see myself as. I don't have a need to go over Lair, Atavisms, or Nightmares because its too tedious to continue to talk about it due to lack of overall interest.. but I felt like it would be wrong not to participate ^^;

                                                    Behold, Leliel the Night Angel

                                                    Family: Ugallu. I am, above all else, scared of two things: Vulnerability and shame. Both tie into my dreams-when I try to sleep, about once a week, I have strange, surreal, chaotic nightmares usually involving falling, being judged, loneliness, or all of the above. Which then end suddenly when I come to with absolutely no mental effects other than annoyance that my sleep has been delayed. I can see that resolving into an alien Old Testament angel crossed with a Grey, embodying both the aspect of cool judgement without malice or mercy, and the border of what is known looking back at the world. This translates into a Lair based on old Semitic temples and advanced technology, embodying my love of myth and science fiction-as well as giving solidity to something beyond conventional human experience, one that understands you a lot more than you understand it, because I'm a high-functioning autistic-let's see how you like being an intruder into a foreign world. This is my safe space, the place I go to clear my head-and where no enemy can touch me. Here, I can be anything I want. Here, you play by my rules, and those who cannot will be shown just how literal that statement was.

                                                    Hunger: Punishment. This ties into the theme of my Lair being my refuge; it's also those of other people. Those who harm my refuge, or the people I am sheltering, will be repaid in full. It also ties into my interest in social justice; I can easily see me developing a similar ability to Null Snyper, learning to feed on people through information technology, especially toxic trolls. Off to the left is the Chamber I am most proud of, formed when I perfected the technique. Yes, it is an altar plus a server room. If you look closely, you can see the screaming faces of exposed KKK members on the taxidermied frogs.
