            Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post

            Since I am morbidly curious, how did your Namtaru's Infestation compare to Builder Bear and Tabula Rasa? (NSFW: fetus-related horror)
            Way more on the Builder Bear's side. Unborn fetuses erupting from the flesh, wailing and crying. All the stories of that short chronicle were created with the purpose of showing a facet of the game to my players and the thing with that Infestation was a particulary nasty Rampant. Made them question what they could become, what sort of things can belong to the Family and whether they still are prone to accept it or not

            Originally posted by ajf115 View Post

            I just thought that an aircraft-carrier-sized blob of living virtually invincible protoplasmic goop with enough strength to throw small mountains would qualify for Anakim.
            Don't get me wrong, they qualify. It's just that, as far as At the Mountains of Madness and The Shadow Over Innsmouth go, I see the mind-breaking effect their mere sight has on people as their most prominent feature. Shoggoths are repulsive, even for the standards of Mythos creatures. So horrifying that even Alhazred refused to believe they existed. We don't see them do much on the physical side but their appearance is enough. Mouth, eyes, protoplasm, "TEKELI-LI" and all that. Then again, it's strictly a matter of personal preference: there's absolutely room for both Families in them.
