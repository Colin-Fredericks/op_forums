    I saw this; thought it was intriging so I decided to share it.
    Originally posted by Nezumi-chan on rpg.net
    I think I've finally formulated what I like about the game, so far, give or take. (Crosspost from Tumblr, plus some additions I may cross-edit back to there)

    I don’t know. I’m still reading Beast: The Primordial, and there are a few worrisome things about the handling of Heroes (the fact that it’s not really a choice ups the horror and tragedy, but has some really unfortunate implications), and I can definitely see why some people are upset by making the “oppressed/marginalized group” analogue literal monsters that scare people to survive… but the entire game and metaphor are really, really resonating with my experiences as a trans woman and autistic person. Heroes are uncomfortably similar to eugenics and Autism cure advocates, to MRAs and other misogynistis, to TERFS and other anti-transers.

    And in a world where I keep getting told I hurt people just by existing, a character who owns that – that can minimize their harm or go all out, but who is what they are and doesn’t have to apologize or deny themselves – comes across as hugely appealing.

    Not to mention that I can absolutely get behind monsters who are actually monsters as PCs in an RPG -- who can be good or bad, who can try to channel it positively and minimize it or go rampaging, but who absolutely have to scare and hurt people, because they're monsters and that's what monsters do and why should we gloss that over or ignore it?
