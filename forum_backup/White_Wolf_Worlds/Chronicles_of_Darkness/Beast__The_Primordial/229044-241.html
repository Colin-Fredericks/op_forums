                    Originally posted by Mechalith View Post
                    Debates about morality, agency, etc aside my problem with the whole Devouring angle boils down to this: nobody in their right mind is going to say yes to the question "is it cool if I eat your soul and turn you into a living Scared Straight program?" The mechanical necessity/function of a soul in the NWoD notwithstanding, the popular conception of what constitutes your soul is a pretty big deal, enough so that most people aren't going to use it as a bargaining chip regardless of the payoff.

                    At least with Vampires the pitch is a relatively easy sell. You can see how someone would be willing to change their diet and give up tanning in exchange for wealth, immortality and some incidental super powers. With Beast though? "In addition to having your soul replaced with a Horror From Man's Darkest Imaginings, you'll have a slightly extended lifespan, some powers that are mostly good for smashing things and/or scaring the poo out of people, and sometimes the neighborhood loser will spontaneously come unglued and obsessively try to murder you."

                    While I have yet to meet anyone swayed by a sales pitch for a defining metaphysical change being presented in the form of a reductive, pop-culture referential, I have seen more than a few RPG players choose to have their characters endure unthinkably drastic psychic and spiritual harm in the name of acquiring power. You don't choose that path because you're antisocial or masochistic; you choose it because it's the kind of character you'd like to explore.

                    Also, you used the word "most." Player Characters seldom qualify as "most" people.

                    Crunch isn't a hobby; it's a calling.
