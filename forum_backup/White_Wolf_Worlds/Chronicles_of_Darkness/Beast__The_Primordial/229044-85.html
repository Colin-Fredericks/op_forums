                    Originally posted by Hawthorn View Post
                    Apologies for the double post but something hit me today while through skimming through first edition NWoD books to kill time. Notably I found the following in Book of the Dead:-



                    So am I the only one thinking within the context of Beast that the Leviathan would make an excellent "ancient" Makara Beast (maybe the first of its kind a direct child of the Dark Mother) that retreated into its Lair. Said Lair was of course linked to the Underworld. Within this same context you could nicely alter things so that the Leviathan is not actually a Kerberos just that Geist's think it is. Its really just a Beast of immense age and power.

                    Which does raise the question of using a Beast's lair that is linked to the Underworld to bypass the rivers and get deeper into the Great Below more quickly. For example having a Makara having a Lair that is a shore or lagoon with black sand (like the example Matt talked about as an example) would seem pretty easy to link to the Ocean of Fragments. That's presuming that otherworldly locations resonate with a Lair the same way suitable places in the material world do. I imagine if one decided to link ones Lair with another realm in particular in addition to the Astral it could work.

                    Regardless I think the Ocean of Fragments would be a lot of fun for a Geist/Beast crossover game to interact with. For example

                    Sin-Eater: Oh crap...that's the Kerbero of this realm, seems we broke one of the old laws. Time to go...

                    Beast: Nah that just my great-great-great-great-great-great-great gampa Leviathan, he's cool, little eccentric in his old age. (waves at Leviathan)

                    Sin-Eater: What's wrong with you! We need to go, as in now!

                    Beast: Chill man, trust me he's cool, look he just wants a hug.

                    (Giant tentacle grasps both characters)

                    Sin-Eater: We are so screwed...(starts to wrestle with tentacle for dear life)

                    Beast: Grandpa you give the best hugs! (hugs tentacle)

                    This is amazing.

                    (BTW, I wrote the Ocean of Fragments, so I'd love to say it was all foreshadowing for Beast, but that would be an utter lie.)

                                            Originally posted by Hawthorn View Post
                                            Also Dark Mother as a "rival" to the God Machine is an interesting idea I feel. She comes from basic concepts of a chaotic primordial world that predates the "order" of human culture and civilization. Something that the God Machine is heavily invested in and embodies thematically via a cold sterile/messy body horror take on order.
                                            The Dark Mother also seems to about primal fear and need, deep and immediate instincts, whereas the God-Machine is all about intricate plans and the long-term. Like you, I don't picture them as enemies - in a way, they're almost too opposite for that.

                                            Originally posted by Shadowflame View Post
                                            The problem I have with most of the above is that they're little more than socio/psychopaths. Primordial Beasts have to be better...have to be more than that.
                                            On the RPGNet thread, there was some talk of Hannibal Lecter as a Beast, but I thought it was more interesting to picture Will Graham as the Beast - he's intimate with fear and monstrosity, in such a way as takes him out of the world, and I could easily see his pursuit of serial killers in terms of a Hunger for Punishment, or even Prey.
