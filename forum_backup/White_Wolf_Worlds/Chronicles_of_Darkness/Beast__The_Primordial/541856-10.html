                Namtaru Collector: Beggar, vagabond, nomad, weirdo, freak, so many names humans call him. The Beast can be seen wandering into the streets asking for handouts and tends to stick around until he is satisfied. Eventually the people have little choice but to either give him what he wants or forcefully eject him from the premises (if they can). This Beast is the ugliness that can be bribed to leave you alone. Whether it's protection money or alms, he is content to go about his merry way once he gets what he's looking for. Those who prove themselves stingy or uncharitable will find the ugly vagrant lingering around the area fouling up the atmosphere. Even if he's chased off, he keeps coming back; the bane of security guards everywhere!


                The Namtaru's soul is a hideous, plague stricken man who's body has withered from starvation and who's skin is covered in unsightly blemishes. They are dressed in filthy, tattered rags with shaggy, unkempt hair. Their nails and teeth are discolored with grime and they walk with a hunched gait from malnutrition. But heroes should not underestimate this Gorgon, for those nails can quickly extend into filthy claws with teeth capable of noxious bites if they feel you need to learn some humility!


                The Beast's lair is a seemingly endless maze of street gutters and back alleyways with garbage cans almost every 20 feet. Travelers will find barely any food to sustain them, forced to dig through the garbage for scraps and eating whatever they can get their desperate hands on. It is a place that drives others to desperation, throwing themselves upon the mercy of others to save them from their suffering. Yet somehow, the Beast finds this despondent place comfortable enough to call it home.

                            I was reading about older portrayals of dragons in one of Bogleech's articles (may Nurgle bless him), and I got such strong Namtaru vibes that multiple connections came to me in quick succession.

                            The members of the Ordo Dracul are called Dragons, and their mission is to remove the weaknesses of the vampiric condition. Their mythical founder, Dracula, supposedly became a vampire as a direct curse from God, not an Embrace. The Ordo seeks knowledge both scientific and occult to benefit from this curse in explicit defiance of God's will.

                            Dragons are also mentioned as being part of Atlantean symbolism, at least in the first edition of Mage. The Awakened are too often vainglorious and haughty, thinking of themselves as gods, explicitly or not. The Mastigos in particular use many demonic symbols in their Imagos and elsewhere (or daemonic, rather).

                            One of the Catalysts in Genius: The Transgression, the Neids, become Inspired due to spite and scorn. In their section, the following is said about them:

                            In previous centuries, Neids were strongly associated with the demons of selfishness and envy. They were Satan the Dragon, and also linked with reinterpreted Norse myths of Loki or the drakes who guarded treasures. Catholic theologians classifying Leviathan as the demon of envy sealed the connections between Neids and Satanic, draconic forces, and Neids have ever since been referred to as Wyrms―behind their backs, of course.
                            Satan is evil and a deceiver who leads mankind to sin in the Abrahamic religions, whether he is a fallen angel, djinn or something else. However, Satanism and its sub-divisions often associate him with virtues such as independence and wisdom. Depending on who you ask, this character can represent a lot of things, and how much he enjoys his job varies, as does his skill and plans for retirement.

                            All of this got me thinking: wouldn't it be cool if there was an ancient Namtaru Nemesis + Ravager whose Horror was a great dragon, majestic to some and rotten to others? He would be an Incarnate with maxed out Lair and epic stats all around, but he doesn't confirm nor deny his origins. His current identity might be a prosecutor or something similar. He's a respected member of the community, but rumors of him abound. As overwhelming as his presence is, he's disarmingly human: if you see him, he's probably reading the newspaper while sitting on a bench in the local park, giving lectures and presentations on matters of how to improve the judicial system, or even going to church.

                            However, this Beast takes the teaching of wisdom through suffering very, very seriously. I'm just having trouble imagining how he can feed his dual Hunger in a way that lives up to his archetype. I'm trying to go for something different from the visuals of the celestial rebel or man of wealth and taste. A more down to earth deceiver who has broadened his view of human nature over the years, but still wields incalculable power and knowledge. His best friend may be someone who managed to learn something from him and became a paragon among his peers, like a Mastigos with Wisdom 10, for example.

                            Could anyone please help me flesh out this concept? I will try my best to clarify anything that might have confused you or sounded too vague.

                                Anakim Collector: Since ancient times, mankind has told stories of its people vanishing into the night without a trace. Snatched up by some monster who found them in a moment of weakness. Some Begotten continue to hunt in this way, but one in particular has discovered a way to profit from it! A member of the human trafficking ring, the Beast abducts humans at night and sells them overseas, likely never to be heard from again. The fear of his prisoners sustains him, while the money he makes goes towards funding other endeavors, such as helping the Family!

                                The Beast's true form is an enormous ogre with dark purple skin, snow white hair and wicked fangs. Standing nearly 13 feet tall and weighing 4 tons of muscle, the obese creature is garbed in lavish jewels and fine dress, all of which is paid for by his illicit business practices. He wears a robe made of white tiger pelt, though it is difficult to imagine what manner of beast produced the hide necessary to fit him.

                                His lair is a decadent palace with marble floors, golden pillars and red carpets. The heart of the lair is the Beast's throne room, where he receives visitors. Servants patrol the halls, catering to the demands of their Anakim master. At times, he has been known to let his relatives use them as well, especially if it is part of a business arrangement.

                                            Eshmaki Nemesis: In the primitive days of mankind, humanity was terrified of outside threats bringing about their demise. But over the course of history, mortals became more and more powerful, to the point where a single human with a button poses more threat to the entire world than some of the most ancient creatures in history. This Beast has capitalized on that fear, becoming a personification of mankind's hubristic need to grab power.

                                            The Beast's horror is a reflection of what modern warfare may eventually become if it does not learn to reign in its thirst for power. A cybernetic super soldier who's augmented eyes glow brightly in the dark. Its sleek body armor hugging to its body like a second skin, providing freedom of movement as well as protection! The electronic voice it speaks in further enhances the intimidation value, as do the retractable blades that emerge from its forearms!


                                            The Beast's lair is a dystopian city sacked by the ravages of war. Neon signs flicker on corner streets, advertising stores long since abandoned. There are no looters in the streets, anybody stupid enough to have remained in the city at the time of the attack are dead, their charred skeletons having been strewn about the city clinging to family members or whatever belongings they felt were more valuable than their lives.


                                            The Eshmaki feeds on those who crave more power than they need, usually those who are on top of the social ladder. Scientists, politicians, military officers, government contractors, anybody who has the potential to bring destruction to society. When it catches them, it shows them that there are some advancements mankind isn't ready for...

                                                    Anakim Predator: Her Horror is a thing of the cold. Appears as a human corpse mummified by the frigid temperatures of its lair so as to be androgynous. Its skin is frozen onto its bones and is harder than iron, its grip unbreakable. Its flesh is a pale, icy blue-white, the only splash of colour the dried blood around its mouth.

                                                    The Beast works as a guide for hikers in the wilderness near where she lives. She's a good guide, making sure that they know all the dangers of the forests. She sees them safely to the next way station and sends them on their way. When she gets hungry, though, she doesn't leave. She stalks the group when they go into the wilderness again. She separates one from the group and drives them out on their own. Then she calls down the cold, letting it creep into their bones and flesh until they can go on no longer. And then she comes to them when they're on the edge of unconsciousness and tells them exactly what they did wrong. Or, if she's especially hungry, she opens a path to her lair and lets the cold and the Wendigo take them.

                                                        Ugallu Nemesis: His Horror is a colossal bird, aquiline in appearance. Its feathers are harder and sharper than any mundane sword, its eyes are like mirrors, and its gleaming beak and talons are strong enough to rend apart steel. To those well versed in Filipino mythology, the Minokawa immediately comes to mind. But where that creature sought to devour the sun and moon, time and time again, the Raptor keeps his attention on the rest of his Kin.

                                                        The Raptor is old, older than he appears, and he is one of the very few who have become a Beast Incarnate. Even before his Inheritance, he involved himself heavily with the other supernaturals of his city, stepping in whenever anyone (or anything) threatened the status quo. Now, as the Apex, he is one of the most prominent powers in this side of the World of Darkness. And while his Kinship with a number of other supernaturals often keeps him Sated, he has no problem feeding his Hunger for Punishment on whoever tries to threaten the long upheld accord.

                                                            Anakim Tyrant: He's a detective, or a consultant at least. He's called in for the cases that no-one can solve, the ones where there's no motive, possible connections to the victim or no possible way that they could have been killed like that in that place. And he solves them Without fail, no matter how impossible, he always solves them. With some, he just looks at the crime scene and says that he did it and when they double-check, there's a connection. With others, he has to spend a little longer but he always gets them in the end.
                                                            His Horror is a creature of absolute precision, silver wires and crystal circuitry powering an intellect beyond any human's. It can calculate both the future and the past, predicting the movements of its prey and extrapolating probabilities from the available data. When it leaves its silvered nest to walk free it is a terrifying opponent. No matter what you try, it knows what you'll do even before you and has a thousand counters for every possible move you could make. It is the fear of redundancy, of being made inadequate and worthless.
                                                            And when the self-styled 'Hero' that came to kill it lies bleeding at its feet while it has not even a single wound upon it, it bends down and whispers in his ear.
                                                            "You are unnecessary."
