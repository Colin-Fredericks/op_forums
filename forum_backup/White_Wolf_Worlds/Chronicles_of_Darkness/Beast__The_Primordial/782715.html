    Which Primal-Fear would Bullemia/Anorexia be?

    12-09-2015, 06:37 AM
    Dear Onyx

    I'm attempting a *serious* discussion on a *crippling* and all too sadly real (and personal ) fear that plague countless people in Our World (and no doubt Both Worlds of Darkness): Bullemia and Annorrexia.

    Irrational though it may have been, to this day I cannot fathom how the mere thought of Dad's disapproving sigh if I weighed two kilograms too many on the weekly trip to the scales compelled me to consume, enjoy and THEN hurl up delicious oily food in guilt and self hatred.

    And thin as a wire my Highschool friend may have been, and hence in no danger of having a coronary bypass like I still do, I shake my head in sadnes and still wonder what possessed her to do the same as I did, until one forceful heave too many bursted a blood vessel in her brain.

    So my question is; no doubt the fear and anxiety caused bullemia and anorexia would be a rich hunting ground, ripe for beasts who would no doubt exploit it.

    But WHICH primal fear of the Beasts would be the root of Annorexia/Bullemia? Neither sickness of the heart seems to be able to fit into the primal fears of giants, darkness, creepy things, drowning or open spaces?

    Curiously Lin Liren

      Honestly? None. As serious of an issue that eating disorders are, they're not a primal fear; this mental illness is the result of a complex interaction of genetic, psychological, and social factors. Primal fears are the ones that are tied into our survival as a species, and they're exceedingly basic.

      I suppose, with anorexia and bulimia, you can argue that its tied into a form of social fear and ostracization, which ties into the whole Tyrant bits - a kind of "I'm prettier than you" social domination. In that light, it would be Hopelessness, which is the specialty of Giants, since you're measuring against a social giant. The two eating disorders you mention (there are more) are a reaction to the moments of social anxiety. Alternatively, its a Fear of Revulsion, with the hideous monster being your own self image. Like I said above, this is a complex thing, with its roots in a lot of different aspects.

      In any case, its moot, and honestly unimportant to the game as a whole. Beasts don't feed their Hunger on eating disorders. They feed on whatever triggers the disorders. Someone makes a comment about you being fat to trigger your bulimia? The moment that you feel worthless because of that comment is the moment of shock that beasts feed on. Everything that happens after is meaningless to the Begotten.

                      Feeding on it is a completely different matter than what fear it represents. When it comes to feeding your Hunger, the exact fear (what Families represent) doesn't matter in the slightest. Beasts really don't even feed on fear directly. They feed on shock and loss and pain. You steal something, when the person realizes they've been robbed, you get Satiety. You beat someone in a fight, even a fairly friendly if intense competition, and they admit you're better, you get Satiety. At best, you can argue that the Begotten is realizing the target's "fear," but it doesn't really have to be something they're really afraid of in the first place for that shock. Come home to find your husband sleeping with another guy, that will be a shock, it will feed Satiety, but no fear involved.

                      I often compare Beasts to sadists, because that's how they feed. In the case of bulimia or anorexia, a beast (low Satiety) going up to someone with one of these disorders and triggering an episode of not eating, or eating then purging? They get power from actively making other people feel bad. They can't pull strength from someone already in the midst of a bulimic purge. To feed your Hunger at High Satiety, you'd probably need to -make- someone into an anorexic or bulimic mess. That requries a lot of effort and cause a lot of pain just to make yourself feel better.

                      So, no, this isn't really a rich field to mine. No more than any other casual cruelty.

                          Having recovered from anorexia nervosa and worked in disordered eating treatment, I think it can depend. But an extremely common misconception is that eating disorders are about weight, and, therefore, about other's perception. While all of that can play a role, disordered eating is about control, and frequently arises in children lacking control in other parts of their life. Anorexia nervosa is nicknamed the little sister disease, and bulimia nervosa the big sister disease. The question only asked about those two, but I'm talking about all disordered eating, including BED and FED-NEC. I would probably classify it under Makara. But again, this is as a general statement, and there are exceptions to every rule.

                                  Namtaru is easiest. That said, there are a lot of potential angles of this depending on what perspective you are taking.

                                  Lets start with the perspective of the anorexic.
                                  * If you are presupposing that fear is the root cause of anorexia (this is a rather huge assumption, but also an appropriate one for beast) then you have a Namtaru that Hungers for Power.
                                  * If you take it from the angle of the person having a disease they are hiding from their families, you have Ugallu with Hunger for Punishment.
                                  * If you take it from the angle of the patient struggling with the disease, then you have to touch on the feeling of hopelessness against it. That would be Anakim with Hunger for Power.
                                  * If you feel overwhelmed at the disease or the treatment and want to just escape, that is Makara with Hunger for Power.
                                  * If you overcome it but feel you haven't really overcame it and you could backslide at any moment and oh no. That is the shadow one whose name I can't remember. Hunger for Prey.

                                  Perspective of the anorexic's support network
                                  * Feeling revulsion and confusion for the person with the disease is Namtaru with a Hunger for Prey.
                                  * Feeling scared that other people will find out your son/daughter/whatever has anorexia because you have failed as a parent / friend / whatever is an Ugallu with Hunger for Punishment.
                                  * Feeling scared that nothing can be done is Anakim with Hunger for Prey
                                  * Feeling overwhelmed by the situation you are forced in to is Makara with a Hunger for Prey. The distinction here is "I want to escape but can't."
                                  * The fear that someone may be Anorexic is ... the shadow ones whose name I cant remember. Hunger for Prey.
