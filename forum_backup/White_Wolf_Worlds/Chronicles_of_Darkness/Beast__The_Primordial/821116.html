                  I remember Secrets of the Ruined Temple mentioning Chimerical Beasts- essentially the Begotten long before the Begotten were even a thing -as some Temenos strangeness Mages may have to deal with. They were mythical creatures made flesh, creatures consumed by their hungers.

                  They also mentioned a second kind of enfleshed Goetics- Eidolons, ancient gods and heroes reborn. If that's not something to look into, I don't know what is.

                  That being said, I find the idea of Ancient Heroes somehow emerging from the Dream and wanting to slap their successors upside the head rather entertaining. What Heroes were originally supposed to be, people who faced the Horrors in the dark and took lessons from the conflict back to the people, sounds really quite awe-inspiring, genuinely heroic in my books. I'm already picturing how these guys would interact with Neolithic Wise as detailed in The Sundered World. I wonder where it all went wrong?
