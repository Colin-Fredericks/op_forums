                                        As to "Agency re: Devouring", I think there are two different kinds of agency getting confused here: Player agency and character agency.

                                        Character agency has been expanded with the change from Homecoming to Devouring, as the character has now chosen their Beastly state, and a character can choose to accept or refuse the "gift" of the Devouring.
                                        Player agency, however, has arguably diminished, as now you have to play a character that chose the life of a Beast, with all attendant features. Quite frankly, the deal itself is damn near Faustian, and either the Beast didn't disclose all the terms of Beast-hood to the proto-Beast, or the person is the kind of person that's totally fine becoming a spirit-monster that feeds on the fear of others. So you're basically stuck playing the fool, or somebody who willingly chooses to become a monster.

                                        I think that maybe Homecoming and Devouring should both be present. Marrying into the family, vs. being born into it.

                                        Unrelated tangent:
                                        Not to mention, your soul is being annihilated and replaced with a monster. I'm frankly amazed that what comes out of something like that resembles the original person in any way, to the point that it somewhat stretches my suspension of disbelief.

                                        Genius: the Transgression 2E is a thing that's being worked on.
