    Beast: How exactly do Lairs work?

    06-17-2015, 09:43 AM
    I'm confused how a Beast enters his Lair and merges with his Horror. For instance if I wanted to do so in order to heal.

    quote:
    -Healing in the Lair
    Beasts can enter their Lairs, merge with the Souls (Horror), and repair their bodies very quickly. This leaves them hungry, however. A Beast in her Lair can spend one point of Satiety to heal all bashing damage; three points of lethal damage, or one point of aggravated damage. If the Beast currently has multiple types of damage, the player chooses which to heal, but can’t heal different types with one expenditure. For example, a Beast with three points of aggravated damage and two lethal damage would have to spend four dots of Satiety (one for each aggravated and one for the lethal) to heal it all.-


    How does one enter their Lairs and merge with their Souls (Horrors)?

    quote:
    -Accessing the Lair
    An empty Lair is just a stage devoid of action, with the Beast’s Soul (Horror) wandering from Chamber to Chamber. The Soul (Horror) is capable of acting independently, but Beasts can see through the Soul’s (Horror's) eyes and direct its actions at any time, or vanish from the physical world entirely and fully inhabit their Primordial self.-

    Is this just the heading for the next group of abilities, like the Primordial Pathway? Because nowhere in the rest of the chapter does it discuss merging with the Soul (Horror) or 'Fully inhabit their Primordial self'. Except in the description of opening a Primordial Pathway.

    quote:
    -Pathways aren’t portals or magical doors. When opened, the Pathway causes the Chamber and the external location to “overlap.” This causes Beasts to merge with their Souls (Horror), as described on p. XX. Anyone in the external location attempting to leave while the Pathway is active instead finds themselves transitioning to another Chamber through one of the Lair’s Burrows. Anyone still in the Chamber when the Pathway closes ends up in the external location. Collapsing a Chamber immediately closes any Primordial Pathways open to it.

    Now, since I don't know what page XX. is referencing, I'm not sure where to look.

    If a Beast must open a Primordial Pathway every time the want to enter their lair, they must spend a Willpower Point every time as well?

    How long can they stay in their Lair, merged with their Soul (Horror)?

    Do they need to open a Primordial Pathway and spend another Willpower Piint and roll to leave and go back to the material world?

      Accessing Lair text mentions that they can do it easily from the location of their chamber. Any other location requires spending a point of WP.

      So, at least how I run it, if you go to the location for your Lair chamber, then there's no cost to try and get your Lair to overlap. if you wanna do it from another place, then you get to roll and you get penalties/bonuses depending on how similar it is to the actual chamber/circumstance of the chamber.
      Beast can stay in their Lairs indefinitely. However, most Beasts will have to go out of their Lair from time to time (to feed).
      When leaving, they do it for free, if they leave back to their chamber, or they spend willpower if they want to go somewhere else.

          How do Lairs work? Awesomely well! *cough* Okay, truthfully I don't know yet, but I want to try it out.

          How to enter the Lair:

          There are four methods. The first one is Soul Communion - just spend a reflexive action to look through your Soul's eyes, or a full action to control your Soul. You're not physically there, so you can't heal, but it may be relevant for other uses, such as potentially spying on someone's onerios from within your Lair.

          The second is to use a Skeleton Key. You'll somehow need to know the location of a Bolthole, Locus, Trod, or Avernian gate (likely by being told by a demon, wolf, changeling, or sin-eater) since you can't sense them on your own, but you can open them.

          Third is the Under the Bed method. Its a bit more complicated than the others - first, you have to indulge your Hunger in front of someone, then spend 1 willpower to make a connection. Whenever the target goes to sleep, you can walk up to the person, spend 1 Satiety, and jump in. There's a few more requirements, such as the target has to suspect you're responcable for causing the initial problems, may need the Family Ties Condition, causes nightmares and hostility in target, and may create a Hero through use. Generally, I think this is best if you have a favorite target to pick on long term, and the entering Lair part is incidental to messing with someone's head.

          Lastly, there's what I like to think of as the default method. Go to somewhere you made a Lair Chamber, or somewhere that resonates with your Lair (ie has one of the Lair Traits). Spend a point of willpower and roll; I think you need an additional willpower point if its just similar instead the exact place, making the total 2*. This way drags everyone into the Lair as the physical and Primordial Dream worlds merge. I consider the same qualifications you need for Imposing Traits (Beast p122) to apply to opening a Primordial Pathway. From the way the section is worded, I think that if the Beast is still in the initial Chamber when she closes the Pathway, she's ejected with everyone else back into reality. However, if anyone moves into another Chamber and then closes the connection, anyone in that second chamber stays in the Primordial Dream Lair.

          Now, for the next question. How long can you stay? Well, I assume that you're going to still need to fulfill your physical body's needs - its a merger, not a supplement. So, you'll need food, water, etc. You still need to feed your Satiety, though if you can reach from your lair to haunt dreams and feed that way, that's not a pressing issue. As long as you account for those things, I'd say you can stay in the Lair indefinitely.

          If you're using the Primordial Pathway method, you need to spend a willpower point every time you use the Path, and, yes, you need to open a new path (complete with willpower point*) to leave the Dream as well.


          * I could be mistaken about needing a willpower point by default, and an extra for someplace that's just resonant. If you only need 1 willpower to open up in a resonant area, and none for the original chamber, then you could open and close pathways to and from the Dream at will, basically. So, if you use the Pathway to get to the dream from Anywhere (TM), you can chill in the Lair, then go back to your Chamber Creation points at will.

          This is a reason why I think that a smart Beast will establish their Safe Place merit at the location of these exit points. Impose all the Traits, all the time! It gets scary with a brood doing this and sharing that one Safe Place trait in common.

                      When you open the Passway, you're not really in the Lair at point, you're kind of in an unstable halfway border realm. Its like crossing the Gauntlet - you need to move a bit deeper before you actually enter the Otherworld proper.
