    Originally posted by Wormwood View Post
    Got a question (or rather, a fetch quest) because I can't find it: Where was it explained what Chambers looked like? I somehow keep thinking they resemble the actual physical space, but with additional changes due to Lair Traits. That seems at odds with me thinking they can easily be whatever you want them to be. Anyone got a page for me? Pretty please? ^^"
    To expand on what nalak42 said, Chambers are definitely based on whatever physical location they were formed in, but also take on characteristics that befit the dreamlike and psycoreflective nature of the Primordial Dream. Beast is a very figurative game; things often look like what they do because "that's how this sort of thing is supposed to look." So in this case, I'd say start with the physical location and then overlay features based on the nature of the event that created the Chamber, the Family and Hunger of the controlling Beast and the controlling Beast's (or Beasts') traits.

    For example, an old warehouse that gets turned into a Chamber for a Talassii spider Horror might be filled up with giant spiderwebs. And if the Beast has Lair traits such as Maze and No Exit, then the warehouse might be filled with boxes that create a maze-like obstacle course for intruders to struggle through, unable to find a door or window to get out. BUT. If instead, a Namtaru whose Horror is a living pile of industrial effluent and who has Lair traits like Heat and Toxic (are those real? I don't have access to my books at work...) might instead turn the very same warehouse into insufferable cesspool with chemicals flowing down the walls and across the floor, and an oppressive heat sapping the strength of anyone who enters. And an Ugallu might have a warehouse that seems to be a mile across, utterly empty no cover or shade and bright lights shining down on intruders, who run and run toward the distant doors while something swings though the rafters far overhead...

                                                            Originally posted by Mad_Maudlin View Post
                                                            So one way of gestating a Horrorspawn is to carry it with you (as opposed to leaving it in your Lair until it's ready). Would a Beast gestating a Spawn look visibly pregnant in normal, human form? Or, since the Spawn is part of the Primordial Dream, is it more like a Fettered spirit superimposed on the human Beast when viewed through the right lens?
                                                            Related: this section claims that you can't just conceive and give birth to a Horrorspawn, and states that all Horrorspawn must be incubated in the Lair. It then proceeds to describe two different methods of creating Horrorspawn (one of which is highly similar to 'just' conceiving, though the act required is obviously more complex than that) both of which allow the Beast to carry the spawn around in their physical body if they choose instead of incubating it in their Lair, and the fluff that follows definitely makes it seem like a physical birth in the material world is going to happen.

                                                            The section on physical bodies describes that, after incubating in the Lair, the Horrorspawn don't have physical bodies and goes on to describe how one can be procured for them. Does this also apply to Horrorspawn that are carried by the Beast herself? Are they 'born' somehow bodiless? This isn't clear.

                                                            These sections kind of read as if the ability to carry and birth the Horrorspawn was a last-minute addition without further adjustments being made to the text to fully accommodate it.
