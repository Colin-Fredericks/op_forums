    OK, that's basically everything for a baseline of what happens when a Beast enters a dream. Now, why would a Beast want to? Well, Feeding is always a good motivator. Dreams are probably the best ratio of potential consequences vs potential gain possible, with even the death of the dreamer inside the dream only causing them to wake up. A Beast can go all out, well almost all out, (the penalty to mundane actions still stands) without worrying about the long-term ramifications of the High Satiety feeding. I mean the dreamer might get freaked out a monster burnt down their childhood home, but it was all inside a dream so they should be fine if a bit spooked. If we want to be a bit messed up, the Beast can abduct the dreamer's dream form into their lair, and (assuming their horror doesn't prey on the dreamer) they have made sure the dreamer's body is in a coma. Any enterprising Beast with Doppelganger can probably see the potential, no need for Shrouds of Observation this time. It's also not a bad way to communicate with/manipulate a cult, or hang out with Incubi/Hobs/Changelings for kinship and friendship.

    The main problem is that entering dreams is both unreliable and willpower expensive. I imagine most Beasts interested in messing with dreams would look into ways to mitigate those disadvantages. This might be a decent area for a homebrew Obcasus Rite. Do a ritual to curse the target with nightmares, that provide enough resonance to enter the Bastion with Pathways. A merit building off of Stalking Ground could do something similar, giving easy access to someone's dreams. If nothing else, Feeding on someone is likely to have that event play out again in their dreams.

Family Dinners with accompanying nightmares is well and good, but "I can shortcut past the things making dreams a source of indirect access to the soul and gain meaningful sustenance from direct feeding there without significant consequence" doesn't feel like it should fly.

                        I mean...would a Beast know either? They might see it as a temporary zone, like a lesser part of the Dreaming that is only accessible for a moment.                         Beasts know when they're in the Astral and when they aren't, because they can feel which way their Lair is and literally physically travel to it. Per the "The Call of Home" sidebar in the BPG:

                        "This uncanny homing sense also serves as a warning when a Beast really wanders off the beaten track — it only functions within the Astral Realms, so a Beast who follows a Primordial Pathway out into an unknown part of the universe is instantly aware that she isn’t in the Dream or its neighbors any more. That the homing sense doesn’t work in the Hedge tells Beasts exploring Kinship with changelings that while they may both explore dreams and nightmares, wherever the fae escaped from isn’t in the Dream."

                        One new thing to bring up is apparently the Poster who started the fan-made Night Horror that started this thread desires to have rules for Changelings exploring the Primordial Dream. Outside of an Obcasus rite to have a permanent portal between the Hedge and the Dream, is there a way to facilitate that?
                        The Dukhan vampire clan from One Thousand and One Nightmares in Dark Eras 2 has a Devotion that allows them to project their consciousness into the Primordial Dream through an unclaimed Chamber with Lair Traits in common with their current location; given that "Sometimes, a great and powerful beast crosses the path on its way down roads where the Fae cannot follow" is an actual line from the Dreaming Roads' writeup, a case could be made for a Contract or a Merit modifying a Contract to allow a changeling to use a limited form of Hedge navigation off the path in the Dreaming Roads to reach a Chamber that's part of a hive.
