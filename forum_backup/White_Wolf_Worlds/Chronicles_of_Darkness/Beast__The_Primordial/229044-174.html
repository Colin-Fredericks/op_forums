                                            Vamps can be harmless if they work to keep the or BP low and only eat animals. It isn't a pleasant existence, but it is possible. And on the more powerful end, they gain nothing from hunting mundane humans and essentially become Hunters, seeking and destroying other supernaturals. But most will have to eat people, which is (on a species level) going to be harmful for humanity, even if they only tried to target "bad guys" and those who consented (not getting into whether it was coerced, uniformed or fully informed consent).
