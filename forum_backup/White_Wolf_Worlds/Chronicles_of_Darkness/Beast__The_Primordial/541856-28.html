        Hey, what about the Defenders?
        Luke Cage, Daredevil etc. reimagined as a Brood doesn't seem that farfetched to me (although Deviant or a microtemplate is probably better if you wanna be more accurate, but they'd just be an inspiration here).
        Haven't seen Jessica Jones yet, but I think they'd fit the bill:

        Luke Cage is an Anakim Ravager who has a bone to pick with people that treat others poorly and drag them into their dirty world. He's coming at them with Unbreakable, Iron Skin and its upgrades to represent his taking hits, and Cyclopean Strength to show his increased power, as well as Looming Presence since he's tall and can threaten a lot of people without them even knowing about his skillset. Lait traits probably include things like Decayed, Cramped and maybe Extreme Heat.

        Daredevil would be an Eshmaki Ravager out to destroy criminal activity, with Shadowed Soul to represent his Super-Senses/Blind-Sight, Relentless Hunter to represent his repeated taking of a beating and getting up again and again and again, and Eye of Heaven and Needs Must to account for his people skills and ability to find trouble. Maybe add in From the Shadows. Lair Traits would likely be Darkness or Poor Light and something like Echoing or Mirages (Noises making people think he is somewhere he isn't.) His Horror would probably be a literal Version of his Daredevil suit. He definetly knows some parkour.

        Iron Fist comes pre-packed with a Horror: Shou-Lao, the undying Dragon. Imo, he's a Makara Nemesis out to defend Rand from anything that would mar his father's legacy, with Limb from Limb, Titanic Blow, and Alien Allure. His Lair Traits include Blizzard and Extreme Cold, as his Lair is the Pass outside K'un-Lun. Lots of different fighting styles.

        What do you think?
        Adding to that, Fisk as an Anakim Tyrant (A terrifying, indestructible kingpin), Cottonmouth as a Makara Nemesis (a vengeful ruler of a criminal underworld), Diamondback as either an Anakim Ravager, or possibly Nemesis (He wants to fuck Luke up, but he seems to really like destroying things), or possible a Hero after Luke. Kilgrave is an Anakim Predator, or possibly Tyrant or Ravager if you want to focus on the powerlessness or loss of control, and Punisher is an Anakim Nemesis.

        Personally, I'd actually peg the "enhanced individuals" as mostly Deviants, but you could look at them through a Beast lens.

        Genius: the Transgression 2E is a thing that's being worked on.

                    They call him 'Ronin,' supposedly because he's a disgraced warrior. Something about being exiled from his original hive and now he can't find anywhere to truly call home. Instead, he travels the world, looking for something to occupy his time. He's lost count of how many humans he's defeated, many of them being professionally trained hunters. He's even taken out some unruly siblings who were making life miserable for the rest of the family. The hoard he collects are trophies from battle, the very weapons that his enemies sought to use against him. His most coveted trophies are those of former Heroes, adding their so called 'chosen blades' to his arsenal and using them against future Heroes. But these trophies are very rare and difficult to acquire, so he keeps them under lock and key until he needs them.

                    His lair takes the form of a Japanese village set in an nondescript medieval period. It is a moon lit night and it is raining quite heavily. Not many people would be walking the streets at this time, save for Ronin. The storm doesn't bother him, in fact, it helps him. Anybody brave or foolish enough to go walking around in the rain in the middle of the night is probably asking for trouble, and Ronin is happy to provide! Like something out of an action movie, he appears with a flash of lightning. For a moment, his shadow appears impossibly large before it fades to reveal his actual image.

                    Family: Anakim

                    Hunger: Collector


                    Horror: A warrior dressed in feudal Japanese gi, along with a necklace of thick prayer beads, a straw hat, and wooden sandals. His skin is the same shade of blue as moonlight and his teeth bare a set of sideways curving fangs that force him to keep a perpetually sneering expression. he has a full set of hair both on the scalp and facial area. It grows in spiked tufts and is black as the night's sky. Even beneath his gi, it is obvious that he is packed with muscle, being in peak physical condition.He is also usually carrying a weapon, which he wields with one hand, no matter how heavy it may appear to be!


                    Lair: Downpour, Poor Light, Thunderous

                    Atavisms: Titanic Blow, Cyclopean Strength, Storm-Lashed, From the Shadows

                    Nightmares: Fear is Contagious, You are not Alone, Run Away

                                    You know how Saitama from One Punch Man suffers from boredom because he can't experience conflict?

                                    I was thinking of creating an Incarnate Beast who is in a similar situation to Saitama, but in order to prevent their Myth from being Destabilized, they teach people not to seek victory and power for their own sake. Instead, they act as this martial arts master and spiritual guru of sorts who no one can beat but is said to be very wise, so people seek them out for all sorts of advice. They get to Feed their Horror by demonstrating their immense fighting skill and cunning, and teach the Lesson of "embrace your conflicts for they will make you stronger". And if anyone threatens their students over this Lesson, they show up to protect the students. Think Mister Miyagi and Master Shifu. They would be an Anakim Tyrant.
