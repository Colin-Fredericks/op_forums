                                            Originally posted by WarDragon View Post
                                            I'd never heard of Synx before. I googled them.

                                            What the fuck did I just read?


                                            Basically, they're a species of (seemingly) genderless parasites who evolved inside the bowels of colossal, space dwelling animals. It's not specified how or why they decided to leave their hosts, but something caused them to abandon their parasitic nature and become more predatory.


                                            Despite their size and appearance, they're are poor fighters and prefer to go after prey that's too weak to fight back. This typically includes children and the elderly, but they will target healthy adults if they're desperate enough. They prefer to consume their prey whole, and often very much alive. They also have the ability to turn their stomachs inside out, should they desire a faster way to swallow their prey.


                                            While a Synx can mimic speech and voices, their brains are like those of simple fish. They're just parroting what they've heard, without actually comprehending what it means.


                                            The hyper contortionist imagery you might see with some Synx images is due to the fact that their demonic appearance is typically a disguise. A Synx's anatomy is more like that of a leech, or a worm, rather than a true humanoid. The appendages resembling arms and legs are actually a set of tentacles, which makes them abnormally flexible compared to normal limbs.
