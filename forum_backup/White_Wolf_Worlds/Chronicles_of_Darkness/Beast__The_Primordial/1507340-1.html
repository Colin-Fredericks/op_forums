    So way back in the wayback machine (2019, it was 2019), when the madness overtook me, I went to Cinder and came up with a laundry list of priorities for what would actually need to be done with Beast. The first two were unanimous-Heroes, and then the Astral/Dreaming. I don't remember, and can't check right now, if Cinder agreed with me on the third, but for me, expanding Kinship was the next major thing-not that what we had wasn't good, but there was definitely more to do with it. My main thing was ripping off Golden Sky Stories' main advancement mechanic and making sure Kin got more out of the relationship (Not that it matters all that much for the Beast, the bigger thing for them) was having gradiated access their kinship benefits so they have more reason to invest into a relationship. But beyond that, I wanted to cover different layers of Kinship by adding the sub-categories of Beast Kinship, Family Kinship*, and Human Kinship.

    My main interest was that I was fascinated by the metaphysical position Beasts have as Temenotic Goetia** meaning that they were a multitudinous entity-or, more accurately, they were a part of a whole lot of other people who had those fears and hungers. That was a angle Beast never really tackled or took advantage. There were a lot of other benefits to Human Kinship, in theory-Beast could directly get into how all fiction has a level of psychological metaphor in it, it allowed us to more readily begin constructing Beast's unspoken notion that it's neck of the world has the internal worlds and external world is one as a more solid and open thing, and it allowed people to more readily understanding Beasts as psychological actors (if they chose to be) and how that ties into their role of mythmakers and legend-takers-but mostly it was a chance to get weird and I like weird.

    Of course, it had to be distinctive from other Kinship, and that became doubly true when I grokked onto Kinship being akin to Deviant's Touchstone "Integrity" System. That there was a power in not really being able to understand themselves by direct comparison to humanity but in how the monsters they related to related to humanity, and how that reflected back on that was a bonus way to think about it and cut a line between the two. Plus my argument about the interesting metaphysical difference has it's clear difference-Beasts are Astral Symbols of the idea of monster and feel kinship to those who arguably bear the same semiotic device, but with humans they get more into the meaning and reason behind the creation of those symbols in the first place.

    (I don't have any place to put this, so I'll just take this interrim to mention I don't think we need really do anything for understanding Kinship and what allows for it-Beasts have a different relationship with humanity than with monsters, and thus it follows, and what is and is not Kin is pretty clear cut, my main evidence for that being that most people were questioning if Deviants were Kin while my handful of remaining questions of what counts include those with the Cursed Merit and the question of if the Ascending Ones' alchemy actually counts.)

    I'll lay out the three particular ideas I had, but admittedly the big thing is that while the whole idea feels meaningful and weighty, that it has bearing that could be of real aid to Beast, it's hard to actually get down into what it should do.

    1) Touchstone. Even with the comprehension about Kinship and it's Integrity role, pretty much everyone I know has said that some kind of Touchstone would be a good idea-and I agree, but I do't know why, mostly because I can't really fathom what it would do. Asides from laying a fat thumb of the scales for Idea 5 from normal Kinship in favor of being associated with human inclined entitites, I'm not quite sure what a Beast should get out of it, or what a touchstone might get in kind.

    2) Knowing THose Like Unto the Beast. THis idea is basically letting Beasts know if someone around them would be in their own Family as Beast, if they would have the same Hunger as them if they were a Beast, or both, and perhaps getting to know the specifics at the cost of becoming a little too much like the person, possibly temporarily taking on Aspirations or what not. Issues of treading on Needs Must and how cheap this makes looking for a Satiation victim be asides (and those are already big issues), it's also....fuck, I knew there was an additional problem, but I can't think of it.

    3) Basically the Lore System. I wanted to give a hook where Beasts could tempt them to their more assertive sides, (even monstrous sides) by offering up their benefits to someone who decided to break societal taboos (Or, hell, just needs the chance to meaningfully step out of their shell) to get what they wanted, allowing them to act just a little more Beastly-and of course, the ability to have it bite them in the ass.

    Over time, I've become dissatisfied with those ideas, but I still feel like Human Kinship is something that should be done, sooooooo yeah, what does that actually look like?

    *Ask me about Beast and Family Kinship sometime, only don't, because I'll cry.
    *Which is now World Soul/Mother's Land/Anima Mundi Goetia laquered up in a lot of Temenotic coping shit, but whatever.

                    I’ve always thought one of the main not really explored parts of Beast was empathy. A Beast gets it, really gets it. They know both being the monster, and being the prey. I think any Kinship must start there. And you know, people can be both too. I don’t think it needs to be anywhere near Needs Must, but I think with even the basic level of Kinship should be able come to a better understanding of what the other person is about. Maybe Needs Must expands this ability to read people’s past Kin. Maybe it’s a much deeper and more specific read.

                    Um, on a slightly different track, touchstones are stabilizing influences. They keep one rooted to reality. Maybe this will sound crazy, but what if Beast Chambers didn’t naturally stick around? What if they required upkeep? Having someone connected to that location, and connected to a Beast through Kinship might help keep it around. And, Insatiable, right? They can steal Chambers and Lairs. (I think, still haven’t bought the Night Horrors.) Having a multiple Touchstone might help keep that from happening, by stabilizing the Chambers as something that’s part of the Beast, and themselves. Kinda like a sleepwalker holding a spell in Mage.

                    So here’s an another weird idea. I’ve been thinking of a group of very tight knit friends who all “share” the Horror. Really, only one of them is the Beast, but they all are close enough for it to work and feed through them and their actions. Might be a double edged sword, to share your heart with someone else so deeply, though. Even if they aren’t the full thing, Anathema can be placed on any of them to affect the rest. Might be an idea for Max Kinship with Humans.

                                Usually my preferences hew towards greater mystery and complexity, but in this case, there's a few reasons I don't swing that way.

                                1) From a purely practical standpoint, the bookkeeping of Kinship can be a pain in the ass if Beast's have to go and ask "Do they count as Kin so I can use a major portion of my tool kit?" Keeping it simple, maybe even making it simpler, let's the game just play.

                                2) Given the role of semiotics in Beast, the icon of monster is inclined to have more housed in it than not, and it would become increasingly incoherent to create more variation on what does and does not count based on the metaphysic Kinship works from.

                                3) Also, from a thematic point, the world of Chronicles is not pre-disposed to lean towards subjective influence-the world has more objective forms and powers and nature, and public perception only rarely has affect. Now Beast is a good place to play around with that, because as you mention in a bit, Beast is a game of managing your brand-but there's a floor to how much I'd go with that.

                                4) On a human care perspective that relates to the humanism of the series, I'm not hyper comfortable with the metaphysical label of monster applying to people, particularly the disadvantaged of our world, in the same certifiable way one actually applies it to the actual monsters in the series. I think there's a line between a Namtaru who toys with the fear of the scarred and disabled and people who are just scarred or disabled that crossing would only do bad things for. The metaphorical and supernatural is one thing, but the instant we say "You are monster because people think you are" in world with actual monsters gets...again, it gets uncomfortable.

                                I'd rather keep the qualifications of Kin simple and finite for entry. In terms of expanding it, I'd rather make it deeper and more relationship work rather than murkier, harder to work with, and somewhat unfortunate.

                                A first thing that comes to mind: You mentioned that you see Beast as a game of managing your brand, and I like that approach. Could Touchstones help you stay grounded, so you know which parts of your reputation overlap with your true identity, and which could break you if you tried to live up to them? I feel that Anathema could be relevant, somehow.​
                                An interesting notion that leans also into Satchel's suggestion, but I run into the issue of "What does that look like?"

If they succeed at it, something not minor, but fixable happens, like a chamber needing repair. If they get an exceptional, then they get an Anathema that makes “sense” for their archetype of Horror.

                                        I had a thought on this on the way to work, so:

                                        As I mentioned, I'm not comfortable with passively allowing humanity's beliefs to change a person's metaphysical mark-just because humanity believes something doesn't make them right-but there is something in the angle of imposition that's a big deal of Heroes that I can get behind. Passively believing someone is a monster won't do it, but I think when we get around to hammering out how people can manipulate the Dreaming, both deliberately and accidentally, as well as how the Dreaming can intrude as a result of actions, the "Force you to be Kin" thing can be viable. But again, it can't just be passive belief, there has to be something where someone(s) take that passive belief and use it like a hammer or a spear. And of course, I'm also open to Heroes having the ability to, at cost, brand someone as a monster with their metaphysical authority (Probably 3 Satiety).

                                        Asides from coming off as "Kin, but it feels wrong" for Beasts, it also creates some fun where there's an ongoing risk that such shit can result in a person being a beacon for supernatural forces, and an ongoing risk that someone becomes a monster as a result. And that fits in with one of the other things I want to see going forward, being the involvement of Astral Gods and mechanics for them making monsters out of people-having humanity en masse be able to do similar is appealing.
