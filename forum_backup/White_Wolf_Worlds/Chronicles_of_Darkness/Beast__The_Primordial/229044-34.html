                                                            Based on absolutely no inside knowledge whatsoever beyond what I could glean from this forum, I'm beginning to think that Dolarhyde (i.e. the Tooth Fairy) from Red Dragon/Manhunter may be a good model for a Beast, with some tweaks. He was already inhumanly strong and tough (look at the number of shots it took to kill him in Manhunter) and quite cunning; those could be jacked up further. I could also see his teeth (which were prosthetic in the book and films) actually becoming a supernatural feature that he can "pop" as necessary to ruin people. As Dolarhyde becomes more "hungry" and needs to hunt another family, people begin having nightmares about the Great Red Dragon and Will Graham becomes a Hero.
