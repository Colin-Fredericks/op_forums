    Thoughts About Genii And Beasts

    12-06-2022, 05:07 AM
    Both Genii and Beasts are outsider-figures whose actions transgress against the status quo in ways that ideally elucidate the problems of society, but seldom actually fix those problems. On the other hand, Beasts are also living cautionary tales about what happens to people who break rules, and Genii are eminently trangressive people. The traditional scenario is "mad scientist creates a monster that is an affront to God and the natural order, and dies at the claws of this monster as a form of karmic punishment for his hubris". But there is more to it than that, of course. Genii usually attempt to construct a better society by enacting some form of paradigm-shift, while Beasts usually want to deconstruct the assumptions of society that they consider negative.

    Beasts are technically Goetia-Claimed, and Goetia are embodiments of ideas, and Genius has "The Power of Ideas" as a core theme. There is cross-over potential in both the resonance and dissonance between their natures. A Genius might look at a Beast and think "you're just an atavistic throwback, but maybe I can help you be of use to humanity...". Meanwhile, the Beast might look at the Genius and say out loud "I like your funny words, science-man. I'll show you all sorts of things to give you ideas, if you join my extended family..." One side wants to bring a torch to the darkness and reveal potentially useful knowledge, but the other side wants to prove the darkness did not become irrelevant for humanity's collective imagination. People mainly fear what they do not understand: Genii are promoters of understanding, and Beasts are harvesters of fear. But what if the roles could also be inverted?

    Genii can use Wonders irresponsibly and thus affect the Astral with fears of technology going wrong or otherwise being used for evil, and Beasts can be drawn to that fear and serve as living warnings for Genii whose actions are literally corrupting the soul of humanity with a desire to go backwards in time to prevent Scary New Tech From Ruining Everything, which is EXACTLY the scenario that Lemuria wants. It could be thematically fitting for Lemuria to keep Beasts as slaves meant to eliminate Peers, like Bond Villains throwing 007 into a tank full of sharks. But what if that shark suddenly started talking like a person and said "I don't actually wanna kill you, you seem like a cool person. Wanna team up so we can both get out of here?"

    Genii in general would strive to prove that the primordial fears represented by Beasts are obsolete, but their Wonders create new types of fears about rampant, anti-human progress. I think you need to have Beasts who are morbidly supportive of their Genii-Kin due to their shared status as idea-born monsters ("I went into the local Goblin Market and brought you this hobgoblin brain you can use as a Component, because you are epic!"). Contrariwise, you need to have Beasts who think Genii should be scared straight due to the latter's Transgressions ("Remember Jurassic Park, Mister Progenitor? Well, I'm a bit of a Tyrannosaurus myself, and I'm getting hungry..."). And between those two extremes are the Beasts who would go "is your master plan supposed to be scaring people this badly? I'm a psychic-hybrid monster and I can literally see and hear humanity's collective nightmares about your plan going horribly wrong. It's kinda awesome, but at the same time, maybe stop and rethink some aspects?"

    Also, here's a fun thought: Genii could be very loosely considered a "variant" of Beasts, but they are Claimed by Goetia of the Bright Dream, thus giving them mastery over technological forces rather than primordial fears. How much of that explanation is purely metaphorical, and how much of it has metaphysical significance?

          Goetia generally don't have Manifestations, but there are circumstances in which they could gain them or be forced into them. Larval horrors are indeed Goetia, though of a kind that's distinct from other kinds of Goetia.

          But Beasts are not Goetia-Claimed in the slightest. Claiming does a very specific thing, and what Beasts go through and what they become is very much something else.

The Horrors are special kinds of Goetia because they can Devour people to make them become Beasts, in a roughly analogous manner to certain Ghosts being able to turn people into Sin Eaters via the Bargain. Which begs the question: what is a "Horror but from the Bright Dream" called, exactly? An Eidolon, a Paragon, an Icon, a Torch, a Sword?
