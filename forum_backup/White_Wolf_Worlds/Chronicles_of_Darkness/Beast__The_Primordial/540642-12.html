            So here's something.

            You Cannot Forget

            They said that oblivion is a gift to the human mind. No, you will not enjoy that gift. Now that you know, you will never forget.

            Dice Pool: Intelligence + Satiety vs. Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance

            Normal: An idea or information takes root in the victim's mind, and she can't get it out. She gains a non-persistent version of the Obsession Condition, where she becomes obsessed with preserving and spreading the knowledge. Also, the loss of 10-again also applies to any breaking point rolls or equivalents she suffers in pursuit of the obsession.
            Unlike most Nightmares, this Nightmare requires not eye contact but speech as a vector.

            High Satiety: The knowledge warps and spasms within the victim's mind, driving him to further fixation. He gains the Deprived Condition, where he is addicted to spreading the implanted information.

            Satiety Expenditure: The victim's mind gets chunks of it ripped out, replaced by the knowledge implanted by the Beast. Both the Obsession and Deprived Conditions become persistent. Furthermore, the knowledge cannot be removed by natural means. The victim will remember it clearly after decades, and neither old age nor brain damage affects it. This time-proof memory is independent of the inflicted Conditions. Curing them simply means she is not inhibited by the knowledge. Removing the memory via supernatural means triggers a Clash of Wills where the Beast rolls Intelligence + Satiety.

            Exceptional Success: The Beast is able to change what information the victim remembers and becomes obsessed with. He still needs to speak the knowledge to his victim, but does not need to roll.

                You Cannot Stop

                You have to keep going. You're nowhere near your limit. You cannot be. You have to keep going. Just until the next tree.... no, the one after that... the next one....

                Dice Pool: Manipulation + Satiety vs. Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance

                Normal: The victim becomes obsessed with a certain simple and repetitive action, doing it again and again - be it trying to dunk that ball for the 200th time, swim some more, run another mile, keep writing that same page, solving rubik's cubes again and again etc. The Victim gains a temporary Obsession of the player's choice, but it cannot be too out of character (a cook will not suddenly become obsessed with martial arts if he hadn't harbored an interest beforehand). For a scene, the obsession cannot be shed. The character takes moderate care of themselves, and stops when taking bashing damage due to the action.

                High Satiety: Obsession is one thing, being totally one-minded something else entirely - the victim gains 8-again on his Obsession. To attempt a roll not related to the obsession takes a Willpower.

                Satiety Expenditure: They victim completely disregards health and safety, and keeps repeating their obsession to the point of total collapse - they will write through night after night (just that one same page though, this is no cure for writer's block!), or keep running until their legs give in. Only the most minute distractions are tolerable. Taking lethal damage as a direct result of the action (e.g. falling due to failing to climb higher) ends the effect.
                (Think Forrest Gump-levels of 'I'll go run a bit')

                Exceptional Success: Even taking Lethal damage does not stop the character from repeating the action.

                    I Know Your Weakness!

                    You cannot match him - everytime you try something, he counters you, as if he'd known exactly what you would do. Why even try? You're just gonna get hurt...

                    Normal Effect: The victim loses their Defense when opposing the Beast for a scene, and suffers a -2 penalty on attacking them for fear of being hit.
                    High Satiety: The Beast may chose a different person to enjoy the benefits of this Nightmare instead. In addition, each time the victim takes more damage than their Composure, they have to roll Resolve+Composure or suffer Beaten Down.
                    Satiety Expenditure: The victim suffers from a Delusion, believing even the Beast's slightest touch causes them tremendous pain and rips them to shreds, and reacts accordingly. The Beast and placeholder can cause Tilts like Leg Wrack and Arm Wrack with but a touch, even if otherwise this weren't possible: the arm is not really broken, but in the victim's mind it is, and thus they cannot use it. In addition, the victim's Stamina is considered to be 1 for purposes of inflicting Knockdown or similar.
                    Exceptional Success: The penalty increases to -4.

                            You Are a Tool (Wizened Changeling/Unfleshed Promethean/Princess Kinship)

                            Helping yourself is not only harder than helping others, it's immoral. You mean nothing except when you serve other, more important beings

                            Dice Pool: Manipulation + Satiety vs. Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance

                            Normal Effect: Victim gets -3 dice to any action that involves helping only themselves. When assisting others in teamwork actions, the victim adds the appropriate skill dots as a straight equipment bonus instead of the usual teamwork roll.
                            High Satiety: Victim gets Obsession(Doing my job) condition until the end of the scene, with "job" defined as either their Professional Training merit profession (if they have it) or their Specialty for the highest skill rating most integral to the character concept. This condition can never directly influence them towards doing something wholly suicidal or that would otherwise cause a breaking point
                            Satiety Expenditure: Victim Gains the Thrall Condition, but it resolves at the end of the scene.
                            Exceptional Success: Victim cannot move at all without either a successful Resolve + Composure roll for every action that takes up a whole turn or sensing an opportunity to help someone else, except to dodge incoming attacks (they keep their Defense). Additionally, at the end of the scene, the target will not remember anything that happened during that scene, for they as a person did not really take part in it.
