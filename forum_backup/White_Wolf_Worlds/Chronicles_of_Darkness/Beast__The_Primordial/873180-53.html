    Originally posted by Arduras View Post
    Is it actually written somewhere that you can't raise Satiety unless the Satiety Potential on your feeding roll is higher than your current Satiety?
    P108, BtP Core Book:

    "If the Satiety potential is equal to or higher than the Beast’s current Satiety level, the player rolls the Satiety potential as a dice pool."

    So if the pool is lower than current Satiety... you don't roll it. It's just not enough to satisfy your horror's hunger. There is no automatic gain of satiety, you just don't get anything at all. It is effectively, an automatic failure (though not d.failure).
