                                      And again, in plain language so the message gets across: Lair Traits are not necessarily Chamber-wide effects, and several of the ones that are still exist as part of discrete phenomena that largely adhere to rules that follow from their manifestations. An Apex whose Hive Trait is Cramped isn't rendering every Chamber in the hive into a tunnel system, it's attached a Cramped tunnel system to every Chamber in the Hive. Thunderous doesn't mean every Chamber is deafening to be in, it means there's a potential source of deafening noise that can reach every Chamber. The Maze that Inoue applies to the hive of Tokyo does not mean all the Chambers are labyrinthine, it means there is a labyrinth adjoining the entire hive.
