                                                    I kind of think that if certain vocal detractors (I have in mind the people who implied that those who liked the first draft condoned horrible stuff IRL) had exercised a little more critical judgment toward the text instead of taking it as face value, a heavy re-writing such as the one we're seeming to get wouldn't have been necessary. Beasts are kind of awful, and know and embrace it even if they can choose to work hard to minimize damage; Heroes are not awful for defending themselves, they're awful for desiring to apply murder as the only possible punishment against a group of people whose crimes aren't all deserving of death penalty (and, depending on the Hero, causing collateral damage against innocents, and not caring about it).
                                                    (Admittedly that there was ambiguity over the free will of Heroes was a problem and did deserve clarification, but whether they have a choice or not, I consider that if your sole crime is vandalism/theft, you don't deserve to be callously murdered.)
                                                    I sure as hell don't remember anybody crying out that Demons were irredeemable for regularly destroying mortals' souls and very existence for their own interests, and how that painted a terrible picture of gears in the system who don't want to blindly follow orders anymore.
                                                    That I saw, of all supernaturals, Vampires being used as an example of harmless feeding was also jarring, and I'd like to know where the hell that came from. If somebody commits what's essentially a physical aggression on you (measurable by blood loss) without your consent, even if you don't remember it, even if it felt good at some point, even if you don't notice consequences or injuries, even if the aggressor felt the need to do it, that's still a physical aggression. If they prey on animals instead of humans, then they tend to kill a lot of animals during their existence as a consequences; and as it turns out, there's several Beasts that similarly kill animals to satisfy their Hunger as well, not all of them pets belonging to grieving owners but wild, untamed beasts.


                                                    That being said, I understand that many people saw uncomfortable stuff, but frankly? That several readers managed to make fans of the first draft feel like shit, and that the final text seems to take a wholly different direction than the one that had seemingly been intended for months, rubs me the wrong way. I command the writers for reacting so fast and taking in account the discomfort of potential players, I really do.
                                                    But unless the next revisions change my mind, I'm growing afraid that, just as much as the first draft wasn't a game for everyone, the final version might not be a game for me. (The biggest shame is that, maybe, if I hadn't read the development thread for months and had ignored the very first draft, I might have liked what was published just now, but there's a lot from the first version that I liked that appears to be excised.)
                                                    You can't please everybody, and I'm just one potential customer out of hundreds, if not thousands.

                                                    TL;DR: yes, I mad bro. Well, not really.

                                                    Part-time table flipper.
