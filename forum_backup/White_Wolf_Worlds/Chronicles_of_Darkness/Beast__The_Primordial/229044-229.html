                                            So I finally just finished reading the initial preview copy and (this has probably been said by others but) while I like the overall ideas and feel the text is trying to do too many things at the same time. The two biggest factors are the competing notions of monomyth and family. With regards to the latter, the preview goes to great lengths to integrate the other nWoD game's splats as "kin", providing ample ammunition for the "fitting in" aspect of family. Unfortunately the flipside -- obligation -- is neglected in its entirety. Alot of missed opportunities for providing additional avenues and tools for game-play there.

                                            Similarly, while the nature of Heroes as these pseudo-humans is relatively well developed, any treatment of other denizens in the Primordial Dream as Heroes, the Gentry as Heroes (this is such an obvious one, dragon hunting is sport for them), and other supernaturals as Heroes is completely lacking. No deeper consideration for what monomyth means and plays out in a true-crossover WoD is developed and so as above, there are a lot of missed opportunities.

                                            Finally, the rule section for ephemeral beings (i.e., ghosts, spirits, and angels), just kind of sits in the middle of the text like a painfully large gall stone. While I get that all of the game lines are supposed to be specializations of the core WoD setting, using the same core rules, I don't think it's a good idea to include rules just for their own sake. What roles ghosts, spirits, and angels play in Beast are never actually suggested or even alluded to. This entire section could be replaced with another chapter that expands upon the game's core concepts of Family and Monomyth or, it could be replaced by a chapter that describes the other ephemeral beings that dwell in (and sometimes escape from) the Astral realms of the Primordial Dream, Temenos, and Oneiros.

                                            That's my 3-pence anyway. I'm looking forward to seeing how this project evolves and shape up. It'd be nice if it turns out to be something I have to tweak not at all (like V:tR 2nd ed) or only slightly tweak (like it seems M:tA 2nd ed is going to be). I do realize it's going to be impossible to match the peerless perfection that is W:tF 2nd ed but, one can hope. (I swear that game is like 1000-piece tool kit, so many endless permutations of the stories that can be told with it.)

                                                        I had problems with beast as written. I wasn't going to back it, as I'm cutting down on my spending, and realistically, I don't need another book i won't ever get to play. With the rewrites, I'm definitely not spending any money on it any time soon.

                                                        I'm just throwing this out there - my opinions and a few suggestions.

                                                        What I liked: Beast lets you play an "other." An "Other" that has always been alienated, that has always known something was off, and then finally gets WHY. That speaks to me. Granted, I Didn't like the otherkin-tendencies.
                                                        What I liked: Lairs. While I'm not a fan of them being mostly-inaccessible other-realm locations, the concept is great. Let me Take you to the Maze Of Leviethan </cenobite>.
                                                        What I Liked: Heroes as antagonists - cWoD captial-H Hunters with a purpose.
                                                        What I Liked: Mythic nature, no mythic purpose - You Feed. I'm not a werewolf fan, so the "hunt" aspect doesn't overlap for me. I liked the variable reasons and ways a Beast fed.
                                                        What I Liked: Much as I don't care for the seven deadlies, Feeding the Greed. Letting all that Wrath out. Envy leading to destruction. Letting lust devour them. Building something worthy of all-eclipsing pride.

                                                        What I Didn't Like: Otherkin tendencies - Hear me out, I have a very specific reason - I Didn't want the gnostic "I'm REALLY a Harpy in the Otherworld" crap - I wanted physical transformation. I'm still annoyed that the nWoD (aside from vampires) doesn't like to let anyone BE a monster. Vampire powers are limited to animal forms, the crinos-equivalent has a timer, etc. I want MONSTERS. "Timmy with spirit tentacles" doesn't do it for me.
                                                        What I didn't like: still seem stuck in the 5 x 5 grid.
                                                        What I didn't like: the 3 "Endings" - Either become a physical monster(yay!) and get murdered (boo), become a dream-monster (boo), and get written out of the story (ugh), or Merge and become...written out of the story (Ugh^2).
                                                        What I didn't like: Family as a theme - I don't see how this works. Legendary monsters may have lineages, but they're rarely encountered in small groups. Most stories apply something like the Conservation of Ninjutsu trope: One monster is a threatening rival. Five are a difficult, but not too-taxing encounter. A hundred are mooks to be mowed down on your way to the end-of-level encounter. Beasts should be End-bosses, not cluster-enemies. Most of the fiction I read felt like beasts were loners, stand-offish, and that makes more sense to me than a bubbly social butterfly flitting between branches of her Addams Family reunion.

                                                        Now, with the revision:

                                                        The "Beastbrace" - silly. Just silly. This kills the one thing that really spoke to me - "You Are Not Them." A literal Soul-selling, for a role that makes no sense.
                                                        The "Teaching" goal - This is... wrong. A person is not a lesson. I would not want to run a game where players are Anthropomorphic cautionary tales. WHY does the dark mother make children, just to teach these lessons? I can see this as a faction goal, but not a whole game. There has to be a better "Goal" for the splat. Survival would have worked just fine.
                                                        The Heroes - I'm fine with them having a similar origin type to the beast- they can be humans with "potential" - But I think the original, "You survived, and your brush with the primordial affected you with supernatural PTSD" is superior. I was far happier with them as Driven, Angry, former victims. If they're a broken half of a humanoid Morality Play system, why haven't beasts lost their part of the task too? What is this lesson that was so important?
                                                        The Big Brother/Sister Role - No. You want to be a transformational catalyst? you want to let your wife come with you into the Dark? That should be a possibility, but not the ONLY way.
                                                        The Big Brother/Sister name - Awful. Sempai/Kohai is better. Mentor is better. Devourer is better. Hell, Soul-stealer is better.


                                                        Suggestion: You need a name for the potential beasts/hunters, and they're your beast-ghoul type - Dreamwalkers or something. Oneiroprancers? Give these undifferentiated bedroom brain intruders a name.
                                                        Suggestion: Have a supernatural force, an enemy for the Dark Mother - much weaker (or otherwise incapable of direct interference), but one that, like the Gods of old, Arms heroes, teaches them a few tricks, and sends them out after her with a Mistletoe Missile, Silver Athames soaked in virgin's blood, or some other Bane in hand. The Gods (or the Dark Mother's other "family") may not strike against her and her work directly, but giving a few gifts to those already fighting her kind? No rules against making the game more sporting.
                                                        Suggestion: Goal - Overall? Survive.
                                                        Suggestion: Goal - Be the Enforcers of the "Veil"/Shroud/Etc. Be the force that keeps the supernatural apart from the Human. Be the Gauntlet. Plant the Hedge. Keep digging the abyss.
                                                        Suggestion: Goal - A single splat being "Teachers" and "Lessons."
                                                        Suggestion: Give The Monsters The Power to Be Monsters. Physically.
