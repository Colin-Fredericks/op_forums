    How Much Separation Is There Between A Beast And Their Horror?

    12-27-2015, 06:42 PM
    While it is not wrong to state that they are hybrid or gestalt entities, Beasts can still think in human terms, even if fear-focused, as shown by their Life trait that accompanies their Legend. Notably, Beasts who undergo any of the Inheritances lose their Life, IIRC. Would "Find a way to become Incarnate but keep my humanity" be a viable Aspiration, or is that metaphysically impossible?

                                          Not at all. I have a much larger post somehwere here where I go into huge analysis of the issue. Its not disingeuous. It might not obvious or something most people will realize at first blush, but that's because people are used to thinking of fuel and Integrity equivalents of being separate, when they're not here. Its a new twist for this game.

                                          Plus, I take a bit of issue with your word choice. I'm being completely sincere when I say this.

                                          Your Satiety is determined through an entirely different mechanism than Integrity or Humanity. I haven't read Idigam, so I can't comment there, but I would argue that it's different from all Morality meters in 1e.
                                          I suggest you read Werewolf then, because the Uratha have breaking poitns at both ends - going high and low over the course of the story without spending any exp is par for the course. Cover, for Demons, works completely differently as well. Humanity, for vampires, is the only one that's like Morality. Even Integrity itself doesn't work anything like Morality.

                                          Every game in 2e, so far, does their Integrity equivalent different.

                                          Yeah, starting Satiety can be as high as seven, but it starts at two. And it's determined largely by one's feeding habits, but it's also determined by the way your character interacts with her kin, Beasts and other critters alike.
                                          You mean, that if you have the connections to feed humanely through Family Dinner, and are careful and don't act monstrous, you'll start with Satiety 7? And having high Lair, acting more like a monster, and being careless indulging your Hunger leads you to Low Satiety? How is that not like high/low Integrity equivalents for everyone else?

                                          If your issue is my direct statement that Beasts "have no morality meter," with a lower-case 'm', I stand by that.
                                          No game has "morality meters" anymore. Not even Vampire, which covers incidents like living too long, surviving something that should kill you, or joining a covenant. Integrity measures your Sanity, ala Call of Cthulhu; it doesn't drop when you commit sins, it drops when you're exposed to mind breaking horrors. Harmony is balacning Flesh and Spirit. Cover is how well you dodge notice. Wisdom is possibly the closest, which covers misuse of magic, and we haven't seen its final form yet, so that's debatable. Morals are too dependant upon where and how you grew up - trying to make one into a mechanic is doomed to failure.

                                          The assumption that Beasts don't follow any form of human morality because of a lack of Integrity equivalent is grossly flawed. No game measures morality, and yet many of the supernaturals do have human morals. They were raised as humans for a long time, after all, and they don't just suddenly lose their upbringing.
