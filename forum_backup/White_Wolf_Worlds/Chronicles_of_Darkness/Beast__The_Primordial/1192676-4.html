                                I must say that I am very happy to see Promethean representation in the kinship writeups for the Families. I am doubly pleased to see that some Beasts aren't alienated by the Pilgrimage. The Bond the Talassii can form with the Created seems particularly strong, which says to me that the Beast would likely respect their Promethean friend's journey even if they don't understand it. Meanwhile, the Ugallu seem positively thrilled to act as mentors on the Pilgrimage. That is awesome and suggests some complex family dynamics between the Created and the Begotten. I really hope those positive relationship carry over to the Necropolis of Hawara in Dark Eras 2.

                                Anakim, Inguma, Makara, and Ugallu all have kinship sections on mummies, some of them quite lengthy. I think we can safely say that the Arisen were not forgotten in this book. I think it's neat how many times psychics are mentioned in the kinship section as well. It's a nice little touch to remind us that the kin aren't just the core splats.

                                            This seems to be the most useful thing to get out of the trait in the outside world, so I'd probably say that, like other Traits, when you impose it the Minions/Thralls sort of appear out of nothing (or through some suitably theatrical means, i.e. rising out of ground or clawing in through the walls) and can start interacting with that area as if it was part of the Lair.
