    Beasts with multiple Hungers

    09-06-2015, 11:50 AM
    The way I understand it, Hunger works rather like the Y-splat for Beasts. Now, to my knowledge several kinds of other supernaturals (vampires, mages, and changelings come to mind) can effectively mix and match one of their splats with the appropriate Merit (vampires and mages can belong to multiple Covenants or Orders by buying the appropriate Status merit, changelings can have multiple kiths by buying the Dual Kith merit or by means of Wyrd evolution). By analogy, I was wondering about a Beast being awakened with or developing multiple Hungers. Thematically it seems to me there would be no obstacle to an Horror developing a less specialized appetite and being able to be sated by different kinds of fear. From a playstyle PoV it would allow the Beast to swap its hunting methods (for convenience or variety's sake) from time to time without having to rely on Family Dinner. Of course the ability to have multiple Hungers would have to paid for with a Merit, but it seems to me there would be most Hunger pairings could combine well in the same character (e.g. Prey and Ruin if the Horror focuses on being a destroyer, Collection and Prey if it focuses on being a hunter, Power and Punishment if it gets its kicks from being dominator, judge, and executioner). By analogy with Dual Kith and Hunger Management I'd price such a Merit three dots. I'm creatively challenged about the name.

          Your other two points of comparison fail on the grounds that they're political organizations with proprietary magic. Hungers are closer to mummy Guilds in terms of metaphysical centrality and changeability.

          By analogy with Dual Kith and Hunger Management I'd price such a Merit three dots.
          Tack a Lair prerequisite on it and/or make it a scaling Merit with the drawback of moving the boundary between Sated and Gorged up the scale.

          For instance:

          Broad Palate (• or ••)
          Prerequisite: Lair 3 (•) or 6 (••)
          Effect: This Merit gives your character an additional Hunger they can gain Satiety from. At one dot, this extra Hunger can only be satisfied when the Beast is Ravenous or Starving, while at two dots the extra Hunger provides sustenance when the character is Sated as well.
          Drawback: The character's Broad Palate dots are added to their threshold for Low Satiety for the purposes of Inflicting Nightmares (i.e. at two dots the character's Horror goes wandering for sustenance at Satiety 5 or lower). Additionally, the character adds their Broad Palate dots to the threshold for High Satiety for the purposes of gaining and resolving Satiety Conditions (i.e. at one dot the character must reach Satiety 8 to resolve the Sated Condition and gain the Gorged Condition).

          Resident Lore-Hound
          Currently Consuming: Demon: the Descent 1e

                      You do make good points there... in fact, here's an idea for an alternate write-up of the Merit. Kinda grasping at straws on the prerequisites. Maybe Wits would be a better fit?

                      Acquired Taste (•••)
                      Prerequisite: Beast, Lair 3, Occult 2
                      Effect: This Merit gives your character a second Hunger, an alternate method of gaining Satiety.
                      Drawback: The Horror's newly expanded palate makes it finicky; meals that would leave most Beasts replete become unsatisfying. For example, a Tyrant/Collector would have to establish dominance in the process of acquiring something new in order to fully match his Horror's tastes. Family Dinner is unaffected.

                                                      You have a point about the main usefulness of Broad Palate - i.e. it essentially gets useful in order to have a second option at Mid or High Satiety. Since the point of having a second Hunger seems related with the notion of a broad palate and rather at odds with the one of having a finicky palate in a thematic and gameplay sense, and the Hunger divisions are kinda artificial to begin with, I think the Broad Palate version realizes the concept better than the Acquired Taste one (even if it may use some adjustments). For the same reasons this ability should be available all the way from Low to High Satiety, even if it gets more useful towards the latter end of the spectrum.                                                       I would propose this writeup:

                                                      Broad Palate (•••)
                                                      Prerequisite: Beast, Composure 3, Resolve 3
                                                      Effect: This Merit gives your character a second Hunger, an alternate method of gaining Satiety.
                                                      Drawback: The Horror's expanded palate makes it gluttonous. Add +1 to the character's threshold for Low Satiety for the purposs of Inflicting Nightmares (i.e. the character's Horror goes wandering for sustenance at Satiety 4 or lower). Additionally, the character adds +1 to the threshold for High Satiety for the purposes of gaining and resolving Satiety Conditions (i.e. the character must reach Satiety 8 to resolve the Sated Condition and gain the Gorged Condition).

                                                      It seems to me the psychic resilience traits may be the best ones to work as prerequisites for this Merit (also by analogy with Hunger Management), and if we combine two of them (and price the Merit at 3) we can safely avoid a Lair one, and tone down the Drawback's severity in comparison to Satchel's version (although his general idea still seems the best one so far for a Drawback).
