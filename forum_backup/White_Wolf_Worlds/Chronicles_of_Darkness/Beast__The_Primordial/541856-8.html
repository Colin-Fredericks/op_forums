                Namtaru Ravager: If there is one thing humans fear more than the ugly, it is the possibility of joining their ranks. Degenerative illnesses, mutilating accidents, whatever might cause unsightly blemishes or maiming. This Gorgon seeks to destroy the beauty of others, feeding upon the terror of their own hideous visage once his prey discovers what has been done to them. In the world of nightmares, his horror is that of a bloated toad the size of a man, skin covered in bumpy warts and toxic mucus leaking from his pores. A tattered, dirty loin cloth wrapped around their waist belies a sense of intelligence behind the bestial form.

                His lair is an infectious swamp, teaming with diseases and festering insects. Putrid water reaching thigh level attempts to slow down trespassers while dense foliage obscures the view of the sky and causes painful rashes when brushed against the skin. Luminescent gas pockets seek to lure wanderers into traps as they stumble through the swamp in search of light or shelter. And in the heart of this inhospitable environment is a squat hut built into the branches of a gnarled tree, where the Gorgon makes its home!

                    Originally posted by Nyrufa View Post
                    Makara Tyrant: "You can't handle the truth." "You don't want to do this." "Beauty is only skin deep." You have heard all these phrases before, but you never really took them into consideration, never really accepted the weight they carry until now. Until you looked beneath the surface and saw what they don't want you to know. Most people go through their whole lives in blissful denial, they reject what they don't understand and impose their own explanations and rationality upon the world just to keep themselves happy. They never consider that their lover is dangerously unstable until something causes them to snap. They expect their leaders to be the perfect role models and are always shocked when they abuse the power entrusted to them. But worst of all, they think they know everything about how the world works.


                    Mortals who care to look past this facade may claim the universe holds dark mysteries, but this too is a state of denial. The universe has no secrets, it offers knowledge freely to all who seek it. Humanity just doesn't want to seek it, because they're afraid of what they'll find. That is the horror of this Leviathan. It is truth, the real truth that humanity doesn't want to hear. Cold, pitiless and uncensored. It does not apologize, does not make excuses for itself. This is the way the world has always worked and it was your choice to step out of your comfort zone. If you can't handle what you learned, that's your fault, not his!


                    The Beast preys on those who's sight extends beyond their wisdom, those who want to learn the truth, but haven't prepared themselves for what they'll find. It's soul is a man almost completely obscured by clothing, who's face is hidden in shadow under their hat, glasses and scarf. A trench coat wraps around their body with gloved hands tucked into the pockets, or holding a clue to some obscure mystery. It's Lair is a library overflowing with stacks of books and paper littering the floor. At times, you can't even tell if you're actually walking on the ground level, or if the paper is stacked too high. Most of the text you find here is illegible, written in a script that your mind immediately shuns as being occult or extraterrestrial in origin. But if you search hard enough, you may come across a book, scroll, note or mural that contains information related to what you're seeking.

                    And then, when you inevitably start screaming "No, that's not true!" the Beast hears you, and he comes looking for who's causing such a raucous in his library.
                    I came up with a Beast like this once, except that it was a Nemesis that punished those who learnt that which man was not meant to know the Horror was something like a great brass clockwork spider its many legs clicking on the endless shelves. Also, the Beast worked with another one in its Brood, a Collector who collected knowledge,punishing those who violated the hoard of lore. He had a particular hatred for Loremaster Heroes and used any and all means possible to hunt them down. I came up with a Nightmare for him, You Do Not Want To Know. It basically dissuaded someone from further investigating or researching something. We came across a bunch of Mages while playing that game. I had great fun getting perpetually irritated at their constant probings at the defences of the hoard.

                        Today's Beast #2:

                        We start from the Lair. Imagine an ocean of blood, eerily clear enough to see perfectly through the "water". The surface is not in sight, even if there's some source of light coming from above, though it's not clear if that's a sun or something else. Everything is distorted by the blood and assumes a red tint. If you go deep enough, you'll start noticing the skeletons of titanical beasts floating inside the gore, the deeper you go, the more you find. With some attention you'll be able to hear a distant beat, like a heart. By following it, you'll eventually get to a seafloor made of bone. Inside its chasms, you'll find the literal Heart of the Lair.

                        The Horror resembles a classical siren as much as the creatures from the deepest abyss resemble the fish from above. Slender, vicious and covered in cartilage protrusions, the hairless monstrosity's visage has nothing in common with a human face. No mouth, no nose, just a pair of wide, milky eyes. She usually feed with the lamprey mouths located on her four clawed hands or pierces the body of her victims with the retractable bone proboscis she can grow from her arm and drains the blood.

                        The character, a Makara Collector, works as a biologist in a renowed laboratory. She prefers to work alone and it's not the sort of person loved by the colleagues, but no one can deny that the few results she decides to share are often ground-breaking. She hoards vials of blood of people she find interesting or peculiar, no regards of their consent. She is especially fond of blood of other monster and people exposed to the supernatural, but the reason goes beyond her Hunger. You see, by analyzing their blood, the one of Beasts and Heroes more than the others, she's trying to find a trace of the Dark Mother hidden inside it, hoping to find some sort of revelation that will lead her to the Mitochondrial Eve of Fear.

                                        Ok, here's a really bastardy character.

                                        Ugallu Predator: He's an angel. Not one of these pathetic milk-and-water angels that people believe in nowadays. No, he's a full on angel of the kind that brought apocalypse to Sodom and Gamorrah with wings and swords of fire. He's the kind of angel that has to say Be Not Afraid. Except he wants people to be afraid. He feeds off of their fear and believes that it is his god-given task to rid the world of heretics and unbelievers. He believes that his kind is Nephilim, humans with the souls of avenging angels, sent to Earth for the purpose of shaping it to the will of the Creator. He hunts down those who decry the one true faith and destroys them. He has to be careful not to overfeed, but believes that God, in his infinite mercy, has given the nephilim this weakness so that sinners may have time to repent. Make no mistake though, if you have not renounced your heretical ways by the time he is ready, you have outworn his patience and God's mercy. He will have no pity for you then.
