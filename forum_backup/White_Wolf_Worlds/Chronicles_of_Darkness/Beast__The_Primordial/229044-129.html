                                                            Wyrdhamster, buddy? First off: Beast The Primordial is long past the second draft stage. We are extremely unlikely to actually see any re-writes at this point.

                                                            Second, the excerpt already gives plenty of food for thought on how Beasts and Werewolves benefit from cooperation and closeness.

                                                            Originally posted by Beast Crossover Blog Post
                                                            In an Uratha pack, a Beast finds the closest expression of Kinship outside her own kind. While a brood lacks the purity of purpose of the family she knows, a lone Uratha might find solace there. Individual characters in a crossover chronicle can build powerful stories about surrogate families and finding a place of acceptance.
                                                            This is the most obvious call to that, one that in addition gives very pertinent suggestions on how to enrich a story in which they mingle, and what themes to focus on that come naturally to both splats.

                                                            The paragraph that follows tells us of consequences that might become very relevant to a chronicle where both Beasts and Werewolves are present, and how those might bring them into conflict. But there is a huge amount of potential in what is left unspoken there. Reading that left my mind spinning at the possibilities. For example, the many ways Werewolves could take advantage of the effect the Beast has in the Shadow. The excerpt doesn't talk about that, but it offers you the facts you need to consider it. You can't expect the storytelling section to spoonfeed you everything. You need to find your ideas and your own way to explore the game in what it lays out for you.

                                                            Also, Beasts, like any other supernatural that could join a pack (regardless of the level), bring as many benefits as their diversity allows. That is to say, the reasons a pack might be interested in a Beast are entirely dependent on who that particular Beast is and what they alone can offer. There are no universal advantages a pack and a Begotten might obtain from cooperating anymore than a team of researchers in hiring someone new to join them for any number of reasons.
