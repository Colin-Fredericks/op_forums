                                            What I like about the rewrite: Beasts and Heroes have a common origin, both being people who “dream deep”. They differ in terms of how they deal with that: Beasts find a way to accept the Primordial Dream, and set up shop there; Heroes reject the Primordial Dream and develop a talent for destroying it and everything connected to it, including but not limited to Beasts. Heroes are drawn to Beasts not because Beasts somehow created them, but because Beasts bring the Primordial Dream into the waking world.

                                            This suggests a possibility for a demi-splat: those mortals who “dream deep” but are still on the fence about what to do about it, or who have chosen a third option that lets them retain their human nature without going “monster slayer”.

                                            What I'm not quite so fond of: the specific raison d'etre that makes Beasts something more than feeding machines. I like that there is such a thing; but “teachers” feels… off, somehow. Maybe it's just the presentation? I don't know.
