                The way I've been looking at it, is thinking about why people are afraid of going out into the water. One conclusion I've come to, is that it is symbolic of knowing that you are going somewhere that is dangerous to you, and risking it anyway. Before people became industrialized, it wasn't a common thing to know how to swim, even among sailors. Generally, if you fell in deep water, you were dead.

                To me, fear of the water is the same as the fear of taking a chance, and failing. It's the plunging feeling when you are playing a game and you need to roll anything over a 5 and the die stops on 4. It's the sinking feeling you get when you have big money riding on the Superbowl, and your star quarterback gets injured in the first quarter. It's when you think "I'll be fine as long as [x] doesn't happen," right before [x] happens in a big way.

                            Well, first, let me say that I'm deeply surprised this hasn't come up at all. I'm personally not thinking about these Families in terms of abstract fears, but what kinds of monsters they represent. The Makara represent more than just the Old Ones and the kraken. First, we have the sirens. Just look at the Atavisms that we start with the the Makara - two of the four are based on having seductive/social powers (Alien Allure and Siren's Treacherous Song). The only two social Atavisms in the game so far. This lends us to start thinking about things like "emotions deep as the ocean," "drown in passion," or something similar.

                            Then, the Makara are designed to be master grapplers, based on the tentacle atavism and the innate Birthright. That means we're talking about monsters like the roper. We could also do something like a sticky slime monster using psuedopods to move and grab things. Or just a

                            When it comes to water, only the Heart of the Ocean atavism deals with ocean. There's also the potential of a Flooded/Suffocating lair, but those are only suggestions, not even an affinity.

                            So, as you can see, its very easy to create a couple different Makara monsters that have nothing to do with the water. All Makara are somehow good grapplers, and many of them have a seductive edge to them. If we put them together while ignoring the water aspects? I can see something like the Alruane as a Makara, or perhaps something based on a monster with multiple arms, like the hundred handed from Greek mythology.

                            Lots of potential here.
