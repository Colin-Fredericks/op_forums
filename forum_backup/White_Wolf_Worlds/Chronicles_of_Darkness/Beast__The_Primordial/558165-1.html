    You can enter any chamber including the heart from any location that resonates with the chamber, but this costs a willpower point. If you use the location you created the chamber at then you don't spend the willpower. The heart is not tied to any location.

    The chamber overlaps with the location you are opening a pathway to or from.

    You can open a pathway to and from other worlds the same as the material one. You can also create chambers from locations in other worlds the same as the material one.

        Lets pull this thing apart and see what is happening.
        Example::
        A Tyrant Anakim whose Soul preys on childhood fears has incorporated an abandoned high school as a Chamber in his Lair. On the run from a group of mortal monsterhunters (though not, thankfully, Heroes) he leads his pursuers into the school and opens the Primordial Pathway.

        The Beast is opening a Primordial Pathway between the high school and his Chamber. Does he roll, spend Willpower? You say yes, so lets move on.
        Example::
        For a few seconds, the interior of the school becomes both in the material world and the Chamber in his Lair, and the Anakim immediately merges with his Soul. Faced by the hulking monster where their target once stood, the hunters panic.

        So the Pathway opens for a number of turns equal to the Beast's Resolve (a few seconds)
        Example::
        Two gird themselves and fight, and the third attempts to flee the school, but while the Pathway is still open she instead transitions through a Burrow into another Chamber.

        This is an effect of the ability, that those that try to leave are transported into another Chamber of the Lair.
        Example::
        Killing one of the other hunters, the Anakim closes the Pathway — the school returns to normal, the Beast appears to transform back into his mortal body as he separates from his Soul again, and the remaining hunter who stayed returns as well. The hunter who left the Chamber, however, remains behind in the Lair, easy pickings for the Anakim’s Soul.

        So, as soon as the Primordial Pathway closes, the Beast appears to transform back into his mortal body. He doesn't stay there with his Horror or with anyone left inside. Also, the one hunter who didn't flee returns back to the normal High School.

        The only person to actually stay inside the Lair is the one who fled to a different chamber and he is left to deal with the Beast's Horror, not the Beast himself.

        Could the Beast stay inside his Lair if he chose to? Or does he always automatically leave.

                    Since it only mentions spending willpower when its not the exact location and that comes after mentioning exact locations, it should be inferred that it doesn't require willpower. If it did then they would need to clarify it. Similarly about not having to roll, there is no mention of a bonus for an exact location.                     I don't see why the Beast would have to move to another Chamber. If the Beast does move then they would be stuck in their Lair when the pathway closes. Why force the Beast to move just for that purpose? The difference would be trivial.

                    Invited Guests and Brood mates get to move around as easily as the Beast wants. They can use Burrows freely and can be immune to Lair traits while in the Lair. It only makes sense to me for the guests to stay if the Beast wants them to stay and the guest wants to stay.

                    Every book ends up with some vague and confusing parts. I would save this as a note and bring it up after the kickstarter is over. They have an editing phase and they usually start a FAQ then too.
