                            Night-Gaunts
                            Every dreamer's Bastion touches upon the Temenos, the collective subconscious of all humanity -- and that includes the Primordial Dream. Sometimes, when a person dies in their sleep, a ghost is left behind in the Realm Material. Other times, however, these ghosts manifest instead in the realm of dreams. The Primordial Dream is not a hospitable place for the ghost of a mortal to find itself, and most such unlucky entities become starved of Essence and are sucked down into the Underworld rather quickly. Those who survive do so by transforming themselves into something more fit for the Primordial Dream's environment: they become Night-Gaunts.

                            Night-Gaunts are essentially ghosts that have formed an Anchor to a Hive Chamber, rather than a person or place in the Realm Material. Such an abstract Anchor is uniquely resilient, but comes at a serious cost. Night-Gaunts lose their Virtue, Vice and the remnant of Integrity most ghosts have. Instead, they have a Scream and an Echo, representing (respectively) the form of fear than most clearly resonates with them, and whatever shred of human identity they still retain. (The Scream functions as Vice would for normal ghosts or Virtue for mortals). The appearance of a Night-Gaunt fluctuates between their mortal appearance and a form that iconically represents their Scream, with most appearing as a horrific amalgam of the two.

                            The Influences of a Night-Gaunt resemble the Traits of the Chamber to which they are Anchored. Most Night-Gaunts, like most ghosts, are only Rank 1 or 2, though it is hypothetically possible for them to gain Rank the longer they remain active in the Hive. Like ghosts, Night-Gaunts regain Essence when they are remembered, but a sleeper remembering the terrible nightmares a Night-Gaunt caused counts just as must as a family member remembering the Night-Gaunt's mortal self. In terms of Numina and Manifestations, Night-Gaunts resemble spirits more than ghosts: They reside in the Hive, and must have the Reaching condition to affect the analogous location in the material world. They can freely enter and leave dreams, however, where all their Numina work without Reaching.

                            Night-Gaunts cannot cross from the Dream into Twilight on their own; for this reason they may be drawn to Begotten or other beings that can open Primordial Pathways. For the Begotten, a Night-Gaunt may be a pest in their Lair, drawing the attention of Heroes and exposing the location of Chambers they'd prefer to keep concealed. On the other hand, a Night-Gaunt can be a useful watchdog on a Lair's entryway, especially when their Influences and a Begotten's Lair Traits coincide. When encountered in unincorporated Chambers, Night-Gaunts may be able to share information about how those Chambers formed with a Begotten, although some are territorial and won't let their Anchor Chamber be incorporated into a Lair without a fight.

                            Night-Gaunts can be driven from their Chambers through blunt force or siege tactics -- destroying their Corpus or starving them of Essence, or using Banes or Abjuration to drive them away. However, it is also remotely possible to convert a Night-Gaunt back into a normal ghost, using any Anchors it may retain in the material world or presenting it with potential new ones. Such a process is complex and risky, but some Begotten with close ties to Sin-Eaters claim to have managed it.

                                    A couple example Night-Gaunts, to round out my post:

                                    The Burning Girl
                                    It hurts...it hurts!

                                    A few years ago, early Christmas Morning, a fire swept through this apartment block and killed nearly a dozen people. The official explanation was a tragic accident -- old electrical wiring, too many holiday decorations left plugged in unattended. But many in the neighborhood distrust such a pat story. What about the strangers who had been milling around in the days beforehand? What about the unearthly noises from the inferno? Since when did electrical fires move so quickly and consume so utterly?

                                    The Burning Girl might know the answers, if she were capable of saying so. This Night-Gaunt has haunted the neighborhood's dreams since the night of the fire, though no one knows exactly who she once was. She typically appears as a twisted, elongated figure wreathed in flame, eye pleading in the blackened flesh of her face. She reaches out a blistered hand for the dreamer's help, but attempting to touch her triggers her Firestarter Numen and causes an instant 3L damage to the target's Dream Form. She will cling to the dreamer for as long as she is able, dealing 3L per round until the dreamer awakens or succeeds in escaping her grapple.

                                    Rank: 1
                                    Power: 3
                                    Finesse: 2
                                    Resistance: 2
                                    Scream: Helpless
                                    Echo: Innocent
                                    Ban: The Burning Girl cannot harm anyone under the age of 15
                                    Bane: Cold water.
                                    Influences: Anchor (1) - Burning, Thin Air
                                    Manifestations: Twilight Form, Reaching
                                    Numina: Firestarter, Emotional Aura (Fear)

                                    Dizzy Miss Lizzy
                                    You picking up what I'm laying down?

                                    If you catch her in the right mood, Miss Lizzy will regale you with stories of her life, some of which might actually be true. Her style of dress, and the slang and pop culture references she uses, confirm that she died in the mid-60s. Sometimes she lets a few truths slip: she's a transgender woman, and at one time she was a sex worker as well. She also really, really hates the police. Astute detectives can put those pieces together for themselves.

                                    Lizzy will trade information gathered from ghosts and other Night-Gaunts, usually in exchange for passage between the Realm Material and the Primordial Dream. When not gossiping, she tends to haunt the back alleys, where she fancies herself as a protector of "her kids" -- the homeless, the sex workers, and other folks dwelling on society's margins. She prefers to haunt the dreams of police or those who call them in, appearing by turns as a beautiful woman and a clawed, fanged monstrosity that lashes out at them. Not to hurt them, she insists -- just to make they they feel the same fear she once did. Then again, Miss Lizzy has a temper, and if she's a little careless from time to time...

                                    Rank: 2
                                    Power: 3
                                    Finesse: 4
                                    Resistance: 3
                                    Scream: Enraged
                                    Echo: Clever
                                    Ban: Lizzy discorporates at the sound of police sirens.
                                    Bane: A knife that has been previous used as a murder weapon.
                                    Influences: Anchor (2) - Downpour, Poor Light
                                    Manifestations: Twilight Form, Reaching, Image, Materialized
                                    Numina: Awe, Dement, Emotional Aura (Fear), Limb from Limb -- Miss Lizzy can spend 1 Essence to use this Atavism (normal Saitiety effect) as a Numina, or 3 Essence for the Satiety-Expenditure effect.
