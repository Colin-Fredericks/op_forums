    Clades 2.0

    06-29-2016, 06:19 PM
    So, kudos to the new stuff I have seen on here. ajf115 and his sorcery rules are awesome and arcanist and deionscribe's merits are sweet.

    A while back,I came up with Clades. What is a Clade? A Clade fits into the Beast's identy, kinship, and overall organic feel.I had the idea that Clades not only have ADAPTATIONS
    (that work like merits or Mage's attainments from a Legacy that can be the actual Horror's form forcing its way into our world like an Atavism), but SKINS (these are things that all members of a Clade get on the onset of becoming part of a Clade, they are bonuses and certain powers). So, how does one join a Clade? Maybe your Horror naturally was part of one on onset or your Beast was brought into a Clade's fold and the horror reshaped itself or was warped to fit in this group. Evolution and all, right? Just here are a few ideas:



    Here are some sample Clades:

    TIBURONERA:
    The Clade members of this group are sea predators that rely on stealth and trickery to hunt their prey. Their horrors resemble fanged, beaked creatures with reptilian and amphibian traits foced on a human form. Primarily found in parts of Central Americaand Caribbean, these Beasts are called Shark Killers by many Changing Breeds.
    Prerequisite: Eshmaki or Makara
    SKIN: +1 die to all Athletics rolls involving swimming;+2 die to all rolls to save against drowning.
    ADAPTATIONS:
    • Deadly Bite:Prerequisite: Lair 2, Brawl 2. For 1 Satiety, the members of this Clade attack with a bite attack that does + 0L, and ignores durability, but he still needs to grapple the target he bites or control the objects he chews. Cost: 1
    • Camouflage Skin: Prerequisite: Lair 3, Stealth 2. Once per 24 hr period, for free, the Tiburonera may get two automatic successes on a Stealth roll in a shadowy area surrounded by natural vegetation or rocks or an underwater environment. For every other time besides the initial use, it cost 1 Satiety to activate this power.
    • King of the Sea: Prerequisite: Lair 4, Animal Ken 3, Manipulation 3, Kinship with a were creature related to water (wereshark, werealligator, etc). For 2Satiety and 1 point of Willpower, the Beast takes control of an aquatic or amphibious creature for a period equal to 5 minutes for each remaining point of Satiety they have.
    • The Shark Killer's Deadly Form: Prerequisite: Lair 6, Kinship with a Changing Breed. For 2 points of Satiety and 2 points of Willpower, the Beast can take on the physical form of the Tiburonera for one scene. The Beast's brawl attacks count as Agrgevated damage. This power can only be used once per 48 hr period, and after it has worn off, the Beast takes -2 die to every one of his or her rolls.


    Athasians:
    The Clade members of this group are elemental beings that seem akin to reptilian terrors. The first of these beings was a Portuguese mercenary from the 6th century AD. Borys, as he was named, was captured by the True Fae and taken to a fae realm of destructive elemental forces and godly draconic beings. His Horror appeared and reared its very appearance in Arcadia, surprising himself and his Captor. This was probably because his Horror was an air elemental-infused, dragon-like being. He returned to Earth with two Elemental Changelings as his aids. he eventually formed a group of dragonesque Beasts that still to this day appears in southern Europe and western North Africa.
    Prerequisite: Anakim or Ugallu
    SKIN: +1 die to all Intimidation rolls, +2 to all rolls to resist harm or discomfort by heat or cold.
    ADAPTATIONS:
    • Drake Mantle: Prerequisite: Lair 2, Presence 3.This power is passive and is always on. This Beast always is mistaken by Changelings as either one with the Elemental seeming or the draconic kith, unless a character rolls an exceptional success to determine the Beast's true nature.
    • The Roar of the Elemental Serpent: Prerequisite: Lair 2, Kinship with Draconic Changeling or another Beast in this Clade. For 1 point of Satiety, the Beast grants +1 die on all rolls for members of his group that are either of the draconic kith or members of the same Clade. This power does not stack.
    • Elements Betray You: Prerequisite: Lair 4, Occult 4, Wits 2. For 2 points of Satiety and 1 point of Willpower, if a target has an elemental power or a power that uses fire, ice, etc. Counter it. This power either fizzles out or never is activated.
    • Blast of Elemental Might: Prerequisite: Lair 6, Kinship with Draconic Changeling or other members of this Clade, Dex 3. Costs 2 Satiety. Same as Dragonfire, but wth either fire, ice, water, electricity, dirt, or lava. Does not freeze, burn, or catch on fire the target, but the damamge is the same as Dragonfire but with two extra die. Target if hit, is knocked prone.

    Kappa:
    The members of this Clade are witty and tricky, they all have turtle-like shells and are known for their mystic powers that set them apart from most of the Begotten. Rumors are that the first Kappa was from the Hedge or others claim that the first Kappa was an idigam, whichever this being influenced various Horrors to take on its image. Mostly mages and giests seek out members of this Clade.

    Prerequisite: Eshmaki, Namtaru
    SKIN: +1 die to all Occult rolls, Can see Spirits as if he or she has the spell Spirit Sight
    ADAPTATIONS:
    • Protective Shell: Prerequisite: Stamina 2, Medicine 2. For 1 point of Satiety, all bashing damage rolls against you take a penalty of -2 per Scene.
    • The Mind Hungers: Prerequisite: Lair 3, Presence 2, Wits 2. Passive Power. The Kappa can pay 1 Willpower to get 1 Satiety to back or if the Kappa makes an exceptional success with an Occult or Knowledge roll, they may get 1 point of Satiety or Willpower back.
    • Gateway to the Yomi Flung Open: Prerequisite: Lair 4, Intelligence 4, Occult 4. For it and its friends, this Beast may spend 2 Satiety and 2 Willpower to open a gate to the Underworld or the Shadow in an area that only it and its cohorts can interact with. When the Kappa returns through the gate, it is sealed permanently and another agte cannot be opened there for up to a week.
    • Users of the Art: Prerequisite: Lair 5, Kinship with Mages: Acanthus or Thyrsus. For 2 Satiety, the Kappa can use a spell from the Mag: the Awakening that is of the Life, Spirit, Time, or Fate Arcana (roll Lair+Occult) up to a ranking of 3 dots.

      While I do love the idea of a Z splat that further refines the Beast into a more specific monster type, this seems lacking in drawbacks. I believe that they should receive Persistent Condition of some sort that makes them more like the monster the Clade represents. Beasts normally look human unless they spend Satiety, right? Maybe these Persistent Conditions could mimic the Glitches from Demon, to represent pieces of the Horror bleeding through, even if there is no Anathema on them at the moment.

      I would avoid shapeshifting or combat Adaptations*, as you can probably use an Atavism for that kind of power. Instead, Adaptations should be about making you more similar to the supernaturals with which you have Family Ties. As for Skins, I'd avoid making them grant immunity to sources of harm**, as that is something that already comes with Atavisms and Lair Traits.

      *For example, Blast Of Elemental Might isn't strictly necessary. You can already adjust the details of Atavisms to fit your concept better (e.g. a Namtaru with Dragonfire who spits acid or biting maggots).

      **See the Skins for Athasians.and Tiburonera.
