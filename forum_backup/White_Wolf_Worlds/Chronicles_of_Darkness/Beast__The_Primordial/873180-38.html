                                                        04-16-2018, 04:47 AM
                                                        Originally posted by IceBen View Post
                                                        I have some questions about the Caught in the Webs atavism (Player's Guide). When using it as a thrown weapon attack, we need a bit more information than what is given in the atavism itself. Is it aerodynamic? What about it's size?
                                                        I have no idea how that matters. It’s a supernatural attack that follows the rules as presented. It has whatever appearance is appropriate to the Beast’s Horror.

                                                        Originally posted by IceBen View Post
                                                        Also how does the Immobilized condition work in this situation? To resolve that, normally one can attempt to tear through it (for this we need a Durability, which we don't have) Or does the atavism ignore this, and keeps the target Immobilized for the scene? (since the dexterity technically stays 0, no way to resolve that, which is strange)
                                                        Unlike mundane versions of the Immobilised Tilt, you can’t simply break out by breaking the substance. It disappears at the end of the scene.

                                                        Originally posted by IceBen View Post
                                                        And as a bonus question, would you allow this to work with Lightning-Strike? It technically requires you to deal damage (so by RAW it doesn't work), but using the touch attack with L-S sounds pretty fun.
                                                        I wouldn’t, but whatever works for your game.

                                                        Originally posted by Anteros View Post
                                                        Hello, I've got questions about a few things:
                                                        ​-Does Smashing Current allow to use Defense against surprise attacks?
                                                        No, if you’re aware of an attacker, your Horror tries to help. But surprise can still surprise you.

                                                        Originally posted by Anteros View Post
                                                        ​-Does Enemy Within allow to mimic the mien of a Changeling?
                                                        Generally not, but if it works for your game, I’d allow it with Satiety expenditure.

                                                        Originally posted by Anteros View Post
                                                        ​-For the purpose of Illusion of Safety's Satiety Expenditure option, do doors and other mundane openings count as portals or is it only referring to magical portals?
                                                        ‘Portal’ was just a shorthand way of saving wordcount from saying ‘doors, windows, trapdoors, hatches, portholes...’ etc. Mundane is what’s intended.

                                                        Originally posted by Anteros View Post
                                                        ​-Can a Horrorspawn hold a conversation?
                                                        If you make an appropriate Horrorspawn, I don’t see why not. It’s likely to be a strange conversation.

                                                        Writer. Developer. World of Darkness | Chronicles of Darkness | The Trinity Continuum
