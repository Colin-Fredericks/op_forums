                            Originally posted by n_a_anyon View Post
                            RE: "abuse"
                            This is the World of Darkness, there's no shortage of people who deserve a little trauma.
                            I would be careful. These terms are thrown around and there is a lot of nuances with them that are other societal and personal.

                            Abuse* is an improper affliction of physical or emotional injury onto another. It usually implies uneven power dynamics or predation, but doesn't have to. What it doesn't implies is success either.

                            Trauma* is harm of the emotional or physical being of an individual. While it is synonymous to injury and it doesn't refer to abuse, many people associate the two. However, psychological harm can come from non-abuse situations, such as loss of a family member to cancer or severe anxiety from exams.

                            What is clear to make the delineation here is that harm of an individual by another does not equal abuse, which I think is the point here. As noted, abuse is improper harm, and there are justifiable uses of it. For example, a cop who shoots a rabid dog threatening a child certainly harmed it, but it is not animal abuse: the use of force and resulting harm were justified. Meanwhile, the cop that shoots a dog because he hates its owner... that is abuse of both the animal and owner. Context defines whether such harm is abusive, and even then, it can be nuanced: being called an asshole and beaten is abusive when it is some random guy, but when it is part of your military training to resist capture and interrogation (SERE), it is not. And while you can argue ad nauseum as to where the line gets drawn, there is a difference.

                            For Beasts, I think this line can exist. The thing is, Beasts need to cause fear. People can fear things without the element of abuse. I fear the ocean because I saw Jaws at a formative point (and live in an area with lots of great whites...damn it). Psychologically harmful? Sure. Abusive? No. Abuse is a means to propogate fear, but as the book and others pointed out, not the only one.

                            For example, how about the Beast police detective who does everything by the books and feeds off the fear of questioning suspects? Is that abusive, if she acts no differently than any other upstanding cop should**? Heck, switch out punishment for prey, and a cop still works: chase down the criminal, with clamping down the cuffs as akin to going for the throat. Or how about a prosecutor? Have prey or punishment, and have him sate it by putting away crooks?

                            Sure, I am focusing on the legal system (us Docs view lawyers in a... particular light). But there are a huge number of positions where those hungers can be fed and thus the Horror sated where the Beast doesn't need to abuse anyone, just play a role in society where is hunger is sated.




                            *Note, when either are attached to "sexual", they are essentially synonymous by legal, medical, and societal standards. Injury due to sex (and yes, I have seen it... God help me) is just called trauma in most documentations to make sure such confusion doesn't occur... I make this distinction here so no one thinks I am ignoring it or belittling it.
                            **Yes, I know there is police brutality. I know people abuse their power in general. My example is assuming the Beast doesn't, nor do they need to to fulfill their hunger. Of course, doesn't mean a Beast can't be an abusive cop...
