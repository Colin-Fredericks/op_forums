    Question Regarding Alien Allure and Be My Guest

    05-01-2016, 07:45 PM
    With Alien Allure's Normal effect, how far does it go?

    It indicates that (aside from Heroes) one can harm someone and yet the next day receive a Good Impression, and "all is forgiven."
    I'm curious as to how far it is intended to work. Would that extend to torture? Crippling, unresolved injury that is still ongoing? What about murder, and the Beast player confronts the ghost?

    What does that really consist of, beyond being able to make social maneuvering checks once per day? Is the NPC (or even PC) compelled to actually forgive them for what they did, or is it just "sees them in the best possible light"?

    ~
    Second, Be My Guest allows someone to enter a Lair the same way the Beast can. Are they able to leave it to go to any given destination in the same way? That seems to be the read I'm getting, but I'm not sure. Hold the Door clearly specifies that their guest can only leave by the one particular method they entered, I know.

    Speaking of, there's no range on where a Lair can open, can it? Like if you enter in San Francisco, you could land yourself in New York.

    We don't allow mages to cast spells, since this is the most unbalancing rule of all.

          Answer the first, as I understand it: basically, you're preternaturally better at interacting with your prey than a normal human being. However, I think, this merely translates to of characters being more willing to hear the Begotten out than anything else, which, in of itself, does not preclude the target weighing past experiences with the character in deciding how to respond, which an ST could simulate by imposing penalties on that interval's Opening Doors roll, based on how bad that deed was.

          Answer the second: not exactly, beyond the Guest's ability to translate between dimensions. A mundane human is more than likely screwed one way or the other entering the Hive/Lair anyway, so venturing out into the Primordial Dream would not improve their situation much at all. Now, a Werewolf or Mage Guest, on the other hand, is a bit better equipped to handle taking the proverbial long way home, with Sin-Eaters and Lost being less so.

          Lastly, sure, although the accuracy of using it to travel is predicated on the character's experience, so having a Chamber at least narratively similar to your desired destination would make that easier.

"He's just that way, you can't escape it." But they don't, they express confusion about it. What we do know is that they are in a deeply involved relationship and then have a fight, and the boyfriend takes it out on the sister. We can assume the other party has some feeling for them?

                                                      Except, Beasts don't have a governing morality that drives them. They lack Humanity, Wisdom, Harmony, Integrity, etc. The only thing that drives them is their Satiety, their hunger. If they have a morality it is arbitrarily tacked on by virtue of their uprbinging and their Life, as soon as that Life goes away they lose what little they have left. It's tenuous. Add to this that they were in a very fucked up relationship from the get go, and that the sister was likely a Mortal.

                                                      A Beast feels family relations to anything that is a monster like them. They don't feel the same way about humans. Let's say I am a Beast, and I'm raised by a human family where there is so much distance between us that I consider them 'my foster family'. This doesn't mean I care nothing about them or their wellbeing, the boyfriend likely wouldn't have killed them in a fit if I didn't, but even if I think my 'foster mom' was an all around okay person, I don't feel the same connections a normal human being would feel for their actual human family. We have a fight about how pulling that is not an okay way to get what you want, and isn't cool. A few weeks later when I feel he's learned that 'lesson' we get back together. It's hard to stay mad about someone dying when you are not overly attached to them.

"All is forgiven" is completely within the scope of 2e's power levels.
                                                      Majesty 1 removes all social penalties for Social Manuevers, and nowhere does it say 'even to the victim while you are hurting them'. It says after killing someone. If you are attacking someone you are in physical combat and social manuevering is long gone. Social Manuevering on average will require actions over a long period of time. Especially because Majesty 1 does not boost your Impression level. It doesn't prevent the person from defending themselves in combat, either. For that you need to Enthrall them, or Majesty 5. Majesty and Alien Allure are vastly different if you actually think about it.
                                                      Majesty is significantly more powerful than Alien Allure because it can be dispelled, and because after the initial Awe, it requires actual contested rolls to inflict people with conditions that they can gain Beats from. Also - Majesty 1 gives a bonus to Presence, not Manipulation. It will not help you sweet talk someone beyond the initial removed social penalty. It will help you socially dominate someone, though. It'll help you intimidate cops after robbing a bank, but it won't fetch you a positive modifier on Manipulating them into giving up.
