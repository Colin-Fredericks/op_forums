    Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post

    Yeah - the Dream Form mechanics were written for Beast, Mage, and Changeling simultaneously. They came about because of my observation in Mage games of how using your Willpower as Health (as 1st ed astral mechanics did) sucked in 2nd ed, where willpower expenditure and gain is more common. So I took the opportunity to fix it, as all three games with a focus on mindscapes were in writing at the same time, and I was writing one and Developing another.

    The 2nd ed Gauntlet was written for Werewolf with an eye for what Mage would need from a Gauntlet chart, too.

    This sort of thing happens. Not quite nearly enough, but it makes for a more cohesive World of Darkness when it does.
    Hooray! This is wonderful news!

    I found the mechanics in 1.0 to be kludgy (for Changeling) anyway so this is really positive.

                    Originally posted by nalak42 View Post
                    What do you mean Sword?
                    There's a bit in the revised beast draft that because Beast horrors exist in their lairs in the astral, they don't have the same protection against a mage snatching their souls that other supernaturals enjoy (mages snatching vampire, werewolf, changeling, is a no go, at least without archmastery), and since beast is a second edition book it's safe to assume, I feel, that they are intending that to remain in second edition, since beast says they are an exception.

                    The issue I have with that is that if mages can snatch the souls, they can play with them in other ways.

                    Put them in other people, put them into a soulless mage, consume the soul for power, put the soul in a trinket to make a beast fetish.

                    Beasts become a power up.

                        To be fair, I'm fairly sure that the entire mages can only mess with the souls of humans and other mages bit was just part of 1es desperate attempt at not having mage make concrete statements about the nature of other lines (like the whole Arcadia thing) mixed with balance based restrictions. Now with a lot of the shared metaphysics being written into the core of the nWoD in general, and the removal of speed bumps in mage, I'm not quite sure how that's going to be handled in 2e.

                        I figure if they're going to go ahead and give Beasts the power to do so, thus already opening that particular can of worms, they'll finally get over that particular hesitation for mage and toss out that specific limitation since the statement on monsters souls has already been stated elsewhere. I'd also guess that if they remove it from Beasts, they'll probably keep the rule as it is for mage (or the more likely answer being not acknowledging it at all, essentially telling us to decide for ourselves).
