                                    Originally posted by ReshyShira View Post
                                    This really isn't the same kind of thing. Beasts lack anything akin to a morality stat, and not only lack one but what replaces it is a need to inflict cruelty and destroy lives (the exact opposite). I do not believe that is comparable, as last I check Hell Boy didn't need to nearly kill people on a daily basis for a "fix." Being a Beast a mandate to hurt people, you choose to carry out this mandate and justify it by saying you will choose who "deserves it." It's all kinds of awful. Being a Beast is the one thing you have the most control over, and then you complain about being given a raw deal?

                                    I saw someone recommend before that the Heroes should create the Beast, and I felt that made the story dynamic much more interesting.
                                    It has been explained elsewhere better, but Beasts do have something of a morality stat Reshy; Satiety. The more sated a Beast is, the more like themselves they think, because their Horror is sated and isn't thinking about frightening others to feed itself, whereas the hungrier the Horror is, the more the Beast looks to others for food. It's an interesting dichotomy in the game, and something that the developers have also discussed - do you become someone who hunts only when they need to, driving your thoughts more feral and risk starving their inner Horror, or do you make peace with your nature and more regularly hunt to keep yourself as you and your Horror satisfied?

                                    As noted elsewhere, again, this ties into the overall theme of (at least the early) World of Darkness gamelines - be monsters so you do not become monsters. A sated Horror is less likely to drive someone to really hurt another when sated than one that is starving - keep your thoughts clear and you're able to keep things better under control, and more likely to have some good come out of it. Again, as noted elsewhere (it bugs me I don't have citations for this) plenty of the horrors of Beast stem from every day fears that Beasts simply employ to their own ends. They're fears we don't want, yes, but having a fear of going into an alleyway alone or of going to a new place of dubious quality are ones that society beat into us simply because, well, it's a way to keep people safe. It's a guidance, fear, and if you understand it you can use it to help keep others safe. Scare them away from a drug habit, keep an abusive family member in line, make thieves reconsider breaking and entering, or even something as mundane as making someone be pleasant to the world. It's a similar tactic to what parents do to keep their children growing up right. Heck, if you're really good about it you could even make it into something like "A Christmas Carol", scaring someone about where their life is going to get them to really rethink their lives.

                                    It's not a marker for overall Morality, of course, but a personal one; a grandmother and a serial killer can both have a Satiety of 9 regularly, but the true morality of them is how they get there. Satiety just marks how much of their morals are actually theirs and how much of it is hunger.

                                    Of course, that's not to say that all Beasts will consider doing this, just as you can't assume everyone will return a good deed or favor done for them. That's just people, and (again) as noted elsewhere, sometimes normal people can be as much monsters as the playable Splats can be. Just look at, say, Hunter.

                                    That's also not taking advantage of the many ways a Beast can get Satiety without hurting one person at all. Given the extended family Beasts have, suffice to say, they have options.

                                                Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                Cascading failure of situational awareness. Got a pitch to get it back on track?
                                                My bad.

                                                Beast: the Primordial gives you an opportunity to do something amazing and evocative heretofore only touched upon by other gamelines, and that is the power to build your own psychic nightmare dungeon where you can literally go hang out with your friends. Beasts have lots of awesome debuff powers, a selection of the coolest things you can get in other lines - flight, regeneration, insane Brawl-based aggravated damage, invisibility, mind control/social domination powers - and are open both in terms of personal character development and in terms of crossover potential, although I can't honestly say that will always be an easy sell, given their power and focuses. You can do, ST willing, basically whatever you want with them. They have no leaders to pass law, no organizations to curtail behavior, and no real INTERNAL pressures - they are also the first to be truly and utterly amoral. Any system or code of behavior you devise works with Beast so long as it doesn't including starving to 0 Satiety. They're ridiculously customizable. And that includes the psychic nightmare dungeons.

                                                EDIT: On the topical subject of how evil you have to be - where the answer to how evil CAN you be is "think of the worst thing you can think of one superhuman entity doing to a defenseless human being, you can use that to satisfy Hunger and it can't even ping your nonexistent karma meter" - the answer is "really not that terrible." You can be an Eshmaki who sneaks into the cabins at the Girl Scout retreat with a shotgun and just...stands there for a moment, and then goes home. You can have invisibility magic so there's not even really any danger of being seen. That works. Now, you're (I think this holds true in the backer version) not making the choice on what's okay IC, that's on your Horror to decide, but you as a player can pick something with a really low impact. Steal things that can't be replaced but maybe aren't MISSED too terribly much. Create a software company where everyone's kinda scared of you but proud of the work they do, with the pride outweighing the fear, that's not so bad. Demolish condemned buildings after checking for the homeless. You can skip the priest-killing home-invading PTSD-inflicting bone-breaking stuff, and if you meet another Beast who DOESN'T skip it, you can murder her with a clean conscience.

                                                So there's another point for the customization angle, you can actually be pretty low on the wickedness scale.
