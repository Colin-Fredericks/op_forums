        Untouchable [Eshmaki]
        Action: Reflexive (Satiety Expenditure)
        Some of the most terrifying monsters in human legends are those that one cannot lay hands on. Leviathans who develop this Atavism are not entirely tangible beings, or perhaps just simply too fast for anyone to strike.

        Normal Effect: The Beast adds his Lair dots to his Defense.

        Low Satiety: As Hunger gains ascendancy within his thoughts, the Beast becomes almost impossible to hit, causing his foes to stumble and harm themselves in their attempts to harm it. All failed attack rolls made against the Beast are now treated as dramatic failures.

        Satiety Expenditure: By spending a point of Satiety after his opponent fails an attack roll, the Beast can react quickly to inflict a devastating counterattack.

                    So, I noticed that there was relatively little space directed to interacting with the Ephemeral, in the 1st draft, at least. So, I figured I'd put together my first Atavism. Tell me what you think and how much of it is trash, if you have the time?

                    WHAT WALKS BETWEEN (Makara)
                    It comforts many humans to believe that the world their paltry senses can see is the only one. When the things that lurk beyond the veil reach out to menace them, they cannot comprehend what is happening in the seconds it takes to be destroyed. You, on the other hand, extend beyond this state of being, a serpent whose coils trail away under the surface into the realm of ephemeral.

                    Dice Pool: N/A

                    Action:Reflexive

                    Normal Effect:The Beast may attune themselves to the strange state of existence occultists call Twilight, allowing them to see and hear(and be seen and heard by) the ephemeral beings that exist within, not limited by any particular "frequency". Splitting their senses like this, however, makes it harder to focus on the world around them, however, and imposes a -2 penalty to Perception rolls.

                    Low Satiety:A hungering Leviathan finds the fetters limiting them to but one state of being fading, allowing for a more perfect quantum duality. The Begotten no longer suffers a penalty to Perception rolls, and may interact with objects and beings in both solid reality and in Twilight as if he were in the appropriate state of existence. The Child may not turn this effect off, of course, leaving him just as vulnerable to the attacks of others as they are to him.

                    Satiety Expenditure: The immense potency of the Beast's Horror exerts itself on the unfleshed entities around him, a world-spanning gravity that bends ephemeral beings to their will. The Begotten gains an Honorary Rank equal to (10 - [Satiety], to a maximum of 5) for the rest of the scene. As normal for Honorary Rank, the Beast counts as a Bane for any ephemeral with a Rank two less than theirs, and they also gain a number of dice against such beings with a lower Rank as if comparing Status. Neither of these effects work against Angels or beings otherwise of the God-Machine. If a character is in a Chamber (whether in their Lair or the area they copied), they add two to their Rank for the purpose of acting as a Bane.

                            A couple based on two very beast-like inspirations: Tesuo the Iron Man and Slenderman.

                            Mechanical Monster (Anikim)
                            The crushing gears, the roaring engines, the pitiless motions of steel and oil. Though not all beasts are drawn to machines many feel a sympathy to the power and utility that such creations represent. Humanity has often anthropomorphised the machines it interacts with taking comfort that the gears didn’t intend to maliciously crush their limbs.

                            Normal: The beast knows machines as it knows the flesh and bone that makes its body. The beast may replace the Equipment bonuses using equipment and tools for their lair rating whichever is higher. The beast’s rolls gain the 8-again quality when interacting with non-electronic machines.

                            Low Satiety: When the hunger grows the tools of humanity are no defense against the beast, what was once a helpful tool is now submissive to the beast’s desires. Beasts may lower equipment advantages that characters receive equal to lair dots up to the point where characters receive a penalty for using their tools. The beast may only commit this effect to a number of objects equal to wits dots. Any object’s bonus that is reduced to a negative loses its durability.

                            Satiety Expenditure: The line between flesh and machine blurs as the beast’s horror makes manifest through inanimate components. The horror reshapes metal and machine to make manifest outside of the Primordial dream. The beast’s body absorbs nearby machinery in order to shape the horror with hinges, pistons, and engines. The beast gains 2/2 armor and their lair dots in expendable health dots. This allows the beast to gain the advantage of merging with the beast (P.99) without existing in the mortal realm by adding points to relevant traits. If the beast loses all expendable health dots they lose the points of armor. At the end of the scene the shell breaks down into useless parts.
                            Note: Expendable health dots are first filled when taking damage, when they are all filled with aggravated wounds they are discarded.

                            Electric Enigma (Eshmaki)
                            Bottled Lightning. The beast feels at home with the hum of electricity channeled into utilitarian ends. The beast feels at home with circuits and programs, not a ghost in a shell, but a being with awareness of the potentials contained within.

                            Normal: Glitches seldom seem to happen around the beast, or if they do they happen in the beast’s favor. The beast sings the song of circuitry, the begotten gains 9 again to Computer rolls and rolls that deal with electronics. Furthermore the beast is immune to electric damage.

                            Low Satiety: When the beast hunger spreads it seems even circuits are aware of the threat the beast represents. The beast may cause a localized brown-out wherever they go, electronics shut down and malfunction within 3 meters x Lair of the beast. This does not damage the electronic circuitry, the electricity itself seems to shy away refusing to appear. The beast may turn this ability on and off.

                            Satiety Expenditure: The beast’s form becomes a sentient electric charge. Using this form the beast may travel through wiring or other conductive material at Speed x Lair+1 per turn. While in this form the beast may control electronic devices at a touch and deal bashing damage with Dex + Brawl using their lair as a weapon modifier. Characters who strike the beast while this is active with Brawl or weaponry receive Lair dots in bashing damage.

                                I'm not sure if it should be a Raptor Atavism, but I made it as such, considering which monster this was inspired by.

                                -=-

                                From the Ashes [Ugallu]
                                Dice Pool: Resolve + Composure (Satiety Expenditure)
                                Action: Reflexive (Satiety Expenditure)
                                The phoenix is a symbol of life, death, and rebirth, long thought to have been kin of the Raptors. Those Nightmares of Exposure who develop this Atavism emulate the mythical firebird's vitality, and perhaps also its ability to defy the chill of death.

                                Normal Effect: The Beast is tied more strongly to the energies of the Primordial Dream, and draws upon it for greater reserves of strength. She now gains additional Health dots equal to her own Lair rating.

                                Low Satiety: As the Beast's Hunger grows, the fires of her life burn more fiercely in its refusal to be extinguished. Every time she is attacked in a turn, she downgrades the first [Lair] points of damage by one level. In the case of bashing damage, this means she ignores the first [Lair] points of bashing damage inflicted in a turn.

                                Satiety Expenditure: By spending a point of Satiety at the moment of death, the Beast's body dissipates as her consciousness flees into her Lair. There, it reforms as an egg, with a Durability and Structure rating equal to her Lair dots.It must then incubate for a period of [10 - Satiety] weeks, during which it is protected only by her Horror. If the egg is still intact by the end of this period, it hatches, allowing the revived Beast to merge with her Horror. The destruction of the egg before its incubation is complete, however, kills the Beast for good.

                                    Does anyone want to play a Voodoo beast?

                                    In My Image (Ugallu)

                                    Many different peoples in different parts of the world once held that taking an image of someone stole and trapped a little of their soul. Of course, in the modern day people scoff at this and treat it as superstitious rubbish, ignoring the fact that people who appear in images constantly – like supermodels, movie stars and important politicians – constantly hide behind makeup and glamour, as if they are filling in or hiding something else.

                                    For some, though, images do not steal. Rather, they strengthen, pouring them back on themselves. For some, images are more than just representations of a person. For some, they are power.

                                    Action:

                                    Dice Pool:

                                    Normal: The Beast is instantly aware when any image of him is captured, by any medium. He does not know its exact location but can roll Wits + Lair to ascertain a rough direction and distance. This image need not be a picture. It could also be a statue or any other representation of the Beast. Once the image has been captured, the Beast may roll again as many times as he likes in order to locate it again. This ability only works if the image is within Lair * 100 metres of the Beast’s current location.

                                    Furthermore, the Beast may look out from any image of himself that he knows the location of within a radius of Lair * 100 metres and may edit any image within that same radius. Both of these actions require a Wits + Manipulation roll. If the image concerned is stored in a computerised form it can only be accessed while it is being displayed.

                                    Low Satiety: As the Horror’s hunger grows so too does its perspicacity. The Beast can now roll Wits + Lair in order to pinpoint the location exactly.

                                    Furthermore, the Beast can now not only see through his images but also exert his other senses and use Nightmares on those who meet the eyes of his image. More than one Beast has used an image of himself, carefully placed in another’s home, to haunt his sleep. This is subject to the same distance limitations as seeing through them, although the distance at which the Beast may manipulate his image is increased to Lair * 200 metres.

                                    Satiety Expenditure: With the expenditure of a point of Satiety, the Beast can not only manipulate his own image but also that of another. With an extended Wits + Crafts roll with a target number of the target's (Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance) rolling once per hour the Beast can craft an image of a person, very much reminiscent of a stereotypical Voodoo doll. In order to do so, the Beast must possess a part of the target’s body. Blood or hair work well. Once the image is crafted, the Beast treats the target as though he were an image of the Beast himself so long as the Beast is holding the image. He can use other uses of this Atavism on him (besides altering the image). If his Satiety is low, he can cast Nightmares on the target. The power of the image lasts for a number of days equal to the Beast’s Lair before fading.
                                    The target rolls (Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance) contested by the Beast's (Presence + Lair) once per day that the Atavism is active. If the target wins, the Beast cannot target them through the doll for that day, although the connection still remains and he may try again the next day. If the beast wins or if the result is a draw the beast may continue to use the image. For every successful roll to resist, the Beast suffers cumulative -1 die penalty to any attempts to use the doll for purposes beyond scrying. If the target gets an Exceptional Success, the Satiety Expenditure effect is cut off, and the Beast cannot re-use that effect until the days that were remaining pass.
                                    Targets with the means to strike back at remotely activated powers, such as Mages, may do so at the ST's discretion. Someone who resists this power for long enough may develop an intuition that the Beast is to blame and a vague awareness of where to find the doll.
