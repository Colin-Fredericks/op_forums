    Chambers are supposed to be very personal and reflect the growth of your Horror's Legend, I thought. So...

    So... I'm kinda confused on the matter?

    I suppose you can also collapse Chambers for protection when one's location has been compromised, or Heroes can collapse your Chambers on purpose.

    And there's whatever Insatiables can do too. So maybe the fight for Chambers as a resource is tougher in areas of heavy conflict?

                                            Originally posted by milo v3 View Post

                                            My players trend to not make characters until after the setting had been made.

                                            Also, it means we can play after character creation rather than "okay now that we've done character creation give me three weeks to make the setting", because I can make the setting and think up plot hooks before the game starts.

                                            Also it means that I can use the setting for future games better because it doesn't revolve around a specific group of characters who might die two sessions in...

                                            Also, I sort of enjoy making settings in the periods where my players are too busy to play, so on those situations the setting was never going to have PCs in it in the first place.
                                            Ok then. Uhm...

                                            -If you're reasonably confident and knowledgeable on what kind of game your players want, then yeah, you should be ok. Stuff like the Region building and creating the local Primordial Dream you should be fine with on your own.

                                            -Creating NPCs is obviously going to be different if you aren't building any that represent specific Social Merits for any of the PCs. Although BaL does offer the suggestion of just having a list of a bunch of NPCs, and only defining the bare minimum of stuff for them (personality traits, physical appearances, general overview of what Skills they're good at and what they're not-so-good at). And either just picking out an appropriate NPC when your PC calls upon one of their Social Merits, or asking your players to look over your NPC list to see what looks appropriate for them.

                                            -Since this is Beast, a good amount of text is devoted to crossovers with the other splats. But if you know fairly well which splats you want to bring in and which ones don't, as well as what your players feel about them without having any discussion, you should be ok. Or ignore the other splats completely if you're so inclined.

                                            Overall...yeah, a single ST should have no problem just making everything on their own. It'll require a lot more work, obviously, but it's doable.
