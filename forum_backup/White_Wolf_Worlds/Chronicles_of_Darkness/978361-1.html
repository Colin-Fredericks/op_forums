    Ghouls with Cruac can do • rituals no problem because they only cost 1 vitae, which is payed for like a normal discipline, so no problem.

    Ghouls can learn higher dots of Cruac and rituals, but they need to use a Vampire or something with its own vitae, to spill its blood for •• or higher rituals, but that Vampire need not be CoC Vampires.

    More often a Ghoul with ••+ Cruac dots are going to be used helping their bosses as supporting the ritual.

        You cannot use mortal blood for Cruac, so no human sacrifice unless you drink them then spill it from the vampire.

        Ghouls count as mortal for the purposes of feeding (suggesting to me that their blood is still mortal blood, though they can't spill their own for rites in any case), so I think it's debatable whether their blood functions. Debatable just means that an ST can interpret it either way, however, so whatever you prefer.

        That said, there's another nifty hook in the Blood Sorcery section that you might use for your Ghoul ritualist: those that practice rites outside the protection of the Circle tend to end up with bad luck or visits from Strix.

                        Ghouls have Vitae to Spend, but not Spill. It must be Vitae Spilled not mortal blood, so human sacrifice without a vampire intermediary to drink the blood to convert it to Vitae and then Spill wouldn't work. Alternatively, a Vampire Sacrifice would work just fine, so that is a fun idea. Thus a Ghoul Cruac ritualist with vampires slaves made to Spill their Vitae either through violent sacrifice, horrific arcane medical procedures (-cough-Ordo Dracul/Cheiron Group-cough-), or through compliance of orders given, is entirely possible and is likely happening somewhere within the world where the Chronicles of Darkness takes place.
