                            Alrighties, not much of an update, but I've done an edit explaining how the Flensers have managed to make Cybernetics and Gene Mods in the present time and why they keep them a secret. Added Parts: "For the Flensers, there were two roadblocks to realizing their ambitions. The most obvious one was the factor of being noticed in their work. In the present time, Flenser’s don’t seem to have the need to be secret about their work. However, back in the 15th Century when science was facing oppression for, as they reasoned, “tampering with God’s domain or with what only the holy book can proclaim”, the Flensers have long since kept their studies a secret.
                            The second roadblock was something along the lines of scientific ethics. If they wished to truly exceed humanity’s limits, they were required to break a few of those rules. Even today, the Flensers conduct experiments that can permanently disrupt the DNA of the test subject or Cybernetics that can go haywire. But like the horrid experiments committed by the Axis Powers of World War 2, the Flensers experiments yield advanced results. This combination of secrecy and lack of scientific ethics is what has lead the flesh hunters to creating their arsenal of Gene Mods and Cybernetics."

                            In other notes, I haven't gotten much of a start on Choruses. Given they're the Z-Splat, the original ideas we had for them was they would be based on plays or operas in their themes and the like. We don't have much of mechanics set up, but I'm open to any and all suggestions. :3

                                            (Now that I've read the thread)

                                            Just wanted to say that I am very impressed with all the effort gone into Siren. You have it in a 90% ready to play build for the guide. Without a doubt I can say that it's topped Leviathan: the Tempest as my favorite aquatic folklore fan made game line.

                                            I plan on introducing it to my crossover game at some point, where anything well developed of official and fan material has the potential to be included. Siren creates a lot of synergy with Princess, the central venue of my game as all of the player characters are Nobility. Princesses and Sirens are both driven to try to change the world for the better with many different approaches, some running counter and antagonistic to each other, and both have a wide variety of opponents towards their goals without having to resort to crossover (even though that is a favorite of mine). Princesses are driven from the cataclysm of Darkness and the Fall of the past kingdoms while Sirens have passed and scene the likely apocalypse of the future Deluge.


                                            In regards to constructive criticism, I would have commented on the GDocs but felt it would better facilitate discussion if done in the thread.

                                            In terms of Celestial Alertations, don't Tropicana's Poison Immunity and Discordia's Dolphin's Pervsion do exactly the same thing?

                                            Poison Immunity
                                            Not all fish are vulnerable to poisons. The clownfish make their home in the middle of the poisonous anemone without fear of their poisons or being eaten. The Siren’s colors shift out into some form or pattern, much like how you can tell how “red and yellow, kill a fellow” applies to snakes. The Siren may add half her Akashic Record, rounded up, to all rolls to resist poisons of all kinds (save pollutants).
                                            Inspiration: Clownfish


                                            Dolphin’s Perversion
                                            How is it that as animals, dolphins are commonly one of the most beloved in media or signs, and yet they are really one of the most depraved amongst the animals in the seas? They rip off the heads of other fishes to masterbate with them, they get high from pufferfish, they rape their own species and drag down other beings to drown and rape. The Siren may match their level in perversion, gaining a dolphin’s dorsal fin upon her face. The Siren adds half her Akashic Record, rounded up, to all rolls to hold off poison and drugs.
                                            Inspiration: Dolphins


                                            In short, both provide the benefit of adding half Akashic Record to resisting natural poisons (excludes BioToxin). Given the wording of Dolphin's Perversion, wouldn't it be better as a sort "faux pax" defense? As like Dolphins, those of the Discordian Waters are willing to go to the extremes to enact change with behavior that, while not criminal, is very much a cultural taboo. Yet they emerge from the act without having their reputation drop or even enhanced because of it? Unless that sounds too complex for an Alteration.
