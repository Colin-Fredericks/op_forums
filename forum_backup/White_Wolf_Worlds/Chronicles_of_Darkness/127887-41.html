        namlugas: the Alarming Alacrity aspect, in which a shifter gains extra speed by the force of their will (lit. "will-speed)
        sakhrih: the Carnivore's Puissance aspect, in which a shifter gains Essence by eating the heart of another (lit. "heart-eating")
        adhkhakizal: the Echolocation aspect, in which a shifter is able to navigate or travel by echolocation; echolocation in general (lit. "to track by echoes")
        basazif: the Invisible Mark aspect, in which a shifter is able to leave some sort of unseen trace (such as a scent mark) for another to pick up (lit. "invisible mark")
        anunumamath: the Spirit Animal aspect, in which a shifter calls on a spirit animal to assist one of its skills or abilities; an animal spirit that is not as powerful as a totem, but acts as an aide or guide to a being (such as the spirit companions of some werewolves, or the aforementioned spirit animals of some shifters)
        yeh'renigi: the Unsettling Eye aspect, in which a shifter's eyes have an eerie, unsettling cast (lit. "eyes of a fearful aura")
        kensefath: the Cull the Weak Aspect, in which a shifter can instinctively discern who is the weakest in a certain group
        uthkaskeer: the Unsettling Cry aspect, in which a shifter creates an unnerving and horrifying cry to frighten others with (lit. "intimidating shriek")
        umaminan: the Feral Collective aspect, in which a shifter can tap into the minds of every animal it shifts into within a certain radius (lit. "beast-bond")
        umagathusa: the Hypnotic Allure aspect, in which the shifter radiates some sort of awe-inspiring or magnetic presence to draw others in (lit. "draw of the beast")
        hithahru: the Parallel Numina aspect, in which a shifter can gain a power similar or like a spirit's Numen
        gehannunhia: the War Heart aspect, in which a shifter gains strength every time they are injured
        adhkhar: to resound; to echo; an echo or repeated sound
        hithahu: a Numen or Numina
        wagsa: sepsis, pus; an abscess, blood poisoning; an infection; things such as the bacteria in a komodo dragon's mouth, which cause extreme infection after the lizard bites its prey
        mudu'na: a bezoar; the Bezoar merit (lit. "gut-stone")
        geh: A blow; an injury; a wound
        namkili: a charm; a talisman
        ni-namkili: a prey charm of the Prey Charm merit

        And now, canon breakdowns of each of the shapeshifter form names, plus some new ones I came up with:

        Hishu: The human form, literally meaning "society-scraping shadow". I'd peg this name as a universal term for the shapeshifter human form.
        Dalu: The near-human form, literally meaning "that which is broad". The name implies a broadening of the features (shoulders, face, frame, etc.) seen in werewolves, but I'd also apply this to other shifters, as they too gain some broad and gangly proportions.
        Shatilu: The war form, literally meaning "that which a fight will end with". The werewolves call this form "Gauru" (lit. "gift of the wolf's maw"), but the term Shatilu is meant to be a universal term shifters use for this form.
        Susmamu: The near-beast form, literally meaning "the renowned beast". The werewolves call this form "Urshul" (lit. "the wolf of younger times"), but the term Susmamu is meant to be a universal term like Shatalu.
        Unamu: The beast form, literally meaning "the wild beast". The werewolves call this form "Urhan" (lit. "the wolf at the door"), but the term Unamu is meant to be another universal term.
