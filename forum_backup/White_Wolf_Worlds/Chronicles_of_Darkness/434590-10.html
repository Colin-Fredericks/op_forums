                                                Does the Marduk Society really need to exist in the present day?

                                                Consider the backstory. The Society broke the power of Leviathans three thousand years ago, with technology that was, perhaps, highly advanced by Bronze Age standards - but whatever they had must have become wholly mundane, or been superseded by mundane technology, long before the present. That surely implies that any Leviathans who made themselves obnoxious would be defeated and destroyed by, well, an ordinary modern military unit, with no access to supernatural techniques at all - never mind what hunter conspiracies like Task Force Valkyrie could put into the field. It would be a much stronger statement of the idea that the Tribe were broken by humanity at its best if, in fact, ordinary technology as wielded by ordinary people is what the Tribe are most scared of.

                                                In my opinion, the Leviathan-eating sub-sect of the Marduk Society is the only thing that really justified its existence in the game. Everything else the Society does would be done just as well or better by hunters and mortals. In your position I would turn it into an interesting historical footnote: an odd secret society of philosophers and engineers that dissolved during the early Roman Empire, leaving only notes on the demons from the sea to molder in libraries where modern power-seeking occultists sometimes discover them.

                                                    There's a flawed premise in your post. You argue that Marduk could be better handled by Hunters, but Marduk is a Hunter Conspiracy. There's a sidebar in 1E that flat out states they are, and if you look at the 1E write up it uses the template for a Hunter Conspiracy.

                                                    And yes. A modern military unit is more than a match for the Tribe. Leviathans, like most WoD critters, have a large weakness for anything that can remain in the air out of reach and pelt them with missiles until they go down.


                                                    What Marduk has is two fold:

                                                    1) Thematic focus. A modern military unit represents society as it is, or perhaps society at it's worse. War is horrible, and the political left is often opposed to the military-industrial complex which means if I use the military as society's best I alienate half the readers (more really, WoD demographics skew). TF:V represents fears of government conspiracies and other sinister things. In contrast, Marduk, with their technology that's more effective if you behave virtueously and their larger than life heroic personalities are laser focused on representing what's good about society.

                                                    You can't make the modern military into a metaphor for what's good about society because they're a real organisation and real organisations are complex. You can't do it with TF:V because it contradicts the established cannon. A new organisation is the only way.


                                                    2) Battle strategy designed to be fun for players over effective. Look at Al-Qaeda or Isis. The USA army (and thus TF:V) has no qualms bombing brainwashed fanatics. Marduk does. That's why they try to lure the Leviathan into the open before unleashing the big guns. A player could not hope to fight VALKARIE, they'd just bomb Innsmouth flat. Seriously, the very first line of their write up "Fact: in December 1927, the US Army raided a number of coastal towns in Massachusetts. The towns no longer exist.". Marduk wouldn't do that. Marduk are nice, they try and minimise casualties. That gives players an oppotunity to misdirect, to evacuate, to role play rather than just dying in a rain of missiles.
