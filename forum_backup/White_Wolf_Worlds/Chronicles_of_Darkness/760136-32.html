            Alright so got a modified version of the older Vassal rules for Servants.

            Can anyone look in the tag below and tell me what else should I put in before I go writing up specific servants.

            I'm looking at .... 1 for all of the races for an infested version. And then a few other specific sheets for Elites.

            Also going to write stuff about an Elite Foreman (normal Foreman is dumb and just acts as a helper for Drone herds)


            Servants are more advanced creatures created by the Monarch to act as her lieutenants. Each is more free willed and intelligent than a basic drone and is able to act as a driver, manager, coordinator, or often, commander in the Monarch’s stead.Servants are created a number of ways. The most basic vassals, the Elite Foreman, is available to all Monarchs at all times. Other Servants require the Monarch possesses or has access to specific genetic templates found in specific Gene Foundries and Provinces. Other times, a Servant requires a specific independent creature be infested. It all depends on the servant you are attempting to create.

            Regardless of the means, Servant creation requires the Monarch order her Colony to perform a Create Servant action and pay the cost in Wealth and takes a number of days equal to Wealth spent. Many servants require specific types of resources in their creation, others do not care about it.

            Pre-existing and soon to be hatched Servants may be modified from the norm. Most templates possesses a number of “Mutations” representing packages of genetic modifications. Each, for the cost for further resources, provides new abilities, augments existing ones, or changes the Vassal’s equipment from the norm.
            In order to apply a Mutation, the Monarch simply pays for the cost of the Mutation as well as possess the Principalities to handle it.
            In the description of each Mutation, it lists which Vassals it may be applied to. In the case of some Mutations, such as weaponry or armor morphs, they may remove existing features or come with statistics penalties, also specified in the Mutation’s description. Each specific Mutations may only be applied once to a Vassal once and obviously, if a Mutation replaces an existing feature from the template, such as a weapon, other Mutations that replace that feature may not be applied.
            Mutations take 1 hour to apply, during which the Vassal is disabled and inside a Cocoon mutation.
            Removing or changing mutations from an existing Vassal or outright disposing of a Vassal does not refund the costs made to produce either.


            As Vassals are part of the Monarch’s Colony, any metanormal effects that involve Authority use the Monarch’s Authority when determining the Vassal’s.
