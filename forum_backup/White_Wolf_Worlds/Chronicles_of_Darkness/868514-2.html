            Chthonians entirely lack humanity, so I see Sin-Eaters of the time becoming more or less embodiments of death, given they're tied now to something that by their very definition never lived. They probably have similar thresholds, but the archetypes themselves are likely skewed to filter through the lens of something without any sort of human perspective sharing their mind. Very few Sin-Eaters care about gatekeeping for the sake of separating the realms and dont directly keep people out or ghosts in, but might have a focus on Gates and how they properly need to be placed within the world, for instance. Far more likely rely on ghost-eating and similar.

            Mechanically, I see Synergy being changed a lot. In fact, they might have something more in reverse; something akin to a lack of embodiment of the Chthonian's nature, retaining their more human perspective, and the lower it goes, the more they become a simple embodiment of some aspect of death and nothing more. I can also see them having Ceremonies or something else that reflect the Death influence Cthonians have, mirroring some awakened spells in a way similar to how proximi do, but always acting as a ritual. Summoners talks about how they might be something ELSE simply cut off from the supernal (like some say pangeans were), so it makes sense, I think.

            Overall, Sin-Eaters would just be... a lot scarier overall. Not someone you want hanging around, and not the cheery life celebrators you see today.

                                                            *****

                                                            Anyways, here's a little something I whipped up for the Gudthabak, based on the 1E Rite of Renunciation but rewritten to fit the 2E Rite paradigm. This is meant to be an extremely difficult Rite to successfully perform, especially given that most places will be some level of Extreme Environment, and the effects of that will start to take a toll on the initiate.

                                                            Rite of Ascension (ooooo)

                                                            Perhaps the most vital of all the Rites used by Bull's children, this is the ceremony devised to create their people, and ultimately, it is the Rite that is altered and transformed to save their people. It requires a subject who has been touched by the energy of a god, so either a descendent of the Gudthabak with a Tell, or one of the Wolf-Blooded. Performing this ritual requires either a fragment of one of Bull's omphalos stones, or a piece of Bull's shattered horns which gives all the rolls of this ritual the Rote action quality. To do so without either item is almost certain death. Should the Rite fail, and the subject is not touched by the power of a god, the subject dies in addition to any negative Conditions suffered by the Ritemaster. This Rite may only be performed on a given subject once.

                                                            Symbols: Dominance, Resilience, Strength, Fertility, Transformation, Bulls

                                                            Sample Rite: It is traditional for the Gudthabak to strip the subject of the Rite of all their possessions and place them amongst the herds of cattle. The Ritemaster is likely in their hybrid form, leading or driving the herd, while fellow Gudthabak take their great animal shape and mingle with the herd. The initiate must endure a gruelling trial, following the herds often into the wilds of Pangaea itself- never stopping, but persevering even in the face of brutal environmental conditions. Eventually, the Ritemaster and the other participating Gudthabak will try and drive the herd in the wrong direction, perhaps deeper into the Border Marches towards an Uratha pack, perhaps towards a region of Broken Time, but either way it becomes apparent that they are heading towards certain doom. The initiate will have to muster all their willpower and whatever physical strength they have left to break from the herd and lead it, guiding it away from danger. The bull-shape Gudthabak will challenge them- perhaps charging, perhaps with the Ritemaster even striking the initiate, but they must hold firm and change the direction of the herd. In triumph, they are transformed from simply being part of the herd, to being those who lead it. (Stamina + Survival)

                                                            Action: Extended (50 Successes ; Each roll represents 1 hour)

                                                            Success: The initiate is transformed into a Primal Urge 1 Gudthabak, having shown that they are indeed worthy of guiding the mortal herd and joining the pantheon of Bull's children. They gain their Auspice and their first dot of Renown from the means by which they took control of the herd during the Rite.
