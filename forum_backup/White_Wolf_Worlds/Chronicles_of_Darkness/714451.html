    What does an Unchained want from a pack of werewolves?

    10-22-2015, 10:26 AM
    I'm running a werewolf game. Recently, a loved NPC died, and the pack decided that they wanted to try and resurrect him.

    Half the group being Supernatural fans, I jumped at the chance to bring in some demonic goodness. So they asked around the Shadow, consulting spirits of health and medicine and even death, and heard rumors of a creature known as Mr. Onyx down in Houston who can supposedly bring people back to life "For a price".

    So what would an Unchained want from a pack of out-of-town werewolves? A Soul Pact wouldn't be worth the effort since the PCs are all Uratha. I don't think a signatory can promise other characters as cultists, and it wouldn't be too helpful anyway since all of the packmates live a five hour drive away.

    A Task might be more likely (after all, a pack of werewolves can destroy quite a bit of Infrastructure if they try), but it lacks the "oomph" that I'm hoping will dissuade them from wanting to ever commission Mr. Onyx's services again. I'm guessing Cover details, like parents, exes, the usual.

    And hey, maybe Mr. Onyx will give them a relatively cheap first deal. To get them hooked and all.

    Mostly I'm just looking for ideas (and plausible Pact ideas).

    "Nihhina kalekal-zidu kal masun, kal manudanadu. Nihhina kalekal-zidu nukal shaghu-desasudu — nihhina kalekal-zidu kal innu-desasudu udhkal samm." Arthur Ashe

                  A dead rival. Demons don't like doing their own dirty work, and the wolf must hunt. Trick is, this rival wears several faces, each with their own separate scent, and they're going to have to kill him four times before he stays properly dead.

                  The blood of the wolf. If you sell your heritage to Father Wolf and make yourself a spiritual bastard, what becomes of you as Uratha? Are the pack willing to find out firsthand, or are they going to have to hunt their own?

                  The creation of a myth. Mr. Onyx isn't forthcoming about why he wants an urban legend to spread about a particular kind of monster in the shadows, so the pack might be rather confused when they come back to Houston to find the monster they fabricated actually stalking the streets.

                  An audience with the lunar angels. Mr. Onyx thinks he can make a deal with the silver choir that will work out to his benefit in the long run. Whether he's right or not, he's going to need help to find his way deep into the Shadow marches to where powerful lunes hold court.

                  The keys to Hell. Hell is a paradoxical place, of course, so you're not going to be able to get the keys until after you've gone through the gates. There's one not far out of town. The Bale Hounds call it a "wound."
