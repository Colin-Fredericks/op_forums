            With the release of Mage 2e to Beast Backers I'm finally able to dump a finalized (well, 1.0) version of my 2e Hacks. Though honestly the release of the book didn't change much, DaveB's spoilers were so complete that I was able to make an extremely accurate assessment of the final version. Or at least accurate enough for my liking. However, the document remains quite long (in no small part due to the pages and pages of Arts), so I will summarize the biggest changes here:
            • No more Ruling or Inferior Arcana. All Spells, even Rotes (though not Praxis), cost a point of Mana to cast.
            • Rotes can be purchased for spells even if you don't meet the Arcana prerequisites, and give you Free Reach equal to your dots in the spell's primary Arcana.
            • All non-Praxis spells require at least one Yantra during casting or they suffer Paradox.
            • Legacies and Arcana based attainments are out, replaced with Mystic Arts. Arts similar to Legacies in many ways, they grant Yantra, Oblations and Attainments, but don't have Gnosis limitations. Arts are where you can gain Sympathetic Magic, Mage Armor and other Arcana based powers.
            • The Seers are out, the Diamond and the Free Assemblies don't get along well, and Nameless Orders hold considerably more sway. This is most setting stuff, little in the way of rules here.
            • There are also a number of modified merits, Obligations as an alternative for those who don't want obsessions, and a little hack for playing Mages who awaken by birth (retooled Proximi).
            As always, this is a personal doc shared here for those who are interested, or simply want to pick through for inspiration. Constructive comments are welcome, as are requests. If you simply don't like the hack that's fine, but please don't shit post, just ignore it and enjoy Mage 2e when it releases to the general public on Wednesday. It is a splendid work.

            Edit: Oh, right, link: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...LWs9Z-SI/edit#

            Edit x2: Also added Enlightenment Conditions, which encourage Mages to act...a little crazy.

                                            Would a comparison breakdown help?

                                            Core Rotes
                                            Cost: -1 Mana (minimum 0)
                                            Dice Pool: Gnosis + Arcana + Skill
                                            Reach: 6 - Highest Arcana in the Spell
                                            Prerequisites: Caster must have all the Arcana involved in the spell at the levels of the spell.
                                            Experiences: 1
                                            - Can only be made by Masters
                                            - The Master who made the Rote gains the Rote Quality on the roll.

                                            Hack Rotes
                                            Cost: 1 Mana
                                            Dice Pool: Gnosis + Arcana + Skill
                                            Reach: One Per Dot you Possess in the Spell's Primary Arcana
                                            Prerequisites: None
                                            Experiences: 1
                                            - Can only be made by Masters
                                            I toyed around with letting non-Masters create Rotes, but in the end I decided against it. I feared it would undermine the importance of true masters in the Awakened world and lead Rotes to feeling slightly less special. Beyond that, the above hack has worked out well in actual play. My players have universally enjoyed the access to 'high tier' tricks and like that they don't need to invest ten plus experiences into an Arcana just to get the single effect they area after. The hack has also lead to some pretty great scenes, like a relatively novice character birthing a god-damned sun to defeat what was otherwise and unstoppable foe.

                                            Oh, and the universal access to Rotes makes being a Rote thief, who collects Rotes, far more viable. And justifies why Orders are so protective of their most powerful Rotes.

                                                            Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post

                                                            Well, that definitely gives me a better idea of how the rule hack works. And the image of a novice Mage being able to conjure a freaking SUN IS awesome, I admit. Also, lovin the fact that having Order-specific Rotes get stolen by an ambitious Mage is a more serious issue with these mechanics.

                                                            For Rotes, though, I'm wondering whether I should go with the 2E core or your rule hack for free Reach. I can probably let the 1 Mana cost be kept for a Mage if he does not yet have high enough dots in the right Arcana, after which he can perform it at no cost. And for Praxes, meanwhile, maybe I could tie their free Reach to their Gnosis dots, or half of it (rounded up).
                                                            Maybe it would it help if I explained my reasoning for the assorted changes:
                                                            • Cost: This one was pure preference. I like my Mages casting, but not all the damn time, and wanted to emphasize the importance of mana and hollows. I deemed that, given the boost in power, Rotes could use to be cut back somewhere, Cost was the obvious choice. As an added benefit, along with the removal of Ruling Arcana, this lets Praxis shine as the only spells Mages can cast freely. My players used their Praxis non-stop, which further focused and differentiated the characters.
                                                            • Reach: This change was for two reasons. First and foremost, with everyone having access to Rotes I needed to differentiate between characters who were actually good at the Arcana and those that merely dabbled. Sure Arcana is already in the dice pool, but 1-5 dice seemed too little, especially given that they could be offset with Yantra easily enough. Reach was the other clear option, and from experience the hack does a good job of making it clear who the real masters are. Speaking of masters, point two I wanted there to be some Reach based benefit to learning a Rote as a master. Making it possible for 5 dot spells to be cast safely by making them Rotes fit the bill.
                                                            • Prerequisites: You already hit this nail on the head, the removal of prereqs gives the Orders a much clearer reason to covet their Rotes, and lets players (and storytellers) give mages interesting powers without having to give them, say, five dots of Time and everything that comes with it.
                                                            I can say, with confidence, that all these changes went over really well. My players were eager to invest in Rotes and prefered casting Rotes over relying on standard Creative Thaumatugy whenever possible. They are just a better value for the mana. The above Sun example also showcased why novices shouldn't be given five dot Rotes, as when the spell was used he had nowhere near enough Reach to hit the scale he was aiming for and ended up nearly killing himself to soak the Paradox.
