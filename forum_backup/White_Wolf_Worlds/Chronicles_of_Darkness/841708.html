    Pitch your campaign!

    03-04-2016, 12:38 AM
    This isn't a recruitment thread, unless something really picks up interest. It's more for rapid fire throwing-out of all your ChroD campaign ideas, all the games you've wanted to run or play in. "Pitches" can be as long or as short as you want.

    Example:

    - In a fairly nice, quiet town called Princeton, you attended a fairly nice, quiet private high school called the Nadir Peak School of Princeton. You had a pretty average life- some hobbies, some friends, and so on. Sure, there were parts that didn't always make sense. Like when you realized your house didn't have an address. Or when you an A on your report card, but it didn't list any classes or anything else and the whole report card was just a blank sheet of paper with a large "A" written on it in marker. Or how your parents are physically identical and neither of them blink. But none of that really bothered you. You had classes and other stuff to worry about. You were in the second half of high school, on the cusp of or just over the line of adulthood when it happened. At the beginning of the year, the very beginning, it was just a little disturbance. In his beginning of the year speech the principal mentioned that the state government had chosen your school as one of the test sites for a "new educational college preparatory standardized educational system" sponsored by Fiber, some "educational technologies" startup you'd never heard of. He tried to make it sound like an honor for the school, but he crammed the announcement in at the end and almost seemed like he was hurrying past it. Also, he was sweating through the whole speech. And there were guys in dark suits and glasses lurking around. But you got used to all of that. Things were maybe a little weirder, a little louder, a little more dangerous, but what were a few more lacerations and broken bones? Only a few kids disappeared, and it wasn't anyone you knew well.

    And then, one night, when you found yourself staying late at the school for whatever reason, it happened. You heard what sounded almost like an explosion from the woods behind the school, you saw a white flash, and you blacked out for a little. When you woke up, you realized that you weren't actually a high school student. You weren't a teenager or a young adult or an adult or however old you thought you were. You weren't human. You were a creature of metal and plastic and living fire and cameras and wires and gun barrels and cement and eyes, you had been placed in that school to do a job- and you had failed.

    Or:

    - The once-prestigious Long Beach Island Oceanographic Institute is desperate for funds and starts using its small fleet of air and watercraft for a smuggling operation. Essentially, Breaking Bad if Walter had a degree in Marine Biology instead of chemistry.

    And, one I've always wanted to try:

    - Evil carnies. They're one of horror'a favorite go-to enemies. But what's it actually like to be an evil carnie? What are their lives like? Basically, all the players are spooky carnies. Funnel cake is required for each session.

      - Five children, five countries, five pillars, and five souls, five guardians. In the year 2006, an event occurred. One you may not have heard because it was swept under the rug by shady organizations. Five children, seperated by vast distances around the world vanished without a trace. exactly one year later they were all found comatose on an island aranged in a circle. Scientists were baffled. It's been five years since they were found and now they wake to find themselves in a conflict between warring nations, underground conspiracies, a darkness creeping over the globe, and themselves.

      What will they choose? The fate of existence rests on their choice. The problem? The don't remember what the choice is.

      - College is when things change. It's when you get to turn your life around. That's the way it was for 6 high school friends as they all move to a peaceful little college town to finnally be free. But now they find they can't leave, the school isn't what it appears, and there's an evil beneath the town just itching to get free. Will they escape their cage yet again?

      - A highway to hell had never been so litteral. A single greyhound bus full of passengers going one way or the next crashed. Many passengers died. A few survived. The bus driver swears that there was a body in the middle of the road. The passengers would have liked to know more before disappeared before their eyes. So now they wade through the fog as they pass the city sign reading "Silent Hill". They hope they find help.

      - They say that nothing happens less than twice. Is that true? For those who've been to Faerie and escaped to tell of it, they sure hope not. But in a city of ruthless rules and lax protection, small club of Changelings might just be the tipping balance in a contract that has been woven and re-woven for over a century. Will they use it in their favor or break it.

      - Ghost stories don't often end well. A lot of people die in them. But when their school starts fluctuating between this realm and that of the dead, 6 friends must brave the school at night and find the truth of it all. They are going to wish they HAD died.

      - No one likes an Infestation. Especially not the Local Ring "Ihr" who see the crytids that having been appearing lately as a beacon for the The Machine. A beacon that must be silenced. It was a simple job, until a very old Hunter Angel returns to seek vengeance against one of the Ihr for past transgressions. Can the Ihr survive a simple clean-up job at the same time as defending and evading a violent angel about ready to fall?

          [Changeling/Demon]
          It all started on a seemingly normal (or at least, normal as such things go) day, one on which a Ring of Saboteurs had planed a strike against a powerful piece of local Infrastructure. Except, when they arrived, they discovered that something else had struck first. The whole Facility was overrun with growth, thorn-covered vines jamming the gears as strange not-Cryptids roamed the halls, the only of the original occupants still alive a utterly deranged Exile and a newly Fallen, near catatonic Demon. A few days later, a small Motley came across a strange circle of standing stones that left them sluggish and drove the local Hobs into a fearful frenzy as they strove to flee from it. This strange back and forth continued for weeks before a Changeling and a Demon who had forged a working relationship found it pertinent to mention this to one another, and in the resultant conversation managed to work out the truth; the Machine and the Hedge (or perhaps Arcadia or the Wyrd?) have gone to war against each other.

          Now the players and their societies have a unique but perilous opportunity. If they choose to take it, they could try to play their foes against each other, try to keep them balanced and weak, too busy sniping at each other to focus on their former servants. If they fail, however, they may empower one of these forces, giving them the strength to launch a powerful offensive. And there's always a few willing to decide that the enemy of their enemy is an ally if it means that enemy's total defeat in the area, no matter if that means sacrificing the other type of escapees...

                          The chronicle I intend to run once Mage 2ED comes out.
                          ​Humboldt county California, home of Redwoods, hidden pot farms and a dark history of Racial tension and violence, first with the native Wiyot people and later the Chinese. The year is 2013 and the reemerging Wiyot people have just announced their intentions to hold a World Renewal Ceremony on Indian Island, the first since the 1860 Massacre.
                          The characters are all between the ages of 14 and 17, high school students attending the same school in Eureka and until now that was the only thing they had in common. The game will start with them waking up in the early morning hours (think 2a.m.) lying upon the lawn of their high school, with bruises, torn clothes and chaffing around their wrists as if from some form of bondage. All they will have of the last twelve hours are feverish memories of the five of them being chained in a circle between stone pillars (there will be five characters, one for each path and if I don't have five players, most likely, the extras will not wake up at the school and will instead now be missing) with visions of dancing smoke, shadow, and wolves howling just out of sight. They have each undergone an 'Ancient Lands' style awakening, though in this case it will be based on a series of five books that all tell the same story each from the point of view of one of five protagonists (and Paths).
                          The only Pentacle Mage in the area is a native Wiyot living on the reservation and she is busy preparing for the coming ceremony. Busy enough that she won't discover the heroes until late in the chronicle, leaving only their different books as their guides.
                          There will be crossover with Changeling, Werewolf and the Circle of the Crone. The story will revolve around the upcoming ceremony, which requires a representative from each path, and the dark and ancient evil that seeks to preempt it.
                          The chronicle will begin with a focus on the protagonists trying to figure out what happened to them and this magic thing that has entered their lives. Then will move on to trying to find their missing classmates. Then they will actively struggle against that dark power and the role it intends them to play in its plans and the ceremony.
