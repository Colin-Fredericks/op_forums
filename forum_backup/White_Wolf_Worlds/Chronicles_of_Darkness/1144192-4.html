                            There's actually an interesting potential Demon angle in the Mongol invasions of eastern Europe, I think. After they entered what is now Poland and crushed a force of, IIRC, Knights Templar, the horde had to turn around and go back to central Asia, as the Khan had just died and their leader needed to go try to make his claim to be the next Khan. Had this death not occurred when it did, odds are the Mongols would have continued into the Holy Roman Empire, France and/or Italy, derailing the Renaissance and the Age of Exploration (save whatever the English and Swedes ended up doing).

                                                    I'd really like to see Genius set in Europe between WW1 and WW2. One of Genius' themes is how human intellectualism can go horribly wrong, and the interwar period has that in spades.

                                                    You have the very idea of "the war to end all wars" that caused a generation to go to war and die. You have the declining ideals of empire. And young powerful ideas, fascism and communism, promise a shineing new future born in blood.

                                                    Players will be brilliant people, scarred by WW1 (weather they fought in the front lines or not) who realised the ideals they fought for were hollow and flawed and must struggle to accept this. Some will find closure, and others, they will learn nothing and they will burn the world with the light of their Inspiration.

                                                            Originally posted by Second Chances View Post
                                                            I'd go with Reganomics and the War on Drugs.
                                                            Mummy Vice? I guess the high concept would be Crocket and Tubbs vs Scarface as seen through the lens of M:tC?


                                                            It occurs to me that Miami Vice debuted in 1984, and that, in addition to the obvious Orwell connection, a lot of pop culture and political stuff from that year would seem to make it an ideal year for just about every CoD game line in some way or another. Besides Miami Vice, it's the same year of the V miniseries, the debut of Highway to Heaven, Lifestyles of the Rich & the Famous, Tales from the Darkside, The Cosby Show, and Night Court, along with continuing series like Cagney & Lacey, Hill Street Blues, The A-Team, Magnum PI, Knight Rider, Cheers, Dallas, Falcon Crest and Dynasty.
                                                            Film wise, it's the year of Ghostbusters, A Nightmare on Elm Street, The Terminator, Streets of Fire, Sixteen Candles, Purple Rain, Night of the Comet, Moscow on the Hudson, The Never-ending Story, The Karate Kid, The Last Starfighter, Gremlins, Friday the 13th: The Final Chapter (pt 4), Footloose, Firestarter, Children of the Corn, The Adventures of Buckaroo Banzai, Beverly Hills Cop, Breakin', CHUD, The Company of Wolves, Red Dawn, Indiana Jones & The Temple of Doom, Dune, Dreamscape, The Natural, Once Upon a Time in America, Repo Man, and 1984.
                                                            In the world of books, it's the year of William Gibson's Neuromancer, Stephen King's The Talisman (with Peter Straub) and Thinner (as Richard Bachman), John Updike's The Witches of Eastwick, Bob Shea & Robert Willson's Illuminatus trilogy, the first of Clive Barker's Books of Blood collections, and Tom Clancy's The Hunt for Red October. Comics-wise, it's the year Alan Moore started writing Swamp Thing.
                                                            Elsewhere, there's the US presidential election (including the first major party ticket to include a woman), the Summer Olympics in LA and the Winter Olympics in Sarajevo, the breakup of the US Bell telephone monopoly, UK coal miner strikes, the Brighton Hotel bombing,and the agreement to eventually turn Hong Kong over the China.
