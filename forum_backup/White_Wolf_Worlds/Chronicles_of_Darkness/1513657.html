    COFD Fan Gameline where you play as a final boss

    06-02-2023, 12:01 PM
    After a great deal of thinking, I started on a fan gameline that I'll need a massive amount of help with. I might even make a discord server for it. It's called Dark Master: The (haven't figured this out yet).

    I'll link to the google drive with it, but I'll explain some of the things I was thinking about when designing it.

    The game is a study of evil and innately evil creatures, what it is like to be one of them and how hard it is for them to be good. Good and evil are often subjective (which is probably part of the reason why Morality was changed to Integrity), so to get around that, I made it so that the main sense of good and evil that matters for a Dark Master is the sense of morality of the god who created them. So, the player, the GM, and the Dark Master could potentially think of something as good but the god who created the Dark Master could think of it as evil. But most gods will think the same basic things are evil that humans think of as evil, like murder being evil. There are some rewards for being good, but they are few and far between.

    Next, Dark Masters are innately evil but they aren't entirely evil. For example, they show genuine sympathy to Demons, Changelings, and Deviants due to their similar situations. They also are disgusted with anyone who is evil and is tricking themselves into thinking they're the good guys, and they believe this applies to the Beasts. The Dark Masters know exactly what they are. They also hate all gods, which means they hate Leviathans.

    Dark Masters are also incredibly arrogant, so you'll see lines from them where they're doing stupid things that'll get them killed. For example, saying "I want to kill Luna" to a group of werewolves.

    In terms of game mechanics, most of them have the whole innately evil thing in mind. I made it very easy for Dark Masters to be evil. Being evil makes them more powerful, doing evil actions might get them more of the supernatural energy stat, and becoming too good will harm them. I tried to make the game mechanics support the Dark Master mindset as well. For example, their virtue equivalent is called megalomania.

    Now, for combat, Dark Masters should be like bosses in video games. They shouldn't be afraid of fighting but they should be cautious of it. They should be the sort who make their enemies fight waves of minions or make their way through a trap-filled dungeon before fighting them. In combat itself, the Dark Masters should be powerful but far from unbeatable.

    To facilitate this, I made the Dark Masters the second most immortal creatures out there. Only Geists are more immortal. However, I put in consequences for their death, namely in how long it takes them to come back. Essentially, a Dark Master comes back from dying but can permanently die under circumstances that are stated in the doc. And even if they aren't permanently killed, they could be set massively back from a defeat. I'm just not sure about the minion numbers and their general power in combat. I'm not sure if they're powerful enough, too powerful, or not powerful enough.

    And that's a good chunk of my line of thinking when I made this. Here's the doc:

          From a basic play issue perspective, there's a major problem with any game where the PCs aren't really capable of forming a group so that everyone playing can play together over an extended period of time. Nobody wants to spend all the effort to find a RPG group, start a game, and then have it dissolve into backstabbing until one player and the ST run a solo game while the rest either leave or have to sit and watch without anything to do.

          In general, this suffers the biggest issue fan-splat suffer: the concept is too narrow. There are lots of existing CofD games that have ways to execute the "evil overlord" concept, without it being the only viable concept of the game. Having the only real "splat" be philosophic means any functional group of PCs need to have compatible philosophies since there's nothing else to connect them together. A fan-splat that's actually made for people that to solo play isn't an inherently bad thing, but if you're going to do that you need to lean into it with more than just pronouncements about how the character type doesn't get along with people enough to form a group.

          The very prescriptive relationships of Dark Masters and the rest of the inhabitants of the setting is also very off-putting and rather out of step with the CofD's preference to let such matters vary by time and location as individual personalities matter the most.

          I can't think of a single character this supports, that I can't make in one of the existing CofD games, which would be more fun to do because then I could play with other people too.

                                                          I will admit that I finally checked out the document, and I am going to have to ask what it was that you didn't like about Beast, because...It kind of seems like you mostly want a reskinned Beast with increased "hard to kill" factor with your project, not gonna lie.
                                                          There are a lot of things that I don't like about Beast, and I will admit that the Dark Master thing is somewhat similar to Beast, but many of my inspirations didn't have much to do with Beast. So, I'll tell you what I don't like about Beast that I'm avoiding here. Because while Dark Masters are similar to Beasts, there are some key differences.
                                                          1. There is no kinship. Dark Masters don't have an innate connection to the other creatures in the Chronicles of Darkness world that makes them more likely to like them.
                                                          2. Beasts seem to be under the impression that they're the good guys, or at least that their actions are excusable because they teach lessons. The Dark Masters know full well that they're evil and most of what they do is exclusively for their own benefit. Though, it is possible for a Dark Master to be good, just incredibly difficult.
                                                          3. The transformation mechanics I'm using are based on the mechanics from Demon but simplified because I like the Demon mechanics more than the Beast ones.

                                                          There are a number of other reasons why I don't like Beast, but they aren't relevant to Dark Masters.

                                                          And now, other differences that aren't connected to things I don't like in Beast (as in, they either aren't connected to Beast at all or they are connected to things that I'm oaky with in Beast):
                                                          1. I don't mind that the Lairs in Beast aren't in the physical world, but the Dark Master dungeons are in the physical world. This is, in essence, to put them in more constant danger. They have to worry about far more threats attacking them than the Beasts do.
                                                          2. Minions are a big part of Dark Master while they aren't in Beast. It's why there are core mechanics tied into Dark Power for their creation and not just merits. I'm also giving the players the hunter the vigil monster creation rules for their minions so they can be incredibly customizable.
                                                          3. Dark Masters do not create heroes or harm people with their mere existence. Instead, they have a very strong urge and incentive to act evil with it even making them more powerful.
                                                          4. The increased durability thing is meant to serve the idea behind how combat works in this game. Dark Masters should not be afraid of combat, but they should be cautious about it. There are still potential consequences for them dying, so they should be the sort to send their minions in first to weaken the enemy before coming in themselves. This is both inside and outside the lair. The increased hard to kill factor was something that I debated with myself a lot before going with it.
                                                          5. The gods are the enemies of the Dark Masters, and I'll probably rewrite it so that the Dark Masters are gathering in groups because they know the gods are coming for them. As for the Beasts, the True Fae respect and fear them and their goddess is fully on their side.
                                                          6. Dark Masters have grand ambitions as a core part of their gameplay. I've seen gamelines (I think a fan gameline, Genius the Transgression, but I don't remember for sure) where a fourth aspiration is added in. What I did was change that Aspiration into their Virtue equivelant (their Megalomania) because it's something that can never be truly satisfied. Basically, Dark Masters persue their own personal goals and such.
                                                          7. The Dark Masters aren't easily set into types. For player versatility and based on feedback I got for a previous fan gameline attempt, I didn't include an X-Splat, just a Y-Splat. Each Dark Master is hand-crafted by a god and can be fairly unique.
                                                          8. I included a rule that gives Dark Masters the option to make their transformation at the cost of weakening the transformation. This also has the cost that it gives the Dark Master a greater need to hide than most other Dark Masters and most (though not all) other player splats in Chronicles of Darkness.
                                                          9. The Dark Masters are fundamentally meant to be final bosses or commanders of dungeons. They are anything from Sephiroth to Sauron to Ainz to the Overlord from the Overlord games to the Dungeon Keeper. They are not meant to be the monsters of myth or nightmares.

                                                          Thank you for this feedback and I hope this helps. If the Dark Masters come off as reskinned Beasts, then it means that I have some massive failings and really need the help. The fundamental premise is supposed to be different than in Beast, and I tried to make the mechanics with the premise in mind.
