        Monument Gold Cigarettes ●
        This carton of cigarettes bears the black and gold coloring and mountain peak icon of the Monument Cigarette brand. Despite the company going out of business in the mid nineties, the cigarettes contained in the carton remain as fresh as the day they were packed, and have a smooth, earthy flavor with a distinctly floral aftertaste. Each carton contains 10 packs of twenty cigarettes, and are only available by special order from adds placed in alternative newspapers.


        Size: 0 Durability: 0(1) Availability: 2

        Powers
        Steady Nerves: Monument Gold Cigarettes calm the nerves and relax the mind. By spending a point of willpower, lighting the cigarette and smoking it, the smoker gains a +1 equipment bonus to Contested rolls and Resistance against fear or mind control and also grants a +1 equipment bonus to Breaking Point rolls until the end of the Scene.

        Drawback
        Habit Forming: Monument Gold Cigarettes were originally pulled from the market due to a potent, highly addictive chemical additive. Anyone that uses Steady Nerves suffers the Deprived Condition if they do not smoke another Monument Gold Cigarette within24 hours. This is in addition to the other negative consequences of smoking.
        The Okami Sundown Custom ●●●
        The Ōkami Sundown Custom is a modified high performance sports bike released in the year 2000 as part of the “Red Sun” series by the now-defunct Ōkami Motors. The design of the bike is sleek and streamlined, with a powerful engine and paint job in the hues of the setting sun. The Sundown Custom was owned by Kazuo Ueda, a talented motorcycle racer and engineer. Uedo was descended from a long line of witches and practitioners of dark arts, and it is said that the vehicle was inhabited by devils. Ueda went missing in 2004 and the bike has passed through several hands since that time, and was last seen in the possession of a Southern Californian street racer.

        Dice Modifier: +2 Size: 6 Durability: 2(5) Speed: 120; High Acceleration Availability: 4

        Powers
        Indestructible: The vehicle also runs smoothly despite years of usage. Even if reduced to scrap metal the vehicle regenerates a point of structure each night until fully restored.
        To use the following abilities, the possessor must spend 3 points of Willpower to gain the Bonded Relic condition.
        Devil Fueled: The Sundown Custom has enhancements both mechanical and mystical. The power of the entity bound to the motorcycle increases the Bikes dice modifier by +2 and its Speed by 10 (these adjustments are already reflected in the Relic’s statistics). It also grants its rider 1 point of Armor.

        Drawback
        Possessed: A spirit named Victory that Devours the Heart has been bound to the machine. The spirit is wrathful and competitive to an extreme, often using its influence over its rider to compel to use any means necessary to achieve victory, up to an including violence.
        If the entity is exorcised or otherwise removed, the Sundown Custom loses Indestructible and Devil Fueled, and becomes a mundane (but still high performance) vehicle.
        Victory that Devours the Heart: Rank 2; Influence (Wrath); Numina: Implant Mission, Left Handed Spanner, Pathfinder, Speed
        Sacrifice: Victory that Devours the Heart must be appeased to retain its favor. At least 1 point of lethal damage worth of human blood must be poured into its fuel tank each day at sunset – failure to do so prevents the rider from using Devil Fueled for one day.

                    Re: Bow Weapon

                    I was intending to do a bow weapon at some point in time, so why not.
                    Second Edition has been very keen to eliminate most instances of multiple attacks in a round, but it leaves a gap of how to deal with swarms or other mob-style enemies in a way that is both efficient and in line with the CofD game style. The best solution is to model the attack as an Area of Effect.Swarms in system are specifically vulnerable to Area of Effect attacks, and I am certain players can think of many other ways to make us of this type of effect. Sample posted below.
                    Zenith Ash ●●●●
                    Zenith Ash is a Manchu style composite bow whose origin dates back to the mid 17th century. The weapon is made from several types of pale colored wood and a bowstring made from horse tendon. Who it was made for has been lost to history, but legends state that the bow fires arrows blessed by the sun and that strike hard enough to burn the flesh of its enemies. It is inlaid with intricate carvings of sun symbols and draws as smoothly and fires as well as the day it was made.

                    Damage: +2 Initiative: -3 Strength: 2 Size: 3 Durability: 2(6) Range: 25/50/120 Availability: 4

                    Powers
                    Damage dealt by the Zenith Ash is considered supernatural damage. In addition, the weapon is completely immune to damage caused by exposure to heat or flame.
                    To use the following abilities, the possessor must spend 4 points of Willpower to gain the Bonded Relic condition.
                    Searing Arrow: Arrows fired from Zenith Ash can be imbued with the essence of light and fire. By spending a point of Willpower and making a successful attack against a target, the damage inflicted is considered to be Fire against any creature with a Bane or vulnerability pertaining to fire. On an exceptional success, the target gains the Burning Tilt.
                    Zenith Conflagration: Once per chapter, an arrow launched from Zenith Ash can be commanded to erupt into a corona of light and heat. Once the arrow is fired, at any time during the scene the wielder of the Bow can take an Instant action to spend 2 Willpower and make a Presence+Occult roll. If successful, the arrow explodes into a brief but intense bonfire that deals 4 Lethal damage to anything within 5 meters of where the arrow landed. On an exceptional success, the explosion also applies the Inferno Tilt to the Area.

                    Drawback
                    Burning: When used, Zenith Ash generates heat intense enough to blister flesh. If the Bow is fired more than once per scene, the wielder suffers one point of bashing damage for each turn they maintain a grip on the weapon.

                                    A few more Relics for today.
                                    The Ferryman’s Mask ●●
                                    The Ferryman’s Mask is a ceramic mask, painted white and glazed with no features except the eye holes. Its specific origins are unknown, but it is said to have been used as a prop in the ceremonies of an obscure spiritualist cult in the 1920’s. According to surviving documents, the mask gave the wearer the ability to interact with the spirits of the departed. The Ferryman’s Mask was last seen as an heirloom decoration on the wall of a home in a suburb of Los Angeles.

                                    Size: 2 Durability: 1(3) Availability: 3

                                    Powers
                                    Crossing the River: By spending a 2 points of Willpower and donning the Mask, the wearer gains the ability to see, communicate with and physically interact with ghosts and similar death-aspected entities in Twilight. However, this also grants such entities the ability to freely interact with the user as well. This power remains in effect as long as the mask is worn, or until the next sunrise. The mask also grants a measure of protection against the charms of the dead, providing a +1 equipment bonus to contest or resist supernatural powers used by ghost, mummies, vampires, and similar dead or undead creatures.
                                    Synaptic Interface Device ●●●
                                    The Synaptic Interface Device (also called a SID) is an a highly experimental technology that reads the activity of the users brain and translates the information into signals that can transmitted via wi-fi, allow the user to mentally interact with electronic devices. The current model of the device is a mesh headgear connected to AR Glasses that give the user a visual component to the information being processed and transmitted. The technology is being developed by Elysium Concepts Ltd., a well funded, independent think-tank dedicated to exploring the boundaries between electronic programming and the human mind.

                                    Size: 2 Durability: 2(5) Availability: 4

                                    Powers
                                    Synaptic Interface: The wearer gains access to the 3 dot version of Technopathy Merit (Hurt Locker, pg. 77)for as long as they are wearing the SID. The wearer uses Intelligence+Computer when activating Technopathy. In addition, the wearer may spend an additional point of Willpower to directly interface with computer systems. The wearer use their skills with any such items within their sensory range (any security measure must still be defeated).

                                    Drawback
                                    Bleeding Edge: The SID is the product of remarkably advanced, but otherwise mundane technology. Any attempts to analyze, repair, or replicate the device suffers a -3 penalty.
                                    Neural Strain: The SID can trigger severe headaches and vertigo. Failing a Technopathy roll gives the user the Sick(moderate) Tilt until the end of the scene. On a dramatic failure, they gain the Sick (grave) Tilt instead.

                                        Name: The Instrument
                                        Durability X(5), Size X(2), Structure X(5)
                                        Relic Rating: ••••••
                                        Powers
                                        Made Just For You: The relic takes on a new form that is just perfect for the user within a single theme as such the Instrument becomes the perfect instrument for the new user.
                                        Music of The Muse: By spending 1 point of Willpower, the possessor gains the Rote quality on Expression rolls until the end of the scene while playing The Instrument
                                        Can't Get Rid Of Me: Even when the owner believes destroyed the instrument it will always reappear near the user back in perfect condition within the next 24 hours.
                                        Unleashed Desires: By Spending a 2 Point Of Willpower The User Roll Presence + Expression - Highest Resolve in the area. On a success, the listeners gain the wonton condition and Charmed condition.
                                        Inspire: By spending a point of willpower the user can give a crowd the inspired condition. Inspired to do what is the question.
                                        Rapture: By Spending a willpower the user can cause a target to become incapacitated with pleasure and grand visions of the universe like an amazing acid trip. The user rolls Presence + Expression vs Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance.

                                        Drawback:
                                        Hedonism: The User May Only Regain willpower by giving into his desires to resist this rolling Resolve + Composure -1 Per Use a day.
                                        Greatness Demands Sacrifice: The Music of the Instrument demands sacrifice.
                                        The user of the instrument is Doomed. This is the means by which destiny brings her life as she knows it to a tragic end such as addicted, betrayed, crippled, devoured, enslaved, imprisoned, maddened, murdered, ostracized, possessed, ruined, or turned. This either kills her or leaves her alive and suffering. Whenever you spend Willpower to avoid the Doom, you add two dice instead of three (or +1 to a static value). However, whenever you spend Willpower on an action that will further the Doom but the roll fails, you immediately regain the spent
                                        Willpower. The Storyteller is the arbiter of which actions delay or hasten your character’s Doom.
                                        Cult of Personality: Around the User, a cult will build answering only to the machinations of the dark voice within the Instrument. Carrying out its unknown plans. Example of tasks Ritual Killings in the name of the owner and putting their name all over the area. Mass suicide for the user.
                                        Addictive: The user feels a compulsion to use the Relic. Whenever confronted by a situation in which
                                        the Relic could theoretically help the character, the player must roll a Resolve + Composure roll to resist giving in. Failure on this roll indicates that he uses the item’s supernatural ability regardless of the situation. Dramatic failure indicates a loss of a Willpower point (in addition to any spent on the object’s activation). Any time the character fails this Resolve + Composure roll, the next instance of resistance is marked with a cumulative -1 penalty. Hence, it becomes harder and harder to resist using the Relic the more the character gives into it. To remove this addiction, the character must spend five days without using the object’s supernatural blessing.

                                        Description: The Instrument takes on various forms depending on the user. It always comes with the same occult symbol of the muse on it made of some kind of stone.
                                        Bio: Through History, it has shown up in the hands of famous music players. The last one was a famous Metal artist by the name of Lord Ivan. He was killed on stage by his fans who in a fit of mass hysteria ate him alive on stage. A cult of his followers then burned down the town as retribution for the killing. In the madness, the instrument vanished.
