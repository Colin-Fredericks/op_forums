    What are you planning for WtF?

    02-06-2014, 02:24 PM
    Right now I'm running a VtR:Blood and Smoke game, but I'm terribly excited about the Idigam Chronicle, so I've already started planning a werewolf "sequel" to my VtR game, which is set in Seattle 1982.

    Assuming that "Furry, rage monster spirit cops" is still the focus of Idigam Chronicle, the idea I've got so far is that what the players were doing in Seattle will cause ripples in the neighboring wilderness of Washington and British Columbia and start coming to a head in the early 1990s. Cue the PC wolves.

    How exactly the spiritual trouble will manifest I haven't decided yet, since I'm still getting into NWoD, but I'm looking forward to it nonetheless.

    What are y'all planning in regards to future Werewolf games, using Idigam Chronicles or otherwise?

                  The Idigam Chronicles isn't even likely to come out for two months. And I just checked and it's been pushed back to more than that.

                  But that hasn't stopped me from starting to think about it since December. Because I'm a goon, a goon who loves Werewolf the Forsaken.

                  Anyways, my Changeling game is over, and while I'm running Blood and Smoke (the players and I enjoying the new system) it looks like I get to be a player again when Idigam Chronicles comes out, woo.

                  Obviously everything could change when the game comes out, but I'll likely be playing a Mental character (my first character was Physical, and my last one was Social, I had a Mental character in between but the game didn't last long) and I've been encouraged by the ST to be a bad guy. So when the book comes out we'll see if that means a darker-themed shaman (huge fan of the Spirit, didn't get as involved in it as I wanted to the first time) or perhaps some other option will be available.

                  Just from what I know of the ST, there'll likely be some repercussions from previous games. I know in our Changeling game we ran across a werewolf that had been severely traumatized by the actions of our werewolves in a previous game, so probably some more things like that. But no idea where the setting will be.

                      Well, I have a game right now that is about rebuilding the city from the events that happened in the previous game. Bale Hounds infiltrated most packs of the city and instigated a total war of Forsaken versus Pure, with the help of an Idigam that was found trapped in Joelma, a building that caught fire decades ago, and is now a major wound. The game ended with the death of the Idigam, but most packs dead, and the ones left alive don't trust each other anymore (fear of Bale Hound infiltration). Lot's of unprotected locus, major Bale Hound infestation in the city, a very nasty place to have a territory. Now seems like the players plan on uniting the packs.

                      When Dark Eras come out, I'm planing a mini-chronicle set in 70s Sao Paulo (Which will probably have a lot in common with NYC in the 70s ;D). Players pack will run the Joelma territory. And one day, an Idigam will arrive and they will hunt it into the building.

                      I also plan on doing a Viking one-shot one day. Might be cool as hell.

So, the eight children and the tribes of Ur-Farah convened. This new commandment was debated, for six days, and seven nights. On the morning of the seventh day a vote was held. Five of the eight voted that them and their tribe would uphold the commandment of Iduth-Nanna, the other three grew angry. They said that humanity would harm the mother Akihana.                                                           My plans are finishing the 100+ page overhaul and revision I've been working on with a friend/Co-ST the last few months (soon!); Idigam Chronicles isn't on the roster yet without any hard info on what's being looked at done; too hush hush and I've still got a few months of games to run for my players. Hell, it may not even fit our ongoing game at all, whereas what's been worked on specifically addresses what we and our players wanted to see. My plans are to get all the Werewolf players on what we've got rolling, play with it, refine it, see what Idigam Chronicles brings for material we want to use, and go from there.

                                                          Once the overhaul doc is done I'll share the link or PDF with you lot to play around with and see what you think; it has a few smaller areas to address, though though overall mechanical meat and some revised explanations of core ideas are done (fiction and similar suggestions will be for later if it turns into a popular fan project or something else).

                                                          Can't please everyone, obviously, but we're already working on converting our players over, and so far they're stoked. The Instincts (now tweaked slightly more) are only the tip of the iceberg. Might fit the tastes of some forumites quite a bit better than what has been bandied about, likely won't be the tastes of some, but there is likely material to be used from it that folks will like regardless of how much is used. Fair warning, it's not going to be the finalized version; we have to play around with it more to see how things roll with our crew, so I'm assuming there will be ongoing tweaks and possibly some eventual incorporation of Idigam Chronicles material some months down the road.
