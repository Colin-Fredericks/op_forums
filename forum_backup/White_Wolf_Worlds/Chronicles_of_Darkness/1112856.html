      I wouldn't really notice some of it, since changelings look like humans, and vampires often look like humans too.

      If I was absolutely sure that it was a vampire draining blood from someone, I would be more surprised than anything else. Incredibly surprised. I don't know about you, but I've lived most of my life accepting that there's no supernatural, and the monsters in our lives are entirely human. Even during my more exploratory periods I've found nothing to really warrant thinking there are monsters tearing people to shreds and ripping their lives apart. So I think even if I saw someone gnawing on someone's neck, and they 'hissed' at me or whatever when they saw me, I'd probably think it was some asshole with fake fangs. It would have to do more to really say, 'Vampire,' to me.

      And so if I saw what I thought was a human drinking another human's blood or attacking someone in a violent fashion, with the other person clearly hurt, I'd get involved. Though from an in-world point of view, a vampire can make 'the Kiss' a pleasurable experience so it just looks like someone is making out with the partner in an abusive relationship.

      If I saw something that was clearly 'inhuman monster' fighting 'inhuman monster,' I'd be a lot more interested in watching than interfering.

                              The bystander effect is a real thing. As a ST, the whole scenario depends: Which one's the PC? The Vampire or the Changeling? Is it a PVP match? is the feeding violent or covert? Do I have an interesting mortal NPC ready to be introduced in such a fashion (doubtful)? Why should I provide a deus ex machina to save the changeling? I doubt the altercation happened in a vacuum, something happened to illicit the Kindred to such Masquerade breach, thus I wouldn't have mortals intervene, but another more rational Kindred definitively would, even if it's after the fact by hanging the episode over the aggressive vampire in blackmail fashion, whilst goading the imbecile to plug this masquerade breach.

                              Context and details are important.

                                                      Originally posted by Magicballz View Post
                                                      I know vampires def. belong to such organization. What about Changelings? Not sure if they have any hook. Supernaturals tend to get each other off the hook because if one goes down they all go down right?
                                                      Not really, and no. Changelings don't have large organizations but it would make sense for their smaller groups to try to get at least one 'in' on something in local government.

                                                      The revelation of one monster does not instantly reveal to the world the existence of every other monster. Vampires are concerned about themselves, the individual vampire. Outing another in order to get them killed is a dangerous but still available option to deal with a rival. Even the 'no masquerade breaches allowed' groups tend to do it for other reasons. The Seers because it might inspire someone to Awaken outside of the Exarchs control.

                                                      Humans would suspect other supernaturals might exist and that would be pretty bad I am guessing.
                                                      Humans know the supernatural exists, for the most part. Near every human in the Chronicles of Darkness has experienced something supernatural, it's part of character creation. But going into your basement and finding a face in the floor that asks you to bring it people to eat in exchange for gold isn't going to clue you in on the secret society of vampires in the city. When it comes to the supernatural, the gamelines are far outnumbered by things like ghosts and spirits.

                                                      In old World of Darkness it's more of an issue, as public 'acceptance' of the supernatural can shift the paradigm of their world. In Chronicles, it just makes things more scary. You feed a few of your friends to that face and now it's a torso and not a particularly friendly-looking one. You fill the basement with concrete, move, and spend the rest of your life avoiding poking around in weird mysteries because you want to live a long life.

                                                      But we aren't raised in the Chronicles of Darkness so we're going to act differently.
