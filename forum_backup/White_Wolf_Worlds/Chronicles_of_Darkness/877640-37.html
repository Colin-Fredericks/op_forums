                                                    Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post

                                                    Aspirations are the game's way of giving the intentions of a character a presence in the mechanics.
                                                    Aspirations are explicitly descibed as a Meta resource. I can have an aspiration for my character to have their S/O die because I want to explore that kind of loss on that character, without my character themselves intending to have their partner die.

                                                    It is also okay to select Aspirations that your character doesn’t want, but that you want to see happen to the char-acter. For example, your character almost certainly doesn’t want to be kidnapped by the man with the gray face that used to be her father, but that might be a story that you are interested in seeing. You can choose Aspirations that repre-sent you trying and failing to do something. You can have an Aspiration like “Fail to find proof of the existence of ghosts,” or “Fail to reconcile with Jane,” if you think that it will make a more interesting story. Even though the character has failed at what she wants, you as the player still get the reward for completing that Aspiration.
