    IMHO, the fact that every Arcanum that isn't Imperial is Ruling already addresses the fact that archmasters are “that much closer to the Supernal”. Heck, consider the implications that single change has for Mage Sight and Supernal Summons: Travelers interact with the Supernal World based on their Paths' symbology and their Ruling Arcana (e.g., Mastigos see Space and Mind as a Labyrinth and Chains; and they sense and summon Space/Mind Supernal Entities, as Imps and Wraiths). A Seeker, with ten Ruling-or-better Arcana, probably can see and summon all Supernal Entities, and sees them in a manner consistent with his Lustrum — which may or may not resemble the symbology of his former Path, depending on what ordeal he goes through during his Threshold Seeking.

                                One thing to potentially keep in mind - spell factors were much more tied to your arcana level in 1E.                                 Archmages being able to use Imperial Factors for anything if they're willing to reach for it is just as flavorful, in my opinion.
