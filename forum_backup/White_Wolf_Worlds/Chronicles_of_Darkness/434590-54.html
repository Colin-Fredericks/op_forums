    Talking of unnatural life, I found another set of Charms which would probably work very well for Fecundity: those of the Great Mother Kimbery. I've got a document here: https://drive.google.com/open?id=188...iONZ6f7bih4hDk

    Really, Kimbery's Charms as a whole work really well for Leviathan, seeing as she shares a lot of their associations - water, parenthood, being really a really shitty parent, fecundity and so on. Could the charm-set based off of Great Mother's Wame be adapted as a Fecundity Channel? I think that one problem with Mother of a Thousand Young as it stands is that it's trying to do too much at once via all of its Adaptations. I propose that we keep MoaTY as a short-term, in-combat Fecundity channel and add a second one for longer-term creatures, with Incubation of the Second Self being based off of the latter. That way we could organize Fecundity as a whole along these lines:

    Ancestral
    - Plant empathy/abilities (photosynthesis etc)
    - Animal empathy/commands (ala Lord of the Sea)
    - "I am an ecosystem unto myself"-type Channel (possibly allowing increasing one skill at the cost of another by having one part of they body-ecosystem devour another and breed more of itself?)

    Descendant
    - Mother of a Thousand Young
    - Hydra's Rebirth
    - Permanent Biological Construct Channel

    Another option would be to shift Hydra's Rebirth into Vitality (TBH I kinda think it ought to have been there in the beginning; regeneration and armour kind of go together) and shifting the "Ecosystem unto myself" channel into Descendent Fecundity. Maybe the plant zombies could be an Adaptation of the ecosystem Channel along with the permanent biological construct Channel?


    And finally, I've got a possible Ancestral Awareness Channel in the works as well. It's kind of a combination psychic/electrical sense, with the latter being based off of what sharks can do. A 'sight beyond sight'-type thing. I'll post it once I've got it in a reasonable state.

        Here's that Awareness Channel I was talking about. It's quite crude for now and I really don't know if it's too powerful or too weak.

        Sight Beyond Sight (Ancestral Channel)
        It is a human thing to equate sight with perception. The leviathan has rediscovered other ways of understanding the world.

        The leviathan possesses senses beyond the traditional five of humanity.

        Depth 1: Birthright of Awareness

        Depth 2: The leviathan can notice astral entities, projecting psychics or the use of psychic powers within their Wake as a niggling feeling of 'pressure' in the rough direction of the activity, although this feeling is nebulous and non-specific. Everything feels more or less the same, save for other leviathans' Wakes and the Sigils of Marduk. Atolls are invisible to the leviathan's senses, even when actively using psychic powers.

        Depth 3: The leviathan gains an ability similar to that of a shark to sense the presence of the Wicked Tribe's ancient bane: electricity. The leviathan can concentrate and roll Sheol + Science as an Instant action to sense any current electricity within (Sheol*5) yards. Within (Sheol) yards the leviathan is capable of sensing the minute electrical impulses released by the movement of muscles, rolling Sheol + Medicine. Those attempting to hide from the leviathan can do so by remaining utterly still.

        Depth 4: The leviathan's psychic sense improves, allowing him to roll Sheol + Occult as an Instant action to locate psychic activity within his Wake. This allows the leviathan to ask one of the following questions per success:
        • How far away is the psychic phenomenon?
        • What kind of psychic power is being used?*
        • In what direction is the psychic phenomenon?
        • Is the psychic phenomenon the result of a human?
        • How powerful is the psychic phenomenon?**
        *This provides broad answers such as 'telepathy' or 'pyrokinesis'.

        **This power provides either an assessment of an astral entity's Rank or the rating of the psychic Merit being used.

        Furthermore, the leviathan's electrical sense is improved. They no longer need to concentrate to sense electrical current, rolling as a Reflexive action and can sense muscle activity within (Sheol*5) yards.

        Depth 5: The leviathan no longer needs to concentrate to locate psychic phenomena within its Wake, allowing her to roll Sheol + Occult as a Reflexive action. Add the following questions to the list of those which the leviathan may ask:
        • In which direction is the nearest individual with a psychic ability?
        • How far is the nearest individual with a psychic ability?
        Some leviathans use this power to locate potentially useful individuals to approach. Others simply do their best to make such psychics Beloved and add them to their Cult

        The leviathan's sense for electricity extends its range, sensing electrical current at (Sheol*10) yards. Their sense for muscle activity becomes finer, allowing the leviathan to concentrate as an Instant action and roll Sheol + Medicine to guess at roughly what the individual is doing.

        Depth 6: The Leviathan's psychic senses pierce the souls and minds of those he analyses, allowing him to identify greater details of the mental abilities of others. Add the following questions to the list of those that may be asked with a successful Sheol + Occult roll:
        • How many psychic powers does this person possess?
        • What psychic power does this person use most?
        • What is this person's most powerful psychic ability?
        • What is this person's least powerful psychic ability?
        The leviathan need no longer concentrate to identify what another is doing via their muscle activity, rolling Sheol + Medicine as a Reflexive action.

        Depth 7: The primordial waters run deep, and to the things that lurk there the subtle fire of the psychic mind is nothing but a beacon, vulnerable by virtue of its openness. When the leviathan focuses the Wake upon a psychic being, human, Lahmasu or astral, he opens a number of doors equal to his Sheol, as opposed to half.

        The leviathan's electrical sense extends to cover the entirety of his Wake, both for current electricity and for muscle activity.

                                            Awareness

                                            Well, we've only got one Descendant Channel left to do here, and it seems to me that about the only thing we're missing in Awareness is some kind of prophetic ability. Now, prophecy is always tricky to do in an RPG, so perhaps we could work with that to make something a little unique as prophetic powers go. What if, instead of having the Leviathan be a 'future-seer', they are a speaker of self-fulfilling prophecies - part prediction and part memetic instrument of will. In essence what I'm proposing is that the Leviathan's prophecy, by virtue of being spoken, increases the odds of a specific future happening partly by giving dice to those furthering it and partly by serving as a statement to the Storyteller of what the player wants, allowing them to work that into the story or present obstacles to it as necessary. The 'weight' a Leviathan can put behind their future increases with increasing Depth.

                                            Elements

                                            Hmm. Well, passive Elements powers are tricky, but I have a couple of ideas. I did always like the old Rain-Dance of the Tempest Channel, so some kind of space-warping Channel based off of non-euclidean geometry seems like a good one, and fitting for Leviathan's themes. As for the other, what about a Channel that allows a degree of control over Resonance and Essence within the Wake? Leviathans predate most divisions, including presumably that between the flesh and the spirit. We could crib some powers from the Idigam's Essence-Shaping abilities. My only issue with that is that the power might be a too powerful to be a passive one, and it might be better as a Descendant Channel, which doesn't really help. Perhaps some kind of natural EMP/tech-disruption aura?

                                            Fecundity

                                            I've already proposed ideas for Fecundity above. With reference to the 'permanent minions' Channel idea, though, I did re-think it a little, and I'm proposing that we base it, again, off of the Idigam's Essence-Shaping abilities, albeit with a more fleshy 'spin'.

                                            Predation

                                            I propose a stealth-booster, escalating to effective invisibility, as per the old Stalker's Shifting Hide. It was a good one. That said, it might be better to shift that up to Descendant and maybe have the Ancestral Channel be something to do with terrifying speed and alacrity. A combination of a mantis shrimp's bullet-fast strike and a sailfish's sheer speed.

                                            For the remaining Descendant Channel, I find myself at a bit of a loss.

                                            Sanctity

                                            Again, Sanctity is tricky. I really Can't think of anything for the Ancestral Channels. Anything I can think of either belongs to a Descendant Channel, as a thing of the Cult or to Awareness.

                                                [Stub]

                                                Self-Serving Idol of Glory (Ancestral Channel)
                                                The Leviathan is no mere creature of flesh and bone, but a living god capable of empowering icons made in their honor.

                                                The Leviathan's Divine nature empowers and curses objects in their vicinity.

                                                Depth 1: Birthright of Sanctity

                                                Depth 2: The Leviathan may distribute up to their Sheol in equipment bonuses or increased weapon damage (to a maximum of 5) to objects in their immediate possession, as the items become sanctified by the Leviathan's Divine presence. These bonuses last until the end of the Scene, or if the Leviathan spends an Instant action and changes targets.

                                                Depth 3: Curses now flow outward, striking down foes blasphemous tools. The Leviathan can degrade the equipment bonus or weapon damage of up to Sheol items in their line of sight, reducing their bonus by Sheol/2, round up (to a minimum equipment or damage bonus of 0). These penalties last until the end of the Scene, or if the Leviathan spends a point of Willpower to changes targets.

                                                Depth 4: [Need a good item boost idea]

                                                Depth 5: Wrath falls upon whatever mortal artifice would dare impede a living god. For 1 Willpower, the Leviathan can make a Presence + Crafts roll with a damage bonus of Sheol to deal points of Structure damage (bypassing Durability) to any non-supernatural object that has harmed or otherwise obstructed their person this Scene. Supernatural objects can still be dealt damage, but do not have their Durability ignored.

                                                Depth 6: Blessings now flow through onto the Leviathan's followers. By succeeding at a Meditation roll, the Leviathan can grant a number of equipment bonuses or increased weapon damage equal to Sheol * Presence (to a maximum of 5 - the Knowledge penalty of the recipient) among their Cult's (or, rarely, other people's) items, divided as they choose. These objects must have been in some way dedicated to Leviathan, such as by having one of their names or depictions inscribed upon it, or being used in a Ritual. These bonuses last until the end of the Scene (or day, for an additional Willpower point), the item leaves the Leviathan's Wake, or the Leviathan cancels the effect.

                                                Depth 7: A Leviathan in Apotheosis can declare specific items or entire categories of object anathema to their presence. Succeed at a Meditation roll and spend 1 Willpower, then define either specific items (such as Gregor's car, or the lab vial with my blood) or a fairly specific category of items (such as bronze swords, or fabric the color red). All such objects within the Leviathan's Wake are treated as if they were a character in an Extreme Environment with a rating equal the the Leviathan's Presence, and their Durability treated as Stamina. The effect lasts for 1 day, until the item leaves the Leviathan's Wake, the Leviathan changes Depth, or if the Leviathan chooses to cancel the effect.

                                                [Comments: Maybe I should add Presence to Depth 5's damage, or just actually make it an attack roll? Might make the 6 and 7 Depth powers restricted to the current Scene's actors, and save the wide area effects for Rituals. Their general powers should be fine as is, though.]

                                                Sidebar: Ancient Gods and Modern Marvels
                                                So, how compatible is the Bronze Age divine power of the Wicked Tribe and recent technological innovation? That's up to the Storyteller. Sure, most of Tiamat's brood has their power sourced in flesh, blood, and brute psychic might, but the School of Sand would very much state that isn't all there is, or at least, must be. One thing that is important to note regarding this Channel, though - items properly warded, such as Marduk's technology, items soaked in or serving as a focus for Virtue, or simply other supernatural protective measures, are resistant or outright immune. Additionally, objects conducting enough electrical current to cause damage may qualify, or at least be immune to Leviathans of less Sheol than the damage their current would deal.
