                                                        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                        I must confess some interest in the mention of errata for the core system — obviously it's been about a decade since Blood & Smoke, and the Contagion Chronicle PG was pretty up-front about some of the shifts in design sensibilities as the Revised Storytelling System rolled out, but can you speak to any particulars or generalities that the errata are for, aside from streamlining the Strix and blood sorcery mechanics?
                                                        Mostly just little clarifications; nothing revelatory, but I wanted to address some of the more nagging issues that have come up over the years in Vampire specifically and the RSS in general. Mostly its collating some of the stuff that's changed since Requiem 2E was published, mixed in with a few judgment calls. One thing in particular was putting all the ghoul family rules in one place, as they're kind of scattered between VTR and Half-Damned, and a little vague at that. The only thing I really straight-up changed is how you learn out-of-clan proprietary Disciplines; it's not that different, but it's ever so slightly less restrictive, and potentially adds an interesting story element to seeking them out.
