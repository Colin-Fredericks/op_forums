                Originally posted by Sothis Ascends, pg. 23
                Medjay are famed archers and are found in large numbers in Upper Kemet, where they mostly find work as soldiers and bodyguards.
                Originally posted by Sothis Ascends, pg. 25
                Medjay are the ethnic group hailing from Ta-Seti, the foreign land bordering on Kemet’s southern reaches. Large numbers of Medjay have entered Kemet over the centuries, and they maintain their own clans in and amongst the greater population. Medjay have no rights as citizens, though are still protected from wrongdoing by the law — they don’t take part in civic labor, but aren’t supported in hard times either, and must find work for themselves. Their people have a long martial tradition of their own, and as they don’t work in other careers most of the year like Kemet’s own citizen-soldiers, many Medjay find work as hunters, bodyguards, soldiers, and constables for local law enforcement. By the time of the Turn, the Medjay working for the nomarchs and Great House make up almost all of Kemet’s tiny “standing army,” and armed Medjay are an unremarkable sight in and around the nation’s centers of power. Arisen who are ethnically “Nubian” are usually seen as Medjay by mortals once their sahu fully forms.

                                                A section to be filled out in its wn GDoc. Legacies. Some of these are familiar from previous books. Others are original concepts.

                                                LEGACIES OF THE FIVEFOLD SOUL

                                                Ab

                                                • Dreamspeakers: All sorcerers can enter a state of mind which allows them to reach the fringes of A'aru. Those initiated into the Legacy are more skilled than most, with the greatest of their number able to cast their Ba to the stars while awake. When Sybaris shapes the dreams of certain mortals, these Hemka work to interpret the omens they bring. (Mind)

                                                • Keepers of the Covenant: More numerous in this age of gods, the Legacy's Awakened are spread out among the Black Land's villages, and the nomadic tribes which wander within and beyond its borders. Far from Re's influence yet untouched by the call of Duat, they seek the favor of the small gods of Neter-Khertet to ensure the prosperity of their own communities. (Fate)

                                                • Singers of the False Heart: When the Shuankhsen and the solar priesthood unleashed the Amkhata they created, they did not realize how much chaos they would wreak upon the Black Land in the decades that followed. Initiates of the Legacy combine their knowledge of Duat and Neter-Khertet to tame or destroy those which should not be. (Death)


                                                Ba

                                                •

                                                •

                                                • Word Bearers: Like the Cryptologos of later eras, this Kemetic Legacy demonstrates great skill in the use of High Speech in their magic. They serve as translators for the Great House and the Weret-Hekau, and are often called upon to ensure the truth behind the words of foreign dignitaries. (Prime)


                                                Ka

                                                •

                                                •

                                                •


                                                Ren

                                                •

                                                •

                                                • The Unspoken: Though feared for dark practices, most members of the Legacy only use their gifts on sorcerers who have fully rejected the Law. They are called upon by the Weret-Hekau to sunder the souls of such heretics, yet forever consign their own to Ammut through carrying out such acts. (Death)


                                                Sheut

                                                • Eyes of Ma'at: Sorcerers who refined their magic to the task of stripping away Sekhem, this Legacy would form late into the war between the three alliances. After the battle at Nekhen-Nekheb, they continue to grow in numbers and prominence as they scour the kingdom for Deathless and Lifeless who chose to remain rather than scatter to the four winds. (Fate)

                                                • Forge Masters: Paying homage to the god, Ptah, the sorcerers of the Legacy work rituals which enchant and imbue items into potent vessels of magic. Some of their works are sought by Hemka of other sects, but most are enshrined within their reliquary at their patron's temple in Inbu-Hedj. (Prime)

                                                • Stone Scribes: In studying the mysteries of a departed soul's Ren, these Awakened sekhau invoke them alongside their utterances to perceive their mark on the Scroll of Ages. Some of these deceased are erased from the Scroll, their names stricken by the Scribes so that they do not harm the future with poisonous influence. (Time)

                                                        To be added to the Per Henet GDoc.

                                                        -=-

                                                        When we are power: The dead must live apart from the living. That is the Law, and we honor it as we walk beneath Khonsu's light. To do otherwise provokes the Devourer, and what's left of our souls are forfeit to her on our final descent to Duat. In Waset and most other cities in Ta-Sheema, the House of Repose rule and guide the Hungry Dead. We dedicate our Requiem in service to the gods, and remind our people of their place in the Black Land.

                                                        When we are in trouble: It seldom happens. But when the Per Henet wanes in power, we have greater reason to stand united. The Cults of Sutek and Shezmu sow only Chaos wherever they reign. And foreign Kindred from lands beyond worsen that discord by spreading their ways. This cannot stand. As the righteous of Ma'at among the Damned, it is our duty to impose Order over Chaos, wherever it may arise.

                                                        -=-

                                                        On a semi-related note, seeing as the Usiri are associated with Azar, I was thinking of having the Per Henet's greatest holdings be at Abdju instead of Inbu-Hedj. Thematically, it's very appropriate. The Hill of Relics was a traditional burial place for Kemet's first kings, and - coupled with their priesthood's reverence for the dead - the House of Repose would have had a perfect place to hold court for all the Hungry Dead in the Black Land.

                                                        Of course, seeing as these are Kindred we're talking about, I imagine the "old guard" at Abdju would have been at odds with those of their kind who made Inbu-Hedj their home. This latter group would have probably been garnering more influence due to living close to the Great House, not to mention Abdju falls in prominence over the years due to the rise of Re's solar priests (and those of Nebthet).

                                                        And then all the crazy stuff that went down in the First Turn would screw everything up.

                                                        But anyway. Back on topic. What do you guys think? Should the Usiri Bloodline emerge as a result of Arisen rule at Abju? And would the Anubi Bloodline also be present here, seeing as Sothis Ascends explicitly mentions their namesake in the Abdju write-up?
