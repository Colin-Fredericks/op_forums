                                                            Originally posted by yrtalien View Post
                                                            I know that this particular lash is intended to grapple but could the cephalisst instead punch followed by the telekinesis power Throw to punch and send someone back a mighty distance potentially damaging them twice, albeit quite flavorfully?

                                                            With all the examples of Variation/Scar entanglement, I tried to keep it simple, not even telling you the Magnitude of the relevant Variation/Scar. You can channel Telekinesis to throw your enemies, as long as your Telekinesis is of sufficient Magnitude, and yes, you can switch different effects if you're channeling a Tiered Variation. Once activated, all Variations remain active until the end of the scene, allowing you to call upon their effects each turn as long as you have the actions to do so.

                                                            Originally posted by lnodiv View Post
                                                            Looking at the latest preview, I see one of the options imposes an initiative penalty...

                                                            That seems kind of needlessly fiddly to me. The rules framework isn't super clear about how one would apply an initiative penalty to someone that has a power that may represent inborn ability, as opposed to gear. Is it always active, because they are always 'equipped' with the ability? Is it only active once they've used it in a conflict scene? Is it only active if they *intend* to use it?
                                                            Initiative penalties with weapons have always felt needlessly fiddly to me, TBH, but the CofD system has them, and so Lash does, too. I'd only impose the Initiative penalty while the Variation is active - just like you'd only suffer a penalty for a sword while that sword was in your hand.

                                                            A good question, since it doesn't say in the text. Given that Stun has its own effect, it might be easiest to say Explosive Lashes must be Cutting and follow the same rules as normal explosives (Agg at ground zero, lethal nearby, bashing farther away).
