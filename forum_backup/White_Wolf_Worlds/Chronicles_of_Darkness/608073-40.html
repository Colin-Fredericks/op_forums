                    To which I replied: "The interesting thing about this group is that ... the cult of Moloch would likely have been the authority in the area where this happened - their rebellion against it is the transgressive act, even if it's a transgression we'd approve of in the here and now. It might mean they concentrate on monsters and cults who occupy positions of power, and who appear to be beneficient (or even are beneficient when paid in horror). It's also a group that doesn't necessarily need a blood component - they could recruit new sacrifices, literal or figurative.

                                                Actually, one of the main plot points in my Geist game is that there's a Ceremony you can use that allows you to foresee when a specific person will have a brush with mortal danger, and then allows you to "modify the narrative" so that the mortal danger becomes an actual death.
