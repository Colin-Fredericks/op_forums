                Cool, thanks for the prestige class, although I'd have reversed a few things, since the gmc won't let you become past rank 2 losing all your anchors and going to the underworld essentially forever the players won't be encountering a ghost with effective arete of 3 or more without dungeon diving in the underworld. Well unless you meant for ghosts to be able to still buy more arete even while dead, or have a system for creatures gaining rank that I missed.
                No you don't improve your Arete, you use Rank instead of Arete. You gain Rank the same way you gain Arete (Experience...). You could disregard entirely the need of an Eidolon to stay much time in the Underworld or near the Anchor, if that works best for you.
                I haven't playtested this prestige class yet. As for the game itself, yes, I've converted a Pathfinder game to this. The game is playing smoothier than Pathfinder or D&D. Though it still has its rule holes and some errors from the book. And spellcasting pool is perhaps a little too high if you start at an experienced level instead of starting from level 1.

                In dnd class is everything, but since attributes and skills are unrelated to further arete purchases I'm not sure how much the non spellcasting classes will care about gaining arete. If you want an even closer emulation of dnd you might want to include an option that arete adds to health levels and attack rolls, using that option from mirrors.
                Actually, the game already has. There is an optional hack in the book, called System hack: High level hit points that adds health levels. I use it. Know that while non spellcasting classes don't gain spells, they still gain class features, AND health (with the hack) AND resistance to supernatural effects, AND more importantly, the possibility to use 2 Willpower per turn!

                I also wonder about balance with the spellcasting classes, have you found that the exp payments for their spellcasting merits and arete prevent spellcasters from basically going codzilla like magic knights?
                Spellcasting merits are not payed for. They're granted by leveling in the appropriate class. Wizard only pay new rotes added from scrolls and other spellbooks.
                .
                As for codzillas, I nerfed the stat boosting spells, and I was careful not to create too many "+1 to this and to that" spells. What you might see is that a wizard might take high Melee, high Strenght and Stamina and "go melee". That's fine. The "meleer" classes are still going to have many class features you will be losing and won't be able to obtain if not by taking "meleer" class levels. You can't prepare too many spells at time like you could in D&D, so you'll better choose your spells wisely. Same for clerics. Focusing on buffing up when you should maybe prepare a dispel magic and more "wizardey" spells, will be a problem.
                Then, use magic device also doesn't exist anymore the same way it did. A "fighter" or "rogue" could take a wand, roll the wand level + Occult to activate the wand itself.

                Anyway, I'm looking forward to give spellcasters a nerf. Concentration at the moment doesn't exist. I'm going to create penalities for spellcasting while in melee.

                As for Bleeding edge, I'll be looking at it to better answer you, as for now I'm in a hurry. Have a good day.

                        Blackmoon
                        Quote:                         "I won't let you leave, you're not going to be a problem to my city any longer!"
                        Concept: Fighter mage at the city guard's service.
                        Class: Wizard 1° Race: Human
                        Alignment: Lawful neutral Virtue: Just Vice: Hasty
                        Attributes: Intelligence 3 Wits 2 Resolve 2 Strenght 3 Dexterity 2 Stamina 3 Presence 2 Manipulation 2 Composure 2
                        Skills: Academics 3 Occult 3 Investigation 2, Athletics 3 Melee (sword) 3 Ranged 2 Stealth 3, Persuasion 3, Socialize 1
                        Merit: Allies (city guard) 1, Area of Expertize (Sword), Extended Spell 2, Professional Training ("Soldier",Athletics and Melee) 2, Resources 2, Silent Spell, Wizard 1.
                        Skill trick: Always investigating (Extra Talent)
                        Health: 8 Defense: 5 Willpower: 4 Initiative: 4 (2 with Sword)
                        Spells: 1st level: Magic missile, Magic Weapon, Shocking grasp. 5 per day.
                        Spellbook: 1st level: Magic missile, Magic weapon, Shocking grasp.
                        Attack:
                        Sword 2L, 7 dice, 9 again.


                        Oreste just killed a couple of guards, who couldn't even scratch the monk because of its speed. Blackmoon was trying to catch Oreste running him over, but he manged to escape thanks to his superior speed (Fastest of them All skill trick). She thinks he's hidden in a granary. She has all motivation to bring Oreste to justice or to kill him.
                        Her inability to catch him costed her many sacrifices, including her status within the city guards. But she's prepared now. She casts magic weapon on her sword to make it a 3L.
                        Blackmoon enters the granary, hoping to find Oreste and defeat him for good.
                        Oreste is hidden behind some sacks, eager to kill his nemesis, He rolls Dexterity 4 + Stealth 3 to hide, getting two successes. Blackmoon is well aware of Oreste's tricks, and watches out for peril. She rolls Wits 2 + Composure 2 + Investigate 2 (thanks to her Skill Trick), but only gets one success. Oreste is silent and sneaky, but as soon as Blackmoon appears inside the granary, he ambushes her with his katar, denying her the Defense.
                        Luckily for her, Oreste misses, even with 8 dices. Time to roll for Initiative.
                        Oreste's Initiative is a 7, Blackmoon's 3. Oreste is angry for the miss from the first time and decides to use one Willpower to get 3 extra dice for his next attack. Blackmoon's Defense is 5. And still no successes, even with 6 dice.
                        Blackmoon's turn now. She starts with a full force swing against Oreste, to get +2 forgoing Defense. She also spends 1 Willpower to get 3 more dices. That's about 5 dices because of Oreste's Defense, a 7. First blood! With one success, Blackmoon scores 4 points of lethal damage with her sword.
                        It's Oreste's chance to take out Blackmoon in one hit once again, and he chooses to forgo Defense and spend another Willpower Point to hit Blackmoon. She's vulnerabile now. 5 successes! Blackmoon is now 6L of 7 Health points! He also takes the Steadfast Condition.
                        Blackmoon is really hurt, almost dead, and Stunned. She can spend her last Willpower Point to act but take -3 to all action she takes. She swings again, against no Defense, but at -3 and cannot spend Willpower. That's 4 dice, 9 again.With 1 success, Oreste is down to full lethal damage. If she wasn't Stunned, she could have casted Shocking Grasp and electrocuted Oreste.
                        The monk crumbles to earth, along with Blackmoon

                        A test for martial/caster disparity. Blackmoon didn't manage to use magic in a really meaningful way, and almost died. If instead of Fastest of them All Oreste had Size Up, he could have rerolled his first attack and probably dropped her dead really fast. Blackmoon only magic was magic weapon (which actually helped her a lot with damage output). She could have tried to beat Oreste a bit and then cast magic missile into full lethal damage, but Oreste would have fled outside of sight, allowing him to break line of sight.

                        Not much to say here for now. First level is harsh for wouldbe fighter mages. I'll be playtesting higher level martial-caster disparity, i hope.

                            Originally posted by Jesuncolo View Post
                            Dramatic Failure: The character’s world view has been changed drastically. The character suffers from traumatic stress. Shift one dot in the appropriate Axis and choose from the following Conditions (or create a new one with Storyteller approval): Broken, Fugue, Guilty. Also, take a Beat.
                            Failure: The character’s world view has been shaken and he probably questions his sense of self, his ability to relate to people, his own moral worth. Shift one dot in the appropriate Axis and choose one of the following Conditions (or create a new one with Storyteller approval): Guilty, Shaken, Obsession.
                            Success: The character has come through the breaking point intact. He might feel guilty or upset about what happened, but he can cope. Choose one of the following Conditions (or create a new one with Storyteller approval): Guilty, Shaken, Steadfast.
                            Exceptional Success: The character somehow manages not only to survive the breaking point, but to find meaning in it, to reaffirm his own self-worth, or to pass through fire and become tempered by it. The character takes a Beat and regains a point of Willpower.
                            I like everything in your alignment grid except for this. Specifically, I don't like that breaking points from different axes use the same list of conditions. I know it might be more to track, but perhaps constructing LAW, CHAOS, GOOD, and EVIL conditions would give the impact of a breaking point more potency.


                            Here are a few examples I whipped up on the spot, they may or may not be functional

                            Compulsive Truth (Law) -- Severe
                            Your persistence in upholding law has suffused you with the uncontrollable urge to tell the truth, repeatedly and constantly. You are driven to interject meaningless details into every conversation, even when doing so would be embarrassing or dangerous.
                            This condition is resolved when the character encounters another Law or Chaos breaking point. This condition gives beats when the compulsive truth places the character in a dangerous situation

                            Compulsive Lies (Chaos) -- Severe
                            Ditto, but for impulse mistruth. You are driven to altar facts whenever you speak, even when such lies would be immediately disproved and damage your credibility.

                            Meadow Convergence (Good) -- Severe
                            Whenever you are out in nature, friendly woodland creatures gather around you for no adequately explored reason.
                            This condition is resolved the next time the character encounters an EVIL breaking point, or if she kills one of the woodland creatures

                            Storm Clouds (Evil) -- Severe
                            Whenever you speak, the weather seems to react in a dramatic and unexpected manner. Cracks of thunder, gathering storm-clouds, that sort of thing.
                            This condition is resolved if the character becomes aware of the phenomenon and calls attention to it.

                            Scratchy Throat (Chaos, Evil) -- Mild
                            You have a cold. Or at least it sounds like you swallowed a frog (whether or not you actually did isn't important). This condition resolves after a few days.



                            ...I know, the examples are cheesy (I was trying to be funny), but you get the idea, yeah?
                            Then again, if you've already started using the system and the prefabricated conditions are working, feel free to ignore my warbles.



                            Also, I just want to take the opportunity to say how awesome everything you're doing here is. Keep up the great work!

                                                    Dragonborn (3.5)
                                                    • Max Stamina heightened by 1. Dexterity costs 1 more px, and cannot be more than 3 at character creation.
                                                    • Size: 5
                                                    • Dragon defense: +1 to Defense against dragons, Immune to dragon's frightful presence
                                                    • +1 to Intimidate rolls.
                                                    • Draconic Aspect: Choose one between Heart, Mind and Wings.

                                                    1. Heart:Once per scene, a dragonborn can use the damaging breath weapon of the dragon species he belongs to. The dragonborn can roll Stamina + Ranged and use the successes as damage. All damage is bashing but becomes lethal at Arete 3. A cone shaped breath weapon is 5 meters long, while a line shaped is 9 meters.
                                                    2. Mind: The dragonborn is immune to sleep magic and paralisis. She gains darkvision 10 m, low light vision. At Arete 3 the dragonborn's darkvision is 20 meters, and he gains blindsense (see True Dragons).
                                                    3. Wings: The dragonborn has wings. These wings are not yet able to let him fly, but are useful for jumping (+3 to jump), and allow the dragonborn to glide, negating falling damage. At Arete 3 the character can fly at its Speed.
                                                    • Languague: Common, Draconic.

                                                    Dragonborn (4th edition)
                                                    • Max Stamina or Presence heightened by 1. Dexterity costs 1 more px, and cannot be more than 3 at character creation.
                                                    • Size: 5
                                                    • +1 to Intimidate rolls
                                                    • Quick healer merit.
                                                    • Dragon breath: a dragonborn can use the damaging breath weapon of the dragon species he belongs to. The dragonborn can roll Stamina + Ranged and use the successes as damage. All damage is bashing but becomes lethal at Arete 3. A cone shaped breath weapon is 5 meters long, while a line shaped is 9 meters.
                                                    • Language:Common, Draconic.

                                                    Thri-kreen
                                                    • Max Dexterity or Resolve heightened by 1. Manipulation costs 1 more px, and cannot be more than 3 at character creation.
                                                    • Size: 5
                                                    • Four Arms: You have two extra arms. While you lack coordination to use them in combat, you can still use them to pick up items, like having two hands occupied and one with a torch. Once per turn you can sheathe/draw a weapon or retrieve/stow an item as a Reflexive action.
                                                    • +1 and 9 Again to all jumping rolls.
                                                    • Chitin: the thri-kreen has a starting Armor value of 1.
                                                    • Natural weapons: thri-kreen's bite and claws deal 0L. Bite can deliver a poison [Injection, Toxicity = Consitution, 1 bashing damage to Dexterity]

                                                    Psychic Thri-kreen (• or ••)
                                                    Prerequisite: Thri-kreen
                                                    Thri-kreen are naturally psionics. These capabilities can be dispelled as any spell, because they are magical in nature.
