    Many splats come with a way to avoid Aging to a degree. Changelings, Werewolves and Beasts with high Supernatural Tolerance are longer lived. By all means, have Magneto be an Elemental Changeling or the like (albeit that does clash abit with the 'Giving up your Ego' Fluff).

    I like Daredevil as an Eshmaki Nemesis (who obviously has Relentless Hunter to keep up with this ridiculous Lifestyle, and Mimir's Wisdom to tell when people lie. Maybe something like Shadowed Soul, too.), but I also like to think of Kingpin as an Anakim Tyrant (with Limb from Limb reflecting his brutal blows and Alien Allure to explain his charisma). You could even make Power Man into an Anakim reluctant to feed his Hunger of Punishment (not the best fit, but workable, with Cyclopean Strength and Unbreakable, possibly Relentless Hunter). Hell, even The Immortal Iron Fist could be fluffed into being a Beast (Makara Nemesis, punishing all those trying to enter K'un Lun) who does not adhere to his Feeding Preference, a Dragon for a Horror and Limb from Limb Satiety Expenditure being his famous glowing fist, as well as Titanic Blow at the end of Season one. But then, I am biased like that.
    I like Spiderman as a Skitterskulk.
    Magik is obviously a Mastigos with high Space and some Mind, maybe some Spirits.
    I would like to see Collossus, but what would he be?

    There was a X-Men Microtemplate in the works, to represent stuff like the Wolverine.

    Any ideas for Uratha or Sin-Eaters?

                            Kraven could be either a Hero or a Huntsman, Venom could be obviously a Deviant, but how about going for Demon? Would change some things about the character(s), sure, but hey, It's a What if...? after all. Most of the Characters will experience a slight change in tone at least.
                            Here are some of my takes on it:


                            Galactus is a Rank 9 Spirit. Let's go with Idigam?
                            Dr. Doom is a Mage with high Forces and Matter (and high Science, so you might alternatively go Genius), Obrimos imo.
                            Punisher could be many things: A Hunter, or even a Slasher. Might go Beast, but I wouldn't recommend it. I like him as a Slasher, actually.
                            Collossus could be a Beast or Deviant. I prefer Beast, which works well if seeing his Metallic mode as spending Satiety on Unbreakable. Deviant ain't out yet, after all ^^"
                            Antman could be a Genius? Otherwise a Moros with high Life and Matter?
                            Black Panther is a Hunter in my Books, unique Conspiracy. His superior power comes from training, but also his suit and some sort of (let's call it what it is) coping. Making use of Elixirs made from herbs, so kinda like an Ascending Ones, and having special Relics (Suit, Claws etc.) like Aegis Kai Doru?
                            Ultron is an Unfleshed Promethean, likely a Centimanus. ALthough Metalfleshed Elemental or Metalfleshed Fairest might work, too.
                            Black Cat is an Eshmaki Collector, which is why she always returns to her nefarious ways.
                            Loki is a Demon, what with his several different incarnations, illusions, fighting the boss deity, trying to carve out a kingdom for himself, and preferance for manipulation over brute force.
                            Scarlet Witch is a Mage, as is Storm. Acanthus with high Prime and Obrimos with Forces and Matter, respectively? Storm might also work as a Beast (Ugallu Nemesis with Storm-Lashed, obviously. Also Wings of the Raptor - She'd be spending a lot of Satiety all the time, though. Mage fits way better)
                            The Thing is a Tammuz Promethean.
                            Mystique is a Mirrorskin Darkling.
                            I like Wolverine and Sabertooth as Werewolves. Hulk could be refluffed into a Werewolf, though I like him as an Anakim who merges with his Horror whenever it get's ravenous ^^"
                            Johnny Storm is a Fireheart Fairest Changeling, and Groot is a Gargantuan Woodblood Elemental =P By this logic, Rocket Racoon could well be a Maker/Smith Beast Changeling with the Small Frame Merit (Although Wizened with an animalistic Kith like Hunterheart might fit too, on second thought. Prefer the first option, though.)
                            Drax the Destroyer could be a Beast or an Ogre (prefering the Ogre here), Gamorra is... some sort of Changeling imo, (let's go with Bloodbrute Render Darkling, seems to reflect her past and current abilities well enough) and Star-Lord is a micro-template human Hunter? (No seriously, what is Star Lord in ChroD?)
                            That would make the Guardians of the Galaxy a mixed troupe of mostly changelings. They fight Thanos and his people a lot, sooo... Thanos the Keeper, Ronin and company Huntsmen?
                            Daimon Hellstorm is a Lucifuge Hunter.

                            What's the Silver Surfer?

                                                            Sebastian Shaw is an Obrimos Mage going at it on his own.
