                                                Vampires could be very effective antagonists. They are known for treating people as resources and many empower servants to act in their stead. While established Vampires tend to avoid what they don't understand, Ordo Dracul and certain outliers could take a keen interest in the Deviants. They could even create them. The Ghouls are of particular note since they are parallel to Deviants in many respects but also markedly different. Ghoul-Regnant relationship is far more personal than what most Deviants get and the Vinculum denies Ghouls even their free will - something Deviants would prize over all else. This makes room for sympathy. Still, finding a Vampire and Ghoul in an affectionate or even respectful relationship might defy Deviants expectations, with unpredictable results. Deviants who care enough to help Ghouls free themselves could fail spectacularly, due to not knowing about the Vinculum, rapid aging or Vitae addiction. Deviants who don't care have living reminders of how much more they can lose.

                                                Deviants could also find themselves supporting fledgling Vampires in freeing themselves from their tyrranical Elders. Regardless of whether it began with pleas for sympathy, blackmail, bribery or mind control, such relationship could turn rotten quickly. How many people would the Vampire throw under the bus to free themselves? More importantly, how many people would the characters? Also, who says freeing a Vampire is a good thing? Characters who would succeed are in for a bitter lesson about the Pyrrhic victories and cycles of victimisation.

                                                Vampires need not even be the masterminds. Certain Mortal groups could simply use them as servants or resources to create new Deviants (admittedly, it works for many splats). All it takes is a pill that makes one resistant to Vinculum, and the dynamics change entirely, with free-willed Ghouls still needing their fix from somewhere. Also, Kindred and other Organisations suddenly becoming aware of each other will end up disastrous for someone. While the most reasonable thing would be to avoid each other, entires sagas have begun with people being unreasonable.
