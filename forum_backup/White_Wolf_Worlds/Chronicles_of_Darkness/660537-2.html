                                        It was at this moment of anguish and despair that Ceno appeared to her in dreams, he showed her dark miracles, and she became greedy for them.

                                                    The new kiths look so frikkin' cool. Can't believe there are even more to come out! (75% done you say?)

                                                    Some minor nitpicks tho'

                                                    The bloodbrute concept is really cool, but two of the blessings sort of... work against each other? You get a bonus to unarmed attacks and improvised weapons, but I always figured that you'd use an improvised weapon to avoid using an unarmed attack... and if you are good at unarmed attacks, you'd rather do them (with lethal damage) than using an improvised weapon. Most improvised weapons would do bashing damage after all, I'm guessing?
                                                    Not sure if it needs change or replacement but it just feels a bit redundant. Maybe 1/1 armor instead of the improvised weapons to show how they learned to shake off blows in the fighting pits or something.

                                                    Secondly, the author seems significantly weaker than most other kiths. The ability to read any language is pretty frikkin' cool (like really cool!), but getting one success on expression rolls concerning writing is not so cool. I'm aware is not about the perfect balance or anything, but still... Maybe turn writing with expression into a rote action or give them 8-again or something a bit more powerful.

                                                    Imagine being an Artist kith trying to write that poem, getting +3 (if spending glamor) and only needing 3 success for a critical success. Not to mention the artist can do it with EVERYTHING, not just writing. It can be making (and performing) a song, smithing a two-handed sword and building your own car. All with the same kith. Comparing the two really makes Author seem like the shorter end of the stick. Again, I know it is not about making it the perfect balanced game, but the difference in strength is pretty big right there!

                                                    As a side-note, I frikkin' love the trolls, being from Sweden I picture trolls more like cunning tricksters rather than the dumb brutes of DnD. <3
