            Well here is my contribution to the community, it is just an idea; I'm OPEN (pun intended) to suggestions:

            Pneumatofagoi ("Πνευματοφάγοι", pronounced "pnevmatofági", Greek for "Spirit-Eaters")
            Ruling Arcana: Spirit
            Optional: Death
            Path: Moros
            Order: Free Counsil (former Nameless)
            Yantra: acts dedicated to satisfaction or intimidation of spirits (+1), ephemera (+1), target spirit's bane (+2)
            Oblations: Perform a favor for a spirit, eat a spirit, allow spirit to possess you willingly, spent some time into the Shadow

            Initiation: Somehow feast from a spirit. Either consume ephemera, a materialized part or find a way to process essense.

            Legacy History & Principle:
            Members of the Legacy proudly claim that it was created near the end of the Golden Age of Athens (circa 410 BC) by awakened Sophists (Teachers and Sages) that rejected Platotic (even maybe Atlantean) cosmology and retoric. Both Sleeper and Awakened lore suggests that Sophists were heavily critisised by prominent figures, like Plato and Aristophanis, as glorified hairsplitting wordsmiths with malevolent intentions.

            While plato-inspired mages leaned towards following a path through the inner self -suggesting astral travels, goetia and finally accention-, the pneumatofagoi believed that they should remain fettered in the fallen world, understanding and becoming one with the world around them. They considered "The Silver Ladder of Accention" either a fool's Utopia or a very difficult task at hand. Supernal magic had its limits and was easily corrupted by the mortal world. No, they needed something else...

            Past Alexander's Time, while the proto-Diamond maped the basis of Astral realms, Pneumatofagoi were experimenting across the Gauntlet. They were facinated by the harmony of the spirit ecosystem and by their paradox-free powers. Driven by their lust to free themselves from the limits of their mortal magick, they shaped their very souls into spirits through the most efficient (and natural to their eyes) way; by becoming part of the ecosystem, devouring spirits.

            Pneumatofagoi that trap two or three spirits into themselves are decent company. But there are always people that want to test their limits. Present Members of the legacy are still treated with suspicion since part of the Legacy is hidding its own nature. Some extremists go as far to say that the legacy openly suggests accepting the lie (namely the shadow) and leave the supernal to the Exarchs, making it a thorn into the newly prestigious image of the Free Counsil as Enemies of the lie. Some Consilii have declared it Left-Handed or Weapon of the Enemy but no Convocation has condemned it thus far.

            As a old Hierarch of Athens put it "You want Spirits to start talking? If you don't have a werewolf clan around, a Pneumatofagos is your next buddy on the list"

            Attainments:
            Sapor Corporis (1st, Know Spirit, instant +1R, sympathetic +1R +1Mana, no optional):
            As an instant action and by spending one Mana, you can eat a part of spiritual residue (be it leaked essense, ephemera, materialised part, etc) and one piece of knowledge per Spirit dots you possess about the specific spirit as per Know Spirit. Name, Rank, list of Manifestations, List of Numina, list of Influences and their strength, Ban, Bane.
            Pneumatopagida (2nd, Opener of the Way, Advance Duration +1R, optional Death Soul Jar):
            Literaly meaning "Spirit-trap", by spenting one scene and taking an action relevant to the Resonant Condition, you can modify it into an Open one for one scene per Spirit dots. For example this may be done by killing something for a murder Resonant Condition or kiss someone for love/lust Resonant one. Remember that the subject might be virtualy anything, a place, a person, an item, etc. Optional (Death 2): By adding banning concepts (circle of salt, runes, etc) into the action, the subject of the Open Condition becomes a trap. The Open Subject functions as a "Soul Jar" but for spirits. The spirit can't leave the subect, possess others outside boundary (if any), or directly target the subject (but influencing others, or pushing other objects is fair game).
            Signa Invisibilia (3rd, Supress Aura, passive): You can become spiritualy invisible. You can hide any spirit or spirit-related effect on yourself from an other spirit or the Spirit arcanum including your Spirit Rank and notoriety in the spirit world. Supernatural powers to detect your spirit-stuff or to identify you as Spirit-Eater trigger a Clash of Wills. By spending one mana you can also hide one target (spirit or effect not in yourself) for one scene.
            Haunted Body (4th, Craft Fetish): Usually applied on a defeated spirit, instead of allowing it to hybernate back into the shadow, you force it inside your body creating as per Craft Fetish, gaining one influence dot or Numina per Spirit dot for up to one week.You can prolong the duration by eating large pieces of spirit corpus with your first attainment for 1 day per corpus consumed. You can have multiple hybernating spirits stiched into yourself but you can only have dots and numina in your "active" arsenal as your Spirit dots. You can shuffle between them by spending one action and one Mana or Essence as you change your spirit-stuff. While in this state the action becomes visible despite the invisiblility granted by the 3rd Attainment. Also, remember that Spirits can wake and break free without other powers banning them.
            Spirit-Whole (5th, No Existing Spell): By spending one Mana you can substitute all your physical attributes with a fettered spirit's Attributes (Power for Strength, Finesse for Dexterity, Resistance for Stamina), size and you can use all its powers with the spirit's stats. Doing so wakes the spirit that manifests superimposed in your body, creating something that resebles a Ridden, a spirit-human hybrid. You must roll a clash of wills each turn to remain in control. Failure means the spirit can treat you as possessed for this scene. Optional (Death): Alternatively, You can devour one of the spirits you fettered into yourself -killing it instantly- gaining powers from that spirit as with previous attainment but fettered with Indefinite Duration. These powers are excluded from the previous attainment restrictions. You can have up 5 Indefinite fettered Powers in your arsenal (in addition to the previous attainment). By activating the Devour-Attainment again you can permanently remove (painfuly riping spirit parts apart from you, or the spirit parts forcibly get out) granted powers for new ones respecting the attainments limit.

                    This is the adaptation of the Forge Masters my Storyteller and I cooked up and we would like your opinion on it. I copied most of the structure from Shadowjim12’s post since it was really well worked out, thanks for that.

                    Forge masters (Powersmiths)
                    Ruling Arcana: Prime
                    Optional: Matter
                    Path: Moros
                    Order: Free Council

                    Initiation, History, Yantra’s and Oblations are all similar to the original Legacy since that was pretty much as good as it was going to get. But for those of you unfamiliar with the Legacy: Powersmiths are item crafters and are especially known for working with Thaumium. They strongly favour working with metal, which is also the material they use for their Grimoires.

                    With regard to the Duration of all the Attainments we planned to follow the scheme laid out in the original Legacy. There a Forge master had to spend a turn working on an object he wanted to apply an Attainment on to make it last for an entire scene. If he spent an entire scene working on an object then the Attainment will last a day. Otherwise we would have it last as many rounds as the lowest Arcanum of Matter and Prime.

                    Attainments:
                    Metal Grimoire (1st, Scribe Grimoire):
                    The Forge masters are known for working wonders with metals and this first Attainment is evidence of that. It is far easier to hide a Grimoire as a ring or bracelet than a book. The size of the Grimoire determines the number of Rotes that can be stored on a linear basis (Size 1 = 1 Rote, Size 2 = 2 Rotes). Optional: Matter, The Powersmith´s Matter dots determine the maximum dot rating of the Rotes stored.
                    Upgrade (2nd, Find the balance and As above so below):
                    With even the slightest adjustment tools can be made so much better, the affected object gains the 9-again quality both for its intended purpose as a tool and for its symbolic purpose as a Yantra. Optional: By spending 1 Mana during the activation of the Attainment one of its purposes becomes further improved and instead gains the 8-again quality. By spending 2 Mana both of its purposes are so reinforced.
                    True tools (3rd, Ephemeral enchantment, Shrink and grow):
                    Works like Ephemeral enchantment. Optional: Adjust the size category of the enchanted object up or down up to the Mage’s dots in Matter. (Alternative suggestions are very welcome).
                    In the forge of its making (4th, Nigredo and albedo/Annihilate matter):
                    Many are the legends about items that can only be destroyed on the very anvil that was used to create them (does a specific Ring come to mind?). This Attainment does exactly that: You can destroy objects utterly. This also applies to Artifacts, but with consequences. When using the Attainment on an Artifact it creates a backlash and the Forge Master will take Lethal damage equal to the Merit rating or Equipment bonus of the Artifact. Optional: By spending Mana during the destruction of an Artifact the Mage can reduce the damage of the backlash on a 1 for 1 basis.
                    From memory (5th, Ex Nihilo):
                    At the pinnacle of reforging his soul the Forge Master’s soul has become a forge itself and can use it as such. The Forge Master can at any moment reproduce any item he has worked on. Optional: Upon creating an object, immediately apply one of the other Attainments to it.

                    We know that the next book (Signs of Power I believe?) will finally give us a lot more information on Perfected metals and we will likely incorporate those in the Legacy. For now we are going to go with this, but we would really appreciate any constructive feedback you could give us. Suggestions for different Attainments are very welcome since we mostly stuck to known spells instead of trying to think up unusual Attainments.

It never had a mana cost associated with it, so it should not fall under the purview of the Mana break paragraph.
                            Now I would like your opinion on what you think would be the best solution to this: 1. Remove the option completely, limiting the Attainment to the 9-again quality, 2. Keep the current solution, 3. Keep the mana cost, but limit the possible 8-again quality to either function, 4. Something else.

Using Annihilate Matter can destroy Artifacts and is about the only way I have been able to find to so, but it does indeed cost a Reach and a point of mana to use that option. That backlash can be mitigated by spending a ton of mana, which is again not part of the original spell.

I also wanted to include the aspect of mystical smithing that deals with how to get rid of dangerous items: Taking it back to the forge in which is was made.

                            Your suggestion for Imbueing items sounds very interesting, but could you elaborate a bit more? What I think you are trying to say is this: a Powersmith could be able to Imbue items with spells that he has in a metal Grimoire (which he uses during the process) without it costing him spell control slots.                                             Since the initiation requirements for a legacy's first attainment require the ruling arcanum at 2-dots. Does that mean that both the first and second attainments can be built with one-dot spells with +2 reach?                                             Legacy creation was my favorite part of 1st Ed and I was pretty salty when I saw how much weaker 2nd Ed legacies seemed to be. But it's not that bad. 1st Ed legacies can be converted to 2nd Ed by increasing the attainments two levels. so 1st, 2nd, and 3rd attainments become 3rd, 4th, and 5th attainments. Assuming the spells used didn't change practices in the edition change, the functionality should be mostly the same. The converted attainments only have their Gnosis and Arcana requirements increased by one point, which isn't bad.

                                            1st Ed legacies typically either had active powers with scene long duration or passive powers with indefinite duration, and almost never had attainments based on attack or healing spells. This can be kept in 2nd Ed by taking the Instant Action and Advanced Duration reaches for active powers and Advanced Duration and Indefinite Duration for passive powers. Some legacies will have problems with spells being limited to touch range, requiring another reach for sensory range.

                                            Reach: Instant Action, Advanced Duration

                                            Optional: Matter 4
                                            Reach: Instant Action, Advanced Duration (additional spell factors assigned to duration)

His conjurations can be as complex as simple mechanical items, including weapons and armor. He gains a number of points to spend equal to his dots in Prime to allocate to the item’s Durability, equipment bonus, and Mana pool. The item starts with a Durability and Mana pool of 1.

                                            The item’s Mana pool comes prefilled with Mana and one point is consumed each turn the item is used as part of a non-spellcasting dice roll. Once all of the Mana points have been spent the item dissolves. The mage can use Channel Mana to refill the Mana pool. When the item’s duration elapses it dissolves and all remaining Mana is lost.

                                            Conjured items may be further enhanced with Primal Tempering or imbued with spells.

                                            Optional: Matter 5
                                            Reach: Instant Action, Advanced Duration (additional spell factors assigned to duration)

If he spent an entire scene working on an object then the Attainment will last a day. Otherwise we would have it last as many rounds as the lowest Arcanum of Matter and Prime.

                                                Attainments:
                                                The Master’s gaze (1st, Supernal vision, Craftsman’s eye):
                                                Prerequisites: Prime 2, Crafts 2 and Science or Occult or Athletics at at least 2 dots.
                                                Reach: Instant casting.
                                                A Forge master can at will mimic the effects of the Supernal vision spell, allowing him to identify Imbued or Enhanced items and Artifacts with ease as well as their Supernal purpose. The Master’s gaze activates automatically when touching one of these types of items. Optional (Matter 1): The Powersmith also knows the intended non-Supernal function of any object he holds when the Master’s gaze is active.
                                                Metal Grimoire (2nd, Scribe Grimoire)
                                                Prerequisites: Prime 2, Crafts 3.
                                                Reach: Instant casting, Lasting option.
                                                The Forge masters are known for working wonders with metals and this second Attainment is evidence of that. It is far easier to hide a Grimoire as a ring or bracelet than a book. The size of the Grimoire together with the Forge Master’s mastery of Matter determines the dots worth of Rotes that can be stored: Size + Matter dots = Nr. dots worth of Rotes in the Metal Grimoire. Optional (Matter 2): By spending 1 Mana during the activation of the Attainment the Metal Grimoire can be made Lasting.
                                                The Master’s hand (3rd, As above so below, Alter balance):
                                                Prerequisites: Prime 3, Crafts 3, Science or Occult or Athletics increased to 3 dots or two of those at 2 dots.
                                                Reach: Instant casting, 8-again quality option.
                                                Use of the Master’s hand infuses a tool with supernal meaning, granting the 9-again quality to any spellcasting roll that incorporates it as a Yantra. Optional (Matter 3): The Mage can choose to include the tools original function as well, granting it the 9-again quality on rolls that involve its intended purpose as well. Optional 2: Either of the 9-again qualities can be upgraded to the 8-again quality instead.
                                                Primal tempering (4th, Ephemeral enchantment):
                                                Prerequisites: Prime 4, Crafts 4, Science or Occult or Athletics increased to 3 dots or two of those at 2 dots.
                                                Reach: Instant casting, Advanced Scale (use Matter to determine Scale).
                                                As an instant action the mage can imbue an item with power and make it solid to all Twilight entities. The attainment only applies to the item’s primary purpose. A ghost could type on a typewriter enhanced with Primal tempering but could not pick it up and use it as a bludgeoning weapon.
                                                Optional (Matter 4): Items enhanced with Primal Tempering also have their Durability increased by a number of points equal to the mage’s dots in Matter.
                                                Primal Forge (5th, Ex Nihilo):
                                                Prerequisites: Prime 5, Crafts 4, Science or Occult or Athletics increased to 4 dots or must attain three dots in a second Skill from the list of possible requirements, or two dots in a third Skill from the list.
                                                Reach: Instant casting.
                                                At the pinnacle of reforging his soul the Forge Master’s soul has become a forge itself and can use it as such. The Forge Master can at any moment reproduce any item he has worked on. Optional (Matter 5): Any spells that the Powersmith uses that target the created item count as one spell against his spell control slots.

                                                        Yes I have a reason for blending Matter and Prime so heavily. For me the Forge masters are all about blending Matter and Prime and I would expect that someone who takes the Legacy would do so as well. Besides that I think that more Legacies should blend their two main Arcana more, since for some effects it just makes more sense for the pre-Legacy Major Arcana to be the one governing an Attainment. That is the reason why I switched the format for the last Attainment.
                                                        It makes more sense to me that a legendary craftsman can instantly recreate anything they ever made and have loads of freedom to then improve on it than that he can recreate an inferior copy of something (although this can cover more complex constructs) and then have the option to reinforce said inferior copy. Then again, this is my personal opinion.

                                                        As for Matter governing the Scale on Primal Tempering, that is my interpretation of the effect of taking Advanced Scale as a Reach effect in the new Reach system. The rules for Legacies state that the Legacies main Arcana governs primary spell factors. Normally this would be Duration for Ephemeral enchantment and Potency would then be governed by Matter. However, since Duration is calculated based on the amount of time spent implementing an Attainment I felt that Prime should determine Potency, as it represents the firmness with which the tool is present across all layers of reality and Matter would then govern the Scale. Granted it can be argued that Prime should still cover the Scale in that case since Potency would here mostly be used to withstand dispellation and Attainments are immune to dispellation. I do not think it Matters that much in the end.
