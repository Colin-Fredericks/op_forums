    Embeds and Non-Demons

    11-21-2013, 10:58 PM
    If Embeds are representative of secret physical laws written into the very fabric of the universe, then isn't it feasible that non-demons could learn about them and how to use them?

      If mechanical tools are representative of physical laws written into the very fabric of the universe, then isn't it feasible that the animal kingdom could learn about them and how to use them?

      Well, they do. Monkeys narrow down sticks to dig ants out of anthills. Orangutans, presented with boats, learn how to paddle them around. Elephants use backscratchers. A man built a little go-kart for his pet bird. She steers it around his backyard with her beak.

      But I wouldn't expect a silverback to design a system of calculus or a chimpanzee to build a Kindle.

      It may be that the mechanical mind of demons has qualities that grants them greater facility with these secret laws, such that they can be called upon reliably and with relatively little outward manipulation. We are the animal kingdom, and our psychology does not work on this occult level of fine dexterity and design. Still, occult laws are occult laws, and nature finds a way. One could argue that psychic powers, sorcerous rituals or simple superstitions efficacious in the World of Darkness are signs of mortals manipulating the same occult physics that enable a demon's Embeds, with a grosser, less facile aspect, working with what we can find in our environment, testing it, and exploring the edges of what causes and effect we can understand intuitively.

                                  Possibly. Mostly, no.
