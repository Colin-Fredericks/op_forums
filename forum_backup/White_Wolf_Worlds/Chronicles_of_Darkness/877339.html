    The Eras of Chronicles of Darkness (Dark Eras and others)

    04-29-2016, 01:02 AM
    This project is borne out of several different desires, but I thought it would be neat to compile all of the historical eras that we know about in one place so that we can see the relationships between them in both location and space. I mean see literally too, since I've been building timelines for them in my massive world history project, and since I can actually post easy to view images online if I restrict the timeline in temporally and geographically.

    Summer 2024 Update!

    Since the new forum software is still somewhat allergic both editting posts and post with a lot of links, I'm going to migrate the timeline off the forums to cut down on the number of times the mods have to re-approve this post. I'm also shifting the entirely of the old timeline over into Aeon Timeline 3, which is a massive amount of work. Best I can tell, I've only gotten about 1.5% of it moved so far. Its gonna take a while. If you want to have a say in what I cover next and hear some lengthier ramblings, there are both polls and commentary for my Chronicler patrons (link is in the signature).

    Going forward, all new versions of all timelines will be found in this dropbox folder.

    Everything below this is the original post (and edits) , preserved so the rest of the thread makes sense.

    Each image is in a spoiler to keep it from messing with people's screens. I'm posting the link to the original sources too, which are on my DeviantArt Page. I've marked the actual eras with a white background on the timeline while contextual dates both before and after the era are on a grey background. These are all very much works in progress, so if you see something, let me know and I will update the timeline. I'm not posting everything at once, but I will get around to it.

    EDIT: Since there is a limit to the number of images you can have in one post, I've included the first four timelines here and the rest have been moved to separate posts at the bottom of the first page and the top of the second. I've kept all of the eras names, dates, and references in the first post to make comparisons handy.

    Another edit, years later: Because I'm also a geography nerd, I've also made a map of all the locations in CofD, which you can find here to cross-reference with their histories.

          Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
          How did you determine the start of the Ur-Sag's reign?
          Complete and total guesswork. The most that I could go off of was the fact that the Ur-Sag fought with King Gudea close to the end of his reign. I went for 12 years since a) it wasn't divisible by 5, b) that seemed liked enough time for the Ur-Sag to get complacent as the gods of Bau, and c) that gives some time time to play with for creating self-contained chronicles. By my own admission, I could be completely wrong on those dates since that era is the one we have the least info on by far.
