    Looking for a Legacy about unlocking doors, gates and personal transformation

    09-21-2026, 10:11 AM
    So, my character Key, a biracial transwoman from New Orleans, is an Adamantine Arrow Mastigos. She signed her name to the Watchtower of Burning Iron after defeating her opiate addiction and claiming the prize. She is still recovering, and she returned from the Mastigos Watchtower with an uncharacteristic Golden Key (curious!).

    She just hit Gnosis 3, and I am now considering Legacies. Having Weaponry 3 with a specialty in Swords, the obvious choice for a Legacy is Brotherhood of the Demon Wind (although I will be calling it the Demon Wind school if I do join), but I really like the idea of keys, doors, and how crossing liminal spaces changes and transforms those who go through, also playing with the ideas of initiation and transformation. I've been looking at the various fan written Legacies. While the Reality Stalkers and House of the Ariadne also appeal, a legacy about travelers, gates and personal spiritual transformations and overcoming challenges appeals to Key's Adamantine Arrow heritage.

    Any anyone help a girl out? (irl, I am Cis :P)

                  How about something like this ? Fate primary, Space optional, themed on traveling the paths of synchronicity and that there is no distinction between physically and metaphysically making your way in the world.

                  Fate 1
                  A Compelling of Fate that nudges events to ensure the subject is at the right place at the right time, replicating Green Light, Red Light. Reach is assigned to advanced duration.

                  Space 1
                  Interconnections with Reach assigned to advanced duration and conjunctional Space 1 allowing them to understand the emotional character of sympathetic connections in broad terms. It allows them to perceive connections and paths forward others don't.

                  Fate 2
                  Lucky Number with Reach assigned to instant use.

                  Space 2
                  Reading the Outmost Eddies with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration. With conjunctional Space 1, the right threads of fate are nudged closer, allowing results to be obtained in an hour.

                  Fate 3
                  Serendipity with Reach assigned to instant use and using any skill.

                  Space 3
                  Break Boundary with Reach assigned to instant use and passing through narrow paths.

                  Fate 4
                  Strings of Fate with Reach assigned to advanced duration. With conjunctional Space 3, you can find the right thread of fate to get results in an hour.

                  Space 4
                  Teleportation with Reach assigned to instant use.

                  Fate 5
                  Forge Destiny with Reach assigned to advanced duration. Since you can give yourself Aspirations, Obsessions and Destiny's without Dooms, it serves for both the symbolism of charting your course in life and changing who you are for the better.

                  Space 5
                  Co-Location with Reach assigned to sympathetic range, indefinite duration and advanced scale. A point of mana pays for Sympathetic Range, Everywhere and indefinite duration. With conjuctional Fate 2, a Key can be applied to it. Creates your own permanent, private travel network.

                          Happy to help. One thing that helps me when creating Legacies is to make one phrase, with no commas or pauses, that defines your Legacy. Whenever creating a new section (Attainments, yatras, oblations, organization, etc), you always go back to it. If it matches the design phrase, you keep it, if it doesn't, you rework it. Coming up with the phrase helps distill your ideas and using it keeps you on theme. A few examples from my own Legacies:

                          Azure Cranes: "Medical care available anytime and anywhere"
                          Anima Meccanica: "Inventing a better tomorrow today"
                          Crystal Navigators: "Navigating our choices"

                          In the case of the one posted above, it was "Making a path through the world". Hope that proves helpful.
