    What would a typical game of Genius look like in 2ED? The sample Chronicles from the book are all over the place. In the official gamelines, you have mechanics which encourage a certain type of gameplay. Arcane Experiences motivate you to investigate Mysteries, Cover Experiences are earned when you make an effort to live according to your human identity, and so on. How would you earn Wondrous Experiences, and what type of gameplay are they meant to encourage?

    Also, I would rather let Geniuses keep using Virtue and Vice, and have the Magnum Opus and Delusion work as special Aspirations.

        Vampire:
        Blood Bond and Ghouling encourages players to addict people to their blood and gain power from it. Additionally, each Clan has Disciplines that encourage them to play a certain kind of vampire, like Ventrue have Dominate, and so on. This encourages players to act like power-hungry vampires.

        This makes the basic game of Vampire easy to figure out; it's about trying to get and maintain power.

        Werewolf:
        Has extra mechanics for tracking someone by scent, hunting, and gives additional bonuses for pack formation. Additionally, maintaining loci health/altering loci is a thing, though it's not entirely mechanized. This encourages players to make a pack, hunt and control territory.

        This makes the base game for werewolf easy to figure out; you hunt and control territory.

        Mage:
        You get xp from finding out weird supernatural stuff, and have an additional Aspiration related to the supernatural. It also has an additional system for discovering more about weird supernatural stuff, and a larger, more dynamic system for spellcasting. This encourages players to run around trying to solve mysteries.

        This makes the base game of Mage easy to figure out; you run around solving mysteries and acting on obsessions.

        How does Genius encourage players to act like mad scientists? Does this make the base game easy to figure out? What is the base game, ie, if I'm ST'ing Genius, what's the first basic idea that pops into my head when it comes time to design a campaign, like politics for Vampire, control a territory and hunt shit for Werewolf, and investigate supernatural stuff for Mage?

                        Originally posted by notanautomaton View Post
                        XP costs and... I'm not sure what to call them. Fractions? Paradigms? Properties? Fields of Study?
                        I've been calling them "Postulates," with "Fields" being the groups the Postulates are placed in. For example, a Field of Parachronics concerned with temporal stuff, that might include a Time Travel Postulate (Skafoi), a Time Control Postulate (Epikrato), a Time Window Postulate (Apokalypsi), etc. The Fields provide the subject being dealt with, and the Axioms act as the lens through which the subject is viewed, giving the Postulate as the resulting application.

                        Basically, make xp costs rather expensive for the high-tier stuff. Then you can gain additional things like 'cyrogenics' or 'weather control,' or whatever Then whenever you build something that takes advantage of that a Field, the xp is reduced.
                        Eh...I'm actually not a huge fan of Wonders costing XP directly anymore.

                        So then a Genius can build tech that's a little better than normal pretty easily, but when they want FTL or a death ray that can destroy a city, they have to pay through the nose. So either you can have a large number of weak Wonders, one great big wonder, or something in between, unless you're taking advantage of your Field, in which case you get the ability to have a greater number of devices.
                        Well, in the new structure, Axiomata work more like a Werewolf's Renown than like a Mage's Arcana. Raising an Axiom's rating makes your maximum effects more potent, but the actual capabilities of Wonders you make are all coming from the Postulates you know.

                        Also keep in mind that that one of the things that I have in mind is making the Havoc rules much harsher, so you can loose your great big Wonder more easily, especially if its effects can be noticed as abnormal by muggles.
                        Yeah, more outlandish Wonders would be more prone to Havoc. Especially if you're Renegade or you decide to cheap out on the components.

                        Additionally, I'm thinking about changing it so that Funding is the fuel stat rather than Mania, so that your great big Wonder is expensive and hurts to lose.
                        No, I don't like that. One of the biggest annoyances in Genius (in my opinion) is the presence of a concrete concern of funding. I'd rather make the building of the Wonder an entirely (or almost entirely) roleplaying-based concern. You aren't spending 5 points of Money, you're knocking over material science labs for monoatomic filament wire and hunting the denizens of the Hidden Neanderthal Valley Bardo for their brains.
