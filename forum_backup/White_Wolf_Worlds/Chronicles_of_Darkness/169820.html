    Share your Compact/ Conspiracy ideas!

    06-15-2014, 12:07 PM
    Well, it was a matter of time before a thread like that would rise again, right?

    On my side, I've wrote Raziel Ltd (an Israeli weapon company), the Ebony Fangs (an ancient death cult which hunts those who escape death) and I'm currently working on the Pythian Oracles (slashers who kill for a better future). Plus, there are many ideas in here, which some I like more and some less. Finally, I lately thought about another "werewolf based" conspiracy:

    (temp name)- The Broken Diamond: a group of hunters which has found a number of crystal- like objects, which grant them spiritual powers when they transplant a part of that crystal into their flesh. The problem with those powers if that they disfigure the flesh of those who get the shards and turn them more bestial, and so they hunt after the werewolves in order to steal from them their secrets of flesh crafting. They don't know that those crystals are actually solidified form of the shards of the different Hosts, and that by using them they become more "host like" by themselves, with the only thing which keeps them "human" is the "werewolf serum".

    P.S- just for convince, give each such group a temporary name if it is not developed enough. You can do a lot with a temporary name :P

    so what are your ideas? (and feel free to re-post old writeups of you =r old favorites!)

              Pretty much every Hunter group, cannon or fannon, falls into one of three modes:

              1) We come from this background, and want to protect people from this background. (E.G. The Union, Task Force: VALKARIE, Ascending Ones)
              2) We dislike and kill a certain kind of monster (E.G. MM, The Long Night, Division VI)
              3) We can profit from hunting and killing a certain kind of monster (Cheiron, LDI, Utopia Now)

              With around 30 different groups; I'm starting to get board of it and looking for other angles; in fact looking at my work, it's been leaning in that direction for a while.


              A Compact of therapist/actors who hire out to monster's unique needs - how much cash will an elder vampire pay to talk to a normal non-judging human about their lives in complete confidence that nothing can be used against them? Or what strange goblin wears could a Changeling pay for someone who can convincingly pretend to be their old family? Their Endowment comes from a True Fae, and guarantees their safety; and the safety of the secrets they're told.


              A group of exiles from, (maybe Lyonesse, Ys or Thuvaraiyam Pathi? There's no shortage of lost lands) who's only goal is to bring back their home. They're constantly reforging links between the far flung cells and tearing them down as groups go into hiding; or assimilate into their local culture. They have powerful Fetishes containing the Spirits of their homeland (River spirits, Hill spirits, etc), and their Endowment is based on the ability to remotely channel the Fetishes your Cell has. The Elemental Masteries could be used as inspiration here. The problem is that those powerful Fetishes are tempting to others.


              A group of honest to god knight errants who just go questing around doing good deeds. Maybe hunting monsters, but maybe just helping out at a natural disaster. Most people know them as a weird subculture. Their Endowment is Talecrafting, but they can only use Chivalric Romance troupes.

                      Close. I'm thinking more Arthurian than Slavic. I'm British, sue me

                      Mirrors of existing groups, I would do it once - because to me "amusing mirror of an existing group" is a niche that hasn't been filled. But you like government sanctioned groups, well don't limit your imagination: TF:V are military, VASCU are law enforcement, MI18 are spies, The Lord Stewards are civil servants. You get my drift?

                      I always liked the idea of the Chinese government hunters being economists, with a mathematical Endowment to predict the future. They make five year plans, and occasionally take care of any unpredictable elements in society. KSI-29, I always saw them as secret police, spies and secret police are similar in many regards but the differences are damn important. Now if Glamourweaver would write them, I'd find out if I'm right.


                      Same with corporations. How many groups do you need that cut monsters up for parts? Crack open a history book or a anti-capitalist newspaper. We have Cherion cutting monsters up, but then there's the Brotherhood Prizrakov - slave-driving miners; with the Underworld instead of the Third World. The differences are big enough to make "corporation" almost useless as a descriptive term, and that's the way it ought to be

                      So we have the vivisectionists, and the slave-drivers. Who else is out there, what other possibilities exist. How about an astral marketing firm? (BTW, every now and again the question of Princess focused hunters comes up. This would be a great example of one, and no need for Hunter or Princesses to actually try to hurt each other.)

                              I have some more:

                              - Helldivers. A Compact of explores and thrill-seekers always looking to find way enter otherwordly realms, only to turn back and share their experiences, possibly with some trophies to brag about.                               - A group based on the Benandanti

                              - The "Keepers of the Locker" (I know, temp name :P) a seafaring Conspiracy that is in contact with a being that refers to itself as Davy Jones (as in, the real one). Davy Jones asks them to find beings, objects and supernatural monsters which, according to him, belong to his locker and to "send them back". In exchange, he promises the Hunters treasures and mystical objects from the depths. Sure, they are only borrowed and some they he will ask them back, but still...

                              - A Compact of thieves, and a very old one. They consider the thieves of fairy tales and myths as their illustrious predecessors, and compete with each other to steal riches and magical objects from the supernaturals. Then they throw those in the bunch or sell them because where's the fun of stealing if you use magic to cheat?

                                          Here are my Guardians of the Vineyard posted on the V:tR forum:

                                          A vile creature poisoned the ancient and sacred vineyards of Dionysus, for She wished to claim the gardens as her own and sustain her dark children on the sweet nectar that was the wine-God's greatest gift to Humanity. Dionysus petitioned to Zeus for help against the forces of darkness. The Olympian chose to ignore his pleas, for Echidna was the daughter of Tartarus -warden of imprisoned Titans. Only when the Mother, her mate Ixion-Typhon and their blasphemous offspring assaulted Olympus and stole immortality, did the Father of Gods and Mortals take action. Ixion-Typhon was bound to a fiery wheel that would never cease spinning, and was imprisoned deep within a volcano. The Mother and her children were allowed to flee Zeus' terrible wrath, to challenge the worth of all future great warriors. We are those Heroes and Heroines. We still seek out the Dark Mother's wretched children, driving them out and reclaiming the sacred vineyards. We will cut their heads off as they feed from the sweet and holy wine of Dionysus, that is the blood of Humanity.
