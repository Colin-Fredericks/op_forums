    Geist as inspiration?

    07-31-2018, 01:54 PM
    Less interested in direct mage/geist crossover, but it seems like Geist has a bunch of things going on that might interest Mages, from mechanics about ghosts to the way the Underworld works, and I thought I'd open up a place for discussing that for anyone interested.

    One of the things that's come to my attention is that there are ghosts who deal with Memories. They can be created, traded and destroyed. With Death, you can probably interact with these directly or indirectly, and it made me think they might work as a good mechanism for describing some of how Logophages work in 2e since they're not ripping things from people's heads with Mind anymore.
