    One word: spirits. Rules for them are provided in the Promethean core book; they’re very important to the Ulgans; and aside from Spiritus Transmutations and the tools available to everyone, the only tool Prometheans have for dealing with them is the Ulgan Bestowment. If you want to use Argentum without reference to crossovers, you can focus on spirits. Indeed, it’s mentioned that Ulgans are the most likely to pursue this Refinement. As for the Spiritus Transmutations, there are only a handful that aren’t useful when the Mystic focuses his attention on spirits: Mask/Cloak/Sense of [type], and Glimpse of Mortal Memory (that last one only because spirits were never human, and thus have no memories of being human).

    I also hope that the second edition of the World of Darkness core book will include something akin to Mortal Remains’ monster creation rules, with a focus on creating monsters that aren’t covered by other gamelines. If this happens, then Argentum becomes even more viable, since it will drastically increase the scope of non-Pyros supernatural creatures that the Storyteller will easily be able to bring to bear without resorting to crossover.

    There’s also a lot of talk in the Argentum writeup about how mortals who interact with the supernatural tend to come back a bit worse for the wear, mentally; I’d like to see something more done with this, though I’m not quiite sure what. Maybe Disquietus rather than Sensoruim should be associated with Argentum, and tweaked to deal more with supernaturally induced madness in general rather than with the effects of Disquiet in particular?

    Argentum doesn’t need an overhaul; just some refocusing to make its non-crossover uses more prominent, with its crossover uses being more of a side benefit.

    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                                            A completely unhelpful request:

                                                            At the moment Promethean has a reputation as pretty much as the greatest game no one plays. For some reason Promethean is great to read, think about, and potentially ST for but not actually play. I'd like that fixed.

                                                            As for the whole humanist thing. Its really hard to pull off in a way thats evocative for players. Its also ind of hard to handle as an ST. What happens when one player finishes their pilgrimage, but no one else does? Is it basically character death? Likewise, if you're gradually dialing up the tension and conflict in a story you reach a point where becoming human doesn't make the uber and horrifying issues you are facing go away, it just breaks your own kneecaps.

                                                            I get that its the point of the game. As an ST I like that focus. As a player, I can understand being frustrated by it. How to handle this needs to be spelled out very clearly, possibly in the ST section.
