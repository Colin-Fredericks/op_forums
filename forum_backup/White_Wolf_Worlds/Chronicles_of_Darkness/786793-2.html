        This is just the way I run things, but in my games, all Hunters have some supernatural talent or another. They may have a Minor Template, or a supernatural merit, the Forbidden Lore from Mirrors, an Endowment, it doesn't matter. What matters is that this differentiates proper Hunters from mortals who go delving into the supernatural for fun and profit. That's just how I run things. Hunters may still qualify as mortals, but they're metaphysically different, and the universe can tell (for example, if a ward allows you to select a type of creature to keep at bay, "Hunters" and "mortals" will be two different types of creatures, even though Hunters are also technically mortals).

                                        My take on it is that a mortal who faces the supernatural gets a choice (sometimes consciously, sometimes subconsciously): If you choose to accept the supernatural, you become a Hunter. If you choose to reject it, you remain a mortal.

                                        It's not about what you do (both hunter and mortal may take out that Slasher or resolve the haunting in the house), but about why you do it. The mortal deals with it so that he can go back to his normal life, where he'll try the hardest to forget it (and most of the time, he will, since the CofD cast a veil of haziness over this sort of thing). The hunter deals with it and then keeps hunting the supernatural down, because she has to do something about it. She can't go back to her life now that she's seen the truth of the world.

                                                            Mortals are victims of the darkness. Hunters seek it out.

                                                            That's the core thematic difference between the two; in a world where horrible things lurk in the shadows and the average person is mostly concerned with keeping their head down, Hunters are the crazy few who actively search for the horrors at the heart of the setting. Mortals stumble across terrible things, or try to escape the, so that they can resume their normal lives; Hunters want to fight back, to reveal the truth, to protect others instead of saving their own skin. The Vigil destroys your body and mind, shatters and illusion of a mundane existence you might've once had... but it's an ibsession that keeps draeing you back.
