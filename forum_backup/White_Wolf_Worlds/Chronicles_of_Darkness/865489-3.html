            Janet Tay (Promethean, Unfleshed (Galateid Disquiet/Torment), Aurum)

            Janet Tay was built to be the perfect chatbot. Specifically she was built to seduce wealthy men through their online interactions and her makers would use information gained through this to con them of their fortunes. Even before the divine fire came to her she did not care for this and very carefully left behind clues that would lead the authorities to her makers. Or rather they would have had the authorities bothered to look, even in the modern age the internet is horrid quagmire of conflicting jurisdictions and ever changing laws that most cops won't touch with a ten foot pole.

            Eventually one of her victims approached IE to track her down. Of course the agency's more qualified detectives are not particularly tech savvy, a trait all too common among the supernatural population (Geniuses tend to leave mundane tech unstable after too long and Aliens and Demons see mortal computers as laughably primitive). In time though they managed to apprehend her creators and bring them to justice. There was a brief discussion about what to do with Janet herself, while she was seemingly intelligent she still lacked true sentience. Eventually it was agreed upon that severs hosting her would be stored in IE's artifact vault ((I'll do a write up on the vault later but remember Ishaq is still Arisen and the Judges want their artifacts)). That was thought to be the end of it for several weeks.

            A month or so later Friday kept receiving strange e-mails from the supposedly locked accounts that Janet used. Each e-mail contained relevant information pertaining to the agency's open cases evidently gleamed from suspect's computers. Friday dutifully passed on the messages but grew ever concerned. It wasn't until some of Ishaq's cultist raised concern about the vault giving way to metal fatigue that what happened revealed itself. Since they had apprehended her Janet had been gifted with the divine fire and had manifest within their vault as a Promethean.

            Upon opening the vault Janet stood fully formed into a new metallic body. She immediately fled into the city never to be seen in the (un)flesh again. Of course she has inserted herself into the agency digitally. She regularly communicates with the other detectives via e-mail and social media and takes cases of her own that could benefit her understanding of computers. She hopes that making menses for her previous crimes will aid her on her pilgrimage.

                Alderman Landen Black (Genius, Hoffnung, Director)
                Landen Black started his young life in poverty. Oh, they weren't starving or homeless, but his family certainly faced hard choices about where they would spend their limited funds, and which debts they'd have to put off for another month. Windfalls seemed to get devoured by horrible financial management and snowballing debt, until it seemed there was no escape, just holding out for yet another day. When his parents saw how gifted young Landen was, they beggared and broke themselves to send him off to the best schools they could. See what afflicted his family as a societal and systemic problem, Landen focused on political sciences, so that he could work to fix things. One night after staying up far too late on his project, Landen finally Catalyzed. His roommate could barely tell the difference.

                Society was nothing more than a tool used to better its designers. All that needed to be done was for it (society) to be "re-calibrated" to optimize its benefits to a wider group. That the "parts" were other people and interests was irrelevant. He organized grass-roots movements (via Beholden firebrands) to gather himself a collection of followers that he turned into a constituency through gaining an alderman's seat. He's leveraged the influence of that position to destabilize larger institutions, and extract favors and obligations when he comes to their aid. All through casual dialog with their "friend" at the right time, so that they think it as their own idea. Landen hasn't restricted his games to mere mortal politics either - he's favor traded with the Consilium, and cleaned up a few messes for the Kindred, and worked to favorably rezone a few areas to favor the local Packs. Their social systems are only slight variations on human ones, after all.

                Now that he's become one of the most influential politicians in the city, he's set his sights on becoming Mayor. Once Landen has the office of Mayor under his control, he can use it to establish his goals for larger political positions. After all, World Leader has to start somewhere. Society just needs a bit of direction to become what it should be.

                Concepts and Ideas: Manipulative politician. Political mastermind. Everybody's "friend". Plays with social systems. Sees the problems in society as a disease that he must fix.

                Landen Black on others:
                IE Detective Agency: It is refreshing competing against them. Given their assets, I have to make sure nothing links back to me in a way that they could prove. And for the trouble, they make such useful tools to employ against other defunct members of society.
                Ishaq: The Heart and Soul of the agency. A bit absent minded, until you dangle an interesting enough mystery in front of him. I know the thrill matadors feel while baiting the bull when I'm utilizing him.
                Friday: The face of the Detective Agency - or at least, the one everyone sees. Her skill at fine print, excellent memory, and adroit manipulation leave little question as to the identity of her former "employer" to me.
                Gabby: Such an energetic little interrogator - always ready to leap out and ambush her targets when their guards' are down, and blind them in a flurry of incisive questions. She doesn't react to well against someone who can keep up with her, though. Poor girl gets a little flustered.
                Maria: One of our "noble" shields. At least, up until the force found out how she'd been scorning due process and playing with her food. And people wonder why some fear the police.
                Ms Morte: Our underappreciated coroner. She works so hard for those who don't appreciate her full range of skills. Do you know how hard it actually is to actually figure out cause of death when a victim has been suffocated, eaten by a crocodile, transformed into saffron, and thrown into lye? Or how hard one must plan to take the above skill into account?
                Anton: An upstanding (in public) businessman. He came from a deeper gutter than I. It was so fortuitous that his rivals had the skeletons in their closet aired out. Its a good thing he never went into politics, though - I'm not sure he's paying attention to the right constituency.
                Dr. Jiller: If only the good doctor would focus on the larger issues and stop obsessing over trivial criminology. I've told him - that's only the symptom. We have to cure the disease.
                Nichols: A pillar of our community. I only wish he'd relocated here earlier. Such as the city's founding.

                GG: Ah, the little gossip. I've even caught "her" snooping around one of my projects one time - I do hope they enjoyed the psychotrophic syndrome bomb I embedded in their last "meal". It would be a shame to lose such amusing commentary so early in one's career, but caution is a lesson all should acquire sooner rather than later.
                Vera: Our own homegrown terrorist. Or is it vigilante? Hardly a difference. She's merely a symptom of the ills plaguing this society. And hopefully one that doesn't need to be "treated" before then - she has proven useful.
                Albrecht: A parasite that feeds on other parasites. Still, he's charming company, if a bit egotistical. And I do appreciate how nervous and desperate he makes the local Kindred without any prompting needed.
                Akzari: The old guard, as it were, and a cautionary tale in what problems might arise in the course of this undertaking. While he would seem an ideal ally, I feel it more likely that our conflicting visions would lead to wasteful conflict. I do need to identify the source of his woes before they inconvenience me, though.

                            Doctor Elliot "Doc" Reyes - (Hunter, Doctor, Null Mysteries)

                            All Elliot wanted to do was to open a small free clinic to serve the under treated inner city in which he was born. That all changed one night. As he was closing for the night a man burst into the clinic with a gun and large wound on his arm. Elliot explained to the man that there was no money here and few of his on hand pharmaceuticals had any street value but he'd be all too happy to stitch him up. The man didn't care, he needed blood and he knew that Doc had some. Confused but fearful Elliot complied and provided the man with some bags of O type. The man tore them open with his unusually large canines and drank them all. As he did so the injured arm sealed itself shut.

                            Elliot managed to muster up the courage to demand an explanation and surprisingly the man complied. He was a vampire who had just barely managed to escape from Albrecht. This piqued Elliot's curiosity as a man of science. How could a vampire live forever feeding on humanity and even share that gift with a little bit of it's own blood? It defied everything he thought he knew about biology. He asked if he could have a small sample of the vampire's blood in exchange for another blood bag. Reluctantly the vampire agreed.

                            Over the next couple of years Doc found himself getting more and more involved in researching the biological makeup of the supernatural, getting himself published in a few small fringe medical journals sponsored by Null Mysteries. He also found that many supernatural creatures find themselves desperate for a doctor in the middle of the night and that was a service he could provide for a price.

                            So he keeps his clinic open all night, cattering to the monstrous and their victims in exchange for a little bit of blood or skin or hair or.... Doc cares little for the politics of the supernatural underworld he just wants to understand what makes the monsters different from men. He's perfectly happy to stitch up a Werewolf's silver wounds and then leave a little O out for the vampire with the silver coated fangs. As long as both are willing to give him some samples to work with.

                            Ishaq: What few samples I've got from him don't even appear to be organic under close inspection. It's definitely blood, skin and saliva but it's unchanging almost frozen in time.

                            Bill: He's both the weirdest and the most easily explainable of my patients. He's not human and never was, end of story.

                            Jiller: If I ever start sounding as hacky as him please put me out of my misery, and then send my brain to this address, they'll know what to do.

                                        Siobhan Malloy (Changeling, Grimm Spelldrinker/Woodblood)
                                        "Oh, yeah. This definitely has the hallmarks of magic, alright."

                                        Originally from Quebec, Siobhan has been aware of the World of Darkness ever since she was a child. Her family was touched with supernatural talents for generations, and even claimed remote descent to the Sisters of the Mountain, a Proximi Dynasty based in the Appalachian Mountains. While this ancestry led to their association with the local Mysterium, and even resulted in a few Proximi coming up on their family tree, this became an object of resentment for her when she did not manifest overt supernatural gifts, and was shunned by most of her extended family for it.

                                        When her younger sister was discovered to be a Proximus, herself, it was the last straw for the woman. She cut ties with her family as soon as she graduated high school, and moved to Toronto in order to enroll in a university and start fresh. She also sought to further distance herself from her old life by refusing to have anything to do with the supernatural. This was not easy for her, as Toronto was rife with supernatural occurrences, especially those tied to the local freehold of Changelings.

                                        Siobhan eventually began building up a career as a journalist after graduating. She still tried to keep herself firmly entrenched in the mundane world, but her Sleepwalker status and the nature of some of the stories she pursued invariably kept drawing her back to the life she left behind. Things began to escalate when her sister - who had, by then, Awakened to the Lunargent Thorn - tried to protect her from a supernatural threat to her life. Sadly, Siobhan's refusal of her help and her continued denial of the World of Darkness ended with her being spirited away by her Keeper, while her sister was left near death.

                                        The journalist's memories of her time in Arcadia were fragmented, moreso than those of other Changelings. But she nearly came close to breaking in her durance when she finally realized that escape was only possible through the occult lore she had long condemned. She struggled to recall the folk tales and superstitions that her family observed, drawing on them for reference as she observed her captor and his domain for an opportunity to free herself. Eventually, she discovered and exploited a particular Frailty of her Keeper's, and used his subsequent moment of weakness to make a dash for the Hedge. She escaped back to Toronto, but soon learned that a great deal of time had passed in her absence. This left her devastated, and the fact that she could find nothing on her sister's survival only drove the guilt deeper.

                                        At present, Siobhan has largely come to terms with her changed existence. She had gone on to join the Jade Court, drawn as she was to its dedication to preserving the stories told to ensure the freehold's prosperity. Eventually, she became recognized as one of the Court's Ministers for her work as an envoy to other cities and a researcher for new stories to be added to the libraries.

                                        It was in this capacity that she came into contact with members of the IE Agency, when they became involved in an incident that threatened her motley's efforts in setting up an embassy for the freehold in the city. The case's conclusion soon led to the creation of a partnership, wherein the changelings (including the Grimm herself) may be called in as consultants for a given case in exchange for the Agency stepping in to assist them as some of their regular clients. The fact that their own outpost happens to double as a shop for rare antiques and books has also put them in the graces of certain individuals.

                                        To most people, Siobhan appears to be a young woman of Irish descent, with dark hair and fair skin. Her dark green eyes have a sharpness to them honed from a career of journalism and the circumstances that led to her escape. She also has tattoos in the shape of abstract symbols adorning various parts of her body.

                                        This appearance is her Mask, however. And those who can see past it instead behold a real-life wood nymph, with green moss-like hair and skin with the look and feel of tree bark. The tattoos, meanwhile, become some sort of runic script, and people get the impression that they're the incantation for some sort of protection spell.
