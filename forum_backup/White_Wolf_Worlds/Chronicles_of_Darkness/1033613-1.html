                    Mages, in my opinion, benefit the least from FS. The types of challenges the Awakened face require a greater flexibility than martial arts, especially when combined with facing Praetoreans that can negate Fighting Styles. You're almost better off with focusing on raw skill and attributes with the occasional spell to back up. Focusing on skills also improves your Adamantine Arrow rotes, which is also a nice bonus. I've seen some pick up a dot or two at low levels, but most Arrows seem to be leering away from Fighting Styles in favor of other interests and areas to invest XP in. If they do need to go into melee or ranged combat, they tend to either borrow or cast Kinetic Blow, which seems to work well for them in general as a set up for casting a spell the next round.

                    Changelings are currently the only non-Mage splats I'm familiar with that have ways of raising their Dexterity, making them excellent with Fighting Finesse styles, coupled with Street Fighting 1, if they choose to go that route. Qualifies them for shiv as well, letting them have some rather potent tactics under their hands. There's also the Elemental FS, which, despite problems with Cloak of Element's duration, is quite potent when active, giving some powerful bonuses to Defense and damage. Given how changeligns can have crazy high Defense, I've seen investments into Unarmed and Armed Defense as well. Lots of 'lings have also been going with the claw route, which sees a lot of Martial Arts and Street Fighting.

                    Vampires... There's a few main routes that I've seen routinely used. Protean claws with Martial Arts or Street and combined with the Frenzy FS. Heavy Weaponry, especially with Vigor. Or vitae lash / chain weapon combined with grappling-to-feeding. Mehket sniper. Yes, guns deal bashing, but with that new set of devotions, there's definite interest. Some of my players are looking into the Crone's new defensive style when combined with Armed / Unarmed Defense.

                    Werewolves Iv'e seen fall into Street, Tactical Shifting and Aggressive, as well as a few grappling FS. Grappling and Cheap Shot are often combined with Killing Blow for some powerful combos.

                    Demons almost always seem to devolve to guns, in my experience. Marksmanship and Firefight. Yes, some demon forms come with swords, and they're nice swords, better than mundane ones by a little bit. But there's a lack of supporting Embeds / Exploits, so my groups shy away from using things like that. And using modern weapons is just more thematically appropriate for demons to the point that I've never seen it really employed. I've seen only one character invest in Heavy Weapons to use a sword-wielding destroyer, and I can count the number of times it came up on one hand.


                    Lastly, let me touch on the Avoidance Fighting Style. If you have an assassin-themed character, this is a great Fighting Style. Irraka and Darklings, in particular, have seen great use of it, angling it to arrange surprise attacks with alarming frequency. I've seen an Irraka HiD to combining it with Killing Blow. I imagine that GotV would make use of it with fair frequency. Avoidance is a great FS, no matter the game, for sneaky-stabby types.

                                        As a vamp your best option there is probably Vigor. Very few mortal merits boost the dice pool for grappling. You can take area of expertise for a cheap +2, but almost all grappling merits give you new maneuvers or modify existing ones rather than increasing your pool. The one notable exception is Strength Performance. Goes great with Vigor and will give you an extra success on any successful strength roll.

                                        Also, trained bite is one dot for an extra +1 lethal to your bite attacks. I would make sure to okay it with your ST, but arguably clinch strike could allow you to grab and bite in a single action even outside of frenzy. Alternatively, the Riding the Wave style allows you to take advantage of frenzy, which gives some impressive buffs.
