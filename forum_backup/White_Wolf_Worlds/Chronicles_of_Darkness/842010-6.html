            Actually, Heroes can be any Integrity level now- it's just the ones completely obsessed with hunting Beasts are of low Integrity. A high-Integrity Hero may be predisposed to think of a Beast as a threat, but when they seek out the monster they can gain wisdom from it- IE. "Hold up, that's not me I don't think. Let me call some people I know so we can sort this out." The second version of the previews mention that it's not just Beasts who cause ripples in the Primordial Dream, it's anything that causes fear on a wide scale, but that Heroes who aren't obsessed with hunting Beasts can be persuaded to tackle the real problems instead of just going "You're a Beast, I'm a Hero, I know how this goes- DIE!"

            Now, when the problem is a Beast, I imagine even these not-obsessed Heroes would want to deal with it- that's just part of being a Hero -but they wouldn't necessarily jump straight to killing.

            I imagine that back in the day, probably before the Sundering when there weren't hard and fast barriers between the different worlds, Beasts could feed exclusively by inflicting nightmares (like they do involuntarily when Ravenous or Starving) if they so desired because just about everyone could feel the Primordial Dream. Heroes were the special people especially sensitive to it inclined to look into the dark and find out what was there, helping people conquer their fears. If the Beast got greedy, dangerous, or just too close, the Beast became a problem and the Hero took care of it. Basically, the confrontation with the Beast becomes a vehicle for finding wisdom. In monomyth terms, Trials leads to an encounter with the Goddess. The Hero gets their wisdom, doesn't necessarily need to kill the Beast, goes home. I think it's important to remember that there was little difference between a standard neolithic mother goddess and 'The Dark Mother'. Puts the whole thing into a bit of perspective.

            I wondered at the original purpose of Family Resemblance and other powers that let Beasts get to know and imitate other supernatural beings, and I think it has something to do about Beasts as the keepers of wisdom while Heroes were the seekers. Hero approaches Beast, ready to confront them over the disruption in the Dream, and the Beast isn't the problem- but they might know what it is through the grape vine.

                            One thing to keep in mind, when talking about the wider CofD, is that tier 3 games like Mage and Mummy have influence on national politics. Tier 1 and 2 games have fingers in all manner of local government. That implies that, yeah, puppet masters. Especially when we talk about the Union Ministry. "Just human" is becoming vanishing small as more games come out. How many monster-related killers do we get (Huntsman, Slashers, Strix, Hosts, etc) before we eclipse the human murder rate in so many places?

                            One of the conceits of the CofD is that magic touches virtually every life, but few believe it (the Lie, God Machine protocols, etc). Just human without a spirit Urging, vampire neighbor, or whatever seems to be the exception instead of rule. Not saying it can't happen, but odds are surprisingly against it, and only getting worse. With Deviant, we're hitting corrupt Big Business next, and that is on top of Mammon, Daeva Corp, the Tokyo one, and whatever the Mummy one is.
