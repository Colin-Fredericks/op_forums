                        Spoilers for a 30-year-old show? Spoilers for a 30-year-old-show! (These are just the ones I thought of; I like the idea that much of the town is mundane.)

                        Twin Peaks

                        Special Agent Dale Cooper: Demon, Messenger/Investigator. Fell when he failed to arrest Leo Johnson for the murder of Laura Palmer, as he wanted to find the true killer. Pacted to add to his backstory, since the has multiple DB Coopers running around as covers. Definitely has Embeds to make people like/trust him.

                        Laura Palmer: Fetch. Until her Changeling's return, she didn't know she wasn't real; that revelation helped prompt her downward spiral. Had a friendly relationship with her Changeling, who now goes by Maddy Ferguson. Has the Echo that lets her use Maddy's powers, hence how she showed up in dreams.

                        Maddy Ferguson: Changeling, Darkling Dearheart. Formerly Laura Palmer. Got taken when fleeing from BOB. Escaped; created a fictional life as a long-lost cousin rather than trying to take her old life back. Somewhat horrified at what her Fetch got up to, but still grieving that death.

                        Audrey Horne: Mage, Mastigos. Only recently Awakened, and there's not currently any other Mages in town to bring her into the fold.

                        Sheriff Truman and the Bookhouse Boys: Hunters. Currently only local, they work on both mundane crimes and those with a supernatural bent.

                        Harold Smith: Promethean, Osiran on the Cuprim Refinement. Liked Laura because she didn't suffer from Disquiet. When Donna and Maddy did, it broke him, hence his suicide.

                        BOB: Strix. Alien mindset, possesses people, no real agenda, has a thing with owls. Strix.

                        MIKE: Goetic demon of a dead Mage, or some kind of ghost-Mage. Strix were the last mystery he was investigating; sadly, his hubris lead to a whole lot of them in Twin Peaks. He's trying to make up for it now that he's super dead.

                        Windom Earle: Angel, Destroyer. Possibly an Exile, possibly close to Falling, possibly Fell already but doesn't care. Mission may be to destroy Cooper.

                        Log Lady: Purified. Spirit of Pines and the Forest.
