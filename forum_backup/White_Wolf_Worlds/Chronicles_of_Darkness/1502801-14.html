        Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
        Yes, one of the intended consequences was making them incapable of committing diablerie.
        Any advice on how to make the bloodline more balanced?
        Maybe keep the weakness, but just don't include the ability to drink human blood past BP5. Like, at that point they both need kindred blood and are prevented from drinking it by their own weakness, so they have to go into torpor. I made me think of maybe a bloodline that watch over other vampires whilst they're in torpor. You can trust that they're not going to snack on you whilst you sleep. On the one hand, that's giving up a lot of power... but also, being BP6+ always seemed a massive hassle to me. Also, if your bloodline are centred on guarding vampires in torpor, then you're maybe less worried about going through the process.

        I suppose the other issue is how you want their weakness to actually manifest? Like, there's a difference between non-human blood just not being 'nutritious', it being actively bad for the vampire, or them absolutely being unable to ingest it. (The latter has issues to do with avoiding the downsides of ingesting other kindred's vitae.)

        The core idea was to create a vampire dedicated to acquire a semi human state (it practices the Coil of Ziva) by ingesting only human blood
        That's not a bad idea. I suppose there's a question of how mechanically beneficial you want it to be?

        Like, I think there's a good in-universe reason to strip your own ability to drink from other vampires... But as a player, that doesn't really do anything, it's basically a purely roleplay thing.

            Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
            The core idea was to create a vampire dedicated to acquire a semi human state (it practices the Coil of Ziva) by ingesting only human blood
            I think your best option would be to make a Scale, not a Bloodline. Both mechanically and (if I'm understanding your intent correctly) lore wise. The advantage of making a Scale is that you can make the rite as hard to enact as you want, or in this case as you need. Indeed, you can go all the way up (or down) to feeding on animals, which is more human-like than feeding on, well, humans.

            But if you want the Bloodline badly, and it is meant for NPCs, well... it's really hard to think of a good counterpoint for this ability without more context. As a Bloodline benefit it has the problem that it doesn't do shit for most of the game, but when it does it changes the whole game, that's not much of a good design. But with better context things can change.

            What exactly do you want? Which characters will have this power? What'll they do? What role they play in your story? It all matters, and it may pay to open a new thread just for this, the answer for your question isn't simple.

                Ok, by the various answers i ve a more complete vision of what i need.

                The character is an NPC, a Pijavica several millenia old now living in the Arthurian Britannia setting of Dark eras 2.

                The idea is that initially she searched for power, but after she had obtained it... it seemed a pointless existence.
                Then she found love, a mortal lover in a village, nothing special but a really human person.
                She realized the weight on her soul caused by the impossibility to have children.
                And then the man died, refusing the Embrace.
                Torpor soon came and the memory of the most important man in her life faded and blurred.
                She swore to protect the memory of that man (the village and his relatives) and to transcend vampire limitation (Developing after centuries the Coil of Ziva).

                Now, the role of this Npc is to appear initially as a monster, using other vampires as pawns to secure the well being of the humans of a whole region (the descendant of the man spreaded).
                Most of the vampires are bloodbonded to her, and some are turned into Pijavica (she uses the devotion on "a thousand years of night), their role is to aggressively protect their master cattle and playthings.
                Lot of rumors are spreaded on The Lady s cruel deeds, mostly contraddictory, she must be an enigma.

                But in secret she is very "human" with humans.
                Using the Coil she conceives Dhampir childrens that then "gift" to trusted families, protecting them from afar.
                But the last one, a young boy, he has the same eyes of that man.. she couldn t resist and she raised him as his son.
                But she knows that he can t remain hidden forever.

                -‐-‐‐-‐--------------------

                The Bloodline was an initial solution to avoid torpor (she doesn t want to lose sight of her children) and to avoid being bonded (she is extremely paranoic about trusting other vampires) but with this developement it would not be necessary.

                -the idea of taking the Unnatural Affinity merit on Dhampirs to resolve feeding restriction could work (or, can minor splats in general be used on this merit) ?
                -the vampire has Blood Potency 10 and a very "evolutionary" mindset. It seems unlikely that she would not develop or adopt a bloodline.
