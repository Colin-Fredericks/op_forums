                            If they are omnicidal and xeno-formers, why didn't they start already? Planetary impact wasn't in the plan? Why'd it take so long to wake up? Considering the tech gap, I doubt humans could have ever made it useful without help.

                            Given a force that explicitly made use of humans, the tech part is easy to explain - the aliens deliberately made the devices and trained the humans in their use (to accomplish the alien's purposes). That the same tools got turned on them would be an oversight.

                            Spinning ideas... what if Earth possessed something the Geodics didn't anticipate (their Bane, in other words). That set the Xenoforming schedule back. A small task force would occasionally awaken and see what could be done about it, but their resources were limited. It was only upon one of their periodic awakenings and maintenance checks that they discovered semi-intelligent tool using hominds. Hatching the plan of conscripting them as slave labor, they developed tools for them to complete the necessary works to restore the main ship (and exterminate the humans once their usefulness has expired). After some time, with repairs nearing completion, their human tools turned on them and wiped out the repair crew before they could activate the ship (thus, betraying them first). Cue the nascent Guardians resting on their laurels and ushing in their brief golden age... before their tools started to break down. Times passes and they slowly fade into irrelevance. Then the event (arrival of the Endless, constant local source radio-waves, automated repairs finally completing, etc.) awakens the rest of the Geodic ship. Who promptly detect the Endless as the greater threat and move to wage war with them. The strange hominds using their tools can wait to be exterminated in due time.

                            Thoughts? It kinda paints the Geodics as the Bigger Bad, though.
