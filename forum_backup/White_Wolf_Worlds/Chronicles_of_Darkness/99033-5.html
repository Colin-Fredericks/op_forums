        Session XVII

        Gawain’s Memories

        I guess it was about 11 when Lori rushed in. She was babbling, excited about something. She needed Calamity. I said I’d watch over the still sleeping Mary, so the two of them left me by myself to guard. I asked Calamity to bring me back a whetstone. I kinda thought she’d be back sooner than she was, but there were more important things going on, I suppose. I managed to stay busy, stretches and stuff, and to stay awake. Lori helped me with that, before she left, with a Spring Court trick.

        It turns out Lori, in addition to wanting gun lessons, wanted Andromeda and her Table to help her Monarch. Lori told us, in confidence, that the Spring Princess had a compulsion to feed on the creative essence of mortals and was both too weak to stop herself and too much of a coward to actually follow through on killing herself, despite feeling bad about it. Apparently, Andromeda thought the Table might be able to remove the compulsion.

        In more important news, Calamity had heard from Lucien that he was following a lead on the Fetch’s whereabouts. He also had a warning for her, that sometimes damage inflicted on a Fetch was also inflicted on the Changeling it replaced. He had tortured his own to death despite this, with the help of a Spring Courtier. So Lori has remained behind as the rest of them, except Ace, go to follow the lead. Ace is guarding the two of them, Lori and Mary, now, and besides, he somehow doesn’t believe that its current transgressions have earned it a death sentence. I am staying behind because Calamity can’t stomach seeing me kill in anger. If this lead Gunnar found doesn’t pan out, she’s staying behind next time.



        (later)

        It’s over. Calamity was hurt, and Jacob and Insincere Marguerite. The Fetch is dead. Mary was hurt by that, but Lori stopped the death blow from being repeated on her. Flores and Andromeda came to help with the healing. I made waffles. To celebrate, even if I’m not sure what. Flores and Calamity talked about business things. We’re closed, tonight. No one wanted to perform, in front of outsiders.

        It’s over.

        May 14th

I am feeling kinda more, well, human now, you know? Still haven’t slept. Maybe I’ll just stop doing that. It’s more trouble than it’s worth, sometimes, you know? And cuddling with Calamity in our room, once I finally got all my stuff up there, watching an awesome TV show called Firefly and then the sequel movie, all at once, was a much, much better use of my time. Kaylee is super adorable, even if Calamity teased me a little bit about having a crush on her. Anyways, after all that, even if I’m maybe not all put back together, I’m getting there at least.

        I need to make something epic for breakfast, I think. There’s a Firefly cookbook on the Internet, maybe I’ll find something from there I can put together with the stuff we have. Then we can put our heads together to find that Table for Jolie. It’ll be nice to just help somebody, you know?


        Lorelei’s Diary

        Okay, so I know at the end of my last journal entry I was super depressed, but excited because I thought I’d finally managed to push a decent idea through my thick skull. Well, the last sixteen hours or so have seen both our fortunes and my mood improve dramatically… though I admit the motley has suffered a few lumps today as well.

        I gently shook D awake just after my epiphany. She blinked and gave me a sleepy smile. “What’s up, babe?” she asked through a yawn.

        “I have to go to the Hollow,” I said quickly. She probably saw something in my eyes, because she put one of her hands in mine.

        “Everything okay?”

        “Everything’s fine,” I said in what I hoped was a reassuring tone. “I just had an idea, is all… something that might help Princess Jolie with her current problems. I need to talk to Calamity.”

        “That’s great news, hon,” she said with a sparkle in her eye. “You want me to come with?”

        I kissed her forehead. “I know you stayed up last night to make sure I slept well,” I said softly. “Go back to sleep, D. I’ll be fine.” On a whim, I leaned forward and gave her a real kiss. “I love you, D.”

        She blinked, but didn’t balk at saying the words this time. “I love you too, Lori,” she replied.

        I smiled. “Good, that’s taken care of then.” I almost giggled at her confused expression. “I mean it, D, and I can’t tell you how good it makes me feel to hear you say that, but there are practical considerations too. There’s a monster out there who wants to hurt Mary’s friends and protectors… and you saying that is the catch for my healing contract.”

        She nodded in understanding. “Guess I’d better make sure I say it to you every day, huh?”

        I nodded and said “it would make me feel better… for more than one reason.”

        I took my leave after that, with D already snuggling down under my blankets, and made my way quickly into the Hollow. Gawain and Calamity were awake, though Gawain looked pretty ragged. I used the Contracts of Spring to siphon off some of his exhaustion and pep him up a bit before drawing Calamity aside. “I need to talk to Andromeda as soon as possible. Can you contact her, or give me her number or something?”

        She pulled out her phone and looked up the alien girl’s contact information. “Anything else I can do for you, Lori?” she asked.

        “Yeah,” I said. “You once offered to teach me to shoot a gun. I want to take you up on that. Do you think Mr. Torrez would be able to get me a gun?”

        She got a sour look on her face. “I’ll talk to him, see if I can get you something you can handle,” she said after a minute. “Look, Lori… I’ll teach you to shoot if you really want me to. I think it’s a good idea for you to learn how to protect yourself when I… when the rest of the motley isn’t around. But this thing with the fetch… let us handle it, please.”

        I gave her a look. “I’m going to do whatever I can to make sure she doesn’t hurt anyone else,” I said defiantly.

        “I know,” Calamity said in response, “but killing someone… even something like this fetch… that’s something you can never take back. Once you close that door, it won’t ever open again. I’d like to spare you that, if I can. Let me handle it.”

        I took one of her hands in both of mine. “Calamity,” I said softly, “you can’t protect me from everything. It’s sweet that you want to try, and I love you for it, but I’m not a child. If it makes you feel any better, I promise that I’m not going to go looking for the fetch… certainly not by myself.”

        Calamity smiled, but it was a sad smile. Then she said something long and complicated that I think was the name of a kind of gun. “One of those would be perfect for you,” she elaborated. “Good stopping power, but not much kick, and small enough to fit in even one of your freakishly tiny stylish purses.”

        The two of us made our way back to the Dream House after that, and I gave Andromeda a call. She was surprised to hear from me, but agreed good-naturedly to come over and speak with me when I asked. Forty-five minutes later she arrived, and I pulled her hastily into an empty room to talk. I explained the nature of Jolie’s dilemma without using proper names or giving real details, and Andromeda listened carefully. The long and the short of our conversation is this: if we can find the Table and get it back into Andromeda’s hands, she can use it to cure Princess Jolie. And she’s willing to do so.

        It will work!

        Most of the motley was back in the Dream House by this point, with Gawain keeping an eye on Mary in the Hollow. I told everyone that I needed to go to Glamour Springs, and they immediately started talking about who would come with me. “Whoah,” I said sharply, “Spring Court business. No chaperones allowed, okay?”

        D took that as written as soon as I said it, and I felt my love for her blossom like a rose in my chest at how much she trusts me. The others were not so quick to do so. It took a good ten minutes of arguing before I got them to agree that I could drive to Glamour Springs and back by myself… a drive I’ve made dozens of times before, I might add. It’s like five minutes away!

        Anyway, I made the drive all by my lonesome, and Dark Kimberly waved me into Princess Jolie’s office, telling me that I was expected. Jolie greeted me with a smile and asked me to sit. I guess she could tell that I was excited about something… though I wonder now if she might have had the wrong idea about what that was. “So, what’s up, Lori?” she asked.

        “It’s going to take some work,” I admitted, “but I might have found a way to help you with your problem.” She quirked an eyebrow in surprise. “You remember last week when I asked you about the Court’s views on Andromeda’s Table? Well, I spoke with her this morning. Without using any specifics, I gave her an idea of your situation, and she thinks that if we can recover the Table, she might be able to cure you.”

        Jolie’s eyes widened, and her face split into a cautious grin. “That’s… Lori that’s incredible!” she said after a moment. “I’m not quite sure about… is there anyone else who might be able to use the Table, aside from Andromeda?”

        I thought for a moment. “Maybe Doctor Grey from the Winter Court,” I suggested.

        She wrinkled her nose cutely. “I think I’d rather take my chances with Andromeda,” she admitted. “Look, I can’t thank you enough for this, Lori. I know it’s only a first step, but it’s more hope than I’ve had for myself in a long time. Thanks for justifying my faith in you.”

        I smiled at her. “Like you said, Princess, this is only a first step. I’ll do whatever I can to locate the Table, but I’ll probably need my motley’s help. I know I promised not to tell anyone your secret, but… well, it might help speed things up if I can let them know what’s at stake.”

        She nodded. “If you trust them, then you have my permission to share my secret. But please don’t let it go any further than that.”

        I dropped my gaze. “There’s something else,” I admitted. “I need to talk to you about your offer. I… I’m sorry, but I can’t be your mistress.”

        She looked disappointed, but not surprised. “Your girlfriend, right?” she asked.

        I nodded. “D would be okay with us having sex, if that’s all you want… but she doesn’t want it to go any further than that. She said she doesn’t want to see me hanging on your arm at public events. It would hurt her, and that’s the one thing I just can’t do.”

        Jolie took my hand. “I understand. A relationship like that would almost have to go beyond the purely physical. I can’t blame her for not wanting to risk what you two have.” She sighed and turned away. “Lori, again, I can’t thank you enough for what you’re doing for me. But that being the case… maybe you’d better go now.”

        I kissed her hand and took my leave. I could feel her watching me as I walked out, and it was all I could do to suppress a little erotic shiver until the door closed behind me. Dark Kimberly gave me a wink on my way out, and we chatted for a few minutes. She seems sublimely happy with her new relationship with Annika. I told her I was happy for her, and I meant it… as far as it goes.

        I got back to the Dream House remarkably un-killed, much to the others’ relief. Calamity said she had talked to Mr. Torrez about getting me both a gun and a license to carry, and he said it shouldn’t be a problem, which is a load off my mind. It’s going to take at least a few days, but it turns out that’s okay.

        I’d been pretty much convinced that my date with D was going to be called off due to homicidal monster attack, but much to my surprise, Calamity pretty much insisted that we make an evening of it. She did give D a look that clearly stated “you’re-her-babysitter-for-tonight-now-don’t-fuck-it-up,” though.

        Mr. Torrez had also told Calamity that his people had found the apartment where Mary’s fetch had been holing up, but she’d left the premises. Calamity, Jacob, and Marguerite drove out there to check it out with Gunnar Friday. Though I was still in a funk about the fetch’s actions, my progress with Princess Jolie’s problem and the thought of spending a whole evening with D had perked me up somewhat.

        I got a call from Calamity about an hour later. Marguerite had looked into the few remaining mirrors at the fetch’s apartment and it was clear that the creature had been going mad for several days. She described it having violent arguments with the meet in its refrigerator. She’d later destroyed half of the apartment with some sort of explosive device (which was how the Summer Court found it in the first place).

        Gunnar had some sort of method he thought he could use to track the fetch; Calamity didn’t share the details with me so I assume it involved some sort of icky occult mumbo-jumbo. In any case, he and my friends were going to track the fetch down, and asked that I stay close to Mary until the whole situation had been resolved. Apparently Mr. Torrez had a theory that there was some sort of sympathetic link between Mary and the fetch, so that whatever damage one of them suffered would also be visited upon the other. This made killing the fetch a worrying prospect, so they wanted to make sure I was on hand to use my healing contracts in case of an emergency. It turned out to be a worthwhile precaution.

        So a short time later, D, Julia and I were sitting in the kitchen of the Hollow House with Mary when she suddenly started screaming. Her skin began to redden and blister all over, and she fell over onto the floor writhing in agony. I rushed to her side and invoked my healing contract, which seemed to sooth her a bit. The damage kept coming, though, and a moment later she passed out. I invoked the contract one more time to ease her suffering a bit… but at that point she seemed to be out of immediate danger. Using the contract multiple times is really draining, and since it looked like Mary would pull through, I decided to save my energy in case my motley mates needed it.

        They needed it.

        Calamity got back first, carrying Insincere Marguerite in her arms. Both of them were really badly burned. Julia started screaming when she saw her lover in that state, and I sort of desperately snapped at D to get her out of the room so that I could work my magic. A few minutes later Marguerite was sleeping peacefully; still unconscious but no longer in any real danger.

        “Sit down, and I’ll take care of your wounds too,” I told Calamity.

        “It’s okay, Lori, I’m…” she began.

        “Sit,” I said forcefully, pushing her into a chair as gently but firmly as I could.

        Once I’d healed what I could, she got up and started for the door. “Jacob and Friday are still out there,” she said with some agitation.

        I nodded, told D where I was headed, and the two of us made our way back into the world. On the way, Calamity explained what had happened. Apparently, the fetch had decided to destroy Mary and all of her protectors in one fell swoop, and take herself out in the process. She had created some sort of large chemical bomb and was in the process of setting it up near the Dream House when my friends found her. Jacob ran forward and tried to stop her from setting the bomb off, but the volatile chemicals caught fire and pretty much everyone was very badly burned. Marguerite ran into the flames to pull Jacob out, and succumbed to her injuries and passed out. Calamity and Gunnar were “otherwise occupied.” No, they won’t tell me what they were doing or why they couldn’t join the fight, but I assume it was important.

        Mary’s fetch is dead. Mary herself would probably be dead if I hadn’t healed the worst of her injuries as quickly as I did. I guess that’s something to be proud of: I got to contribute to this important battle, but instead of committing violence, I saved an innocent life.

        When we got to the edge of the Dream House property, we could see smoke rising from the woods, but there was also a huge, dense storm cloud over the area, and it was starting to hail. I’m guessing Gunnar had something to do with that, though I suppose it could have been Jacob; he has a real affinity for ice, snow, and other wintry weather. In any case, whoever summoned up the storm, it did an admirable job of putting out the fire before it could spread.

        Honestly, that fetch was willing to set a fire to a bone-dry section of Southern California woodlands with summer right around the corner! Doesn’t she watch the news?

        Jacob wandered out of the woods as we were looking at the sky, looking about three inches from death but smiling and reveling in the unseasonable weather. One look at him was enough to let me know that I wouldn’t be able to heal him properly without recharging my internal reserves… and some of the others were still in pretty rough shape too. So I pulled out my phone and called Glamour Springs. Dark Kimberly answered, and when I let her know the situation, she said she’d send a few people over to help out.

        I waited for our guests on the front steps, and it wasn’t long before Flores and Andromeda showed up. I escorted them into the Hollow, and they set about making sure everyone was ship-shape again. Julia, eyes red, held Marguerite’s hand until she woke up, muttering darkly to herself in French. I caught a few words, something about a “fucking stupid suicidal thing to do.” After she’d recovered, she, Julia, and Flores disappeared into one of the back rooms for a while, and I can’t blame them.

        Mary’s going to be leaving us now. We told her she was welcome to stay as long as she liked, but honestly… it’s probably for the best that she’s leaving. The Spring Court has people who can help her through the trauma she’s endured. Sometimes the death of a fetch can have unpredictable effects on their changeling, I’m told, and the Court wants to keep an eye on Mary to make sure she’s coping okay.

        As things were winding down, I yawned and stretched theatrically. “Well, I’d better get back to the real world and grab a shower,” I said in an offhand way. “I have a date tonight.” D looked stricken for a moment, and I couldn’t stop myself from laughing. “With you, dummy,” I reminded her.

        “Right,” she said, looking oddly sheepish. “So, want me to meet you over there in an hour or so?”

        I gave her a meaningful look. “You can come with me if you want, sweetie,” I said warmly. Turns out she did want. The shower was fun, but it took longer than usual.

        D still hadn’t told me where we were going or what we were doing. This was supposed to be a night for us to do something she enjoyed, so I let her direct me when I was picking out an outfit. It wasn’t a huge surprise when she told me to get punked out… but it did eliminate a night of Laser Tag as an option.

        We ended up club-hopping for most of the evening, traveling between various Santa Monica establishments, drinking a bit, and taking in a wide variety of musical acts. I can’t even remember all the bands we saw last night: Love Händel, PFT, The Scraping Fangs, Zanzibar, King of Loud… mostly punk, but with a little variety thrown in here and there just for fun (The Scraping Fangs were unsurprisingly a fairly hard metal act, while Zanzibar seemed to be an unlikely fusion of rockabilly and zydeco).

        I had a blast, of course. I can usually enjoy music of just about any sort, and the fact that D obviously loves music too makes me unspeakably happy. Her hand stayed on my ass like it was glued there for most of the night, and we made no secret of our relationship as we moved from club to club. There was dancing (both with each other and with various temporary partners of both genders), there was beer, there was making out… all in all, a fantastic evening!

        We were closer to D’s place when we decided to wrap things up, so we decided to crash there, and slept through most of the morning. I’ve been up and writing in this journal for about an hour now. D’s still asleep, with a tangled diaphanous white sheet pulled up over her, leaving one leg and one breast bare, like a priestess of Aphrodite. Look at me, getting all classical and shit!

        I love her. Yesterday I proved that in the face of Herculean temptation. She loves me; enough to drop her über-cool tough-girl façade and show me her jealous, vulnerable side. The fetch is gone, and Mary is safe. I have good, powerful friends who care about me and want to protect me from the monsters that lurk in the shadows. I’m partial owner of a business in which I am allowed to sing as much as I want, and make people want me.

        Treasure these moments, Lori. Today, life is good.

        Lorelei Grace

            Wednesday May 14th

            The gorram fetch is dead. Mary is not. Gawain is putting together a Firefly themed lunch from the internet and smiling again. That's enough that I don't even mind that the sun is riding high in a sky of shameless perfect blue. I am so glad that the damn business is settled.

Give me a problem with a face that I can crave off and I'm great but for this I was fucking useless. And, of course, there wasn't a fight or anything to break the tension.

I may have to chat with my little space girl about the possibilities when I get a chance. Range is a bit short and it could have more power but it's small and easy to use and conceal which is important if Lori is going to keep it hidden in one of her tiny purses (or tucked in a garter belt... fuck that would be hot. My arms and ribs have the bruises to prove it. One time she aimed a blow right about neck height. Luna was to my side, so I couldn’t side-step the attack. I also didn’t want to mess with her concentration. So I just dropped. Literally just let my legs go loose and dropped. Almost to the ground I rolled to my right and Joan’s left and came up with a quick strike aimed at her ribs. Right between three and four. Almost had her too, but Gawain reached over at that moment and blocked my attack with his shield. I turned my crouch into a back roll and got out of his reach. Together they made a very effective team. Of course Calamity, Luna and I made a pretty good team ourselves. Things might have been a little different if those of us with knives broke out the dirty fighting, but we kept it clean for the purposes of a friendly sparring match.

                                There’s still a part of me that rails against sparring with folks so freely here. It’s telling me that the more I do it, the more they will learn about my fighting style and more specifically about my weaknesses. My motley won’t hurt me, at least for the moment, but Joan is under no such obligation to “do no harm” to me. I don’t want anything she gleans to get used against me later, but that paranoid part of me reminds me that I also found out a good deal about her fighting style as well. Fair trade? Plus, I just wanted to have fun, and that fight was fun. Both fights were fun, and I really enjoyed them both. Given the expression on Calamity’s face during (and after) both fights, she enjoyed it just as much, if not more than I did. I’m trying to put that paranoid part of me to rest, but I don’t know if it will ever fully go away. I’ve lived like that for so long, I think it’s just going to be a part of me no matter how much time passes.

                                So much of what happens to us sticks around for so long. Sometimes for the rest of our lives. I was thinking about the Lady’s brand and my scars earlier and it got me to thinking. There are some injuries I know left scars, but I can’t see them anymore. If I run my fingers along the right spot I can sometimes still feel them though. It’s like they’re hiding, or they’ve become so much a part of us that they don’t stand out as separate anymore. I wondered if they ever truly go away or just camouflage themselves really well. And if someone really looked would they be able to find them? The wounds I inflicted on others can’t be seen on my skin, but those they did to me can. What can you tell from my scars I wonder? Can you map out how the fight went? Who won, who lost? I suppose if you’re good enough you probably could. If television has taught me anything, it’s that forensics can uncover just about anything. Not sure how much of the stuff in TV shows is verifiable, but I’m sure enough of it is, at least in part. And what would happen if they never faded? Would the pain, anger or hurt stay fresh too? Would the loss? Or the guilt?

                                I’m sure that if forensics can look at the scars on a person and tell the tale, then those that were trained to give and take those kinds of wounds could do it too. I’ve been walking around shirtless like an idiot in front of Calamity and Luna. What did they find out about me? What did they see? What did they guess? What does Luna read with her finger tips as she runs her hands up my sides? And will I let her read more tonight?


                                Insincere Marguerite’s Whispers

                                A fair amount of time today has been spent considering an alliance with a Court. The Warlock of Night had never seemed to have much preference, beyond seeing the Seasonal Courts as too slow to change and thus easier to infiltrate or harm for the Gentry. While I have been considering returning to LA to consult her and ensure that such behavior will not been as anything more than making contacts, I feel myself bristle at thinking such is necessary. This isn’t LA, and I left for a reason. Still, irking the Warlock of Night is low on my to do list. Obviously.

                                The party last night went well, and I used the opportunity to talk to some Autumn Courtiers. Madam Spidertoes was polite and receptive, and interested in the possibility of allying with the Autumn Court. Indeed, she was interested in possibly something more formal between the Courts than simply myself involved, but I am not diplomat. I am just a passing grub. Still, she complimented me and told I to consider something along those lines. She did imply it need not be discussed outside the Courts in question.

                                De La Rocha was rather disquieted by the discussion. She felt Spidertoes was flirting with I. She had no problem with Spidertoes flirting with someone per se, or so she said, but felt upset at the notion of I getting involved. She did not seek to tell I not to, for it was not her place, but expressed disquiet at how Spidertoes acted toward I. That and Lorelei’s having a fling with the Monarch left her rather twitchy for the evening, I think. Julia, for her part, did not perceive Spidertoes as flirting, though she was up front about the fact that while they had shared several pleasant cups of tea, she knew little of Spidertoes beyond a polite mentor to the local Darklings who was held in esteem and a little dread, as is only proper for a revered Darkling. Definitely. She was not crazy about such a woman being involved with I, but she knows full well she will never be parted from I.

                                Honestly De La Rocha’s jealousy and disquiet were pleasing to me. I am not really proud of the fact, but not ashamed of course. I simply found it interesting to observe.

                                I also spoke to Moon and Dionisia. Dionisia fielded many of the questions about the Court that I felt too trivial for a woman of Spidertoes’ station. Dionisia was full of good answers. Terrorizing people is not how I prefer to spend time enjoying myself, but it is as close to a weapon as I wield. I am not the warlord Gawain is nor the assassin Calamity is. I am simply a grub who gets herself in trouble, and if faced with conflict I cannot escape, fear seems a viable weapon. The Autumn Court is a darker Court, which pleases me, and are a court of magic and secrets. If I must choose a seasonal Court to collaborate with, they seem to work. Also, Julia is in the Court.

                                Spring is not completely off the list, but they are a court of light and frivolity…I don’t really know how Davie can stand it. My desires are, if anything, the biggest enemy I have. I cannot reach my full potential or achieve true greatness if they distract I. I don’t know that the Spring Court would be the best fit for I, in the long term.

                                I was planning to speak to Queen Charlotte, but she seemed in a rather dark mood, that was not helped by Lorelei running off with Princess Jolie. I elected to restrict my interviews to Madam Spidertoes and Dionisia.

                                Still, it was a party and I had fun. I danced with Julia several times, Jacob a couple times, and De La Rocha once. I hung out with and flirted with Flores and Dionisia as well as the usual suspects. When Calamity, Jacob, and Gawain left with Calamity’s mentor Luna, Dionisia apparently decided to make her move. Boldly.

                                I had returned to the table with her when I found Moon curiously missing, and when I sat I swiftly found her hands to have taken up exploring. She was rather direct, and Julia and I wound up at her home. I had been warned she fixates on Faerie Tale logic at times, so I was careful to not make too good an impression, though I nonetheless found her rather impressive. Definitely.

                                Her bedroom, with a lot of pink and a rather fantasy oriented motif, was insufficiently Goth for Julia. Julia is a bit strange. Dionisia also showed I considerable favor, which might have put her off. But she mentioned the Goth thing several times. I will endeavor to not be insufficiently Goth, though I cannot imagine she found my bedroom terribly Gothic, at least compared her rather…extensive use of black in her private chambers.

                                Much like Flores, Dionisia made absolutely clear she wanted to continue to have a fling. This worries I slightly. Again because I have concerns she will see I as her true love or something. I realize I may be casting aspirations but she is a member of a kith prone to such.

                                At least she is terribly attractive.

                                I returned and De La Rocha, again, warned I Spidertoes had displayed interest last night. I looked deep in her eyes, and I believe I saw fear. Perhaps she think I might simply take up with Spidertoes and abandon her. Or perhaps she has some other reason she is deeply worried we will get together. I need to talk to her about it when I can do so in private. I don’t want to upset her, and I don’t want to upset Lorelei, or make her think De La Rocha is not devoted.

                                The afternoon was spent watching Jacob, Gawain, Calamity, and Luna spar. Eventually Joan came by and joined them. I don’t follow fighting in sunlight on purpose. I found a spot under the shady tree to lay across Julia, De La Rocha, and Lorelei’s laps. Joan seemed to be doing better, but she is still clearly insane. I saw she buttonholed some of the group at the party, I am glad I was not there for that. She seems to have gotten together with Andromeda, which is good. That bundle of nerves seriously needed to get laid. Maybe she be more stable. Hopefully.

                                Luna clearly favored Jacob, which is interesting. Because he is a knife fighter perhaps? She does not seem the sort that would care that he is pretty. Which he is. She was not really flirting exactly (she does not seem to be the swooning type) but there was decided interest. Clearly I need to find out more about this girl.

                                Gawain brought up that he had been approached by Felicity, a Day Courtier in the area. However, rather than discussing how to take all of her stuff and sell it on the black market, for some strange reason Gawain seemed inclined to make a deal with her. Deal with the Day Court? Seriously? What next, the Gentry? Julia looked at I as soon as they mentioned she was Day Court, she at least grasped the gravity of the situation. De La Rocha less so, perhaps she was distracted by something.

                                We can’t actually align ourselves with the Day Court, can we? I mean we must have standards, right? Surely they are the enemy of anyone sensible.

                                Dionisia was back and on the prowl tonight. She put some moves on but I managed to beg off for the evening. If nothing else, Julia is exhausted and has been looking forward to just sleeping for once. She is not as athletic as I, though to be fair I am not as artistic as she. Still, her plan seemed sound.

                                Sleep for the little grub in the big house.

                                End Session XIX

                                Not much to add about session 19, though comments and questions are welcome, as always.

                                We decided after the game to have some downtime. We wound up jumping ahead from Friday the 16th into the 17th to Monday, June 9th, or a bit over 3 weeks. This was done as we were at a decent stopping point, and the character relationships had evolved due to real world time passing perhaps faster than they should have. So some downtime will help that. Also, it gave us a chance to spend some of the XP the Motley had been accruing for the Dream House and for the Hollow.

                                The next session was somewhat abbreviated due to being on a different night (where the players all had to work) and missing both Ace and Gawain. The usual night would have missed Ace Gawain and Calamity, which would have been too many, but we could move it to a night where at least Calamity’s player could show.

                                Abbreviated or no, there will be journals for it, and we will have some downtime discussion. I’ll see you then, comments and questions welcome.
