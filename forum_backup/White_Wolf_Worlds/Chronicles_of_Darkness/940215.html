    A Requiem Thought Experiment: Vampire "magic items'.

    07-30-2016, 04:32 PM
    I'm on a break from running a LARP and while I was in charge I was playing with an idea: The Curse of Vampires is Viral. If it can find a way to infect itself upon something it will.

    So while I was on this line of thinking I thought up of how a character could hypothetically implant the presence of The Beast into a person or object, and that line of thinking raised the internal question of: What if vampiric "relics" were just that, taking a chunk of the curse/beast and infecting it into an object via blood-thinga-majiggery in the same way that a demon can make a Gadget.

    So, what? Have players who can add Vigor to melee weapons for more bonus dice? Add Resilience to armor to do the same thing?
    No, that didn't interest me.

    However, say a book that forces the reader to write down its deepest thoughts a la Revelation. A coat that affects the shadows around you to refine intimidation attempts by investing nightmare. I even thought of how cool it would be to use Vigor to draw an enchanted weapon from their body through a bleeding wound in the same way that a vampire can enhance their muscles using the dicipline.

    Obviously I have an idea for a mechanic to balance the sudden ability for vampires to have another means of power available to them(also, come on, you think transplanting a part of your personal beast into something won't have consequences?!?), however I'm posting this as a thought experiment because I'm wondering how much "leg" this idea has to stand on.

    If a vampire could create "relics" that are themed around the powers of the disciplines(no covenant, too easy, but devotions and Bloodline powers, maybe,) can people think of some peculiar powers that aren't just direct transplants that kinda fit with vampiric themes?

    Exampes aside from above:
    Putting Resillience into his Rolls to allow it to never degrade and recover from light scrapes.
    A pin that through Obfuscate allows the wearer to have private conversations in public.
    An auspex pen that allows telepathic messages to be passed through the ink to the reader.

    How many weird/cool/fun ideas can people come up with?

      Well, if you are passing on the Beast, you might want to start by modelling the process on already does that. The infection can be spread as an embrace, a bloodline adoption, reverent, ghouls, and even blood-bonding.

      In these examples, you don't pass on disciplines but the Beast itself. The concentration is all that differs.

      So, a relic could be:
      Blood-Bond: His object. Bathe in his blood, it never seeks to do him harm and tries to obey to its utmost. Sensible vampires remind us that objects don't have a will to bend, so this phenomena is underdeveloped among the Kindred as, not only do many dismiss it out of hand but it rarely occurs to them anyway.
      Ghoul: As above but with a gift of power. This really brings questions of Will to the fore. First, the vampire must consciously gift the power to the object, not something that occurs to most. Second, the object must be able to use that gift. Yet, do ghouls power disciplines by their nature or their borrowed nature? Perhaps by this stage, the Beast-object can exert itself.
      Reverent: This is the metaphysical jump; the spontaneous embrace. The object has a Beast, of the general vampire sort. With a Beast comes a Will. At this stage, the object can seek its own intent. For complex machines or those with a strong essence, a struggle akin to the Humanity-Beast conflict might inform that desire. For simpler or ambiguous items, the only will is a thirst for life. For blood. Auspex users start noticing a faint aura around the item.
      Clanmate: A rarity of a time when humanity accepted an animus world. Vampires back then knew better than to discount the spirits of everyday objects. Noticing the Reverent, some enterprising vampires might attempt to uplift the object. The Beast imparted now is much stronger, and even has a clan personality. The items that merely thirsted for blood now manifest that urge in clan-specific ways. The object has powers, ways to obtain their desires.
      Bloodline: Possible but unknown. The object itself might join a bloodline in the same manner it was embraced. Equally possible and the truly terrifying is that it may create a bloodline. In the same fashion that founder influences bloodline Beasts, the object itself is awoken by the Beast-object and shapes itself back. An existentialist at this point would say the object is free. In practical terms, its power specializes further and its desire becomes creative.

      I enjoyed writing that nonsense. :P

      Seriously though, that is one way I'd approach this. Vampires as persons crafting magical devices is cool and all, but vampires as a species creating relics should be ambiguous and undirected. The pin that you recommended doesn't just hide your conversations from public; it is slowly devours you. You find yourself becoming more of a wall-flower, more invisible to the world, until eventually you have being completely consumed. Adds a layer of mystery horror to these items.

      Plus, as laid out, it is a nice one-to-five point merit. :P

              I'm on my phone so I'm away from my work, plus its still a work in progress, but I thought the same thing too that Kindred needed their own type of magic items. I called the Vestiges. The way I have them work is that a Kinred knowledgable in the construction take the heartsblood of another Vanpire, the last remaining physical part of them killed through ritual or preserved at final death for the ritual. The Heartsblood can be crystalized vitae that looks like a jewel, or an atrophied organ, or a sliver of bone, whatever the crafter desires. The process to craft a Vestige is cause for a Breaking Point.
              The magic of the Vestige can mimic and/or give bonuses to Discipline dots or Devotions. Give unique powers or bonuses that inhibit or boost the vampiric condition. Really the sky is the limit. It even works for Ghouls and mortals, just it always leaves a taint or drawback on all who would wield them. I just wanted Kindred to have their very own type of magic items with flavor unique to them.
              There's more fiddly hits and mechanica to this, its just I'm on my phone right now.
