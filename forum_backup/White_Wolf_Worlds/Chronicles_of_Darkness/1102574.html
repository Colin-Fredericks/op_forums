    Oh, It's just a Yantra

    06-11-2017, 03:19 PM
    For some reason the phrase "Oh, It's just a Yantra" has become a staple of almost every game session. It is often used as a way for mages (both PCs and NPCs) to get a free pass on some questionable or odd behavior (occasionally you can instead use "It's just an Oblation" to spice things up). Examples:

    Why do you have a noose hanging in the middle of the living room? It's just a Yantra.

    Why are did you buy these 10 galons of pig blood for? It's just a Yantra.

    Why are you having sex with a stranger on my bed? It's just a Yantra.

    Why are you drowning your cabal mate in the tub? It's just a Yantra.

    Why do you have so many jars full of water from rivers all around the world stored in this closet? It's just a Yantra.

    Why are we robbing this old flag from the museum? It's just a Yantra.

    Why did you walk into my room without knocking first while covered in spiders? It's just a Yantra.

    Why have you spent the last two weeks playing online video games? It's just a Yantra.


    True examples by the way. Any weird stuff your Mages do while using the Yantra / Oblation excuse?

                          I think most games don't bring up unusual Yantras because they're often not that good, and most player characters don't have enough Yantra slots to fill with more than the most common Yantras. Mudra, Persona, Mantra and demesne are great yantras. The first two can exceed 2 dice while Mantra is universal and demesne will fit the spells that you normally tend to cast. If you most of the time manage to incorporate at least three of those four Yantras in your spells (which, in my experience, is common) then you're able to routinely fill all your Yantra slots from Gnosis 1 to Gnosis 4.

                                      I had a thought, back in first edition before talk of Yantras became public, of allowing a Mage to use the Occult skill to create a pentagram (like a summoning circle) that would allow them to stack additional tools to ward off Paradox (a cup on one point, mirror on another and coin on a third etc...) in a ritual casting. This would boost the value of a secure sanctum as a properly drawn and kept circle could be used over and over again. It would also add to the value of a mentor or more advanced Mage as they could loan out their circles to lesser Mages to use. If desired the ST could require a different circle for different Arcanum combinations, necessitating the keeping of multiple such in a sanctum.
                                      ​You could also give equipment bonuses on the creation of the circle for using valuable or hard to find materials, such as gold, silver or ground cryptid bones.
