        Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post

        Patch and raising is going back to Integrity 7 area - and not even this mean they should be precisely 7 at scale to work and live more or less. By actions of typical 'ghost story villain' they will plunge down in to Integrity from level of 7, maybe to like 4-5. Then they will try to re-establish themselves, so they go to Integrity about 6 - it's enough to go on in normal life, more or less, but it's also leave you place to return to your obsession that lead you astray in the first place. Until you are becoming shaman/exorcist, nothing in Integrity rules marks you to go up to 8 or even 7 for that matter. After becoming murderer or having heavy trauma experience, there is no need to become saint, rules wise. You just want to return to 'rather normal' - and many times it's just Integrity 6. And this is why most character will never be Integrity 8 mortals in the first place.
        Why would Integrity 6 be the point where the ghost-to-be person reestablishes himself but still keeps his obsessions? Why not 7, 8, or 9? I say with certainty that Integrity 10 is the only point where one wouldn't have any regrets or obsessions left, but only because it's literally the maximum point. Any point below is still grounds for character flaws.

        Raising your Integrity isn't about becoming a saint. That has connotations of morality and/or ethics. It's reaffirming your strength of character and force of personality. A murderer may justify his actions, or develop a twisted sense of self-righteousness. In fact, isn't that how murderers who don't confess or commit suicide out of guilt work? They justify themselves. That's what raising Integrity represents, not redeeming yourself.

        Integrity is not about being moral, or ethical. It never was. It is about being a person, a human being. And humans can be so many beings, unrepenting murderers and obsessive lovers included. "Villainaous ghosts should have low Integrity" will be true if Integrity is about being moral, ethical, or sane by contemporary societal standards. But it's not. You can have a stable psyche and still be a villain.

                                                        It's important to remember that ghosts can't raise their Rank outside the Underworld for the most part, and thus the higher baseline of cognizance afforded to a Rank 2 starter ghost serves to make people with solid self-images the basis for most convincingly personlike ghosts that stand a chance of resolving unfinished business cleanly.

                                                        Ghosts you can talk to without engaging in minor blood sacrifice are rare if you're after one that hasn't been to the Underworld and eminently more human in their outlook. That's the whole point.
