                                    * p95 and p98. Wolf senses, smell and Hunter's Aspect. I'm confused about how identifying werewolves works. Wolf senses says that this is a rolled Instant action, but the start of the Hunter's Aspect section says it's automatic.
                                    * p98 - 100. There are no hyperlinks or page cross-references from the list of hunter's aspects or renown conditions, which makes it quite hard to look up what these do. As a general point, it would be fantastic if every reference to a Condition was hyperlinked to the Appendix (I understand there may not always be space for a page reference, and it's much easier to flick between sections in a physical book).
                                    * p100 - 101. There's inconsistent use of some terminology in the Reaching rules. E.g. the text in Reaching talks about "the spirit world" when the table of modifiers talks about "the shadow".
                                    * p87. Similarly, the Touchstone rules talk about physical touchstones giving you a bonus to avoiding "harmony loss" rather than "breaking points towards flesh"
                                    * p100 - 101 and p 104. The section on Reaching explains that Harmony 8 / 2 lets you cross the gauntlet without a locus, but not that Harmony 10 / 0 characters cannot cross over. The Harmony section only mentions that Harmony 10 / 0 characters cannot cross over, and doesn't mention than Harmony 8 / 2 can cross without a locus. It would be better to have all of the Harmony/Reaching rules interactions in the same place.
                                    * p204 - The Beshilu artwork covers up text at the bottom of the first column

                                                            Posting this here per recommendation in another thread ( http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...ages-questions )

                                                            Are the Advantage(s) granted by a Totem limited to +1 Dot? All of the examples seem to imply yes, but there's nothing explicitly stated. If multiple dots are allowed, does that mean the totem can bring a WW or Wolf-Blooded to 7 or higher? If multiple dots are allowed and traits can not exceed 6 (or PU+1, whichever is higher), what happens?

                                                            Do people gaining a merit as an Advantage need to meet the prerequisites? If yes, and they don't qualify, what happens? If no, how does this work for Merits that simply make no sense for that individual pack member? Anchored for a human character, for instance. If no, does this include granting Supernatural merits to werewolves?

                                                            Can "Totem" merit and "Dedicated Locus" merit be granted by the Totem Merit? As a related question, can a Pack have more than one dedicated locus.
