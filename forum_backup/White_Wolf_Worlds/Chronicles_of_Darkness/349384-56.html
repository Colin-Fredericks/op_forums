Uratha!Luffy would be different in the sense that he is willing to kill, but only does that when he thinks there's no other way.

            If you want an Uratha!Luffy, you can take my write up for the Changeling version and do the following:

            1) Remove Mask, Mien, Seeming and Kith. Add Auspice (Rahu), Tribe (Blood Talons), Blood (Challenger) and Bone (Guru).

            2) Replace the Specialty for the Hedge with a Specialty for the Shadow.

            3) Add the following Renowns: Cunning 3, Glory 4, Honor 4, Purity 5, Wisdom 2.

            4) Remove Wyrd and Clarity: Add Primal Urge 7 and Harmony 6.

            5) Replace Changeling specific Merits with the following Merits: Anchored 1 (Straw Hat Pirates), Blood Or Bone Affinity 5, Controlled Burn, Favored Form* (Dalu 3), Fortified Form (Dalu 5, Gauru 5), Living Weapon (Dalu 5, Gauru 5), Moon Kissed (Brawl, Survival), Pack Dynamics 5, Self Control, Weakest Link and Tactical Shifting 5.

            6) Remove Contracts. Add the following Gifts, Facets and Rites:

            Full Moon (Killer Instinct, Warrior's Hide, Bloody Handed Hunter, Butchery, Crimson Spasm)
            Dominance (Primal Allure, Lead The Lesser Pack)
            Elementals (Flesh Of Earth, Tongue Of Flame)
            Insight (Prey On Weakness, Read The World's Loom, Scent The Unnatural)
            Inspiration (Fearless Hunter, Pack Triumphs Together, Unity, Still Small Voice)
            Nature (Pack Kin)
            Rage (Berserker's Might, Perfected Rage)
            Strength (Unchained, Preadtor's Unmatched Pursuit, Crushing Blow, Primal Strength, Rending Claws)
            Change (Father's Form, Quicksilver Flesh, Long Limbs*)
            Pack (Pack Awareness)
            Rites (Sacred Hunt, Twilight Purge, Forge Alliance, Banish, Shadowcall, Supplication, Hidden Path)

            *Luffy's Favored Form gives him Exceptional Success with 3 Success on Athletics, +1 Strength, and Exceptional Success with 3 Successes on the Long Limbs Facet.

            *Long Limbs (Facet Of The Gift Of Change, Wisdom)

            The Uratha's flesh extends like rubber to reach out toward distant targets.

            Cost: 1 Essence
            Dice Pool: Stamina + Athletics + Wisdom
            Action: Reflexive

            Dramatic Failure: The Uratha suffers 3 lethal damage and an appropriate Tilt, such as Leg Wrack or Arm Wrack.

            Failure: Nothing happens, but the Uratha may attempt to activate this Facet again with a cumulative -1 penalty.

            Success: The Uratha may extend her limbs, neck, torso and other body parts. In order words, she may make appropriate rolls at a distance, such as Brawl, Athletics, Larceny and Investigation, with the Ranges being [Wisdom x 10/Wisdom x 20/Wisdom x 40] in yards. She may bend her body párts around obstacles and corners to surprise, disarm or spy on foes, but doing so doubles the Range penalties. This lasts for a number of turns equal to Wisdom, but may be extended with additional Essence expenditure and no roll.

            Exceptional Success: The Facet lasts for the scene, and bending around corners and obstacles does not increase Range penalties.
