    Legalized Monsters

    12-17-2016, 03:36 PM
    Hi everyone! Wasn't sure where to post this since it covers various different angles, so thought I would just do it here.

    Has anyone every played in a world where society knows that stories they were told as children are real? What if, in one country, it became legal to be one of those monsters? I've been reading a book series where this happens. It is early in the stages so there are still a lot of issues (no voting privileges etc.).

    What I want to ask, is how do you think this would effect your stories if all of a sudden, the country you are in legalized this. That it would be considered murder to just kill own without a warrant. How would your hunters act? Or your monsters? What happens when the rest of worlds monsters come flocking to your country so they don't have to worry about those pesky humans?

      Well, it would mean many different things depending upon gameline you pick to legalize.

      Vampires, Werewolves, Sin-Eaters, and Beasts would have the easiest time being accepted as real since they can be a lot more easily recognized. They would have a hell of a time being accepted into society on the account of their diets (even Sin-Eaters, can see the religious outcry now) and predisposition as predators (The Geist will have it's day in the sun after all.)

      Mages, Changelings, and Demons would have a difficult time being accepted as real because of Quisience, the Mask, and Cover respectively. Although if they were accepted on some level they would probably have the most easiest time being accepted since they can be a lot more human in appetite and goals. (Unless the whole soul stealing stuff comes up for...well, hot damn, all three gamelines!)

      And Promethean and Mummy I cannot see happening at all on account of Disguiet and Sybaris.

      This scenario could lead to some interesting social Chronicles though. Might need to run one sometime.

                                      People don't have to react so poorly in such an universal basis just because Lunacy. Our world already have ISIS, AIDS, contagious airborne diseases, criminals, and a lot more, and mostly our societies don't just go medieval on everyone who can cause problems. That certainly can happen on some chronicles, because it is a Dark setting, and certainly some people will have prejudice to no end and with murderous intent, but on a general basis it isn't needed, or even realistic.

                                      More probable, the first thing that will happen is that a lot of distinct ideas will form about how to deal with, from the "Lets All Be Happy Friends All Is Well" to the "Lets Burn Them", and everything in between and tangents. While this happens, monsters are likely to be regarded as normal people, expected the same rights and to obey the same laws, until society can adapt. The multiple discrepancies will be tackled by the system in a case-by-case basis, with results depending on a lot of factors. A vampire can be forbidden by law from feeding, while other is pardoned from any crime committed in the name of feeding.

                                      I think that mostly, though, normal law will keep in place, because really, most monsters are capable of following it. Vampires and Werewolves have to deal with anger issues, but we already have to deal with people with anger issues. On their daily lives, Uratha are not required to kill people, practice theft, or anything like that. Vampires can have willing donations, and don't need to harm too much. Mages have no reason to argue against ethics, except the same reasons as any researchers.

                                      Society will have to adapt, but that will be mostly creating special politics to deal with special necessities, something we already have. Some places are prone to ignore it and hunt down the monsters, but those are the same places that condemn homosexuals for the crime of being such. Special units on every service, including security services, are likely to rise just to deal with the supernatural in their context, and most likely including the monsters themselves as personnel.

                                      Also discovering the monsters is mostly a matter of such discovery beginning. With all the ways to hide oneself from others, as a species each one won't be that hard. A given vampire, werewolf, mage, changeling or whatever can hide indefinitely, but enough evidence from a given type is enough for its discovery. And probably all the answers will come from the individuals who think they can benefit more in revealing themselves.

                                      What is most likely to change with this isn't actual human society, or the lives of lone monsters, but shadow societies. They will get attention, and the expectation to conform. Members will be able to walk back to mortal institutions, now adapted for them. Most shadow societies don't demand crimes, but they don't care, either. It will change, to some degree. Most of how those groups work is based on their necessity to hide. Suddenly this will be gone. I can imagine, lets say, the Lancea et Sanctum loosing members to some regular christian denominations that accept Kindred (not that all of them will), and having to choose between either assume a more mainstream outlook, maybe finally becoming a part of the Roman Church, or a more cultist, but still acceptable, one to keep it as the Weird Religion of Vampires, keeping more of its independence in trade for loosing some faithful, or finally going completely underground, but that time in a context where it isn't expected to be underground just for being Kindred. All of this will change a lot how the Lancea works and how it interacts with vampires' society.
