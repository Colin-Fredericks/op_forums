There's no greater honor in being barely alive over a dead foe than in a flawless victory. So we have the Duel Arcane.                                     I sadly agree with the mechanics issue pointed out in the thread. It doesn't engage the mechanics-enthusiasts among us. But narratively great. Same for the Duel Arcane.

                                    Just ... maybe a bit more than "attack or defend." Maybe each declared attack is matched with a declared defense, in contested rolls. And whichever one fails, that Arcanum can't be used again or takes a -1 like Defense does.
