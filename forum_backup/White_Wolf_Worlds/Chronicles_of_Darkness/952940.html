    Werewolves and Religion

    08-17-2016, 10:46 PM
    alright I have been doing some tinkering with the werewolves for my Old World Of Darkness setting (yes I use the Forsaken because I like them better than the Garou) and I have come up with an idea that could work for a new and old world. A possible lodge or multi pack belief that equates Luna to the Virgin Mary. This belief was popularized by werewolves of Christian upbringings in the dark ages to reconcile their faith with their new lives after the first change. I did this for a dark ages game which I used the Forsaken as NPC's in Bishops Lin in England. I have been doing this with some other faiths, Paganism I find to be easier than the Judeo-Christian faith in reconciling their beliefs. The intention though of equating Christian faith with the werewolf creation myth is redemption from mother luna. The belief is that Mary was either possessed by a Lune or an avatar of Luna herself. Of course this is up to interpenetration and like all faith I do not have it be factual. I would like to hear any input on this and if any of you had done something similar

          Reconciling faith with reality is one thing.

          Trying to mesh two different mythologies together is another.

          These days, I find the former to be far more interesting than the latter. Like... I do not need Jesus to be a werewolf (for example). He can be God incarnated as Man, or the second-to-last prophet, as per existing claims, just fine. It's just more interesting to have a werewolf, who must hunt, who has to negotiate between the spirit world and the Flesh, and also believes that he must follow Jesus, who brings not peace but a sword, but also tells him to turn the other cheek sometimes, who tells him to root out the plank in his own eye before he gets whiny about everyone else, and if his right hand causes him to sin, he will seriously chop it off and regrow it after a few weeks because that shit needed purifying.

          I call the Integrity-analogue the "subjective stat".

                                                      In the Hunting Grounds: Spain I'm currently writing, I've done something similar.

                                                      The Lodge of Saviour is very strong in Spain, but they are divided by theological reasons:
                                                      - One faction (the Lunists) believes than Virgin Mary is a manifestation of Luna. They even think than the myth of the apparition of the Virgin in Covadonga (the legendary beginnings of the Reconquista) was a real manifestation of Luna. Some of them are also allied with a faction of Baal-Hadad who identifies Helios with the judeo-christian God (so, Jesus Christ, in this theology would be the son of Helios and Luna).
                                                      - Another faction (the MarIanists) sees this as heresy. They are more "orthodox" catholics (for the werewolves standards), who try to mantain apart the Uratha and the Catholics myths. They believe than Mary and Luna are two different beings, and than maybe Mary, as the Mother of God, might have ascended to an status even higher than Luna.

                                                      I also have created for Spain the Crusaders of Santiago, a Lodge founded by a spirit of Reconquista in Middle Ages, but that has became the main Lodge of the country. That Spirit, the Sword of the Prophet, has grown in power since then, and currently has became the Spirit of Spain itself.
