      That's nothing. This one time, in V:tM, I made this Brujah vampire who had Celerity 5, Potence 4 and three katanas (see, he had Melee 5 with a Specialty of "Katanas," so he could have one in each hand and one in mid-air; he juggled between the three so they were always moving, making him ultra-super-totally-badass). So we go to this meeting of the elders because they heard how much of a badass I was, and they're all like, "We want you to kill all the Sabbat," and I'm like, "I don't take orders from you" ('cuz I'm a Brujah, and that's method acting!), and then they're all like, "Ha ha sucker we're the Sabbat" and they pulled off their faces and they were all wicked powerful Sabbat vampires (I could tell because they all had like a thousand eyes or faces without eyes or mouths with huge teeth that if you opened them had eyes inside) so I jumped at them pulling out all three katanas at once and I had such a good initiative roll that before they could act I cut the heads off two of them and cut the arm off one so he could no longer use his Tremere magic and then they pulled out guns (ha!) to shoot me but I blocked like a million bullets with my katanas (I call it the Katana Storm!) and I reflected them all back at the vampires and they all died because the bullets were made of silver (ha ha stoopid vampires) and then the room went all dark and this huge badass vampire came in and said "I am Caine you have spilt blood in Leeseeum" (or some other gay name that the GM made up) and I said "Come get some" and I threw one of my katanas at him and it hid him right in the heart and he couldn't move then I ran up and chopped off all his arms and legs until he was just a head on a torso and he begged me not to kill him but I said "Hail to the King baby" and cut off his head. Then they made me the head of the vampires and I said "let's make an army of vampires to rule the entire world" and they said "okay" and I turned the world into a vampire army.

      In my last Shadowrun campaign, I played this physical adept (read: ninja) who made this crossbow that fired Dikoted katanas that had flame bomb spells anchored on them, so he'd load a katana and shoot it and when it hit the enemy it'd cut him in two and then blow him up with fire and then the two guys next to him would catch on fire too. And by then I'd have reloaded, because I had 6d6+16 Initiative or something crazy like that. It cost me like 1000 karma to enchant all the katanas, but I'd found this ancient elvish artifact that gave me 10 karma every time I killed a dude, so I was wicked stocked. Anyhow, one time, this Mr. Johnson hired us to break into this megacorp arcology and kill all the dudes and he was like "I'll give you one million nuyen" and I said "ha, make it ten million or I'll kill you, I'm the world's most expensive ninja" (because he was a human and I was a troll and trolls don't like humans; method acting! don't let anyone ever tell you that you can't be the most badass-totally-awesome-kewl actor roleplayer ever and still play a ninja! always have ninjas!). So we settled on six million, because I didn't want to kill him unless I really really had to, and we went to the arcology. Our rigger pilot flew a hundred feet over the roof out of the range of their rockets and I jumped out and fell but I didn't take any damage when I landed because I'd told the GM that I knew how to roll when I hit the ground and not take damage (in real life I studied jiu-jitsu for like ten months and my GM saw me test for yellow belt and he knows I'm a badass). SO then there were all these drone guns with armor-piercing rotating barrels but because I'm a ninja I was invisible to their sensors. I dropped in the roof hatch just like in Goldeneye, the most badass ninja video game evar (I always win at Goldeneye because I put mines on the respawn points and when you respawn you just die again) and there were a hundred dudes but I just lay flat on the ground and shot my crossbow a hundred times while spinning around (just like in Kill Bill, the most awesome movie about samurai evar) (omg Uma Thurman is so h0ttt) and then I put my crossbow away and pulled out my super katana, which was a Weapon Focus that gave me mad combat pool dice so I could block bullets. Then there were these two dozen trolls who all had Vindicator miniguns and they all fired at me but I twirled the katana and deflected all the bullets and I said "join me brothers so we can fight the man because you're oppressed" (method acting!) and they said "okay please don't kill us super ninja" so we all ran down to the main computer complex and there were all these cybered-out security guards. They had wired reflexes to the max and dermal plating and cyberarms with guns built in them and the guns fired arms that held guns. And they had infrared vision so they could see through my ninja invisibility. So the troll guards all got pieced first like in Saving Private Ryan, but I rolled on the ground and they all missed me, then I cut one of them in two right up the middle and then I decapitated the other one and by now I was in the middle of them so they accidentally shot like 5 of their own dudes (ha ha) and then there was one last dude who dropped his assault rifle and pulled out two katanas (!!!!!!!!!!111) and we fought for like nine hours and there were other guards but they didn't interrupt us because ninja duels are sacred but I finally disarmed him and broke one of his swords and he said "I have lost my family honor" and he committed seppuku with the other sword (because ninjas have ninja honor). And then I threw his other sword at teh computer and it exploded and killed all the other guards but I escaped because I rolled. Then my pilot picked me up and we flew away but I had to kill him to protect my identity. The guy playing the pilot was like "omg wtf" but I was like "dude I'm just playing my ninja" and the GM was like "he's true ninjas rock." So I got 450 karma and 6 million nuyen but I still killed the Johnson anyway.
