    12-27-2023, 08:52 PM
    The Seeker's of Night's Edge


    On The Hunt For Magic




    A good Mage never stays idle, he seeks his Obsessions, he tries to find out secrets whenever they may hide he wants to know, to explore, to research. But a good researcher never stays idle, he goes into the field, knee deep into the mud looking for the magical gold. The Seeker's of Night's Edge are named for their common goal, to discover all the secrets of this world and a thousand others.

    Discovery is like a drug and they're quite addicted to it, they are not like the Mysterium who believe magic is dying all around them, on the contrary, they believe it simply needs to be perfected a bit and it will grow. that's why they go hunting for it when they feel it near.by

    They don't limit themselves to what comes from the Supernal either, anything magical is fair game.

    Mages join The Seekers of Night's Edge When they decide the other Orders can't teach enough , and their path must be walked as fast as possible. When they need to learn, when their Obsessions become all-consuming


    Core Beliefs: To take Power

    Magic has many uses, while the advancement of Gnosis is still the main goal of the Seekers, they still seek power for its many practical and esoteric uses, or simply because their Obsessions demand it. In a way, their main beliefs might make them seem simplistic, after all they simply hunt for knowledge to increase their Gnosis, there doesn't seem to be a higher calling about it. When this criticism is made,they simply counter it by saying that the knowledge, power and connections they made are anything but simple in any way.



    Use the Power


    Knowledge and magic are useless if locked in a vault or hoard, once the magic of the world has been liberated from its source and taken by the Seekers, it must be put to use, that can take many forms, for continuous use of Artifacts to the reading of grimoires to the exploration of Emanation Realms. All of it has a use, and all must be taken,for study if nothing else.


    Accord with Shadow

    The many shades of the supernatural in the Chronicles of Darkness held many secrets, more often than not it's unwise to simply steal what belongs to them under the cover of darkness, they are more accustomed to it anyway. Better to deal, hustle and batter for secrets with inhabitants of the other worlds for their secrets. Once those relationships and bonds are created though, they are hard to break and several network between this Order and the supernatural societies perdure to this day



    Perfect the World

    The Seekers have an interesting relationship with Perfected Materials, they fit well in Seeker philosophy. For when a material is elevated to a more supernal form, it becomes filled with secrets, something in the process of elevating a material to a higher status creates new lore to be discovered. Perfected jewels shine with Mysteries, Perfected Fire burns in a way that must be studied.

    They believe the whole world can be perfected the same way, through morality, spirituality a brand new state can be achieved by anything you can imagine. And this new stage brings in new secrets


    Caucus

    A Seeker Caucus is usually loud and chaotic with the sounds of debate and new discovery, and experiments either working or blowing up. New members are called simply Hunters, and teams of those Hunters are led into missions to recover powerful magic by Researchers. And when it's necessary to negotiate with supernatural creatures, they are led by Mediators

    The high ranking leaders of the Order are called Sages



    Mysteries


    You'd be hard pressed to find a Mystery the Seekers don't go after. They look through all of the Mysteries of the world with the eyes of those who already half own them. But they have a particular taste for the ones who change the nature of magic itself around them


    Magical Symbolism: Seekings


    The Seekers of Night's Edge want to know everything. And their magical Symbolism reflects their hunger for knowledge, the rush of taking something powerful and making it there's
    never quite fades away.
    The Seekers are the Venatio Draconis, The Hunt of the Dragon, representing that the heirs of Atlantis will never sit idle. Theirs is the will to change the World


    Hubris

    Some things are better left alone, and the Seekers all too often fail to see that. They have released their share of ancient things, Bound and curses in the world simply because they wanted to capture it for study.





    The Shining Ones



    Magic is Here


    The Shining Ones claim the supernal-fallen division is an inaccurate statement at best, they believe magic can be found everywhere, even the dullest rock can be polished until it shines. They build sanctums and demesnes and look for every way the Supernal touches the Fallen. They rarely try to lay claim to those places of power, rather they see themselves as gardeners that keep those places alive and growing, or jewelers who make sure their glow never fades. When enough places of power are created and rediscovered, when enough Artifacts shake the heavens. Then the Supernal’s Truth will be plain to all to see.

    Mages join The Shining Ones when they want more people to see the wonder of the Supernal, they believe that everyone needs magic in their lives, they are community minded



    Core Beliefs: Ex Nihilo


    The Shining Ones believe all magic ultimately created itself, it's sentience or lack of it is and Whether there is moral character to magic itself is hotly debated. To a Shining One magic will simply appear somewhere,anywhere, if you give in enough time, but it's better for Mages to hurry it along. The most common name for this belief is “Ex nihilo”. They also believe that Magic must be guided but not fully controlled, hence their reluctance to try and take control of any Supernal Mysteries they meet.




    Physicality and Metaphor are one

    Some say The Supernal World is made of Symbols and the Fallen World is seemingly made of pure physical matter, nothing could be farther from the Truth. A tree and the symbol of strength that describes it are One. Their separation is largely part of the Lie.



    Build Communities

    Going at it alone is a fool's game, a lone Mage has no hope of truly bringing glory to the world, much less to himself. That's perhaps the biggest stain among the Tyrannic Cults, they serve only themselves when they serve the Exarchs. Communities are the key to handle magic and bring it on the light


    Record the Knowledge


    The children are the future, and thus they need to be taught. Of all the Orders the Shining Ones makes the most use of Grimoires and Palimpsests. They know future generations will have their share of Awakened and they will need teaching,thus no knowledge will be withheld. Rotes and all other forms of knowledge are saved for the future


    Caucus

    The Shining Ones have very little in the way of hierarchy, but that doesn't mean they aren't organized. Instead of a tiered organization,they create specialized roles for each job that needs doing. Visionaries are for instance, the ones who study Aedes.


    Mysteries


    They look for Mysteries of Supernal origin more often than not. When something is not working the way it's supposed to, especially if that something is the laws of reality, it quickens their interest, they just might use it to bring their goals into fruition.


    Magical Symbolism: Epiphany

    The Shining Ones prever quick and sudden revelation than the slower and more ponderous kind. They are always looking for ways to induce it And use methods from entheogens to meditation and beyond to induce it.
    In Atlantean Symbolism they are Gloria Draconis
    The glory if the dragon, bringing the majesty of the awakened city to all


    Hubris:


    The Hubris of a Shining one manifests in a know it all attitude, they can often find themselves in a path where they simply won't accept their way is not the best way.
