    Awakening the Traditions: Sahajiya

    02-19-2023, 11:21 AM
    I have been a fan of mage the Ascension for decades, but have recently come to realise that Awakening actually fits my gaming needs better.

    The thing I miss about Ascension, and the thing that I think provides a barrier to entry for many Ascension players is the rich thematic archetypes of the traditions.

    To be honest, I don't miss the traditions themselves (and it took me a long time to realise that) because they are a bit of a mess and don't really make sense in a lot of ways. In fact I have come to like the awakening setting much more than the Ascension one in pretty much every respect.

    The factions within the traditions however strike me as an excellent resource for legacies - and especially ones with strong themes that veterans of Ascension like me can easily latch on to.

    With that in mind, I have decided to start revisiting the traditions and convert their factions into awakened legacies.

    I am starting with the Sahajiya for two reasons. Firstly because when I first played Ascension in my late teens sex, drugs, and rock n roll seemed like an excellent way to do magic; and secondly because they fit really well in the awakened metaphysics - they are basically thyrsus.

    There are a LOT of Sahajiya factions across various supplements, so I am going to stick to the ones from the core cult of ecstasy book for the time being, starting with the historical factions (because despite being the most problematic in some ways they are also the core of the tradition) and ignoring the political factions because they seem like pressure groups rather than magical legacies.

    I am going to take a look at each of the factions in turn. To start with I am not going to worry about specifics, I am just going to look at parent path, order (if one seems relevant), basic themes, and primary and secondary arcana for each faction. Once that is done I can go back and revisit them filling in details and creating specific attainments.

    Like I said, I am very new to awakening so would appreciate any and all assistance in this (clearly over ambitious) endeavour. All assistance, critique, advice, and guidance is welcomed with gratitude.

    EDIT;

    I am going to post links to the legacies here to make them easier to find;

    Historical Legacies
    The historical Legacies are the remnants of adjacent ecstatic cults. They were pushing the boundaries of acceptability in a time when values and availability were very different than they are today, leaving many of them struggling to find a place to fit their practices in the modern world without condemnation becoming witch hunts.

    Erzuli Jingo - mostly female voudun who use the blessings of the Erzuli Loa to provide healing from within the astral.

    Fellowship of Pan - Heirs to the ancient cults of Dionysus who seek supernal inspiration through drink, drugs, and hedonistic revelry.

    Kiss of Astarte - Preistesses of the ancient goddesses of sex and violence
    Maenads - Savage preistesses officiating over wild rites that draw attention from the shadow.


    First up then:

    (With details from a wiki)

    Erzuli Jingo - mostly female voudunistas devote themselves to healing the minds of others by clearing their dreams with the help of their Loa, Madame Ezuli. Their magic involves astral travel and spirit channeling, and occasionally curses and other sorts of black magic.The Jingo call their arts with ecstatic dance, frenzied religious experiences, and fire.

    These seem to be clearly Thyrsus to me (like most ecstatic factions).

    I figure their primary arcana is probably mind for astral travel, healing minds, and clearing dreams. Much of their spirit channeling may be Goetic rather than strictly spirit as well. A secondary arcana could be spirit, although fate or even death might fit

                  Its not unusual, but its not incentivized, since you lose out on a third Ruling Arcanum and that makes a huge difference in your mana economy.

                  You don't need Mind, but it is the most effective. Ultimately Clear Thoughts can just blast away most mental conditions, it allows summoning a goetia to confront your fears or deal with them via a controlled vision. Technically a Clavicularius who knows what they are doing is one of the best psychologists most people could wish for.

                  In this case, it feels like a Thyrsus Legacy, one that is Time primary and Spirit optional. The Tradition was also called the Cult of Ecstasy because they used various methods to go beyond their senses, to hear the heartbeat of creation and adjust their perspective and thus alter reality. It actually makes a lot of sense, Time is how we frame reality, how we keep track of how things change. If your specialty is to reframe your view of reality, making adjustments to Time will be far more intuitive than any other Sphere.

                  In this case, they may eschew Astral manipulation in favor of change via concrete, tangible actions. They use Time to help you figure out where to go in order to change course, then they use Spirit to help you perceive the zeitgeist of the moment and harness it to make the most of your chance.

                              Next up: the Fellowship of Pan
                              Inheritors of the legacy of the Cults of Dionysus, these Ecstatics believe that a human must embrace their primal nature in order to find Ecstasy. They believe mankind is at its purest when in the grip of its unconsciousness, such as when drunk or making love or art, and tries to capture and harmonize with this unconscious beat. They excel at healing, soothing minds, and inspiration. The Fellowship is rather loosely organized and for an Ecstatic Faction that's saying a lot.

                                  I am going to rattle through my first take on the factions as quickly as possible, so I can get started with actually drawing up the details of the legacies.

                                  Kiss of Astarte

                                  modern pagan sect with a Dianic foundation. Many of these are lesbians, and some even have ties with the Black Furies. Their rituals focus into healing, rejuvenation and retribution.

                                  I have no idea what to do with these. Astarte was a goddess of love, sexuality, war, and hunting. Probably healing as well, and is popular among modern pagans (hence being a modern sect I suppose).

                                  Their original specialty was spirit, but I think they fit well with life for the hunting and sexuality aspect. Going with that, I think life and spirit seem to be a likely combination for their attainments.

                                  Given their membership of the Sahajiya they are probably thyrsus, but I can see them fitting just as well as acanthus.

                                  They seem to fit best in the adamantine arrow.


                                  ​​​​​​Maenads

                                  fierce and violent lot, even with their impressive and old legacy, the modern female followers of Dionysus have learnt to be more subtle and discreet than their ancestors. Still, every full moon they reunite to dance, revel and scream under the night, and even if some of them try to direct their rage killings into deserving targets, that is still quite subjective and many members fall into Jhor or Madness.

                                  Another group that seem to fit within the adamantine arrow. They also fit the Thyrsus well.

                                  These are the violent counterpart to the fellowship of Pan and I would probably give them a similar mix of mind and life (again, these were the original specialty spheres). Rather than the benevolent inspiration and healing given by pan however, the focus for these is on dragging people into their violent brand of madness and using life to enhance them into killing machines.
                                  clearly wisdom loss is a constant danger and they may even be left handed.
                                  Primary: mind, optional: life

                                              K'an Lu
                                              Taoist ecstatics dedicated to perfecting the self. The K'an Lu seek to perfect themselves, they aren't seeking to heal people or moving others towards enlightenment. They follow a decent portion of the Code of Ananda, but they seek to Ascend and free themselves from the Karmic cycle. They work to untie the Yin and the Yang principles within themselves to perfection. Few Cultist could stand the rigid structure of the K'an Lu's practice, though a few of their Taoist beliefs have made their way to the Western World. The Faction seems to be dying out and they are hunted relentlessly in their homeland of China by the government. Even so, they are considered one of the greatest masters within the Cult.
                                              they have extreme practices that seek a balance between the chaos of a world without boundaries, and the discipline of the trained mind and body.
                                              Specialty Sphere: Time, Prime, Mind

                                              Despite their extreme practices, their focus on seeking a balance between the chaos of a world without boundaries and the discipline of the trained mind and body suggests mastigos to me.

Mind makes sense for self perfection, as would life. I am struggling to see where the suggested specialties of time and prime might fit but I am open to suggestions. My initial instinct is for a mastigos legacy with primary life and optional mind.
