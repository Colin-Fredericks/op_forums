    I'd like to contribute to this thread but I have limited knowledge of 2nd Edition mage and my ideas aren't about getting rich per se... My ideas are things my PCs have done to avoid needing money any more. Once you live the life of a mage, you start to loose connection to things like a mundane job, but you still have bills to pay, right? I think that any of my ideas can be converted from 1st Edition to 2nd Edition by people in the know.

    Forces, ATMs, and other things -- This has been mentioned and is being discussed right now. In 1st Edition is a was a simple matter to have a universal password for any electronic device. With an ATM this works on two levels (or one) in that you are telling the computer that to access an account with the right PIN and relinquish money to you. Fallen World ethics might tell you that this is wrong and it's stealing. Supernal logic might tell you that money is just a symbol representing the energy needed to get things done. My Obrimos character would have never done this. My Acanthus character did this whenever it was necessary.

    Also, having a place to stay is a snap because hotels all use electronic locks. You can gain access to any room and stay as long as you want. You will want to be aware of when the room will be booked so you don't get caught, but it's really easy to just explain yourself. Someone (not you) made a mistake and this room is taken. Or you can use other magic, Time and Fate, to know which rooms will be available and for how long and then just schedule your squatting rights accordingly.

    Forces (or 2nd Edition Matter) makes it a simple matter to avoid being detected by artificial means. Cameras, motion detectors, etc are not going to capture your presence. So you can avoid getting caught as long as people don't see you.

    Food and stuff -- Life and Matter magic make it really simple to have plenty of food. You can create edible things out of non-edible things if you want or you can be a master gardener, growing enough food to feed an entire family with only the simplest of resources.

    Gasoline -- If you've got a motorcycle or something like that, maybe even an electrical generator, you can collect rainwater and transmute it into fuel with a Matter 2 spell and then use it power your stuff so you never have to worry about paying for gasoline again. You have to make sure you extend the duration long enough to use to fuel, otherwise you will have water in your gas tank and that won't be good for your engines. Also, if you don't mind stealing, Matter 2 makes it exceptionally easy to siphon gasoline from other sources and move it to your container or what not.

    Lottery Tickets and other things -- My Acanthus character only did things like this for emergency cash. It's too time consuming to spend all your time finding winning tickets and scratching them off. It's much easier to use your Fate magic to cast a spell that give you great luck and synchronicity all day long. You also catch the bus, you're never late, you find 20 bucks, kind strangers pay for your lunch, etc.

    If you're single, only having to worry about taking care of yourself, and you can find four walls and a roof, magic makes it easy to have all the basic necessities. Your clothes are always clean and new and fresh. You have easy access to food and fuel and heat (a single candle could heat an entire house). And if you're trying to remain anonymous, you can hide your presence pretty well, too. People might just see an empty apartment or an abandoned house.

                                                        How much Forces is needed to manipulate electronic banking into having a non-depletable bank account? or failing that access to any specific account with a flick of one's wrist?
                                                        Seems like you should be able to make an ATM pop out cash or a card reader go "yes, absolutely" without even charging an account with just Forces 1 Compelling; that would just require you to manipulate the electronics in front of you to do something they are naturally capable of.

                                                        Enchanting a bank account to always have money in it would be trickier, but it would allow anyone with the right card and PIN to withdraw cash without the use of magic. I don't think you can cast spells on conceptual things like "my bank account", but would rather have to cast on the physical storage media/computer that handles the bank transactions. It would probably be a relatively simple Forces 2 Ruling spell, but unless you find the server farm and break in you would have to cast sympathetically and with a lot of factors, possibly targeting several locations depending on how the network is set up. You could presumably Borrow Threads the connection between the relevant card and the account it's connected to - unless you're of the opinion that people form sympathetic links with objects but objects don't form links with each other, which some people are.

                                                        Alternatively you could cast a large-scale Compelling (Ruling?) spell on every relevant device in a particular area that makes them not charge the specific account when the card is used.
