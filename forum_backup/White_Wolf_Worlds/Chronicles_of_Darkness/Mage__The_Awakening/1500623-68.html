                Originally posted by Alphari View Post
                Does time under Veil of Moments still counts as natural aging?
                While a subject is under Veil of Moments, any effects that get worse over time are suspended, aging being the most consistent example of "the deleterious effects of advancing time" on a subject.
                Can I do temporal summoning of myself, then send myself (by leaving instructions) with Corridors of Time? And will it be cancelled if duration of summoning ends before CoT?
                There's no point in using Temporal Summoning on yourself before using Corridors of Time, because Temporal Summoning replaces a subject with an earlier version of itself and Corridors of Time sends a subject back in time to inhabit their past self.
                There is some lack of attainment-making instructions. But from what I understand, they works similar to basic attainments or spell. Can someone share example of atypical attainment? Or is it something similar to 1st edition attainments with merits?
                The Attainment-making instructions in the book are that you create an effect like you would create a spell, with limits on Reach and spell factors based on the level of the Attainment and your current rating in the Arcanum used to derive its effect. The example of an atypical Attainment the book provides is that a fire-worshipping Legacy that derives their Attainments from the Forces Arcanum might be limited to developing Attainments to do with heat and fire — so no Attainments that e.g. replicate Transform Energy to turn sound into electricity for them.
                By the way, core book said that each attainment can have optional arcanum. Does that mean one and only one optional arcanum per attainment, or one active (aka we have fire shield and can combine it with either life or death to get something)?
                It's one optional Arcanum per Attainment.
                From what I know, there are groups of mages who became spirits/goetia/ghosts etc. I dont remember their Legacies in books, but I suspect they just change their nature if they got 5th attainment. I believe same attainment will have slightly different effect, maybe inverted version of similar spell = temporarily transform from spirit to flesh. Can someone explain where is a border or red line of such powers? What about Time 5 keeping Acanthus (partially) detached from normal time & causality & ability to shift her age/temporal state by whim? It sounds slightly questionable; at least in comparison with Life 5 and hypothetical biological immortality & perfect health.
                Becoming an ephemeral lich after death may be a side effect of reaching particular Attainments in certain Legacies, but the mechanical effects of a Legacy Attainment are constrained by the mechanics in the book that use spell creation as a baseline. Anything more involved than the reliable tricks provided by Attainments requires the use of actual magic, with the risks that entails.

                            You can't use the Time Arcanum at conventional Practices to pull things physically out of "the past" (which is not a place that exists separately, it is part of a subject), you can only transform a subject's nature relative to its timeline.

                                                Originally posted by Primordial newcomer View Post
                                                What are the "conflict" weakness of Fate and Time magic? By this i mean if a mage has great enmity with a mage who's adept (Disciple if you want to say minimum level) at Fate and Time and DOESNT have any ability in those Arcana themselves, what are they to do?                                                 Fate and Time are notable in rivaling Forces for sheer "if you aren't being subtle this Arcanum goes off the rails fast" potential re: Paradox risk, which means managing more than preparing the stage to cause problems for you is really going to be pushing their luck with a toolset that largely amounts to adding bonuses to mundane actions — against which "fortune favors the prepared" cuts both ways. Feats of improbability out of legend and Looney Tunes may have good odds of success, but they'd either be stretching the bounds of Reach at higher degrees of mastery or involve a great deal more micromanagement than casting a single spell.

                                                This is to say nothing of the usual rejoinder about casting spells on people who aren't around, i.e. it's Reach-intensive, possibly requires multiple supporting spells before they can even target you, and increases the likelihood of failure unless they go to extra trouble.

                                                In bigger-picture terms, there's reasons none of the core Paths have Fate or Time as an Inferior Arcanum, and one of them is so nobody needs special tutelage to reach below the heights of mastery in the most generally-useful Arcana for preparing the way ahead of you.
