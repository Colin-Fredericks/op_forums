    Using Fate to deal with Cause and Consequence

    02-22-2018, 05:44 PM
    I have been thinking a lot lately about using Fate in order to deal with Cause and Consequence, as well as Order and Chaos.
    I know neither of those are stated directly as the Arcanum´s purview (despite we getting a spell called "Chaos Mastery"), but I feel like Fate would deal nicely with those (in a sense, I think it already does), especially for a character with a more "scientific" understanding of the Arcanum.

    I was curious about how you would deal with a mage using Fate in order, for example, to find out a causal factor of something (eg: "Was this cat the one who broke this vase?" or maybe "Is this factory´s pollution the cause of this person´s sickness?") or even divine a chain of effects (like the one established by a gossip- tracking back each element of the chain to its originator).
    Would you allow it? Keep in mind I understand such knowledge could be gained with other Arcana, it´s this specific use of Fate I´m concerned with.

                                      I like this question and I have thoughts on it...

                                      My opinion is formed from intensively playing an Acanthus character for a while and the way she approached Fate and Destiny and things like that.

                                      Cause and Consquence -- I'm reminded of the country song "Third Rock From the Sun" where the lyric says, "cause and effect, chain of events, all of the chaos makes perfect sense!" When another poster says Fate gives you a narrative understanding of things, I tend to say Fate give you an intuitive understanding of cause and consequence. If you're scientifically minded, then the entire universe is a random event in which Fate gives you some sort of insight into how things came to be and where they will likely go. If life was a Rube Goldberg machine, Fate can let you know Step A and Step Z, but you won't really be aware of all the little steps in between.

                                      This is why Fate covers Intention. This is why we have the spells Interconnections, Reading the Outermost Eddies, Serendipity and the like. That's just Fate 1. Awareness.

                                      That's one of the things that I found fascinating about playing an Acanthus character: having this awareness to understand the consequence of actions, of choices, of inaction. It makes you seem like a crazy person because you do things with no seeming logic behind them. You put money into a vending machine, but don't buy anything. You wait to cross the street until the third time the crosswalks signals that it's safe to walk. You sit at a red light instead of going. You choose the red shirt instead of the blue one that day. Things like that.

                                      I might agree that Fate won't tell you the whys of something, but I would say that's because it doesn't matter why. It just matters. "Whatever you do: don't get on that 3:30 flight...I've got a bad feeling." Stuff like that. It can become an Obsession, being superstitious or paranoid. What if you imagined that other people know the things you know or have your awareness... then there's no such thing as accidents and random chance. Things happen because people choose to make them happen. What if you have a twisted sense of justice or a vindictive personality? Bad news for people who cross you and don't even know why.

                                      This harkens back to the Euthanatoi and the old Entropy Sphere of magic.

                                      Does that make sense?
