    This is simply bad form.

    A pack of, say, 3 werewolves or a group of 3 vampires would make short work of a "group of thugs." Both werewolves and vampires have *innate* regeneration and defensive powers that not only make small-arms wielded by thugs laughably ineffective, but also outclass mages armors *with innate, baseline powers* until the mage hits gnosis 4 minimum. The only time a mage can truthfully be said to outclass werewolf/vampire innate passives it at Gnosis 5+.

    Trying to frame mages as unfairly hard to kill is narrowminded at least, if not deliberately untruthful.
    By the time Mages surpass Werewolves and Vampires defensively, they are Master Mages and are rivaled by Elder Vampires and Chieftain Werewolves: all of which treat normal human thugs like snacks.

    So complaining about mage armor while ignoring the fact that all other supers minimalize the threat of thugs... Well, see above.

        While it was not mentioned in the post originally this was assuming 1 Mage, not 2-3. However, the power scale is quite different when you look at the individual capabilities of 1 Mage, 1 Werewolf, 1 Vampire. So, lets do a power breakdown to analyze the weaknesses/strengths of each power splat.

        Werewolves have Gauru form which allows them to regenerate all lethal damage at the end of the round and is quite a useful regeneration. Now, Gauru is the war form and the ultimate form a Werewolf would transform into when fighting, so this seems feasible for this scenario. Now, in Gauru form they are allowed their Defense Against Firearms, so let's assume they have 3 Athletics and Wits & Dex both at 3 so that gives them 6 Defense. They also get Down & Dirty Combat with all mortals, meaning they can pretty much shred apart any mortal that decides to get in their way. Let's be fair and say half (5 out of 10) the humans manage to not lose it from the Lunacy and they open fire on the werewolf. Now, the werewolf is not wearing any armor and do not get any while in Gauru form so all they have is their defense. The humans have around 7-8 (spending a WP) dice to start with and let's say they use autofire for an additional +3 allowing them (11 Dice - 6 Defense) 5 dice against the werewolf. Let's say they all hit with at least 1 or 2 successes and werewolves don't get any resistance to bullets from my understanding, thus the werewolf is on average downed the first round of combat (10 Thugs = 12 Lethal at least with at least 5 of the scared thugs dealing a total of ~3-5 damage). Over. Maybe next time Werewolf.

        Let's go with the Vampire, see if maybe they fair better since they convert gunfire into bashing damage innately as the defense you mentioned in your post. Vampires don't really have any real forms, but they can "ride the beast" which allows them much more power in order to take on a bunch of humans. Unlike the Werewolves, they don't get lunacy so all 10 of these humans get to unload on the Vampire. Now, let's say the Vampire has some good Resilience, perhaps 2 (starting Vampire after all), so they have a total of 9 Health. The Vampire also for good measure has Celerity, allowing them to add 1 Point of Defense against firearms. The Vampire rolls unluckily and gets attacked by all the thugs using their autofire (11 Dice - 1 Defense) granting 10 dice so they on average score around 3-4 successes. Now, the Vampire does have Resilience and can spend 1 point of Vitae to subtract damage much as if they were wearing armor granting them 3/0 General Armor against the thugs. They take maybe 2 Bashing Damage and proceed to attack next round, perhaps using Obfuscate much like a Mage to slaughter their opponents by the time it is all said and done. So, Vampire wins taking minimal damage.

        Alright, now let's take a Mage to see if maybe they can fair a bit better against mortals. Now, the Mage has Space Armor, also has a Decent amount of Wits/Dex + Athletics giving them a total of 9 (starting Mage). Ok, so the Mage much like everyone else does not get to go the first round so all the thugs fire on him. Well, with a die pool of 2 (11 Dice - 9 Defense) you might get 1 success out of every 2-3 thugs. Let's be generous and say the thugs manage to score a total of 4 Lethal Damage against the Mage. The Mage can also reflexively spend mana to send the attack back at the target, potentially killing them and doing so takes out of a couple of thugs next turn. Sure, they may have taken 4 lethal damage, but there are now 2 less thugs and their turn begins. On the Mage's turn they decide to use Incognito Presence (Mastigos Mage with the Space Armor) and stealthily kills the remaining thugs. Mage wins, albeit taking the most damage. However, they would have been very similar to a Vampire had it been a general armor Mage allowing them to completely negate the damage suffered all together.

        I am not sure what you are talking about regarding power scale. If you have some more examples of 1 Werewolf/Vampire surviving against a group of thugs, by all means please present them. But there are several conditions that must be met in your examples: They must be starting supernatural being (no experience), they don't get initiative the first round (let's say the thugs get the drop on them), there are 10 thugs, and no one has armor currently equipped. I used 10 thugs because that was the amount I had originally intended to have in the encounter.
