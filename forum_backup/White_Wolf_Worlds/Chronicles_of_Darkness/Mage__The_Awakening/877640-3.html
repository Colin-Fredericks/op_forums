                                                            Good question Mrmdubois. I personally am under the impression that should a spell not exist in the 2E core book but exist from previous books (including 1E Core) that they are probably still viable, because a lot of the spells from 1E crossed over into 2E with minor alterations with perhaps spell functionality and sometimes lowering of dot level because of the new strict Practices guideline.
