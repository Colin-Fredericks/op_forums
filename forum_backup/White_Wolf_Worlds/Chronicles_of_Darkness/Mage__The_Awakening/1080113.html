    Monster themed legacies

    04-17-2017, 09:15 PM
    Unfortunately i don't know much about legacy design but i was wondering how hard it would be to create themed legacies for mages based around the other templates. For example a wolf blooded thrysus who wanted to call on aspects of being a werewolf as legacy attainments, or a ghoul who wanted to tap the power of vampire blood for attainments powered by vitae. It just seems like it would be a good way of working a mage into a crossover is to have them fit the theme of the main group.

      So,mechanics-wise i don't think it would be that different from any other legacy. Choose an arcanum that fits the splat (spirit/life for werewolves, death for vampires) and choose some attainments that fit that creature's power and that's most done already.

      Fluffwise- it's unclear if Mage can be wolf-blooded or ghouls, but discarding that you are reshaping your soul to be more like that of a supernatural creature. That probably has some side effects, but unless it is covered in the Crossover Chronicle, it is mostly up to the ST what precisely those effects are.

                  I've always assumed a Mage could be ghouled, since they wouldn't seem to have any particular immunity to it. From a narrative point of view it would be interesting to have a ghouled mage develop the Legacy to be more like their master, and then have the bond lapse, leading to a lot of resentment over the now-unwanted Legacy.

                  It's also worth considering that the Attainments wouldn't have to really mirror the powers of the other type, but could be built upon the themes or symbolic nature of the other creature.

                                  About the Werewolf one, here is the basic:
                                  1st and 2nd Attainments are basic for Wolf Senses.

                                  1st: Analyze Life (Life 1). Reach for Instant Activation
                                  Op: Know Spirit (Spirit 1). Reach for Instant Activation

                                  2nd: Heightened Senses (Life 1). Reach for Instant Activation and Increased Duration.
                                  Op: Exorcist’s Eye (Spirit 1). Reach for Instant Activation and Increased Duration

                                  3rd, 4th and 5th Attainments are for Wolfing around, healing, crossing the gauntlet and stuff.

                                  3rd: Transform Life (Life 3). Atypical duration, you can maintain this form for Advanced Duration, despite it's Reach being on Instant Activation.
                                  It's the werewolf form, with +1 size, Claws and Leaping. When the mage gets more Life he can further enhance his wolf form.
                                  The Atypical part is that you enter on a kind of Frenesi after spending more than Stamina+Gnosis turns transformed in a given Scene, and you need 3 sucesses on a Resolve+Composure to revert to your human form.
                                  Op: Gossamer Touch (Spirit 2). Reach for Instant Activation and Increased Duration

                                  4th: Mend (Life 4). Reach for Instant Activation (Cause we like heals)
                                  Op: Reaching (Spirit 3) Reach for Instant Activation and for opening Irises.

                                  5th: Shapechanging (Life 4) Reach for Instant Activation and Increased Duration.
                                  Op: Shadow Scream (Spirit 4) Reach for Instant Activation and Aggravated Damage.
