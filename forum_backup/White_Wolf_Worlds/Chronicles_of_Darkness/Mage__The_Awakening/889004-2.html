                            Originally posted by Lasombra View Post
                            Is it that powerful? i mean, i read the spell description and i don't think i fully comprehend it. Like, if i find myself in front of my enemy, and i have the correct temporal simpathy (for example a pic of him during his childhood) given the right reaches and penalities.. i can just win the whitstand and turn him into a 6 years old??? like.. what the heck. all for a whitstand of 3 and a clash of wills??
                            It lasts a single turn unless you take penalties or spend a Reach to make the Duration Advanced or the primary spell factor, it's Withstood by at least two different things if you're using any Temporal Sympathy worse than Unchanged (which already means you need to tack one onto whichever the worse rating was that you needed to beat, plus another one if you don't know the right name), it takes two Reach to affect a supernatural creature with it at all, you have to spend the Reach for sensory range to use the necessary Attainment, and of course doing any of this on an immediate scale like you'll probably want to do if you "find yourself in front of" your target takes another Reach.

                            Rewrite History is an Adept-level spell. You have two free Reach on it at most.

                            Undoing a supernatural template for a scene as an instant action at Mastery requires…

                            2 Reach to affect a supernatural target.
                            1 Reach to use instant casting (which you can convert to 1 Mana with Time in a Bottle).
                            1 Reach to use sensory range (as required for Temporal Sympathy).
                            1 Reach to use the Advanced Duration table.

                            You need Gnosis 5 at minimum for Mastery of a single Arcanum and this setup already puts you at two Reach over your safe budget, which means six Paradox dice — four if you've got an appropriate Dedicated Tool on hand (the use of which already makes the casting take multiple turns thanks to the required Yantra for Temporal Sympathy), and as little as zero if you're willing to spend multiple turns and blow two thirds of your maximum available Mana on mitigating Paradox after the Attainments' surcharges.

                            You also have to contend with a Withstand rating of at least 4, assuming you know the target's sympathetic name and they don't have a Resolve rating higher than 3 (and if either or both of those things are not the case then you either wasted a spell or need to pump the Potency up with penalties that may not be offset by any extra Yantras you used).

                            Depending on your Wisdom, you may have to eat a breaking point roll for releasing any resulting Paradox (which itself can cut the Duration back down to a single turn with one success) or else deal with the complications that containing the Paradox entails.

                            You can lessen some of these problems by having a rote of the spell and Gnosis 4/Time 4, but this further contrivance tends to harm this kind of argument. "Absolutely broken" it ain't.

                                                    Another Acanthus trick:

                                                    Lucky Mana Reactor: At chargen, set your Gnosis at 3 by spending all your bonus points on it. Then set your highest Arcanum to Fate 3 and pick Exceptional Luck as a Praxes. Gnosis 3 + Fate 3 = 6 dice, or, on average 3 successes. Since it's a Praxes, you'll be considered rolling an exceptional success on your spell casting roll with three successes. One of the options on a exceptional success is to not only regain all mana spent, but to gain one additional point of mana. Considering it's omni-applicability you'll be casting it a lot, and with that extra Reach you'll be casting it quickly. This means on average you'll be generating more mana than you put in to it.
