        Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post
        Time between damage tick overs would decrease as your Gnosis increased though. So eventually if you raised it enough even Bashing damage would kill most targets pretty quickly.
        Hmm. Right. So, since the 3rd Attainment is minimum Gnosis 4 (1 Hour). Potency would be 3, for 3 Bashing (every hour). Bashing heals every 15 minutes, so they'd be completely healed by the time of the next interval (though if they took additional Bashing, they'd effectively only heal that at a rate of 1 per hour, due to the 3 from the Attainment). Reach would only be limited to 1 as a 3rd Attainment, so I'm assuming it would be to push the duration to Advanced. The Attainment would take a Scene to prep. Potency scales with Arcanum. Duration scales with Arcanum (1 day at Arcanum 3, 1 week at Arcanum 4+, due to Advanced Duration).

        Assuming no change in Arcanum rating..
        Gnosis 5: Every 30 minutes. At this point, the Bashing damage will ever-so-slowly kill someone throughout the day. Since they have a net of 1 Bashing ever 30 minutes, at about 2.5hrs (for the average Health of 7), the bashing will roll over into Lethal, and the downward death spiral begins. It should take another 2 hrs to fill them with Lethal and trigger Bleeding Out. However, this all assumes a base 15 minutes to heal Bashing. Medical care (or anything that boosts Healing times) will dramatically affect the math. One such boost allows the target to recover 4 Bashing every 30 minutes instead of 2, beating or tying the damage inflicted until Arcanum 5.
        Gnosis 7: Every 10 minutes. Death in short order. Since the average person can only regain 1 Bashing every two intervals, they'll be comatose and dying inside the hour. Quick Healing can reduce the effect, but not by much, unless stacked.
        Gnosis 9: Every minute. Yeah, pretty much dead before they can even start to heal the Bashing.

        Malkydel: "And the Machine dictated; let there be adequate illumination."
        Yossarian: "And lo, it was optimal."
