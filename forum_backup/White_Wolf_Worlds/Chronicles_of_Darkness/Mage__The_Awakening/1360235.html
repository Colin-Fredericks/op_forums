                          Originally posted by The Throne View Post
                          I never said "there is two defence rating". Only that some mage armor give a bonus to Defence If surprised, you have only the bonus from Mage Armor. I dont found written: "Mage Armor grant your Defence also on surprise."
                          When surprised you normally do not get to apply your defense rating. Mage armors that increase defense (which include: Fate, Mind, Life, Space and Time) work vs surprise.
                          So what happens when you activate one of these 5 mage armors? Well you add some bonus X to your defense. There is no separate stat called "mage armor X" that you could apply separately vs a surprise attack the only thing you can apply is just the defense rating which is not modified by the mage armor.
                          Therefore if you are surprised while having of these mage armors on you get to apply your defense rating to the persons attack as normal. Your defense rating while under one of these armors is
                          usually "lowest of wits/dex + athletics" + "arcana rating". You cant separate the bonus given to defense and use that vs a surprise attack.

                          If you have 7 defense and gain 3 from mage armor then you have defense 10. There no 3 to use vs a surprise attack because like i've said you cant split the defense up like that. You have defense 10 and are using one
                          of the mage armors that work vs surprise and that mage armor modified your defense which is what is used vs an attack.

                          Had the text been "the bonus gained from mage armours that add to defense works against surprise attacks" then you would be correct but it only identifies what armors work vs surprise and it isnt a condition that the mage armors bonus only works vs surprise.

                          The "works vs" text could be clearer tbh.

                                          There is, however, implicit precedent in the system for calculating Defense differently in varying situations, such as Defense vs Firearms - people often think of this as a binary state where you either have Defense vs Firearms or don't, but in reality I can think of at least two systems off the top of my head that give you some measure of Defense vs Firearms without allowing you to include your full Defense rating (or leaning on it at all).
