    The Keepers of the Mansus, a Mastigos Legacy of engineers of chaos

    07-11-2025, 08:38 PM
    Origins

    “Chaos is a science of process, rather than state, of becoming rather than being”
    -James Gleick

    Nickname: Seneschals
    Parentage: Mastigos, any Pentacle Order

    Background: The earliest known account of the seneschals dates back to 9th century France, during the Carolingian Renaissance. Multiple cabals reported the sudden appearance of an enigmatic Verge appearing as a mansion. Those who delved inside would always come back with guidance, and some would even emerge with this Legacy, as well as a new perspective on how to find the path forward. Yet the manor itself would never stay in one place for long.

    This elusive estate, which would come to be called the Mansus, would at times transform inside and out. Because of this property, it has been suggested that it is a living manifestation of chaos theory. A few speculate that the Mansus might have even existed since ancient times, sporting a different look to match each new age.

    Certain tales speak of a tutelary entity called the Concierge, who would find prospective members, or sometimes appear within the Mansus to counsel those within. This has led some to speculate they and the Mansus are a creation of an archmage, an occult computational machine built for some grand design.

    The truth of the matter remains elusive, in no small part because the Mansus shares the seneschal's ability to confound temporal senses. What is certain is that it has manifested all over the world, and sometimes calls mages to it, who would find thresholds opening to its halls.

    The seneschals would thrive in the Pentacle. Within the Silver Ladder, many a théarch would use their knowledge to edify their humanitarian efforts. Researchers of the Mysterium and Free Council would utilize their models to develop new occult theories. The Adamantine Arrow and Guardians of the Veil, ever watchful, would use their predictions to uncover and counteract new threats.

    On rare occasions, mages outside the Pentacle have made their way to the Mansus, and even joined the seneschals. It has led to Prelacy hopefuls being set on the path of defection, Banishers refocused to hunt down dangerous Scelesti, Rapt undertaking projects that would prove vital, amongst some of the known anomalies.

    Some seneschal believe promoting similar outliers should be encouraged, trusting whatever system is at work in the Mansus. Others warn that this could open the Legacy to potential abuse, casting dangerous ripples onto the future. A few even suspect this could have already happened, and that even now these rogue seneschals are setting their plans into motion.

    Appearances: Ripple and wave motifs are commonly found in the seneschal's outfits, a subtle reminder of the effects of chaos theory. The color green is also favored, symbolizing their philosophy of setting up events to organically fall into place. Yellow and orange are often seen, reflecting their optimistic outlook towards the future.

    In their Sanctums, a plethora of fortune telling techniques are combined with an engineer's approach to prediction. Pendulum dowsing and statistical analysis reinforcing each other, algorithms paired with rune castings, crystal balls readings feeding data to simulations, amongst many different methods, all carefully scrutinized and documented.

    Seneschals can be often found studying different divination systems, from occult Ceremonies, to the auguries of changelings, the prophetic dreams of werewolves, the unique ripples the timelessness of the Arisen create, and many more.

    During formal Consilium events or Legacy meetings, seneschals often wear a piece of jewelry, often a necklace or pin, with a spiral mandala. The mandala itself symbolizes the totality of the Tapestry. The spiral represents how history is cyclical, yet constantly moving forward and evolving. Mentors often engrave the spiral with a statement expressing their own approach to chaos theory.

    Doctrine

    “Chaos is merely order waiting to be deciphered”
    -José Saramago

    Prerequisites: Time 2, Dream ●●

    Initiation: A house is chosen for the ceremony, one whose interior allows the prospective initiate to follow a looping path of doors. Phrases and words in High Speech about chaos theory are written atop each threshold, and every door is left open. The initiate starts a moving meditation, walking multiple times through the path, crossing each door while focusing on the sequence of words. When they enter a meditative state, they spend a charge of the Dream merit, causing them to vanish as they cross a threshold, and reappear inside the Mansus.

    Exploring it leads the Mansus to show them new insights into their life. Should they persist, they will uncover one about their possible future, alongside a clue on how it may be changed for the better. It is this process that allows them to internalize the Mansus as a framework for understanding chaos, completing the initiation.

    A similar ritual is conducted each time they seek to learn a new Attainment. It is customary for the mentor to join the student on these journeys, providing advice to nudge them towards the needed realizations.

    Organization: Seneschals in a Consilium tend to be tightly-knit, building on each other's endeavors, while also ensuring their own systems are not in conflict with each other.

    Sometimes members take turns putting themselves in the mind of each other's adversaries, pointing out possible enemy tactics, as well as vulnerabilities in each other's plans, providing a fresh perspective and helping to make their models more refined.

    Whenever pondering on a particularly difficult problem or searching for new leads, members sometimes choose to visit the Mansus. Because leaving the Mansus sends one to where they are supposed to be, whether a visitor is aware of the reason or not, seneschals may end up running into each other during these journeys.

    Students are often chosen based on their meticulousness and lateral thinking ability. Most important of all, however, is a sincere drive towards creating a brighter future, a wish to make better, more informed choices and an earnest commitment to use their predictive abilities in service of the community.

    Theory: The future can be shaped, every decision we make sends ripples throughout the world, the potential to enact meaningful and lasting change lies within everyone. However, seemingly random events and coincidences can affect our choices, in powerful and unexpected ways. This is what we perceive as chaos.

    The subconscious can be taught to tap into an intuitive understanding of chaos, through the medium of dreams. Using certain visualization techniques, the conscious mind may be trained to parse and refine this awareness.

    With the insights gained serving as a foundation, a system can be created to find the underlying structure in chaos. Through research and experimentation, models can be developed to bring about any configuration of the future imaginable.

    Discover the order within chaos, take true ownership of your choices and use them to pave the road towards progress and prosperity.

    ​Magic

    “You must always be able to predict what’s next and then have the flexibility to evolve”
    -Marc Benioff

    Ruling Arcanum: Time
    Yantras: Succeeding on an Occult or Investigation roll to discern a pattern or make a prediction (+2), butterflies (+1 for symbolic representation, +2 for live ones), keys (+1, +2 if they incorporate perfected or supernatural materials) spirals, fractal patterns or similar symbols of order within chaos (+1, +2 if prevalent in the environment or in perfected materials), divining tools (+1, +2 if they incorporate perfected or supernatural materials), ripples (+1), turbulence (+1, +2 if enough to cause a Tilt or Condition), lighters, lanterns or flashlights (+1, +2 if they incorporate perfected or supernatural materials), statistics or other predictive models (+1, +2 if relevant to the spell), bonsai trees (+1 for symbolic representation, +2 for live ones).

    Oblations: Performing non-magical fortune telling, developing or analyzing the output of a predictive model, researching divination techniques or chaos theory, advising others on future activities, preparing contingencies, planning or making improvements for the future, teaching divination or chaos theory.


    Attainments

    First: Attractor Detector
    Prerequisites: Initiation

    When developing a system to predict the future, the more data one has, the more accurate it will become. The first thing a seneschal learns is how to detect the attractors that pull someone’s future towards them. While they study a subject, they visualize themselves reading a newspaper within the Mansus, which contains [Time] headlines. Each one has a single statement or clue to something occurring in the area or about a pertinent issue, which may initially seem completely nonsensical. Characters may attempt to unravel them using a Wits + Investigation roll or with research efforts. With the correct interpretation, this allows the seneschal to gain insights they might otherwise not have.

    This is a Potency primary Knowing of Time, with Reach assigned to instant use.

    Optional: Space 1

    The seneschal carefully examines the connections between a subject's attractors, allowing them to extrapolate how they will come together to affect them in the near future. They imagine themselves within the Mansus, standing in front of a ringing 1960s-era black Bakelite telephone, one that lacks a rotary dial and instead has a single white knob. When they answer it, they receive a vision of a significant event that will occur sometime in the next [Time] days, which is predisposed to be a warning of danger, but may also present an opportunity.

    This is a Potency primary Unveiling of Space and Time, with Reach assigned to instant use.


    Second: Emergence Appraiser
    Prerequisites: Time 2

    It is in the nature of complex systems to have unexpected interactions, leading to the emergence of new properties and phenomenon. The seneschals have learned how to evaluate which ones will prove useful or troublesome. Bringing their attention to the subject or topic to be examined, the seneschals see themselves inside the Mansus, holding a brass encased lighter with High Speech engravings. When they light it, the flame burns green if the subject will prove beneficial, red should they be baneful or purple should it be unclear.

    This replicates Momentary Flux, with Reach assigned to instant use and sensory range.

    Optional: Space 2

    If the seneschal is to bring their goals for the future into fruition, they must ensure their predictive models are not uncovered and countered. After spending a scene creating a false prophecy for a subject (writing one down, recording it, drawing one, etc), they imagine a typewriter in the Mansus writing down the false account, which is then magically filed. The ink on the pages shifts at the seneschal's whim, producing false data to deceive their opponents.

    This replicates Shield of Chronos, with Reach assigned to advanced duration and conjunctional Space 2 allowing them to create a falsified history through examining the threads of possible pasts and futures, sampling those closest to the desired deception, and using them to create a convincing ruse.

    Third: Ripple Tracker
    Prerequisites: Time 3, Dream ●●●

    Within the study of the Mansus, the seneschal can access its vast stores of knowledge. They need only hold the questions about a subject’s future in their mind, and open one of its heavy gauge steel filing cabinets, to find a file containing all the predictions they sought. It is these insights that form the foundations for their plans and countermeasures.

    This replicates Divination with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range and asking specific questions.

    Optional: Space 3

    A magnificent dome with a grand star chart can always be found gracing a ceiling within the Mansus, seemingly moving of its own accord. A seneschal privy to the Mansus' secrets,however, can will it to be wherever they wish. Furthermore, by shining the light of mana upon it, the map ripples into a divining pool, allowing them to delve further into the future.

    Whenever they use an Attainment to predict a subject's future, the seneschal can spend a point of mana to target any subject they have foreseen with one of their Attainments, including the subject of the prediction themselves. Should the Attainment chosen cost a point of mana, the seneschal only pays one point of mana in total to activate both. It is by leveraging this methodology to map the future that the seneschal can set up and build more robust systems than ever before.

    Fourth: Future Pruner
    Prerequisites: Time 4, Dream ●●●●

    Even the most experienced seneschals can make mistakes or be caught unawares. When those situations come to pass, they can visualize a subject's history as a bonsai tree within the greenhouse of the Mansus. With a carefully calculated snip, they are able to prune their timeline, granting them the opportunity to grow it in a new direction.

    Some seneschals utilize it so they are always prepared for ambushes and surprises. Others use it to cultivate an air of omniscience, seemingly always anticipating a contact or visitor. Many also combine it with the use of the star map, creating a safeguard for when a disaster comes to pass.

    This replicates Shifting Sands with Reach assigned to instant use, advanced duration and going back a scene. A total of one point of mana is used to activate Time in a Bottle and, with conjunctional Space 4, Everywhere as well.

    Optional: Space 4

    Passion is one of the most influential attractors when designing any model of the future, its ripples felt in every decision. Within the lodge of the Mansus, however, lies a means by which they may be undone, shifting the course of the future subtly but powerfully.

    The seneschal imagines themselves feeding a tass log into its fireplace, making it roar with power, before tossing a symbol of a sympathetic connection into it. The flames quickly consume it, burning away the link entirely. Rioting mobs can be made to lose interest and disperse, lifelong friendships may be ended, vengeance abandoned and love extinguished.

    This replicates Cut Threads, with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range and advanced scale. A point of mana is used to pay for both Time in a Bottle and Everywhere.

    Fifth: Perturbation Modeler
    Prerequisites: Time 5, Dream ●●●●●

    A holistic predictive model must be able to infer more than the most likely future, it should also be able to extrapolate how multiple possible scenarios can affect one’s plans. Visiting the cinema room of the Mansus, the seneschal loads a film reel representing the subject into the movie projector. When the feature film plays, it displays a simulation of the scenario they wish to anticipate.

    This replicates Prophecy with Reach assigned to instant use and sensory range. With conjunctional Space 3, the seneschal can analyze the emotional context of the simulation as well, reducing the number of Doors in a foreseen social maneuver by [Time].

    Optional: Space 5

    Whereas extinguishing passions can dissuade others from making certain choices, enkindling them can create a powerful drive to pursue them.

    The seneschal weaves mana into a snow globe representing a new sympathetic bond and places it within the depository of the Mansus, adding it to their collection.

    Undying loyalty can be created, deep bonds of trust woven, burning hatred sparked and alliances forged. Paired items can be planted to create sympathetic links or teleportation relays, discreetly laying the groundwork for their plans.

    This replicates Create Sympathy with Reach assigned to instant use, advanced scale and connections never fading. A point of mana is used to activate both Time in a Bottle and Everywhere.

    What were you thinking design wise ?

    The first Attainment grants the seneschal cryptic clues about the future. Should they be able to decipher and interpret their meaning, they will gain insights they might not otherwise have. It is inspired by the Automatic Writing merit, based on the idea of the Awakened having studied different systems of divination and adapted their methodologies to create new spells.

    The first optional Attainment allows the seneschal to see the next approaching major event, allowing them to prepare to prevent it or make the most out of an opportunity. It is based on the Omen Trance Numen, with Potency used to determine how far ahead it can look. While it starts definitely weaker than the Numen, being able to see three days ahead at most, a master can see just as far ahead as an ephemeral being. The benefits it has over Omen Trance is that it requires no roll, no activation ritual and no Essence cost, making it always useful and available.

    Both of these Attainments may provide the seneschal with knowledge of things that might have blindsighted them, or topics they did not consider to use divinations on. They can then use their other Attainments to examine and refine their understanding of them. Because both of them provide knowledge that was not actively inquired about, their visualizations are ways in which information is delivered to the Mansus. The Hotline from Control served as an inspiration to the optional Attainment's visualization.

    The second main Attainment gives them an uncannily good gut instinct. A seneschal can always examine someone or something, even a plan or idea, and know what will be beneficial and what will not. The visualization is based on John Constantine’s lighter from the movie starring Keanu Reeves, and builds on the symbolism of fire as one of the most ancient methods of divination.

    The second optional Attainment prevents the seneschal’s methods from being used against them. By creating a clever cover story, the seneschal and their allies can fly under the radar of other diviners, or maneuver them into making mistakes that can be capitalized upon.

    While it is highly unusual to have a Time primary optional Attainment, placing this on the main path would delay the progression of the prophetic abilities, in this case Momentary Flux. Simultaneously, the ability to counter other diviners seemed like an indispensable part of their toolkit, so it was moved to the optional slot instead. Falsifying predictions and using them as a subtle tool of manipulation felt much more thematic than simply being able to block those visions. The seneschal does this by looking at possible pasts or futures, then uses conjunctional Space to find those that have greater sympathetic proximity to the false history they are trying to create. By using samples from them, they are able to create a convincing forgery. The visualization is a tribute to the Black Books of Hermaeus Mora from the Elder Scrolls franchise, whose letters flow and shift as they hide the forbidden knowledge within.

    The third main Attainment allows them to predict the most likely course of events, providing the first tool to examine the future in greater detail. It can be used to know where a target is fleeing to, who they are going to meet, when an adversary will strike again, and much more. Its visualization is an homage to the Quantum Ripple files in Quantum Break.

    The third optional Attainment lets them chain prophecies, examining the future from far more angles. The caveats of each divination method remain, and the deeper you delve, the more mana you are committing and the more speculations you are making. In this way, it builds on the feel of chaos theory while also providing a challenge of managing resources and predictions. The visualization is inspired by the star map in the Endmost Bethel of Baten Kaitos: Eternal Wings & The Lost Ocean, and the star map scrying pool was influenced by the Divination card from Magic:The Gathering.

    The fourth main Attainment acts as a panic button, creating time to prepare for anything that would have surprised them, as well as allowing them to undo recent mistakes. When combined with the third optional Attainment, it can be used to create a Groundhog Day style of prophecy, or even create a contingency that triggers if a disastrous event comes to pass. The ability is inspired by Orikan the Diviner from Warhammer 40000, who is able to go back in time to ensure his prophecies always come to pass. Continuing the theme of using different rooms of the Mansus, I chose to utilize a greenhouse that contains the target’s timeline as a bonsai tree, with a careful prune undoing recent events, allowing their history to regrow in a healthier direction.

    The fourth optional Attainment allows them to influence a target’s choices by altering the emotional context surrounding them. Camaraderie can be shattered, bad blood forgotten and even trust eroded. Because its effects are Lasting, it means this will not be picked up by Peripheral Mage Sight unless another mage is nearby when it's being cast. Because of the advanced scale factor, multiple bonds can be targeted at once, or entire crowds affected (preferably after the seneschal and their allies use Ward on themselves first). The visualization alludes to the classic movie trope of burning emotional tokens in a fireplace, a symbol of breaking old bonds and starting anew.

    The capstone Attainment allows the seneschal to consider multiple ways in which a scenario can play out, adjusting their plans to ensure they benefit from the outcome. By anticipating how a conversation will flow, they are able to much more easily convince their interlocutor to follow a path they prescribe, similar to what Moebius does in the Legacy of Kain series. The visualization uses the idea of seeing multiple cuts of a movie, in order to decide how they wish to compose the final version.

    The optional capstone now complements the fourth optional one, creating the emotional bonds that drive decisions. Similar to its counterpart, it can have a powerful effect, but not ping the Peripheral Mage Sight, making it so that the only ones who would detect it are those savvy enough to even look for the sympathetic links. It is inspired by how the Oracle manipulates the redpills in the Matrix trilogy. Why is Moebius so loyal to the Oracle ? Because she used this Attainment on him. Why does he have so much faith in Neo ? Why do he and Trinity fall in love as predicted ? Because she foresaw their meeting and used this Attainment alongside the third optional one. Since the sympathetic bond was carefully made and protected from diminishing, I chose the snowglobe as its visual metaphor. It can also be used to discreetly build a teleportation network with paired tokens, which can even be planted on an ally or adversary, allowing the seneschal to cast on them sympathetically when the time is right.

    The original concept behind the Legacy was “finding the order within the chaos”. It was inspired by Phoog ‘s evocative depiction of the Dream merit in the Dimensions Unseen AP, and by speculating on how mages might expand upon it. The ability could represent a connection to some kind of primordial augur, which the mind could only understand through dreams. So in order to further develop this connection, mages could use occult visualizations, allowing them to start seeing it using grounded and familiar symbols, which would act as a way to understand and explore the ineffable. This approach reminded me of how players interacted with the Mansus in Cultist Simulator, slowly peeling back the peculiar logic that governed it and navigating their way through it.The abilities could then be linked to representative tokens, similar to the mind palace technique, and then called upon by visualizing oneself using the items.

    Building further on this, the idea of finding the order within the chaos seemed very similar to chaos theory, which led me to look into how Quantum Break and The Dragon faction of The Secret World approached it. Mages could see their divinatory abilities as a way to refine it, using them as part of the toolkit to build better systems of prediction, which is why the name of the Attainments sound like tools.

    Regarding the Mansus, making it into a physical location, rather than a purely mental construct, allowed it to become a tool of chaos theory. Its ability to change its appearance and layout was inspired by the House of Mystery from DC comics. Its property of taking those that leave it to where they are meant to be is a tribute to Avalon from the Gargoyles series, allowing the Storyteller to use it as a source of plot hooks and as a possible vehicle for globetrotting adventures.

    In order to make the Mansus easier to run, I chose to make it into a Verge of Time. It explains some of its properties, expands on the types of objects and beings that may be found within, as well as provides clear mechanics by leveraging the expanded ones from Signs of Sorcery.

    Its origins are left deliberately unclear. Perhaps it's a way a cabal of the Exemplar entente uses to sneak some help past the Pax Arcana. Maybe it's an advanced occult computation device, bringing about some grand design. The Concierge could be an ananke, a member of the cabal, or something else entirely, like a qashmal. Maybe it's an emergent property of the Tapestry itself. Perhaps it's ancient, and only in the Carolingian Renaissance was the pattern identified, opening the possibility of a conspiracy, or even time travel shenanigans.

    When writing their background, choosing the Carolingian Renaissance as their earliest known appearance served two purposes. The first was to link the tribute to Cultist Simulator to a fitting place in the setting, as a mansus was a unit of land assessment in medieval France. The second was to open the possibility that the seneschals could have hidden many occult secrets in Paris, many still being left to uncover in one of the occult capitals of Europe.

    When considering whether the Mansus could be used by others, I chose to make that into its own Mystery. Some historical records show Seers, Rapt and even Banishers having positive change, but it's a rare thing. Maybe it could be worth the risk to carefully choose who to lead to it, but it also comes with the risk of it going terribly wrong. Perhaps something has already gone wrong, but it's kept low key.

    The senechal’s appearance is carefully engineered to benefit them, utilizing color psychology to influence how they are perceived. The color green represents growth, balance and renewal. The color orange conveys energy, approachability and creativity. Yellow represents joy, optimism and focus. When I noticed it had become a tribute to the art design of The Dragon, I knew I was on the right track. The ripple and wave motifs can be discreetly woven into their attires, symbolizing their role as agents of change.

    The design of their Sanctums is meant to combine the feel of a seasoned occultist with an engineer’s approach to research and development. Different methods of divination are tested, analyzed and documented, with the results used to create new predictive systems and spells.

    Their symbol uses a mandala to represent the fullness of the Tapestry. The spiral moves in a single direction, acting as an analogue for progress. It follows a known pattern, making it a metaphor for order as well. Simultaneously, it can widen and tighten as it moves, symbolizing a degree of chaos. In this way, it acts as a metaphor for the Legacy itself, walking the path of order within chaos, with the phrase a member writes upon it symbolizing their personal approach.

    The initiation ceremony is partially inspired by SCP-4240, The Roundabout Game. The moving meditation represents traversing the Mansus. As they enter a deeper meditative state and cross more mental thresholds, they are symbolically drawn closer to it, allowing them to tap into the primordial augur to transport them there. I imagine that ritual is one of the earliest patterns they discovered, and that they could bring their cabal along by guiding them through the process, and maybe even spending the Dream charge for them.

    During the design of their organizational structure, two key details came to mind. The first was that the second optional Attainment meant that, with each seneschal protecting themselves and those they care about, they could inadvertently sabotage the prediction of other members in the Consilium. Because of this, they would require a good amount of communication and cooperation to be effective, leading members to be closely-knit.

    The second was figuring out how a Legacy about manipulating the future could be integrated well into mage society. Because all of the different Orders could resonate with the seneschals, I chose to make them open to all of them. Due to their altruistic outlook, I do not think the Seers or any Left-Handed mages would normally be interested in joining them.

    The yantras are meant to encourage searching for patterns and using a mix of occult and mundane ways of finding them. Keys symbolize opening new paths to the future and unlocking new rooms in the Mansus. Lighters, flashlights and lanterns represent illumination, guidance and hope. They also built on the symbolism of the seneschals traversing the estate of the Mansus. Turbulence in fluid dynamics (water, air or even smoke) is also used to represent chaos, with a higher benefit when they are significant enough to affect the environment, such as mid-flight turbulence or a storm at sea.

    The oblations incentivize them to constantly seek new insights through different prediction methods. Because the seneschals are able to use the Dream merit to expand their awareness even while asleep, they synergize to create a feel of someone who is constantly gaining deeper understanding of the chaos surrounding them.

    That concludes the notes. I hope you had as much fun reading them as I did writing them, thank you in advance for the feedback. A huge thanks to orathaic , Aendrinastor , NuriaAge and EnigmaticTea for all their help with this Legacy.
