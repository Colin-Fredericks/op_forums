    Or, cast as an area of effect and the spell will be withstood by the highest composure within range, and then *everyone* will be susceptible to the caster's words of truth.
    No. An AoE means that everyone within the area is a subject and each subject applies Withstand separately. If you cast it at Potency 3 over an area, everyone with Composure 3 or higher will be unaffected while everyone with Composure 1 or 2 (or who chooses not to apply Composure as their Withstand rating) are affected while they remain within the area.

            Honestly, there's nothing that says that mages can't do it with werewolves. It's just that the two published spells only works on Sleepers and mages, respectively.
            Werewolves being a fusion of flesh and ephemera doesn't factor into it; they still have souls (or something that is close enough) as evident by idigam being able to rip them out. If mages can't do it with spells, it's because their souls are considered part of the template and can't be messed around with.

            Tremere does it through their own special traits (that honestly should be called Hollow Attainments or even Dread Powers; not just "Other Traits") so them being able to do it doesn't imply anything about the possibility of doing it through spells. (And in a reversal, they can't do it on Beasts because they have Horrors instead of souls, but Horrors can explicitly be separated from Beasts using spells. This kills the Beast.)

                                    Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post
                                    Geckos can, so I was hoping to enable it in the same way Nightvision enables bat echolocation or a shark's bioelectric vision.
                                    Nightvision is an Unveiling spell. It lets you take in more information, which is exactly what Unveiling is supposed to do.
                                    You also can't compare animal features because "gaining/emulating an animal feature" is not a Forces effect. It's a coincidental side effect of that particular spell. If anything, it'd imply you'd require a Weaving spell because that's the practice of Transform Life (which grants animal features).

                                    Compelling spells are supposed to "nudge a preferred but possible outcome into reality." While what's "possible" is stretched really thin with many spells, the spirit of the rule is that it shouldn't require much force/energy. Moving around things with little to no mass, shaping things with little to no substance, activating pre-existing processes and systems, etc, are the most common effects.

                                    Also, if you look to the three Influence spells that are Compelling you'll see that they all only direct existing forces and are explicitly incapable of increasing them.

                                    To emulate a gecko you need to significantly increase the electrostatic interaction to support a huge amount of weight. That goes far beyond the description of Compelling as well as the Forces Compelling precedents we have in the book.

                                                        Originally posted by Scarlet Witch View Post
                                                        How unlikely is it to see a Guardian of the Veil as a Hierarch? I haven't read their book, and am not clear on the subtleties of their idea that all thrones are false in juxtaposition to their own political authority within and without the Order.
                                                        Depends, how desperate are they ? The Guardians are the secret police of the Awakened, so the level of transparency required of a hierarch position would not come easily to them. Because of this, putting an Eleventh Question as Hierarch could work, because they are committed to the truth and can make good liasons, or intimidate with how much they know.

                                                        Another option is Bearer of the Eternal Voice, but they would need to be very discrete as to not let people know they are one or using their gifts.

                                                        There is also the matter of need. If there is a crisis so serious that only the Guardians can deal with, then having them take the lead is natural. But that also means they may fabricate the incident that tilts the scales to this outcome.

                                                        Another option is if there is too much political infighting and only the Guardians can mediate to keep everyone in line. Lets say there are few Ladders in a Consilium, the Mysterium and Free Council constantly bicker about research and access to information. In that case, an Arrow and Guardian alliance may keep the order, since Arrow philosophy is against taking a permanent role of power, the Guardians may be the only ones for for the job.

                                                        In summary, it all comes down to whether circumstances are grave enough that you would agree to be lead by spies.
