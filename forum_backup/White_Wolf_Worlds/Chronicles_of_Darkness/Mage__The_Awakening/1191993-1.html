                Originally posted by rickayelm View Post
                The whole idea that the Free Council favors democracy is a lie. The last time I checked real democracy's don't condone the whole sale slaughter of everyone who voted against the winners of the last election, which is what happened in "The Great Refusal."
                You've misread it. The Great Refusal predates the forming of the Free Council. It's a co-ordinated plan to gather information through appearing to co-operate with the Seers before launching an all-out attack on the Order. However some of the Nameless actually join the Seers. It's not a vote; it's a plan the Seer Nameless choose to betray. Plenty of real life democracies will execute traitors, especially in times of war.

                Also, most democracies do have provisions for defending their constitutions from elements that want to overthrow it. Signing on with the magic Nazis isn't just a difference of opinion, it's a massive betrayal of everything the order stands for.

                Additionally, they didn't slaughter them all. It's a major plot point in Mage Noir that the FC was willing to burn it's goodwill with the Silver Ladder to reintegrate Seer Nameless.
