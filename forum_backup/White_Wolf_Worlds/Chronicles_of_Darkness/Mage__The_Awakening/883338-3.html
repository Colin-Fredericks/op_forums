    Other Path elements that need to be covered:
    Materials
    Each Path has specific materials that elevate a Magical Tool from merely an object to something resonant with a Supernal Realm, as well as weapons that can replace the traditional knife.
    • Acanthus: Glass, crystal, silver, reflective materials; Rapier, bow, precision weapons
    • Mastigos: Iron, brass, leather, worked materials; Curved sword, whip, cruel weapons
    • Moros: Lead, bone, gems, buried materials; Hammer, mace, crushing weapons
    • Obrimos: Steel, petrified wood, gold, perfected materials; Double-edged sword, spear, noble weapons
    • Thyrsus: Wood, copper, stone, natural materials; Axe, sling, hunting weapons
    I'm thinking that some overlap might be appropriate here: e.g., the Sophros really ought to have an affinity with wood, and the Aretos should feature perfected materials.

    • Aretos: copper, mercury, sulfur, purified materials
    • Axios: gold, silver, gemstones, valuable materials; daggers, batons, concealable weapons?
    • Kleos: glass, crystal, transparent materials?; whips, nunchuks, flexible weapons?
    • Mæstros: brass, pewter, alloyed metals; scimitar?
    • Sophros: wood, bone, natural materials; sickle, staff, repurposed weapons

        With the rethinking of the Axios, their former astrology-oriented imagery doesn't work so well anymore. Hermium Mirror? Great for looking at the Firmament to examine the Stars. But merchants and diplomats don't have any special connection to any of that. So I need a new Watchtower, a new Realm, and a new type of Supernal Entity for the Bargainers.

        While I'm at it, I'm examining some of the other Paths' imagery. The Kleos, for instance: the Tin Flute is all well and good; but 2e has opened up a possibility that wasn't available when I first devised these Paths back in the 1e days: namely, there isn't a Watchtower of the Stone Book anymore. With the Thyrsus shifted over to the Singing Stone, the record-keeping sages can now have a Book- or Scroll-based Watchtower. So, for reference:

        Aretos: was Copper Mallet; still works, though I wouldn't mind something Hermium-based.
        Axios: was Hermium Mirror. No clue what it is now.
        Kleos: was Tin Flute. Possibly something Bookish.
        Mæstros: Brass and Flame. Non-negotiable.
        Sophros: Wooden Cup. No reason to change this.
