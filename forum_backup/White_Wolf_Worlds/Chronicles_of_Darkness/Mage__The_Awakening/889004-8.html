                                                            You have a Coin Flip+ to gain Mana, and practically no risk of losing Mana.
                                                            Exceptional Success refunds you mana that you put into the Tass, gives you one extra, and Tass still has this mana. You not only regained all mana you used up, you also created one mana extra that you could give to other people, for example. I think it's pretty clear why creating Mana out of thin air on pretty consistent basis is bad for economy? You can do stuff like have an Artifact or some other Mana battery, use Platonic Form to create Tass, get your Mana refunded by ExSuccess, and then move Mana from your Tass to your Mana Battery, then let your Platonic Form crumble. Direct consequence: if your party has an Obrimos, they have no need for any kind of Mana regeneration, because they can refill it at any time by pestering Obrimos to do the Platonic Refill. Hell, you could just sell it to other Mages.

                                                            And yes, you do restore Willpower. Exceptional Successes on spells always restore 1 point of Willpower, no matter which option from the menu you pick.
