        Supernal Summoming has great fluff in 1st Edition!!

        Here's how it works. You roll to get your target successes and if your ST is willing to let you get a named entity, and likely will if your shooting for Supernal Familiar, then you'll get that specific entity. No reason not to if the ST wants to play ball.

        IN GAME, though, Mages have *no idea* what they're going to get, and you can ABSOLUTELY do specific rituals like Mrmboyo said, but, the Mages STILL have no guarantee what will show up.

        The reason is fascinating. Your Summoning is almost analogous to creating a soft-verge or a... Supernal Locus in which Entities might come through, and you attract them like baiting an animal to come out of its hovel. The Summoners book described it as if you attract Supernal entities sense of curiosity about you, and then the first one to take the bait is what shows up.

        So while your rolling dice and accumulating successes you can guess how long you still have to go...

        But the Mage has *no idea* because to them, they're just opening up a resonance to bait something through and hoping it shows up sooner rather than later, it could soon, or *hooouuurs* , with no way of knowing.

                                Oh, I see. Fair enough, let's assume subpar stats then. Most Mages will have Resolve and Composure 3, but let's assume they spent their points on other pretty attributes leaving them with Resolve and Composure 2. They use their free Resistance Attribute on Stamina so they can fight good.

                                The following conditions in play with 5 million simulations:
                                Gnosis 1, Demesne Bonus, No Paradox, Base Duration, No Sleepers, 2 Occult Bonus (4 successes on Occult Roll, thus only 2 point reduction), No Abyssal Protection, Resolve and Composure 2, No Willpower Spent
                                Rank 1: 0%
                                Rank 2: 7%
                                Rank 3: 13%
                                Rank 4: 37%
                                Rank 5: 56%

                                You are correct, at that point summoning a Rank 4-5 supernal being gets dicey at best with a good chance of incurring abyssal incursions. Assuming you don't pass out before then when you get to the 4-5 range anyway.

                                If you really want to test some limits, let's say the Mage spends Willpower on each roll until they are out, which should be equal to their Resolve + Composure. Assuming that happens and let's add a 1 Duration Bonus since we don't want the good ol' Supernal Being getting hurt instantly upon landing:
                                Gnosis 1, Demesne Bonus, No Paradox, 30 Min. Duration, No Sleepers, 2 Occult Bonus, No Abyssal Protection, Resolve & Composure 2, Using All Willpower
                                Rank 1: 0%
                                Rank 2: 1%
                                Rank 3: 6%
                                Rank 4: 26%
                                Rank 5: 40%

                                With all those factors, it is pretty safe at Rank 4, and a moderate chance at Rank 5. It is looking much better than before.

                                Let's say you have fairly decent Willpower, Resolve 2 & Composure 3. Given the same conditions the following is your chance of letting the abyss into your summoning:
                                Gnosis 1, Demesne Bonus, 2 Occult Bonus, 30 Min. Duration, No Paradox, No Abyssal Protection, Resolve 2, Composure 3, Using all Willpower
                                Rank 1: 0%
                                Rank 2: 0%
                                Rank 3: 5%
                                Rank 4: 14%
                                Rank 5: 27%

                                Let's say you went extreme and have an exceptional willpower, Resolve 3 & Composure 4. Given the same conditions the following is your chance of letting the abyss into your summoning:
                                Gnosis 1, Demesne Bonus, 2 Occult Bonus, 30 Min. Duration, No Paradox, No Abyssal Protection, Resolve 3, Composure 4, Using all Willpower
                                Rank 1: 0%
                                Rank 2: 0%
                                Rank 3: 2%
                                Rank 4: 5%
                                Rank 5: 15%

                                All in all, summoning Supernal Beings is a pretty safe bet for even the weakest of mages and a sure thing for moderately willed mages. With an exceptionally willed mage it is nothing to summon an extremely powerful supernal being.

                                Based on my calculations, Abyssal Protection changes your odds by about 2% for every 1 success dedicated. This is pretty laughable given how rare it is for a moderately powered mage to summon a powerful supernal being and let the abyss slip in. You might use it in the event you want to have a long duration for the supernal being to be safe.

                                        So, as long as you don't risk Abyssal Intrusion you succeed. So, subtract the abyssal intrusion from 100% and that is your chance of summoning a Supernal Being of the specified rank successfully. You have no limit on the rolls you can make, you just have a Safe Limit, which is equal to Resolve + Composure + successes allocated for Abyssal Intrusion Protection. Your main goal is to remove as much required successes as possible and increasing your die pool as much as you can.

                                        Based on a Starting Mage with Gnosis 1, Arcana 3, Resolve 3, Composure 2, No Paradox for the week, a Demesne assigned to the path, no duration bonus, 4 successes on their occult roll, no other mage involved, no sleepers, and spending all willpower will summon a Rank 1 Supernal Being in 2 turns with 0% abyssal intrusion chance.
                                        The same mage with the same parameters would summon a Rank 2 Supernal Being in 4 turns with a 1% abyssal intrusion chance (basically meaning 1% of the time required more than the 5 Safe Turns) This means 99% chance of success
                                        The same mage with the same parameters would summon a Rank 3 Supernal Being in 6 turns with a 5% abyssal intrusion chance. 95% chance of success
                                        The same mage with the same parameters would summon a Rank 4 Supernal Being in 8 turns with a 13% abyssal intrusion chance. 87% chance of success
                                        The same mage with the same parameters would summon a Rank 5 Supernal Being in 11 turns with a 25% abyssal intrusion chance. 75% chance of success
