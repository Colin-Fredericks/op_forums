                                                            Originally posted by angryicecream View Post
                                                            Can you elaborate on how Lasting works? Says spells that can't be undone but what does that mean? Anything can be undone, can it not?
                                                            Say that you use a Matter spell to change the shape of a rock. After you are done, do you need any magic for the rock to stay in the shape that you gave it? Not really, the laws of nature will do that for you, it would require more energy to change the rock than to keep it the same, and thus the effects of the spell are Lasting. Or when you throw fire at a pile of hay, causing it to start burning. When your spell ends, the pile will still be burning, and without an external force to snuff it out you would need more energy to stop it than to let it continue, so it keeps burning like a natural fire. The fire will eventually die out, Lasting does not mean "eternal", it just means the effects of the spell will keep on carrying on like a natural effect.

                                                            So wounds from a spell are Lasting not because they will never heal (they will heal naturally), but because they will not spontaneously heal. You would need more magic to heal them again, so it would not make sense for that effect to vanish when your spell does.
