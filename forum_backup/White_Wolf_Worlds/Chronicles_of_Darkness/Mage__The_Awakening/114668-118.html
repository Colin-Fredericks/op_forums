After penalties have been applied for the desired spell factors, the player may move the primary factor up its chart a number of steps equal to the character’s rating in the spell’s highest Arcanum minus one. "

                                                    I don't seem to be able to find it anywhere that states the potency of 1 can be increased without taking a penalty and I couldn't find anything about rotes and potency.
                                                    Well, there are 2 ways of interpreting your first quoted bit of text there. The first way, which I assume is how you're reading it, is that a primary factor of potency simply allows you to raise potency up to the dot rating of the arcana by taking penalties and so conversely you would be unable to raise potency unless it is primary factor, otherwise there would be no difference between primary and normal spell factor. And even further it implies that only the primary factor can be raised by taking penalties.

                                                    The other interpretation is that you can make potency any number between 1 and your arcana rating for free without taking penalties (the 'may' referring to the player having the option, so they don't have to pump out the spell at full power if they don't want to).

                                                    So if the first interpretation were true then we'd expect that in your second quote it'd mention that potency has to be the primary factor in order for you to take the penalty, which isn't the case, it instead says you can raise the potency by taking a penalty, no qualifiers or limitations. So there are a few indicators which imply the first interpretation should be used which are missing, whereas the only issue with taking the second interpretation is that the second quote has the wording '...Potency beyond the first...' which implies that any raising of potency requires a -2 penalty, however it can also be taken to mean that 'the first' means free potency, though that is a bit of a stretch I think it's more reasonable than implying additional swaths of words that by all rights should have been included if they were intended. Given the lack of conflicts and much lower amount of bookkeeping and restrictions with the second interpretation I think it's the only one that can be reasonably taken in the situation.

                                                    In addition rotes won't change anything about potency automatically, the effects of a rote are: you gain access to the mudras yantra (which can be up to about +11 outside of the use of magic for more skill dots), you can cast non-ruling arcana spells without spending mana, you can roll with rote quality if casting at double ritual time from a gimoire (or you are the master who encoded the rote), it cloaks you nimbus and you are treated as having 5 dots in the primary arcanum of the spell for the purposes of free reach only. So the rote just gives more option to raise potency (can spend more reach without risking paradox, which means you can make potency primary factor with less risk and the mudras bonus is a very powerful yantra which means you can raise potency far higher while still being sure you'll succeed the casting roll.)

                                                        I can't help you right now with quotes, but reducing the process to simple steps:
