        Crystal Furnace (Forces •••)
        Practice: Perfecting
        Primary Factor: Duration
        Withstand: N/A
        Recommended Rote Skills: Academics, Crafts, Science

        A rote developed by the entrepreneurial Nameless crafter Rinaldo, who provided aid to vampire hunters in exchange for their quarry's ashes. It provides the ideal conditions to crystallize carbon into diamonds. In addition, each level of Potency halves the time needed to create them.

        After extracting the carbon from the vampire ashes, Rinaldo was able to transform them into highly sought after yantras. The Thrice Great use them in their rituals involving Venus, a few Libertines employ experiments to create supernatural quantum computers. Some mages instead use this rote to transform the ashes of the fallen into memorial diamonds, as a final way to honor their contributions to the Diamond Orders.

        +1 Reach: The time is instead reduced to a quarter per Potency.        ​

            Glámr's Vigil (Space ••)
            Practice: Veiling
            Primary Factor: Duration
            Withstand: N/A
            Recommended Rote Skills: Expression, Intimidation, Subterfuge

            It is said that when Grettir slayed the dreaded Glámr, the revenant cursed him to be tormented with visions of his ghostly eyes in the dark, wearing at his sanity. Inspired by this tale, the Panoptes Déraciné developed a rote to keep her adversaries in line by the mere fear of being watched.

            Subjects feel like there is a presence constantly watching them, just out of the edge of their vision or in the one corner they haven't checked. This afflicts them with the Paranoid condition.

            +1 Reach: The paranoia grows so strong that subjects tie certain objects to their mysterious watcher, such as windows, reflective surfaces, ringing telephones or closed doors. The mage can specify up to [Potency] Keys that grant the Shaken condition.

            +1 Reach: The spell can be fine tuned to only work when certain Key is present. The feeling might only trigger when they are in the presence of another member of their cabal, when inside small spaces, when outside of a settlement or even when the subject is alone.

                I made a bunch

                Deja Fu (Time ooo)
                Practice: Weaving
                Primary Factor: Potency
                Summon from the past circumstances that led to the last instance of bashing damage on your target. Potency must be equal to the amount of damage inflicted. The target experiences a stutter of time in which it relives the moment of infliction. The damage properties and circumstances remain the same, even if a bullet is spent or a blade is broken.
                +1 Reach: you may instead duplicate damage from any instance in the scene.

                Lightning Rod (Fate ooo)
                Practice: Weaving
                Primary Factor: Duration
                The target of the spell becomes rife with bad fortune; if a negative effect occurs in the vicinity of the target, it must preferentially include the target. Spilled drinks, clumsy right hooks, embarrassing faux pas all seem to befall the target instead of their intended recipients (if any).
                +2 Reach: The effect may influence supernatural powers with a clash of wills.

                Proxy (Space ooo)
                Practice: Fraying
                Primary Factor: Duration
                Balance an object larger than your silhouette on a spatial precipice, allowing you to reflexively (wits+resolve?) swap places with it a number of times equal to potency. If the structure of the object becomes destroyed, the spell falls apart as the balance is disrupted. This doesn’t prevent you from being targeted by supernatural effect, but does allow skirting of aimed spells and firearms you are aware of.
                Add Forces: inertia and other forces can be transferred to the proxy.

                Godiva (Forces o)
                Practice: Compelling
                Primary Factor: Potency
                Eases the friction between substances, causing simple fastenings that are under tension to become undone, such as knots, velcro and buttons. At potency 3 more complex fastenings like buckles and straps can be affected.

                Essence Sway (Spirit oo)
                Practice: Perfecting
                Primary Factor: Potency
                Channelling essence equal to potency into a target may illicit changes in its behaviour or nature. A stout wall may be weakened by the essence of decay, a frantic person may be calmed by tranquil essence, and a meek businessperson could be made to viciously close a deal with predatory essence. The amount of essence must overcome the target’s resolve, composure or structure, whichever is higher.

                Bi-Location (Space ooo)
                Practice: Fraying
                Primary Factor: Duration
                You precariously straddle two different locations and are present in each physically. Taking actions simultaneously in each location is difficult and you take a -2 to any attempt unless under the effect of One Mind, Two Thoughts. As you are present in both locations you can take advantage of your surrounds, for example climbing stairs in one location to seemingly levitate in another, or submerging yourself in water in one location to walk through fire with less risk in another. Otherwise, each action you do is performed in both locations.

                +1 reach: Your movement can be asymmetrical in each location, allowing you to act in different directions. Facing yourself in the same room for example.

                    I don't know if I'll ever get to play this character, but I came up with the idea of the Acanthus who seeks to help ghosts pass on to wherever they go after being stuck here. To facilitate this she has a praxis to give her information on the next steps they need to take on their journey.

                    A Path to Somewhere (Death • + Fate •)

                    Practice: Knowing
                    Primary Factor: Potency
                    Suggested Rote Skills: Empathy, Occult, Survival
                    In order to pass on to whatever comes after their existence post death a ghost must resolve their anchors. This spell allows the mage to gain insight not only into what a ghost’s anchors are, but also what the next step to resolving it might be.
                    For each level of potency the mage learns one of a ghost's anchors and the next step that can be taken towards resolving that anchor. The anchors chosen are always the strongest anchor the ghost has, and then works down to their weakest.
                    +1 Reach The mage may allocate potency into learning more about a single anchor, as opposed to learning about a new one. The mage gains insight into the next steps that should be taken towards resolving that anchor for each potency past the first allocated towards it
                    +1 Reach The mage may learn about any anchor they wish, instead of the strongest working their way down.

                                Gearing up to play a Tamer of Blood and wanted to make a Praxis that worked for her, so here we are!

                                Known by Blood (Life •)

                                Practice: Knowing
                                Primary Factor: Potency
                                Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Science, Survival
                                By understanding someone’s blood you can understand them. This spell allows the mage to analyze blood to learn about the person the blood came from. The mage may ask a number of questions equal to the potency of this spell about the source of any blood she has, the answers gained through this spell give information about how the subject was when they lost the blood, not as they currently are. The following are example questions;
                                • What species is the subject?
                                • What sex is the subject?
                                • What is the total number of physical attribute dots the subject has?
                                • What illnesses is the subject suffering from, if any?
                                • What sort of diet does the subject have?
                                • Is the subject postpubescent or prepubescent?
                                • Was the subject under significant stress when the blood was separated from the body?
                                Add Space ••: By using Sympathetic casting, the questions give information on the subject based on how they are now, not how they were when they lost the blood.
                                +1 Reach The mage can determine if there are multiple sources of blood. If they have Space • they are able to tell which blood came from the same source.
                                +1 Reach For the duration of the spell the mage recognizes the blood in people. They can determine if someone they see is the source of the blood, or if the person has had a blood transfusion, or similar procedures, from the original source of blood.
                                +1 Reach The mage can ask for the specific number of dots in each physical attribute.

                                    We’ve Paid the Price (Death ••)
                                    Practice: Veiling
                                    Primary Factor: Duration
                                    Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Stealth, Subterfuge
                                    Those who sail along the rivers of the Underworld without a boatmen attract the attention of the Chthonians that live in the waters of the rivers, but, having paid a boatman for their services acts as a sort of shield against the Chthonians. While the reason for this is not known, this spell allows mages to trick the Chthonians into believing a boatman has been paid.
                                    For the duration of this spell, the vessel that serves as the subject of this spell appears to have a boatman that has been paid to provide safe passage along the rivers of the Underworld.

                                            Dead Man's Tales (Death •••, Mind •••)
                                            Practice: Weaving
                                            Primary Factor: Duration
                                            Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Occult, Persuasion

                                            Not all deaths create a ghost, and sometimes a case has gone cold for so long that a specter might not even exist anymore. In those situations, a Guarding may choose to reanimate dead neurons, restoring vestiges of consciousness to a corpse.

                                            When used on a dead subject, it restores their body and reanimates their brain enough to answer any questions of the caster. It answers honestly and remember all it knew in life perfectly, but their answers will be colored by personal experience and biases. Because it is an echo of a mind, it cannot give an opinion it had not considered in life, cannot learn new information, make new theories or even remember anything that happened since their time of death. Many an Arrow warn of leaving bodies of dead comrades behind, all too aware of the danger of their knowledge falling in the wrong hands.

                                            New experiences are the font of creativity, when seeking inspiration, break your routine.

                                            The Agathos Kai Sophos, an Acanthus Legacy of strategists (Mind/Time)

                                                            Disassemble (Matter ••••)
                                                            Practice: Unraveling
                                                            Primary Factor: Potency
                                                            Withstand: Durability
                                                            Recommended Rote Skills: Crafts, Intimidation, Science

                                                            A rote developed by the Vidantus, as a way to provide less than lethal solutions for talons. The targeted object has all it's parts fall apart, landing quietly and harmlessly on the ground. A gun's components will be separated down to the smallest piece, a section of wall will break apart into lumps of bricks and cement, ropes, kevlar and tapestries will unravel into individual clumps of threads; armor will have it's plates fall off and even cars will be left unusable in moments. Talons have found it a great, if not particularly subtle aid for rapid infiltrations and sabotage.
