    The broken topic! Power hungry players, let's discuss. (NECRO)

    05-12-2016, 07:10 AM
    Hi folks! this is going to be a topic in between both questions and considerations on the spells or combo that u might consider broken/overpowered ... here is my list but i want to hear others opinion as well. Help this poor narrator gamemaster avoid his players from simply bypass everything.. (jk, i am more than used to)
    This topic is not (only) purely intended for a min/max lab and powerplayer room but for understanding and bettering the power differences and problems with the setting.
    In my mind, i always try to balance things out and if something might be abused i have to find a way to turn it arount the players or build a setting or story that won't permit players to abuse it- NOT frustrating their actions and spells, but rather offering a better chance of challenge and reward after. Like for example, there is a reason why mastigos or mages with space simply don't go around casting hexes to anyone and mind controling an entire city (i'm exaggerating of course), and that's probabl because every mage learn at least 1 dot of space or is well aware of the danger of that arcana.- Why the idea of mind controling a werewolf or a vampire from ur sanctum isn't a good one? paradox risk, finding the right connection or simpathy, haveing to deal with an ENTIRE pack or coterie and all their allies (spirits with unusual powes that can contest the mind control) ecc...

    1) time 2- veil of moments- i'm not sure if i understand this spells correctly, but it seems to shield the mage from basically everything, from poison to conditions to physical trauma. So if i stab the mage that has this spell on... do i kill him or not? does the knife slice through his flesh and "ruins" it or does the time stop permit the mage to basically be immune to ANYTHING that time might stop . I discussed it with my players, and we come to the conclusion that the mage does still sufers damage from direct attacks, but he doesn't worsen with time so for example if he gets sliced on his wrists he won't lose 1 healt dot per turn or if he is set on fire, he won't suffer damage on consqeuient turns... but he DOES get hit or stabbed or shot ecc... so the damage resister on his health bar.
    If on the other hand im wrong-- either i will house rule this spell or make it that for the duration the mage doesn't suffer any damage, but at the end of the spell every conditon or healt level he would have suffered is inflicted at once on him.

    2) the acanthus defense- i'm untouchable !!" seriosuly, if an acanthus really wants he can become impossible to hit or almost, at least according to the rules. Since fate armor attainmen adds fate to his defense, and time acceleration adds potency to his defense when dodging, this means that if an acanthus has both effects on has a huge amount of dice for dodging and probably won't suffer a scratch for as long as he is dodging. Did i calculate it right? For exmpale, if an acanthus with fate 3 and time 3 has acceleration and doges, assuming his base defense is just 4 he would dodge at 20 dices ? Do i add all the fate and time dots to defense?
    let's do the math defense 2+ fate armor 3+ time acceleration effect 3= 10 defense, when dodging 20. 0_0
    according to the rules.. yep.
    i would rule this spell as "vulgar" and a breaking point and quiescence for humans in the area, if they see something like a guy dodging like a celerity vampire elder.
    Luckily, a simple mage with prime dispellation.. and your dodging ability simply disappear

    3) In general, space. Space is one of the most powerful arcana in the book. Not only it allows u to scry or block the passage/attacks of enemies alltogether or seal a person in a dimensional pocket impossible to escape (all with space 3), but the simpathy casting--- isnt't it a little too easy to actually pull off when u have the components? Like, seriously tell me if i get it right. Let's assume i have some kind of connection with a vampire or another supernatural being.. i cast on him a hex or something else and all i have to do is whitstand simpathy+1/ Or his resolve/whatever attribute +1? According to the rules, when u whitsand for multiple factors (Like simpathy casting for example) u simply add +1 for each additional whitstanding to the HIGHER whitstand rating. So if my connection is a strong one like an object or a body sample, i could always find and cast on a poor guy /vampire/promethean ecc... all for his whitstand attribute+1? Am i right or wrong here? tell me please ...
    also... seriosuly to target a guy i only require to have met him maybe a couple of times and build a representational tool? Like a doll? Or do i also need body sample or personal belongings to target him.

    How i solve it in my head- space is simply broken and essential for all mages. Even newbies and non combatant learn at least 1 dot of space
    Vs other supernaturals- since usually the mage is going to cast with a ritual casting on simpathy, most supernaturals will have time to react/move their allies/influences- ask for help. A vampire can mobilize humans or call for help his allies, a werewolf might step in the umbra (and thus it would be impossible for the mage to reach him unless he has spirit right?)... etc.. ecc..
    also i would house rule that if the subject of the spell is in a crowded area where humans gather and the magic is too "flashy" the effect would be paradoxical and the abyss would reach to hit the arrogant mage... what do u think?


    3) turn momentum, forces 3... simply allow mages to dodge anything? Since everything in physics and reality needs momentum to hit a target...

    How do i solve it in my head- either the mage needs to see the targets before he cast the spells (so in 1 turn he has to activate and decide which objects he will deflect) or he also has to apply scale and size factors for it to be effective. Like dodging a car or a werewolf would require adcanced scale. What do u think? And of course, if a mage use this in a place full of humans shooting at him POINT BLANK and not hitting him... it should call for a paradox roll.

    4) Does the spell 2 death.. suppress life.. suppress life??
    like i can simply cast it on a dude and he will faint and collapse like a dead corpse for the duration.. that seems broken to me or at least it should call for a whitstand... or maybe i'm just not understanding the spell at all and the subject "appears" like a zombie but still walks and act normally, his heart doesn't beat and the body temperatures drop.
    Same with ghost gate. It seems to imply that a mage simply touch a person and can teleport him in Twilight without even whitstand the effect (?)

    5) Hung spells-- with fate added. Anyone here who is or was a dungeons master knows the danger and power of spells like contingency... but how many spells exactly can i hang? The spell says spells equal to potency so a potency 3 hang could suspend maximum 3 spells at once? Do i have to cast them all at once?
    And most important.. the factors of the hanged spells can be modified right? I can apply reach, factors and penalities so that when the spell is relead it will have advanced potency, duration ecc... am i right?

    Luckily hung spells are easily to become double edged knives.. if the player doesn't specify well planned conditions he will suffer from hang spells released at inappropriate times or nor released at all. (example "it will activate when i get attacked!" but if someone ambush the mage and he doesn't realize it, he will get hit normally, or if he gets simply dominated or charmed).
    also.. do hang spells activate in the same turn if multiple, and does it take an action to have them? Or its simply a reflexive thing and they pop up when the condition sets in?

    6) Prime. I LOVE prime. seriosuly. It's one of my favourite arcana. It solve magically all of the above problems with the swing of a wand. Did u hang spells, prepare ur force field, cast ur magic armor on u ecc. ecc.??... well son i have bad news for u..

    what do u think? what are ur personal broken combo/ overpowered stats or spells that u house rule or simply have to deal with?

    I like a world where things even if unfair, get balanced (or not, like real world). For exmple, vampires might not be able to curse u while they sit in their havens, but they can easily ruin ur life and moving entire "armies" of contacts, allies, ghouls.. ecc.. and if they really wanted to give a mage an hard time.. they could just use humans! Humans are just the worst for a mage, especially if in a crowd. While u have to think twice before call a lighting or curse the leech, he can simply catch u in a street with tons of people and rip ur earth out while obfuscated (ecc..ecc.. the possibilities are endless).
    werewolves have packs, gifts and fucking spirits. Powerful spirits like totems can contest the arcana and get angry if they find out someone is messing with their guardians. not even talking about the actual act of kiling a werewolf since they regenerate everything and can rage in hulk mode ignoring mind control too (almost).

    What do u guys think? Show us the horrors of ur goup/players!

      Pun-Pun like Mastigos trick:
      Klara the Mastigos likes Psychic Domination, Enhance Skill, and Enhance Attribute. She likes them so much that she picks them as her Rotes, which key off her favorite Skill.
      Klara casts Enhance Skill using her Rote, targeting her Favorite Skill. She pays Mana and Reach to push it over Human limits. Her Favorite Skill is higher, so her Psychic Domination, Enhance Skill and Enhance Attribute dicepools are now bigger. Which means that she can cast Enhance Skill again, with more Potency! This means that her Skill will become bigger, by a point or two than in previous casting, allowing her to repeat this until she arrives at Infinity.
      After gaining Infinite Skill, Klara decides to become Infinitely Smart. She casts Enhance Attribute, using her Infinite Dicepool to set her Traits at Infinity. After becoming Infinitely Smart, she understands now what should be done to humanity.
      She casts Psychic Domination, with Duration set to Indefinite, and pays Reach to get most powerful command ever imagined. She puts Infinite Factors into everything, so whole Universe is covered by her spell, which has only one command:
      WATCH. CAT. VIDEOS.
      And then, Klara the Mastigos ascends, becoming Dark Mittens Mittens, Exarch of Control Through Kittens.

                      Present as Past - you automatically win initiative against everyone but other Time mages. With Temporal Sympathy, you can use Temporal Summons to turn your enemy back into a completely human child, or a Rank 0 mote, anything. As long as you can beat a Clash of Wills and get enough dice, Temporal Summons can defeat anything by rendering it into a powerless form unable to fight back.

                      When taken together, Time Mastery means that you always go first, you're never surprised, and when you do act, you win. If you're Acanthus, then expect to get lots of Fate boosts to make sure you never fail the rolls either.
