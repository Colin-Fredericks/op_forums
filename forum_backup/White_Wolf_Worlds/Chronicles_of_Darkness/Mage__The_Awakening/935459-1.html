    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
    I have a real problem on this front - How to not make cabal split because of jobs? Literal example from last game session - In group we have academic profesor, collage student and nurse. First two are free on weekends, so cabals 'active time' is moved there. But nurse have shifts any day of week. How can mage in jobs like that 'miss' they work on cabals call and not be fired?

    Now, I'm coming from D&D, but ... do you mean in-game, or just from a theoretical standpoint? If someone makes a character, and this character cannot join the cabal's mystery-hunts because the character has to work all the time, and always prioritises work above mysteries ... then that's a bad character ill-suited for the game? Sounds like making a D&D-character that doesn't want to adventure and have the player say "Well my character has no reason to join the party", in which case most DM's would say "Well make one that has a reason to go on adventures". If they prioritise mysteries, they'd have to find a way to deal with work.

    More theoretically, wouldn't it to some textent be mitigated by just making magical excuses? Call in sick when you're investigating mysteries. Use Life to give yourself signs of having been ill/injured when you return to work. Use Mind to convince your boss that your signifcant number of sick days is not a problem. Or use Mind to "renegotiate" the terms of your employment, to get more vacation days and a more flexible schedule. Whenever your boss is annoyed, use any appropriate arcanum to do something really great at work, to smooth things over. Or use magic to ensure that when you aren't there, stuff goes wrong. Make it so everything just runs smoother when you're there. Maybe you could help your boss out with a personal problem. Whatever works, depending on the job.

    And if you don't have the right arcanum, maybe the rest of the cabal can help out.

    If the mage isn't willing to do that - or if it doesn't work in the long run - wouldn't the mage just choose? Remain at the job, don't go looking for mysteries, and then don't get a lot better at magic. Become some unimportant mage. Or just change jobs to a job where you actually can have a flexible schedule.

                                                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post

                                                            Well, it works with most characters concepts, yes. But this particular Nurse is also from Guardians of Veil, so she strife to not 'over use' magic. Have idea how to help her?

                                                            I guess the first question should be: do you, as a Storyteller, want this to be an issue? If you have her job call her when she's working with her cabal, what do you hope to get out of it? Do you expect that her character will ditch work, or ditch her cabal? If you think she'll ditch her cabal, no questions asked ... is that desireable for the other players? If it would be, I'd ask the player why she's made a character that prioritises her mortal life above her magical life in all regards. And why does this character choose to do so? Is there a reason? What does the player want to get out of making a character like that? Can it lead to something that's enjoyable for everyone?

                                                            If the player doesn't really have a great idea, maybe you could work something out. If she does prioritise her cabal, then her work-related issues could be something for the entire group to solve. Perhaps she needs the money for some magical project that will benefit the entire cabal, so perhaps her cabal can help her set up some scenario that proves how indispensible she is. Perhaps they can work to get her boss to be replaced. Or something along those lines.

                                                            Or if no one is really interested in that particular aspect of the character's life, you could just choose not to call her in. If you need an in-game reason for it and not just Storyteller fiat, someone in the cabal could be casting Fate spells to have her not be called in. Or use Mind to have the boss just choose others first. Or just use mundane means to get into her boss's good graces. There's always bribery or extortion, as well.
