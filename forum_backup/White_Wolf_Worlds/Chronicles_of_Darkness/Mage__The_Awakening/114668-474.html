                                                            Persona and a lot of other Yantras that are dependent on the caster are all represented through Merits in one way or another, which naturally disallows Supernal beings from using them because they don't have Merits. I also think Path Tools might not work because while all summoned Supernal beings align with a Path, they don't actually have Paths.
                                                            Most other Yantras are mystically significant in their own regard rather than reliant on the caster. Concentration is the only one that sticks out to me as caster-centric despite not being flavoured by the caster.

                                                            That said, there's something pure about the idea that they only use their natural dice pool and not any Yantras. It means instant casting truly is instant and their dice pools are naturally large. Additionally, by setting an upper bound on their dice pools, their spells generally comes across as more direct; more here and now. They have trouble pushing up spell factors to ridiculous levels and instead generally go to the lower ends of the Advanced factors if the Standard factors aren't enough. Even Potency can be increased with their infinite Reach by swapping primary factor, at the cost of having Duration be one turn or scene (before spending dice).

                                                            I kinda like that. And if necessary, they generally have enough dice to spend on spell factors anyway so it's certainly not a hard limit. But on the other hand, it's always cool to have the Trial take the form of a material offering that the entity uses as a Sacrament to fulfil your request. I suppose that, if fitting or if required by Arcana Attainments, I'd allow for Yantras but generally avoid them as to not disturb the percieved purity of direct and unaided casting.
