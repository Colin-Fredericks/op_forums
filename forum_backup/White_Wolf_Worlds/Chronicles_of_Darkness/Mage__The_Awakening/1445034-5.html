        Later than promised but it is time to tune up the names. After contemplating I figured I liked the names to have a little poetry to them. "Conqueror's and experts confidence" it is. I also realize that names that are like commands or statements are not my style. "Concession's surrender" it is. The fourth Attainment is difficult though. I want something that summarizes the effect, the "proven master" and "the superior master" don't seem to do it completely and I want to be succinct. If I can't find an apt title by the end of this then I will default to "the superior master". Maybe "the superior wielder"? I am satisfied with the last one.

        I am question if my clarification of two Yantras was even necessary or if I just burdened it down with unnecessary complications.

        Back to naming. Pledges. The first one will be "pledge of lawfulness", then "pledge of magnanimity", then "pledge of parley", then "pledge of retreat", then "pledge of victory".

        Finally some nicknames. "Cheaters" will of course will be derogatory and by the resentful who still dispute the legality of the Legacy. "Tyrants", "champions" and others depends on politics and whether they personally effected the speaker. The only nickname so common as to be tantamount to a formal name is "foregone winners/victors" or just the "foregone".

                                First Attainment: Conqueror's Confidence

                                Every Duelist was literally-soul forged in victory, leaving them aware of arcane conflict in ways even the Mastigos and Obrimos struggle to understand. To see the dance of competing Supernal signs and symbols, and to know and appreciate what it means when one of them is their own will forms the basis of their first lesson. The burst of power from further breaking their opponents in and the refreshment from winning the Duel are the same in the Mysteries of the Legacy. When the cosmos first held their hand up in victory the Duelist learned how to consolidate that into Doors during the Duel.

                                Every time the Duelist opens a Door they may invest some or all of the bonus made from doing so into forging additional Doors. This decision must be made before the next spell and cannot be reversed. Every five bonus dice invested can be made into a Door with each use of this Attainment, which is reflexive and costs one mana.

                                Optional Fate one: pledge of Lawfullness.

                                As Champion founded the Legacy she realized she was paranoid and didn't trust in the referee. So she conscripted a Fae to do the job instead. Just as she did she realized that she established the foundation allowing for different Pledges, representing different enigmatic guardians of Mists and Thorns, to bring strange possibilities to the Duel.

                                The Duelist can modify the Sacred Dueling space before engaging the the actual Duel by using an instant action and one mana to invoke a Pledge. Multiple Pledges may be used. Agreement with an opponent on using a Pledge will circumvent the mana cost and allow some parts of the Pledges to be renegotiated, but only if the opponent actually understands the terms, even if he thinks he does. Naturally, knowledge of the Pledges are spreading rapidly to stop any trickery, but maybe some Nameless didn't get the memo. The Pledge of Lawfulness will take away a Door from the an attempting cheater and lose the Duel for a (very short term) successful cheater. Fate will clear away mundane interferences to the rules and magical ones get two clashes of wills of Gnosis and Fate, one for each contestant. The rules are to keep the violence in the magical simulation and to not arrange any external aid or distraction. Further rules can be negotiated.

                                            Second Attainment: Expert's Confidence

                                            After learning how to get a premature taste of victory a Duelist takes a look at how will gives rise to magic, then reverses the relationship. Thereafter every Duel benefits from insulation of the will. At the start of every Duel the Duelist gains Doors equal to her Prime dots.

                                            Optional Fate two: Pledge of Magnanimity

                                            As Champion considered her first Pledge that seemed to account for everything, she realized that it really only accounted for the letter of the law rather than the spirit. The world was full enough of cruelty already, so why add bad sportsmanship to it? After the semantic monster of the first Pledge she called forth a subtle adjudicator of relationships to represent the next.

                                            If the Triumphant abuses the Defeated then the Triumphant condition will be lost without a beat. If already lost Fate will reciprocate abuse, though alternative or unequal punishments can be negotiated. With meaningful reparations the Triumphant can relieve the Defeated of his condition which still grants a beat. The reward for doing so is a Persistent Triumphant condition that allows one chance for every social fail for a social save that preserves the condition. Alternative rewards can be negotiated for, any mundane or arcane condition gained from Exceptional Success is fair game.

                                                Third Attainment: Concession's Surrender

                                                Between studying the gaps in focus for a mage trying to negotiate and how the Prime Arcanum regulates vows as opposed to the Fate Arcanum, Champion figured out how to conflate the symbols of concession and surrender. Terrible Thorns of light and blood spew forth to render the High Speech runes of promises visible and bound, digging into the innards of speakers. This even reveals previous promises and payments. The drawback is that once unleashed the power doesn't discriminate, so a Duelist better not concede.

                                                Pay one mana and roll Gnosis and Prime as a reflexive action once per turn to make the opponent seal their selected offer to the Duelist by sacrificing their own Doors, but should the roll fail the Duelist must seal the selected offer with her own Doors. The opponent may contest the roll with Gnosis and Prime as well, additional rolls with different dice pools may occur according to the mechanics of whatever shielding spell or Attainment the opponent is using.

                                                Once unleashed neither mage may make further offers without paying the price for it for a number of rounds equal to the Duelist's Prime dots as the magic spreads throughout like fire, but just like how even fire can be mastered the winner of the clash of wills is immune if they scored an Exceptional Success. This immunity lasts until the winner's Door opens. Despite how brutal and restrictive it looks the Thorns don't inconvenience movement and disappear when the Attainment expires.

                                                Optional Fate three: Pledge of Parley

                                                Champion soon realized that without some regulation no one was ever going to negotiate with her mid-Duel ever again, so she called in another kind of peacekeeping Fae. This one resolved standoffs from small disputes to deadly war, though in this case it won't be speaking, just facilitating speaking. On the other hand, Champion didn't make this Attainment to delay "Concession's Surrender" forever.

                                                For one time or as many as negotiated a mage may call for a time out. The remaining uses for this privilege is tracked independently between the opponents. Once used the Fae dispels all spells extant and upcoming for the cease-fire. It lasts until both agree to comence again or someone is just stalling for time, the one who didn't call for a cease-fire is allowed only one minute to find a reason or make a plan that is more than stalling. This may even immediately waste a use of the privilege, additional or alternative end conditions can be negotiated.

                                                During the Cease-fire offers can be made and accepted in relative safety. "Concession's Surrender" cannot be used here, any lingering traces are removed and any offer made during this time is immune until the mage making them runs out of uses of the pledge. Once vulnerable "Concession's Surrender" no longer costs mana and gains the rote quality when used on those offers from cease-fire. Given that there may be dozens of rejected offers and the Duelist will probably get an Exceptional Success, this may lead to disaster.

                                                            Fourth Attainment: Superior Wielder

                                                            It is ironic how the realm of pure truth can be rather reductive. The differing abilities and specialties of the mages involved in the Duel is summarized as "this one is more right than the other". To Champion, this is confirmation that the universe is ruled by strength rather than morality, so someone must speak truth to power. Contemplating this, she wondered if she could divide that binary assessment by the Arcana and enjoy the benefits early like the first Attainment. Her remaining question is if this made it more or less reductive.

                                                            The only requirements of this Attainment is opening a Door in the Duel using an attack spell. The Duelist automatically gains an immunity to the respective Arcanum she attacked with. This can work with multiple Arcana in the same successful attack spell, permitting high-risk and reward Dueling. There is no further effect if the Duelist already has the respective immunity, the Supernal agrees that the Duelist made her point.

                                                            Optional Fate four: Pledge of Retreat

                                                            Upon her ascent to greatness, Champion realized that many would rather resort to sabotage than agree to a doomed Duel against her and that sometimes she had little heart to finish those that panic in the Duel against her. Sometimes an arrogant asshole realizes that he is out of his depth and wishes he compromised instead.

                                                            To solve this Champion enlisted the help of a Fae that oversees surrender negotiations, foreclosures and regretful promises. Those that wish to escape can attack themselves with their own magic. The Pledge requires that the mage in someway makes it obvious that he is doing it to herself. No immunities prevent this. Progress is made for every sacrificed immunity, including the one for the third Attainment. The default target number is ten minus the Fate dots of the retreating mage. If the attack roll is a Failure than it will be clear that the mage tried something, though the mage will still lose the immunities and make no progress. A Dramatic Failure will still lose the Door. An Exceptional Success will keep the Door even as the immunities are shed.

                                                            Meeting the target number avails nothing if mage actually lost the last Door. Upon retreating any further Social Menuavering or Duel Arcane will on the relevant issue will use the remaining Doors.
