    Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post
    The moment Shackle used Dream to ask the question, I thought "Wait, the only place in Astral space with that answer is the dreams of the Annunaki, oh no !" , but he did get a clue: he is touched by Joining. On the positive side, paying tribute to Voidfisher and actually distancing himself from Jessicca is an upturn to his mental wellness. Provided he doesn't take the Abyssal offer, there is only madness that way.

    I'm glad Blackeye was able to help the father and daughter, it was a very Wise choice. Since she only has one Anchor left, and it's not the strongest one, hopefully Jack Volume can use the Pass On Ceremony so she isn't consumed by the Underworld.

    Dio is on the hunt, I'm curious to see where this cat and mouse game will lead.

    Chron has now placed Aurora in quite a dilemma. She craves power, but would definitely not trust the Tick-Tock Men with a sample of her blood and hair, much less want to be a minion in a cult. But she still wishes for the secrets of initiation. Dexter being able to read and answer the Divination questions was a great way to establish him as dangerous, the clock bomb was also cool. It was also intriguing that Dexter only told him what one timer meant, setting up for them to make more deals for more information.
    More on Shackle and Noface next session.

    Jack Volume is pretty new to this Sin-Eater thing, and Blackeye and he didn't really get much of a chance to chat yet, so I'm afraid the daughter is most likely destined to go to the Underworld. If the characters ever get around to going down there, we might see her again.

    I've implied in the past that the first clock on Chron's wrist reached 0 when he handed the deathshead watch to Aurora. A decision was made, and destinies were altered. The second clock stopped when he joined the House of Ariadne, his own destiny heading in a different direction.


    Originally posted by lbeaumanior View Post
    Phoog
    I dont get it if Pentex can make / buy Perfected Tin ammo, why can't they also get Perfected Iron and instead use "weird lumps of metal that have been crudely hammered into bullet shapes"? Those can not work with real weapons, it will literally blow up in the barrel permanently damaging the weapon and probably maiming the user's hand.


    The rest, great as usual.
    Pentex is not infallible. While there are supernatural elements in their organisation (the Seers have a stake, as does the God-Machine, and probably some vampires too), most of this massive company is run by normal humans who are doing their best to deal with/and profit off supernatural entities. They're not well informed, and their facilities have a habit of exploding, so they aren't the best at retaining expertise.

    It's not clear where they got the Perfected Tin from, but since it's the only bit of Perfected kit they have it's safe to assume they haven't figured out how to manufacture it and probably bought them from a third party. Or maybe one of the Seer wranglers gave it to them as a gift.

    As for the Cold Iron...I'm pretty sure regular iron would work pretty well against faeiries and Changelings (it just has to be pure iron, and it has to be hand-forged. Not necessarily cold-hammered). This is a case of Pentex believing the legends a bit too fervently, and using cumbersome ammunition because they don't really know any better. Changelings and the Fae are definitely not the most common type of entity they encounter, either. That would be ghosts, spirits and vampires (werewolves are also up there, but less numerous). They've got pretty advanced protocols for handling them.

    Madmarcus is correct, there'd be at least one oldschool souped up musket-style gun that is designed to fire non-standard bullets in the armoury that Shackle grabbed. Licence of the Dayshift would raise the same objections, and probably figure out which gun the Cold Iron bullets could be used with. Or Blackeye could sort it out easily with their second Forgemaster Attainment.

        There is, notably, a slight difference in nomenclature between 1e and 2e Changeling: what 1e called "cold iron" — iron that is pure enough to be called "iron" rather than steel or another significant alloy — is just called "iron" in 2e, while "cold iron" in 2e is what 1e referred to as "hand-forged iron"; 2e also extended the aggravated damage effect of cold/hand-forged iron to the Lost as well as the Gentry, whereas in 1e changelings treated damage from hand-forged iron as no greather than that from cold iron.
