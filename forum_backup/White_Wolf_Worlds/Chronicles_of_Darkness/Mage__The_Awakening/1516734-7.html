    Chapter 64
    11/14/2025
    The Cabal had made a decision; Beria would be the Praetorian they tried to leverage against Conquest. He would be easy enough to find at town hall, but one problem did present itself. Giger had been seen on T.V. not too long ago acting as a terrorist. To try and get around the problem being a wanted terrorist in town hall would bring, Giger rewrote his own history so that he had plastic surgery a decade ago, altering his face. This spell released a powerful Abyssal manifestation in the form of an Abyssal Revision environmental tilt <Nameless and Accursed page 101> that covered his entire apartment. The cabal decided to leave and cast any more spells they needed to before meeting Beria on the drive over. This rewritten version of Giger had driven Leopold off however with his obsessive need to perfect his appearance, and so the cabal had to drive themselves. <Likely the first and last time Giger will be behind a wheel due to his own will.> On the drive Maxwell reached out to Alpha Centauri, who had previously agreed to give them information on resurrection that would be deemed ethical by the Golden Order to let him know they were ready. Alpha Centauri suggested they meet that evening and Maxwell agreed, giving him the address to Eye of God's apartment.

    Once the Cabal arrived at city hall all of their peripheral mage sights detected something, and opening their eyes they saw that town hall had been replaced by a massive pyramid with an angel looking down over it. They realized that this was an Exarchal Aedes known as the Omni-Caste, details on this within the Golden Order are limited, but Giger was told about it, if he wants to progress within the ranks of the Egregore he must unravel the secrets of this Aedes and present his findings to the Mysterium.

    Once inside the cabal made it past a security checkpoint that had been set up, except for Giger who was immediately recognized as the terrorists who had bombed downtown Los Angeles. He did manage to escape and use Shifting Sands to return to his apartment before he cast the Rewrite History spell. He cast it again, and changed nothing except for when he had gotten the plastic surgery on his face, changing the timeline one week ago instead of years ago.

    Beria was easy to find once inside town hall. His real name was found online and through the research Chronos did on Giger’s behalf and the office was labeled. The cabal lingered outside his office, listening in to a phone call he was having where he encouraged whoever was on the other side of the phone to engage in an undescribed behavior against the residence of Korea Town, telling them no one would care, that any repercussions wouldn't hurt their career, and any if anything did happen to them it would be more like a paid vacation than punishment. Once the call was over the cabal entered. Beria was completely alone, and wasn't happy to see them. He looked over the computer at the reception desk to find they had no appointment and told them to call his office to make one once his secretary had returned. Giger told them why they were there and Maxwell, noticing cameras, created a small, localized EMP blast to shut them and any recording devices down. Beria didn't seem bothered by this, sitting back in the chair and enjoying some coffee and a donut while informing the cabal he'd be sending them a bill for the damaged property.

    The cabal tried negotiations with Beria, starting with lying to him about the threat Jötunn posed to his ministry and telling him Conquest didn’t care. Beria laughed and said neither did he, that he had determined Jötunn was only a threat to the Golden Order, cast a spell quickly, and determined that was still the case. In fact he did this mockingly several times throughout the conversation. So, the cabal shifted tactics, trying to convince him that he could convince Conquest his visions had changed, but Beria wasn't interested in this, calling it “their dirty work”. The conversation went in circles for a bit before a plan was laid out that Beria accepted; it was revealed that Beria did not think Conquest would be able to defeat Jötunn unscathed, but he also did not think he could dethrone her and take her place at the top of the Ministry even if she was wounded due to Abea's influence. Therefore, once Abea's power had been destabilized by the Arcane Luminaries of the Expanse, Beria would act, revealing that his Time magic reveals a new future where the local Praetorians are destroyed by Jötunn, forcing Conquest to act or look cowardly. However Beria said he would not act until after he was sure Abea was dealt with. Giger also struck a personal deal with Beria much easier, telling him he wanted him to not support the current mayor of Los Angeles in the future. Beria said he already knew how “that” was going to play out and knew which side he'd be on.

    The final scene took place at Eye of God's apartment with Alpha Centauri, but before he was willing to hand over any information on resurrections he had the cabal confirm how they had acquired a soul. When they revealed that they had set up soul jars around nursing homes he seemed slightly uncomfortable but admitted it didn't violate any sort of laws, so he told them of four paths he had found to bring Eye of God back. The first was simple, a master of Death could resurrect him, but the spell would be vulnerable to being dispelled. Second, he had discovered a Supernal Entity, a Shade, that would resurrect someone for the price of a life. Third was traveling to the Aeons and petitioning Typhon for the power to permanently resurrect their friend. His final option was to attempt to discover Mictlan, although he didn't point out that the Lantern Keepers cabal had been trying and failing to do this, and that it might not even exist.

    The Cabal debated over two things. First, which path should they take. No one wanted to risk a spell that could be dispelled, while Giger thought a Supernal summoning and then the murder of someone predetermined as worth killing by the tenants of the Guardians of the Veil could kill two birds with one stone. Maxwell and Ragnar were against this, seeing Mictlan as a better solution. The second point of debate, brought up by Ragnar, was if Eye of God even wanted to be resurrected. <This was also a meta game question for Eye of God/Delver's player, who answered that the Cabal should move forward with what they thought their characters would want to do.> They decided to perform a Supernal summoning to ask a Shade what Eye of God wants, and then if he did want to be resurrected they would attempt to find Mictlan first, resorting to killing someone only if it came down to that as a last resort.


    Chapter 65
    11/17/2025

    <Giger's solo session to finish joining the Guardians of the Veil>
    For Giger’s next test to prove he'd be a good fit for the Guardians of the Veil he was sent to Hollywood to meet with Vulcan of the Adamantine Arrow. Her order had become aware of a ritual site that had resulted in three deaths and wanted the Guardians to investigate. They decided to send Giger with the instructions to handle this as a Guardian, not a mystagogue. He arrived at the ritual site, an out of business bar that was now mainly used as storage. Vulcan showed him to the cellar where the ritual had taken place. Three corpses lay around a summoning circle, and the walls were covered in runes. Giger recognized the ruins as similar in intent to the ruins on the walls of the location where the Arcane Luminaries of the Expanse battled Abraham de Moivre; strengthening Death while weakening Time. Each corpse had killed themselves it seemed. A closer look at the summoning circle revealed Abyssal anti-symbols and Giger realized that they had been summoning an Abyssal manifestation. He was able to look back in time and learned that the three mages here had been planning this summoning for two weeks, somehow convincing people to clear the cellar out and then setting up the ritual site while referencing a book that Giger recognized as The Eater Codex, a grimoire filled with Abyssal knowledge on how to consume magic on behalf of a powerful Abyssal manifestation known as The Eater. It also became clear that someone had discovered this ritual site and was investigating it but was invisible to Giger's view of the past. Whoever they were, they discovered The Eater Codex, dropped it and quickly returned it to its hiding place, and then returned the next morning to retrieve it. Then, that night, the cabal that was using the grimoire returned and found it missing.

    An argument happened with one mage saying they should call off the summoning until they found the grimoire and could complete the preparations, but the side that wanted to attempt the summoning with the current preparations won out. The summoning failed, with the presence of The Eater leaking through just enough to drive each mage to suicide, ending the summoning before the manifestation could fully come into the Fallen World. Then, the following morning he and Vulcan arrived. Giger informed Vulcan of what he saw and then used Prophecy to ask questions of the future.
    1. When will I first encounter this book? When you find the sanctum of the mage who took it.
    2. What is this mage's name? Virgil, but you know him as Dante.
    3. What will I learn about Virgil's plan for the book? That he currently has no plan. He is unsure if he should destroy the book, turn it in to some greater authority, or hide it away. He has no intention of using the book though, but if he does hold on to it he will be tempted by the knowledge in the book like any mage would be in that situation.
    4. What would The Eater gain by influencing the summoners to kill themselves before it had been fully summoned? Nothing.
    With this information he called Virgil, who seemed tired over the phone. Giger asked if they could meet to discuss an investigation he was leading and Virgil agreed, telling him he could meet him in thirty minutes and giving him the address of his sanctum. Giger filled Vulcan in on what was going in and she left. With no immediate threats and just an interview of a mage who wasn't part of the Golden Order to begin with she had no reason to stay and plenty of reasons to go investigate other threats. She shook Giger’s hand and they went their own ways.

    Giger arrived at Virgil's sanctum with time to spare but decided not to break into the sanctum. He did arm himself with his pistols, but waited for Virgil. Virgil seemed shocked to learn how rich Giger was, as evident by the limousine he was waiting in. In turn, Giger was shocked by Virgil's sanctum, as it was much larger and more filled with tombs and magical items than he had expected. Virgil revealed that it has been tradition for a century for Virgil to pick a Dante, train him, and pass all the lore they had acquired down to their Dante upon their death. The Dante would then take the Shadowname of Virgil and continue the process. When Thread-Cutter had been brought to justice, the then Dante got all of the stolen lore that was taken from his Virgil and finally felt he could take on his master's Shadowname. While he described this he closed and covered a book that had been left open, but Giger didn't push yet.

    Giger caught Virgil up on what had happened with the Eater Codex, and to his surprise, Virgil admitted he had it and turned it over to Giger. Touching the book made Giger feel like he was being bitten over and over again at the points of contact by millions of small mouths and it was no shock to him that Virgil was so ready to hand it off to anyone else. However Giger wasn't sure if just retrieving the grimoire was enough and so he questioned Virgil more. Virgil admitted he had found the book and was shocked by its contents, and that he had stolen it in hopes that it would dissuade the owners from completing the summoning. Giger confirmed that he hadn't read it by looking at the book's past, seeing he hadn't opened it in the past day, and also checked Virgil's past, who hadn't hidden himself from Time since returning with the book and instead went into the Astral looking for something, but failing to find it.

    It was at this point two things happened. Giger noticed there was a large spider on the ceiling of Virgil's sanctum and at the same time he received a text from an unknown number telling him Virgil must die. He tried calling them back but the line was dead, so he used Fate magic to text a random number to contact them, asking them who they were. They did not answer, instead they just said “This order comes from higher up than either of us.” Virgil asked what was happening but Giger told him not to worry, again casting Prophecy.
    1. If I keep the Eater Codex will I use it? You'll have the same temptations to use it that Virgil would have, but it is not guaranteed.
    2. What will happen if I don’t kill Virgil? This question was blocked, hidden by powerful Time magic and he didn't get an answer.
    3. Who is giving the order to Kill Virgil? Epopt. <Giger recognized this as a rank, not a name.>
    After much agonizing, Giger decided not to kill Virgil. He had stolen the Eater Codex, and not studied it at all, in an attempt to stop the previous owners from summoning the Eater. He was not looking to use the book and would only be tempted by its secrets if he had held onto it, but he had already handed it over to Giger. So, he thanked him and decided that the Guardians were not the order for him. Still, he received a text saying to meet at the Wiltern the next morning to inform Arachnid of what had happened, and he decided he would go to try to save Virgil’s life.

    At his apartment he cast a indefinite duration Time veiling spell to hide the inside of his secret vault from Time magic, so everything inside would be hidden, and then cast a second indefinite Time spell on the living room of his apartment to make it look like the Shadow That Walks had broken in and stolen the Eater Codex the same night Giger had returned with it, foolishly leaving it on his shelf. Both spells were safely relinquished.

    The next morning Giger went to the Wiltern and met with Arachnid. She was with two others, a robed figure, whose face was hidden by a mask, he was chained to the ground. The second was an unremarkable woman in street clothes. Giger informed them of the ritual, the fate of the Eater Codex, and why he had spared Virgil. Archanid and the other woman were obviously upset that the Codex had been stolen, but Arachnid praised his willingness to not kill Virgil, “The Guardians need mages who hold to their morals, not attack dogs.” Giger was welcomed as a member of the Guardians of the Veil and Arachnid left. The woman told Giger that the masked and chained man was a Goetia who would help Giger learn the secret of making his own Masque.

    Giger meditated into the Astral and was standing on top of a skyscraper, looking down over an egg shaped realm. A river split the egg down the middle before splitting again at both ends, making four sections; the section he was in, a far section, and a middle section that had been split in two. He did not explore his Oneiros and instead, with the help of the goetia, created his first Masque.

                Last chapter of 2025!

                Chapter 66
                12/12/2025

                Upon waking Maxwell, Ragnar, and Delver each learned through the news that The Shadow That Walks had returned, and they had struck Giger's apartment. They all learned he was okay, but that a book he had stolen on behalf of the Guardians of the Veil had been taken by the Shadow That Walks the previous night before he could hand it over. Leopold spoke with the police, as Giger was supposed to have committed suicide in the bombing before. Special Agent Olivia Miller was on sight and seemed suspicious of this but without any sight of Giger she didn’t push Leopold, instead she instructed him to find a new place to stay for a few nights while her people conducted their investigation. <Good thing the cabal had moved Eye of God's body to his apartment to meet with Alpha Centauri.> Giger got himself a cheap hotel to lay low in and contacted Chronos. They discussed what happened and Chronos asked if Giger planned to return to Los Angeles for this, but Giger told him he'd still be in Japan. Chronos then reminded him that his mother wanted to come visit and that he should make sure he is in Japan when she does, implying something that Giger was unable to pick up on. When the rest of the cabal arrived at Giger's hotel he talked them out of investigating this theft as he was much more interested in bringing Eye of God back. So the cabal agreed to meet at an old rural cemetery that night to summon the Mausoleum, a Shade that Ragnar knew about that could create a ghost and compel it to answer questions to the summoner in exchange for the summoner being willing to be haunted.

                During the day Giger and Raganr both took mana from their hallows while Maxwell, starved for mana since his hallow had stopped producing mana, went to Otakus Anonymous to draw on the mana from Eye of God's old Hallow. He drained it all by reading from the full collection of Fullmetal Alchemist and then purchased some heat up ramen which the tass had settled in. He left without asking the woman behind that counter, who was an old friend of Eye of God, why she seemed so exhausted.

                In the Graveyard that night the summoning was simple. Maxwell used Forces magic to hide the ritual, which only took three hours to complete with Ragnar leading. The Mausoleum rose from the earth, a fifteen foot tall structure of black marble. With it came a slab of the same marble, which Ragnar wrote Eye of God's name on. The stone started to fall off, like ice melting and sliding away, until a human form was made from the stone. Then the sound of a chisel could be heard as details were carved into the stone. The whole ordeal took nearly twenty minutes, but at the end a black marble statue of Eye of God stood before the cabal. Ragnar asked his question; “Do you want to return to the living?” Eye of God responded with, “Sure, why not?” With the question asked, the Mausoleum returned to the Supernal and Eye of God remained, a ghost, haunting Ragnar.

                <The rest of the session was research on Typhon and Mictlan, with Ragnar discovering a path to the Whorl and Giger making contact with Crypt to learn about Mictlan. Legends were given about Mictlan and a recent discovery made by Crown of Lead was shared, but Crypt informed the cabal that the Lantern Keepers were trying to find the way to Mictlan to protect it from Seers, so if they discovered it that had to swear not to tell anyone about it. In the end though only Maxwell wanted to return to the Underworld for over a month, and the cabal decided to seek out Typhon instead.>

                Before splitting up Maxwell decided to help teach Delver magic by exposing him, forcefully, to the Aether with an Apocalypse spell.
