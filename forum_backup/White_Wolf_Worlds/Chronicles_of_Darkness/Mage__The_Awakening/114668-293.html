        Originally posted by Berdryn View Post

        Dubious usage. This depends entirely on whether your table allows aoe spells to be centered on mobile subjects that aren't themselves 'areas'. If they do allow that, 'bullets' should be fine.

        Originally posted by Berdryn View Post
        Could you use ban and time to create a space where time is accelerated? Like a training dojo where you could train yourselves up a few months at a time in exchange for a week in the area?
        I think banning Time would work more like Veil of Moments, but there has been other discussion of this further back in the thread.

        Originally posted by Berdryn View Post
        Can I use death and ban to say ban a warehouse sized field from any zombies or undead by simply saying undead or the like?
        Sure

        Originally posted by Berdryn View Post
        Would vampires be restricted or get a clash of wills?
        What on earth would they Clash with? Unless they have an specific ability that allows them entry into that explicit area, there's no grounds for a CoW. You don't roll Clash just because another splat is involved, it's a means to resolve conflict between supernatural powers with directly opposing effects when the direct mechanics of each don't resolve on their own (or if the powers specifically call for one).

                            It’s not about having a strong enough connection, it’s about what Scrying actually does and doesn’t do and what it doesn’t do is let you see through the eye-like equivalent of your subject. Patterning can be used to actually braid two subjects together so tightly that on the metaphysical level they’re one thing which is why they can share senses.

                            Anyway, an easier method if you’re camped out in just one spot would be to open a Scrying window on the area you’re in with as big a Scale as you can get. Skip the drone entirely.

                            Alternatively, and even better than Scrying, use Inward and Outward eye. It becomes nearly impossible for your group to be ambushed then as well as wide angle surveillance giving you more bang for your buck and continuing to avoid the drone problem.

                            Both those alternatives also require only one spell control slot, and I don’t know what your Gnosis is, but running multiple Scrying windows when you could use one spell is a little sloppy.

                                                        01-17-2018, 07:59 PM
                                                        Originally posted by CharonFerryman View Post
                                                        Hey guys, new player here just want to clear some questions on character creation:

                                                        1) What are rote skills? I can't find the page that talks about them in more detail.
                                                        2) What is a Praxis, which page can I read more about them?
                                                        3) How many Dedicated Magical Tools can a starting mage have?
                                                        1: Rote skills are a holdover from 1e, they grant a +1 dice bonus to the Mudra Yantra when the Rote you're casting uses one of your Orders Rote Skills. So say you're Mysterium, and you have a Rote that has the Rote Skill of Occult, then when you use the Mudra Yantra for that Rote which grants you a number of dice equal to your dots in Rote you can add one more die because of your Order's Rote Skill.

                                                        2: A Praxis is a personal spell that you tend to cast a lot. You get one for free everytime you purchase a dot of Gnosis, and you can purchase additional Praxis for 1 XP each. A Praxis is basically like any other spell, except that when you cast it you get an Exceptional Success on 3 Successes instead of 5. I'm sorry that I can't remember specific page numbers. There's a sidebar that details your options when you get an Exceptional Success on a spell. I can't remember where exactly that is either, but it's things like bypassing Withstand, gaining a Condition that can result in an Arcane Beat or replenishing Mana spent on a spell +1, etc.

                                                        3: Any Mage can only have 1 Dedicated Magical Tool. Although, when Signs of Sorcery comes out there will apparently be rules for trying to have more than one.
