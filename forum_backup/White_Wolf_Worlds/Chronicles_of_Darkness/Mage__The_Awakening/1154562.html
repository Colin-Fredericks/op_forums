    Running caucus meets

    10-31-2017, 08:36 PM
    How have you handled a mulit order cabal caucus gatherings at your game table if your group have mages from different orders in the same cabal. Do you just run a short interlude for each player at the table? handle things through emails away from the game table?

      Mage isn’t like DnD, there’s no rule against splitting the party, and it can be really good to have opportunities for players to get some sole spotlight time. If a cabal member is the only member of his Order in the cabal and there’s a story reason their caucus is meeting then they should be able to just go (Barring other story complications. Sometimes it’s fun to have a meeting you really have to attend and an urgent problem that needs your attention simultaneously. The best way to pressure a Mage is to have opportunity costs for their actions rather than trying to stump them directly). You can split it up with giving something to the rest of the cabal to do while they wait for their erstwhile member and move back and forth between the group and the individual. In my experience most players don’t mind someone getting some solo action in as long as they’re not left hanging around twiddling their thumbs, especially if they know they’ll also get their own solo stuff going on down the line.

      This kind of thing has come up in every game (regardless of game line, even DnD) I’ve ever played, and it’s always worked out pretty well.

      As the ST just attempt to use good pacing.

              I use this techniques for running Caucus scenes in multiOrder cabals:
              1. Guests - if meeting is about main plot and/or this Caucus could be interested in particular topic - I just let non-Order PCs come as guests. They do not stay for 'super secret cultish stuff', but most Caucus meetings have parts open to non-Order Pentacle mages.
              2. Run NPC - I let non-Order players to run some NPCs, mostly not story important. Let them just made on spot some this Order NPCs so the can expend Order rooster and give more real persons responses to meeting.

              Hiding a lot of stuff before other mage-players is no sense - most dedicated players will still uncover info themselves. At most, I take one player from gaming group to talk to him important info in not more than 5 minutes - if I would need more, I just run scene with those two previous points.

                              I thought I would just drop in to put in a good word for the written option. I do lots of split/party stuff in my sessions as well, but for something as lengthy and involved as a caucus or consilla meeting I prefer to schedule some time with my players on our Discord server. Its actually really handy if your doing lots of NPC banter, or political scheming. gives you plenty of time to make sure your keeping all your facts straight, and players can come back and look over things they missed. Particularly good if you have 5 players to keep track of like I do.
