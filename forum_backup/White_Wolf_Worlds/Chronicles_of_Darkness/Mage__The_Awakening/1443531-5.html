        I feel the first Attainment is trying to do too much. Creating a Node is a Prime 5 effect or multiple geomantic effects. Creating an artificial soul stone is a pretty powerful effect, perhaps for the fourth or fifth Attainments. The Resonance should probably fit the mage rather than be fixed by the Legacy. Some additional commentary should probably be dedicated on how it mitigates Paradox for the caster, how individual pieces are connected to their Nimbus and if they can be used as Dedicated Tools. Finally, the incorporation of so many optional Arcana seems like spreading itself too wide, especially since the effects don't all follow a central theme (the effects for Space and Time seem to have no correlation to the growth and serenity themes).

        The first part of the secondary effect is a Prime 4 Patterning and therefore way too powerful to be implemented at this level. The optional effect might run into problems if the shaping does not respect the laws of physics, causing the garden to collapse once duration ends. In addition, there may simply not be enough organic matter for all the required fortifications.

        The third optional Attainment feels like its trying to do too much with too many Arcana.

        The fourth main Attainment replicates too many effects at once. Ephemeral Enchantment, Words of Truth, Stealing Fire, protection from Breaking Points (which seems unrelated to either Arcana) and granting rote quality (which should cost a mana and only affect a limited number of rolls). It needs to be distilled into one effect.

        The fourth optional Attainment probably is a Patterning of Prime and Life, therefore it requires advanced scale (to cover the entire garden) and advanced duration for optimal results, leaving it one Reach over the limit.

        Regarding the fifth Attainment, the spell Create Truth is being replicated. Normally it requires mana equal to 5 * Potency, which even truncated by Legacy mana rules means it should cost a mana and last up to a year. In addition, the spell is resisted by the dot rating of the Hallow, meaning it cannot have Prime dots in rating, even applying the pseudo dice for extra factors.

        The optional fifth Attainment can create a different garden via a permanent transform life and even give the plant a quick reproduction or growth cycle. However, the caretaker becoming a Sleepwalker is the result of Stealing Fire and giving them an Obsession is the result of Forge Purpose, which means the Attainment has 3 effects rather than one.

        Overall, they feel like the theme is being spread too thin between: one with nature, an honest place and a place that cultivates the self. Right now it would be a good idea to choose one to focus on entirely.

        Hope that helps.

                                Wow today has been eventful. I’m just home from work, and a flat tire, and a major car wreck (I wasn’t involved, but I’m pretty someone didn’t make it). Standard disclaimer about tiredness aside, I will try to answer this in full. Maybe it will even make sense when I try to read this in the morning.
                                Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post
                                What I am not clear about is: what are the Roots ? Tamer Legacies relate the physical phenomenon they tame to a metaphysical concept, seeing them as one and the same. The Tamers of Fire see their element as passion given form and power. The Tamers of Rivers see them as being change made manifest, the Tamers of Wind see gales and gusts as knowledge and so on. But what are the Roots ? What do they symbolically represent and how are they being tamed, harnessed ?
                                The Roots are Ley Lines and Nodes, being the roots of Magic. Magic is a Living thing, and is often compared to a tree, specifically a Tree of Knowledge. Ley Lines look like the Roots of tree, with Nodes being the source or the stem rising out into the fallen world. Fallen plants are basically the same thing, drinking in unseen nutrients and water. In the metaphor, the ground the tree sits on is the Lie, and the legacy may be able to destroy the Lie by covering the world in Gardens, but that is a extremely distant pipe dream, and may not even work.
                                The legacy works by investing a part of themselves into a crack in the lie, widen it through their Attainments, and draw on that power for further Attainments. Since I’m working with plant metaphors, a garden results from the excess ley energy from the Node, making the area even more sacred. Eventually both the Supernal and the Fallen world around the crack in the Lie are filled with plants, whose roots further widen it. Of course the Mage is tied to the land now, and must tend to it lest others destroy what they have made. Hmm maybe I should have done a King and his Land are One as the summarization.

                                Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post
                                Even though you mentioned the themes of the Roses of Eden are not what you are looking for, there exists a 2ed conversion whose Attainments seem to capture the feel of what you are looking for:

                                http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...01#post1180101
                                The Roses of Eden are almost the same thing as this legacy, because they are Tamers Of Thornless Roses or Tamers of Flowers. The thing is, their symbolism and point is kind of weird, being caught up in their new garden of Eden metaphor, and managing to still cover lot of related concepts slightly. They also get weird with path symbolism, and other things. I think the big difference is that the Roses focus on Gardens as People, building cults and becoming perfect pacifistic plant people, becoming the thornless Roses of Eden. Meanwhile the Tamers of Roots focus on Gardens as a Place in the World, building a Place of Power and becoming Rooted. That in my mind is a big enough difference to separate the two. After all, the Silver Ladder and the Free Council are similar, they both focus on being rebels, and sleepers, but I don’t think anyone is seriously suggesting that there is no need for the Silver Ladder because the Free Council exists... in real life anyway. Maybe you disagree on the two legacies being distinct. Which is fine, I probably will slowly continue to rewrite the Tamers of Roots for a little more.

                                With all of that said, yes there are similarities. Both legacies draw from some of the same metaphors. Both even use plants, in different ways. Which means that the Attainments are going to overlap somewhat as well. That being said, maybe I should focus on making Yantras to further separate the Prime parts, and maybe focus on more plant manipulation as the life part. By no means is this legacy finished, I just rather suddenly have much less time to work on it.
