                        Every ephemeral and Supernal entity has dots in an Advantage called Rank, which notes how self-aware and powerful the entity is. Rank technically ranges from 1 to 10 dots, but those with more than Rank 5 are overwhelmingly powerful and too alien to the physical world to support themselves with the Conditions that lesser beings use; their presence in a chronicle should come about through critical conjunctions and momentous events.

                                    Originally posted by bonerstorm View Post
                                    Regardless... withstand 5 for a "Minor God" is a little ridiculous.
                                    The things are rocking consistent base dicepools in the double digits.

                                    Withstand reduces spells' effective Potency, and the vast majority of spells that specifically mess with ephemeral beings are Duration-primary. Those that aren't are Exorcism analogues, or else relatively constrained stuff like:

                                    1. Know Spirit (which gives you less information for higher-Ranked spirits)
                                    2. Command Spirit (which only lasts for the Duration, Clashes with commands that violate its self-interest, and may be an Act of Hubris if it's not Inured)
                                    3. Rouse Spirit (which is Potency-intensive even without being Withstood)
                                    4. Shape Spirit (most of the same problems as Command with the saving grace being that it doubles as a healing spell)
                                    5. Annihilate Spirit (which explicitly gives a better-than-usual Clash roll to avoid and is a Master level spell targeting a class of being that explicitly applies its Defense to every type of attack if you don't Reach to manifest the effect directly on top of it).

                                    Withstand 5 is the highest most characters have to deal with from a single source and the relative frequency with which Incarnae are both secluded and accompanied by other spirits means getting to the point where their Withstand rating is the biggest problem you have in casting on them is a trip and a half to begin with.
