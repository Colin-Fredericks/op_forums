    Pentacle City continued [Actual Play]

    07-19-2016, 03:40 PM
    So in the long gap of waiting for the mage book, things fell through with posting this actual play and there's a lot of missing ground between then and now in the game. After a particularly dramatic session I've decided to start telling the story again. I'll start from this session and try to give the information that's needed to help catch the reader up. As I go along I might start to fill in some of the past events that I've missed.

    The Story So Far

    A group of criminals is gathered together by their common friend and contact Emilio Baylon. They have a simple enough job, even if it seemed strange from the start. A mysterious client is willing to pay millions for a rare book.

    Though they never managed to steal the book and Emilio Baylon was murdered, in the hunt for it they learned about the existence of the supernatural and much much more. Eventually they were exposed, some willingly and some not, to a mysterious magical drug called Prism that seems to break all the rules.
    Strangest of all a pure dose of Prism seemed to reliably cause a person to Awaken.

    So far as the players are aware they are the only people to have received a sufficiently large pure dose of Prism and they have all awakened. What's more is the players are part of the very small group of people who know what Prism is, how it's made and what it can do. Although the only two methods they know of creating Prism, a bargain with the Abyss or sacrificing people with a great destiny, are not something that they're eager to experiment with.

    While they try to figure out what to do with this information, they're also trying to deal with their new found magic. They've finally met the Awakened mages of the consillium of New York City and each managed to find their own unique apprenticeships to learn about the world of magic. After six months following their own paths they've come back together again as a cabal to try to figure out their future.

    Cast of Characters

    Martina the Artist (Acanthus, Free Council). A young struggling artist, who was lured into working as a forger to provide security for herself once she found out she was pregnant.

    Scipio the Mob Enforcer (Thyrsus, Adamantine Arrow). Worked for years with the mob and finally became a made man, just as werewolves murdered the head of the crime family. Now trying to unite the family and claim his place at the top.

    Lin Wu the Chemist (Moros, Mysterium). By day a successful corporate researcher, by night a manufacturer of illegal drugs. One of the finest scientific minds of her generation turning her attention now to understanding magic as well as she once understood the laws of the Lie.

    Matty the Fixer (Mastigos). The man with all the contacts. Matty is now the protege of a mysterious immortal crime lord Di Mercurio who has somehow claimed the title of spirit king of New York.

    Valkyrie the Assassin (Oubrimos, Guardians of the Veil). Trained to kill for her family, she has left that life behind to rescue a good friend. Now she kills for herself and perhaps some day to protect the veil.

    Darren the Driver (Acanthus, Free Council). Running from a ruined hollywood career as a stunt driver, Darren was the finest get-away driver that money could buy. When you need to get somewhere ten minutes ago, he is the one you need.

    Lucas the Hacker (Oubrimos). A computer hacker and friend of Martina's who started helping with creating fake identities to pay for his family's medical bills after an accident left him in a wheelchair and his parents badly injured.

    Part One: Home Sweet Home
    Summary: After six months of downtime our newly awakened mages pool together 14 merit points to purchase a lot of group merits. This is the story of how they get their new home.

    The cabal have decided to work together to obtain a sanctum and after a couple of weeks Matty lets the rest of the cabal know that he's found a place “within their price-range”, he even thinks the place has a Hallow. So they drive to Roosevelt island to take a look at a large luxury apartment building. As the cabal approach and activate their mage sight, they face a clash of wills against a potent veiling spell.

    Only Martina and Lin Wu succeed in seeing through the effect. The building appears mundane to everyone else, no sign of any Hallow or magic. Martina's fate sight though sees a dark shadow over the building, showing nothing clearly but the resonance of lives and fates interrupted and controlled by dark steel chains.

    Lin Wu's death sight catches a glimpse of a graveyard on the shore nearby that suddenly seems closer when looking past the building, lensed as though space is distorted around the building for deathly energies. She identifies it as a sure sign of a magical Ban that includes the Death arcanum. Lin Wu closely scrutinizes the area with her sight and attempts a revelation against the surface level of the mystery. She succeeds and discovers that the building was veiled with a rote spell cast by another awakened mage one or two years ago and has a number of other warding spells hidden beneath the veil.

    Using a Dispel Magic rote, Lin Wu, Valkyrie and Lucas work together attempting to dispel the veil. Suddenly the magic is clear to everyone's senses as the veil is permanently destroyed. The building is warded, banning any Ghosts, Goetic thought-patterns or Spirits from entering. Above the building a powerful Hallow, like a miniature magical sun, shines mana down through the skylights into the penthouse level. However to Scipio's life sight, there's a corrupt aura of sickness and infirmity soaked into the upper levels of the building as though long term suffering has polluted the resonance.

    ~Scipio isn't very active from this point onwards during the session as the player needed to leave early. In game Scipio gets a phone call about a problem that he needs to help resolve with the Trovato family and leaves to deal with it.~

    Curious now the Acanthus mages, Darren and Martina, decide to use their time magic to investigate the past of the building. Matty veils them with mind magic so that no observers will notice them performing magic. Darren (who knows the rote) brings out a small silver mirror and smears it with his blood, chanting in High Speech as he commands it to reveal the past. Martina chants along assisting by gathering a few flakes of paint from the wall as a sympathetic link to the past and drops them into her crystal chalice. Together they achieve an exceptional success and decide to extend the spell a little further to allow the entire cabal to witness the events they're seeing.

    They discover that they can see all the way back to 2006, when the building underwent major renovations which disrupts the Temporal Sympathy as the building changed too much for their spell to reveal. They're not sure exactly when to look though as that's a very large time window. Martina finds a solution though and weaves a Fate spell to randomly guess the best time to look at. Thanks to the spell they find themselves looking back at an event from 1am, January 1st 2014.

    At first they see nothing but they hear a car door close and turn the mirror to a new angle to look at a police cruiser parked and the officer heading around to the rear of the vehicle. He escorts a figure from the rear of the car, dressed in simple sweat-pants and a T-shirt but heavily manacled and bound. The figure is filthy and weathered with long matted hair that makes it difficult to spot any features, but he slowly shuffles out of the car assisted by the police officer.
    Without a word the police officer puts a gun to the back of the man's head with one hand while with the other he removes the manacles. Our mages watch as in the past they see the man make arcane gestures in the air, rote mudras for the warding spells that are still on the building. Despite their cabal including mages from most of the Orders none of them recognize the specific mudras that are being used. Once the wards are complete the officer re-cuffs the man and puts him back into the car to drive away again.

    Since they're not sure how best to proceed Martina casts the fate spell Serendipity to grant herself an omen of a way to find out more. An idea pops into her mind instantly and she moves the mirror to clearly view the license plate of the police car. Lucas is able to immediate confirm via some computer records that he almost certainly shouldn't have, that it's the genuine number of a NYPD vehicle. He says with a bit more time he can probably track down more details.

    Instead though Martina tries a different approach and using Fate magic once again decides to try to get in contact with the long haired prisoner she saw in the vision. She randomly dials a number on her mobile phone and uses Fate magic to try to make the random number be the closest phone to the prisoner they're searching for. A sudden pang of hunger distracts her from the spell's Imago though and instead she finds herself talking to the nearest pizza store. While they wait for the pizzas to be delivered she tries again and this time feels much luckier.

    Matty takes the phone and talks to the woman who answers, “Hi, you're calling from a private number. Is this John?” She asks. Matty, whose shadow name is John Smith is caught a little off-guard but says Yes and listens to her talk, “I'm a little busy at the moment, I thought you were going to call me after work. I can't really talk now.” However he's already taking advantage of hearing her voice to use a Spaces spell to detect where she is. The spell works and he recognizes the area, part of a small technological industry area in the same neighbourhood as Lin Wu's day job.

    He asks her a question in High Speech which leaves her completely confused and mystified, saying that there was something really creepy about the strange sounds she just heard. Matty lies and blames it on a bad connection. They end the conversation with Matty saying that he'll call back later.
    Lucas researches the address that they found and quickly finds that the company seems to be fake. They have a website about researching mould cleaning solutions but he's not convinced that it's genuine. Using a Prime spell to reveal deceptions Valkyrie is able to confirm that the website is completely lying and because of an extra reach on the spell she's able to reveal a symbolic glimpse of the truth. In her mystical vision words are suddenly redacted on the screen, thick black bars removing everything except for.

    ###Look###########
    ###somewhere else.##
    None of############
    ######your business.#
    #####Secret########
    plans.#############

    They realise that the website is a complete sham and has no genuine information, however the spell was at least able to reveal that it exists to cover up and deflect attention from some sort of secret plan.

    Martina decides to try a slightly different approach and rings the woman back planning to try to talk to her boss. She uses a Fate spell called 'Fools rush in' to magically convert her ignorance of the situation from an obstacle into an advantage. When the woman answers Martina says she's in the car with John and needs to talk with their boss. The woman, at first confused suddenly sighs as she thinks she figures out the situation. “Oh you're the John from the delivery company not the other...” clearly embarrassed she admits to Martina “I was talking to a John on Tinder and I thought that was who was calling.”

    The woman on the phone hands the phone to their boss who says “Hello, this is Dr Acre.” Valkyrie's deception revealing spell is still active and so she realizes that the name is a lie. However the Doctor part is genuine. The name she can sense is just one of many that the person uses but gets nothing more from the spell.

    Martina switches to high-speech, talking very quickly and just making up what she's saying as she goes along “Oh I'm so glad you're there, I wasn't sure I'd reach you in time.”

    Dr Acre responds in high speech, “Who is this? What are you calling about?”

    Martina hands the phone over to Matty at this point who uses a Mind effect to improve everyone in the cabal's Presence attribute to give them more of a sense of gravitas and importance. “I'm John Smith, I'm calling about the building at (the address), is that something you claim as your territory?”

    The voice on the other end sounds confused for a few moments before suddenly relaxing into a more confident and certain speech. “Oh, you're that John Smith. Hmm, how is it you think that you knew about this building?”

    Matty tries to explain a bit about his guess that the building was important and had to have a Hallow and it matched the lay lines of the area, however the explanation doesn't seem to hold together.

    Dr Acre laughs and says “I think if you know about that building then someone must want you to know and I wouldn't dream of interfering.”

    As Dr Acre hangs up the call Matty notices a slight pattern of grime on the windows of the building seems to spell out in High Speech runes “Welcome home John Smith”. However only from the angle he's looking and no one else seems to spot it when he points to the window.

    To be continued
    ~So this was a slight bit of Storytelling trickery. Matty's player had discussed the building with me out of character and they'd already discussed spending the points for the Hallow which was how Matty's player knew about it. This was putting the player in a bit of an awkward position as I was demanding in character that he answer how his character knew something we'd only ever discussed out of character. However the players quickly realized what this was... Due to his unusual awakening Matty has the 'Whispers' merit. The mysterious certainty that he had in character (from knowing about it out of game and thinking he'd discovered it in character during the downtime) was actually due to the idea being implanted into his mind as a message from the Exarchs~

      Part 2: My first mystery
      Summary: The players discover that there's something very unusual going on inside the building.

      The cabal have seen the surface of the mystery now but know that it must go deeper. Matty's player, who hadn't chosen an obsession yet, decides to make this mystery his character's obsession. The rest have obsessions of their own but with the prospect of a Hallow and a fairly secure location, along with the mystery itself, drive them all to investigate together.

      Martina points out that they were here to receive a tour from the real estate agent but they've been leaving the person waiting. Matty is confident though that with the multi-million dollar deal on the line, since they're planning on buying out an entire floor of appartments, the real estate agent will put up with a lot. True to his prediction, assisted partly by the entire cabal having five presence each, the elderly man waiting for them in the lobby is very polite and gracious as Martina apologizes for keeping him waiting.

      They take the elevator up to the top floor of the building so that they can view the penthouse areas for themselves and they question the realtor as they go. They're satisfied rather quickly that the realtor doesn't know about anything supernatural, however his description of how some of the UN translators live there and he often hears very strange languages being spoken catches their attention. A few questions makes it seem very likely that the realtor has heard high-speech in the building and the rationale comes from the Quissence blinding him to supernal language.

      Now that they're higher up Valkyrie can clearly see the Hallow, focused on the central area of the top story between the wings of the apartment building. Meanwhile Lin Wu can sense the corrupted energies of sickness and decay two floors beneath them. They ask the realtor to look up some information for them about the building and its owners while they take a look around to investigate.

      The group splits up into three separate teams to investigate. Matty decides he wants to purchase the entire building rather than just some apartments. While this would be at least half a billion and certainly isn't easy to manage, Matty has resources 5 and the fixer merit so it's actually a possibility especially with the total number of merits the players have invested in their new sanctum. Lucas stays to help him with research and other minor details.

      Darren and Lin Wu both head downstairs to investigate the corrupted aura. The entire corridor for that level seems tainted but closer now they can tell that it's actually coming from inside the apartments on either side. Lin Wu is concerned enough to place a Soul Armour effect, shielding their souls from any tampering on both herself and Darren, spending two willpower to relinquish the spells so that she doesn't have to divide her magical attention on too many spells at once. Darren uses the Time spell momentary flux to get a clue as to whether their current actions are leading towards good or bad results. The glimpse he gets is slightly bad result, but more dealing with something unpleasant rather than danger or harm. Now prepared the two plan to get into one of the apartments.

      Upstairs Valkyrie and Martina have gone to the place where the Hallow can be sensed and drawn upon. Large skylights let both sunlight and Mana flood a large empty circular room, with seemingly nothing more than a few indoor plants features and decorative benches. Valkyrie's spell to pierce deceptions however spots that part of the wall isn't actually a wall. They take a closer look and find a disguised cabinet, but while they can open up the concealed camouflage to reveal it the actual cabinet is locked with a combination lock.

      Valkyrie uses the prime spell Sacred Geometry to reveal how the lines of power flow through the area and determines that they are inside the Hallow, which has been designed to feed the Mana into Tass inside the cabinet. It also shows that the Hallow is on a convergence of ley-lines that carries a magical resonance of “Remembrance”, however it is slowly in the process of being corrupted by the resonance of the building and will eventually shift.

      Locks aren't much good against Mages though. Valkyrie heads downstairs to check on the others while Martina opens the locker by fate magic and luckily guessing the combination. She discovers that inside the locker are pumping mechanisms and pipes, sending a red liquid infused with Mana in the form of Tass flowing down through pipes in the wall to somewhere downstairs.

      Meanwhile a little Matter magic opens the mechanical lock on the first apartment door for Lin Wu and Darren. Inside they find a completely bare apartment set up with a medical gurney and more life support equipment than most hospitals. An unconscious man is plugged into the life support equipment, with an IV feeding red liquid into his veins from a socket in the wall. Even Lin Wu's death sight can't determine which side of life or death the figure is on as he's kept balanced perfectly between the two.

      To be continued

      ~So as a bit of a rules note, Lin Wu's player asked whether the figure would count as dead for the purposes of the Death arcanum. This would be a very difficult question in 1st edition, however the 2nd edition has an easy way to handle this. My answer was simply that yes they could use the Death Arcanum since they were close enough to be under the influence of Stygia but it would require an additional reach since they weren't truly and completely dead. It didn't come up in game, but it's handy to have such a simple method of resolving this sort of ambiguity.

          Part 3: Fools rush in?
          Summary: Things don't go according to plan. The cabal take drastic action to deal with a mistake that they made.

          Cast of Characters:

          Iris: The cabal's main contact with the Consilium of New York has been through the herald Iris. An Oubrimos Silver Ladder who dresses as a bicycle messenger but is actually working for the Consilium rather than a delivery company. She's frequently accompanied by her younger brother Robin Goodfellow, an Acanthus and herald in training. However Robin isn't in this particular session. They mostly know Iris from her helping to “match-make” them with mentors for their apprenticeships.

          The 'Devotion' Cabal: Iris' cabal is a group of five Mages, mostly silver ladder and Oubrimos, who all work for the Consillium. Their service to the community is their uniting theme. The other members are:
          Adraste, a Silver Ladder Mastigos Sentinel.
          Nahundi, a Silver Ladder Oubrimos Lictor.
          Phoenix, a Guardian of the Veil Oubrimos Sentinel, Emissary and Valkyrie's mentor.
          Robin Goodfellow, a Silver Ladder Acanthus Herald in Training.

          The situation is bad enough, with mysterious spells hiding a creepily set up Hallow and unconscious prisoners that they decide it's time to raise the alarm. They call Iris, one of the heralds of the Consilium and give her some rough details asking her to bring a Sentinel to the location. Iris agrees and starts heading to the location along with one of her cabalmates, the Sentinel Adraste.

          Whoops: So just to correct an omission on the earlier post, the players actually called Iris once already. Earlier on in the session when they first discovered the spells and wards on the building they called to ask if it was the territory of any of the Mages of the Consillium since they didn't want to get in trouble for violating another Mage's sanctum. When they discovered that it wasn't they advised that they wanted to claim it for themselves.

          While they wait for the cavalry the cabal continue to investigate the scene. Lucas goes downstairs to distract the real estate agent while the rest explore the apartments on that floor, finding room after room exactly the same. Twenty life-support set ups in total, all occupied.

          They try to determine whether the prisoners, for lack of a better word, are alive or dead. Lin Wu's medical knowledge combined with the information on the screens of the life-support system shows their current condition as technically alive, however it appears to be a mere biological technicality. Matty connects telepathically to one of them and can feel nothing but the faint humming mental pattern of the brainstem keeping the body alive. Their minds seem completely gone.

          Lin Wu's close examination with Death sight discovers that they all possess a soul, but not one that appears to be properly attached. She quickly determines that these souls do not belong to the bodies but have instead been implanted into the bodies through a slight variant on the Soul Jar spell. (Lin Wu possesses this spell as a rote and so has a good working knowledge of how souls are manipulated).

          Darren uses postcognition multiple times, taking advantage of the rote he knows, going to all the rooms and looking into the past. Each of the patients has a Temporal Sympathy that he can use, however it only goes back to when the soul was implanted in them as the change of soul was too great a change for the level of Temporal Sympathy. This allows to observe however that three people, which the cabal names “doctors” were the ones to implant the souls in the bodies.

          At least that's their guess judging from looking at the moments immediately after the souls were implanted. One body was as recent as a month and a half ago, the others were going back several years. They even found one that was implanted on the 1st January 2014 at 4am, just a few hours after the wards were cast in their previous vision of the past. They also saw approximately ten people, which the cabal names “nurses” who were attending to basic medical needs of the prisoners every few days.

          Martina, still holding to her religious beliefs feels that the bodies should get their appropriate last rites if they are dead. While not a priest herself so she can't give the last rites, she goes from room to room and prays for each of them hoping it will count. While Martina prays for the souls, Lin Wu studies them more directly and comes to a frightening discovery. Unlike the body, which has the faintest tattered aura of a sleeper, the soul itself is an Awakened soul glowing with faint echoes of the Supernal. In fact all twenty souls are Awakened.

          Matty immediately calls the Consillium again, this time speaking directly to the Hierarch's Provost and explains the situation. Twenty misplaced Awakened souls, and the implicit suggestion of Reapers, gets the Hierarch's attention. The Consillium's people start to descend upon the location. First of all Iris and Adraste arriving and securing the location, then followed by more and more awakened Mages who move around the place like a crime scene. There's some polite, if not necessarily subtle, hinting for the cabal to move out and let them do their jobs. Although it's confirmed that they have claimed the site as their territory, investigating a possible breach of the Lex Magica takes priority. Yes it's their claimed sanctum, but it's also a crime scene. Iris does promise to give them word once they know the place is secure however.

          The Cabal head down the road to a cafe and get something to eat and drink while they discuss and figure out what is going on. The first thought, to check missing persons records is a failure. Lucas brings up the missing persons records for New York City and is saying that he wished they had a program like the one in the movies that could just match the faces to the records. Martina obliges by telling him to look at a particular date, guessing and using Fate magic, however there's no matches on that date. When Valkyrie suggests looking at obituaries instead, it turns out that the date suggested by Fate is actually for the obituary of the man from the first apartment that they examined.

          Rules note: Martina's player cast the spell lucky number to guess the right spot to look and rolled a critical success. However unfortunately the people weren't listed as missing people which made it difficult to resolve the critical success properly. Fortunately Valkyrie's suggestion, while I was figuring out what to do with the spell, was perfect so it allowed Martina's guess to be for the correct one instead.

          Now knowing where to look they quickly identify that the bodies there were the corpses of the recently deceased who had passed away from apparently natural causes at the nearby hospitals. They quickly figure out what was happening, if not why. The bodies of the recently deceased were being re-animated through medical technology and although braindead they were just alive enough to be able to host a soul inside them. The set up of the Hallow was pumping Tass into their bodies in a special manner which kept the Souls stored in them fully Awakened and as vital as they would be while inside a Mage.

          The cabal decides that although they're happy for the consillium to investigate they can't just stop trying to solve the mystery themselves (their first session as Mages and they're already in the spirit of it). They have a good idea of a lot of the details and although the crime scene is being investigated already they have a second lead to follow up on.

          First though Lin Wu tries an experiment to confirm whether there is a link between the mysterious events at the building and the place that they rang earlier with the random number. Even though it was before she awakened she'd worked right nearby the other location for years. Matty uses an unveiling spell to grant her a perfect memory and she tries to remember anything she might have seen.

          A memory from last October flashes into Lin Wu's mind in magically enhanced detail. She was at the Starbucks near her work and heard one of the staff call out “Dr Ah-Ka-Re” struggling to read the name off the coffee cup. The same familiar voice they heard over the phone corrects it as “No, Dr Acre.” and collects the coffee and a bag of pastries before leaving and heading back across the road. Now knowing his face, the figure that she saw was indeed one of the three “Doctors” that they saw in the post-cognitions of the building, seemingly implanting souls within the human soul jars.

          They drive as quickly as they can towards the location, with Darren behind the wheel it doesn't take long. However as soon as they get there they find that the location is on fire with emergency Services on hand dealing with the blaze. Magesight shows that the fire isn't entirely natural either but some form of Forces-Death spell that imbues the flame with the ability to burn away resonance and disrupt metaphysical evidence.

          The cabal asks a couple of questions and discovers that the fire started just twenty minutes after their phone call when Martina cast 'Fools rush in' and talked to Dr Acre. Looking at the burning building the cabal decides that they may have mishandled things, of course it doesn't have to stay that way. Darren offers to use Shifting Sands to unmake the last three hours for all of them.

          Thinking of the movie Back to the Future, Darren gets everyone to sit within his car to help him focus on the spell as he begins speeding down the road to reach 88 miles per hour. It's more than just a pop-culture reference though, thanks to Darren's Free Council Techne of driving it becomes a Yantra for the spell. One of the police cars from the fire chases after them, they see the lights flash, hear the Siren and a voice call out “Pull the vehicle ov...”

          A moment later and three hours earlier they find themselves standing together outside the building. Martina has her phone in her hand, a number dialled and about to press the 'call' button. Instead she presses the cancel button. The spell was a complete success with no paradox incurred as they find themselves back in the past ready to try another approach.

          End of the session.

              So this was basically the first session with the players as Mages. When they first awakened I gave them each 1 Gnosis and 1 in each primary Arcanum through innate understanding of magic but until their apprenticeships they didn't receive the full Mage template. So they'd not used the spellcasting system much before this session. Less than half the players had ever played any edition of Mage before.

              Despite that, by the end of this session everyone was using the system without any hesitation. The players would just be saying things like "Right, one reach for instant, one for advanced duration. This many dice for Gnosis, Arcanum and my Yantras. Take away this many for extra duration. Right. That's a chance die for Paradox."

              Such a complicated system but within a couple of hours of playing with it, this was just second nature. Everyone could leap straight into the mystery and work on using their powers to solve it without feeling hampered by the system.

              This session also saw 22 arcane beats and 8 normal beats (this is in total between six players, we use the group beat system). Combined with some arcane beats from earlier sessions this was just enough to give the players their first arcane Experience.

                      The past isn't written either.
                      Where we all get the hang of 2nd Edition time-travel.

                      Part 1: Time is the simplest thing

                      At the end of the last session the players went back in time to the phone call, however it wasn't really clear which of the two phone calls they were rewinding back to. We discussed that at the start of this session and made a minor retcon that instead the players went back all the way to the beginning of the last session as they were crossing the bridge to Roosevelt island.

                      Scipio, the one member of the group who was not part of the time travel spell, notices something strange with his peripheral senses and activates his mage-sight. He can see a powerful spell suddenly affecting everyone else in the car, the Shifting Sands spell that brought them back to the past.

                      Martina however notices something different. As Darren pulls over the car, her time-sight shows a faint disturbance of temporal ripples down the road continuing where the car had been at that moment in time in the previous timeline. That one is hard to notice, since very little of any consequence was changed by the tires stirring up dust in one timeline and not in the other. However the change to Scipio, now perceiving magic and receiving the explanation that they've traveled back in time is far more dramatic. From him she can see significant time distortions rippling out as their actions alter the past. They realized that time travel may not be a very subtle phenomena to use.

                      Matty uses a mind spell to give Scipio the approximate memories of what happened. (We just used the down and dirty spellcasting rules here, because it was purely for meta-game convenience. Scipio's player was able to read the summary of what he missed last session, so it just allowed everyone to get straight into the game). Together the group plans their actions, this time deciding not to go to he building and meet the real-estate agent. However they realize that even though they've no longer called Dr Acre, at the time that they would have called him his phone will show temporal disturbances that will give something away. They don't know for certain whether he has Time sight or not, but decide that it's not something that they want to risk.

                      Darren drives back to the facility that they found in flames at the end of the last session, they only have about fifteen minutes to make it but between Darren's driving skills and just enough magic not to drive every sleeper along the way insane, they make it there before the first phone call ever happened. Along the way they prepare for the situation to become violent with a lot of spells, the most significant being Soul Armour, Augment Mind (Wits), Body Control, Pierce Deception and Heighten Senses on everyone in the cabal. A lot of temporary willpower is used releasing spells, although Valkyrie declines to release her spells even for such a short duration since she feels it's a potentially dangerous abuse of magic (I can already tell the Guardians of the Veil are going to love her).

                      Martina chooses to use Divination instead with an extra reach for additional information or more complicated questions. The potency and reach gives her detailed answers to three questions.
                      "What will we be faced with if we just walk in there asking for Dr Acre?" - Deception at first, however it's balanced on a knife's edge with much more danger possible.
                      "How long will it take us to find the information we want after we go in?" - They'll learn more information the longer that they're in the facility but to get a full understanding will most likely be around 30 minutes.
                      "What will Dr Acre look like when we meet him?" - Martina sees a vision of a balding middle-aged man with an olive complexion wearing glasses and a lab-coat.

                      They arrive at the facility and see that it's quite a large campus with a three story tall building, several small buildings around it and a fairly high level of security for a corporate site. Other than the lobby however, all the windows are reflective and they can't see into most of the building. There's a checkpoint going in and they see a security guard in the checkpoint booth, another in the lobby and another wandering on a patrol around the building. However the checkpoint doesn't trouble them at all as they drive in with the security guard simply lifting up the boom gate as their car approaches. During the day it doesn't seem as though they're particularly locked down from visitors.

                      Lucas finds something odd about the building. He accesses their wireless network, especially easy when Martina uses Lucky Number to guess their password. However thanks to both pierce deception and his experience as a hacker he realizes that a large amount of the traffic on the wireless network is fake. Simply data being sent around to give the appearance of a large facility's network traffic without anything important like financial details, business records or anything else a real corporation would actually have. There are security cameras but they don't appear to be connected to the wireless network. Lucas and Darren remain behind in the car while the rest go into the building. (Darren's player was still at work and Lucas is an NPC, so this was mostly just for out of game convenience).

                      The group is armed and there's a metal detecting archway at the lobby door, however a quick Machine Invisibility from Lin Wu keeps it from detecting any of their weapons as they pass through. There are two receptionists at the desk and one security guard. Martina uses interconnections and discovers that the fate of all three people are still their own, however someone has a lot of mundane control over them and has heavily influenced their lives. She feels a connection between the two receptionists, as though they're friendly co-workers and a slightly weaker link to the security guard as though they're co-workers that don't spend a lot of time together. They are all connected to the corporate logo on the front of the reception desk and consider themselves employees of that company. (Even though pierce deception shows the company name as a lie, or at least a cover).

                      Martina manages one more Divination to check the future, which again provides three detailed answers.
                      "If trouble starts where will the first danger come from not including the security guard in this room" - Her attention is drawn to a small balcony one level up that has no stairs up but has two doors leading back into the facility. A large window is between the doors but it too is reflective from this side.
                      "If it turns to violence what opposition will we be faced with?" - A very strong opposition to start with but the impression of an almost endless level of escalation and reinforcement possible behind that.
                      "What's the best escape route if trouble starts" - Run out the doors behind them, do not try to get deeper into the facility, go straight for the car Darren is in as a get away vehicle.

                      Matty confidently approaches the desk and with an air of authority demands "Take me to see Dr Acre immediately. He is expecting us."
                      Rules: This was a case of Social Maneuvering where Matty immediately attempted to force the doors. The receptionist had two closed doors from her resolve attribute and although this wasn't usually something she would do, it didn't affect her aspirations, vice, virtue or any other details so it was just two doors in the way. Matty didn't need to make any sort of persuasion roll however since he also cast Emotional Urging to give the receptionist a feeling of corporate intimidation, as though talking to a high ranking executive. With 3 potency on the spell this was enough to open all the doors.

                      The receptionist says "Right away sir" and leads them over to the elevator. The other receptionist, not included in the spell, decides to follow procedure by paging ahead. With their heightened senses however they hear her press the intercom button. Lin Wu uses Remote Control, with enough reach to affect the entire room for a scene and she compels all the mechanical interface for communication devices to fail during that time. With no announcement ahead of them they get in the elevator and head down to the basement.

                      In the elevator Scipio uses Web of Life, over-reaching slightly to cast it on top of his current spell control and to use advanced scale to cover most of the facility. Despite using his dedicated tool (a gaudy cross necklace he wore) and spending a mana to reduce paradox to a chance die, that die came up '10'. Scipio successfully controlled the paradox however and only suffered a level of resistant bashing damage from containing it. The spell still works though and the results above him are fairly typical for what he'd expect from a large corporate facility. However beneath him, on a level that the elevator didn't show, he can sense a large number of people, dogs and apes (the spell was filtered to only show anything larger than a house-cat). The basement level that they exit on, only appears to have four people present in different rooms on either side of the single corridor that they can see. The corridor ends in a T intersection but Scipio can't sense anyone else beyond on this level. Relying on everyone's heightened senses to catch it, Scipio whispers "Living bodies on a level beneath us" too quietly for the receptionist to overhear.

                      The receptionist knocks on the first door on the left and announces "Dr Acre, your visitors are here to see you." Matty says to the receptionist "Thankyou, you can go" in a forceful way. She seems a bit put-off by the tone, since the Emotional Urging spell is no longer affecting her, however since she doesn't want to be there either happily leaves. From inside the room they can hear a person moving over to the door and it opens enough to reveal Dr Acre looking through at them. Both Martina and Lin Wu recognize him as a result of spells that they have previously cast.

                      Dr Acre, looking out at them asks who they are and Matty answers "We're here about the Octagon." (The building they were investigating)
                      "Yes but who are you." Dr Acre says without budging an inch or showing any noticeable reaction. "I'm John Smith."

                      Martina with active Time and Fate Sight feels a strange sense of timelines synchronizing as despite all the changes they've made Fate seems to have somehow dragged events back to a common point between timelines. At the exact moment he said it in the previous time line, Dr Acre smiles and says.

                      "Oh, you're that John Smith."

                          Part 2: Discretion is the better part of Wisdom.

                          The Cabal step into the room, a fairly small office set up for reading, writing and computing rather than a doctor's office. There's enough space for them all but it's tight. Dr Acre remains standing and leans against the wall while the rest crowd in. Then Matty's phone rings with the caller ID showing as Iris calling, he hands the phone to Martina who takes the call and steps out into the corridor.

                          Iris asks whether they've been using time travel, which Martina confirms using a very vague statement to sound casual. Iris, asks whether someone else is there listening which again Martina confirms with another vague statement. Iris, using the Pierce Deception spell for a little extra clarity, is able to get a fairly clear idea as she clarifies the situation with a couple of other questions. After determining that the cabal is safe, or at least not requiring an immediate rescue, Iris ends the call asking them to call back once the situation is dealt with.

                          During this time Matty has been questioning Dr Acre and getting very little out of him. Dr Acre doesn't lie at any point, which the group recognize since they all have pierce deception active however he frequently ignores or even outright admits that he won't answer a question. When Matty says that "discovering evidence of reapers meant that it concerned everyone", Dr Acre looked amused and said "Ah, everyone you say." Matty finally asks directly whether Dr Acre is a reaper, however this gets the direct statement "I have no intention of answering that."

                          A little frustration seems to be setting into the group at his refusal to answer, but before they can try pushing for more Martina outside the room decides to cast a divination. Dr Acre, responding to the magic, glances up towards the corridor where Martina is standing and the cabal can see him gathering his will for an Imago.

                          Rules: At this point, we rolled initiative. Dr Acre rolled well on the dice but the cabal's magical enhancements gave a lot of bonuses. In the end through luck Dr Acre was going around third in the initiative cycle but both Matty and Lin Wu beat him.

                          Matty launches straight into a Psychic Domination saying the command "Don't Cast", targeting it directly at Dr Acre and preparing it with enough potency to be certain to affect the target. However Dr Acre, seeing the spell directly targeting him switches his action to countering it using the first dot Prime attainment. Dr Acre has a much larger dice pool and both he and Matty spend Willpower on the resulting clash of wills. Dr Acre wins and the spell is countered.

                          However as Matty's player points out the Psychic Domination is an act of hubris, he rolls and fails. Not only does he lose a point of Wisdom but he also gains the condition "megalomaniacal". He chooses though that he'll make Psychic Domination an inured spell. Lin Wu begins to chant in preparation for a spell the next round however going around the group everyone is beginning to hold their actions and it looks as though the combat is going to sputter out. Martina rings Lucas and has her phone in her pocket so he can overhear what's going on and steps back in the room. I'm about to declare combat over when...

                          A megalomaniacal Matty casts a powerful Psychic Domination with enough extra reaches to include suicidal commands and gives the command "Submit" to Dr Acre. He spends a willpower and clashes again trying to counter the spell and fails. Despite having the larger dice pool, Dr Acre doesn't manage to counter the spell and is under the effect of the Psychic Domination. Matty demands he say his sympathetic name which he does (Antonio Papanicolaou).

                          Despite Martina trying to calm the situation, Matty (under the influence of the hubris imposed condition) escalates further and orders Scipio to break Dr Acre's hand. Using a pair of brass knuckles Scipio does just that, having plenty of practice working for the mob. A rather scary six lethal damage would have been inflicted, however although Dr Acre doesn't dodge due to psychic domination his Death Shield attainment is still in effect and drops the damage down to six bashing.

                          Matty meanwhile attempts to read Dr Acre's mind using the spell Read the Depths. However although he succeeds no one will ever know what he found out since...

                          A worried and concerned Martina decides that this timeline isn't going much better than the previous one and casts her own Shifting Sands spell, bringing only herself back to that moment where they drove across the bridge to Roosevelt Island.

                          Here's where I started drawing diagrams to keep track of timelines as well.

                          Rules Note: To avoid any abuse of the time travel inside time travel" possibility I used the following rule. Martina could rewind to any point up until the moment they first traveled back to without any problems. However if she wanted to go back earlier than that moment, she'd have to calculate the Reach as though travelling back from the real present, rather than the position in the current time line. I also decided that (despite being somewhat Supernal) the Wisdom loss and inuring against spells can still be undone through time travel.

                          Additional Edit: Just to clarify, Matty ordering Scipio to break Dr Acre's hand wasn't part of a spell or anything. Scipio has the virtue "loyal" and tends to be quite a team-player. Generally if one of his cabal-mates asks him to do something, most of the time he'll do it. Since he's a professional knee-capper for the mob, breaking someone's hand as an intimidation tactic wasn't a very unusual request for him.

                              Part 3: Third time's the rote.

                              Once again we're back at the start of this little adventure, however our time travelers reach the present this time around and so (for now) this is the genuine timeline of events.

                              Thanks to mind magic, Martina shares her memories of the previous timeline with the rest of the cabal. However they only have the memories from Martina's point of view, this is why Matty's Read the Depths never got answered (Martina's player chose to time travel while we were still looking up the telepathy rules). Since Martina has a photographic memory though, that gives them a lot of detail.

                              This time around the cabal decides to call the cavalry straight away. Martina rings Iris and they organize to meet at a cafe nearby along with Phoenix and Andraste, two sentinels from Iris' cabal. The cabal informs the consillium about everything, with the exception of Matty's strange experience with the whispers merit and Lin Wu's knowledge of the Soul Jar rote. They share Dr Acre's sympathetic name as well when Phoenix asks. This time they allow the consillium to take care of everything themselves and go into hiding while it happens. Phoenix suggests that they find somewhere safe and since the cabal don't have their own safe place yet, Phoenix suggests asking the Renewal Cabal.

                              The Renewal cabal is a small group with only four members. Although they're part of the Free Council, they have a lot of respect within the diamond orders since they're a very helpful and charitable group who don't really play politics. They're also friends with the players' cabal. Ash, one of the Renewal cabal, is the mentor of both Darren and Martina. The Renewal cabal own a small junk-yard in Queens which has both a demense, a hallow and a ley-line nexus (all aspected towards, not surprisingly, 'renewal'). Even after being warned that it may be dangerous, the Renewal cabal insist that they're happy to take the players in while the consillium investigates the situation. In exchange the players' cabal brings a pizza dinner for everyone.

                              To Martina the piles of junk, the crowded workroom filled with half-finished projects and the crash-space of sleeping bags and bean bags is a wonderful artistic place of mystery and enchantment. To Lin Wu it's a million different sources of possible infection, when she arrives Lin Wu uses a Life spell to try to sterilize the environment. Matty and Valkyrie see other risks in the place and, after asking the Renewal cabal's permission, start placing several wards and protections on the site just in case. The junkyard was already hidden with a powerful veil, but Renewal doesn't have a Mastigos to place any sort of Spaces wards and aren't paranoid enough to have a lot of other protective spells.

                              The Cabal spend several hours there before they receive a phone call from the sentinel Phoenix. Since the group was so cooperative in sharing information with the consillium, Phoenix offers to debrief them on what they found out as a fair exchange. They meet back up, this time at a local IRS office which gives Matty heart palpitations to deliberately walk into an "audit". The IRS office however is just one of the places where the consillium handles its secret business, figuring it's one of the last places anyone would look for a group of pentacle mages.

                              The consillium didn't have much more luck than the players. The facility was destroyed once again with the strange Death-forces fires that burn away metaphysical evidence, although Phoenix tells them that they're pretty certain the fires were the result of a powerful supernal artifact rather than a spell. Dr Acre, along with several other staff at the facility, were evacuated by a heavily armed helicopter landing on the roof. There were at least several mages evacuated and they took steps to cover their tracks to make themselves very difficult to follow magically. The consillium is continuing to investigate but suspects that at this stage the investigation will be a long slow one. They haven't used their knowledge of Dr Acre's sympathetic name yet, preferring to hold onto that sort of advantage in secret until it's most useful.

                              They discovered that for each awakened soul stored in the living soul jars at the octagon, there was a soul-less person originally at the facility. However when the facility was evacuated some of the presumed prisoners were killed with a lethal injection, others were allowed to wander out onto the streets dazed and confused (having been without souls for so long that they were empty husks of people). Phoenix believes the explanation is that there's no way, magically, of removing all traces of sympathy between a soul and its rightful owner since the two are truly one and the same. Once the consillium had the souls of these prisoners, retaining the bodies was a liability to Dr Acre's group which they dealt with coldly and efficiently.

                              Martina is concerned whether the prisoners can be healed and have their souls returned to them. Phoenix explains that it's certainly possible, but they're checking for safety first and trying to find out who the prisoners are to make sure it's not some sort of trick or trap. He expects within a few weeks that they'll be able to do just that. For the dead prisoners Martina asks to be allowed to go see them and pray for them. Phoenix says that the bodies have already been released back to sleeper custody and tells Martina which morgue to go to.

                              As for the appartments on Roosevelt island, the consillium has already removed all the creepy facilities and taken custody of the re-animated corpses used as soul jars. Phoenix says that he doesn't believe that Dr Acre's group is going to return, with the amount of bridges burnt behind them they're unlikely to ever go back to a compromised location. While it isn't confirmed, the largely unspoken assumption around the meeting table is that Dr Acre and his group were Seers of the Throne. The hallow and building are now the property of the players' cabal (at least in the eyes of the pentacle mages), granting the players their sanctum, safe place and hallow dots they've purchased at last. It will take a little more time to get the sleeper authorities to recognize the cabal's ownership of the building, but for that we now go into a period of downtime.

                              Note: This actually wasn't the end of the session. However it was the end of that particular story. For most of the rest of the session we took care of some downtime administration, XP expenditure, players' personal subplots and that sort of thing. Towards the end we did have some more significant story events which I will recap shortly, however they will require some additional explanation since they are heavily tied into some past sessions that I haven't covered in any of my recaps so far.
