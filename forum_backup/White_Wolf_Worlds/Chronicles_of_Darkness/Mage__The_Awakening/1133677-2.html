    Shadow Name: I would probably go with something akin to P'tah or Bastet. I dunno why, but I simply love the Egyptian Aesthetic. That and the reasoning for the latter is because it would match up well with how I go by "Sally" at most places, but am actually male (short story: nickname from a best friend).

    Path: I feel that I would be best suited as an Acanthus.The reason I feel strongly with this one is perhaps because at heart, I am a huge procrastinator and tend to take care of things either at the last second or already have things planned out so I don't need to worry about them. Not to mention that I feel I would resonate abit with some of the things in their symbology, such as seeing things as if they were a story or creating stories and what not and following the story's rules rather than that of reality's. Not to mention I do feel as if fortune (not predestined fate, more like fickle fortune) is a thing.

    Order: The best order I could imagine myself in would have to be the Mysterium. To put it in abit of perspective, remember how Hermoine was the most excited to learn about magic and Hogwarts and a part of her character seemed not to understand why Ron and Harry weren't as excited as she was? Because she had lived most of her life in a world without magic, and now being able to learn it she wants to learn as much as she can about it. And that line of thinking is why I feel I'd want to be in the Mysterium. I want to learn everything I possibly can.

    Legacy: The one that interests me the most would have to be the Daoine. There's many things I like about them. Etiquette, politeness and bringing good fortune to those who need it and raining bad fortune on those who deserve it, and a delicious sense of irony. Not to mention forcing corrupt people to keep their promises is something I would love inflicting.

    Arcana: In order of most to least importance and out of five Arcana, Fate, Life, Time, Prime and Space. Fate because in this world, nothing is certain and being able to shift that is something I would love to utilize. Life because of certain benefits and to stay healthy. Time mainly for augury and what not. Prime because it's always good to have it to drain mana or break spells. As for Space, I feel I would only use it for Sympathetic Casting and nothing more. Might learn up enough to open portals if I particularly need it but other than that, I wouldn't master it.

    Daily Business: I think what I would first do post-Awakening is attempt to see if it was real and not just some fanciful dream. Then after some testing and confirmation that I could bend reality, I would try to do a mix mash of "trying to keep it a secret" and "trying to see if there's anyone else like me". Given where I live, I heavily doubt there's a magely presence befitting a consilium, but I wouldn't mind that.

    Provided I did get accepted into the Mysterium and the Daoine though, my first and foremost business would be a desire to learn more about the supernatural world. About vampires, about werewolves, about changelings, etcetc. I would probably try to do this by trying to be polite and respectful, asking for an interview or two and offering good fortune (be it fate magic or money) in exchange for it.

    Granted, given my want to also live an easy life, I would probably try to utilize lotteries and the stock market in order to get loads of money and lose the need for a job. GRANTED, I would try to be secretive about it. Trying to do this post in a nutshell. http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...48#post1007948

    Archmage: I feel if I knew it was possible (and if magic were real, I would believe that anything's possible), I would try going for it. If I actually made it though, I would prolly be kind of like Featherine Augustus Aurora from Umineko. Trying to stave off boredom by watching other people's lives, sometimes adding abit of "spice" to it to make it more interesting. I'd prolly try to be good about it and only give the good guy a feel for it.

    Granted, I also feel I would use my newfound power to rip a new one on the True Fae and helping some Changelings escape.

    Currently Doing: Siren the Drowning (Fansplat Ressurection) http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...e-resurrection

                    Shadow Name: Vagus (Latin for Wanderer) Pretty much to the point of what i'll be doing with myself on the path to enlightenment.

                    Path: Obrimos speaks to me in many ways. I tend to be a very optimistic if not Hopeful person. The world can be an ugly, cruel place, we should strive to do better, and for me as a mage in a "World of Darkness".... i'm gonna be busy. Though i don't condone the zealotry or possible dogmatic bent some obrimos may have. That over-complicates if not muddies the efforts that a obrimos mage can truly do. I'd rather go for a open-minded if not diplomatic approach to my encounters. Mage, human , or the supernatural. Though strangely enough my personality often strongly reflects the thoughts and behaviors of an Acanthus. Free-spirited , Hating to be tied down and sometimes playing the fool. But in the end i know where my convictions lie. In a nut shell. "Mind of an Acanthus--Soul of an Obrimos"

                    Order: The Free Council would probably be my way to go. My desire to help those in need without being tided down in Mage politics or traditions, suits my personal outlook just fine. Helping, inspiring, and motivating Mortals (Sleepers) and fellow mages. If they want my help great, if not i can move on to the next person that may need it. Also i can learn new things in the council that the traditions may never catch wind of.

                    Legacy: The legacy i feel the strongest to is the Perfected Adepts. There tempered philosophy and focus on humility is something that i relate to. Also taking martial arts as a life time hobby of mine, makes this legacy extremely comfy for me. They understand their still human and we should always keep that in mind. They also want to bring there wisdom back down to humanity. I find that mixes very well with my ideals to aid Mortals if not the world.

                    Arcana: Now the Two Arcana that i would focus on Mastering is Prime and Life with at least a discipline on Spirit and Mind. As three out of four of those arcana reflect to the martial teaching of perfecting the Mind, Body and Soul. Though i may take a initiate level in Death. To be able to see and speak to ghosts. With the Spirit and Mind Arcana i can also cross into the Shadow and Astral planes. Which will feed into my love in going to new exotic places and learning from my experiences,

                    Daily Business: For me i would become a Wanderlust mage/monk. Globetrotting all over. Helping sleepers mostly and aiding/dealing with the supernatural. I would play it low-key. Wearing casual travel attire if not being a Back-packer. Riding on foot, buses, trains,etc. Not truly having a destination per say. Just going with the flow, and seeing where i turn up. I could learn the space arcana to help me travel, but then i'd miss the rest of the world around me, and overlook people or super natural beings that i would have skipped over. Besides it's more enjoyable for me to enjoy the ride. While on my travels I'd call in with the Council and if need be, the Arrow every now and then to keep them informed on certain "Hot-spots" that may deal with magic or issues with the super-natural. Though i wouldn't only travel the world ,but also the shadow and astral planes. Learning from spirits. Diving into dreams to learn or help mortals with there issues as well as aiding changelings. Becoming the Do-gooder planes walker/drifter.



                    Archmage: If i ever reach that level, i'd think my wanderlust for the world may wane a bit, and i would focus more on the other planes of existence. Most likely the Astral plane and the Hedge. Getting in cozy with Changelings out of all the supernaturals. Become more of the youthful sage helping to enlighten in the Material world and in Dreams. Going on journeys of self discovery and improvement. Fighting Inner--demons, Beasts, True-Fae. Never-ending journey.


                    "I am a daydreamer and a Night thinker."~
