                                    If we're getting back to "things we like about the new edition"... almost everything, really. Yantras are great. The new Paradox system is great (I've mentioned a few times how happy the player with the Guardian, Death-focused Moros in my group is that he can actually use his primary Arcanum Covertly now). The Reach system meaning spells actually stick to their Practices now is great - you immediately know what dot level a spell is based on what the spell does (and with the Practice of Ruling being 2 dots in both editions, "all spells to control things are 2 dots" means even starting Mages are scary). The Reach system meaning Paradox is something you want to risk rather than a result of casting a prohibited spell is excellent - my ST is pleased about that one to know end, as he's been trying to tempt us with Abyssal stuff forever to very little success. Praxes are a wonderful new edition - we finally have "signature spells" that actually are your signature spells, rather than something invented by some hoary old Master in your Order and taught to you by them. The changes to Mage Sight and Nimbus are also super neat. I also like the changes to Legacies - getting into a Legacy at Gnosis 2 is pretty great, and I can't say no to 5 Attainments instead of 3.

                                    On the fluff side of things, I really like some of the changes/clarifications there, most of which I've mentioned before. I like that the cultish "secret tenets" Guardians from their Orderbook are expressly stated to be a minority, with the majority of Guardians being the ones presented in the original corebook. Likewise, I like that there's room for philosophical/ideological Banishers in the new edition - "their souls are just broken and if they were like us they would believe what we do" was far too neat and tidy a way of sidestepping the idea of there being Mages who believe that no, fallible humans don't deserve to have the powers of gods. I also like the redefining of the relationship between the Diamond and the Free Council, and the renewed emphasis on the Free Council as the Sleeper Culture Order rather than just hyper-modernist technomages. The Free Council being the one Order that saw value in the Fallen World and its works in and of themselves (rather than as "scattered, surviving remnants of lost Truth" was what drew me to them in the first place.

                                                For things I'm sad about - I hoped that Rote Skills will either disappear, or they will be more open and free to pick from. I really disliked 1ED Rote Skill propositions, and literally no player I played with had occasion to get that +1Rote Skill bonus; it ended up being like Mana, forgotten and never used, simply because players had other skill ideas in mind and favored rotes with different skills encoded in them.                                                 If you really want to give each Order 3 rote skills, at least pick one from each skill category, so you can showcase typical physical, mental and social activities of typical Ordermember, instead of making me hate myself for playing mental oriented or physical oriented Ladder instead of Social one
