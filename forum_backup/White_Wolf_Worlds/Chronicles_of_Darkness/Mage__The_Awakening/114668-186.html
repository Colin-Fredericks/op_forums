        So Dave posted saying that you can spend willpower while say mind controlled to raise the withstand stat and if it decreases the potency of the spell to zero then you are no longer effected by it for 3+ seconds per willpower spent.

        Withstand reduces the effect of a spell's Potency, it doesn't remove the spell. If someone's withstand rating changes part-way through a spell's Duration, up OR down, it gets reassessed. If your mind-control spell that isn't potent enough to beat someone's resolve is still active when something else reduces their resolve to below Potency, your mind control spell begins to work. Alternatively, if you mind control someone to kill themselves, they *can* spend Willpower at the last minute to up their Resolve for a turn. Or another mage can cast a spell that increases their Resolve, with a longer duration than your mind-control one.
        This means that spells such as "Befuddle", "Degrading the Form", and "Alter Integrity" behave oddly...so probably need different withstand traits. Personally i see no reason not to add an additional command of "Dont resist" to all mind control spells now.
