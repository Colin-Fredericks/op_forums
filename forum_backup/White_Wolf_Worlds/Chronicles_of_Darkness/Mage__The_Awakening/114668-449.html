Since nothing seems to prevent a Soul-Shocked character from re-entering the Astral before the condition is resolved, is there anything official on what might happen if an already soul-shocked character re-enters the Astral and "dies" again?     2. Generally speaking, a Mage does not have to worry about witnesses causing Paradox while in the Tenemos, correct? They can still over-Reach, but, barring truly exceptional circumstances, there's no actual Sleepers around, just Goetia.

    3. A bit off-the-wall, but a Harrowed Banisher would still be able to use magic in his Oneiros without fear of Paradox like a normal Mage, correct? Presumably he'd still suffer the normal Harrowed problems, regardless.

        The consequences of being "killed" in your own Oneiros by its own contents suggest that "dying" in the Astral otherwise still exhausts your Dream Health, which needs time and/or magic to recover as normal.

        Also, I don't think 1e's hazards stopped being risks of Astral travel in the edition change; the Ecstatic Wind will still warp your soul, things from the Temenos can still warp your mind, and self-destructive behavior still has a reasonable chance of being an Act of Hubris.

            Soul Shocked means a diminished sense of self. So what happens when you are shocked again ? A couple of things come to mind:

            1- Your lost sense of self connected you to another mage's Oneiros. You probably saw the Iron Door and regretted becoming an Egregore. Then again, that is the least of your problems, your Daimons seems.... off...somehow (you swapped a Virtue/Vice/Aspiration/Obsession).

            2- Rather than returning to the real world, you fell into an Astral Emanation and need to exit it to return to your physical body, better hurry before it starves.

            3- Something was invited into your mind. Perhaps a drone of the Morphean Continuity, The Unforgotten or even the mythos of your Shadow Name.

            4- You're fine, what were you worried about? You also found a hidden operation of the Praetorian, too bad you have no idea who is the Seer whose body you inhabited. You should probably worry about where their mind went.

            5- Your lesser sense of self makes you sensible to others, too sensible. Whenever you fall asleep, your mind is projected to a being talking or thinking about you, forcing you to share their senses for a scene. These beings gain a Strong sympathetic connection to the mage until the next time they dream of someone else or their sense of self is restored.

                        Orders aren't "in" a Caucus, a Caucus is a the local political organization for a particular Order. So you might have the Adamantine Arrow Caucus of the Pacific Northwest that includes Arrows that are members of all Consillia and/or Assemblies in the PNW region.

                        If you meant "Consillia" then they're just separate things. Order Status lets you requisition from your Caucus, or other Caucus of the same Order at a penalty. Consillium Status lets you requisition from the Consillium. Consillium requisitions should be broader, but shallower: you have a bit of everything available to every Order represented in the Consillium, but only what the members can/will provide.

                                Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post


                                I see. But Signs of Sorcery seems to imply members of the same Concilia can be under the influence of more than one Caucus, but a Caucus seems like they would have very big areas of influence. So they can overlap ?

                                A single Caucus covers a large region, but each Caucus is Order specific. So while the Arrow might have a single Caucus covering the entire PNW, the Free Council might have three Caucuses in that same area, the Guardians might have one for the entire North American West Coast, and so on. So a single Consillium, say Seattle and its environs, would be under five different Caucuses (one for each Order), but a different Consillium; San Francisco might be under the same Caucus as Seattle for the Free Council, the Mysterium, and the Silver Ladder, but a different one for the Adamantine Arrow and the Guardians of the Veil.


                                A single Consillium is "part" of every Order Caucus for which it has members of the Order and falls under their region of influence. A single Caucus will usually cover multiple Consillia. Each Order has its own set of Caucuses, and each Order's Caucuses do not necessarily have the same regions of influence as another Order's.

                                        Sensory range means that the subject you will teleport can be anywhere within your sensory range. By default you need to touch them or can only teleport yourself.

                                        The destinations can be anywhere within sensory range.

                                        A sympathetic cast can be used to teleport the subject to a location outside of sensory range. So you could send them to Mars if you had a picture from the NASA rover.

                                        Alternatively, the sympathetic cast can be used to affect a subject outside of sensory range and teleport them to any point within the caster's sensory range. In this way, you could pull an imbued item from your Sanctum.

                                        You could use sympathy to cast on a subject outside of sensory range and move them somewhere outside sensory range. But you would need the one mana for the Attainment, commit two yantra slots and deal with a Withstand of the weakest connection +1, plus any other wards they may have used. So its doable, but would probably need preparations.

                                        In most mundane situations, Co-Location is sufficient. But without expending Reach on a Key, anyone with the most basic notion of Space magic or granted Space Mage Sight can use it. Even with a Key, it can be followed back for scrying, remote casting, etc.

                                        Teleportation is a lot cleaner, no magical doorway required, just a quick rewrite of the location from A to B. Well, hopefully, if they don't follow any other Nimbus impressions left by the caster.

                                        Hope that helps.

                                                Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post

                                                That makes it a lot clearer, thank you.

                                                Regarding requisition, how exactly does the Order Archive merit behave in those scenarios?
                                                In addition to the Order Archive Merit's extended action roll and security benefits, it helps the ST determine what is most readily available to the Caucus.

                                                A 3-dot Lorehouse might have Imbued Items, Grimoires, and Mentor as its Assets.
                                                A Libertine wishing to requisition a Familiar would add one to the availability rating, as Familiars are not one of the Assets of the Lorehouse.
                                                If instead they sought the benefits of the Library Merit (not an Asset, so +1 availability) for research into the burial rites of the Chumash peoples (-1 availability because it pertains to Sleeper culture) they would simply requisition it at the dot rating of the Library.
                                                And if they were seeking an Imbued (an Asset) cell phone (Sleeper technology) they'd be able to grab it at -1 availability from its actual rating.


                                                Also, it's not clear, but I think:

                                                The character treats her Status Merit as one dot lower if
                                                she requisitions the resource from a Caucus not her own
                                                but within her home Consilium or Assembly, two dots
                                                lower if from a Caucus within the same Convocation,
                                                and three dots lower if from a Caucus unrelated to her
                                                home one.
                                                is combining Consillium/Assembly requisitions and Caucus requisitions. So an Arrow using their Consillium Status to requisition an item from an Athenaeum (Mysterium) would treat their Consillium status as one lower; the same Arrow using their Order status to requisition an item from an Armory of a different Arrow Caucus whose members are part of the same Convocation treats their Order status as two lower; and if the Armory is completely unrelated their Order status is three lower.

                                                But that's just my interpretation, and could be wrong; my game's Consillia and Assemblies are more akin to Convocations already, covering large geographical areas (all of the French speaking regions of Europe share one Consillium, likewise the German speaking regions, &c; the PNW, South Western US, &c. have single Consillia or Assemblies; &c.)
