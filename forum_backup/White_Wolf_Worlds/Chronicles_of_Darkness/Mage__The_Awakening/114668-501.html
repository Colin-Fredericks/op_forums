                                I like to think of the Platonic Form spell in more metaphysical terms; you're altering the Mana (which I can regard as an energy that kind of reinforces the reality of situations or subjects) into the form of a Supernal template of things, sans the gross material they would normally take the form of. The template can include a thing's properties (in the philosophical sense), and expenditure of Mana reinforces those properties through action; it does not require the Phenomenal thing (although conversely the Phenomenal thing does not require expenditures of Mana because it exists).

                                I can see Matter spells that would reinforce its properties, but I'm thinking you can't make a Platonic Form physical enough that it's not spending Mana every time it does something, because by the point you've added enough matter that it doesn't need Mana anymore you've probably just supplanted the template with an actual thing. Like, it seems like the level of the Matter Arcanum where you're shaping some available instance of suitable substance into an actual object, which renders the template form a bit unnecessary.

                                            I would say that a Patterning of Mind can transform your own unmoored consciousness into a Goetia sure, although I'd say the communication back would need to be a matter of an additional spell to communicate back information.

                                            Either that or it could be a Numen of the Goetia form. It looks to me like usual spells to create ephemeral entities don't inherently give you power to define things like Numina, so I'm thinking that would also be a case with magic to transform one thing into something ephemeral; an additional spell could be used to modify them. However, I'd also say that an ephemeral being assumes a form according to a general parameter that can include Numina useful for the intended function; not enough to mean the mage never has to cast such additional transformation, but enough to not be too inconvenient.

                                            Originally posted by KaiserAfini
                                            Additional effects seem to include some variation of Shielding from entropy (Shielding of Death or Veil of Moments), something to keep them active via mana (Patterning of Prime and Mind perhaps) and maybe some weird variation of teleportation.                                             The Shroud of Observation is an 8-dot Artifact, so I think its spell is just straight up Practice of Entity* of Mind to fully transform the Sleepwalker into a Grigori, where things like the bodily preservation or Resonance with and teleportation to a defined target are innate to the definition of what a Grigori is. I think the Mana cost could be regarded as a necessity of something casting an Imperial Practice, particularly if it's according to a recurring template.

                                            If it's an Imperial Practice, then Dispel magic won't work on it (it takes the Practice of Excision to end Imperial spells), and apart from the limited power I think the transformation could be regarded as going beyond the scope of what counts as a constraint on free will for Sever Oaths to be able to affect it.

                                            * I'd very much say that kind of magic or equivalent is the basis of all Seer servitors; the Tutor that creates Hollow Ones is a bit different, but I'd say any tetrarch who observes it would find the effect to follow the same basic principles as Excision, and could achieve the same effect on a subject themselves with that Practice (having a creature to do it is simply more efficient, and I suspect allows the creation of Hollow Ones on a much greater scale than Grigori, Myrmidons or the Hive-Souled).
