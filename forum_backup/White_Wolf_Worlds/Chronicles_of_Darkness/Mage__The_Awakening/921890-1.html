                    Maybe it's because I'm up to Changes in the series, but I don't see Harry as a Vidanti. He's constantly throwing down challenges to bad guys and rivals. Heck, he gets more prone to challenge people head-to-head as the series goes on.

                    The below will contain spoilers up to Changes, so be warned.

                    Harry is definitely an Obrimos- his go-to spells are wind and fire, with some lightning thrown in as conditions allow. His stunt with Sue bespeaks either insane luck or some talent with Death, aided by the necromantic energies being gathered for the Darkhollow and the fact that there was a convenient leyline.

                    Molly is a Mastigos who started out with major talent in Mind, but the White Council's prohibition on tampering with other's minds led Harry to coach her in other Arcana, particularly Forces, to the point that she can essentially become a one-woman rave through the creation of lights and sounds.

                    Carlos shows off a lot of water magic, which is Matter. Luccio could be an Obrimos, what with that laser-beam-of-white-hot-fire spell in Dead Beat. Rashid, being the Gatekeeper, shows a lot of talent with Space, but his true power seems to rest with Time; the dude definitely has pretty high Wisdom, seeing as how he knows exactly how to get people to act without altering the future without changing things too drastically.

                        I'm current with the series, so I will try to offer opinions without spoilers:

                        I'm on board with Harry being Obrimos / Arrow. He definitely started out as Apostate, just trying to stay below the Council's radar and not get in any more trouble. Basically he ended up getting forced to choose and the only choice available to him was Arrow. He still would prefer to not -have- to fight. He has reiterated on multiple occasions that his joy is magic itself - just studying it and experimenting with it for its own sake - but when push comes to shove, Chicago is HIS city and he WILL defend it from all comers using every tool in his vast magical arsenal.

                        What I'm -not- sure how to reconcile via Mage or other Chronicles rule-sets is the more recent stuff (from Changes to present) regarding Mab and the choice he makes there.
