      Teamwork for ritual spells works the same as normal teamwork, but instant casting speed spells do not benefit (see casting time 2e core pg 114).

      Mages without any dots in the spell's highest arcanum, along with sleepwalkers, promixi and sleepers may assist in the casting by helping with ritual chanting, lighting incense, or other appropriate activities relevant to the ritual. They collectively counts as an environment yantra bonus (+1 dice).

      Mages with at least 1 dot in the spell's highest arcanum (**should this be all required Arcana?) May roll a dice pool of Gnosis -3 (untrained penalty) providing any successes as bonus dice to the primary caster.

      Mages able to cast the entire spell may roll as normal, adding any successes as bonus dice to the primary caster.

      Each secondary participant must roll for paradox, and any uncontained paradox will affect the final spell (allowing for clever mage/Scelesti to sabotage the endeavour).

      (See pg 119 of 2e core rules).

      Other ways to help:

      Life: Body Control (see pg 148/149 2e core) metabolism control reduces the need for bio breaks/sleep (may be useful for longer rituals, especially at low Gnosis)
      (add combined Mind to remove the need for sleep).

      Prime: As Above, So Below (see pg 166 2e core) granting secondary caster(s) 9/8-again on their yantras to help increase the number of successes.

      Mind: Ritual focus (see pg 55 SoS)

      Time: Choose the threads (see pg 187 2e core)

      Death: Ritual Attunement (homebrew, see Below).

      Cf advanced rules from SoS (pg55) which are (optionally) really good.

      Also for Mysterium member see their order merit's first dot (pg 101 2e core).

          Homebrew:

          Ritual Attunement
          Death (••) Ruling
          Primary factor: duration
          Suggested rote skills: Crafts, Occult, Persuasion

          By making their souls more attuned the mage may help ritual practicioners synchronize their performance. Adds 1 die for any secondary caster assisting with a ritual spellcasting.

          This spell must last the duration of the ritual, otherwise the lose of Attunement will reduce all secondary spellcaster's die pools by -2 (see also interruption rules, SoS pg 56)

          +1 reach: the combined soul Auras makes the seperate signature nimbuses more difficult to discern. Surface information about a spell cast while under the effect of this reach will reveal a single Nimbus which appear to be a mixture of all the casters combined (deep information will still reveal the seperate nimbuses).

              Ritual Attunement
              Death (••) Ruling
              Primary factor: duration
              Suggested rote skills: Stealth, Occult, Subterfuge

              By making their souls more attuned the mage may help ritual practicioners perform more synchronized. The subjects may add 1 die per potency of the spell to any empathy checks between them and other subjects. And adds 1 die for any secondary caster assisting with a ritual spellcasting.

              This spell must last the duration of the ritual, otherwise the sudden change will reduce all secondary spellcaster's die pools by -2.

              +1 reach: When used during a Supernal Summoning, the subjects may be attuned to a single path. This negated the usual modifier for mage from the wrong path being present.​
              Death might be an Arcanum concerned with souls, but it feels like an unfitting choice for what the effect of a spell such as this would be. Bonuses to Empathy checks feel distinctly out of place with Arcanum and the overall focus of the spell's effect, and possibly functionally useless if they're all engaged in a ritual spellcasting for the duration.

              I also feel as though an extra distinction about the spell needing to be as long as the ritual is a bit pointless, considering how rituals only make a single roll anyway. It doesn't really make much sense that somebody would cast a spell to coordinate a team for only one hour when it's going to take another two hours after that before they even make their roll, because then they got nothing out of it at all. For that matter, the description of a "sudden" change seems odd, when it's likely that it would be a long time after the spell before they even get around to making their rolls.

              Actually, not just likely; at minimum, a ritual could take three hours against a one hour spell, in which case the spell ends prematurely and they've got that amount of time to recover from it. It only takes one step higher on the extended duration chart to make a spell last a day, which is longer than any ritual is capable of being (and considering that you've made this have Duration as the primary factor, even though Potency is the main thing it benefits from, all it takes is one Reach to ensure this spell lasts for a day by default). It feels like a bit of an unnecessary detail when I think most people can be assumed to recognise that a spell such as this is something that has to work across a ritual, rather than be tossed in for a few turns at the end or something.

              Being able to make it so that other mages don't count as different Paths feels like it would be a bit beyond the scope of Death 2, given the nature of Supernal Summoning.
