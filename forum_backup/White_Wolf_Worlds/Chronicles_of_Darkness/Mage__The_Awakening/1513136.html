    Tamers of Nightmares [Homebrew Left-Handed Legacy]

    05-28-2023, 08:03 PM
    This Legacy would probably be considered Left Handed in almost any Consilium or Assembly.

    The absolute most morally upstanding version of the Tamers of Nightmares is a Dark Knight style Batman: a vigilante that takes the law into their own hands in an attempt to scare villains straight, i.e. a criminal who does nothing to address the root causes of societal ills despite the power to do so.

    The majority of Tamers of Nightmares simply see a Mystery and pursue it for their own "enlightenment," if the Mære​ have a home in the Pentacle it is likely with the Mysterium, in the Seers they're most at home under the Raptor, Exarch of Life.

    Tamers of Nightmares

    Humanity has always believed in monsters.

    And that’s because monsters? They’re real. There are things out there that go bump in the night, who tear human flesh from bone, stalk people down dark alleys at night.

    You know, you command them, and they do your bidding. Some you have bred yourself with magic, others you’ve repurposed from natural animals and made stronger. And all have been enhanced with the stuff of humanity’s very own nightmares.

    Because those nightmares? They’re also real, etched indelibly into the Temenos, the shared soul of humanity in a region called the Primordial Dream. And this shared fear is so deep seated and powerful it creates physical spaces within this otherwise non-physical world, spaces that connect between sites of profound fear and terror to other such locations based on the shared resonance. These are the Primordial Pathways, and they are the reason that monsters can disappear so quickly after the attack, or be in seemingly impossibly far away locations one after another, because human fear knows not mundane distance.

    Navigating these Primordial Pathways is not without risk, however, an Awakened human is still a human, and those seeking to travel uninvited through the lairs of monsters best be prepared to handle themselves… until you’ve become powerful enough to etch yourself as a Monster in humanity’s collective consciousness and establish your own Lair in the Primordial Dream.



    Origins

    Parentage:     Thyrsus

    Nicknames: Mæres (Mares), Terror Bringers.

    Background: Those with an obsession for monsters, a fascination with the root causes of fear, or those who desire not just their enemies’ demise, but their complete mental and emotional destruction.

    Appearances: The Tamers of Nightmares either dress to intimidate, wearing clothing meant to evoke fear in onlookers, or to hide themselves from view, allowing their pet monsters to “do the talking.”


    Doctrine

    Prerequisites:     Mind 2, Intimidate 2, Animal Ken 2, and a predator, scavenger, or classically frightening animal with whom they have the Bonded condition.

    Initiation: The prospective Mære must work with their animal to bring terror to a person. Those Mæres worried about “morality” or “being a good person” choose targets they believe deserve to be subject to a living nightmare, but the truth is they’re scaring the everliving shit out of someone because they want to, there is no judge or jury involved.

    Organization: Mæres have no real organizational hierarchy: those with the largest Lairs have the biggest impact on the local Temenos and can assert some degree of influence over others, and Mæres with a Verge attuned to Life can gain significant social currency by allowing their fellows to use it as a breeding ground for monsters. Unless two Mæres are on opposite sides of the Pentacle/Seer conflict or involved in inter-Cabal/Pylon rivalries they will usually see each other as kindred spirits and will share information on Primordial Pathways.


    Magic

    Ruling Arcanum:     Mind

    Yantras: Places writ large in the local conscious as being places of terror (+2), the location of a recent (less than a week old) fear-related breaking point (+2), generally “spooky” locations (+1), terrifying images or sounds (+1), causing a fear-related breaking point (+3).

    Oblations: evoking feelings of fear, horror, or discomfort related to those emotions.

    Theory: Nightmares are an essential part of humanity with clear supernatural effects. By mastering these nightmares we master our own humanity and our magic.


    Attainments

    First: You Cannot Hide

    Prerequisite:     Initiation

    The first step in her studies of humanity’s relationship to its own Fear, the Mære and any beast with whom she has the Bonded condition gain the ability to sense the exact location of anyone within sensory range with a fear related Condition or Tilt (e.g. Spooked, Beaten Down) by feeling the weft and weave of the local Primordial Dream. Any mundane attempts to hide from the Mage or her pets automatically fails, supernatural attempts to hide provoke a Clash of Wills on which the Mage enjoys a +4 duration bonus; this is a Mind effect. Additionally, the Mære and her pets can sense Irises to Primordial Pathways, if one is not in sensory range she instead feels a tug towards the nearest one, and can open the Primordial Pathway for a point of Mana and a Willpower; however, traversing Primordial Pathways without invitation is not without its own dangers (see below).

    Optional: Life 1

    Within the Fear is knowledge, the Mære gains the ability to speak with all manner of frightening beasts as though under a persistent Speak with Beasts spell, though rather than being limited to a single species, the Mage can speak with any animal strongly associated with feelings of fear in the local Temenos and any with whom they have the Bonded condition.


    Second: But You Should Run

    Prerequisite:     Mind 2, and either Intimidate 3 or Animal Ken 3

    As a Mære’s research expands, she quickly realizes that merely sensing fear is not enough, she must also measure the effects on the Primordial Dream that occur when fear is enhanced. As an instant action the Mære can cause a wave of supernatural fear to emanate from either herself or one of her bonded animals, enhancing the terror of anyone nearby. This Attainment cannot cause fear from nothing, but will inflict the Cowed or Demoralized Conditions on anyone already feeling fearful, if the subject already has one of those conditions (and it wasn’t caused by a spell or Attainment) then instead inflict either Frightened or Beaten Down. This Attainment has potency equal to the Mære’s dots in Mind, is Withstood by composure, and affects an area equivalent to a small warehouse or parking lot (large warehouse or parking lot at Mind 4).

    Optional: Life 2
    By harnessing the results of the primary portion of this attainment the Mære fuels the power of her and her monstrous pets, against anyone experiencing a fear based condition both she and her pets enjoy 9-again on their Physical Attribute rolls and Social skill rolls affected by appearance against the target. If that Condition is Frightened or the target has the Beaten Down tilt they enjoy 8-again instead.



    Third: So I Can Chase You

    Prerequisite:     Mind 3, both Intimidate and Animal Ken at 3 dots.

    Through her research into the effects of fear on the Primordial Dream, the Mære can now create her own Lair, with its own Primordial Pathways. She gains a Lair rating based on her Mind dots (see the Lair table below) which dictates the number of Chambers that can be incorporated and the number of spells the Lair can support (spells equal to or under this limit do not count against spell control so long as they’re contained wholly within the Lair), one of these Chambers, called the Heart Chamber, is unconnected to a physical location and forms in the Temenos purely from the Mage’s own psyche.
    Chambers can be created from any location at which a human suffered a fear-related breaking point within the last month, to do so the Mære spends a point of Mana, a point of Willpower (which does not add to the roll), and rolls her Gnosis + Mind, on a success the Chamber is incorporated into the Lair, on an exceptional success the Mære regains the spent Mana and Willpower, and on a failure the Mære cannot add the Chamber and cannot try again until another fear-related breaking point occurs. Incorporating a Chamber marks the location with the Mære’s Signature Nimbus, and the Mære can open a Primordial Pathway to her Lair from any location bearing her Signature Nimbus.

    A stable Demense can be established in the Lair, but the only Soul Stones that can be used are the Mære’s own (one soul stone is enough to cover an entire Chamber); other rare objects capable of creating Demenses may still be useable within the Lair.

    Optional: Life 3

    With her Lair comes easier access to the stuff of humanity’s terrors, and the ability to incorporate those traits into herself or her pets. After a scene of mystically manipulating either herself or one of her Bonded animals the Mære chooses a number of traits equal to her Life dots as though she had cast the spell Transform Life with normal size restrictions ignored, any Bonded animal can be affected. These changes last a day (a week at Life 4).



    Fourth: Because My Pets Need Sport

    Prerequisite:     Mind 4 and Life 4, at least 2 Chambers incorporated into the Lair

    With this Attainment the Mære has discovered how to allow Nightmares to be made flesh, drawing even more power out of the Primordial Dream. The Mære can fuse Goetia from the Primordial Dream with her bonded animals to create Manticores per the Manticore Companion merit (Deviant: the Renegades pg. 101) with the following changes: the dot rating of the Manticore is equal to the bound Goetia’s rank, and the Goetia can assign its Attribute dots to raising the host animal’s Attributes (Power dots can be assigned to Intelligence, Strength, or Presence; Finesse to Wits, Dexterity, or Manipulation; and Resistance to Resolve, Stamina, or Composure). The Goetia does not suffer Essence bleed while bound, and can spend essence on its Influences, but its Numina are replaced with Variations. Rank 2 and below Goetia can be forced into service, but Rank 3 through 5 Goetia must be convinced and can end the merge at any time (otherwise the merge is permanent). Rank 6+ Goetia are too powerful to be contained within the body of an animal. A Mære can have a number of Manticores equal to her dots in Life.


    Fifth: Before the True Monster Comes

    Prerequisite:     Mind 5, Life 5, Intimidate and Animal Ken 4, at least 4 Chambers incorporated into the Lair.

    The culmination of her research, the Mære now gives birth to a creature of true Nightmare: their own Horror. The Horror uses the highest of the Mære’s Intelligence, Strength, or Presence as its Power; the highest of Wits, Dexterity, or Manipulation as Finesse; and the highest of Resolve, Stamina, or Composure as Resistance. It then gains two additional points per Chamber in the Lair to assign to its Attributes. Its size can be up to the Mære’s Gnosis, and the Horror can be a swarm. The Horror can freely use any Praxis the Mære knows, it uses the Mære’s Gnosis + Arcanum rating to cast, cannot use yantras, and cannot contain Paradox (the Mære suffers the normal breaking point for releasing Paradox if released it’s released by her Horror). The Horror is the Mære’s soul, and the Mære is immune to any effects that would attempt to steal her soul; however, if the Horror dies the Mære immediately begins suffering the effects of the Thrall condition, the Horror reforms when the Mære gets a new soul and meets the prerequisites for this Attainment.

    Primordial Pathways, Chambers, Burrows, and Lairs

    Primordial Pathways can allow a Mære to travel anywhere in the world with human inhabitants, but the collective Fears of humanity are not safe by any means. Every time the Mære travels through a Primordial Pathway other than one to or from a Chamber incorporated into her Lair the Mære must roll Wits + Resolve with bonuses and/or penalties as outlined below.

    Dice Pool: Wits + Resolve

    Cost: 1 Mana + 1 Willpower

    Action: Instant

    Roll Results

    Dramatic Failure: the Primordial Pathway leads to the Lair of a hostile monster (represented by a Horde type Nightmare, Chronicles of Darkness pg. 143, a Rank 3+ Goetia immune to Mind magic, or a Beast from Beast: the Primordial). If the Mære attempts to escape a Chase ensues with the Lair’s owner having the edge.

    Failure: the Mære gets lost, ending up in an unknown and potentially dangerous location and cannot attempt to open another Primordial Pathway for a Scene.

    Success: the Primordial Pathway opens and lasts for the Mære’s Resolve in turns.

    Exceptional Success: the Primordial Pathway opens and lasts for the rest of the scene.
    Modifier Situation
    +3 The Mære has a Strong sympathetic connection to the destination.
    +2 The Mære has a Medium sympathetic connection to the destination.
    +1 The Mære has a Weak sympathetic connection to the destination.
    +0 The Mære has been to the destination before, but does not have a sympathetic connection.
    -1 The Mære has scryed the destination.
    -2 The Mære has a picture or video of the destination.
    -3 The Mære has only heard of the destination or seen its location on a map.
    -5 The Mære is travelling to a random destination.
    +2 A powerful local Primordial Dream denizen has guaranteed safe passage.
    -3 The local Primordial Dream denizens are hostile to the Mære.
    A Chamber’s size is based on how large it loomed in the mind of the person suffering the breaking point, not the physical size of the location the breaking point occurred. A breaking point that ended in a failed roll produces a Chamber the size of a house, whereas a dramatic failure can produce a Chamber the size of a warehouse.

    Burrows are what connect Chambers in a single Lair. The owner of a Lair and any of their guests can freely move between Chambers, anyone else must roll Intelligence + Resolve contested by the owner’s Power + Resistance (or Gnosis + Mind for a Mære’s Lair without a Horror). The owner of a Lair may also designate only certain Chambers as being for entry and/or egress (some put the entry in one chamber and the egress in another), with the only requirement being that every Chamber is connected to another by a Burrow.

    The Heart Chamber is the most important Chamber in a Lair, its destruction causes the entire Lair to collapse and need to be rebuilt, for this reason Heart Chambers are usually behind several other Chambers and well fortified.
