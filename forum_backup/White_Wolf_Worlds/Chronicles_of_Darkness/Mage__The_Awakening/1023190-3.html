                        Some other ideas I am playing around with:

                        Basing the degree of the Curse or Appearance on the number of Blessing dots they have. That gives a range of 1 to 30. Example: Atlantis, Ltd. become visibly more cybernetic as their power grows, like [Blessing dots]% of their body is noticeably "artificial" (so, barely visible at 1 dot, up to 30% with all Blessings taken).

                        Use the basic template for an "anti-Proximus" - basically, instead of casting their Blessings, they use them as a prerequisite for countering those spells that others cast.

                        Using the Curse to enforce certain casting methodologies and styles. Like making a Gunslinger-like Dynasty only use Aimed Spells, or another unable to cast at quicker than Ritual length, or another having their Forces restricted to Cold effects.

                        A Merit to let Proximi cast spells in their Blessing Arcana, but not listed as their Blessings, likely with many limitations or requirements.

                            Feedback would be appreciated.

                            Name: Walkers On Nails

                            Nickname: Danger Magnets

                            Appearance: The Walkers On Nails often have scars from glancing blows they dodged just in the nick of time. The soles of their feet often display hardened textures similar to tree bark, stone or iron. When invoking their Blessings, they seem to move with preternatural agility, making them look like a blur but also making their steps release strange sounds that attract danger to them.

                            Parent Path: Acanthus

                            Blessings: Fate – Quantum Flux (1), Reading The Outermost Eddies (1), Serendipity (1), Exceptional Luck (2), Fools Rush In (2), Shifting The Odds (2), Warding Gesture (2), Superlative Luck (3)

                            Time – Divination (1), Green Light/Red Light (1), Momentary Flux (1), Postcognition (1), Hung Spell (2), Tipping The Hourglass (2), Veil Of Moments (2)

                            Space – Correspondence (1), Outward And Inward Eye (1), Break Boundary (2), Scrying (2)

                            Curse: The Walkers are never too close or too far away from Fate, always trying to escape from trouble or get into it. This makes their lives unstable to say the least, but makes them valuable as scouts and spies, for conflict of all sorts gravitates towards them before other people.

                            Persistent: The Walkers suffer a -2 on all rolls to avoid drawing attention to themselves or avoiding harm from incidental sources.

                            Beat: If forced to get into a situation where they’ll draw attention to themselves or risk considerable harm, the Walker gets a Beat.

                            Severe: Danger comes to Walkers one way or another. If they avoid situations where they would suffer the Persistent Condition, Fate conspires to create an Environmental Tilt with them as the epicenter, or apply Personal Tilts to as many people as possible, including the Walker.

                            Oblations: Juggling sharp objects, Actually walking on nails, drinking poisons, Getting lost in a bad part of town or in the woods, Extreme Sports

                            Character Concepts: Adrenaline Junkie, Reluctant Explorer, Brave Martyr

                            Bye.
