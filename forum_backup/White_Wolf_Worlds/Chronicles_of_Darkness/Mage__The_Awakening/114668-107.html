                Originally posted by Monalfie View Post
                Are the origins of Atlantean dragons ever expanded upon? As far as where they are from, where they actually went, what their goals were, etc. The most I ever personally saw was speculation from other mages in books.
                Not in second edition, no. In fact, Second Edition is much more clear about the fact that Atlantis is a myth, and the Awakened City never existed in the reality the game takes place in. The Fall wasn't just the collapse of Atlantean civilization, it was a cosmological retcon that erased Atlantis and the entirety of the Time Before from history. So, the origins and fate of the dragons is largely academic. They never really existed, at least not in the version of history that Mages currently exist in. There weren't giant winged reptiles that walked the earth long ago and brought magic posthumously to the Atlanteans, but the story of the dragons is significant to Mages as a symbol of predecessors to the Awakened, who's being was one with magic, and existed in a time before the Time Before, to which modern Awakened owe their knowledge of magic.

                My pet answer, is that the dragons were to the Time Before as the Exarchs are to the current reality - sentient Supernal symbols which defined the laws of that reality, and may or may not have been Mages in a previous reality that was removed from history when they Ascended.
