                        02-01-2021, 04:56 PM
                        Originally posted by Emerus View Post
                        Thanks for the replies everyone! Much appreciated.

                        Got another question. If a mage uses the Prime 2 Spell "Words of Truth" on a Sleeper to convince the Sleeper that the Mage is indeed a mage and not delusional, would that work, or would the Lie overcome the effects of the spell?
                        It would work perfectly well, its not an issue. The Sleeper would have an amazingly certain gut feeling that its all true. That is not the real obstacle, plenty of Sleeper retainers for the Orders know.

                        The problem is that witnessesing any Supernal magic breaks their souls. They have to roll for a Breaking Point and then suffer Quiescence. That is why The Sleeping Curse is insidious. You can believe in magic, know its real, but the raw Truth is too much for their soul to process.

                        The way to make someone safe to be around magic is to use Stealing Fire with indefinite duration. But unless that was cast using a Legacy's Attainment, then there will always be the risk of it being dispelled (by rival mages, walking into an Ansho, having the Imago eaten by a resident of a Lower Depth, etc). When that happens, their sanity will shatter like a fine thread of glass, which can be devastating if they saw a lot of supernatural phenomena. Best case scenario, you end up with a babbling maniac. Worst case scenario, maybe they could become Slashers or even Banishers.

                                                    Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post

                                                    No. Temporal Sympathy can only be used with spells that calls for it. Shifting Sands rewrites the most recent history of the subject, with Potency or Reaches determining how much. What you're describing is Corridors of Time, the Unmaking spell.
