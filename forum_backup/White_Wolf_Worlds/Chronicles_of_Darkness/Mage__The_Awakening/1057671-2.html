        I had some time to think about Fate Attainments. Here we go:

        First Attainment

        Quantum Flux .(Fate •, +1R Instant casting)
        It's a useful Attainment, but it doesn't resonate with Thread Cutter's philosophy all that much.

        Serendipity (Fate •, +1R Instant casting)
        It would provide knowledge how to repair subject's Dharma. Useful and resonant with the Legacy.

        Automatic Successes on Active Mage Sight for a single Arcanum
        Number of successes is equal to number of dots of the Relevant Arcanum. In this case it's Fate or Death. The sample Legacy in the Corebook has this as the first optional Attainment.

        Second Attainment

        Interconnections (Fate •, +2R special effect)
        I suggest modifying it for your use. With some changes, it could emulate the first Attainment from "Legacies: the Ancient". The casting time lasts a Scene, but if the Attainment only measures karma, that makes it limited compared to the base Spell. You could consider making it Instant.

        Third Attainment

        Exceptional Luck (Fate ••, +1R Instant Casting, +1R Duration)
        It's a great and versatile Spell that can be used both for punishment and reward. Other effects, like "Grave Misfortune" are also appropriate, but are limited to curses.

        Warding Gesture (Fate ••, +1R Instant Casting, +1R Duration)
        Good for protecting the subject's destiny, but only from outside tampering. Thread Cutters with this Attainment expect getting into a fight with other powerful entities. As such, it's not for everybody.

        Fourth Attainment

        Sworn Oaths (Fate •••, +1R Instant Casting, +1R Duration)
        One way of keeping their subject on the right track. This Attainment requires the Mage to make themselves known. As such, it's not for everybody.

        Fifth Attainment

        Shared Fate (Fate •••, +1R Duration OR Instant Casting, +2R special effect)
        Very thematic as a tool of poetic justice. Sort of reminds me of the Ghost Attainment from "Legacies: the Ancient". However, It needs a lot of Reach to be useful.

        Divine Intervention OR Strings of Fate (Fate ••••, +1R Instant Casting, +1R Duration)
        Like Sworn Oaths, only more powerful. You might want to play with Reach if you want to make it a fourth Attainment. However, it makes Sworn Oaths slightly redundant.

        Pariah (Fate •••••, +1R Duration)
        Tricky to cast, but powerful in it's own right. Useful when you want to isolate the target from society, without killing them.

            And now Death Attainments:

            First Attainment

            Soul Marks .(Death •, +1R Instant casting)
            A nifty source of information that's resonant with the Legacy. Thread Cutters need to judge a Soul before they determine its fate.

            Second Attainment

            Decay (Death ••, +1R Instant Casting)
            Only slightly resonant with the Legacy, but versatile and useful.

            Soul Armor (Death ••, +1R Instant Casting OR Duration)
            Guiding a Soul to its end requires protecting it. When cast Ritually it can protect multiple people. When cast Instantly it's only good for the Thread Cutter - you might consider making it permanent.

            Speak with the Dead (Death •, +1R Instant casting, +1R Duration)
            Used as a curse it can drive killers mad with voices of their victims, or push others to make amends for their misdeeds.

            Third Attainment

            Decay (Death ••, +1R Instant Casting, +1R special effect)
            As above, only more powerful.

            Ghost Gate (Death •••, +1R Instant Casting)
            Push the target through the portal. Let them out after a few hours. They will be naked and sorry for everything. It's also good for spying.

            Soul Armor (Death ••, +1R Instant Casting, +1R Duration)
            As above, only better for protecting others.

            Touch of the Grave (Death ••, +1R Instant Casting, +1R Duration)
            Like "Speak with the Dead", only now Ghosts can punch them.

            Fourth Attainment

            Ghost Gate (Death •••, +1R Instant Casting, +1R special effect)
            As above, only you don't need to wrestle with your targets. Also, everyone keeps their clothes.

            Withering (Death ••••, +1R Instant Casting)
            Deal Lethal Damage to your victims without suffering Wisdom loss.

            Fifth Attainment

            Withering (Death ••••, +1R Instant Casting, +1R Sensory OR Aggravated)
            Deal Lethal (possibly Aggravated) Damage to your victims without suffering Wisdom loss.

            ??? (Death •••••, +1R Instant Casting)
            At this point you can kill with a touch. If you beat their Withstand rating and they're not otherwise protected, they die.

                    Ok this is what I have so far


                    Attainments
                    The attainment of the Euthanists always revolve around Fate, be it the Beginning, the being, or the Ending. For this reason their main Arcanum is Fate. Since they are infamous with the methods of their endings they are more closely related to Death as an Optional Arcanum.

                    First Attainment:
                    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Fate 2, Empathy
                    Serendipity (Fate •, Instant Cast, 2 Turn Duration, Potency = Fate)

                    Second Attainment:
                    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Fate 2
                    Interconnections (Fate •, Instant Cast, +1 Special, 2 Turn Duration, Potency = Fate)
                    Optional Arcanum: Death 2
                    Soul Marks (Death •, Instant Cast, +1 Free Soul Special, 2 Turn Duration, Potency = Death)

                    Third Attainment:
                    Prerequisites: Gnosis 4, Fate 3
                    Exceptional Luck (Fate ••, Instant Cast, Advanced Duration, 1 Day duration, Potency = Fate)
                    Optional Arcanum: Death 3
                    Rotting Flesh (Death •••, Instant Cast, 2 Turn Duration, Potency = Death)

                    Fourth Attainment:
                    Prerequisites: Gnosis 6, Fate 4
                    Sworn Oaths (Fate •••, Instant Cast, Advanced Duration, 1 month duration, Potency = 1)
                    Optional Arcanum: Death 4
                    Devouring the Slain (Death •••, Instant Cast, Not Injured Special, 3 turn Duration, Potency = Death)

                    Fifth Attainment:
                    Prerequisites: Gnosis 8, Fate 5
                    Shared Fate (Fate •••, Instant Cast, Advanced Duration, +1 Special, 1 week Duration, Potency = Fate)
                    Optional Arcanum: Death 5
                    Withering (Death ••••, Instant Cast, Aggravated Damage, 3 Turn Duration, Potency = Death)

                                    I've noticed several things:

                                    1. Most of your Attainments shouldn't really have a Duration, not even one that lasts for several Turns. No matter what the tables say, "Serendipity" or "Withering" simply apply their effect all at once. They don't repeat themselves and there is no cool-down period. Only "Exceptional Luck", "Sworn Oaths" and "Shared Fate" should have a Duration.

                                    2. Speaking of those three Spells. How did you calculate their Duration? "Exceptional Luck" and "Shared Fate" are Potency Primary, so they should last a Scene, but could be extended with Spell Factors up to a week. "Sworn Oaths" is Duration Primary, so it's length should depend on your Arcanum rating.

                                    3. Also, I didn't realize this earlier, but with "Potency = 1" "Sworn Oaths" is only good for blessings. If you apply Spell Factors you can raise Potency up to 3, but its use is still limited to the weak-willed.

                                    4. You've left an empty slot for the first Death Attainment.

                                    5. "Withering" does Aggravated damage, so it requires Mana with each casting.

                                    6. "Devouring the Slain" makes other attack spells more redundant. Also, it's unusually predatory for the Legacy's beliefs.

                                                        My reading is based on this example:
                                                        Originally posted by M:tAw pg. 198
                                                        For example, if a member of a fire-summoning Legacy who possesses Forces 5 activates her Forces-based Legacy Attainment. Its primary factor is Potency. The Attainment scores 5 Potency automatically. If Duration is required, it lasts for three turns (as per Duration rating with –4 dice penalty). It scores 5 successes. If the Attainment also harnessed Spirit and the mage possessed three dots in that Arcanum, the Duration would decrease to two turns, since the secondary factor uses the lower of the two ratings.
                                                        The text doesn't specify the level of the Attainment, merely caster's Arcanum rating. That's why I assumed it applied to all of them. In this context "Attainment's lowest prerequisite Arcanum" refers to Spirit, because it's lower than Forces. Spirit ••• allows for 1 step to increase Duration. If the Mage in question had Spirit ••••, they could have made 2 steps. The text you quoted can be read two ways, but I think if your interpretation were correct, the text would have said "Attainment's lowest prerequisite Arcanum rating".
