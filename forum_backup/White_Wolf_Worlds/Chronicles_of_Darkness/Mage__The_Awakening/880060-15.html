        I know this is a personal opinion, but I really didnt like the Egregore merit.

        I mean, isnt the real applicability of Egregore too restrictive? All other "Order Special merits" (Adamant Hand, Lex Magica, Masque and Techne) are not applicable only to one's order and offer benefits much more immediate or simply way better.

        Lets see the first level, Arche. It modifies the mage's contribution to teamwork spellcasting waiving the penalty for not having all arcana and providing a success if you do have all of them, but only if everyone is from The Mysterium (as Mysterium status is required, like other order merits).
        Comparing that to Techne, in which you can use a mundane focus as a tool AND, IF everyone is from Free Council, you get 8 again in teamwork spellcasting (for only one merit dot more), not only Egregore is weaker but really, really dificult to come into play.

        The other levels reflect the same experience as hardly ever coming into play. How many times did my mages go to another caucus or put to good use a deep connection to everyone on her order (while she already is in a sanctum anyway, so she already could affect them pretty well) without having to invest deeply in other merits such as status, contacts or mentor or a specific Space-based build? Seldom.

        How many times did I simply lose my magick tool in such desperation that would justify a 4 dot merit just to change it immediately? Never, and I wouldnt have this merit even if it happened often, for the -2 paradox and +1 yarna only.

        How many mage cabals with only Mysteriums I ever played? None. And when I play I am almost always the Mysteirum one, usually the only one.

        I get it Mysterium are more close-knitted than the other orders, but as a player I (and my RPG friends, from what I discussed with them) would never, ever use this merit, simply because it would almost never come into play and the benefits arent remotely good enough justify that, even in the rare cases that it would apply. And thats why I ask if it would be possible to change its applicability somehow, so that would be able to be applied in more stories.
