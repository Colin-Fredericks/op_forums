    I don't think that you want to put your most powerful spells in attainments, btw. While you get surefire activation and counterspell-no-no, you lose access to Reach and can only assign +2 spell factors at best. They are wonderful for utility spells that you want to cast often, discreetly, and without giving people opening to counter spell them away, but you are stuck with their default state, which doesn't seem very impressivie (at least not for something you would dub as "most powerful spell"). While you might be tempted by Direct-Damage-Fireball Attainment, it's probably better to invest into something like Shifting Sands Attainment, which is a major game changer that reshapes your approach to everyday life immensely.

                I guess. A lot depends on answer to the very basic question: is "primary factor" of Attainment benefiting from the standard rule of "free factors equal to your Arcana"? Because it's a big difference; Auto-Ball with 1 Potency that can be cranked up to 3 isn't very impressive (in fact, lots of people would just shrug it off with their resistance stat!), while Auto-Ball with guaranteed 6 Potency is a totally different beast. Then again, if Combined Casting still exists and is in similar form to the Combined Casting (that thing that allowed you to cast multiple spells at once for -2 penalty, not "spells combining different arcanas" thing), it might the "best" way to spam direct attack spells.
                Also, now I want to see how good would be a MTG Blue legacy based on Counterspelling and saying "nope" to opponent's magical actions . Again, dunno, because it depends on how Counterspell works (my gut says "Clash of Wills", but how CoW interacts with stuff like Potency and Factors?); AutoCounter with Potency 3 is probably garbage, Potency 6 could be good.

                EDIT
                Relevant blog fragment:
                Automatic Activation: In most cases, a Legacy Attainment can be activated with an instant action and do not require die rolls. If the Attainment requires spell factors, it automatically confers factors that would impose a penalty up to the mage’s dots in the Attainment’s highest prerequisite Arcanum. If the Attainment would require a measurement of its successes, it automatically scores a number of successes equal to the mage’s dots in the highest prerequisite Arcanum.
                For example, if a fire-summoning Legacy Attainment requires spell factors and requires Forces 3, a Legacy member with Forces 5 harnesses spell factors that would normally impose a -5 penalty to die rolls—though remember that you don’t actually have to roll dice.
