    Minervan Praeceptor (Homebrew Mind/Fate legacy, 2E)

    05-02-2017, 09:01 AM
    This is a legacy built for an Acanthus character, who likes to run support for the rest of his cabal. It's mostly done, but I'd like to ask for input regarding the prerequisites. I'm debating whether or not to change it from the Merit requirements, and over to reading ”Empathy 2, 2 dots of Persuasion, Intimidate or Subterfuge” instead, modifying the following requirements as fitting.

    I'd like to hear your thoughts and criticism.

    Minervan Praeceptors


    Mentor was the foster brother of mythical Odysseus, a man renowned for his wisdom and especially for the way he imparted it unto others, and guided them to greatness. It is told, that he was Odysseus’ most trusted advisor, and the one in which Odysseus left his only son, Telemachus.
    So great was his wisdom, that Athena took on his guise when Telemachus grew of age, and counseled him into searching for his long lost father and protecting his mother and his kingdom from the unwanted advances of the many fortune seeking suitors.
    In the days of Imperial Rome, Pallas Athena became Minerva and Mentor grew from a person to a concept. Minerva represented the divine virtues of wisdom and strategy, and her role as both teacher and general as the perfect inspiration for a splinter group of théarchs in Philadelphia in the 1850s. Fearing what was to come and sensing the war on the horizon, the Silver Ladder Mages sought to refine their souls into the vessels to guide others to greatness.


    Origins
    Parentage: Acanthus or the Silver Ladder.
    Nickname: Mentors, Counselors
    Background: To many Mentors, the act of guiding and instructing comes naturally to them. Therefore, it would make sense that most of them would come from the ranks of teachers, guidance counselors or professors. While this is true to a certain extent, what most mentors have in common is rather a powerful ambition and a heightened sense of caution. If you win, you might only be number two. If you lose, however, you’re still in the race.
    Appearance: The Mentors dress in clothes appropriate to the arena they’re trying to influence. A high-class Mentor might dress in designer dresses and elegant shades, while another, trying to steer his gang towards dominance would fit right in wearing the colors. They don’t usually attempt to be the most well-dressed person in a crowd, though. They save that for the one in the line of fire.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Mind 2, 2 dots of either Allies, Contacts, Status or Retainer, Empathy, Persuasion or Intimidate 2

    Initiation: A prospective Mentor is usually chosen as the subject of their teacher, and the real test of this matter, is discovering that the teacher is actually pushing you in a certain direction, and knowing that you are being used.
    Organization: The Minervan Praeceptors usually only function in pairs of two. The teacher and the student. The asymmetric relationship doesn’t usually continue after the prospect gains the first attainment, the traditions call it poor taste, as the two Praeceptors are considered peers. They often coordinate their efforts if they are pushing towards similar goals.
    Theory: Greatness does not come alone. Heroes are made, not born. The tenets of Minerva speak of Wisdom. Our obligation is to guide the Awakened closer to the Supernal and our reward is a place at the right hand of the greatest among us. This is done to strive for a better world, but altruism is not Wisdom.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Mind
    Yantras: Dispensing useful advice to an associate (+1) or the target of the spell (+2), laying out a plan for the long-term success of the spell’s target (+1 to +3 depending on the detail of the plan), putting in a good word, and succeeding on a Social roll, for a friend (+2).
    Oblations: Mapping the hierarchy of an organization, discussing future plans with a trusted associate, working a room as an associate’s “wingman”.

    Attainments
    First: Destined for greatness
    Prerequisites: Inititation
    A Mentor who wants to be successful can’t just pick the first person he meets when looking for a subject. She needs to be careful about who she chooses and may go through hundreds before she finds the right person for the task she has in mind.
    When using this attainment, the Mentor divines the nature of her subject, in a fashion much like the Know Nature rote (p. 159), except she learns only the subject’s Vice, Virtue, Aspirations and Obsessions and not the subject’s Mental or Social attributes. (Reach: Instant casting)
    Optional: Fate 1
    When choosing a subject, the Mentor acquainted with Fate, knows better than to go for those who have a reputation of making themselves vulnerable to untrustworthy people, being the targets of hidden curses or, worst of all, breaking sacred Oaths.
    This attainment functions like rote Interconnections (p134). (Reach: Instant casting)

    Second: Grease the wheels
    Prerequisites: Mind 2, the Mentor must attain two dots in a second skill from the list of possible requirements.
    Most Willworkers know, that being paranoid is a lot better than being dead, so when a stranger offers to help you gain all the power you’ve dreamed of, it helps to bring a little magical persuasion into the mix.
    The Mentors often use this Attainment on themselves when making contact, but at least as often to help their subject gain headway towards his goals. A skilled Mentor is welcomed almost everywhere she goes.
    This Attainment functions like the First Impressions rote (p 160). (Reach: Primary Factor: Potency)
    Optional: Fate 2
    The Praeceptor can induce an air of competence in even the most tongue tied subject. If he pushes Fate a bit to allow his subject a few lucky breaks, they will usually make it through even the most unwelcoming crowd. This Attainment functions as the first Reach effect of the Fools Rush In rote (p 136) in that it gives the subject a dice bonus on Mental or Social rolls equal to the Potency of the Attainment a number of times equal to Potency, with the same stipulations about preparation as the original rote. In addition, the bonus to impression levels still remain. (Reach; Primary spell factor: Potency)

    Third: The sharpened tool
    Prerequisites: Mind 3, Empathy 3
    There’s no use in putting your energy into a dullard, but sometimes, even the most competent needs a little push. This attainment allows the Mentor to push her subject (or herself) towards greatness. The Attainment mimics the Enhance Skill rote (p 162) and lets the Mentor increase the subject’s skill by the Attainment’s Potency. (Reach: Advanced Duration)
    Optional: Fate 3
    The Mentor needs her subject to act in accordance to her plans, and nothing disturbs even the best-laid plans worse than the meddling of other mages. This attainment allows the Mentor to put up a protective ward that disrupts others’ attempts to control or influence her subject. This attainment functions like the Warding Gesture rote (p. 136). (Reach: Advanced Duration, Instant Casting)

    Fourth: Bestow Greatness
    Prerequisites: Mind 4, Empathy 4
    The number of idiots who have made their way into power is, contrary to popular discussion, incredibly small. To take the reins, you need to know how to make alliances, when to break them and you need to be smart or convincing enough that people will put their trust in you.
    Most subjects chosen by the Praeceptors live up these demands in some way, but the Mentors have found it necessary to give their subjects a little push none the less. This attainment functions like the rote Augment Mind (p 161). (Reach: Primary factor: Duration, Advanced Duration)
    Optional: Fate 4
    When her subject needs to navigate the shark-filled waters of social interaction, a little nudging of his destiny goes a long way. The attainment functions like the Superlative Luck rote (p 137). (Reach; Primary factor: Potency, Advanced Duration)

    Fifth: The right tool for the right task
    Prerequisites: Mind 5, Empathy 5, the Mentor must attain three dots in the skill chosen for the second attainments prerequisite.
    Sometimes, the subject gets a little too independent for the Praeceptor’s liking. He might have become too “big for his britches” or his goals may deviate too far from the Praeceptor’s own.
    The Praeceptors have an answer for this. Scrupulous Preaceptors have been known to go so far, as completely rewriting the subject’s mind, others have just gently planted suggestions in him.
    This attainment functions like the rote Psychic Reprogramming (p 164) and allows the Sorcerer to change the subject’s Vice, Virtue, Obsession, Aspirations, non-physical Persistent Conditions or move a dot between two Social or two Mental skills. (Reach: Primary factor: Duration, Advanced Duration)
    Optional: Fate 5
    The Master Praeceptor’s plans for her subject are often grand and far-reaching. The Praeceptor is willing to push Destiny as far as it will go to make it come true. This attainment allows the Mage to mimic the Strings of Fate rote (p 138), bending Fate to present opportunities for her plans to come to fruition. The rote also allows for the punishment of a rebellious subject or the termination of one who has outlived his usefulness. (Reach: Advance Duration, Grant opportunities daily)
