    Ascension/Awakening Translation Question

    10-08-2016, 04:24 PM
    Lately, I was making a Mage game based around importing certain features of Ascension to the CofD. One of the things I like about Ascension is the concept of "Factions", and I've tried to think how to bring them to Awakening. The way I see it, each of the gamelines could be said to have such Factions, in the following way-

    Awakening- Diamond Orders, Free Council, Seers of the Throne, Scelesti, Tremere, Banishers
    Ascension- Traditions, Technocracy, Nephandi, Marauders, Disparate Alliance

    Considering that the "core setting" is Awakening, this games Factions are going to be used with certain changes- first, I'm going to split a bit the connection between the Orders and the Free Council, making them into two independent Factions which don't work together as core assumption. Second, I'm going to make the Tremere into a true "Nameless Order", accepting all Paths with the House system being some kind of "Order Bonus" of some kind. The Banishers would likely be treated as a real "Order" like in 1E, perhaps with few changes to how they operate and function. Both the Tremere and the Scelesti are going to be heavily based around Left Handed Path (one of my favorite Mage books).

    As for Ascension, some things are easy, while others not so much- the Disparate Alliance, for example, shares a lot of territory with the Free Council IMO, so I have little qualms about letting the later absorb the former. The Nephandi, likewise, could be treated as some sort of unification of dark coalition of Left Handed mages which deal with the Lower Depths (that is, they are to Lower Depths Legacies what the Scelesti are for Abyssal mages and the Tremere would be for Reapers). The Technocracy I want to transport, more or less, as whole with few adjustments, but I kinda stuck with how exactly would they work in the setting. I'm also not that sure about the Marauders, and if they should be treated as a Faction or dropped into a phenomena, mixing some parts with Mads or the Banishers or something. The Traditions present, to me, the greatest dilemma- as they are the main protagonists at Ascension, it doesn't feel "fair" to me to simply dispose of them. On the other hand, I can't think about a place for them in the setting- the best I can think about is mixing most of them in the Free Council, with perhaps only the Order of Hermes acting as its own Factions, and perhaps building the rest of the Traditions around it (not to mention that the Translation Guide seem to offer a possibility of the Celestial Chorus acting as a separate Faction, a path I don't think I'm going to use but still need to be considered).

    One of the ways I've thought to use in order to focus the different Factions was using the Ententes from Imperial Mysteries (I may even use that name instead of "Factions") as rough guidelines for each Faction. The Alienated may be tied to the Disparate Alliance (which would mean both them and the Free Council would be different Factions), while the Siddha could be used for the Traditions (the Aswadim, Exemplars and Tetrachs are obvious, while the Bodhisattvas may be used as inspiration for the Chorus as separate Faction).

    The greatest obstacle for me in the setting is, in a way, the most important one- what each of those Factions/Ententes actually wants? For the more antagonistic groups, it is kinda obvious- the Scelesti serve the Abyss and in search for its "freedom" and "enlightenment" in the madness it offers, the Nephandi seek to feed their angry masters of the Lower Depths, the Tremere want to unlock the true nature of the soul, the Seers serve the Kings of the Lie for selfish ends and preserving the status que and the Banishers simply kill people. The problem rise more at the potential protagonists- the Diamond Orders, for example. Each of the Orders has its own motivations, but as whole I assume they search to create a perfect, well functioning magical society, but is it a strong enough motivation when there are so many other competitors in the field? The Free Council say that humanity is magical and find power in the creation of human cultures- but how exactly should I phrase that into a single, simple motivation? The Technocracy want to cover up magic, maintain control over Sleepers and integrate magic into daily life, but considering that much of their existence is based around Ascension's cosmology, how can I adapt them to the new system? And that is without mentioning the Traditions/Order of Hermes, Marauders (if they are not a phenomena) and the Celestial Chorus (if they are an independent Faction). Ironically, if the Disparate Alliance is a separate Faction than the Free Council, it has more "meat" than the rest, being worshipers of the Old Gods and such.

    So- does anyone can offer help about how to make this setting, and how should those Ententes (I think I like this term better) operate in Awakening's setting?

      I want to see how these dozen globe-spanning factions interact in the occult underground of Omaha, Nebraska.

      The Nameless War ended differently in this timeline. A significant number of Nameless accepted the Seers' offer to create a world where technology and magic intertwine in the name of power over Sleepers, but the new Technocratic Union composed of both former Seers and Nameless soon turned against the Ministries in a second war, choosing to pursue a greater dream where Enlightened Science dethrones the Exarchs and rules over humanity by will alone. The remaining Nameless organized themselves into the Free Council, and made a peace treaty with the Diamond (and others?) without actually joining them as the Pentacle.

      I think the Marauders are pretty much just the Mad, trying to differentiate them would just be splitting hairs. I can't imagine how they would organize with their single-minded Obsessions, they'd be more like dirty secrets within the Ententes.

      The Diamond have shared views about magic itself as a force and phenomenon which proceeds from the Supernal World, and the Fallen World as a reflection of the Supernal rather than a thing with unique creative power and value. They want to restore the Time Before, a semi-mythical enlightened society (fundamentally different from the societies of the Fallen World) in which humanity claims its magical birthright without fear of Paradox. They generally do not pursue this as a literal global project with a specific timeline in the way that, say, the Technocracy does. The key idea is that they are basically looking to restore and rectify the cosmos to its proper former state, rather than creating something new.

      Honestly, I would incorporate many of the Traditions into the Free Council or the Diamond, given that the Council's thing is finding the magic inherent in various Sleeper cultural practices, and that's kind of what the Traditions are about. Perhaps the Traditions were some of the groups fighting the Nameless War.

Again, I tend to over do things. Anyway, that's what I have for now, including the agendas each Entente has-

          The Atlantean Diamond- "Magic is a Virtue". Magic is, by its essence, an ideal thing. A perfected world is a magical world, as magic is birthed by the perfected symbols of the Supernal. As such, investigating and spreading magic is, by essence, a good thing, which brings Truth to the Fallen World and leads others to the light.

          The Free Council- "Humanity is Magical". Magic is everywhere. People express it through their art, craft and civilization. It is not some isolated power from a long lost Heaven- it is right hear, you only need to learn how to see the Truth behind the facade of the Lie and its servants. The Supernal is inside of us, waiting to be revealed.

          Seers of the Throne- "Magic is Payment". The world is ruled by the Exrachs, the Kings of the Lie. As the rulers of the world, their will express itself everywhere. Serving the Kings and Queens of the world is only the natural way of life, and if their wills are tyrannical and selfish then being tyrannical and selfish is just. Show your loyalty to them, and you will be gifted with their power.

          Scelesti- "Magic is Limited". Magic is meant to be the truth of the world- but "truth" is such a subjective thing. Each see a different version of it, one side which limits our way of thinking. By embracing the unknown, what is not, what that have never was and may have been, you become more than magic- and by inviting madness, the Abyss would grant you freedom.

          Tremere- "Souls are Magic". There is nothing more magical than your soul. It is what gives us our sense of self, the ability to change, create and define the world. With a soul, magic fails. Souls are not simply a channel to the Supernal, they are the Supernal- pure magic, made from the essence of Heaven and Hell. By understanding the soul, you understand magic- and by taking another's soul, you take her magic to yourself.

          Banishers- "Magic is Hubris". The very act of magic is immoral. It invites the Abyss and allow the mage the ability to remade to world according to a selfish will. It is not a natural thing, but a sin against the very structure of the universe. By using magic, the mage breaks the world in the name of egoistic desires, and by destroying magic the world could heal itself.

          Traditions- "Magic makes the World". The 10 Arcana are the very building blocks of reality. Magic is the power which binds those elements together, creating different patterns and complicated structures. It doesn't matter who rules the world, or if the Lie blinds Sleepers to it- the most important thing is to make sure the world actually works, and by understanding the Arcana you understand magic itself.

          Technocracy- "Magic is a Lie". The Lie is what makes the world, weaving magic through Patterns and maintain its stability. Calling magic directly to the world burns those strings and threat the balance of the universe. By working through the Lie, humanity could achieve much greater heights than by working against it- and by using the weapons of the Exrachs against them, humanity could take their place in Heaven.

          Nephandi- "Magic is Corruptible". The world is doomed, gnawed upon by many little things. Entropy rise constantly, destruction always await. Eventually, everything would fall down to the Lower Depths, and even magic itself would be destroyed and devoured. By making deals with the Depths and feeding them magic willingly, mages could gain power while it is still matter- and if it damns their souls, it is only the natural way of life.

          Marauders- "Magic is Primal". The Supernal existed long before mankind learned how to speak, and magic is just as ancient. When man overthrew the Old Gods to take their place, he disrupted that primal state of grace, dooming the world with it. The only just thing is serving those ancient deities and restoring the old order, even if it means destroying mankind in the process for their sins.
