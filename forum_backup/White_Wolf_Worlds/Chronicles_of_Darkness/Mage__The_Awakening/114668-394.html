                Originally posted by Therian View Post
                Just to make sure I’ve got this right, a Mage can reveal and scrutinize a mystery if any of the Arcanum involved are in their Mage sight?
                You need to be using Active Mage Sight to be able to enter Focused Mage Sight, and you can only use Focused Mage Sight with Arcana that you're using Active Mage Sight with; since adding Arcana beyond your Ruling set to your Active Mage Sight costs Mana, Scrutinizing a Mystery relating to something outside the field of your Path or Legacy is more expensive.

                Any Arcanum can provide you with the basic surface information of what Arcana the Mystery falls under, but if it's not the one you're using then that's all you're getting apart from a little extra on an exceptional success.

                The advantage of using multiple ones is just increasing their dice pool? So if a Mystery is primarily Time magic but has a Space effect, a Mage could do everything they needed to do with Space?
                The advantages are that you count more than one Arcanum in the pool and get the information about that Mystery that pertains to the additional Arcana at the cost of failed rolls raising the Opacity sooner.

                            Originally posted by Therian View Post
                            The advantages are that you count more than one Arcanum in the pool and get the information about that Mystery that pertains to the additional Arcana at the cost of failed rolls raising the Opacity sooner.

                            So are you saying you would not be able to get all the deep information from a Mystery unless you're using all the Arcanum in your Mage Sight that are involved in the Mystery?
                            After (Gnosis+ Arcanum) rolls, every failed Scrutiny roll increases Opacity by (Gnosis/2), rounded up. This increase affects everyone. For every arcanum added after the first, this happens one turn sooner. Opacity becomes exponentially more difficult to reduce at each level.

                            So if you had 1 dot in every arcana, it would cost you 1 WP point to activate Revelation, plus consume up to 7 mana (3 free Ruling Arcana plus the cost of the other 7 for normal Active Sight, but I doubt a Mystery would involve all 10) and would make you have to worry about Opacity increases up to 9 turns sooner than normal when Scrutinizing. So you would have to use Miracle, or thow a ton of mana at it (1 mana used equals one more success counted, but only counts if you got 1 natural success) or use some other ace up your sleeve to avoid it backfiring.

                            So what is optimal ? First figure out the key Arcana via any of the sights. Then you can analyze it under each individual lens, which is easier the better you are at it. If you have multiple mages, the WP cost is spread between them.

                            So can you use raw power to punch through it in one go ? Yes, but it requires quite a bit of it. As Opacity rises, this becomes increasingly impractical.

But if the scale is above default, EVERYTHING inside it gets hit, including allies and the structure nearby.
