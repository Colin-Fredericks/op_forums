    Hung Spell is applying a piece of Time onto a spell. It makes sense that Hung Spell can postpone the effects of the spell. It doesn't make sense that Matter can store a spell in a potion because Matter isn't about storing stuff. Rather, this seems to be something akin to Imbue Item, a Prime Attainment. I can see a Prime/Matter Legacy focused around brewing potions, though.

    On a related note I think it's a boring concept in Mage. Not because the concept itself is boring but because there's no reason it has to be potions rather than snackbars or pills. There's nothing inherent in the ingredients chosen to make the potion that makes it work. It's a spell cast on the potion that makes it magical, making the potion itself incidental.

                Honestly, it's a hold-over attitude from 1e which was more explicit about the fact that Forces could control the language by which computers function. It still basically holds true though since a computer relies on electrical impulses in order to faithfully follow it's programming. There's definitely some overlap with Matter though, much like how corpses might have some overlap between Matter and Death depending on the spell you may want to cast.

                A spell specifically to craft or modify programming via a burst of electrical impulses wouldn't require a Reach, but giving that program specialized or empowered ability to execute its function would. In other words, a spell to modify a program is not just a Reach tacked onto Influence Electricity, it's a completely different spell. Theoretically, you could create a microprocessor using just Alter Conductivity and that it would be reasonable to append a Reach cost to, but it would be simpler to create a spell whose purpose is to turn an object into a microprocessor.

                            Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                            Hmmm... Could a mage create a computer entirely out of electricity using Forces? What rating would that have? I'd think Forces 3 minimum, but Control Electricity is Forces 2, so I could see an argument for that.
                            I definitely think it's possible, since a computer is essentially just electricity moving in complicated ways, but what Practice it falls under depends on what you mean by "entirely out of electricity". If you have blank circuit boards to direct the electricity through it's arguably a Ruling spell, but probably with some Reach for the complexity. In the case of just pulling a computer out of a wall socket though it is definitely at least Weaving.

                            Note that unless you have a keyboard and a screen at hand to "plug into" the electricity-computer, you're going to need additional spells to give it commands and receive its output. It would take a Forces 4 Patterning spell to make it display as an actual screen, but reading information straight from a computer is arguably just Forces 1 Knowing.

                                    A potentially useful spell I just thought of:

                                    House Whisperer (Space •)
                                    Practice: Knowing
                                    Primary Factor: Potency
                                    Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Streetwise, Survival

                                    The mage tunes into a location to learn its secrets. With an instant action, the mage can ask a number of the following questions equal to the spell's Potency:
                                    • What is the purpose of this location?
                                    • How is the location demarcated?
                                    • Where are the entrances and exits?
                                    • What is the location's dot rating in Safe Place/Sanctum/Haven/etc?
                                    • What is the nature of the location's inhabitants?
                                    • Are there any Irises within the location?
                                    • What is one of the location's flaws?
                                    +1 Reach: When asking about Irises, the mage also learns the location of one Iris (if there are any) and a general idea of where it leads.

                                    Add Death •: The mage can ask if there are any anchors in the location, or if the location itself is an anchor.

                                    Add Forces •: The mage can ask for the location for a source of energy within the location, or if the location is under the effects of an Extreme Environment.

                                    Add Spirit •: The mage can ask for one of the location's Resonant Conditions, or if the space is a Locus.

                                    Add Time •: The mage can ask how the location has changed since a given time or event. Temporal Sympathy applies.
