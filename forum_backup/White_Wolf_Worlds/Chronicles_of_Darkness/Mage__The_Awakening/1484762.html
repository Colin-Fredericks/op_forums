      I like this idea a lot, it gives mages more options for creating magical items. Its far less convenient that an imbued item, but with an improved accessibility level for non-mages. It has more complicated steps for creating them, yet is accessible to all Paths and tempting enough to ensure many would consider the investment. Its also a very nice system for creating Mysteries that crafting focused mages, especially those in a crafting centric Legacies, could pursue. Looking at the document itself, a few thoughts come to mind:

      * In the Designing a Wonder chapter, there is a lot information being delivered at once, for which readers do not have full context. I think it might be a good idea to start the chapter with something similar to page 67 of Signs of Sorcery, a cheat sheet that allows for a top-down look at the systems and acts as a quick reference.

      * Since the system for creating Wonders could be seen as balancing a zero sum equation, then perhaps you could utilize the Blood Bathing ritual from World of Darkness: Immortals as a reference for the formatting. While it is what was implied by the writeup, I think utilizing such a format would make visualization easier.

      * The Operator Drawbacks are probably not always negatives. If a Wonder can only be used by Mysterium Egregores, then it means there is no point in stealing it, since it would take extra work to get a traitor to use them.

      * Environmental Hazards could probably be broken down into more details. I think each Drawback point could become 1 per Environmental Hazard level. Similarly, perhaps the area of effect and duration could also be tied to points. This might lead to a situation where clever mages incorporate the drawback into the system, like a Wonder with Control Electricity that causes thunderstorms it can harness, but I would say that is working as intended.

      Those are my initial thought, hope they help

                      Thanks! As for your questions:

                      * The cloning machine counts as an Active Wonder, and it needs Advanced Duration [+Indefinite Status] so the clones last indefinitely. It also needs Advanced Scale [Subjects] which determines how many clones it can produce per activation. And this would be just Life 5 (Making), plus Conjunction with Mind 5, as per the Create Life spell. You could use more basic Life Practices if the cloning machine has certain limitations (e.g. it has to actually grow the clones in a synthetic womb over time, rather than manifesting an adult human ex nihilo). Do remember that souls cannot be created, not even with Death 5 [Making].

                      * Trust me, [Mystery Keys X 10] Successes is nothing. Consider that a Mage with Mind Arcanum 3 can cast Enhance Skill to get Crafts 10. Not only does that increase dice pool AND the maximum number of rolls, there is an official rule in ChroD Core saying any Exceptional Success on an Extended Action can reduce Target Successes by an additional amount equal to [Skill]. And that is before the various dice tricks you can get via thematically fitting spells, usually Ruling or Perfecting. Let's not forget they can benefit from the Equipment, Merits and Teamwork with Cabalmates.

                      * Well, that begs a question. If your cloning machine doesn't include Fuel, Parts or Maintenance as Drawbacks... how does it even work? The Drawbacks have to make sense, even if based on esoteric laws of reality. I'll try to add examples of Wonder Invention to the document later.

                              5- A "magnet" that looks like a chip with High Speech shaped circuitry on it. It that can be attached to any device, transforming it into a Perpetual Motion machine.

                              6- An extremely comfy pillow that gives a bonus to Willpower recovered by a good night's sleep. Kotatsu and sleeping bag versions are very popular as well.

                                          Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post
                                          1- A universal remote that uses a sensory version of Coaxing the Spirits.
                                          Name: Digital Bypass Key

                                          Description: A device which resembles an USB drive, and whose plastic exterior is in the shape of a spider-like key. When inserted into a computer and wound 7 times much like a key, the digital-spirit connected to the computer is compelled to deactivate any mundane security measures that prevent the Wonder's user from accessing the features of the computer. Supernatural security measures trigger a Clash Of Wills.

                                          Inventor's Arcana: Spirit 3

                                          Effects: Coaxing The Spirits [Digital Technology], Channel Essence [Digital Resonance]

                                          Rating: Highest Practice Level [2] + Extra Practices Taken [1] + Extra Reaching [0] + Every 2 Dots Of Extra Factors [7] - Praxes [1] - Fifth Maneuver Of The Hyper-Tech Prodigy Style [3] - Relevant Digital Or Shamanistic Legacy [1] = 5

                                          Free Reaching: Instant Casting, More Finely-Tuned Coaxing, Piercing (Coaxing The Spirits), Advanced Duration [+Indefinite] (Channel Essence)

                                          Extra Reaching: None

                                          Free Factors: Standard Potency 3, Standard Duration 1, Standard Scale 1 (Coaxing The Spirits), Standard Potency 3, Advanced Duration 1, Standard Scale 1 (Channel Essence)

                                          Extra Factors: +6 Potency (+3 For Coaxing The Spirits, +3 For Channel Essence), +4 Dice For Clashes Of Wills Via Piercing (Coaxing The Spirits), +4 Duration (Channel Essence)

                                          Drawbacks: Attention 1 [The Computer's Screen Shows Brief Glimpses Of What Is Beyond The Gauntlet Around It], Fuel 2 [Essence], Maintenance 1 [Computers], Parts 1 [Sophisticated Microchips Blessed By The Manipulation-Influence Of A Rank 2+ Digital Spirit, Once Per Day For A Full Lunar Cycle]

                                          Development: 5 Keys

                                          Example Keys: Find Raw Materials [Assistance From Suitable Rank 2+ Digital Spirit, And Sophisticated Microchips], Designing Schematics & Diagrams [Step By Step Electronics & Programming Guide, Written In Spirit Tongue-Based Script], Prototyping & Testing [Hacking Into The Computer Of A Werewolf Of The Iron Masters Tribe]

                                          Notes: Piercing is a house rule. It is a generic +1 Reach Option which, once selected, allows the Mage to apply a -2 to spellcasting in exchange for a +1 to Clashes Of Wills, up to a -8 penalty for a +4 bonus. This does not stack with bonuses for Clashes Of Wills granted by Advanced Duration. Instead, Piercing effectively allows you to improve Clashes Of Wills for spells whose effects don't make sense for Advanced Duration. This is especially useful for combat and healing spells. For this Wonder, 2 extra dots of Spell Factors have been applied to Piercing. Since +1 dot of Spell Factors requires a -2 penalty to the spell version, then the Wonder has 2 X 2 = 4 bonus dice for Clashes Of Wills involving Coaxing The Spirits.

                                          The Channel Essence spell has been included in order to give onboard storage of up to 6 Essence for the activation of the Wonder. If the user wants, he can coax the digital-spirit into reestablishing the security measures once the user is done hacking, creating decoy files, changing dates of access, and other auxiliary tactics to make it seem as if the computer was never hacked. However, this still does not allow the digital-spirit to do anything that would require an Extended Action, and a dedicated and perceptive IT professional may still notice something is amiss since the Wonder does not include any Veiling effects. Supernatural means of detecting that the computer has been tampered with will work normally. This Wonder has a small spider-like tell in its exterior design to make the Rank 2+ Digital Spirit feel happy, because he looks like a spider made of 1s and 0s.
