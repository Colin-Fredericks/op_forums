                        Originally posted by Phillip Morin View Post
                        Are spells casts in Verges not subject to Dissonance? And those cast in a Desmene? Does Paradox stills applies as well?
                        The Dissonance protection only applies to natural Verges. And the paradox protection only applies if A) the spell doesn't leave the Verge/Demense and B) a sleeper isn't present for the spell's duration*.

                        *Some people interpret this differently, as paradox dice roll happens before the spell's duration begins, and so mechanically what happens when a sleeper will appear is not clearly written out.

                        One option is to take the spell's casting time, the other is to retroactively apply paradox after the spell is cast.

                                        Originally posted by Phillip Morin View Post
                                        Is there a limit to the number of familiars you can have? And their Rank?
                                        In pages 101 and 183 of the core book, it states that Familiars can be of Rank 2 at most. There is no limit the amount of familiars a mage can have, save for how high the scale of the Familiar spell they can obtain, how many times they purchase the merit or successfully perform the binding ritual (described in Witchfinders and costing a Willpower dot).

                                        Keep in mind that it is a willing bond and can be terminated at any time. This means there is a shared interest, like mutual protection, a trade of favors for Essence and many more. Moreover, spirits operate with a very different logic than humans, a known familiar can damage the mage's reputation if left to their own devices and they are Connected sympathetic links that a Space practitioner can exploit. Not to mention ex familiars may have privileged information on the mage.

                                        In summary, managing a large familiar network requires a very diplomatic, careful and and prevident mage. Its certainly doable, its also a path to great versatility and mana reserves, but it requires a whole lot of work.

                                        New experiences are the font of creativity, when seeking inspiration, break your routine.
