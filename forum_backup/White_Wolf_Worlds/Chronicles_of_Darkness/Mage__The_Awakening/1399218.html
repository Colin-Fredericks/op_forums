    Rapt, Tremere, Archmastery and ST-shenanigans Workshop

    06-27-2020, 02:12 AM
    Can archmages go Rapt? Can Tremere achieve archmastery? What happens when a Rapt recovers a dot of Wisdom and temporarily recovers, but then can they become a Tremere? Do they keep their Fault? Will satisfying their Fault or using their Magical Savant Ability reduce them to preta?

    What of this thing became an archmage? What if a ex-Rapt Tremere goes archmage and then becomes a preta?

    Help answer or discuss all or none of these questions, but I've got scheming to do!

    I can't imagine Tremere can become archmasters. Nameless and Accursed suggests a hint of contempt for archmasters from the Tremere, thinking even they are deluded. Plus, the Golden Road is formed from the Archmaster's soul, which Tremere do not have.

    But what if a campaign has the first Tremere archmaster, and what horrifying thing could let this come to pass that no other Tremere can accomplish?

    Could you imagine what a Tremere Archmaster would become if it became a preta? Probably no scarier than a normal preta... unless it became something completely new!

    That's what I can think of for now. But I'm very interested in exploring this.

      Oddly enough, I literally started writing up my personal conversions to 2e for Imperial Mysteries just this week... Huh. Guess it's about time.


      You could go multiple ways on almost all of these things. I will concentrate on the ideas that jump out rather than all permutations.

      One approach is that the inverted/corrupted/paradoxical soul of a Tremere can't quite reach the true Supernal, but the powers of the boundary realms is such that some similar transformations are possible. Ascension for such a creature would represent the Abyss claiming a significant part of the Supernal Realms themselves. This would be a Bad Thing as far as most of the Patrons in the Ascension war are concerned. Even la Luna might intercede to head that sort of outcome off, but... immediately? Pfft.

      If you're going that way, I'd have them forge the Black Road straight into the Abyss. Their tests would be fairly similar but reframed quite a bit. The Supernal Test isn't them redefining their soul and creating a Lustrum to survive the Supernal Realm, it's dragging enough of the supernal spark down into the abyss to perform Februus and create their Centimani. (except theirs is Abyssal)

      They purge the false divisions in their perceptions and gain a truer form of magic. Basically, they would all start with a direct link to an Abyssal realm that is attuned enough to their nature that they can survive there indefinitely. Probably would give them bonuses for dealing with Paradox and some penalties to certain types of magic...

      It's possible that their spheres lose a number of Reach, but they gain a fundamentally new form of magic, if you're (or someone helpful is) in the mood for hombrewing up a Soul Arcana for them.

      Another approach is to decide that Tremere automatically become a Qliphoth. (IM p61)

      That said, a Tremere that goes mad in the process of ascending would pretty much certainly become a Qliphoth, since that's really similar conceptually to both. You might, if you are feeling froggy, also include the Rapt rule atop that, I suppose. They are already 2e-defined.

      Another take:

      Abyssal power packaged up with an amazingly powerful shell... that's gotta be bad. A Tremere going mad in such a fashion might allow even more of the Paradox to control their nature. (This could even be a required part of their Seeking) A being which can channel or even house or become a significant part of a powerful abyssal entity could use the former mind and "soul" fragments as a guide. Where an archmage has a realm made out of their soul, the arch tremere might be formed of something more sinister.

      From their perspective, arch Tremere become Dragons: Children of the Seventh. They have realms inside that consume various different things and melt the lies and they master the power of their soul. They can create arbitrary rules for their internal realms by conscious acts of will in ways that dazzle lesser magi, confounding spells and prediction. This can trap and ensnare intruders into their soul.

      I'd suggest their soul consumption now requires mage souls at weakest, and they have some otherwise unprecedented capacity to snare archmage souls, but no longer are they bound to Time. They don't wear down by not eating, they empower by eating in some frightful manner.

      Soul Dragons would be nasty abyssal creatures that could appear remarkably normal or even like those souls that they possess. Focusing on the attempt could permit one to even fake an oneiros using the pieces from one consumed.

      Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.

          Archmaster become Qliphoth instead of going Rapt - they always have. Qliphoth were *invented* for the gameline as failed archmasters, and the Scelesti method of making them was added later.

          RAW, Tremere can’t be archmasters as they don’t have Gnosis. Perhaps a highly advanced Tremere can transform in some other way.
