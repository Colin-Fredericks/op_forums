    Stone Scribes, again.

    05-07-2017, 02:35 PM
    Third time's the charm, right?

    What I'm thinking is that since it's Time primary what the Stone Scribes probably do is collect information about people, places and thing and then examine where those things intersect in Time in order to recover a Final Name by the "hole" left in history by a person's missing Final Name. If they have the person on hand, or a recent corpse it's pretty easy to reconstruct a Momentary or Final Name, but the legacy spends a lot of energy focusing on the lost Final Names because it hopes to find the Names of Mages from the Time Before.

    What I'm wondering is, since Time only bothers to concern itself with the Sympathy of its subjects own past selves, can it be used to determine that for instance, "Was this book was in this library at this time?" if you're able to target the book and the library simultaneously? Or, "Or was this shield and this sword were held by the same person at the same time." I think if it can, then Stone Scribes can work on recovering a Final Name by triangulating with as many pieces of intersecting paraphernalia as possible till they hit an information critical mass that results in the discovery of the Final Name.

    That's just the Time part, haven't figured out the Death bit.

    Thoughts?

                                                      I'm thinking the first attainment should be a sort of inverted Momentary Flux, where rather than giving you information about the future and whether a person will be beneficial to you it takes a glance at a subject's past and determines a brief general descriptor. So you use it on a woman passing by and Time turns up the most significant identity signifier from her Unchanged Past of Devoted Mother. This can serve as a Momentary Name, and then if it's done in their final moments can also signify their Final Name. I think the second attainment should then be a Knowing effect that allows you to determine time stamps for significant changes in their lives, for instance you know what the Devoted Mother's Unchanged Past is, but this attainment could tell you at what point she went from being Enthusiastic Partier to Devoted Mother, or Rambunctious Child to Enthusiastic Partier. That could be contested by Temporal Sympathy, but by Time 3 that wouldn't be much of an issue anymore. Then you throw in Postcognition using the added information from the second attainment as jumping off points to try and narrow the area in time in which the moments before death occurs so you can witness that and use the first attainment to get the Final Name. Obviously, need to work out the attainments, but how's that sound?
