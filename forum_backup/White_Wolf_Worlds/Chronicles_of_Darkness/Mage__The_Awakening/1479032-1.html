    Originally posted by Stupid Loserman View Post
    The Silver Ladder are communists. The Free Council are anarchists.
    As an actual communist, I must correct this. The Silver Ladder are Socialists, Communism is the common utopia both orders share, with one believing you need Philosopher-Kings to achieve it, the other believe you need Complete Freedom.

    Just to clarify definitions: Communism describes a society of equals where no state is necessary and the means of production belong to everyone. Socialism is the idea that the best/only way to achieve this society, or approach it, is through a state that organizes society towards this end and stomps threats to it. Anarchism (or at least this branch of Anarchism) is the idea that the best way to achieve this is to tear down the state itself, so no artificial structure can stop individuals from building this society or be used as a tool by power-hungry people.

    Socialism and Communism are used interchangeably on many texts, and sometimes given disparaging definitions, usually by detractors or people with little first-hand understanding. Those definitions above are what you'll see in the Manifesto of the Communist Party by Karl Marx, and other works on Marxist theory (I'm not a Marxist myself, but I find those definitions more effective in describing those political theories without too much bias).

    Other than that, I think you made as good an analogy as possible for those Orders, and I agree that political analogies don't hold so well with the others.

    Even this analogy is sketchy precisely because the game doesn't actually take into account things like economic system or anything, they're at best dealing with the broadest strokes of a political vision without ever going in details about how a political system should work on its many aspects. The bread and butter of political parties is simply not the concern of even those most political Orders, as their focus as such isn't really in dealing with Fallen politics.

    If you have a Mage character that is actually invested in politics it will be unlikely that their political party/ideology has anything to do with their Order unless a specific context calls for it (like the Orders and Exarchs in a city engaging in politics as proxy for their own conflicts). As long as they can justify their ideas to themselves and don't let them interfere with the Order's activities, anything goes.

It is not a balanced distribution, but it is really far from a clear majority towards Fascism or Fascism-like. Most aren't even interested in politics, as this ethos is also heavily about self-identification and discipline, about being instead of about making society be.

                                    Hence, @Ibeaumanior, the Ladder and the Council can be reasonably mapped onto political ideologies because they're defined in large part in a political manner. The Ladder defines itself through its completion of the Ladder, a political goal, and the Council is marked by the Great Refusal, a political moment that shaped its entire unifying philosophy. The other Orders do not have such direct consequences in politics from their spiritual beliefs.

                                    A political party or movement is defined by its ideas on things like governance and justice. The Arrow, the Mysterium, and the Guardians do not have unified ideals on such topics; individuals, Legacies, even factions, certainly might, but the Orders themselves provide no real guidance. The Ladder and the Council do have such opinions.

                                    It may be more fruitful for you to look at how Chinese or Japanese cultures handled the balance of multiple religions in daily life and public ceremony than to look at political spectra: there's a separation of concerns, usually, that lets the average person navigate the different systems of belief, and that's often how Order politics play out in a Consilium. Looking at how political flashpoints in history divided things may help illuminate how coalition-building and side-taking can happen in a Consilium.
