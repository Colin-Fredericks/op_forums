                                                        I imagine there are multiple interpretations, since how a mage experiences magic is subjective, but here are some ideas, alongside ones for Surface Information under Focused Sight:

                                                        Sacrificial Relinquishment: The Imago appears to be made of materials used in tombstones, with cryptic words for the means of death incribed as a bizzare epitaph (tecpatl, breath drinker, the thorned plume cuts, moon scented maw, etc). Under Focused Sight, observers see shadows near the Imago form into a stylized shadow puppet show of the moment.

                                                        Reaping Relinquishment: The Imago writhes as something beneath the surface tries to get out. Sometimes a face jumps at observers, trying to convey a message desperately, its jaw too mangled for their lips to be read. Under Focused Sight the Imago cracks, flooding the senses of observers with the scent of brine, revealing a tortured humanoid shadow trying to flee, only to be pulled back in by what looks like an animated wall of the Great Below, before it seals itself back perfectly.

                                                        Broken Relinquishment: The Imago sometimes tears to reveal a mouth, screaming the unfiltered, incohesive internal thoughts of the victim, in their mother tongue (since its something people can default to it when under extreme emotional stress), at the moment of trauma. Under Focused Sight an eye tears open through the Imago, its gestures replicating the ones in the moment, other times a hand reaches out, desperately reacting to the looping trauma that piece of soul is bound to.

                                                        I think perfected food would always be fresh, cooked perfectly, last indefinitely, maximize nutritional value and invalidate drawbacks. Perfected fugu does not poison you, perfected bread never molds, perfected fruits will taste as if just picked at the peak of the season and will even become ripe if it wasn't before, meat will be smoked or aged perfectly to maximize umami, fish will become as fresh as if it was just caught, etc.

                                                        I imagine the perfecting spell would correct anything that made no sense to the mage, like removing any Coca-Cola a madman used as "cooking water" for sphagetti, a highly traditional chef's spell might remove pineapple from pizza, make pasta al dente or the pasta not too dry (yellow to orange colored pasta has been speedily and excessively dried to hasten the production process), remove chili jam from egg fried rice and so on.
