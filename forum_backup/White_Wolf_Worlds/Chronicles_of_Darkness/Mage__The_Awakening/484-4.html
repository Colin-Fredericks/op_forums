                Originally posted by Suleri Drals View Post
                Okay, sorry.
                If you must make a life-extension Bless (The Proximi House isn't the question), how many years by dot seems reasonable to you ?
                Well that depends, as I'm not a huge fan of flat "add x extra years per dot" I prefer to have it tied to something more story based.

                For Example, look at the Dáirine blessing Youth of Oisin. That blessing ties the years granted to the severity of the oath sworn, potentially providing eternal youth. Conversely a Telyavel blessing may accomplish a similar effect by seizing years of life from a victim, like a mosquito feeds from a man. In that case I'd probably have it provide one year per success.

                On the Thaumaturgy side I could imagine a Qigong Sorcery that suspends and reverts the effects of aging for a set amount of time, probably one month. A Guru might devise a similar restoration spell that removes years lived from an aged, ailing woman (though they may return at an accelerated rate). In that case I'd likely favor the five years per success, with years returning at a rate of one per day.

                        Originally posted by Llewyd42 View Post
                        Hey PenDragon,
                        I'm playing a wayfarer in my friends game. I'm really enjoying it but I have some questions.
                        Hot damn that's good to hear. I'm never certain how much use, if any, outside my own table this homebrew sees, so posts like these are most heartening. Now, onto questions:
                        Originally posted by Llewyd42 View Post
                        I love the interactions between my familiar and the character but can I still use him as a mana battery?
                        Most certainly, yet another way Channelers benefit from having a familiar.
                        Originally posted by Llewyd42 View Post
                        How long does the rite of communion take? Is it an instant action or reflexive?
                        Assuming you use your familiar? Instant. It's a shared action between the Channeler and the Spirit, and spirit possession is an instant action.
                        Originally posted by Llewyd42 View Post
                        Also if we aren't using the integrity system, would having my character make a successful resolve and composure roll work?
                        Sounds good. I might change it to Willpower when Mage 2e comes out.
                        Originally posted by Llewyd42 View Post
                        Also if we invoked the yantra rules do I need dedicated yantras to my magical style or can I just use suitable symbols? Just want your advice.
                        Style is the name of the game here, for both Proximi and Thaumaturgy, so suitable Yanta are important, but more that they are suitable to the style than to the exact effect. Let me put this way, Mages put the effect first, then pick Yantra as appropriate. Thaumaturges select Yantra ahead of time, and the effect will bend thematically to fit. Make sense?

                        Originally posted by Llewyd42 View Post
                        Also the preview of the AA proximi look intense but very suitable to AA's themes. Do just their enchantments cost mana to activate or their "legacy" attainments too? Can't wait to see what you come up with for the Guardians!
                        I won't be putting up any new brew until Mage 2e comes out, I don't want to release a hack for a system that will be obsolete in a few months (and the new casting system looks like a god-send). When Mage 2e drops my plan is to update Proximi and Thaumaturgy first (I already know the changes to be made), then move on to Imbued.

                        That said, there are going to be some structural changes in associations. I'm breaking the Proximi = Ladder, Thaumaturgy = Mysterium, etc. structure I have going. It felt artificial and served no practical point. There will still be a bit of a divide, more along the lines of Poximi = Diamond, Thaumatugy = Free Councils, but even that won't be rigidly enforced. My ideas for Imbued have changed quite a bit as well, broadened if you would. Though the Heirs to Olympus will certainly still exist with largely the same abilities, I have hammered out what I think is a better structure overall.

                        Anyway, super glad to hear you are enjoying the system. If you (or others) have any further questions, stories, relentless praise, etc. don't hesitate to post.
