                                                        There's also a Life 3 + Prime 4 spell (Adamantine Arrow too, I think, but since it's a spell it can be improvised by anyone) that has a scene-long duration and allows you to deal aggravated damage with your hands. Since it's magical, you could fluff it as soft style MA attacks that happen to deal devastating damage, using the same pools as fists or kicks.
                                                        It only augments your damage type though, so you don't get a die bonus, and it costs 1 mana per attack (still, dealing agg at a sub-master level is pretty scary; at Prime or Life 5 you could possibly do something like add the primary arcana as a weapon bonus)

                                                        If your character doesn't have Prime you could do something conceptually similar - using spells that don't deal damage themselves, but augment the character's damage-dealing capabilities for the scene at the cost of Mana, like spreading rashes, fungi, infections, contact poisons or whatever with a touch, essentially just changing the aesthetics of the attack without changing the mechanics of it. (You could justify it with things like "Using the Life arcana, I transfer my muscle strength into concentrated bursts of life force, thereby not having to punch to deal damage, as anything that touches my fingers feels the pain my body can inflict"). Though just that might actually be just Life 2 (Ruling) as it doesn't actually do anything besides cosmetically change the appearance of attacks without changing their capabilities. (it might even be argued to be Veiling, as you're hiding physical aggression, something that does fall under the Life arcana).

                                                        At higher levels (i.e. 3, 4 and 5) you could add on to it with more powerful or longer-lasting effects, like adding your Life rating to attacks, making them lethal/aggravated or inflicting penalties due to warts/fungi/etc. I'm not sure about the actual balance though, as it's not exactly a direct-damage situation, so tweak the ratings as necessary.
