      Sisterhood of the Blessed (Ladder or Seers / Acanthus)
      Eleventh Question (Guardian or Mysterium / Moros) - already in the corebook
      Perfected Adept (Arrow or Council / Obrimos)
      Cryptologos (Council or Mysterium / Mastigos)
      Orphans of Proteus (Mysterium or Arrow / Thyrsus)

      The Adepts, Sisterhood and Cryptologos are all back-of-beermat converted already for my own game, as they're the Legacies my players' characters have taken. The Redoubtable Neall Raemonn Price is working up the Orphans as a hobby project.

      Bonus round, for if we find ourselves overburdened with time, will be:

      House of Ariadne (Mysterium or Seers / Acanthus)
      Forge Masters (Arrow or Council / Moros)
      Clavicularius / Bene Ashmedai (Ladder or Seers / Mastigos)
      Dreamspeakers (Council / Thyrsus)
      Daksha (Mysterium or Guardian / Obrimos)

      Although they will have to wait until after Signs of Sorcery comes out - the Clavicularius and Forge Masters in particular have Attainments based on spells from that book.

          So what's the deal with making almost all of the Legacies have two Orders? I do get a few of them, like the Eleventh Question, Cryptologos, Forge Masters, and all of the "or Seers" ones barring the House of Ariadne. On the other hand, some just really don't make sense to me, and feel kind of arbitrary; I mean, what's especially Libertine about the Perfected Adepts that makes the Free Council a Parent Order along with the Arrow over any of the other Orders? Or what's so Guardian-y about the Daksha? Or Mysterium-y/Arrow-y about the Orphans of Proteus?

          Now, I fully understand that any Legacy's doctrine can mesh well with that of any Order, but I believe a Legacy's Parent Order(s) should be exemplary matches with the Legacy, and not just OK fits, which is what's weirding me out by some of the above choices.

              Perfected Adept FC: Probably buying into the idea of bodily perfectionists, the wave of crossfit and new age means of reaching satisfaction if not enlightenment with your body.

              Daksha Guardians: They're basically racist against non-transgender, especially Sleepers, and the Guardians have a kind of split, on the one hand they have to protect Sleepers on the other their lives would have less hassles if they didn't need to protect them so assiduously.

              Orphans of Proteus Mysterium: The Mysterium want to explore the limits of the world via magic and the place of magic in the world and Orphans let them either blend in and minimize the observer effect or experience the outlandish strangeness of being some other kind of species, mineral, etc and apply their magical insight.

              That's just off the top of my head really rough idea, but anyways, since any Mage from anywhere can join a legacy is they can make a Praxis that the first attainment can be based on then it makes sense that people from multiple Orders might show an interest. Being an exemplary match doesn't have much to do with it, as long as you can manage to make yourself an ideological match somehow.
