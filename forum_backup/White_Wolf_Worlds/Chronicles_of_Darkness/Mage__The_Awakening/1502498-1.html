            I think it can be taken to vary.

            For one thing, I feel as though a lot of information on Supernal Entities should be informed by a distinction in the write-up for Totems of the Primal Wild against spirits of the Shadow, describing the former as possessing autonomy and choice compared to the more nature driven spirits. From that I draw a notion that whatever principle it is an instance of, a thing of the Supernal is still meant to have a genuine sapience, a capacity to make decisions and act according to changing needs.

            You could have some Supernals with principles or bans that make them more strictly adhere to the terms of an agreement or obligation, and some might come across more like forces of nature than thinking creatures with a sense of self-preservation. Still, I think the baseline will be that they place avoiding the Abyss as the top of their hierarchy of needs, prioritising it above all other concerns within reason (like, make a good faith effort to fulfil what they were summoned to do).

            I have approximate knowledge of many things.
