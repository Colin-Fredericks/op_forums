    Hey Satchel,

    As always glad to see your posts as I often find them enlightening. I see your spell analysis and I thought I would also poke some glaring holes in this spell that seems to not be considered yet by anyone either.

    Hey bonerstorm,
    So, I am going to assume this spell casting is related to the Spell Ban, which is a 3rd Dot Practice under the Space Arcanum.

    Ok, so let's assume you have this spell as a Rote, which happens to grant 3 Free Reach since having a Rote grants you Master status when casting the spell.

    Let's say to stay within the safety zone of casting this spell without Paradox you can perform the following reaches free:
    1 Free Reach for Sensory Range
    1 Free Reach for Advanced Potency
    1 Free Reach for Advanced Duration

    This would mean you have to be able to see the creature to cast this spell safely, but since our Mage is a Mastigos and has Space 3 they also have the attainment of Sympathetic Range, which adds an additional point of Mana from my understanding and requires some sympathetic connection to the target plus a yantra. Idigam are constantly changing shape, but if they have solidified into a standard shape which I think is required for Rank 5 then maybe you can get a drawing for a Representational Yantra which provides no bonus or penalty to spell casting roll.

    Ok, so that gives you a standard duration of 1 Month without any modifications. You then add Fate 2 for the Conditional Duration, which does up the duration by 1 point, so this puts you at 1 Year total and capping you out on Advanced Duration if not using a Reach plus Mana and taking a -10 penalty for Indefinite.

    The Mage I assume has no Spirit since they are a Mastigos and have Fate 2, which kind of seals the deal leaving Mind 1 as the only available option, so getting to the Shadow Realm is your first obstacle, which maybe you know a Thyrsus.

    So, you get into the Shadow and now need to cast a spell for 15 hours as mentioned by Satchel without anything harming you or disrupting your ritual to gain the +5 Bonus to your spell casting die pool.

    So, you are already at 1 Thyrsus Mage and 1 Mastigos Mage, this is now becoming a group project. You also mentioned Superlative Luck, which requires an Acanthus or someone that has Fate 3 and knows the Rote, so now it is three Mages. But also note the following:
    +2 Reach: The subject’s player may apply the spell’s effects to ritual spellcasting rolls, which doubles the Gnosis-derived casting time of those spells.
    So your spell now takes 30 Hours to cast instead of 15. This will require a Stamina Roll just to stay up in my opinion since you are going well over 24 Hours just to attempt casting this spell and I am sure it is a taxing thing to begin with and you keep it up for that long is got to take a toll.

    Next, as mentioned by Satchel, this creature is probably going to be huge so you are going to need to accommodate for a space large enough to capture it. So, you need to bump it to let's say the size of a small warehouse. This requires a Reach to improve the Scale to Advanced Scale and requires a -2 penalty for the Size. Let's say you spend a point of mana to negate the Paradox to reduce this to a chance die.

    Know, let's determine the Potency required for this spell in order to land the effect, it is base 1 since Duration is the Primary Factor leaving you with at least a required 8 Potency.
    1 (Sympathetic Range w/ Strong Sympathy) + 1 (No True Name) + 5 (Rank) = 7
    Your casting die pool is the following: 2 (Gnosis) + 3 (Space) + 5 (Ritual Casting) - 14 (Potency) + 4 (Rote Mudra) + 3 (Willpower) - 2 Size = 1 die w/ Superlative Luck (rote quality).

    You might get that spell off... might, but let's assume you don't die in the Shadow and manage to get 1 success with at most 2 die rolls.

    The creature is trapped behind your Ban, which it does not need to Breathe but probably needs Essence to survive. It rolls its attempts on Clash of Wills which for an Idigam is probably equivalent to the following if I recall correctly: 5 (Rank) + 5 (Influence) + 3 (Willpower) = 13.

    The Mage's Clash of Wills is the following: 2 (Gnosis) + 3 (Space) + 2 (Willpower) + 1 (Advanced Potency) + 4 (Year Duration) = 12 Die.

    Now, a Mage probably has max starting out somewhere around 7 Willpower vs. Idigam which will have 10. I think the Idigam is going to win. That is assuming the Idigam does not have a Mage Ally willing to help out.

    You also needed a small cabal to perform this feat. Granted a more powerful Mage would be able to instantly trap the creature with much higher chance of success, which I do find interesting. This of course is assuming the Spirit of Rank 5 does not have other protections in place, which I honestly think they would.

                                You're basing your argument here on doesn't-say, which is generally the point at which an argument ceases to be compelling.

                                The narrative role of Scrying based on individual sympathy is targeting individuals for personal interaction and the narrative role of Ban is to seal off a defined space. If you make use of yet another spell (e.g. The Outward and Inward Eye with its +2 Reach effect) or some other contrivance (like sending a Familiar to infiltrate the place) to piece together what its location is like from the limited view afforded by personal Scrying, you might be able to get the kind of picture needed to incorporate the symbolism of a place into your Imago, but by that point you're looking at three or four different spells sustained on the bus space of Gnosis 2 spell control just to look at the thing and target the area around it — and adding Exceptional Luck only further complicates the matter, particularly since the "add 2 Reach to apply a boon to spellcasting rolls" is specifically listed under the "add 9-Again" subtype.

                                So yes, if you have a starting character with three specific rotes, the assistance of a pack of werewolves, the time and resources to blow on spending half your Mana pool on reducing Paradox risk, and the good fortune to have a target that will stay still for long enough that you can ward a specific location over the course of nearly a full day of casting from a different location while almost certainly lacking the ability to account for the chance that it isn't there, you can theoretically block off a minor god from interaction with the wider world in a manner that is blindingly obvious to anyone who looks at the Ban.

                                This is not a trivial application of arcane might, to say nothing of the problems that can still arise from any servants the creature made still running around, the impact of cutting off a part of the world from the rest of the world, the possibility that the idigam is in Twilight in proximity to Sleepers, the chance that another mage stumbles across your unshielded spell and gets curious, or you just plain fail to contain the Paradox and wind up losing a vital spell factor from the mix or attracting Abyssal phenomena to the area through your Nimbus.

                                To treat these as non-issues is disingenuous.
