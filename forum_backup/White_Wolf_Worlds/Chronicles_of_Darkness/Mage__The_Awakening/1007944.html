    Helping my non-mage player

    11-25-2016, 12:53 AM
    So i just started up my first awakening 2e game, in fact my first NWod game in years and the first time with this group. i have 3 (likely 4 soon) players and two of them are quite happy with their characters, the setting and their magical powers, easily getting into it. One player however; while she enjoys the setting and the plot, does not enjoy being a Mage. She finds the magic system a convoluted headache that left her afraid to do anything all game and the magic itself overly full of spells. i don't blame her as assigning reach, sorting out spell bonuses and penalties and even PICKING your spell can be quite the hassle. In short she wants to keep playing mage she just doesn't want to BE a mage.

    Now this leaves me with a bit of a problem as i don't know what to have her play that she'd enjoy. My first thought was to let her play a sleepwalker who was aiding the cabal but the problem is she enjoys punching things, solving her problems with violence and generally being a badass, and i'm uncertain she'll be happy once the group starts leaving her behind in terms of power, martial arts and a shotgun can only go so far and batman levels of strategy just isn't her style. My second thought was to let her play a proximi, perhaps with some bonus merits at creation, so she can have the powers she wants without being buried in them, but i realized she'd still need to understand and use the magic system she's already expressed dislike of, so i'd prefer another option.

    Now the best solution for her would be to pull up requiem or forsaken and let her player a Gangrel or a Rahu which i know she'd enjoy. the problem is i wasn't really planning on incorporating either world into the mage game, i was thinking of maybe throwing in a couple of vampires in as a one off and i wasn't planning to use werewolf at all. vampires would have alot of problems going to many of the places the cabal went, both due to a lack of otherworld based magic and that pressing sunlight allergy, and the werewolf would cause the shadow to somewhat dominate a game that was only minorly shadow based (particularly annoying in that she was the only one who even had Spirit and even then only 1 dot, so the other player would be SoL), and i had no intention of bringing in the tribes or auspices.

    I'm hoping i'd be able to find a minor template that i might tweak a bit but i'm not seeing one that wouldn't be a whole lot of work. does anyone know a good solution to the problem i'm facing right now?

      Beast is pretty mechanically straightforward — it's explicitly designed to be crossover-friendly, none of the powers are scaled, the characters have precious non-Yantra bonus dice to offer as a teamwork aid, its primary otherworld is an obscure corner of one that no other game but Mage presently deals with, and the way its Integrity equivalent works can help keep up with the gains Arcane Experience represents.

      Character creation might take a bit of sorting out, given how front-loaded some aspects of it are, but if her primary issues with Awakened spellcasting are the breadth of examples and variable overload, Beast lends itself fairly well to "punch things good" as a problem-solving tool.

          Loading her up on Supernatural Merits, which can only be taken by mortals anyway, looks promising.

          Or you could just use the mechanics for, say, vampiric Disciplines or werewolf Gifts for her powers without saying that they actually are those same powers.

          For example, maybe the character has Super Strength, Super Stamina, and Super Speed. Within the game world, people say that her body is infused with Supernal energies, making her a subtype of Proximi whose Blessings are her supreme physical prowess instead of sorcery. However, on the character sheet, you give her dots in Vigor, Resilience, and Celerity from Vampire, using its effects while replacing the Vitae cost with either Willpower or Mana as you see fit.

              Remember that any character with Supernatural Powers/Merits is treated as Sleepwalker for Supernal Magic. So your non-mage player can easily have any Supernatural Merits she wants, in any configuration. Most of those from 1E Second Sight book are updated in 2E CoD Rulebook. If she will change her mind on proper Mage mechanics, you can easily Awaken her later. ​ Like 21C Hermit mentioned, you can say she was infused with Supernal Energies. Or just she is result of other weird phenomena, full in World of (Chronicles) of Darkness - and by this - she is living Mystery on it self and by this great source of Obsession for two other proper mage players.

              I would then advise to give 1 extra Obsession slot for mage players, to have them 'for free' non-mage player character as common Obsession. I done the same thing in my Mage/Promethean crossover game - my Awakened players are all Obsessed over our Created character.
