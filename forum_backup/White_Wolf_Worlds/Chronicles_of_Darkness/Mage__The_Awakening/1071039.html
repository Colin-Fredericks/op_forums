    Idigam

    03-31-2017, 12:53 AM
    So, I read this and noticed that there is a whole lot of overlap available between Abyssal spirits and Idigam. In fact, the only space I am spotting that there wouldn't be is that Abyssal stuff can't be really true (but, given the Lie conceit, that doesn't mean it's not represented in the fallen world) and that Idigam weren't or aren't but could, theoretically, be. Also, idigam were bound moonside and hate werewolves while abyssal stuff generally is assumed not to care, overmuch.

    The differences are subtle enow that an enterprising storyteller may well decide none exist.

    It occurs that they could be handled as a strange form of Bound that was imprisoned on the moon by werewolves instead of Atlantis.

    (I ran a game in oWoD that had an abyssal spirit that acted very much like this, oddly enough.)


    The only gotcha with this interpretation I'm seeing is that their fluid nature requires that they lean on something real which sounds more Lower Depthsy than Abyssal. ('Course, there is a lot of overlap twixt those two, also.)


    Has anybody played around with using Idigam as a major Mage antagonist? Thoughts on story hooks/quirks with magic could be fun to discuss.

    Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.

          Well I consider myself more a Mage player than a werewolf player, but I do like the idea of making the Abyss a more encroaching threat on all gamelines, so I did entertain the idea of the Idigam being abyssal in some fashion. However, the Idigam are far more interesting when you keep them as Lovecraftian horrors, so they should be hard to understand, even to Mages.

          The most important thing I would establish first is that any Mage who is trying to tackle the Idigam is going to be getting into werewolf territory, so they either need to ally themselves with the werewolves or be prepared to spurn them and get on their bad side--which is not smart.

          You can throw all the potential answers to what Idigam are and keep Mages distracted or arguing about it except for the PC's, who either require werewolf help to get a better understanding of the Idigam, or at least a fighting chance.

          And the other thing I wanna mention is that the Idigam might actually be real, genuine, of this reality spirits--not abyssal at all. They can seem that way, but even gulmoths and acamoth tend to stay as one thing when they appear. They're manifestations of a lie, so they're corruptive of the nature they'd normally represent.

          Idigam are not like this. They're spirits of concepts that cannot or no longer exist. In Mage lore they could be victims of Exarchal nonsense from the time before, perhaps even surviving spirits of the time before that only existed in deep space.

          They can also be void spirits, spirits not of the abyss, but just proper spatial void. A spirit that formed in deep space with nothing around it from stray essence floating through space. Like if quanta essence of stars, but from stars that hold different resonance, collided in space enough times and formed a spirit, but it has nothing around to resonate with so it becomes a spirit of nothing. It isn't from the abyss, but it is like the abyss because of its unfortunate creation.

          If you want to mage it up even further, you could have them be a bizarre entity that can be both from the shadow and the astral realms---a spirit of the sidereal wastes that came out of the astral in the actual deep space that this former goetia originated from. Being a goetia in the Shadow of deep space invariably twists it into a shadow-spirit with no known resonance, driving it alien and mad and drifting to space until it hits our neck of the woods... enter werewolves (or mages). Or it is captured by Luna.

          But with that in mind, do try to make sure that Mages are interested in the antagonists from other gamelines with hubris in mind. Idigam are not a Mage thing, so when Mages take interest, it is due to an Obsession, and Obsessions = hijinks and trouble. Always remember the Hubris aspect.

          In fact the only gameline I think has more clout to be all-encompassing is probably Demon. Hell Demon even has this weird thing where apparently the Strix know about Infrastructure and mess with it, and it is implied a few times that the God-Machine uses the abyss as some sort of fuel source occassionally.

          Demon also has a fascinating idea that Idigam are somehow aborted god-machines, creating this weird dilemna where the God-Machine might be the Spirit of the Future and Idigam are Spirits-of-the-Past, such as before or during the formation of the Earth, or even from the Time Before.

          What if one, just one, idigam survived to the end of the universe and then traveled back in time to guarantee its own survival and omnipotence, becoming the God-Machine in the process. Probably has a sort of Durandal-esque survival scheme it is hatching. ((Durandal being a mad AI from the old Marathon games by Bungie---Durandal wants to survive the end of the universe)).

                  Those would be very unusual wolves. Werewolves take care of themselves, are very pack oriented. They're a little bit xenophobic. It would also depend on what the design of the werewolves are--if they're partial to Wisdom renown, maybe, but if your werewolf pack are Glory or Honor renown focused, then they're going to refuse or even compete with mages to take down the idigam before the mages do.

                  Werewolves might know more about the Idigam than you think too. In-fact, if your scenario focuses on mage characters, it is more interesting for them to know less than the werewolves. If you are running a more werewolf focused game, it is more interesting if the mages know more but want to be unhelpful--like the only mages who know the info you need are either Mad, Scelesti, or Seers! Perhaps even Banishers! No bueno!

                  The werewolves in either case need a good reason for helping Mages, especially if they're going to play second fiddle.

                  Have fun solving this idea!
