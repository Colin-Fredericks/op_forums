    Distillers of Primordial Elixir (Homebrew Legacy)

    08-30-2023, 04:03 AM
    Distillers of Primordial Elixir
    Most Moroi are alchemists; they naturally find deep mysteries of transformations and distillations around them. Most Moroi are not interested in unseen kingdoms of Spirit and wonders they hide. And thus most Moroi skip over the most deep and primordial type of alchemy, done by acquiring, distilling and mixing various types of spirit Essence.

    Most, but not all of them.

    Distillers of Primordial Elixir is an ancient Legacy. They claim to be as ancient as Dreamspeakers, saying that before humans even understood concept of matter, shamans and mystics understood spiritual Essence and potential it holds within itself. They claim that Lune herself gifted this Legacy to earthbound Awakened via her fickle Lunes, in exchange for working her labors on Earth. Many think that those claims are but boasts - many Legacies claim ancient heritage - but it's hard to deny that variations of this Legacy are found all over the world.​

    Origins
    Parentage: Moros, Free Council, Mysterium​

    Nickname: Distillers, Witches, Brewers

    Background: Most of Distillers were either shamans or alchemists even before they joined the Legacy; all of them share same fascination with Shadow, it's creatures and Mysteries within. Sizeable minority of Distillers were Wolf-Blooded or Shadow Occultists before their Awakening, and decided to keep and refine their praxis to use with their newfound powers of Arcana.

    Appearances: Distillers prefer to dress practically; if experiment goes wrong, there will be no time to change the clothes so they aren't eaten by aggressive materialized ephemera. Many of them prefer to keep as many elixirs as they can on them all the time, so garments with many pouches, pockets and straps are commonplace.

    In the Orders: Most of Distillers are either libertines or mystagogues, either engaging in cultural shamanism rooted in Mysteries of Shadow, or exploring high concept of alchemy in application to spiritual Essence. Arrows gladly use results of such alchemy, but rarely interested in mastering the craft itself; thearchs and Guardians rarely show any interest in such areas. Most Seers pursue easier and less gruesome paths to power, with exception of those who worship Raptor, who enjoy turning machines of ephemera into vessels for bubbling power to use as they please. Distillers are also have connection with Proximi Dynasty of House Telyavel, whose Scions usually join this Legacy as continuation of their semi-vampiric, semi-alchemical praxis; many Proximi of this house serve as assistants to members of Legacy.

    Among the Paths: Two Paths primarily fill this Legacy: Moroi and Thyrsoi. They move from two different directions towards the same concept; while Moroi wish to deepen their alchemy and move closer to primordial truths, Thyrsoi want to refine and capture in matter the primordial truths they already know. Other Paths don't show much interest in Legacy Mysteries or symbolism.

    Character concepts: Folk medicine expert, entheogen designer, illegal doping provider, spirit hunter/poacher, polluter of the Hisil, herpentologist, collector of trash, modern alchemist with laboratory goggles.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Spirit 2, Crafts 2 with Alchemy or Shadow Lore specialization, one of the following additional Skills at 2 dots or higher: Medicine, Occult, or Science.
    ​
    Initiation: Successful initiation is not hard, but dangerous for careless and unprepared - two qualities Distillers actively don't want to see in Legacy. For successful initiation, prospect must successfully brew potion out of spirit Essence - process which usually involved capturing spirits, extracting Essence from them, distilling, mixing and letting it brew. After that, prospect must consume the potion and survive the process, while keeping calm mind. Successfully metabolized potion changes soul of prospect, initiating him into Legacy.

    Organization: For most of history, Distillers of Primordial Elixir did not have any organization in the Legacy; instead, knowledge and secrets were shared from teacher to the student verbally or in elixir form. As people all over the Earth became more literate, more and more members of Legacy began to record and translate their knowledge of primordial alchemy; but only with formation of Free Council Legacy started to become a truly global phenomenon. Today, a lot of Legacy tutors are in direct contact with each other; talks about forming a global Mystery Cult are brewing, but millennia of isolation are not easily overcome.

    Theory: Both living and unliving things are transformed in presence of energy and time. Spiritual ephemera has qualities of both, mercurial, shifting and changing by nature. From these qualities, spiritual ecologies arise, there spirits hunt each other in endless cycle of predation. They dare not mix their Essences, lest they start to mutate in unpredictable fashion; but it is exactly in those mutations where deep, primordial truths about Essence and it's nature are hidden. In the light of both Sun and Moon, the distillation will begin, uncovering many wonderous and terrifying secrets to those are brave enough to find them.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Spirit/Matter

    Yantras: potions and pouches, alchemical equipment and laboratories (+1-2); imbibing drugs, poisons and raw Essence (+1, +2 if given negative Condition); creating spell in Loci (up to Rank of Locus in question); voluntary possession by spirit (+2); sacrificing spirits and processing their ephemera (up to Rank of spirit in question)

    Oblations: brewing, distilling and consuming potions; curing or inflicting diseases and poisoning with potions; growing and harvesting rare plants and fungi; tending to Resonances and Loci and harvesting their Essence

    Rote Skill: Crafts

    Attainments
    First: Taste the Brew
    Prerequisites: Initiation

    First thing Distiller learns to do is to get a taste of ingredient and know what is it good for. By touching and tasting a tiny bit of spiritual Essence, Distiller can immediately ask number of questions about this Essence up to his dots in Spirit, emulating Taste the Essence spell.

    Optional: Matter 1

    With Matter knowledge, Distiller is able to use the same effect on any unliving matter, asking of questions about this Essence up to his dots in Matter.

    Second: Acquired Taste
    Prerequisites: Spirit 2, Crafts 3

    Once Distiller starts his practice in the earnest, he must be able to protect himself from spirits who will come to see him as an arcane predator that he is, hunting them down and harvesting their Essence for usage. After a scene of preparation by imbibing elixirs, Distiller can replicate Ephemeral Shield spell, Reach to Advanced Duration.

    Optional: Matter 2

    With Matter knowledge, Distiller is additionally able to protect himself against effects of any regular dangerous substance, or even those imbibed with power of Shadow, such as Azlu's poisons.

    Third: Brew and Distill
    Prerequisites: Spirit 3. In addition to the Crafts 3 requirement from the previous Attainment, the Distiller must increase the other Skill she used to meet the requirements of the Legacy to 3, or must attain two dots in a second skill from the list of possible requirements.

    At this point, Distiller is already proficient experimenter, creating all kinds of wonderful and terrible brews. But now he can start doing real primordial alchemy and replicate Forge Talen spell. This Attainment requires captured and bound spirit. By ritually using Attainment on him, Distiller can pick spirit apart, tearing talens and fixing them down as distilled potions; this process takes at least an hour and a point of Mana per potion created in this way. Like natural talens, those are one-time only, but using them does not return spiritual energy to the spirit, who must heal his wounds naturally - if Distillers even let him go, which they are unlikely to do. Brewed potions must be used in a month, or they expire - sometimes with catastrophic effects for those who drink stale potion.

    Optional: Matter 3

    With Matter knowledge, Distiller is additionally able to imbibe a material item with a potion - coating sword or bullets in poison, adding a salve to pouch, and so on.

    Fourth: Primal Distillation
    Prerequisites: Spirit 4, Crafts 4

    With this Attainment, Distiller is able to process spirit whole, without tearing chunks out of him. This Attainment replicates effect of Craft Fetish spell and requires spirit in hibernation. By ritually using Attainment on him, Distiller distills his Essence into bubbling spiritual protoplasma bursting with power. Depending on type of Influence and/or Numina in it, one must either drink potion for it to have effect, or splash it on target to have effect. Distiller can "recharge" potion by carefully adding more Resonant Essence to it, but adding too much will allow spirit to reform itself with disastrous consequences.

    Optional: Matter 4

    With Matter knowledge, Distiller is able to give his potions any form he wants to. Using this option on talen-potions and spirit-potions can make them gaseous or solid (usually they are liquid-like ephemera which needs to be contained in the potion), and can give them any size Distiller wants them to be, allowing to carry as many as he wants on his person.​

    Final: Primordial Elixir
    Prerequisites: Spirit 5, Crafts 5

    The crowning achievement of Legacy, at this point Distiller is able to brew Primordial Elixir. This Attainment replicates effect of Mercurial Dissolution, dissolving all but strongest of spirits into mercurial screaming mass of chaotic Essence; while effect is Lasting, Distiller can redefine nature of spirit by force-feeding Essence of desired Resonance or just using Shape Spirit directly. Or he can avoid doing this, instead using dissolved spirit as solution basis for other acts of Primordial alchemy.

    Optional: Matter 5

    With Matter knowledge, Distiller is able to dissolve anything - to distill it later, of course. This Attainment allows to create Lasting Perfected Water, or Alkahest, out of normal pure water. Combined with previous Attainment and containers prepared to hold Alkahest, Distiller is able to force-feed anything which can be dissolved by Alkahest to dissolved spirit, and then use Brew and Distill to create unique potions which can't be produced by anything else.​


    So What Is Primordial Alchemy?
    ...whatever you want it to be in your game. Generally, things like Premium Grade Triple-Distilled Lunar Spirit from Eleven Shaman Blessings and alike Shadow secrets are a part of it, and just allowing curious Awakened to find and exploit them is perfectly possible. But even better choice is to use Supernal Invention system with it and allow your Distillers to brew all kinds of wonderful and terrible potions. If this system is used, usual Drawbacks from Wonders are Parts, Hazards and Fuel; they often also has Location And Time. Also do remember that unlike many other Wonders, potions are usually one-time; Wonder is recept of potion, and Scale determines how many potions one can produce with given Parts and Fuel.

    New Spell: Taste Essence (Spirit • or Death •)
    Practice: Knowing
    Primary Factor: Potency
    Suggested Rote Skills: Science, Investigation, Medicine

    Caster analyzes the Death or Spirit Essence, tasting it and understanding it's properties. Per dot of Potency, the caster intuits one piece of information about Essence. For example:
    • Resonance of Essence;
    • Amount of Essence;
    • Mutagenic properties: caster knows how Essence will change spirit or ghost tasting it, and can generally understand how quickly it will happen;
    • Original source of Essence;
    • Symbolic properties and bonuses it can grant.
    +1 Reach: caster can also intuit facts about Essence source.​​

    New Spell: Forge Talen (Spirit •••) ​
    ​Practice: Fraying
    Primary Factor: Duration
    Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Intimidating, Weaponry

    Mage is tearing out part of willing spirit’s energy, fraying his Essence and coalescing part of his power into small trinket called Talen. This spell is usually used after negotiating with spirit in question, which agrees to cede part of itself in exchange for favors or services from the Shaman.

    Storyteller and player create the resulting Talen together; as general rule, power of Talen is roughly equal to single use of spirit‘s most powerful Influence. Storyteller may also use spirit’s Numina or werewolf’s Gifts for inspiration. Powers of the Talen should be linked with spirit thematically; you cannot create Talen which allows to cure wounds from spirit of pain. Player may also choose to just tear out the Essence of the spirit; Talen holds (Potency) of Essence with resonance of the spirit, which may be used with Channel Essence and alike spells.

    Talen exists and has semi-material form for the Duration of the spell; or until it is used or destroyed. After this, it dissolves, releasing bound spiritual energy, which returns to the original owner. This spell causes resistant lethal damage to the spirit equal to Potency of the spell. This damage is immediately healed when Talen ceases to exist. Talen has Strong sympathy to the spirit it was created from.

    +1 Reach: Spirit does not have to be willing; the spell becomes Withstood with spirit's Rank.

    +2 Reach, 1 Mana: The Duration of spell becomes Lasting, and talen persists until it is used or destroyed.

    Add Matter •••: Instead of creating new form for the Talen, shaman can immediately bind torn energy to the existing material item, which will become Talen. This item will not dissolved when it's used as Talen, but Talen is still one-use only.

    Add Prime •••: As above, but Imbued Item can be used instead.

    Add Space •••: As above, but ritual space or chamber can be used instead.​

    New Spell: Mercurial Dissolution (Spirit ••••​•​)
    Practice: Unmaking
    Primary Factor: Potency
    Withstood: Rank
    Suggested Rote Skills: Persuasion, Brawl, Science

    Spirit is defined by it's nature. It's fundamental to it's existence; without it there is no spirit.
    Or so they think, those who think themselves Wise.
    True Masters know better.

    With this spell, mage destroys nature of spirit without ending it's existence. It collapses to pure chaos of Essence, falling mad without knowledge of what it is or how it can exist. It permanently loses all Influences, Numina and Manifestations; it can feed on any kind of Essence without being magath (arguable being in a much worse state anyways). Spirit exists in this state, until it manages to coalesce and redefine it's own nature by filling it's Essence track with Essence of certain Resonance, or until mage gives him his nature back with Shape Spirit (Spirit ••••); such changes will be Lasting.

    +1 Reach, 1 Mana: The resulting "null"-spirit is especially fickle and prone to change and is able to coalesce momentarily by act of his own will by fixating on certain thing.
