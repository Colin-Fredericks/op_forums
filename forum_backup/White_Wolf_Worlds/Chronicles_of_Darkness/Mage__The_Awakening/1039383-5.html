In a way, the Unmaking method is a lot more horrifying (though possibly less terrifying) than the more gruesome Unraveling method.

                                                    I'm coming in late, but the discussion about the Sympathetic Connection chart isn't applicable to discussing a spell that would sever your limb from your body. The discussion about Unmaking in the chart applies to the Sympathetic Connection only, for instance if you wanted to sever a person's Sympathetic Connection to their own arm for some reason (maybe it would make it impossible to use Mudra that rely on hand gestures?). If you want to sever the arm itself, the withstand would be based on their pattern, likely Stamina, because you aren't adjusting the Sympathy between a person and their limb. The Connected Sympathy would immediately begin to degrade once the limb is severed due to the naturally occurring phenomena around Sympathy, similar to how the Sympathetic Connection for bodily samples is only Strong. That's a side effect, though, not a direct assault.

                                                    Example: Unmaking a Mage's Familiar, that is Sympathetically Connected to them, should only take Spirit 5. Despite the Sympathetic Connection, it wouldn't require Space 5. I think the issue here is mainly arising due to the Space Arcanum covering both Sympathetic Connections and Distances.
