                                                        On a side note, one thing I'd like to see is how the various Legacies view each other. Now, every Legacy isn't going to have an official view on every other . . . but some of them are clearly going to bump into each other (a lot). For example, the Clavicularius (Clavicularii?), Fallen Pillar, and Fangs of Mara all work extensively with the mentally ill - surely they have stereotypes and opinions of each other?

                                                        So - word count permitting - it might be nice to give each Legacy's general attitude towards the 3-7 Legacies they run into the most (or have the strongest opinion on). Asymmetry would be fine, too - just because Legacy X (say the Transhuman Engineers) have a strong opinion on Legacy Y (the Dreamspeakers, perhaps), that doesn't meant Legacy Y even knows that Legacy X even exists. Helps underscore that many of the Legacies have been around for centuries/millennia, with their own feuds/alliances/etc.

                                                        As for what Legacies should be in the main book (assuming you're still taking suggestions), I definitely support the idea of the Brotherhood of the Demon Wind and the Clavicularius. However, while I'm a big fan of the Elemental Masteries, it might make more sense to hold off, and do all five at the same time. They are all interrelated, after all, and kind of represent the path not taken (to organize mage society, as opposed to the Orders).

                                                        As for the Free Council Legacy . . . I understand why people want the Dreamspeakers. They fits the Free Council themes well, and the Dreamspeakers were a memorable group. I wonder, though, if it would make more sense to use the Roses of Eden or the Blank Badges - two of the anti-Atlantanean Legacies the Order developed to fight the Nameless War/Great Refusal.
