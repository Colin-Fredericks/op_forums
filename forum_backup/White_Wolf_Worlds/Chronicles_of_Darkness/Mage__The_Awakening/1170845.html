    Sympathetic Connection of a Spell

    12-26-2017, 11:23 PM
    Hey everyone,

    So I have a question, can you use Correspondence on a spell to track down the person that cast it, because according to the description for the spell it seems perfectly possible:

    Correspondence (Space •)
    Practice: Knowing
    Primary Factor: Potency
    Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Empathy, Medicine
    We are all of us defined by our connections, and through this spell a mage learns those definitions. For every level of Potency, the mage learns one of the subject's sympathetic links. The spell reveals the subject's oldest and strongest links first. She understands these connections in the same manner the subject thinks of them (e.g. "my childhood home," not "1414 Willowbrook Drive, Columbus, OH"). If the other half of the sympathetic link is within the mage's sensory range, she knows that and knows its exact location.
    +1 Reach: The mage can "follow" a trail of sympathetic links. When she learns of one of her initial subject's sympathetic connections, she may choose to learn one of that subject's connections, and so on. For example, she might follow a wedding ring to its owner, then its owner's spouse, then the spouse's place of employment.
    +1 Reach: The mage understands the emotional character of the connection in broad terms. "My childhood home" might carry notes of comfort and safety or fear and loathing, depending on the subject's upbringing.
    +2 Reach: The mage may specify the connections she wishes to uncover in general terms ("an object with a strong link to his childhood") or ("those she hates"). Again, the knowledge is contextualized based on the subject's perceptions.
    +2 Reach: If the subject is a Keyed spell or Iris (p. 243), the mage may use one level of Potency to discover the Key instead of a link.
    I ask because one of my players noticed a spell was cast in an area that they were investigating and decided to get the sympathetic connection to track down the mage that cast the spell initially. They didn't spend any extra reach to get a trail, but I did give them kind of a vague understanding of where the mage was when casting the spell.

    Or would the spell if analyzed reveal "Creator" and show an image of the person that cast the spell since it is based on how the subject "thinks" of them.

                          That’s precisely what we're talking about. Note how the word follow is in quotations, in other words it’s not a literal following but jumping from one set of Sympathetic Connections to another. Space spells dealing with Sympathy seem to generally target one or the other of a connection or a subject via its connection. Correspondence targets the former, whereas something like Locate Object can target the latter.

                                  Originally posted by Yuukale View Post
                                  Btw, what if you follow the connection to a dead person? Do I tell the mage he sees a dead body or do I say that the connection he wants doesn't exist?
                                  You only get a general sense of the nature of the connection, at best you also receive information of the emotional content of that connection. If the person that’s your subject has Sympathetic connections to someone whose died then whether they know it, and how long the person has been dead will change the strength of the connection and the information that can be gleaned from it, but the connection would still be there until it naturally decays over time (or is altered by magic).

                                  So, say you cast on someone searching for their Sympathetic connection to their father. It’s strong, and carries a loving emotional resonance. You Borrow the Threads, ask what dad’s name was and open a Scrying window to the father. Turns out he’s lying dead on his living room floor, looks like a heart attack that happened the night before.

                                  You release the borrowed thread and tell the kid the bad news, and then watch the connection’s emotional content change to include grief. It’s still there, but it will eventually fade as one half of the relationship is no longer there to maintain the connection.

                                  It could be argued that ghosts exist in part to make sure those connections never fade.
