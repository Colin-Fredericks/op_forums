                            I like getting things. I have a whole list of 'Gifts I Want' for my werewolf. Non-Affinity Gifts cost 5 Experiences, as much as a Primal Urge, the werewolf equivalent to Gnosis, while Facets for Gifts you've already opened up just cost 2. So I wasn't exactly getting these every session but I was spending my Experiences when I gathered enough to get something from my list. And the times I had 5 or more Experiences saved up were rare.

                            I grant this is not a problem for everyone, some people are entirely comfortable and patient playing game after game, watching the Experiences stack up and not purchasing any of those things they've wanted, or not wanting something else to begin with. It might just come up less in mage, since Gifts aren't a thing and Gnosis acts as a sort of hard limiter for Arcana.

                            But 5 Experiences is a lot to save up for. How much people seem to get per games varies, but there's usually not more than 1 Experience gained per session, so you're looking at at least 5 sessions to pick up that Gnosis. While with the same 1 Experience per session you can pick up those Merits, the non-character creation only ones at least, piecemeal.

                            More might depend on what the game is going to be like. If it's a short game that's going to have 1 session a week for a month, purchasing that Gnosis upgrade just won't come up. And if your game is, say, taking place isolated on a deserted island, Merit dots in Mystery Cult to represent a legion of followers might be prohibited. And, of course, a lot is going to depend on your character concept. If you get the Gnosis, figuring you can buy your Fighting Styles as the game progresses, you might not want to start with a martial artist cult leader who's a high ranking member of the Guardians of the Veil. I don't think there's an objectively better choice without taking in factors like these.
