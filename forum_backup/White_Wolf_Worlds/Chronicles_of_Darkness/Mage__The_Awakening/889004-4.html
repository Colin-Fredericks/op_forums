    Here's a Seer trick:

    &quot;The Reason The Pentacle Doesn't Stand A Chance&quot;
    1. Be Mastigos (not necessary, but it helps)
    2. Join the Seers - join the Panopticon.
    3. Get Seer Status ••• and Prelacy •••.
    4. The Crown of Vision gives you a Weak sympathetic connection to anyone you have no connection to.
    5. You now have access to all the Space bullshit.
    6. ???
    7. Profit!

    At the weakest (Space ••), your Glorious Seer Overseer can use Correspondence and Borrow Threads to rearrange Sympathetic Connections between anyone on the planet. Or you can just use Veil Sympathy to carpet bomb some random schmuck's sympathetic links for shiggles.

    It just goes up from there.

    Technically, the only way to block you from attacking someone sympathetically if you've got the Crown of Vision is if it gets suppressed by Veil Sympathy or a similar effect - destroying the link means you have no connection to them, so Crown of Vision gives you a Weak connection to them.

    The only drawback (besides being at the beck and call of a hateful and tyrannical being - since you're a Seer of the Throne, though, that's what we call a perk) is that you can't improve your fake Weak connection (since it only exists for the purposes of determining whether or not you can use the sympathetic range attainment). On the other hand... you can't be targeted through it.

    The Mysterium gets a tweaked version of this (Egregore ••••• gives you a Medium connection to every other Mysterium member as long as you are in a Mysterium sanctum), but it doesn't have the same overwhelming advantage of choice, and it allows people to hit back, which the Crown of Vision doesn't.

    The name is only slightly facetious.


    Another Seer trick!

    &quot;When I Say Jump, You Say 'How High'&quot;
    1. Be a Mastigos (Mastigos master race! But seriously.) Let's say your shadow name is Mentok.
    2. Join the Seers - join the Hegemony.
    3. Pause for the Crown of Obligation (steady Mana gain is lovely), but we have loftier goals.
    4. Grab Mind ••••• and Seer Status •••
    5. Spend 2xp to grab a Hollow One.
    7. ???
    8. Profit!

    All Mind spells cast on Hollow Ones are Indefinite. For free. They also start with all Mental and Social Attributes and Skills at 0 dots.

    We want to drop a few spells on the Hollow One.

    1. Social Networking (Making) - 5 dots of Allies. Out of nowhere.
    2. Psychic Reprogramming (Patterning) - Give them a Virtue of Obedient to Mentok, and whatever else you damn well please.
    3. Amorality (Unmaking) - Now delete their Vice. They now act in a way entirely consistent with their Virtue.
    4. Augment Mind (Perfecting) - Give them as high a Manipulation as possible. It'll be at least a five unless you push the Potency higher.
    5. Gain Skill (Patterning) - The Hollow One now has Persuasion •••••, at least.
    6. Enhance Skill (Perfecting) - Sorry, I misspoke: Persuasion ••••• •••••¹.

    Now we hand them a phone. And tell them to make a few requests.

    This is all stuff you could do to yourself, or to an ally, or whatever. But the Hollow One is a low maintenance (one Mana every few days - you literally get Mana back every time you do something fun), won't betray you (you get to define all of their memories, and there's the bit where they won't willingly act against their impetus to obey you), and is a great patsy (when the NSA comes knocking to find out who asked one of their employees to upload all that porn onto their servers, you have perfect accountability).

    Is it immoral as fuck? I'm not even going to dignify that with a response, straw man who is asking me questions.

    1. Technically speaking, if we're going to be doing silly stuff, applying Enhance Skill to a skill rating you got from Gain Skill is kosher. You ain't stacking anything, because only one of those is actually a bonus. If you find this doubtful, drop Enhance Skill and just pump Gain Skill's Potency instead.
