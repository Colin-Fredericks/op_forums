              Mechanically, it is a Creature Scrub. In M:tG, exiling removes counters and other persistent effects from the creature and "resets" it to normal. So a Time reset or cleansing effect?

              Based on the exact wording, this also fixes control of the creature to you permanently (if, for example, you had control of the creature until your next turn, and you do this, you will keep control because the original creature is "gone" and this "new" one is under your control). Creating a copy of a being that has any temporary effects on it made permanent sounds kind of Abyssal, but maybe Time (again) or Prime might fit?

                                  Conjurer's Closest is primarily used as a way to grant a Creature pseudo-Vigilance or (most usefully) to retrigger Enter the Battlefield (ETB) effects.

                                  As an artifact in MtAw I think it would be best represented as something that could reset a character's attributes to those they had at the beginning of the scene/day/chapter (as appropriate). An actual temporal stutter forward would not be appropriate, the way the card is worded there is no time between the creature leaving and reentering the battlefield, priority isn't passed and even State Based Actions aren't checked.
