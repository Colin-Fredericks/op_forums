    Chronicle of the Rhamnousian Codex

    10-09-2025, 04:55 PM
    As I promised, I will start to do the write up of the chronicle I run with my friends. Today I will start with an introduction to the main characters and a bit of history of how we started. Be my guest in the future to ask more info or for resources of things I used

    A bit of backstory before starting. I only once tried to run a mage the awakening campaing, and I had never played any other chronicle of darkness game (or any other world of darkness game neither), not even vampire. The time I tried to run the game was like 7 years ago in a biweekly open game night. At that time I got overwhealmed with the system, not only by the mechanics of it but by the lack of a pre existing campaign and the complexity of the whole system. Last time I storytelled I got blocked under stress when we were playing, and it was an awful feeling, so I decided if I ever was going to run mage again was when I had things well prepared and figured out. In 2024 the DM of my party of friends stopped Dming (she moved away) so I decided it was the time to run the Mage campaign again (I was fed up with Dnd at that point). But I decided to first have things figured out, so I spent months preparing things, to the point my gf (who really wanted to resume playing) told me run it already, and we started playing in january of 2025.

    The chronicle runs in modern day Britain, at the start centered in London. Since noone had ever played a world of darkness game I decided everyone was going to play a sleeper staring before their awakening. So we could slowly get into the rules (social combat alone took me a session of explaining, and I still don’t get it well). Also, I thought Rping the awakening was a cool concept, and was one of the highlights of when I run mage many years ago. I told them about the paths of mage, so their characters would be thought having in mind in what they will transform into. At first I asked them to have a rough sketch of their characters but further it time I asked them for a detailed background (I sweet talked them offering a full xp point of they did so).


    So without further ado meet the players:


    Alexander Lestrade -the hard nosed and destined to be Moros detective

    Lestrade’s player and I had been playing together for more than 10 years now. He is the only one that played too when I tried to run mage like 7 years ago, and at that time he played mostly the same character. He is a full fledged powergamer, and normally plays the serious man against the group’s antics, both ingame and in real life. His pleasure comes from understanding the game mechanics and optimizing things to 11 (he is an engineer and a Factorio player). Since I’m also a powergamer when I play I don’t mind (I’m a failed engineer and an Oxygen not Included player), and I try to use his nature to my advantage, asking him with help with the rules and him being in charge of reminding the other players how to earn beats and don’t do things suicidally stupid.

    Lestrade considers himself, first and foremost, a detective. Not only by profession, but by nature. His father was also a detective, and he inherited the instinct and drive to sniff a case no matter how complicated it seemed. He had a mentor in the force, Braxton, who was assigned to his father’s murder. Lestrade considered Braxton a good man and a bad detective. It wasn’t just because he couldn’t resolve his father murder, the thing is he wasn’t willing to take that extra step, often outside the law, needed to make justice. Most men in the force couldn’t. Lestrade wasn’t one of those men.


    Ratman – the hobo rat and drugs enthusiast, destined to became a Thyrsus

    His player is the kind of person that seeks chaos. Is not the kind of player that likes to derail the campaign, he just wants to have agency and do bizarre things, so I told him before starting for this campaign the tone was going to be a bit more grounded and serious than other times, so I asked him trying to think a character accordingly. And, of course, he ignored me and made a meme character. And I decided, sighing profusely, to accept it. His gist is that he has rats with him all the time (we rolled a dice and he has like 9) and most of the rats have joke names. One is called Ratalie Portman, other Sphincter (a reference to Splinter from TMNT), 4 have portmanteau names based on the Beatles, a fifth is just Yoko Ono, etc. Besides that he has been very ambiguous about the backstory of his character, and some of his ideas were so meme I had to prune them a bit.

    Little is known about Ratman. That seems to be everyone’s thought, including Ratman. What is known is that he lives in a dark and forgotten station of the London Tube, assisted by his surprisingly well trained and borderline self aware rats, he grows his own hallucinogen mushrooms in the train tracks, and he peddles small amount of drugs with some selected customers. As far as he knows, he has no name and was raised by the rats themselves. He is a minuscule man, far shorter than 1.60, and his most notable physical characteristic is his large overcoat (in its pockets the rats travel with him all times) and of course the cultivated lack of hygiene that surrounds him like an aura.


    Akihiro Matsudaira – the contemplative keeper of a Japanese garden and destined to be Obrimos

    When my gf asked me what kind of character should she play I told him that mages tended to be quirky people. She ruminated on the idea, and at some point we saw the movie “Perfect Days” of Wim Wenders and, together with her obsession with Japan, she decided on this character. As a player she likes chaos and to have agency as well as Ratman’s player, but less in a meme way and more in the way of developing the things she likes IRL. She has been advocating for some time for me to let her include our dog in the chronicle, she even created the character sheet (a Mastigos, if you are curious) but even I have some boundaries. Is worth noting that while the other players gave me less than a page of the backstories of their character, she gave me 5 pages, completed with IRL pictures of the imaginary relatives.

    In you wonder into the Kyoto Garden in Holland Park you might find, perhaps feeding the koi or combing the sands of the dry garden, a calmed Japanese man, conscientiously doing his tasks. More often though, you will find this man, Mr Matsudaira, caring for the capybaras that live in the premise, particularly Samani, his favorite one (there are no capybaras in Holland Park, just in someone goes there and starts blaming me for the disappointment). Mr Matsudaira is 64 years old, but he looks more like 50, and has enjoyed a life of quiet contemplation as sole caretaker of the Kyoto garden for many years now. Almost noone knows about his past, about how he decided to travel from Japan to England to search for his family’s secrets. Specially few people know how, inside this perfect example of a thoughtful man, there is a person that would love public attention, and he enjoys in the cover of darkness and anonymity go to sing at Karaoke bars, Babymetal being his favorite one, though he also likes the classics, that being Britney Spears and the likes, of course.


    There is more in the story of the players which I will develop it as I tell the rest of the chronicle, and there is a 4th player whom will be introduced in the future.

                  Okay, I’ll try to keep this posts in a weekly basis until I’m up to date, I don’t have a diary of the campaign (just some sparse notes) so I’m trying to reconstruct all this by memory. I appreciate the recommendations KaiserAfini, sometimes is good to have a reminder of stuff


                  First of all, the campaign starts without the characters knowing each other in advance.

                  In the case of Inspector Lestrade, he received a call at his precinct from a woman called Laila Fatayri. She explained that she was worried for his brother, Jeoffrey, since he had been showing an erratic behavior in the past weeks and he hasn’t been answering any calls or messages. She was abroad, but returning to London in 2 days. Normally this wouldn’t call for a detective to intervene, but perhaps because Lestrade felt pity, or maybe because he was just bored, he decided to check on the apartment of Jeoffrey.

                  Since Layla wasn’t convinced the police was going to help she also contacted her friend and bonsai handling instructor, Mr Matsudaira. Mr Matsudaira, besides caring for the Kyoto Garden of Holland Park, also gave some classes of different Japanese related crafts in the premises, like Kintsugi, Origami, Ikebana, etc. He, being an honorable old man, told her that he was going to check on him.

                  Finally we have Ratman, who sometimes worked as courier for the waitress of the Wenlock Arms, a pub. She was called Keiko, and the packages he had to deliver were the kind of “don’t open, don’t ask what’s inside” type. He owed her for a botched previous job so she “kindly” ask him to repay his debt by search for a book in the possession of Jeoffrey. All she told him about the book was that it seemed someone important was interested in the book, and she was wondering why.

                  What they all know about Jeoffrey is that he was a professor of archaeology at the University of London, and he returned from an excavation a couple of weeks ago. Normally a calmed and reclusive fellow, in his talks with his sister after his return he seemed excitable and even aggressive.

                  He lived in a small apartment a second floor in Shoreditch, the direction provided by Laila, and the first to arrive was Lestrade, who stayed in his car for a couple of minutes checking the surroundings. The second one was Mr Matsudaira in his bicycle, who knocked on the main door and waited. Finally Ratman appeared from a corner, and decided that the best course of action was to throw a rat up the roof to help him enter the apartment. The rat in question was Yoko Ono, and even with a shitload of penalizers he scored a critical success (he heavily invested in dex and animal ken). The rat went up in the sky, inside the chimney and mission impossible his way into the apartment, all this while Lestrade was looking from inside his car. Flabbergasted by the happenings of the last 5 minutes, Lestrade he went up and caught Ratman before he could see him coming. After a brief interrogation on why he was throwing rats into roofs and a failed lie from Ratman trying to minimize the situation, he grabbed Ratman by the coat and decided he was coming with him to the station after checking on Joffrey. At the door, they meet Mr Matsudaira, who politely bows to them as he is waiting from a response from inside. He explains he came after Layla also called him to check on Jeoffrey. An old lady appears, the owner of the apartments who lives in the first floor, and says she hasn’t seen Jeoffrey since 2 days ago, but at that time she heard a commotion in his apartment. After Lestrade showing his badge and a successful persuasion roll, they walk up the stairs. They knock Jeoffrey apartment’s door, noone answers and Yoko Ono opens the lock for them (again, a succesful roll aganist a gazilion penalizers). Inside they find signs of struggle and broken stuff, and they understand that the situation is serious. Ratman explains he was sent to find Jeoffrey for a book and Lestrade decides to let him go, work together for now and focus in what appears as a crime scene. After an extended investigation roll they find that: a) no valuable things were taken b) Ratman finds a stash with a small amount of heroin hidden in the bathroom, which he hides from the rest c) they find a coaster with “Red Basement” written on it. Mr Matsudaira also tries to access the computer, and after a fail on the computer’s check the player decides to take a critical fail, causing a security program on the PC frying all the info in the hard drive. If the player had succeeded they would had found a mail of someone called Kuber offering Jeoffrey to buy a book he found in his recent archaeological trip for a very steep sum of money.

                  Lestrade check on the police’s information about the Red Basement and he founds out a nightclub in a nearby neighborhood with connections with the Russian Syndicate. They head there together, but because of Ratman smell he has to travel in Mr Matsudaira’s bycicle. When they arrive the find in the side of a building a stair, that going down lead to a red door. Mr Matsudaira and Lestrade decide to walk through the red door, while Ratman checks around the building finding a small window that leads to the bathroom. It is early in the night so the place is just opening, and there is a large bouncer just after crossing the door. Mr Matsudaira passes his roll to enter but Lestrade fails, making him show his badge, causing him to be rudely stopped at the entrance. With a thick Russian accent the bouncer tells him people like him are not welcomed in the place. Lestrade’s player opts for an intimidation roll before resorting to violence, but he fails and takes a critical fail, and gets the condition leveraged. The bouncer recognizes him and tells him his people knows the things he has been accused at the police. He leaves waiting outside the stairs. In the meanwhile, Ratman has entered the bathroom using his small body (he is almost children size) and decides gods knows why make an investigation roll in there. Since he gets a good amount of successes but not enough to get anything mechanically helpful I tell him he finds copy of the communist manifesto. Going back to Mr Matsudaira when he enters the nightclub he sees seated in the bar an elegant woman in his 50’s, who is looking with curiosity at him. An empathy roll tells him she has an aura of authority and is probably the owner of the place, and later talking he will find out she is called Kalinka. However the player had written the aspiration of the character to sing karaoke, so he goes straight to a microphone in the stage. In the almost empty nightclub he sings a song of Atarashi Gakko and scores a critical success. I let the player choose a condition and since I had printed a document I found on the internet called “overly specific conditions conditions cards” she takes “too sexy for my shirt” as the condition, furthering sinking the session into Monthy Python’s levels

                  Kalinka will be the first social combat of the game and I explain them they need different successes in different skills related to making the other person like them. I also tell her that the success singing karaoke improved the first impression of him. Mr Matsudaira sits right next to her, orders himself a whiskey and, as she looks at him puzzled, he silently makes an origami of a crane and gives it to her. With that success that’s a door down. She tells him she likes mysterious men but is wondering what is he doing at his club. He explains he is making a favor to a friend, searching for his brother Jeoffrey, and with a succesful roll of empathy there goes another door. And since the player has the “too sexy for my shirt” condition she uses it as Mr Matsudaira tries to seduce Kalinka with a pick up line while he unbuttons his shirt. With a critical success she blushes, tells him he is charming and they can meet later, but that is hardly the place to do that. Those are enough doors, so she tells him that Jeoffrey used to frequent the bar. He was a nice young man, albeit quiet, but they talked a couple of times. She knew about his drug addiction but in the nightclub they didn’t sell drugs, however she knew his supplier was some local gangsters in Tottenham, called the Downstreet boys.

                  Going back to Ratman in the bathroom, Lestrade finds the window of the bathroom and tells him through it he needs a photo of a rat inside the kitchen. Ratman finds a door close to the bathroom door that leads to the kitchen, and sends Yoko Ono again inside. He manages to take a picture of the rat inside but as Yoko Ono fails the stealth roll, and he takes the crit fail and Yoko Ono finds her demise under the boot of the cook of the place. Since Mr Matsudaira already got all they needed, he escapes again through the window, and with a photo of the rat in the kitchen Lestrade goes to the Bouncer. Now with a good intimidation roll he tells him he needs all the information they have on him erased or the health inspectors will sure find a picture of a rat in the kitchen interesting. The bouncer concedes and Lestrade loses the leveraged condition.

                  Next time the meeting with the downstreet boys

                      Finding Jeoffrey

                      After finding the information they were searching for in the red basement they head together to Tottenham, to find the Downstreet Boys. After some convincing Lestrade accepts Ratman into his car, despite the foul smell. A good roll of contacts of Lestrade with the police office gives him an address, and they head there. They find a car repair shop, and behind a storehouse. They enter cautiously, Ratman going first since he is the less suspicious to be there of the group. Inside they find 4 men, 2 close to the entrance dressed as mechanics, one in a desk and close to him a large man with a bodyguard attitude. They all seem roughed up, like they had been in a fight not long ago. Ratman and says he was looking for a client of him, Jeoffrey, which causes inmediate tension in the men in the warehouse, as they go to grab different blunt objects. Mr Matsudaira enters and tries to defuse the situation, wisely Lestrade stays outside just in case things gets violent. Matsudaira is honest and explains they are searching Jeoffrey cause he disappeared a couple of days ago. After some good rolls they explain the situation. Jeoffrey came the day before, searching to buy. He was looking bad and behaving strangely, he is normally just another white collar junkie but he was being blunt and seemed nervous. They made a joke at his expense and he snapped, charging towards them. He attacked all them, and even the large bodyguard couldn’t contain him. The bodyguard hit him with a knife, Jeoffrey grunted in pain, but just took the drugs and fled, with the knife still stucked on him. The group asks the downstreet boys if they know where could he had gone after and they tell them there was a crackhouse close by, where the junkies go to be left alone.

                      After that the group head there, is already quite late in the night, and the place seems deserted from outside. It is the classical abandoned house in the bad part of town, in the edge of a small field, covered in graffities. When they get close they see in the floor 2 young males, dead. A quick medicine check indicated they died product of injuries made by a blunt instrument. They group changes to high alert, Lestrade draws his gun and Mr Matsudaira find an iron pipe in the floor to wield as a weapon. They hear glutural screams as they enter the place and find a trail of blood heading deep inside. They follow the trail through a corridor, that heads to a room. Inside there is another person laying on the floor, with a knife stuck to his shoulder. The description is the one of Jeoffrey and he is clearly dead. As they start investigating the room a large white man, with his upper chest naked showing neo-nazi tatoos and covered in blood appears in the door, and with a deranged look on his face he screams agresivelly at them. First combat of the campaign. The man charges at them, with a wrench in his hand, and Mr Matsudaira blocks him with his steel pipe. He has defensive combat, and a lot of ranks in weaponry (since he was trained in kenjutsu since a child) so he has a lot of defense. Lestrade shoots, hitting and adding some damage while Ratman uses cheap shot to lower the attacker defense. I made the attacker extra hardy (he had a berzerk tilt transforming lethal into bashing) and expending willpower I manage to land a hit on Matsudaira. However is a quick combat and works well to introduce all us to the basics. After the fight Lestrade checks the body and I make him take a whistanding roll: inside the dead attacker was a spirit of rage and now he wants to fetter inside Lestrade. Lestrade fails and keeps feeling weird and aggressive after that, but manages not to lash out against any of them. Besides the corpse of Joeffrey (who seemed to die by the wounds of the combat against the neo-nazi) they find a book close to him, the one they seemed to be searching for. Lestrade calls the police to cordon the area, and then he calls Layla (Jeoffrey sister) telling her the news. She is heartbroken but thanks the help of all them to find out what happened to him. After they decide to go first to the waitress who Ratman owed the book. It is very late but and the pub is about to close. Keiko (the waitress) is still there and asks them to come in. She sees Lestrade and tells him if he can talk just for a second in private. He doesn’t understand but comply, and while he is looking away she removes the spirit of anger from him, without him noticing. After that they go back to the group, checks on the book and seems disappointed. She says the debt is repaid and they can keep the book, that somehow is now linked to them, and they will have to find out how.

                      About the book: if they want to know more about the book I make them made a weekly long extended roll of intelligence plus academics, and no one in the party has lots of ranks in those. So the info they are getting has been slow. In that first roll they find out the book seems a compilation of different texts in different languages, all looking extremely old. In the next sessions they will continue rolling, and mostly failing, so they know so far that: besides the different texts all of them seem to have annotations made by a single and modern handwriting, which seems to be coded. Some of the languages can be identified as very ancient Greek and Hebrew, cuneiform writing, hieroglyphs, ancient Chinese writing, Mayan symbols, etc. After their awakening they checked the text and found no obvious magic on it, but a strong fate connection to the group.

                      Mr Matsudaira had already told the Kalinka, the Russian woman that managed the Red Basement, that he was going to see her in the night. And after some decent rolls, they have a good night together, earning him a dot in contacts in the russian syndicate. Lestrade goes to his house to drink whiskey alone still baffled of how a favor to just check on a missing person ended up that way. And Ratman goes back to the subways where he lives. That night they all get very weird dreams. They all dream of London but in all their dreams it seems different, Mr Matsudaira a shiny city made by almost pure light and strange clockwork machinery, Ratman a city overrun by nature with impossible trees and animals, Lestrade a dark city, quiet and full of shadows. They also see different people in their dreams: Ratman sees first someone looking as from prehispanic southamerica, then an african man with traditional cloths and finally a white blonde woman with Celtic clothing. Mr Matsudaira sees a Japanese looking woman as well in traditional clothing, a bearded man dressed as an ancient Greek warrior and an old man with curly grey hair dressed in a colourful long tunic. Finally Lestrade sees a Mediterranean looking woman in a black tunic, a large and bearded man in similar clothing and a middle eastern looking man in a copper disk armor. In the morning Mr Matsudaira and Lestrade get a call from Layla telling them the funeral of Jeoffrey is going to be in 2 days, and she would like for them to go. This gives the party some time to do things. First Ratman decides to visit Holland Park where Mr Matsudaira told him he works and lives. Mr Matsudaira has been very worried since he returned cause his favorite Capibara, Samani, is about to give birth. Ratman comes right in time, since he has actually extensive knowledge in roedent reproduction and a shitload of ranks in animal ken. He helps Samani, and she gives birth to many healthy offsprings, and Matsudaira is delighted (and feeling in debt with Ratman). They get together after with Lestrade and together they try to find out more about the book. Ratman knows a bookseller guy, they go and check on him. He says it looks like a very interesting book but he has no info about it (even if he is definitely willing to buy it). I don’t remember if they did anything else after, so next update will be Jeoffrey’s funeral and the awakenings.

                          The funeral at Highgate

                          It has been 2 days since the group found Jeoffrey’s body and they head together to his funeral, at Highgate cementary. In this days as I said before they were all having this strange dreams, and they feel all a bit confused and tired by the lack of good sleep. It barely rains, and I describe their arrival as they go up in a cobblestone road over a mild hill, in a place where besides the mourners there are some tourists. Personally I had been to Highgate and it is a lovely place, with the old gravestones hidden between the lush green, and I tried to share that feeling. The group dresses soberly for the occasion, even Ratman is bathed and somewhat groomed. As they are walking from the car to the main gate they walk past a homeless man sitting in the street, and the homeless man calls to Lestrade. Lestrade gives him a more intently look time, a man in his late 60’s, with a massive grey beard, unruly long and wavy hair that works as a frame for his wrinkled face, where the most significant feature is the bright blue deep into his sunken eyes. He asks for Lestrade to lean closer to him, and Lestrade after some hesitation does so. He enigmatically whispers to him “Sisyphus loves peanuts” and shows an amused grin after seeing the look of puzzlement on Lestrade. Lestrade drops a coin in the hat in front of the man, the homeless man nods gratefully still with the amused smile on his face and Lestrade continues to the funeral. In the place where the service is being held they see a small number of people, between them Layla. She is a petite young woman, and her large black eyes and long black hair contrasting with his white face denotes her Lebanese’s roots. He his crying inconsolably and they group closes to her to pay their respects. Even broken by all that happened she says she is very grateful for them searching and finding what happened to him, even if they didn’t had to, giving them a hug each. After the service the party goes out on their own. Lestrade sees in the entrance a statue of Sisyphus, and since he is curious he goes out to the vendor’s stalls that are close by, buys a bags of peanuts and leaves in under it. Nothing happens, as he imagined, and he mutters at curse at himself about hearing old crazy men’s rambling. They continue their way to the car and halfway there a group of 4 man walks in front of them and intercept them. 2 of them are dressed with black suits and wearing glasses, looking like your average bodyguard. One is weirder, a bit taller than the rest, wearing a raincoat with a hood on him. Under the hood he is wearing an Oni mask, and no skin of him seems uncovered. Finally the last one is the only one that does the talking. He has the factions of someone from India, in his thirties and wears a nice grey suit with no tie that along with the expensive watch makes him look like a successful entrepreneur. He says first he is sorry about Jeoffrey’s death, and a empahy rolls tells the group that he isn’t. He introduces himself as Kuber and he says he was trying to make a deal with Jeoffrey, about buying a book he found, and his sources tell him that now they have this book. He tells them he is willing to pay handsomely for it. Mr Matsudaira, talking for them, tells him they will consider this proposition and contact him with a response. Kuber answers he would prefer to conclude this business as as soon as possible, and they can go together to get the book. A perception roll tells them the bodyguards and Kuber are all concealing weapons, and they start moving subtly in a way for them to envelope them. They play along for a while and head to a group of cars in the other side down the road, and in the meanwhile Lestrade looks around: he can’t see the homeless man anywhere, but he sees around the statue of Sisyphus a bunch of squirrels congregated to eat the peanuts. Then he sees a guide dog of a blind man barking furiously at the squirrels, and as the dog thrust forward the blind man falls to the side of a kebab cart, dislodging the wood wedge it had to keep it from moving. Lestrade, sensing this is fortuitously connected to them gives the group a sign and they make for a break: Ratman makes a cheap shot to the large hooded man in the Oni mask making him loose balance and falling prone, and Mr Matsudaira and Lestrade shove the rest of them pushing them away, as they all run towards Lestrade’s car. While armed, they don’t seem to be whiling to draw their weapons with many people close by, and the body guards run towards the group to tackle them, while Oni mask men stands up and Kuber just stands there. While they were doing that, the kebab cart, now free of the wood wedge, starts falling down the hill towards them, while its owner seems distracted by the ruckus of the blind man falling and the dog charging into the squirrels. One of the body guards jumps over Ratman and the other over Mr Matsudaira, both of them manage to dislodge from the grapple. However Lestrade feels a strange force, like an invisible push, forcing him into the ground, feeling unable to move. At that point, the Oni masked man is standing, and about to charge them, but suddenly the Kebab cart, falling of the hill behind them hitting both him and Kuber. Lestrade feels free of the force pushing him down, while the bodyguards are distracted by the Benny Hill-esce scene happening behind them. The group uses the distraction, runs to the cart and dart off away from the attackers.

                          They will head all to their respective places to have a rest after this, the lack of good sleep and the fact they realize something weird is connected to the book and now them is weighting on them. And at the night they will have the weirdest of dreams.

                              Awakening in the Primal Wild

                              It has been a couple of strange days, even for Ratman’s standards. Hiding in some unknown spot of the London Tube, he was feeling knackered, and thought he was going to pass out of sleep, hoping for a dreamless night. It wasn’t the case. He felt awakening in his subway station, but the place looked different. He saw the deep roots of massive trees entangling in the archway of the structures, vines creeping through the edge of the white tiles of the walls, pools of mug and weird insects crawling through the floors, and this primal and unidentifiable background noise, as is the whole world was awake and humans had never set foot of it. He walked across the station, and despite the dilapidated state he realized it wasn’t the one where he had his lair, it was the Kew Garden station. Realizing not always he had such lucid dreams he decided to do some exploring.
                              Walking up from the traintrack area, he saw who paths leading out of the station. He looked more carefully at them, and saw in one that the walls had this strange glyphs, that reminded him something out of the prehispanic America part of the British Museum. He decided to walk that path out of the station, and found as he was leaving that in the other path something can only be described as a Dragon lurked in the way. Then he decided, against his common sense (also known as the Storyteller telling him not to do that) to befriend the dragon with the intention of making him his pet. Despite the really good roll, still wasn’t enough to make an avatar of Dragons to befriend him, and the creature started attacking him. The pain of the wounds felt particularly real for a dream, so he wisely decided to run away. By the way, for each time they got wounded in the supernal the players got an aggravated wound, so they got they couldn’t screw around very much.
                              He ran towards Kew Gardens, and as he was dodging the massive dragon chasing him he could see the surroundings. The whole city was overrun by vines and vegetation, the buildings completely covered in green, sprouting from the walls and windows. In the sky a hurricane like massive cloud rotated menacingly, centered en Kew Gardens. And in the center of Kew Gardens he saw a light beaming to the sky, calling him, where should be the eye of the rotating cloud. As he run to the gardens, he saw close to the perimeter wall a tree close enough for him to climb it and jump to inside the premises, escaping from the angry dragon.
                              Inside the gardens, the vegetation was untamed and overgrown as in the rest of London, and he found himself close to a large pond of water, between him and the way toward the light that was calling him. As he draw closer to the pond he saw his reflection, but instead of him he saw a large rat, with bright patters of lines in all his body, very similar to the lines he had as a birthmark. Before he could make any conclusion of it, something hissed behind him. A masssive cat, with lines similar but in a different color to the ones he saw in the rat, appeared behind me. -Finally, my prey, you returned to me. I had waited so long to taste you again- Said the creatuve, before lunging at him. Ratman, again, ran around the pond, into a thick jungle that separated him from the beaming light. As he run the massive can followed him, and he scurried between the trees and roots to avoid it’s claws. Deeper he got into the jungle harder was the wind, as he barely kept from lifting from the ground. At some point when was reaching the end of the jungle he found himself cornered, but he realized there was a tiny path signaled by the growth of corn plants between the bushes. He took the path, escaping the massive cat and the storm, and he found himself facing the place that emitted the light into the sky.
                              It was the Temperate House greenhouse of the garden, and the structure seemed pristine clean, contrasting with the surrounding. He entered the place, the first floor was teeming with strange and exotic animals and plants, all oblivious to his presence. When he climbed to the second floor, he was 2 rooms in opposite directions. One was fully stashed with all the drugs he had ever imagine. In the other was a pond, and a small wooden bench besides it, and he felt his destiny was connected to that room. In his mind he understood the decision, was one room or the other. With a successful temptation roll he went to the pond room. In front of the small wooden bench a brassier with coals burning. In his mind an idea was resonating: he had to sacrifice something. Around him he had the glimpse, in the cristals of the greenhouse that three people were watching over him. He decided to sacrifice one of the rats, and with effort he throw one in the coals, and as the animal burned the air filled with smoke. He felt the smoke numbing him, until he was finally unconcious again.

                              Awakening in Stygia

                              Lestrade also wished for a peaceful night. As his dreams transported him to a dark and silent version of London realized it wasn’t going to be the case. He recognized the place, he was in the tunnel of Towerbridge Road, in the south side of the Thames, under the traintracks. Around him the city looked still modern but like noone had lived there for many years. Some buildings were crumbling, some showed veins of what looked like precious metals. The sky was dark and silent, lacking stars or moon, just a faint glow that came from nowhere specific. The streets were illuminated by candles instead of modern lamps, and most notably, there was nothing alive. No grass, no trees, no birds. He did saw in the distance, to the north, a faint beam raising towards the sky, and he felt the urge of getting close to it.
                              As he walked Towerbridge Road towards the north, he saw a large boulder of what it seemed like pure silver in the street. As he got closer, the boulder started to move, taking a humanoid and massive shape, and with just the sound of the metal contorting he charged at Lestrade. Seeing the creature coming at him he couldn’t avoid getting hit, but after he managed to scurry to a nearby park. There were no trees but instead ornated tombstones, and hiding behind the tombstones he sneaked his way closer to the river. When he got there he saw that the Tower Bridge, and all other bridges crossing the Thames, was destroyed, and the light he had seen seemed to come from the Tower of London. Also, looking around the shore, he saw many people. They all looked a bit odd, like they weren’t completely solid, and one of those persons was walking towards him.
                              As the figure got closer, he recognized him. It was his late father, and he seemed a both worried a and puzzled. Lestrade went closer to him and before the figure could speak he hugged him. “You shouldn’t be here, it is not your time yet” he said. “Still, is good to see you kid”. Lestrade was over 40, and his dad looked younger than him, like when he was in his prime, but the “kid” part still fitted. “I don’t know how I got here neither, but last time I checked I was alive” said Lestrade. His father pointed at his arm, looked solid, not partially transparent as him “seems somehow you still are”. “Anyways, she sent me, told me you would need helping crossing. There is someone that can help, come with me” said his dad. “who sent you? And most important, how you died?” Lestrade was still in shock, but he was, over all, a detective. “Who sent me is not important. And I think you know the answer to the other question. I was killed.”. They were walking fast, his dad seemed worried and searching around for threats. The people around them just ignored, focused in looking at the river. The Thames was much darker than he had ever seen, the waters streamed to the east as always and it seemed even if it looked calmed they moved a strong pace. Crossing it by swimming seemed as a bad idea, and his father warned him not to touch the waters at one point he came too close. Looking at the river, he saw upstream, to the west, a faint glow, different from the light that came from the tower of London. “that is for us to get there, when we are ready” said his father “not for you. Before you ask, no one knows what is there”.
                              They continue walking, reaching where was a pier. In the end of it, a small wooden boat, unmolested by the current, waited peacefully. “That’s your ride kid. I can’t come with you, sadly. One last thing, if you still are fixed in my death, as you seemed, I hided in the shed behind our house a case with the info you might need” his father hesitated for a moment. “This is the kind of things you forget to tell while you are alive and you have a lot of time here to think about saying: I’m proud of you”. His father hugged him, and Lestrade felt something weird he hadn’t felt in a long time: a knot in his throat. Finally his father handed him a black ball of wool, telling him she told him to give him this, and waved as Lestrade jumped on the wooden boat.
                              Inside the wooden boat there was and old man with an oar. He seemed kind but tired, and Lestrade asked him cross him to the other side. The man nodded, but he showed his hand, like asking for his payment. “The pay remains the same, a silver coin”. Lestrade looked at his wallet, hoping to magically find a silver coin stored there. Sadly his wallet was empty, only two pictures remained: one of his mother and one with his mentor in the PD. “one of those is enough” said Charon, as he continued extending his hand. Lestrade didn’t understand what it meant, but he understood that there were consequences, bad ones. He took him a while to decide, knowing there was no other way to cross the river, and finally handed the picture of Braxton, his mentor. Charon took the picture. and touched with it the water of the Thames. After, he returned it to Lestrade as silently started rowing. The picture slowly faded in front of Lestrade, and as it was fading he started remembering all the times with Braxton in the force, all the cases worked together, the excursions for a pint in the after hours, the many times he went to have dinner at his house. The memories started to feel like dreams, until he find himself unable to recall them anymore, and in his hand he only had a blank paper. As Lestrade was wondering what he was thinking about, the boat arrived through the Traitor’s Gate to the Tower of London.
                              Inside there are a bunch of tombstones and urns, one of the urns has gemstones and coins. Lestrade tries to grab some but a spectral hand comes from the ground hurting him. He decides to go inside the castle, crossing the door he find himself in what appears to be a labyrinth. After getting lost a couple of times he decides to drop the ball of wool in the floor, to see the ball rolling by its own movement across the labyrinth. He follows the yarn, until he hears the sound of a large creature, and knowing enough of Greek mythology to know the Minotaur was near, he sneaks his way past it, throwing to a distant spot a rock to distract him.
                              Finally he arrives at a set of stairs that go to the last floor of the White Tower. There he finds a room which looks filled with pictures and newspapers cuts connected with red string, and in another room an executioner’s stone. He also feels his destiny is in the executioner’s stone, so he manages to avoid the temptation of the room filled with clues. He hears the sound of bells, telling him he has little time left, he sees that the paintings around him seems to show 2 men and a woman that look at him intently. He feels shadows behind him, and sees a massive axe close to the stone. He finally kneels, placing his head in the hole of the stone. The shadows draw nearer, he can sense one graving the massive axe, he closes his eyes as the wind of someone lifting the axe just behind him and then absolute darkness.

                              Awakening in the Aether

                              Mr Matsudaira is in his small shed watching the Japanese Gardens of Holland Park. He slowly lets himself drift into sleep, as the occurrences of the last days still hang in his thoughts. In his dreams he appears to be in a Pub, The Olde Cheshire Cheese, and he heads to the streets. He walks out to Fleet Street, but London looks and feels different. The city looks perfect, like it was build in a white and bright stone, with no dents or imperfections. Between the buildings seems to be cables and machinery built in bright brass, as the things one would expect in a factory of the end of the XIX century, but in a perfect state, humming vividly. In the skies the clouds seems moving at vertiginous pace, heavy a bright blue colored, behind a completely white sky. As the clouds move they hastily rain on the streets, leaving puddles of bright fluorescent water.
                              Down Fleet street, to the east, he also sees a golden light, pulsating towards the sky, and he feels it is calling him. Everything is so luminous, that the eyes of Mr Matsudaira takes a bit to get used to it, and while he does something appears in front of him. A massive humanoid, perfect in his beauty, with white wings. The angel looks at him, intensely, and as he does Mr Matsudaira feels an acute pain in his chest. The angel speaks, his voice is beautiful as well, but there is something mechanical and alien in the tone. “Foreign being…. Filtration… Human? Mage… I sense something else, something it shouldn’t be. I see righteousness but… contamination…” The angel stirs, his face fills with determination and anger, as he rises his sword “Corruption, I must purge.” As he says that his sword falls. Mr Matsudaira, awestruck with the angel, refuses to run, and tries to convince him he is no enemy, to no avail. The sword falls hurting him, and the angel prepares to strike again. Mr Matsudaira finally understand there is no talking his way out, and seeing Fleet Street is starting to get filled with similar creatures, he runs towards an alley. As he runs there the angel follows him, but as he enters the alley he sees something in the wall. He recognizes it as an octagonal mirror from his studies in Shinto, and as the angel crosses in front of the mirror, a type of golden light flashes into him, temporarily blinding him. Mr Matsudaira takes the opportunity to hide, running through the alley to the entrance of a building. He realizes in order to get to the pulsating light and avoid Fleet street he has to get through the building.
                              Outside it seems like an office building but inside the massive mechanism of an unknown machine operates, with perfect golden cogs and pumping through glass pipes this bright blue water. He sees he could go though the cogs, but he realized there is a conveyor belt that goes across the precinct. He jumps, grabbing from it and gets to the other side. In the other side there is a wall with control panels and not that far away a door leading out. There he sees guarding it a being that appears made of fractals of different lights floating. He decides to just push his way out of the factory, getting a bit hurt in the process, but manages to hide himself again in the alleys. Finally, he arrives to Paternoster Square, and realized that the gold light pulsating to the skies comes from St Paul Cathedral, which is located passing the gate at the end of the square. Also he sees that what in normal London would be a large column in the center of square, here is a tall column of fire, and also close to the gate there is another angel looking being. He gets close to the column of fire to see a symbol in base, of a sun shining with golden lights, which he recognizes as the symbol of Amaterasu, the sun goddess from Shinto. He understands he has to place his hand in the column of fire, and as he does he reaches and finds the katana he inherited from his family, the one he took from Japan and has been training with since he was a child.
                              In the path to the cathedral there is a gate, casually guarded by an angel. He sneaks around the angel, hits him with the katana, stunning him, and runs towards the cathedral. In the way appears a large being, made by a central eye, rings with eyes in it and 6 wings, floating fast towards him. He runs and avoids the ray of lights, darting across the gardens, until he reaches the doors. The creature stops following him at the door, leaving, as he enters. Inside the cathedral seems calm, the stones faintly glowing, and the stained glass seems to have figures watching as Mr Matsudaira moves inside. He feels again a pain in his chest for a second, but soon lapses and reaches the center of it.
                              In the altar there is a large cauldron of gold, with burning liquid inside that creates a great flame. In the sides of the church there is all matter of bonsais, the most beautiful he had ever seen. He sees lines carved in the church floor, to the sides were the bonsais are, and he understands that if something is dropped in the cauldron the burning liquid will spill and head towards the plants. He feels he has to sacrifice something important to the cauldron as well if he wants to fulfill his destiny. Barely able to do it, he throws his heirloom katana. The katana first burns and dissolves in the liquid, as it spills through the cracks in the floor, first setting ablaze the bonsais, and then the whole building, with Mr Matsudaira inside it.
