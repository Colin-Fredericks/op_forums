    Creation of Free Council - Equality as Mystery?

    03-05-2018, 01:51 PM
    I'm in very early planing of historical Mage 2E chronicle about Nameless War and creation of Free Council. When I started thinking about this plot more, I remembered how important are to Mage 2E the Mysteries as story fuel. Basically - it's not MtAw 2E if płot is not revolving about some Mystery. As I wanted to make chronicle about clearly political topic - creation of new ( Global) Order /Sect - I have a large conundrum. How to make political matter into (supernatural) Mystery for PCs to uncover?

    And then it hit me - If Exarchs are Living symbols of Tyranny that Seers uncovered and studies - maybe Free Council members can find forces representing Equality / Democracy? That Nameless PCs will create Free Council to use power of those 'things', like Seers use Tyranny? And here is our topic - If that would be the case - What would be those 'Forces of Equality'? Maybe Atlantean Royality that build Watchtowers?

      The FC politics are an outgrowth of their three core tenets which are already based on a Mystery.

      Humanity is Magical- The Mystery the FC is built on. A phenomenon that appears to allow humanity to influence the Supernal, upending the idea that hierarchies are the natural order of existence with everything flowing down from the Supernal.

      Destroy the Followers of the Lie- The architects of the Lie are the Exarchs, and the Exarchs are an hierarchical structure of reality that refutes the the Mystery of Humanity is Magical that the FC believe in. Obviously their followers are not only working to destroy that sacred Mystery, but are also reflections of the Exarchs themselves because of Platonism, and so must be destroyed in order to increase the chances of studying magical humanity. Incidentally, they’re also warping the Supernal because the FC believe in that kind of bottom -> up influence.

      Democracy Seeks Truth- If the above two points are true, reality doesn’t just flow down from the Supernal because humanity is magical, and those who seek to create hierarchies to destroy that Mystery, then as a reaction the opposite of hierarchies (Democracy) must be maintained to study, manipulate and grow the magical humanity Mystery.

      If you just chuck something into the setting that acts as a counterweight to the Exarchs by being a force for democracy that isn’t the Free Council itself you’ll just be watering down the Free Council st best, or warping then into mirrorhouse Seers at worst.

          Looking over Tenets of future Free Council maybe real answer on the topic of main chronicle Mystery.

          Humanity is Magical - It's not doing many things to the revolutionary and democratic impulses of Nameless War beside 'we all are Nameless and working <<humans>> magic, overthrowing Awakened high-class'. So I will move past this in rest of deliberations.

          Democracy Seeks Truth - It's nice slogan of future Free Council - But's what does it really means, as Mystery? Because, nowadays, it looks like political advise: 'And mage governing should be democratic, to unfolding the Truth'. And there is nothing... well, 'mysterious' about it. However, if we reverse and change this statement ( like in Boolean algebra ) we get that 'Truth Points to Democracy'. And this means that some elements in MtAw setting should support Democracy as force in universe. Solving what would be those 'Forces Pointing to Democracy' would be really answer on the topic's Mystery behind creation of Free Council - at least, from narrative point.

          Going further, if Truth Points to Democracy - so ( by Boolean algebra ) Hierarchies are Pointing to Lie. It's not far from the Destroy the Followers of the Lie that is next official tenet of once created Council. So fixing on statement Hierarchies are Pointing to Lie should connect the plot of chronicles to creation of two of three Free Council tenets.

          Okay, but HOW to implement 'Hierarchies are Pointing to Lie' in living, action game? And for that matter - How can you point that something is 'Pointing to Lie' in Mage, in the first place? MtAw is not 'Matrix' universe to say 'they are machine, they are fake'.

              Humanity is Magical is a (And the -only-) Mystery (Of the FC), and you're failing to engage with it as such. The two other tenets that follow from it are political motivations that cause them to protect that first tenet, which is the Mystery they obsess over.

              You would be better served to focus on creating elements in your setting that point to Humanity is Magical as an overarching theme. Democracy vs. Heirarchy(The Lie) should be the lens you use to paint the picture of how the nascent FC view the conflict. Hierarchies are bad because they crush the idea that Humanity is Magical. Democracy is good because it's the opposite of how Heirarchies work, and so support Humanity is Magical.

              From the FC position it's obvious that Hierarchies point to the Lie. All other Mages believe that reality is a Platonic relationship, the Supernal shape the Fallen, but the FC has observed that the Fallen is able to shape the Supernal, which is the complete and polar opposite. The group that most reverently adheres to this ideology are the Seers, and they are also the most strictly hierarchical faction of Mages. Couple that with the fact that almost every Mage (Including Seers) believes that the Exarchs created the Lie in the first place means that the true potential of Humanity is Magical cannot be realized while servants of the Lie continue to work in the Fallen twisting the Supernal, and Hierarchies must be overthrown via Democracy because the architects of Hierarchies are the Exarchs.

The laws of physics as modern science knows only work because a bunch of mages (who'll get pissed if you call them that) worked damn hard to convince the Sleeper masses that they do work. Which means that in the past, you could have things like dragons and giants walking around Europe, square-cube law be damned.

                                                  However, the Consensus isn't universal across the globe. Because truth is subjective, local beliefs shape local reality. Hence, Reality Zones. You can expect things straight out of science fiction work in a closed lab of hyper-advanced science (read; another form of magic), which will fail to work if taken out to the streets. You can expect witchcraft to work in those secluded towns far, far from cities where people's culture and lifestyles haven't changed much from the last century or two, but that witch becomes impotent in the big city.

Their theory of human culture conjuring new Supernal symbols is exactly that; a theory. Of course, this should be isolated incidents in Awakening, much like how Ansho only happen in Tokyo and the Astral intrusions of Los Angeles, unlike in Ascension where the entire world works that way. What could be causing the Supernal World to become to malleable? Some sort of Prime magic? An obscure form of Emanation? A Bound stirring from its slumber?
