                                  As a Yantra? Probably not. While certain weird Legacies and Nameless Orders might facilitate it, it they're basically just doing a hyper focused version of what a particular cabal in the Free Council would be pursuing as their iteration of Techné, and denying themselves a whole bunch of resources that comes with it to boot. The Diamond Orders would see the hardcore implementation of such as getting in the way of the core elements that actually matter, and Nameless Orders and Legacies that wouldn't fall neatly into the a Free Council-esque organization would likely have similar feelings.

                                  It takes a certain kind of engagement with Algorithms and the Scientific Method to really wring the Supernal resonance out of it, and the number or organizations outside of the Free Council invested enough in getting that are increasingly small-and it's not the sort of thing you're going to get without some organizational oomph into digging into the occult value such. Mechanically speaking, Techné also remains the best way of articulating that sort of benefit, with other means of expressing it being ill-fit or odd angled.

                                  So, as a nutshell, you wouldn't as an individual, most organizations outside of the Free Council who would provide it are the sort of organizations that, logistically if not ideologically, have more to gain by joining the Free Council than not (which is not to say you couldn't, you would just need to have a good reason the organization on the whole presents a strong enough case to not avail themselves of limited edition lightsabers and airline tickets), and Techné outside of tailored Legacy Attainments is going to be the main model for it.

                                  EDIT: As a rough way of modelling this, you're effectively looking for the effects of Techné without being in the Free Council, unless again you really wanna dig deep and weird and make this a Legacy, which is a different subject. In the Free Council, You can get Techné at Status 1. In a Nameless Order that facilitates this thing, you would get the hyper-focused version of this at Status 3 at best, and by yourself you would need to do the equivalent work of attaining Status 5. If the Nameless Order or solitaire in question follow a train of thought more akin to the Diamond or the Seers, add 1 to those counts.

                                  Now there may be reasons to go ahead and do this, but you have to have a real knowledge of why that is.

                                  The resources and refinements on understanding that come with the (collected) centuries of experience that is made easy access to the Orders, even the Free Council, is a real and tangible benefit. It's not exactly something you can just ignore.

                                  EDIT: And yes, if you wanted to make it so any implementation of either or those worked as a Dedicated Tool, you would need Broad Dedication. If you want it as just a Yantra, Techné or a Techné-like is needed.

                                      Originally posted by Verge View Post
                                      I think you have it backwards. Dedicated tools may be used as Order tools,
                                      To clarify: Dedicated Tools can be used as Order Tools, if they also fit as an Order Tool. Just being a Dedicated Tool does not qualify as an Order Tool. On the other hand all Order Tools (that are physical items) can be dedicated to become Dedicated Tools. If you have a more abstract Order Tool (such as a practice gained through Techné) then you need Broad Dedication.

                                      Originally posted by Verge View Post
                                      a soul stone's creator may use it as a dedicated tool,
                                      Yes. In addition to using your own soul stone, you can always dedicate someone else's soul stone as your Dedicated Tool without additional Merits.

                                      Originally posted by Verge View Post
                                      A mage could replace a destroyed dedicated tool with almost anything, but from Awakening until its destruction, this tool is typical of the Path. And that's no reason not to allow it, if the symbolism is still apt (for example, computer networking is a powerful means of conquering space and mind, and us its own Hell), so long as the material aspect is kept (perhaps a USB security key).
                                      As long as the material aspect can be said to be a part of the larger Yantra (i.e. USB security key is actually used and not just left dangling from a key chain while you work an unrelated computer network) then you should be able dedicate it just fine. If you don't have a material component, then Broad Dedication is needed.


                                      Originally posted by lbeaumanior View Post
                                      Ok, but back at the original question, can I use Algorithms or the Scientific Method (or any other abstraction, as long it is within the frame of "...every Libertine may use complex mechanisms and systems as Order Tools" ) without the Techné merit? Or the Broad Dedication?
                                      You asked a few different questions all nested together so let's break it down a bit.

                                      First of all, does either fit the description of the Free Council Order Tool?
                                      This is up for interpretation and I'm not comfortable ruling one way or another. Does the text refer to systems as in organised procedures (like the scientific method), systems as in complex structures (like algorithms), or both? I can't say.

                                      If either is deemed not to fit the regular Order Tool description, can you still use them through the Techné Merit?
                                      Techné is a defined practice, and as such algorithms are immediately disqualified. You could instead use programming as Techné. The scientific method is definitely a type of practice, but it's not its own field, if that makes sense. I would allow it if a player really wanted it, but generally say no.

                                      If they are viable Order Tool Yantras (either as regular Free Council Order Tools or Techné Order Tools), do you need Broad Dedication in order to dedicate them?
                                      Yes. Neither is an item, and being an item is the only explicit limit on what can be a Dedicated Tool.

                                                      "Algorithms" is too broad a category to be a meaningful symbol in the specific case very often. It's just this side of asking for "casting" as a tool. The problem isn't that ephemeral things shouldn't be tools, it's that a process for finding or generating symbols should not be confused with being the effective symbols.

                                                      If you allow "algorithms" then they should only apply to situations where the selection of or operation of algorithms in general are part of the action of the spell itself. That is: if you allow such a thing, no declaring a random number generator algorithm as part of casting a fate spell and turning around and hitting a life spell with an exponential growth equation, as neither of those are algorithmic processes of themselves. The specific example algorithms might apply just dandy, but neither is what was selected; If algorithms are the tool then algorithms as a concept must be the symbol that applies.... in precisely the same way one doesn't take "wands" as a dedicated tool and get all wands, but rather a specific wand.

                                                      That said, this attempt to stretch the rules would be a perfectly reasonable research path for a character and a great justification for both of the mentioned tool merits and a nifty basis for a custom legacy.
