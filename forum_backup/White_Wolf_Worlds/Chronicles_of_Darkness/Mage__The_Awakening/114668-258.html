    Hi guys, I have a whole bunch of mage questions. It’s my first mage game and it’s a really dense book so I’m just trying to figure this all out and it’s way more challenging than I thought. There are a bunch so sorry in advance. Didn't want to make a whole thread about it.

    I have a couple questions on familiars.

    Can one communicate telepathically with a familiar? Do you have to talk out loud to it? That seems very inconvenient and conspicuous, but I don’t see anything in either the merit or the condition that suggests otherwise. Can you have a familiar without possessing its respective rank 2 attainment? Mind’s Eye for Goetia, Eyes of the Dead for ghosts, etc.

    If you can’t communicate with a familiar telepathically without a spell, would said spell need to be a combined spell, using Telepathy (Mind 2) with Speak with the Dead (Death 1) for example? Or could one use Creative Thaumaturgy with just the corresponding Arcana? (Needing only spirit for a spirit familiar, etc.) And with what Practice, Ruling?

    I have a couple questions on Path/Order tools.

    The book says each path has 5 path tools. Do these tools have to be from the materials listed in the sidebar below as well as one of the items listed? Can it be one or the other?

    And what counts as a rod? Could a measuring ruler count as a rod? A pen? A stick? An instrument vaguely shaped like one, like a chisel or a ice pick? Could I use a lead pipe as a Moros to count as either a weapon or a rod as the need suits me?

    Second. Is the bonus from Perfected Metals (from the perfected metallurgy preview) in addition to the bonus from being a regular tool? For example, let’s say I have a chalice made of silver, and I perfect the silver into lunargent using the Hone the Perfected form spell.

    According to the book:
    Effect: Each item used as a Yantra adds +1 die to the spellcasting pool.
    Cups or other drinking vessels can invoke healing, intuition, perceptual magic, and gathering together. Drinking from a shared cup is a common way to spread a spell among a group.

    And according to the preview:
    Lunargent offers a +2 tool Yantra for spells associated with seeing such as other places, other times, and across the Gauntlet. Additionally, it reflects things in Twilight.

    So if I’m casting Exorcist’s Eye, a Spirit spell with increased scale so that myself and my whole cabal can peer across the Gauntlet, do I get the higher of the two bonuses (+2 from lunargent), the combined bonuses of the item (+1 from Cup, +2 from Lunargent for a total of +3) or would I need to count them each as a separate yantra for a +1 and +2, separately?

    Can you use different parts of the same item (A knife with a gem encrusted handle, for example?) to count toward the same tool?

    Last one on Twilight
    There is a Death spell that allows you to pull items from twilight, and then one that allows you to shape ephemera, can you combine those to shape say…. A key for the door? (provided you already know what the key needs to look like because of that one matter spell). What are you pulling from? Is there just a twilight version of everything in the real world and you can just pull a desk from twilight and shape it into a key? I know what the shadow looks like, but what do Mind and Death attuned twilight look like? What’s there? I know it’s a state and not a place, but I’m not sure I understand what I see when I peer into Twilight when I look, aside from Ghosts and Goetia. Does it just look like black space? Is it just ghosts superimposed over the room I'm in? Does Shape Ephemera refer specifically to ghosts, what other ephemera is there?

    I’m also not sure I understand Artifacts, Imbued items (especially) or Enhanced items (or the different purposes for each), but I probably won’t be using them so I’m not going to even bother.

    Thanks for your help, guys.

                                Amravanti

                                Familiars: You can telepathically communicate with your familiar, share senses with them, and they're solid/visible to you all as a default part of the benefit of having one. This comes from the fact that they share a Connected Sympathetic bond with you which makes them metaphysically a part of you.

                                Path/Order Tools: In my games we assume that a rod is a thing you can point with that's made out of the appropriate Path material. So yeah, a lead pipe would work just fine, and I'd probably let it double as a mace as well.

                                When presented with the Yantra bonus of perfected materials you take the higher value, so with your lunargent example you'd take just the +2.

                                Twilight: Can be cluttered with whatever you want, or the lack thereof. Things that are suddenly destroyed often also leave "ghosts" behind, non-sentient ephemeral versions of their former selves. If an Avernian gate opens in the same area as them I assume they'd be sucked into the Underworld, but I don't know. From inside Twilight the regular world looks pretty much unchanged, but blurry and out of focus, so from outside Twilight looking in I would assume that the world looks pretty much unchanged but with an overlay of ghosts or goetia.

                                You can in fact pull ephemera (If it's available, from ghosts or other sources) from Twilight and shape it into useful objects like a key. I'm personally not sure if you're supposed to be able to do that with ectoplasm though, which is basically ghost snot slathered on shit so they can Manifest.

                                Artifacts/Imbued Items: I'm not sure anyone knows how these are actually supposed to work, the rules appear to be incomplete. They'll probably get back to them in SoS.
