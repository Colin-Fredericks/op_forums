                                                            Lower Depths - I know there is an official/ish statement that the Inferno is considered part of the Lower Depths, but this has felt forced to me, here is why (Apology for the word wall).

                                                            The Lower Depths are supposed to be thin worlds - empty worlds. Often Lacking Arcana in whole cloth.
                                                            While Inferno is definitely a broken realm, and lacks Virtue, the mechanics for its denizens are different from the two Lower Depths natives described in Mage supplements, which have nearly identical mechanics.

                                                            Rather I place Inferno as a Verge. The entities of Thought must be cloaked in a True Name to sustain themselves. And are fed by Acts of Vice. (This aspect of feeding, does touch upon feeding on a soul just more indirectly - but mechanically feeds upon Willpower).

                                                            The Lower Depths - Note from DavidB, writer of Seers of the Throne, the Tutor:
                                                            "Oh, and as the guy who inflicted the description of the Tutor on the world, this is what I had in mind...

                                                            The Lower Depths, as I think of them, are as far from the Fallen World as the Fallen World is from the Supernal. They're not Abyssal - they're *empty*. A Thin Place, where strange things move through their grey void of a world taking in what few scraps of Mana they need to survive.

                                                            The Tutor is undefined (to the point where you can't really perceive it) because it's not meant to be in this reality. It can't move by itself, or free itself from its half-materialized state because our world is just too *real* for it. It's functionally immortal because its home world (if its home can be said to be a world at all) is so far from Arcadia that Time doesn't exist.

                                                            Imagine a world where the Ten Arcana don't exist - no Forces, no Death, no Spirit, no Life and so on - or are present in only tiny amounts due to the Supernal being so very far away. If it's hard to conceptualize - good. If a mage went there, they'd be like a Supernal being coming to the Fallen. That's what I was shooting for."
                                                            /END Old DavidB QUOTE

                                                            - Bunch of references about the Lower Depths...

                                                            Circumstantial evidence on WHERE the Lower Depths reside (Greek – Tartarus is below Hades' underworld:
                                                            In Summoners it is stated that “[Chthonians] allegedly know at least a little about the doorways to the Lower Depths situated in the deepest parts of the Underworld”. - Summoners, p. 46 (i.e. that in in the deepest portions of the Underworld one can reach the Lower Depths). In Seers of the Throne, p. 217, “The Ministry now believes that the Tutor is a being from the Lower Depths, the distant and mysterious realm believed to be as far from the Fallen World as the Fallen World is from the Supernal.” In Tome of the Watchtowers (p. 76) a story of the Breaking of the Celestial Ladder details an army of entities from the Lower Depths emerging into the world, and eventually later barred from the Fallen World by a bone portal in the Underworld.

                                                            Indications on WHAT/WHO dwells in the Lower Depths (Greek – Defeated titans, cyclopes, offensive mortals and their native jailers):
                                                            In Summoner's, p. 62, it is suggested weakly, “Some say demons — of the genuine 'demons from Hell' variety — dwell within the Lower Depths, believed to exist beneath even the Underworld“. Further strongly suggested, “Other Warlocks look [to Pandemonium] for information on 'true' Demons, the inhabitants of Lower Depths that, unlike the Demons of the Kingdom of Nightmares, feed on human vice and depravity.”
                                                            In Summoners p. 63 - “these beings [From the Lower Depths] are inimical to life. They despise all things with an intensity that rivals that of the creatures of the Abyss”. This description is in fact contrary to the treatment the infernal demons receive in the book Inferno – those demons are, in fact, lovers of Vice, and a dead world would remove their most prolific sustenance.

                                                            Paradoxically, the mortal dead, must free themselves from their fetters/passions/vices to descend to the deepest portions of the underworld. Is this why the Lower Depths were such a good prison? Or another contradiction?

                                                            If the Demons and Angels of Inferno dwell within the Lower Depths, then WOD: Inferno's details about the Abyssal and Hell conflict applies: “The Abyss seeks a cosmos without meaning or form—at least not in the way humans understand it—but Hell relies upon the world, loves it even. The world is a furnace of sin, and demons warm themselves at it.”

                                                            This is the difference then between Abyssal entities and Demons. Abyssal entities are nihilists/corrupters if substance always, Demons want a total embrasure of Vice in all its forms, and want it to go on forever, not nihilism, but eternal degradation of the self.

                                                            Two Explicit Mage Examples: – Tutor, Seer's of the Throne, p. 217-8, “It is a thaumivore, but it has another appetite as well: it eats the human sense of self.” It does not manifest fully in the Material or Twilight, its form is heavily distorted.
                                                            Decay, Summoner's p. 62, Ages/decays a space in seconds (Perhaps is converting Structure into Mana?), it speaks through the soul, not the mind. Acts as Acamoth and wants returned to its home. Has no physical form only its effects upon a space indicates its presence.

                                                            Neither example consumes based upon acts of Vice, unless Decay is a demon of Pride and Tutor is a demon of Gluttony. This really infers a third class of imported entities, the condemned titans and cyclopes of Greek mythology, perhaps? Their manifestations are similar to low ranking Demons, but their range of power is much higher. One can alter the structure of a victim's soul, the other can grant powers.

                                                            In WOD Virtues and Vice help one recover Willpower. How does this help an entity survive the Lower Depths?

                                                            Demons and Angels (As described in Inferno) engage in extreme versions of their Virtues and Vices.
                                                            They require extreme amounts of Essence, or a conduit to channel Essence to their forms.

                                                            So mechanically and thematically I see the Lower Depths too worn out and alien to be the Inferno. Not saying it is not, just not how I see it, nor play it.
