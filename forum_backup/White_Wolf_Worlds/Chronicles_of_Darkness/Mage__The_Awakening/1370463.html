    Paradox Conditions, Questions

    02-25-2020, 06:51 PM
    Two Questions
    ​1.The example conditions offer scouring of a condition as resolution, seemingly contradicting the text. What is your interpretation?
    2. Why does it seem that Paradox conditions affect the wise for longer than the unwise? This seems counter intuitive. What reasoning exists behind this?

    So I'm able to follow the paradox system pretty well except the Paradox Conditions. The text appears to be contradictory.
    My understanding is as follows.
    You can gain a paradox condition by 1) Dramatic failure on a spell-casting roll where paradox was invoked. 2) By spending reach on from a successful uncontained paradox 3) Failing to remove all successes from a contained paradox.
    So far simple.
    Paradox Conditions are all negative effects.
    Paradox conditions can resolved by specific actions or letting them lapse. Until resolved the condition has negative effects that cant be gotten around with magic.

    The length of time until lapse is given with higher wisdom mages having more time to resolve the condition, but also suffering from it longer.

    Resolving it gives a normal arcane beat.
    Letting it lapse gives an arcane beat but infects the character. They suffer a +1 paradox die until the lapsed condition is scoured for 1 resistant lethal damage.

    But then the examples given are unclear.

      Originally posted by Manifoldpersona View Post
      Two Questions
      ​1.The example conditions offer scouring of a condition as resolution, seemingly contradicting the text. What is your interpretation?
      Scouring doesn't resolve the Condition but is necessary to get rid of the Paradox boost from letting it settle in.
      2. Why does it seem that Paradox conditions affect the wise for longer than the unwise? This seems counter intuitive. What reasoning exists behind this?
      Longer time to resolve it without letting it settle in.

      Given the general thrust of Paradox as a badge of honor among some stripes of mage, it may be wise to interpret them as Persistent Conditions with a one-time Beat criterion of letting it settle into your Pattern and give you the +1 Paradox die and Resolution criteria of "[criterion specific to the Condition], or Scour it out of your Pattern after the interval determined by your Wisdom."

              I have a few I wrote for the forums, but most of the time I make them up on the fly. If you enjoy these I can try to track down others I've written.

              All of the Conditions are meant to have an implied "or you scour it or the condition lapses."

              Ferrous Snowfall - Abyssal Environmental Tilt (3 reach)

              The snow falling takes on a pinkish hue and smells and feels like some combination of iron and rust. Level 2 Extreme Environment. If a player attempts to open his or her eyes without protective eyewear or other covering they suffer 1 bashing damage and the blinded tilt for the duration of the scene. Players may roll Resolve+Stamina to open their eyes for a turn but will suffer the 1 bashing damage each time they do so.

              Blasphemed Ground - Abyssal Environmental Tilt (3 reach)

              Something about this place just feels wrong. In reality the local area has become antithetical to Truth. Everyone in the area is affected by the Distrustful condition.

              Distrustful: the character is prone to disbelieving just about anything, even his closest friends. All attempts to communicate information with him require a Wits+Persuasion roll contested by the character's Composure+Resolve.
              Resolution: the character fails his wits+composure roll five times, or is otherwise unmistakably convinced he is being delusional.
              Beat: The character acts against his own interests because he is convinced a friend is lying to him

              Hungry Flames - Abyssal Environmental Tilt (3 reach)

              Flames in the area cannot be extinguished by conventional means. Their size cannot be reduced by one per turn using a hose or fire extinguisher. Instead, situations that would normally completely extinguish a fire (oxygen being removed or being submersed in water) will reduce the size of the flame by one per turn.

              Lightning Rod - Paradox Condition (1 reach)

              Character is affected by the Lightning Rod tilt or condition:

              Lightning Rod (Tilt): The ST rolls a die in secret at the start of each turn. Once the accumulated successes on the rolls exceed ten, the character is struck by the nearest electricity source.
              Lightning Rod (Condition): The ST rolls a single die once per scene. If the roll succeeds, the character is struck by an electricity source, at the ST's discretion.
              Resolution: the character accumulates ten bashing damage worth of electrical damage

              Condition best applied to Forces-wielding mages in a thunderstorm

              Tu quoque - Paradox Condition (1 reach)

              The character suffers from a one-directional Shared Fate with the person physically closest to them. This effect functions as the Fate 3 spell Shared Fate with the +1 Reach, except that the mage receives the shared fate of whomever is closest to them.
              Resolution: The link is broken by magical means or the duration expires
              Beat: The character harms this person (and therefore himself)

              Memory hole - Paradox Condition (1 reach)

              The mage suffers from the Amnesia condition but only pertaining to events that happened during this scene.
              Resolution: The memories are restored, by magical or nonmagical means. This may constitute a breaking point.
              Beat: Something problematic arises due to the lost memories

              Unhinged Fortune - Abyssal Environmental Tilt (3 reach)

              All failures in the area become dramatic failures.

              A Curious Gathering - Spell Gone Awry or Abyssal Environmental Tilt (2 or 3 reach)

              The amount of creatures in the area spikes by an order of magnitude. There are suddenly ten times as many birds, rodents, insects, or other wildlife in the area than there were previously. Their attention is fixated on the mage. They don't behave unnaturally, but they could easily be provoked...

              Spirit Caucus - Spell Gone Awry or Abyssal Environmental Tilt (2 or 3 reach)

              As with the above, only instead of creatures, spirits of lesser and medium rank gather around in increasing numbers.

              Water Water Everywhere - Abyssal Environmental Tilt (3 reach)

              Water in the area becomes nonpotable, it evaporates instantly when it comes into contact with human flesh. This causes 1 bashing damage per turn of contact. Water also gains superfluid properties: creeping up surfaces in seeming defiance of gravity.

              Skipping clock - Abyssal Environmental Tilt (3 reach)

              Time skips in the area for seconds at a time at random intervals. All recollection of the intervening seconds are lost. This effect makes extended actions all-but impossible. All bonuses to initiative are void.

              Pallor mortis - Paradox Condition (1 reach)

              The character is affected by the condition Corpselike for the duration of the paradox.

              Corpselike: All social rolls suffer a -2 modifier, and all Impressions suffer by one level.
              Resolution: The mage successfully heals someone else with a life spell
              Beat: The condition significantly hinders the character from achieving some social goal

              Algor Mortis - Abyssal Environmental Tilt (3 reach)

              Heat doesn't last long in this area. Heat sources seem to provide no real warmth or comfort. Inflicts a level 1 extreme environment. If relevant may also inflict the Icy tilt.

              Twisted path - Paradox Condition (1 reach)

              Per the spell Lying Maps, the mage is convinced the correct route to his objective is in some wrong, random direction. Attempts to navigate to the correct location, even with GPS, are reduced to a chance die.
              Resolution: The mage successfully arrives at the intended location
