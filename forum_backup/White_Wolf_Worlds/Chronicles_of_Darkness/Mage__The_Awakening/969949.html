                              Funnily enough, I'm planning on using something like Abyssal Claimed in a game I'm running tonight. Inspired by various media, I'm doing a "cut off island" that the players group can explore thanks to a friendly Walker in the Mists (the island is enshrouded in a fog which people become lost in and can't penetrate but the walker in mists can pass through). Those that died are then claimed by minor abyssal spirits that then rise and continue slaughtering the islands inhabitants. The reason I'm using Abyssal Claimed is so that, as the game goes on, the enemies the players encounter will become increasingly stranger and dangerous.

                                  Abyssal creatures are supposed to invert rules systems and have contradictions in them. Someone had a good example of an Abyssal spirit of betrayal whose ban was to never betray anyone. So I would argue for something radically different than a normal claimed. There are a lot of ephemeral entities in the Chronicles of Darkness. Sadly too many of them are described as having "Alien intelligences" which "aren't comprehensible to humans", they are often "Single Minded" etc. It kind of just seems like the same thing after awhile.

                                  For any abyssal claiming, it would have to be compared as somehow worse than the many many terrible claimings (and similar things) that could happen in the Chronicles of Darkness. Just spirits alone have huge potential to be horrifying. Beasts, Geists, Demons, Angels, Inferno Demons, Strix, Exiles, Ghosts etc. all have these creepy aspects of body stealing, possession, and being consumed by a supernatural force. I think the abyss, due to its "anti-reality" nature, should have something different than this very large set of phenomena.

                                  My idea:

                                  When an Abyssal spirit claims a human, the abyssal spirit loses its identity and empowers the possessed to become a Proximus-type lesser template capable of various Abyssal talents. The personality of the human will change radically, but it is ultimately them in control.

                                  The idea is that unlike normal spirits, which are beyond selfish, an abyssal spirit is altruistically giving up its identity and will to help spread the abyss through its version of the claiming manifestation.
