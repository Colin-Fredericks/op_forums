                Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post
                So if I get this correctly, you still require something that symbolizes the past of the thing you're trying to reach beyond just the thing itself? I'm sorry, I'm terrible without concrete examples.
                Pretty much. Pieces of a target can generally be taken as good for getting back to the point of separation, as can lost personal effects — if you kicked off a shoe and lost a fingertip running from a rogue chainsaw-devil, the shoe and the fingertip could both be used to return to the scene of the pursuit. Mementoes of significant events likewise serve as markers of a change, whether they're a piece of the rope from an outlaw's hanging or a party hat from a young relative's eighth birthday, and thus can bring you into closer alignment with those spans of time.

                You could even use a person who attended an event as a Sympathy Yantra for scrutinizing the past of the location where it took place, probably, but that's a markedly different thing from using them as an Instrument for enhancing the view of their own past.

                                        Forces governs the force of gravity, but mass is a property of matter and should fall under Matter. Weight is the result of gravity acting upon mass so both Forces and Matter can change weight, but does it in different ways.

                                        Since Shrink and Grow doesn't specify I'd assume mass changes with size so that density is unaffected. Otherwise I think the language would mention volume instead of just Size. I can also add that the trait Size seems to loosely correspond to weight in addition to volume when reading other rules.
