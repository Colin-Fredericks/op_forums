    Help with Rules Questions -- been battling some players about these

    03-01-2023, 04:37 PM
    I need some help with some rules questions.

    We're running a relatively high power mage game. Players are single arcana masters with 5 gnosis. They have decent stats, but not crazy. Some players min-maxed, some didn't. You know how it goes.

    A lot of things seem unclear. Could the community help me understand some things?

    1. Raising stats with magic
    There are a number of rank 3 spells that allow a mage to raise their skills or attributes. As I understand, these are hard capped at 10. They soft cap at 5 first, which can be overcome with a reach and a mana, and then duration increased to a year for a Mage to have 10 dexterity and say 10 firearms and athletics for the year, increasing their base defense to 15 or 20, and their firearms rolls to 20+ base. This isn't even hard.
    5 gnosis + 5 arcana
    Yantras
    rote 6
    shadow name 3
    high speech 2
    location 2
    ritual 5

    28 dice pool plus Fate 2 exceptional luck to add 10 more, for a 38 dice pool to cast. Make potency the primary so it starts at 5, and then -10 for 5 more potency, and a couple minuses for duration and then split it between two stats for +5 (or more) to each.

    Now the issue. I mentioned the cap at 10. This comes from interpretations I've seen here and the stat cap in the book. The spells are worded as such that they raise the stats up, simulating the character buying them with xp, so would hit the same caps that buying via xp would hit.

    The player is saying that Vigor from vampire adds to strength in the same way that the spell honing the form does, and isn't capped at 10, as a blood potency 6 vampire can have a 6 strength + 5 vigor for an 11. They are being pretty rude about the 10 cap being one rule for mage, and one for everyone else.

    What is your interpretation here? Should honing the form be able to give a character a 15 natural strength indefinitely?

    2. Rote yantras
    In the section it says it is capped at 5, but the Order specialty then adds a die. So if a mage has say 5 in Intimidation, and is an Adamantine Arrow, do they add 6 to their rotes from the mudra yantra?

    3. Exceptional Luck
    The Acanthus is using this to add 14 to their skill rolls, and literally double all their starting spell casting dice pools. This seems...insane. How do you manage this at your game? Do you care or does it affect your game?

    4. Charmed Condition
    If it isn't clear, the Acanthus is pushing the boundaries as much as possible.
    So there is a spell that can grant the Charmed condition. For 3 reaches, it can be cast reflexively. So is there anything that stops him from simply casting it anytime something tries to hit him, you know, past the 19 defense and whatever armor, to grant the Charmed condition every time and then resolving it so the attacks never do more than 1 damage?

    I feel like the distinction between tilts and conditions would limit this to like once per combat scene. I know there will be an argument about why it cant constantly be used, so wanted to understand what others thought.

    5. Paradox on long duration spells
    I read somewhere that when witnesses are present, at the end of the scene or chapter the spell would need to check for paradox if it originally had over reach. I can't find it now. Does anyone know anything about this?


    6. Dissonance and long duration spells
    How do you do Dissonance with 10+ potency over reach spells? if a mage has a huge spell on them with an indefinite duration, do you consider it apparent at casual observance, or only when they do something bonkers?

    My thought is that high potency, over reached spells with indefinite duration 'unravel' when around sleepers naturally, and so if they go out in public just loaded with insane spells, the presence of sleepers begins to unravel their spells. This is something I'm going to constantly be rolling for, but more of a storyteller effect where they can't just super cast in their demense and then count on uber spells to always be up all the time.

    7. Aimed spells and multiple targets
    From my understanding, when a spell is cast with touch distance, it can then be fired at a target using Gnosis + Athletics. If the spell takes the multi target factor, then you just roll full Gnosis + Athletics for each target; you do not take a multi-target penalty. I feel this is true, as the multitarget penalty is baked into the autofire rules, and isn't based on normal attacks.

    8. Armor
    I have players with kevlar jackets and reinforced clothing coats, for 2/3 and 1/0 armor ratings. Do you think these should stack? What about using magic to make the coat a 9/0 armor and then stacking it with Mage Armor?

    This isn't even difficult. A starting mage can get a dice pool to cast of
    3 arcana
    3 gnosis
    3 shadow name
    6 rote yantra
    5 ritual cast
    8 exceptional luck

    20 without Fate, 28 with
    20 with potency primary, one reach for advanced duration, - 14 for 7 more potency, willpower point, gives 7 dice to cast a month long 10 potency spell.

    9. Can't Raise the stat, add to it!
    If number 1 is so, would a creative thaumaturgy spell that added dice to strength dice pools stack with it?
    Say, 1 month duration, and +_9 to strength pools, so the mage rolls 10 strength + 10 brawl + 9 added strength dice + dble specialty 2 = 31 dice to swing a punch? All with 3 life and 3 mind total
    Does this seem right to you?


    ​10. Shadow Name
    In the Shadow Name, it states and gives an example of say Thor as a Shadow Name. Then when the mage does things related to the idea of Thor, say lightning and thunder powers, increasing strength to hit stuff with a hammer, jumping really high, they can use their Shadow Name as a Yantra. Now lets say Thor wants to use Fate 2 to cast bigger spells; would they be able to use Shadow Name as a Yantra?
    How do you then use Cabal Theme in relation to this? Do your players just always have a +4 yantra at their disposal?

    11. Nimbus
    This one is rough. So I saw the clarification that it doesn’t affect the mage who is having the Nimbus effect. Does Nimbus then apply to everyone in the vicinity, friend or foe, if say it has a 9 potency? Does this mean a mage with -1 resolve, and +2 Firearms would give their foes +2 firearms and then what, 9 again on firearms checks?
    This happens every time a mage casts? Does this cause any issues at your table with slowing things down?

    12. Spells Off Screen
    If a player spends time say, each week, to cast a big ol spell off screen, do you require a certain number of dice to make the spell work? Do you offer exceptional successes at a certain breaking point? Say 9 for praxis and 15 for regular spells?

    13. I’ll have more, I’m certain of it.

    ​

      Exceptional luck grants a boon.

      The rules for Boons explicitly say : "Grant a dice bonus equal to Potency on certain actions (usually a single Skill) for a number of rolls equal to the Potency of the spell during its Duration. Multiple boons cannot grant their bonus to the same action;​"

      Spellcasting is not a skill.

      As others have said, granting 9-again on a spellcasting roll is allowed for a boon. But +[potency] dice pool or a beneficial condition is not.

      Most people cap rote bonus at +5, though by the RAW i can see the extra from rote specialization adding an extra die.

      Shadow name is not applicable to all spells, as a 3 dot Merit it should be a well developed identity which has clearly limits.

      (See page 292 for more info on shadow names.)

      High speech is always out loud and takes an extra turn (not great at combat speed). It is also arguable whether this would be consider obviously magical if sleepers are watching.

      Location +2 requires a Demense/Verge. Both only apply to a limited set of spells. Also a Demense is limited to the mage's Sanctum.

      The limits for the Yantra bonus begin with utilizing the ruling arcana of a verge, but further "A Verge or Demesne’s Yantra bonus is limited by its semiotic associations, as is usual for Yantras. A Stygian Verge that’s filled with metal but no aspects of Death won’t help a mage bind ghosts, but will help him create strange alloys"

      So not all spells of an associated ruling Arcana will qualify.

      For further info on the uses of demense see page 119/120, where it discusses Order based Demense decorations, possibly further limiting the application.


      Ritual +5 means 6 ritual durations, there are some good rules for taking breaks from rituals or dealing distractions in SoS. At Gnosis 5 this is only 3 hours, but distractions can still occur, especially if Fate happens to conspire against them.

      At Gnosis 3 this is 6 hours. Plus any preparation time, materials needed, narrative description of how the ritual focus helps them build the imago. This may be the specifics of how the environment is decorated, what the order taught mudras are practiced, or how the character is performing their shadow name.

      Make the players put in the cognitive work to give your game flavour or you are just rolling dice (roll playing rather than role playing).

      @5. As others have pointed out, paradox is occasionally used casually to refer to all the effects of the lie (Dissonance and Quiessence). The only place the paradox rules are used for long term spells is for unsafely relinquished spells, but this isn't an effect of the lie. It just reuses the paradox rules for altering how spells behave to save word count. I love it as a mystery particular is an NPC dies (or the players kill someone) all their spells will be unsafely relinquished - and potentially weeks or months later may start changing subjects, scale, etc. - this can change a spell to advanced scale if you wanted, or impose a condition of the subject... It could be a subtle mystery and investigation revealing that it was the player's own fault for not cleaning up after their kill could be a nice story point.

      (Also note, i highly recommend unsafe relinquished spells as a way to reduce spell control. It is cheap, and the long term effects generated are interesting narratively, trading immediate power -more spell control - for long term risk/drama is entirely in theme with mage).

      @6. Indefinite duration spells will not be that long once dissonance drops their duration one step (which may be pretty fast unless it is a duration primary spell).

      @10. If the cabal theme is based around defending the local Consillium, they get the bonus for any actions directly involved in this theme, but not when leaving the consillium and defending someone else, or attacking the Seers. The Cabal theme should be designed to fit with their purpose as a cabal and thus be applicable most of the time for the main work the cabal does. But likely doesn't apply for a lot of side projects.

      @11 Nimbus tilt can be caused by casting a spell or intentionally glaring their Nimbus. To be affected other characters need to see the Nimbus, which means active mage sight for spell casting (which is when a character can attempt to use the counter spell attainment) or when visible to even without mage sight if you deliberately flare it.

      (Aside, i should use these deliberate Nimbus flares more often, even without using the tilt).

      EDIT: do not argue with your players. Discuss these issue out of character in advance of your next game.

      If your players can do a thing and everyone agrees then they are also allowing your NPCs to do the equivalent ridiculous spells.

      And the really high skills/attributes thing. That is fine (until they are trying to perform superhuman feats in public) but you can always throw them a curve ball and give them an unexpected situation where the particular skill they have prepared isn't relevant. Other mages, Abyssal threats, non-physical issues, character's families needing help, social conflicts, are all things which defence 10 isn't going to help with.

          1- It seems there are two different issues. The first one is that being rude is affecting the enjoyment of everyone, which defeats the point of a cooperative storytelling game. Call your player to talk one on one, explain the issue. Be clear and firm about it, because its a major disruption.

          Now on to the second item, the spellcasting. You are using at least 4 yantras, which based on the limits on page 84, suggests a Gnosis 6 mage at the very least. If they do not have that much Gnosis, they don't get to use so many yantras.

          Looking at them individually, 5 times ritual casting time means a 2 and a half hour casting time for a Gnosis 5 mage, this is fine.

          Rote mudra means no combined spells. Plus they invested in a rote for one specific spell and its on an Order skill that they sunk the XP to get to max. So extreme resource investment to get 1 thing done consistently well.

          Shadow Name 3 can only give a bonus to spells that resonate with their symbolism. So Odin can get a bonus on necromancy, but not on magic to control the tides. If the rote is not within their symbolism, no bonus, at all.

          High speech is slow, it takes a full turn to complete. Meaning mages will know what you are casting and have enough time to get Mage Sight up to counterspell. Sleepers will just find them creepy, High Speech is not subtle.

          A +2 location yantra only occurs in Verges and Demesne, which are rare, keyed to a Realm that has that Arcanum as Ruling. So if you cast Exceptional Luck on a Stygian Demesne, you get nothing. Same goes for Goetic Summons on an Aetherial Verge. Since these are not accessible everywhere, it shouldn't be considered ubiquitous for every spell.

          Regarding Perfecting spells, page 126 clearly states that spells can boost traits (skills and attributes) to the target's maximum, going beyond requires a Reach and a mana. In the case of mortals, its 5. Supernaturals derive it based on their power stat, such as Gnosis for mages (see on page 82 how Gnosis 6 is the first milestone to boost the maximum) and Blood Potency for vampires. Then you start figuring out what Vigor adds to that.

          2- Yep, +5 is the cap, but it can be boosted to +6 with the bonus for using an Order rote skill (list found on page 82)

          3- Working as intended, Acanthus are supposed to be lucky. See the limits in item 1 for how the mechanics reign that in. Moros can use Matter 2 and enough spell factors to lift a house in midair and drop it. Thyrsus have a universal remote control via Coaxing the Spirits. The key is how much the mage can get away with given the constraints of the problem they are facing.

          ​4- One small correction, its two Reach and a mana. That changes the casting time to reflexive. But more to the point, yeah, they can use it anytime. However, the main issue is mana.

          Gnosis determines your per turn mana limit. Which means you may not have enough to use Charmed to deal with everything and you also need mana to raise mage armor. Plus mana is not super common until higher levels, so they could simply burn through it quickly.

          This is why Arrows train athletics, higher defense plus mage armor gives you more breathing room and efficiency in combat.

          5- Its on page 118, on the Spell Control section.

          6- Page 299 covers Dissonance. It only happens at the end of a scene where the Sleeper witnessed obvious Supernal magic. So if the mage has Velocity Control to make them faster than a car, for example, but only move at their normal speed within the Sleeper's perception, no Dissonance.

          7- Page 113 clarifies that aimed spells are always AoE.

          8- Reinforced clothing and riot gear are mutually exclusive choices. Please note the downside in Defense, Speed and how conspicuous kevlar can be. Mage armor can stack with normal armor, if applicable.

          Mage Armor is an Attainment, so they don't get yantras. Please note different ones protect in different ways and from different things. Prime armor is useless on mundane attacks, Forces armor does nothing to mental attacks and Mind armor is useless against non-sentient beings. More information can be found on page 193.

          9- No, only the strongest Awakened spell takes effect, see the spell stacking rules on page 118.

          10- Thor was a warrior. Spells to become physically stronger get a bonus, sure. Performing feats of bravery, calling down lightning, drinking the ocean, calling back their weapon to them all make sense. But Thor was neither a mage or lucky, since it doesn't fit the theme, the Shadow Name cannot act as a yantra. Same goes for Cabal theme, it needs to a theme that cohesively matches the Shadow Names of the members and their vision.

          11- Mages are not affected by their own Nimbus Tilt. The tilt cannot give bonuses to skills, only dice tricks, see page 90. So at most it would be -1 Resolve and 8-again to firearms. Lastly, page 89 states only those using Active Mage Sight need to check if they are affected by the Tilt. Unless the player flares the tilt, no one besides them would normally need to check for the effect.

          12- Nope, spells require one success to work, with exceptional successes requiring 5 and Praxis 3. If the mage can narratively explain how they vanish for so many hours each week, plus they can deal with the exhaustion and interruption rules from page 56 of Signs of Sorcery, then everything checks out. Additionally, spells are cast as an instant roll, regardless of whether its ritual cast or not, so there are no milestones one they get to roll.

          It sounds like you are dealing with a power gamer, which can be exhausting. The fact they are constantly applying big hammer theory should probably have narrative consequences, since it doesn't demonstrate they are in control of their magic, they are always trying to call down more power, its not the precision a Wise mage should have. They are movers and shakers in the Consilia, if they do something without forethought, it can cause diplomatic incidents and escalation due to the sheer power they can throw around.

          The Guardians should be keeping an eye on them, at the very least. Drive home the consequences of not using finesse, of not knowing how to be low key when their families or Sleepers need help.​

          Lastly, I recommend the 2 part episode AwkwardGMCorbin and Drew from Occultist Anonymous did on Mage, its incredibly helpful for rules clarifications.

          Hope that helps.
