        Originally posted by Flinty View Post
        A spell for the Fate Arcanum that is a bit out there.... Not quite sure wether it should be in the Time Arcanum instead (and mind could probably do something very similar aswell).
        The concept touched upon here are "consequences".

        No Tomorrow (Fate ••)
        Practice: Veiling
        Primary Factor: Potency
        Withstood: Resolve
        Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Persuasion, Subterfuge

        Every action, even the tiniest movement, has consequences. This spell renders its subject unable to perceive the long-term consequences of an actions he contemplates taking. For example a man on vacation fails to see the damage an affair could have on his marriage.

        +1 reach: The mage may fabricate a false consequence the subject perceives to be the logical outcome of his actions. Said man might be made to think that having an affair be ultimatly benefical for his marriage.
        +2 reach and 1 mana: Short term consequences are hidden aswell. The subject can contemplate the act of jumping from a building, but is unable to perceive what the fall will do to him.
        ThisI does look more like a Time spell, and seconding Mrmdubois for making it Withstood by Composure. Maybe a Mind spell that does something similar by Veiling it's victim's logic and rational thoughts would be Withstood by Resolve.

        And another dickish veiling spell.....

        Predetermination (Fate ••)
        Practice: Veiling
        Primary Factor: Potency
        Withstood: Resolve
        Suggested Rote Skills: Science, Intimidation, Subterfuge

        A favourite among Seers that serve Ruin, this spell veils the fact that your own actions matter from the subject. The future seems to be set in stone, no matter what you do it seems impossible for the subject to make a difference. Any attempt the subject makes to further a goal, defined by the mage upon casting the spell, costs 1 Willpower. Even then dicepools are penalized by the spell's Potency.


        Normally veiling spells aren't withstood, but since these play havoc with the subjects actions I think a withstand is appropriate.
        I'd say the Willpower cost of the second spell be a +1 Reach effect, but that's just me prefering one effect at a time for spells.

        Great spells, btw. Very evil.

                                                So here is a spell of one of my players. It might look like a Weaving but I figured that you can go away with Rulling if it's done with 'Potency bursts'


                                                Hand of Benediction (Space ••)
                                                Practice: Ruling
                                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Subterfuge, Athletics, Medicine

                                                This spell allows caster to interact physically on a simple basis (e.g. Touch) within sensory range. Number of possible interactions equals potency, while the spell is active. Everything the caster touches or works with have it's full effect on her, e.g. interacting with a flame causes burns and blisters, as if it was touched. Touching a moving object requires usual test (dexterity + athletics/brawl/firearms).

                                                +1 Reach: Subject of the spell may perform more complicated tasks, like fixing a mechanism or writing on a keyboard.
                                                +1 Reach: Subject of the spell may perform extended mundane tasks, each interval counting towards the total number of interactions granted by potency.
                                                +1 Reach and 1 Mana: Tasks may be done remotely. (e.g. through Scrying spell)
