                                                    Originally posted by LadyLens View Post
                                                    How does one establish a link to a location? Asking seriously.
                                                    The slow way of building Sympathy is always a physical connection. Be there. Visit the place to the point where you've built up a sense of belonging, like a home or a personal work space. You could probably get Weak sympathy by carrying out emotionally significant actions there, like proposing to your partner. Such a connection would fade faster.

                                                    The fast way to do it is with spells. There are three spells that can grant you sympathy to place (or an object or a person; works just as well):
                                                    Borrow Threads (Space 2) to borrow another person's or object's sympathy with the place. Correspondence (Space 1) can be used to figure out who to cast Borrow Threads on.
                                                    Web-Weaver (Space 3) can be used to step up the marginal sympathy of standing in the location to a Weak connection.
                                                    Create Sympathy (Space 5) can straight up create a sympathetic connection between two subjects.
                                                    You could also just cast any spell that has the location as a subject (probably through AoE due to Size limitations) since any subject under a spell has a Weak connection to the mage who cast the spell.

                                                    For Sympathetic Yantra you could just take a photo of the location with your phone. That's why my Mastigos did in preparation of freeing Sleepwalker prisoners in the cellar of a Seer Pylon's headquarters. He cast Co-Location and moved the prisoners and the other Cabal mates in one part of the building to the designated escape spot while the priest themed Obrimos of the Cabal was stalling for time by literally preaching towards the two highly shocked Seers about trying to steal away the Sleepwalkers that were under our protection. We also got away with a Grimoire that wasn't ours, but who's keeping count?
