    An unorthodox approach to using imbued items

    08-11-2020, 02:30 AM
    Recently saw in SoS the clarifications to imbued items which really did clean things up. But a few things I saw really got my gears turning.

    One thought that occured to me, that I don't belive I've seen, what if individuals using an imbued item tried to activate it in a similar way to teamwork spellcasting?

    While definitely unorthodox it could be one way that sleepwalkers could activate imbued items more reliably since spell factors impact such a small dicepool. While they can use ritual intervals to boost the dicepool in some cases it might not be enough.

    Of course, as each participant is in a sense activating the item I could see the mana cost having to be paid for each participant contributing to the teamwork spellcasting in this situation.

    In addition to exploring this idea, what ways have you used or played around with imbued items in your own games?

      Keep in mind that there needs to be enough room for the teamwork to be possible. For example, 5 people turning the switch of the sunlight emitting flashlight will get pretty cramped.

      Another option is to find a theme to all the imbued items, then have the Sleepwalkers research a spirit that has that purview. A familiar ritual done the old fashioned way requires a lot of research, rare components, a long ceremony and investing a Willpower dot if everything goes well. But its a reliable way to activate them.

      Another option is to find a ghost mage to do it or even direct them to research, legends and rumors that addressed ways for Sleepwalkers to wield magical items. Maybe information can be found in an abandoned Sanctum, a shady Lorehouse vendor (a Scelestus or Chancellor affiliated merchant in disguise) or a diary of an Aegis Kai Doru member.

      In terms of mechanics, you could create a Cerimonial Magic shared merit. They could, for example, conduct a Taoist ritual, with a [Cerimonial Magic] dice bonus and each roll taking an hour. Each success attaches a Fu talisman to a chosen item, which can be used to activate it once. Up to [Cerimonial Magic] talismans can be active at one time and the participant needs a token of their ritual authority to use it.

      This could be repurposed to a Hermetic system, which could then make the Sleepwalkers learn from a Thrice Great lodge. This could create an interesting favor economy and build the foundations to having mentoring should they ever Awaken.

              There is also the option of jury rigging them. Let say the Sleepwalkers contracted a guide or found a map of open trods, they could then go to goblin markets. Maybe they could purchase something that allowed them to use the items, have the triggers modified with Hedge magic or pay a craftsman to do it for them. The Hedge is always perilous, the price might be steep or have unintended consequences. But its still doable.

              Ultimately I think they will need to delve into magic that is less stable than a Supernal solution, use shortcuts or proxies that come with strings attached. Can this be a path to power and understanding ? Absolutely, but it will require them walking a tightrope of danger. One example is Jesse Faden from Control. She was able to reliably use, connect to items and wield great power. But it placed her as an "arbiter" in a conflict she didn't fully understand.

                  While I am personally a fan of crossover, in this specific case I am concidering imbued item usage utilizing a teamwork action to allow one possible method of making imbued items more reliable.
