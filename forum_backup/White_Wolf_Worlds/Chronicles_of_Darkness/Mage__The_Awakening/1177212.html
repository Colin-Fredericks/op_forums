    Creative Imperial Thaumaturgy

    01-19-2018, 05:45 AM
    My players are on the verge of archmastery (one of them is undergoing the Trials now), so I thought I would start a thread for creating imperial spells that work with 2nd Edition rules. These spells assume that the Arcane Beat and Mana cost from Imperial Mysteries is followed, and that the Practices are pretty much the same as listed in the book. Spells are Withstood instead of contested, of course. I don't expect there to be a ton of spells in here, but I'm curious to see what people can come up with.

    I'm also going with a few guidelines, which people might debate or have different opinions on:
    1. Mechanics for these spells are based on these Imperial Mysteries 2e Update Rules.
    2. The general idea of "take the spell, make it a bit better, and have that be a Reach effect" is still in effect. Similarly, Reach and Mana cost rules from the Creative Thaumaturgy section are followed (e.g. 2 Reach to affect spellcasting rolls).
    3. Dynamics spells can only propagate under conditions relevant to the Arcanum unless other Arcana are added to the spell. Some conditions may be general enough that they're not really attached to an Arcanum, and are unaffected by this rule. This assumption is mostly for stuff where there's a clear separation between the Arcanum purviews, like propagating a Mind Dynamics spell through the Internet or sympathetic connections. I would say that needs the assistance of Forces or Space.


    I will start with a simple Mind Dynamics spell, a memetic version of Psychic Reprogramming. It's pretty much a copy and paste of Psychic Reprogramming with the Dynamics rules tacked on.

    Meme (Mind ••••• •)
    • Practice: Dynamics
    • Primary Factor: Potency
    • Cost: 2 Mana
    • Withstand: Resolve
    • Example Quintessence: Editorial articles, Temenos realms related to the changes, a regal decree from a ancient, powerful monarch.

    The archmaster inflicts a mental rewiring that spreads by memetic means. Similar to the Psychic Reprogramming spell, the archmaster may change one of the following aspects of subjects for each Potency of the spell: Virtue, Vice, Short-Term Aspiration, Long-Term Aspiration, Obsession, a non-Physical Persistent Condition, or may move one dot between two Social Skills, or between two Mental Skills. The archmaster must choose the conditions under which the spell dynamically propagates or initially affects subjects. Most commonly, this is when an affected subject talks to or sees another person. When a subject is affected by the spell, they Withstand with Resolve, and the spell affects them for its entire Duration. Each subject is affected for the spell's Duration that was set when it was first cast. The spell remains under the archmaster's spell control until it expires from every subject.

    Add Another Arcanum ••: The spell can propagate by dynamic conditions under the purview of the added Arcanum.

    +1 Reach: The archmaster may also move dots between two Social Attributes, or two Mental Attributes per Potency of the spell.

    +1 Reach: The spell may inflict different changes on each subject it affects, as stipulated by conditions set when the spell is cast. For example, the archmaster may specify that people above a certain age get different Aspirations than those under that age.

    +1 Reach: The archmaster instead specifies a general goal or motive that affected subjects must try to achieve. In this case, the Storyteller decides what changes the spell inflicts on the subjects, but they must be in line with the archmaster's goals and wishes. This Reach effect is usually combined with the above Reach effect for maximum effectiveness.

      Here is another one based on descriptions in Imperial Mysteries.

      Conscious Spell (Mind ••••• •)
      • Practice: Dynamics
      • Primary Factor: Potency
      • Cost: 2 Mana
      • Withstand: Resolve
      • Example Quintessences: An Imago, a Paradox created by the subject, Essence from a complex supernatural intelligence.

      The subject can create Imagos that react to conscious thought. While under the effects of this spell, the subject may use an Instant Action to transfer spell control of one more ongoing spells to another mage or a Sleepwalker. If the subject of Conscious Spell is mage, they also gain full conscious control over any spells under their control: In addition to being able to willingly lower Spell Factors, they can also raise them--but only to their original values if they were reduced in the first place.

      Spell Control cannot be transferred if it would put the target over their own Spell Control limit. This spell can transfer a number of spells up to Potency, although other abilities granted by this spell remain until its Duration expires. This spell is capable of transferring spell control of an Imperial spell to a lesser mage or even a Sleepwalker, but this is a breach of the Pax Arcanum.

      +1 Reach: Spell Control can be transferred even if it would put the target over their Spell Control limit.

      +1 Reach: For one point of Mana, the subject can also swap Spell Factors one-for-one, such as decreasing duration by one step to increase Potency by one. Such alterations cannot exceed Conscious Spell’s Potency.

      +2 Reach: For one point of Mana, the subject can apply--but not remove--Reach effects to currently existing spells under their control. These may generic or spell-specific Reach effects. If applying Reach would cause the spell to exceed its controller’s free Reach, the spell’s controller must make a Paradox roll. This dice pool can be reduced with Mana, but the controller can only spend an amount up to their Gnosis-derived Mana per turn limit. Paradox caused by this effect has the severity of an Imperial spell.

                  Here's a new spell. This time, from the Space Arcanum.

                  Unerring Compass
                  • Practice: Dynamics
                  • Primary Factor: Duration
                  • Cost: 2 Mana
                  • Example Quintessences: A compass Artifact from Pandemonium, an original copy of an arrest warrant for the subject.

                  This spell provides the archmaster with unerring direction to what they seek. When cast on a subject, the archmaster knows the subject’s exact location in the Tellurian. Unlike the lesser Locate Object spell, this spell also tells the archmaster exactly how to get to the subject’s location, based on the archmaster’s desired method of getting there (such as “fastest way” or “subtlest way”). The spell is capable of adjusting itself if the archmaster’s desired method of approach changes. Thus, the archmage always knows exactly where to teleport, what alley to walk down, or what train to take. Concealing magic provokes a Clash of Wills.

                  +1 Reach: The archmaster is guaranteed to always have surprise in the first round of combat with, and always have the Edge in a chase with, the subject.

                      Here's another Mind spell. This time it's an Entities spell that allows the archmage to change XP costs for the target. Not sure if this is the right way to go about it though.

                      Enlightenment (Mind ••••• ••)
                      • Practice: Entities
                      • Primary Factor: Potency
                      • Cost: 4 Mana, 2 Arcane Beats
                      • Example Quintessences: A memory where the subject learned something new related to the chosen categories, a symbol from the subject’s Oneiros representing a desire to learn.

                      The archmaster radically expands or alters the subject’s ability to learn and process new information. The archmaster may change the cost the subject pays in Experiences in a single category, such as Skills, Merits, or Arcana. Each Arcanum counts as one category for this spell. Some categories are harder to adjust than others: the Potency of this spell converts directly to “Experiences,” and the archmaster must “spend” an amount equal to the category’s base cost in order to adjust its cost by one. For example, four Potency is required to increase or decrease the Experience cost of an Arcanum by one. The minimum cost for a category cannot be reduced below 1 Experience.

                      +1 Reach: The archmaster may split the spell’s Potency to alter another category. This Reach effect can be applied multiple times to alter the Experience cost of multiple categories.

                      +1 Reach: The archmaster can add to, remove from, or change the type of Experience required for the category. For example, the archmaster could make it so that the subject can only spend Arcane Experiences to gain Merits, or that they could spend any kind of Experiences to gain Merits. The type of Experience must be one the subject can spend. For an additional Reach and one point of Mana, the archmaster can substitute any type of Experience. This effect cannot remove the ability of the subject to spend Experiences completely.

                              Here is another Space spell. I am not really happy with its current mechanics. I would like suggestions on how to better implement a "self-repairing Ward" that don't involve too much screwing around with the Clash of Wills rules.

                              Autonomous Ward (Space ••••• •)
                              • Practice: Dynamics
                              • Primary Factor: Duration
                              • Cost: 2 Mana
                              • Example Quintessences: A literal shield used to save a man’s life in a great battle, a heavily fortified compound


                              This spell is similar to the Ward (Space ••) spell, but with the additional ability of being self-repairing and having dynamic Keys and conditions under which the Ward allows magic to pass through it. Like the regular spell, “magic that uses the sympathy of Warded subjects or attempts to warp Warded areas provokes a Clash of Wills” and the archmaster knows when one of their wards is attacked. Any attack upon the Ward causes it to repair and adjust itself to make further incursion more difficult. If the ward is attacked by powers that it has been attacked by before, add the spell’s Potency as a bonus to the Clash of Wills roll. Further, any effects that reduce the Spell Factors of the Ward are only temporary, assuming they didn’t dispel the Ward outright. One scene after the Ward is affected, it returns to full strength.

                              +1 Reach: The mage may specify a Key that allows use of Space magic on the Warded subject. This Key can be based on generalized, intuitive desires of the archmaster.

                              +2 Reach: "The mage may cast Autonomous Ward upon an Iris or its Key, preventing the Iris from opening while the Ward remains in effect. Supernatural powers opening the Iris provoke a Clash of Wills."

                                              Here is a revised version of Autonomous Ward, as well as a first iteration of the Aegis spell.

                                              Adaptive Ward (Space ••••• •)
                                              • Practice: Dynamics
                                              • Primary Factor: Duration
                                              • Cost: 2 Mana
                                              • Example Quintessences: A literal shield used to save a man’s life in a great battle, a heavily fortified compound

                                              This spell is similar to the Ward (Space ••) spell, but with the additional ability of adjusting to block attacks and having dynamic Keys and conditions under which the Ward allows magic to pass through it. Like the regular spell, “magic that uses the sympathy of Warded subjects or attempts to warp Warded areas provokes a Clash of Wills” and the archmaster knows when one of their wards is attacked. If the ward is attacked by powers that it has been attacked by before, add the spell’s Potency as a bonus to the Clash of Wills roll. Each time the ward is attacked, during a Scene, this bonus is reduced by 1, down to a minimum of 0. At the end of the Scene, the bonus returns to full strength.

                                              +1 Reach: The mage may specify a Key that allows use of Space magic on the Warded subject. This Key can be based on generalized, intuitive desires of the archmaster.

                                              +2 Reach: The mage may cast Autonomous Ward upon an Iris or its Key, preventing the Iris from opening while the Ward remains in effect. Supernatural powers opening the Iris provoke a Clash of Wills.

                                              Aegis (Space ••••• •)
                                              • Practice: Dynamics
                                              • Primary Factor: Duration
                                              • Cost: 2 Mana
                                              • Example Quintessences: Stuff

                                              The archmaster warps space in such a way that the affected subject or area is dynamically protected from passive and active harm. By reflexively warping space, all obvious attempts to harm the subject fail or are greatly penalized. The subject is completely protected from all mundane sources of obvious harm. Gunshots pass through or warp around the subject. If the subject would accidentally walk over a pit, a Ban would prevent the subject from falling in. Active supernatural attacks can still affect the subject, but trigger a Clash of Wills. The protection provided by Aegis is rather blunt. Passive, lingering dangers will cause the spell to form a Ban around the subject as long they remain in the area, which prevents interaction with the environment.

                                              Add Any Arcanum •••: By adding another Arcanum, the archmaster can fine-tune the Aegis. Any passive obvious phenomenon under the added Arcanum’s purview that would be harmful to the subject is altered to be non-threatening. This lets subjects enter dangerous places such as the Lower Depths, exist indefinitely in the vacuum of space, and so on while still being able to interact with the environment.

                                              +1 Reach: The archmaster may specify a Key that allows magic to bypass the protection of the Aegis. This Key can be based on generalized, intuitive desires of the archmaster.

                                              +1 Reach: The Aegis protects against one non-obvious phenomenon per level of Potency, such as invisible toxic gases. What is non-obvious is ultimately up to the Storyteller. The subject does not need to know about phenomena beforehand. When first encountered, the subject immediately learns of the harmful effect, and may choose to add it to the aegis’ protection as a reflexive action at any point thereafter.

                                              +1 Reach: The dynamic spatial warping extends into the subject’s sympathetic links as well, increasing the Withstand that must be overcome by the spell’s Potency.

                                              +2 Reach: The Aegis has enhanced protection against supernatural attacks. If a supernatural attack requires a dice pool to hit, it is penalized by the spell’s Potency. This includes aimed spells, and affects a number of attacks equal to the spell’s Potency.
