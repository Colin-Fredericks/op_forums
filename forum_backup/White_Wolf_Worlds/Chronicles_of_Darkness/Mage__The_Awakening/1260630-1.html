        On the subject of dispelling, it's worth pointing out anyone that does get ejected from the past back to the present probably has a setup for it. If not the first time definitely the second or third. In the form of some kind of super sanctuary they spend months on making in the form +10-20 dice worth of reusable yatra or has a dozen or so sleepwalker friends or family they cast the spell on to run concurrently. So even if the spell on them gets bestest, the dozen or so others on friends (who they know go undetected from past to present by this point) just seamlessly pickup the slack without interruption.

        It's what Walks-On-Clouds did in another idea thread. She was a neolithic mage that used a 101 ideas to grow, expand, and maintain a bloodline for the intent of having dozens of banner bearer/shield maidens. For the express use of having dozens of copies of the life spell keeping her forever young and healthy running. If you dispell twelve of them, the others seamlessly keep going. If you start dispelling any of them them your going to have the instant ire of a thousands of year old mage -with all the power and experience that entails- instantly on you. (The only reason she died in the micro story was staying to fight a life or death battle she knew was potentially lethal to defend someone she loved.)

        Edit: TLDR: That is spell stacking keeps the affects from scaling up. Nothing is stopping you from creatively laying spell on top of spell with the exact same affect so at least one is maintaining your litchdom at any one point in time.

                Your a time mage. Ideas for protecting the spell include:

                1) Creating a powerful imbued item other would covert and want to protect or horde. Laying your lichdom spell(s) in it. Hiding the spell and it's connection to you with not quite archmage levels of trickery and potency. Releasing the spell. Then donating it to some third party.

                2) Pull a Voldemort. Creating a runed item to maintain your litchdom. Make it practically invulnerable. Use your magic to find a hiding place no one can find or get into without spending a whole chronicle on purely doing that. Hide the item there. (The whole point of being a litch is having decades to spare!)

                3) Make a item like in 1, but instead sit on it for years. Like making a vase in 1675. Wait until it's 2020 (well, maybe not that long). Hide the vase in a Mysterium or museum excavation. Let them covet and protect your litchdom thinking they're just protecting pretty artwork they found from a long dead civilization.
