    "Star Breathers" a Homebrew Legacy

    01-25-2025, 06:50 PM
    here is a Legacy created for Several characters in my Chronicle, i have never made a legacy before so criticism would be greatly appreciated.

    The Star Breathers

    Origins:

    Parentage: Thyrsus, Mysterium or Free Council

    Background: Originating in the Sacramento Consilium during the 1960’s, the star breathes were founded based on insights into Astrophysics and Humanities first Forays into other worlds held on the physical plane. Curious Mystagogues and Libertines banded together to develop new magical Praxes based upon the final Frontier, their methods quickly spreading across the continent.

    Appearance: Applying a method of Scientific analysis to the path of Ecstasy, the star breathers vary wildly in appearance, ranging from well groomed academics to the unkempt explorers, most fall somewhere in between as their practices are best done far from the light pollution of citiesbut still need access to scientific equipment and circles.

    Doctrine:
    Prerequisites: Forces 2, Science 2 and two dots in either Occult or Academics as well as a specialty in any of these skills reflecting knowledge of the stars

    Initiation: Star Breather Initiation begins with the tutor training the aspiring starbreather in the various fields of astrophysics, astronomy, astrology and ecology. This period of training also includes occult knowledge of the Shadow and the spirits of various stars and planets, as well as training in astral journeys. Once the aspirant has sufficient background knowledge they begin to focus on studying one particular star, this star cannot be one already studied by another starbreather (the legacy keeps meticulous records) and so generally it is an obscure one rather than a part of major constellations, they learn everything they can about it, its size, its life stage, what bodies orbit it and how it affects the stars around it. Once they have learned all information possible to learn from earth they make an astral journey to the sidereal wastes to observe that stars soul, they watch it drift through the Vacuum, and see it Exhale its life into the universe, they breathe in this life and then exhale stardust, now a member of the legacy.

    Organization: Star breathers are split into two distinct yet wholly friendly factions, the Mystagogue faction and the Libertine faction, the Mystagogues are the least Hierarchal, only keeping to the authority of Master and Apprentice, whereas the Libertines form “houses” based on different fields of study (exoplanets, Quasars, Asteroids etc) and the most knowledgeable are referred to as “house leaders” who manage sets of Senior members who in turn manage Junior members, only Senior members are allowed to initiate new members but have complete freedom in choosing who they allow entrance to the legacy.

    Theory: The Star Breathers reason that, scientifically speaking, all life in the universe (which they are certain occurs on planets beyond earth) feeds upon external sources of energy, humans feed on animals, animals feed on other animals, those animals feed on plants, those plants feed on sunlight, but the stars themselves do not feed upon other sources of energy, they are the “first breath” of the cosmos and by Shaping their souls to emulate stars they themselves will gain the power of creation. Stars are thought to be Shards of the Primal Wild that act as “hearts” oxygenating the blood of the cosmos with energy and life. Other Celestial Bodies are given Supernal Correspondences according to their cosmic functions.

    Magic:
    Ruling Arcanum: Forces

    Yantras: Sun high in the sky whilst Casting (+1); Stars Clearly Visible whilst casting (+2); Star studied for initiation Ascendant during casting (+3); Diagram showing the energy economy of the mages environment (+1); Listening to CMBR (+1); predicting then observing a celestial phenomenon relevant to the spell (+3)

    Oblations: Watching stars for at least an hour; reading or writing astrophysics papers; making models of solar systems entirely with natural materials; conversing with a spirit from outer space for at least an hour

    Attainments:

    First: Hearing Cosmic Breath
    Prerequisites: initiation

    The universe's breath extends beyond the visible light spectrum, to grasp the full pictures you must see every colour, this attainment Replicates the “Night Vision” spell, with reach applied to instant use.

    Optional: Spirit 1
    Living Shadow permeates the cosmos, every star and planet has its living counterpart, this attainment replicates the “exorcists eye” spell with reach applied to instant use

    Second: Feeling Cosmic Pulse
    Prerequisites: Forces 2, Science 3

    All living systems rely on the star's breath to survive, therefore the star breather may introduce their own life force to affect the flow of that system. This attainment Requires a scenes worth of preparation spent observing the flow of energy around themselves and imagining the change they desire, at the end of which the star breather exhales towards the sky, emulating the “control weather” spell, with reach applied to prolonged duration, the spell is considered to have potency equal to the star breathers Forces dots.

    Optional: Spirit 2

    The Star breather Channels the cosmic pulse to enhance resonance, allowing spirits a greater presence in the material world, with a scenes worth of preparation (which can be done concurrently with the attainment above) at the end of which the Star breather exhales onto the subject, emulating the “opener of the way” spell with reach applied to prolonged duration.

    Third: Seeing Cosmic Bones
    Prerequisites: Forces 3, an occult specialty in the Sidereal wastes

    Star Breathers find frequent reason to visit the dreams of the universe but it is a perilous journey, any star breather knows the danger the cosmic oversoul poses to astral travelers from their initiation, so the star breather learns to protect themselves whilst walking amongst the thoughts of the stars. Whilst traveling the sidereal wastes the mage may emulate the “environmental shield” spell with reach assigned to instant use and prolonged duration, but instead of protecting the mage from material weather phenomena this attainment protects them from the ecstatic wind whilst within the sidereal wastes. This spell does not protect the mage from other environmental threats within the sidereal wastes like Intense heat or the winds of Neptune.

    Optional: Spirit 3

    Spirits are the songs carried by the cosmic breath, but they are as deadly as they are beautiful, so the star breathers developed ways to protect themselves from encounters gone awry. This attainment requires a scenes worth of preparation, during which time the Star Breather hums a song representing the resonance of the spirits they wish to protect themselves from, at the end of the scene the mage chooses any number of resonances she has encounter before and gains general armour equal to (Forces+Spirit)-(number of resonances the mage wants to protect themselves from) against any spirit containing those resonances, this also adds to withstand against the spirits influences and numina. This attainment lasts for 24 hours.

    Fourth: Smelling Cosmic Skin
    Prerequisites: Forces 4, Spirit 4, Science 4, a speciality in both academics and science reflecting knowledge of the stars.

    Indirect observation can only take a star breather so far, the Pilgrimage across the sidereal wastes illustrates this lesson, but sometimes even an astral journey isn’t as enlightening as direct physical contact, to this end the star breather learns to pull objects from the grasp of heaven. Helios' solar current expands through the solar system, by manipulating the course of this current a Star Breather can change the course of any object not within a planet's atmosphere and within the limits of the solar system. This emulates the “Turn Momentum” attainment with reach applied to sensory range (usually viewed through a telescope) and controlling where the object is redirected to. The attainment only works on one object up to size five (no shifting the orbit of jupiter) and is usually used by star breathers to bring distant asteroids crashing into deserts and seas so that they can be retrieved and examined, but more than one has weaponized this attainment. successfully calculating the precise direction an object will need to go at its current speed will require and extended Intelligence+Science roll requring success equal to one per 0.1 astronomical units (average distance between the earth and sun) the Precise force attainment applys to this roll.

    Fifth: Tasting Cosmic Flesh
    Prerequisites: Forces 5 Science 5

    So in tune with the cycle of Cosmic breath the Star breather is now able to share in cosmic creation, giving life and energy to the universe, this attainment requires a scenes worth of preparation where the star breather meditates, focusing their attention entirely on their heartbeat and breathing and contemplating their supernal correspondences, at the end of this scene the Star Breather exhales new life in the form of a miniature star, Emulating the “Create Energy” spell, a size 1 sphere of light that continually creates a type of energy of the mages choice with reach applied to prolonged duration, this attainment lasts for a week and has potency equal to the mages Forces dots. the star can be moved around with appropriate forces spells (Control Light, Control Fire etc) and If “Eradicate energy” is cast on this spell then the star collapses in a brilliant fashion, Dealing Aggravated Damage equal to the number of days left on the stars lifespan within a range of (damage dealt*10) yards.

    Optional: Spirit 5

    The Star breather gives the energy of both ephemeral and material life, when the mage uses the attainment above they may also allow the miniature star to Produce up to five Essence of a resonance the mage has encountered before every ten minutes, producing Essence for as long as the miniature star exists. This emulates the “Essence Fountain” spell with reach applied to prolonged duration.


    for this last attainment im going off the assumption that the Essence produced by the essence fountain spell is lasting which im not sure of. that combined with the fact that normaly instantaneous spells repeat if cast at prolonged duration produces a whopping 5040 Essence over the course of the stars life, but i think its balanced as this energy will be quickly absorbed into the enviroment and by nearby spirits with appropriate consequences and frankly a Gnosis 8 master of the spirit arcanum should be able to wreak potent changes to portions of the spirit world.

      I think your design holds up really well, with minor caveats.

      The first Attainment is solid.

      I take it that Feeling Cosmic Pulse lasts a day, which extends to a week at Forces 4. You didn't specify, but since the Primary Factor is Potency, such a Duration is in keeping with the rules.
      The Optional Attainment appears to have Duration as Primary (like Opener of the Way does by default), which means it will last anywhere from day (at Spirit 2) to a year (at Spirit 5). This means the durations will be mismatched between the two effects.

      I think giving Seeing Cosmic Bones Reach for Instant Casting is inefficient. Environmental Shield has Duration as a Primary factor, so it will last from a week (at Forces 3) to a year (at Forces 5). The Mage can just meditate for a hour once, and have the effect running for a long time. The Potency of this effect would be 2, rising to 3 (at Forces 4). I think it's fair to make this Attainment protect from material weather as well as from Astral one. It's really restricted otherwise.
      How does the Optional Attainment interact with the Spirit Mage Armor? I assume the difference is that for specified Resonances, you get the benefits of Mage Armor, but better and Mana-free. It's fine, though you could clarify for other readers. I think it's neat how this Attainment introduces a necessity of preparation and of discovering new Resonances for these Mages. This theme even returns in the fifth Attainment. I assume its Duration extends to a week at Spirit 4, as usual.

      Here is the first major objection I have: When looking at the target through a telescope, Sensory Range is not enough. You need one extra Reach for using an intermediary (the Corebook explains that on p. 114). You could decide that this is where that inefficient Reach from the second Attainment goes. You could also argue that they get that Reach for free because of how limited this effect is, but I would just let it work on Earthly phenomena (a scene is a long time to cast, but you can still sabotage vehicles and intercontinental ballistics). Mages live dangerous lives, and it's fair to let their tricks be useful in their daily lives. The same goes for your Environmental Shield.
      There is no Spirit component to this Attainment. What happened?

      The fifth Attainment is kind of beautiful. I love the way it feels. Seems unique, but pretty balanced.
      The way all Attainments' Ritual time is fixed, I think the star would generate Essence once per Scene, and not once per 10 minutes.

      On the fluff side, notice that you Legacy shares Arcana and interest with Thrice-Great (and to a lesser extent with The Celestial Masters). There is room to build on inter-Legacy politics and squabbling. Your Legacy is pretty modernist, where Thrice-Great came from astrology, and can be really regressive, sometimes.
      Also, there are not that many mages around. There should still be some prominent "unclaimed" stars that initiates can race for, which just makes things more exciting. "My Cabal called dibs at the whole Orion's belt. You had no right to just take over!"
