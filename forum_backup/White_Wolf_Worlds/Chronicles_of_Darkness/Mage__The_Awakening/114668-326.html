    Tessie's answer was what I remember from that section as well, in case you haven't had a chance to look into the matter more. Basic premise is that the language will say what something is not, instead of what it is. It doesn't sound like a very easy langauge to work with, so I imagine it has some sort of inconceivable trick that allows it to actually function, something a human mind could not cook up, but something a superintelligence like the lonely exarch could.

    If you're familiar with the greek philosopher Parmenides, think of the parametic language as Parmenides' worst nightmare. Parmenides said something to the effect of &quot;you can't describe something by what it is not&quot; and the Lonely Exarch is all &quot;hold my beer&quot; and made a language that is purely that.

    I guess a working example, if I'm not mistaken, would be like... somewhere on the forum someone said something like a &quot;bird that isn't a bird knocking on your door&quot;, so the parametic language might work by defining the bird-not-bird by saying, well, you're not a bird, you're not a raptor, you're not a mammal, and you're not a spirit, and entrapping and commanding the abyssal by using the definitions of what it isn't as a sort of lattice to trap or compel it? Especially if used as a yantra?

    Not a whole lot to go off of as the parametic language was literally only described in a paragraph, but, its a fun thought experiment irregardless to have a language that works in the complete opposite fashion of all other languages.
    Yeah, thought so. So maybe making my homebrew Merit (in the respective thread) for it require High Speech isn’t a good idea afterall, hmm...
