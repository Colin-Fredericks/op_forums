    Influence of Summoning Supernal Beings

    12-03-2017, 08:31 PM
    Hey Everyone,

    So, I was reading over the Summoning Supernal Entities section in the Mage the Awakening Book and noticed in the Ephemeral Entities section regarding Influence & Arcana the following statement:
    a Supernal being possesses dots in two Arcana equal to its Rank, one of which must be one of the Ruling Arcana of its Realm, and may have additional Arcana at lower ratings.
    So, this is a little confusing to me, so let me throw down a scenario for you and let me know:
    A Moros decides to summon a Rank 3 Supernal Entity and they chose the Recondite with Life as the secondary Arcana, which means this would be a Death 2 Life 1 or Death 1 Life 2 Supernal Being, correct?
    Or would it be a Death 3 Life 3 Supernal Being?

    If it is the former, it would logically mean you have to summon a Rank 6 being to get a Death 5 Supernal Being. Which, would be impossible since Mages cannot summon beings greater than Rank 5. I imagine it is the latter, but it also raises the question about how you decide the additional Arcana at lower ratings since it seems it has a number of dots equal to its Rank in two Arcana and without Numina they can't trade it for additional influence.

    Let me know if I am understanding correctly, thanks in advance!

      If you summoned a rank 3 Supernal entity with Death as its primary arcana then it would appear with Death 3, and if you targeted it to have Life it would also have Life 3, and it might have other arcana at 2 or 1 dots and whether or not it would have these additional arcana is entirely up to the ST. That's how I read it anyway.

      Edit: Supernal entities are by far more difficult to come by than any other kind of entity that might be entreated for assistance, so it makes sense there would be a commensurate power boost to make it worthwhile. Also, even once successfully summoned they have some kind of esoteric trial to overcome before you can actually sit down and ask them for help, and that trial gets increasingly more difficult the higher their Rank. So really, before you can do anything with a Supernal Entity you have to clear two hurdles.

      The one exception is when a Mage accidentally summons a Supernal Entity via a dramatic failure with their Scrutiny rolls, in which case there are other attendant dangers to be dealt with.
