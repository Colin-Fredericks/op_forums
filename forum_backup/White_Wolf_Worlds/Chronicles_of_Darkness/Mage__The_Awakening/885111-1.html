    I have a few contributions, 2 original and 1 a reimagining of an existing 2E one...

    New Merit: Time Shifted (3)
    Requisites: None.

    The mage’s complete history has been erased due to the effects of a time traveler or some potent Mystery. This could have been caused by the mage himself, directly, indirectly or not at all. Time magic used to divine her past doesn’t result in any useful information, but he keeps most of his memories intact. Once per chapter, the player can choose a character he has just met to have had a relationship with him in his original timeline. The mage with this Merit recovers one point of Willpower and gets an Aspiration of “Create a relationship” targeted towards that character. Work with your Storyteller on the details of that relationship.

    New Merit: Savant (2)
    Requisites: Awakened, Intelligence 4, Mysterium Status 2, Speciality in Occult.

    The mage has memorized an extensive amount of occult knowledge. Most of this knowledge is academic or theoretical in nature, not practical. She could have memorized hundreds of different mudras for spells she cannot cast, or learned everything the Mysterium knows about Vampires, for example. You may add your Mysterium Status to any roll made to recall information about any of your Occult specialties, instead of the normal +1.

    New Merit: Techne (2)
    Requisite: Awakened, Free Council Status 1.

    Choose an aspect of human culture. This can be anything like “baseball”, “alternative grunge rock”, “electronic sports”, “contemporary sculpture”, “18th-century poetry” or “MMA wrestling”. The mage obtains a free specialty related to it on one of their skills, 1 free dot of contacts or allies who are related to that field, and can use elements from that aspect of culture as tool Yantras. Finally, when casting a spell as a Ritual, if the effect of the spell is not obviously magical, Sleepers can participate on the Ritual as if they were sleepwalkers.

        I tried to do a rewording of your Arcane Asceticism Merit, and came up with this based on some stuff PenDragon did for his House Rules. I'll probably reimagine the other benefits of your version as separate Merits, in their own right.

        Arcane Ascetic (• to •••••)
        Prerequisite:         Awakened
        Effect: Shunning tools and external resources, you refine your magic to new heights based on raw will and arcane insight, reflected in the Supernal Truths engraved upon your Awakened soul. When casting a spell whose Primary Arcanum's dot rating is equal to your dots in this Merit, you may reflexively invoke this Merit to gain free Reach equal to half your dots in Gnosis, rounded up.

        Drawback: Internalizing the spell in this manner is a double-edged sword, as you are unable to incorporate external symbols into its Imago. While making use of this Merit for your spell, you cannot use Runes or Magical Tools as Yantras. Environmental Yantras, High Speech, and Mudras work normally, however.

                Here are a couple of Merits aimed at augmenting Runes, one of which was based off a 1E Merit made by PenDragon. I also have a 2E conversion of the Countermagic Stance Merit from the Mage Chronicler's Guide.

                Glyph Lore (o) Converted from Tome of the Mysteries
                Prerequisites: Awakened, Skill at Two Dots
                Effect: Those Awakened who demonstrate efficiency in the use of Runes can learn to further imbue power into their meaning. This can consequentially reinforce the spells they support. Choose a Skill when purchasing this Merit. When using Runes as a Yantra in your spell, you gain an additional dice bonus equal to half your dots in that Skill, rounded up, if the spell is resonant with its symbols. This is reflected by a spell's suggested Rote Skills, though the Storyteller may use others when appropriate. If the Runes are being applied to a rote spell, they are considered to have the benefits of a Dedicated Tool.


                Runecraft (o to oooo)
                Prerequisites: Awakened
                Effect: You have learned how to use Runes as more than just conduits for power and anchors for your spells. For each dot in this Merit, you may apply Advanced Spell Factors to a spell's Duration, Potency, Range, or Scale without having to spend Reach, so long as it incorporates Runes as a Yantra.

                Drawback: Each benefit granted by this Merit is considered a separate Yantra, counted to your limit based on Gnosis.


                Countermagic Stance (o to ooooo) 1E from the Mage Chronicler's Guide
                Prerequisites: Awakened, one dot in the specified Arcanum
                Effect: You are not necessarily a master in a given Arcanum, but you have deeply studied its principles in order to counter any of its spells more effectively. Choose an Arcanum when purchasing this Merit. Whenever you employ the Arcanum's Counterspell Attainment, you may add your dots in this Merit to the dice pool.
