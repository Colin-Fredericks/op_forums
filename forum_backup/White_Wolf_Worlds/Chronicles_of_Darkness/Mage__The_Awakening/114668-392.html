                    Originally posted by Neos01 View Post

                    ok but then the phrase "If she uses one ritual item in many ways, each individual use counts as one Yantra for this limit." In wich cases applies?
                    Let's say Anne the Mastigos is casting a universal language spell on Bob the Obrimos. Anne decides to use a radio antenna as one of her yantras. An antenna is both a wand and a symbol of communication, since both symbols would apply (Anne is targeting someone {wand} and casting a spell involving communication) she could incorporate both meanings into her casting, but each meaning would count as a separate yantra despite being the same physical object.

                    The -2 paradox is a rider, not an independent use of an object's symbolism.
