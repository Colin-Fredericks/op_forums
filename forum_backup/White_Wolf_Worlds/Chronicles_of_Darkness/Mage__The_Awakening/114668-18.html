                    These questions might not be simple, but this seems like the best thread to ask.

                    What options to Archmages have to become immortal, that is, never die from old age? I figured that, rules as written, they would need to use the Practice Of Excision to remove some part of their Pattern linked to the process of aging. Life, Time and Death all sound fitting for this, but what other Arcana might work? If I remember correctly, you can stop the aging process with 5 dots of Time and Reach in 2ED, but you'd be one dispellation away from being reduced to a pile of ash and bone chunks, like an elder vampire suffering Final Death. A spell cast with an Imperial Practice is much more sturdy, I bet. That being said, creative use of magic could achieve the same goal with "just" the Practice Of Dynamics. Again, Life, Time and Death sound like the most likely options.

                    If the Potency of spells in 2ED is Withstood by Stamina, Resolve or Composure, does that mean a Rank 5 entity with the Simplified Resistance Attribute at 15 is nigh impossible to affect without tons of Reach and Yantras? It is appropriate for a being of such power, I admit, but it seems even a Rank 3 entity with Resistance 9 could prove very hard to control without a lot of Paradox risk. Am I reading the 2ED rules right?

                    Imperial Mysteries says that entities of Rank 6+ can duplicate the effects of the Common Practices of any Arcanum, as long as the effect is related to its Influences. All Mana costs are converted to Essence. So a Rank 6 entity is treated as having an initial Primary Factor of 6 for all these Influences-As-Arcana, right? What would be their equivalents of Reach and Advanced Spell Factors? What would they have to do if they wanted to imitate an Unraveling spell that deals aggravated damage (which requires 1 Reach + 1 Mana in 2ED)? They already get [Rank] bonus successes on rolls related to their Influences, but could they spend Essence to boost their Power and Finesse to improve dice pools for Influences? Am I looking at it from the wrong angle and missing something obvious?

                    Bye.
