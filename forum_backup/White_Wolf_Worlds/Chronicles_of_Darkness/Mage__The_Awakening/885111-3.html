    More Summoning-based Merits.

    Grand Summoning (••)
    Prerequisites: Awakened
    Effect: You have a a rare talent for summoning multiple Supernal Entities at once in a single ritual. While this raises the risk (and number) of Abyssal intruders that might exploit the summoning, the potential rewards from dealing with multiple Supernal beings potentially compensates for the danger. You may now summon multiple Supernal beings based on your Gnosis, similar to your limitations for combining multiple spells.

    Drawback: You add two successes for each additional Entity after the first, and allocate successes separately for each Entity's Rank and how long it may manifest in the Fallen World before it begins to take damage.


    Otherworldly Lore (•• or ••••)
    Prerequisites: Awakened
    Effect: You have reached out to the Supernal World and communed with its denizens enough times to glean a number of insights on the nature of the Tapestry. Such insights make the summoning process come more easily to you. At two dots, you benefit from the 9-again rule on all summoning rolls. At four dots, this becomes 8-again.


    Supernal Beacon (•)
    Prerequisites: Awakened
    Effect: Whether through proficiency as a summoner, or simply the sheer strength of your Awakened soul, your Nimbus shines like a guiding light whenever you call down beings from the Supernal World. This halves the time interval made between rolls for a given summoning ritual.

                    Inviolate Name (• • •)
                    Effect: The character's sympathetic name is somehow protected from outside influence, guarded by some supernatural patron. Her sympathetic name cannot be used as a Sympathy Yantra; it simply fails to carry any magical resonance with her identity.
                    Drawback: Her true name is metaphysically "owned" by some other being, who protects it at a cost for her. This NPC should built together with help from the Storyteller, and is not meant to be adversarial; rather, they are needy in much the same way as those created with the Mentor merit. Some especially paranoid Seers have this merit, reflecting deeper servitude to their patron Exarch in exchange for protection from their Awakened peers. Also common are fae and demons, who ask strange favors for their continued aid.

                                    Mr.F.I.X. Did a revision of your Familiar Yantra Merit, plus gave it a more evocative name.

                                    Symbolic Bond (• to •••)
                                    Prerequisites: Familiar
                                    Effect: You have an especially deep and potent bond with your Familiar. And you can call on its symbols for power as others invoke their Shadow Name. As long as the Familiar resonates with the spell you are casting, you may tap your bond with it as a Yantra. The bonus of the Familiar as a Yantra is equal to your dots in the Merit, plus the Familiar's Rank. This Merit may be purchased multiple times, with each instance applying to a single Familiar.

                                    Note: If you are also using the Supernal Companion Merit, you may apply the Symbolic Bond Merit to a given Supernal Companion. In addition to the bonuses provided by the Companion's Rank and dots purchased in Symbolic Bond, a Mage may also apply the bonus dice for Scrutiny rolls to the spellcasting pool.

                                                arthexis. Made some tweaks to your Time Shifted Merit.

                                                Time Shifted (•••)
                                                Effect: The Mage’s complete history has been erased due to the effects of a time traveler or some potent Mystery. This could have been caused by the Mage himself, directly, indirectly or not at all. Time magic used to divine her past doesn’t result in any useful information, but he keeps most of his memories intact. Once per Chapter, the player can choose a character he has just met to have had a relationship with him in his original timeline. The Mage with this Merit recovers one point of Willpower and gets an Aspiration of “Create a relationship” targeted towards that character. Work with your Storyteller on the details of that relationship.

                                                Drawback: Because his timeline has been irrepairably tampered with, the Mage himself registers as a temporal anomaly to Time Sight and other supernatural senses capable of perceiving him as such. This is likely to attract unwanted attention from various entities, and even affect your interactions with other Awakened.

                                                            Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post

                                                            Summoner’s Soul (•, •••, or •••••)
                                                            Prerequisite:                                                             Awakened
                                                            Effect: A minority of Awakened have a latent connection to the Supernal World which, if developed, shows itself as an inherent talent for summoning beings native to them. On a level far below conscious thought, such Mages have an instinct for the rules that govern other realities. Scholars refer to this gift (or curse, if you like) as a Summoner’s Soul. When summoning Supernal Entities to the Fallen World, a Mage gains a number of benefits based on which version of the Merit she has.

                                                            The one-dot version strengthens her ability to sanctify the location of a summoning against Abyssal intrusion. Whenever she adds successes to the target number, each grants her an additional number of extra rolls equal to half her dots in this Merit, rounded up.

                                                            The three-dot version shores up the summoner's connection to her Path Realm, extending her ability to call down more powerful entities within shorter spans of time. She starts with a base number of five successes. Furthermore, she adds only three successes to the target number for each Rank of the desired entity above 1.

                                                            The five-dot version further intensifies the Mage's connection to the entity's Realm. This allows her to not only filter out the resonance of both the Lie and the other Realms, but also draw upon the presence of those who walk her Path. She only add successes to the target number for every number of Sleepers equal to her Gnosis, or every few Mages of different Paths equal to half that amount, rounded up. Furthermore, she may subtract successes for each Mage present who shares her Path. This cannot reduce successes below the base number, however.


                                                            Supernal Beacon (•)
                                                            Prerequisites: Awakened
                                                            Effect: Whether through proficiency as a summoner, or simply the sheer strength of your Awakened soul, your Nimbus shines like a guiding light whenever you call down beings from the Supernal World. This halves the time interval made between rolls for a given summoning ritual.
                                                            These two seem like they should have some drawback, even if minor ones. The flavor text of Summoner's Soul says it might be referred to as a curse, and I'd think a stronger Supernal Nimbus would attract some attention (Abyssal or otherwise).

                                                            Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post
                                                            Supernal Anchor (• to •••••)
                                                            Effect: One primary reason for which Mages craft soul stones is the creation of a Demesne, a space attuned to a willworker’s Path Realm and which functions, effectively, as a Verge for the purpose of shielding spells from Paradox. Some Mages, however, aren’t content to divide their spirits in this manner, regardless of how useful it might be to have a place in which they can cast some of their most powerful magics. One answer to this conundrum is to be found in the process of creating a Supernal Anchor.

                                                            A Mage with this Merit can use a Supernal Entity whose Rank is equal to or less than the Merit's dot rating to forge an artificial soul stone. He need not be the one to summon it, but it must be resonant with his Path for him to affect. He must either defeat the Entity in combat by fully reducing its Corpus, or successfully convince it to agree to the binding. Either way, he can spend a Willpower point and Mana equal to the being's Rank to reflexively reshape the being into a corporeal fragment of the world in which his Watchtower stands. The spirit becomes bound in this new form (and within the Fallen World), acting as a soul stone belonging to the Mage for all intents and purposes, save that he cannot be held in thrall by losing the stone.

                                                            Drawback: Crafting a Supernal Anchor is an Act of Hubris against Enlightened and Understanding Wisdom, and is considered a Left-Handed practice by most Mages, even the Seers of the Throne. Furthermore, unless done in a Supernal Verge keyed to its Realm, destroying the anchor causes the Supernal Entity trapped within to immediately vanish into the Abyss.

                                                            Note: The system for creating a Supernal Anchor can be found here.
                                                            Seems like this should require a Willpower dot, but maybe that's just me. It's only a 1XP per Supernal Anchor cost/tax anyways.
