                    Devour the Slain does resistant damage? That's a new one. Is the Willpower drain resistant too?

                    Anyway, majority of the Infinite Mana Loop threads boil down to :yeah, it's possible, but not very convenient in middle of action, and regaining mana during downtime is already so easy that it's mostly used to say something about your character instead of being a real economic thing. Mana is supposed to be used, not hoarded; game encourages you to spent all your mana during the scene and then just regain it doing whatever when it's not interesting anymore. It's a resource that's interesting within a context of a single encounter, a tactical one; not a long-term strategic resource like Vitae. If mana would be hard to regain, people would just play passively and defensively around it being precious and rare, and that's rarely encourages interesting gameplay patterns. Like, if you can't reliably get your mana back, you will be less likely to blow all your Mana doing stuff, which means you will never encounter a situation where you have interesting choices of "do I risk not using the mana here, or do I go full gonzo and burn it like candles"?

                    EDIT
                    Now I wonder if you can make someone "Dead (Resistant)" using Death Magic.
