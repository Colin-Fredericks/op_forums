            I suppose if you ignore the flavor text, it is left vague, but the flavor text states the following as Mrmdubois has stated:
            By cloaking her subject in Aetheric symbols of victory and indomitability, the mage shields the subject from the effects of hostile magic.

                            The way I read it is that, whether or not W/S is under your spell control, IT Withstands "hostile magic" and not you.

                            Meaning:
                            A) It works on any hostile effect that has you as a subject (hostile being defined by caster intent, common sense and GM fiat)
                            B) Since the spell is withstanding and not you, you can't control whether it withstands a spell (though since it only works on "hostile" magic it really doesn't matter)

                            Examples:
                            A) Your sexy witch/warlock friend is playing wizarding BDSM with you and casts a bashing attack spell. Since the intent is not hostile (the intent is sexy times) the spell is NOT withstood by W/S.
                            B) Your mortal enemy wants to embarrass you by turning your hair pink and, to his chagrin, you really don't mind because you wanted to get it dyed anyway. Regardless, no matter how much you want free pink hair, W/S withstands the spell because the intent is hostile.

                            Regardless, HERE'S THE REAL QUESTION:
                            When someone casts Supernal Veil on you does it work on all active spells that have you as their subject? Or is it useless in that scenario unless you use Scale to cover each spell separately? That's a BFD when it comes to making that spell useful.
