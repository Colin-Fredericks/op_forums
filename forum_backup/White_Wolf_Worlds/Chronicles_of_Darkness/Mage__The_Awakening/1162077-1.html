                        Originally posted by Eunomiac View Post

                        Granted: It is entirely within the purview of the Storyteller to answer these questions, and to then use those answers to inform their response to such a potent working of the Spirit Arcanum.
                        Starting with the basics, everything has a reflection in the Shadow. But a lot of that is going to be dormant or absorbed into a larger aspect (a sheet might be incorporated into a bed spirit).

                        The Shadow is their native habitat, though. They don't belong in the real world. If they come into the real world, they do so in Twilight Form, then potentially Manifest further from there.

                        As for what would? Just like humans, you need a few things when it comes to crossing over. Opportunity and means, as not every spirit is powerful enough to get across on its own or has a locus to access. And motive, as a bed spirit is going to be fairly content as long as someone keeps sleeping in its bed, but an anger spirit is out of 'food' once people stop being angry (this is reflected in the books, conceptual spirits are more likely to cross, because their sources of Essence are more fleeting). There's some more complexity to it, as it takes some drive to cross the Gauntlet, more so than just hunger, I think. Spirits risk destruction when entering the real world, and even if they manage to survive it there can be no coming back (simply because that place a spirit thought wasn't enough might be occupied in your absence).

                        But... where is the guidance to those Storytellers? There is literally *nothing*, and to me, that seems like an obvious gap that needs to be filled.
                        Most of it is in werewolf, as they deal heavily with spirits. There's also the 'blue book' the Book of Spirits.

                            Originally posted by Eunomiac View Post
                            But... where is the guidance to those Storytellers? There is literally *nothing*, and to me, that seems like an obvious gap that needs to be filled.
                            The gap is literally already filled by the facts that these are questions for the Storyteller to answer in this storytelling game that they are notionally acting as a director and mediator for, that a spirit's Rank broadly scales with its significance, and that most spirits that dare to cross the Gauntlet rather than simpy warm themselves at a compatible locus occupy a high-middling stattable Rank rather than the equivalent band occupied in Deathly Twilight by the vast majority of ghosts.

                            Spirits that hang around outside of the Shadow are powerful enough to give mortals significant trouble and harried enough by the way reality works that they need a sponsor or an anchor to stay there long-term. The fact that you've targeted a shopping mall and given it an ample buffer of Scale factors softens some of the blow in terms of being able to leave the spell alone, but "call a large number of entities within a limited area without accounting for those entities not usually being outside their home dimension in force" is one of those things that conceptually kneecaps itself no matter how impressive the spell's numerical ratings are unless the ST throws you a bone or you've knowingly leveraged an unusual circumstance.
