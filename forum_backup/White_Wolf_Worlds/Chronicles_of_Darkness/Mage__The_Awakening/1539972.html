    Reality Stalker 2e Conversion

    08-07-2024, 12:25 AM

    Reality Stalkers
    Sneaks
    Pages 173-176 of The Mysterium and 77 of Nameless and Accursed for Lore


    Parent Path: Mastigos
    Parent Order: The Mysterium
    Prerequisites: Space 2, Mind 1, Larceny 2, one of the following additional skills at 2 dots or higher: Athletics, Streetwise Subterfuge, Survival
    Initiation: Travel through a fracture after having accepted the philosophy and teachings of the legacy.
    Ruling Arcana: Space
    Yantras: Cracked glass (+1); A key (+1); Casting while unseen (+2)
    Oblations: Discovering a secret; Breaking into a building without magic; Traveling long distance without using magic


    Attainments


    First Attainment: Noticing the Cracks

    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The first skill a sneak must learn is to notice the fractures in the fallen world and understand them. This attainment has two functions, the first allows for the mage’s peripheral mage sight to notice all irises, even those that are closed, as if they are an active magical effect, and allows the mages active Space sight to see all irises, including closed ones. The second power gained from this attainment allows the sneak to learn the key of any iris within arms reach after studying it for one turn.


    Second Attainment: Slipping Through the Cracks

    Prerequisites: Space 2, 3 dots in Larceny
    The sneak learns quickly how to get out of traps and prisons, slipping out, or in, as if stepping through the fractures. This attainment functions like the spell “Break Boundary” (Space 2, MtAw 174) with reach assigned to instant casting.

    Optional: Mind 2
    The sneaks have learned the need to protect themselves from being discovered and have developed this attainment to mask their thoughts from those who may be able to pry to learn their intentions. The attainment functions as “Alter Mental Pattern” (Mind 2, MtAw 160) with reach for advanced duration. The sneak must spend one hour in meditation to activate this attainment.



    Third Attainment: Gazing Through the Cracks

    Prerequisites: Space 3, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 3 or a second option from the list must be raised to 2
    Traveling through the fractures of the fallen world can be a dangerous task, and not knowing what you’ll find on the other side of one can result in injury or death, therefore, the sneak learns to see the other side of a fracture so they can determine the safety of crossing through it, or any other preparations that might need to be made. This attainment functions as “Scrying” (Space 2, MtAw 174) with reach assigned for instant casting and sensory range. If used on an iris, the sneak can see to the other side of it.

    Optional: Mind 3
    The sneak knows that attention is something to be avoided, so they learn quickly the practice to have the eyes of others slide off of them. This attainment functions like “Incognito Presence” (Mind 2, MtAw 160) with reach for advanced duration and instant casting. It takes one mana to activate this attainment.


    Fourth Attainment: Using the Cracks

    Prerequisites: Space 4, 4 dots in Larceny
    By this time, the sneak understands how to create small fractures in the lie on their person. Any pocket or container they carry can be widened by manipulating the space within it. This attainment functions as the spell “Optimal Container” (Space 3, SoS 66) with reach for advanced duration and instant casting.


    Optional: Mind 4
    The truth of the matter is, sometimes sneaks get caught, sometimes someone sees through their magic, or discovers what they are planning, or any other number of things that could put the sneak in danger, this attainment was developed for these moments. By touching an individual the sneak can hide the memories away as if they had cast the “Memory Hole” (Mind 2, MtAw 160) spell, with reach for instant casting, sensory range, and advanced duration. Eventually the person will remember, but by then the sneak will be long gone.
    ​


    Fifth Attainment: Crack the Fallen World


    Prerequisites: Space 5, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 4 or a second option from the list must be raised to 3 or a third option must be raised to 2
    The master sneaks, those who have truly internalized the teachings of the legacy, can shatter the space in the fallen world long enough to move great distances, making them nearly impossible to trap, or to keep them from intruding. This attainment functions as “Co-Location” (Space 3, MtAw 176) with reach for instant casting, reach for sympathetic range, and reach to create a portal for the duration of the attainment, which appears as a fracture in the fallen world. This attainment takes one mana to activate.


    Optional: Mind 5
    The final attainment a sneak learns allows for them to turn any item into something that is coveted by others, either to be used as a bargaining tool or a distraction. The attainment functions like the spell “Give Me That” (Mind 3, SoS 66) with reach to make the attainment activate instantly, with advanced duration, and to change the primary faction to potency. The attainment is withstood by composure.

      Hello all. Reality Stalkers, started working on this a long time ago and finally sat down tonight to finish it up and got stuck. You'll notice that the fourth attainment is completely empty, and I didn't really know what I wanted to do for the optional 5th attainment either.

      The philosophy was that the Space attainments interact with the cracks in the fallen world, while the Mind attainments function as a sort of smokescreen, helping the sneak avoid detection as they run around the fallen world, likely ending up in places a lot where they aren't welcome.

      For the fourth attainment, Originally I was going to give them the Break Boundaries spell, but that fely like a placeholder until they got the fifth one, so I wasn't impressed.
      For the fourth optional attainment, I'm playing with either the idea of Imposter, making it so that other people see them as someone else, or Universal Language, which does break my Mind theme of ofuscation and such, but with them running through portals and such, being able to speak with anyone wherever they might end up would be a useful skill.

      So, ideas?

      This is a legacy that one of my PCs is planning to join, eventually.

          I think you could move the order a bit to fix the issue, since Scrying would require you to touch the potentially dangerous thing to create the window. In addition, Space Sight already detects all Irises (core book page 91) and a Knowing spell can uncover the Key.
