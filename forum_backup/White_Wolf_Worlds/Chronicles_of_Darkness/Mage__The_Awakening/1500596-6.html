    Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post
    Congratulations on you daughter's birth !

    Shackle befriending the moonlight rabbit was very cool, perhaps one day they will chat while eating mochi.

    Did the Lunes attack because the Battle of the Three Armies was making a mess ? Was it because those lunatics wanted to join in ? Did they attack the cabal because they caused it ?

    I wonder if Blackeye will try to fight the goetia of his addiction at some point.

    From sidewinder missiles to a Prelate of Mammon and managing his career, Chron is having a very productive day, if a very tense one.

    You know you should worry when Ares, of all people, tells you to control your temper. The Demeter Mystery thickens, striking at an Exarch after infiltrating their Ministry is hugely ambitious, and they need to play it close to the vest while also telling Dio what she needs to know.

    I am getting Ring of the Nibelung vibes from Dio's dream. I am really enjoying how the Dream merit is being portrayed. While the questions are yes or no, the visions and the symbolism that comes with the answers can be very enlightening.

    The Asylum beckons, madness stirs. Looking forward to seeing where it all goes.
    It was nice for Shackle to meet a supernatural entity in an non-violent way for once!

    I could have been clearer, but the Lunes were being commanded by Salut. The Hare says as much to Shackle, and at one point they form a large wheel around her before raining down on the Bentley.

    Blackeye's cold turkey approach is not going to be easy. If they relapse one or two more times, then indeed some more drastic measures will be called for.

    I am indeed going for the Ring of the Nibelung at RPA! None of my players have clocked it yet, but the influence of that particular tale is quite strong there. More on that later. Probably Story 7 or 8.

            12-11-2024, 05:23 PM
            Overall a great wrap up for this year's recap. The solution to protect Steve's failed Awakening was simple, elegant and created way too much trouble for Crassus to deal with without making a scene.

            The initiation for the Illuminated Path was very evocative, revealing the Truth in measures of enlightenment.

            I am happy to see Phemonoe, she is such a cool antagonist. Her entrance was precise, shows she is a deliberate planner and perfectly calculated to unnerve. Yet it also shows her paranoia, indulgence in Time magic and her unsettling comfort with whatever course of history has been determined.

            I wonder if Gearstick is also starting to consider befouling spells. He could wield the Abyss against the Exarchs and go further down the list.

            Aurora is manipulative, vindictive and dangerously good at what she does. Its not surprising even Chron Legacy is wary of her.

            But above all, Voidfisher's death hit like a truck. He might have been paranoid, but he chose to spend the last moments of his life passing on the torch of selfless service. He was a Guardian for all the right reasons, it really is a thankless job.

                                I like Judas Jones, a badass normal that seems to have had a pretty good relationship to the Order. Shackle still has to figure out how to be a Guardian, but hopefully in helping each other they might find a path forward.

                                Between his Order status, personal Resources merit and probably an Order Archive that grants Resources due to Mammon's contributions, Crassus probably doesn't even suffer a dent to his budget for recruiting so many Sleepwalkers. If anything it probably makes him look good to any visiting Seers.

                                The plot with Dio's mom thickens.

                                What Judas Jones said struck a chord. We never have seen Singsong physically outside the tower. During the call with Shackle, it felt like he needed to know the forbidden knowledge, despite the offer to spare him the consequences. Alongside his habit of projecting power, I get the feeling that he works extremely hard to hide the fact that he is a prisoner to the very paranoia he incites in others.

                                The Last Call cabal might just start a supernatural prison break.
