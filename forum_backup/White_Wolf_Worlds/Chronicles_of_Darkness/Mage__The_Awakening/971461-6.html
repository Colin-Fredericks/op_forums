                    Originally posted by Axelgear View Post
                    I, for one, welcome our Awakened overlords.

                    More seriously, Branford, weren't you objecting to the comparison of the Seers to lawyers who take on clients they know are bad recently? Or was that someone else?

                    I did indeed object to broad comparisons of the legal profession itself as stereotypically greedy or evil. However, just like all humans, I would be the first to admit that individual lawyers may have selfish or amoral motives, and the profession certainly provides an ready outlet for the small minority of these individuals. The discussion of criminal defense attorneys, the clear majority of whom are both very liberal, meagerly compensated and represent the poor and needy, is particularly problematic, and what raised my ire in the earlier thread.

                    I also sadly recognize that given the American constitutional and cultural peculiarities of the legal profession, and the perception of attorneys in mass media, even if generally wrong, still sometimes makes lawyers a good example to convey simple propositions. Those making such comparisons should just be careful to clearly define their points and not use stereotypes as fact (just like when discussing any other large and diverse group).

                    In any event. I happen to very much like the Seers as Mage antagonists.

                    They are both unremittingly evil and oppressive, yet entirely relatable and understandable, and thus function as mature and fun villains.

                    Despite lofty rhetoric, it's also easy to see how on an individual level, many arrogant and hubristic Pentacle mages are little to no better that their Seer brethren. For what it's worth, the Seers at least acknowledge their evil.

                        Are Technocrats evil ???

                        Well from my view point, and how I've run them they are the Villain with a point. http://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.ph...llainHasAPoint

                        There was one Technocrat book that started with. You're Welcome. and then took credit for the welcomed changes from the high magic ages. When I read that I was kind of struck with the idea, wait how? what?

                        The idea of an entirely brainwashed conspiracy was a little too much buy in for me. And you could still see the flickers of the Order of Reason ideals in the machine. They were dying but those ideals were there. The spark of rationality is there, glowing but a small ember in a dark place. What did the order of reason fight for? Physics, Science, Man's institutes. The common man against the Wizard's tower, against fae, and against monster lords. In the darkages you would be playing these guys as underdog protagonists.

                        Did they kill free magic? Oh God Yes. But have you seen humanity? I barely trust us with nukes. Humanity needs at the same time not a lot and plenty of pushing to use destructive force. BUT and this is the one thing I can see and reason behind the techs. It's Humanity's choice. No Goblin. No Dragon. No Wizard. No Unicorn. No Gods. No Supermen. No Heroes. No Monsters. Just Human. The reason I can see the techs as at some level agreeable, is that they're humanity's Triumphs and Failings. Technology is the great equalizer.

                        If anything you can draw a direct parallel to the Imperium of Man from 40k. Violently against the Other in space. They are Oppressive yes. but when the other is any number of horrible tortures worse than death. They're the Villain with a point. At the low points, the non enlightened are just people with a day job. You and me at the temp office, pushing papers. Not bad guys just people making ends meet. Do they care about Hal8000 in the upper server room zapping traditions in the cyberworld? well they don't know, can never know and they don't care.

                        Another parallel, The Matrix (I know you're going UGH right now, but bare with me.) Those low level non enlightened personal are all the normies in the Matrix. They're still plugged into our reality. Our being the collective reality. The reality we find rational and understand. I once stop a cabal of mages from raiding a Tech Hospital with a Receptionist named Pam. Good mother if absent a lot of the time, great paying job. I gave her a damn good mind shield pendant. They wouldn't hit her. "There are no good people in the Techys" You don't need brainwashing if you pay your assets well. Is this any different from you and my lives right now? We work to get feed, and enjoy the time we have.

                        To really win the Tradition's War they're going have to dramatically change mankind's outlook. Which isn't going to happen overnight, and probably wouldn't. That's bleak but that's the truth. Mankind can not hold onto a world without tragedy, conflict and pain. We need all these things to understand the world, and survive. Even in peace time we nave tragedy conflict and pain, just closer, and more personal. Utopia is the Dream we'll never collectively catch, and the Darkages were a nightmare everyone forgets was a nightmare. So we're left in this middle ground, but that's not terrible, its actually pretty hopeful. The past was horrible but we moved beyond it, and the future can be so much brighter.

                        And before anyone brings up Room 101. ANY mage with enough Mind has a Room 101.

                        Now you can start to get a little more into the theme in the middle management and higher ups. But at some point they're in the know. something is -not- up. and so there's the question: Do you still believe in the fight? Do the ends justify the means argument. For all the terrible things you had to do or not do. depending on how evil you ran the techs, can you still see the Reason in the machine for continuing? Some people will say Yes. A lot will say No.

                        The two things that make the Tech greater than themselves is the TimeTable and keeping Reality in check. The TT is the schedule they have for mankind's collective enlightenment. At first it sounds like a noble goal, but most of these end games are terrible for everyone, but they're logical scifi conclusions. All metalmen, The matrix(Oh Wait that convention defected and therefore this a good ending now) Imperium of Man, A Brave new World 1984 or whatever dytopian we've got in the works right now. The Syndicate is actually happy about the world state right now. Incidentally I always run the Syndicate as the normalizing force in the Techs and perfectly will to accept the status quo.

                        The Syn believe, and kind of legitimately, that they've already won. The people that want something have to fight or earn it. They look at the 9-5worker who wants to drink his beer and watch his TV, and say well he's happy enough or at least content. They give the golf clap to new awaken mages. "Congratulations Now figure it out." Because to them you've earned your spot utmost the enlightened, and therefore have a right to a say in the bigger picture. They have the right to rebuttal, with guns, but yeah. They maybe greedy bastards, but as long as you're willing to pay for it they're your guy. CoughPaymentIsMagicCough

                        As far keeping reality in check. Well every Tuesday's spaghetti night ... For those who survive Cthulhu rising out of the subway tunnels. As far as the Tech V Trad conflict Those are self interested groups going after the same resources. Also mages be craycray sometimes. Tech vs others, is the Tech v non humans, which was there mainstay.

                        One of the first imagines of the Men in Black was actually as a Paradox effect. It was deeply rooted in the conspiracy of the "Man" taking you down. Men in Top Hats and mirror shades, Take you away because you kept abusing your powers. And they help get the verisimilitude of the setting by scrubbing all the bullshit players pull in games. And by legitimizing these paradox agents, they adopted the Masquerade model of Conspiracies keeping the truth from the world. and because the setting is modern day, we end up in an arms race of bullsht.

                        TLDR: The seers are better written villains because they slowly buy into the brainwashing. While the assumption that all technocratic agents are brainwashed is a little hard to shallow, when they're the Villain with a point.
