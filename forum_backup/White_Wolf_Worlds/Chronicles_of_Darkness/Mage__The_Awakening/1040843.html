                                  I always ruled that Awakenings happen in spite of Fate, Destiny and Time. I like the metaphysical implications (that Awakening is such a great act of magic that it can deny and overrule "certain" Fate and "certain" Future) and I like how it impacts the setting - this way each person might turn from a Sleeper into Awakened in blink of an eye, and it makes spotting new Awakened harder, which in turn is more dramatic; I prefer when Mages find Orders themselves, or are found out only after they had some time on their hands with magic, giving them room to grow bad habits or do Stuff That's A Very Good Idea From Your Perspective But Actually Terrible Idea If You Have Bigger Picture.

                                              The Awakened have rules. If there is no one there to hold your hand when you Awaken, you're going to transgress against these rules. When you buy mice to feed your pet python, you're not going to realize sacrificing rodents to draw mana is going to make you roll your wisdom.

                                              If Fate and Time can't track Awakenings, there is no way for either the Diamond or the Seers to spot newly Awakened mages, which would mean they would have no way of establishing a society of the Awakened. Awakening is hard, it's not like losing your virginity or defending your doctoral thesis. You're entering a new normative system, and if there's no-one there to guide you you're going to do things that fall outside of its rules.
