        And that made it click.

        Immundi are ultimately creatures that embody disharmony and imbalance. They do not care, they only consume: natural Gluttony is all about getting enough resources to survive another winter, have some young, but Infernal Gluttony is about eating to deny others resources or because it is pleasurable. Normal Death recycles minerals, Infernal Death sterilizes the land. They kill for pleasure, not for food.

        But, to get on topic:

        The Numb: A realm that lacks pain completely, and with it, empathy or caution. It invades the world to hurt itself, and so actually get a good sense of what it is and capable of.

        The Bleak: A Depth that lacks Hope. Imagine a constant Lovecraftian story, this is that Earth-looking world. Depending on the inhabitant, it might invade to steal some actual quality of improvement to maintain stasis, or out of spite that even the Fallen World has more positive change than they do.

        The Searing: A realm that is missing darkness, physical or moral. Which sounds nice, except everything from there is blind due to light not impacting anything, and is at the complete mercy of the frequent natural disasters born from interaction with other depths, as it cannot comprehend the self enough for free will or survival instincts. Invaders tend to desire complete purification of the Fallen World, are corrupted by the allure of Vice, or worse, both.

            So while the Lower Depths as whole are not specified around missing an Arcanum, but only certain aspects of reality, here are 11 realms with each being connected to one of the Arcana (and the Abyss) by being defined as lacking one of their aspects (including very rough names and description)-

            The Unwritten- lacks the concept of language and communication. Its denizens are all locked inside their minds, unable to interact with the outside world or each other in any meaningful way. When finding their way to the mortal world, they devour words, speech and communications in order to gain the pleasure of expressing themselves beyond their limited brains, dooming their victims to a hell made out of their own thoughts.

            The Crossroad- lacks the concept of direction. No matter where you go, you get to the same place. No matter which choice you make, you can move on. The demons who lurk in that dread realm are forever lost, unable to make any progress to achieve any goal they had in mind. Those shadow people and black dogs sometimes lost their way into the Fallen, where they consume directions and trap people in labyrinths from which there is no way out.

            The Winter- lacks the concept of warmth. Fire burns in that realm, but it freeze those which touch it. The sun shines, but its light is nothing more than the radiance of snow. In there, frost giants and snow ghosts roam to absorb every bit of heat which somehow fell to that icy realm. When they enter the physical world, the temperature immediately drops. Clouds cover the sun, and frost rise from the ground. Life is devoured by that cold, leaving nothing but frozen corpses.

            The Heresy- lacks the concept of worship. Nothing is holy in that realm, no ruler can tame its eternal chaos and only brute, physical strength is to be respected. Its demonic inhabitants are forever locked in blood struggles, betrayals and murders. When leaving their own private hell, they cause the system of faith to collapse upon themselves. Things which were once sacred lose their meaning, and nihilism celebrates its way to the world.

            The Rot- lacks the concept of healing. Everything in this realm is found in a constant state of decay. Every wound is deadly, every sickness means death. The whole world is a collection of rotten organisms, bound in constant pain as the very land screams in agony. When entering the Fallen, those spirits cause mass plagues and contaminated everything they touch. creating founds of fungi and faceless ooze.

            The Object- lacks the concept of metaphors and allegories. In that realm, what that is is what that is- items exist without any meaning, objects lacks any abstract principles to define them. Imagination is impossible to its denizens, seeing beyond their own world is impossible. That world, in fact, creates an almost identical image of the Fallen, only that nothing has a name, and nothing has a purpose. When entering the physical, those demons suffer from a trauma so great that they fall into a catatonic state. Around them, however, the world lose meaning- things no longer mean anything but their own physical form, and spiritual resonance fades and vanish without context.

            The Formless- lacks the concept of shape. It is an amorphous hell, where nothing has form and everything defies definition. Its natives are beings without form, ephemeral and hollow. No mortal mind can recognize them, for their appearance can not be defined through the sense. When entering the mortal world, everything lose its shape- the states of matter become unstable, the laws of physics shatter and entropy rise out of control while nothing can no longer be defined.

            The Eternal- lacks the concept of ending. Everything which ever started in this world lingers yet, degrading for all eternity without ever knowing rest. Those who inhabit that realm are ancient beings, birthed with the start of the universe, unable to die out, while their youngsters have turned the world to ever crowded. When they escape their damned world, they steal the ending of others, freezing life in the same moment and unable them to die out so they could bargain with that stolen death with others of their kind.

            The Chaos- lacks the concept of roles and purpose. There is no reason in that plane of existence- events happen without any correlation to each other, and there is no connection between cause and effect. The mad beings which lurk in that world exist only in the present, knowing that no matter the past, their future is forever undecided. When they manage to escape their prison, causality and probability go out of control. Grand wins and terrible loses await, the impossible becomes possible and the laws of newton turn null and void.

            The Sands- lacks the concept of memory. In that desert world, no one leaves any legacy behind- no shadows are cast under the sun, the steps are left in the sand, no tombstones are built in respect to the dead. No one remember anything which happened even a moment ago, and once death claims a victim they are being forgotten for all eternity. Those dust demons who roam that world have no history of their own, no stories to tell and no knowledge other than their instincts. Once they enter the Fallen, names degrade into nothing, memories vanish and lost items are never found again, as if they all were dust in the wind.

            The Ladder- lacks the concept of freedom. It is said that this realm was created from the ruins of the Celestial Ladder, as its foundations were cast to the depths of the world. In that realm, there is no such thing as free choice- everyone are born to a certain level of the ladder, and are forever bound to their place. The denizens of that realm are trapped in their position, and are unable to act outside of a very defined role and place in society. Those who are destined to leave their realm feed upon the freedom of others, turning them into nothing more than automata, and create a world where there is only one path open before any outcome, eliminating free will or alternative possibilities.

                    Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post
                    The Eternal seems like a gone horribly right kind of way to achieve lichdom.
                    That what you should think.

                    When I made those sort writeups, I've wanted to emphasis the variance of the collection of worlds commonly known as the Depths. Some of the worlds were created to be directly aggressive toward life (like the Winter, the Rot and the Sand). Others were created to be horrifying, but as a consequence of them lacking a fundamental aspect of existence and not a malignant will (the Unwritten, the Crossroad and the Formless). One of them (the Object) was even made in a way which may generate sympathy toward its denizens (their presence in the Fallen is just as terrible for them as it is for those they feed upon, not to mention that they look perfectly human like- they just can't recognize that "red" means anything other than the color, or that if you call someone "sweet" it doesn't mean you should taste them). And some (the Heresy, the Chaos, the Ladder and the Eternal) were meant to be used- in case you are willing to suffer the consequences.

                    Of those four, the Heresy is in fact the least dangerous one. If you summon one of those demons, the chances are that it would do exactly what you are asking for- destroying a certain system of faith. Sure, it may get out of control and ruin things you didn't want to ruin- but that's the risk at summoning any kind of spiritual being, be it from the Depths or not. The Heresy doesn't cheat or lies- it is brutally honest about what it wants, and if your interests align it would be your greatest ally. The Chaos is more dangerous, for it can only destroy probability- not direct it- making it so it is almost impossible to be controlled, but it doesn't hide the danger, and if someone is foolish enough to summon one of its beings to win the lottery, it's their fault. The Eternal and the Ladder, however, are far more insidious.

                    The Ladder does not looks dangerous at first. Its demons are very obedient, and won't try to trick the summoner- after all, they come from a realm were everything is decided and nothing is left for free will. There is a trick, however- for the thing about the Depth is that they are what they eat. They eat freedom, for they lack it in their system- but they can utilize that stolen freedom for their own needs. Give them enough freedom, and they could even break the spell you used to bind them- and then there is no one in heaven or hell to save you as they drain you into an hollow husk dedicated into "building the Ladder".

                    The Eternal, on the other hand, has dangerous of its own- after all, there is a reason its demons use death as a coin in their realm. When those beings feed, they take away your ending- your whole ending. You will not age for a single day. You cells simply stop dying from this moment forward- but as they don't die, they don't need to reproduce. That means that injuries don't naturally heal. Magic may help, but its help is temporary at best. Every wound you suffer, every scratch or bump or broken nail- it will never heal. Every disease you suffer from won't stop trying to kill you. Every stretched muscle or sunburn would never, every go away. Eventually, body parts start falling apart, as the number of wounds exceed the tolerance of the mortal coil- but it doesn't stop you from feeling them. The pain never goes away. Even if someone where to grind you body or burn you to ash, you would still be alive. You will suffer from all eternity in the form of dust in the wind, unable to communicate with the world, unable to die, bound in pain which would turn your mind into living hell.

                    Don't make bargains with the Depths, children. No matter how nice they look, their number one goal would always be to bite you in the ass.

                        The Painting is a world that lacks a third dimension, it's inhabitants live out lives where lines become insurmountable and their cursed hearts jerk sideways like knives. The demons summoned the the mortal realm exist only in two dimensions and quickly go mad in our world. Unable to relate to something so completely outside their own existence. Going there is a trip to agony and insanity.
                        The Infinite, lacks the concept of limits everything goes on forever without end or reason. When they manage to slip into our reality behind the stars and underneath black holes they steal the limits to make themselves comprehensible if only for a short time and leave whatever they stole in endlessness.
                        The futility is a realm without purpose, everything just exists without any meaning or goal the demons here come to this world to find meaning and reason for their actions, the people and things that lose their purpose quickly fall to pieces or commit suicide.
                        The present lacks the concept of the future. The demons here only do things that make sense in the moment or in the past and as such nothing has changed and they undergo terrible famines every winter that is completely unexpected while revenge seekers drift aimlessly around hoping to find the people who took their loved ones away.
                        The garden lacks the concept of sin, the demons here commit the worst atrocities without remorse as innocent as the newest babe. When the steal the sin from others they either break down into formless sobbing at what they have done and the people who lack sin become the worst monsters imaginable.

                                    The Purged Library: ask their denizens and they will tell you this place lacks stories. Ask the creatures of the Supernal or of other divine realms and they will tell you the same. How misleading. When the hundredth demon says "stories" they don't explain still what that element actually is, leaving one to assume that they are talking about ideas in minds and books. What they really mean is the continuity of time itself. Separating the infinite possibilities into "happened" and "did not happen", and binding different events into a coherent whole.

                                    Just as your arm is a part of you, your past is a part of you too. When your arm is amputated it is no longer a part of you, and the same with your past as this place shreds your time. Past events are loosed into the present as separate entities and phenomena and their marks on their present versions will disappear. Random possibilities from stillborn timelines invade as the boundaries of canon break down.

                                    This world is endless static: which is endless, muted, impotent possibilities. Only able to coalesce as they dissolve our world, then inevitably break down again.
