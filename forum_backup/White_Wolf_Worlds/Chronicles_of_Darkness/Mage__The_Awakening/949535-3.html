        I don't care that much about whether the effects of spells are Lasting or not (although I like the idea that spells are the quicker and easier, but not Lasting, Libertines are such plebs). However I care about whether there is a way, albeit a difficult one, that allows you to have an alloy/amalgam that is Lasting. If you look at the Alloys and Amalgams sections again, it seems to me that magically created alloys and non magical Hermium amalgams are substances with distinct unoverlapping effects. You either non-magically create an amalgam, where you get the combined effects of multiple perfected substances or you use magic to combine them, lose the original properties and gain the new ones.

        Ideal solution would be to either clarify that Amalgams keep the original effects on top of getting the new effect by combining the substances (that would also make them better than conventional spells) and that the spells are simply a quicker and less effective way of doing things (whether you want the spells to be Lasting or not. Although if they aren't, maybe the purists shouldn't be regarded as only doing things out of habit.).

        The other option would be to keep the division, but have the spells keep the Lasting option, so that you can even have Thaumium that is undispellable. These solutions would keep the usefulness of all the methods and you wouldn't have to be pissed off every time an enemy Asshole Obrimos dispells your Dumanium armor.

        Because frankly, stories are full of "breaking through wards", "dispelling spells" and "turning people to their true form" by in some way ending the spell, but I have yet to encounter a story where you "dispel" Excalibur's magical powers and render it to be just a sharp trophy. There may be stories like that, but they really aren't that common. Undispellable Adamantium, Dragonsteel, Starmetal and whatnot are pretty much the standard.

        tl;dr: Let's make sure there is at least one way, by which can any given type of perfected substance or their combination be made Lasting.

            Some quick thoughts:

            * Seems a bit "catalogue-y". Here are the major known perfected forms, here's what they do, here's how you make them . . . Seems to lack that certain element of mystery and wonder. Particularly when a number of the perfected metals have the same basic properties - seems more atmospheric to focus on the exceptions, and the speculation on why they're the exceptions. Might want to look into more poetic/mystical names for the non-metals, too

            * It also seems like there should be cultural/Legacy issues in play. If nothing else, the Elemental Masteries should probably have strong opinions on things like perfected fire and water.

            * Shouldn't items like perfected mirrors pick up more metaphysical properties, too? Like maybe showing the truth of the person's soul (a la the Frog Prince/Hans the Iron Giant), or make it easier to teleport/scry/enter Shadow?

            * As for the Free Council's new way to make perfected metals . . . yeah, I'm not wild about it. The idea that a "quick fix" is better than the traditional way seems odd, particularly given the context. Its not a matter of duration - what bothers me is that the result is equal to doing it the long, slow, "natural" way.

            And there is precedent. If you look at real world efforts to artificially reproduce diamonds, we've had quite a bit of success - from cubic zirconia to industrially manufactured ones. However, people generally feel that even the best synthetic diamonds are somehow inferior to the "real" thing. Even if its purely for cosmetic purposes, an artificial diamond just doesn't measure up.

            Seems like the Free Council's method might be a way to build on that intuition. That the perfected metals their rote produces are somhow inferior - not as high bonuses, missing properties, don't last as long . . .

            Plus, given that the Free Council prides itself on being both the heirs of the ancient Nameless, and the mages who pioneered the search for the new magic, seems like there should be pretty serious internal disputes over whether the classic or modern method is better.

            * Building on the idea of this being the Fallen World, it might be worth playing around with an antagonistic phenomena. Something that actively decays/corrupts perfected elements

            * Not quite sold on the name "Brontium" for perfected copper. My alchemy's a little rusty ( ), so I'm not sure if that's a historical name, but it sounds a bit too much like a scientific element, off the periodic table

            Originally posted by Tauspher View Post
            Also question: why is every way of making perfected blood and flesh should be Falling Wisdom sin? There is willing blood donors and you can probably get surgically removed tumor and perfect it. Bonus points if tumor was cancerous . .
            So then we'd have magical super cancer

            If you're looking for a really nightmarish magical organic artifact, take a look at the Speaking Gun (particularly the version the future Suzy Shooter has) in Simon R. Greene's Nightside series. A weapon created by chopping up some poor bastard, and sewing him back together in the shape of a gun, it can kill anything. And it wants to, too - its in horrible pain, and the only way it can know peace is if the universe is destroyed.
