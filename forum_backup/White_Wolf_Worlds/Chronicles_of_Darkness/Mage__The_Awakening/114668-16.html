All of those would require 3+ Arcana dots and/or Reach
                            Forces 2 to Shield yourself from gravity. Wait for air resistance to slow you down.

                            Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                            I mean, you could tell the wasps to go back to the original spot of the tree and reshape themselves into the tree's normal form, but that carries great risk of Paradox.
                            It doesn't. Paradox is only a risk at the moment you cast the spell. What happens afterward doesn't matter. If it happens in front of Sleepers, there will be what was Disbelief in 1e and will be Dissonance in 2e. Fortunately, you don't care about them destroying your spell, since you're ending it anyway. So pretty much the only effect is they'll edit the weirdness out of their memories later.

                            Also they might freak out and they'll suffer through an Integrity breaking point.

                            Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                            Could I hide such strange evidence with illusions through the Mind Arcanum as I revert the transformations?
                            Could you Veil the transformation so nearby Sleepers won't notice it happening? Sure. Could you hide the evidence afterward so they don't notice that either? Also yes. You have a limited number of spells you can keep active at any one time, though.
