    [2E] Stone Scribes Legacy Update

    01-25-2017, 09:56 AM
    Having joined a pbp game of mage recently I decided it was about time to try and update one of my favorite legacies for the day a couple extra beats find their way into my keeping. I know others have attempted to recreate the legacy before, but I much prefer the Fate-Death combo to Time-Death and have defied the blurb in the core book to keep it that way. Comments, critiques, and suggestions are welcome as I'm still feeling my way around the new system.

    Stone Scribes
    “You will be remembered.”
    It is a commonly accepted truism, even amongst Sleepers, that names have power. For Mages, who can spend their entire lives working to separate the magical and mundane components of their identities, the sovereignty of such appellations can become indisputable fact. For members of the Stone Scribes this knowledge becomes a focal point of their craft, and eventually, a path to mastery. Members of the legacy accept that each individual bears a Sympathetic Name that was given to them early in life and has since become intimately bound into their sense of identity; an identification number given to them by the Exarch overlords of the prison that is the Fallen World, but the Scribes also question if that is the only name an individual may have. They believe that throughout each person’s life their connections with others, and even the nigh-imperceptible fluctuations of their soul, give rise to a different form of identification, a truer name that becomes fixed only upon that person’s death. The Stone Scribes refer to these resonance patterns as Final Names, and they have dedicated themselves to the task of recording and archiving as many of them as possible.
    In their efforts to fulfill the duty of their legacy many Namers become caregivers of one sort or another, seeking employment in hospitals, nursing homes, or even on the field of battle. Once ensconced in these liminal places Scribes can minister to the dying and in the case of some, preserve a fragment of them for all time. Traditionally these records were carved into stone tablets, a habit that led to the development of the Legacy’s name, but in the modern day many different mediums are accepted by Namers. From hand bound manuscripts to freshly burnt CDs or thumb drives, all submissions have a place in the many Bibliotheca that the legacy maintains. While some of the more contemporary members consider it a pipe dream, the Scribes were founded with one clear objective in mind: to uncover ever more ancient Names, employing their arts to reach farther and farther back, until at the pinnacle of their work they can reconstruct the Final Names of those who fell with Atlantis itself.

    Origins
    Parentage: Moros, Mysterium
    Background: For the majority of Namers, death has had a profound influence upon their lives. Some have Awakened because of it, others have had friends, lovers, or parents taken by it, but for all, its passage has been felt and mourned. Those who choose to embrace the path of the Scribes are the ones who after experiencing such loss have decided to accept this unavoidable ending, and rather than rail against it, choose instead to capture some fragment of the departed, preserving a relic of the deceased.
    Appearance: As befitting the seriousness of the task they hold as their solemn duty, most Scribes dress in a somber, elegant fashion as a mark of their reverence for their work. While the specifics vary according to the occupation of the individual, Namers as a whole are prone to choosing solid patterns and muted hues to avoid seeming frivolous or uncaring.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Fate 2, Occult 2, and one of the following at 2 or higher: Empathy, Crafts, Academics, or Expression.
    Initiation: Prospective Namers must study under another member in order to prove their understanding of the legacy’s history, and also to begin training in the subtle observations required to perceive and comprehend a Final Name. Only when the student can perceive and describe (if not transcribe) their own Momentary Name are they deemed ready to be inducted into the deeper mysteries.
    Organization: The vast majority of Stone Scribes are members of the Mysterium, and as such are quite familiar with the hierarchical system employed by their parent order. When multiple Namers reside within a Consilium they will often petition the local Athenaeum for space to be set aside as a Bibliotheca, a library of Final Names that each can contribute to and maintain. Lone Scribes may curate a private collection of names, but are usually expected to provide a copy of their work to the nearest Consillium for safe-keeping. The bonds between mentor and apprentice within the legacy are considered especially important, and it is common practice for the pair to give each other a private appellation for use when they are working together. While seniority within the legacy is the ultimate determining factor in who has the greatest say in decisions for a gathering of their number, those who have amassed the greatest collection of Names, or contributed one of especial age or significance are often granted additional respect.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Fate
    Yantras: Employing a target’s Final Name when casting on them (+2), Including a Momentary Name as a Sympathetic component to symbolize a target (+1), Performing a full numerological assessment of a target before casting (+2), Studying the family tree of a target before casting (+1)
    Oblations: Practicing gematria, meditating upon one’s own Momentary Name, copying Final Names into lasting media, extending a family history back another generation or discovering a new relative on a family tree.

    Attainments
    The Namers’ belief in the power of the Final Name is the guiding principle behind the Legacy’s attainments. Initiates of the Stone Scribes first learn the basic rituals that allow them to inspect then to scribe the Final Name of a person, preserving it for all time. Later, they learn to pull knowledge and connections from Final Names as well as developing the skills they will need to handle ghosts conjured through the Names they gather. Finally, a Scribe masters the greatest attainment of his Legacy, putting to use the names he has recorded by temporarily making them his own, and when needed, calling their owners back.

    First: Name the Echo
    Prerequisites: Fate 1, Initiation
    The Final Names collected by the Scribes are valuable stores of information and discerning the connections that linger in each is the most basic display of skill demanded of each prospective member. By cultivating their knowledge of sympathy and the links that exist between people, a Namer may take a moment to consider the physical repository of a name, and through this scrutiny reveal the sympathetic connections of it’s owner, as well as the existence of a destiny should the bearer have possessed one. This divination does not reveal the names of any people on the other end of the connection, but does provide a sense of the nature of their relationship to the bearer of the name used and a brief glimpse of their appearance as remembered by the originator of the name. Objects that held a sympathetic connection to the bearer of the name are similarly revealed, though the Namer receives an impression of what they meant to the source of the name and a vision of how they were employed instead.
    Optional Arcanum: Death 1
    In order to better understand the names they gather, and to make best use of them, members of the Stone Scribes spend a great deal of time examining the energies and resonances of the soul. Their close study of this subject and their familiarity with how such things change over time and in response to outside stimuli permits them to perceive and analyze the spiritual condition of those around them. This functions as per the Soul Marks spell, requiring an instant of concentration from the Scribe to reveal a number of marks on the target’s soul equal to the Namer's dots in the Death arcanum minus the target's Resolve. If some power has been employed to conceal a target’s spiritual signature, the Scribe automatically achieves successes equal to their Death dots to pierce the veil.


    Second: Memoriam
    Prerequisites: Fate 2, Occult 3
    The foundational power of the Stone Scribes permits them to perceive and transcribe the resonant signature that they refer to as a subjects Name. Throughout an individual's life the interplay of the web of sympathetic connections they generate, their interactions with destiny, and the subtle fluctuations of their soul combine to create an ever shifting pattern of resonance that Stone Scribes refer to as their Momentary Name. This emergent truth can be read by those with the appropriate knowledge and by means of this attainment it can be translated into High Speech and inscribed into a physical medium. While many Scribes prefer to enact this sacred duty with a great deal of ritual, it takes only a instant’s effort and the expenditure of a point of mana to unveil a living target’s Momentary Name so they may then copy it for storage. The stored name remains accurate for a period of 24 hours after that initial revelation, but if the subject of the attainment should die during that time, their resonance becomes fixed and is then referred to as their Final Name, which persists indefinitely. After studying a Name, be it Final or Momentary, a Scribe is considered to have a Medium connection to the person named so long as they continue to hold the physical repository of the record.
    Optional Arcanum: Death 2
    Due to their frequent exposure to the dead, Stone Scribes often encounter ghosts during the day to day execution of their work even without summoning them. This familiarity as well as their deep understanding of death and faith in the proper order of endings can be marshaled as a shield against those who linger, acting as a permanent Ghost Shield effect. Any Death aligned numina, manifestation or influence used against the Namer must achieve more successes on a Clash of Wills than the Scribe has Death dots in order to take effect.

    Third: Unearth the Stone
    Prerequisites: Fate 3, One of Academics, Empathy, Crafts or Expression at 3
    By studying a target's Final or Momentary Name, the Scribe can gain exceptional insight into their nature. The Scribe gains the Informed condition relating to the target, and another instance of the condition for a number of the target's sympathetic connections equal to one less than the caster's Fate dots. If the Namer has first spent a turn uncovering the Named person’s Sympathetic Connections with Name the Echo they may choose which links to become Informed about. This supernatural understanding lingers for up to one day or until used. Resolving these conditions does not grant beats, as per the normal rules for Fate granted Boons.
    Optional Arcanum: Death 3, Crafts 2
    While many Scribes consider the actual process of setting the Final Names they uncover into stone to be a sacred duty, time does not always allow for the such luxuries. To accelerate this task, Namers with sufficient mastery of the Death arcanum can employ their power to erode objects as a craftsman might a chisel. This acts as the Decay spell dealing direct damage to the target’s structure equal to the Scribe’s Death dots. They may choose to degrade the object entirely, or merely to damage the surface in a targeted fashion, carving away material to leave any mark they can imagine. Precise use of this power requires touch, but when attempting to decay a target entirely the Scribe may project the effect in the form of an aimed attack.

    Fourth: Requiem
    Prerequisites: Fate 4, Occult 4
    While it is the duty of every Scribe to record the Names of any who should die in their presence, there are simply too few of them to be present at each and every death. As time passes many great individuals are forgotten and go unrecorded due to this simple fact, and it was an attempt to remedy this failing that produced this attainment. By building sympathy between themselves, and the loved ones or possessions of their desired subject, a Scribe may reconstruct the Name of even the long dead. The Namer must have a Known or better sympathetic connection to a number of people or objects who in turn had Medium or better connections to the person whose Name they wish to recover. The number of connections required is equal to 10 minus the Namer’s Fate dots, and after assembling the necessary links the Scribe must spend a point of mana while ritually casting the numbers to dissect the names of all involved. At the end of this process they can piece together the target’s Name and may record it for posterity.
    Optional Arcanum: Death 4
    While the most ancient spirit’s conjured by a Scribe will often be quite powerful, many ghosts lack the necessary self-awareness to be a reliable source of information. Those skilled in the Death arcanum; however, can reinforce a phantom’s Pattern, strengthening them, and granting them greater latitude of thought. This effect requires a moment of concentration from the Scribe as well as knowledge of the target’s name, be it Final or Sympathetic. The Namer may increase the targeted ghost’s attributes by up to their Death dots, split as they choose, or to heal their corpus by the same amount. If the Scribe elects to raise the phantom’s attributes, the effect lasts for up to a week or until they dismiss it.

    Fifth: Seal the Breath
    Prerequisites: Fate 5, One of Academics, Empathy, Crafts or Expression at 4
    In a demonstration of their true mastery of onomancy a Stone Scribe may assume any Final or Momentary Name in their possession. By invoking the Name and ritually divesting themselves of their own identity, the Namer is able to temporarily mask themselves as the original bearer of the Name. For a period of up to one week from the completion of the ritual the Scribe has full access to the sympathetic connections of the Name they have assumed as though they were their own and additionally their aura, resonance, Virtue, and Vice all appear identical to the person whose Final Name they have taken up. Attempts to pierce this veil provoke a Clash of Wills, on which the Scribe achieves automatic successes equal to their Fate dots.
    Optional Arcanum: Death 5
    With mastery of the Stone Scribe’s arts comes access to one of the most sought after abilities of the legacy: The power to summon and commune with ghosts who have long since departed the mortal coil. While standing in the vicinity of an Iris to the Underworld, the Scribe may perform a ritual invoking the shade of a Final Name in their possession whose Rank is no greater than their Death dots. This rite places the Open condition on the area surrounding the Iris and causes the ghost being called to manifest within it. Once enacted the effects of the ritual linger for up to 24 hours, or until the Namer dismisses the entity.

                  Ah I see what you mean. That take works very nicely, though the temporal symapthy versus normal sympathy thing was part of why I abandoned Time as a viable option. I like the idea of the Name being a construct comprised of all of someone's sympathetic links to others and their destiny enmeshed. Time didn't really work with that for me. Your reasoning for how to make Assuming the Final Name work is actually far better than my working model, but I've chosen to just assume their advanced understanding of the target allows them to fool others in a way that wouldn't normally be possible. It might actually be a sort of complete assumption of the targets destiny with other traits shifting to conform to what the world now sees as 'the proper alignment,' but it's a matter of taste. As promised here's how I spent the Reach:

                  Attainment Breakdown:
                  First - Fate - Knowing practice based on Interconnections with reach for casting speed. I expanded the effect a little to include the visions (ala Oaths Fulfilled), but also limited it to only work with Names.
                  Death - Speak with Dead, reach spent for instant casting, though it now occurs to me that this wouldn't last long enough to be worthwhile. Plus the Death arcana attainment makes it sort of gratuitous. Maybe I'll replace this one with Soul Marks?

                  Second - Fate - It's really just a Knowing/Unveiling effect that allows you to perceive this sympathy/destiny/soul energy signature they call a Name. Actually copying it down is an entirely mundane action you take after having seen the pattern. Based on that you're spending Reach for instant casting and one for Advanced Range. The primary factor would have been Duration so I feel 24 hours is justified. I made it cost mana so people couldn't just throw it at everyone they met freely to get a stock of Momentary Names. You could also explain that as needing to send out a pulse of energy to 'highlight' the target's resonance for a better reading.
                  Death - It's Ghost Shield with Reach spent on Advanced duration. After using your free factors to bump that up you get a 24 hour shield. I honestly hate the time management mini-game that is trying to list all the rituals you do every morning to maintain your defence spells, so I handwave it and say the activation scene is rolled in and make the shield 'always on'.

                  Third - Fate - Exceptional Luck used to grant a condition (Informed). I spent Reach on Advanced Duration and Casting Time while using factors to bump it up to a day. It could have been pushed farther really, but I was happy with it as is.
                  Death - It's Decay with reach for direct Structure damage and instant casting. The range is left at touch/aimed spell and the ability to carve with it is just expanded scope based on the legacy's methods.

                  Fourth - Fate - Honestly I think this is a weird Unveiling practice. You're examining the sympathetic links you gathered to see the 'holes' left behind from where the target would have connected. You then reconstruct the name from that info in what amounts to a very difficult bit of magical math homework. I made it cost a mana because it mimics some of the second attainment, and if I'm being very banal I probably spent Reach on instant casting and advanced duration with the reasoning that the actual activation scene is spent doing the calculations rather than activating the magical bit of the power.
                  Death - Quicken Ghost with reach for instant cast and advanced duration. Factors were spent to boost it to week long duration and I added in the name bit...because I felt like it really. There's no mechanical requirement for it in the base spell, but given the way the legacy works it seemed right.

                  Fifth - Fate - This one is weirder, and I think it is technically a very odd Weaving effect. You're copying the traits of one person and imposing them on yourself. I'm not going to pretend I think all of the effects are actually possible with Fate alone, but it's pretty underwhelming as fifth attainments go so I don't feel too bad about it. Reach would have been spent on Advanced Duration, and any spare change given up in favor of expanded effects. Duration was then bought up with factors so it lasts a week after you go through the ritual.
                  Death - Ghost Summons with Reach spent on Advanced Duration, creating the Open condition, and Underworld access. I've artificially limited the duration because I felt having an uncontrolled ghost stick around for a year was silly.
