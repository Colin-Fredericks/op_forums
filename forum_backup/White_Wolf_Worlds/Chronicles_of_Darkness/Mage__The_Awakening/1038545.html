    Left-Handed and Lex Magica

    01-26-2017, 04:06 PM
    So I have interesting problem – How to trial Left-Handed mages in Consilium system, based on Lex Magica? My London players helped lately to capture ( not simply kill ) cabal of Scelesti Libertines that summoned powerful Abyssal Being and before this group send out on the city powerful Acamoth that almost drive it insane ( King in Yellow ). BUT they did started it out of Obsessions and curiosity of what would seem NOT dangerous Legacy ( Children of the Stars ), and then where overwhelmed by Abyssal entity when they made contact with.

    Tetragrammaton punishments ( see Sanctum & Sigil for this legal system ) seems to not work with this model, as there is not any simple ‘kill’ punish – closest is Spiritual Oblivion, but it’s destroys accused soul, letting him live more as soulless. This does not seems right for Scelesti, as Soulless ends mad in the end, just without magic. Also, it’s said to be marked whole Ruling Council for using this horrible and cruel act as declaring weakness of Council or making precedent to go for war with accused ones allies. (Still, I could see Hierach Civitas in London going for this punishment on his paranoia-level of adhering to ‘older ways’ and disrespect for Free Council mages.)

    On the other hand, less potent punish - Spiritual Scourging – is sending soul of accused to some far away realm. This would seem as reward for Scelesti, when they can get back their souls if their dedicated enough and start to summon Abyss once more, restarting the cycle.

    In this example, even ‘kill’ punishment seems not right, as it simply ends life of Scelesti. They cannot fix nothing, they will not change in any other way and more probable than not – their released souls will make Abyss stronger.

    So my questions are simple – How you, as Ruling Council, would punish Scelesti? Knowing they were half-aware what they were doing? And do effective killing of accused is practiced punishment in Pentacle society?

                              Checked 2E corebook and found sum up on the matter of Left-Handed mages and Lex Magica:

                              Originally posted by MtAw, p. 69
                              Consilia also take action against Left-Handed mages. While the exact definition varies from Consilium to Consilium, a mage is frequently regarded as Left-Handed if her Obsessions or magical practices involve the abuse or destruction of souls, the destruction of the world’s magical potential, routine interference with a Sleeper’s Awakening, the cavalier abuse of Sleepers, Abyssal corruption, contact with the Lower Depths, or the evasion of death by extraordinary and ethically questionable deeds.

                              When a Consilium finds a mage guilty of a crime against the Lex Magica, it levies one of several punishments. Lesser crimes merit public reprimand, payment of debts, or acts of penance on the behalf of the Consilium or wronged party. Serious crimes result in imprisonment, banishment, death, and for the most terrible crimes, death combined with the destruction of the accused’s soul.
