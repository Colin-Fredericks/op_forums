                I have a couple characters that i enjoyed naming mostly because their shadow names fit their character so nicely
                Verit - an FBI agent whose awakening happened in the midst of his agent exam. a Mastigos member of the 11th Question, seeking a sister only he believes to still be alive, and uses his FBI credentials to that end in his free time.
                Aria - a violin prodigy whose awakening involved her moving steadily toward a bright light that became so intense she clawed our her eyes and used the remains to sign her name on her Watchtower, thus leaving even Masters of Life unable to restore her sight. she passes herself off as an Obriomos with strong ties to Mind, but is actually a member of a hidden path she calls the Stygian path (optional lore that doesnt really change her much). she always carries her violin case with her, as she learned mudras associated with the fingerings she uses while she plays, and incorporates magic into her music, imbuing it with Emotional Urging and other spells. her bow and her cane are both dedicated items to act as wands for her casting. in the story she's in right now, she is actually responsible for the Chicago Fires due to being unable to exhibit full control at first.

                        I like Shadownames as part of the Persona the mage shows to other mages - it should be truthful (Prime arcana I´m looking at you!) enough that it is not an actual lie - like just a false name with no significance. symbolism is important - better so if it´s multilayered - that´s why the name of gods, spirits or literary characters are so powerful.

                        In one of my stories i had a guardian who was mostly working for her order as a spy - she went by "Sayeh" (persian for: shadow, also her prefered magic). After her beloved cabal mate died, she left her cabal and named herself "Tanha" (persian for: alone). She also used a complete "persian" persona when she was among mages.

                        I do not titles as names much as they are sometime quite cumbersome.

                        Gamelines: Requiem, Lost, Awakening, Trinity Continuum: Aeon, some V5.
