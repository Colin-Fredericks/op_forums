    Originally posted by Thorbes View Post
    Regarding werewolf, why are you changing the rule instead of using it as is? It does not increase Withstand, it just applies a penalty to the spellcasting roll same as with any other power.
    I'm not thenate, but IIRC the RAI behind Withstand mechanics was that it makes no sense for a subject's natural qualities to penalize spellcasting rolls, because spellcasting is all in the mage's head. Dice penalties to spellcasting thus only represent something that disrupts that inner focus required to build an Imago.

    But Primal Urge is a supernatural quality, not a natural one, so I'd say it's a grey area. I wouldn't mind Primal Urge times two being added to Withstand, even if it makes even only moderately powerful werewolves in Kuruth practically impossible to mind-control. That way mages will have to wait until Kuruth runs out, just like everybody else who has to deal with raging werewolves.

    Of course, all this hangs on the assumption that Kuruth vs. mind-control magic doesn't use Clash of Wills, in which case the penalty becomes much smoother to apply. For me, this trumps the above ruling.

                        Pure Willpower? Absolutely not, I think. Clash of Wills doesn't automatically use the activation dice pool. It's just a coincidence that mages use the same dice pool as their unmodified casting dice pool. A CoW dice pool is supposed to be Power Rating (Arcanum for mages) + Supernatural Advantage (Gnosis for mages). If the power has no rating a fitting Attribute is used instead. Proximi does have ratings on their powers, so the first part would be the dot rating of the Blessing they've cast.
                        As for the second part; that would be up to the individual table. I'm personally in the camp that they simply don't have anything standing in for Supernatural Advantage (as that would make the maximal dice pool 9, including WP expenditure and a Reach for Advanced Potency), but there exists one other precedent in the VtR book Half-Damned. Dhampir substitute Supernatural Advantage with their Resolve.

                        Related to all of this we really, really need official rules or guidelines on handling CoW when not playing a major splat. This has been lacking since GMC Rules Update was first released several years ago.
