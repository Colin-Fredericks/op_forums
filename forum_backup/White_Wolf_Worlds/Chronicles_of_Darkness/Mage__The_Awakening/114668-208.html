                                                    Originally posted by rwknoll View Post
                                                    How does lucid dreaming work? I know the Mage 2E book has rules for how to start a lucid dream, but what about creating or changing the dream state once you're in it? Rolls and mechanics are probably not needed in most situations where someone is in their own dream, but what if a Mage enters your lucid dream using Mind magic?

                                                    Would Clash of Wills be the primary mechanic to help you resolve issues here? Or maybe contested Resolve + Composure rolls?

                                                    I'm thinking like if you wanted to go into someone's dream to scare or torture them, for example, and try to will them into being tied up or in some kind of danger. Can you do that? How does the person fight back?
                                                    Using the spell Dream Reaching (with the Reach to be a part of the dream) the mage would interact with the dream as if it was physical (albeit by using dream form rules), and the ST should determine whether or not those actions are enough to change the themes of the current dream. You can't just will their dream into anything, but by directly interacting with the dream you can affect their dream self in ways that can change how they dream. Like killing one of the dream actors in front of their eyes will most likely turn a happy dream to a dream of fear and possibly grief. But it might also wake them if it's too jarring.

                                                    The easier way would be to just use the spell Dream of the Just to directly control the dreamscape they're in. That way you can scare them, torture them, and do whatever you like without risking that they wake up.

                                                    Clash of Wills only apply when two conflicting supernatural powers comes up against each other. Unless the subject has a spell or power that protects their dreams and the mage is using a spell to affect their dream there'd be no Clash of Wills roll.
