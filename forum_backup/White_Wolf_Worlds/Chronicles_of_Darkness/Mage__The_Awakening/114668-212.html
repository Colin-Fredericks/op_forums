                I need some help to understand better some aspects of sympathy link. The book says: In case of a mage using a sympathetic link to cast a spell in a subject the spell is Withstood by Space itself, but in some cases is easer to cast a spell using sympathetic link instend to do it in the subject directly. E.g.: a subject has stamina 4 and a weak link with the mage, if the mage try to cast a spell to turn him in a pig this spell will be Withstood by his stamina in this case 4, but if the mage use a sympathetic link the spell will be Withstood by the sympathetic link in this case weak so 3.

                Is it right?

                    05-08-2017, 02:34 PM
                    When you're faced with multiple Withstand ratings (which can happen in a few ways, including casting sympathetically or casting on someone who's subject to the spell Wards and Signs) you simply choose the highest rating and then add one to that per additional rating.
                    In your example Withstand would be 5 since 4 is the highest and you add 1 because you have the sympathetic Withstand.

                    Edit: It's all described in the section "Withstanding" on pages 114-115. Casting sympathetically is not meant to be easier. In fact, it's always more difficult unless you're planning on bypassing Withstand entirely by scoring an Exceptional Success on the casting roll.

                                        Originally posted by Horodrigo View Post
                                        The 2nd edition merit Adamant Hand functions how?
                                        Is it like, you add your skill dots on Brawl as a Yantra for spells regardless of it being a Rote Spell?
                                        If Brawl is your Adamant Hand skill then when you make a Brawl attack it can also count as a +1 Yantra on a spell you cast the next turn, and you can do this a number of times equal to your Yantra slots. So if you have Gnosis 3, you could hit a bad guy 3 times with Brawl (The number of Yantra slots you have) and receive +3 Yantra dice to a spell you cast on the fourth turn. Essentially, attacking things boosts your spell's power.
