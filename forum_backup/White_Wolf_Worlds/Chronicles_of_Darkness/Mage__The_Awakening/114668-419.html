            Originally posted by Prometheus View Post
            2 questions:

            1. Can you have multiple instances of the Familiar or Supernal Watcher Merits?
            2. The Legacy +1 Yantras take a turn to use. Does that mean they have to take at least a turn like the High Speech Yantra? Or can they be used reflexively if they are the first to be used like other Yantras that take a turn to use?
            1- I don't see why not. If your mage wants to deal with more ephemeral entities, then its fine. The Familiar bond can be severed anytime, so its all about how interested the being is in obeying.

            Are you helping a ghost tie up some loose ends, figure out why they can't move on, finish their life's work, find their last descendants to give them something ? Is being around you saving a spirit from being hunted by the Seers (like a Magath whose domain undermines them), is it simply eating very well by hanging around with your character or are they your allies in some grander plan of Shadow politics? Is the goetia mentoring you in a skill ? Is it a part of someone that showed up in your soul and you are working to figure out why ? If the context makes sense, go for it.

            Supernal Watcher could represent entities that teach you a unique skillset. For example, one for supernatural smithing techniques and one for alchemy, or one for Abyssal exorcism and one for tracking Scelesti. The main problem is that they will watch, judge and possibly interfere, perhaps they could have contradictory goals. But its another way to flesh out your character's themes and create plot hooks, which sounds pretty good to me.

            2- Same as always, first non-mantra use of a yantra is free, subsequent ones require an action.

                                                            Originally posted by Khalendas View Post

                                                            Thanks, the Magath possibility is something I did not think about, and is quite interesting.actually. But shouldn't it be specific for Spirit eating other spirits only?
                                                            Technically yes, but the Realms Invisible are weird and unusual interactions are bound to happen. One Twilight could have doors to another, one Realm leading into the other. A door in the Underworld could lead to the Astral. Does that mean you can bodily enter the collective unconscious ? Yep, maybe even take things out to the material. How that works in that instance is a Mystery.

                                                            So maybe the old pier throws all active beings from Death Twilight to the Shadow during the day and to the Death one during the night. The desperate ghost of the sea captain eats the ocean spirit in a gamble to avoid the Great Below and becomes a Magath of death at sea. Now its up to the confused mages/geists/werewolves to figure this situation out.

                                                            Perhaps some medicine spirits found an aisle in their Shadow hospital that lead to the Underworld of bubonic plague victims, but couldn't find their way out. Running low on Essence, they devoured the ghosts that represented the affliction, becoming Magath of parasitic healing. Now you have all you need to make your own version of the Valley of Defilement from Demons' Souls. The bosses are the highest ranking spirits and everything else is a warped and devoted ghost. Perhaps you could even make the holy weapons into famous imbued items or Artifacts that setup another plot hook.
