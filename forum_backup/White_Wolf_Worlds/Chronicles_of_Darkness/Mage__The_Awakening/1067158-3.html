                                                        Originally posted by Michael View Post

                                                        I dunno about Dave's official version, but I don't really see it. I mean, unlike the Diamond Orders, the FC has genuine ambitions to replace the consilia system. That directly flys in the face of the SL who effectively run that system.

                                                        Personally, the SL inviting the FC to join the Pentacle looks a lot less like an idealistic move, and more like a clever co-option.
                                                        Yeah but hey, at least they care. Like, it's a common fallacy in political science conversations that everyone falls on the political spectrum; some people literally don't give a shit. The Silver Ladder (as a generalised organisational view) spends all it's time saying "fight the Lie! The Lie is shit and the Seers are total bastards! Achieve enlightenment! Grasp ascension! Let us unify and be free!" and the Mysterium, Guardians and Arrow are all varying degrees of "that's not really our core philosophy but sure yeah I guess, if I'm free."

                                                        The Free Council enthusiastically say the same thing. They disagree how to do it, they disagree on how to organise Mage society and how to inspire Sleepers etc, but their core ideas are all about fighting the Lie, freeing Sleepers from Quiescience, overcoming the Seers and their Exarch masters, giving Mages a voice and a chance to fulfill their potential etc. That's the whole deal with the Great Refusal.

                                                        So while the Free Council and the Silver Ladder might have a number of ostensible idealogical differences, and a number of ostensible practical ones too, in reality for a lot of Silver Ladder and Free Council Mages they find themselves caring about the same things. And while the Ladder says "look at this cultural Magic, isn't it cool how they managed to capture a fragment of the Supernal Truth!" and the Council says "no, the Supernal Truth is created by Cultural Magic!" in reality they're both kind of right, and both are actually interested in trying to help Sleepers reach the Supernal, just via different structural systems.

                                                        The fact that the Assembly is posited as an alternative is something that threatens the Ladder's position as the Order with the most political sway, but at the same time, the Concilium system works in ways the Assembly system doesn't, and it's more likely that they'll find a degree of balance based on the location rather than anything else. Remember, the strength of the Consilium is that it accepts that Mages are not all equal and powerful Mages at the top ensure a society which is ordered enough for people to live in, and even the Free Council sometimes have to accept that.
