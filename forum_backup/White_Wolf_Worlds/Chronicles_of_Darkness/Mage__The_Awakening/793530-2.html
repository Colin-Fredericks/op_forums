                For the Mastigos slot, I am torn between Clavicularius/Bene Ashmedai and Reality Stalkers. The former tap into some very powerful themes and the 2nd Edition mechanics could really give them a chance to shine. The latter provide not only a less standard (not Mind based) view on the Path, but also an obvious seedy underside to the Mysterium. Their actions comprise everything from, on one end - spying, smuggling and theft, on the other - pocket dimensions and Abyssal threats. Also, the Ladder slot is already taken.

                For Thyrsus/Free Council - Dreamspeakers. The Legacy was written up before Astral Realms was released. Writing them again with new material in mind is a challenging undertaking, but definitely worth the effort. Also, they are a perfect fit for the Order.

                For Obrimos/Adamantine Arrow - Tamers of Fire. Mostly due to what LokiRavenSpeak said.

                                    I'm coming out of lurking to weigh in on this subject just because it is so dear to my heart.

                                    First off, I'm surprised that there's only one vote for the Daksha. Everyone should vote for the Daksha. The Daksha are the best worst mages ever. Vote for the Daksha.

                                    Secondly, as much as I love the Clavicularii/Bene Ashmedai, including them would kind of limit choices to the other two Paths if you want to also include all the Orders, as you're now stuck with two Silver Ladder Legacies (which is never actually a bad thing).

                                    Thirdly, and call me crazy for saying this, but why not make totally new Legacies? You can get whatever matchups you want to fit the whole 5 Paths and 5 Orders thing, and you give the readers a totally new treat instead of just a revision (also not actually a bad thing - especially if the Daksha are involved). Another choice could be to detail one/a few of the Legacies that were only mentioned in various books but never fully written up (like the Haruspices and the Hotspur Brigade).

                                    In the end, though, if I had to make three choices from the existing Legacies, I'd go for the Daksha, the Brotherhood of the Demon Wind, and the Dreamspeakers. Alternatively, I could go with the Whipping Boys (which would now be open to the Arrow as well) and the Sphinxes for the former two instead.

                                    Daksha.
