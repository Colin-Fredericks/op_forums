            Chapter 56
            6/27/2025

            Ding Ding! This Chapter began with the ringing of shop bells as the door to Otakus Anonymous opened and Johnny <Eye of God> and Brandon stepped through together for the first time. Brandon was proud of what he found, bragging to his friend about what a great place he found. Johnny was pulled in, to the fresh high school graduate, this place looked like heaven. A woman with a name badge that read “Hi! I’m Jaquoline,” approached them to see if they needed anything. They both said hello, while Brandon spoke to her Johnny explored, quickly finding Fullmetal Alchemist manga and looking through them. Brandon came over to tell him that the store was hiring, and the two friends resolved to work here together as a way of continuing their friendship forever into the future.

            <Then I rolled a one.> The sword of the mage sliced across Eye of God’s chest, cutting deep and, while missing his heart, drew enough blood to end him. He fell into Giger’s arms where they had only a moment to speak before Eye of God bled out; during this time they had Eye of God ask Giger to look after Jessie, to take care of her and make sure she doesn’t fall into the “darker side” of anime. With his last request finalized he bled out.

            Finally Giger was able to bring Maxwell back with Time, returning him to how he was before their trip into the Underworld. While he acted fast, using Gravity Supremacy in an attempt to pull the duel wielder back towards them, for he had started going after Ragnar once he finished Eye of God. it wasn't enough and Abraham de Moivre used Temporal Stutter to remove the living mages and Moon's Tears from time. Just before the spell ended, the mage who had killed Eye of God smiled at Giger, knowing.

            Ragnar woke up to find a man he did not recognize standing over him, an open portal behind him leading to a library he didn't recognize. The man is enraged, telling Ragnar to get his shit together and protect the Codex if he insisted on carrying it around, telling him he had protected him and it from the mages he faced, but he didn't have time to baby sit. <I probably should have let the mages get the Codex from Raganr, but Eye of God dying felt like enough consequences here.> A moment later two First Talons appeared, Paladin and Lacuna. They asked where his cabal was and he took them back to the fight. Several active spells were going on. The three warriors prepared themselves for a potential battle and Paladin dispelled the Time Stutter spell, bringing everyone back but Eye of God. Ragnar broke down when he learned of his cabal mate's death, and while the First Talons tried to get information about what had happened, Giger and Maxwell instead looked for their friend's body. Giger looked into the past to see his body vanish, a clear sign that someone veiled against Time magic also veiled the body before moving it. Maxwell discovered the body snatcher's nimbus to be a purple sun whose rays turned into a hypnotic circle around the sun, but none of the present mages knew who the nimbus belonged to. Moon’s Tears wanted to continue the hunt but Maxwell talked her out of it, she wasn't happy though, citing consequences.

            The mages returned to the physical world and left to their different order headquarters to be healed. While there, Uriel offered her condolences to Maxwell and invited him to St. Leon Armenian Cathedral once he was ready. When he said he wasn't feeling religious, she insisted it was about what happened in the Shadow and what is happening in Los Angeles with the Abyss, not religion. Giger was found by Delver who asked about Eye of God. When he discovered he had been killed and his body missing, he asked for his permission to join the cabal to help uncover what happened and get revenge for Eye of God, Giger agreed and the mages all met at Delver's mansion. <A reminder that Delver is Mark Slim III post Moros Awakening and is now being played by Eye of God's player.> The cabal played pickleball to try and distract at Delver's instance but it didn't help. After losing Ragnar called Vulcan, who offered her condolences, and asked about any Death masters she knew. Immediately she knew what this was about and warned against trying to resurrect Eye of God, but he insisted on talking to a Master. She promised to find him someone to talk to and sent him to see a Moros of the Silver Ladder, Alpha Centauri. He agreed to meet them that evening in his cabal's sanctum that evening which was hidden inside Griffith Observatory. He insisted that resurrection was risky being they wouldn't need a soul to fully restore himself, but Ragnar insisted on trying. Alpha Centauri told him he would look into potential Shades that could be summoned but that they would first need a body, which was missing.

            In order to find the body the cabal went to Eye of God's apartment to get his computer and find a Space mage to use the sympathy to him to find his body. While there, Jessie wanted to know what happened. Raganr explained to her that he failed to protect him and he was killed. This caused her to have a breakdown, where she claimed herself for not protecting him, sliding into self loathing in front of everyone and then discharged a blast of electricity that harmed the entire cabal before she fled.

            <Me and Eye of God’s players spent the week talking about all possible outcomes. Does he want to die, live, or let the dice determine the outcome? If he doesn’t die what consequences does he want from this scene (as there should be some no matter what, even if death isn’t the end.) If he does die, who does he want to play? I think we came to some pretty good decisions along the way and I am excited to see where this goes. Ragnar spent most of the session reading through the core book looking for ways to resurrect Eye of God.>

                                                Chapter 57
                                                7/25/2025

                                                <Very Short session that basically revolved around Maxwell trying to find out how to track down Abraham.>

                                                Ragnar got a phone call from Vulcan, asking him to meet her at her sanctum. Once there she told him about Mictlan, a Dead Dominion that her cabal was trying to find. If they did, they might find a way to bring Eye of God back that way, according to the legends. <I had forgotten this was the entire point of the Lantern Keepers as a cabal, but forgot to bring it up when Ragnar asked Vulcan about resurrection in the last session.> Ragnar then did some research in the Adamantine Arrow library and discovered the Aeons, specifically Typhon, who may be able to grant the ability for a true resurrection if Ragnar made it to his dominion. Ragnar placed two different soul jars at two different retirement homes.

                                                Maxwell tried to do his own research but no one in the Mysterium was willing to babysit him, so instead he went to the St Leon Cathedral in Glendale to see what Uriel had offered to show him. She was there waiting and his peripheral mage sight started aching. He opened his sight to see the cathedral change into a massive golden cathedral that disappeared into the clouds above, and he felt certain that the structure went all the way to the Aether. Angels dressed in white robes came and went, carrying scrolls with them, sealed as they went, and open as they returned. Some had trumpets. Uriel welcomed him, apparently knowing via Time magic he would be arriving today, and took him inside. Inside the building normal mortals went about their business, saying prayers in corners of the building or working, while smaller angels floated through the halls singing. Uriel explained that this was an Aedes known as The Cathedral and existed to help Obrimos like themselves to understand their own paths in life. The angels that could be summoned from here could help a mage understand their own fate and destiny. Inside the chapel there was an angel of pure gold sitting atop a throne with a golden staff in hand. Behind the chapel were two more mages, Paladin the Arrow that had arrived to save them in the Shadow, and a Guardian of the Veil. A Hallow existed in this space as well, and a curtain hung from the wall, blocking something off from view that Uriel explained as “an artifact that assists the Choir. Paladin and Uriel explained to Maxwell they had learned of him through this artifact and the aedes, and wanted to invite him to join the Choir of Hashmallim, a legacy of Obrimos that specializes in the destruction of the Abyss. When he asked how they did this Paladin gave a nod to Uriel, who summoned an angel, which they called an avatar. The angel wished to battle Abyssal manifestations on the spot, and Uriel apologized that there were none to face, allowing it to return to the Supernal. Maxwell agreed to join and Paladin explained that he would need to locate an Abyssal manifestation to destroy and then Uriel would assist him in summoning his own avatar, together Maxwell and the avatar would destroy the manifestation and then the avatar itself would induct Maxwell into the legacy. Maxwell decided he would summon an angel from the aedes that evening with his cabal to learn about Abraham and where he would have him.

                                                The summoning was successful and each mage had to give mana to the angel in order to read from a scroll she had in her hand; Maxwell had to steal mana from the Choir's hallow to have enough, but once ten mana was given the scroll was offered and Maxwell took it. He wanted to know where he would find Eye of God, and the scroll showed him his cabal maye surrounded by demons and their allies <Abyssal manifestation and scelesti>. When he asked where he'd find Abraham he saw him in three locations, beneath the Redondo Pier with the mages he had been with in the Shadow, in the Underworld next to Hel, and again next to Hel in the Astral. He saw the same locations when he asked where he'd kill Abraham. Then he asked about the God-Killer Sword and how he'd use it and learned it had three abilities; striking anything in Twilight, it could grant him information about gods <Rank 4-5 ephemeral entities and Supernals> and granted him heightened defenses against their magic while also allowing him to strike their magic with the sword to dispel it.

                                                <We ended kinda abruptly here because everyone had had a long week and it felt like a good place to end. It is important to note that when Maxwell saw Hel in the Astral and the Underworld he realized it was the same entity in both places. Also, I forgot when it happened in the timeline but Delver used Mind magic to help Ragnar recall the conversation he had in the Astral with the creature living in the Realm of the legacy he has joined; “Finally someone has come after all this time, now the master's work can continue.”>

                                                    Chapter 58
                                                    8/01/2025

                                                    <Ragnar was not present for this chapter. Therefore some things happened I was planning on waiting to do for a bit longer for - so that Maxwell could control Abraham - but without Ragnar they didn't want to go down that path right now.>

                                                    The cabal had information on Eye of God, and had his computer in order to pursue the sympathetic connections to their old cabal mate. They gathered in Giger's apartment and contacted Blood, who agreed to help them. Blood arrived quickly and worked his Space magic to connect a portal to Eye of God, who was laying, arms folded over his chest, on an altar in a church. Maxwell expected a trap or a fight due to what he learned from the Angel but after investing through the portal, and then stepping through said portal and investigating more, nothing seemed off other than the body being laid out in public the way it was. Still not fully trusting it, Maxwell used Gravity Supremacy to pull the body through the portal. Blood apologized for the loss of their friend and left, leaving the cabal to hide Eye of God's corpse in Giger's apartment.

                                                    As the cabal discussed what to do next a new portal opened, this time a man in pure white armor with ruby trim that came together to make a rose stepped through, wielding a large shield and a halberd, both made of the same white metal his armor was. The man braved himself to charge, pointing the tip of the halberd at Giger and demanding Giger declare his allegiance. Giger declared he was a member of the Mysterium and made the sign of a member who had passed through the first mystery. This satisfied the man, who removed his helmet to reveal he was Giger <from this point on White Knight Giger>. White Knight Giger was relieved, as he did not want to have to kill another Giger, but was certain that the Giger of this timeline would have become a Scelestus, given the appearance of the Clocktower in the sky and the fact Time was ending here. White Knight Giger told Giger a third would reach out to him in the near future to help direct the effort to save Time here and left through the portal he had come through. Delver and Giger both noticed that the other side of the portal seemed to be in a desert with giant bronze hourglasses resting in it.

                                                    While Giger was excited to meet other versions of himself he recalled Eye of God and the promise he made before he died, so being they did not have a soul yet, he used his Fate magic to cause him to find Jessie so he could help protect her. As he and the others wandered Los Angeles they came across her in an alley, being handed a slip of paper by a man they had never seen, who then transformed into a dove to fly away. They approached cautiously, remembering her electrical burst she had given off last time, but she seemed more like her old self, although angrier. Maxwell asked who the man was but at first she refused to tell them. Maxwell and Giger explained they had Eye of God’s body and were close to resurrecting him, revealing they had hid the body in Giger's apartment. Jessie had no interest in seeing it. When confronted on this she admitted that the slip she had been given was the location of someone like her, a promethean, who could teach her to be stronger, and until she was strong enough to have been able to protect Eye of God she couldn't bring herself to see him, dead or alive. The cabal understood and let her go.

                                                    The final scene saw Giger and Maxwell drinking in Eye of God's honor. Giger paid the bar owner to make a drink named after his friend and together they drank till past midnight. As they were leaving with Leopold driving away, a homeless man knocked on the window. Giger gave the man some water and money and then noticed an old man standing across the street. The man approached and told him he wanted to speak, but not until Giger was sober. Giger rolled up the window and used his Time magic to bring up the version of himself who had not yet gone drinking and used Prime magic to learn the man was a mage, with mastery over Time and Fate, adepthood over Matter Life as well as his weakest arcana. Giger invited him in where Maxwell accused him of being another Giger. The man seemed sad looking at Maxwell, and confirmed he had once used that shadow name but not for a long time. Giger asked what he went by now and he just said “Ted.” Ted confirmed that he was here trying to save all of Time from the Clocktower and needed Giger’s help, that this was more important than resurrecting Eye of God. Giger asked if Eye of God had died in his timeline but he hadn't, it had been Maxwell who was executed for falling to rapture while hunting Clausius who had died, and he tried to save him but failed. Giger agreed to help Ted, and was given a task. He needed to find the Seer known as Conquest. She was a servant of the General who had already been hunting Scelesti in Los Angeles but Ted believed she would get in the way of his efforts, so told Giger and Maxwell to direct her focus to Jötunn, who was the mage hunting Ragnar. He further revealed that she had no sleeper name or past, having burnt it up upon awakening, she was now just a tool for the will of the General. The cabal agreed and asked how to contact him to confirm they had succeeded but Ted didn't believe it would be necessary, saying he would know.

                                                            Chapter 59
                                                            8/15/2025

                                                            <Ragnar was not around for this chapter.>

                                                            The cabal began their search for Conquest by researching her lord, The General. Maxwell was not allowed into the Athenaeum but Delver and Giger where they learned the general <lol> information about the Exarch of Forces. Once they had their information Giger cast a Fate spell to cause him to meet Conquest, but it was dispelled after it had been cast. He cast it again, hiding it this time by combining it with Prime magic and this time it was not dispelled. <Giger got an exceptional success on this second roll, and I allowed him to put the charmed condition on Conquest for when they would meet, which will come up later.> Leopold then drove the three mages around Los Angeles in no meaningful direction. The spell ended after an hour. Looking around them they were in an old warehouse district. They decided to search the nearest warehouse but found nothing of interest.

                                                            It was the second building where Delver heard the sounds of footsteps on a higher floor, along with voices and something that sounded violent. They crept together to the higher floor where they witnessed a woman using her fists on a man’s skull, destroying everything from the jaw up. She seemed to take no pleasure or displeasure from it. When the work was done she calmly walked to a nearby skink to wash the blood off of her hands, and a robed figure provided a towel to dry her hands on. Only after her hands were dried did she acknowledge the cabal, opening the conversation with a question, “Are you Scelesti?” When they answered that they were not she asked if they were the ones looking for her, and Giger confirmed they were. Giger immediately opened with the threat of Jötunn and told Conquest that he thought he would be a worthy adversary. She was uninterested in that though. Giger tried several angles but she took no interest in hunting a powerful Scelestus on behalf of the cabal. Giger then asked who she had killed, but Conquest didn’t know, just a Scelesti. Giger used Time magic to watch her kill the man. He realized this wasn’t an interrogation, she didn’t ask questions or use magic to learn about him, she just tied him to the chair and killed him. Delver used Mind magic on her to learn her aspirations and obsessions during the conversation; “Mystery Command (Kill Scelesti), Martial Arts, Enforcing the Symbols of the General, Life Patterning to Mana,” were her obsessions, with “Maintain her position in the Iron Pyramid” and “find an apprentice,” being her aspirations. Delver asked if they could have a moment to talk, which Conquest was fine giving. Before she let Giger go she asked if the cabal needed the corpse, Giger said he’d take it, but as he went to his cabal she used Forces magic to turn the body to ash, dusted off the chair, and sat waiting. Delver passed on the information he gained and Giger went back to talk to her.

                                                            Even with the new information, especially using the fact she wanted to keep her position in the Iron Pyramid, he couldn’t convince her. Finally she announced she had no interest in running errands for the Pentacle and killed the robed man she had with her. His body split in two at his death, which opened a portal that she stepped through. Giger, with his cabal, followed her to a land that was covered with supernatural darkness, streams of blood ran over the ground. There was some torchlight that lit where they arrived, but beyond that was pitch black. The portal closed behind them and Conquest was pissed, showing emotion for the first time, to see them. She used magic and then told Giger to use his Time arcana to return before they were killed. He refused until she agreed to help them with Jötunn, so she asked Giger who he cared for more, and then broke Maxwell’s nose with a punch. Giger agreed, and went back a few moments, just in time to see her murder the robed man and open the portal again. Once back Giger lied to her, telling her he had just come from a future where Jötunn had destroyed her and the entire Pyramid and while she seemed reluctant, he could tell she believed him, but to his frustration, she left before agreeing to help them.

                                                            <Technically I think she should have killed them all for following, it made more sense to me given where they followed her to, but I decided to let them live because I wanted to game to go on, and the Charmed condition seemed like a good reason to justify her not ending them on the spot.>
