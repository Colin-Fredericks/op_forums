                            Originally posted by GreatHornedRatkin View Post
                            I find that a tad hard to believe, with how large and widespread the Cryptid, UFO and Ghost Hunter communities have shown to be. They hold national conventions for this kind of stuff.
                            And those communities are broadly considered to be harmless eccentrics at best, because learning the things they believe in are actually factually real tends to shake the worldview and self-concept of most people brought up in the setting.

                            Second Edition's switch from Morality to Integrity as the root human "code" means even nonviolent, nonvisceral expressions of the supernatural risk degrading one's confidence in who they are and what they've learned all their life, which combines with the way Awakening works and the Awakened being the metaphysical 1% to explain where certain elements of mainstream mage society keep their contempt for other people's spiritual integrity in an edition where that's no longer described purely in terms of what crimes they can commit without internal consequence.

                            I will add that the lack of explicit "you assume Hishu form when you die" note is supplemented by the fact that this edition also makes Gauru the most expedient way for a werewolf to avoid dying and the most consistent way to enter Hard Rage (locking you into Gauru for what is likely to be at least an appreciable fraction of an hour) is to spend too long in Gauru (like, say, relying on its accelerated regeneration to stave off death in a protracted violent encounter with something that can't be frightened off by the powerful Lunacy pouring off of it). I personally wouldn't assume a werewolf killed in Gauru form stays in Gauru form when their Death Rage is cut short.
