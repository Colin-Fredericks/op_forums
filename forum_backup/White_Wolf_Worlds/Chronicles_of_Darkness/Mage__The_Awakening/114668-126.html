                                        12-11-2016, 11:32 PM
                                        Originally posted by HerbertIsBestBert View Post
                                        Are there any limitations as to what abilities an Ephermal entity can use to influence the physical world while in a state of twilight?
                                        I would imagine Influences can be used, but what about Numina? Especially stuff as potentially dangerous as Blast or Drain.

                                        Can Mages also cast Magic at Twilight entities, and out of Twilight at physical entities at will? Or does it require a conjunctional Arcana on the relevant frequency?
                                        'Entities in Twilight can only attack or be attacked by other entities in the same phase of Twilight, or beings that are able to touch them through magic or other supernatural means (unless the attack utilizes the entity's bane).'

                                        Regarding the first part of your question, only if they see them, with certain exceptions. Something like Awe requires the ephemeral to be seen, so not unless they're able to see you in Twilight. Blast is an attack, so no. But Left-Handed Spanner allows you to just superimpose your Twilight form over the target.

                                        For the second part, you need to be either in that Twilight state, or have a power that lets you interact with them despite it. Spirit and Death, for instance, have powers that let you interact with and see ghosts despite not being in Twilight. Some of these powers last on a duration so don't require other spells used against ephemerals to be conjunctional, but if you're in a hurry and don't have anything set up like that yes you would have to use multiple arcana. A key exception is banes. A Matter mage can fill a room with knives and it won't harm an entity in Twilight, but if that entity has knives as a bane it will affect them.
