    Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post
    So, I just reread the bit on palimpsest, and it specifically says that the scraps of previous rites in the palimpsest that creates those extra magical effects are Mysteries with Opacity. The significance of this is that yes, a Mage can determine exactly what a spell out of a palimpsest will do before casting it.
    Compared two pieces of text about Palimpsests and they are a bit contradicting themselves here - before there is:

    'A mage can glean scraps of the rotes inscribed previously in a palimpsest through the echoes of their Pattern, though she can never learn them with Experiences. Once she's figured out how to cast these echoes, she can develop her own rotes based on those spells. Each rote echo is its own Mystery with Opacity equal to the total Arcana dots used in the rote + 1 for every century that has passed since its original inscription, to a maximum of +5. Once she has fully scrutinized the echo with Focused Mage Sight, she may cast it from the Grimoire with a two-die penalty to her spellcasting roll. '

    Here 'spell echoes' are treated as Mysteries based on previous Rotes - clear Rote, clear effect, recasted in modern times. Simple idea - one spell that you can 'uncover' under the original Grimoire.

    On the Scribe Palimpsest spell itself we have this:

    'She does so using a previously-scribed Grimoire with its contents erased, scrubbed, scribbled out, painted over, or otherwise made unreadable. The Storyteller chooses one Arcanum when the character casts this spell. Whenever a character later casts the rote from the completed Grimoire, it acts as though it incorporated dots of the chosen Arcanum equal to this spell's Potency, creating unpredictable blended effects.'

    Here what in previous quote was 'spell echoes' is totally missed and added is 'wild magic' effect - Maybe it's only wording thing but it's sounds as Scribe Palimpsest totally miss the previous Rotes content of previous version of Grimoire.

    I suppose that end rules will assume 'spell echoes' incorporation as needed to cast Scribe Palimpsest and, in reality, Scribe spell will get you conjuncted second Rote on those that you write, yes?

    Example: You have old Grimoire about magical plant rising, with Life spells about it. When you made this book into Palimpsest about Forces magic control of fire, you will get Control Fire Rote, that when cast - activates also Plant Rising spell in the target.

    Am I reading it right here?

            Originally posted by WHW View Post
            You know, we cant really judge palimpsets without having full rules on adding optional spells to arcana - something that i heard could be expanded upon in this book.
            Why not? If I understand concept correctly ( going with 'spell echoes' idea ), Palimpsests are things like this:

            We have Grimoire book, let's say it has 500 years. In previous version it was Grimoire A, with 5 Rotes A. Now it's scrapped clean. All Rotes A are becoming 'spell echoes' on the book itself. Then comes Mystygoug or Libertaine and he records his Grimoire B with 5 Rotes B. Now, all Rotes B are in reality connected spells of Rote A and Rote B of particular Grimoire idea.

            So this works if in our example Grimoire A is about 'Magical plantation' and Grimoire B are 'Forces attack spells' - we end with Palimpsest's final 'Forces attack spells that increase growth of plants' spells. All of which we simple can have from 2E corebook. In 2E rules Grimoire is just magical list of Rotes - you just pick up number of spells and put them in to book/DVD disk/write in to the stone/whatever.

                I want to point out that to create Palimpsests - like real world term in textual studies - you need to have original text of scroll to use it. In Mage, it simply maybe that you need to have vague idea of previous version of Grimoire what Rotes have in it, i.e. general theme of magical book - to create your Palimpsests effects.

                Key here is that those should be 'spell echoes' of previous magic in it - not totally unrelated random effects going only from Arcana. If Palimpsest was previously book about magical plant growth, all new Rotes in it should also do some wild plant growth, even when caster may not be sure 100% what will be where growing. To make spell usable on game sessions and Awakened world - it should be at least somehow predictable from 'spell echoes', not totally random.
