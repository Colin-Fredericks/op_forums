    Your experiences running Boston Unveiled?

    08-02-2016, 01:33 PM
    I ask this because most people seem to see the Nemean as a plot device, the typical moustache´d villain waiting to be taken down by the PCs, however I recently ran a chronicle where he actually turned out to be the "good" guy(he is still a douche to people, but he works behind the scenes to stop the Prince of 100.000 leaves) and the Brotherhood of the Ineffable(?) Truth turned out to be a powerhungry and warmonger cabal, eventually falling under the influence of the Prince. I tried to present the Nemean as a tragic figure, someone who holds a secret so horrible that it has weighed on him heavily over the years, molding him into an old resentful men.

    I did this mostly to mess with my PC´s expectations, I knew they had also read the source material and so I gave them plenty of false red herrings before they finally realized the Noose were actually the "good" guys.

    What are your experience running this awesome scenario?

              My experience was to just remove him and and the witchcraft cabals from my game. I like boston as a setting, both in RL and the way was presented in mage it is an interesting setting. However one of the weakest is the cabals, not the member themselves but the cabals weirdly enough.

              The main issue for me was that we know what fuels and keeps the main conflict to overboard is the secret accord however in typical NWoD fashion the book throws up it hands and makes what the actually entails completely up to the DM, which to me is problematic because whatever the accord turns up to be it will affect the personalities and goals of the 2 main cabals. And as a mistery it only affect the PCs who will give a crap about that and wanna risk it as the last person who investigate was murdered unless one wanna make the Nemean hostigate the players until they have enough and that they decide to follow up with that particular plot instead of just killing him outright in typical PC fashion.

              The main cabals themselves are bloated just to give them superior number agaisnt the PCs, making bunch of their member just forgetabble or not making that much sense. What i find weird though is that now in 2E boston doesnt work as city as mage forge a concilium apparently around a big mistery but openly investigating the secret accord is declaring oneself as a political enemy of the nemean and the rest.

              The NPCs themselves are interesting but the cabals themselves are, to me dull, the 2 main cabals smell to much of ascension. The arrow cabal who is obsese with duel is kinda dull just like the casino robbing and guardian cabals, the members are fine and their locations are interesting but the cabals themselves range from aimless to apolitical to secretive enough that PCs wont interact with them unless the DM force them to it.

              However i find the barest of bones of the main conflict interesting enough that being "2 powerful cabals fighting for dominance with 1 leader figure keeping it barely contained"

                                              I've just finished reading the 20 months worth of forum posts I missed while I was away from the game, and even though I still have 3 months to go I thought I would share my experience.

                                              I absolutely LOVE Boston Unveiled. I don't agree with most of the criticisms that get leveled against it. I ran a highly successful chronicle using a ton of material from this book as well as other 1e source material.

                                              The loose structure I used was:

                                              - Gloria Mundi Demo early parts
                                              - Beast of Burden (Boston Unveiled)
                                              - Gloria Mundi Demo middle parts
                                              - Legacy Inductions (Homebrew)
                                              - SAS: The Abedju Cipher
                                              - Gloria Mundi Demo final parts
                                              - SAS: Lines of Power
                                              - Danvers Asylum (Boston Unveiled)
                                              - Mama Desta (Boston Unveiled)

                                              All of this with the Prince/Secret Concord plotline running in the background.

                                              I ran 60 sessions in total. The AP can be found right here: http://mage-doomcrew.obsidianportal....=oldest&page=1

                                              It starts a little dry and improves a lot as it progresses.

                                              PS: I would really appreciate anyone who enjoys it to become a fan for future updates. I'm no DaveB, but I'm pretty proud of the work I put into it.
