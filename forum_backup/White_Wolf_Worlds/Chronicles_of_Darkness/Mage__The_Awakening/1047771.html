    [2E, Custom Legacy] Wakanda, the Totemists

    02-12-2017, 01:41 PM
    Hello.

    I've homebrewed another custom legacy for my group's chronicle, and I wanted to share it with you all.
    This one plays a bit more loose-and-fast with the rules than my last one, having two optional Arcana and a bit more loosely defined Attainments. However, between the fact that the new ruling Arcanum will usually be the same as it was before entering the Legacy, and one the Optional Arcana being the parent path's inferior, I feel it is relatively balanced.

    Input is, as always, very welcome.

    Wakanda
    When white folk imagine the lives of the American natives before the arrival of their own race, the image most commonly conjured, is the one of a peaceful tribe of noble savages living off the bounty of the land, taking what they need and giving back what they can.
    Anyone who has lived in the wilds know this to be a dangerous delusion.
    Before the Europeans made their way to the New World, Willworkers had made their impact on the way of life of the First Nations. The North, a land of harsh winters, deep forests and dangerous beasts, fostered hard and equally dangerous warriors, Sleeper and Awakened alike.
    The Awakened quickly found, that the deep dark woods surrounding the Great Lakes housed more than bears and wolverines. Dark creatures stalked the lands and made several unfortunate hunters and isolated tribes their prey.
    The Wakanda, as they were known to the people of the Sioux tribes, found they needed assistance from creatures equally mysterious and forced their way across the Gauntlet into the nebulous realm of Shadow.
    Here, in woods taller, deeper and even darker than their counterparts in the Fallen World, the mages sought out the spirits revered by their people, and forged strong alliances with them. They allowed the spirits to feed on a small shard of their souls in return for guidance and power on the other side.
    This power altered their souls, bringing them closer to the spirits they bonded with, and often bestowing great strength or wisdom upon them.
    Whether the Wakanda were ultimately successful remains unknown, but a few of their number still exist.

    Origins
    Parentage: Thyrsus or Adamantine Arrow
    Nickname: Totemists, Hunters.
    Background: The Totemists are a diverse group, but common to many is an experience with adversity greater than they could overcome themselves.
    A Totemist could be a grizzled hunter, a new-age revival hippie, a metropolitan urchin or a spare-time hiker. Many have some sort of connection to the wilds, but this is not universal.
    Appearance: A Totemist usually dresses in in utilitarian clothing, but many feature some sort of reference to the nature of their bonded spirit, a wolf’s-head pin or a feather hanging from a chain around the neck. Some choose to display the Native American heritage of their legacy through imagery or jewelry.
    The farther the Totemist progresses in the legacy, the closer the bond between the spirit and the mage becomes. This will make its mark on the Wakanda’s countenance. A Wakanda with a high Gnosis might begin to emulate the spirit’s appearance, a Totemist bonded to a murder spirit might have faint bloodstains on his hands and a wolf Totemist might grow a grizzled beard
    .
    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Spirit 2, Occult 2, Survival or Empathy 2
    Initiation: A prospective Wakanda must travel across the Gauntlet and find his own bonded spirit. The Wakanda must either defeat it or persuade it to bond with him. This journey does not need to come of his own power, and indeed, many Wakandas make this first journey when their tutor finds it appropriate.
    Organization: The Wakanda are loyal to their kin and their spirit, but usually operate loosely regarding the orders and the Consilium. Most are individualists, but they usually show a strong connection to their cabal, sometimes referring to them as their “war party”.
    Theory: A solitary man is easy prey. A hunter needs an ally and the spirit world provides. The hunter and his spirit are one and they are separate. The hunter must take the lead. Nothing is given freely and blood owes blood.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Spirit
    Yantras: Successfully hunting or tracking the target of the spell (+2), Calling on the bonded spirit for guidance if the spell or the target is relevant to its Influence (+1 to +3, depending on the situation).
    Oblations: Successfully hunting difficult prey, communing with the bonded spirit, journeying to the Shadow.



    Attainments
    First: Know your prey
    Prerequisites: Inititation
    The Wakanda are both hunters and hunted - and they understand, that knowing what you are hunting or what is hunting you is a step away from its unexpected jaws.
    The Wakanda’s bonded spirit helps the Totemist understand the perils spilling over the Gauntlet and allows her to call on its knowledge when he is confronted by a new enemy, or ally, as it may be.
    This attainment mimics the Know Spirit rote (p. 180), but it also allows the Wakanda to know if a mortal subject is influenced or possessed by a spirit.
    Optional: Mind 1
    The Totemist who has a small understanding of the workings of the mind, may also use this attainment to know the subject’s superficial mental status, as per the rote Mental Scan (p 159), provided they have had contact with a spirit within a number of days equal to the Totemist’s Gnosis.
    Optional: Life 1
    The Totemist who knows Life, may also use this attainment to analyze the physical condition of the subject, as per the rote Analyze Life (p 148), provided they have had contact with a spirit within a number of days equal to the Totemist’s Gnosis.
    Second: Mask your scent
    Prerequisites: Spirit 2
    The Wakanda knows that to be a successful hunter, you must control when you confront it. When using this attainment, the bonded spirit guides the Wakanda’s steps and shrouds him in its mystical energy, leaving him unseen by the spirits that may stalk him.
    This attainment functions like the Walk Unseen rote (p. 181), except that it only pertains to Spirits and not users of the Spirit Arcanum. However, during its duration, it also masks his trail and suppresses his resonance and his nimbus to spirits.
    Optional: Mind 2
    A Wakanda who has at least 2 dots in Mind also applies the above to other Mages or supernatural creatures trying to follow him. Supernatural attempts to break this, provokes a Clash of Wills.
    Optional: Life 2
    A Wakanda who has a good knowledge of his pattern, can make it difficult for even natural predators to follow him. His feet conform to the ground, leaving little to no trace. He applies the above effect to mundane attempts of tracking as well.
    Third: Channeling the Spirit
    Prerequisites: Spirit 3, Occult 3, the Totemist must increase the other skill used to qualify for Legacy membership to 3.
    The bonded spirit gives the Wakanda the power he needs to survive in a world of danger. The bonded spirit allows its ephemera to flow across the Gauntlet and lets the sorcerer use it for his own goals. Each chapter, he has a pool of Spirit Guidance equal to his dots in Spirit. Each time he uses a point of Spirit Guidance he may either gain the rote quality on a single mundane roll chosen before he rolls the dice or reroll a single mundane action after he sees the result of the roll (although he must take the second result). He may spend a point of Willpower when calling upon his Spirit Guidance to affect a spellcasting roll.
    Optional Mind 3
    A Wakanda who is a disciple of Mind, may add his Mind dots to a Social roll a number of times per chapter equal to half his Gnosis rounded down.
    Optional: Life 3
    A Wakanda who is a disciple of Life, may add his Life dots to a Physical roll a number of times per chapter equal to half his Gnosis rounded down.
    Fourth: The Prey’s Territory
    Prerequisites: Spirit 4, Occult 4.
    When hunter becomes the prey, it will usually withdraw to a place of safety. When the prey is a spirit, this place will be the Shadow. When the Totemist becomes powerful enough, his bonded spirit can be used as a path to cross over into the Shadow. This takes a toll on the spirit and the Totemist must pay the debt with a point of Mana.
    This mimics the rote World Walker (p184), except it only applies to the Totemist.

    Optional: Mind 4
    The Adept of Mind may use his abilities to truly understand the language of the spirits. While spirits do understand High Speech, the use of it is offensive and crass to them, leaving the Willworker to use it with a bad first impression. This attainment functions at all times when the Wakanda remains in the Shadow.
    Optional: Life 4
    While the Mage’s form in the Shadow is constructed completely of ephemera, it is still constructed from his pattern in the Fallen World. An adept of life has mastered his pattern so much, that his Shadow self can take exactly the form he wants. He can change his appearance while in the Shadow in one minute, and may spend a point of Mana to change it as a reflexive action.
    Fifth: Killer be killed
    Prerequisites: Spirit 5, Occult 5, increase either Empathy or Survival to 4 dots.
    The purpose of the hunt is to kill. This attainment represents the ultimate destruction of the prey spirit. The Wakanda devours its prey in the final act of the play of hunter and prey. The bonded spiriti joins him in this feast growing stronger and bigger when the two hunters share the taste of spirit blood.
    When the Wakanda activates this attainment, he attempts to destroy and consume the spirit completely. The target spirit may spend an Essence to roll Power+Finesse in a Clash of Wills against the Wakanda’s Gnosis+Occult+Spirit. If the Wakanda is successful, he gains an amount of Mana equal to the Spirit’s Rank.
    Optional: Mind 5
    The master of Mind gains a tremendous amount of satisfaction from finally capturing and vanquishing his prey, and may completely refresh his Willpower, as if he had indulged his Virtue.
    Optional: Life 5
    The purpose of the hunt is usually to bring sustenance to the hunter. When the master of Life consumes a spirit, he may reknit his pattern. He can heal an amount of damage (any type) equal to the Spirit’s rank.

      Originally posted by Shadowjim12 View Post
      Well here is my contribution to the community, it is just an idea; I'm OPEN (pun intended) to suggestions:

      Pneumatofagoi ("Πνευματοφάγοι", pronounced "pnevmatofági", Greek for "Spirit-Eaters")
      Ruling Arcana: Spirit
      Optional: Death
      Path: Moros
      Order: Free Counsil (former Nameless)
      Yantra: acts dedicated to satisfaction or intimidation of spirits (+1), ephemera (+1), target spirit's bane (+2)
      Oblations: Perform a favor for a spirit, eat a spirit, allow spirit to possess you willingly, spent some time into the Shadow

      Initiation: Somehow feast from a spirit. Either consume ephemera, a materialized part or find a way to process essense.

      Legacy History & Principle:
      Members of the Legacy proudly claim that it was created near the end of the Golden Age of Athens (circa 410 BC) by awakened Sophists (Teachers and Sages) that rejected Platotic (even maybe Atlantean) cosmology and retoric. Both Sleeper and Awakened lore suggests that Sophists were heavily critisised by prominent figures, like Plato and Aristophanis, as glorified hairsplitting wordsmiths with malevolent intentions.

      While plato-inspired mages leaned towards following a path through the inner self -suggesting astral travels, goetia and finally ascension-, the pneumatofagoi believed that they should remain fettered in the fallen world, understanding and becoming one with the world around them. They considered "The Silver Ladder of ascension" either a fool's Utopia or a very difficult task at hand. Supernal magic had its limits and was easily corrupted by the mortal world. No, they needed something else...

      Past Alexander's Time, while the proto-Diamond maped the basis of Astral realms, Pneumatofagoi were experimenting across the Gauntlet. They were facinated by the harmony of the spirit ecosystem and by their paradox-free powers. Driven by their lust to free themselves from the limits of their mortal magick, they shaped their very souls into spirits through the most efficient (and natural to their eyes) way; by becoming part of the ecosystem, devouring spirits.

      Pneumatofagoi that trap two or three spirits into themselves are decent company. But there are always people that want to test their limits. Present Members of the legacy are still treated with suspicion since part of the Legacy is hidding its own nature. Some extremists go as far to say that the legacy openly suggests ascenting the lie (namely the shadow) and leave the supernal to the Exarchs, making it a thorn into the newly prestigious image of the Free Counsil as Enemies of the lie. Some Consilii have declared it Left-Handed or Weapon of the Enemy but no Convocation has condemned it thus far.

      As a old Hierarch of Athens put it "You want Spirits to start talking? If you don't have a werewolf clan around, a Pneumatofagos is your next buddy on the list"

      Attainments:
      Sapor Corporis (1st, Know Spirit, instant +1R, sympathetic +1R +1Mana, no optional):
      As an instant action and by spending one Mana, you can eat a part of spiritual residue (be it leaked essense, ephemera, materialised part, etc) and one piece of knowledge per Spirit dots you possess about the specific spirit as per Know Spirit. Name, Rank, list of Manifestations, List of Numina, list of Influences and their strength, Ban, Bane.
      Pneumatopagida (2nd, Opener of the Way, Advance Duration +1R, optional Death Soul Jar):
      Literaly meaning "Spirit-trap", by spenting one scene and taking an action relevant to the Resonant Condition, you can modify it into an Open one for one scene per Spirit dots. For example this may be done by killing something for a murder Resonant Condition or kiss someone for love/lust Resonant one. Remember that the subject might be virtualy anything, a place, a person, an item, etc. Optional (Death 2): By adding banning concepts (circle of salt, runes, etc) into the action, the subject of the Open Condition becomes a trap. The Open Subject functions as a "Soul Jar" but for spirits. The spirit can't leave the subect, possess others outside boundary (if any), or directly target the subject (but influencing others, or pushing other objects is fair game).
      Signa Invisibilia (3rd, Supress Aura, passive): You can become spiritualy invisible. You can hide any spirit or spirit-related effect on yourself from an other spirit or the Spirit arcanum including your Spirit Rank and notoriety in the spirit world. Supernatural powers to detect your spirit-stuff or to identify you as Spirit-Eater trigger a Clash of Wills. By spending one mana you can also hide one target (spirit or effect not in yourself) for one scene.
      Haunted Body (4th, Craft Fetish): Usually applied on a defeated spirit, instead of allowing it to hybernate back into the shadow, you force it inside your body creating as per Craft Fetish, gaining one influence dot or Numina per Spirit dot for up to one week.You can prolong the duration by eating large pieces of spirit corpus with your first attainment for 1 day per corpus consumed. You can have multiple hybernating spirits stiched into yourself but you can only have dots and numina in your "active" arsenal as your Spirit dots. You can shuffle between them by spending one action and one Mana or Essence as you change your spirit-stuff. While in this state the action becomes visible despite the invisiblility granted by the 3rd Attainment. Also, remember that Spirits can wake and break free without other powers banning them.
      Spirit-Whole (5th, No Existing Spell): By spending one Mana you can substitute all your physical attributes with a fettered spirit's Attributes (Power for Strength, Finesse for Dexterity, Resistance for Stamina), size and you can use all its powers with the spirit's stats. Doing so wakes the spirit that manifests superimposed in your body, creating something that resebles a Ridden, a spirit-human hybrid. You must roll a clash of wills each turn to remain in control. Failure means the spirit can treat you as possessed for this scene. Optional (Death): Alternatively, You can devour one of the spirits you fettered into yourself -killing it instantly- gaining powers from that spirit as with previous attainment but fettered with Indefinite Duration. These powers are excluded from the previous attainment restrictions. You can have up 5 Indefinite fettered Powers in your arsenal (in addition to the previous attainment). By activating the Devour-Attainment again you can permanently remove (painfuly riping spirit parts apart from you, or the spirit parts forcibly get out) granted powers for new ones respecting the attainments limit.
