            First pledge: no cheating. If you attempt to cheat you lose a Door. If you succeed you lose the Duel.

            Second pledge: be magnanimous. You lose the Triumphant condition if you abuse the Defeated, but through reparations you can relieve the Defeated of their condition and make your own more resilient or broader in benefits.

            Third pledge: allow for negotiation. You can only use this if your last spell was for defense. It takes effect between spells and briefly stops the casting to allow for negotiations. Therfore you cannot trick your foe into casting and getting punished. If you attack before you both agree to resume the Duel then while the ban lifts for you both the spell still fails and the opponent gets the respective immunity. The Attainment to force your foe to pay with their own Doors cannot be attempted during this Duel and you can even get permission from your foe to keep some of your immunities.

            Fourth pledge: you can forfeit without forfeiting everything. This assumes that the default result of backing out of a Duel in progress is loss. We know animals are dangerous when cornered and Sun Tzu even attests to the power of those without an escape. You can break the will by making cowardice an option, a deceptively hard one. Whatever the mechanics will be it is difficult to attempt without losing Doors in the process. On the low chance you escaped with some then you will be vulnerable to haggling depending on how few Doors are left.

            Fifth pledge: you can cement your victory. Cast a spell right after your victory that affirms the terms you won. The Sacred Dueling Space waits on you before closing down. The spell manifests as a Master level Legacy Attainment. It must strictly be used to enforce the terms agreed upon in and before the Duel. The storyteller may expand the scope as though the terms were a Legacy's Theory if she wishes.

                    Maybe I need to elaborate on the story of Champion. Some inspiration came from my own life and some came from how I imagine others suffered.

                    You ever experienced helplessness? Namely in the form of cults or legal bullshit? Champion was raised in a high control group. She saw her friend go back to an abusive father because the courts backed him. All in all, she knows intimately that life is unfair.

                    Now comes the Awakening. The story of the Exarchs. Power, belonging, purpose. Joining the Free Council was a given. The intoxication of fighting against tyrants. Then that all went to shit when Trump was elected. Before that, Champion named herself so to answer the drudgery and disappointment and trauma from before the Awakening. Then the craziness of Trump revealed all the excuses made for the ignorant as bullshit. The members of the Free Council was wrong about democracy seeking the truth, and Champion was furious at them for lying to her. Champion remembered how her own sister took the side of her abusive father, and was furious at herself for not learning her lesson.

                    So many of the problems of the masses was endemic to the masses, and cannot be culled like tyrants and conspirators. How can she be Champion if she is suspicious of the victims, or unable to forgive millions? She was left in a crisis that dangled over the edge of the Abyss, nearly committing to a number of Left-handed options. Eventually she found her anchor: the Duel Arcane. Having realized she can use it to force the squabbling idiots of the Pentacle into fixing the world she dedicated everything to it like a drunken escape. She joined the Adamantine Arrow to train all the time so she can kill the mages too hostile for the Duel and defeat everyone else in the Duel. Might in place of trust.

                    But however good she was, there was always someone better and she needed to tip the odds in her favor somehow without violating the Lex Magica. Cheating was not sustainable in a society of expert secret keepers and Mystery solvers. She needed to be unbeatable and allowed to be so, and thought it efficient to do both at the same time.

                    Looking back, she finds her name to be an ironic joke. Still, she is Champion one way or another.

                    Some of you guys had opinions on the Theory of her Legacy, so now you know why it is like this.

                                                Defining cheating, okay. By default the rules are the same as the normal Duel and allows for the Legacy's Attainments. As in: no actual casting and no outside interference. They may be renegotiated every Duel assuming the opponent understands that he can and will be magically held to it. Whatever happens all Duelists involved who have Fate initiation at least will be aware of any interference that had to be fated away and what couldn't be fated away. That last one will end the Duel automatically, without any winner and the Dueling mages will suffer the consequences of lost Doors as per the fourth pledge.
