    Harsh Insight (• - •••••)
    Prerequisites: A dot in that particular Arcana
    Effects: You have a peculiarly strong view of one of your Ruling Arcana, manifesting itself with violence upon your perspectives. You are always considered to be under the effects of active Mage sight incorporating that Arcana - this does not allow you to use focused Mage Sight and does not carry the normal penalty unless you actually enter active Mage Sight. In addition, you may spend one Mana when casting a Knowing or Unveiling spell belonging to that Arcana to use your dots in Harsh Insight in place of that spell's base Potency.

            Came up with this to make Runes a little more usable for my current character. It seems like it'd mesh well with the other Rune merits in this doc, so feel free to include it. Not 100% sure on the balance for it, so please let me know what you think.

            The idea is that her and her cabal will all have various runes tattooed onto them.
            In this context 'The spell being used' = The name of the spell, eg 'Telepathy', 'Mend', 'Decay', etc

            Disjointed Runes (••• or •••••)
            Prerequisites: Awakened
            Effect: You possess an innate understanding of how each component symbol of a given rune functions on its own.
            With the three dot version of this merit, you may inscribe runes that describe aspects of a spell. You can combine these into a single Imago, instead of inscribing a set of runes that describes only a single Imago.

            In order to use the Runes Yantra, all aspects of the spell must be described on the subjects of the spell. Aspects include:
            Any Reach effects applied to the spell
            Any Spell Factors
            The Spell being used.

            If the mage has existing runes describing these aspects, they may cast the spell without inscribing new runes.

            With the five dot version of this merit, the Runes Yantra gives a +1 bonus for each aspect that your runes describe, instead of a flat +2 . You may use the Runes Yantra without describing all aspects.

                Based on a misconception of how the Artifact Arcana worked, I bring you this merit, that lets them work the way I thought they worked (more or less). I'm restricting it to Sleepwalkers to make it interesting - you could probably run a game/Chronicle of PCs with this, and influence what Arcana they have access to and when by controlling what Artifacts they have access to at the time.

                Relic Adept (••+)
                Prerequisites: Sleepwalker, Relic Attuned
                Effect: You can invoke more complex spells than an Artifact possesses, using a combination of your arcane knowledge and the Artifact's Supernal power. Pick one Practice - when holding an Artifact, you can cast spells using that Practice as if you were a Mage with the Artifact's Gnosis and Arcana. You must still pay for the Mana costs with the Artifact's Mana, any you might possess (such as through Cup Bearer), or Willpower (such as through Relic Attuned), and you still lack Wisdom to contain Paradoxes with. You have Spell Control equal to a Mage with the same Gnosis as the Artifact. Spells are automatically ended at the end of any scene where you no longer retain ownership over the Artifact (unless you Relinquish it).

                For each additional dot, you can learn a new Practice of that level. For instance, learning Compelling would add one dot, while learning Fraying would add three dots.


                Thoughts?

                    Conductive (•, •••, or •••••)
                    Prerequisites: Sleepwalker
                    Effect: Through some quirk of Destiny, biology, or magic, you conduct Supernal energies far easier than the rest of the Fallen World. Whenever a spell is cast on you, its Potency is treated as if it were 1, 2, or 3 higher (respectively for the one, three, and five dot versions of the merit), solely as it applies to you (the Potency is its original value against all other targets it might effect).
                    Drawback: The Potency is increased for all spells - including non-beneficial ones.

                    And the inverse, though I'm worried about this being too strong

                    Grounded (•• or ••••)
                    Prerequisites: Non-Awakened
                    Effect: Something in your nature impedes the flow of Supernal Truth, reducing the Potency of any magic that affects you by half of the dots in this merit. Since this is reduced Potency, and not merely an increased Withstand rating, it even affects spells that aren't Withstood (such as Fraying spells).
                    Drawback: The reduced Potency cannot be lowered and affects even beneficial magic.

                    Just for fun, some potential Advanced versions of the above. Result may vary wildly, and access is something a ST should consider thoroughly beforehand.

                    Conductive, Advanced (•••)
                    Prerequisites: Conductive •••••
                    Effect: Supernal energies flow through you quickly and readily. Any spell that targets you has an extra free Reach, albeit it is only effective as it pertains to you. This could mean increased Potency, including you "for free" by applying it to Scale, or an additional Reach effect.
                    Drawback: The free Reach is available to less benign spells cast at you as well.

                    Grounded, Advanced (•••••)
                    Prerequisites: Grounded ••••
                    Effect: Supernal energies avoid you and disappear into the void of your attachment to the Lie. Any spell targeting you must spend an additional point of Reach, solely to affect you.
                    Drawback: The increase Reach cost applies to all magic that targets you - even spells you'd welcome or that could save your life.

                                So, I've been thinking about cryptopolies lately, because I am always thinking about cryptopolies. While this isn't really much of a homebrew merit, it is something I'd appreciate feedback on and can't think of anywhere more appropriate to post it.

                                Building on the structure of 1e's information in the Ladder book, a cryptopoly is a collection of interwoven cults and conspiracies at the top of which sit Illuminates - thearchs and their trusted Sleepwalkers - who are directed in their activities by the local deacon. Outside of their official capacities for the deacon, though, a cryptopoly cult's resources are open to those of other thearchs to use as needed.

                                To represent the cryptopoly system, two merits are necessary: Mystery Cult Influence and Allies/Contacts (Cryptopoly).

                                Mystery Cult Influence is in the Mage core. In a cryptopoly, the three dot level is available for every thearch member of the Caucus, representing the basic advantages of being a prince of the heavens. Four dots are usually reserved for Illuminates, who have more direct control over a cult. Five dots is reserved for deacons. Exceptions exist but this is the general structure.

                                While there are no hard-and-fast rules in designing the benefits of a cult, the "standard" cryptopoly spread for three dots are two dots of Allies (Cryptopoly), one dot of Contacts (Cryptopoly), and one dot of Resources. This can (and probably should) be tweaked depending on the cryptopoly's local structure. One that has deep connections focused on law enforcement, say, may not be as financially powerful but its members are always able to find friends in the local police (switching Resources for Allies (Law Enforcement)).

                                And that brings me to the actual modestly homebrew part of this:

                                Allies/Contacts (Cryptopoly)

                                You are a thearch; part of the ruling class of the Awakened Nation. For that reason, you have been given unparalleled access to a subversive network that has infiltrated the structure of the region to a degree that many might be truly astonished by. Wherever you have need, you will find a friend indeed.

                                This merit functions similarly to those of the same type, except that it may theoretically apply in just about any situation. The reach of the Silver Ladder is longer than many suspect. Even if the Mage personally has no direct means of making connections, all it takes is a phone call or a letter couriered to the right person and the doors to the halls of power will open. In short, in any situation where the Mage might use the Allies or Contacts merits, this merit may apply (with ST approval; the Ladder's reach is long, but not universal).

                                In addition, the cryptopoly merit may be allowed (again, with ST approval) to stack with other Allies dots that the player already possesses; triggering additional agents within the organization to make a given task less onerous or risky on their contacts. Thus, a Mage with Allies 2 (Police) and Allies 2 (Cryptopoly) may use both at once to treat themselves as having four Allies dots for a given favour. Doing so counts as a use for both merits, however, and pings the Drawback of this one as it would if used alone.

                                Drawback: There is a downside, of course, to the connections you tug upon not being personal. Everyone can find out what you've been doing, just by asking. While Illuminates are rarely profligate with their secrets, they know what you've been up to and that means they can tell anyone. Thus, unwise is the thearch who abuses these benefits too readily. While thearchs tend to be lax on certain definitions of "abuse of power" - no-one looks askew at the thearch who uses the cryptopoly to pursue their Obsessions or even just for basic ambitions, like getting city council to speedily approve your business plans - using it repeatedly to get out of trouble with the law or in ways that are harmful to Sleepers will draw the eyes of their fellows.

                                If a character uses personal Allies combined with cryptopoly ones, an Investigation roll is needed to find out what they were up to, with a penalty (or bonus) equal to the difference in the number of personal vs. cryptopoly dots used (that is, if the character used three personal dots and two cryptopoly dots, the subsequent Investigation roll would take a -1 penalty).

                                (Note: For adjudicating the above, expect that any thearch can learn about the cryptopoly uses of any thearch of equal or lesser standing in the Mystery Cult Influence background.)

                                                Inspired by the aimed spell thread....

                                                Magister Virgae (Style, • to •••)
                                                Prerequisites: Awakened
                                                Effect: Your character is particularly adept with the use of a particular magic tool. So long as she is casting spells using this tool as a Dedicated Magical Tool she benefits from the effects of this Merit. The tool must be something wielded by hand.
                                                Conduit (•): When casting an aimed spell, the mage receives a +1 bonus to the die roll to aim the spell.
                                                Mnemonic (••): The mage traces Atlantean runes in the air with her tool as she casts a spell. Her tool now gives an additional +1 bonus to the spellcasting pool when used as a Yantra.
                                                Corpus (•••): The tool's durability increases by 1 and the mage's sympathy with it increases to Connected. She is always aware of its location and whether or not it is being wielded. For the purposes of locating her tool, she benefits from the Direction Sense merit.
