                                                    I took and wrote down my versions of Illuminated Pentad - nice reading and I look forward to comments.

                                                    Illuminated Pentad 2ED (Pre-corebook version)

                                                    Once new and rising stars of Boston Concilium, two years after Nemean official “disappearance”, Pentad is clearly showing some cracks. This Silver Ladder cabal has always close to social and political issues in the city. But when Khumeia finally joined Ruling Council after her role in solving Nemean problem, there grew a distance between her and Faustina that is clearly to this day. When her apprentice decided to become Subtle One, under supervise of Simon from White Putnams, new Councilor took it very personally. Still, they remain to be one cabal, even if there are rumors that’s it all only publicity stunt to let Khumeia become Hierarch.

                                                    Even then, Illuminated Pentad is known of his shared Obsessions over Supernal Alchemy. Three members of the group see human minds as “gross matter” of common life that can be perfected into ideal state in that new Hieraconis of Silver Ladders legends will emerge with this transmutations. This theory works in over way also – minds can shape substance all around them, changing environment around you. In the other words, Illuminated Pentad want to remake Boston’s street and buildings to remodel the people – and model the people to remake the city. For their Alchemy it’s one and the same. Perfect the “substance” to perfect the mind. And perfect the mind to perfect the “substance”.

                                                    With their connections and alchemical lore, Pentad has become one of focal points for the Silver Ladder Cryptopoly in Boston. There always were rumors they “heritage” connections from Guardians of Veil in Order of Golden Dawn era. Cabal do not confirm and do not deny those statements. Truth is they have a large sway over Boston’s mystery cults.

                                                    Khumeia (Concilium Councilor)
                                                    Quote: We have this city. Now we can make it shine.

                                                    Background: Yvonne Saint-Martin was born into a war. Half of her wealthy, influential Parisian family was a lineage of Proximi, with a handful of mages among them, while the other half constituted the leadership of a Sleeper Exarch cult. Her parents, Proximi both, were killed in a plane crash when she was six months old, leaving the only child an orphan and sole heiress to a vast fortune. Her second cousin Luc, arranged for her to be sent to a branch of the family living north of Boston, far enough away from the conflict that it would not touch her until she was ready to fight back. With only a few vague snippets of prophecy from Luc to go on, the few Saint-Martin Proximi in America did what little they could to try to guide Yvonne toward an Awakening. At the age of 20, to the great relief of her kinsmen, she found her way to the great iron watchtower of Pandemonium. Her great-uncle, a Mastigos of the Silver Ladder, came overseas to teach her what he could before he had to return to the struggle in France. He instructed her in basis of Uncrowned Kings and her first two Attainments.

                                                    By a few years later, Yvonne (now calling herself Khumeia, from the Greek: “cast/pour together, alloy,” a possible origin for the word, “alchemy”), had inherited her parents’ wealth and was ready to become part of local Awakened society. She found the politics of the Concilium not much to her liking, however, and quickly resolved to change the situation, opting to fight a different battle than that which occupied the rest of her European family. Her upbringing had stressed subtlety, patience and cunning, and so she set the foundations for a new cabal by purchasing the top two floors of one of the downtown skyscrapers in which she discovered a Hallow. She then sought out prospective allies who shared her vision of freeing Boston and reuniting it under new leadership – by Legacy Sympathy she uncovered presence of Thanatos in Boston area, other Uncrowned King. And he was more than happy to join her “great social experiment”, almost decade ago. Seven years past from now, she rescued young sorceress from her own newly Awakened powers that become Faustina, but their relations are now tense.

                                                    Since that time, she has steadily worked to sever Boston from Salem’s control, though always through open and politically acceptable means. So far, she has made big headway, with helping to move Nemean from power. Rumors said it’s she and Illuminated Pentad that are behind his “disappearance” and she was granted seat in his place for that. Since it she still need to gather stable connections in community and look over the Ebon Noose and White Putnams rule in the Boston area. She thinks that key to everything is getting Secret Concord and break it, somehow.

                                                    Description: Whatever physical imperfections Khumeia might have had before her majority have all been erased by the world’s best plastic surgeons. She is a statuesque, stunningly attractive woman of about 40, with long, black hair, hazel eyes and a flawless alabaster complexion. She favor dark lipstick, but wears little in the way of eye makeup. Her clothing is the best money can buy, consisting mostly of elegant custom-tailored suits. She speaks politely and with great propriety, but makes it quite clear that she knows her opinion to be the correct one, even when she is forced to concede an issue. She is sometimes to be found with dirty hands, oddly contrasting with her otherwise perfect form, from the next avenue she tries to work as Uncrowned Queen.

                                                    Her Nimbus appears as golden halo of light, manifesting from nowhere and spreading across her back of her statue, extending to be nearly twice as long as she is tall.

                                                    Storytelling Hints: Khumeia is interested in the alchemy of the spirit, the process by which the individual transforms and, ultimately, transcends herself, becoming something far greater and more wondrous. She wants to rule a reborn and independent Boston Concilium, but recognizes that such a goal may be decades in coming, should it ever come to pass. She is imperious and self-assured, thinks that her lead of Boston will brought some Golden Age to it, but does her best not to fall to hubris, well aware of the tales of the Exarchs and their legendary arrogance.

                                                    Dedicated Magical Tools: Leather handbag
                                                    Real Name: Yvonne Saint-Martin
                                                    Path: Mastigos

                                                    Thanatos
                                                    Quote: To master matter is to control all life.

                                                    Background: Irwin Paul has put his Sleeping days behind him. He was reborn out of his own death, Awakened and, for the first time, truly alive. He learned his craft from (and choose shadow name to remember) cabal of Necromancers in Germany, where his father was stationed, but was later cast out by them for some crime or has run from them after something they did to him (He has occasionally intimidated that it was those same Necromancers made he despise Death magic and decided to joined the Legacy based on Alchemy.) He returned to Boston, where he had been born and had spent summers with his mother, who had passed away two years before. Well aware of how difficult it was to get by as a solitary mage, he readily accepted Khumeia’s invitation to join the new cabal she was forming.

                                                    Now, he uses Alchemy and experiments on various effects it has on surroundings, trying to shape perfected and independent Boston Consilium. It is slow work, as the mages of behind Secret Concord made this place resistant to changes. Still, he has time; more time, he imagines, than most. Until then, he steadily builds his “alchemical empire”, collecting lore and Sleepers supporters, working hard on items and favors for Awakened, waiting for the day that he will be able to use them to topple the Ebon Noose and the White Putnams, when the cabals of Boston will finally band together to drive out the open conspiracy that rules their collective fate. Being best Alchemist in the city and cabalmate to Councilor has its perks – many people own him the favor. Time of payment is coming.

                                                    Description: Thanatos is a man of slightly taller than average height and slim, with somewhat broad shoulders. His dark brown hair is worn at medium length and never worn in any particular way for too long. His eyes are deep, and his stare is deeply unnerving, like looking over depth of ocean. His garb, always formal, usually seems vaguely antiquated in some way that no one can quite put a finger on. Perhaps it’s simply that even new clothes look somehow old on him. His voice is deep (some say ghoulish), and he speaks slowly, carefully considering each new idea before talking.
                                                    His Nimbus manifests as half-seen gaunt, skeletal figures, hovering over him, like spurned lover he chase away.

                                                    Storytelling Hints: Perhaps the most important thing to keep in mind about Thanatos is that his creepiness is not an act; he really is that screwed up. The gems and elements are more real to him than the living in many ways. He covets power over the Awakened, but his true hunger is for control over Boston’s “treasures” – stashes of alchemical works and lore from Order of Golden Dawn era. Needless to say, this makes him a perfect ally in Khumeia’s book, as she knows that he is not likely to stand in the way of her bid to become the Hierarch of Boston. He has no sense of the impact of his disturbing nature on others; it simply doesn’t occur to him to notice or care.

                                                    Dedicated Magical Tool: Lead forceps
                                                    Real Name: Irwin Paul
                                                    Path: Moros
                                                    Order: Silver Ladder
                                                    Legacy: Uncrowned King

                                                    Willpower: 8
                                                    Wisdom: 4 (Fixation)
                                                    Virtue: Patient (Prudence)
                                                    Vice: Greedy (Greed)

                                                    Uncover secrets of Alchemy, Shape city of Boston, Collect owned Favors

                                                    Faustina
                                                    Quote: That’s well and good, but what does the current regime actually do for us?

                                                    Background: Brianna Reed was given the name Faustina (after the Roman empress, Faustina the Elder, remembered for her charitable works) when she first met Khumeia, five years her senior. After nearly drowning in a lake, Brianna Awakened, and her experimentation with her newfound abilities brought her to Khumeia’s attention. Brianna enjoyed debates with Khumeia (who inducted her into the Silver Ladder) and agreed to join her new cabal on one condition: that they should establish a system for finding, protecting and instructing the area’s newly Awakened, so that no one should have to stumble through their first weeks, months or even years without proper education.

                                                    Over seven years later, the program still hasn’t really hit its stride, mostly on account of the fact that the Pentad still lacks the clout to make such a far-reaching goal feasible, even with Khumeia as Councilor. Faustina, then, distant herself more and more from her mentor for not fulfilling her promise. She started to take long strides in the city where she met Simon from White Putnams on his “dance” as part of Subtle Ones. Surprisingly, they hit it off and started to meet in secret for few weeks to talk to one another, behind the backs of their antagonistic cabals. Brianna has been inducted by him into Legacy, which quickly came to light. Khumeia was personally affected by her decision, but they still remain cabal mates and Conciliar promise that program will be created.

                                                    One of the reasons Faustina joined Ahl-i-Batin is that she can concentrates on her work as an architect, incorporating the mystic mathematical principles she has learned into her designs, in the hopes of eventually learning some (perhaps lost) trick of willworking that will enable her to exercise her magic effortlessly between any two points in the city.

                                                    Description: Faustina is a good-looking woman, but by no means holds a candle to the resplendent beauty of Khumeia. That suits Faustina just fine, though, as having the spotlight seems to her to be more of a hassle than it could possibly be worth. Her build is a bit taller than average, and slight, and her red-blond hair is worn short. She only rarely uses makeup but dresses well, at Khumeia’s insistence, when meeting with others. However, Faustina prefers her sneakers, sweatpants, jeans and T-shirts when on her own time.

                                                    Faustina’s Nimbus reveals itself as shifting, interlocking geometric patterns that move across her skin like living tattoos.

                                                    Storytelling Hints: Faustina is quite passionate about the causes she supports. Her desire to reign over the Boston Consilium is all but nil, though she recognizes that positive change cannot be made until the Secret Concord is unseated and new leadership steps in. She lacks the discretion and subtlety of Khumeia, so she often holds her tongue, but the occasional thoughtless comment on someone else’s part is known to get her hackles raised. They relations are still strained – and will be, until Faustina see that Khumeia really is dedicated to the cause.
