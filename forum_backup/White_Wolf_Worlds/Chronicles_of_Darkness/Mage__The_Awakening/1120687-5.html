        Originally posted by Malus View Post

        Nah, ascension isn't an "I win" button.
        I mean... it sort of is? It really depends on the type of ascension and for most types its just kind of a plot device. Only for archmages who complete their omens we do have a system. And changing lunas nature/killing her probably would be like a level 4, maybe 5 omen. Level 5 omens are stuff like destroy the whole earth. Archmages don't fuck around with their goals, that said no they don't automatically win because they're Archmasters. They're just crazy enough to try shit like this... and powerful enough that when they play their cards smart they might just pull it off. Their goals are grand, their hubris transcends even legends.

        That said what I initially really wanted to say in response to this post: Archmasters aren't ascended. I think they are sometimes described as half ascended due to their ability to interact with the supernal in the form of Imperium Rites (which is honestly a holy shit scary ability), but otherwise they're just more powerful mages still trapped in the fallen for most of the time as they can only stand small doses of the supernal. Even their level 6+ Arcana are rooted in the fallen world only drawing down supernal symbolism into the fallen (though their level 9 power arguably could be seen as a semi short time ascension... though they still don't really make an imprint upon the supernal with that, so its still a fallen world power.)

        So in the end noone was ever talking about ascended beings here. Just extremly powerful gods (at least rank 6 ephemeral beings, likely higher. For example you could see the Aeons as the patrons of the individual Arcana and they're rank 8 beings I believe) and Archmages (who can rise to similar heights of power). Now I'm honestly not too versed in CofD Spirit cosmology (more knowledgable about owod, have mostly dealt with mage in CofD), no idea what exact rank Luna is. What I was saying is that those kind of entities are certainly not a plaything Luna can toy with without a serious chance for being found out and nasty blowback. Even if she is a rank 10 spirit, that'd be like saying a rank 5 spirit carelessly toying with a rank 3 spirit could never go wrong. Or a Master mage using a Disciple as his plaything is not playing with fire. Sure you're a master, good for you, but a Disciple can still fuck you up if he catches you on the backfoot (or has luck in a clash of wills) or he could rain ruination upon your schemes while you're otherwise engaged or... You can do that (and so could Luna, I'm sure), but it's certainly not something Luna does all the time to all of them casually. Pretty sure she's too good a trickster to be that careless and got at least a rudimentary survival instinct.

        All of this aside, Luna (and earlier Father Wolf) sound like they would be the primary sponsors of Werewolves.

                                My reasoning for Exarcharial patronage of vampirism is slightly different, see below.

                                Originally posted by Flinty View Post
                                In my personal headcanon vampires (as far as MtAw games are concerned, I wouldn't run it that way in a VtR game) have been created by the Psychopomp as a means of stifling humanities potential to awaken. Vampires still have souls, but they're crippled things, unable to awaken and they infect those they feed upon with a little bit of themselves. Sure you can still awaken even if you're being fed from regularily by vampires but in my personal headcanon the chances decrease (and are really small for ghouls).
                                That said I think it depends on the theme of the chronicle who should or should not be the sponsor. If I rand VtR and this question was of any relevance (I really don't see how a vampire game could ever get there to be honest... but who knows, prove me wrong!) I'd most definitly not throw in Archmages or worse Exarchs. They do nothing for the themes of Vampire. In a mage game on the other hand I can see Silver Ladder mages fervently defending the theory that Terata in general are part of an exarcharial plot to cripple individual souls and haven them cripple others too due to their parasitic feeding practices. The truth may of course be very different... or at lest far more layered.

                                tl;dr: Focus shouldn't be on which supernatural splat has the biggest dick or wether another splat is diminished by archmages meddling. This question demands an individual awnser for each campaign that suits the chronicle.
