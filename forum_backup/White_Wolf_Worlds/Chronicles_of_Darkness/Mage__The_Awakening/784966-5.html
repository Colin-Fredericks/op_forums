                Second draft for the Orders of New England in 2E version of Boston. Any comments and thoughts are more than welcome.

                Caucuses

                Pentacle Orders in New England are very loose associations of mages spread around terrain of whole Massachusetts State and some borders counties to it. Proper power of Boston is not very much centralized as inner wars of Bostonian Awakened in XX century and waves of scattered emigrants lead too many sorcerers being left alone to their own devises in small towns and settlements of the region, often ending in Nameless collection in places really isolated from Consilium. Nominally, Orders local societies are full of various ranks many members across New England take on themselves. Realistically, proper Boston’s day to day mage community is understaffed and many times isolationists with not enough mages to run smoothly tasks of Orders in the city, because members are scattered on the map of the state and region.

                This situation changes when Caucuses are called in. Representatives of whole Order come once a month or bi-monthly meetings, exchange knowledge and power in their secret halls. Then mages of New England looks as proper community and force to be reckoned with. And Favors or knowledge exchanged there make lasting relations with all participants. Even if most mages in New England are only nominally subordinate to the Boston’s Consilium, however, they respond to call of colleagues and friends from the Order of the city itself. Counting on their return of Favor if necessary.

                Adamantine Arrow: Windham Manor – Holy Ground

                On a sea cliff in Gloucester perched Windham Manor. A network of caves extends beneath the house, carved into the rock by millions of years of tides. A sprawling graveyard is located at the back of the property, overlooking the water, but it is almost completely overgrown. It’s here where New England’s Arrows meet bimonthly (usually sometime during the third week of the month) on request of Ferrum – Adamant Sage. This place is chosen on the tradition for family ground of Hydra - Sage Emeritus – that’s family and cabal Gravediggers have protected Boston and region for centuries. At least once a year, Ferrum likes to put on an exhibition of the Duel Arcane, inviting any and all mages of the Arrow to observe or, even better, participate.

                Apollonia from The Brotherhood of the Ineffable Truth is Vidantus – working mostly as teacher of new Arrows, trying to not go for more bloodshed in her life, understanding it’s her time after being defeated by Ferrum and the death of Ares.

                Free Council: Emerald Scroll Bar – Assembly and Lore House

                Located at Roxbury neighborhood waterfront, this Tarot’s Major Arcana styled place is open Lore House to any mage that will enter. Leaded by Davy Jones column of Dead Wrens can sell you anything they can get hands on. And make great parties. They even sell alchemical potions as drinks! Even then this place name is still affront for the Guardians for taking password of their sacred XIX century Labyrinth.

                Emerald Scroll is also Assembly meeting place – treated mostly as Free Council Caucus once a month, in weekend long ‘members only’ event – it’s open on day-to-day basis to any mage that want to trade Mysteries or sell magic for quick cash. “Local” Assembly can be called almost every day, but it’s rulings are taken only to represent Boston mages self-interests – Dead Wrens understand that beside them, every few days visits of SMG and city passing Libertine or two – day-to-day rulings of Assembly do not really reflect New England community at large.

                Note: I want to remake Dead Wrens as Libertines only column – they mix of Orders in 1E looks as dictated only old stereotype of “Warriors=Arrows” or “Explorers=Mysterium”. Also, they are pirates and thieves, god dammit, how much Nameless can one be more!

                Guardians of Veil: Order of the Golden Dawn, true Emerald Scroll and Oubliette – Labyrinth

                Once, Guardians Labyrinth was glorious in Boston – on wave of occult interest in and of XIX century and first decades of XX century, it grew and grew, spanning from one secret society to the next. Order of the Golden Dawn split itself in to a lot of cults and sects, each with it slightly different dogma. But each answering to secrets signs of Emerald Scroll – code name for group of Cultors from the Order. Sadly, it’s now only glorious past.

                Today, Guardians still control many of connections between cults, but after Free Council revolt – and even Libertines claiming the name for secret cabal as bar name! – It’s much, much weakened. Restoring Labyrinth to its former splendor is one of priorities for Shadow Chorus and its leader Culsu – Epopt of the Guardians. Over watching Boston Consilium after „disappearance” of Nemean is second. Both they oversee from their secret sanctum called Oubliette, door’s to one can open anywhere in the New England. Once a month, in the settlement of whole Massachusetts, portals to it are open and Guardians come to plan their next moves in reclaiming the region and safe keeping the magic. Februs, Culsu’s cabal mate, is appointed Interfector of the Caucus.

                Mysterium: Academy of the Brotherhood of the Ineffable Truth - Athenaeum

                Academy apartment building in the Beacon Hill area, with north wing working as sanctum for fellow Arrows from Exalted Knights of the Gryphon to defend the Supernal Mysteries stronghold. As name suggest, this particular Athenaeum works mostly in Academy model (see Mysterium book), teaching newly Awakened under guise of private school for “exceptional students”. Of course there is Scriptorium part of complex and giant libraries Athenea are known of. Interesting, there is not pointed obvious Censorium in place – maybe Boston’s Guardians to the Veil are having something to it. As institution, Athenaeum is run by Aurem, one of most dedicated and oldest Mystygoges of Caucus.

                Mysterium caucus in convened at 8 P.M. on the 15th of every month (barring emergency), are rigidly ordered affairs. They are run by Potestas – eldest (literally) mage of Mysterium and Hierophant of Order, that rivals Aurem for the power in local Order.

                Silver Ladder: Magisterium and Cryptopoly

                Tearchs of New England are both divided and united – they started as network of Favors leading to Chariot of Epona cabal in beginning of European colonization. With each Favor the Nameless mages of region took, each come closer to Star Precept and many finally embraced traditions of Silver Ladder and Greco-Celtic mysticism of Epona Awakened. Still, new converts stayed very independent in their ways, leading to tradition of local Tearchs to be very separated from Order. This resulted in the unorthodox way the Magisterium works – organization is splinted in to regions ruled by one Grand Magister each - Vermont, New Hampshire, and Maine under one. Connecticut and Rhode Island in to second. And Massachusetts as last one. They are filled with local Magisters, but they have lower standing in Order as such.

                Problem came with Nemean – his tyranny was once a pretense, designed to unite mages in Boston against him, but that he no longer wished to release his grip on Hierarchy for any reason. Thearchs saw the Lion symbols in his appearance and Shadow Name as a forgotten code for his role and hoped to find someone strong enough to make him honor it. Finally, Khumeia with help of her allies made him “disappear” ( in truth, take him to Chicago on trial under other Magisterium ) and lead to freeing Boston from his iron grip. But then come next problem – what to do with new found freedom? Khumeia become Deacon and Magister in title, but not in her Arcana and planning – she is still only Adept and her vision of Boston to come shows signs of bit lack focus. She also is denied, at least for now, Grand Magister title until she become Master – that result in lowered standing of Boston as whole in New England Caucus. Rest of Boston Tearchs presses her to quicker her Mind Arcanum studies, rest see this as sign of weakness and use it as next excuse to attack her leadership.

                Boston’s Cryptopoly spreads from Lodge of Eight Chambers – Khumeia’s cabal Illuminated Pentad sanctum on the two top floors of downtown skyscraper. This entrusted with Masonic and alchemical symbols place is heart from which starts to flourish part of Bostonian occult underground. Many secret societies and cults owe them allegiance or are manipulated by Lodge. What’s odd, most of those groups have history with Order of Golden Dawn that put’s in question how Pentad got hold on those groups. Some rumors states they stole them from uncovered Guardians XX century Cultors notes – but even Shadow Chorus do not dares to accuse them publicly.

                                Originally posted by arthexis View Post

                                So, I am going to take the opportunity to ask: What other mysteries would you suggest for the area? (Aside from the ones already discussed) Doesn't have to be super elaborate, but maybe a few things I could use as plot hooks or to search for more information by myself?
                                I'll give it a shot. Not sure which "area" you want (greater New England, Massachusetts in general, Boston-specific) so I'll try to pitch a couple of each. All of the things here are true, though possibly slightly exaggerated for the sake of being in the World of Darkness. I can go into more detail/throw more stuff from a specific area, too.

                                New England:
                                -Vermont used to be a state of industry, of production, of creation. Now it's a state made primarily of forests. Official history ties it to the water wheel becoming obsolete, but there must be something more to it. Did a powerful Thyrsus achieve archmastery and claim the area as her domain? Did a high-ranking spirit of Technology get destroyed, leaving little Nature spirits to grow in its place? Or was all that productivity stirring up something sinister?
                                -The city of Killington tried to succeed to New Hampshire, ostensibly for tax reasons. The attempt was absurd and doomed to failure, but why was it made? Did some legal manipulation go awry? Was it meant as a cover or distraction for another act? Or did the location of the city get re-written?
                                -Country borders don't hold a lot of mystical significance, generally. So why is it that spells can't penetrate the Canadian border from the US side? It works just fine the other way. Is this some Acanthus prank? A quirk from revolutionary times? Or a recent reflection of current national tensions?

                                Massachusetts
                                -Recently closed, the Higgins Armory Museum held the second largest collection of arms and armor in the country. While the collection was supposed to be moved to the Worcester Art Museum, a number of pieces don't appear to have been kept. Were they sold privately to interested collectors? Stolen by rival Mages or ardent Hunters? Or were they fated to fall off the back of a truck into the hands of the one who needed them most?
                                -Speaking of armories, Springfield has been holding guns and ammunition since being commissioned by Washington himself. The going theory is that some of the collection is haunted. How else to explain the sounds of gunfire from deep inside on moonless nights?
                                -In the Pioneer Valley, there's been a rash of murals. It started with a couple officially-sanctioned ones, but since then, more and more walls have been painted over in the same abstract style. No one's taking credit or tagging it, and there's yet to be an arrest for defacing public property. Is one of the original murals a source of memetic infection? Why are the images so resistant to being scrubbed away? And what happens when the surfaces covered reach critical mass?
                                -After the protests in the 60s, UMass Amherst was redesigned architectually to be a riot-breaker. It doesn't seem to have helped, though. What is it about the campus that drives students into a destructive frenzy? Does it have anything to do with the bricks that are spat out by the 26-floor library?
                                -There's a little store off I-91 that sells delicious smoked meats, but only when taking exit 25 southbound. If you take exit 25 northbound, you find a dinosaur museum instead. Trying to backtrack will bring you back to 91, and coming from any other exit will show neither place. What the heck?

                                Boston
                                -On Sundays, the 10:14pm outbound Red Line train always skips Porter station. It's not that it blows by; those on the train hear the announcer go from Harvard to Davis, while those at the station never see the train at all. Does the train go through a fold in space and time? Is it briefly held in another reality? Or does the machinery simply shunt it through the maintenance line?
                                -The streets of Boston are almost impossible to drive through, with winding one-way roads taking you far, far from your intended destination. It's not uncommon to see the building you want to go to, and end up circling it endlessly in your car, until and unless you find somewhere to park and go in by foot. What is it about cars that the city seems to hate? Has an old spell stuck around from the horse-and-buggy days? Has some Supernal interpretation of "anti-magnetic" been applied to certain buildings?
