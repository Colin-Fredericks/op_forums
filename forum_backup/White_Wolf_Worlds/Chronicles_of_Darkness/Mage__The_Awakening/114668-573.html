                                                Originally posted by Cauthon View Post
                                                Pinoccheo question. Say a Mage creates a humanoid Golem, then uses Pattening to transmute it into a flesh-and-blood human. Is the resultant "human" Soulless?
                                                I assume yes, but am not sure if it's actually a human or just a biological Golem at that point.
                                                My general assumption would be that turning something into a human wholesale with Patterning instead of functionally turning it mostly human in a piecemeal fashion via Weaving effects turns it into a Soulless human, yes.

                                                Come to think of it, could you use Golem on a corpse, or is that too far outside of Matter's wheelhouse and too far into Death's?
                                                Golem works on objects. A sufficiently processed corpse might be symbolically distinct from a corpse, but a regular dead body is A Dead Body, not a meat-sculpture.

                                                If it is indeed Soulless, and you attach a soul to the Golem, what happens once the Patterning spell ends? Does the soul behave as if the owner had just died?
                                                Same thing that happens if a spell making a subject a valid target for another spell ends: the spell remains, but stops meaningfully doing anything — Soul Jar is active, but it no longer has a person-body to bind the soul to, so it's no longer able to bind the soul to the body, so the soul is released as normal.
