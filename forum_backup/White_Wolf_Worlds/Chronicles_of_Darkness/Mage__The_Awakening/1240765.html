    The best possible mage hacker?

    07-31-2018, 04:42 AM
    An antagonist in one of my games will be a mage who is dead set on using magic to make themselves the best hacker possible, not only mastering technical systems but hopefully also being no slouch in direct physical and social infiltration as well. What do you think would be the most optimal arcana for such a character?

    My current thoughts on how to build the character:
    Primary arcanum - Forces
    Seems like the obvious best arcanum for directly manipulating and twisting software to your wish. Has the added bonus of making electronic security, cameras, alarms and such a trivial concern, as well as letting the character become invisible to security guards. This arcanum even helps to pick physical lock by manipulating magnetism and telekinesis. In my game it is also possible to make a Forces-based golem much like the Matter 4 spell, which functions almost like an elemental in a fantasy game and can be good if you want backup without relying on other people with their own agendas.

    Secondary arcanum - Matter or Mind
    Being able to manipulate the hardware and not just software of electronics, machines and computers and eventually bypassing physical obstacles entirely is always a bonus, but is maybe not strictly necessary. I almost view Mind as more relevant to help the hacker fulfill the social aspects of hacking: social engineering, infiltration as well as even better stealth abilities than Forces due to the (in my opinion) overpowered Incognito Presence. Eventually Mind would also let the character raise various skills (or attributes) to cover up weak spots in their repertoire as well as summoning Goetia if you need backup.

    Tertiary arcanum - Space or Fate
    Space is almost as good as Mind for sheer utility; being able to affect things at a distance is excellent if you are already able to instantly hack into any security camera network for example. It also allows you to physically pass through even the smallest openings and cracks and see through physical obstacles. Fate is also a nice arcanum to improve all your skills and probabilities overall and would almost be as good as Mind were it not for all the especially good benefits that arcanum has for specifically hacking.

    Possible other arcana:
    Death - Being able to step into Twilight is a great ability but the arcanum is otherwise rather messy and niched for this character in my opinion.
    Spirit - Is also able to step into Twilight but also has a wider array of abilities beneficial to hackers than Death. This arcanum could basically fill in entire niches if the mage can find spirits of Stealth or Electronics for example and make them familiars, or slaves.
    Life - Enhances the mundane physical stealth abilities of the mage, but Forces and Mind already does all of that and more.
    Time - This is quite interesting in that it grants many of the abilities that Fate gives to affect your odds and also gives excellent divination abilities.
    Prime - Being instantly able to spot lies and masking your magical aura and nimbus are valuable but are also just about the only benefits I can see with this arcanum. Though, if this character is supposed to be able to "hack" other mages as well (the last frontier and greatest challenge for a character with these ambitions) then this arcanum will get hugely important.

                              The ideal of the hacker is the super planner who is able to outsmart his way to forbidden treasures. But most mages already do that, its what they are built for, uncovering secrets. So to build a villain with an infiltration theme, I propose the following Arcana (in no particular priority order, I leave the fine tuning to you):

                              Mind: Hackers are intelligent, this Arcanum is great for social engineering. Need to infiltrate an organization ? Make them think you are the leader. Need a password or the location of an ultra secret vault ? Read the target's minds. The PCs really made the villain mad ? A simple Ruling can cause a loved one to get Alzheimer (which is masked by the combo with Prime), now you will hurt them far deeper than any blade as the person inches towards a vegetative state. Need to cover your tracks ? Cause a Rashomon Effect. Need to blend into the crowd ? Incognito Presence. Out of Willpower ? Clear Thoughts has you covered. And once you have the data, this lets you become a master at whatever skill you need to make the most of it. The key theme of this choice is Social hacking.

                              Life: Mages can hide secrets in the weirdest places, this ensures he is able to access them safely. One of its best features is how great it is at disguising your appearance, at the drop of a hat you can imitate any voice and be any person, no biometrics scan is your match. Play the paranoia that he can be anyone, anywhere, the kindly grandpa playing chess at the park, or a co-worker, the clerk at the gas station. With Transform Life he can squeeze through doors like a slug, survive radiation like a cockroach, go down a laser shoot Mission Impossible style with his spider silk, jump huge distances like a flea, lots of cool utility. With Shapechanging, you can now spy by being a pigeon, a fly on the wall, become a blood mist to enter any place, reach that deep sea VALKYRIE facility by becoming a Plesiosaurus. The key theme of this choice is Physical hacking.

                              Prime: Orders are no boundaries to the true hacker. With this you can hide your spells, transform your Nimbus into that of any other mage, protect yourself against scrying, dispel any spells that stand between you and your goal. Plus you can usurp command of any imbued item, incentivizing you to build a collection of "gadgets". A great hacker does not fall for cons, no one can lie to you, and even if they do, you can tell what the truth is. With Words of Truth you can both build trust in your honesty and, at the same time, manipulate people by not telling the whole truth, letting others assume things and not correcting people with whom you are speaking. With Life you can become any person, with Prime you can magically pass for a sleeper or any mage and Mind takes care of the rough edges. But lets face it, no matter how good he is at scheming, eventually he will get caught. But if he offers his rare talents and throws in a few of the imbued items he made, a dangerous impasse can become good business. The key theme of this choice is Magical hacking.

                              For a Legacy, I suggest the Bearers of The Eternal Voice, a Mind Legacy dedicated to being silver tongued manipulators that can alter or delete memories. Make the players doubt their own motivations, turn their allies against them, make them question if their memories are even their own. Was that an unimportant blank in your memory ? Just a fake out ? Or was it just the right thing to ensure everything proceeds by his design ? Here is a 2ed conversion: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...ed-to-2e/page4

                              Some bonus benefits to this build:

                              Durability - This villain can survive in any hostile environment and can heal wounds, Life is a tough, durable Arcanum. This ensures he can survive the players going at him with their all. It also helps leave a speck of doubt if he apparently meets his doom.

                              Pattern Building: This hacker cannot do everything on his own, he cannot guess passwords with Fate or detect all traps with Matter. This makes him need to find the right people with the info and talents, or the right imbued items. He is flexible and discrete, but he has to do some setup, this can allow him to leave a trail of breadcrumbs for the players to follow, while Prime will make them think outside the box to find out where and who he is at the time. I feel like the most rewarding thing about beating a hacker is when you drag him from the shadows, outsmart him and claim the prize you were racing to get.

                              Hope that helps, rather than cover everything a hacker could possibly need, I tried to give him flexible tools that left interesting chinks in his armor for the players to aim for.

                                  Fate will manage everything you want except for some forms of security that are beyond the mundane. Ghosts, Goetia, Spirits, Abyssal and Supernal Entities, Angels, etc. All of those could potentially be a problem. The other problem you might encounter would be purely magical security. Prime handles both of these nicely. It's easy enough to extrapolate from higher level prime powers that you could design a rote to see all levels of twilight simultaneously, you can dispel or trick magical security or force magical doors to open, close, etc, and you can mask your presence. Forces, Space, Mind could all do *some* of these, but are far less expansive and can individually only manage some of these. Fate, imo, is a very overloaded Arcana. Prime is also extremely useful for just a few key abilities at the lower levels. I've never found it useful to go to 5 dots, and 4 dots is only a marginal upgrade. You could leave it at 3 dots forever and feel just fine about it. With that, you've got everything you need, imo.
