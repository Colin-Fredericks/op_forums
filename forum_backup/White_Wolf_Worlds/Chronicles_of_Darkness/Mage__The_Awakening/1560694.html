                  The written rules are that you don't spend Reach in any manner whatsoever in codifying a Legacy Attainment.

                  Like, Reach effects are permanently embedded into and inflexible for Attainments, how exactly would substituting mana work for that? Is your total mana pool permanently down by one?

                  Frankly, considering that the primary purpose of Reach is to have something dangling over a player that they can overspend to risk Paradox, and that's just fundamentally not a function of Legacy Attainments, I think it's a good idea to read Legacy rules less in terms of applying Reach literally and more in terms of it being a useful metric to establish the limitations of the Attainments.

                  Which do generally amount to "do you want your Attainment to be used instantly or to last longer than a few seconds" and "higher Arcanum Attainments are often low Arcanum spells with a lot more conveniences".

                  Unless of course this is a proposition of being able to choose back and forth between spending mana to give an Attainment that otherwise has basic Duration the Advanced table, which refers back to the explicit rule that Legacy Attainments are inflexible by design.

                          • Attainments
                          • First: Physician's Insight
                          • Prerequisites: Initiation

                          The most fundamental skill for healing a patient is making an accurate diagnosis. The Azure Crane reads the flow of the patient’s Qi circulatory system, allowing them to holistically assess their health and tailor their treatments accordingly.

                          • This replicates Analyze Life with Reach assigned to instant use.

                          Optional: Matter 1

                          When dealing with deadly poisons or dangerous drugs, time is of the essence, and antitoxins may not always be on hand. Furnace lords have learned to use their Qi to help their patient's bodies fight off and break down these dangerous substances, reducing their toxicity by [Matter]. Should the toxicity be reduced to zero, the patient is cured of it. Because of this, it is said no furnace lord has ever lost a drinking contest.

                          • This is a custom Compelling of Life and Matter, with Reach assigned to instant use.

                          • Second: The Subtle Elixir
                          • Prerequisites: Life 2, Medicine 3, Area of Expertise (Alchemy)

                          The Azure Crane focuses their Qi into a healing pulse, which purifies the twelve main and eight extraordinary meridians, allowing the patient's Qi to flow uninterrupted once more, curing any diseases.
                          • This replicates Purge Illness. Reach is assigned to instant use.

                          Optional: Matter 2

                          Patients that have lost body parts often feel as if they were still attached. The Azure Cranes know this is caused by the Qi still flowing to the scar, a fact which can be leveraged to restore them.

                          The Furnace lord must craft or instruct someone in realizing an articulated prosthesis with artificial meridian channels and then attach it to the stump with special made acopunture needles.
                          It can be made of any materials but silver, gold and porcelaine are the most used.
                          By leading the Qi flow to synchronize with the prosthesis the lord can give to patient the ability to control its movements as a perfect replacement of the lost limb.

                          • This replicates Remote control, with conjunctional Life allowing to give control to the subject. Reach is assigned to complex tasks and Primary factor becoming Duration. The subject uses his own skills instead of Potency while using the limb. Permanence Attainment is added for Advanced duration, but detaching the prothesis cancels the effect in any case.

                          • Third: Essence of jade
                          • Prerequisites: Life 3, two dots in a second Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills

                          Utilizing their extensive alchemical knowledge and finely honed control, the Azure Cranes have learned to direct and concentrate the flow of Qi to alter the body's processes. They learn to prepare Qi infused needles, which are applied to the patient's acupoints over the course of a scene. This treatment has many beneficial effect on the various bodily functions of the subject.

                          • This replicates Body control. Reach is assigned to Advanced duration and scale (multiple subjects only).

                          Optional: Matter 3

                          The three dantian are the core of the Qi circulatory system, and by altering their nature the whole body can be strenghtened. The furnace lord is now able to infuse the dantians with protective Qi, allowing them to shield their allies, safeguard their patients and persevere on dangerous battlefields.
                          By adding needles anointed in an alchemical preparation of herbs and metals to the previous treatment the whole body of the patient can be hardened like rock or silk.


                          • This replicates Aegis, with conjunctional Life to enhance the Armor effect of Body control in the same casting. Permanence Attainment is added for Advanced duration. Reach is assigned to Advanced scale (multiple subjects only).

                          • Fourth: Meridian Reconstruction
                          • Prerequisites: Life 4, Medicine 4, Interdisciplinary Specialty (Alchemy)

                          The Azure Crane’s research has expanded their finesse to the point where they can boost and redirect the Qi circulatory system.
                          By pressing the meridian points near the injury and pushing inside a stream of Qi the healing rate briefly but drastically accelerate, sealing all but the most grievous wounds.

                          This application is very valuable on battlefields and disaster sites.

                          • This replicates Mend. Reach is assigned to instant use.

                          Optional: Matter 4

                          The Chinese word for Alchemy, liàndānshù, means "method for refining cinnabar" from the belief that inert materials like this were one of the key ingredient for attaining immortality. The furnace lord's Qi can now synthetize such elixir from a mixture of mercury, gold and various minerals.
                          The resulting pill can be activated anytime by a quick infusion of Qi and then must be ingested.

                          While its effect lasts it grants total immunity to aging but also to mundane poisons, illness and environmental hazards (but not direct damage and supernatural ones triggers a Clash of will).
                          The Crane is also able to prepare an ointment made of herbs and powder of an appropriate animal (or human) part to enhance a prothesis potential to house artificial meridian channels.
                          Limbs can restitute sensory feedback now, but an ingenious lord can build a fully functional replica of any organ (the level 2 Attainment must still be used for installation but after that simply applying the ointment renews the effects).
                          The Crane is now able to perform these and other surgical operation with no risks of infections and rejection. Patients remains conscious but fully anesthetized and doesn t requires a recovery time.


                          • This replicates Wonderful machine, with the conjunctional Life 3 effects described previously. Reach is assigned to changing primary factor to Duration and instant use (it is assumed that the user has a reasonable quantity of ready ointments and pills at any given time). Permanence Attainment is added for Advanced duration.

                          • Fifth: The Azure Furnace
                          • Prerequisites: Life 5, two dots in a third Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills

                          The furnace lord's Qi becomes both pill furnace and alchemical flame.
                          By concentrating Qi to the hands they becomes hot to the touch, allowing to execute an advanced form of Moxibustion.
                          The treatment creates new meridian channels to the patient, empowering muscles, nerves or circulation.

                          • This replicates Honing the form. Reach is assigned to going over subject limits, Advanced duration and scale (multiple subjects only). As a special effect the boosted attribute doesn t need to be the same one for each target.

                                          Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                          Have you considered that this is a proposition that would make Legacies based in the use of the Matter Arcanum just plain better than all others, by virtue of reading the rules in a manner that breaks the Fixed Reach rules? The limitations of Reach in Attainments is supposed to be one of the big constraining points on them compared to still being able to use rotes or improvised casting, and here comes a proposition of "don't worry Matter mages, you are uniquely less burdened in this department than users of all other Arcana, for reasons".
                                          I understand your balance concerns, but think about this:
                                          It s not the Legacy per se that gives this Advantage but the Arcanum.
                                          What i mean is that Matter, Fate, Time and Space have Utility attainments that greatly buffs spell factors at the cost of a single mana (not much in my experience).
                                          That said i would not consider those arcana overpowered.

                                          Legacy attainment in general only replicates spells that the mages is already capable of doing, but giving 3 advantages:
                                          -no roll, and Permanence don t modify this
                                          -no quiescence, and Permanence don t modify this
                                          -no paradox
                                          The advantage is only relative to paradox, and of course i would not underestimate it.

                                          But consider this:
                                          If you specify as a prerequisite for a Legacy Attainment dots in another Arcana (and by rules i think that you can), you can apply its Utility Attainment on a Legacy without that Arcana.
                                          I prefer this approach because Utility attainment like Sympathetic range sometimes really integrates with the flavour of a legacy that maybe has Force/Time as arcana.

                                          In conclusion, the core book gives instructions about creating those attainment but then violates them many times (the fifth attainment of the eleventh questions giving access to 2 different matter spells and using two reach, instant and sensory - the fourth even add sympathetic range!) for the sake i think of atipicity flavor.
                                          It s obvious that an approach like this generates many different opinions on what are the limits of an abusive Attainment.
                                          So i perfectly understand your doubts, but considering this uncertainty i admittedly prefer to adopt the "rule of cool", in good faith that this would not break the balance

                                              I think the attachments of prosthetics would be a Weaving. When designing the furnace lords, I was assuming that making or finding prosthetics of comparable quality to those of Götz of the Iron Hand was not an easy thing when they were created. If I understood correctly, this offshoot has a greater focus on the chemistry side of alchemy, being more effective at creating medicine and adjusting the body's chemistry. Some possible ideas for Attainments:


                                              Life 1

                                              Analyze Life with Reach assigned to instant use.

                                              Matter 1
                                              Purge Toxin with Reach assigned to instant use.

                                              Life 2
                                              Purge Illness with Reach assigned to instant use.

                                              Matter 2
                                              Discern Composition with Reach assigned to instant use and sensory range, with conjunctional Life 1 allow the user to discern all medical uses and to even scan the substances in a living being (like a troll liver being used in curing an occult plague).

                                              Life 3
                                              Heightened Senses with Reach assigned to instant use, advanced duration and changing primary factor.

                                              Remove impurities from a subject's body, allowing their senses to fire with clean, pure feedback.

                                              Matter 3
                                              Alchemist's Touch with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration. With conjunctional Life 2, the subject is also protected against lethal damage.

                                              Become able to handle the most hazardous ingredients.

                                              Life 4
                                              Mend with Reach assigned to instant use.

                                              Matter 4
                                              Golem with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration. It field is expanded to also include medicine. A single point of mana pays for Permanence.

                                              Create the perfect medical assistant, nurse, orderly or deploy more field medics.

                                              Life 5
                                              Body Control with Reach assigned to instant use, advanced duration, changing primary factor and quartering healing time per Potency. With conjunctional Matter 2 they also half lethal damage recovery times, as they can precisely control the release and production of chemicals in the body.

                                              Matter 5
                                              Transubstantiation with Reach assigned to instant use, changing primary factor and advanced duration. A point of mana pays for Permanence.

                                              Synthesize any material or medicine you have ever seen or theorized about on the fly.
