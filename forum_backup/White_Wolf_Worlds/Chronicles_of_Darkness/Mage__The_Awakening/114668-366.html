                                                        The spirit itself is a definite yes. But you can cast it on the ephemera on this side of the Gauntlet, which are like spirit algae, alive but not terribly bright. Its what Coaxing The Spirits does, it targets either a spirit or its physical representation and forces it to do an instant action. So you can turn the car on, you can also tell a pile of rocks to cause an avalanche, a safe to unlock, a door to close or a computer to let you login as admin. Anything that is not human is within its purview, including animals. All that without any need to cross the Gauntlet, it gives freshly Awakened Thrysus a lot of free utility.

                                                        Of course, if you want something fancier, then you cross the Gauntlet to find a spirit and barter for a service, convince it to empower a talen, bind it to a fetish or establish a Familiar bond. That opens up access to its Numina and Influences (Manifestations too for the Familiar option), which are much more powerful and useful. But all these options also come with a lot of interesting problems.
