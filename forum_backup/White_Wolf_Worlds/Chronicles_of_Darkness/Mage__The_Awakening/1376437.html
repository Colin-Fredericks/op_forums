    Nimbus Tilt and self.

    03-24-2020, 05:52 PM
    Greetings,


    Some questions about the Nimbus tilt:
    1. Can you affect yourself with the tilt? The core book says "If for whatever reason a character is aware of the effects, she can willingly submit to the Tilt regardless of her Resolve." and that seems to cover yourself too.
    2. In Nameless and Accursed, a character Nimbus grants a bonus to Initiative, but the core book says that only Attributes or Skills can be affected, are there other stats that can be affected besides Attributes and Skills? If so, which ones?

    Thanks for the answers.

      The Nimbus tilt does not affect the mage, otherwise they would be constantly benefiting from a buff or fighting off a debuff. A Weaving of Prime can be used to make it affect the caster.

      Initiative is Dex + Composure, so I would say applying it is reasonable.
