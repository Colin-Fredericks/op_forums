                        Okay, here is the House of Ariadne's attainments all worked out into one cohesive rough draft.
                        For more information on this Legacy, see Legacies: The Sublime, p 53 - 61

                        DOCTRINE
                        Prerequisites: Time 2, Resolve 3, and one of the following skills at 2 dots or higher: Investigation, Stealth, Survival, Streetwise, Socialize, Subterfuge
                        Initiation: Prospective Metropolitans must demonstrate patience by exploring their city and discovering a piece of their city’s secret history to share with their local House.
                        Organization: Each city has one House, regardless of the city’s size. Each House is divided into the Trackers and the Weavers. The Weavers are the elder, experienced members of the Legacy and, while they train the Trackers, they plan the City’s future and manage its secrets according to the City’s will. The Trackers spend their time finding lost people and items, uncovering the secrets and the history of their City.

                        Theory: The House of Ariadne understands that a city’s culture -- special points of pride or contempt, its modes of speaking and thinking and doing -- will outlast even its buildings. A city’s subtle, deathless power shapes mortal lives. A city is a god. It does not rule the concrete, brick, glass and wiring or the traffic, the businesses and the beggars. The City-god is them, everything, at once. Metropolitans believe all the many destinies, resonances and interconnections of Fate actually do form a diffuse entity with which they can commune and attempt to understand. Their methods provide indirect but definite proof of the City-god’s reality.
                        MAGIC
                        Ruling Arcanum: Time
                        Yantras: a place or time of great import to the city or its history (+1) finding a lost item meaningful to a community in the city (+2); teaching about the city’s culture and history (+1); using self-annotated maps of neighborhoods or blueprints of buildings relevant to the casting of the spell (+2); getting lost and navigating to a familiar area (+1); photos and souvenirs from the city relevant to the casting of the spell (+1)
                        Oblations: A walk in the city to find something you never saw before; exploring the subway tunnels; watching a sunrise or sunset from a penthouse apartment; visiting a museum or other tourist attraction; attending a party with a “list;” serving food at a homeless shelter; sleeping on a park bench.
                        First Attainment: Urban Communion
                        Prerequisites: Initiation
                        Once attuned to the city, she is its mother, sister, daughter, all at once. The Metropolitan spends one scene in communion with her city by whatever means she deems appropriate: a morning jog; feeding the pigeons in the park; reading the morning news in a random coffee shop; tagging graffiti in an alley; people-watching from a bus stop bench. For one day, the mage finds it exceedingly easy to get anywhere she wants to go in her city. This attainment works similarly to the spell “Green Light / Red Light”: doors open for the Metropolitan, taxis and elevators arrive in the knick of time, and she never catches a red light or construction detour, etc. Reach is used for Advanced Duration.
                        Optional Arcanum: Fate 1
                        If the mage spends a scene wandering the city streets, taking in the sights and partaking of a slice of the city life, the City will “speak” to her and give her guidance on how to accomplish her stated goal. This attainment works similarly to the Fate 1 spell “Serendipity” with +1 Reach.
                        This one is pretty straightforward. Metropolitans have a communion with their city that makes it easier for them to get around and mingle with folks. The city is her constant companion.

                        Second Attainment: Stir of Echoes
                        Prerequisites: Time 2, Investigation 2

                        Once the mage has reached communion with her city, she can use the Stir of Echoes attainment in order to get a feel for the city’s history. This resembles the Time 1 spell “Postcognition,” with Potency equal to the mage’s Time dots. The Metropolitan spends a scene wandering the city as she follows Ariadne’s thread and the city begins to speak to her, relating its past to the mage. While the mage might not know names, she can tell if and when a man was murdered on this street corner, or if a couple made love in a park or if a mage committed a vulgar spell in a movie theater. The symbolism of the city makes itself known to the mage as the city’s resonance applies meanings to different things. +1 Reach is assigned to Advanced Duration; +1 Reach is assigned to Sensory Range for Temporal Sympathy.
                        Optional Arcanum: Fate 2
                        The mage can also get a sense of the destinies of people, places, and objects that she observes in her city and in its history. The attainment duplicates the effects of the Fate 1 spell “Interconnections” with a Potency equal to the mage’s Fate dots. +1 Reach is assigned to Advanced Scale; +1 Reach is assigned to detect alterations to destinies, including possession and supernatural mind control.
                        EDIT: Altered the Reach Assignment to account for Temporal Sympathy/Sensory Range. This is the reconstruction of the First Edition Attainment. I'm still unsure how a fixed Potency attainment can overcome the Withstanding necessary for Temporal Sympathy. I guess it's just a matter of more power/knowledgeable mages having the ability to look back further in time. What do you guys think?

                        Third Attainment: Discovery Mediation
                        Prerequisites: Time 3, Fate 3, Investigation 3. Additionally, the Metropolitan must have at least two dots in two of the other possible skills required to learn this Legacy’s attainments.

                        This power is the bailiwick of the Metropolitans. Given enough time (and patience) Ariadne’s thread will always lead the mage to whatever she is looking for. The mage must focus her mind on what she is trying to find and spend at least one scene wandering her city to follow the Knot of Ariadne’s thread. The mage can search for any item or person, general or specific. She is even capable of finding more abstract subjects like Hallows, spatial rifts, or a specific murder-spirit (though she doesn’t have the means to observe or interact with them without the appropriate Arcana). As long as the subject is in her city, the mage doesn’t need to have sympathetic connection to the subject. Ariadne’s thread will lead her to where it is, but the path will not be direct. This process is actually an amalgamated and oblique use of Time 1s “Divination” and Fate 2s “Shifting the Odds” with Potency equal to the mage’s Time dots. Reach is assigned to Sensory Range.

                        Following the thread of Fate may take some time, and if the mage seeks a kidnapped colleague, the mage has no guarantee the person won’t be dead when the mage finds her. The meditation will not work if the subject leaves the material plane; the trail simply goes cold. If the subject of the Discovery Mediation is kept behind considerable defenses, whether locked doors, vaults or magical protection, Ariadne’s thread will not give the mage any abilities to bypass the security. The thread will surely lead the way to the desired subject, but does not tell you the best, or safest, way to get there.
                        EDIT: Assigned Reach to Sensory Range. This is the reconstruction of their First Edition 2nd Attainment. I think this works out pretty well without the need to assign Reach and stuff. What do you guys think?

                        Fourth Attainment: Into the Labyrinth
                        Prerequisites: Time 4, Investigation 3, Streetwise 3. Additionally, the Metropolitan must have at least 3 dots in one other Doctrine skill or two dots in three of the possible Doctrine skills.
                        In times of dire need, the Metropolitan can call upon her city to to protect her from adversaries. Ariadne’s thread leads her to exactly the right place at the right time, while Fate itself conspires to keep her enemies at bay. Anyone foolish enough to follow her will find themselves in a place that has suddenly become a confusing maze filled with danger. The mage spends 1 mana and an instant action to activate this attainment, which confers the benefits of the Safe Place Merit equivalent to the mage’s Time dots for one scene. As long as the mage stays in the same general location (inside an apartment building, wandering the same city block, in a crowded mall) she is afforded this protection.
                        This is a new effect inspired by the whole Ariadne myth. I know that the House of Ariadne stirs up trouble and I want to give them an attainment that works as a form of self-defense. Again, the idea is that the Metropolitan is in communion with her city and she is never alone. The city itself will rally to her defense when she needs it to. I wasn't entirely sure if this should be the Third or Fourth Attainment. I think both effects are pretty cool and powerful. I think I made it the Fourth Attainment just to break up the pattern of divination powers.


                        Fifth Attainment: Geomantic Meditation
                        Prerequisites: Time 5, Resolve 4, Investigation 4. Additionally, the Metropolitan must have 3 dots in three other Doctrine skills or two or more dots in all possible Doctrine skills.

                        With this power, the House of Ariadne move from their role as discoverers of their city’s secrets to the Keepers of their city and its destiny. To use this attainment, the mage must fix a question in her mind and being walking the streets. She will find an answer, if such an answer exists at all. This spell duplicates the effects of the Time 4 spell “Prophecy” with Potency equal to the mage’s Time dots and +1 Reach assigned to Sensory Range. The mage obtains advice on the consequences of a planned course of action or a course of action to bring about any desired result. At the Storyteller’s discretion, the mage may walk for one hour or one day, but she must keep walking until she gets her answer. Ariadne’s thread will eventually lead the mage to a series of symbols that give her answer. Although the answer may not be apparent to any companion to the mage, she will understand its meaning and what the geomantic meditation is telling her. If the city does not know the answer to the question, or the question is too vague, the thread may lead the mage in circles or nowhere at all.

                        Optional Arcanum: Fate 4
                        If the mage also has Fate 4, she finds that her city bolsters her resolve to accomplish her task. She gains the benefit of the Fate 3 spell “Superlative Luck,” with a Potency equal to her Fate dots, for any roll related to acting on the information learned during the Geomantic Meditation. +1 Reach is assigned to Instant activation; +2 Reach is assigned so that this effect works with ritual spell-casting rolls dedicated to acting on the information learned by using this attainment.
                        EDIT: Assigned Reach to Sensory Range. This is the reconstruction of the First Edition attainment. I added the optional Fate component because it's in keeping with the House of Ariadne's theme and beliefs.

                        Does this stuff work or is it totally off the wall?

                                    It's sensory range because the actual time that you're targeting cannot be sensed or reached by your mundane abilities. It makes total sense for them to commune with the city physically for their answers, but in the mechanics of the game a Reach still gets spent for Sensory. If it makes you feel better you can think of it as reach out to touch not only the stone of today, but of yesterday/tomorrow as well, communing with the city on its entire temporal spectrum which you simply can't do with mundane senses.

                                    You don't need Advanced Duration to cast a spell, all the effects occur at the end. If feeding the pigeons is a necessary ritual to achieve the effects of the spell the results of the spell still only need to be Instant. Walking the Thread doesn't need to be the spell itself (Though have a look at Correspondence from Space which basically does what Thread Walking did minus hints of significant threads from Fate) as it could also easily be the ritual scene that kicks the spell off.

                                            Name: Blank Badges

                                            Optional Arcanum: Prime
                                            Example Yantras: Acting against the rules in a way that poses risk to the mage (+2), breaking taboos (+1),
                                            Example Oblations: Participating in "alternative" culture, Attending a meeting not invited, Blending in the crowd. Escaping pushinment from authority.
                                            Requirements: the recruit needs at least two dots in Occultation. Accesing the higher lvl attainments requires aditional dots. Higher lvl attainments require also dots in Subtefuge

                                            1. Attainment: Us or them
                                            Req: Mind 1, Occultation 2
                                            When you're fighting in a secret war you need to know who you can trust (to a certain degree) and who you can't. This effect mimics the effects of the spell Mental Scan though the only information the mage can gleam is how the subject feels towards the Badges ideology. The possible results are aproves/indifferent/disaproves.
                                            Optional: Prime 1
                                            In addition the mage can pierce any lies told by "the enemy" (everyone that got registered as "disaproves" by the basic version of the attainments).
                                            I had some problems with coming up the first attainments but I think they're ok

                                            2. Attainment: We are Legion
                                            Req: Mind 2, Occultation 3
                                            The mage can become a part of a collective perfectly blending in the crowd becoming anonymous. This replicates the effect of the spell Incognito presence but it can only be used if there's a big enough group of people to blend in. This effects gets an aditional reach to access advanted duration but the mage must be part of a group of sleepers for it to work.
                                            Optional: Prime 2
                                            The mage's Nimbus becomes nondescript as if an amalganam of many. This mimics the spell Cloak Nimbus with the exception that he distays a collective Nimbus of the Legacy. This makes investigation incredibly hard but is a fairly specifc effect and someone who has previously seen a Nimbus of a member of the legacy using this attainment can recognise the effect.
                                            These felt pretty much natural

                                            3 Attainment: Not a Number
                                            Req: Mind 3, Occultation 4
                                            The mage not only blends into the crowd, he trully belongs and knows how to act. When interacting with a group the mage gains dots equal to his mind dots in a single skill that would allowhim to blend better with the group. While speaking with members of a street gang he gets dots in streetwise or larcery. Running in a marathon would grant athletics, while attenting a activist gathering grant dots in politics. The mage must have at least one dot in the skill.
                                            Optional: Prime 3
                                            The mage can hide himself or his spells as the effect of Supernal Veill with an additional 1 Reach to spend (mostly used to access advanced duration)
                                            Nothing awesome here but i think the effects fit

                                            4 Attainment: Sleeper agent

                                                            Celestial Orrery Rough Draft (Further fixes) One thing I'm not entirely sure on, does looking into the future require Temporal Sympathy? On the one hand it makes sense, you're reaching into a Time that isn't the Present, on the other hand there's this quote from the book and the fact that the 11Q spends Reach to hit Sensory Range which implies (But doesn't directly state) the use of Temporal Sympathy. It could just as easily be about the advantage of being able to look at a target to determine what it will do next and how to get it to do what you want to do without having to touch it. This is kind of important in terms of the Augurs which as I've noted don't give much of a damn about changing the past since they focus on the future. That being said, I'm going to change up the 3rd and 4th attainments specifically to more closely mimic the original legacy. Does it cost a Reach to combine spells? It's late so I could really use the clarification. Thanks guys:
                                                            Magic that affects the “unspun” time of the future is unaffectedby temporal sympathy; the shifting potential timelines have no “substance” for magic to contend with
                                                            Keep the Oblations, needs Yantras

                                                            Attainments:

                                                            1)
                                                            Time: Momentary Flux Reach spent for Instant activation
                                                            Prime: Pierce Deception Reach spent on Instant activation

                                                            2)
                                                            Time: Divination Reach spent on Instant activation and Sensory Range.
                                                            Prime: Made up Compelling (Or Ruling) effect to duplicate the 1e effect of a Hallow paying the mana cost of a spell equal to Potency (Though it can't exceed the amount of mana currently in the Hallow). Reach spent on Instant activation.

                                                            3)
                                                            Time: Combines the effects of Shield of Chronos and Constant Presence. Reach is spent for Instant Activation and Advance Duration.
                                                            Prime: Sacred Geometry Reach for Advanced and Indefinite Duration as well as the +1 Reach Clause

                                                            4)
                                                            Time: Temporal version of Web Weaver. Reach is spent on Instant Activation and Sensory Range
                                                            Prime: Words of Truth. Reach spent for Instant Activation, Advanced Scale and the Reach Clause of the spell.

                                                            5)
                                                            Time: Prophecy: One Reach spent for Instant Activation and the Sensory Range.
                                                            Prime: This mimics the effects of Geomancy with Reach spent on Advanced and Indefinite Duration and all left over Reach being spent on an effect to convert a Node into a Hallow with dots equal to Potency (2 if I understand secondary factors correctly).
