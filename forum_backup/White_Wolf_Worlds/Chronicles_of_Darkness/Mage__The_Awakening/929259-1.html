                        Yeah - bear in mind that some Legacies will end up rather different from their first-edition versions, which is a good thing; the changes to the setting, especially the historicity of it and the clearer post-Order books view of how Mage Society functions, would be short-changed if we just stuck with updating Attainments.

                        Look at the Eleventh Question's backstory in 2e compared to the original in Guardians of the Veil.

                        In 2e, the House of Ariadne were a Seer Legacy that had a Diamond-based offshoot appear via stolen soul stones. (Why should such things always be portrayed as going the other way? This isn't Masquerade's Antitribu)

                        In 2e, the Cryptologos are a Prime Legacy with things like Words of Truth as their Attainments.

                        We tried to seed lots of hints into those two-sentence write-ups in the corebook. The Daksha allowing Guardian members is no accident. It just looks weird at the moment.

                                            Actually, you don't need a student mentor thing if you don't want to.
