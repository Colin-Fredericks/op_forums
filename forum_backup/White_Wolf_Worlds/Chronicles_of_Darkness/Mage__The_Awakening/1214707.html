    X-Ray Vision

    05-03-2018, 02:58 PM
    "Tri-Clops, master of Vision! I will need your gamma vision which can see around solid objects!" "The Diamond ray of Disappearance," from the original ​He-Man and the Masters of the Universe.

    A simple question: how would you represent the ability to selectively see through opaque matter, more or less the way Superman's X-ray vision is depicted? Space-based scrying doesn't really seem to fit the bill.
