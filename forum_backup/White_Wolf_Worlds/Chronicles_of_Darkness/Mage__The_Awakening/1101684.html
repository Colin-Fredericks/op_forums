    ​[2E Mystery] A town with bad time.

    06-08-2017, 01:59 PM

    http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...y-sheet-for-2e
    A town with bad time.
    Other names: The Longview Affect,
    Concept: Town designed to harness various mystical energies to imbue cult leaders with Immortality via an Abyssal realm that annihilates Time and causality within a twisted timeless misty version of the town.
    Range: Isolated to a single town, but potentially could affect several other locations that meet the criteria
    Location: Longview WA
    Important NPCs: Asah arath githagarum athi kuth, (An ephemeral being which acts as the heart of the town and this Mystery, Translated means Tainted-Slave-Town-Trial),
    Described in: Certain restricted mage libraries in the Seattle WA Assembly and Portland OR Consilium,
    Locked With Mysteries: Ordeals (See Below)

    Peripheral Mage Sight,
    Clash of Wills: Yes
    Dice Pool: Asah arath githagarum athi kuth 10

    ActiveMage Sight
    Main Arcanum: Time
    Supernal Magic: No

    Focused Mage Sight
    Opacity: 7

    Surface Information ​
    The town of Longview Washington is a known to be a place where mages go missing, especially mages knowledgeable in the Time Arcanum, and the entire place is abyssally tainted. The Consilim of Portland has forbid any exploration of the Mysteries or setting up residence there and Assembly of Seattle a strong official caution against those activities as well.
    Prerequisite to look deeper: Must actually be within Longview or use postcognition on an item or being from Longview to the time when it was within Longview.

    Rest of Arcana: Death for soul, Vitae and Plasm draining; Spirit for Essence draining; Prime for Mana draining; Fate for Glamour draining
    Resonance: Hidden
    Age: The 1920's
    Power Level - Arcanum Rating: 5 Practice: Unmaking

    Deep Information

    Level 7 Upon piercing this level of Opacity it becomes obvious Time is warped horribly here. Looking into the past always causes those that do so to gain a Paradox condition, even if they are not using supernal magic, and even piercing any level of this Mystery counts towards this end due to the glimpses of time itself implied by the revelations. The weaker the natural pre-modified Temporal Sympathy the more horribly twisted the past become, people and animals become unrecognizable monstrosities of oozing lumbering gibbering slavering insanity and the landscape becomes a fleshy alien hellish vista as Temporal Sympathy fades to weak. Using magic to bolster the Temporal Sympathy does make viewing the past easier but doesn't alleviate this abyssal perspective. Since the founding of the town is the threshold past which the city only has weak Temporal Sympathy with itself, viewing the landscape of Longview in those times can cause severe mental duress justifying breaking points for Integrity, Humanity, Clarity, and Wisdom. Viewing items taken from Longview back to a time when they were in Longview carries the exact same effect, when viewing that item.
    Prerequisite to look deeper: Must have resolved and be clear of all Paradox conditions gained by looking into the past within Longview. Must actually be within Longview.

    Level 6 Irises appear that swallow people into a twisted mist-filled timeless version of the town that appears reactive to souls that enter it, in particular to their sins real and imagined. Those that pierce this level of Opacity are subjected to this, and if they succeed in overcoming Ordeals caused by their soul's sins they find an Iris out, gaining an Unssen Sense that allows them to find other Irises into the realm within the borders of Longview. Within the realm the Time Arcanum operates as the Space Arcanum losing all its normal abilities in the substitution, and the Space Arcanum does not function at all. Any use of the Time Arcanum always results in gaining a Paradox Condition while within the realm. It appears that every version of the town exists simultaneously that can be traversed through the heavy mists. When Ordeals are failed they leave a stained perverse version that can be discovered by those exploring the realm, a memorial to that soul's failure to deal with their sin. Irises are subjective available to keyed specific souls, rather than objective phenomena anyone can experience, meaning an iris available and sensed by one person are not the same as those experienced by another.
    Prerequisite to look deeper: Must have gained the Unseen Sense from overcoming the Ordeal. Must have resolved and be clear of all Paradox conditions gained by looking into the past within Longview. Must actually be within Longview.

    Level 5 Upon seeing past the veil of this opacity you have the Supernal image of every member of the Mystery Cult of Fellowship of the Framer's Fidelity of rank Enlightened and below etched into your mind as you receive the revelation members of the cult will likewise recognize you as Enlightened, a flood of many secrets of the town, and how the town of Longview is going to ultimately devour you literally or symbolically (Granting Mystery Cult Initiation 3 in Fellowship of the Framer's Fidelity). How the town is destined to devour you can be terrifyingly revealing of tidbits about deeper levels of this mystery, as odds are it's the investigation of this mystery that are the direct or indirect cause of that event. You also know for a fact looking deeper will somehow bind you to the abyssal intrusion, but not exactly how. You know you'll need to be in another Ordeal to gain more insight.
    Prerequisite to look deeper: Must have resolved and be clear of all Paradox conditions gained by looking into the past within Longview. Must actually be within Longview. Must willing choose to enter an Iris to the Abyssal Realm this town creates and use the focused mage sight there.

    Level 4 An epiphany assaults the mind of the mage that what was first learned upon starting to unveil this mystery is far worse than initially suspected: it isn't a mere filter of perception of the past but the ACTUAL past which has changed. Things with only a weak Temporal Sympathy did exist in those abyssal states while within Longview, because the abyss is overwriting the actual past as its links to present become weak. This does not change the memories of the past, which remain based in how things were when the past was the present but has horrific consequences should one of the abyssal replacements be brought forward in Time. Doing so is essentially summoning forth an abyssal intrusion into the present where it had only existed in the past. The Mage gains the Fugue, Broken or Madness Condition in addition to the standard Paradox Condition for seeing through this level of the mystery. The knowledge that having merely been in Longview, you and everyone else that has a section of their history in which you're all an abyssal intrusion and to have seen that as a Supernal Truth is traumatic. You know to look deeper is to welcome the intrusion yourself personally.
    Prerequisite to look deeper: Must actually be within Longview.

    Level 3 You are swallowed by a Time Iris to a time where you were present and actively participating in a ceremony causing the Abyssal intrusion under the town during its founding, swear a Blood Oath of Fealty to an Invictus Vampire named Thomas Adams, one of the main conspirator of the town's creation in a secret chamber under the towns center that remains Unchanged (and indeed is unchangeable) since the day of its creation. The ceremony involves the Fealty Oaths of the Framers around a polygamous marriage bed/altar upon which Adams consummates his vows with his various Wild Rose brides as they also do a Blood Oath of Fealty to him as mages that have pierced this level of the mystery appear and disappear to contribute their Supernal will to the effort to create the intrusion. It appears the stones of the chamber were mason in the Time Before, an Atlantian ruin/Artifact in perfect repair. You just know the room was meant to be a small cache library unaffected by the ravages of Time but the ceremony corrupts that affect with the Abyss and is the source of the Intrusion. Having pushed this far, knowing that you were welcoming the intrusion you have predestined yourself to using your supernal magic to actively assist in the intrusion during this event. This may not be countered or edited out of Time. Roll your Time Arcanum with a success indicating you are consumed mind, body, and soul into the abyss by the magical forces in play, failing indicating you contributed but were not found to be a valid fuel source. Upon the end of your part of the ceremony another Time Iris returns you to the instant of your departure (Granting the fourth dot in Mystery Cult Initiation in Fellowship of the Framer's Fidelity). If you already had the fourth dot you instead gain the fifth dot, and if you had the fifth dot you gain no other benefit. You know you'll need to defeat another Ordeal to gain more insight, and that insight will only come if you attempt to see past the mystery before entering the material realm.
    Prerequisite to look deeper: Must actually be within Longview. Must willing choose to enter an Iris to the Abyssal Realm this town creates and use the focused mage sight at the site of the exit Iris before leaving.

    Level 2 You sense anyone having participated in the Marriage Ceremony which corrupts the town can will themselves to be transformed and bound to the timeless nature of the abyssal realm created by the town, if they will it so as they pass through the exit. (This grants justification for the purchase of the fifth dot of Mystery Cult Initiation in Fellowship of the Framer's Fidelity)
    Prerequisite to look deeper: Must actually be within Longview. Must be in the presence of Asah arath githagarum athi kuth.

    Level 1 This town acts as an abyssal trap which converts the past into a growing swath of the abyss, capturing beings into cycles of torment disguised as immortality. The town hungers for power and is actively feeding upon the world. It is fed like a leech on massive leylines and essence flows which make starving it impossible without complete rearranging the Pacific Northwest, from the Cowlizt and Columbia Rivers to the I-5 between Seattle and Portland. It devours mages with particular zeal, but does not limit its appetite to solely them. Any type of supernatural power is drained and taxxed by the town.

    Gnosis Equivalent: 10
    Realms Invisible: Abyss
    Specific Nature: Abyssal Realm
    Effects on the World: Draining souls, Vitae, Mana, Essense, Glamour, and Plasm; Empowering two Mystery Cults, Creating an abyssal realm and the Ordeal Mysteries
    Scale and Strength:
    Similar to Mysteries: Yellow Sign, The Nemesis Continuum
    Arcana Relation:
    Actions Involving:

    Phenomena and political environment:
    Blood spilled on Unmarked Graves within the town of Longview WA produce one less Vitae than would be expected. Gangrel who've noticed this call it the town's blood tax and there are rumors some Devotion of Unmarked Grave by a slumbering elder under the town may be the cause, in particular the name Thomas Adams is whispered in these rumors, along with his Wild Rose brides which are known to represent his interests in local vampire courts in the pacific Northwest. Traveling Gangrel have also noticed that Longview seems a pit stop where those that would cause trouble disappear but those just needing to rest before moving on seem somehow protected (by those that would confront them within the town disappearing); this has given rise to the local adage "Do not pursue quarry into Longview".

    The sparse Hallows in Longview can be counted on a single hand, the Attic at 805 Lone Oak Rd on Beacon Hill, St John's Hospital Helipad, an abandoned clearing atop Mt Solo, and the roof of the Pacific Telegraph & Telephone Building. These provide less than normal Mana than would be expected by how powerful they seem, which isn't much in the first place. New Hallows are extremely hard to create, and given the politics of Portland and Seattle no one really tries. Poaching of the publicly accessible Hallows is common, and Guardians of the Veil from the Portland Consilim are rumored to lose people every so often that raid the "rumored" Scelesti residents of the Lone Oak Road Hallow.

    The Portland Privateers frequently raid Longview for those they trade to the True Fae in the Portland Saturday Goblin Market, and the Fetches created somehow seem wrong, this doesn't particularly bother the Privateers as they merely want a portion of the Fetch's unemployment check or part of their meager earnings and not actual service. It is unsettling (and frequently dangerous) for the changelings attempting to reclaim their lives that were taken from Longview, and that suites the Privateers just fine as well. No glamour-bearing fruit grows within the boundaries of Longview's Hedge.

    Longview does have haunts and its fair share of powerful ghosts and geists, but the dead are hungrily more active, which makes it seems like it has more than its fair share. This is because the ghosts are having their Plasm drawn down like a fine string into the town itself, slow but steady. Weak ghosts don't last in Longview, meaning only those with the force of will to find sources of Plasm last, making the town much more interesting than the usual ghostscape.

    The spirits of Longview are in a shifting ecosystem where the town is struggling between collapse under meth addled unemployment and gentrification. Cheap Hydroelectric power and large waterways to dump into with little downstream keeps chemical plants humming, but the lumber industry has left its mark on the spiritscape. The divide between poverty and comfort is as stark as opposites sides of the same street in some neghborhoods. The Essence flows are powerful with long established routes and while the Loci produce less Essence than expected that small loss doesn't reduce the spiritual complexity nor does lead to a lack of spirits. If anything the spirits are just as numerous and powerful if more hungry and active. Werewolves packs stalk this area fully aware of Asah arath githagarum athi kuth, the city spirit which isn't a spirit. Werewolves and even spirits watch their figurative toes around the being as it is eager to send those that cause trouble in the material realm to another realm that is unlike hisil, where nothing generates Essence. Savvy werewolves call the realm Asah arath githagarum athi kuth's belly, and those that have escaped by undergoing an Ordeal seek to avoid a repeat visit. All this said, that does not stop the wolf's need to hunt, and the hunts here are just more subtle in the material world as befits a small town with little violence. This has also led to Longview being viewed as neutral ground by the Western Washington and Northwestern Oregon Uratha.

    Name: Asah arath githagarum athi kuth Type: Abyssal Ephemeral Entity
    Rank: 5 Size: 5

    Power: 15 Finesses: 10 Resistance: 14 Essence: 50/50
    Corpus: 19 Willpower: 10 Initiative: 26 Defense: 10 Speed: 31

    Ban: May never leave Longview/Kelso township

    Influences: Passages 5 Manifestations: Twilight Form, Discoporate, Gateway (Abyssal Realm), Gateway (Hisil), Gateway (Hedge), Fetter (Longview)

    Numina/Aspects:

    Unbreakable

    The creature is nearly indestructible. Any attack that does not score an exceptional success inflicts only a single point of bashing damage. Exceptional successes inflict damage as normal. If the creature has a weakness of some sort, attacks incorporating that weakness bypass this power.

    Resistant by wyrdhamster

    Entity resist Supernal Magic much more efficiently than other of her kind – she get’s automatic Withstand source equal to its Rank against Awakened spells. That means she generally have Rank+1 Withstand, as its Attributes are normal source of Withstand, see rules of Withstanding, MtAw 2E page 114.

    Flesh of Abyss by wyrdhamster ( idea from amechra )

    Entity is build from the stuff of Abyss itself. That means, Supernal Magic have really harder time to interact with it. Each spell cast on entity is treated as one Spell Factor lower – that means that attack spells makes one damage lower, Scale is treated as needing to get Size 6 to work on it and Duration is shorter than caster imagined when making Imago.

    Presence of Paradox by amechra

    The ephemeral being is thoroughly tainted by the stuff of the Abyss - treat it as if it were an entire crowd of Sleepers for the purposes of calculating Paradox dice pools, except it adds dice equal to its Rank rather than just one. In addition, it may spend Essence reflexively to cancel Mana spent to mitigate a Paradox dice pool on a one-to-one basis.

    That-Which-Must-Not-Be-Named by me

    The ephemeral being is capable of being seen in any spectrum or frequency of Twilight by beings capable of seeing into Twilight, and is able to interact with any being in Twilight and when in Twilight counts as a valid target for things that can affect beings within Twilight. It however does not count as a spirit, geotia, ghost, geist, supernal being, or any other 'type' of ephemeral being for purposes of targeting effects. It has no "type", other than being an Abyssal Ephemeral Entity. As an abyssal entity it uses the rules on page 252 MtA except where it lists the specific affects of being a Gulmoth or a Acamoth, as it is neither of those. Also for purposes of Sympathy casters are considered to not know its name, regaurdless of how it is or is not addressed.

    Immortal

    As per the Dread Power in CofD (Please note this is above what Spirits are cabable of as lacking Essence upon death is no issue, and is the next scene to reappear). Bane is a dagger carved from the bleached bones of a long term resident of Longview who moved away and never moved back. Resurrection Clause: The Newmoon after someone enters an Iris to the Abyssal realm that the town creates.

    That-Which-Must-Not-Be-Seen by me

    The ephemeral being is anithetical to the discovery of secrets and powers that reveal information. Such active or deliberate use of powers while the entity in the perception of the user applies Broken, Maddenss, or Fugue Condition those attempting to use the power. This applies from Auspex, various Uratha gifts that reveal information, Numena like Omen or Seek, to anything more than Peripheral Mage Sight, and any other power which is reasonably similar to those examples. Also the Condition is considered to be caused as if from resistent source meaning supernatural affects fail to remove the condition, and they must be resolved naturally. Additionally rolls of Failure are downgraded to Dramatic Failures, Success are downgraded to Failures, and Exceptional Success are downgraded to a mere success, after the completion of all the rolls in the case of extended rolls.

    Absence of Time by amechra
    A version of this Numina exists for each Arcana; the ephemeral being is always considered to be an invalid target for Supernal magic belonging to that Arcana. In addition, any spell of that Arcana that interacts (even indirectly) with the ephemeral being unravels as if exposed to 5 successes on a Dissonance roll.

    No Escape by GibberingEloquence

    The entity is considered to perceive everything in its surroundings as if it had the "Outward And Inward Eye" spell cast with the +2 Reach option, which cannot be dispelled. All spells cast with the intent of getting away from the entity, transporting it, warding against it or summoning outside help trigger a Clash Of Wills consisting of Power + Rank vs Gnosis + Arcanum.

    Furthermore, Paradoxes generated in its presence form sympathetic links between it and the sources of the Paradoxes. Every 2 Paradox Reaches may be spent to create a Weak Spatial Sympathy between the entity and a target, or upgrade the Sympathy by one step, up to Strong. The entity may use Scrying on any subjects it has at least a Weak connection to, and may activate its Numina and Influences Sympathetically with 1 Essence (but not Manifestations, and only some Abyssal Aspects). These links last for the normal period listed under Paradox Anomalies.

    Surprise Entrance

    No matter how secure its victims think they are, by spending 1 Willpower the creature may suddenly appear in
    the scene. Able to appear anywhere within Longview, and nothing within Longview can bar its path. Any character witnessing this entrance must succeed on a reflexive Resolve + Composure roll or gain the Shaken Condition.
    If the characters have actively taken appropriate measures to keep the entity out (Warding against it, confining yourself to a small space which doesn't have room for it and you, ect...) it will take the creature
    one full turn to break through, giving the characters time to react. If not, it’s just there as a reflexive action and can take one more action before anyone can react.


    Eerie Disappearance by me

    No matter how safe its victims think they are, by spending 1 Willpower the entity can suddenly draw any being to which it has a weak or greater Spacial Sympathy into a scene where it currently is. Any character witnessing the character's disappearance must succeed on a reflexive Resolve + Composure roll or gain the Shaken Condition.
    If the characters have actively taken appropriate measures to keep the entity from taking them (Warding against it, Trying to get people to pay attention to them so it is harder for them to disappear, ect...) it will take one full turn of the entity's effort for the character to simply pop into the intended scene, giving the character time to react to the sense of impending dread of being taken. If not, it’s just happens as a reflexive action and the creature can also take one more action before anyone can react.

    Ordeals
    Other names: Soul Trials, Personal Purgatory
    Concept: Deadly journeys of self discover that manifest due to an Abyssal Realm reacting to the sins on a character's soul.
    Range: Isolated to a single town, but potentially could affect several other locations that meet the criteria
    Location: Longview WA
    Important NPCs: Asah arath githagarum athi kuth (An ephemeral being which acts as the heart of the town and this Mystery, Translated means Tainted-Slave-Town-Trial),
    Described in: Certain restricted mage libraries in the Seattle Washington Assembly and Portland Oregon Consilium,
    Locked With Mysteries: None

    Peripheral Mage Sight,
    Clash of Wills: Yes
    Dice Pool: Asah arath githagarum athi kuth 10

    ActiveMage Sight
    Main Arcanum: Death
    Supernal Magic: No

    Focused Mage Sight
    Opacity: Varies by individual, see surface information.

    Surface Information ​
    Each being with a soul that enters the abyssal realm created by Longview causes a unique mystery each time it enters. This mystery is the specific way mists of the realm react to the sins of the soul, generating an Ordeal which is a potentially fatal journey of self discovery. Each level of opacity the mystery has represents an encounter that must happen in the twisted version of the town to find the excape Iris back to the material world. More supernaturally potent beings have more complex multistaged ordeals, beings must undergo Supernatural Tolerance stat +1 encounters to have the option to excape. Seeing past each opacity level will reveal how to locate and initiate the next encounter. For those without focused mage sight they must uncover Clues by investigating the town, trading a bonus of +5 worth of clues for access to the next encounter, as the town reveals its desires cloaked in a veil. It is possible to investigate other people's Ordeal both with mage sight or investigating through teamwork. There is no way to excape until the character's Ordeal is done.
    Prerequisite to look deeper: Have the ability to examine the being going through the Ordeal.

    Rest of Arcana: Mind
    Resonance: Hidden
    Age: The 1920's
    Power Level - Arcanum Rating: 5 Practice: Unmaking

    Deep Information

    Level 11-3 Merely the location and how to trigger the next encounter needed to progress in the mystery, and what that victory criteria was for the encounter that was resolved before attempting to pierce this level of Opacity (Top level revealing entering the realm was the "victory" criteria).
    Prerequisite to look deeper: Achieving the victory criteria for the current and previous encounters.

    Level 2 As above, plus realizing while within misty town during an Ordeal death stops for you, you will not die unless you fail an encounter trying to escape, and time relative to the world outside of the abyssal realm does not pass, meaning the relative clock only starts after the finish of the Ordeal. This leads to strange interactions when travelers in the middle of their Ordeal who entered at different times meet each other.
    Prerequisite to look deeper: Achieving the victory criteria for the current and previous encounters.

    Level 1 The nature and symbolic significance of the sins that formed the basis of the encounters are revealed as well as an Iris leading to the material world which only exists subjectively for the person who resolved their Ordeal. This is revealed if the encounter is resolved even without Mage Sight.

    Realms Invisible: Abyss
    Specific Nature: Abyssal Realm's reaction to souls
    Effects on the World: Shapes and molds access to the various versions of the town within the abyssal realm ; Empowering two Mystery Cults
