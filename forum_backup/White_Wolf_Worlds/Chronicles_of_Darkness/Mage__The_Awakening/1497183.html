          Nimbus Spinners
          A legacy of Nimbus Artists

          Nicknames: The Crowned, Coronals, Spinners, Pollyannas (Derogatory)
          Organization: The Spinners are often social, and many maintain regular contact with other Spinners, to discuss techniques, new Nimbus related phenomena or to give and receive criticism. Small groups sometimes form, but many prefer to do their actual work by themselves. A dedicated cabal is unheard of, many instead attach themselves to other cabals.

          Many Spinners are involved in the trade of Magic Services, adding decorations only visible to Mage Sight to Sanctums and examining and advising others on shaping the Nimbus. some even act as detectives, deducing how and why a mysterious Nimbus appears the way it does.
          Theory: The Nimbus is the most Sublime Medium
          Background: The legacy is thought to quite old, but little remains of the original Artworks. The Legacy's modern origins are traced to a Nimbus weaved Banner, containing the combined Nimbuses of a Cabal. Many Members of the Legacy are eccentric, due to the requirements of methods of shaping their own Nimbus. Anyone can join the legacy, as the Initiation is well know to the boarder Awakened community.


          Parentage: Obrimos; Free Council, Silver Ladder
          Ruling Arcanum: Prime
          Yantras: Thematic Nimbus Imprints (+1), Mundane Artist Tools(+1), Imago Modifies or examines the Nimbus (+1), success on an Expression roll relating to the spell (+2)
          Oblations: Detailed Planning of New Art, Examining a New Nimbus, Giving Heartfelt Artistic Criticism

          Prerequisites: Prime 2, Taste (Nimbus Art), Expression 2, Occult Specialty (Nimbus)
          Initiation: Creating your first Nimbus Art, and Scrutinizing it

          Artist’s Eye
          (Prime ●) Initiation
          Working deeply with Nimbuses gives an Spinner insight into what gives the Nimbus it's form.
          Nimbus Tuning
          Potency = Prime
          Duration = N/A (1)
          Scale = All extra factors to Scale
          Range = Touch
          Casting Time = Instant
          COMMENTARY: The artist should know their paints


          (Space ●)
          One Learns quickly that the canvas is almost as Important to Artwork as the Artwork itself
          The Outward and Inward Eye
          Potency = Space
          Duration = Space (Reach to Advanced Duration )
          Range = Touch
          Casting Time = Ritual

          COMMENTARY: Really only the basic Space spell that fits, a Artist needs to see their work from every angle .


          The Work
          (Prime ●●) 3 ● of Nimbus Merits
          The Spinner Makes Nimbus Art out of their own Nimbus
          Supernal Signature
          Duration = Unique (Lasts like a normal Nimbus Impression)
          Potency = Prime
          Scale = All extra factors to Scale
          Range = Touch
          Casting Time = Instant (Reach to Instant Casting)

          NOTE: Creates artwork form the Nimbus. The shape and details of the signature, as long as it related to the themes of the nimbus. For example, one with a Nimbus of an angel protector may make angelic statues, shields, feelings of warmth and safety, or the sensation that one is being watched over, perhaps all at the same time. The artwork can create emotional conditions, much like other kinds of artwork.
          COMMENTARY: The first Artwork. (no, I couldn't think of a better name)

          (Space ●●)
          Locate Object
          Potency = N/A (1)
          Duration = Space
          Scale = All extra factors to Scale
          Range = Unique (Only targeting own Nimbus Art)
          Casting Time = Instant (Reach to Instant Casting)

          COMMENTARY: A Side spell, mainly to make the artwork more useful in and of it's self.


          Chose the Hue
          (Prime ●●●) Expression 4, 5 ● of Nimbus Merits
          The Spinner focuses and works their Nimbus into a new palette.
          Nimbus Forgery/Alter Nimbus

          Extra: (Reach to modify all three Nimbuses)

          COMMENTARY: Customize your nimbus. At this point the Nimbus should be rather powerful, and being able to freely customize it should be fairly potent. When combined with the second attainment, one can make art of anything. This blog might help with examples.

          Secrets and Storyhooks
          ● A well respected, but ageing Coronal has announced their retirement, along with their plans to more or less permanently move to a pocket dimension of their own creation, leaving their sanctum estate to their apprentice. Their apprentice, however seemingly wants nothing to do with the place, and privately seeks out several up and coming cabals to challenge them to find and return a specific Grimoire to them from inside the estate. Do that, and the Sanctum is theirs. What could possibly motivate the apprentice to give up such a prime location? What's in the Grimoire? How does one even make it past the foyer mobius strip?

          ● The local Spinner has gone rapt. At least that's what everyone assumes, when their Artwork all simultaneously burst open like a over ripe fruit, leaking raw Supernal energy into the nearby surroundings. Worse still, they appear to have been way way more productive than anyone thought, with their Art showing up in large quantities in the absolutely weirdest places. Some even reappear after being destroyed. No one can seem to find the Spinner, and people are starting to get desperate.

          ●[WIP]
