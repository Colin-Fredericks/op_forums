    Originally posted by Emerus View Post
    Question: how can someone enter the shadow? Can someone just walk through a locus?
    Without a Verge or supernatural "assistance" it would require having some sort of ability to cross the Gauntlet. A Locus is just a place where the Gauntlet is so thin it becomes a lot easier to get through the Gauntlet there (on top of a few other effects) but without the ability to pass the Gauntlet it doesn't matter how thin the Gauntlet is.
    A Verge is where the Gauntlet has a hole in it. Those you could just walk through. Depending on the nature of the Verge it might be a little more complex than that.

                                03-29-2020, 09:03 AM
                                Originally posted by Emerus View Post
                                Question: Would an item (like a ring) that would affect its wearer with a spell passively (like nightvision) be an enhanced or an imbued item?
                                Also is anyone aware of any previous post or an external source that explains the process of imbuing an item a bit more than the core 2e book?
                                I don't think it's possible to create such items except through homebrew.
                                Enhanced items have spells cast upon the item itself. You could have the spell be AoE, but then the spell would constantly be active on the ring as well as anyone (or anything) close enough to the ring regardless of whether the ring is worn or not. There's a Fate spell for exempting subjects from an AoE spell, but that would increase the dot rating and might be detrimental for other spells you might want to use this way. For example, a ring of invisibility would always be visible even when you wear it.
                                An imbued item is much closer if you set the trigger to wearing the ring, but then you still need to cast the imbued item's spell and you can just as well cast it on anyone within range. The spell also wouldn't end when you take off the ring.
                                Since an Artefact is way too rad for a single passive buff spell, I think the best way to handle it is either through a custom Fate and/or Prime spell, or using a homebrew Yantra type that only activates during certain conditions and on certain subjects (very much like the Basilisk Yantra in Signs of Sorcery).

                                Imbued items are re-explained and expanded upon in Signs of Sorcery.
