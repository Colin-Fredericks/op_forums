                          Originally posted by Geckopirateship View Post
                          Just wondering, what is the maximum scope of what you can change with Wonderful Machine?
                          Potency properties from other things can be melded onto a single thing.

                          Can you turn a pile of junked cars into a supersonic jet?
                          No, junked cars do not have the properties of a supersonic jet. You cannot create new properties, just copy existing ones to something else.

                          What can you do to make the transformation permanent, if it isn't already, or expand the scope to be able to do that?
                          Add Advanced Duration (one reach or one mana with the Matter 2 Attainment) and Indefinite duration (an additional one reach and one mana).

                          And could you compensate for lack of sufficient parts using other arcana?
                          No, that requires Imbued Item or casting the spell directly. If it's cast directly you probably don't want to relinquish control.

                                                      I'd say it does make a difference really. If a car driving down the road is merged with a traffic light (say giving the light the quality of locomotion), what happens to the car? Does it teleport up to the lightbox? Does it just low down naturally? What about the passenger?

                                                      Does this destroy the original objects? It means more resources must be spent to purchase the original items again. Time could be spent to fix the original objects if it's a physical change. If it's a supernal quality swap, sleepers have no idea what's going on as the objects look to be functioning normally but that danged engine just isn't running. One casting is subtle while the other is grossly magical.

                                                      One example has the possibility of teleporting parts or machines flying through the air (when cast at sensory in particular) while the other leaves them wholly intact and appearing normal.

                                                      I'd go with the idea that the qualities are what exchange spots, interweaving the supernal patterns of the items in a way that exchanges or reflects the other qualities. It becomes a metaphysical change instead of a physical one.
