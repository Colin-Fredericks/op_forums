    Something that I thought up today

    Crystalize memory (Mind 4 + Prime 4)
    Practice: Patterning
    Primary Factor: Duration
    Suggested Rote Skill: Crafts, Science, Empathy
    Cost: 1 mana (minimum)

    The mage congels a memory into pure tass removing it from his mind. Properties of the tass are determined the same as for the creating it with Prime alone. As the tass doesn't have the same safeguards and complexities as a mind of a living being anyone can read the memory using a Mind Unveiling spell but the attempt is withstood by the Durability of the tass. If a mage absorbs the tass the memory enters that mage's mind.
    +1 Reach: The mage copies the memory instead of removing it from his mind.

    Akashic Repository (Mind 3)
    Practice: Weaving
    Primary Factor: Duration
    Suggested Rote Skills: Socialize, Academics, Science

    Though the Akashic Records are unaccesible as far as any willworker knows, a Mage can turn himself into a smaller repository of knowledge. The spells allows all of it's subjects share a number of the mage's of a number of skills. Choose a number of skills equal to the spells potency. Whenever one subjects needs to make a skill with one of the choses skills roll he or she can use the mage's dot rating. This doesn't effect rolls to activate supernatural powers such as rotes or the abillities of other beings.
    +1 Reach: The subjects share the use of skill with each other not only with the mage
    +2 Reach: The subjects share all skills, not just the chosen ones
    +1 Reach: The mage can also choose a single mental merit to share, this reach effect can be taken a number of times, each time choosing a different merit.

                Create Tass (Prime 3)
                Practice: Weaving
                Primary Factor: Potency
                Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Craft, Occult

                With this spell, a mage can channel mana (normally a cabal-mate's) into a tangible form, such as water in a sports bottle, creating tass. This tass is subject to the normal rules concerning its use. Each point of Potency creates approximately 120ml of drinkable liquid tass, 100 grams of edible solid tass, or 80 grams of non-edible tass. Edible or drinkable tass are the most common forms. By way of reference, 120ml is effectively a third of a typical soft drink can, and 100 grams is the mass of a large sausage roll or a small orange.

                +2 Reach: the effect is Lasting.

                            Cool Water (Matter 2)
                            Practice: Ruling
                            Primary Factor: Potency
                            Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Science, Survival

                            Water is everywhere, often in places one would not expect. This spell draws water from the very air, condensing vapour into a usable liquid. Each point of Potency yields enough water for one person for one day, assuming it's kept in a suitable container. This effect is Lasting.

                            +1 Reach: The mage can draw water from the extremely dry air of cold deserts, such as glaciers and the polar regions. Contrary to popular belief, hot desert air typically holds a very great deal of water.
