            Originally posted by HelloCthulhu View Post
            The thing that I wanted to do, however, is change it from the insta-omen of Serendipity to a longer lasting effect that represented getting in tune with the Lex.
            Otherwise, I sorta feel like he'd have to go around activating the Legacy all the time, and it'd feel a bit more contrived.
            Well you are attuned to the Lex, as it is now you find information about or relating to in some way information about the Lex everywhere you look. Think about the activation of the ability as one of those "Aha!" moments of insight people sometimes get when reflecting on religious texts. Except that a member of this legacy has those almost all the time and at will from constantly thinking about the Lex. Now, what the attainment doesn't do right now is prevent you from using it to gain an omen about any course of action you want to take, it's not restricted to the Lex. Maybe it was designed that way to make life a little easier for members of the legacy. On the other hand if you add the restriction to the Lex you can give it some more oomph, but I don't have any good ideas for that.

            Primary attainments should all be from the same arcana, Optional Attainments should all be from the same arcana. Fortunately, Prime is your Optional Arcana so Words of Truth can be an attainment, just not the Primary one. As I noted before Words of Truth is a spell you cast on someone else so that they can hear and understand you and be convinced of the truth you're speaking. To that end if you make it Optional attainment #2 and spend the Reach on Instant Activation then it will work on targets you're touching (Up to 2) or on targets in an area centered on the Mage (Up to a Large room at Prime 5). The effect will last at best 3 turns (Usually enough time to testify , but if not they can just keep reactivating). Also because the spell is Withstood it won't be useful in an offensive manner (Forcing people to believe you) until you hit Prime 3 and above. It will never work on some people or ephemeral entities. Fortunately, most of the people you'd use it on probably want to hear what you have to say anyway. If you restricted it to allowing you to only talk about the Lex or on Lex related matters you could get another Reach to spend, perhaps on Advanced Duration.

                        I like it. It makes sense.

                        I would keep in mind that Attainments can kind of stretch the boundaries a bit of the normal rules. I know you want to avoid something being overpowered, but I think that something like that is a problem on paper rather than in actual play. If the Attainment works for your theme and sticks to your theme, then it's hard for it to be overpowered. In many cases you'll find us saying that something sound very underpowered once you get to your third or fourth attainment because that instinct to downplay things is still kicking in when you're writing.

                            Thanks to all your great suggestions, I have come up with five Attainments. I'm still missing the optional for the fourth one, so I'd love suggestions for that.
                            I'd also like to hear what you think about these.

                            Attainments

                            First: Know Transgression
                            Prerequisites: Inititation
                            Themis knows when the natural Law is broken. When the Themistopólos of the next violation of the Lex Magica in the area ruled by his Consilium and the path he will need to take to get closer to uncovering it.
                            This imitates the Serendipity rote (p. 135) in that it leads the Judge closer to his goal, but has the added restriction that it only applies to violations of the Lex Magica.
                            Optional: Prime 1
                            A Judge who possesses at least one dot of Prime, will automatically see through any attempts to hide transgressions against the Lex Magica or the rulings of the Consilium, as per the rote Pierce Deception (p 165). If hidden through magic, this provokes a Clash of Wills.


                            Second: Keep the Word
                            Prerequisites: Fate 2, the Judge must increase either Empathy, Investigation or Politics to 2 dots
                            A Judge keeps his word and stands by it. This is known by his peers. However, the Judge knows all too well that this trust will not always be rewarded.
                            The Judge learns to keep an eye on those who swear oaths to him.
                            Whenever the subject of this spell breaks the Lex Magica or an agreement specifically made between her and the Judge pertaining to this, for example an agreed upon castigation or an oath to uphold it, the Judge receives instant notice of this, briefly has a vision of the transgression and can track the violator until a day has passed. This emulates Oaths Fulfilled, p135. (Reach spent: +1 Advanced duration, +1 vision, +1 tracking, bonus reach gained from limitations)
                            Optional: Prime 2
                            The Judge is known to speak the truth. Those judges who are gifted in the Arcana of Prime can back this up with their magic. If a Judge possesses 2 dots of Prime, those listening to him can hear him and understand him clearly, regardless of distance, noise, or language barriers. Moreover, all subjects know, on a soul-deep level, that what the mage says is true. This only holds up, if the Judge speaks words he knows to be true. (This emulates Words of Truth p.168)

                            Third: Swear the Oath
                            Prerequisites: Fate 3, Academics 3, the Judge must also increase the skill used to gain the previous attainment to 3 dots, or increase one of the other possible skills to 2 dots.
                            An oath sworn to a Themistopólos is not a just a collection of words. It carries in it the Supernal order of things and must be in concordance with the Lex Magica.
                            This attainment emulates Sworn Oaths, p.137, but may only be used to craft an oath to uphold or enforce the Lex Magica. This attainment has Advanced Duration.
                            Optional Prime 3
                            A Themistopólos who possesses at least 3 dots of Prime, may grant the subject an additional protection from magical attempts to stop her from fulfilling her vow.
                            The magic inherent in the vow surrounds the subject and makes her able to Withstand hostile magic with the Potency of the Attainment.

                            Fourth: Grant Forgiveness
                            Prerequisites: Fate 4, Academics 4.
                            The Law defines what is right and what is wrong. It describes how to punish those that go against it. But it also tells its subjects what to do with those who have served their time.
                            The role of the Judge is to investigate, to know and to understand, but it also is to forgive. The Judge may decide the subject has served his time, or that the punishments levied against a subject by others has been unjust and so, may relieve their duress.
                            The Judge consults the Lex Magica and the rulings made and describes how the subject may overcome their afflictions. The subject’s task depends on the crime or the power of the spell in question. This Attainment functions like Atonement, p138.
                            Optional: Prime

                            Fifth: Pass Judgment
                            Prerequisites: Fate 5, Academics 5, the Judge must also increase the skill used to gain the second attainment to 4 dots, or increase one of the other possible skills to 3 dots.
                            There is no law if there is no punishment. If upholding the law is the same as breaking it, the law has no consequence but is purely philosophy.
                            The Themistopólos makes sure it is not.
                            The hardest part of a Judge’s work, is understanding a person’s crimes, holding them against the Law, and declaring them guilty. Nonetheless it is also the most important part.
                            The Judge can levy the power of Pariah, p140, against a breaker of the Lex Magica. The subject must be proven guilty under the Law, and may be given the chance to defend themselves. Refusal to do so, means a forfeiture of the right.
                            Optional: Prime 4
                            The punishment of these supreme Judges is terrible indeed. The world should know of the transgressor’s crimes, and the Judge skilled in Prime, makes sure they do.
                            When the Judge passes the sentence, they imprint upon the subject a mark that describes them as a breaker of laws and an enemy of the Truth. This is visible to even Peripheral Mage Vision and is instantly understood by all Awakened. Their reaction is not guaranteed, however.
