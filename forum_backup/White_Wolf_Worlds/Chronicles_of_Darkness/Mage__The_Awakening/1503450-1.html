        Yeah basically the Sahajiya was one of the 9 traditions. It was originally known as the cult of ecstacy, but I prefer the new name.

        Despite being a single tradition, the Sahajiya consists of a number of internal factions or sub traditions. Identity tends to be stronger to the sub factions than the traditions, which act like alliances or coalitions of sorts.

        I have highlighted so far the historical factions within the Sahajiya. There are also a similar number each of progressive factions, dissonant factions, political factions, and yet more that are mentioned in places other than the core cult of ecstacy books.

        Each of the traditions is split into multiple internal factions in a similar way.

        Because of the number of factions/legacies involved, as well as the thematic differences, I think I will probably deal with different traditions in different threads if I ever get that far. Given that I have never created a single legacy and I am about to attempt a dozen of them just for the Sahajiya that might be a big if and/or take a while.

            So I have made a start on the Erzuli Jingo legacy. I am not sure how to make spirit work in the way I want it to, so I have settled on Mind primary with a focus on using Goetia as astral guides.

            Here are my initial thoughts, but it may change as I get a better understanding of both the arcana and the astral. I am open to suggestions: In particular it would be useful to have a way to enter the astral without needing specific locations - would this be possible with mind magic?

            EDIT: changes to yantra and oblations following advice from Temple Builder. Added attainments.

            Note: I am not entirely satisfied with the attainments, especially the mind attainments. As such I will probably revisit these one I have more experience with the system and practiced creating spells.
            Erzuli Jingo



            Origins

            Parenting: Thyrsus, silver ladder?

            Background: The erzuli jingo are a historical legacy originating in the voudun cults of Western Africa and spreading to the western world along with the populations of slaves.
            They are still strong in Africa and have cults in the Americas and Haiti where similar beleifs have spread.

            Appearances:
            Preistesses called Mambo make up the majority of the legacy, although the occasional ismale houngan is not unknown. For the most part they tend to wear traditional African dress, and many carry herbs and fetishes.

            ​​​​​​

            Doctrine

            Prerequisites: I am unsure. Probably spirit (2?) Along with dots in occult and medicine. Maybe intimidation, expression, and survival?

            Initiation: the initiate is taken on an astral journey through her oneiros and into the astral. she is introduced to the astral reflections of the loa and welcomes them into her oneiros.

            Organisation: mambo good regular meetings with voudun cults where they practice their rites and enter trances. The majority of these cults are made up of sleepers however. Meetings between awakened mambo take place on a regular basis to practice rites, assist each other in astral journeys, and train attainments.

            Theory: all creation is divine and therefore all creation contains the power of the divine. Spirits and Loa can be awakened in everything, and can then be reasoned with. By entering ecstatic trances near shrines dedicated to the Erzuli, the loa can be persuaded to enter worshippers minds and lead them deeper within themselves.
            By entering dreams through Ecstatic trances leading into the oneiros mambo can heal the minds of themselves and others, or punish transgressors for harm they have caused. The erzuli jingo call spirits to assist them on their astral journeys, often trading time in possession of a mortal body in return.

            Magic

            Ruling Arcana: Mind optional: life

            Yantras:
            Bags of herbs (+1)
            Fetishes (+2) - the fetish must contain a spirit with influence related to the spell
            Shrine dedicated to relevant loa (+2)
            Ecstatic dance (+2) - because of the nature of the trace and the time needed to work up to it, this can only be used for ritual casting.


            Oblations:
            Entering the oneiros through Ecstatic trances, leading others into ecstatic states, nurturing relationships between others

            First attainment : Riding the Loa
            Prerequisites: Mind
            Primary factor: duration
            Reach: advanced duration


            The biggest problem with astral travel is that it leaves your body comatose. With enough time to prepare a mambo can attach part of her conscious to the loa, thereby split her consciousness between her body and her astral journey. This is only possible while not taking any strenuous physical actions in either world, meaning that the mage is forced to pause her actions in the astral in order to check in on her physical self, but it does mean her body is not left senseless.
            By preparing for a scene, the mambo can split her conscious thoughts into two tracks - one in the material world and one in the astral. She may perform extended tasks mental and social tasks at the same time in both the physical and astral worlds. Anything more strenuous such as performing a physical task, performing an instant task of any kind, or casting a spell causes her attention to be drawn away from the other world.
            This replicates the spell One mind, two thoughts.
            (I think it would work like this. It would be better with extra reach in order to expand what she can do, but that would bump it up the attainment list. at least it enables her to keep a check on her body).

            Optional: Life 1
            Primary: Potency
            Reach: Instant casting

            Initiate Mambo are taught how to cleanse the body of toxins, allowing the subject a bonus equal to potency to resist the toxins effects for the duration of the spell.

            Second attainment: Erzuli's Protection

            Prerequisites: Mind 2
            Primary factor: duration
            Reach: advanced duration

            Inviting the Loa to take possession of their minds allows Mambo to protect them from the attentions of others. By spending a scene in preparation, the Mambo gains a mental sheild that provokes a clash of wills against any goetia numina, influences, manifestations, mind spells, and supernatural abilities affecting the mind that target her.

            Optional: Life 2
            Primary: Potency
            Reach: Instant casting

            By channelling the power of the Loa, a mambo can purge her subjects body of diseases. Reduce the illness rating by the attainments potency. If this reduces it to 0 it eliminates the illness from the subject entirely.


            Third attainment: Erzuli's Guidance

            Prerequisites: Mind 3
            Primary factor: duration
            Reach: advanced duration

            After a scene of preparation, the mambo can harmonise her mind with the Erzuli Loa allowing them to guide her thoughts and granting her a bonus equal to potency in either a mental or social attribute for the attainments duration.

            Optional: Life 3
            Primary: Potency
            Reach: Instant casting

            Channeling the Loa, the Mambo heals 2 boxes of bashing injury per dot of potency.


            Fourth attainment: Bridge of the Loa

            Prerequisites: Mind 4
            Primary factor: duration
            Reach: advanced duration

            With the aid of the Erzuli Loa, the mambo creates a dream bridge connecting her oneiros with that of her subject. This bridge functions as described in astral realms.

            Optional: Life 4
            Primary: Potency
            Reach: Instant casting

            The mambo is now capable of healing even lethal wounds, healing 2 lethal levels of injury for each dot of potency.



            Fifth attainment: Familiar Loa

            Prerequisites: Mind 5
            Primary factor: duration
            Reach: advanced duration, instant casting.

            The Mambo creates a familiar bonded between a mage and a Goetia, who must both be subjects of the spell. The Goetia must not be greater than rank 2. The mage gains the Familiar merit, and the Goetia gains the Familiar manifestation condition for the duration of the attainment.
            Both parties must be willing and can end the bond if they wish.

                Now this is all my opinions, but I have made a few legacies before so maybe this will help.

                So, Legacies are made of Background, Oblations/Yantras, and Attainments. I’d say you already have a solid background, so I’m going focus on the other two for the most part.

                I’d skip order parentage for now, until you have a solid grasp on their policies, and stuff. I’d say they don’t perfectly sound like Silver Ladder, because while they seem set up to act as spiritual leaders, they aren’t really following the Ladder’s policy on the relationship between Spirits and Humans, namely that humans should be in charge of the spirits. They might fit better in the Free Council or maybe even the Guardians

                One could use the Dream merit as a perquisite, along with mystery cult influence or something representative of being a spiritual leader.

                One could also use Spirit as the Optional, since Mind and Spirit actually work well together.

                As for Yantras:
                the herbs are fine as a +1
                Fetish use is neat.
                Shrine should be a +2, as locations are a universal yantra, and this is like specialized version of that.
                Dance is fine.
                Not sure where the cursing someone bit comes from, or why they would have particular influence there.
                Likewise, I’m not sure where the fire bit comes from.

                I think you have too many Oblations, I think 3 is the rule of thumb. I like the entering the Oneiros one, since it actually can negate the cost of entry which is nice, and woks with their identity. Leading others in trance is good too, since it backs up their role of being a spiritual leader. The other three are kinda weak, in my opinion. Oblations should be an effort, and reinforce the legacy’s mystical theme. They don’t seem to do so very well from my perspective.

                Edit: I’d put “nurturing relationships between others” as an Oblations, if I’m understanding your previous commentary about them correctly.

                                In order to gain access to the Astral without a Hallow, you would need a Making of Mind to open a mental gateway. In more practical terms, you would need the Astral Adept merit, which can be granted by a Ruling of Mind, but only to mages or Sleepwalkers. Another option is to use a Patterning of Mind and Spirit to grant the Gateway Manifestation to a spirit or goetia, which comes with its own logistical challenges.

                                Regarding transporting goetia, normally you need to do a Goetic Summon from the destination point, which may require Reaching or ritual casting, plus a Hallow. Alternatively, they could Co-Locate the goetia using sympathetic range and Mind 3 to teleport then to or from the Astral. So basically the fortes of Mastigos, which I think would be a solid pick for parent Path.

                                The restriction on Attainment Reach is the price paid for their consistency. Being able to ignore Dissonance, Disbelief, dispellation and not even have to roll dice means that a helpful Attainment can be a major addition to a character's toolkit. There is a homebrew rule that is often adopted and might be helpful, which is to add a meaningful restriction or complication to justify one extra Reach.

                                Regarding Attainments, my suggestion is to distill the core idea of your Legacy into one phrase, with no commas or references. An example for the Tamers of Fire's beliefs being explained as "The fires of the world and the flames of the spirit are one and the same", the Transhuman Engineers embodying the idea of "Building the Singularity draws the Supernal and Fallen closer together" and the Universal Harmony's philosophy being “Music is an agreeable harmony for the honor of God".

                                Once you have that core phrase, filter your ideas through it, any Attainments that reinforce it are kept, any that do not synergize need to be reconsidered. That will help build a toolkit that builds on the themes.​

                                                Fellowship of Pan

                                                Origins

                                                Parenting: Thyrsus,

                                                Background:
                                                The legacy was founded in ancient Greece by cults dedicated to Dionysus and quickly spread throughout the western world. Although often derided, and sometimes even persecuted they have continued to thrive wherever people have gathered for hedonistic celebrations.

                                                Appearances:
                                                The fellowship vary wildly with the only real commonality being that they are almost always in the grip of mind altering drugs of some sort.

                                                Doctrine


                                                Prerequisites: Socialise 1,

                                                Initiation:
                                                Initiation in the cult is pretty informal, mostly involving attending a celebratory revel to mark the occasion. The specifics vary depending on the makeup of the local cult, but "hazing" rituals reminiscent of college football initiations are not uncommon.

                                                Organisation:
                                                The fellowship are remarkably loose in organisation, with no form of hierarchy or strict rules amongst them. Nevertheless, where more than one gather for any length of time they tend to meet regularly for wild ecstatic rites. These are not generally closed affairs, and it is not uncommon for others to be invited along.

                                                Theory:
                                                Inheritors of the legacy of the Cults of Dionysus, these Ecstatics believe that a human must embrace their primal nature in order to find Ecstasy.
                                                They believe mankind is at its purest when in the grip of its unconsciousness, such as when drunk or making love or art, and tries to capture and harmonize with this unconscious beat.

                                                Magic

                                                Ruling Arcana: Fate

                                                Yantras:
                                                Having an alcoholic drink or taking a drug (+1)
                                                Being sufficiently inebriated to have the drugged condition (+2),

                                                Oblations:
                                                Engaging in revelry, partying, or celebrating with others. Engaging in purely hedonistic activities.


                                                First attainment: Hedonistic Inspiration
                                                Prerequisites: Fate 1, Initiation, socialise 1
                                                Primary factor: Potency
                                                Reach: Instant

                                                By losing herself in hedonistic persuits and focusing on the ecstacy of bliss, a fellow of Pan may gain inspiration on the best course of action to take to accomplish a stated objective. On activation she receives a clear omen suggesting a course of action that will lead her closer to her goal. This seldom provides immediate success but can lead to an important breakthrough and is often found at the bottom of an empty glass.

                                                Optional: Mind 1
                                                Primary: Potency
                                                Reach: discover aspiration and obsessions

                                                By spending a scene interactingwith their subject the fellow of Pan may come to understand them, discovering their virtue and vice as well and how many dots in mental and social attributes they have, as well as their aspirations and obsessions.


                                                Second attainment: Luck of the lost
                                                Prerequisites: Fate 2, socialise 2
                                                Primary factor: Potency
                                                Reach: advanced duration, hour limit

                                                A fellow of Pan never lacks what they need for continued revelry and hedonism. By spending a scene engaged in hedonistic persuits, whatever they are looking for will cross their path within an hour.
                                                As long as whatever they are looking for could potentially appear I it will.
                                                The attainment can find someone with a specific trait, occupation, or context specific quality, but it always finds the closest or most available subject.
                                                Alternatively she temporarily gains access to a merit ( ally, contacts, resources, mentor, or retainer) with dots no greater than potency as she stumbles across dropped cash, unattended items, useful strangers etc.
                                                The merit may only be used a number of times equal to potency.

                                                Optional: Mind 2
                                                Primary: Potency
                                                Reach: advanced duration

                                                By spending a scene becoming more immersed in the unconscious ecstacy that builds through the fellows activities she gains the ability to alter her basic mental flow, changing her subconscious thoughts and surface emanations to reflect any emotion she wishes. This sheild her from supernatural abilities that attempt to read her thoughts or Peirce her veil of lies and misdirections.
                                                Potency is added to relevant subterfuge rolls and supernatural powers that attempt to read her surface thoughts or emotions provoke a clash of wills.


                                                Third attainment: Bumbling Competence
                                                Prerequisites: Fate 3, socialise 3
                                                Primary factor: Potency
                                                Reach: change to potency, advanced duration, instant

                                                Fellows of Pan spend a lot of time either drunk or under the influence of drugs and despite the fact that they crash around with as little coordination or foresight as you would expect from someone who is out of their mind they retain a surprising degree of competence where it matters: stumbling out of the way of attacks, accidentally pressing the right buttons, hitting on just the right topic of conversation etc.
                                                This does not increase their competence above what it would normally be, but after an instant action to focus it does allow the attainments potency to cancel any penalties caused by the drugged condition on a number of actions equal to potency.

                                                Optional: Mind 3
                                                Primary: Duration
                                                Reach: Instant

                                                Inebriation and ecstacy break down barriers to language, just as surely as they break down barriers to action and by spending an instant action focusing a fellow can speak, read, and understand any language they can perceive.



                                                Fourth attainment: Go with the Flow

                                                Prerequisites: Fate 4, socialise 4
                                                Primary factor: Duration
                                                Reach: Instant, advanced duration, add potency to mundane actions

                                                Fellows of Pan often act without planning or real preparation. So long as they have little detailed knowledge about a situation before they enter it they are able to act with perfect grace and timing, acting without conscious thoughts and allowing their actions to align perfectly with the flow of events. A turn or two studying the situation is fine, but detailed breifings or reconnaissance reduce the randomness that the attainment relies on. Luckily they rarely pay attention to such breifings anyway. They do not suffer untrained penalties for the duration of the attainment and may gain a bonus equal to potency on a number of rolls equal to potency.
                                                If the situation is a social one, their first impression also improves by 1.

                                                Optional: Mind 4
                                                Primary: Duration
                                                Reach: Instant, advanced scale, change to duration
                                                Withstand: Composure

                                                As they become one with the flow of events around them, ecstatic states of mind reached by Fellows of Pan become infectious allowing them to project emotional states at her subjects using the rules for advanced scale. She chooses to project a positive or negative emotion at the subject, opening or closing one door for the purposes of social manoeuvring. This does not have to benefit the fellow themselves, but can benefit anyone dealing with the subject during the duration of the spell. The fellow may also choose doors previously opened, making it harder for others to acheived their goals.


                                                Fifth attainment: Dionysus Fulcrum

                                                Prerequisites: Fate 5, socialise 5
                                                Primary factor: Potency
                                                Reach: instant, affect spellcasting, advanced duration.
                                                Withstand: composure

                                                The fellow gains some control over the serendipitous events surrounding her, allowing her to bless our curse a subject with exceptional luck.
                                                This grants them either a boon or a hex which can affect spellcasting rolls.

                                                Optional: Mind 5
                                                Primary: Potency
                                                Reach: Instant, advanced duration

                                                The infectious nature of the fellows mental state expands allowing her to create false sensory input in her subject, tricking her senses and creating a hallucination that seems completely real to her subject. It affects sight, sound, taste, and smell but is unable to affect tactile senses.​

                                                        Kiss of Astarte



                                                        Origins

                                                        Parenting: Thyrsus,

                                                        Background:
                                                        They have been known by many names over the ages. Worshipped under the guise of many goddesses. Astarte, Ishtar, Innana, Aphrodite. Since the first civilisations there have been ecstatic cults revering a goddess of love and war (or more accurately, sex and violence). These cults saw the first sacred prostitutes drawing power from ritual sex, and the first preistesses empowered to wage war. Often challenging the grip of the underworld and death itself.
                                                        Today they are most commonly named after Astarte, in whose name they fight and fuck in equal measure, rising high on a tide of ecstatic bliss.

                                                        [Note that rather than the modern interpretation of Astarte as a feminist icon of female empowerment, I have gone with the more ancient (and probably less palatable) interpretations derived from Ishtar/Inanna. I also dropped the lesbian angle because I could find no historical sorry for it]

                                                        Appearances:
                                                        Most Astartites are female, although some of those are trans and there are a smattering of men most of whom are gay or bisexual. The cult is common amongst sex workers, and those who awaken reflect this. That said they are a widely varied group, with the only real commonality being that they are always seductive, and they are always dangerous.
                                                        Doves are a common motif, sacred to many of the goddesses with whom they are associated.


                                                        Doctrine

                                                        Prerequisites: manipulation (seduction); brawl or weaponry

                                                        Initiation: Initiation into the cult invariably involves participation in ritual sex, and is rumoured to involve a martial test of some kind.

                                                        Organisation:
                                                        The legacy is organised thorough an ancient and hierarchical cult.

                                                        Theory:
                                                        The astartites prefer to approach ecstacy through the intimacy of shared communion. They build their partners to an extasy of shared sexual climax, using them as tools through which they can see past the lie and reach for the supernal. A similar moment of ecstatic truth can be found at the moment of death, and while it loses the power and immediacy of intimate communion, war can replicate this symbolically on a huge scale.


                                                        Magic

                                                        Ruling Arcana: Life

                                                        Yantras:
                                                        Intimate contact (+1)
                                                        Sex (+2) can only be used for ritual casting
                                                        Sacrificing a dove (+1)
                                                        Killing an enemy (+2)


                                                        Oblations:
                                                        Having sex, preparing a war or violent confrontation, entering and returning from the underworld
                                                        First attainment: Sensual awareness
                                                        Prerequisites: Life 1, Initiation,
                                                        Primary factor: Duration
                                                        Reach: advanced duration
                                                        By spending a scene (probably off stage) engaging in intimate activities that center her awareness on her senses, the astartite can heighten her senses becoming intimately aware of the world around her
                                                        and gaining a bonus to perception equal to potency.


                                                        Optional: Death 1 - spiritual awareness
                                                        Primary: duration
                                                        Reach: instant

                                                        Astartites are able to see and speak with ghosts, enabling them to interrogate the fallen, and even enlist their side at times.


                                                        Second attainment: Embodiment of Astarte
                                                        Prerequisites: Life 2,
                                                        Primary factor: Duration
                                                        Reach: advanced duration

                                                        By spending a scene enacting erotic rituals, the astartite embodies the supernal truth of the warrior goddess, gaining complete control of her bodily functions. Each level of potency gives a rank in the breathing, metabolism, reflexes, and scent, and healing aspects as per the spell "body control".

                                                        Optional: Death 2 - Astartes Sheild
                                                        Primary: duration
                                                        Reach: instant

                                                        Astarte and her predecessors are rumoured to have journeyed into the underworld itself, proving that death had no hooks over then t. Astrtites are well prepared for similar journeys, gaining protection from ghostly numina, influence, manifestations, death spells, and death related powers of other supernatural creatures. Powers attempting to Peirce the sheild provoke a clash of wills.


                                                        Third attainment: Form of Astarte
                                                        Prerequisites: Life 3,
                                                        Primary factor: Duration
                                                        Reach: Instant

                                                        In both love and war, appearances can be paramount. The Astartite learns to mold her form to whatever is needed, becoming more beautiful or more terrifying in an instant.
                                                        Within the confines of species and age, she may transform however she wishes altering any physical aspects of her body.
                                                        She may also redistribute her physical or social dots as she sees fit so long as none go below 0 or above her maximum.

                                                        Optional: Death 3 - Shadow Armoury
                                                        Primary: duration
                                                        Reach: instant

                                                        An astartite is never entirely unarmed. She can mold and shape Shadows into physical items with a durability of 2. Weapons have and armour have a rating of 2, otherwise equipment gains a rating of 2.



                                                        Fourth attainment: Curse of Astarte
                                                        Prerequisites: Life 4
                                                        Primary factor: Duration
                                                        Reach: advanced duration, instant

                                                        The Astartite may withdraw the goddess favour from her target, reducing their abilities to wage war and make love.
                                                        One of the targets physical or social attributes is reduced by one dot for each level of potency.

                                                        Optional: Death 4 - Sexual Vampire
                                                        Primary: duration
                                                        Reach: instant, may affect healthy individuals
                                                        Withstand: resolve

                                                        The Astartite may pull energy from the subjects ecstatic experiences into herself. If a subject is experiencing excruciating pain, death throes, or orgasmic pleasure, the mage may harvest willpower or scourge the subjects pattern for mana.
                                                        For each level of potency the mage may take 1 point of willpower or scour the subjects pattern for 1 point of mana, causing a lethal wound in the process.
                                                        This counts towards the number of times per day the mage can gain mana through scouring.

                                                        Fifth attainment: Goddess of War
                                                        Prerequisites: Life 5
                                                        Primary factor: Duration
                                                        Reach: instant, sensory range, advanced duration, affect supernatural creatures.
                                                        Withstand: composure

                                                        The seductive influence of the Astartite overrides even the strongest wills and with a simple gesture she can render opponents slaves to their impulses.

                                                        This operates as the spell "control instincts"

                                                        Optional: Death 5 - Open the gates
                                                        Primary: duration
                                                        Reach: instant

                                                        Just as the goddess returned from the underworld, so too can the Astartite fling open the gates of death, creating an iris between the material world and the upper layer of the underworld within the area of effect. Opening the gate causes the area to gain a death resonance and the gateway condition for the duration of the effect.
