    THe notion that a mage should be able to go toe to toe with a werewolf is nonsense perpetuated by the idea that a Mage, because of the flexibility of their capabilities, can do anything that the other splats can. Technically, that's true, but that doesn't encapsulate what a mage does best, no matter what the supremacists might suggest.

    A Mage trying to fight on a werewolf's terms is losing that fight. A Mage does better with long-planned, high-scale escalation in terms of response. A mage who tries to match his peers on the in-the-fly moments might barely catch up, which makes it all the worse when a werewolf breaks out their gifts and ruin whatever the fuck the mage thought was good and possible in the moment. A mage in those cases is better off stalling until they can run or until a fellow pulls a fucking rabbit out of the hat;

        I'm not a Werewolf fan, so I don't know what are their capabilities, but Obrimos is probably the safest bet for aspiring martial artist - both Werewolf punching and generally punching. Their Arcana spread makes sustaining a lot of buffs easily, as Dispel Magic allows them to safely unsafely release spells, as long as they Dispel them before they go sour. This takes at least a day, so Obrimos Kung Fu Puncher can pretty much keep them indefinietely ,and incorporate dispelling and recasting them into their morning martial arts routine, which is a boon in terms of flavor and reinforcing your magical identity. This pretty much makes problem of having to manage spell control irrelevant, so Obrimos who dedicates themselves to be always ready for physical conflict - which sounds like something an Arrow would do - is limited less by their resources, and more by their imagination and Arcana. By bypassing the spell control limitation, they can preemptively cover any angle of attack, as long as they can both think of it and get proper dot ratings to do so. And luckily, Forces is really good about optimizing physical performance while also turning yourself pretty much invincible to being damaged by impact, energy and other typical forms of damage. So if you want to punch werewolves, get Prime 1, some Forces, and 2 or 1 dots in stuff that can protect you from non-physical means of werewolf attack.

                                        It's kind of a weird question, especially if you open it up to other things. But it's the sort of thing that comes up a lot. Yes, mages can fight in melee, some of their artifact weapons are incredibly powerful too and they have spells that can enhance their abilities. They can also learn Fighting Styles and Brawl and Weaponry with the rest of people.

                                        I had an interesting combat scene when a Beast attacked a group of three mages. She really hurt them pretty badly, and they had been burdened down with Conditions and Tilts that basically gave them no chance to really do anything. They had been prepared but not incredibly, only having their armor and a couple other non-related spells on. One was knocked out of the fight instantly, the Beast's Looming Presence inflicted the Beaten Down Condition, so she ended up just using boons on the other two. The Beast had created a snowstorm and deafened them but with the boon and another spell to add Firearms skill they managed to take the Beast down. She was a Merger, a Rampant Beast that didn't have Lair. If she did she probably would have killed someone. Not sure if you have the book but her stats were based on Starless, p241, though some were changed a bit.

                                        If she played it more smart, as well, but she wasn't aware they were coming and was being driven mad by an Aggressive Meme. If the mages were alone, they would have died, at least if they kept fighting. At least two of them would have been able to just get away, which would have been more likely than standing out there and fighting to the death with a giant bird monster.

                                        Just the way the game is made, once you start going cross-splat, it's really hard to say anyone is even against anyone else, the powers are so different, even the innate abilities are game changers, which is more to the point of the game.

                                                            Depends on what you mean by 'on their own terms'. A Mage would be hard pressed to become a more vicious, rapid healing murder machine than a Werewolf in Gauru (though, if I had to try it'd probably involve Life, Forces, and Time with lots of hung healing spells). However, if you merely mean to beat the wolf in a mostly straight up hand-to-hand fight. Yes, a Mage can absolutely do this.

                                                            Mind you, the Mage will need to be a little purpose built, but there are plenty of spell and style combinations that can make a Mage terrific in close combat. Just a pair from a recent thread (the supernatural fighting style thread):
                                                            • A mage with Acceleration and the third dot of Armed Defense can hike their defense to preposterous levels (like 50+ dice) which can then be converted into damage against anyone who attacks them. Get a scene long casting of Present as Past up to negate any form of surprise, and put Sands of Time on Rote to reset if things go south. Then get some spirit to deal with the Shadow side of Werewolf and you've got a strong contender.
                                                            • Mages can achieve absolutely ridiculous ratings for Armor and Weapon enhancements.Between Matter 3 and Prime 3 a mage can perfect tools to an absolutely staggering degree. 10 points of Armor and +10 Weapons are not out of the question. They also find it trivially easy to overcome a Werewolf's regeneration (both Prime and Matter can achieve this). It takes a damn well spec'd Werewolf to survive close combat with numbers like that.
                                                            Now this is not to say that Mages will kick a werewolf's ass, nor that Werewolves don't have tools and means to defeat the above examples. We could go back and forth about what trumps what forever, literally. I'm just saying, yeah, Werewolves aren't uncontested at the top of the fighty pile.
