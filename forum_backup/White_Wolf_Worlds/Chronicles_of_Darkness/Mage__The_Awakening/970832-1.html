            Something like a spitting cobra or mace sprayed in the character's eyes? A flashbang grenade's effects? Those things should be pretty easily curable by Life magic, yes. A simple Life spell can help flush the toxin, but to heal the Condition would require further magic. I wrote a small section in Hurt Locker to address supernatural healing and durability with Conditions/Tilts and Extreme Environments. If you suffer Blinded from something besides an injury, curing it is fairly simple. Basically, you heal the Tilt in place of a point of lethal damage (bashing for Deafened), though for mages, the answer's already there under Knit (Life 3, p. 150). With a Reach, you can heal physical Personal Tilts, such as Arm Wrack. This doesn't extend to Conditions that temporarily act as Tilts, such as if your character is permanently suffering the Blind Condition, which acts as the Blinded Tilt in combat. But if you're blinded by pepper spray, you can "spend" a single point of lethal damage you would have otherwise healed through some means to recover from it, or simply use Knit with the proper Reach.

                Look at Knit and Regeneration.

                Creative Thaumaturgy says that removing a condition is always at least a Ruling (**) spell and otherwise follows the same rules as creating them.

                That means removing a Persistent Condition is Patterning or Unraveling, as makes sense to the Arcana and Condition present. For physical Persistent Conditions, this is probably in the purview of Regeneration, which requires Potency 2 to affect eyes and cannot be made Lasting. Why? Hell if I know. Other healing spells are Lasting. They are basically the one type of spell specifically called out as Lasting in the sidebar. Lasting rules are inconsistent and make no sense. But there you have it.

                Mind lets you cure mental Conditions with Clear Thoughts (***) instead of four dots, and with two reach it can make it Lasting. It cannot affect Conditions granted by Paradox, but it says nothing about either Wisdom or Persistent Conditions. This means it probably works on both. Psychic Reprogramming can also muck with Conditions, but there is no Lasting option. Neither option grants Beats.
