                            05-04-2016, 12:10 PM
                            Ok, so I found one of my local players actually got the Beast add-on and we have been going over the book together for almost 20 hours straight yesterday, this is what we've got...


                            Sympathy related Questions:


                            Temporal Sympathy mentions that a "sympathetic yantra" is required in addition to the subject being present (p. 186). So, if I want to see the past of a person I am touching, I don't need an additional sympathetic yantra, correct?


                            Does the corpse of a person count as Material or Representational sympathy for the purpose of seeing into the past when they where alive? I imagine death counts as a "mayor change".


                            If I am holding the brick of a house, can I use Temporal Sympathy to use Postcognition on the house or do I also need Spatial Sympathy if I am not standing on the lot where the house used to be? Can I even see into the past of a thing that is already destroyed? P. 193 seems to say no, because I must "be casting on the subject as it exists now". However P. 186 seems to say I can, because "a different building on the same lot" is an example of Weak temporal sympathy.


                            Can Temporal Sympathy be used to cast any spell back in time, or just Time spells? If it can be used to cast any spell into the past, can I toss someone in the past a fireball or similar to kill them in the present?


                            If I go back in time and prevent someone's death, will that person be normal? Or do they gain the Souless condition? I think they should be Souless because the Present is "objective" and if they died, their Soul already moved on and the book says no arcana can bring souls back. If they don't become Souless, then Time could be creatively used to retrieve souls even after they have moved on.


                            A bunch of Withstand related questions and issues:


                            In p. 115 it says that if a spell has two Withstand ratings, you just use the largest + 1 per extra withstand rating. So, if I want to use Psychic Domination on a target with Resolve 3, the Withstand is always 4 and it doesn't really matter if the connection is Weak or Strong. Is that correct? Intuitively, it feels like it should be 7 if the connection is Weak and 4 if Strong because it incentivises players to find a better connection.


                            In the Spell Place of Power (p. 182) the Withstand is "Gauntlet Strength", so if I am in a "Dense Urba Area" (original Gauntlet Strength 5) would I need Potency 6 just to lower the Gauntlet down to 4? But if I am in a Locus, those same 6 points of Potency can actually raise it to Gauntlet Strength straight to 6 (since Withstand is just 1 and I have 5 leftover Potency)?


                            Why is the Gauntlet Strength Withstand (calling it GSW now) value measured from 0 to 5 but Space and Time Withstand's are only in the 0 to 3 range? The disparity makes it so that the Reaching spell in a Dense Urban area (which has GSW 5) always requires a Potency of 6. Most games take place in Dense Urban areas, so this would be the most common GSW used. At Potency 6 a Disciple would need to take a -10 die penalty to cast (or +1 Reach and -6 Penalty to switch the Primary Factor). Because it is so high, it also means that targeting a Withstood spell at a Rank 5 spirit with a spell through the Gauntlet is exactly as hard as targeting a Rank 1 spirit through the Gauntlet while in a Dense Urban Area. And it also means attacking a high Ranking spirit is better undertaken in a Dense Urban area actually (since they could more easily strike back while I am at a Locus).


                            The Shadow Name Merit (p. 104) doesn't seem to work well with the Withstand rules. It supposedly adds a Withstand rating equal to the merit, but since it doesn't stack with your original Withstand, (per p. 115) what happens is this: When your Withstand attribute is 2 and you have Shadow Name 1, your total Withstand is 3, but when you up Shadow Name to 2, your Widthstand stays at 3! When your Withstand attribute is 3 and you have Shadow Name 2 your total Withdstand is 4 and when you raise Shadow Name to 3, it stays at 4 again. Is it supposed to work like that?


                            In P. 114 it says "Before dice are rolled for spellcasting, the caster must consider if her subject can Withstand her magic, as it could affect the dice pool and outcome of the casting." However Withstanding does not affect the dice pools directly, instead it gets subtracted from the potency of the completed spell. The quote makes it confusing, more if you take into acount that the Mage is not supposed to know the target's actual Withstand value.



                            Specific spell questions:


                            Why is Ghost Summons (P. 131) "Perfecting"? Shouldn't it be Weaving? Since Spirit Summons (P. 182) is also Perfecting, it feels like it is done on purpose. What was the logic followed to decide on the practice?


                            Can the bonus to Enhance Skill be used to increase the bonus provided by a mudra Yantra in a rote? Or is the rote limited to the unmodified skill? Would the bonus from my Nimbus Tilt stack with a similar bonus provided by this spell?


                            Can the subject of Psychic Projection return to their body somehow before the spell ends while long as the projection is alive? Is there a way for the projection to die aside from supernatural sources? If I project a Sleeper who is uncouncious at the time of casting the spell, will the Sleeper's projection cause Dissonance and/or Paradox?


                            Can I fully Unmake a Mind with a Mind 5 spell? Mind Wipe only erases portions of the mind, but what if I want to eliminate it completely (say I want to use the body for something else, like Psychic Genesis)? If it is doable, would it be a new spell or would it be a +Reach on Mind Wipe?


                            Is the intention behind Supernal Veil (p. 168) to hide you from magic/supernatural detection or does it actually hide you from everything, even mundane observation? The text doesn't make it clear.


                            In Hallow Dance (p. 170) the Withstand is "Hallow rating". Does it refer to the rating of the existing hallow or the desired rating? If it refers to the existing one, does that mean that raising a hallow from rating 1 to 5 requires just 5 Potency but lowering it from 5 to 1 require 9 Potency? (So it's easier to upgrade Hallows than to degrade them for some reason).


                            Instead of using a very Mana intensive "Create Truth" spell to create a 5 dot Hallow (which costs 5 mana per Hallow dot), would it be possible to create just a 1 dot Hallow with that spell (spending just 5 Mana) and then use Hallow Dance (which costs no Mana) to improve it to 5 dots?


                            If I use Fate 4 to goad a subject into completing a task like "kill X person" can I use Space to find a connection between the subject and the person I want them to kill? What level of sympathy would that connection be?


                            Annihilate Matter (p. 158) uses Supernatural Tolerance of the object's creator as Withstand. However after a quick scan it is literally the only spell that uses it (except those which use Rank). As per the general rules for Withstand in P. 127 the correct Withstand should be either Rank or the Arcana (or related power rating) dots of the Item creator, but never Gnosis.



                            Nimbus questions:


                            Is the intention of Immediate Nimbus flaring that it can be used to provide a repeatable buff / debuff to other Mages watching you with every single casting? So let's say I cast a simple Discern Composition spell on my clothes (no withstand) and I have all my cabalmates next to me. My cabalmates willingly submit to the Tilt regardless of their Resolve. I have Gnosis 1, and I have chosen to allocate my bonus dice to grant +1 to a Investigation, which also grants 8-again. Can I do this over and over again every time I cast even a 1-dot spell?


                            Can you choose to not apply your Nimbus Tilt? Do Nimbus Tilts from different Mages stack? Or does the newest / strongest Nimbus win and remove all previous Nimbuses and their Tilts?


                            The character sheet in the book contains no space for writing down your Nimbus Tilt. Could you add one pretty please?



                            Miscellaneous Questions:


                            In P.198 "If the Attainment would require a measurement of its successes, it automatically scores a number of successes equal to the mage’s dots in the highest prerequisite Arcanum" But no spell depends on number of successes. Otherwise, can you give an exaple of an attainment where this would matter? Even in the 11th Question write-up it mentions the automatic successes again (see The Unobvious Answer and its reference to Postcognition), but if you go back to the Postcognition spell, it doesn't use the successes for anything. There appears to be a mechanical disconnect there.


                            If I am in my Demesne, can I Reach infinity times without incurring Paradox? We can't find any rule that says we can't.


                            What is the mechanical/narrative reason for a 0 Potency Spell to stick around and use a control slot? It causes no problems for the Mage because they can get rid of it with a reflexive action. Or does is it intended that the mage doesn't know the spell was withstood and thus doesn't know they need to cancel it? Or is it possible for something else to lower the Withstand value and cause the spell to start taking effect as long as it still has a Duration?


                            When casting combined spells (p. 118) it says that spell factors and Yantras affect the entire spellcasting pool and that effects must use the same scale. Does this mean that if I am casting 2 spells combined I take -2 dice and each of the component spells gets 1 bump in the scale chart? Or does that mean that I must take -4 to bump them both unless I spend a reach to bumo them independently? Does it work the same for other spell factors such as Potency?


                            In Dedicated Tools (p. 123) it says your DT will penalize Paradox, but that you can also use it as a path or order tool. If I use it to gain both effects, does it count as using 2 Yantras? P. 119 says that if you use the same item in "many ways" each way counts as a use. However, it is not clear if both effects of the DT are part of the "same use" or individual uses of it.


                            If I burn a locket of hair with the intention of using it both as a sacrament and as a sympathetic yantra, does it use up 2 Yantra slots? If it does, does it take me 1 extra action to burn it (since it's more than one yantra?).


                            The Targeted Summoning attainment (p. 194) is weird: it allows you to do exactly the same thing you can do without it already. There is no "Supernal Summoning" as an attainment, and the rules in p. 94 already cover everything that Targeted Summoning does without even needing it for reference. Or is it the intention that Targeted Summoning provides some ability not already stated in p. 94?


                            Understanding Wisdom (p. 88) indicates that causing "long-standing injury" is an Act of Hubris. When do you roll this, when you cause the injury or when it becomes long-standing? If I heal someone after causing injury to them to ensure it will not be long standing, is it still an Act of Hubris? Or is this situation better left to Storyteller preference?


                            In p. 118 Spell Control states that a reliquinshed spell can go wrong without "maintenance". Does this imply that a Mage can do something to regularily mantain relinquished spells and prevent them from going wrong?
