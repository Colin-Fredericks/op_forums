        Muscle memory is actually imparting knowledge. Those are learned reflexes based on experiences; not just instinctive reflexes like pulling away from pain, perceiving potential dangers, and dodging. At most Life could increase Physical Skills since most of them could theoretically benefit from enhanced instinctive reflexes, but I'd personally represent enhanced reflexes with bonuses, dice tricks and/or changes to Perception, Defense (specifically using the higher of Wits and Dex) and Initiative.

        As for pheromones, I think that should be considered an equipment bonus (applicable to social maneuvering where the other party is susceptible) instead of actually Attribute increases. Attributes should be universally applicable, while pheromones would only work against a few related species at a time (which ones decided at the casting of the spell), and only at close range. You shouldn't have to ignore Attribute dots because you're talking over the phone or using Animal Ken to calm the guard dog you're trying to pass.
        Similarly, using Life to become more attractive (or other appearances which could help you) would instead grant Striking Looks rather than Pre/Man.

        That's one way of introducing any change you make to the players while maintaining in-game consistency. "We could just retroactively say it was bonuses applicable in those situations rather than actual Attribute increases."
