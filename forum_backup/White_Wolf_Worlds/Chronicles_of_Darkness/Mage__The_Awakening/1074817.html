    [2E] Children of the Stars - aliens loving Legacy

    04-07-2017, 03:46 PM
    This is 2E version of my own created Legacy for fringe cultists of aliens beings in Free Council that look for the Truth on the start of civilizations and Time Before.

    Notice: This Legacy is loosely based on Scientology, Ancient Aliens theory and similar beliefs, but also Assassin's Creed series. It’s not made to mock them, only to show magical potential in them.
    Children of the Stars

    Ezio: "You are... gods."
    Minerva: "No. Not gods. We simply came... before."
    ―Ezio Auditore da Firenze and Minerva, on the nature of the First Ones, 1499.


    She looked over Artifact. Patterson gazed on the item, knowing that even her Time Arcanum will not show proper date of the thing, giving nonsense results, like any of Time Before devise. First Ones made sure their own magic-technology would not perceive what hybrids wanted to know about their creator’s civilization. Still, her Attainments of her personal spiritual advancement could show her to what it was used by Those That Came Before. Maybe it will give her lead how to contact their multidimensional Home World. Or just how to open this damn doors in the Temple she is locked it!

    First Ones
    "We built you in our own image. We built you to survive."
    ―Minerva, 1499.

    Atlantis is a Lie. In eons past not primates ruled the world, but cosmic entities, from both high and distance place. Those First Ones build they civilization’s colony on small planet in classical solar system. We call this this planet Earth. There they found primitive primates and come to idea of great experiment – mixing they DNA and primates for making perfect hybrid species. After years of research, and with help of their magic-science, they were succeeded. And this is how Homo Sapiens species was born.

    First Ones were using most humans as slaves, but also schooling most promising in basic of math, astronomy and technology. They rise monumental structures and educated Homo Sapiens, and best of them choose as mating partners to sustain their species existence on Earth. Hybrids, even if looking mostly human, were given part of powers of they First Ones ancestors, now described as willworking. But with each passing millennium they colony was weaker and weaker of dilution of First Ones blood. And there come Toba catastrophe, almost wiping most of true aliens and humans. Rest decided to abandon Earth and returned to their home in stars.

    Now planet was left in hands of Willworkers, most promising of human-First Ones hybrids. When their discovered their ancestors powers, they had a dreams that were signals from aliens Home World with secret commands about continuation of great project started eons before. There is said that hybrids that use special techniques ( i.e. Legacies Attainments ) will return to Home World on they death ( what many equate to what other mages call Ascension ), because this technology works only for souls, not bodies. But for now they must guide humanity and remind them about their true ancestry. And Children of the Stars remember and make you to not forget.

    Legacy say’s its roots are ancient and really obvious to anyone just wanting to see their proofs – Mayans Long Count calendar dotted with prophesies of cosmic-scale raptures, pyramids in Egypt, Asia and Mesoamerica, Stonehenge, Temples of Time Before all around world. Children ask how someone cannot see their side and that point they only showing Truth to rest of Awakened.

    Other mages will say group come to being at the high of New Age fascination in end of 1960s on the West. When authors like Erich von Däniken shown their views, some Libertines in Free Council agreed that this version of ‘Atlantean Heritage’ is not able to swallow for most of the Diamond Orders and their ‘Golden Age’ ideology. Joined by some fringe Nameless following this new Techne, they created small but also vocal faction in Council of Free Assemblies. Now Children of the Stars are strongest proponents of archemoancy in Libertines circles, wanting to shown others they have right in their theories.

    Circa years 2000 and 2012 Legacy seen some revival, with many young Awakened questioning old Orders dogma and wanting to dwell deeper in to non-classical lore, under influence of ‘2012 craze’. Some even waited for the First Ones to come and sweep their believers in the Ascension to the Home World. None of this come to being, but larger numbers of archeomancers years after made many Lorehouse full with ancient Artifacts and exotic lore, making Diamond in some area jealous of Libertines commitment.

    Origins

    Parentage: Acanthus or Free Council

    Background: Most Children of the Star are based of two type people. First are those that truly believe human species is not alone in the universe. Even if they in Sleeper lives didn’t believe in this, their Awakening showed them that other intelligence life is watching humanity and want to contact them – only our limited minds put them in forms of angels, demons, fey, shades or primal beasts. Second type of people are those that found inconsistencies in Atlantis Myth. Runes don’t match. Laws of many Time Before sites are illogical. Evidence are somehow just wrong. Then they look for other explanations, those that don’t put Awakened as truly First Civilization. And sometimes they found answers in Stars – and nearest Children coven.

    Appearance: Many Children look neatly on the outside – but with some kind ‘fringe’ look in their eyes. They are ones that can tell you that your species was created by the alien overlords with a smile – and even make you believe them.

    Concepts: New Age astrologer, UFO conspiracy theorist, Libertines archeomancer, fringe evolutionist

    Doctrine

    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Prime 2, Occult 2, one of the following Skills at two dots or higher (Academics, Science, Survival, Expression)

    Initiation: A potential Child need to ‘communicate with First Ones’. Most of the time it works as making precise astrological calculations and on next astronomical event – like eclipse, comet flight or planetary conjunction – watch over items from Times Before for whole day before, until they hear ‘answer’ from it in the exact time of event. Most Children coven will burrow needed Artifact or relict for their future candidates if requested by mentor in Legacy.

    Organization: Children of the Stars, in reality, are like small Nameless Order inside greater Free Council. They operate in great cabals called covens, with members still being parts of their own private cabals. Contrary to columns, covens are dedicating to uncover the Truth - less to fight servants of Lie - research of Mysteries and worship of First Ones. Covens are known from experimenting with questionable ways of uncovering secrets of universe and calling many archeomancers on expeditions they organize, contrasting those organized by Silver Ladder or Mysterium.

    Theory: Magic is different from what Diamond let you believe in. It is evolutionary advancement of our race, hailing from aliens-gods that we call First Ones. With enough dedication, we can pierce secrets they hidden from rebellion our race started against them. With enough power and time, we can become First Ones.

    Magic

    Ruling Arcanum: Prime
    Yantras: ‘Mapping’ the sky for favorable astronomical event(+1); Using exact astronomical even to spell (+2); Using item from Time Before ( +2 )
    Oblations: Looking at open night sky and searching Home World or divining from stars, analyzing First Ones devices, fervent speech about First Ones to public.

    Attainments
    "They're gifts, Mr. Miles. From those who came before."
    ―Dr. Warren Vidic on the Pieces of Eden, 2012.

    Children powers are based on collecting ancient power and releasing it in proper time.

    1st Attainment: Sense the First Ones
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    To contact First Ones, one must find traces their left. This power works as Supernal Vision, that can be instantly turned on Child. Power also marks nearest places of Time Before phenomena to users eyes. Child can ask up to his Prime questions mentioned in spell.

    Optional: Time 1
    By connecting in his mind’s eye with First Ones, Child can sense their vast knowledge about things that going to happen. It works as Divination, with Potency equal to Time dots of member.

    2nd Attainment: Past of First Ones
    Prerequisites: Prime 2, Occult 3
    By pointing to a true facts about his research, Child can make even greatest disbeliever to weight his opinions. This works as Words of Truth and member need to speak his statement. Anyone of Composure lower or equal to Prime dots of user will believe him and support his cause.

    Optional: Time 2
    For Children, even Time Before problems of looking in to past are ease. When looking over item connected to Time Before and clouded by ‘dating’, he can see past it, directly how item was used. He cannot point when it was used, he only have vision of how it was used. It works as modified Postcognition spell, with Potency equal to Time dots of user.

    3rd Attainment: Presence of First Ones
    Prerequisites: Prime 3, Second Skill at three dots or a Third Skill at two dots
    Child can recreate wonders of First Ones. They can summon Platonic Form, with Potency equal to theirs Prime dots and based on the evidence or inscriptions they found in Time Before sites. Many times Children use this power to solve convoluted riddles or daring traps in this dangerous places. Or to recreate wonders they seen, but were lost when site collapsed, often under Dissonance of Sleepers.

    Optional: Time 3
    If Child have enough Time, she can restore once destroyed site of Time Before or item from it to it’s former glory. It works as Temporal Summoning with Temporal Sympathy of which Child has even to Time Before by invoking First Ones. This power is cast in ritual time, often invoking astronomical events, like those at initiation.

    4th Attainment: Future of First Ones
    Prerequisites: Prime 4, Occult 4
    By following First Ones procedures, Children can create new wonders under magic-technology of their forbearers. Member can use rules for Imbued Item Prime Attainment, but instead create Artifacts, ‘summoning items from Home World’. It works as cast in ritual time power, always under astronomical event like those in initiation.

    Optional: Time 4
    At this level, Child is so much infused with First Ones, they open her to the all possible timelines they see from Home World. Member can use this power as Prophecy spell, with Potency equal to his Time dots.

    5th Attainment: Truth of the First Ones
    Prerequisites: Prime 5, Second Skill at four dots or Third Skill at three dots or a Fourth Skill at two dots
    By summoning ultimate Truth of First Ones, mage can change the world as it should be, just like Those That Came Before done eons ago. He utilize their magic-technology and creates Eidolon of anything they seems appropriate. Being Attainment, this construct is resistance to Dissonance.

    Optional: Time 5
    By recreating Home World, Child can give oneself all the time he needs. It works as Temporal Pocket cast in ritual time – without typical need for astronomical event – and with Duration set up to one year. Returning from those moments of frozen time, many Children seems more than odd.

              They'd be the black sheep of the Order, but the Silver Ladder's precepts can be warped to meet this particular Legacy's philosophy. Specifically, let's look at the Ladder's beliefs again:
              • Magic is humanity's birthright. As inheritors of the First One's blood, of course their superior technology (ie. Magic) belongs to the Awakened.
              • Imperium is the sovereign right of humantity, ie. "humans deserve better than the Fallen World." Ditto for above.
              • The Awakened are one nation. Mages have a common bond to the Time Before, ie. the First One's civilization.
              • The Sleepers follow. It's sad that some people, even some mages, can't see the legacy of the First Ones in front of them. So the Children must lead them and build once again the First Civilization.
              • The Silver Ladder is the path to victory. Some humans just don't have the enough bio-spiritual potential to Awaken. That's okay, because even if they're descended from servitors among early humans who weren't chosen by the First Ones, their siblings and kin can still lead them and care for them..
              Remember, the Ladder doesn't need to believe in the Awakened City as a literal super-human city that existed. Saying that it was an extradimensional colony will be looked at askance, but not immediately rejected. All they need is the belief in an ancient civilization whose society, law and culture is something that modern mages should aspire to emulate. Dismissing the Ladder, or any other Diamond Order, as "Atlantean" is the bane to understanding what the Diamond Orders really are. Just saying so, since you seem fixated on interpreting the Diamond as imposing the Atlantean cultural hegemony and dogmatic orthodoxy or something, which has never been the case. (Maybe Hellenistic culture, but even that's a stretch since the Diamond globalized itself long before globalization was recognized as a concept)

              And the Seers... The Exarchs are very human in their expression and definition of Tyranny. Seers don't fawn over any entity that comes off as oppressive and authoritarian, seeing as how they don't worship high-Rank ephemerals. Also, the Exarchs may be mysterious but they are immanent and pervasive, and are certainly not some long-gone precursors. Just ask the Prelates. Because of this, even if the First Ones were the master race with humans as the slave race, that still doesn't automatically equate them to the Iron Seals. Unless these extraterrestrial and transhuman entities have for some reason the exactly same ideologies as humans.
