    The Clavicularius, updated to 2E (Mind/Spirit)

    10-24-2026, 07:44 AM
    Origins

    "Knowing others is intelligence; knowing yourself is true wisdom. Mastering others is strength; mastering yourself is true power ”
    -Lao Tzu

    Nickname: Solomonists
    Parentage: Thyrsus, Silver Ladder

    Background: The Clavicularius, or Bearers of the Keys, see themselves as successors of king Solomon, who they claim created their Attainments, naming him their spiritual founder. The historic Solomon was a Guardian monarch, who ruled wisely—or Wisely—Sleepers and Awakened alike during the Scholastic Age. He was famous for his command of beasts, demons and djinn, and for his travels to the Astral realm, where he met goetia that history recorded as angels. Artifacts related to his career abound, foremost his signet ring, table and his Key of Brass & Flame, a mighty Astral Grimoire. However, his role as founder is nebulous at best, with the greatest skeptics questioning if the Solomonist’s use of Hebrew incantations were not latter-day additions meant to give a feeling of authenticity. The Solomonists, for their part, take this in stride, saying the historical accuracy matters less than striving to follow in his footsteps.

    The earliest recorded instance of the Legacy comes from France in the 17th century, to a cabal called Les Enfants de la Severité, which was composed of one member from each of the four Diamond Orders, formed to investigate rumors of infernalism in King Louis XIV's court. Although the cabal found nothing more than titillating occultism and clumsy political poisoning amongst the Sleepers, their investigations led them to uncovering a trio of Scelesti who held "black masses" to masquerade their own goals.

    Solomonists still hold Les Enfants as an example to aspire towards: unrelentingly virtuous, magnificently firm and goetically adept. Although they attracted many to their fold, they were just as quick to leave, finding their ways unnerving. The cabal members managed to train 11 skilled apprentices, who went on to initiate others, with many modern Solomonists tracing their line to one of the original four.

    Appearances: The Solomonists often dress in fine outfits to reflect their sovereignty over their kingdom, favoring tailored clothing at the heights of political or business fashion. Nearly every member wears an article of jewelry inscribed with one of the Keys of Solomon—a tangible symbol of their commitment to the great work.

    The pursuit of self-knowledge is reflected in their Sanctums. Visitors might encounter anything from operatic arias and jam sessions to elaborate cosplays, animation storyboards, marble sculptures and clicking typewriters. In the act of creation, the Solomonists gain greater clarity into their own thoughts and can better process them.

    Doctrine

    “Self-mastery is the challenge of transforming yourself from your own worst enemy into your greatest ally”
    -Wes Fesler

    Prerequisites: Mind 2, Astral Adept

    Initiation: The initiate ingests a powerful hallucinogen, the mentor carefully sculpting the vision into harrowing images of the initiate's subconscious—drawing out their deepest fears, repressed memories, and core anxieties. If the initiate is unwilling or unable to acknowledge the raw state of their psyche, they are unready to begin the great work.

    The mentor acts as the guide through their journey, questioning them at each step while attempting to trick the apprentice into revealing any insincere reasons for seeking the keys.

    As they progress through each step, the ordeal culminates in a journey to the Great Temple of Solomon in the Temenos. Inside they will find themselves at the foot of a golden throne, sublime in its geometry. A surreal experience then occurs, unique to the initiation, the candidate is simultaneously seated upon the throne as a ruler and forced to kneel at its base as a subject, bowing and swearing fealty to their higher self. When the vision fades, they return to reality as a Solomonist. All subsequent initiations into advanced Attainments involve a similar hallucinatory journey of introspection through their mind's kingdom. When mentors admonish their students, they often ask "Was this worthy of the person sitting on the throne or the one kneeling before it ?", reminding them of the ideal they should strive to uphold.

    When one initiates themselves with a Grimoire, they ingest the hallucinogen and intone an incantation, words of brass and fire that sear themselves into their consciousness, anointing their Daimon to conduct the trial.

    Organization: The Solomonists hold no formal chain of command, instead identifying themselves based on levels of initiation.

    New members, who have learned the first and second Attainments, are called Magistrates, for at this level they are learning to listen to the testimonies of their goetia, weighing their advice, establishing personal responsibility and making a choice.

    Those that have reached the third Attainment are called Wardens, for they defend the borders of their kingdom.

    Masters of the fourth Attainment are called Keepers, for they hold the keys to the kingdom and custody over their domain.

    Finally, those who have achieved holistic mastery are called Lawgivers, because they are the ultimate arbiters of justice and authors of the laws of their kingdom.

    Solomonists often know many others, even if they don’t live in the same area, and keep in regular contact, especially master and apprentice. Members share a level of candidness that seems bizarre to outsiders, openly sharing their failings and weaknesses with one another.

    However, the Clavicularius are not a mutual comfort society. Because their magic demands absolute, unvarnished honesty, they have no patience for a peer who coddles their own weaknesses or makes excuses for their failures. Fostering a fellow member's self-pity is to help them lie to themselves. When a Solomonist stumbles, their peers don't offer sympathy—they meet the failure with sharp, biting critique and demanding advice on how to seize back control.

    Paradoxically, this makes them incredibly tight-knit, resembling a club of extreme sports enthusiasts. They share a unique brand of daring and bravado, constantly pushing and goading each other to dive deeper into their own Oneiros, confront darker personal shames, and take terrifying emotional risks. Every time a member successfully faces down a toxic compulsion or survives a grueling internal ordeal, it is celebrated with fierce, genuine admiration. In the eyes of the Solomonists, there is no greater prestige than watching a peer strip away their own ego-defensiveness and emerge with a newfound mastery of their own mind.

    Because of this, the Solomonists do not turn away mages who are deeply flawed or psychologically unstable, finding the effort and struggle to grow into more complete individuals laudable in itself.

    Master and apprentice sometimes walk the halls of the Great Temple of Solomon, a sprawling edifice of white marble erected in the Legacy's Temenos Realm by Les Enfants in the 17th century. It levitates ten feet off the ground, a strip of gold running along each wall, bearing the various sigils and pentacles of the Keys of Solomon. It is within its halls that many prospective initiates are first taught their meaning.

    The temple itself does not see many visitors, as Solomonists are often busy with their own projects. Members sometimes catch glimpses of others going about their business. Large gatherings are uncommon, with the only exception being when new members are initiated. Mentors often spread the word, and their acquaintances often attend to take the measure of the new Solomonist.

    Theory: A great sovereign is first a ruler of themselves. The mind is a kingdom, when it is not governed well, it will fall into desolation and suffering will spread.

    A wise monarch must know their domain, their subjects and their needs. They must reign with a firm hand, their decrees a source of order and stability, without fostering fear and deprivation. It is in seeing ourselves with absolute honesty where clarity lies, in assuming the responsibility to decide what parts of ourselves to embrace, to find a greater harmony with or to reject. It is in continuously striving to discipline our minds that we pave the way towards the happiness and prosperity of our kingdom.

    Know yourself, claim responsibility for the kingdom of your mind and achieve the discipline to live more fully.

    Magic

    “The purpose of discipline is to live more fully, not less.”
    -Jerry Thorpe

    Ruling Arcanum: Mind

    Yantras: Keys (+1), rings (+1, +2 if made of perfected material and bearing the Seal of Solomon), temples (+2), pentacles (+1), circlets (+1), the Keys of Solomon (+1), dream journals (+2), making an Empathy roll related to the spell (+2).

    Oblations: Exploring an Oneiros (theirs or someone else's), engaging in psychological therapy (either as the therapist or as a patient), inscribing the Keys of Solomon in a durable medium, dream journaling, artistically or physically representing a troubling thought.

    Attainments

    First: Noetic Court
    Prerequisites: Initiation

    In order to achieve true mastery of themselves, one must first listen with clarity to their inner demons and best impulses—heeding their opinions, deliberating and choosing. This is the beginning of personal responsibility.

    The Solomonist starts a scene long meditation (or guiding a subject through one), in which they envision themselves sitting with their goetia in a place they feel safe, where their strength is drawn and they feel the most authentic, opening their senses to their voices.

    The subject hears the advice of their goetia in their mind about a topic at hand, including their Daimon. If they so choose, they can take heed of the counsel of one, allowing them to aid in a Mental or Social roll. The subject suffers no untrained skill penalties and can make a teamwork roll alongside the goetia, in which it rolls [Potency] + [Potency].

    However, opening themselves to a goetia's voice means their influence on the subject is strengthened, giving the subject a Condition appropriate to the goetia consulted.

    Reach is assigned to advanced duration.

    Optional: Spirit 1

    A disciplined mind must remain vigilant that their thoughts remain their own, free of influence and manipulation, and always open to hear the voice of their goetia.

    In a dark or darkened room, the Solomonist draws a Triangle of Art beneath themselves or their subject. The subject must then light a candle and stare into their own reflection, without averting their gaze. Over the course of a scene, they must watch it warp, shift, and move to convey a message or advice from their subconscious mind.

    This replicates Exorcist's Eye with Reach assigned to advanced duration and conjunctional Mind allowing the effects to extend to goetia, as well as the presence of mental compulsions and manipulations.

    Second: Seal of Fortitude
    Prerequisites: Mind 2, Meditative Mind •

    The greatest acts require the strongest wills. Solomists forge theirs in the fires of conviction, tempering them to resist supernatural powers and persevere through adversity. The Solomonist (or their subject) spends a scene writing a letter from themselves to themselves, listing the deep qualities they admire or envy in others. Once completed, they read it aloud and stamp it with a Seal of Solomon, committing themselves to expressing them in their daily lives.

    This replicates a Potency primary Ruling of Mind, which increases the dice or Resistance trait bonus gained through Willpower expenditure by [Mind], up to trait bonus limits. Reach is assigned to advanced duration.

    Optional: Spirit 2

    Whenever a goetia's sway grows too powerful to handle with an even hand, the Solomonist binds and isolates them. In doing so, they are afforded an opportunity to speak to them without being influenced, allowing them to understand them, examine healthy ways to reintegrate them or remove purely toxic ones completely.

    Over the course of a scene, the Solomonist inscribes the Seal of Solomon into a suitable vessel and empowers it with a point of mana, transforming it into a Djinni Bottle. It may imprison a spirit or goetia of up to Rank [lesser of Mind and Spirit], although the wielder must find a way to trap the being. It is capable of projecting into the mind of the holder alongside them, with any being captured being sealed in the real bottle, and beings can be similarly released into mental realms.

    Reach is assigned to Lasting duration and a point of mana pays for Lasting duration.

    Third: Bastion of Clarity
    Prerequisites: Mind 3, Meditative Mind ••

    The Solomonist's thoughts are theirs alone to command. After donning a pentacle or drawing one on the ground (or doing so for another), the subject spends a scene laying bare their current psychological state, confessing all that weighs heavily on their mind, exploring how each of those burdens truly makes them feel. Following this confession, they explicitly draw their psychological boundaries aloud—deciding what they choose to tolerate from these thoughts, as well as what they utterly reject and cast out from their self-identity, sealing their minds against external and internal intrusion.

    This replicates Mental Shield with Reach assigned to advanced duration and defending from attacks by goetia.

    Optional: Spirit 3

    The Solomonist allows no djinn to control them, fortifying their defenses against the influence of those that seek to usurp their sovereignty.

    This replicates Ephemeral Shield with Reach assigned to advanced duration and defending from attacks by spirits. It is activated alongside the main Attainment.

    Fourth: Temple of Discipline
    Prerequisites: Mind 4

    The Solomonist issues a sovereign decree over their own mind, planting contingencies or forcing themselves to carry through hard choices. When combined with the Bastion of Clarity, it presents them with a profound choice: obey as the one kneeling before the throne, or assert their will as the one sitting upon the throne. When their authority is turned outwards, it allows them to command disobedient goetia or even extend their dominion over others.

    This replicates Psychic Domination, with Reach assigned to instant use, advanced duration and complex commands.

    Optional: Spirit 4

    The Solomonist calls forth one of their subjects to serve them in the material, and binds disobedient ones just as easily. They spend a scene tracing a magic circle, before they (or another person) step into it. The Solomonist commands the goetia of the one inside the circle to appear, who acquiesce to their sovereignty.

    A goetia can be summoned into a nearby Djinni Bottle. When the bottle is opened and if the duration has not expired, it Materializes nearby, allowing the Solomonist to unleash powerful ones in moments. Should it still be bound in the bottle when the duration expires, it remains sealed.

    This replicates Goetic Summons with Reach assigned to changing primary factor and advanced duration. With conjunctional Spirit 2 granting the Materialized condition and conjunctional Spirit 3 letting them summon from the Oneiros.

    Fifth: The Regal Self
    Prerequisites: Mind 5, Meditative Mind ••••

    When the mind has been truly disciplined, earnest and meaningful change can be made in moments. The Solomonist can commit wholeheartedly to any Aspiration or Obsession they choose to pursue, or when aiding others with theirs. They can even genuinely change their perspective, allowing them to deeply understand and empathize with others. When turned outwards, they can extend this benefit to their allies, or recruit their adversaries to their side.

    This replicates Psychic Reprogramming with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration.

    Optional: Spirit 5

    The Solomonist understands that the great work of the self is ever ongoing, the kingdom only thrives when the monarch diligently strives towards prosperity. In order to uphold this ideal, the Solomonist calls forth an Almadel, a living embodiment of their Art, their greatest ally and mentor.

    The process is similar to a targeted Supernal Summoning. The Solomonist needs to prepare a summoning circle, but it does not require them to do so in a Supernal Verge or Demesne. While the process remains an extended action, its nature as an Attainment means the process never invites the Abyss. Similarly, dramatic failures do not cause Abyssal intrusion, although the Solomonist still suffers the aggravated damage. The Almadel remains vulnerable to the corrosion of the Fallen World, taking Corpus damage as normal. However, they always return to the Supernal safely if they perish outside of the summoning circle. The Solomonist does not risk Wisdom degeneration from the Almadel’s death, unless they were personally responsible for causing it.

    The Almadel is a Rank 5 demon with Mind and Spirit as their primary Arcana, possessing the maximum traits for their Rank and may grant the Sins Revisited Boon. They act in the Solomonist's best interest and are uniquely in tune with them, regaining all their mana when the Solomonist fulfills their Virtue, and a single point when they fulfill their Vice. Their Virtue is Firm and their Vice is Ambitious.

    Their Trial is for the Solomonist to prove they have risen above their pride and ego-defensiveness. The Almadel will ask a single question that lays bare their deepest shame (their Vice, Hubris, latest shameful action, etc). Each participant has one sentence to either validate the Almadel's accusation or speak a hard truth about the Solomonist's blind spots. The summoner then has one sentence to answer the question with absolute, unvarnished honesty, with no attempts at deflecting or justifying via Willpower expenditure or mental augmentation. Successfully doing so (which may be done with a Resolve + Composure roll) means the Solomonist integrates the critique, whereas failure grants the Guilty condition. If any participant contributed a genuinely biting, honest insight, the summoner receives a +2 bonus to the roll. The Trial doesn't count towards the summon's duration.

    What were you thinking design wise ?

    The first Attainment allows them to hear the different voices in their mind more clearly, to interact and understand each of them, giving them a greater opportunity at self knowledge. It also allows them to choose which ones to pay attention to and gives them a bonus for choosing to work together with them. Since coming up with the traits for each goetia would require a lot of bookkeeping from the storyteller, I instead abstracted their traits with Potency. In order to balance this out, they receive a thematically appropriate condition from it. Since those grant Beats once a scene, it incentivizes them to use them. But if they overly rely on it, then they will accumulate multiple Conditions. If they over indulge, the accumulation of Condition will create problems for them. But if they do not, then they restrain their potential. So finding the right balance is key with this Attainment, reflecting their philosophy. The activation ritual involves creating a mental space where they feel at ease to interact with their subconscious and to give their impulses form.

    The first optional Attainment allows them to know when they are under a mental compulsion and to always be able to interact with their goetia. The activation ritual shows them a glimpse of their Jungian shadow.

    The second main Attainment reflects the fierce force of will they would cultivate by constantly disciplining their mind, allowing them to resist spells, shake off supernatural abilities and power through adversity. It is based on the Zeal spell from the Seers of the Throne book. Since it increases the dice bonus of Willpower, it maps to a Ruling of Mind. The activation ritual requires them to set an aspirational standard to work towards and to make an active commitment to pursue it, with the Seal of Solomon rendering their decree to themselves official.

    The second optional Attainment is based on how Solomon sealed the djinn. It is inspired by the Soul Jar spell, but specialized to affect goetia and spirits. It allows them to not only capture dangerous ones, but to use it for self knowledge as well. Whenever a part of themselves becomes overwhelming, they can isolate it, talk to them without being influenced and then calmly determine if there is a healthier way to harmonize with them, or if they are purely toxic and need to be cast out. It projects into the wielder’s mind so it remains useful during Astral travel and to make it convenient for them to trap or release goetia from their own psyche. The activation ritual is based on how the demon sealing bottle was enchanted in the Constantine TV series.

    The third main and optional Attainments focus on protecting their minds from being influenced or controlled, as well as allowing them to safely deal with goetia and djinn (spirits). The activation ritual is to clearly define their psychological boundaries, making them more resilient.

    The fourth main Attainment shows how they have disciplined their mind to the point that they can plant subliminal commands or push through difficult decisions. It can be used on goetia or spirits in Djinni Bottles as well, in order to make disobedient ones cooperate or to extend it outwards to command their adversaries.

    The fourth optional Attainment is the classic demon summoning circle, allowing them to summon the goetia of their mind or of others anytime and anywhere. It can also be used to store them in a Djinni Bottles, allowing them to do cool things such as having a demon in a bottle or throw a demon grenade. It also allows them to call loyal ones to their aid with ease.

    The capstone Attainment allows them to meaningfully and genuinely change themselves as needed, allowing them to gain Beats and Arcane Beats from pursuing any Aspiration or Obsession they are focusing on, or those they are helping their cabalmates with. It also lets them benefit from appropriate Virtues and Vices, or more easily understand others by adopting those traits in themselves. It also allows them to turn any enemy to their cause. The Attainment is named after the term they used in 1ed for the higher self they see sitting upon the throne in the initiation.

    The optional capstone serves multiple purposes. The first is to take into account that personal growth is a continuous process and have a mentor to thematically embody that. The second is to solve the challenge that there aren’t enough Attainments to cover all the flavorful goetia and spirit manipulating. Having a powerful Supernal Entity that has both Arcana and unlimited free Reach, plus is a mighty allied being, solves that in a fun way, even if the length of the summoning and the time they can remain acts as limiting factors.

    The Trial itself invites a short but intense moment of roleplay, where the character has to take a hard look at themselves. If time is short or if roleplay alone was not enough, the roll can be used to abstract it. Not being able to use Willpower or Mind augmentations means that the demon will not allow them to cheat themselves. The ability to grant the Sins Revisited Boon symbolizes how the demon is there to promote the Solomonist reflecting on their flaws. Its Virtue and Vice embody the quintessential ones of the Clavicularius. Gaining mana from the summoner’s Virtue and Vice symbolizes how the Solomonist needs both to be a complete individual. The entity is named after the Ars Almadel, the fourth and final book in the Lesser Key of Solomon, which focuses on invoking angelic beings.

    When they are put together, they paint a Solomonist as someone with a well disciplined mind and skilled command of ephemeral beings. Their Attainments also mean they have internalized Solomon’s signature tools: the ring (Seal of Fortitude), the temple (Temple of Discipline) and his table (the Almadel).

    The core design phrase for this Legacy was “master yourself”. When looking at the 1ed version of the Clavicularius from Legacies:The Sublime, it had some very flavorful ideas, depicting them as repressed and chronically relapsing goetic thrill seekers, which created a lot of roleplay potential. However, it also mentioned they studied the Astral frequently and named engaging in psychotherapy as an oblation, which seemed at odds with their methodology. In addition, while their lore had a lot of flavor, the Attainments did not mechanically embody the results of their self-control and command of goetia, being very situational. So when designing the update, I chose to make the Mind Attainments reflect their mastery of their own mind and the Spirit ones to focus on the command of goetia and spirits, drawing a symbolic parallel to their mind as the kingdom they would rule as its Solomon. Their power comes from turning those tools outwards, even if that was not their original purpose. I also changed their methodology to utilize Jungian shadow work, with their activation rituals all being based on exercises that help promote it.

    The inclusion of Astral Adept means they can access the Astral and their Oneiros anytime and anywhere. Having Meditative Mind as an Attainment prerequisite is meant to reflect their constant work on disciplining themselves and their focus on mindfulness, as well as give them one more way to resist mental compulsions.

    The background mixes elements from Legacies: The Sublime, Grimoire of Grimoires and Tome of the Pentacle. When looking at Solomon’s entry in the Scholastic Age, it stated he ruled beasts (Life), djinn (Spirits) and demons (Mind), which sounded to me like a Thyrsus with Mind as their third Ruling Arcanum. When combined with their great focus on the psyche and the Astral, I decided to make Mind their Ruling Arcanum and to make Thyrsus the parent Path, strengthening their connection to their spiritual founder. It also aligns with their updated philosophy, since it focuses on Boundaries and Intercession.

    Their appearance is similar to 1ed, but their Sanctums reflect the changes in their approach.

    The initiation is similar to the original one, but focusing on making the prospective member take a hard look at themselves, forcing them to acknowledge the current state of their mind before they can start working on disciplining it. The addition of the self initiation is meant to explain how the ritual could proceed if they could not find a mentor, with their Daimon taking over the stewardship of it.

    The organization retains the spirit of constantly challenging the Solomonist to do better and to seek greater challenges. While they still applaud each other’s successes, now rather than scornful rebuking members when they fail to defeat their flaws, they instead direct their critique at them when they are lying to themselves. Since second edition Legacies have five Attainments instead of three, the ranks were changed to reflect that. Since it is expected for members that were initiated to have the first two Attainments, they fall under the same rank. The titles are meant to represent higher positions of authority as they administer the kingdom of the self. The fourth tier references the Legacy’s name and the highest rank alludes to Solomon as the supreme arbiter of justice in his kingdom.

    The Greater Rite of Castigation was also removed, since the character was not present when it happened in 1ed and, even when hit with the backlash once their goetia was returned, it was not much different from what they were already doing.

    The yantras were made to embody the symbols of Solomon, with special attention being given to the ring. A circlet was chosen to represent the sovereignty over their own mind, while also staying away from symbols of the Exarchs. A dream journal allows the Solomonist to document any changes in their psyche and acts as a great tool for the storyteller to deliver prophecies, warnings and foreshadowing, and keeping it up to date is further incentivized in their oblations. The use of Empathy rolls is meant to mechanically drive them to look at a situation through their emotional intelligence.

    In the case of oblations, the Rite of Castigation was phased out, because even though it was cool, it involved a very ritualized way for the Solomonist to express what was on their mind, and even then only characters with Occult knowledge would be able to parse it out. The use of art means they express it in songs, sculptures, poems and other equally haunting ways, but in a manner that makes it easy for any player or NPC to engage with. Lastly, gaining mana from exploring the Oneiros means they have a reason to take deep dives into their own minds, or to volunteer to help other characters understand theirs.

    This updated version of the Clavicularius should feel unnerving to outsiders due to their intense authenticity, and because their brand of magic requires people to be vulnerable with themselves and the Solomonist in a way that would make their skin crawl. Granny Weatherwax from Discworld provides a great example of what a fully realized Solomonist could be like. Yoko Nakajima from The Twelve Kingdoms is an example of how a Solomonist’s character arc could look like. Her journey from the start of the series, up to her first proclamation, has a strong focus on self-discovery, looking at her honest thoughts and deciding what to integrate and what to reject.

    That concludes the design notes, hope you had as much fun reading them as I had writing them.
