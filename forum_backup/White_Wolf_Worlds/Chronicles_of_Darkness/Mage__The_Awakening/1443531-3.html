            This legacy comes from a desire for more high fantasy alchemy, and from the realization that Matter can enhance the effects a substance has on a body.

            Tamers of Roots

            description

            Better Living Through Chemistry

            There have always been those that seek knowledge of root and stem. Dirt and Stone have ecologies of their own, with ore vein root and flowers.

            Origins

            Parentage: Thyrsus, Free Council, Nameless, Seers
            Background: Many come from medical backgrounds,
            Appearances: Many are warped and twisted by the strange chemicals running in their veins, with signs of their preferred method of delivery.
            Nickname: Blights, Alchemists

            Doctrine

            Prerequisites: Matter 2, Life 2, Medicine 2, Science 2, Occult or Crafting 1
            Initiation: Consumption and survival without magic of an extremely toxic mix of nightshade and Aperion
            Organization: Pre-Great Refusal: A council overseen by the strongest legacy member. After Great Refusal: Master Student chains
            Theory: Certain Materials when consumed or with exposure, effect the body and mind. The Tamers of Roots brew potions that elevate these effects to a wonderous degree.

            Magic
            Ruling Arcanum: Matter
            Yantras: (+1); (+2); (+2); (consumption of a prepared draught+1, a draught congaing supernatural materials +2)
            Oblations: tending to a garden; studying chemical formula; self-experimentation;

            Attainments

            First: The Formula
            Prerequisites: Initiation.
            The Tamers must know the properties of their materials before anything else. The Tamer may take a instant action to study a non-living object and learn it’s properties and use in recipes. The Tamer gains the informed (material use) condition. This includes any supernatural properties a material may have.
            Discern Composition with advanced duration.
            This may not be a properly built attainment and I’m open to suggestions. I was going to do craftsman’s eye, but I don’t have the reach to learn the uses, so this is me comprise. I want them to just know how to use their chemicals and herbs after just looking at them as the first attainment
            Optional: Life 1
            When using the first attainment, the tamer may scan still living creatures to see what properties their body parts may have, both while still living and when they turn into matter.

            Second: The Immunity

            Prerequisites: Matter 2, Life 2, Medicine 3
            The Tamers consume and use toxic materials on a daily basis, immunity to the negative effects of their draughts. The tamer is immune to negative conditions caused by their creations, in addition they are immune to bashing damage caused by handling toxic materials, and reduce lethal damage by their Matter dots. The mage also adds Matter dots to stamina rolls to resist the effects
            Alchemist's Touch and Cleanse the Body with advanced duration

            The Tamers of Roots handle toxic materials regularly, they need inbuilt protection.

            Third: The Elixir

            Prerequisites: Matter 3, Science 3, Crafts 2 or Occult 2
            The Tamer can create a potion, a blank state material that can absorb up the Tamers Matter dots in sacrificed materials to absorb a property of a sacrificed non-living material. This includes any supernatural properties a material may have. The potion can last up to matter years before expiring, or for a additional mana may be indefinite.


            Spell Potion and Wonderful Machine with advanced duration. The extra mana comes from the matter 2 attainment.

            I'm not sure if this should be third or fourth attainment.

            Optional: Life 3
            .The tamer may use living materials, like perfected blood or flesh, or vampire blood, in their potions.

            Maybe I drop this and allow the weaving of certain properties into the body when consumed?

            Fourth: The Refinement
            Prerequisites: Matter 4, Medicine 4.
            The tamer may enhance the effects of a given substance, adding matter dots to the chosen effect. If the substance is used in The Elixir attainment, the enhanced properties remain if selected.
            Matter Perfecting with advanced duration
            Optional: Life 4


            Fifth: Clever name
            Prerequisites: Matter 5, two dots in a third Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills


            Optional: Life 5.

                This legacy comes from a desire for more high fantasy alchemy, and from the realization that Matter can enhance the effects a substance has on a body.
                I'd recommend making this a Moros Life/Matter Legacy, rather than a Thyrsus Matter/Life Legacy for two reason:

                The first is flavor: Moroi are heavily steeped in the "alchemy" symbolism and already have the nickname "Alchemists."

                The second is overlap: there's already a Matter/Life Thyrsus Tamer Legacy, the Tamers of Rivers.

                I would look at the DE2 Legacy "Kitchen Alchemists" for a solid starting point on what a Matter/Life Alchemy Legacy could and should do.

                            Yeah, I'm expecting some rewrites of this one. I've had one person argue for a Thyrsus Matter legacy before, I figured the difference in theme should be enough from the Tamers of Rivers, but I can redo this as a Moros version.
                            The main reason I'd say Moros is less about the Tamers of Rivers (though that is a small consideration), and more the heavy "Alchemist" theming to the Moros.

                            Assuming you swap this to Moros:

                            For the first Attainment, I'd just make it a combined attainment of Life and Matter that reveals what would happen if the substance were ingested or otherwise used in an alchemical concoction, basically looking at something would give you a list of its properties a la Elder Scrolls. Advanced Duration, requires an Instant Action to study the object.

                            Second is fine as it is, protection is always a good idea, the bonus to rolls should be Life, though, not Matter.

                            Third: Life side I'd go with Body Control with the +2 Reach option for halving Lethal healing time and Advanced Duration from the Matter 2 Attainment Permanence since this would be tied to an alchemical potion, however it would only work on the Mage, instead acting as a toxin with strength equal to the effect's potency of (1 + lower of Life or Matter dots)/2 and a duration based on the Mage's Life dots on the Advanced Duration chart, roll every Lethal healing interval.
                            For the Matter Attainment I'd go with the ability to spend a scene to create a potion that, when ingested, causes the effects of Transform Life on the imbiber with the effects of Shrink and Grow as one of the potential options (each potency allocated to Shrink and Grow would be a potency not allocated to a different Transform Life effect). This Attainment would cost a Mana (Permanence Attainment), the transformation would last a scene, and it would spend its reach on Advanced Scale: Size. The Mage would have to choose which effects took place in what order in case the imbiber decides to Withstand. The potion itself retains its potency for the Mage's Matter dots on the Advanced Duration chart.

                            Fourth: Life side: Honing the Form with Advanced Duration (Permanence), the ability to split between two attributes, and the ability to boost an attribute above the normal maximum rating, costs 1 Mana. Same negative effects on anyone other than the Mage consuming it as the Third Attainment, consuming another concoction changes the Attribute bonuses. Takes a scene to prepare a concoction, but a whole bunch can be made if the Mage wants to constantly flip-flop their Attributes.
                            Matter side: can create a more potent potion with a shorter duration that can be consumed by anyone. Honing the Form with reach to change the primary factor to Potency, Advanced Duration from Permanence, and the ability to boost attribute above the normal maximum rating, boosts a single Attribute of the imbiber by the Mage's Life dots for a Scene (attribute chosen during potion creation). Costs a Mana. The potion itself retains its potency for the Mage's Matter dots on the Advanced Duration chart. If the Mage drinks their own potion the boost stacks with the boost from the primary portion of the Attainment, rather than overwriting or giving only a transient boost.

                            Fifth: I'd put Hone the Perfected Form here with the +2 reach option and Life conjunctional to work on a living person, Advanced Duration from Permanence, and Indefinite for the final reach. This would only work on the Mage in question, killing anyone else that consumed the potion, what the effects of being Perfected would be on the Mage is something I'll have to think about, but it seems cool, so...

                            I'd also make all of the above require special ingredients to keep with the theme.
