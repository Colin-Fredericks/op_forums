    Chapter 38
    10/18/2024

    The conversation between the mages and Jessie continued, with Jessie feeling nervous about the idea of reattaching her missing limb, but she decided to trust Eye of God's judgment and consented to trying. Together Eye of God and Maxwell went to the Athenaeum to request the help of a Life mage. They found Architect, Darkened Winds, and the same elderly mage from before. Architect heard out their request and sent Eye of God to the part of the temple that had records in spirits to find a mage named Shadow’s Muse, while he sent Maxwell to find a mage named Uriel.

    Shadow’s Muse was reading from some sort of scroll when Eye of God found her, and he recognized her as “M”, another censor. Uriel was reading from a Bible in a small side room of the temple, the room was filled with books, scrolls, Stone tablets, and other records. Maxwell looked at a stone slab that seemed to be instructions on how to deal with Abyssal manifestations but didn't spend too long in the room, leaving with Uriel, but not before peaking at the part of the Bible she was reading, Revelations. The two mages were instructed to assist Eye of God and Maxwell with reattaching the arm of a sleepwalker as an act of Guanxi. Neither mage seemed overly concerned when they heard what they were being asked to do and consented to helping immediately.

    When the four mages arrived back at Eye of God's apartment they found Jessie watching Fullmetal Alchemist. As they prepared to reattach her arm Jessie asked if the main characters of the anime reminded him of them, but couldn't figure out how to elaborate on what she felt, so he wasn't really able to answer that question. Uriel spent some time sewing the severed arm back on the Jessie, while Shadow’s Muse cast a spell to reduce the pain while that was happening, and then cast the spell she was asked to cast, allowing the natural healing properties of the body to work where they normally wouldn't, allowing the body to reattach the arm over a period of time. The four mages spoke for a moment about the logistics of the spell and how long it would likely take for the arm to be fully restored before leaving. On her way out Uriel gave Maxwell a look up and down, giving the impression she knew about him and was sizing him up for something.

    The three spoke for a bit, trying to decide what their next step should be now that Jessie's arm was slowly healing. Maxwell decided to step out and call his father, telling him he was planning on visiting home tomorrow. At first he was worried that something had happened, but once Maxwell assured him everything was okay he was excited to see his son and quipped that he was looking forward to the home cooked food Maxwell's mother would be making. Maxwell started discussing with Eye of God about helping him get through airport security the next morning when both of them felt something happening as Jessie grimaced and cried out in pain. Both of them could see with their mage sights that something was changing about her arm. Eye of God sprung into action, opening a portal to the Getty and running down to find Shadow’s Muse, calling out that something was happening and they took off together back to the portal and leaped through.

    In the apartment a battle ensued as the reattached arm broke and fused the bones in the forearm and hand into a bone blade to fight back the mages, while the spot that was connected to Jessie began chewing. At first all three mages used magic to try to destroy the arm, with Jessie failing to do any real damage while striking at it in a panic, but eventually Eye of God began using his Mind magic to recall the notes he had skimmed over in the hospital and magic to help enhance his own mind so he could make better sense of them. While he did gain information the amount of paradox his body had to withstand was staggering, and he almost began dealing real damage to his body. He ended up getting the faintest sensation of stepping back through that portal to The Garden of Eternal Rot <See Chapter 2> but pushed it away as he realized what needed to be done. By this time Maxwell and Shadow's Muse had managed to use magic to blast the arm out of the stitches, so it was flailing itself around on the ground, the part of it that was connected to Jessie like a lamprey’s mouth. All three mages realized that their magic was targeting the arm as if it was a separate creature from Jessie, damaging the arm but not her.

    Eye of God gained two different insights from the alchemist's work. Firstly, the ritual the alchemist was performing is one of fallen magic and seems to date back to Egypt from the time of the Pharos, drawing on the mythology of Osiris. A body must be cut into 14 pieces, and then put back together with one of these pieces missing, submerged in flowing water, but beyond this it is unclear what was done, but he knows it only worked after a fever dream the alchemist had, and it only worked once, on Jessie. The second thing he learned was of a source of power the alchemist called Azoth, with two different forms, like Yin-Yang or the two sides of a coin, Pyros and Flux. Creatures of flux were attracted to creatures of Pyros and would try to consume that pyros. Using his Prime magic, and assisted by Maxwell, he tried to draw out the flux from the arm but couldn't, so instead Shadow's Muse bound the arm in iron and took it to the Athenaeum to censor it, agreeing with Eye of God not to write up any details on how it was found until they were there together so he could be as vague as possible about Jessie.

    Both mages then went to their hallows, Maxwell drawing the mana out with magic and Eye of God meditating, trying to avoid Brandon who was there working, and trying to keep Jessie calm, for she refused to leave his side.

    Once back at his apartment she went into a panic attack, saying she trusted him and it backfired, that she didn't understand what had happened to herz she didn't understand why her capture has done what he did, didn't know why she had such strange memories, and didn't know how she was supposed to defend herself. Eye of God could sense something was happening with his mage sight, and saw that there was a core part of Jessie that was trying to change into something else as she struggled with what had happened. She begged him to start taking her with him in adventures so she could learn to defend them both, or so that she could learn about what she was. He didn't want to because of how bad she was at fighting, so she asked him to put her in contact with Ragnar so she could learn. At that moment something changed in Jessie, that part that Eye of God saw was trying to change, changed, taking a new shape and with it, Jessie changed, becoming more aggressive and standing taller before him. He challenged her to stroke him, trying to make a point. With the help of his Space armor attainment she missed, but it was clear to him that she would have broken his nose without it. He let her text Ragnar, and she deleted the text. He reminded her he could just read her mind and get the answer to which she responded, “Maybe the fact that I deleted it should be enough for you to know that you shouldn't.”

        This was supposed to be a Halloween session but nothing I did inspired me to want to run one, so we ended up just having a normal session. Happily though, Giger’s player has returned, so while Ragnar is still MIA dealing with the workload of the season, our Acanthus is finally back. His return made the session a little chaotic but we live for that.

        Chapter 39
        11/2/2024

        This chapter started with Eye of God the morning after the battle with Jessie's arm. As he left his bedroom he found her not in the couch she was usually relaxing on, but instead next to it doing sit-ups. He wasn't able to not notice how tired she had become literally overnight, whatever had happened to her had not only changed her personality but had altered her body somehow. Eye of God offered her a second chance at striking him, and still with his Space armor she missed, this time leaving a hole in the drywall. She seemed proud of that and left for a run.

        Giger and Maxwell worked on getting mana from their hallows, but Eye of God had a strange experience while at his. Brandin, his coworker and friend, came up to him while he was reading the manga that the hallow stores the mana in, to ask if he needed help checking out. Brandon has no clue who Eye of God was, clearly disturbed by the familiarity that the mage had for him, and was even more disturbed when Eye of God asked about him collecting snot. When he returned from work he told Jessie that Ragnar invited her to his dojo to train and she left immediately.

        Giger went to spend time with Mark Slim. It was clear that he still wasn't sleeping over the past week, still obsessing, still insisting that The Shadow That Walks was still out there, insisting that The Shadow That Walks is pathological in his theft and murder. Giger, wanting to help calm him down, ended up taking him to 71 Above, a restaurant and bar, where they both drank together until Mark finally seemed relaxed. Once back, Mark Slim III seemed relieved and helped Giger get him to bed before asking if he had talked to Eye of God recently. Clearly there was something more there but Giger couldn't pick up on it.

        Once back at his apartment Giger texted the others that he had spent the last week studying and believes he discovered a way to deal with The Shadow That Walks Abyssal and to meet at his apartment, but not to bring Uriel <Maxwell had brought Uriel but once the God-Killer Sword was mentioned she started asking questions and Giger rewound time to avoid her being there.> Eye of God brought Mark Slim III with him as his apprentice. Here, he asked to cast a spell on Eye of God, rewritten some of his history where he “gave it his all” while trying to summon the sword but the summoning still failed, so Giger finally decided to accept the inevitable. <For months now Giger would give Eye of God crap OOC about not spending a point of willpower during that summoning to give him the extra dice he needed, causing them to fail by one success. Finally that feud is over it seems, was a funny reuniting scene.> The cabal decided it was time to attempt the summoning again, and as they prepared they heard police sirens in the distance.

        The summoning was successful after only three hours of work, smoke billowed out of the summoning circle, threatening to choke the mages out before finally receding and revealed a cave mouth. Giger told Maxwell he needed to enter alone to pass the trial. The cave turned quickly into a perfect square shaped hallway covered in High Speech, with curtains having negative space that hurt Maxwell to look at for too long. He started to walk through the hallway but triggered a trap and took an arrow to his leg <no, not to his knee> and decided to levitate across instead. Then he came to a doorway that was covered in more runes and images of dragons and gold. Above the door, in High Speech, read “I am a dragon's distant cousin, small, bright and true”. Instead of trying to answer the riddle he used his Prime magic in the door, forcing it to open enough to get in. Once inside he saw the God-Killer Sword, a silver-blue great sword. Around the corners of the room were other artifacts and piles of gold. He took the sword, which was surprisingly light, and then went to look at another artifact, a helmet-crown that gave off an aura of authority. His Prime magic revealed that the artifact had Mind and Fate magic associated with it, and he decided to take it also. Once he left the treasure room the entire hall started shaking and he heard a roar come from somewhere deep within, “THIEF! YOU STEAL WHAT IS NOT YOURS!.” He got out as quickly as he could, the helmet vanishing once he left the cave and the sword growing heavy in his hand and stabbing through the floor of Giger's apartment.

        Giger and Eye of God were able to hear the roar of the dragon as well, and even after the ritual ended, both mages were able to see a dragon coiled around Maxwell in their mage sights. Eye of God’s Space sight showed him many new eyes <Sympathetic connections> staring at Maxwell with hatred and fear, and Giger saw golden threads spew from the sword and weave themselves into a thick rope around Maxwell's neck, wrists and ankles. Giger also suddenly felt uneasy with Maxwell in his apartment and decided they should get the sword back to Maxwell's sanctum. Maxwell used gravity magic on the sword and they drove to his apartment, where the three mages worked together to cast a Prime veiling spell to hide the Supernal nature of the artifact for the time being.

        The final moment of the session had each of our mages hit with a shockwave of magic and Eye of God saw the world around him change, becoming dark and falling apart. When he heard Maxwell groan from the overwhelming magic it sounded like a far off whisper and bitch Maxwell and Giger seemed to be almost invisible to him. When he looked down at himself he was in black robes that were bound to him with a chain. All three of them saw Mark Slim III was overflowing with Supernal energies, and Eye of God realized that his apprentice was awakening and that he had been pulled into the Mystery play.

            Chapter 40
            11/11/2024

            <This chapter happened before Giger returned in Chapter 39, and was a solo session with me and Giger.>

            <In order to understand the gravity of this chapter for Giger, we need to understand Giger’s history that has not been revealed to the cabal. In 1999, Giger was still a child of 10, obviously unawakened, when his mother and father were killed brutally in an art deal gone wrong. This eventually inspired him to become The Shadow That Walks, as he decided that no one should be killed over priceless items, because no one should have that much to begin with, so he began stealing from the ultra wealthy, those who flaunt it. In addition, Leopold has always been in his family's service as a butler, although Giger would learn of his time in the British Intelligence Forces until he was older, he was a member of it until his father asked him to leave to serve the Murphy family as he had, convincing Leopold to stop being a spy and start acting as a butler for at least the past thirty years.>

            This chapter begins with Giger leaving the Dragon’s Hoard after successfully summoning and completing the trial of The Thief King’s Cloak, an artifact he knew would give him the capabilities not just of a master thief, but of a thief that would lead other thiefs, he was also aware of the doom that came along with the destiny; betrayed. He watched as golden threads erupted out of the cloak and became a rope that bound his arms, legs, and neck. <I pointed out to him that there were no accusations of being called a thief like what had happened in Chapter 39 with Maxwell, but Giger currently has no context for that. There was also no smoking or roaring chasing him out. When Giger stole from the hoard the Dragon remained asleep.> As he recovered from this he heard congratulations coming from the doorway to his apartment. Gyges, the mage he and Eye of God had sworn to help reclaim truths from the Abyss, stood in his apartment, clearly pleased.

            Naturally, Giger was curious as to why Gyges was here. Gyges told Giger <I am regretting my naming choice> that he has been waiting for Giger to retrieve this artifact from the Dragon's Hoard so that he could continue shaping him to his purpose, needing his Gnosis to be the correct shape in order to be able to steal from the Abyss as he needs it to be. In order to work towards this, he had a job he needed him to do. Twenty-three years ago, in 1996, a mage known as “The Clockmaster,” was arrested for practicing Abyssal magic, specifically, trying to pull alternate timelines from the Abyss. He was eventually excommunicated, as his grimoire went missing, which was the strongest evidence for his intent. No one knows if he hid it or destroyed it himself, but it was never found. Gyges wanted Giger to return to his life in 1996 and retrieve the grimoire before it went missing. While it isn't exactly what Gyges is trying to do, it is adjacent, and therefore might prove insightful.

            Giger agreed, so Gyges cast some spells on him, transforming himself into his seven year old form, warning him that he'd need to hide the unmaking effect on himself to not draw attention, and swearing an oath that he'd lose his limbs if he didn't allow Giger to dispel the unmaking effect so he could return to the present, before finally sending Giger to the past.

            In the past Giger found himself in Beverly Hills, in his family's home there. He could hear the shower running, and saw his mom, reading not far from him. He ran to her to hug her, which she was pleased with. Giger made her promise to take him to play at the park later before running to his room to hide and cry. Once he had his cry he cast Time veiling to hide that he was displaced in time and noticed his spell did not trigger his peripheral mage sight, somehow Gyges had blocked it, so no one would notice Giger using spells.

            Giger went to find his father, who was freshly showered and visiting with his wife. Giger ran to him, told him he loved him and announced that they were going to the park later to play and that his dad should come. His father agreed and looked sadly at Giger, but Giger couldn't figure out why, <0 Empathy> but his Time sight showed that Time around his father seemed to be chewed at the edges somehow. He would investigate later.

            Giger eventually got to himself and used Fate magic to dial a random number to bring him closer to The Clockmaster. On the other end of the phone a familiar voice answered, after talking for a bit he realized it was Blood. He told Blood he was investigating The Clockmaster and wanted to exchange information. Blood agreed and they met at a cafe nearby. Giger used magic to make him look like his adult self and met with Blood, who was completely different from the Blood Giger knows from the present. They discussed what they knew, Giger revealing that he knew The Clockmaster was a mage of the Mysterium and Blood sharing that they knew he was active only within Los Angeles. Blood told him of another Thyrsus, Eternal Elm, that could perhaps get them into the Athenaeum. They met her, explained they were looking for an Acanthus of the Mysterium, and asked if she could help. She seemed to be friends with Blood, so she agreed. Together they went to the Getty.

            Beneath the Getty they were confronted by a mage who introduced himself as Adam. Giger explained what was going on, and that he was also a mystagogue, flashing the sign he had learned from the First Atlantean Mystery, and Adam responded by showing him the sign saying he was higher along that Giger was. Adam was happy to help them regardless, but insisted that he would be nearby to keep an eye on Giger, and that Blood was not allowed to touch anything.

            They went to the Spiral Room, where all the Arcadian/Acanthus lore was stored. A book rests outside this room, and when a mage writes what he is looking for he can hear the room rearrange itself so that whatever information he sought would be at the center of the spiral. Giger eventually found information on a battle between Adamantine Arrow and Guardian mages vs members of the Doomsday Clock. At first he thought nothing of it, but then realized that there was no mention of the Mysterium being involved in the battle, so the mage who wrote this must have been looking backwards in time to see it. The Abyssal monster the Scelesti were using could not be destroyed so instead they trapped it, killed two of the four Scelesti, one killed himself by accident when his paradox sprung out of control, and one escaped. The author of these notes was Chrono, and Giger decided to investigate this Chrono more. Adam said he knew Chrono, he was an Acanthus studying a specific Fae that could tap into all magic from the past, present, and future, and was trying to find a way to do this himself.

            He, Eternal Elm, and Blood went to where his sanctum was in Beverly Hills, in an apartment complex. Giger had the other stay behind and checked the room out. No one seemed to be home so he broke in to find the living area filled with books. Looking over them, they were on the topic Adam had said Chrono had been interested in.

            Looking further he found what would have been a bedroom filled with more books, a dozen ticking clocks, a grimoire, and some Abyssal symbols written along the wall. As he opened the door he felt a spell go off and a portal open next to the grimoire to a study somewhere else, a study he recognized, the study he wasn't allowed in, his father's study.

            <I forgot to mention this earlier, but Giger was constantly casting Time unveiling and knowing magic to try and find information on The Clockmaster, but kept losing the clash of wills.>

            His Father, Chrono, The Clockmaster, stepped through the portal. He did not seem mad nor happy, more like he was resigned. Giger introduced himself and the two spent some time sizing each other up, his father told him that he assumed he was powerful, because he couldn't get through his defenses with his own Time magic, and Giger used Prime magic to discover his father was a second degree master of Time and Fate. Eventually of course, Giger revealed his true identity, allowing his spell to end and reverting to his seven year old self in front of his father. Chrono held “Teddy”, sobbing.

            Giger asked if he knew he and his mother would die, Chrono revealed he did, no matter what he did he couldn't seem to escape that path. Giger pushed, revealing he needed the grimoire, but also asking why he would do this in the first place if it would lead to Giger growing up without parents. “I killed her,” was his father's response. During his awakening, which happened right after Giger was born, he accidentally erased Giger's younger twin sister from existence. He had spent all his time since then trying to find a way to restore her, and had eventually turned to Abyssal magic.

            Giger told him that he would grow up without his mother and father because of this, to which his father replied that they were all living without his sister, but after a moment, agreed with Giger. He rose, cast a spell on the grimoire, and handed it to Giger. He would go back into his own past and not delve into the Abyssal magic, he would stay true to the Supernal so that Teddy would have his parents. Before he did though, Teddy, along with his parents, had a beautiful day in the park, playing catch, chasing each other, and otherwise having a day as a family.

            That evening, before dispelling the unmaking spell keeping him in the past, Giger went to see Leopold. He needed Leopold to take him to Paris to gather information on a rival he had in the present. He revealed to Leopold, who he knows is a sleepwalker, that he was from the future, to which Leopold responded, “I know.” Leopold, who had served his family Giger’s entire life, Leopold, who looked exactly the same in 1996 as he would in 2019. With unveiling magic, Giger realized that there was a hidden spell in Leopold keeping him from aging. When Giger inquired about it, he simply said “That's a conversation for twenty -three years from now.”

            Giger did make it to Paris and gathered some dirt in a future rival.

            Once back in the present Giger gained a second set of memories, memories of the new timeline where his parents lived, and his father was a hierophant and mentor to him. He now went as Chronos instead of Chrono, and it was a secret of their relationship to other mages of the Mysterium, both as mentor and student, and father and son. Gyges took the grimoire from Giger and told him he made an interesting choice, thanked him for his work, and left. Giger is now suspicious of Gyges, who is doing magic that is similar to what had gotten his father banished from the Golden Order.

            <Giger had spent XP and moved some merits around to purchase his father as a five dot merit, but until he takes infamous mentor, that relationship is a secret.>

                                Chapter 41
                                11/15/2024

                                <This is just going to be a summary, followed by my thoughts on the session and why I feel like it didn't go well in another post when I get home tonight. Eye of God saw himself in a prison made of brass, the God-Killer Sword seemed rusted over and covered in dry blood.                                     Alright, I wanna express some thoughts and frustrations I'm having with myself over Chapter 41, you don't have to read this, this is for me.
