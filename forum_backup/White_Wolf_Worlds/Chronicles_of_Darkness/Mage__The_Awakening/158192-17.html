                                        The Pit Baiters

                                        Origins:
                                        Parentage: Obrimos. Members are primarily Silver Ladder, Guardians of the Veil, and Free Council for very different reasons. Mysterium is surprisingly well-represented because they are one of the places back-up Daimonomicons were stored when continuity of the legacy could not be assured. The heavy casualties and lack of focus on specific Order have left questions as to the origins.

                                        Background: Most members had someone die or be seriously damaged by a spell going awry or an abyssal intruder. Their backgrounds often overlap with those of Banishers, a detail which these are well aware of.

                                        Appearance: Mostly trends follow the parent organizations that a lineage is most aligned with. Silver Ladder tend to be immaculately groomed and dressed. Guardians tend to match their surroundings. Free Council tend to be flashier or comfortable, while the Mysterium members tend to be comfortable or academic.
                                        All of them, however, move like they are important. They have missions and their missions are important.

                                        Doctrine:
                                        Prerequisites: Fate 2, Prime 1, and 6 dots in Occult and Academics (no fewer than 2 in either)

                                        Initiation: An individual member must seek out a significant example of a Paradox and mitigate it, returning to report their findings. The mission is fully monitored and the direct mentor is allowed to help. Initiates are sometimes shocked to discover that success or failure for initiation is in the learning from it. The report is the critical element.
                                        After discussing the results with the prospective student, the ranking members of the legacy discuss the findings with the student. If they are impressed, they take him to the safest and most well-guarded ritual space available to them and cast a powerful spell with the prospective student, to transform the prospective into an initiate.

                                        Organization: The Abyss Baiters see themselves as members of a high-risk, high reward society. They like to keep a close eye on members. This is both to prevent left-handed practicioners from cropping up, but also to back each other up when facing the incalculable horrors that they attract.
                                        Mentors are expected to introduce their various protegees to each other and their mentors and peers. It's common to host events to get to know each other.
                                        They try to foster a more filial than hostile arrangement, but mages are mages.

                                        Theory:
                                        They their art as tying up the Abyss in bands of supernally empowered fate, making it less likely to affect the world. After that, their goal is to repair the damaged section, removing the flaw permanently. As far as these see it, the Supernal Realm defines what is possible, while the Loom of Fate defines what is. The Abyss represents what isn't and cannot be. If appropriately handled, then, the forces of Are Not can be carefully handled by weaving carefully and ending the trouble so caused. Paradoxes are snarls in the weave of reality, which a dedicated weaver can go back and repair, but is better served by preventing initially... and most mages aren't such great and skilled weavers as to be able to make all the needed repairs.

                                        They figure that filling in the damaged weaves with proper ones can completely remove the gaps where threads are simply missing.


                                        1st: Weaving the Dark Thread
                                        Prerequisites: Initiation

                                        The mage learns the primary force of the legacy, by tracking the trails of the Abyss in the world. This effect is largely via the Lie's interaction with the manifestations of the Supernal. When using Mage Sight of any sort, Paradox and abyssal phenomena can be spotted and examined.

                                        Paradoxes that occur in the area, whether internalized or released, are visible to the mage.

                                        Abyssal things trigger peripheral Mage Sight.

                                        Lies are apparent as such to both Mind and Prime Active Mage Sights whenever active. If either must pierce supernatural concealment, it automatically scores successes equal to the mage’s Fate dots.


                                        2nd: Scattering Shadows
                                        Prerequisites: Fate 2

                                        The mage can fight the myriad minor distractions of the Lie. The thousand gnats that drain the will and draining obstacles can be dispelled. This only goes so far, but it's a nice respite. The mage activates Quantum Flux with Advanced Duration equal to Fate.

                                        (Quantum Flux, Fate 1: 2 Reach: casting, duration)

                                        Optional: Prime 2

                                        Fighting the darkness is easiest with illumination. Any lie is penetrated, of course, but this reveals information about the Lie, as well as informations about what is True.

                                        (Pierce Deception, Prime 1: 2 Reach: duration, insight)

                                        3rd: Cursing the Darkness
                                        Prerequisites: Fate 3
                                        The mage must study the target abyssal phenomenon or effect to determine the types of damage that it represents respecting the Supernal World. Through this careful scrutiny and probing, the mage comes to understand a means by which it can be contained and, ultimately, removed. The mage then ties the problem in carefully crafted bonds of fate to secure this ultimate outcome.

                                        The mage actually has little control over the specifics, but the Fate will make sure that the mage has a chance. Usually, a set of choices per dot of Fate appears, but this aspect is Withstood by Rank. If the effect is an active power, there is no Withstand, but a Clash of Wills is sometimes imposed. Against an Intrusion, the storyteller ought to pick a Withstand based on extent of the intrusion---Use the advanced Scale chart to set the Withstand.

                                        The stronger the mage's command of Fate, the simpler the requirements, the easier the path, or the sooner the result will appear.

                                        The Mage and Target both gain complementary Destinies. The key to this ability is that the mage learns a way to remove the target abyssal effect.

                                        Abyssally Tainted creatures always end up with a Bane imposed by their tainted power as a result. This converts the power into a vulnerability.

                                        Optional: Prime 3

                                        Warding an area against the Lie is a useful tactic. Tne mage can spend a scene preparing a location to attune it with the heavens and to drive back the Lie. The result is that any casting that can use an Environment Yantra of the area gains the 8-again property.

                                        Abyssal things are decidedly uncomfortable, making Sleepers avoid the area.
                                        When the mage learns to create a symbol of the supernal, he may work that into a tool yantra and apply this attainment to that, as well.

                                        Should the mage desire to affect Abyssal creatures or powers when effecting a Ban with Space, this attainment permits the effect.

                                        (As Above, So Below, Prime 2: 2 Reach: duration, 8-again)

                                        (Previous edit was essentially: (Exceptional Luck, Fate 2: 2 Reach: casting, sensory))

                                        Optional: Prime 3

                                        Warding an area against the Lie is a useful tactic. The mage can spend a scene preparing a location to attune it with the heavens and to drive back the Lie. The result is that any casting that can use an Environment Yantra of the area gains the 8-again property.

                                        Abyssal things are decidedly uncomfortable, making Sleepers avoid the area.
                                        When the mage learns to create a symbol of the supernal, he may work that into a tool yantra and apply this attainment to that, as well, while inside this space.

                                        (As Above, So Below, Prime 2: 2 Reach: duration, 8-again)

                                        4th: Chain the Abyss
                                        Prerequisites: Fate 4

                                        The mage learns to craft a device from pure silver or another specially sacred material befitting the mage's watchtower. (individual parts must be pure, but the device can include multiple materials) This device is part of a pattern that repulses the untruth of the Abyss by sympathy to the mage's own Path and Watchtower.

                                        The device can be anything that the mage can wield in two hands to something small enough to fit on the back of the mage's hand. Size matters very little, though the smallest it can be is a few inches in diameter.

                                        A mage may only retain one per level of Fate. The item may certainly be dedicated as a Path or Personal tool, but that's not required.

                                        Abyssal and abyssally touched creatures who contact the device are affected by Grave Misfortune with Potency equal to the mage's Fate. The effect is an area.

                                        (Since there are loads of details weakening the effect, the following seems fair)

                                        While wielding the device, a mage may turn Abyssal powers away. This allows a Clash of Wills against any abyssal powers affecting the area projected. (maybe add/replace with Withstand 2)

                                        (Grave Misfortune, Fate 3: 2 Reach: casting, duration)

                                        Optional: Prime 4
                                        By touching a Sleeper, the basic kernel of taint all sleepers bear can be pushed back and suppressed. This is not any sort of minor effect, either. It can last Indefinitely, given potent enough Prime.

                                        (Stealing Fire, Prime 3: Reach 2: duration, casting)

                                        5th: Naming the Nameless
                                        Prerequisites: Fate 5

                                        The mage can twist fate around an abyssal creature and direct its behaviors, within limits.

                                        (Divine Intervention, Fate 4: 2 Reach: casting, duration)

                                        Optional: Prime 5

                                        If the mage's device is placed on a weapon, that weapon can be used to slice through any layers of existence, from twilight and material on. To abyssal creatures, it counts as their Bane. (Note this is more specific: Abyssal creatures must be almost entirely impossible or a spirit with significant abyssal essence. Sleepers do not count unless extraordinarily infected.)

                                        (Ephemeral Enchantment: Prime 3: Reach 3: (2) Bane, duration)


                                        It is rumored the 6th Attainment could trigger Awakening or banish a powerful Acamoth back to the Abyss.


                                        I'm still not thrilled with the attainments on this, but I consider it improved from the 1e version I wrote up.
