    Just to chime in sense I still haven't left the hospital and thus can't touch a computer to read this/write a full response (plus I have been awake for 36+ hours and could be incoherent/unintentionally hostile):

    Mrmdubois summed up most of what my points were and covered them well.

    My point on Pathless was that there never seemed to be a Path preference in either recruitment or the view inherent to the Legacy, not that there was no Path of Origin. All Legacies are founded by mages, each mage has a Path, thus all Legacies originated from one Path or another. The Moros is as good as any for the original path of the Quiescent's founder, I just doubt based on their description that the founder ever limited to them or the Legacy philosophy/view was wholly dependent on the Moros Path.

    As for Orders, Mrmdubois hit most on the head. Nameless are mages not affiliated with or recognized as members of an Order, the Nameless Orders are associations of such groups. Groups that once were Nameless who joined with an Order lose the Nameless status. Association with multiple Orders just means that the Legacy found like minded individuals amongst more than one group. Just because a Legacy has an outside structure and hierarchy doesn't itself make them a separate Order, as long as the members actively work within and fulfill their roles within the Order(s); if having a heirachy or association beyond strict Order lines meant a Legacy became a separate Order then nearly all Legacies would technically fit the definition.

    The Tremere are Nameless because while they can come from or technicamy hold nominal affiliation with any of the Orders, they aren't themselves a part of them. As Mrmdubois pointed out, the recognize different goals, a separate heirarchy, and actively work to advanced the goals of the Tremere over their original/infiltrated Order, even if they still act along their Order's general goals. Remember, they are a group dedicated got he harvest of souls to undermine the entire basis of the Paths and Watchtowers by raising an alternative Tower (from possibly the Abyss) and destroying the originals; no Order would support this as it actively undermines the very basis of the known Supernal. And if discovered, the infiltrated Order would strip the Loch of their status and kill them. It is the same as if a Seer was to infiltrate a Pentacle Order, or vice versa. The Tremere aren't the model that describes other legacies; they are examples of how the Left Handed can operate as a cohesive organization and maintain themselves in the face of such overt opposition.

    My point was Quiescent don't hide their status; they (based on the text you quoted) simply don't place it above their Order, but alongside it. They might not be trusted, but they still work within their Orders goals and heirarchy, simply pursuing the use of subtle magic. Nothing about them expressly puts them outside the Orders except the general mistrust at that time for this new movement.

    Anyway... I need to get back to notes.
