                        I think the average level of the interior art in the 2E core is on balance higher than that of the 1E core but there are some serious rough patches and it is a real shame that the Order pieces are among them. I mean, they're not on the level of Silver Ladder iPod guy with rickets, but while they're mostly fine pieces on their own merits, I don't think many convey the vibe of their respective Orders very well at all. The Path art is by contrast much better communicated even if a couple of the illustrations are a bit rubbery in that Photoshoppy digital media way - the figures are clear and communicate something.

                        There are random bits of interior art throughout the book where I'm just like lol, like the guy who is SUPER STOKED to be talking to a rat, but some of it's fantastic and the chapter opening pieces are uniformly gorgeous. They communicate the vibe of the game perfectly, with flat planes of color broken up by formal, geometric compositions that call to mind hieroglyphs and stained glass and architectural drawings.
