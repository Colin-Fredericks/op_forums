                                                            In general, I like local and low-power games much more than global high power games. Thankfully, in general all I needed in order to make Mage little more like that was restricting 90 percent of mages in the world to Gnosis 3 or less, making Gnosis 4 rare and anything beyond that equivalent of Archmastery. I suggest that for anyone complaining about Mage power level, especially in comparison to other splats.

                                                            One thing that bothers me even at these levels of play is how powerful Shielding and Veiling spells are. But that's mostly because I find blanket immunities and blanket undetectibalities lame.Invisible zombie squads, invisible mages battling other invisible mages using invisible means, etc. So far my preferred fix for it was nerfing blanket can't be perceived to "will be casually overlooked unless Exceptional Success" and "suffers Potency in penalty to be actively detected"/"gains Potency in bonus to avoid detection" [player trying to sneak into a crowded building will gain bonus, player being sneaked upon will gain a penalty to their detection roll].
