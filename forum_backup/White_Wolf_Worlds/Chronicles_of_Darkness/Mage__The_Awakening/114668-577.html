Like my favourite spell Eidolon (Prime 4/5, Life 4) turn a person into a living mana pattern, does it still have the properties of a living being (needs to sleep and eat - regained willpower from sleeping) or does your imago make all Life functions completely redundant (no need to sleep, no regaining willpower, no need to eat, just gains mana from some oblation to refuel?).

                                                        The only examples of Matter Veiling we have published are Hidden Hoard and Machine Invisibility. Hidden Hoard works by removing the conceptual indexing tag that designates a target as an object. Anyway, the question then is if that's a Veiling effect, a Weaving effect, or a Patterning effect. Probably also depends on if you're borrowing the tag from an existing example or not...
