                                  Even a starting Mage can grab Sympathetic Connections though, you only need Space 2. You can use Correspondence to figure out the strongest connection that won't be missed as well, or the strongest connection period and use Borrow Thread's Reach option to completely copy the connection.

                                  Say you stumble on a murder scene and find a bloody knife, you can borrow the connection from the blood to its owner, and using the blood as material sympathy you can either locate the person who was killed, or scry on them. You don't need to know their identity, though not knowing does raise the Sympathetic Withstand level by one. You can probably pick up and copy the fading Sympathetic connections from the body as well (And if you can't then that's a clue that you're not dealing with a normal murder), and their murderer will probably be near the top of that list of connections. Borrow Threads again, create a symbolic yantra of the murderer where details can be somewhat fudged but throwing in as much accurate information as you have and you can then track that person down too and decide what to do about them.

                                  The real roadblock here is spell control, but all's that means is you may want to spend some dice and Reach on these temporary Sympathetic Connection spells for as long as you estimate you'll need them and then unsafely relinquish them with a point of WP.

                                  tl;dr Sympathetic Connections aren't as big of a hurdle as people seem to think they are.
