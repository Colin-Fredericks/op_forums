    Imperium Rites, Ascension and it's consequences

    01-16-2017, 08:26 AM
    Okay, so here is my question. Let's assume we have Aswadim (for example, Theumiel from Imperial Mysteries), who have accepted Supreme Earthly Omen - to destroy all Earth by flooding in the Abyss. The Pax Arcanum does not apply to Imperium, so technically Exarchs can't just punch him in the face outright without other Ascended Aswadim to retaliate (let's assume they care enough about our Aswadim for some reason). Our Aswadim prepares his Rites, create doomsday cults and prophecies, create his Anti-Messiah, Seals his Omens, then Exalts them and poof! He is Descended and Earth is destroyed.
    What happens next? As I understand, destruction of Earth is Supernal Truth on some level now - perfect and unassailable. Do Exarchs re-create world from the scratch? Can they do it without destroying Supernal Truth about destruction of Earth? Is changes brought by Ascension are immutable and perfect, or they are just as any other Supernal Truth - malleable by sufficiently strong power?

This is the kind of event that may well justify radical changes happening in the setting, such as say Quiescence, the Gauntlet, and all kinds of Masquerade collapsing and the CoD becoming an Unmasqued World.
