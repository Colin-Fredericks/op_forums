                        The first one allows Yantras, further rules to be refined. Considering making Dedication a requirement.

                        The second one acts as a shield. Whether in the form of additional Doors or a penalty to offensive magic.

                        The third one expands on Arcana immunity from evading damage by adding another option for obtaining it. Not decided if the enemy is aware of such.

                        The fourth forces the enemy to pay for their offers with their own Doors, if failed the Duelist must sacrifice a Door as usual.

                        The fifth is some kind of terrible offensive power. Considering using the rote quality for using an attribute rated above five dots.

                                                        There is also the concern of why anyone would Duel such a mage. Since the effects of the Attainments are very overt, they will become evident by the end of the first bout. The Duel Arcane is a matter that needs to be authorized by the Consilium, often happening as a public event and always recorded by the Lex. Therefore keeping a low profile is improbable.

                                                        The purpose of the Duel Arcane is to act as a final conflict resolution method, a way to prevent escalation when traditional mediation has failed. When people know that someone is specialized in the Duel, they may refuse to engage them. Since a mage who chose this Legacy is likely Duel happy, it means more escalation, which is a net loss for the Consilium. In which case, it would make sense to issue a law where the mage's opponent may demand they appoint a proxy in their stead and another that states that the mage themselves can only act as a proxy to another Duel if both parties agree. All this would lead to a very weird dynamic to how the character may approach it.

                                                        Lastly, I agree that basing the Attainments on more traditional spells might be better. Not only does this make calibrating for balance a lot simpler, but also forces you to contemplate the nuances of how to build your toolkit to better synergize for the Duel. How do they plan to get the time to setup such abilities? What is the general modus operandi of their approach, honorable, utilitarian, something else ? Right now its not clear.
