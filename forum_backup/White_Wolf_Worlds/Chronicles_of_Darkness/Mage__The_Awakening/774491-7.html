                        Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                        It occurs to me that it might be possible to show sleepers magic without getting hit with paradox or dissonance.

                        Ok, so out of sight you conjure some sort of object with magic. Maybe by making a bit of tass, doesn't really matter. Then you show the sleeper the object, and then cancel the spell. The object vanishes. Ta da!

                        Quiescence is different from paradox and dissonance.

                        Quiescence always causes a breaking point in normal humans (anyone without a supernatural or Sleepwalker merit) whenever they witness magic AND forces them to totally forget about it or rationalize the event as non-magical (no roll to resist). Dissonance is a related effect where the presence of sleepers breaks down spells over time.

                        The importance of Quiescence is that a mage can never really share his magic with friends or loved ones because it injures them on a soul deep level and they will not even remember.

                        Note that the Veil is not a choice by mages to protect them from humanity, but a means for mages to protect sleepers. Mages' alienation from humanity is set by an absolute rule, the Quiescence, and its elimination would indeed significantly change the setting. Creating some "exception" for the sole crossover reason of sleeper Hunters having an easier chance of hunting mages is contrary to one of the most important aspects of the mage setting. It's also completely unnecessary as not only can sleeper Hunters still hunt mages, albeit with a great deal of difficulty, virtually every Tier 3 Hunter is already immune to the Quiescence because they have an inherent supernatural ability and the Sleepwalker Merit is a measly one dot choice.

                        Originally posted by LokiRavenSpeak View Post

                        As for quiessence, problem isn't that bob the clerk cannot go toe to toe with a mage but that while fluff talks about hunter as a threat they arent.
                        The fluff of all the major splat books discuss humanity collectively as a threat to supernaturals, not individual humans.

                        There's a reason why Tier 3 Hunters exist, and why the monsters in the Hunter books are decidedly less dangerous and powerful than the other actual gameline splats, mages and otherwise.
