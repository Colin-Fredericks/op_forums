                                Starting a fire is clearly an application of Forces. To simplify very slightly, a fire is the transformation of chemical potential energy into thermal energy in the presence an oxidizing agent. So starting a fire seems to fit nicely under Forces 1 or Forces 2. A fire-starting spell is inherently transitory, and the fire is Lasting (you start a fire, it keeps burning, no more magic needed). But would thin be a Compelling (Forces 1) or a Ruling (Forces 2) spell? I can easily see either being a Praxis of Rote in a low-tech era because it's so incredibly useful not having to mess around with flint and steel and tinder, or worse, a firedrill or firebow.
