        Diyu is the underworld of Chinese mythology. It is characterized by being composed of different courts, which each preside over different hells that punish ghosts via resurrective torment. The number of hells and courts vary based on whether you are looking into the Taoist or Buddhist version, but they are consistent in being a highly organized system for governing the punishment and reincarnation of the dead (originally attributed to the Jade Emperor structuring it as another part of the Celestial Bureaucracy).

        An example is that, before reincarnation, souls needed to drink the Mengpo soup at the Bridge of Helplessness, offered by Grandma Meng. This would wipe their memories before reincarnation, which souds a lot like the effects of the Ocean Oroboros. The system itself sounds like a reinforcement of the cycle of torturous reincarnation the Psychopomp is fond of.

        So considering that many different realms of the dead exist in the Underworld, its plausible Diyu might as well. It contains a structured system for work (organizing and processing the dead within the system), a "legal system" for punishing crimes, provides for ghost a way of surviving (the hells could be Dominions and receiving a day's worth of punishment could grant Essence) and offers a road to reincarnation (which should be verified for misdirection). Because of this, I believe it stands as a possible Mystery and philosophical quandary to be examined by such a Legacy.

                                                    Legacy Attainments have a fixed ritual cast time of one scene (Mage core book page 198), which is unaffected by Gnosis.

                                                    Ceremonies take, at minimum, 15 minutes per dot (Geist core book page 125), meaning 5 dot Ceremonies will always go above this limit. With the highest bonus modifier, they would take one week per dot.

                                                    So a one minute cast time is not possible without a dedicated Attainment to reduce the time, which would require Time 5 and Death 3.
