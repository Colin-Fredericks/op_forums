                The Eye of Ahriman is a 12 dot found on page 208 of the Mysterium book. It replaces your real eye, but refuses to bond with anyone with less than Destiny 5. It grants several powers, including the ability to be under permanent Nightvision, use Scrying freely, permanent Death Mage Armor, the ability to summon shadows and the ability to make minions from shadows, both these end if exposed to sunlight. So if you ever played one of The Darkness games, it has those vibes.

                It was discovered in an Atlantean ruins and they only figured out how to use it when a Scelestus came for it with the sister Artifact. So Signs of Sorcery now clarifying it was a scepter and a ring, which were never detailed anywhere AFAIK, and adds to the confusion of where the eye falls in the set.

                    Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post

                    It is very definitely not clarifying that it was a scepter and a ring, because the Eye of Ahriman was an amulet and the sidebar wouldn't be talking about characters uniting those two Artifacts speculatively if that was definitely how it was discovered that the Eye of Ahriman was activated. For all we know the sister Artifact — and it was a sister Artifact, not the sister Artifact — could have just been a different Eye of Ahriman.

                    More probably, given the book this sidebar is in, it's using Ahriman as a mythological reference in the same way that Dahhak claims to be the son of the Adversary of Zoroastrianism as an example of the sort of being to whom Artifacts of the Profane Forms on the same page might belong — the Dethroned Queen's regalia from Reign of the Exarchs are definitely known, but possessions of the Enemy of Truth with powers of the types accorded to Rings and Scepters paint a certain sort of picture of what an ambitious Seer might expect to get a set bonus from.

                                                    Originally posted by lbeaumanior View Post
                                                    What would count to the bolded part? Would vampire Dominate / Majesty count? Theban Sorcery? Cruac? Any example of death-related powers from the other gamelines (Vampire, Werewolf, Changeling, etc)
                                                    Per the PGCC, Disciplines are defended against using the appropriate Arcana for their effects, which remains a viable means of defending against Haunts as well. It's the same deal as Ephemeral Shield working against Gifts and Rites — unless the root of the power comes from a specific Arcana (even if it manipulates other phenomena), Ghost Shield is only going to defend against powers that leverage ghosts, shadows, ectoplasm, the energies of death, and so on in their outcomes.
