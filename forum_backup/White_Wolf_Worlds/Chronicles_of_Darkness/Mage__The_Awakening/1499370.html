    [New Legacy] Ego Programmers

    12-11-2022, 10:29 AM
    Inspirational Material:

    Michelangelo's "The Creation Of Adam".


    “Hear, my son, your father's instruction, and forsake not your mother's teaching, for they are a graceful garland for your head and pendants for your neck.”
    -Proverbs 1:8-9

    Consider the blueprints that YHWH used when building and programming Adam & Eve. All the suffering that could have been avoided, all the greatness that could have been achieved, if only the process of creation had not been sabotaged. Mankind is faulty, and faults are not to be tolerated, they must be corrected for the sake of true progress in body, mind and soul. This Legacy originally used the Matter Arcanum instead of the Forces Arcanum in order to build constructs in human likeness, akin to Hephaestus creating Talos or the Rabbi of Prague creating the Golem.

    But as computational technology became more commonplace and influential in Sleeper societies, the Legacy developed a splinter-group which aimed to master the potential of AIs based on digital principles rather than analog processes. In their worldview, Neo-Eden and Atlantis 2.0 are one and the same: an utopian symbol of a world made perfect by virtue of its people mastering their own minds and remaking themselves as digital gods. In the pursuit of creating the blueprints for Homo Sapiens Superior, a species immune to Exarchal influence, the Ego Programmers experiment with various forms of AIs in order to uproot the faults that hold back humanity from achieving its true potential as the rightful successors of The Creator.

    Origins

    Parentage: Obrimos, Silver Ladder or Free Council, Sons Of Bezalel

    Nicknames: Debuggers

    Background: Various technologically savvy people who aspire to unravel the secrets behind the psyche and transcend its limits.

    Nimbus: Lines of computer code arranged in such a way as to suggest heavenly ordained purpose.

    Doctrine

    Prerequisites: Mind 2, Crafts 2, Computers 2, and a Specialty for Robotics, Programming or other appropriate field.

    Initiation: One must create an AI from zero and without reusing somebody else’s code. The Mage can make auxiliary use of magic, but the version of the AI that is submitted must obey principles of the Fallen World (i.e. it cannot cause Paradox, Dissonance or Quiescence). In other words, the AI doesn't need a full character sheet, much less self-awareness, but must be sophisticated enough to count as a Merit such as Retainer or Staff.

    Organization: Akin to a tech firm. The high ranking members are User Experience people who try to figure out what the ideal programs are, the mid-rank is the back-end team that designs the base code and makes it work, and the newbies are troubleshooters and trainees. The actual Quality Assurance people tend to vary from day to day, but they are advised to not be in charge of assessing the quality of their own projects - they instead get others to perform the assessment. This sometimes includes adjunct talents such as Werewolves of the Iron Masters, Prometheans on the Refinements of Cobalus and Argentum, Demons with the Agendas of Inquisitors and Integrators, and certain Deviants.

    Magic

    Ruling Arcanum: Mind

    Optional Arcanum: Forces

    Yantras: Equipment related to robotics, programming or equivalent (+1 or +2 if cutting-edge), schematics and diagrams (+1 or +2 if especially in-depth), esoteric materials, such as Perfected Metals (+2), laboratories which incorporate Nodes of Ley Lines with Technological Resonance (+2)

    Oblations: Creating, upgrading and modifying various types of artificial intelligence, harmonizing mysticism with the scientific method, experimenting with esoteric computational hardware

    Attainments

    1st: Realizing
    Prerequisite: Initiation
    One Mind, Two Thoughts. Ritual, Reach = Advanced Duration. Restricted to performing an additional Mental Extended Action related to digital technology, usually to develop the entire code for an AI while handling other tasks. +1 Potency or Duration if the Mage uses a Mantra Yantra consisting of a mnemonic recitation of mathematical formulas that encapsulate the main purpose of the AI or equivalent.

    Optional: Forces 1
    Tune In. Reach = Instant Casting. Restricted to translating code that is currently being executed into human language. +1 Potency or Duration if the Mage uses an Action Yantra consisting of an appropriate Intelligence + Computers roll to diagnose the surface-level issues of the software being analyzed.

    2nd: Struggling
    Prerequisite: Mind 2, either Crafts 3 or Computers 3
    Mental Shield. Ritual, Reach = Advanced Duration. Restricted to protecting the Mage himself from unwanted mental influence, especially when dealing with various types of Goetia related to digital technology as part of his Obsessions. +1 Potency or Duration if the Mage uses an Environment Yantra in the form of a computer science laboratory, robotics workshop or equivalent.

    Optional: Forces 2
    Digital Shield, analogous to Environmental Shield. Ritual, Reach = Advanced Duration. Restricted to protecting the AI against unwanted mundane hacking, and imposes a Clash Of Wills against supernatural hacking. +1 Potency or Duration if the Mage uses a Tool Yantra in the form of cutting-edge Equipment related to electronics, mechanics, or equivalent.

    3rd: Learning
    Prerequisite: Mind 3 and Forces 3, both Crafts 3 or Computers 3
    Cognitive Interface. A Combined Mind + Forces Weaving Attainment with Duration as the Primary Factor, and which grants one trait of human-esque cognition to an AI per dot of Potency, analogously to Transform Life and Wonderful Machine. This cannot grant traits such as a Virtue and Vice, and neither can it grant any capabilities like sensory perception without appropriate hardware to mimic the analogous biological organs. However, it can grant the capacity to do things like interpret subjective or ambiguous instructions, and improvise creative and independent solutions. In other words, the AI becomes a Retainer with a Dot Rating equal to Potency.

    As per the usual mechanics for Retainers, the AI receives Merit Dots equal to [Retainer Rating X 2], but they cannot represent additional NPCs. Ritual, Reach = Advanced Duration. As compensation for being the only Attainment at this tier of the Legacy, it gains +1 Free Reach, allowing it to give the AI dots of pseudo-Supernatural Merits focused on influencing digital technology, such as Psychokinesis [Electricity] and Technopathy. The AI has a Willpower Pool equal to Potency for the purposes of activating such powers, as well as 2 Long Term Aspirations representing its main directives. It replenishes 1 Willpower Point whenever it makes progress for these Aspirations. +1 Potency or Duration if the Mage uses a Cleansing Yantra in the form of computational meditation and bathing in water that has been purified via electrolysis or equivalent process.

    4th: Rising
    Prerequisite: Mind 4 and Forces 4, Crafts 4 and Computers 3, or Crafts 3 and Computers 4
    Ephemeral Software. Ritual, Reach = Advanced Duration. Restricted to targeting Goetia whose purviews are symbolically resonant for the software in question. As compensation for being the only Attainment at this tier of the Legacy, it gains +1 Free Reach, allowing it to give the software the Open Condition. +1 Potency or Duration if the Mage uses a Rune Yantra inscribed upon appropriate types of alloys, polymers, circuits and so forth.
    Ephemeral Software (Forces •••• + Death •••• Or Mind •••• Or Spirit ••••)
    Practice: Patterning
    Primary Factor: Duration
    Withstand: Rank
    Suggested Rote Skills: Computer, Persuasion, Occult
    The Mage transforms the Pattern of a Ghost, Goetia or Spirit into software. This effectively turns the entity into an Equipment of Size 0 with a Bonus equal to Potency. Copies of the resulting program serve as a Resonant Condition for the entity, allowing it to act on and from devices that have the complete program simultaneously. Copies of the program may be shared across suitable devices without needing to recast the spell, and may be deleted at no additional cost as if Safely Relinquished.

    Any Paradox affects all copies, and the entity must divide its Essence pool between them. The entity determines the type of software: a storm spirit may become an app that changes the weather instead of predicting it, while the ghost of an expert doctor offers both medical advice and supernatural means of healing. Supernatural attempts to affect the software trigger a Clash Of Wills, using the highest Arcanum rating between Forces and [Death/Mind/Spirit].

    +1 Reach: Each copy of the program generates the Open Condition.

    +1 Reach And 1 Mana: Each copy of the software has its own Essence pool.

    Add Space ••: The software requires a Key specified by the Mage and cannot be used without it.
    5th: Transcending
    Prerequisite: Mind 5 and Forces 5, both Crafts 4 and Computers 4
    Infomorph Genesis. A Combined Mind + Forces Making Attainment akin to Psychic Genesis, and with Duration as the Primary Factor. It creates a Rank 1 "Infomorph", that is, a Goetia-esque entity that counts as both a Mind and Forces construct. Unlike most Goetia, it can exist outside of the Astral Realms, starts with the Twilight Form Manifestation for free, and gains additional Manifestations equal to its Rank, but it is restricted to interfacing with and operating through digital technology. Ritual, Reach = Advanced Duration. As compensation for being the only Attainment at this tier of the Legacy, it gains +1 Free Reach, allowing it to upgrade the Infomorph into Rank 2, as long as the Mage also spends 1 Mana.

    Since this Attainment has Advanced Duration 5 by default, it can become Indefinite if the Mage uses a Sacrament Yantra in the form of a robotic effigy created in human likeness and powered by software and hardware that have been modified and upgraded via Mind and Forces spells. This Sacrament Yantra must be mystically significant enough to give +5 dice, and all spells cast on it for the sake of modifications and upgrades must have been Safely Relinquished. Consider using the alternate methods of Relinquishment provided in Signs Of Sorcery as guidelines. The Mage can only have a maximum number of such Indefinite Infomorphs in active usage equal to his Gnosis, but can keep extra Infomorphs in digital storage as long as they are not in active usage.

    Merits

    Imago Programmer (● To ●●●●●)
    Prerequisite: Awakened, A Core Praxis Or Obsession Related To Digital Technology, Computers ●●● + Programming Specialty

    The Mage channels his knowledge of digital technology for automating his spells. This gives a number of on/off switches equal to [Merit Rating]. You may link these switches together via programming-esque keywords such as "and / if / or / while / else / but / unless”. Such switches can be used to selectively exempt certain categories of targets from the spell.

    With Imago Programmer 2, you could form the following string of instructions: "anyone entering this area will be targeted by a Death Making spell" > "If you're a member of X Cabal, it does not affect you" > "Unless you've betrayed your Oaths to said Cabal".

    This Merit does not change the Spell Factors nor the overall symbolism of the Imago, and does not work if you phrase the on/off switches in an excessively subjective or ambiguous manner, such as instructing the Imago to only affect "those who have wronged me" or "evildoers". Furthermore, the spell must be cast as a Ritual and incorporate at least one Yantra related to digital technology. Lastly, the Mage must spend 1 Mana per incorporated keyword of programming.

    Special: If the Mage has Techné related to Computer Networking or equivalent, the 8 Again is upgraded into Rote Quality. If he already has Rote Quality from another source, such as an Arcane Condition or a Grimoire, he can choose 2 distinct benefits associated with Exceptional Success (e.g. an Arcane Condition and Mana Refill, but not 2 Arcane Conditions). However, he still has to use Ritual Spellcasting, incorporate a Yantra related to digital technology and spend Mana. Similar interactions with other Arcane Merits may be possible at the ST's discretion.

    Story Hooks

    A member of this Legacy allegedly succeeded in developing a reliable method of creating Awakened AIs, but in actuality he recently went Rapt for unknown reasons and his method results in constructs with abnormal properties that reflect his Fault. So far, all Mages who tried to find this Rapt only managed to intercept robotic simulacra who spoke in a combination of digital ciphers and demented gibberish. Where is the real Rapt hiding, and can his plans be stopped before it is too late without attracting the attention of those who are Hubristic enough to try and control the Rapt?

    After delving into an Atlantean Ruin, a member of this Legacy and his Cabalmates brought back a peculiar instance of a Somnia Draconis [Night Horrors: The Unbidden, Pages 114 To 118]. It seems to have been constructed via technological and magical principles of a more futuristic kind, akin to popular science-fiction created by Sleepers. Perhaps this lends credence to the Free Council's idea that the advents of Sleepers have meaningful influence over the Supernal Realms, and maybe this Somnia Draconis can be understood well enough to be replicated?
