                                    Originally posted by nofather View Post
                                    2ptTakrill didn't limit it to 'a simple handshake.' Handshakes have a lot of symbolism and a lot of meaning, to the point that there are secret handshakes and secret signals sent by handshakes. It's entirely within the bounds of the game for a handshake to be a Mudra, for instance with a Socialize, Persuasion, or even Occult or Politics Skill.

                                    A mundra is only a Yantra as part of a specific Rote. If you have a Rote of a spell, with a skill specialization in something like Socialize or Persuasion, a handshake can certainly be an expression of the rote skill used to receive the Mundra bonus as part of the Rote.

                                    However, a handshake, no matter how semiotically connected to a spell, would not provide a Yantra by itself without additional merits or a legacy connection.

                                    Only the delineated acts provide Yantra bonuses (e.g., concentration, High Speech, Rote Mundras, runes, etc.).
