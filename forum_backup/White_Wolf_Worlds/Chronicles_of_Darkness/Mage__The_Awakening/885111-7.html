                                        Passionate Sorcery (••)
                                        Prerequisites: Mage
                                        Effects: You can draw upon your emotions to fuel your magic. You gain a +1 Yantra bonus as long as you are under the effects of an appropriate Strong [Emotion] Condition - this increases to a +2 if you are currently under the effects of Obsessive [Emotion] for the same emotion. If casting a spell would let you take a Beat from either Condition, you may take an Arcane Beat instead.
                                        Drawbacks: Letting your emotions rule you this way is... unwise. If a spell benefiting from the Yantra bonus granted by this Merit would be a Wisdom Sin, you take a penalty on the roll to prevent degeneration equal to the Yantra bonus.

                                        Edit:

                                        Awakened Feng Shui (Style; • - •••)
                                        Prerequisites: Hallow •+
                                        Lay the Arrangement (•): You may spend a day carefully aligning and arranging your Hallow, giving it an Open Condition themed after the Resonance of one of the contributing Ley-Lines. As long as this Open Condition remains, that Hallow can hold additional Tass equal to twice your dots in Awakened Feng Shui. If the Open Condition is removed, any Tass past the normal limit immediately spoils.
                                        Resonant Harvest (••): As long as the Open Condition remains on your Hallow, any Tass you take from it is concentrated. Whenever you spend the Tass, any Persona Yantra you get from it increases to +2.
                                        Resonant Redoubt (•••): As long as the Hallow has the Open Condition, you may reduce the amount of Tass the Hallow can store by 3 to increase your Gnosis by 1 for the purposes of Spell Control - you may do so a number of times equal to your dots in Awakened Feng Shui. These additional Spell Control slots can only be used to hold spells that match the Hallow's Resonance.
                                        Drawback: If the Open condition is removed from your Hallow, any spells supported by the Hallow are immediately relinquished as if you had spent a point of Willpower.

                                        =---=

                                        Supernal Truespeech (Style; • - •••)
                                        Prerequisites: High Speech, Prime •+
                                        The World Listens (•): As long as you are speaking in the glossolalia of High Speech, you cannot be silenced - you could clearly and audibly enunciate the words in a gale-force wind or vacuum, and can intelligibly speak with a slit throat or crushed voice-box. You also gain a bonus on any Social roll made while speaking High Speech equal to half your dots in this merit, rounded up.
                                        Speaking the Higher Name (••): You may combine the Mantra Yantra with the use of someone's Sympathetic Name as a Yantra with devastating results. While doing so still counts as using two Yantras, you may add your Yantra bonus from High Speech directly to the spell's Primary Factor instead of adding it to your dice pool.
                                        Supernal Filibuster (•••): You may combine the Mantra Yantra with the Concentration Yantra, babbling platonic truths while you maintain your spells. As long as you continue to speak while concentrating on the spell, ignore any Paradox the spell caused - it is delayed until you finish speaking, at which point it applies immediately.
                                        Drawback: Delaying the Abyss in this manner is dangerous - increase the Paradox dice pool as if you had Reached past your safe limit.

                                        =---=

                                        Nightmares (• - •••••)
                                        Prerequisite: Banisher, Wits •••, Composure •••
                                        Benefits: Unlike most Mages, your dreams are a surreal nightmare realm of pure, unrelenting terror. You may enter your own dreams without a meditation roll whenever you fall asleep. In addition, your dots in the Nightmares merit count as dots in the Safe Place merit for the purposes of your dreams - use Occult instead of Crafts when determining whether or not you can leave traps, which can be bypassed with a Finesse + Mind roll, penalized by your dots in Nightmares.
                                        Drawbacks: You cannot avoid any damage caused by any traps you leave in your own dreams. What's worse, you suffer a level of Lethal damage whenever you enter your own dreams, and do not regain any Willpower on nights where you do so.

                                        =---=

                                        Ideosyncratic Arcana (•• or ••••)
                                        Prerequisites: Gnosis 3+
                                        Benefits: You have stumbled upon a fragment of Supernal knowledge unknown to the majority of the Wise. Upon taking this merit, define a new Arcana - pick a "truth" and a number of words equal to twice your dots in this merit to form that Arcana's purview, and work with your ST to determine the precise effects it has with regards to Mage Sight and its Attainments. If you took the two-dot version of this Merit, treat the Ideosyncratic Arcana as if it were an Inferior Arcana - for four dots, you can treat it as a Common Arcana.
                                        Drawbacks: This bizarre partial-arcana is fragile and feral - each dot you learn is a Sin against Falling Wisdom, and spells that belong to the Arcana treat any Dissonance roll that scores three or more Successes as an Exceptional Success.
                                        Special: If you do not already have a Legacy and have 4 dots in this merit, you may found a Legacy that uses your Ideosyncratic Arcana as its third Ruling Arcana.

                                        (Yeah, this merit is for pretty "special" games. You really want to ask your ST permission before taking this one.)

                                        =---=

                                        Wise Sleepwalker (••)
                                        Prerequisites: Proximi
                                        Effects: Your mindset is closer to a Mage's than you'd think - you lose the Integrity advantage. Instead, you gain Wisdom at a rating equal to your previous Integrity score, as well as a single Obsession. You may spend Arcane Experience to purchase Blessings.

                                        Edit of the Edit: I rebalanced Supernal Truespeech a little.
