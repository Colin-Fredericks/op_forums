                                Originally posted by Solar View Post

                                I saw it, I thought it was quite good

                                That said I'm curious as to why you think a GM wouldn't allow it. A CoW is caused by two supernatural effects colliding and Mages know their spells are being clashed. The purview is clearly that of Prime (potentially Mind as well, because throught at knowledge) because it's magic and also because it relates to controlling Truth, which Prime covers. It's clearly a veiling effect, too.

                                So put the two together and you get a perfectly legitimate Prime Veiling spell.
                                I think that might be "the mages know, and are absolute detectives" part of setting. *shrug* but maybe it isn't so controversial to change as I thought.

                                Originally posted by WHW View Post
                                Funny thing, if active spells were more common and pinging PMS in presence of other mages, hiding such thing in a plain sight would be much easier.

                                This is actually a question about common sense and what customs would emerge from world working as it works (from mechanics). So. Let's talk customs.

                                PMS is tingling for every non veiling or veiled spell that is in vicinity. Is it irritating? What is vicinity? Does it go off for your spells?
                                Every ping is a potential attack.
                                Mage is always aware of CoW. He is not aware if he won, which would be weird but lets say.
                                Sanctum is a place guarded by golden laws. Sacred and safe.Every



                                1. If Peripheral is irritating it means that all magic in Sanctum should be veiled for cabalmates to not get crazy.
                                2. If it's not irritating it could mean that after a while it becomes something like fridge buzz or a clock ticking, something that you are only aware when thinking about.
                                a. you can't detect any of the other magic
                                b. you can distinguish between familiar buzz and something new
                                Host - Guest situation
                                1. First option leaves us with quiet Sanctums which shouldn't be disturbed by a lot of spells. You should drop them when entering. I don't think that this option is possible. There are spells that can't be dropped, augmentations, temporarily repaired disabilities and gender issues. It must mean that Peripheral is not irritating, otherwise mages would hate other mages.

                                2.a All spells should be veiled - it's an equivalent of setting the alarm in case of burglary. You can't detect supernatural attack if it hums constantly. When you are a guest you must drop all active effects and ask for permission for every spell you cast inside to make sure that the host is aware whats happening.

                                2.b Spells in sanctum don't have to be veiled but still you should ask for permission to cast a spell in somebodies house. Kind of like with smoking. Still every ping can be a potential attack on privacy or well being. Generally don't do it, it's uncomfortable.


                                The common thread is that you shouldn't cast without asking first. It's rude. Leave your wizard staff at threshold to not disturb the sanctum. No mage sight without asking first or being told to.

                                Q: What is a range of PMS?
                                "an Aimed spell’s short range is Gnosis x 10, medium is Gnosis x 20, and long is Gnosis x 40." Maybe this could help. If we go with medium it is still encompassing whole sanctum. No hiding.

                                Can it go through walls? I don't know. Help me with this.
