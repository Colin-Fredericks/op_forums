    Originally posted by quelsec View Post

    See im asking about things that in my opinon could be problematic. I know more or less whats writen ruleswise generaly in the book. Im asking about specific instances and how would they work in practice. In this example i found it strange that you could potentialy get a magic teacher with a spell. Also was unsure how the limited use thing would work in this instance. Exploits are a thing and the developers did not think about everything.
    One way to look at it is that the spell brings you a mentor quickly and conveniently for a few favors. After it expires, its up to you. Cast it again and you get the most likely one, which might not be the same person or have a similar dynamic.

    If you clicked with that mentor, you can cut a deal or do something personally significant to gain a temporary Mentor merit, proportional to how meaningful your actions were. It has the caveat of requiring more maintenance than the normal merit and having no Sanctity of Merits. Essentially, the player is paying in active attentiveness to the mentor rather than XP.

    So yeah, hopping from one teacher to the next by destined mentorship is just as viable as finding the mentor and then choosing to invest in the bond (via more involved roleplay or XP)

            In the end it all boils down to consistency, maintenance and active effort.

            This is not really a Mage thing. Any character can leverage circumstances and traits to get this kind of effect. A blue book detective can cheat in a gamble or steal money, seek hints of someone who can teach a skill or weirder ability and so on.

            Without a Merit all those things still work, but it requires focus in game and can come with all kinds of problems and unpredictable results. A supernatural power amplifies the good and the bad, but otherwise the dynamic is the same, and mundane methods can be pretty effective.

            Merits give you convenience. Not only that it is always there without hassle, but also that you know exactly what to expect out of it, which is crucial to be able to take maximum benefit.
