                          As the OP posted, there are two types of music. a)The one you play through the session for mood and b) the one you take inspiration form. Also, it depends on the setting and the feeling you want to evoce on your Chronicle. Also, different people regard each song differently. One might relate to a song due to its music, its title, part of lyrics or an emotion/memory he tied the song too. Anyway, here is my list.
