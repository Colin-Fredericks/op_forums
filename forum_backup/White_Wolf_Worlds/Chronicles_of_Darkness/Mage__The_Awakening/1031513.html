    [2e Fan] Pygmalian Society

    01-14-2017, 05:49 AM
    Obviously the Attainments are not finished yet but I thought I would get others opinions as I work on this project.

    Pygmalian Society
    The Sculptors affect change in the world around them and seek to awaken all the sleepers of the world. (for detail see Legacies of the Sublime, page 63).

    History: (for detail see Legacies of the Sublime, page 65).

    Society and Culture
    The first principles of the Pygmalian Society grew out of Western occult traditions. Although most Pygmalian’s don’t dress up their magic in the formal principles of Rosicrucian practice any more, (for detail see Legacies of the Sublime, page 66).

    Origins
    Parent Path or Order: Acanthus, Free Council (for detail see Legacies of the Sublime, page 64).

    Nickname: Sculptors

    Background: (for detail see Legacies of the Sublime, page 65).

    Appearance: (for detail see Legacies of the Sublime, page 64).

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Mind 2, Expression 2 and one of the following at 2 or more Empathy, Occult, Persuasion.

    Initiation: Varies and informal (for detail see Legacies of the Sublime, page 65).

    Organization: No formal Organization (for detail see Legacies of the Sublime, page 65).

    Theory: Art, in all forms, carries with it the emotions and the energies of its creator. This intense emotion can be overwhelming at times and can be used to control those that witness the art being performed.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Mind

    Yantras: Instrument/tool of your Art (Dedicated Magic Tool and a +1 Legacy Tool); Perform your Art in a place significant to your Art (+1 Environmental); Materials of your Art, i.e. Sheet Music, canvas, etc. (+1 Persona); Mudra (+Performance)

    Oblations: Meditate in a place significant to your Art; View another’s work relevant to your Art; Create a piece of work for someone else, showing it to them, and then destroying it; Spontaneous performance of your Art; Audience at the end of a scene where you performed/displayed your Art.

    Attainments
    The attainments of the Pygmalian Society deal mainly with the manipulation of emotions, thoughts and memories. The attainments are never used instantly and take a scene to enact (with the exception of some of the optional Time effects). (for detail see Legacies of the Sublime, page 69).

    First Attainment: Read the Audience (Initiate)
    Prerequisites: Initiation (Gnosis 2, Mind 2 (Primary), Fate 1 (Conjunctional), Expression 2)
    The Initiate learns to scan his audience for emotions and this is used mainly to enhance that emotion. It is easier to amplify something that is already there then to create it from scratch. This attainment enables the initiate the use of the Mental Scan spell but only for reading the emotions of the subject. This takes a scene to cast and uses Advanced Scale.
    Conjunctional: If the initiate also has fate he can use it in conjunction with the read emotions and see Interconnections of his subject. This also takes a scene to cast and uses the Advanced Scale.
    Optional Arcanum: Time 1
    Alternatively the initiate with the time Arcanum can enact Perfect Timing as the spell. This attainment is cast instantly.

    Second Attainment: World in a Grain of Sand (Apprentice)
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Mind 2, Expression 2
    Emotional Urging.
    Optional Arcanum: Time 2
    Choose the Thread

    Third Attainment: Arrows of Desire (Disciple)
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 4, Mind 3
    Memory Hole
    Optional Arcanum: Time 3
    Shifting Sands

    Fourth Attainment: Eternity in an Hour (Adept)
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 6, Mind 4
    Read the Depths
    Optional Arcanum: Time 4
    Prophecy

    Fifth Attainment: Connections (Master)
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 8, Mind 5
    Social Networking
    Optional Arcanum: Time 5
    Temporal Pocket

          Basically as I said the attainments deal with the manipulation of emotions, thoughts and memories. I tried to stay within that theme. I took the attainments from the Pygmalian Society from the Legacies of the Sublime and placed them as best I could, not knowing the spells that they mentioned I tried to match up descriptions. I took the old Attainment level 1 and placed it at Attainment level 2 (with the exception of leaving the conjunctional effect at level 1), I took the old Attainment level 2 and placed it at Attainment level 3 and took the old Attainment level 3 and placed it at Attainment level 4, then tried to come up with Attainment level 1 and Attainment level 5.

          The thought behind Attainment level was to get to know their audience to better manipulate them and that is why the Innterconnection was put at Attainment level 1, The thought behind Attainment level 5 was that by that time they are almost irresistible and that will open social doors that they didn't have until now.

              Bit of Left-Hand Path trivia twisting your legacy attainments is one way to go Mad. Since this is an attainment set up that doesn't follow 2e guidelines I'd assume it's a twisting of your attainment and thus would drive the Mage using it Mad.

              Why isn't it legal? Because, you have the one spell effect of Mental Scan, and you have attached the second spell effect of Interconnections to it, clearly this isn't a conjunctional effect but two spell effects combined. It could work if Fate was the Optional arcana, but it's not, so it breaks with 2e's attainment guidelines. Since it's basically a combined spell turned into an attainment and you can't cast combined spells until Gnosis 3 this combined effect shouldn't be possible until Gnosis 3.

              This is one case of following the 1e legacy guidelines too accurately not being a great idea.

              Why do you have Temporal Pocket in there?
