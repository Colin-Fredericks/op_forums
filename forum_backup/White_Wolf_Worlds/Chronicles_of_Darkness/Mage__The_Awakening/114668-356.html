                                                    Originally posted by Shadowjim12 View Post
                                                    A Life-Matter Adept travels with his Matter (Life-ignorant) Disciple.
                                                    The Adept transforms an unlucky pigeon into a stone pigeon statue (Life 4, Matter 4).
                                                    Can the Disciple alter the now transformed pigeon with Matter? If the Disciple transmutes the transformed pigeon from stone to metal, can he make his spell last longer than his master's, keeping the pigeon into it's metal form or will the pigeon revert back to its animal form with an active Matter spell to reanimate it into metal if/when it becomes again stone?
                                                    The Life/Matter spell turns the subject into Matter, it no longer falls under the purview of Life and falls under Matter as long as the spell holds.

                                                    The subsequent Matter spell can thus affect it, as it is now Matter.

                                                    Should the first spell cease, the first effect will be undone, and the pigeon returns to life.
                                                    Without a valid subject to affect, whatever inherent effect the Matter spell achieved is now undone.

                                                    This of course, may leave you with rather a mess of pigeon if the subsequent Matter spell crushed the pigeon statute to rubble through its effect, but perhaps not if the very spell was to change the shape of the pigeon statute into something else. It depends on whether the change was as a result of, or part of the Matter spell's effect.
