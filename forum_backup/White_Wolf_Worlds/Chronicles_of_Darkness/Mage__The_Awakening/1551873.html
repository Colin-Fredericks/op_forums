    The Groundskeepers of Amma Su, a semi-satirical Nasnasi Legacy

    06-26-2025, 01:21 PM
    To preface the atrocity that is this legacy, due to schedueling conflicts there have been many occasions during which i've had to cancel my mage the awakening sessions when someone couldn't attend, however the rest of us still wanted to play so i suggested starting a low stakes chronicle where the players play scelesti. One of my players rolls up "Crazy Jane" a genderbent variant of Crazy Dave from the Plants VS Zombies series who has since become the groups favourite character.

    in addition to the legacy i have added a list of rule changes i made to the Nasnasi ruleset in order to make them more enjoyable for player characters.

    Crazy Jane started play as a master of Life so i have written the factors of the attainments with this in mind, figuring out the power of attainments for greener (get it?) mages will require some math.

    the book is very unclear on how befouled attainments works but the "hands of destiny" states that the Nasnasi can spend stored paradox to increase factors so i've just assumed this is how all befouled attainments are supposed to work.

    The Groundskeepers of Amma Su


    Origins: The lunatic Nasnasi “Crazy Jane” Incidentally created this legacy by obsessively pursuing her fascination with pitting mutant plants against reanimated corpses, the legacies powers allow her to enhance and command her abominations.

    Parentage: Thyrsus, Nasnasi

    Doctrine:

    Prerequisites: Life 2, Joining 2 Crafts 2, Animal Ken 2, Brawl or Weaponry 2, gardening specialty in crafts and any specialty in Weaponry or Brawl

    Initiation: Kill 5 people who are then turned into zombies, then grow 5 plants from seed to fruition which are then turned into mutant monsters, then pit both sides against each other and eat the victors.

    Organization: none, crazy jane is the only member of this legacy and frankly probably always will be.

    Theory: Plants are primordial beings who are dominant over the fleeting human race and can demonstrate this through enacting warfare, a nonsensical idea that, nevertheless, has captured Jane's imagination since she first awakened. But on her quest to her Dur-Abzu she embraced the lie that humans are dominant over nature and its inhabitants, that plants are to be confined to greenhouses and garden beds for human amusement. Crazy Jane is too stupid to consciously recognise this contradiction, but her abyss twisted Gnosis channels it into paradoxical power.


    Magic:
    Ruling Arcanum: Life

    Yantras: Casting within a garden or morgue (+1 or +2 if filled with the mages creations or victims) Gardening implements (+1) Cutting up a plant or Human body before casting the spell (+1 or +2 if still alive) drinking broth made from the remains of plants and zombies that fought each other (+3)

    Oblations: Composting human corpses, planting seeds into soil that will never support life, walking a battlefield collecting souvenirs, Researching weapons of mass destruction.

    First: Whispers from the blood soaked soil

    Prerequisites: initiation

    The gardener learns to whisper to their horrid creations and becomes adept at wrangling their behaviour. This attainment is activated with an instant action and for 10 turns replicates the “Speak with beasts” spell restricted to communicating with plants created or modified by the gardeners spells or attainments. The gardener gains a +3 to any animal ken rolls involving the plants. As a befouled attainment may spend stored paradox to increase the dice bonus to animal ken rolls.

    Optional: Death 1

    The corpses of the fleeting human race form the soil from which Amma Su feeds, but the gardener can slow the jaws of their Dur-Abzu allowing corpses more time to be of service. After a scene's worth of ritually sprinkling the corpse with dirt and fertilizer the Corpses rate of decay halves for the attainments duration as determined by the prolonged duration table. as a befouled attainment the gardener may spend stored paradox to halve the rate of decay further.

    Second: The cruel urges of the vines

    Prerequisites: Life 2, Joining 3, Crafts 3

    Amma Su reaches into the world through the bio chemistry of plants and human corpses, by amplifying its presence within flora the gardener can further effect control over her creations. This attainment is activated as an instant action, for 30 turns the plant benefits or suffers from a condition of the gardener's choice replicating the “Control Instincts” spell. Gardeners typically use this attainment to whip their plants into a frenzy before sending them into battle, as a befouled attainment the gardener can spend stored paradox to add another 10 turns to the duration.

    Optional: Death 2

    By amplifying Amma Su’s presence within inanimate matter the Gardener renders it too much, better to feed her creations. As an instant action the Gardener can reduce an object's durability by their Death rating, replicating the “Decay” spell. This attainment can target a size 6 subject at Death 2 and a size 7 subject at Death 4. As a befouled attainment the Gardener can spend stored paradoxes to increase the possible size of the subject.

    Third: Mutagenic Mulch

    Prerequisites: Life 3 Joining 4, two dots in a second skill on the initiation list

    Amma Su’s cancerous touch breaks down telomeres and other protective proteins, promoting mutation and chaos. In a scene-long ritual of tending to plants in polluted soil the mage may induce a number of changes to the organism (which must be a plant) equal to their life dots as per the “transform life” spell. This attainment lasts a day, extending to a week at life 4. As a Befouled attainment the Gardener may spend stored paradox to create additional changes.

    Optional: Death 3

    Reversing the flow of power the gardener learns to devour the life of others. As an Instant action the Gardener can touch a creature that has at least 1 health box of lethal or aggravated damage and scour its pattern for mana or absorb a point of willpower as per the “Devouring the Slain spell” with bonus factors applied to potency. As a befouled attainment the Gardener may spend stored paradox to increase potency.

    Fourth: Chloroplastic Fury

    Prerequisites: Life 4, Joining 5, Crafts 4
    Amma Su’s relentless growth can be channeled into the stems and roots of the Gardeners creation, granting them the power to enact horrifying displays of brutality. After a scene-long ritual of fumigating the plant with toxic smog and spending a mana the subject gains a bonus to a single physical attribute equal to the Gardeners dots in Life replicating the “honing the form” spell. This attainment lasts for 1 week and can take the subject over its maximum for that attribute. As a befouled attainment the Gardener can spend stored paradox to increase potency. (this attainment technically has 1 extra reach over the limit but frankly potency should be honing the forms primary factory anyway)

    Optional: Death 4
    The Gardener can induce the feverish pace of Amma Su into the stillness of the graves, raising human corpses as savage warriors. After a scene-long ritual of watering a corpse with heavy-metal laced water the corpse springs to life as per the “Quicken Corpse” spell. The zombies last for the Gardeners death dots on the advanced duration table and use the spells potency (3) as a dice pool for attacks, and has a Defense 3, Initiative 1, and Speed 6. As a befouled attainment the Gardener may spend stored paradox to target additional corpses.

    Fifth: Garden Warfare

    Prerequisites: Life 5, Joining 6, Crafts 5, a third dot in a skill on the initiation list.
    At the pinnacle of the chrome and smog Ziggurat the Gardener can now draw plants directly from Amma Su. After a scene-long ritual of filling a plastic plant pot with contaminated soil a mutant plant springs into existence as per the “Create Life” spell. The plant can be designed from the ground up or can be a replica of a mundane plant then modified with “mutagenic mulch”. This attainment lasts for 1 year and can be up to size 7. As a befouled attainment stored paradox can be spent to increase maximum size.

    Optional: Death 5
    The Gardener may now infuse corpses with a semblance of personality as well as vitality. This attainment modifies the optional effect of “Chloroplastic fury” allowing the gardener to bind an artificial ghost to the corpse as per the “Create ghost” and “Revenant” spells. The created revenant is rank 1 and has a personality and occupation of the mages choosing (“Soilder” or “Scientist” for example”) and may use its potency as a dice pool on appropriate rolls (Firing a gun or concocting chemical weapons). The revenant has influence and numina as normal but naturally has no essence, it may gain essence by eating the flesh of living humans or plants mutated by the Gardeners attainments or spells. All revenants have a ban requiring them to enact violence at least once every 24 hours and have fire as their bane.

    House rules:

    1) When controlling the paradox in a befouled spell the Nasnasi rolls Gnosis+Joining instead of just Gnosis, additionally if they use their dedicated magical tool as a yantra they do not remove two paradox from the paradox pool, instead adding 2 dice to their Gnosis+joining roll

    2) When controlling the paradox of a befouled rote the Scelestus achieves an exceptional success when they successfully control 3 paradox instead of 5

    3) When creating Abyssal environment tilts with befouled spells a Nasnasi may choose its specific manifestation rather than leaving it to the storyteller (Why was this ever a rule?)

    4) Nasnasi performing abyssal summonings are not limited by half their joining rating in maximum rank but may only summon up to rank 5 Abyssal entities

    5) Nasnasi gain a persisten version of the "Abyssal Nimbus" condition when they reach joining 5 as normal, but it does not grant an arcane beat at the end of every scene when it should lapse and it does not add unsafe reach. instead a Nasnasi at Joining 5 may spend stored paradox to increase the strength of their nimbus whenever it flares, gaining +1 to strength or adding another die of penalies of bonus' per spent paradox.

      Seems serviceable at a glance, mechanics-wise and thematically, for all that I'm not terribly familiar with PVZ. The Optional effect of Garden Warfare may benefit from using the systems that Quicken Corpse uses to give the revenant Retainers a field of expertise, for which they roll twice the Attainment's Potency. (Speaking of which, given the rationale for how you've interpreted befouled Attainments to work, why have Chloroplastic Fury's optional effect juice Scale instead of Potency?)

      Originally posted by Communist Owl View Post
      3) When creating Abyssal environment tilts with befouled spells a Nasnasi may choose its specific manifestation rather than leaving it to the storyteller (Why was this ever a rule?)
      Same reason deliberate Abyssal summoning has a Rank cap: the Scelestus is not the one in control here. Maybe the Annunaki they've hitched their wagon to vibes with their intent on any given day, but the name of the game is unreliability when it comes to the Abyss and that doesn't scan if the Nasnasi can produce their desired Abyssal Environmental Tilts at will and spend less Paradox Reach to do so — FAFO is in full effect for the nihilistic gambling addicts who've been given enough rope to hang themselves with.

      (Also, the Nasnasi aren't Baalim; any Abyssal Environmental Tilts they're responsible for are steering their own Paradoxes toward collateral damage or produced by luck from accelerating those of another mage, not generated on the fly.)

      5) Nasnasi gain a persisten version of the "Abyssal Nimbus" condition when they reach joining 5 as normal, but it does not grant an arcane beat at the end of every scene when it should lapse
      The Persistent version of Abyssal Nimbus the Nasnasi gain does not grant Arcane Beats at all on account of how it doesn't lapse on its own; it may provide a Beat once per scene when the ordinary Condition would resolve, as usual for a normal Condition made Persistent.

      I'm also not sure how being able to spend Paradox successes on something as fleeting as the Nimbus Tilt is preferable to being able to put any mage who's subject to it at risk of Paradox that you can then worsen, but that at least doesn't seem to be coming from a misunderstanding of the text.
