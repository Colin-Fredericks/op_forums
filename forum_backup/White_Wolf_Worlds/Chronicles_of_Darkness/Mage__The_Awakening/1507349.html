    Onikaze (Brotherhood of the Demon Wind 2e Conversion)

    04-03-2023, 10:04 PM
    Brotherhood of the Demon Wind

    Wave-Men, Onikaze


    Pages 157-160 of Adamantine Arrow for lore


    Doctrine and Magic


    Parent Path: Mastigos

    Parent Order: Adamantine Arrow

    Prerequisites: Time 2, space 1, 2 dots in Weaponry, Quick Draw merit, one of the following additional skills at 2 dots or higher: Athletics, Brawl, Streetwise, or Survival

    Initiation: Travel to a holy place with your mentor to train with both blade and mind until you are attuned to the Demon Wind. After the initiation is complete the new Onikaze can never return to that location.

    Ruling Arcana: Time

    Yantras: Casting a spell in combat (+1); Addressing the present situation with a brief aphorism or koan (+1); Voluntarily forgoing Defense for a turn in a conflict situation (+2)

    Oblations: Practicing martial arts or training with a blade; Reading or writing philosophy; Defeating someone in combat.


    Attainments


    First Attainment: Yanagi ni Kaze


    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The Demon Wind can be channeled in such a way to allow the Wave-Man to act with perfect timing. This attainment emulates “Perfecting Timing” (Time 1, MtAw 187) with reach for advanced duration. It takes an hour of meditation or training with a blade to activate this attainment, however, the mage does not have to sacrifice their defense while planning their action if the bonus is used on an attack.


    Optional: Space 1
    The Demon Wind transcends both Space and Time and by attuning themselves to it the Wave-Men are capable of touching that transcendence. While activating the primary part of this attainment the mage can also activate this piece, which functions like “The Outward and Inward Eye” (Space 1, MtAw 174) with reach for advanced duration.


    Second Attainment: Tazei ni Buzei


    Prerequisites: Time 2, 3 dots in weaponry
    As the mage’s soul becomes more attuned to the Demon Wind the Benefits of touching it increase. This attainment functions like “Choosing the Thread” (Time 2, MtAw 187) with reach for instant casting.


    Optional: Space 2
    The flow of the Demon Wind is not obstructed by physical objects, neither then, are those who follow it. This attainment functions like “Break Boundary” (Space 2, MtAw 174) with reach for instant casting.


    Third Attainment: Akuma no Ken


    Prerequisites: Time 3, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 3 or a second option from the list must be raised to 2
    At this stage of their training the Onikaze truly can ascend both Time and Space, allowing them not to just touch the transcendent nature of the Demon Wind, but to embody it. This attainment is activated during the same meditation as the other attainments and emulates “Acceleration” (Time 3, MtAw 188) with reach for advanced duration. It still takes one mana to activate.


    Optional: Space 3
    When attacking with a blade the Onikaze can allow the blade to flow along the Demon Wind, allowing them to strike at a distance. They can treat their Weaponry attacks as though they had range increments of Space dots/2x Space dots/4x Space dots. If the Onikaze has the Fast Spells merit then the attacks are treated as firearms attacks for the purposes of the target's Defense.


    Fourth Attainment: Tazei ni Buzei​

    Prerequisites: Time 4, 4 dots in weaponry
    Those who study the Demon Wind can pull themselves into it, allowing it to carry them back into the past. This attainment functions like “Shifting Sands” (Time 3, MtAw 189) with reach for advanced duration and to return to the beginning of the scene. This attainment also uses the “Time in a Bottle” (Time 4, MtAw 196) for instant casting, and therefore takes one mana to activate.
    ​
    ​
    Optional: Space 4
    The Wave-Master flits from the ravages of spatial storms as easily as the temporal. With a thought and an Instant action, a space around him up to the size of a small room, or a large room at Space 5, is sealed away for 5 turns, or 10 turns at Space 5. The space becomes impermeable to all that would cross it, including light and sound, sending all that would cross one border to the edge of the opposite border. Then, he may activate Space Sight as a reflexive action even if Space is not Ruling for him, costing no Mana. This replicates the spell "Ban" with Reach assigned to instant casting, and a special effect.
    ​

    Fifth Attainment: Inochi Atte No Monodane


    Prerequisites: Time 5, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 4 or a second option from the list must be raised to 3 or a third option must be raised to 2
    The Onikaze can see the Demon Wind and watch how it reacts to the actions of others. While meditating to activate their other attainments the mage can activate this one for two mana. It functions as “Present as Past” (Time 4, MtAw 190) with reach for advanced duration and advanced scale. The “Time in a Bottle” (Time 4, MtAw 196) attainment from being a Time Adept also applies, making the attainment instant.


    Optional: Space 5
    The Onikaze may run along the Demon Wind with their entire body, emulating “Teleportation” (Space 4, MtAw 178) with reach for instant casting and sympathetic range, although they must use a mana to move along their sympathetic connections.​

          The second Time Attainment might need to be tweeked. I'm not sure how you'd be activating the first Time Attainment in combat, and Choose the Thread works better as an Instant Cast bit of divination. Right now, the Wave-man can reroll any one action, but then has to spend an hour to reactivate it to benefit again. With Instant Casting instead, they're using the "momentum of battle" to plan their actions out a few turns in advance, selecting the optimal course of action

          Instant, Self-cast Temporal Stutter might be interesting for the Adept Attainment, allowing them to vanish for a few turns and evolving well into Teleport. Especially if you add a codicil to be able to use the Sympathetic Range option for the requisite point of Mana - they go from being able to momentarily vanish, to vanishing to distant locations, to eventually just teleporting anywhere they can see.

          Is Past As Present being Aimed? I assume you can "aim" at yourself and have the effect center on you, but otherwise it currently needs you to be physically touching all intended targets.

              In the third optional Attainment, I think it would be easier if the spell simply granted the same attack range bands as their current Gnosis.                                   Teleportation can move you to any destination within sensory range by overwriting your position (page 178 of the core book). Since it would probably be used to allow the mage to reposition to use their ranged melee, sensory range would be unnecessary.

                                  In the case of the fourth main Attainment, using Temporal Stutter at sensory range just means that you can remove a target from the stream of time at sensory range (although they can still Withstand with Stamina) and, since its Potency primary, it only lasts a turn. It doesn't determine where the target reappears because that requires moving across space rather than time, so it would need conjunctional Space 2 and sympathetic range to do that. It would still costs only one mana due to Legacy rules, but it requires the optional Attainment's spot.
