    Pure Sovereigns
    The Sovereigns are a Ladder Legacy based around mastering body and mind through intense internal control. The underlying principles rely on the idea that Mind and Life are deeply intertwined, and that by only controlling one you fail to truly utilize the whole. Once you understand how your body works, it's easy to use it effectively, or even apply that understanding to others.

    Organization: Bluebloods tend to be ambitious, but cooperate readily as a Legacy when goals align. Most Bluebloods prefer to focus primarily on master-student relationships, with several acolytes working under the direction of a senior instructor. On the other hand, Bluebloods tend to drift towards Cabals with complementary Legacies, such as the Sisterhood of the Blessed. Consilli with influential Sovereigns will often have a Cryptopoly based around their body-control exercises, a surprisingly reliable source of Sleepwalker assistants that endears the Legacy to their peers.
    Theory: Before one can master the world, one must master one's self.
    Background: (See the 1e book Silver Ladder for official fluff.) A Legacy of mages focused on self-mastery, with a history that extends back into antiquity. Though exact origins are spotty, they are generally accepted as a cousin of the Perfected Adept family of Legacies, possibly a Silver Ladder variation that has gained sufficient independence to become it's own tradition. Major lines of impeccable pedigree have been recorded in the British Isles, Nepal, and India, but the practice has become diffuse enough that it isn't tied to any region.

    Modern practitioners have often made jokes about the similarities between themselves and the Bene Gesserit of the Dune novels. This has begun to influence the Cryptopolies Sovereigns run, with references to Gom Jabbars and Spice Agony appearing in the rhetoric particularly flippant cult-managers.


    Parentage: Silver Ladder; Mastigos, Thyrsus
    Ruling Arcanum: Life. Thyrsus instead gain Mind
    Yantras: Being surrounded by faithful attendants (+1), interpretive dancing or full-body mudras representing the spell (+2), mentoring lesser beings through the use of, or supported by the use of, the spell (+2), enforcing order (+1), benevolent ministration (+2), emotionally-resonant oration (+2)
    Oblations: Exercise katas; dictating your significant thoughts to your attendants; mentoring lesser beings

    Prerequisites: Life 2, Mind 1; at least one dot in Athletics or Brawl; at least one dot in Science, Medicine, or Occult; at least one dot in Empathy, Expression, Socialize, or Subterfuge; Specialty (Body Control) in one of the previously listed Skills
    Initiation: Initiation into the Sovereigns requires that a prospective member gain a sense of bodily awareness that exceeds normal pain-and-pleasure responses. Often this requirement is met simply by joining a Sovereign Cryptopoly, but it is not unheard of for unaffiliated practitioners to develop their own methods. Usually achieved through specific exercises, a prospect will eventually reach a point where they can optimally filter their sensory input - not just experience temperature, touch, sight or sound, but sort those data streams into usable conclusions. A successful prospect will reach a tipping point, where the optimization becomes instinctive and automatic and the first Legacy Marks scribe onto their Gnosis. A common superstition is that practicing the exercises in a Demesne or other place of power will accelerate the process, but cautious mentors claim this can have odd effects.

    Achieving the Blood of Silver is little different from the Mind of Amber, and more often than not a Blueblood achieves both refinements simultaneously. The exercises involved are more extensive and full-bodied, but are frequently taught concurrently because they combine disparate lower-level movements. The rigors of this process serve to unify the flow of physical and psychological feedback loops, making it difficult to shift one without the other.

    Achieving the Flesh of Gold is a harrowing experience. Some practitioners are exposed to increasingly intense toxins and illnesses, some are subjected to overwhelming pain that is not caused by actual physical damage. Ultimately, the Sovereign must exercise their bodily understanding to repair what they can, purge what they must and ignore what they cannot change. Flippant practitioners often refer to this as the Gom Jabbar test, and set up the ceremony to ape it.

    Achieving the Incarnation of Thunder is highly introspective. Every Sovereign eventually begins to wonder what the limits of their bodily control is, and the quest to find these limits is in of itself the process to scribe their Gnosis.

    Those rare texts that detail how to achieve The Sage Ascendent describe it in painful terms, involving strange concoctions and tumultuous Astral journeys. Occasionally called "The Spice Agony", little is known about the process beyond the fact that a Sovereign will be forced to confront something deep in the Temenos realm of their Legacy. Some claim it is the accumulated memories of their predecessors, others that it is a Goetia Manifestation of the practitioner's "genetic memories", and still others that it is a spark of knowledge left behind by the lost Kings of Atlantis. Whatever the truth, Sage Sovereigns manifest prowess with mundane skills that few humans can match unaided.

    Special: Thanks to the increasing control over -and optimization of- their biology, each level of initiation slows the Blueblood's aging by half. That is, an Initiate would only age a day for every two that pass, a Disciple would age a day for every eight, and a Master would age a day for every 32. This is just a rule of thumb - exact biology may vary between practitioners, and generally none of it will come up in play.

    Mind of Amber ●
    (Life ●, Mind ●) Initiation
    Though a human's senses are somewhat pathetic by the standards of the animal kingdom, a great deal is simply lost due to insufficient processing power. The Pure Sovereign's first refinement eases that limitation, enhancing their natural faculties for both perception and comprehension. They add [Potency] Dice to Perception rolls.
    Heightened Senses
    Potency= Lower of Life and Mind
    Duration = Lasting
    Casting Time = N/A
    Range = Self
    NOTE: Consider allowing the player to purchase a Survival Specialty to track by scent alone

    Refining the flow of information within mind and body also gives the Sovereign a deeper understanding of how the two halves interact. By careful optimization, the Blueblood can separate out their consciousness into parallel streams, one housed in grey matter and the other in pure psyche.

    The Blueblood can take an additional Instant Action, or sustain an Extended Action, each turn. However, said action must primarily take place within their mind. Actions that rely on external inputs, such as Perception or Empathy rolls, are acceptable, but a separate task with each hand is not.
    One Mind Two Thoughts
    Duration = Lasting
    Potency = N/A
    Casting Time = Lasting
    Range = Self

    Simulfow and 1e both dictate this Attainment, though it's actually coming in pretty early this edition.



    Blood of Silver ●●
    (Life ●●, Mind ●●) Interdisciplinary Specialty (Body Control)
    To progress beyond being a mere initiate, a novice Blueblood practices a routine of precise, deliberate movements passed down to them by their master. They advance into Apprenticehood in the moment they finally execute the routine perfectly, the Blueblood becoming acutely aware of the inner workings of their body. By fine-tuning these processes, the Blueblood refines the efficiency of even their most humble cells.
    Body Control
    Potency = Lower of Life and Mind
    Duration = Lasting
    Casting Time = N/A
    Range = Self
    NOTE: Gains both Reach effects.

    The first step to self-mastery is to tame the body. The second step is to tame the mind. The two halves work as a seamless whole, and invasive attempts to alter one create a dissonance in the other. Bluebloods are able to shrug off external attempts to affect his emotions or control him, his limbic system or psyche re-asserting the natural order if their counterpart is assaulted. This absolute control has a secondary benefit, allowing the Blueblood to select their current emotional state as an Instant Action. Furious? Extinguish the rage. Unhappy? Will it away. Sad? Put a smile on that face (and soul).
    Mental Shield/Emotional Urging
    Duration = Lasting (Two Reach to Lasting)
    Potency = N/A (2, 3 at Mind 4)
    Casting Time = N/A
    Range = Self
    NOTE: Clash of Wills uses [lower of Life and Mind + Gnosis]. It explicitly does not protect against mind/emotion reading. Changing your own emotions does not trigger PMS

    Alternative - use the Deviant power Superhuman Resolve's 3-Magnitude special ability, using the lower of Life and Mind in the Clash of Wills.


    Flesh of Gold ●●●
    (Life ●●●, Mind ●●●) Trained Observer 1 and at least 6 dots spread in any combination amongst the Initiation Skills
    The Bluebloods strive to exemplify Thunder - self-sufficient paragons that inspire others to seek the Imperium. To do so they must be seen as unassailable, shrugging off physical travails with but a moment's thought. Even more than other Life-focused Mages, the Sovereigns are masters of self-healing and bodily control.

    This has several benefits;
    • The Blueblood's accelerated healing from Blood of Silver now also applies to Aggravated damage
    • The Blueblood can use Pattern Restoration to repair Resistant Damage, and can heal Aggravated damage at a rate of 3 Mana per health level
    • The Blueblood is automatically aware of any poisons, toxins, or diseases entering or affecting their body, and automatically provokes a Clash against Supernatural efforts to cloak such intrusions
    • The Blueblood can take a Instant Action to force a resistance roll against any disease or poison they are aware of that is currently affecting them, consciously directing their biology to combat intrusion
    • The Blueblood adds their dots in Life to all rolls to resist disease and poison, and Exceptional Successes on such rolls only require three successes, rather than five. An Exceptional Success purges the offending condition outright, even if it would normally be incurable.

    Forcing the roll does not trigger PMS. A combination of a bunch of different things - Purge Illness, Cleanse the Body, and a bit of Mend, at a minimum. Maybe Always-On Life Mage Sight focused on their own body? Anyway, Blood of Silver is already boosting Bashing and Lethal healing and a brace of resistances, and "self healing" is the theme I chose to emphasize, so I don't think it particularly breaks anything.

    Plus, it's more or less what their 1e incarnation got at this level.

    A young Sovereign's understanding of their own physiology responses gives them great insight into the minds of others. A more advanced Sovereign has sufficient understanding and self-mastery to begin "mastering the world".

    By modulating their pitch, tone, and word choice to trigger limbic responses, the Sovereign may issue short, one-to-two-word commands to all human and human-adjascent entities within earshot. This works as if they had cast Psychic Domination, but the Sovereign must be clearly heard and understood by the target. If the Sovereign focuses on a single specific target, they may instead tailor their modulation to issue a complex, sentence-long command as if having used the first Reach effect.

    This Attainments does not trigger Peripheral Mage Sight or provide immediate knowledge that the target is being unnaturally commanded, though hostile targets may become suspicious if they abruptly accommodate their Blueblood foe.
    Psychic Domination
    Potency = Lower of Life and Mind
    Withstand = Resolve
    Duration = 2 turns, 3 Turns at Mind 4
    Casting Time = Instant
    Range = Sensory
    NOTE: Earshot Area "Advanced Scale" if using a simple order, "Complex Orders" Reach effect if focused on a single target.


    Incarnation of Thunder ●●●●
    (Life ●●●●, Mind ●●●●) Trained Observer 3, least 8 dots spread in any combination amongst the Initiation Skills
    The Blueblood's mastery of the self has begun to flower - by engaging in a deep meditative trance, they are now able to reshape themselves into their humanist ideal. This is no mere deceptive mask thrown across an imperfect framework, but instead the assertation that their form is a nascent Supernal Truth.

    The Blueblood's accelerated healing also benefits, allowing them to regrow teeth, organs, and limbs. Mark the health box that caused Persistent Conditions such as Blind or Crippled, as if they were Arm/Leg Wrack, and resolve them in the same way. Unless killed outright, they are capable of recovering from anything.
    Many Faces
    Potency = Life
    Duration = Lasting
    Casting Time = Scene
    Range = Self
    NOTE: Doesn't need Time 3 to affect apparent age. Each activation changes the caster's "default" form.

    Plus something close to Regeneration

    A Sovereign of Thunder needs little rest, and can heal their mind as easily as their flesh. A successful meditation roll is treated as a full night's rest, and restores a point of Willpower. The Sovereign can also resolve up to [Mind] Mental Conditions or Tilts, though they gain no Beats for doing so.
    Clear Thoughts
    Potency = Mind
    Duration = Lasting
    Casting Time = Ritual
    Range = Self
    NOTE: "Willpower" Reach, "Lasting" Reach

    No exact parallels anywhere (except maybe a DnD Elan). But I like it, it feels like it fits the theme


    The Sage Ascendent ●●●●●
    (Life ●●●●●, Mind ●●●●●) Astral Adept, Eidetic Memory, and at least 10 dots spread in any combination amongst the Initiation Skills
    The Blueblood's physical and mental perfection has more or less blossomed - even in a task they have never attempted, their every action is suffused with competence. Some claim the Sovereign is tapping into genetic memory, others that they are drawing knowledge from the Astral, but the Legacy remains tight-lipped.
    • The Sovereign never suffers untrained penalties, and is always considered to have a single dot in every Skill in which he has no dots.
    • Once per Scene, until the end of the scene, the Sovereign is considered to have three dots in a single Skill of the player’s choice.
    • Once per chapter, until the end of the scene, the Sovereign is considered to have three dots in every Skill in which he has fewer than three dots
    • Once per chapter, until the end of the scene, the Sovereign is considered to have five dots in a single Skill of his player’s choice
    Any Skill dots gained from this ability do not count for the purposes of learning Specialties or Merits.

    Omnicompetence. Will you go hard into Attributes, leaning on your connection to the Astral? Or will you be your own person?




    Legacy Secrets and Story Hooks
    ● Mages with an understanding of the Chrysalides Legacy sometimes wonder if there is a connection between the two traditions. Did the first Swan arise as a way to keep Seers with body dysmorphia in the fold? Was the first Sovereign a disgusted response by the Silver Ladder when the first Swan became known to them?

    ● Sovereigns gain great health and power through their institutional exercises, and often set up Cryptopolies teaching truncated versions to Sleepers. This becomes a problem when a trigger-happy foreign Guardian begins investigating an outbreak of Flesh Intruders in the area. Believing them to be practicing Sinister Organ Qigong, the Guardian begins a campaign against the local Sovereigns, using methods both subtle and overt. Whether the Guardian is right is up to the Storyteller. Are the Pure Sovereigns as a whole, in fact, less "Pure" than they claim?

    ● A Daimonomikon titled "The 25 Purities Path" has surfaced in the Consillium. Some young, impulsive Mages have used it to initiate themselves into what seems to be a Pure Sovereigns lineage, but are rapidly falling into self-mutilating madness. Is the Daimonomikon cursed, flawed, or a trap? Or do the erstwhile initiates simply need a proper mentor to help them see their "imperfections" as flaws to be corrected rather than cancers to be excised?

    ● It has become well-known that Flesh of Gold Sovereigns can force others to obey them with but a word, and that traditional cues do not make such commands obvious. Now, the Consillium is blaming any inconvenience or poor decision on Blueblood influence. Is this just a natural reaction to a somewhat concerning ability, or is someone actively driving a wedge into the Pentacle?


    Typical Blueblood Cryptopoly
    Promoting themselves as a holistic wellness organizations, these cults often attract middle-managers and office worker who are dissatisfied with their sedentary lifestyle. The most advanced cultists are able to brush at the barest edges of the Astral, approaching the Sovereign's Tenemos Realm through a lens of "genetic memory"
    ● Gain Specialty (Body Control) in Medicine, Athletics, Empathy, Expression, or Subterfuge
    ●● Gain the Interdisciplinary Specialty (Body Control) Merit
    ●●● Gain the Eidetic Memory Merit
    ●●●● Gain three dots of the Trained Observer Merit
    ●●●●● Gain the Astral Adept Merit if a Sleepwalker or Mage, or three dots of the Dream Merit if a Sleeper. If the latter, they may choose to convert those dots into the Astral Adept Merit if they ever Awaken or go through a change that would normally make them a Sleepwalker

        Scribblings of a Moros Mind Legacy. I have the powers of the first two tiers about where I want them, but not much else. The Apprentice tier reads a lot like the Uncrowned Kings, in that the Uncrowned got both those powers in 1e. I can see a couple of ways to go with it.
        On the one hand, an always-on sense of human-level intellects nearby screams "paranoia" to me. It's a broad utility, but it would probably have a lot of use for spies. Similarly, Soul Marks would be good for identifying what entities are in the area - you'd have to concentrate on a specific subject every turn to learn specific information, but that would make it perfect for something like Project Nightfall. Identify the enemy agents, and eliminate. Taken from that perspective, later tiers should emphasize stealth and misdirection, which would drift them into something similar to the Subtle Ones. Or possibly the Quiessent, for a less goal-driven paranoid.

        On the other hand, it could just be a straight-up monster hunter. I'm iffier on what later-tier powers would be then - choosing a specific brand of enemy to fight would be best, but even just working on the basis of them being Théarchs who are viscerally offended by the amount of entities out there who maim human souls gives the idea more direction. Dedicated combatants of the Tremere, maybe?

        In either case, the Apprentice powers are just pragmatic.

        Spy/Paranoia Route:
        On the one hand, Alter Mental Pattern is pulling double-duty with Mental Shield. On the other, having two lines of defense against mental intrusions is a good thing. The question then is whether the AMP is layered on top or the MS, or if AMP itself is the secondary defense.

        Come to think of it, the Ticktock Men had something similar in 1e... so another question would be whether to have it be an always-on power with one fixed personality, that keeps "reacting" in the manner that personality would be expected to. Probably not, that would work better in the Legion of the Lost or the actual Ticktock Men. Plus, the relevant Ticktock Men were full-on Abyssals.

        Surpress Aura is mostly a counter-espionage power in this context. Keeps other supernaturals from identifying them. Plus, if I'm reading the spell right, it also subtracts dice from attempts to pierce AMP. Again, always-on might be neat, but that Empathy penalty would probably be detrimental in the long-term. Hard for people to trust someone they can't read.

        Adept is a bit late to be getting around to Incognito Presence and Machine Invisibility, but they're also goddamn useful. Especially in tandem. The Optional requiring Matter is probably the more correct choice, especially if it's a Moros Legacy, but I also like the idea of their mastery of Death just... fuzzing out any surveillance on them. Not actually make them invisible, but rather make them into a horror movie monster who turns the screen to static whenever they enter the field of view. Which might actually be a disadvantage in some scenarios- that Temple Guardian Golem might be intelligent enough to start punching in the general vicinity of where their vision keeps failing.

        Cut Threads would also be a viable Optional.

        I'm not sure what the capstone would be. There's plenty of old fallbacks for Mind - Psychic Domination, Read the Depths, Hallucination, etc. But none really jump out at me, nor much for the Optional. Empty Presence and Forge No Chains might be a good combo, but A) this isn't a Death-primary Legacy, and B) the Adept tier is already doing a lot of the same stuff.

        Any Other Route
        I have a jumble of possible stuff, but a lot of it isn't cohesive.
        1. For a Death Optional at the Disciple tier, maybe make Death Armor's effects be constantly on, and able to stack with other kinds of Mage Armor? Death Mage Armor is the only one that doesn't directly add to your Defense (and thus scale with your Arcana), iirc. Maybe have the Mind side reduce wound/fatigue penalties in some way? Combined, the tier would have a "my body is just a shell I pilot" feel
        2. Attack spells in the Adept slot, Bashing-tier but able to be done at Sight Range. Combined spells. You ever think about how deadly combined attack spells are, even un-Attainmentized? It's pretty much doubling your damage for only a -2 penalty, for the tradeoff of not being able to use Rotes.
        3. Mental Scan, only usable against things detected by the Initiate? Might be useful, dunno what would be a good Optional.
        4. Sever Soul might be interesting, but that's a pretty Legacy-defining power to have in the Optionals. And I have no idea what would be a good main-track pairing, beyond maybe Possession or a really callous application of Read the Depths.
        5. A non-spell equivalent power? I keep thinking of the Arcane Curse for Sidereals in Exalted, but that's a big thing.

                    Originally posted by Cauthon View Post
                    Fate:
                    • Any "lucker" spell. That is, stuff like Quantum Flux, which eliminate penalties, and Exceptional Luck / Superlative Luck which add dice or give Rote qualities.
                      EL makes an OK Apprentice Attainment with either Instan Casting or Advanced Duration, or as an Adept Attainment if your ST allows the "spellcasting roll" Reach.
                      SL makes a good Disciple or Master Attainment, using the exact same logic as EL.
                    • Serendipity as an Disciple Attainment (Instant Casting, "Any Skill") will make you into a true McGuyver - in any mundane scenario, you can immediately twist thing around to a Skill of your strength.
                      As an Apprentice Attainment (Instant Casting, "Same Skill Group"), you're still skilled, but still limited based on what your skill-spread is. Making it Scene Casting, "Same Skill Group" is still useful, but you can't do it on the fly.
                      As an Initiate, it's a good way to become a minor prophet or detective, regardless of if you make it Instant Cast or "Same Skill Group"
                    • Fools Rush In isn't as useful as the stuff above, but it still keeps you from being at a disadvantage in most scenarios.


                    Apprentice Shifting the Odds is effectively useless without Advanced Duration. I'd either swap it and Warding Gesture around to bump it up a slot, or make it Scene-cast, Advanced Duration.

                    Chaos Mastery is fine, but Miracle is actually less useful than it seems at first glance. Because of how little reach you have to work with, Miracle is basically going to be Chaos Mastery again. And both suffer from "Touch/Aimed" syndrome. In general, I find that the best spells for a given slot come from a practice at least one level lower; Adept spells are usually better in the Master slot than Master spells, Disciple spells over Adept spells in the Adept slot, and so on.
                    What is your opinion on Sworn Oaths at Adept level? Instant Casting, Advanced Duration. You can buff yourself with boons (9-again on every skill roll, potence bonus to any skill roll, lots of favourable conditions) as often as you need it, and you can dispel the attainment forgoing the boon but also the hex if your boon is no longer applicable... Without counting the Clash of wills to avoid being stopped from doing something your really want.
