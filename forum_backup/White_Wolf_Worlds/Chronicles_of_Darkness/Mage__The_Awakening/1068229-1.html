                            Moinen no it forbids performing a physical task at the same time that you're trying to maintain two separate thoughts. You can still perform physical actions over the course of the spell. So if you were maintaining consciousness in the Fallen and the Astral and you got into a fist fight in either realm then one of your streams of consciousness would freeze while the remaining stream would concentrate on the fight. After the fight if the spell hasn't run out of Duration you can resume paying attention to your split streams of thought in both realms. The exception is if you have the fist fight in the Astral and the Reach clause active to allow simultaneous "Physical" actions in which case you can fist fight in the Astral and perform a simultaneous mental action in either realm.

                            Again, this spell is what becomes the most basic ability of Dreamspeakers who are supposed to be able to exist in the Astral and the Fallen at the same time.

                                                I hate being late to the party, bit if I can necropost a bit. Mrmdubois , I mentioned in another topic that Dreamspeakers were giving me problems and you asked me why. In the meantime, I was looking up if anyone had converted them successfully, and it seems you were trying to solve the same problem I was having, that Dreamspeakers do not work without their first attainment, but in 2E the first attainment is either impossible, or is so demanding on reach that you need to put it as a second, third, or maybe even 4th attainment. Which is so awful!

                                                Here's my primary concern though. You're saying One Mind, Two Thoughts allows one train of thought to remain in the body while the other is in the astral. I'm not entirely convinced. I mean I would prefer you to be right and I can see you won over Moinen, but my concern is that entering the astral realms is basically astral projection, except that while the Mind 4 spell allows you to do such a thing indiscriminately and into the physical world's twilight, entering the astral realms is already a part of the Mage Template as a whole, obviating the need for Mind dots.

                                                So it seems like Mind 4 is analogous to entering the astral realms, which are part of the mage template and do not follow a clean dot rating. But when you use Astral Projection, your body goes unconscious, similar to how astral travel requires your physical body to go unconscious. One Mind, Two Thoughts originally seems like it wants all your trains of thought to follow where your mind is going. While you can add another train-of-thought to your mind for astral travel, your mind is in the astral, not occupying your physical form at the time--only your brain is left behind, but your magical mind-self has gone elsewhere, and it carries the One Mind, Two Thoughts spell with it.

                                                Another reason I think this is because the Astral Travel is so mentally taxing--you form basically a goetia of yourself for the journey, you get amnions, and if you die there you get Soul-Shocked. I really think this all means your Mind-self has left your body during Astral Travel and it is unable to leave one thought-process behind and split attention between the physical form and the Mind-self-on-astral-travel.

                                                My only other thought is that the Dreamspeaker legacy could get away with a very liberal interpretation of "atypical attainments", but, I do not think that One Mind, Two Thoughts as a praxis is enough to fully duplicate the Dreamspeaker's 1E first attainment even if you spend as much reach as you can.

                                                Because as I said, dying in the astral leaves you soul-shocked. It sounds more dramatic if it requires ALL your mental processes, including ones tacked on with Compelling magic, otherwise it would be pretty weird if a non-Dreamspeaker Mind Initiate with a demense could pull this trick off, go get a coffee at a cafe, get into a fight in the astral, and suddenly slump over at his table in the cafe from soul-shock.

                                                Although that might be my tastes... I guess someone else would see that as plenty dramatic? Fitting in with the Dreamspeaker's motiff of looking like madmen/women? But again, I would see that more appropriate for the Dreamspeaker proper and not just any ole initiate of mind?

                                                Thoughts?

                                                    Scarlet Witch Okay, so when you want to go to the Astral the first thing you need to be able to do is to meditate, which is an extended mental action. If you cast One Mind, Two Thoughts, you can split off a second stream of consciousness from the one that's doing the meditating. The meditating consciousness goes to the Astral, the non-meditating consciousness...doesn't. There's no reason for it to, it's busy doing something else, reading a book perhaps. Essentially, since one stream of consciousness doesn't make the dedicated effort to breach the Astral Barrier, it can't get in, so it gets left outside by default, running your body presumably. Also, to some extent, Mind has brains as part of its purview or Mind damaging spells wouldn't focus on doing actual physical damage. Psychic Assault is my prime example for that. If brains are sort of part of Mind and your actual brains can't go into the Astral then to me it stands to reason that you can have a stream of consciousness active in your physical brain even while another stream is otherwise occupied in the Astral. And again, the Dreamspeakers simply -don't- work unless this is all true, which is the exact problem you're having. Might I suggest following the path of least resistance?

                                                    By the way, you would only need to spend 1 Reach and that's for Advanced Duration, the ritual activation would obviously be the process of meditating into the Astral. That effectively restricts the Dreamspeaker from having a general One Mind, Two Thoughts effect to one that specifically maintains an Astral/Physical divide necessary for the legacy to work. So it works as the very first attainment! Usually Mages get their first two primary attainments at the same time, so the 2nd Mind attainment would probably be something that lets them dive into, and navigate the Astral faster, although it wouldn't bypass the meditation rules completely (If we're hewing to 1e as much as possible).

                                                    As an aside, if you used One Mind, Two Thoughts -before- or combined with Psychic Projection you would end up with one stream of consciousness floating around in Twilight and another stream of consciousness (Man I wish there were a shorter term) running your body. Because I agree, going to the Astral is a form of mental projection, as a result it needs to be subject to the same kind of rule interactions. The Psychic Projection rules are written with the view that -only- this spell was cast, travel to the Astral was written as if this was -all- you're doing. All the spells or abilities are written like that. It doesn't remove the ability for spells to interact in a synergistic manner.

                                                    Moving on, other Mages not in the Dreamspeakers using spells to copy the Dreamspeaker attainments: I don't see a problem with it. Naturally they won't get the attainment benefits of it being impossible to dispel or the automatic activation, etc, but what's the point of being a Dreamspeaker if your Astral travels don't come with a few perks? You say that a Mage suddenly slumping over his table at a cafe isn't dramatic, I say something hella exciting must have happened in the Astral, and if that Mage was a PC it got screen time so it was definitely dramatic. Even if it was an NPC then with a good storyteller it could be dramatic. The important, dramatic thing in that event isn't what happened at the cafe table.

                                                    The whole Soul-Shocked Condition thing: Souls are weird, souls in Mage are especially weird because they're not the seat of personality or anything that we'd typically equate with souls, they seem more like the seat of "humanity" by which we observe that Virtue is supposed to be preeminent and the source of drive. If you're duking it out in the Astral then I think it'd be likely that your sense of drive is typically more engaged there at the moment even if your body is enjoying a nice mocha with your other stream of consciousness. That's where you're invested, that's why you suffer Soul Shock when you get burned in the Astral regardless of your physical activity. If you're in the Fallen and the Astral at the same time, one will inevitably be more eminent. You would have to be crazy (or a Mage) to be involved in a gun battle in the Fallen and a duel in the Astral at the same time, and regardless eventually, survival of your Fallen self or Astral self will have to be prioritized because of the limitations of One Mind, Two Thoughts. A sane Mage (Maybe) would probably prioritize their Fallen self, even though being Soul Shocked will suck.
