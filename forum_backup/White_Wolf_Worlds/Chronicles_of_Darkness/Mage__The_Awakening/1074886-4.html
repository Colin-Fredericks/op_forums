    Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post
    Reactions also vary by Order - a Silver Ladder mage who casts a Befouled rote once to achieve a set goal and never touches it again is a badass if he gets caught, a Guardian of the Veil who does it will be lucky to walk away with intact frontal lobes.

    Part of the reason the Orders dress Befouling up in Babylonian terminology and make Rabashakim out to be different, somehow, is that they don't want neophyte mages knowing how easy it is.

    But just as abstinance is the worst sex education policy, making abyssal magic more Mysterious than it is just means those who find put about it and experiment with it do so without a safety net AND go hardcore quicker. you get way fewer Rabashakim, but more of them blow up into Nasnasi.

    Again with the Tremere side-commentary, but the major fallout of the Tremere's banishment is that well-meaning Legacies who have extreme longevity or immortality among their practices have to hush it up in polite company, and the modern Pentacle knows way less about Souls and how they work than their ancestors from a few thousand years ago, because Souls were the Tremere's "thing".
    How saddening and infuriating even the most enlightened and mentally/spiritually advanced supernatural offshoot of mankind has not yet learned the lesson in millennia about the pettiness, stupidity, and (self-)destructive nature of demonization of knowledge, guilt by association, and moral panic. Especially given the utter, overwhelming futility of pursuing willful ignorance for a supernatural splat whose defining psych trait is obsessive curiosity. Then again, their mundane cousins have not yet really learnt the equally poignant and inescapable lesson in millennia that prohibition of pleasurable stimuli never ever works.

                        I think you use the Befouled praxis one too many times until you have an abyssal awakening at a ziggurat? In 1E there was a count-down to when your overuse of antinomian magic in a short time-frame would result in the Joining, with or without direction. The countdown was decided by your willpower dots. Each time you used a befouled spell, you got an increasing penalty to resist the temptation to cast your next spell as antinomian as well--if the penalty reduced your dice pool to a chance die, you immediately became nasnasi, even without a teacher. You then gained the abyssal insight needed to expand your powers--or you could turn your back on the abyss, a painful process, and free yourself--but it would also make you unable to ever again use antinomian magic at all.
                        Dunno if that helps explain it, but if you need to homebrew temporarily, maybe that's a good start? as mrmdubois said, Obsession can get you places :3
