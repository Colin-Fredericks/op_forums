            Originally posted by Phoog View Post
            The main thing to note is that, in the Shadow, spirits are flesh and blood, so are definitely more susceptible to Life magic than they might otherwise be.
            Not at all. The flesh and blood of spirits is ephemera regardless of whether they're in the Shadow or in the material realm. Life magic does not work any different on spirits just because they're in the Shadow. In fact, anything material that enters the Shadow is converted to ephemera, so it's honestly unclear whether Life and Matter magic would work as well on living beings and objects brought into the Shadow.

            You might've mixed up how the Shadow works with how the Astral Realms work. In the Astral Realms, goetic entities are affected by Mind just like how spirits are affected by Spirit, but they're also affected as if they were the thing they represent. For example, a goetic entity representing a person or animal would be fully susceptible to Life, while a goetic entity representing a ghost would be affected by Death as if it was a ghost.
            This only applies within the Astral Realms, though. Goetic entities summoned to the material realm are just ephemeral entities, so Mind is the go to Arcanum, just like Spirit is for spirits.

            That is not to say spirits are fully immune to Life. Any Life spells not concerned with the living body could potentially work just as well on spirits. (Not that there are many in the core book.) Something like Control Instincts (with the Reach for affecting supernatural subjects) should work since spirits are extremely driven by instincts. Whether or not you'd require adding a dot Spirit depends on how strict the ST is with adding Arcana to spells.

                    In my game I'd probably rule that spirits that have a 'Life' resonance would still be affected by Life magic - stuff like animal spirits or disease spirits and such.
