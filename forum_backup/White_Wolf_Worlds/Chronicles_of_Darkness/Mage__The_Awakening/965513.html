      Ok, a few things first:

      Concentration is a bad Yantra if Bob wants it to last more than a few turns or an Scene at most, as it will end the moment Bob stops concentrating, even if the Duration left was a week or more. If Bob wants it to last for a day, maybe he should paint some Runes with a permanent marker somewhere . The Dedicated tool could or could not be of much use. It can help mitigate Paradox, but may not give him any bonus dice. Difficult to say without knowing what it is.

      1) His Int will never factor in the spellcasting pool and Medicine only if it is a Rote, not a Praxis. Maybe you had those both confused and you meant it to be cast by rote. If a Praxis Bob's Base Dice Pool would be 8-9 (gnosis 2+life3+high speech2+runes1+maybe 1 for the tool). If a rote you have to add Medicine 3 to that for a total of 11-12.

      Even in that last best case scenario, the required Potency of 9 would put Bob at a Chance Die (base Potency of 3 from his dots of Life, 6 additional potency being -12 dice) even before increasing Duration. To increase the Duration to a Day, he'll need Advanced Duration (1 Reach) and a further -2. As he already was at 0 (zero) dice and didn't reach his max cap of -5 dice pool, he could still pull it of.

      2) At that point he could spend Willpower for 3 dice, leaving him with a single, regular die. Just one success (33'33 % chance) and he's cast it.

      3) If a Praxis, he would need 3 Reach (1 for the Advanced Duration, 2 to affect the three Attributes) wich is 2 over his safe casting, so 2 dice of Paradox, wich will be reduced to a Chance Die due to the Dedicated Tool. If a Rote he is within his safe Reach, so none.

          First things first, Bob cannot use concentration, High Speech, and his dedicated tool; his Gnosis of 2 only allows him to use two Yantras, as indicated on the Gnosis chart.

          Spellcasting pool: Gnosis 2 + Life 3 = 5 dice to start,

          Bob has 1 free Reach because the spell Bob hopes to cast is Life 3 and his Life is also 3. If he wants this spell to last one day, that's Duration being modified - and we're in luck, because Honing The Form has Duration as the Primary Spell Factor. Looking at the Duration table in the Reach section, we need to spend one Reach to get us up to Advanced Duration and then take a -2 to the spellcasting pool to have the spell last one day.

          Gnosis 2 + Life 3 - Advanced Duration up to one day (2) = 3 dice for Bob, which is definitely possible!

          Honing the Form increases one Attribute by the Potency of the spell, which is 1 by default; if all of his Physical Attributes are 2 and we want them up to 5, we need to bump that Potency from 1 to 3; the description for Potency as a Spell Factor explains that each +1 to Potency imposes a -2 to the spellcasting roll, so we're now at a -4 penalty because of that.

          Gnosis 2 + Life 3 - Advanced Duration up to one day (2) -increasing Potency to 3 (4) = 0 dice, which doesn't look super great for Bob!

          Now Honing the Form only increases one Attribute by default. By reading the Reach section, we see that Bob can hit multiple Attributes with an expenditure of 2 more Reach. Unfortunately, he's in some trouble; this Reach ability divides our Potency among all the Attributes! Raising all three Physical Attributes (Strength, Dexterity, Stamina) from 2 to 5 requires raising the spell's Potency from 1 to 9! If we remember that the penalty for raising Potency is -2 per, then that's a hefty -16 penalty to the spellcasting roll:

          Gnosis 2 + Life 3 - Advanced Duration up to one day (2) - increasing Potency to 9 (16) = -13 dice. Bob can't even cast the spell now, and even if he could would have to contend with 3 Reach; 2 of those 3 Reach will cause Paradox dice.

          Now Bob is frantically looking to his Yantras to help. If he goes for Concentration and High Speech (each giving a +2 bonus), his spell is still at -9 dice and his Paradox pool would be at 2 dice. If he used his dedicated tool and either Concentration or High Speech, his spellcasting pool would be at -11 dice and his Paradox pool would be at a chance die. If your spellcasting pool is in the negatives, you obviously can't even attempt the roll.

          Bob isn't going to be casting this on himself any time soon. He could always ask someone else to cast it for him, but might look to learn the spell as a Rote in the meantime, which would help him out a little bit; he could use his Medicine 3 as the Rote Skill for some extra dice, but should still probably look into raising his Gnosis for extra spellcasting dice and Yantra slots to use. Trying to cast this as a Praxis isn't doing Bob any favors, because he shouldn't be worrying about getting an Exceptional Success when he's still so far away from even being able to cast the spell. Bob might also look into contenting himself with only increasing one Attribute, as those Potency penalties are slaughtering his poor little dicepool.

                              Originally posted by rtemplar View Post
                              Bob will be using Yantras of concentration, high speech and his dedicated magical tool
                              As noted by others, Bob will need to choose. He can only use two Yantras due to his Gnosis of 2. Concentration is a bad Yantra for such a long spell. Using a Mantra (that is, High Speech) is +2, and a Dedicated Tool provides no direct dice bonus but instead a -2 on Paradox rolls, though I'll assume you're also going to use it as a Path Tool, which will provide a +1.
                              1. how many spellcasting dice do I get
                              Bob's base magical pool is Gnosis+Arcanum, with an additional +3 from Yantras. You can only use one Yantra per turn, Reflexively, and only a number of Yantra per spell based on your Gnosis, which at Gnosis ● and ●● is only 2 Yantra per spell.
                              2. how many successes do I need to pull this stunt off
                              Only one. But that's easier said than done, as I'll get into in a moment (I was actually brought to this thread by atamajakki wanting someone to check her math)
                              3. Paradox dice (if any)
                              Provided you cast it as a ritual, none, though we'll burn that bridge when we get to it.
                              Any other information you think might be handy to know
                              Here is how spellcasting is done:

                              >Step One: Declare Intent
                              "I want to Hone my Form!"
                              >Step Two: Determine Arcanum and Practice
                              Life, Patterning
                              >Step Three: Determine Effect and Cost
                              Raising Attributes, which requires no Mana
                              >Step Four: Determine Withstand Trait
                              None, though if someone really didn't like the idea of being stronger, probably Stamina.
                              >Step Five: Determine Primary Spell Factor
                              Duration, which at 1+[Life-1] is 3. With Standard Duration, this is 3 Turns.
                              >Step Six: Cast the Spell
                              Though first we'll need to math.

                              The Default spell has a Potency of 1, a Duration of 1 turn, a Scale of one person or roughly five feet, a Range of touch (the caster is of course always touching themselves), and has a Casting Time based on Gnosis, which for a Mage with Gnosis ●● is 3 Hours. Additionally, the Spell's Primary Factor is always Potency or Duration. Whichever it is moves an additional [Arcanum-1] steps up the chart.

                              Our Spell So Far:
                              Potency:                               1
                              Duration: 3 turns
                              Scale: 1 subject
                              Range: Self/Bob
                              Casting Time: 3 Hours
                              This clearly isn't what we want it to do, so now we tweak the spell using Reach and Spell Factors.

                              So, let's start with Reach.
                              1 Reach for Advanced Duration, which will shift the time from three turns to one week.
                              2 Reach for effecting all two additional Physical Attributes
                              Additionally, we're going spend 1 Reach to switch the Primary Spell Factor; the benefit of this is that it will make Potency much stronger, making it 1+[Life-1]. It will also bring the Duration back down to the first step on the Advanced Duration chart, which is 1 Scene.
                              You have "Free" Reach equal to the amount your dots in the Arcanum meets or exceeds the level required to cast the spell. The Honing the Form Spell is Life ●●● and Bob has Life ●●●, so he only gets one "Free" Reach. Each Additional Reach (of which we've spent 4 total, 3 over) increases the Paradox risk of the spell by an amount determined by Gnosis, which for Gnosis ●● is one die each.

                              The spell is now as follows:
                              Potency: 3
                              Duration: 1 Scene
                              Scale: 1 subject
                              Range: Self/Bob
                              Casting Time: 3 Hours
                              Paradox: 3 Dice

                              Now for Spell Factors.
                              -2 to increase Duration from One Scene to One Day.
                              A whopping -12 to raise Potency from 3 to the 9 necessary to bring three Attributes from ●● to ●●●●●.
                              This takes us to a total of -14.

                              Our spell is now:
                              Potency: 9
                              Duration: One Day
                              Scale: 1 Subject
                              Range: Self/Bob
                              Casting Time: 3 Hours
                              Paradox: 3 Dice
                              But there's still a problem... Bob is now at -6 to cast this spell, assuming he uses both his Mantra and Path Tool Yantras. Bob is literally incapable of casting this spell as its penalty is greater than -5.

                              Now, if instead of his Tool and instead of using the Spell as a Praxis Bob uses a Rote that uses Medicine, then his Medicine 3 will come into play, bringing the total casting pool up to -4. This is possible, but also ridiculously unlikely. But there's hope. If instead of being greedy, Bob chooses to only increase his Attributes ●● each, then he will save himself -6 in total Spell Factor penalties and be able to cast Honing the Form, as a Rote, Increasing his Strength, Dexterity, and Stamina each from ●● to ●●●● with a total casting pool of two dice. I would suggest using Willpower.
                              As previously stated, it only requires a single success, but the roll itself will come after three hours of meditation and chanting. Alternately, for an additional die of Paradox, you could cast it as an Instant action (though this would still require two turns to use your Yantras). Your original question suggested using a Praxis, but especially when you want to tweak Spell Factors, you want to use a Rote, as Rotes provide quite a few extra dice; 3 more dice in your case, though if you were a member of the Adamantine Arrows (or were using one of the Mysterium Rote Skills of Investigation, Occult, or Survival) you would add +1 to the Rote Yantra.
                              The other benefit of a Rote is that Bob will have "Free" Reach as if he was casting the spell with Life ●●●●●, which would mean that the Paradox is only 1 die (because casting as if you have Life ●●●●● means meeting and exceeding the spell's requirements by 3 dots), or 2 if you cast the spell as an Instant action over the course of two turns.

                              ​Also, atamajakki, to correct your math, you forgot to increase the default Duration by [Life-1], which actually sets it's default to One Week. But since that could be better served elsewhere, I used Reach to shift it, making the total penalty less. All told, you added -6 more than was necessary between raising Duration and Potency.

                              Thorbes You made the same mistake I did in assuming Potency was the default Factor for Honing the Form. Additionally, as Jakki says, you can only use Yantra limited by your Gnosis. At best, [and assuming Rote, not Praxis] Bob can have a base dice pool of 10: Gnosis ●● + Life ●●● + Medicine ●●● + High Speech (2) [+ Willpower (3)]. The rest of your math is correct (aside from the necessary "swap Factors" Reach taking him over the limit). Due to the Yantra limit, he really wouldn't be able to use his Dedicated Tool.

                                          Thorbes I made the same mistake originally. Had to go back and rewrite most of it.

                                          rtemplar Not quite. I'm not sure where you get -4 from, and you aren't keeping track of your Reach (which is how you get the 1 Paradox). You'll need 1 Reach to increase the Duration to Advanced, 1 Reach for making Potency the Primary Factor[1] and 2 Reach for splitting it between an additional two Attributes. Since you're over your "free" Reach by 1, you get 1 Paradox die[2][3].
                                          For the penalties, it's -2 to push Duration up one step on the table, and then -6 to increase Potency by +3.

                                          [1] This is because otherwise going to Advanced Duration would make the spell last one Week, which is more than you want. Additionally, it would cost a total of -12 to get to Potency 6, which is a lot more penalty than Bob can handle; in general it's better to conserve Spell Factor penalties in favour of Reach. Paradox can get super out of hand, but at least you can cast the spell. It's useful to tweak things with Reach when you want to do something different than the default. Generally speaking, if you want a spell to be stronger or last longer than the default, but don't want to change the Primary Factor too much, you should switch them.
                                          [2] This is based on your Gnosis. Paradox is dangerous, but it can be mitigated by spending resources. Mana can lower paradox dice (though even at a Chance die it's still rolled), and you can Contain a Paradox by Contesting the roll with your Wisdom and taking damage equal to the amount of Paradox successes your Wisdom successes cancel out. If there are more Paradox successes than Wisdom successes, you're inflicted with a Paradox Condition, though; never a good thing.
                                          [3] All in all, taking chances with 1 or 2 or even 3 or 4 Paradox die isn't that big a deal, and is something most Mages will be capable of handling through either Containing the Paradox, spending Mana, or both. Unfortunately this becomes less and less viable; By Gnosis ●●●, each Reach over your "free" amount is two dice per Reach, and even at ● and ●● each additional Paradoxical spell is +2 more, so it's definitely not something viable for combat or tense situations; but for situations like this, where you're meditating and performing a ritual, it's worth the minor risk of a success or two on the Paradox roll, provided you're Containing it.
