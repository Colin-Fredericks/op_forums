                                        Despite its name Mage Sight interacts with all senses. If you hear someone, you also hear the Supernal symbols relating to the Arcana you use in your Mage Sight. The problem is just that humans depend mostly on sight so an invisible person who doesn't speak will often not grant you any information at all unless you're focused enough to perceive their foot steps, breathing or odour.
                                        As for the invisibility, you wouldn't get to Clash since it doesn't interrupt any of your senses that would've perceived the hidden person had they not used the spell. And it doesn't really matter for you personally since the outcome of the Clash wouldn't change anything for you.

                                        I also think (but this is only my personal preference) that your sensory inputs needs to be somewhat recognisable (even if you personally don't recognise them) before AMS shows you anything. If you hear a single step your Matter Sight will let you hear the Availability ratings of floor and shoe, but if you hear several steps your Life Sight would start reporting life signs of the life form that is making those steps.
