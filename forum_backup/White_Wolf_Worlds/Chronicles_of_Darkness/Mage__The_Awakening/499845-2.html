                                                    Originally posted by Axelgear View Post

                                                    While I disagree with your second point (a vampire with Majesty/Dominate maxed is quite different from one with Protean/Vigor), the first is immensely true.
                                                    I agree that ability wise, this is correct. But the approach a Hunter will take is going to be similar. Assuming they know what they are hunting:

                                                    For Vampires: Daylight, Fire, Stakes,Object to remove a head (ok, this kills nearly everyone, but still).
                                                    For Werewolves: Silver bullets. Silver knives. Grenades with Silver Shrapnel. Did I mention Silver?
                                                    For Changelings: All the lore discusses this "cold iron" thing...
                                                    For Prometheans: Torches and Pitchforks (w/o the pitchforks), flamethrowers.
                                                    For Spirits/Ghosts: Find bans, find Banes, destroy fetters, exorcise

                                                    Mages are weird. Theoretically, you can kill them just like any normal human, even though they can shoot lightning and stop time. But sometimes you can't. Some might have magic/attainments that allow them to heal ridiculously fast. Some might have armor/damage resistance that makes tanks blush. Some can flash step out of the way of any harm. And those are just some of them.

                                                    That is what makes mages so frustrating to hunt. Short of paradox, their are few limits that exist for what a mage might be able to do. So it is hard to prepare for mages beyond confronting them with lots of sleepers (which raises the whole issue of bystanders), since you don't know what they can do.

                                                    (The only other gamelines I know of that can be similar are Mummyand Geist. Mummies are forces of nature, and hunting them just seems...a bad idea. Geists have their mementos, which if destroyed do weaken them, but that is assuming you can get your hands on one. I would throw demons on this list, but I can't remember if they have any weaknesses/supernatural resistances.)

                                                        I made new Endowment to talk about and making restraining witches possible.

                                                        Magic Warding

                                                        When Hunters get the witch in the confinement of the Safehouse, they need to make sure he will not leave it. Or that magic from the outside will interfere with they safety. This Endowment make sure that spells will be outside of area or locked inside. When used on item touching the witch, it can even close her magic within. Using this Endowment needs marking the warded territory or item with symbols of some kind – destroying the markings, ends the effect immediately.

                                                        Cost: 1 Willpower.

                                                        Action: Extended; each roll represents one minute of marking area or item with symbols. The Endowment may be “rushed,” reducing the time per roll to one turn at the cost of incurring a -1 penalty on the roll. If used on the area, hunter must roll a number of successes equal to the number of square yards the blessing’s protection encompasses. If using it on the item he must accumulate items Size in successes,

                                                        Dice Pool: Resolve + Endowment Merit

                                                        Dramatic Failure: Warded area or item empower magic, not diminish it. Every power used on the target of Endowment gets Endowments Merit bonus to the spellcasting rolls.

                                                        Failure: No progress is made toward warding the area.

                                                        Success: The character makes progress toward warding the area or item from magic.

                                                        Exceptional Success: Penalty to active magic in area or by the character touching item is three times Endowment Merit.
                                                        ​
                                                        At successful usage of Endowment, warded area or item touching witch gives her penalty equal twice the Endowment Merit to all spellcasting and magic activation rolls.

                                                        Suggested Modiﬁers
                                                        +2 Item or area are connected to the idea of restrain – like shackles, prison cell or chain.

                                                        Variants Example: Consecrate shackles marked with cross on inside ( Benedictions )
