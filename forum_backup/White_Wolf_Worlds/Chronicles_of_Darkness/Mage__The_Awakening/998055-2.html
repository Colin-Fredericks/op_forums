                                I would say that the Mirror Dimension is Twilight on the Death Wavelength: it deals with reflections of the material realm and Death Twilight is ghosts as well as epherma reflections of buildings and other objects. It would fit the idea of flexible and easily manipulated "matter" that reflects the real world, allows mortals to walk through it while those in it don't interact with them, and being a state someone could be trapped in while still close to the world.

                                That said, I also agree that if there was a "Space" frequency of Twilight (though given that Twilight seems to correspond to subtle Arcana, it would be wierd) then it likely would be pretty spot on to what the Mirror Dimension is.

                                Also, the Mirror Dimension may be a fun House Rule to reflect how Fuel Arcana are fought: a parrallel realm that Mages jaunt to where they can fight on epic scales without destroying the world or facing paradox. While the Astral and Shadow kinda fit this already, having one in the material realm that is more closely tied to it would be pretty cool.
