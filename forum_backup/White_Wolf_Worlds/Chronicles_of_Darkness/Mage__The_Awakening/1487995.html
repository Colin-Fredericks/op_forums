    Ignorance WAS Bliss - Knowing/Unveiling Spells In Everyday Life

    07-26-2022, 06:06 AM
    Knowing and Unveiling spells are the humble workhorses of the Arcana; you always want the most information possible for the best results. However, knowledge doesn't always bring joy. What Knowing or Unveiling spells do you personally consider to have the highest potential for mundane nightmare fuel?

    Personally, I would be most scarred by Web of Life. I have NO desire to know how many roaches are nearby at a given time. Max-strength Repel would be active at all times.
    Mind spells? I'm already insecure, not much would change.
    Prime "Truth" spells? Might actually help with that insecurity.
    Death? Better to know than live in fear.
    Fate? Every Destiny has a bane.
    Forces? Seems like it would be trippy and kinda fun.
    Matter? Mostly practical, nothing hugely scarring beyond how much fecal matter is in a given item of food
    Space? Seems pretty useful, all things considered.
    Spirit? Esoteric enough to not fill me with mundane terror. Rot/decay spirits or insect spirits might make it a close second though.
    Time? Really bloody useful.

                          Forces could be used to gain a tremorsense comparable to Toph's from Avatar. This would let you detect what is going on around you in much greater detail, which includes violence, any kidnap victims, people being followed or watched, and many more things hidden behind the scenes.

                          With Receiver, they can hear everything going around them. Every rumor and whisper behind their back, the robbers planning a heist in the basement of the room across the street, the mafiosos planning to shoot someone and the corrupt cop taking a bribe.

                          Then they start using Tune In to parse through data transmissions, discovering backroom deals, emails from unknown higher ups going to people like janitors or cashiers, hints of something being setup for the dark web, messages you are sure hide terrible things under code words and all the things everyone around you is searching and talking about.

                          Forces can give a lot of information, almost too much, maybe enough that you would need to multitask with Mind to parse through it all. All of that requiring no mics, listening devices or traceable software (maybe that is how Guardian spooks learn so much about Sanctums without touching anything).

                          But it also shows you how fragile your mundane life is, how so many layers of danger and corruption are much closer to home than expected.

                          Now you know, now you can be prepared. But the people around you don't. Can you even afford to drop those spells, for their sake and yours ? What is your responsibility for having this knowledge? You can't hint how you got it, you were warned about mixing your Shadow and mundane persona, but how can you help without exposing how you got such a detailed amount of data, and will that paint a target on you ?
