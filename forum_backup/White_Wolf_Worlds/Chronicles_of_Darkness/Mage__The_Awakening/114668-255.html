        Charlaquin

I added the part in parenthesis to help you. It should be understood because there is a clear choice between number of subjects and area of effect, but obviously not everyone can see it.


        Satchel
        I'm not sure how Pocket Dimension comes into play. The factor is a choice between number of subjects and area is irrelevant or area of effect where number of subjects is irrelevant. After choosing area of effect, I think a "reading that relies on the predicate that the spell's effect must remain in the area" is natural. Choose number of subjects otherwise. The redundancy issue is not a minor thing. It is a system breaking thing. They should have wrote that spells are either subject or area oriented and use the appropriate column if that is what was wanted. A clear choice counter to that was made when the text says "the factor instead determines how large the area covered by the spell is, applying the spell effect to anyone or anything within".

        If you want to create an area that simulates the effects of a spell on a subject for everyone within the area, subject to discriminating subjects using Fate, you are covered. You are also covered if you want to apply the spell to multiple subjects by choosing number of subjects as a factor to increase by Reach and taking penalties. The "sticky effect" reading prevents spell effect on an area to create a choice between discriminating subjects or not.


        I think an area of effect spell that affects the area rather than subjects that happen to be in the area at the time is pretty intuitive. The nature of the spell then dictates whether the spell's effect is on the area or subjects within the area. Obviously, Without a Trace has no effect on an area directly as areas leave no traces by default. The nature of some spells require the effect work on subjects so you choose between simply casting the spell on multiple subjects instead of multiple times on each subject or cast on an area so subjects within the area are affected by the spell effect with the limitation that the magic works only within the area.

        *Maybe create a thread to discuss the "why it works" because I think the question of "how it works" already has an official answer. The same scenario is going to get the same answer.
