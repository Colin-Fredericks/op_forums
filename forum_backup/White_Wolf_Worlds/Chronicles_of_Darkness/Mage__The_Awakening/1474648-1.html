        One of the advantages of the general neo-platonic approach to magic is that, in opposition to Ascension where your imago needs to accomodate the entire way you understand a thing via the lens of your paradigm, you more or less just need the idea of the thing you're doing/creating/subverting/whatthefuckevering, and nature of reality between the Supernal and the Phenomenal/Fallen (however you go at understanding it) will fill in the blanks. All the things you do to affect your casting of a spell allows more control and more "substantial reality" to your "idea", and if you fail to keep a grasp on your Reach, Paradox fills in some of the blank-

        But again, you don't even need to understand how pressure differentials created by the velocity of air going over a wing shape throws a plane into the air, because a mage's dream of flying is stronger than physics. If you need a cat, you don't need to know how a cat's urinary and digestive tract work, or even, strictly, that a cat needs to poop and pee, any more than you need to know the role of cat in Ancient Egyptian religions or the psychological effect of relationships between cats and humans as it relates to each side of the party.

        You need the idea of a cat, and you need to keep that manifestation away from the non-idea of a cat, and that's it.

                                                        I never scanned anything that came from the projector itself. I look at labels, sure, to confirm the general model; not the specific unit.                                                         There's no high level projector repair system. They're projectors.

                                                        An example that much better deals with what you're talking about are cars. Cars have a VIN, VINs are placed in at least three different places on a car. Why? Because car theft (both whole and parts) is a profitable business. VINs are part of how cars are tracks to ensure they're not stolen, even if you take them apart and put them in other cars. VINs are required to be scanned even over relatively minor repairs by shops, just to cut down on car theft related crimes. So if you magic a car into existence, it will have a VIN (that's part of the ideal of "car"), but unless you do something about it, the VIN is going to be wrong, which is a big problem if it ever gets scanned (which is going to be necessary for things like keeping it licensed and registered).

                                                        These sorts of bureaucratic hoops only exist if there's a reason for them to exist. For the vast majority of consumer products the serial number is probably never scanned after it leaves the factory.

                                                        Oh, and I'll make an ignorant question of MtAw: if quiescence hits at this late time, is it defined if the mage gets paradox then?
                                                        Quiescence is simply the aspect of the Lie that causes normal humans to forget or rationalize magic, and assume mundane explanation, so that they don't question their reality. That's all. This is why Awakening only concerns itself with obvious magic: magic that challenges the Quiescence and until a person can't handle the conflict between what they know they're experiencing and what the Lie is telling them isn't real. As noted Paradox governs what happens in the moment of casting, and Dissonance covers what happens if a Sleeper sees obvious magic after casting.
