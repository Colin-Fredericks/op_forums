    Monks (Perfected Adepts 2e conversion)

    05-19-2022, 11:49 PM
    Perfected Adept
    Monks
    Pages 349-351 of Mage the Awakening First edition for lore*

    Doctrine and Magic

    Parent Path: Obrimos
    Parent Order: Adamantine Arrow
    Prerequisites: Life 2, Forces 1, 2 dots in Brawl, one of the following additional skills at 2 dots or higher: Athletics, Medicine, Intimidation, Survival, Streetwise


    Initiation: The initiate spends a full day cycling between martial arts training, sparing, and meditating on the teachings of the legacy
    Ruling Arcana: Life
    Yantras:Casting in a place of physical training, such as a gym, dojo, or running track (+1); swearing an oath of service or performing another genuine act of humility (+1); fasting for at least a day (+2); discarding something of value or a vanity (+1-3 depending on significance).
    Oblations: Drilling or training in combat techniques, including katas, sword forms, sparring, punching bag work, etc.; feats of endurance, such as running a marathon or long exposure to the elements; successfully meditating (Mage, p. 208) for at least an hour.

    Attainments

    First Attainment: Body Temple ​

    Prerequisites: Initiation
    With an hour of focused meditation, Perfected Adepts are able to enhance their senses. This is the first step in truly mastering the body. This attainment functions as “Heightened Senses” (Life 1, MtAw 148) targeting the monk, with reach for advanced duration.

    Optional: Forces 1
    The meditation does not only need to apply to the health of the body. Those initiates in Forces can learn how to focus on the energy that flows through their bodies, enhancing the body's use of that energy. This attainment functions as “Kinetic Efficiency” (Forces 1, MtAw 141) targeting the monk, with reach for advanced duration.

    Second Attainment: Chi Force

    Prerequisites: Life 2, 3 dots in Brawl
    Meditations now give the monks enhanced control of their own body, allowing them to control and enhance its natural functions. This attainment functions as “Body Control” (Life 2, MtAw 148) targeting the monk, with reach for advanced duration.

    Optional: Forces 2
    Greater control over the body can lead some monks to greater control over the kinetic energy that flows through their body, allowing them to strike with more lethal attacks. This attainment functions as “Kinetic Blow” (Forces 2, MtAw 143)targeting the monk, with reach for advanced duration.

    Third Attainment: Perfect Body

    Prerequisites: Life 3, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 3 or a second option from the list must be raised to 2
    Monks who have reached this level of mastery over their own bodies are able to push their body past its limits through their meditations, removing their physical weaknesses. This attainment functions as “Honing the Form” (Life 3, MtAw 150) targeting the monk, with reach for advanced duration.

    Optional: Forces 3
    At this point of their training the monks can now release the kinetic force of their body through the air. With this Attainment the Adept can strike their foes from a distance. They can treat their Brawl attacks as though they had range increments of Forces dots/2x Forces dots/4x Forces dots. The Kinetic Blows effect of the optional second attainment can be applied to these attacks. If the Perfected Adept has the Fast Spells merit then the attacks are treated as firearms attacks for the purposes of the target's Defense.

    Fourth Attainment: Restoring Form

    Prerequisites: Life 4, 4 dots in Brawl
    Bruises heal and broken bones snap back into place at the will of the monk. This attainment works as “Knit” (Life 3, MtAw 150) targeting the monk, with reach for instant casting and to allow the mage to heal personal tilts such as arm wrack.

    Optional: Forces 4
    The Perfected Adept can unleash an astonishing flurry of blows. The Perfected Adept treats all their Brawl attacks as having the Medium Burst property (MtAw 219).

    Fifth Attainment: Mastery of the Self

    Prerequisites: Life 5, Life 5, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 4 or a second option from the list must be raised to 3 or a third option must be raised to 2
    The Monk’s mastery over their own body allows them to increase the speed at which their body heals. It only takes them a moment of focus to heal wounds they suffer in combat. This attainment functions as “Mend” (Life 4, MtAw 152) targeting the monk, with reach for instant casting. Additionally, by spending a mana they can heal aggravated damage instead of lethal. The mage as such control over their body that they can heal any wound.

    Optional: Forces 5
    The monk can channel the kinetic energy of their body into a destructive force on the world around them, destroying buildings around them. This attainment functions as “Earthquake” (Forces 5, MtAw 147) with reach for instant casting.

    *The Perfected Adepts also have a small write up in “Nameless and Accursed” p. 26.

          I was thinking about the situations that the first Attainment would help with, but Heightened Senses with Reach assigned to advanced duration seems more versatile. It would allow them to sense any sources of poison and detect ambushes. Cleanse the Body would be less useful in combat. Since Purge Illness can be consistently used with Life 2 to solve the problem entirely, it diminishes the aid Cleanse the Body provides.

          I think it could be interesting to move Kinetic Blow to the third optional Attainment, since being able to use the extra Reach to have an equivalent of Stunning Strike sounds pretty fun. In its place, you could use a spell inspired by Control Gravity. This allows the subject to walk on walls or ceilings. Reach would be assigned to advanced duration and you could activate in the same interval as Kinetic Efficiency. With that, you cover most acrobatics short of platforming on leaves and raindrops.

          The fourth Life Attainment could be a variation of Shrink and Grow, allowing the mage to alter the size of any living being. Reach could be assigned to instant use and advanced duration. This allows them to have an even easier time staying close to enemies, gives them some extra Health (since Size affects that) and makes them far more difficult to target with spells. A master of Life would have a size of 5 (base) + 5 (Potency) + 2 (Legacy extra factor dice) = 12. This means all enemies need at least that to affect them or take extra time using something like Warding Gesture (which not everyone will have) or cast with AoE factors, which risks putting themselves and their allies in the crossfire. This way you avoid a functionality overlap between Mend and Knit, while also gaining an ability that is useful both in and out of combat (shrinking has a lot of creative stealth applications).

          The fourth optional Attainment is based on Flurry of Blows, correct ? In that case, utilizing a spell as a basis, I would say this is similar to Gatling Assault, with Reach used for instant use and medium burst autofire. It takes a turn to setup and van be used [Forces] times, but makes it unblockable if you can't defend against firearms.

          The capstone Attainment can be used on both the monk or anyone they touch, by default spell rules.

          The last optional Attainment of Earthquake might be a bit too wide hitting for the self displine approach of the Legacy. An alternative could be to use Turn Momentum with Reach assigned to advanced duration, reflexive use and redirecting objects up to 90 degrees. This allows them to protect themselves plus cabalmates and control some of the chaos around them to their favor.

          Those would be my first thoughts on this conversion, the idea was to synergize the toolkit to create a highly mobile and versatile close range brawler. Hope it helps.
