          Fools Rush in!

          Using Space you can learn about sympathic connections and trace them to find out what is connected to the safe, definitely some way of tracing ppl who might know a thing or two.

          Forces (and maybe a bit of space with Inward/Outward eye) lets you magnify things from a distance, but also scan radio frequency -> xray signal, allowing for a examination of the mundane physical side of things.

          Prime Unveiling to see hide spells and traps, maybe even non-supernal magics (which if you want to play dirty is a real blind side for mages)

          Spirits do tend to know what is going on, since anything of importance to humans is going to generate some resonance, so going to the shadow and finding that the building is possessed by a guardian spirit might be an interesting recon.

          Death also allows travel to the Underworld, where all the dead things go... So if this was a Pharaoh's Tomb and they bricked the builders up in it to seal the secrets of its construction away, then that information died and might still be accessible in the Underworld somewhere...

          Time is the player's friend on this one. Postcognition to see when the traps were installed, and looking into the future to see things like 'if i just walk straight in, what is the most likely thing to go wrong' (i forget the name of the spell). Also Choose the threads, which mechanically works completely differently, but feels very similar.

          Fate to weave a destiny where they succeed?

          As for traps. Honestly i wouldn't go wild. Space Ward to prevent teleporting in/out. Time Ward maybe (to prevent postcognition). A guardian spirit or ghost bound in twilight.

          But then double bluff, with some trinkets and the real target hidden behind a secret door/portal to a pocket dimension/Soul Eye to what remains of an archmage's golden road... And a Prime shielding/Unmaking effect to suppress all magic in that real vault. Letting them walk in but really messing with their ability to use the powers they rely on for getting back out.

          My 2 cent at least.

              A few evil traps come to mind:
              • A temporal spell that keeps a room from collapsing or a bomb from exploding, catches dispel happy cabals.
              • A room that subjects them to Behold the Unthinkable Present, nothing like breaking down a character's will before triggering an alarm.
              • A room with a mix of Memory Hole, Sleep of the Just and Dream Reaching, if the invaders don't remember they were struck by magic, they will starve while resting on the laurels of a "successful mission".
              • Fleshcrafting spell (Shaping of Life) that causes Blinded, Deafened and/or Immobilized.
              • Turrets with thaumium bullets, never get overconfident just because you have Shielding spells. Secondary salvo of siderite bullets, in case they have a skilled Matter mage.
              • A quickcarpet into a pit of poisoned spikes, sometimes all you need is the basics.
              • Teleportation trap, anyone who fails to use the main entrance is sent to the bottom of the Mariana's Trench or, if the owner values information or wants an Obrimos proof solution, dropped into the Ocean of Fragments.
              • Zombies (humanoids and animals) rigged with explosives.
              • The bioengineered enemies from XCOM 2, created by the combination of Transform Life and Temporal Pocket.
              • A variation of the Imposter spell, everyone here is an enemy dedicated to killing you, and you are surrounded, its you or them.

                              I'd go over orathaic's examples in a bit more detail later, but for the moment I'd comment that most of them fall short of the stipulation that the mages find out without going there. Like, a lot of that is analytical magic that would need to be cast on the thing being analysed.

                              I think the most sure fire way mages have of finding out at least the beginnings of information on a subject that they otherwise have no leads on is summoning a Supernal being. I'd say something as specific as this, that deliberate effort has gone into concealing, should warrant an entity of at least Rank 3, (possibly lower if they have well-documented information on a being that might be well specialised towards providing that sort of secret), but it's still a method of divination that can bypass some of the usual limitations of Awakened spell casting.

                              Originally posted by Cauthon View Post
                              Co-Location is never specifically mentioned either way, but I'd probably require Death conjunctive there too if you're try to use it on a location in the Underworld. So... discount Create Avernian Gate at Space 3 + Death 2, I guess?
                              I think the greatest limitation of using a Space spell to cross over like that is the necessity of not only going there, but going there in a manner that forms a sympathetic connection (which requires some degree of intimacy). Or at least something that has been where you wish to go that you can transplant the connection from. I'd think it's generally an inferior option to the Death Making spell, that can alternately let you cross over to what's in your immediate vicinity at will, or has a fairly minor Reach requirement for direct access to even places that one has had incidental contact with.

                              Like, I think most mages well acquainted with the lore of the Underworld think it's a bad idea to become immersed in any part of it well enough to form a sympathetic connection, and other things that are so closely associated are rather uncommon in the world of the living.

                                      Originally posted by Cauthon View Post
                                      I mean... you could borrow the Sympathy from something -likely a ghost
                                      I referred to the capacity to transplant sympathy exactly.

                                      I also referred to the fact that things with sympathy to the Underworld are difficult to come by.

                                      If a ghost has that kind of sympathy, that means that it's in the Underworld. Departing from it is not a simple task, since the whole nature of the Underworld is to ever draw the dead inwards and downwards, descending to the Ocean of Fragments.

                                      I would say there are other complications to acquiring sympathy in such a manner. Maintaining it, for a start; that might entail letting things cross back and forth with some frequency (or else dedicating a lot of spell time to preserving an otherwise fragile connection). Then there's the matter of what it might do to a place to have sympathy with the Underworld, even if it isn't co-located to it.

                                      Some of these probably ought to count as Acts of Hubris, unless the mage has put a lot of effort into understanding the nature of what they're connecting a part of the living world to, never mind what how it might be viewed by other mages. I think Avernian Gates can generally function as more controlled phenomena.

                                      If it's easier (setting aside the challenges of gaining access to necessary sympathy), I'd say it's in the same way that it's easier to mind control somebody into doing something for you, rather than using the lighter touch of emotional urging (or even no direct magic at all). The kind of ease that flows a bit directly into Rampancy or Megalomania.

                                              Anyway, back to processes for information gathering. Depending on how ruthless one is, one of the most direct methods might be to start off with some kind of proxy, bolstering it up a bit, and setting it up with some magic that might transmit information back, and just have it walk in and experience the defences first hand.

                                              Although I think if one is really serious about holding something against mages, they'd need to prepare some kinds of layered defences, something where the first attempt to penetrate or counteract it sets off contingencies, because a lot of physical, technical options are the sort of thing that Ruling spells could handle, and anything predicated on being a spell itself is pretty vulnerable to Prime.

                                              Which could be taken to mean that any investigative methods would be smart to conclude that they're drawing up a list of the most obvious things to deal with, and that they'll need to be prepared to think on their feet as they set off each secondary trap, alarm, or defence.

                                                      I have to say going to the Underworld for recon on a heist is was a bit of a stretch to begin with.

                                                      But if there are good plot reasons to do fit, A) you can go through any Avernian gate or B) you have found something which gives you a clue - maybe a partial blueprint or note from a construction worker who was killed - and that item may have the requisite sympatethic connection*.

                                                      In the former case, you might be using Fate to give you Omens of what to do next, and not really know why you are going to the Underworld, in the latter a co-locate directly to the Underworld is pretty strong.

                                                      Lastly, finding a ghost with a connection to the Underworld may be difficult, but Geists and Reapers are much more free to leave the Underworld and likely to have deeper connections to it

                                                      *If you rip a book in two, both parts will have strong sympatethic connections (initially) if you destroy one part and it becomes a ghost object (castoff), i presume that connection will remain even if it is weaker.

                                                      Alternatively a person who has died might be more likely to leave a ghost, and their living relative might have a connection - but particularly if they are an anchor. However in that case you just need to talk to the ghost anchored in the material world not go to the Underworld.

                                                      Isator Levi
                                                      I'd go over orathaic's examples in a bit more detail later, but for the moment I'd comment that most of them fall short of the stipulation that the mages find out without going there.
                                                      X-raying the building does require going there, just not inside. But it is an Unveiling cast on your self not on the target.

                                                      Other Unveiling spells (Death, Spirit, Prime, maybe Fate or Space) would help greatly once your sensory range has been increases.

                                                      Or going to the Shadow and peaking through the gauntlet... Again, you do have to go there in some sense (and hope they haven't out up defences in the Shadow/made the gauntlet harder to pierce...)

                                                      As an alternative to summoning a supernal being, you could summon a goetia (or just plain go to the astral**) relevant to investigations, and send it in to investigate - though you may also need to give it Prime sight as they can't see spells on their own.

                                                      **The Astral will contain information that any two human have shared. So with enough effort you might be able to find the right temenotic realm... Unless the defenders has killed all the ppl who knew. In which case refer to the Underworld part of the conversation.​
