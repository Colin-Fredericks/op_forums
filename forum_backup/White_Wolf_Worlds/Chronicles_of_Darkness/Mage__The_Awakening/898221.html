    This one is pretty straightforward. Post any Nameless Orders you've made or translated for Second Edition. I'll start with one of mine.

    The Epistolary Society of Boundless Inquiry

    Mages have always been a little more well-traveled than their Sleeper counterparts due to the opportunities created by magic. However, such means were always somewhat cumbersome, and limited to those with access to the Space Arcanum. It was only with the advent of widespead postal services that long-distance correspondence became a popular means of sharing ideas among reclusive scholars. In the same vein as the later epistolary circles of Howard Phillips Lovecraft and his associates, mages with little interest in joining established orders but still craving colleagues with whom to share their love of the Mysteries began to converse through letters.

    The practice expanded to solitaries and apostates of all sorts throughout the United States and Western Europe, and the original writers grew into adepts and masters of the Art. Soon they had more to share such as grimoires and imbued items, and dissertations on their long years of experience with Artifacts and ruins from across national borders. The two Epistolary Societies were separated by an ocean until the telegraph and telephone emerged, and soon messages were being exchanged across the Atlantic too.

    The Society grows with every advance in communication and transportation, and its scattered archives and instructional materials are more accessible than ever. They have, unbeknownst to most, become a legitimate Order in their own right - a decentralized, horizontally organized one with strong values of secrecy and security against outsiders, and limitless development and exchange from within. Left-Handed practices and personal Legacies are being allowed to bloom and spread as casting times and bandwidth permit.

    Order Tools: Cloaks, masks, secret communications, and writing utensils.

    Initiation
    1: Investigation (Cryptography)
    2: High Speech
    3: Rote Specialties (Academics, Investigation, Occult)
    4: Library (Occult) 3
    5: Grimoire 3

              A 2E Conversion of the Elemental Masteries.

              Elemental Masteries
              As old as the Diamond Orders and the Seers, and perhaps predating either if some sects are to be believed, the Elemental Masteries are one of the most widespread of the Nameless Orders. The ancients who founded them had a very different understanding of the way the universe works. To them, abstract concepts could not be divorced from material being. It wasn’t that elemental fire, for example, symbolized inspiration and courage. Fire literally was inspiration and courage. They were the same thing. If you saw a fire, you weren’t looking at an embodiment of courage and inspiration, anger and destruction, you were looking directly at those things. It wasn’t that elemental stone symbolized fertility, craftsmanship and creation. Stone was those things. The Elemental Legacies still teach this. By extension, they say, all things were all other things. Everything is part of a vast unity, a totality. Everything, every person, animal, object, thought and concept is part of the whole.

              Order Tools: Resonant Environments (raises their Yantra bonus by +1), Symbols of the Classical Elements

              (o) -- A Mage of the Elemental Masteries begins is first taught how to use the language of the universe to lend power into her spells, and receives the High Speech Merit for free. Most other initiates find the curse of Quiescence peeling away from their eyes, granting them the Sleepwalker Merit.

              (oo) -- As the initiate advances through the teachings of the Elemental Masteries, he learns to make himself more receptive to their secrets. He gains a dot in the Meditative Mind Merit.

              (ooo) -- While the Pentacle and the Seers tend to focus on mudras in their approaches to the Art, the Elemental Masteries emphasize the power that comes from personifying the elements. Mages gain either a dot in Occult or two dots in the Shadow Name Merit. By this point, they may already be approached by any of the Tamer Legacies for further instruction. Sleepwalkers who reach this level of initiation are instead counted on to serve as aides and minor leaders within the Order. They gain a dot in Occult, or two dots in the Retainer Merit instead.

              (oooo) -- Whether Awakened or Sleepwalker, an initiate who reaches this level of understanding gains greater insights into the inner truths of the universe. This is reflected by him receiving three dots in the Astral Adept Merit. Awakened practitioners who are are already Astral Adepts may instead develop three Rote specialties based on their primary element, while Sleepwalkers can gain the Banner-Bearer Merit at three dots. This tier is the highest which the latter can ever hope to attain.

              (ooooo) -- Only the Wise occupy the highest positions within the Order, and those who do reach this have learned at least the fourth Attainment within a Tamer Legacy. True Elemental Masters receive a soul stone that belonged to a deceased member of their Legacy. And whenever they use it to target a member of the Order at Sympathetic Range, they raise their effective Sympathetic Link with them by a number of steps equal to half their Status dots, rounded up. This is crucial to the Elemental Masteries' ability to coordinate together despite being separated by vast distances.

                  The Whispered Hand is relatively young for a Nameless Order, and those they encounter often mistake them for idealistic Arrows or technophilic Libertines; their true origin is far less likely to earn them friends.

                  There's an aging contingent at the higher echelons of the Iron Pyramid that still reject or fear the ever-evolving tools of the modern era, and only the Panopticon and upstart Pantechnicon seem to truly grasp their real potential. A small number of these technocrats saw the tide turn against them during the Arab Spring and rejected the chains they revered, breaking away to form a bright new Order that understands the magical potential of social media. For the Whispered Hand, the invisible network that binds people together across the gulf of space has more inherent worth than dusty tomes or gilded artifacts.

                  The Hand's founding cabals are primarily focused in North Africa (primarily Egypt, where they keep a watchful eye on the new government), but they're reaching out across the globe, hoping to overthrow oppressive systems with the twin powers of the Supernal and the wireless. Of particular interest to them is China, where overwhelming censorship smacks of Seer manipulation.

                  Order tools: smartphones, graffiti, symbols of anonymity and unity. As embarrassing as it may be, many in the Whispered Hand adopt the cartoonish masks adopted by Western anarchists and hacktivists.

                  1: A Socialize or Computers specialty in (Social Media).
                  2: A dot of the Contacts merits (Local Activists), reflecting Sleepers they hope to forge into tools of revolution.
                  3: One dot of the Computers skill. No Hand worth her salt gets by without a little bit of technical know-how.
                  4: A three dot Safe Place, reflecting a nearby safe house with its own source of electricity and internet access. The Whispered Hand understands that people too often 'disappear' when they make their voices heard.
                  5: Two dots of Occultation and one dot of the Shadow Name merit, reflecting a mystical persona they've grown that exists as an online presence that only seems to reach the right ears. The mage is difficult to pin down, but is on her way to become a symbol for rebellion.

                  The Whispered Hand has been lucky so far, and their defection isn't known to the wider Iron Pyramid - yet. There are still incredibly bitter Panopticon and Pantechnicon loyalists in the nations that experienced the Arab Spring, and they would love nothing more than to bring the souls of these breakaways to their patron Exarchs in blazing chains. Potentially angering Seer factions further abroad will get them into trouble they aren't armed to defend themselves from (et alone overthrow) but they've yet to make political inroads with the Pentacle that they need to survive. Such negotiation might not be possible should their origins come to light.

                                      I don't have it written up fully, but I did fun translate the final fantasy version of summoners. They're both a nameless order and a legacy which (attainment wise) is mechanically mostly taken from wyrdhamster's conversion of the Choir of Hashmallim crosschecked with the summonses book. I tinkered with the early attainments, but the whole idea is that Summoners are the ground level version of the Alienated. Summoners begin by earning a familiar, based on their Path. Each summoner makes pilgrimage to various sarira-like idols in order to commune with specific supernal beings with the desire to make a pact with them (also limited by Path). I tried to keep it with the feeling of an ancient Atlanta order while also being considered too wreckers and perhaps presumptuous to stand along with the other orders.

                                      Storyline wise, the nameless order is small. After a conflict long ago, the Summoner's order was officially considered left handed. Summoners try to maintain their lore, but over thousands of years, the true origins of the order and the mysterious primal entities they commune with are lost. there are legends--the mighty Ifrit is said to have been a harsh warlord who'd sacrificed his men but just as easily die himself for an idea, for example. But because summoners are already rare, and the Trials of the summoned monsters are harsh, there is very little firsthand knowledge amongst them, and almost all of it comes from the words of but a single Eidolon. While all ten of the eidolon are known by many names, not all have an active summoner to share their knowledge. indeed, some eidolon dream in sarira-temples lost in the mortal world and accessible only via the astral.

                                      A summoner's path often begins by goetic or animistic summoning and binding, swindling goblins into alliances, understanding angels, or necromancy. If another summoner mentors them, or if their soul connects to the summoner's path, they will be contacted by a familiar--an Obrimos is contacted by the thoughtforms of the holy familiar Carbuncle, acanthi are met with by the quiet and omnipresent Mogri, etc. This familiar is their envoy to the dreaming gods. Once they are initiated into the third attainment, they can challenge an Eidolon for the right to summon an avatar. Each has its own associated symbology, and no Summoner can summon a supernal Esper outside of their path unless they have Order Status 5 or are an archmaster.


                                      The benefits and drawbacks of an Avatar are why the legacy-order is considered left handed. while the summonses are quick to point out that an avatar is a mere ochemata reflected through the summoner, many conservative mages (especially Mysterious and Guardians) find the idea of such regular exposure of the supernal to the fallen as blasphemous. And they aren't wrong. avatars suffer when exposed to sleepers (and thus often react violently to them) and avatars do harm to sleeper souls. but the benefits are just as clear, as each Eidolon is vastly aware and full of lore, in addition to the infinite reach applied to spells cast by supernal beings. Even in their days first mastering a weak avatar, summoners can sacrifice dice pool for free reach. It is said that by the fourth and fifth legacy attainments that the summonses gain sudden and startling power. to this day, the mysterious still refuses to support any local summoners, referencing long forgotten wars in which summonses unleashed godly avatars that cost more lives than they were worth to finally contain them. the legendary wyrm king Bahamut is whispered to have single handedly destroyed a cityscape of magi.

                                      The order has a big secret that is the true reason it is considered illegal--like the seers of the throne, the division between archsummoner and summoned is not upheld. not only do the small handul of archsummoners directly train and aide their students, they also begin to teach them the basic cosmology of the imperial mysteries. They understand the world-beings of greater gods, they understand ochema, they understand the war of ascension and pax arcana. they are not taught the extent of imperial practices, nor involved in the petty vendettas or the invisible masters. they are not even taught how to reach the second horizon. however, there has never been an archsummoner who did not understand exactly what they were getting themselves into.

                                      Archmasters find themselves becoming friends and allies of the alien eidolon. they seek the lost and apocryphal bound and other esters. they all are Alienated, and speak of a Warring Triad.... three man made rank 9 beings, according to the lore, as an experiment in the time before. the three or four archsummoners active today whispered that oracles or exarchs created the warring triad as an experiment. as part of the omens of their numenon. because all say that the warring triad was killed by the exarchs, after the triad did the dirty work in the titanomachy, three ultimate summons that the exarchs unleashed to defeat the old gods..... if that is true, perhaps the warring triad are not dead, but instead a part of the massive tyranate.

                                      But that's just what a Mogri told me, and he was drunk on redcap bloodwine. summonses don't really exist, much less supernal gods tied to humans or a warring triad of superweapons employed during the titanomachy...

                                                          Written for the Kingdom of Yamatai Dark Era I work on from time to time.

                                                          The Daughters of the Sun (o to ooooo)
                                                          Prerequisites: Sleepwalker or Awakened
                                                          Effect: You are one of the few women who have been granted the honor to serve Himiko as a handmaiden and disciple. While your newfound position is heavy with risk and responsibility, you stand to gain much as you work to earn the Sun Queen's favor.

                                                          (o) -- You have been named a Daughter of the Sun, and are now training to navigate the politics of Himiko's court. You gain the one-dot version Trained Observer.

                                                          (oo) -- As a Sun Priestess, you are taught various subjects such as languages, etiquette, history and warfare. You gain either a free Specialty to one of the following Skills (Academics, Occult, Politics, Expression, or Socialize), or the Area of Expertise Merit tied to an appropriate Specialty.

                                                          (ooo) -- You are a true initiate of Himiko, and have learned to discern supernatural influence whenever it manifests within your sight. You gain the Eye for the Strange Merit. Awakened members can choose instead to take the Keen Periphery or Supernal Taxonomy Merit.

                                                          (oooo) -- You have spent much time within the Sun Queen's Astral Realm, and have learned how to access it without the need for a place of power. You gain the Astral Adept Merit. This is the highest level of initiation for most Sleepwalkers.

                                                          (ooooo) -- You are one of Himiko's favored servants, and it is likely that she will choose you to be her successor if you prove yourself worthy. Until then, the prestige of your position gives greater weight to your words and actions. You gain three dots to allocate between Inspiring, Cohesive Unit, and Small Unit Tactics.
