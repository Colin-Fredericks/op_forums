        Seed of The Wyrd (Fate ••••• ••)
        • Practice: Entities
        • Primary Factor: Potency
        • Cost: 4 Mana
        • Withstand: Composure
        • Example Quintessences: A gift from an Old One, the blessing of a local freehold, proof of outsmarting a goblin marketeer, paying off the debt of a goblin queen

        By weaving aspects of the Wyrd itself into a subject's pattern, the Hedge becomes receptive to them in the same way it might be to fae beings. After the casting of the spell, the character is treated as having Wyrd 1 and Clarity 3 for the purpose of navigating the hedge, hedgespinning, and otherwise imposing their will or goals on the Hedge, and are treated like a Changeling for the sake of any limitations. Note that the effective Wyrd does not apply to anything else, including Supernatural Tolerance or Potency. These changes are Lasting.

        +1 Reach: The spell's potency can be split between additional effective Wyrd and additional effective Clarity, up to the normal maximums

                    How's this one look?I hope nobody else has written up such a spell already.

                    Dynamic Rote (Any Arcanum ••••• •)
                    • Practice: Dynamics
                    • Primary Factor: Duration
                    • Cost: 2 Mana
                    • Withstand: Total Arcanum dots of all Arcana used in the spell being scribed (Dynamic Arcana default to 5 for this total)
                    • Example Quintessences: The first draft of a published work, the last remaining copy of a published work, an editied version of a published work that was never printed.

                    By weaving the practice of Dynamics into a Rote as it is formed (this spell must be cast during the use of the Create Rote Attainment), the Mage can create a truly wondrous Rote which can adapt to the circumstances as needed.
                    While incapable of creating Rotes for the Imperial Practices, these dynamic Rotes can modify their Rote Skills and Practices within their original Arcana as required.
                    This Dynamism will only extend however to such Arcana as the Archmaster has achieved the Practice of Dynamics with, all other Arcana included in the spell will not be able to be modified in application.

                    For example, an Archmaster who has achieved Dynamics with Prime could forge a dynamic Eidolon Rote which could transform into any other Prime Rote.
                    If the Archmaster also had Mastery in Mind, then this original Rote could also include the potential for the creation of a Psyche within this Eidolon, as well as any other Prime creation the Archmage creates as they dynamically shift their Prime-Arcana. However at this point the 'Mind' element could not be changed, meaning it could only be used to create a Psyche within whatever Prime effect the dynamic rote creates.
                    However if the Archmaster had achieved Dynamics with Mind, then the Rote could shift both its expression of Prime and Mind to any form it desires.

                    While the Rote's creation will be Lasting, the Rote's dynamism will only last for the duration of the spell. When the spell ends, the Rote will remain in whatever form it is at that time, and any attempts to copy the Rote into a Grimoire will 'merely' create whatever Rote it is at that point (unless a further use of Dynamics is made upon the Scribed Grimoire).

                    +1 Reach: If the creator is Mad, then the Rote may shift across any Arcana so long as it involves their 'specialist' Practice

                    Optional Arcana (Any ••••• •): The Rote may be created to accomodate the ability to include any other Dynamic Arcana the Archmaster knows, including Arcana not used in the initial form of the spell (however these will contribute to Withstand).

                    Optional Arcana (Prime •): The Archmaster may immediately create a Grimoire containing his Dynamic spell, rather than imbuing the Rote he has learned with Dynamism

                                        A Prime spell, this time it is Dominions. Some people might argue that this is Excision, which is definitely a possibility. In the old edition, it is definitely excision. I picked Dominions, because mages' connection to the Supernal always seems to relate to the soul and the astral, which is a world of its own. Given that it is an assault directly against a mage's supernatural being, it departs from the normal creative thaumaturgy guidelines and is Withstood by Gnosis.

                                        Annihilate Supernality
                                        • Practice: Dominions
                                        • Primary Factor: Potency
                                        • Withstand: Gnosis
                                        • Cost: 6 Mana, 5 Arcane Beats
                                        • Example Quintessences: ???

                                        One of the most feared tools of the Imperial Masters. This spell inverts and destroys the alterations made to a mage’s soul when they Awakened, effectively un-Awakening them. This spell removes the mage template from the subject. The subject becomes a Sleepwalker. Any mage-only merits are refunded through Sanctity of Merits. The archmaster must have Prime at 8 dots and have at least the same amount of dots as the target in the appropriate Path Arcana in order to undo the Awakening. Otherwise, this spell can only disrupt an Awakening in progress. This spell cannot undo an archmage’s Threshold Seeking.

                                        +2 Reach: The internal metaphysics of the target are so thoroughly annihilated that the subject becomes a Sleeper again. The entirety of their time as a mage is immediately affected by Quiessence, and without proper precautions, will cause the target to become irrevocably insane.

                                                        I have updated the Annihilate Supernality spell as suggested. In the meantime, here are two more Prime spells. This also brings into question exactly how an Excision spell should affect targets. I've been designing the spells with the idea that Excision just straight up removes the relation of a target to the Arcanum, where as Entities can alter it (including reducing it), but never completely removing it. As an example, consider the difference between this spell and the Enlightenment spell I posted earlier.

                                                        Sever Arcanum
                                                        • Practice: Excision
                                                        • Primary Factor: Potency
                                                        • Withstand: Subject’s dots in Arcanum
                                                        • Cost: 4 Mana, 2 Arcane Beats
                                                        • Example Quintessences: ???

                                                        This spell removes the ability of a target to use an Arcanum. The archmaster must possess both Prime and the desired Arcanum at 7 dots. The subject loses all dots and Attainments in the Arcanum, and may never learn or utilize the Arcanum again. The subject cannot make use of imbued items or artifacts incorporating the Arcanum, and if a Prime spell grants them Mage Sight in the Arcanum, they cannot perceive the Supernal through that Arcanum.

                                                        Awaken
                                                        • Practice: Entities
                                                        • Primary Factor: Potency
                                                        • Withstand: Resolve
                                                        • Cost: 4 Mana, 2 Arcane Beats
                                                        • Example Quintessences: A piece of a Watchtower, the soul of the subject from an alternate timeline where they Awakened

                                                        The spell that all lesser mages wish they could have, but many archmasters resent having access to. By means of this spell, the archmaster Awakens a Sleeper to the full truth and power of magic. This subject of this spell immediately undergoes an Awakening. Once the Awakening begins, the ultimate fate of the subject is out of the archmaster’s control. The subject’s Path, what they do in the Supernal, and whether or not they actually manage to successfully Awaken or merely become a Sleepwalker are all beyond the reach of this spell. Use of this spell is one of the most dangerous and blatant violations of the Pax Arcanum: it almost always produces a Banisher, and attracts the attention of the Exarchs. With extensive preparation, it might be possible to reduce these dangers.

                                                        Add An Arcanum ••••• ••: The archmaster may add up to three Arcana to the spell, granting these as the Ruling and Inferior Arcana for the new mage. These must be valid combinations of Arcana according to Path. For one Reach, the combination may be anything. If the archmaster does not add three Arcana, the Supernal chooses the rest.

                                                        +1 Reach: The archmaster can at least guarantee that the subject will Awaken and become a mage.

                                                        +1 Reach: The archmaster may target Sleepwalkers with this spell, giving them a second chance at Awakening.
