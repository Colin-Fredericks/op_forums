                    As a rule, I have limited interest in antagonist sourcebooks and even less in the horror element of the CoD, so I usually buy Night Horrors books only out of a completist drive, if ever. However, 2e rules for Wisdom 0 and Abyssal mages fill a important hole in the updated system and setting, so I'll give this book a substantially higher priority than its siblings. I care little for the Tremere, but my huge interest for immortal characters makes me very eager for any additional material I can get and use for Liche mages, as long as hopefully the gameline doesn't go out of its way in the infamous Blood Treachery sense to make them unplayable or take a 'bad wrong fun' approach to transhumanist players who have no time or patience for the pro-mortality bias of mage society, except perhaps as a prejudice plot-hook to be defied and circumvented in play. But I'm moderately hopeful this won't happen and Liche PC in practice shall suffer no worse playing penalty than a bad reputation and a taboo against getting political status in Awakened society, the stuff we more or less already know and which I can totally live with.

                    On an loosely related note, however, I cannot but notice and lament a much more important and useful book, Signs of Sorcery AKA the de facto player's guide/second half of the corebook's magic system chapter, has been languishing for 26 months in development and counting. Three months ago they told us it was almost done, 93,000-out-of-97,000 words developed, and the last bit was proving tricky. Sadly, I guess the last 4,000 words have been exceptionally tricky, and I'm kind of curious what it is holding back everything else so much. Frankly, this huge delay makes me exceedingly skeptical any MtAw book of any sort is actually going to make real progress in the production pipeline until I see SoS clearing development hell. Although I am seriously interested in Fallen Worlds and to a lesser degree Tome of the Pentacle too.

As I said, if things stand for Liche protagonists as indicated from available info, and they are playable short of having a bad reputation and being unable to get political status in Awakened society just as Archmages, I'm satisfied.
