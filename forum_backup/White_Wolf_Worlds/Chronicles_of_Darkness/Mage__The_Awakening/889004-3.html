    Originally posted by JayTee View Post
    Another Acanthus trick:

    Lucky Mana Reactor: At chargen, set your Gnosis at 3 by spending all your bonus points on it. Then set your highest Arcanum to Fate 3 and pick Exceptional Luck as a Praxes. Gnosis 3 + Fate 3 = 6 dice, or, on average 3 successes. Since it's a Praxes, you'll be considered rolling an exceptional success on your spell casting roll with three successes. One of the options on a exceptional success is to not only regain all mana spent, but to gain one additional point of mana. Considering it's omni-applicability you'll be casting it a lot, and with that extra Reach you'll be casting it quickly. This means on average you'll be generating more mana than you put in to it.
    On d10's you're not going to average 3 successes on 6 dice. You'd be averaging 3 successes between 9 and 10 dice. Granted, you could be spending willpower on every one of these rolls but then you're just trading Willpower for mana.

                                If you're just inflicting tilts via hexes, then RAW the answer is no, they can't just 'heal' them because the resolution condition for both Arm and Leg Wrack specifies that this only works if the tilt is inflicted as the result of an attack.

                                This makes a certain amount of sense; when you think about the fictional/mythical curses that hexes represent, they don't tend to be "limb breaks", but crippling pain in the limb or it going numb and unresponsive. That's how I would present it to a player; there's no physical injury, but it's too painful/numb to get use out of it.

                                That said, I'm pretty sure a Werewolf in Gauru specifically recovers from Arm and Leg Wrack tilts every turn, so that effect should still work?

                                    Technically, the only way to block you from attacking someone sympathetically if you've got the Crown of Vision is if it gets suppressed by Veil Sympathy or a similar effect - destroying the link means you have no connection to them, so Crown of Vision gives you a Weak connection to them.

                                    The only drawback (besides being at the beck and call of a hateful and tyrannical being - since you're a Seer of the Throne, though, that's what we call a perk) is that you can't improve your fake Weak connection (since it only exists for the purposes of determining whether or not you can use the sympathetic range attainment).
