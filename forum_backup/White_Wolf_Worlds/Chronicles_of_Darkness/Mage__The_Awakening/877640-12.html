            Hello, long time lurker, first time poster here, I need a bit of help refining some spells for my NPCs:

            for the first one, for my Acanthus adamantine arrow i was thinking something like:

            Just a flesh wound (Mind **)
            Practice: Ruling
            Primary factor: Potency
            Withstand: Composure
            Suggested rote skills: Expression, stealth, subterfuge

            Mechanics:
            The target of this ability believes that his attacks have the opposite outcomes. Attacks that strike the target seem like they missed, attacks that missed seem like they hit. Enveloping all the senses, someone swinging, and hitting with a sword p.e., wouldn't feel the impact of the blow nor hear the cry of his target, but instead would "see" the defender dodging out of the way. Similary, someone throwing a fireball, and missing, would instead see, and smell, his opponent being chared to "really wounded, but not quite dead" state. The actual attack is resolved normaly despite the perception of it (so you swing a sword and hit, you think you missed, opponent seems healthy, but in reality he does take, and note the damage and/or assosiated penalties)

            This spell doesn't fool the one actually getting attacked (the target of the attack still knows, and feels, the full burn of the attack)
            This spell is not limited to "attack roll" actions, but instead is applied to everything the "attacker" thinks is an attack (so even social slander and etc, or sensory spells)

            +1 reach: The caster can now reflexivly decide if an attack looks like a miss or a hit.
            +1 reach: This spell fools even the one getting attacked (The ST should keep his life total a secret)

            I'm not certain if ruling is strong enough for this, would also love to be able to simplify the writing of the spell (english not my native language) but have a bit of a problem doing so. Lastly, opinions on the actual spell (power level, flavor, utility, etc are always welcome.

            In my mind, this is a spell best utilized by a high defence/resist character so as to appear to keep taking damage and keep the enemies attention ("we almost have him!" and "he hits like a truck!") while keeping the focus away from his weaker allies (" those weaklings keep dodging but at least they don't do any damage!")
            with the reach option that even the one taking the damage is fooled, it becomes quite deadly, since one doesn't know when to retreat, defend, and overcommits to a fight till he falls

                                Safety Between (Spirit ••••)

                                Practice: Patterning
                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                Suggested Rote Skills: Science, Larceny, Empathy
                                Withstand: Gauntlet Strength

                                The subject becomes wrapped into the very fabric of the Gauntlet, existing neither in the material world nor the Shadow. When inside the Gauntlet, the subject is kept in stasis until the spell ends. This means the subject cannot take any actions or be further affected by Conditions or Tilts it is currently suffering from.

                                When the spell ends or the mage wishes to dispel it as usual, the subject reappears where they entered the Gauntlet. If a large object is in the way when they reappear, the subject will end up Knocked Down at the objects closest edge from the exit point.

                                +1 Reach: the mage can choose that the subject doesn’t have to reappear on the side it entered.
                                +2 Reach: the subject can take mental actions like thinking and casting spells. These spells however will first take effect when the subject leaves the Gauntlet, but Paradoxes can still occur. For 1 Mana, the subject may take physical actions at a -2 penalty and move around at a speed equal to their Gnosis within the Gauntlet. If the subject move, it will reappear where it has walked to.

                                                Constantinople (Fate** Space**)
                                                Primary factor: Duration
                                                Cost: 1 mana

                                                Named after the legend of the founding of Constantinople, this spell allows one to find the perfect location for the task at hand. The caster sets the general parameters (nothing TOO specific) and fate takes care of the rest. Mages still argue if the spell slightly changes fate to accommodate the caster, or if it simply finds the perfect thread of fate to guide him.

                                                For the duration of the spell, as long as the targets of the spell are within the area of effect, they gain +Potency to non-magical skills pertaining to the requested task.

                                                Examples:
                                                "What is the best hiding place in this building" with an personal area of effect, might point out that one closet in the next hallway that the guard won't bother to check (+stealth rolls)

                                                "Where is the best place In the city to defend ourselves from our pursuers? " with an area of effect equal to a room, might point out to a specific room in an abandoned factory, where, if the targets fight in there, they will have +potency on their non-magical rolls for the combat

                                                "Where can I find the seer headquarters? " is too specific, but "Where can I find some seers in the city" might point out to a park where some seers will be present at some time, granting +potency to perception and/or investigation rolls.

                                                And etc

                                                (Effectively, instead of a normal, unconditional skill boost, you pigeonhole yourself to a specific task and location, that, furthermore is NOT chosen by you, but on the flipside, you get a boost to ALL relevant skill checks, AND you might gain some relevant clues from the actual location you are sent)

                                                    Spirit Marriage (Spirit 0000)
                                                    Practice: Patterning
                                                    Primary Factor: Duration
                                                    Withstood: Rank
                                                    Suggested Rote Skills: Politics, Persuasion, Socialize

                                                    This spell invites a spirit to possess a mage for a while, lending the mage its powers and capabilities. The mage essentially becomes a living fetish for the spirit in question. This has a few benefits and a few drawbacks for the duration.
                                                    First, the spirit's attributes boost those of the mage. For each Potency of the spell the mage may" borrow" an attribute dot from the spirit's Power, Finesse or Resistance to his own attributes in this category. For example, a point drawn from Resistance may boost Resolve, Stamina or Composure.
                                                    Secondly, the mage gains access to the numina of the spirit and may use these as if he were a living fetish, using the spirit's attributes (which are diminished if the mage had elected to borrow its attributes).
                                                    Third, the mage gains access to the Influences of the spirit, and may fuel them with Essence and/or Mana.

                                                    This spell may not be applied to any familiar since these are already bound in a special way to the mage.

                                                    Drawback: The spirit possessing the mage is neither slave nor servant no matter what the mage thinks. It will be an opportunistic parasite at best and a dangerous lunatic or troublemaker at worst. Any time the spirit sees an opportunity to strengthen its resonance it will try to wrest control from the mage. For example, a fire spirit wants to ignite flamable materials while a greed spirit wants to gain wealth and power no matter if it's legal or moral. The mage may try to appease the spirit with a social roll. If this fails, the mage will have to win a Clash of Wills versus the spirit which will cause the spirit to immediately eject from the body of the mage. If the spirit wins it gains control of the mage for the duration of the spell.
                                                    Also, the mage will have a slight change of appearance depending on what type of spirit he marries with. If it is a fire spirit he may gain uncanny glowing eyes and / or may smell of phosphorous or exhude ash with every breath. If he is possessed by a spirit of greed he might gain a strange green hue to his eyes or lick his lips a little too often (or maybe don a top hat). This effect will be noticeable after a while to sleepers, and nearly obvious to other shamans, werewolves and characters with high Occult ratings.

                                                    +1 Reach: the spirit is effectively enslaved for the duration of the spell, nullifying all of its potential of possessing the mage. The spirit is still not willing and will surely not appreciate this treatment.
                                                    +1 Reach: the mage can optionally change his appearance radically, taking the form of the spirit materialized.

                                                        Shadow Golem (Death ****)
                                                        Practice: Patterning
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Expression, Occult

                                                        The mage constructs a moving construct out of shadows, mimicking the capacity of Matter Adepts to create golems. The Potency of this spell is divided between the Durability and Retainer rating of the Golem. The golem is size 5 or lower, as the mage sees fit. The Shadow Golem is incapable of combat and has no Defense rating. It´s Structure will determine its Health.
                                                        The shadow golem is in no way capable of decisión-making and can only obey short and simple commands given by the mage.

                                                        +1 Reach: The mage may install a ghost to act as a guiding intelligence for the golem, which also grants it the ability to use Numina. This will also offer the Golem a basic Defence rating equal to the Potency of the spell.
                                                        +1 Reach: The mage may make the golem capable of defence, granting a Defence rating equal to the Potency of the spell.

                                                        Add Spirit ****: The mage installs a Spirit as a guiding intelligence, granting the Golem the capacity to use Numina.
                                                        Add Mind *****: The mage installs a synthetic mind as a crude guiding intelligence.
