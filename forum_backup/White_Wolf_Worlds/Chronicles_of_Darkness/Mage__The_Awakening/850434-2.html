                Yeah. I'm not saying I'm looking for a highly functional, flawless Consilium, but rather an archetypal one. As in, there's an alliance between the Council of Free Assemblies and the Diamond, there's a Hierarch, Mages haven't broken off ties in favor of lawlessness or paranoia (yet), etcetera.

                Anyway, I'm getting some ideas for conflict.
                • Tensions between the Pentacle Mages and the Apostates who live in the city. Presumably they are also attracted by the same Mystery that whets the appetites of Pentacle Mages, but for their own reasons. Thematically it does tie into the diversity proposed for Toronto, while also engaging in the tensions that arise from having people from very different places/perspectives living side by side. There's also probably conflict between the Diamond and the Council on what to do with Apostates that cause trouble and the like.
                • The Chalice Compact. I'm thinking less that the Tremere secretly run the Consilium and more that they have a few plants in the various Caucuses. Should it be a secret that the Chalice Compact even exists, or should it be one of those things that's a rumor, a strawman you use against people you don't like and a well-founded fear?


                Do those sound like good ways to complicate the Consilium? Do you guys have any additional suggestions?

                                    So I was mulling it over randomly today, and I had one possible idea for a big Mystery that multiple groups might be interested in.

                                    What if, in Toronto, there are certain places where, if the proper pattern of behavior or activity is observed, cracks open in reality, that allow instantaneous transportation to random places? It could be another part of Toronto, or it could be another city in the world, like Baghdad, or it could be another realm, like the Shadow, or even weirder places, like Atlantean ruins. The problem is, these places where you can attempt to open these cracks in reality seem to appear in totally random places for limited time frames, and few Mages seem to understand how to make it open up to the place you want to go.

                                    Part of the problem is that it's a two-way street. You open up a crack to the Underworld, you can go there, but whatever is on the other side can come through, too. If it opens to Dubai, you can go there, but whoever is in Dubai can go back too. Mages from around the world might be interested in figuring out how to make it work both in Toronto and where these cracks open up, and groups like the Seers and the Pentacle might be very interested in controlling a means of effortlessly accessing anywhere from different cities to different timelines.

                                    They might conceptualize it as a device, though I'm thinking that it's part of the metaphor (or possibly a crazy theory) that it is a device that does this.

                                    What do you guys think of that?

                                    My inspiration is that I think portals to anywhere are cool, a versatile storytelling device, a means of accessing a lot of different and interesting locations, and the Legacy called the Reality Stalkers, who might see Toronto as the place where their legacy's philosophy and actual capability is expressed most powerfully. Might give me an excuse to update them to the 2nd Edition.

                                    I'm totally open to feedback that might tighten or focus this concept, too, if anyone thinks there are ways to improve it.
