    Bit of a query; I recal from somewhere (not sure where) that Astral travel was always supposed to be a bit of a thing when it came to the spread of the Orders and the connections between Mages around the world, and I always liked the idea too. Mages being naturally inquisitive types, it totally makes sense to me that a Mage in Japan and a Mage in, say, South Africa might meet on an Astral journey, communicate, perhaps share the investigation of a mystery together on the Astral plane, and also share some mystical knowledge that then feeds back and forth. They might even be in there investigating the same mystery feeding into the physical world in their respective locales! This wouldn't happen all the time but it probably would ease the spreading of ideas and concepts through Mage society because, at the end of the day, they all have this ability to explore the Astral and while in there the physical distances between them are irrelevant.
    It was a common practice in the pre-Sundering era so I imagine it did stick around. The biggest problem is entering a Temenotic realm held by another culture. I imagine, it's not that you can't go there, but that you have to be able to make the right mental associations to pull it off which means you could have a Japanese Mage and a Nordic Mage both visiting the realm of the Sea, and be completely missing each other by accident.

            The Silver Ladder is not the Path to Victory because they conquer all other Mages, the Silver Ladder is the Path to Victory because they lead other Mages. The real ideal of the Silver Ladder isn't making other Mages serve you through fear (the Lion) or them allowing your weak leadership in lieu of true direction (the Stag) but to display wisdom, knowledge and strength so that others look to you for leadership and guidance (the Sage).

            The common perception amongst many fans about what the Ladder does is kind of misrepresented, I think. They don't rock up to places and say "we're in charge now!" They rock up to places and talk to people, take part in the community, provide systems of law and social understanding, and through actually dedicating themselves to the study of what makes a good leader, they provide leadership and guidance to others. Remember, the Silver Ladder's Atlantean Shadow Name doesn't invoke the Kings and Queens of Atlantis, it invokes the Priests, Judges and Viziers, those honoured for their wisdom, knowledge and just edicts.

            Whether the Silver Ladder has ever actually had an element of monarchism in terms of yearning for the return of the ancient Atlantean Kings and Queens or whatever, I forget. Is that something to do with the Oracles? Regardless, the Ladder is not made up of all conquering imperialist Mages. It's made up of idealists, really.

                        What I kind of like though is that even though the Ladder sort of see the Free Council as being vaguely parochial and lacking respect, and the Council see the Ladder as being vaguely stuffy and arrogant, the Pentacle definitely isn't an alliance of convenience these days and they do actually share a lot of viewpoints that could practically translate into working together.

                        The cultural magic they are interested in they come at from different angles, but at the end of the day, they're both looking for pretty much the same ideal; that magic to be freed from the Lie. Of all the Orders/Sects, they are the two most opposed to the Seers who are the enforcers of the Lie, of all the Orders/Sects, they are the most interested in actually improving the lot of Sleepers (because both of them believe, in general, that the freer mortals are from the most brutal and crushing aspects of the Lie, the more likely they are to seek the Truth), of all the Orders/Sects, they are the ones most interested in actually giving Mages a voice and a way of resolving issues in society. They disagree on how to do that but they both agree, more than the others, that such a system should definitely be there.

                        The core ideals of the Silver Ladder and the Free Council differ in rhetoric substantially, and they ostensibly differ quite a bit in reality too, but in reality, I found that in our games, Free Council Mages and Silver Ladder Mages often found their viewpoint on any particular subject aligning, especially when it came to matters of how Mages should act towards Sleepers etc. That was quite cool, and I dunno if it's intentional or if I'm just reading into it too much, but I do like that what are on the surface the two most opposed elements of the Pentacle actually might find themselves agreeing in practise on a lot of policy.
