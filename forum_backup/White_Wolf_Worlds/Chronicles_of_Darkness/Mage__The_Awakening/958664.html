    Two Legacies - Seeking opinions!

    08-27-2016, 06:16 AM
    Well, so I just created 2 Legacies for my players on a mixed game of Chronicles of Darkness where we have 2 Vampires, 2 Mages, 2 Werewolves and 1 Hunter.
    So the Mages I designed the Legacies for are:
    Ignis, the Mysterium Obrimos with a Spirit Familiar is basically going around meddling with Spirits and Magic, trying to make the world a better place. [Onmyoudou]
    Manimemos, the Guardian of the Veil Mastigos is focusing on Life magic to change his body, like becoming other people and making gross manipulation of its body (clawed three limb beast with 100 eyes being his goal). [Yee Naagloshii]

    Opinions, criticism, changes?

      Yee Naagloshii
      (shifters)

      Origins
      Parentage: Mastigos, Guardians of the Veil.
      Background: The original Naagloshii were more akin to Demons than Humans. Capable of shifting forms, they were able to infiltrate human settlements and consume them from within. In time, the name was passed down to a sect of Native American tribal Willworkers who used the Arcana of Life and Mind to emulate their inner Demons to better understand and defeat them. Founded by Mastigoi Mages focused on the mastery of the Self (both Physical and Mental), it evolved into a Legacy of transformation and adaptation, to environments both wild and urban.
      Appearance: Shifters usually dress themselves in subdued colors, the better to avoid attention. Some, the more traditionalists, will adorn their bodies with tribal markings or use some sort of tribal adornments.

      Doctrine
      Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Life 2, Mind 2, Medicine 2, Empathy 2.
      Initiation: A prospective Yee Naagloshii must prove their aptitude with both their Inner and Outer selves before they are allowed to know the secrets of the Legacy. This usually takes the form of a ritualistic combat occurring simultaneously in the Fallen World and the Astral Planes.

      Magic
      Ruling Arcanum: Life
      Yantras: Blood (freshly drawn from oneself inflicting 1 point of Bashing damage, +1); Totem (+1); Breaking a finger (inflicting 1 point of Lethal damage, +2); Eating raw flesh (+1); Tasting the blood of the target of a Spell (+2).
      Oblations: Stalking a target; ritually killing an animal; studying a mark; exercising the body or mind; meditating on one’s breathing rhythm.

      Attainments
      First: Purity of Two Forms
      Prerequisites: Initiation
      The first thing a Shifter learns is how to control its body. By replicating the effects of the Spell “Cleanse the Body”, a Shifter can better survive in inhospitable conditions by scavenging without having to worry about poisons and toxins.
      Optional: Mind 1
      Knowing is an important tool. Being able to recall details can sometimes mean the difference between life and death, or between a perfect disguise or a flawed attempt. This reproduces the effects of the “Perfect Recall” Spell.

      Second: The Hunter’s Gait
      Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Life 2, Medicine 3
      Focusing one’s control over its physical form, a Naagloshii can spend a scene meditating to regulate its breathing, heartbeat, metabolism, reflexes, scent and even regeneration as per the effects of “Body Control” with Advanced Duration.
      Optional: Mind 2
      Another application of the Hunter’s Gait is the ability to move unseen… or rather, “unfelt”. At the end of the scene of meditation a Shifter can move through crowds without fear of recognition, as if under the effect of the Spell “Incognito Presence” with reach applied to Advanced Duration.

      Third: Another Self
      Prerequisites: Gnosis 4, Life 3. Medicine 3, Empathy 3
      To a Shifter one’s body is as readily malleable as clay. As per the Spell “Many Faces” a Shifter can alter its own body as it wishes, gaining the ability to shift its Physical Attributes and/or its physical traits (height, weight, sex, hair color, skin tone, etc.). If the Naagloshii tastes the blood of a subject, it can faithfully copy its physical appearance.
      Optional: Mind 3
      Once a Naagloshii has taken a bit of the essence of a mark it can reproduce the target’s mentality more faithfully. This gives access to the memories as if the Spell “Read the Depths” has been cast on the target.

      Fourth: Shifting Forms
      Prerequisites: Gnosis 6, Life 4, Medicine 4, Empathy 3.
      Cleansing one’s body. Controling it. Changing its form. All of these skills lead to the ability to reshape itself. While not yet inherently able to Shapeshift like the Demons of yore, a Naagloshii can now adapt by giving itself new characteristics as by the effects of the Spell “Transform Life” with Advanced Duration.
      Optional: Mind 4
      The ability to gain the characteristics of another being is of no use if one does not have the Skills to better use it. And so, as the Naagloshii can shift the form of its body it can also reshape its Mind reproducing the effects of the “Gain Skill” Spell.

      Fifth: Skin-Walker
      Prerequisites: Gnosis 8, Life 5, Medicine 4, Empathy 4.
      Finally in touch with its inner Demons and able to form its body to reflect such, the pinnacle of a Naagloshii’s ability is reflected by the “Shapechanging” Spell with reach dedicated to Instant Casting and to retain consciousness without risk of losing oneself to the mind of the new shape.
      Optional: Mind 5
      The Mind of a Naagloshii is as mutable as its body and can be made to expand to fulfill whatever task it sets itself upon. This Attainment reproduces the effects of the Spell “Augment Mind” with Reach applied to Instant Casting, and two more Attributes

          Onmyōdō

          Origins
          Parentage: Obrimos, Adamantine Arrow or Mysterium.
          Background: Originating from Japan, this Legacy focused on the averting the many dangers that endangered the country and the Emperor, as well as recording the passing of time, studying the natural world and astronomy. Many centuries later, the Legacy has spread throughout the whole world, still clinging to its ancestral trappings and mystical symbology of Yin-Yang and the Five Elements, keeping alive their mission to protect the Fallen World from the Shadow, recording and cataloging the inhabitants of the Shadow.

          Appearance: Onmyouji come from different walks of life but most have had some sort of interaction with Eastern Occultism. Most Onmyouji will carry with them Fūda, Mala, the Tai-Chi or a pentagram representing the Five Elements.

          Doctrine
          Prerequisites: Spirit 2, Academics 1, Crafts 1, Occult 2 and Sciences 1.
          Initiation: The prospective Onmyouji must pass three tests which will quiz him on his knowledge about Spirits, the way of Yin-Yang, the Five Elements and the natural world.

          Magic
          Ruling Arcanum: Spirit
          Yantras: Reciting a Mantra relevant to the Spell (instant action, +2); using a mala (+1); using a fūda with a relevant prayer as a focus (+1, +2 if the fūda is burnt); using a Mudra relevant to the Spell (+1); wearing traditional Onmyouji vestments (+1);
          Oblations: Chanting the Mantras while sitting seiza; preparing fūda; exorcising a place/person; finding constellations; writing down information about the Shadow.

          Attainments
          First: Lifting the Veil
          Prerequisites: Initiation
          To be able to deal with Spirits, one must first be able to see them. This Attainment reproduces the effects of the Spell “Exorcist’s Eye” becoming able to see their presence in the Fallen World. Optional: Prime 1
          The Mage may also become aware of spiritual Loci as per the effects of the Spell “Sacred Geometry”.

          Second: Sensing the Currents
          Prerequisites: Spirit 2, Occult 3
          By looking at a Spirit, the Mage can easily glean information about it, making it easier to command, subdue or parlay with. This reproduces the Spell “Know Spirit”, allowing for as much information as the Mage’s Spirit dots.
          Optional: Prime 2
          Oft times the Onmyouji will find themselves facing other willworkers or the results of their art. This replicates the effects of the Spell “Supernal Vision” with Reach assigned to Instant Casting and sensing the nature of other Supernaturals.

          Third: Raising the Walls
          Prerequisites: Spirit 3. In addition to the Occult 3 requirement from the previous Attainment, the Onmyouji must increase the other Skills she used to meet the requirements of the Legacy to 2.
          To protect the Fallen World means protecting its people and places from the influence of the Shadow. An Onmyouji is well aware of that fact and as such Barriers are of second nature to her. By carefully redoing the Feng Shui of a room or building, or reciting the correct mantras and endowing the correct talismans upon a person, an Onmyouji can assure that no Spirit Magic can be cast within its Kekkai. This duplicates the Spell “Ephemeral Shield” with Reach dedicated against Spirit’s physical attacks and Advanced Duration.
          Optional: Prime 3
          Sometimes, more worrisome than the powers of the Shadow is the powers of other practitioners. This replicates the effect of the “Wards and Signs” Prime Spell with reach assigned to Advanced Duration and Advanced Potency.

          Fourth: Undoing the Bindings
          Prerequisites: Spirit 4, Occult 4 and the other Skill requirements of the previous Attainment.
          Sometimes a Spirit on this side of the Gauntlet is but a nuisance. Other times they may prove themselves troublesome or even perilous. It is up to the Onmyouji to undo the harm that they wreath unto the world. By spending a scene in ritual, writing fūdas and aligning the Feng Shui of the room an Onmyouji can protect it from Spirits by making it so that they cannot Manifest. This replicates the effects of the “Erode Resonance” Spell with reach applied to Advanced Duration.
          Optional: Prime 4
          A wise Onmyouji knows that spells can be as much of a problem as Spirits, and as such must be prepared to undo curses laid upon the people. With enough preparation and time, even the mightiest curses can be laid bare. This attainment works as the Spell “Dispel Magic” with reach dedicated to making the dispelation Lasting.

          Fifth: Fūda
          Prerequisites: Spirit 5. In addition to the Occult 4 requirement from the previous Attainment, the Onmyouji must increase the other Skills to 3.
          Sometimes the Spirits cannot be appeased, or they cannot be simply banished. When one of the inhabitants of the Shadow becomes too unruly, it’s up to the Onmyouji to get rid of it by any means. The mark of a Yin-Yang Master is to take the bad and turn it into good. By the means of this Attainment an Onmyouji can take the remnants of a Spirit it has subdued and craft a Fetish for future use. These usually take the form of Fūda that allow the Onmyouji access to some of the Spirit’s Influences, as per the Spell “Craft Fetish” with Advanced Duration.
          Optional: Prime 5
          Additionally, a skilled Onmyouji knows when to store and when to release the powers of a Spirit in a Fetish into a more useable form. By taking measures to create a suitable form out of the energies of the World, the Onmyouji creates a construct which a Spirit may inhabit by placing its Fetish inside, as per the Spell “Create Eidolon” with reach assigned to Advanced Duration.

          Also, a Merit adapted from the Lu Merit of the Taoist from Magical Traditions

          Merit: Senji Ryakketsu (● to ●●●●●)
          Prerequisites: Onmyouji ●●●, Occult ●●●, Academics ●●
          Oft times the Onmyouji might be faced with situations to which she wasn’t able to properly prepare. An Onmyouji with access to the Summary to Judgements of Divinations is able to summon a Shikigami, a small Spirit that can perform one single task. The amount of Spirits in the copy of the Senji Ryakketsu, their maximum Rank and the complexity of the tasks they can perform are related to the dots in the merit, as follows:

          Dots Nº of Spirits Rank Complexity of Task
          ● 2 ● Doesn’t require Dots on a Skill;
          ●● 5
          ●●● 9 ●● Requires one Dot on a Skill
          ●●●● 18
          ●●●●● 36 ●●● Requires up to three Dots on a Skill;
          The Storyteller decides what services an Onmyouji can obtain from the Senji Ryakketsu. Typical feats for the greater Shikigami include luck, protection from various hazards or curing disease.
