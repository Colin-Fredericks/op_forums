                                                          So a starting character has 10 merit dots and 6 Arcana dots, at least 3 of which must be in their Ruling Arcana. So just follow that as a basis. First you set their starting Gnosis based on the expected power level, which cannot be increased at character creation. Next you give an amount of normal XP that cannot be used to purchase Arcana, then give Arcana dots on top of that. Purchasing above your Gnosis limit costs two dots instead of one. That would mean the rules for generating mages of each tier could look something like:

                                                          Trained in the basics: Same as standard character creation (hereafter referred to as CC)
                                                          Basic field experience: Gnosis 2 + CC + 2 Arcana dots + 3 XP
                                                          Skilled magus: Gnosis 3 + CC + 4 Arcana dots + 5 XP
                                                          Seasoned sorcerer: Gnosis 4 + CC + 5 Arcana dots + 8 XP
                                                          Knowledgeable master: Gnosis 5 + CC + 7 Arcana dots + 10 XP

                                                          The idea for a new mage remains mostly the same. Those with a bit more experience can be double Disciples with two dots in something else or broaden their horizons further, with 3 mundane XP being used to show they are acquiring skills or building contacts, but are not quite there yet. When the third tier is reached, you can be a double Adept in your Ruling Arcana or an Adept in a non-Ruling one, depending on how you go about it. The XP value is adjusted to account for continuous progress.

                                                          When Gnosis 4 is reached, something interesting happens your Arcana cap does not go up. So in this case the Arcana dot progression is less than the previous tier jump, but the XP boost is higher, signifying the mage focusing on the skills, knowledge and resources that make them all the more effective in their specialties. Lastly, tier 5 is a powerful boost to showcase the culmination of all the hard work that leads to mastery.

                                                          I think that should help give them the freedom to create anything between very specialized characters and jacks of all trades. Hope it helps !
