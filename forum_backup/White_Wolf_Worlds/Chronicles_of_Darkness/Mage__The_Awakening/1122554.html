    Tremere 2e Homebrew

    07-29-2017, 09:02 PM
    I am working on a 2e version of the Tremere, based on what's in the 1st edition core book, Left-Hand Path, and the scant few notes about the actual 2e rules that have been released.

    The document is here. I absolutely consider this a rough draft, nowhere near ready for playtesting yet. I am mostly looking for comments on the practicality and balance of the rules. I expect the idea to go through several revisions, potentially bordering on complete rewrites.

    In short: The Tremere harvest souls to fuel their immortality and strange powers. Instead of Gnosis, they have Hollow. Hollow is much more variable than Gnosis, and while Tremere at Hollow 10 are very powerful, they can be consumed by the the living void of the Final Watchtower if they are not careful. Tremere liches manage their power in cycles, ramping up their connection to the Watchtower over a few years (or months) to perform powerful feats of magic with their Call of the Void ability, before going into the mystical sleep of death to regain some sense of self.

    The powers are based off of what's in 1e, plus my own inspiration. I've explicitly moved away from the Tremere being a Death-based legacy with these rules. Instead, my idea is to characterize the Tremere as eternal searchers seeking to achieve complete dominion over all five subtle Arcana.

          Currently I have this regarding losing Hollow: "If the lich would not have enough Hollow to access Hollow Attainments or Hollow Praxes, then they do not lose the abilities themselves; they just lose access until their Hollow increases enough again.                       These are good points, and are exactly what I'm looking for in feedback. The idea for the Thrax was inspired by the Cloud Infinite Legacy in Left-Hand Path, which is similar in a way, although probably with less bookkeeping. Ideally I'd prefer to keep bookkeeping to a minimum. Honestly, if I was the ST in that situation, I'd just make up stats on the fly when the Tremere player requested them. I will try to think of ways to keep the "legion feel," but with less bookkeeping. At most I would want a Thrax player to keep track of the number of souls they have, and maybe their relative strengths, on a broad level (e.g. "I have 5 strong souls, 2 mediocre ones, and 3 weak ones").

                      As for souls and the death of the owners, I'm not sure this is addressed even in first edition. House Heo Sel has a similar situation with Crafts from the Grim Hall. If they can use the soul's owner's Skill with their created tool, what happens when the owner dies? I'm inclined to say that even if the soul's owner is dead, the relevant dots persist--at least for some time. Perhaps it starts to decay after a while?

                                          Originally posted by Dark Archon View Post
                                          From what I have read I have trouble understanding why would Tremere reduce their Hollow.

                                          Currently, there is a sort of magical "safe number" for Hollow around 6 or 7 where it's fairly powerful but also not very dangerous. At 8 and then again at 10 it becomes more dangerous, but only if the Call of the Void Yantra is used. The main driver for Call of the Void's danger is that you get beats when using it at high Hollow. I would like to resolve this safe area problem somehow. I'm thinking about detaching a lot of the scaling benefit from Call of the Void, so that it's just a regular +1 to +3 Yantra, and then shifting the benefits of high Hollow elsewhere. Problems: I don't know where to shift it, and it doesn't really solve the safe space problem (if anything, it makes it worse).

                                          Much of the danger from high Hollow is also supposed to be story-based. The local Consilium will probably take notice once a bunch of souls start going missing when the high-Hollow Tremere is slurping them up by the bucketload.
