                            As to why certain types of people are on higher or lower rungs of the Ladder, according to orthodox thearchs:

                            It's about the responsibility of the enlightened to give guidance to those less enlightened. That's why it goes mage-priest-technocrat-manager-bully-famous-follower-monster.

                            Clavigers tell student thearchs not to apply these classifications on face value (is a musician influential because she's a truly excellent composer-songwriter, or because she win pop idol? Is a businessman an actual good manager dedicated to his employee's interests, or a bullying blowhard? Is a priest just mouthing doctrine, or does she genuinely tens to spiritual needs of her followers?)

                            And they're especially not supposed to judge other mages by these standards. Although they totally do, leading to things like the stereotypically one-sided relationships between Ladder, Council, and Arrow. Common wisdom is that the Ladder have more regard for libertines than Arrows because of where they would fall on the Ladder if they were Sleepers, despite the Arrow liking them way more back than the Libertines do.
