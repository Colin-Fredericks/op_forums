                                1 - The Monad, the everything, the Unity

                                3 - The trinity, building on the symbolism of the Father
                                4- Visible nature, the four corners of the world, aka Space, the Eye

                                Perhaps the other numbers could be deduced based on it.
                                The big trouble there is that, from the admittedly limited resources I looked at, even 5-7 alone seem to have focal connotations that are in some ways opposite to the Exarchs, and the Prophet and the Ruin specifically have the odd syzygy of focusing on methods of control that seem more appropriate to each other's Arcanum. Still, offhand…

                                5 - Change, unpredictability, the journey through the senses, the Raptor
                                6 - Equilibrium, the bridge between the microcosm and the macrocosm, the Nemesis
                                7 - Cycles of the universe, aka Fate, the Ruin
                                8 - Infinity and cause and effect, aka Time, the Prophet
                                9 - Foundation, completion, the cusp of a new beginning, the Psychopomp
                                10 - End state, new baseline, the physical kingdom in the Tree of Life, the Chancellor

                                Some of these are just derived from what the m.o. of the Exarch in question corrupts, and I won't pretend there isn't also a simplistic reason for assigning all of them except 5 (which is in some ways given to the Raptor by process of elimination).

                                It's also worth remembering that the Seers' standpoint on who the Archigenitors are is part of the larger fact that the contents of the Supernal as we encounter it from the perspective of non-transcendent beings are only ever eternal at any given moment and the Exarchs in particular are fairly unstable magical identities when it comes to existing on their own — they don't do what the other Sects have done and shore up the Primal symbolism of the Ministries that serve them, they basically consume their Ministers, and regardless of how effective it is, their servants' primary means of discerning their will without direct orders is to use nonmagical fortunetelling and other methods indistinguishable from superstition, and those same servants take it as an article of selfish faith that their service will be rewarded by Ascension to the ranks of lesser Exarchs.

                                All of which is to say that the numerology of the Archigenitors and the Iron Seals exists for the Seers, who are mages who can leverage any symbolism they can recognize, and whose Order-Tools-cum-Patron-Tools just need to invoke the Exarch in question, which is likely going to be a little more overt than e.g. putting an eye on a square to scry on somebody.

                                                    As listed, the example spells are assumed to be things you can use as rotes (with the recommended rote mundras) so a Mind spell to increase either a mental or social attribute, or a Life spell to increase any physical attribute is one spell to learn as a rote (either of them would be a single rote...).
