        Ruptor Cantaminis (o to ooo, Style)
        Prerequisites:         Countermagic Stance, Wits 3
        Effect: You are highly profficient in a style of combat magic built around skill with the Counterspell Attainment. The name itself roughly means Spellbreaker in Latin, and practitioners are indeed capable of crippling their rivals' ability to cast their most powerful magic.

        Oculus (o) -- When countering the spells of another Mage, you are able to gain a measure of insight on whatever imago you are attempting to disrupt. Whenever you invoke the Counterspell Attainment, you may reflexively roll Wits + Gnosis vs. Gnosis + Composure. Each success in excess of the opposing character's allows you to learn one of the following information on the spell: Practice, Primary Factor, Spell Factors, Reach effects, and Withstand rating.

        Signum (oo) -- A successful Counterspell normally disrupts whatever magic is being cast by your opponent. However, you are able to strengthen this effect to further hinder a Mage in using a specific spell. When you succeed on a Counterspell roll, you may spend a Willpower point to inflict the Imago Retorta Condition keyed to the target spell for the scene.

        Repletio (ooo) -- Mages must exercise caution in how they utilize their reserves of Mana, as it is integral to performing the most potent of spells. Your successful uses of the Counterspell Attainment allow you to replenish some of your energy by sapping it from the spells you disrupt. On a successful Counterspell roll, you gain half the Mana spent on the countered spell, rounded up.

        ---=---
        New Condition: Imago Retorta
        Your character's ability to visualize a particular Imago is hobbled. Each time he attempts to use the associated spell, he suffers a dice penalty equal to the inflicting Mage's dots in the spell's primary Arcanum.
        Resolution: The mage successfully casts the affected spell.
        Beat: N/A.
        ---=---

                                                    Added some extra fluff to amechra's Brutal Sorcery Merit. Critique is welcome for this.

                                                    -=-

                                                    Brutal Sorcery [Style; • - •••]
                                                    Prerequisites: Stamina •••, Resolve •••
                                                    Effect: By inflicting harm upon yourself, you can strengthen your magic in a manner that may overwhelm even an Awakened of greater skill in the Art.

                                                    Agony Ascendant (•) -- Magic always has a price, and you pay that cost in blood in order to weave your spells more effectively. You may treat scouring your Pattern as a Yantra, with a bonus equal to your dots in this merit.

                                                    Pain to Perfection (••) -- In the face of adversity, you find yourself instilled with the will to push forward. You may choose to take an additional level of Resistant Lethal damage when scouring your pattern - if you do, you gain one Willpower.

                                                    Beauty in Screams (•••) -- Pain is unavoidable, and that which cannot be healed through magic allows your spells to inflict the same. As long as you are suffering from Resistant Lethal damage, all damage dealt by your spells is also Resistant.
