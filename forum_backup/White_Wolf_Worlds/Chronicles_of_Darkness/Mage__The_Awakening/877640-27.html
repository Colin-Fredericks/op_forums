        Body Control (Life ••) halves the natural healing time per Potency during the spell's Duration. Bashing heals every 15 minutes, which is equal to 300 turns. At Potency 8 the spell would heal 1 Bashing every turn. Each Potency above would double the amount of Bashing healed each turn. Two Reach and Potency 15 would start healing one Lethal (and over 100 Bashing) per turn.
        The amount of Potency required to heal one point per turn is really not manageable for most Mages. Especially not Lethal damage. I crunched some numbers quickly and I don't think a Mage can get above 18 Potency on this spell (at Gnosis 10 and then only having a single die left for the casting) without teamwork or preparation spells that grants bonus dice on casting rolls.

        Your best bet is convincing your ST to allow a Reach on Knit or Mend that spreads out Potency over a number of turns (until all Potency is spent), but that effect requires the Fate 2 attainment to deactivate a combi-cast Dispellation (to suppress the effect of the spell until wanted) when damaged instead of immediately starting to heal the subject (assuming you want it as a preparation spell).

        So, technically yes, but not in a way that is particularly useful.

        Edit: I forgot about Primary Factors. 20 is the highest Potency I calculated, but I doubt anyone under Gnosis 6 will realistically be able to heal Lethal each turn.

                    Added something I posted on here:

                    Disperse Wounds (Death •••)
                    Practice: Fraying
                    Primary Factor: Potency
                    Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Survival, Empathy
                    The necromancer calls forth entropy, but he may unwind it for those he favors. For each level of Potency, the mage may convert one point of lethal damage the subject suffers into two points of bashing damage. This spell does not work against lethal damage upgraded from accumulated bashing damage, including accumulation from this very spell. So, if a subject with 8 levels of Health had suffered 4 lethal damage, this spell may convert them into 1 lethal damage and 6 bashing damage at Potency 3, or 8 bashing damage at Potency 4. If that same subject had suffered 5 lethal damage instead, even at Potency 5 this spell would still leave him at 2 lethal damage and 6 bashing damage.
                    +1 Reach: The spell instead converts one point of lethal damage into one point of bashing damage per level of Potency.
                    +1 Reach: For one point of Mana, the spell instead converts one point of aggravated damage into two points of lethal damage instead. The limit concerning damage accumulation applies as normal.
                    Add Life ••: The spell also heals any Tilts connected to the Health box affected, even if the box is technically uncleared and thus the Tilt should remain.
                    Add Time ••: The spell may also heal scars and physical Conditions related to the healed wounds. With the Temporal Sympathy Attainment, the spell may force persistent Conditions caused from past injuries into transient Conditions, so that they naturally heal over time.

                                    Kali's Caress (Life ••••)
                                    Practice: Patterning
                                    Primary Factor: Duration
                                    Cost: 1 Mana
                                    Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Occult, Animal Ken

                                    With a gesture the mage grants the target the innate capacity to recover from wounds.
                                    The spell applies the Recovering tilt to the target

                                    Spend 1 additional Mana and +2 Reach: The spell applies the Regenerating tilt instead.



                                    Recovering
                                    Your character's pain diminishes on its own at remarkable rates. Cuts close, and bruises vanish in moments.
                                    Effect: Every turn your character heals one bashing damage
                                    Causing the Tilt: This tilt is caused by spells or other supernatural effects
                                    Ending the Tilt: You heal all bashing damage

                                    Regenerating
                                    Even significant wounds close rapidly and bones click into place and mend at miraculous speeds.
                                    Effect: Every turn your character downgrades one point of lethal damage to bashing. If you have no lethal damage, you heal one bashing damage
                                    Causing the Tilt: This Tilt is caused by spells or other supernatural effects
                                    Ending the Tilt: You all lethal and bashing damage.



                                    Go figure, Kali's domain is regeneration too
                                    I wasn't sure whether to make those things Tilts or Conditions. So thar we go, healing over time. Figured if you can set someone on fire for damage over time, why not some healing over time too.

                                    Ending the tilts by being fully healed (to the extent they can heal) should neatly sidestep the "I cast regeneration with duration of 1 year" potential problem I think.
