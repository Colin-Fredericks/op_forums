                                            If that's the case then my suggestion isn't what you are after, but to clarify what I was aiming at was a city where the Mages aren't part of a global conspiracy, where they where concerned only with their own and as far as the Chinese (or any other group) was concerned the Irish better stay on their side of the street.                                             ​If what you want is the standard Consillium but with a complication then give them something they don't agree on, a mystery that the Arrow see as a threat, the Guardians think should be quarantined, the Mysterium want to study and the Council intend to share, all while the Ladder thinks it rightfully belongs to them. Make it something that CLEARLY isn't Atlantian nor obviously of the Abyss, something of obvious value that may just up end the whole Atlantis story.
                                            ​Another alternative, a city where the Seers are dominant and they have found a way to almost completely stifle awakenings. It's a city where nothing major seems to happen because the powers that be stomp down anything potentially disruptive before it can happen. Treat the Consillium like an underground criminal organization fighting against a tyrannical government, taking every new Awakened as a vital gift, while they desperately search for the secret of how the Seers did it.

                                                    An Order isn't just a hobby or a political alliance; it (usually) reflects a profound belief in the nature of the cosmos and one's purpose within it.
