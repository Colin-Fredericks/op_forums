                                        I think it would be beneficial to also read the expanded details on teamwork casting in Signs of Sorcery page 55. But to summarize how every caster is affected:

                                        * Secondary participants with no dots in the Arcana used: can be used as environmental yantra

                                        * Secondary participants with some dots in the Arcana used, but not enough to cast the spell: Roll Gnosis-3 and add successes as a dice bonus to the leader. If everyone has Egregore, they just roll Gnosis.

                                        * Secondary participants who can cast the spell: roll Gnosis + Arcanum, adding successes to the leader's dice pool. If everyone has Egregore, they gain one automatic successes, meaning they guarantee at least a +1 bonus to the leader

                                        * All of this is modified by rehearsal bonuses and penalties

                                        In addition, each Path probably has a way to gain exceptional successes. Acanthus and Obrimos are easy, Mastigos just use a high powered Ritual focus spell and high Willpower bonuses. Thyrsus could arguably intensify and alter the Resonance of a Hallow, making the bonus from Resonant mana much greater. Moros can probably intensify the bonus from a sacrificial yantra and provide perfected metals to the casters, giving a much higher chance of an exceptional success.

                                                    Then looking at the teamwork rules in page on page 119: "Any secondary caster who meets the Arcanum requirements rolls her own casting and adds her successes as bonus dice to the leader."

Secondary casters with partial qualification do not suffer the dice penalty and secondary unqualified casters act as environmental yantra as usual.
