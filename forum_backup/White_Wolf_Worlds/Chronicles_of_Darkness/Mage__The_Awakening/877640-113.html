    I have to thank you Tessie for opening my eyes on this. Several things now make much more sense. I still have some points of confusion which i'm going to ask in a different thread to avoid further cluttering this one.

    In this thread I'll say that I would really like to see anyone post an interesting yet uncontroversial creative thaumaturgy spell dealing with Death's Souls purview. At this point it seems like the nature of souls qua Death is defined almost completely through the existing Death rotes that work with souls, and since we may not ascribe Death souls any other behavior based on our intuitive culture-based understanding of the word "soul" there just isn't any other safe foundation for defining other effects. I suspect the answer to any interesting, novel Death Souls spell is either "there's no evidence souls can do anything like that" or "that power relates to something the book poetically calls a soul, but isn't actually a Death purview soul, and so you must use this other Arcanum."

    I mean beyond trivial stuff like "I hide a soul with Veiling."

        Depends on your definition of interesting. Searching through the thread I've found examples of attacking the soul (applying Soulshocked), preventing the progress of soul loss, and creating a pseudo-soul to reverse soul loss.

        The problem with doing interesting things with a soul with only Death is that, outside of basic manipulation (inspect, shield, move, destroy), the effects are spread across all five subtle Arcana. The core book mentions that in the description of Reapers way back in the antagonist section, and the Nagaraja in Dark Eras sets a precedent where all Death Attainments are just the published soul manipulating spells (plus one for just destroying a soul) while any actual use they gain from the soul comes from using the optional Mind Attainments.

            Practice: Patterning

                                Another spell I've been pondering. So, I really like the potential for deep esotericism in Mage. Not just weird stuff, but the sorts of things that on their face make no sense or have no value, but which carry potentially frightening implications. Signs of Sorcery has a little of this, with certain spells and techniques that most Awakened wouldn't probably consider unless they were already starting to keel off the deep end, but there's always room for more. So:

                                Create Null (Death •••••)
                                Practice: Making
                                Primary Spell Factor: Duration
                                Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Larceny, Subterfuge

                                This spell brings into being a type of object known as a null; it is intangible, imperceptible, and, although the mage can make it any size within the dictates of Scale, takes up no space. The only way to even know that a null exists is with either Death Sight, or a Death or Prime Unveiling spell.

                                On its own, a null does nothing; it has no qualities beyond the fact that it exists. Many Awakened are wary of nulls, however, and rumors easily spread about their dark purposes: footholds for the Abyss, Rapt-lures, signs of the Psychopomp's passing, and more. On occasion, spiteful mages have cast this spell as a means of distracting a rival with a pointless Mystery. In some Consilia, Guardians of the Veil keep tabs on any mage known to have cast this spell, and the Iron Pyramid of Northwest Arkansas treats casting the spell within the borders of Bentonville outside the sanction of the Exarchs to be punishable as a Left-Handed act.

                                Add Any Arcanum ••: One phenomenon of the chosen Arcanum's purview does not function within the null. Time does not flow, destinies can't be fulfilled, the Gauntlet becomes impenetrable, anyone entering the null immediately comes out on the other side, and so on.

                                                Your arcana combination and spell name inspired me.

                                                Reiterate (Prime •• + Time •••)
                                                Practice: Weaving
                                                Primary Factor: Potency
                                                Withstand: Composure
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Persuasion, Intimidation, Expression

                                                The Prime 2 spell Words of Truth forces a target to confront a Supernal truth, risking a breaking point if they ignore or refute it. This spell takes that mental bludgeon and hammers the target with the truth of the caster's words again, and again, and again, and again, and again. The truth of the caster's words echo across time, returning to the subject's mind until they either accept that truth or go utterly mad.

                                                ============
                                                I was thinking about what else a Death mage could use his dominion over souls for beyond stealing, storing, and consuming them. This is what I came up with, based off the assumption that Willpower is a manifestation of the soul, particularly the Mind portion there-of. One of the themes of these spells is that the caster leans on his soul to achieve greater effect but at risk of soul damage if whatever he's pouring his heart and soul into fails anyway.


                                                Soulfire
                                                Death 3
                                                Perfecting
                                                The mage stokes the soul of the subject and suffusing their being with the terrible flames of will.
                                                The target gains dots of willpower equal to the potency of the spell, to a limit of what the target could achieve with maximum Resolve and Composure. A willpower enhanced roll that results in no successes causes the subject receiving a negative Condition from the strain on their soul, though this can be contested by the Death 4 Inviolate Soul attainment.
                                                +1 Reach: Instead of +3 dice, spending willpower grants a bonus equal to willpower dots. Doing so causes the target's nimbus to flair as if casting a spell, with a bonus to their effective gnosis equal to potency
                                                +1 Reach: By spending a mana the mage may exceed maximum attributes
                                                +1 Reach: By spending a mana when casting this spell, the mage allows the subject to spend a point of willpower to enhance the Potency of a Legacy attainments by 1 per 2 dice the point of willpower would have granted.
                                                .
                                                Soulcutter
                                                Death 4
                                                Patterning
                                                The subject vanishes, a cut appears, and then the subject reappears along the path of the cut. Though this might appear to be an anime-esque slicing dash, in truth it is a spell of transformation. The subject becomes the act of cutting, not the kinetic act of swinging a blade, but the entropic act of destructive severance. If there can be said to be a blade it is the subject's own soul enacting their will upon the world. Because of this the spell determines the accuracy of the attack and the distance/size it traverses, but the lethal damage weapon rating of the attack is determined by willpower dots of the subject. The subject may reflexively spend a point of willpower to avoid cutting undesired targets in their path, including after the discovery of a supernatural defense but before contesting against it. If the subject strikes a target but is successfully rebuffed to deal no damage (by armor for instance), the subject receiving a negative Condition from the strain on their soul, though this can be contested by the Death 4 Inviolate Soul attainment.
                                                +1 Reach: By spending a mana the subject deals aggravated damage and ignores damage reduction from mundane armor.
                                                +1 Reach: the cut only affects souled entities and inflicts negative soul-based Tilts as it damages the soul
                                                +1 Reach: for the duration of the spell, the cut inflicted by this spell cuts the platonic form of what the victims SHOULD be. Magic less then Making can only fuse together the cut as an act of transformation, not healing or mending. Matter 2 Shaping would be able to put together the parts of a cut vase, but a subsequent casting of Matter 3 Alter Integrity would "fix" it back into being in multiple parts
                                                +2 Reach: the cut inflicted by this spell cuts the platonic form with a Lasting duration

                                                Soulbleed
                                                Death 2
                                                Ruling
                                                The subject's soul overflows and seeps out to suffuse the secondary targets of this spell. If the subject is a supernatural entity, this inflicts their nimbus tilt or equivalent on the secondary targets. The subject gains constant awareness of the locations of the secondary targets and may reflexively spend willpower points to aid or hinder a secondary target's roll. Furthermore, the spell grants the 9-again quality to teamwork rolls between the secondary targets and the subject. If one of the secondary targets successfully attacks the subject, the subject receiving a negative Condition as their soul is pitted against itself, though this can be contested by the Death 4 Inviolate Soul attainment. The same can also happen to a secondary target as the subject's soul presses down overbearingly on their own if they dramatically fail a roll affected by the subject's willpower.
                                                +1 reach: teamwork between the subject and the secondary targets uses the 8-again quality.
                                                +1 reach: the subject may divide the benefit or malus of their spent willpower point between multiple secondary targets.
                                                +2 teach: all secondary targets receive the full benefit or malus of the spent willpower point.

                                                Soulplate
                                                Death 3
                                                Weaving
                                                The protective layers of the target's soul that defend against soul-loss expand outwards and becomes a physically manifest armor that turns aside harm by sheer force of will. The target gains ballistic and general armor equal to their willpower dots and a Withstand equal to their willpower dots against more esoteric effects directly affecting him, even those of Abyssal entities. The armor formed by this spell is BLATENTLY obvious and supernatural and the potency of this spell contests attempts to conceal it. As with other soul-based effects, should the target's soulplate be overcome the subject receiving a negative Condition from the strain on their soul, though this can be contested by the Death 4 Inviolate Soul attainment.
                                                +1 reach: the soulplate's form is mutable and the subject can manifest it as extra limbs with the normal strength and dexterity of the caster. The limbs do not grant extra actions per turn, but once per turn the subject can reflexively convert up to Potency points of armor into increasing both the strength and dexterity of the limbs, to a maximum of willpower dots. Or reflexively convert back limb attributes into armor. If the subject wishes, the limbs can be formed holding 0L melee weapons.
                                                +1 reach: the target can perform the reflexive conversions twice a turn

                                                    This spell takes elements of Sworn Oaths and Warding Gesture to create a classic effect of mythology.

                                                    The Paladin's Vow (Fate •••)
                                                    Practice: Weaving
                                                    Primary Factor: Duration
                                                    Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Crafts, Politics

                                                    In ancient times, Awakened craftsmen would enchant their creations to only reveal their true capabilities to those they deemed worthy. By modern standards, these would be called Terms of Service.

                                                    When applied to an item, the caster defines a set of conditions. Any magical enhancements they applied to the weapon become inactive unless the user agrees to uphold them.

                                                    Nothing forces the user to accept, but they do get a gut feeling of how to "bring out its true power". If they violate the terms, the enhancements become sealed for them while the spell lasts.

                                                    +1 Reach: Violating the vow also causes the item to leave the user, usually in their time of greatest need.


                                                    Forge of Kitanitowit (Spirit •••, Matter •••)
                                                    Practice: Weaving
                                                    Primary Factor: Duration
                                                    Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Science, Weaponry

                                                    Algonquian mythology says that the god Kitanitowit created all spirits and objects from his ideas. A shaman is able to harness echoes of that creative force to their advantage.

                                                    This allows the caster to shape a point of Essence into a simple tool, one that is compatible with its Resonance. The tool is solid in both the material and in Spirit attuned Twilight (or additional phases if the proper conjunctional Arcana are employed).

                                                    However, the caster decides if they are mundanely visible.

                                                    +1 Reach: Complex tools can be created.

                                                    +1 Reach: The tools gain 8-again.

                                                    Lucky Charm (Fate ••)
                                                    Practice: Shielding
                                                    Primary Factor: Duration
                                                    Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Crafts, Occult

                                                    Every civilization has crafted its share of lucky trinkets to ward off misfortune. While there is more truth to them than the Sleepers imagine, mages can also create their own wards against the vagaries of destiny.

                                                    The mage names up to [Potency] sources of harm (falling off a cliff, being shot, electrocution, etc) and fate rearranges itself to protect them. Any mundane sources are fortunately avoided, whereas supernatural ones provoke a Clash of Wills.

                                                    +1 Reach: The user can reflexively forgo the passive protection from one type of harm to be actively shielded from it once. Free Councilors have often called this a luck version of reactive armor.

                                                        Sense Tremors in the Web (Space •)

                                                        Practice: Knowing (Sympathy)
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Withstand: Sympathetic Range Withstand (it's more difficult to detect the formation of weaker connections)

                                                        For the duration of the spell, the mage detects any new sympathetic connections that are formed with her.

                                                        Ward-like effects provoke a Clash of Wills.

                                                        +2 Reach: The may specify a particular meaning that a new connection must carry in order to for this spell to detect it. This meaning aligns with manner in which the entity who has the connection "thinks" of it or its mystical nature, e.g. "an enemy," "someone I admire," "my current target," "the subject of one of my spells," etc... This is similar to the +2 Reach option for Correspondence.


                                                        Follow the Strand (Space •)

                                                        Practice: Unveiling (Sympathy)
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Withstand: Sympathetic Range Withstand (it's more difficult to trace weaker connections)

                                                        For the duration of the spell, one of the mage's sympathetic connections is visible to her eyes in a manner similar to the Prime spell Sacred Geometry. The mage may follow the connection across the physical terrain to the location of whatever or whoever lies at the other end. This spell only functions so long as whatever lies at the other end of the connection inhabits the same state of existence as the caster. For example, the mage could not trace a connection that terminates in Twilight or an Emantion Realm while she inhabits the material realm.

                                                        Ward-like effects provoke a Clash of Wills.

                                                        As the connection itself is metaphysical, its visualization runs between its two terminals as a perfectly straight line, paying no heed to physical obstructions. The visualization of a connection between the mage and something on the other side of the earth would lead down into the ground.

                                                            Orient (Space 4)
                                                            Practice: Patterning
                                                            Primary Factor: Potency
                                                            Withstand: Strength
                                                            Suggested Skills: Academics, Crafts, Survival,

                                                            Direct a thing to be facing a specific direction with respect to some declared other object. Without Reach, this is a very specific point in space. This doesn't prevent movement and wiggling, but without overcoming the spell, the overall effect cannot successfully rotate the form.

                                                            Reach 1:
                                                            The rotation can be set more flexibly, permitting, for example, rotation to see all of a given object. Scope rules can be added to permit shifting from one valid subject to another. Each rule is a target and can be defined essentially in the same manner as Ban.

                                                            Add Matter 1: A specific component of an object can be set to be the part fixed, freeing up the rest of an object to rotate freely.
                                                            Add Life 1: With Life, a specific body part can be set fixed in orientation without requiring the rest of the body to be locked in place. It's permitted to target all similar organs or senses with this effect.



                                                            Pin (Space 3)
                                                            Practice: Weaving
                                                            Primary Factor: Duration
                                                            Suggested Rote Skills: Intimidation, Science, Stealth
                                                            By changing a subject's current location in space into a fixed value, the subject cannot move from their location. The spell requires Scale factors to apply to anything greater than Size 5. They can rotate, wiggle around, and so on, but their overall center of mass cannot move. This negates Speed. This doesn't negate momentum, without Reach, so the effect is a bit like running into a wall.

                                                            Reach 1: The area of permitted motion can be expanded using Scale factors.

                                                            Add Matter 1: A component of a material inanimate object can be set as the part so fixed.
                                                            Add Life 1: A body part can be set as the reference point on a subject.
                                                            Add Forces 2: Momentum can be put on hold until the end of the effect or simply canceled outright. This also allows setting the boundary to be a force drawing the subject back in rather than an impenetrable barrier.
                                                            Add Forces 3: The energy put into fighting the spell can be lashed back on the character. Successes on rolls attempting to force movement when pinned deal Bashing Wounds to the target. If this is cast as a Combined Spell with Rend Friction, the caster can choose to make the damage of that spell only apply outside the designated area.

                                                            Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.
