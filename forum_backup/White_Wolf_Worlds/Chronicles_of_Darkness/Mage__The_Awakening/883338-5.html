            Sorry for the necro but I’m thinking about incorporating it in my games

            For the tarot cards associated with the Path

            Aretos: The Emperor prince of the material world and The Star light of the night. The two cards are linked in a way. The Emperor is the stereotypical powerful man but the Star depicts an act of blessing an creation. One creates by taking and ordering and the other by giving and letting grow. Furthermore, the Star in the Awakened Tarot is Prime.

            Axios: The Lover is a given as the original design of the man between two women indicates choices and social situations. For the second The Magician could work. It’s associated with Mercury and could work as the Fate card with an emphasis on the trickster-role. I would certainly not taken it for the Caelum version of the Path but it could work with the archetypes of merchants.

            Kleos: The High Priestess being taken by the Obrimoï is not really fitting IMHO and reeks of someone wanting to put the two religious figureheads on the Path. Both interpretation of the card, the intuition or the bookish knowledge can work for the bards. As for the Time, Judgement is both interpreted as something coming back from the past or a messenger. It could present a dilemma: Keep knowledge secret or disseminate it?

            Maestros: The Tower is actually apt for these guys. I mean they’re the lightning and the fire and command to the elements. There is a reading of the Tower card where what breaks the edifice is not thunder from above but something escaping from within. The Chariot is a reminder that the power should not be mastering you but also depicts an act of taming appropriate for the path.

            Sophros: The Empress mother of nature is a given but I’m wondering if The Hanged Man would be appropriate for the second card. The Hanged Man can be read as a period of rest and growth. When you get it in a reading the message is generally stay put. For druids attuned to the seasons it could be an interesting dilemma: Whether to force growth or decay or let it happen on its own.

                                The Watchtower of Brass and Flame is in the Realm of the Elemental Nation and inhabited by Elementals, including the djinn.

                                I chose Elemental Nation using the Primal Wild as my inspiration: an adjective-noun pair. I chose Elemental because the Realm is composed of elemental forces: fire, lightning, heat, light, sound, etc. But those forces move with purpose, guided by alien intelligence; and the denizens are likewise composed of purposeful forces — it's a realm where everything is not just alive, not just sentient, but sapient. It's not all one mind, and the individual entities in this Realm are organized by hierarchies of mastery and service into something resembling civilization. Essentially everything in the Realm is part of a vast Nation, including the Realm itself.

                                    Let’s see how these Paths get to the Seers

                                    Aretos are even more suited to Mammon or Pantechnicon than Moros. They consider themselves as craftsman, using Matter and Prime to gird their brothers in the faith with magical armaments. For the Sleepers they would be concerned with potentials, not in the sense of Destinies (which with their Inferior Arcanum being Fate, most won’t perceive) but mundanely. Those whose advances can help the Exarchs are showered with gifts while their enemies received cursed items enchanted to bear their doom.

                                    Axios are imbued with the power to see what’s important to you and bind you with your own words. Faustian Pacts are the first image that comes to mind when describing Axios Seers. As Seers their pacts always drive the victim to a position where she cannot awaken. Sometimes that means they work and are caught in material riches, sometimes they are destroyed by the promises they have made. Also Fate will enable them to see who is important and who is not.

                                    Kleos: Nothing springs immediately to mind as this Path is the one I understand the less. 1984’s style operatives who manage knowledge of the past and erase Atlanteans Ruins from the memory? Inquisitors who use their Arcanum as a means to decide guilt or innocence? (That’s one is nice for the Guardians of the Veil though)? The theme of the Path seems to be knowledge so false teachers are a given but beyond that.

                                    Maestros are in command. Over their spirit-slaves, over the elements and the Sleepers. They are the warriors of the Seers using destructive magic at their leisure. Like the Aretos are actually better servants of the Chancellors, I would see the Maestros as the servants of the General and at such they will use spirits to incite violence and Forces to burn their foes. More subtly Forces means no modern technology can really stop you and few secrets in computers are secrets for you.

                                    Sophros are masters of growth and decay, two processes that makes them uniquely suited to cater to the mortal charges of the Seers. Not only rot and decay will bring fear to most people but controlling the food supply makes you lord over pretty much everyone. More largely they would be pretty terrifying as I see blood sacrifice and traditional Witchcraft being a big part of the Sophroï praxis.

                                    In other matters a tentative list of Aeons

                                    Aretos: Vulcanus (Matter), Venus Ourania (Prime)
                                    Axios: A trickster like Mercury or Coyote, perhaps seek in Asian mythology for kitsune
                                    Kleos: Thot? Prometheus? White Buffalo Woman? Culture heroes?
                                    Maestros: Al-Rayman (Spirit), a great super-intelligent bull, and Falak (Spirit), a great flaming serpent
                                    Sophros: Xipe Totec (Life), Santa Muerte (Death)

                                                        I do not have it completed but this is what I have for the Sophros so far. Of course not everything listed below is my work. This was a community effort.


                                                        Sophros
                                                        The Druids


                                                        Magic is the Wheel on which the world turns. The wheel turns bringing balance to the universe. Life and Death is balanced upon the wheel. The cycle continues to turn the wheel. We Awakened in Concordant Opposition, and drank from the Wooden Chalice. The Cosmic Tree (World Tree, Tree of Life, Yggdrasil, Opham Tree, The One Tree) sits at the center of the Wheel connecting to all the realms. The realm of Concordant Opposition is the Cosmic Tree and to get to the other realm one travels the branches and roots.

                                                        The Great Wheel can be seen everywhere and permeates existence itself. The very cosmos turns upon the wheels bringing life, death and rebirth to the stars. The Sophros are the caretakers of the wheel and insure that the balance is maintained. They have existed in one form or another since the dawn of man documenting the turning of the wheel. Since its infancy man’s journey upon the wheel started and with each turn of the wheel man enters a new age. The ancient Greeks wrote about the ages of man starting with the Golden Age, the creation. It progresses through the Silver, Bronze and it will end with the Iron Age. These wide reaching views can be broken down further but they show the big picture.
                                                        It could be said that everything is on an individual wheel, as each man starts his life thus starting his wheel. He starts in the spring of life progressing though his summer, autumn and finally his winter. However this is not and individual wheel is just represents his position on the great wheel. Looking at the wheel we know that the closer you get to the center of the wheel the faster the wheel turns giving the perception of an individual wheel. Stepping away from the wheel we see that what appeared as an individual wheel is contained within the section of mankind. The further we step out the more insignificant the things at the center appear.
                                                        The Sophros know that man’s time on the wheel will end giving birth to something new. We do not fear the end, we celebrate it, as it is proof that the wheel is turning. We are continually fighting the lie that we have a choice in how it turns, we do not. The path on the wheel is forever consistent with our position on the wheel. The ride will end when it is supposed to and nothing can stop that. Even those that claim immortality will face an end and then they do it will be seen that their path was only slightly further from the center than everyone else’s.

                                                        …

                                                        Sophros Magic
                                                        Ruling Arcana
                                                        Life and Death: They are the basis of balance, representing the eternal wheel: Elysium energies

                                                        Inferior Arcanum
                                                        Time: all things happen in their own time, and the Sophros must honor that. Thus, messing with the flow of time is hard for them.

                                                        Symbols and Myths
                                                        The Empress, mother of nature. The Hanged Man can be read as a period of rest and growth. When you get it in a reading the message is generally stay put. For druids attuned to the seasons it could be an interesting dilemma: Whether to force growth or decay or let it happen on its own.

                                                        Druidic Brews
                                                        Three Druids
                                                        Brenden, an avid hiker, loves the majestic scenes of the mountains. I was on the mother of all hikes that his life changed. At the end of the Pacific Crest Trail after crossing the Bridge of the gods he mis-stepped and tumbled into the canyon below. At the bottom his mind slipped into darkness and his consciousness journeyed to Elysium. He awoke with the ability to heal his own body. His style of magic expounds on the feeling one gets from being outdoors. He is an adrenalin junky and enjoys stimulating that feeling in others. As a member of the Adamantine Arrow he now defends the nature he loves with the fierceness of nature itself.

                                                        Adaline was awakened in collage as she studied to get her Doctorates of Internal medicine, her dream was to become a surgeon. With the powers of Life and Death at her disposal she has gained the acclaim that she always desired. As one of the few druids to take to being a Free Council she marries science, technology and magic to make the world around her a better place. Closely watched by the Guardians she holds the line not to be crossed even though she knew she could have saved the ones that she let slip into death. But even at that she was able to help guide the soul and make the most out of death.

                                                        Jose is more interested in challenges one faces after death then what had happened before. Although he doesn’t just study the death of a body, he feels that everything essentially experiences death. When a building erodes and falls to the ravages of time, is that not a death in and unto itself. With this in mind he studies decay of material was fervently as he studies the death of a body. As an archeologist he has had the pleasure of studying the remains of a dead culture, he has wiped away the decay and witnessed the beauty of the original piece, if only for a moment before the spell allows it to return to its new and equally beautiful state/ And yes he never misses Día de Muertos.

                                                        Druids in the Orders
                                                        The Adamantine Arrow: These druids go by several name, Eco terrorist, Militant Druids, Tree huggers, whatever name you want to call them they are the protectors of the natural world. There are several organization that have sprung up in support of these druids namely green peace and PITA.
                                                        The Free Council: There are very few Druids in the Fee Council, given that technology doesn’t play much of a role in Life and Death. Those that do join the free council usually do so to manipulate the media and utilize the internet to further their cause. They also find that technology is a great way to hide their Life and Death magic as most things can be explained away as a technological miracle.
                                                        The Guardians of the Veil: Life and Death are extremely suited to the views of the Guardians. Life is uniquely suited to the covert surveillance that the guardians are famous for. The use of the Death Arcamun as punishment for committing treasonous acts only strengthens their fear factor.
                                                        The Mysterium: The Druids hold a solid footing amongst the Mystagogues Sages. Not only for their knowledge of Life and Death but for their relentless tracking of historic events. They have cut a place for their beliefs into every culture and civilization. They have played the role of Tribal Shaman, Village Healer, Wise Hermit and modern Doctors. The Wise Sages are been sought out for all sorts of matters and are generally looked at as the holders of the secrets of life and Death. That is because they are.
                                                        The Silver Ladder: There Druids of modern time have lost some of the foothold that they had with the théarch. Once they were considered the Wises around and therefore natural judges, however they have stepped back from that role and stepped further into their studies. A druid that has found himself a place within the ladder still lends his wisdom to the enlightenment of all mankind.
                                                        Seers of the Throne: are masters of growth and decay, two processes that makes them uniquely suited to cater to the mortal charges of the Seers. Not only rot and decay will bring fear to most people but controlling the food supply makes you lord over pretty much everyone. More largely they would be pretty terrifying as I see blood sacrifice and traditional Witchcraft being a big part of the Sophroï praxis.
