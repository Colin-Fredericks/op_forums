    Originally posted by Moinen View Post
    I just dislike spells that grab other arcana by establishing a rule. Like Shrink and Grow for example - of course Matter is all about transmutations and shaping but the principle of this spell (it's description) could be used by any arcana. Reach effect says that you can't do it without Matter.

    So if we allow Spirit to activate Fetishes, which I would do, and allow Prime to affect supernatural in general, which Inodiv would do, Word of Command can be easily broken into two spells. Except the mana part.
    The fact that you can use a Reach and Life as a conjunctional Arcanum with Shrink and Grow to affect living beings doesn't necessarily mean you can't just cast Transform Life on living beings to achieve the same effect. So why would you use Shrink and Grow instead of Transform Life? Because that particular mage might have more free Reach with Shrink and Grow, have Shrink and Grow as Praxis or Rote, want to affect both living beings and objects without having to cast a combined spell, or get better Yantra options for Shrink and Grow, and so on.

                If using the Possession Manifestation the resulting revenant would look just like the corpse it was before it was possessed, but it appears it won't further decompose with time. If you wish to hide the effects of blood having pooled in certain parts of the body, or the open wounds (if any) from whatever killed it I suggest using Corpse Mask to make it appear the revenant died of a heart attack a few seconds ago. Other than that you'd have to order your ghost to breathe and you'll probably be able to take your revenant out among people. Just don't have anyone come into prolonged contact (i.e noticing the lack of body heat) or taking the pulse of your revenant or your ruse might be up.
