    Children of the Stars - aliens loving Legacy

    12-07-2013, 11:58 AM
    Here is Legacy idea I’ve got for couple of months now in my mind. Now one of my players in new game made character perfect for it, so I’m adding this as his future would be concept. Please comment and say what’s wrong with it.

    Notice: This Legacy is loosely based on Scientology, Ancient Aliens theory and similar beliefs, but also Assassin's Creed series. It’s not made to mock them, only to show magical potential in them.

    Children of the Stars

    Atlantis is a Lie. In eons past not human ruled the world, but cosmic entities, from both high and distance place. Those First Ones build they civilization’s colony on small planet in classical solar system. We call this this planet Earth. There they found primitive primates and come to idea of great experiment – mixing they DNA and primates for making perfect hybrid species. After years of research, and with help of their magic-science, they were succeeded. And this is how Homo Sapiens species was born.

    First Ones were using most humans as slaves, but also schooling most promising in basic of math, astronomy and technology. They rise monumental structures and educated Homo Sapiens, and best of them choose as mating partners to sustain their species existence on Earth. Hybrids, even if looking mostly human, were given part of powers of they First Ones ancestors, now described as willworking. But with each passing millennium they colony was weaker and weaker of dilution of First Ones blood. And there come Toba catastrophe, almost wiping most of true aliens and humans. Rest decided to abandon Earth and returned to their home in stars.

    Now planet was left in hands of Willworkers, most promising of human-First Ones hybrids. When their discovered their ancestors powers, they had a dreams that were signals from First Ones mother world with secret commands about continuation of great project started eons before. There is said that hybrids that use special techniques ( i.e. Legacies Attainments ) will return to Frist Ones mother world on they death, because this technology works only for souls, not bodies. But for now they must guide humanity and remind them about their true ancestry. And Children of the Stars remember and make you to not forget.

    Parent Path or Order: Acanthus or Free Council ( thought about Obrimos, but there are about 5 now Obrimos Legacies for this Order and none for Acanthus characters ).

    Nickname: Star Gazers

    Appearance: ???

    Background: Children of the Star are based of two type people. First are those that truly believe human species is not alone in the universe. Even if they in previous lives didn’t believe in this, their awakening showed them that other intelligence life is watching humanity and want to contact them – only our limited minds put them in forms of angels, demons, fey, shades or totem beasts. Second type of people are those that found inconsistencies in Atlantis Myth. Runes don’t match. Dating is illogical. Evidence are somehow just wrong. Then they look for other explanations, those that don’t put Awakened as First Civilization. And sometimes they found answers in Children of the Stars coven.

    Organization: ???

    Suggested Oblations: Looking at open night sky and searching "home", analyzing First Ones devices, fervent speech about First Ones to public.

    Concepts: New Age astrologer, UFO conspiracy theorist, Libertines archeomancer, fringe evolutionist

    Attainments
    I was thinking about powers connected with Time, and after this with Prime. First Arcanum for “remembering” things from ancient times and perfectly synchronizing with stars. Second for cosmic power to be bestow on Children.

                              Okay, so finally we got proper idea of Legacy for our player as cabal is moving in to choosing they're own. Doing some brainstorm, we come to this Attainments group. Would love to hear from you on this.
                              Attainments

                              1st First Ones Gaze
                              Prerequisite: Gnosis 3, Time 2 (primary), Prime 1
                              Stargazers are connected to the magic-technology of First Ones on fundamental level. Just by gazing on they ancient items, they can see how and when it was used. It works as Time 2 "Postcogniton", only limited to the object itself. There isn't any time limit on how far they can see in the items past, but always their gaze must be locked on item itself. If item is also Imbued or Artifact, the Star Child instantly knew it's properties - it works as Prime 1 "Analyze Enchanted Item", with number of automatic success equal to Prime rating of character.

                              2nd Commune with First Ones
                              Prerequisite:Gnosis 5, Time 3, Prime 3 (Optional)
                              On this level of initiation, by gazing into Stars, Children can send they're conciousness on First One mother world and ask questions on they collective mind, but only for few seconds a day. It works as Time 3 “Divination”, only character isn't restricted with Temporal Sympathy.
                              Optional: If character have also Prime 3, she can unlock secrets of First Ones ancients technology and generate energy just from Ley Lines nodes by means of ritual under the stars. It works as Prime 3 “Ley Lines”, only effect is Supernal law, not magic, so it isn't vulgar.

                              3rd Will of First Ones
                              Prerequisite:Gnosis 7, Time 4, Prime 4 (Optional)
                              Now the conciseness of Stargazer is one with First Ones, even if his body is still on Earth. She is granted visions and plans of ancient masters, and is told how to fulfill her part in grant Plan. It works as Time 4 “Prophecy”, only instructions are much more clear and accurate.
                              Optional: If character have also Prime 4, she can destroy part of irrelevant mundane objects, so remanents souls of ancient First Ones be free, and collected by her before they journey to the Stars. It works as Prime 4 “Siphon Integirty”, ony as Supernal Law, not magic, so it isn't vulgar.
                              How do you think about those powers?

                                          I present to you mine example cabal-coven from Children of the Stars based in Royal Greenwich Observatory in London. Just as the Legacy at large they are outsiders to main London Consilium, leaving to be in the cellars under the hill. They are all cooperating with making coming of Yellow Sign to the London reality – only Sitting Bull is outside of this conspiracy, as he is too newest part of coven to know this secret. Rest of groups suspects he will not share they devotion to Yellow Sign cause – and they would be right.
                                          1. Precession - Acanthus of Free Council, leader of the coven and 40-something female astronomer by day and New Age astrologer by night. She was raised as white British in London, but went to New York for studies, where she met Sitting Bull, who was inducted in to Legacy with her. She left NYC community for few years, going around few Consilia, always a bit out of place. Until she return to London in 2002, to take over her post in the Observatory and started Children’s coven here – members joined in few next years, with last local – Iphis - added in 2011. Rather reasonable demeanor, but truly fixated on the “cosmic” aspect of the Legacy. She was convinced by the rest to dwell in to Yellow Sign phenomena as scientific/magical experiment to prove.
                                          2. Hastur - Mastigos of Free Council, 30-something British male s-f horror writer, the UFO conspiracy theorist in reality. He said he was “taken” at age of 19 and from them on he tries to remember all the details of his abduction, in way to solve how to be with aliens once more – in reality, it was his Awakening. He sees Children of the Stars on quasi spiritual level, trying to piece the elements need for the Second Contact. He thinks the Yellow Sign is a way to bring Them here, to commune with rest of Children – and so he choose name Hastur – from the being that “abducted” him in his Awakening.
                                          3. Patterson - Obrimos of Free Council, 30-something black female archaeologist, Libertine archeomancer. When she was Sleeper, she was making her studies on Temples of Time Before and she discovered that dating is impossible – and Awaken by this fact. Joined Children because she ferociously disapproves Atlantis Myth, seeing there is more to the Truth. She choose Shadow Name from Clair Cameron Patterson, a scientist who produced one of the most accurate measures of the age of the Earth, however he had a lot of trouble calculating this due to human pollution altering the results. As Obrimos historian, she is curious how Hallows can develop in to the same star clusters now and again – and she wants to see what, in reality, is King in Yellow.
                                          4. Iphis - Thyrsus of Free Council, 30-something white transvestite fringe evolutionist. He likes to walk as women for a few hours a day, but most of time he still thinks himself as more male persona – rather fluid, in the end. He believes that genders are mutation that was now planned from our Creators - First Ones made humanity as genderless, just like modern UFO myths describes “Grays”. He want to found where this happened, exactly, to try to revert this – at least for those that feel alien in their bodies. He spoke with some Planetary Spirits of Solar System and they sound to concur his theories. Yellow Sign, to him, could be validation of those theories and key to final understanding.
                                          5. Sitting Bull – Obrimos of Free Council. 40-something Hindu male anthropologist, researching theories of primitive cultures meeting aliens. He Awakens on his study in New York City, about the same time as Precession. They were both instructed by the same mentor in the Legacy. They probably were even lovers… They part the ways when Precession wanted to travel the world, and Bull wanted to study Native Americans believes in New England. In few years he rose to position of part modern shaman, rising a bit in Spirit Arcanum to discern Truth under myths of Indians. His Shadow Name fullfiled itself when he helped local tribe to fight the government invasion lead by Seers pylon – from then he taken active part in Free Council war with Followers of Lie. Finally, he traveled back to India, but in few years understood there is nothing he can learn there. When traveling in Europe, he get in touch with Precession and shortly joined her group in Royal Greenwich Observatory. It was only less than half year ago, so the rest of coven is reclusive to him – even when they leader is glad to have him here, probably still having feelings to him. Bull is still obvious to the plans of coven on Yellow Sign – he knows they have private meetings, but he is not looking too hard on these issues – he is more a guest than member and respect those boundaries.
