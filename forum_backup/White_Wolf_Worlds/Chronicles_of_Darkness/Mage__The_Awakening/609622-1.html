            As someone who started out with the same idea as the OP (Why the F would I join any of these?) let me try and help you:

            Why would you join?
            Because you don't know better.

            You Awaken, you finally understand how the world truly works, you have cosmic powers able to bend space and time....and then it hits you like a truck - am I alone? Am I mad? What should I do with this power?

            And then someone finds you. She's kind, warm-hearted, friendly. She teaches you the basics of what's going on, tells you you ain't alone, presents you to other Mages. She invites you to party, she tells you how great it is that she was the first to find you, because the others wouldn't have been so kind.
            You see, the Adamantine Arrow are murderous warriors thinking of Magic only as a weapon.
            Then there's the Mysterium, thinking of others as tools in their researches.
            The Free Council, anarchists and rebels.
            The Silver Ladder, devoted to open this power to everyone on earth, rather than making sure that only wise people get this gift.
            The Seers of the Throne, those who conspire behind layers and layers of lies and trickeries, those who want to keep the Magic to themselves beyond everything. Fear them the most.

            And then there's us - The Guardians of the Veil. We, your friends. Come, we have so much to teach you.


            Now take this story and bend it towards every order, including the Seers, and you'll see how easy it is to gather mages in your order. You don't explain the fundaments behind every other order - you make the new guy fear the others and teach him why they should like you. What's your goal - not theirs, you couldn't care less about what the Mysterium does.

                                            I started this game late when a new person joined my gaming group and brought the blue book and requiem with a request that we try it. I said I was game, so they handed me the books and asked if I could have a game ready the next week. Thus was born the vampire Nazi hunters, yea I made a lot of mistakes. I stumbled across mage while searching the net for ideas on opponents and was taken in by the first magic system that actually felt like you were playing a wizards instead of somebody with a bunch of neat, static tricks. I started with only the core, bought online, and like you found the brief descriptions of the setting somewhat lacking.
                                            The history of Atlantis I decided was shaped more by the new age myth than the other way around, instead the real Atlantis was made up of only two truths:
                                            1-There is a common written language that is found hidden in relics of the ancient world on every continent and in every known culture.
                                            2-There are supernal relics of the ancient world that modern mages cannot recreate.
                                            That's it, two facts that became embedded in the sleeper mysticism the newly awakened were already familiar with. I figure the awakened version of Atlantis started like this:
                                            Hoary old master to new apprentice: I will teach you about the first city, now lost with only fragments to prove its existence.
                                            Apprentice: Hey, I've heard of that. You're talking about Atlantis right?
                                            Sound of a hard slap to the head.
                                            Master: No! I'm not talking about some Sleeper bull designed to sell the gullible books! I'm talking about the first civilization founded on knowledge of the supernal.
                                            Year later the now former apprentice meets another mage of his generation and talk turns to their experience with their teachers, to which one of them says : I always thought it sounded like the story of Atlantis.
                                            That's it, familiar sleeper mysticism meets awakened archaeology and Atlantis is born. That's only in the European west, in Asia they argue about an entirely different mythology and so do the native Americans. Who knows what the original cultures of Africa and Australia believe, they didn't have much in the way of written records beyond the symbols of the common language hidden among their art.
                                            As for the orders, you awaken to magic and a whole new social circle.
                                            You discover that dark horrors exist in the world and so like the thousands who join police forces and sleeper armies every day you join the Arrows to protect those you care about. In time you internalize this new sub culture, just like those police officers and soldiers.
                                            You were always fascinated by history and exotic cultures and now you learn there are secrets hidden in human history, so with visions of Indiana Jones dancing through your head you join the Mysterium and their sub culture.
                                            You were class president, or a cheerleader, or just a bleeding heart activist and now you meet like minded people who actually have the power to make the world a better place, so you join the Ladder.
                                            Let's face it, you were always something of a paranoid nut case and that was before you knew there really were things in the shadows that wanted to suck your brain out through your nose. You join the Guardians and get your decoder ring.
                                            Awakening didn't really change your outlook on life, it just made you one of the smart people. Steve Jobs ain't got nothing on you so you join the Council and become part of the nerd herd.
                                            You are a member of a superior group of people, or maybe you just really want stuff, so you put on your white sheet, snappy uniform, gang colors, what ever replaces your morals and justifies your crimes, you join a Seer group. Belief in the Exarchs was an option I chose to drop.
                                            Normal people join cliques all the time and the awakened are still just people. Even the outsiders who all sit at the same table because they aren't welcome at any of the others.
                                            I've played games long enough that house ruling a system or setting is now reflex and this was my take on the core setting outlined in the first book.
