    Originally posted by thenate View Post
    Orient (Space 4)
    Practice: Patterning
    Primary Factor: Potency
    Withstand: Strength
    Suggested Skills: Academics, Crafts, Survival,

    Direct a thing to be facing a specific direction with respect to some declared other object. Without Reach, this is a very specific point in space. This doesn't prevent movement and wiggling, but without overcoming the spell, the overall effect cannot successfully rotate the form.

    Reach 1:
    The rotation can be set more flexibly, permitting, for example, rotation to see all of a given object. Scope rules can be added to permit shifting from one valid subject to another. Each rule is a target and can be defined essentially in the same manner as Ban.

    Add Matter 1: A specific component of an object can be set to be the part fixed, freeing up the rest of an object to rotate freely.
    Add Life 1: With Life, a specific body part can be set fixed in orientation without requiring the rest of the body to be locked in place. It's permitted to target all similar organs or senses with this effect.



    Pin (Space 3)
    Practice: Weaving
    Primary Factor: Duration
    Suggested Rote Skills: Intimidation, Science, Stealth
    By changing a subject's current location in space into a fixed value, the subject cannot move from their location. The spell requires Scale factors to apply to anything greater than Size 5. They can rotate, wiggle around, and so on, but their overall center of mass cannot move. This negates Speed. This doesn't negate momentum, without Reach, so the effect is a bit like running into a wall.

    Reach 1: The area of permitted motion can be expanded using Scale factors.

    Add Matter 1: A component of a material inanimate object can be set as the part so fixed.
    Add Life 1: A body part can be set as the reference point on a subject.
    Add Forces 2: Momentum can be put on hold until the end of the effect or simply canceled outright. This also allows setting the boundary to be a force drawing the subject back in rather than an impenetrable barrier.
    Add Forces 3: The energy put into fighting the spell can be lashed back on the character. Successes on rolls attempting to force movement when pinned deal Bashing Wounds to the target. If this is cast as a Combined Spell with Rend Friction, the caster can choose to make the damage of that spell only apply outside the designated area.
    I don't think the Matter or Life stuff is doing anything here that you can't just do with Space. I don't really understand the first spell though, why is it Patterning to make a thing spin?

            The Unseen Scribe (Fate ••, Prime ••)
            Practice: Veiling
            Primary Factor: Duration
            Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Expression, Subterfuge

            One of the simplest, most effective ways to hide crucial information in a Censorea is to encode it under a seemingly fake copy. While the thieves scramble about searching for "the real text", the dangerous knowledge remains untapped and the Guardians gain more time to retrieve it safely.

            This allows the mage to add invisible script to a target. They can specify up to [Potency] conditions under which they become visible, such as only becoming visible under the light of the moon of the same day as they were written. More complex images might require Crafts or Expression rolls.

            +1 Reach: The conditions can be something only detectable under Mage Sight.

            +1 Reach: The caster can specify up to [Potency] additional conditions. If the primary conditions are fulfilled but these aren't, the image will be warped into a false positive, making them seem reasonable when they are utterly incorrect. For example, a map to an Abyssal Iris may lead to an empty glade in the middle of the forest or directions to a lost mage's hoard may lead to digging up a patch under the insane asylum.

                The Light Tool (Forces •••, Matter •••)
                Practice: Weaving
                Primary Factor: Potency
                Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Expression, Science

                Sometimes you have the right tools for the job, but not the ability to reach the target. The Libertine mage Bao-Dur once found himself in that situation when he was caught in a death trap. He devised this spell under such a dire situation, allowing him to transpose the functionality of his tools into his flashlight, using the light it emitted to disarm the killer device.

                But perhaps the most terrifying use of this spell was by a Banisher called Zahak. When he was cornered by a cabal amidst a rock concert, he used it to transpose the function of a pry bar with the drums. The amplified sounds reached all over the building and the frenzied drumming removed all the bolts within, bringing the structure down on them all.

                This spell transposes the properties of [Potency] tools or machines to a force emmited by a target. The mage is free to specify what uses correspond to which use of the target. For example, for a hammer, hitting a gong one side hammers and the other side pulls.

                With conjunctional Fate 1, the mage can specify which conditions activate which tools. By substituting Matter for Spirit, it can be used on fetishes. By substituting it for Prime, this spell can be applied to imbued items.

                    An attempt to make some Acanthus styled healing, partially inspired by the Dresden Files.

                    Breath of Aceso (Time ••)
                    Practice: Ruling
                    Primary Factor: Duration
                    Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Science, Survival

                    After observing the rapid healing of werewolves, Arrow mages developed this spell as a means to replicate their resilience. This speeds up the recovery time of the target, each level of [Potency] halving the time needed to recover from bashing damage. In addition, the time required for sleeping and resting are similarly affected. Targets do not require any extra nutrition to fuel the process, neither do they prematurely age or "use up" their limit on cell divisions, making it an ideal tool for times when medical aid is scarse.

                    Some of the more zealous members of the Faceless faction of the Guardians heavily favor this spell. It allows them to quickly heal from plastic surgery and better infiltrate hostile cabals, since most mages usually check for supernatural changes in appearance. The Order tends to keep a close watch on these agents, seeing this as a common symptom of an insidious supernatural meme, one that leads down the path of the Reaper.

                    +1 Reach: The healing time for lethal damage is also affected.

                    +1 Reach: Every level of Potency instead divides the time to a quarter.

                    Tenacity of MacLeod (Fate ••••, Time ••)
                    Practice: Patterning
                    Primary Factor: Duration
                    Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Science, Survival

                    Having to earn their freedom over days dueling in a colosseum in the Hedge, the Arrow mage Worf developed this spell to help his cabalmates recover between fights. This spell allows the target to heal faster and better all types of damage, allowing them to stabilize their condition and heal aggravated damage without medical attention. The process is so enhanced that not even scars remain. In addition, every level of Potency reduces by half the time needed to heal.

                    +1 Reach: Every level of Potency instead divides the time to a quarter.

                                Fortuitous Relinquishment (Fate •••)
                                Practice: Perfecting
                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Investigation, Occult

                                Lucky surges of inspiration can have a powerful effect, the right intonation of mantras and intuitive adjustments can elevate the quality of one's Imago. After much experimentation, the Mystagogues deviced a reliable way to use it to create enhanced items.

                                For this spell's duration, the target can choose [Potency] exceptional successes on a spellcasting roll and allow safe relinquishment as one of the possible benefits.

                                Laurels of Nike (Fate •••)
                                Practice: Perfecting
                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Expression, Occult

                                An experienced Théarch knows that a great leader does not only achieve success, he elevates and redefines what it means. Why should their Imagos be any different ?

                                For this spell's duration duration, the target can choose [Potency] exceptional successes on spellcasting rolls. Those successes allow them to choose to add a Reach to any factor, not only the primary one.

                                +1 Reach: The target is able to pick a second benefit for the exceptional success. However, they cannot pick the same one twice. An extra Reach and mana refund are a valid combination. But double mana refund is not.

                                            The Old Switcharoo (Space •••)
                                            Practice: Weaving
                                            Primary Factor: Potency
                                            Suggested Rote Skills: Larceny, Stealth, Subterfuge

                                            Transportation of magical artifacts is a dangerous job, even the most well hidden stashes might be betrayed by a suspicious twitch. To prevent this, as well as aid the "liberation" of artifacts scheduled for incorporation into Athaneums, the Mystagogues developed this spell. It allows them to switch the contents of two containers in sensory range. Without added scale, one of them must be in the target's person. The caster can set up to [Potency] filters for items to be taken or excluded. This is normally used to discretely swap the contents of bags of holding, or tooths of holding for extremely sensitive packages.

                                            +1 Reach: The swap does not show any deformation in space for the duration, so long as it can contain the item. A sofa under a cover can be swapped with a golf ball from a bag of holding and the cover will still retain the shape of the sofa. This does not hold up to scrutiny.

                                                    Sanctify Space (Prime ••• + Space ••)
                                                    Practice: Perfecting
                                                    Primary Spell Factor: Duration
                                                    Withstand: Safe Place
                                                    Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Persuasion, Survival

                                                        Kinetic Battery (some combination of Forces 2, Forces 3, Life 2, and/or Life 3)
                                                        Practice: Ruling
                                                        Primary factor: Potency
                                                        Withstand: Stamina
                                                        Suggested rote skills: Athletics, Occult, Brawl

                                                        Kinetic force applied to the subject is stored in the subject's muscles and circulatory system for later use by the subject. Up to Potency of (kinetic) bashing damage at a time is negated and stored within the subject (who can store up to Potency of kinetic damage at once). The subject can expend stored energy to add dice to physical rolls or increase the weapon rating of their unarmed or melee attacks but the stored energy can pose a risk to the subject. If the subject takes non-negated lethal damage, the stored energy violently vents from the wound, potentially dealing damage ot the attacker at the cause of inflicting an additional point of bashing on the subject.

                                                        Reach [varies[: several of the reach options from Kinetic Blow,
                                                        Reach +1: this spell can negate lethal damage
                                                        Reach +2: in addition to lethal damage, this can also negate aggravated damage at the cost of point of mana.
                                                        Reach +1: The spell also allows the subject to store the reaction force (Newton's Third Law) of their own blows, up to the lower of the number of successes rolled on the attack or Potency. This does not decrease the damage the blow deals but instead means that there's no recoil, so for instance striking hard surfaces would not be painful.
