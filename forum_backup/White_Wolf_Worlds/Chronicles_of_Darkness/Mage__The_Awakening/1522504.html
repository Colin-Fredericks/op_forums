    Seven Planetary Courts (Homebrew)

    09-13-2023, 03:51 AM
    Hey everyone! After discussion in this thread I've decided to write out the Mystery behind Thrice-Great in more detail for my own games; after some thoughts I've decided to share it, both to get some feedback and new ideas and for somebody else to use it if they wish to.

    Seven Planetary Courts

    Mystery of Henads
    Great and ancient Mystery of Shadow Realm which was focus for Awakened - especially Thrice-Great Legacy who made that Mystery their focus and primary source of mystical might - over untold millennia, Seven Planetary Courts are spirit courts of seven "planets" of Solar System - in Ptolemaic, geocentric system. Even though Awakened magic and Sleeper science disproved that system untold ages ago, the Mystery itself remains.

    Those spirits, called henads by Indo-European Awakened or mulhithim by Uratha, differ from their Earthbound cousins. For any mage who did not ever met a henad of particular planetary court before, they count as Mystery with Opacity (or Keys, if using Mysteries as Investigations) of (3 + their Rank); until Mage hasn't successfully learned their Mystery (and by extension - Mystery of their court), they Withstand attempts to use Knowing spells (of all Arcana) on them with (3 + their Rank) and double their Rank for Spirit spells meant to affect them. While henads know First Tongue and speak it normally, they often have weird accents and inflexions which make it harder to understand them for those who only spoke to earthbound spirits before, imposing -3 penalty on any social rolls with them.

    But it's not their alien Supernal symbolism which is the main Obsession of Awakened all over the world, but unique, strange Resonance-signatures they have. Sleepers know other planets to be dead, lifeless balls of rock or gas giants; one would expect their spirits to be the same - spirits of cold, dark matter endlessly circling the distant sun. But they anything but; every planetary court has it's unique symbolism and resonances, including very human concepts such as desires, emotions or ethical principles. The chicken-and-egg debate over source of such Resonance-concepts is going for several millennia now with end nowhere in sight; while most Diamond and Seer mages think this to be result of Supernal over-symbol influences (with many mages arguing that planets themselves may be connected to hypothetical Celestial Ladder and it's shattering during the Fall), Free Council mages think that influence of humanity is more far-reaching than anyone have thought, somehow influencing distant Shadows.

    No matter the reason, henads themselves are powerful, if alien, allies to Awakened when called upon and properly bargained with. They are able to grant Patron Yantras with bonus equal to their (Rank/2, round up), linked to concepts of their planetary courts; their very presence counts as Environment Yantra for such spells. They are able to aid and empower acts of ritual magic, acting as Sleepwalkers or even fully Awakened for such spells. While they lack knowledge of Arcana themselves, not being Supernal Entities (though sometimes they act like they are distant cousins of them), many of them know ancient secrets, including Rotes and magical techniques lost to time. And of course, there are their Influences, which can be used for wide range of tasks.

    Their requests, however, are as alien as henads themselves; not having spheres of influence in terrestrial Shadow, many of them are interested in bizarre quests in it nonetheless - sometimes destructive, sometimes restorative, but always strange. A lot of henads seem to be interested in human worship; given that they are curiously similar to likes of many ancient pagan gods, this raises questions about genesis of many Sleeper religions. Powerful and prepared Awakened can try to defy them and wrestle and beat them into submission instead, but their Mystery is powerful one; henads wield many strange and terrifying Numina, some of which seem to be especially effective against Supernal magic, and a lot of too hubristic mages have met their end this way.

    The Mystery becomes even more perplexing when most knowledgeable Awakened start to consider effects of planetary courts on Earth as a whole. While choirs of Warden Moon prohibit the direct entry to their celestial cousins (strangely, all henads coming to Earth uninvited and on their own are full of hate and wish to cause as much suffering and destruction as possible), mages know that astrology, while being thought as a fake pseudoscience by contemporary Sleepers, has a kernel of truth (and Truth) in it. Influence of distant planets does manifest itself in Earth's Shadow, creating tidal waves of certain Resonances and affecting related choirs - and thus affecting material realm indirectly. Those "stellar winds" are deep, ancient and elusive Mystery, especially by the fact they are slow, subtly affecting the Earth over course of centuries and sometimes even millennia, making their study a multi-generational effort for entire Legacies to focus upon.

    The lesser, but still curious, Mystery is connecting alchemy and astrology. There are seven well-known planetary courts and seven Perfected metals - and they resonate with each other. When magical tool made out of Perfected metal is used as Yantra in summoning a henad of corresponding planetary court, spell gains one free Reach. The reason for this is anyone's guess, though.

    Known Planetary Courts
    Luna/Moon
    Physically closest to the Earth, Lunes are lunar spirits most familiar and known to Awakened; they are also serve as messengers and heralds from Mother Luna to her earthbound children, Uratha. There are five different "castes" of Lunes roughly corresponding to different moon phases, but Lunes overall are capricious and shapeshifting beings linked to concepts of transformation and madness. They favor Acanthus mages, especially those privy to shapeshifting magic and oracular madness.

    Curiously, they are also bound to protect the Earth Shadow from invasions from other planetary courts and void spirits, and they often corral and command Uratha and sometimes even mages to act in protection of Shadow from such intruders. Fortunately for mages who want to communicate with henads and study them, they rarely react to a quick summoning, but any Awakened trying to bind the henad more permanently will quickly become object of their attention and anger; the more powerful henad in question is, the more deadly such attention will be.

    Lunes are resonating with lunargent.

    Sun
    Spiritual children of the Sun itself, Helions are rarely seen in the Shadow Realm - but still much more common than other henads. They are powerful, prideful beings linked to concepts of wrath, justice, revelation and rejection of lies and darkness; they often manifest as blazing fireballs, geometric forms of pure light, or even armed humanoid angel-like figures. They favor Obrimos mages calling them, especially those who fight lies and demand justice - no matter what kind of justice it is - but demand respect (even obeisance) and fealty. Helions grant raw power of revelation and truth, but demand that mages use it fully, without restrictions or holdbacks.

    Helions are resonating with orichalcum.

    Mercury
    The most guile and intelligent of henads, angels of Mercury are ones most interested in Awakened themselves. They are smart, inquisitive beings linked to concepts of intellect, communication, uncovering secrets, trade and theft; they often manifest as lightning-quick shapes constantly on the edge of vision, or even borrow the image from mind of summoner. It was they who helped to create hermeticism as philosophy of thought, and they are henads most mages seek to summon and deal with. They favor all Awakened regardless of Path, but those who summon them have to embrace their Obsessions. Mercury henads hold a lot of secrets traded from Awakened all over the history, and rumors have it that they hold even greater secrets of their own well-hidden under the surface of Mercury.

    Mercury henads are resonating with hermium.

    Venus
    The most human-like henads (thus fueling Free Council theories on Mystery's source), angels of Venus are desire personified. They are seductive, charming beings liked to concepts of desire, inspiration, fertility, joy and mirth; they take beautiful, healthy and appealing forms to cajole and trick Awakened into summoning them. They favor Thyrsus mages, especially those focused on nurturing, protecting and inspiring life itself. Venus henads hold many secrets of healing and inspiration, and able to raise individual to height of his inner potential.

    Venus henads are resonating with brontium.

    Mars
    The most aggressive and dangerous henads, angels of Mars seek to destroy all other planets for the sake of conflict itself. They are warlike, hostile beings linked to concepts of raw emotions (especially anger), warfare, conflicts and violence; but Mars henads also resonate with concepts of boldness, determination and even heroism. They often take forms of red and brown dust and shining tied into ragged, storm-like form ready to unleash power on offender. They favor Mastigos mages, especially those who wield emotions as weapons against other. Mars henads will gladly join Awakened conflicts, but price would be that they will do anything to make them as meaningless and destructive as possible.

    Mars henads are resonating with siderite.

    Jupiter
    Henads of most massive planet (except for the Sun itself), angels of Jupiter see themselves as rightful rulers of all planets - and heirs to the Sun itself. They are ambitious, power-hungry beings linked to concepts of leadership, power, nobility, growth and expansion. They often take massive, powerful, thunderous forms, often vaguely human-like, but sometimes just swirls of wind and force. Like Warden Moon is protector of Earth, Jupiter henads spread their influence all over the Solar System, capturing void spirits in gravity traps and absorbing them and their power. They favor Obrimos mages calling them, but especially those who reach for power and are willing to pay any cost for it.

    Jupiter henads are resonating with kassiterum.

    Saturn
    The most distant henads, angels of Saturn rarely make their way to the Earth on their own; thus most information about them is gained by Awakened through summons and study of stellar winds. They are broody, melancholic beings liked to concepts of obstruction and distress; they command depression and sorrow, and inflict death, sickness leading to endings. They are not malevolent, though; Saturn henads also resonate with overcoming those obstructions to become better. They often look like multicolored, reflective shadows of red and orange hue, constantly fading until they wither away at the end of summoning. They favor Moros mages, especially those who developed a more positive, outward personality after paying with Leaden Coin. They also hold keys to sealing away powerful entities, and experienced Thrice-Great suspect that many such entities are lying, chained and bound, on moons of Saturn.

    Saturn henads resonate with apeiron.

    Distant Planets
    Traditional Hermeticism knows seven planetary bodies; but as Sleeper science has proved, there are more of them. Awakened hold great powers of Arcana, allowing them to know easily there are more than seven major planetary bodies. This is a flexpoint at your table; there are three major possibilities to explore:

    Hidden Influences
    Of course, Awakened Hermetics knew about Uranus, Neptune and Kuiper belt objects; Forces Arcanum is better than any telescope, allowing to focus distant light and explore the night sky. But since contemporary Sleepers were unable to gaze upon those bodies, they never understood obscure references to those distant bodies in Awakened notes and grimoires; desire to keep the Veil stifled the progress. Only now modern occultists have the chance to update old doctrines and fully understand the astrological influences previously only understood by their Awakened mentors.

    Fractured Courts
    There are no great henad courts of planetary bodies beyond Saturn. The Mystery ends there; only dispersed Essence-starved spirits of cold winds persist in the clouds of Uranus and Neptune, and Kuiper Belt objects only hold a few void spirits clinging to barren surfaces. While Libertines theorize that spiritual influence of humanity ends near the Saturn orbit, Diamond mages whisper about broken parts of Celestial Ladder falling into the Abyss, stripping symbolism and meaning from those henads stuck in the middle. Some of them even think that Uranus and Neptune are creation of Exarchs, created to destroy the very possibility of rebuilding Celestial Ladder again.

    Seed of Possibility
    In this variant, Mystery ends with Saturn, but not completely. While Uranus does not have it's own formed henads yet, it has a lot more activity than Neptune or Kuiper Belt objects. Various Resonances appear and disappear, as if Shadow of distant planet is itself in the flux. In this variant, planetary court of Uranus is forming; process is astronomically slow and may take centuries or even millennia, but eventually a new type of henad will arise, and perhaps even eighth Perfected metal will appear, linking new planet correspondence to Supernal.

    Where is Gaia?
    Two Ilusahim, Helios and Luna, rule over the Earth's Shadow from above; their existence is known, if arcane, occult fact shared by Awakened and Uratha alike. Existence of Ilusahim of other planets in solar system is implication born from fact that henads and planetary courts exist; it seems obvious that those courts have their overspirits.

    But Earth itself is a spiritual paradox in this regard. Compared to the universe above and beyond, terrestrial Shadow is place of incredible diversity; thousands of different umia exist in Earth's Shadow, with many of them having their own Ilusah or even, possibly, Ilusahim ruling over them. But this begs two questions: is there a planetary court of Earth? And where is Gaia?

    Henads of Earth
    There are two major possibilities for planetary court of Earth which could be explored in your games:
    1. Henads of Earth exist, but Gaia is nowhere to be found. In this variant, Earth is like all other planets; there is a court of planetary spirits of Earth travelling across the void, born out of reflected sunlight; they may protect the Earth from invading void, like Warden Moon does, or they can invade other planets in attempt to seed life there. They can even be hinted to be the ultimate reason other planetary courts exist, giving more credit to Free Council theories. From their existence, Gaia's existence is implied, but this Ilusahim, probably more powerful than Helios or Luna, is still nowhere to be found - which maybe it's own desire.
    2. Henads of Earth do not exist, because Earth is too diverse. There is just no "unifying" symbolism in Earth's shadow - it's too diverse, too vibrant, too ever-changing. It's also cracking with power, with it's own Ilusah and maybe Ilusahim rivaling power of other planetary overspirits. Gaia's existence cannot be implied from these observations, and perhaps pressure of Helios in Earth's Shadow is more sinister than it looks like, preventing court of planetary spirits from forming - and playing their role in conquering the void.
    Does Gaia exist?
    …this is not very important for average Mage game. Even if it does exist, it's at least Rank 8 (probably more!), standing equal to Luna and Helios, and if such entity wishes to remain unnoticed, nobody except archmages and Ascended Beings can do anything about it. So mere fact is not important enough; the question is much more interesting. Mages and archmages obsessed with that Mystery can go on to grand and dangerous adventures, trying to find Gaia - or even create her, if she does not exist yet.

    Void and Stars Themselves
    If henads of distant stars exist, no mage was able to summon them and tell the tale. Perhaps more conservative Thrice-Great are right, and stars are resonant with Supernal itself, while yawning void beyond Solar System is beginning of the Abyss, eating up summoning spells. Or maybe there are such henads, but they are not able to cross the unimaginable distances without becoming void spirits themselves, being forced to subsist on Essence of nothingness - and Awakened magic is not able to create a bridge to them. Strands of sympathy start to break down after heliopause; while distance is Lie, nothing here seems to be really connected, Space-wise, to there. Of course, Forces magic allows to create vessels moving close to speed of light, but so far, mages attempting such expeditions have failed in their attempts, being forced to return or disappearing in the endless expanse of empty space and hungry void.

    On the other hand, void spirits certainly do exist - and their alien, distorted natures give more arguments to those linking those beings to Abyss. While they are certainly not Gulmoth, they share many properties with beings from the Void. They are hungry for Essence. They distort natural laws wherever they appear. They are alien, unlike known henads, barely able to speak First Tongue and be understood. And they wish to invade, trying to create beachheads and strongholds to call more of their own and feast upon reality itself. Because of that, in most Consilia, summoning void spirits is forbidden. Even though Awakened magic is able to feed them and protect world from their influence, doing that invites conflict with Lunes and Uratha. Mages being mages, many ignore that rule to their own peril, quickly finding out that while void spirits are alien beings, they are far from being defenseless.

      I too tried to figure out how to bridge Uratha and Thrice-Great perspectives on Henads. I wondered if the Mages don't actively cultivate desired resonances in contacted Spirits, by feeding them the right essence and making agreements that reinforce their desired symbolism. All that to stop them from being hostile forces and to put them in sync with the hypothetical, pre-Fall paradigm. That would raise a chicken and egg question: Did the Hermetic influences come first, or did the Thrice-Great efforts did?

      This is also why I think Uranus and Neptune would be extremely divisive among the Legacy, even to the point of conflict. Are those two planets even "meant" to exist, and if so, what association to cultivate in them? Should Uranus be granted domain over stillness and preservation befitting its icy nature, or should it be rebellion and deviance representing its wild axial tilt? I imagine arguments like that being common, with a third party shouting that the Planets should develop without interference, and a fourth screaming that all of that is courting the Abyss.
