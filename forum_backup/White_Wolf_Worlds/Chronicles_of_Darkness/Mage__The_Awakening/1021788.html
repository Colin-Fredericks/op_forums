    Houseruled Combo, Mage + Purified. Possible issues?

    12-24-2016, 11:28 PM
    Hello, so I am working on a campaign idea using the Sundered World setting from Dark Eras. My players will be Wise (that settings version of mage) who will later on be given the gift of Purification (pg 96 or so in the Immortals book) by a quest. While I am still thinking on just what I want available, I thought just leaving it as written and just adding it to the mage as an extra template.

    Obviously, there could be some lore issues and I know it says they cannot become mages but, for my game, I thought it would be an interesting dynamic because my players are starting play sometime around 5000 BCE. With Purified Siddhi for the most part being locked behind finding a spirit to teach them along with any numina, they would just be damn hardy Awakened.

    Another worry is the life span of an immortal Awakened and not letting it get out of hand. Though, I'm honestly open to a high power game. If the players buy up Arcana and stuff, why not? The game setting is mostly just a sandbox with a storyline thrown in for flavor. As a purified, they can be trapped or enslaved to account for large chunks of time. Maybe just treat gametime as active time with anything outside of that will see jumps in time regularly but I kinda want to just avoid Modern day all together or just prolong it so they can play around in history.

    Considering they need to buy mage things and purified things with the same xp I award, I don't think it would get too powerful. Especially when you are playing in a story where the long life span isn't that important, more for a way to see these guys survive antiquity.

    Comments, questions or suggestions? Thanks in advance!

      If they're just hardy Awakened, why both with the Purified aspect at all?
      Also you can easily deal with the immortal aspect by just not letting them "wait it out".
      Apply pressure, make them deal with problems in the now, if someone gets trapped, then they have to free them.

      Also unless you're forcing them to buy Purified abilities, and removing the opportunity for Arcane Experiences, there will be nothing which stops them advancing in power just as fast as most Awakened do.

      Also if all you want is for them to survive antiquity, you can just throw them into a high-dot Time spell to shunt them into the future. No need to bother with Purified stuff and messing with non-canon cross-template abilities at all.
