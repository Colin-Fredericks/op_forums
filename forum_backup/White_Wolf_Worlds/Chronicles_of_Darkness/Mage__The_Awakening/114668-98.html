                    If that was so, then Mages can't take beats from Persistent Conditions since they cannot be "solved", but do award beats when something happens. Also, damage awarding beats is pretty consistent throughout the game lines. All game lines have the exact same 6 categories that award beats (except for Mage and the taking lethal one):
                    - Fulfilling an Aspiration or making headway towards one;
                    - Resolving a Condition;
                    - Persistent Conditions when they negatively affect you;
                    - Turning a failure into a dramatic failure;
                    - Taking lethal damage on one of your last health boxes;
                    - At the end of a session;
                    But, since you can include "taking damage on the last health boxes" and negative impact from Persistent Conditions as fulfilling the "Any major dramatic event the Storyteller deems appropriate can award a Beat." condition, it can be included in the list from Mage.
