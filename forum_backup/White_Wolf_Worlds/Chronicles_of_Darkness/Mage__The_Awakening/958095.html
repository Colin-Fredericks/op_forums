                              Potter wasn't written based upon the Mage rules so it isn't going to be the same, but that doesn't mean there aren't similarities or more importantly, ideas to be mined from the books/movies. The Death Eaters could be used as inspiration for a legacy of Reapers and if we follow Voldimort's example a Lich Legacy.

                                              I'm inclined to agree with the thesis with the soul stone and horcrux comparisons and in fact I'd say that LofR example of the One Ring being a soul stone is not too far off the nose.

                                              Looking at the Sauron for a moment. He was a mortal~ish? (Kind of confused whether he was an angel or mortal race) That crafted powerful artifacts and gave them as poison gifts to his allies. And using those rings he controlled them on his march of conquest. In battle he died, but due to his OneRing never truly being destroyed (I'm looking at you Man) His soul never died. Then he became a Disjoint Eye. Far removed from mortal experiences. I'd say that is a cracked and broken state. And if he ever got his OneRing back he'd be whole and unstoppable.

                                              It's the Lich's phylactery, how you model it is up to you. And with Awakening starting as a soul stone is not too far off. As long as there's a interesting Mystery and story to go with it. I'd think you'd have good game.
