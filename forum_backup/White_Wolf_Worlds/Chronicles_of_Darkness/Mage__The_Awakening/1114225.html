    Silly Thought But... I want to make a mage that is in a musical

    07-10-2017, 02:45 PM
    So like the title says I want my Mage to be in a musical and to have the whole world act in accordance. Like other people can sing, and maybe he should give buffs to things if they do, but you get the whole back up dancer thing going on, and you have the fact that a large portion of the does doesn't seem to notice it going on.

    This is where you come in... I need help figuring out what Arcana I need at what levels to pull this off. I figure I probably need Fate, Mind, and Space (Isolation will either have folks backing off or ignoring me)... probably want forces as well unless... I guess I could use Mind to produce the actual music.

      Well for starters you ought to get a Shadow Name relating to music/musicals in some way and your Long-Term Nimbus will take care of a lot of the basic theming. In terms of Arcana, Mind is pretty much mandatory to press people into service as background characters. Ideally at 4 dots so you could use Gain Skill to make your backup dancers/singers actually competent, but assuming basic character creation rules you'll have to stick to 3 dots and hope they took choir in high school so you can use Enhance Skill. Maybe make your Nimubs Tilt +1 Expression so you have something to work with?

      Other than that the only obvious thing is Forces to become a living stage (lights, sounds, pyrotechnics etc.), but any of the Arcana might prove useful. Fate to assure a succesful career, Time to get things set up in a flash, Matter to make props and costumes, Space to project your performance and keep the haters off the stage, Life to become really, really, ridiculously good looking, Prime to build enchanted instruments, Spirit to nurture a brood of spirits of performance/ecstacy/music, Death if you want use ghosts and shadow for darker performances.

      Really, this is one of those concepts you can fit into basically any combination of Arcana. It just depends on what flavor you're looking for. Mind is hard to get away from if you really want the whole "involuntary background singers" aspect, but don't forget that ghosts and spirits also have the ability to control people, or serve as background performers in their own right.

                          Demons would sing bits of Carmina Burana continually.

                          A Nimbus that literally causes people to sing was quite doable in 1e*, but in 2e, as mentioned, it can also help them do it well.
                          (*As either Obrimos with Mind and/or Space or a Mastigos with Prime and/or Forces.)


                          Since nobody seems to have mentioned it, yet: Familiars
                          Shadow denizens include spirits of song and dance.
                          Temenal beings of music and musicals would exist, too. (If nothing else, you have the muses---Terpsichore, in particular.) For that matter, some concepts of music transcend humanity, so denizens of Dreamtime could fit, too.
                          Aether denizens may well include beings that could do this.

                          Those with applicable Influences would love to help, since they'd feed.
                          Adjustment in the Astral Planes could allow a person to make singing spontaneously a more normal thing in a given group,
                          Spells that force people to sing and dance could be cast using Mind... or Fate. (They'd look a bit different.) An area effect that follows the character around and targets those minds within is a bit of a stretch for a basic mind effect. That said: An archmaster's spell or artifact could render such an effect that adapts to circumstances.
                          The Mad could operate in a similar way, centered on their attention, for instance.
                          An archmaster could, for some reason, simply render the entire world a musical.

                          Man, you got choices. We talking a musical where people talk like normal for basic communication but when things get important, they burst into song? That seems like a really wacky Fate and Mind effect.

                          Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.
