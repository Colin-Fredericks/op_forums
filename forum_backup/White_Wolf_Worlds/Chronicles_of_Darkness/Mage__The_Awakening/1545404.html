    [MtAw Dark Eras] Origins of Orders - WHEN Orders spread on World? [Tome of Pentacle]

    11-05-2024, 04:39 PM
    So let’s return from 2E point of view to idea of Orders cultural exchange – and they export over the Fallen World. Remembering that ‘Proto-Diamond’ Orders are based from Darshanas in Rome circa 3rd century BCE – When they could influx local mage cultures of Far East, Africa, and alike?

    Looking over Rome’s own timeline:
    • Africa – Punic Wars ( 264 till 146 BCE ) with Carthage would lead Darshanas to the Norther Africa, with Carthage and Egypt in just 3rd century. Creating Mouseion in Library of Alexandria as Keepers of the World headquartering 295 BCE is best example.
    • Asia, West & Central – From times of Alexander Empire, Persian and Indian mages were influenced with Darshanas ideas - so they would join 'Proto-Diamond’ as early as 330s BCE, following Alexander expeditions into Asia. ( Rome had small but existing community of Indian emigrants already in Roman Empire times, probably earlier even. )
    • Americas – Tome of Pentacle mentioned that first Vikings mages, already in Diamond, come to modern Canada in 1021 CE. Adamantine Arrow dogma strongly resonates with Native American supernal traditions.
    • Australia – I suspect ‘last found continent’ did not have Diamond mages before any contacts with Aboriginal propel of European colonists in 17th century?
    The biggest question for me is..
    • East Asia ( Far East ) – China and Japan – They clearly would have vibrant preDiamond Darshana cults traditions. But when 'Proto-Diamond’would ‘show up on their doorsteps’? When they tradition would intermixed with Diamond Orders dogmas? ToP points that founding of Nalanda, first university in Magadha, India in 427 CE is spreading Diamond lore – but it sounds VERY late to me. Some history scholars points to evidents of first China-Rome relation as early as 1st century CE – with ‘Diamond almost ready’ then, I do not see reason why it should be not time for propagating Orders in this region at this time.
    EDIT: Topic analysis below points China's Six Dynasties ( 220 CE – 589CE ) from it's start - I still would see starts for circa 220 BCE, would already have basis of (proto)Diamond in themselves. 😮
