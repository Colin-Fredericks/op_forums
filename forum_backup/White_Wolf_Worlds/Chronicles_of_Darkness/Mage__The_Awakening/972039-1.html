                                    All the merit does in regards to Beasts is make the mortal hard to feed from.

                                    Most nightmares are created FROM fears, but don't actually directly cause fear, they create situations that are scary, but they don't magically enduce fear directly.

                                    It would defeat the purpose if they did. Its mean to scare you by its horrifying nature, not magically induce the emotion of fear in someone.

                                    To put it in another way, Nightmares give the target something to be afraid of, but it doesn't compel someone to be afraid.

                                    What does it do? It blocks magic that causes fear or disgust. So it blocks magic that directly trigger emotions of fear and disgust, like the Changeling contract Paralyzing Shudder, or a high Wyrd Changeling using Bedlam to induce fear, but it doesn't stop a Changeling from creating Illusions and effects intended to scare you or creating Nightmarish dreams (although they won't scare, the dream itself can be created, it just doesn't work as intended).

                                    Also the merit has an inherient exception for stuff that connects with your vice or conditions, so if you have greed as your vice, and something tries to scare you into believing that you've lost all you life savings, that magic can still work on you to scare you.
