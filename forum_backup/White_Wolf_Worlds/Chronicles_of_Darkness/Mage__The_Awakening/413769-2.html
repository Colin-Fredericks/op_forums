                        Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                        Well, no, but it appears to, to the mages that end up there by whatever means. Clearly the phenomenal has something of a solidifying effect on them. I mean, this is just wild conjecture, but isn't the idea that the less-than-mundane realms are some sort of vestige of the supernal archetypes that govern them at least plausible?
                        It is more like this: imagine I have certain ideals concerning imagination and childhood innocence. I then use my considerable wealth garnered from my films to create a theme park that is a reflection of those ideals. At no point were these ideals physically there, nor is this park suddenly somewhere on the extension of reality between the physical world and those ideals. But if I walked through the park, I could see the overlay of my ideals much more easily than, say, Detroit (though I could still see elements there).

                        That is the relationship I was talking about. The Supernal Symbols of the Primal Wild may be more active or present when a Thyrsus walks in the Shadow, and the sights of the other paths may be influenced by those symbols as well (The Obrimos see Symbols of power as taking the forms of beasts, etc). At no point was the Supernal and those realms connected more so than any other part of the fallen world, they are just the "theme parks"

                        Supernal Verges are the step above, where those ideals have somehow become manifest.
