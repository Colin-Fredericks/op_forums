    Artifacts of the Seven Seas

    02-28-2026, 09:00 PM
    In taverns across the seven seas, stories are told of powerful Artifacts from the Golden Age of Piracy. According to the legends, they are drawn to the adventurous and free-spirited, setting sail with them towards new horizons and legends worth retelling. Some even believe they were made by the same entity that created the Codex, forming a set with it, embodying the connection pirates have with the sea. Even now they await the call of a new brave soul.

    Captain's Hoard (••• ••• •)
    Gnosis: 4
    Arcana: Matter 5
    Mana Capacity: 14
    Effects: Endless Bounty , Remote Control
    Utility Attainments: Permanence
    Description: A key carved from a single piece of petrified coral, it feels heavy in the hand and sturdy, despite being porous. Under the moonlight, an intricate carving of a ship can be seen along its shank, heading away from the bow.

    In order to activate it, the key must be pointed at a subject, or make contacts with it, while the user extends a finger along the shank, "pointing" the direction the ship sails. Turning it clockwise activates Endless Bounty and counterclockwise uses Remote Control. It is said many pirates have used it to arm and supply their troops with plentiful wealth, food, drink and armaments. A few use it to break their crew out of jail, unlock chests of loot or open their path. Some skilled users have even used it to bring their ships to life, making them far deadlier in ship combat, or a living terror against mutineers. Whatever treasure chest, or gate, this Artifact is supposed to unlock, is a Mystery that still challenges many an adventurous soul.

    Fair Winds (••• ••)
    Gnosis: 3
    Arcana: Time 4
    Mana Capacity: 10
    Effects: Green Light / Red Light, Veil of Moments
    Description: A brown tricorne hat made of the full grain leather of a giant, seafaring, bird cryptid. It seems to naturally fall into its shape, without the use of pins, laces or buttons. The interior lining looks like fine wool, but feels like down feathers. It is waxed by the oil the creature used on its feathers, making it completely waterproof. It is always a perfect fit on the user's head and will not fall off by itself, not in strong winds, not when the user dives underwater, or even if they are upside down. However, it does seem to have a sense of humor, as it will fall off if they trip.

    Whenever the user sings or hums a sea shanty, its abilities can be activated. It can allow sea journeys to be made much faster, for treasure hunters to stay ahead of their rivals, or used to sow chaos on one's pursuers. In dire situations, it can allow the crew to survive without rations, persist when there is no time to rest or keep a crewmate alive until a healer can be found.

    Fathomless Tide (••• •)
    Gnosis: 2
    Arcana: Life 4
    Mana Capacity: 8
    Effects: Transform Life
    Description: A long coat of excellent quality, which adjusts itself to match the measurements of the user. It is woven out of a midnight blue silk, its interior lined with a golden yellow velvet that is completely waterproof. It has an ornate silver braid trim, with silver buttons engraved with High Speech runes. A stylized depiction of tentacles is embroidered in silvery, serpentine patterns on the sleeves.

    Whenever it is buttoned in a specific sequence, its effect can be activated while it remains closed. It has been used to explore the most remote depths of the ocean, survive in the most inhospitable edges of the map, adapt when fighting a dangerous foe, escape deadly situations and to use devious tactics at sea.

    Horizon’s Call (•••)
    Gnosis: 2
    Arcana: Fate 3
    Mana Capacity: 6
    Effects: Serendipity
    Description: A meticulously crafted nautical compass, with a square case made of oak, with a dark varnish. High Speech runes are carved on all sides. The top of the case has them arranged on a geomantic compass.

    When a user opens the compass with a goal in mind and says in High Speech "show me a new horizon", the runes glow like blazing coals. If they roll it on a surface (a paper, a table, their arm), it imprints a map guiding the user to their desired goal, with the legends of the map giving tips. Once they use up all the rolls it grants, the map vanishes.

    Kraken's Eye (••• •••)
    Gnosis: 3
    Arcana: Space 4
    Mana Capacity: 12
    Effects: Scrying, Zoom In (as an Unveiling of Space)
    Utility Attainments: Sympathetic Range
    Description: An iron spyglass made of an organic looking material, which is warm to the touch and never corrodes. The length bears intaglios of tentacles reaching out from the objective lens, and both lenses are crystal clear.

    Whenever the user says "Open the eye of the storm", it causes the lens to become a mossy green cephalopod eye, glowing with an eerie inner light. Looking through the Kraken's Eye in this form allows it to utilize its ability. Navigators have used it to chart a trail through storms, lookouts have used it to spot islands on the horizon or enemy ships, captains use it to scout a location or keep an eye on their enemies.

    Parlay's End (••• ••• ••)
    Gnosis: 4
    Arcana: Space 5, Fate 2
    Mana Capacity: 16
    Effects: Banishment (Space ••••, Fate ••) (see below)
    Utility Attainments: Everywhere
    Description: A flintlock pistol assembled entirely from interlocking bricks of magnetite loadstone, with a carving of a lung dragon flying down the side plate. It is held together by intense ferromagnetic forces, yet it operates smoothly.

    Although it can be used as a normal flintlock, activating its effect requires the user to place a thumb in the point of the side plate where the dragon coils, before pulling the trigger. When a subject is hit by the spell, they are instantly teleported to where they will be the most delayed. They can end up on a deserted island, a busy tavern, amidst an emotionally charged situation, or in sight of their enemies, in whichever scenario inconveniences them the most.

    Red Sky (••• •••)
    Gnosis: 3
    Arcana: Forces 5
    Mana Capacity: 12
    Effects: Control Heat, Environmental Shield
    Description: A cutlass made of finely wrought steel, its surface is always polished to a mirror sheen, the occasional ripple appearing in reflections. Its circular handguard has a golden finish, with a stylized engraving of the sun. The handle is made of a deep blue shagreen.

    A user must address it by name, calling upon it as if it were sentient to use its abilities. Wielders invoke its aid when dueling foes, using bitter cold to disorient and weaken opponents without damaging potential loot. Some command it to overheat blades and force foes to disarm. When boarding an enemy vessel, captains will address it as a trusted crewmate, charging it with calling down scorching heat and biting cold upon the deck, weakening their foes even as their own crew fight protected from the effects.

    The Wave's Price (••• ••• ••)
    Gnosis: 4
    Arcana: Death 4, Matter 4
    Mana Capacity: 16
    Effects: Castaway's Plunder (see below)
    Description: A boarding axe with a head carved out of an ocean blue, crystalline material, which looks like frozen ice. The handle is made out of dense whale bone and wrapped in whale leather. Whenever sunlight hits the axe head, a caustic light pattern dances inside it.

    When the user holds it from the bottom of the handle, allowing for the maximum momentum to be built, they can unleash its effect. Any pieces of an object destroyed fall away as salt, and the user can choose to replenish either their stores of mana or the Wave's Price.
    ​
    New Spell: Castaway's Plunder (Death ••••, Matter ••••)
    Practice: Unraveling
    Primary Factor: Potency
    Withstand: Durability
    Suggested Rote Skills: Larceny, Survival, Weaponry

    Description: A spell developed by mages during the Golden Age of Piracy, so they could heal and restore their power whenever they found themselves stranded exploring the Mysteries. Whenever an object is hit by this spell, it loses [Potency] Structure as its Pattern is thorn apart and mana is harvested from it. The caster receives an equal amount of mana as the object lost Structure.

    What were you thinking design wise ?

    After having worked on a pirate themed Legacy (The Crystal Navigators) and reading Dark Eras 2: The Devil and the Deep Blue Sea, I was inspired to make pirate themed Artifacts. They were designed to represent the equipment of a pirate king, both as a display of their wealth and as the tools they would use in their adventures. The material they were made of and their names would also symbolize their connection to the sea.

    The Captain’s Hoard is a treasure key, inspired by the one Davy Jones had in Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Man’s Chest. It allows them to make any treasure chest a source of infinite wealth, it also acts as a skeleton key that can open any lock. It can allow them to take control of their ships, like Blackbeard did in Pirates of the Caribbean: On Stranger Tides. Lastly, it acts as a potential plot hook, with what it could possibly unlock being deliberately left open.

    Fair Winds is made out of a roc, and is a captain’s hat that is easy to wear in any situation. It can be used to tilt the winds in their favor when time is of the essence, help them get away in high action chases, or allow them to persevere when things get rough. It's named after the idiom “Fair winds and following seas”, a blessing sailors use for a smooth and safe voyage.

    Fathomless Tide is a long coat that allows the pirate to be suited to explore any part of the sea. It can allow them to take on a Cthulu-like appearance as well. The design is inspired by Vergil’s trench coat from the Devil May Cry games.

    Horizon’s Call is inspired by Jack Sparrow’s compass, as well as the Mao Kun map. It creates a treasure map to anything the pirate seeks. Since it is cast with Fate 3, I imagine it would normally be used with Reach assigned to instant use and using a chosen skill, allowing the pirate to find a path to their goal that best leverages their abilities. The compass case is made out of oak for two reasons. The first is that oak is an antiseptic wood, so getting a splinter won't kill a user, which is why many ships at the time were made of it.The second is because it symbolizes strength, endurance and wisdom. The varnish is used to make it waterproof.
    ​
    Kraken’s Eye is a spyglass that allows them to keep a better lookout on their surroundings than ever before, and is also a powerful tool for scouting. The pirate can also scry on their enemies, helping them stay one step ahead of them. The design is inspired by the Necronomicon cutscene in Cyanide’s Call of Cthulhu game, and it is implied that the Artifact is alive.

    Parlay’s End allows them to teleport away any foes the pirate cannot negotiate with and is not ready to face. The target is even placed in a scenario that keeps them too busy to teleport back or continue to give chase to the pirate. It is made with magnetic loadstones, which could be found at sea, and were used to create compasses. In this way, Parlay’s End is a magnet for trouble to those it shoots. The design is inspired by the Mosaic Culverin from Salt and Sanctuary.

    Red Sky is a cutlass that helps a pirate fight dirty. Control Heat can be used to penalize an opponent’s actions by creating an Extreme Environment. When used at higher Potency, it can be used as a constant source of damage, as well as instantly superheat or freeze objects, which has lots of creative applications. Environmental Shield guarantees that no matter how rough weather gets at sea, the pirate and their crewmates can ride out the storm. The name is based on the old sailor’s adage of “Red Sky at night, sailor’s delight. Red Sky in morning, sailor’s warning”. The golden sun engraving symbolizes the ability to create a scorching heat, and the blue shagreen (shark’s leather) represents its ability to call forth a freezing cold. Lastly, the activation command was inspired by Sir Mirror from the Castlevania show, and was suggested by Cauthon.

    The Wave’s Price is a pirate mage’s survival tool, allowing them to scavenge mana from just about anything. The design is inspired by stalhrim weapons from the Elder Scrolls series, and the use of whale bone by the Dishonored series.

    That concludes the design notes, hope you had as much fun reading them as I had writing them. A huge thanks to Cauthon and NuriaAge for all their feedback.
