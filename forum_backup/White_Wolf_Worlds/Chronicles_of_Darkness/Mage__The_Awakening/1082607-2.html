                            Originally posted by 21C Hermit View Post
                            Doesn't "nothing and no one else" imply that the Loving entity in fact has a Strong connection?
                            It implies that the Loving entity is the only thing that can have a Strong connection maintained with it, but that in no way necessitates that a mystical tie of that strength does exist.

                            Feelings and awareness are not the sole determiners of sympathy, and if it did work that way then there'd be very little incentive for mages to seek out pieces of their enemies for the sake of sympathetic magic and very little use in trying to track a mage through the sympathies of their victims even without the time limit of borrowed sympathies, or contrariwise there'd be very little reason to not endeavor to care about as little as possible for as much of the time as possible unless you needed to benefit from someone else's sympathetic magic.

                            And what do you think about the relation between B and C (in my example)? You're good with logic and language, so your input would greatly help.
                            I think you're putting stronger emphasis on the causative role of knowledge and emotion in relation to a supernatural quality that is held by knives and hair in addition to people.

                            Consider the role of the supernatural in the ChroD setting; it's regularly obscure, either deliberately or due to some quirk of existence, which means most phenomena aren't going to be attracted directly to people in frequent contact with a deep and wide net of human connections. Odds are good that if you're Loved you're at least isolated enough to be open to the possibility of having the entity in question become just about The Most Important Thing in your life in terms of casual consideration and how your circumstances tend to arrange themselves.

                            Only being able to maintain a Medium connection with most people doesn't meaningfully impact the emotional content of your social life; Strong connections are the domain of direct biological relatives, bodily samples, and central long-time nonfamilial relationships, and the only stronger form of connection comes with pregnancy or magic. Being Loved only messes with your dating pool or your nights on the town to the extent that having the attention of any known or unknown supernatural force does in this setting.

                            B is obsessed with A with or without a sympathetic bond and continues to be a source of A's Merit. A does not know about this and doesn't really see much in the way of inherent drawbacks to the mere fact of the cap, because a Medium bond accommodates the upper limit of metaphysical intimacy for the vast majority of relationships any given person will have with any given other person. If they have children or their parents are still alive then they might have to exert a bit of extra effort to be quite as close with those people as they would without the damper, but they're not prevented from loving their family just because they're Loved by B.

                            A and C become dear friends or lovers or whatever other signifier you would opt to describe the example of a Medium connection with, and this only remotely begins to present an obstacle to their becoming closer in that the natural progression of the universe does not grace the relationship with the same frequency of recurrence as it would if they were best friends/lifelong spouses/murderer and murder weapon and A hadn't caught the eyelike appendages of some spooky nonsense from a dark corner of their house's level geometry.

                            My understanding was it's something like Gauntlet strength, and how it can be high from one side but lower from the other as result of +1 Reach on Place of Power, but is usually the same from either side even when manipulated. There's one connection between two subjects, but its strength could vary in certain circumstances.
                            And this is managed at present by things like Keyed application of Veil Sympathy, but the Gauntlet is a barrier to smooth interaction between two worlds that are inherently reflected at a somewhat-distorted remove from each other where a sympathetic connection literally exists solely as a magical quality signifying how "close" two things are.

                                Its really simple. Sleepwalker A has the loved merit coming from B. B Loves A and so has a Strong connection with A. A might love B back (B might also have the loved merit) and have a strong connection to B, A might also have a strong connection to C. But C cant have a strong connection to A because of the merit, so only has a medium connection. Nothing else can have a strong connection to A (only B can), A can have a strong connection to as many things as he likes, even to C even thought C can only have a medium connection back. Note B might only have a medium connection to A, but B is still the only person capable of creating a strong connection with A.
