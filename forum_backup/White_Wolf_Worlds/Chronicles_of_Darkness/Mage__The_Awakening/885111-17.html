Corrective commentary from any devs waiting in the wings is welcome.

                        Sanctum Bindings(• to •••)
                        Prerequisite:                         Sanctum ••+
                        Effect: Through sacred geomancy, you have learned to provide stability to released spells in your Sanctum.

                        Benefit: For each dot, one released spell is stable and doesn't risk paradox for its duration as long as its direct effects do not leave the area of the Sanctum. Once leaving though, the countdown begins and will not stop, even if the spell's effects once again are limited to only the Sanctum. Generally, this is used with imbued items to have less worry about their effects going awry.

                            Somatic Gestures (•••)
                            Prerequisites: Proximus, Mystic Tools 1+
                            Effect: You can incorporate the gestures and mudras common to Rotes to bolster your Blessings. Pick one Skill for each Blessing you possess or gain - you can use that Skill as a Yantra for casting that Blessing.
                            Note: The Storyteller must approve the Skill chosen for the Blessing. and is encouraged to keep to the suggested Rote Skills for that spell. Some thematic deviation is allowed, but having the same Skill for all of the Blessings is frowned upon.

                                Alright, so I had an idea for a merit to give Proximi a bit of extra free reach with their blessings, and looking back through here it's kinda similar to Vent0's "[Arcana] Prodigy" stuff but at the same time it's a bit more limited and streamlined and goes in a different direction so, just see what you think:

                                Enlightened Understanding (•, •••, or •••••)

                                Prerequisites: Proximus, Occult ••

                                The proximus understands the fundamental principles behind her blessings a little better than most, and can use that understanding to use said blessings to greater effect without risking paradox.

                                With one dot, instead of a single free reach when using a blessing, the proximus instead receives the number that a mage who was capable of casting all of the blessings she knows (and had no more skill in the arcarna than was required to do that) would, in the same manner as an artifact.
                                In other words, a proximus who knew a one, two and three dot blessing based on spells from the life arcarnum would receive three free reach for the one-dot blessing, two for the two dot blessing, and remain at one free reach for the three dot blessing.

                                With three dots in this merit she receives an extra reach on top of this for all blessings. With five dots, two extra reaches.

                                                Merit: Way of the Platonic Forge (●)
                                                Prerequisite: Prime ●●●, Adamant Hand ●●, "Platonic Form" rote
                                                Effect: An Arrow is never defined solely by weapons, but they’re usually handy to have when exploring the world’s dark places. Being able to forge a blade or shield on the anvil of your own will is a neat trick, but Mana resists being trapped in mundane constructs for any length of time. Platonic Form’s steep cost will quickly empty the reserves of any mage in an extended fight. An inventive Obrimos, keen on traveling light, devised this technique to compensate.

                                                If you Reach to cast Platonic Form with Advanced Duration, each use of the construct as a tool or weapon no longer costs 1 Mana from its supply. The construct’s maximum equipment bonus cannot exceed the mage’s Prime dots, and the spell cannot last longer than one scene.

                                                    [Thread stumbles out of a Time Iris]

                                                    So, I had the idea for a Merit (or possibly even micro-template) based upon a Sleeper that switches to Sleepwalker (or even Sleepwalker+, with some Spells) when exposed to Magic. But that wears off and they go back to Sleep (only for the cycle to continue the next time conditions are met).

                                                    This is basically me just spit-balling ideas for now - no expectation of it being done or serious is implied.

                                                    Insomagus (• to •••••)
                                                    Prerequisites: Sleeper
                                                    Effect: Your grasp of the Supernal ebbs and flows, leaving you drifting in and out of the Lie. Whenever you are exposed to powerful magic use (such as someone gaining an Exceptional Success on a spell casting roll or triggering Dissonance on an effect that has Withstand beyond dots in this merit), you absorb some of that magic and partially "wake up", gaining the Awoken Condition. When you have the Awoken Condition, you gain access to a number of Arcana dots equal to the dots in this merit as determined by the circumstances of your temporary Awakening. You are considered to have them all as Ruling Arcana, and can spend Willpower in place of Mana.
                                                    Drawback: This confers no protection against the actual Breaking Point that triggers activation. Additionally, once the Awoken Condition Lapses, it is usually replaced by another Condition such as Amnesia (regarding the Awoken time), Addicted (regarding experiencing it again), or Frightened (of repeat circumstances).

                                                    -Condition-
                                                    Awoken
                                                    You've seen passed the Lie and become aware to the deeper Truths of the world. While this will fade given time or exposure to Paradox, you can grasp the nature of the world like the Wise do. You gain Peripheral and Active Mage Sight, temporarily swap Integrity for Wisdom, and have an effective Gnosis of 1.
                                                    Possible Sources: The Insomagus merit triggering, Breaking Points due witnessing Supernal Magic, Powerful Prime spells.
                                                    Resolution: After a number of days equal to your current Wisdom (it can resolve immediately if Wisdom drops below this timespan).
                                                    Special: Paradox Potency can reduce the number of days before the Condition resolves on a 1-for-1 basis.

                                                    [Comments: I'm thinking of the "trigger" might also absorb the magic (ES spell fails instead, persistent magic ends immediately) to fuel the effect. Also thinking of exact mechanics - Arcana dots seems a bit variable, though flexible in effect. I'm thinking it also needs a more drastic downside, though the Breaking Points incurred, and limited duration impede the "utility" of it. Also, not sure how balanced things are between the Condition and the Merit.]

                                                            (Triple posting, ugh. But no one has replied, this is new, and I don't want to complicate Quoting and Liking by having a giant post with different ideas/topics...)

                                                            Stub for another Sleepwalker (mostly) Merit:

                                                            Supernal Inspiration (• to •••)
                                                            Prerequisites: Non-Mage
                                                            Effect: You can gain understanding from the mystic truth that has been entrusted to you and leverage it to empower your own abilities. Whenever you are holding a Spell for a Mage, you gain this merit's dots in a bonus to uses of your own Supernatural merits and abilities that are thematically similar to the Arcana in the spell.

                                                            [Comments: The idea is - holding a spell gives you a bit of a boost, encouraging such behavior. It is limited to Supernatural powers, so it isn't a general bonus, and it needs to be "thematic" to the spells/magics' Arcana. So holding a Forces spell can boost you Pyrokinesis, but not Omen Sensitivity. Thoughts, though?]

                                                            This one is really more intended for Proximi, since their own inherent spell casting limitations reduce the benefit from "free spell control" to "I actually have spell control now". Still, the other restrictions may limit it enough for general Mages, and there isn't any particular reason why they wouldn't be able to do the same. Depending on how flexible the Storyteller wants to make it, they could either have the "bonus" Spell Control used first (so Gnosis derived Spell Control remains free) or added on "after" Gnosis (which means you'd have to end all "bonus" spells before you can cast on other targets without Reaching).

                                                            Body As Sanctum (• to •••••)
                                                            Prerequisites: Proximi or Mage
                                                            Effect: Some people say they consider their body as a temple - for you, it is a bit more literal. You can treat your body as a Sanctum, increasing allowable Spell Control as long as the spells are limited to your person, up to the lower of you current Stamina or the dots in this merit.
                                                            Drawback: If your body is a temple, violating it or making it unclean renders the benefits null and void. You lose the benefits of this merit for 24 hours if you Scour your Pattern, gain a Paradox Condition, or (for Proximus) trigger the Severe form of your Curse.

                                                            Here's more of a plot hook, but based on a merit. It could have wider uses, as well.

                                                            Astral Voyeur (•••)
                                                            Effect: Whenever you connect deeply with someone (such as by helping them fulfill an Aspiration, or satisfy their Virtue or Vice), the next time you sleep, you automatically enter that character's Oneiros.

                                                            Astral Stalker (••)
                                                            Prerequisites: Astral Voyeur
                                                            Effect: You can return to the souls of those you have connected to previously with Astral Voyeur. By sleeping or meditating, you can enter their Oneiros instead of your own as per the systems for entering the Astral.

                                                            Personal Oblation (•••)
                                                            Prerequisites: Sleepwalker
                                                            Effect: The Sleepwalker's soul forms a connection to the Supernal, even if they can't perceive it. They may perform a special ritual (chosen at the time this merit is selected) once a day to generate a single point of Mana. Unless they possess some other way to store it, the Mana condenses into Tass (such as special liquids if the ritual involves brewing, or enchanted bars or coins if it involves smelting, etc.). The Sleepwalker cannot generate more Mana if they are already near 3 or more points worth in Tass form, though they can store excess amounts elsewhere.
                                                            [Comments: Sleepwalkers as Mana factories. More useful for powering other Mana-based abilities if they don't have access to Mages or Hallows, and as a spring-board to other Merits - such as a generalized Imbued Item merit for Awakened-lite Mad Scientists. The variable ritual nature can evoke witches brewing Tass potions, Enlightened Scientist conducting experiments to charge Tass batteries, and more besides.]

                                                            Mystic Panoply (•••+, Special)
                                                            Prerequisites: Sleepwalker
                                                            Effect: You have magic inherent to you that manifests in your belongings, as opposed to what you can wield directly. Treat this merit as a special version of Enhanced Item, except the magic is tied more to you, and you can modify which object(s) is/are affected and the spell effects by conducting a 3 hour long ritual (specific to the character) and spending a point of Willpower.
                                                            [Comments: This gives a little specific magic to a character, without the considerations of full spell-casting. Because it is limited to Enhanced Item - persistent, rather than activated effects - this reduces the potential cheese the owner can get up to.]
