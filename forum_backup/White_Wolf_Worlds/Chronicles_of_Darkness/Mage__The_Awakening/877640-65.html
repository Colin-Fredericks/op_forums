                    Spell: Reveal the Shadows' Secrets (Death ●)
                    Practice: Unveiling
                    Primary Factor: Duration
                    Suggested Rote Skills: Investigation, Occult, Stealth

                    Let the Obrimos magnify trace glimmers of starlight and the Thyrsus borrow the eyes of cats; the Moros claim they know what it is to truly see in darkness. The shadows hold no secrets from the mage, but she loses the ability to see colors; the eyes of the dead see only ten thousand shades of grey.

                    +1 Reach: The shadows do more than lift their veils; they whisper, and in their susurrus reveal a myriad of hidden secrets. The mage can see in all directions and from all points within her sensory range simultaneously, ignoring walls and other obstacles, so long as those points have some substantial measure of shadow or darkness; beneath the blazing eye of a noontime sun or the moonscape glare of a stadium's floodlights, the shadows are too weak and thin to offer much guidance. While within or targeting such a point, the mage penalizes ambush attempts by Potency.

                    (Reference to https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F157geaXp_w while casting may, at GM's option, be used as a +1-die Yantra.)

                            Originally posted by Hardwire99 View Post

                            The bolded sentence is the Purview of space, By definition the sensory range is blocked by obstacles, and shifting your senses to a different location is remote viewing.
                            Something being the purview of one Arcanum doesn't mean it's solely the purview of that Arcanum, especially if other Arcana only have access to limited versions appropriate to their purview. Life for example has spells that shift your senses into other living beings. If Death alone lets you create physical objects from shadows and bodily turn into shadow, I'd say it lets you extend your senses through them as well.

                            The phrasing is a little weird, though. I guess "sensory range" is meant to imply the same thing as the Space version ("She cannot perceive things farther away than her normal perceptions might allow") but either way I'd personally key it to the spell's Scale factor.

                            The description of the Reach effect also implies something more narratively interesting than "see everywhere all the time". I prefer the idea that the shadows actually talk, pointing out objects of obvious interest such as hostiles but have to be actively questioned on matters such as "on what floor lies the Chalice of Arat?"

                            e: or rather, something being the purview of one Arcanum doesn't mean you can't achieve similar results with other Arcana

                                    Originally posted by Terrorforge View Post
                                    Something being the purview of one Arcanum doesn't mean it's solely the purview of that Arcanum, especially if other Arcana only have access to limited versions appropriate to their purview. Life for example has spells that shift your senses into other living beings. If Death alone lets you create physical objects from shadows and bodily turn into shadow, I'd say it lets you extend your senses through them as well.

                                    The phrasing is a little weird, though. I guess "sensory range" is meant to imply the same thing as the Space version ("She cannot perceive things farther away than her normal perceptions might allow") but either way I'd personally key it to the spell's Scale factor.

                                    The description of the Reach effect also implies something more narratively interesting than "see everywhere all the time". I prefer the idea that the shadows actually talk, pointing out objects of obvious interest such as hostiles but have to be actively questioned on matters such as "on what floor lies the Chalice of Arat?"

                                    e: or rather, something being the purview of one Arcanum doesn't mean you can't achieve similar results with other Arcana

                                    Pretty much, yes. I borrowed from "The Outward & Inward Eye" verbatim for that line, because I wanted to stay on the right side of "it's fine if different Arcana accomplish similar things at different ratings" vs. "this application of Arcana X makes a core capability of Arcana Y obsolete." Death will let you know there's someone hiding in the shadow behind a closed door; Space would let you trace his silhouette on the door, which is why Space 1 keeps exclusive access to reducing range/cover/concealment penalties and reduces ambush attempts to chance dice (and Space would function regardless of ambient lighting).

                                    I like the idea of having to pause to make out details. I'm not sold on the shadows literally talking at one dot; giving them that kind of "life" feels more comfortable at higher ratings (like 1e's "Shadow Slave.") But I could see a situation in which the shadows reveal five men creeping up the stairs in the darkened apartment building, and you have to take an instant action to scrutinize their faces and pinpoint which one is the Seer leading the mooks.

                                                So, I've made this list to get a better picture how a technomage would work technology (mostly computers) into spells. What do you guys think?

                                                Accessible Computing (Forces •)

                                                Practice: Compelling and Unveiling
                                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                                Suggested Rote Skill: Computer, Expression, Persuasion

                                                This spell allows the mage to give commands directly to a device without the need for any input devices like keyboards, computer mice, touchpads, etc. As well as allowing the mage the mage to see the computers responses without any output devices like monitors or speakers. Both effects still happen at about the same speed as if those devices were used however.

                                                This spell can bypass any penalties from Condition or Tilts that would normally prevent or hinder the use of any electronic devices (like Blind, Arm Wreck, etc.).

                                                Magnetoreception (Forces •)

                                                Practice: Unveiling
                                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                                Suggested Rote Skill: Science, Survival, Animal Ken

                                                The earth's magnetic field has a strength of 0.5 Gauss (Unit) and animals use it to orient themselves. But this presents a paradox, for the strength of these fields is many orders less than an elementary chemical act.
                                                As well as not being strong enough to generate appreciable heating of tissue or cause irritation. So, how do animals sense it? who knows? I don't! But with this spell the mage can sense and use the earth's fields as well as most any animal.

                                                This spell gives the mage the benefit of the Direction Sense Merit, but only so long as the earth's magnetic field can be detected and isn't being altered by any strong magnetic forces in the area.

                                                +1 Reach: In addition to the earth's magnetic fields the mage can sense electrical fields in general. This can be used to locate living beings, in the same way as some sharks do. These sharks can locate prey even when said prey has buried itself, the mage now has the same ability.

                                                Firewall (Forces ••)

                                                Practice: Shielding
                                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Computer, Subterfuge

                                                A shielding spell knows what harms its subject and prevents it. Much like how a Matter shielding spell can protect the mage from a caustic atmosphere and yet allow a normal one. So too does this spell prevent any harmful software and traffic from reaching its technological subjects while still allowing the harmless.

                                                Supernatural attempts to cause digital harm provoke a Clash of Wills.

                                                Rewrite System (Forces ••)

                                                Practice: Ruling
                                                Primary Factor: Potency
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Computer, Expression

                                                With this spell a mage can change a system’s programming. This means she can rewrite existing programs or install wholly new ones. This can be used to create handy applications or to instantly give systems a virus or spyware.

                                                The changes are not instant. The spell gives the system all the information and commands needed to make the changes, but the system still needs time to install/apply these.

                                                The effects of this spell are lasting which means that the changes made can be noticed.
                                                If the mage makes only subtle changes however apply the following rules for discovery by any users:

                                                - A user knowledgeable about either this specific system or about systems of this type in general may make a reflexive Intelligence + Computer roll penalized by the mage's Computer Skill to discover any changes the mage made.
                                                - If the user is not familiar with the system, or failed the reflexive roll, but still suspects tampering they may make an extended Intelligence + Computer penalized by the Mage's Computer Skill with a target number of Potency to discover it.

                                                +1 Reach: The effects of this spell take place instantly.

                                                The Future Is Now (Forces •••)

                                                Practice: Perfecting
                                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Computer, Science

                                                This spell allows computer hardware to go far beyond its limits. For every level of Potency double the hardware’s performance. For example, Potency 3 would be eight times the performance.

                                                This spell increases a computers equipment bonus. For personal computers, this bonus is equal to Availability and normally ranges from +1 to +4. This spell doubles the bonus per level of Potency to a maximum of +4.

                                                Die bonuses for anything beyond the scale of PCs and similar devices are Storyteller fiat. This includes things like supercomputers.

                                                For supercomputers, as a rule of thumb assume that they are about 10.000 times faster at the calculations they were designed for than your average pc. So, you’d need Potency 11 to bring an average pc up to supercomputer level. Using this spell on a supercomputer would probably result in record breaking performance.

                                                Digital Citizen (Forces ••••)

                                                Practice: Patterning
                                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Computer, Intimidation, Persuasion

                                                With this spell the mage can temporally make digital systems aid the subject regardless of the subject's actual skill in interacting with such devices. Systems will try to consider the subject's intentions rather than only following his physical inputs into the system. This spell doesn't actually increase the subject's knowledge, but effectively increases the subject's Computer Skill by Potency up to the normal maximum.

                                                +2 Reach: By spending a point of Mana, the Computer Skill may be raised above the normal maximum.

                                                Technostress (Forces ••••)

                                                Practice: Unraveling
                                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                                Withstand: Composure
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Computer, Stealth, Subterfuge

                                                In the face of ever more ubiquitous recording devices and a greater ability for people to communicate with one another, the Guardians often have a difficult time protecting the Veil. But the Guardians have ways of denying people these advantages, such as this spell.
                                                With this spell the mage can temporally make digital systems oppose the subject. Every system and device that the subject uses will start having problems or otherwise do anything but what the subject requires.
                                                This prevents the subject from gaining any aid from electronic devices whatsoever.

                                                Hologram (Forces ••••)

                                                Practice: Patterning
                                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Craft, Science, Expression

                                                The mage can bend light fields to create holograms. These hologram looks like the real thing for all intents and purposes. But make no sound and any attempt to touch them will reveal they are not real but holograms.

                                                +1 Reach: The holograms can now also create sound.

                                                Add Death 3: If a ghost is included in the spell's subjects, the ghost may control the holograms.
                                                Add Mind 3: If a goetia is included in the spell's subjects, the goetia may control the holograms.
                                                Add Spirit 3: If a spirit is included in the spell's subjects, the spirit may control the holograms.

                                                Social Engineering (Forces •••••)

                                                Practice: Making
                                                Primary Factor: Potency
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Politics, Socialize, Subterfuge

                                                Social media facilitates the creation and sharing of information and ideas, with this spell the mage creates social networks where none existed before.

                                                The mage chooses a target organization or group and fabricates a favorable digital history between the mage and the target. How this history looks is entirely up to the mage, maybe records show the mage has worked there, that he is an important customer or that he has an appointment at three. None of this history actually happened of course and people won't recognize the mage. But so long as the mage pretends it did, and nobody calls his bluff, they may make use of these organizations as the Merits Allies, Contact, Status or even Resources.

                                                For each level of Potency, the subject gains one dot in one of the following Merits: Allies, Contacts, Status or Resources.

                                                Drawback: This spell takes advantage of the fact that in large organizations nobody can know everybody. Consequently, this spell cannot be used effectively on smaller groups as they are far more likely to see through your ruse.

                                                Neo-Luddite (Forces •••••)

                                                Practice: Unmaking
                                                Primary Factor: Potency
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Science, Survival

                                                Within the area of the spell the mage destroys all manifestation of electromagnetic forces that are commonly used in technology. Electric currents, wireless transmissions, lasers, etc.
                                                This spell is used in a process called "rewildening" by a Free Council anarcho-primitivist philosophy that supposes that technology is purely an instrument of the Lie and that the future Atlantis will be ruled by magic utterly without technology.

                                                Add Matter 5: This spell also destroys any manifestation of advanced materials. This could be plastics as well as any alloy more advanced than bronze. Although the specifics depend on the mage casting the spell.

                                                Update:
                                                Changed 'Cyber self-defence' to 'Firewall'.
                                                Updated 'Rewrite System' to include Potency as a target number.
                                                Rewrote description of 'Social Media' and renamed it as 'Social Engineering'.
