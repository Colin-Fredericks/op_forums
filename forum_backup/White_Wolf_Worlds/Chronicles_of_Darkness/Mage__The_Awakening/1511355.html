    [Homebrew] Half-Awakened: Magical Dynasties

    05-11-2023, 10:33 AM
    A homebrew I wrote for the Proximi, wishing to both boost them to be more powerful and influential in Awakened society and change their themes slightly to be a magical pseudo-royalty, amassign power over generations like Nasuverse magi do. I actually playtested it, and it works pretty good, putting experienced Proximi with extra experience somewhere to the level of starting Awakened.

    Half-Awakened

    Proximi are half-Awakened Sleepwalkers, who inherit their link with Supernal Realms with their magical blood. They don't have the full power of Arcana, yet they have power of their Blessings. What's more important is that Proximi are much more likely to Awaken than other Sleepwalkers. Usually at least one Proximi in generation Awakens to the parent Path of it's Dynasty. That Proximi becomes the Scion, holding all magical and political power in his Dynasty. Proximi Awakening is always hereditary; only Scion's descendants are able to Awaken, which leads for unusual obsession (by Awakened standards) with legacy and family line.

    Proximi are called half-Awakened, because Blessings are clearly Supernal in origin; Scrutinizing them will show the symbols of Parent Path of Dynasty, weaved together in clear and beautiful Imagos. Those Imagos are the same for all members of the Dynasty, as if they were using Rotes. Lacking intuition of Gnosis and knowledge of Arcana, Proximi are unable to construct their own Imagos and have to rely on Blessings, though they can improve their concentration with a few Yantras which resonate with them. The better part of that deal is that Proximi never have Paradox; their attempts to expand spell result in spellcasting failure and Curse flare. Mages theorize that Curse somehow shields Proximi from Paradox, filtering it and not allowing Abyss to enter the Fallen, while hurting Proximi via equivalent exchange rule, but details are unclear and not studied well.

    While power to use them hides inside Proximi blood, Blessings require time and effort to learn. First few come naturally; Proximi explain them as especially enthralling thoughts and bright dreams about the world. Those thoughts and dreams are filled with Supernal imagery and carry Imagoes inside them. But after that, hard work starts. Proximi have to learn their Blessings and push themselves to spontaneously develop them with hardships and trials. Scions often guide their family through this process, and they are uniquely suited to do this; but if there is no living Scion, Proximi will have to do with books, descriptions and their own dreams.

    The origins of Proximi are unclear. Some Dynasties were created with proven Imperial magic, results of ancient or recent archmasters wanting to have servants for centuries-spanning plans for their Ascension. Some seem to be byproducts of successful Ascensions warping the conceptual reality and enthroning Ascended in Supernal Realms; fall of Camelot and Merlin's Ascension created nine lines of Pendragon Dynasty, six of which survive to the current day. But there are many more Dynasties those origins are not that clear; their symbolism only hints at their possible origins. The most knowledgeable scholars of Mysterium and Silver Ladder think that Proximi Dynasties are result of Supernal "touching" the Fallen for long enough time to become part of blood and inheritance - as in Supernal Verges, Emanation Realms... and sometimes, ruins of long-lost Atlantis.

    ​Magical Dynasties

    Because of nature of Proximi's Awakening and fact that they always happen on the same Path, it's possible for Scions to transfer their arcane knowledge and power to their successors. Sometimes that happens while Scion is still alive, but often new Scion Awakens only after old one has passed on. Nevertheless, over ages most of Proximi Dynasties amassed serious magical and political power, accruing it generation over generation in the manner not unlike kings of old. Despite that, Scions are still a very small minority against Awakened, and while they do have a headstart, many Dynasties have fallen when they tried to rule other Awakened as kings did. Those who survived did that by allying themselves with Orders, in many cases subjugating themselves to their will and oversight.

    Proximi Dynasties are often insular and elitist, setting themselves apart from common rubble and thinking of themselves as magical elite. In some sense they are; Proximi's Blessings are almost as powerful as Awakened magic and make most "lower" sorcerors green with envy. That alone would win them positions of power and prestige within Orders, but many Proximi are also cunning politicians, groomed for servitude to the allied Order and possible Awakening. Because of that, most Proximi with Blessings will be a relatively high-ranked member of their Order, raised above common Sleepwalkers and sorcerors and even recently Awakened.

    Unfortunately for members of dynasty, that means excellency is expected. Those who fail cannot expect a humane treatment, and will find themselves relegated to menial tasks - or, sometimes, something even worse. Living as a Proximi is like being a member of royalty - there are privileges, but also duties you are expected to perform. Even those who did not develop Blessings are duty-bound to their Dynasty - and one of those duties is bearing children and not allowing Dynasty to die out. Thankfully, "genes" of Proximi is dominant, allowing children of Proximi and non-Proximi to develop Blessings; but that does not stop stereotypes of "marrying below". It is also not advised to marry someone who isn't "in the know" simply because it's next to impossible to hide nature of Proximi from outsider for too long.

    The Scion is dead, long live the Scion

    Sometimes, Scion dies without children. Sometimes, he dies with them. In this case, inheritance line is broken, and Dynasty is doomed to extinction. Attempts to sire children with his seed or her eggs posthumously will fail, or produce unique Horrors. Other Proximi can have children of their own, but without living Scion, their seed will weaken, until no Proximi with Blessings are born, and line is considered extinct. At this point, all hope is lost.

    Or so it would seem. Sometimes, descendants of Proximi Dynasties will Awaken. If they Awaken on the same Path as their ancestor Dynasty, there is a small chance that Dynasty will be reborn in them, making them new Scion and empowering their seed or eggs to carry inheritance further. This is always a unique and fated event, often appearing in omens and hinted at by powers-that-be ordering to help the nascent Dynasty resurrection - or stop it.

    ​Servitude in Alliance

    Historically Proximi were separate to Orders; some of them, like infamous Merovingians, considered themselves "above" the Mages, explaining that with their Supernal blood and theories about being descendants of Atlantis royalty. As Diamond and Seers number grew, they started to clash with Proximi Dynasties more and more; while Proximi Scions had the advantage of inherited arcane knowledge and Blessings of their family members, ultimately they were outnumbered and outgunned. Some Dynasties refused to bend the knee and were decimated; others flew away into hidden realms and Fallen frontiers; a few allied themselves with powerful Legacies and Nameless Orders, but most of them decided to secure their survival and peace by agreeing to become part of Diamond or bending the knee and serving the Iron Pyramid.

    Eventually, once Dynasties regrew their numbers, they became an important political forces in their Orders. Their inheritance allow them to build arcane knowledge and resources over generations, giving them an edge over those who started as a Sleeper; this same inheritance allowed to nurture connections and build long-lasting alliances. This inevitably creates conflict; Proximi conflict with other Sleepwalkers, sorcerors and non-Proximi Awakened inside Orders for positions of importance. Ultimately, though, while Proximi have a lot of pull, they are outnumbered and outgunned; for each Scion there are dozens of "common" Mages.

    Orders strive to influence Dynasties. Their Scions are pushed to be exemplars of their Order; much is expected of them, and many find their hands full with duties right after their Awakening. Even common family members are expected to at least pay a lip service to the Order ethos; those who refuse to do that universally met with derision and contempt. Free Council often proclaims that authoritarian nature of Diamond Orders is open to sight in those relationships, but in practice they treat "their" Proximi Dynasties in the same way, if not with worse disdain for "silver-spooned" Proximi who did not "earn" their magic.

    Price of Power

    Like any power, power of Blessings has a price. As soon as Proximus develops his first Blessing, the Curse of his Dynasty will start to manifest in his life. This Curse is connected to the Proximus's Blessings; it worsens when he reaches too far in his attempts to use his power. Many Proximi have attempted to remove the Curse, and some have even succeeded at that, but price is always the same: they lose all their Blessings and can't develop them ever again.

    Mechanically, Curse is a two-part Condition. The first part is a lower-level difficulty that should come up in play, but is not crippling. This stage of the Condition is persistent and cannot be removed or suppressed even by magic, but generates Beats when it impedes the character. Proximus character can try to remove the Curse, but it will require epic effort, intervention from powers-that-be and terrible price: losing all the Blessings without Sanctity of Merits covering the costs. Curse cannot be removed by any means short of Imperial Magic unless Proximus truly and freely rejects his lineage.

    Second part of Curse kicks in when character is forced, by magic or otherwise, to try to escape the curse, or when Proximus Overreaches on his Blessing and rolls successes on his Paradox roll. This part of Curse is also a Condition, but is less permanent, more severe and should have quantifiable effect which can get progressively worse with more Paradox successes.

    Mechanics

    Parent Path: Proximi are always linked to one of the five Supernal Realms, and except in extremely rare circumstances always become members of that Path if they Awaken. If Proximi Awakens on the "wrong" Path, he is not considered a Scion, and other of Scion's descendants will likely Awaken and continue the Dynasty.

    Blessing Arcana: Proximi may purchase Blessings from their Parent Path’s Ruling Arcana and one other Arcanum, determined by the Dynasty.

    Dynasty Skills: Dynasties have 3 Skills for using Blessings, but the Skill being used must make symbolic sense as with any other Imago. For example: House Pendragon has access to Crafts, Weaponry and Expression, while the Sons of Bezalel have access to Academics, Crafts and Science.

    Dynasty Boons: Dynasties have their unique Boons - situations or conditions resonating with their symbolic nature, in which their Blessings can be exceptionally powerful.

    Mana Capacity: Proximi have a Mana pool like a mage, with a maximum Mana capacity of 10. They can regain Mana by meditation at a Hallow like a mage, and every dynasty has at least one Oblation that can recover Mana in the manner of a Legacy, gaining a single point of Mana each time. Proximi may spend one Mana per turn (taking longer to cast in larger expenditures like a mage) to pay for Blessings with Mana costs and to mitigate Paradox dice.

    Integrity Checks: Due to prolonged exposure and training, Proximi do not suffer Integrity Breaking Points just for using their Blessings and other supernatural traits/powers. They do not usually suffer Integrity Breaking Points just for observing supernatural phenomena either, but might still suffer them if targeted by an especially violent or terrifying supernatural power.

    Occult Training: Proximi do not receive +1 Resistance Attribute Dot since they did not fully Awaken, but they can receive 1 bonus dot in Occult to reflect teachings received from the Orders (see below). They can understand and communicate via High Speech, but cannot apply it as a Yantra like Mages can.

    Nascent Obsession: Proximi have 1 Obsession, but since they lack Gnosis, they cannot acquire additional Obsessions. Instead of Mage Sight, the Proximi have Unseen Sense (Everything), which is not limited to one use per Chapter, and only inflicts Spooked Condition if the detected phenomenon is Supernal or Abyssal.

    Order Alliance: Almost all existing Proximi Dynasties are allied with Order; they are considered to be parts of that Order, no matter what individual Proximus may feel about that. All Proximi have free Honorary Order Status Merit; those who actually become part of that Order have free Status (Order) Merit and free dot of Occult representing Order training.

    Scion's Awakening: At the moment of revelation, a Proximus loses her Blessings and Familial Curse, though the Sanctity of Merits rule still applies. All experience spent on Blessings is immediately used to buy dots in corresponding Arcana up to possible maximum; Aptitudes (see below) become Praxes; any left experience is spent to buy Gnosis. Note that this can result in Scion being a second-degree Disciple from the start, breaking usual Gnosis maximum. At the Storyteller’s discretion, some of the Blessings removed by an Awakening may be converted into unique Rotes encoded in Dynasty's Skills and symbolism.

    Merit: Honorary (Order) Status (••)

    Prerequisites: Not a member of (Order).

    While your character is not a member of the selected Order, he spends enough time around them and has enough respect of local members to be considered a valuable friend and ally.

    Effect: This Merit provides a handful of advantages:
    • Your character receives a +1 bonus to all Social interactions with (Order) members, as if he had a single dot of (Order) Status. He cannot purchase (Order) Merits such as Adamant Hand or Egregore.
    • Once per story, your character can request a resource as (Order) member can through Order Archives rule (see Signs of Sorcery), and counts as having (Order) Status 1 for purposes of that request. If he is willing to owe favors, Storyteller may increase that Status proportionally to weight of favor owed.
    • If he ever formally joins the (Order), he sheds this Merit and adopts two dots of (Order) Status, essentially jumping up the ranks beyond the average recruits.
    Drawback: While character is not an (Order) member, once per story local (Order) member is able to ask him for a small favor, which is expected to be given. Refusal to lend it may lead to losing this Merit.

    Dynastic Blessings

    Every Proximus has the capacity to learn up to 30 dots worth of Blessings, chosen from the three Blessing Arcana determined by the Dynasty. Blessings may be based on any spell of up to three Arcanum dots. Proximi characters buy their Blessings from the list for their Dynasty as Merits.

    Proximi are able to cast this spell in the following way:

    Casting Dice Pool: Proximi use Highest Resistance Attribute + Dynasty Skill or 10 (whichever is lower) as their casting pool, penalized for spell factors. Proximi can use limited amount of Yantras (see below). Their ritual casting interval is five hours.

    Reach and Spell Factors: Proximi are considered to have 1 dot in the relevant Arcana for the purposes of Primary Spell Factor and Free Reaching. They can buy the Greater Blessing Merit to improve their abilities.

    Paradox: Proximi gain one die of Paradox risk per extra Reach (which they can mitigate with Mana as Mages do). A Proximus automatically contains Paradox like a mage, but instead of rolling Wisdom or getting Paradox Condition, any Paradox successes rolled while using a Blessing completely cancel the effect and cause the Proximus’ Curse to worsen. If there is a need to determine exact effects of a Paradox that worsens the Dynasty Curse, consider modelling it as a Pseudo-Paradox Condition analogous to the ones from Mage corebook, or any other ones written by ST. Obvious Blessings with Sleeper witnesses provoke Paradox Risk, trigger Quiescence, and are subject to Dissonance in the same way as Awakened spells.

    Blessing Control: Proximi have one "spell control slot" for purposes of Overreaching. They can relinquish spell control like mages do, safely and unsafely.

    Merit: Greater Blessing (Arcana) (•• to ••••)

    Prerequisite: Proximus

    Your Proximus have greater understanding and occult power than others in his Dynasty, making him almost a match for full-fledged Awakened when he uses his Blessings.

    Effect: With two dots, Proximus is considered to have 2 dots in the relevant Arcana for the purposes of Primary Spell Factor and Free Reaching. With four dots, he is considered to have 3 dots.

    Proximi Yantras
    While Proximi cannot use all Yantras like Mages do, their Blessings still benefit from some of them. They can the following Yantras to increase their dice pool, and can use any amount of them on any Blessing:
    • Path Tools and Correspondences: Parent Path Tools resonate with Proximi Blessings in the same way they do with Awakened spells; they can give +1-3 to casting.
    • Dynastic Tools and Sacraments: Using Dynasty-determined tools with Blessing gives another +1-3.
    • Ritual Spaces and Environments: Proximi can prepare their own ritual spaces and environments, filled with Parent Path and Dynasty symbolism; they can give +1-3 to casting.
    • Dynasty Inheritance: Proximi can buy Inheritance Merit and use that Yantra as usual.
    Proximi can always boost their casting dice pool in the following way:
    • Willpower Spending: Proximi are able to spend Willpower to boost their casting dice pools.
    • Longer Ritual Casting: if Blessing is created ritually, each ritual interval after the first gives +1 to casting, like it does for the Mages.
    Dynasty Boons and Aptitudes

    Unlike Arcana, which are learned after Awakening through Gnosis, Proximi Blessings are, in some sense, external to Proximi themselves. They are innate abilities having their own theme and reason, resonating with certain Supernal Truths and manifesting them in the Fallen World. While some Proximi balk at being given a purpose without them having a say in it, other learn Blessings deeply, giving them a hint of Realms Above and powering their nascent Obsessions, which can flower into full Awakenings.

    Those deeply understood Blessings are called Aptitudes. Proximus can have up to (Highest Dynasty Skill) Aptitudes. He can achieve Exceptional Success with 3 Successes while using Aptitude, as long as the Aptitude usage matches the Dynasty Boon. For example, Pendragon Dynasty has a Boon: "Perform Blessing while defending the weak of or at the behest of the Knight’s Lord"; member of this Dynasty may have Aptitude with Kinetic Shield Blessing and receive Exceptional Success with 3 Successes while protecting Sleepers from hideous Horrors. Those Exceptional Successes have the same effect on Blessing as they would have on regular spell.

    If Proximus Awakens and immediately has necessary Arcana to cast Aptitudes as spells, he should purchase them all as Praxes; Storyteller can make exception for PCs, but usually Aptitudes naturally become Praxes.
