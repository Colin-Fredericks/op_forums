                                        The only thing that I really didn't like is the way that Supernal magic affects Integrity. I have no problem with the insta-kill spells that Masters of certain Arcana can use in 2ED. My problem is how Hunters would (at least theoretically) have little recourse against an enemy whose abilities constantly wear down their souls and can't even be remembered. You can houserule that taking up the Vigil also makes you a Sleepwalker, of course, but Hunters would still have to deal with constant Breaking Points. I normally don't say this because far too many people use the term willy-nilly, but all of this makes mages sound a bit Mary Sue-ish. Sure, there's the Abyssal Shard, and hubris goes hand in hand with great power and the following sensation of invincibility. I don't want to nerf mages, no way. I just think the playing field could be less uneven. Hopelly this will be taken into account during the development of Hunter 2ED.

                                        On a more positive note, it's nice to have a thread like this with people giving their feedback civilly. Mage 2ED may be in the final stages of development, but it's still good to see constructive criticism.
