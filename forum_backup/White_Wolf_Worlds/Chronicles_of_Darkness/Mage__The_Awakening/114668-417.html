            Life 4 + Forces 4 can turn a person into a being of living flame. Using the same requirements to turn a person into a being of living lightning isn't different enough to make me want to give myself headaches over it.

            As for Shadow Flesh, I mentioned that in the errata thread, and it got passed over for getting errataed, so it's probably safe to say that it's intentional. Any reasoning you come up with is going to be a rationalization, but the one I've settled for after talking about with with other folks on the forum is that everyone has the capacity to be dead. Temporarily turning yourself into a corpse with Patterning is no big deal. As such, turning yourself into a different aspect of Death, I.e. darkness instead of a ghost, is also just not a big deal.

                                            I have to agree, most of the times the more complicated spells came out were in combat, especially against threats that are not 'A guy standing across the room from you.' They were pulled out against things like a giant owl monster with a deafening hoot in a blizzard, an idigam resembling a slug of stone plates the size of two football fields, a trio of invisible dogs, a car full of who knows what (armed men) driving after them after they pissed off some local Seers. Two harbingers of the Apocalypse.

                                            If you're looking for dramatic failures, my players took them when they arose but they didn't arise much in spellcasting. We had one memorable failure-to-Dramatic during some Mage scrutinizing some bodies (remnants of the idigam) and a Supernal entity hopped in and made the entire experience awful for her. The players were experienced with Chronicles stuff but only partially with mage, so they weren't dealing with any 'If I lose Wisdom my character is ruined' types. Paradox came up a decent amount through spells.

                                            Anyways the idea seems to be stressed situations. Whether the character is on edge because something is about to happen to them or someone or thing they care about and they just need it to stop, or they don't have much time, or they're in a strange place and are unaware of local enemies or allies. Let them sit in their sanctum and they'll cast the safest spells all day. Once they -need- to use a Reach to get to cast a spell fast, then you have your foot in the door. Raise the stakes.
