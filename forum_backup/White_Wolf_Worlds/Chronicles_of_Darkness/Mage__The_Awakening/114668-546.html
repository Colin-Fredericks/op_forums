    Question for your subjective opinions - in your vision of the Fallen World, how widespread is it for members of one Order to learn the Order-specific merit of another? Say, a Silver Ladder learning Adamant Hand, or a Mystagog learning Techné?

    As written, it's possible to do so by working closely with one Order, and attaining Order Status 1. You can't go any higher, but that's all you need. In terms of theme though, you might run into symbolic issues with everything but Adamant Hand - the other merits are based deeply in Order-dependent philosophy.

                As written, it's possible to do so by working closely with one Order, and attaining Order Status 1. You can't go any higher, but that's all you need. In terms of theme though, you might run into symbolic issues with everything but Adamant Hand - the other merits are based deeply in Order-dependent philosophy.
                I imagine it can be thought if there is enough of a philosophical understanding between the mage and the Order that normally teaches the merit.

                A Guadian could have a track record of solving things diplomatically, trying to vote as fairly as possible. But when there is trouble, they are the first to investigate and the most methodical in rooting out those that threaten the Pentacle. Such a mage might have enough respect with the Silver Ladder to be taught the Lex Magicka.

                A Mystagogue could have become known for facilitating the containment of dangerous lore or artifacts in the Censorea. Maybe he has a reputation as the voice of reason that keeps other Mystagogues from going off the deep end, but recognizes that doing that can be just as much a burden as a necessity. Such a mage might be taught a Masque.

                There needs to be enough good blood between the person being taught and the Order. The student respects and resonates enough with their principles, even when they don't commit as deeply as with their main Order.

                An analogy is that you are a competent enough martial artist, you are mindful of their philosophy and integrate it into your own. You are not a grandmaster or willing to dedicate a lifetime pondering its depths, but you are earnest and dedicated enough to be taught some exclusive techniques.

                    Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post
                    A Mystagogue could have become known for facilitating the containment of dangerous lore or artifacts in the Censorea. Maybe he has a reputation as the voice of reason that keeps other Mystagogues from going off the deep end, but recognizes that doing that can be just as much a burden as a necessity. Such a mage might be taught a Masque.
                    But a Masque isn't like some one-off formula you can be taught, nor is it related to the part of the Guardians that is specifically within the containment or advocacy. It is mystically emergent from the philosophy and lifestyle in which Guardians of the Veil constantly lie. Like, their original Order book talks about how a lot of the time Guardians will even lie about unimportant things, just to maintain the practice. It's that in combination with the degree to which they sacrifice of themselves, become detached from their own wants in favour of a greater good.

                    Of all Orders to suggest getting in on that... the Mysterium have severe proscriptions against lying about magical subjects (and kind of discourage doing it in any event), and are all about the personal life journey of finding and developing what resonates with you specifically. There's too much ego in there for the dissolution of self that allows one to be replaced by a Masque, and the kinds of practices that is likely to entail anyway are the sort other mystagogues will often find abhorrent.
