                                                            Yeah, Artifact's mana battery function got nerfed - it was self-regeneratiing in 1Ed. You can't use it for free mana regen any more, and it's a good thing, I think - that function honestly was more of an system artifact that intended feature (because people kept buying cheap Artifact as a mana source and mana pool extension, instead as anything else). 2nd ED Artifacts still can extend your mana pool as a side bonus to main dish of being, duh, an artifact. I think it's healthier for the game.

                                                            *Extended mana pool is still a great benefit. Because it's so easy to regenerate mana while you are not chased by abyssal horrors or whatever, ability to have some extra mana on your person can be much more useful than another source of mana regeneration.
