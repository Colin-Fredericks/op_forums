                                                    For personal use I will probably go with "you need to know how to make this thing or it needs to be simple enough that you can 'picture' it in its entirety, including its insides" , so the complextiy of the platonizable items will scale with the skill dots of the character...but that's for my personal use, because we like having a direct connection between mundane abilities and magical capabilities (mostly first enhancing the second). So if you can't form an imago that "grokks" how the thing works, you will get a pretty but empty inside model case of a, eh, let's say nuke.

                                                    But I'm also interested in how the "vanilla" platonic ideal should work, so without making a personal table ruling for it. One of things that actually was asked about is Platonic Form of a...book. "I want to form a platonic ideal of a specialized work about bugs!", for example. Would it work? My guts say "yes, but the book will evaporate after X uses due to mana limits and how good it is is based on equipment bonus". How do you people think it should work by vanilla reading, and how would you handle it on your table?
                                                    I'm actually more interested/scared about things like that than actually conjuring tanks out of thin air, to be honest .

                                                    This question is sponsored by Grimoire Spoiler and which resulted in interest in playing a Prime based Obrimos with a thing for books.
