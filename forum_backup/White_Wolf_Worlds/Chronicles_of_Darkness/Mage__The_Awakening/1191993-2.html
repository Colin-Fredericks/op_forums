    In the end, I just do not found Humanity is Magical very much different from the what Diamond was doing by millenia. Diamond already found promising mages from very different backgrounds and offered them membership in their exclusive club.
    It's fundamentally different.

    The Diamond sees magic as filtering down from the Supernal. If sleeper sciences and traditions have elements of real supernal knowledge, that's because the sleepers picked it up from the Supernal.

    The FC believe that humans produce symbols and those then alter the Supernal. It's a bottom up approach. The Sleepers don't just passively receive Supernal symbolism, they produce it as well.

    Now, in theory that's maybe not that radical, however the Diamond is fundamentally based on the belief that the Supernal is supreme over humanity. They divide into four castes because that's what they believe the Supernal says they should be. Masters justify their hold on power within the Diamond because they're the ones closest to the Supernal. Even the ideologies of each Diamond Order are fundamentally based on that belief.

    The FC challenge that. If even sleepers can make their own truths, then what right does a Master have dictate the truth? It radically challenges everything the Diamond stand for.

                                            Well, again it’s because the view of the FC and the Diamond and Orders are somewhat diametrically opposed. The FC believe that magic filters from humanity up and then back down again to the Fallen. The Seers believe magic comes down from and is ruled by the Exarchs. The Diamond believe mostly the same thing, but they reject the premise that the Seers are in charge and instead believe magic comes down from the Supernal itself and that the Exarchs are interlopers.

                                            This difference in belief that the FC has means they must philosophically oppose the hierarchy of the Seers with the Exarchs at top because clearly (to them) the Exarchs are not the authors of magic. They must also reject the hierarchy of the Diamond because clearly (to them) the Supernal isn’t the only source of magic and is in fact influenced by humanity. When you get to the point where you’re rejecting the two prevailing hierarchical magical theories of magic you start looking around for something that fits your worldview better. Democracy in its purest form is not an hierarchical structure so it’s fits what the FC see as the actual structure of reality.

                                            It’s a pretty clear cut line, Humanity is Magical so magic cannot be structured in an hierarchy because an hierarchy undercuts the idea that all of humanity contributes to magic. Put another way, if there are some forces, people, etc which generate magic that preempt everything else by forming an hierarchy of order then humanity cannot be magical, or at least not recognized as being as magical as it should be.

                                            There’s also a level of convenience to it. One Nameless group can look at another and they both say to each other, “Look, we might believe different things, but I won’t trample your beliefs like the Diamond or the Seers, and if you don’t trample mine, and we act like we are equals we might just stand a chance of not getting squashed or absorbed by either of them.” Which, canonically is exactly what happened. Democracy is keeping the different factions of the FC from stepping on each other’s toes and creating a framework they can work inside of to stand together against the other two factions that at the time of the Nameless War wanted to destroy or absorb them for their “heretical” belief that Humanity is Magical.
