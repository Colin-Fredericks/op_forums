      Attainments are constructed as spells with predefined Reach effects from a budget of the Attainment's rank plus one (as though the rank of the Attainment were the Arcanum rating of the spell being replicated), a bonus to the primary factor as normal, and the ability to assign additional spell factors (contextually not more of the primary factor, judging from the example) that would apply a penalty equal to the rating of the lowest Arcanum the Attainment uses (so multi-Arcanum Attainments are disincentivized).

      If an Attainment's Reach budget doesn't have anything allocated toward Casting Time, it takes a scene to cast; it doesn't require a roll to use and counts as having rolled successes equal to the mage's Arcanum rating for the purposes of effects that measure how many successes were rolled (this doesn't change the way spells that are Withstood work; those still compare Potency to Withstand as normal).

      Basically, Legacy Attainments are inflexible but reliable compared to spells, such that it's built as a spell when you get the Attainment and has its stats updated as your Arcanum ratings go up, you use it by declaring that you're using it, and if it's an effect that's Withstood you reduce its Potency by the target's Withstand rating; being free (or nearly so, in the case of Mana costs) of most of the drawbacks of a comparable spell is the price for this reliability, with greater power or variant utilization requiring the use of the spellcasting system to pump the spell factors or assign different Reach effects. If you understand how spell factors work, the above caveats just plug into that.

          Sure thing, so the key details to keep in mind:

          1) An Attainment has Reach available as if you were casting a spell with the equivalent Arcanum dots. So if your Time 1 Attainment is Postcognition, you have one Reach, since its the equivalent of casting it with Time 1. If its the third Attainment, Postcognition gets 3 Reach and 5 Reach if its the fifth Attainment. Meanwhile, Temporal Stutter could only be in the fourth or fifth Attainments, since it requires Time 4, having one Reach available if its in the fourth slot or two if its in the fifth. How the Reach is used is fixed when the Attainment is designed, so if you decided the Postcognition in the second Attainment slot would use instant use and advanced duration, it will always be so. Attainments cannot over Reach either, the inflexible Reach is the tradeoff for their reliability.

          2) If an Attainment costs any amount of mana to cast, the total cost is truncated to one mana. So lets say you have Acceleration as your Time 4 Attainment. So you activate Time in a Bottle, then apply your Reach for advanced duration and rote property dodge. Normally you would pay two mana total (Time in a Bottle plus rote dodge), but since its an Attainment, you only pay one point of mana.

          3) Any Attainments cast in ritual time always takes a scene to be used.

          4) Attainments require no roll to use, you just declare you are using them, which means they are one of the most reliable options in your toolkit.

          5) Attainments never provoke Quiescence or Breaking Points, so if you use a Teleport Attainment in front of a Sleeper, they will remember.

          6) Attainments cost no spell slots and you always retain spell control. So if one Attainment makes zombies, for example, you can have as many zombies as you can make.

          7) Attainment spell factors can be adjusted at casting time by using a number of pseudo-dice equivalent to the Arcanum's rating. So if you have Time 2, you can increase spell factors as if you had two dice to spend. With Time 4, you would have 4 pseudo-dice available and so on.

          Hope that proves helpful.

                  Bene Ashmedai
                  Parentage: Mastigos or Silver Ladder
                  Ruling Arcanum: Spirit
                  Yantras: Summoning circles (+1, +2 if made of exotic or expensive components); target is indulging a Vice (+1, +2 if it’s the mage’s Vice); the spell is cast in the service of others (+1); the spell is in keeping with your Vice (+1).
                  Oblations: Acting as a guru or other spiritual authority; conversing with goetic demons or other goeticists; demonological research; engaging in a seduction (either as the initiator or as the pursued); giving in to Vice without concern for the consequences (or inspiring another to do so); indulging in the fear and/or adulation of others; meditation on past moments of weakness; psychoanalyzing oneself or others.

                  Initiate (Diabolist’s Circlet)
                  Exorcist’s Eye - Instant Casting
                  Perfect Recall - Instant Casting

                  Apprentice (Goetic Bulwark)
                  Shadow Walk - Advanced Duration
                  Alter Mental Pattern - Advanced Duration

                  Disciple (Daimon’s Measure)
                  Ephemeral Shield - Advanced Duration, "Spirit’s physical attacks"
                  Mental Shield - Advanced Duration, "Goetia’s physical attacks"

                  Adept (Goetic Struggle)
                  Gossamer Touch - Advanced Duration, "Carried objects", "Unarmed attacks"
                  One Mind, Two Thoughts - Advanced Duration, "Instant Tasks", "Physical Tasks in the Astral"​

                  Master (Pandemonium’s Mirror)
                  Birth Spirit - Advanced Duration
                  Psychic Genesis - Advanced Duration​


                  It is mainly focused on the defensive side, as befitting to those that usually indulge in Vice, they need to be protected from it. I also tried to get close to the original attainments described in 1ed, with a side of knowing other people weaknesses and Astral improvements.

                      I would like to propose an unorthodox approach to their Attainments.

                      Because they are more focused on understanding their vices and indulging them in a controlled manner, I thought they resonated more with the Thyrsus, so I inverted the Arcana.

                      Since they have a demonic influence in their origin, I also aimed to make their toolkit feel more unnerving, from their talent for quickly reading a situation, to their ability to influence the world around them without moving a muscle.

                      Lastly, I also tried to reinforce the design phrase from the original writeup: "I am a prince amongst demons and a servant to men". This is what I have so far, please let me know what you think:

                      Mind 1
                      Passions drive everything, by opening their senses to the psychic imprints around them, the diabolist can read the emotional resonance around an object or perceive important events tied to a location. They are able to ask [Mind] yes or no questions about them to the Storyteller, or any of the following questions:
                      • What’s the strongest emotion here?
                      • Who remembers this moment the most?
                      • Am I missing something in this scene?
                      • Where was this object during the event?
                      • What breaking point caused the event?


                      This is a custom, Potency primary, Unveiling of Mind based on the Psychometry merit (CofD page 58), with Reach assigned to instant use. Since it's an Unveiling, using sensory range is unneeded.

                      Spirit 1
                      Exorcist's Eye with Reach assigned to advanced duration and conjunctional Mind allowing them to extend the benefits to goetia as well.

                      The diabolist is keenly aware of the actions of those who would manipulate the emotional energy around them.

                      Mind 2
                      Mental Scan with Reach assigned to instant use and reading surface thoughts. Since it's an Unveiling, using sensory range is unneeded.

                      The diabolist always sees through the facades people put up, peering into the true motivation behind their actions.

                      Spirit 2
                      Coaxing the Spirits with Reach assigned to instant use and sensory range.

                      The diabolist needs only a thought to motivate the motes surrounding them into action.

                      Mind 3
                      Know Nature with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range and determining Aspirations and Obsessions.

                      To one who delves deeply into understanding their own vices, reading those of others can be done at a glance.

                      Spirit 3
                      A combined spell of Mental Shield and Ephemeral Shield, with Reach assigned to advanced duration and protecting from physical attacks. Two pseudo dice are used for the combined spell.

                      Mastering oneself requires the clarity to indulge one's vices free of outside influences.

                      Mind 4
                      Emotional Urging with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range and advanced duration.

                      Through their controlled self indulgence, the diabolist can fan or quell the flames of emotions in others.

                      Spirit 4
                      A Potency primary Patterning of Mind and Spirit that creates a [lowest of Mind and Spirit] Retainer in Spirit Twilight by transforming some motes into a mind and using it to animate other ones, with its field being of the diabolist's choosing. Reach is assigned to advanced duration.

                      This is a Mind and Spirit variation of the Golem spell, used to create a better version of Unseen Servant from D&D. Similar to their own Vices, the diabolists shape the emotional energy around them into a powerful ally.

                      Mind 5
                      Psychic Reprogramming with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration.

                      The greatest sign of mastery over oneself is to be able to define one's passions.

                      Spirit 5
                      Familiar with Reach assigned to indefinite duration, with a point of mana paying for indefinite duration and conjunctional Mind 4 allowing goetia to be bound as well.

                      A prince of demons requires a court.
