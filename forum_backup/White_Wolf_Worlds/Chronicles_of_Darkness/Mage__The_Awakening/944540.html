    A few questions

    08-05-2016, 05:58 PM
    Hello!

    I would like to clear something for myself, if possible. Also, if these questions were answered somewhere else, please direct me to those answers. So, without further ado:
    1. Why do the Disciple level direct damaging spells deal only bashing damage? I mean, all the weapons in the game are dealing Lethal damage, so where is the logic that, for example, wind blades (Matter - Windstrike) deal bashing damage?
    2. When casting an area effect spell with a duration, does the duration affect the area or the people or things in it? For example, if I cast AoE Enhance Skill with 24 hours duration, does the duration affect only an area and everyone who remains in it, or does it affect the people gathered in that area?
    3. If the spell is affecting an area is it possible to specify whom would it affect? Something like a Key in Space Arcana? For example, previous AoE Enhance Skill affects an area and anyone who enters it and says a short phrase would gain the benefit as long as they are in the area.
    4. For Keys, would it be possible to specify something more unusual? For example, spell affects anyone who is enraged. What practice would it require?

    Of course, I have a my own ideas about solving these things, but I would like to hear other opinions.

      Originally posted by bjelokrili View Post
      Hello!

      I would like to clear something for myself, if possible. Also, if these questions were answered somewhere else, please direct me to those answers. So, without further ado:
      1. Why do the Disciple level direct damaging spells deal only bashing damage? I mean, all the weapons in the game are dealing Lethal damage, so where is the logic that, for example, wind blades (Matter - Windstrike) deal bashing damage?
      Seems most like a conscious decision of the authors' parts. Apparently directly damaging someone is just difficult for magic to do. Mages tend to disable opponents in different ways, so that the game gets more creative than just damaging spells over and over if combat does break out. There are ways to instantly disable someone in the first three dots of any arcanum, so I would not worry about being weak because you can "only" deal bashing damage.

      2. When casting an area effect spell with a duration, does the duration affect the area or the people or things in it? For example, if I cast AoE Enhance Skill with 24 hours duration, does the duration affect only an area and everyone who remains in it, or does it affect the people gathered in that area?
      It's pretty vaguely written, I will grant you that. It is not made very clear what the Subject is when casting an Area spell, but obviously the Area cannot be the Subject itself, as that would make most area spells pointless. I think the easiest way to see it is this: an Area spell makes everyone/everything (as appropriate) within it a Subject of the spell. So it would buff the people in that area, and they are then free to leave the area and maintain the buff, but new people entering the area later are not buffed. I could be wrong, though, this stuff is confusing.

      3. If the spell is affecting an area is it possible to specify whom would it affect? Something like a Key in Space Arcana? For example, previous AoE Enhance Skill affects an area and anyone who enters it and says a short phrase would gain the benefit as long as they are in the area.
      Look up Warding gesture, p. 136.

      4. For Keys, would it be possible to specify something more unusual? For example, spell affects anyone who is enraged. What practice would it require?
      Why not? I don't think it needs a second arcanum at all, Space keys seem fully capable of this on their own. But if not, it would probably be Add Mind, to peer into emotions. Don't think it is needed though.

                          Dave said 2 or 3 pages ago in a Ask a Simple Question that Area Spells simply affect all viable subjects inside the area. So instead of choosing to raise corpse you touch (no scale factors), or 5 chosen corpses (advanced scale: subjects), you raise all corpses in a small room (advanced scale: area) that then can leave the area normally. So basically, it's correct.

                          EDIT
                          If you want to make an Area a Subject, you simply need a way to perceive "Area". This seems like a job for either Space Sight, or a Space Knowing Spell. Mage 2 subject system is pretty good - you can target pretty much anything, from people to thoughts, but you need a way to properly perceive them first.

                                          Sure, if you will prepare in advance to defend against that specific damaging spell, it will work. Problem is that once Mages hit 3 dots in in an Arcanum, they can creative thaumaturge up infinite variations of their damaging spell using all of the different things falling under it's purview - Death might tear you up with shadow tentacles, assault you with cold of grave, or make your meat rot.
                                          This is why I'm personally happy with Shadow Name merit being so powerful - Mages will cling to their Shadow Named patterns, thus if you know you are going to end up fighting Burner McBurn, you can expect him to try burning you at least once, so it's a good idea to get Resist Fire.

                                          As for Keys...remember that Keys are special feature of Space Arcanum. Think carefully before allowing to replicate them using different Arcana.

                                          As for "shaping stuff as blades and things" - you are welcome to create a spell that does that, but it will probably require an Attack roll (something like Gnosis+Athletics/Firearms) and won't give you benefits of normal damaging spell - it won't be sure-fire. It's a good trade off, I think. They also often require more actions than one and won't act immediately; "transforming water into blades" would be one spell, and then throwing it at your opponents would be another, taking two actions at least.

                                                  You need to have your action for the turn left unspent, observe the act of spellcasting under appropiate Mage Sight, and then use up your action in response to being cast at to contest the casting.
                                                  The big problem about this is that you give up your action that could do something productive to attempt a chance at cancelling other's party move. Situation has following resolutions:
                                                  a) You win, dude doesn't complete his spell.
                                                  b) You lose, dude completes his spell.
                                                  Either way, you give up your action. If there is no one else to stop the guy, he can just try again next turn. Counterspell is a very situational power.

                                                      Well, a damaging spell isn't so sure fire if it has to be thrown, which very well may cause paradox as well. But in general, yeah, mages don't need to be more powerful when it comes to killing things. Combat is a thing because fantasy, but Mage is about solving Mysteries. There's a reason mages don't go around picking fights with whatever is in their way at the moment: wisdom is important to hold on to for survival. You go around killing things on a regular basis and you'll start losing a lot of wisdom. And if you inure yourself to wisdom loss for your attack spell, then you'll be knee deep in paradox. And possibly having your spell countered.

                                                      As to choosing who you affect, I think there's a fate ruling spell that does that...

                                                      With keys, I typically restrict that to the Space arcanum, but the keys themselves can be whatever you want them to be. A particular state of mind, some rare concoction, a particular time of day, or even a password. The sky is the limit.
