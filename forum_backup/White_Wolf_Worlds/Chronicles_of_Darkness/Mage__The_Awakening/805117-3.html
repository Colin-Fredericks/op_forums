        Here is a rough, fluffless draft of a 2e version of the Bene Ashmedai. Let me know if anyone has any ideas for alternative effects for each Attainment.

        Bene Ashmedai

        Special Rules: Spirits affected by Bene Ashmedai Attainments must be of an emotional choir, having clear origins in human activity. Goetia may only be affected if they're from someone's Oneiros.

        Attainment 1
        Spirit 1: Exorcist's Eye [Applies to Goetia automatically if mage fulfils requirements]
        Mind 1: Know Nature

        Attainment 2
        Spirit 2: Command Spirit [Applies to Goetia automatically if mage fulfills requirements]
        Mind 2: Psychic Domination [Limited to actions that fulfill the mage's Vice or Aspirations/Obsessions]

        Attainment 3
        Spirit 3: Spirit Summons
        Mind 3: Goetic Summons

        Attainment 4
        Spirit 4: Bind Spirit [Applies to Goetia automatically if mage fulfills requirements]
        Mind 4: Psychic Reprogramming [Limited to changes the ST feels would would make the subject fulfill the mage's Vice or Aspirations/Obsessions]

        Attainment 5
        Spirit 5: Birth Spirit [Can replicate Psychic Genesis too if the mage fulfills requirements]
        Mind 5: Amorality [Can only remove someone's Virtue]

                                It is a bummer. I've been struggling with a Legacy of my own and I'm still not satisfied with it.

                                I agree that mind-control doesn't seem to fit the Legacy. They are manipulators but nor slavers, and in the 1st Edition they felt responsibility towards Mortals. Still, if you made "Psychic Domination" the fifth Attainment, that would have been some scary stuff. Purple Man scary.

                                When it comes to designing Attainments, it helps if you have a good grasp on what the Legacy does. Try to think on what exactly you think their self-avowed purpose is, and which Spells fit most. As far as Mind is concerned, I think they're:
                                "Know Nature" - a 1 dot Spell as the 1st Attainment, with +1 free reach. Versatile and useful.
                                "First Impressions" - a 2 dot Spell as the 2nd Attainment. I'm not clear on how to crunch the numbers, but I think a personal bonus to Social Maneuvering is the right fit.
                                "Mental Shield" - a 2 dot Spell as the 3rd Attainment, with +2 free Reach. It allows you to protect both yourself and others, from all sorts of influences.
                                "Goetic Summons" - a 3 dot Spell I'm not sure how to calibrate. 5th Attainment? Having a helpful Vice Demons would be a high level Attainment, even if we limit the Spell to Mage's personal ones. The Mage would probably still need to negotiate, as opposed to command, which fits the theme of the Legacy. Spirit 2 keeps them Materialized which is perfect.
                                "Amorality" - a 5 dot Spell I'm not sure what to do with. The Virtue severing effect is frankly underwhelming, when used on oneself. Using it on others seems like a breach of conduct. If we use it for the 4th Attainment, it needs to be bundled with an additional effect.

                                                            That's what I've been banging my head against for the last few hours.

                                                            This is what the section on Automatic Activation says:

                                                            Unless specified otherwise, activating an Attainment is an instant action.
                                                            This is what the section on Reach says:

                                                            Most Attainments use one Reach for instant “casting”; those that don’t (being based on ritual spells) take one scene of preparation to use.
                                                            The most logical interpretation of the text is that, when you design a Legacy Attainment, you lock the Reach in place and can't change it again. Most Legacy Attainments just use their one Reach for instant casting.

                                                            In fact, most of the Eleventh Question Attainments specifically point out what their Reach is assigned to, and instant activation isn't free. That wasn't in the original preview, and since I skimmed most of the text that had already been posted on the blog, I didn't notice until now.

                                                            That's going to have a huge affect on how Legacies are designed. You're more likely to see low-level spells adapted to high-level Attainments so they can get the extra Reach, and you're probably going to have problems finding appropriate spells for low-level attainments.
