                  Originally posted by Omegaphallic View Post
                  Okay now that we know what Changeling looks like, how do you think Mages will interact with all things Fae?

                  Can mages have Hedge Ghost Familiars?
                  In theory, mages can do anything. Its all a matter of getting the research and experiments in. And ST approval.

                  Can a Mage with time and fate retroactively cause someone to become Fae Touched by creating a meaningful oath between the would be Avowed and a Changeling?
                  Yes, they can use Time to Rewrite history and lead to someone making different choices to become fae touched. Cue disclaimer on all temporal shinanigans expiring, etc.

                  Can Mages open gateways to the Hedge?
                  Yes. Open, close, into the hedge, out of the hedge, create their own gateways at Fate 5 going either way.

                  Can a dreaming Mage interact with Eidolons?
                  Good chance that eidolons are just your goetic vices and your super ego. Mages are better at interacting with them through the astral than changelings are. And mind mages can enter dreams as well as a changeling can.

                  It turns out any Emphereal being manifests physically in the Hedge, so I guess visiting Goetic Familiars can manifest there as if they we're in the Astral.
                  Maybe. They could just go intangible like hedge ghosts. Goetics are funny that way, because, storywise, they're not supposed to ever be able to Materialize without some physical body to possess, or be transformed by a Patterning spell.

                  How do you think the Drain Numina works when used on Essence Emphereals by Glamour based Emphereals and vis versa?
                  There are no glamour-based ephemerals. Hedge ghosts aren't in twilight (even when Ironside), aren't made of ephemeral. Drain Numina targets material beings in the first place, though, no way to target a hedge ghost. Targeting a fae creature will drain willpower.

                  Can Mages collect Glamour the way they do essence.
                  See first answer.

                  And how far would a Mage go to possess the Crown of Thorns if he knew about it?
                  As something to study for their Obsession? Almost any length. As a tool to use? Very little.

                  What would an Archmages Eidolons look like?
                  Same as any other former human's.

                  Could there be Abyssal Hedge Ghosts?
                  Anything is possible. That said, they won't be showing up the same way an acamoth or golmoth would.

                  Can Mages get the Hedge Denizen & Goblin Queen Condition?
                  In theory, anything is possible. Not very likely, but possible.

                                                          Originally posted by Knit-I-Fine View Post
                                                          These are all based on ST fiat in my opinion, so I had some fun with them. Here's a very pro-changeling take.
                                                          ...that violates canon.

                                                          Seriously, there's a monster entry for mages who confuse Supernal Arcadia and Changeling Arcadia in Dancers in the Dusk, and get turned into hobgoblins by the Keepers for their trouble. It outright says that mages keep their powers until they get soul-mutilated by Gentry.

                                                          Besides, these answers are boring, not a single plot hook among them. So, my pro-changeling answers:

                                                          Okay now that we know what Changeling looks like, how do you think Mages will interact with all things Fae?

                                                          As with anything else: prodding it with a stick and seeing what Arcane Experience falls out.

                                                          Can mages have Hedge Ghost Familiars?

                                                          They'd have to meet first, but with the proper Fate and/or Death spell, nothing insurmountable.

                                                          Can a Mage with time and fate retroactively cause someone to become Fae Touched by creating a meaningful oath between the would be Avowed and a Changeling?

                                                          Yes, but it'd result in some major temporal paradox. And Paradox. That's a guy's life you're remaking there and messing with the Hedge in the process!

                                                          Can Mages open gateways to the Hedge?

                                                          No, but they can figure out the key with it.

                                                          Can a dreaming Mage interact with Eidolons?

                                                          Those are parts of a dreaming mind, so yeah.

                                                          It turns out any Emphereal being manifests physically in the Hedge, so I guess visiting Goetic Familiars can manifest there as if they we're in the Astral.

                                                          Yes, but it's common for them to become Hedge Ghosts. They are already free-floating bits of soul and emotion after all, becoming bits adapted to the Hedge isn't hard.

                                                          How do you think the Drain Numina works when used on Essence Emphereals by Glamour based Emphereals and vis versa?

                                                          Different fuels, different Numen needed. A spirit able to do that is a half-goblin magath, and a ghost able to has gone native.

                                                          Can Mages collect Glamour the way they do essence.

                                                          Yep, though they need Fate to keep it stable and can't really use it without conversion to Mana or as bribes.

                                                          And how far would a Mage go to possess the Crown of Thorns if he knew about it?

                                                          Depends on the mage.

                                                          What would an Archmages Eidolons look like?

                                                          Not that different from normal eidolons. They dream like everyone else, it's just a more powerful mind dreaming.

                                                          Could there be Abyssal Hedge Ghosts?

                                                          The Abyss is everything and nothing. How it got into the Hedge is anyone's guess, but it's hardly impossible; if there can be Abyssal Goetia, there can be Abyssal Hedge Ghosts (maybe it wasn't a good idea for retroactive Fae-Touched..).

                                                          Can Mages get the Hedge Denizen & Goblin Queen Condition?

                                                          On purpose, with the proper Legacy to get around that "goblins won't sell to me" issue. It's not worth it. Interesting method of lichdom, though.
