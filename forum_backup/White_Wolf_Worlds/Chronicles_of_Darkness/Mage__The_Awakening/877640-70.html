                                It feels a little weird that a temporary (or at least removable) spell would give you the ability to permanently spend experience points on something like Rites. It would make more sense as an Attainment, and/or False Rite could be its own spell.

                                I'm also not wild about keying the regeneration to the subject's Supernatural Tolerance. It don't see the in-universe justification; Gnosis, Blood Potency, Primum etc. represent very different things from Primal Urge, and neither Spirit nor Life has any real way of manipulating the things they represent. Maybe with conjunctional Prime (or as appropriate for the subject, e.g. Death for a vampire) I could see it, but I'd just lock it at regenerating as PU 1. If you don't want to do that (and even if you do) consider giving the appropriate penalties for the simulated Primal Urge, like the dietary restrictions and need to hunt. Bonus points if the spell explicitly does not give the subject a werewolf's cast-iron stomach, and/or the spell applies further side effects. One way or another I feel that trying to steal the Uratha's thunder should singe you a bit. The most obvious way would be to staple some sort of pseudo-Death Rage "berserk mode" to the spell's effects.

                                Oh, and is it meant to give the subject honorary Rank as a werewolf with her fake Renown? Whether the answer is yes or no it should probably be spelled out.
