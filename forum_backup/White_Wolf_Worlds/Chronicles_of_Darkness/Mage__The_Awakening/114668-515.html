        The Imbued Item entry on 2e Mage's core book (page 102, I think) is pretty explicit that you're spending at least one point of mana to activate the spell
        I think this is where some of the confusion lies. Because the attainment also clearly says

        "The caster decides whether the spell imbued into the item is persistent (always active) or contingent (triggered by a word, gesture, or condition)"

        If it is contingent, then 1 mana per activation and a Gnosis+Arcana roll.

        But if it is persistent, then it seems like it only has to be activated once and is always on (which suggests indefinite duration to me, and is something the merit suggests imbued items can't do).

        The downside is that there is only ever one copy of the spell effect, whereas if you triggered it, you could cast the effect multiple times on different subjects (like giving honing the form to each member of your group one at a time for one mana each) with seperate durations.
