In his eyes, only a fool laments that which has already come to pass, unless he somehow possesses the power to change it. Instead, Potestas works on establishing a firm position for the next generation of the Academy, from which they might chip away at the influence of the area’s Silver Ladder in general and the Secret Concord in particular. He is connected to many willworkers in the city by the Egregore he is center, but has nowhere near the information network he has led others to believe. In truth, he finds great amusement in watching others scramble to figure out who his spies and informants are.
