        Sense Presence Spells

        Mages can't keep their Mage Sight on all the time, but there are times when you need a magical alarm; the spell which detects potential threats and targets. The applications are wide: protection of sanctums, spying, use in combat and others. Unlike Mage Sight, those aren't as taxing and don't require from Mage to filter raw Supernal meaning, instead processing it and giving information.
        All those spells are usually cast with Advanced Scale for greater area of Effect and Advanced Duration for longer durations; so while those spells are all technically require just Arcanum 1, in practice they require at least Arcanum 3 to cast without Paradox risk. They also usually being cast ritually, except in combat situations, there duration isn't as useful.
        All those spells are Knowing practices; as such, they automatically fail to notice persons who use Veiling practice of Arcanum in question to hide themselves; all those spells can for +1 Reach include Unveiling practice to automatically attempt to pierce deception.

        Sense Undead (Death •)
        Practice: Knowing
        Primary Factor: Duration
        Ghosts aren't bound by physical obstacles, and naturally can't be seen or felt by human senses. This spell tell the mage, if something undead enters the area of spell and gives the location of it.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows Rank and Influence of ghost who is entering the area.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows amount of dots in attributes of ghost who is entering the area.

        Sense Vibrations (Force •)
        Practice: Knowing
        Primary Factor: Duration
        Mages can become invisible, but thought going completely silent don't happen to many of them, because sight is the most important human sense. This spell exploits this error, listening closely to vibrations in the area and telling mage of their location.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows Size and approximate mass of being who is entering the area.
        +1 Reach: The mage can closely listen to being who is entering the area, if he chooses to.

        Sense Life (Life •)
        Practice: Knowing
        Primary Factor: Duration
        The hunter knows the jungle by sight, hearing, and smell. This spells goes further and detects the very feeling of being alive: heartbeat, breathing and desire to be alive. This spell tell the mage, if something alive enters the area of spell and gives the location of it.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows general well being of person who enters the area - it's Health and Conditions like Tired, Poisoned, etc.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows amount of dots in Physical attributes of person who is entering the area.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows specific amount of dots in one Physical attribute of person who is entering the area.

        Sense Pressure (Matter •)
        Practice: Knowing
        Primary Factor: Duration
        This spell is different from other is regard that it isn't cast on area, but on structure in question; the mage can feel pressure on structure material and detect location of creatures who move inside of it.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows Size and approximate mass of being who is entering the area.

        Sense Thoughts (Mind •)
        Practice: Knowing
        Primary Factor: Duration
        The spell listens to thoughts and emotions of being inside of area, telling mage of their location. This spell can be very taxing on psyche of mage who cast it if location is happens to be in area with high human traffic.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows amount of dots in Mental or Social attributes of person who is entering the area.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows specific amount of dots in one Mental or Social attribute of person who is entering the area.

        Sense Magic (Prime •)
        Practice: Knowing
        Primary Factor: Duration
        Most mages prefer for their sanctums to be devoid of alien magic. This spells tells mage if somebody tries to use magic inside of area and telling mage of their location.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows the exact Arcana and it's level of the person who tries to use magic inside the area.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows the exact spell which is used inside the area.

        Sense Bending (Space •)
        Practice: Knowing
        Primary Factor: Duration
        Space magic is a great way to enter locations by making your own enters and exits; naturally there is a desire to detect such intruders. This spells tells if some person tried to bend space inside the area (with purpose of move; spells for sympathy and alike don't get noticed) and tells location of him to mage.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows level of Space of the person who tried to bend space inside the area.

        Sense Spirits (Spirit •)
        Practice: Knowing
        Primary Factor: Duration
        Ghosts aren't bound by physical obstacles, and naturally can't be seen or felt by human senses. This spell tell the mage, if some spirit enters the area of spell and gives the location of it.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows Rank and Influence of spirit who is entering the area.
        +1 Reach: The mage knows amount of dots in attributes of spirit who is entering the area.

                        I could imagine Prime 3 weaving would let you "borrow" a location's symbolism and attach it to an object to allow some Potency-limited # of invocations of that location as a yantra while not actually present there. Some random ideas (haven't been keeping up with the thread, apologies if I retread any ground here):
                        ----------------------------
                        Memento/Souvenir/Etc. (Prime •••)
                        Practice: Weaving
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Occult, Larceny

                        This spell invests an object with the significance of an Environment. The object can be used as a Tool Yantra in situations where the location's symbolism would be relevant, providing the usual +1 bonus. It may be evoked in this fashion up to once per level of Potency.

                        +1 Reach and 1 mana: The spell may invest an object with a Verge or Demesne's symbolism. The Tool provides a +2 die bonus and, while it cannot totally negate Paradox, does act as a Dedicated Tool.
                        ----------------------------
                        Palimpsest (Prime •••)
                        Practice: Weaving
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Crafts, Expression

                        This effect allows additional spells to be anchored to a single set of High Speech runes. Casting an additional spell into the runes requires the expenditure of one mana during formation of its Imago. The secondary spell may make use the yantra bonus of the runes (without having to take a ritual interval to inscribe them) and is also disrupted if the runes are erased or defaced. The Palimpsest can hold a number of spells equal to its Potency (in addition to the first spell.) If the Palimpsest is dispelled or its duration lapses, the secondary spell is lost.
                        ----------------------------
                        OotS Reference etc. (Prime ••)
                        Practice: Shielding
                        Primary Factor: Potency
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Crafts, Intimidation

                        This spell alters the function of a set of High Speech runes. When the runes are invoked as a yantra during the creation of an Imago, in addition to providing the usual dice benefit, they will "contain" the spell anchored to them. If the Potency of this spell meets or exceeds that of the anchored spell, the anchored effect does not take place, remaining ineffectual until OotS Reference is dispelled or lapses. If cast upon an active set of runes that are already anchoring a spell, OotS Reference can temporarily suppress an anchored effect (Potency permitting.)

                        +1 Reach: If the runes are marred or defaced, OotS Reference will end and the anchored effect will be released instead of being disrupted, taking effect on whatever the runes were inscribed upon for the rest of its duration.
                        +2 Reach: As above, but the released effect will instead turn upon the person or object that damaged the runes, if they are a valid target.
                        I like these [insert exclamation mark that I cannot do because my keyboard had a stroke]. While my gut instinct for Souvenir was to add Space to it, I really like the idea of using just Prime to do it by treating the subject like a smaller part of greater Environment Symbolism and kinda like using Prime to rebuild lacking parts around it like a hologram and ducktape it for these few scenes.
                        Palimpsest is one of the rare spells I would bother to Safely Relinquish.
