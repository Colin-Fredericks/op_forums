    Create a NPC Cabal Competition

    03-26-2020, 09:37 AM
    Greetings all!

    I'm currently fleshing out a setting for a PbP game I'm starting in the next few weeks. I want to collect ideas for some NPC Cabals I can drop into the setting to add a little local colour and perhaps generate plot hooks further down the line. I figure a collection of these created by the fine minds on our forum might come in handy for other Storytellers in future too. Not too sure what I can offer as a prize, but I can pledge to post updates if I use someones ideas and they come up in the game itself.

    If people are interested they could post their idea's in a format like this.

    Name:
    Theme/Obsession:
    Order(s):
    Example: (An example member of that Cabal.)

    For background on the game itself, its set in the city of York and the surrounding area, so anything tapping into that cities history would be interesting!

      Name: In Vino Veritas
      Theme: Interested in researching altered states and how they relate to revelation of truths, but they do have a marked preference for enjoyable experiences.
      Order makeup: Primary: Free Council, but they have a Mysterium member, as well. They also had a Silver Ladder who moved on to bigger and better things, though they keep in touch from time to time.
      Example member: Charlie Mops Free Councilor, Obrimos
      de facto leader of the cabal.

      His Nimbus is disorienting, providing sensations as if hallucinating on entheogens while drunk, but the symbols have meaning. It's reminiscent of Mage Sight and tripping.

      He awakened when he was depressed and drank himself into a stupor.,Discovering something more awakened a thirst for deeper knowledge.He's been experimenting ever since, but he formalized that when his mentor wanted an explanation for why he was almost never sober. After successfully demonstrating practical insights he'd gained from the practice, his mentor actually started participating in the research. Charlie doesn't lie about the fact he enjoys his research, but it is still a magical Obsession as part of his path. He studies the barriers of the mind that hinder magical understanding and has been developing various means of bypassing these.

      Most people think he's a dilettante and drunk, which is an impression he prefers and cultivates, but it's far from the truth. He's not only an intense scholar, he's been working on magical combat skills using his insights into altered mental states. He is believed to be a triple Disciple, but he's actually already mastered Prime and extremely advanced in Forces, Life, and Mind. He's keeping that on the DL, though.

      Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.

          Name: Le Cuisine de Amontillado

          Theme: They believe that the Awakening is an ecstatic experience, it requires the right place and state of mind for one to see beyond the Lie. The culinary arts allow one to mix a tangible sensory experience with the subtlety of the ambiance. Therefore, the right combination should allow Sleepers to Awaken. But to find it requires bold chefs, ones willing to go beyond mundane cuisine to create their menu.

          They run a restaurant of the same name that serves as their Sanctum, but have some lands outside the town walls where they grow ingredients.

          Order makeup:

          Bocuse - Silver Ladder Moros, Gaoler of Ialdabaoth: The pâtissier and head chef. Specializes in cultivating the right spirits, "recruiting staff", sculpting new ingredients fom Essence and uncovering the secrets of Awakened gastronomers past.

          Rikyū - Free Council Obrimos, Forge Master variant (Matter primary with Prime secondary): The tools specialist and a master of the Japanese tea ceremony. He seeks out new ways to sculpt the lanscape to have the right Resonance to pierce the Lie. Also whips up all sorts of new gadgets and imbued items for the cabal.

          Hannah Glasse - Adamantine Arrow Mastigos, Brotherhood of the Demon Wind: A virtuosa of 18th century cookery with a specialty in utilizing kitchen knives. Scoring, butchering or chopping vegetables hair thin, her bladework accomplishes it all with fluid ease. She leads the team in field work, whether exploring uncharted lands for new ingredients or hunting down a cryptid.

          Parmentier - Guardian of the Veil Acanthus, Bearer of the Eternal Voice: An expert mixologist specializing in molecular gastronomy, creating innovating drinks and then using supernatural ingredients to elevate them to new heights (he would probably offer the cabal his version of the Aviary's Jungle Bird upon their first visit). He is the biggest campaigner for promoting the cabal's work to other Awakened (and he keeps them out of trouble through information gathering).

          Rumor has it that he delved too often into the Hedge to negotiate for rare ingredients and part of its madness clung to his soul. Some say he took a fascination with how Changelings harness Glamour and learned to distill it into a liquid that composes his best cocktails. Others even go so far as to say that the only feeling he distills in this process is exquisite fear and that he is a Fang of Mara in disguise.

          Hebe - Mysterium Thrysus: The medicinal cooking specialist of the team, she focuses on cultivating ingredients. She alters any organic or mundane ingredients so they can be mass produced. From pate de foie gras and sushi you can pick off a tree to kelpies with lemon risotto bones, her experiments blur the line between the organic and inorganic. Rumor had it she is codifying these skills into a Legacy (Matter & Life). In other words, she can be depicted as being anywhere along the gradient from unorthodox chef to a mad scientist.

              Name: Those Dicks
              Theme: There isn't a cabal theme, but there is a cabal ethos: We watch each other's backs because other mages are crazy dicks.
              Makeup: a Guardians and Free Council. Also some unaligned.
              Example: Holly Torn Acanthus
              Holly was a careless girl before Awakening. She Awoke through a horrible fairy story playing out, where she saw people buying and trading their lives and fortunes to beings with no sense of human compassion, as she was being captured. She stole back her name stone and fled her captors into the wilderness. She found a cave to hide it in which contained many other similar stones.

              Holly isn't carefree .any more and studies Fate and Death primarily, looking for portents of disaster and means of bypassing them..Some suggested that the cabal should choose a theme revolving around the underworld, but only a few of the members actually participate in that research. It's not that she became neurotic, but simply that she is normally somber, now.

              One of the Free Council members is a master of disguise and works perfecting the art of infiltration. Holly can usually still tell that he's alive when he fakes his death, now, but it's grown harder than ever before. For his part, he considers fooling her an accomplishment worth attempting. She doesn't like when he sneaks into hostile mage territory to steal secrets but her predictions suggest that it's part for their continued survival.

              Holly is a fairly quiet sort. She doesn't like large groups, especially not groups of mages. Even the entire cabal is a bit of a strain and she actually like most of them. She is certainly not the leader of this motley crew, but she is a respected member of their team. Her two closest friends are the leader, Ishmael, and Democritus, who shares fascinating insights into death and transformation.

              (Ishmael is leader in fact, not it any official position, which has been hindering the function of the cabal but Ishmael is apparently the most hesitant of the cabal to make it official.Holly Torn checked all the prophecy, and if they don't unify soon, it could spell disaster. Now she needs to break it to her friend that he needs to step up.)

              Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.
