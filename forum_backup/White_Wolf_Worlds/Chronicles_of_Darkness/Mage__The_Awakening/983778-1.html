    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    Something to consider as we drift away from the initial complaint, is that the newer production models allows Onyx Path to take a middle ground approach to these sorts of things:

    They can sell Legacies individually/in small groups as cheap pdfs, and sell a big book of them. The big book will be cheaper than each individually, but if you only find 1/3 of them worth using, you can just get those.

    While I like mechanics (including Legacies) presented in supplements to highlight their themes, I also appreciate having the big books of z-splats because there's a lot of cool ideas that might not get published because they're not appropriate to a specific supplement.

    I will take more bloodlines and legacies any way that I can get them. Lodges in 2nd Edition have been growing on me as well. I would even be willing to pay for 'DLC content' where one legacy is released at a time and sold for 99 cents or something. That seems the worst case scenario to me, but I'd unquestionably do it.

                I would welcome a few legacy books, but far more if the theme of the book was an interesting topic with rules everyone could tap and the legacies were interacting with.

                IMnsHO, Left Hand Path spent too much time on fluff (most of it was obvious, so was tedious to read) but the descriptions of how the factions worked were welcome.

                A compromise between that sort of supplement and the old legacy encyclopedia would be welcome.


                A few things I'd like to see:

                A book on magical crafts with a list of related legacies.
                A full-fledged source book on Awakened Alchemy, all intricate and optional; It could comfortably include 2e Uncrowned Kings (fleshed out and expanded like Tremere in LHP, say) and a few others.
                Abyssal practices, so not (just) a list of scelesti legacies, and not Intruders: The Reprint, but focus on other aspects of the Abyss and its power in the world. The Abyssal Shard and Disbelief (Cover a minor template for the people who go towards the Abyss rather than away from it when exposed to Supernal magic), Abyssal intelligences and their effects such as tainted spirits and truly abyssal entities, Abyssal Awakened and, Quippothic Arcana, and so forth. Could include Banisher legacies here.

                Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.
