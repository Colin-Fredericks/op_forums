                      First, a comment from the developer about Atlantis: http://theonyxpath.com/the-fate-of-a...the-awakening/

                      Before I get to your problems I'll go with the sell. You specifically asked how the Pentacle and the Seers can work together to make a modern drama/horror game? Well, did you like the Matrix and the world it posited? Because you have all those elements: A secret, world spanning, conspiracy that controls not just the government, but everyone's fundamental understanding of reality as it is. A conspiracy that is not content to rule people's wallets and homes, but also minds and souls. Then you have a secret group of people who have seen through the lie of the world and who fight to free humanity from its shackles. Remember the Oracle and her group of talented kids? She was an Acanthus of the Mysterium. The Pentacle are just what would happen if you really delved into the different factions of people who had Awakened from the Matrix, but didn't really have a Zion to go to.

                      Cypher was a Seer sympathizer and recruit.

                      Now, you can play Mage while ignoring the Pentacle and the Seers and it's still a great game. You can totally be a mage from a cultural Tradition exploring the Shadow, but you did ask me to sell you on specifically the Pentacle and the Seers.

                      And if you want something based on real life people, the Seers are as human as you can get. Look around at society and tell me that most people wouldn't support a theoretically corrupt government as long as it kept them fed, housed, and entertained.

                          I was always a fan of Mage: The Ascencion. Well, certainly I don't expect the same game. But Requiem and Forsaken didn't disapointed me.

                          Mage came with some really neat stuff. I had some minor issues with the magic system, but I really liked it overall. I like the idea of True Magic and of a splat with Dynamic power.

                          The take on the origin of True Magic being Plato-inspired was an interesting twist, if not all that unexpected. Again, I liked it.

                          So you can see that I do like a lot of things in Mage.

                          But them things start to go sour. The Watchtowers aren't all that bad. I think they could be a bit more freeform, with the five presented being common, but more open to possibilities. But i can live with that.

                          Atlantis went too far, though. Nothing against this kind of legend by itself. The problem was how pervasive it was on the setting.

                          When I see a line for WoD, one of the things I like is to imagine all kind of people turning into this splat and figuring out things by themselfs. Mage society isn't comprised by Mages from eons ago, but by people Awakening today. So... why should anyone follow those stories and those orders? Why this society works that way? And all I saw in the core book of 1st ed was a bunch of orders strongly based on a mythic society no one remembers, that only make sense in the context they create to themselfs. And it failed to convince me that a recently Awakened would join most of them, even less believe in all its ideas. It failed to convince me that someone who Awakened to true power would even care about them or pay any attention. The Seers should be the major order, but they seem even worse.

                          For me, it killed the setting. And that's a shame. It seemed all the potential for the line just falled. It could be so much more. In some respects, Ascension seems to me much more nWoD-like than Awakening. Awakening seems more like D&D with bad combat rules.

                          Yet, there are a lot of fans for this game. So I saw evidence that maybe I just misinterpreted the game, or it changed for the better with time. But I don't have much time and when I remember the 1st core the will to read more books vanishes. I started to read the blogs about the Orders, and a do liked most of the content, tough not everything, but that's ok. Just not enough yet to get rid of my Atlantis problem. And the Seers seem as silly to me now as they seemed back them. The new take on the Orders seem good, but the core principle of the pentacle is still devoid of appeal or sense to me.

                          Now I'm here. In another thread the OP asked for reasons to play Changeling: The Dreaming. It is an interesting thread and gave me the idea. I just wanted to see what the fans had to say about the game. Sorry if this was so annoying.
