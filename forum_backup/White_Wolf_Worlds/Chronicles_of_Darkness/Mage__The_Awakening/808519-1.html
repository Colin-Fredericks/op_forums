                I've given three quite serious ones so far. High Speech. Astral projection. Rote. The whole paradox bomb is perhaps a little out there, but I like throwing out additional thoughts that get inspired by the conversation.

                If that's not enough...

                * Imbue the skull with your signature nimbus, then while it's still imbued rush through the astral realms to the Anima Mundi where a special new realm has opened up. If you don't reach it before your nimbus fades, the door to the memory realm is invisible to you.

                * Scrutinize the skull with active magesight until you attract the supernal guardian of the skull's attention and it approaches to open your mind to the memories.

                * Use a full unmaking effect on the skull as a sign of faith. The physical skull appears to briefly vanish while the memories flood into you. You can then relinquish the memories anytime you want to recreate the physical crystal skull.

                * Assemble the sacred materials of each of the supernal realms, each one fashioned in a unique supernal symbol that can be used as a Yantra. However there are only four supernal tools and five realms, the ritual doesn't work with one of the new order based Supernal implements. You have to find something far older still, the missing fifth sacred implement of the supernal realms.

                * Summon a being from the Supernal realm of Pandemonium as only they can access the secret lore stored within.

                * The activation phrase must be pronounced perfectly, but no one has mastered the high speech to that degree in over a thousand years. Maybe if you could somehow bring the skull to the realm of the Omphalos and breath in its sacred mists, but how can you bring the artifact into the astral realms and what kind of attention will that draw?

                * Or perhaps a great linguist with one of the language based legacies of the present world can access the skull.

                * Only the first person to touch the skull when it was retrieved from the tomb can access the memories in the skull, but that person was a sleeper archaeologist. Can you show a sleeper such a mighty artifact or is that blasphemy against the mysteries. Would the sleeping curse in his mind destroy the memories if he was given access to them? The link would be broken and the skull could be used by anyone again... if the sleeper was killed.

                * When you access the memories (which turns out to be surprisingly easy) the passage onwards is sealed by an iron barricade with the symbol of an eye upon it. The exarchs want no one to have access to this information. But perhaps a recently defected seer knows the secret of how to open the gate of barred souls, if their defection is genuine and you're not just handing the secrets of the skulls over to the greatest enemy.

                Any of those okay?

                                How about this: the skull is actually a form of Supernal compressed storage. In order to decompress the Astral information contained within, the skull must be pressed against the forehead of someone with no memory. The skull then dissolves as the information it stores is copied into the mind of the recipient, whose personality and memories become identical to that of the ancient individual used to make the skull in the first place. Once this occurs, the individual's Oneiros becomes the gateway to the information. As a bonus, the person becomes a Sleepwalker if they were a Sleeper.
