    Questions about the New York setting presented in Tome of the Pentacle

    08-09-2024, 07:26 PM
    So, I'm trying to run a game in the setting presented Ch. 5 of Tome of the Pentacle and I really like what I'm reading but I have an issue, it feels incomplete, and not in that leave space for players to create their own kind of way, but in a structurally incoherent way. Normally, I'd just fill in the blanks myself but knowing that my players may stumble across those excellent short stories that catalog several interesting elements (characters/cabals/sanctums/caucus dynamics) drives me crazy.

    Two Questions about it:

    1) Is there anyone who's taken the time to make a concise list of those published story elements (characters/cabals/sanctums/etc.)? I like what's there but I'd hate writing up a Lictor for Seshat's caucus and discover one was mentioned in Left Hand Path or whatever. I get that its a massive setting but, setting aside descriptions for stuff like the Princeton Athenea, the Cryptopoly, or Labyrinth, unless I missed it on my rereading, they didn't mention the name of Seshat's Cabal, only that Heirophant Imuthes is a member. I'd be fine with this if Seshat had outgrown her cabal, but to know she belongs in one and a political ally in dire straights is in it. . . (quick rant: Oh My God? I get not using the cabal alliance chart but why didn't they just give the disclaimer "there's more than can be listed" then offer a simple summary of stuff in this campaign setting?)

    2) The fact that every concilium/caucus has it's own nuances aside, does anyone know what any of these cults look like when they gather? I mean, I've run enough games to work it out but I feel like the writer of Ch. 5 had ideas that didn't make the cut or didn't have the word count to make it coherent, and it drives me crazy. I mean, again, I can make a sanctum, I can build events for a convocation and/or make layers of Councilium politics, etc., but if I were running this in Boston, I'd grab the Boston Unveiled book open to the section about Order dynamics and know immediately, but in this staggeringly more complex setting of New York, there's dramatically less information peppered throughout this PDF, collected across a meek 30ish pages, and sprinkled through several short stories in two editions.

    Please, tell me someone has taken research notes to make it more coherent. I'd lose sleep if such an interesting setting went unused but it's just so insubstantial as presented. It's just that as a gamer with a BA in English, I'm sure some storyteller out there has a Word Doc with the missing information, some of which may be too shallow for publication, but way more articulate.

                      So, Carter and Locksmith are from the corebook fiction; Fern and Niniane are one-time, name-only mentions in the Fallen World Chronicle Anthology; Riley and Cade are from the opening fiction of Tome of the Pentacle; Aldin, John Welsh, Arabelle, and Zazu are from that summary I can't find anymore; and Shabbatai is the sample Kabbalah character from 1e's Magical Traditions who is from New York, hence his seemingly odd placement here.
