    Mana Management, or Are You A Mana-Hungry Tass-Grabber?

    10-26-2016, 12:43 AM
    I wanted to ask a few questions about how you find the Mana supply working in your games.

    I'm coming from a Werewolf game where Loci offer a lot more Essence than Hallows do Mana. Despite that, our last game had unreliable access to Loci and we found most of our Essence coming from other sources.

    How much Mana do people seem to use, per session? What are their most common methods of getting Mana back? Hallow seems limited, especially with the other nice Merits though once you've got a Legacy Oblations seem the easiest method. Supernal Revelation and Exceptional Successes seem nice but uncommon unless you're more or less built for it, and lets pretend you're not the spamming-praxes type. Scouring looks like it would be a great method in an easy-going game that skips a few days, allowing characters to gain the Mana without suffering the penalty during playtime. Has anyone had mages resort to Blood Sacrifice?

      Mana is one of those things that sits around for a while and then goes all at once. When it goes, it usually stays gone for quite a while, because it's usually in the middle of an adventure and no-one has time to sit around praying to their ancestors for an hour. You can generally find ways to recharge during downtime but, during play, you're stuck with tass and familiars for the most part.

      Personally, I've wanted to try the blood sacrifice thing with a Thyrsus. It seems far less ethically dubious when you actually eat the animal after.
