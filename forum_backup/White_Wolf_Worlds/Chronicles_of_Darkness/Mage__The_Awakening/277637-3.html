                            So you want to use one of those legacies but can't pick which? Figure out her personal philosophy and goals and then pick the legacy that meshes best with that.

                                            Originally posted by Freemind View Post
                                            Just to add: if there is ever a name that sounds odd /not appropriate as a Shadow Name in your native language, despite the concept working, you can always use the Latin, Greek, Persian or Hindi words, since those languagea are the basis of many of the Orders terminology. Latin especially works for western mages, as it still forms the basis of legal, medical and scientific terminology across most fields.
                                            (...)
                                            Also, people of certain cultural/ethnic background could use their native tongues. Two of my players are playing Irish immigrants in the 1940s, and they use bastardized* Irish Gaelic to come up with their Shadow Names.
                                            Thanks for this advice - I forget sometimes that Order were created from Hellenistic exchange with India's culture. Indian names can be especially prominent in Britain, as it was one of greatest colonies of British Empire. It's very good idea to translate working simple terms to exotic languages.

                                                        I have a Moros 11th Question called Keats. He wanted to be a writer, then after Awakening realized he could never write anything meaningful again that could be shown to Sleepers for fear of subconsciously slipping Supernal truths on it, and thus limits himself to writing only shallow meaningless drivel. He solves crimes to pass the time and avoid writing at all if he can.

                                                        Other characters with good names from my players:

                                                        23: An Acanthus FC that saw the number 23 everywhere (it was his Unseen Sense reaction).
                                                        Herod: Sent to assasinate the leader of a cult that was being manipulated by an Abyssal thing (this is based on a real-life cult in northern Mexico called Fidencistas, their leaders style themselves in the image of the Niño Fidencio, which was in turn a kid which hosted supposed reincarnation of Christ and remained a child all his life while working miracles).
                                                        Zakariya Al'Razi: A young FC self-styled Alchemist.
                                                        Jakobson: A young FC Obrimos Crypotologos with an obsession for linguistics.
                                                        Ozymandias: A SL Sisterhood of the Blessed owner of the once most important Hallow in the city, leader of a critical cabal. Everything fell apart eventually.
                                                        Tzitzimime: A SL Mastigos hierarch, young and inexperienced though.
                                                        Astarte: A FC Mastigos girl from Russia that always worn horns and looked crazy but inoffensive, eventually turned out to be a banisher from the start. Managed to get the FC of the city to eat the flesh of a Mage she killed by slipping parts of it into the meals while pegging her crimes on the Seers.
                                                        Marquis of O: A Mastigos SL with a sadistic bent and member of the Reality Stalkers, always dressed in 100% red clothing.
                                                        Persephone: An Arrow which Seers predicted would become the host of an Ochema of the Unity. In her attempts to prevent her fate she became infested by Abyssal entities and things went downhill from there.

                                                        Others from NPCs:

                                                        The Adversary: A Mastigos Councilor that does his best to antagonize literally everyone just to cause them existential doubt.
                                                        Mr. X and Ms. Y: A couple of Seers with powerful influence over one of the police precincts in the city.
                                                        Adramelech: A Mastigos Reaper which devised an operation that could make kids under 12 yo permanently incapable of evil acts.
                                                        Iscariot: An ex-SL Obrimos which believes he can ascend through embodiment of the concept of Redemption through Treason.
                                                        The Monitor: A Seer from Panopticon which some think can communicate with her selves from other realities (literally from other chronicles where she appears).
                                                        Acteon: A sleepwalker politician (yes, he knows that he needs a shadow name despite not being a mage and despite him being a public persona) that actively seeks mages to work for them in exchange for boons. Mages who cross him have a bad time.
                                                        Joe Wasp: World renowned fashion designer by day, high-ranking Praetorian dealing in weapons trafficking by night.
