    Homebrew: Arcane Archery

    12-06-2017, 10:52 AM
    I had a lot of free time and tried to develop a new fighting style for mages based on archery and inspired by the Arcane Archer class in D&D. I'm not certain it is a very good / useful / balanced / flavourful Merit so I would appreciate criticism.

    Arcane Archery (Style, 1-5)
    Prerequisites: Adamantine Arrows Status 1, Firearms 2 (or 2 dots in whatever skill your group uses for archery), Wits OR Composure 3 and one Arcana with dots equal to or greater than dots in Arcane Archery.

    Humans have been using bows and arrows for many thousands of years and the symbolism of accuracy, precision and penetration is well ingrained into the fallen world. Slowly but surely some warlike mages have learned how to use and apply this symbolism and weave their spellcasting and archery together as one. This style is actually 10 different styles; the mage dedicates one Arcana to their Arcane Archery and will have different benefits depending on what Arcana they chose.

    At the Storyteller's discretion this style can be applied to modern firearms as well. While this is a style exclusive for archers and based on the thousands of years of archery traditions, firearms have been around for a few hundred years so it is not unreasonable that progressive mages have worked this style into these weapons instead.

    1 - Zen Archery
    Using bows and arrows as strong symbolism for truth, accuracy and precision the mage can use their magic to massively enhance their mundane vigilance, gaining a bonus to mundane perception equal to their Arcane Archery dots.

    2 - Imbued arrows
    The mage has learned how to allow spells to travel with the arrows they fire. If they spend 1 mana and take a turn to cast a spell on an arrow that arrow can then deliver the spell to where it is fired. Thus, the mage avoids spending Reach to make a spell Sensory. This of course requires a successful shooting roll and the spell will dissipate if it is not discharged during its duration. This can only be done with spells from the Arcanum the mage chose for this Merit, so a Death archer can only deliver Death spells this way.

    3 - Archer's Eye
    The mage can spend 1 mana to reflexively use their magic to gain a one-shot boost to their natural archery skills. This can be done even after the dice are rolled. The bonus received depends on their dedicated Arcana:
    Death: Arrows travel partially through twilight, ignoring penalties due to concealment and cover equal to dots in Arcane Archery. The mage in this way can also deal damage to ghosts and other deathly ephemera in twilight. In addition, this damage is metaphysically strong and is considered Resistant.
    Fate: Using a bow and arrow the mage can get an Exceptional Success with 3 successes instead of 5.
    Forces: Forceful arrows causes the Knocked Down Tilt in addition to damage.
    Life: A combination of enhanced muscle-memory and perception eliminates penalties due to range and concealment equal to dots in Arcane Archery.
    Matter: Gain a bonus to armor piercing equal to dots in Arcane Archery.
    Mind: A successful hit pierces mind as well as flesh and bereaves the target of Willpower, up to the archer's dots in Arcane Archery.
    Prime: Ignore Mage Armor equal to dots in Arcane Archery.
    Space: Seeker arrows eliminate penalties due to range and concealment equal to dots in Arcane Archery.
    Spirit: Arrows travel partially through twilight, ignoring penalties due to concealment and cover equal to dots in Arcane Archery. The mage in this way can also deal damage to spirits and other deathly ephemera in twilight. Alternately, the mage can pierce the Gauntlet with her arrows to strike at spirits on the other side. Spirits targetted this way gains a bonus Defense equal to Gauntlet strength.
    Time: The mage automatically goes first in initiative this round. If another character has a similar ability the two characters compare initiative normally.

    4 - Eldritch Arrows
    The mage never needs to carry ammunition again if they do not wish so. At the cost of 1 Mana the mage simply creates arrows automatically when they fire their bows for the duration of the scene, whether using ambient materials such as light (Forces), air particles (Matter) or microorganisms (Life), or even using the forces of chance (Fate), entropy (Death) or truth itself (Prime).

    5 - Arrow of Unmaking
    If the mage pays 1 Mana their arrow is fused with Unmaking itself. If successfully hit by the arrow of the mage, the target must succeed on a Clash of Wills test against the Gnosis + Arcana of the character or die.

                          Your second level (Imbued arrows) sounds more like it should be Prime's schtick to me than a general benefit, even so.

                          Creating weapons from nothing is a pretty major ability. Fate ammunition should be a roll to see if you luck out and stumble across something appropriate. Spirit and Death should have penalties based on being intangible or immaterial with the concomitant advantage of having the substance quite available. Matter could have the drawback that coalescing or drawing an arrow takes a moment, but is just shy of always available. Mind could be fun: instead of physical damage, the arrows could be imbued with emotions or target Doors. That deserve some more thought. Space, well, I'm actually kinda thinking that establishing a Sensory bond to an arrow fired for a few moments rather than providing ammunition could work. Maybe allow conjuring arrows from wherever they happen to be, instead. Life could allow drawing of an arrow out of a tree, maybe. That would fit the flaw from Matter. Perhaps allow Life to disease or poison a target, though, perhaps even crippling them.

                          Another take is that the merit allows spells of the arcana to be shot from a bow as if an arrow, without needing a real one. That's still quite useful.

                          5th is absurdly overpowered. I honestly don't mind an insta-kill very much for Death, (though that would be Making) given the arcana requirements you listed for the style, and the fluff fits Life, as well, but otherwise, it's pretty off-theme for the rest of the arcana. Spirit Bane, Ghost Eater, and things like that sound better. Of course, there really isn't any point to even have such a level in the merit. With the spread you already have listed, you just need to imbue an Unmaking (or Making Death) spell into the arrow.

                          Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.
