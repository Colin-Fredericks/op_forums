                  The storyteller uses it - either to apply a new Reach the player didn't intend or undo one they did.

                  So, say someone tried a touch range spell at advanced duration, released the paradox and the ST rolled two extra reach. The ST could make the spell short-lived and targetting some random bystander, by undoing the advanced duration reach and slapping a new one onto range.

                  The trick of Scelesti (mechanics still being tinkered with) is that they can try to control where these paradox reaches go. Most mages can't; it's in the gentle hands of your Storyteller, who is encouraged to fuck with you.
