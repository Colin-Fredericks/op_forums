    Let’s try finding a way to get a dragon with all the Arcana.

    Death: Get a ghost, then use your own magic or another ghost’s Moliate Numen to get a dragon shape. Or try to catch a draconic Chthonian.

    Fate: Luck yourself into coming across a dragon. Which is likely a fairy, given how Fate interacts with them extensively.

    Forces: If you have Prime too, Imbue some fire and flight magic into a monitor lizard or an alligator.
    Alternatively, be always ready to throw fireballs whenever your pet reptile ‘roars’. For the people who object, throw more fireballs.

    Life: Fleshcraft and breed your own dragons!

    Matter: Jury-rig yourself a transforming dragon mecha from flamethrowers and drones.

    Mind: Summon a Goetia.
    Alternatively, befriend a Beast with a draconic Horror, and either ask him for a piggyback or adopt a Horrorspawn from him.
    ​​​​​​​Alternatively, conjure a hallucination of a dragon.

    Prime: See the above bit about Imbuement. Alternatively, throw enough magic to track down Verges where draconic Cryptids may live.

    Space: As scouting for Verges above, but this time look for interstices that contain ‘real’ dragons.

    Spirit: Summon a spirit. Surely some specimens among the courts of fire, waters, earth, and gold look like dragons.

    Time: Wow this is hard. No clue myself!

                                            In Second Edition, the Diamond Orders don't necessarily literally believe that the ancient progenitors of magic were something that could be described as dragons any more than they think the Awakened City was some kind of ocean spire.

                                            The dragon fixation is a holdover from when the Tremere were in the sect.

                                            Anyway, Temporal Pocket doesn't create accelerate time within a space, it causes a subject to experience the flow of time detached from everything else.

                                            And ends the moment they consume things.

                                            You'd end that spell and find a very fine pile of dust.

                                            Although if you cast it over an area, you're far more likely to have the ending condition of "the animal walks out of it in search of food".

                                            Also, evolution is not magic, accumulated traits need to be within things like biochemical possibilities, possible to reconcile with existing morphology, and basic physics. You can't evolution your way into something being able to defy gravity (birds can fly with the benefit of being incredibly light).

                                            "Select for fire proof features"; fire's effect on tissue is a function of what temperatures in excess of several hundred degrees does to protein structures, you cannot evolve into something resistant to that any more than you can evolve into a rock.
