    [Custom Legacy, 2E] Scales of Themis - help needed.

    01-17-2017, 08:17 AM
    I'm working on a custom legacy for my Acanthus Théarch, and wanted to build one that centered around truth and justice and working with the Lex Magica in a more direct way than any of the ones I could find.
    EDIT:

    It is now presented in what is probably gonna be its final form.
    Many thanks to those who helped. Especially Mrmdubois and Troy.

    Scales of Themis.
    Themis was a pre-Hellenistic deity said to be the embodiment of order, law and justice. She represented the divine order of things, in a manner that was far larger than any Man could have thought of. In ancient Sleeper society, her will was often ascribed to that which was undeniably right.
    The Greeks developed the concept further, and eventually, themis became a word that meant, “To put things in their right place”. She became known as the organizer of assemblies, particularly those that dealt with the communal order of things. Her symbol was a perfectly balanced set of scales. This has later spread across the Western hemisphere, as the ideal icon of justice.
    It was natural for the Silver Ladder to accept this ideal as their representation of the Lex Magica, and while the order at large strived to enforce this, some mages saw the need to “balance the scales” as they were. The strove to perfect the laws of Awakened society and make sure that the laws that ruled the Supernal realms carried as much weight in the Fallen world.
    The legacy was developed by a cabal of mages, who saw how easily law turned to tyranny and how the will of the Exarchs and the Silver Ladder grew dangerously similar.
    They felt the need to correct these laws, to put them out of reach of corruption and personal ambition. They saw in the tales of Themis, how law should neither serve Man nor punish them, but instead provide a frame for man, Awakened and Sleeper to exist.
    The Blind are not a police force. They do not spend their time infiltrating law-breakers or investigating crime scenes. This, they leave to the Guardians of the Veil. The Scales of Themis are strong believers in the organization of the Diamond, and are satisfied to make the Lex Magica support this, and leave the different orders and legacies to their own devices – if in concordance with the Law, of course.
    The Scales of Themis work to influence the local Consilia and Convocations in their treatment and development of the Lex Magica, and usually develop considerable political acumen and power. The number of Judges to have fallen to either personal ambition or Tyranny are not few, and the legacy often keeps a good rapport with the Guardians to work against this.
    An experienced Themistopóloi is an asset for a Consilium, as their knowledge of the law is usually unparalleled in a given area. Most Awakened respect this knowledge, and, as the Judge always passes judgment with the letter of the law in hand, their rulings are generally accepted by those involved.
    The Judges usually do not care much for the Free Council, as they see their ideals of democracy as almost antithetical to true law and the rightful order of things. If the law can change according to the whims of those it governs, it is not truly a law.

    Origins
    Parentage: Acanthus or the Silver Ladder.
    Nickname: Judges, the Blind, Themistopóloi.
    Background: Many Judges come from a background of Law. Some were police officers, others lawyers, and some even judges. The Judges often have a background in academia and some were engaged in politics, even before Awakening. Common for nearly all of them, however, is an experience with injustice, and a desire to set it right.
    Appearance: The Scales of Themis usually present themselves in muted colors and unassuming clothing, the better to subsume the Man when presenting the Law. This also means that keeping up a proper appearance is important, as the Judge represents the Law, and cannot provide others with any means of discrediting them.
    As the Mage progresses in the legacy, the spirit of law works its way into his Pattern. A Judge of high Gnosis often has a stern look, and as his understanding of the Law grows, his understanding of humans grows dimmer.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Prime 2, Academics 2 with a specialty in Law.
    Initiation: A prospective Themistopóloi must demonstrate a thorough understanding of the local Lex Magica and how it interacts with Sleeper laws and the customs of mage society. Sometimes, this takes the form of an extended debate or lecture, other times, the prospective judge is called upon to serve in a trial, as counsel, judge or jury member.
    Organization: The Scales of Themis operate mostly at the command of the local Silver Ladder and the Consilium. Their loyalty should never be divided, as they focus themselves on the Law. Sometimes, this entails being sent to a different Consilium’s territory, if the need for judgment is great enough there.
    Theory: There is only Law. The Supernal Truth should govern Awakened society as it did before the Fall. A true law protects and guides those it governs, whereas the laws of Man protects the interests of those who write them.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Prime

    Yantras: Succeeding on an Academics roll related to the spell (+2), verbally explaining how a specific law pertains to the situation at hand to an associate (+1) or the target of the spell (+2), researching the laws concerning the target of the spell (+1 to +3, depending on the amount of time spent).
    Oblations: Studying or reciting the Lex Magica, debating the law or a specific case with a peer, standing in judgment of a law-breaker.

    Attainments
    First: Know Transgression
    Prerequisites: Inititation
    Themis knows when the natural Law is broken. When the Themistopólos activates his attainment, he has a small omen of the next violation of the Lex Magica in the area ruled by his Consilium and the path he will need to take to get closer to uncovering it. This only applies to the area under direct and continued sovereignty of the Consilium.
    This imitates the Serendipity rote (p. 135) in that it leads the Judge closer to his goal, but has the added restriction that it only applies to violations of the Lex Magica.
    Optional: Prime 1
    A Judge who possesses at least one dot of Prime, will automatically see through any attempts to hide transgressions against the Lex Magica or the rulings of the Consilium, as per the rote Pierce Deception (p 165). If hidden through magic, this provokes a Clash of Wills.
    Second: Keep the Word
    Prerequisites: Fate 2, the Judge must increase either Empathy, Investigation or Politics to 2 dots
    A Judge keeps his word and stands by it. This is known by his peers. However, the Judge knows all too well that this trust will not always be rewarded.
    The Judge learns to keep an eye on those who swear oaths to him.
    Whenever the subject of this spell breaks the Lex Magica or an agreement specifically made between her and the Judge pertaining to this, for example an agreed upon castigation or an oath to uphold it, the Judge receives instant notice of this, briefly has a vision of the transgression and can track the violator until the duration lapses. This emulates Oaths Fulfilled, p135. (Reach spent: +1 Advanced duration, +1 vision, +1 tracking, bonus reach gained from limitations)
    Optional: Prime 2
    The Judge is known to speak the truth. Those judges who are gifted in the Arcana of Prime can back this up with their magic. If a Judge possesses 2 dots of Prime, those listening to him can hear him and understand him clearly, regardless of distance, noise, or language barriers. Moreover, all subjects know, on a soul-deep level, that what the mage says is true. This only holds up, if the Judge speaks words he knows to be true. (This emulates Words of Truth p.168). This affects those witnessing the activation of the attainment, in which the Judge must recite the oath to be surveilled.
    Third: Swear the Oath
    Prerequisites: Fate 3, Academics 3, the Judge must also increase the skill used to gain the previous attainment to 3 dots, or increase one of the other possible skills to 2 dots.
    An oath sworn to a Themistopólos is not a just a collection of words. It carries in it the Supernal order of things and must be in concordance with the Lex Magica. Swearing and confirming the oath takes a scene.
    This attainment emulates Sworn Oaths, p.137, but may only be used to craft an oath to uphold or enforce the Lex Magica. This attainment has Advanced Duration.
    Optional Prime 3
    A Themistopólos who possesses at least 3 dots of Prime, may grant the subject an additional protection from magical attempts to stop her from fulfilling her vow.
    The magic inherent in the vow surrounds the subject and makes her able to Withstand hostile magic with the Potency of the Attainment. This attainment has Advanced Duration.
    Fourth: Grant Forgiveness
    Prerequisites: Fate 4, Academics 4.
    The Law defines what is right and what is wrong. It describes how to punish those that go against it. But it also tells its subjects what to do with those who have served their time.
    The role of the Judge is to investigate, to know and to understand, but it also is to forgive. The Judge may decide the subject has served his time, or that the punishments levied against a subject by others has been unjust and so, may relieve their duress.
    The Judge consults the Lex Magica and the rulings made and describes how the subject may overcome their afflictions. The subject’s task depends on the crime or the power of the spell in question. This Attainment functions like Atonement, p138. This Attainment has Advanced Duration.
    Optional: Prime 4
    The Judge skilled in Prime may wash away the tell-tale signs of sins from their very Pattern. The Judge may remove the marks upon the subject’s soul if she successfully atones for the crime.
    This mimics Cleanse Aura, p. 168, but works to remove marks on the soul as well as the signs of Awakened interference. It only works for the marks of the crimes she atones for.
    Fifth: Pass Judgment
    Prerequisites: Fate 5, Academics 5, the Judge must also increase the skill used to gain the second attainment to 4 dots, or increase one of the other possible skills to 3 dots.
    There is no law if there is no punishment. If upholding the law is the same as breaking it, the law has no consequence but is purely philosophy.
    The Themistopólos makes sure it is not.
    The hardest part of a Judge’s work, is understanding a person’s crimes, holding them against the Law, and declaring them guilty. Nonetheless it is also the most important part.
    The Judge can levy the power of Pariah, p140, against a breaker of the Lex Magica. The subject must be proven guilty under the Law, and may be given the chance to defend themselves. Refusal to do so means a forfeiture of the right.
    This attainment has Advanced Duration.
    Optional: Prime 5
    The punishment of these supreme Judges is terrible indeed. The world should know of the transgressor’s crimes, and the Judge skilled in Prime, makes sure they do.
    When the Judge passes the sentence, they imprint upon the subject a mark that describes them as a breaker of laws and an enemy of the Truth. This is visible to even Peripheral Mage Vision and is instantly understood by all Awakened. Those witnessing the glyph understand the nature of his crimes and instinctively see through all the transgressor’s attempts to conceal or hide the truth.

                          The Legacy -- I think this is an awesome concept for a Legacy. It's surprising that something like this hasn't been done before because this Legacy's purpose is one of the core principles behind the Silver Ladder.                           Might I suggestion reversing the Parent Path and Primary Arcanum...

                          The Attainment -- I like the Attainment and I'm left wondering how much Prime can offer as you progress in your skill with the Arcanum that will also be applicable to the themes and purpose of this Legacy. Prime is good for seeing past deceptions and even illusions and stuff, but once you get to a certain point, Prime no longer applies to those things in a way that would be beneficial. Fate on the other hand is much more in line with the "Scales of Justice" and things like that.

                          Parent Path -- If the Parent Path were Obrimos, with their themes of faith, judgment, justice, and conflict, it would make a lot more sense to my mind. These are Silver Ladder Obrimoi who have learned Fate to help them balance the scales against all the falsehoods they can see with their innate magics. Their Awakenings gave them a glimpse at True Law (which is a very Obrimoi type of thing -- very religious sort of experience along the lines of Moses or Mohammed). They gravitate toward this legacy where they can put their keen sense of justice to use by engaging in legal conflicts and things like that ... defending the innocent, prosecuting the guilty, mediating understanding between parties using their knowledge of True Law (Destiny?).

                                          I suggest taking a look at the Sisterhood of the Blessed and the Daoine for inspiration on the Attainments. The Sisterhood's focus is personal, the Daoine's focus is retribution ... the Scales of Themis' focus would be on others and on restorative justice. So, having the Prime abilities to see through lies and illusions is a really good tool to have. Fate gives you the ability to find broken promises and know when something is true even if the person speaking doesn't know it's true. Then you also have the power to then know how things are supposed to be; that is a great tool as well. I can see those abilities being the lower Attainments. Then Fate gives you the power to "put things right" with as much or as little subtlety as you want with all kinds of conditional hexes and curses and blessings and things. Also, don't forget to tie the Attainments power into the Lex Magica. A Silver Ladder Legacy would have it no other way. Perhaps your Attainments work especially well when your dealing with upholding the Lex or something.

                                          I was also going to suggest something for the Doctrine... Rather than Academics (or in addition to it), perhaps Politics would be a good choice? Politics represents the ability to get things done using the right rules, the right people, the right ins and outs of a system.

                                          I think it's a great idea... using the True Law as a metaphor or stand-in for other concepts like karma and destiny and things like that. "That's not just a run of bad luck you're having. It's restitution. You're paying your penance. You have transgressed against the Law. I will bring you justice."
