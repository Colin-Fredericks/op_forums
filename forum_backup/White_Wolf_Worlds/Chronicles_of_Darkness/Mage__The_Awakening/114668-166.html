                                    My rationale for what you assumed as ex post facto reasoning was this:
                                    Compelling is like placing a bowl when it's raining to gather water.
                                    Ruling is like scooping water from an open body of water, like a river or a lake, to gather water.
                                    Weaving is like digging a well to tap underground reservoirs to gather water.
                                    Patterning is like melting snow or condensing dew on leaves to gather water.
                                    Making is... well, making water.

                                    To me, Essence is always present and available as long as you have the right tools, like river water. Mana requires work to draw, even if you know for sure that it is there, like underground water. Hallows then would be the equivalent of already-developed wells placed in the middle of the town.

                                    Shadow Flesh has an argument for working without Life that Satchel made some time ago. Can't remember where it was, though. I think it was about the spell supposedly transforming the death and entropy inherent in and infused with someone's Pattern into darkness, resulting in a person intertwined with darkness to become a pseudo-mass of shadow.

                                    Of course, the Mage FAQ could plausibly debunk all this and make Shadow Flesh require Life and Channel Mana into Ruling. Still, I find rarefied and non-physical things like Mana and shadows not working as we think they should adds to the atmosphere.

                                        Originally posted by 21C Hermit View Post

                                        Of course, the Mage FAQ could plausibly debunk all this and make Shadow Flesh require Life and Channel Mana into Ruling. Still, I find rarefied and non-physical things like Mana and shadows not working as we think they should adds to the atmosphere.
                                        Directing the flow of a thing without altering its composition is a function of the Practice of Ruling (p.123). Thus, to channel mana, essence or anything else within the purview of an Arcanum should only require 2 dots in such Arcanum. Similarly, transmuting something from the purview of one Arcanum to another under the express rules, such as Life to Shadows, requires proficiency in both the subject and target Arcana.

                                        It was my understanding the Mage 2e was attempting to strictly enforce the abilities and expectations of the Practices, without balancing or exceptions. While anyone can try to establish a rationale for an exception, and you or other may like the exception or rationale, it still violates the ostensible clear purview of such Practice without any canon explanation of how or why such exception exists.

                                        If things like mana and shadows are exceptions to the expectations of the purview of a Practice as you suggest, I would simply like some clear guidelines within Creative Thaumaturgy to know when, how and why to apply such exceptions, even if the reason is "game balance".

                                            I think that it's because of the definition of Mana and Essence.

                                            Mana is (Mage rulebook):
                                            The palpable, measurable bleed of the Supernal into the material. It’s a Prime energy, a quintessential force that comes from the energy trapped when the Abyss came into existence, or sometimes comes into existence from the glory and greatness of the world. In a Hallow, it can suffuse a physical object, forming tass.

                                            Essence is (CofD Rulebook):
                                            A spiritual energy created by memories and emotions.


                                            Then we have Ruling:
                                            Ruling spells grant fuller control over phenomena than a mere Compelling spell. A Ruling spell can’t fundamentally alter its target’s abilities:
                                            Water can be directed, but not turned solid or gaseous. Time can be altered, but not overwritten. An animal can be commanded, but not made stronger or fiercer.

                                            And Weaving:
                                            Weaving spells can alter nearly any property of a target without transforming it into something completely different.


                                            Mana needs to be weaved into your pattern cause it's a more complex energy. But Essence is a product of your emotions and memories, and really, is like everywhere, so it's something that you already have around you.

                                            Of course, a FAQ would be great. But that's my honest opinion.
