                            Not yet but I have added some to the Attainment section

                            Attainments
                            The attainment of the Euthanists always revolve around Fate, be it the Beginning, the being, or the Ending. For this reason their main Arcanum is Fate. Since they are infamous with the methods of their endings they are more closely related to Death as an Optional Arcanum, however some choose to substitute their own Parxis for the Death option. The Optional Arcanum is limited to Life for the Beginning, Mind for the Being, and Death for the Ending. This Substitution would require that the Attainment be purchased as an Untutored Legacy Attainment.

                                Originally posted by Hardwire99 View Post
                                What I don't understand about the thread cutters is the above, does that mean that there are 3 versions of the Legacy?
                                No there's only one version of the legacy, but they classify their symbolic associations according to the taxonomy and purpose of the Fates. Basically, the Pentacle is agnostic-ish, it sees the ten arcana and their purposes as each being separate, the Thread Cutters are deistic and acknowledge the difference between them, but they also organize them in clusters attached in purpose to one or the three Fates. The text you're quoting is talking about their symbolic associations for casting magic, not about being in three different legacies. Note that each of those clusters is 4 arcana which is at least one arcana too many to start basing attainments on.
