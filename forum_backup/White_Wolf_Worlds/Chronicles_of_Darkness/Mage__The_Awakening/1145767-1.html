            Looks good. I only found a few things.
            1.) The casting time seems to be one turn shorter than it should be when using more than one Yantra.
            2.) Mudras are limited to +5. Now, my group has house ruled that limit a bit so I'm totally fine with the Mudra granting more than +5, but you could put up a warning.
            3.) The Mudra dice bonus doesn't update when changing the Skill rating. This is a bit annoying since changing to a Rote automatically adds Mudra as a +1 Yantra.
            4.) There's no option to specify that the Rote was created by the caster and therefor grant the Rote Action quality to the roll.
            5.) There's no option to cast from Grimoires. When casting from Grimoires you can't use Mudra as a Yantra and you can't change Casting Time from Standard (ritual) to Advanced (Instant). And the spell gains the Rote Action quality.
            6.) Certain boons (especially Boons from Fate) can grant 9 Again, 8 Again and/or Rote Action to spell casting rolls, but the only bonus option is adding additional dice.

                                                            Originally posted by aceDiamond View Post
                                                            Lastly, I was curious if there could be something to play around with casting time with the Adamant Hand merit, which would allow the Order tool to be used reflexively and not necessarily take up the action for that turn. Also, the rulebook says that runes take a ritual time to draw, but once it's drawn/tattooed/etc, should that still take the full ritual time?
                                                            Adamant Hand is only Reflexive on the turn when the spell is cast, otherwise the action being used as a Yantra is Instant as per normal. So the going example is if you use Brawl 3 times in 3 turns you get a Reflexive Yantra of +3 that you can apply to a spell, or you could use Brawl 3 times and on your third turn also cast a Reflexive speed spell on that turn with a +3. Basically, Adamant Hand is a little fidgety, but overall works.

                                                            As for the Runes, yeah, once a Rune is in place it should be possible to cast the same spell with its benefits without too much trouble. For instance if you tattoo Runes for Knit on a cabal-mate then you should be able to take advantage of those Runes whenever you cast Knit on that cabal-mate, you can even use them as the Reflexive Yantra you're entitled to on the first turn spent on casting. From my reading each set of Runes should be unique to a particular spell though. Bright side, some forward thinking with strategic placement of Runes can give you a real edge in certain circumstances.

                                                            As an aside. Invisible Runes should, imo, be considered a way to bypass the time requirement for carving Runes (Well within Prime's purview for messing with Yantras as far as I'm concerned), but you need to have Mage Sight active to take advantage of it when casting a spell that incorporates the Rune Yantra. It's a decent benefit, with the potential downside of making it harder to take more mundane actions while you're using it because MS is up.
