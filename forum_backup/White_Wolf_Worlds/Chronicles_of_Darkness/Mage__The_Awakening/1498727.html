    The Glasaugen - Mastigos Forces / Space Homebrew legacy

    12-03-2022, 11:17 PM
    Glasaugen
    Spies, Big Brothers (Derogatory)


    The Cold War has a reputation for espionage and surveillance and this is due to the high prevalence of espionage and surveillance. The Wise were not immune to the effects of paranoia brought about during this time. One such effect was the Glasaugen (“Glass Eyes” in English), a legacy with the intent to master the surveillance states that were emerging. Both the Guardians of the Veil and the Panopticon Ministry practice this legacy and both claim the other stole it from them. All that is known for sure is that the legacy originated in Germany, if the founding mage was a Soviet or a member of the Allies is unknown but there was no shortage of mages on both sides who joined. Until the fall of the Soviet Union the Glasaugen fought its own cold war within its ranks, as both the Seers and Guardians tried to eradicate the members on the other side, and also the Allied and Soviet mages did the same. While the national conflict ended in 1992, the war between the awakened continues. Assassinations are not as common nowadays, but both sides are keeping an eye on the other.


    The Glasaugen see the enlargement, use, and maintenance of a surveillance state as a holy endeavor and in this endeavor they have won. Everyone should be watched or, if it is not possible to actually watch them, they should be made to believe they are being watched. Those who believe their every move is being observed are less likely to act outside of societal norms and therefore, less likely to awaken. Those spies within the Guardians are more likely to emphasize that those who are watched are more easily to maneuver into the labyrinths if they are found unworthy, but this philosophy has led to tensions with the Free Council, who see them as a branch of the Panopticon Ministry that have infiltrated the Pentacle. Of course the Guardians deny this, but stories of members of this legacy defecting to the servants of the Eye are much more common than the reverse.


    It is not just the surveillance state that is a matter of interest to the Glass Eyes, they also study the mysteries of parasocial sympathy. So much of their work involves observing and learning about others who have no clue they exist. This still results in sympathetic connections, but they only go one way. These sympathies are considered sacred by the legacy, and if someone they are surveying ever needs to be killed the Glasaugen with the connection to them will never be the one required to perform the assassination.


    Background: Many of the Glass Eyes come from intelligence agencies, with many more once being private investigators. Some are paranoid of "big brother" watching them and later discover that they have become what they feared.


    Appearance: The Glasaugen dress inconspicuously, appropriate for whatever environment they find themselves in.


    Organization: Like any intelligence agency, the Glass Eyes are organized from the top down, with those above giving orders to those below. Promotions within the legacy also come with the teaching of new attainments.

    Theory: Magic is holy when only the one performing the magic can see it. When the person who is being affected by magic has no clue the magic is happening, that is divinity. It is not divine in the sense that it is next to god, inspired by god, or a reflection of divinity. It is divinity within the fallen world. Magic, to The Glasaugen, is an expression of divine knowledge. Not abstract or philosophical knowledge, but useful, practical knowledge about a someone or something. You study a person, place, or thing (from the shadows) in order to build up a parasocial sympathetic connection to them so that you can perform magic, but the construction of that sort of sympathy is also magic. ​


    Doctrine and Magic

    Parent Path: Mastigos
    Parent Order: Guardians of the Veil or the Panopticon Ministry
    Prerequisites: Forces 2, Space 1, 2 dots in Subterfuge, one of the following additional skills at 2 dots or higher: Computers, Investigation, Larceny, Stealth
    Initiation: Gather novel information on a target assigned by your mentor. The information needs to be novel to the initiate, not the mentor, and must be gathered without using magic.
    Ruling Arcana: Forces
    Yantras: Casting magic remotely through surveillance equipment (+1); Casting magic on someone you have investigated thoroughly (+2); If the mage successfully lied or hid information during the scene, or saw through a lie or found hidden information during the scene (+1-3 depending on significance of the lie or information); If the mage knows a secret about the target that the target is unaware that the mage knows (+1-3 depending on significance).
    Oblations: Shadow a target to learn information on them; Spend time watching security footage, live or recorded; Convince someone to act in line with society because they are being watched by some shadowy force.

    Attainments

    First Attainment: Information Everywhere

    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The modern era is filled with radio waves, cell phones providing data, and people with iWatches on their wrists. While sleepers cannot interact with all this free floating data, the Glasaugen learn to be aware of it constantly. After an hour of meditation the mage is able to see data in the air. This attainment functions as “Tune In” (Forces 1, MtAw 141) with reach for advanced duration.

    Optonal: Space 1
    Data is all around the mage, and so are the potential enemies shadowing her. The mage must learn to always see everything happening around them. This attainment activates with the same hour of mediation and functions as “The Outward and Inward Eye” (Space 1, MtAw 174) with reach for advanced duration.

    Second Attainment: Unseen by the Eyes of Others

    Prerequisites: Forces 2, 3 dots in Subterfuge
    A spy must learn how to move through the world without being seen. This attainment allows the mage to do just this, after an hour of meditation the attainment acts as “Invisibility” (Forces 2, MtAw 143) with reach for advanced duration.

    Optional: Space 2
    Spies need to be able to get into places they aren’t welcome and they learn early on how to. This attainment functions as “Break Boundary” (Space 2, MtAw 174) with reach assigned to instant casting.

    Third Attainment: Data Mining

    Prerequisites: Forces 3, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 3 or a second option from the list must be raised to 2
    At this point in a spies' understanding they are now able to transmit the data they are seeing, preventing others from seeing what they don’t want them to see, or making sure that anything they want to be seen is seen. This attainment functions as “Transmission” (Forces 2, MtAw 144) with reach for instant casting and advanced duration.

    Optional: Space 3
    Spies need to know as much about a target they are watching as possible, including where to find them and how to blackmail them if needed. This attainment helps with that by emulating “Correspondence” (Space 1, MtAw 172) with reach for instant casting, sensory range, and to allow the mage to follow the trail of sympathetic links. While this does not give the spy the information they need per say, it does give them information on where to find it.

    Fourth Attainment: Sight from Afar

    Prerequisites: Forces 4, 4 dots in Subterfuge
    The spy needs to be able to observe from afar, away from other mages who might be able to see through their disguises or magics. It is at this point in the training of the mage that they learn how to do so. This attainment functions as “Zoom In” (Forces 2, MtAw 144) with reach for advanced duration and instant casting, along with the ability to zoom into the molecular level. This point in their training usually involves the study of science so that the mage can recognize poisons and other dangerous molecules.

    Optional: Space 4
    Due to the conflict, both national and awakened, within the history of the legacy, and the legacies affinity to uncover sympathetic links, the spies developed a technique to hide their sympathetic links. This attainment functions as “Veil Sympathy” (Space 2, MtAw 175) with reach for advanced durations and and changing the primary factor to potency, and the bonus effect to hide all of their sympathetic links.

    Fifth Attainment: From Dust to Ash

    Prerequisites: Forces 5, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 4 or a second option from the list must be raised to 3 or a third option must be raised to 2
    The final attainment allows the spies to kill those they are required to with a touch, vaporizing them. The attainment is withstood by Stamina, and is not a dramatic end. The person is simply vaporized on a cellular level, leaving nothing behind but a pile of ash to be blown away in the wind.

    Optional: Space 5
    Throughout their career spies create and discover many sympathetic links, this attainment allows them to capitalize on these links. With an hour of work the attainment functions as “Co-Location” (Space 3, MtAw 176) with reach for advanced duration, with reach to create a stable portal between the two locations for the duration of the attainment, and with the ability to specify a Key needed to use the overlapped space (usually the key is themselves or something personal to them so rival spies cannot use their portal network).​

                      Minor question about the Space Apprentice Attainment; it's not at Sight Range, so do you imagine they're usually touching the target, or Aiming at them? Both make for interesting visuals.

                      If you don't mind suggestions on the existing ones, I'd actually move Scrying up to Adept so it can use Sympathetic Range natively rather than needing to spend Mana. You could replace it in the Disciple slot with Veil Sympathy (Reach to Advanced Duration, Potency to Primary), which would make sense as a protective measure. Like Satchel said, it makes sense that a Mastigos Legacy could sustainably have both Seer and Pentacle houses.

                      On the other hand, your Space Attainments seem to be more about observation, while the Forces ones are more generalized. Locate Object might be useful as a Disciple or higher. Instantly cause a touched subject to be trackable for an advanced duration, even if it leaves your presence.

                      As TempleBuilder said, theme will matter for the Adept and Master Attainments. Again, the trend I'm reading in the Space side is enhancing the users observational power, so much as I'd like to recommend Advanced Duration Force No Chains in the Master slot, it does deviate from the pattern.

                      Web Weaver (Especially if you allow the Time 2 expansion) would be a very useful at Advanced Duration, Instant Casting, and Potency Switch. You could get away with dropping Instant Casting (or Advanced Duration, if you don't mind reactivating it every few seconds), but it ultimately becomes a way to temporarily create really strong links to utilize while still being able to drop them at a moments notice.

                      For the Forces side, a hologram duplicate would be a neat distraction power. Thats... Weaving, probably? Put it in at least Adept for Instant Casting and Advanced Duration. I'd actually say that you could modify Invisibility in the Apprentice Attainment to behave as a Forces version of Machine Invisibility that protects you from all visual-surveilance tech but not living sentinels, then put actual Forces Invisibility into the Adept slot. Put a hologram-generation power into the Master slot, then you'd be able to generate a duplicate to cover your sudden disappearance.

                      Or possibly, swap Transmission and Invisibility. Transmission is still viable with one less Reach, while Invisibility works better (in my opinion) if it's Instant-Cast. Drop a hologram-dulpicate into the Adept slot then.

                      Rapid Access Memory would make for an interesting capstone, although the Prime requirement doesn't really fit with the rest of the Legacy.

                      Sorry for the ramble, design is fun. The Legacy looks good so far!

                          Fourth Attainment could be Zoom In, with Reach assigned to advanced duration, allowing them to see for miles and perceive dust sized particles. Now they can have their secret base protected from mundane detection, the area hiding their tools or incriminating evidence will not be found, nor will the locker they placed the dead drop in, unless its its someone in the know.

                          The capstone could be Control Weather with instant use, advanced duration and drastic changes. With conjunctional Space 4, they can spend a mana to active Everywhere. Now the mage can have a meeting with a contact while heavy winds make it difficult to listen on their conversation. They can isolate enemies with a Blizzard, cause chaos in their base by sneaking in invisibly and creating an Inferno or destroy their important documents by having the building Flooded. All can be seen as freak accidents that do not betray their presence.

                          The optional capstone could be Co-Location, creating a portal. Combined with Secret Room, this means they can have a vast secret network of infiltration. Reach is assigned to advanced duration, sensory range and creating a portal, with a mana spent on sympathetic range.
