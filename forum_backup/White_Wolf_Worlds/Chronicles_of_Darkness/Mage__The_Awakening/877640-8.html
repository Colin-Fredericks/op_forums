            Stabilize (Life •)
            Practice: compelling
            Primary Factor: Potency
            Suggested Rote skills: Medicine, Athletics, Survival

            If a character is gravely wounded they might undergo surgery under sleeper care to stabilize (If bleeding out) Or simply help them recover. The roll is an extended Medicine + Intelligence roll once per hour with successes equal to boxes of damage (See WoD Pg96) Potency of this spell counts as successes already gained on this roll greatly increasing the chances of such an action succeeding within the time limit.
            It's subtle enough to not be noticed even under direct scrutiny of sleepers, Rates of bleeding from major arteries slow but don't heal, organs continue to function with diminished blood where they might otherwise shut-down.

            A weird one but a character I play did actually use this one time when an ally was begin operated on by a sleeper doctor. The doctor didn't notice the "Extra" nurse who silently slinked in sat in the corner of the theater. Not stealing his thunder, just giving him a helping hand.

                                    Practice: Weaving
                                    Primary Factor: Duration

                                        So again a few ideas:

                                        Never lost (Space*)
                                        Practice: Knowing
                                        Key factor: Duration
                                        A mage with basic understanding of Space is never lost. This spell allows the subject to instinctively locate known places. He can determine the direction in which he'll find any one place per dot of Potency he has visited. Alternatively for one dot of Potency he can know which way is north.
                                        +1 Reach: The subject can determine his general location ( "Jakarta", "Saharan desert", "the Amazonian Jungle")
                                        +2 Reach: The subject knows his specific locations ("apartment 5, baker street 29, Misteryville")

                                        Surestep (Forces **)
                                        Practice: Rulling
                                        Keyfactor: Duration
                                        The mage manipulates gravity and friction is such way that allows the subject to walk on any surface able to support his weight be it slippery ice or a rope stretched between two buildings. Ignore any relevant tilts or conditions
                                        +2 Reach: The subject can also freely walk on vertical surfaces.

                                        Illusion (Forces ****) [could be Forces ****** and making]
                                        Practice: Patterning
                                        Keyfactor: Duration
                                        The mage manipulates light to create an mirage. The spells Scale factor determines the size of the mirage, while the mage can create one illusion per dot of potency. It is fully sight dependant and static unless the actively spends a turn concentrating and controlling it,
                                        +1 Reach: The illusion also has audible effects
                                        +1 Reach: The mage can program the illusion to change and move in a predermined way.
                                        +2 Reach, Mind ***" Not only is the illusion mobile but it is capable of reacting to what's happening around it in a basic way the same way something it represents would react For instance an illusion of a person might bleed when shot or an illusion of a cat might flee is someone aproaches.

                                                Warden of Life (Life ••)
                                                Practice: Shielding
                                                Primary Factor: Duration
                                                Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Brawl, Expression

                                                The body isn’t just meat and bones; It is a vessel for the soul. With this spell a mage can protect the subject’s living pattern, or the pattern of living beings in the area, from being tampered with by the Life Arcana. Any attempt to alter the subject’s living pattern must succeed at a Clash of Wills.

                                                +1 Reach: Magic and powers from other supernatural beings that would alter the living pattern of the subject are likewise affected.
                                                +1 Reach: The mage may specify a Key that allows the subjects pattern to be altered.
                                                +2 Reach (Edited): The protection afforded by this spell extends to physical attacks from living beings, where the attacker suffers the potency of the spell as a penalty. This protection doesn't work if inanimate objects are used, like swords, clubs, ect.

                                                Note: Not sure about the Suggested Rote Skills.

                                                            Phasing (Space ••••)
                                                            Practice: Patterning
                                                            Primary Factor: Duration
                                                            Suggested Rote Skills: Science, Brawl, Stealth

                                                            You are here; but at the same time, you aren't. By carefully manipulating space your body occupies, you are protected against all physical attacks - they just pass through your body. People are still able to see, hear and smell you; mental and magical attacks work as normal. Liquids and gases don't affect you, but you are still able to be blinded, deafened and so on.

                                                            +1 Reach: By passing the light through the body to the other side, you are invisible as well.
                                                            +1 Reach: You can manipulate objects, carry items and physically attack your enemies.
                                                            +1 Reach: Aimed spells won't work on you - enemy needs to attack your Pattern directly to harm you.
