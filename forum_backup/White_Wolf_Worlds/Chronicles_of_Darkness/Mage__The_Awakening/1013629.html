    Rote Factors in 2nd Edition

    12-07-2016, 04:00 AM
    I was thinking of adding Rote factors into 2e but applying it into Reach instead and here's what I've got:

    Bound Rotes: Bound Rotes are rotes that have incorporated certain symbolism to their Imago, making it easier for a Mage to reach beyond their ability if they use it's encoded Yantra. A Bound Rote's Yantra can be however difficult a Master wants them to be, indeed many Adamant Sages have used the Adamant Hand technique into their Rotes specifically so that it's power can only be fully used by their order.

    Systems: A Bound Rote has an optional effect which gives you free Reach but only to a certain option and if you use the Yantra that is required for the Reach. There is technically speaking no limits on how many Yantras 'bound' to a specific Rote but it requires 3 extra success make a Bound Rote, giving it a Yantra and it's associated Reach, and another 2 success to for each Yantra after that. This can only be done when the Rote is being created, a Rote cannot be turned into a Bound Rote even by the master who created it.

    Each Yantra used in the Rote can only 'give' 1 free Reach, an effect that requires 2 Reach must use two different but related yantras(Attacking a second leg after successfully hitting the other one for example) and anything that requires Mana must still be payed. Yantra's still give their associated bonus dice or effect(such as using Temporal Sympathy)

    Thoughts?

                  It feels like this needs some sort of drawback on the side of people learning the Rote in question. The extra successes needed to encode the Rote is reasonable, but fairly trivial for a Master to accomplish (They're probably going to have a dice-pool of 10, which means up to 10 rolls, and that's more than enough to stack on a lot of extra success for this unless the spell uses a number of different Arcana). Once they're made though... there's pretty much no downside to them.

                  Why would you ever get a normal Rote instead of a Bound Rote if you have the chance? The Bound Rote gives you everything the normal Rote does, but you have the chance to get some free bonus Reach out of it if the conditions are right (and there's certain Reach options that are pretty much always going to be useful for a given spell).

                  This also feels like it would make Masters+ obnoxiously powerful. They get to create their own Rotes to get five free Reach on master level spells... which seems like it's going to really not mesh well. A lot of the bonus Reach options at that level are aimed at either constraining using those options for ritual casting, or forcing overreach if you want to use them quickly. With these rules a master of Prime can make permanent Hallows in a few seconds.
