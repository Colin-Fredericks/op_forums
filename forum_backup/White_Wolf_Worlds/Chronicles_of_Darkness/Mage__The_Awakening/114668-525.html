                        Originally posted by Cauthon View Post
                        Geists are effectively spirit-ghost magaths, aren't they?
                        They're ghosts who functionally treat a type of deathly Resonance as a much less limited form of Anchor, which, combined with their immunity to Essence bleed, allows them much more mobility than most ghosts, but they only interact with spirits insofar as they have anything to do with death. Ostracism from the choirs for being an abomination and ostracism by other ghosts for being a terrifying and incomprehensible specter are separate types of solitary, and the former doesn't enter into ghostly existence.

                        You could certainly try some sort of long-term project to make something that inhabits an interstitial frequency of Twilight between Shadow ephemera and Astral ideaform, but established lore alone is just going to get you ways to give spirit qualities to ghosts or ghost qualities to goetia rather than declare that this thing is three wildly different things at the same time without thinking about what that means — remember that forming an Imago entails coming up with a mental representation of the spell's end result.

A simple Perfecting spell will double an object's velocity for every level of Potency, so start off with something fast enough and add enough dice to a pool dedicated to just one factor...
