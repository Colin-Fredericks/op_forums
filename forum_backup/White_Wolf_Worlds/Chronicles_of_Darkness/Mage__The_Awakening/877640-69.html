                                            Ex Nihilo has a clause that explicitly forbids things that do not "operate according to known physical principles", which I'd presume applies to other applications of Matter. It's a little unclear to me what the in-universe justification for that is, but since a mage doesn't have to know anything about cell phones except that cell phones exist to make one it's not simply a matter of learning how a quantum computer would work, were one to exist.

                                            You could definitely create a magical better-than-quantum computer with some additional Arcana, though. Which ones depend on what you want to be using it for. Fate for complicated probability models, Time for crunching huge amounts of numbers very quickly, Life for advanced biological simulations, Space for calculating relativistic distortions etc. etc.

                                                        So, this a list of spells that don't really have much of a unifying theme. More of a bag tricks really.

                                                        Protect the Balance (Spirit •)

                                                        Practice: Unveiling
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Science, Animal Ken

                                                        With this spell the user can see the interaction between flesh and shadow and thereby the natural order of things, or at least what is natural for a given locale, and anything that would disturb it.

                                                        This allows the subject to see the Resonance of an area, and so figure out what spirits would be attracted to it.

                                                        The subject can now tell which Choirs are the most Dominant in the area

                                                        +1 Reach: The subject’s ability to sense Resonance is enhanced. The subject can now detect which Descants are the most Dominant in the area
                                                        +1 Reach: The subject’s senses are now particularly attuned to the Resonance of human sin. This allows the subject to know if any Immundi inhabit the area.

                                                        Cult Leader (Prime ••)

                                                        Practice: Ruling
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Persuasion, Intimidation

                                                        This spell causes the very air around the subject to be filled with what sparse mystical and magical potential is still present in the Fallen World, a reflection of the Aether itself.
                                                        As a result people feel as thought they are in the presence of some sort of great authority, some might even mistakenly believe the subject is an angel or some other form of divine agent.

                                                        In Social Maneuvering this removes Doors equal to Potency.

                                                        Secret Identity (Space ••)

                                                        Practice: Veiling
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Suggested Rote Skills: Investigation, Politics, Subterfuge

                                                        This spell enhances the Shadow Name Merit's ability to hide one's mundane identity. Any investigation to uncover the subject's mundane identity that uses evidence created while in their Shadow persona automatically fails.

                                                        Any attempt to use any Sympathy or Temporal Sympathy created while the subject is in their Shadow persona to identify their mundane persona provokes a Clash of Wills.

                                                        This spell has no effect on those that already know that both the subject's Shadow and mundane persona are one person.

                                                        +1 Reach: Rather than a Clash of Wills any Sympathy or Temporal Sympathy created in the subject's Shadow persona cannot be used at all while in their mundane persona.
                                                        Any attempt to do so will only tell the scrying mage that the target 'does not exist'.
                                                        +1 Reach: The spell can now affect any mage with a Shadow name regardless of whether they have the Shadow Name Merit or not.
                                                        +2 Reach: The spell can now affect anybody with the Alternate Identity Merit.

                                                        Strongman (Forces •••)

                                                        Practice: Perfecting
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Suggested Rote Skills: Science, Athletics, Brawl

                                                        The mage may improve the strength of the kinetic forces used by the subject's body. This improves the subject’s Strength by Potency. This increase affects any Advantages or other traits derived from the Attribute’s level. The effects are subtle, though observers can detect subtle hints of changes to balance or strength of the body. Strength cannot be raised above the subject’s maximum (5 for normal human beings).

                                                        +1 Reach: By spending a point of Mana, the mage may raise Strength above the maximum rating for the subject.

                                                        Power Glows (Prime •••)

                                                        Practice: Weaving
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Occult, Expression

                                                        Mana is normally invisible and intangible. Under Mage Sight it may appear as a fiery, fluid energy of pure white or a prismatic color. With this spell the mage condenses the Mana in his pattern into a more concentrated form.
                                                        The benefit of this is that the mage's pattern can contain more of this condensed Mana than regular Mana, increasing the mage's Mana capacity by Potency.

                                                        However, the drawback to this is that the condensed Mana is visible to all and causes the mage's body to emit a faint light. At high Potency the mage's veins may even start to glow up and light may emit from the eyes and throat.

                                                        If the spell expires, any Mana beyond the mage's capacity is lost.

                                                        Add Any Other Arcanum •: By using the relevant Arcanum/Arcana the mage may now condense the mystical energy (Essence, Vitae, etc) of other supernatural entities as well.
                                                        This will carry the same drawback of making that energy visible in some way for all to see however.

                                                        Anima (Spirit •••)

                                                        Practice: Perfecting
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Suggested Rote Skills: Survival, Empathy, Expression

                                                        There is a symbiotic relationship between the Shadow and Material world, for example, if there is a Barren in the Shadow than on the physical side plants will wither and people will feel passionless and apathetic.
                                                        With this spell the mage improves the beneficial effects of this relationship for the subject. Plants and animals will have improved health and people will feel more alive and creative.

                                                        If the subject is human, and already has one dot in Expression, then increase Expression by Potency.

                                                        +1 Reach: By spending a point of Mana, the mage may raise Expression above the maximum rating for the subject.
                                                        +1 Reach: If the other side of the Gauntlet is a Barren then this spell will negate any penalties to spiritual powers that it would normally generate for the subject.

                                                        Spiritual Symbiosis (Spirit •••)

                                                        Practice: Weaving
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Intimidation, Persuasion
                                                        Withstand: Rank

                                                        The mage must cast this spell on a Claimed subject. This spell causes the spirit's and host’s mind to clash against one another rather than merge as per normal, the result is that one will dominate.

                                                        All the normal effects of the Claimed Condition (Changed appearance, can use Influences not Numina, Dread Powers, susceptible to Ban and Bane, etc) still apply.

                                                        The spirit may now try to take complete control over the merged subject.
                                                        To resist, the subject has to roll Resolve + Composure penalized by the amount of Attribute dots the Claimed Condition has granted.
                                                        If the roll fails the spirit takes control for the rest of the scene but cannot choose to end the Claimed Condition if the spell is still in effect.
                                                        If the roll succeeds the subject remains in control for at least one scene and is no longer affected by the Urged Condition, though if the user tries to ignore the spirit's Ban that provokes another roll immediately.

                                                        A particularly unwilling and uncowed spirit may force a roll every single scene, whereas a weaker and more intimidated spirit may only force a roll once a day or less.

                                                        +2 Reach: The spirit may no longer try to take control over the subject. But only so long as the amount of distributed Attribute Dots does not surpass Potency. Ignoring the Spirit's Ban still provokes a roll however.
                                                        +1 Reach: The number of gained Attribute dots no longer increases past Potency. Combined with the previous Reach option this means the subject no longer needs to fear losing control over time.

                                                        Gestalt form (Life ••••)

                                                        Practice: Patterning
                                                        Primary Factor: Duration
                                                        Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Occult, Science
                                                        Withstand: Stamina

                                                        This spell allows two or more living being to fuse into one, creating a fused life form. The minds of the subjects fuse together forming a new merged personality with access to all the memories, knowledge and Skills of the subjects. The mage may determine which Attributes and appropriate Merits the being inherits. No Attribute can be higher than the highest level of that Attribute found among the subjects.

                                                        If the subjects are all of the same supernatural template, then this template remains preserved after the fusion. The mage must determine what abilities the fused being has, again, no ability can be higher that the highest among the subjects. If the subjects are of differing templates then the mage determines which one template the fused being becomes, only the abilities specific to that template can be inherited.
                                                        A mage can only have so many spells active, when fusing mages determine which spells will be under the new entities spell control.

                                                        Once the Duration ends the subjects return to normal and any damage sustained is split equally among all subjects regardless of how much damage they had before. Willpower values are unchanged from their pre-merger values.
                                                        Memories of the time spent fused will seem distant and hazy, dreamlike, but the subjects will remember the experience to some degree.

                                                        Add Mind 4: The mage may determine to which of the subjects' memories, knowledge and Skills the fused being has access to.

                                                        Update:
                                                        Added Reach options to 'Secret Identity'
                                                        Rewrote both 'Protect the Balance' and 'Spiritual Symbiosis'
