                                                            While the principle is sound I don't think I'd allow a mote of "not being socially awkward" to be just floating around a person at any given time. Emotional spirits aren't common, and even when they are there they're more likely to be anchored to something other than a person. In the Book of Spirits there's a mote of happiness but it doesn't follow around the girl that generated it, instead it stays in her room where her happiness is generated and waits for her to come home to prey on her. I would use Coaxing the Spirit from 1e to give an equipment bonus to a piece of equipment the Mage is using to try to not be socially awkward. For instance showing up with a bottle of wine that's been boosted with Coaxing the Spirit to make a good impression at a house party. You can generate essentially the same effect, but have to put more thought into your props. I'd run it that way for 2e as well.

                                                            I still disagree with OP that Spirit is only good for talking to spirits, even in 1e that's clearly not the case.
