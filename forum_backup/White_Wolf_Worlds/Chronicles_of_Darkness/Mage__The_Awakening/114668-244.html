                                                        Hmm, Warding Gesture. Here's how I understood the spell working the first time I saw it.

                                                        Let's say I'm a magical arsonist who plans to set a movie theater on fire to make a point. I'm planning to cast a big-ass Forces spell on the entire building, as an instantaneous Area effect. But my Sleeper friend may or may not visit the theater, so I cast Warding Gesture on him. Now, if I cast my Forces spell on the theater, my friend will miraculously emerge unharmed from the fire.

                                                        Now my plan changes, and I don't set the entire building on fire. Instead I station myself across the street, and plan to hurl a Forces spell that causes spontaneous combustion for its Duration at anyone who comes out of the door, as a Target effect. To protect my Sleeper friend whom I may not recognize quickly enough with all the ensuing chaos, I cast the Reach effect of Warding Gesture on him, with enough Duration to outlast the Duration of the Forces spell. Now, even if I accidentally target my friend with spontaneous combustion, I can suppress my Forces spell's Duration long enough for my friend to get the hell out of there.
