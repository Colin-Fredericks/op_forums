        Originally posted by WHW View Post
        A full fledged Mystery Cult Sheet would probably have something like...

        1. It's name.
        2. Space for five steps of initiation, each of which needs space for describing what kind of merit it provides (example: "All initiates learn to cut through red tape to fulfill their later duties. They gain a Politics Specialty in Bureaucracy.", but also "The highest-ranking Brothers and Sisters were mostly first contact survivors. They’ve seen more of
        the God-Machine’s influence than most anyone still standing. They gain a modified version of the Encyclopedic Knowledge Merit relating directly to the God-Machine.")
        3. Space for name of it's controller, and for name of it's secret controller (example: "High Priest Joahim (Silver Ladder Cryptopoly of William Blacktone)"
        4. Space for it's PURPOSE (example: "Mammon believes in the almighty dollar, and its inherent power. Followers of Mammon are expected to
        obtain material wealth and power at any cost. Fortunately, their networks help initiates quickly claim promotions and transfers into more prominent areas of influence.")
        5. Space for it's RELIC (similar to above)
        6. Space for it's DOCTRINE (similar to above)
        7. Space for listing members
        8. Space for stuff like location, meetings, schedules, etc
        9. Space for enemies, rivals, allies
        Oh, cool. Thanks! I'll add that to my list. If I any questions once I start, I'll send em your way.
