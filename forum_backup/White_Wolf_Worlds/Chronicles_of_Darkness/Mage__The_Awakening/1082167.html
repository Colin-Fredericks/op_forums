    Big title - lots of little questions: I'm new to Mage, and there are some details that I need some assistance on that I may have missed in the books so clarification would nice :

    So question - with Augment Mind spell for example:

    The mage is capable of increasing the subject’s mental or
    social capacity. The mage can increase one of either the subject’s
    Mental or Social Attributes by one dot per level of Potency of
    the spell. This increase affects any Advantages or other traits
    derived from the Attribute’s level. The spell cannot increase the
    subject’s Attribute above her normal maximum allowed by her
    Gnosis. The benefits of this spell are not obvious to a casual
    observer, but those who know the subject may notice an increase
    in her intellect or charismatic nature.
    +1 Reach The mage may increase an additional Attribute with
    the spell for each additional Reach, dividing the spell’s Potency
    among them. For example, the mage may spend +2 Reach with
    a Potency 4 spell and increase the subject’s Intelligence by +1,
    Resolve by +2, and Wits by +1.
    + 2 Reach By spending a point of Mana, the mage my increase
    her subject’s Mental or Social Attributes above the normal
    maximum allowed.

Could my Unchained make Pacts with the Mage for free attribute dots (like a pact of intelligence for an intelligence dot? ) - I'm fairly new to Mage and obviously I may be mistaken on things, so clarification is always nice.           You increase subjects mental capacity by taking a -2 dice penalty on the casting roll for each dot you want to improve. You get a base potency equal to your mind arcanum -1. You also need to take a -2 penalty per duration step you want (and 1 reach to make it advanced duration). Note your only going to get 1 free reach unless you have this spell as a rote in which case you will have 3 free reaches and only incur paradox dice on the 4th reach.

          If you have mind 3 then your base spell has a potency of 3 for each -2 on the casting roll you get +1 potency spell factor. So for +6 (3 base + 3) intelligence you need to take a -6 penalty & use +2 reach to go over max. You then need to spend another -10 penalty and another +1 reach for a indefinate duration (not permanent) advanced duration factor.

          Your base dicepool with gnosis 1 will be 4, you can add 1 yantra (say +2 from highspeech), additional +1 per ritual casting time to a max of 5 (instant requires another reach) and willpower for a total dicepool of 14 - 16 = -2 (you cant cast a spell if the penalties after bonus takes it lower than -5) so you have a chance die and the spell will take a base of 3 hours + 3x5 additional hours so 18 total hours to cast. If not cast as a rote your 2 reach over your free reaches, if a rote your ok for paradox (unless seen by sleepers etc).

          Also remember mages have a limited number of spells they can control and will probably need to relinquish them, but theres nothing stopping them from buffing the entire group either, they can take some reach more penalties and increase the number of targets. So the above spell could give 2 subjects +6 intelligence and be at a total dice pool of -4 which is still a chance dice but possible.

          Id need to read up on demon pacts but remember making pacts with mages is probably as dangerous as making pacts with a demon, fate can allow them to ignore, change and do nasty things to oaths etc.

          So theres nothing stopping a friendly mage buffing the group except spell control, paradox mitigation, willpower recovery for relinquished spells etc etc.

                          Originally posted by Volke View Post
                          what do you mean about the willpower investment?
                          Making a spell not count towards your Gnosis limit of controlled spells without causing the effect to degrade over time requires that the caster spend a Willpower dot, which costs experience to repurchase. Until then, that spell counts toward your spell control and effectively reduces the number of spells you can have active at the same time by one until you end the spell, which makes it harder to cast other spells without risking Paradox.

                          Also does Reach/Mana per spell have a limit?
                          Reach doesn't have a hard usage limit (though the dangers of Paradox are present), but Mana can only be spent on instant-cast spells as fast as your per-turn limit allows you to and you can't spend Mana you don't have.

                          And permanent & Until Dispelled are different I assume in that permanent can't be dispelled
                          Permanent would be in the vein of actually buying the new Trait dots with experience; spells mostly don't accomplish that on their own and so can be undone by other mages with a relatively simple spell.

                                                  Of course, the Vampire himself is considered a Sleepwalker for banner bearer and could carry the spell himself.

                                                  As a practical matter, a starting mage with an Augment the Mind rote can grant + 3 Intelligence & + 2 Resolve for 5 individuals Indefinitely.

                                                  It would be Gnosis 1 + Mind 3 + 2 High Speech + 4 Rote Skill + 5 Ritual + 3 Willpower = 18 - 10 (Indefinite) - 4 (Extra Potency for Resolve) = 4 dice.

                                                  It's a 15 hour ritual with a 75% chance of success.

                                                  It requires +1 Reach to enhance two stats + 1 Reach for Indefinite + 1 Reach for Advanced Scale and +1 Reach for Advanced duration = 4 Reach

                                                  This means the paradox pool will be 1 die. Fairly easily contained.

                                                  Personally I would do it for 1 Year and include Wits for +2.
