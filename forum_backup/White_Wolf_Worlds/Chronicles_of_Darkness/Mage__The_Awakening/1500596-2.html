                                                    Originally posted by lbeaumanior View Post
                                                    Amazing as usual Phoog

                                                    Doubts, why doesnt Chron just Shifting Sands away with the knowledge they have? All together could kill the seers in the Atheneum AND fight as a better organised force those that are upstairs.
                                                    There's a few reasons Chron didn't go back:

                                                    -Chron can only go back one or two hours safely. After that he starts eating 2 Paradox die per hour. He's not sure when the Seer operation started, but in order to adequately warn everyone and pull together a defensive force, he's looking at at least 6-8 Paradox die, minimum. Given that NoFace tracks people based on their spell usage, there's a good chance all he'd do is summon NoFace and either get killed or lure it into the Athanaeum. Either of which would ruin whatever plans he might have to foil the Seer attack.

                                                    -At this point, Chron isn't aware of the scale of the attack. There's more Seer and Pentacle mages in the building than we've seen, so far. Going back with incomplete information isn't that helpful. And I can tell you right now - the Pentacle doesn't have many forces to commit to this attack. There's not much more they can contribute to the Athanaeum defence than has already been committed. Also, from Chron's perspective, him and Blackeye have just killed a Seer. They're winning.

                                                    -The Seers also have Time mages. Remember all the Clashes of Wills against Chron's Shield of Chronos? It's no coincidence they decided to attack at the same time as the Last Call Cabal invaded the Athanaeum. If Chron goes back and sets up some defences, the Seers will also have an opportunity to respond - or call off the attack.

                                                    -If Chron goes back and warns the Consilium, they will have some *very* hard questions about what he was doing in the Athanaeum in the first place. Remember that they're invading one of the five Pentacle Order's HQ. Warning them might well paint a big target on Chron's back, and get them executed/excommunicated.

                                                    -Chron has already got what he wanted out of the Athanaeum. He wanted information, and he wanted the deathshead watch. Going back in time, he gets to keep the information he's learnt, but he loses the deathshead watch - and may well never get another chance to steal it, if the Pentacle rallies around defending the Athanaeum. Defending it against Seers - while atruistic - isn't something he's willing to risk all of the above on. As he sees it, NoFace is the true threat. At this point, there's only two Mystagogues left (one, now that Buckminster is dead), so their Order is basically done for in Sydney, and the Athanaeum is lost. It's not something he's going to risk his life for unecessarily.

                                                    And finally, from a metagame perspective we didn't want to rewind back 4 sessions worth of events (and loot), because it'd be kinda tedious to go through again.
