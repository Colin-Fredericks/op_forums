    Tremere timeline

    10-01-2025, 06:42 AM
    I'm wondering about the eldest Tremere possible.

    It seems that before they crashed the Diamond and got themselves recognised as an Order they were likely something of a nameless order for a few hundred years.

    Some time c400 they free a God-monster that becomes their 7th Watchtower. And after that you can have Hollowed Tremere as we know them today (though maybe they are unlikely to have survived this long).

    Were they immortal before that? Vampire Ancient Mysteries talks about Breath stealers, but I'm unclear in whether they were Immortal and whether any survived? Or were the five who were transformed by the 7th Watchtower? Were earlier Tremere mortal awakened monster hunters?

    Could they have been stealing souls before that go conquer death?

      Originally posted by orathaic View Post

      Were they immortal before that?
      I'd say it's not out of the question that some earlier Tremere would have experimented with lichdom as a means of exploring their ideals of supremacy, although perhaps not intrinsically and without argument from some of them. While some specific tenets of common Moros beliefs about the natural progression of death seem liable to be dismissed as lies of the Five Dragons, I could see some extending their philosophy to an idea that mortality imposes a pressure to aggressively pursue imperium.

      Originally posted by orathaic
      Could they have been stealing souls before that go conquer death?
      I mean, Reaping is not exactly a necessary method towards immortality, and even kind of an inefficient one compared to other options. That was always one of the big criticisms of the Tremere as they appeared in the First Edition core; even allowing for an origin that was imposed upon them, the sales pitch seemed pretty weak. That's part of why Left-Hand Path expanded them.

      And I think even with their current write-up, people get a bit too hung up on the immortality, when my read of their tenets make it almost incidental to their concerns or motivations.

      What I would say is that I think there certainly would have been Tremere invested in Reaping before they had those with Hollow. Perhaps not intrinsically; I could see the modern Tremere being a lot more invested in taking souls for the sake of their studies because the way their powers facilitate it and their condition necessitates it selects a lot more for that as the common approach. Whereas I think that before that, you could get more of them who'd want to study it less invasively, even if it is a common Obsession amongst them. Either way, it's still more in the interests of understanding its secrets and why exactly humans are so uniquely positioned to Awaken and Ascend.
