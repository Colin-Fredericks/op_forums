                        I'm totally willing to agree with you that the core puts way too much emphasis on Atlantis, and describes the Orders in a way that probably over-emphasizes the Atlantean caste they claim to draw from. I've found that the descriptions of what the Orders see magic as are much more interesting hooks to what the faction is about, both for myself and new players- not sure if you've seen the blog post about this already, but they are: (source: http://theonyxpath.com/new-order/)
                        • Adamantine Arrow: Challenge is Magical
                        • Free Council: Humanity is Magical
                        • Guardians of the Veil: Magic is Fragile
                        • Mysterium: Magic is Alive
                        • Sliver Ladder: Magic is Humanity’s Birthright
                        • Seers of the Throne: Magic is Payment


                        I think they make a lot more sense in terms of understanding how mages get recruited, as well as in basing your understanding of each Order, rather than their Atlantean class correspondence. A new mage chooses amongst/joins Orders based on which one they feel does the best job explaining this new thing in their life, magic, in a way that speaks to them. Mages (usually) don't join the Arrow because they want to be soldiers, they join the Arrow because they are willing to buy into the idea that "challenge is magical"; that by pushing yourself, by dedicating yourself to a difficult cause, you can gain the power to change the world.

                        Conceptually, for me at least, the rest of the Order flows from that central idea of what magic is. The Arrow believe "Service is Mastery" because they see devoting oneself to another as more challenging and more meaningful than acting solely for one's own sake-- saving the town is a lot harder than saving your own skin. They swear Oaths as a way of challenging themselves to uphold them, to act instead of just hesitating. They volunteer as the primary fighters of mage society because it means lots of opportunities to face challenges. The Silver Ladder comes along and says "you guys represent our symbolic ideal soldier caste in our theoretical utopia!" and the Arrows look up from lifting weights and treating lepers to stick their chins out in contemplation, nod, and say "yeah, alright."
