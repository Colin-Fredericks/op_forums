    Originally posted by WHW View Post
    Step 1: Grab your desired material.
    Step 2: Make it smaller, turning it into Size 0 object using Shrink and Grow
    Step 3: Pass it once in and out of Twilight
    Step 4: Congratulations, permanent perfected material
    Step 5: Dispel Shrink and Grow

    If you feel that's cheesy, then make it Size 1, reduce it's Durability to 1, and pass it 10 times in and out.
    Or just break it into sand sized grains. Each grain is now a size 0 object. Simply pour bags of the stuff through two stacked twilight conversion portals into a bin beneath them.

    You can even automate the process, just enchant a couple of hoops, one converts all physical things passing through it into twilight, the other turns them back again. At this point you can seal these together into a tube, conceal it in a larger machine then have sleeper employees (who don't know what they are really doing) and machines run a factory producing the stuff. Or you could replace the sleepers with golems, I guess, but that would make this require more than two spells/imbuements. Or with sufficient technical aptitude you could use robots in place of sleepers.

    You could make the hoops really long, with many tubes attached funneling things through it and many parallel operations running side by side. You could have say a dozen inputs and outputs (input 1 connects to output 1, input 2 connects to output 2, etc) each with conveyor belts attached.

    This automation method even works if the formula is based on higher of size or 1 instead of size, you just need to keep track of how many times a batch has been put through the machine. And raw materials would be easier to procure as I'm pretty sure large pellets/small ingots of metals are more common than metal that has been reduced to sand sized grains.
