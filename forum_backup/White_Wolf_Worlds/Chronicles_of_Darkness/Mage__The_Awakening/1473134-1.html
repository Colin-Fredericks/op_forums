    Thoughts on Space: Inward and Outward Eye combines really well with Nightvision. Scrying and Sympathetic range open up a number of possibilities (which in turn makes Wards desirable). But Ban is one of my favorite spells. Cast it on yourself to get protection while you prepare shenanigans. Cast it on an enemy to take them out of the fight. Cast it on your friend the rampaging werewolf/vampire to put them in timeout. Cast it on an area to contain hazards. Tis a fun and versatile spell.

    The longer I study science the more I am convinced that it is functionally indistinguishable from what our ancestors would refer to as sorcery. And I would know, being both scientist and sorcerer.

                                        Good luck trying to engage directly with Enchanters. Actually, poor choice of words there, sorry. You're not going to have good luck. With their Ruling Arcana, they'll spot you coming the moment you formulate a plan of attack. They were always just here a few minutes ago, always just around the corner, right in the next room, keep going, you're almost there. They'll make it so you have to go on a quest to gather the like seven crystals of Rem Lezar before you can even think about approaching their Sanctum. Accept that the fight will be under their terms and conditions, that your every move will bring a new flavor of curse, a new rule to follow or forfeit, another oath sworn. ("You didn't read the sign out front that stepping on the blue tile means you agree not to cast Unmaking spells on pain of losing an arm? It was plain as day! Maybe you need to brush up on your Old Burmese. . .") Sometimes the unscrupulous ones will foist a Destiny on some poor sap to fight you in their stead (or even worse, a Doom). Strategy? That's a kind of intent, isn't it? How do you effectively fight someone who can change what you're fighting over mid-battle?

                                        Overwhelming force and unpredictability, most likely. Striking fast and decisively means less risk of getting bogged down in Acanthus bullshit. (Arrow Caucuses may be the only place where the concept of a "preemptive defensive attack" is genuinely justifiable.) In the old tales, it's usually another fairy who manages to undo or at least lighten the curse levied by one of the fae, and having a Witch of your own on your side can work wonders to even the playing field, hexes and boons cancelling each other out in a side-game of prophecy-tag, so long as you understand that you are absolutely being used as a pawn in some greater conflict.

                                                    Just responding to the opening comments on forces.

                                                    A Master of Life can fly, simply Patterning into the body of a bird, better still an Eagle eyed mage can now use sensory range comparable to the forces master. Weaving into her pattern camouflage and natural armour gives stealth and protection. Combined with sonar for echo location and electroception her sensory range can be expanded far beyond human capability; even a blank spot not echoing any sound would be detectable to echo location (if they have merely stopped sound rather than redirecting it around themselves).

                                                    For blunt offensive effects, the Master of Life is limited to living beings, with difficulty directly targeting Golems, Eidolon and possibly Zombies along with Spirits, Ghosts, and Goetia. However so long as their target has a living body they can directly Fray the pattern. Can inflivt poison, blindness, or deafness. She can cause arm racks or immobilisation (very useful for non lethal effects), or remove the target from combat by triggering its flight responce or making it fall asleep (or possibly reduce your dexterity to the point where you can't move?)

                                                    Some of the larger scale effects require a bit more subtlety, Making a new disease which will spread among multiple people (and possibly follow you while you retreat).

                                                    Lastly their ability to Know life patterns gives them vital intelligence when it comes to how much damage is necessary to kill/incapacitate each target. She can prioritse who to attack and with carefully controlled potency not kill targets who need to be taken alive.

                                                    And that is all assuming you want to directly compete with the Forces Master style of magical violence from the opening.

                                                    Another Master of Life might just aswell sneak around as a tiny animal, or fly overhead as a Robin, before shapeshifting into an armoured Bear and landing directly on the target to quickly rip them apart.

                                                        Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post
                                                        I believe the next topic should be Space and it's uses in combat. However, I don't know much about that. So taking a cursory look at Space, looks like it has great awareness of the physical, (Can Space see Twilight?), and Scrying makes sensory range magic quite powerful. It seems Space should be able to bypass armor from both Forces and Matter by having weapons and such phase through the boundaries, though I am uncertain as to what Practice that falls under or if it's a reach effect of Break Boundary. Veiling Location could replicate classic d&d spells like Mirror Image and Blur, though I think that's covered by the Mage Armor. Maybe one can do things like turn a melee attack into a ranged one?
                                                        Space has (at least) one extremely effective combat option, with 2 extra reach, Co-location allows the Master of Space appear reflecively, attack and then disappear reflecively. Basically blinking in and out without giving anyone a chance to respond.

                                                        The spell can be keyed to allow allies with some space do the same while preventing enemies from taking advantage of their spell.

                                                        They can potentially do this from a sanctum, making retreat to a safe (possibly warded) location trivial, while also having easy access to all the resources of that location to use in the fight if needed.

                                                        But even without a sanctum, they can walk up behind someone and (potentially) push their weapons or armour across, effectively Teleporting away their equipment. If the target can see them (requiring they have some space magic, or an extra reach from the Master) she can act as a distraction, impervious to attacks while able to reflecively attack and return to intangiblity.

                                                        The only downside of this Co-location is it is very easy for the enemy to retreat** out of the area it covers (if they know how large it is), forcing the Master to recast it. But allowing her to secure a vital location. Perhaps removing hostages, artifacts, or other mission critical objects

                                                        This one spell makes a Master of Space extremely useful in a fight.

                                                        In a far more subtle sense, she can manipulate the strong sympathetic connections to reduce an enemy group's bonds. Can learn what is important to any given target and threaten it to further undermine group cohesion, or strengthen connections to make each member of a group care about different (perhaps competing) interests.

                                                        Undermining group cohesion before a fight even becomes necessary can grant victory before you even start.

                                                        Lastly, Perfect Sympathy gives a unique ability to redirect spells cast on the Master of Space at sympathic range to a different target (Clash of Wills required) - though the ability to cast lasting destruction of sympathic connections after the fact is already there.

                                                        EDIT: space can NOT see twilight, because it isn't a space (it is a state of being). Though co-locate can make the Master of Space appear intangible (as if they were in twilight), it is a different effect entirel (ie they are completely tangible on their side of the Co-location).

                                                        Scrying requires a sympathic connection, usually, i suppose you can cast a scrying window on something you can sense, and then walk off/teleport home.

                                                        I don't think break boundaries can bypass armour, though it can breach panic rooms and similar.

                                                        Not sure about melee attacks becoming ranged.

                                                        **EDIT2: i should also mention, leaving an area which a Master of Space doesn't want you to leave may not be as easy as i made put above. Between a blunt Ban (which will immediately invoke attempts to dispell) and a more subtle Lying Maps/Veiling exits/alter direction, you can make labyrinthine confusion which is harder to see.

                                                        Or you can go all out and Quarentine the area.

                                                        EDIT3: come to think of it, a small Ban plus co-locating in some mundane fire to burn up all their oxygen will incapacitate most targets (at least those that need to breath). Much easier than actually fighting them.
