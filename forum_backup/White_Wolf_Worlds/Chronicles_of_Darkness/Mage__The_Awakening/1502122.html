    How long does it (on average) take to become a (multi) master?

    02-04-2023, 03:34 PM
    Greetings,


    The template on the Core book, is for a mage with some months of training, passing from 3 Arcana dots to 6, and getting some Order skills (rotes, High Speech, Occult dot, Order Status). On 1st edition becoming a powerful mage was difficult as stats were exponential, but with the changes on second edition experience is linear (even if it is a lot when counting beats).

    On average:
    1. How long does it take for a Mage to become a Master of a Ruling Arcana?
    2. How long does it take for a Mage to become a Second Degree Master of their Ruling Arcana?
    3. Assuming a Legacy, how long to have Third Degree Ruling Arcana mastery?
    4. Assuming a Legacy, how long to reach 4th-5th Degree on the Legacy?
    5. Mechanically, a starting Acanthus has the potential to learn faster from the start: Exceptional Luck Praxis with reasonable Gnosis (3) + Fate (3) + Willpower, can give multiple long lasting conditions that grant beats. In game, does it make sense too to go for lost of beneficial conditions ASAP?
    Thanks for the time and attention,

      Do you mean in lore or in game time?

      Lorewise, it's greatly up to your setting (just like the total number of mages etc.) But my impression based off of the stories of published NPCs is that gaining a dot of Gnosis takes on average 10 years, with more precocious mages (like the Nemean?) averaging closer to 5 years. And others being more like 20.

      Personally I prefer masters to be rare and aloof, with the assumption that most mages never reach mastery in even one arcanum. Just as most professionals would never reach 5 dots in the skill they practice every day for decades.

      Though for PCs it depends entirely on how liberal you are with allowing advancements and the timescale of your stories.

                      It's important to remember you can only get one Beat from resolving conditions per scene. (I'm actually not sure if you can get both a regular and an Arcane Beat from conditions in one scene?)
