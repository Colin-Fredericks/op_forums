                    Signs of Sorcery states under "Reach" that the base Reach of an Imbued Item is set by the creator, always defaulting to the same Advanced Factors and spell Reach options, and says that the item entails Paradox risk if the imbued spell exceeds the user's free Reach. An item creator can provide the option for flexible Reach, but this only allows the item's user to reduce the amount of Reach used by the spell, not increase it.

                    Hence, if an Initiate in an Arcanum imbues an item with a spell of that Arcanum, they have the option of giving it advanced factors in everything and all the extra widgets, which will mean the item entails a lot of Paradox no matter who uses it. If the creator opts to provide the option (which makes the process of creating it require double successes), a user can elect to reduce the factors and remove the extra spell effects. If the creator opts to only stick to their free Reach, the imbued item cannot use additional advanced factors or spell effects at all.

                                        Originally posted by lbeaumanior View Post

                                        1. Do you actually wake up rested?

                                        These are mechanically the same question, and the answer is no, because you're not sleeping, much less resting.
                                        3. Can you choose to "wake up" (leave the Astral) after a specific number of real-time hours? (or is there any way to track real time, so you can start the Astral departure process)
                                        The time that passes in the Oneiros is however much time you experienced subjectively regardless of how much time passed in the real world, and the Sidereal Wastes' reduction of everything to actions (and need to make use of the right shortcuts to avoid actually experiencing the amounts of time involved for physically traveling astronomical distances) plays havoc with assumptions of outside duration, but between their proximity to collective humanity and the world it experiences and the fact that Beast doesn't make any special statements about the passage of time in the Primordial Dream I think it's safe to say an hour in the Temenos, the Dreamtime, and the Anthropic Redoubts is generally the same as an hour in the physical world.

                                        You could probably rig a spell to let you keep track of the outside time despite any differentials through combining the same principles as One Mind, Two Thoughts.
