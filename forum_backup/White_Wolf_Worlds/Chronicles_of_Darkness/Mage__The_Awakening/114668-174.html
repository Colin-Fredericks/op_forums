                                        Boons specify that the 9-again and 8-again options only apply to mundane rolls without extra reach. Boon specify that adding potency to dicerolls only affects mundane rolls. The section on conditions makes no such distinction. Exceptional Luck does not speciy it only works on mundane rolls, but it does include a +2 reach option to affect spellcasting. Either that is a really weird way to harken back to the reach options included in the boon section, in which case no particular restriction is given in the spell itself, or it is an option to make everything about the spell apply to spellcasting.

                                        Either interpretation allows Steadfast to be used with spellcasting rolls.
