                          Originally posted by Michael View Post
                          What exactly don't you like about it? The concept? the mechanics? Both? It helps when making suggestions to know what the issue is.
                          I like the concept, I think that emphasizing the free council's focus on human culture is great. The problem for me is that it's too weak. A +1 Yantra is basically worthless, since they're so ubiquitous. If you have enough time to use computer programming as a Yantra, you could've found any number of other Yantras much more easily. Heck, you arguably could've just used computer programming as a Yantra anyway, even without the merit! That's not worth the exp cost at all.

                          Then the fact that you need multiple characters to have the same 2-dot merit, just to get 8-again on teamwork spellcasting is way too much. Sure, it's nice, but you could also get that from a 2-dot Prime spell. Even worse is the fact that ALL characters participating in the teamwork spell must have Techne in order to give the bonus. Not only that, but they all must have the exact specific variety of Techne that you have. Consider the implications of that. If your cabalmates aren't Free Council, then they can't help you without ruining the Techne bonus. But there's a good chance that your cabalmates are strong enough that their contribution could easily offset the Techne bonus. In which case, Techne is giving you no benefit. Then, even if your cabalmates ARE Free Council, they probably aren't using the same kind of Techne you are, in which case they still can't help you.

                          In other words, Techne is useful in such an incredibly narrow sliver of situations, that I don't even think it's worth a merit at all. I think the concept of focusing on human culture is perfect for the Free Council, but the execution is far too weak, niche, and expensive to be practical.

                          A simple fix would be that Techne comes for free when you buy 2 merit dots that somehow relate to the aspect of human culture you're studying. I'd like to see a more comprehensive fix though.

                              Good point about pointing out what I don't like about Techne. Well, basically, it gives you nothing that can't be emulated by either picking a proper Dedicated Tool, adapting a fitting Shadow Name, or just using appropiate yantras - with the only unique benefit being the Sleeper thing, which isn't something I can see every single Libertine - or forget that, a majority of them - actually wanting enough to put two Experiences into. Basically, I feel it's doesn't change your gameplay patterns and doesn't provide you with unique experience that cannot be achieved without it. Each of other special merits gives you options that can, potentially, come up every session and change your play style. Techne just...is.

                              So, a wish list - note that not all of these must be checked at the same time:

                              - Something that can be used at least once every two game sessions assuming "standard" games of Mage that respect character focus (so if we have a character whose character sheet yells "PUT ME IN A CAGE WITH A DRAGON I WANT TO FEAST ON IT HEART", Storyteller will throw a fight or two every now and then)
                              - Something that emphasizes one of their universal tenets - so it's fitting both when you are Libertine Revolutionare, and when you are Ancient Tradition Goat Sacrificer, and when you are Mag Scientist
                              - Something that changes their gameplay patterns, offering or emphasizing options that help characterize the Assembly
                              - Something that is worth putting Experience into it;
                              - Something that is "exciting" to use - active things that need to be declared or "activated" are more interesting than passive bonuses that just float somewhere

                              For the "Egregore but Free Council", I would see something like this:

                              First dot - unchanged. Collective magic sounds proper.
                              Second dot - unchanged. Again, this emphasizes the importance of being THE GROUP
                              Third dot - I really like this Egregore dot, so something similar to it but based around idea of Lorehouse or political pull would be really cool
                              Fourth dot - Again, this fits unchanged. It sounds more like Techne than actual Techne, even.
                              Fifth dot - No idea what to replace it with, to be honest.

                              Actually, I think something like 4th dot of Egregore + extra frills to make it good enough standalone would probably be cool enough for me.

                              Example of idea I would like:
                              "Pick a magical style rooted in Sleeper world. You gain a special yantra slot that can be only used to incorporate an instrument from that magical style in your magic. It provides a yantra bonus appropriate to it's nature - so a tool will give you +1 bonus, while a rare sacrament will give you a +2 bonus - and in addition, always counts as a Dedicated Tool".

                                      I have also been looking for a new Merit for FC, but in my case, it would be in addition to Techne. This is because I am already using this version for my games:


                                      Techne (2)

                                      Requisite: Awakened, Free Council Status 1+.

                                      Choose an aspect of human culture. This can be anything like “baseball”, “alternative grunge rock”, “electronic sports”, “chinese taoism”, “contemporary sculpture”, “faith healing”, “18th-century poetry”, “MMA wrestling” or anything else. The mage obtains a free specialty related to it on one of their skills, 1 free dot of Contacts or Allies who are related to that field, and can use elements from that aspect of culture as tool Yantras. Members of that culture do not count as Sleeper witnesses when the culture is used as a Yantra if the spell doesn’t risk Paradox already. Finally, when casting a spell as a Ritual, Sleepers from that culture can participate on the Ritual as if they were Sleepwalkers if the spell doesn’t risk Paradox. This Merit may be purchased multiple times, once per cultural aspect desired.

                                              Forewarning: I like to break the game for dramatic effect.

                                              That being said, my house rule for the Free Council merit is that when Libertines ACTIVELY engage Sleepers in their spell casting, they can get some pretty massive bonuses. When they use this merit, free "reach" is applied towards the primary spell factor (which includes potency in my games). The more effort that is put into grounding the spell in Sleeper society, the more the players are able to move up the chart in spell factors.

                                              An example from my current game - a FC cabal in Chicago:
                                              The cabal just claimed a very large territory of about four suburbs south of the city, and attention turned to security. They wanted to set up a perimeter where any Supernal magic done within its limits pings on their radar. The area required would put this in the range of mastery or arch-mastery. However, the cabal spent nearly 4 months of in-game time (and about 4 gaming sessions) setting up a community watch program in these neighborhoods. They spent a massive amount of time withdrawn from Mage society and building the Sleeper institution into the imago of this massive spell. The night their neighborhood watch caught its first petty thief home invader, the imago of protection and defense was complete and they were able to release a spell with enough free juice to completely max out the AoE spell factors- for free! They spent the rest of their reach (obtained normally) on other effects.

                                              Is this incredibly broken-- yes! Is it incredibly fun -- hell yes! I and all of my players have a huge soft spot for the Free Council, so anything to drive home the message that humanity is magical and the FC as guardians and shepherds of humanities magical potential is permissible.

                                                  I rewrote all the order merits as 1 to 5 dot progressions when they were spoiled. This is what I came up with for Techne, hopefully it helps.

                                                  Techne • - •••••

                                                  Prerequisites: Techne reflects a personal investment in a sleeper tradition, therefore a character can qualify for it with any merit that represents his connection to that group: Contacts •, Mystery Cult Initiation •, Allies •, Status •, etc.
                                                  Effect: Your character uses Libertine practices in order to use cultural magical styles, sciences, and art forms as magical tools.
                                                  Area of Interest (•): You may treat the presence of Sleepers employing the yantra of your spell as a separate Yantra, with a bonus based on the number of sleepers involved (+1 for a few to +3 for a large group), as long as the spell is not obvious.
                                                  Group Project (••): Sleeper assistants apply their Paradox modifier as a bonus to the spell. A small group provides 9 again, a crowd 8-again, and a significant congregation grants the Rote quality. Again, only if the spell is not obvious.
                                                  Think Tank (•••): The Yantra bonus provided by Sleepers now counts as an additional dedicated tool, reducing Paradox by two.
                                                  Interdisciplinary Studies (••••): Multiple Libertines with this merit may add their sleeper congregation's relevant Yantra bonus to their own teamwork bonus, even if the spell being cast wouldn't normally resonate with their Yantra.
                                                  Gestalt (•••••): Sleepers participating in your ritual are considered sleepwalkers with regards to all spells they help you cast. The limit on non-obvious spells is also removed.
