    The Parliament of the Needle, updated to 2E

    09-03-2026, 07:02 PM
    Origins

    "History is full of people who out of fear, or ignorance, or lust for power have destroyed knowledge of immeasurable value which truly belongs to us all. We must not let it happen again."
    -Carl Sagan

    Nickname: Cousins
    Parentage: Mastigos, Free Council

    Background: In the later part of the 18th century, the Nameless War was in full swing, claiming the lives of many Awakened. Within the ranks of the Nameless, few were more infamous than Kosciej the Deathless. Yet despite being one of the most successful guerilla leaders of the Revolutionary Age, each comrade's life claimed by the conflict weighed heavily on his heart. With each one lost, with every bit of their research forgotten, seized or destroyed, future mages would be deprived of their memory, their mentorship, their life's work.

    Driven to preserve their knowledge for future generations, he and his cabalmates set to the creation of an Astral lorehouse containing all their knowledge, one would be accessible anywhere, an Anarcho-Communist Assembly founded on the belief that knowledge of the Mysteries is meant to be shared with the community, rather than hoarded like the Orders.

    In the early days of development, members would camp inside Kosciej's Oneiros as a temporary commune called The Forum. It is a time that older members swap stories about, but one which no one wants to go back to.

    Kosciej was the first member to reach full mastery of the Legacy, which allowed him to lay the foundations of a permanent realm in the Temenos, as well as develop the Daimon swapping method of initiation, making induction of new members much easier.

    With the foundations now laid, the next step was to build a community atop them. When Mictlantecuhtli became the second fully powered member, he would strive to set the realm’s form into that of a city, becoming the Architect of the project. Unlike the modern incarnation of Mictlan, his initial version only contained the Hub and the Regions, which stretch out into infinity.

    The third to become a master was Chronos, who had a vision of transforming Mictlan into a flying city. He would start by setting its limits, declaring a boundary and creating the Outer Shell, despite Mictlan remaining infinite in size on the inside. In defining the exterior of the city, he could now change how that exterior interacted with things beyond it. When he finished the construction of the Engines of Chronos, Mictlan became able to fly between the Astral and Underworld.

    When retelling the story, the Cousins often say that Kosciej had the plan, Mictlantecuhtli built the city and Chronos made it fly. In time other Cousins would build upon their work.

    During the Nameless Wars, the Cousins developed a degree of infamy by using younger members as Trojan Horses, allowing more experienced battlemages to possess them to enter and sabotage Diamond and Seer sancta.

    Due to their network of eyes, ears and souls in Assemblies across western Europe, the Cousins were amongst the first to recognize the pattern that would lead to the Great Refusal, being pivotal in its orchestration. Now their talents would be turned towards killing traitorous Nameless, as well as their Seer allies. When the Free Council formed, they were amongst its first members, and have remained in good standing since, despite multiple attempts by Guardian Caucuses to have them declared Left-Handed.

    Appearances: Advocates for the democratization of knowledge, the Cousins can often be found promoting open source initiatives, championing the creation of public knowledge infrastructure, community based education and taking a proactive role in knowledge philanthropy.

    In the past, due to their start as guerilla fighters, members avoided using any sort of distinctive marks. In the modern day, the Cousins favor an emblem inspired by the Aztec sun stone, each ring having a stylized depiction of a layer of Mictlan. The central layer shows a spool threading a needle, with the thread forming the Legacy's High Speech rune. It is normally worn on pins, rings, necklaces and other accessories to identify each other outside of Mictlan.

    Mictlan itself can be thought of as an onion, or four spheres inside one another. Each layer is actually infinite within itself and under no requirement to look like a sphere, or obey any laws of gravity or geometry at all.

    The innermost area is the Hub, an eye-bending Escher-city that wraps around itself. The Hub is shared equally between all the Cousins, who can bring things into it from their own Regions by thinking about it. Navigation is a matter of walking, though should one get lost, they can alter the structure to give themselves a direct bridge or doorway to wherever they were trying to get to. Most of the Hub resembles a vast Free Council lorehouse crossed with an Escher painting, and its architectural style and decoration change according to what's fashionable among the Cousins. There are two notable locations that remain more or less stable. The first is the Library of All Worlds, a central archive of knowledge made up of donations from the Oneiroi of the Parliament's members. The second is the Deliberative, the meeting-hall where the Cousins hang out and debate what they're going to do.

    Around The Hub is the Gateway Ring, the area where the portals to each member's Oneiroi are located. It usually appears as one area containing all of the gateways, which then extends into a network of passageways, bridges, canals or other paths that go from it into the Hub, connecting to each Region and bypassing the Regions entirely to the Boundary.

    Each Cousin (and Departed) has a Region, the part of Mictlan that is their own private territory, but open to the others to enter. If their Oneiros is analogous to their residence, their Region is akin to their garden - it's where they hold parties, meet their friends and experiment in dream-shaping. A Region is a sandbox, able to be reconfigured according to its owner's whim (with practice), so there's a certain amount of showing off involved. The Departed Regions are sealed, shrunk down to virtually nothing so that running them doesn't strain Xolotl in the absence of their owner.

    When a new Cousin is bound to arrive in Mictlan, their Region is shaped by Xolotl, after a vote by the Cousins, to thematically reflect their Shadow Name, complete with appropriate decor and Drudge staff.

    Beyond the Regions is The Boundary, the outer limit of Mictlan. It's as infinite as the rest of the realm, as there's no wall one can stand on, beyond which is the Temenos, but it has a definite feeling of being empty, somehow. Great industrial things resembling clockwork or engines litter the place, and the majority of Cousins don't spend time here. It is, however, the place for experiments and the place where Mictlan connects with the wider universe. Kosciej, for example, has built himself a tower here which reaches out (while being entirely within Mictlan) into the Underworld, from which he Contemplates Death.

    The Unseen Observatory can also be found in this area, which houses vast telescope-shaped apparatuses, built by Cousin Jodrell to gaze at the strange stars of Astral Space. Another major landmark is the Two Gates - the Gate of Life, which leads to Astral Space, and the Gate of Death, which leads to the Underworld. They are both closed and barred, guarded by Cihuateteo, and each Gate is flanked by two massive structures, called the Engines of Chronos. The Engines aren't what most people think of when they hear the word "engines" - Mictlan doesn't really fly, because it's an entire realm, there's nothing to fly through. They do serve the same purpose, though, in that they change Mictlan's relationship to the other realms. Each pair of Engines is actually a great, big, portal-creating Artifact that makes a connection to the Temenos or Underworld realm of the user's choice. Because the deceased Cousins no longer have physical bodies, if they wish to leave into Astral Space, the Underworld or into Mind Twilight of the material, they must use the Engines to do so. When the Gates are open, things can come through them from the other side, allowing Mictlan to welcome visitors.

    Doctrine

    “The great project of humanity is the collaborative construction of a shared understanding of the world.”
    -Pierre Lévy

    Prerequisites: Mind 2

    Initiation: The prospective member must merge part of their Oneiros with Mictlan. The initiate swaps Daimons with their mentor, creating a powerful connection through them to Mictlan. The mentor's Daimon then uses the Stitch of Kinship Numen to make the connection permanent, completing the initiation.

    A mage attempting to initiate themselves through other means must instead make their way to Mictlan and utilize a Patterning of Mind themselves to create an indefinite Astral bridge from their Oneiros to Mictlan. The candidate must then journey with their Daimon through their gate to Mictlan, where their Daimon permanently weaves their Oneiros with the realm, completing the initiation.


    New Numen: Stitch of Kinship
    Requirement: The user must be the Daimon of a Cousin capable of using the capstone Attainment.

    Description: Spend a point of Essence and roll Power + Finesse, on a success the subject gains an Astral Threshold to Mictlan in their Oneiros for a scene.

    By spending another point of Essence, an initiation candidate who hosts the Daimon can be linked back to Mictlan permanently, completing the initiation. An unwilling subject can contest this with Resolve + Gnosis.


    Organization: Egalitarianism is a core pillar pillar of the Cousins, as such all members are considered equals, regardless of their power or experience. Whenever deciding what they are going to do, members debate in the Deliberative and put it to a vote. Each Cousin has one vote, and the majority is binding. If a vote is locked, one may attempt to persuade any abstainers to join their side.

    Because they are all a permanent part of the same Astral community, a strong sense of respect is fostered. While any of their Oneiros may be accessed from Mictlan, entering one without permission is seen as a fundamental breach of trust.

    Members share a strong sense of camaraderie, often referring to each other using the term Cousin and their chosen Shadow Name (Cousin Chronos, Cousin Galatea, Cousin Daedalus, etc).

    The term Uncle is sometimes used for the founders when they are trying to assume more control than their fair share, as a gentle reminder that they are one amongst equals.

    On rare occassions, some members opted out of persisting in Mictlan as morpheans; those that have already passed are known as the Departed. Their Regions are sealed and can only be accessed with the aid of Xolotl.

    Xolotl itself is an artificial ghost-goetia, which manifests as a huge dog, or dog-headed man. It has the thankless task of running the thought-city and maintaining Mictlan's integrity. Without it, the Cousins would have to do it themselves, which is possible (Xolotl was created in 1918) but distracting. Any Cousin can, by concentrating, override Xolotl to make changes to the Hub, the Boundary or their own Region. In a sense, it is the communal Daimon for the communal Oneiros that is Mictlan.

    In addition to Xolotl, Mictlan's constructs also include the Drudges and Cihuateteo. The Drudges are vaguely humanoid, mindless and obey verbal commands, acting as lifters of heavy objects and cleaners.

    The Cihuateteo are attack spirits created by Cousin Cihuacoatl to repel any invading Ghosts or Astral entities. They look like skull-faced women with eagle talons instead of fingernails, and perch in two Aeries near each of the Two Gates.

    The final inhabitants are the Shadows, the Daimons of the Cousins, who are confined to each member's Oneiros. They can be summoned into the Region of their host and banished at will.

    ​
    Theory: Knowledge is not the exclusive right of any one group or organization, it belongs to us all. It is by sharing it freely, preserving it and building upon it that we pave a path to a better tomorrow. That is our duty to future generations.

    The study of the Mysteries is no different. When a mage is killed, or their research destroyed, their point of view and insights are lost alongside them, which diminishes us all.

    By connecting our Oneiri, we can create a communal Astral space, a hub of knowledge that can outlast the death of any individual, persevere through every attempt at destruction and censorship. Drawing on each other's knowledge and life experience, we can take communal ownership of the Mysteries, deepening our understanding like never before.

    Share your knowledge, drive the engines of progress and enlightenment through collective effort, and lay down the foundations that will edify the future.


    Magic

    “We must learn to think together in an integrated, synergistic fashion, rather than in fragmented and competitive ways”
    -Joanna Macy

    Ruling Arcanum: Mind
    Yantras: Library, archive or other repository of knowledge (+1, +2 if specialized for the occult), threads, spools or needles (+1, +2 if perfected), a notebook or journal containing one's research notes (+2), sticky notes, flashcards or stationery goods (+1), honeycombs (+1), bees (+1 for symbolic representations, +2 for a swarm or hive), acting to protect knowledge from being destroyed (+2).

    Oblations: Pursuing research or helping others in theirs, engaging in peer reviews of theories of magic, teaching or studying, disseminating knowledge, contributing or donating knowledge to a public lorehouse, open source archive, community library or similar communal knowledge repository.

    The Ties that Bind: Because of how they are initiated, all the Cousins have a Connected sympathy to each other and to Mictlan.

    Whenever a Cousin dies, their memories and accumulated knowledge remain accessible within their Region. A Cousin can also choose to persist in Mictlan as a Rank 2 goetia, so that their unique perspective and life experiences will not be lost. In this form, they retain their Arcana and Attainments, as well as their rotes and Praxis as Numen. It is possible for any living Cousin to turn this ability to remain as a morphean on or off for themselves, via an instant action while they are in Mictlan.

    ​
    ​Attainments

    First: The Clear Account
    Prerequisites: Initiation

    The most fundamental ability of the Cousins is to ensure memories are stored in Mictlan with accuracy and rich detail. When extended to their own memories, or those of others, the subject can remember their experiences with a similar degree of clarity.

    This replicates Perfect Recall with Reach assigned to instant use.


    Optional: Space 1

    The Cousin makes a cursory search on Mictlan's archives about an issue at hand, finding a relevant fact or detail about it.

    This replicates a Knowing of Mind and Space, with Reach assigned to instant use.


    Second: The Open Floor
    Prerequisites: Mind 2

    The Cousin fine tunes themselves with Mictlan, allowing them to more easily maintain a presence within it. After a scene making the rounds in Mictlan and catching up to what the other Cousins are up to, they become able to focus on multiple mental tasks at once, allowing them to more easily maintain a constant presence in Mictlan while continuing to act in the material.

    However, it is also common for them to use it to dedicate one of their mental threads towards utilizing some of their more mentally demanding Attainments, without losing track of what they are currently doing. In such a case, they may choose whether to use their remaining mental thread to act in either the Astral or the material, which leaves their body unmoving in the one they didn’t choose. Alternatively, they can choose to act in both at once, which does tend to make them look a little crazy, seemingly talking to people and interacting with objects that exist only in their minds.

    Should they wish to extend this ability to multitask to others, they can accompany the subject as they both check in with the Cousins.

    This replicates One Mind, Two Thoughts with Reach assigned to advanced duration and doing instant actions.


    Optional: Space 2

    The path to Mictlan is always open to the Cousin, ensuring that no matter where they are, they are never far from their community. When used on themselves, another member, or a subject they are mentally connected to, they can enter Mictlan instantly, arriving at the Cousin’s Region.

    In addition, it also allows the subject to bypass any mental barriers at no cost, such as entering their dreams or Oneiros, crossing the Astral Threshold and similar. In either case, no lengthy meditation or place of power is required.

    This is a Ruling of Mind and Space, withstood by Resolve, with Reach assigned to instant use.


    Third: The Communal Archive
    Prerequisites: Mind 3

    The Cousin curates a collection of research materials from Mictlan's archives. Journals, interviews, movies, documentaries, live accounts and more are all made available to the subject's mind, allowing them to benefit from them anywhere they go.

    This replicates a Potency primary Ruling of Mind, granting [Mind] dots in Library or Advanced Library. Reach is assigned to instant use and advanced duration.


    Optional: Space 3

    The Cousin extends their perceptions to connect with those of others, allowing them to either share their own senses with them or to observe things from the subject's perspective. It is often used to allow the Cousins and their allies to provide remote support, lending their expertise through spells, Mage Sight or even just as witnesses. Spellcasting through these senses requires a range of remote viewing. The observer is able to switch between these senses and their own as a reflexive action.

    This replicates a Potency primary Unveiling of Mind, withstood by Resolve, that allows one to see through someone else's senses. Reach is assigned to instant use, sympathetic range and advanced duration. A point of mana pays for sympathetic range.


    Fourth: The Wider Perspective
    Prerequisites: Mind 4

    The Cousin draws on the shared insights and life experiences of Mictlan's community, expanding their own point of view, or that of the subject.

    This replicates Augment Mind with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration.


    Optional: Space 4

    The Cousin reaches out to other minds, allowing them to unearth new insights from the subject's memories. Should the target be another Cousin, they may also share one of their memories with them, allowing complex scenarios, plans and research to be conveyed in moments.

    This replicates Read the Depths with Reach assigned to instant use, sympathetic range and advanced scale. When targeting another cousin, the optional Reach effect to share memories can also be used. A single point of mana pays for both sympathetic range and Everywhere.


    Fifth: The Collective Spark
    Prerequisites: Mind 5

    The Cousin channels the collective know-how and practical experience of the entire commune, allowing them to reach a depth of understanding beyond what any one person could achieve. After spending a scene parsing through this knowledge in the Library of All Worlds, or doing so alongside the subject, they are able to establish a link to their shared understanding of a skill.

    This replicates Gain Skill with Reach assigned to advanced duration and changing primary factor.


    Optional: Space 5

    The Cousin is able to not only share their knowledge, but the entirety of their minds, allowing them to bring all of their presence, skill and magical power to the aid of each other, as well as their allies. In order to do so, they must spend a scene meditating, slowly bringing their focus to their subjects.

    In some cases they take on a symbiotic role, acting as a support presence in their allies' minds. But when dealing with enemy subjects or highly sensitive missions, they instead take full control.

    During the Great Refusal, and later the Nameless War, newer members would be used as Trojan Horses, allowing senior battlemages to enter and sabotage Diamond and Seer sancta. It was the use of this tactic that would earn the Cousins a degree of infamy.

    This replicates Possession with Reach assigned to sympathetic range, advanced scale and advanced duration. A single point of mana pays for both sympathetic range and Everywhere.

    ​
    What were you thinking design wise ?

    The first Attainment explains how they are able to keep detailed and accurate memories in Mictlan, and is very useful to help remember any important information or details that were otherwise overlooked.

    The first optional Attainment is based on the Encyclopedic Knowledge merit, but applied to any skill. It does a surface scan of the information in Mictlan with Mind and then uses Space to find related bits of trivia and put them together into something helpful. It allows them to be able to apply the knowledge of Mictlan from the moment they first join.

    The second main Attainment is based on their original one from the Soul Cage Active Play. In this case, I am utilizing the fact that some of their other Attainments could be keeping a train of thought busy to justify their Dreamspeakers-like interaction with both the material and Astral at once. The Reach effect allows them to be able to be simultaneously active in both the material and Astral. The activation ritual helps attune them to the multitasking needed to pull it off, while also serving to create opportunities for meaningful interactions and plot hooks.

    The second optional Attainment is a specialized, Mind variation of Break Boundary, allowing the Cousin to bypass any mental barriers, like the one they meditate through to enter their dreams, or access their Oneiros, it also allows them to pass through the Astral threshold or similar barriers for free. Since one of their original Attainments allowed them to enter Mictlan from anywhere, and because they have a connection to it, this is how they can make their way there fast. The fact it requires no place of power means they can use it anytime and anywhere.

    The third Attainment allows them to consult Mictlan’s stores of knowledge from anywhere. Since Library is a merit and gives a dice bonus for extended rolls in its specialized mental skill, it maps to a Ruling of Mind. It allows them to instantly provide anyone with a comprehensive source of research materials, on a wide variety of topics, without having to go to the location of a physical library.

    The third optional Attainment is based on the writeup in Tome of the Pentacle, it is Unveiling of Mind that allows them to see through their subject’s eyes, or allowing someone else to see through theirs, utilizing similar rules to a Familiar. Since all Cousins have Connected sympathy, being able to provide aid via this Attainment, especially if they are already talking in Mictlan, can be very versatile. With an appropriate sympathetic yantra, it can also be used to spy on their enemies.

    The fourth Attainment allows them to tap into the intelligence, including the emotional intelligence, of their other Cousins, thus allowing them to widen their perspective, represented by an increase to their mental or social Attributes.

    The fourth optional Attainment is inspired by their writeup in Tome of the Pentacle. It allows them to look into the memories of other Cousins, or even view multiple memories of the same one, in order to witness firsthand whatever they might have experienced. The Reach effect ensures the memory sharing is mutual, making it so that a Cousin can have other members benefit from their own shared memories just as much as they benefit from their community’s memories. It also grants them the ability to communicate detailed memories or complex ideas quickly to other Cousins en masse, an ability which no doubt allowed them to have a formidably quick information network during the Nameless War and Great Refusal. With the right sympathetic yantra, they can even look into the memories of rivals and adversaries.

    The capstone is based on one of their first edition Attainments. It allows them to gain or enhance any skill using the knowhow from their shared knowledge base. When combined with their other Attainments, it means they can grant anyone the Attributes, skill and research materials on just about any topic, making them exceptionally good researchers. Since there is no associated cost and they last up to a week, it incentivizes them to use it on their cabalmates and allies, thus providing a mechanical incentive to use teamwork rolls, building on the theme of taking collective ownership of the Mysteries.

    The optional capstone is based on their signature combat tactic from The Soul Cage, which Tome of the Pentacle confirmed as being an Attainment. It allows them to possess or coordinate multiple Cousins at a time, or to do the same to their enemies if they have a sympathetic link to them. While it requires ritual casting time, the fact it works at advanced scale means that they can tag multiple allies at once, helping to coordinate them and jumping to their aid as needed.

    The first edition version of the Parliament of the Needle was Mind primary and Death secondary, and that was reinforced with all the references to Aztec mythology and its underworld, in how members who died remained as ghosts, or how Mictlan was being constantly pulled to the Underworld, requiring soul stones to anchor it, as well as a steady supply of mana. In the second edition writeup, they have Mastigos as their parent Path and Mind as their primary Arcanum, which would make Space their secondary one. One of my design goals was to preserve as much of their lore and themes as I could, while also accounting for the changes in second edition.

    The nickname Cousins comes from how they referred to both in and out of character. The background lore is based on a combination of Tome of the Pentacle and A Tourist’s Guide to Mictlan (from an intermission chapter of the AP). In his talk to the Exarchs in The Soul Cage, Kosciej said he was born on November 5th of 1871, whereas in second edition he was already a well established mage by then, resurfacing with the Parliament of the Needle fully formed in those same years, making him much older, while also confirming their members participated in both the Nameless War and Great Refusal.

    I considered making them also have the Mysterium as a parent order, and to cover Elysium, the realm from Persephone’s offshoot of the Legacy from the end of the chronicle, but I thought it would make the writeup confusing.

    Regarding their appearances, I speculated that, as a Legacy in good standing, they would probably extend their philosophy of communal ownership of knowledge to mundane one as well, promoting its access to all. The sigil is inspired by the map of Amoni-Ram from SCP-001, transposed to the multiple layers of the Aztec sun stone. The appearance and organization of Mictlan were taken from A Tourist’s Guide to Mictlan, as well as Daimon Kosciej’s conversations with Persephone in chronicle.

    In order to keep the Daimon swapping initiation, I made the ability into a Numen that only Cousins with full mastery have. The process to initiate yourself requires at least Gnosis 4 to happen, and Gnosis 5 to do it without over Reaching. The Daimon swapping method can work on Gnosis 2 members as normal, making it so being initiated by a member of the community is easier than initiating yourself. In both cases, the process has a theme of weaving a mental thread connecting the prospective member to Mictlan, then Mictlan to the prospective member and weaving those threads permanently together.

    The yantras incentivize the preservation of knowledge and making a record of what you know. The needles, spools and threads symbolize how they weave their minds into a collective. Stationery tools, sticky notes and flashcards represent organizing and leaving behind a tangible form of knowledge. Bees and honeycombs represent their collective effort to gather and preserve the treasures of knowledge for the community.

    Lastly, the Ties that Bind make their ability to persevere as morpheans (as there was no good way to have them remain as ghosts) inherent to Mictlan, with the morpheans utilizing the mechanics from the core book. It accounts for the Departed by allowing them to turn the ability to remain as a morphean on or off, but making it so their Region remains. Since parts of their Oneiros are woven together when they join, having a Connected sympathy to each other and Mictlan is the logical consequence. It allows them to easily use their Attainments and sympathetic spells on each other. But the stronger sympathy means they pose a greater risk to the Legacy if they are captured, and that a member being mind controlled is a risk to them all. Overall, I think it makes for a really interesting cost-benefit.

    That concludes the design notes, hope you had as much fun reading them as I had writing them. A big thanks to NuriaAge and Desdendelle, for all their feedback. But above all, a huge thanks to Dave Brookshaw, for inspiring so many Mage players over the years, I hope this writeup was able to do your concept justice.

    New experiences are the font of creativity, when seeking inspiration, break your routine.
