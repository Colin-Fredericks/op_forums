    Originally posted by Emerus View Post
    A mantra is a sacred utterance, a numinous sound, a syllable, word or phonemes, or group of words in Sanskrit believed by practitioners to have psychological and/or spiritual powers. (Copied from Wikipedia, so take this with a grain of salt)

    So using High Speech as a Mantra specifically asks for the mage to speak the words out loud. However, I would argue that there could be other ways to incorporate the High Language as a yantra, only it would not be High SPEECH. Runes are one of those ways. Transmitting the High Language through body language could be a variation of High Speech with different complications. You would have to perform strange and possibly "unnatural" looking gestures (to a Sleeper's eyes anyway), but it is still a bit more subtle that speaking gibberish out loud. So maybe it could be a Merit that requires you to already possess the High Speech Merit, that would allow a mage to do that. You would possibly still have to spend an extra turn to perform the gestures because of the time limitations of the fallen world, and then it could function in a similar manner as High Speech. I would probably not allow a spell to benefit from both of those uses of High Speech concerning die bonuses, because the idea is that you are still using High Speech, but you have found a different way to express it, other than a Mantra.

                                                            Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post
                                                            Would a Compelling of Life and Death be sufficient so that, when the caster succeeded in an Intimidation roll, it intensified the fear they created so they could apply a related condition such as Cowed, Spooked, Frightened, Madness or Nightmares ? I am not sure if it would be enough and if it could be applied at the mage rather than the target.

                                                            More specifically, I am trying to see if it could be a viable first Attainment.
                                                            When you get an Exceptional Success you either get a Condition or you apply a Condition to someone else. Compelling might reduce the threshold for Exceptional Success to three successes. At least, that's how I'd design a Compelling spell around that idea.
                                                            For an Attainment I would instead have it that an intimidating social interaction against a subject would apply a fitting Condition to that subject as long as the Potency (Arcanum dots) exceed the subject's Withstand (in this case Composure). Advanced Duration as a secondary spell factor, after which the Condition fades if not resolved yet. It also fades if the mage applies the Attainment on another subject.
