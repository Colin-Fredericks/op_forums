    I remind everyone that Great Cults are 'magical mythos explained' before great unification by Atlantean Orders.       In coming games I may need Norse/Vikings Great Cult - so here are my first rough drafts of it. I will edit this post with updates on material. 😎

      Seiðr (? Not sure on name )

      What is magic? Magic is boon and curse from Gods, both Aesir and Vanir. It was stolen by them from Jotunn, Giants.

      What is Fallen World? World is carcass of great Giant Yamir, killed by first Gods.

      Who are Exarchs and Oracles? Exarchat by Norse are known as Giants, first evil gods that rules universe. Oracles in that cosmology are Aesir 'good' gods.

      What is Abyss? Abyss is great void of Ginnungagap - non-existence between Nine Worlds themselves.

      What is Supernal Realms? Supernal is called Nine Worlds, with Midgard ( Earth ) being only one of those relaties. Seiðr mages think there could more Paths than those typical found. ( General, Fallen Worlds Realms associations are in that topic. )

      1. Midgard: The world of humans. The physical/material/Fallen World.

      2. Jotunheim: Probable Transcendental Realm, ruling over other Supernal, where Oracles and Exarchs origin? ( See Silver Ladder book on that. )

      3 & 4. Asgard & Vanaheim - Primal Wild

      5 & 6.​Alfheim & Svartalfheim: Arcadia

      7. Helheim - Stygia

      8. Muspelheim - Aether

      9. Niflheim - Pandemonium - Norse version of 'Labiyrynthine Realm of remose' is mist bonud icy realm, not in demons and fire.​

      Yggdrasil is Emanation Realm connecting all the rest of Nine Worlds.

      What are local Darshanas (proto-Orders)?
      • Bearserke ( Adamantino Velos ) - Sacred warriors, touched by Odins mad rage. High in their armies are Valkyrie, mostly women chosen by gods.
      • Brokke (? From Brokkr dwarf - Velos & Ajivaki, future Nameless ) - craftsman and servants, they practice magic by serving. Legacies: Forge Masters ( Legacies: The Ancient )​
      • Gothi ( Omphalos ) - Priest and judges in temples of great Aesir. Each Gothi circle is lead by Hofgothi - high priest. ( SL Deacon equivalent )
      • Skaldi ( Gnostikon ) - Lore keepers and bards, sustaining lore and telling Sagas of local heroes. Legacies: Skalds ( Legacies: The Ancient )

              Rough draft for Awakened Christianity - for my Rome games. ( It's very important for Rome Empire in 2nd till 5th century AD - and probably from there on. ) Probably similar would work probably for Judaism and Islam versions of it.

              Awakened Christianity

              What is magic? Magic is boon send from God and their angels.

              What is Fallen World? World was created perfect by God. With Atlantean fall of Towers to Heaven - God cursed whole creation to become Fallen World as it is.

              Who are Exarchs and Oracles? Exarch AND Oracles are both emanations of One True God. Many times they are called as Archangels on particular Path.

              What is High Speech? High Speech is language of Angels - later known as Enochian

              What is Abyss? Absence of the divine light, pointed to the theology of St Augustine of Hippo. Some intellectual in Awakened Christianity posits it's parts of Creation rejected by God. In later centuries other posits it's literal Hell, or spread of Hell, directly from the Devil.

              What is Supernal Realms? Supernal Realms are higher planes, outside of normal reality, each governed by one Archangel. They are still only 'filters' of One True Higher reality, ruled all at once, by God themselves.

              Aether - Havens of All Mighty, under Archangel Micheal
              Arcadia - Under guidance of Gabriel, past World BEFORE Creation of Humans - perfect, but also somehow pagan.
              Pandemonium - Purgatory, ruled by the angel of judgment Phanuel. ( In Middle Ages, it's changed into literal 'Hell of sinners, ruled by Lucifer' )
              Primal Wild - Garden of Eden AFTER creation of Adam and Eve, especially with Tree of Knowledge, under guidance of Rafael
              Stygia - Mausoleums and Cathedrals of previous dead, under watch of Uriel ( See Driftwood Cathedral for inspiration )

              What are local Darshanas (proto-Orders)?
              Christianity was created just AFTER first integration of Atlantean Orders in Ancient Rome, circa 1st century BCE. ( With Christianity creation on whole 1st century AD. ) It's widespread of this religion in Mediterranean Sea area is joined with creation Diamond as organisations Alliance. All of Darshanas of it ARE Atlantean and Seers Orders - even in modern times, it's rather minority percentage but still sustained in general multicultural tapestry of Diamond Cults beliefs systems, when more and more Awakened eshew more religious roots of their magic.

                          From what we see, I think the Great Cults represent cultures that are stable and homogenous enough that everyone in them can more or less agree on a single cultural cosmology. Whoever you talk to in Greece, they more or less see magic in the same cultural idiom. They're not exclusionary exactly, it's just that there aren't that many people from outside the culture.

                          That's why the era of To The Strongest is so important. Large numbers of mages from different Great Cults meet each other and realise that things they though were very important, are indeed simply cultural idioms.

                          Christianity by definition doesn't have that. It's surrounded by a pagan culture that has radically different ideas. You can't really be a mage and think only in a Christian idiom, because other mages are blatantly able to interact with the Supernal in the same manner as you without it (I suspect those who can't accept that, go Banisher). It also won't have a consistent cosmology for a long time. I think you'd need to wait till a millennia or so late for Christianity to have that kind of intellectual hegemony.

                                          So I can simplify the "Christian Great Cult" and "Islamic Great Cult" real nice and simple: there never would have been any; there would be, at best, Nameless Orders and Legacies.

                                          The Synhedrion aren't described in great detail in the Tome of the Pentacle, but between the fact that their one major entry implies that all theorizing about one Yeshua ben Yosef was provided to the Diamond by the Synhedrion and the way Dave frames it when he talks about them (I will namely highlight "If every Great Cult provided something to the melting pot of the Diamond, their main contribution was a sense of responsibility."), the Judean Great Cult was pretty well in the mix* by the time the Diamond officially forms by the Alexandrian Convocation of 265 CE-and as many other people have pointed out, there undoubtedly would be Romans involved, arguably enough to make it reasonable to question if there ever was a Roman Great Cult or if they had co-mingled enough with the proto-Diamond that you effectively just had the Awakened side of the Republic/Empire split between the proto-Diamond conglomerate and Roman Nameless Orders and Legacies. By the time Christianity was legalized and incorporated into the Roman Empire's state with the Edict of Milan in 313 CE, the Diamond had already had 48 years of being formally organized.

                                          Christianity, in the context of Awakening, is a religion born into a word where a faction is already in full swing archetypalizing national myth and belief and reaching for the root behind the symbols, and it's own factionalism through it's nascency pretty well means the religion just couldn't have the time build up enough unity to be a Great Cult before collision with the Diamond basically gave "Christian"(and I'll get to those quotations) mages a better option.

                                          And forget Islam. The Prophet Muhammad isn't even born until 570, and that's all smack dab in the heart of the Diamond Belt. The Diamond is not all that far off, temporally speaking, from the Tremere Adoption, so they are near the end of their struggling steps as being a unifying Middle Eastern Sect.

                                          And of course, both Christianity and Islam run into a handicap where they are distinctly religious structures rather than spiritually national structures. The faith and practices of the Great Cults before them were intertwined with their cultures so much as to be almost unquestionably inseparable, where Christianity and Islam still in a period of being parallel to their host nations, an outsider that needs to integrated into their adjacent surroundings, and that's disregarding the violence perpetrated against these cults through the period.

                                          Where that matter is that Awakening already does a lot to basically kill your faith-it's hard to believe a religion you grew up in when the light of Supernal Truth kind of shines a light for just how many, many holes are in the weave. The traditional Great Cults found their native myths and beliefs a fairly useful starting point, and the pantomiming of those practices had tangible benefits, and honestly the whole thing just very deeply rooted to a point where even if you don't believe a word of it, you still speak the religious and mythological language** but there was a reason the Diamond emerged from a truly world changing mass-integration of cultures rather than clutching their pearls against the blasphemies and heresies against their faith-it's a convienent language, but that Persian's stories fill the holes left in mine, and mine his, and when more and more mages combine their stories and understanding, the more Truth makes sense, and clinging to an absolute belief in your bedtime stories in that context is rather silly.

                                          Christians and Muslims, by contrast, do not grow up in a world where their version of their faith is literally spoken and portrayed all around them to a point of being background radiation, and so it's value to the individual is that much more contigent on being a hidden/argued truth(or put more simply, it really has to be true to be worth it) of the world that everyone else has to come around to....and then, upon Awakening, find themselves confronted with an even more hidden, more contentious, and far more substantively and objectively truthier truth of reality, all with a surprisingly large organization who can explain it and prove it way better than their forms of Christianity or Islam could.

                                          Yeah, there's no fucking way a Christian or an Islamic Great Cult ever happened. Surely you had cabals, Legacies, even Nameless Orders, because every mage has some Lie they believe in, but by the time either religion was substantial enough to have the same grounding effect Great Cult's mythos did, they'd be overshadowed by the Diamond and their nearest rising competitors, the religions already turned into tools as part of the arsenal of these entities.

                                          The fact that the Tremere emerged as the first major organization to come forward and be regarded as close to a peer to the Diamond at the time (coming to bargain from Scandinavia, central Europe, and India, no less) without so much as an attempt to articulate any Awakened attempt to unite under a Christian/Islamic identifying mass organization of any sort pretty well states it just wasn't happening, nevermind a Great Cult.

                                          As for the Nordic Great Cult....Did I mention that one of the directions the Tremere came from was Scandanavia? Yeah, think that answers that one, chief.


                                          *Granted, not fully incorporated, since the first thing the Consilium Age includes a mention that the Synhedrion didn't feel the need to fully integrate, but that's "One God Above All Others" monotheism for ya, baby.
                                          **For the same reason I don't believe in God but will still say "Thank god" or "God damnit" after being raised in a world where Christianity is intertwined with my culture.

                                                          Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                                                          So I can simplify the "Christian Great Cult" and "Islamic Great Cult" real nice and simple: there never would have been any; there would be, at best, Nameless Orders and Legacies.

                                                          The Synhedrion aren't described in great detail in the Tome of the Pentacle, but between the fact that their one major entry implies that all theorizing about one Yeshua ben Yosef was provided to the Diamond by the Synhedrion and the way Dave frames it when he talks about them (I will namely highlight "If every Great Cult provided something to the melting pot of the Diamond, their main contribution was a sense of responsibility."), the Judean Great Cult was pretty well in the mix* by the time the Diamond officially forms by the Alexandrian Convocation of 265 CE-and as many other people have pointed out, there undoubtedly would be Romans involved, arguably enough to make it reasonable to question if there ever was a Roman Great Cult or if they had co-mingled enough with the proto-Diamond that you effectively just had the Awakened side of the Republic/Empire split between the proto-Diamond conglomerate and Roman Nameless Orders and Legacies. By the time Christianity was legalized and incorporated into the Roman Empire's state with the Edict of Milan in 313 CE, the Diamond had already had 48 years of being formally organized.
                                                          The Synhedrion were...

                                                          1) Completely - and I mean completely - embedded into the Sleeper organisation of the same name.

                                                          2) Based on the contemporary-to-them Merkabah mysticism rather than Kabala, as Kabala won't be invented for several centuries. The Synhedrion were scholars and rabbis who recorded, debated, and attempted to understand their Awakening visions and Mage Sight in the context of their religion, not Golem-creators.

                                                          3) Were the hardest of hardcore about magic only being the province of those wise enough to not misuse it.

                                                          When we were writing Tome of the Pentacle, we had it in mind that

                                                          a) Canonically, the Seers-to-be and Diamond-to-be at the time were two differences of opinion / political parties / arguments within the same organisation, named by later Diamond historians as the "Tyrannic Antitype" and "Atlantean Antitype". The difference was, essentially, should mages rule Sleepers as an expression of the natural hierarchy of the worlds and their Awakened status, or should mages regard themselves as a people apart from Sleeping Empires and Nations, governing themselves? The Diamond Precept is "The Awakened are one Nation" - it's that declaration that expels the Exarchists into a millenium of Nameless Orders and eventually the Seers, and makes the Five Diamond Orders out of their Darshanas.

                                                          b) We (by which I mean I) do not believe in bloodless revolutions and clean transitions. Every great change in the makeup of the setting should have refuseniks and outliers. I wanted to show a Great Cult that *didn't* go into the Diamond, but was a peer and influenced some of its thinking. The Synhedrion would fit - it's the Hellenic culture that got left out of To The Strongest for space.

                                                          c) The conflict between the antitypes is all well and theoretical, but the proto-Seers would have had to do something to get Unnamed by the rest of the Awakened community. First edition presented Diamond mages in the Roman Empire as being fringe thinkers and Greek imports, with power in the Empire held by Seers (conflicting with the fact that Seers shouldn't exist yet) - especially the Praetorian Ministry, or their ancestors anyway.

                                                          d) Seers of the Throne sure do have a lot of Jewish terms in their lexicon. Why the fuck is a Profane Urim called that?

                                                          e) While we're on the subject, there's an inherent conflict between a Synhedrion that tries to interpret the mind of God from their visions while remaining morally-centered and, well... How Prelacies work for Seers. Interpreting what you see in Mage Sight to determine the wishes of the divine is what a Prelate does, for certain values of "the Divine", and that's probably why the Synhedrion are so careful with their practices. They're competition to the Tyrannics, who won't worship the Iron Seals like they "should".

                                                          f) So if we take that forward and adapt it to second edition, we have Tyrannic-minded mages buying into the Empire, a Great Cult tied to a culture that's about to get horribly persecuted by that Empire, our desire for a Cult that stays "outside", our need for Tyrannic mages to do something terrible in the eyes of their Atlantean peers, parallel or conflicting practices between the Cult and the basic Sight>Prelate loop of a Seer, modern Seers of the Throne who mock that Cult's religion with their terms for things...

                                                          ... It all adds up to "things do not end well for the Synhedrion".


                                                          Which is why the final chapter glosses over them. It's both too complicated and if handled poorly full of potential antisemitic pittraps. Somewhere out there is a rpg freelancer who's versed in Merkabah enough to write intelligently about it (and is willing to do so despite it being a closed practice maybe not suitable for turning into a game for outsiders) and depict the effect the first Jewish-Roman war and the destruction of the second temple had on them.

                                                          But that freelancer is not me.

                                                          As it stands in the gameline, the Synhedrion's fate is mysterious. Maybe they did join into the Diamond. Maybe the final straw for the Diamond kicking the Prelates out came later, or had a different cause. Maybe we don't have to have yet another Jewish faction of mages in a roleplaying game murdered to make a point. We don't really use the appropriated terms for Seer artifacts any more (notice how the line tries to say "Profane Robe" most of the time) and they have a perfectly good explanation even if they do stay - Paternoster, when it first started up over a millenium later, looted a *lot* of things. Including the Teraphim, which is one of the items in question.
