    I know that this book is still undergoing the errata process, but I'm curious about Contact High, from Signs of Sorcery.

            Something that popped up. We are up against a vampire with the swarm form merit. The ST says that the first mages spell will fail since he has only 1 person as a target, and the he needs to either change it to an area of effect spell, or do something else.

            The player, a Mastigos, argues that it shouldn't matter as it's still one target, the vampire, and not every crow he became.

            The ST counters that a size 7 bear is also one target, but you need to account for the fact that it is size 7 in the imago. The same applies to the swarm.

            I'm not sure where I fall on this as it is such a grey area I have never encountered. What is the general consensus?
            I think it depends on the spell.

            For a Mind spell, there is still only one actual mind controlling the swarm, thus it still only has one target (the brain is not the mind), however you still must account for the increased size of the swarm. And yes, this means a swarm could potentially defeat a spell by expanding to a larger size.

            Other spells might have to account for the additional physical bodies: most Life (or Death, in the case of vampires) spells would need to target each individual body in the swarm because the spells are specifically affecting the bodies, which are decidedly numerous.

                    I'd argue that a mind has no size stat. Still, I'd side with the ST.

                    The scale table in MTAw 2nd Ed. core rules p. 114 details this.
                    If you go by RAW, the swarm can either be targetted as individual subjects (size of the largest subject is below 5 and thus irrelevant in this case) or by applying the area of effect column and determining the volume of the swarm (possibly also hitting other targets swarmed over in the area).

                    Keep in mind, that even tho the spell is targetting the mind as an abstract concept, the spell still plays out in the fallen world, where the subject is the swarm, not the mind directly that controls it. It 'affects' the mind of the swarm by affecting the swarm as its subject(s).

                                        Originally posted by Caimbeul View Post
                                        1. Highest arcanum of the spell vs your own -> matter 3 vs matter 3
                                        The text actually says that it's the mage's highest Arcanum (which would be Prime 4 in this case) that determines free Reaches, but I would personally rule it as you say, take the highest Arcanum required for the spell. My rationale is that the highest Arcanum of the spell is what determines the base effect of the spell; any other Arcana just add to the effect or just fulfil some prerequisite for it. That's how you determine the dice pool so it's more consistent. If multiple Arcana are tied, I'd take whichever of those that would grant fewest amount of Reaches. For example, a mage with Life 4, Matter 5 that wants to turn someone to a pillar of salt (Life 4, Matter 4) would have one free Reach.
