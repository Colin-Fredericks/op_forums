        When required, the Attainment is also considered to have acquired additional spell factors that would incur a penalty (if cast as a spell) up to the Attainment’s lowest prerequisite Arcanum.

        To get an additional spell factor you need a -2 penalty dice.
        So, having the Lowest Arcana involved at level 2 or 4 will give you +1 or +2 secondary spell factors.
        The examples showed in the book backup this, as having a Arcana of 5 involved you can bump +2 spell factors, to get Duration 3 (since it is already 1), and having Arcana 3 involved you only get +1 spell factor and get Duration 2.
