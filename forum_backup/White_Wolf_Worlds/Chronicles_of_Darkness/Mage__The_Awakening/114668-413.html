                    12-06-2019, 10:40 AM
                    Originally posted by Snakesandsuns View Post
                    I was under the impression that you had to pay experience points for merits? These items are merits just like order status, shadow name, contacts, or any of the others which you have to buy with experience. Is there a section I am missing?
                    Your mistake is thinking that the Merits are necessary for having Grimoires or imbued items, but they're not. If you buy the Grimoire Merit you gain a Grimoire, but if you gain a Grimoire through other means (creating it yourself, or pilfering it from a rival mage) you have no reason or obligation to buy the Merit.

                    And, as noted, Merits also serve as a way to determine "Availability" for temporary requisitioning and such.

                    In short, the item isn't the Merit, and gaining the item doesn't force you to buy the Merit.
