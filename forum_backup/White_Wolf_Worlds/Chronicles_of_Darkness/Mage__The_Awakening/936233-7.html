            The thing about Shifting Sands is that there's no Duration-based snapback, you don't inhabit even your near-past self, and the effects on "undone" spells are as near as the spell gets to interacting with Temporal Sympathy through the whole "spells don't start existing before you cast them" aspect.

            Fraying history as it settles may result in "time travel" in a superficially similar fashion to unmaking the established past, but as the errata make clear, one allows the subject to completely alter that branch of the decision tree their timeline branched down with the only remaining consequence of the original history being "this course of events led a Master of Time to rewrite history" and the other sends your altered self back in time to try to change things despite the scars the original timeline has still left on them.*

                                                            Originally posted by Moinen View Post
                                                            I'm not, but you seem to.

                                                            One can only Unveil things that are under Arcana purview. I don't see sound under Life's purview but you say that you Unveil it with Heightened Senses.
                                                            You're the one who mentioned Forces, not me, so let's not get confused on that point. Now, do you hear things with your ears? Yes. Are there things that fall under Life that have better hearing than humans? Yes. Thus using Life you can use an Unveiling technique to observe information (audio or otherwise) that other organisms could potentially perceive. Now, if you used Forces then you can also perceive things that are beyond the ability for things that fall under Life, and you can use Unveiling with Forces to derive sensory information that is the same as what you might be able to perceive via Heightened Senses. Let's not forget that many arcana can actually achieve the same or similar types of effects after all. You might want to argue that gaining sensory information that's similar to another organism via Life is Weaving (Transplanting a property of one organism onto another) and you could. On the other hand Unveiling deals with revealing information that isn't mundanely available to you, which is exactly what Heightened Senses accomplishes hence it can also be Unveiling. You can look at this as similar to the way in which different arcana can achieve similar results, but instead of arcana sometimes practices also can achieve similar results. Usually when using a higher Practice though it's not unreasonable to expect the effect to be more powerful than using a lower level result. For instance using a Weaving version might grant a Condition, or 8 again, etc.
