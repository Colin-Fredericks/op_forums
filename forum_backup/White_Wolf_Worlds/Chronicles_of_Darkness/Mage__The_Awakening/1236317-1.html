                                    ObrimosG'd, if you want more balanced CoD game as crossover friendly in setting as Mage - I propose Beast: The Priordrial. It's highly based on Astral Space ( like parts of Mage ), encourage cooperation with other monsters ( even mechanical ) - and is much more manageable, power-wise, on general. Beast also share with Mage a lot of themes - like Mystery seeking ( for getting new Lairs Chambers or finding Dark Mother ). Also, using one or two Awakened PCs in Beast game is totally fine - and with only 1 or 2 mage characters, you can more easily uncover their drawbacks in stories.

                                    Yes, Beast was secret crossover game I thought Mage was for whole 12+ years - that I always IMAGINED Mage could be, but internal Mage mechanics made them unplayable in long term. Go figure that...
