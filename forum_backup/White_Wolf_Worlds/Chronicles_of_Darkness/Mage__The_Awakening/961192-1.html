        Let’s look at the benefits of joining an order.

        The High Speech merit at one dot for free. Yeah that’s a big help.

        One dot in the Occult Skill for free. Two experience points worth of benefit, also helpful.

        One dot in Order status which grants access to the Order’s special merits. What makes Order status special is that it is basically a modular advantage. Anything you need that the order can provide can be gained with Order status. If you join the Seers of the Throne that dot of Order status also counts as a dot of resources.

        Even Nameless mages get three rotes.

        Four experience points of stuff for free. Thus ends the benefits.

        Drawbacks of joining an Order.

        You now have the Connected condition with regard to that order (mage p 315), or at least that’s how I read it. Basically whatever your order asks you to do you need to try to do it, as inconvenient as that might be. If you’re a Seer you also have to contend with backstabbing peers and getting orders from pitiless inhuman masters. In other words Plot Hooks.

        I can see the appeal of being a nameless mage, honestly. You get to really choose your friends for yourself and focus on your own life. Your cabal can help you when you need friends and even without joining an order a nameless mage can still get status in the Concillium. Just think about it. She has no order so she is ideal for helping other mages settle disputes. Have mages across the Concillium start coming to her as a neutral arbiter. Have the Concillium teach her a rote for Display of Power (Prime 3) and offer training in Empathy and Persuasion. She could easily have Concillium status with no other duties than to preside over duels. What if people want to cheat? Does she accept bribes? What if someone wants to duel a Cabal mate? If she doesn’t play favorites she can have most of the benefits of order status with few of the drawbacks.

        As far as plot hooks go all you have to do is involve the rest of the Cabal to get the Nameless in on the action. Just make it so the Cabal can’t do the assignment without her.
