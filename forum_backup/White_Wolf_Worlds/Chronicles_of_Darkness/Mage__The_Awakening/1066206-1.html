        Depends on the player and depends on the ST.                     My mage is hugely paranoid, it's something that comes up again and again in my game. The social constructs are mainly built to help alleviate fear of betrayal. Still, when you are good friends with someone who can change time, or re-design your memory, how can you not always feel a little suspicious towards them? What spell can you cast to alleviate that fear? If you're scared of your friends, my goodness, what about your enemies? My mage had a car fly out of nowhere, miles away from known danger, in an attempt to crush her like a bug. She had strange puppets spying on her sanctum in the woods. A slightly insane associate was murdered in a safehouse by a mage presumed dead.

                    Paranoid yes, paralyzed no. Mages are bold and overconfident and quick to judge because they need to be to survive. And when they make horrifying mistakes they suck it up and carry on.. fun times.

                    As a disclaimer, when I began playing WoD, many many moons ago, I swore I'd never play mage as I thought it was too complicated and boring. I no longer think so.

                                                            Originally posted by Axelgear View Post
                                                            I've got a PC cabal in my Ausgame that didn't have one character with it until recently. Even then, he couldn't really afford to cover all the spells everyone might have up all the time. Not without risking Paradox.

                                                            Edit: I'd say hiding a Spirit spell from Mage Sight is way more the dominion of Prime, because the part you're hiding isn't any element of the Spirit arcanum. You're hiding that it's magic at all.
                                                            Hiding *from* Magic Sight is definitely Prime (and Death), which is why you can hide any spell no matter what Arcanum using Prime, but what I'm saying is not "hiding from". It's using "hiding a thing belonging to my Arcanum from everything else". You can Veil things so that they are hidden from things that fall under your Arcanum, *or* you can hide something that falls under your Arcanum from anything else. And I don't think there is anything more "of your Arcanum" than spells created by it, don't you think? I mean, you already can target spells with stuff like Hung Spell.

                                                            As for not being able to hide all spells, I'm curious what stops your PC? If it's blank "I can't afford potency to target that number of individual spells", use Area as a Scale instead of flat number of subjects, targeting every single spell in the room.
                                                            Which brings me to another thing: if it's ok for Jake to layer himself with a Mind Shield, and it's ok for a Jane to layer herself with a Ghost Shield, and it's ok for Ruby to layer itself with Wards and Signs, what is so wrong about them saying "Hey guys, I'm doing my yearly renewal of a Mind Shield, drop by our Sanctum and I will Reach so it covers all of us"? Or even "Hell, if I'm investing a Willpower Dot into a Mind Shield, then at least let's make it worth it, MIND SHIELD FOR EVERYONE"?
                                                            I see a lot of artificial barriers here that IMHO create a dissonance with the emergent gameplay coming from the spellcasting rules. Which I find funny, because I have a feeling that people would love Merits that mimick these spells, but would be in arms against functionally identical spells that were Safely Relinquished - which, in practice, turns them into Merits worth one XP that don't fall under protection of sanctity of merits.
