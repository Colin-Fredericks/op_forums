                                        Originally posted by WalkingEye View Post
                                        Can a mage release a spell without losing a willpower dot in their own Oneiros without consequence since there is no risk of Paradox there ever?
                                        Unfortunately not, because Will is the fuel, not the program.

                                        So even if you run your reality bending program in a highly controlled environment, it still needs resources to function. Normally your Gnosis supercomputer does that for you, by very efficiently using your Awakened Will.

                                        But if you cut it off from it, you need to provide some infrastructure for it to run.

                                        So you have two options. First one is to allocate the resources and permanently dedicate a senior programmer from HQ to keep it bug free (relinquishing with a WP dot).

                                        Second option is to just allocate resources, let it run unsupervised forever and see what happens (relinquishing with a WP point).

                                        Either way, you still need something to execute the program. The big advantage is that your Oneiros guarantees it will be bug free forever.
