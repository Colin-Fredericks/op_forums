    Originally posted by Azunth View Post
    Couple of questions regarding interpreting the practices in the new version of Mage.

    One: Can Compelling of Matter Spells be used to lock/unlock things such as doors and windows, open doors and windows, cause a car to start, make a ball or skateboard roll across the ground, causes dishes to fall off shelves and counters, etc? Y'know, minor poltergeist like stuff. My rationale is that Doors can open and lock as "things they would normally do", balls can roll as "things they would normally do", so you can use compelling spells to get these effects. I figured it would be really in theme for the Moros, and help blend together the Necromancer/Alchemist strands.
    Yes

    Two: Could Ruling of Matter spells, with reach, be used to almost TK effects on objects- pull a glass along a counter, cause a door to refuse to open even if it's unlocked, etc. Normally, glasses don't slide along the counter, doors open when unlocked, and rocks don't fly, but ruling allows you to force something to do something in wouldn't normally do under the circumstances. I'm just thinking, a lot of creepy "ghost-story" effects can be done with matter
    Not as a statted spell.

    Three, under the interpretation of the Ruling practice, could you puppet a fresh corpse with a ruling of Death Spell? It's still got muscles and everything, so it's got the parts that would let it move, but it just wouldn't normally move.
    Yes.

    Four: Forces spells let you affect the weather. Does that mean that Ruling and Compelling-level forces magic could be used to create a gust of wind?
    Yes. Also Matter.

    [quote]Five: In "The Creative Arts" preview, a veiling spell that lets one walk past a camera unnoticed is given as an example of a Veiling spell that conceals something from the arcanum's purview. Does that mean that electronics have been moved to under Forces Purview?[/quote[

    "moved"?

    Six: In Mage 1e, there was a 1 dot spell called Alter Conductivity. It was awesome. If I'm interpreting things correctly, should this be a weaving spell now?
    It's a Matter 3 spell now.
