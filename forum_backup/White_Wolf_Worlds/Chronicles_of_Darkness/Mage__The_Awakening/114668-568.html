                                                    I would say a Shielding spell would do the trick. Make it Potency 2 to protect from 2 types of hostile forces (kinetic and heat), spend a Willpower for the extra Potency dice, use concentration to avoid detection, mantra if you find a particularly unobserved or loud corner, AoE to protect everyone in the blast area. Shielding is a very good Practice, since it can no sell mundane dangers under its purview.

                                                    Alternatively, just use a Shielding from fire on the IED from sensory range. It becomes kind of useless if a spark does nothing to the chemicals, and it can be dismissed as being badly put together. Less resource intensive and does not risk breaking points for Sleepers.
