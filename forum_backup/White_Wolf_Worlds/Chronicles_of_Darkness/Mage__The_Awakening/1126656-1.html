    Telekinesis provides a force that can be used to manipulate an object any way that you could by yourself, but it's limited in how much it can lift because you have to assign potency to strength.

    Levitation can only move things, but you can potentially levitate an entire truck without much difficulty (advanced scale means you just need -4 to the roll, instead of - 26 or so).

    They're fundamentally different effects and human/object distinction has absolutely nothing to do with it.

        Originally posted by HardcoreHannes View Post

        I disagree. Pretty much everything you can do with matter can physically be explained via manipulation of the (binding)-forces between atoms. I don't see a single spell in the forces section, which does anything like that. Concerning atoms, Matter is the scalpel and Forces the sledgehammer figuratively.
        Not really, Matter ignores all of the actual energy and forces of such a change. Matter is the "cheat", so to speak, allowing you to simply do on a large scale, such as converting lead into gold. Forces could also turn lead into gold but would have to do so by mucking about with the actual strong force to do the same. Matter is the chemical and the alchemical, Forces is forces and energy. Matter isn't explicable by manipulation of fundamental forces because it explicitly bypass such forces and simply acts on things that fall under the symbolic header of Matter; conversely Forces works on anything under the symbolic header of Forces... which includes forces.

        And Forces most certainly does interact at the subatomic level: all the Electricity spells, all the Heat spells and all the spells that affect Radiation (solidly in Force's wheelhouse, including alpha particles which are basically Helium nuclei). On the other hand, I don't think anyone considers quarks or electrons to be "matter", nor would a single proton in a nucleus be manipulable by Matter (unless it was an H+ ion, I guess).

                                                And all of that is why thinking in terms of Physics is wrong. Mage doesn't work that way, it is all about symbolism. Atoms, linear connect energy, and all that don't matter.

                                                Elfive is correct, I read the spells again and see what he means about Telekinesis. It weaves the ambient kinetic force into a controllable ball that can then be used to manipulate other things. It doesn't have a withstand rating for this reason, you never target a person with the spell, but you could utilize the force you are controlling to target a person. You are Weaving the property 'controllable' into the force and then you can control it.

                                                Levitation is Patterning because you are patterning the forces that exist in the target from being static, hold them in place forces to move around freely forces.

                                                Both the witch's broom and witch flying alone examples would be levitation, the broom would just be an Imbued item.

                                                            I think grafting together something with Forces + Life and/or Mind might be a good way to put together a Duration based flight spell, whereby a person controls their vectors either by bodily instinct (like a superhero) or thought (like Magneto).

                                                            You might also use Potency to increase maximum speed, either by a flat table or by some modification of your base speed and use free fall damage rules for any collisions that might occur - unless you throw in another forces spell, like a shield, to help you mitigate any unforeseen crashes.

                                                            Not sure if this is at all useful for your character, but I think something like flight should be rudimentary for a mage. It just might be primitive when compared to all other modes of transportation.

                                                            EDIT: I'd also like to add that one form of interpreting gravity is as acceleration.
