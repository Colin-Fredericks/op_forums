    The Crystal Navigators, a Mastigos Legacy of free-spirited pirates

    01-14-2026, 10:27 PM
    Origins

    "It's not about making the right choice. It's about making a choice and making it right."
    -J.R. Rim

    Nickname:Skippers
    Parentage:Mastigos, Adamantine Arrow, Guardians of the Veil, Free Council

    Background: The origins of the skippers trace back to the pirate republic of Nassau, to a picaroon called Storm Kraken. The sole survivor of his cabal's slaughter by the Hegemony Ministry, he would dedicate himself to combating them everywhere he could, eventually rising to become the captain of his own cabal.

    It is said Storm Kraken discovered the Crystal Sea while searching for a way to stay one step ahead of the Seers, shaping his Attainments to assert his free will while cleaving to the picaroon’s ideals of personal discipline.

    While the Company of the Codex were notoriously bad record keepers, tales of his daring exploits are still told in the Caribbean to this day, in songs and legends. They speak of his voyages to unusual Verges and Atlantean ruins, encounters with cryptids, of his legendary tools (such as his compass and map) and his cunning defeats of agents of the Throne hidden in the crown's service.

    We do not know how his career ended, but stories say he was searching for the pirate king, the supposed archmaster that created the Codex to champion the cause of freedom. Some even claim that, in his later years, he and his crew had joined the pirate king's fleet. The truth of these tales is lost in the tides of history.

    What is known for certain is that Storm Kraken was a prolific mentor, initiating disciples freely amongst those who would stand up against tyranny, as well as those seeking to take a greater amount of personal accountability. In this way his teachings spread across the seven seas, enduring to the present day.

    Appearances: The skippers recruit people from all walks of life, the most important thing for prospective members is to demonstrate a sense of discipline and personal responsibility.

    The skippers also have a passion for treasure hunts, with their old journals, maps, tools, Sanctums and even old ships containing clues leading towards Daimonomika. Some mentors prefer to organize one as a way to test the prospective member's character, guile and adventurous spirit.

    Because of their studies of divination, members can often be found carrying various divination tools on the field. Within their Sanctums one can often find a collection of journals, which allow them to view the author's choices in their own words. Due to their efforts to understand the symbols of the Crystal Sea, it's common for members to delve into sailing, navigation and oceanography.

    Whenever attending a formal Consilium or Legacy event, the skippers tend to arrive dressed as pirates, their clothes bearing their cabal's sigil as their flag, a gesture meant to honor Storm Kraken's tale.

    Doctrine

    "True freedom is impossible without a mind made free by discipline."
    -Mortimer Jerome Adler

    Prerequisites: Time 2, Dream ●●

    Initiation: The skippers have developed different divination tools to help with the initial attunement to the flow of the Crystal Sea. In the past, the most common one used to be a type of geomantic compass, utilizing Supernal symbols. In modern times, these symbols are instead placed in a type of compass whose design is inspired by an alethiometer.

    The prospective member focuses on a goal or impending decision, using their divinatory knowledge to, over the course of a scene, align the tool to query about it. Once this is accomplished, they receive a prophetic vision, which contains new insights about it. It is the process of interpreting this vision, often with the aid of their mentor, and deliberately choosing the next steps that completes their initiation.

    Organization: The skippers hold no formal hierarchy. Whenever they come together to deal with a major issue, a leader is elected by a simple majority vote, by merit of their skill and experience. When discussing particularly important matters, members gather in pirate cave Sanctums of present or past members.

    Mentors and students often have a friendly relationship, sharing exploits, drinking together, discussing personal experiences and telling tales of other skippers.

    Theory: The future is unwritten, its path created by choices yet unmade. Countless shards of possibility float around us in an ocean of crystal, drawn together by our decisions to compose the mosaic of history.

    Through the arts of divination we are able to navigate the tides and currents of the Crystal Sea, allowing us to predict the most likely outcome of our actions. Yet divination by itself is not enough, for the waters of the Crystal Sea are constantly in flux, drawn in new directions by people's passions and attachments. In order to fully grasp the impact of our choices, as well as those of others, we must understand the emotional drives that shape them. It is by balancing logical prediction and emotional understanding that we can make more enlightened choices.

    Master the arts of divination, seek insight into people's choices, so you may gain true understanding of the Crystal Sea. Only then will you have full ownership of your decisions, and be able to chart a course towards a brighter future.

    Magic

    “Truly successful decision-making relies on a balance between deliberate and instinctive thinking."
    -Malcolm Gladwell

    Ruling Arcanum: Time
    Yantras: Navigation or divining tools (+1, +2 if incorporating perfected materials), journals (+1, +2 if relevant to the spell), flowing water (+1, +2 if large body of water or if enough to cause an environmental tilt, +3 if both), glass or crystal (+1, +2 if perfected or prevalent in the environment).

    Oblations: Reading or keeping a journal, performing non-magical fortune-telling, people watching, navigating, sea travel.

    Attainments

    First: Opportunity's Tide
    Prerequisites: Initiation

    The skippers reads the tides of the Crystal Sea around them, revealing shards of potential futures starting to coalesce. They uncover [Time] clues or statements about something occurring in the area or about a pertinent issue, which may initially seem completely nonsensical. Characters may attempt to unravel them using a Wits + Investigation roll or with research efforts. By correctly interpreting them, they can gain insights they might not otherwise have.

    This is a Potency primary Knowing of Time, with Reach assigned to instant use.

    Optional: Space 1

    The skipper opens their senses to the currents of the Crystal Sea, seeing them ebb and flow around them. By focusing their senses on a subject for a turn, they receive a vision of a significant event that will befall them sometime in the next [Time] days, which is predisposed to be a warning of danger, but may also present an opportunity. Skippers are known to use these visions to help keep themselves and their allies one step ahead of their foes, as well as always being ready to make the most of fair winds and following seas.

    This is a Potency primary Unveiling of Space and Time, with Reach assigned to instant use.

    Second: Intuition's Helming
    Prerequisites: Time 2

    The skipper trusts their gut, being uncannily good at feeling situations out. By examining the ripples a choice or subject has on the Crystal Sea, they are able to predict whether they will create waves or a storm.

    This replicates Momentary Flux, with Reach assigned to instant use and sensory range.

    Optional: Space 2

    The skipper protects their choices and those of their allies from being predicted, preventing any prying diviners from interfering with them. After spending a scene spinning a false tale about the subject, such as writing a journal entry, making an audio log or even telling a story at a tavern, the Crystal Sea responds in kind. Its waters shift to hide the subject's history, drawing shards of false possibility closer, spinning a tall tale fit to deceive all but the most clever temporal scrutiny.

    This replicates Shield of Chronos, with Reach assigned to advanced duration and conjunctional Space 2 allowing them to create a false series of events.

    Third: Sagacity's Charting
    Prerequisites: Time 3, Dream ●●●

    The skipper follows the flow of the Crystal Sea to predict its most likely course. It is by using this ability with guile that they can account for the consequences of their decisions, as well as anticipate an opponent’s most probable course of action.

    This replicates Divination, with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range and broader questions.

    Optional: Space 3

    The skipper studies a subject’s current route through the Crystal Sea, uncovering the sympathetic waypoints they use and how they orient their journey. Should they wish to uncover specific connections, they are able to divine the right ones to examine.

    This replicates Correspondence, with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range and understanding the emotional character of the connections. With conjunctional Time 1, they can uncover any specific connections they are looking for.

    Fourth: Hindsight's Tacking
    Prerequisites: Time 4, Dream ●●●●

    No matter how experienced the skipper, navigating the Crystal Sea is a challenging task. Whenever they sail into stormy waters, the skipper is able to seize the momentum of their history and leverage it to turn back time, using their foreknowledge to chart a new course.

    This replicates Shifting Sands with Reach assigned to instant use, advanced duration and going back a scene. A point of mana is used to activate Time in a Bottle.

    Optional: Space 4

    Passion creates powerful waves within the Crystal Sea, altering the course of one's choices.The skipper can selectively quell these waters even from the other side of the ocean, causing alliances to crumble, vengeance to be extinguished, riots to be dispersed, lifelong attachments to fade and unhealthy connections to be abandoned.

    This replicates Cut Threads with Reach assigned to instant use, sympathetic range and advanced scale. A single point of mana is used in total to activate Time in a Bottle, Sympathetic Range and Everywhere, per Legacy rules.

    Fifth: Earnestness’ Beacon
    Prerequisites: Time 5, Dream ●●●●●

    The greatest skippers can follow the flow of the Crystal Sea's undertow, seeing the potential timelines stretching out from the present, examining the possibilities of the future to achieve a greater clarity about their choices than ever before.

    With conjunctional Space 4, they also gain insight about the emotional context of any foreseen conversations, allowing them to skillfully navigate discussions, reducing the number of Doors of their interlocutors by [Time].

    This replicates Prophecy with Reach assigned to instant use and sensory range, with conjunctional Space 4 allowing social interactions to be affected.

    Optional: Space 5

    The skipper reaches out to the waters of the Crystal Sea, summoning forth tempest and eddies, torrential rainfalls and tsunamis. By carefully orchestrating the storm of emotions, they can create new passions to light their subject's route, causing them to change course.

    Undying loyalty can be created, deep bonds of trust woven, burning hatred sparked, alliances forged in advance and even teleportation routes prepared.

    This replicates Create Sympathy with Reach assigned to instant use, sympathetic range and advanced scale. With conjunctional Time 4, the skipper can design the connection to be resilient to future changes, causing it to be Lasting and unfading. A single point of mana in total is used to activate Time in a Bottle, Sympathetic Range and Everywhere, per Legacy rules.

    ​What were you thinking design wise ?

    The first Attainment is inspired by the Automatic Writing merit, it allows the skipper to read the tides of the Crystal Sea and see incoming storms, provided they can properly read the signs.

    The first optional Attainment is based on the Omen Trance Numen. It starts weaker than it, but eventually it can look even farther ahead. The main benefit is that there is no need to spend Essence or make rolls to activate it, and since it's an Unveiling, they need only focus their senses on a subject to receive a vision. It allows the skipper to be aware of any upcoming trouble or opportunities.

    The second Attainment represents intuition, the skipper's ability to make a quick call when there is uncertainty.

    The second optional Attainment leverages the pirate's penchant for telling tall tales and legends to protect them, and their cabal, from other diviners.

    The third Attainment is a fundamental part of their toolkit, allowing them to examine the most likely future in a focused and detailed manner. It lets them evaluate their own choices, work on a daring plan and predict their opponents.

    The third optional Attainment lets them understand what are the strongest attachments a subject has, as well as what they mean to them, giving them an idea of who they are and what influences their choices. If they want to find a specific relationship, such as the subject to their cabal's or other allies, or their connection to a place, they can divine the right thread of sympathy to examine. When combined with the main Attainment, it helps build a bigger picture of the subject's current and impending choices.

    The fourth main Attainment fosters bold action and guile, allowing them to have a panic button if things go poorly and enabling time rewind shenanigans. The name is based on a sailing maneuver, where a ship going against the wind uses its momentum to change direction, so that they sail with the wind at their back.

    The fourth optional Attainment allows them to shatter powerful bonds, erode alliances, wither vendettas and even disperse entire crowds. It can be done subtly at a distance, allowing the skipper to deal with any passions that could grow into a storm.

    The capstone allows them to examine the myriad possibilities of the future, giving them more insights into their choices than ever before. The ability to reduce Doors in a conversation represents a swashbuckler's charm.

    The optional capstone allows them to forge alliances, foster mutinies, set up for any situation they have foreseen and even build teleportation routes (similar to a pirate's smuggling routes). Its effects are very subtle, not even triggering the Peripheral Sight, but potentially very powerful.

    The key design phrase for the Legacy was "navigating our choices". Originally I wanted to make a future seeing Legacy, but was unsatisfied with how the themes and mechanics came together with the Keepers of the Mansus, but I felt the Attainments were on the right track. So I decided to revise the concept from the ground up. I figured that the way Mastigos would approach a prophetic Legacy was to use it to boldly push the boundaries of free will and personal responsibility as far as they could.

    The idea for the Crystal Sea came from how the flow of time is visually represented in Quantum Break. CalvinSiler pointed out that the themes of navigating the sea and freedom aligned with pirates, so I decided to build on those ideas.

    The nickname skippers indicate an informal sailing term for those in charge of a ship, usually earned as a recognition of their merit and experience. They may be the owner of the boat or its captain, but not always. In some naval traditions, such as the Dutch, it is the term for the captain. It's also a term pirates would have used as a recognition of someone's skill. In this way, it implies the skipper is in charge of their own free will and that, should they assume a leadership role, it would be due to merit.

    When considering their origin, I wanted it to resonate more with the themes of freedom and self determination. So I created a founder who fought for those ideals, even while living a life of adventure, and drew a connection with the lore of the Company of the Codex. It would allow myths and legends to be used for plot hooks, or to delve into the Mysteries of the Codex.

    When thinking about the kind of members they would recruit, it made sense that they would seek out anyone who inherited some of the spirit of the picaroons. When CalvinSiler mentioned he visualized someone finding the old journal of a skipper and sending them into a treasure hunt for a Daimonomika, I thought it was a very fun idea and incorporated it into the initiation. Apart from that, studying divination and the ocean would probably be common interests. Their formal attire is a homage to their origin.

    The initiation takes the Dream merit, a starting point for divinations, and combines it with divinatory navigation tools, such as the Mao Kun map from Pirates of the Caribbean or the alethiometer from His Dark Materials. It is in interpreting the divinatory trance and taking decisive action that the initiation is completed.

    Since pirates had a very informal hierarchy, I decided the skippers would as well, with mentors and students seeing themselves as fellow adventurers and drinking buddies. The simple voting system is a reference to how leaders were elected in the pirate nation of Nassau. Their tradition of gathering in pirate caves for important events is a homage to the Brethren Court from Pirates of the Caribbean.

    The yantras and oblations were designed by considering how Storm Kraken would connect with the Crystal Sea in his life as a pirate. The oblations were also designed to drive skippers to be in situations where they would uncover people's impending choices.

    That concludes the design notes, hope you had as much fun reading them as I had writing them. A huge thanks to CalvinSiler for all his feedback.
