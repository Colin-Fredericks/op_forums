    Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
    "Serpent", however, is a term that has connotations that are distinct from "Snake", and in particular is used in modern and particularly pop-cultural conceptions of Christianity where in snakes are used in demonic-or-otherwise-evil contexts, and you yourself seemed to be hung up on the Edenic Serpent and it's darker thematics and tropes that you seemed to be having trouble seperating yourself from.

    These things stack on top of each other, and sometimes a shift of words, even if they strictly mean the same thing, can be just the to help a person break out of the rut they were trying to get out of, and firmly establishing what a thing is not alongside what it is can help to fully make the mental divide needed to make progress.
    About the only time I hear the term "snake" applied in a positive manner is to say something is as fast as one, which is usually followed up with "and twice as ornery". Every other time tends to be comparisons and allusions that have equal connotation with "serpent". "Trecherous snake", snake-in-the-grass", and "snake-like grace" come to mind.

    ​That being said, I view snakes and trickster-mentors in a fairly positive light, so I didn't intend for my interpretation to come off as dark.

                                                            The peripheral sight triggers when anything supernatural is activated within sensory range. All of those are supernatural effects, so it triggers. The problem then is knowing the details.

                                                            It could be a vampire mesmerizing someone to feed, perhaps its a deliberate move by a Banisher to see who reacts to the bait or a spirit just used an Influence. Who knows, all you know is that something supernatural happened around here (unless its a Veiling effect). Which is really nice because it means mages will pick up on very subtle uses that others will not.
