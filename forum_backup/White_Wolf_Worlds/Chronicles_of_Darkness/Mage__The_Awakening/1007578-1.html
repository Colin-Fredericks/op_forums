                                            Originally posted by Axelgear View Post
                                            I didn't say you can't create/grant Merits with a Making spell. I said you don't have to be. But you're right, a Making spell doesn't have to pop people into existence.
                                            I just wanted to point out that those Merits requires Making since Allies was specifically mentioned in the post you replied to. But you're right about social Merits in general.

                                            My take on Merits granted through spells: To increase Merits requires just two dots in most cases (Supernatural Merits are an exception, see below). To create Merits can range from three dots to five dots depending on the Merit.
                                            Holistic Awareness should require a third dot spell since you basically just increase your knowledge, just like with Enhance Skill. Same thing with Area of Expertise and Interdisciplinary Specialty.
                                            Fighting Styles should probably require a fourth dot spell. The Familiar Merit (if bought naturally) is still probably caused by a mentor casting the Familiar spell so that's a Merit that always is caused by a fourth dot spell.
                                            Supernatural Merits can be bestowed through a fifth dot Fate spell. The same spell is also required to increase the dot rating of those Merits.

                                            Originally posted by HardcoreHannes View Post
                                            I am aware of this, but while I also agree that it doesn't seem to make sense for the game in general, why would I cripple my ability to cast awakened magic by diverting my manapool into essence and mana just to use some crappy Numina, while I could just use my cool awakened magic for the same purposes for free. It just seems like a lot of trouble for no benefit.
                                            There are a few advantages as others have noted, and if you have a Familiar or an otherwise bound Spirit nearby you can always cast Channel Essence whenever you're about to use a Numen. And if you ever find yourself low on Mana, just top it off with Essence and you're not completely helpless if your Ruling Arcana were to be less than helpful in that particular situation.

                                            I'm still not personally sold on granting Numina through spells, but if it works in your games, it can be quite a useful resource when Awakened magic for one reason or another doesn't cut it. It would certainly be nice to have some Numina while in a Sleeper's Oneiros, for example.
