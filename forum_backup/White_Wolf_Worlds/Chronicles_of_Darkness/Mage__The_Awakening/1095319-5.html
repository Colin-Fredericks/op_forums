    We played another 4 sessions and I thought I'd share an update:

    The PCs decided to track the Red Word's "HQ" via the kidnapped little girl's Perfect Recall of her brief abduction. They narrowed things down to a 5 block area and the Obrimos Perfected Adept in the cabal used Web of Life to find unusual clusters of people in the area. He found an unusually large number of people gathered in the basement of a Coptic church in the Back Bay area of Boston.

    Infiltrating the church led them to Zee, Annie and 4 other cultists about to perform a human ritual sacrifice and cannibalism ritual on a middle-aged woman strapped to a large table.

    I took some of the suggestions here and all of these cultists had increased Stamina via Honing the Form and a Potency 4 Wards and Signs. Half of them had guns and the other half had melee weapons.

    I expected this to be a tough fight for two Disciples, but the Moros surprised me by Shaping Shadows to plunge the room into complete darkness instead of targeting the cultists directly, while the Obrimos gave both PCs Nightvision. Then the Moros improvised a Weaving spell with area scale we called Shadow Binding that created a solid blanket of shadow that entangled most of the cultists for half the fight, while the Obrimos unleashed multiple Telekinetic Strikes to devastating effect [since the earlier spells had been immune from the effects of Wards and Signs I actually forgot to subtract their potency from the TK, but honestly it would not have mattered much to the outcome of the fight as the Obrimos likely would have simply entered melee with his devastating Subak instead.]

    It didn't take long for the cult to realize they needed to retreat, so the leader held up a fetish [open mandible child's skull] that activated a Gateway into Shadow and they retreated, except for Zee who was still trapped under the shadow blanket.

    They rescued the woman and called Maggie Yang (reporter contact) to come and help get her to safety and the Moros promised to give her the exclusive if she helped the woman get to a hospital while they take Zee for interrogation.

    With the help of Elegua (Special Media Group) after agreeing to a mutual defense pact between their cabals, they managed to learn about the Cult and their worship of the Prince, but not the nature of the Prince. The Mastigos PC that plays sporadically went with the Obrimos to Zee's apartment to retrieve his computer. There they briefly fought some cultists and drove them off and managed to retrieve Zee's partially erased unusable computer.

    They Psychic Programmed Zee to turn himself into the BPD and identify some of his fellow cultists and admit to his crimes and gave him a Memory Hole regarding the Cabal.

    After getting back to The Loft (Sanctum), the Obrimos reconstructed the computer's OS with a Forces Perfecting Spell and managed to retrieve 3 Fragments of the Prince, his mother's address in Waltman's Neck (coastal fishing village), and some emails from Wendigo about a Summer Solstice feast soon to be held at Waltman's Inn.

    They didn't immediately take the bait and decided to research Khonsu Tohut (The Prince) more. They found one of the 3 Fragments in a book published by the publishing house that Zee edits for. By looking through the other published books they managed to find 8 more Fragments. With the help of Adgos (Brotherhood of Ineffable Truth) they figured out that the Fragments tell some kind of story that reads like The Bible. The found out many names for the Prince, but still have no clue as to its true nature.

    A day after Zee turned himself in, they got a call from Maggie Yang that he disappeared from his cell. The cabal figured he was rescued by cultists using the Gateway Fetish (and they're right).

    They finally decided to go to Waltman's neck to investigate Waltman's Inn. They were constantly followed by Raven spirits loyal to Wendigo, but no one in the cabal has Spirit so they just get the occasional PMS ping that is annoying them.

    They met Jules Bagron and ate a hardy lunch at the Inn and he spiked their beer with a sedative. Having had more than everyone else, the Moros fell asleep unexpectedly in their room after lunch. The Obrimos suspected foul play and prepared to cast Cleanse the Body on the cabal when the session ended ....

                                            The Spirit Arcanum is Wendigo’s major asset. Him not using it would be contrived, especially since you’ve established that Chenoo help him. However, if you’re worried that the PCs won’t be able to handle them note the following:

                                            • Gossamer Touch (Prime 2) will let Mages strike against Spirits, even if they remain invisible. Such a fight would force players to be creative.
                                            • Although the Mages can’t see Spirits, Spirits can see them. If the PCs present evidence of Wendigo’s duplicity, they can convince the Chenoo to stand back, or even to help.
                                            • There can be a Verge within the Sanctum leading to a specifically “cultivated” area of the Shadow. The PCs would be in unfamiliar territory, but could actually see their opponents. You could also show how Wendigo’s practices affected the Shadow. On that note, you can start with something subtle on the Material side. I recommend reading this: Link
                                            • Seriously, read this…
                                            • Read it!

                                            As for oddities and tactics within the Sanctum:

                                            • Let’s assume that Wendigo can create one-shot Imbued Items (Prime 3) and used it to leave traps. Touching the wrong thing can activate any of the spells Wendigo knows.
                                            • “Damned if you do, damned if you don’t” scenarios. There may be traps, trials and gateways that can be safely interacted with only by cannibals. At the same time there may be effects that make cannibals especially vulnerable. The characters can choose to take a sip of each others blood to proceed, but what happens if the slip into a Left-Handed vision quest?
                                            • In fact, if you make Wendigo’s Sanctum into a sort of training gauntlet for the Cult, you can excuse many Dungeon-crawly elements. There can be still-bleeding messages cut into hides of live animals, or forming from writhing maggots, or heard in a drug-induced haze – all clues or lessons for supplicants.
                                            • If Wendigo adopted the Jade Pendant Legacy, he might be trying to transform a captive materialized Spirit – something the prisoner is unhappy about. Will the PCs free the Spirit or will they be discouraged by its obvious mutations?
                                            • The Sanctum may be guarded by Claimed animals, people or objects. The second option may prompt the PCs to disable the enemy for further exorcism, instead of hurting them. The third option gives you a neat visual of animate sculptures, hedge-animals (like in the Shining) or plastic flamingos.
                                            • You can place Fetishes laying around – ready to be send to the Cultists with instruction manuals. The PCs know how to use them, but don’t know what Spirits are trapped within and what the side-effects might be.
                                            • Also, whatever tactic The Red Word Cult could use, Wendigo can do too. You can refresh your memory on that old post: Link

                                                    So both my games have had sessions since my last post. I'll probably give more details later, but I thought I would share how the attempt by the enhanced Red Word cultists to take out the cabal went.

                                                    The session started with Wards and Signs 5 falling over the Inn. This never really mattered for the fight. For some reason the PCs just completely changed tactics and didn't try any AoE spells, even though they didn't know the Inn was protected. The ping to their PMS did put them on alert though. They thought about trying to leave by the window, but the gathering storm coming in off the Atlantic changed their minds. They thought it was part of the attack, but it was merely there to set the mood.

                                                    The Obrimos used Web of Life and realized that a group of humans were making their way upstairs to them. He waited until they were in sight on the landing coming up the stairs to attempt dispellation of their personal Wards and Signs 4, but he failed.

                                                    The Moros was determined to end the fight against the cultists (all Sleepwalkers, btw) decisively by attempting Ghost Gate on all of them. In our game it's Withstood by Stamina if targeted explicitly. The cultists were buffed with Honing the Form and all had Stamina 5, which when combined with W&S gave them Withstands of 6. The Moros assumed that 6 Potency would do it and the spell failed. He had Gnosis 3 + Death 3 + WP 3 + 1 Tool = 10 dice -6 for +3 Potency =4. He used his free Reach for instant, then +1 to target them directly, +1 Sensory, + 1 switch Primary, + 1 for A-Duration and +1 for 5 targets. I was surprised to see the Paradox Pool hit 10 - 2 Dedicated tool - 3 Mana spent = 5 dice. He ended up with 2 R-Bashing after containing what I rolled. It was a Scene-ending spell and I was impressed he could possibly pull it off, even though it fizzled due to Withstand considerations.

                                                    They fired a round of shots at the cabal to varying degrees of success and then Agdos turned their guns into liquid metal. They tried again with melee weapons and a full charge, but she did the same State Change again rendering their weapons useless, so they ran, but not before 3 Chenoo materialized to attack with their talons.

                                                    The Obrimos cast Kinetic Blow and started tearing through the cannibal spirits like butter, but they were enough of a distraction that the Red Word escaped.

                                                    It was a fun fight, but I learned that Sleepwalkers, even buffed and armed to the teeth were not a much challenge for 3 Mages. On the other hand, I learned that Paradox could be a thing at Gnosis 3+ if they aren't careful, particularly as combat gets into later rounds.

                                                    I'm still prepping for the big confrontation at Wendigo's Sanctum with a lot of the ideas in this thread. I'll let you know how it goes.

                                                        I'm not 100% certain, but I think "Wards and Signs" over the Inn should have prevented "State Change" from working (maybe "Kinetic Blow" as well). AoE Spells affect all valid targets within their sphere of influence, for as long as they stay within that sphere of influence. This means that to stand a chance against the Cultists, the PCs would have needed to figure out what's going on and lure them out of the Inn. Considering that they didn't, it might have been a good idea to ignore that Spell anyway, so it all turned for the best. It could have been brutal otherwise.

                                                        Speaking of which, who cast the AoE Spell? Did they have Awakened support on the scene?
