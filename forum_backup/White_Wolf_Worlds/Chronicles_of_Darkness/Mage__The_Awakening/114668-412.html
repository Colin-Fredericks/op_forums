    Originally posted by Therian View Post
    What type of Mind spell would let you enter an unwilling Mage’s Oneiros and what would be the advantage of that over using Mind spells to get information or change things?

    I think you would need a Weaving to force them into the meditative trance so you could follow them in. Weaving is enough to open the gates of the Arcanum's related phenomenons.

    The advantage is simple, discretion. For example, if you convince the goetia responsible for a memory that locking it up will be in the mage's best interests, they will, which leaves no trace (provided you wiped any traces of your Nimbus after casting the entrance spell). Convincing their daimon could make their Obsessions change. Empowering or humiliating certain goetia will alter their weight on the mage's mind. Better yet, alter the data for an important memory or flee into the Temenos with it. Altering the subject manually is a lot more work, but it can have very powerful and long lasting effects.

    New experiences are the font of creativity, when seeking inspiration, break your routine.

                                        Originally posted by Prometheus View Post

                                        By the way, did you mean by the way you answered that Attainments receive benefits from restrictions? That's a new information for me. I only remember the section on Attainments stating that you can slightly modify their scope and that overall they shouldn't have their power level changed too much from the baseline. Do you mean that by restricting one thing, you are allowed to increase another aspect of the Attainment in power? I guess that would make sense given how the Attainments are defined.
                                        Granting an extra Reach in exchange for limiting a spell factor or effect is common for homebrew Attainments (because many people find the number of Reaches very underwhelming due to them being tied to the Arcanum prerequisite). I'm not saying house rule because the Attainment creation guidelines are only guidelines that can explicitly be ignored, but it's also not an option presented in the book.

                                        As for restricting Blessings; as written they are Supernal spells that the Proximus is able to cast. What you're looking for seem to be closer to internalised abilities that they're able to activate. I would design those as Supernatural Merits with the Proximus Dynasty as a prerequisite (much like how the limited Attainments are unique to a Legacy) or bake it into their Curse, somehow.
