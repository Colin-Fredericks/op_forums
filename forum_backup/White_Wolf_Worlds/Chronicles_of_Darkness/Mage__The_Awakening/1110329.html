    Ley Lines Cause Me Physical Pain

    07-01-2017, 04:31 PM
    Alright so I am desperately trying to figure out Ley Lines, Nodes, and related stuff. Prime Magic seems to be pinned on Ley Lines and changing them but as far as I can see they do practically nothing. There seems to be no benefit to them existing for the Prime Mage in questions. Yes you can have your Electric Kettle have limitless power, never break, and grow your own tea leaves in no time at all (Mind you that is on a node with 3 different Vulgar keywords, and having a prime mage tweak them)... but how does that help me feel like a part of the story? How does that make it feel like I am getting any bang for my buck?

    Moving on from that I love the line on page 241 "Spells cast at a ley line may harvest its Resonance" WHAT DOES THIS MEAN? Does it mean I can use free mana? If so WHY NOT SAY THAT? Does it mean I get some sort of a Yantra? If so what kind? Resonance gives essence normally so can my spells spend that essence, if so WHAT DOES THAT MEAN?

    Moving on from there Nodes exist...

    And then Hallows give you mana, cool, and if you spend that mana you can use a Persona Yantra for a whopping +1... look out spell casting rolls here it comes! Now I understand that having that Mana available is AWESOME, and I am not trying to down play that... its actually a thing my current prime mage is having trouble with because I didn't start with a Hallow, but other than Hallows what do Ley Lines do? And beyond that what does the ability to manipulate them actually mean/do mechanically speaking because I cant imagine Onyx Path not applying some sort of mechanics to what they do here.

      Leylines primarily do 3 things:
      - Influence the local environment in line with their Resonance
      - Create Nodes which can be channelled for benefits as listed on Page 242
      - Create Nodes which when combined with a Hallow generate Resonant Tass

      Really though, their primary benefits are their secondary Node elements.
      I also think (while I can't find where it's said) that a Leyline can count as an appropriate Environmental Yantra given its Resonance.

      Also Prime is hardly pinned on Leylines. That's one tiny thing that the Arcanum can affect. And one might I add that's not very useful in pro-active terms.
      Tass-constructs, Truth, Counterspelling-aides, Dispelling, and Ephemeral Enchantments and so forth are infinitely more helpful.

      Still, when you need to build an Outback Sanctum, and don't want to keep having to buy more petrol or set up a solar farm, being able to get all your power needs from a nearby Node can be a godsend. Same with the Space and Time benefits when fighting/moving through a known area.

                  I will confess, resonance has always been a little bit tricky for me as well. The reason that it's tricky is because it's not really a mechanical element, and it's not really defined well as a narrative element. Examples of usage is not really something they go into where I've read. So - allow me to clear this up and try to help define it for you. Keep in mind - this is my inferance, and it may not end up the same as other's perceptions.

                  Resonance, from what I've been able to infer, is a 'quality', usually applied to an entity - any entity really, be it an object, person, spell, or ephemeral substance such as essence. All things have their own resonance, and most things tend to take upon a resonance similar to the objects and enviroment around them. If you have ever entered into a room that felt cold, even though it was clearly high summer - or touched an object that simply -felt- like someone, then you have experienced resonance.

                  Resonance is important when defining things which are not defined with the usual senses. A mage who casts a spell has a 'resonance' to their magic. My assumption would be that using a leyline to harvest it's resonance would allow you to paint another resonance onto your magic, or onto objects or other things.

                  For an example: Say you have a leyline in a glade. The leyline is a comforting place - because it's resonance is 'comforting'. A mage uses prime to harvest the resonance of that area, and uses it to paint over the resonance over a knife. Normally, knives are not something people are comfortable around - but for whatever reason, there is now something 'comforting' about that knife. What applications that may have may differ upon the beholder of the knife.

                      So, my take on ley lines specifically, I assume that when you change the Resonance of a thing it changes to fit that Resonance as nearly as possible, as quickly as possible. So running a Resonance: Justice ley line through a Resonance: Corruption area will flood that area with sudden spikes in police attention or protests as people get fed up with the corruption. Eventually the place may be just be Justice resonant even if you let the ley line revert to its natural course, but it also won't have that Justice supernaturally supported. I also have this feeling that ley lines might be a way to end run the usual area factor restrictions by dragging a point on the line really far off course before pinning it though that would require ley lines to be able to stretch indefinitely. Patterning might be able to create ley line "tributaries", and Making can probably make ley lines wholesale.

                      It's definitely a time and resource intensive task, but Prime can probably do a lot to subtley manipulate the world around a Mage to be something he prefers without bothering with the haggling of spirits or the metaphysical mutilation of identity and purpose granted by Mind and Fate. Essentially, the Obrimos deal directly with the laws of Fallen reality by rewriting them rather than haggling or working on the individual level. It really works with their innate problem of dogmatism.

                                      What is it? Resonance is the nature of any given thing---which eidos is represented by most things in an area. (In 1e, it was more specifically the nature of the thing in question and everything mages manipulated was different forms of resonance. Mana was the quintessence on which everything was built, so different things were simply different resonances in that specific substrate.)

                                      So, you can get that +1 Personal Yantra for free by spending mana of a fitting resonance. (+1 is a bit lackluster, in my opinion, but you can also get that +1 environment bonus out of the same Hallow.)

                                      Nodes have those nice overtly magical effects that you noted. These are just the intersections of ley lines. I'm considering house ruling that these scale based on power and number of lines intersecting.

                                      Resonance determines the essence generated by the area in question and affects the region. This is agonistic, you see. Just changing the resonance does little, on its own. It gently pushes people and events into the new form, but the increasing essence means that the spiritual side begins to shift as well. When the spirits get strong enough, they will start using their Influences to manipulate things and the effects of the resonance shift become much more powerful.

                                      This makes this both an extremely slow and extremely powerful way to alter the world.

                                      Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.

                                              Nodes and Hallows are easy to define but to nail the OP's question I think we should first define the ley lines more

                                              What does a ley line look like under spells or the various degrees of mage sight?
                                              What is the shape of a Ley Line?
                                              Is it an actual line or can it be other shapes (like a countour plot)?
                                              Is the event horrison thin or wide and what are the boundaries of the phenomenon? (need to be on same room. same block or same city to be affected?)
                                              Is it of continous shape or not? (In a ley line connecting London with Manchester. can the ley line stop existing close to Nottingham?)
                                              With high or low curvature? (straight lines from A to B or can take turns and fold)
                                              Are they close to a planet's surface? (or are there ley lines only accessible by flight?)
                                              What is their direction, can they start from earth and point towards the sun or the emptyness of space?

                                              Since ley lines also change over time, I think ley lines might be better defined not by lines but by hypershapes (like Tesseracts, 4D triangles, hyperspheres etc)
