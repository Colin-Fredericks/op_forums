                            Think of it like this: A Supernal spell isn't so much a thing you create as it is you giving local rewrite control to a set of Supernal symbol interactions you create in your Imago. When you cast a spell to produce a given effect, it rewrites that local reality to produce the effect of the Imago. If you cast a second spell on top of that, you're not essentially changing anything; you're just recreating the first local rewrite, so the results are the same. In effect, your target is the same underlying physical reality.

                            Long and short of it: Supernal spells do not stack; only the most potent effect manifests.
