            Chapter 60
            8/29/2025

            <Full session was a short chapter this last week. Lots of prep.>

            With Conquest gone the cabal decided to turn to other things, Abraham de Moivre. Maxwell contacted Uriel and asked to meet outside the Athenaeum where he informed her he had an Abyssal manifestation he wanted to kill. She cast a spell and sighed but agreed to help. He wanted to go immediately but she said no, they would meet that night at the Cathedral and summon his avatar, and then gave Abraham de Moivre that morning.

            That evening the cabal met but before beginning the summoning they all prepared themselves for the upcoming fight. Once they had finished their spells, some ritually cast they completed the summoning of Maxwell's avatar. It took the form of an angel with nebula-like wings, stars for eyes, and a golden God-Killer Sword in hand. It asked only one answer of Maxwell; “Where is the demon?”

            <The following spells were cast:
            1. Maxwell used Prime shielding to give any attempts at counterspelling any of the present mages a -9 to the attempt.
            2. Giger cast “Control Chaos” with three potency.
            3. Giger cast “Hungspell” and Maxwell stored a potency six “Celestialfire”.
            4. Ragnar granted everyone a +3 to perception.
            5. Ragnar cast “Ghost Armor” on all mages present.
            6. Ragnar and Delver worked together to grant everyone a +4 general armor rating.
            Also, Maxwell is now Gnosis 5.>

                    Chapter 61
                    9/13/2025

                    <Here is a link to the playlist we used during this battle. I can’t describe how many close calls this battle had, multiple times a turn one of the enemy mages would roll 4 successes to counterspell a player, just to have that player roll a 5. Very insane fight and the soundtrack definitely contributed to that.>

                    This Chapter started with the cabal and Uriel already under the Redondo Beach Pier where the sand had been hardened by Matter magic and shaped into a tunnel. They traveled through the tunnel to a spot where the walls had symbols carved into the wall, symbols of Time and Death. A quick look over the place allowed the cabal to understand that the entire location had been turned into an environmental yantra for any magic that would reinforce Death or weaken Time. Ragnar used this to his advantage to enhance his familiar's attributes. Only another couple of minutes of walking and the cabal found Abraham de Moivre and the others. Abraham stood before a gateway to the Underworld that was flanked by two of the zombie-like ghosts that had been encountered in Hel. The other two mages that had been present during the battle in the Shadow were present as well, as was a large wolf-like creature with a skull instead of a standard head and some abyssal tainted coming off of it. Abraham welcomed the cabal and then looked around confused before asking, “Where is Eye of God,” which enraged Maxwell who had Giger release the Celestial Fire spell on the duel wielding mage, beginning the battle.

                    <The battle itself was chaotic and instead of recounting it turn by turn, I'll describe everyone's contributions.>

                    Maxwell's preparations for the battle paid off, namely with the Prime shielding spell that penalized anyone trying to counterspell as Abraham de Moivre had done in their last battle. In fact not a single member of the cabal had their spells countered during this engagement. In addition to this Maxwell unleashed powerful Celestial Fire spells on the resurrected mages and Abraham, causing massive damage. Abraham was so wounded from this in fact that after only a few moments in the combat he retreated through the portal into the Underworld. The Angel that Maxwell had summoned flew after Abraham and by the time it finally caught up to him Abraham had already entered the Underworld.

                    Delver, being the newest mage, struggled at first to contribute much to the battle. However he did end up trapped the mage covered in metal by transforming the sand beneath him into “cartoonish quicksand,” trapped him for several turns.

                    The metal mage unleashed his own Celestial Fire, causing massive damage to Maxwell, Ragnar, and Giger, which Giger reversed by using Time magic. The duel wielder also tried speeding himself up with the Accelerate spell, but Giger countered each attempt. On top of that Giger used his Control Chaos spell he had already cast to hamper their opponents attempts at using their weapons, while boosting his cabal's attempts.

                    Ragnar had his wyvern familiar lock the wolf down using the Awe ability, while forcing the ghosts flanking the gate to the Underworld to pursue and fight Abraham de Moivre. When Abraham retreated into the Underworld he also sent the Twin Ravens to spy on him. He dueled the mage that used the two swords, but wasn't able to deal much damage to him. Instead he decided to disarm him. Delver had been trying to alter the integrity of the mage’s swords throughout the battle to no avail, so Ragnar, remembering that the swords were Abyssal in nature, used a Death and Matter spell combined with a binding spell to bind the Abyssal swords to the Codex.

                    Uriel summoned her own avatar, which helped Ragnar battle the sword mage, and then spent the battle casting shielding spells on those who needed it and counterspelling whenever she could.

                    Finally Maxwell cast a powerful Supernal Dispellation spell, ending the spell that had resurrected the two warrior mages in the Shadow during the last battle, and ending the spell on the wolf creature which caused it to vanish. Ragnar could see a large group of ghosts marching out of Hel through the portal to attack them through the eyes of his ravens, along with the summoned Angel battling Abraham in the Underworld. As the ghosts marched through Delver managed to, somehow, take control of one of the ghosts away from Abraham, and Ragnar used Matter and Death to pattern the remaining ghosts into gunpowder, which his wyvern then exploded with Firestarter.

                    Maxwell used his Forces magic to speed himself up and rushed through the portal to engage Abraham alongside the Angel. Once in the Underworld Abraham asked Maxwell if this was before or after Eye of God, and Maxwell answered that it was after, to which Abraham responded, “Then why do you look so angry with me?” which enraged Maxwell further. Abraham tried to kill Maxwell with Death making magic but he and his cabal managed to keep it countered as Maxwell burned Abraham using Celestial Fire, with his Angel delivering the killing blow. As Abraham burned up he said “I think this was the first time, so I'll see you soon.” Upon killing Abraham Maxwell found that he had changed in his own mage sight, he now had a sheath for his own God-Killer Sword, and a scroll filled with knowledge, as he took on a more angelic form; slaying this Abyssal Intrusion alongside the Angel had changed his Gnosis and he became a member of the Choir of Hashmalim.


                    The chapter ended with a few flashes of things happening after this. Maxwell burned up the bodies of the mages who had been resurrected and then sent the ashes into space with his Forces magic. Ragnar discovered that one of the water bottles he had turn into a soul jar had been filled with the soul of someone who had died. The giant clock that exists in everyone's mage sight rung loud, and lastly Abraham de Moivre stood next to Hel in the Underworld, badly burned but grinning.

                                                In Chapter 62 Ragnar underwent a Supernal trial to take on the history of a different timeline of himself. Part of that in a metagame sense was changing up his merit and skill dots, and in doing so he gained an artifact. Here is my offical write up on it after we figured out exactly what he wants it to be like.

                                                Vapenära (•••••)

                                                Gnosis: 5 Death: 2 Fate: 3 Mind: 2 Prime: 2 Spirit: 2 Mana: 10

                                                Vapenära is an Arcadian artifact that has been handed down through the ranks Adamantine Arrow for fourty-seven generations, each mage that wields it is meant to become a duelist. Vapenära does not assist these mages in becoming duelists, instead it simply gives them the opportunity. The mages of the past who wield it have always taken on a shadowname that ties them to warriors from the Viking Age, although not always from the cultures of the Vikings. Many mages took on the names of English, French, and other European mages, all that is important to Vapenära is that they embrace this era of human history. MAges that have acquired the axe have changed their shadownames if they did not already fit its symbology in order to wield it. Vapenära takes the form of a hatchet (Size 1, +1 Damage, -2 initiative, Strength 1) that appears normal to the eye. A historian of the Middle Ages might mistake it as a well preserved historic relic from that era.

                                                Vapenära witnesses oaths sword by its wielder, enforcing and protecting them. If the mage wielding Vapenära swears to battle a single enemy the artifact activates, casing the Fate spell Sworn Oaths (MtAw 137), although it casts both the mage and their enemy in the role of duelist. For the duration of the duel each participant gain access to a numbers of boons equal to the highest of their Fate arcana, including the Fate of Vapenära. The winner of this duel gains the rote quality on all physical actions for one month, while the loser suffers as all their physical actions become a chance dice.

                                                Legend around Vapenära says that it is not concerned with the legacy or status of its wielder but of itself, and will not witness the oaths of battle against targets that are weaker than the mage swearing the oath, only mages that are equal in strength or stronger than the mage with it witness. Rumors also exist of powerful mages swearing to do battle against the mage who wields Vapenära and the axe witnessing that oath, although these are not confirmed. Temporal Sympathy can be used to witness the past battles of Vapenära, which is used as evidence that the weapon has some sort of intentions of its own to forge a legacy for itself.

                                                Vapenära also shields both duelists against interference. This effect functions as a universal counterspell that automatically succeeds to stop any spell that would directly affect either participant, and as a Fate shielding effect to keep others from physically interfering with the duel. Participants can be talked out of the duel, however Vapenära considers anyone talked out of the duel to be the loser and inflicts its hex.

                                                A strange effect of Vapenära that scholars have not fully worked out is its interactions with ephemeral entities. If the duel is sworn against an ephemeral entity that entity becomes manifested, seemingly of its own intent, for the duel. If that entity loses all of its corpus it does not vanish, but instead appears next to the mage wielding Vapenära at their mercy.

                                                One final note on the history of Vapenära; in the modern age only mages are capable of using Vapenära, along with some Proximi families, but during the Middle Ages Vapenära was wielded by mages, sleepwalkers, and sleepers alike. This ended in 1065 A.D.

                                                    Chapter 63
                                                    11/10/2025

                                                    <This chapter is a solo session for Giger to become a Guardian of the Veil. The little mini story was meant to be played between two different chapters, so the second half of this story will be Chapter 65. This story begins once the mages returned from the Hel in the Underworld. Giger, seeing the dangers of left-handed and Abyssal magics confided in Blood that he wanted to help the Guardians track and deal either these mages, and thus was introduced to another Guardian to train him in the orders methods and philosophy, Arachnid.>

                                                    Giger met Arachnid in her sanctum, a place full of webs and spiders that seems to crawl in patterns that whispered of some sort of secret that Giger has never been able to piece together while being here. She is the one who has been training Giger, teaching him the Exoteric Tenets. Today she informed him of a series of sleepers who have gone seemingly catatonic in a town several hours North of Los Angeles, Salinas. Specifically, these sleepers are exhibiting the signs of having lost their souls. Salinas is outside of the range of the Golden Order, and the Awakened of San Francisco rarely travel that far South, so the Guardians have decided to send Giger to investigate and deal with any problems he may come across the way a Guardian should. He was given the name of the most recent victim, an Ethan Jose, along with his address and the intel that all of the victims were at the Salinas Regional Hospital psych ward as they were conscious but unable to feed or clean themselves. Giger was also given a pair of glasses imbued with the Soul Marks spell so he could discover if these sleepers really were soulless or if something else had happened.

                                                    Before leaving Giger cast a Fool's Rush In spell and Exceptional Luck to ensure things would go in his direction on this mission.

                                                    Leopold had driven Giger to Ethan Jose’s small house, which had several newspapers out front. Before entering Giger looked through the papers to get a sense of what might be happening in Salinas and found an interview with the doctor that is treating the four patients. While none of the information shared in the article was new, Giger learned the name of the doctor who was treating them, Elijah Cullings. Elijah was a friend of his growing up as their parents were friends. Elijah’s father owned a pharmaceutical company but instead of taking it over Elijah went to medical school to try and directly help the sick. They were friends when they were children but had grown apart as life causes adults to do.

                                                    Inside Jose’s apartment Giger checked with Mage Sight and felt a powerful signature nimbus, one of the sensations of drowning in despair. Using Time magic he looked into the past to see that four days ago Ethan was moved by stretcher and ambulance to the hospital in a catatonic state, no spell was on him. Two women, one older than Ethan and one closer to his age, cried as it happened. The day before Ethan spent the day on his couch doing nothing, no T.V., no phone time, nothing, while the two women yelled and pleaded with him. Giger learned this was his girlfriend and mother. The day before that Ethan came home drunk with another whiskey bottle ready to go, wearing his work uniform. He got into a fight with his girlfriend and started drinking on the couch after she left. This time he was under the effects of a spell. Giger then took the liberty of looking around the apartment. It was clear Ethan's girlfriend had not been living here the past few days. Nothing interesting was found except for some medical bills for a check up that Ethan had at the Salinas Hospital's clinic a month before.

                                                    Giger went to his place of work next, a nearby gas station, and looked into the past again. Nothing odd happened over the month he looked through. The only thing was that a spell was cast on Ethan while at work, but Giger couldn't see anyone who would have cast it. He came to the conclusion that it must have been either cast remotely on him, or a Space mage was involved.

                                                    After the gas station Giger decided it was time to see Ethan and the other victims so he went to the hospital, convinced the receptionist he was a cousin of Ethan's, and got past the security into the psych ward. He found Ethan and barricaded the door to make sure no one would disturb what he was about to do. Using the glasses he saw that Ethan did in fact have no soul, and neither did the other man in the bed next to Ethan. In addition to this, neither man had any Fate about them, as if it had been taken. Ethan didn't respond to any of his questions, which he expected, so Giger used Temporal Summons to bring the version of Ethan he had seen in the past, when he had returned from work drunk. The spell worked and Ethan quickly became agitated by being in a hospital all of a sudden with a stranger. Giger managed to calm him down by having Leopold bring up an expensive bottle of whiskey and offering it to him. Leopold got it past security using some sort of magical effect Giger wasn't able to identify before letting Leopold into the room, which ended the effect.

                                                    Bribed with whiskey Ethan talked. Unfortunately not much information was gained. Giger convinced him that he had been taken to the hospital because of an act of biological terrorism and Giger was trying to find the terrorists. Leopold was able to identify the other two victims but Ethan didn't recognize his roommate at the hospital nor the other male victim. He did recognize the woman without a soul as a bartender at a classy bar, but didn't know her beyond that. He hadn't gotten in any fights either. Before the conversation could go further there was a knock at the door and a nurse tried to enter, calling for help when she realized the door was barricaded. Giger ended the Temporal Summons and reverted time one turn in order to keep his whiskey bottle from shattering. When he moved the chair away from the door two nurses, two guards, and Elijah entered the room. Elijah instructed the guards to get the police here, but Giger quickly cast a Chaos Mastery and the hospital was struck by a random lightning bolt, causing power to be lost and patients in the psych ward began panicking. He then used this distraction to leap out the window, making it to his car and raising a glass as a toast to Elijah before driving away.

                                                    In the car driving away Giger used Divination to peak into the future for some extra insight into this situation.
                                                    1. Q. Is this linked to the alternate timeline Gigers or another Fate mage? A. Another Fate mage.
                                                    2. Q. How will this Fate mage destroy the Fate of future victims? A. They won't.
                                                    3. Q. Will this Fate mage have future victims? A. If they are not stopped.
                                                    4. Q. Where is this mage taking the souls of his victims? A. To their sanctum by the sea <he also gained the address.>
                                                    5. Q. Where will this mage and I first meet? A. You already have.
                                                    6. Q. What name will this mage go by in a day? A. Elijah Cullings.
                                                    7. Q. What does Elijah accomplish with the devoured souls? A. He steals their Fate and luck.

                                                    Giger had Leopold drive them close to the cabin that Elijah had turned into his sanctum and donned the Thief King's Cloak, the artifact he had stolen from the Dragon’s Hoard Aedes in his apartment building. He cast a potent Chaos Mastery spell in himself and entered the building. The inside was dark except for a fire coming from the lower floor. Using Chaos Mastery he put the fire out and stalked into the darkness. With his mage sight active he could see a book glowing on the mantle and see a figure hunched over the fire trying to restart it, their body glowing as a mage armor protects it. Giger reignited the fire with a second use of Chaos Mastery and spoke with Elijah. Elijah told him he knew he was coming and was curious why, “Certainly you don't care about those at the hospital?” The conversation continued and Giger learned that Elijah was convinced God himself had given him dominion over other humans to use as he saw fit and if anyone got in his way he'd destroy them. He pointed his fingers at Giger, ready to snap them together, and offered to let Giger go. Giger shot Elijah, blowing his fingers off of his hand without any blood, confirming to Giger he was using Death armor, and used his last chaos mastery charge to make it dark again. The battle was one sided, with Giger clearly being the more powerful mage, he countered every spell Elijah tried to cast while shooting him from different angles. It took three bullets to make Elijah begin begging for his life while stumbling towards the fireplace again, he offered the book he had, and to teach Giger what he knew of the book. Giger told Elijah to bring the book to him, with the intent of knocking him unconscious and bringing him back to the Guardians. When Elijah pulled the book from the shelf and presented it to Giger, Giger punched him and the blow ended up killing Elijah, whose body had suffered damage from the earlier bullets. A quick look through the book revealed that Elijah’s awakening had convinced him that he was God reincarnated on Earth. Giger also recognized it was a grimoire as well. Giger felt sorry for Elijah, who had undergone his awakening away from the reach of the orders and fell into this sort of madness. He took the body back to Los Angeles with him.

                                                    The book he hid in his apartment and the body he offered to Arachnid, who covered it in her spiders as Giger explained what had happened. Arachnid told him he did well, Elijah had tasted the power of the soul not once, but four times, and was beyond saving. She told Giger now his soul had a chance to reincarnate and provide stability to the Diamond Wheel instead of harming it. Giger asked about his victims’ souls, but Arachnid said she could not help with that, encouraging him to ask the Mysterium for help there if he cared about the sleepers. Then he admitted he felt as if he couldn't control his magic well, and Arachnid told him he must become more wise if he wished to have more control over his own magic.
