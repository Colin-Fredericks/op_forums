    Okay, I am back.

    Perhaps reading two giant books whole while distracted by mental issues is a tall order for "one week".

    Each of the game lines reference other game lines, yet are not consistent with each other. I think the primary reason is that they were not all reimagined for the second edition at the same time. This means there are inconsistencies for which I must pick and choose what is canon.

    For the most part I will rule that Geist: the sin-eaters 2nd edition takes precedence.

    In any case:
    I am sticking to my plan that the Death Arcanum will primarily deal with sin-eating, the bound, and the expected field work of Krewes. While the Prime Arcanum will primarily deal with the Krewe itself, such as other celebrants, the Regalia, and Ceremonies. This is a soft separation of function because these purviews are interrelated.

    One of the most basic Death spells allows the mage to produce plasm from any orifice. There are no mechanics for its value in "plasm points", nor any other mechanic explaining how this doesn't result in an endless convenient supply for Sin-eaters. Maybe that isn't a problem. I have noticed that Geist is filled with sources of plasm. Sure, most sources require some payment or effort, but by draining Bound and stuffing them into a ghost's vicinity during the Pass On Ceremony you will find that the after-life is a goldmine.

    Since the basis of Haunts for the Bound are conditions or tilts then perhaps we can have a Legacy Attainment that interacts with that basis, a power that synergizes (get it?) well with any Haunt. Maybe helping establish enhancements, shielding the condition, or ending it.

    Perhaps a power that "pays death off"? The Doomed Condition, the price of resurrection for the Bound, Avernian Gates, the Ferry toll.

    Maybe turn the Death Priest into a living Anchor?

    On the subject of Prime.

    Most of these will be handling the Krewe. So then maybe we should have some abilities enhance Ceremonies above and beyond normal. One should provide automatic successes for each dot of prime and extend Legacy Wisdom to any use of Ceremonies. Another should adapt the Platonics Forms to substitute absent materials.

    Ceremonies are but one part of a Krewe and I should not dedicate too many Attainments on that one subject. Maybe two at most?

    As for the Regalia, maybe a chance to use the final effect more than once?

    I have more thoughts, but maybe we should save them for tomorrow.

        Hey, you’re alive! Good to see you again.
        Prime and Death are both great choices by themselves for running a death cult, and frankly all sort of interesting when combined on their own.
        I would personally advise against clearly dividing Death and Prime for function, be flexible with attainments.

        As for creating plasm, just say it makes potency units of plasm. Also that spell isn’t Lasting, so that plasm disappears once duration is done. Oh and you have to spend Mana on it if it’s a legacy attainment.

        Haunts and more specifically Keys can be linked to resonance if you want to use Prime for them. According to the Players Guide to Contagion, Fate is used for Dooms, and Space of their use as keys to the Underworld.

        Turning the Death Priest into a anchor can probably be a Death/Prime ruling spell to make your personal resonance/nimbus into a anchor. There are probably other ways to do it. Some might be better.

        As for substituting platonic forms…don’t. It’s a waste of mana. Instead combine materials conceptually with Yantras (because they are basically the same thing), and have have attainments to substitute in or find whatever a given ceremony need. Every spell that’s says it affects Yantras, you can say affects Ceremonies. Heck, use Scribe Grimoire to write down Ceremonies so whoever uses the Grimoire can use the ceremonies, and benefit from the other benefits of Grimoires!​

        To whomever reads this, I hope you have a good day/night. May you be Happy.
