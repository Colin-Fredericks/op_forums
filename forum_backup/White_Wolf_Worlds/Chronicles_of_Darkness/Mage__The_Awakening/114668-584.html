            This is how I run it, buuuut there’s not much ground for me to claim it’s The Right rule.

            Ghost Gate’s normal effect: Duration applies to the gate. The gate poofs away when its Duration expires. Thus, any Twilight-ness caused by passing through the gate is Lasting. Creatures can pass through another gate later to regain material form. Items however have their material form destroyed, becoming ephemeral.

            Ghost Gate’s Reach effect: Duration applies to the Twilight-ness. Anything turned Twilight/ephemeral becomes fully material again once the spell expires. This also means that items don’t have their material form destroyed and turned into ephemera; just remove the spell, and it’s fresh to go.

                            Originally posted by KaiserAfini View Post
                            Maybe it would be more helpful to state the intent instead. What Arcana combination would be needed to replicate the Hanged Man and Man in the Mirror stands from Jojo ?
                            Those two Stands are, as more than one person has highlighted by putting dialogue about the former next to demonstrations of the latter, different types of phenomena.

                            The Arcana are not generally built to produce weirder phenomena as simple expressions of their lore, and Stands are the kind of weird phenomena that in ChroD would likely have a metaphysical system behind them akin to the Fire, the Wyrd, or the Machine — you're not going to build in "this thing can access an inverse world through any reflective surface/physically hide in reflections" from first principles without really stretching how spell factors are considered — but Man in the Mirror's general shtick is Space-related with a side of Fate and Hanged Man likewise accomplishes what it does through Forces with a side of Space.

                            The simplest way to accomplish the specific goal of replicating those Stands is, yes, to use Spirit or Death to create an ephemeral being with Numina that do those things.

                                                    My first thought about a mage animating mirror images is making the image inside of the mirror move around in ways that aren't a reflection of what's in front of it, possibly as a way of communicating or freaking people out. I'd actually see such an application as just being a Ruling of Forces (and possibly Matter as an alternative).

                                                    If it's an idea of a reflection stepping out of the mirror and doing stuff... that basically just seems like conjuring up a mobile hologram in a way you choose to be very showy, but the level of Forces you're employing to do so don't really limit you to working with existing reflections, and as Satchel said the Arcana don't really tend to work in terms of being super esoteric such that you could treat a reflection as an entity as a matter of course, or otherwise make drawing it from the mirror more special.

                                                    I do think Forces could construct it as a sort of Star Trek hologram where it has physicality, but again you could do that anywhere. For an Awakened mage, doing it with a mirror seems more like the kind of thing you do circumstantially, if you want to create a scenario where somebody thinks their reflection is popping out to do stuff to them.

                                                    I could see an archmaster using the Practice of Entities to create a reflection entity with fitting powers in a way that involves messing around with mirrors, but that's a whole other thing.

                                                    A mirror-themed Legacy... it depends on what you want to do with it, but I'd say the limited scope of Attainments combined with them not being constrained by Paradox, dispellation, or spell control limit is the kind of thing that lends itself to making them more tied to particular circumstances. It makes a lot more sense to have an Attainment that limits your hard light hologram creation to something that you can draw out of mirrors then it does to make a regular spell like that, especially if it's in service to some message about the value people place in mirrors or whatever.
