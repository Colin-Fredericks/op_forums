    Mysteries at Sea

    02-20-2019, 01:54 PM
    I'm running a Mage chronicle right now in tabletop that has the players as a cabal that's on a 19th century sailing ship that's on a circumnavigation trip with the main destination in Ceylon but with stops along the way at Rio de Janeiro, Polynesia, New Zealand, Southeast Asia and China before reaching Sri Lanka, and then heading towards Zanzibar and passing the Cape of Good Hope before getting shipwrecked and marooned on the coast of East Africa with them going up the coast towards Morocco to get back home in England.

    Beyond the central plot which revolves them being Diamond mages trying to secure a powerful, unknown artifact to swing the Nameless War (which they will find out is already swinging with the Free Council's earliest formations, with them playing an indirect part in the formation of the Pentacle), but most of the game will take place on the ship in between each of these destinations, though I intend to use the Astral Plane, Shadow Realm, Underworld and other Irises as a way to get out of just being on a ship all the time. The Seers are the major antagonists as they are the ones who are guiding the voyage and the cabal is attempting to thwart by taking over the ship from within and finding out what they plan, and diverting the cargo to Diamond hands once it returns from Ceylon rather than its intended destination.

    I wanted to see what ideas the thread might have for specifically nautical themed Mysteries. A central conceit of the setting will be focusing on the Atlantis myth, the concept of "dragons" and their ties to leylines, and the Astral will play a relatively large role. As well, the ship is infested with ghosts and malevolent spirits, as a former slave ship captured by privateers and turned into a penal transport ship (which is indeed one reason the voyage is stopping at New Zealand, to deliver a small group of convicts to Van Diemens Land in Tasmania and a lot of provisions, before picking up exotic goods in China and India.)

    Mostly I just want a dozen or so plot hooks and mysteries to use as connective tissue between the destinations and get people's interests, in case the Obsessions and Aspirations of my players don't immediately jump out as obvious plots.

      Ghost ship or mysterious island that isn't on any of the charts because it isn't normally there (exists in the Underworld perhaps).
      A becalming fog rolls over the ship, bad enough in and of itself, but they catch glimpses of something moving in it or find themselves surrounded by the sounds of battle.
      A distant light on the watery horizon at night, but no sign of anything during the day.
      Floating wreckage that they can't identify, complete with a mysterious artifact.
      They rescue a stranded survivor who's keeping a secret (might not be one of the living or even human).
      An encounter with a barely glimpsed sea monster.
      Dracula climbs out of his coffin at night to feed on the crew.
      The captain or a crew member are smuggling something to Van Diemens Land, weapons for a prison revolt perhaps.
      They encounter another ship that is pursuing its own 'White Whale'.
      Seer politics interfere with the sea voyage.
      A Spirit pirate ship that feeds on plunder.

          That is one cool lineup, lots of different mythologies to draw from. Here are some mysteries:

          1- The Polynesian god Maui was said to draw the islands from the sea, lasso the sun and split the sky from the earth, but how ? Was that some Atlantean lore that survived or was he a post-Fall mage that found one of their ancient terraforming devices ?

          2- Sleepers speak of a whale that comes with a chilling fog, but its so much more than that. Part of it is the animal, yes, but other parts are ancient ruins seamlessly integrated into its flesh, other parts of it are clockwork machinery. Its breath exhales mist and its mouth is an Avernian Gate. Except, ghosts that emerge from it appear in a mist form, halfway between twilight and the material. And up there, in its head, behind some kind of transparent metal panel, is that a dragon bone ?

          3- The waters between Africa and China are full of oddities as of late. Ships that suddenly sail into the air, a violet rain that consumes life sometimes floods areas of the mainland, creating temporary rivers. Impossible beasts haunt camps and make panicked people sabotage their own operations, only to vanish without a trace by morning. Webs of silk tangle ships and every surviving sailor's tale seems to contradict all others. This is the only one I made with an answer: there is a war in the Shadow, the magath composed of the ghost of Lao Tze and a spirit of the sea is at war with the spirit of Anansi. They both have powerful courts and want to end the slave trade, but disagree on the methods.

          4- There exists a closed archipelago that can be sung open with High Speech. Inside it ships sail in the void, crossing a labyrinth of portals filled with alien life (imagine giant interdimensional deep sea abyss flora and fauna that can fly, the Dark Rift from Skies of Arcadia is a good reference for it), occasionally runes of High Speech can be seen lighting the darkness. Sometimes they interlink into shapes, weapons and creatures that guard this gateway. Beyond this path lies a futuristic ruined city, frozen in time during its cataclysmic tidal end. In the distance you can see a compound standing out. Is this a real place or an Astral domain ? A machination of the God Machine ? Some kind of Supernatural Verge of Time and Mind ? Or is this Hierachonis to come, somehow flung to the past in its darkest hour ? Perhaps its none of them, maybe its an artificial timeline, another piece in the grand chess game of archmasters, placed here until the right trigger arrives. (Chronopolis from Chrono Cross is the inspiration for this one)

                  All of these ideas are fantastic! Thank you.

                  I think only one of my players visits these forums, so JP, please don't read ahead. I would love some ideas though as to the Nameless, Seer, and even Diamond antagonists they will face on the stops to help fill in between my own ideas.

                  Madeira -> Here the players will learn of the Little Belt Affair which was one of the sparking events of the War of 1812. The mainly English crew will be outraged and the players will have to mediate it. There will be only a single cabal (Diamond) on the island, but they will encourage if they become part of the politics the position of the third mate, which is to ask for a letter of marque and take a sojourn to the Carribean. This might actually result in a longer voyage but it's going to be heavily weighted that players don't go because it would cause issues with the Company and their mission, but they will, either way, have a run-in with a privateer that is one of the Nameless, and through that learn of the Brethren of the Coast order (which is indeed descended from Blackbeard and his ilk and yes I am going full Assassin's Creed style historical revisionism to have mages being involved with that, just I have for the French Revolution in two of my PCs' backstories.) Said Nameless will also be a lich, which ties into my desire to kind of portray the Nameless, at first, as being as threatening as the Diamond thinks they are, which plays into a later issue.

                  Rio de Janeiro -> This will be the first interaction the players have with the Nameless and I definitely want it to be antagonistic. They will be radical republicans inspired by the waves of revolution in South America and the tradition from Paris. And I want to confirm some of the worst ideas of the Diamond to start, because my players are mostly sympathetic so I want to challenge those sympathies by having a Scelestus cult having taken hold, which is orthogonal to the main plot. I have a plan for what happened to the Seers here but keeping it vague in case people listen, but could definitely use ideas for specific mages/a cabal, and specifically how left-handed magic became accepted among the Nameless here.

                  Matavai/New Zealand -> The players will meet with a Nameless Order (attached to a Legacy) that recognizes the concept of Atlantis but in a very different way than the Diamond does, steeped in a mix of Polynesia and Aboriginal myth as they stretch across Oceania. They have, as the Maori did, resisted the encroaching influence of the Seers, who have come with the forces of Empire, but they recognize the Diamond as more like kin, but unfortunately, the local Diamond cabals do not and view them heretics. This will be the first more positive experience, and since they will be visiting Matavai first, rather than Resolution Bay, they will get the Nameless perspective first, and have a chance to broker at least a peace when the Seers do inevitably attack. I do have the Seer pylon here detailed, but could use ideas for the local Diamond.

                  Guangzhou -> Though the Diamond has been ensconced here for centuries through transmission via Buddhism in India, there are dozens of sects (cabals) of Taoist inspired Nameless Orders that have begun to form the early stages of a Free Council. However, here the Nameless and the Diamond are not at a hot war, but a war of words and philosophy, and this is actually where the first known Assemblies that include both Diamond and Nameless mages have begun to form, and I want this to be an opportunity for the players to first get an idea of how they could work together, even though they are still sworn enemies. However further complicating things is going to be some vampire shenanigans and the fact the Seers are allowed into the Assemblies as well, vaguely representing a Confucian philosophy in the traditional three-way debate and even syncretism, as they will meet with a cabal that has members that are Diamond, Seer, and Nameless! Ideas for specific mages or a name for that cabal I will gladly take.

                  Calcutta -> While Madras is a Diamond (and Seer) city, Calcutta has had a major Nameless revolt. What is interesting is that the Diamond has been ensconced in India forever. They are the forces of tradition there, and when European mages came, they found the local orders familiar but also somewhat divergent. Over the last hundred years they have been transmitting back and forth ideas and getting closer, but there's still some uneasiness. However, the Seers had been almost completely destroyed here, and the Diamond now openly wars with them. However, that has put them on a much more war-like stance when the Nameless, who are mostly representative of the forces of modernity in India and breaking away from old systems of thought, still incorporating local mythological traditions but also a creole of new ones, which is similar to what happened to the Carribean which is also a major place where the Nameless are powerful (as is Continental Europe). Here the local Consilium has become majorly corrupt in a flipping of the Rio scenario, with the Tremere-afflicted having infiltrated the highest levels of power, but with the PCs falsely accused (or perhaps rightfully accused at this point, there will have been an opportunity for one or two to switch sides by now) of being spies for the Nameless, they have to decide whether to plead their case (and possibly miss their departure on the SS Astarte) or risk major political implications with an escape from custody.

                  After Ceylon (which is a Seer stronghold but where rival Diamond mages, some Arisen mummies which I haven't mentioned above that serve as external antagonists and part of the central Mysteries of the setting, and Nameless cells are all competing with the PCs to steal this artifact), I have kept things pretty open because that's the climax of the story, and Zanzibar is intended to be place where there's a major confrontation with one of the main antagonists and also them learning some of the deeper mysteries of the setting. Cape Town is kind of an intended denouement where they will meet with one of the strongest Nameless Orders, find one of the leaders they have been reading the correspondence of ("Bogomil") that has been instrumental with the formation of what is now officially the Free Council, and decide what to do with him as he has both sent people to stop them, but also now seems willing to negotiate, because the Seers have also come to him and he's committed to pushing back against them.

                  What happens after that I'm keeping open. There will be a journey through the Dark Continent to whatever their destination is, whether that's to London to deliver the artifact (and possibly convince the London Consilium to back the Paris Assembly against the Seers), to Egypt in order to return it to where it came from (and thus remove it from the politics of the fighting), or to Paris to deliver Bogomil's vote (and possibly the artifact) to resist the offer of the Seers and open negotiations with the Pentacle. Plus tying up any other loose ends.

                          What's the time frame for the voyage? I mean, the creation of the FC is 1900, but this seems to imply it's set in the 1810s?
                          The Nameless War canonically raged over the entirety of the 19th Century, starting early on and building outwards with an official formation on 1899.                               For Guangzhou, you have a traveling party of adventurers seeking to enlighten the Diamond with the knowledge that the creation of the Pentacle is best for them, whether the players know it or not, correct ?

                              If so, you have everything needed for the symbology of Journey to the West, along with a plethora of potential Shadow Names. Tripitaka is a Silver Ladder, Sun Wukong is an Arrow, the dragon is from the Mysterium (all of the power, but lacking the proactivity), Sha Wuging is a Guardian (powerful yet discrete) and Zhu Bajie is a Nameless (skilled, yet lacking humility and sometimes common sense).

                              The Seers are the Celestial Bureaucracy with a Minister of the Psychopomp being the Jade Emperor. Jade is associated with souls, but he is a Spirit focused Thrysus, this means he focuses not only on presiding over souls but also commanding the natural world by building a court of spirit bureaucrats. Naturally, using such a name in service of the Exarchs speaks of his boldness and ambition.

                              The Scelesti are the Buddhists, which makes their leader, Kwan Yin, play a very interesting role as patron of the whole quest.

                              The sacred text is, borrowing from Dave's spoilers on Grimmoires, a Palimpsest. The stops of the journey are there to let them collect the parts, which then need to be taken to a specific Astral domain to be fully understood. The book contains prophecies an ancient Mysterium sage uncovered to, at the right time, reforge the Atlanteans into becoming stronger than ever.

                              The grimmoire also has a secondary function, which is tempting enough for the Seers and Scelestus to take the risk: a one time use of a prophetic Autorictas Arcanum, handed down by Glorianna herself.
