        Axel has it right, Incognito Presence is what you're looking for, man.

        And just because you're in a Werewolf game doesn't mean that you're not going to be up against other Mages -- quite the opposite, in fact. First off, Mages and Werewolves often come into conflict over resources -- many Hallows form on Ley Line nexii, which also conveniently spawn Loci frequently.

        Secondly, the Shadow's denizens are actively hostile toward all but a small subset of Mages (those with Spirit 4+), and that includes Werewolves. So yeah, have fun with that. The pack tou run with might make exceptions because they know you, but every other werewolf you encounter is going to see you as an interloper, Forsaken and Pure alike.

        My advice: learn the Prime Veiling effect that changes your Nimbus type as a Rote, pronto. You're going to be using it a shit-ton.

                                        I had the exact problem with my Moros Mage and his katana. Incognito Presence (Mind) would solve your problem, as would Hidden Hoard (Matter).                                                 So here is what I figured out for my artifact.

                                                The blade was gifted to me by the Seraphim Sudrushael. The sword is the physical manifestation of righteous wrath, and judgement forged in the heart of Aether from the concepts of victory, steel, and battle. It was tempered in the idea of nobility, and honed against the wheel of duty. It's guard has the appearance of gold and shaped into two angelic forms with their wings out stretched their hands caressing the blade, and the blade shines with a luster of mirror polished metal.

                                                It was gifted to me for service to the angel, durring the day that my body slumbered while my soul was in Aether, for how long I cannot guess, my journy to the watchtower was interupted when following my guide Lashaelmuel I spotted a woman in danger. She shouted at me to not leave the path or my soul would be in peril, but She was in danger something was going to kill her and I couldn't stand by and do nothing. Of course I hadn't signed my name to the tower yet so, as far as magic went I had none and I was about to step in to a battle between a Seraph and a…you know I never found out what that was? Some sort of beast of the realm I'm guessing. Maybe an invasive spirit from another supernal realm? Either way she was loseing to it and would soon be torn apart, so I did the only thing I knew to do. The stupid man thing. I picked up a rock and flung it. Yep, humanity has come so far, look at us flinging rocks instead of stool.

                                                Either way that rock hit it clean in the head and got it's attention Lashaelmuel looked on in horror, muttering something about not loseing this one, and other things that sounded like she would be in big trouble if I died. Funny how the senses liven up with you channelge a Lion the size of a grizzlebear to a fight and your armed with…rocks.

                                                I probably should have died there but when your sleep walking the path of aether you do have a small edge. You can see your death coming, and that is the differnce between being reduced to a red schemer and living to see another day. So I did the only logical thing looking at this thing coming to reduce me to a lumpy red sauce. I reached down and grabbed another rock as I rolled under its cleaver Claws.

                                                Many rocks later the woman came limping over and I realized she was an angel. To reward me and and apparently dodge a life debt she offered me her sword, her littoral sword. I politely refused twice before accepting the third time figuring it would be rude not to at that point.

                                                When I awoke after the ordeal the sword was there next to me.

                                                The blade burns with angelic wrath, calling upon it the fires of Aether. This power is automatically cast when the blade is drawn, and lasts until it is returned to it's scabbard or the second power is used. As a by product of this effect the temperature in any room or location drops by 20 degrees, as the blade draws in and magnifies the ambient thermal energy. For 1 mana per attack this damage may strike empheral targets.

                                                The blade also has one other power. It may call forth a pillar of angelic golden flame, this counts as a blast with a damage of 2, A blast area of 3, and a force of 3. The damage counts as coming from fire for the purposes of weaknesses and immunities, and for a mana the blast area damage may hit empheral targets.

                                                This effect cancels the first, which may be reactivated by succeeding on a 4 die roll.
