            First Attainment: Through the Looking Glass
            Prerequisite: Initiation
            Arcana: Death
            Primary Spell Factor: Duration
            Reach: Advanced Duration
            Reflections have long been thought to show glimpses of a person's soul.
            The Spectators know that these reflections show evidence of distortion in the essence of both people and objects.
            With this attainment, a Spectator can see these distortions in shadows.
            Through the Looking Glass also creates shadows where none currently exist.
            Objects and entities in Death attuned Twilight cast shadows into the Material Realm.
            Hidden objects and entities cast shadows as if there were no obstructions to hide them.
            Objects and entities hidden by supernatural means resist this effect causing a Clash of Wills.

            Optional Yantra
            When using a reflective yantra such as a pool of water or a mirror, any objects or entities whose shadows are revealed by Through the Looking Glass appear in full color in the mirror's reflection.
            This allows for creatures like vampires to properly appear in mirrors.

            Add Mind or Spirit *
            Objects and entities in the corresponding Twilight cast shadows into the Material Realm.

                            Does this clear up the wording of the second attainment? Or do you think it should be something completely different?

                            Second Attainment: Reflection Sculpting
                            Prerequisites: Death 2, Crafting or Expression 3
                            Reflections, like shadows, are images of their source and just as a shadow can be manipulated so too can a reflection.
                            This attainment duplicates the effects of Shadow Sculpting (Death **) with one reach for instant casting and a second for animation.
                            When animating the Spectator's reflection, their reflected shadow may be manipulated to form objects with Durability 2.
                            These shadow objects are treated as solid by Twilight entities reflected in the mirror by Through the Looking Glass.
                            If used as a weapon against Twilight entities the shadows have a weapon rating of 2.

                            Optional Matter 3
                            For a point of mana, the Spectator can cause reflections to happen in reality.
                            Damage done in this way is always bashing and reduced by 2.

                            And does this clear up the third attainment? Really it's just Ghost Gate but without the whole strips you naked on return problem. And an optional mirror hopping ability.

                            Third Attainment: Mirror World
                            Prerequisites: Death 3, Empathy 3
                            Reach: Advanced Duration
                            The Spectator may perform a ritual to use a mirror as a portal to Death attuned Twilight.
                            Ghost Gate (Death ***) is similar to this attainment, but objects may be retrieved through Mirror World without Touch of the Grave (Death **) and become true material objects with the appropriate properties.
                            While the portal is open the mirror reflects its destination, from the Material Realm it shows Twilight and from Twilight the Material Realm.

                            Optional Space 3
                            The Spectator causes the mirror to become a portal to another mirror instead of to Twilight. This effect acts as Co-Located (Space ***), but may only overlap spaces reflected in mirrors that the Spectator knows about.

                                        I'd focus less on the Twilight end of things, it kind of diffuses the thematics in a way that I don't think make a lot of sense. Like, it makes sense to be able to see Twilight in reflections, but it's weird to treat reflections as a sort of Twilight. Plus, like I said, why grab the reflection of a thing when the thing is right there? I'd be inclined to make this a Death/Space legacy if you really want to keep the transportation angle, drop Mind, Spirit and Matter, and treat each reflection as a kind of pocket realm you can step into which is exactly the size of the reflection, and you can't leave the reflected area or change your perspective, like you can go behind a building but there isn't anything "there" because the mirror doesn't show it, and it can do weird things with perspective. Like, imagine having a hand size mirror and being far enough away to reflect a skyscraper in it. You can prop the mirror up and step into it and wander off toward the skyscraper in the reflection shrinking as you get further away from the "surface" of the reflection. Or you go into the mirror so you can talk to the reflection ghost that you can see from inside it because it reflects Twilight (Though it's a reflection, so maybe there's no sound or mind there since those are intangibles that don't show up in mirrors. You could certainly look though, and the Optional bit could let you communicate). Or you can drag someone into the mirror and then leave it and trap them there. Though I think I'd do a weird, and have the real person/thing put in the mirror leave a projection out into the real world which matches the perspective/size of the real person/thing that's in the mirror. I'd use the Reach for Advanced Duration, the applications are limited enough from having to use a ritually prepared mirror that that's the only thing I'd worry about.

                                        Then for the Space optional you can do a neato and use a reflection as a Representational Yantra (Technically this isn't even part of the attainment since you could do that anyway), and if you have Sympathy with something in the reflection (And a point of mana per use) you can say reach into that reflection of the skyscraper (Or use Shadow Sculpting) to squish the reflection and do some damage to the thing being reflected or mold it in other ways. This would be limited by Potency I imagine and be a sort of weird version Space-y version of Shared Fate.

                                        Not sure about the 4th attainment proper. Perhaps you could set a reflection so even if the mirror moves it still shows the thing or area it was reflecting when you used the attainment. Great for capturing images of people to interrogate later and have easy access to when you pop over to them via the Optional bit, copies of sensitive documents, low key Scrying, etc.

                                        For the Space optional you could have a Co-Locate portal style effect which allows you to tie mirrors together with so you can step from one to another when you enter them, prep work required for that one, and the number of mirrors you could have synced up would be limited by Scale. Use the Reach on Advanced Duration and the portal effect.

                                                        Mrmdubois The association with Twilight specifically is really only founded on the fact that seeing into Twilight with a mirror fills a trope. If you can see into it and you can use it as a portal it seems like the portal should take you to Twilight. I personally like this because it provides me an opportunity to "fix" the whole objects are destroyed when converted to death ephemera (and need to use Touch of the Grave to get them back out.) There's also some interesting metaphysical implications that this could explore, like the fact that Ghost Gate is only weaving, meaning people and objects already "are" death ephemera in the sense that the difference is just a property away (like opacity or maybe something like weight.) So entering Twilight through a mirror could allow exploring that connection by allowing you to interact with the ephemera aspects of material objects.

                                                        That said, a full on mirror universe could be interesting. Especially if any source of specular reflections nearby contributes; so a polished gold watch might create a narrow cone of gold tinted fuzzy reflections in the mirror world, but unless it's connected to the one you came into, you can't get there (the spaces are only as big as the reflection even if for effect you can see across them.) I would probably allow mirrors to freeze any captured ghost in them, as in the actual ghost and not just a reflection, to avoid weird questions about what exactly a reflection of a ghost is. People or ghosts trapped in a mirror should be visible from outside looking into the mirror, but I think an actual projection might be a bit much (though a ghost might be able to partially manifest out?)

                                                            If you're stepping into the mirror then I don't see why you couldn't also enter Mirror Twilight, but you wouldn't be in the real world in Twilight. Though this would let you do funky things like grab the ephemera in Mirror Twilight, shape it with the 2nd attainment by molding the reflection, and climb up to the top floor of the skyscraper via an ephemeral staircase. Then step back out of the mirror and onto the ledge. Or with later attainments, you don't step into the mirror you just sympathetically tie yourself to your image and then distort the reflection so that impossibly, you're reflected as being in the air and your real body reflects that by also being in the air.

                                                            The idea of the projected reflection is that moving a real thing into the mirror displaces the reflection which has to go...somewhere. It still acts as a reflection too, so it mimics whatever you're doing in mirror world in real life. It's like a shadow after all. The whole concept as I'm envisioning it is...not very suited for like being stealthy, but you can do some reality bending stuff with it regardless.
