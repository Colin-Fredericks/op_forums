    Grave Misfortune would work as I've described earlier. There's a set number of charges in the area. They get used up by whoever meets the conditions to use them until the charges are gone. Even access to the charges is granted, not even distribution of charges to all users. It's like having a vending machine. There are only so many snacks in there, and someone can purchase multiple snacks.

    Withstand operates normally. If their Withstand exceeds the Potency of the spell then they act as if they are not under the effects of it and under no compulsion to consume charges from it.

    There isn't an extra step either. This area gives +2 Dex, the area is the subject and the +2 Dex is the effect. Same goes for spells with charges: This area has 3 charges for Grave Misfortune, so that's what it's got.

                            What I find strange is how the charge per area approach can affect certain cases.

                            For example, lets say honing the form for Dex is cast on your sanctum with Potency 3 and then 10 mages are inside. Those 10 get +3 to Dex. But if you cast a Potency 3 boon for fine motor tasks, only 3 people will get the boon once. So it becomes oddly difficult to use Fate to make a lucky workspace for your cabal.

                            So my original interpretation was:

                            * An area of effect affects everything inside it, meaning it auto renews so long as you stay within a certain radius of the center (or re-enters once charges are expended). So a lucky zone is whan an Acanthus would make to defend their sanctum instead of, for example, the golem area that a Moros could employ.

                            * Throwing a target out of their comfort zone or moving that zone is an interesting way around these effects, with Forces, Matter and Space providing the most obvious ways to do so.

                            * Certain spells can become especially dangerous as an AoE effect, such as Shared Fate. But using them without setup also places the caster and their cabal mates at risk.

                            * Additional spell work is needed to make sure only select targets are affected by the effect. Therefore Prime or Fate pose a real risk of affecting these filters.

                            * It becomes interesting to prioritize Scale and Duration over Potency for AoE casts, but it adds the risk of easier dispelling. Balancing these factors is the drawback of a wide reaching effect, in addition to how easily it would cause Disbelief.

                            It all came from the following though experiment: Moros and Obrimos make a tool arsenal. Mastigos can carry all they need with them or hide their caches extremely well. Thrysus have fetishes, talens, spirits, trained animals and perhaps cryptids at their disposal. But what kind of tools and sanctum defenses does an Acanthus usually make (apart from Warding Gesture, Monkey's Paw and The Right Tool enhanced personal possessions) ?
