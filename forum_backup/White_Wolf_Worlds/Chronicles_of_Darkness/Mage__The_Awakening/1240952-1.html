    Originally posted by 21C Hermit View Post
    What was the definition of the Realms Invisible and the Lower Depths again? Or more specifically, what was it that separated them again? Because the Underworld fits the “devouring,” and “lacking something” marks pretty well, which IIRC are traits of Lower realms. At least the Shadow has positive feedback via Resonance (even though its natives are mostly hostile), and the Astral exists inside/between everyone’s souls.
    Iirc 1e (think it was in Summoners) stated that if the supernal realms are above the fallen world the underworld is below it, further apart from the supernal than other invisible realms. The lower depths are something even farther away... though personally I wager that the distinction becomes moot the lower you go in the underworld. In my mind its basically a gradually developing lower depth, with the autochtonous depths being a more or less normal invisible realm, though with a sort of gravity pulling away from the fallen world. as you cross more and more rivers the more alien things get.

                                        So, we know that Plasm contains Essence, and that Geists can gift Essence to Ghosts via Plasm with a 3:1 conversion rate. I'm not clear on whether this is a natural ability of Plasm, or something that relies on the metaphysical nature of Sin Eaters, though I'm guessing it's closer to the latter, or else Ghosts would just consume Plasm on their own to regain Essence without having a middleman.

                                        So, if I wanted a spell that converted Plasm to Essence at that same ratio, what would it be?
                                        • Ruling, because Plasm is already capable of turning into Essence? (We must assume the conversion is inherent to Plasm, and the ghost's inability to process it on their own is due to something they explicitly lack, not something that Geists have)
                                        • Weaving, because Plasm and Essence aren't wholly separate, but Plasm is incapable of converting on its own? (We must assume that Essence and Plasm are just different expressions of the same thing)
                                        • Patterning, because Plasm isn't Essence, and it's magic unique to Sin-Eaters that allows the conversion (We must assume that references to Plasm 'containing' Essence are not literal, and merely figurative references to the ability of Sin-Eaters to convert it)
