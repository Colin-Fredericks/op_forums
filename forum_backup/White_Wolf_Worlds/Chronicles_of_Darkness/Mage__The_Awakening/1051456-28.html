            I kept thinking about the 1e Coalburner art when I was trying to design a Spy Legacy. So... here's my take on the Uncrowned Kings.

            Uncrowned Kings
            Nicknames: Coalburners, Crafters, the Sanctimonious (Derogatory)
            Organization: See Mage 1e for official fluff

            Theory: Enlightenment is earned by the sweat of your brow and the strength of your back alone.
            Background: See Mage 1e for the fluff

            Parentage: Free Council, Moros
            Ruling Arcana: Mind
            Yantras: Using the Nigredo Attainment on themselves (only applies to Subtle Arcana spells cast on themselves) (+2); Technical manuals and instructive literature (+2); Tools crafted specifically for the relevant spell (+1); Broken symbols of authority (+1)
            Oblations: Spending at least an hour practicing a manual craft; Spending at least an hour directly educating others on a manual craft; Spend at least an hour staying up-to-date on techniques and advances in a craft that the Coalburner practices

            Prerequisites: Mind 2, Death 1, Crafts 2, Occult 1, a Crafts Specialty or Crafts as an Asset Skill
            Initiation: Use Mage Sight to study an item you created with your own two hands, analyzing it for flaws and traces of your Nimbus. There will come a moment of allegorical enlightenment, where the Mage's own flaws stare back at them. In that moment, they have taken up the mantle of the Uncrowned Kings. New initiates can be hesitant to use Nigredo on themselves, as this revelation is often deeply disheartening when first learned. Elder Coalburners know that despite how daunting the task ahead appears, the fruits of such labor are all the sweeter.

            Ritual Activation: The Coalburner must practice a manual craft for at least an hour to activate several of their Attainments. One project can simultaneously activate all such Attainments. Activation requires them to create a complete product, which must have a practical use. The Extended Action should typically have a base Target Number equal to or greater than their Attainment tier (1 for Initiate, 3 for Disciple, 5 for Master, etc). The Coalburner may use tools, so long as they are performing the majority of the work by hand. Tending a stewpot, for example, would generally be applicable, but chopping up ingredients to cook in an automated crockpot would not. Finally, a project used in this way cannot simultaneously be used as an Oblation.

            Nigredo
            (Mind ●, Death ●) Initiation
            Though the Uncrowned Kings are an inwardly-focused Legacy, it is important to take the measure of their materials as a whole. By studying others, for both their strengths and their weakness, the Coalburner is better able to identify the dross they must shed from themselves.

            The Coalburner can analyze another human (or human-adjascent entity) as if he had cast Know Nature and Soul Marks as a Combined Spell on them. The Coalburner need not directly touch the target, but must directly interact with them. Sharing a polite greeting (or cursing each other out in traffic) is just as applicable as a firm handshake, so long as both parties acknowledge each other. The Coalburner may also use this on themselves, by intentionally inspecting a recently completed project.
            Know Nature
            Potency = Mind
            Duration = N/A (1 Turn, 2 Turns at Mind 2, 3 Turns at Mind 4)
            Range = Self/"Touch"
            Casting Time = Instant (Reach to Instant Casting)

            Soul Marks
            Potency = Lower of Death and Mind
            Duration = N/A (1 Turn, 2 Turns at Mind 2, 3 Turns at Mind 4)
            Range = Self/"Touch"
            Casting Time = Instant

            Commentary: 1e's optionals were a strange mix of Matter and Prime. This doesn't have quite the same feel, but I think I captured a good chunk of it.


            Albedo
            (Mind ●●) Occult 2
            It is not enough to merely identify flaws and work to remove them - one must also protect the material from corrosion and deterioration. By practicing their craft, a Coalburner raises a protective scaffold around their mind, replicating the spell Mental Shield.
            Mental Shield
            Duration = Mind (Reach to Advanced Duration)
            Potency = N/A
            Range = Self
            Casting Time = Scene

            Commentary: 1e's original Attainment. Not much to say.

            (Death ●●)
            It can be easy to forget about the soul - after all, they are fungible to a certain extent. You just have to be willing to take one from someone else. The Coalburners shun this mentality, treating their souls as reverently as they do their mind. The Coalburner's mental defense now also applies to attempts to harm or remove his soul, as if under the effect of Soul Armor.
            Soul Armor
            Duration = Lower of Death and Mind (Reach to Advanced Duration)
            Potency = N/A
            Range = Self
            Casting Time = Scene
            NOTE: Activates simultaneously with the Mind portion

            Commentary: 1e had this in the 2nd-tier Optional slot, and that edition had it as a Prime spell. Thankfully it's a Death spell in this edition, so less MAD. I moved it down to this slot, rather than the Disciple-Adept range the converion would normally put it at, because it fits the Reach and is much easier to tie in to Mental Shield.


            Citrinitas
            (Mind ●●●) Crafts 3
            The Uncrowned draws down a spark of the Supernal into themselves, banking the coals of their mind around it. With effort, they can stoke that spark into a purifying crucible.

            By practicing a manual craft, the Coalburner increases one of their Mental or Social Attributes by [Mind] for an extended period of time. The Coalburner selects which Attribute is enhanced when they first learn this Attainment, and cannot change it. Further, the enhancement cannot yet bring them above their Gnosis-derived Attribute maximum.
            Augment Mind
            Potency = Mind
            Duration = One Day, One Week at Mind 4 (Reach to Advanced Duration)
            Range = Self
            Casting Time = Scene
            NOTE: Only applies to one Attribute, chosen upon learning the Attainment

            Commentary: 1e again. 'S a good choice. I think I'd choose my Resistance Attributes, how about y'all?

            (Matter ●●●)
            A craftsman must not leave themselves the excuse of blaming their tools for shoddy work. As the Coalburner's consciousness expands, they gain glimpses of the true purpose of all things.

            When under the aegis of the Mind portion of Citrinitas, the Coalburner gains the benefits of the Craftsman's Eye spell with the "How To Use/8-Again" Reach effect. However, this power fails to work on tools that remove human participation from industry. A sewing machine would be acceptable, but a machine that automates the process would not, for example.

            Computers and digital technology are an uncomfortable grey area for the Legacy because of this Attainment. While a vocal portion of traditionalist members look down on any such tool, a growing modernist faction point out that utility items such as digital levels and studfinders register perfectly fine to their enhanced sight.
            Craftsman's Eye
            Duration = Conditional Lasting (Reach to Advanced Duration)
            Potency = N/A
            Range = Self
            Casting Time = Instant (Reach to Instant Casting)
            NOTE: 1 Reach to "How To Use/8-Again" Reach effect

            Commentary: So! This is my first big departure from 1e. I'm not sure I articulated the clauses well, but basically it's not meant to work well with modern industrialization. Some tech registers normally, some are featureless edifices, and some just spaz out. It is meant to create some internal tension between old guard and new blood within the Legacy, with the minor implication that an Uncrowned makes for a good renaissance man. Jack of all trades, master of none, but better still than a master of one!


            Rubedo
            (Mind ●●●●) Craft 4, Occult 3
            The Coalburner's internal crucible has sheared away sufficient dross to support greater luminance. They may now choose a second Mental or Social Attribute to enhance, and both enhancements can exist simultaneously. The Coalburner activates both Attribute-ehnancements at the same time and with the same crafting project, and can choose to spend a point of Mana while doing so to exceed their Gnosis-derived Attribute maximums.
            Augment Mind
            Potency = Mind
            Duration = One Day, One Week at Mind 4 (Reach to Advanced Duration)
            Range = Self
            Cost = 1 Mana
            Casting Time = Scene
            NOTE: Reach to "Exceed Mortal Caps" Reach effect. May now be used on two Attributes simultaneously.

            Commentary: I don't think I articulated this one well, though I think the intent is fairly clear. Another lift from 1e, though there it was the capstone power. My intent is that you only need to spend one point of Mana to break the caps on both Attributes, but interpreting it the other way is hardly going to break anything.

            (Matter ●●●●)
            Where the Coalburner's enlightenment shines, the shadows of imperfection weaken. By touching an object, the Coalburner can reshape it as he deems suitable. This explicitly must be creative rather than destructive, but otherwise is only limited by the Coalburner's imagination. Tearing down a wall or warping a sword would not be acceptable uses, but opening a new doorway or transforming the sword into a bucket would be fine. Preparatory work, such as dicing vegetables or shearing wood into lengths, is also acceptable.
            Shaping
            Potency = Matter
            Duration = 3 Turns
            Range = Touch
            Casting Time = Instant
            NOTE: Reach to "Solid Objects" and "Repair Bonus" Reach effects

            Commentary: I like this, but I do worry that it goes against the whole "fruits of your labors" thing. Also considered using Permanence, so it only needs to be activated once, but I think having to keep reactivating it is fine. Makes it seem more like work, rather than "paying" for complete control. That really seems like it goes against the spirit of the Coalburners.


            Magnum Opus
            (Mind ●●●●●) Craft 5, Potent Nimbus 2 or Potent Resonance 2
            Every Coalburner Master experiences a different revelation upon their final refinement as a Traveler - the writings left behind by one imply that accepting yourself for who you are is the truest refinement, while another spoke extensively on defeating his Demons by denying them battle. All are unified by the aura of inner peace they radiate, and the awe felt by those who deal with them.

            For the purposes of social maneuvering, other entity's first impression toward the Coalburner is always [Mind - Entity's Composure] steps more positive. This only applies to entities meeting with the Coalburner in person; entities who can only perceive them remotely are entirely unaffected. The area of effect of the "average" Uncrowned King Master is theorized by the Mysterium to be roughly 50,800 square feet, though reliable test data is obviously sparse.
            Approximation:
            First Impressions
            Potency = Mind (Reach to switch Potency to Primary)
            Duration = Lasting (Two Reach to Lasting)
            Scale = Large House (Reach to Advanced Scale)
            Range = Self
            Casting Time = N/A
            NOTE: Affects everyone directly interacting with the Coalburner, rather than a specific target.

            Commentary: The main text should be clear, but just to specify - this makes them very persuasive, it does not make the person they're interacting with easily convinced by everyone present.

            I think this is a good capstone, with some saintly overtones, but I'm very open to criticism on it. The radius is somewhat irrelevant, but it's also pretty damn big. I would be fine changing it to standard Scale.

            (Matter ●●●●●)
            Though the Forge Masters are the most well-known craftsfolk Legacy, the Uncrowned Kings are artisans in the own right. A Second-Degree Master's understanding of Supernal refinement has grown enough to be able to purify even base Matter. When the Coalburner achieves an Exceptional Success on a Crafts project, they may spend a point of Mana to Lastingly Perfect the item. The ST should apply this in strange ways as appropriate to the item; books behaving as if they were made of Perfected living materials, or dishes that never spoil or sicken, for example. However, the Coalburner can only affect items that have a practical purpose, and explicitly cannot Perfect symbols of authority.
            Approximation:
            Honing The Perfected Form
            Duration = Lasting (Permanence and a Reach to Lasting)
            Potency = N/A
            Range = Touch
            Cost = 1 Mana
            Casting Time = Scene
            NOTE: 2 Reach to "Any Material"

            Commentary: Ooof... this one is a bit of a MEH. My headcannon for the Forge Masters is that they can do something similar - FMs can spend Mana when crafting an item to Perfect it. The difference being that FMs get it sooner, and apply it to any project they work on, while the UKs apply it to any craft they poured their all into.

            On the other hand, it's the Optional capstone. Quite aside from most games not being likely to reach that point, it's not unreasonable to assume most Legacy Masters have their own variation.

            Anyway. I imagine UKs with this like to make gifts for other Mages that are intended to be Dedicated Tools. Which makes the Silver Ladder one of those friends who're impossible to shop for :P
