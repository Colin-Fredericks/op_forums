                        02-04-2026, 06:15 AM
                        Originally posted by Nebulous_Bat View Post
                        Not mechanically but in terms of what a mage actually perceives, what exactly is the difference between Active and Focused Mage Sight? Like if an Acanthus were studying some Thorns wrapped around a Mystery, how would their view change in switching between Active and Focused?
                        With Active Mage Sight, you see symbols of your Path concerning the Arcana that you've incorporated it overlaying the world as a whole. This gives you a general sense of the interplay of things as they relate to the Path. Arcadia has a perspective of things unfolding with a sense of narrative, with a particular sense of some things having portentous significance or highly variable future, so you'll recognise the interplay of circumstances corresponding to that; primarily, that something progressing in a manner with a narrative format is occurring, but not any of the finer details. A Mystery whose nature corresponds to any Arcana included in your Sight will be highlighted strongly; you'll know it has greater significance than the things surrounding it, that the narrative effect it is drawing has supernatural potency.

                        Focused Mage Sight contracts your vision to the point of a single subject, a single Pattern. You go from a general overlay upon the world into seeing the subject of scrutiny entirely in terms of your Path symbols, often with the aid of mana released to form more coherent patterns of it. Successful dice rolls represent the process of the meaning of these symbols being understood by you, allowing you to discern the driving nature of them. For a Mystery, that means acquiring information about its features. For a mundane subject, that means seeing what its experience of reality through the lens of your Path is. When you Focus your Sight on a person you noticed is caught up in something that stands out in Arcadia, you can get an impression of what their role in events is, what has brought them to this point, and the possibilities in their future.

                        If Active Sight is where you see the Thorns and Mists gather around a person in a way that indicates that they have stuff going on, Focused Sight is where those symbols coalesce into a rose in their breast pocket, a rapier at their side, and tears streaming down their face to give the understanding that theirs is the story of a torrid romance (although I'd say it helps to include the Mind Arcanum to have a clearer sense of the emotional context).

                                                            I think the book "Night Horrors: The Unbidden" is instructive here. It's a First Edition book, but I think it's consistent with a lot of the presentation in Second Edition, and indeed is arguably prototypical.

                                                            It depicts a lot of weird beings and phenomena that are a result of Awakened magic, but aren't strictly consistent with the kind of magic that the rules enable one to cast. They run more on a narrative premise of "unconsidered consequences and magic left to decay can turn freakish and disturbing".

                                                            So when it comes to something like a magical item breaking in a magical location as the background for a strange phenomenon, you're not really bound by the limits of how this stuff works for the sake of being applied by players. At best, investigation might determine that a confluence of particulars led to that outcome, and were specific enough as to not be easily replicated.

                                                            It's like all the oddities Star Trek gets up to with transporters. You get somebody improvising a beam through a unique atmospheric phenomenon under constrained circumstances, and bam, two Rikers.
