                        Prerequisites: Sanctum •+, Gnosis 3, Wits 3, Occult 3

                        (•): Your defenses are harder to dispel than normal. When assigning Reach to Advanced Potency, the spell’s Withstand ability against dispellation increases by your dots in this Style, up to a total of +5.

                                Regarding discussions on how Imbued Items work (specifically, their piddly dice-pools for low level spells when used by Sleeper/Sleepwalkers), which of these looks better?

                                Enchanted Proficiency (• to •••••)
                                Prerequisites: Sleepwalker, Occult 2+
                                Effect: Long, practiced, studied use of enchanted and ensorcelled paraphernalia has given you greater ability with using Imbued Items successfully. You made add your dots in this merit to the casting rolls for spells from Imbued Items.

                                or

                                Enchanted Proficiency (• to •••••)
                                Prerequisites: Sleepwalker, Occult 2+
                                Effect: Long, practiced, studied use of enchanted and ensorcelled paraphernalia has given you greater ability with using Imbued Items successfully. You get a +3 bonus on casting rolls for spells from Imbued Items with Arcana ratings equal to this merit or less.

                                Option 1 applies to all Imbued casting rolls from the get-go, but starts very small. Option 2 give a nice, reliable static +3, but you need to get higher rating to use higher spells. One or two dots would be great for most users, with only those finding/using Adept and Master level Imbued Items needing the higher ratings.

                                            Prerequisites:                                             Wits 3, Occult 2
                                            Effect: You have learned to focus your Mage Sight through a collection of tools, and thus open yourself to greater insights from a Mystery. So long as they can be semiotically linked to divination, revelation, or understanding, you may use any number of Path and Order tools as Yantras in Focused Mage Sight.                                                 Agony Ascendant doesn't replace the Mana you gain from scouring - it just also gives you a bonus to spellcasting if you Scour while doing so.

                                                Yeah, Pain to Perfection's a bit of a bum trade - I think I'll swap that over to +1 Mana, +1 Willpower (so Scouring gives you 3mp for 1L(r), or 4mp 1wp for 2L(r), as well as a +2 Yantra if you incorporate it into your spells).

                                                My fault on Beauty In Screams - I actually have an alternative to that. Heck, I'll just replace the merit:

                                                Brutal Sorcery [Style; • - •••]
                                                Agony Ascendant (•) — You may Scour your Pattern as part of casting a spell. If you do so, you may claim a Yantra bonus equal to your dots in this merit in addition to any other benefits gained from Pattern Scouring.
                                                Pain to Perfection (••) — Whenever you Scour your Pattern, you may choose to take an additional level of Resistant Lethal damage. If you do so, you regain an additional point of Mana as well as a point of Willpower.
                                                Beauty In Screams (•••) — You may forgo the normal 3 points of Mana gained from Scouring your Pattern when doing so as a Yantra. In exchange, you gain one additional free Reach for that spell, over and above the limit dictated by your dots in the appropriate Arcana.
