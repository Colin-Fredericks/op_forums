                                    Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post

                                    Then explain the parts of Prime that do cover all supernatural creatures or effects.
                                    There are very little such effects. With a quick glance I only found two. Prime Mage Armor (which seems to me more like a concession to me it less useless) and Supernal Veil which can also affect supernatural creatures.

                                    Against that stand a ton of other spells. Dispel Magic says it can't dispel other supernatural effects, Supernal Vision allows to see the auras of other creatures with a reach but at the same time makes clear that unlike for mages you don't get any nice instinctive knowledge from it but can at best recognize something you've seen before, Word of Command makes you add other arcana to command non supernal magical items.

                                    But this is getting out of hand for the simple question thread. To summarize:
                                    It never specifically said that prime does not apply to other supernatural powers. If you take Supernal Veil and Prime Mage Armor as the norm and say that the other examples to the contrary I listed are flukes then you are correct and prime does govern all supernatural phenomena. If you take it the other way around it doesn't. The spell descriptions are paradoxical here and it was never outright stated in 2ed wether prime covers any and all supernatural phenomena or only supernal ones. Personally the fluff gave me the impression that it was the latter, but this a call that ultimately the ST will have to make for his own game.
