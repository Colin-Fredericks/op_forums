                    AoE effects affect only those in the area, if the subjects leave the area they are no longer valid subjects for an AoE spell.

                    Your example of a Shielding Spell is a spell that has multiple subjects and is not actually cast on the area at all, as you pointed out.

                                                        The Oneiros is both your soul and your mind (in a way) but when you die the mind dies and the soul disappears into the unknown. As such I think that the Oneiros becomes cut off from the rest of the Astral, or at least changed in a way that makes it impossible to visit.
