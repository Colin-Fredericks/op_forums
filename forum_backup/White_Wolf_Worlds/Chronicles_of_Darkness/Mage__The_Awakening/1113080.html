    Focused Mage Sight, or Supernals in Your Scrutiny

    07-07-2017, 11:44 PM
    First off, this isn't about disliking Mage Sight. I like it, your own feelings are respected but I'm looking for what people did with or plan on doing with it.

    While performing a Scrutiny, if you get a Dramatic Failure, no successes are accrued. However, 'If the player has already made a number of rolls equal to the unmodified dice pool, a Supernal entity of the mage’s Path takes note of the mage and may affect him with its powers as long as he maintains his Mage Sight.'

    What are these Supernal entities going to want to do? My only experience with them throughout the books involves mages summoning them when they want something. What does a Supernal entity do when it takes note of the mage? Is it angry or curious? Does it help or hinder?

      I kind of get the impression it's annoyed since Mage Sight is essentially a brute force method of examining the Supernal. It's the metaphysical equivalent of knocking on the gates of heaven with a battering ram.

      That said, Supernal Entities don't seem to be able to help Mages who can't pass their Trial, so it seems appropriate to me that they would take the opportunity to burden the Mage with their Trial (Probably ramped up somewhat from their standard summoned version) as a way of forcing the Mage to do penance. If you can pass, at least they'll leave you alone ("Helping" by not interfering further), maybe if you pass with flying colors they'll offer their assistance.

      I figure handling it like that rams the idea home that a Mage who insists on trying to stumble forward despite their inadequacy to the task (Breaking Opacity down) is an AoH without necessarily incurring the Wisdom risk and may also be an experiential learning opportunity.

      A particularly clever/hubristic/desperate Mage may even use a Dramatic Failure on Scrutiny on purpose, as an attempt to bypass the usual Summoning rules that carries a risk of catching the attention of the Abyss. They wouldn't get the benefit of targeting their summons to a particular kind of Supernal Entity though.

                          The way I see it, an eidolon's attention is incomprehensible. You have a fundamental concept that defines some significant aspect of whatever you are examining suddenly paying attention to you. That's some strange stuff.

                          There are many ways this could go poorly, from it deciding that it doesn't like you to deciding that it does like you. There are even more ways it could go well or simply oddly. (That could also involve its curiosity, positive, or negative attentions.)

                          If the eidolon in question favors the things you are and are doing, it will likely be favorably disposed. If you more strongly represent some oppositional definition, it will more likely dislike you. Forcing a trial on you could be its prelude to doing something nice to you, but that could involve a lot of pain and suffering to gain something you didn't actually want. A spirit removing your vulnerability to injuries caused by weapons as a punishment for something could conceivably happen, say.

                          Seems more likely to me that the interaction would usually be really intense and strongly related to the manifestation of that thing. A leadership eidolon might force a character to try to demonstrate leadership, improving their abilities or demonstrating their weaknesses. Alternately, it might make the character review all leadership choices made and run through some theoretical scenarios. It might even remove any concept of the character being a leader from all minds, if displeased, too.

                          I figure most should be pretty neutral and involve a sort of weird investigation by the thing, experienced by the mage something like Mage Sight scrutiny. (meaning that they see many things about the symbol in question (whether direct or opposite) respecting their self.)
                          I kinda like the idea of a parting effect, something like a Brand, from the eidolon in question that marks them as favored or not of that eidolon, should they leave a sufficient impression and sometimes even if not.

                          Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.
