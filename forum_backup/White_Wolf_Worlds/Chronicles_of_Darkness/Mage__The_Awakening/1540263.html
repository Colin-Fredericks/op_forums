    The Priesthood of the Obfuscating Eclipse - Moros Prime/Death homebrew legacy

    08-13-2024, 01:51 AM

    Priesthood of the Obfuscating Eclipse
    Eclipse Clerics

    The history of the legacy is, appropriately, obfuscated behind layers of misdirection, false information, and half-truths to the point where even the modern masters of this legacy do not know its true origins or history. It is believed to have formed in antiquity, perhaps in Persia, Greece, or Egypt, but some claim that it formed after the Roman Empire adopted Christianity, and still others claim that it formed during the crusades. The only thing that is agreed upon is that the legacy formed when a group of Moros, rarely considered to be a cabal, came together under a solar eclipse to perform a Supernal summoning. It is unknown if these Moros were servants of the Father, or if they summoned a specter that was aligned with the Exarch by accident. Whatever the case, Eclipsed Truth appeared before them, and when the Moros left, the Priesthood of the Obfuscating Eclipse was formed. These Eclipse Clerics had shaped their souls with the help of the Stygian shade to fulfill the work of The Father, hiding the Truth.

    It is important to note two facts, or really the lack thereof, when it comes to the origin story of this legacy. Firstly, many Seers of the Throne claim that the mages who founded the Priesthood of the Obfuscating Eclipse were also Seers who served the Father actively and consciously but there is no evidence for this, it is just as likely that the Moros that had gathered under the eclipse that day were all Banishers, trying to find a way to hide the mysteries from sleepers and thus have less awakened in the world. Secondly, the truth of the eclipse is not known. Those who have summoned Eclipsed Truth since then claim that the sun is blocked out by its presence, so it is unclear if the eclipse was already happening when the summoning was being done, or if it began after the shade had been summoned.

    Whatever the case, the Priesthood persists into the modern era. Its ranks are primarily filled with Seers of the Paternoster Ministry, but ideological banishers also join the legacy, albeit in much smaller numbers. While the Seers see the nameless Banishers as thieves of this legacy they rarely strike out against them, instead they see them as useful tools, as they go about the fallen world doing the Father's work without ever asking for help or resources, leaving the Seers to handle more important works. The only true issue the Seers have with these Banishers are that they tend to get caught by the Pentacle more often, and as such the secrets of the legacy are more likely to fall into the hands of the Pentacle because of this. This has led the Pentacle to believe that this is strictly a legacy of Banishers, so when all the damage is added up, the Seers are quite okay with the results.

    Background: Most mages who come to this legacy have a history in religious or occult organizations that emphasize secrecy, even from its fellow members. Trust is something to never be fully given in those spaces, and it tends to serve them well as an Eclipse Cleric.

    Appearance: The Priesthood the Obfuscating Eclipse tend to dress mundanely as to not draw attention to themselves. Many are leaders of a religious group and dress appropriately for that, but otherwise you would never think twice about seeing them walking down the street.

    Organization: Top down, with Eclipsed Truth being seen as the true leader of the legacy, but in practice those mages that have mastered the legacy's attainments lead, and those who still must learn follow.

    Theory: The mages of this legacy believe that magic should be hidden from all, sleeper and awakened alike, and their magic facilitates this. The Eclipse Clerics are still mages and therefore still obsessive, and tend to take a “none for you, all for me” mindset when it comes to who should be experiencing magic, and even then only if it is part of one of their obsessions. They will even hide magic from other Seers of the Throne. These mages see the sun as the greatest symbol of the Truth, with light representing smaller pieces. You can, and should, snuff out a candle but the sun will always shine, so it is best to block it as best you can. However, when it comes to the awakened, each carries a light within themselves, the soul, and that light should be snuffed out.

    Doctrine and Magic

    Parent Path: Moros
    Parent Order: The Paternoster Ministry or sometimes Ideological Banishers
    Prerequisites: Prime 2, Death 1, Subterfuge 2, one of the following additional skills at 2 dots or higher: Investigation, Larceny, Occult, Persuasion,
    Initiation: Summon the Eclipsed Truth and study its symbolism.
    Ruling Arcana: Prime
    Yantras: Casting under a solar eclipse (+1 for a partial eclipse, +3 for a full eclipse); Veils (+1); Casting magic that only they are aware of (+2); Obscuring a light source (+1)
    Oblations: Keeping a secret; Successfully hiding magic from someone searching for it; Destroying light sources, magical or mundane.

    Attainments
    The attainments of the legacy are not taught but mages, the members of the legacy are discouraged from creating soul stones, and creating a Daimonomikon is punishable by death. The only way mages should learn the attainments is by summoning Eclipsed Truth and studying it.

    First Attainment: First Contact

    Prerequisites: Initiation
    Working in the shadow and darkness is the mission of the Eclipse Clerics, and that is a difficult task when you pick the wrong fight with the wrong mage, especially one more powerful than you. The first lesson taught by this legacy is how to identify the power of the mage who has left behind their magical mark. When scrutinizing the signature nimbus of another mage they may activate this attainment, which functions as “Nimbus Tuning” (Prime 1, SoS 94) with reach for instant casting.

    Optional: Death 1
    Darkness obfuscates, but not from the Eclipse Clerics. This attainment allows the mage to see in non-magical darkness without penalty. The attainment has advanced duration and takes one hour of meditation for it to take effect.

    Second Attainment: Penumbra

    Prerequisites: Prime 2, 3 dots in Subterfuge
    The Priesthood of the Obfuscating Eclipse’s primary goal is hiding magic, and they use this attainment to bring about this goal from early in their training. This attainment functions as “Supernal Veil” (Prime 2, MtAw 168) with reach for advanced duration.

    Optional: Death 2
    The Eclipse Clerics must be able to identify and differentiate between mages and non-mages, as well as identifying how powerful the mage is. Picking the correct target to interfere with is an important part of the Eclipse Cleric’s job, and this attainment assists in that task. The attainment functions like “Soul Marks” (Death 1, MtAw 128) with reach for instant casting and sensory casting.

    Third Attainment: Partial Eclipse

    Prerequisites: Prime 3, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 3 or a second option from the list must be raised to 2
    Some mysteries are too well known for the Eclipse Clerics to simply tuck away, but they can obfuscate the details of the mystery, sending those who study it down false paths. This attainment functions like the spell “Path to Jerusalem” (Prime 2, SoS 28) with reach for advanced duration and the bonus reach effect allowing them to plant false pieces of information in the mystery, or hiding true pieces.

    Optional: Death 3
    In order to hide magic, the members of this legacy also learn to hide themselves from the sight of mages, blending in with the sea of sleepers. The attainment functions as “Suppress Aura” (Death 2, MtAw 129) with reach for advanced duration and instant casting.

    Fourth Attainment: A Darkened Sky

    Prerequisites: Prime 4, 4 dots in Subterfuge
    Eclipse Clerics can return the veil of darkness in each sleepers soul to sleepwalkers, darkening their eyes to the Truth. This attainment is withstood by the resolve of the sleepwalker target. Should the withstand be bypassed the sleepwalker becomes a sleeper for the duration of the attainment. The attainment has reach for both advanced duration and instant casting and the primary factor for the attainment is potency.

    Optional: Death 4
    Darkness is a tool of the Eclipse Clerics, and with this attainment that becomes literal. The attainment functions like “Shadow Crafting” (Death 3, MtAw 132) with reach for advanced duration and instant casting.

    Fifth Attainment: Totality

    Prerequisites: Prime 5, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 4 or a second option from the list must be raised to 3 or a third option must be raised to 2
    The Eclipse Clerics can end nearly all spells they come across, only the spells of masters are safe from this attainment.​ After meditating for an hour near a spell they wish to end, the mage spends one mana to activate this attainment, which functions like “Supernal Dispellation” (Prime 4, MtAw 170) with the reach effect to make the attainment’s effects lasting.

    Optional: Death 5
    It is said that mages who have mastered all the attainments of the Priesthood of the Obfuscating Eclipse do not exist. This is not true, but one must become a master of the legacy in order to know this, and it is all because of this attainment. The attainment costs one mana to activate, along with one hour of mediation. It copies the effect of “Empty Presence” (Death 5, MtAw 133) with reach for advanced duration. ​
