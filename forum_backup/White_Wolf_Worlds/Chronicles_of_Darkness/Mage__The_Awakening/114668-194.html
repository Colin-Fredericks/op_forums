    The answer to both questions is that the soul is now a part of you and, when you cast Shifting Sands, it's affected by the spell in the same way you are. Just as you go back in time and find that there's no second you waiting for it, it does the same.

    In other words, no, there's no ability to duplicate souls here. In the first scenario, you go back in time and the Sleeper's soul vanishes from them at the moment of your arrival, from their perspective. In the second scenario, same story.

    Now, does this have to be the way it works? Of course not. You could have fun with this and make a Mystery out of it. Maybe, instead of vanishing retroactively, the Sleeper has a kind of temporal ghost of their soul. It looks like a soul, but it's fluxed to hell with Time magic and, as soon as the time comes when it would have been harvested, it vanishes.

    Naturally, your curious Reaper might then start experimenting with this; reaping people's souls, then hopping back a few minutes to watch the process. Maybe simultaneously examining the soul in the soul jar while this happens; examining Time and Space sympathies, etc.

    And what happens when you try detaching a temporally stuttered soul and cast a Time Shielding spell on it? What happens if you then put that soul back in the Sleeper?

    Before you know it, a small coastal town is being destroyed by an enormous tornado, there's two moons in the sky, etc.
