            You know what? To the Lower Depths with silk gloves. I will be fully honest in this post. If you feel that my grievances do not apply to you, then please don't reply saying you were offended and that I should check my privilege. Nobody is forcing you to read this.

            Please stop turning the subtext of entertainment into major political issues.

            Do LGBT+ people suffer because of their differences? Yes, absolutely. Do they have the same rights as non LBGT+ people? Yes, definitely. At the risk of sounding like a falsely apologetic bigot, let me say that I have a trans friend. I do not tolerate her existence, because the word "tolerate" implies her existence is wrong. She is my friend, and that's it.

            The world, viewed as a Big Picture, does not give one particle of significance to your personal struggles. It is only when there is reunion and clear, reasonable goals that it makes a difference. There IS internal conflict and prejudice in the LGBT+ community. Founding a group/movement with noble intentions does not forever save it from bigotry if humans are supposed to join. Do you think I'm slandering? Simply do a quick Google search for "tumblr hypocrisy" or anything similar. The LGBT+ movement remains because there are still a lot of very stubborn people who dismiss or attack it without so much as one question asked.

            Am I somehow trying to sneak in a message that all LGBT+ people can only be physically affectionate where no one can see it and that they cannot mention their LGBT+ related experiences in public? No, do not put such words into my mouth. When I see a non heterosexual couple making out, I do my best to not turn that into an excuse for talking politics with someone who is bothered by the sight. My only problem is on the extremely rare but real scenario where said couple does it for attention and controversy fodder, rather than love. That is messed up.

            Yes, entertainment can be a political tool.             But you need to have common sense when deciding if a given piece of entertainment is worth discussing in regards to its possible undue influence on people's lives. Is "Jackass" a psychic virus meant to turn all those who watch it into an army of drooling puppets for [INSERT ANYTHING HERE]? No, just no. Do you wanna discuss works like Orwell's "1984" or Ayn Rand's "Atlas Shrugged"? By all means, go ahead and be mindful of Wheaton's Law. Please don't try to convince me that all art is equal. Your chances of succeeding are microscopic. They are not 0% because I can change my mind with proper dialogue. All art is only equal in its right to exist, if other basic rights are being respected.

            You CAN include and address LGBT+ issues or any other type of prejudice in your TRPGs, bar chats or whatever. However, you should make it obvious that being part of one or more minority groups that have been mistreated for a long time is not the sum of the issues you care about. That would make you a willing caricature.

            This little soapbox o' mine could be a lot longer, but I feel like I made myself clear without aggression. I'll just leave a few last observations.

            Depicting is not synonymous with endorsing.

            You can and should fight for equality before the law, but don't expect universal and permanent acceptance on a cultural level.

            "There is a time and a place" doesn't mean you are being censored. "Do not talk about this, or else" is what censorship means.

            Lastly but not least, hidden agendas are not omnipresent.

            EDIT: regarding magical nomenclature, I would suggest calling the True Name the "Birth Name", and keep Shadow Name as is. You can be okay with your Birth Name or not, neither are automatically wrong. The fact that jerk Mages can use your Birth Name which you don't like to both humiliate you and cast sympathetic spells on you is actually a plot hook if you think about it. You might be Jane now, but Jack did bad things when he was feeling alone and misunderstood. It's up to you and those who think you really deserve help to clear your reputation, so that said jerks will no longer want to Sympathetically Fray you. Or at least face heavy consequences for doing so.

            Bye.

                In my opinion, your True Name is the name of your soul. That is to say that it is something beyond "Mike", "Samantha", or "Bibembop", but it is the core identity of who you are and who you're meant to be. It CAN change, but it is very difficult to do so (requires you consciously choosing to be something other than "yourself")

                As far as Mage is concerned, I think that means that everyone is born ignorant to their True Name. Yet some people, somehow, come to realize who they truly are, and their place in the Fallen World. This is what we think of as Awakening. At the end of your Awakening (be it Mystery Play or Shadow) you come to your watchtower, and have a choice. You can either 1) inscribe your True Name upon the Watchtower, accepting fully who you are and your soul's identity. OR you can 2) reject your True Name, and inscribe YOUR name (however you want to present yourself) on the Watchtower, which is what creates Banisters. (the reason they hate Magic is because, on some deep level, they envy those that could accept who they are, and are ashamed that they could not [or, scarier still, their True Name scared them shirtless]). This being said, Joining a Legacy is difficult because it DOES involve changing your True Name, and even more so for becoming an Archmaster.

                Those are just my immediate thoughts off the top of my head, so feel free to disagree/agree/question/whatever.
