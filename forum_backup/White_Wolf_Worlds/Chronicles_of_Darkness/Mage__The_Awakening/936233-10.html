    Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post
    If you look at the errata you'll see its in fact not a Ruling spell. So once you have correct information we can discuss this.
    Yeah I missed that. But anyway, my argument stands. Heightened Senses does not give you the vision of an eagle or the echolocation of a bat. It explicitely says so. The spell only improves the information you get from your existing senses. I can live with that in the way that the spell does not actually improve the sensory output your organs create (it does not give you the nose of a dog NOR the information the nose of a dog would get) but it improves your interpretation of the information your existing sensory organs get. Some blind people develope astonishingly good sense of hearing for example and I can see how this is Unveiling instead of ruling.

    However Heightened Senses does not give you any information you simply cannot percieve with your own senses. The spell explicitely says that it does not grant new senses. So you don't get nightvision, or echolocation or infrasound or anything like this from the spell at all. And of course it is possible to determine what an eagle would see, but not with Life. To do this, you must know what is actually the difference between an eagles eyes and a humans (percieving 150 images/s, ultraviolet light, faster focusing and so on) and develope a spell that does percieve this information.

    While I guess, we still agree to disagree, I at least wanted to make my argument clear.
