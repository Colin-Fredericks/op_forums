                            The AA value scars, they see them (usually) as badges of honor that tell a story about the person who has them. The Guardians probably value scars too since they're probably the most likely Order to engage in self-harm as a sin purging exercise and the scars would serve as a reminder of how they scar their souls for the greater good. The Free Council would be more fluid on the meaningfulness of scars since it would depend on whatever culture any given Freak is drawing his magical associations from.

                            Users of Death specifically have a spell for inspecting the scars on a soul.

                            Being a story game though, the best way is probably to have an NPC(s) who proudly displays his magical scars, or who takes great interest in the scars of others and see how the PCs react, do they embrace it, reject it, does it create tension or conflicts?

                                Originally posted by Moinen View Post
                                Ashenrogue It's not exactly a system that forces or even encourages scars. I edited the opening post to be more up to date.
                                I guess the question for me becomes why would you want to force scarring? I mean sure, you could say it's realistic, but this isn't really a simulationist style game to begin with. I'll be straight with you in that I don't really like the idea of forcing scars onto players who don't want them. Though if you feel you just have to, you could instate a rule where Lethal (or Aggravated) damage over Stamina always leaves some sort of scarring. To what extent should probably fall to the player to decide. Maybe give them a persistent Scarred Condition for whenever someone questions how they received such weird and nasty scarring and it causes some sort of difficulty.

                                Now if you just want to encourage keeping scars I'd say to run it like Mrmdubois and have the other characters in the setting value meaningful scars, and have them wear theirs as a badge of honor or something. If all of the coolest NPC's in the chronicle are rocking badass battle scars then it might encourage your players to do the same. You could also still go with a Scarring Condition for anyone with particularly strange or noticeable scarring.
