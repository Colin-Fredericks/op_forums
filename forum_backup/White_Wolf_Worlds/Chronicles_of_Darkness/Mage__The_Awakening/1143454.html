              As a chemistry student I disagree with the big boom. Lithium is very reactive, but it doesn't just go boom. Of the alkalimetals it reacts the slowest. If your players were going for Rubidium or Caesium, then an immediate boom on contact with air would be very appropriate.

              BUT you also need to consider that, given what we've seen in the preview, perfected materials seem to be relativly inert. Both mercury and lead, which tend to be quite reactive, are alot less so in their perfected forms.

              Given all these things I would personally say that perfected Lithium, unlike normal Lithium, does NOT react with air. However when specifically reacted with another substance it is an absolute beast of an reductive agent, posessing a highly negative normal potential. Which incidentially would allow one to produce very powerful batteries of it (especially when paired with perfected fluorine. I hope you have a very very high potency version of Alchemists Touch on when handling perfected Fluorine however )

                          The reason I called Lithium as the "representative" of the Alkalies is because it is a part of what is sometimes called the "representative elements"- you know, the first and second rows of the table. While because of their atomic properties they are quit different from the rest of the table, they are considered as "laying the ground" for the next elements in their column. Hence the concept of Lithium as the "symbol of the alkalies".

                          As for the fact that in Awakening there is not "mind shapes reality"- again, I know that. However, Awakening's cosmology is Platonic, and as such reality is only the shadows cast by the Supernal symbols. The way I interpreted that cosmology is that just as there symbols for each of the alkalies, those symbols are, by themselves, incarnations of much deeper symbols, such the the symbol of the alkali metals. And Lithium, as the "representative element" of the alkali metals, is much closer to the symbol of the alkalies in general. Hence the concept of perfected Lithium becoming the "symbol of alkalies", and the rest of the alkalies being defined through the symbol of Lithium.

                          In sort, the way I see it, the symbol of Lithium is much closer to the symbol of the alkalies than any other alkali metals, and as the properties it express in the fallen map themselves in a way that humanity would place it in the top of the alkali metals, and as such make it the representative of the alkali metals. It is not human awareness which decides the importance of the symbols- but it is the human mind which collect those symbols and put them in such a way that it recognize the patterns in the fallen universe, and tries to adapt those symbols into a way which is the closest to the Supernal- even while it is unaware of its actions. The periodic table, which is actually a collection of symbols used in order to name every material on the face of earth, is such an example of humanity unknowingly recognizing the way the Supernal symbols express themselves in the Fallen patterns.

                          In sort, as it much more of the philosophical debate about the meaning of the Supernal symbols in Mage than about the actual properties of the element, I'm not going into delving for into that argument- I have just wished to present my opinion on the subject, and as your interpretation pf Mage's cosmology decides how perfected Lithium would be presented in the setting, unless some canon rules would be provided for such case I don't see a way we could agree about the subject :P
