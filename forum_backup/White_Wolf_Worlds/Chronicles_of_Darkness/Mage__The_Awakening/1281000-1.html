        In actuality, I had all the other attainments penned down, but had no clue what to do for Death 2. So I remembered reading a long time ago that one of the most useful abilities in Quietus (a VTM discipline focused on assassination) was a very early one that created a zone of silence (I think its called Silence of Death).

        After thinking about how it synergizes with the others, it seemed too interesting not to use. I wasn't sure if it fell in Death's purview (without requiring some investment in Forces) or at what level, but at worst it could fall into the "Strange Attainments" clause as something only the Nizari could pull off so easily. It didn't seem game breaking and resonated with the theme, so it felt like a worthwhile addition to their toolkit.

        On a side note, the third Space Attainment doesn't truly use a pocket dimension, its actually a Weaving practice used to create a "Schrodinger's Space" in which the mage can choose what "state" the "cat" is in.

                    Hi!! I've read the draft made by KaiserAfini , and I'm just in love with his aproach.

                    I've been tinkering with a few 2nd edition legacies by now, and one thing I miss from legacies from first edition is the posibility of using, in adition to the main arcanum of the legacy and the optional, a third conjuntional Arcanum.

                    Using this, you may solve the problem of silencing your steps or anything related to sound, and integrate a few quirks with the usage of Forces as a Conjuntional Arcanum.

                    A first aproximation to the solution, could be something like this:

                    First Attainment:
                    Death 1: Spend a scene building a writ of sanctioned assassination for the target, requiring a representational yantra and some data on them. This grants the assassin a variation of the Informed condition, useable only for actions to kill the target. If the writ is destroyed, the Condition goes away.
                    Optional Space 1: After a scene of meditating on the mission, your divine mandate wards you from interference. This applies an inverse variation of Isolation upon yourself or an ally, giving the penalty to any Unsanctioned who try to interact with the beneficiary. You can also apply normal Isolation on a target. Any Sanctioned can interact with them normally, but its a two way street.
                    Conjuntional Forces 1: If the mage knows Forces 1, the writ might be replicated in digital media, thus protecting it from destruction via backups. You can only mantein a maximum of up to Forces copies of the writ, which can't be restored if destroyed (or deleted) unless you cast the attainment again. Recasting the attainment destroys all previous copies of the writ and they need to be replicated again.

                    Second Attainment:
                    Death 2: Makes the mage's aura to look the same as the aura of a sleeper as per the Supress Aura spell, with reach spent on advanced duration.
                    Space 2: All your Attainments can now be used with Sympathetic Range. This opens up a lot of shenanigans, especially with the higher Attainments.
                    Conjuntional Forces 2: If the Mage also knows forces 2, for the duration of the Death attainment, he may silence himself, imposing a penalty to enemies' Perception rolls equal to his Force dots.

                    Also, I' would modify the death part of the Fourth Attainment as follows, as I tend to make the Legacies as selfcontained as possible:

                    Fourth Attainment:
                    Death 4:                     An attainment that combines Deepen Shadows and Without a Trace that allows up to 5 Sanctioned see normally within the shadows, and to be affected by Without a Trace for the Duration. Any Unsanctioned who use supernatural effects to try see within provoke a Clash of Wills, earning [Death] automatic successes for the assassin. Reach are spent on Instant Use, Advanced Scale and Advanced Duration.


                    Hope this is helpful!!

                                        Eleizel Using ectoplasm to create a mold and transforming it into a part of their pattern is a very cool idea, very resonant with the Moros. But I had an idea that was a bit different, the Gardeners use Life as Ruling and Matter as optional. Mechanically, they are an attempt to create a healer Legacy without only focusing on healing spells, mixing in some Tzimisce/Yuuzhang Vong horror into the greater focus on medicine. Thematically, they are the mages that wept at the fates of Gilgamesh and Enkidu (one of their Attainments even reflects this), swearing to do what they could not, in eternal defiance of the Exarchs. They are ancient, recently rediscovered and reintegrated into Pentacle culture by a joint Mysterium-Free Council project, much to the horror of the Guardians.

                                        Here is the first draft of the Attainments, if there is any interest, I can flesh them out:

                                        Life 1
                                        Analyze Life, with Reach assigned to instant use. If used to reveal medicinal applications of a living being, it shows all possible uses of every part.

                                        Matter 1
                                        Craftsman's Eye that instantly reveals all medicinal applications of an object (medicine, tools, exam machines, chemical substances, etc). Techno-organic beings are also within its purview.

                                        Life 2
                                        Purge Illness with Reach assigned to instant use. It manifests as tears that can be stored for later use, but can only cure one Illness at a time. It can also permanently cure transplant rejection of a specified cause. If used on a being successfully analyzed on the previous turn with the first Attainment, it bypasses the disease's Withstand rating (like tailoring the treatment to their needs).

                                        Matter 2
                                        The Gardener can a specify a medicinal goal (cure a disease, find a given herb, perform an operation, find a blood donor, find the patient's dossier, etc) and this will highlight everything organic, inorganic and techno-organic that is relevant to that goal, but not why. It will also highlight up to [Matter] medical supplies the Gardeners specify, making them great field medics and able to quickly take stock of supplies wherever they are.


                                        Life 3
                                        Their signature fleshcrafting ability. A Life variation of Shaping with Reach assigned to instant use and sensory range. It can be used to graft parts from one being to another. Changes are permanent yet still subject to the rules of the Fallen World. In other words, incompatible parts will require the second Attainment to stop the body's rejection of them.

                                        It can be used instantly to mold eyes shut, close mouths, bind legs to one another or for improvising "handcuffs", pull organs from the person, etc. Withstood by Stamina.

                                        Transplanting limbs, organs and so on requires extended Intelligence + Medicine rolls, but incompatible ones suffer from transplant rejection.

                                        It can also be used to replicate the voice and/or appearance of a being of the same species, or to alter one's own, using an extended Intelligence + Crafts roll, but it cannot affect Size.


                                        Matter 3
                                        A version of Shaping with Reach assigned to instant use and altering solids. If used to create medical equipment or prosthetics, it reduces the number of successes needed by [Matter].

                                        It can also be used to integrate mechanical augmentations and prosthetics into a living beings using extended Intelligence + Medicine rolls. Fullmetal Alchemist style automail and Adam Jensen style augmentations are all possible if they have the components ready.

                                        Many determined fleshcrafters use this in the hunt of cryptids with valuable organs, ripping walls and barriers open with their bare hands.

                                        Life 4
                                        A Gardener must be able to go anywhere to find a new remedy or heal their patients. This acts as Transform Life with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration, creating up to [Life] transformations.

                                        Gardeners normally keep this effect active whenever they can, altering the properties of cell division to create new cells ever closer to their peak years, granting them eternal youth and an ability to remain in active duty indefinitely.

                                        Matter 4
                                        The Gardener becomes able to transform their limbs into bio-technological medicinal tools (look up the Shapers of the Yuuzhan Vong for a good representation of how this looks). This allows them to receive a bonus of [Matter] to any Medicine rolls for the duration. Furthermore, they can take an extended Intelligence + Medicine roll to degrade up to [Matter] points of damage of their choice, one per success. Lethal becomes Bashing and Bashing is fully healed. Attempting to degrade Aggravated damage requires spending a point of mana. Reach is assigned to instant use and advanced duration.


                                        Life 5
                                        Transform life costing one mana and one Willpower point, requiring a scene of preparation to activate. It allows up to [Life] characteristics to be added, has a Lasting effect and allows them to be inherited. When used on a being the Gardener personally fleshcrafted, they can allocate Potency instead to allow one of those changes to be passed down, at a ratio of one point per change. Plants, animals and techno-organic beings are all valid targets for this Attainment.

                                        Matter 5
                                        The Forge Masters may be the virtuosos of Perfected Metals, but the Gardeners of Gilgamesh are the masters of Perfected Medicine. This is a variation of Hone The Perfected Form, costing a point of Mana, with Reach assigned to instant use and making the effect Lasting. It allows the Gardener to suppress the negative or positive medicinal effects of a substance. A chemo treatment can be made to have no negative effects, alcohol can be perfected to leave a pleasantly drunk sensation without dulling the senses or attacking the liver, unhealthy food can be made to cause no harm and drugs can be stripped of side effects, but it cannot stop a scalpel from cutting.

                                        With conjunctional Forces 3, radioactive materials can also be affected, creating radiation therapies with no side effects, X-rays that will not endanger the internal organs, etc.

                                        Remember, nothing says "Destroy the followers of the Lie" like a hand made army. I am sure those rumors of how the Legacy first disappeared when they lost control of their creations and were wiped out in a tide of horror are probably just Guardian propaganda.

                                                Ok, bringing this back into focus, I'm not sold on the informed condition for the first Death attainment, Informed works best as a one time use condition, I mean you could take it like that if you want, but for something like this I think we can go for a custom made condition. Took a page out of werewolves Siskur-Dah condition for this though.

                                                Sanctioned
                                                (Persistent)

                                                Your character has been contracted by the Nizari and now has a mission to assassinate one person, for the duration of his mission, he gains bonus dice equal to the Death's arcanum mastery of the contracting mage for all actions that involve carrying his mission or they can be used to impose a penalty to anyone that would try to stop him from completing his mission this is chosen when taking the condition, on the other hand, any action that is not working towards advancing his mission suffers a this bonus as penalty. For Sleepers an Sleepwalkers, this is always a breaking point with a -3 penalty as you are setting yourself to commit murder (normal penalty would -5 for premeditated murder), Awakened mages can become inured to this using the normal methods but otherwise count this as an act of hubris on the understanding level.

                                                Resolution: Completing their mission, destroying the contract writ.

                                                Beats: Any exceptional success carrying on their mission grants a beat.

                                                Too crazy or just enough crazy? as for inuring, it would make sense that mages of the Nizari would make an Assassin's Masque where they are inured to the act of murder under certain circumstances. I'm not a fan of just inuring the act of murder.

                                                    I looks a bit too potent for an Initiation level ability. Remember this is the 1st Attainment, yet its giving a Perfecting bonus and Fraying penalties. So if you mix the penalty of this Attainment with the one from the reverse Isolation, it does escalate its power by a good amount, at least making it powerful enough to require Conditions and boons to fight it. It needs to be useful and something you would want to prepare often, but not so much as to compete with the higher Attainments.

                                                    I like the Connected custom condition Mrmdubois suggested:

                                                    +2 to all mundane actions involving eliminating the target. It can also be resolved to upgrade a mundane action to an Exceptional Success. Lasts as long as the Attainment does, in other words Advanced Duration, which means master Nizari having up to a year to kill the target , ones with Death 2 having an entire day, Death 3 having a week, which is a pretty generous time between casts.

                                                    it gives a really useful benefit while also allowing for a thematic resolution with the finishing blow. I am also a fan of the conjunctional Force components Eleizel came up with, giving the Nizari more flexibility.

                                                    As for the Wisdom loss, you can see the contract as an extension of Legacy Wisdom. With it, the Nizari who crafted it can avoid the Act of Hubris, because he confirmed his sacred duty through the ritual. But it only works for that Nizari, other Nizari who did not craft their own writs are not empowered with the authority to carry out the execution. So destroying it might make more Wise Nizari withdraw, giving the target a bit of breathing room. But the cabal mates do not get the benefit, they are empowered to aid in the task, but have not been deputized to become divine executioners like the Legacy members. This means that there is an incentive for the Nizari to strike the finishing blow or Guardians with the proper Masques (or those entrusted by the Order with using Masques wisely), which resonates with their role as the police of the Awakened. Its also an idea that comes from my wish to make them self contained, give them the assassin starter kit and they are good to go, without needing to spend 4XP on an additional merit.

                                                            Legacy: The Nizari




                                                            Origins Parentage
                                                            • Path: Mastigos
                                                            • Order: Guardian of the Veil
                                                            • Concept: Assassins
                                                            • Appearance: The Nizari are encouraged to not make many waves, in their mundane lives they could be just about anybody, they many faces and backgrounds, quite a few of those are actually fabricated, they make heavy use of their masques to have several background ready for what a job would require.

                                                              They only recruit from members of the order, those that show a great level of dedicaion and self sacrifice draw their attention and the very exigences from the order cull those that wouldn't be a good fit.
                                                            • Background: The Nizari Fidai are what the old order of assassins was called, dated back to the 11th century days they were born in what is now the ruined Alamut fortress on the South Caspian province of Gilan near the Rudbar region in Iran, lead by a mystierious "Old man of the mountain" they were a political and religious faction of Shia Islam, or so history would have you believe, in reality they were a sect of Guardians that during their time used the political and religious turmoil of the era to further their secret agenda.

                                                              Much of the legacy's past was lost with the destruction of their forstresses and the burning of the library of Alamut, when they were invaded by the Mongol Empire during the invasion of Khwarism or so they would have you believe in reality they orchestated their own fall to cover the legacy in another layer of secrecy and so they could spread their numbers further accross the world, somethings are commonly known about the order though, it's founder was a Guardian Epoch of the Mastgos Path, Hassan-i Sabbah responsible for the widespread nature on the middle east, though only a limited group of Nizari were actually awakened members of the legacy. He saw and suffered under the regime of the Seers of the Throne that manipulated events to steal the reign of the Caliph orchestating a coup and putting one of their own in the throne, captured for a time he took the opportnity to meditate and plan in his prison waiting and bidding his time untilt the moment was right to act, he was changed by the experience and in his very soul he rejected the Hubris of the Seers, change as he was he founded his movement taking over the fortress of Alamut and spreading from there. He used an army of sleepers to combat the influence of the Seers of the Throne using asymmetrical and psychological warfare and surgical strikes particulary their assassinations of key individuals.

                                                              And for the better part of three centuries they were a force to be reckon with, having succefully assassinated key figures across their early history, two known Caliphs, many viziers, sultans and crusader leaders.

                                                              Nowadays the legacy no longer subscribes to a particular set of beliefs religious or otherwise, you can see traces of them among the history of the world, the cold war was basically their playground, in more modern settings they can be easily found as part of black ops teams in any miliatry or as hitmen among the criminal underworld, some venerate the Santa Muerte in the mexicans cartels, so work in any of the soup letter intelligence agencies across the world, their background may vary, though their purpose and MO remain the same.
                                                            Doctrine

                                                            Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Death 2, Status 2 Guardian of the Veil and at least one mastered Masque.
                                                            • Thesis: It is the nature of the Diamond Wheel to advance, refining all souls while flattening the trail for the arrival the Hieromagus, who shall redeem all sins and reclaim the Imperium Mysteriorum for mankind. The Exarchs would chain its movements so this does not come to pass.

                                                              But there is one Truth they forget: All things contain shatterpoints, precise locations that can be stricken to utterly destroy them. So too does destiny, for a simple phrase or speech can change the course of history or an army can be rendered ineffective by the death of few key figures. For the chosen few, even the fetters of the Tyrants can become evident. See the chains of the lie, become the blade that will sever its weakest link and allow the Diamond Wheel to turn unhindered.
                                                            • Organization: The unifying doctrine of the Nizari is their dedication to their cause, what is their cause changes from Caucus from Caucus, but some things remain constant, they are never the ones leading, they can hold authority over others, but never are they the leader or heriarchs on the concilium, they are followers, very dedicated followers, but just followers in the end, if such would take such lofty ambision for himself it better be on a temporary manner and setting the stage for a proper leader to replace him one way or another or he would see himself eventually culled from the ranks of the Legacy. No, the Nizari themselves don't rule, they are the power behind the throne others are the sword and shield of the Pentacle orders, the Nizari are happy to be their silent blade in the night.

                                                              There is a reason for this though, a Nizari for the most part try to avoid the trappings of hubris and one of the easier ones to fall comes from sitting in positions of power so they limit themselves this way, there is also the belief that they are just paving their mythical messiah, the Heriomage who they will submit under for judegement of their dedication to its cause, the unfaithful would be cast aside while the steadfast and loyal will be recieved with open arms, finally having sanction from above actually empowers them in their missions allowing them to carry out their assassinations wihtout risking their wisdom in the act.

                                                              They work within the order, they are Guardians of the Veil first and they follow the orders traditions and practices, they make it to the rank os Susceptors most of the time where they settle down, there are occassional grand masters in the legacy but their focus is on recruitment and management of the order members in the local caucus under the vigilant eye of the Epoch, they aren't really numerous as they not many have the temperament to dedicate themselves to the levels that the Nizari do.
                                                            • Initiation: The Nizari ritual of initiation is rather straightfoward but by not mean easy, quite the contrary, in fact the prospective Nizari is tested against an impossible task, this test the initiate in their resourcefulness and dedication, he's pretty much set out to fail in a controled scenario, most commonly he is set up against a disguised member of the legacy willing to play the part of a mark, though to prevent misshaps he is not told to kill his target, just to capture it alive wich normaly adds another layer of difficulty to the task as he's told to do it discreetly and without being caught on the act. The mark is prepared for this and stacks the deck against the prospective new member, occasionaly with his mentor help in a way that is made sure to mess up his plan, how he reacts and adapts under this circumstances makes or breaks the prospective Nizari, though if he actually succeeds against all odds he's quickly inducted into the Legacy.
                                                            Magic

                                                            Ruling Arcana
                                                            Death

                                                            Optional Arcana
                                                            Space

                                                            Yantras
                                                            Detailed files about a Mark (+1), Having been Sanctioned to act by a superior (+2), Tool or weapons of assassination (+1), Using stimulants to keep focused (+1, +2 if a negative condition is caused), While in the presence of their mark and undetected (+1)

                                                            Oblations
                                                            Studying a target's life, a long session of planning a hit, Shadowing a target, Infiltrating a target's social group, meditatiing on why the target needs to die, announcing the intent to kill the target while explaining why he is going to die.



                                                            Attainments First Attainment: Deadly Sanction
                                                            Prerequisite: Death 1
                                                            • Spend a scene building a writ of sanctioned assassination for the target, requiring a representational yantra and some data on them. This grants the assassin and any that have signed their names on the writ the Sanctioned condition, useable only for actions to kill the target. If the writ is destroyed, the Condition goes away.
                                                            Sanctioned: The character enjoys a +2 to all mundane actions involving eliminating the target. It can also be resolved to upgrade a mundane action to an Exceptional Success. Lasts as long as the Attainment does, which means master Nizari having up to a year to kill the target , ones with Death 2 having an entire day, Death 3 having a week, this condition also prevents member of the Legacy from commiting an sin against Wisdom when he set's up to kill his target, but only when it comes to direct actions against his target or targets, killing allies, retainers or bystanders still counts as an Act of Hubris, non legacy members however don't benefit from this.

                                                            Optional Arcana: Space 1
                                                            • After a scene of meditating on the mission, your divine mandate wards you from interference. This applies an inverse variation of Isolation upon yourself or an ally, giving the penalty to any Unsanctioned who try to interact with the beneficiary. You can also apply normal Isolation on a target. Any Sanctioned can interact with them normally, but its a two way street.
                                                            Second Attainment: Deadly Silence/Reaching Hands
                                                            Prerequisite: Death 2
                                                            • Renders a target (the Nizari, the victim, the murder weapon, etc) completely silent. Reach is assigned to instant use and advanced duration.
                                                            Optional Arcana: Space 2
                                                            • All of your Legacy Attainments can now be used with Sympathetic Range.
                                                            Third Attainment: Death's Touch/Hidden Armory
                                                            Prerequisite: Death 3
                                                            • As the Decay spell with [Death] potency and Reach assigned to instant use and Structural damage.
                                                            Optional Arcana: Space 3
                                                            • Physics tells us that no two objects can occupy the same space, but such things cannot get in the way of an assassin's divine mission. After a scene of preparing to go out on the field, they can link objects together, allowing one to exist at a time while the other is in a "pocket dimension". Swapping between them is a reflexive action. So a pen can be used to smuggle a gun in, a ring can become a knife or a trusted friend can unknowingly be a time bomb. Up to [Space] pairs of objects can be linked at a time.
                                                            Fourth Attainment: Mantle of Darkness/Garrote of the Heart
                                                            Prerequisite: Death 4
                                                            • This attainment combines Deepen Shadows and Without a Trace that allows up to 5 Sanctioned see normally within the shadows, and to be affected by Without a Trace for the Duration. Any Unsanctioned who use supernatural effects to try see within provoke a Clash of Wills, earning [Death] automatic successes for the assassin. Reach are spent on Instant Use, Advanced Scale and Advanced Duration.
                                                            Optional Arcana: Space 4
                                                            • Twists the nature of sympathetic links, but not the strength. So a person's link to their spouse could go from "love" to "violent repulsion". Had you borrowed the "love" link, you could alter it to "trusted confidant". Or even alter the link one has to a pet from "loyal friend" to "pathological fear". Reach is assigned to instant use and advanced duration.
                                                            Fifth Attainment: Supernal Death/Umbral Roads
                                                            Prerequisite: Death 5
                                                            • Spend a mana to kill all Unsanctioned supernatural effects in the area Withstood by their potency rating. Reach is used on instant use and mana is spent on activating Everywhere.
                                                            Optional Arcana: Space 5
                                                            • As the Teleportation spell with Reach assigned to instant use.
