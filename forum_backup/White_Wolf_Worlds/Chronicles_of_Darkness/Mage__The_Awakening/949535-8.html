    Originally posted by Prometheus View Post
    I was thinking about the Living Perfected materials and why you can't perfect the entire organism and there is one perfect explanation for that:

    Sympathy.

    Or, more specifically, Connected sympathy. An entire body (plus soul stones, familiars, etc...) has a Connected sympathy to the individual's soul and, on some level, *is* the soul. This poses a problem for when you want to perfect the body, because you would essentially be attempting to perfect a part of the soul as well. As parts of the soul are intensely interconnected (again, with Connected sympathy) and are essentially the same object, you can't just choose to perfect a part of it, to perfect a soul, you have to perfect the entire thing. Attempting to perfect the body means to try and perfect only a part of the soul and that change will simply not take.

    Parts of the body, on the other hand, possess only Strong sympathy to the soul and so can be perfected.
    I thought it said you can't perfect any whole living entity, not just humans? Which also raises questions when considering plants, given that, for many of them, you can pull off a piece, stick it in the ground, and it turns into a new plant.

        The plants would be an interesting experiment. I would guess they count as separate entities as long as they contain in themselves the entire essence of what it means to be a "plant", which would be the capability to grow into a new one. You have that capability, you are a whole entity.

        Maybe the reason for why things work this way is the capability of souls of living beings to cause self initiated change of themselves - that's practically the difference between Life and Matter as well and the reason why you can perfect a stone, when stones have representation in the dreamtime as well. The changes that living beings go through would erode the perfection they would possess. So yes, you can target living being with Life magic, even indefinitely. You can Perfect their body with a perfecting spell by imposing a bunch of symbols from the Supernal on the body, but you can never make the mundane substance more perfect without using magic as a Lasting effect, as you can with metals and other materials. Or, well, you could Ascend. And yes, other living animals can apparently too. Read the Wellspring chapter in Astral Realms. It also says nothing about Ascended stones.

        You can perfect a severed limb, because it no longer has a soul, or at least doesn't have a soul that can change itself.

                                            As best I can understand, what your soul is is the thing that connects you to humanity as a whole. It's your twig that connects to the larger branch of the Temenos (and in turn to the trunk of the Anima Mundi). If you don't have a soul, you've effectively lost your Humanity Membership Card. Souls are interchangeable because as long as you've got a card, you're a human, and you get to participate in the club. Non-human beings don't have souls because they don't need souls. A dog can be a dog or a spirit can be a spirit without needing a soul that says they are.
