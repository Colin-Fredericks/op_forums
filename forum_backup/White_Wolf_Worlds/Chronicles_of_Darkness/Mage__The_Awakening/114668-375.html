            I'm trying to understand the rules for summoning a Supernal Entity. But I'm not getting very far.
            There is a lot written about summoning in the Mage the Awakening Second Edition book, but I haven't found much about it on the forums.

            Let's make a fairly weak Mage that wants to summon a very powerful Specter.

            The target is a Specter of Stygia called "Utnapishtim", a Master of Death and Mind.
            Am I correct in assuming this must therefor be a Rank 5 Supernal Entity?

            The summoner is a mage who specializes in summoning.

            Relevant stats:
            Gil (Gilgamesh)
            Path Moros
            Gnosis 2, Death 3, Mind 3, Matter 1
            Mana 11/2 (total/turn)

            Health 6, Speed 10, Will 10, Wisdom 7, Init 9, Def 6


            Attributes

            Int 2, Wits 2, Resolve 5
            Pres 1, Mani 1, Comp 5
            Str 1, Dex 4, Stam 1

            He doesn't scare easily and with his high Initiative and speed he can run away really fast if it comes to that.
            When on the job he'll use Mind 2 "Augment Mind" to boost his Intelligence to 5 for however long this is going to take.

            I think for Skills only Occult is relevant and I don't need to stat out the rest.
            Occult 5, Skill Specialty Summoning, Asset skill.

            Also lets give him Athletics 4, Skill Specialty Running.
            He may end up needing to outrun a Gulmoth after all.


            Arcana
            Matter 1, Death 3, Mind 3


            Merits

            High Speech, Order status (SL), Sanctum 1, Safe Place 1, Demesne 3
            Professional Training 5 page 106
            Profession: Summoner
            Networking
            Contacts
            (his teacher)
            Contacts
            (another Summoner)
            Continuing Education
            (9-Again for Asset Skills)
            Breath of Knowledge
            (Not relevant here)
            On the Job Training
            (Not relevant here)
            The Routine
            (Spend Willpower to reroll all failed dice on the first roll)


            Gil is in his Sanctum in his summoning room. He has no helpers because this is confusing enough as it is.
            The room lies in a Demesne.

            Calling up Utnapishtim requires that Gil kows how. Fortunatly another summoner told him all about this powerful Specter.
            Gil is a Moros and a Disciple in all paths Utnapishtim has, a requirement for summoning an Entity of Stygia.

            Summoning a Supernal entity is an extended action. Each roll takes an hour.
            Starting the ritual costs 2 Mana because Gil has specified that this summon must have Mind as a second Arcanum.

            Gil needs a base of 10 successes to bring the entity across the Abyss and into the Fallen World.
            As this is a Rank 5 Entity, we add five successes per Rank of the entity past 1.
            The needed total is now 30!

            He wants the Entity to talk to him regarding what tasks it wants in exchange for helping Gil, and on later summonings Gil may ask Utnapishtim to cast a spell for him.
            Supernal Entities having infinite Reach can be quite useful. We add 2 needed successes so Utnapishtim can stick around for a hour without taking damage.
            32 successes needed...

            We subtract a whole 3 needed successes because Gil is in a Demesne oriented to the Realm Stygia.
            29 successes needed.

            Gil has his summoning circle in a specially prepared room, themed after Stygia. Wallpaper with ghosts on them, paper skeletons, scented candles (lavender), even a plastic skull he can wave around and brood cryptically over while performing his ritual. That's how you know he's a Professional!
            This enviroment lets us roll Intelligence + Occult. Every two successes on this roll removes one success from the target number of the summoning spell. His (boosted) intelligence is 5, his effective occult is 6 because he has a Skill Specialty. He re-rolls the failures because he has the Professional Training Merit.
            Lets say he gets 8 successes. We subtract 4 from the summoning total.
            25 successes needed, it seems impossible.

            Here's where I start to get fuzzy on what to do.

            To get the needed successes we need to roll some dice.
            The number of rolls we can make is fixed to our Gnosis + Arcana. So 5. This is how it's written in the segment on Extended Actions (page 214).
            Our dicepool is also 5.

            If we could keep making rolls after 10 rolls (and 10 hours!) we start risking Abyssal intrusion (page 95).
            We get this number from the character's Resolve + Composure + any successes allocated to avoid Abyssal intrusion.
            We didn't assign any, would that have been wise? I don't see that being usefull unless you're, say, Gnosis 10, Death 5 and so get 15 rolls.
            Even then, you only get an Annunaki poking a tentacle through if you are extremely unlucky.

            Gil gets only 5 rolls anyway. He's at zero risk, barring a Dramatic Failure.

            We are likely to get around 10-12 successes after 5 hours of chanting, dancing and brooding ominously.
            I don't see how he could summon this being. Is there no way to get more rolls? Or subtract more from the needed total?

            Gil might just manage a Rank 1 Specter for a hour (12 successes needed). But even at a higher Gnosis, he won't be able to do much more.
            Would Teamwork, (page 214) make a diference? Get some more Moros to help? Would they need at least a dot in Mind as well?
            Even one of the helpers getting a bad roll is so punishing it seems you might als well start over with the ritual.

            I must be missing something, or misunderstanding something. English is my 2nd language.

            Thoughts?

            Ps; reading posts on the forum is a lot of fun, but making a post takes a lot of effort!

            Pps; More Gnosis, while expensive to get, seems to help al lot. You get 1 extra roll with 1 extra dice per roll for each Gnosis after all.
            At Gnosis 5 you average about 24 successes.

                            While I agree that the spell doesn't necessarily work off of objective truth (this would break the setting and I actually had a whole thing written up about that before realizing it's not the point), I believe, in your example, the spell would still work.

                            Unknowingly propagating a lie doesn't change the fact that the scenario in question is an active deception and one that should ping Pierce Deception. The fact that it's being borne by an unknowing pawn doesn't change that fact. What matters, in my opinion, is whether or not the scenario in question is born by an intent to deceive, regardless of where, specifically, that intent originated.
