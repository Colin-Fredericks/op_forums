                                                        I honestly don't think it has been codified anywhere.
                                                        I'd base the mechanics on fire, actually. Three stats instead of two, though.
                                                        Coverage: How much of the victim is covered in acid. From 0 to 3, where 0 is just a small drop, and 3 is completely drenched.
                                                        Strength: How much damage the acid does per turn. From 0 to 3. With three being highly concentrated sulphuric acid, or aqua regis, or so.
                                                        Type: What type of damage does it do. From Bashing to Aggravated. Although I would keep Agg acid for the magically created stuff and so.

                                                        Anyhow, after exposure it to the acid, you decide how much coverage the victim got, and the strength of the acid, and apply that amount of damage of the chosen type, repeat every turn until the acid is washed away, or the victim dead.

                                                        Armor reduces coverage, possibly down to none, depending on how much it covers, but the acid will work on that to...

                                                        Overpowered? Probably, but I just pulled this off of the top of my head, so don't expect perfection.
