                So for me, the three that I would pick are:
                Thyrsus - Dreamspeakers
                Obrimos - Perfected Adepts
                Mastigos - Clavicularius

                Few reasons for this.

                I think the Dreamspeakers are themselves just a great Legacy to illustrate the Free Council not as technomancers or something similar. They emphasize the Libertine ideal of drawing on human belief systems and working outside of the Diamond and Seer frameworks. It's a solid obvious Thyrsus Legacy and also a good "break stereotypes" Free Council one.

                I like Perfected Adepts since I think the one group that seems to have a dirth of Legacies in general seems to be the Arrows overall. It's also nice since it shows a bit of how the themes of a Path outgrow into other Arcana in general. And if there's going to be an Arrow-specific one, I'd greatly rather have the about physical self cultivation than what in effect is a bit too on the nose modern samurai wankery thing.

                Clavicularius just seem neat, a very iconic bit of magical thematics to utilize and stand out nicely I think as a result. I think this is the one that I would say is most negotiable on my tastes though.

                If there's a real need for a Mysterium/Mastigos one, the Sphinx is neat for being very Mystegogue and metamagic-based in its goals. But also just some Tamers of the Cave might not be bad either.

                        I want to see the Whipping Boys... I like those...
                        But since it's unlikely that they will get chosen as the Obrimos example, I'll say that I'd be really happy with either of the Adepts, preferably the Ascended.

                        I agree with the Dreamspeakers for Thyrsus. It is shamanism on a whole different level, plus the fact that Thyrsus have Mind as Inferior has been a bit of a problem for them in my campaigns. Having a Mind-focused Legacy would be really nice.
                        But if their reliance on the Astral is a problem, perhaps the Keepers of the Covenant would fit? They are an enhancement of the Thyrsus archetype, after all.

                        For Acanthus I'd like the Daoine. Going all-out on the Fate part.

                        Finally, attached to no specific path, I'd like to see an update of the Scions of God. I like their ideas of being kinda-sorta a spirit. But their fluff needs serious fixing, as it is extremely unfocused.
