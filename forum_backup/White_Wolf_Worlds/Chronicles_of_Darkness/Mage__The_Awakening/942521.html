    Archmage Hacks

    08-03-2016, 02:10 AM
    So, The Imperial Mysteries are likely to be achieved in one of my games soon. Most of it looks like it will largely be able to work as written, with a few minor adjustments. A couple of things seem like they're going to need more adjustment, though:

    1) The Arcane Experience Cost for actually crossing the threshold. The normal process has you expending one Arcane Experience per roll during some parts of the process. Given that 2E experiences are worth more than a 1E Experience Point, should this be adjusted to one Arcane Beat per roll in 2E? Should it stay as one Arcane Experience? What about other things that cost Arcane Experience Points?

    2) Imperial Spell Factors
    A) Potency seems easy enough - 2 points of potency per step instead of one, in addition to making the spell undispellable without Imperial Practices.
    B) Duration would have a basic level of One Day -> Month -> Year (or say, ten years instead?). Should Lasting be an option on there?
    C) Scale - this is the one I have a serious problem with. All of the scale factors listed in IM are less than what is achievable by merely Advanced Scale in 2E. Thoughts on an appropriate progression?

                              First: I don't like just giving Advanced Spell Factors to archmasters for two reasons: first, it clovers all of those Utility Attainments that are built around “use Advanced Spell Factor X without the need to Reach”. Second, as pointed out above, there are unintended consequences to doing so. As well, a Seeker is probably going to have quite a bit of extra room to work with within the existing Reach mechanics: remember that the more you exceed the spell's Arcana requirements, the more Reach you can use without risking Paradox. So even as is, an Archmaster will be pulling off Master-level spells just as effectively as a Master pulls off Adept-level to Initiate-level spells. If it ain't broke, don't fix it.

                              As well, the shift from Mage to Archmaster means that all of your Arcana are either Imperial or Ruling: that is, none are Common or Inferior. This, too, has implications that should not be ignored.

                              The way I'd handle Imperial Spell Factors would be this: spend an Arcane Beat on a spell factor, and that factor's dice penalty gets halved. On the downside, the spell is treated as if you used too much Reach even if you haven't; i.e., you always roll a Paradox pool, even if it's just a Chance Die. And you can't use Advanced Casting Time when enhancing a spell in this way.

                              Imperial Practices work as written, too, but with “it must be an extended action” replaced by “it cannot use Advanced Casting Time”.

                              The next issue involves Imperial Attainments. Where the one-dot Attainment lets you see the Supernal and the three-dot Attainment lets you summon a Supernal Being into the Phenomenal World, the six-dot Attainment lets you physically enter the Supernal World. You can conduct an Astral Sojourn while physically in the Supernal World,

                                  Universal attainments to consider:

                                  Identify Quintessence - Archmasters of an arcana are able to intuit how the nature of that arcana applies to a desired effect in the Fallen World, predicting the required aspects and understanding to achieve the desired effect. They also know when they have witnessed such sorts of patterns when they learn from it.

                                  Resort to Golden Road --- This sounds more like a property of the archmaster template than part of the
                                  Create Chantry --- Arguably either a part of the template OR knowledge of the arcana.
                                  Yantra Schmantra --- Archmagi understand confluences of magic. When casting a spell, they can apply any number of Yantras. (Maybe they don't count any Yantras that are also quintessence against their Yantra limits.)
                                  (My old thread on supernal trump---buried somewhere on the old forums---could provide some ideas for specific examples.)

                                  For the most part, I'd suggest simply using the systems pulled in straight. However, there's Imperial Spell Factors to consider as well as the ritual spell differences.

                                  Fix Duration Spell Factors by making them big and still the same order of expansion as Advanced Duration Spell Factors. 'Twas a silly oversight. (You need a math-minded sort to review these sorts of things, guys.)

                                  Imperial Casting Time: An archmagus can cast breathtaking effects for an investment of appropriate time and effort. For each Yantra* used in a spell, a level of Imperial Casting Time may be applied to a casting. This allows extended casting rules be applied. Each level allows one further roll be made to cast the spell, cast as normal for a ritual casting, but applied together as an extended roll. (*Possibly for each quintessence, arcane experience, permanent willpower, mana or something else.)

                                  Appropriate Conditions may be added per the normal rules and the Storyteller is free to add reasonable options (and we are free to come up with Attainments that interact here, too) but all spells can benefit from a set of spell-specific additions.

                                  * Reduced cost - Spell costs 1 less mana, willpower, or Arcane Experience
                                  * Faster Caster - Further casting rolls take half the casting time
                                  * Task Juggler - Caster may hold the imago crafting for a scene before starting the next roll.


                                  You also have Excision, even if the rules otherwise remain the same. Removing the limit of yantras one is allowed on a spell might simply be an attainment. Of course, Yantra could be unlimited while Quintessences replace them as the limited factor. If not, though, that's something for an archmage to update. The maximum benefit of Yantra could be raised to Gnosis or removed.
                                  Those would work wonders for raising the roof.

                                  Quintessence could be limited in quantity as per Yantra but have no limit on benefit, automatically, while Yantras simply work as they normally do. Quintessences could provide free Reach and dice, actually, and that would offset a lot of penalties.

                                  My favorite approach would be to rule that a single Quintessence applies to a single spell. (though a Prime attainment should allow fudging that by merging multiple) Yantras apply as normal, but do so first; each way in which a Quintessence applies as a valid Yantra applies a single bonus die, over and above the Yantra limit, and a bonus Reach. If any aspect of the Quintessence is used as one of the Yantra, that Yantra slot is used up and the full Yantra bonus dice are used instead of a single bonus die in the Quintessence bonus calculation, and is not factored into the net Yantra bonus.

                                  Thus, finding the perfect, most appropriate Quintessence is part of the casting mechanics and Yantra rules, both. This thing, I like.

                                  Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.
