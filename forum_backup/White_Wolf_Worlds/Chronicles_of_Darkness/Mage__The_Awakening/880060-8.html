                                    1) Would a mage using a grimoire to ritually cast a healing spell with an advanced duration use their base ritual casting time or the modified ritual cast time to determine how often the healing recurs?

                                    2) Maybe this should be in the errata thread instead, but I'm not sure if it's an actual mistake or just a difference of interpretation between myself and the developers. It feels like the effects of Control Gravity and Gravity Supremacy have been swapped. All of the other example "Control" spells in the Forces Arcanum strengthen or weaken the force in question, which is what Gravity Supremacy does. Control Gravity lets the mage radically change the direction of gravity, which feels more like a weaving spell like Turn Momentum.

It still feels like strengthening/weakening gravity should be a Forces 2 Ruling effect while changing gravity's direction should require Weaving (Forces 3).
