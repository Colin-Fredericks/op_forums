                I've attempted, where possible, to translate the name into Polish although that really is a best guess since I don't speak Polish.

                1. Precession (Precesja): This is the name of the slow change to how the Earth spins, so that where the stars are in the sky changes very gradually over the years. (This ties in Time as an Acanthus to her interest in astronomy). Also Precession slowly changes what star signs are born in what month, so it can be viewed as a symbol for changing Fate.

                2. Thumb (Kciuk): This is a reference to the way a hitch-hiker (autostopowicz) shows that they want a car to stop and pick them in. In this case it's a reference to wanting the Aliens to stop and pick him up again.

                3. Patterson (Patterson): This is a reference to Clair Cameron Patterson, a scientist who produced one of the most accurate measures of the age of the Earth, however he had a lot of trouble calculating this due to human pollution altering the results. This is a reference to her Awakening and belief that the rest of Mage society is blind to the way they're hiding the reality of the past.

                4. Iphis (Iphis): This is a reference to Iphis, the Daughter of Ligdus, a character from the Roman poet Ovid's Metamorphoses. Iphis was born a girl, but grew up disguised as a boy by her mother. This was to stop the father from killing the child because they couldn't afford a daughter's dowry. When Iphis falls in love with another woman, she prays to the Gods who physically transform her into a man to allow them to get married.

                Those are a couple of ideas, hopefully they might help.
