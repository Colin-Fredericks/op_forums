    [Custom Legacy] Unifiiers of Flesh and Stone - need help

    05-24-2018, 08:15 AM
    For the most of human history people percieved living beings as separate and fundamentally different from innate matter. Early sleeper alchemists attributed this to vis vitalis, something with living beings posessed and innate matter did not. Then everything changes 1828 when Friedlish Wohler managed to synthetize Urea, a compound previously believed to be imposible to create without the power of living beings. Soon, other such discoveries followed, By awakened standards it wasn't anything out of the ordinary but the fact that a Sleeper managed to do it was a different story. Those willworkers with more interest is sleeper science didn't know what to do with this revelations.

    On of those mage's was a french Alchemist called Alembic bellonging to the Mysterium. He proposed a bost: all the Gross Arcana are in reality aspects of one phenomenon encompassing all of physical existence, and the separation between them is the fault of the Abyss's influence. Uniting them would bring the Fallen closer to the Supernal or perhaps even destroy the Abyss itself and the first step in achieving that would be unification of the Life and Matter arcanum. Alembic wasn't particulary popular before he announced his revelations and the audacity of his theories didn't help, the was laught off and made a fool. Feeling offended, Alembic left the Mysterium a tried searching for like minded mages among the Nameless cabals. Soon he found himself a decent amount of followers and developed his legacy. When the Free Council was formed the Syntetists, as they were now called, were among it's members though their theories were still deemed as interesting but most likely wrong. People could maybe agree that living beings are just some refined type of matter but everyone could see that a lightning bolt is different from a rock. Then came Einsteins famous equation and the Synthetists were ecstatic.

    Nowadays the Unifiers have a lot more arguments to support their theories. It is argued that gravity is just caused by a distortion in space, people use robotic prosthetics, organs can be grown in a test tube and replaced and organism can be altered on a fundamental scale by modifying their DNA. All of this gives the Synthetists more hope, along with allies and recruits. They still focus on the Life and Matter Arcana, although many want to go further, most agree and going one step at a time is the better choice.

    Path/Order: Morors or the Free Council

    Primary Arcanum: Life

    Required skills: An initiate to the Legacy needs to have at least 2 dots in either Science or Medicine.

    Yantras: Prosthetics (+1), Performing organic synthesis (+1), Experiments that change a living a being using tools that originate from non-living sources (+2)

    Oblations: Studying anatomy or biochemistry, Participating or watching a surgical operation

    Attainments:

    1st - Diagnostic scan - Life 1

    A body is a chemical factory producing hundreds of compounts depending of cicurmstantes and it's state. So by measuring levels of those chemicals a Synthetist can gain a lot of information about the beings state. This reveals any sickness, physical Tilt or condition that the subject suffers, as the spell Analyze Life.
    Optional: Matter 1
    The mage can also analyze non-living subjects replicating the Discern composition spell.

    2nd - Hormonal rush - Life 2

    Living beings are controled by the chemicals released in their bodies, and by changing the levels of those chemicals a Synthetists can excercice a certain degree of control over the subject as the spell "Control instincts"
    Optional: Matter 2
    Todays tools aren't the simple hammers and swords of yesterday, with the amount of moving elements not yet quite reaching those of living beings but close enough. By making subtle adjustments to the subject the Unifier can make it work in a more optimal way. This can be used on a complex object, such as a car or computer. It gainst the following benefits:
    - it requires half the energy/fuel it originally needed
    - if it is a vehicle increase handling by 1
    - if it is a different type of tool, it works flawlessly, turn the next dramatic failure while using it into a normal failure.


    3rd - Jury Rig - Life 3, requires science or medicine 3

    Parts between machines can be swapped and as living beings are just machines made of tissue and bone they shouldn't be no different. The Synthetist can grant a being feature from a different living beings as the spell "Transform" life though he needs a source for that feature serving as a blue print. Activating this attainment takes a scene to activate and it uses the advanced scale Reach effect.
    Optional: Matter 3
    The mage can implant a living being with a feature from an object or vice versa. This can range from somewhat sensible (having steel claws) to bizarre (breathing fire). This replicates the matter spell "Wonderfull Machine" with obligatory use of it's Life 3 optional effect.

    4th - Reconfiguration

    The difference between different living beings seems extreme but the basics are mostly the same. The same aminoacids, the same nitrogenous bases, also mostly water. By working on a living being for a scene the mage can replicate the Shapechanging though the mage needs a blue print of what he wants to change the subject into. An actual thing would be best but an anatomy text book might suffice. This attainmnent used the advanced duration factor.

    Optional: Matter 4
    Living beings have the capacity for evolution to adapt to circumstances causing specialisation and the Synthetists can grant this capacity to a tool. The tool changes into a form that lets it better perform it's purpose. However just like natural evolution this effect doesn't make the tool strictly better just better adapted to do a particular specific thing. The mage must choose a specific use of the tool and grants it the tool the rote-quality when used for that particular thing, as the spell Crucible with it's 1st reach effect. However when used for any other purpose the object loses the 10-again quality. For instance a if a machete was improved for "cutting plants" it would help while moving through overgrowth but would inconvienience the mage in a fight.

    5th - Point of Origin

    The Synthetist can create a living being from scrath but he needs the required materials, though what materials the mage uses should be left to an agreement between the mage's player and the storyteller. One synthetist might stitch together organs while another might use basic elements in proportions equal to those existing in the human body. This replicates the spell Create Life, takes a scene of work to activate and uses the advanced duration factor.

    Optional: Matter 5
    The Synthetist can animate an object he or she contructed, creating an animated doll of something extremely close to a robot only fueled by magic. This replicated the spell Golem. Activating this attainment is an instant action though the mage must contruct the golems body beforehand, it uses the advanced Duration scale factor.

    So yeah, It still needs a bit work, In particular I need help with the 4th and 5th attainment and with additional Yantras and Oblations. Appreaciete any help and other suggestions.
