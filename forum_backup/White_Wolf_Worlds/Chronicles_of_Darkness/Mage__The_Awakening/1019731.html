          Assuming 2E rules Defense and Armor are not the same as said before. Generally speaking defense discribes the ability of a character to move out of harms way, while armor is some protective element that absorbs damage, when you are already hit. Even though the attainment is called "Fate Armor" it doesn't actually give any armor, but additional defense. Defense can normally not be applied against firearm attacks, since a normal human is to slow to react to bullets. There are some execptions which give the ability to use defense against fire arms, like most Werewolfforms, which have quicker instincts and reflexes or some of the armor attainments, because: magic.
          An attack which explicitely does not apply defense, won't apply the additional defense from the attainment either. I guess you just can't move out of the way if you are completely sourrunded by bats. If you on the other hand have any armor, a kevlar vest e.g., that would apply, since the bats cannot gnaw through it.
