                        Originally posted by Letalis View Post
                        Hello all, new questions:
                        1) Is it possible to create an astral sanctum? Especially one with as many trappings (eg safe place, ritual casting space, etc.) as possible for a real sanctum such that a cabal could be spread out around the world, but meet up in the astral.
                        There's not really much point to making a Sanctum that does that, given most potential threats are not going to be able to reach it, and you won't be going there bodily, and the Astral is rather thin on sources of Paradox that aren't nigh-omnipresent anyway. The Begotten have their Lairs, but there's no Wending-equivalent state for the soul-world of a dead Beast to get around the problem of tenancy and/or host management.

                        2) Would it have to be in the Temenos, or could it be in the Oneiros of a particular member? Or even more exotically in the Anima Mundi?
                        Putting it in the Anima Mundi is a bad idea for many reasons, most prominently the Ecstatic Wind, and putting it in someone's Oneiros is a recipe for mental problems that requires a pretty major extension of trust. Paths and Legacies have realms in the Temenos, so building the cachet necessary to generate a shared space in the dreams of humanity is about as much of a prerequisite as needed to put it in the Temenos, which is the best-fit least-objectionable layer of the Astral to keep a collective meeting place.

                        3) I've read here why a hallow is a bad idea in a person's Oneiros, but could they exist in the Temenos or Anima Mundi?
                        Similar problems: Temenos Realms that are host to a Hallow start to disconnect from their surrounding bits of the human unconscious, while Dreamtime locals don't take kindly to the feature and take steps to erase it.

                            2) Would it have to be in the Temenos, or could it be in the Oneiros of a particular member? Or even more exotically in the Anima Mundi?

                            In order for a Mastigos to accomplish what what you want, I reccomend a 3 layered solution:

                            1- Increase the storage space of a portable item to be able to hold a person (coat pocket, wallet, tie pocket, etc)

                            2- Create a Sanctum, ward it heavily, store in a Pocket Dimension, Reach for an Iris and make it lead to an entrance inside an item made in the previous step. Add a Key to make it only accessible to those that were told it was there and do not wish to steal or damage its contents.

                            3- Co-locate the interior of the other items to lead to the entrance made in step 2.

                            With that you have a portable Sanctum, free of Paradox, easily accessible for hiding, coordinating logistics and moving the cabal across the globe. It will take a lot of time and Willpower to make it happen, but its an incredibly powerful resource to be able to access on the fly.

                                                            Originally posted by Emerus View Post
                                                            So...silly question here. Does anyone know what the line next to each Arcanum is for in the character sheet? What are we supposed to write there?
                                                            It's just a leader connecting the names of the Arcana with the fillable dots. It makes the document more readable. You're not intended to write anything there. The fact that you can is just a bonus, so you could fill in stuff like Ruling/Inferior/Path if you'd like. Same thing for Skills; the leaders are primarily there for readability since many Skills don't leave enough space for proper notes, but you could use those lines to note Specialties if you'd like.
