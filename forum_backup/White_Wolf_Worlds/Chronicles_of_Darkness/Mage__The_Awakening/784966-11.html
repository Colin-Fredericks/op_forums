                                    So, reading more on Egregore ( called Atlantean Mystery in 1E Mysterium Order book ) I see that Potestats need at least some changes in Arcana department - as Hierophant should have Time ( and other high-ranking Mysterium do not have those ) to point others what their Mysteries could be to dwell in to, so I added Time 2. I’m still not sure on this, as 2E ‘Divination’ spell has different wording, but from look on it, with Reach option in it those should work for Egregore in Order. I left him Fate 2 as Potestas sounds he may need Accelerated Advancements in Egregore on his students, even just hos to show them what they can achieve if they overcome obstacles. Then I added him Obsessions based on Mysterium book – in my eyes, Potestas is dedicated in finding partner for his last dot of Egregore. In meanwhile, he research magic and spells as general, because he is preparing for the last Egregore ritual. Also, I think more about tone of character and how it is played in my game. So here is rewrite of NPC - point me where it could be developed more.

                                    Potestas ( Consilium Councilor, Hierophant )

                                    Quote: Do you think yourself wise, young one? Important? Powerful? You are none of these things. You are a whelp with delusions of grandeur. Allow me to illustrate to you your insufficiencies..
                                    Background: Potestas has been at the Academy for as long as anyone can remember. He is seen as akin to the sea, both tempestuous and eternal. Despite his obvious desires to the contrary, he has never and, at this point, will never hold the office of the Hierarch, though he has been a Councilor through the reign of the Nemean. It is a position Potestas has held since early in the ascension of the Nemean and from which the Nemean could not dislodge him, despite the other’s efforts. Potestas is the loyal opposition, devoted to the Consilium, even as he stands against the power of the Ebon Noose and the White Putnams after Nemean ‘disappearance’. He was the one most strongly in favor to Khuemia rise on position of Councilor, showing her as great opening for new Silver Ladder. In the end, on his private thoughts, he still thinks Consilium is ‘grow men’s club’ and wanting to be proclaimed Hierarch.
                                    As Mysterium’s Hierophant, Potestas has a lot of ties among Boston’s Awakened, more than anyone else cares to admit. Some believe that he has at least one spy in all but one or two of the other cabals of the area, though this is almost certainly an exaggeration. He is a master of misdirection, keeping enemies at one another’s throats and leaving allies guessing as to his true capabilities and motivations.
                                    Description: Potestas is a tall, lean man with an intimidating presence. Now in his mid-80s, he looks as though he keeps standing solely on account of his unwillingness to yield to age or weakness. His beard and receding white hair are worn short, and his face is a network of wrinkles, deepening to shadowed grooves when he frowns in displeasure, which is often. He invariably wears fine suits, often in black, dark gray or dark blue, and uses a small, glittering black gemstone of unknown composition (he claims it is Atlantean) as a tie tack. His grammar is impeccable, though he always sounds a little hoarse. His instinct is to talk down to others, and he holds it in check with great difficulty (and then only when he truly wishes to).
                                    Storytelling Hints: Potestas believes that wisdom conveys the right to power, not the other way around. He is tremendously disappointed with the fact that he was never able to successfully break with the Mysterium’s unspoken policy of remaining a second-tier power in Boston, but pretends to be far more bothered by it than he actually is. In his eyes, only a fool laments that which has already come to pass, unless he somehow possesses the power to change it. Instead, Potestas works on establishing a firm position for the next generation of the Academy, from which they might chip away at the influence of the area’s Silver Ladder in general and the Secret Concord in particular. He is connected to many willworkers in the city by the Egregore he is center, but has nowhere near the information network he has led others to believe. In truth, he finds great amusement in watching others scramble to figure out who his spies and informants are.
                                    Dedicated Magical Tools: Watch with mirrored case

                                        I'm a bit late to the party - and worse, I haven’t read the entire thread as in depth as I would. This abrogation of forum etiquette must arise of arrogance, for which I shall have to chastise myself at a later point (ie: when time permits). Further exacerbating my sin, circumstances afford me only enough time to present my initial whims as a sketch of an outline.

                                        Consilium: Not sure what the mystery of the Consilium would be. Maybe something about the concentration of places of worship, higher learning and Stephen King is a huge Red Sox fan? But, I agree with having cabals spread out across New England. Instead of a consilium, maybe make the post-Epona's Chariot Ebon Noose formation a tight-knit thearch+arrow caucus and/or experiment?

                                        Also, if I skimmed correctly, the chronicle's primary mystical motif and metaphor is alchemy. To me, this implies themes of change and the direction of enacting those changes; Tao vs. Western Alchemy; greed vs. inner growth; materialism vs. ephemeral/spiritual.

                                        Thus, for Seers, I imagine Mammon and/or Patrenoster would be fitting. (Maybe the decline of the Catholic church in the area plays into this. See: Pedo priests & Cardinal Law.) (Also, Harvard has generated a lot of federal-level economic advisors.) (Also, this could tie into Khumeia’s backstory somewhat.)


                                        NPCs perhaps interested in the 'philosopher's stone'/directly reflecting alchemy:
                                        • Have Potestas be undergoing the Final Key.
                                          • Maybe this ties into your central chronicle. But awakened might consider this to be the ultimate inner-transformation.
                                          • Either way, he’s pulling away from “Earthly concerns” and Aurem is essentially Councilor, etc.
                                        • Khumeia is “stalling” (stagnation) (Could also be applied to Apollonia, more readily, but I’ll admit to not quite being sure what to do with her.)
                                        • Tiamat & the Alchemy of Life (and Shadowname)
                                        • Vision could be an interesting ‘pentacle-side’ option for the materialism aspect of alchemy; but could also be interesting if he has some sort of ‘mid-life crisis’ and is trying to change himself and evolve into a mystagogue seriously.
                                        Cabals
                                        • The Shadow Chorus:
                                          • Rumors that they are there because of the past and the Emerald Scroll. Personally, I think they are (primarily) Massachusetts natives who the nation-wide GoV (playing “the inter/national” game) has assigned the task of re-taking Harvard from the Seers.
                                            • Everything else is (probably) smokescreen.
                                            • Being antagonistic to the FC will only make them more paranoid and wary of dangers?
                                          • Theme-wise:
                                            • Masks are transformative -> They allow a person to assume (if only while donned) another guise/skin/etc.
                                            • The Shadow Chorus as written (imo) are meant to be agents for some sort of change.
                                            • Alternatively, if you go with the war vs. Seers option, maybe emphasize or contrast with the underlying themes of their nemeses.
                                        • Ebon Noose
                                          • With the Hierarch gone, they’ve lost a lot of knowledge and really are back to being the Fool. Will the cabal evolve and shed its Thearch past for something more Arrow-like?
                                          • Has the Moros gone insane? What about Rubida/Tiamat’s “protection” from the Brotherhood?
                                        • Putnams have change built right into their premise. A weak Noose likely means Simon & Moriah go on “the offensive.” (They want to change their cabal as a whole.)
                                        • Numa/Sanguine Laurel could be used for “change” plot points in terms of human growth. (Pregnancy/Birth, children, puberty/adolescence, divorce, empty nest syndrome.)
                                        Anyway, maybe something here will tickle someone's fancy. But inevitably, choose to do what most inspires you.
