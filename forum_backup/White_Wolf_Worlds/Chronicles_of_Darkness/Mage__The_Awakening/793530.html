    Which Legacies do you want in the post-release batch?

    12-27-2015, 08:38 AM
    Hello!

    So, Mage is in layout now, which means release is edging ever-closer. I'm also 3000 words off having (for the first time in two years) no contracted writing to do for other Developers, so I've been planning some bits and pieces that will accompany second edition.

    One of those is a set of four Legacies, so that you have five of them. And I am having trouble deciding on what three of them should be.

    To clarify:

    The Eleventh Question (Moros or Guardians/Mysterium) are in the 2e core. I want to have at least one Legacy for each Path, and preferably for each Order, available as examples.

    I have already converted the Sisterhood of the Blessed (Acanthus or Silver Ladder/Seers) because I needed it for my own Chronicle.

    So!

    Out of the remaining 93 published Legacies, we need a Mastigos one, an Obrimos one, and a Thyrsus one. Preferably with some combination of the Arrow, Council, and Mysterium as the Orders. Does anyone have any requests?

    Dave Brookshaw

                          I agree with at least Clavicularius for the Mastigos one. In the same vein I will add other Legacies which I feel are iconic and that I have seen chosen by players more than once. Since I am not really able to pick favorites I will list more than three, but these are all Legacies that see play often in my games and LARP. Hopefully that helps.

                          Brotherhood of the Demon Wind / Perfected Adept: These combat oriented Legacies are often part of the Adamantine Arrow in my games. Easy to understand and reference, they are simple player choices. Between these too I think the Brotherhood is the cooler one.

                          Keepers of the Covenant: The Hierarch and other important PCs and NPCs are part of this Legacy. A good update since this Spirit-based legacy would get to explore the new Ephemeral systems from GMC and fits with the new Thyrsus ideal as a man-in-the-middle.

                          Cryptologos: While this Legacy is currently attached to Mastigos, I've always felt it also plays well with Obrimos. My girlfriend plays an Obrimos hyperactive FC linguist Cryptologos with an Obsession on discovering Truths devised by ancient and modern cultures, through etymology and grammar.

                          Pygmalian Society / Sisterhood of the Blessed: Good Acanthus fits and also two Legacies which are related. The Sisterhood has come up a little more often. Unfortunately Sisterhood are also SL, so if I had to choose between these two I would choose the Keepers of the Covenant.

                              I'll put in a couple of suggestions:

                              Everyone tends to love the goetic Mind Mastigos legacies, but I would personally love to see the Reality Stalkers (Mysterium). Since 9/11 she has studied to be an architect and joined the Katsinam Suukya with the spirit guide of Skyscraper (a modern take on Community - if I was to time jump her 200 years in the future her spirit guide would probably be Archology).
