                                                            Originally posted by branford View Post


                                                            How broadly would you define "buried materials" for Moros?
                                                            I try to look at it through the lens of mage sight. So does it fit thematically within the realm of a Moros. Weapons buried in a crypt, iron and lead hand forged into something symbolic, diamond and other gems in amulets or other tools, etc.

                                                            I actually give No risk the most leeway since their entire world view is shaped by Matter. Wooden/other once living materials or simple primal ones (obsidian daggers), or those things that have been well refined/ascribed to other paths, are usually the only ones that I outright exclude. And even then, it depends: a human skill would fit a Moros, but a leather hide drum might not.
