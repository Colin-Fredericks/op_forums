                        Originally posted by LadyLens View Post
                        Reading Regeneration, I find it extremely irritating that the regeneration is strictly transitory. Would it cause any significant problems to allow Regeneration to be permanent for, let's say, +2 Reach, then spend of Mana, or both? I can't see any.
                        What you want is magic that leaves Lasting results as a side effect instead of as an intended effect. You could permanently regenerate a hand if you gave a subject the regenerative properties of something else, maybe a starfish, and tweaked it to allow for reproduction of human body parts. The spell isn't intended to regenerate a limb, it's intended to give you the ability to regenerate limbs, any limbs that result are merely Lasting byproducts of the spell, not subject to the existence of the spell itself.

                        Another alternative is to harvest some stem cells from the subject and force them to grow into a replacement limb that can then be surgically grafted onto the body. It's not that much different from using Matter to bend steel into a shape that it stays in when you're done.

                                        05-12-2018, 08:10 PM
                                        Attainments encounter major issues when brushing up against Withstand. That is intentional. They're supposed to be less flexible, and less powerful than a version of their effect performed through spellcasting with sufficient effort.

                                        If you want to get past some subject's crazy high Resolve/Stamina/Composure, then gather some Yantras, spend Willpower, and cast a normal spell with enhanced spell factors.
                                        Attainments do their best work when they're self-enhancements, or don't directly affect other people in a way that involves Withstand.
