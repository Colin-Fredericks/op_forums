        Since there was some talk about Shadow Names, I had a question about mage culture.

        Using your Shadow Name around your mundane friends and family is a no-go, because it apparently forms sympathy between that name and them, which is bad.

        Using a Mage's mundane name around them is considered very taboo.

        What do mages do in mixed company? What if they go to university together and have to talk in the halls? At the library? What if they go to the same party by chance?

        I kind of figured sympathetic names weren't a big deal anymore, given how complicated such interactions are and the fact that the penalty for sympathetic magic without a sympathetic name is so mild now, but then I saw the bit about how super taboo it is.

        How do mages deal with cases where mundane and magic friends collide? It's bound to happen. Is it covered in the book?

                                I figure a photo has the same sympathy to a place as a person does to a cup that they're holding. In other words, in the moment of taking it there's a really fragile connection that breaks apart at the end of the scene. At that point the photo works well as a representational Yantra but no longer has any useful Sympathetic connection the location it represents. Naturally, if you have Space you can reinforce its Sympathy before that breakdown occurs and get some more use out of the picture as a result. I wouldn't be surprised if there are Space Mages who barter Sympathetically enhanced photos with each other. In other words a photo is about as important to a place as a paper cup is to a person (Look at Web-Weaver). For a weak sympathetic connection to a place you'd have to take a replaceable object from it, like say a wood chip from a playground.
