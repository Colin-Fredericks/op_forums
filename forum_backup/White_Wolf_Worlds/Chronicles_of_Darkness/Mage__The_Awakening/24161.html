                                  Once 2nd Edition becomes available and I have a chance to read through the rules portions at a minimum, I'll be doing MtAw 2e on Roll20. Since that's going to be an indeterminate time from now I'm not posting or recruiting for it just yet.

                                  I had been planning to run a game for almost a year now, but my IRL group of about five years broke up recently. So I'm currently learning the Roll20 ropes playing in a Burning Wheel game, while I plan out what I'm going to do with Mage based on all the existing stuff and Dave's spoilers/previews.

                                  Since it will be a new edition with some relatively big changes, I'm going to start with a small sandbox and then work my way up to a larger scale setting. Then when I'm no longer learning and teaching the game quite as much things might be more free-form.

                                  But you might want to look at Roll20 for something more current. Unfortunately, Roll20's game searches are based on a pre-defined dropdown and they lump all of the WoD games (old and new) into one option, so you might need to slog through a lot of listings for things you don't want.
