    Originally posted by espritdecalmar View Post
    Foreshadow (Fate ●)
    Practice: Compelling
    Primary Factor: Potency
    Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Expression, Subterfuge
    When a mage casts this spell, he chooses a destiny (or Destiny) of which he is aware. The spell causes the strings of fate to manifest one symbol per dot of Potency of that destiny between the time of casting and when the fated event occurs. For instance, if a Sleeper is destined to get into a car crash, the spell might cause her to see a commercial about auto insurance, or she might drive by the site of a car crash the day before. The effects of this spell are normally minor and subtle, but they can be useful for getting someone to believe in the power of fate and symbolism. Alternatively, the mage might wish to cause foreshadowing to manifest for particularly obscure destinies in order to analyze a mystery from a different point of view.
    +1 Reach: The mage chooses how the foreshadowing manifests.
    I was messing about in my homebrew document, trying to do something with Apophenia and Pareidolia; got inspired by the above spell; and made this. Yes, that's quite a lot of reach effects . So, good, or overdesigned?

    Foreshadow (Fate •) Practice: Compelling
    Primary Factor: Duration
    Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Expression, Subterfuge
    This spell manifests one symbol per point of Potency that gives insight to the subject about their destiny. The effects of this spell are minor and subtle, but they can be useful for getting someone to believe in the power of fate and symbolism.
    Prolonged exposure to this spell (roughly a day per dot of the subject’s Composure) causes the spooked condition.
    A character that is aware of the Destiny may drop pertinent hints during a social encounter and reduce the subject’s doors by 1.
    +1 Reach: The omens are not really subtle, and sometimes quite frightening; while the subject has the spooked condition granted by this spell, witnessing further symbols grants the shaken condition.
    +1 Reach: The subject is subconsciously primed to pertinent events related to their destiny. When reacting to events related to their destiny, the subject gains a +potency bonus to initiative.
    +1 Reach: The subject witnesses omens pertinent to a Destiny belonging to someone else.
    +1 Reach: As long as the caster is aware of the destiny, they may choose how the omens manifest.
    +mind•••: the spell causes the Obsessed condition instead of the spooked condition.

                            Yeah, it would, for more general consideration, casting a Veiling spell at all doesn't seem to invoke Peripheral Mage Sight. Veiling spells are supposed to be something that slips by notice after all. That said, a Mage who employs MS, or an appropriate spell, with the right arcana might be entitled to a CoW and pierce it. This is perfectly in-line with Mages being among the ultimate investigators of supernatural goings-on. One of the core themes of Mage after all is piercing deception, and the other is being so obsessed with piercing falsehoods that they will burn the world down to do so. It can be less a question of if they find out and more what they'll do when they do so.

                            One saving grace for the ST is that unless they use a spell to gain the CoW they won't know it's happened. Though naturally if they win the CoW even if a spell isn't involved they gain all information that such a victory would make them privy to.

                                                    Logical inverse of Gremlins. Gremlins make failure --> dramatic failure. So this makes success --> exceptional success.

                                                    Loyal Tool (Spirit •)
                                                    Practice: Compelling
                                                    Primary Factor: Potency
                                                    Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Persuasion, Socialize

                                                    The spirit of an object can be cajoled into helping its owner during its use. When a character succeeds on a roll using this spell's subject as equipment, the spell converts the success into an exceptional success. The spell converts a number of successes equal to its Potency.

                                                    +1 Reach: As long as the object is within sensory range, the mage can choose which successes become exceptional successes.
