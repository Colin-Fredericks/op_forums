      Well the only published 2E materials are the core, Dark Eras and Dark Eras Companion IIRC. Reading stuff in that order sounds nice.

      Among 1E books, the developer himself recommended Astral Realms, Left-Handed Path, and a bunch I don’t remember at the top of my head. I’d personally throw in the six Order “splat book” as well.



      From an Ascension background... first, magic doesn’t come from belief. Your cultural upbringing and worldview informs you how you interpret what you see and what you decide to do as a mage, thus indirectly shaping your magic, but it is never a source of your magic. Objective truth, subjective interpretations.

      Related to this, the Diamond Orders aren’t imposing an “Atlantean dogma” like the Traditions are doing with the nine Spheres model. This may seem unrelated and out of the blue at the moment, but a LOT of recent debates and discussions here in the Awakening forums ultimately stem from this.


      EDIT: See also the thread named “Great Cults of Major Religions,” at the moment right below this one.

                  Lets see, there are plenty of great resources. Both Mage games are incredible, but have different feels to them. So here is what I will do, I will list generally good resources for the many focuses your campaign can have, then you can pick the ones best suited to your players. If any topic is missing, let me know and I will fine tune my suggestions.

                  The 2ed book is very good point to start, Tome of the Mysteries for a lot of cool ways to use magic, Invaders:Encounters with the Abyss has some excellent plot hooks, Book of Spirits fleshes out the Shadow very well, Astral Realms for trips to the collective unconcious, Book of the Dead (a Geist book) for the Underworld, Imperial Mysteries if you want to make grand chessmaster archmage NPCs and Secrets of the Ruined Temple if you want a more Indiana Jones globetrotting campaign.

                  From these forums, I cannot recommend Dave's APs enough, they are fantastic, you can find an index here:

                  Dave's Actual Play Thread, can someone give me the links? - Onyx Path Forums
                  http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/main-forum/the-new-world-of-darkness/mage-the-awakening/31354-dave-s-actual-play-thread-can-someone-give-me-the-links
                  Hey can I get the link to Dave's Actual Play threads that seem to get nothing but outstanding reviews? It was Broken Diamond right? Or were there multiple ones? Can someone post the Links?


                  If you want a condensed version, try reading Bleak House (a one shot) and his initial notes on Broken Diamond, they are tremendously helpful for getting the feel of the setting. I recommend them in general, apart from being very entertaining, his and the player's notes are a master class on storytelling and GMing, while being clear and concise. There is also these descriptions of Active Mage Sight that give a cool glimpse on how mages see the world through the lens of their Path (the links are in the last post of the page):
