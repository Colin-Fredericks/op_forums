    Bikeshedding a Condition

    04-30-2020, 01:12 AM
    Twould be a good thing to clean up the language on this and make it more consise. The idea, though, I think is pretty clear. Let me know if you think otherwise. It's a Condition to represent being aware of too much happening at once.

    Prismatic Soul (Condition)

    A being with this Condition perceives ephemeral things alongside physical things, simultaneously. The effect appears similar to schizophrenia, except the scenes and senses being presented to their senses are demonstrable parts of reality, just not limited to physical reality. The difficulty is that this condition comes with no particular guidance about what is being viewed at any given time, and, in fact, doesn’t usually discriminate.
    Ephemera intrude into the senses of such a person even without any special connection or place. The senses include impressions from multiple realms at once, which is disorienting, even when one becomes accustomed to the Condition.
    These visions are distracting, imposing a -1 distraction penalty on any non-reflexive action and on any sensory tests, reflexive or otherwise. The uncertain nature of the ephemeral senses imposes a -3 penalty due to the haziness of the sense on top of that -1 for distraction. The hazy images of other realms are patchy and indistinct, but are not clear enough to trigger the Split Focus Tilt.
    Some particular visions snap into greater focus, generally when interacting with something. Anything not focused upon becomes harder to witness, suffering a -4 distraction penalty. The physical senses remain, but the realm or realms that hold attention gain full clarity, overwhelming the capacity of a human mind to properly handle it all, imposing the Split Focus Tilt.
    The subject can spend a point of Willpower and then roll current Willpower as a die pool. (include the willpower spent to activate this in the die pool) Success indicates that the senses can be consciously willed to focus on a specific phenomenon witnessed or to defocus if already snapped in.
    (Perhaps there is a similar but less severe Condition that deals with only one realm intruding at a time.)
    Effects that externalize and direct one’s ephemeral senses, such as astral projection or an astral journey, have an unusual effect on Prism Souls. Where these normally dislocate a mind and its frame of reference, the Prism Souled merely shift their ephemeral senses. Their waking mind and physical senses are otherwise unaffected.
    A dramatic success on meditation can quiet the ephemeral side if it focuses on a visualization of self and being in the moment. This removes the Split Focus Tilt and removes the normal distraction penalties.
    This Condition interacts with quite a few merits. The following list is just a set of examples.
    Astral Adept: The merit can be developed to use the connection from focusing on an astral object, removing the need for a ceremony or ritual to engage the ability.
    Unseen Sense: If the Unseen Sense is towards a type of ephemera, that type of focused vision is triggered without a roll or willpower cost when something interesting of that sort occurs in sensory range. Neither a roll nor a willpower are required in such a case. If the user simply wishes to view that sort of phenomenon, that’s now allowed a roll as normal.
    Medium: Contact almost always counts as a triggering event for the focusing vision.
    Possible Sources:

    Ecstatic Wind branding, Taking soul shock or degeneration in a verge, traumatic Awakening
    Resolution:

    Removing sensitivity to other worlds, patching the soul wound that caused the perception, The Lie blinding the subject

    Updated version for consideration:

    Dreamwalker (Condition)

    A being with this condition is sensitive to the resonance of the unseen things of creation. In particular, they are sensitive to things connected to their personal ancestry and the dream of the local area. Ephemera related to their ancestral history can make themselves known to the Dreamwalker's senses. The trouble with this is it becomes difficult to distinguish what comes from where when different aspects of reality collide.

    One's personal dreaming ancestry sets the things that are revealed to this sense readily. Anything resonant with the personal dreaming is unveiled. If the character is following a Songline, the sacred sites along the path also similarly shine through. This includes spirits, ghosts, or other phenomena resonant with the persona dream or the dream of the Songline being followed. Things that are resonant and not magically concealed are visible to the Dreamwalker. Things that are not resonant are generally invisible as normal, but a partial resonance can sometimes make flicker into visibility, especially if they attempt to interact.

    A -1 penalty is applied to rolls involving perception or penalized by distraction due to the various images that flit in and out of awareness rather frequently. If multiple forms apply, the penalty rises to -3.

    At an Iris that is resonant with the Dreamwalker's personal dreaming or current Songline, the Dreamwalker is drawn in automatically through the portal, provided they fulfill the entry requirements. Many Irises have this Condition as a valid alternate Key. A willpower can be spent to resist the pull.

    Beats: Take a beat every time you live out or learn the lesson of an ancestor's vision.

    Resolution: There are only 3 ways to regain control of one's mind. One may reject their personal dream, complete its path, or transcend its limitations.

    To overcome the condition requires breaking ties with the personal dram in some dramatic way, generally defiling sacred sites or destroying some symbolic and irreplaceable (from a personal standpoint) token of the dream.

    Harmonizing with it requires one to follow the entire Song, visiting and honoring its sacred sites until all lessons are learned. Once the mage leaves the last test site, the visions cease.

    Those possessing deeper insight may seek to shape their dream, rather than merely follow it. At the final site, they meditate on the lessons learned, and gain insight into their Condition. The Dreamwalker Condition actually expands, in this case, permitting the Dreamwalker to focus attention on another and connect to their Dream. This resolution grants the Sleepwalker merit, if the character is not already considered one.

      This Condition is extremely over-engineered, imo. The basic effect is that the character is distracted, so have a flat and simple penalty for being distracted (perhaps -3 for actions that can be influenced by distractions, and -5 on rolls based on perception). If you want to allow the character to ignore the distractions, a Willpower point should do it without a roll. Rolling current Willpower points is not a mechanic that is used except for Soul-Shocked, and then it's because it directly deals with your number of Willpower points. Removing the roll means the player doesn't risk wasting a WP when they could've just spent it on +3 dice to partially or completely negate the penalty without any risk.
      Instead of focusing on a single thing, I recommend removing all distraction penalties for a turn or a number of turns equal to Composure.

      For a similar Condition applying only to the Shadow, check out Mystified in Werewolf.

                          Since the inspiration for the Dreamspeakers is Australian Aboriginal mythology, you could lean into that. I also mixed the Gift of Men from the Silmarillion to define the resolution.

                          New Condition: Songline

                          By effort, chance or instinct, you have gained a connection to your Songline. Some call it the sacred narrative of creation, where it is said one's entire ancestry exists as one and their sum knowledge is accumulated. Legend has it the most ancient ones traveled across the land before the Fall, creating sacred sites in their journeys. These living tales come to the mage to be passed on.

                          Unfortunately, the human mind has long forgotten how to understand them. The mage has automatic perception and can physically interact with the Mind or Spirit attuned Twilight of a region that is resonant with his Songline's ancestors or symbolism (like a specialized variation of Shadow Name). These images overlap with the material, applying a -2 to perception rolls.

                          When more than one Twilight is resonant, this penalty becomes -3 instead as the mage's mind struggles to differentiate each phase of reality. However, this is usually a portent indicating an important locale in their Songline.

                          In resonant Loci or Synesne, the mage is drawn into the Realm Invisible immediately, but can spend a Willpower per turn to resist the pull.

                          Beats: Take a beat every time you live out or learn the lesson of an ancestor's vision.

                          Resolution: There are only 3 ways to regain control of one's mind. One may defy the Song, harmonize with it or move beyond it.

                          Defying it is the path of the Banisher and it requires one to destroy or defile all sacred sites they can currently perceive in their Song, thus severing the sympathetic connection.

                          Harmonizing with it requires one to follow the entire Song, visiting and honoring its sacred sites until all lessons are learned. Once the mage leaves the last test site, the visions cease.

                          Those possesing deeper insight may seek to corroborate their right to shape the melody rather than just listen to it. At the final site, they meditate on the fresh enlightenment before leaving, following the Songline in their soul deep into the Dreamtime. There they will face the final trial of the Dreamtime entity representing their Song. Those that pass return with the authority to speak for the Dreamer.

                              Dreamland (Merit: 1 dot)

                              A Dreamland is a place of power that connects a site in the physical to a specific and stable astral location. This is usually Temenos but can be the Dreamtime. The site counts as a Synese to the Dreamland. These are always locked by a Key and the Key always includes a specific feature of the site that must be maintained or access is lost.

                              The physical location of such sites is generally miles apart.

                              Such sites have often been used for generations as storehouses of supernal wisdom. Uncovering how to access the information, however, is a Mystery that this merit does not represent solving. This merit specifically represents fairly certain access to such a site and access to the key. Safe Place, Library, and Advanced Library are often appropriate to represent unlocking the trove.

                                                      My current thoughts on the summoning process:

                                                      1. Learning: Who are you trying to call? You need to figure out who they are to properly call on them. Their name is required, and it must be a real name. Many hide behind masks and false names..You also need to understand what they are. A token representing their nature is required, later on, that might be uncovered in this phase. This is largely a mental phase.

                                                      2. Rights: Dreamborn are sapient entities with goals and prejudices. There are fundamental laws governing their interactions and then there are their own laws. Fundamental laws require a token representing the being in question and some sort of appropriate ritual to open up the conduit. The token must be a meaningful token gathered from a songline quest and the ritual requires personal effort, generally both physical and astral. If the Dreamborn considers the Seeker to have the right to call on them, such as if they are a descendant or adopted into their family, then that will help at the Appeal phase as one step up on the Impression chart. If the request is something promised by the Dreamborn or framed as an obligation that the Dreamborn accepts, multiple Doors would be opened.

                                                      3. Preparation: The area into which the entity is being called must be prepared with the tokens in a manner befitting the entity called. (Some vision quests prepare the seeker's Oneiros to the purpose. All?) This involves placing proper symbols around the meeting place and often involves music and fires. There are minimal requirements to be met to successfully create the conduit. Then there are things that are wise to include because the Dreamborn in question prefers them. Depending on how perfectly these details are met, this can raise or lower the impression by a step.

                                                      Generally, an effigy of the being sought is set up as a symbol of the subject. Permanent effigies in the community (e.g. totem poles, churinga, shrines with statues) can often provide this to many generations of seekers.

                                                      4. Opening: The Seeker must perform the rite, expending the energy as required to create the conduit that opens the site to the Dreamborn.
                                                      If the Dreamborn's name that is used is incorrect, this isn't selective and may be a bit insulting to the intended target. (Which might reduce Impression by a step) Other things may sneak in, in such a case. This is also possible if the Abyss sneaks into the summoning. (If possible)

                                                      The rolls for this part of the rite is meditation. Success on this phase means that the conduit is formed, opening a path to the Dreamtime. Sufficient success (meditation must reach the Dreamtime) reduces the difficulty of the interaction on the part of the Dreamborn. An exceptional success makes interaction trivial, and so normally opens a Door automatically. This phase includes a difficult trial related to the nature of the dreamborn. Calling on Death, for example, requires the character face their fear of mortality to simply open the Conduit.

                                                      5. Appeal: The dreamborn is contacted, but that doesn't mean it cares.If this is the first contact the two have made, the Dreamborn's first impression of the character is affected by the performance in the Opening trial. At this point, the Dreamborn's personal challenge is faced. That's challenge is usually not specified in words, and, instead, comes in an astral trial of some sort. This takes place in the strange timeframe of the dreaming earth, so it takes a surprisingly short amount of time to those not participating. Failing the Dreamborn's challenge reduces the impression by a step. An average impression generally means that the Seeker gets one very brief shot at impressing the Dreamborn before it closes the Conduit forcibly. (That is, a single roll to attempt to improve the impression) If the impression ever becomes hostile, the Dreamborn will usually strike the Seeker down, shattering the conduit and flinging them back to their body in soul shock. Failing to improve an average impression can sometimes end this way, as well. If the challenge is failed and the Seeker returns home to try again later, the travel is as normal.

                                                      6. Treating: The Seeker has the attention of the dreamborn. Let Social Combat begin. Make your case to the strange ancient spirit.

                                                      With this approach, the summoning actually must take place in multiple strata. Maybe even prepare and cast in each strata to create the conduit.

                                                      (Hmm. Maybe replace "conduit" with "trail" or "track"? Serpent themes are pretty common in the stories I'm finding.)

                                                      Teamwork should be able play a role in the summoning, if only as a sort of moral support and grabbing stuff you need.

                                                      Rank should make things more difficult for a seeker, even if the Dreamborn is friendly, I think. Perhaps the trial of the conduit/trailblazing phase should have stages to overcome based on the Dreamborn's rank. Of course, if we make it too lopsided, it won't make sense.
