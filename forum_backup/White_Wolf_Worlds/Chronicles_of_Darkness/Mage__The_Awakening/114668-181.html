                        It's quite possible to explore other planets, but it requires some planning and a cabal that has the right Arcana. Forces and Matter seems to be the most important since they can protect against vacuum, grant local gravity, recycle air, keep a space vessel together, etc. Life might also be good to not starve. Space can get you to some bodies in the solar system, but that's kinda it for Space. Spirit is a must, though, if you plan on visiting the Moon in a game where WtF is used. The idigam should be pretty keen on tagging along when you leave, and probably won't take no for an answer when it comes to escaping their prison.

                        There are at least two mentions of aliens, as in beings that are not originally from Earth (or other realms of existence).
                        First of all we've got the Visitors from Dark Eras Companion. They're the classic brain parasite puppeteers from the movies. They have the line "Very few Visitors exist on Earth, yet for some reason, they choose this era to dispatch a reconnaissance mission." in their part of the text box. It's not explicitly said they're from space, but they are obviously designed to invoke that feeling.
                        Then we got the "alien spirits" that the Moon-Banished idigam can call down from "the void and the stars beyond Luna's boundary". However, they take just a week to arrive and does so on meteors and other physical objects. Unless they're capable of accelerating physical mass to several times the speed of light they wouldn't be able to originate from outside the Oort Cloud.

                                            Depends on how the ST rules it. For a very broad definition of sensory range you'd be able to travel to other stars. But if you limit how clearly you have to see a subject to be able to cast on it, and how long time delay a "live" feed can have before it acts like a recording rather than remote viewing, then even getting to the moon might be a challenge.
                                            For example, you might be able to target the moon via sensory range, but then you'd have to account for its Size, which is probably a lot higher than any mage can target. But if you want to target an area on the moon instead I'd say you'd have to be able to visually distinguish that area, which, depending on the size of the area, could require a telescope more powerful than the Gran Telescopio Canarias. And even for a mage it would be difficult at best to borrow such telescopes. And I'm not sure if you can get a live feed from those anyway.
