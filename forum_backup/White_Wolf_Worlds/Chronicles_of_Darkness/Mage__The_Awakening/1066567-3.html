                Originally posted by 2ptTakrill View Post
                This is the old problem of meta-game thinking; players roll dice and figure that means something had to happen.
                ​This is something the ST has to deal with and the easiest way is to first hide his dice whenever rolling in apparent opposition, then make it clear that he is going to roll those dice anytime you ask him to, even if there is nothing there for his roll to represent.

                Player-"I got a success! What did I find?"
                ST-"Nothing."
                Player-"He beat my roll?"
                ST-"Or there was nothing there to find, you don't know."

                ​The player can even add Willpower dice to the roll, all the time knowing he may be wasting them on a snipe hunt.

                I've been playing roughly ten years now, and I can handle meta-gaming just fine. As long as the character isn't drugged, crazy or under magical attack that induces false alarms CoW can't ping randomly. It is a direct confrontation roll, not a perception roll.

                Player-"I got a success! Did I hit him?"
                ST-"Nope."
                Player-"He beat my roll?"
                ST-"Or there was nothing there to hit, you don't know."

                Sounds quite weird if it isn't part of the plot or enemies ability.
