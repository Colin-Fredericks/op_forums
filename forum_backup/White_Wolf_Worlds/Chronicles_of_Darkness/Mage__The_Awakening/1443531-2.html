                                            Sorry for the delay, some unexpected things came up, along with some things I totally forgot about, but should have remembered. Anyway, I'm starting with the Singers in Silence, because they are somewhat easy to convert to 2e. I'm about a third of the way done, but I ran out of time today, and wanted something done by today, so here we are. Edit: and I ran out of time again.. I will work on it later today. Edit the second: nearly done, now needs refinement. Final edits!

                                            Thank you everyone that helped me with this, especially proindrakenzol who suggested many of the final attainments, and provided some nice flavor text. the parts in quotes is their writing.

                                            Singers in Silence

                                            She always had a smile for every occasion, and could halt the world with the beauty in her songs. Now that smile is long gone, but she can still bring tears to your eyes when she sings. She sings now for the funeral service, and all who hear it seem to find some measure of comfort there.

                                            A Song for The Dead

                                            In ancient times, people died. They were mourned through tears and wails, thorough story and song. Over time, the role fell to fairy women who came to mourn the passing of lives. Some families even came to have their own bean sίdhe, or banshee, to presage their own death. The Singers in Silence were born of that grief and loss, to know the truths of the passage of those we hold dear.

                                            Origins

                                            Parentage: Arcanthus, Mysterium
                                            Background: The Banshees come from no single background. Most experience a great tragedy just before they enter the legacy.
                                            Appearances: Those that take on this legacy grow skeletally thin, as if eaten away by hunger. Their features become sunken, their bodies bony, and many grow deathly pale.
                                            Nickname: Banshee

                                            Doctrine

                                            Prerequisites: Death 2, Fate 1, Empathy 2
                                            Initiation: After a tragic loss, a initiate must spend a day fasting. Upon the final hour they must find their way to a place of death and silence, and must listen with mage sight. If they are open, they will start to hear the melodies that will haunt them until the end of their days.
                                            Organization: Really intense Master student chains.
                                            Theory: Death comes for everyone, eventually. The Singers in Silence sing songs of mourning, so we all can let go.

                                            Magic
                                            Ruling Arcanum: Death
                                            Yantras: (In a place of Death (graveyard, funeral home, etc) +1); (On someone Fated to die within the next 24 day +2); (Succeed on a expression roll related to the spell+2); (With a corpse +1, performing a funeral service +2 )
                                            Oblations: Singing; Creating Laments ; Preparing a Body for a Funeral ; Ritual Prayers for the Departed; Performing Euthanasia

                                            Attainments

                                            First: The Knowing

                                            Prerequisites: Initiation.
                                            The Banshee sees the Dead and the Doomed. She may see and speak with the dead, even in Twilight. The Banshee can focus on a single ghost and reveal the number of Anchors the ghost has, along with Rank of the ghost. Furthermore, the Banshee knows if any within her perceptions are fated or cursed to die within the next day. The knowledge comes unbidden, it cannot be wished away or easily ignored. This perception is distracting, if there are more ghosts or doomed individuals within the Banshee’s senses than the Banshee’s Gnosis + Composure, she takes a -2 penalty to any extended action rolls . The Storyteller may rule that a particular ghost is being more or less distracting, raising or lowing the effective amount ghosts present in the area.

                                            Permanent Speak with the Dead, with a side of Doom seeing.

                                            Second: The Seeing

                                            Prerequisites: Death 2, Expression 2, Empathy 3
                                            The songs of death and doom flow into the Banshee’s soul. When touching a corpse, a Banshee may activate this attainment to see
                                            the circumstances behind a persons doom, even knowledge of what the doomed person saw and felt in their final moments. This knowledge takes the form of a keening song, a story of fated demise. If the Banshee wishes, she may gain a positive condition related towards singing the death song of the departed soul.

                                            Forensic Gaze, reach towards instant and seeing through the dead’s eyes.

                                            Optional: Fate 2.
                                            The Banshee being associated with ill omen, can insulate herself from false Fate.

                                            Warding Gesture, with reach towards advanced duration.

                                            Third: The Calling

                                            Prerequisites: Death 3, Empathy 3, Expression 3
                                            "The Singer's song calls to the dead, and draws them to her; ghosts, zombies, vampires, and other restless dead that can hear the Singer's haunting melody and have Rank or Composure lower than the Singer's dots in Death are compelled to travel towards the Singer by the fastest safe route possible, whether the affected undead is willingly or forcefully drawn to the Singer their impression level starts one higher than normal." "In addition, those in mourning that hear the song have the sharpness of their pain blunted, allowing fond memories of the deceased to come to the fore and for the bereaved to come to terms with their loss more quickly."

                                            Ghost Lure with reach towards first impression and advanced scale: area

                                            Optional: Fate 3
                                            A Singer’s song may provide a measure of Fortune. Those that accept the Singer’s blessings are insulated from their dooms, with a clash of wills from the Singer for Fate to see their Fate triggers.

                                            Fabricate Fortune, with reach toward instant cast, and advanced duration.

                                            Fourth: The Releasing

                                            Prerequisites: Death 4, Empathy 4.
                                            "Some ghosts are reticent to move on to the afterlife and they cling desperately to the material world by possessing people, places, or objects that they should not. The Singer's song can bring such ghosts back from madness and allow them to move on to the Underworld. After a scene of performing the Singer strips away a number of Manifestation Conditions from a ghost or its host equal to the Singer's dots in Death, this is Withstood by the ghost's Rank. The ghost cannot reestablish these Manifestation Conditions while the Singer continues singing, but the Singer must make a Stamina + Expression roll for each minute they sing."

                                            Exorcism with the reach to not allow the ghost to reestablish manifestation conditions

                                            Optional: Fate 4
                                            The Singer's songs may carry a bitter tone, and ill fortune befalls any that invoke the Banshee's rancor. This melody of ill tidings causes the Dooms of all who hear it to trigger, and individuals cursed have their Curses trigger. Even those insignificant to Fate find their accidents harder to overcome, taking the Singers’ Death? extra damage, or intensifying their negative conditions and counting as doomed to Fate effects, including the Singer’s Knowing. This lasts while the Singer continues to sing, but the Singer must make a Stamina + Expression roll for each minute they sing. Those with a Composure equal to the Singer’s dots in Death are immune as are those under the Singer’s third attainment effects.

                                            Grave Misfortune with a reach towards advanced scale and a reach to trigger the dooms

                                            Fifth: The Keening
                                            Prerequisites: Death 5, Expression 4
                                            Although ghosts truly do not belong in this world, the Singers know the dead must sometimes linger in this world to complete important tasks. A Singer may use this Attainment to sing or cry out a wordless lament, marking those of her choice that hear her voice. Those that bear this mark linger as ghosts when they die. Singer's are informed of the marked ones doom as if they were always within sensory range of the first attainment. She may also use this attainment on the recently dead, either at the physical remains or the location that they died at, to raise a ghost as if they had been marked. These ghosts are not under the mages control, but start with a higher awareness of their situation than normal ghosts of their rank. The ghosts are here to fulfill any remaining business, and have the minimum anchors appropriate for such.

                                            Linger with reach toward advanced scale: targets, sensory range, instant cast, and advanced duration

                                            Optional: Fate 5
                                            The Singers are masters of dooms, with a heart felt song of sorrow, they may free bound ephemeral entities, or souls, change the effects of active hexes, modify or negate an oath or other supernatural agreement reinforced by fate, change or dispel a conditional trigger, or modify a subjects Doom.

                                            When the subject is a ghost, one more option appears. With the ghost's cooperation, the Singer may let a Ghost Move On, regardless of the remaining requirements a ghost may have before they can do so. This allows ghosts, who by chance or fate couldn't finish their business, the chance to properly move on. The Attainment truly lifts all fetters that could stop the ghost from truly moving on, whatever they fate may now be. Many Singers believe that the dead unerringly find their rest in the Supernal Realms, but none can say for sure.

                                            Sever Oaths with reach towards lasting.

                                                Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post
                                                First: The Knowing

                                                Prerequisites: Initiation.
                                                The Banshee Permanently has Death Active Mage Sight activated, with no cost. This combines with The Eyes of the Dead Death 2 Attainment to mean the Banshee sees ghosts and souls in Twilight. She may speak with the dead, even in Twilight. Furthermore, the Banshee knows if any within her perceptions are fated or cursed to die within the next day. The knowledge comes unbidden, it cannot be wished away or easily ignored.

                                                This is in my opinion the best way to replicate their iconic power. I’m leaving out the distraction penalty, storytellers can determine that for themselves. Semi replicates speak with the dead, but doesn’t show rank or allow speaking with ghosts that don’t speak your language.
                                                Speak With Dead without the reach option also doesn't allow speaking with ghosts that don't speak your language.

                                                Always on Active Mage Sight has significant drawbacks: -2 to all rolls unrelated to perceiving or using magic (much harsher than the distraction of the 1e version, which only penalizes things requiring "significant concentration) and it leaves you perpetually susceptible to Nimbus Tilts.

                                                Speak With Dead with reach for Advanced Duration (thus requiring a scene of prep time to activate), the knowledge of whether any in her perception are fated or cursed to die within 24 hours, and a -2 penalty to Extended Actions while in the presence of more Ghosts and/or doomed individuals than their Composure + Gnosis rating more closely aligns with the 1e version of the power.

                                                There's no real reason to restrict the ability to see the Rank of a Ghost.
                                                .

                                                Second: The Calling

                                                Prerequisites: Death 2, Expression 2
                                                When she wills it, the Singer’s voice conveys haunting power. The Banshee sings a song that resonates with the dead. When activating the Attainment while singing, all Ghosts who can hear her voice are beckoned to approach as if by Lure/Repel. The Ghosts that do treat the Banshee as having Good first impression for Social maneuvering, and must stay until the end of the song.

                                                Ghost Lure with reach towards first impression.
                                                As written this requires a scene of Activation, lasts for only a few turns, only works on weak ghosts, and has almost no range at all. You're also trying to replicate Ghost Summons (a Perfecting spell) with Lure and Repel (a Ruling spell).

                                                Forensic Gaze with Instant cast and the ability to witness the final moments might be good here; Ghost Shield could also be a good choice to protect from dangerous ghosts you might encounter.

                                                Optional: Fate 2.
                                                The Banshee may raise both her Death and Fate mage armor at the same time, and gains her Fate dots in General Armor when she does so.

                                                I’m a bit ambivalent about the fate optional, the extra Fate armor is from the original version, but I’m not sure it’s in theme.
                                                All the Arcana defensive spells provided Armor rating in 1e, in 2e they work differently. While allowing both Death and Fate Mage Armor to be up at the same time is... fine? I think Warding Gesture with Advanced Duration makes more sense.

                                                Third Attainment
                                                Lure would work well here, I would do Advanced Scale: Area and good first impression, lasting for as long as the Singer sings:

                                                "The Singer's song calls to the dead, and draws them to her; ghosts, zombies, vampires, and other restless dead that can hear the Singer's haunting melody and have Rank or Composure lower than the Singer's dots in Death are compelled to travel towards the Singer by the fastest safe route possible, whether the affected undead is willingly or forcefully drawn to the Singer their impression level starts one higher than normal."

                                                Fourth Attainment
                                                Ghosts Summons with Instant cast, Open Condition, and a modification so that its duration is for as long as the Singer is singing (basically concentration, if they're disrupted the effect ends).

                                                Fourth alt
                                                Grave Misfortune with Instant cast and Advanced area with the same "while they hear the Singer singing" caveat as above.

                                                Fifth Attainment
                                                A Death Patterning spell with Sensory Range and Advanced Duration that makes it so that if the target is capable of leaving a ghost behind when they die they do so as long as they die within the Mage's Death dots on the Advanced Duration chart.

                                                Fifth alt
                                                Sever Oaths with reach for Lasting, maybe? Since it can modify rather than simply remove.
