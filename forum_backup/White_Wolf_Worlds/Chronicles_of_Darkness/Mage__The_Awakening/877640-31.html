                    If a Death Mage can possess someone then Possession probably goes like this: Enter Twilight, use Death 4 to transform your ephemeral form into a Ghost Mage with the Possession Numina, find acceptable target, or just use Death to "force" your ghost self to possess a target. It's a multiple step process at least.

                    As for the effects of a spliced soul Satchel's spell made the most sense to me. Alternatively, just say that the spell is simply lifting the restriction that soul stones be made of inanimate objects and grants all attached benefits of a soul stone. So the Mage and the target would share a Connected strength Sympathy and the Mage would be able to use them as a Dedicated Tool that could reduce the Paradox die pool by 2. I don't think I'd allow such a soul "stone" to be used to establish a demesne or anything though. Note though, the spell's effect is just "Lift this restriction" all these other effects come from actually creating the soul "stone" which isn't a spell. If the spell ends then the target would no longer be a suitable vessel for the shard of the Mage's soul and it would count as a destroyed soul stone. This wouldn't be a good spell to cast on a Sleeper because you'd potentially be exposing them to a lot of magic they couldn't handle, plus a Sleeper witness to your magic would reduce the effectiveness of making them a soul stone in the first place. On the other hand, it would be a pretty good way to keep track of someone, even Sleepers, because of the lack of Sympathetic Withstand. It wouldn't allow sense swapping though, that's specifically part of the familiar manifestation and not the Connected Sympathy.

                    By the way, the Arcanum Leech, I'd change that from siphoning off a specific number of spells to it having to last through a story. After a story is usually when you get to spend XP anyway, and it removes the clunky bit about having to check if the person it's attached to has cast 10 spells of the appropriate arcana or not. You can just say: Well, this spell has been running without interference for the entire story, so that's my justification for spending XP on this arcana. I'm not sure the reach to effect mundane skills is appropriate since usually only Mind gives those, and even if it is appropriate then this spell should be flipped to benefit mundane skills first with a reach to benefit a designated arcana as well.

Unlike a ghost of a mage, which are nothing more than ephemera-made replicas of the person, a Ghost Mage retains his Awakened powers.                                                             How is this.

                                                            Ghost Form (Death ••••)
                                                            Practice: Patterning
                                                            Primary Factor: Duration
                                                            Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Empathy, Survival.
                                                            This spell allows the mage to transform a subject from twilight state into an Ephemeral Entity known as a ghost. For the duration of the spell the subject has the following traits determined by the mage at the time of casting. If the subject was a mage they use mana to power their abilities and they keep the Arcana ratings that they have, otherwise the newly formed ghost uses essence to power its abilities.
                                                            ** Drawbacks: This power comes with a price. For every hour that the subject remains in ghost form they must roll Power + Resolve + Gnosis - 1 per previous roll. If they fail they start to become disconnected to their former self gaining the Driven condition (see below). If they ever dramatically fail this roll they gain the Amnesia condition forgetting there previous existence believing that they truly are just a ghost.

                                                            ** Driven Condition: (persistent)
                                                            Your character loses interest in everything except one task. This could be brought on by a tragedy, a mental break, or a spell gone tragically wrong. Whatever the reason your character loses interest in everything not related to fulfilling their goal.
                                                            Game Effect: Your character loses interest in the current task at hand in favor of a new task which they will single mindedly peruse. All rolls not related to their new task is at a -2 dice.
                                                            Resolution: Accomplish the goal.
                                                            Beat: Character fails to fulfill an obligation due to pursuing her task.

                                                            Ghost Traits:
                                                            Rank: 2
                                                            * Power, Finesse, and Resistance: Average the dots in each category to arrive at the new rating for each.
                                                            Influence: The mage assigns what the ghost has influence over, this influence is at rank 2.
                                                            Twilight Form: The ghost has the Twilight Form manifestation.
                                                            *** Manifestation: The mage may assign 2 additional manifestation (page 258 – 261 or Chronicles of Darkness page 133 - 136) of his choice on the ghost.
                                                            Numina: The mage may assign 3 Numina (page 261 – 263 or Chronicles of Darkness page 136 - 138) of his choice to the ghost.
                                                            Essence/Mana Max: 15
                                                            Bans: The mage assigns a ban to the ghost.
                                                            Banes: The mage assigns one bane to the ghost.
                                                            All other traits listed on page 256 are calculated accordingly.

                                                            * Edited to follow the suggestion from the Chronicles of Darkness page 127, the states that the Ghosts usually use the average rating in each category from when they were alive.

                                                            ** Edited added a drawback and a condition. It isn't needed but it could bring some interesting situations.

                                                            *** Edited reducing the number of manifestation to that rank of the ghost.
