    2E Legacy Interpretation: Tamers of the Cave

    07-27-2018, 03:05 PM
    So, a preface: this is going to break some Legacy Attainment assumptions.

    This is an update of the Tamers of the Cave from 1E. This strays heavily from conventional assumptions on the construction and power of attainments owing to the signature substance of the Legacy—ether—being limited and limiting, forcing attainments to work within its context. Given the role of the etheric mirror as a yantra even before they existed (in 1E it could function as another Dedicated Tool) and my own desire to give elemental meaning to it which was absent its description in 1E beyond that it was “the void,” the Arcana involved have been altered slightly as well.

    Secondly, the attainments do not map neatly to other spells in their powers or effects. Breakdowns are provided after the Legacy. I will say I’m not fond of how the basic attainments and optional powers are doing two different things, but the 1E Tamers of the Cave had that too and I was trying to reproduce their style. Might do another version where I rip out the wizard vomit and focus on their guidance and martyrdom concepts (probably Fate-primary).


    The Tamers of the Cave
    Background and further details can be found in Legacies: the Ancient

    Sobriquet: Socratics
    Parent Path and Order: Mastigos, Elemental Tamers
    Ruling Arcanum: Death or Prime

    Yantras: Ether (+1), Socratic Debate (+2), Wound Penalties (+1-3, equal to penalty), Taking Risks for the Sake of Another (+1, +2 if a Sleeper), Facing Certain Death (+3)
    Oblations: Community service, fasting, meditation in a solitary place, mentoring



    Initiation
    Requirements: Death 2, Prime 2, Mind 1, Occult 2, Empathy 1, Expression 1
    One must find or be noticed be a current Tamer of the Cave and show a sincere desire to follow the Legacy’s desire for the improvement of the human condition through their own self-sacrificing martyrdom. A prospective Socratic meditates on paradoxical koans under the guidance of their mentor, fasts, and enters a state of near-death for three days. Upon coming out of the trance, they are now members of the Legacy, understanding and capable of calling on the element of the void. The Socratics, like with all the Tamers, view their role to help humanity, and their particular brand of it calls for an ability to understand and be understood by others. A philosophical understanding of Socrates is valued, but not necessary.

    Both Death and Prime are required for the Legacy’s attainments. Moros may choose Prime as a Path Arcanum, while all other Paths learn Death.



    Attainments

    Conversation
    Some of the Optional abilities of the Attainments require the Socratic to engage and remain in conversation. This is defined as in a state of engagement in which no non-reflexive actions may be taken except for those associated with Social Maneuvering, such as the Legacy’s attainments or certain spells cast with conversational yantras and Imagoes (pointed looks, insightful phrases, etc). Given the potentially-long, potentially-time-compressing nature of Social Maneuvering, basic necessities such as sleeping, eating, or bathroom breaks do not break conversation.


    Initiation: Etheric Communion
    A Socratic emerges from their death-trance transformed, linked with and understanding the power and value of nothingness; all springs of it, all returns to it. It is meaning. They are capable of secreting starry ether from their orifices as an instant action, similar to the Death spell “Ectoplasm,” though it is incapable of being used for Manifestations. At this stage the viscous substance cannot be shaped, only smeared (such as on the surface of a mirror). If used in spellcasting, it can function as a Dedicated Tool. The ether vanishes after a scene, or once it has been used in a spell as a yantra.

    Optional: Mind 1, Empathy 1
    The Socratic must understand others to guide and inspire them. This duplicates the Mind spell “Mental Scan”


    Second: From Nothing, Something
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Death 2, Prime 2, Expression 2
    With an instant action, the Socratic may shape their conjured ether into solid forms, similar to the Death spell “Shape Ephemera.” This lasts until the ether vanishes, either at the end of the scene or when it is used as a yantra. When ether is now used as a yantra in spellcasting, the spellcasting roll gains the benefit of 9-Again.

    Optional: Mind 2, Empathy 2
    Through a disarming smile, an intimidating glare, or a placid look suggesting utmost empathy, the Socratic understands enough of others to put them at-ease with them even during the direst of circumstances. As an instant action, the Socratic can improve their first impression by the subject by levels equal to its primary factor of Potency. This is Withstood by Composure.


    Third: Etheric Doorway
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 4,Death 3, Prime 3, Occult 3
    The Socratic smears ether across a portal—such as a sizable mirror or door—or on a surface into the shape of a doorframe. As an instant action, the Socratic turns this into an Etheric Doorway, lasting several turns. Anyone or anything passing through the gateway enters Twilight as a faded, faintly-starry version of itself. This state is capable of perceiving and physically interacting with all forms of Twilight, and vice-versa. Disembodied souls are visible but remain insubstantial without the use of further magic.

    Optional: Mind 3, Empathy 3
    Sometimes called “the Inescapable Question,” with one sentence and 1 point of Mana the Socratic can begin the process of turning enemies into friends, allies into betrayers, and doubters into acolytes. The Socratic asks the subject of this Attainment a question, forcing them into a moment of evaluation. For as long as the Socratic maintains conversation with the subject—typically in the form of Socratic debate—the subject endures the Beaten Down Tilt. This can go on for days or weeks, the participants pausing only to tend to their needs, and is used as much to prevent fights from happening as it is to stop them. This Attainment is Withstood by Wits, its Primary Factor is Potency, and benefits from Advanced Duration.


    Fourth: Etheric Transfiguration
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 6, Death 4, Prime 4
    Sometimes mistaken as the final Attainment of the Tamers of the Cave, they become the ether. The Socratic meditates for a scene, reentering the death trance from their initiation. At the end, ether issues from their orifices, covering them and transfiguring them into a being of pure ether. In this state they may use the Legacy’s previous attainments on themselves, taking an instant action to function as an Etheric Doorway for others, to shift into Twilight themselves, or to alter their forms with “From Something, Nothing.” They might flatten their forms to fit beneath doors, shape their limbs into tools or weapons, or change their shape to appear less human.

    The Socratic may remain in this state for up to three days, requiring no food, drink, or sleep. Those willing to explore deeper mysteries of the void, or commit themselves wholly to a cause or action, may shed their mortal coil and persist as etheric shades for a month, disappearing completely once the attainment ends. This extends to a year at Death/Prime 5.

    Optional: Mind 4, Empathy 4
    The Socratic can force others to revisit and reflect on their actions or share their own for the sake of teaching. With a few words or a gesture, the Socratic and the subject are transported into a moment from either’s memories, one intended to evoke a specific emotion. They can wander about this illusion, remembering the smell of flowers or feeling the coldness of snow, but to observers both subjects remain where they were, still conversing. In the event of physical confrontation, calculate traits and consequences as dream forms. This opens or closes one Door for the Duration, which can end abruptly should the Socratic cease conversation or they are “killed.” This Attainment is Withstood by Composure, its primary factor is Potency, and benefits from Advanced Duration.


    Fifth: Apostle and Tomb
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 8, Death 5, Prime 5
    The Socratic meditates for a scene, reconnecting with the death trance of his initiation like in Etheric Transfiguration. Unlike the previous attainment, at the climax the Socratic spend 1 Mana and suffers 1 Resistant Aggravated level of damage as they rip their etheric form away from themselves, creating a mindless apostle similar to the Prime spell “Eidolon.” The Apostle possesses a Structure equal to the Socratic’s Gnosis, the ability to be the subject of the Legacy’s previous attainments (as Etheric Transfiguration), a Connected sympathetic link, and functions as a 5-dot Retainer for a week after which it fades back into nothing.

    What isn’t widely known is that, should the Socratic die during this time, the Apostle immediately becomes an Anchor for their ghost and is considered Open by the Socratic. This may be the source of some of the strangest rumors of the Legacy, that they sometimes rise from the dead and leave no ghost.

    Optional: Mind 5
    The Apostle, in its base state, may be endowed with intelligence and self-awareness, allowing it to more faithfully serve its creator in its role as retainer or participant in their passion play. The Apostle gains traits and essence as a Rank 1 goetia, the Mortal Mask numina, and can now choose to take advantage of the Legacy’s Attainments on its own, shaping itself, shifting itself and others into Twilight as needed, and engaging in debate. It cannot duplicate Apostle and Tomb.








    Design Breakdown
    I wanted to try my hand at some of those attainments mentioned in the core that are mentioned as impossible to develop praxes for. Them not mapping to arcana is part of the point.
    The first attainment somewhat mimics a Death 2 spell, but the inability to shape it or use it for ghostly manifestations I think puts it in the first attainment category. It functioning as a dedicated tool was in 1E, and I think Prime suits that well enough. The secondary power is just a basic, instant Mind spell.

    The second attainment mimics the Death 2 spell Shape Ephemera with more Reach than would otherwise be possible; I feel its effects are justified given it is limited to ether, and its tendency to vanish during spellcasting. The 9-again mimics As Above, So Below, but persistent specifically to ether; again, I feel this limitation justifies it, and the Socratic needs to barf up some more as an instant action each time he wants to cast with it anyway. The secondary power is Mind 2’s First Impressions with the primary factor changed; this is a personal choice, since I feel any spell with a Withstand not having Potency as its primary is ridiculous. If you'd rather run it more orthodox, just assign the Reach to swapping factors and give it an activation set-up of a scene since it's already meant to be used as part of lengthy debates.

    The third attainment is modeled off of Ghost Gate but utilizes conjunctional Prime to attune users to a universal Twilight. I don’t feel it’s a stretch, given Prime already possessed the ability to make something capable of interacting with all layers of Twilight. The secondary power is a custom Ruling spell. Ruling is capable of inflicting Conditions and Tilts, and Mind Armor can already inflict Beaten Down. The mana cost and requirement to remain in conversation are there to keep it from just being a tool to whittle down an opponent’s WP. The conversational element is more restrictive than what is “needed” to balance it, but it matches the Legacy’s aesthetics and synergizes with the third and fourth secondaries.

    The fourth attainment superficially resembles Death 4’s Shadow Flesh. Prime element is just that you’re a mutable ether person who benefits from all his attainments. Secondary power is a bit hard to pin down: it resembles some combination of Mind’s Emotional Urging, Dream Reaching, and Hallucination. The mechanical effect is from Emotional Urging, the aesthetics are more the other two. Unlike Hallucination, the Mage is part of the experience too. Unlike Dream Reaching, the conversational element makes it easy for the subject to end the effect. I believe these mitigating factors, along with the mechanical effect being from a Mind 2 spell, justifies dressing things up for drama since the only extra advantage this might confer comes from the narrative.

    Fifth attainment is just Prime 5’s Eidolon, slightly different and costlier—1 resistant aggravated isn’t anything to sneer at. The Death effect doesn’t really have a precedented spell, but cut the guy some slack, they’re a second-degree Master and they’re dead. They’ve only got a week left at most. The secondary power is just adding Mind 5’s Psychic Genesis to it, something Eidolon already had available as a conjunctional effect. Numina are Mortal Mask and their Attainments. An argument could be made that this might be a bridge too far, but the character is a third-degree Master with Gnosis 8 and by that point their servant having the arguable equivalent of 1 extra numina is the power equivalent of complaining about acne on a leper.
