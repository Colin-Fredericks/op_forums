    03-30-2020, 04:12 AM
    I was planning on playing a mage assassin with 4 prime. I was going to use platonic form for extreemly powerful 7 +damage throwing knives, and wards and signs for defense. Any other ways you can think of to use prime to quickly defeat another mage?

          Prime is great for it, some immediate uses that come to mind (apart from dispellation):

          1- Wards & Signs is great to defend yourself or your proxies (warded Sleepers with guns are terribly effective against mages).
          2- Supernal Veil, because if their peripheral sight doesn't ping, it means you can setup lots of traps.
          3- Cloak Nimbus, because if your target cannot know where the attack comes from or who setup the trap, then the psychological effect is heightened.
          4- Pierce Deception, because a wary target may try subterfuge against you.
          5- Retexturing objects using Veiling, it can lead to subtle trickery (refer to Hisoka from HunterXHunter and the Reality Makers Legacy for examples).
          6- Platonic forms of weird combined properties, for example Hisoka's Bungee Gum ability
          7- Lots of imbued items. They allow for scalability (with enough mana you could arguably affect the entire world and still only use one spell slot) and high spell factors on command. Words of Command means you can use them far more reliably than anyone else.
          8- Nimbus Forgery, because sometimes tensions are so high and a target is so willing to believe their enemy/rival/adversary did something that they forget to apply due diligence to the evidence. Sometimes all you need is to give someone an excuse and they will escalate things.
          9- Stored spells to let your cabalmates prepare some rapid fire buffs. Pair it with a Mastigos's Space magic and a Prime version of Remote Control to create a rapid fire damage burst.
          10- Make the target seem like a crazed liar by Veiling the truth with a reverse Words of Truth ("Help, someone is trying to take me out" "Sure they are buddy, sure they are"). This might need Mind/Fate to fine tune to specific topics, otherwise it will be very suspicious that the person suddenly became a "blatant pathological liar"

          With major political goodwill with the Mysterium, Guardians or Arrow, its arguably possible that they could have a Timori Daimonomikon for black ops agents. But this would probably come with major political and supernatural strings attached, like having a summoned Fate entity seal an oath not to use your Attainments on Pentacle or Nameless mages without sanction, with a rule of 3 escalating fate based punishments. That is in addition to becoming an ageless thaumavore with a crystalized body, but nothing ventured, nothing gained.

          Lastly, if you just want to play an assassin and Prime is not necessarily a requirement, then I also recommend the Nizari:

          http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...acy-the-nizari

                                          Sure thing. First you place a bunch of attack spells in an item using Stored Spell. Then you do the Prime equivalent of Remote Control to activate it at a distance.

                                          Now you have a Space mage Co-locate/Teleport them so they touch the target. You spend a mana to activate and boom, all those touch attack spells go off at once.

                                          So lets say you have a cabal of 3 Gnosis and Arcanum 3 mages, including yourself.

                                          So you made 2 Potency 3 Stored Spell pieces of paper that last a week. For your last spell slot you make a 2 target remote trigger spell.

                                          The Mastigos opens the co-location and stores 2 attack spells and decides to over Reach to store a third.

                                          The third cabalmate stores 3 attack spells.

                                          Now you teleport the pieces of paper so they touch the target, spend 2 mana to use the stored spells and boom, 18 damage. This is without even considering your cabalmates using yantras.

                                                          Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post
                                                          So, it's being adjusted for clarity in the errata pass, but the idea is that Combined spells use their overall Potency for each "spell", but if the spells do the same thing they don't stack.

                                                          So if you combine a life 3 direct attack with a reach for inflicting arm wrack with a mind 4 direact attack doing lethal, you end up with potency in lethal plus arm wrack. The greater damage spell "wins".
                                                          This is the closest thing I could find to that clarification. It seems it only applies to combined spells. But I couldn't find any other reference to per turn damage limits, so stored and hung spells should still be usable for burst damage.

                                                          Its basic and will certainly not be as good as applying conditions on stronger foes, but its still a decent tool for simple assassinations.
