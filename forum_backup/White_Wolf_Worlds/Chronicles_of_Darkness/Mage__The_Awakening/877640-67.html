                        Second edition added cold to Death's purview. So I decided to make some spells dealing with cold. A cryomancer's toolkit:

                        Coldproof (Death ••)

                        Practice: Shielding
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Occult, Survival

                        Become immune to any damage caused by cold. Note that this only stops cold related damage, a blizzard may not harm you but it's perception penalties would still apply, same with penalties for a slippery ice floor.

                        Supernatural attempts to cause cold-related harm provoke a Clash of Wills.

                        Body Heat Camouflage (Death ••)

                        Practice: Veiling
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Science, Survival

                        The mage invites cold to surround the subject, distorting any heat they radiate. This leaves the subject invisible to any thermal imaging technologies or heat detecting abilities.

                        +1 Reach: The heat is now funneled away from the subject, leaving a thermal image of the subject somewhere else in sensory range. Somebody looking around through thermal goggles might think the subject is on the far left side of the room when he is actually on the far right.

                        Freezing Slash (Death •••)

                        Practice: Weaving
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Science, Weaponry

                        Infuse a weapon with the ability to inflict biting cold to its targets.

                        This spell increases a weapons weapon damage by Potency to a maximum of 5. But has no effect on creatures unaffected by extreme temperature lows.

                        +1 Reach: This spell may affect unarmed attacks and grapples. Without negatively harming the user with its cold.
                        +1 Reach: The spell applies the Knocked Down Tilt.
                        +1 Reach: The spell applies the Stunned Tilt.

                        Cryo-Phasing (Death •••)

                        Practice: Weaving
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Empathy, Intimidation

                        This spell enhances any subject in Death-attuned Twilight to radiate cold into the material world. Every material object the subject phases through will find ice forming on it, this can cause surfaces to give the Ice Tilt. This is involuntary, the subject cannot choose to not freeze something.

                        +1 Reach: The subject has more control and may now choose to not freeze anything she doesn't desire to.
                        +1 Reach: The subject may now choose to radiate even higher levels of cold than before. This can give the area around the subject the Extreme Cold Tilt for so long as he stays there.

                        Freezing-point depression (Matter •••)

                        Practice: Weaving
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Science, Survival, Persuasion

                        Raise or lower a material's freezing point, this can cause water to freeze at room temperature or remain liquid even when well below 0 degrees Celsius (32 F)

                        By lowering a material's freezing point ice and snow can be melted. And so remove the Ice Tilt and reduce the Blizzard Tilt to an Extreme Cold and/or Heavy Winds Tilts.
                        By raising the freezing point. Materials will freeze even add high temperatures. This can be used to create the Ice Tilt.

                        Bomb disposal (Death ••••)

                        Practice: Patterning
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Science, Firearms

                        In the latter parts of WWII, the British, in order to disarm German bombs started soaking them in liquid nitrogen. This froze the battery within until it couldn't pass a current and so the bomb could no longer trigger.
                        With this spell the mage can perform the same trick. Though instead of using liquid nitrogen the mage simply drops the temperature directly with magic.

                        Despite the name this spell works on all types of batteries, not just those used in bombs.

                        Drawback: The Germans didn't know the British were using this trick and thus developed no counter. Today's bomb makers do know however and may develop their bombs to either be immune or to go off when this trick is tried. Any mage would be wise to investigate before using this spell on any bomb.

                        Pykrete (Matter •••• + Death •••)

                        Practice: Patterning
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Science, Survival

                        During World War II, Geoffrey Pyke proposed pykrete as a candidate material for a supersized aircraft carrier for the British Royal Navy. Pykrete features unusual properties, like only being useable in cold temperatures, having a relatively slow melting rate due to its low thermal conductivity, as well as being equal in strength and toughness to concrete.

                        With this spell the mage patterns matter into pykrete. Death is used to create it with the cold temperatures required though without a cold environment or further cooling it will heat up and start to sag.

                        Pykrete has a Durability of 3 and a Structure of 9. *

                        Add Matter 5: Create the material out of nothing.
                        Add Death 5: With this the material maintains its own cold temperature.

                        *See also 'How Strong Is Pykrete?' - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UtIwPfrvTyw and 'Shooting Pykrete' - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OzhFnNY0OQI

                        Megacryometeor (Forces ••••)

                        Practice: Patterning
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Science, Firearms, Survival

                        With this spell the mage changes the weather to cause a very large chunk of ice, a megacryometeor, to impact the current location of a chosen target. Megacryometeors are an actual real-life phenomenon. They weight between 0.5 kilograms (1.1 lb.) to several tens of kilograms.

                        The meteor will form in several minutes, the target location is set however. The meteor is thus easily avoided by moving around. Impact by the meteor deals 10 Lethal damage but Armor and Defense both apply. When impacting objects the damage is 10 minus Durability in Structure

                        Add Fate ••: The impact location is no longer set. And will instead try to impact whatever target the mage desires. A roof or other such cover might still save the target however.
                        +2 Reach: A megacryometeor in Brazil has been recorded to weigh in at more than 50 kilograms (110 lb.) and one in Scotland was reportedly 2 meters (6 ft. 7 in.) in size. Your meteor is now in the same class as these and can now deal even greater damage. 15 instead of 10.

                        Cryonic Sleep (Death ••••)

                        Practice: Patterning
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Occult, Science
                        Withstand: Stamina

                        The body is brought to a state close to death while low temperatures are used to preserve organic tissue. This allows the subject to be cryonically preserved to be revived at a later date. This is not perfect however and causes one Aggravated damage per year that cannot be healed until the subject has been revived. If the entire health track is filled with Aggravated damage revival is no longer possible.

                        Add Mind 2: The subject's mind remains active. If the subject is a mage this will allow them to keep casting spells.
                        +2 Reach: Spend one Mana. The preservation process has been perfected, the subject no longer suffers Aggravated damage and can be revived no matter how much time has passed.

                        Ghost Fire (Death •••• + Forces ••••)

                        Practice: Patterning
                        Primary Factor: Potency
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Science, Intimidation

                        By combining the properties of death, cold and fire; cold flames are created. These flames function like normal fire, except that they do not radiate any heat at all, this does not prevent them from burning anything suitably flammable.
                        In addition to this these flames cannot be put out by lowering the temperature or suffocating them. Only the running out of fuel or the use of Magic can end these flames.

                        +1 Reach: For one Mana, the flames are particularly destructive they now deal Aggravated damage.
                        +1 Reach: The flame burns the souls of those who touch it. This causes the Soul Shocked Condition.
                        +1 Reach: When burning a souled being instead of dealing damage based on the size and 'heat' of the flame, deal damage solely based on the sins (Integrity) of the soul. A person with high Integrity could survive infernos, whereas a person with incredibly low Integrity might not even survive contact with a small candle. Also see Neros' Soul Fire.
                        +2 Reach: Not even the lack of fuel stops these flames as they feed on the entropy that would normally end them. Only Magic or other supernatural means can end these flames now.

                        Élivágar's Ice (Death •••••)

                        Practice: Making
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Survival, Intimidation

                        Rather than creating normal cold, this spell creates cold filled with the power of entropy and death. Anybody who touches the ice this cold creates will find themselves in excruciating pain and feel terribly, terribly cold.

                        Mechanically the victim suffers the Extreme Cold Environmental Tilt, however, this Tilt doesn't disappear until the spell expires.
                        Until then no attempts to warm or heat the subject will have any effect unless it's supernatural. In that case, it causes a Clash of Wills.

                        Absolute Zero (Death •••••)

                        Practice: Making
                        Primary Factor: Potency
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Occult, Science, Intimidation
                        Withstand: Stamina

                        The mage dissipates all heat from the subject leaving it at almost absolute zero. This spell will prove instantly lethal if used on any living creature not capable of resisting extreme cold. If cast on an area the it will kill virtually all life there as well as any life that wanders into the area.

                        Update:
                        Added two Reach options to 'Ghost Fire' for dealing with Souls
                        Update2:
                        Added a new Reach option to 'Body Heat Camouflage'
                        Removed thrown weapon Reach on 'Freezing Slash'
                        Renamed 'Freezing Point Depression' to 'Freezing Point' and added the option to heighten the freezing point rather than just lowering it.
                        Can now apply Defense to 'Megacryometeor'
                        Added an optional Mind effect to 'Cryonic Sleep', also added Withstand
                        Renamed 'Demonic Ice' to 'Élivágar's Ice'
                        Expanded the description of 'Absolute Zero', also added Withstand
