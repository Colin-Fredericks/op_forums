    Opinions Thread: Astral Consilium

    02-08-2023, 11:20 PM
    What assumptions would you make about a Consilium who conducts all of their official buisness -or at least, as much as is feasible- in the Astral? That is, weekly/monthly/periodic Consilium and Caucus meetings held in a relevant Tenemos realm -or a member's Onieros, depending- with no physical meetups unless absolutely necessary. This includes things like mentorship - new apprentices being given means to enter the Astral and trained in their own Onieros whenever possible.

    Spoilering my personal assumptions. Please post your own first, if possible, so we can see similarities and differences!
    1. Hallows and Demesnes are common, and possibly so are Synesi
      1. Mind magic is very common - Create Synesi is taught to all Masters.
    2. Merits like Astral Adept and Shadow Self are common
    3. Imbued Items that affect sleep -bestowing the Astral Adept Merit, casting Dream Reaching, etc- are very common
    4. Local Legacies tend towards Astral traditions, such as the Dreamspeakers, Clavicularius​, Bene Ashmodai, Imagineers, etc.
    5. Some combination of paranoia and actual physical danger encourage low-contact
      1. Seer/Banisher/Other Splat dominance of the local?
      2. Habits ingrained from a time when Seer/Banisher/Other Splat dominated the local?
      3. Alternatively, everyone is too spread out to engage in-person in a reasonable timeframe.
    6. A more widespread aversion to and distain for Paradox than normal, since Paradox is easier to surpress in the Astral.
      1. Lower opinions towards Sleepers, since Sleepers are a big reason the Astral is so much safer for magic?
      2. Alternatively, higher instances of Paradox, since the Astral's lower risk conditions Mages to be more reckless in the waking world?
    7. Heralds are relied on to service Mages who can't reach the Astral for whatever reason, giving the post a lot of influence
      1. Mages who can't enter the Astral get significantly less say in local politics
      2. Cabals with insufficient means for all their members elect a primary representative?
    8. Few Mages know each other in person - most have only ever seen the rest in their Dream Forms.
    9. Space Magic is out of favor as a communications medium
      1. Paranoia make Sympathy-Shielding too common for remote contact via Scrying?
      2. The Astral is easier to use, since it relies less on knowledge of Arcana to facilitate remote contact?
      3. A local Mystery futzes with Space, like in a couple of the Dark Eras settings?
    Last edited by Cauthon; 02-08-2023, 11:36 PM.

          Wouldn’t something like this have it’s own Tenemos realm just by being a organization?

          From what I remember of the Sundered World, this actually was a canon practice. It was impractical to travel, and you might be the only Mage in your village, so Mages would communicate through the astral.

          I can see a larger acceptance of unwise behaviors, due to the safety net afforded by the astral. People would act slightly different, kinda like how people act on the internet. The counter point to this is that one can probably spot the unwise, due to how one’s astral appearance works.

          Superficial Looks would be even more influential on social stuff, since in the astral they actually tell you something about people’s conception of themselves.

          People would be more relaxed about going into someone’s Oneiros. Most people really wouldn’t show the inside of their heart to others, with some exceptions. Partially because it’s embarrassing, and partially because it can be used against you and the consequences are really bad if someone does so. I think the pool of people allowed inside *might* be expanded.
