                    2E doesn't seem to have the requirement of Life in order to transform something living into something else. Death alone is enough for Shadow Flesh.                                                 Originally posted by Horodrigo View Post
                                                Some questions about Time:
                                                1 - How does someone perceive the Tell-tale signs that something is different after a Lasting effect like Time 3 Shifting Sands?
                                                2 - How can anyone get back to present time after altering the past? The present is frozen? Cause if not and you change things at normal time rate, you'll never catch up with the present cause every 5 minutes you rewinded will have passed on the present.
                                                3 - Reverting time can save people souls from death? What are the cons of doing this?
                                                1) When you're in the past the various actors who are there if they're able to perceive your presence and the tell-tale signs of your time traveling can protect themselves from your meddling. Alternatively, Mages can shield themselves from temporal tampering prematurely as much as possible to avoid having their pasts changed on them without them noticing. For instance, you're going to an important meeting with the Heirarch and you're ahead of another Mage who uses Time in the list of guests, you might shield yourself from temporal shenanigans until the meeting is over so they can't go back in time and jump the line.
                                                2) The present remains frozen from the perspective of the traveler. To the people in the Present the traveler doesn't appear to do anything different, the situation has simply changed to what it's always been.
                                                3) Unintended consequences, say two people one a friend and the other an enemy died in one turn before you were able to go back, you go back in order to keep your friend from dying, but as a result the person you were trying to kill is also alive now when you catch up the present. Stuff like that.
