    [2E Dark Eras fan] Hollow Keepers

    06-18-2016, 06:58 AM
    My partner choose to play exotic Legacy from Dark Eras Sundered World – The Hollow Keepers. There is not very much on them beyond short paragraph, so I will quote this for the Legacy fan write-up idea.

    The Hollow Keepers: This Legacy seals part of their souls away in clay urns that they bury and hide. Thus protected, they truck with spirits and the dead, their souls safe from harm. The Hollow Keepers undertake the most dangerous of ventures into the other realms to deal with their inhabitants. Of course, as the tales go, get hold of a Hollow Keeper’s soul-urn and you become her master.

    So idea is that Hollow Keepers can work as technically without the Soul, but still with them tucked away in their sacred urn. Here are my rough ideas, will be polished later in topic.
    Hollow Keepers

    Origins
    Parentage: Thyrsus
    Appearance & Background: In Sundered World, Keepers, as part of Holy Potters cast, are looked with awe and fine dressed. They are connectors to gods, protectors from unknown and make your souls secured for the afterlife.


    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Death 2, Occult 2
    Initiation: Initiate need to lock his own soul in to Soul Jar made from urn and buried it in the ground.
    Organization: None. Legacy is officially forgotten in modern times. In Vinca culture they were high caste of priest-potters, connected by Tutor-Student relation.
    Nickname: Potters
    Theory: Soul is more than a shell Death Arcanum let you to operate. This Legacy seals part of their souls away in clay urns that they bury and hide. Because you lock only part of yours soul, you are not really Soulless, even if looking as such. Thus protected, they truck with spirits and the dead, their souls safe from harm. The Hollow Keepers undertake the most dangerous of ventures into the other realms to deal with their inhabitants. Of course, as the tales go, get hold of a Hollow Keeper’s soul-urn and you become her master.


    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Death
    Yantras: Creating sacred runes on ceramics ( +1 ), Creating Sacred Urn to lock things inside (+2).
    Oblations: Preparing Sacred Urn for other, Tendering the site of burial of urn, communing with ghosts or spirits.


    Attainments:
    First: Souls Sense
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    Dead are only shells of once living souls. Speak with them and you can learn great lengths of those still living. Works as Death 1 Speak with the Dead with used Reach for Instant casting.
    Optional: Spirit 1
    Not only ghosts are parts of souls. Keepers believe that spirits are more vibrant, living parts of human souls, now split and around us as spirits. This Attainment give them Exorcist's Eye with Reach for Instant casting to see and speak with them.
    Second: Sacred Urn
    Prerequisites: Death 2, Occult 3
    The core of Keepers philosophy is to locked up Soul in the urn. This Attainment let them create it. By following secret arts of Vinca holy potters, engraving High Speech runes on the urn and smolder them in the furnace, they can replicate effects of Soul Jar and Soul Armor spells on it. Once placed in it, all usual effects that affect the Soul fail automatically. Stealing Soul requires finding the Urn, or casting Sympathetically through the ones body of it. Sacred Urn creation is done in Ritual Time and are Lasting to item.
    Optional: Spirit 2
    Without the Soul, those that sacrificed it to urn would be quickly Soulless. But the Soul is not lost, only moved to other container. With this power, Urn is so changed that target locking inside it is omitting drawbacks of Soulless Condition as long as his Soul is in Sacred Urn, bind by Spirit magic to be still ‘alive’. If the Soul would be freed from Sacred Urn, it’s original owner would instantly start a process of Soul Loss.
    Third: Shed the Soul
    Prerequisites: Death 3, Occult 3 with Specialization in Souls
    With prepared Urn, Hollow Keeper can lock the Soul in it. This Attainment works as Sever Soul spell on Sleepers with Instant Casting. It also makes possible for the Hollow Keeper to use it on himself, locking his own Soul in Sacred Urn. With Death 5, Keeper can use this Attainment on Awakened also.
    Optional: Spirit 3
    With this option, those how’s Souls where taken by Hollow Keeper into Sacred Urn are instantly under the Ephemeral Shield like effect with variant that protects from spirits physical attacks.
    Fourth: Soul Travel
    Prerequisites: Death 4, Occult 4
    With Soul secure in the Urn, now Keeper can go on the journeys he needs to. Just as Souls can change realms instantly on the moment of birth and death, so does the Keeper. He can transfer himself and any one he touches in to ghost related Twilight, as Soul is journeying in to afterlife when people are dying. It works as Ghost Gate spell with Instant Casting and transformation ephemera options.
    Optional: Spirit 4
    Following spirits to their realms, he can make himself or anyone he touches to travel instantly across the Gauntlet. It works as World Walker spell with Instant Casting option.
    Fifth: Edge of Soul
    Prerequisites: Death 5, Occult 4
    Ultimate power of the Keeper is to travel where everything ends – or everything begins. He can Open Avernian Gate with Ritual Casting time, but he can join it to anywhere he was before in Underworld.
    Optional: Spirit 5
    As master of Souls arts, Keeper is potentially immortal until his own Soul is locked in the Sacred Urn. Whenever his body would be killed, his Soul returns to near the Urn. From there he lives as semi-Ghost Mage with Sacred Urn as Anchor and can overtake someone’s body that open Keepers Sacred Urn. Souls then are swept and Keeper lives on in body of previous host. He need to create next Sacred Urn to put his own Soul once more into it. Those Ghost Mages are known to look in Twillight for the unsuspecting victims, to become their new hosts. This power was reason that in the end, Hollow Keepers were hunted down and defeated at the first time.
    And here is alternative Attainment for my partners character, based more on Egyptian mythology, but still in the spirit of Keepers. Probably it was created by Potters from the regions of Nile river, centuries later than Vinca.

    Alternative Attainment

    Fourth: Sanctuary of Soul
    Prerequisites: Death 4, Occult 4
    Body is the Souls temple. But it’s also weakness. Hollow Keeper can cut his own organs out of body to make himself immune to magic on them – and by this on those Arcana. It works as Death based Supernal Dispelation spell casted in Ritual Time with Indefinite Duration, with restriction that proper organ will be left in Urn. Keeper is totally fine with them in Urn, sustained by undead magic of Death Arcanum. Each organ fills one Sacred Urn that need to be created separately with previous Attainments.

    Organs in Urn / Arcanum Resistance
    Brain – Mind
    Heart – Life

                          First attainment looks fine, you going to spend the one Reach they get on Instant Activation or Advanced Duration?

                          Second attainment and third attainment are the same spell effect. Why? I think you should make the 2nd attainment Soul Armor and spend the Reach for Instant Activation. I'd make the Spirit Optional a ritually activated Ephemeral Shield and use the Reach for Advanced Duration. It's for when the Hollow One knows he's going on a journey that will have him dealing with spirits or ghosts, it also automatically gets the Death 2 benefit protecting against the abilities of ghosts.

                          So, 3rd attainment, I say break the rules a little, it's ritually prepared, right? But the two Reach you have are spent for Advanced Duration and the Lasting effect which give the Hollow Ones just a tiny bit of a discount on the power. It's not going to break the game if they can make permanent soul jars so screw it. For the Spirit effect I'd use Shadow Walk with one Reach for Advanced Duration and then another Reach for Advanced Scale so that Shadow Walk targets the Mage and the Soul Jar he's just made. This actually provides significant protection to the Mage's soul (Which is what they're worried about after all, right?) and the Mage himself. If automatic secondary factors weren't half arcana rounded down you wouldn't need the Advanced Scale, kind of a bummer about that. You could consider breaking the attainment a bit for that too, but -with- Advanced Scale you can target enough other people to make the Mage a kind of shepherd through perilous ephemeral situations. Following the examples of other spells that Spirit has that can add in Death or Mind to have an effect on ghosts and goetia I'd say that Shadow Walk also automatically veils the targets from ghosts as well as spirits.

                          Fourth attainment: Sever soul, ritually done, Duration and Potency have no effect on this spell so blow the 2 Reach for a made up effect that protects the subject from the separation of its soul. For the Spirit Optional I'd say a Place of Power effect spend the Reach for Instant and Advanced Duration. This plays into the Hollow One's tendency to want to have strong protection from the ephemeral. Also, when he hits Spirit 5 he can just drop the Gauntlet and walk into Shadow whenever he feels like. That is what can happen when the Gauntlet is reduced to 0, right?

                          5th attainment: I'd consider something like Spirit Manse, etc.

                              Attainment 1

                              Speak with the Dead (Death O)
                              +1 Reach for Instant casting

                              Exorcist's Eye (Spirit O)
                              +1 Reach for Instant casting

                              Clear and obvious choices. There is one thing left to decide, though. Does this Attainment only work on the caster, or can this effect be shared with others by touch?

                              Attainment 2

                              "Sacred Urn" (Death OO)
                              The very idea behind the Legacy is that they sever their Souls for security. If they're to do it from the start, this Attainment should allow it. Normally, Soul Jar can be cast upon any container and contain any Soul. If we assume these Mages only make one consecrated Urn and only use it for their own Souls, the effect is much more limited that the basic spell. The same goes for Sever Soul. Normally a third dot spell - it works on any Sleeper. The Hollow Keepers only cast it on their Soul and only to move it to a single container. I think this is a place to make a non-standard Attainment. A modified version of Soul Jar + Sever Soul + Soul Armor. The last one stems from the fact, that the Mage has no Soul on them to target. All usual effects that affect the Soul fail automatically. Stealing it requires finding the Urn, or casting Sympathetically through the Keeper. The Soul transfers to and from the Urn are done through Ritual casting and are Lasting.

                              "Sacred Urn" (Spirit OO)
                              If the Mage has no Soul, they're a walking Paradox risk, they're deteriorating and they can't access the Astral. This won't do. If you want more control in their life for the Keeper, this Attainment can fix all symptoms of Soullessness. If you want to force them to interact with their Urn more often, you can keep some sort of restrictions. I'm hesitant to write more, because each choice has important ramifications for the game.

                              Attainment 3

                              Ghost Shield (Death OO)
                              +1 Reach for Instant casting
                              +1 Reach to defend against Ghostly physical attacks

                              Ephemeral Shield (Spirit OO)
                              +1 Reach for Instant casting
                              +1 Reach to defend against Spiritual physical attacks

                              These offer more security since that's what the Keepers aim for. To make those fit within the Legacy's metaphysics let's assume they only work on the caster and only when their Soul is in the Urn. You could make these the fourth Attainment, if you make them protect against Werewolf Gifts, Strix shadow powers and other strangeness. Also, consider if these effects can be shared with others by touch. It would take some tweaking of the metaphysics, but it can be done.

                              Attainment 4

                              Ghost Gate (Death OOO)
                              +1 Reach for Instant casting
                              +1 Personal belongings aren't destroyed in the process
                              With Spirit 4 the Mage can create Spirit aspected Gates as well. They can also cast this spell while in Twilight, to move to a different frequency.

                              Reaching (Spirit OOO)
                              +0 Reach for Ritual casting
                              +1 Go to the Shadow and back
                              The one remaining Reach goes to enhance the Ghost Gate Attainment.

                              Wrapped up in protective Attainments, the Keepers can now venture into dangerous territory. Both Attainments can be used to take companions. However, they might be dependent on the Keeper to get back, so trusting everything on one person is a risk.

                              Attainment 5

                              Open Avernian Gate (Death OOOOO)
                              +0 Reach for Ritual casting
                              +1 Go anywhere in the Underworld that the Keeper has been

                              "Discorporate" (Spirit OOOOO)
                              Until now, we've had a certain symmetry between the Death and Spirit Attainments, but no longer. Since it is the ultimate power of the Legacy I suggest something big. If the Keeper would die, they can Discorporate like a spirit would and be reborn upon the site of their Urn. Of course, for a time they're much more vulnerable. If this feel too powerful, I suggest being in Twilight, Shadow or Underworld as a requirement for this power to work. If the Keeper died in the Material, they'd be out of luck. Alternatively, you can give them Regeneration (Life 4) or outright make them unable to die as long as their Souls are contained. Entities able to kill the character at this point likely have other ways to make their life miserable (like dismemberment). Also, if you give the Keepers a time limit for reclaiming their Souls, such conditional invulnerability might not be overpowered.
