                This version of the Tremere was not really designed with the whole "Theban captivity" idea in mind. In my head-canon, these Tremere stumbled upon their truth of the soul in any number of ways, possibly multiple ways given how dwellers in the Supernal can rewrite history. Tremere is/was/could have been a mage who Ascended, almost Ascended, or just barely escaped the Time Before, or even a conglomeration of people that coalesced into one legendary person over time that delivered the secret of the Final Watchtower. Or maybe it was never a person at all. Perhaps the Final Watchtower forced itself out of the Abyss into the Fallen World and touched the first liches.

                In that head-canon, the "breath-eaters" would probably be a conquered Reaper Legacy of the Tremere. That Legacy's history records the Theban Captivity, and their Attainments would focus around stealing the "breath" (souls) of people. These mages would have been interesting to the Tremere because they have Reaper-like powers, but don't seem to actually steal people's souls. Not to mention the connection to vampirism. The House in modern times would be full of innovative Attainments based on the original Legacy. They would by far have one of the weirdest sets of House Attainments. They would be a House that explores the Supernal concepts behind vampirism.

                The breath-eaters could also be some Tremere transformation gone awry, like the Preta. in that case, they would be a parallel development of sorts, but still connected to the main Order. Perhaps an enterprising Kindred elder got his hands on a would-be Tremere before they could complete the initiation. Elder attempts to Embrace the soulless initiate, and you get something weird instead. You could consider them a minor template of the Tremere, maybe? You could even weave them into the original idea: some time in history, a kindred elder (the Theban?) accidentally created some weird half vampire/half mages in the way described in this paragraph. The Tremere later absorbed this transformation as a new House, with the weird Attainments and focus described in the first paragraph.

                            Hey ! Basically, I adore your take on the Tremere (the mechanics are elegant, and their new status of being beyond the Supernal divisions of the Arcana is just mindblowing).

                            I'm currently trying to elaborate Houses for Arcana that have not yet a Tremere House specialized in them, and I thought it would be interesting to share it here. I may edit this post if I manage to wrap it up, but I'm not sure I will have the time, but I'd very much like to get some feedback about the way these Houses can incorporate Soul-Magic in their Attainments (I feel like I've skipped the problem by making "normal" Legacies Attainements, which is not half as interesting as exploring the Art of the Liches).
                            HOMEBREW HOUSES


                            House Xolotl
                            Xolotl was the Aztec deity of death and lightning: a grim figure, with the face of a rabid hound, a flayed body, and feet pointed backwards: its priests knew how to diffract the Souls of human sacrifices in waves of pure energy, When the Conquistadors came and sacked Tenochtitlan, the few surviving members of the clergy received no less than three offers, from each of the European Awakened Sects. The Pentacle offered them shelter and peerage, if they gave up on their Reaping ways. The Seers offered them inclusion within the New Occidental Order, at the cost of enslavement. The Tremere simply offered revenge. And Xolotl’s children chose revenge.

                            House Boon: None of Nature’s accesses of fury is unknown to Xolotl’s devotees: they revel in storms and earthquakes, and dance amongst the ruins left by men. As long as they maintain the Hollowed state, Xolotl Liches can turn spend one Mana to convert any maluses stemming from environmental Conditions or Tilts, up to an Extreme Environment level of half their Hollow (rounded up) into bonuses.

                            House Weakness: Impatient like a rabid dog, touchy as if their nerves were laid bare, and clumsy as if their limbs were switched: indeed, Xolotl’s servants are a living (or unliving) portrayal of their god. They add five to the target success number of every extended or teamwork action they’re involved in (even magical ones, like Scrutiny, Supernal Summoning or entering the Astral). Also, they’re considered as having two less Doors than they should normally have in Social Maneuvering.

                            Third Attainment; Whims of Tlaloc (Control Weather +2R)
                            Fourth Attainment: Rattling of the Tzitzimime (Influence Sound +3 R
                            Fifth Attainment: Fires of Xibalba (effect about Soul-consuming Flames)


                            Prime : House Israfil
                            Founded and mostly attended by former Obrimos with a monotheistic worldview, the House Israfil forms peaceful Angels of Death: not fighters, but able to resolve any fight nonetheless. They believe that by reaping Souls, they further the rebirth of the One Soul of the Fallen World – whether they name it Sophia, Schekhinah, or Khwajah-al-Akbar: in turn, that Soul shines like a halo on their brow, allowing them to perform miracles through the Souls they freed.

                            House Boon: An Israfil maintaining the Hollowed Condition may add half his dots in Prime (rounded up) to any of his Arcana rating for the purpose Mage Armor. If he’s also suffused by the Supreme Coin, he may forego this benefit and activate two different Mage Armors in the same Scene. (However, the Awakened Soul is destroyed at the end of the Scene).

                            House Weakness: If all magic stems from the Divine, how could an Israfil try to evade its effects without transgressing God’s will? Israfil Liches suffer a -2 penalty to Clashes of Wills and all non-Physical-based resistance rolls against Spells and other supernatural effects targeting him. Further, they gain the Spooked Condition if they witness or are targeted by a successful Spell garnering more successes than their Ego.

                            Third Attainment: Gerasa (Dispellation: Instant Casting, Lasting)
                            Just like Jesus banished the Legion of demons who took over the Gerasene and ordered them to enter the mind of swines, the Israfil seizes the wretched, divided magic of the Awakened and seal it within the lesser souls he harvested.

                            Fourth Attainment: Gethsemane (Words of Power: Instant Casting, Sensory Range, Condition-Inducing) All the apostles fell asleep between the olive trees of Gethsemane, for none was fit to bear the inner agony of the Christ, already conscious of his nearing death. Drawing upon the echo of those words, the Israfil states his own objective in objectively true terms.

                            Fifth Attainment: Armageddon (Display of Power: Counterspell made easier, Instant Casting)
                            The Israfil’s Nimbus flares impossibly wide, forming three sets of radiant wings who deploy and circle the zone he’s in, trapping up to twenty of his foes in a blank landscape illuminated by their eerie light. A Duel Arcane has begun.


                            Space: House Carcosa

                            House Boon: While they maintain the Hollow Condition, Carcosa Liche’s Sympathetic Connection to anyone whose face they have seen at least once and who’s currently under a Mental Condition is raised by one step (two if the Condition is a Persistent one).

                            House Weakness: Each Carcosa Lich is a replica of the Dement City, and brings it in his wake: that makes them quite easy to follow, as their trails becomes hallways, traces of their presence shine like signposts, and all roads bend to lead to them. They’re permanently under the Easy Prey Condition. Further, they must expend one additional Mana to magically affect any material used to restrain them physically – be it cuffs, cell walls, or even the flesh of a being Grappling them.

                            Third Attainment: Have You Seen The Yellow Sign? (Scrying, Instant Casting, Sensory, Remote Casting)

                            Fourth Attainment: Behind the Pallid Mask (Perfect Sympathy, Medium Connections, Fool Magic)

                            Fifth Attainment: Rip the Tatters of the King (Borrow Threads, Instant Casting, Sensory, Remote)

                                Most of the Attainments from first edition seemed to be modified versions of spells that could make use of benefit the soul's owner had, or was otherwise linked to the soul. The third Attainment of House Israfil could use the Arcana ratings of a soul in place of the mage's own, for example. But keep in mind, the House Attainments follow the rules for Legacy Attainments. So the dispellation Attainment would require the mage to touch the target (which may be hard, if it's an area spell). In general, the Prime and Space Attainments seem easier to work souls into. The first House that controls the weather is a bit more difficult for me. Some Tremere Attainments don't have to directly utilize the soul, though. House Heo Sel has one Attainment that doesn't. For the Aztec Forces House, it may be good to keep Control Weather as-is and then use the other two with souls.

                                Also, I usually try to keep the House Boon be some addition of Arcanum dots to something, although this is mostly because I have not bothered to really explore other options.
