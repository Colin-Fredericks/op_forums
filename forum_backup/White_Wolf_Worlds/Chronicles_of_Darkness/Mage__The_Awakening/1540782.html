                                              Originally posted by Yumlum View Post
                                              I think there is still think that belief still kind of effect reality in Chronicle of Darkness, with how the Supernal is affected by Humanity, the Blood Bather ritual never being the same, even when if the ritual was taught by a mentor, the existence of Astral Realm, and other Psychomatic Realm, and the Xenotechnology Endowment from Hunter the Vigil, all evidence that belief does affect reality of Chronicle of Darkness, while it's not as pliable as World of Darkness reality, it's certainly isn't rigid.
                                              "Certain elements of reality are shaped by human belief" is not the same thing as "if you convince enough people that something is true, it becomes objectively true."

                                              Humanity's passive influence on the Supernal is largely in terms of creating new symbolic correspondences. The parts of the Astral Realm that correspond to human culture notably have little direct influence on the material world and/or communicate in human terms because the characters they are communicating with are humans. Even the part of the cosmology where literal figments of human imagination can interact with physically present entities do so at one or two removes or after undergoing transformations that change them to not-insubstantial degree.

                                              The blood bathing ritual changing from person to person throughout the years is as readily explained by occult variables changing over time (Dreams of Avarice puts forth a mythology in which the literal creator of the universe aims for a world with is constantly changing in opposition to Ammut's drives toward stasis and destruction). Ditto for Xenotechnology gizmos, which need "aliens cores" and tissue samples from monsters and facilities — the need for constant maintenance is as significant as the diversity of takes on the same schematic.

                                              In Chronicles, the human soul is capable of influencing the world through magical processes, which Mummy and Mage's crossover material implies is because the world and the human soul are made from the same building blocks. These processes exist independently of human belief — the God-Machine essentially performs miracles at an industrial scale by leveraging symbolism most people will never see, High Speech messages can be found in the world with no human origin, and assorted other magical effects can appear spontaneously with a pre-existing connection to human culture that's tenuous at best.

                                              The biggest non-localized changes in setting metaphysics on the books are either the product of attempts at Ascension or the consequences of a major interaction with a godlike being (e.g. the Sundering, the change from Oracles to Heroes, etc.). Deviant presents a framework in which setting-altering consequences can arise from the Projects of conspiracies, but that's a double-whammy of active human efforts and meddling with forces beyond their full comprehension. Belief affects reality in Chronicles the way human occupation affects an environment — deforestation might happen to the woods at the edge of town, but it happens because people with axes went and chopped down trees for building materials, not because they thought the boundary between the parts of the land with trees and log cabins was closer to the river.
