    12-27-2016, 01:07 PM
    Ephemeral Wraiths
    There are many hot topic and heated discussions when it comes to souls, ghost, reincarnation and life after death. These points of view are as varied as the cultures that spawned them and even within cultures there are diverse beliefs. The study of death, ghosts and of the soul is an obsession for many different groups. Most never take this study past an academic curiously, a hobby or just a fanciful whim. They sit around and wonder about life beyond the grave and romanticize about reunions with lost loved ones. However there are the few that will take their studies further looking at the cause an effect, trying to come up with some rhyme or reason to explain what lies beyond. However they are stopped due to a lack of a true understanding of death.

    The awakened have learned to look beyond the lie and to start looking at the fundamentals of what it means to die. They break down the process and examine the pieces through the lenses of the different Arcana. Still very few of them can grasp the intricacies that surround the human soul and fail to look past their limited views defined by their own Arcana. The Moros have master the concepts of death, ghosts and the soul moving on to the topic of how to affect them. They know that they can heal the soul, harm it and even destroy it. The same goes for a ghost. Then there are those among their ranks that look to have an even closer understanding.

    There are multiple groups among the Moros, and other paths, that delve into the intricacies of the soul but three of these groups have taken their studies of the soul to extreme heights. The Tremere Lich whose only goal is to devour it to extend his own miserable life. The Shadowbinders that take a ghost and bind it to their soul for healing their soul, forcing the ghost to serve as a familiar during this time. Then there are the Ephemeral Wraiths, they wish to transform their essence into a hybrid form, a meshing of awakened being and that an Ephemeral Entity becoming a Ghost-Mage.

    The Wraiths, as that are called, do not look to control the soul or harm it in any way. Their goal is to understand it so that they may one day transform into a Ghost-Mage, the liches can get there but through a much darker path. Their studies begin with viewing the souls of the living, looking for the markers that will determine why a ghost can be formed upon a person’s death. Studies show that not everyone that dies will become a ghost condemned to walk in twilight.

    As their studies progress they learn to strengthen these souls and to manipulate the souls of the living. They study ghosts in great detail to determine what makes them different than other ephemeral substances. What was triggered upon death that allowed them to form? They spend endless hour watching ghosts as they go about their bleak existence. They spend just as much time studying the souls of the living looking for the markings that will determine if a ghost will form after death. Then they study their on soul to see if they can foster such markings within themselves.

    The more that they study this phenomenon the deeper they discover the rabbit hole goes. They learn to tap into the souls of the living and the ghosts of the dead, not harming them but to get a deeper understanding of their existence. They learn to influence the subjects of their studies in subtle way as to not disrupt their existence. They learn why a ghost is dammed to walk the earth and why some are force into an endless loop repeating the same task over and over again. Learning that the anchor they are tied to can also be manipulated, changed or even replaced. They are searching for what happens to a soul upon death, there are those that would say that it is destroyed and just as many that say it is releases to a realm that is beyond our comprehension. Whatever happens to it the mage knows that it can no longer be manipulated by awakened magic, therefore all intents and purposes it is gone.

    Finally they learn to transform their own twilight infused ephemeral substance into that of a ghost while still maintaining their own awakened abilities. They learn how to avoid the endless droning existence of the ghost. They have reached the pinnacle of their existence and have gained power over it. But they also discover that there studies do not end there. There is more than just existing in the twilight but for those answers they must travel to the Underworld and that frightens them to the core of their new existence.

    Origins
    Parent Path or Order: Moros, The Mysterium

    Nickname: Wraiths; Half-Souled (derogatory)

    Background: No one really knows where and even why this Legacy started or ended, as records were shallow at best, but their demise was around 300 BCE. However early in the 15th century the studies began again. The heart of the Legacy sprang up in London and it has been growing ever since. Over the years since it's resurrection it has been suggested that this Legacy was originally part of the Pancryptiates and was defunct during the struggles between the Keepers of the Word and the Pancryptiates. Reportedly this group was primarily dedicated to finding a way for the Pancryptiates to leave the sleeping world behind. It is also believed that they might have been one of the “Hand of the Mind” that was stealing knowledge from the other orders to further the goals of the Pancryptiates, this could have been responsible for their downfall and eventual dissolution.

    Since its revival the group has been under the watchful eye of the other orders, and there has been some debate as to the morality factor of this group. There are those that would say that the Legacies obsession with the transformation has lead them down the Left Hand Path, fearing that they will end up following the path of the Tremere Liches. Some say that their practices and core beliefs are nowhere near that of the Tremere and the other Left Hand Legacies. For the most part they are being watched and put on a tight leash their every move scrutinized for the slightest impropriety.

    The heart of the order has remained in London since its rebirth and is thriving well there. And it is needless to say but the Ephemeral Wraith and the Tremere Liches are not on good terms.

    Appearance: The Wraith come from all walks of life, from the rich to the poor, the noble to the peasant, male, female, young and the old. Even though they can come from any nationality their numbers are mainly European, this is expected to change as the practice becomes more widely spread.

    As they start into this Legacy their skin takes on an ashen hue and their body looks ever so slightly gaunt. As they progress through the ranks, and with each attainment, these conditions worsen until they reach the fifth attainment. By this time they are unmistakably pale and they take on a more emaciated look. To the trained eye there is no doubt what is happening, to the layman he looks sickly. Where do you think the saying “He looks like he has one foot in the grave” came from.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Death 2, and either Intimidation 2, Medicine 2 or Investigation 2.

    Initiation: An extended and meaningful interview with a sick and dying person and with a ghost.

    Organization: The relative recent revival of this legacy makes it hard to form any unified organization, however the majority of the Legacy are in London where they have formed a council to deal with major issues. They try not to interfere with the lives and studies of their members as this style of study is very personal. They do have functions and collect fees for their services to members and non-members alike.

    Theory: There is a delicate balance between life and death. There is also some correlation between a soul and a ghost that is stuck in twilight. Just what the correlation is remains the topic of discussion within this Legacy.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Death

    Yantras: Ephemera (Legacy tool +1), Soul Jar (empty +1 or full +2), Viewing the soul of the subject (Persona +2), Funeral Shroud (Legacy Tool +1), A coin recovered from a corpse who’s soul never crossed the river Styx (Legacy Tool +2).

    Oblations: Extensive study of death rituals in different cultures; Studies of the different beliefs of the afterlife; hours of watching a ghost; Extended viewing of a living soul; Contemplating in a place where a death occurred; Wandering in Twilight.

    Attainments
    The attainments of this Legacy show the different levels of understanding. They are the culmination of years of personal study. They start with the viewing of souls and ghosts into the direct contact with them, culminating with the transformation into a Ghost-Mage the ultimate goal of all in this Legacy.

    First Attainment: Soul Sight
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The initiate uses this spell on an almost continuous basis, as he studies both the souls of the living and the dead. This attainment enables the initiate to see, but not interact with, the souls of the dead and living alike. They can use the effects of Soul Marks when viewing these souls. This is always done as an instant casting and may determine one of the following per level of potency. She can discern the presence of Persistent Conditions, if the subject is Awakened, if the subject is a supernatural being, if the subject has created a soul stone (see “Soul Stones” p. 98), if the subject has had her soul tampered with, if the subject is Possessed, the presence of any Gnosis 5+ Legacy Attainments, if the subject has eaten or otherwise consumed another’s soul, or if the subject is suffering from a Paradox Condition. Reach used is for instant casting.
    Optional Arcanum: Prime 1
    The initiate can also gain information about the target by studying its connections to the Supernal. Even though the information gathered isn’t specifically tied to death or the soul it is still useful information to have. This attainment enabler the initiate to gain the abilities of the Supernal Vision spell. Reach used is for instant casting.

    Second Attainment: Produce Ectoplasm
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Death 2
    The apprentice can produce ectoplasm from his mouth, regurgitating the thick slimly substance. This ectoplasm is identical to that left by a manifested ghost and is crucial in the understanding of the ghost phenomenon. Once produced he can shape it into any form that he wants and it will remain in that form until the spell ends or he reshapes it again. The attainment enables him to produce the same effects as the spell Ectoplasm. Reach used is for instant casting.
    Optional Arcanum: Prime 2
    Instead of Concentrating on a ghost the initiate with this variant is concentrating on twilight itself. This is to determine if it is twilight itself that hold the power to form ephemera into a ghost when a person dies. By studying the ebbs and flows of power he can see the flow of energy and how it interacts with the ephemera. This variant enables the initiate to use the abilities of the Sacred Geometry spell. Reaches used is for instant casting and to determine if the node sits atop of a hollow.

    Third Attainment: Travel on Death’s Trail
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 4, Death 3
    A disciple at this level of understanding can enter and leave Twilight on a whim. You can only fully understand something and if you have walked a mile in its shoes. The attainment enables the disciple the ability to enter twilight as the spell Ghost Gate except that he turns his body and items into the ephemera of twilight instead of opening a gate. This ability requires a full scene to activate. Reach used is for not needing a gate.
    Optional Arcanum: Prime 3
    The dangers of being in twilight are all too real and the smart disciple is prepared to hide if need be. He can also hide others when the need arises making him a valuable ally no matter what realm he is in. This variant allows the disciple the use of the Supernal Veil spell. Reaches used is for instant casting and Advanced Duration.

    Fourth Attainment: Strengthen Ghost
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 6, Death 4
    An adept can do some truly amazing feats and gets a greater level of understanding of the weakness of the ghost. He learns to bargain with the ghost to offer it increased power, albeit for a limited amount of time. This attainment enables the mage to strengthen the ghost’s ephemeral substance as if using the Quicken Ghost spell. This attainment costs one mana as the mage also raises the rank of the ghost by 1. He must touch the ghost and he must spend a point of mana. Reach used is for instant casting and to raise rank by 1.
    Optional Arcanum: Prime 4
    At this level the adept is capable of some horrific damage should the need arise to defend himself. This variant allows the adept to call forth Celestial Fire tainted with the Death-attuned energies of twilight. This fire burn with cold energies and takes on the appearance of a dark swirling vortex. This variant enables the Adept to call upon the Celestial Fire spell. Reach used is for instant casting.

    Fifth Attainment: Death’s True Form
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 8, Death 5
    A master’s level of understanding is unmatched and he is truly an authority in the field. At this level he can actually transform his Ephemeral Form gained by entering Twilight into an Ephemeral Entity. This Entity is none other than a ghost. This transformation is especially awe inspiring as he still has access to all the abilities, including magical abilities, while in this form. This attainment enables the master to use the abilities of the Ghost Form spell (listed below). He may spend the reaches in any manner that he sees fit as each master’s view of their ghost form is different. Be careful as there are still things that roam in that realm that are bigger and more terrifying than you. This is an instantaneous transformation and has advanced duration.
    Optional Arcanum: Prime 5
    The Master versed in prime can use this variant to enchant the very ephemera of the twilight. The subject of the spell can affect beings and objects in twilight as if he were in twilight. He can enchant an ephemeral object that is a weapon and that weapon will act as a bane to the entity so attacked with it. This variant enables the master to gain the effects of the Ephemeral Enchantment spell. Reaches used is for instant casting and to enchant a weapon.

    New Spells:

    Ephemeral Vision (Death •)
    Practice: Unveiling
    Primary Factor: Potency
    Withstand: Resolve
    Suggested Rote Skills: Empathy, Medicine, Occult.
    The mage can gaze into the very Ephemeral of all that reside in the Death-attuned Twilight. The mage must have the ability to see into Twilight albeit by magical or by actually being there. By studying an Ephemeral patterns of an entity or object the mage can gather information as to its general abilities and condition. The mage must study the pattern for a minimum of one turn after which he can gather certain information about it. For each level of potency that mage can ask one of the following questions.
    • What is the Rank of the entity?
    • How many manifestation does it have?
    • What one of its manifestations? * This question may be asked more than once.
    • What is its General level of decay?
    • Is the pattern bleeding essence?
    • What is its Ban
    • What is its Bane
    The symbols of death and decay start to appear around the entity or object. The subject may continue to study the Ephemera and continue to get more answers as long as the spell lasts.

    Splice Soul (Death •••)
    Practice: Weaving
    Primary Factor: Duration
    Withstanding: Resolve
    Suggested Rote Skills: Intimidate, Medicine, Occult.
    For the duration of this spell the mage can splice a sliver of his soul onto that of a Living soul. The target must be a sleeper and for the duration of this spell he temporarily becomes a sleepwalker with all that entails. Any breaking points due to witnessing magic and Quiescence effects the subject would normally suffer are held in abeyance until the spell’s Duration expires, only to come crashing down all at once when the spell ends.
    +1 Reach: As long as the spell’s Duration lasts, its wielder may spend one point of Mana at any time to “recharge” this effect, and the sleeper remains a sleepwalker.

    Ghost Form (Death ••••)
    Practice: Patterning
    Primary Factor: Duration
    Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Empathy, Survival.
    This spell allows the mage to transform a subject from twilight state into an Ephemeral Entity known as a ghost. For the duration of the spell the subject has the following traits determined by the mage at the time of casting. If the subject was a mage they use mana to power their abilities and they keep the Arcana ratings that they have, otherwise the newly formed ghost uses essence to power its abilities.
    Drawbacks: This power comes with a price. For every hour that the subject remains in ghost form they must roll Power + Resolve + Gnosis - 1 per previous roll. If they fail they start to become disconnected to their former self gaining the Driven condition (see below). If they ever dramatically fail this roll they gain the Amnesia condition forgetting there previous existence believing that they truly are just a ghost.

    Driven Condition: (persistent)
    Your character loses interest in everything except one task. This could be brought on by a tragedy, a mental break, or a spell gone tragically wrong. Whatever the reason your character loses interest in everything not related to fulfilling their goal.
    Game Effect: Your character loses interest in the current task at hand in favor of a new task which they will single mindedly peruse. All rolls not related to their new task is at a -2 dice.
    Resolution: Accomplish the goal.
    Beat: Character fails to fulfill an obligation due to pursuing her task.

    Ghost Traits:
    Rank: 2
    Power, Finesse, and Resistance: Average the dots in each category to arrive at the new rating for each.
    Influence: The mage assigns what the ghost has influence over, this influence is at rank 2.
    Twilight Form: The ghost has the Twilight Form manifestation.
    Manifestation: The mage may assign 2 additional manifestation (page 258 – 261 or Chronicles of Darkness page 133 - 136) of his choice on the ghost.
    Numina: The mage may assign 3 Numina (page 261 – 263 or Chronicles of Darkness page 136 - 138) of his choice to the ghost.
    Essence/Mane Max: 15
    Bans: The mage assigns a ban to the ghost.
    Banes: The mage assigns one bane to the ghost.
    All other traits listed on page 256 are calculated accordingly.
