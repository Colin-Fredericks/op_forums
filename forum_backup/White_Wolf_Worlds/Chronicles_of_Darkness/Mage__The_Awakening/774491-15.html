                    Originally posted by Kumiko View Post

                    No, it can't. It's nothing like the Masquerade which is entirely a socio-political agreement or Lunacy which is Primal Fear. Both of which can be fought against by willpower, talent, skill and information.

                    It's curse that is soul deep that the people in charge of the prison do not want changed EVER. It literally erases all evidence of any form or any kind if you are a sleeper Because the Masters of reality want it so. DEAL WITH IT and become a sleepwalker. And yes if you're tier 3 and actually equipped to hunt Awakened Mages you are in fact a sleepwalker. That level Conspiracy requires it just to enter.

                    Given Awakened Mages and MtAw operate on tier 3 at the least it makes total sense that if you want to hunt them you at least better be able to see magic and the world as it really is.
                    Except it doesn't need to be. I don't need to see you cast magic to shoot you dead in your face. I don't need to see you cast magic to know you can cast magic. I don't need to see you cast magic to track you down. I don't need to see you cast magic to Hunt you. Period. End of discussion. There is nothing you can say to counter this, it is all fact.

                    I don't need to be a Sleepwalker to know you're a mage, track you down, and kill you. There is nothing about Quiescence that prevents me from doing any of those things. It does not change evidence, it does not change fact, it does not do anything except prevent a person from remembering magic as magic. But even if a person doesn't remember magic as magic, it doesn't mean they can't figure out from circumstantial evidence, because circumstantial evidence is not changed by Quiescence, that you are able to cast magic. They don't need to be able to remember what you did to know you did something.

                    Another thing to keep in mind is that it only alters the memories of people who have memories of the magic itself to begin with. If, while you are perceiving magic, you tell someone else who can't perceive it what it is you are seeing, even after you've been made to forget it yourself the person you told will remember what you told them. What you tell people while witnessing magic is not magic itself and is thus not susceptible to Quiescence. So with this in mind, if you set up a system similar to what I proposed earlier, there is nothing about the Sleeping Curse as we know it that would interfere with that. Is it flawless? No. Is it foolproof? No, But nothing is flawless and foolproof, not even magic. Which seems to be something you need to learn.

                    So as I've said, no, you do not need to be a Sleepwalker to hunt a mage. There are so many ways to off an unsuspecting mage it isn't even funny. Without knowing it's coming and time to prepare, mages are nothing but human, and just as easy to take out as any Hunter.
