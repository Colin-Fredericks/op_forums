        And I just realized that I kinda-sorta posted this in the wrong thread, so I'm reposting it here.

        I decided to try converting the Sphinxes to 2nd edition to see what would happen. My first observation is that the Sphinx Attainments are a lot wordier than the Mage 2nd Attainments. There are a lot of specifics, exceptions, and hedge cases that make it more complicated than it needs to be, and it really makes me appreciate Mage 2nd's standardized approach. Beyond that, it seems my suspicions that Fixed Reach was the best possible design decision was correct; it's much easier to choose Attainments when you can add or remove Reach to move spells around. I also added some commentary about my thought process in case it'd be useful.

        Sphinxes



        First Attainment: Surface Reading
        Prerequisites: Initiation

        The Sphinx becomes attuned to the patterns that emerge within the Fallen World. This Attainment mimics the effects of Quantum Flux (p. 135). However, the Sphinx can only "aim" actions meant to solve puzzles, riddles, conundrums, or similar problems. This Attainment spends one Reach for instant activation.

        Optional: Mind 1

        After examining a subject for one scene, the Sphinx may learn information about the subject's thoughts. This functions as Mental Scan (p. 159). This Attainment's reach is spent on sensory range.
        These were easy choices. Quantum Flux allows a Mage to quickly gain a short burst of contextual knowledge relevant a situation and accordingly, while Mental Scan effectively allows a Mage to read someone's body language in order to learn more about them. One focuses on the instinctual knowledge of the Sphinx Legacy, while the other focuses on their careful study of meaning. I find it strikes a nice balance. Allowing Sphinxes to "aim" actions related to riddles and gain Quantum Flux's benefit to solve riddles is a reference to the +3 bonus they got on Mental Skill rolls.

        Second Attainment: Hidden Meaning
        Prerequisites: Fate 2, Investigation 2

        The Sphinx becomes aware of the connections between people and objects. This Attainment replicates the effects of Interconnections (p. 134). Its Reach is spent on Advanced Duration and instant activation.

        Optional: Mind 2

        When using the optional effect of her first Attainment, the Sphinx also learns information revealed by Know Nature (p. 159). Reach is spent to reveal Aspirations and Obsessions and to use the Attainment at sensory range.
        Interconnections was part of the Sphinx's original Attainments, so it was an obvious choice. Thematically, it broadens the scope of the Quantum Flux effect and allows Sphinxes to make broader connections between an immediate context and the rest of the world. Likewise, Know Nature builds upon the effects of Mental Scan. I hesitated about whether or not Know Nature and Mental Scan should be combined effects, but it was a much more elegant solution than keeping them separate.

        Third Attainment: The Leading Answer
        Prerequisites: Fate 3, Investigation 3

        The Sphinx may activate this Attainment in order to grant herself the benefits of Exceptional Luck (p. 135). The Sphinx may only use this Attainment on herself and may only grant herself boons. This Attainment's Reach is spent on instant activation and Advanced Duration.

        Optional: Mind 3

        After studying someone with the optional effects of the firs two Attainments, the Sphinx gains the benefits of First Impressions (p. 160) towards the subject of her observations. This Attainment's Reach is spent on sensory activation and Advanced Duration. Once its duration expires, the Sphinx must examine her subject once more in order to update her profile and reactivate the Attainment.
        Exceptional Luck was part of the Sphinx Attainments, but my initial choice for this was actually Reading the Outmost Eddies. I went with Exceptional Luck because it was simpler.

        First Impression was also part of the original Sphinx Attainments, but I mostly kept it because of how it builds upon Know Nature and Mental Scan. You study someone to learn more about them, and then you use that to approach them later on.

        Fourth Attainment: Truth Without Knowledge
        Prerequisites: Fate 4, Investigation 4

        When entering an unexpected situation, the Sphinx may activate this Attainment to gain the benefits of Fools Rush In (p. 136), including its first Reach effect. In addition, the Sphinx may always activate this Attainment to gain extra dice for extended rolls to solve a puzzle, navigate a maze, conduct research, or deal with similarly difficult problems. Reach is spent on instant activation, Advanced Duration, and the spell's first Reach effect.

        Optional: Mind 4

        The Sphinx may gain the effects of Universal Language (p. 162). Reach is spent on instant activation and Advanced Duration.
        Universal Language was another of the Sphinx's original Attainments, it was significant enough to the Legacy's themes that I wanted to keep it. I just needed to find a Fate spell to go along with it.

        Fools Rush In is actually quite weak for an Attainment, but when you apply its first Reach effect to extended rolls appropriate to the Sphinxes (which Quantum Flux can't do), it becomes just good enough. Besides, it's thematically appropriate for a Legacy like the Sphinxes; you instantly read a situation and figure out how to deal with it, even if you knew nothing about it beforehand.

        Fifth Attainment: Writing The Pattern
        Prerequisites: Fate 5, Investigation 5

        The Sphinx contemplates the world for one scene, after which she activates this Attainment to replicate the effects of the spell Miracle (p. 140). This Attainment's Reach is spent on Advanced Duration.

        Optional: Mind 5

        The Sphinx may activate this Attainment in order to gain the benefits of Incognito Presence (p. 160), including its Reach effect. Reach is spent on instant activation, Advanced Duration, Advanced Potency to add +1 to Clash of Wills rolls, and the spell's Reach effect.
        Chaos Mastery and Miracle where the only Fate spells that seemed thematically appropriate for the fifth Attainment. Chaos Mastery was too complicated, so I picked Miracle.

        Using Incognito Presence for the Sphinx's weird stealth effect is something I knew I wanted to do before I even got started. I thought I couldn't neatly pull it off because I couldn't find the reference to Advanced Potency buffing Clash of Wills rolls in the Spell Factors section, but then I found it in the Clash of Wills section.

        Anyway, Miracle allows you to manipulate the pattern, while Incognito Presence allows you to hide inside it.

        Overall, working with Fixed Reach instead of against it made things much, much easier. Moving spells to higher Attainments made it a lot easier to capture the Sphinxes' core themes. It's also much easier if you focus on the original Attainments' narrative functions and try for similar effects instead of looking for a 1:1 conversion.

            I'm working on the House of Ariadne and I have sort of hit a stumbling block. Input would be greatly appreciated.
            DOCTRINE
            Prerequisites: Time 2, Resolve 3, and one of the following skills at 2 dots or higher: Investigation, Survival, Streetwise, Socialize

            Initiation: Prospective Metropolitans must demonstrate patience by exploring their city and discovering a piece of their city’s secret history to share with their local House.
            Theory: The House of Ariadne understands that a city’s culture -- special points of pride or contempt, its modes of speaking and thinking and doing -- will outlast even its buildings. A city’s subtle, deathless power shapes mortal lives. A city is a god. It does not rule the concrete, brick, glass and wiring or the traffic, the businesses and the beggars. The City-god is them, everything, at once. Metropolitans believe all the many destinies, resonances and interconnections of Fate actually do form a diffuse entity with which they can commune and attempt to understand. Their methods provide indirect but definite proof of the City-god’s reality.
            Atypical of Acanthus is the dour demeanor and great patience of the House of the Ariadne. It is one of their key defining traits and it is emphasized several times in their write-up in Legacies: the Sublime, so I thought it was important. In order to reflect that, I chose to make uncommon Resolve part of the prerequisite for joining the Legacy in addition to the Arcane and mundane knowledge.

            MAGIC
            Ruling Arcanum: Time

            Yantras: a place or time of great import to the city or its history (+1) finding a lost item meaningful to a community in the city (+2); teaching about the city’s culture and history (+1); using self-annotated maps of neighborhoods or blueprints of buildings relevant to the casting of the spell (+2); getting lost and navigating to a familiar area (+1); photos and souvenirs from the city relevant to the casting of the spell (+1)

            Oblations: A walk in the city to find something you never saw before; exploring the subway tunnels; watching a sunrise or sunset from a penthouse apartment; visiting a museum or other tourist attraction; attending a party with a “list;” serving food at a homeless shelter; sleeping on a park bench.

            ATTAINMENTS
            The House’s geomancy requires mastery of divination, but instead of focusing on a small item like a crystal or a pack of cards, Metropolitans use the expanse of the city to divine the past, present and future. The mage who wishes to follow Ariadne’s thread must study Time and Fate. Much of the Metropolitans’ powers come from walking a self-made Knot in the city. After seeing Ariadne’s thread, the mage must attune herself to the city. A member of the House of Ariadne can attune herself to any city she is in. She will spend at least six hours walking through the city, following Ariadne’s thread. During this time, she gets to know the city as the city itself gets a feel for her. The House of Ariadne never takes the direct route.
            The trickiest part here is deciding what could be a Yantra vs what could be an Oblation. For the House of Ariadne they are practically interchangeable.

            First: Urban Communion
            Prerequisites: Initiation
            Once attuned to the city, she is its mother, sister, daughter, all at once. The Metropolitan spends one scene in communion with her city by whatever means she deems appropriate: a morning jog; feeding the pigeons in the park; reading the morning news in a random coffee shop; tagging graffiti in an alley; people-watching from a bus stop bench. For one day, the mage finds it exceedingly easy to get anywhere she wants to go in her city. This attainment works similarly to the spell “Green Light / Red Light”: doors open for the Metropolitan, taxis and elevators arrive in the knick of time, and she never catches a red light or construction detour, etc. Reach is used for Advanced Duration.

            Optional: Fate 1 If the mage spends a scene wandering the city streets, taking in the sights and partaking of a slice of the city life, the City will “speak” to her and give her guidance on how to accomplish her stated goal. This attainment works similarly to the Fate 1 spell “Serendipity” with +1 Reach.
            I’ll admit that I’m still wrapping my head around the way Reach is used and the way Duration works. This is my understanding: “Green Light / Red Light” has a primary factor of Duration, meaning it normally lasts for turns. By adding +1 Reach the Duration moves to the Prolonged chart, beginning at One Scene. Since this Attainment requires no roll, it gets two successes (2 dots in Time = 2 successes), even if it suffers the -2 penalty for making it last One Day per the chart -- still 2 successes.
            Is that how it works? I really need help here.
            As for the choice of the attainment, I am getting real Jack Hawksmoor vibe from the House of Ariadne (for more information on that character, read Warren Ellis’ run on Stormwatch. In that vein, I figured it would only be natural for the Metropolitans to have a sort of symbiotic relationship with the city. A keeper’s and guardians of the city, the Metropolitans are kept and guarded by the city in turn.

            I’ll have some more attainments for them once I understand Reach and Temporal Sympathy and Withstanding better. I’m not sure how those things apply to Attainments, if at all, and the Eleventh Question write-up doesn’t seem to help me.

                        Originally posted by Troy View Post
                        I’ll admit that I’m still wrapping my head around the way Reach is used and the way Duration works. This is my understanding: “Green Light / Red Light” has a primary factor of Duration, meaning it normally lasts for turns. By adding +1 Reach the Duration moves to the Prolonged chart, beginning at One Scene. Since this Attainment requires no roll, it gets two successes (2 dots in Time = 2 successes), even if it suffers the -2 penalty for making it last One Day per the chart -- still 2 successes.
                        Is that how it works? I really need help here.

                        No, it's not, you're smooshing several things together.

                        First, the total successes don't matter (except for Exceptional Successes). Only Potency, Duration, Scale, and Range matter. Legacy Attainments don't roll to activate, they just automatically do. I honestly don't know why the "number of successes" line is in the Attainments section.

                        Let's say you have the following spell, 2 dots in Fate, and Gnosis 2:

                        Fate **

                        Green Light / Red Light
                        Practice:                         Ruling
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Empathy, Science, Subterfuge
                        Make a light green or red for the duration.

                        Normally it would have a base duration of 2 turns (for your two dots in Fate), and 1 in every other factor (Potency, Area, &c). You would have a base dicepool of 4 (Fate dots + Gnosis) and 1 free Reach. If you wanted to increase the duration even more you could take -2 penalties to further increase the duration, or you could increase one of the other factors by one step for every -2. You can also use Reach to make the spell instant cast (rather than ritual time) or use the Advanced spell factors table for a particular factor (Potency, Duration, Number of Subjects, Size, Area of Effect, and making it Sensory instead of touch each require an individual Reach).

                        But that's for a regular spell, what about an Attainment?

                        Attainments have fixed characteristics. Let's say your Attainment is the second Attainment and it reproduces GL/RL with Reach spent on Advanced Duration and your Fate is still 2 dots. Your Attainment GL/RL (AGLRL) can only be cast as a ritual because the Reach wasn't assigned to making it Instant cast, so it'll take 1 scene (i.e. 1 hour) to cast; but it does have Advanced Duration! But unlike a normal spell the factors are automatically assigned: your Arcanum dots improve the Primary Spell Factor, and secondary factors are improved by Arcanum/2 (rounded down).

                        So AGLRL with the user at Fate 2 would have a duration of One Day (Duration is the Primary Factor and is Advanced), Potency 2 (base of 1 + 2/2), and Scale 2 (again, base of 1 + 2/2).

                        If you used AGLRL with Fate 3, then the duration would be One Week, but Potency and other factors would remain 2 (base of 1 + 3/2 rounded down). You also would not gain any additional Reach, because Reach is fixed to the Dot rating of the Attainment with regards to the Dot rating of the spell, not the Dot rating of the user.


                        If instead you had a Forces/Time Legacy attainment based on Turn Momentum with the Time 1 conjunctional then the secondary factors (in this case Duration and the Scales) would be based on the lower of Time dots or Forces dots divided by 2, instead of on Forces.




                        [EDIT]I'm actually iffy on if each type of Scale is separate or Scale is one thing. So it might be one Reach for Scale -> Advanced Scale and each step up on Scale steps up every type of scale.

                                **Comment by Dave (Developer) on page 7**

                                [Fan 2ED] Legacy: Tremere Lich
                                Path: Moros
                                Order: The Tremere have their own Nameless Order

                                The Tremere are an iconic Mage antagonist.
                                I am elated to see them return for 2nd edition with all the temptation Reaping brings.
                                Inuring spells make spells more flexible for stealing souls and make it less critical to make Attainments for it.
                                The need to keep using up souls balances the attainments power.


                                Legacy Attainments


                                1st Eternal Need
                                Prerequisites: Initiation

                                Upon joining the Legacy the Mage stops aging.
                                His soul also slowly decays.
                                After a year the soul has decayed completely and the Mage becomes Soulless (P.318).
                                If the Mage gains the Thrall Condition, he ages the years he has been in the Tremere Legacy instantly.
                                This may be fatal if enough years have past.
                                The mage must therefore replace his soul at least once a year.
                                The Attainment gives no benefit beyond this.


                                2nd The Mirror of the Soul
                                Prerequisites: Death 2, Subterfuge 2

                                The Tremere's soul appears like a normal Awakened soul without a Legacy when examined,
                                for example with the "Soul Marks" spell.
                                This keeps suspicious Mages from discovering that the Tremere has been eating souls.
                                Hiding the 4th and 5th Attainment in this manner provokes a Clash of Wills.
                                The Tremere's Soulstones do not gain this protection.
                                Upon gaining this Attainment the Tremere's soul erodes faster, needing a replacement every 6 months.
                                If the Tremere attaches an Awakened soul to himself it will take twice as long to erode.


                                3rd Stolen Power
                                Prerequisites: Death 3, Occult 3

                                If the Tremere attaches an Awakened soul to himself, he gains Dots in one of the Arcana the owner of this soul knew.
                                The new Arcana cannot have more dots than the Tremere has dots in the Death Arcana.
                                The new Arcana does not stack.
                                When the Soul has decayed, the new Arcana is lost.


                                4th Vessel for the Soul
                                Prerequisites: Death 4

                                As the spell "Soul Jar" (P.129). Three Reach is spent on both Reach options listed in the spell.
                                Attached Sleeper souls now decay at a Rate of 1 per month.
                                Awakened souls take 6 months.
                                Joining a Tremere House (P.238) May change this Attainment.


                                5th Tithe
                                Prerequisites: Death 5

                                As the spell "Sever the Awakened Soul" (Death 5) P.133
                                Attached Sleeper souls are now consumed at a Rate of 1 per month.
                                Awakened souls take 6 months.
                                Joining a Tremere House (P.238) May change this Attainment.
