    [2E] Multinational Status in Order

    01-06-2017, 08:30 AM
    To my London/globetrotting game about Earth's Chakras join player with Moros Libertine character of Treasure Hunter that sell his found Artifacts and Grimoires from Temples of Time Before to highest bidder, often to Free Council Lorehouses. As he jumps around the world and sell items to members of many Consilia - it would be good if his Status ( Free Council ) 3 work in any of that locations. And I have many other characters that could benefit from shared Status, beside their home Consilium.

    I know that Mysterium has Egregore 2 Merit that let members to use full Status in any Order Caucus in the world. But what about rest of Orders? Maybe some special Merit that let you use one dot lower Status than your home Consilium?

          If you do that you're removing one of the more valuable pieces of Egregore from the merit. The Mysterium are explicitly the ambassadors between the Orders internationally because of the Egregore. That said, inter-Order actions between Caucuses can probably be handled by using your Status in your home Caucus to requisition some sort of symbol of status that can be presented to another Caucus to vouch for your character and if they practice the Rite of Emeritus they'll respect you for your Status in your home town.

          If you're from a SL Caucus and you want to get in good with an Arrow Caucus in another country then you'd go to your Mysterium buddy for a voucher and in the other country your first stop would be the Mysterium Caucus so they could verify you via the Egregore and they would introduce you to the Arrow Caucus since the Arrows know their local Mysterium better than they know you.                               First draft of custom Merit for topic problem.

                              Well Travelled (• to ••)
                              Prerequisites: Awakened, Status ( Order ) 2
                              Effect: Your character has traveled to many Caucuses of his Order and recognized in all of them. Or she made very important actions for it, that her inter-Order fame is proceeding her. One way or another, she is known in locations outside her home Consilium. Her effective rating is one level higher than normally would be for each dot of this Merit. So on Well Travelled (•) she can used her Status in Order as in Caucus of her home Consilium as normal, will have -1 effective level if it is a member of the same Convocation, and -2 if it is unrelated. Well Travelled (••) gives normal usage of Status everywhere in your home Convocation and only -1 if Caucus is unrelated.
