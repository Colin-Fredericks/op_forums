                                                    Mechanically it could be represented in multiple ways:
                                                    a) Penalty from a Tilt or Condition "Panic" or "Losing my ground" or something, causing spell roll to fail
                                                    b) "Just" a penalty causing spell to fail
                                                    c) Tilt or Condition that allows you to fail a roll, including spellcasting one, to gain a beat. Or straight out tells you that you lost it.
                                                    While it boils to the same thing, translating it to a pre-estabilished mechanic makes interacting with it using other pre-estabilished mechanics easier and allows for interesting combinations.
                                                    Which one you use also tells a lot about your Chronicle.
