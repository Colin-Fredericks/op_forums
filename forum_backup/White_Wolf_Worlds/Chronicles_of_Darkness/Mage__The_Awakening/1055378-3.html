                    A beginner mage runs into a pack of werewolves, or a coterie of vampires, a group of hunters...

                    The mage is just starting off and has Gnosis 1 and Mind 3, giving him a pool of 4 dice. However, he does have a rote in psychic domination. Lets just say that his skill is 4 plus he has a magical tool giving him another dice, so he gets 5 bonus dice for having his Yantas, the maximum. So he has 9 dice. He will try to dominate the group and assumes he must overcome 3 resolve to get most of them so he subtracts 6 dice to get 3 Potency. He can still roll 3 dice which is still a pretty good chance of success.

                    Next is reach, since it is a rote he gets 4 free reach.
                    He spends 2 to get the group to do anything he wants as per the spell
                    he spends 1 to get the spell instant rather than have to wait 3 hours
                    he spends 1 to cast at sensory range
                    he spends 1 to affect 5 people, rather than 1
                    he spends 1 to extend duration to a scene

                                Originally posted by Fortunatus View Post
                                So, if I cast a spell that causes a gout of flame to rush through a large house, not knowing how many people are in said house, and therefore not using Scale to affect a discrete number of people, but using Duration to make sure that those affected burn for the next few rounds, I must have that gout of flame remain raging throughout the entire house?
                                If you're doing that, you're pretty much invariably using more Reach to add an Environmental Tilt to the effect, because otherwise you're just getting an AoE fireball that recurs once per casting interval.

                                Spells that create areas of effect have persistent effects tied to that area. Spells that affect things that aren't themselves areas treat Area Scale like adding splash to the spell's effect, but don't cause extended Duration to make a persistent effect-field; Alter Direction works on anything coming into its area of effect because it's altering a quality within the area — the effect doesn't persist outside its bounds, which cannot be said for, say, turning every person in a small house into a frog without Dynamics (which is the difference between "everyone in this house is a frog until the spell ends" and "anyone in this house is a frog until the spell ends.")
