    The Silent Bulwark, an Acanthus Legacy of mage vanquishers (Prime / Fate)

    12-16-2025, 06:47 PM
    Origins

    “One should not confuse striking at evil and doing good, lest good become the act of striking.”
    -David Verburg

    Nickname: Nullifiers
    Parentage: Acanthus, Adamantine Arrow, Guardians of the Veil

    Background:     The origin of the nullifiers is subject to a lot of speculation, the few fragments of information known were retrieved from their ancient workshops around the world. A common aspect of all these sites is that they all have some method to empower those who take an oath to combat an abuse of magic. It is said undertaking such an oath reflects the profound commitment of a nullifier to their mission, and those that fail to complete them are said to return as ghost mages, driven to fulfill them.

    In Consilia where Liches and ghost mages abound, the ghosts of past members are known to emerge from the Underworld. Bearing masks on them, they are driven to drag these foes down to the Great Below. Some have suggested they originate from a forgotten Dominion, although conclusive evidence has yet to be found. The nullifiers highly prize these spectre's Deathmasks, believing they will help uncover a pathway to a lost workshop and whatever secrets of their history it holds, as well as the Kerberos that oversees it.

    When the nullifiers joined the nascent Diamond Orders, some of their numbers found their way to the Tremere Order. Seeking novel ways to defeat corrupted mages, they would turn their abilities towards the hunt for powerful Supernal materials, deeply committed to the Order's quest to push the boundaries of humanity's magical power. Despite these old masters having long lost the marks of the Legacy, the shame they represent and arcane secrets they hold make them a high priority target for modern nullifiers.

    In recent times, some nullifiers have become members of the Bellerophon Group, leveraging their expertise to capture the Rapt, protecting those nearby during the rehabilitation process and visiting Consilia to assess potential ones in hiding. Rumor has it that some have become so hardened by their experiences that they have become ideological Banishers, vanishing those they deem at high risk of Rapture.

    Appearances: Deeply fascinated by protective magics, nullifiers often wear lucky charms and talismans. They are also known for purchasing these whenever they can, everything from worry dolls, to omamori, agimats and more, even buying them as souvenirs or gifts. Supernatural ones are especially prized, with nullifiers usually going out of their way to procure new magical defenses, as well as exotic materials to craft their tools with.

    Nullifiers are known for applying their research of protective magic to their Sanctum's defenses, which manifest in many different ways, such as hanging dreamcatchers above the beds, arranging objects according to feng shui, engraving hexafoils in certain areas and more. Because of this, these areas normally have a strong protective Resonance. Esoteric components are commonly found near their workbenches, organized so they may quickly procure the Bane of any kind of ephemeral entities an enemy mage can recruit.

    Given the inherent danger of their work, nullifiers train extensively for combat, honing their techniques to be ready to face the most skilled opponents.

    Whenever attending formal Consilium events, the nullifier's traditional attire is deceptively simple, consisting of a charged thaumium talisman with their oath inscribed upon it, a symbol of their pragmatism and their constant commitment to uphold their duty.

    Doctrine

    "Use only that which works, and take it from any place you can find it."
    -Bruce Lee

    Prerequisites: Prime 2, Esoteric Armory ●

    Initiation: The prospective member must modify a weapon utilizing their Occult knowledge, making it into a tool that disrupts mages. A common choice is to make it hinder either an enemy's ability to aim spells or deliver close ranged magical attacks, although mentors prize novel ideas and will provide guidance in developing them.

    Although these upgrades do not last long, they help teach the initiate to think outside the box, relying on more than Supernal power to defeat their foes.

    When the modification is completed, the candidate swears an oath to combat the abuses of magic, which completes the initiation.

    Organization: Working closely with their Order's crafters, nullifiers tend to gather into workshops, developing new spells and tools to more effectively combat dangerous Awakened. Some workshops specialize in a particular type of foe, such as the Tremere or Scelestus, creating custom equipment designed to defeat them.

    In Consilia where they have a stronger presence, nullifiers tend to push towards fortifying their Order's emergency arsenal, so they will be prepared in case the worst comes to pass.

    Because of the potential danger a rogue nullifier poses, new members are chosen with great care. Mentors and students tend to have a close bond, working together to defend the Consilium, keeping an ear to the ground for potential threats, often as agents of the Lex Magicka. The Orders keep a close watch on nullifiers, ever wary of them falling down the path of the Banisher, or the Legacy falling into the wrong hands.

    Nullifiers are especially keen at finding Sleepwalkers in law enforcement, recruiting their aid to investigate and combat rogue Awakened, supplying them with training and supernatural equipment. In cases where a particularly promising ally reveals themselves, some nullifiers use their mastery of Prime to make them into permanent Sleepwalkers, concealing their spellwork with Veilings.

    Such prospects are inherently dangerous, as not only do they thread a fine line with the Lex Magicka, but there is an ever present risk of creating a witch hunter. Some nullifiers even believe confronting the supernatural in such a way, the good and the bad, can guide their allies towards an Awakening driven by the symbols of protection, while more paranoid mages instead fear it might lead them to a Harrowing Awakening.

    Theory: Whenever the abuse of magic brings suffering and horror to reality, those with the will to act have the responsibility to do so.

    The Awakened wield some of the most diverse and flexible abilities imaginable, in order to defeat them, one must be decisive. When attacking, one must have solid, reliable defenses in place, allowing one to focus on ending the fight quickly.

    In order to create the strongest defenses, one must draw on knowledge of defensive magics from many sources, even those beyond the Supernal. Thus protected, your foe will be unable to stop you as you press the attack.

    When attacking an enemy mage, the best warriors are able to disrupt their abilities and break their flow. One must be vigilant for any materials or knowledge that can be wielded for that purpose, and be meticulous in transforming them into useful equipment. In this way when battle comes, one will be able to strike with power and precision.

    Be ready to face dangerous mages at a moment’s notice, be diligent in your preparations, so when the time comes, you will be able to protect those who cannot protect themselves.

    Magic

    “Attack is the secret of defense; defense is the planning of an attack."
    -Sun Tzu

    Ruling Arcanum: Prime
    Yantras: Weapons, shields, body armor, mechanical traps or explosives (+1, +2 if they incorporate non-Supernal powers), handcuffs, ropes or similar restraining tools (+1, +2 if they incorporate non-Supernal powers), the subject's Bane (+2) , talismans, wards, charms or symbols of protection (+1 for mundane ones, +2 for those made with the Occult skill, +3 if incorporating non-Supernal powers), abjured subject (+2), casting within an area warded through abjuration against the subject (+2), esoteric armory, occult library or similar locations dedicated to hunting the supernatural (+2), forge, workshop or similar locations used for crafting weapons and armor (+1).

    Oblations: Crafting an occult item, fortifying an area, investigating a mage, repairing, maintaining, customizing or upgrading weapons and armor; drilling or training in combat techniques, conducting a binding or warding abjuration, researching a mage’s allied ephemeral entities or a Supernal being, performing an apotropaic ritual.

    Attainments

    First: Watchman's Sight
    Prerequisites: Initiation

    The nullifier sharpens their senses, allowing them to perceive enemy mages and their ephemeral allies moving about in different layers of reality. After a scene of crystal gazing, or guiding a subject through the process, their senses expand to allow them to perceive all layers of Twilight at once. However, this does not confer to them knowledge on the types of beings they are observing, requiring additional abilities, spellwork or Occult knowledge to uncover it.

    This is an Unveiling of Prime, with Reach assigned to advanced duration.

    Optional: Fate 1

    Ever vigilant to their surroundings, the nullifier is able to see through any illusions and disguises with consummate ease. After a scene chanting in High Speech while the subject looks through a hag stone, they develop a keen sense for perceiving hidden truths.

    Mundane illusions, like mirages or Pepper's Ghosts, become instantly apparent and are seen through by their senses. Disguises are similarly affected. Supernatural concealments, such as cognition filters, invisibility and shapeshifting, provoke a Clash of Wills to allow them to discern the truth.

    Lastly, the subject is aware of the location of anything or anyone deliberately hidden within the area of effect, but has no knowledge of what they are detecting. Supernaturally concealed targets cause a Clash of Wills. Because of this, it is said it's nearly impossible to sneak anything past a nullifier, a few even claiming the nullifiers never sleep.

    This is a duration primary Unveiling of Fate and Prime. It allows the subject's senses to detect anything intended to keep something from being noticed, or attempts to disguise something, and reveals it for what it is.

    Second: Warden's Mantle
    Prerequisites: Prime 2, Esoteric Armory ●●

    Nullifiers are well versed in how to protect themselves from magical senses, denying their foes avenues of remote attacks and the ability to track their moves. After spending a scene drawing a magic circle in salt around themselves, or doing it around the subject, the nullifier creates a barrier around them against supernatural detection. Since it prevents foes from learning what magical equipment they carry or what preparations they have made, facing a nullifier in combat is always an intimidating prospect.

    This replicates Supernal Veil, with Reach assigned to advanced duration.

    Optional: Fate 2

    The nullifier's defensive magic goes beyond mere protection from dangerous spells, they can even turn an enemy's own spells against them.

    After the subject spends a scene touching a reflective surface, while the nullifier chants in High Speech and spends a point of mana, the subject is covered in an invisible barrier of reflective magical scales.

    Whenever they are hit by a touch or aimed spell, the nullifier may undergo a Clash of Will. Upon success, the spell is sent back at the caster, leaving the subject unscathed. The nullifier makes an aimed spell attack at the caster, representing how accurately the barrier can reflect the arcane energies.

    Sensory or sympathetic spells cannot be deflected, as they do not move towards the target, instead appearing on them. Area of Effect spells are similarly not affected, since the spell's energies are not moving towards a target.

    With conjunctional Fate 2, the barrier is also given [Potency] charges. A charge can be reflexively spent so that the barrier to refract spells based on the subject's intent. Whenever they redirect a spell that turn, the subject may attempt to hit any other target within range, requiring an aimed spell attack as before. As long as the spell’s duration lasts, the subject may spend a point of mana to fully replenish these charges.

    This is a duration primary Shielding of Prime, with Reach assigned to advanced duration. Conjunctional Fate 2 allows to refract spells, with a point of mana paying for the effect.

    Third: Sigil of Vigilance
    Prerequisites: Prime 3, Esoteric Armory ●●●

    The nullifier is ever watchful of the flow of Supernal magic around them, constantly assessing potential threats, always ready to react to sudden spells.

    After spending a scene using mana to draw a High Speech rune upon an Awakened subject, the nullifier enhances their ability to perceive Imagos. They can always see them without the need of Mage Sight, including those of spells being cast, with any attempts to hide them causing a Clash of Wills.

    In addition, being able to perceive Imagos with greater clarity allows them to target their weaknesses more easily, granting the rote property to counterspell rolls. Nullifiers with this Attainment tend to develop a reputation for never being caught unawares, always ready to protect their cabal from a lethal spell or prevent a suspect from teleporting away.

    Optional: Fate 3

    A seasoned nullifier knows that the most dangerous mages are those able to tactically combine mundane combat techniques with Awakened magic, and few opponents are more effective against mages than heavily armed Sleepers.

    The nullifier bends fortune into a defensive array, protecting them from all sources of death or harm that do not meet all of [Potency] conditions of their choosing (such as being attacked by a siderite weapon forged under a full moon, the presence of the seventh son of a seventh son, throwing money at them first to pay a fee for the attack, have their explicit permission to attack them, etc) , with supernatural ones provoking a Clash of Wills. Because this is normally hidden by the Warden’s Mantle, those not in the know think the nullifier and their allies enjoy an uncanny amount of lucky coincidences.

    This is a duration primary Shielding of Fate, with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration.

    Fourth: Armor of Stillness
    Prerequisites: Prime 4, Esoteric Armory ●●●●

    The nullifier encases a subject in a potent barrier of Supernal stasis, rendering them immune to all but the mightiest spells.It is common for them to apply it to protect their allies, as well as upon any dangerous magical items, keeping them from being spirited away.

    With conjunctional Fate 2, the barrier is constructed to not interfere with the nullifier's other Attainments.

    This replicates Wards & Signs with Reach assigned to instant use, advanced duration and changing the primary factor.

    Optional: Fate 4

    Reliability lies at the very core of the nullifier's methods. Whenever a critical moment is upon them, such as a deadly ambush or when a brief window of opportunity presents itself, they are able to seize the moment, allowing them and their allies to pull through.

    This replicates Exceptional Luck, with Reach assigned to reflexive use and sensory range, with conjunctional Prime 2 allowing spells to be affected as well. Conditional duration is also activated, with the condition that the Attainment ceases after it has been active for a minute.

    A point of mana in total is used to pay for both reflexive use and Conditional Duration, per Legacy rules.

    Fifth: Edict of Silence
    Prerequisites: Prime 5, Fate 2, Esoteric Armory ●●●●●

    A nullifier at the pinnacle of their craft is one of the most fearsome foes an Awakened can face, for they are able to quell all magic around them, while their own power remains undiminished. Enchanted items become mundane, transformations are reversed, Artifacts cease to function and a profound silence can be felt through the Peripheral Sight. While the effect only lasts for a few precious moments, it is often more than enough for them to seize victory, and for even the most hubristic mages to know fear once more.

    This replicates Supernal Dispellation with Reach assigned to instant use, advanced scale and with a point of mana paying for Conditional Duration. The condition is for the Attainment to cease after it has been active for a minute.

    With conjunctional Fate 1, they can selectively choose which spells to suppress.

    Optional: Fate 5

    The greatest nullifiers are not only able to keep others safe from the magics of their foes, but also from their own, allowing them to wield their full might without having to worry about hitting bystanders.

    When combined with the Armor of Stillness, the nullifier can use it to lock down a subject from being affected by any magic without their permission.

    Furthermore, they can protect their allies from ambient magics and from any attempts to subvert their free will.

    This replicates Warding Gesture with Reach assigned to instant use, advanced duration, as well as AoE protection. With conjunctional Prime 2, the nullifier can selectively exclude the subject from their Attainments and spells.

    Tools of The Nullifiers
    The nullifiers tend to build an eclectic collection of magical items over their careers. Below are some examples of items that may be found in their armory, as well as some of the unique spells they use to fight the Awakened.

    Token: The Burning Heart (•••••)

    Description: A heavy revolver made of a lustrous dark metal, intricate golden engravings are etched all over it and its handle is made of manzanita wood.

    Effect: Any bullets it fires are wreathed in flame. On a single attack success, it incinerates the target in flames as large as a bonfire (+2) that burn hot enough to melt metal (+3), in addition to causing the Burning tilt. Armor can mitigate the damage from the bullets normally, but the fire is only affected if it has some flame-resistant properties.

    Drawback: Firing the last bullet in the cylinder causes some backfire to hit the user, dealing one point of lethal damage.

    Catch: Having one's passion run hot is exhausting. A scene after activating the Burning Heart, the user suffers the Lethargic condition. If they already have the condition, the penalty increases by one and they must roll immediately to stay awake.

    File Notes: Some mages like to increase their healing rates to such high levels that they instantly regenerate from all but the most grievous wounds, this gun counters that nicely. It packs all the power of a flamethrower, but is much more portable and concealable. Just like with a flamethrower, having someone versed in Forces is handy to control any potential blaze, doubly so if you need to capture your opponent alive. A solid tool for difficult fights, but keep in mind your reload discipline or you will end up worse for wear.

    Fetish: Homeward Satchel (•••)

    Description: A leather satchel bearing symbols of protection normally used to guard households, such as images of a komainu or a closure depicting a Celtic shield knot.

    Effect: When activated, the user can draw an item of up to size 3 from any place of their choice that they consider home (their apartment, Sanctum, childhood home, it must have at least a Medium sympathetic connection). Alternatively, any item of up to size 3 can be placed in the satchel and be sent to the home of the user's choice, being stored right where they would have wanted it to be.

    File Notes: Normally our Sanctums tend to attract spirits of protection. If they are strong enough, we cut a deal. They help keep our cabal and our belongings safe, and we let them feed off the protection Resonance we generate on the job. Then it's just a matter of finding the right crafter.

    The satchel gives you access to all your Sanctum's resources anytime and anywhere, making you much more effective on the field. It's also very helpful when collecting evidence. The best part is, it's not a liability if stolen, since it will connect to the holder's home instead.

    Memento: The Moment of Silence

    Key: Stillness

    Description: A mechanical wristwatch with a cracked glass, the time permanently stopped at 7:19 PM. The watch has multiple complications added, all still functioning perfectly.

    Effect: Whenever the chronograph is activated, the area around it is affected by the Silence environmental tilt, making it so no sound exists within it. In addition, everyone within it also gains the Mute tilt for the duration, or until they leave the area. The effect ceases once the chronograph is stopped.

    File Notes: A great way to stop mages from using High Speech. If an enemy doesn't have a telepathic hub or know how to communicate with signs, then it really throws them off. Keep in mind that it will also affect your team.

    With judicious use, it can be a great asset for ambushes, assassinations, divide and conquer tactics and the like. Just be sure to remember that once people notice everything has fallen dead silent, it will put them on edge.

    Phurba's Tether (Prime ••)
    Practice: Ruling
    Primary Factor: Duration
    Withstand: N/A
    Suggested Rote Skills: Brawl, Firearms, Weaponry

    A spell the nagas secretly taught the Arrows, as a means to help them bind evil. When the subject utilizes a piercing weapon, such as a knife, bow, crossbow, nail gun or stake, it reveals to them how best to strike in order to restrain their opponents, allowing them to inflict the Pinned tilt on a hit.

    +1 Reach: A hit also inflicts the Stunned tilt.

    +1 Reach: Whenever attempting to stake a vampire, reduce by [Potency] the amount of damage needed to pierce their heart.

    Magician's Scourge (Prime ••••)
    Practice: Patterning
    Primary Factor: Potency
    Withstand: N/A
    Suggested Rote Skills: Brawl, Firearms, Weaponry

    First developed by Banishers, this spell enchants a weapon, which may include a subject’s body, so its strikes cause a foe's mana to ignite into Aetherial fire. Whenever the subject is hit, their mana is burnt to inflict lethal damage on a one to one basis, up to the spell's Potency. Because this spell is incapable of inflicting damage to a Pattern with no mana, Arrows favor it both as a way to limit the potential risk to Sleepers and for its ability to turn a foe's own power against them.

    +1 Reach and a point of mana: The damage inflicted is aggravated.

    Supernal Exorcism (Prime •••••)
    Practice: Unmaking
    Primary Factor: Potency
    Withstand: Rank
    Suggested Rote Skills: Expression, Intimidation, Occult

    A Supernal summon who serves the Exarchs is an enemy of the Pentacle, but to destroy it is to feed the Abyss. In order to solve this dilemma, the Guardians developed this spell to unravel the tethers that hold a summon in the Fallen World, casting them back to the Supernal safely. While it does mean a summon can be called upon at a later date, normally removing them from an area tends to be more than enough to tip the scales. An entity may expend a point of mana to try to resist, rolling Power + Finesse in a Clash of Wills to avoid being sent back.

    Ochemata and entities above Rank 5 are unaffected. The Bound and Temple Guardians are too strongly tied to their areas to be sent back, instead discorporating and reappearing safely at their binding location.

    +1 Reach: The entity's mana is also depleted before being exorcised, sublimating it into the world, where it is likely to be absorbed by the nearest Hallow. In the past, this has been used to contain rampaging Bound.

    ​What were you thinking design wise ?

    The first Attainment is True Sight, a spell that allows the nullifier to perceive any mages fleeting to or from Twilight. It also makes it difficult for an opponent’s allied entities to sneak around in a layer of Twilight they cannot perceive. When paired with Ephemeral Enchantment or sensory range spells, it allows them to affect them. However, Prime only reveals things to the nullifier, it's up to them to figure out what they are looking at. The activation ritual is based on the symbolism of crystal gazing as a means to grant supernatural visions.

    The first optional Attainment is Pierce Conclealment, a spell which grants the nullifier the ability to see through invisibility, shapeshifting, Veilings (or Obfuscation if their target has a vampire ally), notice magically hidden traps and supernaturally concealed holdout weapons. It also provides them with a spidey sense to detect assassins and ambushes. While it cannot detect lies like Pierce Deception, it does make it much harder to sneak anything past them. The activation ritual builds on the myth of hag stones being protective talismans that let you see through the disguises and trickery of the fae.

    The second main Attainment protects them from being tracked by magic or perceived with magical senses, closing an avenue of attack. It also denies information in combat, leaving enemies to wonder what kind of defenses and enchantments they have up. When investigating, it allows them to use Mage Sight and things like Unveiling spells without triggering the Peripheral Sight (which is especially useful against Harrowed Banishers) and making the nullifier harder to investigate. The activation ritual is based on salt's ability to create a barrier against unwanted magics.

    The second optional Attainment allows them to turn their defense into offense by reflecting spells. It is the first part of their toolkit that makes facing a nullifier in combat an intimidating idea. Since this is potentially a very powerful ability, I added some caveats to balance it mechanically. The first is that it requires a Clash of Wills to seize control of the magic, meaning it's a defense that is all or nothing. The second is that it can only affect the flow of magic, similar to lightning redirection in Avatar: The Last Airbender, so sensory and sympathetic spells, that just appear on target, aren't affected, nor are AoE spells that just explode near the target. The third is that you still need to aim the reflected spell, and it can only be sent back to the caster. So while it can potentially turn back a very dangerous spell, the caster has a Clash and their defense to try to save against having it turned on them.

    With conjunctional Fate (or Forces) you can instead refract it to another target. The charge system is based on the Crucible spell. The activation ritual is based on the belief that mirrors can reflect back the evil eye at the witch who cast it.

    The third Attainment provides a better version of counterspell. Normally it requires the caster to be within sensory range and for the counterspeller to be using Mage Sight, but that is very situational. So instead of doing something like Display of Power, which uses a Weaving to make Imagos visible to everyone nearby, it instead uses a Perfecting to enhance the nullifier's ability to perceive them. Now they are able to always see when someone is trying to sneak a spell past them, to notice sudden spell attacks, protect a cabal member about to be hit by an Unmaking or stop a foe from teleporting away. It also allows them to quickly take stock of any spells, enchantments and imbuements in the vicinity, building on their reputation of being always watchful. The activation ritual draws a protective tattoo with invisible runes.

    The third optional Attainment protects the nullifier from mages with combat training, as well as assassins (such as snipers), mundane dangers and from being attacked sympathetically with an Unmaking spell. It also defends them from the classic mage hunter strategy of using a heavily armed squad of Sleepers, while simultaneously making the nullifiers more effective at applying that strategy. The spell is inspired by the prophecy around the Witch King of Angmar's death, which kept him from being slain until the condition was met. But because of the second main Attainment, discovering the conditions is deceptively difficult.

    The fourth Attainment is an all purpose defense against Awakened magic. Now the nullifier can instantly place protective magic on anyone, which at mastery levels would leave them with a base Withstand between 5 and 7. Because of how spell stacking works, they can place longer duration versions once a week and then layer one with higher Potency on top of it as needed. Allies and bystanders can be protected, evidence can be locked down from being stolen by magic and subjects can be made resistant to scrying.The conjunctional Fate 2 effect is merely for quality of life.

    The fourth optional Attainment is the ultimate panic button. Because it's an Attainment, it requires no roll, and since it is reflexive, it can be used at any moment so long as the nullifier has mana. Enemies can be interrupted with Knocked Down, disarmed with Arm Wrack, damage can be minimized with Charmed, just to name a few. In addition, it increases the reliability of the nullifier and their allies, allowing them to have a better chance at a critical roll or to get that one important spell cast.

    The capstone shuts down most Supernal magic for enough precious turns to finish off most mages. It also acts as a powerful psychological tool, unbalancing foes from the sheer shock and awe of being in an undispellable, uncounterable, expansive area of antimagic. The Fate 2 requirement serves two purposes. The first is to extend the duration with a Conditional Duration, making it so it lasts for a few more turns. The second is to allow the selective exclusion of targets, such as debuff on an enemy mage, buffs on allies, attack spells being used on enemies and more. While it cannot suppress Attainments, the nullifier's other Attainments means they and their allies are well defended, and can focus on seizing the opportunity, with the fourth optional Attainment ensuring any key moves can be pulled off reliably. The stylistic choice of it manifesting as a deep magical silence is a reference to the Profound Still spell from Dark Souls 2.

    The optional capstone allows the nullifiers to defend from AoE spells and other area affecting supernatural abilities. Furthermore, they can now selectively exclude anyone under its effect from their own spells and Attainments, allowing them to more easily fight large numbers of foes using AoE spells. With members of the cabal choosing not to contest the Clash of Wills, it can grant protection from their AoE spells too. When combined with the fourth main Attainment, they can now make anyone they choose resistant to spells the cabal didn't authorize. Lastly, it provides protection from mind control (including situations such as the enemy mage having a vampire ally with Majesty) and luck manipulation.

    The original design phrase for the nullifiers was "a reliable way to fight mages", with characters like Mike Ehrmantraut and Cerrit Agrupnin serving as inspiration, both characters being very pragmatic combatants. When considering the most consistent way to defeat the Awakened, the words of Gaiden Shinji came to mind: "The best techniques are passed on by the survivors". The greatest mage slayers probably have solid general defenses always up, that way they can focus on blitzing their opponents, dedicate more spell control to deal with any unique abilities of their current foes and survive their own mistakes, giving them the chance to learn from experience. The name Silent Bulwark represents their mission to silence magical mayhem and their commitment to stand as defenders.

    The main reason I picked duration primary spells with advanced duration is so they benefit from the dice bonus in Clashes of Wills, making their defenses even more formidable.

    The background was made to provide plot hooks based on different variations on mage slayers. The ghost mages focus on the Mystery of an ancient duty, the Tremere are those that became disillusioned and the Bellerophon members showcase their ideals degenerating into zealotry.

    Their appearance emphasizes how they must look superstitious to the average person. Their Sanctums are inspired by Constantine's workshop in the movie, creating a space for them to transform supernatural materials into equipment to fight the occult. The Kamijou residence from Index is another inspiration, showcasing how they integrate knowledge of protective magic into their everyday life.

    Given the inherent pragmatism of their approach, nullifiers probably don't neglect mundane combat training, both as a way to become more effective in battle and to have a higher chance of surviving the most dangerous confrontations.

    Taking inspiration from the grams from The Kingkiller Chronicles, their traditional badge of office doubles as a source of magical defense.

    The initiation is inspired by the expanded Occult crafting rules from Hurt Locker, its purpose is to put the prospective nullifier in the mindset of seeking novel use for any supernatural items they find. The suggested example is for a weapon that, upon hitting a mage, applies the modifier as a penalty to either aimed or touch spell hit rolls. It incentivizes players to build their own arsenal of custom weapons. Since mages have such varied abilities, fighting them using supernatural powers they don't understand can give both a psychological and tactical edge to the nullifier, and it helps build on the Van Hellsing feel I was going for.

    Organizing them into workshops is a tribute to Bloodborne, creating an avenue for them to develop new gear to protect the Consilium. Working alongside the crafters of their Orders also puts them in a position to be under more scrutiny. Since they are the closest thing to sanctioned Banishers, the Orders need to strike a balance between trust and being mindful of their mental stability, and mentors need to be very careful in their choice of new members.

    Simultaneously, working with Sleepwalkers in law enforcement incentivizes them to walk a fine line between being more effective at protecting people from rogue mages and accidentally creating hunters. Using Stealing Fire to recruit someone is the type of calculated risk that a nullifier might take.

    The yantras and oblations are there to incentivize building their own arsenal, being mindful of defensive magic and always having an ear out for any signs of trouble.

    When making the items in their arsenal, I wanted to create one example of a weapon, one utility item and one tactical item. The Burning Heart works as an anti-regenerator weapon. Power wise it's stronger than a flamethrower, but weaker than an incendiary grenade. The lack of ability to hit multiple targets at once makes it useful for creating controlled burns.

    The satchel makes it easy for them to access their Esoteric Armory anywhere, I figured the effect would amount to three dots, based on the scale used by the werewolf core book, powerful yet very specific.

    The Memento was inspired by Joel's wristwatch from The Last of Us. Silencing an area is a simple effect, but one I believe lends itself to creative uses.

    The first spell is the classic ability to pin a demon to a spot with a phurba or immobilize a foe by pinning them down. It leverages Prime's purview of revelation to tell them where to strike. Environmental awareness is key with this spell, the mage needs to know whether a wall, tree or even the ground can be leveraged to pin the target.

    The second spell is a specialized type of paladin's smite that sets a mage's mana on fire. It allows them to burn through a mage's resources while simultaneously being harmless to Sleepers. The caveat is that, once the target runs out of mana, they will stop receiving any extra damage.

    The final spell was my answer to the question "how can they Wisely defeat an enemy summon in a Prelate's Temple ?". It builds on the ability of Supernal Dispellation to cast spells across the Abyss and back to the Supernal, then applies it to Supernal beings.

    That concludes the design notes, hope you had as much fun reading it as I had writing it. A huge thanks to orathaic and Aendrinastor for all their feedback.
