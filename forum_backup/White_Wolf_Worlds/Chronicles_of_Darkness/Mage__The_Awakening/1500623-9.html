                                Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post
                                So Ectoplasm can be shaped, and produced by Death, and opens stuff to ghost. Do you think a similar substance can be produced for Spirit?

                                Edit: or Mind? I’ve seen arguments that it’s the substance of ephemeral entities.
                                The thematic and mechanical headspace of ectoplasm is such that it exists as a means for ghosts to cling to the world they are formed in; spirits are intruders in the material world, and rely on Manifestations to maintain their grip against the tidal force of the Moon drawing them back into Shadow.

                                If such a substance exists, it's unusual enough to be a Mystery or at least the product of a lot of legwork to discover how to reliably produce it; even a locus isn't Open to every kind of spirit, for all that any spirit could conceivably shift its nature to align with a locus over time, and spirits aren't quite as limited in the things that support their presence to need an equivalent of the Great Equalizer in common circulation.
