            Personally, I'd say it's a Clash using Gnosis+Mind or Gnosis+Resolve/Composure.

            For the first, it's because Life enhances the physiological aspects of Pattern Restoration, which means Mind isn't too outlandish a choice to assist in resolving a mental condition.
