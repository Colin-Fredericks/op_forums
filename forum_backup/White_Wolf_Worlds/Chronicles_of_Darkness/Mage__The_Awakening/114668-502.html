                                                    Originally posted by orathaic View Post

                                                    If the spell is withstood by a 10, the extra success cancel those out. No?
                                                    Getting past Withstand is always a matter of raising Potency above it or using the effect of Exceptional Success to ignore it. Spells are something where rolling it is only ever normal success or exceptional success, number of dice that turn up in between or above that does not matter.

                                                    Exceptional success on a dispel casting will affect any spell yes, although keep in mind that it takes 2 Reach to make that Lasting. Otherwise the spell effect will return when Duration expires.

                                                    Annihilate Spirit... it's really dealer's choice between using Exceptional Success or regular Potency to overcome the target's Rank, because with Potency as primary factor it's always going to have enough to take out most spirits by default and will only need a -2 penalty to go after your Rank 5s (it can't hurt higher spirits anyway). That being said, that spell is an odd one when it not only provides Clash of Wills as an additional layer of protection, but gives it the unusual dice pool of Power + Finesse. Spirits that were already very powerful are going to fairly reliably be able to roll enough successes to not be disintegrated in one go.

                                                    EDIT: Actually, a few other things to consider about Annihilate Spirit:

                                                    A rank 4 or 5 spirit is very likely to be bigger than size 5, and that's going to require some degree of additional penalty to cast on in the first place.

                                                    You're only ever going to have one free Reach on the spell, and you're probably going to want to use that on making the casting instant. That means that making it something you can use at sensory range or going for advanced scale to ease the dice penalties for targeting on larger spirits is going to cost three Paradox dice each (more if you're the stronger kind of Master). I think that's not an inconsiderable portion of your Mana pool to mitigate it, and could delay the casting by a bit if you went for both and wanted to reduce to a chance die.
