                    I've been playing around with a Prime legacy with some unusual attainments. It has a Zen flavor with a focus on subtle magic, but I'd like to know what people think of the mechanics side. Open to Free Council and Paternoster, all Paths. Probably requires Occultation and Composure to advance. They exclusively use Prime for Attainments.

                    1st: gain the persistent condition Insightful and one free dot of Occultation.
                    2nd: when using Universal Counterspell, your Clash of Wills has 8-again. Also, you may spend 1 Mana instead of a Reach to use Advanced Potency if your spell would not risk Paradox.
                    3rd: when you incorporate a legacy yantra into a spell, your spellcasting roll gets 8-again. You may spend 1 Mana instead of a Reach to cast at sensory range if your spell would not risk Paradox.
                    4th: you may regain up to your Gnosis in Mana from legacy oblations each day, and this Mana has a Quiet/Subtle Resonance. You may spend 1 Mana instead of a Reach to cast instantly if your spell would not risk Paradox.

                    Possible 5th attainments: add Prime to spell control cap. Supernal Dispellation. Use Mana instead of Reach for all purposes if spell would not risk Paradox. Create magical dead zones. Rewrite a subject's Obsession, even one of the Mad.

                    Insightful
                    Your character has attuned herself through zazen to the principles of nonduality and no-mind and all that good stuff. Take +2 to Composure-based rolls.
                    Beat: Gain a Beat whenever your character poses a question, riddle, or koan (whether esoteric or practical) that causes another character to reconsider a course of action and do something different.
                    Resolution: once per Chapter, resolve this Condition to receive a "sudden realization" insight into a problem or question your character is aware of. This should take the form of a true statement related to the subject. Regain this Condition by spending an hour in sitting meditation.

                    2E Legacy Updates
                    Brotherhood of the Demon Wind
                    Choir of Hashmallim (plus extra Summoning content)
                    Storm Keepers
