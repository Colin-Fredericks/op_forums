    Mage 2E Char Sheets

    04-27-2016, 12:42 AM
    EDIT: Mr. Gone ended making fan sheets for Mage 2E. Here is link for them - http://mrgone.rocksolidshells.com/mta2nded.html !!!

    So we need to wait at least one more week for 2E corebook. I thought that we need to occupy ourselves with some project and remembering how we need to wait few weeks for Werewolf 2E sheets after premier – I propose we made our version of sheet so Mr Gone could make them in the meantime. I invite you all to comment on those ideas and maybe propose your own.

    First, let’s look on GMC Mage sheet for now…





    In my point of view, first page is all good, really. Until we do not know if there will be some large changes as critical mechanics, first page can be as it is. We only would need to change fonts for Second Edition ones, but it’s minor thing.

    The we go to second page – and here is list of things I would change:
    1. Shorter Active Spells section – in 2E it’s only equal to you Gnosis, so 10 lines max here.
    2. Longer Nimbus section – We now have Signature Nimbus and there was mechanic that give environment your Nimbus Tilt.
    3. Removing Paradox Marks section – from Dark Eras we know that now Paradox effects works as Conditions, so they end on first page.
    4. Shorter Rotes Section – I do not know any character that would fill it in their gaming career – maybe 15 lines?
    5. Enchanted Items and Combat moved lower, on bottom of page.
    6. Instead of Familiar ( that is niche thing ) give here Praxis and Wisdom Inures sections. Familiar can easily become next sheet for those that use them, but Praxis and Inures are more crucial to all characters.
    7. Magical Tools – Do not think it’s need changing. Maybe renaming it’s just for Yantras section?
    Addition: We have new Mage font samples from Dark Eras – if someone recognize those, please write the name of font.
