    Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post
    New questions for today:
    1) The Ascending Ones have access to elixirs. Can a mage replicate or enhance the fallen magic with matter, or is life/ whatever arcanum the effect falls under necessary as well?
    I think Signs of Sorcery had rules for making potions - I’d skim those to try replicating elixirs. Most likely, you need an arcanum for the spell effects and matter to make it a potion. I don’t think enhancing is possible, since spells of the same effect don’t stack, but prolonging it might be possible with Time.

    2) At what point do life patterns become matter patterns? Example: if a mage takes a branch from a tree, at what point will it respond to matter spells to shape it?
    After they’re finished transforming through Death. I would say that If the tree branch can still be planted and grow a new plant, it’s still alive. If there’s some kind of preservation, or processing, or something to make it a workable material, it wouldn’t be matter until after that occurs. Trees specifically are a little tough for me to grok, but like with animals, once decomposition has finished and you’re left with bones, that would be the point Death finishes and leaves Matter.

    Bonus Question:
    5) Are mortals with a supernatural merit more or less likely to awaken? Do endowments count as a supernatural merit?
    5.5) I vaguely remember a elixir that increases the roll for morality degradation, making it more likely that they achieve the required exceptional success. Does that mean there is a large ( for a mage population) number of ex Ascending Ones? Can they still access their endowments?
    From what I recall, having supernatural merits means that the character is much more likely to keep investing in those powers, rather than seek deeper levels of enlightenment.

    There was a great quote from Dave B (prior to SOS, so it may have changed), that Sleepers Awaken when they have enough Arcane EXP to buy a dot of gnosis. Having some powers can ensure that you have more novel experiences with the supernatural (the primary way mortals could earn Arcane beats) without dying or going insane. But the character is much more likely to spend that on the powers they have / know are available / help them with short term goals, rather than save up to Awaken - which they might not know exists, and which could still go horribly wrong.

    They lose other intrinsic supernatural powers when they awaken, but I believe they can keep purely external merits (like Relics). I don’t know enough about the Ascending Ones to guess how much is lost for them specifically. If it took a merit to make elixirs for themself, I believe that would be lost.

            Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post
            New questions for today:
            1) The Ascending Ones have access to elixirs. Can a mage replicate or enhance the fallen magic with matter, or is life/ whatever arcanum the effect falls under necessary as well?
            Enhancing the effects of an Ascending Ones' potion would probably be Matter 3 Perfecting, diminishing its effects would be Matter 3 Fraying.

            Replicating the effects would be the spell Spell Potion (Matter 3 + Prime 2, Signs of Sorcery) and then whatever other spell would replicate the effects of the specific potion.

            2) At what point do life patterns become matter patterns? Example: if a mage takes a branch from a tree, at what point will it respond to matter spells to shape it?
            Once it's no longer dying, it is a bit of a grey area.

            3) Can Matter perfecting enhance the effect of substances on Living creatures without Life? Examples: making something extremely addicting and making the withdrawal symptoms even worse. Or increase the effect of performance enhancing drugs.
            Yes, Matter perfecting can enhance the effects of anything under its purview, even if that thing is then used on a living creature.

            4) If I made a legacy based around the idea of Enlightenment through Drugs or just Chemistry, based on the above, should the ruling arcanum be Life or Matter?
            Probably a Thyrsus Matter/Life Legacy.

            Todays questions were inspired by the Blight from dead by daylight, and me asking myself how I would do a mammon seer. Yes, I am aware the Blight most closely resembles a Ascending One slasher or Broken, but I think a rapt based of it would be interesting.

            Bonus Question:
            5) Are mortals with a supernatural merit more or less likely to awaken? Do endowments count as a supernatural merit?
            Mortals with Supernatural Merits are probably slightly more likely to Awaken, but being told about the Supernal makes you less likely to Awaken.

            Some Endowments count as Supernatural, others don't. Advanced Armory definitely doesn't, Benedictions and Castigations definitely do, and Thaumatech (the Cheiron kind) are borderline. Basically, if it's an inherent power, it makes you count as a Sleepwalker, if it's an external power it doesn't.

            5.5) I vaguely remember a elixir that increases the roll for morality degradation, making it more likely that they achieve the required exceptional success. Does that mean there is a large ( for a mage population) number of ex Ascending Ones? Can they still access their endowments?
            Probably not, and no.
