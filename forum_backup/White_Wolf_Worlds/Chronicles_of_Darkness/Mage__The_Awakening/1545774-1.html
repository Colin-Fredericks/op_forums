        Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post

        The Synhedrion were...

        1) Completely - and I mean completely - embedded into the Sleeper organisation of the same name.

        2) Based on the contemporary-to-them Merkabah mysticism rather than Kabala, as Kabala won't be invented for several centuries. The Synhedrion were scholars and rabbis who recorded, debated, and attempted to understand their Awakening visions and Mage Sight in the context of their religion, not Golem-creators.

        3) Were the hardest of hardcore about magic only being the province of those wise enough to not misuse it.

        When we were writing Tome of the Pentacle, we had it in mind that

        a) Canonically, the Seers-to-be and Diamond-to-be at the time were two differences of opinion / political parties / arguments within the same organisation, named by later Diamond historians as the "Tyrannic Antitype" and "Atlantean Antitype". The difference was, essentially, should mages rule Sleepers as an expression of the natural hierarchy of the worlds and their Awakened status, or should mages regard themselves as a people apart from Sleeping Empires and Nations, governing themselves? The Diamond Precept is "The Awakened are one Nation" - it's that declaration that expels the Exarchists into a millenium of Nameless Orders and eventually the Seers, and makes the Five Diamond Orders out of their Darshanas.

        b) We (by which I mean I) do not believe in bloodless revolutions and clean transitions. Every great change in the makeup of the setting should have refuseniks and outliers. I wanted to show a Great Cult that *didn't* go into the Diamond, but was a peer and influenced some of its thinking. The Synhedrion would fit - it's the Hellenic culture that got left out of To The Strongest for space.

        c) The conflict between the antitypes is all well and theoretical, but the proto-Seers would have had to do something to get Unnamed by the rest of the Awakened community. First edition presented Diamond mages in the Roman Empire as being fringe thinkers and Greek imports, with power in the Empire held by Seers (conflicting with the fact that Seers shouldn't exist yet) - especially the Praetorian Ministry, or their ancestors anyway.

        d) Seers of the Throne sure do have a lot of Jewish terms in their lexicon. Why the fuck is a Profane Urim called that?

        e) While we're on the subject, there's an inherent conflict between a Synhedrion that tries to interpret the mind of God from their visions while remaining morally-centered and, well... How Prelacies work for Seers. Interpreting what you see in Mage Sight to determine the wishes of the divine is what a Prelate does, for certain values of "the Divine", and that's probably why the Synhedrion are so careful with their practices. They're competition to the Tyrannics, who won't worship the Iron Seals like they "should".

        f) So if we take that forward and adapt it to second edition, we have Tyrannic-minded mages buying into the Empire, a Great Cult tied to a culture that's about to get horribly persecuted by that Empire, our desire for a Cult that stays "outside", our need for Tyrannic mages to do something terrible in the eyes of their Atlantean peers, parallel or conflicting practices between the Cult and the basic Sight>Prelate loop of a Seer, modern Seers of the Throne who mock that Cult's religion with their terms for things...

        ... It all adds up to "things do not end well for the Synhedrion".


        Which is why the final chapter glosses over them. It's both too complicated and if handled poorly full of potential antisemitic pittraps. Somewhere out there is a rpg freelancer who's versed in Merkabah enough to write intelligently about it (and is willing to do so despite it being a closed practice maybe not suitable for turning into a game for outsiders) and depict the effect the first Jewish-Roman war and the destruction of the second temple had on them.

        But that freelancer is not me.

        As it stands in the gameline, the Synhedrion's fate is mysterious. Maybe they did join into the Diamond. Maybe the final straw for the Diamond kicking the Prelates out came later, or had a different cause. Maybe we don't have to have yet another Jewish faction of mages in a roleplaying game murdered to make a point. We don't really use the appropriated terms for Seer artifacts any more (notice how the line tries to say "Profane Robe" most of the time) and they have a perfectly good explanation even if they do stay - Paternoster, when it first started up over a millenium later, looted a *lot* of things. Including the Teraphim, which is one of the items in question.
        Well that clarifies that. I stand corrected and edified.

                                    Rough draft for Awakened Christianity - for my Rome games. ( It's very important for Rome Empire in 2nd till 5th century AD - and probably from there on. ) Probably similar would work probably for Judaism and Islam versions of it.

                                    Awakened Christianity

                                    What is magic? Magic is boon send from God and their angels.

                                    What is Fallen World? World was created perfect by God. With Atlantean fall of Towers to Heaven - God cursed whole creation to become Fallen World as it is.

                                    Who are Exarchs and Oracles? Exarch AND Oracles are both emanations of One True God. Many times they are called as Archangels on particular Path.

                                    What is High Speech? High Speech is language of Angels - later known as Enochian

                                    What is Abyss? Absence of the divine light, pointed to the theology of St Augustine of Hippo. Some intellectual in Awakened Christianity posits it's parts of Creation rejected by God. In later centuries other posits it's literal Hell, or spread of Hell, directly from the Devil.

                                    What is Supernal Realms? Supernal Realms are higher planes, outside of normal reality, each governed by one Archangel. They are still only 'filters' of One True Higher reality, ruled all at once, by God themselves.

                                    Aether - Havens of All Mighty, under Archangel Micheal
                                    Arcadia - Under guidance of Gabriel, past World BEFORE Creation of Humans - perfect, but also somehow pagan.
                                    Pandemonium - Purgatory, ruled by the angel of judgment Phanuel. ( In Middle Ages, it's changed into literal 'Hell of sinners, ruled by Lucifer' )
                                    Primal Wild - Garden of Eden AFTER creation of Adam and Eve, especially with Tree of Knowledge, under guidance of Rafael
                                    Stygia - Mausoleums and Cathedrals of previous dead, under watch of Uriel ( See Driftwood Cathedral for inspiration )

                                    What are local Darshanas (proto-Orders)?
                                    Christianity was created just AFTER first integration of Atlantean Orders in Ancient Rome, circa 1st century BCE. ( With Christianity creation on whole 1st century AD. ) It's widespread of this religion in Mediterranean Sea area is joined with creation Diamond as organisations Alliance. All of Darshanas of it ARE Atlantean and Seers Orders - even in modern times, it's rather minority percentage but still sustained in general multicultural tapestry of Diamond Cults beliefs systems, when more and more Awakened eshew more religious roots of their magic.

                                        The Primal Wild is the world without humans as far as I understand
                                        To the extent that the Supernal Realms should be regarded in terms of being literal, coherent places, the Primal Wild is not nature as it would be without human intervention, it's something representative of the idea that all living things are interconnected (including the idea that everything that seems whole is itself built out of smaller organisms), that all actions are consequential (and those consequences endure and perpetuate themselves), and the visceral, ecstatic sensation of drifting through these bonds. It's not a world without humanity, it's humanity as inextricably part of the world.
