        Originally posted by quelsec View Post
        In Awakening mind, prime and death have their own spiritual realms with their own summoned spiritual creatures. What makes Spirit stand out as an Arcanum?
        Prime does not have its own spiritual realm in the standard model, but what makes Spirit notable is that the Shadow is the most accessible realm and its denizens are notionally the most durable and sustainable, which makes it the most useful one for getting low-investment familiars or traveling unseen without sticking to Twilight.

        The Underworld can largely only be physically accessed through Avernian Gates, which are limited by/affect local Resonance, and while training can allow characters to overcome the geographical requirements for meditating into the Astral its contents don't normally have any sort of predictable interface with the world outside of dreams. While Shadowed Gates are uncommon, technically any spirit can cross the Gauntlet from anywhere it can feed from and loci are just particularly easy places to cross.

        Ghosts need specific and limited phenomena to gain Essence and, while Goetia are the only common invisible entities to which the "regain their Rank in Essence every day in their home realm" mechanic actually applies and they theoretically have no greater difficulty finding Essence than a spirit of the same concepts, they're severely limited in their ability to affect the material world between lack of native Manifestations and inconsistent ability to leave the Astral. Spirits feed on the energies of the same things that created them and have a survivorship bias toward representing concrete phenomena, and their ecology is such that despite their limited free Essence they can replenish themselves from the stores of similar beings.

        tl;dr Regularly making use of ghosts and Goetia requires extra preparation and support that spirits don't typically need, and the Shadow hews relatively close to Earthly geography and doesn't have a hard limit on where you can enter and exit from overall. The Spiritual frame has the most "natural" interface with the material world and its denizens don't require special treatment to be effective tools and allies.

                Spirit stands out as an Arcanum because the animism premise gives it a few things to engage with that might not otherwise be expected, because the ecological and social fabric of the Shadow is a bit more sophisticated to be able to engage with (ghosts and Goetia are kind of self-contained to their own particular subrealms), and because it's always right next to you.

                                                Originally posted by quelsec View Post
                                                Cant you just summon any of the 3 to interact and it will be the same?
                                                No.

                                                Adding to what others said and also a maybe simpler explanation:

                                                1. They won't have the same stats, variety and ease to summon. Spirits will be by far easier to find and summon within almost any parameter you may want.

                                                2. Won't be the same. Interaction is different, bargaining for services will be different and opposing them if needed will be different. And again Spirits will offer you way more versatility in every aspect.

                                                3. Even within the same Rank and Influence they have distinct perspectives and means of interaction that affect what they can do in practice. Goetia, ghosts and supernal beings in this regard have highly specialized strengths, while spirits have vastly broader applications.

                                                So you can summon any of the four, but what it takes, what you can do with them and what they can do for you isn't equal, and spirits far outweigh the others three in overall ease and usefulness. And that's only talking about you summoning them, which is the smallest use case for all four Arcana.

                                                More important is all the rest. You need the respective Arcana to interfere magically with the respective realms, their phenomena, their interactions with the Material, any denizen that comes your way and the materials and energy that comprise them.

                                                It then becomes extremely relevant the fact that the Shadow is way bigger, meaner and closer than the other three realms, by orders of magnitude. Between the three, the Underworld is by far the closest to the Material and still the points of contact are few. Only in the premises of an Avernian Gate do you have interactions between realms, and those gates are maybe a few dozen in a metropolis.

                                                Only one point of contact exists between the Material and the Shadow, though. It is called the whole fucking universe. The realm is so close to the Material that areas where they're not in direct contact with each other are scary anomalies rarer and more dangerous than a gate to the Supernal (and you don't come back from one of those if you fall in).

                                                Loci aren't just maybe 10 times more common and easier to make than Avernian Gates, they are not just contact surfaces, they're damned holes in the fabric of reality with a thin layer of inconvenience separating them. Get away from one and everywhere you'll still be close enough to the Shadow to influence of directly, be influenced by it directly, be at reach for a lot of spirits, be surrounded by spirits in the Shadow and so on. It's so overwhelmingly everywhere that ghosts in Twilight interact with things from the Shadow more frequently than with anything coming from the Underworld.

                                                You also have greater variety of environments, phenomena and materials in the Shadow, with means everything said about the differences in summoning denizens applies to pretty much each and every imaginable use of contact with another dimension.

                                                        Originally posted by orathaic View Post
                                                        I would argue for the Astral actually being bigger than than the Shadow, or at least more vast...
                                                        The Shadow is a measurable infinity: the whole material universe exist there, plus areas that only exist there. That also mean it has at least every possible environment that exists in the universe, plus several impossible variations of those environments, plus who knows how wild it can get with completely unique environments and, on top of that, it is both expanded by human thought and imagination, but not limited to it.

                                                        It doesn't mean it's necessarily bigger in literal and absolute sense, but we can more readily grasp a notion of it being super fucking huge in a manner that's also easier to come up with uses in game that bring this hugeness to bear.

                                                        But the Astral is certainly the next contender. It has potentially every conceivable place and environment, which may or may not be more than what we get out of the Shadow. At least the Shadow is theoretically limited by having to make some sense, which the Astral bypasses. It has also something I wouldn't call more vast, but certainly more applicable vastness in that you're not bound to regular space there. There is no reason to think the Shadow doesn't have distant stars, but in the Astral you can get there without the inconvenience of journeying across several parsecs. At least without magic.

                                                        Supernal reality and the Underworld are small in the narrative sense, which is what matter here. Even if you could travel for real to the Supernal Realms they'd be homogeneous in any comparison with the Material. Even by their definition, since they only have the perfected form of concepts, while the Lie has the infinite imperfect variations, the Supernal can't hold more diverse experiences. But you can't really go there or anything close to it, not without you ceasing utterly to exist in any known sense of the it, so it's basically whatever you summon plus brief weird phenomena and the eventual Reality Thrash Can opening so you can dump stuff that won't ever come back.

                                                        With a pretty samey description of an endless cave with trickling streams, a shore with some cities, rivers of stuff, geographically limited island Dominions and the Ocean of Memories as a very well known practical end, Underworld is the most limited known realm as it has a non trivial chance of being spatially smallish. Like, add to it all that the others exist beyond human life and experience, but the Underworld is only for human created ghosts, and it has a pretty decent chance of not being larger than Europe if it isn't spatially mapped over the Material the way the Shadow is.

                                                        Of course, this is just a fun exercise. Their size isn't literally measurable or comparable, their application in game is, and then that's it: Astral and Shadow more or less tie in regards to how varied is the shit you can pull out of them, but the Shadow has too much more immediately applicable relevance by being a superimposed mirror reality in constant interaction with the Material everywhere and that is effectively an integral part of the Material's composition and regular physics. Hence having its own Arcana.
