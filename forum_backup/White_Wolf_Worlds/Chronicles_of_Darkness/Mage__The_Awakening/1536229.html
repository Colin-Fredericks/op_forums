              I have (mostly) written a modified awakening which removed the gnosis from the Mage and replaced it with a type of Synergy.

              Basically like the Walkers where they remain in a coma (trapped in their Onerios) but their magic expresses itself as a Greater Tulpa.

              When a Walker generates a Greater Tulpa, her soul and psyche merge with the entity. This allows the Walker to escape the prison of her own Oneiros temporarily, fused with her Tulpa and briefly able to influence the world around her. This fusion does not alter the Greater Tulpa’s characteristics or traits, but the mage’s conscious will inhabits and directs it.
              So inspired by this, I have Tulpaic Awakenings, where the (not a Mage anymore) dissassociates from their Gnosis, rejecting it, and leaving what would be the equivalent of their Greater Tulpa trapped in their Onerios.

              Now Synergy* with that entity seems the more appropriate system to use for managing power level.

              Likewise, I wanted something like Clarity† to replace Wisdom, since they are trying to work their will be imposing Supernal truths while rejecting the reality of what they are doing. Never really finished it, but as an antagonist it could make an interesting NPC.

              *See Geist the Sin-Eater
              †see Changeling the Lost

                                                  When I was starting to get back into the game more I had an idea about mages who essentially volunteer themselves as living prisons for the Bound, functioning as a bit of a loophole in what they're bound to; still imprisoned, but in something with mobility and agency that can carry out their requests of where to be and what to do. I had some kind of idea that something about the process would make their need to eat magic more acute, to the point that they'd not only suck up the likes of Hallows or try to draw mana out of mages and other receptacles, but would basically decay and ultimately destroy any Mystery they became engaged with at the same level as if their host had just been investigating and understanding it as normal.

                                                  I'll admit, I feel a bit less confident in the idea now than I did at the time. I have a lot of regard for the inventiveness behind things like the Rapt and version of the Tremere that we've ultimately gotten (and even Harrowed Banishers seem to accomplish quite a lot with a straightforward but very significant template addition), and it makes it hard for me to feel like some other ideas I might come up with for a kind of mutated mage template don't amount to just treading already established ground.

                                                  I have approximate knowledge of many things.
                                                  Write up as I play Xenoblade Chronicles.
