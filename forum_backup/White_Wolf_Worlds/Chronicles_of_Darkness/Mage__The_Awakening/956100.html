    Threat: The Egohackers

    08-22-2016, 11:08 PM
    The Silver Ladder views magic as the birthright of the entire human race; likewise, the Free Council sees mankind and its works as inherently magical. Many in these two Orders dream of the day when the Sleepers of the world will rise up as one and shatter the spiritual chains that bind them all, and some radicals among them conduct elaborate occult rituals and social experiments alike to attempt to Awaken the magical spark within the human soul. These usually end in failure at best and shattered sanity at worst, as Wisdom dwindles and Quiescence makes itself known.

    Sometimes, however, these attempts work, though never as intended.

    It isn't known how it happened, but an unknown mage or cabal working as part of a joint effort between the thearchs and the Libertines managed to tie a superficial understanding of Mind magic into a supernatural smartphone app. Those Sleepers who download it have their minds violently exposed to the existence of the Astral (often causing disastrous harm to their sense of self), but after the initial onslaught find it incredibly easy to traverse the roads of thought as lucid dreamers. Guided by their mentor to smash the oppressors that bind them to the world, they did exactly that... and severed his soul from his body, leaving it to forever wander the universal subconsciousness of man.

    Branding themselves the Egohackers, these "liberated" Sleepwalkers seek to destroy the symbols of harm and tyranny within the Temenos, violently pursue anyone who tries to impose any control on Sleeper minds, and spread their magical phone app to anyone and everyone. One part anarchist movement, one part Banisher cult, and one part memetic virus, the Egohackers are spreading like a spiritual-digital plague from their point of inception. Those Awakened who discover their existence and find out their purpose are terrified, with some among the Guardians of the Veil already making preparations for an apocalyptic purge of Sleepers. Anyone with an internet connection is at risk of joining the Egohacker banner, and while they're currently small and leaderless, they could easily grow to become an existential threat to the Awakened as a whole.

    Psychic terrorists who spread like a disease. What's not to like?
