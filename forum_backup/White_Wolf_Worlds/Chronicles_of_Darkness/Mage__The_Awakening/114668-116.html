        Originally posted by nofather View Post
        Do people have tricks for Mage NPCs that might get into combat scenarios?

        Do you have them rely on rotes, while taking advantage of any environmental opportunities? Cause there's a lot of versatility in their spells and whipping things together on the fly can seem daunting.
        Generally speaking, the first rules of combat are:

        1. Don't get into combat.
        2. If you break rule 1, try to get away if doing so would likely prevent serious injury or death.
        3. If you cannot comply with rule 2, go for the jugular.

        (Optional rule 4: If you must fight, do it where you have home-field advantage.)

        Generally speaking, as a Mage gets more powerful, the kinds of pain they can inflict on an enemy are really limited only by their imagination and their ethics. There are some truly awful things you can do to someone with Life magic that make the prospect of facing a Thyrsus Adept with a grudge pretty brutal. As a result, winning a fight between the Awakened becomes less and less a question of how you'll win and more and more how you want to win.

        Weaker Awakened, meanwhile, will rely on combat rotes, skill-boosting rotes, and "debuffs". Truth is, of course, that some Ruling spells are so vicious as to be fight-enders in their own right.

            In general, figure out a spell effect that cannot be easily counteracted without having ability to manipulate Arcanum used to cast it, and then use it to win fight by creating unfair situations. "Ban" is poster spell for this, so is gravity manipulation, Darkness spamming, Twilight Gating opponents, and so on. Put your opponents in a position from which they can't fight, and they will be dead, eventually.

            EDIT
            Combat Mage is a Mage who:
            1. Knows how to disable their enemies with one reliable spell
            2. Has easy way to compensate for lack of sensory input. If you are a combat mage, it's not unreasonable for you to have always-running spell that will allow you to still cast spells even when your mundane senses are impossible to use
            3. Even when forced to use magic defensively in fight, can use it to gain progress towards victory

                Do people have tricks for Mage NPCs that might get into combat scenarios?

                Do you have them rely on rotes, while taking advantage of any environmental opportunities? Cause there's a lot of versatility in their spells and whipping things together on the fly can seem daunting.
                The different approach to combat in 2E is really helpful here. If you're getting into a combat situation, remember to ask what everyone's Intent is. It might be "kill/injure the other guys," yeah, but it also might be "get the hell out of here" or "capture someone" or "make my enemies lay down their arms" or "distract them while my friend finishes the ritual." When you know what everyone is thinking, it narrows down their options.

                With NPCs, it also might be good to play loose with the spellcasting rules a little bit and use Down and Dirty Spellcasting. You don't need to overthink a specific player-focused spell effect, just look at the NPC's dot rating and decide what they're trying to do (kill someone, hinder them, escape, etc), then describe it in a cool way and roll Gnosis+Arcanum+2 for whatever Yantra they're assumed to use. If it's a damage effect, it does [Arcanum rating]+[successes] damage. If it's a dice penalty or bonus, it gives [successes] dice for [Arcanum rating] turns, or vice versa. If it's a narrative effect like teleportation or dimension shifting, it just happens how you want unless the players counterspell it.

                        11-11-2016, 08:57 PM
                        Figure out their Shadow Name. Good Shadow Names usually lead to fairly "obvious" and "intuitive" method of attack. Darcy The Sun Lord will whip you with a fireball, Nancy The Undying Shadow will probably have a favorite shadow spell. So it's a good habit to figure out few spells for each important NPC that they like to use, not only for combat, but for other situations too.*

                        *
                        I'm a person who believes that each thing you write down and prepare beforehand will help you immensely when "improvising", so my advice for "how to improvise on fly" is to "prep beforehand".

                        EDIT
                        Quick and Dirty:
                        Figure out beforehand typical magic pool for your casters, assuming "probable" yantra budget
                        Next, check how many spell factors you can squeeze out of it before going down to 4/5/6 dice (pick one of these options)
                        Write down the trimmed down spell pool and number of factors to spend
                        Now you can spend factors fast and down bother with calculating the whole thing over and over again.

                                Originally posted by Axelgear View Post
                                Can a person move from their own Oneiros to someone else's, either directly or through connection to a shared Temenoic realm?

                                That is, to say, could a cabal member enter the Oneiros of another cabal member by, say, following the threads of symbolism of their cabal and/or its sanctum into the Temenos and then following it out into another member's Onerios from there?
                                Yes. There's definitely some spells in Astral Realms to do it, although those really need to be adapted to 2E. You can also enter someone else's Oneiros instead of your own if you're linked with Telepathy while you both meditate.

                                In 2E there's a way to do it with no special spells. There's a sentence tucked in the Astral section somewhere that says you can enter another Oneiros if both of you are currently thinking about a very specific thing while meditating or dreaming. You follow the symbolism about that specific thing in your own Oneiros, and then presumably when you reach the shared "cell" about that subject, you can transfer over to theirs. This would be easy enough to arrange with your cabal, and you could spy on other people too if you know what's on their mind.

                                I'm not totally clear on how the Temenos interacts with individual Oneiroi. I don't know if there are naturally direct portals, you probably have to use spells or Numina to break into someone's dream vault.
