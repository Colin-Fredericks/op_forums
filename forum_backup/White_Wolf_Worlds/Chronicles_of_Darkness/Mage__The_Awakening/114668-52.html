        As DaveB has mentioned, a Mage's best bet when they expect their spell to Clash is to pump extra duration into it (which adds built in bonuses to the Clash dice pool).

                Originally posted by WHW View Post
                ...Also, is there really anything stopping Ruling Life or Death from just making something die? It seems to fall within their Purviews and "what can Ruling do"; would it be "be dead for the duration of the spell" kind of deal? Are there any reasons, outside of obvious game balance, for it not being possible?
                Well, I would rule that you can "Rule" someone already dying (all health boxes with lethal, rapid-killing disease, suicide tentions, etc) to die right now {Potency Ruling Death Spell}, or someone with a serious heart issue to have a heart attack {Potency Ruiling Life Spell} the same way as "Rule" can affect a bored worker to take a break now (Potency Ruling Mind Spell). But without pre-existing firm conditions, "Rule" can't cause aneurisms or decomposition to deal damage or make stuff happen. This falls under "Fraying"

                Speaking of the Devil, with Fraying (and other Practices) one can for example "Fray" someone with Death, decomposing his flesh until he is dying. Then he can affect him with the Ruling Practice to die. Or first mark him with a Ruling Duration "Die Faster" spell, then Fray him and take his victim by surprise

                As already said, Ruling magic cant fray by itself, but maybe it can give conditions like "Heart Attack", "Speed Decomposition" or "Fasten" that are damaging with their own effects but requires some setup like people with existing chronic heart disease, people that became angry in this scene etc...

                To sum up, here is my interpretation of the "Cool" Practices:
                Compel is for "I slightly nudge stuff"
                Rule is "Turn existing stuff into Environmental/Situational Advantage"
                Fray is "average Firebending control"
                Pattern is "Taste my lightning sucker"
                Make is "You're my bitch"
