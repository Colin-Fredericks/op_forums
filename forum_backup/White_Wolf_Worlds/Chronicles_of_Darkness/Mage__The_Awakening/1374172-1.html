        Originally posted by Caedus View Post
        I did not notice the nazi part :O. Are they magical racists or just normal racists?
        Magical racists. To them, anyone with a greater template and isn't a mage is a child of the Sixth Watchtower, and thus inherently corrupted, as are Supernal beings while they're in the Fallen World; all inherent restrictions and weaknesses are symptoms of it, and thus the Tremere have a duty to destroy them before they entrap other mages worse than even the original Watchtowers do, or enslave them in order to get some good out of their state.

        There's also a rather cynical motive in there; for greater templates, a Tremere can't steal their souls... or if they can, their souls are just as long-lasting as another mage's. Which means that the Tremere doesn't need to murder people who, in another world, might have been their peers (Beasts, Unchained, Prometheans, Arisen, and Bound are immune, but everyone else is on the menu). So, to the Tremere, they get what they see as a less morally suspect couple months by stealing the life of what they feel to be an inferior, and their ideology has been made to agree with them.

                        The Gnosis tendencies are too high imo, for what you get out of the various forms of corrupted mages. For example, defiled spells are supposed to be a gateway, but they use Gnosis for abyssal reaches, and require very high Gnosis to be worthwhile. Instead of rolling Gnosis, I would more suggest something like (10-Gnosis) autosuccesses, to make spell defiling darkly tempting to newer mages. The quick path to power and all that.

                        Similarly, I kind of think "roll Gnosis or become a preta" for new Tremere is a bit overly punitive -- low Gnosis mages are already disadvantaged. I would suggest Gnosis+highest subtle arcanum. I would also lower the requirements on Tremere soul grappling. There is simply unlikely to be much incentive for grappling at the ridiculously high levels.

                        Finally, Stress fills up a bit slowly. A Rapt needs to be thwarted about six times to fill their Stress track on average, and most of the time will only release Lesser Tulpa. Rapt are supposedly NPCs, but their iconic mechanic means that unless a Rapt is not only in every scene but avoids their Fault constantly, nothing is likely to happen. Also, does fulfilling your Fault empty Stress?

                        Essentially, the tendency is for villain options to be weighted to far, far beyond becoming a Master, the point at which whatever mechanical distinctions of the accursed mage are overshadowed by the supreme power of mastery. The opposite tendency would be better.
