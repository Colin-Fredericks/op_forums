    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
    How are people inclined to handle improvised casting of spells that might be regarded as more esoteric or non-intuitive?

    For example, let's say the Stone Scribe spells for Final Names are still a thing, under either Fate or Death. They're a bit unusual as either Arcanum goes. Would people portray that as the sort of thing where any mage with the right Practices would repeat them with a snap, or the sort of thing where a bit of explanation is needed in character to help wrap one's head around the casting (not taking a form of spending experience or anything)? Perhaps even scrutinising the spell with Mage Sight.

    Or how about total improvised casting? Would you go along with a character being all "yeah, I can totally make a record of a person's final Pattern and sympathetic connections inscribed in written form" independently, or want them to at least encounter a Mystery that introduces the concept?
    It would depend on the person an how they view magic. No two people are the same after all. Personally, I do like the idea of someone organically learning how their magic works rather than looking at all the example spells and just picking the best one. Though, I personally prefer introspection to figure out rather than a concrete Mystery. Analyzing their own pattern analyzing other's patterns and their sympathetic connections. Then analyzing corpses to put 2+2 together.
