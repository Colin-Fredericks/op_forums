                                            I haven't looked to much at mage preview yet but I can give some educated guesses. Sleepers are much the same as in 1st edition as I imagine, extremely prized. They're the personal assistants and bodyguards to the Wise in villages and cities. They help with the bigger rituals that a Wise or a group of them might try to cook up.

                                            As to how aware people are of the supernatural would in part be up to the GM. I would look to history in terms of that question. Three hundred years ago we were burning witches in Salem because people believed in the devil and sorcery even as we claimed it was the age of enlightenment. In a world with basically no technology/science to turn to, the only explainations to problems are of the supernatural variety. Given the text of the chapter I'd say the people back then were very aware of the supernatural.
