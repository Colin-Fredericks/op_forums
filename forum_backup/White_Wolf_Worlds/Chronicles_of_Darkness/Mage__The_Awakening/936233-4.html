                    Well, my own thought so far is a simple HR, to adapt it to the needs of my group.

                    Temporal Sympathy can still cast spells back (or forward) in time but it can NOT change the past, only those particular spells (Shifting Sands etc) can do that. The main benefits of it would be that unless the spell lasts all the time up until the present... no spell control required. Casting it into the future? Sure, it'll count against spell control... at that point, not before.

                    In essence you can gather information from the past, even using other arcana, or other things that won't actually change the timeline noticeably (and yes, this is subjective, that's what you have a Devil/GM for) and you can cast spells forward in time without having to actually have spell control wasted while you're waiting for them, and you also save on duration factors since it's duration starts from that point.

                    I, personally, think that this might work for out group but I will be honest, I'm still wary of the fact that I might have a blindspot so... if you see something broken in it tell me now please? :P

                        To quote myself from another thread,

                        Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
                        So, the Temporal Sympathy chart works just like the Sympathetic Range chart, only instead of casting across distance and the strength of the connection being determined by how closely associated the subjects are with each other, Temporal Sympathy casts across time and the strength of the connection is determined by how different the subject's present self is from the version of the subject you are casting on in the past. Often, the more time has passed, the more the subject will have changed. For example, a 50 year old man probably has a Medium connection to his 5-year-old self, maybe even a Weak connection. On the other hand, a 500 year old rock might have an Unchanged connection with its 50 year old self if it has remained relatively untouched in that time. Conversely, if that rock gets carved into a sculpture tomorrow, that sculpture will probably only have a Weak connection with the rock it was carved from, even though it just happened a day ago.

                        The numbers in the far right column are used as the Withstand when you are casting a spell on a past version of something. For example, if you tripped and broke your arm, and wanted to cast Exceptional Luck on yourself before you broke your arm to hopefully prevent that from happening, the connection would probably be Strong, giving your past self a Withstand of 1 against the spell. Unless you waited until after the injury had time to heal up a bit, in which case you would probably have a Medium connection to yourself before the injury, and the Withstand would be 2. The numbers in the second column from the left are used as the Withstand when you are casting a spell on the Temporal Sympathetic connection between two subjects. For example, if you wanted to make everyone forget the historic significance of the a landmark, you could cast a spell to weaken or destroy its Temporal Sympathy with itself from before it was considered significant. For example, the Liberty Bell probably has Medium sympathy with itself before it was cracked, so that connection would have a Withstand of 2.
                        Of course, that post was written before the errata, so now you would need to also combine the spell you wanted to cast on the past with an appropriate Time spell.
