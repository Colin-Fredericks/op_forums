        Technically speaking, if the Silver Ladder got their way completely and totally, notwithstanding any magical or social complications...

        Anyone who desires Awakening would Awaken. Anyone who didn't have that kind of obsessive magical drive would not be in charge of running reality but would be a citizen - the Ladder and the Free Council are tied for 'most gung-ho about humanity.' All the spirits, the other supernatural splats... well, they're gonna be down and out in Hieraconis, because the Silver Ladder explicitly and ONLY cares about humanity. Vampires would be extinct, werewolves would be employed as enforcers and proxy rulers of the Shadow's spirits at best, and so on. Changelings might get help from the Ladder on the grounds that they are human victims of an outside force, I suppose.

        The Silver Ladder is not, in fact, anti-Sleeper. They just think only the Wise know enough about the universe to make the right decisions - they are quite explicitly interested in building an esoteric variation on Plato's Republic. It's not a democracy, but it is intended to be a well-run society in which humanity rules in splendor over the world. An attractive proposition! Also a wildly hubristic one, since they're not just explicitly utopian, they admit that the current laws of reality don't support the creation of their utopia, which is why the laws must be changed.

        You can't fault them for idealism. You can fault them for pragmatism, and for the classic 'well if we had a perfectly just ruler, why would we need elections?' rhetoric. They are not, in fact, angling to be tin-pot tyrants, any more than the Free Council is advocating for total stateless anarchy.

        Of course, they're a long, long way from any of this coming to fruition.
