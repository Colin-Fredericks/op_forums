    The Argus Protocol, an Obrimos Legacy of counterintelligence agents

    12-03-2023, 03:55 PM
    Origins

    “Security is a process, not a product.”
    -Bruce Schneier

    Nicknames: Datahunters, overseers, peacocks (derogatory), Pinkertons (derogatory), specters, watchmen.
    Parentage: Obrimos, Guardians of the Veil, Seers of the Throne

    Background: The first specters trace their history back to Prague in the early 2010s. Ample investments in the technology sector had catapulted the city into becoming a technological hub, but alongside the innovation came an increase in Abyssal anomalies. The only common factor in all these Mysteries was that they would always have sequences at their core, sometimes hidden in the static of a ghost radio station, in nonsensical Braille letters in a vending machine, as an unforgettable tune, a subliminal message in a movie, etc. Eventually these incidents became common enough to prompt an alliance between the Pentacle and Seers to investigate the matter.

    The first breakthrough in the case was realizing that all the sequences could be mapped to Baudot code, making each anomaly a code module. Whoever the Scelestus causing this was, they were extremely subtle. The Argus Protocol was developed as a counterintelligence doctrine, allowing mages to have a means of quickly perceiving the Abyssal code and surveying the city for the Accursed.

    Eventually the Scelestus Bartmoss would end his test runs, attempting to run a city wide program that would bring a digital Annunaki into the Fallen World. In a city wide operation coordinated by the specters, the Pentacle-Seer alliance was able to disrupt the summoning ritual before completion and capture Bartmoss, sealing away the fragments of his research across different Censorea.

    Ever since, both the Pentacle and Seers have put the specters' expertise to good use, placing them at the forefront of initiatives that require constant vigilance.

    Appearances: Specters often dress in whatever way that allows them to fit in without drawing too much attention, often with surveillance equipment discreetly hidden, some of which they might have developed themselves.

    Depending on the needs of the operation, they may even wear disguises. Some specters prepare outfits that may be donned in less than a minute, studiously honing the sequence of steps in such a way that they can be done without suspicion in public, allowing them to lose surveillance quickly.

    Security is often enhanced in their Sanctums, employing a wide range of methods, from false networks to sophisticated sensor systems, to camera arrays, to something as simple as a trained guard dog, or placing pieces of paper in doors to see if they were opened. The use of furniture with secret compartments is also favored, ensuring sensitive information can be stored without provoking suspicion, with experienced specters teleporting caches in and out of hollowed parts of the furniture.


    Doctrine



    “We always monitor the flow of information, intelligence, and threat streams to see whether we have any indication that there's an imminent one. We work hard to identify potential cells and disrupt them. This is one of the reasons we put so much emphasis on intelligence gathering.”
    -Michael Chertoff

    Prerequisites: Space 2, Investigation 2, Trained Observer •, two dots in one of the following Skills: Computer, Crafts, Empathy, Larceny, Subterfuge.

    Initiation: The mage is given a mission to monitor a possible threat, with the mentor acting as the agent's handler. Success is obtained once they find confirmation whether the threat is real or data that provides an edge against it.

    Organization: Specters often have a loose association with each other, preferring to trade information instead, limiting what secrets leave their own network while simultaneously acquiring new intel. This dynamic extends to technology as well, ensuring that members remain on the bleeding edge of counterintelligence gadgets and software.

    Mentors choose prospective students based on their potential as a future infobroker, building reputation with them to become future preferential customers. The Guardians have also been known to favor recruiting those on missions that require constant oversight, such as protecting a Censorea or tracking down the Scelesti.

    Theory: Security can only be achieved through constant vigilance, one must be able to know the enemy's actions without exposing one's own.

    Information is obtained through tradecraft, by building an intelligence network and utilizing cutting edge technology.

    When the enemy's plans are uncovered, they can be undermined, unraveled, turned to one's advantage. The more valuable secrets one knows, the more favors one can purchase, the more unique resources can be procured and the more robust one's countermoves.

    Uncover the hidden moves, become the nexus through which information flows and wield it to secure the success of your operations.

    Magic

    “To competently perform rectifying security service, two critical incident response elements are necessary: information and organization.”
    - Robert Davis

    Ruling Arcanum: Space
    Yantras: Alternate identity (same as the dots), blueprints of the area (+1, +2 if they reveal secret passages), disguise (+1, +2 if rehearsed the role), giving a signal related to an operation (+1), hacking a network (+2), succeeding in an investigation roll relevant to the spell (+2), retrieving a dead drop (+1), surveillance footage (+1, +2 if relevant to the spell), surveillance gear (+1, +2 if developed by the specter), wall of surveillance/multimedia screens (+2)

    Oblations: Scanning an area for security vulnerabilities, developing new counterintelligence technologies, preparing dead drops, analyzing intelligence data, installing or maintaining a security / surveillance system, meeting with informants, practicing the sequence of steps for a quick disguise, rehearsing a cover identity.

    Attainments

    First: Fly On The Wall
    Prerequisite: Initiation​



    A fundamental operational maxim of counterintelligence is to never look back, because you are never alone. Simultaneously, one must always remain mindful of their surroundings, vigilant for possible threats and key intel. The first thing a specter learns is how to remain alert without betraying hints to others. After spending a scene training situational awareness (identifying entrances and exits, watching people without staring, paying attention to nonverbal cues, etc), the specter becomes able to perceive their surroundings with more clarity than ever before.

    This replicates The Outward & Inward Eye, with Reach assigned to advanced duration.

    Optional: Forces 1

    Across a crowded room or amidst a bustling street, specters are always listening for valuable secrets that are exchanged in hushed conversations.

    When activating the main Attainment, the specter fine tunes their listening to perceive whispered conversations, incoming ambushes and even frequencies beyond the human spectrum.

    This replicates Receiver with Reach assigned to advanced duration.

    Second: Sensor Network
    Prerequisites: Space 2, Investigation 3

    The watchman always knows when their caches are found, dead drops delivered or safe houses breached. After a moment visually scanning a target and committing every detail to memory, the specter is able to install a sensor on them. By utilizing a vast network of sensors, the specter is able to keep on top of the latest developments of a scenario.

    When deployed in an area, these sensors notify the specter when something passes into or out of a sensor's area of effect, such as someone entering the room or one of the items being teleported out.

    When they place the sensor on an object, in addition to its normal functionality, it also allows the specter to know if it was opened, such as when a secret compartment was found, a Grimoire unlocked, a package opened.

    The sensor is only able to perceive information that normal sensory input could. The specter is able to specify up to [Potency] Keys that are exempt from notice. Should the specter set a Key of “my cabalmate Decker”, for example, then anyone that looks like them will be ignored. Should multiple people with his appearance be present, the sensor will not be triggered. The specter’s sensors always call home when activated, letting them know their location.

    This is a custom Knowing of Space, with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration.

    Optional: Forces 2

    The specter’s vision is augmented, allowing them to perceive more than even the most sophisticated vision enhancing goggles would allow. Night vision, thermal imaging, electromagnetic field vision, sound and kinetic energy perception, they are even able to automatically adapt to sensory overload, such as when faced with flashbangs and floodlights. The activation requires the specter to wear sunglasses for a scene, protecting their eyes as they make fine calibrations to their sight.

    This replicates Nightvision with Reach assigned to advanced duration and auto adjusting to extreme stimuli.

    Third: Surveillance Sweep
    Prerequisites: Space 3, two dots in a second Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills

    The watchman can quickly scan their surroundings, allowing them to perform tight, efficient work. Whether searching a room for cameras and microphones, picking out a target from the crowd, verifying if the other party is armed or locating a data drive, they can immediately find whatever they are looking for.

    This replicates Locate Object, with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range and advanced scale.

    Optional: Forces 3

    An experienced specter is aware that mainstream media is influenced by many supernatural agendas, from vampire disinformation campaigns, to the machinations of the God-Machine, Awakened interests and many more. When searching for new leads, one has to turn to tabloid publications, those written by investigative reporters, hunters and those who had recent encounters with the supernatural.

    After spending a scene reading digital tabloids, the specter becomes attuned to the flow of data transmissions around them, even becoming able to decrypt scrambled signals and military grade cyphers at a glance. Their ability to process it is similarly enhanced, allowing them to analyze digital media with meticulous efficiency.

    This replicates Tune In, with Reach assigned to advanced duration, granting 8-again to rolls to parse through digital data and the ability to decrypt the signals.

    Fourth: The All Seeing I
    Prerequisites: Space 4, Investigation 4, Trained Observer •••​



    Some supernatural areas inherently disable technology, others are warded against it, none of it can stop a watchman from gathering intelligence. The specter has learned to become a mobile surveillance network, a living nexus of monitoring spells.

    This replicates Scrying with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range and advanced duration.

    The specter can make the scrying window selectively one-way, allowing only specific people to perceive her from the other side of the window. This is usually utilized to create secure communication channels.

    Optional: Forces 4

    The specter’s eyes see all and reveal all. After spending a scene examining microdots, the specter is able to notice even the smallest details of the world around them, from fingerprints, to individual pieces of hair and chemical residue. Furthermore, their sight range is similarly enhanced, allowing them to see targets miles away. Combined with their omnidirectional vision, it allows them to monitor their surroundings with unmatched detail.

    This replicates Zoom In with Reach assigned to advanced duration, seeing for miles and perceiving dust sized particles.

    Fifth: The Invisible Hand
    Prerequisites: Space 5, two dots in a third Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills​



    Successful execution of a high level counterintelligence operation requires more than robust information, all pieces must fall into place at the right time. The greatest specters are able to easily move key components within the smallest windows of opportunity. Allies can breach a target location in moments, or be exfiltrated immediately, incriminating evidence can be placed in just the right place to have maximum impact, potential threats can be disarmed before engagement, dead drops can be placed or retrieved at a distance, and contacts met on one's own terms.

    This replicates Teleportation with Reach assigned to instant use and sensory range.

    Optional: Forces 5

    All technology that falls under the watchful eyes of a grandmaster specter is a potential asset, all surveillance equipment can be seamlessly incorporated into their own network. A target’s smartphone can be made into a tracker in seconds. With a casual hand gesture, they can disable even the most robust security system, or shut down cameras and microphones, allowing them to conduct meetings unobserved. With a glance they can seize data streams and broadcasts, download the contents of a database or upload their own programs, send custom video messages or overwrite radio broadcasts.

    This replicates Transmission combined with Influence Electricity. Reach is assigned to instant use, sensory range, advanced scale and advanced duration. Two spell factor pseudo dice are used for the combined spell.

    What were you thinking design wise ?

    The first Attainment starts building the feeling that they are always vigilant. They have eyes in the back of their head, are always aware of everything that is being done in the room, know what is happening behind closed doors and are nearly impossible to surprise. The maxim of never looking back comes from The Moscow Rules, which were used by the CIA to infiltrate the Russian capital during the Cold War.

    The first optional Attainment starts building the idea that they are more aware than others. They can hear noises only dogs could, sonars, minute sounds, it makes them often able to pick up on cues that others simply could not. The activation ritual being the same as the main Attainment is just for convenience, rather than being a combined spell.

    The second Attainment is based on the Intrusion Warning spell from the Guardians of the Veil book. Its main function is to allow them to cultivate an air of never being caught off guard. Was the cabal’s Sanctum invaded while they were away ? Did someone just open the train locker with one of their supply caches ? Did the vampires start to close in on them while they were searching for the data ? Now they will always be aware. Since in 1ed there was no indication if the caster knew which alarm triggered, a new tracker system was added, inspired by how, in Justice League Doom, Batman's files called home when decrypted.

    The second optional Attainment allows them to expand their sight even more. Now they can perceive radiation, thermal energy and sound vibrations. Their sight is so acute that they can function perfectly well in the dark, and their eyes adjust to light extremes, making flashbangs effectively useless. Nothing is concealed from their sight. The activation ritual is a reference to a line from the original Deus Ex.

    The third Attainment perfects their ability for surveillance. Within a few moments they know where the person they are looking for is, whether the people they are talking to are armed, detect wires and bugs in the vicinity, locate the document they are searching for and much more.

    The third optional Attainment allows them to monitor all digital communication in the area. Police radio, instant messages, phone calls, audiovisual streams, all are laid bare before their eyes. A free Reach was used for a custom effect where they can decrypt and unscramble signals, which allows them to access even data protected by military grade cryptography with practiced ease, adding to their intimidation factor. Another Reach was used by taking a cue from Craftsman’ Eye, allowing them to efficiently scan through a torrent of data. This reinforces the theme by having the surveillance agent able to parse through multiple screens of data, while also incentivizing them to incorporate digital media into their methods. The ritual to activate it is a reference to the scene in Men In Black where they find a new lead in the tabloids.

    The fourth Attainment gives them the ability to become a walking surveillance network. They can plant Scrying windows in any area, item or individual they can perceive, which can be placed alongside the second Attainment’s sensor to not only know what triggered an alarm, but also get immediate visuals. They can use it to monitor large areas of a city, follow conversations they could before only see or hear whispers of. One of the most convenient aspects is that, since the network depends on them, it makes them into information brokers that are too useful to eliminate.

    The ability to make one way selective screens originally required Fate 2, but having secure communication channels to one’s agents and contacts seemed thematic enough that it felt like it warranted the expanded functionality.

    Depending on the circumstances, they might cast The Outward & Inward Eye with higher Potency and with the 2 Reach effect, so it temporarily overwrites the normal Attainment and allow them to extend their enhanced perceptions across Scrying windows. Given that the Legacy has Obrimos as the parent Path, casting Pierce Deception and True Sight would allow them to further expand their perceptions and filter out any falsehoods.

    The fourth optional Attainment represents a major power spike. When combined with the other perception enhancing Attainments, it allows them to scout in 360 degrees an area measured in miles, with a staggering level of detail. Furthermore, by seeing dust sized particles, they can perceive when their drinks were spiked and even see fingerprints. They can also search an area for important people and objects using Locate Object, as well as setup a Scrying camera or mobile Scrying “drones”, all while being miles away from it. Lastly, they have a vastly expanded range for sensory spells, which is a major advantage.

    The final main Attainment allows them to leverage their expanded perceptions to great strategic effects, by giving them the ability to move themselves and anything else they can perceive within the area. They verified that the negotiators are waiting for them and that they are armed ? Their weapons are now in the motel room the mage rented a mile away, they will fetch a great price. The suspicious informant just arrived at the location ? Now the talks will happen at a table the mage reserved, far away from the meeting point.

    The final optional Attainment allows them to achieve mastery of technological surveillance. They can use it to upload or download data from anywhere they can perceive, giving them access to databases at a distance. They can call anyone within range, or create a custom radio announcement, even talk to them via a screen. When entering an area, they can impose radio silence, only being able to be monitored if they wish it. They can control all electronics and lights, allowing them to plunge an area into darkness or disable even the most modern security system.

    When put together, it makes even meeting them dangerous. In a few moments, the specter will be able to locate everyone’s phone. After doing that he tags the phones, always being able to call them whenever. If the cabal takes too long, they will have set up a Scrying window, now he can always know precisely where they are with a remote viewing Knowing of Space, keep track of all they are doing, and have easy access to a remote casting window. They are the only ones who can hear the changeling’s pacemaker, but it's enough for them to control it, now they are on the Seer’s payroll. They can even use Teleportation to isolate individual members and meet them on their own terms, which has great potential for mind games.

    The original concept for the Argus Protocol was inspired by the NBN faction of Android: Netrunner, a media megacorp that wielded power through monitoring, analytics and manipulation of public opinion. Since their goals aligned well with the Panopticon’s methodology, the core ideas could be used to inspire a Seer Legacy based on the theme of Sinister Surveillance, a by the book application of their methods, with the objective of providing a fun way to create some paranoia fuel for the players.

    When selecting the appropriate skills, the purview of NBN proved too broad. Their journalism angle heavily uses social skills, whereas their surveillance and data mining aspect focuses on mental skills. With this in mind, the focus was given to obtaining the data rather than utilizing it.

    After the Attainments were ready, the toolkit seemed like it would be pretty useful for spy themed characters, so making it available to the Guardians became an additional goal. This was done by changing the initial theme from Sinister Surveillance (or the Panopticon’s maxim of Vision is Power) to Counterintelligence, allowing there to be just enough of an intersection between the goals of both Orders to make it available to both.

    The background is set in the 2010 so that wifi technology is more widely available, ensuring the more advanced Attainments remain useful. Prague served as a setting by being a city with a long history and a tech hub that is less featured, it was also a tip of the hat to Deus Ex: Mankind Divided. The Annunaki is based on Apex from Android: Netrunner, a sentient virus that mixes elements of AM from I Have No Mouth and I Must Scream (which is where the Baudot code reference came from) and a bit of Edgar Alan Poe’s The Tell-Tale Heart. The name of the Scelestus is a reference to Rache Bartmoss, the hacker that caused the DataKrash, a major event in the lore of the Cyberpunk tabletop game. Since the development of the Legacy was done by both the Pentacle and Seers, the more one progresses down the Attainments, the more the names reference Illuminati tropes.

    The organizational structure needed to be something neutral enough that it would work for both Guardians and Seers, so information trading seemed like a good fit. They keep the latest prototypes, the greatest gadgets and the most clever software in the hands of the Legacy. This way they are always benefitting from each other’s skills and contacts.

    In the case of the teacher and student bond, the dynamic is similar to the one between Central and Monst3r in Invisible Inc. They are old acquaintances, with enough respect to keep each other in the loop, trading for the latest gear at a discount. It's not sufficient to easily have their aid in operations, but enough to call in a favor for something major.

    The talks by Jonna Mendez, the former head of disguises of the CIA during the Cold War, served as an inspiration for the appearances, yantras and oblations of the specters.

    Regarding yantras, the hacking one is there to reflect on their creation in the modern era. The blueprint yantra is a callback to the tactical shopping scene in the first John Wick movie. The screen of monitors simply felt thematic, hearkening to the Illuminati ending of the original Deus Ex.

    That concludes the notes. I hope you had as much fun reading them as I did writing them, thank you in advance for the feedback.

    New experiences are the font of creativity, when seeking inspiration, break your routine.
