            The meteor isn't the subject so its Size is irrelevant in this case. Throwing down a campus sized meteor would result in a crater that is many, many times larger than that campus. Think instead that the immediate wave of destruction resulting from a meteor impact should be contained within the AoE. Windows will still shatter far beyond that range, but buildings should still stand.
            Remember that the spell is about making something happen within the AoE, and the meteor is just the delivery. It would be completely different if you designed a Forces/Matter spell that directly controls the meteor (though in that case you would use the Size part of the Scale chart since the meteor is the spell's subject).

                                    Temporal sympathy isn't a mystical bond as much as it is similarity to the self. You could definitely artificially increase it, and maybe decrease it through magic, but you can't transfer temporal sympathy to someone else. You also can't create temporal sympathy between two subjects unless they're actually the same thing at different times in it's personal timeline (though then it would already have temporal sympathy to itself).

                                                Originally posted by thenate View Post
                                                It's a valid plot element if you wanted to run it, though it'd be easiest if you decided that temporal irises are created by archmasters or some esoteric phenomena.
                                                It has to be esoteric phenomena, in Awakening's metaphysics — the many-worlds "anything that can happen happens somewhere" version of alternate timelines is not the default means by which Time works, and the most prominent examples of alternate timelines are maintained by Infrastructure, produced as weird currents in the Ocean of Osiris, or don't-exist as greater Abyssal entities (including the most-developed Annunaki in the setting so far).

                                                By default, if alternate timelines exist as anything more than pockets of weirdness of varying scale, they do so from different configurations of the Supernal than the one a character occupies in the present. Meddling in the Supernal may make it so that things have always been the other way, but if that configuration exists at the same time as another unfolding of events then the one is at a smaller scale than the other.
