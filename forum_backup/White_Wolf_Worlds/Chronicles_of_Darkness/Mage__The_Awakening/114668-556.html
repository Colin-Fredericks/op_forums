                            Originally posted by Cauthon View Post
                            If you have Tune In active, do you need to have Transmission set to Sensory Range in order to affect signals you're receiving?

                            Sometimes, the description for Tune In states "Satellite internet and TV programming, closed walkie-talkies, CB broadcasts, and radio transmissions all become open to her senses, as well as wireless communications.", meaning that the mage can see data being transmitted in the area they are inside (like satellite internet, radio or a wifi network) or things they can observe rather than touch (such as data being sent over cables).
