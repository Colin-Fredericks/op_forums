                        Great Mystery of New England – Spirit of Land
                        There are forces in New England lands. Secret whispers between the sights of wind. The sounds of running river are the same as rhythms of Supernal incantations. Lines of power are crossing land, pointing to forgotten symbols. Immaterial beings are hunting those places for centuries – and yet they work in to something greater, like common mind. Natives American say this land has its own soul. They may not be wrong…

                        From the time first European witches and sorcerers come to the New World, they tried to communicate with this intelligence. It is commonly called „Spirit of Land” – but Awakened are not sure is it spirit, goetia, powerful ghosts – or even just some presence they feel when they are alone in the woods. All they want to be study it. And to get the power from it.

                        Many attempts on this gone badly – Spirit, if it’s even exists, is fighting with mages to not get locked up by them. Salem Witch trials are said to be result of first famous attempt. Hubristic Awakened tried to play with forces they could not understood – and innocent people paid the price. Or at least it’s what Guardians of Veil from New England say.

                        Secret Concord from Boston is to be the latest successive attempt on that. Whoever – no, whatever - is third signer of the document is probably the „The Spirit of Land”. This is why Ebon Noose and White Putnams so much fortune favors them - the land itself helps them to maintain power. Or so rumors in Awakened circles say.

                        Possible solutions to Mystery:
                        • Spirit of the Land – Mysterious being most Awakened sought to know in New England is a powerful Shadows spirit of this Lands. It’s Rank 7 being, on par of Wolf gods the werewolf worship or just a bit below ephemeral beings like Sun or Moon. Many wonder how spirit of surrounding can be this powerful and wonder if Atlantean civilizations that left ruins in New England woods have something to do with it. ( See Boston Unveiled, Chapter Four „Off the Map” )
                        • Bound unbound – Other theory states that this being is Supernal god, thrown into our world when Exarchs took their Thrones. Instead of being bound in some ancient prison, this being freed itself and roam freely the land. It would explained local Seers interest in to phenomena, as one of their most important dogmas it to lock and sentry this kind of beings. It would also clear why some Awakened disappear in the middle of the night when left alone in the woods – Bound are always hungry for Mana, pouncing on anything that has a greater amount of it in his Pattern. Awakened are then ideal meal.
                        • Prisoner from Abyss – Most disturbing theory is that „Spirit” is powerful Acamoth, abyssal being living in Astral Space. Most seek mages to use their Astral link with Abyss to go over their home they were separated eons ago. But the New England being had somehow evolved and can travel between strata of Astral on its own – it lies in Anima Mundi most often, leaving imprint on the land and beings in region. Still, it likes to play with Awakened, offering lore and power. For the price.
                        And what do you think about idea on Great Mystery of Boston and New England? Liked my write-up? Myself, I plan to go with the third variant in my game…

                                Orders of New England 2ED

                                Caucuses
                                Pentacle Order in New England are very loose associations of mages spread around terrain of whole Massachusetts State and some borders counties to it. Proper power of Boston is not very much centralized as inner wars of Bostonian Awakened in XX century and waves of scattered emigrants lead too many willworkers being left alone to their own devises in small towns and settlements of the region. Nominally, Orders local societies are full of various ranks many members across New England take on themselves. Realistically, Boston’s day to day mage community is understaffed and many times isolationists with not enough mages to run smoothly tasks of Orders in the city, because members are scattered on the map of the state.

                                This situation changes when Caucuses are called in. Representatives of whole Order come once a month or bi-monthly meetings, exchange knowledge and power in their secret halls. Then mages of New England looks as proper community and force to be reckoned with. And Favors or knowledge exchanged there make lasting relations with all participants. Even if most mages in New England are only nominally subordinate to the Boston’s Consilium, however, they respond to call of colleagues and friends from the Order of the city itself. Counting on their return of Favor if necessary.

                                Adamantine Arrow: Windham Manor – Holy Ground

                                On a sea cliff in Gloucester perched Windham Manor. A network of caves extends beneath the house, carved into the rock by millions of years of tides. A sprawling graveyard is located at the back of the property, overlooking the water, but it is almost completely overgrown. It’s here where New England’s Arrows meet bimonthly (usually sometime during the third week of the month) on request of Ferrum – Adamant Sage. This place is chosen on the tradition for family ground of Hydra - Sage Emeritus – that’s family and cabal Gravediggers have protected Boston and region for centuries. At least once a year, Ferrum likes to put on an exhibition of the Duel Arcane, inviting any and all mages of the Arrow to observe or, even better, participate.

                                Apollonia from The Brotherhood of the Ineffable Truth is Vidantus – working mostly as teacher of new Arrows, trying to not go for more bloodshed in her life, understanding it’s her time after being defeated by Ferrum and the death of Ares.

                                Free Council: Emerald Scroll Bar – Assembly and Lore House
                                Located at Roxbury neighborhood waterfront, this Tarot’s Major Arcana styled place is open Lore House to any mage that will enter. Leaded by Davy Jones column of Dead Wrens can sell you anything they can get hands on. And make great parties. They even sell alchemical potions as drinks!

                                Emerald Scroll is also Assembly meeting place – treated mostly as Free Council Caucus once a month, in weekend long ‘members only’ event – it’s open on day-to-day basis to any mage that want to trade Mysteries or sell magic for quick cash. “Local” Assembly can be called almost every day, but it’s rulings are taken only to represent Boston mages only interests – Dead Wrens understand that beside them, every few days visits of SMG and city passing Libertine or two – day-to-day rulings of Assembly do not really reflect New England community at large.

                                Note: I want to remake Dead Wrens as Libertines only column – they mix of Orders in 1ED looks as dictated only old stereotype of “Arrow=warriors” or “Mysterium=explorers”. Also, they are pirates and thieves, god damit, how much Nameless can one be more!

                                Mysterium: Academy of the Brotherhood of the Ineffable Truth - Athenaeum

                                Academy apartment building in the Beacon Hill area, with north wing working as sanctum for fellow Arrows from Exalted Knights of the Gryphon to defend the Supernal Mysteries stronghold. As name suggest, this particular Athenaeum works mostly in Academy model (see Mysterium book), teaching newly Awakened under guise of school for “exceptional students”. Of course there is Scriptorium part of complex and giant libraries Athenea are known of. Interesting, there is not pointed obvious Censorium in place – maybe Boston’s Guardians to the Veil are having something to it. As institution, Athenaeum is run by Aurem, one of most dedicated and oldest Mystygoges of Caucus.
                                Mysterium caucuses are convened at 8 P.M. on the 15th of every month (barring emergency), are rigidly ordered affairs. They are run by Potestas – eldest (literally) mage of Mysterium and Hierophant of Order.

                                Guardians of Veil: Order of the Golden Dawn, true Emerald Scroll and Oubliette – Labyrinth

                                Once, Guardians Labyrinth was glorious in Boston – on wave of occult interest in first decades of XX century, it grew and grew, spanning from one secret society to the next. Order of the Golden Dawn split itself in to a lot of cults and sects, each with it slightly different dogma. But each answering to secrets signs of Emerald Scroll – code name for group of Cultors from the Order. Sadly, it’s now only glorious past.

                                Today, Guardians still control many of connections between cults, but after Free Council revolt – and even Libertines claiming the name for secret cabal as bar slogan! – It’s much, much weakened. Restoring Labyrinth to its former splendor is one of priorities for Shadow Chorus and its leader Culsu – Epopt of the Guardians. Overwatching Boston Consilium after „disappearance” of Nemean is second. Both they oversee from their secret sanctum called Oubliette, door’s to one can open anywhere in the New England. Once a month, in the settlement of whole Massachusetts, portals to it are open and Guardians come to plan their next moves in reclaiming the region and safe keeping the magic. Februs, Culsu’s cabal mate, is appointed Interfector of the Caucus.

                                Silver Ladder: Cryptopoly

                                Note: I was thinking about making Deacon someone other than Khumeia, but as I see in Boston Unveiled, there are not any other Silver Ladder candidates for this. Even Khumeia cannot be Deacon because she would need to be Magister first, by Silver Ladder book’s standards. Without this, Silver Ladder sounds more as collection of individuals than proper Order.

                                    Consilium of Boston 2ED
                                    Boston’s Ruling Council is in flux. Two years after sudden “disappearance” of previous Hierarch – infamous Nemean – there is slow power shift and still real change cannot be made in Consilium. Mysterious event is less than a puzzle for most of Awakened citizens of Boston, as there were several tries to overthrow a tyrant – so almost everyone assume he get what he deserved. Rumors says that Silver Ladder Convocation has done something to him, but on day to day life in Boston, most do not care about it. Pentacle mages are just happy that monster stepped down from the throne. Councilors are in fighting against one another on words, looking for weakness to exploit in the race to the now vacant seat of Hierarch.

                                    It all comes from the simple fact that previous leader of Council run it with iron grip where he found it fit. Nemean maintained his power largely through a combination of scare tactics and a hands-off philosophy of rule. He normally roused to act only by immediate and forceful threats to his authority. When such a threat emerges, however, he used the massive power bloc encompassed by the Secret Concord (about a third of then Boston’s Consilium willworkers) to leverage such action as he desires, usually action of a harshly punitive variety.

                                    Now, system is slowly degenerating. Or remaking, as others would say. With rumors of her participation in getting rid of Nemean, the ambitious Khumeia assumed the position of Councilor in few months after. Chain Parris also rose on absence of former Hierarch, going from his Provost to proper Council member. This shacked decade’s long status quo, were two new Councilor hailing from the Boston proper area and no one from Salem. Cormant House hadn’t been used for Ruling Council meetings and more and more Ebon Noose is worrying if White Putams are not preparing to change conditions of Secret Concord. With each passing week details of the Concord and the identity of mysterious third signer of it are more and more hot topic in backstage of Pentacle meetings. It looks that Ebon Noose was not thrown out of Consilium only because it has so many combat skilled mages under lead of Anacaona de Xaragua. But the upcoming elections for Hierarch seat in the Council can change everything...
