    Jack Viper – Pitying Poisoner

    Little is known with certainty about this ghost, and he isn't confirming or denying anything if he can help it. Most historians among Sleepers agree that he was born to a mentally ill prostitute in London, sometime during the middle of the 19th century. His mother gave birth to him inside a mental asylum, and his father could not be found, nor could his mother be convinced to give any coherent information about how she became pregnant. Jack Viper's real name is likewise unknown, and most historians speculate that his mother's condition compromised her memory, which led her to give her son various names as if suffering from early dementia.

    The ghost doesn't demonstrate any preferences for how to be addressed, and shows an ambivalent attitude toward his almost mythic name. Despite his background, he managed to lead a normal life by the standards of his society, and demonstrated an abnormally high talent for the medical field, especially toxicology and pharmacology. Records claim he would frequently visit his mother in the asylum, but after one of his visits, his mother displayed a brief capacity to remember everything perfectly and demonstrated a type of happiness and calmness previously considered impossible for her by the asylum's staff. She died shortly afterward, with the alleged cause of death being a mistake committed by one of the nurses when administering her various medications.

    Jack Viper, if he was indeed only one man rather than a conglomeration of hearsay and unreliable sources, supposedly caused his mother's death by giving her an untested medication meant to cure her mental illness so she'd remember who her son was. Legend has it that the accidental death he caused while trying to help his mother ended up serving as the initial motivation for him to seek additional, unwilling test subjects in order to perfect his medication and cure all mental illnesses. However, Jack Viper at one point left too many corpses of important people in his wake, and the policemen of London eventually cornered him in his hideout.

    Most of the policemen either died as a result of exposure to chemical traps, or developed incurable forms of psychosis. Jack Viper committed suicide before being taken to prison by drinking an instantly fatal poison from an assassin's teapot, which became the Anchor of his ghost. Many years later, an Acanthus of the Guardians Of The Veil who goes by the Shadow Name of Nostradamus acquired this Anchor and turned the ghost into his Familiar. The two of them cooperate on solving mysteries and crimes both mundane and supernatural, and although their relationship is strictly professional, it shows hints of ideological friction. Nostradamus intends to use his magic in order to develop ways of accurately predicting and reliably stopping all crimes, ideally in such a way that the culprit is publicly caught in the act. Jack Viper does not explain his motivations for such cooperation, and usually restricts himself to smiling vacantly, offering tea and insisting that there is no need to worry.

    Quote: "They were too beautiful, too captivating, too pure."

            Marconi, Machine's Best Friend

            Virtue: Loyal
            Vice: Mischievous
            Rank: 2
            Attributes: Power 4, Finesse 4, Resistance 6
            Willpower: 10
            Pyros: 15
            Initiative: 10
            Defense: 4
            Speed: 18
            Size: 3
            Corpus: 9
            Armor: 2/2
            Influences: Computers 2, Happiness 1
            Ban: Must leave behind dog-themed and alchemy-themed digital files on any computer he affects with his Influences, Manifestations or Numina
            Bane: Strong Magnets
            Manifestation: Materialize, Collapsible, Digitalize
            Numina: Aggressive Meme, Blast (Electricity), Occult Sight, Mortal Mask

            Notes: Marconi is considered Materialized by default, instead of existing naturally within the Twilight State. Besides digital technology, Marconi treats Prometheans and any other entity, object or location with Pyros as a source of the Resonance Condition, which he can feed from once per day with the usual roll of Power + Finesse, as if he was a Spirit. He regains Willpower from his Virtue and Vice as if he was a mortal. He can communicate verbally, although his understanding of various concepts is still dog-like. Such communication also triggers Disquiet, using Radioheart's own Azoth as the dice pool. He generates the same type of Disquiet as an Unfleshed.

            Whenever Radioheart regains Pyros, he can reflexively transfer any amount to Marconi, as long as the latter is within Radioheart’s immediate vicinity and perceptible. When using Mortal Mask, Marconi does not assume the form of a mortal, he assumes the form of a normal dog. Without the Mortal Mask, he is obviously a robotic canine, but not overtly supernatural. Marconi does not heal Lethal or Aggravated Damage over time, and instead relies on the Digitalize Manifestation or Repairs [Intelligence + Crafts] with an effective Equipment Bonus equal to his Rank due to the significantly modular and streamlined nature of his supernatural hardware and software.

            The Repairs take 1 hour per -1 on the Repairs roll, and each -1 taken removes 1 Lethal Damage. Healing Aggravated Damage via Repairs requires 1 Pyros for each 1 point of Aggravated Damage. Marconi is not affected by Disquiet from Radioheart, but can be affected by Disquiet from other Prometheans. Marconi has General and Ballistic Armor equal to his Rank for free, but does not go into hibernation like a Spirit does when his Corpus is filled with Aggravated Damage, he is simply destroyed.

            The Digitalize Manifestation requires 1 Pyros and lets him exist as an intangible entity of information that can travel along digital networks. This lasts for hours equal to Successes on his Power + Finesse roll, and during each hour he simply inhabits an active digital device but does nothing except rest, he heals 1 Lethal Damage. During this period, he can be detected via suitable programs and might register as malware, but any mundane attempts to detect or remove him suffer a dice penalty equal to his Successes on his Power + Finesse roll, and supernatural attempts trigger a Clash Of Wills against his Resistance + Rank. The Collapsible Manifestation lets Marconi reflexively assume the form of any mundane digital device of up to Size 3 with an Equipment Bonus equal to his Rank, and he can spend Pyros to use his Influence over Computers on himself while in Collapsible Form.

            The dog playfully bites Krieger's ankle and he feels a brief electric shock. Nothing intense enough to hurt, but it certainly startles him due to how damn fast the dog is with his bite.​

            After rather intense and focused work, Krieger decides to take a break and switch brain into dispersed mode. For some time, he wanders around his Sanctum, consciously removing work information from his brain. It kinda doesn't work, so he decides to study Marconi in detail to get some now insights into advanced robotics. After getting some more coffee (damn, he really needs to Perfect coffee maker some time), he goes looking for the dog.

            Krieger remembers Radioheart mentioning off-hand that Marconi is a mischievous dog, so it is entirely possible that the robotic friend is hiding in plain sight, ready to pull a prank on Krieger.
            But how would such a large and distinctive-looking entity even hide?

            Krieger sips the coffee. It's obvious that this dog have some kind of stealth field. Or chameleonic surface. Or... wait, it kinda was built to be compact, wasn't it? What if?...
            Krieger starts looking around for brand new objects he never seen around before.

            Krieger doesn't have to look very long: there is a toaster on the hologram table that the Cabal uses for elaborating strategies. Its visuals contrast very starkly with the futuristic Sanctum, it looks a lot more like something from the 1950s. Krieger is tired to the point that he cannot tell with 100% certainty if this toaster was ever there before, but chances are that Marconi is bad at hiding.

            - Found you! - Krieger pokes at the toaster, wondering, if his tired mind doesn't play tricks on him now.

            The toaster responds with a "ching" noise and two slices of toasted bread. No barking, though. There appears to be something written on them. How? Krieger doesn't remember installing any toasters with graphic design functions...

            One toast has "#1" written on it, and says "look at #2". The other toast has "#2" written on it, and says "look at #1".

            It appears that Marconi has a goofy sense of humor. He tries to sneak up on Krieger from behind while he is looking at the so-called brain-frying paradox, but accidentally slips on a puddle of coffee that Krieger forgot to clean up and reveals his position.

            "Oh... you caught me. Well, I'll get you next time!"

            - You can talk? You are better than most dogs I know, then. - Krieger sips coffee again. - Do you often succeed at startling people?

            "Of course I can talk! I just don't do it often because it messes with people's heads, but Umbra took care of that. She also cured my fever! She is a very nice, clever and pretty lady, and I like her very much! She's almost as cool as Radioheart, and that's no mean feat! Hey, you got something crunchy around here? I'll startle anyone you want if you give me a nice toy to chew on!"

            - Yeah, people are easily impressed with talking dogs. Do you eat organic food too? Or you eat metal?

            "I can eat anything that doesn't immediately kill me. I could eat rotten meat but not a grenade, for example."

            - So you are like your friend, then? Did you escaped along with him?

            "I think I am like him, yes. But he found me looking for food in a dumpster. I was scared of him at first because I thought he was a human, but he sang to me to calm me down, and it worked like a charm. We've been best friends since!"

            - Are you scared of humans, then?

            "Yes, terribly so! But Radioheart told me that he is doing a thing called a Pilgrimage. Some sort of spiritual journey to become a human. He says that it is possible I can go on my own Pilgrimage, but I don't know if I would turn into a normal dog, or a really smart dog who talks."

            - Hmm. Losing sentience wouldn't be a great result of such grand journey. Where did he learned about this traveling thing? Do they know anything about what happens with animals like you at the end?

            "He says he gets these visions and dreams sometimes. And no, I don't think he knows. My hunch is that I will only become the best version of myself when he completes his journey. But that's OK! I love him to bits and I'll always be there for him!"

            - Visions and dreams? That's interesting. You want to play hide and seek again?

            "You ask me if I want to play! Do Mages like Mysteries?"

            - Some of them hate them, you know. Broken ones. - Krieger sips the coffee again and puts down the cup. - You want to hide or you want to seek?

            "Let's change things around, you hide! How much time you want to find a spot?"

            - Give me a minute or so. That sounds fair to you?

            "Sure! I'm gonna close my eyes now. Get ready!"

            - And to make things more interesting... Supreme Light-Bending! - with those words, Krieger pictures light bending around him with clever use of metamaterials and makes himself disappear from plain sight. After that, he tries to find a place with a lot of sounds and be very quiet here.

            After a minute passes, Krieger can faintly hear Marconi sniffing and snooping around. The robot dog grows increasingly confused, but no less enthusiastic. When he is running out of ideas, he sits and makes whirring and beeping noises.

            "Think, Marconi, think..." He delves deeper into the Sanctum, and comes back with a water pistol in his third pair of limbs. He is listening intently for any possible noises, firing water here and there. It seems that Marconi is assuming that a layer of water molecules would refract the light in such a way as to give away Krieger's position, which demonstrates that the robot dog has some understanding of physics.

            Now that's a smart dog. Damn, is there any mystical dog shelter? I'd like to adopt one. - thinks Krieger, while trying to figure out how to not get smacked by water pistol. Trying to bend the light again? That's going to be pretty complex... Instead, Krieger pictures a portable gravity generator, able to control the vector. After inserting the gear and turning the device "on" in his mind, Krieger slowly changes the vector, lifting off and drifting to the ceiling. He hopes that Marconi won't go there to look and does have some 2D thinking flaws. He has four legs, after all.

            "You are very good at this! But I won't give up just yet! If you aren't on the ground, then by logical extension you are above me!"

            With internal shrug, Krieger floats up to the middle of the room and starts rotating vector slightly to keep himself in the middle.

            Marconi looks around inside drawers and cabinets, until he finds a rubber ball. "Ah-ha! Prepare to meet your doom, Krieger!" He tosses the rubber ball with a surprising amount of force, which starts bouncing around wildly.

            Now that's just nasty! - Krieger thinks. He is trying to put his intuition and knowledge of physics to trying to guess the trajectory of the ball and keep himself away from it with gravity control.

            Krieger easily dodges the rubber ball. Marconi watches it slowly lose momentum, completely enraptured by the crazy trajectories. After some time, he looks around and does a shrug-like gesture.

            "Well, I can't explode our home just to find you! I surrender, you win!"

            - You were close. - Krieger drops the invisibility and slowly goes back to the ground before dismissing gravity control. - I kinda expected you to find me by smell of coffee, to be honest.

            "...that smell was you? I thought it was one of those things which tried to eat Radioheart once," Said Marconi semi-jokingly.

            - Which things? And yes, my smells are coffee, dust, metal and those weird living things you can find in roaches and eat. Remember that if you'll ever need to find me by smell! - says Krieger also semi-jokingly.

            "I don't know what that thing was called, where it came from, or anything like that. But it looked like a statue at first."

            - Huh. Now that's scary stuff. I once made living metal statue going haywire. Tried to kill me before I melted it down in a hurry. - Krieger winces. - Now it's your turn to hide. How much time do you need?

            "A minute. Go ahead and start counting!"

            Krieger starts counting to 60 seconds.

            After the countdown is over, Krieger looks through the Sanctum for any object which sticks out from the rest. He is not finding anything with the naked eye alone, but Marconi did not give any impression of expecting Krieger to not use magic.

            - Now, now, how can I find one smart little dog... Analyze Frequencies! - Krieger declares in High Speech, extending his hearing range into ultra- and infrasound frequencies. He tries to find some unusual sounds which could be made by Marconi's inner mechanisms.

            Krieger hears some unusual sounds coming from the cafeteria section, little beeping and whirring noises. As Krieger focuses on the source, he has the impression it is coming from somewhere above him.

            Krieger goes to cafeteria section and grabs another cup of coffee, making impression of not having a clue where to look, while actually listening closely to sounds above. Is it Marconi?

            Krieger hears a very faint canine equivalent of laughter coming from above him. That and the familiar noise of helices turning.

            Krieger looks upward and points at source of the laughter.

            - I caaaaaaaan heeeeeeeeaaaaaar youuuuuuuu!

            Marconi has adopted the form of a semi-transparent and toy-like drone, the sort that children play with via remote control.

            "Ah, drat. I let my excitement get the best of me yet again!" He slowly descends to the floor and turns into a dog again. "You were a good sport about this, thank you very much."

            - And so were you. Laughing is the part that got you. I didn't get the chance to study you in detail, will you allow me to do so?

            "But of course. As long as I remain in one piece by the end of it. You won't be using me for any crazy and highly dubious experiments, will you?"

            - Not without your consent, no. And I won't even use a plasma torch! Just look at you weirdly and drool a bit.

            "Drool? That's weird, I thought I was the dog in this situation. You might wanna ask Reptilian to fix your salivation glands. Anyway, go ahead and scan me."

            Krieger begins to Scrutinize him with Focused Mage Sight.

            Krieger sees that Marconi is not just an alchemical construct; he is a genuine living being whose inner workings simply happen to obey principles of metal, silica and plastic. He possess a potential for evolution and adaptation, albeit interlinked with the spiritual development of Radioheart. It is safe to assume that if Radioheart were to die or become irrevocably lost, Marconi would stagnate until he found another Promethean to bond with. It is almost as if whatever entity/force/system which is responsible for animating Prometheans has created Marconi specifically to be an incentive for Radioheart to become human. If this is true, other such sidekicks and pets might exist out there, which implies some degree of intent or purpose behind the Divine Fire. And if that phenomenon is capable of communication with humans in a decipherable manner, a Mage like Krieger might stand to learn unprecedented secrets about artificial intelligence if he can manage to contact an avatar of the Divine Fire.

            -Hmm. You like Radioheart, do you?

            "Absolutely, without a shadow of doubt! He is my very best friend! When I was sick, he would do his best to fix me, and would cry into the little hours of the morning for not finding a cure. Even though he has only a backpack of possessions, he is always looking for things to give to me as toys. He taught me all he knows about humans and is an inspiration to me!"

            - Well, stick to him. He needs you more than you or he think. You are something of a catalyst for him to grow, understand? You mentioned he has visions?

            "Well, yes. But you are better served asking him about those visions, don't you agree?"

            - Yes, but I am not asking about content of visions. How does he looks like when he has them? Did you noticed something strange about him?

            "There is not much of a pattern, I am afraid. Sometimes he looks utterly terrified, other times it looks like he is completely at peace with himself and the universe. But these visions never seem to lead him astray, even if they cause him a lot of pain in some way or another."

            - Yeah, okay. - Krieger rubs his chin. - You are a good dog. Keep it this way, will you?

            "Affirmative! Like that one movie says, all dogs go to heaven. But it would be terribly lonely up there without Radioheart."

            - Nah, I am pretty sure you'll end up wherever he goes. - Krieger stands up. - Thank you. I'd wish I had a dog like you. Radioheart is terribly lucky in his own way.

            After this, Krieger returns to his work on AI prototype.​​

            Bye.

                            Zappy (Digital-Spirit)

The Reptilian employed Zappy to monitor the Internet and its important Resonances inside Seattle, especially those pertaining to misinformation regarding the Wonders that the Making Science Cabal was inventing to share with Sleepers. Making Science had the shared goal of challenging the influence of local Pylons of Ministries such as Pantechnicon, Mammon and Panopticon, who sought to hijack scientific progress of humanity in the name their corresponding Exarchs.

                                Turns out the notes I had on this character were very vague, and he didn't really make an on-screen appearance. So I expanded him into a revised iteration.

                                Quincy Jones - Salakhiel, The Pardon Of God

                                Quote: "Across time and space, lost people are trying to find unreal homes for broken hearts. The symphony of the spheres grows quiet in anticipation of something grandiose, for better or worse."

                                A Messenger Integrator+Tempter whose vision of Hell is "Bring Our Heavenly Father Out Of His Coma So He Can Behold Our Greatest Work". He takes it as a matter of unhinged, ramshackle faith that the God-Machine must be benevolent, because if Quincy was made in His image and only wishes to help humanity, then by logical extension the God-Machine must be a force of benevolence if His works are seen from a cosmic perspective that accounts for all the relevant factors. However, Quincy has no intent of forcibly subjugating humanity in the name of Our Father. He believes Earth MUST become something that makes Our Father feel proud of us as a species, instead of just another failure to be relegated to the Apocalypse Vault. Humanity must choose salvation, otherwise Earth is but a scientific experiment with diminishing results and whose upkeep of human losses cannot be justified.

                                Therefore, Quincy continues his Angelic mission of inspiring forgiveness between humans so they can stop warring and instead collaborate on turning Earth into the best possible approximation of Heaven. His central message is that cooperation is destined by God's will to triumph over subjugation, but God is in a coma and humanity is effectively on its own, and prone to repeating mistakes that are accumulating into a potential extinction. This means that Quincy is an alarmist and insecure person who seeks constant reaffirmation that there is still hope for mankind to forgive itself. Therefore, he has the Virtue of Merciful, but has the Vice of Doubtful. His Catalyst is Causality in the sense that he is spreading a message of his own, because he deep down suspects that maybe Our Father will never wake up from His coma, which means forgiveness must be kept alive in the hearts of mankind despite the final absence of God.
