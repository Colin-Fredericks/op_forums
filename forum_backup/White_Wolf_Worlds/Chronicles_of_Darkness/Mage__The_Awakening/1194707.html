    [NPC] The Superhero Spirit

    03-12-2018, 10:52 PM



    Aegis, Unconquered And Incorruptible
    Rank: 5 (Spirit)
    Attributes: Power 15, Finesse 13, Resistance 15
    Willpower: 10
    Essence: 50
    Initiative: 15
    Defense: 13
    Speed: 178 (Species Factor 150)
    Size: 6
    Corpus: 21
    Influences: Courage 3, Justice 3
    Manifestation: Twilight Form, Claim, Fetter, Materialize, Possess, Unfetter
    Numina: Aggressive Meme, Armored*, Awe, Blast (Solar Vision), Fist Of The Hero*, Heal*, Host Jump, Mortal Mask, Occult Sight*, Omen Trance, Regenerate, Seek, Teleportation*, Unyielding Steps Of Conviction*, Price Of Power* x 2
    Ban 1: Cannot refuse to save an innocent life in danger within his presence
    Ban 2: Must keep the true identities of those it Claims a secret from those who wish them harm
    Bane 1: A weapon used to murder a great hero in cold blood in front of their friends while their back was turned
    Bane 2: The badge of an important authority figure who turned to a life of crime due to a horrible tragedy and never looked back

    *Armored: A spirit with this Numen is difficult to injure, and are often manifest impressively armored forms. The Spirit spends 1 Essence point to gain Armor equal to its Rank until the end of scene. This armor does not apply against the Spirit's Bane.

    *Fist Of The Hero: Aegis' melee attacks deal supernatural damage, ignore all mundane Durability/Armor and have a Lethal Damage Bonus equal to his Rank, which can become Bashing at will. Whenever he fills someone's health with bashing or lethal damage, he may prevent them from falling unconscious or bleeding out, respectively. Whenever he attacks, he may spend 1 Essence and roll Power + Finesse vs Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance. If he wins, the target immediately suffers the Beaten Down Tilt, even if they would normally be immune to it.

    *Heal: The entity can heal others’ wounds, typically for a hefty price. This Numen costs 1 Essence and a Power+ Finesse roll to activate. Each success heals 2 bashing damage or 1 lethal damage. Two Successes can be used to mend aggravated damage, but each point of aggravated damage cured costs 2 additional points of Essence. This power may also be used to fight off disease or the effects of poison — each Success adding to the subjects’ resistance rolls on a one-to-one basis.​

    *Occult Sight: Aegis is sensitive to supernatural phenomena. He notices the active use of supernatural abilities within the range of his senses and may make Perception rolls to approximate their distance and direction. In addition, by spending a point of Essence, he can see through powers of concealment, illusion and deceit with a Clash Of Wills using his Power + Finesse as his dice pool. Lastly, he always knows when he is being targeted by a supernatural power and may mimic the Aura Reading Supernatural Merit, but without drawback.

    *Teleportation: When the entity moves, it may reflexively spend 1 Essence to vanish and reappear at another location within its line of sight up to its (Rank x Speed) in yards away. Doing so adds +2 to its Defense for the turn. If the entity has the Seek Numen, it may teleport to the nearest location with a suitable Condition by spending 3 Essence. The entity can spend an additional 2 points of Essence to take a passenger with them (an unwilling passage may resist with a roll of Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance).

    *Unyielding Steps Of Conviction: Should the target have any supernatural means of protection, escape or mind control, Aegis triggers a Clash of Wills using his Power + Finesse. On a Success, the opposing power is canceled for the scene.

    *Price Of Power: By taking on an additional Ban or Bane, an entity can increase its repertoire of abilities. Each additional Ban or Bane can give it one of the following.

    * Two Numina, Influence dots, or Manifestations.
    * An increase of its maximum Essence pool by 5.
    * A Dread Power.

    An entity may take this Numen a number of times equal to its Rank.


    NEW MYSTERY CULT: The Fan Club Of Aegis

    1st Dot: Allies 1 (Vigilantes).

    2nd Dot: Investigative Aide for Investigation, Empathy or Streetwise.

    3rd Dot: Iron Will. This disregards the prerequisites.

    4th Dot: Inspiring. This disregards the prerequisites.

    5th Dot: The Transformation Bracelet of Aegis, which gives one the Omen Sensitivity Supernatural Merit (using Resolve instead of Wits if higher) and allows one to summon and get Claimed by him with an extended Resolve + Composure roll requiring 1 minute per roll and 10 Successes. This Claiming happens instantly, is temporary and causes Breaking Points as normal. And so might the acts one performs in the name of heroism while Claimed by Aegis. He may use Seek and Host Jump Numina to instantly transfer the Claimed Condition to anyone who also possesses this Relic within the appropriate radius.
    Last edited by GibberingEloquence; 03-16-2018, 12:55 AM.

    Bye.
