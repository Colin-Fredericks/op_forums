            Zéteó Theriaca

            He was talented chemist, worked for the East India Trading Company even. But as I hear it, he came back a few months ago, and has locked himself in the family estate, only coming out to order rare ingredients.

            Better Living Through Chemistry

            There have always been those that seek knowledge of root and stem. In time, one group rose to prominence in a ancient land and traveled to others who plied similar trades. Eventually a number of nameless Herbalists arose from these and put their trust in a great library in Alexandria. From the ashes, the nascent order took it upon themselves to carry their legacy and declared themselves apart from the others. They shunned both the servants of tyrant gods and those who sought control them. Eventually they found allies in other outcasts and began to expand, drawing knowledge from anywhere they could to expand their arts. Eventually, members of the legacy were caught up into the Nameless War, and when it became clear that their allies would side with the Diamond, those same Members refused to follow. This rift eventually resulted in first word, then fist, and finally fire. For the second time, Fire robbed the legacy of it's home, and of it's strength. the few that remained fully threw their support into the Free Council, knowing full well that to do otherwise would invite destruction. And yet, the servants of the lie have learned the legacy's secrets, far more detailed than the mere theft of a soul stone would allow... And something blocks that Fateful day from Time, so none may see the Truth, whatever that may be.

            Origins

            Parentage: Moros, Free Council, Nameless, Seers
            Background: Zéteó Theriaca primarily pull their members from those with backgrounds in pharmaceutical chemistry, herbalism, and mortal alchemy.
            Appearances: Many are warped and twisted by the strange chemicals running in their veins, with signs of their preferred method of delivery.
            Nickname: Blights, Herbalist

            Doctrine

            Prerequisites: Life 2, Matter 2, Medicine 2, Science 2, Occult or Crafting 1
            Initiation: Consumption and survival without magic of a mix of nightshade, lotus root, and Lunargent.
            Organization: Pre-Great Refusal: A council overseen by the strongest legacy member. After Great Refusal: Master Student chains
            Theory: Certain Materials when consumed or with exposure, effect the body and mind. The Tamers of Roots brew potions that elevate these effects to a wonderous degree.

            Magic
            Ruling Arcanum: Life
            Yantras: mundane chemical or alchemical ingredients +1; supernatural alchemical ingredients +2; consumption of a prepared draught +1 (+2 if made with supernatural ingredients)
            Oblations: tending to a garden; studying chemical formula; self-experimentation; spending at least a hour preparing a concoction

            Attainments

            First: Taste the Root
            Prerequisites: Initiation.
            The Herbalist can spend a scene to prepare a concoction to open up their inner eye to the secrets of the alchemical universe, after which the Herbalist can determine the alchemical properties of plant and mineral with naught but a simple examination. The effects of the concoction last for the lower of the Herbalist's Life or Matter dots on the Advanced Duration chart, and the examination requires an instant action and tasting or otherwise handling the examined substance.
            This insight allows the Herbalist to replicate the feats of Mortal Alchemists, the Herbalist treats the lower of their Life or Matter dots as dots in any specialized form of Alchemy available to Mortals such as the Ascending One's Elixir Endowment or the Magnitude trait of the Alchemists described in Promethean: the Created 2e. If the Herbalist already has such a Merit, use the Merit's value if higher and refund the experience per Sanctity of Merits if lower.

            Second: Internal Alchemy

            Prerequisites: Life 2, Matter 2, Medicine 3

            The Herbalists consume and use toxic materials on a daily basis, immunity to the negative effects of their draughts. The Herbalist is immune and this Attainment grants the Herbalist resistance to the negative conditions caused by their creations: they are immune to bashing damage caused by handling or consuming caustic or toxic materials, and reduce lethal damage by their Matter dots. The Herbalist can also attempt to immediately flush their system of toxins, spending an Instant action to roll immediately to resist the toxin's effects, they add their Life dots to rolls to resist the effects this roll.
            This Attainment negates any backlash or physiological drawback to consuming a potion derived from Mortal Alchemy, but not any mystical backlash or drawback.

            Third: The Bitter Draught

            Prerequisites: Life 3, Science 3, Crafts 2 or Occult 2
            The Herbalist may spend a scene preparing a concoction, The herbalist chooses a number of effects from the below list equal to their dots in Life to affect the potion's imbiber, the imbiber may Withstand the transformation with Stamina if they are unwilling:
            • Breathing: Each rank slows down the subject’s breathing, halving Halves the amount of oxygen she the Herbalist needs to function normally.
            • Heartbeat: By slowing down Slows the subject’s Herbalist's heartbeat, she can double doubling the interval at which a toxin affects her.
            • Metabolism: Regulating the subject’s Regulates the Herbalist's metabolism allowing her to subsist on half as much food, and doubling the amount of time between checks for deprivation or fatigue.
            • Reflexes: Add +1 to the Herbalist’s Initiative.
            • Scent: The mage Herbalist can change the subject’s her scent to any that her body could naturally produce, eliminating (or increasing) body odors, controlling pheromone release.
            • Healing: Halve the healing time for bashing and lethal damage.
            The effects of this potion last for an amount of time equal to the Herbalist's Life dots on the Advanced Duration chart.

            If the potion consumed by anyone other than the Herbalist that created it, it instead acts as a deadly poison, with strength equal to the effect's potency of (1 + lower of Life or Matter dots)/2 and a duration based on the Mage's Life dots on the Advanced Duration chart, rolling every Lethal healing interval.

            This attainment functions as the spell Body Control with the Permanence Attainment and 2 reach towards lethal healing.

            Optional: Matter 3
            The Herbalist may spend scene and a point of Mana to prepare a concoction capable of transforming whoever drinks it. This potion may be exist for Life steps on the advanced duration chart before losing it's potency. Once taken the transformation begins in the subject gains Life ranks in which to gain effects. These effects are:
            • Claws: The subject gains savage claws, granting her a +1 weapon bonus.
            • Environmental Adaptation: Like creatures adapted for life deep underwater or inhospitable deserts, the subject gains the ability to survive pressure, extreme heat or cold (up to level 4 environments), and arid or moisture-heavy environments. This effect may be chosen multiple times, each time choose a different environment.
            • Gills/Lungs: The subject may breathe underwater (or in air, if normally aquatic) freely.
            • Immunities: The subject gains resistance to toxins or poisonous materials, like a rat’s ability to devour even rotten food unharmed or herbivores’ ability to fully digest plant matter. This effect may be chosen multiple times, each time choose a different immunity.
            • Leaping: Gaining powerful legs like a frog’s or kangaroo’s, the target gains a bonus to jumping rolls, increasing the distance leaped by each success on the Athletics roll.
            • Prehensile Tail: Gain a bonus to rolls related to balance or climbing equal to Potency.
            • Scaly Armor/Thick Hide: The subject gains 2/1 armor (must be at least Size 2).
            • Senses: The subject gains a new sense, such as echolocation, primitive infravision, or a keen sense for vibrations, granting a bonus to Perception rolls and the ability to perceive phenomena outside the human senses. This effect may be chosen multiple times, each time choose a different sense.
            • Size: Each level of ranks either adds or subtracts one from the subject’s Size. If reduced to Size 0, subject can be made roughly the size of a dime. The subject can grow to a size of up to 1 + (the lower of the Herbalist's Life or Matter dots)/2 on the Advanced Scale chart.
            • Tentacles: The subject may make grappling attempts against targets up to her Size in feet away.
            • Venom: The subject gains fangs or small stingers (damage bonus 0) capable of injecting venom with a Toxicity equal to the Herbalists Life dots. Must grapple first unless a second level of adaptation is taken. If taken with Tentacles, the Venom may be delivered automatically by any successful move during a grapple.
            • Wall-Crawling: The subject (which can be no larger than Size 7) gains the ability to climb on surfaces without equipment
            • or even handholds, provided the surface can bear her weight.
            This transformation last a scene. The potion, if unconsumed, retains its efficacy for the Herbalist's Matter dots on the Advanced Duration chart.

            Fourth: The Putrid Serum
            Prerequisites: Life 4, Medicine 4.
            The Herbalist may spends a scene and a point of Mana to prepare a concoction that enhances the Herbalist's physical attributes. When creating the concoction the Herbalist assigns 1 + Life dots/2 potency to up to two physical Attributes, drinking this concoction increases the Herbalist's Attributes by the chose potency for an amount of time equal to the Herbalist's Life dots on the Advanced Duration chart and can increase the Attributes above the Herbalist's normal limit. Drinking a second concoction ends the effects of the first and applies the new Attribute bonuses, however the duration of the effect is measured from the creation of the concoction, not its consumption.
            If the potion is consumed by anyone other than the Herbalist that created it, it instead acts as a deadly poison, with strength equal to the effect's potency of (1 + lower of Life or Matter dots)/2 and a duration based on the Mage's Life dots on the Advanced Duration chart, rolling every Lethal healing interval.
            This Attainment replicates the effects of Honing the Form with the Permanence Attainment, the ability to boost attributes above the normal maximum, and the ability to increase two attributes.

            Optional: Matter 4
            The Herbalist can spend a scene and a point of Mana to creates a similar, but more potent and shorter lasting, concoction than the above. Rather than a long lasting, but small, boost to their own Attributes, the Herbalist can instead make a potion that increases a single physical Attribute of the imbiber by the Herbalist's Life dots for a single scene, this effect can take the imbiber above their normal Attribute maximum. The potion, if unconsumed, retains its efficacy for the Herbalist's Matter dots on the Advanced Duration chart. If the Herbalist imbibes the potion the effect fully stacks with the Attribute bonus from the primary Attainment, rather than overwriting it or stacking transiently. A subject can only safely drink one such potion, each additional potion counts as a toxin with toxicity equal to potency and an interval of 1 minute.
            This Attainment replicates the effects of Honing the Form with the Permanence Attainment and reach to change the primary factor to potency and the ability to boost attributes above the normal maximum.

            Honing the Form with reach to change the primary factor to Potency, Advanced Duration from Permanence, and the ability to boost attribute above the normal maximum rating
            Fifth: The Final Transmutation
            Prerequisites: Life 5, Matter 5, Science 4. Crafts or Occult 3
            The Herbalist makes one final potion, their Magnum Opus. This potion kills any without the Strongest Internal Alchemy and only work once, the Herbalist cannot benefit from further applications of this potion, even from other Masters. If anyone other than the Herbalist that brewed it consumes this potion they die immediately. After consuming the potion the Master Herbalist becomes something more than they once were, and possibly something less: they are a Perfected living being. The Herbalist gains the rote action quality on any non-spellcasting action whose unmodified dice pool is 8 or greater. However, for any action whose base dice pool is 3 or less their failures are automatically dramatic.


            Hone the Perfected Form here with the +2 reach option and Life conjunctional to work on a living person, Advanced Duration from Permanence, and Indefinite for the final reach.

Many Flower Girls obtain Crypto-Flora, Goblin Fruit, and Mandrakes from other supernatural creatures. -Small addition that amused me.


                                                            Fifth:
                                                            Prerequisites: Prime 5, Life 5, Craft or Survival 5, Expression 5
                                                            The Garden finally grows to its truest potential. Both the Garden and its Flower Girl are connected on such intimate level that is functionally no difference between the two. And from the union of Gnosis, Root, Soul, and Stem comes a living symbol of that connection. The Soul Plant becomes a Living Soul Stone. The Soul Plant has a number of properties that separate it from a normal Soul Stone. It acts as a +3 yantra for it's Flower Girl. The Soul Plant makes no Demesne or Annullity. The Soul Plant does not reduce the maximum Gnosis of the Flower Girl. The Soul Plant can only be dedicated if the Flower Girl has the Broad Dedication Merit. It is a normal Soul Stone in every other regard.

                                                            Instead of a Demesne or Annullity, The Garden Perfects it's Node, and Extends it's effects onto Living Things. The Node effects becomes:

                                                            • Forces: The Attuned need no rest, nor food. They always are full of energy. They ignore wound penalties.
                                                            • Life: The Attuned can heal from any wound, given enough time, unless they outright die. Scars fade, Limbs regrow.
                                                            • Matter: Unless totally destroyed, the Attuned gain a general armor equal to 1 +(Prime/2).
                                                            • Space: Those who are Attuned know where everything is around them, they don't need to roll to find a path or trail. They add Prime dots to Roll requiring precise movement though space.
                                                            • Time: The Attuned's memories become sharp and clear. They don't need to roll to remember. They add Prime dots to rolls to predict consequence and to rolls to understand the Consequences of their actions.

                                                            From the Soul Plant comes special seeds. Every solar year, a number of seeds equal to the Flower Girl's dots in Life can be harvested. These seeds, when planted in a Ley Line that is connected to one of the ley lines from the original Garden, grows into a new Garden, with its own Node. New Ley Lines emerge from the new Node and the process continues. For the purposes of Attainments, these new Gardens are considered extensions of the original. The new Gardens only grow to an area as if they had added another Ley Line to the intersection. Many of these new Gardens attract caretakers, many of whom become Attuned Sleepwalkers over a extended period of time.-(The seeds act as their own Ley Line, making a intersection if there was not one before, this only happens when the plant sprouts after being planted)

                                                            If instead the seed is planted into the Flower Girl's Garden, it instead expands the radius of the Garden by one Ley Line.
