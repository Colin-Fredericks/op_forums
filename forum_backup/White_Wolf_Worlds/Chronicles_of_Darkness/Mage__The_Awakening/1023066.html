    Start-to-finish examples of different spells being cast?

    12-27-2016, 10:09 PM
    I know OPP was fighting for every last page with the rulebook, but it seems kind of like a glaring omission that there aren't a few pages dedicated to literally just giving examples of the spellcasting system at work. I'm starting to ST now and it would be so helpful for new players to get just a few examples, start-to-finish, of casting different kinds of spells with different kinds of spell factors on different kinds of subjects. It would answer so many common questions I'm getting about things like advanced spell factors, withstand ratings against multiple opponents, etc. I feel like 5-6 official examples starting with the Imago and going all the way to paradox resolution would really help.

    Is that something that already exists somewhere, or something we could hope for in an official blogpost? Is it a resource we could put together as a forum? If no one else has something like this ready, I'm going to go ahead and do it up myself for my players and will share back here when it's done...

    My ideas are bad and I should feel bad.

      My players usually write it all down at the table. It tends to follow the steps it's given in the book. I'll be surprised if I didn't make any mistakes, but here's how I recall one of the first spells I made a character cast going.

      A Doomsday Clocker popped up in the basement and was going to go upstairs to kill someone, not realizing that the players were there. Someone heard him and took a shot at him, and he figured maybe they had gotten the jump on him but could still be handled. First things first, he would make it difficult for them to attack him, and so he'd cast Cold Snap.

      He had Gnosis 6 and Death 4. It was a Rote spell, based on his Intimidation, which was 3.

      The Imago is developed.

      It's a rote spell at Death 3 so he gets 3 Free Reach. He wants to change the Casting Time to Instant, that's one Reach. He wanted to increase the Duration, but just a scene/hour would be good so that's two Reach. It's a large house above him, so he wanted to enhance the Scale, that's three Reach. But he didn't want to cast it on the basement, just the stairs leading to and up, so that was Range, making it four Reach, potentially allowing for Paradox, but that was okay because he was a scelestus. Potency could remain where it was.

      As a Rote, he used his Intimidation for his yantra, then threw in a tools to round it out. This was the first spell I cast in the game and sort of an easy one, because it didn't require any moving up steps or any negative modifiers. That all rounded out to a +4, giving him a dicepool of 14.

      But there was Paradox, so I rolled 3 dice for that, due to his Gnosis. There was 1 Success. Fortunately earlier that day I was checking up on the forums and read where someone pointed out the Scelesti System on p240. So we rolled Gnosis, and he got enough successes to control where that Paradox went. I couldn't think of anything useful for a free Reach, but looking back at the spell description, another reach could basically be used to make it colder, adding the Extreme Cold on top of the Ice effect. So I went with that as his Paradox Reach. The success still acted as a dice penalty to his spellcasting roll, which was knocked down to 13.

      I think he got something like 8 successes. It was an Exceptional Success, at least. I ended up using the 'bonus step in the primary spell factor,' result, which made the spell last a day.

      And so the house was shrouded with cold and ice formed on everything. The Ice Tilt cuts Speed in half and all Physical Rolls and Defense works at a -2 penalty. Dramatic Failures on Physical rolls cause the Knocked Down Condition. And Extreme Cold sort of negated natural healing, which came into play when people started getting knocked unconscious.

      The next turn the Moros player cast Deepen Shadows which gave almost everyone the Blinded Tilt. I believe it worked a lot like Cold Snap with a much smaller dicepool.

      Hope it helped. I'm surprised you haven't gotten more replies. I know the developer posted a step by step guide for how a player cast a death curse on an NPC, but I forget where it was posted.

                  Alright, I'll try my hand at this. Lets say that the mage in question here is Mastigos with Mind 1, Space 2, Life 3, and Gnosis 1.

                  To set the scenario, lets say that the mage here due to lack of paranoia and a bout of carelessness has found himself ambushed, knocked unconscious, and kidnapped by a group of Sleeper ne'er-do-wells. The scene begins with the mage regaining consciousness, only to find himself battered, bruised, gagged, and tied up alone inside what appears to be his assailants van.

                  Well now, the mage is in quite a bit of pain, though nothing immediately life threatening (1 Lethal, 4 Bashing, out of 7 Health boxes). Luckily our mage knows quite a bit of Life magic, easily enough to fix himself up (lets say he knows the Knit spell as a rote). Before moving around too much he decides to inspect himself to see how much damage he's sustained (he knows he's hurt obviously, but how much is the question). So he casts an improvised Analyze Life spell on himself.

                  He's currently tied up and gagged, so he isn't really in much of a position to use many of his usual yantras. Now while he definitely doesn't want to spend time casting this at ritual speed (which will cost him a Reach), he only needs to cast this on himself, and only really needs a basic success, so he doesn't really have much need to increase any other factors or spend anymore Reach, and with 3 free Reach (He has Life 3 and this is only a Life 1 spell) he has plenty to spare. That said, with the lack of any yantras he's casting unmodified, but he's pretty good at Life so he doesn't feel it's that big a deal (Though since this is an improvised spell from his non-Ruling Arcanum it will cost him 1 mana to attempt to cast).

                  So he casts the spell, spending 1 Reach to instant cast and 1 mana because it's non-Ruling. His Dicepool is Gnosis (1) + Life (3) for a total of 4. Let's say he gets 1 success, so his spell works flawlessly and he instantly learns exactly how hurt he is, and knows exactly how much effort he needs to devote to fix it. Sweet!

                  Deciding that being tied up is making this a lot more difficult than this needs to be he quickly decides he needs to slip his bonds before proceeding. So he decides to cast another spell, this one being Space 2's Break Boundary. He's still tied up, and he isn't quite as good at Space magic as he is at Life, and the fact that he's going to have to Reach a bit more than he knows he safely can he'd going to spend a bit of Willpower making sure this spell goes through. Space is a Ruling Arcanum for him as a Mastigos, so he won't have to spend mana just to cast the spell.

                  So he casts again, spending 1 point of Willpower to increase chances of success, 1 Reach to instant cast, 2 Reach for Break Boundary's effect that lets him escape from things even when not able to move. He only has 1 free Reach since he only just meets the spells requirement, so he's Reaching 2 over what he's capable of safely handling. With Gnosis 1, each point of Reach over what he can handle only adds 1 Paradox die, so he'll be rolling a Paradox Dicepool of 2 for this spell. He gets lucky and scores no successes on the Paradox roll. He then rolls his spell Dicepool of Gnosis (1) + Space (2) + Willpower (3) for 6 dice. He again gets the success he needs and the spell goes off as intended, freeing him from his restraints.

                  Now that he's free he's able to properly attend his wounds. He fishes out a feathered quill from his inner jacket pocket, his Dedicated Tool. Now he's ready to cast. He uses the spell Knit on himself. Knit heals 2 bashing per Potency of the spell, but the Primary Spell Factor of Knit is Potency, so the base Potency of the spell, even unmodified, will be equal to his dots in Life (3). Since he has knows Knit as a rote, his Reach is calculated as if he were as proficient in the spells Ruling Arcanum as the master who encoded it (5), so he has 3 free reach for this spell (1 for meeting the spells base requirements and 2 for effectively being 2 steps above said requirements). He spends 1 Reach to instant cast, 1 reach to be able to use Knits Reach effect to heal 1 Lethal per Potency instead of 2 Bashing. He then spends 1 additional Reach to use a non-standard Reach effect allowing him to mix and match which point of Potency is used to heal which type of damage.

                  So he casts the spell, spending 1 reach for instant cast, 1 reach to heal lethal, and 1 reach to mix and match which damage type he heals. Because it's a rote, he doesn't have to pay the 1 mana for the spell being outside of his Ruling Arcana. His Dicepool this time consists of Gnosis (1) + Life (3) + Rote Mudra (3 from Medicine) for a total of 7. With a Primary Spell Factor of Potency, the base Potency of the spell is 3 (equal to his dots in Life). With it, he spends 2 Potency to heal 4 bashing, and the third to heal his 1 point of Lethal damage.

                  Now, unbound and fully healed, our mage is ready to plot his escape and take down the baddies!

                  (I'll probably continue this is a part 2 after getting some sleep.)
