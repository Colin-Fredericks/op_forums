                                                08-29-2023, 09:41 PM
                                                Originally posted by Johnny Awesome View Post
                                                I like it quite a bit.

                                                I could use some help with it if anyone has time.

                                                My issue is that this is an Acanthus that wants Spirit as a Ruling Arcana. He already has Know Spirit as a Rote and Postcognition as a Praxis.

                                                In an ideal world the Legacy attainments wouldn't overlap with those, but would be Spirit attainments with optional Time and maybe Fate.

                                                It's hard to imagine the Legacy without having Know Spirit as the 1st attainment and Postcognition as the Optional Time 2nd attainment, but I'm open to other ideas.

                                                The character wants to be a Spirit Historian essentially.

                                                My current breakdown for the 1st couple attainments is Know Spirit/Momentary Flux and then Shadow Walk/Postcognition, but I don't completely love it.
                                                Let's see. Signs of Sorcery notes that Acanthus view Spirit as the forbidden places in the stories and the darkness beyond the light. So, Acanthus who view history as strange times in a strange land would lean to Spirit as means of interacting with the spirits that actually lived through history, and familiarizing herself with that unfamiliar world.

                                                With a theme like above, something like......



                                                First

                                                The historian inquires a spirit's history as Know Spirit (Spirit •), but with the list of available facts expanded to include the following:

                                                • How long, roughly, has the spirit been here, and has it ever Manifested or fell into slumber in the meantime?

                                                • What actions, broadly, has the spirit taken here?

                                                • How has the spirit, if ever, changed in Rank or Influences?

                                                Notably, the first time a historian inquires a spirit via this Attainment, she always gets an answer to one of the above expanded questions (chosen by the player) in addition to whatever this Attainment's Potency allows for, circumventing Withstand.

                                                Optional: Time 1

                                                When inquiring the above expanded questions, the historian may now pin-point when it happened. This cannot circumvent lacunae; if one has happened between the historian's inquiry and the relevant event's true moment of happening, the Attainment provides the lacuna as the answer.


                                                Second

                                                The historian awakens the spirits to relive the past. This functions as Coaxing the Spirits (Spirit •), but instead of acting in accordance with its nature, the spirit or its representation reenacts a past action to its best ability. Reach is assigned to instant casting and sensory range.

                                                The reenactment is performed on the spot; if a spirit clawed at a human, for example, it will make clawing gestures in thin air instead of flying off to maim the original victim again.

                                                The historian must specify the exact past action he wants to see reenacted. Alternatively, he may designate a past moment, and have the subject reenact what it did in that moment.

                                                Optional: Time 2

                                                After coaxing the subject, the historian may trigger a vision as per Postcognition (Time •), with Reach assigned to instant casting and retaining awareness (Defense) in the present. Temporal Sympathy can be applied as usual.


                                                Third

                                                Resonance is the means by which the world connects Once and Now, and the historian works to encourage it. She may spend 1 Mana to 'summon' past Resonances of an area, with a ritual that takes time as her Gnosis-derived ritual interval. (Quick reference: three hours at Gnosis 1, one hour at Gnosis 3, thirty minutes at Gnosis 5, ten minutes at Gnosis 7, and one minute at Gnosis 9)

                                                The summoning lasts for hours equal to the historian's Spirit dots, during which mundane attempts to weaken the summoned Resonances fail. However, actors caught up in the meantime may undertake actions that reinforce the Resonances, making them last beyond the Attainment.

                                                Anyone affected by a summoned Resonance gains a dice bonus equal to half the historian's Spirit dots (rounded up) on any dice rolls to investigate past events relevant to the Resonance.

                                                Optional: Time 3

                                                Anyone affected by the summoned Resonances are drawn closer to the moment when the Resonance was present, represented by their temporal sympathy becoming stronger by one level. (The Temporal Sympathy Attainment becomes easier to use on them, while manipulating the sympathies per se becomes harder.) However, Unchanged sympathy cannot be achieved this way.



                                                No idea for the 4th and 5th Attainments! Maybe something like summoning spirits from the past?
