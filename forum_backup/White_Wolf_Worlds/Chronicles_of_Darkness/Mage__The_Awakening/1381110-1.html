    Alright, final build (probably!)

    Gnosis 2, Forces 2, Mind 2, Space 2
    Rotes: Kinetic Blow, Telepathy, Nightvision
    Praxes: Ground-Eater, Outward/Inward Eye

    Kinetic Blow I can cast in 2 turns with 7 dice if I use High Speech, giving me 4 reach for quick cast, a full day duration, and both Knock Down and Stun Tilts. If I drop one or both of the Tilts and/or drop the duration to one scene, I can grant this buff to myself and 4 allies in sensory range.

    Telepathy can be cast in 2 turns with 7 dice using High Speech, affecting 5 targets with the ability for all targets capable of sending and receiving messages that lasts for a scene/hour. Alternately I can target 1 subject at Sympathetic Range for 1 Mana.

    Nightvision can be cast in 2 turns, 7 dice, High Speech, affecting 5 targets at sensory range, lasting 1 scene/hour or 1 day, with the protection against sudden extreme stimulus.

    Ground-Eater can be cast in 1 turn with 6 dice on myself at potency 2 for 1 scene/hour, with 3+ exceptional success.

    Outward/Inward Eye can be cast in 2 turns, 7 dice, High Speech, lasting 1 scene/hour or 1 day, 3+ ES. If I combo this with Nightvision, I get perfect perception at sensory range and a solid chance of ES for recharging Mana/Willpower.

            It might be simpler to use one of the pre-existing ones. But anyway, lets take a look at Legacy creation.

            In general, I recommend building the theme first, then figuring out everything else. Mastigos are about Transgression and Confrontation . They understand their vices, confront them, gain mastery over them and thus enlightenment (at least in theory). So your main Arcanum is probably going to be external to the Path (to get a third Ruling), while the optional from it (Space or Mind). Examine how that theme would be aboarded under those lenses, the ones for their Order, their personal philosophy, etc.

            Then you can determine yantras based on flavorful behaviors within that theme. Oblations are habits that further reinforce said mentality.

            For Attainments, a simple way to think about them is:

            1st: General utility, something simple that you want them to use often.
            2nd: An ability to defend or hide from the main concern of pursuing the theme.
            3rd: The tools needed to operate on the field, whether a way to improvise them or prepare them beforehand.
            4th: Something that demonstrates high mastery of the theme.
            5th: Something that brings them all together.

            For a good format, I recommend the one in the 2ed Legacies thread. The Legacies in my signature also have a design breakdown. It goes through my train of thought for creating them, perhaps it can be of aid.

                        Originally posted by ZenBear View Post
                        I might actually make Hector his real name and come up with a Shadow Name later when I get the Merit.
                        Note that you don't need the Merit to have the Shadow Name; you just need it to be able to leverage it in a way that matters to gameplay, i.e. as a Yantra. That's what the XP cost is for.

                        Having a Shadow Name is good hygiene for Wisdom, even if you don't lean into it, so you should have one even without the Merit.

                        Originally posted by ZenBear View Post
                        Is it expected for a Cabal to know each other’s real names? That would leave them vulnerable to sympathetic magic, right?
                        It would, because the technical term isn't "real name"; it's "sympathetic name". Part of the point of a Shadow Name is to play up your "I'm a Mage!" side, but it's also, yes, a great defense mechanism against both Nimbus bleed and people trying to track you or your family down.

                        Thus, it's a major sign of trust for the Cabal to share sympathetic names with each other. I'd advise not sharing it by default, simply because it can make for a great bonding moment when you do share it. There are thousands of situations where it could make sense anyways, but as with most defaults, you should have a reason and be deliberate when you mess with it. There was a good AP I read once, for instance, where the cabal's banner-warden had something to prove and gave his sympathetic name to the cabal as part of an aggressive, "I am super-dependable" thing. It's not all that different from handing everyone a loaded gun and then choosing not to wear any defenses around them.

                        Also, because sympathetic names don't get a lot of use in most play, it's a good opportunity to have a completely unpronounceable one. :P

                                                            Having a Supernal entity familiar should probably be impossible. I was imagining that the trial would involve inhering the seat or being chosen by a spirit of the Legacy. Since goetia can't Manifest, this opened up the possibility of a spirit or ghost of a former knight being called forth by an ancient covenant with the entity. Perhaps they already need a specific kind of familiar to undertake the with them or the goetia gains the ability to Materialize as a sword via the initiation. It all depends on what direction you want to go with their lore. Maybe they only receive it as part of the higher Attainments.

                                                            The Attainments need to be compatible with their level of the Arcanum. With that in mind, here are some general ideas:

                                                            Forces 1: Nightvision with Reach assigned to instant use. A simple skill that helps them fight and perceive the world around them in more detail by seeing through the dragon's eyes.

                                                            Mind 1: A knight must be careful of who they serve and protect. He must also know how to establish a rapport with his allies. To this end, the first thing they learn is how to understand people. This acts as Know Nature with Reach assigned to instant use.

                                                            Forces 2: Kinetic Efficiency with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration. This represents their ability to duck, weave, climb and jump to adapt to the battlefield.

                                                            Mind 2: Emotional Urging with Reach assigned to instant use and sensory range. To compensate for the extra Reach, it can only be used when they are disarmed. This covers a knight's ability to be inspiring to his allies and terrifying to his enemies.

                                                            Forces 3: Gravity Control with Reach used for instant use and advanced scale. It can be used as touch or as an aimed spell. So it covers both Lashings at once, as the dragon roars or draws breath.

                                                            Mind 3: After summoning Sir Lambton and passing his trial, you receive a Rank 2 goetia familiar with the ability to Manifest as a simple weapon. When creating the familiar, the Blast Numina can be used for sword beams or as a replacement for ammo.

                                                            Forces 4: Spend a scene and a mana to ritually dedicate a weapon to your cause, allowing it to deal aggravated damage (not 100% sure if this is possible). It represents wielding the dragon's fire.

                                                            Mind 4: The goetia gains a variation of the Stalwart Numina that grants its Power as base armor for a target. This manifests as an energy armor (like Dragonborn's Dragon Aspect).

                                                            Mind typically has no way to make someone a Sleepwalker, that is normally the purview of Prime or Fate. I use it as the fourth optional Attainment of the Szary Strażnik Legacy, which utilizes both.

                                                            I try to design the capstone to elevate the utility of all the Attainments.

                                                            The Sophos need time to make plans. Without it they have no benefits from hung spells, no rewrite histories, no rote action, nothing. Furthermore, plans need to coordinate multiple individuals. So their capstone lets them always have an army of loyal, well coordinated, peak human skill supernatural specialists. Not only that, the optional one gives them the time to plan. Together they equip them to implement their philosophy in any situation.

                                                            Now they can make a temporal bubble, astrally project to scout and setup goetia. Then Veil of Moments gives them the comfort to heal and scour for mana. Then they can setup everything before time resumes. So in a turn, the invaders who kicked in the door of the cabal's Sanctum go from having the edge to being routed.

                                                            The Strażnik get a reinforcement to their theme of indirect manipulators, ghosts in the system. The main Attainment gives a lot of flexibility to control the board, shifting Obsessions and merits as needed. The secondary one lets them prepare care packages and is a discrete method of manipulation that maintains plausible deniability. In short, they synergize with the other Attainments.

                                                            With that in mind, here are my current ideas:

                                                            Forces 5: Levitation with Reach assigned to advanced duration and free flight. This represents the idea of a dragon taking flight.

                                                            Mind 5: Psychic Reprogramming with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration. It ends if the target suffers any Breaking Point and they invariably know its the caster's fault. After all, knights need to adhere to a higher standard than causing suffering to those they "conscript".

                                                            This should allow them to sneak in to convince or recruit the opposition. Alternatively, they can use it to rain death upon their enemies.

                                                            Added to the Space Arcanum, it gives them a lot of freedom both in and out of combat. The biggest problem is that I accidentally made Talion and Calebrimbor.
