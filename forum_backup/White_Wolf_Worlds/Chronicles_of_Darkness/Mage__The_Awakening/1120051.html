    Portland Oregon - Chronicles of the Crystal Kingdom

    07-24-2017, 04:39 PM
    Hi there! I'm TotallyNotaTremere, it's great to meet you all after lurking for so, so long in the shadows. My friends and I just completed our first story in Mage: The Awakening 2e. It's hella fun and we've made it a near weekly meet up for at least 3 of us if not more when others can make it. The Storyteller is new to Mage and CoD in general, and had never ST'd a game in this setting before. He did amazingly. As I've had a few years of gaming in the WoD and 2E I volunteered to iron out mechanics, and basically act as the Chronicler who writes out the games as they happen to save him some work since it can be a lot bringing a world to life and I believe in sharing burdens with potential soul snacks...I MEAN FRIENDS. POTENTIAL FRIENDS. This has kind of become a labor of love for me, and I hope you all enjoy reading this very, very long post.

    Anyway, without further ramblings, I give you

    Portland Oregon - Chronicles of the Crystal Kingdom

    Themes
    Magic is alive.

    This is a common principle accepted by any Mystagogue, written about for thousands of years.

    Magic, is Alive. In the sense that there is Tass that has sentience.

    This is a new fact of life, one that has caused a great deal of struggle and debate for the Diamond mages of Portland Oregon.

    Do these beings have rights? Can they be trusted or is something so alien looking simply deadly tools sent to destroy us? What effects these...things? Were they human, or perhaps a form of aetheric goetia? The questions are endless, and for the Mage known as Lux, they are his life now. His most favorite thing to study. Himself. He was turned into one against his will. You'd think this would make a bad first impression but....Lux has taken to championing them, saying they have a right to exist like anyone else...but is he still...him? He certainly seems to think so and thus far has acted as he always has.

    Setting as introduced to us in the beginning
    Portland Oregon has recently been experience an extreme amount of leyline activities, with many unstable irises popping open all throughout the surrounding wilderness, several mages were lost when they collapsed midway through these tunnels to an unknown verge. No one seems to know why, but strange beasts, a few humanoids and caves filled with stranger mana filled...people?... have been reported. All made of this blue crystal, hard as magma forged steel and crazy as hell itself. Along with this, in the city Hallows have been acting oddly, bursting forth with strange resonances that causes them to seemingly overload with power. There were admittedly a few fires at first.

    Lexicon

    The Living Mana Fjords - A Verge nestled in between the Aether and Pandemonium, it gives gives birth to creatures of crystalized tass seemingly spontaneously. Some are more magically inclined than others, and some have genuine sentience of their own.
    Crystal Race aka The Cryss: Race of Sentient Minds with bodies made of crystallized tass. The least amongst them can turn their bodies into different shapes, for both offensive and defensive purposes. The most powerful can wield Supernal Magic posessing a Gnosis of their own.
    Crystaline Mage - A Human who has joined the Legacy of the Crystal Mages and taken on the Crystaline Form (See Conditions). They are restricted only by their path and gnosis in terms of magic they can cast. They come in many heights, but their features are smooth like blue crystal manikins.
    Crystal Knights - Humanoid creatures from The Fjords, they possess a Gnosis and can cast spells that they have the appropriate dot level for. Most are restricted to Prime and Mind, while a few can also move the powers of forces and space.
    Crystal Pilgrims/Citizens - Can only cast from the Crystal Attainments, which are common attributes of this "species" They have access to various levels of attainments based upon the Potency of their creation.
    Profligate - A Human Mage who is petitioning to join the Legacy.
    Pura - Also known as Pudichitium(True Name), now remembered only as The Mad Creator after Lux ordered the majority of him forgotten for his crimes of trying to Pura-fy the human race at the point of a magical sword while offsetting the Cosmic Order by building a Lighthouse and elevating his Verge to a new Supernal Realm for his people, which would have swallowed the fallen world in the process. He created an Artifact titled The Mallets that effect the Crystalized ones in strange ways. They also call out to The Drum that may sound to the souls of those beings born in The Living Mana Fjords.
    Lux (Shadow Name) - Also known as The Herald (Title), The First Kindled (Title) The Crystal King (Title). A Mastigos of the Mysterium who happened to intrude on a dream visitation his room mate was having. When his room mate, an Obrimos known as Chord, faltered in accepting the offer that would allow them to leave the dream alive, presented by Pura, a ten foot tall supernal being made of crystallized tass who wielded magics...Well, the pragmatic Mastigos took the deal instead, to preserve their lives. This deal was eventually renegotiated by Pura, who turned him into a Crystal Being against his will, forever changing him. Lux, severely unhappy with this given his more hedonistic nature and not wanting to be a mind slave, was freed from the Fjords by his mentor and friends. Lux also decided to renegotiated the deal, you know an eye for an eye. He called upon the most powerful mages known to him and summoned Pura, delivering him right into a trap. Together they destroyed the Form of Pura, leaving only his spark. Capturing it, he used it to correct the unstable Verge, and alter the Lighthouse to finish creating the Legacy without upsetting the Cosmic Order.

    Prologue

    Two weeks ago...



    The Local Mysterium of Portland Oregon have been discovering new hallows left and right. Leylines are shifting. No one knows why. It's....problematic. Several bases of operations have been powered down and not just for the local Mystagogues. A few of the Guardians safehouse hallows randomly lost power, while one burnt down the entire place in celestial fires that local crews could not put out. Many, many questions came of this, and finally, Iris' began to open for short periods of time. Two local Mysterium mages were sent to investigate. They brought back some...strange, things. A Crystal Lizard that is alive, no spells animate it. It's like it is the spell...Tass that shone with a deep fire beneath it. Tales of other things seen at the far end of the tunnel. Buildings? Some kind of broken light house?



    Six Months Later

    There is a table of mages shrouded in spell work to obscure them and guarantee they're left in peace. They prattle on well into the night, clearly on edge about something. It's deep in the stacks of an old college library used as a research post for the Mysterium on the west coast, and even with the spells the voices are hushed, almost in awe. Talking. Whispering. About what? About how the head of the northern chapter revealed some kind of Eidolon, cut like a marble statue made out of blue crystal to the whole Academy it was alive of it's own volition, had a unique pattern, the whole nine yards.

    Further it was almost instantly carted out by some guy with an attitude calling dibs. What was stranger was the Head of the Academy just kind of let it happen. It gets weirder from there though. Some young upstart Psychonaught and his Punk Rock Obrimos buddy damn near tore the fucking city off the map, mana wise. One of them wanted to dislocate all the ley lines and block off the Aether, and it was the damned Obrimos of the two t'boot. He was fucking terrified. Heard the Mastigos involved was the same guy that got that Crystal Eidolon thing too. We actually have it confirmed he was later turned into one of those...whatever the hell they are. Whole thing gives me the hee-bee's. Can't be good can it?

    About a year later.


    Up north in Canada or maybe over a little into Alaska some rumors are spreading about how a Mystagogue named Lux was suddenly thrust into a new state of being, and used it to found a legacy in Portland Oregon. One that he defended. One that nearly started a war. A recording of his speech that got them accepted into the Mysterium and thus offered a level of protection and rights under the Lex Magica, has recently surfaced to spread word. In it the Heads of the Mysterium and Guardians for the area were both invited to sit in as well as representatives from the Arrow. Notes and a projected recording of the events are being passed around, so that these new...people...don't scare anyone.

    The Mastigos Mystagogue known as Lux, who had learned somehow to shape his soul and join these Crystal things managed to do the impossible. By the end of the meeting, sighting this speech as the reason, the Cryss had found a protector in him, and Lux's newly formed Legacy found protection. The recording is being sent around to those who hear about the Crystal Mages, though it's only needed to be sent out a handful of times thus far, to areas where elder mages who had lives that were naturally coming to a close or who were fighting off death as many mages try to do, were offered a chance to preserve their knowledge and themselves. Of course, as word spreads, others may come to join, and they too will be tested.

    A man with a cheeky and prideful voice speaks as you press a rune on an old rock and a holographic recording projects outward;
    "No. You fucking listen. I get it, you want to destroy them all. They're six foot tall walking statues that can terminator-two themselves all up into walking blenders with axes, and spears for hands....and some of them even have Supernal Magic. It's scary. (Luceat, the head of the Portland Guardians raises a hand, but is quickly dismissed by the Mastigos) No no hold on I have a point put your hand down. Everything from the Supernal is scary thus far. Supernal Beings have been around for thousands of years if you're lucky, and untold aeons if you're not. Dammit if it isn't all pants shittingly, terrifyingly awful. All that which is born over there. It all tries to kill us, sometimes thinking it's helping us, because that is how apart from humanity it is. Most of it's more powerful than us. Completely and eldritchly alien to us."

    "Why are these Crystal things any different? Because they're new. Every single mage in the Academy studies ancient texts, the Guardians hide them away and the Arrow battle them sure and they all point out the old terrible things. These are new beings. They can learn. You heard from Victor. His testimony of them being child like, learning at great speeds but still. They tried to eat the mans cookies for fucks sake and they don't have mouths! (He laughs.) then they felt bad and cleaned his house for him. Children. That's what these Supernal Beings are, and we can guide them. We can give them humanity, you saw my First Knight, I gave him a name, Bruce! He's been with me here for ages, and he's got all the humanity of a Vulcan sure, but that's so much more than the Fae, the Goetia, the Spirits..."

    "What's more those who join our ranks can study magic...forever. No souls stolen. No murder to steal away the victims years, no spells that can fail because someone takes a grudge and turn you to bones and dust. Just preserved magic, preserved soul, encased in tass and driven by human willpower and more importantly human empathy. Your knowledge inside a vessel to be passed on to other Mystagogues until your light is extinguished or you achieve ascension...What more could the Mysterium ask for? The only thing that makes them evil is their leader, Pura. He's clearly gone mad. Germany is pretty great now, but the Seers took over the Nazi's once. These guys are just mislead by Supernal Hitler. So...the question you should be pondering isn't whether or not you should accept me, and them, it should be...who wants to help me kill Supernal Hitler and teach these beautiful creatures what humanity is all about?"

    Several Mysterium members were given oversight as well as Guardians who were sent to mediate to be sure that all this was true, that they were capable of their own unique development and they could be shaped not unlike children into something good instead of the simple army they were meant to be for Pura. The Mad Creator was finally destroyed with a combined effort of the Mysterium, Guardians and Arrow, his mana-form destroyed utterly by a spell of Celestial Fire that drained mana and his Spark used as the new focus for the Broken Lighthouse. His mind was destroyed as his spark ignited properly for the first time and Lux's Legacy was founded using the transformative magics of the Lighthouse.

    Lux, being the Herald, was next in line to lead the Crystal People, and he would. After the study on the beings and Lux's newly shaped legacy were completed, they were accepted into the local caucus, given a measure of status in the Mysterium and with that all the protection it provides. If you see these creatures, approach with caution, as you would any other mage, but I beg you, do not attack them. Some are weak, and just want to be friends. Some are strong and want to protect you from harm. Some were human once, and you wouldn't kill a human for no reason other than they look a little off...would you?

    The History of Puditchitium

    Some time ago, no one is sure how long as Time doesn't really function the same in a Verge or the Supernal, there was a Spark in the Aether, and this Spark traveled, and saw and as it saw it learned and continued traveling all the way to the edge of the Aether, following a different light only it could see among all the stars that shined so, so bright nearby. This light came from the border, from what the Spark would later come to know as a Verge, far far down below...or was it across?, but this Verge was unique. The Light of Pandemonium shone through it too and into the Aether. Pura crossed the gap somehow, into the Verge and there a form was born here from such a small spark, when he realized he needed a name it forged...so, so much. An awakening of sorts happened, giving him Gnosis and access to the Mind and Prime Arcana.

    Eventually from Pura, the Crystalline Army would be born. Brave knights who wielded the power of the Aether and Pandemonium. Pilgrims who were less gifted with supernal magic, but were no less magical themselves. Even beasts were born here. Dragons of raw tass, willed into being by the fire within the beast, it's mind unlike anything known to the mundane plane of earth. Of course there were more, smaller lizards, bears, reflections of magical and lesser creatures from the fallen world constantly thinking themselves into existence in the Verge that has been named The Living Mana Fjords.

    The Fjords

    The Living Mana Fjords, (called The Fjords for short) is a large Verge of not just one but two Supernal Realms. As such it gives a +1 bonus to Mind and a +1 bonus to all Prime magic cast within it, or around hallows that resonate with it. Upon stepping through the Iris leading from wherever on earth to The Living Mana Fjords you typically come out on the edge of a long cliff side overlooking the Aetherian Bay. These cliffs, ending in two twin peaks at either side, come down in a crescent shape, the middle of which meets the beach on both sides and rises again to the opposite peak. The opposite peak is home to the Restored Lighthouse, which once imperfect was made perfect by Lux, also known to his people as The First Kindled, The Crystal Herald, Slayer of the Mad Creator and King. (First of his name, Stormborn et cet).

    Everything here with any substance is made of tass, save for the ocean the Fjord rests upon which is made of Celestial Fire, roaring in waves and crashing against the blue and red crystalline walls of the cliffs. Everything here is magic, including the residents, though some are more magic than others. Indeed, even the sky itself is filled with sights to see. Instead of any sun, light just seems to be. No night shines here, mostly because the natives need no sleep, only time enough to perform their oblations and harvest their mana. The sky is filled in it's upper reaches with creatures bursting forth, like fireworks coming into being, then deciding to be a crystallized dragon or bird, the occasional swan and pterodactyl then wondering once more what it would be like not to exist and in a reign of crystal flame briefly, there it goes out of existence once more.

    Above that chaos there lies what looks like the cosmos stretching before you. Closest in view however are two planets, twins in a sense. One is earth with the overlaying realm of Pandemonium clear to see if viewed with vision enhancing magics, red and black and flame and changing astral forces all around. It's sister sphere seems to be much the same, except the Aether with it's own swirling vortexes of forces and magic. Though these be mere representations the sight itself is no less awe inspiring, or frightful depending on the viewer.

    Lux, along with the help of his fellow Mysterium mages have set up an Athenaeum in the Fjords using a combination of many magics to will buildings into being, though only a handful of mages know this tightly kept secret at the time. Once all reinforcements to the Kingdom are completed however, the secrecy can be relaxed and study can begin in earnest. They've also moved their Censorium, given that the previous one was designed by a traitorous Moros who could not reconcile the differences between the crystal people and humanity. In time, it may become one of the most heavily defended centers of study the Mysterium possesses, and one of the most valuable given it's location and level of available protection by not only it's residence but the very location itself.

    The Kingdom
    After his success Lux was given the rank of King among his new people. His first decree was to make more than just the Lighthouse. and give the Cryss something other than caves to live in. He gave them homes introducing the concept to them through mental spells. More than that though, he ordered the construction of a city. Walls of mana imbued with spell work and celestial fire that stretch around the entirety of the cliff side on which the Lighthouse rests.

    The city is stratified, at the base of the cliff where the Aetherian Bay meets the 'sand' more aptly described as crystallized tass granules, sit the housing for new students, and visitors. Along the path as you climb upward along the cliff side is housing for the Pilgrims, and Knights, though the Knights more often maintain the wall itself, for security. Then there is a marketplace, where one can find food for the fleshier inhabitants as well as other assorted items, some of which are clearly magical in nature and function, though nothing so powerful as artifacts or grimoires and the like. After this is the Athenaeum proper, a practically palatial building cut into the very side of the cliff and exiting at it's top to the final path leading the the newly perfected Lighthouse where new Crystal Mages are made. Though Lux had nothing to do with it's naming, he has not dissuaded the local Cryss populace from referring to the city as "The Kingdom".

    The Culture of the Cryss
    Before Lux, they were mostly cold and distant, though with the capability to learn and feel. After giving them a city to inhabit, Lux, with the aid of the Knights of his people wove a Mind and Prime spell invoking his own memories that create the defining roll of humanity from the view point of the rational hedonistic warlock. He imparted eleven Truths to them in the form of his memories. These truths, summed up into one word each are as follows; Lust, Pride, Inebriation, Patience, Temperance, Altruism, Courage, Hope, Love, Meritocracy, and Joy. After giving this gift to his people, they became more human than ever before, experiencing most of the joys of life vicariously, with very little of the existential crisis that comes along with aging, disease, and various uncertainties. Their threats were specific, but armed with the humanity to power themselves onward Lux felt hopeful for his people.



    Conditions

    Crystaline: Prerequisite (Surviving the Ritual of the Beacon) Permanent unless downgraded to Brittle or Spark or with Archmage level magics.

    Upon bathing in the light of the Beacon the initiate must have their Mental Shield (Mind 2)/Prime Attainment Shield active to work as a cocoon along with the ritual prep worked through with their Mentor to create the cocoon spell itself. As the energies channel into you, your soul fires, changing your body from the inside out turning it into pure molten tass that solidifies into your new form. Roll Resolve+Composure-2 if you fail, automatically subtract two points of wisdom for the Hubris inherent in this action. If you succeed only remove one. Many mentors will spend time in council and attempting to aid in the recovery of this Wisdom loss over time. All new Cryss are watched carefully, out of care for everyone including the newly immortal mage.

    Your mind has not only survived the power of The Lighthouse it has been reforged in the celestial fire and mental agony of The Beacon. In other words, your body has been reforged into a body of tass strong as steel impervious to age and earthly disease, your soul rekindled and turned into pure aetheric flame burning within your new form and binding it together. Indeed, soul stealing of Crystal mages is possible, but it requires Prime 3 to manipulate their form to unbind the soul from the tass itself, in addition to the proper death arcana.

    Your body has been completely changed, and taken on a look very much non-human. While it still reflects your genders build, it is devoid of any biology. Life magic does not detect this new form and some effects such as Life healing, or shapeshifting do not work due to the lack of organic material to effect. Instead you are statuesque in build and look as though cut from a geode. Your face and body smooth unless you will it into other states using Mana Shift (See Spells section.), a unique spell developed by the Crytsal People. By default however, you appear as a marble mannequin, smooth with only slight fractals rippling across your crystalized self.

    In terms of a colorized description there is really only a few shades in particular. These range from bright white to deep blue within the Crystal, reflecting the fire burning bright beneath, instead of anything resembling skin, meat, bone or flesh. You have no eyes or mouth, and no longer require food or water or even air. Instead, you see through mage sight, as explained in the Legacy write up, and you require mana to live spending one Mana a day as your soul burns through your statuesque form like a body through calories. You speak through a unique form of psychic projection that seems to work only on the aural stimulatory portions of where your mind thinks your brain is. It can be blocked, but must consciously be done with mind magics. Otherwise, all Crystal Mages are silent, unless you've mastered the Prime magic of the Aether and can sing of it's majesties. Even then, your true voice can not be used for normal speech and mind magics must suffice.

    You possess a base durability of 3 and your health turns into structure, to determine "health" dots, Stamina increases after the transformation still increases the amount of health in your Pattern. You are reset to your base size (5 for most humans)+durability in health. This form does not allow you to withstand the Supernal Winds that keep those who have not achieved Ascension out of the higher realms, but given the nature of a Crystal Mage it puts them on what they feel is the best path to Ascension, many feel they will withstand those Winds one day, that much is certain.



    Brittle: Prerequisite Condition (Crystaline)
    A Crystal Mage or indeed even a Knight or Pilgrim are beings connected to mana in a way unseen by any until very recently, and now known mostly only to the Mysterium and Guardians of Portland. Their form has been permanently strengthened in many ways, but there are still many weaknesses for a Crystal Mage to shore up. If the Crystal form is removed of all its mana it becomes Brittle. One by one durability and then structure is converted into a single point of mana, one at a time until only the Spark is left, bereft of all power without a body to channel magic and focus its mind. With each point of structure lost, at structure 3 or less, magic or attainments are cast at a -3 as the state of your mind is to distracted by your breaking form to create an Imago, the body is trying to contain it's soul spark and thus can not release attainments with the normal ease of practice. At structure three or less the Cryss finds itself in a dire situation, no willpower may be used.

    Further, while Brittle any targeted attacks result in the Limbless Condition.

    Spark: Prerequisite Condition (Brittle)
    The awakened soul of a Crystal Mage or Knight. Due to it's form as celestial fire, it can not possess other forms or cast other spells, if spotted for a long period of time it may be subject to disbelief. Without any ability to truly think, the spark will run on instinct to the nearest source of open mana it can find, excess tass, or a hallow. Maybe even towards an iris into a verge. If it does not find one within twenty four hours, it's last point of mana will go out and the spark will be extinguished forever. If it finds what it seeks it must consume 5 points of mana before it can take full shape and cast spells again. Once the fifth dot is done, the mana coalesces into crystal tass around the flame once more and structure+durability is reset to the minimumm of 5 restoring the Crystaline Condition.


    Merits

    Status: The Crystal Kingdom
    The Hierarchy of the Kingdom was put in place long ago by the Mad Creator, though it was less defined having no previously human mages among its ranks. As each mage gains status, they're awarded a title once they've proven themselves in various ways. Sometimes this is simply through showing enough control to master the strange skills their body produces in the form of attainments and merits, others such as Duke and Agent, are bestowed by the King himself. While Lux did not choose the title of King, he seems to show no signs of renouncing the it either, much like the name of The Kingdom.

    Prerequisites: Status; Mysterium 3 and surviving the Ritual of the Lighthouse.

    • Initiate; The first thing taught to initiates of the Path of the Crystal Mages is how to survive in the Living Mana Fjords. The mage gains the skill specialty (Mana Fjords) added to your survival skill.

    •• Thaumist; After much training adapting to a Crystal Mage's newly formed pattern, the mage learns to attune his sense of himself, but also the sense of mana around him. Gain the one-dot merit "mana sensitivity"

    ••• Councilor; Crystal mages that reach this point are taught about the fallen world and down and dirty magic. This is where their true potential is realized, but not achieved. But due to the divine nature of the Crystal Mage's purpose, their nimbus is overbearing to most entities. Gain the "Potent Resonance" merit with the tilt " Insensate" due to the overwhelming nature of their nimbus.

    •••• Duke/Duchess/Agent of the King; This is the point where the Crystal mage is given a choice. Continue serving the Kingdom in the Mana Fjords, or become an official agent in the fallen world. If the mage chooses to stay in the Fjords, they are given three dots in the Merit "Safe Place", which is designated as the crystal lighthouse, this expands their land into any shape they wish, shaping the very Mana in the ground into buildings and landscape of their choosing. (This is cumulative with all 4-dot members) they also gain a great deal of access to the Athenaeum.

    Agents who choose to serve the lighthouse in the fallen world are given the proper training to bring back as many potential recruits and mysteries as possible. They gain 3 dots in the Merit "trained observer". They answer to only the King, and are the only ones allowed access to the Censorium without approval from the Censor or King.

    ••••• King Only one crystal mage can rise to this level. Currently this position is held by Lux, though once the Mad Creator was called such. Once at this point, you are given the Drums of Purity, the artifact created by Pudicitiam to form crystal beings, as well as the army of Crystal people and total access to the Athenaeum and Censorium.

    Artifacts

    The Mallets 2 Dot Artifact Merit- Two wooden drum mallets, when held give +1 to Prime or Mind Spells. They also count as a dedicated magical tool.Outside of these benefits it also confers on its owner the ability to issue commands to crystal beings, and imbue changes in their personality with Decrees. This is evidenced when Lux chose his First Knight, and gave him the name Bruce, growing the mind and introducing the Cryss to new concepts quickly, instead of taking weeks or months of study. When struck against a Cryss or other mana imbued objects it makes a ringing sound, not unlike a tuning fork. Various pitches represent various types of magics as a sort of revealing spell.

    The Drums The Drums are a 4 dot artifact that reside in the Mana Fjords, protected by the Cryss there and the local Mysterium. They are wielded only by the King, who can use it's magical powers to create Crystal beings, and imbue them with minds using the appropriate spell below. Playing them in combination literally creates sparks in the air that pull together into greater forms, creating an endless army. To activate this effect requires one mana and is intended to give it's owner a near endless army though without the time to cast the Mind 2 spell below, they are mindless. This makes them no less deadly, of course. The Wisdom behind using it for such purposes however has been called into question by its current owner. -

    The number of successes allow for the number of words used to describe the being being created in high speech as the drums are played, the actual character attributes, dots, personality et cet are left up to the ST based around the words decreed by the King. Roll: Prime+Expression+Artifact Drums (4)+Artifact Mallets (+1) + Gnosis


    SPELLS

    Form Shift (Prime 3)
    Functions as the Life 3 spell Transform Life, however, this spell can only work on Living Tass and the Living Tass retains it's original coloration and Crystal appearance, making it very difficult to blend in without other spells or attainments in effect.

    +2 Reach allows for the Crystal Mage to become a living walking pillar of Celestial Fire for a time. Once per turn spend one mana and reflexively roll as as though casting the Celestial Fire spell with instant casting doing dots in prime in lethal damage on a successful hit. Anything touching you also takes your prime dots in damage per turn, making grappling especially effective. This is of course incredibly destructive to almost everything around you that is combustible. With another reach this spell may be upgraded to aggravated damage.

    Endow Mind (Mind 2)
    Factor: Duration
    Cost: 2 Mana, 1WP

    Many creatures created in the Fjords either of their own will bursting forth, or by the design of the one who Wields The Drums come in maddened, with minds as limited and feral as that of any beast of the earth. This spell, developed by the Portland Mysteriums own Victor Frankenstein (4 dot Mastigos Mentor) endows creatures with mental acuity and grace. In all mindless creatures it grants a +2 to intelligence, and +1 to composure and a -1 manipulation. With crystal beings this spell has a kindling effect on their soulfire, and grants them +1 gnosis, if before they had none. They may use this gnosis in addition to their intelligence attribute to recast Endow Mind on themselves periodically to continue their existence.

    Swapping Forms
    Factor: Duration
    Cost: One mana upon activation. (See description below)
    Rote: The Last Resort (Mind+Occult+Gnosis)
    Mind 4 Prime 3


    By separating a suitable appendage from the body of a Crystaline Mage, and using it to grow a separate "body" (Using the Prime 3 spell platonic form), the mage can create a vessel using their pattern from the severed appendage, in case he or she face the worst. This spell then creates a psychic link projected into the clone body which shares the same pattern as the crystal mage.

    Keeping that link open, the mage creates an escape from death itself, rolled reflexively just before the moment of death (when one or zero health boxes remain) for the price of one mana to snap the crystal mages conscious soul into the new body preserving their life to fight another day. The Crystal Mage receives the Soul Shocked condition upon waking in her/his new body. Obviously, keeping a statue that is basically you around can be incredibly dangerous. Most crystal mages opt to leave them in the Fjords, and use a space spell, or an imbued item to hold the connection between them in place rather than risk bringing them along on their journeys.

    SHOCKED
    Soul Shocked: Your character has been killed while on a sojourn outside her physical body, resulting in a shocked sense of self. Upon gaining this Condition, roll her current Willpower points (not dots) as a dice pool. Ignore 10-Again on this roll, and do not roll a chance die if she has no Willpower left. She keeps one Willpower point per success, and immediately loses the remainder. While this Condition is in effect, your character does not regain Willpower from Virtue, Vice, or equivalent traits. She still regains Willpower from rest, surrender, and any other means of regaining Willpower.
    Resolution: Regaining full Willpower.


    Crystal Familiar (Prime 3)
    Factor: Duration
    The mage casts a spell that creates a Familiar bond between a Crystal Beast and a Crystal Mage, who must both be subjects of the spell. The Lesser Crystal Beings stats are determined at creation by the Story Teller. The mage gains the Crystal Familiar Merit the Crystal Beast gains the Bonded Mana condition, replacing Animal Ken with Occult due to the nature of Crystal Beasts existence. Both parties must be willing, and can end the bond whenever they wish.
    Bonded Mana: Your character has established an extensive bond with a specific crystal beast. She gains +2 on any rolls to influence or persuade her bonded animal. It may add your Occult to any rolls to resist coercion or fear when in your character’s presence. The animal may add your character’s Occult skill to any one die roll, and gains the Nimbus of whoever it has bonded with and all the subsequent effects associated with that Nimbus. For one mana the Crystal mage may see and cast through the eyes of their familiar, but their body is left behind unprotected for the duration of this effect.
    Resolution: The bonded animal dies or is otherwise parted permanently from the character.

Due to these differences I feel Prime 3 is level appropriate.

    Crystal Mage Legacy Write-up 4th Draft.

    Origins
    Parentage: Mastigos, Obrimos, or Mysterium.

    Background: Crystal mages come from all parts of the Mysterium, but many have one thing in common. They seek to be closer to the Supernal. To magic. To distance themselves from the Fallen World while still aiding it, or pilfering its artifacts and information. To become living vessels of magical knowledge and power. Further, they seek to pass on these things to future students until their spark goes out, or they attain Ascension. Given the lack of physical difference, it is ideology that binds their backgrounds together, as all else slowly fades away to the pull of the Mysteries.

    There are many perks that would draw a profligate to the Legacy. A genuine passive form of immortality, passive meaning one without theft of soul or the need for murder or other Left Handed magics. While not perfect, you still can die...it does give you eternity if you are careful while being more attuned to the magical energies you obsess over.

    However, magic is always a double edged sword in the Fallen World, and a verge is no more the Supernal than an apartment is a mansion. This new body is far from perfection, though for the right mages it is closer. After the transformation the Initiate loses the ability to enjoy the things that make life worth living for many mages not completely detached from the more human elements of life as a mage. No longer can you enjoy the thrill of sex or the deliciousness of food. Inebriation is impossible without magical aid, and certainly mingling is far more difficult. However, given that their leader is a Mastigos and hedonist, there are studies being done to advance those elements in the directions of responsible indulgences and there is a seeming eternity to see how to make it more enjoyable for those interested in such baser pursuits.

    Appearance: See Crystaline Condition. Otherwise, the mages of the legacy more often than not are wearing imbued items, or magical artifacts rather than any specific style. Pragmatism, and magical function above all else. It's worth noting that despite being able to cast images of humans, the moment a woman or man moves in for a kiss they surely would notice a lack of lips and a hardened stone like substance that runs very hot due to the soul fire beneath it, rather than flesh. Suspicions would almost certainly be raised from any level of intimacy with sleepers, or anyone else the mage was trying to conceal her true form from. This could lead to spell failure, and possible death via the literal unraveling of your very being due to the forces of paradox and disbelief. Because of this, many Crystal Mages choose to call the Pure Fjords home, traveling out as necessary into the Fallen World to seek their Obsessions.

    Doctrine
    Indoctrination; Prime 3, Mind 2 (though technically you could join with Prime 2 Mind 1) Status: Mysterium 3.

    Initiation: To join requires the Ritual of the Lighthouse, which requires a higher level of skill in the Ruling Arcana to join the legacy than most other legacies require. The reason for this is that you must be able to shield yourself from Prime and Mind magics in order to cocoon ones self to survive the ordeal, further these attainments come naturally to the Crystal Mages as they progress in their studies and power, therefore much of the mentor student bond begins long before the Profligate undergoes the Ritual of the Lighthouse Beacon, which is only allowed when the Mentor deems his Pupil ready, and not a moment sooner. When a Mentor walks his pupil up the stairs of the Lighthouse, which is inaccessible without at least one Crystal Mage in company, due to the nature of the building and the need for Soulfire to trigger the beacon. The sponsoring Mentor is often walking a friend who he will know for potentially centuries to come.

    By willingly signing your Shadow Name to the Lighthouse of the Living Mana Fjord in mana and blood, and lighting the Beacon with Celestial Fire from your mentor you can alter your form forever, should you survive. You must climb the stairs and undertake the reforging of your body, mind and soul into one being of pure tass by celestial fire. The Lighthouse Beacon directs the energies of Pandemonium and the Aether into a painful transition from Human Mage to Crystal Mage, rewriting your physical pattern in the process. Upon survival the newly minted mage gains all the positive and negative traits of the Crystal Species, and is welcomed as a brother or sister among their new people, whose primary hope in their nearly immortal life is to study existence through magic. (See "Crystaline"in conditions)

    Theory: Though the lighthouse was created to forge an army for Pura and turn humanity into magical slaves, killing all those who could not withstand its light, it has been repurposed, and Lux has formed it's changes into a proper Legacy with purpose far from its baser creators vision.

    Knowledge must be preserved. Magic is Alive. The Mysterium teaches these truths, but the Crystal Mages live them. The vision Lux has imposed by shaping his soul and leading this Legacy is by and large, nothing less than the hope that every mage inducted will become paragons of the Mysterium, much as he himself is attempting to do. By using the Lighthouses gift properly, and with wisdom, they can insure first hand knowledge is taught to those worthy of it for centuries to come, perhaps even longer.

    Magic
    Yantras: Soul Fire: +2 then +1 every turn there after. A Crystal Mage may lay their spells with their inner Soul Fire, increasing in strength as the flame grows.
    Oblations: Shaping tass into sculptures. Communing with other Cryss. Meditating on the meaning and purpose of Humanity, and how best to preserve magic within the ideals the Legacy puts forward.


    Attainments
    1st Attainment: 1 The Crystal Sight (Prime 1)
    With this attainment the mage gains use of the Prime Sight attainment at all times, almost like a background program running quietly over the blue haze of life through crystal sight. It also does not cost a Willpower to Scrutinize, though the time limits are still in play. This makes them unparalleled investigators into occult happenings. The new form, fully crystallized as it is, allows the user to "see" normally as well, though it is as if looking through blue or azure tinted glasses.

    Optional (Mind 1) As above, with Mind Sight. It is worth noting that most Legacies do not function in this way, thus proving the power and mystery of the Lighthouses transformative properties. Some think that the fusion of attainments into a more permanent state is due to the change fusing part of the soul into the very tass that makes their up their anatomy.

    2nd Attainment: The Crystal Veil.
    With this attainment the mage may instinctively use the magic inherent to their form to veil themselves in a manner similar to the Prime 2 spell Supernal Veil. +1 Reach: instant cast.

    Optional (Mind 2) The mind of a Crystal mage is not like that of a human. The Beacon forever reshaped them, changing every aspect. Their mind has been reworked from it's previous biological pattern into cavernous and reflective thing. Intruders may get lost in if they aren't familiar to the mind within. This attainment acts like the Mind 2 spell Mental Shield (Different than the Mind 2 Attainment Mind Shield) with success equal to Mind dots.
    +1 Reach: to protection against spells from the Mind Arcanum. +1 Reach to instant casting.

    3rd (Prime 3): The Crystal Form
    The body of a Crystal Mage is pure, living tass. It does not age, nor starve or waste away with time or die of natural causes. To some, this makes the Crystal people an affront to nature but to the Crystal People it simply is how they are. Nothing more, or less. With this attainment, the crystal mage can shape their body to their will, as though using the Prime 3 spell Form Shift, a spell unique to the Crystal People.(see "Spells").
    +1 reach: instant cast.

    Optional (Mind 3) Along with the ability to reshape their body they can use their Crystal Form to project an illusion through themselves making them look like another, usually human, person, though there's no reason to think that another crystal mage couldn't assume the form of another size five creature, after they've rearranged their form properly with their prime 3 attainment. This attainment functions as the Mind 3 spell Imposter.
    +1 reach: instant cast.

    4th (Prime 4): The Eternal Spark.
    Deep within every Crystal form is a spark of the Aether, and with this attainment that Spark can be turned into a roaring fire, to protect the dimensional traveling students of the Crystal Mage Legacy. Functioning as the Prime 4 Spell Celestial Fire.
    +1 Reach: instant casting and +1 Reach: In lieu of damage, the mage may assign Potency to instead destroy the target’s Mana. One level of Potency so assigned destroys one point of Mana, and Potency may be freelysplit between Mana destruction and damage.

    Optional Mind 4 The swirling and refracting patterns of fire beneath the crystal mages form are sometimes found to be incredibly hypnotic. By directing the soul fire in certain patterns, the mage can Befuddle their marks just like the spell of the same name. This of course makes the target easier to slip past, or overwhelm mentally in order to more efficiently cast other mind magics to further the ends of the Mysterium or their King.
    +1 Reach: instant casting. +1 reach: to dividing the spell potency between two traits, if the mage wishes.

    5th (Prime 5) Celestial Majesty
    With this the Crystal Mage learns to speak with her true voice, not simply a projection from her mind out, but a song that sings forth from them as their Celestial Fire flares within, like water over crystal it sounds, piercing fallen reality into the supernal, creating a Hallow. This attainment acts as the Prime 5 Spell Create Truth.
    +1 reach: instant casting. +1 reach: Opens an Iris linked to the Verge of the Living Mana Fjords, with or without a key if left open afterwards at the casters discretion.

    Optional (Mind 5) As the Crystal Mages learned earlier, their minds are vastly different than humans, having learned to harness the spark within, they can use that flame to cast shadows, like Platos illusory cave except made real with will and magic to aid in protecting the Crystal Mage in their eternal quest for knowledge. This attainment functions as the Mind 4 spell Hallucination.
    +1 reach: instant casting. +1 reach: As above, but the mage can make the hallucination tactile to the subject. The illusion cannot attack or harm the subject in any way, but when he interacts with it, the subject believes he can feel the illusion. This may mean that a large illusion, such as a wall or other barrier, may make the subject believe he is incapable of passing through, barring his way.

      Story II, Act I: The Cult of Ak'tung (2nd Draft)

      Having defeated Pura, and instilled the Cryss with a portion of humanity. Having founded The Kingdom of the Lighthouse in a Verge. One would think, Lux might take a day or two off. However, as night fell over Portland he wouldn't have time to even contemplate his victory or what it means to be King. As he walked through his newly created Athenaeum having finally finished the "construction" he noticed something out of place. Something he nor the others created.

      There stood in one of the halls a ticking grandfather clock. It shone with a strange Nimbus, and when investigated a man quickly appeared. He was short, fat, with long greasy dirty blonde hair tied back in a pony tail. He stank of garbage and his eyes were a deep dark black, around them it seemed was caked and dried blood that started at his tear ducts and went all the way around, like some grim finger painted racoon. His nimbus, clearly abyss touched rang true and for a moment, everyone in the palatial sanctuary held still as the threat presented itself. He introduced himself as Locksley, and he came bearing a warning. If he could get in, so could others from a dangerous cult he had recently escaped from. The Cult of Ak'tung.

      Immediately Lux grabbed his trusted friend Chord, the punk rocker Obrimos who was willing to sever all of the Aether from Portland to keep it out of the hands of Puras army. Together, using the contacts the Paradox touched Locksley left, they found their way to an old Junkyard, where one of the Cryss had come through several months earlier. The place felt different. Things had...changed. Lux called in a favor to his old friend Victor, and had one of their cabal members meet them here. She could lead them passed the traps. Allow for a meeting with the possibly dangerous scelesti that lurked within. From what the pair could gather, she was their former mentor and guide. This would be...difficult, but not violent...at least that was what Pheobe said.

      Pheobe was odd. Victor and Sylvia didn't tlak about her much, but they were still cabal mates. Some rumours around the concillia state she had accidentally murdered one or several people when a construct of matter and mind went awry. As such she lost some of the respect of the locals, and had gone into a self imposed exile. This was, as far as Lux and Chord knew, the first time she had been out and about. Her pupil, Machina, had disappeared shortly after that, and only resurfaced recently. Hiding and ever so paradox ridden, judging by the Resonance of the area.

      The Junk Yard was a maze, literally. It had been warped by space and matter magics. Patrolling monstrosities of old cars turned into towering golems patrolling the maze like so many locked up mechanical Minotaur. Chord gulped. Lux would have if he still had the ability. The whole thing wreaked of oil, metal, and blood. Grinding gears and cogs screeched. Having to fight their way through this maze would have surely been the place of the Arrow, not two Mystagogues. Instead, they followed Pheobe. Hopefully not to their Deaths.

      Eventually after twenty or so minutes of direct navigation, what could've surely been hours or days in the space riddled maze, they found its center. Atop one of the Golems sat Machina, he appeared strong, but haggard. Around his eyes the same blood smeared paradox touched wounds oozed. He saw through Lux's projected self almost immediately. He gave a laugh, and looked over to someone else, who introduced themselves as Karma.

      Karma, unlike his younger, fitter protege was tall and slender. He had many scars all along his body. He too had The Eyes. He too could see. He too projected fear. This whole thing, as Lux analyzed it with his various skills and magics, came into focus. This was a fortress. They were hiding. As Pheobe and the two talked briefly, Lux factored everything in and consulted with the powers of Time. These were no longer cult members. They had...escaped? Bought freedom? The questions kept piling up. This was the work of the Guardians.

      The Crystal King spoke up. "Where can we find more information on your previous allegiances?" The question shook the two. They hadn't told the Mastigos that and one scowled as he realized he'd been distracted by Pheobe.

      "Why would we tell you?" It was Karma. His voice had a grating sort of tone.

      "Because you're running." Lux's mental telepathy echoed loudly in the center of the Maze. It spoke with Truth. They could not argue.

      "Fine...you wish to find The Cult? You wish to seek Ak'tung...." The two debated back and forth what information they would give. They didn't want any of it to lead back to them, and thus it could only be minute. Specific.

      There was a fresh outpost established. They heard about it after they left but before the information had spread to every contact they held. It was an occult shop, just south of portland. Close enough for it to make sense someone from the Portland Concilia might be snooping. Pheobe simply made a face, and left. Her favor 'complete'. Not wishing to spend more time in...this place....Lux opened a portal to elsewhere in the city and from there they could head south.

      When the two arrived they found the what amounted to a recruiting station filled with books on the Abyss and Paradox. During the first infiltration Lux and Chord headed inside, triggering several alarms due to Lux's state of being. After a few smooth lies from the Mastigos and some distraction ala Chord breaking several of the glass bottled 'potions' on the shelves, the two had managed to get Chord kicked out and Lux lured deeper into a secret sanctum underneath the building. There were all kinds of books on paradox, the abyss, forbidden lore any mysterium member in their right mind would lock up. The Censorium however, was lacking in its current state. Lux would need to call the Guardians in on this later....or maybe sooner. Recently, the Warlock had some breakthroughs in the school of Time. Thoughts began to circulate and a plan formed. There would be a few things to do first. Memorize a couple of books. Milk as much information from the cult members here as possible. Figure out what to do about the horde of vampires they passed on the way in... Fire? Fire.

      Having gathered the information he wanted, Lux cast his spell. Shifting Sands.

      In a blaze of light Lux reversed the world around him by simply stepping through a door. Suddenly he found himself outside a ways from the shop with Chord once more, plotting how they would get inside. Lux explained he had what they needed. The Guardians could take care of the Rest, they'd just help out. Using a scrying window he contacted his Guardian Allies, the same crew who made sure he wasn't plotting to over throw the world with his army when he became the new King. They were good, methodical, and everything you'd hope a Guardian was. Easily they breached the first part of the shop, but let Lux and Chord have fun with the Vampires given their skills with Celestial and regular good old fashioned Fire respectively.

      Ah, yes, the vampires. The vampires were easily dispatched, between the Obrimos' skill with Forces, and Lux's newly learned ability to turn himself into walking Celestial Fire. Lux called in the guardians to question the cult members, two awakened mages that The Crystal King knocked unconscious with a powerful mind spell. The Guardians gave their word, infused with Lux's prime magic to make them speak only the truth that he would get a full and accurate report for the safety of his Kingdom. As the ashes settled, only a single woman remained, a sleeper, bound to a post and blindfolded. At first it was assumed she was just a snack for the now-dust-previously-vampire, but upon further examination it was found, her soul was missing too.

      There were a few other things of note. A ritual to keep away a mysterious being known only as Ak'tung. It required the blood of a soulless lover. It seemed, much like Locksley these cultists were trying to get away from their master...

      As if that wasn't enough, Lux still had other things on his mind. Obsessions about Destiny, his Kingdom, aspirations of finding new mages to join his legacy. Worthy ones. Furthering the collection of his Athenaeum. Fear that this Aktung might destroy everything he'd achieved thus far, or that his headstrong friend Chord might make the wrong move and they would once more be at odds as they were when he wanted to sever ties with the Supernal.

      Story II, Act II: The Devouring Pool (First Draft)
      After setting ablaze the Scelesti Cults dungeon, destroying several vampires with the help of his Orbrimos companion Chord, The Crystal King wanted nothing more than to return home to his Verge...which was still an odd thought rolling through his mind, and await the details removed from the cult members by his allies in the Guardians of the Veil. Finally, he had some time to himself. Time to reflect, and understand his new position in the Cosmos. He spent several hours creating and imbueing a crown. At first it was just a focus for his oblation, a chance to quiet the demons running through the Mastigos' mind. But soon, it was too perfect to simply disperse back into the tass that formed it. Instead he imbued it with some protective traits of the Time magic he had been studying with Sylvia, Victors Acanthus wife. The Mysterium Witch had managed to give the Warlock much to work with, though he understood it differently than his counterpart.

      Through the lense of this Warlock time was just a facet of space and your minds awareness of that moment. After all, time wasn't really around in Pandemonium but here, in the Fallen World it was simply the effect of your position in the universe and how long you've sat in entropy. Space, chaining all things together made scrying through time a matter of something...not all together strange. The chains he pulled on certainly felt odd. Instead of demons rustling back, it was almost like he was pulling cogs this way and that on the other end to accomplish his Imagos framework. As he focused his will and added the spells,the Disciple of Time created an anchor and shield in the crown, with the spells Constant Presence, and Shield of Chronos, as well as employing his skills in the Mind Arcanum for a modified form of One Mind Two Thoughts linked to his body double that he kept upon the throne as a last means of escape should the worst happen. Now, he could always be able to answer his subjects as necessary without having to limit his exploration of the Mysteries. This application of the spell with the unique body double was something of a discovery for Lux, as he continued to find benefits within the magical form his mind and soul now resides in.

      Another benefit was the inability and lack of need to sleep, surely he would find no more rest or reflection for a time. With something of a practiced sigh, The Crystal King stood as he heard one of his subjects approaching from behind him. The now crowned King bowed his head respectfully as the Cryss before him bowed respectfully, and lower. It was Adam. The first pilgrim to come through an Iris and be discovered by the Mysterium. Lux had saved him from being magically dissected for study by a darker soul. Brimstone, the Moros Censor who the Mysterium had cast out after Lux's intervention had captured Adam before Lux could get to him. After Adam recovered from the traumatic experience, he became an able bodied organizer, and somewhat of a strategist during a few moments where Lux needed someone to look after those who were less combative of the Cryss. For this, Lux had made him an advisor in the Kingdom, and named him a Curator of the Athenaeum. His role was mostly insuring the knights went on their rounds through the building, and cataloging the various finds the Mysterium stored here as well as hearing from others about the explorations some of the bolder members of the species had undertaken.

      Adams voice shook with concern as he spoke. "Your Majesty! I hate to interrupt, I know you're busy. But we uh...have another problem." His Kings mind raced, wondering what on earth it could be. Locksley again? Ak'tungs henchmen? Chord got his head stuck in a keg? As the last one hit, the Mastigos banished those distracting thoughts. "What's your report?"

      "Well sir. We've had a few of our citizens go missing, as well as some Knights." That was worrisome.

      "Where? How?"

      "They were exploring outside of The Kingdom, your Majesty. Beyond that I do not know. One of the knights survived, and made contact the usual way." The Cryss taped the side of his head, a little mana-fire flaring beneath the geod like surface.

      Knowing they were connected by a mental link, Lux took a moment to steady himself and joined in on the Mind spell. After the link was established, he opened a portal, calling forward the a rune for travel he had studies some time ago in a very different library. It grew and crackled with soulfire and bright, bright light. A high pitched hum echoed within the palace as the portal stabilized. He then stepped through to the other side, where a lone Knight crouched down. It shined with a deep blue beneath its sculpted features, and bowed awkwardly, as it tried to stay hidden behind the rocks. Carefully, it peaked over the edge, down the cliffs edge below before moving the twelve or so feet and standing before the Mage.

      "Your Majesty! You...you came yourself?"

      Lux would've grinned if he were casting a human facade. "I'm not welcome?" He said jestingly. The Knights fire flared, almost like a blush. Embarrassment? Is that how we show embarrassment? The Mage King wondered. He had been in this form, with these responsibilities for less than a few months at this point and was still learning. Good thing he didn't have to hide facial expressions here. In his Kingdom. In his Verge...Inwardly the mage also wondered, and not for the first time, if he was in over his head. Still, if you're going through hell, the Mastigos definitely knew to keep on going. Until you found a nice spot. Then you set up your own Kingdom, with hookers and blackjack. Or at least memories of hookers and blackjack. It was a start... "I'm kidding, Knight. Thank you for alerting Adam, and yes. I've came myself, what's your status?"

      The Knight shared memories with his King. He had left the safety of the walled Kingdom to explore the unknown and see what else was out there in The Fjords. His party had come upon this plateau after a several days worth of surveying and mapping out their route. Then they came to this cliffs edge, and found a path down leading to a pool of water, instead of the aetheric flames that make up the surrounding ocean. As they approached several were attacked. Lured in by some sirens call and dissolved, growing the Lake, which errupted violently in foamy splashes. The Knight couldn't make out most of what happened, but managed to be the only one to get away instead of standing around getting lake-murdered. Lux couldn't fault him. Lux also was not about to approach that damned lake by himself...
