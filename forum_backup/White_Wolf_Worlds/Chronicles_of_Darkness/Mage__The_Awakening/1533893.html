    Homebrewed Legacies 2nd edition

    04-09-2024, 12:37 PM
    I drafted these legacies up for my players. I'm still tweaking things but figured I would post them up for some feedback (my players are not overly familiar with Mage) and just in case they spark anyones interest. Each one is designed with a particular characters paradigm and their obsessions in mind but I would like to see how people think they hold up on their own as I have never looked much into legacies before in 2nd edition.

    House Rule: Normally if you already had the primary arcana of the legacy as ruling you would not get much except an XP discount on buying dots but that feels sucky, So I have written every legacy to have a secondary associated arcana. If you already have the primary arcana as ruling from your path, then you gain the secondary as ruling instead.

    -----
    Sin Eaters
    Arcanthus Variant

    Origins
    Parentage: Mastigos with Arcanthus variant, Guardians of the Veil
    Background: Born of catholic welsh tradition and reflected in a number of other world cultures, these nameless superstitions found their way into the Esoteric Tenants of the Guardians of the Veil who found themselves disgusted at the callous acts of hubristic necromancers. The act of taking another's burden onto themselves was already a common practice among the guardians and the order has allowed the legacy to flourish beyond its ranks as a way of teaching outsiders some small semblance of the Esoteric Tenants.
    Appearances: Many Sin Eaters are or become religious, and chose to don the paraphernalia and uniform of their chosen faith. The particularly hard working may begin to put on weight from the eating customs required and form shadows and bags under the eyes from countless sleepless nights from carrying the hardships of so many others.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Death 2, Empathy 2, and two dots in one of the following: Persuasion, Socialise or Expression
    Initiation: The aspirant must show it can treat the dead with respect and understanding and help a quarrelsome ghost resolve its anchors.
    Organisation: Sin Eaters only gather in mass for two reasons. The first is the occurrence of a large and sudden amount of traumatic death in an attempt to ease so many new ghosts and save as many as possible from the horrors of the underworld. There have always been a small number of sleepers who continue to practise Sin Eating without the advantages of supernal insights, but on occasion members of the legacy have encountered far stranger beings. Former sleepers that have merged their souls with powerful ghosts to fulfil the same role, and even claim to have founded it.
    Theory: Sin Eater mages believe that the phenomenon of a ghost is no mere echo, but a fundamental component of a soul stripped away from its whole. They do what they can to resolve anchors when possible, rather than destroy them, to assist the ghost in transcending the fallen world and reconnecting with its supernal self. They often practise this through the sharing of a meal and simple listening skills as oblation. Some go further, absorbing the ghosts' own traumas and fateful misfortunes into themselves, or taking up sworn oaths to fulfil promises after their parting. Traumatic or unresolved death can tear the sacred soul. For the Soul to heal, it must be rejoined with its lost echo. For this to occur, the Ghost must transcend their death by accepting it, but as they are damaged few possess the fortitude to do so. Since time immemorial people have bonded and shared their troubles over simple acts of hospitality. To share a meal with the dead is to remind them of life and forge that vital connection needed to pass their sins and troubles to one whose soul is still strong enough to bear them. To a Sin Eater, the Underworld is a cursed place, a lower plane even farther from the supernal truth than the fallen world. The more religious call it for how they see it. Hell.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Death
    (Secondary Arcanum: Mind)
    Yantras: Succeeding in an empathy check relevant to the spell (+2), Eating a prepared meal requested by the ghost subject to the spell (+2), Delivering a message on behalf of the deceased subject to the spell (+1),Religious symbols, tools and sacraments (+1)
    Oblations: Creation of a memorial. Consuming a meal made of ectoplasm. Indulge in the vices of another in their place. Delivering an obituary or eulogy.

    Attainments

    First: Speaker for the Dead
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The first requirement for any Sin Eater is to be able to communicate with the deceased without reliance on the limitations of mage sight. As per ‘Speak with the Dead’ spell on p128. Cast instantly. Lasts for 1 day. Target self.
    Optional: Fate 1
    The mage reads the imprint doom has left on the dead. With a glance, they reveal the influences, bans and banes of any Ghosts of Rank 2 or lower.

    Second: Stoic Preparation
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    Dealing with the dead is a risky business. Even well meaning ghosts can often become dangerous, corrupting the body and mind of those they have fixated upon. A sin-eater hoping to last long, needs to ward himself appropriately. As per ‘Ghost Shield’ p129, Ritual casting. Last for 1 day. Target self.
    Optional: Fate 2
    De-escalation is the sin eaters first defence. Especially when dealing with a ghost for the first time. Gain dice bonus of +3 for 3 social rolls of your choice against ghosts for the duration.

    Third: Conduit of Twilight
    Prerequisites: Death 3, Gnosis 4
    Ectoplasm is the substance that bridges the ephemeral with the material for ghosts. It is the substances their manifested bodies are made out of and it is often left in the wake of their numina. It can be shaped and repurposed to allow the ghost to more easily interact with the world. Sin Eaters use it to share a meal with a ghost. As per ‘Ectoplasm’ p129. Instant casting. Lasts for 1 week. May be used to create the “Open” condition. Target self, touch, or aimed.
    Optional: Mind 1
    There is an innately psychic quality to ectoplasm that a skilled will-worker can easily tap into. If the ectoplasm worked is consumed by another, you may learn their virtue, vice, and aspirations.

    Forth: Exorcism
    Prerequisites: Death 4, Gnosis 6
    Desperate ghosts can cling too hard to save themselves the pain of the fallen world. Often a Sin Eater needs to be stern with his charge and remove it from whatever object or living person they have fettered to. As per ‘Exorcism’ p132. Instant cast. Remove 4 manifestations. Lasting. Does not prevent further manifestations. Target self, touch, or aimed.
    Optional: Expression 3
    With a practised Abjuration, draw the ghost back to its nearest anchor and bind it there, inflicting the Cowed condition. Lasts for the scene. This will have no effect on a ghost with no anchors.

    Fifth: Pollbearer
    Prerequisites: Death 5, Gnosis 8
    Many ghosts are not capable of revelation. They are too badly damaged by their own death. Often, they require uplifting and strengthening before they have the capacity to resolve their anchors. As per ‘Quicken Ghost’ p131. Instant casting. Lasts 1 week. Spend 6 potency on the ghosts attributes. Increase rank by 1. The ghost becomes fully aware of its death. Target self, touch, or aimed.
    Optional: Prime 3
    The ghost loses all capacity for lying and self-delusion, and must confront its raw and unadulterated Truth. When the duration lapses, if they have not resolved their anchors, the Ghost suffers a Breaking Point.

    Paladins of the Siege Perilous


    Origins
    Parentage: Mastigos, Mysterium and Seers of the Throne
    Background: Steeped and inspired by Arthurian legend and the grail quest, the Paladins of the Siege Perilous believe the story of camelot, the grail and the fisher king is all a mutilated myth either inspired by or directly referencing Atlantis. The fisher kings' wound and mundane activities being symbolic for the fallen world's fate, with the lost holy relic of the grail being synonymous for Atlantis itself. As a result they don the mantle of quester knights, like percival/galahad, and look for ruins and relics of the lost city. They do have a small controversy however, as while the legacy is largely members of the mysterium, there is a split-off faction belonging to the seers of the throne who follow the same. The title “siege perilous” refers to the seat merlin reserved for the one who finds the grail and another translation for that could be “Throne”. As such some have accused the legacy of originating with the Seers entirely, or even being an indoctrination cult for them but the Mysterium denies the latter as paranoia.
    Appearances: Paladins embrace the aesthetics of mediaeval chivalric knights of legend when appropriate. Particularly for their rituals.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Prime 2, Space 2, Academics 2, Gnosis 2
    Initiation: The aspirant must swear a binding oath to the round table.
    Organisation: True to the ideals of the round table, the Paladins are an order of equals, with rank being purely honorific. Any significant decisions are made by vote. They hold court in a Temenos stronghold reflecting the ruins of camelot.
    Theory: The legacy practises a fusion of space and prime magic to scry and explore magical phenomena along leylines, as well as protect themselves against millenia old spells that may have been warped or corrupted over time. In their eyes the prize of the Siege Perilous is a reflection of their own journey to bring the world back to a better time, a perfect time, when humanity was free to explore the mysteries of soul and when the supernal truth was not obstructed by the abyss. But such a prize must be earned. The Grail represents all the knowledge that was lost, and all that must be regained before the Paladins can march on the Supernal Gates.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Prime
    (Secondary Arcanum: Space)
    Yantras: Ceremonial Sword (+1), Succeeding an Academics check relevant to the spell (+2), Reciting your Arthurian oath (+1), Girding of armour and regalia of a mediaeval chivalric knight (+1-2)
    Oblations: Creation of a memorial. Consuming a meal made of ectoplasm. Indulge in the vices of another in their place. Delivering an obituary or eulogy.

    Attainments

    First: Follow their Footsteps
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The Paladin must read the power in the land if they wish to find their treasure. As per ‘Sacred Geometry’ p166. Instant casting. Lasts for 2 turns. Will reveal Hallows. Target self.
    Optional: Space 1
    To truly glimpse the supernal workings of the land gives a Paladin versed in Space an additional awareness of their own environment. As per ‘The Outwards and Inward Eye’ p174. Also reveals the presence of hidden spaces (though does nothing to let you see in darkness).

    Second: Eye of the Merlin
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    It is not enough to know magic when you see it. It must be understood. As per ‘Supernal Vision’ p166. Instant Cast. Sensory Range. You learn: The highest arcana present, and its rating, its nimbus, and the supernal world it aligns to.
    Optional: Time 1
    Any experienced relic hunter will know that quick decisions can save your life. A basic understanding of Time can tell you if what you’re looking at is going to be trouble or not. As per ‘Momentary Flux’ p187.

    Third: Fisher King
    Prerequisites: Prime 3, Gnosis 4
    Casting supernal magic is often like shooting a flare into the sky. Any willworker and magical ward worth its salt will notice. Often it pays to dress down for the occasion. This will hide your workings from passive supernal perceptions. As per ‘Supernal Veil’ p168. Instant cast. Lasts the scene. Targets self and the next 2 spells cast.
    Optional: Space 3
    A space mage can be even more cautious, and look before he leaps. As per ‘Scrying’ p174.Window is one way and must be used with at least a medium sympathetic connection.

    Forth: The Purity of Galahad
    Prerequisites: Prime 4, Gnosis 6
    All too often inquisitive and clumsy mages can corrupt the very mystery they are looking to solve. Any student of the Mysterium should know to clear up the mess their nimbus might leave. As per ‘Cleanse Pattern’ p168. Instant casting. Sensory Range.
    Optional: Space 4
    A nimbus can leak through spatial connections and sympathies, but with enough knowledge of Space you may choose to permanently sever any connection you have made with the target.

    Fifth: Excalibur
    Prerequisites: Prime 5, Gnosis 8
    A true Merlin of the Siege Perilous can render any magical threat safe for long enough to finish their task. As per ‘Supernal Dispellation’ p170. Instant cast. Lasts for 3 turns. Sensory range. Potency 8
    Optional: Mind 3
    When attempting to outsmart a magical threat, it helps to be smarter. As per ‘Augment Mind’ p161. +2 to Resolve and Wits attributes for the duration, potentially above their normal maximum.

    Rivermen of the Hifton

    Origins
    Parentage: Thrysus, Silver Ladder
    Background: Born of Mandaean myth, this millenia old legacy credits John the Baptist as its founder, though centuries of rivalries with Seers, Lycans, and other sleepwalkers have obfuscated much of its origins. The ethnic religion that inspired it has become very endangered and none raised by it have awoken to the supernal in almost a century. To survive, the legacy has been forced to diversify to ensure its teachings and traditions are observed, as well as its hopes to achieve its ultimate goal.
    Appearances: Traditional ceremonial wear is plain white cloth. They seem to regularly develop what many dismiss as a “lazy eye” as they quickly learn to see in two realities at once.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Spirit 2, Persuasion 2, either Athletics, Crafts, or Survival at 2
    Initiation: Baptism is at the heart of the Mandaean belief system, and so baptism is required. To cleanse the soul as well as the body. To be a true Riverman however, more is required. A spirit-walk from one natural locus to another, alone, naked, and with nothing to aid you. The Rivermen are hesitant to encourage initiation until they are certain the supplicant has the skills and knowledge needed to survive and successfully negotiate the brutal politics of the spirit courts without ending up as prey.
    Organisation: Their numbers are small, and widely spread. Since the 70s it’s only master, the last surviving awakened Mandaean, has prioritised safeguarding the scattered remnants of his persecuted culture from further attacks, leaving his students, talented as they are, to largely teach themselves and each other as best they can.
    Theory: The Rivermen of the Hifton see mandaean myth as an inspired interpretation of the relationship between the “World of light”, which they translate as the Supernal, and the “World of darkness”, which they see as relating to the Shadow, or spirit world. There are many stories and legends within supernatural circles of The Fall, though they differ wildly in their interpretations and terminology. One such legend is of a border land that once existed between the Material and the Spiritual worlds, but after some terrible event, or as a slow and inevitable consequence of Atlantis’s fall, this borderland collapsed in on itself to become the Gauntlet, the veil between worlds. The Lycan’s call this lost world “Pangaea”, a name that has seeped into academic studies of ancient land masses. The rivermen believe that glimpses of that ancient lost land can be seen and studied within the Gauntlet, and that it still exists. They believe that it was borderland not only between the material and the spiritual, but the supernal as well, similar to how the Astral Realms allow for ascension. That Pangaea is not just some lost land but a road to Truth that can be followed. A sacred river that runs through all reality.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Spirit
    (Secondary Arcanum: Space)
    Yantras: Ritually scribed ceramic bowl (+1). Successful Persuasion check to Negotiate (+2). Following a boundary or divide (+1), Twilight (sunsets and sunrises, or whenever the Sun and the Moon are both visible in the sky) (+2)
    Oblations: Cleansing remains a devotion of the Rivermen though any awakened can do so. Unique to them are devotions of: Negotiating a compromise between two opposing parties. Crossing a locus at twilight. Travelling along a river.

    Attainments

    First: Through the Clear Waters
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    As a Riverman first refines his soul to understanding the nature of the Guantlet, peering across it becomes a simple matter of Will. As per ‘Exorcist’s Eye’ p180. See any Spirit in twilight, reaching or fettered and see across the gauntlet to the Shadow as though reaching. Cast instantly. Lasts for 1 day. Range Self.
    Optional: Mind 1
    It is a difficult thing for a Riverman to learn the workings of the Mind, but rewarding, as they no longer become distracted or overwhelmed by the difficulty of seeing two worlds at once. They suffer no penalties to any perception while this attainment is in use.

    Second: Prepare the Vessel
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    To walk between worlds and without protection is to invite challenge. Too many would-be Spirit-Walkers fall to possession and undue influence of selfish and maligned spirits. As per ‘Ephemeral Shield’ p181. Does not protect from physical attacks. Cast as a ritual. Lasts for the week. Range self.
    Optional: Space 1
    As the Riverman grows to understand the nature of the Gauntlet, he should, ideally, be working towards understanding of the Arcana of boundaries as well. With a basic grasp of the Knowing practices of Space, he will always know where the nearest Locus in the gauntlet is while this attainment is active.

    Third: Honoured Walker
    Prerequisites: Spirit 3, Gnosis 4
    To know the gauntlet is to know the Spirits. As per ‘Command Spirit’ p181. Instant casting. Lasts 5 turns. Grants 3 commands to 3 spirits Rank 4 or below. Sensory Range.
    Optional: Space 2
    With enough space Arcanum, the mage can ease the Gauntlet for the affected spirits and grant them all the “Reaching” Manifestation for free on all their Numina while under the spell's effects.

    Forth: Ferry the Passage
    Prerequisites: Spirit 4, Gnosis 6
    For a Riverman, the Gauntlet is a door easily opened. As per ‘Reaching’ p182. Instant Casting. Opens as an Iris to the spirit world any can use. Lasts for 5 turns. Only works on gauntlet strength 4 or less. Touch or Aimed.
    Optional: Space 3
    For you, the door is veiled and hidden from casual observances. Attempts to magically detect it provoke a clash of wills against you.

    Fifth: The World Within
    Prerequisites: Spirit 5, Gnosis 8
    If the theory holds, then the Gauntlet is more than just another barrier. So in order to explore the world hidden within, it must be at its strongest. As per ‘Place of Power’ p182. Ritual Cast, Lasts 1 month. Strengthens Gauntlet by 5 levels. Advanced Scale 2 (small warehouse or parking lot). Advanced Potency. Touch or Aimed.
    Optional: Space 4
    Finding the lost Pangaean roads may be a long endeavour, but a master of the Hifton can simulate his goal on a small scale. Expands the space within the Gauntlet to create a halfway pocket realm Gauntlet strength in either direction is half the total strength rounded up.

    Alchemists of the Tabula Smaragdina
    Aka: The Glenn Seaborg Foundation
    Free Council Variant

    Origins
    Parentage: Moros, Silver Ladder with Free Council variant
    Background: Emerald tablets. Ancient Hermetic philosophies. Blah Blah Blah. The Libertines left all that old Diamond Order Dogma behind long ago. When Glenn Seaborg, a sleeper chemist, successfully synthesised the act that has plagued and inspired amateur alchemists for a millenia, he proved the tenets of the Free Council without ever having heard of them. He turned lead to gold with not a single spark of supernal influence. This act convinced many disillusioned members of the Silver Ladder legacy of the Tabula Smaragdina to finally jump ship and ascribe to the Libertine ideas. Trailblazers or traitors, they collectively took the legacy down a new path as a splinter faction known as The Glenn Seaborg Foundation. Tensions between the two factions are still understandably tense, with regular accusations of thievery of relics and grimoires and petty vandalism between the two.
    Appearances: Heterochromia is a universal affliction among members of both factions of the legacy.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Matter 2, Science 2. Status (Scientist) 2
    Initiation: Before becoming a sanctioned GSF Researcher, interns are required to have a published thesis in a physical science field of study by a reputable sleeper run institution. They are then expected to recreate the experiment that earned Mr Seaborg the admiration of aspiring alchemists.
    Organisation: The GSF is a registered and recognised research foundation with a central laboratory located in Chicago. While its executives are all awakened, it was established with the goal to have mages and sleepers working closely together to push the mundane sciences to greater heights, and most members of the legacy usually join one of these mixed research teams for a year or more, though the obsessive allure of the Mysteries inevitably draws them away and back into the wider world before too long. The trademark logo of the foundation is the Sigil of the Squared Circle hidden within an atom, the ancient symbol for the philosophers stone and a tongue-in-cheek reference to their roots to wear over the breast pocket of all their lab-coats.
    Theory: The purpose behind the ancient Hermetic goal of a ‘Philosopher’s Stone’ is to turn lead into gold. This goal has given such alchemists a misunderstood and often unearned reputation for greed similar to the cautionary legend of Midas’s touch, but this goal was only ever intended as a symbolic stepping stone to the alchemist's true ambition. Lead represents the matter of the mundane world, while the gold reflects the perfection of the Supernal. The next step is to apply this simple transfiguration to the Soul itself. The Soul is an Imperial Mystery that draws from many arcanums but none understand the importance of purposeful and deliberate refinement and change better than an Alchemist. The foundation of the GSF has redefined this goal. It no longer merely seeks the power to wilfully uplift and empower the soul to greater enlightenment to an extent that few, if any, have achieved, but to make it a matter of mundane science. Just as legacies create magic that works within the bounds of the fallen world and no longer risks the paradoxical nature of the abyss, the GSF are hoping to apply the same theory to sleeper enlightenment and awakening. Some particularly radical researchers believe it is possible to make the Supernal itself one day irrelevant if the Fallen World can be transfigured to match it.


    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Matter
    (Secondary Arcanum: Forces)
    Yantras: Sigil of the Squared Circle (+1). Chemical Formulae or Numerology relevant to the spell (+1). Successful Science skill check for chemistry (+2). Applying a chemically reactive substance to the subject (+2).
    Oblations: Synthesising precious metals by mundane means. Building a particle accelerator. Giving an extended lecture on nuclear chemistry or particle physics. Attempting to replicate a uniquely magical effect by mundane science.

    Attainments

    First: Observation
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    A good Researcher needs to know what he is looking at. As per ‘Discern Composition’ p154. Instant cast. Sensory Range.
    Optional: Prime 1
    Not all of the diamond’s ideas are *entirely* worthless. Some knowledge of Prime can reveal the subject’s platonic form. In a single word, you learn what the subject’s True purpose or function is (This can be subjective to its current actualised use. A wrench might be ‘Tool’ but a wrench used to murder someone might be ‘Weapon’, one plated in gold and mantled might be ‘Trophy’)

    Second: Emergency Button
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    Small mistakes are inevitable. A good Researcher will always have a contingency plan. 3 selected mechanical devices up to size 7 (bear or motorbike sized) grind to a prompt halt. Instant casting. Sensory range.
    Optional: Forces 1
    Welcome to the digital age. Up to 3 electrical devices up to size 7 in sensory range also immediately shut down as though switched off.

    Third: Risk Assessment
    Prerequisites: Matter 3, Gnosis 4
    Seen as how one of the main two methods of synthesising elements is via a nuclear reactor, it pays to be prepared. As per ‘Alchemist’s Touch’ p155. Instant cast. Lasts for the scene. Immune to bashing and reduce lethal by Potency 5 by the substance (does not affect weaponry). Self only.
    Optional: Forces 2
    Not all hazards are physical. With enough Forces, you can also ward yourself from any extreme heat, radiation or electrical discharge that emanates from the subject. Reduce the strength of all such conditions and damage by Potency 5

    Forth: Pure Sample
    Prerequisites: Matter 4, Gnosis 6
    Contaminated materials produce anomalous effects and corrupt your data set. Thankfully, there's a way to avoid that. As per ‘Hone the Perfect Form’ (SoS p61) you refine a subject metal into its perfect, supernal form. Perfected metals have many magical uses. Ritual cast. Lasts for 1 year. Costs +1 mana. Touch or Aimed.
    Optional: Forces 3
    With knowledge of the aetheric, the perfected metals can defy the laws of thermodynamics. As per ‘Perpetual Motion’ (SoS p65) the metals and things created with them can store and perpetuate energy without entropy. Batteries never lose charge, they never lose heat, Kinetic energy never degrades as though in a vacuum.

    Fifth: Seaborg’s Miracle
    Prerequisites: Matter 5, Gnosis 8
    A senior researcher knows the core act of synthesis well enough to recreate by will. As per ‘Transubstantiation’ p158. Instant casting. Lasts 1 year. Potency 4 for purposes of quality and purity. Scale 2 (two size 6 subjects / small room). Touch or Aimed.
    Optional: Prime 3
    As above. so below. To refine the material is to enrich the soul. As per ‘Channel Mana’ p168. Ignore mana per turn limit for this effect. Up to 5 targets.

      I think it might avoid confusion if you call the Sin Eaters something different from the Geist term, perhaps Panteones, after the epitaphs from the Day of the Dead tradition.

      Regarding the wording of the fourth Attainment, the effect is Withstood by the ghost's Rank, so they might be able to remove less Manifestations than expected. I think saying its Potency primary would be easier and that similar clarifications might be helpful for the factors of the other Attainments.

      The fourth optional Attainment would require an Abjuration roll, which is a constested action to inflict Cowed, but I think Shaken might be more appropriate. The same can be done with a Potency primary Ruling of Death, Withstood by Rank, with Reach used on instant use, advanced duration and sensory range. It does not force the ghost to return to their Anchor though. Thematically speaking, the effect sounds more like intimidation than empathy.

      The capstone helps Rank 1 ghosts be more aware, but doesn't help them in seeking to resolve their Anchors. I think the Revenant spell might be more useful, so they can Materialize and be more active.

      The optional capstone would make ghosts more unstable, since they cannot recover Integrity once lost. So it will possibly make it harder to help them to pass on.

      Mechanically speaking, the Attainments seem to focus on understanding the ghost and protecting people from them, buying time for the mage to try to help them resolve their Anchors. Would that be the theme you are aiming for ?

      Since the optional Attainment use Prime, Fate and Mind rather than focus on a single Arcanum, it means the player will spread themselves out much more when using Arcane XP, it might be worth confirming if that is their intent. Hope that proved helpful.

          I kind of like the idea that the Bound and the Awakened both contest the name, even though their definitions of it are completely contradictory. The other big supernatural splats are not playing a particularly big role in my game. But i might brainstorm an alternative for if I decide to use the legacy in another game somewhere.

          i forgot about withstand rating there. Its a bad habit I keep catching myself on. I’ll have to reword that one a little.
          as for the optional effect, i saw it as a kind of beefed up lure/repel spell. I may reword that one too. I put the requirement as expression, not empthy, for the performative nature of it but I see your point as it could easily be intimidation.

          i disagree about the capstone, but that might be more to do with how I run ghosts and anchor resolution.
          i am treating anchor resolution, as opposed to the far easier destruction of anchors, as an unsolved Mystery the legacy was born of. That it requires a conscious revelation on the part of the ghost and that directly forcing it with magic would be difficult, dangerous, and almost certainly an act of hubris. A rank 1 ghost on my books is not particularly mentally capable of that level of introspection and is usually not even fully aware of sentient of their situation. Thus raising their rank and stats gives them the capacity to reflect and stop being quite as much of a broken record player, but its a risky move as your also making the ghost more powerful and capable of harm.
          the optional part of it is the heavy handed one, and if used carelessly would be an act of hubris for the same reason. Taking away the capacity for denial from a ghost can be a cruelty akin to forcing a sleeper to witness the abyss. It’s very much a ‘now or never’ deal that gives the mages until the attainments expiration to resolve the turmoil associated with the anchors or risk driving the ghost even more insane and taking a wisdom hit.

          Thats why its more about buying time then direct interference in anchors. I consider the act of making a ghost move on and transcend their death, whatever that means, and to be a pretty major mystery. Does it just fade to nothing? Is there some relationship it has with the missing soul they had in life that still calls to it? Its a legacy designed to address the callousness of many death mages who dismiss the emotional turmoil of ghosts as ‘meh, you’re not a real person. Just psychic leftovers’. By speculating about the opposite.
          if you want a quick and easy way to get rid of a ghost, there are a dozen quicker and easier methods then anything this legacy will give you. Which is kind of the point. Fixing the thing that makes the ghost a ghost, even with magic, should be hard.
          Revenant would learn a lot more onto that day of the dead theme of sin eater from geist that I wanted to contrast against, and its already kind of doing that with ectoplasm manipulation.
