            Mage functions best when you look at it like this:
            Long term mana management is pointless. It hides in every corner of the world, as long as you take care to pick it up.
            Mana matters when you don't have time to go looking for it. When a monster chases you. When you are under pressure. Basically, it's like these video games where you start each fight with full health - it's not important that you will get it back before next fight; it's important that it can run out during the scene.
            Mana management in Mage matters in context of *scenes*, where characters in general want to achieve a certain goal and they don't have the freedom to spam spells to fully recharge. Like, it's like arguing that Bashing damage is pointless, because it will disappear in an hour or something, so you can walk it off.

                    Look at how mana restoration options are designed:

                    - Hallow gives you passive mana, yes, but it *also* allows you to literally get mana potions for use in field
                    - Obsessions give you Mana when pushing them forwards, which rewards you for pursuing your Obsession in field so you can squeeze little more Mana in scenes where your Obsession comes in
                    - Legacy Oblations give you access to Mana when pursuing your Legacy in field, again, allowing you to sustain better while in middle of doing your stuff
                    - Scouring can be performed right here, right now, giving you a choice between gaining power now and suffering later, allowing you to refuel in middle of the scene at a price of feeling bad later

                    Have you noticed a pattern? They pretty much want you to go stuff outside of your sanctum and explore, rewarding you for doing so and giving you resources to keep going a little longer. Praxes are no different! They are there to reward you for performing your Signature Magic, so they can reward you for picking a style and sticking to it (which of course creates a pattern, and thus a weakness) while giving you some extra fuel. This game REALLY doesn't want you to go "Ok, dudes, I'm sorry, Mystery Investigation Over, I just had a pretty nasty Paradox twice in a row so I kinda blew all my Mana, sorry, I have to go home eat some Manasandwiches". It wants you to go "holy mother of mana nugget, I only have 10 points of Mana max, and I need THIS MUCH MANA TO CAST THIS SPELL SAFELY?! HOW WILL I SAVE MYSELF FROM A DEBILITATING CONDITION, TILT OR DEAR EXARCH IN HEAVENS, BEING WOUNDED?!"
