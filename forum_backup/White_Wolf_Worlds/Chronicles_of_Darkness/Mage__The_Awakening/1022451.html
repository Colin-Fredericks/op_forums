          Terpsichore, Thyrsus Libertine, created own Forces legacy about the primal energy of Music.
          Delilah, Obrimos Mystagogue, Awoke when her lover Ascended, entirely Obsessed with following her.
          Amaterasu, Obrimos Thearch, Japanese member of the Scions of God, ego the size of a small moon.
          Tlazoteotl, Mastigos Guardian, practices goetia as a Clavicularian, some proficiency in life to compensate for side effects of rampant vice indulgence.
          Chantarelle, Thyrsus Libertine, likes experiementing with Life magic to create new breeds of animal and fungus, especially those with naturally occurring medicinal properties.
          Gimli, Acanthus Arrow, incredibly short, took the name in jest but made it his own, only uses very subtle Magic, carries an axe.

          Psiclone, Mastigos Arrow, takes mental fortitude and potency to new heights, always running at least 2 sub-minds, likes creating mental vortexes that rend the psyche or trap people in their own minds.
          Strife, Acanthus Arrow, member of the Awakening Gambit, criticized by his fellows for his gambits being a bit too mentally tortuous. Purposefully ruins or introduces conflict into every relationship he has so his life never gets predictable, and he can continually learn from his actions.
          Thermalation, Moros Libertine, tries to fuse Forces and Matter together into a new state of existence. Solid energy. Currently working with telekinetic constructs as a stop-gap as he pursued his dream.
          Stasis, Moros Guardian, created Time based legacy called "The Campaign for Real-Time", seeks to send every time traveler back to their time. Frustrated by any Temporal effect she can't deal with. Very unpopular with many Acanthus in the local Consilium, but the Hierarch is also irritated by the concept of temporal manipulation and the butterfly effect, and has introduced laws to promote Stasis's agenda.
          Shadowidow, Moros Thearch, despite all the evidence to the contrary, seeks to find a way to recover lost Souls, and combine them with recovered ghosts, and Death unmaking spells to return the Dead to life. The Guardians are highly suspicious of Shadowidow, her raids on suspected Reapers for the purpose of recovering souls are welcome, but can ruin their attempts to discover Tremere networks, and they fear what extents she will resort to after she cannot find any more Reapers. She has displayed a complete disbelief in the reincarnation principle suggested by some ancient Atlantean texts.

                                          Do they have a theme, or are they mostly random? Anyways, assuming the latter......

                                          Kinslayer: Mastigos who Awoke after an accident that claimed the rest of his family. He suffers survivor's guilt severely, henceforth branding himself. He joined the Arrows so that he can be strong enough to prevent hurting his kin ever again, which is also his Obsession. He is studying Time magic so that one day he may redo the accident, but is curiously finding that particular day obscured from his view.

                                          Deathforge: A blacksmith who Awoke to Stygia after a series of animal sacrifices that culminated with offering his own life into the forge, only to rise unburnt and his masterpiece complete. He has yet to join an order, but is attracted to either the Arrows for their focus on perfecting the self and the Libertines for fellow smith-mages in there. He dedicates himself to Matter magic, with a few Prime and Death rotes.

                                          Winter: An old mage, even rumored to be an archmage, who has been the ur-mentor of the local Guardian caucus for years. She hides details about her life behind a curtain of ice both metaphorical and literal, conjured through potent Mind and Death magic. While she looks mostly Mastigos or Moros, the few who have personally met her theorize that she may be Obrimos instead, dedicated to the grand order of the passing seaons of which she guards one-fourth of.

                                          Man-Thing: A former white-collar businessman who has retreated into the mountains to become part wildling and part urban legend, after Awakening to join the bestial broods of the Primal Wild. While he is apostate, he maintains connections with the Silver Ladder that some of his former coworkers and bosses turned out to have been working for. He uses Life magic to blend in with the flora and command the fauna.

                                          White Wolf: A Mongolian Thyrsus who claims to be the incarnation of the son of the Blue Wolf and the White Doe. He is followed by a falcon-spirit who is his familiar, and belongs to the Free Council whom he considers as a facilitator of his cultural heritage. He relies on Spirit magic and the aid and Influences of his ephemeral allies to get by in a world far from home.

                                          Starkiller: Acanthus Thearch who Awoke while overcoming the disasters that befell his city after a comet passed by. She is Obsessed with finding such celestial signs, using auspicious ones to benefit his Cryptopolis and "killing" the ominous ones to protect her community. Fate and Prime spells have recently pointed her to a powerful nexus of occult energies that appear to be related to Sothis, something she has decided to check up just in case.

                                          Vanity: A Seer whose personality is exactly as she introduces herself as. Draped in the most expensive garments and jewels, conjured and perfected with Matter magic, she preaches the gospel of the Chancellor. As she only deigns to interact with those matching her own wealth and regards all others with contempt, she is deeply rooted into the higher echelons of society and is notoriously well-protected from her many enemies.

                                          Status Quo: Obrimos Banisher who is Obsessed with preserving the ley lines and nodes of the city under all costs. She uses Prime and Spirit magic to 'purify' and 'sterilize' all types of Resonance that deviates from the status quo, and while she does not hunt for mages who don't affect ley lines she has started to hurt or even kill Sleepers who are unwittingly messing up the Resonances. She babbles something about invisible angels and the hands of God that other mages do not recognize.

                                          Talon: A dangerous and fanatic servant of the Raptor who revels in bloodshed. This Seer has appeared in sites of great violence and turmoil, feeding the chaos into bloody extremes and occasionally joining in it with his formidable Death and Life magic. He has been seen accompanied by several bird-like spirits that appear to be made of shadows, though witnesses report their behaviors are too cunning and malicious to be normal spirits.

                                          Eye of Mirrors: Mastigos Guardian whose Obsession is to make criminals repent by showing them their own twisted and wretched shadow-selves. He has a rather Infamous Mentor, who is a high-ranking Seer of the Panopticon grooming him to become her partner, successor and lover. He is in furious denial of her ways, but a part of his particularly vicious vigilance is part of over-compensation on how he finds himself agreeing with his mentor more than he'd like to admit.

                                          And that's where my creative juice ends for today.

                                                          A few concepts suggested by previous posts:

                                                          Titan: Apostate Acanthus mage who has sworn never to return to the Silver Ladder, nor to any other order, till he is given recompense for the deaths of his former cabalmates. He almost totally rejects the social habits of other mages, living in an out-of-the-way city that has its own tourism center, a few necessary stores, one bottled water company, and little else. Here, he chases small and obscure mysteries that fail to interest other mages, while keeping himself ready to mount a furious defense should a major threat to his adopted home over be found. However, Titan does maintain periodic communication with the nearest large Consilium via the internet.

                                                          Morlock: Thyrsus and member of the House of Adriadne, Morlock believes that the Fallen World does not conceal Supernal secrets merely beyond veils of otherworlds or in temples swallowed by forest, jungle, or sea. Truths are to be found in the overlooked corners and passageways where Sleepers fear to tread. His personal journeys have taken him through the catacombs of Rome, to the depths of Mammoth Caves, and into the dirtiest and most choked of slums worldwide. Fellow Awakened may gain his services as a guide and protector in exchange for insights into the man who keeps appearing to him, begging him to avert some nameless calamity. Morlock's Death magic gives no indication that the apparition is a ghost, though its repetitive behavior suggest so.

                                                          Enchantress: Mastigos Thearch who builds her influence within the order by becoming an indispensable manager of the local Cryptopoly. To Sleeper followers, she's a knowing leader with whose uncanny insights into their lives and personalities are proof the the reality of their faith. To other Awakened, she is an uncompromising pillar of the Ladder. However, in recent months she's become more distant, and her requests from local Ladder resources deal with the summoning of Supernal entities.
