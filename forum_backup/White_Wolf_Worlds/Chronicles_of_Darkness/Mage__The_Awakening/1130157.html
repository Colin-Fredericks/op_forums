    Lesser Ministries anyone?

    08-17-2017, 02:19 AM
    Hi Wise Willworkers, Dusk here.

    Due to some friends of mine possibly getting into Mage the Awakening recently I delved back into some of my old PDFs, and as always had to re-read my favorite Mage Supplement: the Seers of the Throne Order book. I absolutely love those despicable bastards, and I might have gotten a bit carried away and made a few Lesser Ministries for possible future Mage Chronicles (technically I made two and updated one I made a while ago.) I feel compelled to share them with those who're interested in that sort of thing, and ask that if anyone else wants to share their own ideas or write ups for Lesser Ministries then if there isn't already a thread for that then there will be now.

    I short handed these to save word count, so they may be simplistic write ups. But I hope if nothing else they help illustrate why these shameless sellouts are so fascinating to me, and why they make such great foils for the Pentacle.


    • Homebrew Ministries

    ~ Penumbra
    > Motto: "Normality casts the darkest shadow."
    > Patron Exarch: The Nemesis.
    > Minister: Relleus, Seneschal of the Weeping Moon.
    > Tyranny: Normalize society's ills and failings, demonize everything unacceptable in society.
    > Allies: Hegemony, Paternoster, and secretly Mammon (hedging bets for Hegemony's fall.)
    > Rivals: Praetorian, and Lesser Ministries dedicated to war and the dissolution of society.
    > Plots: Investigation into the spirit-hybrid swarm entities, sometimes referred to as the Host by Shapechangers, has increased in recent years as the Ministry seeks to create Servitors of their very own. If such a creature could be domesticated then it would be an invaluable means of control due to it's naturally invasive nature.
    > Failings: The direct manipulation of Spirits and the Gauntlet as a means of influencing the Shadow has drawn a lot of unwanted attention over the years. Suffice to say, the Ministry has it's fair share of enemies from both Spirits, Shapechangers, and sensitive Sleepwalkers alike.
    > Mysteries: Exarchal messages in High Speech hidden in popular advertisements and brand logos. The lost names of ancient Gods of fear, alienation, and complacency. A silver blade engraved with words in the tongue of the Spirits that grants it's wielder Renowned amongst the Spirits so long as they prove worthy.
    > Inspiration: Of course the Ministry's namesake, but also Pentex and other corporate evils that utilizes the spirit realm as a raw resource.


    ~ Hyades
    > Motto: "Abandon hope."
    > Patron Exarch: The Ruin.
    > Minister: Lorrian II, King of the Shrouded City.
    > Tyranny: Cultivate defeatism and escapism through despair, tedium, and self-destructive habits.
    > Allies: All four Great Ministries.
    > Rivals: All other Lesser Ministries.
    > Plots: The Ministry currently has Pylons working on weaponizing both corporate America and drug culture to further the Exarch's goals, but has yet to make inroads into the entertainment industry. Hyades seeks to remedy that as soon as possible since recent auguries indicate that doing so would allow further expansion for the Ministry.
    > Failings: The Ministry is small compared to even some of the other Lesser Ministries. Hyades' reach only really extends to the Pacific North West of North America, and even then there are some regional Nameless Orders that are still larger in scope and influence. The fact that they remain active to this day is solely because they made themselves invaluable to the region's Tetrarchy.
    > Mysteries: Artwork depicting abstract prophesies made by depressed Sleeper artists. A living office complex that feeds off the literal hopes and dreams of it's workers. A narcotic drug that traps non-lucid dreamers in an Astral Realm of languor while their bodies become comatose and highly susceptible to magical manipulation.
    > Inspiration: Contemporary depictions of the King in Yellow's servants, and some depictions of the Lotus Eaters from the Odyssey.


    ~ Charon
    > Motto: "As in life, so in death."
    > Patron Exarch: The Psychopomp.
    > Minister: The Director (It's not what it seems.)
    > Tyranny: Manipulate people's worldly passions in life so that their anchors will be more easily controllable after death.
    > Allies: Panopticon, Praetorian, and Mammon.
    > Rivals: Paternoster, and all Lesser Ministries truly loyal to the Psychopomp. (More on that soon.)
    > Plots: Charon's greatest asset is it's corporate Mystery Cult, Fulcrum International. In recent years the Ministry has invested countless resources into making it's pet corporation into a true power player in the world market. Today it can rival companies like the Deva Corporation and Last Dynasty International, and hungrily consumes lesser players into it's growing list of subsidiaries.
    > Failings: The problem with Charon is that it's a Heretical Ministry. It's Minister, if it ever truly had one, has long been replaced by a powerful supernatural entity known only as the Director. While the Director still fulfills the same role as Minister, it's agenda is clearly more geared towards controlling ghosts and growing it's Mystery Cult. (The true nature of the Director is left purposefully vague so that it can better fit the needs of one's individual Chronicle. The only thing known for certain about it is that whether or not it's Supernal in nature it's well aware of the Wise and took over their organization so it could utilize their Supernal powers. One of my favorite personal theory about the Director is thus: It's the Supernal Symbol of Fulcrum International itself. The Ministry's use of the company as a sympathetic symbol led to it gaining Supernal weight and awareness. It see's itself as a nascent Exarch and seeks to use the Ministry to perform a "hostile takeover" and devour the Exarch of Death, thus replacing them.)
    > Mysteries: Factories in the Underworld that mirror abandoned Fulcrum Facilities and staffed by the ghosts of former employees. Unique ghosts and other chthonic entities that defy the laws death. Bleeding-edge technology that serves as an anchor for ghosts, creating mechanical-ghost Claimed.
    > Inspiration: Obviously Pentex and the Umbrella Corporation, but also Praxis from the podcast Alice Isn't Dead.

                  while I've never wrote it, I've always imagined that a Ministry dedicated to the Ruin would be something like this-

                  I've always imagined the Ruin as a mad dancer who draws the whole world toward eternal doom as she spiral around himself, taking a place almost satanic to the other Iron Seal- not like the Gate, but near enough the bottom that the Seers know that worshiping her as a patron is not the smartest thing to do. I've always imagined her Ministry as an fatalistic cult, driven by pure nihilism. You are bound to a fate not your own, living a story written by another. They enforce the Lie by making others fall into such hopelessness, making them believe that they can't change the paths which was laid to them. They were to a certain class and die as such. Where the Prophet would focus on the great people who make history move, the Ruin would focus on the grey people who work like cogs in the machine- secretaries and bureaucrats, protesters and anarchists, spies and assassins. They are the faceless mob who brings the revolution and manage the terror afterwards. They are never in the front, never taking the place of the protagonists- but the mechanism which brings fourth the Deus Ex Machina.

                  You never see the Ruin's climax to the plot before it's too late, but you always know that it is coming. That's the thing about good tragedies- you know how things would end, but you don't know how. You think the heroes are going to stop it, but each of their efforts just drives the story forward, toward their ultimate doom.

                  Let the heroes take the front stage- they are the ones to die in the end. And when the applause would rise and everyone would cheer for the poisoned lovers or the stabbed knight, from behind the screen you would know that you fulfilled your role, and brought fourth the script just as your patron wrote it.

                  yes, it was a fine tragedy.

                          Originally posted by Dark Archon View Post
                          Aren't Ministries global organizations, one for each Exarch? Even Lesser Ministries should commands dozens of pylons all over the world and have global influence.
                          That's actually a common misconception. Only the Great Ministries, and those who would contend with them, are truly global. Lesser Ministries are more akin to Nameless Orders in size and scope. The cost of not being a global power is offset by having these Ministries be far more specialized in purpose.

                          For instance, the Seers of the Throne book gives the example of a Lesser Ministry that's operations and power base stretched as far as just one city. That Ministry was a potent power player in that city, but nowhere else. Because of that though even the Great Ministries would utilize that Lesser Ministry whenever they needed an operation done within that city. This made the Lesser Ministry valuable, but at the trade off of only being able to exert power within that city.
