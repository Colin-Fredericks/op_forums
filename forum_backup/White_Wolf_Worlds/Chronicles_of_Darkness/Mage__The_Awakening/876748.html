      The author Iain M. Banks ran with this idea in The Hydrogen Sonata, one of his Culture novels. He liked to get very "meta" with his work, and - reading between the lines - he strongly hints that the characters are just part of a massive computer simulation. With the irony that one of the key plot points involves them running a massive computer simulation . . .

      Edit      : And others who may (or may not) have gained special abilities by learning to manipulate the simulation.
