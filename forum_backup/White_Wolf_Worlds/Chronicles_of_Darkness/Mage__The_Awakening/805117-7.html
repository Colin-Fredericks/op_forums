    Off the top of my head:

    Creative Thaumaturgy: A Time effect modeled after "Web-Weaver" (Space OOO) to enhance Temporal Sympathy. The original Stewards had it.

    Creative Thaumaturgy: A Time effect modeled after "Shifting Sands" (Time OOO) +1 Reach for Instant casting, +1 Reach for Sensory Range, +1 Reach for rewinding a Scene
    A tragedy turns out to have been a warning from an upcoming future. Legate's companions may receive them as well. In a way, it's a Spell invoked by the Player as opposed to the Character.

    "Choose the Thread" (Time OO) +1 Reach for Instant casting, +1 Reach for Sensory Range, +2 Reach for re-rolling supernatural powers
    Seems to be an appropriate spell. The Legate invokes a higher power keep the timeline on the right track.

    "Temporal Summoning" (Time OOO) +1 Reach for Instant casting, +1 Reach for Sensory Range, +1 Reach for Advanced Duration, +X Reach to give the target a Condition - Summoned people are Inspired to do the right thing, summoned objects have an improved Equipment Bonus. May summon targets from a possible future to serve as warning or encouragement.
    Sometimes a warrior grows infirm before their fated battle, a witness is murdered before trial or priceless knowledge is destroyed. This Spell brings them back. Not for good, but for long enough.

    Since Stewards stem from the Obrimos Path, extra Reach can be spent on inflicting beneficial Conditions, such as: Charmed, Guilty, Humbled, Inspired or Steadfast.

                One thing I have been kicking around and considering implementing in my conversions is that if the power has a significant limitation in its flexibility, or a drawback that is associated with its use of always being on, it acts in place of a reach requirement.

                1) Limitations of the attainment are significant compared to the base spell. For example, Tamers of Fire. Their forces attainments only influence fire, despite the practices their attainments are based on usually would allow them to manipulate all elements. Thus, I would grant them finer control with that attainment in exchange for the loss of reach, and grant 1 more reach for determining reach for that spell (so they can instantly rule fire at sensory range as rank 2 attainment, for example.)

                2) Drawbacks are inherent and actually limiting, especially if the mage can't remove them. For example would be making a strong life or mind shield permanent, but having it that the mage must apply the effects against all spells that are not his own, including beneficial ones. Or things like the Tremere, who have a pretty big drawback (though Dave has mentioned they essentially with have a new template, so maybe not as applicable). So while making the effect indefinite normally would eat 2 reach, it eats one as the drawbacks "pay" for one of those Reach.

                3) Reach benefit is situational and doesn't always provide a bonus, or the effects they create are situational and don't provide a benefit that outweighs a temporary one significantly.Another aspect is if the indefinite has as much of a drawback as having to restore the effect once in a while. Take moving ley lines. Permanently shifting them could lead to as many complications as allowing them to lapse, as resonances may change or the location may no longer be as friendly to the mage as it had when the lines were first moved. The fact that having them permantly that way do to attainment use means the mage would have to work to undo what he created, and others could exploit the creations without having to invest any effort. On the other hand, having to maintain the lines adds a time commitment to the mage, but also means they can let them lapse and not let a rival seize their work unless their rival invest maintaining it, and any mistakes made can be allowed to lapse on their own. When this scenario occurs, I would consider offering the flexibility.

                Now, I am asking for feedback on these ideas; these are thoughts I have had to balance both recreating the 1e Legacies while also keeping them balanced. I ranked them by the strength of my comfort with such implementation (I.e. I feel that #1 is most easily justified, while 3 is more tricky) and I wasn't extensive (under #1 I would consider the effects of the concentration Yantra a fair limit, but didn't want to flesh out the mechanical implementation here yet). But any feedback would be appreciated.

                        I've done this when updating my Legacy. The Spell "Correspondence" can detect Sympathetic Connections of a particular type for +2 Reach. When designing an Attainment I narrowed it's scope to the point it would normally require that Reach, but I made it unable to find Connections of any other type. That's why I ignored that additional cost. It's not an exact science. I feel that Reach spent on Spell Factors is usually more potent than Reach spent on additional effects.

                        Originally posted by Freemind View Post
                        2) Drawbacks are inherent and actually limiting, especially if the mage can't remove them. For example would be making a strong life or mind shield permanent, but having it that the mage must apply the effects against all spells that are not his own, including beneficial ones. Or things like the Tremere, who have a pretty big drawback (though Dave has mentioned they essentially with have a new template, so maybe not as applicable). So while making the effect indefinite normally would eat 2 reach, it eats one as the drawbacks "pay" for one of those Reach.
                        Shielding Spells can be cast on others without spending Reach, as long as you touch the target. If such Attainment only affects the Legate, I consider it limiting enough to enhance the Spell in a different way. If the Attainment uses it's Reach on Instant Casting, it can be refreshed every few turns with no risk of failure. If it uses it's Reach on Advanced Duration, it takes longer to cast but doesn't need refreshing that often. At this point I feel the Attainment can as well be always on. More than that, I feel it should be possible to activate and deactivate with a turn's concentration.

                        One more thing I did was to give a few Attainment an honest to God extra point of Reach. To balance it I decided the Mage needs to constantly focus to uphold the Spell, otherwise it ends. I'm not sure if it's sufficiently balanced, but I do hope so.

                                Since I'm here, I'd like to repost my update where it will be visible.
                                Originally posted by Teatime View Post
                                Fangs of Mara
                                Nickname: Nightmares

                                Parentage: Mastigos (However, in such a small Legacy tutors rarely discriminate based on the Path) Nameless, Seers of the Throne, Guardians of the Veil
                                Background: Legacies, the Sublime, page 139
                                Appearance: Legacies, the Sublime, page 138

                                Ruling Arcanum: Mind
                                Yantras: Succeeding on an Empathy or Intimidation roll relevant to the spell (+2), Withstanding or inflicting torture (+2), Any records of nightmares and trauma (+1, +2 if tailored to the spell), Comfort objects (+1, +2 if tailored to the spell)
                                Oblations: Studying or recording relevant Lore, Inflicting nightmares or trauma, Leading a successful therapy session, Brainwashing or deprogramming, In-depth psychoanalysis, Facing or indulging one's fears

                                Attainment 1
                                Prerequisites: Mind 2. Empathy 2 or Intimidate 2. Must have entered dreams of an Intruder

                                "Soul Diagnostics" (Mind 1) modeled after Soul Marks (Death 1)
                                This Attainment works like the spell it's based on, except it utilizes the Mind Arcanum. Fangs use this power to detect the tell-tale marks of Acamoth influence. The Attainment can even be used to recognize Intruders on sight, but it's notoriously unreliable in this capacity. 1 Reach assigned to Instant casting.

                                Optional: Space 1

                                Correspondence (Space 1)
                                Fangs of Mara always focus on what others don't want to confront. They can easily read Sympathetic Links to sources of trauma, anxiety and self-destructive behaviors. While it doesn't give them insight into the nature of these traumas, it gives them a springboard for investigation. The Wiser Fangs occasionally use this Attainment on themselves, to resolve their personal issues. 1 Reach assigned to Instant casting. Searching for specific Sympathetic Links usually demands +2 Reach. However, in case of this Attainment this cost is waived, since the Attainment is of limited use compared to the original Spell.

                                Attainment 2
                                Prerequisites: Mind 2. Empathy 3 or Intimidate 3

                                "Fear's Icy Hand" (Mind 2) modeled after Exceptional Luck (Fate 2)
                                Fangs know first-hand how erroneous thought patterns and unregulated emotions can lead to failure of the worst kind. While their standards demand that they shouldn't freely inflict what they can't take. By this point of their development, they can take a lot. This Attainment imposes a dice penalty equal to Potency to all mundane actions the subject takes for as long as the Fang can focus on casting. 1 Reach assigned to Instant casting. An extra Reach is assigned to Sensory Range, so to balance the effect, it's Duration drops to length of concentration.

                                Optional: Space 2

                                "Nondetection" (Mind 2) modeled after Supernal Veil (Prime 2)
                                Fangs make enemies easily. When spying on Intruders, Scelesti or more respectable Mages, it is better not to make ones presence known. This Attainment works like the Spell it's based on, but it only covers Legacy Attainments and Mind Spells cast by the user. 1 Reach assigned to Instant casting. Since the Attainment is so easy to cast, it can effectively be always active. The Fang can turn it on and off with a turn of concentration.

                                Attainment 3
                                Prerequisites: Mind 3. Empathy 3 or Intimidate 3. The other Skill at 2 or one of the following at 2 (Investigate, Medicine, Occult, Stealth, Subterfuge)

                                Befuddle (Mind 3)
                                By studying imperfections of the mind, Fangs can reach the underlying framework beneath thoughts and feelings and interfere with what they find there. 1 Reach assigned to Instant casting. An extra Reach is assigned to Sensory Range, so to balance the effect, it's Duration drops to length of concentration.

                                OR

                                Augment Mind (Mind 3)
                                The Fang carries the trauma of their initiation like a badge of honor. Or a shield. The knowledge of what they survived gives them the strength to continue on whatever path they choose. When this Attainment is developed, the Fang chooses to increase a single Attribute, which can't ever be changed. The Fang can boost this Attribute beyond their Gnosis maximum. This Attainment is mostly used to enhance Resolve or Composure, but Presence is not unknown. 1 Reach assigned to Instant Casting. The spell can't be used on others. Since the Attainment is so easy to cast, it can effectively be always active. The Fang can turn it on and off with a turn of concentration.

                                Optional: Space 3

                                Web-Weaver (Space 3)
                                Dark portions of the Astral? Dreams of Intruders? A strange Emanation Realm? Everyone is bound by fear and whatever the medium, Fangs learn to tap into it. When using Legacy Attainments and Mind Spells through Sympathetic Casting, the Fang treats their Sympathetic Connection to the target as one step stronger for every point of Potency. This can step up a non-existent connection and bring the Sympathetic Withstand down to zero. The connection is strictly one way - it does not make the user any more vulnerable to retaliation. 1 Reach assigned to Instant casting. Since the Attainment is so easy to cast, it can effectively be always active. The Fang can turn it on and off with a turn of concentration.​

                                Attainment 4
                                Prerequisites: Mind 4, Empathy 4 or Intimidate 4

                                Hallucination/Terrorize (Mind 4)
                                This Spell resembles Hallucination, except it can only evoke nightmarish visions. The Fang may choose to tailor them to the target, or to let target's subconscious and perhaps other factors to create their content. As long as the Spell is in effect the Fang can inflict the Insensate Tilt on their victim or let the Game Master choose an appropriate Tilt. This Attainment can be used for communication, but all transmissions become strongly disturbing. Fangs regularly use it for this purpose anyway. 1 Reach assigned to Instant casting. An extra Reach is assigned to Sensory Range, so to balance the effect, it's Duration drops to length of concentration.

                                Optional: Space 4

                                Dream Reaching (Mind 2)
                                The Fang can enter a dream of a sleeping creature - Be it a Sleeper, Awakened, Intruder or something else besides. Even before initiation into the Legacy, this is the first spell prospective Fangs learn - one they will use multiple times until the end. The Spell can be cast Instantly when in contact with the target, or with a scene's meditation when not. Either way the effect lasts a scene, unless repeated. Curiously, this Attainment gives Fangs a recognizable tell when in the Astral. Their Silver Thread turns into a heavy chain. Some Legates are still studying the ramifications. 1 Reach remains to be spent upon use either on Sensory Range or Instant Casting. 2 Reach are assigned to an additional effect - the Fang can shift between observing the dream and being an active part of it with a turn of concentration.

                                Notably, entering the dreams of Intruders is not entirely reliable. Gulmoth that approximate physical creatures dream reasonably often, but the anatomy of their dreams can "surprise" even an experienced Fang. Sometimes, investigators can't step back from being participators in the dreams. In some cases, suffering Soul-Shock is a lethal proposition. In all cases delving deeper into the Oneiros is... advanced. Acamoth, being Goetia, don't dream except in very rare cases. The brave Fang who entered one such dream has been since kept sedated and comfortable. New Fangs are brought to him to hopefully learn something and then leave laughing nervously about "Abyssception". Rare, strange Abyssal phenomena dream even though they don't approach being a discrete entity. Fangs have managed to quarantine an Abyssal Verge after discovering that the very narrative of entering it seemed to dream.

                                Attainment 5
                                Prerequisites: Mind 5, Empathy 4 or Intimidate 4. The other Skill at 3 or one of the following at 3 (Investigate, Medicine, Occult, Stealth, Subterfuge)

                                Psychic Reprogramming (Mind 4)
                                For their studies, Fangs use this Attainment to alter their target's psyche and see if their susceptibility to Acamoth influence changes, but it has many other uses as well. Some Fangs use it for therapy or even alleviating the damage they left. The Attainment can be used either within Dreams through a scene-long ritual or Instantly through physical contact. In the last case, sitting on a sleeping target's chest is a Legacy's tradition. 1 Reach Assigned to Advanced Duration. 1 Reach remains to be spent upon use either on Sensory Range or Instant Casting.

                                Optional: Space 5

                                "Back Door" (Space 4) modeled after Supernal Dispellation (Prime 4)
                                Fangs take the lessons of their Third Attainment and take them to their logical end. This Attainment works like the spell it's based on, but only as an aide for using Legacy Attainments and Mind Spells and only to suppress Wards and similar protections than stand in their way. 1 Reach assigned to Instant casting. 1 Reach assigned to Sensory Range. Since the Attainment is so easy to cast, it can effectively be always active. The Fang can turn it on and off with a turn of concentration.

                                Fangs of Mara and the Astral

                                Fangs have all the means and reasons to maintain a stronghold in their Temenotic realm. Considering their need for secrecy and how easily each individual's experiences can be lost, Fangs regularly network and review their results. Unfortunately, either due to their actions, or their usual thoughts, the area was frequently a target of Acamoth interest. Fangs have made lemonade out of lemons and took the opportunity to study the Enemy on their conditions. The realm had to be erased a few times to prevent corruption, and each time "non-vital" knowledge was lost. It's one of the reasons Fangs often squirrel away records in the Material Realm. Now, with proper Security in place, most Acamoth are either unaware of the place or are captured and quarantined. The more worrying are the ones that deliberately stay away. Even those that don't intend a confrontation can sell their knowledge to someone who does.

                                In the depths of the Astral, off the road between Temenos and Anima Mundi there is a realm containing the rawest of human nightmares. It has always been of interest to Fangs of Mara, but between their numbers and the challenges of studying the place they still feel woefully ignorant. Even then they count among their number the foremost experts on the region. Their research allowed them to discover an important phenomenon - intense experiences, especially those caused by the supernatural, leave a temporary Imprint in the realm. Patrolling the realm allows to discover sites of recent supernatural activity, including places where Sleepers were damaged by Dissonance. Fangs can use this phenomenon to search for Scelesti and Intruders, but those who do usually become quite informed about other Things that bump in the night. Some Fangs use the information to earn goodwill with the local Orders - a way of keeping oneself useful. However, there exists a factor that discourages careless entry. Aside from the usual dangers of the Astral, Fangs occasionally become targets of scrutiny by a powerful presence. What happens next varies. Travelers have found themselves taken to an important Imprint - possibly of their own making, confronted with their fears and desires, trapped with a monstrous Goetia, or even back in the Material Realm, far off from where they meditated. Of those who survive the ordeal, most reports have one thing in common - a sense of deranged love Fangs feel directed towards them. It's an unnerving experience and as far as Fangs know, there are no records of other Mages going through it.

                                                        Daoine

                                                        I took a crack at the Daoine while I was born. Once again, I can't shake the feeling that it would be much easier to just build a new Legacy from the ground up than trying to convert an existing one, but to hell with it.

                                                        First Attainment: Black Cat's Passing
                                                        Prerequisites: Initiation

                                                        The Good Folk know the best way to punish the sins of the guilty. This attainment mimics the effects of "Serendipity" (p. 135), save that the Mage can only use it when she wishes to learn the most effective way to punish or hurt someone. Reach is spent on instant activation.

                                                        Optional: Time 1

                                                        The Daoine spends one scene contemplating an omen she received using the first attainment. She may then ask specific questions about how her omen can come to pass, per "Divination" (p. 186) and its Reach effect.
                                                        Why Time instead of Mind? For one thing, using a non-ruling Arcanum for optional effects is kind of a dick move. For another, I don't think the Daoine used Mind very well in the first place. Plus, I thought of a really cool thing you can do with Time 5.

                                                        Attainment-wise, Serendipity is a nice little effect that has some good synergy with Divination.

                                                        Second Attainment: The Faerie Mask
                                                        Prerequisites: Fate 2, Occult 2

                                                        The Daoine spends a scene in seclusion and masks her destiny, allowing her to evade the curses of others. This attainment functions as "Fabricate Fortune" (p. 136). Reach is spent on advanced duration.

                                                        Optional: Time 2

                                                        By reading the weave of Time, the Daoine may distinguish saints from sinners. This attainment functions as "Momentary Flux" (p. 187). Reach is spent on instant activation and sensory range.
                                                        This one's mostly a filler, since I couldn't find any Fate or Time effects that worked here. I do like Momentary Flux though.

                                                        Third Attainment: The Evil Eye
                                                        Prerequisites: Fate 3, Occult 3

                                                        The Mage touches her victim and places a terrifying curse upon her. This attainment functions per "Exceptional Luck" (p. 135), save that it can only be used to place hexes on someone. Reach is spent on instant activation and advanced duration.

                                                        Optional: Time 3

                                                        The Daoine may supplement any use of the third attainment with the effect of "Hung Spell" (p. 188). The effect of the hex only takes place when Hung Spell's duration expires, when the Mages chooses to relinquish the Hung Spell effect, or when some conditions set by the Fate 2 attainment Conditional Duration are met. Regardless, using this optional attainment always costs one point of mana. Reach is spent on instant activation and advanced duration.
                                                        This one is the bread and butter attainment. The name doesn't really work, but I do like the idea of touch-based curses. It's unsettling, and it leads to the last attainment nicely enough. Hung Spell has very good synergy here.

                                                        Fourth Attainment: The Eternal Wheel
                                                        Prerequisites: Fate 4, Occult 4

                                                        The Daoine spends one scene meditating upon the skein of destiny, after which she gains the benefits of "Interconnections" (p. 134). Reach is spent on Interconnections' additional Reach effects, as well as advanced duration.

                                                        Optional: Time 4

                                                        While the main effect of the fourth attainment is active, the Daoine may spend a point of mana in order to study a specific subject under "Postcognition" (p. 187). This can allow the Mage to see how a subject's destiny has changed over time. Reach is spent on instant activation, sensory activation, advanced duration, and the reach effect to "scrub" the visions.
                                                        Not much to say here. Interconnections and Postcognition are both staple powers, and they work fairly well together.

                                                        Fifth Attainment: Doom of Promise
                                                        Prerequisites: Fate 5, Occult 5

                                                        The Daoine confirms an oath with a touch or handshake, sealing it with "Oaths Fulfilled" (p. 135). Reach is spent on instant activation, advanced duration, and all of the spell's optional Reach effects.

                                                        Optional: Time 5

                                                        The Good Folk have no need for first chances. Whenever someone breaks an oath made with the fifth attainment's standard, the Daoine may spend one scene in meditation and spend one point of mana in order to return to the moment the oath was sanctified per "Corridors of Time" (p. 191). Once there, the Mage must curse the oathbreaker using the Legacy's third attainment, after which she returns to the present. Reach is spent on sensory activation in order to make use of Temporal Sympathy.
                                                        This one's my favourite. Oaths Fulfilled is a direct conversion from first edition Daoine, and pairing it with Corridors of Time lets you go back in time and retroactively curse people who have betrayed your trust. I love it.
