    Micro-Template: Awakened Engineers (or, yet another Genius take)

    03-28-2018, 01:24 PM
    Preface
    I was digging around in the Homebrew Mage Merits thread and was brainstorming a way for Sleepwalkers to use the Enhanced Item and Imbued Item Merits easier. Sure, they just can have said items, and acquire new ones by just "buying" more instances of those Merits, but what if you want them to have those options in Mage absent and restricted settings? I made a person-focused Enhanced Item Merit (of questionable quality), where they can switch around the bonuses. Imbued Item, with activated spells, Mana costs, and dice pools, is harder.

    But what is someone who can make Imbued Items, but not perform the actual magic they hold? Basically a Gadgeteer, or (one concept of) a Genius. Now, there are more mechanics than just "you can make Imbued Items", and allowing anyone to just make whatever spell effects they want (and can pay for) is excessive, putting it into a micro-template structure gives more options while slowing down the power growth.

    Phase 1: Brainstorming

    So, let's call the baseline Merit Insight at the moment. Each dot of Insight will allow for a Mana Pool, at least, for the powering of various Mana-dependent items. If we are representing devices and Enhanced and Imbued Items (as per the Merits), this is going to get XP costly, fast. So what about allowing them to have Obsessions as well? In theme, and allowing the Arcane Beats (and XP) to be spent on Enhanced and Imbued Item Merits off-sets the big costs. Insight also needs to grant access to "spells" (Arcana + Practice) to be able to incorporate into Enhanced and Imbued items they "make". Either it can be tied directly to Insight, or Insight can grant free instances of tag on Merits that can be purchased separately to expand their repertoire.

    Follow-up questions:
    Mage-Sight - Core ability (in limited form) or purchasable Merit?
    Attainments - Granted by Insight, unlock via virtual Arcana, or make it a spell effect of the equivalent Arcana rating?
    Mana acquisition - Allow Pattern Scouring, Hallow Oblation, Legacy-like Oblation? Part of Insight, locked behind a Merit, or restricted entirely?
    Power acqusition - Let them buy Arcana outright (though only usable for determining Enhanced/Imbued Item effects), or use a Arcana + Practice combo?
    Other Abilities - Beyond building Enhanced and Imbued Items (however useful and flexible those are), what other powers and perks should they get?
    Drawbacks - With Great Power, Comes Great Insanity. Or, how should being this micro-template suck worse than being a "normal" Sleepwalker?

                                      So, some basic thoughts that may or may not be useful:

                                      First, you mentioned the experience cost, which does seem like kind of a problem. Perhaps you could build something along the lines of any of the following:
                                      • A ‘multiplier’ effect that lets the Gadgeteer create more points’ worth of gadgets for the same resource investment.
                                      • A multiplier effect along a different axis: Let the Gadgeteer create certain basic Imbued/Enhanced items in multiple groups: These could then be traded or given to other group members to help expand abilities.
                                      • A number of ‘free’ dots in Imbued or Enhanced items that cost no merits and may be switched out fairly easily (thus hopefully making the users slightly more versatile).



                                      Mostly, I figure that one of the balancing factors for Imbued Items is that mages are very versatile and an imbued item is basically useful to them as both a mana battery and a way to expand into abilities they do not possess. Gadgeteers don’t have a use for Mana, and don’t have any inherent powers to expand their abilities away from, so might need a bit of a boost to have their Imbued Items be an interesting choice for them.

                                      With that particular bit of unsolicited opinion out of the way (:P):

                                      Mage-Sight - Core ability (in limited form) or purchasable Merit?
                                      Between your choice, a purchaseable Merit but preferably one that manifests as an item. Gadgeteers are crafters, so let them get it as a particular form of magic item. Probably by treating it the same way you end up treating Attainments.

                                      Attainments - Granted by Insight, unlock via virtual Arcana, or make it a spell effect of the equivalent Arcana rating?
                                      Would you like them to have Attainments as a power for themselves, or for use within Gadgets? For the former I’d go with the equivalent Arcana rating. For the latter I’d go with granting them through virtual Arcana, subject to the same limits as Imbued Items already have.

                                      Mana acquisition - Allow Pattern Scouring, Hallow Oblation, Legacy-like Oblation? Part of Insight, locked behind a Merit, or restricted entirely?
                                      Mana production through scouring seems like a good idea, given that it’s inherently a limited act. As a bonus, you could probably spin it into a merit of its own and that Merit would be a useful thing for a Sleepwalker to have even without them being Gadgeteers. Or at least, it would be a useful thing for a Mage with access to that Sleepwalker.

                                      Power acqusition - Let them buy Arcana outright (though only usable for determining Enhanced/Imbued Item effects), or use a Arcana + Practice combo?
                                      I’d go with outright Arcana buying, given that their powers already cost experience to obtain, so I wouldn’t make it any more difficult than it is already.

                                      Other Abilities - Beyond building Enhanced and Imbued Items (however useful and flexible those are), what other powers and perks should they get?
                                      A general bonus to Crafting rolls might be a nice touch. Perhaps take some inspiration from the vehicle modding merit that’s...somewhere. Maybe even let it work best on anything that’s already an Imbued or Enhanced Item. That way, your gadgeteers aren’t limited to modern science or sheer occultism but can combine both.

                                      Perhaps add in a way for Gadgeteers to come together more in a particular way. One of the reasons Mage has a society to begin with is because they have stuff like Rotes sitting right at the top of their Attainment pyramid. Perhaps an equivalent ability for Gadgeteers (Schematics? I’m kind of drawing on Pendragon’s Sons of Bezalel here) would give them good reason to congregate and allow a bit more differentiation between Gadgeteers.

                                      Drawbacks - With Great Power, Comes Great Insanity. Or, how should being this micro-template suck worse than being a "normal" Sleepwalker?
                                      From a storytelling perspective, these people would be valuable, even more so than regular Sleepwalkers. So one thing that immediately comes to mind is “they routinely come to the attention of Mages, which is a pretty bad thing for anyone who is not a Mage themselves”. Regularly attracting powerful people who would probably love for you to work for them to make stuff is probably not very good for your health and sanity.

                                      I can’t really think of a particular inherent disadvantage, other than that we are essentially talking about people who draw upon the Supernal without having written their name on a Watchtower. Archmages can do that because they’re powerful enough to create a safe space for themselves in the Supernal. Proximi do it because of spells cast by very powerful supernal beings.

                                      These guys? They don’t. A Mage that goes into the Supernal erases themselves from history. Maybe Gadgeteers should have a similar issue where they’ve got more and more trouble interacting with ‘normal’ people as their arts effectively put them at one remove from the world? I dunno. It doesn’t feel quite right to me.

                                                      Originally posted by Vent0 View Post

                                                      Well, as we've seen, Imbued and Enhanced Items are very expensive, XP-wise.
                                                      Creating, and safely relinquishing, an Imbued or Enhanced item costs between 0 and 1 XP per spell, assuming you always buy back lost Willpower dots. Unsafely relinquishing is always free, and if it's an Enhancement that lasts for only a week or so it's not even dangerous to do so. Purchasing the item with merit dots represents you getting it from someone else, not you making it.


                                                      That said, for a Sleepwalker artificer I'd do something like this:


                                                      Supernal Imbument (*** - *****)

                                                      When a character takes this merit they choose one Arcana. That Arcana defines the types of items that can be crafted and shapes the character's life, should they ever fully Awaken they will inevitably Awaken to the path for which their chosen Arcana is a ruling Arcana.
                                                      The character gains a mana pool equal to the number of dots they have in this merit, can only spend 1 mana/turn, and gains the ability to transfer mana to any Imbued item they created. They also gain the ability to scour their pattern for mana once per day (p. 87 of M:tAw 2e).
                                                      At three dots in this merit, the character can learn to Imbue items with one dot spells of the appropriate Arcana, four dots in the merit grants access to two dot spells, and five dots to three.
                                                      The character automatically knows a number of spells equal to their dots in this merit, and can purchase additional spells for 1 XP per spell.
                                                      When Imbuing the character rolls their dots in this merit as their dicepool and otherwise obeys all the rules for Imbuing items (p. 195-6 M:tAw 2e).

                                                          Here's a rough concept - on available Arcana - thoughts?

                                                          For each dot of Insight, the Artificer has access to one Arcana (as if they had 1 dot in that Arcana, so Knowing, Unveiling, and Compelling). They can pick up a Merit (•• or ••••) that grants access to Arcana 2 and 3, respectively in the Arcana they have access to (with an even more optional Advanced version for Arcana 4 and 5). This gives access for up to 5 Arcana, at ratings of up to 3 (or 5).

                                                          OR have each dot of Insight grant a "free" dot in an Arcana. That would allow specialization into one field, or branching out. Make additional dots purchasable.

                                                          Actually making items is handled via the Imbued Item rules, and the Artificer pays the XP for the permanent item. They can break down items they've made for the "resources" (IE, Sanctity of Merits).
