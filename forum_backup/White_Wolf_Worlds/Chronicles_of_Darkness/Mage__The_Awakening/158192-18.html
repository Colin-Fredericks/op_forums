    Can I get general or specific thoughts on a Legacy I'm working on? Either as to the flavor or the attainments or just in general:

    Originally posted by Kvark View Post
    Daydreamers - Mages that aspire to greatness who are sought out by the Oneiropolis, the place in the Astral where unrealized fantasies and unfulfilled ambitions coalesce. They carry a piece of the Astral with them at all times and use it to craft their destiny in the fallen world. (Fate, Optional Mind)
    Daydreamers

    As she comes to the fork in the road, she pauses to reflect. She mutters in a language she has never spoken before or since. In seconds, she knows exactly which way she needs to go. She wonders, just for a moment, if this was the right choice before she chose it, or if it’s the right choice now because she chose it. It doesn’t matter, she knows the world is conspiring to take care of her.

    WHAT HAPPENS TO A DREAM DEFERRED?

    There is a place deep in the Astral Realm where faded aspirations and lost genius effervesce and coalesce. It manifests as a vast fluid of possibility, with rapidly changing form that engulfs and drowns out anything it touches. The ideas and memories within are diverse, but they share one thing in common: they seek instantiation and actualization in the real world. This place in the Astral Realms, called the Oneiropolis, is the nest of dreams unfilled and ambitions unrealized. It subtly seeps into the waking world through the unconscious acts of all sapient creatures, yet it yearns to be acknowledged, to be unforgotten, and to be empowered. The Oneiropolis will latch onto certain mages, embedding a sliver of itself into their minds and weaving itself inextricably into their fates. It will then slowly, subconsciously, put ideas into the minds of the prospective Daydreamer guiding him towards an appropriate mentor and set fate into motion to arrange for a chance meeting between the two.

    The first references to the Legacy date back to early Greek history, in the first few centuries preceding the Common Era. It’s unknown exactly whether the Oneiropolis reached out to the first Daydreamer, or if the founder of this Legacy instead stumbled across it in the Astral Realms. Regardless, almost all students of such matters agree that the meeting was not an accident (indeed, Daydreamer scholars might argue there are no true accidents). Daydreamers have been found scattered across all corners of the globe, brought together by apparent coincidences and inspired to seek each other out by arcane dreams or nightmares.
    In more modern times, the Daydreamers are a rare but far-reaching collective of mages, with a great dedication to the pedagogy of their Legacy. Though they come from diverse backgrounds, almost all Daydreamers believe themselves destined for greatness (if they seek it) and so tend to be very involved in the world of mundane affairs.

    The Mystagogues have found a particular affinity for the Daydreamer, knowing that their riddled dreams and bewitched minds are usually the vessel for great Mysteries or obscure Truth. Most of the Daydreamers count themselves among the Mysterium and aspire to find their greatness within the Order’s hierarchy. Select few other Daydreamers are drawn to the Free Council, whose belief that all of reality is magical resonates well with the Dreamer modus operandi, wherein they quite literally will often find magic in everything.

    ORIGINS
    Parentage: Any Path, Mostly Mysterium or Free Council
    Background: The Daydreamers tend to be people of great vision and imagination (though not necessarily great success). The Daydreamers count many artists, scientists, and entrepreneurs among their Legacy.
    Appearance: Daydreamers come from all backgrounds and have no physical traits in common prior to joining the Legacy. It’s been said that those that join end up appearing a little scatterbrained and skittish or otherwise “not all there,” though anyone engaging in a conversation with a Daydreamer usually quickly realizes this is a superficial quality.

    DOCTRINE
    Prerequisites: Fate ●●, Investigation ●● or Expression ●●, Destiny ●, Dream ●
    Initiation: The mentor will guide the prospect through the Astral realms several times before finally diving into the Temenos and the Anima Mundi to see the Oneiropolis, who will impart the first Attainment.
    Organization: All Daydreamers view themselves as equals before the Oneiropolis. This is not due to any sort of mind control or undue influence, but rather because the act of communicating with the Oneiropolis itself is very humbling. Daydreamer mentors consider pupils to be part of their life’s work and dedicate great time and care to making sure that those under their tutelage embrace the Oneiropolis.
    Theory: Daydreamers believe themselves to be vessels of humanity’s suppressed thoughts, unsubstantiated ideas, and forgotten memories. They do not have any sort of “mission,” but rather share a common drive-- to produce great works and become a greater part of the Oneiropolis. They borrow the ambitions and memories of others and use them to sculpt their own fates.

    MAGIC
    Ruling Arcanum: Fate
    Yantras: Succeeding on an Expression roll (+2), referencing a relevant work of genius (+1), completing a relevant research piece or artwork (+3)
    Oblations: Finishing a manuscript or a work of art, convincing someone to appreciate her works, traveling to the Astral to commune with the Oneiropolis, learning a new skill or practicing a new hobby

    1st Attainment: Waking Dreams
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The mage’s mind becomes a beacon for the Astral, and the Oneiropolis can sing directly into it, subtly guiding her fate with bits and pieces of supernal truths. The mage’s increases her maximum potential dots in Destiny and Dream by her Fate. She still needs to purchase these additional dots with experience, however.
    Optional (Mind ●) - The mage may use her Dream merit as an instant action without taking the time to enter a meditative state.

    2nd Attainment: Whispers in the Dark
    Prerequisites: Fate ●●, Investigation ●●● or Expression ●●●, Destiny ●●, Dream ●●
    The mage turns to the Oneiropolis for advice on how to proceed. The Oneiropolis responds in a combination of foreign memories, whispers, and strewn images that the mage interprets as an omen of where to go. This attainment functions as Serendipity (Fate ●) with instant casting and the skill substitution reach.
    Optional (Mind ●●) - The mage becomes comfortable delegating certain tasks to the Oneiropolis, and grows more confident in his ability to interpret the resulting visions and dreams. In practice she gains the ability to use One Mind, Two Thoughts with instant cast time and advanced duration, as well as the reach to perform two instant mental tasks at once. This attainment can only affect the mage herself.

    3rd Attainment: Conspiring Thoughts
    Prerequisites: Fate ●●●, Destiny ●●●, Dream ●●●
    The Oneiropolis watches out for its disciples and forges success through a chain of seemingly random events. This attainment functions as Shifting the Odds (Fate ●●) with instant casting and advanced duration.
    Optional (Mind ●●●) - The mage communes regularly with the Oneiropolis inside and outside of her conscious state. She may use an effect similar to Dream Reaching (Mind ●●) with advanced duration and the ability to be an active part of any dream, or to dream lucidly.

    4th Attainment: Hypnagogia
    Prerequisites: Fate ●●●●, Destiny ●●●●, Dream ●●●●
    So great is the Oneiropolis’s pull on the mage’s Destiny that she can transform would-be failure into glorious success. This attainment mimics Superlative Luck (Fate ●●●) with instant casting time and advanced duration.
    Optional (Mind ●●●●) - For the duration of Hypnagogia, the mage may mimic the effects of Enhance Skill with instant casting and advanced duration.

    5th Attainment: From Many, One
    Prerequisites: Fate ●●●●●, Destiny ●●●●●, Dream ●●●●●
    Cost: 1 Mana
    Failure is no longer an option for the Daydreamer, nor is it something she has the time to tolerate. This attainment mimics Miracle (Fate ●●●●●) with an instant cast time.
    Optional (Mind ●●●●●) - The mage and the Oneiropolis are truly one, working together at all times. The mage can rely on the collective wisdom and intelligence of the Oneiropolis, and thus can use Augment Mind (Mind ●●●) with instant casting time, advanced duration, and the ability to exceed the normal maximum attribute limit. This effect may only be used on the mage herself.


    EDIT log: Per Zombiecakes's feedback, replaced 3rd attainment with something less redundant.
    EDIT log #2: Expanded the flavor text.
    EDIT log #3: Changed first attainment (now uncaps dream and destiny by fate, instead of providing one free dot of destiny). Changed 3rd optional to more thematically appropriate Dream Reaching, changed flavor text of fourth attainment, and changed optional effect of 5th attainment to be mage-only.
    EDIT log #4: Replaced second optional attainment with something more balanced.

                        Originally posted by Kvark View Post
                        Vox Auctoritatis - A legacy started by a Guardian who manifested his authority through the power of his voice. Commanding, intimidating, and even imperial in nature, when these mages Speak, the whole of the Fallen World listens. (Forces, Optional ???)
                        So I realized I've personally had enough of the strong Greco/Roman Mage influences and reshaped this into something of Nordic origin. By request, here is a half fleshed out version of this Legacy.

                        Askatalare

                        He is silent in this mediation. Indeed, he is silent in almost all negotiations these days. His words are precious to him, and he does not waste them on trivialities. The discussion begins to break down as his less reserved colleagues fling bitter insults at one another. Both sides fall silent as he pulls down the scarf covering his mouth; they are not fools that fear his voice, but wise men who fear his Truth.

                        WHEN THE WHOLE WORLD IS SILENT, EVEN ONE VOICE BECOMES POWERFUL

                        The traditions of the Askatalare date back to the Viking North, when mages of great power and stature were expected to demonstrate their strength both on and off the battlefield. The Askatalare, or Thundertalkers, are comprised of men and women who demonstrate strength and composure equally well in combat as in negotiation. The Askatalare carry the weight of their most prized possessions, their voices, at all times. Knowing and respecting both the physical and metaphysical power of their sound, they are warrior-poets who seek to bring Truth to the Lie.

                        ORIGINS
                        Parentage: Mostly Obrimos, Silver Ladder or Guardians of the Veil
                        Background: The Askatalare are prominent in northern Europe, and many tend to be people who have demonstrated their skill in both combat and arbitration. Businessmen, lawyers, judges, mediators, doctors, and performers are all prime candidates for receiving the training the Askatalare require.
                        Appearance: The Askatalare tend to cover their mouths when not speaking (or even when talking). They have great respect for their voices and take care to protect their mouths and their throats. It’s not uncommon for an Askatalare to wear a scarf even in warmer weather, or a tie even on informal occasions.

                        DOCTRINE
                        Prerequisites: Forces ●●, Expression ●● or Intimidation ●●, Athletics ●● or Firearms ●● or Weaponry ●●
                        Initiation: The prospective Askatalaren is arranged to spar with increasingly senior members of the Askatalare. The combat is watched by local members of the Legacy, who usually stand in deafening silence in a circle around the combatants. Should the prospect be defeated or be victorious, he is scheduled to spar again. The initiation process is over when the prospect, instead of fighting, talks his opponent out of combat (through any combination of persuasion, intimidation, or expression). A prospect can go through as many as a dozen rounds of fighting before realizing that combat is not the correct solution to the initiation ritual.
                        Organization: Askatalare are diplomatic, usually electing local leaders based on perceived strength. As there are not too many Askatalare and they are regionally confined to northern Europe, elections are an informal process governed by vocal votes. Typically one or two members offers to mentor a new initiate, who will then receive increased training in the art of Aska Tal.
                        Theory: The Askatalare are trained to craft their voice into a vessel for their magics. The Askatalare know that the voice is a unique weapon, in part because it doesn’t have to be a weapon at all. The Askatalare seek out positions of power and then serve with righteous authority. They feel (and perhaps rightly so) that their training has prepared them to avoid most conflicts when possible, but to win any that may arise.

                        MAGIC:
                        Ruling Arcanum: Forces
                        Yantras: Singing or similar recitation (+2), de-escalating a conflict (+2), winning a battle of wits (+2)
                        Oblations: Arbitrating a disagreement, meditation in absolute silence, singing as part of a chorus, abstaining from speech for extended periods of time


                        First Attainment: Voice of the People
                        Prerequisites: Initiation
                        When speaking or screaming, the mage may increase or decrease the volume of her own voice by one step per the table on pg. 146.
                        Optional (Prime ●) - Pierce Deception with instant cast time.

                        Second Attainment: Ear to the Ground
                        Prerequisites: Forces ●●, Expression ●●● or Intimidation ●●●
                        The subject heightens her influence over sound dramatically. She gains the benefits of Receiver (Forces ●) with instant cast time and advanced duration. Apply the Potency of this attainment as a bonus to dice pools involving stealth or avoiding ambush as appropriate.
                        Optional (Prime ●●) - Words of Truth with instant cast time.

                        Third Attainment: The Obedient Voice
                        Prerequisites: Forces ●●●
                        The mage gains the ability to influence sound more directly. Functions as Control Sound (Forces ●●) with instant cast time and advanced duration.
                        Optional (Prime ●●●) - The mage’s voice resonates with undeniable Supernal Truth. By spending a point of mana, the mage enchants her vocal cords for one scene. Her voice can be heard by creatures in all forms of Twilight (ghosts, spirits, angels, etc.). Her spells and attainments may now affect these beings provided they incorporate her voice as a primary element of the spell.

                        Fourth Attainment: Speak Truth to Power
                        Prerequisites: Forces ●●●●
                        The mage channels sound into a roaring blast focused in one direction. Per the Telekinetic Strike (Forces ●●●) with instant cast time, and applies either the Deafened Tilt or Knocked Down tilt.
                        Optional (Prime ●●●●) - The mage may automatically attempt to resist any action, or spell that would prevent her from being able to use her voice. Any attempt, mundane or magical, to silence the mage provokes a Clash of Wills against her Gnosis + Prime.

                        Fifth Attainment: Thunder without Lightning
                        Prerequisites: Forces ●●●●●
                        The mage’s voice is now a force of nature to be reckoned with. By shouting she induces an effect similar to Earthquake (Forces ●●●●●) with instant cast time.
                        Optional (Prime ●●●●●) - At the mage’s discretion, victims of this attainment also suffer one point of aggravated damage per Potency.

                                            Dreamspeakers

                                            To keep this briefer, I'll simply refer to the original in Legacies: The Ancient.
                                            For the most part, I'm just updating the parts that need updating.

                                            Doctrine
                                            Prerequisites: Mind 2, Spirit 1, 4 or more dots across two or more the following: Academics, Empathy, Occult, Dream 1, Members must have shattered their inner Astral Barrier.


                                            Initiation:
                                            Initiation requires the initiate's personal astral barrier to be removed. Initiation into the legacy creates a pressure that has one of only two outcomes: permanent removal of the individual's Astral Barrier or a breaking point. To overcome the internalized astral barrier requires going through a challenge to the normal limited worldview, and certain signs are well-known to prepare a soul for this legacy.

                                            Those who are already in the process of an Dreaming Awakening can be accepted and guided through to its conclusion essentially certain success. Encounters with the Dreamborn can sometimes trigger the Dreaming Awakening process. Mental breaks can launch people into these sink-or-swim mental challenges, as well, though not all mental illnesses are equivalent.



                                            Organization: The Dreamspeakers are tied to various specific cultures. These cultures have a wild variety of structures for organization. As a rule, the Dreamspeakers are extremely hierarchical, but in a familiy sense. The higher ranking members tend to be treated as respected elders of the culture that the specific group comes from.

                                            Between cultures, there is very little formal structured communication. The members' Order membership often overrides in such cases, since there is often strong enmity between twixt various communities that goes back centuries, especially between hidden medicine societies.

                                            The various incarnations of the legacy keep their own traditions and knowledge about initiations and Dreamborn. Almost any specific tradition will take the plight of a seeker lost in their Dreaming Awakening as a moral obligation, even if the subject is their enemy. (That said, some might *kill* certain sorts to resolve the problem, but they *do* resolve it.) This is one of the ways that can bring hostile rival traditions together.


                                            1st: Dreamwalking
                                            Prerequisites: Initiation

                                            The Primal's Dream self is their true self. This was probably always true, but for Primals it becomes an unavoidable truth.

                                            Those who bear this attainment are always partially in the Dream.

                                            This has advantages and disadvantages. First, the only things that a Primal can do in one side without doing the same in both are sedentary things like sleeping or sitting.

                                            Primals need not meditate to enter the astral realms, already being in both, and do not, thus, require mana to pass the Astral boundary.

                                            Because the normal refreshing shock of waking out of the trance is no longer an option for them, however, the Primal has certain risks regarding their own Oneiros that other mages normally do not. Even more dangerous, they cannot simply will to wake up. When killed in the astral, the mage doesn't die, but their mind falls into a coma.

                                            This can sometimes lead to a vision of a Dreamborn and it often leads to death.

                                            A Primal can participate in Dream Shaping.

                                            2nd: Dreamform
                                            Prerequisites: Mind 2
                                            Primals understand the Astral as their native home. Attempts to navigate deeper or return are almost assured. Primals add successes equal to Mind when moving between strata or returning. The same benefit applies when navigating the various realms to return along the path trod, as well.

                                            Optional: Spirit 2

                                            The Primal grows a protective skin around their soul. This applies to goetic powers and also reduces harm from any environmental hazards encountered in the Astral Realm by Spirit dots. This applies to the Exstatic Winds.


                                            3rd: Dreamwings
                                            Prerequisite: Mind 3
                                            As an Instant Action, the mage may Co-Locate his Aquaster astrally to any location to which the mage bears Connected sympathy. At the end of the effect, the mage may choose which location to remain in, and the Primal may cancel the effect reflexively.

                                            This actually involves visualizing and *symbolically becoming* something that can move rapidly, so it's possible to become a version of you that literally has functional wings. The cost of using the effect increases by 1 Willpower if the form doesn't fit one of the realms. If it doesn't really fit either, it still works if you pay 1 Mana, as well.

                                            Should the Primal wish to retain the characteristics after the effect ends, that requires a Dream Shaping roll.

                                            Optional: Spirit 3

                                            Astral and spiritual phenomina blur to the bearer of this attainment.
