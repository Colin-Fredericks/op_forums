    Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post
    13) can a life/ mind spell physically transport you to your oneiros?
    Typically, you’d use Space 4 + Mind 2 to physically transport yourself into the Astral. But, after giving it some thought, I think you can pull this off with Life 4 and Mind 4:
    Step 1: Convert your body to astral-ephemera with a Life/Mind Patterning effect.
    Step 2: Use Mind Perfecting to reverse-summon your newly ephemeral body into your Oneiros.
    Step 3: End the spell from step 1, and ta-dah you are now physically inside your Oneiros.
    Step 4: When you are ready to leave, end the spell from step 2.

    The limitation of this approach (aside from needing Mind 4) is that the reverse-summoning spell would be duration limited. You could theoretically use Mind Patterning as a sort of teleportation that wouldn’t run out of duration, but then you’d be stuck in your oneiros. You couldn’t reverse-summon yourself out, because you’d no longer have any kind of anchor to summon yourself from. However, adding Space 2 would fix this, since you could cast the reverse-summoning spell with Sympathy. (You wouldn’t need Sympathy to pull yourself into your Oneiros, just like you don’t need it to summon things out of it, because you carry your oneiros within yourself. But once inside the astral, you’d have no way of targeting anywhere in the physical world without Sympathy).

    Short version: yes, an sufficiently clever Life 4/Mind 4 spell (probably costing a reach above normal to get you into your oneiros) will let you bodily enter the astral, but when the spell’s duration runs out, you’ll be kicked back to where you entered. (A persistent version of this spell could exist, but would be inadvisable unless you have the necessary Space to get back out.)

    Edit: oh, also this spell would only bring your body, no clothing or equipment.

I'd be fine with the idea that everything in the astral is purely conceptual, but I don't think the books are explicit/consistent about it. 1e definitely leaned in that direction, but then again, the rules for ephemeral entities, the different phases of twilight, etc. weren't fully fleshed out. As I recall, the 1e goetic summons spell required Spirit on top of Mind, wheres the 2e version only requires it if you want to Materialize the summoned entity. Basically, I don't think 1e had a real conception of mind-ephemera as a thing, where 2e definitely does. If 2e astral space were not intended to be composed of the same mind-ephemera that astral entities exist as in the physical world, then I would hope that the 2e descriptions would make mention. As it stands, 2e seems to imply that the Astral/Shadow/Underworld operate on the same basic rules, except where otherwise spelled out. So, from a purely 2e standpoint, I don't see any reason to think that mind-ephemera isn't the basic substance of the astral. Otherwise, things like Goetic Summons and the Los Angeles verges would have to also create/slap together an ephemeral body for the entity crossing over.

                                            There's enough room that I'd understand if someone wanted to rule the other way for their game. But I don't think it's the intended reading for 2e.
