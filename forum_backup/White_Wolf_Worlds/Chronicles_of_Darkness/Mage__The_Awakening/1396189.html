      Bob the Obrimos
      Primary Symbolism: Electrifying Air
      Long Term Nimbii tend to be dominated by path but I would be sure to make it reflect the personal themes a bit. In this case, exciting situations would be a bit more common.Not always bad, either.
      Immediate Nimbus: A feeling of raw potential accompanies the smell of ozone and a literal crackling halo of electricity.
      Nimbus Tilt: Penalty to Composure, Melee, and Athletics as forces and emotions build up faster than normal. (Loop through in that order as Gnosis improves)
      Signature Nimbus: A smell of ozone and signs of electricity and heat

      Richard the Mastigos
      Primary Symbolism: Air of Desire
      Long Term: Finds people who either buy into or oppose his schtick.
      Immediate: A rush of sexual energy, some of which is always directed towards Mack, spikes. There's a sensation of gentle touches which is, of course, usually creepy.
      Nimbus Tilt: Penalty to Composure.
      Signature: Smells like old sex. Has some yellowing.

      Blood the Thyrsus
      Primary Symbolism: Heartbeat
      Long Term: High pressure situations appear around them
      Immediate: Blood's heartbeat is audible and everyone cannot help but hear and feel their heart beating.
      Nimbus Tilt: Bonus to Wits and Dexterity
      Signature: Signs of a blood splatter

      Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.

          Z3R0 the V01D (Moros)

          Long Term: Future designs seem imminent and daunting. Machine’s functions are improved. Haunts and ghosts quiet in expectation.Digital and analog displays glitch strange formulas, numbers or countdowns.
          Immediate: The gravity of inspiration comes crashing down. Objects appear cleaner, glossy and newer. A moment of change both frightening and exhilarating hangs in the air. The formulas, numbers and countdowns reach their terminal point.
          Signature:A sense of awe and wonder linger behind. Devices feel more appealing and user friendly. Bittersweet memories of endings come unbidden. The displays leave answering patterns to unknown questions, zeroes or circles.
          Tilt Intelligence and Presence gains a +1 bonus.Crafts and Expression gain the 8-again quality while Composure suffers a -1 penalty. Z3R0’s Nimbus grants people more self-confidence and greater insights, makes devices and objects easier to use or create, but a sense of disquiet comes over those who felt secure in the permanence of their world.

                          I don't really go here [I love Mage, I just don't have much to share], but here are some for different starting characters I've messed around with.

                          Beacon
                          Obrimos, Mysterium, Gnosis 1, Potent Nimbus 1
                          Immediate Nimbus: Beacon's immediate Nimbus is blinding, as if she were a star condensed into a human shape. Rays of celestial light cascade from her form, burning away all darkness.
                          Signature Nimbus: Everything touched by her Nimbus glows brightly from within, as if it were heated metal or simply just radioactive.
                          Long-term Nimbus: Beacon's long-term Nimbus propagates knowledge, bringing others exactly the information they seek. Revelation and enlightenment spiral out from her.
                          Tilt: -2 Wits

                          Drowned
                          Water-Wise [Neolithic Moros], Gnosis 2, Potent Nimbus 2
                          Immediate Nimbus: Drowned's immediate Nimbus manifests as an oppressive, murky haze, as if underwater, accompanied by the panicked sensation of drowning.
                          Signature Nimbus: Her signature Nimbus is waterlogged, and smells of rot.
                          Long-term Nimbus: Drowned's long-term Nimbus claims the old. Around her, weathered buildings succumb to water damage, rivers consume their banks, and her elders stumble into streams and ponds.
                          Tilt: 8-again on Athletics rolls, -1 Wits and Composure

                          Ecstasy
                          Mastigos, Free Council, Gnosis 1
                          Immediate Nimbus: Ecstasy's immediate Nimbus is an iridescent green haze billowing from her mouth. Within the haze, viewers see scenes of the hedonistic debauchery they lust for.
                          Signature Nimbus: Her signature Nimbus covers objects in beads of green condensation that smell like the observer's guilty pleasure. One person might smell his favorite brand of cigarette, while another scents lemon-lime soda.
                          Long-term Nimbus: Ecstasy's long-term Nimbus gives people what they want, encouraging revelry.
                          Tilt: -1 Composure

                          If you have it, Nameless and Accursed has a lot of great Nimbus examples. In fact, it was largely why I recently gave a signature and long-term Nimbus to each of these characters [since I have never actually played any of them].

                              Fenris (Mastigos)
                              Primary Theme: Shattering the chains that bind you.
                              Long-term Nimbus: People forced into situations they don't approve become more rebellious and hostile.
                              Immediate Nimbus: Blinding rage accompanied with the feeling of invisible bindings scrapping against your body.
                              Signature Nimbus: Weak smell of iron and sweat.
                              Nimbus Tilt: Bonus to Strength and Athletics, Penalty to Dexterity and Wits.

                              Parsec (Mastigos)
                              Primary Theme: Distance
                              Long-term Nimbus: Weakens relations between people. Friends lose contact, lovers fall out of love with each other, mortal enemies get less angry against each other. People rarely interact face-to-face, preferring other means of contact such as phones or conference calls.
                              Immediate Nimbus: Space around the mage seems to stretch. Distances between people and objects grow. Intense feelings mellow out.
                              Signature Nimbus: Objects touched by the Nimbus appear a bit farther than they really are, which makes handling them tricky.
                              Nimbus Tilt: Penalty to Dexterity, bonus to Composure.
