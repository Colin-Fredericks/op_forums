                                        Does the Oneiros exist without a soul? I don't think it does. The clue to its nature is in the name personal soul. It's not called a human soul; it's called a personal soul. The Oneiros is created through the interaction between a human mind and its companion soul. (In the case of a Tremere, their not-soul probably plays a similar role.)

                                        I've been giving some thought as to why the human soul is special in the Chronicles of Darkness. The soul isn't unique in its ability to stabilize the mind or identity of a person. Both the Vampire's Beast and the Beast's Horror do this job adequately. It is unique in its ability to stabilize a mage's Gnosis, but that's an aside. What makes the soul unique is that it provides a blank canvas for the mind. It doesn't alter the identity it provides a substructure for unlike the Beast and the Horror. It allows you to be you, uncorrupted.

                                        So what changes to the Oneiros should we expect when a soul is swapped out? Probably nothing much. There may be some residue from the previous owner, but all in all, it should continue to play its role and allow you to be you.

                                        What happens to a person that is inside the Oneiros of a person whose soul has been severed? Since the Oneiros no longer exists, I'd assume they get punted back to their body instantly. They may or may not experience other consequences, but I think it's fair to assume they get back unharmed.

                                        This is what I'm comfortable with right now. It may not be what works for everyone else, but it makes sense to me.

                                        Of course, some additional questions arose from all this soul searching. What changes does a mage's soul go through that allows it to help reconstitute Gnosis better than a non-mage's soul? Does the mage's Gnosis itself change the nature of the soul? Is it permanent? Do Vampires and Beasts have something similar to an Oneiros where twisted Goetia dwell disconnected from the soul of humanity? These questions are probably best answered in particular chronicles, but I thought they were intriguing.

                                        Something else I find intriguing, souls provide a bridge to the Supernal (and the Abyss) and can only be interacted with via Supernal magic or by a Supernal being, and I also suspect that Abyssal beings are able to do the same thing. Now this is pure speculation, but I think it's possible that souls (human, humanity's, and the world's), the Supernal, and the Abyss are all spawned from the same super reality. Maybe it's where souls go when their owners die. Maybe this is the reality that all others sit on top of?
