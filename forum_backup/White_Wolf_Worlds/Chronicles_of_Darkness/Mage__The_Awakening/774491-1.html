    To be fair, to people who don't know Summoners mechanics "Attainment: Summoning" is pretty misleading. If anything, it's more like "Commune/Contact Symbolic Being" than what is usually summoned to the mind when you hear "summon".
    It's hard to tell without looking at the spellcasting system in it's full glory, but I feel that Yantras are kinda necessary if you want to have big strong effects running around. Common yantras seem to be +1, uncommon and somehow resource expending (be it time, sacrament, opportunity or something else) seem to be +2, and iconic unique means give you +3 (for example, Lex Magica at pinnacle of it's power will give you +3).

                            Actually, starting Werewolves have 3 dots of Renown, 4 Gift Facets and also 2 dots of Rites.

                            Vampires have 3 dots of Disciplines. 2 must be in-Clan, 1 dot free choice. This dot could be spent on Cruac or Theban Sorcery and using the BS:S&B rules that would net you 3 dots of Themes (Blood Sorcery Arcana look-alike of wich there's only 5 against the 10 Arcana).

                            Mages have 6 dots of Arcana spread like: 2/2/2, 3/2/1, 2/2/1/1 or now in second edition also 2/1/1/1/1

                            Unfair?

                            Let's see what each of them gets for free, without spending a single dot in the powers department (I'll consider werewolves without any dots of Renown even if that's imposible):

                            Werewolves get, for free:

                            1) Auspice ability usable once per chapter

                            2) Predators Aspect usable at will

                            3) Passive Free Regeneration. Boosted Regeneration for a cost

                            4) 5 different forms, each with an especial ability, 4 with bonuses to physical Attributes and natural weapons, 3 with increased Defense against firearms, 3 causing Lunacy, 1 with uber-Regeneration. 4 of them can be used freely while the most potent one is limited in its controlled use to a few turns once per Scene.

                            5) 2 sets of improved senses, with bonuses to perception tied to the forms, above.

                            6) Moderate Damage reduction (Aggravated to Lethal)

                            7) Access to the Shadow (only at a Locus)

                            Vampires get, for free:

                            1) Regeneration for a cost

                            2) Great Damage Reduction (Most damage to Bashing)

                            3) Unaging

                            4) Predators Aura usable at will or at a cost

                            5) The Kiss, Its source of power also means damaging the enemy

                            6) Vitae adiction and Vinculum

                            7) Ghoul and Vampire creation

                            8) Physical Augmentation

                            Mages get, for free:

                            1) Mage Sight at will and improved version at a cost

                            2) Pattern Restortation (Regeneration at a cost, most likely worse than any of the others)

                            3) Tilt-inflicting Nimbus, likely at will, maybe at a cost

                            4) Access to the Astral (only at a Hallow or Demesne)

                            Mages get more dots of "learned powers", mostly because the others have a lot of innate template ones making up for it. A Mage without access to Arcana is just a human with a broad Unseen Sense a supernatural aura and a ineficient alternate method of healing*.

                            *At least in 1st edition it was a kinda underwhelming 3 Mana for each Lethal.
