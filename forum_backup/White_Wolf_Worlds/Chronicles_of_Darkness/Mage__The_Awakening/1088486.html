    Mage Booklist

    05-05-2017, 05:10 PM
    This is a handy booklist tracking all of the resources that the community thinks are useful for Chronicles including the Awakened. I am only starting with a partial list for now, but I'm hoping to update it tonight with the massive library of 1e books that exist for Mage. I am also taking suggestions for non-Mage books that people think are particularly useful in a Mage game. For example, I am including Conquering Heroes on the Mage booklist, since it contains rules for a new Proximi dynasty. If there is a book you think I've missed, please let me know what it is and why it is useful for Mage, and I will include it.
