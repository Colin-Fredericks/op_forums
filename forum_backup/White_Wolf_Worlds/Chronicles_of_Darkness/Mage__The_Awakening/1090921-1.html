            Originally posted by totalgit View Post
            Explain to me how damaging decay suddenly makes things better? It'd slow it down yes, or even stop it, but unravelling decay is not reversing it.
            You have to consider things in a Greek perspective. Fundamentally, things exist as reflections of a platonic ideal. Each tree, for example, is a partial reflection of the fundamental supernal symbol of trees. Trees, as all things, are not collections of molecules, bound by the laws of conservation of matter and energy, but are indivisible reflections of their corresponding symbols. The only way for a tree to deviate from the fundamental symbol of tree, is through the incorporation of other symbols into its pattern. When you chop a tree into wood, you are not breaking it apart. Things are indivisible. Rather, you are creating a new symbol of wood, and introducing a Death pattern to the tree itself. If you were to remove this Death pattern, the tree would spring back, fully formed, all of its branches and roots intact again, because there would be nothing stopping it from reflecting the fundamental tree symbol once again.

            So in other words, all decay, both active and past, is a foreign influence on other symbols. It must fall under an arcanum, because all things fall under an arcanum, and the most fitting arcanum for it is Death.

                                                "How is it that a geist, a creature of pure living death, can bring life back to its Sin-Eater host? Theanswer is bound up in the fact that the Bargain doesn’t heal the Sin-Eater’s wounds. Geists cannot actually restore life or heal a living mortal, but as archetypal figures of death, they do have an ability to manipulate death itself. In essence, the Bargain, and the attendant merging of the human soul with the inhuman geist allows the shade not to give life, but to take away death. This concept seems baffling, especially to humans who are accustomed to thinking of death as the absence of life, but Sin-Eaters recognize that death has a powerful energy of its own. By pushing that energy away from its new host, the geist staves off death long enough for the living body to repair itself — whether that be fighting off the disease that ravages it or knitting together bones and organs shattered by gunfire".

                                                As for the exact modalities of the healing:

                                                "The Bargain brings the Sin-Eater surging back to life, but it doesn’t exactly heal her. Whatever injuries brought the Sin-Eater to the brink of death linger; whatever disease killed her goes into remission but doesn’t vanish overnight. Many Sin-Eaters come to understand what they’ve become while lying in a hospital bed, recuperating."

                                                    I think the reasonable interpretation in my opinion is not whether Death should be able to remove damage, clearly it should deal with the issue of damage removal. How is the trick.

                                                    My sensibilities are something like this:

                                                    Fraying being able to remove one potency per Bashing/Lethal, but it has a normal spell Duration, and not be able to affect Aggravated at all.
                                                    • +2 Reach for making Bashing removal Lasting.
                                                    Bonus: This affects any type of being capable of being damaged since the injury is the target, and leaves no scars.

                                                    Unraveling being able to remove Lethal or Bashing Lasting on a one Potency to one damage ratio, at a Lasting duration.
                                                    • +2 reach it may affect Aggravated damage at the same ratio, but has a normal spell duration and isn't Lasting.
                                                    • +3 Reach and 1 Mana, it may affect Aggravated damage at the same ratio and is Lasting
                                                    Bonus: This affects any type of being capable of being damaged since the injury is the target, and leaves no scars.

                                                    ----

                                                    This distinguishes it from how Life heals, making it in specialized situations more advantageous, and in common situations less, which helps makes the overlap nuanced, meaningful and interesting. Also you may ask why is healing Bashing so hard? I'd answer Death Mage Armor converts damage to Bashing and being the best at healing be a Life and Death thing amuses me, and discourages one Arcanum-stop-shopping
