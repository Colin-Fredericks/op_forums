                Ok, question concerning Verges:
                A Verge is blatantly supernatural, with giant onyx towers shaped like clawed habds, salty barriws full of ghosts who answer questions if fed blood, and so on, but theae efects are often hidden in Twilight.

                What does 'hidden in Twilight' mean, exactly?
                ● Sleepers can't peeceive them, obviously.
                ■ Can Sleepwalkers/Proximi perceive them?

                ● Do mages interact with Verges without the need for magic?
                ■ Do they need Active Mage Sight up to do so?
                ■ Need a mage enter some sort of Twilight frequency?
                See the relevant section in Signs of Sorcery:
                "These paralocations can only be seen from inside of the Verge; from the other side they’re conveniently obscured by fog, look and act like ordinary places, or vanish. The Lie conceals them through the most plausible, expedient means. If the Verge is destroyed or a paralocation somehow strays from its bounds, it degrades into ordinary phenomena. Sky castles fall to earth as Gothic follies."

                Given the lack of ​elaboration on the bit from the corebook that you're referencing, "disguised by Twilight" can be taken to be being used in a similar fashion to how Demon sometimes uses it: "This location/these phenomena exist in a way that mostly disregards the surrounding material world except where necessary." A Verge might only be able to be entered from certain points and outside of the heart of it — where a Thura might exist — any particularly obvious manifestations of the Supernal World are on a parallel track to what any surrounding mortals can see and/or are naturally veiled from recorded observation. If you've entered the Verge and haven't left it the you can see and interact with anything that's not separately invisible or intangible.

                                                        Originally posted by lbeaumanior View Post
                                                        The Familiar spell, shouldnt it have a Lasting Reach option? Otherwise every familiar (goetia, ghost, or spirit) is one dispellation away from breaking the bond.
                                                        No, that's the point.

                                                        The spell is already a Duration-primary Adept-level effect that only works on mutually-willing parties, meaning anyone who can create a Familiar bond can make it last for a year with basic Advanced Duration and only needs one extra Reach, a -2 penalty, and a point of Mana to make it Indefinite if they aren't a Master or using a Rote.

                                                        Remember also that dispellation spells default to suppressing the targeted spell for their Duration, that Familiar definitionally has at least two Subjects and needs at least Potency 5 to overcome its Withstand rating, and that without dedicating extra factors or Reach to Duration the dispellation will last a single turn before the spell reasserts itself.
