    Originally posted by Axelgear View Post
    On this... Not quite. Spirits of the Shadow are very much defined by the hunt for sustenance, but that doesn't mean ghosts or goetia don't have a persistent need of it too. Essence sustains them and, importantly, is what grants them the ability to manifest and affect the world. Ghosts, remember, only regain one Essence a day spent in the presence of their Anchors. Their home realm is the Underworld, not the Fallen World, so their access to Essence is really limited. Likewise, if they want to do anything beyond the very limited range of their Anchors, that costs one Essence per hour.
    Ghosts can get Essence other ways, actually. 'Ghosts regain a point of Essence whenever someone remembers the living person they once were. Visiting their grave, simply sitting and remembering them, or recognizing their Manifested form as the person they used to be all qualify.'

    But it's clear we're locked in disagreement here. It'd be a problem if we were gaming together but we're free to interpret the game how we want at our tables.

                        Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
                        I saw a house which took away Mind's ability to boost Social Attributes and gave it to Spirit. Can anyone tell me the metaphysical justification for this? Would it be a bad choice to allow both Arcana to boost Social Attributes? I suppose you could also make an argument for Forces being capable of boosting Physical Attributes.
                        It might be a play on words with how we tend to describe charismatic people as "spirited" or having "animal magnetism" or such. The word charisma actually comes from a Greek term meaning "gift" and in old Christian parlance refers to an extraordinary power bestowed by the Holy Spirit. The thinking might also make a connection to the power that religious figures of all stripes seem to hold over people. Divorcing Social Attributes from Mind implies that that Arcanum holds domain only over the logical, while also expanding Spirit's remit to include matters of the soul. I don't think there's any problem with allowing the spell under both Arcana, though you may want to distinguish them in some way, such as different Reach effects.
