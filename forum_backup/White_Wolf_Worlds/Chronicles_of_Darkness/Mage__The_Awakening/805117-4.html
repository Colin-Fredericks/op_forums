            Originally posted by Aiden View Post

            That looks like a contradiction that should be mentioned in the Errata thread.
            Yeah, I already pointed it out. I figure it'll be added to the FAQ if it's not a mistake.

            I don't think it's necessarily a massive nerf, but it does make some things a lot more complicated.

            Say you want to convert the Reality Stalkers. Their attainments are very straightforward and most of them are converted or easy to figure out.

            First Attainment
            Space: Spatial Awareness
            Mind: ????

            Second Attainment
            Space: Scrying
            Mind: Incognito Presence

            Third Attainment
            Space: Co-Location
            Mind: Imposter

            Fourth Attainment:
            Space: ????
            Mind: Hallucination. It's not quite a perfect fit, but it's close enough.

            Fifth Attainment:
            Space: Pocket Dimension
            Mind: ????

            On the surface, you just need to account for the fact that Legacies now have five Attainments, and that Pocket Dimension is Space 5 rather than Space 4.

            But if you look at Reach, it doesn't work.

            On the Space side, Spatial Awareness is going to have a Duration in turns, although that might not be a major problem, Scrying won't do anything because it won't have enough Reach for a sensory effect, and Co-Location will only affect spaces you can touch. Only Pocket Dimension will work fine since you don't really need to cast that one instantly. On the Mind side, Incognito Presence, Imposter and Hallucination will only work at touch range, and their Duration is going to be in turns. In order to fix the problem you'll have to move the Attainments up.

            First Attainment:
            Space: ????
            Mind: ????

            Second Attainment:
            Space: Spatial Awareness
            Mind: ????

            Third Attainment:
            Space: Scrying
            Mind: Incognito Presence

            Fourth Attainment:
            Space: Co-Location
            Mind: Imposter

            Fifth Attainment:
            Space: Pocket Dimension
            Mind: Hallucination

            At the upper end, it works better since your Fourth Space and Fifth Mind Attainments are filled out, but you need to find new spells that are still useful if you have only one reach to spend in order to fill out the first two Attainments.

                        Behold, version two! Or is it three, since it's the second version of an adaptation of an original?

                        Bene Ashmedai

                        Origins
                        Parentage: Mastigos, Seers of the Throne or Silver Ladder
                        Nickname: Diabolists
                        Background: see Silver Ladder, pg. 58
                        Appearance: see Silver Ladder, pg. 57

                        Doctrine
                        Prerequisites: Spirit 2, Empathy 2, and one of the following skills at 2 dots or higher: Expression, Occult, Persuasion, Socialize, or Subterfuge
                        Initiation: The prospective Diabolist must make a mutually beneficial arrangement with the Goetic demon of their Vice under the observation of her tutor.
                        Organization: The Bene Ashmedai have learned that gathering in numbers worries mages who aren't part of their Legacy due to their kinship with humanity's inner demons. Egotism doesn't help much with that either. As such, Diabolists do not often gather in numbers larger than mentor/student pairings, and otherwise remain aloof of their fellows.
                        Theory: Weakness comes from denying the self and its desires. Bene Ashmedai give form to their desires as demons, and make common cause with them. They find power and pleasure both in this alliance, and by the process of perfecting it seek the ultimate form of both - Ascension.

                        Magic
                        Ruling Arcanum: Spirit
                        Yantras: Succeeding in a Social Maneuvering roll against a spell's target without magic before the spell (+2), fulfilling your Vice (+1) or Obsession (+2) as part of the spell, making use of pentagrams or ritual circles as part of the spell (+1; +2 if they take at least a scene to implement), monologuing about the motives and desires of the spell's subject (+1; +2 if you talk about your own and make yourself vulnerable)
                        Oblations: Acting as a guru or other spiritual authority; conversing with goetia or other goeticists; demonological research; engaging in a seduction (either as the initiator or as the pursued); giving in to Vice without concern for the consequences (or inspiring another to do so); indulging in the fear and/or adulation of others; learning another person’s Virtue, Vice, or magical Obsession.

                        Attainment 1
                        Prerequisites: Initiation
                        Exorcist's Eye [Applies to Goetia automatically if mage fulfils requirements]
                        Optional Mind 1
                        Know Nature

                        Attainment 2
                        Prerequisites: Spirit 2, Empathy 3
                        Channel Essence [Applies to Goetia automatically if mage fulfills requirements]
                        Optional: Mind 2
                        First Impressions

                        Attainment 3
                        Prerequisites: Spirit 3. In addition to the Empathy 3 requirement from the previous Attainment, the Diabolist must increase the other Skill she used to meet the requirements of the Legacy to 3, or must attain two dots in a second skill from the list of possible requirements.
                        Gossamer Touch [Applies to Goetia automatically if mage fulfills requirements]
                        Optional Mind 3
                        Mental Shield

                        Attainment 4
                        Prerequisites: Spirit 4, Empathy 4, and the other Skill requirements of the previous Attainment.
                        Bind Spirit [Applies to Goetia automatically if mage fulfills requirements]
                        Optional Mind 4
                        Goetic Summons [Can be Materialized if the mage desires it]

                        Attainment 5
                        Prerequisites: Spirit 5. In addition to the Empathy 4 requirement from the previous Attainment, the Diabolist must increase the other Skill she used to meet the requirements of the Legacy to 4, or must attain three dots in a second Skill from the list of possible requirements, or two dots in a third Skill from the list.
                        Banishment [Applies to Goetia automatically if mage fulfills requirements]
                        Optional Mind 5
                        Amorality [Can only remove the subject's Virtue, but makes them Open to any ephemeral entity with a strong tie to their Vice]

                                            I think that Attainments should use Arcanum dots of Attainment level as Reach. This way:

                                            1st - Instant casting or 1 Reach variant.
                                            2nd - Instant casting + 1 Reach or 2 Reach variant.
