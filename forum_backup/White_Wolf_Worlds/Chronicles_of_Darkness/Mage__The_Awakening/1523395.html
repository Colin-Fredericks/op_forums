    Five Atlanthean Mysteries and Egregore in Second Edition

    10-01-2023, 04:08 AM
    In first edition, as I understand it, Status in the Mysterium and experiencing Mysteries were tied to each other. You could not climb the ranks without experiencing Mystery of corresponding level; experiencing the Mystery bestowed upon you the benefits of increased status amongst mystagogues. But in the second edition, Egregore is now a style requiring only first dot of Mysterium Status; it can be increased without Gnosis or Arcana requirements. Theoretically, starting character with 5 dots in Egregore is possible. While I fully support that from mechanical point of view, this creates a gap between mechanics and fluff (of first edition), where very young mage experienced and won Mysteries which are far out of his reach, yet. How one could reconcile old fluff and current mechanics?

          I think it's better to draw inspiration from the original version to lean in to the distinct tracks, rather than try to retain it directly.                                           There's also always the divide between "I've met the mechanical pre-requisite" and "In game, no-one is willing to teach me". The second must be used carefully by an ST, balancing what makes a good story, because blanket stonewalling can be just as un-fun to play with. Maybe there's a heretical Heirophant out there who will initiate members of other Orders who've achieved Mysterium Status 1, but requires a major task before he'll do so. Suddenly this turns into a sub-plot, rather than someone getting a bit Munchkin-y.

                                          (That being said, I do think the Silver Ladder teaches the Lex Magica Merit to others much more readily that the Mysterium would hand out Egregore. It's useful to Sentinels and other Consillium officials, and those are the most likely to have some tie to the Ladder "Look! Participating in the system we set up makes you better at magic! Other people can recognise the symbolism of symbols of authority at a more superficial level, but you need to really have "Magic is Humanity's Birthright" to make spells out of it.
