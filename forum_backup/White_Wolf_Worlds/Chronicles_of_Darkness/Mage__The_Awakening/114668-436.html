            The Mudra draws from memorisation. There can be no memorisation of spells that haven't been studied and learnt, and spells that are studied and learnt are Rotes.

            Edit: Mrmdubois is right. Signs of Sorcery does have something similar based on transferring memorised techniques from one spell to another, but with a drop in effectiveness since you're basically creating two imagos at once. The improvised spell needs to be of the same Arcanum and Practice, and the spell you draw from is still a Rote that you have learnt.

                                            Originally posted by Emerus View Post
                                            Regardless of High Speech though, I think there should be some way to incorporate actual body movement of some kind (as opposed to the mnemonic techniques of a rote) as a yantra in a spell, considering that so many other things can serve as yantra.
                                            Mudra, certain forms of Techné, and baking it into a fitting Persona. In the latter case the movements would only be a part of the actual Yantra and not have any meaning on their own (and you'd probably be forced to incorporate those movements anytime you want to cast with your Persona Yantra).
                                            Stretching it even further would be certain Path Tools that are interacted with through dance (though dancing around a pole as a rod type Path Yantra is the only thing that comes to mind, and I'm not sure it's compatible with the given wand/rod symbolism).

                                            Other than that is starting to come up with homebrew Yantras. Dance could be an Order Tool for a Nameless Order (either one that is a basic benefit, or a more involved Yantra that requires an additional Merit like Adamant Hand) or a minor Legacy Yantra. You could also come up with a Merit that does allow you to express High Speech through body language (with the same benefits and drawbacks of the Mantra). The High Speech Merit would certainly be a prerequisite.

                                            Originally posted by Emerus View Post
                                            Both Mantras and Concentration are under the Actions category, so I'm guessing that other types of Actions could find their way into the yantra family. My idea, based on my interpretation of the book and the things said in this conversation so far, is that it could be another type of Action yantra. Thoughts on that?
                                            Unfortunately we don't really have many good examples of Action Yantras when it comes to extrapolating new Action Yantras. They're all unique in some way with non-standard benefits and/or drawbacks.

                                            Originally posted by Emerus View Post
                                            Also, I believe that the Broad Dedication Merit is irrelevant in regards to whether body movement can be a yantra or not, its only function is to turn it into a dedicated "tool" IF it can first be a yantra.
                                            Everything can be used as a Yantra. The question is whether it grants dice or not. If it doesn't, the Yantra is simply worthless rather than invalid. That allows you to Dedicate any object you want, including objects that are normally never used as Tool Yantras. Broad Dedication would allow the same thing for non-Tool Yantras, which would grant an actual benefit to dancing if it'd otherwise be a worthless Yantra.
