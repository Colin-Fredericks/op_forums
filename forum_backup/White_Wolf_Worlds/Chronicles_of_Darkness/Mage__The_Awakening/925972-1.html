            • Copies by me
            The entity is ‘one in many’. She can make copies of herself, working like effects of Manifold Presence spell, giving many possible instances of it. They all are connected to proper entity, but not so hurting. Each copy can be destroyed with full Bashing damage of Corpus from main entity and takes one Willpower from entity pool. Each copy is treated as entity itself for using it’s powers or targeting it’s targets.
            • Flattening by me
            Being can make 3D being into 2D beings image, that is also living on surface. He need to win Grapple Contest and spend 1 Willpower on each target.
            • Flesh of Abyss by me
            Entity is build from the stuff of Abyss itself. That means, Supernal Magic have really harder time to interact with it. Each spell cast on entity is treated as one Spell Factor lower – that means that attack spells makes one damage lower, Scale is treated as needing to get Size 6 to work on it and Duration is shorter than caster imagined when making Imago.
            • Freighting by me
            Anyone seeing Entity need to roll Resolve + Composure each turn, or they will run for their lives. Mind Shield spell makes it Clash of Wills on Gnosis + Mind of caster. On losing, victim makes run as fast as it can from Entity.
            • Madness by me
            Anyone seeing Entity need to roll Composure + Supernatural Advantage each turn, or they will go mad. Mind Shield spell makes it Clash of Wills on Gnosis + Mind of caster. On losing, victim makes any commands of Entity uder it's mad influance.
            • Resistant by me
            Entity resist Supernal Magic much more efficiently than other of her kind – she get’s automatic Withstand source equal to its Rank against Awakened spells. That means she generally have Rank+1 Withstand, as its Attributes are normal source of Withstand, see rules of Withstanding, MtAw 2E page 114.
            • Summoning by me
            Entity can summon Abyssal Beings of it's original type from half of it's original Rank ( rounded up ) by spending 1 Essence for each one.

                No Escape (Numen)

                The entity is considered to perceive everything in its surroundings as if it had the "Outward And Inward Eye" spell cast with the +2 Reach option, which cannot be dispelled. All spells cast with the intent of getting away from the entity, transporting it, warding against it or summoning outside help trigger a Clash Of Wills consisting of Power + Rank vs Gnosis + Arcanum.

                Furthermore, Paradoxes generated in its presence form sympathetic links between it and the sources of the Paradoxes. Every Success on the Paradox may be spent to create a Weak Spatial Sympathy between the entity and a target, or upgrade the Sympathy by one step, up to Strong. The entity may use Scrying on any subjects it has a Strong connection to, and may activate its Numina and Influences Sympathetically with 1 Essence. These links last for the normal period listed under Paradox Anomalies or the Duration of the spell that incurred Paradox, whichever is longer.

                        Altasaire shared a technique for coming up with Abyssal Influences with me.

                        You can use this random word generator until you have a selection of words that sounds semi-coherent but perverse, weird, scary or any combination thereof. After that, you visualize how that Influence could work depending on the order and interpretation of the words.

                        For example: Af, The Angel Of Anger is a Rank 2 Abyssal called forth by the failed summoning of an Obrimos. Af has Influence 2 (Authority Revenge Blister Honesty Snake Paper). This can work in several ways.

                        1) Snakes made of revenge slithering across written things and warping them by their passing in a way to pervert the authority of the words by making them honest. The snakes eat legalese and leave clear speech but speech cleared in such ways as to engender revenge.

                        2) An image of a horrible serpent that spreads across paper. When seen by authority figures, it implants a curse upon them. This curse covers them in blisters if they are reprimanded by someone seeking revenge against them for not being honest.

                        The Influence can be both simultaneously. The Abyss is supposed to be maddening and paradoxical, after all.

                                Speaking of, GibberingEloquence made a good find. Here's one I just got from the word generator: The defining words are (expressive horoscope afraid grandiose damnation injury)

                                The Face of the Inevitable is a Rank 4 Abyssal Jaggling with the Influences of Art and Doom, called by an iteration of the Shard revolving around a post-apocalyptic dystopia. She embodies a form of Fate that inverts the relationship between Destiny and Doom-under her power, all things with notable Fates invariably meet tragedy and abject failure until the Escape Clause is found to break her prognostication. How it happens is that she composes epic artworks and leaves them to people who may be frightened by the subject matter despite the fact that they aren't horror works (she's partial to painting, but she's no slouch when it comes to literature or even short films). If it works, her victims see something in the normally cheerful if bizzare work that greatly alarms them, and they have dreams that night of fortune tellers giving them terrible fates. Invariably, her proscribed fates are always ones that inflict major harm on the bearer or the world around them.

                                The Numina she uses is Idea of Tragedy; a variant of Aggressive Meme that attempts to roll her raw Power + Finesse against the target's Resolve + Composure + Occult, as knowledge of her symbols reveal the fundamental ridiculousness of much of her subliminal dooms (after all, it depends on a world that doesn't actually exist). If the first roll fails, it then rolls for a normal Aggressive Meme, but with the instruction to share the triggering artwork with someone else until the true poisoned Fate is implanted. An exceptional success voids this roll, as the target detects there is something not just silly in the artwork, but malicious-without actually being frightened by the art itself, only the artist.

                                    That-Which-Must-Not-Be-Seen by Pale Crusader

                                    The ephemeral being is antithetical to the discovery of secrets and powers that reveal information. Such active or deliberate use of powers while the entity in the perception of the user applies Broken, Madness, or Fugue Condition those attempting to use the power. This applies from Auspex, various Uratha gifts that reveal information, Numina like Omen or Seek, to anything more than Peripheral Mage Sight, and any other power which is reasonably similar to those examples. Also the Condition is considered to be caused as if from resistant source meaning supernatural affects fail to remove the condition, and they must be resolved naturally. Additionally rolls of Failure are downgraded to Dramatic Failures, Success are downgraded to Failures, and Exceptional Success are downgraded to a mere success, after the completion of all the rolls in the case of extended rolls.


                                    That-Which-Must-Not-Be-Named by Pale Crusader

                                    The ephemeral being is capable of being seen in any spectrum or frequency of Twilight by beings capable of seeing into Twilight, and is able to interact with any being in Twilight and when in Twilight counts as a valid target for things that can affect beings within Twilight. It however does not count as an angel, spirit, geotia, ghost, geist, supernal being, or any other 'type' of ephemeral being for purposes of targeting effects. It has no "type", other than being an Abyssal Ephemeral Entity. As an abyssal entity it uses the rules on page 252 MtA except where it lists the specific affects of being a Gulmoth or a Acamoth, as it is neither of those. Also for purposes of Sympathy casters are considered to not know its name, regardless of how it is or is not addressed.

                                        Eerie Disappearance by Pale Crusader

                                        No matter how safe its victims think they are, by spending 1 Willpower the entity can suddenly draw any being to which it has a weak or greater Spacial Sympathy into a scene where it currently is. Any character witnessing the character's disappearance (including the character themselves, if they are conscious) must succeed on a reflexive Resolve + Composure roll or gain the Shaken Condition.
                                        If the characters have actively taken appropriate measures to keep the entity from taking them (Warding against it, Trying to get people to pay attention to them so it is harder for them to disappear, ect...) it will take one full turn of the entity's effort for the character to simply pop into the intended scene, giving the character time to react to the sense of impending dread of being taken. If not, it’s just happens as a reflexive action and the creature can also take one more action before anyone can react.

                                            Paradoxical Puppetry (Numen)

                                            The Abyssal may blur the lines between its own will and that of its target. The target must be within line of sight (unless the Abyssal has a power that imitates Sympathetic casting, like No Escape), and the entity must spend 1 Essence and roll Power + Rank vs Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance. If the entity succeeds, it and the target share senses and thoughts for the remainder of the scene. During this, either one may spend 1 Willpower point to make the other take an instant action.

                                            At the end of the scene, the target must roll Resolve + Composure with a penalty equal to the entity's Rank. If they fail, they gain a Vice of Cooperative and a Resistant Delusional Condition (The Abyssal And I Are One). This means the Condition cannot be Resolved through supernatural means. Whenever the target acts on this Delusion, the Abyssal gains 1 Essence, which it may use to extend the effects of the Numen. The Abyssal may have this Numen affecting a numer of individuals up to its Power + Rank. Furthermore, any supernatural power meant to coerce either the Abyssal or its target into doing something may be redirected to the other with a successful Clash Of Wills against Resistance + Rank or Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance.

                                                    This one might be too free form. What examples come to your mind for this?

                                                    Gaze Of Liminal Existence (Numen)

                                                    This Numen may only be used by Abyssals of Rank 4+. The Abyssal may inflict a curse that makes the target's Pattern exist in-between two Arcana, but not belong fully to either. This curse must be fundamentally tied to the Abyssal's unnature. The target must be within line of sight (unless the Abyssal has a power that imitates Sympathetic casting, like No Escape), and the Abyssal must spend 5 Essence and roll Finesse + Rank vs Composure + Supernatural Tolerance. Any attempt to undo the curse requires both Arcana, +2 Reach and a successful Clash Of Wills agains the Abyssal's Resistance + Rank. The curse lasts for a number of days equal to Successes. Certain exceptional Abyssals may acquire additional instances of this Numen, but only up to Rank.

                                                    Examples:
                                                    • ​Life/Death: Life persists beyond all cessation of biological functions, allowing its victims to temporarily be cursed by a nightmarish half-dead experience.
                                                    • Mind/Fate: People act in accordance with a design, with no capacity to deviate from it. Free Will is destroyed.
                                                    • Time/Space: All conceptualisation of travel and location is lost. The victims conceptualise themselves as existing at all times, and all places within their lives.
                                                    EDIT: Would it be easier to use if this Numen selectively eliminated the target's links to aspects within an Arcanum's purview equal to the Abyssal's Successes? This Numen would affect one Arcanum each time it is purchased.
