    [New Legacy] The Anthropic Custodians

    03-26-2018, 07:31 PM
    The Anthropic Custodians believe that the only way to free the world from the Lie is to change how its people work, rather the systems and societies around them. And so they have taken it upon themselves to discover new ways to reshape the population bit by bit according to a grand design that, according to them, will result in a new age of perfected humanity. They focus on fixing the shortcomings of the human blueprint and expanding upon it through a variety of surgical processes and modifications, with the end goal of removing weaknesses and flaws that lead to faulty decision making and undesired infirmity. Their vision for the Fallen World is one where disease, hunger and even the basic errors of human nature are a thing of the past.

    Origins
    Parentage: Obrimos, Silver Ladder
    Nicknames: Cultivators, God Pretenders (Derogatory)
    Background: Geneticists, artists, philanthropists and similarly beneficent and scholarly types make up the ranks of this Legacy.
    Appearances: Many possess bio-arcane modifications lurking just beneath their skin which they can call upon to express their ideal selves. They are all singularly well-groomed and overflowing with a healthy joy of living, and it is not uncommon for the flora and fauna around their homes to grow according to their Shadow Name and Praxis.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Life 2, Medicine 2, Science 2
    Initiation: The initiate must program a new type of bio-arcane modification meant to be implanted into living Sleepwalker and Awakened test subjects for the purposes of developing new and useful magical properties. Extending that magic to Sleepers or making it become an inherent part of their Patterns is something the Legacy aspires to.

    Organization And History: The Cultivators present themselves to the Sleeper masses as a charitable, research focused NGO that only hires the best and brightest, but they actually want to change the world for the better by directly changing the makeup of its lifeforms and their magical potential. So far, they do not seem to possess a central leadership, and work more like a collaborative of like-minded innovators than a proper organization. However, they do have members who are considered first among equals for their considerable contributions, and the Legacy defers to their judgment when deemed necessary.

    They have borrowed Proximi members from several Dynasties in order to train and experiment on them, and their results range from the inconclusive to the marvelous, and such altered Proximi make up the assistants and middle management, as is usual for the Awakened. The most illustrious member of the Legacy is its founder, a brilliant and highly eccentric Obrimos of the Silver Ladder who is shrouded in myths both amazing and terrible. He is the owner of many pharmaceutical companies and does a lot of charity work around the world for noble causes, but rumors have it that he created his Legacy in order to somehow replace Sleepers with a better version. Some say he already has a prototype, somewhere. Such rumors about the Legacy are not few, which makes it dangerously close to being branded as Left Handed.

    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Life
    Optional Arcanum: Prime
    Yantras: Medical tools (+1), rare chemicals (+1), surgical procedures (+2), gardens and greenhouses (+2), special fruit of your labors (+2).
    Oblations: designing a new bio-arcane modification, experimenting with Ley Lines and chemicals, testing your creations out in the field

    Attainments

    First: Blueprints
    Prerequisite: Initiation
    Cultivators can read the symbols of genetics just as well as those of Mana. This Attainment acts as the Analyze Life spell, with Reach assigned to Instant Casting. This Attainment only works on living beings, but achieves bonus Successes equal to your Life rating on Clashes of Wills.
    Optional: Prime 1
    In order to know what solutions must be created for the people, one must know their flaws and their potential. This Attainment mimics the effects of the Supernal Vision spell with Reach assigned to Instant Casting.

    Second: Sterilization
    Prerequisite: Life 2, One of the Prerequisite Skills At 3
    It is not enough to understand magic, one must cleanse their body as well to avoid infections and other complications as a result of the surgical process. This Attainment mimics the Cleanse The Body spell, with Reach assigned to Instant Casting and making the resistance roll immediately.
    Optional: Prime 2
    Once the Cultivators cleanse the patient's body, it is time to prepare them for arcane modification. The Cultivator must get rid of any spells within the target's Pattern that might intervene with the modifications in a dangerous way. This Attainment emulates the Dispel Magic spell, with Reach assigned to making the effect Lasting.

    Third: Optimization
    Prerequisite: Life 3, Both of the Prerequisite Skills At 3
    One's body must be more than cleansed, it must be taught how to operate at peak efficiency in order to keep up with its future modifications. This Attainment mimics the Body Control spell, with Reach assigned to halving the healing time for Lethal damage per Potency level.
    Optional: Prime 3
    The modifications the patients will receive are a wonder of Mana, and that precious resource must come from somewhere. This Attainment mimics the Channel Mana spell, with Reach assigned to Instant Casting.

    Fourth: Augmentation
    Prerequisite: Life 4, One of the Prerequisite Skills at 4 and the other at 3
    The Cultivator now understands how to improve the lackluster functions of the human body with features from other lifeforms. This mimics the Transform Life spell, with Reach assigned to Advanced Scale (Subjects) and Advanced Duration.
    Optional: Prime 4
    Working hand in hand with the previous Attainment, this one creates miraculous cybernetic implants out of Mana. This works like the Platonic Form spell, with Reach assigned to giving the implants 8 Again and Advanced Duration.

    Fifth: Rebirth
    Prerequisite: Life 5, Both of the Prerequisite Skills at 4
    The Cultivators who are the closest to their peak potential can easily bring back lost limbs and organs should a valuable test subject or assistant suffer grievous injuries. This mimics the Mend spell, with Reach assigned to healing scars and Aggravated damage.
    Optional: Prime 5
    At this level of mastery the Cultivator is capable of easily preparing the soil inside a Sleeper soul for Sleepwalker and then Proximus status. The Abyssal shard is neatly severed, allowing the Cultivator to proceed with other experiments to develop the patient's magical potential and pass it onto their descendants. This Attainment mimics the Stealing Fire spell, with Reach assigned to Advanced Duration, Instant Casting and Advanced Scale (Subjects).

    Bye.

      I like it, I've got some suggestions though.

      I'd personally make those Yantras a bit more activity-based.
      Something like "Succeed on a Medicine roll relevant to the spell (+2), gather biological information your subject (+2, separate to Material Sympathy yantras), take a moment to inspect the subject's biology (+1), presence of medical or cultivation tools (+1)"

      Also Oblations are generally non-Magically based actions, they're mundane goings-on which turn your soul into the Sacred Place to reap mana. Perhaps Vivisections/Dissections, studying biology, or so forth.

      Also the Optional Arcanum isn't defined in the Legacy, anyone who makes atypical Attainments can use their own optional Arcanum, and you can really use any, but those from the parent Path are most common.

      First Attainment is good, but I'd offer the Informed Condition, relating to the exploitation of the subject's biology, in addition to the information provided. Otherwise there's no benefit to it beyond casting a spell any Mage with the Arcanum at 1 can give. Given it's unlikely to be dispelled, trigger quiescence, or cause an Act of Hubris.

      Second Attainment is neat, but the Optional Prime element could be a problem. You get Potency equal to Prime dots, which generally won't be sufficient to do much, and the casting time due to a lack of other available Reach is a scene. Perhaps I might suggest using that ritual period to grant an Advanced Duration Wards and Signs? Basically keeping the subject Magically 'sterile' for a while after your ritual?

      Third Attainment is neat. Remember to mention it takes a scene to activate, or make it persistent, and state the Potency is 2 (rising to 3 at Life 4). Optional Arcanum is questionable in utility, limited Potency is such that offensive uses are minimal, and non-offensive uses can be just as easily performed by an Obrimos without an Attainment. Perhaps grant As Above, So Below with 8-agains to a specified Legacy Yantra?

      Fourth Attainment, I'd personally suggest instead of using Advanced Scale, making it Instant Casting. That way it can be used on the fly, and since Attainments don't have spell control, you can just keep using instant actions to modify more and more patients, without needing to perform a big Ritual for only a few. Also there's an issue with the Prime Optional, you need to spend additional Reach to create complex form such as implants or weapons. Perhaps state that they can only be simple, tying it into the first half of the attainment, adding things not normally found in nature, but which could be? Like Wolverine Claws?

      Fifth Attainment seems a bit lacklustre. Perhaps grant automatic regeneration? Given your Reach allocation, your healing still takes a Scene to activate, which for a Gnosis 8+ Master seems a bit weak. The Optional seems a bit neat, but remember it's not "quite" severing it, as it returns a the end of the suration.

              That's a very dangerous clause to add to Attainments.
              By design they're supposed to be limited application, and Mages acknowledge that they have their limitations and resort to regular spellcasting when they don't quite work the way they want.
              Being able to breeze past Withstand just by using it in a certain way seems like exceeding that limited power element.

              Personally to encourage more thematic attainment use, I just restrict their operation to certain methods, in exchange for additional reach.

              e.g. Make a 2nd Attainment Body Control persistent in effect, in exchange for limiting it to self-only, over normal Advanced Duration.

              Let weaker Mages use their Attainments on weaker subjects.
              A Death 3 Reaper with a soul-stealing Attainment should prey on average Joes, and gain the benefit of his immunity to Acts of Hubris.
              When he reaches Mastery, then he can start stealing the souls of all but the most wilful.

              Unless of course they can trick their subjects into lowering their Withstand, by putting them in a relaxing situation of trust.
              That, I feel is a better way to go about it, getting them to lower Withstand, rather than ignoring it.
