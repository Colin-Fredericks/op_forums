                                                        It bothers me that you can get infinite willpower and mana. The willpower from when your paradox roll gets a dramatic failure, and mana from getting an exceptional success on a spellcasting roll. The way I see it,you can sit around in your sanctum practicing your praxis, and that somehow restores your mana and willpower over and over and over again. That bothers me because repeatedly casting a praxis is something that's perfectly reasonable from an RP perspective, but quickly leads to complete nonsense from a mechanical perspective. What if the mage has Prime 3? What's stopping him from taking Platonic Form as a praxis, and producing a steady stream of tass water out of nothing?

                                                        I imagine the headlines in all the Consilia: "Apprentice discovers one weird trick Hierarchs don't want you to know about! Break off of the Hallow grid today!"

                                                        Infinite mana tricks annoyed me in Mage 1.0, since it makes no sense that hallows or mana should be valuable if mana is so trivial to produce. I'd like to stamp out infinite mana loops in my Mage 2.0.

                                                        So for possible solutions. Conditions have the whole "one beat per scene" rule to rein them in, but that doesn't quite work here. Resolving a condition takes time, is involved, and most importantly, is interesting. Even if your mage is just handicapping themselves for the purpose of challenging their body, it still produces drama when they're caught in a Seers raid with their pants down.Casting a praxis, on the other hand, is as simple as it gets, and I don't see it producing much drama.

                                                        Say you limit the willpower/mana restoration to once per scene. Even that becomes cheesy. All of a sudden you can't change scenes without giving the players a willpower and mana, and your narrative choices on when to switch scenes now instead become a matter of accounting for all the resources the players are accumulating. "Do I give them a brief scene at the sanctum before we head out? No, that's just yet another opportunity to restore mana and willpower."
