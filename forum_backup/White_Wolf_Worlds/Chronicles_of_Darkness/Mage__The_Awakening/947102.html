    08-09-2016, 02:04 PM
    Hi Guys! Nice to be on the "posting side" of the forum for a change

    I`ll be brief.
    The life 4 Attinument says that any supernatural power that will injure the life mage, will trigger a Clash of Will.

    The Attinuemnt says also that the clash is applicable when the Mage`s body is modified in some way.

    Do you think that any direct damage spell ( like the Arcana 3 and 4 ones ) will trigger this Clash of Will? Or do you think that only magical effect that produce a damage effect, activly modfying the target`s body will be affected by this Attinument ?

                              Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post
