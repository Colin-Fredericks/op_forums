                    I've played or made...
                    Indigo, Acanthus AA, named after the color of her nimbus.
                    Kali, Moros AA, named after the Indian goddess, of course.
                    Jordan, Obrimos AA, Steward of the Celestial Orrery, named after the river, the site of one of Jesus' miracles (his sister was named Cana).
                    Jude, Acanthus AA, House of Ariadne, named after St Jude, the patron saint of the Chicago Police department.
                    Vale, Moros AA, named for the word because of its homophonic meanings.
                    Raze, Mastigos GoV, undercover AA, Fang of Mara, named for his penchant for breaking things, objects, people.

                    And too many NPCs to count, each named for whatever purpose they would serve in the story: mentors, politicians, antagonists, stuff like that. So, a PC was named Phobos, so his NPC Mentor was named Mars, stuff like that.

                        Duke(Arrow Mastigos) is an awfully loud and impolite American military contractor. It's hard to imagine he's been as temperamental and quick to yell his whole life, or he would have surely ruptured an artery by now. It's not clear to most London mages if he named himself after the University (whose mascot would be appropriate for the Mastigos) or if he obnoxiously pretends to royalty, or, some chortle, he named himself after the character in the American children's cartoon, "GI Joe." Those who know him a bit better know he sees the whole world as a battlefield in chaos, in need of someone to start shouting commands; in need of a dux. (You could probably also make Duke a woman, in which case the fact her name isn't "Duchess" makes it even more apparent upon investigation that the name is about the origin of the noble title, and not the noble title itself.)

But I rather strive to "non-warrior" Arrows, as to break stereotypes that all Arrows are paramiliatry guys. For example, we have assasin Moros Arrow Argus Panoptes ( that is PC and exGuardian ), "Doctor Strange wannabe" back alley surgeon Obrimos Arrow Doctor Weird and Thyrsus Arrow modern knight Sir Gawain ( memeber of mine Order of Round Table Legacy, Solitare Sentinel to Consilum ). The Adamant Sage is Hesiesh, Obrimos Tamer of Fire that is veteran of Falklands War. We even have a young Obrimos Arrow Padawan that firmly believe she is Jedi-in-training. ( Jediisim is 7th major religion in UK! )

                            With that I would rather not use military contactor as charcter concept, wanting to break "warrior archetypes" in Arrows - it's one of problems I have with Adamintine Arrow Order at large - it's hard to think character for it that in not direct "warrior" in their Sleeper lives. This is why I loved so much Sorcerer Supreme animated movie, as pointing that neurosurgeon is great Arrow character concept.

                            Now thinking about it maybe I would use Scotland Yard detective that see minds of psychopaths as forest in Sympathy forest that he need to point to, like Professor X with his Cerebro. But still, it's paramilitary organisation, even if character is not so much focused on combat himself. On the other hand, police officer is very much in tune with Visus Urbem general theme of "watchers of London".

                            Hmm, maybe then steal other half of Doctor Strange background and make neurosurgeon Arrow that dwells in to psyche of humans? Or use DaveB's from the DC character Mara as example Arrow - psychologist that try to help Sleepers and Awakened to cope with trauma? But those do not sound as Sentinels ideas...
