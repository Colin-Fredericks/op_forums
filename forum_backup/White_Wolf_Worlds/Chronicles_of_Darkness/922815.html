                          Along with the Departed, Boondock Saints is another possible source of inspiration, being set in Boston, and with a heavy Irish-Catholic cultural influence.

                          With the Red Sox, Bruins, and Celtics, and Patriots, Boston is a sports heavy city, i.e. 90% of the time if you go into a bar, that's what will be playing instead of music. Bostonians hate New York. Publicly supporting the Yankees in particular can get you in bar fights.

                          Reaching out to the regional sense, Boston isn't so much of a Metropolis like NYC or London. Rather, it's more of a central hub to a number of suburban areas. Many people who work in Boston don't live in Boston. They live in Cambridge, in Somerville, in Watertown, in Weymouth, etc. If someone says they are from a town you haven't heard of, there's a good chance they'll follow the name of their town with "It's just outside Boston." Boston proper has plenty of neighborhoods though. Southie (south boston) is particularly considered to be a rough section.

                          People in Boston think that the state ends just west of Worcester (pronounced 'Wuss-ter'), which is half an hour west of Boston. You might want to look into how the names of towns are pronounced if you aren't familiar with the area.

                          Boston is one of the oldest cities in the US, and so appropriately has a lot of ghost stories and local legends. Salem, of the witch trials fame, isn't far, and many events of the early Revolutionary War happened near or in Boston.

                          Hope some of this helps!
