                                        Pardon my lack of writing, Midterms are killing my soul. However, I did get an idea for something else in this game. I've read that in the Bible, Leviathan was associated with the Primodial Sea, whereas Behemoth is an opposite associated with the Primordial Earth. There's also a third associated with the Primodial Sky called "Ziz". The idea I had was that these could be used in making the Siren Backstory. I imagine that with the figures we have (Ceto, Echidna, Leviathan, Behemoth and Ziz), this is the idea I have.

                                        Behemoth is the monster who stirred the earth and shaped it according to his being to be strong and immutable. Ziz was responsible for bringing the law of nature and life onto the land. Leviathan was responsible for the creation of life, and thus brought the waters that were essential to life. I imagine one of the first of these beings born was Ceto, whose duty brought by Ziz was to nurture the world they created and ensure it survived. Ceto did her job, peacefully and calmly without complaint. Behemoth too desired to have something of his own, and with Leviathan's help, he gave life to humanity, this being a literal allegory to Humanity being referred to as "children of the earth". From this, Ceto fell in love with humanity, and had the idea to use Humanity to help her quest as even she had her hands full sooner or later. With an ok from the three, Ceto introduced her duties to the humans who would listen to her plight, and those who did and followed her were granted the ability to survive on both land and Sea. In other words, she brought forth the Sirens. Ziz was happy enough to watch from the heavens, as everything would follow it's laws. Things were overall fine....

                                        Until Behemoth started to get jealous of Leviathan's power. Behemoth alone couldn't create life. He could shape the immutable and turn coal to diamond, and could even strengthen the life on earth because part of his blood went into it....but the power of creation was not his to have. Only Leviathan could wield it. As such, Behemoth started testing his boundaries, turning Humanity and the creatures of the Earth against Leviathan. Since they were acting in Ziz's laws, Ziz did nothing. I don't have any ideas for how any of this was carried out, all I have in mind is that it ended in Leviathan's Death and since one of the fundamental parts of reality that represented water and life died, the Deluge broke out to kill all life in a tidal wave of water. Think of it like Posiedon's Death in God of War 3. The water level rises and floods occur, killing anyone who could not evacuate. I imagine it to also be a time when it was Siren versus Siren too, since some would relate more to Behemoth than the other three.

                                        Anyways, with the Deluge breaking out and Behemoth still not having any of Leviathan's power, knowing the chaos erupting because of it, Ceto decided to go a step further and fill out the missing Leviathan's position her self. Behemoth, still mad with rage turns it onto Ceto. Ziz comes down, feeling that the laws it created were perverted and made useless, and with Ceto constructed a special barrier, created from the crushed hopes and dashed dreams of the lives that lived unfulfilled in Behemoth's recklessness. This barrier is known as "The Song", and it was designed in mind not only to keep Behemoth at bay, but to also send out Ceto's plight for those who have the heart to survive even if all hope seems lost to join her in her quest.

                                        What else can be said? Echidna's story has a few additions. She believes that holding back Behemoth is futile, that the world is doomed no matter what. So she does something different. She decides instead that, if the apocalypse is gonna happen, then it's best to simply wait it out and survive it. Let the barrier fall and let Behemoth drown in the wake of the Deluge. And to do that....she tries to plunder and recreate the dead Leviathan's mutagenic powers, which to an extent is successful. However, she's still pretty mad and like a eugenics scientist, still trying to find what she can do to make the ultimate race to survive. Until then, she at least in part supports the Barrier remaining up, but when she finds the ultimate race, there's no question that she's going to withdraw her support.

                                        I also imagine more than a few game terms could benefit from this backstory. Their fuel stat "Pneuma" is literally "divine breath" and part of a human's soul. In a sense, it could be Ziz's divine laws in immaterial form that gives Sirens strength. And how they get it? From indulging in their love with a certain Human, bathing in a water from where they surfaced in or killing a person, they regain Pneuma because it serves as a reminder that their duties are being rewarded and thus are not in vain. Not to mention that the Siren's Ascended splat literally has to do with the wind/air and as such, could connect more wholesomely to being a part of Ziz's divine laws or upholding them more personally.

                                        And what of Behemoth? If I remember correctly Reaper, your idea was that Echidna spoke to Sirens in Maelstrom and the idea of Floodings and places filled with Resentment had growing animals and the like? As an idea, what if we shifted the Maelstrom chatting to being Behemoth's insane whispers, and the Floodings/Resentment places being tainted with Behemoth's hatred? For the former, Sirens are created from air, sea AND land and thus could get input from Behemoth. As for the latter, the seal that is the Song isn't perfect. There's sometimes a note that's dissonant, which gives a crack that Behemoth's power may slip through.

                                        Sorry for the long post, but what does everyone think of these ideas? It still needs to be fleshed out, but I feel it works with the Siren's backstory in a nutshell. :3
