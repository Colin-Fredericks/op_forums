                        I went into this book expecting it to be Armory 2E. It was not, but I still like most of the things I found in it.

                        The new fighting style merit system is really cool, and I like that there are a lot of alternative style merits in there. The archery styles made using a bow not seem silly, though it is a bit confusing how they work with range bands, and I actually really like the Falconry set. I'm playing a mortal game set in 2030, so the Drone style could actually see use.

                        The concept of Supernatural Merit Templates is really exciting to me, and I will probably have to homebrew a few of my own. I'm a big fan of Videodrome, so I really enjoy The Dreamers, especially Not A Bug But A Feature. I am a bit sad to see The New Flesh with The Infected and Holdout Storage with The Lost Boys for that reason. Some people in my RPG group don't particularly like the range band combat and cinematic ammunition stuff, but I actually prefer it. I'm a little confused over the explosives rules, but that is probably an errata issue rather than a design issue. If Hurt Locker had been out when my game started, I would have probably asked my ST if I could make my character a Lost Boy, since it makes sense with his background as a WWIII veteran.
