        Ok, that does make more sense than Lovecraft writing a positive portrayal of turning into a fishman.

        Anyway, back to the topic at hand. There is definitely support in Leviathan for a "miscegenation-fuelled" horror story. The most obvious would be a story that starts with the PC getting their first dot of Mutations and starting an investigation, ending with the discovery that they're turning into a Leviathan. The Mahhu would also do great, Lahamin's bloodline curse of isolation creates an appropriate mood and scatters the clues far and wide for a nicely challenging investigation.

        But the thing about miscegenation horror, at least in my opinion, is that it works when the protagonist is investigating their own past and by default Hybrids are NPCs. Especially with Leviathan, I could just see the PC Leviathan looking at a Lovecraft deep one and going "is that it, my cursed genetics are far worse than yours". There's definately room for Hybrid/Lahmasu to support miscegenation-horror. But I don't think miscegenation-horror could work as the core of Hybrids.

        And if nothing else. I do think that the setting really does needs thematic equivalents of Elder Things (and Deep Ones, I never said Lahmasu were good); I think that the way Lahmasu can blur the boundaries of mankind and the tribe makes a more interesting setting.

        I want Lahamsu to be able to do miscegenation horror; but I also want them to be able to do more than just miscegenation horror.

                            So 1E Hybrids. Back in 1E there was hard rules about how intelligence and Depth interacted. I consider that to have been a mistake on my part, hence why it's not in 2E.

                            To expand upon that. I don't think the idea of mutations reducing a Hybrids intelligence was a bad idea. I just think giving it hard rules was a bad idea. Firstly those rules didn't allow for exceptions, and secondly it didn't take into account the different themes and concepts of each Hybrid strain.

                            So in 2E Hybrid mutations can still have reduced intelligence, there's just not rules about it. Just create the character with however many dots of Intelligence you think are appropriate.

                            As for the Hybrids with "animistic intelligence" (they were a bit smarter than that actually. On the level of a human feral child) - they're not in 2E yet but they will be. I'm going to represent them with a Genetic Condition. Whether they have Integrity... no idea really. I'll leave that unstated and let the storyteller decide.

                                Sans the fluff text, here's the next Elements Channel.

                                Swallowing the Sun

                                Depth 1: Birthright of Elements
                                Depth 2: The Leviathan may spend one Ichor and make a roll Contested by Resolve + Supernatural advantage to inflict the Blinded Tilt on a single target.

                                Alternatively the Leviathan may spend an Ichor and roll to create the Low Light Environmental Tilt in an area with a diameter of Sheol * 10 yards. Each Success increases the severity of the Tilt by one and the effect lasts for a scene. At this Depth the Leviathan may not darken natural sunlight or any supernatural light source. If a character has fulfilled their virtue in this scene (including the Virtue Merit available to Marduk and Empyerian Thurgists) then they may immunise technology from this Channel with a touch. A torch they hold or any lights they switch on will cut through the Leviathan's darkness.

                                Depth 3: The Leviathan's darkness conceals the hearts of men. If the Leviathan has created or enhanced the Low Light tilt, the penalty also applies to mundane uses of the Empathy Skill. At the Storyteller's discretion supernatural abilities that reveal the hearts of men may also be affected.

                                Depth 4: The Leviathan may spend two Ichor and make a contested roll vs Resolve + Supernatural against a Wake vulnerable target to try and trap them in a pocket realm of pure darkness for a scene. Though physically unharmed they may be emotionally damaged. Roll Resolve + Composure

                                Exceptional Success: The victim comes out their ordeal strengthened. Take the Steadfast Condition; alternatively if it is dramatically appropriate they may emerge as a Hunter, an Ahab, or something else entirely.
                                Success: Take the Shaken Condition, also roll for a Breaking Point with a +2 bonus. Characters who do not have Integrity take the Condition but ignore the Breaking Point.
                                Failure: Take the Broken, Madness or Phobia (of darkness) Condition. Alternatively become Beloved. Also roll for a Breaking Point.
                                Dramatic Failure: As per a Failure, but automatically loose a dot of Integrity if you have it.

                                Alternatively the Leviathan may spend two Ichor to summon a cloud of pure darkness. It covers an area with a radius of three feet per success, less if the Leviathan desires, and no natural sense or technological scan may penetrate into the cloud. Supernatural attempts trigger a Clash of Wills against the Leviathan.

                                Depth 5: The Leviathan may now create darkness even in the presence of natural sunlight or supernatural light sources. The latter may require a Clash of Wills. Like technology, supernatural light sources can also be immunised through virtuous action, but only if the virtuous person is able to operate the light source. In addition the Leviathan's immunity to their own use of this Channel now extends to their Beloved.

                                Depth 6: The Leviathan's ability to create darkness expands to truly prodigious levels. When using the Depth 2 iteration of this Channel to create the Low Light Tilt the affected area becomes that of the Leviathan's Wake.

                                Depth 7: The Leviathan may spend four Ichor to create the Primordial Darkness tilt in an area with a diameter of Sheol * 3 miles. The Leviathan swallows the sun and returns the world to a purer chaotic state that existed before the gods created the world, if the myths of many different cultures are to be believed.

                                Primordial Darkness requires regular darkness to exist, but any source of darkness will suffice. The Tilt lasts one scene.

                                Low Light (Environmental Tilt)

                                Within the affected humans take a penalty of -1 to -5 to all sight based perception. Ranged combat takes an equal penalty which increases by one at medium range and two at long range. Other creatures take a greater or lesser penalty depending on their natural or supernatural abilities for low light.

                                Primordial Darkness (Environmental Tilt)

                                Within the affected area all technology reduces it's equipment bonus by the penalty of the Low Light Tilt. Occult technology is also affected, if the dot rating is equal or lower than the penalty of the Low Light Tilt it ceases to function. Finally all mundane uses of Social Skills other than Intimidation take a penalty equal to the penalty of the Low Light Tilt.

                                Anyone who fulfils their Virtue (including the Virtue merit available to Marduk and Emparian Thurgists) is becomes immune to the Tilt for the reminder of it's duration, and any technology they use also becomes immunised. They must be able to operate occult technology to immunise it.


                                (All that stuff about virtue immunising is mostly there because it would be so much fun to play a Cloverfield like disaster movie story set after a Leviathan unleashes Primordial Darkness and the Virtue rule makes a good reason thematic reason for PCs to be immune. While I won't put crossover mechanics into the corebook. Princess Invocations also immunise. Judge Vestments don't immunise, but it would fit for Judges to get a Verdict that makes them uber effective inside Primordial Darkness. They're god's personal hard man for hard times.)
