    Contagion Chronicles: Global Outbreaks has dropped

    12-15-2022, 10:50 AM
    The Advanced PDF has been released to CC backers. Seven Scenarios: Antarctica's Inner Fire (all), Chicago's Paradox Children (Mage and Changeling), Houston's Prosperity (an Idigam attack on the God-Machine), São Paulo's False Memories (Mummy and Beast), Gotland's Lethargy (Sin-Eaters and Vampires), Sharm El Sheikh's Creeping Decay (Werewolf and Changeling), and Melbourne's Contagion of Impulse (all). As well, the Appendix addresses Dyads, the new general term for the “secondary templates” of the various lines: ghouls, wolf-blooded, Sleepwalkers, Pandorans, fae-touched, ghosts, Sadikh, and stigmatics. (I'm surprised they went with Pandorans and not Alchemists, clones, or at least Sublimati as the Promethean secondary template.)
