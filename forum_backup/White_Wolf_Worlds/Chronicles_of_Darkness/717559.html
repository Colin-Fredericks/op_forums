    Kindred and the CDC

    10-23-2015, 02:29 AM
    I've mentioned before that I'm brewing up a VtR game set in Atlanta. Most of the pieces are in place for the city's infrastructure, and without any character sheets on my plate yet I'm mostly just spinning my wheels and filling in the gaps in the setting.

    One of my earliest hooks was that a sect of the Ordo Dracul are deeply entrenched in the campus of Emory University. It's a medical school, obvious fit in the first place, and there's the even more tantalizing promise of the Center For Disease Control right there, the seat of medical research with tunnels rumored to stretch across to other CDC campus and the city's major hospitals. That said, the nature of the beast means it's going to be heavily guarded. My original idea was to have a kindred embedded down there in some quarantined lab, but that seems simply not feasible given the level of security in the place. What's more likely is that there's some high ranking Dragon who's ghouled some doctors with clearance into the place. Mad science abounds and all that. Were she so inclined, she could visit a plague upon the city and all that jazz. It's unlikely to be a part of town the players would visit any time soon nor be a plot thread they'll pull in the near future, but the thought of a heist on one of the nation's most secure research laboratories is a tantalizing one. I could use some inspiration though. I might not draw on it, but it would be fun to hear about.

    What would you guys do if you had a juicy prospect like the CDC planted square in the middle of your setting?
