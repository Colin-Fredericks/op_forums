          Like, an alternative setting in which the gauntlet never existed? Or one in which it did, but was somehow destroyed? The former would probably look a lot like Pangea, (and I do think a Pangea game could be a lot of fun!). The latter would definitely have some pretty big consequences. For one thing the Azlu would probably be in a hell of a panic, maybe even willing to ally with the Uratha to try to find a way to restore it. There would be sprits all over the place, obviously. A lot of it is difficult to speculate on, because it's a pretty fundamental assumption of the universe to break.

                              It really results in a hell on earth scenario for humans, they would quickly find themselves much further down the food chain than they already are, I wouldn't be surprised to see society break down into a city-state system for the survivors. The Pure would also probably learn quite quickly why you should be careful what you wish for, it would also most likely result in a major change in how they and the Forsaken interact on a regular basis, I see their relationships becoming much less antagonistic (but never openly friendly by human or werewolf standards.)
