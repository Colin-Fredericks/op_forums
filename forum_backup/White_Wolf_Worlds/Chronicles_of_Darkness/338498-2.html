        Next stop- Changeling, and like every good fairy tail, it is full of mysteries.

        What is the Hedge? No one really knows. Unlike most other realities, it doesn't seem to stick to a single place in the multiverse- it seems to defy categorizations, sharing features of many of the levels of reality. Like the Supernal, it has a strange interaction with souls, calling them into it. Like the Abyss, it manipulates time and brings madness. Like the Lower Depths, it lacks a defining element upon which it feeds- creativity, in that case. Like the Underworld, it is guarded by strange laws and mad tyrants. And still, it seems to be an integral part of the Fallen, changing and adapting together with the world. The truth, as much as "truth" could be used in that connection, is that the Hedge is part of EVERYWHERE. It grows from the very bottom of the world, moving through the Lower Depths, sending twisted tendrils into the world of the dead, clawing itself around the Fallen, piercing the Astral, bridging to the Abyss and blooming in the Supernal while feeding from the light of the Empyrean. It may be some defense mechanism of reality made to protect the world from alien intruders. It may be the Tree of Knowledge planted by God in Eden. It may be an alien intruder by itself. No one knows- the only thing that is known is that no one is safe from the Hedge, and it kidnaps lost souls from all over the multiverse.

        There are, however, few defining traits of the Hedge:

        1) it is intelligent- it has some kind of agenda of its own, and it seems to react toward the actions of those who inside it

        2) it collects sins- unlike Inferno, it doesn't feed upon sins as a way to destroy morality. Instead, it encourage them, gardening them. Each plant in the thorny wall is a sin committed by someone, making it into something objectively immoral. That supports the claim of it being the Tree of Knowledge- the Original Sin, after all.

        3) It is independent- it doesn't fall under the rule of the True Fae or the Wyrd. While they may use it (it uses them? maybe some kind of pact or pledge which was made between them?), it is free to do as it will. This is why those who get lost in the Hedge don't become Changeling- you need to find your way up to Arcadia for that. Instead, it would absorb you, using your sins to get a grip upon your heart, until it would turn you into one of its many Hobgoblins (a proof for a pact between the Wyrd and the Hedge shows up in the form of the Huntsmen)

        4) it love us- in a twisted way, of course. It isn't some uncaring force of reality, or some distant God. It also doesn't try to torment humanity for some evil purpose or use it without caring. It is really attached to mankind, and while it makes people to get lost inside of it it doesn't mean to harm them- it is just that, for some reason, it believes to do the right thing... unlike the Gentry.

        Lets speak about the Gentry.

        In order to understand the Keepers, one must first understand the force which motivates them- the Wyrd. The Wyrd was long before the Gentry. It predates Lemuria, Irem, Arcadia and even Atlantis. It is not God- but a force of creativity. It was even before mankind, existing during the times when the Old Ones roamed the land under their scaly skins. Among all things, the Wyrd is closest to the Principle- for just as the Principle was the first Dogma in the world, the Wyrd was the first story to be told. It was born with the first intelligent mind upon the face of Earth, and would die with the last. While it came before humanity, it quickly adjusted itself when mankind rose to rule- their stories were the most interesting ones, filled with comedies and tragedies, with laughter and tears. In a way, it represent the destiny that mankind wishes for- they want to live a meaningful life, where the wicked get punished and the maiden finds her prince charming. The Wyrd is more then happy to fulfill such desires- but there is always a catch. After all, not every story has an happy ending, and the World of Darkness is an horror story, and a good horror story always requires some sacrifices to be made. Some may wonder how a simple story gained life of its own- but for some people, stories ARE living things. The Wyrd, like the Hedge, keeps it mouth shut about the subject. Mystery is always good for creating stories, after all.

        So- if the Wyrd predated Arcadia, why is it connected to True Fae? The answer- Atlantis. When the Exrachs came to the Supernal, they fought against many Old Gods- and just as they didn't invested enough effort in throwing them to the Abyss, they didn't even made sure that every God they defeated was truly dead. Many were just broken, hollow shells of their former self. While most thought they had no place in the world any longer, the Wyrd decided to give them a second chance- after all, the Exrachs seemed to tell a very boring story- and a story about vengeance was a lot more interesting. Besides, while the Wyrd was a story, it needed authors to navigate it, making it more... consistent. More coherent. And so, it offered them a pact- as the true will of mankind, it agreed to allow the Gentry to kidnap humans and manipulate them as tools in exchange for them becoming its storytellers. The Keepers agreed, and the Wyrd moved them to beyond the Hedge, where they were protected from the wrath of the Exrachs- for the thorns of that realm could strip even the new gods from the souls and powers. That was the original pact- to give mankind the story they wished for, in exchange for them becoming the characters in the stories of others.

        While the Wyrd may be powerful, it is not without competition. I am not talking about the God Machine, the Exrachs, Fate or any other of the thousands of mad destinies which try to manipulate mankind. Instead, I'm talking about the Wyrd's "brother"- the Echo. Just as changelings have their Fetches and the True Fae have their Nemesis, the Wyrd has its own counterpart. This power is the force which allows the creation of Fetches in the first place, creating an hollow double which reflects something real. Where the Wyrd is a force of creation, the Echo is the force of mimicry- when the Wyrd creates a single story, the Echo would take it and make thousands of copies out of it for pure profit. When the Wyrd would make a single picture, the Echo would copy it and use it for made in China shirts. When the Wyrd would invent a new patent, the Echo would steal and mass produce it, hiring a crew of lawyers to claim it was to one to think about it. If the Wyrd is the Artist of the World of Darkness, the Echo is the marketing man. While both may be necessary, they don't really like each other. While the Echo is the force behind Fetches, it is not its only "creation"- Goblin Markets and Goblin Vows also fall under its domain, and it is reflected in every advertisement, every mass produced item and every new brand.

        The next part in the Changeling's cosmology is the Skein- the realm of dreams and subconscious, the Skein is the very base for the great mountain called the Astral- it is the boundary between the physical and the spiritual, and contains a tangled web of every dream which is dreamed, was dreamed and will be dreamed. You can think about it as a library, or perhaps an archive of some kind. There are many wonders and terrors in the realm, and many old beings, such as fallen Aeons like of Moira, has found their escape in that place. It is the realm of exiles and tricksters, of gods and demons. Some claim that in the very center of that mad labyrinth one could find an old house, which represent the first dream ever. They say that in that house lives a mysterious woman who can make every dream into reality, and that it is actually the center of the whole reality. As such, she can grant any wish and free transport people and objects from place to place. They also say she always charge a price for her "favors", and while the price is always fair, it isn't cheap either. If that theory is true, she apparently has some connection to the Wyrd- or perhaps its Echo. Still, being the first dream ever, it is possible she is powerful by herself.

        Finally, let's talk about the powers which govern the Courts. Autumn, Winter, Spring, Summer and Solstice. North, South, East, West and Center. Dawn, Day, Dusk Night, Eclipse. Those forces are real- they are neither stories told by the Wyrd, dreams from the Skein, illusions from the Hedge or mockeries of the Echo. They are also not Supernal Symbols or powerful spirits from Hisil. Like Nature, they are real, and they are alive. The Changeling has made pacts with those aspects of reality in order to protect themselves from the Gentry, and they do so by simple being REAL, real, intelligent and powerful.Winter is winter, Dusk is dusk. And this fact, that reality itself is alive without the need for some complicated cosmology to explain it, may be the greatest mystery of all

                    It is time for this thread to rise- and bring the Mummies with it!

                    (ST spoliers alert, if it wasn't obvious)

                    The Cosmology pf Mummy is two folded, and each part is found outside of existence. The first is Duat. That hungry realm is one of the many Fallen Depths, and in many ways it isn't different from the rest of them. It isn't the closest, the furthest, and scariest or the nicest- it is just another realm in Hell. What that does make it different is what it lacks- Memory. Duat is the realm of Oblivion, hungering forever for the history of mankind. Using that defining trait, its original residents has became mighty rulers who can no be bound- after all, their very existence was cast in doubt thanks to the fact they left their very True Name behind, becoming Nameless. The so called Nameless Gods are the mightiest among those beings, and they were formed from the earliest dreams, legends and fears of mankind.

                    Wait- so things can be created in the realm of ultimate consumption?

                    Yes, and it is so natural for it to happen.

                    History fades into myths. Myths die as legends. Legends fall into stories, into riddles, into superstitions, into figures of speech. Eventually they fade out, vanishing into the dead realm of Duat. Yet, Duat doesn't recognize the difference between truth and false- memory is memory, and as long as people believed in it, it tastes all the same. Duat is the realm of lies, and by transforming those lies truths could be made- this is why figures and names are so important for the beings of that realm. They are the only power which connects them into the world, and by controlling those names and telling different stories, the Nameless could be controlled. Only the Gods are protected from that brutal form of control, for they have hidden their names in places no one could find. Still, they need to state the hunger of that realm, and so they make pacts with the Chatonians and the Psychopomps of the Underworld- hence the strong connection between the realms. Sacrifice and names are the key principles of Duat, and by controlling them you could find a way to live forever- you can't destroy a lie who turned into truth, as long as you keep feeding the beast. While Azar and the 5 great Nameless Beasts are the best known Gods of that realm, other Nameless Gods roam it- Nebthet, the Nameeless Flower who dared to rebel against the will of the Shan'iatu and so they stole her Name and banished her forever, Bast, the Nameless Cat who hunt the dark corners of Duat, Hathor, the Nameless Cow who gives nourishment to all until she becomes a great murderer, Hor, the ruler of the skies who gained the blessing of the Judges of Life, Khnosu, the Nameless Nomad who gambles with the lone souls who dare to challenge him, Nit, the Nameless Huntress who fight for any price, Sebek, the Nameless Crocodile who devours the weak and Tauti, the Nameless Monkey and the Scroll of Ages who maintain the arcane secrets of the realm. All of them were born out of lies and half truth who gained life of their own.

                    And what about the Judges? What about the Devourer? What about the Shaniatu?

                    those are another story.

                    First the Judges. During the days of Irem, the Shaniatu searched for a way to ascend to Duat as kings, and part of that project was the creation of false gods to be worshiped by their servants. When Irem fell to Duat, those stories fell with it. Gaining life of their own, they should have been destroyed without anyone to remember them- but someone did. The Deathless, who were risen into worshiping them since childhood and that had their souls bound to the memory of their once living body kept remembering their old faiths and traditions, and from those memories the Judges were born. No longer they were lies told by inhuman tyrants- now, they could change the world by controlling the flow of Sekhem and using their Deathless servants. They by themselves do not know what happened to the Shaniatu- after all, the original plan was making their creators into gods and rulers over Duat as the Judges of Death- but if that were to work, the Judges wouldn't have been created. Still, the Judges don't invest too much thought to that subject. They have bigger problems to handle.

                    Like Ammut, Oh, great and hungry Ammut.

                    Ammut is not another Nameless being clinging to existence in Duat. No- she is Duat. Just like the God Machine is a great Infrastructure and Gaea is the Shadow, Ammut is the manifestation of the realm itself. She is hungry- always hungry- and the only things which satisfied her hunger for even a bit is the pure existence of the world. While the Nameless and the Judges try to feed her dead things, she grew tired of eating junk food. She wants some real food- something fresh, filled with newborn life. This is why she keeps Shuankshen in hand- and this is why she taught her secrets to the Shaniatu. She wanted them to feed her, but she can't stop eating Irem as long as it exist. Only once every Arisen, every relic and every record of the Nameless Empire would be erased, she could move to her next course- but it doesn't mean she can't have a snack from time to time.

                    Well, that's all for the below.

                    Let's talk about the Above.

                    The Supernal is not the edge of existence. There are things above it- like the Empyrean. Yet, the Empyrean is too powerful for living things to exist on it- but some souls find their way close enough to the light without getting burned. That empty void between the Empyrean and the Supernal is called the Sidereal Sea- many little realms of shining light who burn to banish the darkness. One of those realms is called Aaru- the shining fields. That is the home for the mythical Judges of Life, the ones to birth the Shaniatu in order to guide humanity into Judgement. When their children defected from their mission, they were disappointed- yet didn't interacted. In order to be so close to the Empyrean, one must not allow herself making too much contact with the Fallen world, and creating the Shaniatu in the first place already took a lot of effort. They still watch the creations of their children, and they still judge the souls of the worthy and allow them into the blessed fields. They have their time- after all, Fate is by their side.

                    And that is because Fate is written in the stars.

                    Fate is the manifestation of the Sidereal Sea. It is the original plan of creation made by God before he left, and that plan keeps on working. It doesn't have angels or messengers of its own- instead, it is using the creations of the Shaniatu as its own. While it has some connection to the Arisen, the one with the Deceived is a lot more powerful thanks to their Temakh, although once the connection is strong enough they could allow the mummy to Ascent, making it a true agent of Fate all by her own. Like Duat, Fate and the Aaru also work using True Names- but were Duat use them as shards of memory, the Judges of Life do so by establishing connection with the original plans of Fate. Yet, since the meddling of the Shaniatu, Fate has started to make mistakes- the new star in the skies made by the ascent of Azar wasn't part of the plan, and it destroyed many of its calculations. Yes, the Shaniatu really messed things up.

                    Luckily, there is a backup plan.

                    Irem wasn't the only early civilization in the world born by the Judges. The Shaniatu had brothers and sisters all around the world, each creating a potential Empire. All of them has fallen, creating the base for the great wonders of mankind- and each has left a different legacy. Some created things which resemble the Arisen, but others has birthed other kinds of monsters and gods. While the list of endless civilizations may be endless, the places include Greece, Mesopotamia, Iran, Mesoamerica, Peru, China, India, Central Africa, the Sahel, North America, Scandinavia, the British Islands and even Australia. Some cultures were created before or after Irem, others fought against the Empire and either lost and were destroyed or were strong enough to withstand its wrath. Those who discover that believe that the Judges of Life have hidden other "Shaniatu" in the world, who are waiting to guide humanity to a better future. Some whisper that Lemuria was one of those primordial kingdoms, and that the God Machine is part of Fate's plan- maybe the part created thanks to the new star the Shaniatu put in the skies. What ever the truth may be, those ancient wonders and horrors are still around, waiting to reveal themselves to any unfortunate stranger.

                    Duat? check. Aaru? Check. What's left?

                    Human Sorcerers.

                    Sorcerers are those rare individuals who has learned how to transform reality thanks to the power of True Names. There are two paths for that type of sorcery- one use Duat to power their rituals, using shards of memory and broken images in order to manipulate the world and turn lies into reality. The other makes contact with Fate and by seeing into the great archive of potential plans and divine guidance they can force their will upon the world. The source for the Shaniatu's power was that they used both paths of that ancient sorcery in order to strength their rituals- they looked into God's plan and spat in his eye by making their own lies into reality. Only the greatest sorcerers can achieve such degree of power, for even many of the "Shaniatu" couldn't achieve such feat. Most walk only in one path- and believe me, walking in a single path is more then enough to make all of your dreams come true..

                    and your nightmares too.

                            well, if you are asking so nicely...

                            vampires- where do they come from and where do they go?

                            this is not an easy question to answer- the reason is that each Covenant, Clan and Bloodline tells a different story about their mythological origin- they are warriors who sacrificed their heart and shadow to vanquish an ancient king. They are the former priests of an ancient goddess who betrayed their mistress and were punished. They are the children of the Mother of Monsters. They are characters from an old children story. They are the creation of Death itself in the form of a golden eyed bird. They are simply dead people who couldn't stay dead. And all of that weirdness and contradicting stories come long before someone notice the more.. exotic forms of vamprisim. The Wicked Dead. Revenants. Dead bodies claimed by hungry ghosts. It is no wonder that sometimes, it looks like each Clan is a unique being, with their only similarity is the fact that they are undead monsters who drink the blood of the living. And there is some truth to that.

                            Some, but not all.

                            It all starts with the start- in the early days of our time, when the Exrachs scribed their name upon the heavens. While the Oracles were united in their opposition against the Kings of the Lie, some opinions were seen as a bit too... extreme. Father was such an example, although he still wasn't banished from the Supernal. Hecate was another, yet she already chose to live in the Abyss instead. Instead, we are talking about someone else. Her name was Lilith- or as she is more commonly known, the Blood.

                            While the Oracles has established the Watchtowers as their vision of a perfect world, so did Lilith planned to create her own structure- only that her vision was one of darkness instead of light. Her version of the truth was so radically different then most of the other Oracles (with the exception of Father), that they made a plan to destroy her- they forged a sword out of a shard of the symbol of the Sun itself and took the form of great Dragons to fill themselves with power against the parasitic death goddess. Yet, Lilith wasn't an easy prey- like the rest of the Oracles, she was a direct descendant of the kings from the time before time, and the Blood of the Dragon burned in her vines, taking the form of a giant, pitch black skeletal dragon. Yet, the five working together were stronger then she was, and they drove the sword into her heart and destroyed her very being. That act has made Father outargues, for his vision supported the unity of all other Oracles. The act has drove him crazy and forced his banishment into the Abyss. Surely, that was meant to be the end of the story.

                            Only that Lilith woke up.

                            Finding herself in a dark place, she opened her eyes and looked around. Her blood flew into the small cracks of the dark ground, taking the form of smoke and shadows. All around her were the bodies- thousands of dead bodies and graves of beings both beautiful and horrible. The bodies of the Old Gods who were killed during the great war. As she wondered how did she came to that place, she noticed that her blood has slithered into the darkness and took shape- the form of golden eyes, black owls. She asked them what this place was, and they answered it was Dis, the Graveyard of the Gods- the place were the divinities who are buried properly come into. Apparently, one good thing was done by her siblings- they didn't just threw her into the Underworld like the Exrachs did, fearing of her eventual return. When she asked where Dis was, they birds told her it was one of the Lower Worlds, a silent prison made for the souls of the gods so they may rest in peace and never awake. The land would drain their essence slowly, turning it into more shadows, like them. She asked how she could get out of the prison, and they told her she couldn't for her living essence was taken from her as she fell into that realm, and without Life to define her she was a meaningless shadow as they were.

                            She asked that if she was meant to die here, why was she awake.

                            They told her they didn't knew.

                            Eventually, she revealed the reason- Dis was the realm without Life, and none of the original Gods who fell into there were alive in the first place. Sure, they were active, and some may have even had symbols of life inside of their being, but none of them truly lived. Lilith, being originally a mage- and so, a mortal- knew what it meant to be alive, and her desperate essence drank the blood of the dead in order to awaken her. While her being a former mortal was what allowed her to stay active, her being a mage gave her another benefit- the ability to use magic. She spent her time strengthening her skills and adapting to her new state, sustaining on the dead deities and offering her own blood to the shadow birds- whom she named as the strix. She planned on using a great Death and Space magic in order to break the boundaries of her prison and claim the Fallen World as her own. All she needed to do was to wait- and she had her time. Eventually, enough gods would die, and she would grow in power with each dead divinity. It may take millions of years, but she had her time. She was dead already, after all.

                            The help of the Chthonians was an unexpected bonus.

                            As the Dead Gods used their authority upon creation to invite the Lower Depths, Lilith heard the invitation and quickly acted. While she, not being an original dweller of the Depths, wasn't included in the invitation, the strixs could escape- and leave her behind. Using a powerful Space magic combined with her new found Blood Sorcery, she bound the strixs and closed the gates of Dis. Before the angry Owls could turn upon her, she offered them a pact- she would allow them to leave, and in exchange they would feed her with some of the life essence they gather and serve her as informants and messengers. The strixs, eager to free themselves from the graveyard and feast upon the living, agreed- and this was the start of the future Coalition of Blood.

                            Apparently, the strixs had a special effect upon the world- they could revive the dead bodies they entered into and give them a semblance of Life by becoming a small Beast which sleeps in their hearts. While only the greatest among the strixs could do it, and the act required using some of the blood of the dead gods which is found in Dis, many dark beings has sought that power- parasitic gods who wished to strength themselves by using servants and pawns. Also, from time to time, one of the dead divinities has awakened thanks to the presence of Lilith and the open gate, and wishing to live again he searched for making a pact with the strixs. The only problem was that because of Lilith's bindings the strixs couldn't make give false life to the dead without her direct permission- meaning, Lilith has monopolized Vamprisim.

                            After long negotiation, Lilith has gathered the most like minded individuals among the parasite gods and forced a pact with them- she would allow them to use the gate of Dis to make vampires, and in exchange they would work for her. While some refused to cooperate with her (especially the other dead gods who saw the strixs as part of their very essence), eventually most of them has agreed. Has time passed, Lilith was more loose with allowing the creation of new vampires, allowing any divinity to cast the curse upon a mortal- in exchange for a price- as long as no one would try to lift her curse. Trying to do so would result with the whole Coalition attacking the betrayer. She also decided that while the membership among the Coalition is optional, anyone who wish to make more then a single vampire must become a part of it- no one may start a new Clan without her direct permission. Finally, she elected five among the Coalition to serve as the formal leaders of the corrupted organization- those five would make the five Greatest Clans who spread upon the Earth, She decided that each such Emperor of the Night would be a dark mirror for the siblings who killed her- five Thrones to cast shadows against the light of the Watchtowers.

                            The five Thrones are the Throne of Bloody Crown against the Golden Key, the Throne of the Dried Rose against the Lunargent Torn, The Thorn of the Obsidian Claw against the Singing Stone, the Throne of the Empty Heart against the Lead Coin and the Throne of the Rusted Mirror against the Iron Gauntlet. While other Clans do exist, and some of their patrons name themselves as "Lesser Thrones", only five true Thrones exist at a certain time- this is, after all, a political position decided by Lilith. This also means that is a current Throne fails the Mother of Owls she may withdraw her gift, destroying the Throne's Clan and giving its position to another. This has happened a numerous times before- the Mekhet devoured the Grettir. The Julii fell and their place was taken by the Ventrue. The Xotol were destroyed when the Nosferatu rose to greatness. The Throne to switch Emperors the most is the Bloody Crown apparently, everyone wants to be part of the Clan of Kings. Sometimes, a fallen Clan finds a way to survive inside the rising star as a Bloodline- a sign that Lilith may give a second chance to the Patron. Sometimes, the Clan is destroyed utterly- and it usually includes the destruction of the Patron- unless they escape the Opposition.

                            Before that, let's name the current Thrones-
                            The Current Emperor of the Bloody Crown is an Empress- Venus, or more exactly, Aphrodite. She was killed by the goddess who would be known as Venus and her Essence was devoured by the parasitic divinity, and after she was awakened in Dis she decided to do the same for her killer- and under Lilith's blessing, devoured her opponent and birthed the Ventrue . Another Empress rules the Throne of the Dried Rose- this is Inanna, the first Sin Eater. Being afraid of her sister's furry, she made a pact with the strixs and Lilith to come to their Underworld instead of her Sister's after her death, and using her divine status among the mortals she created a great death cult that eventually birth the Daeva- and now, she dreams about overthrowing Lilith herself and making her into the ruler of the Coalition. Echidna is the mother of the Gangrel and the Empress of the Obsidian Claw- she gained her rank out of hard work and surviving were other failed, and she is one of the most honest members of the Coalition. The Emperor of the Empty Heart is Set- or Sutek, the Nameless Serpent. One of the Nameless Gods of Duat, he sacrificed his place in one Underworld and joined the other in order to defeat an invasion of monsters from a dark dimension upon Egypt, turning his followers into the Mekhet- and by doing so, creating an alliance between the Nameless Gods and Dis. Finally, the Emperor of the Rusted Mirror is Zmei, the Great Worm below the Earth who created the Nosferatu and is motivated by simple hunger and hate against the divinities who banished it from the light of the sun out of disgust from its shape. For now, Lilith is quite happy with the current Thrones, although she knows about the plans of Inanna and Set's lack of satisfactions. Maybe Yama and his Jiang Shi and Cybele and her Formosae could be good replacements for those two.

                            Talking about replacements, let's talk about the Opposition- the Seven Opposition.

                            Not all divinities were happy with the leadership of Lilith. Some wants to replace her. Some want to destroy her monopoly and bring Capitalism into Vamprisim. Some were thrown from the Coalition by Lilith and seek after vengence. Few even just want to vanquish all vampires from the face of Earth, and so they use fire against fire. Those powerful beings have created a group of their own, dedicated for the destruction of the Coalition as a common interest. The group was founded by Minerva- a member of the Roman Pantheon. Because of the parasitic nature of her Pantheon, Jupiter has decided to make pact with Lilith to strength their Empire and rule the Night as they ruled the Day. Minerva, seeing that nothing good would turn out of working with the dark deity, objected- and so she was struck down by the King of the Gods and was sent to the graveyard. Thanks to her many followers, she awakened and mutated her blood so that her Owls would be freed from Lilith's rule- granting her servants eyes in the color of red embers instead of gold. Refusing to rise her own Clan, she made herself into Lilith's antithesis, granting her blood sorceries to mortals so they could fight against the darkness. She used her strixs (or "tengu" as they are sometimes called) to offer an alternative for Lilith and gathering her own allies. While there are quite a few members in the Opposition (who are still less then the Coalition), there are Seven great members who has made vampires of their own- Shaddad, a renegade Shaniatu from the time before Irem who has made its own mockery for the Nameless Empire and made the Akhud, The Seven Ghosts, spirits of the murdered princess of the Betrayed, Pazuzu, a wind demon from Irkalla and messenger of Ereshkigal who still hopes to strike Inanna down and created both the Edimmu and the Itani, Mars, who left his pantheon out of anger after the destruction of his Julii by Lilith, Napi, the maker of the Sta Au who wish to break the curse of vamprisim and guide the lost souls for redemption and Supay, who birthed the lost Clan of Princes of South and Central America which was eradicated by Lilith for "breaking her rule"

                            And that more or less sums it- the Coalition, the Opposition and Dis. The only thing left is Blood Sorcery. Blood Sorcery works because of the divine nature of the vampire's soul- they carry the blood of the dead gods inside them, and through it they can make contact with the living ones. There are three major types of blood sorcery- Theban Sorcery, who makes contact with the "civilized gods", like Christian Angels and Saints of Egyptian Nameless Gods. On the other, there is Curac which requires calling upon the name of the "wild divinities", like the Celtic and Germanic pantheons. Finally, there is Veneficia, the magic which requires perfect balance between the man and the beast and calls upon the "masked gods" of the empires- like the Roman or the Incan gods, although they could also work with more modern principles. The other Blood Sorceries work by making a more.. specific contact with the gods, instead of just touching the divine through the dual aspects of the Man and the Beast. Ahranite Sorcery, for example, allows Shaddad to rest in the soul of each vampire who use its magic. Lithopedia makes contact with the spirits of the elements, while the Gilded Cage works with the spirits of the cities. Vodoun works by calling upon the Loa- mysterious spirits of the Underworld who resemble Geists in many ways. Merges Sorcery seeks after the blessings of Pazuzu and his demons. Other blood magics may also be found- and each allows the vampire to being his divine self into existence.

                            Finally, few words about Father. After searching for eternity in the Abyss, he knew he couldn't make his dreams come true without the help of Lilith- yet she wasn't willing to help him. He was too mad, she told him. His touch would destroy vampires. Yet, he tried- creating a dark infestation which used the vampires as a gate to the Abyss. While that attempt is, more or less, a failure, another one was kinda successful. Mages, drawn by their hunger for knowledge and immortality, has made contact with a strong vampire who wished to see the Heavens, and using Minerva's support he helped them turning into the Breath Drinkers. Using that opportunity, he tricked the vampire who called himself Theban into sending his Breath Drinkers against a few powerful mages who were close to Archmastery- and after the fight revealed himself before them. Now, he had mages who were also vampires- eaters of souls who could unite the other Watchtowers into a single vision...

                            Neither Lilith or Minerva has forgiven his betrayal.
