    [Setting Hack] Domains of the Supernatural

    05-04-2020, 02:37 AM
    In the CofD setting the supernatural can potentially be around any corner, under any roof. But what if that wasn't true? What if the supernatural had a far more limited amount of territory? Let's explore this scenario. Fan setting hack inspired by the Agent of Hel trilogy.

    In this scenario the supernatural can only truly appear, or even survive in the case of supernatural creatures, in two types of places.
    One: archipelagos of small islands with a bloody enough history. Like the islands of the Caribbean.
    Two: towns & cities with supernatural patrons.
    We're going to focus on the second type of place. Let's look at how such a place starts & some essential players.

    ***

    A powerful supernatural creature (the domain's patron), too powerful to measured by any kind of mechanics or numbers, decides it wants its own domain. Where it comes from is not important & it could be argued that creatures of such power do not need a domain to live in in order to survive.

    The creature could be a mighty spirit noble, a kerberoi, one of the Judges, a protogenoi, or something else entirely, the only thing that really limits the decision is the need for it to be at a certain level of power. Let's say that this creature is Sagrim-Ur, Red Wolf.

    Red looks for a good town or city to set up his new domain & decides upon a small, dying city in Midwestern USA. As he travels towards the city he builds for himself a "Lair" which he will attach to the domain & rule from. This Lair should be appropriate for whatever creature the domain's patron is, in Red Wolf's case it should have technological feel. When he arrives at the future domain, he sets his Lair aside for the moment & proceeds to "define the limits of the domain". These limits can be smaller than the town/city limits, they can also be a little larger but only by like half a kilometre. Red Wolf must exert a measure of power to succeed at this, temporarily weakening him.

    Now that the limits of the domain have been defined the Lair must be bound to some place in the domain & have a door or way to go between the Lair & the domain as a whole. There are no real limits to the location that the Lair is bound to, there should be some logic to it. Red Wolf has chosen to bind his Lair to an abandoned junkyard & way to reach his Lair is to place your hand on one of the wolf silhouettes found around the junkyard, close your eyes, & wait for a minute thinking of wolves. Should you follow these instructions you will open your eyes to see the Lair of Red Wolf. Other potential doorways are to walk between two specific trees at the right angle or to shut yourself in a mausoleum after sundown.

    Now that the domain has been officially established other, less powerful supernaturals start to arrive. Where do they come from? Other domains & those blood-soaked islands. Some of these will be looking to be of a higher rank among their own people than they were back where they came from. Some will be running from enemies they have made back home. Some will just be looking for a new place to live, not really caring how its new. Some, particularly the average Promethean, will be "domain-hoppers", spending their lives bouncing from one domain to the next, never really stopping lomg enough to call any of them home. There are potentially other reasons behind the arrivals of these new supernaturals, but these are the most common ones.

    Most of the arriving supernaturals start to figure out whose got what in the new domain. A group of Nosferatu descend into a poorly upkept section of the sewage system & declares it to be their Necropolis. Some of them (here a Geryo & an idigam) plant themselves somewhere in the domain & say that this is theirs & theirs alone. No one's going to argue with them, because when you've got the guts to say that to every other supernatural in the domain, you've got the power to back up your words, to defend your claim.

    One of the arriving supernaturals is here to challenge Red Wolf. It's one of the secondborn, the various children of Red Wolf & his siblings. They have decided that they want to own this domain, they've got power to do so, & Red Wolf is still not yet recovered from defining the limits of the domain. Unfortunately for the secondborn, Red Wolf while weakened is still a crafty & strong opponent. The secondborn finds themself losing & runs for the hills to lick its wounds.

    Meanwhile, this now being a domain means that the barriers between the various worlds have gotten thinner. It is possible for places like the Shadow & the Underworld to be accessed here.

    Ordinary people wake up to find they have awoken that they now have some supernatural element to themselves. Certain creatures (minor templates) are not killed by not being in a domain, but rather the supernatural element to them is rendered dormant, unable to affect anything at all.

    Red Wolf starts to look for an agent, someone who he can bless to speak & act with his authority behind them. He starts with the Uratha that have arrived, but finds no one he considers suitable, which is very annoying. Casting his gaze further afield he finds an Otodo in their last year of high school. Acknowledging that the Otodo's current schooling would prove to be an impediment to being his agent, Red Wolf decides to hold off until graduation.
    Shortly after they graduate, Red Wolf will approach them, make them his agent (somehow) branding them with his "mark" which will light up whenever they are "on the job" & can easily be seen by anyone of a supernatural persuasion. The job of the agent is to make sure any laws the patron declares, are followed & enforced. Red Wolf says that the first law is the Tribal Oath of Iron Masters.

    Red Wolf, by dint of this being his domain, will be the Apex of the domain's Begotten, complete with Lair Trait.

    Various Compacts, Conspiracies, & other groups of hunters will set up shop here. Either to destroy the domain, thus ridding the world of "another den of wickedness & evil", or to police it by whatever means they can.

    Finally, remembering that this was (& maybe still is) a dying city before Red Wolf chose it as his domain, the supernaturals question between themselves how they will breath life into this place. They decide to the domain into a bit of a tourist trap, with the focus of the tourism being all the supernatural creatures living here, though they make sure (well, try to) everyone is all pretty, shoving the darker aspects of their natures away. Someone also starts to get technological stuff being made here as a secondary source of wealth.

          Things I just realized I might need to mention about this setting.
          You don't need to use this particular domain to set your games in, a different domain, a different patron could change a lot about the stories that get told. Even if you do use this domain it would be a good idea to have some ideas for a few other domains.
          Your game could be one focusing on a group of domain-hoppers.
          You could have a game looking at the fallout of what happens when the domain's patron dies or otherwise leaves, & no one is strong enough to fill the patron's shoes. Everyone supernatural would naturally have to evac to other domains before they died.
          You could set your game on the blood-soaked islands. The thing with these islands is that no patron is needed for them to be healthy for the supernatural. It's something to do with how much blood per plot of land, & only small islands with bloody enough history have that. I used the Caribbean islands as an example because they feel like they'd fit really well, what with the slavery among other things.
          Finally, how does the patron's agent make money? I suppose they could be paid for their work as the patron's agent, but that seems unlikely to me & I think there should be some sort of day job. To help avoid it getting in the way of the work as the patron's agent, say that the patron themself got the day job for the agent.
