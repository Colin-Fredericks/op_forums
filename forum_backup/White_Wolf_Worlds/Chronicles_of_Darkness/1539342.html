    The Polaris League, an Acanthus Legacy of ambitious businessmen

    07-26-2024, 05:59 PM
    Origins

    “When your ambition is greater than your fear, your life will get bigger than your dream”
    - Farshad Asl

    Nicknames: Highfliers
    Parentage: Acanthus, Free Council, Silver Ladder, Seers of the Throne

    Background: The early years of "La Serenissima" Republic of Venice were suffused with potential for the ambitious. Within its political system, successful traders could climb to the highest strata of society, earning seats in the Grand Council, creating a system of rule by a merchant nobility.

    Emboldened by the thriving Republic, a cabal of merchants shaped their souls to epitomize the prosperity of the city, committing to expanding its trade further than ever before and promoting talented prospects to the Grand Council.

    Some stories say they would draw Goblin Markets to the city, aiming to transform it into a true nexus of commerce. Rumor has it that those gateways still lie all over the city, with their lost Sanctums around the world hiding records of their locations, as well as the secret Trods they used as trade routes.

    Another tale speaks of the founders bartering the secrets of mercantile success with the True Fae, using them to sculpt their Gnosis. Legend has it that some deeds from that time still remain, containing secret accords that have yet to be fully negotiated. Proponents of this theory often point to the similarities between the abilities of the highfliers and those of Baron Fairweather.

    In time their efforts would pay off, with the Grand Council swelling with talented merchant families and a new guild of traders establishing themselves in the city.

    This period of expansion would end in 1297, when the Serrata established that seats in the Grand Council would only be inherited. Seeing this as a betrayal of the core pillars of the Republic, the highfliers would reorganize themselves into their own independent mercantile empire. When the Venetian Republic was dissolved in 1797, they had already expanded into new markets, from corporations to organized crime, already paving the way for a new era of business.

    Appearances: Ambition runs in the highfliers’ veins, with mentors often choosing pupils with a drive to prove themselves and reach the highest levels of prestige by their own effort. Initiates normally run businesses that have achieved a modicum of success, or aspire to build them.

    The highfliers usually dress to convey professionalism, staunchly believing that in order to achieve excellence in business, one must look respectable. Whether it's a crisp, neatly pressed shirt or freshly polished shoes, they endeavor to look sharp. As their prosperity grows, so does their wardrobe, with many investing in fashionable clothes and luxury accessories. Elegance is key in their attires, with gaudy choices being frowned upon. Clothes and accessories created by supernatural masters, such as hedgespun suits, are held in especially high regard.

    Great variety exists on the types of business the highfliers operate, from hotels, to criminal syndicates, banks, tech corporations and more. Common to all of them is a culture of directed and streamlined operations, maximizing efficiency and minimizing costs. Their teams of employees are often minimal, but carefully selected, well trained and trustworthy. Should further manpower be necessary, it is outsourced from other organizations.

    Their Sanctums normally act as their offices, being carefully planned to portray a facade of normalcy, while also having private, and often secret, areas to conduct Awakened business. Their employees usually assist in both their mundane and occult business. Enterprising Sleepwalkers are often drawn to their employ, offering their services in exchange for a steady paycheck and benefiting from the highflier's Attainments.

    When attending formal events of the Legacy or Consilium, members wear pendants, rings, lapel pins and other accessories with the League's symbol: a stylized depiction of the Polaris star. Aquamarine and sapphire are often the materials of choice, paying homage to their past as seafaring merchants.

    Doctrine

    “Success means having the courage, the determination, and the will to become the person you believe you were meant to be.”
    - George Sheehan

    Prerequisites: Mind 2, Persuasion 2, Staff •, Resources •.

    Initiation: The prospective member is given a mundane item of low value, which they must exchange for something else, starting a series of trades that will end when they either obtain a valuable supernatural item or secure a supernatural business deal.

    The initiate is not allowed to use their business or wealth in this process, they must also abide by the terms of each trade and deliver the expected level of quality.

    It is common for mentors to decide on an item by summoning a Supernal Entity of commerce, consulting on the best choice to start the chain of trades. Some instead choose to read the threads of Fate, choosing the right item to set their potential pupil on the road towards a particular item or deal.

    Completing this challenge corroborates the initiate's ability to become successful by their own hand. With a new perspective on opportunity and holding the fruits of their labor, the new highflier is now poised to make their business soar.

    Organization: Since their webs of connections reach multiple strata of society, it is not uncommon for highfliers to run into each other on the field. While these interactions may yield anything from negotiation, cooperation, rivalry or even antagonism, they are always guided by a strong undercurrent of respect, an implicit recognition of the relentless effort each has invested towards their ambitions. In some cases, their interest may align enough to establish a partnership, with members pooling their resources towards a common goal.

    Mentors and pupils often keep close ties, exchanging information and providing assistance in the pursuit of each other's agendas.

    In Caucuses where the League has a significant presence, it traditionally holds an annual event known as Il Stellarium. Hosting a carefully vetoed selection of influential mundane and supernatural guests, the highfliers create opportunities to forge gainful new alliances.

    Whereas these events are known for their extravagance amongst the Pentacle, their Seer equivalents tend to favor decadence, often weaving Exarchial rituals and symbolism into the festivities.

    Theory: The course of the future is charted by those who have reached greatness, by visionaries whose influence send ripples throughout society.

    Influence is an exceptionally valuable, but highly volatile commodity. It must be carefully managed and wisely invested in order to pay dividends. With a combination of business savvy and political astuteness, they can cultivate their clout and secure all the funding they may need.

    Similarly to how a merchant builds supply lines and trade routes, a sagacious visionary creates a vast network of contacts, manufacturers, purveyors, informants and more. Conducting business reliably and professionally, they attract skilled individuals into the fold.

    Thus when the opportunity arrives that their ambition lies within their grasp, they have all they need to make the most of it.

    Build a thriving business empire, wield vast resources and realize your vision, because true greatness can only be seized by one's own merit.


    Magic

    "Don't sit down and wait for the opportunities to come. Get up and make them."
    - Audrey Hepburn

    Ruling Arcanum: Mind
    Yantras: Sigils and corporate logos (+1), consulting Contacts, Allies or using Persuasion to gain information related to the spell (+1, +2 if rolled), depictions of the League's symbol (+1, +2 if made of sapphire or aquamarine) fashionable clothes and accessories (half the availability rating, rounded down), currency (+1), ledger (+1, +2 if containing information relevant to the spell), exclusive clubs or locations (+2), market, bazaar, auction house or similar trading hubs (+2).

    Oblations: Negotiating a business deal, organizing an auction, directing a business, hosting or attending a private or elite event, researching the state of the market, toasting a successful venture, mentoring someone on business.

    Attainments

    The Terms of Service: The highfliers uphold a code of conduct designed to promote prosperous trades, drawing power from their commitment to it. Its rules are woven as conditional durations into many of their Attainments and are as follows:

    A highflier must deliver quality goods and services whenever they willingly make a deal. They are allowed to veto anything that could harm a client in its current state, as well as any items that could endanger reality.

    Different Terms of Service apply for all other subjects, requiring them to deal in good faith with the highflier, being honest and just. Such an arrangement is mutually beneficial, providing the highflier with reliable suppliers, manufacturers and allies, whereas the subjects benefit from their Attainments.

    First: An Eye for Talent
    Prerequisites: Initiation

    The highflier focuses their keen business sense, allowing them to read the wants, talents and even the current disposition of those nearby. With these insights, new business partners can be uncovered and negotiation strategies refined.

    This replicates Mental Scan, with Reach assigned to instant use.

    Optional: Fate 1

    A skilled businessman must be like the north star, a steady source of guidance amidst the most turbulent seas. The highflier senses the opportunities stretching out before them, expertly choosing the one best suited to their skillset. This ability has created a reputation for the highfliers being excellent advisors, utilizing their savvy to provide counsel tailor made to their allies.

    This replicates Serendipity with Reach assigned to instant use and conjunctional Mind allowing the mage to choose a given skill.

    Second: All Access Pass
    Prerequisites: Mind 2, Persuasion 3

    A highflier and their allies are often found in the most prestigious social events, from charity galas, to major concerts and even a mafia don's private party, they always seem to be welcome and can easily arrange for others to be as well.

    After a scene of preparing themselves or someone else for a major social event (discussing the key figures attending, choosing an outfit, etc), the highflier is able to grant a subject with enough clout and smooth mannerisms to open new doors in society. They are easily able to procure an open invitation to any social function, event or mundane society they wish. When attending one, they intuitively know how to blend in seamlessly, with magic smoothing over any slip ups. Any attempt to discover they don't belong provokes a Clash of Wills.

    This is a custom, duration primary, Veiling of Mind, with Reach assigned to advanced duration.

    Optional: Fate 2

    A well conceived venture must often draw from a diverse pool of skills, hiring freelancers to provide support at different points. The highfliers are well versed in procuring their services, often being able to hire them at excellent prices.

    After a scene of reviewing the current action plan of the subject, analyzing all the professionals they will need to hire and spending a point of mana, circumstances align to help them realize it. When procuring services, events facilitate the negotiation of the contractor's rate, reducing the Availability cost by [lowest of Fate and Mind], up to a minimum of zero. Because of this, some highfliers are seen as having a vast network of connections, who discretely favor their business partners.

    This is a custom, potency primary Ruling of Fate and Mind, with Reach assigned to advanced duration and a point of mana is used to include the Terms of Service.

    Third: The VIP Treatment
    Prerequisites: Mind 3, Resources ••

    The highflier's influence is far reaching, allowing them to invest themselves or those they favor with authority. This grants [Mind] dots in phantom Status in a mundane organization or group of the highflier's choice. No one questions what right they take charge or what gives them permission to access a restricted location.

    This is a custom, Potency primary, Veiling of Mind, with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration.

    Optional: Fate 3

    Shakespeare once said “fortune brings in some boats that are not steered”, nothing could be further from the truth for the highfliers. When opportunity stands before them, they know how to take the helm of their own fates, sailing themselves and those they favor towards success.

    This replicates Exceptional Luck with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration.

    Fourth: Trailblazer Network
    Prerequisites: Mind 4, Persuasion 4, Resources •••

    Networking is an indispensable component in business, and the highfliers’ mastery of it is second to none. Members of their inner circle always seem well informed, expertly coordinated and able to draw upon a cornucopia of talented individuals.

    After a scene sharing a meal with a subject, or a light repast (such as afternoon tea or meeting at a good café), the highflier is able to add them to their private psychic network.

    It is customary for the highflier to pay for the entire meal, honoring the Venetian tradition of sharing a meal as a way to foster trust between business partners.

    This replicates Telepathy with Reach assigned to advanced duration, sending only intended messages and allowing free flow of information.

    With conjunctional Fate 2, the Terms of Service are automatically applied, with a point of mana being used to pay for conditional duration.

    Optional: Fate 4

    Whether it's favors from friends in high places, vast amounts of capital, a skilled informant or finding the right person at the right time, the highfliers have an uncanny ability for obtaining all they need for whatever project they undertake, and their allies seem to benefit from it as well.

    However, their fortunes unravel when they stop conducting good business. Should the subject violate the Terms of Service, this effect ends and cannot be used on them for the remainder of the scene.

    This replicates Shifting the Odds with Reach assigned to instant use, switching primary factor to Potency, advanced duration and getting results within an hour. The condition compensates for the extra Reach.

    Fifth: The Executive Mindset
    Prerequisites: Mind 5, Resources ••••, Staff ••

    It is often said that the sign of a master highflier is that they only work with the best, a rumor that is often reflected in the superb skill their allies display. But similar to the highflier's business success, that greatness is the fruit of their constant efforts.

    After spending a scene in a training course, workshop or similar, as well as spending a point of mana, the subject's diligence towards expanding their professional horizons and their drive to pursue excellence unlocks hitherto untapped reservoirs of talent.

    This replicates Augment Mind with Reach assigned to advanced duration and going above trait limits.

    With conjunctional Fate 2, the Terms of Service are automatically applied.

    A point of mana is used to pay for both going above trait limits and conditional duration, truncated to one point per Legacy rules.

    Optional: Fate 5

    The greatest amongst the highfliers are true paragons of prosperity, seemingly predestined for success. People treat them with respect and are more willing to help them, being blessed by good fortune in return. Meanwhile, their adversaries are often beset by obstacles. Even those favored by the highfliers seem to share in their good fortunes. Those in the know recognize it as the result of a carefully cultivated public image.

    After spending a scene doing brand management (funding community outreach events, overseeing employee scholarship programs, etc), or supervising a subject doing it, as well as spending a point of mana, the highflier is able to turn the world in the subject's favor, which has the following effects:
    • Anyone who encounters the subject instinctively feels comfortable around them, intuitively sensing the blessing. They treat them with candor at worst, but often with friendliness. When socially maneuvering, their impression level is increased by one. In other cases, the subject receives a bonus of [Fate] to convince others to aid them or treat them with kindness.
    • Any action aimed at helping the subject or giving them aid suffers the effects of a boon.
    • Any action aimed at harming the subject suffers the effects of a hex. This includes all forms of harm, from outright murder, to theft and lies that cause the subject inconvenience.
    This is a custom, Potency primary, Making of Fate, with Reach assigned to advanced duration and a point of mana being used to apply the Terms of Service.

    What were you thinking design wise ?

    The first Attainment focuses on scouting potential talent. The highflier can now know who is very good at what they do, and also what they want. Not only does this allow them to find allies, but it also contributes to building an image of being able to gauge potential at a glance. Since it's an Unveiling, which expands the subject’s senses, utilizing sensory range is not required.

    The first optional Attainment allows them to play to their strengths, giving the impression that they always know how to seize an opportunity to leverage their talents. When combined with the main Attainment, it allows them to offer great advice to their allies, creating a reputation of the highflier being well informed, savvy to how the situation is going to unfold and providing counsel that is perfect for their allies.

    Since Serendipity’s two Reach effect is trying to focus the spell to be able to understand how to accomplish a goal with a determined skill, utilizing conjunctional Mind should allow them to obtain that information more easily.

    The second Attainment is based on the Barfly merit. It portrays the highflier as someone who is in with the in-crowd, since not only can they easily procure invitations to the biggest events of the season, but they also fit in naturally. Their allies also seem to land these invitations just as effortlessly and blend in just as smoothly, making the highflier’s patronage seem like the key to high society.

    Activating is done as part of the preparation work for an event, so the cabal can hatch a plan to make the most out of its opportunities and figure out how to support each other if something unexpected happens.

    The second optional Attainment combines elements of Shifting the Odds with a Ruling of Mind to make it seem like the highflier always arrives at just the right time and knows just what to say in order to find a contractor at an amazing price. Those working with them also seem to benefit from their network, being able to procure the right crew for their goals. The activation ritual incentivizes the cabal to have a habit of organizing their thoughts and planning out the next steps.

    The Terms of Service were designed to encourage behavior that promotes good business. Once a deal is made, they are incentivized to take every measure to deliver on it. This builds a reputation for them for professionalism and reliability, making them a valuable business partner and a valuable procurer of rare resources. Simultaneously, anyone benefiting from their effects is encouraged to deal honestly and fairly with the highflier, which allows the highflier to build a pool of reliable allies, which is an invaluable advantage amongst the Seers. Since the condition is something that requires active choice to come to pass, the duration bonus is set to +2.

    The third main Attainment allows them to become a VIP in any situation, whether it's to enter a military base, sit down in a meeting of organized crime bosses or even direct a police investigation. They assume the mantle of authority and others fall in line. This is inspired by a similar ability the Blank Badges utilize (which acts as a magical version of the Bavarian Fire Drill), but unlike them, the highfliers focus on taking charge rather than infiltration. Since they can invest others with similar authority, being in their good graces carries a lot of clout.

    The third optional Attainment means that whenever opportunities present themselves, circumstances align so they can seize the moment. They can be inspiring, focused, receive the information they need or have a good turn happen to them at the right time. Their good fortune even seems to favor their allies.

    The fourth main Attainment allows them to create a communication network. Not only does this mean they can always be able to talk to their allies, which in itself is deceptively powerful, but their allies can also communicate and wordlessly coordinate with anyone on the network. Since the network only needs to be renewed annually, it means working with the highflier grants one access to their vast network of talents.

    The activation ritual is based on the old tradition of sharing a meal as part of establishing trust with a client or business partner.

    The fourth optional Attainment means the highflier has an uncanny ability to procure what they need, when they need it. Whether it's fabulous wealth, favors from friends in high places, skilled informants or just finding the right person, they are always able to make things happen, and they can extend that benefit to their allies.

    The capstone Attainment allows the highfliers and their allies to become the type of visionaries needed to accomplish their ambitions, whether it's by being brilliant scientists, genius businessmen or having a force of personality that moves entire crowds. Not only does the highflier have a beautiful mind, they can coax out that potential from their allies.

    The optional capstone is an inverted version of Pariah, called Paragon, which makes the highflier and their allies seem destined for success. They always make a great first impression, those that help them seem to share in their good fortune, meanwhile their adversaries are constantly beset by obstacles. The activation ritual is inspired by Gustavo Fring's training video on brand management, which helped him build the reputation to cover his illegal activities.

    When it all comes together, they present the highflier as a self-made man. They have a mind ahead of their time, with enough charm to effortlessly be invited into the most exclusive venues, able to cut amazing deals with skilled professionals, wielding powerful influence and vast amounts of wealth with casual ease, and even have the clout to command respect in any situation. Circumstances just seem to align to bring their ambitions to fruition, and those that aid them often benefit as well.

    The original concept for the Legacy was "I am a self-made man", that a character becomes successful through their own efforts and determination. David Xanatos's story in the episode Vows of Gargoyles was a major inspiration, showcasing an archmage level plot to prove that he had earned all he has. The concept was then scaled down based on how his time as a mage might have been.

    Winston and the Bowery King from the John Wick franchise, as well as Gustavo Fring from Breaking Bad, as well as Darth Plagueis in his titular novel, all provided sources of inspiration. All of them earned their power, influence and fortunes through their constant efforts, and proudly declared that they are the linchpins of their institutions. The Attainments were designed to allow a member to wield the kind of wealth and influence these characters project.

    Another design objective was to make their philosophy neutral. Members could encompass anything from crime lords, altruistic executives, people climbing the corporate ladder, fixers, and more, with a common element of working to climb to the top.

    The Republic of Venice proved a great historical setting where ambition would be rewarded. In the first centuries, their political system allowed any successful merchant to become a part of the Republic's policies, with a combination of a robust political system and ambitious merchants being indispensable for its success. Because they transported goods from all over the world, their trade routes and travels could be a great potential source of Mysteries.

    The Legacy's name pays tribute to Venice's history as a maritime merchant republic, with Polaris being the north star, a constant source of guidance for navigators. The original members probably saw themselves in a similar goal, guiding Venice to glory and those with potential to realize it. In those times, they called themselves the Polaris Arti / Guild, a occult guild of the city of canals.

    After Venice's politics stopped embracing meritocracy, members had established enough contacts to pursue their own agendas, leading them to change their name to the Polaris League and start to drift across the world.

    Baron Fairweather also proved an interesting connection for the highfliers, having possibly crossed path at different times and wearing the garbs of different cultures, creating a shared history that could be drawn upon for plot hooks.

    The appearance section reinforces their concern with presenting themselves as true professionals.

    Their approach to management is inspired by Marshall, Carter & Dark. This allows the highfliers to create the image of the faceless corporation through contractors, while simultaneously managing it through a few key NPCs. The discrete integration of their mundane and supernatural businesses is a combination of the approaches of Gus Fring and MC&D.

    The use of the League's symbol is a small nod to how the Illuminati identify themselves in Gargoyles. The Sapphire Star from Monster Hunter serves as an inspiration for the emblem.

    The initiation was suggested by orathaic and was inspired by the red paperclip story, in which a person started with a red paperclip and, through a series of trades over the course of a year, ended with a two story farmhouse. Since a prospective member has a moderately successful business, this initiation proves that they are able to obtain success even in the most challenging situations.

    The organization style incentivizes intrigue, as the Legacy members conspire to push their own agendas. They may also choose to focus their attention on an overarching goal, becoming a formidable force, similar to the Providence partners from the Hitman games. The yearly event is inspired by The Gathering, from the Darth Plagueis novel. The festivities are used by the titular character to forge powerful alliances, exchange valuable information with crime bosses, set up future operations and steer the politics of the Republic to the character's favor from behind the scenes.

    Lastly, the yantras and oblations encourage them to be very active socially, as well as constantly looking for new chances to negotiate.

    That concludes the notes. I hope you had as much fun reading them as I did writing them, thank you in advance for the feedback. A big thank you to orathaic for all his help.​​

                          Mystery Cult: Navis Polaris
                          Description: Descending from an ancient Venetian merchant's guild, the Navis believe in the power of commerce to chart the course of society. Followers pursue excellence in business, using their skills to secure the supernatural items, contacts and insider information needed to realize their ambitions. The Navis scouts for potential initiates amongst different businessmen, regardless of whether their operations are legal, for those with the drive and mettle to elevate their businesses by leveraging the supernatural market.

                          Mystery Cult assets: Reduce by one the Availability rating of any requisitioned Artifacts, Imbued Items, Library, as well access to supernatural crafters and merchants. Sleepers can only requisition dots in Library.

                          • Initiates receive extensive training in techniques to build long lasting and profitable business relations, granting them the Pusher merit.
                          •• Full members are granted access to the Navis' network of connections, gaining a dot of beneficial Contacts for their business field.
                          ••• Seasoned merchants benefit from the Navis' many business partnerships, obtaining the Fixer merit.
                          •••• The patricians of the Great Council direct the Navis' operations. In order to elevate their ventures to the next level, they are provided with a loyal and highly skilled right hand man. While their origins are a closely guarded secret, they always seem perfectly paired with their councilor, having the perfect skill set to support their ambitions. This grants three dots of the Sleepwalker Retainer merit.
                          ••••• The members of the Signoria preside over the Great Council. A non-mage member will find their assistant is suitably prepared for their new endeavors, the Signoria providing them with the equipment, skills and personnel to stand amongst the most skilled aides in the world. This grants two dots of Sleepwalker Retainer and one of Staff.

                          Mages in the Signoria receive the revelation that their aides are Supernal Entities of commerce, which have been contracted to provide a lifetime of faithful service to their corresponding member of the Signoria. The Entity then gives them a choice: a better mortal aide or a Supernal aide. The first option provides them with the same benefits as non-mage members.

                          The second option instead grants one dot of Sleepwalker Retainer and the Supernal Watcher merit, representing their aide’s ability to return to their true form. The assistant is fully human in their mortal form, they do not bleed mana in their Ephemeral form and can switch between them with an action. Should they be killed, they return to the Supernal safely and can be brought back with a targeted summon by their Signore.


                          Observation: Regarding the 5 dot benefit, the mage may purchase the option they didn’t choose with XP normally. They may also invest to make them a Rank 2 Supernal Watcher.

                          Design Notes

                          Since the Navis Polaris operates on a similar logic to the highfliers, they focus on providing ways for members to earn their wealth and success. Their focus on meritocracy means there is no maximum rank a Sleeper can achieve, but rather that they become more capable of interacting with the supernatural as they progress.

                          The Mystery Cult assets are based on the types of resources that Marshall, Carter & Dark would have available. Following the guidelines from Signs of Sorcery, it is assumed they have, by default, 3 dots of assets available.

                          The first dot is a reference to the tradition of building trust between a merchant and customer before bartering.

                          The second dot provides the member with valuable networking opportunities.

                          The third dot amplifies the effect of the wealth members gain, making them seem well connected and versatile.

                          The fourth dot provides the merchant with a capable assistant, an Owen to their Xanatos, a Walter to their Integra, a Charon to their Winston.

                          The final dot is a reference to the deal Puck proposed to David Xanatos: “One wish from Puck, or a lifetime of service from Owen. He chose Owen”. In this case, switching between a helpful mortal and a Rank 1 Supernal Entity opens a lot of interesting problem solving options.

                          The final two ranks are based on the political structure of the Venetian Republic. Lastly, their name is Navis Polaris, rather than La Nave di Polaris, because Latin was the official language of the Republic until the 16th century, and the secret guild was founded in its early days.
