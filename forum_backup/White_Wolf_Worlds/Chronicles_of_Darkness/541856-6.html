    Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
    I was reading about the Hannibal TV series by Bryan Fuller, and that really sold me on the idea that the eponymous serial killer could be a Beast just as much as a Slasher. And I kept thinking about Beasts with vibes of medical horror. Soon a concept came to me.

    A Namtaru who chooses to be a street surgeon despite graduating with great honor from an admired institution, much to the disgust and shame of his family and colleagues. He simply doesn't care, because he believes true knowledge and breakthroughs are only possible when one disregards useless rules. He does not reject the Hippocratic Oath outright, for that is equally foolish. For the most part, he follows his own code, despite caring little for philosophical discourse.

    Besides cold hard cash, he also demands payment in the form of new things to study, the things no one else wants to be near, yet alone touch. All he asks for is assistance in entering the dark corners where marvels are waiting to be dissected (i.e. help with using Skeleton Key). He has no real home besides his Lair, where he has built a surprisingly normal house amidst all the preserved organs and anatomical diagrams of critters that shouldn't live.

    He is a Collector, but not because he kidnaps people and experiments on them (he finds that method laughably theatrical). The shock is caused by his pragmatism and disturbingly accurate empathy. People near him are often revealed to be sick in more than just the flesh, which he uses as leverage. His lesson, when he bothers to teach one, is that there is something ugly inside everyone, and that pretending it's not there will only make it fester.

    Despite all this macabre curiosity and clinical detachment, he still has a desire to be seen as a source of safety and warmth. To help with this, his Lair has a Chamber dedicated to unorthodox cuisine, which he calls &quot;medicine's more extroverted sister&quot;. There you will find things you never imagined could be edible, yet alone delicious. Good luck with getting past the unappetizing exteriors, however. Any mortal or supernatural who behaves is welcome.

    He probably has Kinship with Changelings and Prometheans. Atavisms would include Mimir's Wisdom, Unbreakable, Limb From Limb, Relentless Hunter and Needs Must. Nightmares would include You Must Obey and We Know All Your Secrets. I don't know what his Horror will be like at the moment.

    Does this sound like a viable character? Is there anything you thought was vague or confusing? What do you think is worth fleshing out (pun intended)?
    I read through this and it just terrified me. So good job! For the Horror, I'm getting an image of an emaciated, vaguely humanoid creature whose mouth is full of shark-like teeth and which stretches almost to his ears. His arms (all four of them) are unnaturally long and bear long bony fingers tipped with long claws almost like scalpels. It was inspired by the Mourngul from Warhammer Fantasy. You can look up some images in Googe or on Forge World.

                                Eshmaki Predator: Beasts represent a primordial fear. And what is more primordial than the fear of being eaten? Since before life was complex enough to have any semblance of thought, we have devoured each other, and it was that fear of being consumed that pushed our ancestors to evolve and conquer their surroundings. But the Beast is not some looming predator, flaunting its might at the top of the food chain. No, that attracts too much attention, too much competition for food. This Beast is a parasite, it lets others do the hunting for it, and then consumes the half digested prey from within the predator's own body!

                                The Beast's lair is not a cave, nor a castle, it is an organism of unimaginable scale. The Beast slithers through its digestive track, finding prey swallowed up by its host and descends upon them. Its soul possesses the torso of a man, but the body of a freakishly large tapeworm. Clinging to the fleshy walls of its lair, its eyeless, sucker shaped mouth creates disgusting noises as it slurps up half digested food. When it opens wide enough to scream, its prey (if they're still alive) can see ring after ring of razor sharp teeth designed to shred them on the way down its gullet!

                                                Eshmaki Predator: Plants are supposed to sit quietly and look pretty. Their sweet aroma is supposed to be alluring, not an airborne narcotic. Their vines aren't supposed to reach for you like prehensile limbs. They're supposed to drink water, not blood. And they damn sure aren't supposed to be lounge singers at the local gentleman's club!




                                                Ugallu Collector: She runs an orphanage "collecting" wayward children off the streets. Children who have lost their parents or were unwanted by them in the first place. Maybe she had a hand in that from behind the scenes, maybe not. The official story is they suffered a terrible accident that nobody could have predicted. But her orphanage is no children's refuge, it is essentially a prison, though you wouldn't know it from the outside. A gilded cage where the owner feeds upon the fears of lost children. Their mournful sobs, their outraged cries, their in denial explanations, their pained squeals as they're disciplined for unruly behavior. Nobody gets adopted here. They just get too old for the government to care, and then she dumps them on the street to make room for their replacements!
