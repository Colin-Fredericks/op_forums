            My interpretation is that you could manipulate all moving parts of an object, but you can't directly move the object. (In the case of cars you'd turn the wheels which leads to movement instead of giving it a push in the direction you want it to move.) A ballpoint pen is mechanical, but I believe the only things Remote Control would be able to do is to rotate the ball and extend/retract the point if it's a retractable pen. Otherwise why would there be a limit on what object you can manipulate with Remote Control?
