    Technically, WW doesn't have to give up ownership of the CofD or Exalted to let Onyx Path have more power over these things.

    WW already allows Onyx Path to sell licensed products through distribution systems that WW isn't using (IPR and Red Bubble for example).

    Assuming Rich wants the responsibility involved, nothing stops WW from giving him the authority to set up and manage a CofD Vault (with their oversight to whatever level they want to enforce) for them instead of doing it themselves. This is goes right back to the frustration I voiced above. If the people running WW view the CofD as both something with strong creative value, and something worth continuing to sell for profit even through a licensee, why do they kneecap it by not handing things to Onyx Path if they're not going to do them? Sure a CofD Vault run by Onyx Path would mean less overall profit for WW (one assumes such a thing being run by Onyx Path would mean both get a cut in some fashion), but a business should weigh the time it would take to realize that money vs. the potential of years of lower profit lost sitting on it.
