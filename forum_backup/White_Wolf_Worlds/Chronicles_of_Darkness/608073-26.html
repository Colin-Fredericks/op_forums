                                Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                                I'm having some doubts as to how this Burnout Mode will work for Deviants, and how the developers would try to make it distinct from Going Loud.
                                If memory serves the word used was blowout, but burnout implies at least one possible difference (and maybe blowout does too): that it's the end for the Deviant. The blaze of glory - or at least the inferno of chaos and destruction - they can choose to go out in when they run out of other options.

                                Originally posted by StSword View Post
                                Possibly not always, the inspiration list includes such works as Dark Angel, in which the characters were engineered before birth to be the way they were, so while odd there might be room for Deviants who were denied ever having a chance at normalcy.
                                Orphan Black's another listed inspiration; while the clones seem to be normal humans (barring maybe an increased healing factor), they're still born into their problems.


                                For other arcane options for where Deviants could come from, Prometheans and vampires have both been plothooked as cocked-up attempts to make mummies, and Deviants could be too (as well as cocked-up attempts to make Prometheans and vampires).

                                Speaking of Promethean, the Ulgan Lineage is all about the way magical initiations break you up and remake you into something new. As a story hook, that works for Deviants just as well.

                                A sorcerer attempting to make a summoning vessel, sacred whore, or homunculus.

                                Someone cures a supernatural condition like spirit possession, but the cure has side effects (no one said Deviants can't be made with good intentions).

                                Someone tries to cure a supernatural condition like spirit possession, but the subject isn't actually possessed, and the "cure" just fucks them up.

                                Despite the usually SF trappings, Mr Hyde and Captain America are basically made with alchemy.

                                What are the long-term effects on the Oracle of Delphi, from breathing in all those mysterious vapours?

                                    The anime series Deadman Wonderland (opening) works well in the Deviant framework. The basic premise is that a conspiracy is rounding up Deviants with blood-based powers, and imprisoning them (under bogus charges, if need be) in a corrupt jail. You see, they want the secret to make more such Deviants . . . and turn a tidy profit at the same time. Thus the Deviants (also known as "Deadmen") are forced to fight each other in illegal pay-per-view death matches . . .with the loser (assuming they survive) having a body part amputated for research.
