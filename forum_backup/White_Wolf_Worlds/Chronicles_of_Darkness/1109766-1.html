            It is incredibly easy to create the Muslim Creed of the Sanctified. Look to Muslim organization and ritual as a basis for the same in the Sanctified. Longinus exists as the prophet for Kindred. Kindred are not human. Adjust the minor detail of the Longinus story to match everything up with that. I'm sure Sanctified of the Christian Creeds would pitch a fit, but they could get along in the same covenant.

            For Jews, I would look to Kabbalah, but I have no idea how the Testament would need to be altered. I don't know enough about it.

            I don't think in the same domain Christian, Muslim, and Jewish Creeds have a place. Maybe Muslim and Jewish would work in the same place. Jews have a legal system too so they could easily have modified Law. Either Law or Oaths. It might be fitting to tie the less devout to the Carthians and Invictus with the Muslim and Jewish covenant/creeds picking one or the other with which to ally.

            Any way you look at it, Kindred will not be devout Muslim or Jew, and the same goes for Christians. Kindred that were devout as mortals could easily transfer their faith to the Lancea et Sanctum or equivalent. I think it is important to consider that. To those that actually understand the Lancea et Sanctum, it functions as a place for devout Christians to go when they become Kindred and lose their faith in Christianity. Muslims and Jews would need to have their place to do that and a covenant would need to reflect that conversion.

                            you know, even if you call the Lancea a Longian religion, Longinus is still a Christian character. Him stabbing Jesus with the spear is an iconic, Christian image. The reason people call the Lancea "christian" is because their ideology heavily relays on Christian symbolism. Sure, you could say that Longinus is "just the fluff"- but heck, the fluff is important. In the same way you could say that the Circle of the Crone don't really need to be pagans, as all of the paganism is just an aesthetic and not what the Circle is really about. While the Lancea are not Christian, they have originated from Christianity, and it still influence the way they structure themselves. Beside, I would also like to remind that we do have an Orthodox parallel to the Lancea at 2E, as detailed in Athens's setting. Covenants are much more localized in the new edition, so it would make more sense for a separate Muslim and Jewish covenants to exist, than them being simply Creeds of the Lancea.

                            Now, we should also remember that a lot of it would depend on the amount of power the Lancea have in the region- if they are the top dogs of the city, be sure that they won't let to any competitors to exist in their turf. They'll probably have Creeds with modified religion, meant to include vampires of other religions who seek for a way to practice their faith even after death, even if in a perverted way. But we should also remember that the covenants are made from the vampires who make it, and that they had their own religion and faith before the Embrace. It doesn't make sense for a setting with a strong Muslim or Jewish (heck, even Orthodox) presence to simply embrace the teaching of Longinus and the Lancea. Jewish and Muslim vampires have probably developed their own heresies, built around their own ideas and belief system which are as different from their original religion as the Lancea are from Christianity.

                            In sort, yes, the Lancea have Christian roots, and yes, those roots matter, and yes, the Lancea are not Christian. Yes, it may have certain factions which try to convert as many undead souls as possible to their Church by bending their faith so it could be more well received among others, and yes, there could still be other covenants which parallel other religions. All answers are correct- and a lot of it depends on the setting. There is no reason why the Lancea couldn't have its own "muslim creed" in order to convert more vampires, but saying that it should take the place of every Abrahamic covenants around the world is ridiculous, especially since it is not meant to be a "catchall" Covenant for every vampire who follows the Abrahamic religion in the new edition.
