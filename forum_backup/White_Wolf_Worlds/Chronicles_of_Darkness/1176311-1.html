                                Food for thought: delivering a touch-range spell, like any other spell, is a matter of intent and Imago. The hand is the natural delivery point for the spell, because the hand represents our primary tool for interacting with the world. So even if you deflect an attack by touching the attacker's arm, I'd still houserule that as *not* delivering the touch spell, because you need more than just "any form of touch contact whatsoever." You need to touch them the way *you* intended, the way your spell's Imago demanded, in order to hit them with the spell.
