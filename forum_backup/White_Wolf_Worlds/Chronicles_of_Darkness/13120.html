      Why would the GM be generous? Its angels are being eaten, after all!

      More seriously: given that ghosts and spirits exist in a different kind of Twilight than angels do, how would this work in the first place? Wouldn’t they both have to Manifest before the meal could commence?

      Finally, you make an interesting observation: since there are now different flavors of Essence (e.g., spiritual, ghostly, and angelic), it makes sense that Aether is the byproduct of the use of the specific kind of Essence associated with angels, and no others. Put another way, “Essence” isn’t one thing, any more than “power stat” is: it’s a collective term for the fuel stats used by ephemeral beings. Implication: when werewolves speak of “Essence”, they’re usually speaking of spiritual Essence; and they’re no more interested in hunting ghosts or angels than, say, a changeling is interested in collecting Mana.

              Actually DW, you're wrong, book of the dead reveals that werewolves can eat ghosts for essence just like any human and the fact that the idigam can manipulate both spirits and ghosts using their essence shaping abilities means that any differences between the forms of essence aren't that great,

              As for how "essence thief" works, I would assume like you that it requires manifestation of some sort, but I didn't write GMC so I don't know what they had in mind.

              But you apparently skipped over the word conversion, since the very word conversion requires by definition the assumption that the types of essence aren't exactly the same, one doesn't convert an apple into an apple. A spirit or ghost with essence thief eats some essence from an angel, option one is that the spirit or ghost converts the angelic essence into spiritual or ghostly essence in their pseudo stomach. Whenever angelic essence is used aether is produced, so why wouldn't the essence thief using angelic essence to make the correct form of essence count as use?

              Option two is that the angelic essence is still angelic essence, in which case obviously when the essence thief uses that stolen essence aether would be produced, because aether is produced whenever angelic essence is used whether by an angel or anything else.

              Hmm since essence thief is just a numina, you could even use that numina to justify crossovers. Why wouldn't a demon love a mage with an essence thief fetish or a sin-eater with an essence thief fetter?

              Now for the record I wouldn't argue with any ST who thinks that's just too much of a benefit, but I don't see any reason other than game balance why it wouldn't work that way.

                  Obviously, this is up to the ST, but I believe that the GMC rules about Ghosts, Spirits and Angels supersedes both Book of the Dead and Book of Spirits info.

                  I believe GMC introduced the idea that there were three different types of essence (one for each ephemeral being) and that these beings do not exist in the same "frequency" while in a Twilight state. Hence why Angels are such a big deal in GMC, as they are the only things that can break this rule given adequate power by the GM. I believe the point is that Spirits and Ghosts only run into each other in rare circumstances now. As opposed to the previous fluff that had them exist side by side in Twilight.

                  Now, this is not to say that the Numina does not work as you mentioned. It does convert different essence types (or at least that was also my reading of it), so it does theoretically let a Ghost or Spirit feed off an Angel. Though I would be inclined to split the Numina into a "greater" and "lesser" version. The "lesser" version allowing the same type of essence to be stolen. While the "greater" version would allow for essence conversion from other types. The first I would make reasonably uncommon among Ghosts and Spirits. The second I would limit to some very select Spirits and Angels.

                  This is just my take on things of course. I just like the idea that Angels should be a little more powerful then Ghosts or Spirits, particularly for a Demon game.

                                                          You just took the words out of my mouth. To clarify, I’m not trying to imply that geists (the plural spelling used in the book) are rare; I’m just stating facts: GMC has revised the rules for Twilight in a way that makes interaction between ghosts and spirits (or between either and angels) highly unlikely.

                                                          As Satchel pointed out, there are spirit-like entities in the Underworld. As far back as Second Sight, the description of the Voodoo Tradition of thaumaturges made it clear that there are godlike entities that counted as ghost-like rather than spirit-like for the purpose of Thaumaturgy’s rituals, but are not ghosts. Summoners described Cthonians. Geist described the Kerberoi. You don’t have to go to the spirit world to find entities that embody concepts related to Death; and it’s far more likely IMHO that the text in Geist that suggests the possibility that geists are created when ghosts consume personifications of death is referring to chthonians (which I use as a catch-all term for any ephemeral entity that’s made of the same kind of ephemera as a ghost but is not a ghost).

                                                          And to be clear: the box that talks about geists and spirits phrases it as “doesn’t that sound a lot like spirits? Why yes, it does. If you want them to be spirits, you can.”

                                                          Finally, Geist has an unusually troubled history in terms of its development, and several of its developers have said that if they had it to do over again, they’d do a number of things differently. One in particular said that he doesn’t like the “ghosts consuming spirits” theory and doesn’t use it.

                                                          All of this is not to say that you can’t have angel-eaters; but it’s not as easy to manage as it would have been prior to GMC.

                                                          My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
