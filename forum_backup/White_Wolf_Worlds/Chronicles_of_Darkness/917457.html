    Which Of The Gamelines Speak The Most To You?

    06-19-2016, 02:18 PM
    Feel free to write as much or as little as you want. I'm not feeling verbose, so I'll be brief.

    For me, it's gotta be Changeling. To sum it up, I love the themes of learning to deal with bad experiences, of not being defined just by what has been done to me, but what I'll do for my growth as a person. To remain capable of seeing wonders in the middle of the darkness.

    Honorable mention to Mage and Genius, for the themes of self-determination, knowledge-seeking and controlling your hubris.

    Bye.

          That's tough.

          Mummy is my favorite gameline, but it doesn't /speak/ to me in any tangible way, it just happens to provide a handy excuse to revel in history and Egyptian myth. If we're looking for a game that actually has an emotional punch, I want to say Promethean; Changeling makes the list too, but (in my eyes) it's a metaphor for experiences I only know second-hand through loved ones, having been fortunate enough to never know abuse myself.

          Promethean, to me, is a game all about identity. It's about rejection and wanting to be normal, about building an identity for yourself and becoming comfortable in your own skin even in a world that doesn't understand you. I absolute adore that it says "humanity can be fucking awful, but it's still all somehow worth it" because that really is the experience of life. As someone both grapping with some gender stuff and dealing with the much more common questions of identity that come with young adulthood, the Great Work feels like a journey I'm on just as much as one of the Created.

          The only game that speaks to me more is Wraith, but that's a topic for the other forum.
