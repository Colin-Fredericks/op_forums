    Game design question regarding Beats

    08-10-2016, 01:16 AM
    So I'm adore CofD second edition's changes and improvements over the first with one singular exception, and I was wondering if anyone here could help me wrap my head around something. Among the many popular mechanics being used in a lot of storygames these days is some sort of reward or resource offered for failure (you see this in Fate's compels offering Fate Points, or the Storypath preview showcasing the delightful new Momentum mechanic) and CofD is no exception, offering Beats for acting in line with Conditions or for accepting Dramatic Failures.

    That's where my brain hits a snag; your reward for failure is Beats/Experience, used to later build your character during the time between games (or at the very least, at the end of sessions) while offering nothing to your character in the moment. It seems to me like Willpower would be a better fit, offering you the chance to succeed later at the cost of failure now and maybe save guaranteed good luck for when you'll need it most.

    Was this ever considered in playtesting, or as a house rule at anyone's table? Is there a brilliance to the Beat economy that I'm missing out on? Would love to hear some feedback.

    Remi. she/her. game designer.

      Well for one it's more intuitive for the player to gain a beat rather than willpower when you fail. Failure is thematically more likely to collapse or drain one's willpower and fortitude than it is to build it up, while Challenge and Failure are thematically tied to becoming better and improving yourself.

      As for gaining a benefit later rather than immediately, to do otherwise leaves the opportunity for the player to fail and then immediately gain benefit towards reattempting that thing immediately.

              I'm pretty sure it came up in playtesting since a few of the books suggest anything that would generate a Beat be turned into WP for NPCs since those don't track Beats (and don't track other sources of WP gain unless the ST feels like it).

              I'm also pretty sure milo and Charlaquin hit the nail on the head on why that's not how it works for basic PCs (small aside: for one-shots, something I've done in the past since it's an issue for any XP based game that XP based incentives are lost like this, is some sort of real world trade in. I've done simple things like candies, to more organized things with a convention where attendees could collect tickets for an end of con raffle and had XP buy those instead. Usually it works pretty well since players recognize the conundrum.).

              I think it's also worth noting that while Beats are used as rewards for incorporating failures into the game to make it more interesting, that's not their sole purpose. Plenty of options for Beats come from positive advancements for the character as well.
