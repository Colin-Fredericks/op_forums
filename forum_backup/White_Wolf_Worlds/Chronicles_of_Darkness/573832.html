    Mixing my "To Do" hunter group list

    06-30-2015, 09:01 AM
    Some time ago, I've decided that instead of waiting for a cannon or an homebrewer to make the groups I would like to see I should start doing so myself. I order to do so, I've made myself a number "To Do" lists for future ideas- one list to "specific target" groups, one for "dark mirror" groups, one for "Ascension translation" groups, one for "antagonist specific" groups, one for "Vigilant Times" groups and one for "regional powers" groups. The first and the last are kinds obvious- the first is about the regular Night Stalkers/Witch Finders/Spirit Slayers/Mortal Remains format and the last is about hunters who come from Regional and Great Powers from around the world. The second is about creating a Slashing Conspiracies who are devoted to killing in the way the regular groups work to protect humanity. The third is meant to convert Ascension's secret societies (Traditions, Conventions and Crafts) as Conspiracies. The fourth is about groups in the style of the first list, but who hunt the more "evil" monsters, which means they may work together with the more "human" ones- strixs, idigams, spirits, abyssal horrors, etc. Finally, Vigilant Times is a project which is meant to similar to Ancient Bloodlines/Mysteries, only for Hunter instead of Vampire- an historic setting which gives all kind of new options.

    As you may see, 6 lists is a bit too much for anyone to handle, and while working on the first list I've found that some of my groups already overlap between the lists, so I've thought that it would be easier to look which hunter groups I could also mix together!

          I'm going to post the Nimrod Movement first and expand the Cage of Shadows into a darker form (and maybe adding some Aki Ten material for WWII), but I do have a concept for them- they were first founded during the Dark Ages and were chased by the Church during the Spanish Inquisition, being seen as heretics.                                   So, after giving it some thought, here are the most developed ideas I have for now. I would like to here which ones you would like to see first

                                  Heirs of the Crown- warlocks who were persecuted during the Spanish Inquisition, they call upon the Lesser Keys of Solomon and wear demons as a face for strange powers
                                  Architects of the Hierarchy- and HYDRA parallel to Task Force VALKYRIE, they are motivated by an idea for a "perfect world" which includes them as its masters and the rest should die
                                  Los Inocentes- dark inquisitors who murder people in the name of faith and wish to purify the Vigil from heresy.
                                  Cult of Moloch- a group which exist since the biblical days, they follow the strange rite of sacrifice, blood and fire in exchange for great and terrible powers and dark gifts
                                  Tecuanimeh- servants of the Smoking Mirror which spill blood in his honor, in order to promise their survival under the upcoming Black Sun and the age of darkness it would bring.
                                  Queen Anne's Revenge- a criminal organization which was formed during the Golden Age of Piracy and steals not only material wealth, but also the souls of their victims, which they use for power
                                  Followers of the Dark Star- magicians who seek after the lost star of the heavens in order to bring salvation to humanity and destroy those who use tyrannic magic to ruin it
                                  Aga Nritya- a thuggee cult which sacrifice those souls which were stolen from the Wheel before their time and send them to Kali, and dance with their Fire in a strange and weird ways
                                  Garden of the Hesperides- healers who walk in the Hedge in search for weird remedies and protect people from the dangers of the thorns and its Fae denizens
                                  Maw of the River- a cult which hunts down relics in the name of a primordial god and devour them for their powers and curses- before seeking after the agents of the Devourer to eat them alive
                                  Maagal HaBaal VehaAshera- a new incarnation of an ancient Canaanite Priesthood, they tends and make their own gods from clay and wood while fighting the influence of the rival undying deities
                                  Chasseurs de Tetes- a ruthless group from the days of the Terror who carry an old idea which says that all stories must end in a certain way.
                                  Divine Artificer- followers of the Grant Maker who hunt after Satan and his creations by using weapons forged from divine metals
                                  Nibiru- one of the oldest groups in the world of hunters who hunt since the days of Pangaea and seal away ancient evils using strange artifacts
                                  Followers of the Heart- musicians and necromancers who seek after the music of life and death and stop the undead who destroy that beat
