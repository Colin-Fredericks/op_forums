            Remote viewing in real time is considered a form of sensory range, requiring nothing but an extra reach.

            The Advanced Range factor is sensory, meaning that the
            mage must be able to directly see, hear, or sense her subject. Viewing
            a subject remotely but in real-time, whether by security camera or
            magic scrying window, requires an additional Reach.
            Meanwhile the space 2 enabled sympathetic reach is a separate thing.
            If the mage has the Space Attainment
            Sympathetic Range or the Time Attainment Temporal Sympathy,
            she can cast spells without the need to sense her subject.
            The familiar ability is simply a consequence of the shared senses. You're just using them as a camera, basically.

            This is all on the range explanation on page 113-114.
