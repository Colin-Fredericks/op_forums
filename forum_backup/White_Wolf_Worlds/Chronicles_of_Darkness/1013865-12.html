        The Chorister merit is the most generous. You gain a dot of Cruac and actress to CoC merits. This qualifies one for Cruac Styles.

        A Lances Sactum character with 5 dots of Theban Sorcery with the Chorister Merit and 1 Dot of Cruac can get the Void Cruac Style at 5 dots and the retainer merit at 5 dots to end up with a Void Creature that has 5 dots of Theban Sorcery. Weird, but interesting.

        An alliance between LS and CoC, might be rare, but scary when they find synergies between types of blood sorcery.

        Also the Cruac God can use parts form any formerily living thing, which can include Changelings, werewolves, stranger things. This spell is amoung the most powerful in the game, it'd make mages nervous.

                        It's not an unspoken rule. P181, 'Members of the Circle of the Crone have access to the following Merits. Unless otherwise noted, they have an additional prerequisite of Status (Circle of the Crone) • or higher.' Each Merits section begins with a statement like that for each covenant.

                        Before someone gets up in arms about something sounds like it wouldn't require Covenant Status, 'With Storyteller discretion, some of the Merits may be used outside their respective covenants. For example, the Carthian Merit “Mobilize Outrage” doesn’t offer any hard reasons why it couldn’t be used outside the Movement, or even by human characters. Its inclusion here reflects the fact that it’s generally taught to Carthians, and is an important tool to the Movement. While the sections state that such Merits require a dot of Covenant Status, use your best judgment for your chronicles. In some cases, for example “Toss That Shit Right Back,” it could apply for non-Kindred characters as well.'
