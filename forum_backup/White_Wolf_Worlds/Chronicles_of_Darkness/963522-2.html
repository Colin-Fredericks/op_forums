                Originally posted by Troy View Post
                You think it's impossible for someone to know and understand they live in a culture of oppression and tyranny and then ingratiate themselves into that culture because the alternative is hazardous to one's survival?
                Strangely enough, yes. I realize that it's counter-intuitive but the human brain basically doesn't work that way for at least the vast majority of humans. There is some very interesting psychological research on this topic about development in oppressive regimes or around state sponsored atrocities. It has a huge impact on people.

                Basically put we're social animals, we want to be similar to the people around us and it causes us distress to deviate from it. While we can be selective to some extent with where we identify and with whom, we really need to be associated with other people who share our value system. Long term cognitive dissonance, holding one value publicly and another privately is extraordinarily damaging to the human psyche. It's why undercover work is such an extraordinary task for the police and tends to result in a LOT of therapy being needed afterwards.

                With the exception of those people suffering a few rare mental disorders, humans need to believe that what they're doing is right even if forced to do so by outside compulsion. Gradually we tend to internalize a belief system that justifies our actions, at least eventually and if deprived of alternatives and coping mechanisms.

                Although Mages may not function the same way as they possess Gnosis and Wisdom, thus they cannot suffer breaking points. So... um, I'm not sure how it would work for them.
