                                          To put it a little more thematically. Exploits are demons pushing the system that is the world to do things it isn't meant to do by exploiting flaws in the system. Almost everything an Angel does is part of the system as it is meant to work, because that's why an Angel knows how to do it. If an Angel does something not intended by the system then they are edging closer to Falling.

                                          As a further thematic point, if you give Angels an ability that is mechanically the same as an Exploit, you run the risk of de-escalating the themetic punch of an Exploit. It may dillute the idea that the Demon is breaking the system if an Angel can do the same thing within the system. Incepts include the idea that they are a special access thing that must be granted in rare cases at least partially to mitigate this (my opinion, I did not write the incepts).

                                              Standard caveat - do what you want in your own game.

                                              As written, no, angels can't use Exploits. Sure, some Exploits may appear more powerful than an angelic equivalent in a very limited way, but that's because its effect is so limited.

                                              Exploits are what demons have managed to hack together through whatever reality access they can make. Angels get the codes and the God-Machine's blessing.

                                              If there's a specific Exploit effect you're wanting an angel to be able to achieve, I'm sure it can be replicated through numina, influences, and Incepts. (And the combinations of these will give more flexibility to the angel, allowing it to do things other than what the single Exploit allows.)

                                              But agreeing with ElvesofZion, Exploits should (generally) be better in their area of expertise. They can do things angels generally can't do, because they're hacks. They follow unexpected reality paths, and they go against routines the GM is comfortable with. Angels have the codes, but they have to follow the rules. Demons don't have that limitation.

                                                          Embeds and Exploits are just remembering things that demons used to be able to do as an angel, and tryign to reverse engineer how they used to do it using back doors instead of system authority.

                                                          To use computer terminology, demons are hacking the system while angels are using Admin privileges to do whatever they need or want to. The end result is that, while both the demons and angels have different methods of achieving their goals, in the end its still the same trick they're pulling off. There's nothing that a demon can do that an angel can't do as well - they're both drawing from the same source of information (the God Machine).

                                                          Actually, no, I lied - Interlocks are supposed to be things that the G-M can't do, but that's the extent. Everything else, including all Embeds and Exploits, is just the demons trying to replicate already existing angel abilities. So, if there's an Embed or Exploit, you can bet an angel can do it (assuming the G-M included such an ability in the angel's design).
