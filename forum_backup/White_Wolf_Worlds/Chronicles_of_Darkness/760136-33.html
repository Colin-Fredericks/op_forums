        So:
        Colony: Inherent stuff Monarchs get. Extends into numerous sub-systems.
        Swarms: Temporary forces the Colony can generate. Distinct from "Colony actions" in that Swarms are for shorter timescale things (attacks, active spying, fast movement of resources).
        Servants: Singular creatures custom built to a task. Enduring.

        Suggestions:
        Colony: Make this handle the general, slower, unrolled actions. Principalities unlock more things that they can do, or let them do things faster. Doesn't have "rolled" actions (on the basis of - if the Colony is doing it, they can take long enough to get it done right).
        Swarms: Make these the temporary, abstract collection of forces. Used for actual Dice pool actions. Keep the previous system where Principalities allow for Size to be traded for ability. They cost to create and have a shelf life, so they aren't always in existence.
        Servants: Model these off Retainer, but expand a bit with Servant-only Merits that can improve them. Make Principality X at Y dots pre-requisites for said improvement Merits (yes you have to spend XP on them). Mitigate the XP costs by: Certain Clades or Castes get "discounts" or "rebates" (Beat or XP refunds). Make them cheaper in general. Fitting the "minion" theme still, let Monarchs recycle them to rebuild new Servants per Chapter/Story.

        Addendum: After thinking about this after posting, I'm now enthusiastic about this approach and will explain more when I have time later. Also, commentary on Principality passive bonuses (if they are still a thing).

        So, some more thought/text:

        I think removing Rolled actions from Colonies themselves might help, both to streamline, and to add clear distinction on purpose. Colony actions would be mostly maintenance or simple tasks that aren't really risky, but they are time consuming. Not sure if you'd place Principality unlocks under the section for Colonies, or on the Principality - it depends on how many there are. Play would basically be: determine what your Colony is doing this Chapter/etc. Unless something comes up, it gets done. Basically, this is the automatic stuff that Cults can do.

        Swarms, then, would be for when you need something you either need done now-ish, or something that Colonies can't just do. Breed/Conscript armies to fight, generate swarms of spy creatures, spawn special harvesters to collect some resources/Wealth before a rival gets back, etc. These have dice pools, and are involved in chancy things like combat. Because they are special purpose, they exist only as long as their task requires, being recycled back into the Colony (as much as possible) after the action. The "trade swarm size for [benefit]" can be seen as the time/resource cost involved in spawning/designing the extra capabilities. This is similar to the rolled action Cults can do, and summoning up cult fighters.

        Servants are the pet projects and favored minions of the Monarch. They are individualized (even if based on a basic template), specially tinkered with and modified to suit the Monarch's goals, but lasting longer than the single purpose Swarms. They functionally serve the same roles as Retainers, subject to the Principality and Province based augmenting Merits that the Monarch crafts them with. Recycling is basically in-universe Sanctity of Merits to rebuild your agents and minions. Once nice thing about Servant Investments being Merit-based is that it lets them be offloaded to the Merits section (rather than bloating the Principalities write up), and allows more flexibility. A Monarch can sink a lot of XP into designing a multitude of new and terrifying creations, similar to (but different) than Mummies sinking XP and effort into their Cults.

        As to Principality granted inherent bonuses, after thinking about it in the vein of XP costs and redundancy, I'm actually against them. If Bows gives free Attributes or Skills, for instance, it either needs to add that cost into the Principality, or why would anyone bother spending XP on "mere" Attributes and Skills when they can get them in a bulk package as part of Principalities (which also have Active powers, Colony upgrades, Swarm Aspects, etc.). Those inherent abilities and traits a master of Blades "should" have? Let them pick them up separately. And maybe use as many of the existing Merits as possible (such as Iron Skin for inherent armor, upgrading to Advanced and Epic versions as needed). For things that are missing: new Monarch (and Servant) only Merits (such as Size increases, etc.). Maybe some fancy new Style Merits, for logically chained improvements?

                                    Draft 1. Colony + Swarm revisions.

                                    Colonies are represented by five attributes, each representing an estimation of the Colony’s abilities at given tasks. These are:
                                    Military
                                    Knowledge
                                    Industry
                                    Espionage
                                    Diplomacy
                                    All are rated from 1 to 10, with a single dot in each at the start, plus an extra dot in the Monarch’s primary area of focus. Raising Colony Attributes is a single merit dot.
                                    Aside from attributes, Colonies possess a resource pool called Wealth, representing natural or geographic Resources harvested from Provinces. Wealth has no real cap, but it does require harvesting.

                                    Swarm
                                    At any time, a Monarch or Servant may order the Colony to dispatch a unit of Drones or even weaker organisms, called a Swarm, to fulfill an assigned task.


                                    The size and number of Drone forces a Monarch can deploy is limited solely by her budget, how many Resources she spend on them. For every point of Wealth she has, she may create a Swarm or increase a Swarm’s maximum size by 1, up to a maximum of 20, ranging from a small pack of dog like animals to a kilometer long strike force.
                                    The Swarm then is sent out and attempts to perform said task, using the Colony’s Attributes as its own attributes.
                                    In the event of combat, the Swarm attempts to protect itself, using the following attributes in combat.

                                    Health: A swarm’s Health is equal to its Military + Size.
                                    Initiative: Initiative is always considered 0, with all Swarms going at the same time simultaneously.
                                    Defense: Defense is always considered 0. It is impossible for a Swarm to evade in a meaningful way with their congestion and mass working against them. Not to mention a complete lack of fear when in numbers….
                                    Speed: Speed is equal to Military + Authority + 5.
                                    Attack: Military + Authority. Represents the Swarm attacking all opponents in close range with it, with successes translating into damage.
                                    The Swarm is also hard to damage from most weapons. Most single target weapons deal only 1 damage. Bashing damage effects are too weak to have an impact most of the time when it’s against a single target.. The Swarm benefits from the racial Endless natural healing, representing natural regeneration and cannibalization of resources taking effect.

                                    Swarms may also be upgraded with Aspects, representing advanced modifications to make them better suited for specific tasks.
                                    However, this process is costly, and gets more inefficient the more Aspects placed onto a Swarm.
                                    As a general rule: each Aspect cost 1 resource plus an extra resource for every Aspect the Swarm already has.
                                    So 1 Aspect costs 1, 2 cost 3, 3 costs 6, and 4 costs 10 and so on.


                                    Example Swarm Actions
                                    Unless otherwise stated, a Monarch does not need to be involved any of her Swarm’s actions. Willpower is not allowed to be spent on these actions either, but Wealth may be spent, representing over budgeting and waste, providing a single










                                    Spy Order your Swarm to Spy on a target location or creature via dispatching smaller Endless. Roll Espionage + Authority to determine their effectiveness at stealth and camouflage. You receive only basic details such as “target is flying away” “target notices us”.





                                    Harvest Order your Swarm to harvest the Resources in a Province. Roll Industry + Authority, with each roll taking an hour or more depending on distance traveled.. Successes translates to a number of Resource being removed from the Province and moved to the Colony, up to the limit the Province has remaining.
                                    Harvested Resources perish or are consumed at the start of a new Story, unless Stockpiled, another action.
                                    Resources cannot be spent on this action unless the Province requires other resources.


                                    Research Order your your Swarm to perform research and experimentation on your behalf using Knowledge + Authority as a dice pool. Each roll is one hour.


                                    Influence Influence another party by dispatching a diplomat of your own to speak to them. Roll Diplomacy + Authority as a social to improve relations or coerce someone. In certain situations, Military might be substituted for Diplomacy, representing aggressive negotiations.
