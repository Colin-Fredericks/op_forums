                                            Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
                                            Well, I don't have the Clairvoyance or Telepathy Merits, so I'd like an explanation. I am honestly curious.
                                            Okay, fine. I thought my various essays over the years more or less spoke for this pretty well, but I can be wrong. More than I think and less than others assume.

                                            It's not too terribly complicated. While I strive to have a sense of nuance, fairness, understanding and more, I have a deep and abiding sense of right and wrong-sometimes moral and ethical, other times simply a matter of information-and the deeper one is an asshole in being wrong, the more and more I...well, I used to get into fights a lot, and I still gets odd muscle tensions and sharp teeth when I confront it. But even the old violence doesn't quite hit the deep satisifaction of breaking a person with truth, particularly with shattering a soul with nothing more than the fullness of themselves. I get a visceral pleasure from efforts to show that a person is wrong, and I'll freely admit that the more it hurts them, the more I smile.

                                            I have asserted, for years, that I am an asshole, and in many ways I am very much the same troll I was when I first went to school or got on the internet. I'm more refined, more caring and thoughtful, and more mature than I ever was when I started-but the old ways remain. My teeth still go sharp time from time, and I will invariably violence people I find wrong with truths they cannot or will not see.

                                            There's a reason Beast resonates with me. To take gleeful viciousness and to try and find ways to curb it or unleash it as appropriate and ultimately beneficial to my community is five kinds of my language.
