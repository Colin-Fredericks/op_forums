    Tilts and conditions:
    Lets say were operating at 2 dot arkana
    1. Can i do damage manifesting a limb wrack condition? From fate someone probably would fell and dislocate/breka an arm
    2. Can i implement the poisoned tilt? That deals damage. Additionaly poisons deal long term damage based on toxicity
    For Fate, any of the published spells that can apply a hex can apply Tilts, which includes the Apprentice-level spell Exceptional Luck. The list of non-custom Tilts a hex can apply includes Poisoned, which is not included on the list of Tilts that can be applied more than once with a hex to get the more severe version.

    More generally, the Creative Thaumaturgy guidelines note that applying a Tilt is usually a Reach effect on an attack spell but may also be done from whole cloth with a 3- or 4-dot spell.

            Its worded poorly, here is a more concise explanation :

            * Calculate primary factor normally, no yantras apply and no roll is required.
            * You can spend pseudo-dice in spell factors equal to your Arcanum rating.
            * If you Attainment uses two Arcana, use the mage's lowest dot rating in the calculation. So if it uses Forces and Time, for example, and the user has Time 3 and Forces 4, you use a rating of 3.
            * Ritual casting time is an hour/scene, but "we are assuming most will be designed to be cast instantly" (hence the confusion)

                                    Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                                    Space intro states that Sympathetic casting is withstood by Space itself.
                                    Does that mean that a conscious subject cannot decide to not withstand the spell?
                                    Yes.

                                    The withstand rating is that of the Sympathetic connection. So two connected this (eg: a mage and her soulstone, a pregnant person and fetus inside them, etc) will have a withstand of 0. Weak sympathy have a withstand of 5.

                                    Then other withstands are added to that (and the subject can resist or not as usual) per the usual rules for multiple withstands.

                                                Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                                                I m confused about the first reach option of the One mind two toughts spell:

                                                -what kind of actions could be considered Mental istant?

                                                Per the description of Attributes on Page 207, any time an action is described as a "[Mental/Physical/Social] action," it means that it's a mundane action that uses an Attribute from that category in its dicepool. Meditation is a Mental action, as is Perception, as is memorizing something.

                                                "Carrying on a conversation" is the closest thing in the spell's set of examples to something that is not the type of thing that is modeled using the extended action mechanics to represent an ongoing project that can suffer setbacks or be left alone and resumed later. It requires a Reach because it allows you to multitask your thoughts at the timescale of an action scene instead of just in the looser timekeeping of scenes that aren't tracking Initiative.

                                                Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                                                Also, would it be balanced (for a level 5 mind legacy attainment) to give the option to enter physicalli into the astral realms?
                                                With an appropriate supporting Arcanum and theory behind it, sure, but not from Mind alone. Spirit or Death would be the most straightforward basis, with their ability to turn a body into ephemera on their own, or Fate might be able to manage the more particular feat of leveraging the Gate of Horn from Changeling, and unless the plan is to rely on Synesi I'd probably include the Astral Adept Merit as a prerequisite as well.
