                There's a variety of reasons why the Lucifuge's Castigations work on a larger group than just demons.

                There is, as many have noted, the the longstanding association of many different kinds of entities through the word demon. There's the benefit of a broad mechanical base that doesn't limit them to just dealing with one kind of monster (coughcoughlookingatyouknightsofsaintgeorgecoughco ugh). There's greater iconic power from drawing from beyond the simplest source that help to keep their nature feeling strange and dangerous and not properly understood. There's not being so simple as to be droll(fuck, I need to say that again, there's not being so simple as to be droll). It emphasizes the depth and breadth of the World of Darkness while also emphaszing that all will never be known. It strongly emphasizes the many themes of the Lucifuge as a group, particularly in the area of not being able to trust what's within, both as an individual and as an organization. So on and so forth.

                These are things that matter. As always, instead of asking why they didn't do it our way, maybe we should be asking why they did it their way.
