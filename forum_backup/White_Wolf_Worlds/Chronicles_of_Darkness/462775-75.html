                                                    Okay, so, just finished reading The Pack and there are a lot of spiritual goodies in there that could make some interesting plot hooks. Figured I'd ask opinions on them here, since some of the implications aren't totally clear to me.

                                                    1) Totems probably shouldn't teach Gifts because it runs the risk of disrupting the totem bond, but if they do teach Gifts they can always sense the general distance and direction of the individual they have marked. Is this something exclusive to the unique nature of Totems or is this true of all Spirits? Because if it's true of all Spirits you had better be careful about declaring Siskur-Dah to force a Spirit to teach you a Gift.

                                                    2) Totems are protected from Essence Bleed by their bond with a pack and regain Essence by hanging around their pack. If I'm understanding this correctly, a Totem is always Resonant with their pack, and the totem bond acts sort of like the Familiar Condition from Mage 2E but supported across multiple people enabling it to sustain a larger Spirit. Uratha can also smell or otherwise sense this pack bond on a totem, which makes them tend to give the spirit a pass for being on the wrong side of the Gauntlet. Could a Mage with Active Mage Sight attuned to Spirit also be able to sense the totem bond?

                                                    3) How far can you push a Totem Spirit's Influences? Could you, conceivably, give them Influences normally reserved for Rank 6+ if you're willing to pump the Experiences into it?

                                                    4) If an Idigam used Forge Fleshbound Spirit on a Totem, would that sever the totem bond, or would the pack now have a slightly more powerful patron? Would the Totem still regain Essence from the bond, or would it still need to feed exclusively on living flesh?

                                                    5) The Magnanimous Totem Merit indicates that because this Spirit is more accepting, it expects more attention, and now packmates need to offer chiminage/gathra for a number of hours a week equal to their dots in the Totem Merit. What form of gathra would you consider acceptable? Would it just involve praying to/paying homage to the Totem for up to five hours a week? Slip a prayer here, a minor sacrifice there, until it all adds up?

                                                    6) How does a large pack go from being just a large pack to being a Lodge? What metaphysical/spiritual changes need to be wrought to change a Pack Totem to a Lodge Totem? Because, in my view, a Lodge Totem of equal Rank to a Pack Totem has significantly more spiritual heft. This makes sense, but I'm wondering how that shift occurs. Do packmates who contributed dots of the Totem Merit to the Pack Totem before it became a Lodge Totem get those Merit dots restored?
