    Thanks, nofather and Reighnhell! I tried cutting back on his Skills and Merits so he's not a Bat God, as the joke goes. Big thanks to amechra for helping me understand the nuances of the CoD rules better. I'll add your recommendations too, Reighnhell.

    ​
    Originally posted by Reighnhell View Post
    Re: Shadows over Gotham

    Excellent Batman! This version feels very much like the street level Batman of the classic Detective Comics (which is my favorite interpretation). The only adjustments I would make is Athletics 4 (Throwing) and Weaponry 3.

    Using your Batman as a benchmark, here is the Joker. I went went something that is a blend of classic Detective Comics, with a bit of Batman:the Animated Series

    Joker


    Virtue: Humor Vice: Chaotic Vice: Cruel
    Undertaking: Maniac

    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 4, Wits 5, Resolve 5
    Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3
    Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 4, Composure 5

    Skills: Animal Ken 1 (Hyenas), Athletics 2, Brawl 3, Drive 3(Getaway), Computers 2, Crafts 3 (Explosives), Expression 2 (Comedy), Firearms 2(Pistols), Intimidation 4(Theatrics), Investigation 2, Larceny 3(Escape), Medicine 1(Poison), Persuasion 2 (Extortion), Science 2 (Chemistry), Stealth 3(Ambush), Streetwise 4 (Crime), Subterfuge 4 (Lies), Weaponry 2 (Blades, Improvised)

    Skill Tricks: Drive (Lose ’Em), Intimidation (Don’t Mess with Me), Larceny (Light Fingers), Stealth (Backstab), Subterfuge (Cognitive Dissonance), Weaponry (Wearing Weapons)

    Merits: Allies 2 (Extortion Victims), Allies 4 (Harley Quinn), Area of Expertise (Chemistry, Comedy, Extortion, Theatrics), Closed Book 4, Contacts 4 (Criminals), Crack Driver, Danger Sense, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Fame 2 (Gotham Criminals), Encyclopedic Knowledge (Comedy), Fast Reflexes 2), Fleet of Foot 2, Hardy 2, Indomitable, Iron Will, Morbid Fascination, Quick Draw (Blades, Pistols), Seizing the Edge, Shiv 2, Sleight of Hand, Style: Fast Talking 4, Style: Improvised Weaponry 2, Style: Light Weapons 2, Murder Expert, Style: Street Fighting 3, Staff 4 (Henchmen), Striking Looks 1 (Frightening), Untouchable, Vice-Ridden

    Conviction: 7 [Mirrors, pg.32-33]
    Integrity: 3
    Willpower: 10

    Size: 5
    Initiative: 10
    Defense: 6
    Speed: 13
    Health: 8

    Condition: Amnesia, Madness, Obsession (Batman, Crime), Notoriety
    Talent: Compelling Madness, Profiling
    Frailty: Intolerance for Chaos, Obvious Lunatic

    First off, I really like those choices for Virtue and Vice. I'd lower his Composure to 4, seeing as how he is capable of losing his temper, such as when someone explains a joke or when he thought someone else had killed Batman. I'd add an Interdisciplinary Specialty for Lethal Joke Items, Mind Games and Chemistry, as well as +1 dot to Medicine, Persuasion, Science and Weaponry. I'd increase his Hardy to 3, seeing as how he's pretty much immune to poisons, especially those of his own making. He'd also have Iron Stamina 2, to laugh at injuries. Harley would be covered by Retainer. He definitely qualifies for Hurt Locker Merits like At Any Cost, Air Of Menace and Living With Death. He could have a Skill Trick or two in Streetwise. Those Conditions are spot on. I especially like your choices for the Fighting Merits, they really get across how he's a dirty fighter who will go for the kill as fast as possible. It's possible that he has a specialized Omen Sensitivity he's not fully aware of. Lastly, if we were to use Nightmare instead of Conviction, I'd give him a rating of 2, maybe 3.

        Lee Ping (From Detentionaire)
        "Huh, physics works in real life."
        Stigmatic Atariya
        Virtue:         Insightful; Vice: Driven

        Str 2 / Dex 3 / Sta 2
        Int 3 / Wit 3 / Res 3
        Pre 2 / Man 2 / Com 2

        Mental Skills: Academics 2, Computer 1, Crafts 1, Investigation 3, Occult 1, Science 3
        Physical Skills: Athletics 2, Firearms •, Larceny 1, Stealth 2, Weaponry 1
        Social Skills: Empathy 1, Persuasion 1, Streetwise 1, Subterfuge 1
        Specialities: Cramped Spaces (Athletics), Never Left (Subterfuge), Oddly Practical Applications (Science)

        Merits: Allies (A. Nigma Student Body) •, Damn Lucky ••, Indomitable ••, Interdisciplinary Speciality (Oddly Practical Applications) •, Manifestum ••, Mr. Lucky •, Parkour •
        Bonus Merits: Omen Sensitivity (Uncontrolled), Unseen Senses (God-Machine)
        Conditions: Legal Minor, Notorious ("Lee Ping the Prank King"), Obsession (Mysteries)

        His Stigmata is the "tattoo" on his left arm. Manifestum is a version of Clear Sighted that immunizes you from mind control instead of mental illusions.

        He also has Legal Minor despite being a character in an Innocents game because there's no Grounded And In Detention For All My Free Time for The Foreseeable Future condition.

        Edit: Aw yiss, managed to fix him up so he's a legal starting character. Because having him be anything else wouldn't make sense.
