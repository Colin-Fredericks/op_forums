                                                Hmm... Franken Fran, a horror manga of somewhat lighter tone than you might expect, is something I would take as a good example of extremely low wisdom, if not actually zero.

                                                Fran herself, I'm not sure what I'd call her in the CofD. An automaton constructed by a mad Professor, who took after him as a surgeon of such quality she can bend the laws of physics, but at terrible consequences for everyone involved (except herself, always ending up spared of harm by the fallout of messing with things they probably shouldn't). She also doesn't have the same sense of right and wrong as normal beings, such as placing the value of life so high that she'll keep people living in an immortal nightmare state, or mutated into barely recognizable, questionably sentient things, if it ensures survival.

                                                Mad science is easy to compare to magic, but there's something different than most cases of either when she manages to teach other people her methods, and have them work, or gives lectures and generally be found to be correct, except for the whole 'lack of human values' thing.

                                                And, while I'm on the topic of horror manga, what would The Enigma of Amigara Fault be? Just an abyssal manifestation of some sort?

                                                            I can see Venom being done as a Changeling, or any of the symbiotes really, specifically a Beast Mirrorskin. Mirrorskins are able to change their features and don't take penalties for not having proper kit for disguises, just as the symbiote can become clothing and basically disguise its host's features, and among the Beast's affinity contracts is Boon of the Scuttling Spider, allowing a Changeling to move on any surface that could support them, with both Seeming bonuses working for it. Add in other contracts for shapeshifting, increased strength and the weapon creation and bang, symbiote.
