        I like this idea, but the Web of Life spell is pretty descriptive in its use of "sensing".

        By tapping into the pulse of the living world, the mage becomes
        keenly aware of any living things nearby. She feels their
        presence by the weight they exert upon the Tapestry, a gravity of
        the life-force that connects all creatures to the same great cycle.
        Because the unfiltered sensing of all life might provide a sensory
        overload, most mages specify certain types of life to detect, such
        as “humans, insects, and birds” or “only dogs.”

        Now, if you do not accept this, I think combining this with a Space 1 conjunction would allow you to actually sense the location of those living things, so with life 1 space 1 you could cast a version that specifically pinpoints them so that you could cast on them using sensory range without having to see them. If not, then a combined spell that is both knowing and unveiling could probably do the trick, which would then require a fairly strong mage to cast it, which then justifies the strength of the spell itself--a strong spell for a strong mage! I also see no reason why casting one spell that "combos" into the next spell should be discouraged. So this sounds pretty fair to me personally.
