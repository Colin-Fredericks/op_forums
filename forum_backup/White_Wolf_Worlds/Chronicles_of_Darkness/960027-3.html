I am well aware that there are Christians who would be offended by me saying "Jesus Christ", or even "jeez" as an expression of exasperation.                                     I hate to double post, but one idea I just had that also relate to time travel is temporal anomolies - that is, people from timelines that are not their own, caused by a temporal portal (a person walking through a doorway from one timeline into another), being summoned by an entity, or just simply being the leftovers of a messy paradox (such as a person whose parents now never met yet is still alive). However they went through, the event changes them, meaning that even if they came back to their normal timeline (should it still exist) they are unable to return as they once were. They know of the changes to time, and are resistant to powers that would alter their past or stop them in time. Their memory of events could be entirely off too - for example, Project VALKYRIE could be named Project YGGDRASIL in their timeline, should they be aware of Hunters, or maybe Vampires have a weakness to helium instead of light. Just an idea.
