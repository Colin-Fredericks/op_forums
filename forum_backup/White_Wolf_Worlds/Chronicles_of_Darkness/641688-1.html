            There's too much open to debate. A Beast is more XP efficent than other supernaturals - they only need to raise Lair and the occasional Atavism instead of both Potency and a Discipline/Renown/Arcana track. That said, that's white room theorycrafting, and lots of Beats also get contacts/allies/mentors/retainers/etc in the various supernatural communities, and some want Nightmares, etc. There's a lot going on here.

            When it comes down to comparing splats, you have to realize each has their own specialties. Vampires excell at controls - self, grappling, mind f@#$-ery, subordinants. Werewolves are hunters, shapeshifters, and pack people. Mages are researchers, investigators, and shielding experts. Changelings are dreamers and illusionists. Demons are spies and religious figures. When it comes to the speciality, that particular supernatural tends to excell beyond others. So, if we did a competition for getting someone to give them a free drink, vampires generally win - blood bonds and mind control tends to beat out everyone else who still needs to use Social Maneuvers. Werewolves can track people they never met by where their shadow passed. That's stuff that's very hard, if impossible, for others to mimic.

            No one has a specialty of "combat master." When it comes to combat, everyone is close enough that luck, environment, and specific builds play too large of a part to make it definite call. A vampire against a demon? Well, the demon will generally win... if it has fire powers to use in combat. A Messanger (without any fire) against a Daeva? The daeva could generally take the demon. There are few who can keep up with a fully armored mage in terms of defense, but a power like Dominate, The Look, or some Dominance Gifts cut through it like butter.

                                                    You are immune to environmental tilts that are shared by your lair traits. This doesn't normally extend to the entire element.
