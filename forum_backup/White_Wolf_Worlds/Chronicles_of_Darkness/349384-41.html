    Regarding the Yonko from One Piece
    . Big Mom is True Fae, given her fairy tale inspired kingdom and gaining power by forcing others into contracts.
    . I see Whitebeard as an extremely powerful Extempore on the refinement of Cuprum. His seeing his crew as his sons is indicative of a Promethean's need for a family.
    . Kaido is an Apex Beast from the Anakim family, his Hunger is Ravager. Everyone in the series is terrified at the mere mention of him, he is referred to as the "world's strongest creature" rather than "world's strongest man" and everywhere he goes ruin seems to follow.
    .Shanks is an Extraordinary Mortal if you're using Mirrors.
    . Blackbeard is the hardest to pin down. Besides his Dark-Dark and Tremor-Tremor powers something is off about him. Characters remark that there is something unique about his body's biology, he's always in the right place at the right time and he also seems to have almost a supernatural knowledge of what's going on. Maybe an Unchained? It would fit in his role as a defector from Whitebeard.

                                                            Merits: Area Of Expertise 1 (Single-Minded), Direction Sense 1, Interdisciplinary Specialty 1 (Single-Minded), Trained Observer 3, Vice-Ridden 2, Greyhound 1, Iron Stamina 3, Parkour 4, Closed Book 3, Iron Will 2, Table Turner 1, Armed Defense 5, Defensive Combat 1 (Weaponry), Light Weapons 4, Killer Instinct 3 (Advanced 2)

                                                            Health: 14 (Size 5)
                                                            Willpower: 26
                                                            Defense: 9
                                                            Initiative: 13
                                                            Speed: 30 (Species Factor 21)
                                                            Potency: 8

                                                            Dread Powers: Immortal, Gatecrash* (Anything Needed), Know Soul, Legion* (Add-Ons), Natural Weapon 1 (Baseball Bat), Nothing But The Mission*, Numen (Aggressive Meme, Occult Sight*, Pathfinder, Seek, Speed), Purification*, Reality Stutter, Regenerate 3, Surprise Entrance, Swift

                                                            *Gatecrash: A Horror with this Dread Power can open a gateway to other planes of existence (such as the Hedge, Primordial Dream, Shadow, Underworld or Astral Realms). The Horror spends a point of Willpower and makes an appropriate dice pool roll; if successful a gateway into the destination realm opens. The creature can hold the gate open for a number of turns equal to its Potency. Gatecrash can only open a gate to a specific realm, but may be selected multiple times, each time applying to specific Realm.

                                                            *Legion: The Horror is followed and served by Brief Nightmares of a similar nature. The amount and power of these Brief Nightmares is roughly proportional to the Horror’s Potency. Use the following as a guideline: at Potency 1-3, a few Minions; at Potency 4-6, several Minions and a few Hordes, at Potency 7-9, several Hordes and a few Lone Terrors, at Potency 10, as many Lone Terrors as desired. If killed, replacements come within a few scenes at most. Most Horrors possess a weakness that prevents them from creating or commanding their Legions.

                                                            *Nothing But The Mission: The Batter is to be treated as if he was a God-Machine Angel for the purposes of his Aspiration. In other words, any supernatural means of making him abandon it automatically fail, and he is immune to Tilts that would make it impossible for him to act (such as Insensate and Beaten Down). He is also treated as having Eidetic Memory, Natural Aptitude, Total Control, Liar’s Tongue and Spoofing (use Composure + Subterfuge).

                                                            *Occult Sight: The Batter is sensitive to supernatural phenomena. He notices the active use of supernatural abilities within the range of his senses and may make Perception rolls to approximate their distance and direction. In addition, by spending a point of Willpower, he can see through powers of concealment, illusion and deceit with a Clash Of Wills using his Wits + Potency as his dice pool. Lastly, he always knows when he is being targeted by a supernatural power and may mimic the Aura Reading Supernatural Merit using Wits + Potency, but without drawback.

                                                            *Purification: The Batter has and his Add-Ons have Influence 5 (Purity). The effects of this Influence are up to the ST. However, the following effects are free:
                                                            • He can see and interact with all frequencies of Twilight.
                                                            • His attacks cause supernatural damage.
                                                            • Any Ephemeral Being of Rank 5 or less whose Corpus he fills with Aggravated damage immediately loses all Essence and is thus destroyed for real, and he restores a number of Willpower points equal to the Ephemeral Being's Rank.
                                                            • He could undo a pandemic by finding the patient zero and bludgeoning them to death. Or heal trauma by finding the people responsible and giving them his "treatment". Perhaps you could add that using the Influence requires the Batter to kill someone.
