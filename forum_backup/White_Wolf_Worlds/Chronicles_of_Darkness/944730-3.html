                                    Originally posted by TyrannicalRabbit View Post
                                    Extremely excited for this project. While some mechanical guidance will be appreciated. I am particularly looking forward to writing on the crossroads where the themes of each splat meet and hopefully some oddball ideas as well.
                                    This. Part of making cross-overs work, to me, is finding the middle ground between the various splats. Sure, you could potentially have your Mage cabal run into a pack of werewolves while investigating some spirit activity and have to adjust, but you are viewing them through the lens of a Mage as secondary characters in a Mage Chronicle. A Chronicle where Mages and Werewolves are looking at the world together through a shared Chronicle is going to be a different beast by necessity. Advice on how to do Chronicles that aren't merely, "You play as Splat X, there are some members of Splat Y and Splat Z around, but they aren't really crucial to the plot" but actually, "You can choose between Splats X, Y, and Z, each of them is equally important to the Chronicle" and where the thematic middle-ground between two (or three, or four, or...) game lines would be fantastic.

                                    Beast has an easier time with cross-overs because it has both crunch and fluff dedicated to explaining how to interact with other splats. The Pack for Werewolf did a nice job of discussing how a vampire or mage might join up with a werewolf pack, what their role might be within it, and what the potential problems of such arrangements are.

                                    Haberdasher's Requiem Conversions and Homebrew

Given that this is to be a Chronicles of Darkness Crossover though, I think we're more likely to stay in the CoD rules & general setting, but perhaps some advice on multi-splat group dynamic & beat/experience gaining (seeing how a mixed group could be both a pack & a throng, while also a motley & cabal & krewe in mechanical terms & narrative terms).
