        I would give Spidey more Intelligence and Strength. Without effort, he is both among the great geniuses and strong hitters of the Marvel-verse. It isn't always apparent because is overshadowed by characters truly focused on such areas, but them we're talking about Reed Richards and Tony Stark, or Thor and The Hulk.             All of that makes sense. I just thought of his web-slinging as being Dexterity + Athletics. That's mostly for crunch: that way, he can have large dice pools for web-slinging without having an unfitting Firearms rating. Besides, I'm pretty sure that Firearms is for actual guns that actually kill, or at least seriously hurt. His Matter Arcanum can be raised to 4, depending on how sophisticated and versatile you want the webs to be. His Wisdom drops to 7 in the comics sometimes, depending on how he chooses to deal with his near-omnipresent problems. There have been moments when his Wisdom reached 9, usually when he had to do unsavory business, but said to himself: "never again". Also, being stalked by a fanboy Deadpool does wonders for temporarily increasing your responsibility, it seems.

            Thanks for the help!

            By the way, if you are playing Spider-Man, Harry Potter or another mega famous character, but adapted into NWOD terms, the franchise centered around that character is replaced with something that has very similar themes, but different visuals and plot details. For example, if you play a Hunter version of Batman, his comics/movies/video games and so on are replaced with a franchise that is also about a traumatized detective that masters his own fears and uses unique resources to combat evil-doers, all the while doing his best to avoid snapping. This Substitute Batman is not called as such, and thus he has no bat-themed gadgets. History as we know it remains much the same, monsters notwithstanding.

                                        I've been writing up NPCs for a Demon game, and I created Fi from The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword as an angel:

                                        Rank 2
                                        Virtue Helpful, Vice Thorough
                                        Power 3, Finesse 6, Resistance 5
                                        Corpus 10, Willpower 10
                                        Initiative +11, Speed 14
                                        Defense 3 (4 with Sword)
                                        Size 5

                                        Influence Courage 2
                                        Ban: Must obey the commands of the wielder of the Sword
                                        Bane: A cowardly human. Conditions, Vices, or even Virtues should be considered for what constitutes as "cowardly".
                                        Manifestations: Twilight Form, Materialize, Fetter
                                        Numina: Implant Mission, Speed, Seek, Pathfinder, Sign, Innocuous, Strike Blind
                                        Special Can determine the Virtue and Resolve of whoever wields the Sword
                                        Weapons
                                        "Goddess" Sword, 9d, 2L, -0, +1 Defense

                                        Fi is bound to a blade - really an artifact of the God-Machine - and silently appraises those who lay hands upon the sword to determine if they are "worthy". Usually she picks those with courageous Virtues or great Resolve. If they are worthy, she reveals herself to them and alerts them to their "Great Destiny", usually an Occult Matrix.

                                        "Nihhina kalekal-zidu kal masun, kal manudanadu. Nihhina kalekal-zidu nukal shaghu-desasudu — nihhina kalekal-zidu kal innu-desasudu udhkal samm." Arthur Ashe
                                        She/her, contributing writer for Scion: Pride 2021

                                                Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                                                You know how the relationship between power and responsibility is one of the main themes in Mage? And how Spider-Man’s adopted motto is “With Great Power, Comes Great Responsibility”? That encouraged me to write stats for him as a Mage, as per the crunch and fluff of 2ed. His Path works the best as Acanthus. His Order is Free Council because he is a hero and admirer of the Sleepers, not an unseen and dusty law enforcer. He is on good terms with the Guardians Of The Veil and Adamantine Arrow, however. Spidey may turn out to be more fitting as a Deviant, at least in crunch.

                                                His Persona Merit (combination of magical style, Shadow Name and personal mysteries that grants Supernal Sympathy) is likely to be reasonably powerful. The Web-Slingers are an Imbued/Enhanced Item. His spider-sense, wall-crawling, enhanced physical capabilities in general, superlative reflexes and impressive luck are a combination of mundane Merits, Life, Mind, Forces, Fate and Time. The costume itself could also be Enhanced for greater flexibility and protection, maybe also Imbued with a Mind Veiling spell to improve the disguise. Spidey also uses Space to conceal his sympathetic connections to his loved ones, and for Scrying both friend and foe. Even without spells, his stats are still very formidable.

                                                I am not sure how to translate his standard back-story into an Awakening, be it a Mystery Play or Astral Journey. His Nimbus might include webs as a symbol of personal connections, binding promises and karma. Spiders themselves might symbolize Fate: both of them can weave and cut webs in their own way, be it to connect, restrain, protect or harm. The members of Spidey’s Rogue Gallery work well as Deviants, Slashers and the occasional unscrupulous Mage. The symbiotes, though? They definitely work as scarily intimate Abyssals. Madame Web is a Mastigos. Aunt May, Mary Jane and similar characters could be Sleepwalkers, depending on how much they know about Peter.

                                                Feedback is appreciated!

                                                Virtue: Responsible/Inventive
                                                Vice: Short-Tempered
                                                Concept: Genius Superhero

                                                Path: Acanthus
                                                Order: Free Council

                                                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 6, Resolve 5
                                                Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 6, Stamina 5
                                                Social Attributes: Presence 2, Manipulation 2, Composure 4

                                                Mental Skills: Academics (Journalism) 3, Crafts (Engineering) 4, Computer 3, Medicine 2, Occult 2, Politics 1, Investigation (Scoops) 3, Science (Biology) 4
                                                Physical Skills: Athletics (Climbing, Gymnastics) 6, Brawl 4, Drive 1, Firearms (Web-Slingers) 2, Larceny 2, Stealth (Ambush) 3, Survival 2, Weaponry (Improvised) 4
                                                Social Skills: Animal Ken 1, Empathy 3, Expression (Humor) 3, Intimidation (Bluff) 3, Persuasion (Diplomacy) 3, Socialize (Office) 2, Streetwise (New York City) 3, Subterfuge (Excuses) 3

                                                Merits: Virtuous, Ambidextrous, Encyclopedic Knowledge (Crafts, Science, Expression), Area Of Expertise (Scoops, Engineering), Interdisciplinary Specialty (Humor, Gymnastics, Engineering), Patient, Common Sense, Indomitable, Professional Training 3 (Journalist: Empathy, Investigation, Persuasion), Trained Observer 3, Danger Sense, Direction Sense, Tolerance For Biology, Demolisher 3, Double Jointed, Disarm*, Iron Stamina 3, Fleet Of Foot 3, Fast Reflexes 3, Hardy 3, Parkour 5, Improvised Weapons 3, Martial Arts 3, Firefight 3 (Web-Slingers), Unarmed Defense 5, Quick Healer, Allies 3/Status 1 (Daily Bugle), Allies 3/Status 2 (Free Council), Contacts 6 (Journalists, Researchers, Law Enforcers, Free Council, Guardians Of The Veil, Adamantine Arrow), Resources 2, Striking Looks +1, Alternate Identity 1 (Spider-Man), Fame 2 (Spider-Man), Fixer, Iron Will, Inspiring, Pusher, Sympathetic, True Friend (Mary Jane, Aunt May), Web-Slinger Style 5**

                                                *Spidey normally disarms opponents with his webs, or fists hardened with Life spells. He can do it with Weaponry too.

                                                **Web-Slinger Style

                                                Eight Eyes (•): Web-Slinging is practically the same as walking for Spidey. Most difficulties that people experience when trying to use the web-slingers never apply to him. Ignore environmental penalties to Perception rolls and Range penalties equal to your dots in Web-Slinger for the purposes of the namesake. Other Perception rolls and Ranges are not affected.

                                                Multi-Purpose (••): Spidey knows very well that the web-slingers aren’t just for swinging around and tying people up. They’re also useful for keeping ramshackle repairs together, making traps, quick patch-ups for wounds and creative expression. He is considered to have an Interdisciplinary Specialty of Web-Slinging with Area Of Expertise built-in, even if the Skill in question is not Mental, lower than 3, or lowered from 3 through whatever means.

                                                Pin-Point (•••): Spidey can calculate angles and distance with lightning speed and amazing precision, especially for disarming and stunning. Every 2 dice he subtracts from his Dexterity + Firearms pool becomes a +1B for attacks with webs. An Exceptional Success on this roll also inflicts a fitting Tilt, usually Arm Wrack, Leg Wrack, Knocked Down or Immobilized. Inflicting the Blinded Tilt doesn’t stand to gain with a reduced dice pool, as it only requires Spidey to roll Dexterity + Firearms – 3 for targeting the opponent’s head.

                                                Arachnid Focus (••••): If he completely concentrates on a target, Spidey is virtually guaranteed to hit as intended. Instead of gaining +2 or more when making an All-Out Attack with the web-slingers, he may receive the Rote Quality. He may use this maneuver at the same time as the previous one, but doing so requires 1 willpower point.

                                                Ultimate Web-Spinning (•••••): It is often when a split second can mean the difference between life and death that Spidey makes the most of his web fluid/Mana. He spends 1 willpower point, and all Mana costs for Reach and mitigation of Paradox are halved, round down. This maneuver may be combined with Arachnid Focus OR Pin-Point. The situation does need to be so drastic for him to use this maneuver.


                                                Health: 10
                                                Willpower: 9
                                                Defense: 6 + Athletics/Brawl/Weaponry
                                                Initiative: 12
                                                Speed: 20
                                                Gnosis: 6
                                                Wisdom: 8
                                                Arcana: Life 3, Matter 3, Forces 2, Fate 2, Time 2, Space 2, Mind 2
                                                I actually once came up with a concept for Spider-Man as a changeling: dual-kith Skitterskulk/Steepscrambler. The "thief" who he ignored in the course of his durance and later shot his Uncle Ben was actually his own imperfect fetch.
