                                    As for Primordial Pathways, it seems to be a Wits+Resolve roll with an instant action to open a Pathway for a character's Resolve in turns, though it ends up penalised if not in the Primordial Dream, the Temeros or having Lair Traits enacted at this newly made entrance.

                                    The Skeleton Key is more expensive... It needs a point of Willpower, then it's a contested roll against the Supernatural Tolerance of the gate's creator. Sure, it's Resolve+Occult+Lair as the Beast's pool, though there is more chance of the Key failing, especially if trying to use the Key on a supernatural with a higher tolerance. It seems more like that it could be used for busting into another Beast's Lair, though I do not see it as a way to make a Primordial Pathway to your Beast's own Lair, rather into other Beast's Lairs if you wanted to do so.

                                                Angelica give a brief hiss before continuing on, sniffing the acidic for a scent, for anything.

                                                I know what you seek but why do you seek it? You have no blood relation. You have no romantic relation. Is it vengeance you're after? One last hangnail to clip? One last scab to rip open?

                                                The Beast gave a roar as it punched into the left wall of the blood soaked corridor. Ripping and tearing with all it's might into the room beyond. It slowly work it's way inside before being confronted with another corridor.

                                                The princess, or I should say Hero, is in another castle.

                                                She howled in frustration. She spend time unknown crawling in this maze of a mind. The being that called it's self The Deconstructionist had the Hero she wanted and was refusing to give it up.

                                                The exit is right behind you. It always was.

                                                She continued, sniffing the air, hunting for her prey.

                                                And yet... You continue. Why? Whywhywhywhywhywhy?
                                                Subverting you own Myth perhaps? Changing the actors and twisting the roles? Can Fate handle that? Does Fate break that?

                                                Angelica turned a corner to see polished reinforced glass and beyond it lay her prize. A broken figure thrashed and writhed upon a table while a darken one operated.

                                                Such weak prey, these Heroes are. Reminds me of the people that I once worked with. Cheap. Fearful. Unable to do what needed to be done to gain the knowledge to wield to fight back. Blinded by self righteous morality when the world is being set upon by monsters outside. Is it not logical to throw those who are unable to see into the maw of the enemy to gain knowledge? Is it not logical to rip the secrets of your enemies out by any means necessary?

                                                The Beast growled and began to claw into the glass, it's talons punching barely punching through it. The Slasher continued his work below.

                                                After a while, the mind starts to see patterns. Roles, actors. themes, and moods. You start being able to grasp just what strings are being toyed with and occasionally subvert the roles. Is it wrong to dismiss the lives of the worthless that were serving no purpose anyways? Is it wrong to use those who were just occupying space? Your kind after all dines on terror and horror. You above all should understand.

                                                The Beast climbed into the hole that it had made only to find it's self confronted with another glass wall. A foot closer to it's prey.

                                                So why are you here? The monomyth has been subverted. The Hero is being saved by the Beast and thus is not a Hero, if he ever was at all.

                                                Anglica redoubled her efforts, smashing and breaking her fists into a bloody stumps. She smelled death in the air, she smelled impending doom. She broke through glass room by glass room. Each just a little bit closer, each just beyond her reach.

                                                Do you not feel it? The man before me has lost his status. He is nothing more than a broken wreak. A shell. There is nothing heroic about him. No desire to harm you now.

                                                The Beast finally had the slasher in the same room as it. It raised it's bloody claw to strike.

                                                Why are you here? I am just killing a man.

                                                The Slasher's body was crushed as Anglica brought her claw down upon it. The edges of the corpse hissed as the dreamscape quivered and wavered. She grabbed the groaning body of the man and took a step back. She felt the rushing wind of the Astral as she was ejected from the dream.

                                                Somewhere in the world, a man in a coma stirred to wakefulness.

                                                Somewhere in the world, a Beast roused it self to the material.

                                                Somewhere in the world, a Slasher awoke and reaches for a set of notes, the answer to his question escapes him still.
