    Ehhh, Pandemonium isn't really "Hell" Hell, y'know? More in the Jacob's Ladder (the movie) sense, in that it's inhabited by the "demons" of your psyche, who cut out the diseased parts of your soul. And vampires are walking corpses that drink the blood of the living and can't abide the touch of sunlight (a powerful symbol of Life).     Honestly, I think the idea of what "Hell" is has changed as nWoD has evolved, because now, "Hell"'s wherever a Demon sets up shop, and the concept of a "demon" in general is quite different, as well...

    But Vampires aren't really fueled by death, in any event. They're untouched by it. They in fact are noted for how little they tend to have to do with the dead or spirits. They are the ones that most fit the Mastigos archetype, as well.

    Basically, before Mummy overloaded it, you could argue a creature a reflection of each Supernal Realm:
    Aether - Promethian (by way of Stealing The Divine Fire)
    Pandemonium - Vampire
    Arcadia - Changeling
    Primal Wild - Werewolf
    Stygia - Geist

                Well, Demon is clearly in its own league, especially since they kept demons that are just a subset of spirits and demons that are robots with the same name.

                As for conflicts, then on the basics, Hunter is clearly playing by different rules, since everything runs on a more streamlined system.

                As for the grand metaphysics, they're generally vague enough that individuals can interpret things as they will (including the outright "here, pick an origin story for the world, it's multiple choice!" for Werewolf) but if you're playing a game of, say, Changeling, they have stories for how all the stuff relating to Arcadia and True Fae came about, and then a giant freakin' hole to explain the fact that there are also werewolves and spirits and other things that Changeling doesn't comport with.

                For that matter, Changeling glamour comes from human emotions and dreams. Spirit essence comes from emotions and dreams. These two things, however, do not interact with each other in any way, and the two types of creatures seem outright blind to one another's existence at times. It kind of begs an explanation why, if there have been Fae and Spirits visiting Earth for time immemorial, they've still somehow never gotten to so much as acknowledging the existence of one another. For that matter, Changelings frequently manipulate the emotional landscape, which SHOULD have a massive impact on spirits and spirit feeding, and have no means to combat spirits or even detect their presence, and werewolf and changeling turfs don't overlap, but there never seems to be a spirit problem for all the things that Changelings do. (Even if glamour harvests screw with essence accumulation, it's heavily implied that Summers, for example, will brew far more anger than they actually harvest...) Needless to say, if you look back and forth between spirits/Werewolf and Changeling, (or try a crossover game,) it seems there's a gap in the explanations.

                Meanwhile, the deathless Fae DO have a connection to the Underworld and ghosts... for some reason, even though proper ghosts don't and probably can't exist in the Hedge.

                In fact, this thread opened up with a question about how or whether the origin stories of Werewolves and Mages can be told in the same shared space. I mean, at some point, someone's going to TEST these sorts of theories, and those tests will have results, no?

                    I don't see why a ghost can't exist in the Hedge, though I can see them not really forming there...it would need to be brought or get really lost.

                    Stuff like the Arcadias being the same (or not) and such are cool and interesting, and never going to be confirmed as true. To have it be official just limits options, and New WoD is all about modular.

                    It IS interesting there are two sorts of Demons (three counting Lilim) who do not seem associated with Pandemonium...there are the Spirit type Demons associated with the Lower Depths and of course the Unchained who are associated with...earth for lack of something better. And the Lilim.

                    As for the original question, it does seem to speak of some half-remembered event, which is interesting. "A long time ago, something happened and shit got real. This lesser world is the result. Is a sign things went horribly awry? Or that darkness was contained to the point that it lingers only in the shadows and it was a victory." Is an interesting question to go poking at and an unlikely one to be answered.
