    My guess is that the Primordial may refer to the mythical ancestor of the race, and if you believe the Beasts all human-born(-or-directly-related) supernatural beings; it is his/her blood that gives humans the power to defy humanity, to become something somewhat less but infinitely grander.

    Off the top of my head, I'd say the birth splats are similar to the Fears from the Fear Mythos, embodying both a primal phobia and a more cerebral version thereof.

    Probably:

    Predation: On the one level, this is the fear of being eaten, the monkey hiding in the trees from hungry lions. It is also the fear of human Lions, and the loss of control to someone who gets it because they were stronger you, the nerd avoiding the bullies. The "brute" splat (though I expect that more intelligent, subtle members will be represented, 2nd Edition seems to have realized playing a dumb thug ain't fun).

    Darkness: There are things that lurk in the dark, whether wild animals, criminals, or simply pits you didn't see until you fell in. At the same time, you wish you actually knew less, because your mind invents sources of the info in your other senses; you can hear human panting, the crack of twigs, that little book bound in strange leather that you swear whispers. The very fact you can't see it makes it worse, especially if you catch glimpses out of the corner of your eye. The unknown is here, and all you know is that it is out to get you. The "spy" splat (because spies are masters of this as a matter of survival; far better you look at the scary sound over there, instead of the ninja over here).

    Isolation: Humans are a social species, it's true. That's why prison is so dreaded, actually, being stuck in the center of a cage with nothing to keep you company but grey walls and guards trained to very pointedly ignore you if that remains an option. It's also why ostracism is so feared, why the loneliest place is the center of the crowd. Having lost everyone, you have nothing but your thoughts...at least, you think they're your thoughts. Didn't shamans believe something about self-imposed exile opening themselves to other worlds? The "mystic" splat (existing apart from society to fully examine themselves and whoever they bring in their orbit).

    Nature: Related to Predation, the fear of natural processes differs in how personal it is; namely, not at all. The Big Bad Wolf does not eat Grandma because he wants to revel in your grief, he eats her because you pointed him to a way to survive another week or so, easily. The hurricane does not kill you because it hates you, it kills you because you were in the way of a tree it was blowing about. Considering that, how important are you (and the rest of humanity) in the grand view of things? The "scholar" splat (studying the amoral processes of the world),

    Entropy: Disease, moral decay, corruption, anarchy-all are built around the fear of things tomorrow being worse than they were today, and that they will never get better. Try as you might, the Second Law of Thermodynamics will have its due, all things are slouching towards heat death, and while it's an open question about whether something will be born from the universe's corpse, it will be something quite unlike anything anyone will recognize, and likely something that thinks the very concept of something before itself is ridiculous. The "social" splat (after all, really far-reaching entropy is subtle).
