    Conspiracy Theory: Tending the Fire

    06-18-2023, 01:32 PM
    Me: *working on my new M:tAw homebrew*

    knock on the door

    Me: "Come in!"

    door busts open

    Hunter: "How dare you!" *throws Tending the Fire right to my face*

    Me: *falling over to the floor*

    moment of silence

    Me: *getting up* "Fine, fine, I'll talk about the companion.."

    So, overall, Tending the Fire has a lot of good things in it. The Social currency system is \really cool, and a lot of the new orgs are very interesting. However, there is a bit of an.. issue. It could be the wordcount confinement, some external pressure or something else, but some parts of the book feel very rough around the edges. This mostly impacts, imo, the conspiracies- most compacts manage to maintain a cohesive and strong theme through their writeup even with the limited wordcount (well, with one notable exception... Yes I'm looking at you Elden Covenant. No wonder you are on the brink of breaking into two compacts- you are two compacts). Conspiracies, on the other hand, feel like they got the short end of the stick. While some manage to do very well even with their limited wordcount, some feel more like compacts than conspiracies, some feel very conceptual, and some have some big question marks about how they operate or even exists. For example, I love the Survivors Club, and I love their Endowments, but their presentation and name really makes them feel like a compact- and believe me, names are important to make sure whether a group feels like a compact or a conspiracy. Also, as we have explored before in this series (in case you missed it), conspiracies generally tend to play on RL conspiracy theories and shadowy organizations. The Catholic Church, the Illuminati, the Templars, the Hashashins, Zaibatsu, Gnostics, Satanic Panic, Men in Black... everything is all about that paranoia and turning it on its head, while still remaining true to the conspiracy roots. Sure, not every conspiracy plays on the same themes to the same level, but it feels like most of the conspiracies in TtF neglect that theme all together. Again, let me be clear- this is not a bad book and I enjoy a lot of it, but the places it in my opinion fails are just very frustrating. It makes me feel like I need to put a lot of job to make some of those groups playable (and together with the fact that most of the conspiracies get around 5 Endowments it also extends to the mechanics), which is not what I hoped it would be.

    But since when I am not just throwing wordcount on Hunter for no good reason?

    So yeah, generally this series was about analyzing the LOOOOOORE that the different conspiracies have and present some wacky theory about what is their true agenda and goals, and how do they seek to change the world. The problem is that those conspiracies, well, don't really have lore- again, limited wordcount and other reasons, basically they are all very straightforward. As such, in here I am going to do some heavy rebuilding instead- well, depending on the conspiracy, some will not need it as much as others. Basically, I will present my own personal take, what are my issues, and I try to resolve them, by using the same paradigm I've used to define conspiracies earlier. I hope that it would also help other people who had issues with the writeups- and at worst case scenarios, it would help me rebuilding the conspiracies into something which is useful to me, and isn't it what truly important in homebrew? :P

    Anyway, Conspiracy Theory season 2- here we go!


    Asclepion

    My personal favorite conspiracy from TtF, I think that Asclepion manages to preform the best among all of the new conspiracies. Name? Check. Strange origin? Check. Filling a missing niche in the Vigil? Check. Overall, Asclepion do well what they are meant to do- a secretive organization dedicated for saving human lives in the Vigil, without needing to pay tons of money to Cheiron Health Insurance. It is clear where do they come from, why do they have Endowments, what their Endowments are meant to be, what is their Vigil and how their Endowments work to help their Vigil. Overall, very solid writeup, very good, A+. As such, unlike the other conspiracies in the book, I don't feel like I need to do some heavy rebuilding to the conspiracy and that I have enough lore to actually start and piece out what this conspiracy is really all about- because let me tell you, the writeup may give us a lot about the conspiracy, but it also opens up more questions- and in a good way.

    Let's start with what we know- the (modern) version of Asclepion is very young, being originated around WW2. They are also, unsurprisingly, Greek, and we know that they had an alliance with the AKD which helped them to become a conspiracy. Their Endowments also seem to be connected to the hero-god Asklepios- or, at least, the a temple dedicated to the deity. They hold a religious undertone which is very dominant within the conspiracy, with one of the factions being dedicated to the mystical angle. As for the Endowments themselves, we know that gaining them requires some kind of meditation/detachment from reality, religious symbolism in relation for healing (be it deities, sacred jewelry or something along those lines), and water- and that those who leave the conspiracy slowly lose their Endowments. This already give us some strong hints about the nature of their Endowments- that it is religious, related to water, disconnects the hunter from reality and is a collective act. Also, can we just note what a miss it is that their logo does not include the Rod of Asclepius? I mean,. come on! Low hanging fruit, guys!

    Aaaaaaanyway, a sort research quickly explains a lot of what we see about Enkoimesis- its name basically refers to a ancient healing practice used by the ancient temples of Asclepios , which also involved dream incubation as part of the ritual alongside the use of opium and similar materials during a 'temple sleep'- which are the circumstances under which the Endowment was discovered during WW2. Basically, Enkiomesis is very straightforward- it is the treatment that the ancient priests have used to heal their patients. The water symbolism, again, is nothing extraordinary- it is a tool for purification, such as the healing baths used by the temples during ancient Greece. Overall, nothing special here. Sure, the weird thing is that it works, but there are documents that show that the temples seem to have actually being not bad in saving lives in the time, and this if the CofD, so magic does work and rituals have power. Nothing weird in here, then. Just weird magic doing weird magic stuff.

    Except...

    Noticed something strange in this paragraph? Enkiomesis is a ancient method used to heal the patients, not to initiate the priests. The ones who are sick should go through Enkiomesis, not the healers- and that means either one of two things: either in the CofD, the texts of the temples have been distorted, or, the more interesting option, that in order to heal others, the hunter themselves must first be healed from.... something. From what, it is not clear yet- but just something to keep in mind that by joining the conspiracy, the hunter gets cleansed of something, and through that purification they are able to become the healers who would save life during the Vigil.

    Now, it should be noted that it is really not clear whether Enkiomesis requires a "recharge" by going through the purification rite on a regular basis or that it needs only one time to get initiated. I am currently going with the second interpretation as the text does not explicitly mentions it is needed either in the setting material or in the mechanics, so it is an assumption that we are doing here- and which has implications. Why? Because if it requires a regular "recharge", it would make sense about how people are losing their Endowments after leaving the conspiracy. However, if it is a one time initiation, something else is going on- something which is probably related to the whole dream incubation thing that those hunters have going for them.

    Basically, when you are talking about something along the line of "dream incubation" in the CofD, the immediate association is the Astral- the collective soul of humanity. That sense of collectivity and religious aspects all resonate very strongly with the Astral, as it seems that the hunters get detached from the mortal world and enter a meditative state. While the book does not explicitly mention it, Enkiomesis was used in 2 different manners by the priests- either as a replacement of anesthesia or (the case which is more relevant considering the whole dream incubation thing) to be visited by Asclepios or gain visions from the god, which were later interoperated by the priests. Taking this into account, it seems that the process of the dream incubation is meant to commune with the force which the conspiracy calls "Asklepios" and gain their blessings- which makes Asklepios, in this instance, a symbol or manifestation of collective healing. We also know that this entity does not depend on the collective in order to exist, as the temples of Asklepios were abandoned for quite a while (hence why the conspiracy is young), yet we need to assume that the origins of Asclepion are much older considering the history established behind the practices the conspiracy uses. Considering the unstable nature of the Astral and how well it is shaped by humanity, it makes me think that maybe some ancient Astral entity which serves as the collective perception of healing may not be the best candidate. It should be something more... stable. More symbolic. Supernal, perhaps? Asklepios as the Supernal symbol of healing may actually fit the conspiracy, especially with the whole religious and mystical practices, but then we have the little problem of the Abyss being a gashing and horrifying ocean which breaks the Supernal from the Fallen. You know, a small issue, nothing of importance- but it does make you wonder: if Asklepios heals the initiate from something, and Asklepios is a Supernal entity, could it be that they heal the hunters from the Curse of Sleep? Something to think about.

    Anyway, whether we are talking about an Astral collective or a Supernal symbol, this may solve the Endowments of the conspiracy- but not the conspiracy itself. After all, there is something.. funky going on in here. The AKD allying with the early Asclepion? Why? I mean, sure "we are all on team humanity" and all of that nonsense- but let's be serious for a second. Conspiracies don't like one another. Conspiracies are very jealous for their secrets. Having a young conspiracy in your home base is the perfect opportunity to assimilate them into your structure and steal their secrets for yourself. Heck, their bases are found in the heart of the AKD's control- even though it is worth to mention that Greece is, om ,many ways, the "cradle of conspiracies". Apparently that's what happens when you have way too many mystery cults. The AKD, Cheiron, the Promethean Brotherhood and maybe even the Cainite Heresy could track their origin to the region- with Cheiron being a yet very important conspiracy in that context. After all, Cheiron is not only another conspiracy of Greek origin that has connection to medicine, but Asclepios in mythology was the student of Chiron, from which he learned the art of healing. However, where Cheiron went to a more "scientific" methodology, Asclepion is much more religious in nature. Still, even if we are talking about it from a modern POV, we still have a strong issue in modern times- Cheiron is a business, after all, and what Asclepion offers is, in fact, a way to ruin the pharmacy business. The very risk should make the Board of Directories to at least move uncomfortably in their chairs. Kinda makes you think about how in mythology Hades requested Asclepios's death as he feared he would rob him from all of the souls as he was just so damn good in his job. Maybe something worthy to put a pin on.

    It should be noted that a alliance between conspiracies is not unheard of- even though it is still relatively rare. For example, we do know that VASCU and TFV do use similar terms, which shows that at least some cooperation took place while VASCU was still part of the government. A more clear example, however, comes in the from of the MM and the Lucifuge- where, again, we have 2 conspiracies who are very close to each other geographically and that have some very close connection as their leaders are in very good relationship. From this we may deduce what are the requirements for two conspiracies to actually work together- first, common theology (both the Lucifuge and the MM are Catholic and agree on the origin of evil), personal attachments (the Lady and Baudolino), and agreement on the goal (punch Satan in the face). Sure, they may disagree on a lot of things, but as long as the MM can accept the existence of the Lucifuge and have a place for them as "not immediately evil" and the Lucifuge remain Catholic enough (or at least Christian), they conspiracies can work together. As a result, we can learn a lot about Asclepion by learning from the closest ally, the AKD.

    The main thing that we know about the AKD theology is that it is Minoan- the Labyrinths, the Mistress of Honey and Asterion, the Flood, all of those are very Minoan in essence. We also know that they are based in Athens, and that the story about the Minotaur is viewed as a preservation of an historic conflict between the ancient Mycenean and the Minoans, which ended with Mycenean victory, so we have some assimilation of cultures here. Anyway, we can fully agree that the AKD are not Monotheistic, and likely are related to Greek mystery cults. Basically, the AKD are pagans, and that already provides a common frame for them to work with Asclepion. The fact that Asclepion does not employ relics in their rituals already means that they do not fight against one another. However, the fact that Asclepion does not target monsters who are not violent towards humans in general kinda puts them on less stable ground with the AKD, who targets witches. However, there is something else that we need to remember about the AKD- they are obsessed with the past. They collect relics, preserve texts, archive ancient treasures of the past- and, in a way, Asclepion is a living relic. They are a piece of uncovered history of Greek origin, of the pagan era. They are precious in the same manner that every relic is precious- they are a piece of Greek history, and as such part of humanity's inheritance- and as such, should be "owned" and protected by the AKD. This also means protecting the practice from the unworthy hands- the AKD probably hate themselves for letting something as dangerous as the Rite of Hecate to run around, not to mention the thought of the practice falling to the hands of witches, or witches infiltrating the conspiracy. They needed guidance to avoid compromise. They needed to be protected, like any relic does. And so, the AKD worked hard to make sure that Asclepion will not fall again.

    But why did they fall in the first time?

    Well, the easy answer is probably Christianity. I mean, the Therapuetae of Asclepios are probably the historical origin of the conspiracy, being the healer cult of Asclepios in the ancient era. The short lived Cult of Glycon and the College of Asclepios and Hygeia may also have been reincarnations of the conspiracy. Still, Christianity is known to adapt things to its arsenal when needed- the Aves Minerva have became the Cainite Heresy, for example, and pagan organizations have survived the ancient period. Sure, many have also died out, so it is nothing too weird- but we are in a Conspiracy Theory thread, which means that things are always murky and we take no easy answers. Heck, such healing rituals could be very useful for Christian hunters, and the Rod and Snakes of Asclepios are, well, everywhere! No, something killed the conspiracy, and it wasn't Christianity.

    I claim it was Cheiron.

    Again, Cheiron are also a healer cult of Greek origin- which means that they compete over the same niche. However, for Cheiron healing means business, while for Ascleipon healing means religion. Cheiron sew the pieces of monsters together to heal their subjects and create heroes, Asclepion cleanse their patients from all supernatural effect. Cheiron seeks to control. Asclepion seeks to provide. The two organizations are, by nature, hostile to one another- but they are also strongly related to one another. Asclepios was a student of Chiron- so what if it is true in thsi case as well? What if the Asclepion have splintered from Chieron long time ago? What if Asklepion is in fact related to Cheiron- maybe it is a rogue member of the Board of Directors? Maybe it is a Deviant created by the early conspiracy, or even a "finished product", a person cured from the Contagion and that now works to cleanse all of humanity? Maybe he was a witch? Cheiron are rumored to have worked with those after all. It could be that Asclepios was a mage student of the powers behind Cheiron who Ascended through the art of healing? Could explain why we see his symbols everywhere. Or perhaps Enkiomesis is the magic behind Thaumatech, the ritual that allows unnatural flesh to bind together against all reason- a specific application of a wider system. Cheiron may have taught him the art of healing as part of a trade, yet Asclepios have abused his arts, saving too many souls, causing imbalance. Death is a trade as well- this is what Cheiron knows and Asclepios ignores. Hades is both the Lord of the Dead and the King of the Treasures Below. Asclepios violated the trade between Life and Death- and if he was a mage, that means violating the very symbols of Tyranny represented by the Chancellor. This was his challenge for Ascension- and Cheiron, from their point of view, might have seen it as a threat to the world. And agreement was reached, the Pax Arcana was preserved. Asclepios Ascended- but his organization was to be destroyed. Trade must be honored, and the Therapuetae have violated it by saving too many lives.

    And that means that even in modern times, the Asclepion is destined to meet their enemies once more- yet where they are still building their power, Cheiron are the movers and shakers of the world. And the only thing they have by their side is their devotion, alliance with the KAD- and the power of a god. Still not enough to defeat capitalism imo, but hey what do I know.

    Anyway- let's sum it up. Asklepios was a student of the powers behind Cheiron during ancient Greece, probably an Awakened but not necessary, who managed to exceed the expectations of Cheiron by generalizing the forces which allow Thaumatech to work into a wider system, and then used the same system in order to become a symbol of healing and Ascend simply because the Chancellor could not have accepted his existence in the Fallen. His arts were so useful that when they spread across the ancient world, they saved so many lived that the balance of the world was at risk, allowing the Contagion to start sipping through the cracks. Cheiron, finding the act as against their mission and a disruption of the "laws of trade", acted to destroy the cult in the first opportunity they had. Millenia later, WW2 happens, bombs drop, the ancient secret is uncovered, Asklepios returns and moves again in order to manage and achieve his goal... healing the world.

    From what?

    Again, this is the main issue that we have. From what the initiation heals people? The Contagion? Could make sense but also a lazy answer, as Cheiron are already doing it (at least, according to the theory- a CONSPIRACY THEORY). But if Asklepion is indeed an Ascended being as I claim, the answer should indeed be the Curse of Sleep. He can't provide Awakening- as he is not a Watchtower and has no control over them. However, as long as members of the conspiracy are part of his symbol through the initiation ritual, then they are cursed from Sleep. His goal, in that case, would be to heal the world- the first step was becoming the symbol of healing, the second is to allow that symbol of heal people from the curses and illnesses of the world, the third should be to envelope the whole world under his own symbol. The endgame should probably, in that case, to heal the gaping wound in the fabric of the multiverse- the Abyss itself. This, in turn, would provide Ascension to the world- creating a world without diseases, without pain, without death. The Underworld and the Depths below it would be gone, the tyrants of Heaven would fall from their thrones, the Contagion would die out. A new world, a healthy world, a perfect world. A world where the Halls of hades are empty and humanity is free from pain. Not a new Atlantis- but something greater. Obviously, that means that a lot of entities will be against this vision- the Exarchs, the Abyss, Inferno, the Chthonic Gods, the Contagion, the Board of Directors, the Judges. The list goes on. That means that the followers of Asklepios would have to tackle each of those, one by one, removing those flaws from the world. Sure, it would take time- but what is a time for a Ascended being?

    Or, maybe, there is a different way- perhaps Asklepios's whole plan is to instigate a War in Heaven by having his cult creating the imbalance of souls. Getting the Chthonic gods mad, the Judges mad, Ammut mad, Inferno mad. Too many are alive, not enough die. The ancient laws are not being obeyed, the Pax Arcana being violated- and from that chaos, maybe, just maybe, the new world will be forged, as long as Aklepios would win. Otherwise... well let's ignore that possibility. But Cheiron doesn't ignore. They can't ignore. The ancient trade must be protected- and if that means fighting their own disci0ple, so be it.

    Or that Cheiron are just capitalistic bastards who wants monopoly over life and death by themselves- but where is the fun in that?

                                                  The Engimatics

                                                  Disclaimer- everything in this post is my own personal opinion. I am aware that not every group is for everyone and that the writing team have put a lot of thought around designing and writing the organizations in the book, under the confinements of what seems to be a very stressed wordcount. I am just someone who have written too much Hunter homebrew for their own good, and do not mean any disrespect to those who have invested time and mental resources in making the book. I am going to be as clear and constructive about my opinions here as possible, and if there are some "harsh" languages it is probably meant as a humoristic tool and is not intended against the writing team. I am also aware that there are people who do not share the same criticisms as I do and it is completely fine- it is a RPG game, and what works for one person may not work for another. I am judging this products according to my own needs and view about Hunter, that's all.

                                                  So yeah, I assume that you could note hat if I have put that disclaimer up there, I may have some strong feelings about the Enigmatics.

                                                  Note- I have no problem with the concept. Actually, I love the concept. This conspiracy is basically the Third Tier form of the Wilde Society from Dream Catchers, just without the fey connections. They are a group of artists who seek to be inspired by the supernatural and ends up making some cursed artwork- nothing bad in that! More than that, it is actually a good thing- between the tropes about "mad artists" and the corruption that takes place in the Arts Market, we really have a place for a conspiracy here. Artistic movements deal with some of the richest, most powerful people in the world, after all- it is not hard to imagine that there is something dark happening in the arts market in the CofD, and that a conspiracy may form from those who could use their art to inspire whatever they see fit. All of that works, at the conceptual level.

                                                  My issue is that it feels like it remains as a concept.

                                                  The first red flag is the name- I mean, I am sure that the team had reasons to leave the name like that. I guess that they wanted it to ring like "Surrealists" or "Classicists" or something along those lines. You know, make it feel like a art movement- and it works as a artistic movement. Enigmatic Art could totally be a thing- but that returns us to the same problem that we had with CWS. The fact that something works for an artistic movement does not mean it works for a conspiracy- and most artistic movements are not so cohesive to actually create something we could call a "conspiracy". They rarely have the numbers for it, and most modern and post modern movements are so scattered and fractured and individualistic. In most times the term is used, it is meant to represent a style- I can make a art of a certain style without being belong to a certain movement. Oh, sure, the arts world have a lot of clicks and academies and institutions- that's why it does make a good foundation for a conspiracy. But the name does not reflect some global institution. It reflects an artistic style. And that's a problem because I don't think it works for a conspiracy, as even fractured conspiracies such as the Les Mysteres and the Council of Bones have actual names for them. And names are important both in character and out of character. it is the first impression you get on a group, and when that first impression is "not really a name at all", well... yeah. Enigmatic Art is a good name for a artistic movement. It is not a good name for a 100 year old occult society. It is barely a name at all.

                                                  Put a pin on that. We are going to return to it later.

                                                  Anyway, let's get back to business. We know that the Enigmatics lack a cohesive history- the writeup suggests that they have been previously a pretty fractured group, with different cells being descendant from different artistic movements, probably as artists had encounters with the darkness which inspired them to create, yet during the last century (probably as globalization and sharing of information became more common), those different groups have started to gather together in order to form an actual conspiracy- or something close enough to it. Again, Hunter has an history of fractured conspiracies, but generally they always have something that binds them together, be it a special social network such as the Chain of the Ascending Ones or an occult force such as spirits for Les Mysteres. We also know that some groups had access to their Endowments before the conspiracy was established- what a conspiracy does is to "mass produce" and "redefine" the chaotic supernatural abilities into a system. That's what separates, let's say, the Lucifuge from the L'Enfant Diabolique, or the exiled witches of Britain from the Knights of St George, or even the mad alchemists of Promethean from, the Ascending Ones. We have, after all, already established in previous posts how Endowments seems to be a process in which a "monster" becomes a "person" within the Code- and that Endowments could, in turn, devolve into Dread Powers when manifested among those who lack that Code, The conspiracy itself claims to be far older than all the artistic styles and movements which currently exist, being some sort of a "primordial art", which is as old as humanity...

                                                  A primordial art crafted by mad artists which produce cursed works of art through some divine inspiration. Why does that sound so familiar?...

                                                  Mummy: starts crawling out of its grave

                                                  No! No! Bad evil corpse! Go back or I'll send a Shulpae upon you!

                                                  Where was I? Ha, yes. The Decieved.

                                                  Like, honestly, it is not that hard to put two and two together to understand that whatever the Enigmatics are playing with is very similar to Seba and the arts of the Deceived. Now, it is not limited for it, obviously- the Divine Fire is another prime candidate for mad inspiration, and one of the example Hunters seems to have created a Pandoran by accident, but the fundamental cursed aspects of Masterworks, and how the key power behind them being the Inspiration which motivated the artist, very much says "yeah, those people are influenced by some starry presence to make them create things that everyone will regret later". The Enigmatics are the product of many disparate Art Cults, probably sponsored by the Decieved to a degree, or even sprouting spontaneously as the mind of the artist degrades due to them encountering the supernatural (meeting new monsters every time is not good for your mental health by the Code and Integrity, after all). Basically, Inspiration is likely the product of a "Seba seed" lurking in their souls, or at least a cultivation of Sekhem through artistic measures that resonates with the stars above and motivates them to create, to embody their greatest fears and desires within paint and clay and song. Note that this "inspiration through fear" thing which they have going on also strongly resonates with Beast- the question, in that case, is if the Beast resonance is essential or not. I mean, Beast's always has its claws at everyone's businesses, and artists could always have the theme of the collective subconscious and introducing madness in others. Is Inspiration, in that case, actually being derived from the Dark Mother instead of the songs of Seba? Could be, but Inspiration as Sekhem Sorcery requires less steps on the way to achieve a goal- we know that Sekhem Sorcery feeds upon sacrifice (such as mental health), related to arts and creates cursed objects. The dark rituals of the Begotten also demand sacrifice and are also inspired by fear, but the creation of cursed artworks is the missing links. On the other end, the way that meeting monsters specifically still gives some strong Beast vibes. Thankfully, we are in Conspiracy Theory, we can freely mix concepts together as long as they make something interesting in the end.

                                                  So yeah, it seems that Inspiration is more or less solved- a form of Sekhem Sorcery method derived from Deceived rituals and/or Seba manifestations alongside potentially some Kinship like mechanism that unites the different artists through fear of the darkness and makes them drawn to the darkness. Basically, the Inspiration from Fear aspects of Begotten Rites is used as a gate to cultivate Sekhem within the artist by the sacrifice of their own sanity through the Vigil while they make contacts with monsters, which is later manifested through Deceived rituals. Makes sense, as much as anything makes sense in this thread. I think that the inclusion of Beast cosmology in here is important because it helps us solve why those artists who leave the conspiracy lose their Inspiration, even though they remain scarred by it- that is, if the Primordial Dream is the gate for Inspiration, the artist must have their ideals resonating with aspects of the Dream in order to be inspired by it. To turn your back against the conspiracy means, in essence, to deny the ideals that you seek to manifest in your art- the Enigmatics, again, are an idea, a style, a concept. By abandoning the artistic style the defines the conspiracy, you lose access to the primordial Fear which inspires you, which in turn denies you the access required to cultivate Sekhem. The use of Mummy cosmology, in turn, helps us answer another, just as important question about the Engimatics- why they don't actually have a name?

                                                  Because they are Nameless.

                                                  See, I told you we will return to this part.

                                                  Being Nameless is a fundamental part of being immortal- steal a subject's name, and it becomes a object in your hands. Steal your own name, and no one can hold power upon you. by denying themselves a name, the Enigmatics can not be targeted by those who seek to disrupt their work- such as the Arisen and the Judges, and even the Decieved, the masters of True Names. The greatest enigma about the conspiracy is that they have no name of their own, allowing their artists to protect themselves from the jealous, angry, hungry deities which roam the Twilight or hunger below the earth. Still, I would personally, call the conspiracy Enigma instead of the Enigmatics- a name which is not a name, hidden from the world by not being described into words and living merely as Inspiration. heck, what if the artistic style itself is the seba which powers the Inspiration of the conspiracy? And that by keeping it nameless, it can not be tracked an harvested by the Deceived. This could also offer a-Beast independent mechanism for how Inspiration remains exclusive in the hands of the conspiracy (as Fate probably decrees who gains access to this primordial Seba in the first place), but I still can't ignore the itch that there is some Dark Mother aspects to this whole process. Again, Inspiration through Fear is a too strong theme to ignore.

                                                  Anyway, with or without Beast, we have probably solved both Inspiration and how the organization forms and operates (again, Fate likely to have chosen which artists to get Inspired and slowly tied them together- perhaps as a means for the original "Enigma Seba" to fully manifest in reality). However, there is still one missing link in here, which is my main criticism about the conspiracy. Their Vigil.

                                                  Look, I get it that members of the conspiracy can hunt down monsters in a violent manner. I get it that some of them probably do so. The text even suggests how those who do it may do it. However, the very first sentence in the Enemy section is "Enigmatics don’t focus on slaying monsters — or even on seeking new materials for their art. Most are content encountering the supernatural". Can the Enigmatics go around and use their Masterworks to hunt down monsters? Yes, obviously. But the text claims it is not the default. Their default Vigil is "go around, meet new monsters and places, get inspired, go back and make some Endowments". That is, their Endowments are a product of the Vigil, not assistance in it. This is, to me, a very big Red Flag as I firmly believe that Endowments should be tools of the Vigil- they are methods to uphold it. Yet if the Vigil of the Enigmatics is "getting inspired", and Masterowrks are the result of "getting inspired", they are not directly in assistance for their own Vigil. Sure, you could say "oh the Vigil is dangerous, they are using those Endowments in order to protect themselves from monsters or control their targets so they could be safe while getting inspired by them". To which I ask "yes, but is it the prime objective of those Endowments?", because it seems to me that those Endowments are a product of the Vigil, the result of hunting down a monster. Also- Important Red Flag- the artists does NOT controls the outcome of the Masterwork. Sure, the player does, but not the artist. We know it because if they succeed at creating a Masterwork, they gain insight to the Masterwork's agenda. This is stated directly in the description text- those objects have an agenda, and it is an evil agenda. Enigma is not under any control over its own action- they are tools at the hand of this primordial scale Seba or Fate or the Dark Mother or the Nameless Gods know what in order to give birth to cursed objects while having no control over their own actions. Something inspires them, causes them to chase the supernatural and create cursed artworks that will harm innocent lives. We know that they have no actual choice in the subject because (a) we know that Inspiration is spontaneous and (b) we know that they are Conditioned with both Inspired and Obsession. They don't actually have a choice in creating. They are not really in choice for pursuing the vigil- they can leave, sure, but they are still Obsessed. Their are slaves to their own creations, to their own inspirations, to the Vigil and the dark forces who abuse it. They must hunt, they must create.

                                                  But they do have one choice.

                                                  What to do with their own cursed creations.

                                                  Look at what we have established- that Masterworks are dangerous. They cause people to get addicted to their own Vices, to encourage cannibalism, turn people into stalkers, steal their memories, demand human sacrifices. They have a will of their own- a cursed will, and they seek to corrupt the world. This is why I think that the true Vigil that Enigma faces against is not towards the monsters- it is towards their own creations. They have to create them- but they can contain them, or use them in ways which are beneficial. Some may use them in ways which contain monsters. Others may seek to entomb them, hide them from the world- or present them only in special, places where they do not cause much harm. Or some may actually want to put them in the open, expose the world to the supernatural- but I assume that they re the minority, and probably the ones who quickly degenerate into madness. Releasing a song of cannibalism to the world or allowing statues to claim human sacrifices is probably not very healthy for your mental health, and those who let their creations roam free will quickly fall under their influence and become slashers or cultists. As a result, I imagine that most of their Vigil is about making sure that their own cursed creations are left satisfied and contained, or, as the writer have said "play with fire" when they decide to use them to actually hunt monsters (I would say "playing with a lava stream" but whatever floats your boat). Their goal is to keep the Masterworks in a place where they won't harm innocents- and stop greedy fingers, such as the AKD or the Deathless, from abusing them.

                                                  Ok, so now we know how the conspiracy works, why they have the name they have (or lack of), what are their Endowments and what is their true Vigil. In that case, what we are left with? Ah yes- their agenda.

                                                  Their "cursed agenda".

                                                  Look, it is very clear that in a vacuum, Engima has no agenda of its own- the Enigmatics create because some external, supernatural force drives them to create. As such, the true agenda of the conspiracy is to not merely satisfy the curses of its masterworks- it is to satisfy the curse of the Inspiration itself. If there is indeed a cosmic level Seba manipulating this whole operation through the stars and Fate, its goal is to manifest- and if the Seba is indeed the artistic style itself, it drives humanity as a collective to perfect the Enigmatic Art style and manifest accordingly. Let is sink for a moment- we are talking about a Seba which takes the form of an artistic style. This means that the power of this Seba would be reality bending- and the curse it would produce would be just as terrifying. This will be the end of the world as we know it. In that case, what would Engima do about it?

                                                  Find a way to contain it.

                                                  Engima are both the hands which would bring the apocalypse and the only ones who may stop it. Them starting to organize in the recent years? It is not merely to share ideas and create more Masterworks- it is to create a system that will bind and contain the Seba, keep it both protected from the Deceived who may try to abuse it and the world from its formation. We know about Neithian Architecture, after all- the use of relics in order to manipulate Sekhem? Well, what if Masterworks are not merely contained in random place? What if they are being distributed in a manner that would create a world-wide Lifeweb of seba in a way that, when the Seba would be finally manifested, it would be trapped, contained, and kept under control. To stop the threat that it would pose upon the world, and cleanse the Art from its own curses. Heck, what if their plan is to truly cure Sekhem, the lifeforce of the world, from curses all together? No more mad statues and paintings which trap souls within them. Seba are, after all, eternal- being drawn from the stars, far from Ammut's hungry maw. Contain the Seba, bind it to Earth, cleanse Sekhem from the touch of the Devourer and make art pure once more. Make the world eternal, free from entropy. Steal the world's true name and make it into an enigma. Make the world nameless.

                                                  And you thought that crafting masterworks is playing with fire.

I really think that shifting their Vigil from monsters to their own creations is the correct way to go, but to each person their own.
