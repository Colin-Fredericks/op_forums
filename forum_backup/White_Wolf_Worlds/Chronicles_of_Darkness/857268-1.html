    Especially if a PC finds that one of their Touchstones is among the 45.
    God Machine needs specific 45* people dead, so it feeds a desperate vampire with occult ritual that shields the vampire from breaking points as long as he kills these specific people in specific way. Figure out what connects victims of the mysterious serial diablerist and stop the chain before he reaches your Touchstone who is on the list!                                         Well, for reference, the character who started this line of thought was already an Elder who was cast out of their prior Domain for having (finally) been proven to have committed Diablerie. They entered the Focus Domain (where the PCs/action takes place) during a period of relative turmoil, and because it had a large contingent of Unaligned to hide amongst. Their previous Diablerie only whetted their taste, as it were, and they were eager to continue their efforts/experiments/addiction. He triggered a few riots, and instigated a few serial murderers to hide his initial abductions amongst. He was also planning on using this to jump-start empower a few thoroughly dominated enforcers.

                                        I made the thread because when I was doing the math involved, it seemed frightfully small and "easy". People are free to take what they want from here for their own villains, though the Humanity checks for Diablerie and Willpower costs (as nofather pointed out), will make sure your serial Diablerist will need to pace themselves.

                                        This was also somewhat instigated/inspired by that one thread that discussed forcing the Embrace. I'll try to link to it when I have more time.

                                                Originally posted by nofather View Post

                                                The other problem is the end result is a sort of top-heavy vampire. They have high Blood Potency but only the Disciplines and Attributes they started with. A neonate could grab them, pull them into the sun and get back inside without harm while the diablerist is soot. Entering any form of vampire society is going to be hard with their obvious signs of diablerism, but also necessary as they now need to feast on Vitae. If you decided to be a loner and just pick away at vampire society you risk Vinculum and exposure, as you don't have the decades and centuries of experience an elder has gained establishing a network of people, supplies and resources.

                                                At least if you commit diablerie on elder vampires you are likely to get some new Disciplines out of it.
                                                Worth noting that the diablerist can pick up a whole load of skill dots. They might not pick up many new Disciplines (Do new vampires spontaneously manifest disciplines as part of the Embrace, or is it something that it's assumed a new character has trained in before play begins?) but those skill dots could be a big help.

                                                Careful selection of the victims would let you get to at least 4 dots in a few useful skills, like politics, which will seriously help you establish yourself.

                                                Definitely something I'd love to see an NPC do - It's a great antagonist for the PCs to disrupt, and an interesting start to a Chronicle could be the PCs escaping from someone trying to do this. And that drawback of being a "top-heavy" vampire as nofather said would make them a pretty good opponent to relatively young vampires.
