                                        Originally posted by Vasilios View Post
                                        So spirits. Don't look 100% like the thing their a spirit of?
                                        As Spirits grow in power, they start to take characteristics from several other things. A very weak Fox Spirit might resemble a Fox in everyway except the intelligent look on it's eyes, while a powerful Fox Spirit, ruler of it's forest, probably acquired characteristics from all small predators inside his turf, while also embodying traits acquired by the actions and expectations of humans and relation to foxes. If a human tribe venerated foxes as a symbol of fertility, said Spirit could have the body of a woman while his head was still of a fox.

                                        In short, a Spirit will acquire features from anything that sustains it with Essence. That's why some of them become Magaths: They eat everything that crosses their path, becoming powerful amalgamations that don't do anything but consume.
