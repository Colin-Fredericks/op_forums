        I'd assume the relative potency of a lesser Exarch, going with the assumption where there are more than 10+1, would scale with the effectiveness and insidiousness of the form of control and tyranny they are. This is why the ten Exarchs are so powerful - their form of tyranny are intrinsically tied to the Arcana themselves, and among them the four Archigenitors are the most successful in the modern world.

        Speaking of which, do we know what form of control the non-Archigenitors are? The Archigenitors are control through religion, violence, nationalism, and surveillance. What exactly are the other six again? I only know they're related intimately to the Arcana.

        And if the Dethroned Queen indeed was an Exarch, what form of control would she have been? Now that she's been ousted, there's at least one thing that cannot be used to tyrannize humanity, unless another Exarch Ascended to take that niche. Assuming that didn't happen, what could the Dethroned Queen have been?
