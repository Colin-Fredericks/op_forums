    Time for some more attainments!


    Lesser Utility Attainments

    For those Moros who can't stay away from the battlefield. A staple of the Adamantine Arrow, this attainment is often critizised for "endangering the sanity of its users"
    Death: The Harvest
    As the living leave the world, those attuned to the mysteries of Death may take in a tiny fraction of the energy that leaves the world with them.
    System: Whenever a living being dies in the presence of the mage he gains one point of Mana, up to the normal Gnosis derived maximum, excess Mana is lost.


    Another modification upon Pattern Restoration, but this time a sort of proactive one, giving a nice boost without eating a spell slot. Spellstacking of course still applies. A Mind attainment doing the same for Mental/Social Attributes would also be conceivable. At ST discretion, the Life attainment could also boost Social attributes.

    Life: Supernal Reinforcment
    Bolstering his own body with mana, the mage increases his physical puissance.
    System: By spending three point of Mana the mage can increase one of his physical Attributes by one for the rest of the Scene. There can be only one version of Supernal Reinforcment active at any time per individual, however a reactivation of the Attainment to shift the boosted dot from one physical Attribute to another is allowed. Activating this Attainment takes an instant action (it is a very specialized form of pattern restoration or in this case rather reinforment).


    Greater Utility Attainments
    Allowing Necromancers to enable their allies to affect the living world in a bind. Or creating a level playing field against the wicked dead around himself. A Spirit version of this Attainment is of course also thinkable, but Mind should NOT be able to recreate its effects for Goetia.
    Death: Anchor
    In a world that has forgotten the dead, the mage can stand as an Anchor for those without hope to cling to.
    System: By spending three points of Mana the mage turns himself into something that is not quite an universal Anchor, but serves a similar purpose. Every Ghost in a vicinity of yards equal to his Gnosis does not suffer essence bleed, becomes tangible and visible. This effect lasts until the end of the Scene.

    Oaths are so thematic, but due to spellcontrol and the big cost of relinquishing spells (even with the tweaks I made) it just often becomes a question of "is it really worth it?" when you're not after the boon, especially for long term oaths. This attainment allows for expedient, cheap oaths that are not worldshaking but still efficient and at least long lasting.
    Fate: Oathkeeper
    In Arcadia no word is spoken without meaning. A mage well versed in the Fate Arcanum allways has the ears of the Fae and no promise he bore witness too shall be broken lightly.
    System: When witnessing a promise being made the mage may spend a point of Mana to seal the promise under the auspice of Arcadia. This works similarily to the spell "Binding Oath", but with a few tweaks. Unlike a proper spell it does not offer a boon (or clashes of wills in favour of upholding the promise, though should he be forced by means of supernatural coercion to break his oath he will not be punished eiter, unless he deliberatly intended to circumvent the oath this way). It does not count against the mages spellcontrol and lasts for a year and a day. Should the promise be broken, the wronged party will through some permutation of chance learn of the Oathbreakers wrongdoing. Should the wronged party confront the Oathbreaker, his first action will be blessed in some way (the Oathbreaker looses his defense against the first attack, a dice bonus equal to the mages Fate dots to a roll to discriminate the Oathbreaker in front of his peers or similar effects).

    If any reach was appropriate for Forces, I think it'd be this one.
    Forces: Overwhelming Power
    An Adept of Forces has power at his fingertips and this supernatural might flows easily into his magic.
    System: When casting a spell, a mage with this Attainment can apply Advanced Potency to it by spending one point of Mana instead of a Reach.

    Just an extension of the published lesser Utility Attainment with more... y'know utility.
    Matter: Eternity
    As Matter is everylasting, the most stable element of the fallen world, wethering the assault of forces and time, staying transfixed in space and watching life pass by. A mage who has reached greater understanding of Matter can imbue his spells with some of that longevity.
    System: When casting a spell, a mage with this Attainment can apply Advanced Duration to it by spending one point of Mana instead of a Reach.

    Not as big a deal as one might think due to the tendency of mage fights to often go win/loose within one action, but aside from that a great boon.
    Life: Rapid Regeneration
    Developed by Thyrsi of the Adamantine Arrow, this technique allows a mage to outlast his opponents in a prolonged fight. The wounds the mage suffered close seemingly effortlessly with a crackle of Mana even as the mage continues to take the fight to his foes.
    System: To a mage with this Attainment, Pattern Restoration becomes a reflexive action.

    Basically a version of what Fate and Death were allready doing for the mind.
    Mind: Iron Will
    The thoughts of an Adept of Mind are not easily confused. Allways aware of his own conciousness, the mage fights tooth and nail against attempts to subvert his will.
    System: The mage can reflexively use this Attainment under the following circumstances:
    • He is subjected to a supernatural ability that confuses the mind, such as Illusions
    • A supernatural effect tries to compel the mage to act against his will
    • He is being targeted by a Numen or Influence by an astral entity.

    This may just be me, but Prime allways strikes me as the Arcanum that should most directly oppose the Abyss. This is the attainment for Theurges who want to go out there and take the fight to the void.
    Prime: Truthfire
    Only the pure platonic flames of Prime can stand against the yawning depths of the abyss. A mage with this attainment is particularily adept at banishing the forces of the void.
    System: A mage with this attainment gains several benefits when opposing abyssal beings. When using Abjuration against such an entity, add the mages Prime dots to the roll. Furthermore the mage can match the abyssal unreality with the purity of Mana. When an abyssal entity uses a Numen or Influence within the mages presence, he may spend a point of mana and roll his Gnosis + Prime in a clash of wills against the abyssal power (use the counterspell mechanics).

    Unlike other greater utility attainments this one wont prevent paradox from overreaching by spending mana instead of reaches, but it makes the pain more managable.
    Prime: Control through Power
    Every mage can ease the scourge of Paradox by burning through his precious stores of Mana. Adepts of the Prime Arcanum are especially apt and efficient when doing so, only expending the minimum amount of effort to fuel ever greater magics.
    System: A mage with this attainment reduces the Paradox dicepool by two dice for every point of Mana spent.

    If you're brave you can let the Size change effect the Scale Factor. Personally I don't really use Size in the Scale factor at all, unless it makes sense (mindcontrolling a Leviathan is not really any harder than mindcontrolling a chicken, at least size wise. Transforming it into a chicken on the other hand might be more of an obstacle), but when I wrote this attainment I thought more about shenannigans rather than a sneaky way of abusing mages weird weakness to size.
    Space: World in a Pocket
    As one realizes that space is meaningless, one realizes that size, too, is just an illusion. Drawing on his connection to Space and thus the concept of dimension, the mage can freely alter the size of everything he touches.
    System: The mage touches the target and spends one point of Mana. For the rest of the scene, the object grows larger or shrinks. The mage can adjust the Size of any object (or person) by his Space dots. Objects reduced to Size 0 don't disappear, they're merely as small as a bug. While the increase or decrease of Size enables new interactions and can impose boni (or severe mali) on rolls at the storytellers direction, it does not affect derived traits (also counting the Scale spell factor). A shrinked person has just as much Health as before and a door grown past it's frame is no easier or harder to destroy.

    In most cases only important for NPCs but damn this one can be really interesting at higher levels.
    Spirit: Greater Familiar
    As the awakened soul grows in power it can sustain heavier bonds to the world of Shadow. An Adept of the Spirit arts is only limited by the growth of his own Gnosis in the selection of his Familiar. With strength come expectations, however. A mighty Familiar is more akin to an ally than a servant and will make demands of the willworker who dared share this intimate bond with it.
    System: While the Familiar bond can usually only sustain Spirits of Rank 2 or lower, a mage with this Attainment can sustain the Familiar bond to Spirit of a Rank equal to half his Gnosis (round up). The advanced Familiar background unlocked through this costs two merit dots per Rank of the Familiar.

    If the Acanthus wins by cheating this way, at least you don't have to replay the entire scene because he jumped back in time.
    Time: Temporal Shift
    Attuned to the chain of events, a cornered Time mage can shift the order of events ever so slightly.
    System: By spending one point of Mana the mage can once per turn reflexivly shift the initiative order around, applying a bonus or malus equal to his Time dots to the initiative modifier of anyone within sensory range. This attainment can only be used a number of times equal to his Time dots during a chapter.

        Due to various reasons its been a while but as I start getting back into Awakening again I wrote a little fluff piece on the Paths and their connection to the lie. In my mind every path sees through certain facets of the Lie and challenges them. It doesn't have to be limited to the five I wrote down here, but these are those that immediatly came to mind in relation to the paths.
        The Five Paths and the Lie

        Acanthus

        Whatever you do, it won't change anything. Ask that girl you met at the bar out or not, it won't make a difference, she'll turn you down anyway. Help a junkie kick the habit and you'll read about his overdose a year later. Be a hero, prevent a terrorist attack. Go you, except there'll be dozens more over the next decade, your moment in the spotlight didn't mean shit. Empires rise and fall, history marches on, unimpeded by our choices. This is a lie. The Lie drowns hope, trying to smother our spirit with the illusion of inevitability. The Path of Thistle stands in defiance of this falsehood. It reveals the strings others have attached to our fates and opens up the pages of the book of what if. We are free to make our own choices and there is no end to what we could accomplish. The world is our oyster, ready for the taking. The freedom we enjoy is exhilarting once you stop and bother to pay attention to it. Every second we make dozens of choices, big and small and they all matter. We shape the world through our choices.
        Mastigos

        You are alone. Others will never truly understand you. Just a face among millions you move among them, but are not a part of them. There are walls that seperate us, of wealth, status, health, age, opinion. You can't even trust yourself. Fear drives us to hold back. Criplles us when we try to seize our dreams. Drives us to stay in unhappy relationships. We are one in a multitude of individuals, doomed to never truly connect with anybody. This is a lie. The Lie scares us into fearing one another and ourselves. The Path of Scourging stands in defiance of this falsehood. The cosmos converges on us, we are a universe unto ourselves. And we're all connected. We are only as divided as we let them make us belief. We can overcome our own demons and fears and in the end the our will stands triumphant. All minds meet in the Astral and in the Supernal Realm of Pandemonium there is no space and all thought is one. The Warlock razes the artificial walls that divide us and divests us of the distractions placed on our minds to keep us asleep. We are not divided.
        Moros

        Death comes for us all. It will creep up on you, diminish you. Afterwards you will cease to exist or be judged. The world around us is allready dead, sterile, leaving us as aware cogs in an unstoppable machine, waiting to expire. Nobody can say what will happen after we die. Death is unknowable and it scares the living hell out of us. Change is allways foreboding, a threat. This is a lie. The Lie makes us despise change, makes us pine for that what was and blind to the bounties of the future. The Path of Doom stands in defiance of this falsehood. Change is an inevitable part of existence, but it is not to be feared. Death is not the end, merely another transition. Even in death we still change, nothing is set in stone. We don't head off to some paradise or hell and become background props just because we die. Existence is a journey and death is only another step in that journey. Death is a transition, not the end.
        Obrimos


        In the grand scheme of things you do not matter. You are a tiny speck, inhabiting a galaxy of no particular importance in an infinitly large universe. Whatever you do, you are powerless against the world around you. You can't change human nature, are restrained by the limits set to your body through biology, confined in our technological advancement by the laws of physics. You can no more overcome these limitations than you hold back a Tsunami with your bare hands. This is a lie. The Lie tells us that we are powerless, that some things cannot be changed, no matter how hard we try, that there are challenges that we lack the strength to overcome. The Path of the Mighty stand in defiance of this falsehood. Theirs is a faith that can move mountains, a voice that can shake the sky to its very foundations and an intellect that comprehends truths of heaven and the movement of the stars in equal measure. There is no obstacle that cannot be overcome, no law that cannot be understood by the mind and subjugated to our will. Humanity has the potential to accomplish anything.
        Thyrsus

        We live. We breathe, eat, shit and reproduce. There is nothing magical about it. Species compete with one another for resources, bodily processes repeat themselves. We are the sum of our parts, the miracle of life is nothing more than an especially complex series of biological, chemical and physical processes. We are a biological machine, just a series of reactions taking place, more complex than a candle flame perhaps, but at the core the same thing really. It's the same with the world around us. An inanimate husk flying circles through space around a ball of heated gas. Our world is just a bunch of unliving matter and our life nothing more than another series of reactions in a spiritually dead universe. This is a lie. The Lie tries to blind us to the magic that is life itself and the impact we have on our world and it on us. The Path of Ecstacy stands in defiance of this flasehood. Shamans know that the fascination, wonder and awe we all feel when faced with the miracle of birth, especially that of our own children, is nothing less than genuine magic. They know that the world around us is neither dead nor uncaring. They see an ocean flowing into the sea and conceive of this elemental union as an act of love. They see how we poison our world and feel the bitter resentment building. They hear the voices of the spirits drifting over from the Shadow and know we are not alone. They walk in both worlds, feel the pulse of both humanity and the world itself. We and the world we live in are alive.

            Tenders of the Cycle


            Nobody can say for sure where the story of the Tenders of the Cycle began. Some covens point to fertility cults in ancient greece, others to celtic, indian or mesopotamian roots. A prevalent theory among the scholars of the Mysterium dedicated to the study of the Tenders (many of whom belong themselves to the legacy) is that the legacy is as old as human civilization, drawing its roots from the very nature of the world and the human condition, as the tamers do.

            The Tenders of the Cycle have dedicated themselves to all things that ebb and flow, rise and fall. They study the turning of the seasons and watch the tides come and go, but of specific interest is the cycle of life and death. They sow seeds, watch them grow and relish in the harvest. Birth and Death, the beginning and end of life flow seemlessly together and form a perfect, perpetual cycle for the members of this legacy. Their body changes as their soul is refined and they themselves become the cycle of life and death, constantly dieing and rising from the ashes like the proverbial Phoenix. For most of the month a Tender is healthy and strong, somewhere between her mid teens and late fourties. Then on the night of the new moon she grows pregnant at an astonishing rate, her body withering and aging within hours. By dawn of the next day she is a shriveled crone and with her last breath gives birth to herself. She dies and is born again. The child grows as fast as her old body aged and by noon she has regained her teenage, ageing roughly a year per day until the next new moon. The legacy is intimatly tied to this cycle of rebirth and thus resonates strongly with the female sex. Men in the legacy are rare, most who choose to dedicate their life to the work of the Tenders change their sex, though some choose to be reborn in the womb of another woman.
            Due to their constant death and rebirth the Tenders enjoy a strange sort of immortality. This was for a long time a cause for persecution in many Consilia, who branded the Legacy as Left-Handed and decried its members as Liches. While technically true the accusation was in many cases unfounded, as most Tenders choose to enter the greater cycle of rebirth at some point in their life willingly and wither away peacefully without giving birth to themselves again. Truly old Rebirthers exist, but tend to keep to themselves.
            In the last century the Cyclists have seen a massive rise in popularity. Aside from a few members who belonged to the Mysterium most Tender covens were Nameless and the birth of the Free Council gave them a home and shelter. The womens' rights movement, progressing sexual liberation and emergence of neopaganistic beliefs bettered their status in society and added fuel to their magic which is in many ways based in female sexuality.

            Origins



            Parentage: Thyrsus, Free Council

            Other Names: Order of Sacred Rebirth, Wheelturners, Circle of the Maiden and the Crone, Sisterhood of the Moon

            Nickname: Cyclists, Rebirthers, Phoenixes, Kitchen-Witches (modern, derogatory)

            Background: Cyclists come from many different walks of life, but most either worked or now work in professions that relate to birth, life and death. Wet nurses, physicians or medical examiners are good examples. Many of them assume multiple such professions over their long lives. In recent years increasingly many members come from feminist and/or wiccan backgrounds.

            Appearances: Rebirthers tend to be practical, warm and down to earth. They are good with people, feeling a strong connection with all living things. They often prefer simple clothing and hand-made tools and wear symbols of both life and death openly, mixing Ankhs, Hearts and Skulls freely in their attire. Some change their way of dress as their cycle turns, switching from flowers in their hair to goth attire over the course of a few weeks.

            In the Orders: The Adamantine Arrow holds little appeal to the Tenders. Some few see the struggle for life as an important part of the cycle and relish the opportunity to study the mystery that is death more closely. For those the Arrow holds some awnsers, but the order tends to the see the predictable period of weakness on the night of the new moon as a liability and encourage initiates to seek out others venues.
            The Silver Ladder seeks to break the cycle of life and death and ascend to the supernal realms. Some Rebirthers see just another cycle in this, as the Exarchs have risen, they will fall. They see their insight into the nature of life as a gift they can give to humanity and tend to try and foster cooperation within the order, acting as valued mediators.
            The Mysteriums has dedicated itself to the study of all mysteries and Life and Death itself are among the greatest mysteries there are. Of all the Diamond orders the Mysterium most activly tries to recruit the Rebirthers as their long life makes them ideal candidates to join the Egregore. However some paranoid voices among the order whisper that it would be in the nature of the Cyclists to ensure that gathered knowledge is in time lost again.
            The Guardians of the Veil hold little appeal to the Tenders of the Cycle. Some become assassins for the Guardians, but those individuals are often imbalanced and have to be put out of comission themselves before long.
            The Free Council has become a home for many of the Tenders, due to their humble origins. While they don't care particularily about the technological innovations of mankind they heartily agree that humanity is itself magical.

            Among the Paths: Thyrsi make up most of the Tenders by far, their intimate connection to life makes accepting the Cycle merely a second step on a road allready begun. Moroi come in as a distant second, seeking to balance their affinity for death and transitions with a study of the life preceeding it. The others paths rarely join the Tenders, especially Obrimoi are rare as their path lends itself to exerting power over the world where the Tenders would much rather live in tune with it.

            Doctrine



            Prerequisites: Life 2, Medicine 2, two dots in one of the following Skills; Animal Ken, Athletics, Crafts, Empathy, Survival

            Initiation: To become a Tender of the Cycle an initiate must have created and destroyed life. This means that the applicant must have given birth to a child (or sired offspring in the rare male cases) and taken the life of another human being with her own hands. Many mentors arrange for their student to peacefully release the suffering of a terminal patient for the latter requirement.
            Her first initiation into the legacy follows the completion of a major cycle in her life. She gets back together with an old partner, takes up an old job again or finishes school to start college. The coven comes together and puts the initiate into a magically created deep sleep, so complete it resembles death most closely. When the initiate wakes up upon the dawn of the new day she feels as if reborn and has joined the legacy.
            Once the initiate is ready to learn the third attainment she undergoes a second initiation. Upon the new moon she engages in ritual intercourse, over the course of the next months she grows heavy with child, her body slowly wasting away. Finally she gives birth to herself and truly joins the Rebirthers.

            Organization: Tenders often organize themselves in local covens, often made up of a few masters and their students. They tend to be quite closely knit and gather at certain times within the lunar cycle. Hierachy within the coven passess from member to member from month to month. Most covens acknowledge three positions of authority: the maiden, the mother and the crone. Though young initiates can end up filling important positions in this fashion they usually posess the wisdom to listen advice by their more experienced sisters.

            Theory: Life is inherently cyclical. Death is not the end and birth not the beginning. They are adjacent points in a perfect circle that has and allways will exist. As above, so below and thus by adopting the truth of cyclical existence into their soul their body emulates the greater circle of rebirth on a lesser scale.

            Practice: Tenders work their Magic through the birth of new things, guidance of the existing and harvesting that which is no longer needed. They prefer to work their spells at the turning points of a cyclic event. Noon, Midnight, Ebb and Flow are auspicious times for their magic.
            Helping someone give birth (or better yet giving birth herself) is an extremly powerful moment, as is the death of another being. Here the death of a person who lived a full life is more powerful to the Tenders than the abrupt destruction of a life yet unfulfilled. Many Tenders who choose to end their life use their final death as a catalyst for a last major working.
            Sex plays a large part in Cyclist Magic, its progress from arousal to climax mirroring the larger cycle of life and death, conception the cornerstone of the same. Intercourse is therefor their preferred way of working magic that will affect another person and symbols of sexuality (from statues of fertility goddesses over menstrual blood to sex toys) are often incorporated as Yantras in their spells.

            Magic



            Ruling Arcanum: Life

            Yantras: Birth (+3 if giving herself, +2 otherwise), Death (+1-3 depending on significance), Cyclic Symbolism (+1), Sex (+2 for intercourse, +1 for smaller acts), Cyclic time or location (+1)

            Oblations: Dying/attending the death of another, being reborn/attending the birth of another, Intercourse, watching a cyclic event unfold.

            Rote Skill: Medicine
            Attainments


            1 – Sense the Cycle
            Prerquisites: Initiation

            Attuned to the ebb and flow of life, the Tender notes the impending change in herself and others. With a moments concentration she can tell wether a person she sees is preganent, if they are about to become sick and whether her subject is becoming sicker or recuperating and other biological cyclical events.

            Optional: Death 1

            By administering the last rites to a corpse, the Tender learns how this life has progressed through the cycle, catching short random glimpses of the subject throught the course of its life.

            2 – Turning the Wheel
            Prerequisites: Life 2, Medicine 3

            With a gentle hand the Tender guides life along its cycle. By touching another living being she can move it's biological cylces along their way, quickening the way hunger grows, increasing arousal or turning fatigue into drowsiness.

            Optional: Death 2

            Death and decay prosper under the Tenders fingers just as life does. With a touch she can put injuries onto the road of festering and cause sickness to take a turn for the worse.

            3 – Becoming the Cycle
            Prerequisite: Life 3, the Rebirther must increase the other Skill she used to meet the requirements of the Legacy to 3, or must attain two dots in a second skill from the list of possible requirements.

            As above so below. The body of the Rebirther becomes a mirror to the cosmic circle of life and death. On the night of the new moon she ages at terrible speed, simultaneously growing heavy with child. At dawn she dies – and gives birth to herself as a screaming but healthy toddler. Until noon she has aged into her early- to midteens and from thereon out ages roughly a year per day until the next night of the new moon. This attainment effectivly grants the Rebirther a kind of immortality as she is constantly dying and reborn. Her mind and memories stay intact throughout the cycle.
            Members of the Legcay who have mastered the life Arcanum can choose to live in a slower cycle, dying and reborn only once a year on new years' eve. They age throughout childhood into their adolescence during spring and enter their 50s in winter, wizening throughout the season until they die.

            Optional: This attainment has no optional effect.

            4 – Spinning the Wheel
            Prerequisite: Life 4, Medicine 4

            Instead of nuding the wheel along, the Tender turns it along forcefully. The hungry begin to starve, the aroused orgasm and the tired fall asleep suddenly.

            Optional: Death 4

            Ghosts are echoes of the living that refuse to move on, separating themselves from the cycle. With a few gentle words the Tender admonishes them for their folly, spurring them on to resolve their Anchors as quickly as possible and transcend the agony of sepatation from the cycle.

            5 – Fuel the Cycle
            Prerequisite: Life 5, the Tender must increase the other Skill she used to meet the requirements of the Legacy to 4, or must attain three dots in a second skill from the list of possible requirements, or two dots in a third Skill from the list.

            With a mere touch the tender causes life to be created. Touching the belly of a woman causes her to become pregnant, while touching the groin of a man causes his first intercourse with a woman to result in conception even if either or both parties were barren.
            In addition this attainment also permanently turns barren recipients fertile again.
            The pregnancy caused or aided by this attainment will allways run smoothly and produce an exceptionally healthy child.

            Optional: Death 5

            The existence of this attainment is one of the deepest secrets of the Tenders, as its use is considered allmost heretical among the Legacy and is reserved only for the vilest enemies. Having dedicated her whole life to unity with the cycle, the Tender learns how to break it. She ritually kills her subject and inseminates herself with his or her blood. Over the course of the next month (her own rebirth is skipped for the month) she grows heavy with child and sick. Finally she painfully gives birth to the Ghost of her subject, forever severed from the cycle and yet anchored to this cosmic principle (and the legacy) itself, preventing it from ever moving on.

            Appendix

            Men in the Legacy

            Men are at a natural disadvantage in the legacy, unable as they are to give birth. A man determined to join the legacy has two paths open to him: he can either become a woman or join the male path of the legacy.
            Gender change has a longstanding tradition within the legacy. Awakened magic can facilitate the necessary physical changes easily enough, but it very fragile. A mage whose gender transformation is dispelled at a critical phase of her cycle of rebirth can count herself lucky if she merely dies. For this reason the legacy has entered several longstanding pacts with spirits of feminity who use their Influences and Numina on behalf of the legacy to change the sex of male applicants in a lasting fashion.
            Alternativly a male path of the legacy exists and no man faces social stigma from his peers for his gender. However as he cannot give birth to himself he must find a woman to do so for him. To impregnate a woman with himself a ritualized form of intercourse is practiced at any time of the month. Often a sleepwalker (or even sleeper) partner is used for this purpose, giving birth to the Tender every month. Should he be unable or -willing to perform this ritual with a woman within the month he will die.

            On the Dead

            Ghosts are an abbaration. The Tenders see it as one of their prime responsibilities to guide those lost sheep that have abandoned the cycle of death and life back into the fold. Separated from their soul (which has moved back into the cycle) those shades are sad and crippled things that need to let go of those attachments that prevent them from living in tune with the cosmic cycle.
            Vampires and other undead creatures earn nothing but scorn from the Cyclists.
            The Final Death


            Even though the Tenders of the Cycle are effectivly immortal their immortality is merely incidential. Their life mirrors the cycle of life and death in a neverending circle. However their "little" death and rebirth are nothing more than echoes of the real deal, as are sleep and waking up. Most Tenders eventually feel that the life of their current incarnation is coming to a close and prepare themselves for the final death. At the end of their last cycle they do not grow pregnant and simply die. Tenders who perish in this fashion never leave a ghost behind.
            Pregnancy


            Should one of the Tenders become pregnant with child herself the cyclical nature of her legacy is suspended to honor the bigger cycle of life and death. As long as she is carrying her child to term she does not age and does not die and rebirth herself. However as her very soul suspends itself she looses all access to magic, becoming essentially a sleepwalker for the duration of her pregnancy. Upon birth it all comes back to her in a sudden rush of power, making it one of the best possible times to enact major workings among the Tenders.

            Plothooks


            It's in your best interest: The ghost of an old lover, friend or relative has become a dear companion to one of the characters. Suddenly the ghost changes her behaviour completly and tries desperatly to escape the trap of the afterlife. Investigation reveals she has been put under a powerful spell, cast by a local Tender. When confronted she argues that its in the ghosts own best interest to move on. How will the character deal with this heavyhanded approach when faced with all the insanity and pain other ghosts endure?

            Out of Balance: In a small town a true babyboom has occurred and a particularily fanatic tender has noticed. Fearing that the imbalance will impair the cycle she decided to balance the scales and commits a series of murders the mundane authorities will dismiss as accident, disease or natural causes. The characters stumble upon evidence of the killings.

            Dirty Magic: In the city a number of women tell disturbing stories no one wants to belief. Some convince themselves it was just a dream, others have a nervous breakdown. They remember suddenly feeling heavy, their belly growing and within hours painfully giving birth to a rapidly growing baby. A particularily callous and sadistic male Rebirther is haunting the city, planting his seed in one night stands and disguising the ritual as a pecuilar kink. The Consilium frowns upon such misuse of magic. The Guardians of the Veil do more than that.

            The Witch: A terrible magical sickness or wound has befallen one of the characters and will surely be her doom. Luckily local rumor has it that a healer beyond compare lives nearby in an isolated valley. When the characters arrive the old witch says that she can indeed help, but for the character to be healed she must first die.
