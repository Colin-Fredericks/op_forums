    A Question about Compacts & Endowments

    04-16-2014, 12:43 PM
    I'm not terribly experienced with Hunter, but I was inspired by an idea I had, and so have been drawing up a Compact. However, part of this idea involves giving the Compact an Endowment. My question is: How hardline is it that Compacts do not have Endowments? I understand that part of the theme of these sorts of organizations is how in the dark they are, in connection to their humanity (as opposed to Conspiracies, which are meant to overtly blur the line between human and monster), but even a lone hunter might be able to scrounge up some sort of supernatural edge using the R&D rules.

    I suppose some background would help.

    The Compact in question is called The Friends of the Deceased, an organization dedicated to protecting people from the predations of mad ghosts, and the ghosts themselves from the predations of selfish monsters who would exploit them for their own gains, drawing aesthetic inspiration from Spiritualist groups, historical societies, and the like. As hunters, they work best at fighting monsters on a social level, and usually see resorting to violence as a last resort. The Endowment in question is called Appeasement, and is a series of small rituals invoked on behalf of the dead. Each ritual aids a ghost in some way, restoring Essence or Corpus, letting one Manifest, granting a Numen, and so on. The reason for the Endowment is twofold: The first is pragmatic in that ghosts normally can't be perceived by mortals, and so any organization dedicated to ghosts would first require some way to affirm their existence. The second relates to the Friends' role as a charity organization. They truly believe that ghosts deserve pity, mercy, and aid, and that the primary cause of rogue specters is when one is denied its due. So, they help ghosts, and sometimes ghosts help them in return. Having Status in the Compact means one can purchase Ghost Contacts/Allies/etc. But it's also the sort of outlook/Endowment that can get them in a lot of trouble, hence why, combined with its limited capabilities, I'm pretty sure Appeasement can work as a Compact-level Endowment. Like the staunch abused pitbull rescuer, a Friend of the Deceased might truly believe there's no such thing as a bad ghost, just bad bereaved. It's the sort of attitude that, in the World of Darkness, is going to get them bit eventually, most likely on multiple occasions. One Appeasement ritual, for example, has the Friend offer her body as a vessel for a ghost, a practice that from an outsider's (even, or especially, an informed outsider's) perspective is pure insanity.

    So, does this idea sound kosher? As an Endowment, does it work for a Compact rather than a Conspiracy? Am I understanding the themes here correctly, or have I made an error?

      Well, the book Compacts and Conspiracies might give you some ideas. Basically, compacts can have an endowment, but it's a more generalized concept. Example, the Union's endowment, Your Friends and Neighbors, is five dots towards community contacts to help you with the Vigil. It fits both thematically, and mechanically. Temporary allies must be repaid in kind, and your contacts can only do so much to help after all.

          Personally, I'd set it as a 1-5 dot Merit, giving them two points per dot each day/scene/chapter/whatever. Each point they spend can either:

          Heal a ghost for 1 point of corpus
          Give a ghost +1 die to activate a numen or Manifest
          Grant a ghost a new numina (I'd say have this cost 2 or 3 points) for the scene
          Add 1 to an Abjuration or Exorcism roll

          No roll necessary, but each point spent requires a full-round (or longer) action to perform the ritual.

                          The endowments of compacts, as of compacts and conspiracies, are always maybe magic maybe mundane at best, although that doesn't apply to the promethean brotherhood in witchfinders that is a compact with a blatantly supernatural edge, but a compact with any mojo whatsoever is highly unusual.

                          However, I take a darwinist approach to why only the conspiracies have supernatural endowments: it's an edge for both recruitment and survival that allows the organization to become a world wide conspiracy, not some "oh you have over 200 members here's some supernatural power," so I'd picture your compact as really a young conspiracy, that has the mojo but not the membership and reach, yet.
