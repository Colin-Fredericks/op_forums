    Post-Punk Elements in CofD

    10-05-2016, 11:14 AM
    Anyway, I was reading the Masquerade Q&A thread, when I read a post (I will not name names) that cued me into something about the nWoD/CofD:

    It's best work came after the writers gave up on punk culture, and in fact started invoking things that are utterly anathema to punk culture (those within the system as heroes, outsiders often being ridiculous and delusional, the human craving for order not being a bad thing). So, I thought about this for a while, and realized that the only game I can think of that is proudly cyberpunk, Demon, portrays the guys who want to change the system so they can rejoin it (Integrators) as being hated for being moral consciences besides their apparent and obvious betrayal in the making, and part of the fear of the game being that you end up causing more harm than good by breaking the God-Machine. Both of these are pretty much fists to the nose of punks in general. Mage portrays the Silver Ladder, believers in a natural hierarchy of people, as big fans of the idea of responsibility and equality of opportunity, with the main problem with them being their worship of the self as an individual moral actor who knows better than everyone else.

    So, I was wondering, has anyone else seen any post-punk and anti-punk elements they like or dislike?

          The conspiracies in HTV are probably one of the most obvious elements. I mean these are the guys who are literally "The Man" and here they are being portrayed as the good guys for all the dirty and ethically questionable things they're forced to do. But that also makes them the favorites of the Hunter line. When everyone makes a new fan-group, it's almost always a conspiracy. When someone needs a story hook, conspiracies have the widest breadth of ideas to use. So in that respect, the conspiracies are definitely one of the most positive post-punk elements of the WoD.

                                              I've always preferred protagonists tied to large organizations, either ones that aren't too morally grey or possibly using unpleasant means to achieve good ends. It gets really, really tiring to see every single church, military, and empire in nerd fiction be made out to be horrific systems of oppression with zero worth to them while all of the scrappy rebels and heretics are perfect heroes. Eclipse Phase threw out a decent amount of what could've been interesting setting material out the window in favor of making every hierarchy evil (the major political body is a cartoonishly evil alliance of hypercorporations who practice indentured servitude, and there's a backwards fascist American holdout that's the only bastion of hated Christianity in the setting and so blatantly awful that they read like a bad Starship Troopers parody) while making anarchism a literally flawless, utopian structure where every single person cooperates in the name of the whole, and it's the most glaring flaw in a universe I otherwise adore. Shadowrun is always a pain for me because I want to play a contractor every time.

                                              What I wouldn't give to see more on character types like D:tD's Integrators or the Conspiracies like VALKYRIE and VASCU that work to redeem systems or use their size and organization ostensibly to do the right thing. I'm so tired of the "structure bad, punk good!" that Star Wars seems to have made nerd gospel.
