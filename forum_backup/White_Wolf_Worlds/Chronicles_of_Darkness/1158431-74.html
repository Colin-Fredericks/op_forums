                    And THAT was one of the the few things that I felt needed to be fixed and wasn't. The other was that there are too few Kiths in the core (yes I know K&K has more, but it shouldn't feel like it's needed, which it does,
                    instead of simply bonus content), and both problems have the same cause, the seasonal courts take up too much space, there should have been one emotional court contract that worked differently for different courts, based on their emotions and/or ideal, and each court in the book should have gotten 1 Royal Contract and 1 Common Contract, instead the Seasonal Courts get everything and the other courts get nothing in regards to court contracts, which means that few people will bother with most of them. To be fair I really only liked the New Orleans Court, but with no court contracts it's not fully ready for use in my opinion. I think we will like get the court contracts for these setting in the companion, which means you need a separate book to make these core settings function. I honestly think if they we're going to do this, they should just have made all the settings in the core Variant Seasonal Courts like they did for Iceland (I might steal the Christmas party Elves courts of Iceland and put then in Canada where the North Pole actually is. Maybe.

                    I just hope that the Companion has the Toronto Freehold and Courts because I'm still really disappointed that it was removed from the book as it's was far more interesting than any of the courts IN the final book.

                    Anyways I sound too negative, the Changeling court has a lot of strengths, I like the Fetch Rules, Hedge Ghosts seem to have more Nuamens, and the seem to be more Dread Powers for other Hobgoblins, which is cool. I know one Contract was improved, the one that allowed to you to reverse time by 10 seconds pure use. Now instead of only being usable once per story, it's usable at will as long as you can pay the cost and it's the automatic reflexive use that saves you from dying that is only once per story. You can now use this contract to save someone else from dying or being caught in a trap, and other cleaver uses. It's really nasty with the Seeming bonuses, moving stealth dots in yards before time restarts (Darklings) and surprise attack (Widen) make it a great combat and defence, life saving power, but the broken things is of you drop the mask you get an exceptional success on it, but you reverse time to before you dropped the mask you will get the 8 again and won't have to drop the mask again, so you don't pay the price or suffer the new gives of doing so. This power is also great against Huntsmen with the Dread Power that allows then to appear in any scene out of nowhere, you can reverse time before the Huntsman appears and feel or set up a trap for it. Easy to fill Loophole too, so often it will only cost 1 willpower. Often worth it.
