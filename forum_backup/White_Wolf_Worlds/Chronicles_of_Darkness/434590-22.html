                                                        Some other ideas for Atoll merits:
                                                        • One that lets them add/replace one of the Leviathan's Aspirations with one of the Atoll's choosing.
                                                        • One that easily lets them impose emotional Conditions. Due to their nature, this might even be done accidently.
                                                        • One that allows an Atoll to "haunt" a Leviathan's dreams: even seperated, the Atoll is treated as nearby, can influence the sleeping Leviathan, and (optionally) count their proximity as "close" for the purposes of remote ritual targeting (the Atoll may be immune, but this lets a manipulative Atoll "request" intervention on their behalf via the cult's rituals).
                                                        • One that negates any ongoing effect by a Leviathan (ex. An Atoll finds an ongoing Call of the Depths, or summoned hurricane, and uses their odd connection to the Wake/Tempest to "dispel" it).
