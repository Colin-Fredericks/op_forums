    Me and my players have a question about iron/cold iron, the book says that an iron/prison cage makes the Changeling unable to use faerie Magic to escape.
    Some of us believe that by the idea of iron being a bane (cold iron) and how it is exposed in the book, being imprisioned by it wold invalidate all contracts and feeric powers the Changeling uses while they are being imprisioned by iron.
    Other players believe that only the powers that would help or make Changeling be able to escape would be nullified by iron imprisonment (example: they believe that a Changeling could use contracts to make rain or a contract that attacked someone, while they changeling using said contract where imprisioned by Iron).
    So the question is: Does Iron prevent Changeling from using any fey magic or only those that could be used by Changeling to escape?
