                                Would anyone have some advice or inspiration for statting out a Hurricane spirit and its entourage?
                                ​Starting point is to think about narrative role and, off the back of that, Rank. What do you intend to do with the spirit? Small, local manifestation of a wider hurricane, or overarching spirit of the entire thing? 'Rampaging monster' causing havoc due to the real hurricane? Powerful spirit noble that must be negotiated with to minimise or exploit damage caused by the actual weather? Tormented victim being harnessed and used by sorcerers or Pure to direct the hurricane to the areas they want to devastate? Etc etc.

                                - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
