                                            I made a thing:

                                            You Do Not Have To Bow

                                            Core Power: Imbuing Mortals
                                            Keys: Stigmata Marionette
                                            Dice Pool: Power + Occult
                                            Action: Instant
                                            Duration: Indefinite
                                            Dealing one lethal damage to himself by I made a thing: drawing blood with a dagger, he makes a mark of blood upon a mortal’s brow. This mark is an “object” with a Size of 1 and a Durability of (Successes), if this mark is destroyed by damage of any sort the Manifestation will end. Otherwise it cannot be removed, except for the Sin-Eater willing it. A mortal marked with this Manifestation gains the ability to touch and see ghosts, as well as Sin-Eater Damage Resistance by spending Willpower instead of Plasm. This Manifestation may not be used on more mortals than (Psyche) at a time.

                                            Link #1: Night Falls...
                                            Keys: Primeval Caul
                                            Trigger: "When an imbued mortal faces a ghost in battle..."
                                            ...he takes a point of lethal damage, automatically Goes for Blood and does not lose Defense from multiple opponents. He grows long, slender claws and fangs to replace frail fingers that do +0L, but each turn he must roll Resolve + Composure - (The number of rounds the battle has gone on), a failure means that he cannot target anything but the ghost and will attempt to devour the ghost when he has brought it to below half of it’s Corpus. Failure to devour the ghost causes him to lose a point of Willpower and the transformation lasts for a scene.

                                            Link #2: ...But The Sun Also Rises
                                            Keys: Stigmatic Pit
                                            Trigger: “When an imbued mortal devours a ghost…”

                                            ...the mark on his brow shines brightly and the ghost dissolves to lay over the mortal, granting him access to one if it’s Numina which uses a dice pool of (lowest Power Attribute) + (lowest Finesse Attribute). This Numen lasts for a week, unless the mortal willingly spends a Willpower dot, in which case he gains access to the Numen permanently. He uses the Numen by spending Willpower.

                                            "There is a remedy for everything but death, a hope for everything but wickedness, and everything will lapse except righteousness."

                                                        For any interested I've made considerable changes to my Vampire, Werewolf and Demon Hacks. As well as added a Beta of Mage 2e House Rules based on DaveB's wonderful spoilers. Summary of changes below:

                                                        Vampire
                                                        • Dropped Clans entirely in favor of Bloodlines, and adapted all five clans, and 12 additional bloodlines to 2e.
                                                        • Refocused Vampire society to include a lot more Revenants.
                                                        • Made changes to Siring to reflect the above two changes.
                                                        Werewolf
                                                        • Dropped Global Tribes in favor of local packs and lodges.
                                                        • Made it possible to shift Auspice as a Werewolf's place in their pack changes.
                                                        • Dropped the Pure entirely. They are too cartoon evil for my tastes.
                                                        Mage
                                                        • In addition to the changes in 2e, I de-emphasized paths, dropped the Exarchs, and lots of other things.
                                                        • Please keep in mind that the doc is a work in progress.
                                                        Demon
                                                        • Made changes to facilitate easier crossover without God-Machine involvement.
                                                        • Glitches are now the primary risk of Cover breach, and Demons are freer to act as invasive weirdos in the games of other lines.
                                                        As always constructive feedback is appreciated. Though I'd appreciate if comments like "I like the original better" be left out of the conversation (seriously, it's a homebrew thread, if you don't like a change ignore it).

                                                            I like your changes to Demon. My suggestions:
                                                            • In addition to the Major Brand or Tell, each Stigmatic has a way of generating Aether as part of the template, similarly to the Potent Blood Merit (Flowers Of Hell page 130). However, the method varies a lot, and isn't limited to hurtful secretions, but it is always detrimental in some way. For example, they might temporarily or permanently remove Attribute and Skill dots like a Mage Scouring their own Pattern, or inflict Conditions and Tilts on themselves at will. The amount of Aether is proportional to the loss, but no Stigmatic can generate more than 5 Aether per chapter. Wise Demons do not abuse their Stigmatic allies, and offer rewards for services rendered.
                                                            • Greater use of Cryptids as spying and Aether-hunting tools. All Cryptids now have the Aether Hive Adaptation. Should they have the [Fuelstat] Eater Adaptation, they convert it into Aether. They still need to spend this resource to remain alive, but many are willing to share in exchange for protection or anything else their strange minds can desire.
                                                            • Demons can dismantle Gadgets to absorb the Aether stored inside, but they cannot learn the installed Embed, Exploit or Demonic Form advantage if they do this. They must touch the Gadget, spend 1 willpower point and roll Wits + Science or Occult + Primum as an instant action. They gain 1 Aether automatically, in addition to Successes. This requires a Compromise roll, following Pendragon's house rules (or pings Aetheric Resonance, if you still want to use that in this situation). The Gadget cannot be a One-Shot or temporary, and if a Demon dismantles a Gadget they created, the Willpower dot is not returned.
                                                            • Allow the Essence Drain Technology to work on all supernaturals, and change its name to just Energy Drain. Ephemeral beings use their [Resistance/2 round up] as their Resolve to Resist the Demon's Wits + Occult + Primum roll.
                                                            • The Extispicy Exploit can now be used to absorb Aether, like a mortal being sacrificed to turn their Integrity into Essence for an Angel. The Aether gained is equal to Successes, capped by the mortal's [Integrity/2 round up]. Exploits that already absorbed Aether, such as Devour Infrastructure, may still do that, and other Exploits may absorb Aether as an alternate effect at the ST's discretion.
                                                            How does that sound, Pendragon?
