                                        Algernon "Al" Frasier (Shadow Name: Silver Fire)
                                        Concept: Bully-Hunting Prankster

                                        Long-Term Aspiration: Out a corrupt teacher without revealing own magic to Sleepers

                                        Al (He hates being called Algernon, and has made sure that basically only he and his parents know that name) was a relatively normal teen, albeit an unruly one, until he Awakened in the middle of church one Sunday, suffering a grand mal seizure. After awakening, he became interested in the occult, guided by the visions of the Watchtower of the Golden Key. His search brought him to a local bookstore whose owner was purported to be an expert in the occult. After helping him fend off an unfriendly Seer of the Throne, the old man agreed to offer him tutelage in magic (hence Al's knowledge of Space and Mind magic) in exchange for working at the bookstore. Al has made it his job to humiliate those who abuse people weaker than them, but has to make sure that his straight-laced Catholic parents don't find him out, as the Christian faith doesn't take too kindly to witchcraft.

                                        Dedicated Tools: A Steel pendant in the shape of Mjölnir, and Al's pay-as-you-go flip phone.

                                        Path: Obrimos
                                        Order: Free Council
                                        Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 2, Resolve 2 (+1 from Path); Al is sharp-minded, tough and clever.
                                        Physical Attributes: Strength 1, Dexterity 3, Stamina 2; Algernon has decent endurance, but isn't a stellar physical specimen.
                                        Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 4, Composure 1; Al is short-tempered but highly charismatic.
                                        Mental Skills: Computer 1, Investigation 1, Occult 1, Science 1; Al is relatively uninitiated in the supernatural, but has some basic knowledge.
                                        Physical Skills: Athletics (Sprinting) 2, Brawl 1, Larceny 2, Stealth 2; Algernon's a bit of a hellraiser, and has become good at sneaking, lockpicking, and making a quick exit.
                                        Social Skills: Empathy 2, Intimidation 1, Persuasion 3 (Fast-talking), Socialize 2, Streetwise 1 (Rumors), Subterfuge 2; Algernon is a gregarious young man, and talented at getting people to do what he wants.
                                        Merits: Mentor (••, Mr. Prodigia, Academics, Occult, Resources), Patient, Status (Free Council, •), Extemporaneous Affinity (•••, Banishers p 41), High Speech (free from Free Council membership), Parkour (••• Flow, Cat Leap, Wall Run)
                                        Willpower: 4
                                        Wisdom: 7
                                        Virtue: Valorous
                                        Vice: Spiteful
                                        Initiative: 3
                                        Defense: 2
                                        Speed: 8
                                        Health: 8
                                        Gnosis: 1
                                        Arcana: Forces 1, Mind 1, Prime 2, Space 2
                                        Rotes: Forces: Influence Sound (•), Receiver (•) Prime: Counterspell Prime (••), Space: Untouchable (••)

                                        Armor: Untouchable (2)
                                        Nimbus: Al's Nimbus resembles flames, except pale silver, hence his Shadow Name. His Signature Nimbus looks like metallic powder, blowing off touch objects like a puff of ash.
                                        Nimbus Tilt: Burning Anger
                                        Algernon's Nimbus manifests itself as silvery embers of flame, that flare up when he raises his voice. While he becomes much harder to intimidate or dissuade in this state, he's more easily goaded. Algernon receives a +2 bonus to resists Intimidation and fear-based effects, but loses the 10-again property on rolls to resist being taunted or goaded.

                                        Mentor: Mr. Prodigia (real name Frank Murphy)
                                        Mr. Prodigia is an eccentric old man (and an experienced Mastigos Threnodist) who first encountered Algernon when he broke into his store late at night. After blundering in and out of his mind-based spell, Algernon managed to distract a hostile Seer long enough for Prodigia to take the advantage and dispatch his attacker. As thanks, he has been giving Al occasional tips and hints as needed. He's very hands-off in his teaching, feeling that Algernon's talents will grow best when afforded independence. If Algernon proves promising enough, Prodigia intends to tutor him into the Threnodist Legacy. Prodigia is always willing to help with a bit of research or occult knowledge, but is rather tight-fisted with unnecessary expenses.
