    What Kind of Uratha Would You Be?

    07-09-2014, 09:34 PM
    If you were to wake up one day and undergo the First Change, what kind of Werewolf would you become? This thread is to tell the rest of us!


    Here's what you should put down, anything else is optional:

    Auspice

    Tribe

    Reason


    ------------------------------------------------------------


    Auspice: Ithaeur

    Tribe: Bale Hound, masquerading as a Hunter in Darkness

    Reason: The world is F.U.B.A.R and most supernaturals would probably admit that. Those who don't are either blissfully naive humans, or still new to the World of Darkness. Everyone who's tried to fix the world has failed, either because they lacked the strength to carry it out, or became corrupted and lost sight of their goals. So why even bother attempting to fix a lost cause when joining the other side is so much more rewarding? I personally bare no ill will towards the Forsaken, I simply recognize that they're kidding themselves if they think they can make a difference.

    That being said, I can't just walk around shouting my true allegiances in full view of the public. I need someone to hide out with. And who better than the Hunters in Darkness, who are known for their privacy and don't take kindly to tresspassers? If I pretended to be one of them, then my irrational paranoia wouldn't seem all that suspicious by comparison! Of course I'd have to be really, really careful not to let them catch me tainting any of their land...

    As for my Auspice, I'm quite drawn to the allure of being able to wield magic, and when I was younger, I would attempt to scry visions out of obscure imagery from water stains, to stick piles, and even the creases in a wadded up dish cloth. The role of a shaman or an occultist would be suitable for me as an Uratha, plus all the delicious Gifts and powers I could obtain from the spirits would sweeten the deal.

          No one should be one whit surprised by this.

          Auspice: Cahalith

          This recent Christmas holiday, the subject of DnD was on everyone's mind for some reason,as was assigning people classes. I was consistently told I was a bard-I have a way with words and emotions that apparently helps a lot of people out. Now a bard and a Cahalith are not the same-DnD Bards are sneakier bastards than Cahalith- but that is a score in the right direction. I am also, despite my darkling nature, a significant presence-I can sneak around when I want, don't get me wrong, I can go unnoticed-but when I'm not trying, I tend to subtly fill a room when I enter, and when I go for attention, I expand the room and grab everyone's attention. I also don't readily back down in arguments or in fights-I may mediate and try to avoid that(some Elodoth tendencies there), but more often than not that's a result of me being convinced I'm right and assuming a fight doesn't change the fact. I have been told that when I invest I am tenacious. I love stories, both the old ones and new ones I make up, and I love them in all their forms. I love music and often wish I could sing or play an instrument or dance well. On both fronts, it's because I have things that I want to say, that I want to rip out of me and into the world. I love glory and seek fame and communal recognition a lot. I love the Cahalith because they feel so damn familiar to me-in fact, it's one of the two x-splats that perfectly sum me up (the other being the Acanthus, namely the original, but certainly FWC's take isn't that far off either). I love them and play them because they are who I am.

          Tribe: Iron Masters

          Now remember when I said bards are sneaky bastards in DnD, and that that very much applied to me? Never let me talk. Br just confirmed it. As loud as I can be, it must be pointed out I am not a nice person who plays by the rules. I and sneaky,devious bastard-as a kid if I couldn't get you upfront, I'd wait until you were in a compromising position and then get you, like waiting until you were buckled and unable to move while the teacher was walking away before I'd pummel the shit out of you. That hasn't changed as an adult-in fact, it's gotten worse, because I tend to talk people into those compromising positions. Part of the reason I can do that is an absolute lack of care about the worth of my word-it doesn't matter to me if I told you I'll do something, it only matters to me if I care to do it. Again, I cheat. I lie. I reject just about anything that limits me, and if I can do so and make you think it will, all the better-your misconceptions are your problems unless I decide otherwise. I adapt by accepting very little as constant and running with what's handed to me. That's part of the reason I love cultures as much as I do-there are cores that remain constant, threads that bear out, but there's also rapidly changing movements and events and attitudes and thoughts. Society is a living tapestry, a flowing ocean of connections that shift and stay the same and just keep going. THe entire Slenderman thing was fascinating to watch, the birth of a new urban legend, and seeing how people bore that out, how it just shift and was hammered until we have the loose stories of today-who knows where that'll be years from now, and that's just an example. I like history a lot, because you can see how that shift happen, but here and now is where that same sort of thing is happening, and despite all we know we can't see fully where it's going. And as a final point, I both love and hate people. People are both glorious and pitiful, of constant shades of glamour and shame to them. I can see the amazing things everyone is capable of, and get frustrated at seeing them keep themselves from it, and i am both enraptured and horrified when they get over that tendency, with thecrazy ass things we create that threaten to improve the world and promise to end it. Again, I play Iron Masters because it's really familiar to me, I love and play them they are who I am.

          And in fact, of all the possible configurations throughout the lines I could be, I think it's this mix that hits the closest to who I am. If I existed in the World of Darkness as a supernatural, I would probably be a Cahalith Iron Master, despite all other options.
