    Nom nom nom

    10-24-2017, 12:32 AM
    There area number of ways that one can devour another as a means to gain dread power. If you are willing to cross that moral event horizon there are a number of shortcuts to power that to require another to pay the final price. From anthropophagy, skin taking, blood bathing, body thievery, to the rites of Hecate, and heart harvesting diablery of the sons of Phobos; power can be had. I’m sure I am missing some others, no doubt there are ways to permanently eat ghosts, spirits and souls to get power.
    A devourer can be a terrible villain. In some cases, like when the prey is more monstrous* the horrific nature of these acts seems more palatable. After all you were going to kill that thing anyways, why not take every part of the monster..

    Possible ideas.
    A devourer kills and consumes monster after monster, person after person, gaining more and more powers as they go. This would be a way to have a Boss NPC that may have started out as a mortal long ago. As far as I can tell there is nothing stopping a full template from the feast either.

    A group of devourers joins forces to share in the hunt, and teach each other their craft. This arrangement probably won’t last for long however the carnage that could be wrought in that time would be terrible.
    Reskinning act of devouring to be more ‘palatable’:
    An alchemist extracts golden exudation full of promise from a subject. A Mad Scientist or doctor could learns the secrets and then harvests certain components from the blood and heart of the vampire on their slab. An occultist could ‘trap the essence’ of a monster rather than eating a pound of it’s flesh.

      This is an interesting theme, but how can we use it? Of course, the simplest route is to have an NPC acquiring Dread Powers from the feast. But what other, more specific twists, can we add to it?

      A Slasher Undertaking could be linked to the devourer. Either of them could work, each painting the character in a new light. I think I'll work it on a chronicle myself.

      Vampires could have a Bloodline and/or Coil of the Dragon devoted to eating the flesh of the living. Since anthropophagy is already a Vampire theme, it does need the extra mileage of flesh and unavoidable death to work, though.

      Werewolves already do take advantage from this, but a secret Gift or Ritual could enhance and further the effect.

      Mages also can use this in several ways. But maybe a twist on the Tremere Lich can make it easier to take the power by indulging in the flesh instead of soul.

      Changelings could have Contracts of Flesh that use human meat as a catch.

      Everyone can benefit of some thematic Merits or Conditions.

      I'll work on this as soon as I have time. I had been out of this forums for too much time, already.

A Legacy based on the act of consumption itself, either raw or a stew, but no potions.                       Changelings could also have also eating-based Oaths. I can picture the giant that swear to eat Jack being able to find out trespassers out of having an Oath to eat'em, or the fabled warning of not eating fairy food being added with a little "spice", like in a tale I heard once about a barrel of wine, gift from a Troll King to a peasant, a delicious wine that never ceased, but the peasant should never open the barrel. When the peasant opens to look into the wine, there are rotten corpses inside.

                      There is no reason for other Refinements to learn how to harvest something from human bodies. Prometheans are natural alchemical furnaces, and humanity is their goal. To process actual humans in several ways seems hardly out of theme.
