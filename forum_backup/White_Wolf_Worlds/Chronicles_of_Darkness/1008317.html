    Dracula's powers in Requiem

    11-25-2016, 07:23 PM
    As corresponding with the Masquerade counterpart thread, what are the disciplines in Requiem that can exactly replicate the powers described in Bram Stoker's Dracula and also future adaptions like Castlevania and such? Especially trying to match the aesthetics of the Dracula style or "traditional" vampire like cloud of bats, wolf, mist, glowing red eyes, pale skin, etc.

      I don't know about Castlevania, but...

      Protean lets you: manifest beast traits, change into beasts, and it lets you shift into a cloud of bloody mist. (Take the Swarm merit to do clouds of bats.)
      Dominate/majesty to get you the power of seducing a grand total of three bride's and some poor sob's future wife.
      Mystery of the Ascedant to do the daywalker thing a bit better.

      What else is there... Pale skin: just never use Blush of Life.
      Red eyes: this could be a Bane or you could choose to be a Nosferatu and have the glowing monster goggles be a manifestation of your Clan's curse.

      EDIT: 's been a while since I read Stoker, but wikipedia tells me you'd also need:
      - super strength, which is gained of course by buying up points in Vigor
      - crawling walls can be done with Protean
      - flight, can be done with Protean. Levitation, not as written. But a custom Devotion might take care of that.
      - there is mention of a sailor trying to stab the Count with a knife, but the knife passes through the monster's body. This can be explained via Celerity or a Devotional refinement of the 5th Protean power
      - traveling to unhallowed grounds. Auspex 5 devotion.
      - telepathy: straight up Auspex 4.
      - illusions: obfuscate and/or nightmare
      - some kind of teleportation-ish escape act... Quite difficult. Can't just be remedied with Celerity. In VtR 1e there was a Boodline called the Carnival if I am not mistaken. Contortionist licks. They might help you out here.
      - command animals: Animalism
      - manipulating the weather... Bloodmagic, probably Cruac

              Red eyes as a Daeva? Get punched in the eyes real hard. As in: get subconjunctival hemmorages all over them. Spend Willpower to make these "scars" permanent. Heal the mechanical damage. Done.

You can definetly manipulate the weather using bloodmagic in VtR 1e, IIRC the Bloodsorcery supplement has rules for this. All you need to do is port those to 2e.

              I looked up the Carnival bloodline, you can easily transform the relevant powers from their Discipline, the Show, into Protean devotions for escape act purposes. Combine with Celerity to creatae a Devotion that gives the impression of teleportation.

              I don't remember Gary Oldman, raising the dead. But if you say so. There is a standard possibility for post-mortem Embrace in VtR 2e, though I suspect that's not what you're looking for. You might want to check out that Bloodsorcery supplement again, though. Really.

              Try to not think in terms of Masquerade when reading through VtR is, I think, an advice you might benefit from on the whole.

                                                  If glowing eyes is that important to you, and you don't think the face of hunger Bane is sufficient, you can just make a custom Bane that does it, or a Bloodline that has it as a weakness, or a Merit that has it as a drawback or something. Like, you have such a specific effect in mind, might as well just make it instead of trying to shoehorn something that doesn't fit your needs.

                                                  Also, re: Dracula's Clan, it's all but canon that he's Gangrel. The big bald bearded dude in the Gangrel chapter? That's his Grandchilde, or at least claims to be. Requiem's take aside, Stoker's Dracula had so much werewolf imagery conflated with the vampire imagery, and such a strong feral/animalistic vibe, there's no question in my mind that he would be anything but Gangrel, even before considering his mastery of Protean and talent for Animalism. Just cause he was charismatic doesn't mean he has to have been Daeva.
