                    The Sleeper must recognize the event AND the event must be Supernal, the Sleeper doesn't have to recognize the event as Supernal.

                    When determining if something is obvious magic ask yourself two questions:

                    1) Is this a spell as opposed to an attainment or secondary consequence of a spell (a lasting effect)?
                    2) Can a Sleeper perceive it?

                    If the answer to both is "yes" then it is obvious magic whether or not the Sleeper believes disbelieves in magic. Ascension is the game that cares about belief, not Awakening.

                        The Sleeper needs to recognise it as magic. It's not enough to just see it. For example, if a Sleeper watches a mage cast a spell to turn on the light without touching the switch, that spell would suffer Paradox. If the Sleeper doesn't see what the mage is doing and can justify the light suddenly turning on as the mage flicking the switch, then that spell no longer would suffer Paradox. In both cases the Sleeper would see the effects of the spell, but in only one of them would the Sleeper recognise it as supernatural. See the sidebar "Obvious magic" on page 298.

                                                That belief doesn't determine Vulgarity, the Fallen World does. Things that mimic things actually possible in the Real World can be used to hide magic.
