                Well, as previously stated, Princesses probably talk (at length) about their own powers and philosophy more than the other "monsters", so some information should be available. Whether Hunter trust it, or have access to it is another matter.
                Thank you, Vent. Glad to see at least one person was listening last time I answered that concern.

                Again, for starter, that book wouldn't have a point to exist if hunters were just like "witches?", because then they would treat Princesses no different than mages and you might as well just read Witchfinders.

                Second, I actually did take the effort to explain how hunters know all these informations; they figured Princesses were using their powers instinctively while seeing them in action (true, psychic do too, but there are enough differences between the two that they wouldn't confuse them), they deduce the link to their emotions because they saw Princesses would get weaker when faced with emotional trauma and stronger when they helped people (I do think they would pay attention to anything that might show them how to weaken an enemy), and assuming they caught at least one or two Princesses transforming, I would assume they would see them pull out the Phylacteries before transforming and be smart enough to put two and two together. I REALLY don't think it's that impossible they would figure out all of this.

                Third, as Vent just explained, Princesses are more inclined to share informations about themselves than, say, Vampires (who are obsessed with hiding their existence for their own safety), Mages (who know most of the stuff they work on would drive humans insane), or Werewolves (who prefers minding their own business and hate having humans interfering).

                Finally, I would like to point out Hunters still lack a lot of informations: they aren't sure what Princesses are, don't trust them, have various theories on their origin and nature... I feel like there still are plenty of dark areas.

                                This is getting silly. A design convention should never be treated as a hard rule. What will actually go wrong if you give a compact access to a supernatural endowment? I do it all the time with hunter groups like Ride to Damascus and they're well recieved. If you want to exclude the Promethean Brotherhood on a silly technicality because their Endowment is risky I point out that the Azusa Miko get a potent supernatural power at Status 1 and another at Status 5, and the Loyalists of Thule have a sidebar in Compacts and Conspiracies saying they have access to all sorts of magic, like rituals from Witch Finders.

                                The simple fact is that a Conspiracy of Sworn who work alongside nobles is a contradiction in terms. A Conspiracy cannot function if most of the members are more loyal to the Princess they work with than to the Conspiracy and it's mission; which most Sworn will be since most are chosen from people with a deep personal intimacy with the Princess. You can have a Conspiracy that fights the Darkness and is a foil to the light, you can have a group of sworn, but they can't be the same group.

                                            So me and Darinas talked it out in the chat and came to an agreement

                                            Part of that is that we're discussing what could be a new morally white/grey Conspiracy. Thus far I have one suggestion, and Darinas asked me to present it here (as well as solicit more ideas).

                                            The idea is a Conspiracy of working class types who fight Darkspawn for minimum wages. Imagine an illegal Mexican immigrant scrubbing up bloodstains that form a Nest's foci. The contrast with the nobility is that while they both do the same job, because of an accident of birth* Nobles get fame, and magical perks, while if they're lucky the Conspiracy get enough to feed their family south of the border.

                                            I was thinking that they might be a religious group. That would justify how they have the command and control structure needed for a Conspiracy without the high status members being bad guys who rip off their workers. They'd be a secret society with members in cleaning companies, but the bosses of those companies would not be members.


                                            * Princesses earn their powers through exceptional merit in their first life. But they only need to be above average in their subsequent lives to regain their powers.
