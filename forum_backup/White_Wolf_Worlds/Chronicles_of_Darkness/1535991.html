    Hunter The Vigil 'Mortal' Remain

    05-20-2024, 12:03 PM
    What would be hunter opinion on merit based supernatural? And how would they deal with them?
    Atariya
    General
    Most hunter called them by the name "Lord/Lady Luck" as they seem to be at the whim of fate, they are alway lucky, even if they seem to be in bad situation, but they often are the only one to survive, they win a lot, and then spend it all away, but they never seem to really mind, because they always get something new in return. While not actively hurting anybody, their luck alway seem to come at a cost of someone else luck, causing accident, misfortune, and even death to occur.
    The Response
    While Atariya may look human, it's actually rather easy for Hunter to find them, since their luck often make them a big time gossip, often going on TV interview, local newspaper, or even online forum. The hard part is actually killing them, since their luck give them huge advantage, best case is they're inexperience and mainly use their luck to do thing, the worst case is they're both skilled and have a potent luck pool, with connection to some real high place.
    Compact
    There're 3 big obstacle to Compact hunting Atariya, 1. They often have connection to high place, that a Compact can't reach or get involve in without being in serious danger of either getting killed, capture, or even just found out. 2. Their luck could spell doom for the hunter, because you can't really predict how the luck will take effect, other than the Atariya being fine, while everyone around them suffer, and Compact can't just suffer a hit like that to their member, so be sure to retreat when you must. 3. They often attract other monster, while this might sound great at first, either the Atariya or the other Monster will die, leaving the one of them very injure, but in practice the Atariya as the Monster fight the Hunters, leaving either of them too injure to go after the Atariya, and when they attract another Atariya, it become almost impossible to kill them.
    Ashwood Abbey
    The Abbey have a very interesting relationship with the Lord/Lady Luck, since there're Atariya among their members, because of Atariya thrillseeking and wealth, they're often prime member for Abbey, the Atariya of the Abbey have no problem killing other Atariya, they see it as one less competitor for luck, and see it as the ultimate thrill killing another person who have the same advantage as themselve. The Abbey will first sent an invitation to the Atariya, they either accept it, or be under the same rule as any other monster.
    The Long Night
    The Long Night are split into three different group of thought, one group think it as a blessing from god, blessing these men and women potent luck. One group think that these lady and lord of luck are just witch who make deal with the devil to take luck but cause other to have misfortune. And one group think that these lady and lord of luck are natural, and shouldn't be hunted down, but teach them how to use their power properly. Atariya within the Long Night often use their luck to give back to the community, and praying to god, thanking him for his incredible luck.
    The Loyalist of Thule
    The Loyalist, are interested in documenting the Atariya more than hunting them, because the Atariya luck ability that seem to develop randomly interested the Loyalist. Loyalist have easy time with Atariya since their activity are easy to track, unless the Atariya don't want them to. The Atariya within the compact are curious about the origin of their luck, and how they could get even more lucky.
    Network Zero Atariya are both Network Zero worst nightmare and best dream, since they often appear on public, testing their luck, and spreading their fame, unfortunetly for the compact, their abilities aren't really obivious, and could be done with simple sfx and editing. They luck also influence the media, making them the biggest sensation in one moment, to a nobody in the next, making spreading their information fickle. Atariya within Network Zero often want to get famous, so they record themselve doing amazing feat of luck, or use that luck to record other supernatural being.

                      Nine Stars
                      The Atariya themselves doesn't really interest the compact, but their luck often attract slasher, Nine Stars usually use the Atariya to lure out Slasher, while the Slasher is too busy trying to kill the Atariya. The Nine Stars carefully start to lay down trap, capturing the Slasher before they could escape or do any more harm. After being used as bait, the Atariya will either join the Compact for the thrill or just escape from Hong Kong.
                      Null Mysteriis
                      The compact have always been interested in studying the phenomena that is luck, but the problem is actually trying to test luck, since luck isn't very tangible, and can't really be tell when it's skill, luck, or just pure chance. The Atariya is the perfect subject for Null Mysteriis, since their luck are much more overt than the average person, giving them much more substantial result. The theory on luck is that it a invisible and intangible force that exist and flow through everything, while it's seem a bit far fetch at first, all the test on on the Atariya seem to point in the same conclusion. Further testing need to be done on how to manipulate this luck force, but the Atariya seem to be happy to help, possibly out of greed or curiousity.
                      SWORN
                      The SWORN have no interest in the Atariya, they see that they're just human who have been bless with amazing luck, so they don't usually interact with them unless they're within SWORN or native territory. They will ususally use legal mean to punish the Atariya if they start to cause trouble to them or their territory. The Atariya usually join the SWORN because of their own heritage.
                      The Union
                      The Atariya isn't welcome to any neighborhood the Union control, their power cause many misfortune for the neighborhood, they often tend to get banish by The Union who are within the area. The Union take extreme measure when the Atariya doesn't leave even after tell them to go away, which leave most Atariya dead, or finally leave the community. Some of the Atariya who been banish by the Union sometime get salty, and use their luck to convict member sometime even entire cell of the Union for crime they might or might haven't done.

                          Conspiracies
                          Thy tend to have a much better time dealing with the Atariya than the compacts.

                          Aegis Kai Doru
                          The Enemy
                          The Atariya often get mistaken for witch by Aegis Kai Doru, because they often tend to have mystical trapping to their power. But Aegis Kai Doru have now learn that, their power doesn't come from the mystical pratice or ritual, but instead from a outside source that is able to manipulate fate. They seem to be attracted to other object and being that can affect or see the 'flow of luck'. Atariya sometime steal Aegis Kai Doru artifact, especially when they're artifact that connected to fate. Recently there is a report on one Atariya who manage to somehow find the artifact that is store in Athen, and the worst they manage to escape the conspiracies grasp. While their luck is powerful it can be disrupted by an artifact that deal with fate, even if it temporary.
                          Response
                          Generally Aegis Kai Doru killed Atariya on sight, but it's harder to do than said, they often escape from trap and ambush. If the Atariya is found to possess artifact especially if it a missing artifact from Aegis Kai Doru vault. They become the focus of every cell nearby, most of the time they retrive the artifact, but very rarely, it ended up leading to the entire area becoming devoid of Aegis Kai Doru hunter.
                          The Sword have the most experience with the Atariya, but know almost nothing about them other than that some of their target seemingly vanish as they about to finish them off.
                          The Temple have not seen the Atariya breaking into their artifact collection since the incident at Athen, but they won't let it happen again, they have slowly been collect artifact of fate and put it all in one location to kill as many Atariya as possible, but so far no Atariya have come even near it.
                          The Scroll have the least experience actually encountering the Atariya but hold the most knowledge about how the Atariya gain their power. It seem that they often gain their power an act that have connection to fate and luck, but if so, how did they manage to get the power when other people who do the same thing don't? Is it just luck?
                          Relic: Astragaloi
                          The ancient greek use dice as divination tool, but this relic influence your fate instead of telling your future. But since the relic is so powerful, it usually only use when the hunter is desperate. The Player and The Chronicler must agree on what count as an important roll before this power activate.
                          Cost: 3 willpower (Once per day)
                          Action: Instant
                          Dice Pool: 1
                          Duration: 24 hours
                          Roll Result
                          1: Lose 6 dice on a important roll, and that roll result become one level lower.
                          2-3: Lose 6 dice on an important roll, and remove one success from the result.
                          4-5: Lose 6 dice on an important roll.
                          6-7: Gain 6 dice on an important roll.
                          8-9: Gain 6 dice on an important roll, and gain one automatic success.
                          10: Gain 6 dice on a important roll, and that roll result become one higher.

                                                          There are plenty circumstances that might lead hunters to anyone. If the goal is to tell a story about a given person being hunted, certainly it isn't hard to do.

                                                          But when the question is if it happens in general, what the default stance would be, then we have to consider how the splat would be perceived in average (as far as averages go).

                                                          So... Would them even perceive the Atariya? I mean, at all? How do you even notice this is a thing, or that someone is one?

                                                          My opinion is that unless something overtly supernatural marks the making of the Atariya, at the very least, they may not notice themselves there's anything special about them.

                                                          Why would a Hunter even target those people? Just because they're lucky?

                                                          Under average circumstances, only a very careful, thorough investigation would reveal this person is unnaturally lucky, and it would be even harder to find out they're sucking the luck of others.

                                                          Even if some Hunter finds out, they're probably assuming this person to be a witch, the same thing as a Mage or any other human who happens to be a magic user. There's zero reasons to distinguish between those types from the point of view of a Hunter.

                                                          And I personally doubt Hunters have a standard approach to such beings. They're humans, after all, and not every Hunter would be willing to just hunt people unprovoked, just because they know how to use some magic.

                                                          Certainly, some Hunters would do just that. Some would find sorcerers of any kind even more abhorrent than inhuman freaks. Others... Not so much, being more prone to intervene only with proof of some crime or something like that. Some would welcome any such magic user who're willing to embrace the Vigil.
