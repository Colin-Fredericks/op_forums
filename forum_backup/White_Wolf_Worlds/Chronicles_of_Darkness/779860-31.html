            Originally posted by Killerclown View Post
            What do you think of the storyhook? Also, what would Echnida herself think of the group. Would she view them as temporary expendable servants or as secondary servants she likes but not as much as her sirens?
            I think it's well enough. I don't think Sirens would have much of an incentive to kill him unless if they knew exactly what he was advocating in his company, or at least knew what his company was into. And even then, I think that the Celestial Currents would at their best, try to kill him and replace him with someone who's more...easily swayed towards ending the inhumane experiments. The Abyssals on the other hand would either make the most of the knowledge, kill him without trying to make things better or place someone more favored towards him and subsume the company to themselves. The first is Cocytus, Acheron and Phlegethon, the second is Styx and the third is Lethe and Flensers. :3

            As for Echidna herself, I imagine that would depend on her level of involvement in the company. She does have her own experiments to pull and she leaves interaction with the prediluvian world to her Sirens. If the company doesn't do well, then she wouldn't exactly care and see them as only good to continue their experiments...but as her test subjects. If the company does well enough and helps expand her "research", then she'll see them as a potential asset to be looked into and protected. Regardless, if they fail then she has her plans to try and take as many personnel into the Celestial Ocean and straight to her, she's always wanting more humans to make into her perfect race and her children, the Vodianoi.

            I would say though, as both a word of god on Siren and because I love speculating this sort of stuff, that Echidna loves all of humanity and Sirens to the point where she's basically blind to how much harm her eugenics studies does to the people she kidnaps. She believes that she knows what's best for Humanity, and everything she does is for their own good. She has a looser leash for Sirens, since she views them as more capable of surviving without her help, but she still wishes for each and every one of them to join her cause. For hers is the one that survived the Deluge itself.
