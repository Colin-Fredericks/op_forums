                                                I am the sort of dev who likes to know what's going into a book before writers start work; deviant has the *structure* of its powers and traits laid out, with some of those traits lacking names. Pool A + Pool B resisted by Pool C kind of things.

                                                In werewolf terms, we know how gifts work and how you gain them by increasing renown, but we haven't named the five renown types or made any example gifts yet.

                                                (Deviant does not use werewolf's gift system. This is for illustrative purposes only).

                                                Some Devs like to give an outline consisting if "we need some form of powers here", and some prefer to hand a writer lines they have to colour within. It's only my second gameline, but I'm definitely turning out to be the latter.

                                                See also Awakening's writer's bible being longer than some of the game's supplements.

                                                Dave Brookshaw
