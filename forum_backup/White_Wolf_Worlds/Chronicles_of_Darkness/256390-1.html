                    In a certain light, some interpretations of characters like Ghost Rider or the Spectre could be inspiration for vigilante-style Sin-Eaters.

                    Todd McFarlane's Haunt comic series is another one about a former priest who's literally haunted by the ghost of his paramilitary secret agent brother, and manifests supernatural powers when they possess each other. (The story and characters were kind of hit-or-miss for me when I read it, but that's true for a lot of McFarlane's stuff and the overall concept still works as inspiration.)
