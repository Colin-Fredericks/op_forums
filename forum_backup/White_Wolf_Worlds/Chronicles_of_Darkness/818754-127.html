                                Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                                My players used when in Frenzy against enemies they cannot overwhelm with just physical attribute and in a lot of other occasions. Always under the principle: Is this the most direct and violent way to achieve your goal without risking final death??
                                Vampires are durable, is the thing, and Majesty and Dominate are the specific purview of the two clans that are most invested in unlife with people around that frenzy complicates at best and annihilates at worst. The Ventrue curse is set up such that the two biggest coping mechanisms in their toolbox are proxies and desensitization, the latter of which only helps with the impact of breaking points and which actively lengthens the duration of failure to ride the wave in practical terms; the Daeva are less directly attached to the get-a-subordinate-to-do-it methodology but also have the most suitable common Disciplines to the acts of violence and/or escape.

                                The Storytelling chapter emphasizes that frenzy is a loss of control for vampires, and specifically contrasts that with the greater control Disciplines afford them — it seems entirely in keeping with the fiction that a character under the thrall of their Beast's immediate desires will be less able to benefit from the specific psychic abilities that let them control people in any sort of refined manner; in Beast: the Primordial's terms, a frenzied vampire leans on Atavisms instead of Nightmares, because animal caution does not afford greater facility with precision tools than blunt instruments and words are a precision tool. The "psychic" Disciplines are going to see more basic use by the Beast than the vampire proper if they see any use at all.
