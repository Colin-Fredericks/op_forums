            I have never understood this forums reflexive distaste for anything that seems vaguely Christian. And remember,this is coming from a non Christian person.                                                 Originally posted by Incendax View Post
                                                what does any of this have to do with the established mythology and tropes of angels or demons?
                                                The idea of a link between christian/gnostic mythology and modern science may in fact have a precursor in the works of Hans Jonas, a german philosopher and student of Heidegger now better known for his contributions to bioethics ("the principle of responsibility"). From his studies of late classical religious texts, he developed an understanding of the phenomenon of gnosticism as a form of "ancient nihilism" parallell to the modern subjugation of nature through the use of technology: whereas early christian sub-sects like manichaeans or simonists believed the world to be a prison for the soul wrought by an uncaring god, thus evil and to be overcome, existentialism and materialism share their contempt based on their own ideological dualism ("anthropological acosmism") between consciousness and (dead) matter. The jump is not too far at all if one considers this cosmological alienation to be common ground.
