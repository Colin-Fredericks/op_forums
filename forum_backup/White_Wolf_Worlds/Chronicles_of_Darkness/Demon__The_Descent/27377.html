          A couple of notes that you may want to change/include:
          Liar’s Tongue: Everything a demon says registers as true. Unless they want it to register false.
          Exploits: Unsubtle powers demons learns by overclocking Embeds. "demons learns". Just a typo here I assume.
          Partial Transformations: For 1 Aether per ability, you can manifest some of your Form abilities without assuming full Demonic Form. You must make a Compromise roll (+1 per each ability not assumed). It costs 1 Aether to put them all away. I would add that you HAVE to put them all away before you can bring out new ones.

                              I think the idea is that it plays into the compromise roll and the Aether expenditure. Each time you partially transform you have to decide which abilities you think you will need to have; the more you have the more effective you can be but the more costly and riskier it is. If you find yourself without an ability that you need then you chose wrong the first time and have to pay for it.

                              It seems to fit the spy genre very well for me, i.e. How much gear do you take on the op? More means you are more prepared but easier to be exposed. It's a balancing act.
