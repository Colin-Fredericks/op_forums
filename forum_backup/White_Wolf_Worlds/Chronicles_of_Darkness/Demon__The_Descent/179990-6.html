                Where I see a power that lets a demon go undercover as another type of supernatural being, biting his nails every step of the way but able to pull it off, you see a game-breaking Sam Haight Special that your players will use to make your game unfun. That's okay. I've been GMing for more than 25 years, and I've banned or houseruled my fair share of powers.

                I suggest checking out some of the many other Embeds, Exploits, and demon form powers that Flowers of Hell has to offer. Apple of Discord has been called out as a fun Cacophony Embed, Associate and Integrate as Mundane, and Demon Car as a nifty (if particularly audacious) Exploit. I'm personally fond of Play Possum, Call Out, Like the Movies, and Open and Shut Case. Strength Through Adversity is just fun and evil, and I can think of a zillion in-genre ways to use the Aquatic Propulsion.
