                                                        If that's what you'd like to do, let me know. I don't want to impose either. I'll have the post ready for here and the IC thread though if that's not the case.
                                                        What sort of stories do you lean toward that are supported better by the other gamelines though, if you don't mind me asking. With no practical experience beyond what we're playing now and a couple one-shots/aborted games over the years, I can only say that what stood out and continues to for me with Demon is the reversal of roles in the starting point for a character. This is the most basic of differences probably and cursory of reads but what I mean is with Vampire, Werewolf, Changeling and Geist---you start human and then have to reconcile somehow with your change, holding onto that humanity also. Mage and Hunter are respectively learning how to be superhuman or just human. I don't know how to fit Mummy into this startling revelation(bolded for self-deprecating sarcasm---just to be clear). Promethean and Demon do it in reverse, in different ways--both that I find really interesting. I just don't feel confident enough to play Promethean yet even if someone offered to ST tomorrow. Wouldn't do it justice.
                                                        Went far afield and didn't come close to answering what you didn't really ask. I'll try now. One story I'm looking forward to playing someday with Demon needs the KS Demon Child supplement to make it happen which I'll just call "Brady Bunch" for now. But something that I feel could be fun out the box would be just starting with a "Leverage" or "Chuck" type agency from the get go. Three to five players and an ST. First session would be a mission. Next few would be one-shots for each player with the ST and the other players respectively portraying people in a flashback to the Fall leading to how the player of the week came to be in the agency. Afterwards, back to your regularly scheduled programming. Something light that doesn't focus on the backstabbing primarily.
                                                        Whether we continue or not, I'm grateful to both of you for where we've come. I'm still super wet behind the ears but it's helped me build my confidence for the future.
                                                        The thing you asked about:
                                                        Ok. I've thought a little about this but more details are gelling together as I'm looking through everything that's happened so far as well as a closer look at the painting. On the surface, the painting was only going to be a payday for Tommy (the cover---I've thought of them as one and the same, which accounts for some of my slip-ups, emphasis on some) toward becoming a partner at Daisy Antiques.
                                                        The original mission detials associated with it that the God Machine deigned to tell Yesod (the once non-infected angel) amounted to little more than sensitive information that needed to be picked up. Yesod was tapped to retrieve it because he was good at quick extraction and asking no questions, like an angel should be. I've tried to show ambivalence about whether Tommy wants to go back, a dab of Integrator.
                                                        In reality, the painting is an unknown quantity. There is a detailed list that was hidden inside of it. Angels who've fallen in the area and two names left on the list of angels to target for falling. Ms. Greene might be a previous name on the list as well as that destroyer from the park. I'm only going off of what was talked about briefly in the OOC thread for supposition purposes.
                                                        The origin of the list is not known or hasn't been made known to anyone. No-one wants to take responsibility as it would tip the intent behind playing the hand. It's possible the God Machine created it for the purpose of tracking those who have fallen and those that appear to be in danger of it. It's possible that the demons who went to the pier that night to try and retrieve it were the original authors. If that's the case, how did they get their information?
                                                        One thing that intrigued me in looking at the painting was the people on the ground are plucking one of the fallen angels and looking at the skyward ones with a sort of supplication. There's a befuddled looking humanoid chicken woman in the upper right being held by an angel. The God Machine might have created the list for the purpose of doing something new with Stigmatics.
                                                        People like Mr. Violet can be made into pliable, more durable vessels to inform and offer their services by harvesting fallen demons and angels. Stigmatics are being repurposed into something that isn't human, exile, demon or angel. Humanity needs to be herded in a direction that will bring them closer to machinery. The GM is working on terraforming by assimilating people.
                                                        Or: the GM is interested in generating reliable bursts of Aetheric energy beyond the waste cast-offs from its operations currently being run. If it engineers falls and skirmishes, it can harness that energy for the purpose of maybe rounding up rebel demons such as Jack(Tommy's hijacker) with storehouse traps.
                                                        At the least, there is value in the list because there are still two angels that will fall. Jack isn't letting Tommy know that explictly but Tommy might infer that was the intent of the list. Even if that happens though, Tommy is still a stranger to his new condition and he has an interloper lurking inside of him that is loyal to the cause that he was willing to die for. He still is being hunted by Ms. Greene, Mr. Violet and an angel probably. There are two different criminal organizations that want money from him: Marzini, who he works for, and Yuri who wants compensation for the vase as well as saving Tommy's life.
                                                        Did Tommy really manage to download every bit of data from the blueprint that was hidden behind the frame of the painting? Even if he did, are there still secrets to be gleaned from the painting itself that he doesn't know about? Can he afford to sell this painting to appease his mundane enemies or does he hold onto it for fear of his estranged angelic family. Could it be his ticket back once the drop window closes. These and possibly more questions are associated with this boned mission.
                                                        That's what I have right now. More might occur to both of us as things progress.
