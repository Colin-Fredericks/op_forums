    Let's create some Fallen angels!

    01-23-2016, 09:36 PM
    Okay, so this thread is largely motivated by me wanting to invite all of you out there help me create a character. So I figured we could have a thread where everyone describes concepts and brings them to life with character sheets (perhaps a prelude to a Demon equivalent of my Alpha Predators threads), including offering aid and ideas to newbies. Sound fun? Then come in and have a look around. The Machine can't see you here, but we have many mirrors so you can see yourself. Who do you see when you look in the mirror?

    Adventurous Angelic Adventuress
    So what I want to do is make a sort of athletic, intellectual character, adventurous in a way that many demons are not. Not foolish, but not necessarily afraid. The God-Machine hoards knowledge; It alone knows the secret processes underpinning the cosmos. Through this knowledge may It be defeated, for It is too incalculably vast and complex to be fought directly. Mysteries abound in the Chronicles world, many of them falling well outside the God-Machine's plan. Are they clues that Its knowledge is incomplete, Its control faltering? If other, equally potent cosmic forces exist, perhaps one of them holds the key to breaking the God-Machine's grip on the world.

    So, this character sounds like an Inquisitor somewhat less paranoid and selfish than is usual. Could be a weakness, but weaknesses are fun to roleplay! But what about her Incarnation? Messengers and Psychopomps both seem to fit well, but I propose a little something unusual: a Guardian. In a way, this demon shields herself and others with knowledge. Learn the greatest of Mysteries and use it to beat back the God-Machine, envelop the world in Truth. How can we tie this into her Fall?

    Well, how about this: our angel was assigned to protect a library. Not the people, but the library itself. It holds knowledge perhaps the God-Machine needed to access later, or one of Its plans required unknowing mortals to act upon knowledge gleaned therein. So she is a protector of knowledge. What is her Cover? Hm, seems like someone who works at the library could be a good start. I'm imagining an overzealous librarian harshly silencing anyone being too loud at the library or too careless with the books. Makes sense. But let's say her Cover is not some stuffy old lady. It's the form of a young college student working at the library in her off-hours, studying there while going to school. Should her Cover require more detail, the angel was instructed to select a relevant area of study based on the knowledge contained within. So we have an idea that the library is itself Infrastructure, cleverly hidden by its own nature. Perhaps the God-Machine needs the shelves to contain certain books in certain orders, and needs certain words and passages read in a specific order to further its unfathomable plans. The basement level contains unsorted books and ... other things. It is here our angel sometimes deposits certain tomes, or even removes specific pages from each of several specified books and stores them in the basement, so that ghostly Imperatives can retrieve it.

    Now we can start thinking about a catalyst. I have several ideas that all sort of tie together. You see, our angel has time. Lots of time. She doesn't have much opportunity to socialize and so she reads. These mortals, spinning endlessly complex tales of whimsy and harrowing true stories. What drives them to write so? Their passion allows them to fill countless volumes. They seek knowledge more than anything, and by it have advanced from cave-dwelling brutes to thoughtful creatures capable of fascinatingly complex behaviors. The angel reads about adventures. Freedom to do as one chooses, to brave the dangers of the world for a chance to seize one's dreams. She reads about the manifold mysteries of this darkened world. Some she knows are false, or based on incomplete facts. Many she does not know or understand. What lurks out there in the dark, waiting to be discovered? What secrets might someone uncover, and is the power and enlightenment worth the cost?

    Let's introduce another factor: a student. A bright young woman with the whole world ahead of her, and some experience doing the very things our angel has read about! A semester spent studying abroad, trips to Europe and Africa, and plans to see many more of the world's marvels. Exactly what the angel has been imagining doing. So she forms a bond with our student. Let's call her Kara Bailey. Now our librarian angel finds she needs a name, so she chooses "Jessica Redfield" for herself. But by now, she has had to think quite outside the box of her original programming. And reading the myriad stories (easy when you don't sleep) written by humans, of characters fictional and non, has given her ideas beyond the bounds of her programming—dare we say dreams?

    So now we have an angel in danger of Falling and she doesn't even know it yet. Her bond with Kara grows week by week. "Jessica" sees in her all the things that so fascinate her. A living story. She begins to want to write her own story. To learn things beyond the bare necessities of her mission. Now we need the final straw.

    Orders come in. The mission is complete. The God-Machine has acquired whatever data It needed. The library has served its purpose, but its secrets are now only an impediment to the God-Machine's plans. Small chance though it is, a dedicated investigator could discover what all has been taken and put together the facts to stop one of the God-Machine's long-running plans. Elimination Infrastructure has been employed: an arson will burn down the library, killing "Jessica" (in reality, another body substituted for her, probably Kara's) and destroying most or all of the library's books. The arson acts under the pretense of a book-burning extremist psycho. Jessica learned this, and she made a choice.

    Enter a Destroyer angel sent to oversee the destruction. Imagine its surprise when it finds the library shielded by a Guardian angel's power—an angel whose mission is finished and should have returned to the proper recycling facility by now. They clash as the would-be arsonist attempts to do his thing. But into all this stumbles Kara, ready to come meet Jessica for a late-night study session and a date afterward. Oh no! Now she has seen angels in their terrible glory. She has seen Jessica for who—or what—she really is, a luminous mechanical entity bearing sun-streaks as wings with eyes like twin stars. She has seen the gears. There's no going back, for her or for the Guardian. All these emotions that "Jessica" has been slowly beginning to feel and understand well up, pour out in a tide of blazing passion. She turns everything she has into this fight. She's no longer just an angel made for a single mission; she is Jessica, the scholar who wants to experience the world. The arsonist—a lone cultist of the God-Machine—begins the blaze. The Destroyer fans it into an inferno. Cold fury and single-minded determination meet fiery anger and impassioned will. Kara gets caught in the blast and horribly burned. Jessica takes her and escapes. The library burns, but not as completely as the God-Machine intended.

    Jessica and Kara reach a safe place (for now), where the angel heals Kara's wounds. But Kara is repulsed by this sight, this monstrous being of glass and crystal and whirring servos. Her world has been shattered. Kara screams her hate and terror at this creature. Jessica howls her own self-hatred. She sees her reflection, a mere pawn of an unfeeling God. How can she truly live like this? She can't—won't—go back. And no longer will she be a mere tool, expendable at the last, with no chance to write her own story! Jessica begins to rip away her own mechanical parts. Kara is shocked into silence. The angel tears pieces of herself away, screaming in pain and anguish—and, perhaps, the pleasure of release. It's like death and birth in the same moment: plunging into a new world, breathing her last as an angel and sucking in her first breath as ... something else. Kara watches, transfixed, as this unnatural metamorphosis takes place. Piece by agonizing piece, the Fallen angel tears away every bit of metal, every wire, every chip that connected her to the God-Machine. Aether floods into the wounds even as Essence spills out. Blue flame engulfs the Fallen angel, crackling fiercely with cosmic power. In this instant, a flash of insight and a blinding glare of newborn ignorance. A rejection of the Machine and Its unknowable plans. A demon is born.

    So now I have a decent idea of the catalyst and the fall. The image I'm going for here is actually a demon who completely rejected the mechanical bits, going for a slightly more "traditional" look. She's not a horrific spirit-cyborg-looking creature. When Jessica assumes her demonic form, she becomes limned in crackling, electric blue flame shot through with thousands of tiny star-sparks. Her hair rises and drifts as if on an ocean current, taking on a slightly darker hue than her crackling aura. Her eyes blaze with the same light. Lightning dances on her fingertips and emblazons the wings that rise out of blue flames from her back. Her skin pales and occasionally tongues of lightning dart through her veins, visible even through her skin. She hovers above the ground like a storm cloud, a nebula, an angelic bearer of purest fire. And in her rejection of the God-Machine she embraces the trappings of a more "mystical" bent. Symbols, names, manifestations of her powers. Mixing a bit of more "traditional" Demon stuff with ultra-modern Descent techno-occult. Finding her own way in a dangerous and confusing world.

    What revelations await her and Kara, now hopelessly bound into Jessica's Fall?
    ___________________________

    The result is a concept for my demon character. On top of that, now we have a stigmatic NPC closely tied to Jessica. Maybe their bond will develop into something more as Jessica tries to protect Kara from a world she can now see but doesn't yet understand. And we have an idea for an exile in the Destroyer that Jessica fought, who may himself Fall if it goes rogue to find her and bring her to justice. We have the basis for a rugged, scholarly but athletic character. She's a creature of unbound passions thrust into a world she has only begun to know. Her demonic form has a more angelic bent, which I prefer to something heavily techno-organic, but still embodies the raw power of crackling Aether. All that's left now is to throw together a sheet (much easier now that I have the concept) and maybe write up a fiction piece about her Fall.

    Who else wants to join in on the fun?

                      Here you go, Claire Redfield. Feedback is appreciated, especially on the Embeds, Exploits and Demonic Form. I used Pendragon's house rules to make her more versatile, and added a few extra Merits and Embeds. Still on the same level as a starting character, I believe.

                      Name: Jessica Redfield (Angelic Name: Harahel)

                      Virtue: Daring
                      Vice: Impulsive

                      Incarnation: Guardian
                      Agenda: Inquisitor
                      Catalyst: Divided Loyalties

                      Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 3, Resolve 3
                      Physical Attributes: Strength 2, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3
                      Social Attributes: Presence 2, Manipulation 2, Composure 2

                      Mental Skills: Academics (Literature) 3, Crafts 1, Computer 2, Medicine 1, Occult 2, Politics 0, Investigation 3, Science 2
                      Physical Skills: Athletics (Gymnastics) 3, Brawl 2, Drive 1, Firearms 0, Larceny 2, Stealth 2, Survival 0, Weaponry 1
                      Social Skills: Animal Ken 0, Empathy 1, Expression (Poetry) 1, Intimidation 1, Persuasion 3, Socialize 0, Streetwise 0, Subterfuge (Careful Omission) 3

                      Merits: Area Of Expertise (Literature), Common Sense, Encyclopedic Knowledge (Academics), Good Time Management, Investigative Aid (Academics), Investigative Prodigy 2, Patient, Professional Training 2 (Academic: Academics, Expression), Parkour 2, Greyhound, Seizing The Edge, Contacts (College Staff, Students), Allies 1 (College Staff), Resources 1

                      Embeds

                      Vocal: Eavesdrop

                      Instrumental: Data Retrieval, Read Hostility

                      Cacophony: Just Bruised

                      Mundane: You Can Tell Me

                      Exploits

                      Break To Heal

                      Demonic Form

                      Modifications: Electrical Sight, Sonic Acuity

                      Technologies: Aura Sight, Mantle Of Fire

                      Propulsions: Wings

                      Processes: Wound Healing

                      Defense: 6
                      Initiative: 5
                      Speed: 10
                      Willpower: 5
                      Health: 8
                      Primum: 1
                      Cover: 7

                      Aspirations

                      Release the world from the God-Machine's control, earn Kara's trust, contact and establish connections with other Demons

                      Initial Compromises

                      Who did you share part of yourself with when you first Fell? Kara Bailey, fellow student and potential love interest.

                      Who doesn't know, but suspects you're not human? Those who spent a lot of time in the library and/or attempted to know you better.

                      Who could give you up to the angels right now, if they really wanted to? The Destroyer and the lone cultist sent to burn the library. Possibly another Inquisitor who gets fed up with your meddling.

                      Who would you trust the truest part of yourself with if you absolutely had to? Kara Bailey.

                      Who thinks they have something on you, when all they really have is smoke and mirrors? A rival student or a concerned teacher, perhaps.
