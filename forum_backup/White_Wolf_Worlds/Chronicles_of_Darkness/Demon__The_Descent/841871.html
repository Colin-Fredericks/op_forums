    Ask a Question: Cover Recycling

    03-04-2016, 10:14 AM
    So, I think in Flowers of Hell it is established that Demons can trade Cover elements between themselves via Pacts (they can't grant each other Benefits, of course, neither being actually mortal).

    Can a Demon trade Cover elements to themselves, as a means of shifting certain traits between Covers?

    Example: Say a Demon, Exampiel, has the Covers, John and Carl. John is married to Mary, and Exampiel is very fond of the relationship. But John's Cover starts to degrade (be it through investigation, or other Compromises). Exampiel forms a Pact with himself, trading the Cover element of "married to Mary" to himself (likely paying the hefty Willpower cost for getting nothing in return), then integrating it into the Carl Cover. So now Carl is married to Mary (and thus Exampiel remains so), even if the John Cover gets degraded into nothing.

    Corollary: Can abstract Cover Experiences get recycled/refunded? Can a Demon deconstruct Cover A, then reconstruct a Patch Job with some elements gleaned from A, plus a few new ones?

    Yossarian: "And lo, it was optimal."

          Kind of funny this is probably the only game I can think of where you can make a valid argument for naming your character example.

          Anyway onto the question at hand. I don't think demons innately posses an ability to let them dissect their own covers for elements. The closest I found in the books was a mention that a demon could willingly dissolve any cover they want without going loud so long as they have another cover to swap into. Though in the case of purposefully dissolving your own cover it just mentions the cover falls to the void. In which case I think its fair to assume that elements of a dissolved cover can't be recovered or kept around for use in another cover.

          Now the idea of making a pact with one's self is rather interesting. Sticking with Mr. Exampiel's plight for a bit. The biggest problem Exampiel has is that John and Carl are for all intensive purposes not actually people they're just really nice suits Exampiel puts on. Thus Exampiels problem is that this would be like trying to broker a sale of your left hand's property to your right hand. The problem is that as both are still your hands all that they have is actually yours and thus the property is not the right hand's to buy nor is it the left hand's to sell.

          Now here's where it gets fun. The most obvious work around to transferring John's marriage to Carl is to acquire a third party. This is the simplest method of handling it as it is just another contract.* Now for a more complicated work around there is a fun one trick. If you can figure out a way to make Mary the owner of John's marriage (or at least recognizable as being authorized to represent John in negotiations) then Exampiel can negotiate with Mary to claim the relationship held by John ,but belonging to Mary.



          *The party of the seller hereto referred to as Exampiel agrees to pay the second party hereto referred to as Broker in exchange for services provided.

          Broker agrees to extract take temporary possession of the history of one John Exa's marriage to Mary Exa ,hereafter referred to as "the relationship, for a period of at least 5 but no more than 7 minutes. At which point the relationship is to be presented to Exampiel.

          Exampiel agrees upon services rendered to surrender (1) incident of a well known car accident brought about by about foolish teenage indiscretion, (2) the subsequent physical scarring along the left arm from the first item.

                  I would allow it, but to not make things so easy.
                  I would determine that you can only take parts of your own cover if you completely dissolve it.
                  Lets say Exampiel has now a cover rate of 5 (and it starts degrading) and he wants to save his marriage before too late. I would say that if he wants to patch his marriage to Carl, he needs to dissolve that cover, and change it back to cover points at a loss (- 1) for the system is not perfect and there is always a loss of energy. Hence he has now 4 dots of cover with each dot necessarily representing a specific trait of his life as John. (meaning that he could not dissolve this into 12 generic cover beats).
                  Even though there is a difference into major and minor traits in contracts, I would rule that each of this 4 dots can gather any (major or minor) aspect of john's life, like: 1 dot - his marriage to Mary. 1 dot - his best friend; etc etc etc.
