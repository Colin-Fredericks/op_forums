            Finders, Keepers (Cacophonic)
            Matter and energy cannot be created or destroyed (baring edge cases). So too with the more abstract resources, as Demons collecting Aether from Angelic Essence know. You can scavenge the energies others spend or lose to replenish your own. When activating this Embed, the Demon chooses some expendable resources they possess (such as Willpower or Aether) and tries to draw in like energies.
            Dice Roll: Dexterity + Larceny
            Action: Reflexive
            Dramatic Failure: You've fumbled your scavenging attempt and end up losing whatever your were trying to take, be it Willpower, Aether, or some other resource.
            Failure: Whatever you were trying to recover slips through your metaphorical fingers.
            Success: Whenever someone else expends or loses the resource declared when activating the Embed this turn, you gain it in a 2-to-1 ratio. You can only recover a number of points in this way up to your Successes. For example, if you are trying to collect Willpower, and 3 Willpower points are spent this turn, you gain 1 point of Willpower. If 4 had been spent, you would have gained 2, unless you only rolled a single Success - in which case, you'd still only gain 1 Willpower point.
            Exceptional Success: You are able to cast your net far and wide, letting little escape your pull. You instead can harvest any expended resources instead of declaring a type (though not all may be useful or convertible, as determined by the Storyteller). Total points collected between types are still limited by the total successes rolled.
            Notes: It is up to the Storyteller how broad the resources harvested can be. Health is probably not a good idea, but it is possible some of the other Supernatural power sources might be converted into Aether for the Demon, perhaps at a reduced rate of 3-to-1 or more. Angelic Essence is probably best left to normal Aether collection mechanisms.
            [Comments: Maybe this should be targeted and penalized, but last the Scene? Modify the efficiency? This makes a great spring-board to various other "leeching" effects, for the inclusion into Gadgets, Interlocks, Lambdas and such. I deliberately left the "resources" collected open ended, in case someone is using non-standard ones in their game, like Fatigue, Strain, etc., or if this is incorporated into a Gadget given to another being.]

                                Inspired by GibberingEloquence's post here.

                                Face Off (Mundane)
                                Sometimes Demons need to change their appearance without changing their actual Cover. When unobserved, they can make alterations to their physical form (but not their clothes - for that, see Quick Change). Note that these cannot be drastic changes - no restored or missing limbs, etc., it is limited to only those that could be accomplished with mundane disguise, given sufficient skill, time, and supplies (but handled instantaneously via the Embed). For more thorough deception, Cover alterations or Exploits are warranted.
                                Dice Roll: Manipulation + Subterfuge
                                Action: Reflexive
                                Dramatic Failure: Your efforts at disguise fail utterly, giving you distinguishing and obvious markings (or making you look like a clown).
                                Failure: You fail to significantly alter your appearance. Maybe some feature is off, but barely anything noticeable.
                                Success: You can alter a number of your physical features, masking your real weight, facial features, ethnicity, etc. These changes last for the remainder of the Scene.
                                Exceptional Success: Your facility with disguise exceeds mere change, letting you impersonate someone whose appearance you know (within the Embeds constraints, of course - if your current Cover is missing a leg, this won't give you a working one even if the person you are impersonating has two).
                                Notes: This doesn't produce supporting documents (see Authorized to fake that), affect clothing (see Quick Change), or alter your voice (see Vox).

                                Form of the Beast
                                Tales abound of Demons turning people or themselves into animals. With this Exploit, those stories have merit. You can alter the facet of a target's (including yourself) identity, specifically that relating to what species they are.
                                Example Prerequisites: Face Off, Animal Communication, Deep Cover
                                Dice Roll: Intelligence + Animal Ken + Primum - Stamina + Resolve
                                Action: Instant
                                Cost: 1 Aether + 1 per change in Size
                                Dramatic Failure: You alterations backfire, causing either an unintended target to transform into what you wanted, or for the target to transform into something you didn't intend (such as instead of turning a charging Stigmatic into a frog, you change them into a rhino).
                                Failure: You try to alter their species and come up with nothing, though the target gets a general sense that you tried to change their shape.
                                Success: The target transforms into the desired animal for the rest of the Scene, or until you voluntarily end the Exploit. Use the animal's Size, Speed, and other inherent traits (such as flight for most birds), but keep the target's Attributes (though the Storyteller may rule that they are expressed differently - you might be a strong squirrel, but you are still a squirrel). You may spend 1 Aether to maintain the change for additional scenes without reactivating the Exploit.
                                Exceptional Success: Your control over physical identity extends beyond shape. You can now also choose to use the animal's attributes instead of the target's (giving yourself great gorilla Strength, or the target the Intelligence of a gerbil). If mental and social scores are reduced to animal level, the target looses the ability to understand speech or act on anything other than their instincts the turn after the change occurs. You can spend 1 additional Aether each scene to maintain this effect in addition to the previous Aether spent.
                                Note: If you transform yourself or another Demon into an animal, they must still act in Cover for that animal or risk Compromise. Beings with multiple forms, such as Unchained with their Covers, or the many forms a Werewolf can take render offensive use less effective - a Demon can switch to an unaffected Cover or their Demonic Form, a Werewolf can change to a different shape, or a Mage can use their magic to transform themselves into another form (or "back" to their old one).

                                        Hey, sorry for the delay.

                                        I don't think Face-Off should have arbitrary drawbacks. I'm saying this mostly to contain my urge of limiting powers. Since this Embed is similar to Quick Change, I believe the Demon should be relatively alone and unobserved to use it. One Success is enough to make it last for a scene, but it doesn't change anything other than the appearance. In other words, you don't conjure new documents or alter the memories of observers (but you can use other Embeds and Exploits to do that). An Exceptional Success might give a +2 to Subterfuge rolls and/or Spoofing, but it wouldn't extend the duration. I imagine this Embed would be used to get out of trouble quickly and slipping into places without having your true face caught by surveillance. Extended infiltration would require more legwork, such as a new Cover, Legend, Allies Into Gold, Forced Relationship and so on.

                                        Form Of The Beast, being an Exploit, can break the "must be alone and unobserved" rule that Demons usually follow when changing their shapes. You could add one scene to the duration with 1 Aether without needing to re-roll. If you spent a willpower point on the activation roll to cancel the Compromise, you don't have to spend more, but changing into an animal that is grossly inaccurate for the area requires a Compromise of its own. You can toggle between your Covers and your chosen animal, but with an Exceptional Success, you may also toggle between different animals. You may assume partial or complete Demonic Form while being an animal, following the usual rules (if you can use Context Matters to explain your robot animal form, you get the usual benefits of that Exploit).

                                        Excellent work. How about an Exploit that allows shape-shifting on the molecular level? Instead of turning the Demon into a different person or an animal, it turns him into an object of roughly similar Size. He can become a complex machine with a minimal Size of 1, and the spent Aether allows him to work as if possessing a full battery. The maximum Size is [Primum + additional Aether spent]. He may distribute his Primum among additional Durability and/or Equipment Bonus. He may use Embeds on himself, on others and his immediate surroundings, but not Exploits. This lasts for a scene.

                                        On an Exceptional Success, the Demon may manifest up to [Primum] Demonic Form advantages as he wants with no additional Aether cost for the rest of the scene, effectively turning himself into a temporary Multi-Demonic Form Gadget. For safer and less Aether-costly items, he should build Gadgets as usual.

                                                    Miracle [Alt name? Eraser?]
                                                    Demons are naturally adept at manipulating relational features of other beings, trading relationships and addictions like currency via Pacts. But sometimes, a Demon needs to get rid of something in a more immediate manner than Pacting will allow, or from subjects who are unwilling to part with such traits. By means of this Exploit, a Demon declares some personal Condition or Tilt the target possesses, removing it from them to fizzle into nothingness. This might be beneficial, such as calming someone's fear (removing the Shaken or Frightened Condition), "healing" the limbs (removing Arm Wrack or Leg Wrack Tilts), or detrimental, such as clouding their clarity of though (removing the Informed Condition).
                                                    Example Prerequisites: Anarchism, Breakdown, On The Mend
                                                    Dice Roll: Intelligence + Medicine + Primum
                                                    Action: Instant
                                                    Cost: 2 Aether
                                                    Dramatic Failure: Rather than remove the desired effect, the Demon gives the target a undesirable (to the Demon) Condition or Tilt.
                                                    Failure: The Aether fizzles into nothing as the Demon is unable to break the connection between effect and target.
                                                    Success: The Demon uncouples the connection between the designated personal Condition or Tilt and its subject. Such effects are removed without actually being Resolved (meaning they don't grant a Beat). If the Condition or Tilt was representing some defect or damage (such as a missing limb), that flaw is also removed. Nothing prevents such Conditions or Tilts from being re-applied, however.
                                                    Exceptional Success: The Demon removes the effect with a flood of power, replacing it with a minor Condition or Tilt of their own (such as Inspired or Shaken).
                                                    Possible Modifiers: Condition or Tilt is Persistent (-4). Target has suffered from the Condition or Tilt within the past day (-1 per occasion/Scene). Demon was the one to inflict the Condition or Tilt (+5). Other Conditions dependent upon one to be removed (-3 per dependent Condition).
                                                    Note:                                                     This can only remove Conditions that are limited to and inherent to the target - Shaken or Swooning are valid, but Leveraged is not because it comes from an external source (No magicking away your Hunted status). The Demon also needs to know (or at least suspect) that the target is under the influence of said Condition/Tilt before using the power. The Storyteller is the final arbiter on what Conditions or Tilts might be able to be removed by this Exploit.


                                                    Banish (AKA Parting the Red Sea)
                                                    Humanity thinks it has a good grasp of physics, cause leads to effect, and things being the sum of their components. For the most part, that's right. But as former servants of the God-Machine, Demon understand that you can also change things by altering the relationship between them, and even the identifiers that describe them. Using this Exploit, a Demon can "delete and block" a detail from the scene from having its natural effect, such as removing the flame from a raging inferno or eliminating the effect a mass of water would have on those in the same area.
                                                    Example Prerequisites:
                                                    Dice Roll: Intelligence + Science + Primum
                                                    Action: Instant
                                                    Cost: 2 Aether
                                                    Dramatic Failure: Rather than removing the Tilt, they strengthen it or cause another one. Additionally, the Demon cannot use this Exploit again in the same scene.
                                                    Failure: Aether floods into the area... and does nothing.
                                                    Success: The targeted Tilt gets removed from the scene and, if it would logically still be applicable, is prevented from reapplying for Successes Turns (or until the Demon cancels the Exploit). This means Infernos disappear and won't reignite, even if the surroundings are still aflame. Odd cases such as removing the Flooded Tilt from a body of water might mean the waters part physically, or that the water strangely is only as restrictive as air.
                                                    Exceptional Success: The Demon can either extend the duration by a number of turns equal to their Primum, selectively choose who would be affected by the Tilt instead of removing it completely, or replace it for its duration with some other Storyteller approved Tilt.
                                                    Possible Modifiers: Extreme environment (- the Tilt's level). Persistent non-supernatural source of the Tilt (-4).
                                                    Note:                                                     If the Tilt was imposed by a supernatural effect, such as a Mage's spell, or a Beast's Lair Traits, they can contest the Exploit with a Clash of Will. Additional attempts to re-apply such Tilts also are subject to a Clash of Will, with success allowing the Tilt to be applied.

                                                            Vent0: I like Finders Keepers, Seance and Fax. I believe Forge The Legend could done through a Gadget containing Show Of Power or Urban Legend.

                                                            Miracle sounds iffy. That kind of power seems pretty well covered by Pacts, and its part of the fun to roleplay your Demon buying an debilitating illness or inconvenient relationship to increase their Cover. It's one of the ways they get a better understanding of what mortals go through, even if it's not in a compassionate manner.

                                                            Banish sounds like it should be temporary, even with an Exceptional Success. The Demon could specify that a group of people are protected (no need to limit the group's size by Primum, just use ST fiat), while others can be affected by the Tilt in a surprise tactic, like how Moses parted the Red Sea and then closed it to drown the Egyptians once the Israelites had crossed. We already have Exploits with names like Sermon, Affliction and Rain Of Blood, so why not go ahead and call this one Parting The Red Sea?

                                                            I like your maneuvers for Phoenix Pattern, except the last one. I know you tried to live up to the Style's name, but Going Loud and keeping even a partial Cover sounds like having your cake and eating it. It also takes away the incentive for Demons to build burner Covers in case they need to Go Loud, thus opening another way to investigate them. It occurred to me that a Style that improves Going Loud and one for optimizing the regular Demonic Form should be separate. They are quite distinct conceptually: the first is about sheer power, but the other is more about minimizing the risks of Aetheric Resonance/Compromise.
