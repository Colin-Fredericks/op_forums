    Guilty, Shaken and Spooked: Old issues, (Not so) new Questions.

    02-03-2016, 07:00 PM
    Hey everyone,

    So I have been recently using lots of my free time to work on a Demon game I am going to run and have been reading a lot of topics here in the forum. Some are a bit too old, and as I do not work as necromancer to bring them back from the beyond, I thought about writing this new topic on some basic issues about these conditions, but that I haven't actually read anywhere.

    The things is: I want to sell my players into this, but I need someone to sell it to me first. Please make me understand what these conditions actually mean and how to resolve them.

    Although I did manage to understand a few things, such as conditions are NOT punishments, especially because they can become a good source for beats, and the fact in compromise rolls from using partial demonic forms people are prone to have exceptional successes more often than not, I feel I need some mid-ground approach to them.

    The thing is, on a normal success you still should get one of these OR roll for temporary glitch, which at the moment, I think is what I am going for (because, again, having problem to understand the conditions).

    I would like to give two examples, and if possible, you guys and girls could point me into the right direction(s):

    1- Lets say a demon uses one of its demonic form to read the mind of human. He/She gets a 2 success, meaning he/she is not going to lose cover but, should get a condition.
    Now: at this point, I can only think about getting "guilty" feelings about that. I cannot understand how one could get shaken or spooked, could someone show me this? And if he/she did get guilty (which also depends a lot on the character, and I dont see many feeling like this), the resolution would be to talk about what happened..... but to whom? He/she should come to the human and say: "Oh dear, this is so embarrassing but I have a little confession to make.... I kinda read your thoughts" (and subsequent compromise roll.......).

    2- Another demon has the rivet arm and decides to use it in a fight, lets say against a cryptid. Now, it has normal success in his/her roll, and should get a condition.
    Guilty? For shooting it? (and how would the the resolution be? putting in a box and burying it in the backyard?); How one would either get shaken or spooked? (these are so much harder for me to understand) What would be a resolution for these other conditions?

    So I know I am demanding a lot asking for examples, but I am feeling at this point is the only way I will get to understand it.

    Also I have seen one topic with some different conditions for these situations, but if someone has created other conditions to correspond to this triad I would be eager to read about it.

    thanks in advance

      I understand Shaken/Spooked as the Demon being aware of a close brush with the God-Machine. In both examples, it's not that the Demon feels Shaken/Spooked for what it did with the Demon Form power (although it could be Guilty for them), it's that the fact that it had to make a Compromise check makes it instinctively aware that God Is Watching and that it came close to being revealed.

                                  A long time ago, someone posted some alternative conditions he homebrewed:

                                  Originally posted by JuvenalianSatyr View Post
                                  I felt that the use of the Guilty, Shaken, and especially Spooked Conditions for success on Compromise rolls was serviceable but lacked a certain thematic punch, and felt that it could use some more splat-specific Conditions for such. I came up with three new Conditions to replace them: Fixated, Jarred, and Exposed, roughly equivalent to Guilty, Shaken, and Spooked respectively. Let me know what you think!

                                  Condition: Fixated

                                  Your character cannot stop thinking about their past actions and their consequences, though instead of overcome with a sense of guilt, one wonders about the ramifications and whether or not it could have been different if they had approached it from a different angle. Your character second guesses their decision constantly, and has a need to vocalize this self-doubt. So long as it is suppressed, it weighs upon everything they do. Your character suffers -2 dice penalty to any and all Extended Actions, as they require your concentration and focus.
                                  Resolution: Your character confesses their doubts and risks themselves or the mission to correct their perceived wrongs, make up for the previous mistake, or recognize their limitations, by choosing to automatically fail a related roll.

                                  Condition: Jarred

                                  The Unchained are already alien to reality, and your character's perceptions have grown out of sync with the external world around them, revealed as purely subjective by the consequences of their Compromise. Everything seems unreal or symbolic of larger occult matrices, and the influence of the God Machine can be seen in everything. They focus on the most minute of details and can see connections where they do not truly exist. While under the effects of this Condition, this tunnel vision and asynchronous perception of reality inflicts a -2 penalty to all Perception rolls.
                                  Resolution: Your character recognizes they are missing the forest for the trees, often through a rude awakening, represented by choosing to fail a Perception roll as above, or an Investigation, Empathy, or Occult roll.

                                  Condition: Exposed

                                  The Cover provides a bulwark against supernatural influence, but the connection between it and the demon within can seem tenuous at best. Though your character has resisted compromising the Cover, the distance between it and the demon seems all the more clear. It is more difficult for the demon to relate through and control its mental faculties, leaving it susceptible to outside influence. Your character has one less Door to resist social maneuvering with, and suffers a -2 dice penalty to resist supernatural influence.
                                  Resolution: Your character is shocked back into self-identity after opting to fail or give into social or supernatural influence, showing just how far they have drifted from the core perception of the Cover.

                                          I use the originals when I run; in part, it's because I realized humans get those on a success/failure on Integrity, which means that since the Demon succeeded at maintaining Cover, they succeeded at masquerading as a mortal - which means they pick up a human Condition.

                                              Well Ben Quo, one of my problems with these conditions, and specially with "guilty" is that they are pretty much human emotions/conditions, when your character is not human:the demon is a bio-technical horror. As well as fear and other emotions that can led to shaken or spooked.
