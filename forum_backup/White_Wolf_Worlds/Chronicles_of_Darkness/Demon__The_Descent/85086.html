    Demon Fiction Pieces

    03-06-2014, 12:28 AM
    Demon has a lot of really good fiction. The opening fiction in particular managed to sell my players on the game in its own right. Kudos to the writers! It even inspired me to write a bit of my own, so I thought I might as well post it here. If anyone else wants to comment on it, or post a piece of their own, feel free!

    This piece is about a very new demon spending their first afternoon/evening acting as their initial Cover, very soon after their Fall.



    Perks of the Position

    Zeruel sat in the patrol car, watching the highway closely. It was harder now; she could only handle so much input at once, only come to so many conclusions. Knowledge came uneasily to her, as if the program was lagging a thousand times behind recognized optimal performance.

    Sera sipped her coffee the way he had back when she was hunting his human disguise. She forgot to grimace at the pain from the heat as it went down.

    'So this is what he meant. Fascinating.'

    There was a certain... satisfaction in coming to that conclusion for herself. An intangible quality that she could not define. It was not at all like compiling battlefield variables, though the unwieldy improbabilities that sometimes introduced themselves in such an environment were the closest fit she had for comparison.

    A car passed by, adhering to the speed limit.

    'I wonder what that human is thinking?'

    After seventeen seconds, she realized that she had no rubric to consult in order to speculate. Was she supposed to frown or smile at this realization? She had only five hours, twenty minutes, and forty seven seconds of freedom to use as a point of reference for these concerns. After a moment, she chose to frown.

    Another car passed by, exceeding the speed limit by five miles per hour. She placed her cup of coffee down in the appropriate on-board receptacle, and she shifted the patrol car into drive.

    The remnants of the concealment protocols available to her informed her that she would get better results by maintaining the frown. Strange, that she would have so much of that left after disconnecting herself from God. Was this intentional design, or a flaw that she had taken advantage of? Much like this human had hoped to take advantage of a flaw in enforcement vigilance.

    Strange... she felt something at the comparison.

    It made her feel hesitant to act.

    She pulled up behind them once they stopped by the road's edge, and left her car. She would ignore the feeling this time; there was a question she needed to answer for herself.

    'Time to see if I'm as good at pretending as he seemed to be.' The tightness in her upper torso suggested otherwise.

    Sera, for that was what she had to be in this moment, waited for the male - physically mature, capabilities uncertain due to concealing garments, incomplete threat assessment - to lower his window. His posture suggested that he was intimidated; he glanced up, attempting to make eye contact with her, and found his effort barred by her sunglasses.

    He began to perspire. Or perhaps it had simply become more apparent, because the ambient temperature was doing similar things to her own body. The lawbreaker spoke first. "G-Good evening, officer. Everything all right?" His lips were curved upward; the gesture felt particularly false. He was attempting to placate her.

    Idly, she wondered if all humans were so easily discomforted by minor variations in heat.

    “Do you know how fast you were going just now, sir?” she asked, replicating the inflections of tone and adjustments in posture Sera deemed necessary to acquire compliance. The tightness from before faded as the man's grip on the steering wheel increased in pressure; it provided her an inscrutable sense of satisfaction.

    However, his voice attained a steadiness it had lacked a moment previous. Her satisfaction quickly faded.“Just... five miles? Tops? It couldn'ta been much, ma'am.”

    “It was enough. Why would you think otherwise?”

    She wanted to him to answer; she wanted to learn the reason behind his act of rebellion against authority. Perhaps he was on some sort of time delay, had a task he needed to complete, and the extra speed of five miles per hour was necessary? Or maybe he was defective in some way, like the ones she had hunted before?

    “It's just five miles. Everyone goes an extra five miles here, I've never been stopped for just that before!”

    'Or perhaps he just desires to provoke me into assaulting him?'

    She considered it, and quickly concluded that this was a probable motivation. However, he was rather securely contained in his vehicle and she was forbidden to use lethal force at this time. Was he unaware of this? Did he derive satisfaction at the prospect of experiencing nonlethal trauma at her hands, and so choose this time purposefully?

    Her immovable demeanor appeared to provoke him to some other response. “Is it money you want? Look, I don't have a lot on me, but if it will get you to just let this go...”

    Attempting to escape harm by providing her with a resource? That was... okay, she had miscalculated in some way. None of her targets had attempted this before, at least not within the operational records she retained, but she was almost certain she would have never accepted the offer. However, now she was capable of speculating about the option presented to her. Sera could purchase food, or get more coffee, without depleting her human identity's primary source of income.

    It was a pleasantly logical alternative to inflicting injury or official sanction on him, and it was apparently within Sera's accepted parameters. She smiled a little. “Well, I suppose I can let you go with a warning this time.”

    Sera would need new coffee anyway; she had left the air conditioner on in the patrol car, so her old coffee would be cold.

    His shoulders and grip relaxed, and he slowly reached for the container he used to carry his money and ID. She remained cautious, considering that this could be a tactic to distract her and escape, but seeing the items for herself relieved the tension. She did not prompt her body to respond to that.

    'What would he have done in this situation? I'll ask him about it later,' Zeruel concluded, returning to her car with forty US dollars and a new array of perspectives to consider. How... interesting, this was quickly becoming.

          Very interesting piece, Aiden. There's the very clear... misadjustment and uncertainty of a new Demon there, I'd think. Quite the well written perspective, let me say. I'm assuming he is a Demon she was sent to destroy but she hesitated and Fell in the process. Because I can tell she's a Destroyer from the narration thanks to the well placed details and the course of her thoughts as she tries to place everything into a context she is familiar with. Threat assessment is also something that a Guardian would think of, but entropy is the first option that occurs to her and she uses the word "target".

          I also liked to read her mild amazement at... having a choice that doesn't involve termination, punishment or destruction and being pleasantly surprised by how she agrees with the logic of the alternative. Learning to exploit and benefit herself as well. Yeah, I can see this one slide down to Tempter eventually.

          When I read her name was Zeruel my mind jumped to Neon Genesis Evangelion for a picture of her demon form. xD

          Though, it seems she might have had or still has inhuman intelligence in her true form, since she notes several times her reduced 'processing capability', and maybe she was also environmental resistant, since there is a curious mention of how she seems surprised at herself that temperature bothers her now.
