    Demons and Plug-ins

    11-17-2013, 10:24 PM
    Well I've made it clear that I think demon and bleeding edge go together like peanut butter and chocolate, but I had some questions I wanted to get feedback on.

    Gadgets normally destroy the normal function of the device, what would you require to allow a demon to make a gadget that doesn't destroy the original function so that a demon can make a plug-in gadget?

    Since demons can already have built in devices, what would you rule on how demon could add plug-ins to their demonic form? Keeping in mind that mechanically there is no difference between an implanted plug-in and a non implanted plug-in, so absolutely nothing stops a demon from using plug-ins in demon form whether they can be implanted or not.

      From Bleeding Edge, pages 14-15:
      If you need a Plugin that emulates a specific supernatural power, it's best to bypass this system at least in part. If, for example, you want a Dermal Chameleon Layer that approximates Obfuscate's Cloak of Night, you may purchase the power at the same cost as a supernatural creature. If the cost is increased for not being a member of a particular group, such as a vampire clan, use the increased cost. However, you do not need to purchase previous powers, even if a supernatural creature of the appropriate type would. In the case of the Dermal Chameleon Layer, the cost would be 21 experience points. If the power has an activation cost in supernatural energy such as Vitae (which Cloak of Night does not), pay this cost in Willpower.

      Plugins adapted from supernatural powers are still subject to Backfires and being Compromised, and Willpower is still given for these events.

      What this means in the context of Demon: It should be possible to make Plugins that emulate Embeds and Exploits. It should also be possible to make Plugins that emulate the Modifications, Technologies, Propulsions, and Processes that are normally exclusive to Demonic Forms. In fact, I’d say that this would be a more fitting depiction of “cyberware” than even the default Plugin rules are.

      I’d diverge from the above quoted text in one way, in a Bleeding Edge Demon game: that is, I’d rule that Plugins are powered by Aether, not Willpower; and that Backfires and being Compromised generate Aether, not Willpower. I’d also rule that Plugins have a built-in capacity to store Aether, as per Stockpiles: for simplicity’s sake, I’d rule that each dot of a Plugin could store one point of Aether.

      My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

          While that's all perfectly valid, it requires that demons have a base understanding of god-machine tech that they don't seem to possess according to the main book, although maybe their technical knowledge will be expanded in a later book.

          There is also nothing stopping a demon from making a gadget out of an artificial arm or a pacemaker or whatever, so an artificial arm with knockout punch is just a gadget, and no gadget needs an aether expenditure to be activated anyway, they just need an aether charge to remain functioning.

          Plug-ins are about emulating supernatural powers with regular but advanced science: An "addictive presence" plug-in would work by releasing special tailored pheromones or emitting mind altering subharmonics or something, and cost willpower as normal, what you're talking about is replication not emulation.

          Technology to actually replicate embeds and exploits is certainly theoretically possible but likely to come around about when human science catches up with mage magic and people can buy reality bending at their local wal-mart, and isn't really so much cyberpunk but science fantasy.

          Not that I don't think it could be a fun game, but not really relevant to how demons would be able to interact with actual non supernatural, human science made plug-ins.

                  I’m talking replication because GM tech isn’t supernatural; it’s Clarke’s Law in action. That is, GM technology is about emulating supernatural powers with regular but (sufficiently) advanced technology. Tha said, I get your point. I do have a caveat to it, though:

                  Originally posted by StSword View Post
                  Technology to actually replicate embeds and exploits is certainly theoretically possible but likely to come around about when human science catches up with mage magic and people can buy reality bending at their local wal-mart, and isn't really so much cyberpunk but science fantasy.
                  I was actually thinking more in terms of Demonic Form abilities. I could list quite a few Modifications, Technologies, Propulsions, and even Processes that would work reasonably well as cyberware; in fact, it might be easier to list the ones that ought to be excluded.

                  Starting with the Modifications: Armored Plates; Blade Hand; Claws and Fangs; Electrical Sight; EMP Field (might be pushing it a bit; but not by much); Fast Attack; Huge Size; Inhuman Intelligence (depending on how advanced neuro-science has become); Inhuman Strength; Inhuman Reflexes; Night Vision; Rivet Arm; Slippery Body; Sonic Aciuty; Spurs; Tough as Stone. The only Modifications that don’t naturally lend themselves to mortal-crafted cybertech are Mental Resistance and Sense the Angelic; and if your Bleeding Edge setting has anything resembling Task Force Valkyrie in it, I wouldn’t even put those past the bounds of mortal technology.

                  The Technologies: Acidic Spit; Barbed Tail; Blind Sense (the flavor text speaks of enhancing the other senses to compensate for blindness); Demonic Horns; Electric Jolt; Electrical Resistance; Environmental Resistance; Fire Resistance; Inhuman Beauty; Mirrored Skin. Troublesome ones would be Aura Sight, Clairvoyant Sight, Essence Drain, Frost Aura, Fluid Form, Glory and Terror, and Mind Reading. TFV could probably replicate a few of those.

                  The Propulsions: Long Limbs, Tether, and maybe Wings (or some other form of aerial propulsion, like Iron Man style jet propulsion). Most of the others stretch the bounds too far for even TFV to manage; though Plasma Drive might work, given some cosmetic tweaks.

                  The Processes: Aegis Protocol; Carniverous Maw; Extra Mechanical Limbs; Memory Theft (limited: the subject must have a headjack); Quill Burst; and Voice of the Angel.

                  My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
