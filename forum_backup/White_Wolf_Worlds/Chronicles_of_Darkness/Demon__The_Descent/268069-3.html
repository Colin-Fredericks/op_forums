I convinced my cover’s parents to adopt Gene, and tried my best to make sure that he had the happiest possible childhood. It was Gene who got us started in the “Unlicensed PI” gig. He loved those old “Encyclopedia Brown” stories, basically devoured anything that was about police procedures or any type of mystery fiction. He was all about the idea of someone using logic and reason to help others. We were about twelve, when he and I set-up my mom’s card table in the front yard with a poster advertising that the Evans’ Boys Detective Agency was open for business. We started finding lost dollies, trading cards, where parents were hiding holiday presents, etc. Gene was great about following the clues and seeing the whole picture.

We were now following up on abusive husbands, cheating wives, drug addicted kids, real film noir style cases. Gene and I turned in the card table for the back of his 1974 Chevy Nova. We kept half of what we earned and tucked the rest away for a rainy day. I knew that Gene really wanted to go to college to study forensics and I figured that was a good enough reason for us both to save-up.

                                        Last year, the weird cases started to trickle through the doors. We were working out of a bar near our apartment in Upstate New York. I’d been pretty successful at keeping us out of the majorly spooky stuff. The first one slipped my notice till we got jumped by Fangers outside a bar in Brooklyn. They weren’t special, just a couple of baby vamps doing muscle work for cash. I had to punch their tickets in front of Gene though; and just like that, he was off to the races. She needed protection from her abusive ex, a young butch by the name of Ferra. They’d recently split up after years of our client being Ferra’s physical and emotional punching bag. Gene naturally refused, threatened to call the police, and made sure Ferra saw the Glock 17 he kept by his side whenever we were on a case. Ferra backed off and left. He called me to inform me what happened. The rest of the night was calm.

She offered us twice the money to lure our client into an obvious trap, left half the cash up front, and left a time and address to meet for the rest. We took the money and told the client. We came to the conclusion that the best move was to get her as far away from Ferra as possible, so we used Ferra’s money to arrange for a new ID for our client and then set her up in a nice new home in Canada. Then we called Ferra to set-up the meet.

Like I said, I was cocksure and secure in the belief that we’d gotten our client away from Ferra. That all went to shit when the Angels showed up.

He was just another dead orphan. I’d arrived just before the final deadline to enroll in classes at the college; it seemed like a good excuse to be an early twenty something that just moved across country. I’m planning to specialize in forensic cryptography. I think Gene would like that.

One who has fought and bleed beside them; who looks upon them not with “mercy”, but with sympathy. It is therefore necessary for me to gather allies. I would also prefer to not destroy my brothers, as they are being sacrificed like cattle for nefarious ends.

This bit of Junk distilled them down to Essence, and I don’t think that’s what my goddamn Despair was headed for, but it would have been fucking nice to get an idea of what the Bastard-Machine could do with this shit.

                                            Me and Vivian broke down what was left anyway. It wasn’t plugged in enough to suborn, but once we’d gotten a real start with our goddamn bit of suborned Infrastructure, Vivian figured we could use the spare parts. Jack and Liz parleyed with the cryptids upstairs. Seems we’ve got a couple of guard moths and a creepy fucking psychopath lair now.


                                            Kilroy’s Transmissions

                                            Music for a Darkened Theater

                                            Scrambled with a side of Chemtrails and a glass of Agent Orange

                                            The morning began simply enough. Naomi dropped me off at the office on her way to classes. I started the coffee distillation and spent a couple of hours maintaining Billy Mac and Vivian's online correspondences while waiting for my fellow unchained to arrive or for anything requiring a higher authority than Cecilia at the front desk.

                                            Speed was the first to arrive, looking a bit less dour than usual. I believe she found last night relaxing. Harvey Cannon was next, though fortunately not until after she'd had the opportunity to assimilate her first couple of cups of caffeine.

                                            Our client had dropped in to check on the status of the case, and while he is certainly eccentric one cannot deny his devotion. He also had a new item to add. His secretary, one Ada Watts, had not come in or answered her phone in the past few days. Her disappearance was timed exactly with the hunters breaking into Drake Paulson's office. As a rule, I never trust in coincidence. It was almost certain that she had either been extracted, detained, or eliminated.

                                            As I updated him on our progress and gathered what information he could provide on the missing woman, Speed used her powers to delve into his mind. Since the night of the break in, someone had implanted a false memory of Micheal White to replace the block that Cannon had previous struggled against. That raises the possibly that the missing woman is a telepath. It seems unlikely she is or was an angel but may well be a stigmatic agent of the god machine or White directly.

                                            In our conversation, Cannon did give us a connection between Paulson and the underground fighting club. It seems that Paulson was the one who tracked down and recaptured their champion, one Benjamin Creel better known to us as Crusher. There was a female acquaintance involved, who attempted to steer Paulson away from the wayward brute. Cannon did not recognize Rosanna but did later give the woman in question's name as Anna Watson. And when Paulson was dealing with them, the ring was using a different location so he would have no reason to suspect what he was walking into. So really, Clytemnestra's early theory of it being a clan fighting ring or some such was far closer to the situation than we suspected. It seems very likely that Creel is a changeling like Rosanna, which gives her and her people much more reason to dislike Paulson for his role in putting the large fellow away for life.

                                            Given the potential connections, I did end up making a call to pull a few strings with Billy Mac's friends and arraigned an interview with Creel for us tomorrow. Speed can see from his aura if he is a changeling and if so it is within our power to negotiate the possibility of freeing him. Speed is firmly disinclined given the violence of his crimes but if it proves to be the only avenue to finding Rosanna and through her Jake and whatever information he has about White we shall have to at least consider it.

                                            In the course of gathering this information we moved from our offices to Cannon's and picked up Clytemnestra and Quietus. It seems Clytemnestra has decided she rather enjoys using Naomi to provoke Cecilia to extremes of emotion far removed from those that she coaxes from the woman as Elizabeth Covington.


                                            Disguises and Deathtraps

                                            Our initial investigation of the secretarial desk at Cannon's office revealed nothing out of the ordinary. After getting the key to the desk's lone locked drawer from Cannon, however, we made a fairly relevant discovery. The drawer contained more makeup, a wig, facial putty, and the stage glasses that combined to form Ada Watts. Interesting that she was apparently able to don this guise at the office rather than wear it in and leave with it. Proof that she was not the woman Canon thought and an accomplice rather than a victim. The time frame is noteworthy. She worked for Cannon for around eighteen months, starting before well before Paulson had any reason to suspect White was anything other than a strange man with a desire to help young women in bad situations. Overall, White's schemes are either deeper than I understand or the man is not entirely rational. Whichever the case may be, I dislike being unable to predict his moves and being forced to deal with him in a reactive rather than proactive manner.

                                            While the personnel file and hiring information on Ada Watts had vanished along with her, Cannon did have a phone number and address in his payroll. The address was an apartment in an area and building that was comfortably middle class, nothing outside the means of her salary working for Cannon. It was the obvious lead to follow up on and so we did.

                                            A quiet examination of her mailbox revealed nothing identifying and a few days worth of generic mass mailings. We made our way to the apartment and knocked. There was no answer. I believe all of us had begun to scout the walkway for witnesses in preparation of entering uninvited when both Speed and myself noticed the scent of natural gas in the air. Clytemnestra shifted into her swarm state and crawled underneath the door to find the apartment had been rigged quite effectively into a bomb sufficient to destroy the entire wing of the apartment complex.

                                            Speed contacted Gabe's police contact while Quietus organized a remarkably calm evacuation of the building's other residents. The emergency responders were understandably unwilling to allow us anything more than cursory access to the apartment which did not give us anything more than Clytemnestra's arachnid swarm had been able to gather. Hopefully Speed's contacts will allow her at least some access to what the police forensic investigators are able to discover once the scene is safe enough to enter. While limited to the strictly mundane, it may prove helpful none the less.

                                            As the public servants went about their task of defusing the apartment, we took the opportunity to question the management and other residents. Ada Watts had only lived in the apartment for two months, right around the time of her initial employment with Cannon. She had moved out and been replaced by a couple that current popular American media would likely term as 'of terrorist decent', which is to say that with the notable exception of the one Indian neighbor all anyone could recall was the wife's very traditional (and conveniently concealing) Muslim garb. The Indian woman had little to add as the woman was very reserved and avoided interacting with anyone but she did place our suspect as being Pakistani. Of the husband, no one seems to have ever actually seen him, though he did handle all of the affairs with the property office over the phone. None of the current employees where present when the couple moved in, the property itself has changed ownership (most likely unrelated though it might be a manipulation to make the trail harder to follow) and while the woman that used to manage the desk was retained, she apparently had a very abrupt and unexpected conversion to some sort of cult and walked out one day. I am far less inclined to write that occurrence off as coincidence. Pity we are unlikely to be able to track her down, one does not generally remove a witness in such a bold manner without good cause.


                                            A Deal with Dmitri

                                            Once we had gotten what information we could from the apartment complex, we withdrew and took stock of our avenues of further investigation. One point that was not lost on any of us was that this trap had quite likely been set for us rather than simply directing us toward one of the ones previously set for Paulson. We did elect to contact Cannon and warn him about the situation as he was the only other potential target. We debated how best to respond to this move and the consensus was to keep our own cards close and not risk revealing anything that White might not already know. In particular, if he has not ascertained that we are Unchained yet it is best to keep that advantage as long as possible. For the moment, I believe gathering more information is our best option. Also, we still have no solid leads on where Paulson is or what happened to him. We decided to proceed by interviewing Creel and returning to the abandoned theater the mothmen were lairing in for a more through investigation.

                                            Once the interview with Creel was set up, I excused myself to change into Vivian and meet with Dmitri. On my way, I stopped to acquire a bottle of insultingly low quality vodka. Such is the nature of the relationship they have.

                                            The Greek diner was an older building, the sort of place that is always changing owners and names and yet always remains basically the same low end greasy spoon. The location suited Dmitri well as he is likewise a bit run down. Though, to be fair, even if he is no longer the chiseled criminal soldier he once was, he remains a physically impressive man, especially when compared to Vivian.

                                            Dmitri was the only customer in the place, having left the young Slavic tough that had accompanied him smoking on the bench out front. The bodyguard was quite tactless in his appraisal and while he clearly saw Vivian as no threat he was intent on frisking me for weapons. As Vivian is not one for such casual groping, I snarled at Dmitri to call off his dog and he laughingly assured the man that “the kotenok is no threat, and if anyone is going to grope her it will be me.” The man shrugged and returned his attention to his cigarette and keeping watch for any interruptions.

                                            Dmitri refused to get straight to business, instead insisting that I join him at his table as it was clear from looking at me that I was in dire need of something more sustaining than the diet of ramen and Mountain Dew that, as a hacker, was all Vivian consumed. He had, of course, already taken the liberty of ordering for us both. I made the expected show of annoyance and presented him with my gift, which he made a point of opening to drink with his meal and savoring with a gusto far removed from the quality of that vile swill. To date, Vivian has never found a vodka bad enough to bring so much as a grimace to Dmitri's expression. I shall have to try harder next time, though I wonder if there exists such a beverage. It would be rather interesting to see Dmitri and Speed drink together at some point.

                                            The food was somewhat more appetizing than the environs would lead one to expect though not enough to make a trip for specifically. Once I had eaten, I pulled out the thumb drive with Dantaine's account numbers and set it on the table. Dmitri slugged his vodka back and smirked. “I must ask. Why would such an infamous hacker trade such a thing rather than make use of it herself? I know my pretty little kotenok would never lie to her good friend Dmitri about what she's selling him...”

                                            I lifted an eyebrow. It was a valid question and we both knew it. I flashed him a sweet smile. “The accounts belong to a very dangerous man, much too dangerous for such a sweet little kitten as myself to risk stealing from.”

                                            He considered me and pursed his lips. I most likely should not have let him know that I understood what his endearment for me meant but he'd used it often enough that it was not unreasonable that Vivian would have looked it up. “You are many things little Vivian, but cautious is rarely one of them.”

                                            “Then you understand what a truly dangerous man we are dealing with.”

                                            He nodded after a moment and lifted a nice leather carrying bag to set on the table. “I have what you asked for kotenok.” I let a suitably hungry look filter into my eyes and expression but did not reach across the table for it. “Poor thing,” he chuckled, “Just how long has it been since you had a proper fix?”

                                            I crossed my arms and glared at him. “Months,” I admitted, “The FBI are sore losers, they refused to release any of the equipment they confiscated as evidence.”

                                            He grinned broadly and pushed it across to me. “Clearly whatever inferior tech you have been reduced to has not stopped you, but I assure you, this is worthy of the great Marley's Ghost.”

                                            I took it from him and smiled. Oh it was indeed worthy. I felt a surge of pleasure just running my fingers over the hardened case. I powered it up and used it to confirm the data for him with a smile.

                                            Oh but how Vivian has missed having the proper tools to work her digital magic.


                                            Mothman Theatrics

                                            While not overly intelligent, mothmen can manage at least limited speech and reasoning. So rather than drive off the pair that have taken refuge in the old theater we decided to attempt to parley with them. To facilitate the talks, Clytemnestra and I opted to stop by a pet store and pick up some feeder mice to offer as a means of opening a dialog.

                                            When we arrived, Clytemnestra nodded to me and asked me to go get the mice. When I inquired if she was staying behind do to some dislike of rodents, she smiled and said she just wanted to get me to do it for her. While I would normally have simply shrugged and gone about the transaction, I found myself moved to what I can only describe as mischief. I was still feeling quite pleased at having finally replaced my rig but more than that, I find that while in my cover as Vivian I am simply more inclined toward such behavior. At any rate, I was struck by the notion that if I was going to do an errand for Clytemnestra, I was going to do it with flair. And once I saw the young boy working the counter, the manner of how to show off for her seemed obvious. I may have laid it on rather think, speaking to him about just how much I loved my snake in decidedly suggestive terms. By the time he had finished collecting the feeder mice into a bag for me, he was quite flushed. Once we had returned to the counter and I had taunted him with Vivian's physical charms, he gifted the mice to me. I am not sure I have ever so thoroughly flustered someone as Vivian before. It was... amusing. I am tempted to return when I have some free time. I imagine that Vivian might well be able to coax the hapless lad into signing away whatever pieces of his soul might prove useful.

                                            However, there was no time for such diversions. We arrived at the theater and met with Quietus and Speed. It seems Quietus had spent the afternoon helping her to acquire some elements toward building a second cover to use in potentially compromising situations or simply when distancing herself from the agency would be helpful. Vivian serves that function for me, though I wonder if perhaps I should see about another cover that I am less attached to for high threat situations.

                                            Clytemnestra and Quietus convinced the mothmen to allow us to search the building and we discovered the trap that White had set here. He had laid out some items to make it appear the storage area underneath the stage was inhabited and had even as we later discovered left a small radio playing softly. The entrance was set with a bear trap so that if Paulson had entered recklessly he would have been immobilized and injured. While the mothmen would not attack a healthy human, they would have been drawn to the blood and quite likely finished off the wounded bounty hunter. A decidedly horrific death and proof that Michael White is both very dangerous and quite sadistic.

                                            Once we'd uncovered and disarmed the traps, Speed and myself continued to search the theater as it seemed likely that there was something to have attracted the mothmen. Quietus and Clytemnestra returned to them and bargained with them, forging a contract with them to increase their intellect and ability to frighten away people in exchange for them watching and guarding the building so that we could use it as a secure hiding place in emergencies.

                                            For our part, Speed and I found some disused and mostly dismantled Infrastructure. It had gathered and distilled Essence from the emotions of the crowd watching the performances. The core elements had been recovered, but some useful components that could be used to enhance a working element should we find one remained. We set to work on them and dismantled them to bring back to the office with us for later use.


                                            Clytemnestra’s Yarns

                                            Yea I was on TV. I was the one with the pink hair. Oh it was a long story.

                                            Okay, so the day started with my math class. I wore this shirt all special for my bitch math teacher. She did not think it was funny. Maybe because she won’t shut up about her goddamn honor student daughter. I mean, I have not banged her, she is like a junior in high school, but you know, if she stays in the area for college I will. You know, to piss her off.

                                            She got in a twist about my homework too. “Naomi if you don’t show your work how do I know you did not use a calculator?” “If you don’t want us to use a calculator you should not assign so much homework! I got shit to do.” No sense of humor at all. Would it kill her husband to plow her now and then so she would not freeze the seat she sits on?

                                            So I get to work and Cecília is waiting like a trapdoor spider to jump on me about my pants and shirt. “Oh what if Lady Covington were here?” “Then she would totally do me.” I am pretty sure Lady Covington would think it is hilarious.

                                            Oh, I’ll meet her on my terms.

                                            But then, THEN, we get away, but not to the apartment complex but to that Cannon guy’s office. His secretary Ada Watts vanished, and Gabe and Billy Mac had picked Jack up and thought she might be involved. Gabe searched the desk but just found some make up and stuff, and a locked drawer. Billy Mac got the key to it from Mr. Cannon, and inside we find a wig, some facial prosthetics, color contacts, and glasses. Ada Watts apparently did not exist. But we had an address that checked out while we went there.

                                            Yea Cannon is the Agent Orange and Chemtrails guy. We are looking for his Bounty Hunter Drake Paulson. Right. With the boxing ring and the Michael White guy. Yea.

                                            So we go to the complex and knock on the door. After Jack messes with the mailbox illegally, Gabe and Billy Mac start flipping out because they smell gas. Jack sort of peers in the window in a sideways sort of way and notices there is a tank of gas near there, and what looks like some sort of jury rig at the door. So of course, I got the fuck out of dodge.

                                            Jack, being the hero in the group, went and started getting the others there to safety while Gab called the cops. Eventually we had a whole bunch show up. I kept a damn low profile before some cop got odd ideas about the “Mexican” girl.

                                            Turns out the whole thing was rigged all to hell. Silverware was left out near the door, and enough fuel was near to be sure whoever opened the door was cooked. Cops went in the window…carefully.

                                            Turns out “Ada” moved out after two months and a “Muslim” couple who no one ever saw the guy of. Her get up was enough to mostly conceal her, and we were pretty sure it was out master of disguise again. There was a fairly Jesus oriented Indian girl there who said she was Pakistani. Billy Mac astutely pointed out we had trails going in all the wrong directions. Fuck, we had enough red herrings to start a fish fry.

                                            Billy Mac wanted to call some contacts to follow up a boxing ring tip Gabe got, and Jack and Speed had a side thing, so I came here. Needed to see a pretty face.

                                            Okay, yea. Legs too. And you got em, babe!

                                            -o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-

                                            Hey Deanna. This whole thing is fucked. I just heard from Gabe. She, Vivian, Jack and Lady Covington went to look into that theatre. The place was full of rats and vermin, apparently, and this White guy set bear traps under the stage. After dropping evidence the girl Drake was looking for was there. Fortunately, we beat him there. Viv and Gabe were quick enough to notice the traps so we did not set them off…but fuck man. They were pretty sure the vermin would have finished Drake off if he were trapped too.

                                            I really, really don’t like this guy. Oh don’t worry babe, I will watch my back. Don’t forget who the tough girl is.
