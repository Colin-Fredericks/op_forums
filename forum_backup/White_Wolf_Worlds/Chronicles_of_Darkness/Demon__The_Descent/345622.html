    Cover-switching

    12-20-2014, 06:38 AM
    Alright, so I need an opinion on switching between several different covers. Can a demon "move between" physically separate covers?

    For example, say a Demon is working to maintain Jerry, an office drone. Realizing me needs to check something in his other cover, he excuses himself to the men's room, where, after a turn unobserved, transfers to Maria, a housewife. Since the Demon is a single entity, Jerry is gone (and the coworkers might comment on his long bathroom trip) and Maria is likewise unavailable during Jerry's office hours.
    But the important aspect is that Maria does not waltz out of the office men's room.

    This feels like a logical conclusion to draw, but the core book is very sparse on cover-switching; I'm hoping someone who's read the ST guide and/or fiction can weigh in.


    And a follow-up. If this is the case, what of the unoccupied cover(s)? The book says "they age in stasis," but there is a big difference from the coworkers stumbling upon a soulless, comatose body in a stall, and a complete disappearance.

                          All Covers, whether in use or not, are part and parcel of the demon's body, and those not in use can be considered collapsed into a potential state within her. So, yes, in addition to what Rose said, when Maria walks out of the men's room (making it a bad place to change Cover in this situation), if Jerry's coworkers get worried and go into the men's room to check on him, he will be nowhere to be found, no comatose body sitting "in reserve."

                          Can this be inconvenient? Hell yeah. Nobody ever said living multiple lives was easy. The psychic reinforcement of Cover does a little bit of the lifting in making sure people assume you're somewhere reasonable, but if you're keeping up multiple Covers, you have to make sure you can show up in the right places as all of them now and then, or they'll degrade.

                                                  Thanks everybody. I'm thinking I'll define an Exploit: Dead Man's Drop. Spend 1 Aether to "step out" of a cover and leave a comatose body behind. After your Prium in days, the cover left behind begins to degrade at a rate of 1 dot per 24 hours left abandoned. While the cover is thus unattended, the demon cannot utilize or benefit from the cover. Separation and merging are both instant actions, with no roll.

                                                  Might be a useful/interesting idea for other ST/players, and I prefer having someone else check that I'm not over-powering my NPCs.

                                                  Drawback: Since the cover is still a part of the Demon, its destructions has ramifications. While the hollow shell left behind has its own health and damage levels (which are added to the current damage when being merged,) its destruction causes feedback to its owner; the Demon suffers its cover rating in aggravated damage, along the lines of the fatality. Eg, visible burns if the body is destroyed by fire.
                                                  Last edited by DarthDoctor; 12-22-2014, 05:58 AM. Reason: Added a drawback after realizing how potent this Exploit is
