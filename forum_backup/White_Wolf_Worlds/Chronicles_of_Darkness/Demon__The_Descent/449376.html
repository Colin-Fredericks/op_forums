    Creating an Angel - Questions

    03-19-2015, 12:38 PM
    So. I want to create anew Angel as an Antagonist.

    Maybe for Demon, maybe for something else, but I want to be able to make them....

    Here's the deal, I can design a Spirits stats, pick Numina, and lay out a mission for the Angel, but it feels like I'm just designing a very focused Spirit at that point.

    Reading Demon described Angels as being more connected to the God-Machine, aware of every other Angel and the God-Machines will, 'going loud' is described as reconnecting to the power a Demon once had as an Angel, furthermore, when they so this they gain access to all Embeds related to their Incarnation and heal all bashing and lethal damage.

    Do Angels have access to all Embeds related to their Incarnation? If yes, do they activate them like Numina?

    Do they benefit from Cover or Spoofing?

    Should they be able to 'go loud' and be able to spend 15 Essence a turn and heal all lethal and bashing when it is within their parameters to do so?

    What else makes Angels mechanically different from Spirits? That's my real question here

      I've always given the angels my players run into whatever Embeds fit for their mission, even if it isn't entirely within their Incarnation purview. When you say 'activate like Numina', do you mean with a roll like an Ephemeral being would, or do you mean with Essence? Because one of the beauties of Embeds is that they don't need fuel at all, and penalizing an angel for using things the fluff describes as simply extensions of their being isn't really fair.

      Why would an angel need to Spoof? Who would they Spoof? Demons are too busy hiding from them to, usually, try and go on the offensive and most angels a Demon would target can sense them coming anyways. They don't even have a Cover in the same way a Demon would. The Cover of an Angel can't erode since it's still tied into all the God-Machine's support networks. It doesn't matter if you do things which seem funny, God has decided that your false life will look fairly normal, so it will. Cover erodes specifically because the Demon is no longer connected to the G-M's support, so every time they do something that causes the way they're tied to the world now to fray, there's nothing to repair it with outside of extraordinary action. Ergo, angels can't Go Loud. To burn a Cover would be akin to Falling, it's disconnecting from a support structure of the G-M, I'm not even sure they could conceive it. More importantly, such an action- burning 15 Essence to heal Corpus -would kill or at least put into hibernation anything below Rank 3, and leave any other angel pretty severely weakened; even a Rank 5 angel.

      Mechanically, an angel is a heck of a lot harder to kill or banish permanently than a spirit. Disrupting resonance or destroying anchors and fetters is easy enough. Taking about Infrastructure is infinitely harder, even for Demons, who naturally know how Infrastructure is put together and how to take it apart. Imagine being human and being confronted with Infrastructure. And the angel will be fighting you on it with everything at its disposal. They also don't need a spiritual connection of some kind. Spirit ecology, the Hisil, the Underworld, none of these things mean much of anything to an angel unless the G-M decides it does. Infrastructure can be entirely divorced from 'flavours' of Essence, so they can be built anywhere the G-M needs, instead of only certain places where it is easy or even possible for Spirits or Ghosts to manifest.

              It would probably be Attribute + Attribute as relates to the Embed. So Presence + Composure, for example, would be Power + Resistance.

              I've always given an angel form abilities the way a Demon has, but more of them, perhaps up to Primum 10 required numbers of Form Powers, but that's a personal preference of mine and the players for every angel to be a terrifying threat. Angels, by default, are in their true form. Being in a Cover is never really explained for an angel, since it's not really part of the world yet, but other threads in this subsection suggest using Mortal Mask as an example. I've played it that angels can't partially manifest Form Powers. It's either full reveal, or nothing at all.

              It doesn't specify in D:tD or the GMC whether Infrastructure always prevents Essence bleed, but it would suggest they do suffer it while away from Infrastructure. However, remember Infrastructure can be huge, spanning whole cities or more. Just because the Linchpin is in one spot, doesn't mean the Infrastructure Condition is limited there. I've always ruled it that any piece of the Infrastructure, and all the area in between, imparts the Condition. So that means an angel is safe from Essence bleed more or less wherever they need to be. If they need to leave their operating zone, possession is a good out. It's even substantiated in the opening fiction for Demon.

                          Many, if not most, Embeds are mechanically useless for an Angel. The reason an Angel doesn't require Embeds, generally speaking, is because much of what an Angel does will be covered by its Mission meaning that the Angel immediately gets to roll Attribute + Rank on that action. While a Demon might need an Embed to mysteriously start a car's ignition switch without the keys, an Angel just rolls Attribute + Rank so long as starting that car directly contributes to his mission. An example would be: Merciless Gunman. An Angel with a gun shooting NPCs with Down and Dirty Combat rules in play might as well just use Attribute + Rank rather than be given the Embed itself as all other effects of the Embed are covered by the Blast Numen. Due to their broad nature, Numen, Influences and Manifestations all do much of what Embeds already do. This makes sense since the fiction explicitly states that Embeds are "reality hacks" that Demons have figured out to regain some of their vast abilities that they once possessed as Angels.

                          An Angel's mission is a specific task, this can be broad or focused depending on the God-Machine's intent for the entity, which makes it a lot more flexible than any Spirit. While a Spirit of Violence can only gain Attribute + Rank on actions related to violence, a Hunter Angel will gain Attribute + Rank on any action directly relating to his mission. The window for what directly relates to an angel's mission is going to be naturally much broader than a Spirit's Resonance because a Spirit's Resonance is generally going to be a single word: violence, kindness, dogs, cats, cars. An Angel's mission is a task and likely going to be a phrase: hunter of demons, chaser of the homeless, smiter of the wicked. This phrase doesn't have a word count limit, it can be complex, even a paragraph or more long, allowing the window of powerful Angels to extend far beyond the reach of a Spirit.

                          Demonic Form Powers are interesting. The Ephemeral Creatures rules explicitly states that an entity, such as an Angel, does bashing damage unless it has a characteristic that logically imposes it would do lethal or something else. Therefore Demonic Form Powers like Blade Hand are made immediately redundant because if your Angel has knife hands, he just does lethal damage with his hands straight and even. Other Demonic Form Powers are covered by Numen. Blast covers the Demonic Form Powers Rivet Arm and Eliminator Cannon fairly perfectly. (Hound, in Splintered City Seattle, demonstrates that you should feel free to modify a Numen to suit an entity. His Blast also shoots a beacon into the sky so that the real hunter angel can come and nab the demon.) However, Demonic Form Powers do make good notations on how features like this might work mechanically, so I use them but do not count them as Numen, Manifestations or Influences. I simply give whatever Demonic Form Powers I want the Angel to have, to the Angel, and try to keep it reasonable as long as there isn't a better way to do that (such as Numen etc.). If the Messenger Angel has to run really fast he might get Long Limbs simply because I give him it (it only confers an athletic bonus to running and a speed increase) but I wouldn't give him the aforementioned Eliminator Cannon.

                          As said above, Infrastructure is a lot more esoteric than Resonance or an Anchor. While Resonances and Anchors can be esoteric they are more often than not going to be pretty straight forward. Again a Spirit generally has a one-word Resonance, an Angel has a phrase for a Mission. So, with that in mind, their Infrastructure is going to be some esoteric cosmic mechanism that either summoned them or supports them post-summoning. Infrastructure can also be anything. One Angel in the God-Machine Chronicle has a teenager for infrastructure. He isn't Fettered to the kid, to my understanding, the boy's life is the Infrastructure so as long as he sticks by the kid he doesn't suffer essence bleed.

                          Since entities use simplified attributes, generally speaking, those attributes will be on average one or two dice higher than a PC especially if you take into account the kinds of actions that an entity is going to be taking. If your entity is going to be attacking a lot, it probably has a high Power or Finesse, but a low Resistance.

                          It is fine to give your Angel Embeds and Exploits (though Exploits I would generally convert to Numen if you can't find a Numen that covers what you want). It is also fine to simply design your own powers for your Angel because you're the storyteller and the entity isn't going into player hands so it only needs to be balanced as an NPC (i.e. it has to be something the PCs can defeat within the context they encounter it). My advice would be to design the Angel in concept first and then build it mechanically and simply design what you can't make work with the systems in place yourself.

                                  All Ephemeral Beings roll Attribute + Rank instead of Attribute + Skill for any task related to their nature. That is: a ghost's anchor, a spirit's resonance, or an angel's mission. Here's an example from Dave Brookshaw:

                                  Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post
                                  Examples - a violence spirit attempts to hack into a computer. It rolls Finesse, but has an unskilled penalty.

                                  The same violence spirit attempts to determine how someone died. Not exactly a direct link to its purview, but not unreasonable. It rolls Power (Intelligence subs to Power), but doesn't have a penalty.

                                  The same spirit punches someone. It rolls Power + Rank. Strength subs to Power, and spirits add Rank instead of skills when they "should" have the skill.

                                  The same spirit makes a perception check - which for mortals is Wits + Composure, a two-Attribute pool. It rolls Finesse + Resistance.
                                  Influences allow the entity to manipulate and change the world around it to be more suitable and comfortable for itself. They are primarily used for making its life easier. Rank is used in the place of any skill the entity should possess, but ultimately how much freedom that entity has is up to you. Manifestations are used to get the entity into the world so that it can do its thing.

                                  In the case of Embeds, many embeds focus around making a task easier or more impressive for the Demon to perform. Example: Authorized makes it easier to get past authorities such as the police to investigate a crime-scene. For a mortal if they were trying to fake it they would roll something like: Wits + Subterfuge or Larceny + Equipment (Fake IDs, Disguises, etc). For a Demon they don't have to be reasonable. They can walk in as long as they have Authorized: they roll Manipulation + Intimidation - the highest Intelligence and enter. So the question is: What does an angel do? Well, you could go to the trouble of giving the Angel this embed or you could simply resolve it as Finesse + Rank, since they're an angel, and it's something the angel has to do.

                                  Many embeds are direct powers but many of those embeds are lesser versions of already present Numen. Example: Merciless Gunman allows you to kill however many successes you get in Down and Dirty Combat or if you're not using Down and Dirty Combat then you gain your successes to the attack roll for your firearm attack. Blast if successfully activates allows the angel to wound people from a distance (they may already be able to if they have a weapon) and allows them then to pay 2 Essence to increase the weapon's potency by +1L. So you see, if you prefer Merciless Gunman, give him Merciless Gunman but Blast does have you covered for the same task within the narrative. (Being a bad-ass and shooting up a room.)

                                  Many Vocal embeds would be handled by Influences. Example: Animal Communication allows a demon on the success to embed a message into an animal who then delivers the message. An Angel could do this with the Manipulate Influence.

                                  The major point of confusion with an angel seems to be: How supernatural can it be when it goes about a skill check? My answer: It's an ephemeral entity, it's quite capable of amazing feats as it's not a person, the difference between what it can do with Rank and a Numen is how direct the action is. Is this a direct supernatural assault of some kind? Then it should be a Numen. Is it a passive buff to everything the entity does? Then it should be a Numen. Is it an action the entity has to take? Then it should be resolved via Attribute + Rank.

                                  This means that mechanically a Demon's embeds are singular actions that are among an Angel's armoury of possible actions. He's stolen one thing that the angel could do with either an Attribute + Rank check or an Influence like Strengthen and sometimes a Numen.

                                  I hope this makes sense (and is correct, as it is my understanding of the rules.)

                                              Setting the Scene
                                              A Demon, by name of Mr. Black, has used his Demonic Form and thus incurred the -3 Compromise roll. He killed three people and immediately went to ground, fleeing the scene but not yet reverting to his human Cover. Unfortunately he failed this roll and so acquired the Hunted (Persistent) condition from the Storyteller. Now, a Rank 3 angel has manifest from nearby infrastructure, about three blocks down the road, to react to the threat it detected while dormant in Twilight. I like to have Hunter-Killer Angels of Ranks, 2, 3 and 4 to serve as generic antagonists prepared for whenever this situation might occur naturally. This guy is a Rank 3 Angel.

                                              A Series of Actions
                                              The Angel is in Twilight and detects the Demon's compromise thanks to the God-Machine noticing and immediately activating him to take care of it. However, in Twilight, he can't do much. So he immediately spends 3 Essence to use the Materialise Manifestation power. Now he has 12 Essence left. At the same time he uses Mortal Mask to hide his horrific true form under the Mortal Mask numen. He is at 11 Essence and his mask will last for 3 hours per 3 successes.

                                              The Angel immediately proceeds to the crime scene itself, which he sensed the location of due to the failure of the Compromise check. By the time he arrives however the police are present and he needs to get inside. He's dressed in a black suit, with a white shirt, and a yellow and purple striped tie. We'll call him Mr. Stripe. Mr. Stripe needs to get into the secure crime-scene so needs to impress on the local law-enforcement that he's FBI or similar.

                                              His mission needs to be defined. So: Mr. Stripe is designed to hunt down and neutralize Demonic threats. He doesn't just "destroy" he also "hunts" as part of his mission.

                                              So Mr. Stripe needs to get into the crime scene but posing as a police officer isn't sufficiently in tune with "Hunting and Killing". This would be a disguise check of some kind, so he would make a Wits + Intimidation or Subterfuge roll. But since it's only partly related to his mission he doesn't get Rank. So he simply makes a "Finesse" check (Wits is a Finesse attribute). He gets 1 success and proceeds into the room where Mr. Black killed three people. Now that he's in this room he examines the crime scene. Examining a crime scene is Wits + Investigation, normally, so instead of that he may take Finesse + Rank. Examining a crime scene is related to his mission because it is part of "tracking his prey". So he gets 3 successes and discovers the window had been opened and Mr. Black got out that way.

                                              Mr. Stripe now goes out into the alleyway outside of the crime-scene to investigate Mr. Black's escape route carefully. Another Finesse + Rank roll and he knows that Mr. Black has gone to ground and is currently fleeing toward a safe place, likely a Bolthole or friend's safe-house. Unfortunately he doesn't know the exact location yet so he can't use his Numen Pathfinder to find the most expedient route there. So he approaches a car, as he needs to be able to move faster but still remain subtle to human onlookers, and take it. Stealing a car helps him with the chase but itself is not an action of the hunt so he only gets his Finesse again. He rolls one success, opens the door and gets in the car and starts it.

                                              Driving slowly down the street he continues to look for the subtle signs of the Mr. Black's passing. By this time the Demon has slipped into his human Cover. Mr. Stripe finds the exact spot that Mr. Black assumed his human identity with another Finesse + Rank roll and then looks about for any possible witnesses. He notices that all along these streets are security cameras. He has the "Beast Eyes" variant Numina which allows him to use machine eyes as his eyes. So with a successful Power + Finesse activation, he looks through all the security camera footage and immediately determines that there is someone out of place - a man in a black suit without a briefcase or any belongings on him. This appears odd, but not fatally, so. He determines this might be Mr. Black, as it's the only nearby candidate.

                                              Mr. Stripe advances on this suited man's location and begins to follow him. Shadowing Stealthily is usually Wits + Stealth vs. Wits + Composure. It's also a main part of "the hunt" so Finesse + Rank vs. Mr. Black's Wits + Composure. Unfortunately, Mr. Black loses out and so Mr. Stripe doesn't lose his tail. Mr. Black is beginning to lose his cool and panic but he keeps walking, calmly and serenely (after all he doesn't need to physically display his panic if he doesn't want to) so as not to raise any red flags with the Angel. Another series of identical rolls are used to represent the Storyteller's efforts to have the Angel follow the poor Demon around a series of winding streets.

                                              As Mr. Black opens the entrance to his Bolthole in a panic to escape the Angel, however, the Angel successfully identifies him as his quarry. Unfortunately it only has a visual record of his Cover, not a name or anything meaningful, so it will take other rolls to give Mr. Black the Blown Condition. However, he does have time to stop him! They are both on an empty street a few feet from each other. So Mr. Stripe immediately raises one of his huge metallic arms, itself a massive cannon that shoots white-hot superheated metal and fires on Mr. Black! He attacks with his Blast Numen. Since one of his limbs is a ranged attack, Mr. Stripe has a ranged basic attack that does 0L. He gets three successes on his Blast Numen and so that becomes a 3L attack, then he makes the attack roll and gets another 3 successes after accounting for Mr. Black's defense, and so inflicts 6 Lethal on poor Mr. Black.

                                              Mr. Black realising he can't hide in his Bolthole any longer as this Hunter would just batter it down, just decides to Go Loud and get out after giving Mr. Stripe a run for his money, because his black-suited cover was only 3-dots anyway.

                                              Conclusion
                                              If Mr. Black were to be switched with Mr. Stripe, he would have had to use several embeds and maybe even an exploit or two to do what this Angel could simply do using the much broader Ephemeral Creatures rules as presented in GMC (and Demon: the Descent for Beast Eyes.).

                                              He'd need to use Authorized or Diversion to get into the crime scene and perhaps would have Advanced Optics or Component Indicators in order to examine the crime scene itself and determine that Mr. Black had been there to begin with. (Poor guy should have used Without a Trace!) All of these options need him to have Skill in Investigation of at least 3 dots to equal Mr. Stripe's ability along with at least one of these two abilities. Moving on from there, Mr. Black now analyses his escape route again with Advanced Optics or Component Indicators using an Investigation roll. When he finds the spot where he had assumed his Cover, he'd need to take over the security cameras. So he'd need Data Retrieval at least, or a whole host of other Embeds in order to take over the camera network and use them as his eyes. Mr. Stripe just needed one Numen to do it all. Their chase would be much the same. If Mr. Black were to shoot himself, he'd need to partially manifest a Demonic Form, and take another Compromise check (albeit with a positive modifier) in order to shoot at himself with Eliminator Cannon in order to mimic what Mr. Stripe had built-in to his character.

                                                          That's right. It also depends on how much you want to stretch the rules to fit the fiction, but remember, story and fun always trump rules.

                                                          One thought I had while writing the above example was using an Influence to perform a feat that is overtly supernatural. I didn't include it because it is a stretchy interpretation of the rules and many might disagree with me. Allow me to explain.

                                                          Technically speaking the Manipulate Influence allows you to minorly alter the characteristics of an object within the purview of the entity's control. If you gave an angel an influence such as "Access", which might be far to broad even for an angel, then hypothetically you may also be able to use Manipulate to change the characteristics of, for example, a door. You might be able to change where that door leads and all it would cost is two essence.

                                                          It depends whether you consider doing something that is physically impossible and high-weird a "minor, major or overt" effect. If you consider it an overt effect changing where a door comes out should probably be a Numen. If, however, you don't it might serve as a good example as to how an Angel might use its Influences to create the reality warping effects you refer to.
