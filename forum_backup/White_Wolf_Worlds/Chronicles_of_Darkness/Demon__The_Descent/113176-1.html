                        When dealing with non-Changelings, the changeling doesn't have to make the sanction known when the pledge is made. The changeling can name it when the pledge is broken.
                        So if the demon lies, and the changeling knows it (the sun is purple), the changeling can call upon a sanction at the time, for example, when they know the Demon has said a lie, even if they didn't use their skills to do so (he's a good liar, but he's definitely lying). Other supernaturals are considered human, in regards to pledges. Making a pledge with a demon to tell the truth to the changeling (or even everyone they come across) isn't really taking away their innate power, pledges don't work in regards to unfair stuff (hold your breath for a billion years, mortal). In this case, as Cleverest of Things pointed out, Angels don't hunt demons by hunting down liars, the Demon has a choice, whether to lie or not, his ability to do so is never taken away.

                        That said, if you've been around the forums a bit you've probably noticed the ruling, something to the extent of, "Your favorite splat gets the benefit of the doubt," which often plays second fiddle to, "Do whatever's best for the game." Depending upon your game, who's doing the pledges, you might have the Wyrd (which is not a thing, mind, but the relationship between all things) patter uselessly off of them like water off a duck's back, or give the Demon a sense of repercussions of a less God Machine bent.

                                Originally posted by Oddness View Post
                                Eh, I think if you make the demon immune to all forms of discovery, you destroy the central themes of demon. Angels have innate ways to find demons - why can't changelings have sly ways of finding them? I think it fits into both themes of the games.
                                Which involves forcing them to reveal their nature after they do something suspicious to draw attention to yourself. Its degrading their Cover until they can no longer do it.

                                In either case detecting a demon or his lies are two different things. The first case has very specific rules, and requires a successful Cover roll - which is why angels do their best to force Compromise checks, and degrade cover until that Cover is either not functional, or has enough Conditions that Cover no longer works.

                                The no-lying/mind reading thing just flat out negates any attempt to magically force someone to reveal the truth. And such a changeling pledge is just another magical truth power. If a changeling wanted to discover a demons true nature, they could investigate and track down information about it, just like mortals, angels, and every other supernatural on the planet. The changeling also has the advantage of dream invasions, tricky pledges, and their own Masks.



                                Besides, by saying that the demon just simply wins, sure you preserve a core characteristic of demon, but you destroy a core characteristic of changelings.
                                This doesn't negate a changeling's innate ability to manipulate the Mask, play with dreams, or to use their pledge magic. It negates a single, non-standard application of changeling pledge magic. The changeling could force a demon into an alliance (which IS a standard pledge to make) through pledges, and his lies won't protect him from breaking that. The changeling can make any other number of casual deals. It doesn't negate anything about changeling's themes, just one minor application that's being trying to be used to negate someone else's major aspects.

                                I mean, by this rationale, a mage should be able to detect a demon's lies, because casting magic and gathering information is what they do. And yet, the book notes it won't work on demons. So, no. I don't think that pledges are any better than a mage spell, which cannot detect truth or lies.


                                EDIT - the main Tasks that one can assign with a changeling pledge are : Alliance, Dreaming, Endeavor, Ensourcellment, and Forbiddance. Under suggested forbiddances, no where does it list "lying" or "telling the truth" as a suggestion. Using pledges to enforce truth is such a minor thing that allowing Unchained to avoid it has no impact on how changeling themes work.
