    Random Demon Questions

    11-20-2013, 03:18 PM
    Where is the xp table? How much xp does a dot of Cover cost?

    With the right combination of options, the Bolthole merit could be the key from the Lost Room. I assume this was intentional?

    If I make a Pact with someone, I end up having the same context to a piece of traded life that they had, right? So, I can't make a pact with a rock star to take the place of their manager, I'd have to make a pact with the manager?

    If I make a Soul Pact which grants the subject a lot of money and then call it in immediately, do I get the money? Do I need to use Legend to get it?

    Can I make a Pact for someone's job? If so, what level is it?

    Is relationship strength objective or subjective? I mean, if a married couple has gone through rough times to the point that they're practically strangers, is it still worth three points? Conversely, if someone is a shy recluse and their only real moment of human contact in a given day is exchanging pleasantries with the pretty barista at the coffee shop they go to every morning and the recluse values that relationship very highly, does it still count as an acquaintanceship (because the barista would certainly consider it such)?

    If I start building a patchwork Cover out of a whole bunch of little relationships and things, do I get to specify the Cover's appearance? Do I ever gain free merits for having a high Cover rating?

    Possibly I just missed this bit, but where are the benefits of having a high Cover described? As it gets more and more embedded into reality, do you gain some of the social benefits of your new life or do you still have to use Legend or XP to get them? If you don't get the social benefits, does reality at least start treating your Cover as more real, with varied clothing and a house that makes physical sense? Is there a table that describes that anywhere?

    If you have multiple Covers, how much time do you need to put into each one before they start degrading? Or does it depend on the Cover? I mean, one assumes that a serial killer can probably go for a long time between killings without much degradation, but an office worker who doesn't turn up for a week is probably going to find their cover wearing dangerously thin. Is that right or did I miss a table somewhere?

                  Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                  Where is the xp table? How much xp does a dot of Cover cost?
                  Three. This will be clearer in the final book; the general Experience cost table is in the Character Creation Quick Reference.

                  Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                  If I make a Pact with someone, I end up having the same context to a piece of traded life that they had, right? So, I can't make a pact with a rock star to take the place of their manager, I'd have to make a pact with the manager?
                  Right.

                  Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                  If I make a Soul Pact which grants the subject a lot of money and then call it in immediately, do I get the money?
                  No, the money goes where you directed it, which wasn't to yourself.

                  Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                  Can I make a Pact for someone's job? If so, what level is it?
                  You can. It's medial.

                  Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                  Is relationship strength objective or subjective? I mean, if a married couple has gone through rough times to the point that they're practically strangers, is it still worth three points? Conversely, if someone is a shy recluse and their only real moment of human contact in a given day is exchanging pleasantries with the pretty barista at the coffee shop they go to every morning and the recluse values that relationship very highly, does it still count as an acquaintanceship (because the barista would certainly consider it such)?
                  It depends on how it matters to the pactee. EDIT: But "matters" doesn't mean "likes." An estranged wife is likely a major part of the pactee's life, even if her role is "I don't understand her and I don't want to be around her."

                  Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                  If I start building a patchwork Cover out of a whole bunch of little relationships and things, do I get to specify the Cover's appearance? Do I ever gain free merits for having a high Cover rating?
                  In character, a patchwork cover accretes elements of the appearances of the people it was made from. Out of character, I'd simply allow the player to select the appearance, and encourage them to incorporate a memorable trait or two from one of the pactees. You never get free Merits from Cover.

                  Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                  Possibly I just missed this bit, but where are the benefits of having a high Cover described? As it gets more and more embedded into reality, do you gain some of the social benefits of your new life or do you still have to use Legend or XP to get them? If you don't get the social benefits, does reality at least start treating your Cover as more real, with varied clothing and a house that makes physical sense? Is there a table that describes that anywhere?
                  There's no table, just the general descriptions of high and low cover. More detail will be provided on this in the Player's Guide.

                  EDIT: And of course the mechanical benefits.

                  Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                  If you have multiple Covers, how much time do you need to put into each one before they start degrading? Or does it depend on the Cover? I mean, one assumes that a serial killer can probably go for a long time between killings without much degradation, but an office worker who doesn't turn up for a week is probably going to find their cover wearing dangerously thin. Is that right or did I miss a table somewhere?
                  You don't have to spend time in a Cover to keep it from degrading, but if you made a pact for someone's office job, you need to show up for it or you'll probably get fired. Getting fired, though, doesn't affect your cover rating.

                  Originally posted by squidheadjax View Post
                  -Is a Pact capable of stripping more general Merits from a mortal into a Demon's Cover? Close relationships could qualify for Ally or True Friend... but it seems that something like trading a rock star's fanbase (Fame) for curing the cancer brought on by a life of smoking everything they could stick in their mouth and light up - which strikes me as a very Demon-y sort of pact - isn't supported by the rules on either end (since I can't think of a Merit that covers 'lacks a case of cancer', either).
                  I'd allow taking a Merit as part of a pact. I'd treat taking the cancer as a medial benefit. Honestly, you're right, there isn't a good Merit for "lacks life-threatening illness," so I'd just try to figure out how big a benefit it is for the mortal and put it in one of the three classes.

                  Originally posted by squidheadjax View Post

                      As I understand Cover can only be bought with Cover Experience, and one Cover is 3 points.

                      If you want to be the manager, you would have to make a pact with the manager. That is his life and thus he has title to it.

                      Any Merits dealing with the external nature of the Cover, like Resources or even Striking Looks should be retained. No, you shouldn't need Legend, although that may be needed to mimic the subjects original natural talents like Expression or such.

                      Jobs are typically major components to people's life, so I would say three, maybe in two in some cases.

                      Relationship strength should be objective. Your wife is a major aspect of your life, even if you hate the bitch. Maybe especially so.

                      You get to pick appearance, but no free Merits.

                      Higher Covers grant bonuses to compromise checks, and generally degrade slower.

                      Time needed varies by Cover.

                      You should be able to affect anyone with an Essence score at base. It's cats that don't have an Essence or Aether score that the effect is up in the air. So ghosts and spirits are fair game.

                                      A Soul Pact should grant any external Merits. You steal the soul of a rich guy, then you are rich. How he got the money, legitimately or otherwise, is irrelevant. The main thing is that you could not immediately collect on a deal like a soul pact for wealth; you would need to wait until the money was accrued and possible until it was cemented as a pat of their persona before you could collect the soul and reap the benefit yourself. Yes this gives you free access to certain merits with a Soul Pact, but 1) It's not protected by Sanctity of Merits and thus is easy come easy go, 2) High external merits tend to draw unwanted attention, and 3) it costs a Willpower dot to close the deal, so I think that's adequate compensation.

                                      A Job would be everyone directly affected. So your immediate supervisor, immediate coworkers in your department, and regular clients. Other departments, irregular clients, your boss's boss, and the government wouldn't be affected.

                                      As far as extra-contractual terms and agreements, a Pact doesn't carry any supernatural enforcement power, but can still be implemented, just like getting to signature through threat of force, which is kosher I believe.
