                                    Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
                                    Actually you produce Aether just by being in Demon Form, so not an issue.
                                    Actually, you're a receptor for aether, not a generator. Aether is a God Machine waste byproduct, not something they make on their own.                                                     IIRC, Mages who teleport in can't teleport back out, just like Demons. It has to do with the metaphysics of the Hedge. In the hedge, reality is loosely defined. Getting INTO the Hedge from Earth works because the earth, and its Gates, are the "anchor" for the user, giving a solid, stable concept that a Mage or Demon can rework/edit to allow entrance. Once inside the Hedge, reality becomes so fluid that hacking the code or rewriting the truth isn't so simple anymore. The Hedge is a place of chaos after all. Of course, getting into the Hedge is NOT the same as getting into Arcadia. It'd be as difficult for a Demon to get there as it would be for a changeling, except that demons would be considered outsiders to the natives. As for aether generation, I'd allow demonic form to get aether while on paths (the stability of the paths, connections to mortal gateways etc) but not while in the thorns (the chaos is almost reality-free, and any God influence is minimal and shredded). Inside of Arcadia, I'd say you can only receive aether if you make a pact with a gentry to be allowed aether (perhaps the Fae allows some aether into their realm, or they duplicate it) but this would have to be done in every individual domain. And above all, Gentry are tier 4 entities. Unless attacked on earth (where the GM/reality requires them to diminish), only a tier 4 being has Any clean shot taking one on. A primum 7 demon that hasn't become an archdemon MIGHT be able to take out an Actor, but itd be a small fraction of the whole Fae. Taking on a True Fae on their scale would probably require an Archdemon, and then things like Pax Arcana kick in. While Pax Arcana is theoretically just in a Mage system, it seems everyone abides from it (the Gentry claim to do so by their own wishes).
