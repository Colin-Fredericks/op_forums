    Unchained in Manch-Vegas: Session 8 - Dirty Jobs

    06-03-2020, 05:57 PM
    Cast:
    • Liberty Griswald - Guardian/Tempter. Works as a Bodyguard for the Empire Security Company.
    • Molly Underwood - Destroyer/Tempter. Works as a Private Investigator and part time movie theater attendant.

    Session Notes
    While Alan and Liberty recruited Colin the dog, Molly was busy staking out the Men in Black office. She spent about four hours getting the lay of the land. She determined that the building only had Infrastructure on the fourth floor, a fire escape was a secondary access point, four human dupes also worked in the office, and that the Men in Black never left the office. Molly called Liberty and they decided to try to infiltrate the Men in Black office and find the linchpin.

    Molly put on her Authorized gadget janitor uniform while Liberty watched her back from across the street. In the basement, Molly is able to find an Aether wasteline from the 4th floor which allowed her to siphon off that energy. Additionally, she identified a way to cause an explosion that might destroy the Facility (Physical building housing Infrastructure) and an air vent that leads to the 4th floor. The logic being that if the building is destroyed the 4th floor won’t be able to operate.

    At the same time, a Man in Black noticed Liberty, identified himself as an FBI agent, and confronted Liberty as a person of interest in a shootout at the cemetery, which shouldn’t have been possible since Molly erased their memory of the event. The Man in Black tried to arrest Liberty, but Liberty managed to dodge every attempt to restrain him.

    During the fight the other Man in Black arrived and pulled his gun out on Liberty. Molly arrived as well and tried to de-escalate the situation. She used her Cool Heads Prevail embed to get everyone to calm down. While everyone was confused she pretended Liberty was her senile father and apologized to the agents. The Men in Black seemed to buy the explanation for the moment, allowing them to leave.

    Back at their base, Molly and Liberty devised a plan to destroy the building, but realized they didn’t have any explosives. Liberty retrieved a smoke bomb and explosive from his pocket with his In My Pocket embed. They knew Liberty couldn’t go back there and still maintain his cover. So he went to a nearby dive bar and forged a soul pact with a drunk redneck, granting the man striking good looks. The deal was too one sided, but Liberty found himself in a new body with a pickup truck and light revolver.

    Back at the Men in Black office, Liberty parked on the main street in front of the building while Molly went back in. The plan worked just as expected: the smoke bomb in the air vent caused someone to pull the fire alarm. Molly snuck into their vacant office and threw a couple of computers and files into her bolthole (Easy Acces merit). She left and waited a few minutes before detonating the explosives. The Men in Black searched for possible saboteurs and chased after Molly. She ducked into an alley and hid inside her bolthole around a corner. By the time they turned the corner she was gone. After a few hours Molly and Liberty regrouped.

    The documents from the Men in Black office revealed information on Infrastructure in the area, that Detective Jane Bullet may be involved in supernatural activity, and a dossier on Liberty. To make a long story short, Liberty left evidence when he shot at one of the Men in Black agents at the graveyard. And since Liberty’s firearm was identified in a recent police report the Men in Black identified him as a person of interest.

    More importantly the Men in Black are scheduled to report this information to Brix, an Angel. And they plan to report Liberty’s connection at Colin’s Dog Park the next day.

    GM Notes:
    • Preparation
      • I didn't do much preparation this time. I knew my players would want to take on the Men in Black this session. But didn't realize they would avoid direct conflict as much as they did.
    • Session Start
      • Two of my players made it.
    • What could I have done better?
      • The Men In Black were not great at melee especially when Liberty has a Defense of 5. I will likely be upping their Dicepool to make them a bit more scary. One has Biokinesis but that only got is dicepool up to 6.
      • I tired to use conditions, but my players never wanted to use them. Mainly because they don't want to resolve a condition like Shaken where you have to act against the party if you want to resolve it.
      • Soul Pact was too easy, but I was running out of time in the session and thought it would be a good idea to streamline that one.
    • What went well?
      • Molly's extended investigation went really well, I had her roll her Cover every hour she staked out the building, removing a dice from the dicepool every time she took another hour.
      • Liberty's soul pact, though I need to come up with a couple of pre-built NPCs for them to want to make deals with. At the moment, their hunting has been relegated to bars.
    • What do I need to not forget
      • ​Jane Bullet
        • This is now a plot hook that the players will likely have to deal with after Brix. Jane's been following Liberty and now Liberty has a hint she's not human.
        • I'm going to start having Liberty have the Surveilled condition possibley.
      • Brix
        • The Hawaiian shirt wearing Angel that looks a bit like Bruce Campbell.
        • If they want they may encounter Brix at the dogpark next episode. But if they don't Liberty is going to get the Flagged Condition.
      • Mrs. White
        • She will likely be very thankful for finding all this new information. I'm planning on giving out some gadgets to help increase their "Armory".
      • Men In Black
        • They know that Liberty is onto them and need to get to a secure location to report to Brix. Hopefully the players can kill them before they reveal what they know.
        • They have seen Molly twice so far, once as a Janitor and once as a civilian, she's associated with Liberty, but they have yet to identify her. Since their equipment is destroyed it'll be more difficult for them to do so.
    • My Fears
      • Because Liberty is the most active player, I've been able to work with his character more. My hope is that I don't put the spot light on him too much. I need to bring in another character I have lined up, but haven't used. Teddy The Guardian Angel that is now protecting the child Molly was assigned to kill.
    On the System (I come from a DnD background with some experience with other systems like Call of Cthulhu 7e)
    • I love a lot about the system, but I'm getting confused with the minutia of rules. Its very easy to just roll with it, but there are about 2-3 steps to each aspect of the game that can stack up without me realizing it:
      • Soul Pacts - Once I make the pact which required my player to lose willpower for each bit that he went over by he has to roll a Wits + Manipulation + Primum to determine its Cover Rating.
      • Compromise Checks - I've been making the mistake of Wits + Compromise instead of Wits + Manipulation. Additionally I didn't realize that Glitches are entirely optional, they are alternatives to Conditions.
      • Cover XP - I haven't been tracking this, additionally I think I need to give out more XP because my players are averaging about 2-3 a session. 1 for exceptional roleplaying, 1 for showing up. Aspirations aren't being used, mostly because we can only meet once every couple of weeks and forget a lot of what is part of the system.
    • Basically I think I'm gonna rework the system a lot to mesh with my group. I know that's to be expected, but I feel like I should write this down, just in case a designer comes through and wants to see what can be streamlined for the game.
    • What we haven't touched yet:
      • Going Loud
      • The Cipher, no one has bought any embeds so the Cipher has pretty much been useless.
    • What I'd like to see more of:
      • Demon Community expanded, so far my players have an Agency they deal with and some independent agents they work with, but there is no community like there is in Vampire. You don't see Demons going to Demon territory or reporting to Demon Lords. It might be a minor thing, but I haven't discovered a reason for why Demons would make themselves know to other demons. Its a risk for their Cover whenever someone finds out who they are.
    Just some extra thoughts. Please let me know what you think and if you have any comments or suggestions.

      Thanks for the write-up! I know how much effort that takes :-)

      Finding a drunk for a Soul Pact: First, what is your player's Primum? They can't have more Covers than their Primum. Also, a Soul Pact is a big hammer to pull out in this situation. It's a permanent effect. You might suggest to the player that they can buy some minor experiences and put up a Facade (described in Flowers of Hell, the Demon Players's Manual). With a Facade, a demon can buy a minor relationship for a short while that gives the a Cover with a rating of 1, cannot be used for Going Loud, but doesn't count against their total number of allowed Covers. It's temporary and it's for exactly this situation. Your player gives the NPC striking good looks for the next few hours and gets a disguise. There's no permanent reduction of Willpower (loss of a "dot") for the Demon so it's much cheaper.

      Moreover, you only get the Cover from the Soul Pact when you annihilate the person's soul, and step into their life. Is that what your player did? Otherwise they probably bought some Cover Experiences. They can work that into a patchwork Cover or a Facade.

      If you are looking for a plot hook, and your drunk NPC still exists, and they made a Soul Pact, then they will have an increasing feeling of existential dread as they come to realize that they really have lost their eternal soul. This may motivate him to track down your PC.

      As for Defense of your PC being too high, Defense does not apply to ranged weapons, i.e. guns. You can't dodge a bullet. Your MIB can also spend a point of Willpower to get +3 dice. (NPC's do have Willpower, but I think the manual implies that it should be invoked rarely). So, shoot 'em up! Your Demon's are much more squishy than they think.

      If Jane Bullet is watching one of the PC's, the PC can lose Cover as their life is investigated. This can strike them as a bolt from the blue, a zap that leaves them shaken as they realize someone, somewhere, is chipping away at their Cover! Now they have to work on counter-espionage.

      One of the themes you might consider introducing is the uncertainty involved in espionage. NPC's can have several hidden layers of motivation. Maybe the MIB are a decoy to lure Demons into attacking so that a Hunter Angel can follow them later. Maybe Ms. White has a hidden agenda. She might want to use the PC's as a diversion (without their knowledge) so that she can do some angel-jacking. This doesn't have to simply be "Mr. X will betray you to the GodMachine." However, Demons are not known for altruism and they can hide their motivations.

              Originally posted by DontEatRawHagis View Post
              Jane Bullet is going to be the first curveball I throw at them. A Demon Hunter who is an Integrator Demon. Complete with a Cult of corrupt police officers..
              I've struggled a bit with Integrator Demons as antagonists. They can't really cooperate with the GM - I think it's pretty well established that the GM wants them recycled. From what I understand of what your wrote above, Jane is acting as a "free agent" when they hunt Demons. This is a nice interpretation of an Integrator: "Only acting as a Demon can I truly serve the GM's interest."

              In my campaign I have adopted a standard espionage trope: a sniveling traitor manipulated by their handler. Angel "A" assured demon "B" that they would "put in a good word for them" and that their efforts would "ensure that "B" would be re-incorporated without any deconstruction" when the mission is over. Of course "A" will do no such thing. They just cruelly dangle this hope in front of "B", who is a rather pathetic character.

              If any other angel discovers "B", they will happily drag them back to the GodMachine, kicking and screaming, for disassembly.
