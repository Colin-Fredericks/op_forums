                                                Originally posted by Cauthon View Post
                                                Can a Goetia or a Ghost Claim someone? Since Goetia typically can't leave the Astral and both have a different skillset than Spirits, I imagine "no", at least not without outside interference such as Magely empowerment. Sin-Eaters and their Giests are close, but not the same as Claimed, as I understand it.
                                                Per the book, Ghost can claim ppl, they are native to the materials world and get a number of manifestations equal to their rank. Thus they may struggle to get all the required manifestations to actually claim until higher rank (or by giving up numena). Of course higher rank ghost can exist due to unusual circumstances, created by occult alignment/rare mysterious events (aka st fiat) so if you want a higher rank ghost - as opposed to a Geist - you can have one.

                                                The other options are geists, reapers, kerberori and Chthonians.

                                                To which i would just say:

                                                Yes to Geists (the pack they make with Bound is not the same but equivalent level).

                                                Reapers are unlikely to be able to do so as it doesn't align with their task, and are more likely to want to drag a ghost who has claimed someone into the Underworld.

                                                Kerberori and Chthonians are less likely to be out of the Underworld and Chthonians in particular don't fair well in the material.

                                                That said, i see no reason a kerberos couldn't have claimed someone and then brought that body back to the Underworld as punishment for violating their old laws. In 1e they got manifestations, so why not?

                                                Goetia on the other hand, don't even get twilight manifestation by default, so summon goetia should really bring over an entity with no manifestation at all. But I suspect this is an oversight in the rules. Errata that they gain twilight manifestation by default. Without manifestations (or a mystery like LA's signature mystery) claiming isn't possible, but also goetia has less motivation to leave the astral. And if they could claim they wouldn't be able to bring their claimed body back to the astral physically. So you would want to have an entity which has a reason to want to stay... Maybe a personal goetia from the Onerios of someone who has died*, with nowhere to go back to... Maybe a temenotic goetia who is trying to restore worship of their religion - the avatar of some ancient God trying to spread more belief. Or maybe a goetia of some aspect of the world trying to restore the eco-system... Some possible interesting plot hooks there. But it would be rare.

                                                *It is possible that on the death of their human this personal goetia becomes a ghost.

                                                Edit: i did find the Claim manifestation in the Geist book, so yes, ghosts definitely can.

                                                    I'm about to run my first Mage campaign (Yay!) and I have a question about the Teleportation spell to help set up my opening scenario.

                                                    It says that you can add an Arcana with an associated realm to teleport the subject to that realm or vice versa. I'd assume, then, that if you add Mind 2, you can teleport into or from the Astral. If a Mage teleported someone into the astral, would it just send their consciousness there and the bodies would fall limp?

                                                    To further elucidate my aim, the campaign is centered around the Pentacle's first (modern) magic academy with newly Awakened teenagers being initiated into Mage society through the school's curriculum. Of course, it's going to go horribly wrong down the road, but we'll get there.

                                                    My opening scenario involves an initiation in which the players are sent to the Astral, have their memories temporarily rewritten to believe they are going to detention for some wrong doing and to forget that they're in the Astral, and have them placed in the Temenos' "Twilight Zone". Specifically, they'll be in the setting of "Five Characters in Search of an Exit". They will have to realize where they are, force themselves awake, and then they'll have a scavenger hunt back in the real world to discover all the nooks and crannies of their school.

                                                    My initial thought was to have a guided meditation into the Astral by the school's Hallow, do the memory alterations, and leave it at that. However, teleporting to the Astral would be a lot less messy. So any thoughts on how to accomplish this would be appreciated.
