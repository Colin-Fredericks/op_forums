                                    Unveiling spells reveal hidden or abstract things, whereas Knowing spells give you information about a targeted subject — before Mage Sight was an Attainment, all the spells that provided a form of Mage Sight were Unveiling spells, and Soul Marks and Supernal Vision similarly are Unveiling because without supernatural powers a character simply would not be able to perceive souls or Arcana competency without being shown them in another manner. There are a few outliers on this front, like Tune In or Web of Life, but generally speaking if a spell deals with a specified target instead of opening up a new avenue of perception it's a Knowing spell.

                                                If you want to differentiate Knowing and Unveiling, I think these guidelines might help:

                                                Knowing is about gaining the information you want to know about. It grants knowledge unfiltered by your senses.
                                                Unveiling is about perceiving more of the world around you. It expands your senses and allows you to observe more information through them.

                                                So with Knowing you should be able to gain relatively deep or targeted* information by casting the spell directly on a subject. Analyze Life, Know Nature and Soul Marks (despite being listed as Unveiling) are definitely this type of spell.

                                                Unveiling complements this by granting you the ability to perceive similar information by augmenting your own senses. The information granted should be cursory or, if relatively deep, require a turn's studying to discern. Speak With the Dead** (the reveal Rank and Anchors part) and Interconnections are good examples of this type of spell.

                                                Supernal Vision breaks this by being the expanded perception type of Unveiling spell that requires a turn's studying, but also grants both deep and targeted information like a knowing spell. I'd absolutely split it into two: One Unveiling spell which grants the common information from the first part, and one Knowing spell which grants a number of facts from the list in the second part of the spell.

                                                There are also other types of spells for both Practices, which helps differentiate the two:
                                                Knowing can grant real-time updates on certain triggers on the subject (like Oath's Fulfilled), or surrounding information like the intended fuction of a tool (like Craftman's Eye***), or by simply locating something within an AoE that you couldn't perceive (Locate Object).
                                                Unveiling can, of course, expand your senses in various wild and interesting ways (Nightvision, Receiver, Heightened Senses, the Outward and Inward Eye, Exorcist's Eye, etc).
                                                A less common type of Unveiling spell which kinda infringes on Knowing by virtue of bringing forth information that simply isn't perceptible, is the one that brings forth that information in order to allow you to perceive it through your senses (Postcognition)***.

                                                Now, for detection spells like Web of Life (listed as Knowing) and Detect Matter (listed as Unveiling), I can't really say which Practice fits best. It really depends on how you envision them. They do grant information you can't perceive directly to you like a Knowing spell, but you could absolutely argue that you're given a new sense that picks up these things outside of your regular senses. As written, I think both those spells should be Knowing as they are written in the book (gaining information about a specific area rather than being sensed by the mage), but changing them to work in an area around the subject (subject detects all things within X meters per Potency rather than using AoE on a specific area) would definitely turn them into Unveiling spells.
                                                If you don't want both versions to be possible, I'd slot this type of spell into Knowing because that's how the two (or three with Locate Object) spells of this type is already written to work.



                                                Tune In is absolutely the wrong Practice because "the ability to hear radio waves" is specifically listed as an example under Unveiling.

                                                *Any spell which grants a number of facts equal to Potency is an example of a spell giving you targeted information. You choose the

                                                **Speak With the Dead should not be cast as an AoE spell, and instead instead work more like Exorcist's Eye. It makes little sense under these guidelines, and is just weird regardless because you can have a conversation with a ghost be cut short because the ghost walked a few steps in the wrong direction left the AoE.

                                                ***Craftman's Eye is listed as a Knowing spell and grants information that I think fits Knowing, but is cast on a subject who then studies the tool for a turn like an Unveiling spell. I'd definitely remove that part and have the tool be the spell's subject.

                                                ****A Knowing version of Postcognition could instead grant you more targeted information, such as the exact time a specific event happened as well as a number of facts surrounding that event, but not allow you to view it.

                                                            Which Arcana are relevant to Vitae varies. In first edition (Tome of the Mysteries) you'd need Prime and Death to detect it. In second edition (PGttCC) it's instead Death and Life. Unless your table has houseruled how Vitae relates to the Arcana, you'd need one of those combinations depending on the edition.

                                                            Spirit (specifically Active Spirit Sight) can tell you which Resonant Conditions apply, which in turn means the resonances need to be strong enough that spirits can be sustained by them. Not the most reliable way of deducing whether someone is a vampire. If you want a less brute force method for discerning resonances, you'd have to use Prime instead.

                                                            As for discerning between vampires and ghouls, if you have either Life or Death in the spell then detecting whether they're alive or dead should be child's play and not require a Reach, imo.
