    Originally posted by Isvan View Post
    The section on Pacts in the book says that non-personal Merits can still be grants, but they take time to transfer and can't be given in large amounts in one go. It doesn't mention supernatural merits, though.

    If a Demon was to give a cultist or an other stigmatic contact Suborned Infrastructure, how would you handle it? They acquire something from another Demon (who isn't very happy)? A GM installation goes offline nearby? I'm guessing it would involve someone not being very happy with the change, though.
    For the merit Suborned infrastructure I do not believe it is a valid merit for a pact for a few reasons. First off it is intended for demons and is demon specific as far as I am aware. It may not say the restriction in its description but the location of the merit is proof enough. Next the merit represents infrastructure the demon has disconnected from the machine for their own use so I don't feel pacts should have the power to directly affect the machine. Then finally this benefit has no benefit to the one signing the pact so in that way I do not feel it should be valid from an OOC standpoint as it is obvious what the intent is.

    However, if you were to allow it I do not feel it would be taken from another demon as that is outside the machine's influence, since the infrastructure has been disconnected. Possibly you could do it with the deed of a random bit of infrastructure now belonging to this mortal. If this was able to work however you would no doubt draw attention from the machine as it tries to identify the cause in this sudden shifting of variables. Now, because it is not protected by the sanctity of merits if this was allowed to occur by an st I'd expect fallout as a result.
