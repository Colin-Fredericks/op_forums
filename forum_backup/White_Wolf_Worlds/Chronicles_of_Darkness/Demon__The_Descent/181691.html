                                  As an Integrator, you see the systems of the God-Machine as useful to your goals. What that means for your feelings on the God-Machine as an entity is highly variable.

                                  I mean, really, let's actually look at the lexicon for a moment, because the operative conflict between the Turncoats and the Thugs is decidedly broader than "these guys hate the Machine, these guys want to rejoin the Machine, let's you and him fight":

                                  Integrators: an Agenda; demons who wish to reunite with the God-Machine, subvert it, change it for the better, or take their old place as angels.
                                  Saboteurs: an Agenda; demons who wish to disrupt the God-Machine’s plans by preventing it from creating Infrastructure and occult matrices.

                                  If your plans include "trick the giant miracle-engine into making magic that serves my purposes," you are by necessity going to come into conflict with someone whose plans include "destroy the system that makes impossibilities happen."

                                  If it weren't for free will, you'd probably be willing to be recycled and return to be an angel, and start doing shit like before you Fell. Why would I trust someone who could easily decide to aid the God Machine?
                                  Because literally the only difference between the Turncoats and the rest of the Agendas is that they are more likely to have a reason for stabbing you in the back that you can immediately discern which is not "I don't have to outrun the bear."

                                      Originally posted by Jesuncolo View Post
                                      Ok, you want to go back to the God machine. But you want to retain free will. There's next to no possibility that will happen. Why should I want someone who wants to betray us in our ring?
                                      I mean...Integrators seem to me more of a "counter-splat", like Banishers, Bale hounds, Seers of the Throne and Loyalists...
                                      Next to no possibility? That's your problem right there. You're assuming the chances are small. You're also assuming that they want to retain free will, and not some other goal, such as reprogramming the local God Machine processes, or they believe that demons still serve the God Machine, but in a different capacity. In Demon, there's a very strong chance that Integrators can pull it off. You have the ability to - the only problem is that your opponents have bigger guns.

                                      I don't see the Integrators as the counter-splat, but more like the "sin-eating" splat: Lancea Sanctum, Guardians of the Veil, or the Winter Court. They're involved in doing suspicious things for the right reasons.

                                      Integrators are the ones most likely to think and act like Fallen Angels, instead of Demons.

                                      And I agree with Satchel - you are painting the Saboteurs with a rose-colored brush. They're driven by HATE. They want to bring down the God-Machine at any cost. Lets not forget that the G-M saved humanity from the Y2K bug and astroids with the power to destroy all life on earth. Lets not forget that, should God-Machine die, there's a good chance that there's something worse out there that's waiting to fill the void. Lets not forget that tearing down Infrastructure tends to have backlashes like, oh, earthquakes and major fires starting up.

                                      How could you trust someone that's so deep in hate that they find all this collateral damage and lives lost acceptable?
