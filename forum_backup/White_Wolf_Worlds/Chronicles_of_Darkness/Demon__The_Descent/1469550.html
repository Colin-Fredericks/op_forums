    Polling interest, Angelic cover

    12-03-2021, 11:25 PM
    I'm currently working on taking a stab at expanding the rules of angels. In particular in regards to cover as I feel that materialize and mortal mask fall short for the narratives I want to explore. I've got about 2k words written up for it and just have to do layout stuff to prepare it for the story teller vault.

    The point of this post is two fold, first to test the waters and see how interested others may be in this idea as well as to see if anyone is interested in potentially being a beta reader. I feel confident in what I've done but feedback is good for improvement. If I wasnt planning to make it look pretty and put it on the story teller vault I'd simply post the text on these forums.

    Largely I want to help enable compromised agencies to be easier to explore without hand waving, as well as give a different way that demons may attempt to disrupt the God-Machines plans.

              ArcaneArts You do make good points, as always, and I should elaborate if I expect there to be meaningful interaction and engagement when submitting a topic such as this.

              To your first point, yes I have seen what Bunyip has said on this topic previously. It has been a helpful reference for intent as well as a good foundation with how they explore the topic. However though it does help, I personally feel that it falls short of completely resolving the matter. As is evident by the fact that others have brought it up from time to time and even years later it has remained in my own mind.

              To summarize the approach that I have taken in an attempt to resolve what I see as still present issue, I would present it as a sort of symmetry between angelic and demonic cover. A demon's first cover and its supporting infrastructure originates from before its fall after all. As such, I have done my best to avoid reinventing the wheel, so to speak. As a demon in its fall becomes entangled in its cover and closer to it than an angel, even remaining material and not ephemeral when it leaves cover, I explore the possibility that angels in cover are a step more distant than their demonic cousins. The approach that I take for this leans primarily on the possession manifestation's mechanics with the exception of duration. However that is not enough to fully resolve the issue alone. For an almost symmetrical approach I looked to how demons may assume their demon form and return to cover afterward and looked to adapt this. However an issue persists with this approach as while the possession manifestation is in place one cannot use numen or influences. The solution I explore here once more leans on demon mechanics and simply uses partial transformation rules to enable a work around at the risk of degrading cover.

              That is also the largest change one may say that I take in my approach, I have angels as capable of suffering compromise. While they may not act out of the norm of their cover, as that would go against their mission, the cover may be strained via transformations, mortal investigation or other potential sources of compromise. Of course in these situation as the cover frays it would not be the God-Machine's notice that would be drawn. Instead the conditions from such compromises would be in relation to other organizations, most often unchained agencies but not necessarily. This approach once more is in the interest of symmetry so that demons may engage with their espionage and attack the God-Machine's plans through another vector and engage with the themes of tech gnostic espionage more directly. As an added benefit it gives an explanation of why there is restoration facilities for demons to suborn in the first place.

              I also explore the process an angel takes to actually take on the cover granted to them by the God-Machine and how that interacts with angle jacking as well as hinting to the process of how the God-Machine just might recycle a captured demon by building off of the implications such an approach has.

              Of course there is a bit that I have left out but I feel this is the broad strokes while still giving enough depth to give a good idea of the approach that I explore in further depth.

              Hopefully this is enough to address your third point as well in regards to why I personally feel that materialize and mortal mask fall short of enabling some narratives, for instance an agency or ring that has been compromised by angelic infiltration. In a sense it is providing cover to angels as an optional feature.

                          That is fair enough. I took this approach primarily because previously, and repeatedly, I did try to do from the ground up and I just couldn't seem to get it how I envisioned it. I do understand it may not work for every game or every player/storyteller but by building off of the existing foundations present in the system I feel what I was able to design better fits within the system as a result. I feel that the end result I've achieved has angelic and demonic covers both contrast in certain aspects while still having comparisons that can be drawn.

                          To address your thoughts on possession and partial transformation interacting, that I primarily did as a way to have a demon being able to use partial transformation being a natural feature of how cover was designed to function. It is far simpler of an approach also than I may have made it sound.
