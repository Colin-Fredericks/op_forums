                                                    You know, I've been thinking that certain Demons might not be fully satisfied with the "shape-shifting" allowed by Covers, the Ephemeral Cover Exploit and Show Of Power. A few might even think it would be cool to live up to the fame of certain mythological fiends that had much more free-form transformations. At first, I considered a Lambda Gadget, but that seemed too external. My current idea is a pair of Merits that expands upon Hellhound and Ephemeral Cover. The name might need some improvement. I imagine that Unchained!Nyarlathotep would have many field days with these Merits, especially when combined with the Cipher that Eremite wrote for him. Feedback is super appreciated.

                                                    Genetic Morphing Field (•••)

                                                    Prerequisites: Hellhound Exploit, Stamina 3, Resolve 3, Science or Occult 3

                                                    Effects: The Demon can use the Hellhound Exploit on himself, with a few differences. Spend 1 Aether + 1 willpower point and roll the normal dice pool as an instant action (the willpower point cancels the compromise roll as usual for Exploits). With a Success, he can transform into any animal of up to Size 10 for the scene. An Exceptional Success increases the duration to [Primum] days. During this, he can shift between different animal forms as an instant action with 1 Aether, and back to the Cover he last used reflexively without spending Aether. Transforming into an animal that is grossly inaccurate for the region requires a compromise roll.

                                                    The Demon does not get [Primum + Successes] as extra dots to his stats. He uses the animal's Physical Attributes, Size and Speed Factor, but keeps his other stats. Additionally, he can assume a partial or complete Demonic Form, with the same benefits and drawbacks as usual. He retains the general appearance of the animal if he does this. He may use Spoofing to register as a mundane animal, with the rating of the Cover he last used. He may communicate as the animal would with a successful Wits + Animal Ken roll. He lacks the necessary anatomy to speak with a human voice, but may use Embeds, Exploits, Demon Form and Gadgets to communicate in other ways.

                                                    Corpus Reconfiguration (•• or ••••)

                                                    Prerequisites: An Ephemeral Cover, Occult 3, Subterfuge 3

                                                    Effects: The Demon can make the most out of a ghost or spirit Cover by changing its appearance to a different sub-type. Spend 1 Aether and roll Finesse + Primum as an instant action. On a Success, the Demon can make cosmetic changes that are substantial enough to be mistaken for another, which last for the scene. With an Exceptional Success, the change can last up to [Primum] days. The ghost of a murder victim will still carry signs of how it was killed, but the method may change from a gunshot to drowning. A spirit of lust can embody another Vice, but can't turn into a car spirit. In either case, facial features and other identifying markers will also change. Spoofing is still available to register as the appropriate ephemeral.

                                                    With the four dot version, more dramatic changes can be made. By spending 3 Aether + 1 willpower point as an instant action, the Demon gains a pool of dots equal to his [Primum x 2] to distribute among the three simplified Attributes and Size. Use the increased Finesse + Primum as the dice pool. Each Attribute has a maximum of 15 dots, and their sum determines the Rank of the Cover. Size can be up to 10. The duration is the same as the ones listed for the Success and Exceptional Success of the two dot version of this Merit. The Demon may steal Essence points from ephemerals of the same type and convert them to Aether with a Power + Brawl or Weaponry roll, but halved round up. If the Demon exceeds the target's Rank by 2 or more, he counts as a Bane. The ephemeral Cover's natural weaponry have a lethal Damage Bonus equal to the Demon's Primum, but every dot above 5 becomes one point of Armor Piercing. Many Demons with the four dot version of this Merit also learn Rip The Gates and Show Of Power to enter the Underworld and the Shadow to further refine their mimicry. At the ST's discretion, a Demon with the four dot version may imitate entities such as Geister and Hosts.
