        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
        A couple things about Knockout Punch as currently presented that I feel are being skimmed over:
        1) It's cumulatively penalized per miss in a scene on top of the resistance by Defense — which, lest we forget, is higher in GMC's rules than it was in the old nWoD system.
        2) Even a cursory attempt to revive the victim works unless you rolled an exceptional success.

        Its utility is drastically undercut in scenarios where you have to deal with any degree of numbers disadvantage that allows one enemy to take a moment to wake their fallen comrade. Meanwhile it's still very much useful as a power for quickly dispatching a lone guard.
        Unless you plan on barraging your players with multiple angels they have no hope of winning again, the rules don't explicitly disallow you from knocking out an angel with it. Even if there were multiple opponents one would still need to waste his turn to revive him making it much better to just knock someone out making the fight easier in general as the character would have to reroll initiative to get back into the fight afterward.

        An ST shouldn't have to look at this power and gauges whether it works or doesn't work every time though. My problem has nothing to do with the power of the embeds but rather how vague broad they are to the point where an experienced ST will easily understand how to houserule the power and an inexperienced st will see his campaign ruined because one of his players decided that sever powers when used in conjunction can win any game.

            This may be a method of resolution that is too simple but in my mind, as a potential player with admitted leanings toward wanting more awesome than not---I might ask if it were possible to use Knockout Punch on an angel who hasn't revealed its splendor (gone loud) so long as I got the jump on it. Basically, if it were obeying what it is to be human. Hypothetical: Angel Bob has been given an order to deal with any demons by the GM in as subtle a fashion as possible. Demon Jim has no compunction about taking down Angel Bob by whatever means. Angel Bob doesn't adapt quick enough to the mission at hand and Demon Jim knocks him out. Now---do I expect to be able to kill Angel Bob in his knocked out state? No. I mean, I could try but Angel Bob is only human to the eye. A gunshot or a knife wound can possibly do in a Demon but an Angel is still jacked to the GM. Doesn't that confer unto it inherent fortitude that would make an attack after this knockout unwise to say the least? "Best" case scenario I would envision being possible is I get the drop on an angel while he's cavorting in the meat suit, balance the desire to kill him with some other Embed/Exploit against the inevitable shitstorm that will follow and hope for the best if I follow through. But I can't imagine there will be any Storyteller that would let me continue to fortuitously knock, drop, and kill an Angel followed by an unimpeded escape with no ramifications. All that said, there's still the fuzzy issue of "Can this work on an angel in any circumstance, I.e. knocking him out while 'human'". There may be value in clarifying (if it isn't already---I probably should have read through the whole thing a couple times before opining) an angel's relative vulnerabilities when moving about as a human. I've seen inferences made based on how an angel doesn't need sleep should indicate its ability or lack thereof to lose consciousness. But that wouldn't fly for me necessarily. Just because it doesn't require rest doesn't mean it can't be imposed upon to lose awareness of being via an uppercut. Those are different things. Sorry for the imprecision but I hope the input of a neophyte in general to all these systems has offered something of value to the discussion. Reading through these posts helps me a little even though it's still heavily abstracted to eventually (I hope) know how to play.
