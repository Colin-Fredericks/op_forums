                            Originally posted by quelsec View Post
                            A question about potency.
                            Lets say i want someone to combust in fire. I get a total of 6 potency. The targets resist trait is 5. Does the flame hit with the power of potency 6 or 1?
                            If the spell is withstood then it would be potency 1.

                            For direct damage, spells are not usually withstood (stamina is taken into account via health levels which need to be overcome in lieu of a withstand).

                            If you are trying to transform them into fire, or turn the energy of their kinetic energy into heat, then that spell may be withstood. Whereas, if you are taking a candle and pushing it to be a fireball (with 6 potency) the subject of the spell is not the target you are hoping to damage, so it is again not withstood by their resist trait...

                                            Originally posted by quelsec View Post
                                            How about i use fate to inflict a poisined condition on the target. Does potency affect its toxicity? or is it more of "anything thats actually probable so ST decides"?
                                            Poison is not directly in the purview of Fate, so Potency is *probably* affecting how many times the subject gets unlucky, or how unlikely they are to be poisoned by this particular rare thing... (More potency meaning you can reach more unlikely things happening...) Which would then indirectly affect the toxicity, I guess.

                                            It is comparable to using a fire which is already burning to harm someone. You use the normal fire rules for damage rather than the direct damage for spells. So a poison's toxicity would depend on the poison in question and standard rules for such things.
