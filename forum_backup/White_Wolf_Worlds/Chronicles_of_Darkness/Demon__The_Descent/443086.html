    Demon Questions

    03-15-2015, 05:10 PM
    General
    - Are there any mechanical areas of Demon to look out for as being troublesome, unbalanced, or requiring a lot of ST arbitration to understand?
    - How well do demons play in a mixed group?

    Bolthole
    - Are the weapons created by the Arsenal effect limited to existing weapons, or can you have like 5L Brass Knuckles?
    - Are Explosives within the purview of Arsenal?
    - Are weapons with special traits (i.e. Silver) obtainable from Arsenal?
    - Just clarifying, absolutely anything or anyone left inside a cover-linked Bolthole when you swap covers is completely obliterated, regardless of their talents?
    - Are you limited to five dots per bolthole, or can you exceed more for additional benefits? If you can exceed more, does the penalty to detect the Bolthole accrue beyond -5?

    Cultists
    - Is it me, or are the benefits granted by the 4 and 5 dot versions of this merit extremely vague? What dicepools do the cultists use for their supernatural merits or to accomplish the "unsavory things"?
    - Can you link Cultists to other merits, like Retainer or Staff?

    Embeds
    - In the case of Embeds penalized by Defense, do penalties and things that lower or negate a target's Defense apply? I.e. Ambushing, the opponent All-Out-Attacking, etc.
    - How Hot is the fire created by Combustion?
    - Does Efficiency stack with Good Time Management to complete things in 1/4 the time?
    - Is Miles Away's duration effectively unlimited until discharged? If that's the case, is it an effective "was I just targeted by an applicable supernatural power" detector?
    - Can you use Momentum on Reflexive actions?
    - Does Strike First negate Surprise / Ambush? I.e. can the character apply their Defense against attacks?
    - If Right Tool, Right Job is applied to a weapon (improvised or otherwise) do successes add to the weapon's damage rating or to dicerolls made to use the weapon?
    - Does Tag and Release function across realms of existence? I.e. can a Demon tag something in the mundane world, walk in the Hedge, and then use this embed to find her way back?
    - I'm having a hard time grokking Alibi. From what I gather, the demon essentially creates a sort of duplicate (more psuedo co-location?) of one of their covers (possibly their own cover) and the demon can't be recognized for who they are / that cover while doing whatever they are actually doing. Right? How does this work with recorded media, or if someone was asked to describe who actually did whatever it is the character does while under Alibi?
    - Can Diversion be used mid-conversation or mid-combat?
    - Does Without a Trace work against various Psychometry or Postcognition-like abilities? Presumably with a Clash of Wills?
    - An aside, how has Just Bruised and Knockout Punch been playing out in people's games? They're on my radar as potential problems but...

    Exploits
    - For Exploits that are Extended Actions, does Efficiency and/or Good Time Management apply?
    - Is Breaking an object for Break to Heal part of using the Exploit, or does it have to be done separately? If part of the Exploit, is there a limit to what object(s) can be broken?
    - When Demon House ends, where does the Demon appear? Where they first used it? Anywhere inside the building?
    - If Echoing Death is under the effects of Efficiency and/or Good Time Management (if this is possible at all) how does that work? Take an instant action, make 2 or 4 rolls as appropriate?
    - With Raise Dead, is there any way for a Demon to actually place a spare soul into the corpse? Can he take one of his Covers gained from a Soul Pact and bring them back to "life" like this?
    - If the AoE version of Swarm is used, how big is the area? 2^(Primum-1)?

    Demonic Form
    - Some demonic form traits are described in a way that makes them easily concealable (say, with a pair of sunglasses or a hat). Is there any reason that a Demon can't walk around with a few Demonic Form traits out indefinitely?
    - Rivet Arm has unlimited ammunition. Does that mean if you score an exceptional success on Merciless Gunman you kill an unlimited number of targets?
    - Am I reading Glory and Terror right? You can reflexively impose Insensate vs several targets with no resist or contested roll?
    - How big of a swarm does a demon make when discorporeated with Insect Swarm?
    - Does being an Insect Swarm limit the demon's actions in any way?
    - Does being inside the area affected by the Swarm Tilt caused by an Insect Swarm count as "touching" for Embeds, Exploits, and Demonic Form powers that require it?
    - How does forcing someone into a Memory Theft attempt work? Grapple and it's automatically applied, or do you have to do it as a Move?

      I don't have time to answer all the questions, so I'll start with the general ones. The main balancing factor in a Demon game is the God Machine. If not for the God Machine, they could walk around in Demonic Form the whole day and use exploits like there is no tomorrow. Some ST arbitration is needed as the ST has to decide whether the God Machine notices certain types of behavior. You will have to decide in advance how strict you are going to be. Don't forget Demons are spies in hostile territory.

      This is also the main problem in crossover. If your crossover game is not centered on the God Machine, the main balancing factor falls away (unless you decide there is another enitity the Demons are hiding from and that will send hunters when it finds them). Another problem is that Demons sometimes loose or shed their covers, making it hard for their other allies to find them again. Also, when his Vampire and Werewolf friends are under God Machine scrutiny, they cannot change covers, so it becomes very hard for the Demon to associate with them. It is possible to run, but you will have to think about those complications beforehand, and of ways to get the group back together when it happens. Maybe it is possible to hack Infrastructure to make the God Machine forget about the Demon's allies for example.

      But still, it would be fun to run a mixed game, with a Demon who pretends to be another splat.


      Unrelated I want to note that I find the idea of a Demon with Rivet Arm and Merciless Gunman killing an unlimited number of targets intriguing. As normally the available number of targets is limited, I don't think it is a problem under normal circumstances. Especially because the same is possible when wielding a weapon with a clip size in the hundreds, like a belt fed machine gun.

          Arsenal:
          Arsenal says the weapons are mundane but effective, so I would say you have pull weapons that normally or at least reasonably exist.Explosives would of course be limited by the damage scope under the merit, but I would say again a plausible explosive would work. Specific things might require ST approval and no promises you won't get backlash in some form from things you pull though. So you'd have to ask the ST on if its ok to pull a silver knife out of your arsenal or something.
          Well things in a cover linked bolthole aren't specifically obliterated, just they cease to be there and demons have yet to figure out where they go. Otherwise yeah anything you leave in there that wasn't part of the initial setup goes bye-bye. As for pushing it beyond 5 the core doesn't list any specific means to do so. Which means you would have to get permission from your ST, and things that result from it are also at the ST's ruling.

          Cult:
          4-5 are considerably high ranked, so the vagaries are meant to make it less limited since this is meant to be a considerable high end power that was hard won. Basically they function as staff, resources, or allies once a story. So as far as dice pool I would say its basically you put out the call and get someone with the right skills that the requested job would require for that level. Stigmatics and supernatural merits are gonna need to be discussed with your ST you have them, but you'll probably need to work out the specifics beyond things like having an information trade with the vampire spying on your cult.
          You might be able to get some of the other merits you list out of cult members, but I would personally not allow a direct link where having a 5 dot cult counts as always having a 5 dot staff or something.

          Oh and on the rivet arm, no obviously you can't kill an unlimited number of targets. Eventually you run out of people to kill and then you need to move on to the next massacre site. Then you make it a movie.

              Embeds:
              The fire on combustion probably isn't too hot. Like I would view it as the same heat for say setting wood on fire. Essentially while you can use it to burn things that wouldn't normally be prone to burning just because say I caused a metal door to ignite doesn't mean the fire is hot enough to melt unaltered metal.
              Efficiency halves the time for the entire action whereas Good Time Management halves the time between rolls. You could get projects done fast with both (assuming the merit applies to the action), however you would still be counting on getting good enough rolls to pass the project.
              As a reflexive embed I would say it only works for the situation in which it was triggered. For example if someone nailed your arm to a table the embed would activate and ,hopefully, allow to deal with the pain until you can remove the nails and perform basic treatment. Since it has to be triggered by pain or serious distraction I would say you can't use it as an always on tripwire for supernatural effects.
              I would say it can in theory, but because reflexive actions are defined as happening on an instinctual level I would say it generally does not apply.
              Strike First does allow you to act in the same round as the ambush, so I would say if you succeed that it could counter an ambush. However, I will say that you would have to be ruled as at least getting the reflexive action on it. As a result someone a person turning out from around the corner and shoots you then you can use the embed to try and protect yourself. It would not apply if your enemy were to take a sniper shot at your head that you didn't know was coming then it wouldn't apply.
              Personally I would say Right Tools, Right Job is not an embed for making weapons. The idea being that the intention I get from the embed is it allows one to use a tool in a way different than how the tool normally would work.
              Alibi only covers you during the scene its activated in. It doesn't make it so you can't be recognized it basically makes it so actions contrary to that cover don't apply to said cover. For example, the cover you establish the alibi for is a known pacifist and the action you take after the alibi is established is shooting a person in the face. Obviously this would be a compromise check, but with Alibi up this is handled as someone else being mistaken for the cover because you were clearly having a pleasant chat with some people at the library. Now if at a later scene someone were to investigate footage of this and basically get a positive ID on your cover then compromise can apply as normal. So it gives you some wiggle room to do something abnormal, but not enough to go full out crazy with it.
              I don't see too much of an issue with using Diversion during those situations.
              I would say supernatural abilities like Post-Cognition could theoretically bypass Without a Trace, however I would probably put the difficulty of the attempt to use that skill as matching the successes from the embed's use.
              I can't speak for Just Bruised, but I imagine if you're worried about Knockout Punch just make it clear to your players before hand that it doesn't work on more resilient targets aka named or story significant targets.

                  Exploits:
                  The merit Good Time Management I would say doesn't apply to exploits. See Good Time Management is a merit that requires two dots in either science or academics and with the wording on the merits description it sounds less like managing the primium and forces that are occasionally used in exploits and more of your just good at balancing a schedule and all the things normally needed to complete a project. Basically the merit seems like it would only apply to mundane things. Efficiency is fair game though if it is ruled that the exploit actually requires time to accumulate those successes. Like if it were a ritual or something.

                  Break to Heal says you need to touch the target and break the object at the same time so it is part of the exploit. I would say you can't break more than one object with it, but if you had a means to say demolish a building with your other hand when you activated the exploit I would say it could work. So I mean it kind of falls to within reason, and I would say the bigger the destroyed object the bigger the metaphorical ping is and so the potential for a cover break becomes larger.

                  I would say appearing anywhere inside the structure is fair game for when you break the merger on Demon House.

                  Echoing Death doesn't seem like it would be effected since I just don't feel its actually doing any kind of action which can be enhanced. If your ST was willing to say it could work then I might then I would say it would be Efficiency and you would get to make two rolls a turn instead of one, buts that assumes your ST is willing to let you use them at all.

                  The description on Raise Dead explicitly says another soul will do if the old isn't available, as well as the fact that the dramatic failure is putting something else into the corpse. You can't technically bring back someone who lost their body due to a soul pact being triggered. This is because the soul is annihilated when the soul pact is called in, so there is no soul to put in anywhere else. Now if your ST wanted to say that the soul was still there or say if the contract was destroyed the soul would exist then its a different story.

                  I would say talk to you ST on how big a swarm of whatever you summon is I'm not seeing any rules for it. If you wanted to you could look up how big a swarm of that creature would normally fill in the real world and use that as your base. I'm just not seeing any rules on figuring it myself.

                          I think this is a case where the actual source of the tilt (Glory and Terror) overrides the Insensate Tilt itself.                                                   Originally posted by MCN View Post

                                                  I don't see anything inconsistent with "Holy Ecstasy" being used in place of "Holy Terror", as both would be Intimidation rolls (this benefits from the +3 Intimidation bonus). Both are present in the Insensate Tilt. Both would be Hard Leverage. Just as you would use supernaturally invoked fear to push a mortal to do as you want, you would also use supernaturally invoked ecstasy to get them to do what you want. It is consistent with all rolls within the power and with the Insensate tilt itself and with angelic themes. Intense pleasure can be sensually overwhelming so as to be considered intimidating. If you decided that ecstatic sensations couldn't be used to intimidate, which I disagree with but that's your call, then yes you'd have to make another power or expand this one to include another set of bonus/rolls to include the possibility of that. However since it's called Glory and Terror I believe that implies both fear and pleasure can be used to intimidate an unprepared human being.

                                                  It's also worth noting that, for example, when Moses descends from Mt. Sinai his fear and awe were painted on his face. He had beheld the glory of God and had that affect him with both awe and fear. In the context of the power carrying this theme I think the power is invoking both intense awe and intense horror at the same time and that it's really up to the storyteller what, if not both, would be the trappings of the Insensate tilt. However, at the end of the day, "pleasure" and "horror" are not mechanical terms they are narrative trappings.
