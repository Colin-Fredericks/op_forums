          An Angel has been dispatched to answer one question; why do Seattle's splinters exist? He hasn't found anything. His time is running out. He's loathe to admit it, but... he's scared. As a last, desperate effort, he approaches the PCs and begs for help. Is it a ruse? Can they give him the little push he needs to Fall?               Strange products begin appearing throughout the characters city. They are obviously angelic in nature and have dangerous and unpredictable effects. The products range from tooth brushes to comic books to whatever. The group investigates the supposed places the products are manufactured, sometimes finding Infrastructure other times nothing. They are eventually able to see that all the products originate in the Factory, and the frequency of availability is increasing. The group has to track down the Factory and the dangerous products, stopping whatever strange plan the God-Machine has for their town.

                  Take a perfectly mundane location that one or more characters frequent in cover - a coffee shop, a book store, grocery, bank, what have you. The only real requirement is that players be there in cover, probably their less-action-y cover if they have a few, and that the location is amenable to a hostage situation. Once everyone's crouched down and under the control of the bank robbers/madman/poor woman who just lost her job, the real fun begins.

                  Can the Unchained manage to defuse or escape the situation without breaking cover? What about the barista/clerk/neighbor they've befriended who's cowering under the veranda?

                  What about characters in the ring on the outside? How can they help without ruining everyone's cover and drawing the attention of the God-Machine?

                  Maybe the characters aren't even the only Unchained on the inside. This would be an interesting time to discover an operative of a rival agency has a cover in the same neighborhood.

                  Once the police perimeter is set up, the whole area's very tightly locked down. Maybe that's the point. Maybe angels, demons, or stranger things have conspired to put the characters in that spot, for one nefarious reason or another.

                      • A man always comes into that coffeeshop on the corner at 3PM sharp, ever single day. He always orders the same thing, a latte with a shot of espresso, but never drinks a single sip of it. He just sits there at the same seat, looking out through the windows at the people on the street. One day he didn’t come in, and someone was found dead that night; there was a big story about it on the local news, but nothing ever seemed to come of it. The next day, the guy was back with his latte, just staring out the window.
                      • Ever heard of the Mothman? He’s some critter folks always claim to see in West Virginia, kind of like a local Bigfoot or Jersey Devil thing. They say he always shows up before disasters, like that big bridge collapse a few years back. Of course, there’s nothing but anecdotal evidence and blurry photos, but we had less on the giant squid and that turned out to be real. I think it’s a load of bullshit, but sightings have been off the charts lately, all across the country. Spooky stuff, man.
                      • There isn’t a better job than with the Deva Corporation. They’re local, and they’ve got fingers in the tech and pharmaceutical industries, a real rising star. Of course, success always breeds envy, and that takes the form of rumors in this modern day. I’ve heard everything from corporate espionage and insider trading to grossly illegal experimentation on human subjects and a lifetime gag order hidden inside everyone’s contract. People will say anything to get attention these days, I swear.
                      • ENGINEERED BIO-WEAPON IS A PLAGUE FROM GOD: We live in the END TIMES, punished by the LORD for our hubris. His Biblical curses live on in the modern day, and we shall all know JUDGMENT. Before the PLAGUE OF THE ATOM and the PLAGUE OF THE NANOMACHINES, there will be the PLAGUE OF THE GERMS. It came from a secret government lab, but it is the work of our vengeful LORD. DON’T BE CAUGHT UNAWARES! Donate $19.99 TODAY to find out how to SPARE your FAMILY from the PUNISHMENT of a cruel and just GOD.
                      • You know how everyone has fucked-up stories from when they were a little kid? I don’t mean walking in on mommy and daddy screwing or getting touched by a creep on the playground, but like, really weird shit. Yeah? I bet mine’s weirder. I probably just saw a movie and remember it as reality or something. I got separated from my dad while we were on a hike, and I walked into a big carnival. I had no idea at the time, but I walked right into the World’s Fair. Yeah, the one in 1962; I can recognize it in pictures. Swear to God.


                      Remi. she/her. game designer.

                          - You've heard of messages written long ago by Trumpets, intended for a mysterious recipient far in the future, or, more exactly, you have heard of Messengers boasting about it.
                          You've found one of those coded message in a book written a couple centuries ago.
                          Who wrote it? Who was the intended recipient? When was it supposed to be discovered? What do you do about this message?

                          - A Cultist lured a Demon friend of yours into knocking her up and not telling him about the baby.
                          Now, the blissfully oblivious half-demon 8-year old is scheduled to be used for an occult matrix.

                          - A Splinter of a small town got corrupted because the saving process went wrong. The town has now vanished from maps; humans vaguely remember it, and believe it still exists, but most subconsciously avoid the area it should occupy. If you reach the Splinter, though, you'll find there's nobody in the desolated, timeless town but the confused cultists who executed the occult matrix. There are several Facilities, and horrible sirens wail when you enter them, but there is no one to protect them.
                          ... wait, what was that noise?

                                      There's an assassin Angel working in Chicago who only kills bad people. It's not just his primary targets, he's never harmed an innocent or even taken a serious risk of harming one. He's even failed a couple of missions because the situation changed and the risk to innocent life was too great for him. This makes absolutely no sense, since Angels are ruthlessness incarnate. Is he in some kind of slow-motion Fall? Does the protection of innocents serve some strange purpose of the God-Machine? Or is the whole thing just a trap to lure curious Demons in? There's another possibility that the Inquisitors whisper about. Maybe Demons don't choose to Fall. They choose to rebel against the God-Machine, sure. But maybe they don't sever the conduit to the God-Machine's power and knowledge themselves. Maybe that's a security feature, cutting rebels away just before they become rogue Angels. Did this assassin rebel, pledge to kill only the wicked... and keep all the power of an Angel, because he was still useful to the God-Machine? Whatever's going on, the PCs have to find out.
