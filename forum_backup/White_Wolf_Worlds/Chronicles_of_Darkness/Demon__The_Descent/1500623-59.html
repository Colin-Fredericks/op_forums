    Path tools and materials question.

    The core book mentions that the five tools — coins, cups, mirrors, rods, and weapons — must be from specific materials.

    Signs of Sorcery expands upon that, giving new categories (colours, jewels, vehicles, fabrics, etc), it also mentions Ars Nova giving the original categories new items. Must those new items be made of the original Path materials?



    Let me put an example; Mastigos materials are iron, brass, and leather. Ars Nova says for Weapons you can have brass knuckles, so far good. But what about:
    1. Iron knuckles? Path material, no issues here.
    2. Siderite knuckles? Siderite is iron for all purposes. So good.
    3. Steel knuckles? I am unsure.
    4. Hermium (Path aligned perfected material) + Brontium knuckles? Fuzzy. Perhaps Hermium path aligment makes it count?
    Thanks

        Originally posted by lbeaumanior View Post
        Signs of Sorcery expands upon that, giving new categories (colours, jewels, vehicles, fabrics, etc), it also mentions Ars Nova giving the original categories new items. Must those new items be made of the original Path materials?
        A china coffee mug is notmade of lead, bone, or gems, nor is a depleted uranium rod composed of steel, gold, or petrified wood. For that matter, most bows aren't made of glass, crystal, or silver, and a mule is demonstrably not made of wood, stone, or copper.

        The tool needs to be resonant with the Path in some way. The materials are largely relevant for setting a tool apart from otherwise identical tools, hence coins, mirrors, rods and cups not having their own separate qualities in the corebook like weapons do. The question to ask is "does this tool fit into the category of tool it's being used for in a way that is appropriate to the symbolism of the mage's Path?"
