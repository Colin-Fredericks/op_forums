          Everyone knows those little out-of-the-way places from their childhood away from the prying eyes of adults. The hollowed-out log in the forest just big enough to store a diary, the storm drain that goes under the roadway, the hallway between the basements of the high-rise buildings that people always thought was boarded-up. Demons who have Boltholes have created or found connections from this world to parts of the God-Machine where it lacks sensors, to pockets created by the strange angles of space-time, or places even stranger. Perhaps they are solidified Intent, formed by the vestiges of the Angel's Fall (if the player starts with it), or Cover Infrastructure unable to be fully "digested" back into the God-Machine upon the Angel's Fall. Perhaps they are a by-product of an Occult Matrix, unobserved, unintended, and unnoticed by the God-Machine. Just some thoughts.

          Once is happenstance. Twice is coincidence. Three times is enemy action. Four times is Infrastructure.
