She narrowed her eyes at me. “So, for the case. Our options are to shake White down, talk to Creel or explore the Hedge, correct?”

                            “Putting that aside, I think that shaking White down should wait til we know more about Creel.” Vivian tapped her laptop. “We need more information.” I nodded.

                            “I agree as well. I mostly bring it up as I had another thought as to how we might lead him astray.” Liz paused fucking dramatically. “We tell him we found Drake, and we’ll be having an extensive interview the next day. Then all we have to do is decide how to deal with the inevitable people he’ll send.”

                            “I like it. And unless he’s got an agent in the Hedge, or he actually kidnapped Drake himself and just sent us on a wild goose chase, it won’t be obvious or obviously supernatural.” Jack said. Another white car. A different dent.

                            Billy Mack had his nose deep in the goddamn internet when I got to the office. I ignored him in favor of a cup of science coffee but once that was done, I glanced over his shoulder and did a fucking double take.

                            “Who the fuck is the Absorbing Man?” I topped off my second cup with a little from my flask.

                            “The superhero identity of Crusher Creel.” Billy Mack said matter of fucking factly as he scrolled through the page.

                            I scanned the page myself. “What kind of fucking nerd builds a fake identity based on the goddamn Absorbing Man?”

                            Billy Mack switched to a tab with Crusher’s rap sheet and mug shot. “That kind?” He shrugged.

                            “Liz’ll like him.” I looked at Jack, who was flipping through his fucking phone. He held it up. “Says he once got turned into cocaine that gave you superpowers when you snorted it.”

                            I stared at him. “I did not need to spend memory storage capacity on that.” He shrugged. I shook my head. “Fucking nerds.”

                            “That used to getting yelled at by Cecilia already?” I smirked at her.

                            Jack crossed his arms. “If that’s weird, I guess neither of them is a roommate?”

                            Billy Mack was back at the computer. “That’s an odd coincidence.”

                            “What is?” Naomi kept poking at her fucking phone too.

                            “Zelia Fisher, the first year chemistry teacher who killed herself - she didn’t have keys for the building she was found in either. There are not many other details however, or any red flags in her digital footprint.” He turned back around.

                            Naomi’s phone chirped. “Deanna says she was tipsy, went by the apartment, the street light went out and then I woke her up.”

                            I turned to Billy Mack. “Think you could see how local that power outage was?”

                            “I think...” He left it at that for a moment as he stared off into fucking space. He brought up a few more web pages. “Aha. A number of social media accounts from campus are saying there was a power flicker at some point - clocks flashing, computers restarted. None of them noticed it go out though.” Naomi was about to say something when the g-man got a goddamn concerned look on his face. “Wait. None of those accounts posted anything between midnight and 1 AM. Nothing.”

                            “That’s sure as shit not right. What time did the teacher die?” I finished my fucking coffee.

                            He looked back at me. “Midnight.”

                            I sighed, then brightened. “Hey! In this world of Big Brother surveillance buzzword buzzword chemtrails the fuck ever... social media logs locations, right? Can you get a map of locations before and after the blackout, maybe let us trace some kinda of fucking pattern or at least a goddamn epicenter?”

                            Naomi snorted. “I’ve got a good guess of an epicenter.”

                            “Yeah, yeah. But it’s always good to get more than one fucking angle.”


                            Since we planned on walking Crusher out of big house on the pretext he was some deep under fucking cover FBI agent, we stopped by the Big and Tall on the way to the goddamn hoosegow. Jack had hammered out the contract that would do the heavy lifting. In exchange for him ‘giving’ us his conviction, we’d hook him up with a bulletproof alternate ID, a bolt hole with all the goddamn fixings, and more fucking cash that I’d seen in a hell of a long time. Considering that non-metaphysically, that was four ticks in the ‘Him’ column of the score sheet, he’d owe us, at least a little.

                            After a shortened round of paperwork, thanks to the g-man, and a minimal groping to check for goddamn birthday cakes, we got in to see Crusher. I’d popper my sensors before he got there, and gave Jack the signal as soon as he did. Fucker was the same sort of critter as Rosanna. Not shy either. “With no disrespect intended, who are you and what do you want?” He spoke slowly, with a deliberate seriousness. His voice was strong and deep and definitely matched the big bull of a man.

                            Jack took the lead, playing the fixer role. “That depends on you and our conversation. Have a seat.” Crusher say, casting a fucking weather eye around him. “I hear you’re in for a long stay.“ The big man smirked. “I understand the Lost don’t care for the concept of incarceration.”

                            That caught his attention, but didn’t kill the smirk. “Nope.”

                            “I wanna help you out with that, but I need a few things myself.” Jack followed Crusher’s glances around the goddamn room. We both noticed how often they fell on the door and occasionally the ceiling. And how he seemed to be calculating just how much fucking closer to the outside he was now.

                            He didn’t show any signs of bolting yet. “Fair enough.”

                            “We need to have a conversation with Rosanna and Jake.” Jack watched him carefully.

                            The big man’s attention snapped the fuck into focus, even more than when Jack mentioned the Lost. “What are your intentions towards her?”

                            Jack held up his hands. “To have a conversation, that’s all. I’m not offering nothing either - we can get rid of your conviction, make it like it never even happened.”

                            The suspicious look didn’t leave his face. “Last time I followed someone out of a bad situation, it got worse. If you know the Lost, you know there’s people who want to take us back - I gotta make sure.”

                            “I don’t have any intentions of doing anyone wrong.” Jack gave him a fucking understanding look.

                            “We got a deal then? I tell you how to talk with Rosanna and you agree not to hurt her or me? That seem fair?” Crusher was staring goddamn intently at Jack, focused on him completely.

                            Jack nodded. “Yeah. Yeah it does.” He glanced at us significantly. “I have a very similar proposal for you.” He laid out the deal we’d discussed.

                            The fish balked at the ‘vacation’. “I gotta be here. I’m Pledged to protect Rosanna.”

                            “Location’s not an issue. I can be anywhere, and the safe house can protect more than one.” Jack brought out the goddamn paperwork.

                            Crusher scowled at it. “Who do you answer to anyway?”

                            Jack’s emphatic “No one” seemed to satisfy the big man there, but he didn’t move to take the fucking pen or stop scowling.

                            “Look, I can read, but not real well. I want someone educated to look that over before I sign it.” He went to cross his arms, switching to resting them on the table when the handcuffs got in the way.

                            Jack nodded. “That’s fair. Do you have a lawyer or someone you trust with this kinda thing?”

                            “Rosanna won’t do me wrong. If she OKs it, I’ll sign.” He got a stubborn look on his face. “Sides, I got something I wanna do before I leave.”

                            Billy Mack looked at Jack and back to Crusher. “If you acquire any more charges before you are released, the contract we have here cannot nullify them.”

                            The big man shrugged. “They won’t be killed in any way a single human could manage. But if not maiming anyone is part of the contract, I can abide.”

                            Jack smiled. “Nah, it just can’t nullify anything if you get caught.”

                            Crusher nodded, and considered for a moment. “You know, I don’t intend any of this to insult your honor. I just know you from no one. I have been utterly betrayed once and it changed me to the very core of my being. I will be dead before I allow it to happen again. I just want someone more educated looking it over.” His fucking speech over, Crusher leaned back in his chair, as best he could while cuffed.

                            “Sure thing man. No insult taken.” Jack gathered up the paperwork and stashed it away.

                            Negotiations over, Crusher gave us a number for Rosanna, and the name of a club she frequents. Billy Mack decided to feel him out on the other details of the case. “Have you ever encountered a man named Michael White?”

He’d been trying to get Crusher to throw a goddamn match and got a knockout punch for his trouble. I went fishing instead. “He might have been involved with fight rigging?” I figured it was easier to jog his memory than go looking for more, if it even fucking existed.

It wasn’t hard to guess that Drake reminded him of a younger self. “If he’s at least got the good enough taste to not associate with fight-throwers, I guess I won’t track him down or anything. I’m very good at finding people.”

I looked back at Crusher, who had a pair of shiny bracelets, each with a short length of chain. “Oops. Your handcuffs broke.”

There was a mental note that nothing seemed illegal and I did have to agree with him that the eight dogs turning to look at you in fucking unison was goddamn creepy.

We finally settled on Billy Mack doing some possession hopping, to get as far in as possible, so the rest of us settled in to wait. After a few minutes, the door guard came back out. I did find that Carla Pierce had some work done right after she was hired by Sanderson. And Stein did as well, back when he worked on the force. Am had three treatments, Pierce one and Stein 4. Me and Bobby McGee was playing over the speakers and the usual crowd of riff raff was scattered about the place, including some fucker in a goddamn bunny suit. I’d seen him before, when Jack called me over to get an extra set of eyes on Vanessa. The bunny fucker stayed away then, and did now too, giving Jack a nervous look. I’ll have to get the fixer a goddamn thank you card.

Casual racism would have most people not batting an eye at the Asian woman speaking one of those Asian languages, and it’d be a hell of a fucking coincidence if anyone could actually eavesdrop.

Vivian had discovered that while she was moving the money. Some of it was now safely tucked away in an offshore account. “And it turns out that a chunk of that insurance money made its way into the bank account of a Team Tomorrow Contemporary Medicine security guard, who just tonight stole a hard drive before being viciously mauled by hive-minded guard bug dogs.”

I wouldn’t be surprised to find him associated with a company that is aware enough to have an idea that the Infrastructure in the Works is special. He or his wife - Gina is not just a pretty face.” She said it like a warning.

As an example... after Puriel first showed up at the dam, White investigated. We hit his laundromat shortly thereafter, as if in retaliation for his interest. The idea has been to get him to be suspicious of the other God Machine forces in the area and...” she smiled tightly, “I like pissing him off.” She took a sip. “Stalking Horse also made sure to have a cover be caught on camera, then we falsified the dam’s personnel records, showing that cover was hired and then ‘covered up’ - the records was sloppily deleted, and easily recovered.”

It is a standard tactic of his, to sweet talk people like TTCM and throw them at his enemies to see who they are.” Colleen leaned back, her hands crossed in front of her again.
