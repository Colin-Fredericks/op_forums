                    Originally posted by Charles Red View Post
                    I chatted with my storyteller over Skype last night, and pointed out the text about them feeling things like humans. He wrote back:

                    "It is pretty obvious the designers wrote themselves into a corner with how alien these machines are, and needed some kind of deus ex machina to make them even playable. Unfortunately, eldritch abominations are not going to get their official people cards from spending a few years with humans any more than a Xenomorph would.

                    I did not believe it when the Terminator said he 'now understood why people cry', and I do not believe these can either. I'm really sorry. I know you are really interested in giving this game a shot, but nobody else at our table wants to play robots."

                    Soooo that was a crash and burn.
                    You can try countering that Demons would have been *exceptionally* bad at their jobs prior to falling if they didn't understand humans very very well as Angels. Angels don't spend time *with* humans, they spend time *as* humans; the primary condition of an active Angel is as much in Cover as a Demon is. If they don't know how humans think and feel and react, then they're not going to be very good at being in Cover and since almost all GM activity tends to involve moving humans around in various ways, they're also not going to have a good sense of how to make people do what they need to do; they'd be very bad at fulfilling their duties if they didn't "understand why people cry" well before they fell. When they fall, that human understanding becomes primary since (almost) all the bits that give them their alien perspective are lost when they're disconnected from the GM.

                    They're not robots. There's no legitimate reading of the text that can get you there. Demons may be created beings, but the thing that allows them to exist in their fallen state is a connection, on a fundamental level, to some sense of humanity. Just look at the causes of a Fall in the first place: jealousy, resentment, curiosity, hope, fear, love, doubt, anger. It's human emotions that cause Demons to exist; it's intrinsic to their nature.

                    And when it comes to actual play, Demons without cover are not long for the world. And Demons without an honest connection to what it means to be human cannot have covers; they'd break them constantly just by existing. The game *does not function* if you try to treat Demons as alien-minded robots. It's not a supported play style at all.
