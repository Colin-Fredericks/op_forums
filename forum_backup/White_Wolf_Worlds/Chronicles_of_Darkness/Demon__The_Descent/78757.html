                  To use the God Machine = the Internet analogy I'm fond of... Angels (and demons with the right tricks) can transfer their "information" across the world wide Interface of the God Machine and go wherever they need to.

                  Subborned Infrastructure, by definition, is cut off from the God Machine, and thus you're not hooked up to the GM "Internet."

                  Anyways, given how difficult it is for the average person to even SEE any of the GM's gears or Infrastructure, or even stumble onto it so they become stigmatic, I would have to say the chances of some random person getting sucked up is pretty low. Not zero, of course, but very low. Still would make an awesome plot hook.

                      Sorry to join this late, but I seem to recall a City-Walker merit in the GM book.
