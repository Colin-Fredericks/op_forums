The one where you point outside into the unknown and say "the monsters are out there" (that fear of the other you were talking about), and the one where you look at yourself and say "the monsters are in here" (what I would consider personal horror). Now, I happen to also love the idea that at least some of the God-Machine's Angels used to be human, and think it has a lot of potential for personal horror, but for completely the opposite reason.
