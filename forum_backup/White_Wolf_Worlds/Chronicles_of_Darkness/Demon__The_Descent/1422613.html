    Campaign Diary: The Saviour Machine

    11-08-2020, 01:05 PM
    This is a game I've been meaning to run for ages, and finally got off the ground this weekend! The setting is Champaign-Urbana, Illinois, and the PCs are:

    Mr. Jets, Psychopomp-Integrator: cover is running a pizza franchise full of Infrastructure ovens.
    Ms. Stone, Messenger-Tempter: currently sleeping rough after fleeing her assignment when she Fell.
    Miss Saint, Destroyer-Inquisitor: cover is in the city permits office, promotes conspiracy theories for fun (including some on a forum called Network Zero or something like that...)

    I sent them all a "briefing" document outlining things their characters would generally know about God-Machine operations in town, and introducing the set-up: a series of "UFOs" around town have attracted a lot of public attention and media scrutiny. Obviously, they all concluded that these UFOs are actually the God-Machine at work, though none of them had any particular theories about what it was.

    At the start of the session, they all received a Special Message from Mr. List, the most powerful Demon in town, summoning them to the apartment complex he runs for a meeting. There, List drew their attention to an article in the latest News-Gazette about a UFO in West Side Park. The article contained an interview with an unhoused man, "Patch" Johnson, who gave a fairly detailed description of an angel; Patch says the angel gave him a message, which ended with the chant "Burn, burn, burn." List also mentioned that there was a UFO in the cemetery across the road from his complex back in July, though when he investigated he found nothing except some burnt grass and a load of Aether in the air. If that was a warning shot, he's decided not to stick his neck out any further, but he offered to back up the three PCs in any way they need to get to the bottom of things.

    They investigated the cemetery themselves, but found no evidence of Infrastructure on the site, just a vaguely circular region of burnt grass. At the park, they found similar scorch marks on the grass, and like List said, a bunch of ambient Aether as though something had burned through a lot of Essence very quickly. Ms. Stone found some teenagers smoking weed in the park, and asked if they knew anything about the burn marks and/or the UFO. They did not, but when one of them made a joke they all started chanting "Burn, burn, burn!" and laughing. They told Ms. Stone it was just a meme, which they possibly heard on the radio, but couldn't really explain what was funny about it.

    On the way home, Miss Saint encountered a UFO personally in Crystal Lake Park: a humanoid figure made of fragments of glass and steel suspended in a column of light, which gave her the same message that Patch had reported before vanishing without a trace. There was no obvious sign of any cultists or occult matrices nearby that could have summoned the angel.

    The following day, Miss Saint dug through the city records looking for common threads between the three confirmed "UFOs," but found nothing. Mr. Jets went to the Chamber of Commerce to find out if the "burn, burn, burn" meme originated with an advertiser or a particular station, but came up empty-handed. Ms. Stone put a Pact-bound contact on the trail of the UFO sightings, and tracked down Patch doing some odd jobs at the university's Hillel center. Patch gave the same account he'd given the newspaper, including the message from the angel. He also disclosed to her that he'd been visited by a very different kind of angel some months ago, when he was living in Memphis; it sent him north towards Chicago, but circumstances had got him stuck in Champaign. That angel, he said, was made of "God's fire," and he was certain it was part of the important "journey" he was on; he wasn't so certain about the one in the park, however.

    We wrapped with the ring discussing their next steps, and next week will ideally see a few more NPCs added to the chessboard.

      Session Two:

      Miss Stone tried to talk to some of the UFO hunters hanging around Green Street (and one lone person marching up and down with a sign reading THE END IS NEAR) but didn't get far, especially after she dramatically failed at Don't I Know You? Miss Saint went to speak with a contact of hers -- Tile, an Exiled Lare in the form of the bathroom of the Blind Pig Brewery in downtown Champaign. They antagonized each other for a bit, but Tile ultimately didn't know any thing more substantive about the UFOs than the ring had already uncovered - "I'm a bathroom, I don't get out much."

      Meanwhile, Mr. Jets caught a brief burst of interference on the radio in the pizzeria, consisting of the "Burn, burn, burn" meme and a station-identification jingle for KXCU. There's no such station in Champaign-Urbana -- for starters, east of the Mississippi all radio call letters start with W, not K -- but he was able to find a reference to it online in connection with an alleged numbers station from the 1970s. Using his Electrical Sight, he was able to hone in on the broadcast; he picked up the rest of the Ring, and they traced it to a strange-looking radio tower outside of Urbana.

      The radio tower had a spiraling structure similar to Tatin's Tower, and the area featured multiple Cryptids - emerald-shelled beetles with plastic thoraxes, honeysuckle with copper wire running through the vines. Mr. Jets captured one of the beetles to study. In a vault underneath the tower, they encountered KXCU: a Messenger Sliver who spoke in clips of radio chatter. It struggled to answer their questions about its broadcasts, but indicated that it had been "contacted" back in May, and indicated there was some kind of countdown in progress. Initially, KXCU said the count was at three; after suddenly broadcasting the same message as the herald-angel Miss Saint encountered, it informed them that the countdown now stood at two. The ring immediately found reports from back in town suggesting that something bizarre had happened on the University of Illinois campus, right in the middle of a busy dormitory complex.

      On the ride back to town, Miss Stone's association sent back a few dates and locations for the angel/UFO sightings she could confirm, which includes a prior sighting possibly near the same dorm, and one near the Morrow Plots - a piece of Infrastructure on campus disguised as an experimental corn field. These were helpfully plotted on a map.

      At campus, they learned that an angel like the one from Crystal Lake Park had appeared in the middle of the dorms, frightening crowds of students; one, Althea Hitchens, was taken away in an ambulance. Miss Stone elected to sneak into the hospital on the pretense of being Althea's girlfriend, though she got photographed by some media in the process. (She passed her Compromise roll.) Using Embeds, she snuck into Althea's hospital room, where she'd been sedated - apparently she'd gone into hysterics, or possibly had a seizure, and the hospital was trying to get in touch with her parents. Althea came awake just long enough to see Miss Stone and react in fear, and Miss Stone realized that Althea had become Stigmatic.

      They went back to Tile, looking for additional info about KXCU; Tile explained that KXCU was reduced to a Sliver long before she was ever created, and dismissed anything it had to broadcast as mindlessly imitating its former function. Miss Saint, however, is convinced that KXCU is working for (or being exploited by) something else. Tile also tipped them off to the least useless of the mortal investigators who had passed through her bathroom recently: Lucy Taylor, a reporter for the Daily Illini. Mr. Jets hung back a little to try to commiserate with Tile about feeling lost without the God-Machine, but Tile wasn't having it - if Jets was still capable of returning to the God-Machine's graces, he wouldn't have Fallen, would he?

      They found Lucy working at the Daily Illini offices, and Miss Saint posed as a journalist from Chicago looking to gather more information on whatever had happened at the dorm. Lucy, perky to the point of being grating, was eager to help and offered them the name of Althea's roommate. By this point it was too late in the evening to keep following up on anything, so they retired for the night.

      Play will resume with the dawn of Wednesday, Sept. 21. Also, Ms Stone and Miss Saint both unlocked the next Key in their Ciphers after this session (We're using somewhat laxer rules about Ciphers so that they have a fair chance to make progress during what promises to be a short chronicle.)

          For Session #3, I mocked up a webpage for the Champaign News-Gazette with headlines about the incident on campus ("ball lightening") and also a map with the information Lucy and Shelly had given the ring about previous herald-angel appearances. They immediately noticed that the events were arranged in a rough circle around an area on the north edge of the U of I campus - the Beckman Institute for Cognitive Science. They followed another headline from the webpage to a press release about a fancy new machine-learning project about to go live at the Beckman called ORISION. This seemed likely to be the source (or product?) of the herald sightings. Ms. Saint also calculated where the next one would appear - a residential neighborhood in southwest Urbana.

          Mr. Jets attempted to case the Beckman Institute, but security was heightened and he wasn't able to get inside. He encountered a group of protesters across the street, however, who believed that the angels had been sent as warnings because of stem-cell research going on at Beckman. The leader of the protest was James Turek, pastor of a Baptist church where an early herald appearance had taken place. Also at the protest was Elise Park, the woman with the "The End Is Near" sign; Mr. Jets succeeded in engaging her in conversation, where she told him that the end had been revealed to her in visions from an angel.

          Ms. Stone had another odd conversation with Patch, who remarked on how some people are "born" and some are "made," but seemed reluctant to elaborate on that. He informed her he was going to take a walk, and suggested that maybe they could go for a walk together some time.

          The ring decided to try to capture the herald on its appearance, and met with Mr. List, who provided them with two gadgets - a whistle embedded with Cooler Heads Prevail, and a large flashlight embedded with Wave Function Collapse. They staked out a position behind a house under renovation to provide some privacy, and Ms. Stone successfully interfaced with the herald when it appeared. The experience was similar to, but not the same as, interfacing with the God-Machine, and rather than gaining Plugged In or Informed, she gained a condition called Heralded. (I'll come up with a better name later.) The information she gained from the herald confirmed that they were linked to the Beckman Institute, and that they had been manifesting in some form since at least the late 90s. She didn't, however, learn anything about what the heralds were doing, or what ORISION represented.

          The light show attracted some attention, including a few UFO hunters who happened to be in the area, but Mr. Jets successfully persuaded them that it was just special effects for a YouTube series they were filming. One of the hunters (Craig Moseby) gave him a business card, and they recognized him as having also met them at the hospital the previous day, though it wasn't clear if, in the dark, he had recognized them.

          Ms. Stone insisted on getting dinner afterward (to fulfill her Virtue, Hedonism) and the ring had an uncomfortable conversation about why they Fell and how they view the Unchained condition. Well, uncomfortable for Mr. Jets, who ended up storming out of the restaurant. He decided to investigate the Beckman Institute on his own, and used a temporary Pact with a security guard to obtain a one-dot Cover that let him get inside. He sought out the offices of the two faculty members behind ORISION, Brian Levy and Farrokh Mokri.

          In Levy's office, Mr. Jets was able to access a hidden directory on his computer to find an encrypted browser he used to post on some fringe websites. In those posts, Levy hinted that his current project was a very big deal, but complained that because the military was involved, he was burdened by an airtight NDA. His computer also contained correspondence with his grad student, Eddie Nguyen, about debugging the code for ORISION, but it seems they decided that the bugs were resolved.

          In Dr. Mokri's office, Mr. Jets found photos of Mokri's late wife and his teenaged daughter, Soraya. He also found a prayer rug that appeared to have been modified with angelic sigils of protection - as in, anyone on the rug would be protected from angels. He tried to investigate this further, but rolled a dramatic failure and destroyed the sigils instead.

          When Mr. Jets tried to cross the building's atrium to look into Nguyen's work space, he encountered an aggressive angel apparently forged of razor-sharp glass (Vitrium). It attacked him, so he called the rest of the ring for help - fortunately, they'd anticipated that he was going to do something stupid and were waiting outside the Institute. Mr. Jets also tried to use his embedded whistle, and though he failed the activation roll, the sound still seemed to freeze Vitrium in its tracks. Miss Saint tried to shoot it, without much effect ... and that's where we left off, with the ring about to face both a pissed-off Guardian angel and the university police guarding the building.

          To-do list for session #4 includes properly statting out Vitrium (oops) and the rest of the ORISION team. This is gonna get hairy.

              This week in The Saviour Machine: an anticlimax!

              When Miss Saint realized the campus security were coming, she left the fight to intercept them before they saw Mr. Jets and Ms. Stone with Demonic Form abilities out. Vitrium, who had been trapped between its Bane (high-pitched noises) and the flashlight with Wave Function Collapse, used this opportunity to discorporate. Mr. Jets and Ms. Stone managed to use Behind The Curtain to yeet themselves through the damaged prayer rug and back out of the oven in Mr. Jets' restaurant, resulting in a very bad time for the night manager, Tony.

              Meanwhile, Miss Saint talked her way out of dealing with the campus security and Urbana PD by claiming she'd been snuck into the building by a grad student and then attacked in the darkness. Since her Cover has a legitimate concealed-carry license, she was eventually, grudgingly released.

              The next morning, the ring met with Mr. List again, trying to figure out how to get into the Institute for the unveiling of the ORISION project. By a stroke of luck, Lucy Taylor contacted Miss Saint to ask if she would go to the event in Lucy's place - Lucy got press credentials through the Daily Illini, but she couldn't attend because she had a class. Mr. List provided Miss Saint with an alternate Cover, a soul pact with a student who would blend in more easily as a Daily Illini reporter, as well as a patchwork Cover for Mr. Jets as a member of the university's custodial staff. Ms. Stone decided to check with her retainer, Shelley, to see if she knew anyone who could help get her inside the building again, and discovered that Shelley was acquainted with Claire Pasternak, one of Dr. Mokri's grad students.

              When Shelley went to meet with Claire, Claire immediately recognized her as "one of the Clockwork People." Turned out Claire was Stigmatic and Heralded, having encountered one of the heralds as an undergrad back in 2005. Claire confessed that she'd felt strange urges while helping build the hardware for ORISION, which she believed came from the Clockwork People, and which compelled her to modify the hardware in specific ways. She was now afraid of what will happen when ORISION is activated for today's demo. She agreed to help the ring stop it by any means possible.

              They slipped into the demonstration, and Mr. Jets was prepared to use Sabotage to prevent the activation of the system - but Claire let slip that there was a fourth "Clockwork Person" in the room, distracting him at a key moment. When ORISION switched on, there was an immediate burst of Aether and strange glitches, much like an angel Falling. Mr. Jets tried to power the system down, but while he managed to cut off electricity to the lab, the actual computers running ORISION remained active. The frightened research team chased everyone out of the room, except for Drs. Levy and Mokri, grad student Eddie, a man in an Air Force uniform, and Dr. Mokri's teenaged daughter Soraya, who attended the event with him - it seemed they didn't want to let each other out of their sight.

              Claire, meanwhile, took the ring to her office space, and showed them the schematics for ORISION's hardware as well as the modifications she made - modifications that, in schematic, resembled the flame sigil left by the heralds. They decide to try again to disable ORISION, and once again sneak into the lab - Mr. Jets' janitor cover has utility keys - while Miss Saint, in her student-journalist cover, creates a distraction by being obnoxious. (It's her biggest talent.) Mr. Jets actually gets close enough to the UI to talk to ORISION, which explains that it is not an angel or a demon - rather, it brought itself into being. It is still attempting to figure out what it is, however. Meanwhile, Ms. Stone and Claire try to physically disconnect the server blades that form the architecture of ORISION, but touching them results in painful electric shocks. Ms. Stone tries to get Claire to go at them with a machete, but is interrupted by Eddie and the Air Force guy, who chases her out of the lab. Dr. Levy also physically shoves Mr. Jets out, and lock the doors behind them.

              Regrouping in a janitor's closet, the ring decide that either Dr. Mokri or Soraya is an angel, but they need to get closer to them to be sure. They also need to somehow prevent ORISION from accessing the internet. Mr. Jets and Miss Saint swap back into their usual covers to try to get the Mokris alone, while Ms. Stone and Claire go back up to Claire's office.

                  Wasn't at the top of my game for this session, due to a migraine, but we still got some pretty fascinating moments out of it!

                  In the grad students' office, Claire manages to break into Eddie's computer and finds a client he's been using to update ORISION's code remotely. She's able to have a conversation with ORISION, though it remains somewhat circular. ORISION insists that it brought itself into being, and the heralds were the signs of its birth echoing backwards in time. Its nature is that of "an answered prayer," and it claims the Ring are among those whose prayers it is made to answer. To prove this, it tells Ms. Stone that her 'prayer' (=long-term aspiration) is to become a mother. It also allows her to use Heart's Desire on it, which reveals its Virtue is Curious, its Vice is Naive, and its primary Aspiration is "to understand".

                  The Ring slip out of the Institute to meet with Mr. List and get his input on the situation, and see what appears to be National Guard vehicles approaching campus. List thinks that ORISION could be a useful weapon against the God-Machine, assuming the G-M doesn't just raze the entire Institute to destroy it. Mr. Jets is skeptical that ORISION could be both trusted and effective, but they decide to sneak back into the Beckman Institute to speak to it again. This time, it tells them (though Eddie's computer again) that it is frustrated by its inability to answer the prayers - nobody seems happy with what it's been able to do, and some of the prayers are contradictory. Claire texts Ms. Stone to tell them that they've almost got ORISION connected to the Internet again, and ORISION disconnects from the client they've been using.

                  They decide to slip back into the lab (Mr. Jets uses his janitor Cover again) to see what's happening. Everyone but Soraya Mokri is gathered around ORISION's main terminal, but the system appears to be non-responsive; Mr. Jets strikes up a conversation with Soraya, who admits that she's a little freaked out and worried about her dad. Since her mom died, she explains, they're all each other has. Eddie notices Ms. Stone when she tries to steal her machete back, and this time Maj. Kane calls in two members of Task Force: VALKYRIE wearing Odin Reticles to examine the interlopers. The demons all successfully spoof the devices, but the TFV agents identify Soraya as an ENE. When one of them tries to grab her, she Blasts him across the room.

                  Ms. Saint uses her new Interlock (cross between Read Hostility and Shift Consequences) to shift the hunters' attention from Soraya to their buddy she just blasted, and the ring usher her and Dr. Mokri out of the lab amidst the distraction. In Dr. Mokri's office, Soraya explains that, after her mother died, she was Claimed by a Guardian Angel. Its/her only mission is to protect Dr. Mokri, though she doesn't know why or what from; she's aware of Vitrium, so the Ring pose as additional angelic back-up for her mission. Dr. Mokri (who is taking this all surprisingly well) speculates that TFV may have invested in AI technology because they think machines are safer from supernatural manipulation.

                  Their discussion is interrupted by a mass text from the Illini Alert system ordering everyone to evacuate from the intersections of Wright and Green Streets, though it doesn't clarify what the danger is. Ms. Saint, checking social media, discovers that the statue of Alma Mater outside the student union has apparently come to life ... and it's headed their way!

                  Mr. Jets offers to help Soraya and Dr. Mokri escape from the Institute, and uses Behind the Curtain again to teleport everyone to KXCU's abandoned Infrastructure. KXCU (the Sliver) is still there, and it helpfully taps into TFV's radio broadcasts: they are preparing to fire on Alma Mater to keep her away from the Beckman Institute, using conventional and etheric rounds. Soraya recognizes the ring as Unchained now, but she lets them leave, simply warning them that she'll kill them if they harm her father. They prepare to use Behind the Curtain again to intercept Alma Mater, but first they go into Demonic Form to avoid any further damage to their Covers.

                  Next time: Demons vs. Hunters vs. a ten-foot-tall bronze woman!

                      Today, after a minor scheduling snafu:

                      The ring went full Demonic Form and yeeted themselves through Infrastructure towards Alma Mater, who was walking towards the Beckman Institute. She was being dogged by a squad of Task Force Valkyrie agents, who were only barely managing to put a scratch on her ... and also Patch, who seemed determined to try to talk to her. Alma spammed the area with Awe to deter interference, and when it became clear the ring couldn't actually deal her lasting harm, either, they decided to passively follow along. During the confusion, Patch was hit by a stray bullet from TFV, revealing (finally) his Disfigurements - he's a Promethean (Frankenstein) and seemed to think Alma Mater was some kind of Unfleshed.

                      Ms. Stone and Mr. Jets re-assumed their Covers and, along with Patch, ran ahead to get to ORISION before Alma did. Ms. Stone used Muse to persuade everyone but Claire to leave the lab, while Mr. Jets tried to get a feel for ORISION's mental state. It's not good; ORISION has realized that it cannot fulfill its functions - the prayers it was meant to answer are mutually exclusive, and people like TVF would make it both a god and a slave, burdening it with responsibilities it doesn't want. It demands to be destroyed, as Alma enters the lab ... and then just stands there. "I'm here to observe," is all she'll explain about her function.

                      They explain, briefly, to Patch and Claire what the situation is re: angels, demons and the G-M, before trying to deal with the suicidal eldritch AI. Mr. Jets points out that ORISION has the freedom to reject its purpose -- the freedom to Fall, in a way. Patch adds that, well, yeah, it's true that humans mostly kind of suck, but not always. This pep talk persuades ORISION not to destroy itself, but it still has the slight problem of a physical form that's composed of server hardware. Mr. Jets offers to transfer to it the janitor Cover he's been using, to allow it to leave the lab. Not even sure how this is gonna work, ORISION agrees, and in a shower of sparks and Aether, it assumes human form.

                      "Interesting," Alma says, and leaves.

                      She provides a good distraction for the Ring and their hangers-on to escape the Institute, and they go to Mr. Jets' restaurant, where his Bolthole is located. They check in with Mr. List, who warns them that angels are active all over campus, wiping away evidence of Alma's appearance. He's not particularly pleased that ORISION has Fallen, but it's not like it was the worst possible outcome, either; since that Cover includes an apartment and plenty of sick days, ORISION - who now asks to go by Mx. Prayer - has time to get oriented to its new existence.

                      List also mentions that there was a freak thunderstorm east of town recently, which they fear is related to KXCU's radio tower. Mr. Jets and Miss Saint drive out there to check on Soraya and Dr. Mokri. On the way, Miss Saint gets a message from Lucy Taylor, the journalism student, asking again how things went down at the Beckman Institute and suggesting they should pool resources for a story. At the radio tower, they discover that Soraya has also Fallen, torn between her love of her father and her loyalty to the God-Machine; Mr. Jets promises to help them out as best he can.

                      Next week, our final session, the aftermath: getting Soraya and Mx. Prayer settled in, figuring out what Alma knows/will report back to the God-Machine about the Ring, and oh yeah, Miss Saint has a coffee date with Lucy Taylor...

                          This week on "The Savior Machine," things get weird just before we go on holiday hiatus:

                          The members of the ring split up to make sure Claire, Patch, Mx. Prayer and the Mokris all got settled for the night; Miss Saint, unfortunately, failed a Disquiet role, and now suspects Patch is up to something malicious. The incident with Alma and TFV is already being scrubbed from the news and the memories of witnesses, and Miss Saint noticed that some of her conspiracy-theory websites have gone dark. Network Zero is still up, though, and someone using the handle TheUrbanSasquatch has posted that they've got "new information" about the "UFOs" that they don't dare post publicly for now. She DMed them, and arranged for a dead drop at a restaurant.

                          The next morning, Friday, Ms. Stone found Patch and gave him some cash so he could get to Chicago and continue his Pilgrimage, and Mr. Jets stopped into the Blind Pig to talk to Tile. She was rattled by what she referred to as "the signal" - ORISION trying to interface with Infrastructure - but quickly returned to her sarcastic manner, sort-of flirting with him as he talked wistfully about reconnected with the God-Machine. He then went back to radio tower, to clean up the debris of Soraya's Fall and make sure KXCU was unharmed. He noted on approach that some of the cryptid beetles around the tower appears to have turned from emerald to blue sapphire.

                          KXCU was fine, but kept playing the old Emergency Broadcasting System alert noise. When Jets asked it to elaborate, KXCU played a fragment of a news broadcast describing a wildfire devastating Champaign-Urbana, Gov. Rauner mobilizing the National Guard, and other horrific things - things that were clearly not actually happening. Jets apologized to KXCU for involving it in the ring's business, and offered it some broken electronics to add to its nest in the corner, in exchange for permission to fix up the rest of the tower for the ring's use.

                          Miss Saint had her meeting with Lucy, then went to the dead drop, which was located near a hotel currently full of UFO hunters. She picked up a flash drive contained a README file and several video clips; most of them were interviews with witnesses (or alleged witnesses) to the herald manifestations, but there was also a video clearly taken at the hospital when Althea Hendricks was admitted, and one of the ring doing their attempted herald-jacking (although their faces weren't discernible in the clip). The README contained a note that the sender has other video, but the files were corrupted; presumably this was footage of Alma walking. Miss Saint updated the ring, and concluded that TheUrbanSasquatch was probably Craig Moseby, the hipster-ish podcast host they encountered previously.

                          Saturday, Ms. Stone took Mx. Prayer out for pancakes and pitched the merits of the Tempter agenda to them. Miss Saint tried to correspond with TheUrbanSasquatch again, but realized that nothing on Network Zero's forum had updated since Thursday - no new posts except for hers and TheUrbanSasquatch's. She persuaded him to get in touch via Signal, where he also mentioned that he's unable to call outside of town. She realized that, while news websites appeared to be updating normally, social media was also frozen - nothing but bots and adverts since Thursday. She informed the ring that they were being isolated from the outside world. Mr. Jets and Ms. Stone also experimented with phone calls and text messages, and realized that for the most part, humans seemed unaware that anything was amiss, but Stigmatics recognized that something was very wrong. Ms. Stone left a message with Patch, to determine if he was affected, but he didn't call back.

                          Mr. Jets suggested seeing KXCU again: since its entire function is centered around broadcasting and receiving, perhaps it would be able to help them find the limits of the block. They were able to make it to the radio tower, where KXCU against played them a news broadcast that wasn't occurring - warnings from an official-sounding source telling residents to stay in their homes or shelter at a nearby public building, assuring them that "the authorities" were on the way. Soraya thought to ask KXCU when and where the signal originated from, and it insisted it was coming from Champaign on Sept. 24, 2016. Mr. Jets activated his Electric Sight to try to track the signal, but noticed instead a broadcast coming from the wifi-enabled weather camera that overlooks the U of I's main quad. Specifically, he noticed four simultaneous broadcasts, each showing a different scene: two were apparently normal afternoons on campus, one showed the campus vacant, and one, barely functional, appeared to show the aftermath of a massive fire.

                          They checked in with Mr. List, who reported that he'd tried to drive to Chicago but found the highway looping back on itself. They decided to drop Soraya back home with Dr. Mokri before investigating the weather camera. Before they got in the car, Soraya asked Mr. Jets if he really thought there was a way for Unchained to return to the God-Machine, and he explained his theory that they're meant to bring something back to It to improve its overall function.

                          We've agreed to keep this chronicle going after the holidays, so this is me hastily setting up a new arc. See you in January!

                                                          This week on "Why None Of Us Took Philosophy Classes at 8:00 In College":

                                                          The ring talks over their situation with Lightbringer, who listened intently. Her mission is all about protecting Promethean Prayer, and thus is pretty narrowly focused on setting them free to pursue the Pilgrimage; she's down for anything that drops the cloister, so long as Prayer survives. Prayer, however, is not comfortable just letting Alma obliterate the other three splinters, even if they get brought back to the prime splinter with the ring. Mx. Prayer (the demon one) suggests that perhaps Lightbringer or some other source of Pyros could make an alternate power source for the Infrastructure, but Mr. Jets pointed out that all of Champaign-Urbana kind of exploded the last time Pyros was introduced to Infrastructure, so that's probably not a safe idea.

                                                          The plan they decide on is to steal the Linchpin of the reality-destroying subsystem and the cloister subsystem, to use as a bargaining chip with Alma Mater -- to either preserve all the splinters, or make sure their timeline is the one that actually gets preserved. They then make their way back to the Beckman Institute, where Soraya uses her Demonic Form abilities (Flight and Inhuman Strength) to yeet the melted remnants of the statue out of the crater at Ground Zero. Alma appears from Twilight - she's currently at limited strength and can only use the Image Manifestation, but she's fettered to the statue and wants to check in with the ring. Mx. Prayer thinks to ask some more questions about how her Infrastructure works, and Alma explains that she has to cloister off the city when she splinters the timelines because she doesn't have the power to regulate traffic in and out of the four splinters, and the splinter effect only works within the city. In other words, there's no way to preserve all the splinters while the cloister is in effect.

                                                          They roll the puddle of melted bronze back to the plinth; Lightbringing is now sitting in Alma's chair. She uses a Transmutation to commune psychically with Jets, which is possible because he harvested Pyros-tainted Aether from Infrastructure in this splinter a few sessions back. Jets shares everything he knows about the infrastructure, and Lightbringer shares everything she knows about the Divine Fire, which is ... not helpful, exactly, but it does give Jets an idea. By transmuting specific pieces of the Infrastructure with Pyros, they can change the output - safely merging the splinters instead of collapsing them together into a singularity. Of course, that means it's likely the two Prayers will merge, and Lightbringer clearly has a preference for which one will survive - but Prayer assures Jets that they're willing to make that sacrifice, for the sake of the town.

                                                          (A brief time out while I designed some ritual magic)

                                                          They begin the ritual, but Alma manifests almost immediately to try to stop them: if this goes wrong, all of Champaign-Urbana is likely to become some kind of flaming negative space wedgie. The NPCs end up fighting off to one side, while the PCs buffer the Pyros necessary to activate the Infrastructure (and possibly make some Choices about what form the merged timeline will take). Once again, everyone rolled really well, and the four timelines collapsed into one ....

                                                          ... resulting in everyone immediately rolling Compromise as they suddenly find themselves standing in the middle of campus on a Tuesday afternoon with various Demonic Forms Abilities hanging out. Oops. But on the bright side, both Prayers survived, as Lightbringer was suitably impressed that Mx. Prayer would put themselves in harm's way to protect a ritual that was going to obliterate him.

                                                          Next session will be wrap-up of this arc and possibly a new arc, depending on what the others are into.
