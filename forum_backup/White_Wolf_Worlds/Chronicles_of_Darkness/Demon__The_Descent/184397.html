    On Patch-Job Covers

    06-29-2014, 11:38 PM
    How exactly do Patch-Job Covers form? Do the purchased aspects of identity just exist in quantum limbo until the Unchained decides to assemble them, or must they be built one fact at a time, layered one on top of the other? And how do foundational physical aspects like age and gender get added in? Is there a way to add in brand new aspects of Cover, as opposed to buying them with pacts (that is, using regular Experiences rather than Cover Experiences)?

      I will preface this with I don't own "Flowers of Hell" so if the question is answered there or something contradicts what I'm going to say that's why I don't know it.

      I imagine there are multiple ways to start a patch-job. The most likely first is just that you got a cover that was really weak( either because you screwed up subsuming the identity on the soul pact that badly or it wasn't that strong to start with if you stole it from an angel) in which case you're just attaching things to the frame to make it stronger and more accustomed to what you want.

      The other is just trading and contracts and a bunch of traits are attached to an identity that hasn't stabilized enough to be used. In this case you've just got the name you've chosen to attach to the cover and whatever trait you've assigned but until it's stable all you've got is those things. For example let's say you made an identity Ivan Dent, but all you've got is playing pitcher for the baseball team in high school and you were forced to take the contractee's alcoholism with it. At this point you'd have a cover name Ivan Dent who played pitcher in high school and is an alcoholic, but those are literally the only traits about the cover that exact, as a result it can't go into the world because it's nowhere near solid enough that existence wouldn't just snuff it it because ontological inertia said it didn't exist 30 seconds ago and it won't start now, but on the plus side it's not stable enough that it needs any maintenance either. So I would rule as the Storyteller that it's just like a word doc on a computer till it's finished enough to be printed so to speak.

      Did that help or make any sense at all?
