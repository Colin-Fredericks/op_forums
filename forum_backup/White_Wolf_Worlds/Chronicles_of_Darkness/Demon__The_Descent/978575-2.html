                Hmmm, on one hand I can totally see where you're coming from, but on the other hand the NWO are also literally the psychologists of the Conventions and I very much like them.

                I think I'll try and alternate between them, making it something like this:

                The so-called Changelings are men and women of the masses who have been abducted by a strange subspecies of EDE (See, Dossier: Fae), and xenografted with alien DNA. Their abilities are often strange and almost nonsensical, suggesting perhaps that they were not designed for the Earthen athmosphere. Likewise, Changelings often possess small degrees of psychic powers, usually manifesting as Propability Manipulation (See: Noetic Theory) or innate talents for hyperpsych or hyperstat. Changelings make excellent field assets due to their subtlety and the ease of convincing them of the ultimate good and necessity of the Union. Most Changelings have already been exposed to some degree of abuse, often traumatizing them, making many almost grateful for the lower levels of Conditioning. The nonstandard approach that a Changeling's xenografts gives them to situations does not lend itself to direct power or brutal strength, but instead makes them clever in their approaches.

                The most important aspects of Changeling metaphysiology is their ability to detect the use of Enlightened Science however, as well as their innate hyperpsych understanding, which allows them to force people into keeping their word. The first of these abilities, refers to a Changeling's ability to practice "kenning" as they refer to it, an innate sense that allows them to perceive both Enlightened Science as well as Reality Deviancy of almost any kind. As this ability is more pronounced with their mental stability, a strategy that involves offerings of trust, openness and friendship is suggested in order to win them over. The second ability refers to a Changeling's ability to bind people to their agreements on the exact words. This ability is of utmost importance, and therefore it is important that one does not stress the desire to have those that show exceptional skill in it join the Union, but instead slowly win them over to our cause in order to not risk losing them.

                Due to their abuse, most Changelings are willing to side with us, once we hint that we may help them with their issues in fitting into society again, this makes them excellent targets for recruitment attempts by us, and in reverse makes them harder for Reality Deviants to recruit since they cannot provide the same promises of social security and stability that we can provide. Thus, the fifth Precept of Damian, and the third Mission Protocol are both revoked when dealing with Changelings as it might still be possible to realign them with our current scientific knowledge.

                In general, a careful "carrots" strategy of approach is recommended with Changelings.

                As enemies of the Union they are incredibly open to psychological warfare and conventional attack methods. Changelings almost universally have a xenografted Dimensional Mat-Trans Emulator that allows them to escape from most forms of confinement with ease, meaning that the most effective ways of containing one would be sedatives and draining of their reserves as well as a constant tending to by Bobs, Lauras or other similar Constructs with low-functioning or muted emotional packages. All of this would have to be reinforced by a constant influx of hyperpsych aimed at reinforcing their trust in lawful authority.

                Proper strikes against an actual Changeling "Freehold" is best performed with a combination of psychological warfare and conventional attacks. An Operative strike team should sow paranoia and take out key leaders to contain with the method described above, while Iterators and Void Engineers would respectively deal with anyone attempting to escape by mundane means and paranormal means. Luckily for us, Changeling xenografts seem to afflict them with an allergy to ferrous compounds, making them incredibly easy targets for even a standard Union-issue railgun, with no need for heavier ordnance such as ERASR rifles or MAULR gear.
                -Operative Jane Bautista

                It's not exactly condescending, but it does go "hey look, Changelings have huge psychological weaknesses, let's exploit them to get them on our side."
