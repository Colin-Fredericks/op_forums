                                                Some ideas I had regarding Form Lambdas for Evolved Form:

                                                Black-hole Eruptor (Eliminator Cannon + Newton's Nightmares + Ripple): Instead of firing explosives, this weapon now launches a charge that temporarily creates a localized zone of intense gravity - a miniature singularity. Anything within double the normal blast radius is sucked towards the epicenter, taking damage as they are ripped apart by the gravational forces. The singularity dissipates at the end of the Turn following the one it detonated.

                                                Blade-Eater Hide (Armored Plates + Disintegrate + Break To Heal): Whenever an inanimate object is blocked by the armor (has any of its damage reduced), it crumbles to dust, healing its Structure in Lethal or Bashing damage on the wearer. Smaller objects like bullets usually can be treated as Structure 1.

                                                Song of Death (Voice of the Angel + Ultimatum + Ripple): This lets out a dreadful keening - those who hear it and cannot resist must listen to the wail or be turned to dust. Yes, being struck deaf by the initial blast counts as "no longer listening".

                                                    Some more Form Lambdas:

                                                    Revenant Protocol (Form Lambda, Resistors + Play Possum + Animate): The Demon enters a death-like state upon the next attack, seemingly slain by the assault... only to rise in undead fury. After activation, the Demon takes no actual damage but appears dead, yet animated by their own power. Only complete destruction of their body will kill the Demon, and at the end of the combat Scene, they are restored as if no damage taken during it occurred.

                                                    ​Eye of Sauron (Form Lambda, Clairvoyant Sight + The Look): You can look in on and paralyze any target you know from anywhere.
                                                    Balar's Gaze (above + Hellfire): Upon your choosing, any target you know can spontaneously burst into flames and ash.

                                                    Digital Summoner (Form Lambda, Data Form + Summon): As long as the target is near any kind of signal, the Demon can pull them through the Internet to their location instantly.
                                                    "By Bell and Datagram, I command thee! In the name of Turing and Marconi, I compel thee! Appear before me Greg J. Walscott of FedEx, and bring me what is mine!"
