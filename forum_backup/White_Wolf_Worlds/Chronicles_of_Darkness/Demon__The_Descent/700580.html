    How would a Demon acquire a true soul?

    10-14-2015, 05:46 AM
    Let's suppose a Demon finds out that his Primum cannot pass onto an afterlife like a true soul can. Maybe it was a Mage who told him this after looking for the Demon's Oneiros and finding that there are only Oneiroi associated with the Covers, and sub-par ones at that. What could the Demon do to alter its Primum into something that works like a true soul? Prometheans can turn their Azoth into a soul when they complete the Great Work, so perhaps the Divine Fire/Principle could do the same for Demons, and even other splats with abnormal souls (e.g. changelings and mummies). Archmages with enough dots in the right Arcana could cast an Excision spell to remove the Demon's template, at the risk of displeasing the God-Machine (if it is indeed a patron of Demons in the Pax Arcana). The Demon could try his luck at becoming an Integrator and convincing the God-Machine to modify him. Perhaps a completed Cipher could show the way, but I'm not sure if Ciphers can do that. Beyond these options, I don't know what the Demon could do. Any suggestions?

      For all intents and purposes, the Demon is a soul, but if you want to do something different, it'd have to be story-level. Prometheans have a long march towards this very end, and Mummies have to wrestle their souls from the hands of the Judges themselves (metaphorically, of course. Very few of the Judges actually have hands).

      I'd rule that, at the very least, the Demon in question would have to have very high, if not maxed, Primum and access to a suborned Recycling Facility with which to edit themselves a soul. This should also count as a massive break in cover - "Going Loud" massive.

      I'm imagining some huge, climactic scene: here are Hunter Angels all over you, there's Aether in the air, you've lost your Cover and the messing around you did with that reclamation Infrastructure is causing some really weird physics to happen around here. Can you get out with your newly-forged soul intact? Well, we're out of time, so I suppose we find out next session.


      Oh, and, while I'm not familiar enough with Mage to know what the Pax Arcana is, I can pretty much guarantee you that the God-Machine is not the Demon's "patron." Certainly of Angels, but Demons are explicitly disconnected from the God-Machine- it's what makes them Demons, and, while he works in mysterious ways, I'm quite sure the Machine would be quite happy to have some of those little pests running around without powers.

                                                  Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                                                  Let's suppose a Demon finds out that his Primum cannot pass onto an afterlife like a true soul can.
                                                  First of all, no one can confirm that. No mage, no Sin-Eater has that knowledge. We don't even know if the afterlife actually exists in the WoD. Mages believe that the "soul" moves on to the Supernal, dumps its experiences, then is reborn, effectively destroying what you were in the first life. This is effectively no different than falling into oblivion, and it should be noted that what the mages consider a "soul" may not be the same thing as a vampire, werewolf, sin-eater, etc would consider a soul. For a Promethean, a "soul" is actually learning to get along with humans, fitting into society and getting rid of the whole Disquiet-inducing uncanny-valley effect of their existance.

                                                  A soul, as per the Soul Loss rules, is nothing more than a spark of personality and willpower that can be removed and interchanged with other souls, serving more as a power core than something more fundamental. When you die (and give up being a ghost), there's no evidence something better comes after.

                                                  Beyond these options, I don't know what the Demon could do. Any suggestions?
                                                  That said, lets delve into what would happen if a demon did believe in some kind of happy afterlife, and wanted to go there. The answer is actually a lot simpler than you think. Its called "join the Interrogator Agenda." Reaching a personal state of enlightenment that you evolve into something more is exactly the kind of thing the Paranoids work at. There is a subset of Interrogator demons that do want to become fully human, get married, have kids, live off the GM radar, and so on.

                                                  How do they do it? Same way everyone else does. Gather information, make plans, break into facilities, develop the Cypher, achieve some form of techno-religious understanding with the universe through bonding with Primum and reaching "Hell."

                                                  TL;DR - its already part of the game.
