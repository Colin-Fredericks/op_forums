            Originally posted by orathaic View Post

            The Subtle Arcana correspond to the Pillars — particular interactions mean the Venn diagram between the two is not a perfect circle, but a character's Ren is part of their Fate Pattern, just as their Ka is part of their Prime Pattern and their Ba is part of their Mind Pattern. There's a reason Sheut-based Affinities and Utterance tiers have so much truck with ghosts, after all.

            There's more to the soul than the Pillars — Sekhem binds them together in addition to being the thing they're derived from, and direct comparisons to either model result in seeming inconsistencies when taken as gospel, like the Life Arcanum being derived from the Pillar of the Heart despite the Essence being the thing that differentiates a living body from a dead one — but the general principles hold fairly firm.
