    Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post

    Truefacs - they're basically Descent's version of Earthbound. The big, old, powerful guys who aren't on your side but aren't on the opposition's side either.
    I've been only somewhat interested in more info about Exiles, mostly just to know more of the setting, but now I can't wait to know more about them.

    What I would want form the ST guide is:
    - More on the Exiles, especially now that they are hinted to be Earthbound equivalents.
    - More on Hell, what is it, how to achieve it etc,
    - Cipher, basically just something that would give me anything more than the fortune cookie sentence at the end. In the end players will end up deciphering their Ciphers for two things, Interlocks and to get closer to Hell. The first is self-explanatory, more power and personal unique powers at that, the second sounds reasonable but has no teeth currently since we have literally nothing on Hell and because I at least have found the so called end goal for the Cipher to be rather anti-climatic. Like said it currently seems to be a fortune cookie line that the ST hopes is something that is somehow revelationary for the character.
    - More on Angels
    - More on Infrastructure and how demons may manipulate it or even create it for themselves, then again I suspect this might be what makes the Exiles powerful especially if they are powerful in anything remotely Earthbound sense.

    Personally I don't feel much of a need to devote word count to historical games in the ST guide, especially since I at least see humanity's technological advancements largely irrelevant to Demons/G-M/Angels except that more advanced the technology humans use the easier it becomes for the G-M and it's agents to monitor and control them through it. No need for Angelic revelations anymore when you can make sure the information you want to distribute get's distributed through a few well placed words on the net.

            To expand on one of the items I mentioned, I find myself a little uncomfortable with the notion of choosing Keys and designing Interlocks for players, since it's an odd subversion of the role of the Storyteller. Generally the ST makes the NPCs (and sometimes their powers), sets up the scenes, and works with the players to build a story. The players design their characters and choose powers they think are thematic, fun, or optimally designed for their intended character concept. I feel almost like assigning powers interferes with allowing the players to design their characters.

            Alternate systems for ciphers that put more power in the hands of the players might be worth having since it decentralizes the role of the ST and is more conducive to pickup games. Sample lists of Interlocks could also speed up game prep or at least provide inspiration for building ciphers. For instance, I know that when I decide to design ciphers, I'll be looking at the 12 or so Interlocks provided in the Ready Made Character Pack.

            All that said, subverting typical expectations for player/ST roles is often a good thing, so regardless of what appears in any sourcebook, I'm going to dive right in and try to make the best ciphers I can for the games I plan to run in the near future.

                    1. Angels! Detailed explanation/guidance with plenty of *examples* of how to use the blue book ephemeral rules for Angels.
                    (i) *Guidance* for when the ST should keep track of an angel's Essence and various ephemeral conditions and when the ST should feel more free-form in handwaving interactions with Angels.
                    (ii) *Guidance* when to use Influences, when to use Manifestations and when to use Numina.
                    (iii) Confirmation or refutation of the fact that Embeds are Numina and, if confirmed, how to square Essence costs for Numina with the free nature of Embeds.
                    (iv) Generally, guidance on how to make interactions between Angels and Demons interesting, while keeping the improvisation elements of Angelic powers manageable
                    I am aware, from the other thread that turned nasty, that a lot of posters on these boards do not consider this to be worthy of attention. But for me, and at least some others, clarification of this would be of great value.

                    2. More guidance on compromise rolls and how often to make players roll them to create a fluid and organic gaming experience. In particular, thoughts on the Partial Transformation beat-generator effect.

                    3. Cipher. And more specifically more on the Final Truth. Also the possibility of starting a new Cipher once the first one is complete.

                    4. Facilities and their interaction with Primum. And generally more guidance on changing Primum.

                    5.Hell

                                                        Originally posted by Tau Neutrino

                                                        The problem is in the corebook, they're not that powerful. Like angels, they're Rank 1-5 spirits with a ban and a bane, no listed ability to heal aggravated damage or inflict it, limited number of Numina, and dependent on acquiring Essence without Infrastructure in the Exiles' case. They'd have to be seriously beefed up to be what the Earthbound were in D:tF, the default antagonists.
                                                        Angels aren't like Demons who need to worry about where their next hit of aether is coming from, sacrifices from cultists can replenish their essence, and seeing as how demons can have cults I see absolutely no reason to think that an exile can't have a cult or two as well.

                                                        Angels quickly gain higher stats than non ephemerals, exasperated by their ability to freely spend their essence to enhance their stats by rank plus 2.

                                                        If push comes to shove, a rank three angel can spend 15 essence to enhance their stats across the board by 5, allowing stats of 13,13, and 14. So no matter how you put the stats that's an attack roll of at least 26, defense of at least 13, speed of at least 29, and at least 18 health levels.

                                                        A fifth rank angel can have stats of 15 across the board, and with essence expenditure they can have 22 across the board, so 44 attack dice, defense 22, speed 33, and 27 health levels.

                                                        Or they can go the other way and use influences, which is basically reality control that can be as versatile as mage arcana, at rank three an angel might be sporting up to 10 levels of influence, 16 levels for rank 5 angels, they just have to be in theme for the angel. Let's see, for example, there is an angel of time in GMC. So time 5, maybe movement 4 and death 4,as related concepts, and maybe space 3 considering the whole space/time thing.

                                                        So in short, ephemerals are basically mages without paradox who often have superhuman stats to boot, if an ST can't figure out how to ruin a players day with that then they really aren't trying.

                                                            To add to the general requests for things in the ST Guide -

                                                            (Nothing new here really...)

                                                            1) Angels -

                                                            Explaining (or reexplainning) the mechanically elements of angels (e.g. exactly how the many, many rules that apply to them between the GMC (twilight form as default? loosing essence every hour outside infrastructure) core and Demon interact (can Angel's have Numina that are basically Embed effects? do/can they have "form abilities" like Demons? how do Angels interact with their infrastructure generated covers?) would be good for clarifying any misinterpretations that people might have at current.

                                                            Expanding out the range of Angelic Numina and lots of examples of angel antagonists.

                                                            Thematic examination of the different roles Angels can play in a Demon game. From "big bad" to "monster of the week". A look at possible non-antagonistic Angel's that Demons might choose to interact with for some reason.

                                                            2) Exiles -

                                                            A deeper explanation of the Exile's state and how it is unique and interesting in its own right.

                                                            How said state stands in relation to Demons and Angels. How they might relate to the God-Machine as both allies and enemies.

                                                            Expanding out Exile archetypes and examples. Perhaps exile unique merits or abilities?

                                                            3) Cryptids -

                                                            More of these monsters, fun examples, more traits and powers to use in constructing them.

                                                            More discussion on how they come about. More info on cryptoflora and how it might be helpful as well as a hindrance to a Demon.

                                                            Maybe some talk about Cryptids created from ghosts/spirits/extra dimensional beings? Previously mentioned as the possible basis for Rank 6+ Cryptids.

                                                            4) Crossover stuff -

                                                            Not a lot of stuff here, but some useful stuff for cross-gameline storyhooks. Some basic discussion on how the God-Machine might interact other "player" supernaturals and the other "big-bads" of the NWoD such as the Strix, Genty, Abyss and Exarchs.

                                                            General crossover rules for template vs template interact e.g. does demon blood give Vampires and odd effect if they drink it? Can Demons be blood-bonded? Are demons effected by Disquiet? etc.

                                                            5) Hell -

                                                            Is their a cannon version? Is their a myriad of different possible Hells (one for each Agenda)? Can multiple hells exist at once? Does each demon have the ability to have their own personal hell? A toolkit to create your own hells for different PC's and NPC's.

                                                            6) The Cypher -

                                                            General advice on handling this from an ST perspective. More example embeds. More examples of Cypher creation. A little more discussion on their narrative use. More explanation about the "gnostic koen" learned at the end of the Cypher and how that can be used as a springboard for more interesting PC development and stories rather then an "end".

                                                            7) Important NPC Demons -

                                                            Not the "signature characters" of the OWoD game lines, but the notable characters of the gameline e.g. Verney for Promethean, Count F***ing Dracula for Requiem. Satan has been mentioned already. Would love to have Lucifer be a different Demon (sometimes they work together). Ms Book from the various stories and examples in the books would also be awesome to see.

                                                            8) Meta-Stuff -

                                                            Some options for the "behind the curtain" questions. Such as why has the God-Machine not solved the "Angel's keep falling" problem? What's up with the Cypher? Discussion on the idea of God-Machine and demiurge? The Satan Signal? Options for a historical myth e.g. the War in Heaven and such.

                                                            Also it might just be me, but I would like another look at the Celebrants from the Core God-Machine book within the Demon framework? They seem to embody the opposite of the God-Machine, but they don't strike me as the sort of beings that most Unchained would get along with either. Good option maybe for another type of Antagonist? This last one might fall under the Hell and Crossover stuff as well I guess.

                                                            That's all for now. Until other ideas strike.
