    Splat stacking - minor to major template?

    09-27-2016, 07:07 AM
    I'm running a chronicle which has quite a few Major Splats already in play (Mage, Werewolves, Vampires, etc. etc.)

    One of the characters is Stigmatic. Like all Stigmatics, she has a supernatural merit from the encounter with infrastructure (Telekenisis), her 'brand' (a glyph tattoo along the side of her body), and, of course, the Unseen Sense: God Machine merit. I know that with other minor templates (such as Wolf-Blooded), those splats keep their templates in the event of transforming into a Major Splat (if a Wolf-Blooded becomes a Beast, for example, that Beast is a Beast with Wolf-Blooded tells). This Stigmatic character has invested in some other Supernatural Merits since becoming Stigmatic, but this is my main question:

    What happens if this Stigmatic is transformed in to another, Major template?
    What if she, say, decides to seek the Embrace from a vampire? Would she then become a Kindred with Supernatural Merits, a glyph tattoo, and the Unseen Sense merit? Would the things she received upon becoming Stigmatic disappear completely, leaving only the Supernatural Merits she's personally invested in?

    Any insight and direction would be greatly appreciated.

                                      'Sometimes within the course of a chronicle, a Wolf-Blooded character may change her core nature. She may experience the First Change, be ghouled or Embraced by a vampire, be drawn into the Hedge, or even Awaken as a Mage, should the story benefit from those changes. In any of these cases, the Storyteller may require the character lose any Wolf-Blooded Merit she has to reflect her disconnect from Father Wolf and Luna. In this case, consult the Sanctity of Merits rules on p. 105. Unless she goes through the First Change, a Wolf-Blooded character retains her Tell if she becomes another kind of supernatural creature.'

                                      Werewolf the Forsaken, p300. It does say 'may require,' and not that the character must lose all their Merits.

                                      As for minor template 'stacking,' I don't see why not. The specific rules for others may say otherwise, but there's nothing keeping you from playing a Wolf-Blooded Ghoul.
