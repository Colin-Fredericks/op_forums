    Actual Play: Oklahoma Demons, Session 1

    05-08-2020, 03:19 AM
    I'm not sure the best way to post this - my DtD chronicle is available online as is the session below. But I thought that I might post the text here as well.
    Characters Present:
    Solomon Tzengeti: Professor of Anthropology
    Lexis Staiffer: Young indie rocker
    Zachariah Ross: Small business lawyer
    Reginald Waters: Therapist
    Jennifer Reddings: Freelance museum assistant


    Backstory: Norman Oklahoma, houses a major GM Infrastructure, the National Weather Center. Previous attempts to infiltrate or sabotage it have been met with overwhelming devastation (e.g. the world record May 1999 tornado outburst). An Agency ("The Damned Few") decided that a small Ring of "younger" Demons would be assigned to monitor the Weather Center, but not interfere with it. However, unexpected developments will lead the players to a different mission....

    The Ring gather for their regular meeting at the Wafflehouse, a safe screened location. Solomon goes to the standard dead-drop, a newspaper vending machine. In response to his putting the appropriate coins in, the vending machine door opens to reveal a cell-phone and a large manila envelope. He retrieves them and joins the Ring back at the table.

    The phone rings - it is Mr. Gone, their appointed contact with The Damned Few. Something odd is going on Eastern Oklahoma. He explains the images in the envelope.
    • "Missing 411 Map:" Some of these disappearances are due to recognized groups: The Cult of 17 out West, the DreamEaters of the Northwest, and a variety of GM harvesting operations. However there is a cluster of unexplained activity in Eastern Oklahoma. Several have happened over the last few years, but there is now a spike in disappearances.
    • Local news reports tell of cattle mutilations that indicate something odd as well - possibly cryptids.
    • There have been recent disappearances near Talking Trees Camp Ground. Normally this woud be covered more widely. The lack of coverage indicates active suppression of news coverage.
    The major GM Facility is at the Weather Center, and it is well observed. However, it appears that there is some other major GM operation going on where none was expected. This alone is very disturbing.

    The PC's are being moved from their observing mission to investigate the source of the disappearances in Eastern Oklahoma. If it is cryptids, that means Aether which means GM activity. However, the GM also hunts cryptids, both to prevent discovery and as possible Demon hunters. So caution is advised.

    The PC's make moderate preparations; those with appropriate firearm skills pack theirs along. They all climb in to Zach's car and drive two and half hours east to the Talking Trees campground, which was now closed and up for sale.

    At the parking lot to the campground there are two separate groups already there, and preparing to head out. The first group is a pair of local hunters, Jeb and Boone, who have heard the stories of some "critter" haunting the woods and have come to investigate and, they hope, get a story worthy of telling over beer. The second group is assembled next to their Bigfoot Hunters truck. The group of four consists of the stars of the former TV show BigFoot Hunters, Bobby Brewster and his older brother Ray, the camera man Mario Santos, and the driver and handyman Lou Weber. The show Bigfoot Hunters ran for a year, and while it initially was quite popular, viewership fell as it became clear that they never found Bigfoot. After their series was cancelled, the Brewsters hope to start a new show with a wider focus on general "Unseen America". They had heard about something interesting in Eastern Oklahoma from their sources on the internet and decided to check it out.

    Lexis Staiffer quickly insinuated herself with the TV crew, acting the gushing fan. A few good social rolls later and she and Jennifer went along to fan out with the crew and look for signs of anything paranormal. Solomon and Zach paired off with Jeb and Boone, with Solomon attempting the Cause and Effect embed to drive the two groups together so that they could be more easily managed and/or protected. A dramatic failure gave the ST some dice to use when least convenient. Reginald Waters stayed behind in the parking lot to do more investigation, which turned out to be a lucky decision.

    Shortly after the two hunting groups had departed a third group showed up. They arrived in three pick-up trucks and gave off an air of severe purpose. Six of them looked the part of hard working farming types packing a distressing amount of firepower. Two of them were noticeably odd: large, bull-necked bodybuilders, clad only in blue jean pants. Shirtless, shoeless and wearing dark sunglasses, they leapt from the trucks with cat-like easy, carrying pieces of steel rebar, wielding them like jo sticks. The party of eight trotted off into the woods at a rapid clip. Waters texted the others about their arrival.

    The two groups spread out into the woods. Solomon tried to persuade Jeb and Boone to meet up with the Bigfoot Hunters (2 Doors, 10 minutes/attempt) and eventually succeeded. As they headed for the other party the heard a scream....

    The Bigfoot Hunters plus Demons had fanned out through the woods looking for anything unusual. Lexis Staiffer had seen something obscured in the bushes - a body. It was at least two days old and had not been gnawed so much as abraded in parts, as if someone had taken a grinder to it in spots and rasped off the flesh. Suddenly the world seemed to go off balance and disorienting, a feeling of vertigo swept through everyone (human) as a scream rang out.

    Some thing had leapt on the back of Bobby Brewster. Its large insectile eyes, each the size of an outstretched hand, were expressionless and its mandibles shredded through the base of Bobby's neck and into his skull. The hornet-like head was mounted on chitinous armored shoulders that were themselves the front part of something large and bear-shaped. A horrible monstrosity with the body of a bear, yet with the head of insect and small, vestigial forearms that shifted Bobby's corpse slightly to better feed.

    There were more screams and now shouts. The vertigo added to the sense of unreality and confusion. Waters fired his pistol at the beast but its bulk absorbed the shot without any apprarent reaction. Jennifer improvised a club from a stick, and Alexis used an Embed to distract the beast away into the woods - nearly sending it directly into the path of the incoming party of Solomon et al. (those dice from the above dramatic failure almost made this much more complicated...). Lexis then caught sight of a second beast approaching from the brush as well. The PC's decided that a strategic retreat was necessary.

    The redneck cavalry arrived. One muscular, bare-chested attacker smashed the skull of the Hive Bear with an iron bar, spraying blood and ichor. The PC's tried to get Ray, Mario and Lou away from the scene, but with great difficulty. Mario did his best to keep filming and had to be abandoned. After several shots were fired the sounds of battle died down. The PCs saw Mario arguing with one of heavily armed victors. The gentleman wanted Mario's camera and was prepared to take it at gunpoint. When Mario continued to object, one of the muscular figures put him in a Full Nelson. The PC's rapidly ran for the parking lot, but could still make out the rattling crack of vertebrae as the argument behind them was finally concluded.

    Solomon invoked an Embed to read Jeb before he also fled the scene, searching for a recent source of sorrow. He read that Jeb was thinking about the heavily armed posse that they were fleeing: "I wish someone would tell off those assholes from The Farm - they push everyone around."

    The PC's decided it was time to rapidly depart.

              Characters Present:
              Solomon Tzengeti
              Lexis Staiffer
              Zachariah Ross
              Reginald Waters

              The Ring decided to hole up in a hotel room, investigate what the could via phone and internet, and make plans for the next day.
              • Lexis recalled the tattoos on one of the muscular attackers. She determined that it was a corruption of an emblem of the Knights Templar, where the crosses had been replaced by gears. It also had the initials "DMV" in gothic font added to the top, where the traditional emblem would have had Deus Vult ("God Wills It").
              • Solomon used his hacking Savant Core to determine that this logo was connected to WatchMaker publications, and in particular their series of pamphlets entitled Divine Designs. These are aimed an Christians interested in Intelligent Design and transhumanism. The address of the publisher is listed as Elohim City. It is a small press, only recently in business. He expresses enthusiasm for finding some way to hack into their system.
              • Reginald learns from his police contacts that the plates of the redneck cavalry are registered to residents in Elohim City, about 90 minutes north of Talking Trees. No criminal record. Elohim City was founded by a conservative religious Christian group and is small and insular. It's close to the border with Arkansas so jurisdiction can get murky. Vague concerns about white supremacists, but the Feds would have more information about that. Local sheriff is Arnold Bryant. He has a reputation of being a straight-shooter, no nonsense type.
              • Jennifer learns from her Contact (Cryptic Expert) the there must be a major source or cache of Aether within a mile or so radius of where they encountered them. Cryptids need it to maintain their unnatural existence. She also suggests that Jennifer be careful - these critters probably need a lot of Aether and the PCs are a source for them.
              This information round-up took hours, and then the Ring talked late into the night. Should they stay in the area and try to figure out the cryptic issue, or follow the leads to Elohim City? How dangerous would it be to do one task versus the other? After some wrangling and indecision the Ring opted to at least drive through Elohim City, and get a general feeling for what it was like. The piled in to Zachariah's black SUV and headed ninety minutes north.

              Elohim City is a bump on an interstate road. The highway slows down to 35mph for a bit and then there is a single stoplight at the center of town surrounded by a half a dozen buildings. The light marks a T-junction. The street comes off the highway, and then forms a “U”, connecting back a further bit down the way.

              A quick glance shows a gas station, a roadside restaurant, antique and hardware stores, a larger corrugated building set back a bit from the road, and a city hall. There are a dozen or so houses as well, and one can see a church steeple, set a block or so further away from the highway. It gave the players an overall impression of a run-down, hick town that must be boring to stop at and soul-destroying to live in. A moments reflection revealed that these feels were being projected, radiated by some sort of Concealment Infrastructure. Furthermore, the city layout did not match the satellite images from Google maps, at all. Something is being hidden here.

              The PCs drive through town and out the other side, and then start on another long discussion on what to do next. They decided that after their long discussion it was safe to turn around and stop in the gas station and get a bite to eat. Solomon would look for wifi to hack, while Lexis would get take-out from Cassidy's, the diner.

              Lexis enters the diner and is told by Marylou, a stereotypical diner waitress, that they don't do take out. As she tries to text the others to this effect, a tall homeless man shambles in. Marylou berates the man (who she calls "Bear") and sends him on his way, while Lexis is puzzled to find that her phone does not seem to work. It a few moments it seems to be restored and she texts and Jennifer comes to join them. Jennifer crosses the street to join Lexis and runs into Bear. He goes on a rambling tirade on how the farm isn't a farm, that there should be "no blood for oil," and that they "plan to blow up Spain!". Jennifer politely excused herself and joined Lexis.

              Cassidy’s Cafe is a slightly run down diner with a Western theme. There’s a front counter with six stools and standard soda fountain and diner fare. There are booths and a jukebox as well. On one wall there is a long mural on showing an old time saloon. It’s a big garish but well done. There is a jukebox that plays vinyl 45’s, which is a bit unusual. The waitress was in her late 40’s and seemed chipper when she offered coffee. Someone puts a coin in the jukebox, and you hear Chubby Checkers, telling people to “Do the Twist.”

              Solomon, Zachariah, and Reginald are still at the gas station. There is much discussion on what to do next. Solomon searches for wifi, but gets nothing. They decide that it would be a good idea to triangulate on the Concealment Infrastructure that is radiating the feeling that deters visitors.

              Back at the diner, Lexis and Jennifer enjoy lunch. Tthe same patron goes to the jukebox and plays it again, in exactly the same motions. The other patrons move their food around but don’t eat much. The same coffee and food orders keep getting placed and repeated. Solomon, Zachariah, and Reginald move the car in front of the diner and join them. Solomon explains that he wants to put on a charade of having lost his wallet so that they have an excuse to wander the streets. Jennifer "decides" that she really would like to have dessert; Lexis gets suspicious and decides to stay with her. She and Jennifer decide to have some pie and someone again plays Chubby Checkers, who again tells everyone to "Do The Twist."

              Lexis examines the place more carefully. The pie is certainly tasty, and she asks the waitress for the recipe. It does seem to be a nice place to sit and rest. While the waitress writes it down, someone again plays the same song on the jukebox. Lexis notices that each of the patrons in the diner appear in some fashion in the mural: the waitress, some of the people at the tables, and painted at one table in the mural is the small figure of Jennifer, and a ghostly image that is looking more and more like herself. She uses some Manipulation+Persuasion to finally get get Jennifer to leave.

              Meanwhile, Solomon, Zachariah, and Reginald each walk in a separate direction to triangulate. Zachariah notices a young teen-age girl waving imploringly at him from the porch of her house. She seems to be urging him to join her on the porch. Zachariah ignored her and moved on. Reginald encountered Bear during his walk and heard the same sort of ravings as had Jennifer. The three Demons determined that the Concealment Infrastructure is in the old church, just a block or so away from the highway. They meet back up and the group decides to investigate. They drive to the church and park. Lexis remains behind the wheel and waits.

              The "True Gospel Church" is a moderate wooden structure (small for a church) that dated from the 1920’s. Its spire is visible from the highway, but it is back a few blocks. Closer inspection revealed that the church is largely abandoned. The windows are broken, and mostly boarded up. There were 2x4’s across the entrance. Zach uses his Embed "Like I Built It" to find a way into the church, and the four Demons climbed up through a low broken window. They found much of it destroyed inside, with the letters "DMV" in paint slathered on the walls in oddly luminous paint. Zach guided the players up to the bell in the church tower. It was connected to brass gears at odd angles and clearly part of the Infrastructure. Close inspection revealed that the clapper was the Linchpin. It was wrapped in a skin bag stuffed with human hair. It would “ring” every 23 minutes, sending out a signal that persuades people that there is nothing interesting here. Zachariah crushed the bag and it vanished in a cloud of black sparks.

              While those in the church congratulated themselves on their victory, a police car pulled up behind Lexis, who was parked outside the church. The sheriff stepped out of the car and approached her....

Barging between the between Lexis and the sheriff, he leaned in through the window of the SUV, brought his scraggly face close to Lexis. Their eyes, met, and his seemed momentarily lucid. He whispered to her "Run."

EM activated his Sonic Acuity Form ability. His ears faded away, leaving an odd grey, fuzzy emptiness on either side of his head. He could hear the approach of someone climbing the stairs. He focussed on the sounds of their approach, the slap of their bare feet, the pounding of their heart and rapid breath. It was a young teen-ager, probably a young girl. He motioned to the others to stand down. A young, blond girl poked her head in: "You four don't have the sense God gave a headless chicken!" She urged them to follow her down the stairs and away from the church. Mr. Zets could sense no menace in her motives and the four followed her down the stairs, and out of the back of the church through a loose board in a back wall.

The sheriff drawled "Bear, you ain't the sheriff around here no more!," and gut-punched the taller man, sending him to the pavement. As he proceeded to boot the helpless man, Lexis started the SUV and pulled away. She rapidly returned to the highway, and drove for a mile before she found a place she could hide the rented truck, out of sight of the highway.

                  Meanwhile the four demons were lead by back-alleys to a run-down house nearby. The house itself, while not a disaster, was in poor shape and decidedly messy inside. The girl introduced herself as Marcy-May Beeman - but she goes by "Mac". Her five year old brother Bobby joined them clutching a blanket and stared at them blankly.

                  Mac explained that she had seen them wandering around town and didn't want them to "disappear like the others." She knew that she had to get them off the streets, especially after she saw that they had been messing about with the church. She explained that things had not always been so bad, that people from "The Farm" were always a bit gun-crazy and paranoid, but that in the last year or two things had gotten completely out-of-hand. The change had happened with the arrival of the publishing company that moved in to town, and their evangelical work on "God's new creation." The Farm community got much more menacing, had chased out the local pastor and many of the businesses, which drove many townspeople away. Most of the people who remained were either part of the company, The Farm, or simply couldn't leave.

                  Solomon asked about Mac's family. She explained that her father had died a long time ago, and that her mother... she was at a loss for words. Bobby asked where their mother was and Mac shushed him. Her explanation was interrupted by a figure that came to the door. Mac gestured to the Demons to relax and do nothing.

                  In walked Marylou, from Cassidy's Cafe, in her waitressing uniform. Bobby cried out "Momma!", but Mac held him too her side and cautioned in a low voice, "That ain't Momma no more." Marylou Beeman took one look at the four strangers in her house, smiled and said "I'll go get you some menus. Can I bring you back anything to drink? Coffee?". The demons exchanged glances, and ordered their drinks. Marylou took their orders and went back out the door. Bobby snuffled tears into Mac's side.

                  Mac explained that shortly after working at Cassidy's her mother had changed. All she was, and all she did was be a waitress. Mac wasn't even sure her mother (or what she was now) even slept. She didn't recognize Mac or Bobby, but treated them as customers. Mac had turned this to there advantage, having her mother "charge her tab" to "her mother." So waitress Marylou's wages paid for Mac and Bobby's food. Mac offered to help if the four of them would get Bobby and her out of town and some place safe.

                  Lexis was working her way through the woods. Moving with a modicum of stealth, she avoided a pair of men patrolling the forest in camouflage carrying rifles. Quietly she headed back to the town and the church, hoping to rejoin the others.

                  At the same time, Reginald decided to work his way back to the church as well, remembering Mac's circuitous path as best he could. However, he got called out by an old woman as he tried to sneak through her backyard. A feisty sort, she reached for her shotgun and fired in his direction, but between her rheumy eyes and his fast feet she missed him entirely. However, the sound of the blast was bound to bring the police soon. (Dramatic Failure). Luckily, he and Lexis met up, and soon they were heading for the SUV. They retrieved the vehicle and drove back to pick up the others, perhaps foolishly driving the same SUV that the sheriff had noticed Lexis in earlier. Although the seven piled rapidly into the car they had not escaped the police. As they pulled away a police car flashed its lights and prepared to pursue.

                  Only to be front-ended by a car that had swerved to avoid a squirrel carrying the cigarette from Zet's Cause and Effect embed from earlier in the session!

                      The Ring drove north of town, to Heavner, and found a hotel for the night. Solomon pumped Marcy May Beeman for information, but while she was cooperative, she could not give him the details of the operations of The Farm (times of patrols, etc.) that he wanted. She explained that the local police were in cahoots with the cultists and that those who did not join the cult were chased out of town. The only people left were too old or too poor to leave. Eventually Solomon planned to make a Pact with her - she would give up her childhood in Elohim City in return for a secure foster home for her and her brother, Bobby. (Greater, Permanent, for Resources, Mentor and Safe House).
                      There was much, much discussion about what to do next. Should the Ring pursue their original investigation of the cryptid's further south, and find out their true origin? Or should they pursue the GodMachine activity they had found in Elohim City? The two were clearly related, since the posse that killed the cryptids was from Elohim City. It was suggested that maybe the Ring could lure a Hive Bear from Talking Trees Camp Ground in the south up to The Farm thereby causing confusion and perhaps destruction. However they judged it impractical to lure a Hive Bear nearly ninety miles (145km) in any reasonable amount of time. After more discussion the Ring decided to break into Watchmaker Publicationsand see what they could find.

                      Zets was assigned to stay with Mac and Bobby (Player late to session), while the other four drove back to the edge of town. The Ring had decided that since the building had few windows and the front door was visible from the street, they would try to enter through the loading dock at the rear of the building. Lexis's bump of direction allowed her to lead them through the woods to the back of the parking lot of the publishing company. There Jennifer was able sense (Like I Built It embed) gave her a sense of the building. Rather than finding an opening through which to teleport and risk Compromise and use up Aether, Jennifer tried to pick the lock, (Right Tools embed). The Ring got in with no problems.

                      The Ring entered the building to find that the majority of it was taken up with a few printing presses, pallets of paper, and cartons of printed books and pamphlets. The printing presses and printed materials registered faintly when Jennifer spent Aether on Aetheric Resonance. She determined that it was imbued with a faint effect that rendered the reader more receptive to the materials printed, and the effect came from the ink used.

                      At the front of the building were the offices, set off from the rest by partitions and a drop ceiling. Jennifer again picked the lock and the Ring cautiously entered the reception area of the front office. This would be where the secretary/receptionist for the office would sit, and her work area included a desk, computer and file cabinets. There was a locked door off this area that lead to a second office. It bore the name plate inscribed "Lyle Smith, Manager."

                      The lock on this door was more tricky, and in picking it Query actually broke the lock. (Dramatic Failure). They inspected the office ceilings are realized that if they were careful they could lift Lexis up through the drop ceiling over the partition walls so that she could drop into the office. After a brief scramble she was able to open the door from the inside and let the players in.

                      Solomon smiled, sat in front of the computer, and got to work. Gold dust glittered in front of his eyes, forming wires that connected him to the machine. Activating his Savant Core hacking skills, he sucked up all the information he could. He scanned through some of it, and sent the rest to cloud storage via his phone. The computing system was fairly simple, and not connected to the internet - only the two office computers were connected, and that was by a hard wire. Emails were handled via the phone lines, indicating an odd lack of connection to the outside world.

                      Lexis looked around the office and found a map of Oklahoma stretched out on the desk. There were numerous small markings and numerical notation written in precise handwriting at many locations. But what caught her eye were the three lines that ran across the map and crossed in northeastern Oklahoma. The occult notation about them indicated that they were ley lines, lines of occult power. "I'm definitely going to take this!" she exclaimed.

                      Reginald sifted through the paperwork in the office and checked with Solomon and several facts were clear. First, this operation was a small one. It had only been in operation for about 18 months, and its reach extended through parts of the central U.S. but no further. The operation did not have they pyramid scheme for some sort of exponential proselytizing growth, but seemed to be targeted. However, it was intense. GodMachine activity was hard on cultists since exposure to Essence and Aether had unhealthy effects. (The odd form of the Weathermen was proof of that.) For this reason cults were often carefully groomed with lots of lower level members who could be promoted as more senior ones became too damaged to be useful. This operation was anomalous in its ruthless exploitation of cult members.

                      The Ring continued to search for the purpose of the operation. It was clear that The Farm was producing something for an occult matrix. There were notations of gallons produced, and some sort of calendar that referenced phases of the moon. What was going on?

                      Solomon sifted through the emails looking for a clue. One caught his eye:

                      To: [email protected]
                      From: [email protected]
                      Dear Lyle,

                                                  Solomon asked Sifrayah about "HR" and learned that she was of middling ability and power. Her area of Influence was Human Resources: she could always find someone, somewhere, to do whatever job she needed doing. That might range from shifty truckers who would carry cargo without asking questions to murderous psychopaths willing to dispatch troubling investigators. Solomon asked Sifrayah to look into her Bans and Banes.

                                                  Sifrayah was intrigued by the goings on in Oklahoma. He claimed that "everyone knew" that The GodMachine had some project that involved moving ley lines, and that it was so phenomenally dangerous that it lead to the disassembly of three previous angels who could not balance the conflicting priorities of the mission. The goal was to tap in to some of the energy in the ley lines, partially redirecting them. Of course, not even The GodMachine would be so foolish as to actually disrupt the ley lines since, well, that would lead to the obvious disaster.

                                                  The Ring decided that their next target would be the final destination on thePandora Trucking shipping manifest. Where had they been taking the Blood Honey? What was being done with it? And what was the connection to the milk produced at The Farm?
