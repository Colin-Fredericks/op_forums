                                            01-10-2016, 09:59 PM
                                            I'll be honest; when I wrote the Satan Signal I initially did lean towards the first alternative, but I didn't want to canonise a Lucifer-analogue for Demon: the Descent because it felt a little too similar to Fallen (even though the concept of canon in CofD's toolkit world is a foggy one). I love the idea of a demon who ascended to become a ghost in the God-Machine, a virus within It that encourages angels to Fall, and if you do too you should use the hell out of it. It's also a really ambitious Hell that can fit, with some tweaks, all of the existing Agendas, if you fancy letting your PCs reach cosmic-tier.

                                            I also quite the idea that in a setting full of crazy conspiracies and mysteries the solution to one of the bigger, more intriguing ones is as mundane as a giant demon-eating monster (not that the Signaler is mundane) that'll probably just eat you if you ever discover the truth. I also love the hell out of cryptids - you're lucky cryptids aren't behind every urban legend - and wanted to show just how powerful they can become.
