    Story Arc: "Are you Happy Now?"

    01-22-2016, 11:29 PM
    Hello everyone,

    I have decided to post here the story I intend to narrate to my players, and would enjoy some positive criticism. It will be the first time I am going to play Demon (even though I have been working on that for a bit of time now).

    So this Post will be really really long, but I will be really thankful to those that read it. Some parts of the ideas I have posted here and there before, but this would be the "most complete" work I've done so far.

    “Are you Happy Now?”


    Arcs: 1- Dream of you / 2- City-Dreaming / 3- Bliss.
    Episodes: 1.1 Dream Catcher / 2.1 Time to Party & 2.2 Soulless City / 3.1 Night of 13s & 3.2 Gate of Fates & 3.3 Song of Bliss.

    Description: For centuries now, the God-Machine is running numbers on human’s dreams, trying to understand what humans wish, aspire, wonder, etc. It has been collecting data and simulating different scenarios, always striving for optimal results in Earth’s projects.

    “Are you Happy Now?” is a (long) campaign that focuses precisely on giving humans what they might think they want/need to be happy and become less prone to resist the GM’s plans.
    In the first arc (Dream of You): the demons are introduced to two of the god-machine’s infrastructures, one where a camera capable of taping dreams is being perfected and another one where they are analyzed. In the second arc, the machine decided to run a scenario, creating a parallel reality were people wishes actually come, but this will require some significant logistical work. On the third and final arc the GM has decided to implement one of human’s most popular dreams: World Peace. But will the sheep finally dream of electric robots?

    Major Influences: The Matrix, Fringe, Buffy the Vampire Slayer and Angel, Assassins Creed (Metaplot)


    Step 1:

    Project: KLM10983-#324556BC
    Codename: “Dream of You”

    Director: Maureen Kellor - “Kolya” – Messenger – Rank 2
    Operative: Asab Himir - “Garuel” – Messenger - Rank 1


    Synopsis: At order from the God-Machine, a camera that can tape dreams is being developed. Earlier prototypes are currently in use and secretely taping the dreams of 3 humans. These dreams are being sent to an Angel watch at local VHS rental store called “Top Rentals”.

    Project Output: Create a final product (camera) that can tape dreams with no side effects.
    Occult Matrix:
    Infrastructure: “Top Rentals” / “CrowTech”
    Linchpin: The current prototypes had different effects on the three individuals being taped: Susane has developed recurring nightmares, which can go “viral”. / Nate has now the ability of enter other people dreams /Anne has now precognitive dreams.



    Background:

    For the past 25 years the God-Machine has put the Asab Himir (“Garuel”) in charge of watching and analyzing the Dreams of humans, as it was perfecting a technology able to record humans’ dreams. Garuel might fall if nudged correctly – after so many years watching the dreams of others, he feels he might start to have some of his own.

    The camera able to do that is being created by the company “CrownTech”, whose only Angel is the Engineer (and Chief of the Lab) Maureen Kellor (“Kolya”). Maureen oversees other 6 scientists and engineers. The Last prototype KLM10 is working well, and should soon be concluded. Once the Camera becomes complete and functional, a destroyer will be send to cut all the loose ends.
    There is one stigmatic at CrownTech, the Janitor Millor Silvano who has seen the cogs of the machine, but kept working there trying to figure out what this all means.

    The humans currently under observation on KLM09 are Susanne Hitchkins, Edward Jacobs, and Anne Fitzpatrick. In the next few days, the new KLM10 will be installed in their houses.

    The cameras send their information to a Van that passes every night by the houses of the dreamers (2h30-5am). The Van is driven by a cultist: Pierce.
    Pierce drives back to his place and every day at 15h22 drops a tape at Top Rentals.
    The Cult is known as the “Dream Catcher” organized by Kolya, and its members act like junkies: they can get high on dreams. Once a week Kolya appears before them.

    Step 2:
    Project: FX3245##561OD9-9
    Codename: “City-Dreaming”

    Director: James Gladstone - “Dravos” (Psychopomp) - Rank 3
    Operatives: Robin Mattel - “Tantaliel” (Guardian) - Rank 2
    Sarah & Selenne Parker - “Mesyria (Messenger) / “Valdoria” (Destroyer) Rank 2.

    Synopsis: the GM has decided to run a scenario where humans might actually get what they wish for. This project however, sensible as it is, must be run to scale, therefore a pocket reality must be created and humans driven to it. The GM itself also decides not to create things from scrap, but rather harvest the necessary materials from our own reality.


    Project Output: Create a pocket reality where human wishes may come true.
    Infrastructure: Clock tower / Football Stadium.
    Occult Matrix:
    Linchpin:



    Background:

    Part 1 - Time to Party: At the center of a (small) city lies a Clock tower. For the past 23 years (and 10 months), every night, at exactly 2 a.m. the clock “steals” 10 seconds of time from the city. The tower, and the “time” stored in the artifact are protected by the Angel Robin Mattel.

    Those inside the city never realized what happens, as when the time comes they are all put in a temporal stasis and resumed 10 seconds later. During this time, the Clock tower causes a space-time distortion which can be “seen” from the outside (but considering a small town in the middle of nowhere kind of scenario it goes unnoticed).

    Once the clock tower completes 24 years, it will have stole a whole day from the lives of people, and the God-Machine will have what it needs: just enough time to dilate and build upon to put inside the pocket reality it is creating. As a consequence, however, after that many time distortions, the city is now in “red-zone”, spatially destabilized and will self-destroy. (Strange meteorological phenomena will start to take place as it slowly, but surely, will be destroyed).

    Part 2 – Soulless City: The God-Machine is also building a new Stadium, with an astonishing capacity for 175,000 people. The strange thing is that the city where it is building it doesn’t even have 100.000 people living in it, and stranger still it will receive – as a kind of last moment arrangement and obviously weird WTF moment for the demons – the Super Bowl.

    The Machine intends to absorb the energy of the people inside the stadium to fuel the creation of the pocket dimension. If succeed it will also transport the souls of all of those inside the during the final game to the pocket dimension, where they will “live” in the new city as if nothing happened, except that their wishes will now come true.
    Two angels are overseeing this step of the project. Sarah (“Mesyria”) is the Mayor’s gabinet working all the red tape (and tilting some ideas here and there), while her “twin-sister” Selenne “Valdoria” is silencing the opposition….

    Step 3:

    Project: ZZ724F35-π3M
    Codename: Bliss

    Director: Trillian Frizz (“Fizzelwenghart” – Guardian – Rank 4).
    Operatives:

    Synopsis: this arc is drawn from by Angel’s season metaplot 4, in which a Jasmine is brought to earth. For the past 11 years I’ve been thinking about this and how to play that in a table, and now thanks to Demon the Descent, my dream has finally become true (hmm… am I in a pocket dimension?). The Plot is simple enough: The God-Machine will bring a godlike entity to earth, which will “bestow” the world with peace and harmony, at the mere cost of humans’ free will. However, bringing such a being to earth is not easy, and again a series of events must take place beforehand.

    Output: Bring the Unnamed Entity to the Earth Realm.
    Infrastructure:
    Occult Matrix:
    Linchpin: It was necessary to create a prophecy about the arrival of the Unnamed Entity.
    Prophecy: “Song of Bliss”

    “Blood shall be shed for the spirit of the people
    And from this will result in the first coming, the Glorious One.
    One that is so full of life, That will bring it from Death.
    Rejoice, for this is the time of the impossible,
    And hence comes the key for eternal bliss,
    That will unlock the gate of fates
    Await in awe for the three signs,
    But worry not, for the pain they will cause
    Shall be remembered solely as the pains from Birth of the new World:

    It will start in the Night of the Thirteenths,
    And the city of Saints will crumble to the ground,
    From its heart a mountain will rise
    And Fire will pour down from the sky.

    you shall know that the time has come,
    And there will be no more War,
    No more Pain,
    No more suffering,
    We shall all live in Peaceful Bliss”.

    So as every good prophecy wording is important, as well as opportunities to mislead the players in their analysis.

    Key entries for the Prophecy:

    “the first coming, the Glorious one, so full of life, that will bring it from Death”
    • The Glorious one is not the nameless entity, but more like grandma or grandpa. It is a human that has been biologically engineered (a plan going back to the 1930’s) that will be able to get pregnant by a vampire OR impregnate a vampire. As in Angel, I have to admit that the idea of a Vampire giving birth is too delightful to not use it.

    “and hence comes the key for eternal bliss, unlock the gate of fates”
    • The key is not an actual key, but it is a person. It is the son of the human and the vampire.
    • The gate, as well, is a person. A Woman, but not any women: a Changeling.
    (Yes cross-over for your life)





    The 3 Signs:

    “The Night of the Thirteenths”
    • The night of the 13hs, will be a night of 13 hours. Ok, so I know that during equinox day and night are not really exactly 12 hours each, but I am taking some liberties here. For some strange motive, daylight saving hours will end this year on a Friday… which happens to be The equinox! (Imagine introducing this subtly around the gameplay). At midnight all clocks will go back one hour, and hence, “13 hours of night”.

    “Crumble to the Ground From its Heart a Mountain Will Rise and Fire will pour down from the Sky”
    • This is pure misdirection. Players will probably expect some earthquake, connecting to volcano activity (the mountain and fire).
    • But actually this refer to the highest building in the world is being built in the city and its construction will coincide with a Solar Flare at end of the year.



    The nameless entity will be born (if the key and gate actually do it, but a messenger is assigned to that) during the solar flare at the top of the highest building in the world. And then, if that was not stopped, the demons are in nose-deep sh*t.

    So this last part still needs a bit more structure, but I guess I will have plenty of time to work on that until we arrive to it.
    Anyway please tell me what you think!
