                Originally posted by DesolateDolly View Post
                Just Bruised: Influence effect 1: Strengthen. Add successes to the Defense of your influence. Thus removing incoming attack dice. If that's not what you like, if you can add to the defense of a loved one by strengthening them, then you can take away damage that would be dealt to them! So, remove successes damage from the target of the influence instead. Numina is not exhaustive list, if these two effects are not suitable for you, then simply make it a 1 Essence Numina that reduces all incoming damage on a target to 1. (Do remember that Angels are always storyteller characters so powers like Just Bruised make them very difficult to kill when they are already statistically superior. There is a mechanical aspect, unless you're just wanting to make a monster that your players cannot have a hope of defeating.)
                Not quite a fit. Just Bruised is reflexive, and can reduce a boundless amount a damage to one. You are describing a few points of armor. The scope is completely different. And of couse, I could handwave a numina. But then, why would this numina be so much more powerful that all the rest? No offense, but that's a rule zero fallacy if I've ever seen one.

                Of course, I agree that this embed can be troublesome for NPCs (of couse, demon npcs do exist). Just Bruised has a 'diminising effort' aspect that can make it somewhat workable, I would not recommend that with an angel's potential dicepool. But that's not the point.

                Cool Heads Prevail: Influence effect 3: Control. If the influence is emotion, you turn it off in everyone within the area of your influence. Note: Mass Create means you can create multiple instances of a condition. There is nothing to prevent you using 3 dot Control to effect more than one person in a scene. Again, create a Numen that replicates the effect otherwise.
                Not so, I'm quoting the book, influence 3: 'An animal's action, one plants's growth , or one object's functioning'. One individual until influence 5.

                Alibi is replicated by the Manfestation: Image. If you are upset about RAW, modify to cost the same to project an image of the angel's form.
                In My Pocket: Angels are always prepared for their mission. They have what they need. When in doubt? Attribute + Rank.
                So, no surprises for angels, right? That's ironic in a game of demons. :-)

                    It could be clearer that you don't have a fucking clue what the Rule Zero Fallacy is, but not without getting into the realm of egregious hyperbole.
