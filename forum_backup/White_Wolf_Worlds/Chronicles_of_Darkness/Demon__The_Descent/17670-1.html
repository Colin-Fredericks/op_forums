                So, a member of my group is getting close to unlocking their first Interlocks. I made the interlocks up a while ago, and I never got any feedback on them. I'd love some feedback and if anything stands out as egregious please let me know.

                One thing you should know, our game takes place in a city divided by a large river. This river is full of weird occult magic and generally messes with God-Machine projects. So, the river is basically a third party of                 its own.


                Mr. Dock, a Guardian who Fell after choosing the river over his charge.


                Lost In The Crowd ▶ Shatter
                Where Did I Put That?
                By touching an object and spending a point of Aether, you are able to force it to mimic the binary quantum state for a short time. This effectively causes the location of the object to continually delete itself from the memories of those present.

                Dice Pool: Manipulation+Stealth - Size of Object
                Action: Instant
                Cost: 1 Aether
                Roll Results
                Dramatic Failure:The effect bounces back to you. The targeted object becomes hidden, and you must make a Perception roll with a -2 Penalty to remember it
                Failure: No effect.
                Success: The object is deleted. Any attempt to “see” the object by mundane means (eyesight, cameras, etc.) requires a Perception roll with a -3 modifier. The object must be Size 5 or smaller. The effect lasts until the end of the scene.
                Exceptional Success: The effect lasts all day..

                Shatter ▶ Synthesis
                Unbind
                Infrastructure is only Infrastructure due to it's location in place and time. With this Interlock you can temprorarily Unbind a person or object "marked" as Infrastructure, effectively "blinding" the God Machine to them.

                Dice Pool: Wits+Investigation
                Action: Instant
                Cost: 3 Aether
                Roll Results
                Dramatic Failure: The attempt to unbind backfires, causing a flare of Aetheric power. You gain the Hunted condition
                Failure: No effect.
                Success: You are able to rewrite the marking process on people and certain objects (up to Size 15) and remove the mark, effectively “blinding” the Machine to the item. This is not without its dangers however, as the effort taken to rewrite the mark caused the Distracted Condition. This effect is permanent, but does not prevent the object from being reintegrated into the God-Machine’s plans should no other action be taken..
                Exceptional Success: This effect does not cause the Distracted Condition.

                Synthesis ▶ Animal Communication
                A Drop in the Ocean
                Whatever rules the River operates by, they are similar to the code of the God-Machine. At least close enough for you to communicate with. You are able to touch the River and “see” along it’s length within the metro area. This functions similar to astral projection in that you leave your mortal body and travel along the river.


                Dice Pool: Manipulation+investigation
                Action: Instant
                Roll Results
                Dramatic Failure: You lose focus as you attempt to read the river. Instead of seeing one place, you see all places. The information overload gives you the Disorientated Condition.
                Failure: No effect. You get wet.
                Success: You can see and hear anything on the river or along the water’s edge as if you were there. You are is only able to see events as they are actually happening. By spending a point of Aether you are able to “speak” through the river.
                Exceptional Success: Same as above, but you may attempt to “rewind” what you see through the River. It is the Storyteller’s discretion as to how far you can see.

                            Originally posted by Geckopirateship View Post
                            I'm kind of wondering what the maximum power level for Interlocks is, and how overt their powers can be (also, whether they can be directly related to demonic form). For example, would creating elaborate virtual reality scenarios be appropriate for an Interlock?

                            ST's call, really. Granted you can say that about anything, so for a slightly more useful answer....

                            Interlocks are closer to Embeds than Exploits.They're generally more covert than overt and not as world-shattering as Exploits can be, but can be more obvious and powerful than Embeds (and can even cost Aether). That said, they can still be pretty damn powerful. Elaborate VR scenarios seems possible, but presumably with limitations on time, space, number of subjects, etc. I can see it working well as something real to the subject but hallucinatory to an observer (think the Matrix; 'Your mind makes it real'), maybe using Fractal Reality/Ripple and Recurring Hallucinations as your Keys.

                            Generally I avoid powers locked to your form, but that's personal preference mostly and in one of the threads in my sig I made a Cipher (The Glutton) based around a specific demonic form (though it was written so you could use all the Interlocks with or without it manifested, though some are far easier with a Cavernous Maw)

                            EDIT: Duthuras, Mr Dock's Cipher is awesome. A Drop in the Ocean seems a tad powerful (it need a time limit at least) and I dislike Unbind being permanent (kinda feel like that should be your Exceptional Success) but the ST is the real God-Machine.

                                This is the interlock I've got for Ms. Wilds, a Demon inspired by a friend of mine. I don't know if I'll ever get to use her as an NPC, but hope does spring eternal.

                                Ms. Wilds started as an Angel who could command cats to act as both a surveillane network, and also as a means to gather the materials for Infrastructure. One night, she came across a kitten whose mother and siblings had died to aether exposure, and the kitten (named Kit by the Angel) was likewise dying. The occult matrix required most of the Angel's essence at the time, so she had little to spare for the kitten, but spare she did to try and bolster its life. She grew attached to Kit, and he to her. Unfortunately, she had to leave him with a mother cat one night to ensure an Infrastructure completed. When she returned, Kit was dead. The Angel fell, and Ms. Wilds stepped out into the world. Fortunately, it was not the end of her relationship with Kit.

                                Cipher: Humanity is but a sliver.

                                Interlock 1: Schrodinger's Cat

                                (Animal Communication + In My Pocket)
                                The essence shared between Ms. Wilds and Kit has cemented their bond beyond their respective Fall and Death. Kit exists in a quantum state; he is both alive and dead, mundane and stigmatic. All Ms. Wilds has to do is find a container he could potentially be in, no matter how remote, and open it to establish what state he is in.

                                Die Pool: Dexterity + Animal Ken
                                Action: Instant
                                Roll Results
                                Dramatic Failure: There is a cat in the container, but it is not Kit nor remotely disposed towards Ms. Wilds. For the rest of the scene the cat stalks Ms. Wilds, attacking, distracting and in general harassing her, and counts as an observer for the purposes of Demonic Transformation and Compromise. When the scene ends, the cat vanishes back into the ether.
                                Failure: The container is empty of Kit.
                                Success: The container has Kit in it, alive as a fully grown, if perhaps overly intelligent and affectionate, cat. Kit does not count as an observer for the purposes of Demonic Transformation or Compromise, and has the True Friend merit towards Ms. Wilds. Kit remains in this world for hours equal to the successes rolled or until he is killed, after which he fades away.
                                Exceptional Success: Ms. Wilds may spend a point of Aether to turn Kit into a Cryptid with a Rank equal to half her Primum (rounded up). Kit may take any adaptations based on Ms. Wilds’ own Form Powers and Exploits, though any skills he gains may be separate from her own. When Kit fades away he returns to his default state, as he is in a constant state of being alive and dead, mundane and stigmatic.


                                Interlock 2: Hollowed Mirror

                                (In My Pocket + Trick Of The Light)
                                At her touch, Ms. Wilds can turn any solid mirror into a portal into another reality, a space unique to that mirror allowing her to store items without risk of them being stolen by most others.

                                Die Pool: Presence + Larceny
                                Action: Reflexive
                                Roll Results
                                Dramatic Failure: The mirror shatters at Ms Wilds’ touch into tiny, fractal shaped pieces, ejecting anything she had stored within it. She suffers 1 Lethal damage from the glass, as well as any damage from being hit by anything the mirror had been carrying.
                                Failure: The mirror remains as such.
                                Success: Ms. Wilds may use any mirror she comes across as a storage container, either hiding or removing items from within the mirror that will fit through its surface. Anything within a mirror will not age or decay, as though it were in a bolthole, and the space within the mirror is as large as the room that the mirror itself is in. Living beings will not require food, water nor age and are able to see out of the mirror like a window, but are unable to leave unless the mirror is broken or Ms. Wilds releases them. Beings within the mirror are able to break it. Anything within a mirror is hazy, requiring a Wits + Composure roll penalized by Ms. Wilds’ Primum to be seen clearly, though the appearance out is as clear as the mirror itself is.

                                Should the mirror be broken, anything larger than the pieces of the mirror will be ejected into the nearest available space. Kit may be retrieved from a mirror by way of Schrodinger's Cat, whether or not he was stored in one.
                                Exceptional Success: Ms Wilds reaches beyond the one mirror she is using, accessing any mirror she is currently using for storage. She may remove or store something in any of her in-use mirrors so long as it may fit through either of the mirrors being accessed.


                                Interlock 3: Cat’s Eyes

                                (Trick Of The Light + Remote Linkup)
                                Even as a Demon, Ms. Wilds has had amazing eyesight. She has found that, by touching and looking deep into substances similar to those that compose her visual hardware, she can have even greater vision. This power works on any precious or semi-precious gemstone.
                                Die Pool: Wits + Socialize
                                Action: Instant
                                Roll Results
                                Dramatic Failure: The gem becomes a gadget with the sheen of a cat's eye gemstone. Anyone who uses the gem will always be pointed towards Ms. Wilds in some way, such as the sheen acting like a compass or summoning a cat to direct them to her.
                                Failure: The gemstone remains as it is.
                                Success: The gemstone becomes a piece of surveillance equipment for Ms. Wilds, allowing her to remotely view and hear through the gemstone as if she were there provided she concentrates. Anytime she actively uses a gemstone in this manner, it gains a sheen similar to a cat’s eye, no matter what gemstone it is. Ms. Wilds may have as many Cat’s Eyes active as she has Primum, but she has no limit in how many she can access at once. A gemstone remains a Cat’s Eye for a week before the effect fades.
                                Exceptional Success: Something about the universe, the moment, is just right. Ms. Wilds may spend a point of aether and a dot of Willpower and permanently turn the gemstone into a Cat’s Eye gadget based on Remote Linkup, allowing her to view through it if she successfully rolls Cat’s Eye. Any gemstones transformed this way gain the sheen of a cat’s eye gemstone as well, even if it normally wouldn’t be possible.


                                Interlock 4: Fur That Talks

                                (Remote Linkup + Animal Communication)
                                Ms. Wilds’ connection with Kit has gone past their previous quantum link. Ms. Wilds’ library of languages now includes those of animals, as well as those of humanity.

                                Die Pool: Manipulation + Animal Ken
                                Action: Reflexive
                                Roll Results
                                Dramatic Failure: Ms. Wilds gets the wrong language, interpreting Song Bird instead of Hawk, or a cat’s body language to a dog’s. Any animal she attempts to communicate with becomes hostile until she recalibrates.
                                Failure: The language barrier is not breached.
                                Success: Ms. Wilds may converse fluently with a particular type of animal for the rest of the scene, words and body language being translated fluently for both parties. Whether they have anything to say to her depends on the animal and their conversation habits, and obeying any commands or requests she has may require Social Maneuvering. However, Ms. Wilds has the option of making a pact with any animal willing, allowing her to make Covers of those animals. She must use Fur That Talks again if she wishes to speak another animal language.
                                Exceptional Success: Ms. Wilds becomes fluent in all animal languages for the scene, requiring no further uses of Fur That Talks, though she may wish to exclude what animals understand what words.

                                        Those are interesting ideas, and if the character (for some reason) goes Pentagram I will consider making those two of her interlocks. However, I would like to point out Animal Communication actually sends an animal on a mission to do something. It doesn't allow true communication between animal and Demon, and that was why I actually made a note about how Fur That Talks doesn't innately allow commands to be made. You may get a Golden Retriever to go grab your keys that you left by your car, but good luck getting a Redtail Hawk to do it without some serious negotiating...or using Animal Communication. Plus, it lets her actually talk with Kit rather than just command him.

                                        As for the pacting animals aspect, I can appreciate a few problems with that (the lack of a signature, for one). I was mostly wanting to give Ms. Wilds the ability to create a cat cover for herself, since Wtiches and, well, Cats were major themes for her character. That said, making the power limited only to cats felt a little much, so I expanded it to all animals while limiting her to one language at a time.
