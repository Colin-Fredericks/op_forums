                                                  Originally posted by PencilBoy99
                                                  I'm sorry to bother you but my group is struggling with the "In my Pocket" embed and was hoping you can provide a ruling. One player wants to use it to pull anything out of a container - for example, a military grade Flamethrower (which i'm guessing would have to do around +5 lethal minimum).

                                                  Another interpretation of it is that this is game-breaking - there's essentially no limit on what you can do with it. Why have any other embed or ever use an exploit? Need to get into some place?                                                   My reply, since he seems not to have seen it:

                                                  Originally posted by me
                                                  There's a thread about this with some pretty good responses, but sure:

                                                  In My Pocket lets you pull anything out of your pocket (or an appropriately-sized receptacle) that hasn't been established not to be there. As such, pulling something out of a pocket that wouldn't fit in said pocket doesn't work, because although we've never established that the elephant wasn't in my coat, it couldn't have fit in there to begin with.

                                                  Likewise, one of the playtesters had a thing where a player pulled a celebrity's cell phone out, but that doesn't work unless the celebrity has not seen the phone in a while *and* the character actually had access to it. Same thing with the "incriminating documents;" pulling them out with In My Pocket presumes they exist, that the character had access to them, *and* that they're conclusively not anywhere else.

                                                  It seems like you have a handle on how it's supposed to work, so run it that way. Also, while Embeds can be pretty potent, remember that any really reality-breaking (or stretching) use, especially repeated use, can cause compromise or get the God-Machine's attention. Don't let your players spam a power or game the system. Not only is the system not set up for that kind of play, it's counter to the intent of the game, and like a microwave, I can't guarantee things won't burst into flames if used in a manner other than intended.
                                                  To address one of the things brought up in the thread: Could you pull a Gadget with Hellfire worked into it from your pocket using this Embed? Answer: Sure, provided that the Gadget exists (meaning you made it already or otherwise know that it exists), that it could fit in your pocket (duh), and it hasn't been established that it's not there (if your ring-mate said earlier in the mission, "hey, Bingo, do you have that fire-make-thingie?" and you say "naw, lent it to my gramma for making creme brulee," then no, you can't pull it out because we know where it is - gramma making dessert. Make sure you cal her later).
