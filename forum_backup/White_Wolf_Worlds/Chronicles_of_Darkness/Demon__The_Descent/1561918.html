    [Brainstorming] Unified Theory — Rebuilding A Core Framework For Demonic Powers

    07-18-2026, 09:38 PM
    As one of the first gamelines in the Revised Storytelling System, pre-dating the introduction of systems like Chases, Investigations, Building Equipment, and organizations, Demon: the Descent has some gaps in its toolkit compared to later gamelines.

    It's also relatively loose with the actual effects of its powers, leaning heavily on the flexibility of Conditions as a freeform mechanic or in some cases providing a narrative effect whose concrete effects are left to the table to adjudicate; gadget design, while nominally encompassing more limited expressions of the Embeds and Exploits they're derived from and discussing what is appropriate extensively in the Player's Guide, is nevertheless largely a matter of taste, and combined effects through methods like Interlocks, lambdas, or Analysts going loud is a sufficient Pandora's Box to prompt a sidebar assuaging the worries of anyone being asked to act as a game designer in addition to playing the game.

    Gadgets themselves link up with the mechanics for pacts, which have been expanded upon in half a dozen supplements (including two different Dark Eras chapters) and which metaphysically are a black box ostensibly divorced from the workings of the God-Machine; one of those mechanics (generational pacts) in turn connects to the stigmatic systems in a way that is mechanically similar to the demon-blooded systems from Heirs to Hell, and stigmatics can result both from a core Exploit and from use of gadgets.

    Exploits vary wildly in scope from rough equivalents of other splats' template features (directly creating stigmatics and cryptids or Installing a power into a living being) to opening up new avenues or applications of Cover (creating ephemeral patchworks or merging a resident with an associated building or fabricating a relationship from whole cloth) to varying degrees of equivalent major powers (animating objects, summoning extreme environments, rewinding time, etc), and demon-blooded characters don't get access to Exploits but may have the less controlled ability to supercharge Embeds that they know. Exploited gadgets serve as Aether stockpiles, which scale in capacity with their complexity in a way that isn't really addressed outside the corebook.

    The Cipher is a mystery that is notionally optional due to reasonable suspicions from a paranoid culture, but serves as a hefty narrative device whose mechanical expression is currently fairly loose for the sake of word economy and the early state of the edition's development at the time. Infrastructure is complex enough that the lack of organization mechanics akin to those put forth in Deviant has been remarked upon, and Infrastructure is one potential source of gadgets not created by the Unchained, as well as more frequently producing cryptids, cryptoflora, and stigmatics.

    Angels are demonstrably an awkward fit into the ephemeral being systems, as demonstrated by the ways in which "angels with Cover-equivalent Infrastructure basically ignore core assumptions of the ephemeral being mechanics like Essence bleed or needing to Manifest" can be inferred, and some of the things the God-Machine can do to buy the loyalty of mortal cogs (and potentially underline the ways it undesirably maintains the status quo) without deploying magical methods can superficially resemble the means by which demonic pacts move resources around, to say nothing of the intentional surface ambiguity between demons, angels, and exiles that has prompted more than one observation that angels should probably use more of the same systems as demons.

    All of this is to say I'm starting this thread with the main goal of assembling a new framework for the powers of the Unchained to try and better integrate the various elements of their toolkit, the toolkit of their loyalist kin, the toolkit of less direct extensions of the God-Machine, and the effects of Infrastructure and occult matrices more generally. This is a big undertaking and I can't guarantee I'll be able to get much done with it, but if I can set a workable foundation and get past my impulses as a serial overdesigner I hope to make something usable.

    Beginning from the basics: enough parts in this machine use directly or indirectly scaling levels of effect that I think it warrants reintroducing dot levels to the power system, even if not all categories use all levels. Borrowing conceptually from Mage and Deviant as well as Demon's own existing systems for balancing pacts, it helps adjudicate what a gadget with certain powers can do, gives particular bargaining weight to a given use of occult power for the purposes of pacts, lends a sense of how much Aether a given bit of Infrastructure might be putting out, provides a ready avenue for Incarnation to provide bonuses, and so on. A simple moderate/major power dichotomy for Embeds and Exploits respectively leaves a lot more wiggle room in a framework likened to comprehensible systems through the phenomenon of techgnosis.

    I'm not sure if the four Embed categories necessarily survive this reframe fully intact or get changed out for something new, especially given how little practical impact they have in Demon 1e, but there should definitely still be a means of arranging the God-Machine's subroutines for the sake of bookkeeping and mechanical hooks. The breadth of concepts in play for Cacophony, Instrumental, Mundane, and Vocal Embeds, if preserved, might warrant subcategories or one or more extra axes of differentiation that could link up with systems for Keys, catalysts, and more rarefied effects, or give a little more mechanical rigor to using an Embed as the basis for developing an Exploit or a near-field gadget. (There's a tricky row to hoe between "the God-Machine clearly has broad categories of missions its angelic assignments fall into" and "there being four Incarnations is demonic superstition and the fifth Incarnation has a solid case for there technically being innumerable subcategories that make Incarnation not a terribly useful classification scheme.")

    The portrayal of angels has some ambiguity and evolution over the line's runtime, such that there's contradictory examples of how unique a given loyalist is and how they're used and disposed of, but at bare minimum it probably suffices to say that angels (and exiles) should be built more like the Unchained with extra resources rather than as a wholly separate type of entity, and demons should likewise probably have more definite mechanics for glitches that should align with ways in which angelic Bans and Banes function. Since the goal of this thread is not to rebuild the ephemeral being mechanics as well, we're limited to figuring out how to mesh a system designed for abbreviated NPC statblocks used by the ST with statblocks equivalent to those of full PCs — that might mean connecting Influence ratings to access to Embeds by dot rating and/or category, converting PFR stat spreads to nine-Attribute spreads while undercover, and linking Aetheric output to comparative subtlety in ways that echo demonic form mechanics.

    There's a fair amount of systems for connecting to Infrastructure and influencing the output of occult matrices, as well as for linking up with other demons or pouring Aether into objects (and creatures), and between that and the Cipher encouraging demons to take dramatic action around things relating to the God-Machine (as well as the recurring plot hook of the Machine seemingly acting against its own interests or demons and exiles attempting to create or become new God-Machines) it probably bears consideration for demons studying or suborning Infrastructure to be able to use it to enhance their own abilities, develop new abilities, or otherwise escalate the plot toward their vision of Hell (or at least get a glimpse of the Machine's big-picture plans for the area). Boltholes, lambdas, cults, facilities, and pacts could all serve as mechanisms for trying to recreate Infrastructure that doesn't require the God-Machine's attention to set up, and a more elaborate version of the Cipher could function as the root repository of secret principles that let a demon sow the seeds of their agenda.

    A page of the character sheet allowing an individual demon to basically upscale themself into a Deviant-style conspiracy with holdings and agents would potentially help with comparisons to the God-Machine in this approach (something similar could be done for modeling Vampire's institutional corruption as well, but this ain't about them), but given that Deviant already balances the inclusion of the most elaborate and antagonist-based form of the organization mechanics with a core template that has the least innate abilities to track it probably stands to reason for such an approach to reduce some of the complexity of the Unchained template — making use of the Resilient Cover system from the Storyteller's Guide in some form, perhaps, or treating separate Covers as assets within the conspiracy framework based on the demon's Primum.

    This post has gotten fairly long and I doubt I'm going to get this whole thing sorted out in one night, so does anybody have any thoughts or questions to help with this pursuit?

      Unfortunately I only read fully the core and has been done time, ao I'm not completely remembering how everything works, but I think we can start by organizing different systems by bullet points to be more visually clear, and discuss what a Demon should have in conceptual terms.

      As states, your project seems more akin to a deep rewrite of the systems while keeping with the spirit and overall goals than just a polishing of the game, even if this rewrite is for a list of particular modules rather than the core system of the splat.

      One thing I think that have to be done is to get rid of Angels as Ephemeral Beings. That works in a mortals-facing game because reduces the amount of specific systems to be made, but as we introduce Demons and Exiles this isn't the ideal anymore. I'm not saying they necessarily need full stats, but they need a more proper set of rules and metaphysical positioning as highly advanced automatons that remain invisible as an effect of design rather than being made of a substance as indistinct as Ephemera.

      I understand the matter of organization rules, but we have to be careful. Despite being a spy game, Demon is way more personal in literary approach than Deviant. The themes are about identity and paranoia on personal relationships, life within the Cover, espionage as experienced by the spy, and in parallel to the Cold War they work for and against powers too much greater and, in contrast to their plight, impersonal. The God-Machine isn't any simple Conspiracy, and its servants form Conspiracies it can form and dissolve without concern as it works towards greater schemes.

      Deviant pits the players against way smaller organizations that operate in a personal level, they don't want the PC on principle, but the PC specifically, and the PC on turn isn't trying to be part of a greater movement, but understand and dismantle it fully. Those Conspiracies are more strictly defined, as is their demise and the conditions to make it happen. This is not just a result of rules, but a direct consequence of a game based on Cold War spy series and other based on superhero and pulp media like Hulk, Wolverine, and most Kamen Rider seasons.

      What do we need to define about God-Machine tech and how they operate regardless of side? Some ideas:

      - Hardware and materials, what they can do as machines.

      - Software and Perception, what they can do as "AIs".

      - Hacks and Tinkering, what they can do due to knowledge of reality as a system.

      - Cover and Relationships, how they integrate in reality to execute their functions.

      - Fuel and Sources, what they run on and where can it be obtained.

      - Pacts and Souls, programming metaphysical properties through Information Theory.

      - Gods and Realms, techgnosticism is interface with the cosmos, divinity, entities and worlds.

      - Infrastructure and Matrices, how they relate to the operational processes of the God-Machine.

          Another ramble, in no particular order and sort of jumping from topic to topic:

          The God-Machine benefits in some ways from comparisons to Vampire (including Radio Sickness being one of the earliest crossover components for Demon-adjacent phenomena (besides an angel impersonating a qashmal in Detroit), as it helps to establish even without the consistent ability to meddle in other realms it can get a lot done in the material world by operating in places people stay away from and manipulating mortal memory and perception (whether magically or culturally/psychologically).

          Regardless of the degree to which the mechanics for angels are adjusted, it definitely feels important to keep "angels and exiles run on Essence and cannot process Aether without means of doing so that are not part of their standard kit." To a lesser extent, the relative commonality of Discorporate as a Manifestation and "cannot leave their worksite" as a category of Ban feel salient to their ties to Infrastructure and facilities as concrete things in the material world.

          On the other end of things, the ability of Embeds and Exploits (and certain angel-exclusive Numina and by extension some of the God-Machine's hacks and projects) have the notably uncommon ability to directly affect abstract or conceptual targets, like social interactions or investigations or relationships, rather than needing to go through concrete vectors like most other monsters' powers. Part of the God-Machine's magical preeminence is theoretically owed to its capacity for grand alterations to reality through mastery of the natural (and supplemented) laws of physics et al.

          The God-Machine may be bigger than any single conspiracy (and historically the cults it maintains involvement with tend to be small and assembled for specific projects), but the interconnected nature of Infrastructure means it's definitely comparable to the Web of Pain in terms of linking together a series of assets with particular focuses and points of vulnerability.

          Given that angelic Rank is directly proportional to importance to the God-Machine, if we are to change the representation of angels out of the ephemeral being systems, a version of the Web of Pain mechanics that gives a Standing-equivalent trait to each Node as a measure of power and attached protections/beneficiaries/responses to meddling might be a solid way of handling them, particularly given the comparison one could make between exiles and abandoned Nodes.

          I personally feel it benefits the goal of this approach to have a view of "the God-Machine" as a series of pieces that connect with each other and potentially alter the rules of the world at various degrees of scope; much of the Machine's work involves creating backdoors, handholds, or vulnerabilities in a given system or population, and Flowers of Hell's systems for hacking Infrastructure make it clear that the Unchained can do the same to the Machine itself.

          In some ways adjusting Embeds, Exploits, and gadgets is peripheral to this, since most of their effects are not geographically constrained as the ostensible products of global adjustments to reality that anyone with the right awareness can make use of, but things like the theory that the automatic demonic capacity for living languages is the product of obscure Infrastructure in the deep Shadow and the statement that Cover is technically maintained by a sort of internal Infrastructure (whose damaging or lack of maintenance leads to discernible glitches) suggest both a high-level pursuit for characters seeking Hell and another vector to plug the systems for pacts into.

          Cover has a consistent list of facets of identity that it includes (sex, gender, ethnicity, socioeconomic class), incorporates the equivalent of Integrity breaking points that include both basic descriptions (e.g. adhering to the image of a "strict corporate boss") and the more conventional sources of moral injury, and can be modified through pactwork in ways that can add new vulnerabilities (losing things that gave you Cover Experiences is itself a compromise) and create new Covers altogether (and a Cover formed from patchwork is inherently less durable below a certain threshold of development than one created wholesale or from a real person). Angels' connection to the Machine manages their upkeep, and exiles being Abandoned opens them up to the possibility of becoming a sliver whose Cover is nonsensical or nonexistent.

          Looping back around to the first point in this set, a lot of Embeds work by creating or magnifying opportunities for things to go a certain way, discerning significant information through small details, or exploiting quirks of human psychology or beliefs about the way the world works — they function well as spy tools by virtue of their ability to go unnoticed, be explained away, or misdirect the most likely witnesses. Others make use of the God-Machine's fundamental medium by tweaking concrete or abstract structures and manipulating meaning, which bridges the previous element (since humans and humanlike entities in Chronicles are the main things concerned with meaning and symbolism) with the fact that Infrastructure on the whole is a connected series of large-scale magical workings

          Bringing it all together, the way the God-Machine works means deriving a unified system for powers it and its loyal and rogue processes use necessitates a bit of settling on the nature of experiential reality in Demon, which, while not providing a definite answer to what the Machine is, does suggest a line of descent from individual powers (which are further limited when expressed through personal-scale gadgets and may be expanded in scope through occult matrices) to foundational adjustments of the sort that produce those powers.

          Some of these powers threaten the users' secrecy, status, or identity by diverging from the system's safe or authorized limits (these often being high-energy outputs like Exploits, supercharged Embeds, Incepts, or Embeds that directly mess with the Machine itself), but most such powers are the domain of the Machine's direct products, with cryptids and ordinary stigmatics being limited to more stable expressions of the Machine's occult nature; normal humans either are not involved or get thirdhand access to their effects through the outputs of occult matrices, participation in reality under the Machine's stewardship, risking becoming stigmatic through use of gadgets, or becoming demon-blooded Offspring or Covers for demons through pacts.

          The overall goal here is to make the mechanical representation of "the God-Machine" (here referring both to the entity/entities whose directives Infrastructure is created in service of and the system demons derive their established and novel abilities from) something that can be explored and exploited by demons (and others) at their peril, whether in pursuit of their Cipher or simply to secure a more specific desired outcome; a large amount of the current mechanics are left to fiat or non-mechanical negotiation in a way that Deviant's conspiracy mechanics or even Geist's Remembrance mechanics or Changeling's Icon mechanics provide a more tangible approach to.

          The Cipher being a non-central element of Descent means this proposed deeper mechanical layer can be ignored by players if they prefer, especially for demons seeking to avoid detection through obscurity and simply try to live a mostly-normal life, but as a means of managing the plans the Machine has for a given chronicle it still feels like a useful tool to develop for Storytellers and players of ambitious demons.

              I don't think Angels need Essence. What they need is to not operate in the same energy as Demons, or at least in the same form of this energy. Angels have to rely on their workplace, where their source is already placed by the God-Machine.

              That could be a number of things. For one, they could need direct authorization to use a source in its standard operation. Angels don't feed on free Aether, only on the fuel addressed to them. To do otherwise wouldn't require much, but will trigger a Descent.

              Keep in mind that even when Demons use a source that's physically appropriate, they're still hacking it, this is not access through proper cryptographic protocols.

              Now, the Web of Pain idea has merit, but let's refine. The God-Machine doesn't need those tools to last or interact directly, and they can't rely only in those. We need this Web to be able to include Cults and Conspiracies, but also to track other assets that may be reused or need maintenance/recycling. It also must track Infrastructure. If we get that, we can say this works as an effective map of the God-Machine at some level.

              Most of the mechanical difference between the Angels and others may come from this Web or Divine Map. Instead of giving them different rules, they have access to Actions related to their connections to other Pieces, something Demons can't despite knowing how to. And the Angel-as-Piece can be our simplified stat block in the end: not because the Angel is structurally distinct, but because their individual properties are secondary to their Integration with God.

                  Angels rely on their workplace for power through the fact that they are potent pieces of the God-Machine and the God-Machine's most potent endeavors depend on the conversion of Essence into Aether. The God-Machine converts Essence into Aether because Essence is the energy of the material world and that's where God-Machine's artificially-enforced power is, which is a thematic statement whose potential mechanical complications are limited by the feeding restrictions of anything else that can siphon Essence and the obscurity of its normal operations.

                  "The God-Machine is a superstructural parasite on the world, and its manipulations are clear to those who have not been made to think of them as normal" is within spec for an entity that exploits the energies of the material world in a way that is less than totally efficient and has chaotic and/or deleterious effects when its oversight is lacking. Some angels are more mobile than others and it does not help them to be geographically confined in ways that are more specific than "preferably they should be within reach of Infrastructure."

                  Demons get their power from waste/byproducts of the Machine's workings and are thus empowered by its imperfect presence in the world and encouraged to engineer more wasteful uses of its resources; the primary published means of allowing angels to use Aether as fuel are a piece of Infrastructure from the Quick Start story powered by captured demons and the Economy Incept (which has a poor conversion rate because its main effect is adding a fuel surcharge to any supernatural powers targeting the angel). The DSG establishes that the Infrastructure Condition can reduce the Essence cost of angels' Numina in the same chapter that introduces a number of powerful angel-exclusive Numina with high Essence costs and an Incept whose only effect is reducing the Essence costs of other angelic powers.

                  Particular mechanics aside, the demonic form replicates its former angelic feats by marshaling the energies that produced the type of end results the Machine burned more natural fuel to get — there might be something to be said here about using magical sympathy to leverage the presence of smoke and ash to make fire by implying a sacrifice, but regardless of analogy the Unchained achieve some of the same effects as their loyalist brethren by duplicating the middle step between spending normal fuel and activating powers.

                  This specific difference in fuel sources and their provenance is as salient to the game as Plasm coming from ghostly Manifestations is in Geist — it directly informs demonic activity and supplies the perverse incentive that makes direct confrontation with the God-Machine's agents in their places of power easier to escalate in ways that might go in the Unchained' favor by virtue of destabilizing delicate arrangements.

                  Aether is an impersonal energy that needs buffers around it because it's transformative in ways that disagree with conventional reality, and while the specific form of the God-Machine's waste heat disposal can vary they're almost universally things the Machine sequesters or disposes of rather than carting it off to an angel who is authorized to use it; while the nature of things in Chronicles is sufficiently "mysterious ways" that one could arbitrarily declare the Universal Garbage Disposal Angel a nonviable form it feels ludonarratively tidier for the explanation to be "the God-Machine doesn't have much direct use for post-process Aether and widespread dedicated disposal (or transportation to less common centralized disposal) would be more trouble than it cares for."


                  A thing that is perhaps relevant for the Web approach is the angelological description of an "angel" as anything that carries out a mission for God, with the specific example from Descent being Y2K magically and manually preventing the Y2K Bug from occurring in technology post-1999. Angels and cults and conspiracies may have actions they can carry out, but they can also produce outputs that are then moved to other parts of the network or serve as support for other parts of the Machine (Infrastructure as a whole being both nested and interconnected, some of this may be managed through similar systems to Privileges or moving Icons from the Devoted Companion).

                  The breadth of potential Covers from the Machine (and Exploits like Demon House or phenomena like Lares) suggest that even things in the Web that aren't mechanically angels still benefit from tagging in a way that demons can also exploit, whether through modeling the ability to maintain multiple Covers or the Conditions from hacking Infrastructure from Flowers of Hell.

                  This does potentially have the risk of turning the web from "the God-Machine" into "a net of general assets, which the God-Machine has fingers in," but I do feel like the combined principles of "it's easier to steal something unattended (whether you engineered that lack of attention or not) than to create it from scratch" and "angels, demons, and exiles all have some level of functionality as big gears in the framework of reality that the Machine operates on, whether loyal, abandoned, or rogue, and messing with tags and assets in semi-defined ways allows for truthful or falsified movement between these statuses" is useful for the mid-level view of the Map.


                  We know from Flowers that Infrastructure can inadvertently produce Embedded gadgets as well as allowing the creation of more potent and unusual gadgets that can store Aether even if they aren't Exploited. Restoration facilities can remove glitches from demons and long-term exposure to facilities in general may reduce a demon's Primum, which serves as their independent connection to reality apart from the Machine as well as their primary determiner of how severe their glitches can get; overclocking a gadget with Aether provides (usually) temporary increases in effectiveness in exchange for permanent drawbacks that require repairs or conceptual replacement to get rid of, and gadgets made from pieces of Infrastructure can only be repaired in Infrastructure.

                  The way Interlocks work currently involves connecting the principles of different Embeds to each other in ways that are partially facilitated by the Cipher providing hints whenever the demon earns Beats in proximity to the workings of the God-Machine.

                  Going loud grants most Incarnations broad access to the principles of all Embeds in a category as well as all Exploits for the scene, and Analysts gain the ability to create Interlock-like combination effects out of Exploits in place of greater access to Embeds. In addition to burning an entire Cover and gaining the Hunted Condition, going loud also gives the character the longest duration for a non-permanent glitch, the greatest odds of being the most severe type of glitch, and the possibility of getting an emanation glitch even if their normal Primum isn't strong enough to support superhuman traits.

                  While obviously a whole Creative Thaumaturgy-like system is beyond the scope of how Embeds and Exploits work as common powers, it may be prudent to come up with something somewhat akin to how Blood Sorcery: Sacraments and Blasphemies put together some general principles from which blood sorcery rituals work but for Embeds, with existing Embeds reverse-engineered into a framework that can also be used to put together combined effects for Interlocks or aberrant outcomes that step into the domain of Exploits (which may in turn be used for gadget creation, including near-field effects and lambdas). Partially unifying systems for glitches from compromise and drawbacks from overclocking gadgets may further help mesh those parts of the system.


                  Cover and pacts are already partially integrated with each other by virtue of how Cover is most expediently gained, but the scope of pacts is notably wider than the corebook's systems and in some ways brings us back to that potential stumbling block with the Web — a lot of aspects of mundane life are potentially up for trade, and balancing the books involves weighting things that include concepts and whole bodily elements as well as skills, possessions, and relationships.

                  Primum being the described vehicle for the enforcement of pacts at least serves as a solid explanation for why angels don't have access to them directly, as well as feeding into the bit where demons might be independent fledgling God-Machines themselves, and that in turn seems like it would hook nicely into the "abstract angels" layer of the web, where pacts are one vector for adding to or modifying the person-scale holdings of a demon-as-conspiracy, with integrating one's Cover (or demonic form) into Infrastructure physically or relationally being the route to more magically potent acquisitions. Exiles of sufficient power nominally have access to the later means of ascension even while limited to a single Cover at most.

                  Pacts and gadgets in some ways have the loosest interaction as subsystems — a bill of sale is a quasi-pact that's built somewhat like a gadget, gadgets can be provided as benefits to the signatory of a pact, and using pact documents as gadget hardware is both easier and can modify or obviate the benefits of the pact, but that's basically all of their overlap in the line so far.

                  Given both the potential scope of magical documentation and the line's periodic references to risks of breach of contract that aren't really given much teeth, we can probably stand to expand the scope of assets a demon can provide via pacts to include Embeds and Exploits they know, especially since both systems use Willpower dots to model significant permanent investments (and, should the "Embeds all use Finesse Attributes and Exploits all use Power Attributes" convention be preserved, Resolve + Composure setting the upper limit on how much a demon can weight a pact in their own favor completes the trifecta).
