The cranes were situated outside of the general warehouse because of the height differential and for safety purposes. There were six out there along with precut girders at any one time for a given job and lifts to move them as well. Two big wheeler trucks flanking each side of the warehouse.
        The fences were about 2.14 meters in height with razor wire up top and 186 square meters across with a half meter brick lining the ground immediately behind the fencing. Keypad with speaker for incoming vehicles and swipe card readers for employees.
        "You don't find it a little peculiar that we've hit no lights and have not seen a single car? Keep your attention more on the road."
