    My main contention isn't that they aren't human emotions, it's that they are and this is on purpose. It doesn't doesn't matter if the Demon can or can not feel "guilt" - putting aside that demons do feel real emotions, and often have to hide them - my point wasn't "your demon fell for humans so here are human emotions;" my point was "your Demon successfully went through a Compromise and passed for being a normal human; here's a human condition to help with the charade."

    Compromises are the equivalent of a Breaking Point, right? So on a success, Demon's get mortal Conditions because they are passing for mortal, and can therefore pass it off as a Breaking Point instead of a Compromise. It's a success in every sense.

    As for my example of hurting a human; well, sure, there are plenty of demons who wouldn't feel bad. That was kind of not the point, either, though; the point was that you'd feel "guilty" (which is different than feeling bad) doesn't have to be related to the human in question. I kind of regret adding that "flesh-person" bit now; it distracts from my main point, which is that a Demon should feel guilty when they realize they're acting like God.
    You know, such as being callous with a human life. You can always have it be different for different Demons, but as a baseline, I think it's kind of brilliant.

"I feel guilty/spooked/shaken because doing that almost blown my cover".
