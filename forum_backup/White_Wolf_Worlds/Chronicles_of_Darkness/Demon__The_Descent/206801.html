    A.I. Overlap - Trends in the Focuses of Former Tools of the Demiurge

    07-24-2014, 03:29 AM
    Three A.M. here and I can't sleep. Figured I'd start a thread for discussing/analyzing the ways the Incarnations and Agendas hook together. Accordingly, in the name of making this first post quick, I suppose I'll start with what I've found to be loosely the most direct correlations:

    Destroyer/Saboteur - Practically goes without saying, but the Agenda's most popular philosophy of Hell is right in the strike zone of the Swords, their Condition's resolution effect has thematic overlap with the literal purviews of Cacophony Embeds (one of which is literally the root word of the Agenda's name), and both of their alternative sobriquets evoke physical actors (when Physical Attributes are called out as almost never the tertiary category for Destroyers).

    Psychopomp/Tempter - Only marginally less obvious than Soldier/Sword, but the Agenda's focus of either building Hell on Earth or finding it in some distant part of the universe resonates with the Wheels' initial function and one of their most common catalysts, their Condition's encouragement of using proxies and treating people as things speaks to the Incarnation's less overt programming quirks, and isn't it just so curious how the Agenda that's described as "quintessential demons" fits so well with the Incarnation whose typical Attribute focus makes them the most broadly competent at using baseline demonic powers?

    Guardian/Inquisitor - Here we get a little more oblique; the Incarnation's most consistent features are adaptability and perceptive acumen, and the blurb for Instrumental Embeds likewise pegs the Guardians as "highly analytical." Meanwhile, the Agenda's less flattering nickname is almost directly the name of a common catalyst for the Shields, and the Watchers' general attitude toward retaining connections fits well with the Incarnation's tendency toward small numbers of focused relationships.

    Messenger/Integrator - In brief: the Incarnation's affinity Embeds include (as of Flowers) two that specifically address the God-Machine's presence or plans, the Agenda's most palatable baseline goal concerns reprogramming the Machine, the Trumpets being the best method-actors suits the Traitors' Cipher well, the Incarnation has the smallest proportion of example catalysts, the Agenda's penchant for proselytizing is a natural fit for the Messengers, and (for the aesthetically-inclined among them) the Trumpets are the primary subset of angels whose function includes their form.

    Thoughts?

              I feel the need to swap Messenger and Psychopomp, oddly enough.

              Messenger/Tempter: Your divine mission, your entire purpose in this world, was to tell people what to do and expect to have it done. That power over others is wholly uninteresting as an Angel, but given free will? It becomes positively delicious. People are easy to manipulate, and you can get them to dance with a look and a whispered word. How far can you push it? What's the line that others wouldn't cross for you? You're a god of manipulation, and you intend to do whatever the hell you want with your gifts.

              Psychopomp/Integrator: Everything has a purpose. Or perhaps, everything had a purpose; you understand what little pushes it took to get the exact result you wanted, and you were privy to the divine mechanisms of the universe. The world is nothing but gears to you, all in their neat little places, and you have lost yours. What does a builder do without plans? What does a cog do when not a piece of the whole? You miss having these answers, and would give anything to return to that perfect unity.

              And another.

              Guardian/Saboteur: Something (or someone) was under your protection, and you knew nothing other than keeping to that duty. Whatever caused you to Fall, it showed you the imperfection of the Machine; it's nothing less than a threat to the world you now inhabit, and that is simply unacceptable. Maybe there's someone you need to keep safe at any cost from the predations of your creator, or maybe all of humanity is under your auspices; maybe you don't want to protect anything but your own neck and think the best defense is a good offense.

                              Upon reflection, the following two make a little more sense as "most-natural fits":

                              Messenger/Inquisitor: The Incarnation writeup notes that the Trumpets tend to be a bit paranoid about meaning, information brokers drawing from the same stock as information gatherers-and-disseminators is a sensible basis, Vocal Embeds keying off of communication/revelation/realization and the Agenda Condition's resolution giving information both work on similar logic to An Eye For Disorder and Cacophony Embeds on Destroyers, and the Agenda Condition's Beat criterion suits "agents of change" quite well.

                              Guardian/Integrator: Second smallest proportion of example catalysts after Messengers, arguably the least classically demonic type of demon (preservation doesn't resonate quite as much as change, creation, or destruction), most likely to have been assigned to work complementarily with other angels, combination suits the Saboteur/Integrator conflict and the painting of Swords and Shields as "opposite numbers," the Incarnation's missions suit the tenets of foundational Integrationism, and Instrumental Embeds dealing with timing/precision/utility and material objects suits interaction with the Machine (particularly given the "open question" about Download Knowledge and the fact that Flowers gives the Guardians what technically functions as an angel-summoning Embed). More tenuously, a lot of Defensive Infrastructure being little more than delivery systems for Guardian angels bears some focus.

                                              Well, since others posted Psychopomps working as Tempters and Integrators, I figured I'd try to write short analyses/blurbs for the other 2 Agendas.

                                              Psychopomp/Saboteur: there is no machine so flawless that it can't be taken down with enough understanding and precision. Not even the God-Machine. The Linchpins of Infrastructures are a testament to this truth. Guardians may have an easier time with handling machinery or sensing opportunities for attack with their Instrumental Embeds, sure. But even if you can see bullets coming and send them back by messing around with probability, that won't do you much good if the enemy can do the same. You need to find out what allows them to counter your every move, and you can't do that by kicking down the front door and shooting everyone. You need to be subtle. An intercepted message here, a false lead there. Soon enough, the important bits are exposed for a moment while the enemy looks for something that doesn't exist. Infiltrate and rip it out the Linchpin. Don't forget to have multiple escape routes and a way to erase your trail. Even if they have big guns, they can't kill you if they don't know where you went.

                                              Psychopomp/Inquisitor: what do you think of when you hear the word "Inquisitor"? Someone who sees turncoats everywhere and has ways of making you talk? That might be the case for some of them who prefer dramatic interrogations over efficient ones. The Inquisitors that the Unchained are truly paranoid about are those who don't even need to make their presence known. They just gather whatever they can about you and move co-workers and family away from you, one by one. For the sake of your Cover, you can't just stay here. While you are away, be it in a hospital visit or casino party, they discover your secrets while protecting theirs. No lives lost, no Angels alerted, just unimpeded and perfect movement of all the pieces.

                                                  With the basic blurbs covered, I figured I'd roll over to look at some of the multifaceted combinations.

                                                  Messenger/[Saboteur/Integrator]: Input affects output. That goes beyond Basic and into the realm of Machine Code. You were incarnated to make adjustments to living things, and available evidence indicates that you, your angelic brethren, your demonic comrades, and the Machine that made the lot are all Living Creatures that rely on outside information to direct internal thought processes. Humans are the same way, of course, but you've seen what happens to them when either God or the Unchained gain enough of an edge to go viral, and that prospect terrifies you. So you fudge the numbers on both sides wherever necessary and hope you can cover your tracks well enough for the conflict to reach a quiet resolution before this four-faced free-agency of yours does you in.

                                                          Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                          It's tangential, but does the prospect of changing which Embed set Going Loud opens up to be based on Agenda (a la Self-Destruct) rather than Incarnation seem ill-advised to anyone?
                                                          Here's the thing - when you Go Loud, you're not really doing it for getting access to a specific Embed. Embeds, by nature, are necessary subtle powers. Going Loud is anything but subtle. Sure, there might be an exception or three with generally useful powers (why don't you have them already?), but generally speaking, Embeds aren't a big deal in this situation. You want the Primum 10 boost and Exploits.

                                                          There are three big changes in this case. The first is that you are effectively throwing out Incarnations in their entirety. Yes, you have that single Embed or Exploit you have to take at char gen, but its effect is minimal. You're taking something you what for your concept anyways. The second is that it -really- hurts the Uncalled. Not only are you not getting an Agenda bonus, but no affinity Embeds when Going Loud.

                                                          The only ill-advised aspect I can think of is when someone starts joining multiple Agendas. Then, we get affinity for all of the Embeds. But, even then, Embeds aren't that big of a deal. Everyone would possibly want affinity for Cacaphony embeds for the combat utility (its a risk when G.L.), but its not really that big of an issue when we're talking about a 2exp difference between buying the combat Embeds and have them all the time versus buying the 2 dot merit for affinity.

                                                          So, no. Not a big deal at all.


                                                          Originally posted by Xenesis View Post
                                                          I can't forsee that ever being a real gamebreaking issue and it solves the "analyst problem" nicely.
                                                          Its not a problem, its a feature. The whole point of playing an Analyst is that you're deliberately breaking the mold from the usual Incarnations. The only defining feature of the Incarnations is the affinity for one of the four kinds of Embeds. If you give an Analyst affinity Embeds, then you're no longer playing an Analyst. Which defeats the whole point of playing an Analyst in the first place. May as well play one of the usual incarnations.
