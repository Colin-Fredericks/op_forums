    I love the idea of demons being able to make hellish minions and this exploit is something I thought of for fun. I would be greatful for any feed back on how I can make it better.

    Horror of Hell
    The fires of hell just don’t destroy but sometimes it creates creatures from the darkest nightmares the universe can imagine. A demon can release the fire of hell that burns with in them to twist and flesh and bring a new horror into the world.

    Example Prerequisites: (Can’t think of any)
    Dice Pool: Intelligence + Occult + Primum vs. Stamina or
    Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance
    Action: Instant
    Cost: Aether = to the Horrors Potency
    Roll Results
    Dramatic Failure: The subject is not only burned by the fires of hell but the demons cover is burned by the fires also corrupting their cover. The Demon Gets 1 Permanent CA strophic Glitch of the GM’s Choosing. The Subject is turned into a random horror of a random potency and theme.
    Failure: The target is not afflicted.
    Success: The fires of hell corrupt the subject. The Demon has can give a general idea of what they are trying to create but the process random. For Example, the demon can be looking to create a Succubus from classic myth. The horror could them come out just as they image or more than likely a creature of lust who eats away the sexual drives of everyone around them rather than making everyone becoming more lustful.

    This Exploit does not, however, impart any particular inclination to serve the demon or the God Machine; that inclination must come naturally.

    Exceptional Success: The Demon has complete control over what the horror becomes.

                This is an exploit I've been pondering on for a while, partially for gadgets, partially because I've not found an Exploit that can do something like this. I'm not sure how balanced it is, but I'm definitely open to critique on it.

                Compression Elimination
                Molecules are the building blocks of life, set in all kinds of patterns that give various properties to the substances they compose. The God-Machine has long since learned how to manipulate them in strange ways, not the least of which reducing the distance between these molecules. Demons, meanwhile, have learned how not only how to use this process but how to reverse it.

                This exploit may be used on people, inanimate objects and animals, including other Unchained. The size difference is small but, as the saying goes, every inch helps.

                Example Prerequisites: In My Pocket, Quick Change
                Dice Pool: Intelligence + Science + Primum vs. (if contested) Stamina + Supernatural Resistance OR Durability
                Action: Instant
                Cost: 2 Aether + 1 to only affect the core target.
                Roll Results:
                Dramatic Failure: Rather than feeling crushed, the target feels invigorated, stronger than ever, their entire being fortified. The target gains two dots of Armor as their molecular structure , as well as a +1 bonus to any rolls requiring Strength. This effect lasts a
                Failure: The target remains their current size.
                Success: The target is subjected to a brief glow, before a crushing sensation comes over them. The Demon selects one of the results below. If the target is garbed, the Demon may pay an additional Aether to only affect the core target itself. A package may strip its wrapping, while a guard may find herself tripping over her own uniform; if the garb is inflexible, it may cause the target some harm. The exploit lasts for the scene, although the Demon can end the effect early.
                * Expansion: The target expands to a new size, gaining 1 Size. If the target is a living being, they gain the benefits (and detriments) of the Giant merit for the duration of the exploit. If the target is an inanimate object, it gains 1 Durability, adding 2 to the object’s Structure; a melee weapon may gain a +1 modifier to its damage. When the target returns to their normal size, if the temporary health box has damage, upgrade the leftmost health box as though the target had taken new damage (this cannot be mitigated by merits).
                * Compression: The target compresses, losing 1 Size. If the target is a living being, they lose their rightmost healthbox for the duration of the exploit but gain the benefits (and pitfalls) of the Small Framed merit. Any damage within that health box remains when the target returns to full size. If the target is an inanimate object, it loses 1 Structure, minimum 1.
                Exceptional Success:
                The Demon finds a quantum sweet spot, allowing them to be more selective with their changes.
                * The target is penalized by -2 for Athletics or Brawl rolls as their limbs are made too large or small to effectively use. Alternatively, the target suffers the Arm or Leg Wrack tilt, their limbs painfully crushed or bloated around their skeleton. Notably, this cannot target organs nor break bones.
                * The Demon may pay an additional three aether to permanently alter the target’s entire size, effectively granting (or removing) the Giant or Small Framed merit. This effect may only be applied to a normal Success result.

                            Follow the Leader (Vocal)
                            People are herd animals inclined to go alone with the group or an authority figure. This Embed reinforces that drive and designates who the authority figure is. The Demon activates this Embed after the primary target takes an action. Each target lower in the Initiative order who fails to resist copies the primary target's action (exactly, if applicable) for their own this turn.
                            Dice Pool: Manipulation + Politics vs Composure
                            Action: Reflexive
                            Roll Result
                            Dramatic Failure: All secondary targets in range do whatever the Demon doesn't want.
                            Failure: Those unaffected continue to do whatever they decide would be best.
                            Success: Secondary targets that don't resist duplicate the same action as the primary target for their action that turn.
                            Exceptional Success: The Demon can grant either the Inspired (as they rally around the decisive leadership), or the Disoriented (representing confusion and broken command structure) Condition to the secondary targets, albeit it fades without Resolution after one Turn.
                            [Comments: This is for spreading out actions, such as if one enemy attacks the wrong target, getting their buddies to do so too, or triggering mass retreat, or conscripting back to the Demon's own actions.]

                                Brimstone With Treacle (Instrumental)
                                To the God-Machine, changing matter from one substance to another is child’s play, both on a small and a large scale. Though it takes Demons far more effort to do anything close to that, some have found liquids to be easier to influence. This embed can only be used on liquids that are contained in some way, without anything entering or leaving the vessel, and on less than a liter in volume. The Demon must touch the vessel, or the liquid itself, before activating the embed. If the liquid being changed has few if any impurities, the storyteller may evoke a -2 penalty to the activation of the embed.
                                Dice Pool: Wits + Science
                                Action: Standard
                                Roll Results:
                                Dramatic Failure: The liquid becomes into foul, bubbling sludge, not unlike molasses in consistency. Treat the liquid as having a toxicity rating equal to half the Demon’s Primum (rounded down), should anyone try to drink it. An hour after forming, the sludge hardens into a brittle, smelly lump.
                                Failure: The beverage remains the same; the Demon may try again in the same turn with a -1 penalty.
                                Success: The liquid changes, various impurities being transformed into another substance. However, the Demon cannot make anything new in the liquid - she is only reducing the ratio of different substances. If the Demon is aware of a particular impurity, be it a chemical, poison or simply a flavoring they don’t enjoy, they can target that solely and reduce or increase its level (or toxicity rating) up to their activation successes. Cheeky Demons are known to induce carbonation this way.
                                Exceptional Success: The Demon has far greater control over the beverage than they would otherwise, allowing them to change its contents entirely. Whether the liquid is spring water or crude oil, the Demon may make it into wine, honey, or even lace a previously safe drink with a drug or poison with a toxicity rating up to half their Primum (rounded down). A Demon with a catastrophic glitch may also use this opportunity to create a drink they are otherwise only able to consume, such as motor oil or battery acid. However, a Demon must be careful when using this effect, as the drink will visibly change color and opacity if the changes are overt.
                                [Comment: This is one I've been pondering on for a bit. The name is from the Mary Poppins stage show, the song "Brimstone and Treacle" - it gave me a lot of imagery for the eventual version of the embed here.]

                                    Little Drop of Poison
                                    In the spy game you have to watch out for poisons, drugs and so on. A demon with this embed cause his body to burn through drugs and poisons before they can do real harm to their cover.

                                    Dice Pool: Wits + Science or Med Action: Reflex Roll Results

                                    Dramatic Failure: The drug or poisons effect takes a stronger hold on the user rising its effect by the 2x

                                    Failure: Poison or drug takes effect

                                    Success: Drug or Poison takes no effect

                                    Exceptional Success: As Above, plus you now become immune to that drug or poison.

                                        Originally posted by Basic View Post
                                        Little Drop of Poison
                                        In the spy game you have to watch out for poisons, drugs and so on. A demon with this embed cause his body to burn through drugs and poisons before they can do real harm to their cover.
                                        While I like the idea of this Embed, I feel it's a tad too potent as is. It outright removes risk from a lot of scenarios, and while the Embed can fail it still grants instant relief and in the thick of combat.

                                        My suggestion would be to make the Success effect "Reduce the toxicity of a drug or poison in the Demon's system up to the Demon's activation successes; if there are multiple toxins, the Demon may split these successes to reduce each one individually. If the drug or poison is based on supernatural origins or methods, or is causing a supernatural effect, it evokes a Clash of Wills." This gives a bit more finesse to the Embed, especially if a Demon wants to only reduce a toxin in their system rather than outright remove it, but it doesn't prevent a Demon from using the Embed multiple times to clear the effect if they're not too bothered by it. It also gives some stress during combat, if a character is being actively poisoned by the enemy. This also applies to something like Vampire blood, which is like a drug but supernatural in origin.

                                        For an Exceptional Success, I'd go a slightly different route; "The Demon purges their system of any drugs or poisons inhabiting it, no matter how strong. If the Demon only had a single toxin running through them, they may also take the Toxic Resistance Condition (read below)."

                                        Toxic Resistance
                                        The human body has a habit of developing immunities to various toxins that manage to infiltrate it. Demons simply managed to find a way to expedite the process. Whenever the character is dosed with a particular drug or poison, treat the Toxicity rating as one lower than it normally would be. If the toxicity rating becomes zero, the character is immune to it. The character may have multiple versions of this condition, each for a specific drug or poison.
                                        Resolution: Shed this condition to become completely immune to the toxin for the rest of the scene.

                                        The reason I go for a condition instead of immunity for the scene (or outright immunity) is that I think it offers a bit more utility for the Demon without being too overpowered, while still allowing them a brief time of immunity should they need it. That said, I'm not sure if making a lingering effect is entirely appropriate for an Embed.
