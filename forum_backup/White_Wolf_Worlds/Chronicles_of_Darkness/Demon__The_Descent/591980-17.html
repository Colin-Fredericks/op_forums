                        The Demon corebook even has an example of this, although strangely enough, it's not for an angel, but the sample writeup of an intruder that might emerge from a dramatic failure at Raising Dead.

                        Although I'd muse that the Embeds which the Unchained develop derive as much from narrower exercises of the principles that allowed them Influence as angels. An Influence over Silence is going to be able to cover much more ground than the use of Hush, but it's expensive, only practical because it can draw on the support of Essence provided by infrastructure conditions and sacrifices. The fallen angel who reverse-engineers Hush from that knowledge develops a narrower ability from what fine manipulation she can work on her own, without that support from the God-Machine's labors.
