    Now... Angelic forms? I've had a LOT of fun with those. In my game, there is a recurring and separate Angelic antagonist referred to as "The Inevitable". He is a Destroyer angel whose mission is to kill the ring and has his own condition tied to how often he will appear to make things go bad. (As a result, he is excised from the normal Hunted and Surveilled conditions under the assumption that he doesn't report in until he gets a kill.)

    The Inevitable's description is as follows: This angelic being first appears as an intricately accurate human skeleton, save the bones are made of glimmering, polished chrome. Within the bone structures are grotesquely bolted organs, not in the correct order of a human; more than one heart, one lung, and otherwise distorted innards all grouped amongst grinding chrome gears lubricated by a thick, indigo oil.
    Across this skeletal chassis are uneven armoured plates that allow plenty of the inside to be seen, also gleaming chrome, and all over the angelic being's form are inscribed skittering, glowing blue angelic script that run in circuit patterns up and over it's body to culminate around the orbits of the skull. Within are spherical cameras, with bare mechanical iris, and from within behind the thin veneer of glass lay what appear to be two shining blue stars boiling with internal fusion.
    The creature observes the world pitilessly with an eternal broad grin of fleshy, pale and transparent gums holding very real human teeth and a mouth with stretched transparent muscles finished with a tongue of three vertical rotating blades.

    With one arm whose fingers ends in large gleaming hedge scissors and another which appears as some sort of long trumpet length filled with valves it is a wonder this creature can manipulate the world.
    Now the Inevitable is pretty interesting, I especially like the Shears of Atropos allusion as well as the Trumpet arm. What are some other angels that appear in your game. I know this thread is for Demonic forms, but hey, angels are the other side of the coin so I don't find it inappropriate lol

        Don't tell anyone that the Shears of Atropos allusion was unintentional. I am not going to claim credit for that to make myself look super duper clever. (But shhh, it's a secret.)

        The trumpet arm however was intentional. (Gabriel's Trumpet)

        Some more angels that appear in my game!

        I wrote up some stat-blocks for the hunter angels that spawn when a demon gains the Hunted (Persistent) condition.

        Rank 2 - G-Men.

        In their natural state G-Men are exactly 7' tall and are made of gleaming chrome. They are featureless figurines, like mannequins, with ball joints in their legs and arms being their only distinquishing bodily features. Their head is simply a chrome dome in the approximate shape of a human head, without a face, and usually engraved with the Enochian symbol for "Destroyer". Often these featureless bodies are clad in black, white collar business suits.

        Rank 3 - Greater G-Men

        Their primary difference is they are 9' tall and they have Enochian runes for "Greater Destroyer" on their faces.

        Another angel that made a brief appearance before the demons killed him in order to save a stigmatic was a man called "Mr. Smiley". This angel was a bizarre angel whom used a creepy-pasta as his occult matrix. The idea being that when someone fulfilled a certain criteria (they didn't re-post the message a certain number of times in defiance of the fanciful nature of it) that Mr. Smiley would come and get them, then torture them until they gained stigmata and then kill them ritualistically to fuel a greater piece of infrastructure than his own.

        Rank 2 - Mr. Smiley

        Mr. Smiley looks like an ordinary human at first glance as he must blend in with society until his mark is attacked. He keeps the lower half of his face (or his entire face) covered at all times. This is because his jaw is massively distended and filled with rusted, iron shards shaped into the form of shark's teeth while his tongue is an animated razor-blade. He will attack his victim by biting them.

        While this next angel is not used in my Demon game, in my Hunter the Vigil game I decided to include a God-Machine angel as a being manipulating certain events because one of my players gave their character Omen Sensitivity. Her name is "Muse".

        Rank 3 - Muse

        Muse's true form is a figure formed of moving gold-dust with three pairs of large wings that span easily it's own height again when fully stretched. These wings are large arcing lightning that curve around from it's back like organic wings would, creating the profile of a more classical angel. Muse has no face or other discerning features.

        Muse's mortal mask is a young woman, in her early twenties, with blonde curling hair and pale skin spotted with freckles across her cheeks. With a very sleight build, and only standing at 5'6'', she isn't in the slightest bit imposing or dramatically beautiful. When in this mortal mask, she has no specific identity, and is a skilled manipulator at convincing mortals that she is who she says she is. Muse can manifest her wings while in her mortal mask, when she does they appear as classic angelic wings, designed after a dove, but large enough to carry her.

                    Here are my Players' Characters:

                    Angel Unit 336 (Guardian/Inquisitor/Saboteur) Originally assigned to protect the linchpin of a particularly convoluted infrastructure meant to reactivate an old but powerful bit of Logistical Infrastructure used to channel Essence across the American south. She fell when she couldn’t bring herself to allow the lynchpin (a sweet kid to whom she’d become engaged) to commit a murder she’d driven him to. Demonic Form - While “Angie” is a lithe and beautiful brunette, she fell while rushing to save her fiancé, and is confined to that form, as such Unit 336 appears (to those who can see her at all) to be a 10 foot long gecko that stands just three feet at the shoulder. Her tale, which makes up half of her total length, ends in a communications array allowing her access to satellite imaging, and her limbs can convert into a high speed rail system, allowing her to move at incredible speed. Her featureless face can open revealing a three foot long ‘tongue’ that allows her to ‘plug’ into a victim’s mind, erasing their memories, a tool used by her often to smooth over her relationship with her Fiancé.

                    Bud (Destroyer/Saboteur) Assigned to the construction of a high rise in downtown New Calais, the demon who only knows the name “Bud” fell in love with the building and couldn’t bring himself to create a flaw in the building’s design that would eventually lead to the slow deaths of everyone who worked in the building. Bud himself is a tattooed giant of a man, standing just under seven feet tall. Demonic Form - If Bud’s cover is big, than his Demonic Form is Gigantic. Standing well over 15 feet tall, bud appears to be a demon constructed from scorched concrete and rebar, with great horns of twisted metal and a long tail constructed from elevator cable and electrical cords and is held aloft on wings of rebar and asphalt.

                    Lady Jue (Messenger/Tempter) Assigned to a New Calais elementary school back in the 1970s, the J.U.E. Unit's assignment was to corrupt and conscript those children who were destined for greatness to the God Machine. She fell forty years later as an old woman, when she decided to conscript them to her own cult of personality. Demonic Form - When Jue must reveal her true form to her followers, she discards her cover as an elderly black woman and dons that of an impossibly beautiful being of flawless obsidian. Upon her head rests a great golden crown, to be touched by her is to be blessed, to be struck by her is to be wounded. When the lady accepts tribute she opens her mouth to reveal a singularity to devour her sacrifice.

                    on a side note, the player and I can't decide what JUE actually stands for, Jue was a name found in a book on demonology, but when we transferred the character over to the Descent we realized it looked like an Anagram. I got one more character, Bart Sweets, a crossing guard, but I've got to find the player's description before I can post him.
