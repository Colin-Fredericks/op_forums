    The Interlocks.

    06-05-2014, 09:50 PM
    I need your help forum-mates. I really do. I'm coming upon the third session of the Demon game I'm running and I just can't figure out a players Interlocks. I finished my other three player's in a night, but this character eludes me.

    She is a Messenger Inquisitor, hacktavist created to spread misinformation and cause havoc through use of the internet. (Basically relying people up so they take rage quitting to the extreme). She fell when she followed the threads of her lies and released just what kind of damage she was doing (Her last message was supposed to cause another Waco incident).
    The Embed she has chosen is Voice of the machine. Her preferred means of getting things accomplished are hacking (really likes to do or attempt it even if the situation doesn't call for it), she employs Parkour, silent moving (her specialty in stealth), and uses fear as a means of intimidation when needed. And I'm stumped. Just completely outta my head with what to do with her. Any help would be greatly appreciated.
    Or maybe just a bouncing board, I wanted to give her something that let her hook up with machine buts didn't want to negate her already existing hacking specialty.

      Interlocks are a lot of work. I find it rewarding work, but I can't deny it takes a lot of effort out of you.

      As it happens, I'm actually playing a hacktivist demon myself, although the two characters sound like dark mirrors of one another. Could you share more about the character's personality and motives? Does she have an idea of what her vision of Hell and purpose for her Descent is, or is she still working that out? What ties her to the world and sparked her Fall, principles, philosophies, group identifications, individual friends or loved ones? Does she feel strongly about certain things or does she struggle with human emotion? Is she mischievous, thoughtful, circumspect, detached, cowardly, daring, deceitful, friendly, charismatic? Is her Cover identity a precious touchstone in the world or a convenient shroud that masks her true motives and nature? As well, what does the player enjoy about the game? Does she like involved mechanics or quick fixes, flashy stuff or deep thoughts? Does she prefer to keep her character out of the fray or is tagging along with the rest of the ring important?

      Any information of this sort will help; these are all just examples of the kinds of things that might suss out a direction to go with.

                  There's a number of ways it could go, depending on which direction one took "hacking with a broader scope" in and what Key was used to get there; Right Tools, Right Job could be used to effectively quick-write a mundane object into a Gadget, for example, or Special Message could be leveraged to turn Voice of the Machine's principles toward decryption.                       Voice of the Machine > Social Dynamics
                      God emerges from the gap between its components.
                      Aether Map: The demon can perceive the exhaust and reverberation of Aether. This enhanced Aether sense, given time to concentrate, can be used to form a sort of echolocation map of Infrastructure and other demonic and God-Machine related activity, and derive information about its nature and functioning.

                      Social Dynamics > Like I Built It
                      Emergent behavior alters the gestalt; know the pieces and you know the workings of the whole.
                      Crowdhack: The demon understands how to use the personalities and relationships of people like variables and operations in a vast algorithm. She can plan out how to use minor social interactions with particular members spread out through a social group or organization to guide the actions and outlook of the whole.

                      Like I Built It > Mercury Retrograde
                      What we assume to be unitary systems are themselves vulnerable to the same method.
                      Soul Programming: Through intense, intimate extended conversation with a character, the demon is able to mutate the character's Aspirations, Virtue or Vice into modified forms relating to their previous state. Reinforcement over long periods of time can shift the character's natural resting point for these traits, making drastic changes possible through persistent application across several transitive states.

                      ALL THINGS ARE BORN FROM INFORMATION.
                      Everything emerges from the tiniest interactions. Truth, beauty and the soul are malleable: to edit their input values is to craft them as you see fit.

                      Control the input, and you become master of the output.

                                          Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                          Got any ideas for her final secret or Keys?

                                          How would an Interlock that allows one to apply the principles of hacking in a broader scope via exploiting sympathy with the Machine sound?
                                          Every time the player tries to add an embed to the cypher, make her say why she thinks that the next key... and take notes, and then use that information to determine what the player is looking for.

                                          For the Final Secret, read the Hacker Manifesto. Get an idea of the hacker mindset, the last two paragraphs especially look like the sort of Gnostic Truth a hacktivist demon might come to during their descent.

                                          The final interlock
                                          YOU CAN BREAK THE INDIVIDUAL: The Demon activates this Interlock when capture, destruction, or reintegration into the God Machine is inevitable. The Demon loses all knowledge or her Embeds, Exploits and Interlocks. These bits of data travel across the world and lodge themselves in the minds and souls of demons, stigmatics, and normal humans who are sympathetic to the Demon's personal vision of hell and have a willingness to strike against the God Machine. This includes the Interlock YOU CAN BREAK THE INDIVIDUAL which will install itself on whatever other demon the character the original user trusts the most. No beneficiary of this interlock may receive more than one embed or exploit per use of YOU CAN BREAK THE INDIVIDUAL.

                                          Concept: This more of a plot device than a usable power but it unites the ideals of hacktivism with those of the Descent : liberation/dissemination of information, a mass movement working to overthrow of a corrupt social order, and the demon leaves an immortal legacy that the God Machine cannot stamp out: becoming a reverse virus that empowers more and more individuals against the God Machine.

                                          From a game play standpoint, a character could explain the interlock as leverage against the angels, like a deadman switch ("Kill me or integrate me, you're just making more trouble for yourself")
