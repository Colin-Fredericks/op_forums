                                                  Well, first: I think if the demon approaches it right - complaining about needing to find a new job, then talking about how you found one in another city, etc. - it doesn't necessarily have to be acting grossly out of character, though I agree that moving without any notice right out of the blue is a pretty good candidate. However, in this situation I'd probably risk the Compromise check.

                                                  This situation, of course, is a little different then how you portray, because I'm not suggesting moving because if you do so you'll suddenly be free of Compromise due to lack of people noticing you're out of character. This entire thread is about how a demon doesn't get any of the memories associated with his or her new cover. If you're in a situation where you're constantly surrounded by people that can catch those niggling inconsistencies between the pactee and the demon's memories and personalities and use them to completely unravel your cover over time - which I don't think most demons would get themselves into knowingly, but it could definitely happen - I think risking a single Compromise by moving away from that environment of extreme scrutiny is worth the risk.

                                                  And I guess it should be noted that when I said that this was a less drastic option, I was comparing it to staging a car accident so you can fake a head-trauma induced personality change a la Fred Flinstone.
