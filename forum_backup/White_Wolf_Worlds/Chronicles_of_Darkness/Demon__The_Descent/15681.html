    What is your God-Machine?

    11-21-2013, 06:37 PM
    We're never going to get a concrete answer to what exactly the God-Machine is, as it is left firmly in our hands to suit for the individual chronicle. So what's your answer?

    I'm not big on a human-created God-Machine, and I don't much like intelligent design (i.e. aliens made it) either. My God-Machine is a kind of cosmic virus, dedicated to self-perpetuity and not much else. This explains why it has contradictory orders or sometimes doesn't make sense; there's no central governing intelligence to it. In a sense, it's a Blind Idiot God.

    Remi. she/her. game designer.

          It depends on the game.

          In my OWoD, the God-Machine is a cosmic control panel that has no sentience of its own -- it requires pilots. Those pilots have changed over the ages, and the most recent ones are the beings known as the Messengers in Hunter: the Reckoning. In my OWoD, the God-Machine is the answer to the dilemma of determinism -- the closed system is fixed because everything is a direct reaction to something else that happened, which itself was a reaction to something else that happened. As such, the only way to enable true agency for those within the system is to allow them to leave and influence the system from the outside. This is the God-Machine. An outside control panel of reality.

          In my NWoD, it depends on the game. In the GMC pseudo-Mage game I'm running, the God-Machine has made itself into a terraforming device. Remember that tale about the old city where the Celestials and humans lived, which was then crushed under the rebellion abetted by "What has risen may fall"? Well, the God-Machine has since made it its ultimate goal to restore the old ways to the entire planet by terraforming the world into having the same properties as that old city, under the mantra of "what has fallen may rise again". A consquence, if this succeeds, is the restoration of humanity as a slave race to the Celestials -- they will have bliss, peace and immortality, but they will not have their freedom. So the players must determine what they value the most and respond from there...

                                          1) An invading limb from a post-technological-singularity alien civilization from elsewhere within the Milky Way, Type II on the Kardashev scale. It is attempting to shape the Earth into as big of an occult matrix as a planet can sustain, which will operate in conjunction with planetary occult matrices elsewhere in the galaxy as part of a spiral of endless growth. In an undamaged reality, it would have had much stronger hold on the workings of the otherworlds closest to us, and all of the God-Machine's components would have, in addition to their normal functions, been carrying out the cognitive processes of perfect bliss. Reality being damaged may or may not refer to the Fall of Atlantis; If it does, the Fall might not have damaged the alien's capabilities elsewhere in the galaxy, most of whose magic it already understands perfectly and which it is happily conquering, just as it may have eventually conquered an unfallen Atlantis, whose metaphysics would have been more familiar to what the God-Machine had perfected its technology for elsewhere.

                                          2) A property of natural symmetry, coterminous with the physical universe. In an undamaged reality, every atomic motion that appears to be merely random heat would be a part of a directed information-processing mechanism which exists to sustain the universe as an inviolable paradise of maximum happiness-experience-efficiency. Because reality is broken, it is reduced to splotchy coverage which still extends an infinite distance through space, but is neither omnipresent, omnipotent, nor omnibenevolent. Its complexity is still such that, even without any opposition, neither the union of the Exarchs nor any Rank 9 spirit-like entity has the capacity to fully understand and repair it or repurpose it for any coherent purpose; Its current actions are quite simply insane, although there are some world-sustaining components left intact enough for us to have survived so far, with haphazard tinkering. Reality being broken refers not to the Fall of the Mages, but to a catastrophe infinitely more distant on any scale of meta-time, without which the peewee Fall of Atlantis would not have been possible. The Ascension of the Exarchs was, in fact, intentionally inspired and assisted by the broken God-Machine as its effort to influence the Supernal from its limited position.

                                              One of the first ideas that occurred to me is that, of the two classical 'first' inventions of man, the mind of the God Machine is the first wheel made(the other classic image of first inventions being fire). I had a hard time articulating why I though the first wheel formed the barest, simplest mind of the God-Machine, until I considered that my favorite idea of what the G-M is trying to do was to create 'Heaven': a strict, orderly place devoid of free will and realized what machines do.

                                              Theoretically, they simplify and improve tasks, and as such improve lives.

                                              A spear makes it easier to kill our meat so we may eat-it simplifies our killing. An aqueduct simplifies the process of getting water by bringing it to us without as much work. A smartphone does a lot of things that make our lives, theoretically, easier. Tools compound functions and reduce effort.

                                              Lame and cliche as it is, it makes sense then that the God Machine is doing what tools and machines have been doing for ages, efficiently and without care(because a wheel doesn't care if crushes a person under itself, and really doesn't care what it does, but it exists for a purpose and fulfills that, and neither does a rifle), to horrific degrees, and much because we still ask so much be taken off our shoulders. It works to completely systematize everything, to make everything simpler for the benefit of lives. Not for gratitude, not for reward, not for anything-it's just doing what it does.

                                              So, did the first wheel just gain a mind? Did someone build the G-M around it? That, I don't know and don't care. I know the entire thing has 'recycled reality' at least once, so that from our perspective, it's always been there. Cheiron's Board may well have fought it and lost. But the linchpin and mind of the God Machine goes back to the iconic stone wheel man carved ages ago.

                                              By the way, Fire? Arguable, but from certain perspectives, astute minds are inclined to say it's been more in humanity's hands than any other. EDIT: Which isn't to say that it developed some certain principles of its' own.

                                                  The God-Machine is essentially the universe's manifest self-perpetuation. It metaphysically runs the universe as it currently exists (the "Fallen"/"Phenomenal" laws in Mage terms). Simply destroying every instance of its infrastructure would result in a cosmic break down, though it is conceivable that it can be replaced by something better functioning. I am planning to have the first time players really destroy a major site of G-M infrastructure to have to deal with a massive up-spike in temporal weirdness, gravitational odd-spots, and general dimensional breaches in the surrounding area.

                                                  What is now the God-Machine, was ONCE an instrument moving toward a particular purpose. That purpose, design/program, etc is "the Principle". Since "the Fall" though (be it the fall of Atlantis, the death of father wolf, the opening of Pandora's Box, whatever) the universe has rolled off those tracks, and the G-M just maintains the universe as is in its current broken state. Qashmallim in contrast serve that original purpose, a universal metaphomorphosis that is the Pilgrimage writ cosmic. That evolution may or may not allow the continued existence of humanity in a recognizable state.
