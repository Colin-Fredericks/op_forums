      #1 - Still up for debate, but I stand with the camp that says that's left over from an earlier rules build. Currently there are no rules for granting stamina or other attributes by pact, though I'd personally allow 1 dot of an attribute as a Greater (rank 3) benefit

      #2 - Demons don't make pacts with each other, but they can TRADE pacts. Since pacts always take the form of a written contract and seem to work along the same lines as Bearer Bonds, Demons can trade any unredeemed pacts, simply by trading the physical contract. So, we've go two demons, let's call them Alice and Bob. Alice made a contract and got a job as a bank manager (mechanically +3 cover XP) while Bob made a contract and got a purebred papered Great Dane (mechanically +2 cover XP).
      I think, upon rereading part of the Pacts section, they can only trade pacts they haven't called in. So, if they haven't spent the cxp from those pacts yet, they can trade them, and Bob would have a pact to become a bank manager (3 cxp) and Alice a pact to own a show-grade Great Dane (2 cxp). Alice would obviously need either a very good reason to make such a trade or some additional compensation to sweeten the deal, but there you go.

      As far as I can tell, once you spend the cxp to 'patch in' the pact into your cover, you can't swap things anymore.

      #3 - I haven't seen anything like that yet.

                          My interpretation is that, because pacts rewrite reality, someone who signs the Cultist pact won't and can't just "walk away". Just as being rich or being beautiful or being Ambidextrous becomes a part of who they are, being a dedicated cult leader becomes part of who they are. Perhaps, over time (years at least) they could become disillusioned, or they could have their minds/personalities altered by further pacts or other supernatural effects, but barring outstanding circumstances, the person who signs the Cult pact simply WON'T just walk away.

                          Being dedicated and loyal to the demon's cult is built into the deal. It's not a choice. It's a metaphysical compulsion, that you by your own free will helped write into reality.


                          To summarize, I think someone who becomes a cult leader by signing a pact that grants the demon Cult merit can be subverted leading to eventual betrayal or abandonment, but they will not ever choose of their own free will without outside influence to simply walk away.

                              The other 'Questions About Pacts' thread had some interesting replies from the author of the Pacts chapter re: this exact topic. Essentially, granting physical competency... physical attributes, in other words... is something that IS in fact in the power of a pact. After all, why should one person be able to ask to be a Movie Star with no problem, but another person can't ask to be a championship bike racer?

                              And here are some contracts for you, if you're looking for NPC documents...
                              ---

                              A contract to remove the condition 'Altered Memories' from a vampire that went into torpor, in exchange for a Vampire informant...

                              "The party of the first part (Hereafter being referred to as The Buyer) will deliver to the party of the second part (Mister ‘V’) clarity of memory that will being into focus one (1) previous lifetime ‘as clear as if he had never slept’.

                              In payment, the party of the second part shall offer his services as a consultant for a period of time not to exceed ten years. In such a capacity he will be expected to deliver intelligence promptly, accurately and without omission. Any attempt to circumvent this obligation through misleading behavior or intentional negligence shall forefeet the agreement and begin punitive measures as described in section B

                              The party of the first part and the party of the second part agree to respect the secrecy and privacy of this arrangement. It is assumed that all correspondence between both parties is undertaken with the implicit assumption of absolute discretion. If at any time this discretion is threatened to the extent that a source can be identified without the explicit and complete understanding of either party, punitive measure begin as described in section B.

                              Section B

                              If this agreement is terminated without agreed upon services being delivered, the party of the second part shall suffer punitive measures equal to but not exceeding : revocation of previously established benefits and further the forfeiture of social influence / power / holding as per the judgment of the aggrieved party. Such an obligation must be discharged immediately and cannot be held against the party of the second part as an ongoing debt.

                              If the party of the first part knowingly fails to hold to her agreements, the party of the second part may dissolve the agreement at his leisure and forfeit any rights or privileges established under this clause."
                              ---

                              A contract to give a Julii vampire the resources needed to help resist being eaten by Strix, in exchange for essentially a vampire slave...

                              "The party of the first part (Hereafter being referred to as The Buyer) will deliver to the party of the second part (Aurelia) keen senses and able reflexes intended to circumvent the natural cunning of her Strix enemies. The party of the second part shall also enjoy a protective treatment which will render her less susceptible to efforts to forcibly alter her mind.

                              The party of the first part shall also provide the party of the second part with a new LIFETIME, properly experienced and cultivated, with all due documentation and according adjustments to causality and history implied by such agreement. This identity (Heretofore acknowledged as the party of the third part, miss Melissa Hughes) shall be possessed of exceptionally secure lodgings that are particularly well suited maintaining privacy and safety in the face of Strix opposition. Also provided shall be fluency and command of modern English as is appropriate to the continuation of Melissa Hughes’ established history.

                              The party of the first part shall also provide the services of one party of the fourth part, to be named and established upon the establishment of this agreement, who will serve the party of the second part in a fashion appropriate to a life sworn and loyal servant for the term of this agreement.

                              In exchange for these services, the party of the second part swears her services to the party of the first part in regards to the matter of the Strix for the full term of this agreement. This oath includes obligations of a serious nature, including but not limited to : violence, self-destructive actions, or any other tactics the party of the first part may deem necessary.

                              Either party may terminate this agreement at any time, forfeiting all benefits therein."
