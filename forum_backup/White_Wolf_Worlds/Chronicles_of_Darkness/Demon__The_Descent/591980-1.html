                                                        I'm not going to start the Descent vs Fallen argument again, but I do feel like Demon has been lacking SOMETHING for awhile now. Maybe its my love of LARPS or that I cut my teeth on Vampire for the WOD. For whatever reason, I like the political aspects of a WOD game most of all...and I don't feel Demon supports that gameplay style very well. That's one reason I made the Las Vegas setting (where the Unchained found a way to "weaponize" sin to drown out the will of the God-Machine in a "signal to noise" fashion- a single omniscient desire lost in the scream of a million petty human ones). I wanted to push the God-Machine a little out of the lime light and give the Demons a chance to make their own lives miserable. As such I welcome the Storyteller's Guide as long overdue resource for addressing my misgivings and helping me explore what the Demons might do if they aren't always looking over their shoulders.
