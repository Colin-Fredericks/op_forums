                                    I have an idea. You could treat melee weapons, guns, armor, Cover and at least one method of keeping in touch with the G-M as inherent advantages of the Angel template. You no longer have to spend slots on Blast or Mortal Mask (but you would still need the Materialize Manifestation to interact directly with the material world). The freed up Numina slots can be spent on more specialized powers.

                                    In addition, Angelic Influences can mimic Demonic Forms, with the dots corresponding to the sophistication in most cases. That is, 1 dot = Modification, 2 dots = Technology, 3 dots = Propulsion, 4 dots = Process and 5 dots = epic advantages that go beyond Processes. You may use an Incept that allows Angels to have Influences rated higher than their Rank.

                                    For example: an Angel with Influence (Sound 4) might have Sonic Acuity and Voice Of The Angel. You wouldn't need to spend a slot on a sonic Rivet Arm, as your template gives you a gun for free.

                                    For 1 Essence, 2 "Angelic Form" advantages may be manifested reflexively. Assuming full "Angelic Form" costs nothing, but does not restore Essence and it triggers Aetheric Resonance.

                                    As for the rating of the free weapons and armor, I suggest using their Rank and function as guidelines. A Destroyer sent after a tough as nails Demon might have a plasma sword that deals its Rank + 1 as a Damage Bonus and bypasses any protection that isn't supernatural in some way. The simple switchblades that come out of the wrists of maintenance Angels should deal +1L at most. You may come up with an Incept to improve all these ratings for a scene if deemed necessary.

                                    That should be more than enough.
