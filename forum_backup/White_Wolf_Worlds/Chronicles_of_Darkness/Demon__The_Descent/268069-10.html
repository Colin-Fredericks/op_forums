    Kilroy’s transmissions

    <2014W47-7T09:47-08:00>

    Shelly's slumber this morning seems more restful. I do hope so, she is a resilient woman but the shock of witnessing Kiki's murderous rampage was quite traumatic for her. The fatigue from improper rest cannot be helpful to her mental state and nightmares would most certainly exasperate it. She has asked Vivian to stay with her again tonight and I have promised to return after the interrogation of our captive reptiod.

    She is in an extremely vulnerable state presently and I am loathe to chance something misfortunate happening. Also, if her insistence on turning on all of the lights in her apartment are an indication the coming of night will be a more trying time than the midday. Nocturnal dread is a deeply ingrained human instinct. Fortunately, I should be back well before the waning of the sun's light to distract and comfort her.

    I have felt the strain that showing such a degree of care and concern is putting on my cover. Vivian Marley was not a selfless woman nor was she prone to such deep attachments. Too many emotional scars I believe. As much as she did crave intimacy she was simultaneously terrified of allowing someone to be in a position to inflict emotional harm upon her. However, an incident of mortal peril seems like the sort of catalyst that would bring an epiphany concerning her feelings toward Shelly Waters. Perhaps tonight or tomorrow (provided Shelly has recovered sufficiently of course) it would be the appropriate time for her to make a somewhat hesitant declaration of her feelings and open the option of possible future commitment. A touch of emotional vulnerability should help quell any doubts Shelly may be harboring. Also, given the volatile state of Kiki's, it would be an opportune time to broach a contract to secure her financially and allow her to return to her studies.

    I should confer with Clytemnestra once her resurrection is complete. The subtle shifting of a cover's paradigm from selfish to loving is an artful one and she has been working on a very similar adjustment with Naomi and her paramour. She likely has some useful insights on making the transition without damaging the cover.

    <2014W47-7T14:07-08:00>

    The interview has proven remarkably productive. Speed monitored the subject's thoughts and thus took a minor role in the actual questioning. Quietus, despite being directly cautioned against any displays of intimidation, found it necessary to threaten her both indirectly and directly. I suppose I really should not have expected a destroyer to grasp the concept of subtle manipulation. It did, I suppose, distract the subject from Clytemnestra's suggestions of devouring her. Despite having Amsterdam present and having already taken the subject's Aether, she seemed rather unaccountably peckish. Her appetite seems to grown of late and been less easily dismissed. Perhaps when we have a quiet moment I should speak to her about it. At least Markizel wasn't present. Given his fixation with fingernail removal and his lack of self censoring he might well have sent the subject into cardiac arrest. At the very least, he would have undoubtedly caused her to soil herself and I already have one too many defecating reptiles in my lab to clean up after. And, speaking of our apparent mascot, Quietus decided to ask the subject about the care and feeding of alligators on top of his menacing behavior.

    It was clearly a mistake to allow him to be present. I should attempt to limit Quietus' involvement in things outside of the purview of his role as a destroyer moving forward. One does not attempt brain surgery with a monkey wrench after all.

    Despite all of this, our subject, who goes by the name of Sleet, did give us a number to contact the leader of her team. She and the other reptoids were apparently recruited by an Unchained who later met with an unfortunate fate. They have been working freelance since that point and seem to be a reasonably competent group. Their leader, Drak, was agreeable enough to deal with.

    Fortunately, it would seem the interest in Maverick's is transitory. It is going to be instrumental in an occult matrix to bring the God Machine's newest Infrastructure online. The reptoids' employer, known to them as Mr Black, wishes to disrupt this.

    The reptoids have never seen or heard this Mr Black. All of their dealings have been through an intermediary who goes by the name of Mr Ludwig. Drak was willing to pass word to him of my desire to speak in exchange for the life and return of his wayward Sleet.

    While waiting for him to contact me, I called Jimmy Marsh. He was able to give me some information concerning the mercenary reptoids and Mr Ludwig. The actual Unchained was believed to have died at Puriel's hands some months ago. It seems likely, given the lack of certain disturbing and highly ingrained behaviors, that the Unchained in question is using the name to conceal their own identity.

    The most likely candidate seems to be a Ms Velvet. She was the partner of Mr Stripe, who formed the reptoid team before overextending himself and falling victim to hunter-killers. She would have known of them and had a powerset that seems suitable to what we know of Mr Ludwig. Also, apparently her file has gone missing from Marsh's records. A troublesome detail and he is going to inventory his files to see what other files may have also gone missing.

    While it is at present just a theory, I believe Valefar is behind the whole affair. It seems to match his usual modus operandi. In particular, I found the use of Vox to call me in my own voice to be a clever touch.

    That conversation did prove to be refreshingly civil. He wants the occult matrix disrupted. His reptoids need to be in place as the Rights to safely sabotage it. He was indifferent to our desire to remove them harmlessly so long as the replacements were in place within five days. Speed will be convincing them that we need them to go on vacation and allow a pair of stand ins to lure the 'terrorists' that have been targeting them out. If the occult matrix completes within the two to three weeks most likely, all will be well. If it runs late, there may be some complications with the Rights not wanting to be away from their business so long. But we shall deal with that if it becomes an issue.

    Now it is time to arrange the return of Sleet.

    Lost in the Chaos

    Kwan Jinghua woke up with a sharp gasp. Her eyes opened, and she noted several things in rapid succession. One, she was alive again. Her last memory was of the frightening Yūrei grabbing her face, and agonizing cold and wetness, as well as her throat tightening. Second, she was stuck to what seemed chillingly like a spider web. Third, a resplendent golden woman, the most beautiful she had ever seen, was crawling down the wall with a set of four golden mechanical looking legs. Finally, a white girl standing near part of the web was messing with her phone.

    “Welcome to my Parlor” the golden woman said, in perfect Cantonese. As she got closer, Jinghua was surprised how human, and beautiful she looked. The gold was a soft glow or haze, and golden hair. Her face was captivating…

    “What happened to me?” Jinghua pulled herself from her reverie. “Where am I and who are you?” The white girl, Amsterdam she would later be told, in the corner of her vision seemed to realize her sleeve was stuck to part of the web.

    Clytemnestra folded her human arms, her four spider limbs lifting her so she was eye to eye with the trapped gangster. “I am now called Clytemnestra, a Goddess of Central America, trapped for many years. I have resurrected you to be my servant. Not my slave, but to serve me within the Tong.”

    Amsterdam attempted to hide an attempt to pull her arm free with a vaguely casual looking stretch. It didn’t work very well.

    “What do I get out of this?” Jinghua was having even less luck than Amsterdam at getting free.

    “What do you want?” The Spider moved several steps back, then forward, on her long legs. “I am willing to negotiate. I am even willing to give you time to think. But if I don’t accept your terms, I will eat you.”

    “You drive a hard bargain…” Jinghua’s eyes were drawn to the movement of where Amsterdam was attempting to walk away from her caught arm. She barely managed to avoid falling when she went too far forward. The gangster looked back.

    “Fine. My name is Kwan Jinghua. I work for a man locally known as Two-Chin. Not his name, but how you will find him. He likes female employees, says men underestimate us. I deal with some of his less acceptable imports. If I skim them, I will be dead and worthless to you.”

    The Spider smiled. “See, from here we can see about getting you rewards, for information. Perhaps once people see your star rise, you can get recruits. But I do not intend to sacrifice you. I do not like sacrificing my-“

    Amsterdam had been gathering her strength and attempted a run, pulling hard as she could. She did a flip in the air, landing on her back, arm and stuck sleeve in the air. Clytemnestra sighed, the long golden legs took her body over, and she wordlessly touched the strand Amsterdam had gotten herself stuck to, dissolving it. She then returned to Jinghua.

    “As I was saying, I do not intend to sacrifice you unless I must, even then I will give you an out. But betray me and you will be a desiccated husk. Work with me and you will be prosperous. Whatever happens to Two-Chin, even if I have to deal with him summarily, I will not make you directly betray him.” A raised eyebrow. “Deal?”

    “It’s a start, I am back from the dead.” The Spider dissolved the webs holding her. Am helped catch her and get her to her feet.

    “Report in, tell them what you like, except that a goddess resurrected you. If someone wants you punished for disobedience, I will deal with them. But we will work together.” The Spider gave a winning smile. For some reason, Jinghua could not help but be uplifted by it. But then she frowned. “Wait…where is my phone…or wallet?”

    Amsterdam gave a sympathetic look. “Must have been lost in the chaos.”
