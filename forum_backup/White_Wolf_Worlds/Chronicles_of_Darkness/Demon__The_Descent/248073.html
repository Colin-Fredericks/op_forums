                      The overall plot of the chronicle I'm running now is basically that an Angel that specializes in creating stigmatic Sleeper Agents has been inactive for about 18 years. There is a new Project on the horizon and the God-Machine needs a specialist group of Stigmatics that can be plucked from their life for various purposes. The special way this angel brainwashes people though is more akin to a spreadable disease. The more the subject is aware of the Angel and the more the subject thinks on the enigma that it causes the more the sickness grows. When the sickness grows strong enough the subject can be completely controlled when the Angel's presence is near. The are then given a Mask that emulates the Angel's presence.

                      The sickness spreads by the subject involving those they know in their "pursuit of answers" or by wearing the masks and targeting others when the Angel chooses the stigmatic to do so. Because this "sickness" grows and spreads faster among children they are usually targets. However it has been 18 years and almost everyone from the original kindergarten class was made as a sleeper except one of the Player's Covers that only escaped this fate by moving away during the year. Now the Angel wants to complete the set and is targeting these other people that the cover associates with.

                      The Angel has no idea that it is targeting Demons and the Demons are freaked the hell out that they seem to be targeted through nothing that they did



                      Of course we are still only in the first story so none of that has really come up. All they know is a couple people with powerful masks were looking at 2 of the characters and that's about it.

                                  My chronicle arc is based around an antagonist angel known as "The Inevitable".

                                  It begins with a mysterious Facility that is built beneath Seattle University and accessible by a building that no human can see on the campus grounds, known as "The Beachhead". The Facility's primary function was to interrogate and detail "Hell" using a demon known as Linchpin. The God-Machine appears to have made a terrible mistake, however, in that It assigned a Messenger angel to interrogate and learn everything about Hell from the captured demon (Linchpin). This inevitably resulted in that angel's fall. This is turn caused other problems as the realisation of choice sank in for other angels working in the Facility, a psychopomp whom was tasked with creating the horrible angelic servitors known as Dalga decided that she did not want to kill to create, but to create properly and nurture the young. She soon fell.

                                  As the Facility collapsed a new angel was activated at the heart of the Facility, known as "The Inevitable", and looking like death-warmed-up, this very powerful Destroyer is tasked with "cleaning up" the Facility's broken parts before self-destructing the structure. It seemed the God-Machine wasn't without a contingency plan.

                                  As a Rank 4 angel at the beginning of the game that is constantly hunting the ring in addition to any hunters they might bring down on their heads through compromise rolls I gave the Inevitable his own condition. The idea being that, as his mission is specifically to clean up the messes of the Facility, he isn't listening in on high-wave angel radio for any possible demon-sightings. Instead he is listening for very specific tells which they may not always show when they make mistakes.

                                  The main story is therefore, a series of stories, including them finding and rescuing the Linchpin to earn a new ally (they did this) and to defeat the Inevitable and/or subvert the Beachhead. Through doing these things they will learn the truth behind the Beachhead and why the God-Machine appears to have made such a simple mistake.

                                  The Inevitable (Persistent)
                                  The Reloaded Ring is hunted by a Destroyer known as “The Inevitable”. This angel's mission is solely to destroy the former angels of the Beachhead Facility hidden in Seattle University. The Inevitable may roll (10 – Cover) dice once per chapter or day (whichever comes first) and must accrue (Cover) successes, when he does this he may appear in a scene and attempt to destroy the ring. If he manages to gain a general profile of any demon's Cover, he gains a +1 to this roll. If he knows their identity he can now harass them regularly and thus gains a +2 (in addition the demon gains the Blown persistent condition). The storyteller may also then in either case have this condition trigger instead of the Surveilled or Hunted (Persistent) conditions as the Inevitable learns to interpret the unique signatures a demon leaves behind when they've been compromised.
                                  Resolution: The Inevitable must die to resolve this condition. In order to remove his +2 bonus upon him identifying a Demon's cover, the demon must abandon that cover.
                                  Beat: Any time the Inevitable enters a scene, the involved demons gain a beat. Any time the ring escape or kill the Inevitable they gain a beat.

                                                          My plans are still very much in the formative stages. I don't like to set too much into motion until I have a sloid understanding of the types of characters that will be in play. Right now the plan is to set my game primarily in the Midtown area of Detroit which has historic ties to the Freemasons. It's an area steeped in history that has fallen on hard times, but is now on the upswing. The general idea is that Detroit was once heavily under the sway of the God Machine, but with concerted effort became something of a way station for the Unchained who managed the city poorly. Recently the God Machine and his angelic agents have made inroads in the Midtown area, and are attempting to seize control for reasons.

                                                          I really want to focus on Free Masons, who are part of a cult of stigmatics attempting to influence the actions of the God Machine, but can alternatively fall under the sway of both Demons and Angels. I also plan to contrast the old Detroit of the 1920s which is still very much alive in Midtown with the newer more vibrant Detroit.
