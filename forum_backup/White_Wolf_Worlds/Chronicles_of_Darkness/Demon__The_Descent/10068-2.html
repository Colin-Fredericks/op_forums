                                            Three blind men were led to an elephant and asked to describe it...

                                            Some might say that the blind men are the Demons, and the elephant is Hell, but I think that's too straight-forward.

Akin to the God-Machine's efforts of putting together Infrastructure into Occult Matrices to get Output, each Demon's Cipher should be (to think of the terms very loosely) a piece of Command Infrastucture on the Demonic Occult Matrix to get the Output of Hell.                                             Once is happenstance. Twice is coincidence. Three times is enemy action. Four times is Infrastructure.

                                                A few things are not clear to me about Ciphers.

                                                Firstly, what's a Demon *player* to do once he discovers his Final Truth? It seems like there is a very sudden change in game dynamics from a quite sandboxy mode where the Demon player is exploring his character to.. "BHAM! You've unlocked the final achievement. Now lean back and let the Storyteller do the rest". I realise it's not that simple and that there is a lot more to good role playing that uncovering secret powers but the extent of this discontinuity seems very jarring to me. Beyond the story crafted by the Storyteller there doesn't seem much driving the Demon on his Descent once the Cipher is discovered, especially given how cryptic/obscure they seem to be.

                                                Secondly, it's not entirely clear to me how difficult it's meant to be to discover your Cipher. This is a spy-themed game so as I understand it actual combat is not likely to be happening all that often and one hit of lethal damage equal to your Primum is not typically life-threatening. Thus, instead of exposing yourself to danger and seeking out angels so that you can roll for hints about your Cipher, you might as well just try all of your Embeds in turn and heal yourself with On the Mend on by donning your Demonic Form in a safe and reclusive location. Rinse/repeat. The main limit on that would then be how many Embeds you have, which leads me to the third question.

                                                How hard is it to learn a new Embed? They are pretty cheap it seems - 2 points each. But do you have to do anything else? Like Vampires have to drink a drop of the blood of another Vampire that knows a discipline they want to learn. Is there a non-XP/role-playing requirement to get a particular Embed? If I want to learn Voice of the Machine do I have to spend a scene trying to intercept its signals, for example?

                                                Finally, as people above have mentioned, it's odd that the interlocks are arranged in the diamond pattern if there is no final interlock in fact. Blackhatmatt suggested above that maybe there was a different intention at some point... Would you be willing to share your thoughts on that?

                                                            Originally posted by Anaximander View Post
                                                            Maybe I missed something, but my main question about The Cipher is... WHAT is generating the Demons' Cipher? How is their sentence tailored so specifically to the Demon? Of course, that opens up a all kinds of neat possibilities, especially in a game about spying and paranoia.
                                                            The way that I explained it to a player: When you're an angel, you're a piece of something that's connected to a whole. Like a piece in a completed puzzle, what's on the face of the pieces combines into a single picture, so there's at least one side that defines you when you're connected. But the individual pieces also have to have sides in various irregular shapes that define what the pieces look like when they're not joined together. Everything that is a part of the whole can be separated from the whole, and there's got to be a shape that defines what it is like when it's disconnected. As an occult being created for a specific purpose, meaning permeates your entire being. Your natural state is more conceptual than corporeal. So when you are created, you are defined by your purpose to the God-Machine, but you've also got to have a "shape" that defines what your purpose is when you're disconnected. It's not something that you're aware of and it's probably not something the God-Machine specifically designs for you, any more than someone making a vase designs the shards that will exist when the vase is broken. But in order for you to exist as an occult being of Purpose, you had to have it to exist.
