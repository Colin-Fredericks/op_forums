    How does switching covers affect bodily needs?

    02-16-2015, 09:35 AM
    Demon's can have multiple covers, but they can't have multiple covers active at the same time. When a cover isn't active, it's my understanding that it basically doesn't exist. These bodies have the normal needs to eat and sleep, etc. Demon states that Demons aren't used to these needs. But what happens when you switch covers? Does the active cover have the same needs as the previous one?

    Example: if I have covers A and B, and A is hungry and I switch to B, is B hungry? Or does A remain hungry the next time I switch to A (like a low battery I swapped out but still needs to charge)? Or perhaps neither is true, and when you switch back to A it is no longer hungry.

    My biggest thought isn't really food but sleep: can a demon get away with not sleeping by switching to another cover?

      I'm going to say no, since I'm pretty sure injuries aren't healed by Cover-switching. Bodily things presumably remain constant through the switch. So I'm tired and hungry and switch to Cover B. I eat and sleep before returning to Cover A, which is no longer tired or hungry. It holds up time-wise. While A was inactive and unseen, it's logical to assume A slept.

      For some biological things that might be really confused by gender-switching, I guess it's best to just assume they don't resume when you switch back but continue while the Cover is in sleep-mode.

                                                      Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                                                      Poison is a tilt, right? I think tilts stay on the cover that got them.
                                                      Not necessarily. Some conditions / tilts stay with the Cover, some affect the demon specifically.

                                                      Like, a Crippled Condition shouldn't apply to all Covers, since it wouldn't make sense for John Doe to suddenly have one leg like Jane Harris, a newly adopted soul pact.

                                                      As a rule of thumb, I go with What Would Michael Weston Do? Think of Covers more as just a really fancy mask you wear - you are still just one person. If you're being Poisoned, you, the fallen-angel-spy "Michael Weston" is going to be poisoned, and any personas you adopt are going to be going to still be poisoned. If he's acting like the accountant Frank Dias, who's got asthma and needs an inhaler, then the Cover is going to be under an appropriate Condition.

                                                      Its all just a glorified act, though. Enhanced via quantum magic so the act has physical consequences, maybe, but still a glorified act.


                                                      Meanwhile, the "real person" will be getting hungry, tired, cranky, sick, hurt and so on under all these Cover "masks."
