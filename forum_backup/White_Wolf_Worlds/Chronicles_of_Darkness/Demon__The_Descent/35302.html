                                          Originally posted by MissMaddy View Post
                                          Is it possible to do this? Is it possible to even take 'Male' or 'Female' as a cover aspect simultaniously, so you might turn a female male and have the 'Female' cover fragment?
                                          Off the cuff I'd say yes, of course. "Male" or "Female" are certainly the kinds of identity aspects like "Mechanic", "Airline Pilot", or "Brother of Jimmy Wells". When it's signed over, they're neuter, not flipped to the opposite gender.

                                          If someone wanted to go FtM, the demon would have to offer someone else's "Male" aspect in trade for their "Female" aspect. Conversely, to go MtF, you'd have to offer someone else's "Female" aspect. In either case, when the Pact is signed, the transitions happen simultaneously without going through "Neuter", if that matters for whatever reason.

                                          If they simply didn't want to be "Male" anymore, (No more obvious target for kicking, no more testosterone-fueled issues, no more social pressures relevant to being "Male"), then you could take it no problem. If they didn't want to be "Female" anymore, (No more monthly wanting-to-kill-the-world-and/or-own-uterus, no more pregnancy scares, no more social pressures relevant to being "Female"), then also no problem. In either case, that gender identity is simply _gone_, including the physical/behavioral/hormonal indicators. Additionally, the Cover aspects are +2 or -2 to whatever relevant side, since it's a major thing that affects just the signee, not the world around the signee.

                                          Once is happenstance. Twice is coincidence. Three times is enemy action. Four times is Infrastructure.

                                          Rule #2: Never let the game-mechanics get in the way of a good story.

                                              The Demon doesn't offer anything of anyone else's. They offer to take, or if this is the Mortal's request on a pact, they offer to give, and the Universe corrects for things. Maybe a transgender person finally gets the reassignment surgery they've been saving up for thanks to a sudden windfall. Maybe a baby that was supposed to be born male is born female.                                               Think of it like a pact granting Resources. The Demon doesn't go out and ruin someone to give their Mortal Buddy extra resources. The Universe just balances the ledger in some way. Demons are salespeople; the Universe is the accountant who makes sure the books are balanced.
