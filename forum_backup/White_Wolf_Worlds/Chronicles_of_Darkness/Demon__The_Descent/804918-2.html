        New Incepts:

        Consolidate

        Consolidate reconfigures an angel, reallocating G-M resources to suit evolving circumstances. By using this Incept, and angel can swap around the dots in Influences. It can only move dots into an Influence it already possess, but it can shift all of its dots into a single Influence if it sees fit. If an angel only possess a single Influence (as say, a Rank 1 angel) It may gain an additional dot in this influence in exchange for shutting down 4 Numina, which the angel cannot access until the Incept expires a

        As a secondary effect, the angel gets Rank + 1 points of armor, distributed between ballistic and general, and ballistic must equal or exceed general.

        Silence

        Leading Inquisitors have speculated on the existence of this Incept, or an Incept like it, based on the G-M’s ability to keep an entire 5th incarnation secret for hundreds, perhaps even thousands of years. No Analyst remembers this Incept, possibly hinting at a mind-wiping fail-safe that activates when Incept privilege are revoked by an angel’s fall.

        Using this Incept suppresses an angel’s essence noise. Any Demon trying to use Aetheric Resonance to locate the angel has their effective Primium reduced by 2. Additional, this Incept does not trigger Aetheric Resonance in the same way that other Incepts do, it just registers as a normal essence expenditure.

        As a secondary effect, any Embed or Exploit that would detect the angel fails, registering nothing, or a normal human, as appropriate.

        Notes: Each one of these was designed a with a very specific angel in mind. Consolidate was meant for The Brilliant, one of the earliest angels to show up in my chronicle, and also unfortunately the least successful. Since I was new to running nWod, and to the rules changes introduced by GMC, The Brilliant was… rather brutally dealt with by my explosion-happy players and failed to come off as the deadly apex predator I tried to portray him as.

        The experience wasn’t a total waste, I did learn some crucial lessons about how using angels effectively, and also how influences scale. An angel is more terrifying with three dots in a single influence than if those dots are scattered across multiple influences.

        Silence was something I dreamed up for Mr. Shivers. After the debacle with The Brilliant, I tried to see what I could do with a low value angel, I borrowed very heavily from Slashers, gave Mr. Shivers some form powers for stealth and combat (hey, if demons have them, angels should have them) and played him as an enemy that relied on patience and hit and run tactics against isolated targets of importance to the characters. Much more affection (among other things, he kidnapped 4 students from the school where one of the player’s worked. Creepiest moment of that session finding all 4 of their corpses beneath the frozen surface of a pond.)

        Bunyip, would be interested to hear your thoughts on silence.

            Hey, sorry for the delay.

            These are interesting. Incepts were designed to be a 'complete set', but it's cool that people have other requirements and ways they can be used.

            Originally posted by Agentwestmer View Post
            Consolidate

            Consolidate reconfigures an angel, reallocating G-M resources to suit evolving circumstances. By using this Incept, and angel can swap around the dots in Influences. It can only move dots into an Influence it already possess, but it can shift all of its dots into a single Influence if it sees fit. If an angel only possess a single Influence (as say, a Rank 1 angel) It may gain an additional dot in this influence in exchange for shutting down 4 Numina, which the angel cannot access until the Incept expires a

            As a secondary effect, the angel gets Rank + 1 points of armor, distributed between ballistic and general, and ballistic must equal or exceed general.
            Okay. First thoughts are that a Rank 1 spirit is only going to have 1-3 Numina (per GMC rules) - it will be incapable of shutting down 4 to gain an extra dot of Influence. So this either needs redesigning (if you do want Rank 1 angels to take advantage of it), or use a different example (if you want Rank 1 angels excluded). I'd be tempted to let angels with Consolidate move dots into Influences they don't have, but require that they leave at least one dot behind, and that the new Influence has to still be thematically appropriate to the angel's purpose. (I'd even consider letting angels with just 1 dot in an Influence move it to 1 dot in something else. I could be persuaded to even let them stack this with other single dots to create a short-lived more powerful Influence. Give the little guys some oomph.)

            I also don't see the link between Consolidate and armor (for the secondary effect), but don't have a problem with the effect itself.

            Originally posted by Agentwestmer View Post
            Silence

            Leading Inquisitors have speculated on the existence of this Incept, or an Incept like it, based on the G-M’s ability to keep an entire 5th incarnation secret for hundreds, perhaps even thousands of years. No Analyst remembers this Incept, possibly hinting at a mind-wiping fail-safe that activates when Incept privilege are revoked by an angel’s fall.

            Using this Incept suppresses an angel’s essence noise. Any Demon trying to use Aetheric Resonance to locate the angel has their effective Primium reduced by 2. Additional, this Incept does not trigger Aetheric Resonance in the same way that other Incepts do, it just registers as a normal essence expenditure.

            As a secondary effect, any Embed or Exploit that would detect the angel fails, registering nothing, or a normal human, as appropriate.
            Hmm, I'm not sold on this one. Incepts are meant to be loud. Their use either signifies that the God-Machine really wants something to happen right now, and doesn't care who knows, that the Output needs massive power that can't be easily hidden away - which means this is something the demons may want to disrupt ASAP, or that 'shit just got real' and some angel is unhappy. Silencing all this runs counter to that design philosophy. But putting that to one side, let's look at Silence under its own merits.

            First question is whether the activating Silence (which just registers as 'normal essence expenditure') benefits from its own effect on activation or not.

            Secondly, I'm not a fan of how the effect works mechanically, because it just takes a fixed amount based on the demon rather than scaling from the angel's Rank. Alternative effect suggestions:

            - The Angel gains an essence 'bank' equal to Rank+1. Essence spent in the scene up to this bank can't be detected by Aetheric Resonance.
            - Each use of essence is dampened by Silence. Each essence expenditure subtracts Rank/2 (round up) to determine its noise. If this is 0 or less, that essence expenditure is undetectable by Aetheric Resonance.
            - Demons subtract the angel's Rank from their Primum for the purpose of Aetheric Resonance. (This does mean powerful angels are much more effective at ambushing demons than lower Rank angels.)

            In any case, I'd have the essence and Incept 'erupt' in noise at the end of the scene, making it hugely detectable unless the angel spends another Willpower to extend the effect into subsequent scenes. Eventually it either runs out of Willpower or needs to find a place to allow the disturbance to happen.

            (I'd also rename 'Silence' to 'Redirect' or similar to maintain the product delivery theme of the other Incepts. Actually, I'd swap the names of the two. I'd change 'Consolidate' to 'Redicrect', and 'Silence' to 'Consolidate'.)

                    Here goes:

                    c.AI.n

                    Incarnation: Analyst
                    Virtue: Relentless
                    Vice: Visionary
                    Rank: 4
                    Attributes: Power 12, Finesse 8, Resistance 10
                    Influence: Surveillance • • • •
                    Corpus: 10 + *
                    Willpower: 10
                    Size: 6
                    Speed: 30 (10 species factor)
                    Defense: 8
                    Initiative: 20
                    Armor:
                    Numina: Aggressive Meme, Eyes Everywhere, Freeze Accounts**, Holy Ground, Implant Mission, Open Investigation, Merciless Gunman**, Murder By Improbability**, Power Out
                    Incepts: Delivery, Efficiency, Space, Units
                    Manifestation: Twilight Form, Discorporate, Dataform***, Possess****
                    Max Essence: 25
                    Ban: c.AI.n must pursue (through agents, if need be) any crime it sees. Being a demon is automatically a crime for the purposes of this Ban.
                    Bane: Proof that an executed individual was innocent. Embeds or exploits that damage or erase data are treated as lethal weapons if used against c.AI.n

                    Other: cult • • • • ( Loyal agents inserted into any number of espionage and law enforcement agencies)

                    * c.AI.n can only exist inside material object, so corpus will depend on what it is possessing
                    **as per the Embed or Exploit. Merciless Gun can only be used through Delivery by a friendly agent.
                    *** Spend 3 essence to manifest as data, treated as the Dataform
                    **** c.AI.n is limited to occupying technical objects such as computers, security systems, unmanned drones and spy satellites.

                    Description: c.AI.n is an unusual hunter-angel that is more akin to a siege weapon than an actual hunter. The G-M deploys c.AI.n when its needs make war on the Unchained. Using hundreds if not thousands of CCTV feeds, radio frequencies, cellphone cameras, c.AI.n begins a systematic hunt for all signs of deviance, mundane crimes result in police and security forces dispatches. More cunning targets might invite c.AI.n to dispatch one of its cultists to carry out an execution.

                    When a demonic ring or agency is discovered, c.AI.n goes into full attack. Open Investigation brings in multiple federal agencies and data form infiltrates school and workplace computers, find out where demons work and where their children go to school.

                    Whenever one of c.AI.n’s agents executes a criminal in the view of exactly 4 security cameras, that soul becomes a human sacrifice to fuel c.AI.n’s occult matrix.

                    While c.AI.n nothing but loyal to the G-M, it has taken to running calculations, simulations of ways that human society might be improved… for their own good of course.

                    Notes: I limited myself to rank four to make this angel deadly because of smart design and tactics, rather than just crushing players with huge dice-pools. With enough limitations, players have the ability to out maneuver and out think and enemy.

                    Nine numina is a hard limit when you have something as flavorful as a Samaritan knock-off. Eyes Everywhere is the showcase, to show how utterly broken the right combination of Incepts can be; by my math c.AI.n can affect either 46 or 78 security systems with a single essence, depending on how you want to fiddle with order of operations.
                    Freeze Account is is nice way of hounding a quarry and blackmailing allies. Merciless Gunman is based on Samaritan/The Machine’s “god mode.” With Delivery it can be used to covertly execute criminals… or turn a SWAT team into a nightmare that cuts apart demons. Holy Ground + Possess + Murder by Improbability gives it a nice control of the. Left hand-spanner disables cars and firearms and Power Out lets it do this.

                    Since the Space incept allows a numen to operate on line of sight, I wanted c.AI.n able to posses UAVs and spy satellites for a nice modern update to Enemy of the State. I was going to add blast to super charge electrical systems and blow up gas mains, but something had to go and that was already covered by Murder by Improbability.
                    While a very well designed angel, I say being an Analyst cripples it's effectiveness at being a weapon. That much of a reliance and communication with human agents or even other angellic units would be too great of a Fall risk. A Destroyer would of been a better choice for the type of mission this is, as this level of focus actually plays to their strengths well enough. Now c.AI. as a Destroyer wouldn't be able to process information as quickly, it's angelic status would be far more resilient as well as being more suited for this sort of counter insurgent/ military level of tactical surveillance and strategic positioning of resources. But then again, that is just me talking...
