                I will probably treat it like I do in Shadowrun for my own sanity's sake, bookkeeping-wise: applying Heal disqualifies someone from receiving it until they get hurt again. This does lead to the occasional scenario where a character (usually the group's Fridge Troll, who is built to survive this kind of thing) gets a giant flesh taco carved out of their abdomen, gets a healing spell from one of the group's casters, takes a tiny little bit of damage from someone trying to shoot them with a pistol, and then the next healing spell is working on taco damage too. It's much more likely that by the time the healer's turn comes around again, there will be another taco to deal with.

                And there are already other effects that imply a need to track individual wounds in Demon, like Shift Consequences. ...okay, maybe that's the only other one. Still, they're there.

                                                        Personally, for an embed I'd have gone with something more along the lines of an "It's only a flesh wound" sort of power.
                                                        A> Has to be used on the target before any one with medical training looks at them and assess the real extent of the damage.
                                                        B> Can reduce any amount of Bashing damage to 1 bashing total
                                                        C> OR can downgrade any amount of Lethal to 1 Lethal and the rest Bashing
                                                        D> OR can downgrade Primum Aggravated damage to Lethal, but must leave at least 1 Agg

                                                        The idea being, a Gunshot is never going to NOT be a gunshot, but in the heat of battle can you really know if it was a through-and-through in the fleshy part of the arm or if the bullet struck the shoulder joint? Not without taking a few moments to examine the wound. Yes, Bob got caught in the edge of the car exploding, and yes, he is Burned (aggravated Damage) but how badly? You don't know. People get struck by Lightning every year. Some of them die instantly, cooked and frazzled, but every so often someone lives, sometimes spending as little as 3 months recovering. If you're skydiving and your chute (and back up chute) fail, well, hitting the ground at terminal velocity also tends to be an insta-kill scenario, with most of the bones in your body shattered. Except there are documented cases of people literally getting up and walking (well, hobbling) away from such a fall, with minor fractures but no breaks. 13000 feet into the ground resulting in what, in game terms, might be 2 lethal and the rest of the bar bruised.

                                                        I'd build the healing power off the same quantum uncertainty that fuels things like In My Pocket. Everyone involved knows the target is injured, hence the power can't remove all injury, but unless or until someone with some real medical skill examines the wounded person you don't really know how badly they're hurt.
