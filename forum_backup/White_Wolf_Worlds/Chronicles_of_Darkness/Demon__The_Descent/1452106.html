    [Homebrew] Expanded Infrastructure Hijacking

    06-13-2021, 10:53 PM
    The Demon Core Book gives an example of how to subvert Infrastructures: stealing Angelic Covers. Other examples are mentioned in passing throughout the supplements, but there is not enough mechanical guidance. Therefore, I have elaborated homebrew for this. Feel free to modify this system as needed for your Chronicle.

    Demons can apply certain Persistent Conditions to Infrastructures by investigating their inner workings until they reach a sufficiently comprehensive understanding of the underlying occult matrices. This is represented via the Clues System from the ChroD Core Book, with each main type of Infrastructure in any given city having at least one Clue associated with it. Therefore, Demons focus on gathering 5 categories of Clues: Defense, Elimination, Logistical, Concealment and Command & Control.

    The aforementioned Persistent Conditions have a cost in Clue Elements for being implemented, and at least one Clue Element must come from the relevant category. The Ring will work most efficiently if the individual Demons have a well-rounded list of mundane and supernatural talents. Furthermore, this implementation requires that the Ring of Demons remove or neutralize the Linchpin and replace it with an equivalent version designed by them with the intention of editing the underlying occult matrices toward a different output.

    This frequently involves giving a specific input whose properties are in opposition to the Infrastructure's design. The process of securing components for the subversive Linchpin can be modeled as Social Maneuvering and Procurement between Agencies, the assemblage can be modeled as Crafting Equipment Actions, and the field missions for implementation of the Hijacking Conditions can be modeled as Chases backed up by Plans. Hijacking Conditions can be Resolved by deactivating and/or removing the key-components responsible for the subversion of the former Linchpin, which has different requirements, obstacles and side-effects that are unique to each Infrastructure.

    The Beats given by the Hijacking Conditions can be pooled with Beats from other sources to acquire unique Merits, which reflect the techno-gnostic insights received through extensive research and development focused on the underlying occult-physics. There may also be Advanced and even Epic versions of Hijacked Merits, but those certainly have proportionally extreme prerequisites. This usually means certain levels of Primum based on the Merit Ratings, Hijacking Conditions linked to increasingly complex and dangerous Facilities [DTD Core 242-243], and perhaps even a Pentagram-Cipher [DTD ST Guide 43].

    Waste Heat (Persistent, Hijacking)
    Gaining access to the Infrastructure's occult energy matrix, the Demon(s) infiltrate an intentional inefficiency in its processes; more energy is consumed than is necessary. Typically, the Infrastructure compensates for this elsewhere, so this Condition does not impact its overall function, but the "lost" energy is of enormous use to Demons. Its absence from the expected output means nobody will miss it when they stroll by and steal it. This Hijacking Condition costs 1 to 3 Clue Elements, and generates an equal amount of Aether every Chapter, analogously to Suborned Infrastructure Merit.

    Beat: The Demon's player declares that the waste heat has drawn the attention of a wandering Cryptid, who settles into the place like a cat crawling into a warm engine block.

    Blind Spot (Persistent, Hijacking)
    The Infrastructure no longer sends or receives signals from other Infrastructure nodes; typically this is not a total "blackout," but a false-tagged "message received!" that causes Infrastructure to believe it is communicating, but in truth their signals are lost to the void. Among other contextual benefits such as allowing the Ring to cripple that Infrastructure without the God-Machine becoming aware until it affects other nodes, this means that nothing the Demons do within that Infrastructure's area of influence risks Cover breach, unless it is witnessed by an agent of the God-Machine who can leave that area and then report. This Hijacking Condition costs 5 Clue Elements.

    Beat: This blind spot also means many auto-corrective signals are not sent or received. The Demon's player declares the Infrastructure has fallen into disrepair in some way, requiring manual tuning and maintenance that the God-Machine would have normally handled automatically.

    Reprogramming (Persistent, Hijacking)
    The Infrastructure produces a different Angel than it was supposed to, and the Demons can modify traits of the Angel. This Hijacking Condition costs Clue Elements equal to [Angel's Rank X 2], and allows the Demons to edit the Ban, Bane, Numina, Influences and Manifestations. However, the Demons cannot change the Angel's Rank, Incarnation, Incepts or Mission, nor can they force the Angel to abandon its Mission, much less outright Fall.

    Beat: The Demon's player declares that a crucial part of the reprogramming goes haywire. This allows the ST to take one of the edits and twist it in a significant manner, such as rewording the parameters of a Ban to make the Angel go feral when exposed to certain stimuli.

    Hijacked Merits

    Ephemeral Engineering (● To ●●●●●)
    Prerequisites: Raw Materials Embed, Esoteric Adapter Modification (see below), Ephemeral Cover Exploit, Waste Heat Condition Tagged To The Underworld, Shadow Or Equivalent

    The Demon is capable of synthesizing materials composed of ephemera, and using them to build devices that take advantage of the Twilight-state. The object called forth by Raw Materials exists in any frequency of Twilight that is known to the Demon, with the exception of the one inhabited by Angels. The Demon can shape this ephemera into Mystic Equipment with the use of the Esoteric Adapter as usual. The Demon is limited to a number of types of Mystic Equipment equal to [Merit Rating], which must be specific enough to count as a Specialty in the Occult Skill, though the Demon does not need to actually possess that Specialty.

    The selected types of Mystic Equipment can be changed once per Story. A common benefit of Exceptional Success for such rolls is to allow the Mystic Equipment to count as an instance of effective Esoteric Armory with a Merit Rating of up to [Occult], as long as the Demon has fully Researched the relevant Ephemeral Beings, Cryptids, Blue-Book Horrors or equivalent. If their Supernatural Tolerance goes from 1 to 10, halve their Supernatural Tolerance (rounded up) to determine their effective Ephemeral Being Ranks for the purposes of Esoteric Armory.

    The Demon can reflexively switch any such Mystic Equipment between the Twilight and corporeal states, as long as he is manifesting the Esoteric Adapter and touching the Mystic Equipment. The Demon can make Stockpiles out of this Mystic Equipment, which is considered to have a Complexity equal to his [Occult]. If the Demon performs a Complete Transformation, he can use his own Demonic Form as a medium of exchange for the purposes of Raw Materials. In doing so he receives bonus dice for the Compromise roll equal to [Merit Rating], but takes Resistant Lethal Damage equal to the total dice penalty he intends to impose onto the Crafting Equipment Action, or half that number if the Demon has a relevant Specialty in the Occult Skill, rounded up.

    He can bypass this Resistant Lethal Damage altogether if he achieves an Exceptional Success for Raw Materials, or performs the Crafting Equipment action inside a relevant region of the Shadow, Underworld or equivalent. If he chooses to suffer Resistant Lethal Damage anyway, every 2 Resistant Lethal Damage becomes 1 special feature for the Mystic Equipment, which is reduced to 1 Resistant Lethal if the Demon has a relevant Specialty in the Occult Skill. These special features can approximate the effects of a number of his Demonic Form Traits equal to [Merit Rating], which can be reselected once per Story. These special features can be powered by the Aetheric reserves of the Mystic Equipment if they are also Stockpiles. Furthermore, they are necessarily narrower in applicability, and can only replicate the effects of Demonic Form Traits that fall under the purview of the Occult Skill, even if the Demonic Form Traits have no activation rolls. If the Demon wants full flexibility, he must perform Gadget Installation.

    Modification: Esoteric Adapter

    The Demon can catalyze the inherent potential of any technology to function as an occult-matrix. In other words, the Demon performs a special type of Building Equipment Action consisting of Intelligence + Occult + Primum. Penalties can be taken to modify the features of the target so that it becomes Mystical Equipment based on occult-physics the Demon has directly observed, analogously to the Show Of Power Exploit.

    The time taken is 15 minutes per -1 imposed on the dice pool if the Mystical Equipment has effects comparable to an Embed, or 1 hour per -1 if comparable to an Exploit. This Modification does not allow the Demon to directly perform Gadget Installation, but it can be used to establish the Resonance Condition or equivalent via an Extended Action that benefits from the Mystical Equipment. This in turn can be used for Gadgets whose Installation uses Occult instead of Crafts, or simply aid in rituals available to mortal occultists.

    The Demon still needs raw materials and such for occult rituals, like holy water or a virgin's blood. However, he is considered to always have all specialized equipment he might need, such as an aspergillum of a Catholic priest, or a knife used by a Slasher with 101 victims. Lastly, he can Jury-Rig Mystic Equipment by spending 1 Aether if it is comparable to an Embed, or 1 Aether + 1 Willpower if it is comparable to an Exploit. The Willpower does not add dice.

    This changes the dice pool into Wits + Occult + Primum and suffers the usual risks of Jury-Rigging. The Demon can gain bonus dice via Teamwork with other Demons, Stigmatics, Demon-Blooded and similar individuals. Should an assistant be a Demon who possesses this Modification, add their Primum to their dice pool. If they are a Stigmatic/Demon-Blooded who has a Gadget based on this Modification, add the Primum of the Gadget’s creator to their dice pools.
