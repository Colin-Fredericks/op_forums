                        I like the idea of summoning lesser demons, anything from hellhounds to lesser NPC demons. As for being summoned, like I said, demons should get something out of it besides just a simple bargain. Something that makes the risk worth it. Ok, here is a thought, so lets say someone does a ritual that summons a demon. The demon "hears" the call. Now, they have two choices, they can ignore it and nothing will happen, or they can answer it and see who is on the other line. Now, some of the benefits could be they automatically get specific embeds/exploits for this person/situation. Ok, say they get alibi, so instead of the demon actually telephoning themselves to the circle, they can send one of their covers (if they have more than one) instead to do the willing and dealing while the real demon is nice and safe. Worse case scenario is an angel is waiting and the demon loses a cover. Secondly, the demon could automatically get hearts desires so they automatically know what the person really wants.

                        Now, flip it around for another game line, you want to make contact with a DTD demon to make a deal, how would you do it? They are all in hiding and unless they are idiots, they cover their tracks pretty well. Summoning is the most common anwser that comes to mind. Look up specific information/rituals on how to summon the demon. Find out what draws it, its nature. Do the ritual and hope the demon shows up.

                        On a side note, As mentioned, an exploit that can hear their name being mentioned and/or summoning would be cool. So some demons could be summoned and others could not. The spreading rumor is cool and has a lot of potential, but like I said. Summoning is so iconic for demons that summoning practically means demons because that is usually what is summoned. I just think it would add a lot to the game.

                                                        Being able to be summoned is a useful power to have, not just for the Summoner. I admit, I did a custom power (its an NPC so I'm not sure if its an Exploit or Propulsion) that let's a Demon set up summoning conditions for them self. When the conditions are met, the demon gets to see the summoning area and choose to answer or not. It's useful because it removes the need to locate the target, eliminates the risk of physical travel, and can be turned down. Downsides are that the Summon doesn't have a return trip, and disguises/ambushes are still possible. To cut down on potential accidents (ie summoner is fine, but the only exit of the room leads to angels), the demon has to be careful who knows how to summon him. Still, its an impressive way to start the negociations for Pacts.
