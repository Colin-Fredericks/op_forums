    Questions About Glitches And Demonic Powers I Haven't Seen In Other Threads

    09-07-2015, 10:26 PM
    Do Embeds, Exploits and Gadgets stack with Merits and other mundane sources of dice bonuses?

    Does a Demon need to know an Embed or Exploit before Installing it into a Gadget? You probably do need the original power if you are building a Demonic Form Gadget, and you can uninstall Gadgets to learn the Embeds, Exploits and Demonic Forms inside them. But I am not sure if you could make a laser cannon without knowing the Hellfire Exploit first, or an Angel detecting radar without knowing the Voice Of The Machine Embed.

    Can you combine Demonic Forms, Embeds and Exploits? For example, a Destroyer is ambushed, and Down & Dirty Combat is not an option. Could he combine his Rivet Arm Modification with the Merciless Gunman Embed and Hellfire Exploit? That would let him shoot the enemies with +3A and 8 Again (Rote Quality if the activation of Merciless Gunman gets an Exceptional Success)?

    Can Spoofing, Going Native, Deep Cover and similar powers temporarily hide Glitches? Brands and Emanations might be hidden with these powers, but Tells might just require a Subterfuge or Persuasion roll to explain away as mundane habits. If it is possible, I'm guessing that it must be very exhausting, thus requiring additional Aether, 1 willpower point, dice penalties or any combination thereof.

    Can restoration facilities cure the permanent Glitches caused by increasing Primum and suffering a Dramatic Failure on a Compromise roll? From what I've gathered, yes. So, can a Demon with Primum 9 and who fails Compromise rolls often enter a restoration facility and come out with no Glitches at all? I do know that restoration facilities are very well guarded, I just want to be sure if I'm reading the rules correctly.

    Is there an upper limit for how many Glitches a Demon can have? If so, what happens if he exceeds that limit? I think it could be interesting if too many Glitches caused Aggravated damage that can only be healed with mundane Medicine rolls or waiting.

      The following is all just my interpretation, so I won't be providing sources or page numbers.

      Does a Demon need to know an Embed or Exploit before Installing it into a Gadget? Yes. It doesn't really make any sense otherwise, how can you make an object that utilizes a backdoor pathway in the rules of reality if you don't know those backdoors?

      Can you combine Demonic Forms, Embeds and Exploits? Of course. There's no reason you shouldn't be able to.

      Can Spoofing, Going Native, Deep Cover and similar powers temporarily hide Glitches? No. The whole point of glitches is that they stick with you, and none of those abilities explicitly say they bypass that general rule.

      Can restoration facilities cure the permanent Glitches caused by increasing Primum and suffering a Dramatic Failure on a Compromise roll? I believe so. They cure glitches, and it doesn't state anywhere that there's a limitation for 'Permanent' Glitches. It makes restoration facilities a high value target for powerful demons with lots of glitches, which has a lot of story potential in my mind. If you're stuck with a temporary glitch, why would you hunt down Infrastructure to remove it when you could just hunker down in your safehouse for a day or two? I'd say Permanent glitches are probably the main meta-reason that those facilities are a thing.

      Is there an upper limit for how many Glitches a Demon can have? No, I don't think so. It's never mentioned anywhere.

                  Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                  Can Spoofing, Going Native, Deep Cover and similar powers temporarily hide Glitches? Brands and Emanations might be hidden with these powers, but Tells might just require a Subterfuge or Persuasion roll to explain away as mundane habits. If it is possible, I'm guessing that it must be very exhausting, thus requiring additional Aether, 1 willpower point, dice penalties or any combination thereof.
                  Spoofing: No, not any more than you could partially manifest a rivet gun and then spoof to make people not notice it. Spoofing just deflects the use of supernatural abilities to detect the Unchained as supernatural beings. A glitch is not somebody else using a sensory power, it's an overflow problem you need to deal with personally, either by hiding it normally and waiting it out or by repairing it. Deep Cover just expands the versatility of spoofing, and so is also of no use here.

                  Going Native: Yes, I would allow Going Native to suppress glitches. They will come back when the power expires, and I'd freeze the timer on temporary glitches for the duration so you'd still have to wait those out as a demon, but Going Native genuinely makes you a mortal human for its duration. Mortal humans do not normally suffer glitches, and Going Native has enough drawbacks and dangers to its use that it generally ought to be very effective at the one thing it does.

                  Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                  Can restoration facilities cure the permanent Glitches caused by increasing Primum and suffering a Dramatic Failure on a Compromise roll? From what I've gathered, yes. So, can a Demon with Primum 9 and who fails Compromise rolls often enter a restoration facility and come out with no Glitches at all? I do know that restoration facilities are very well guarded, I just want to be sure if I'm reading the rules correctly.
                  Permanent glitches incurred by compromise: absolutely yes. Most temporary glitches are short-lived enough that it's simply not worth finding a facility. Infiltrating a facility is enough of a big deal that, yes, it ought to be able to patch you up from ongoing glitches from compromise.

                  Permanent glitches acquired by high Primum: I would say no. Compromise glitches result from a short-circuit of your Cover: they can be traced to a cause and corrected. Primum glitches result from having such strong Primum that it overflows past the human seeming of Cover. It's a narrative drawback attached to surpassing power, similarly to vampires' need for increasingly potent vitae and werewolves' growing need to hunt and consume. It's coming from inside the house.

                  There is a way to get rid of Primum glitches: reduce your Primum below the glitch threshold (which ought to be something that can be done by hijacking facilities in a similar manner). If you try to strip the glitch without reducing your Primum, your overflowing Primum is going to need another release vent. If it's not a new glitch to replace the old one, it's probably even worse.

                                                  Also, I have another question. Characters inside the Astral Realms look like how they imagine themselves to look. If a Demon used Rip The Gates to enter the Astral, does he end up looking like his Demonic Form, or does the Cover stay strong? I'd say that Demons with a Cover of 6+ don't suffer any inconvenience in that regard, but if their Cover is 5 or lower, they become easier to track through the Astral, as they leave behind trails that clever Astral travelers can identify. This is not a problem with the Demon's emotional self-control, but a problem with his Cover. Also, when they enter the portal to the Astral, where exactly do they emerge? Their Oneiros?
