                                                        Ok, take a look and see what you think. Hope this will work. I left a section for Breaking Points on page 2, but if it's needed, let me know.... Think I covered everything else though. If you think it's done, then I'll list it on the site.

                                                        Mostly good, but the section on Interlocks isn't very helpful. If it were just lines for the name of each Interlock, then you'd only need three lines, because you can't have a fourth Interlock (under normal rules); if it's meant to contain the rules for each, it's entirely too short. I don't know how much work it would be to fit everything you'd need into the second page, but my recommendation would be to use the Interlock block from the normal Demon sheet, the one that takes up like half the second page. Just listing the names of the Interlocks is really unhelpful because they're custom powers and you can't just look the name up in a book.

                                                        Demon-blooded do in fact have Integrity breaking points like normal mortals. You can probably get away with leaving space for them on the sheet out if you have to, though; breaking points are relatively freeform.
