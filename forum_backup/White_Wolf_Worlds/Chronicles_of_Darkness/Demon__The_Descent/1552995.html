    Lambda Gadget Creation: Blade of Water

    08-07-2025, 08:03 PM
    I have been running a long term game of Demon. We have a player creating a new character and joining the game late. Most of the characters in the game have been focused gun bunnies and had gadgets build or purchased to turn them into even more dangerous firearms wielder. However, this new character wants to use weapons and avoid firearms altogether if he can avoid it. To support this and because there is not merit to support starting Gadgets. I have decided to allow him to come in with a gadget weapon to be on par with the others in the group.

    I have decided to create a Tier 1 Lambda Gadget sword for him.

    I am looking to use the Embed Ripple and the Exploit Break The Dam as the basis of this gadget. I'm looking for a gadget sword hilt that when activated would gather the moisture in the air to form the blade of the weapon. When not active it looks like maybe an strange anime sword hilt. I think thematically, these fit but I am having trouble with a designing the mechanics. Would appreciate some suggestion.

      Without knowing what else you want this thing to be able to do, it sounds like making it a lambda might be overengineering the thing; a form gadget using the described water-based variant of the Laser Cutter form power would probably do the trick if all you need is a rationale for having a sword hilt that serves as a projected hydrocutter.

      If that's not workable, remember that regardless of the design process that gets you there and the breadth of functionality, a lambda is made out of multiple gadgets and can be no smaller than the largest of its components, and gadgets are by design more limited (and lateral, in the case of near-field effects) versions of their incorporated Embeds and Exploits. What gadgets that derive from Ripple and Break the Dam were used to make this thing? Were all its components made by the same demon? To what end were the pieces made and how did the PC come into the lambda? The answers to these questions may help connect the lambda and its bearer to the larger world in a way that also expands the story, especially if they can be linked to the character's answers to the Five Questions detailing potential sources of compromise.

      On a more practical level, while it's nice to have a niche to focus on, it's probably worth noting that if the character intends to focus on melee weaponry to the exclusion of firearms altogether then they are angling either much quieter or much more distinctive re: solving problems with violence, such that it's important to know if their intended playstyle would benefit from being handed an obviously unnatural weapon. In terms of the quadrants laid out in the Storyteller's Guide, is this player participating in a Smashing, Insurgent, Gritty, or Silent game?

Was it made with Suborned Infrastructure? (Was it found in Infrastructure?) Was it borrowed or stolen from another demon who made it with a specific purpose in mind?

              A first-order lambda may not be quite on the scale of the Dreaming Machine, but when dealing with the closest thing techgnostic espionage has to storied artifacts it's helpful to be able to track the history of a gadget potent enough for its aetheric resonance to be detected from a block away by the weakest of demons in the absence of protective shielding.

                  I spoke to the player about the situation. I created these


                  Resonant Dagger (Ripple Precision Engineered Embedded Gadget)


                  The Resonant Dagger is a simple and extremely dangerous dagger. Forged from Beryllium Copper, the sword seems to glow with a warm sunset light,etched with wavy, curving, angelic, circuitry-script.


                  Activation and Triggers: (Reflexive) To activate the dagger there is a switch in the handle that changes the line of script on the handle. Once ready, a simple tap on a hard surface causes the dagger to ring like a chime and remains this way, until the dagger hilt is untwisted.


                  Effect: When activated the blade becomes an incredibly efficient damaging weapon. Targets who receive damage from the blade are infused with destructive sonic energy. When the target next moves, this energy bursts forth inflicting additional damage. Half of any damage (round down) +1 inflicted by an attack of the Resonant Dagger at the beginning of the target’s next action.


                  (eg. Thomas strikes a monster with Resonant Dagger inflicting 5 points of lethal damage. On the monster’s next action, the power of the dagger triggers inflicting 3 points of additional lethal damage 5/2 (round down) 2+1 = 3.)


                  Resonant Dagger

                  Damage +1 Initiative 0 Strength 1 Size 1 8-again Enhance: Crafts or Survival


                  ---


                  Vampiric Tongue (Break the Dam Exploited Gadget)


                  The Vampiric Tongue is a dangerous gadget. Shaped like a dark, hollow, metallic, ice-pick, with a three sharp thread like that on a screw edged in silver-white. The blade is sharp and has a wicked hungry edge to it.


                  Activation and Triggers: (Reflexive) The weapon hungers for blood and fluids. Whenever the weapon strikes a target, the wielder flicks a switch hidden in the crossguard of the blade.


                  Effect: After striking a target, the wielder makes a Presence + Medicine + 3 - target’s Stamina. Each success inflicts an additional point of lethal damage, as the blade draws out the blood of the target and into the hollow. Unfortunately Vampiric Tongue has no effect on targets without blood.


                  Vampiric Tongue

                  Damage +2 Initiative 0 Strength 1 Size 1


                  ---


                  Obscuring Clouds (Lamba - Resonant Blade & Vampiric Tongue)


                  Created to be a powerful weapon in the hands of the Unchained, this beautiful and deadly lambda controls the moisture in the air and turns it into a weapon. The lambda looks like the hilt of a sword made with 3 intertwined streams of metal, one obsidian black, one silver and the last copper red. At the bottom of the hilt, the 3 streams of metal flow and form into a Dara Knot with 4 stones in different shades of blue at the center of each quadrant of the knot.


                  Activation and Triggers: Instant. The wielder can activate the Lambda by sliding the 4 stones towards the center of the Knot. Moisture is pulled from the air forging the deadly weapon. This weapon can take 1 of 4 forms depending on the configuration of the stones.


                  Effect: In the Lambda’s default form the Obscuring Clouds can be used like a Sap. Once activated, the blade of the weapon pulls moisture from the air and forms a white, misty, edge that functions and cuts just like steel. Due to the light weight nature of the weapon the strength requirement and initiative penalty to properly wield the weapon is reduced by 1.


                  Hilt Damage +0 Initiative –0 Strength 1 Size 1

                  Knife Damage +1 Initiative –0 Strength 1 Size 2

                  Katana Damage +3 Initiative –1 Strength 1 Size 3

                  Great Sword Damage +4 Initiative –4 Strength 3 Size 3 9-again, Two-Handed
