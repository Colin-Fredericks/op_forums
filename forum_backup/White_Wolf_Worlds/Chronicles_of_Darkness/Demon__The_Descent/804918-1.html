            Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
            Have 3 new Numina that are very useful for Angels. Feedback is appreciated.
            You got it.

            Branding: Conceptually, I loved this. It recalls the scene where Agent Smith deletes Neo's mouth in The Matrix. It's creepy and give a nice sense of body horror: not only can the angels smite you from on high, they can rip you apart on the genetic level. Also, you reference Eyes Everywhere

            Mechanically, though, it's frustrating. This Numen reveals a demon, so Spoofing applies. Then Supernatural Resistance should be added in the resistance roll. Demons use their Cover, which is typically much higher than Primum. If their Primum is higher they have the option of Supernatural Bulwark. This Numen isn't reliable unless a demon is careless enough to have a low cover and low Primum, or the angel boosts it with a few Incepts. That's built into the setting; cover is the most critical protection a demon has, so it must be robust against supernatural assault. Note that the most effective Demon-identifying Numen the setting offers works by targeting everyone else.

            Triggering glitches isn't something an angel needs to do at close range. If the angel is close enough to use this Numina and knows they're dealing with a demon, they're in a position to do more interesting (e.g. violent) things, or just drive to the demon's home and tell her family some interesting details ("Did you know your wife was originally sent to run you over with a car? Funny but true." Since the angel is present in the scene, their rank would count against the compromise roll)

            I see the use for preventing a demon from fleeing into another cover, but this Numen feels like it should be re-worked into a tool that flushes a demon out of hiding, a techgnostic dye pack in a bank robber's bag. Maybe the angel could activate it by touching someone who has a cover-pact with the demon?

            Healer: Very useful for angels recruiting cultists, or leading them into battle. Also setting appropriate, as the GMC mentions that angels cut deals with mortals just as demons do, they're just not as savvy or as cut-throat.

            Three suggestions/observations:

            First, try trimming the word count and mechanical crunch. Most antagonist abilities in CoD are very sparsely described with simple mechanics to allow a story-teller to apply the effect without slowing the pace.

            Second, include a provision where an angel can undo the healing. It's thematic since the original GMC makes it clear that breaking a deal with an angel is a Very Bad Idea, and as far as the G-M is concerned, a body's health is all just data that can be manipulated, including the mystical version of "ctrl+z". From a narrative perspective, it puts anyone an angel helps in exchange for service on a very short leash. Dealing with a sleeper or simulacra is unnerving. Dealing with a man who serves an angel because his son can die with a snap of the angel's fingers? That's chilling.

            Third, that this Numen covers that same ground as Regenerate from the GMC, and does more besides. Maybe Regenerate is a prerequisite for this Numen?

            Enduring: Not quite up to the other two, unfortunately. Angels, as ephemeral beings already have the ability to boast their attributes by rank + 2 by spending essence, and the main GMC already has the Numina Stalwart and Regenerate.

                Angelic covers are exactly whatever the Angel is supposed to be. They're reinforced by the God-Machine, so no amount of mundane digging will reveal them to be anything other than what they claim. A school principal who has worked there for years has always worked there for years, even if she just Outputted from an occult matrix yesterday. An angel with cover as a building will appear to have occupied that site since the records show the building was constructed, even if the God-Machine moved things around (like a Dark City) to place it there.

                Angelic cover doesn't follow Demon rules because of their solidity. Angels can be detected by doing things very outside cover, like throwing around Numina and unleashing their full power, but even these tend to go away with time - even quicker if it uses the Witness Protection Numen.

                Edit for clarity: Angelic covers are more plot device than something to be encapsulated in rules. They're impenetrable to mortals, except when they're not for plot reasons. A person on the street will never notice the building that wasn't there yesterday, except when they do because the Storyteller needs that person's unbelievable story to reach the ears of certain demons.

                                            Yes, I sympathise with the confusion. I asked the same questions when working on the DSTG.

                                            Angels performing their function don't have to worry about essence bleed. Either the G-M continually tops them up, pays the cost for them, or has some other reality hack in place. The specifics don't matter, losing Essence from simply existing isn't an angelic concern.

                                            Similarly, angels whose function requires a body simply have a body. They're either Outputted with one, or they permanently take over some poor human's shell, or they materialise. If their function requires permanent physical access, there's no cost to the angel.

                                            Not every angel's Function require this. Some are meant to be incorporeal and have to use Numina to gain physical access (following normal spirit rules).

                                            In short, functional requirements are more important to angels than strictly following rules for ephemeral entities. This is particularly true with physical form.
