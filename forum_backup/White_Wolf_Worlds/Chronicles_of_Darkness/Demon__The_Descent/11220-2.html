        @Ryu

        I asked these questions on another thread. If you think they're good questions, could you add them to the Q&A?

        The exploit for Raise Dead p 259 indicates that demons use it in pacts with humans. Page 291 indicates that all benefits a demon can grant a mortal come from reality hacking that is automatically successful with the proper expenditures of willpower.

        Can demons resurrect family members through a pact without the Raise Dead exploit?

        Here's how I understand the rules so far: Giving a mortal a merit, healing damage, or removing persistent conditions (such as an illness), or increasing skills or attributes can be handled through the reality editing of the pact. Anything outside of that might require an embed or exploit.

        I would guess that a demon cannot grant immortality through any means that would add a major template to the mortal (Vampirism, etc), but the demon could do so by granting a merit based minor template, like as "Thaumaturge" or "Psychic". Example: April asks the demon for immortality. The demon grants him the External Alchemy merit and tells David to start brewing immortality potions.

        Can pacts exist that don't involve reality hacking? Example: David Talbot (Mortal) doesn't just want to be immortal. He wants to be a Vampire, but since Louis (vampire) won't turn him, he goes to Asmodeus (demon) for help. Asmodeus can't mystically transform David into a vampire since the mystery of Vitae isn't in the demon's purview, but he's had amicable dealings with Lestat (vampire), so he calls in a favor and has David embraced to hold up his end of the pact.

        Last question: can a demon grant a mortal the ability to use an Embed without just giving the mortal a gadget? I ask because this is one of the things classic DTF demons could grant mortals in exchange for Faith.

                                            I suppose it depends of the specialty.A Craft specialty in Butchery, taking the example from the book, can be a breach if the current Cover is that of a vegetarian. However, if at startup, that same Demon has a second Cover as, say, a chef or a hunter, then this is a perfectly reasonable specialty for them to have, and they're going to get a 2nd Specialty that may endanger this Cover, or at least that's how I'd do it.
