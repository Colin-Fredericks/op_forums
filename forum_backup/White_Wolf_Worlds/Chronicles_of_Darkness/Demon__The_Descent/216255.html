    Demon: Redescention, or DtF + DtD + DtR

    08-01-2014, 07:16 PM
    Or, welding together Demon: the Redemption,Demon: the Fallenand Demon: the Descent. Angels who wrote the universe, a Demiurge with it's boot to the throat of the world, angels enforcing the divine workings of the demiurge, demons escaping the pit to possess humans, filled with Torment. Cool things. The general thrust of the setting will be this;
    • Angels exist in the setting, and are at a cold war with the Fallen. Think the USSR and the US, the Pit is the home of the demons, and the Heavens for the Angels, the mortal world is where divine workings happens, where demons make pacts for souls.
    • Demons come from either the great Fall when the Universe broke (the Death of Father Wolf, the fall of Atlantis, ect) or from Angels who lose their way, and Fall themselves. Newly Fallen are pulled into the Pit and develop Torment like their brethren.
    • Cover is switched for Torment, which makes it easier for Angel's to sense demons, and deforms and harms the host of Demons, at 10 Torment a host will die (working idea. Replaces the idea of Covers going away at 0)
    Power-wise, I suggest using the Dominions of Demon: the Redemption. They represent powers corrupted from their Angelic Influences, giving them dominion over natural forces of the world, which the Houses helped write. (Cross over-wise, in my setting, the Angels are the one's who wrote the symbols that compose the Supernal Realms, and the Fallen were shoved into the Lower Depths where they mutated, and got corrupted; their True Names twisting to the Torments of the Pitt [Or perhaps the Inferno?])

    I love the concept of Occult Matrices, and wish to keep the basic idea, but stripping away the techgnostic facade, leaving only the Gnostic (hopefully). I'd call them 'Divine Workings' and have them, basically, like Imperial Rites. They require people, conditions, linchpins, to run. Probably replace Infrastructure and gears with occult places, ritual trappings, cults gathering Aether, and other such things.
    Edit: The Dominions written so far can be found here:     https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...ZAs7VpYo8/edit
    Main Document: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...9L2cAdFsk/edit

    Anyways, here are the Character Creation rules I have, so far;
    Character Creation
    • 5/4/3 to Attributes
    • 11/7/4 to Skills
    • 4 Specialties
    • Choose a House
    • Choose a Creed
    • Pick two Dominions, one from your House, and the other from what you wish.
    • Create an Apocalyptic Form, 3 Transfigurations, 2 Projections, 1 Conveyance, and 1 Countenance. Then pick the High Torment traits for your form, 2 Transfigurations (or 2 Projections), 1 Projection (or 1 Transfiguration), 1 Conveyance, and 1 Countenance.
    • Select 10 Merit dots.
    • Create a (Demonic) Urge, and (Angelic) Purpose
    • Create 3 Aspirations – 1 long-term, 2 short-term
    • Answer questions (Any help with the five questions for the Fallen?)
    • Calculate Advantages: Reminiscence is 1, Torment is 3, starting Aether is 10 – Torment, Speed is Strength + Dexterity + 5, Size is 5, Health is Stamina + Size, Willpower is Resolve + Composure, Defense is (lower of Wits and Dexterity) + Athletics, Initiative is Dexterity + Composure.
    Innate Abilities of the Fallen
    Liar's Tongue: As per the Unchained
    Immunity to Possession: Because the Fallen are already humans possessed by the Tormented essence of a Fallen angel.
    Invocation: System Pending
    Damage Resistance: In Apocalyptic Form, all Fallen have 1/1 Armor.
    Apocalyptic Form: Demonic form, per Descent. Spend 1 Aether to shift into Apocalyptic Form, shifting back to Human is free; can spend any time you want in Apocalyptic form. May have an increase of Torment in Apocalyptic Form, not sure yet.
    Aetheric Healing: Reflexive Action, 1 Faith per damage, Lethal before Bashing.
    Language Knowledge: As per the Unchained.

    Reminiscence is the Power Stat of the Fallen, measuring how much they remember of their Angelic glory, and Demonic might. Raising your Reminiscence will be a sin against Torment (I'm not sure what level it will be). The Integrity stat for the Fallen is Torment. It measures how twisted the Fallen is by their time in the Lower Depths, and by their own hate. Torment is a reverse stat, 1 Torment is saintly, where Torment 10 is a complete monster. I will probably keep the concept of Temporary Torment points. Unlike a normal Morality stat, failing a Torment roll gives you a certain number of Temporary Torment points too your total; when you reach 10 temporary Torment points, your permanent Torment score goes up by 1. When you do deeds to drop your Torment, you subtract the points from temp. Torment points, until you get to 0, then you take a dot and subtract any remaining from the 9 left. (E.G, you get -2 Torment points and have only 1 temp. Torment, and 4 perm. Torment. You would make your perm score to 3, and gain 8 temp. Torment.)

    Fallen spend Aether which will be fluffed as the original stuff of Creation. A more refined Pyros, the stuff above Mana, a less base Essence. Moving it from the waste heat of the God-Machine, to the divine essence. Angels get theirs from Above, while Demons must harvest scraps of it from humans (or other ensouled beings) with acts of Revelation. Which do give the mortal a Breaking Point... that's up for the demons to debate the morality of it. Cults are another source of Aether for the Fallen. Many of the demonic Cultists are in a pact with them, and are at Reminiscence 0 with a Minor template.


    That's about the extent of the system I have so far, if you have any critiques, suggestions, or something you want to see, please do so.

      On Angels, and the Demiurge
      Angels, servants of the high heavens, implacable enemies of the Fallen, and former comrades. You come from the same source, but are part of two different cultures, and existences now. In Demon: the Descent, a lot of the horror of facing the God-Machine's angels was the fact they could take you back, and remake you. Losing your free-will, or just be used for spare parts. The Fallen are different, and the Elohim are different than the angels of the God-Machine. Still Gnostic, and still deadly, implacable foes. What makes them scary? My wife suggests that the Elohim can still recycle a demon who's host is destroyed, and who goes into Twilight form. They can cart off the shell of the Fallen back to Heaven and remake you into an angel. Personally, I think this makes the Integrator/Reconciler Creed nonsensical. If one of the Creeds wishes to be redeemed, isn't the easiest way to throw one's self into the arms of the Hunter seraph and let it recycle your celestial essence into a pure, non-Tormented form? It seems to easy for me. It's an interesting idea.

      I've established that the Demiurge still makes new angels for Missions, but when the celestial Truename is brought into existence, and it's symbol and form spun it's eternal. It lasts for a long time, perhaps an eternity. The reason that the Fallen are, well, Fallen is because angels do not mutilate, and cannot truly kill their own. It's why the Fallen fear the Pit, nothing can end the torture, and the it's twisting effect. Or perhaps, the Celestial arn't that tough, they can be killed? Perhaps it's part of the Fallen's curse that they are always consumed by the Inferno, and the angelic host can die, or that the crafty Demiurge can bring one of the Fallen before him and get a new servant. One of the ideas I had is that Reconcilers KNOW that God is gone, and an impostor sits upon the Throne of Heaven. The Reconciler's new goal would be working with, and convincing Angels of this, and perhaps looking for a way the Demiurge. They'd need to stay pure themselves, because they view themselves as Angels who were screwed over for knowing the truth. This is thematic, but changes the direction of Reconciler's quite a bit. I'm leaning towards the last one, but am not sure about the subject of the Elohim being able to turn demons into angels, I'm not sure if it's thematic to this hack.

      Moving on. I mentioned the Fallen and Angels having a truename. Much like Fallen (and Redemption) both the Fallen and Elohim possess Celestial names, and Truenames. The Celestial name is what you use to cast an Invocation (or listen on to it) when a Celestial name is spoken, the being that possess that Celestial name can listen in on, or establish a two way connection. It gives a way for Angels and demons to talk. Or the Fallen to talk to their fellows. It's much like getting the cellphone number of the specific being. (except with added perks). When an Angel or Demon uses their senses to investigate Aetheric Resonance, they can pull a portion of the user's Truename, or their Celestial name from the sense of the Resonance. Truenames allow greater control over your victim, making abilities easier to use against them, it gives you a sense of their Angelic Purpose, and Demonic Urge. That kind of stuff.

      Also, I've gotten permission to translate Demon: the Redemption, and at the very least, I will do so for the Dominions, and post them in a GoogleDoc for all to see, if you're interested.

          Embeds & Dominions (and the Cipher)
          I've been thinking recently. Dominions are nice, BIG effects that the Fallen can use. It touches upon their nature as creators of the universe, Embeds (Which I need a new name for? Any suggestions) touch on the aspect of 'secrets paths of the universe' which make as much sense for the Fallen as it does the Unchained. It also gives cheap, subtle, powers for the Fallen to get, and it also opens up using the Cipher for the Fallen. Mr. Paurdo's hack for DtD names his (more demonic) demons as their 'Malpraxis' which is a good, thematic name, but I'm bouncing two ideas to what the Cipher (Malpraxis?) for the Fallen is. One is that it's something the Creator encoded into them, that they only realize when they Fall. Perhaps hinting at the Fallen were supposed to always Fall. Or, I was thinking that the Cipher can go down two paths, one when the Demon starts to redeem himself, and become more Angelic, or one where they embrace their new nature (Beneficence and Malpraxis? Maybe Stupid Loserman's suggestion of Mysteries would work better). In any case, this new Cipher would be tied to an integral Dominion and Embeds that they buy, otherwise, it's built much like an Unchained's Cipher just flavored by the chosen Dominion(s?).

          Speaking of Dominions, not everyone is familiar with Redemption. The Dominions are, basically, corrupted Angelic Influences. They are aspects of reality that the Fallen can control with their Aether. Things like the Dominion of Flames allows the Fallen to manipulate, strengthen, create, and basically have Dominion of fire. One's like the Dominion of Radiance is about controlling people with a supernatural Radiance (think Presence from Vampire), Dominion of Ghosts allows one to, you guessed it, interact with and control the Dead.

          Embeds, Cover, and Torment
          Now, Embeds sometimes require a Demon to make a compromise check. Now, the Fallen don't HAVE Cover, they have Torment. Now, I haven't homed in on what Torment entails. My initial thought is that any time a Fallen uses a Dominion's Evocation they roll for Torment (Resolve + Composure + Reminiscence) with a modifier based on how high or low their Permanent Torment is (Below 3 = +2, and above 6 = -2) with extra minuses if the Fallen tries spending more than their natural limit of Aether on an Evocation (so if a low Reminiscence Fallen can only spend 1 Aether per turn, but wants to use a 3 Aether Evocation when they finally cast it, it will be at a -2 to the Torment roll) this allows for most powers to have a slim chance of Evoking Torment, but keeps it a risk. Purposely Evoking Torment gives you 1 temp. Torment automatically. Tormened Evocation are always more destructive, and harming. In some situations it should be tempting to Evoke Torment on your Evocation, but that comes with a price. For instance, a Evocation of the Dominion of Flames may cause the fire to grow larger, and hotter while it turns an unnatural color, and spreads more than the Fallen intended. This may be helpful in hurting additional antagonists, but it may also harm allies.
          The intent is to have Torment replace the compromise checks of Exploits. With that said, Torment can be sensed by Aetheric Senses, and Angels are attuned to the dark energies, and can pick up Torment at a range depending on the amount of Aether that was spent on the Tormented Evocation.

          I've noticed that not a lot of replies have been here. I will continue to update this, but I am up for any critique or suggestions. To those watching, thanks. My wife and I are working on translating Demon: the Redemption's Dominions to English and as soon as that is done, I will post them.

              Special Systems

              Faith as a Weapon
              The mortal's higher self is a fearsome thing to behold when it is levied against the demon. A mortal who starts an Abjuration (page 231 of the God-Machine Chronicles), and beats the Demon's Presence + Resolve with their Resolve + Composure rolls inflicts the Abjured Condition upon the Demon.
              Abjured
              A faithful mortal has Abjured you with his higher self. You find yourself unable to aproach or harm the mortal. Any attempts to approach the mortal fail, and you suffer a -5 to all actions within sight of the mortal. Attempting to attack the mortal causes you to take your Torment points in Bashing damage, and you do not successfully attack.
              Resolution: Move away from the mortal, leaving the scene.
              Beat: Attempt to talk down, or attack the mortal.

              Holy Ground
              Mortal places of faith, imbued with the divine shard from above, is anathema to demons. The Heavenly Host has spent millenia turning the human mind, and self against all that the demons are, and stand for. While in a place of great faith, the psychic residue and disdain for the Fallen concentrates. While on this ground the Fallen suffers a penalty to all actions that ranges from -1 to -5, as the holy rage and pain fills his mind.

              Consecrated Objects
              Just as a mortal's wrathful faith and abjuration can harm the Fallen, and just as holy places can burn the Fallen, so too does the blessed weapons of mortals greviously injure the demon. When an object has been ritually prepared and imbued with the true belief of the mortals that it is holy, it does Bashing damage equal to the Fallen's permanent Torment rating. For example, a blessed sword , when used to strike a Fallen with 4 Torment, would do 4 extra Bashing damage on top of the damage it would normally do. The damage comes in the form of cracking, and burning flesh.

                          For today, we have Torment and the first Dominion I've done. My wife and I translated 5 or so Dominions form Demon: the Redemption fully, and translated the capabilities of others... we found them... kind of boring. (sorry Uxas! >.<) Okay, not all of them, but some of them we felt could use more flavor. So, we hashed out a system that was inspired by, but not, the original Dominion system.

                          For those of you who don't know, the Dominion system of DtR was basically, you bought a power package. Only once, they were expensive (20 XP in the old system per) but they gave a lot of power. The powers were delineated by how much Faith you spent on the Evocation. To put another way, the 1 Faith cost power, and the 5 faith cost power were different abilities. (sometimes... other times it made the one power's numbers bigger)

                          I'm going to take inspiration from that.

                          Each Domain has 4 powers a Minor Evocation, a Lesser Evocation, a Median Evocation, and a Greater Evocation. They cost 0, 1, 2, 4 Aether in that order to use them. They cost 6 XP per Dominion and you start with two.

                          But before we get to the Dominion of the Celestial, I want to present the rules on Torment.

                          Torment
                          The Fallen have been locked up by the decree of the Elohim. They transgressed Heaven's law, and were punished accordingly. They committed sins too great to be unwritten from existence, or to be recycled into angels, untainted by the feelings they had, and the rebellious nature within. No. The Fallen were made an example of, locked in a place so far away from the Light of Creation, trapped in a place where reality is ragged, and unwhole, where they truly learned to hunger for the Aether they had supped from mortals in the previous War. It was a pain greater than fire, or needles, or even the pain of oblivion. It was a constant feeling of hunger, of distance from what made an angel what it was. It was a place of corruption, and sin... and Torment.
                          Torment is a malefic force that resides locked in the psyche of all the Fallen. It's the collective corruption of the sins forgotten, and of an eternity spent in exile out of reality. But the prison was not perfect, when the Fallen flee the Depths, they are torn ragged, and weakened (humbled some might say) before they merge with the human host. The memories, the feelings and the humanity of their hosts changes the fallen angels in a fundamental way. They know more than rage, hunger and hate. With memories locked inside their shattered soul, the new Fallen is free from the burden.... but only for a short while.
                          The corruption lurks beneath the skin, painful memories, and atrocities lie just beneath the surface of the Fallen's thoughts, many of them without context, but they all come with feelings of anguish, and wrath. With these feelings comes the dark energy known as Torment. It seeks only to feast, destroy and defile. It is the birthright of the Fallen, a sin they made for themselves.

                          Torment Rolls
                          Dice Pool: Resolve + Composure
                          Dice Pool Modifiers
                          Torment 9 – 10: -2
                          Torment 6 – 8: -1
                          Torment 3 – 5: +0
                          Torment 2 – 1: +2
                          Point of Aether above turn limit: -1
                          (Example, if you use a Greater Evocation when you can only spend 1 Aether per turn, you get a -3 to your Torment roll)


                          Intentionally Invoking Torment
                          Intentionally evoking Torment is a risky move. It's filling the Fallen's heart with hate, and pain, and anger. There is no point to do so unless the Fallen wants to hurt the target, and hurt them badly. Gain 1 temporary Torment when the Fallen intentionally Evokes the Tormented aspect of his Evocation. Apply the normal Torment rules for by-standards harmed by this Evocation normally.


                          Dominion of the Celestial
                          It is said from Aether all things spring, it is the divine essence, and that which sparks the soul of sentient beings, like humans. The core of the Fallen is made up of their Aether, it houses their memory, their forms and transmits them between all the realms of the universe. It was the first of the Elohim's task to distribute the Aether and start the process of the universe's formation. To this day, Dominion over the celestial essence of Aether is prized among the Fallen. The Dominion of the Celestial is about finding, manipulating and transmitting the essence of Heaven that is known as Aether.
                          System: Learning the Dominion of the Celestial gives you the Minor Evocation of Lamp of Faith, the Lesser Evocation of Celestial Visions, the Median Evocation of Fires of Heaven, and the Greater Evocation of the Hand of God.

                          Minor Evocation: Lamp of Faith
                          Using the Evocation the Demon can sense in the same range as their Aetheric Senses the reservoir of Aether a mortal has in it's soul. The Aether inside the mortal's soul appears like a luminous, white light that comes from within the mortal's core, a Fallen instinctively knows what the levels of light correspond too. Also, the Demon can see the crackling energy appear around other creatures who have an Aether pool as a radiant aura.
                          Dice Pool: Wits + Occult + Reminiscence
                          Action: Instant
                          Cost: 0 Aether

                          Roll Results
                          Dramatic Failure: Immediately roll Torment at a -2, gain the Spooked condition if you fail the Torment roll, as the appearance of sin and darkness manifests in a very grotesque fashion.
                          Failure: The Evocation fails.
                          Success: On a success, everyone in range's aura of Aether appears, and the player knows what Soul Strength of each mortal in range has, and how much Aether a each creature in the range has in their Aether pool.
                          Exceptional Success: Count your Reminiscence as two higher for purposes of range, and get the Inspired condition.
                          Torment: On a failed Torment roll, count your Reminiscence as 4 higher than it is for range, but you see the black wounds of the soul instead of their radiance. You can tell what the Integrity (or similar stat) of all you can see, manifesting as bleeding wounds or dark wisps of energy. The Torment of your fellow Fallen is especially evident, and manifests before your eyes giving them a monstrous, wounded appearance.

                          Lesser Evocation: Celestial Visions
                          The Fallen all can speak to one another by invoking the Names of their brethren. This is an aspect of Aether, an energy that comes from the Monad, the Unity, and binds life, matter and energy together. Using this principle, the Fallen can communicate despite any distance. This Evocation takes that same principle to the extreme; with it a Fallen can invoke any target within line of sight, or any target with a Celestial or True Name at any distance, and send them visions that are like a waking dream. Using this Evocation, the generals in the War in Heaven could give detailed, elaborate orders from any distance to any target. During the same war, it was also discovered that you can use it to Torment a foe from any range. When sending antagonistic visions penalize the roll by the target's Resolve + Reminiscence.

                          Dice Pool: Manipulation + Expression + Reminiscence
                          Action: Instant
                          Cost: 1 Aether
                          Roll Results
                          Dramatic Failure: Immediately roll Torment again at a -2. You are also sent corresponding visions that have the same effect as if you rolled an Exceptional Success on yourself.
                          Failure: You fail to convey any information through this power.
                          Success: For each Success you can convey one piece of information (a sentence, a small scene or description), with antagonistic visions, it levies a situational -2 penalty to actions of concentration.
                          Exceptional Success: With helpful messages, the target gains the Inspired Condition as a feeling of being watched and guided from above fills them. For antagonistic visions, the target gets
                          Torment: The visions sent invariably turn dark, and torturous tinged by the malefic influence of Torment. Targets take the Demon's Torment in Bashing damage as their nose bleeds, and their brain is assaulted by horrific visions that wrack their brain.

                          Median Evocation: Fires of Heaven
                          Aether is a fiery, and powerful substance that blazed forth into the material world. It is the basis for all energy and matter (so say the Fallen) but in it's natural state it is a divine flame, the same flame some say Prometheus stole for humankind. To the knowledgeable they can incite Aether's natural inclination to burn and stir it into a destructive force. The Fallen channels his Aether into his target. Pure white flame erupts from inside the target consuming them from the inside out. This fire is non-flammable, and will not catch anything it touches on fire.
