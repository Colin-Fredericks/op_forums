    Bardic Circle Bard's are social and political creatures. they usually play a heraldic role in the cities Consilium and tend to be behind the scenes manipulators of awakend politics. As such, the legacy responds most strongly with members of the Silver Ladder and Mastigos mages. The Attainmemts of this legacy all involve enhancing the characters social abilities. As bards are famed for their prowess at singing and oratory, thier attainments are always directly linked to their voices and require that at Target hear them speak or sing before taking effect. the primary Arcanum is Mind. 1: How Do You Do? this attainment basically replicates the effects of the spell first impression which is always active. 2. Voice of an Angel the second attainment allows the mage to add his dots in Mind to either his presence or manipulation stat for up to one hour. with optional life, the mage can also simulate the spell Image of Striking Beauty with successes equal to dots in LIFE but the beauty it creates applies to the mages voice rather than physical appearance adding a bonus equal to life to any Expression, Persuasion, or Socialize rolls where he uses his voice to influence someone. 3. Pied Piper this a attainment allows the mage to raise the other of Presence or Manipulation by his mind dots when using his voice in some way to influence others. With optional Life, the name age can also create an effect similar to this spell Trigger the Lizard Brain with a prolonged duration and an area of effect equal to those who can hear the mages on unaugmented and unamplified voice.

                Yeah, Mastigos themes are usually all about pushing through limits, purging weakness, mastering one's self, and suchlike. Mind makes perfect sense for these guys as a primary arcana, but not so much the Path of Scourging. You could work up a bardic legacy that pushes the limits of what the voice can do, perfecting the art and the attainments would follow that. You certainly don't need to, but if you wanted to shift the theme over, I'd go something along the lines of the following,

                1st: Support of Breath - reqs. Perform 3, Specialty in some vocal art, Life 2, Mind 1
                Character adds Life to Stamina for purposes of breath and voice. This helps with endurance and holding one's breath, but it's a result of refined breath support. This also grants 9-again to performances in a vocal specialty. (All Bards develop at least one specialty, but most develop all facets of their voice.)
                The character gains the ability to read an individual or a crowd's moods as a reflexive mental action once a round. (The information is per Aura Perception, but is often received through smell or hearing)
                Finally, the character gains the ability to purchase Eidetic Memory with experience points.
                2nd: reqs. Perform 4, Life 3, Eidetic Memory
                The character can perfectly pronounce anything they hear within the realm of natural capacity. (That is, they must obey the physical limits of their natural voice.) All words and notes in anything heard are perfectly stored, and retrieval takes a reflexive thought. If something is difficult to remember, the number of mental actions to retrieve it are equal to the normal level of penalty assessed, reduced by one per level of Mind. (If the mage has Mind 2 and the thing recalled is so complex it would have a penalty of -4, it would take 4-2=2 actions to grab. Taking the reflexive action and the characters instant action for the round suffices.) These rules only apply when they improve Eidetic Memory.
                This grants their voice a magical quality, which grants 8- and 9-again to any vocal efforts that apply to a vocal specialty or would be aided by Striking Looks.
                Optional Mind 3:
                The character may speak and understand any language. This includes flawless idomatic understanding and usage, as well as perfect tone and voice.
                3rd: Silvered Tongue reqs. Perform 5, Life 4, Mind 2
                The character can make any noise that a human can hear, using just their voice. For a willpower, the bard may add Life to any vocal rolls.
                Further, the character may use his superlative voice to manipulate a crowd. The effects are essentially the same as Emotional Urging.except the activation roll is Presence + Performance or Persuasion (As most appropriate) and the Potence is equal to successes. Ranks in Mind set the levels of Area on the Advanced Area of Effect chart for a ritual casting. Anyone within this area is affected by the spell. Target factors can be added to include anyone within earshot or exclude anyone in the area, but the bard must specifically interact with any of these. This means the target factors come out of the mundane roll. Attempts to conceal this apply further penalties.
                Optional Mind 4
                The character can force an audience to see the images conveyed. When using their superlative vocal specialty, a bard can impress information into the minds of the audience. The effects end up as per the spell Hallucination (Duration and Potency are set by the character's Mind, but detail and quality are defined by the mundane roll) or a perfect mnemonic can be implanted. (This is almost like Psychic Reprogramming, except it can only set a piece of the performance to be unforgettable.) The performance puts the image in the mind, while the attainment locks in the pieces desired. Only one mental scene or concept can be placed in the mind per performance, but this can draw on any or all of the performance to create the image. This is simply an image or idea that keeps popping up. A song becomes an ear-worm, or the phrase echos in their mind. It doesn't force them to accept the idea or like the image.


                For the version you got there, some thoughts:
                First Impressions is actually a really nice fit. Might toss in something further about his natural blessed charm and apply a shielding effect or even a fate sight like Sybill's or Interconnections which allows a bard to find the things he needs to witness and record.
                Voice of an Angel --Want a mystical voice, here? How about just adding Mind to all vocal rolls or something. A willpower to gain rote quality on any vocal action. something like that, perhaps? Works especially well with a Mind/Fate combo.
                Pied Piper--Don't get me wrong, stat boosts can be sweet, but I just think that this should get something more specifically bardic... I like the optional Life side more than the main part, here, accordingly. I point out that Mind can certainly do that same effect.
                An Optional Fate component could allow for a Gaes/prophecy/oath, by the by. (The ability to fling out a curse in death would seem appropriate, potentially.)
                Curses entirely devoted to a person's story would be especially fitting. "You will be forgotten by any who would revere you. Your name will become a joke." or "No one will ever forget your sacrifice. Your story will become common in lorehouses the world over." would be the sorts of blessings or curses I'd allow here, with a requirement to craft an appropriate story or something to charm the Fates with and seal the deal.

                Anyhoo, some thoughts to consider.

                Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.

                            One idea I'm fiddling about with is based on the idea of a karmic debt turning someone into a dark immortal. In particular, I figure the character should be, in essence, hosting the formation of a really destructive spirit, but one that emerges from the character's soul. In other words, metaphysically, this is a specific form of Spirit Claiming. Mechanically, it's a legacy.
                            The three levels currently kinda look like:

                            Akuma
                            1st: Dark Aura - The aura of death makes the Akuma fearsome and frightening. Social penalties and so forth are offset by huge benefits to intimidation and combat.
                            2nd: Shadow Self - The Demon Self grows enough to take over and enhance the Akuma's combat abilities. Downside is that they become dangerously volatile. (Maybe move this up to the third) This isn't a stupid and beast-like rage, though. The demon doesn't lag the normal mind as clever. In fact, it enhances that. It just removes the safeties and uses whatever martial and magical skills are most effective. Not sure how this ought to interact with Wisdom.
                            3rd: Dark Awakening - The Akuma has merged with their Demon Soul, to become something more dangerous. May allow access to Harrowing or Blast numina and an Essence pool.

                            Mechanics are the hard part, since I want the progression to be balanced and reasonable throughout.
                            I'm thinking that, given the death and human suffering essence that causes this condition, this should be a dangerous form of geist that forms. That geist could be treated as an immaterial familiar in most ways, and the attainments simply grant it further powers, perhaps. I dunno. Once the third attainment is known, the mage isn't immortal, but they will merge further with their Demon Self. That could be what converts the spirit into a geist... and also explain the whole "becoming immortal" idea. (Especially if this thing can restore the body in some way.)
                            Thoughts are welcome.

                            Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.

                                                It occurred to me that the karmic burden might pervert the Akuma's Daimon into the spiritual force driving improvement of his killing arts. That grants it all sorts of drawbacks automatically, and it's already part of the mage's pattern. Something akin to:

                                                Some mages perfect their killing arts. The burden of all the killing can have a grave effect even if the mage's Wisdom doesn't shatter under the strain. Each attainment requires a dramatic amount of killing on the Akuma's part. Rarely, only one or two deaths are needed, but that's only when the fights themselves are exceptionally flawless or the slain are especially skilled. All the essence and killing stain the character's soul... and eventually spawn something dark.

                                                1st: Demon Aura -- Spirit 2, Death 1
                                                The Akuma's Daimon has become tainted by the killing. Perfecting the ability to kill becomes an important part of the self improvement it promotes. It now, also, actually considers survival of the Akuma an active priority. A consequence is that the Akuma's aura becomes tainted with blood red blotches and black flames. This interferes with the ability to examine the character's aura, as if a 2 Potency shielding effect. This signifier is perceived before anything else, if order matters. The Akuma's Nimbus is also altered by this taint in a manner appropriate to the mage's former Nimbus.
                                                The effect grants Unseen Shield at Spirit potency.
                                                Optional Mind 2
                                                The aura makes people uneasy. As an Instant mental action, the mage can impose a fear of himself into any target in sensory range. Potency of the effect is equal to Mind. This can be reflexively used as part of any mundane Intimidation attempt.

                                                2nd: Demon Shadow -- Spirit 3 Mind 3
                                                The Akuma's Daimon now considers the Akuma's killing art to be the most important aspect of his being to improve. This attainment grants it the ability to extend part of itself into Twilight while remaining inside the Akuma's Oneiros. This is known as the Shadow. The form it takes is part of the Akuma's Aura. Manifesting this is reflexive with a Indefinite duration, and does not count as an action taken by Akuma. This also means it is not an action the Akuma retains any control over.

                                                Create the Daimon's Shadow as a normal disembodied familiar, including the familiar bond. The actual Daimon is an astral being of much greaeter power than this projection, and can act utterly independently of it. This Shadow has access to the Akuma's skills and most merits, whenever appropriate. When using the Akuma's body, the Shadow uses the Akuma's attributes. Unlike normal possession, the Shadow does have access to the Akuma's complete powers unless the Akuma is actively resisting it.

                                                If the Akuma's mind is outside of his body or inside the Inner Realms, the Shadow can control the body uncontested. For a willpower, the Akuma can grant the Shadow control of his body for a scene. The Akuma can give it goals and restrictions, which it will follow unless they directly conflict with the Daimon's goal of improving the Akuma's art. One of these restrictions can be to relinquish control when something is accomplished. "Kill these three attackers and relinquish control" is perfectly valid, for example. (Add rules linking Composure to control?)

                                                The Shadow, Daimon, and Akuma are linked tightly. Any skill training done by one aspect is affects all. They share an experience point pool. It's not uncommon for an Akuma to use this to train while doing other things, like meditating.

                                                Optional Death 3
                                                The Daimon can use Sculpt Ephemera at will.

                                                3rd: Demon Soul -- Spirit 4
                                                The Daimon now considers the improvement of the Akuma's killing arts to be improving the Akuma. (As part of that, the Daimon remains focused on self-preservation.)

                                                The Rank of the Shadow is now set as the lesser of the Akuma's Death and Spirit. Influences and Numina are normal for the rank, but then add Numina: Blast, Harrowing, and Materialize. Its Influence remains the primary killing art of the Akuma.

                                                (strike: At this level of attainment, the Daimon can begin the process of claiming the Akuma as a more normal spirit might claim a human.)

                                                Optional Mind 4
                                                (strike: The Daimon can act inside the Inner Realms as well as the physical simultaneously. If so desired, the Daimon can externalize the inner eye as a mental projection. Both of these can process mental actions independently. Also, the Daimon has more than just a Familiar bond, now, and can fully communicate with the Akuma.)

                                                Maybe something along these lines.
