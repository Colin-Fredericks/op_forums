                            Is the errata for this finished? Because the following entry in the expanded inspirational material is simply wrong.

                            "Ghost in the Shell (1995, dir. Mamoru Oshii). A classic anime film exploration of where machine ends and soul begins. The series that serves as its sequel, Stand Alone Complex
                            (2002-2005, directed by Kenji Kamiyama), expands on the problems of artificial intelligence that gains sapience."

                            In point of fact, Oshii's '95 film (and it's '04 sequel Innocence) is one adaptation of the original Ghost in the Shell comic (manga) by Masamune Shirow. The Stand Alone Complex series is an alternate take on the same source material (and has its own sequel in movie form, the 2006 Solid State Society). Ghost in the Shell: Arise is yet a third adaptation of the same source material (and, most ironically spawned its own spin-off comic (manga)).

                            Further while each of the versions explore the area of artificial intelligence gaining sapience the real focus is on exploring where the machine (your body) ends and the soul (or self) begins. The question posed is 'how do I know what I'm experiencing is real if all I am is a disembodied brain stuffed in a mechanical body suit?'

                            Bonus trivia: This same topic of self-identity versus our corporeal existence also appears in the short series Bubblegum Crisis and A.D. Police Files.

                                        Originally posted by Kirby Jerusalem View Post

                                        So, how does it fit in when there are several Demon powers that do point 2? Is it just a matter that with the Dynamic Legend option, every demon has an option for it?

                                        Edit: Also, I might as well drop my questions about powers here.

                                        1)Angelic Reflexes has no duration listed.

                                        2) "Roll Power + Finesse – Subterfuge to determine if the humans within the angel’s range notice what the angel is seeking." - so can angels see through the eyes of every person affected by Translocation, or what? How do they get that information? Also, what exactly happens when someone uses Translocation and a Demon spoofs it? They register as human but still can't be moved (or seen through, as above)? How do angels register that one?

                                        3)What's the essence cost for Incepts that have a primary ability without a listed cost, like Economy, Efficiency, Units, etc?

                                        4)Is there a cap on how many essence you can spend for Scale's primary effect? Are you only limited to Numina when using it, or can you apply it to whatever dice pools and modifiers you wish?

                                        5)I've been trying to be objective here while I'm asking questions, but I have to ask: you can seriously use Space to obviate someone's Defense? :/

                                        6) How does Scale work with powers that do multiple things, like Angelic Reflexes?
                                        Hi Kirby, sorry I didn't see you questions edited into your post until now.

                                        1) It lasts the scene. Since a combat is likely to be a scene, you could say it lasts for the duration of the conflict.

                                        2) When activating Translocation, the angel taps into the general awareness patterns of every human in its range. Through this, it can identify the 'thought spike' that indicates a human has seen what it searches for. This isn't looking through the eyes of every human, or even reading their surface thoughts. Think about a time when you were somewhere public and unexpectedly saw someone you knew. That brief spike of recognition and the thrill of seeing them (or concern of having to avoid them) is what the angel looks for, but it has 'planted' the spike trigger condition across everyone. The angel will notice supernatural 'gaps' in its awareness and can sense when they interact with its human network, but doesn't know what they are unless it investigates further. Demons who successfully spoof against Translocation won't even be noticed by the angel. Their readout will seem human, and they won't have any 'spike' for the angel to latch onto.

                                        When the angel chooses to swap places with someone, it either homes in on the 'spike' (which means demons won't ever be targeted if the angel is looking for some trigger), or if the angel is simply wanting to move quickly though the area, it will always swap with some unsuspecting human in the area, and won't even consider how the Numen chose which random human it swapped with - it simply considers the spoofing demon a human that the power didn't choose. If a demon is the only being in an area where the angel wants to be, the Numen won't even spot her to require spoofing, and she's immune to the swapping power. If a demon was in an area with humans and successfully spoofed, and then every human in the area left, the Numen would register that every human had left and wouldn't notice the demon still there. Demons are a particular blind spot to Translocation. Additionally, Translocation relies on the presence of normal humans to notice anyone in an area at all. If everyone in a room had a supernatural tolerance rating, the angel would just register it as an empty room.

                                        Edit: Though 'demons' are a blind spot, a specific demon that the Angel knows the cover for, or one where the angel has something specific to latch onto is fair game. Here, the angel is relying on humans to notice the specifics, rather than generic 'demon'. So, an angel that knows lawyer 'Steve Johnson' is a Cover for Angel Damiel could make humans on range sensitive to the cover. Similarly, if the angel knows a demon in range has just taken possession of a specific object, it could home in on the object. It may get many false hits if the object is generic. 'I'm looking for the person carrying a red umbrella' will generate more false leads than 'I'm looking for the person carrying the red umbrella with white rose pattern and a silver handle'.

                                        3) If they don't list a cost, they cost 0 Essence to use, just the Willpower to activate.

                                        4) Incepts technically work according to the usual ephemeral being spending limits, ie, no more than what they currently have. If this is too much for a group, the ST may limit it to Rank+1 or Rank+2. Scale specifically calls out dice pools and Social maneuvering, it's applicable to more than Numina.

                                        5) Yes, if the ST decides that the way the angel manifests the effect through Space prevents the target from defending.

                                        6) If a power does multiple things for a single cost, Scale enhances all of them. If a power has multiple effects that are activated separately, Scale must be applied separately to each. For Angelic Reflexes, Scale enhances Initiative boost and Defense boost for a single cost.

                                        The possibility of an angel using Incepts should be terrifying, and should open up potentially massive displays of enormous power. Storytellers should be mindful that angels don't use Incepts lightly - these are the powers that destroy entire cities (or countries), or move comets from their orbits, but they're flashy and noticeable. Most of the time, the God-Machine hasn't authorised their use, and an angel simply wouldn't consider using them. They're the antithesis of the subtlety the GM usually prefers. An angel exercising its free will to use Incepts could potentially disrupt other subtle GM projects in an area, and is probably dangerously close to falling - in fact, demons who fell because they used awesome, noisy power for what they considered 'the right reasons' may make up a disproportionate percentage of Integrators who want to be allowed back in, never intended to rebel, and don't really understand what they did wrong.

                                        Incepts are a basic integrated design component of angels, incorporated back in ancient times when the GM needed to throw around awesome amounts of cosmic energy to create massive feats of planetary realignment and geoengineering. They're a design legacy that exists in the heart of every angel as a temptation to go beyond mission, and possibly fall. The GM would probably remove them from angels if it was capable of thinking about it and redesigned their core components. The fact that it hasn't either means that it can't, won't, or doesn't care about the loss of a few angels from pre-installed temptation subroutines. This could be an interesting philosophical point for angels and demons to consider for some chronicles.

                                        Incepts may be an 'I win' button for an angel, but their use usually means the God-Machine has already lost.
