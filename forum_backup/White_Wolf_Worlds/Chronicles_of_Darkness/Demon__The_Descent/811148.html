                  I was thinking of something similar to Cover, but singular. Actually, Torment might be a good choice to copy. But instead of high torment equaling evil, it means how much the demon is now able to sympathize with, their ability to feel compassion and/or regret.

                  How's this:

                  1. Replace Cover with Torment. This represents how the infernal spirit has merged with the victim's soul, and thus has the capacity for sympathy and virtue.
                  2. If the demon dies, he loses a permanent dot of Torment out of 10 (which becomes 9 and so on), and must then find a new host. This represents the demon having to maintain their sanity after consuming yet another soul, each having conflicting personalities and memories. This means a given demon has a maximum of 10 "lives" before the Void claims him utterly.

                  Of course, we could change the name to avoid confusion with cWoD's mechanic, if too out of place.

                                              How's this for a possible mechanic?
                                              Souls

                                              A soul can be used for several different effects. One, it can be allowed to form a shade within Abaddon, granted a total number of Iliaster per day equal to Willpower, provided the harvester succeeds in tormenting it.
                                              Another option is to create a number of cacodemons (cacklings for short)--minions barely more intelligent than a five year old human--equal to dots in willpower its owner had in life. The last option is, of course, to be used for creating a Cover, as per normal. These latter options require the demon to be the rightful owner of said-soul, however, meaning either a contract with the lemure or its current master must be made first. Otherwise all attempts will immediately fail.

                                              Creating a Cacodemon
                                              As stated, the number of cacodemons that a single soul can produce is equal to the number of Willpower Dots the soul had in life. Often, a cackling reflects one of the soul's primary vices, eg; a wrathful soul will create cacklings with a penchant for violence. If the soul was known for expressing said-wrath via arson, one or more of the created cacklings might showcase signs on its person, such as being wreathed in flame, or with skin the pigment of charcoal.
                                              Beyond aesthetics, the nature of the soul should define what powers the hellspawn acquire, with all new cacodemons having at least 1 numen, embed/link, or rank in Influence. Alternatively, shards of the used soul can be fed into a single cacodemon, adding additional stats to the creature as listed below.

                                              Overview:
                                              • All cacodemons start at Size 1, using the stats listed under "Tiny". They also acquire 1 numen determined by the ST, a link/embed from the demon creating it, or a single rank in one Influence as if it were a spirit.
                                              • If WP Dots are fed to the cacodemon, the caster may divvy them between increasing size category (resetting the cacklings attributes to match the new form)--or add an additional numen or rank in Influence (max of Rank 5) per dot. The caster can also add embeds/link or exploits/chains for equal price, but they must be ones that the demon already knows. Finally, a cackling can gain skill points from spending 1 WP (equaling 4 points worth, each), or through appropriate training.
                                              • All cacodemons have Soul Sense as a bonus power.
                                              • Cacodemons can understand any and all mundane languages, even if incapable of speech. Their Intelligence is equal to Rank, but permanently capped at 3 unless they achieve apotheosis.

                                              Tiny (Mouse, Frog, Sparrow, Lizard)
                                              Physical Attributes: Strength 1, Dexterity 1-3, Stamina 1
                                              Speed Factor: 1-2
                                              Size: 1
                                              Health: 2
                                              Attacks: Inflicts no real damage save maybe venom if appropriate.

                                              Small (Cat, Crow, Snake, Iguana)
                                              Physical Attributes: Strength 1-2, Dexterity 2-3, Stamina 1-2
                                              Speed Factor: 2-7
                                              Size: 2-3
                                              Health: 3-5
                                              Attacks: Inflicts Strength as lethal damage.

                                              Medium (Wolf, Cobra, Vulture, Monkey, Eagle)
                                              Physical Attributes: Strength 2-4, Dexterity 2-5, Stamina 2-4
                                              Speed Factor: 5-9
                                              Size: 4
                                              Health: 6-8
                                              Attacks: Inflicts Strength to Strength +1 (L), depending on species.

                                              Man-Sized (Cougar, Ape, Condor, Deer)
                                              Physical Attributes: Strength 3-6, Dexterity 3-6, Stamina 3-6
                                              Speed Factor: 5-10
                                              Size: 5-6
                                              Health: 8-12
                                              Attacks: Inflicts Strength +1 to Strength +2 (L), depending on species.

                                              Large (Horse, Bear, Dolphin, Komodo Dragon)
                                              Physical Attributes: Strength 4-6, Dexterity 3-5, Stamina 4-7
                                              Speed Factor: 5-12
                                              Size: 6-8
                                              Health: 10-15
                                              Attacks: Inflicts Strength +1 to Strength +3 (L); may kick or trample for Strength +3 (B).

                                              Heavy (Cattle, Moose, Bull Seal)
                                              Physical Attributes: Strength 4-8, Dexterity 2-4, Stamina 5-8
                                              Speed Factor: 5-10
                                              Size: 9-13
                                              Health: 14-21
                                              Attacks: Inflicts Strength +1 to Strength +3 (L); may kick or trample for Strength +3 (B).

                                              Huge (Elephant, Hippopotamus, Sea Lion, Orca)
                                              Physical Attributes: Strength 7-10, Dexterity 2-4, Stamina 7-10
                                              Speed Factor: 4-6
                                              Size: 11-15
                                              Health: 18-25
                                              Attacks: Inflicts Strength +1 to Strength +2 (L); may kick or trample for Strength +2 (B).

                                                      Cacklings are basically cryptics here, meaning they run the gammut of minions to pets to enemies and more.
