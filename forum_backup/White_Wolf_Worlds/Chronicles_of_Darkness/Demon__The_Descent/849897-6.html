                Three more for you guys!
                __

                Essence Tracer ( Angelic Technology)
                Hunting demons is not as difficult as one might think. The Unchained are a paranoid and suspicious lot by nature, especially concerning anything that may have connections to the God-Machine, and a hunter-angel with the right tools can exploit that curiosity with ease. This Technology allows the angel to discreetly inject a mortal with a species of Essence that degrades over time and releases pulses of Aether – turning said mortal into an occult red herring and fishing lure all at once.

                Appearance: One of the angel's hands become a medical injection tool with a repository that holds a small amount of liquefied Essence, which glows an ethereal blue-green.

                Mechanics: The angel is capable of summoning or dismissing the injector at will as an instant action. To utilize the injector, the angel makes a touch attack by rolling Power+Finesse – the subject's defense. On success the injector penetrates the skin but causes no damage, instead infusing a number of the angel's Essence points that the user elects to transfer to the mortal- up to the number the user can normally spend in a single turn. These Essence points are subtly altered by the injector upon infusion; normally Essence that is trapped within a human's body begins to fester, causing the human to sicken as if poisoned, but these points instead will dissipate at a rate of one point per hour until all injected points are gone. When these essence points do dissipate they release a small burst of Aether, which demons can detect and harvest as normal. As an added bonus, the injected Essence doubles as an etheric tracking device, allowing the angel in question to be aware of the mortal's exact location as long as she is within a radius of half the angel's Rank in miles ( minimum of one)- providing there is still Essence in her system. Seemingly normal humans that randomly release Aether are sure to attract the attentions of the Unchained that may be hiding in the area, and as a result the hunter-angel gains a +2 to rolls related to searching out and discovering hiding demons.

                It should be noted that such exposure to Aether over the protracted period of time may lead to the mortal in question to become Stigmatic, though the chances of this occurring vary with the amount of Essence infused into their system. At one or two points, the chances of becoming Stigmatic are very low, at three there is a good change the Aetheric pulses will impart a Stigmata on the unwitting dupe. At four to five, the subject will become Stigmatic. However, the storyteller does possess full discretion when deciding whether or not this transformation occurs or not, should they or should they not wish for this Technology to unintentionally create Stigmatic liabilities for the angel in questions' Mission.
                Note: This ability can be retained by demons, though it injects Aether instead of Essence, making it a great tool for providing distractions to occupy angelic pursuers and their agents.

                Phase-shift Quickswitch ( Angelic Modification)
                Being ephemeral beings angels can quickly, if not easily, phase between material and immaterial states of existence. With this Modification the angel can benefit from her immaterial nature for a brief instant while remaining a physical entity.

                Appearance: The angel's body seems to become insubstantial for brief jolts of time, allowing tiny particles of dust and floatsam to pass through it's form, though the transition is too quick to allow larger, more solid objects to pass through.

                Mechanics: This Modification allows the angel to benefit from it's Twilight Form Manifestation for brief instant without having to resort fully becoming invisible and immaterial. By spending a point of Essence to activate it, the angel partially phases into it's twilight state, then returns to material form an instant later. By doing so, the angel is able to reflexively avoid any single attack that affects purely physical objects and retain it's instant action during the same turn( example; using Phase-shift Quickswitch to avoid the melee attack of an opponent and then responding with a melee attack of one's own). This Modification can only be used once per turn against a single source of harm- it does not function against multiple attackers.

                Note: Being fully material entities, demons have a slightly altered version, allowing them to instead shift from physical form to Twilight Form as an instant action – though shifting back to material form costs a point of willpower on top of the Aether cost and inflicts a single point of bashing damage.

                Burning Sword ( Angelic Modification)
                Destroyers are most often deployed to hunt down and annihilate rogue operatives and other occult undesirables that plague the God-Machine. To ensure operation success, the Machine routinely outfits it's warriors with the highest grade weaponry for the task. Angels armed with this form ability are fearsome demon killers, for their swords inflict grievous wounds upon the flesh of doomed Unchained.

                Appearance: One of the angel's hands is a great and terrible sword of fiery Essence, it's shape and intensity varying widely from angel to angel, the only thing they have in common is that for demons they bring almost certain death.

                Mechanics: Burning Sword shares weaponry traits with the demonic Blade Hand Modification, with two exceptions. The first being that the angel has the option of adding it's Rank to the weapon's damage modifier with an expenditure of two Essence, stoking the sword's flames to a greater intensity. The second being that when the angel makes a successful attack against a demonic opponent or a Cryptid with Aether in it's system, the flaming blade reacts to those Aether stores violently, inflicting aggravated damage.

                Note: should the angel possessing this Modification Fall, the weapon is warped by the resulting energies and loses these characteristics, becoming the Blade Hand Modification.

                Increased Essence Storage ( Angelic Process)
                Essence is the life blood the God-Machine, and by extension angels- without it the former would be diminished and the latter would fall into hibernation forever. This does not deter the Machine from sending Its servants on long missions where the possibility to regain this vital resource is scarce. To compensate for this potential liability, the Machine builds these servants with additional storage systems in order to remain well fueled on their assignments.

                Appearance: The angel has some sort of external container of some kind on it's body. I could resemble anything from a back mounted propane tank to an array of solar panels to a layer of fat on a fleshy angel's body.

                Mechanics: Increased Essence Storage allows the angel to store an additional five points of Essence per dot of Rank, to a maximum of five.( ie, Exampiel 1.0 is a Rank 1 angel, so he holds an additional 5 Essence, while Exampiel 3.0 is Rank 3, so she is able of storing 15 extra points, and Exampiel 5.0 as a Rank 5 angel can store a frightening extra 25 Essence) These stores are housed separately from the angel's primary Essence pool, unusable until it transfers essence from extra storage system into her main “tank” as it were. Increased Essence Storage can be refueled as easily as the angel itself, though Essence is only added to the Process once the angel's main stores are full.
