    Interlocks

    12-26-2013, 09:19 PM
    before Christmas we had our first demon game, my players love it so I'm planing on making it more long term. Which I didn't want to do because I only have the preview, but as long as it is all going well I'm in.

    I just finished making one of the players Cipher and interlocks and was wondering what everyone's general thought were.
    I will not be posting the final secret for obvious reasons.

    Cipher:
    First Key: living recorder Pg. 211
    Second Key: Synthesis, Pg. 199
    Third Key: Shatter, Pg. 187
    Fourth Key: Identity theft, Pg. 207

    Interlocks:

    Past records: allows a demon to experience past events with in an area. The demon must be present with in the area and the records are experienced in real time. Leaving the demon unaware of what is currently happening around her. The demon must specify an exact time. The farther away the events are the harder they are to retrieve, each day levying a cumulative -1.
    Dice pool: Wits + investigation, Cost: 1 Aether, Action: instant.
    Dramatic failure: an incorrect or alternate history is experienced by the demon.
    Failure: nothing happens.
    Success: Works as expected (all or nothing effect).
    Exceptional Success: The demon spends half the time experiencing the events. Six hours in the Past records equals three in the current time.

    Breaking point: with this interlock a demon can retroactively shatter an object. This power alters the time line as gently as it can. The demon needs to touch the object and it cannot be anything larger than her size.
    Dice pool: Wits + Crafts, Cost: 1 Aether, Action: instant.
    Dramatic failure: From the intended breaking point onward the object gathers occult energy. Exploding once the demon uses this interlock on it. Dealing 2L damage for every day spent gathering occult energy.
    Failure: nothing happens, and the object remains unchanged.
    Success: Each success allows the Demon to reach farther back in time. A stationary object measures successes in days and a moving object measures successes in hours.
    Exceptional Success: allows the demon minor manipulations in the objects ret-coned history.

    Identity loss: The Demon can dislocate a person from her identity. The victim completely forgets who they are. All the under pinning concepts of who they were, are lost on them; they don’t recognize their own face let alone their name. Gender, relations, address, nationality are all lost and in addition to all that the victim finds it difficult to except or understand the truth, and always prefers to rationalize that they are nobody.
    Dice pool: Manipulation + Subterfuge - resolve, Cost: 1 Aether, Action: instant.
    Dramatic failure: the victim gains a subconscious hold on their identity. +3 to resist any compromise of their identity; including but not limited to mind control, manipulation, interrogation, and hallucinations.
    Failure: nothing happens
    Success: Each success adds to the penalty for the victim to reintegrate back into their life.
    Exceptional Success: Successes are their own reward.

    Memory theft: The Demon can pull and upload memories from people, choosing to either leave the memory or erase it from the victim. The demon must either know roughly the memory she wants to take or the time frame of the memory in question. The demon must touch the person
    Dice pool: Manipulation + Subterfuge - Resolve, Cost: 1 Aether, Action: instant.
    Dramatic failure: The victim’s memory becomes highlighted in their mind turning the details in to an obsession.
    Failure: nothing happens.
    Success: Each success allows the demon to take about an hour’s worth of memory.
    Exceptional Success: Allows the demons fine control over the details she leaves behind.

                  I've been thinking about how I want uses of breaking point to play out and I've realized that I miss-spoke when I said the object shatters. how it breaks should be relevant and convenient to the situation. also like any other Demon power over use should result in a compromise roll, and over use could come quite quickly depending on what my player has in mind.

                  here is a quick example of how i would like it to go: Okay our three Demon PC just leave a building while a sniper awaits on a rooftop down the street. one of our Demons gets shot and goes down potentially dead. Our remaining two take cover and make their way towards the shooter who gets off several more shots; maybe hitting a second character (details become relevant shortly). but in either chase our Demon who knows Breaking Point gets to the buildings stairwell just as our shooter is trying to make his escape. they fight until our Demon touches the sniper rifle and uses Breaking Point.
                  Now lets say an hour ago the sniper rifle breaks. The path of least resistance means that the shooter dosen't notice, he gets his orders and climbs into position see our three demons leave the building down the street lines up his first killing shot and fires, the gun explodes in his hands. A little detail but clearly an important one.
                  At this time our demons must have heard the miss fire because they are now making their way towards our gun man, (the reality of it is that by design I would like not to replay much and thus details will be taken for granted.)
                  Now while the first time there were two of our demons were running towards the shooter now there are three. if the shooter has a second gun, most likely a pistol it isn't like he would have made those pot shots as our demons encroached, and the second demon wouldn't have been hit either. (although if i can digress a little if it were PC on PC violence or i really wanted that second character shot i would only alter the damage done by the difference in the weapons damage.)
                  and now we are back where we were with reality altered in our favour.

                  Now I can only hope it goes as smoothly in game, but that is the design.

                  I would say that a lot of Identity Loss victim become mental patients, and hopefully after a lot of therapy and meds (mechanically positive modifiers) they could recover.

                  As for the bit about pacts I was thinking that once lost the pieces shouldn't be accessible, as the person can not sign away what they don't have.
