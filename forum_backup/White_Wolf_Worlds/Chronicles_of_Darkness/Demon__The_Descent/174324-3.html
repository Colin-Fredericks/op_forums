                                            Beware of the wall of text below. I hope it's as interesting to read as it was to research it!

                                            Originally posted by Heirs to Hell page 17 "Demon-Blooded Genetics[B
                                            "[/B]]... Demon-Blooded have the recessive Fractal (f) and/or Offspring (o) traits, while mortals with no Demon blood in them have the dominant Human (h) trait.

The Offspring are Demon-Blooded with the (o,o) or (f,o) combination (the (o) trait only becomes dominant when paired with the Fractal trait). Latents either have the (h,f) or (h,o) combination, and all humans have (h,h)

                                            Fractals and Offspring born to Demons are always (f,f) or (o,o). After that the Demon-Blooded only pass on only one of those (f)'s or (o)'s so the Fractal passes on the (f) and the Offspring passes on the (o). Because all Mortals who don't have demon blooded pass on the dominant trait (h), the children of Fractals and Offspring are always Latents ((h,f) or (h,o)) unless the other parent is a Demon-Blooded (either a Fractal, Offspring, or Latent).

                                                    Heh. I actually wrote up a similar table when I was writing that sidebar, and much of yours matches mine.

                                                    The design goal was to come up with a way for Offspring (and to a lesser extent, Fractals) to exist who didn't have demon parents. Demon-blooded children of demon-blooded were one side of that. The other side was to address the tropes of "I didn't find out I was demon-blooded until a weird cult kidnapped me and tried to sacrifice me to their god" and "Little Johnny is different from the other kids at school, and we have no idea why." These are conventions of the "demon child" sub-genre, and I didn't want to ignore any of them, so I tried to come up with something reasonably logical to explain why all these different situations were possible. This was my solution.

                                                    As the sidebar says, demon blood isn't a literal gene.That is simply a useful shorthand to explain why most Offspring give birth to Latents, but occasionally two Latents will produce an Offspring (or, rarely, a Fractal) child they are not at all equipped to care for. For Fractals, Offspring, and Latents, demon blood pretty much behaves the same way as recessive genes. As soon as a demon becomes involved, though, Gregory Mendel is going to throw up his hands.

                                                    It's really quite simple:

                                                    - If a demon pairs with a demon or a Fractal the result is always a Fractal (ff). The involvement of the demon "maximizes" the demon blood and leaves the children as close to demonic as the demon-blooded can get.
                                                    - If a demon pairs with an Offspring or a Latent (basically, any non-Fractal with demon blood), the result a Fractal (ff) half the time and an Offspring (oo) the other half of the time. The demon's involvement "maximizes" the demon blood half the time (ff) and leaves it on not-quite-the-maximum-setting the other half of the time (oo).
                                                    - If a demon pairs with a human with no demon blood, the result is always an Offspring (oo), since there is no demon blood to intensify.
                                                    - A pure demon is never the result of any children born of demons or demon-blooded (unless you really want it to be that way in your chronicle, of course).

                                                    Once that demon blood is out "in the wild," the genetics metaphor works fine most of the time. Demon-blooded parents don't produce Fractals or Offspring unless the other parent is also demon-blooded (including Latents, who might not realize they are demon-blooded), although they do make Latents (100% of the time for Fractals/Offspring and 50% of the time for a Latent parent).

                                                    Latents also have kind of a weird catch. If something "activates" their demon blood, their "demon-blooded genetics" change, adding an o (making them Offspring). Depending on whether their demon blood is f or o, they may end up pure-blooded Offspring (oo) or hybrid Offspring (fo). In neither case do they wind up as Fractals (again, unless your chronicle benefits from such an event). So yes, the child they have before that encounter with the God-Machine only has a 50% chance of being a Latent (assuming the other parent is pure human), while any children born after that nasty shock will always be Latents (and might be Fractals or Offspring, depending on the status of the other parent).

                                                    Does that help make more sense out of this?
