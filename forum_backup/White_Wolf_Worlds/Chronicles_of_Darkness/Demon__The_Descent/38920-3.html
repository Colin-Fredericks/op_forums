            I hope it's not too late to submit this. I hoped to get through Chapter 1 before posting.

            First of all, I want to say I'm so glad Onyx Path got this out to the backers so we could help spot errors. It sounds really nerdy but highlighting and proofreading WoD books is more my hobby than playing the actual games.

            Sorry if I resubmit ones that were already said. Most of what I saw have to do with hyphens and commas, which are highly contextual and difficult for automated systems to pick up. The missing words & spaces are probably the most important revisions though.

            PDF formatting -- The PDF bookmarks for all of chapter one are filed under "To Serve in Heaven." I think the intention was to have the sub-bookmarks of Chapter 1 be ("To Serve in Heaven," "To Reign in Hell," "Life as a Demon," "Descent," "Antagonists," "Embedded Agents," and "Infrastructure")

            Incorrect word -- p. 33 Second-to-last sentence of second-to-last paragraph before "To Reign in Hell,": ... kept studded by constant recycling." should probably be "...kept stunted by constant recycling."

            Confusing -- p. 33 Second sentence of first paragraph below "To Reign in Hell" reads: "Images of his taut muscles, light brown skin and sharp laugh..." laughing typically doesn't produce images. Perhaps the first word should be "Memories"

            Missing space -- p. 34 First sentence under Guardian: "...something in orderto ensure..." missing a space between "order" and "to"

            Not mistake, just a suggestion -- p. 35 Under Inquisitor, the phrase "ahead of the day when" is used twice in quick succession (adding "inevitable" doesn't reduce the repetitiveness). I would change one to "long before" or something similar.

            possible Extra word -- p. 35 Last sentence of Tempter uses the phrase, "cult of the personality," I've never heard it with a "the." The Wikipedia article is "Cult of Personality" so that's what I'd go with.

            Confusing -- p. 36 Third paragraph below "Why We Fall", second sentence: "The contradictions inherent in being an angel — obedient but independent, detached but objective" Obedience and independence can oppose one another but detachment and objectivity are usually considered complementary traits.

            Missing word -- p. 36 Fifth paragraph below "Why We Fall", fourth sentence: "He starts to defy orders small ways..." Should be "He starts to defy orders in small ways..."

            Extra comma? -- p. 36 right-hand column, second paragraph from the top, last sentence: "talk to a human without lying to them, once in a while" The comma between "them" and "once" seems unnecessary to me since it's still part of the clause.

            Missing hyphen -- p. 36 right-hand column, third paragraph, third sentence: "...angels are well aware..." well-aware is usually hyphenated, like well-liked and well-respected.

            Extra space -- p. 36 last sentence of second-to-last paragraph: "...kill him on sight ." unnecessary space between "sight" and the period.

            Missing word -- p. 37 First sentence of second-to-last paragraph: "Although it can like an agonizing eternity..." Needs a verb between "can" and "like". Should perhaps read: "Although it can feel like an agonizing eternity..."

            Plural error -- p. 39 Last sentence of first paragraph in the "With Fallen Scales" sidebar: "...a city of hundreds of thousand humans..." A more conventional phrasing would be "...a city of hundreds of thousands humans..."

            Wrong/missing word -- p. 39 first sentence of third paragraph under "The Mask": "...whatever it takes together impressionable humans..." should probably read as "...whatever it takes to gather impressionable humans..."

            Missing comma -- p. 40 Under "Cover and Identity", sixth paragraph, last sentence: "...Cover is a matter of life and death and identity is rarely more than a matter of convenience." Life and death is a phrase, but I would definitely put a comma before "identity" to help with clarity.

            Missing space -- p. 40 last sentence of second paragraph in the right-hand column: "...saving moneyor undertaking..." space needed between "money" and "or"

            Suggestion -- p. 40 last sentence of third paragraph in the right-hand column: "If a demon wants to lead a totally different kind of life..." Not grammatically necessary but beginning the sentence with "However" or "But" might make it read a bit better.

            Hyphenate? -- p. 40 second-to-last sentence of second-to-last paragraph in the right-hand column: "...step into a fully realized human life." Seem like there should be a hyphen in "fully realized"

            Extra word -- p. 41 First sentence under "Incarnation, Agenda, and Humanity": "...the Covers that let them walk seem like humans." Either "walk" or "seem" should be kept, but not both.

            Missing comma -- p. 41 Second sentence under "Incarnation, Agenda, and Humanity": "In part this is because" Ought to be a comma between "part" and "this"

            Missing hyphen -- p. 41 second-to-last sentence of second-to last paragraph before the "Psychopomps" header: "...and a short tempered Destroyer..." Should be a hyphen in "short tempered" especially noticeable because "short-tempered" is correctly hyphenated earlier in the sentence.

            Missing hyphen -- p. 42 third sentence after "Inquisitors": "...a side effect of spending..." Should have a hyphen between "side" and "effect"

            Missing word? -- p. 42 Last sentence of last paragraph before "Integrators": "They believe that they tread lightly..." Do you mean "They believe that they should tread lightly..."

            Missing parenthetical/commas? -- p. 42 Last sentence of last paragraph before "Integrators": "...and for
            better or worse they will never..." I usually see the phrase "for better or worse" offset by commas or parentheses.

            Wrong punctuation -- p. 43 Second paragraph after "Fallen Flesh": third/fourth sentence: "...chosen human death, They hope that..." The phrasing and random capital letter make it fairly clear this comma was meant to be a period.

            Incorrect plural -- p. 43 First sentence of fourth paragraph after "Fallen Flesh": "...spend most of their times hiding..." Should be just "time" instead of "times"

            Missing hyphen -- p. 43 Second sentence of sixth paragraph after "Fallen Flesh": "...terrifying shape, hard pressed to find mortals..." Most dictionaries I've seen hyphenate "hard-pressed"

            Extra word -- p. 44 First sentence under "Fallen Mind" "...emotional outlook is resembles that of a human..." Don't need "is" between "outlook" and "resembles"

            Unnecessary capitalization? -- p. 44 Second and third sentences after "Fallen Heart" both capitalize Demon in the middle of the sentence. Not technically wrong, but inconsistent with the convention of the rest of the book.

            Missing commas -- p. 45 second paragraph, third sentence: "The slender Polynesian woman you speak to on Thursday could be a bearded six-and-a-half foot tall white guy on the following Tuesday." There should probably be commas or conjunctions between a bunch of these adjectives. "bearded six-and-a-half foot tall white" should definitely have at least one comma (maybe two) and possibly also between "slender" and "Polynesian" because they're both being used as adjectives here.

            Missing word + missing hyphen? -- p. 45 Last sentence before "What Do Demons Do All Day?": "It’s almost impossible to be sure that a given demon is true believer..." First of all, we need an "a" between "is" and "true." Second, I usually hyphenate "true-believer" but then I might be a bit overzealous about hyphenating two-word terms.

            Missing word -- p. 45 Last sentence in the third paragraph from the bottom reads: "...demons come feel the same way..." should read "...demons come to feel the same way..."

            Missing hyphen -- p. 45 Second sentence of last paragraph: "commitment of a full time job." Full-time is usually hyphenated.

            Suggestion -- Were it me, I'd visually differentiate the example stories at the beginning of sections like "I Saw Satan Fall Like Lightning" and "What Do Demons Do All Day?" from the rest of the text, such as having it in italics. I seem to remember the core World of Darkness and other books formatting it like that.

            Missing comma -- p. 51 First paragraph after Exiles, third sentence: "In some cases the occult matrix..." comma missing? Shouldn't it be "In some cases, the occult matrix..."?

            Missing commas -- p. 57 Second complete paragraph from the top, first sentence: "A lone demon quietly making the best of her new life surrounds herself with humanity but is not truly a part of it." Shouldn't the phrase "quietly making the best of her new life" be offset with commas?

            Missing comma -- p. 58 Under Insurgent Agencies, first paragraph, fourth sentence: "Lesser demons tend to have difficulty convincing potential early recruits..." missing a comma between potential and early?

            Missing comma -- p. 58 Under Compromised Agencies, third paragraph, second sentence: "In almost all cases a compromised Agency..." Should probably be "In almost all cases, a compromised Agency..."

            Incorrect word -- p. 112 Under Initial Cover, first paragraph, fourth sentence: "...hate the Cover identity they began with, thought, seeing it as a last sign..." should be "...hate the Cover identity they began with, though, seeing it as a last sign..."

            Extra word -- p. 195 Under Demonic Forms, Full Transformation, first paragraph, first sentence: "Changing into demonic form is requires very little effort." the word "is" should probably be removed

            Missing comma? -- p. 195 Under Demonic Forms, Full Transformation, first paragraph, "...health box upon changing into her demonic form." Comma missing between 'box' and 'upon'

            Wrong word -- p. 195 Under Demonic Forms, Full Transformation, third paragraph, third sentence: "Regardless of the presence on onlookers" the word "on" should probably be replaced with "of" or "of any"

            Hyphenate -- p. 197 Under Blade Hand, second sentence: "...allowing for nothing more than routine maintenance to keep them well honed." I would hyphenate well-honed just like well-known and well-kept.

            Wrong word -- p. 205 Under Wings, first sentence: "...whether its the need to get to high places..." Classic mistake we've all made. Should read, "...whether it's the need to get to high places..."

            Wrong word -- p. 205 Under Processes, Aegis Protocol, System, second sentence: "...but does not impart an penalty to his Initiative." This should probably read, "...but does not impart any penalty to his Initiative." or "...but does not impart a penalty to his Initiative."

            Suggestion -- The Appearance for Magnesium Flare (p. 206) reads more like the specifications of the System than as suggestions for how it could appear. That said, the appearance and system are sort of one and the same for this.

            Confusing -- The Dice Pool for Memory Theft (p. 207) is "Manipulation + Subterfuge - Composure". I assume this means to subtract the target's Composure. If that's the case, it would likely help with clarity to say so. I couldn't come up with a reason why a demon with high Composure should be worse at this.

            Conjugation -- p. 207 Under Quill Burst, second sentence: "...fitted with a self-defense mechanism that allow them..." should be "...fitted with a self-defense mechanism that allows them..."

            Spacing -- p. 208 Under Rain of Fire, Success, last sentence: "...only remain for one turn/ Primum dot." There is a space only on the right side of the '/' which doesn't fit the style of the rest of the book.

            Wrong word -- p. 208 Under Voice of the Angel, third sentence: "...are still capable to sending electronic signals." Should be "...are still capable of sending electronic signals."

            Hyphenate? -- p. 208 Under Voice of the Angel, System: "...letting out an ear piercing screech..." Should be "...letting out an ear-piercing screech..."

            Confusing -- The Dice pool for Voice of the Angel, isn't in its own separate "Dice Pool:" section. Also it has a similar phrasing issue as Memory Theft where "- Composure" seems to suggest you subtract the demon's Composure. Unless that's the intended meaning.

            Hope this helps. I just leapt at the opportunity to help make a World of Darkness book better.
