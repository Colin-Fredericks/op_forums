                                  Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
                                  Kiss of the Succubus merit states "Mortals" are affected. Covers are mortal. While in the cover, would the demon be addicted to the vampire's bite?
                                  Well, this is more of a Vampire question than Demon. The Kiss, by default, only affects mortals. It doesn't affect any supernatural.... unless the vampire spends a point of willpower, at which point it does.

                                  So, I feel like this merit modifies what the Kiss does in general - whenever you would inflict Swooning, you also inflict Addicted. You bite a supernatural and spend a willpower point to inflict Swooning on them, then yes, I would say that KotS Addiction would affect the supernatural as well.

                                  I disagree that the Swooning and Addicted condition only affect the Cover, however. To me, that's akin to saying that damage should only affect individual covers, or that a mind control ability from a fellow supernatural only affects a cover one at a time. That's not how the game works, though. Damage carries between all forms. A demon that's been subjected to an Addiction Exploit or the Word power would be affected by those powers no matter the Cover he's in. Vampire powers should work the same way.

                                  Now, ACTING like you're addicted to the bite, or Swooning, in a Cover that shouldn't be is cause for compromise check, sure. But that doesn't mean it shouldn't still be there. This is the reason why Demons get to hide their emotional responses so well. So they can be afflicted by love and lust and desire and rage and fear towards things, but be able to not show it through an inappropriate Cover.

                                  I feel that only affecting a single cover is both detrimental to Vampire's themes, and to Demon's. Covers are just that - covers. They're basically masks you put on. This is still a single person experiencing all this. You don't suddenly fall out of love with someone just because you swap a Cover. You don't lose a mental Condition afflicted. Why should the Kiss be different?

                                  Next question. If your demon is in a building with no windows or any chance an outsider could see the shift.. would they need to roll a cover check?
                                  I go with StSword's take. If it would raise a red flag somehow, then compromise check. If not, then go with the flow.

                                  If your cover is a homeless boy, if you move into a home does your cover degrade or is that ok?
                                  Ehh... to me, that sounds like a situation that would involve a cover that's growing. As in, moving into a new home represents MORE connections, and thus justification on raising your Cover through Cover Exp. Or, rather, its something I would require raising Cover for, when next they had enough Cover exp.

                                      More on the vamp X demon crossover really quick -

                                      A random vamp and a random demon are at the bar. Somehow, the vamp and demon end up deciding to get a room together for an hour or two. The vampire bites the demon.

                                      Now, at this point, the vampire has absolutely no reason to suspect that the demon is, well, a demon. The vamp thinks he's biting a mortal - there's absolutely no reason for them to be spending a point of willpower. The demon will very likely be spoofing the taste of their blood as well, so the vampire should remain absolutely clueless through this entire exchange. Meanwhile, the demon will remain aware of someone biting their neck, drinking their blood, and that it feels really good, likely stunning a mortal victim. At this point, the demon has a choice - act like he knows the mortal would be reacting to the bite (as if Swooning / Addicted) or break cover and teach the vampire a lesson.

                                      If the demon chooses to remain in cover, then they will ACT like they're addicted to the Kiss. They're completely unaffected by the Conditions at this point, and they can break cover at any point they want. Until they do, the vampire should remain blissfully unaware that he's with a supernatural. That will likely be a persistent thing with that Cover from then on, up until the demon decides to break cover. After all, this is what a spy does. This is what is thematic for Demon.


                                      Now, should the demon somehow be compromised and the vampire finds out, then he's in trouble. The vampire will know he's dealing with a supernatural, and know to spend that extra effort of will to inflict the Kiss. This, too, is thematic for Demon- exposure is their bane, their weakness, just as sunlight and fire are vampire banes. They're vulnerable to people that know their secret identity.


                                      In conclusion, a demon being bit by a vampire will likely have a Cover that acts like they're Swooning or Addicted, though they won't have the actual Conditions. If the demon is compromised, however, all bets are off.
