    Originally posted by AinSoph View Post
    Y'all have made a lot of custom Legacies ^_^
    I posted about, what, five or six 1 ed. legacies? That's somewhere around a 5th to a 7th of what I've written up. I suspect I'm not unusual in holding back until someone requests something on a theme or just get in a mood to share one.

    I finally got around to porting some of those.

    The old model of '1st: see the thing and, generally, shield from it; 2nd: Start controlling it' mostly breaks down in 2e. With easy Mage Sight as a normal feature of all mages, making a 1st attainment an improvement which isn't superceded by the second dot attainment is tricky. I've been playing with ideas.

    Some of those:


    Rely on Arcana Attainments for sensory perception, and use Legacy Attainments for other things.
    Provides an appropriate Knowing effect as Instant action.
    Grant some utility effect.

    Grant the Unveiling effect at a high enough level to duplicate the effect sought.
    Reach for Casting Time and Advanced Duration are pretty much enough, since that means an Instant action places the effect for the rest of the day, minimum. That means one attainment level higher than than the casting level---usually the 2nd attainment---will suffice for an effectively always-on perception, and should something end it early, it's an Instant action to replace. Not quite as good as Indefinite perception + Reflexive replacement as many granted in 1e, but close, really.

    Make do with an ability that is hard to dispel though hard to replace:
    Place reach into Duration and not casting time. Such legacies would need some sort of Oblation/preparation ritual to make sense, but it's an option. As soon as you hit Arcana 2, that puppy bumps up to all day. That attainment is a bit of dead weight until that 2nd arcana dot is achieved, since you get a scene sight for a scene of prep..

    Upgrade Mage Sight:
    * Penetrating: Within the attainment's purview: If the mage has the ability to perceive some phenomenon, but without the ability to penetrate mystical concealment, such concealment now provokes a Clash of Wills based on the perception in use. (Whichever is strongest, plus one for each multiple after the first) If the mage already gets a Clash of Wills to penetrate concealment, that roll now adds automatic successes equal to the Arcana rating. (If the ability granting the option is a spell, this counts as stacking and burns through the effect per those standard rules.)
    * Encompassing: Limited, of course to the attainment's listed purview: The mage can perceive the phenomenon more easily. If some detail is normally only available through some Knowing effect, it becomes one of the forms of information available to Focused Mage Sight. If the property is normally only visible to a mage with an active Unveiling effect, it can be seen with Active Mage Sight. Finally, if the thing is normally perceived through Active Mage Sight, it becomes similarly clear to Peripheral Mage Sight. As the mage learns attainments that expand perceptions, the requirements may shift.
    * Expanding: Some legacies should provide a very different view than any normal spell or Mage Sight reveals. For example, unveiling the "Chains/Puppet-threads of the Exarchs" effect (whatever it was called) from Reign of the Exarchs would certainly qualify as something unusual to reveal to Mage Sight. There may be a way to cast the effect with basic magic, but it's not a general Attainment. A Legacy is a perfectly good place to add such an improvement. (Ironically, though, to keep parity with Arcana attainments, there is a case that these, too, should come at 2nd Attainment.)

    Fals Lich

    Parent Path: Moros
    Primary: Mind
    Order: Free Council, Mysterium

    The group studies death and the undead to understand the lessons of mortality. They are focused on learning the secrets of the dead, be they secrets of the afterlife or simply how unliving can so freely manipulate the physical world in material ways. Vampires and ghosts are especial focuses of their interest.

    Initiation:
    Their general concept is focused on the necromantic aspect of the Moros Path, yet they have found how to teach those who would share their insights. It involves trust, of course, since they must kill and restore anyone who wishes to join them.

    Mystical Factors:

    Mortality
    A true immortal is *not* a being that simply won't die at a normal time, but one for whom Death does not *apply*. "Killing" an immortal god doesn't work, it simply renders them angry and weak. They don't actually die. That's a requirement of true immortality. Undead, like vampires, who can be destroyed are not living but still mortal. Death can eventually return and finish its work.

    Underworld:
    In the Underworld, Death is closer. Casting is as if one has a higher rating of Death, but only for Reach. This effect also applies to this Legacy's attainments. This isn't a free reach to apply flexibly, unfortunately, but a specific form of expansion that applies when in the Underworld.

    1st: Dead Eyes
    Prerequisites: Mind 1

    Dealing with the dead is not a safe prospect. Their mental state is critical to monitor. In the cases of spectral death phenomena, mental state is largely definitional. Those who would study the dead to learn from them should learn to observe their minds.

    Fals Lich learned a way of observing the minds of the dead with the powers of Stygia.

    Mental Scan (Mind 1, Casting)
    In the Underworld, the minds are more clear, revealing brief related thoughts, per the reach option.


    Optional Death 1:

    The Fals Lich may perform a seance. (Technically, this can be any oblation for Moros that involve memory or communion with the dead or any ritual that would allow communiion with or summoning of the dead.) At the completion of the ritual, the Fals Lich may opena conduit o the Other Side, and Speak with the Dead. (Death 1, Duration)

    This effect can be cast on the Lich, specifically, or onto any marked area that the Lich is fully inside that is small enough to fit under the basic Scale of the effect. (At Death 4, this is a large room) Those inside the effect can speak with any ghosts also inside the effect.

    Scale must fit the ritual and a locally defined space.

    Duration may be cut off by the mage prematurely, which makes sense, given that it can last up to a week.

    In the Underworld, this can be cast in a scene and permits discourse with the dead without limitations of language. (That's two, count'em, bonus Reach... in exchange for the heart of the effect being useless)


    2nd: Probing Gaze
    Prerequisites: Mind 2
    The Fals Lich can examine a mind to see what it really is. Effectively, this is Know Nature. (Mind 1, Casting, Sensory)
    When used on a dead thing, however, Obsessions are revealed, as well.

    In the Underworld, the ability reveals Aspirations and Obsessions from anything examined.


    Optional Death 2:

    Moros come back from the dead, wiser. The Fals Lich tap that symbolism to learn things about the dead closed to most magi. Through a ritual preparation of a body for death, the Fals Lich may wrap it in the Mantle of Stygia. This state, also known as "wearing the Shroud", is a mystically significant event that shapes how reality treats the subject. On its own, the effect is a rather simple Suppress Life effect. (Death 2, Duration) This extends to all magical and mystical things, though, as the subjct counts as dead.

    A mage who has achieved this attainment can don or doff the effect reflexively once a round, to count and appear as dead or living however they desire, after casting the effect. This ability requires the full ritual, initially, and ends at the normal duration thereafter, but it isn't ended by simply choosing to be deemed living again for a time.

    In the Underworld, the mage can forgo the ritual and simply activate the Shroud as an instant action. The Shroud in the Underworld is a spectral-looking but literal thing. It dissolves if removed unless by one who with this attainment.



    3rd: Cloak of Lead
    Prerequisites: Mind 3

    The powers of Stygia can protect a mind. Any dead subject can have its mind protected, per Mental Shield. (Mind 2, Duration, Casting, Sensory) Moros do not intrinsically count, for purposes of this attainment, but the caster, vampires, and ghosts do.

    In the Underworld, any mind can be so protected.


    Optional Death 3:

    Relationships to Death are clear to the any Death-aspected Mage Sight. With this attainment, those symbols cast a sort of inverted light that the mage can use to percieve the entire world. This acts as Nightvision while using an active Mage Sight that incorporates Death. (Forces 1, Casting, Duration, Protected from Extremes)

    Avernian and Graveyard gates are automatically visible to peripheral Mage Sight to a mage with this attainment. While this attainment is active, concealing either form of gate provokes a Clash of Wills. Graveyard Gates can be percieved at Sensory range. (up from 10') This sense is distorted, however, while the mage counts as living. Rolls are automatically successful, but must be made to home in as normal. If the mage counts as dead, Graveyard Gates are simply visible.

    In the Underworld, the spell permits Scrutinty through Focused Mage Sight to reveal any Keys for gates and laws for Realms. To use the latter, something symbolic of the Realm must be scrutinized. The safest thing is usually the boundary gate.



    4th: One of the Boys
    Prerequisites: Mind 4

    The Fals Lich learns how to release Pneuma by performing a ritual passing. This is to say, the Fals Lich prepares the body to die. (As this implies, the attainment requires a living, physical body) Writing a will, making peace with people, or anything else symbolically important. If being used on another, the Fals Lich must participate in the preparations and the subject must, as well. Obviously, some might find the experience a bit intimidating.

    At the end, the Fals Lich symbolically indicates death. (Representationally "killing" the subject and actually killing the subject both work, but so does simply ending some representation of the subject's life.) The subject must release a breath during or after this point, which is what activates the effect.

    The subject frees from their physical form. Their body goes comatose (unless othe preparations were made to render it dead or something else.) as their mind moves free of physical constraints. This is Psychic Projection (Mind 4, Duration)

    In the Underworld, this may be cast as an Instant action.


    Optional Death 4:

    Those wearing the Shroud may be protected from the powers of the Dead or undead. This is an extended form of Ghost Shield. (Death 2, Casting, Duration, Extended to All Undead)

    In the Underworld, this protects against even physical attacks.



    5th: Death's Mantle
    Prerequisite: Mind 5

    The Fals Lich can call on the authority of one who understands death. Only the true immortals are immune to this power. The Lich can command anyone else per Psychic Domination. (Mind 2, Casting, Any Action (2), Area) If the Lich wants the command to apply even if suicidal, they must add a mana when invoking this attainment.

    The caster may exempt himself and, optionally, everyone wearing the Shroud from a given command. He needs to use Fate to be more selective than this.

    The effects are far more lasting in the Underworld, improving the effect to Advanced Duration.


    Optional Death 5:

    When using the 4th attainment, the Fals Lich can imbue a subject's projection with Death. The result is a Ghost for the duration of that effect. This soul is a projection of the subject's will, and is created as a Rank 1 ghost that has the subject as a fetter. (Create Ghost, Death 5, Duration)

    If the subject is a mage, the ghost is created as a Ghost Mage (though still created at Rank 1) appropriate to that mage. The subject retains access to any supernal magic, but use of any that the ghost is incapable of using separates the two, injuring the ghost for a level of Lethal, and launching the subject's consciousness into the world as a psychic projection. If the subject can cast Psychic Projection, they can project from the ghost as a reflexive action, simply understanding how.

    At the end of the duration, the ghost form dissolves, and, if the mind of the subject remains inside it, it is returned to the body automatically. Otherwise, that is handled according to the projection effect.

                        The Tamers of Lightning

                        A bolt of lightning is symbolic. It is a symbol of loss of ignorance. It also represents the punishment of humans from the Gods. In dreams, lightning is a symbol of a terrible event and negativity.

                        As a divine manifestation it has been a powerful symbol throughout history, and has appeared in many mythologies. Drawing from this powerful association, the thunderbolt is often found in military symbolism and semiotic representations of electricity. .

                        Lightning plays a role in many mythologies, often as the weapon of a sky and storm god. As such, it is an unsurpassed method of dramatic instantaneous retributive destruction: thunderbolts as divine weapons can be found in most mythologies. The most familiar thunderbolt weapon in the West was that of Zeus or Jupiter. His thunderbolts, manufactured by the cyclopic children of Gaia, were used to strike down impious criminals and divine opponents.

                        The followers of this legacy strive the be like the symbols of lightning, destroying ignorance, heralding terrible events and, if need be, deal retribution.

                        Punishers often have Just or Patient as Virtue and Pride or Wrath as Vice.

                        Parent path: Mastigos
                        Nickname: Punishers, Teachers, Retribution
                        Oblations: Spending an hour in a storm, Observing the area from a high vantage point, Getting struck by lightning

                        Attainments

                        - First: Touch Power
                        Prerequisites: Initiation
                        Emulates "Influence electricity"

                        Optional: Prime 1
                        Lighting reveals the truth and destroys ignorance, the Punisher gets the benefit of "Pierce Deception".

                        - Second: See Power
                        Prerequisites: Forces 2

                        The Punisher can sense all physical phenomena related to the presence and flow of electric charge. In other words she can sense lightning, electricity, magnetic fields, radio waves, etc.

                        This emulates and expands upon "Tune In" but without the ability to listen in on transmissions.

                        Optional: Space 1
                        The Punisher gets a feeling of being high up in the air and seeing all on a flat plain. Get the benefit of "The Outward and Inward Eye".

                        - Third: Withstand Power
                        Prerequisites: Forces 3
                        Emulates "Environmental Shield" but in addition to the normal effects the Punisher becomes immune to all non-magical forms of lightning damage.

                        - Fourth: Join Power
                        Prerequisites: Forces 4, Space 3
                        This Attainment can only be used during a storm either natural or created with "Control Weather". The Punisher is hit by lightning and disappears, seemingly evaporating. But the Punisher actually comes down in another lightning strike in the area of the storm, unharmed.

                        Optional: Space 4
                        For one Mana, the destination doesn't have to be in the same storm but could be in any storm on the planet. Or even a storm on another planet if desired.
                        The ability to travel to other planets gives the legacy some fame among awakened astronomers. Some astronomers may join this legacy just for this Attainment.

                        - Five: Become Power
                        Prerequisites: Forces 5
                        Sources of electricity now heal instead of damage.

                                                        Well, you certainly don't need that for a fourth attainment, Falcon777 since that's already the Space 4 Arcana Attainment.                                                         Some useful ideas:
                                                        Space 3:
                                                        Ban and Co-Location both fit well.
                                                        Space 4:
                                                        Alter Direction requires a Reach to curve, so for your purposes it's a pretty good 5th.
                                                        Honestly, though, the last three attainments could all be Co-Location with different Reach permutations.

                                                        Warp(3) and Collapse(4) could fit, too, if you want to make things somewhat martial.

                                                        1st: Inward and Outward Eye: (Sp1=1 Reach: casting) Instant action for a few rounds of warpy sight.
                                                        2nd: Ground Eater: (Sp1=2 Reach: casting, duration)
                                                        3rd: Ban: (Sp3=1 Reach: duration) This is activated by whatever pseudo physical act actually warps the space.
                                                        4th: Co-Location: (Sp3=2 Reach: casting, duration)
                                                        5th: Alter Direction: (Sp4=2 Reach: curvy, duration)

                                                            Bridgewrights (Pontifeces) - Thyrsi worshipers of the spirits in all things, particularly those that inhabit natural barriers like rivers, mountains, chasms, etc. Like the animist priests of old, a Pontifex believes that these boundaries are meant to be respected and crossed very carefully. (Space, attainments could include ground-eater, ban, alter direction)

                                                            Bloomer - Mastigoi who seek to tap into the collective intelligence of humanity, astral voyagers attempting to connect to and learn from all consciousness. (Mind, with optional Fate. Attainments could include telepathy, universal language, enhance/gain skill)
