                A bit late, but some thoughts

                ~ Restrictions on building an army of Hellhounds - it costs a lot in terms of aether. How do you have enough saved up? Sure, technically using a gadget won't cost aether, but then we've got them living for a single scene, and transporting from a kennel is going to be a scene shift. That means we're talking investing aether in keeping the hounds alive. These cryptids are also obvious unnatural, so the situations they can be used are limited without the hoard of angels.

                ~ Exploited Gadgets reek of aether. It might not be a compromise, but its still a huge flag. They're also obviously unnatural, so any observer will possibly trigger some Compromises due to exposure.

                ~ Making a bunch of hellhounds and running away, leaving angels to clean up the mess is likely to not work. Their Mission is to capture the rogue, not kill hellhounds. Someone else can clean that up. Or, worse. The hunter angel has some kind of animal-control power that can turn your hellhounds against you. God can clean up any surviving witnesses later.

                ~ I'd say that long term hellhounds probably should be bought as Retainers. That's how Vampire handles ghouls, including animal ghouls.

                ~ I can't think of any reason I'd ever allow a player to have a Rank 5 cryptid. They need to work with the ST, which means veto power is in play, and I can't think of any reason why I'd allow that many attributes and skills above 5 or supernatural merits/Adaptations.


                Sure, there's not a lot of rules, but I think its clear that the intent is that you're going to end up with something just slightly stronger than an average animal. That's not enough to make it to Rank 5.
