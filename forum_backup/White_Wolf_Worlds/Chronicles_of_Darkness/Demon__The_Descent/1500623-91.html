                    Originally posted by quelsec View Post
                    Do mages in this system increase attributes magically permamently? or is the table showing just how big they can be while using temporal busting spells?
                    Mages, like other supernaturals, have a set maximum of trait limits (skills and Attributes) that can be purchased with XP, determined by their power stat. In the case of mages, it's set by Gnosis, with Gnosis 6 being the first milestone allowing them to buy post-human traits. You can find a chart with more details on page 86.

                    Spells can boost traits up to the subject's limit, normally through a Perfecting. It costs some Reach and a point of mana to go above trait limits.

                    In other words, let's say you have a Augment Mind with no Reach options and a Potency of 5. If you have Gnosis 7 mage and a Sleeper, both with an Intelligence of 2, the spell will boost the Intelligence of the mage to 7 and Sleeper's to 5.

                                                    Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                                                    It is possible to put Attainment like Time in a Bottle in a imbued item?
                                                    Originally posted by Mage, the Awakening 2ed, page 195
                                                    The mage can imbue an item with any spell she is capable of casting. Imbued items retain their magic indefinitely; a spell cast using an imbued item might run out, but the imbued item itself does not. Attainments may only be included if they modify a spell; for example, mages can imbue items with spells using Sympathetic Range, but may not imbue an item with Mage Armor or the ability to create Rotes.
                                                    So it seems like just imbuing Time in a Bottle by itself is not possible, but you can use it as part of the spell being imbued, which will increase it's activation mana cost. Since an imbued item has a battery of 1 mana by default and the spell would cost at least 2 (1 to activate the item + 1 for Time in a Bottle), you might need to spend more time imbuing to increase the capacity.

                                                    Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                                                    It is possible to add a conjunctional Mind 3 or 4 to the Web weaver spell to determine the kind of feeling associated to the modified Sympathy?
                                                    Since the primary function of Web-Weaver is to bolster the connection, the conjunctional Arcana is not expanding it's functionality within it's focus. So I would say you require either a conjunctional spell or a separate cast of Correspondence.
