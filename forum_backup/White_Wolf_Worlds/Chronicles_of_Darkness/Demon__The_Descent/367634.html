    The Angel Factory- My Angels & Their Infrastructure

    01-12-2015, 03:01 PM
    So, having recently been given the responsibility of being a GM for a nWoD game set in my hometown of Winnipeg- and being slightly crazy, I went and decided to undertake an absolutely insane endeavour. To create a fully-fleshed angelic hierarchy, with Infrastructure, for the city. These angels are only those that interact with the physical world on a regular basis- they don't include angels working in various 'off-world' conditions.

    A few things to note. I was deeply inspired by Mr. Paraduo's Angelic Hierarchy, and as such my angels have a serial number alongside a name (My personal notes use Enochian glyphs to take the place of letters in the serial number, but the forums probably don't support that). The name is for use amongst other angels, and occasionally cultists, but their human Cover typically has a name as well. Meshing the Cover traits and rules for the Unchained with the increased stats of the Claimed, I think I've given a pretty good model of why angels are such a threat to Demons. Especially since mine use Embeds and possess an Angelic Form as well as wielding Numina and Influences. My first angel is, technically, a bit of a cop-out, since I figured it would be easy enough to take the quickstart and add my own flare to it as a way to introduce my players to the nWoD, but it gives a pretty good idea of how I've decided to design my angels when compared to the original version.

    *****

    Falcon Lake Lotions

    This particular facility is the culmination of several pieces of simple Concealment Infrastructure supporting an important piece of Logistical Infrastructure which could aid in facilitating smoother operations for the God-Machine and its angels in Winnipeg and the surrounding area. To all intents and purposes, it looks like a simple lavender farm with on-site processing facilities. However, in reality, it is a recycling plant for the God-Machine nearing completion. All it needs now is a few more Demons to act as the refining engines- turning Aether back into Essence.

    Concealment: The farm itself serves as concealment- the various buildings, the gift store, and the lavender fields -all of it serves to conceal what’s really going on at the farm. However, there are other pieces of Infrastructure concealing the Facility from prying eyes. The Linchpin of the ferry system is its route. While the ferry is important, it can be replaced. The real linchpin is beneath the water. To get to the farm, one must take a ferry across Falcon Lake, and buried beneath the ferry’s highly specific “scenic” route are submerged containers in the lakebed which are etched with numerous occult symbols, and in fact are filled with various products from the farm. The ferry’s seating plan is arranged to evoke significant mathematical and geometric patterns- which when combined with the cramped conditions of the boat, funnels the Essence of mixed boredom and annoyance into the containers, which combines with the Essence of calm the lavender produces, and ultimately mixes into an aura of blissful ignorance on the farm itself. In the winter, when the farm ‘closes’ and visitors stop coming, this additional layer of Infrastructure begins to wear thin, but is revitalized by the first ferry trip of the spring. The Logistical Infrastructure of the recycling plant is concealed beneath the lavender field itself. Ports will be hidden beneath particularly large plants, while the Linchpin is a robust example of cryptoflora.

    Logistics: Angels need Essence to keep running, and Aether is useless to the God-Machine. Not only can angels not use it, but Aether runoff can create cryptids that make concealment more difficult. This recycling plant, hidden deep within the south field, funnels the recycled Essence into the Linchpin, which is connected to the processing plant on-site. The Essence is imbued into objects that eventually find their way into the hands of angels, typically via surplus stocks that are supposedly donated to charities. Currently, until the core Infrastructure of the facility is completed, the Aether generated by the occult matrix of the Concealment Infrastructure is simply pumped into the fields as the God-Machine has found that lavender cryptoflora is very useful in creating mind-altering substances that can be absorbed through pores in the skin. These include all products made on-site. They are distributed through commercial channels to stores that carry these products and cater to a local market. These will continue to be produced after the recycling plant is completed. When/if the recycling plant is completed, the Essence filled product will be transported via unmarked vans to U-Haul facilities where they will remain overnight, and will be picked up by a cultist-run dummy corporation the next morning. They are then stored in supposedly defunct warehouses that are owned by a shell corporation of the cult, where individual shipments will be picked up by members of a second cult and dead-dropped at several thrift shops around the city, where they will be retrieved by members of the angels’ staff posing as charity workers.

    Angel Summoning: In order to summon an angel, an occult matrix must be formed, Essence must be gathered, and Infrastructure must be in place. At Falcon Lake Lotions, all of these are fully supported. The ferry ticket stand and loading dock serves as Concealment for it, and houses most of the strange technology necessary to facilitate it, but the true engine of the angel summoning is in the public bathroom attached to the building. A circuit is completed when the ferry docks. The mathematical and geometric designs which reacted with the occult matrix running the Concealment Infrastructure on the farm resonates with similar occult patterning in the building, causing tickets which are purchased there to be treated as sacrifices, every four tickets producing a single measure of Essence. Ultimately, in order for the summoning to be effective for this particular angel, 22 Essence must be generated. However, the angel burns one Essence a day to stay alive while it waits, incorporeal, in this building, and after a couple of days, the Essence burns out and Aether leaks into the lake. This produces the occasional cryptid. The bathroom itself is Logistical in nature. The motion activated hand dryer scans fingerprints and assesses the aura of a potential target, the toilet samples epithelials and runs DNA for compatibility, and the way the target responds to various posters and potential problems with the bathroom- a jammed door, lack of toilet paper, etc. -assesses their rough capabilities. If all the boxes are checked, the bathroom locks and this triggers an occult matrix that violently sacrifices the person, then reconstitutes them as a Cover for the angel. By the time the door has unlocked, everything appears normal and the angel is embedded into its Cover. The person cannot be rescued or recovered- they only exist to conceal the identity of the angel now. The Linchpin of this particular bit of Infrastructure is the smoke detector in the exact centre of the roof, hidden behind a protective metal cage.

    NOTE: If a Demon enters the bathroom, its scanners will figure it out, and the character receives the Flagged Condition. Chances are, any Demons investigating the farm won’t determine the purpose of the bathroom, and will only see it as part of the Concealment Infrastructure. Most will leave it alone. If Quorum Falls or dies, and the facility remains active, a new angel will be crafted, a new Cover will be produced, and work on the site will continue. If he dies and one or more of his duplicates is still alive, one of them retains the Cover, the other shares the Cover, or simply becomes a guardian of the site, maybe being reworked by the God-Machine into a true Guardian or Destroyer. If he Falls, then the remaining duplicates share the Cover, and may or may not always have a bead on where he is at any given moment.

      Boel (Gregory Markov, S-1:/NA/C/M/W:1-G/S-1)

      Boel means “God is in him” and that is a suitable name for this angel. It is one of a group of prototype angels with the prayers of Benedictions written into their code that can be accessed as Embeds. He serves as one of the more secretive angels in Winnipeg, under a group of Analysts. As per the nature of Analysts and the God-Machine's experiments, Boel and his kin are almost entirely unknown to the rest of the angels in the city.

      Attributes
      Physical- Strength: 5 (4) Dexterity: 4 (2) Stamina: 4 (3)
      Mental- Intelligence: 3 (1) Wits: 4 (3) Resolve: 3 (2)
      Social- Presence: 3 Manipulation: 3 (2) Composure: 4 (3)

      Factors
      Size- 5 (Adult Human) Health- 9 Willpower- 7 Defense- 8 Initiative- 8 Speed- 14
      Rank- 1 Max Essence- 10
      Ban- If commanded to identify himself in Russian, Boel will recite his serial number and a significant line of code, which happens to be a protective benediction [H:tV Pg. 159- roll Resolve + Rank, the angel is protected by a barrier that extends five yards in all directions around it, and can only be crossed by a being with Presence greater than the angel’s Presence + Supernatural Trait]
      Bane- Any depiction of St. Agrippina of Mineo [It interacts badly with a core segment of code; the Benediction that is recited if it is commanded to identify itself]

      Skills
      Physical- Athletics: 4 Brawl: 3 Drive: 1 Firearms: 3 Stealth: 3 (Shadowing Targets) Weaponry: 2
      Social- Intimidation (Physical Threats): 3 Streetwise (Winnipeg): 2 Subterfuge: 2
      Mental- Investigation: 1 Medicine: 1 Occult: 2

      Influences- Fear: 2 Virtue- Suspicious Vice- Dispassionate
      Numina
      Omen Trance: Once every 24 hours, the entity may enter a trance to gain a vision of the future- typically warnings of danger. It spends 1 Essence if it’s on its own behalf, and spends 3 Essence if it’s for someone else. Roll Wits + Occult
      Pathfinder: Determines the quickest route to a destination. Spend 1 Essence; the effect lasts for the scene, during which time the entity should be able to get there. If the destination is a Safe Place, the roll is contested by the lowest Resolve + Supernatural Trait among the owners. Roll Intelligence + Streetwise

      Holy Trinity Ukrainian Orthodox Church

      Like virtually every religious institution the God-Machine has hijacked instead of creating, very little of the Infrastructure here has anything, at all, to do with the priest, the church’s finances, or the congregation. In spite of the dogma and cultural outlook of some of these older, more traditional churches, most of the parishioners are not easily susceptible to a cult mindset, and many are simply too darn stubborn to be persuaded by all but the most potent Embeds. It isn’t worth the Essence to use various Numina to establish a cult. Instead, this church serves as an important tether point- farming Essence, ascertaining its resonance, and broadcasting that information to Command Infrastructure in the city.

      Concealment: Surprisingly, the primary Infrastructure here that serves to camouflage the God-Machine’s work is not inside the facility itself, but directly across the street from it in full view of the church’s doors. The sign for Blackletter Books & Collectibles contains several layered memetic agents, and the Linchpin is a novelty model skull inside the store. The Aether is stored in an air conditioning unit attached to the apartment above the store. The first layer of defense here is an embedded pull. Those stepping out of the church automatically find themselves compelled to look at the sign. The next layer is a simple mind wipe, to make people forget about anything unusual they may have seen inside the church. [Instantly does so against those with a Wits rating of 2 or under] If that doesn’t work, the next layer is a contested roll to compel the individual to call the number advertising psychic readings next to the sign. [Resolve + Supernatural Trait vs. Manipulation + Subterfuge] If they do call, they gain the Amnesia Condition regarding the unusual thing they have just seen, and only remember getting a reasonably good reading from a telephone psychic [who happens to actually be the angel in charge]. One of the Unchained who calls will receive the Flagged Condition, but will not be affected by Amnesia.

      Another piece of Concealment Infrastructure is the crosses atop each of the towers. While they look more or less normal, at sundown on March 27th people who find themselves looking carefully can see the shimmering outlines of angelic glyphs along the length of the cross. This serves to encrypt the data the Logistical Infrastructure is sending. The Linchpin is a large stained glass window in the fifth, central tower. One that cannot normally be reached from inside the church. The Aether is stored in an icon of St. John of Damascus that is hidden in a locked chest in the basement of the church.

      Logistics: The only part of the Infrastructure actually inside the church, the four main corner towers and the large central one form a broadcasting array that collects and reads the resonance of Essence before sending it to Command Infrastructure. For some reason, maintaining the Shadow of the North End the way it is happens to be important to the God-Machine, and it will likely oppose any attempt to help the area that can’t be stemmed by the local Spirits. The Linchpin of the Infrastructure is a set of five bells, one set in each tower. Made of cold iron, plated in meteoric nickel, and leafed in blessed silver, when the bells are rung in unison they harvest Essence from the surrounding area, read its resonance, and transmit it to the Command Infrastructure it’s tethered to. Replacing one of the bells would slow the process down, but all five would have to be replaced or destroyed to actually take down the Infrastructure for good. The Aether container for this particular piece of Infrastructure is a finely crafted statue of the archangel Gabriel. The statue has been known to frequently become covered with an unusual patina that is almost like a metallic mold [in fact an example of cryptoflora]. The church staff believes that it is a natural side effect of the metallic compounds in the statue’s enameled surface. On November 8th the statue has been known to glow and exude miraculous rosy oil [Aether runoff].

      Defense: Any and all attempts to tamper with the Linchpins triggers a silent alarm that is rigged into the very mind of Boel- he will come running, at the very least, to investigate. The angel is also present for the end of mass at noon every Sunday, when they ring the bells. Boel himself serves as Defensive Infrastructure that supports the Concealment Infrastructure across the street, as he is the proprietor of the store, runs the phone psychic line, lives in one of the apartments above the store, and has been known to make people asking too many questions disappear. Not too hard to do in this neighborhood- sometimes it even can look normal. Others living in the apartments tend to be very nervous and very cautious. They know that the landlord Gregory Markov keeps the gangs from breaking in, but they’re also frightened of him, and kitchen accidents with knives are far too common for it to be a simple accident.

      Angel Summoning: Gregory Markov was an invention of the God-Machine, a clever, well-crafted patchwork Cover made from the failing memories of elderly Eastern European immigrants. As such, when he arrived and took on the job of landlord’s assistant, the people were rather happy and trusting of him- all save one. The true landlord of the time was a stigmatic cultist, who worked to establish the angel’s way into the world by small sacrifices. In the basement of the building, the water heater runs off boiling blood and Essence. Then, the Linchpin was a particular cuckoo clock, but now it is a combination of the boiler itself and the ghost of Dmitri Burkov- whose beloved cuckoo clock is now his Anchor. It is Burkov’s ghost who causes the knife accidents, small sacrifices of blood that run down the drain and somehow, miraculously, make it to the boiler where they continue to fuel the angel Boel.

          Uvb (Andrea Friesen, S-1:NA/C/M/W:1-G/S-2)

          Soul Gallery Inc

          Not all Infrastructure needs to be an obvious, stand-alone structure, or accomplish extremely unusual effects. Most examples, even full facilities, prefer to instead piggy back off of pre-existing mortal institutions, and use as little supernatural intervention as possible. With Winnipeg, the problem isn’t a lack of support or resources, but a lack of a number of people. Paradoxically, the more people live in a city, the larger it is, the easier it is for the God-Machine to affect causality or edit people, places, or things out of existence. In a city like Winnipeg, you need misdirection, and this bit of Infrastructure helps support that in full.

          Concealment: In the gallery, there is a single bronze piece- a flat disk with arcane symbols engraved beneath the surface -that is tied to an identical piece that was more obviously engraved with positive imagery that sits at the centre of a labyrinth at the nearby Pembina Fisher Park. These two together are the Linchpin. Any efforts to destroy one without destroying the other simultaneous will trigger additional Infrastructure meant to defend the site. Individuals passing the park are compelled to go and walk it, and in spending a few minutes walking serenely, meditating, finding oneself, they lose the destination at the core. The journey becomes more important than the destination, and as such, it becomes more difficult to find the magically connected gallery. It most definitely helps that the gallery is in a private residence that is only available to the public for a very limited time each month. The Aether receptacle is a large block of clay in the basement that always seems to replace itself, and when fired creates pottery that is much more durable than normal. Andrea Friesen has taken credit for this, and claims that it is from a particular deposit up north which gives it these special durable properties. As Andrea is always at the gallery before anyone else, no one questions that she replaces the clay.

          Logistics: The main Infrastructure here is supported directly by the angel known as Uvb. It doesn’t really require Essence, nor does it need an Aether receptacle, because it is supported by the work of the angel’s hands. Any and every statue she makes is intended to evoke a specific response, which will then turn it into the focal point, perhaps even the Linchpin, of a God-Machine plan in the future. The gallery is frequented by members of a different angel’s staff, always on the lookout for suitable pieces to set up the groundwork for altering reality or causality itself.

          More advanced Logistical Infrastructure is hidden behind another wall of Concealment Infrastructure. In the gallery is a guest book with a metal cover that is always warm to the touch. Using a pen that uses a mixture of Aether and blood for ink, a cultist signs their true name, and where they’re taking that particular art piece. After doing so, their name changes to an ironclad false identity with some inane comment about the beauty of art attached to it. Any attempt to trace the buyer’s financials suffers the same supernatural hindrance if they were to attempt to trace the buyer themselves. The Linchpin to the Concealment Infrastructure is the pens. They always use the same model to sign, and to remove the Infrastructure every pen must be destroyed. The Aether receptacle for the Concealment Infrastructure is a vat of blood at an undisclosed location that must always remain at body temperature unless in use. The Linchpin for the Logistic Infrastructure is the guest book itself. From the moment a cultist signs it, it inputs the information about where to ship the art piece into the GPS of several delivery trucks at a warehouse near the airport. The drivers are oblivious to what the trucks are being used for, but find it odd they don’t ship much more than art. Destroying the guest book obliterates the GPS data, and unless more overt action is taken, the art pieces won’t make it to where they need to go. The Aether receptacle is a decorative crystal orb attached to a chandelier in the basement.
          It is worth noting that while the angel known as Andrea Friesen is the only supernatural being at the gallery, art is still well known for being able to generate Essence, especially that tied to emotions. Those who come to the gallery are always on the lookout for other pieces that could serve as the focal point for Essence farms or be used to begin to alter the Shadow of an area if used as urban art.

          Defense: All attempts to tamper with the Linchpins pull at the attention of Uvb. The angel counts as a piece of Infrastructure herself. Depending on the severity of the assault, she may even shed human form and go on the assault. However, that’s the least of a Demon’s worries, as trying to take down the primary Concealment Infrastructure is problematic in and of itself, and destroying any other Linchpin is less overt but equally as likely to get one of the Unchained in trouble with the proprietor, and maybe the police. If a ring of Demons does the impossible and takes down the Concealment Infrastructure, a defensive failsafe kicks in. Hidden behind a decorative shield on the wall is an antique clock, this clock is always tied to the Linchpin, which is an identical clock in Andrea Friesen’s studio apartment in Osborne Village. In the event that one of the Linchpins for the Concealment Infrastructure is deactivated, the failsafe reverts time back to whatever time it says on the clock. Anyone involved is transported back to wherever they were at that time. The Aether receptacle is a paper shredder in the apartment.

          Angel Summoning: Andrea Friesen was originally a person, a starving artist trying to live her dream in Osborne Village. She eventually stumbled upon one of the God-Machine’s reality alteration rooms, which she used profusely in an effort to improve her life, and give her more time to focus on art. As she grew less and less focused on life, she became more and more obsessed with perfecting her art; leading to very valuable sculptures she created being destroyed by her hand- in reality, sacrifices to the angel waiting in the wings. When she was eventually erased, the angel was ready to step into her shoes, and did so. It continues to make, and then sacrifice, beautiful statues to keep itself running, and the small studio apartment is connected to a Bolthole via a closet. The Bolthole is equipped with a full chemistry lab, which is never empty, so that it can replenish its Elixirs, and a full armory. It is also attached via another door to a broom closet at the gallery, enabling Uvb to get to the site extraordinarily quickly. The Aether receptacle for her summoning and continued survival is a toolbox for her art in the apartment. Most of the art implements are now embedded gadgets.

              X-C:H/39: (Josiah Miller, S-1:NA/C/M/W:1-G/S-4)

              An experiment by the God-Machine into whether angels operating as Claimed would make effective agents. Fused with a human psyche, they would be closer to the Unchained condition, and bonded to their host they’d be unable to Fall. So far, no one has been able to take the full load for very long- the angel’s AI-like psyche is anathema to the human brain, and eventually they destroy each other. This particular example manifests a few form powers, but otherwise uses fairly mundane magic alongside Embeds.

                      Interesting question. It may seem as though I have, but this is really just a preference for having each and every angel be a threat. The fairly low stats of a Rank 1 angel didn't give me that vibe, and I figured that if a Demon used to be an angel, and they have a Cover, the most likely angels to fall and have Covers are the foot soldiers, IE. Rank 1s. I just figured it would be a bit different to have the direct counterparts of the Unchained be such a massive difference in power. They're got a range of form powers similar to a Demon at Primum 10, they have Numina, they have Influences- in other words, they're exactly like Demons, but with a significant power boost. Take Uvb, for example. When you stack up her form powers, like Long Legs and Plasma Drive, then combine them with the Speed Numen, she gets a Speed of roughly 108 for the rest of the scene. Functionally, my players insisted I start treating her unarmed attacks when she's moving that fast like a car crash- and they absolutely loved it. Of course, with one solid hit she can pretty much lay out just about any character not in Demonic Form, but they loved the tension and the skill that fighting something with that sort of overwhelming power came with.

                      335 Donald Street
                      An old facility, the abandoned, boarded up, but still pristine Masonic temple on Donald Street has managed to avoid being torn down, repurposed, or even being sold, for almost fifty years. Completed in 1895, and closed down as a Masonic Temple in 1968, the angel running the Infrastructure, and its minions, managed to have it repurposed as a restaurant in 1978 which served as Concealment while they upgraded the Infrastructure. The building was finally abandoned in 1999, though enterprising businessmen have poured over 3.2 million into upkeep and renovation before mysteriously abandoning their projects. The building is known, in Winnipeg’s occult scene, for being haunted- though not quite in the way they imagine. Sergeant Josiah Miller usually tours the building, for kicks, about once a month.

                      Logistics: No one is sure what the original purpose of this Infrastructure was, save for perhaps Castiel, the local Principalities, and the God-Machine, but whatever it was, it took around 73 years for the project to complete. The ritualized and staged process of Masonic rituals, money donated to the temple by a specific internal faction, and art objects that would occasionally go missing fueled the building to do… something. Whatever it was, it required a ludicrous amount of Essence to pull off, and the neighbourhood has remained incredibly resistant to change ever since, which is probably what this Infrastructure is meant to do now- maintain the status quo. Whatever output this Logistical Infrastructure was supposed to pull off has been completed, and it blankets the area totally. It doesn’t regularly cycle Essence anymore- in fact, it seems like a dead zone, sucking up Essence that goes off to some unknown destination. The Aether, which is by now about 16 years old, still sits in the old oil drums for the fry cookers. No one is really sure if it’s still there, or if Aether that’s been sitting for that long is even safe to use anymore. It could be toxic- even for a Demon.

                      Defense: The most obvious form of Infrastructure to this end in the facility is the angel Castiel and its minions- invisible and unseen -that serve as the inspiration for stories of hauntings. The building itself is defended by, apparently, ironclad documents of ownership. The individual who oversaw the sale of the building was working, knowingly or unknowingly, for the God-Machine. Only the rightful owner of the building may do it permanent harm. Otherwise, thanks to the sturdy Logistical Infrastructure, any damage done to the building will simply warp out of reality thanks to the “backup” that Infrastructure has stored. The Linchpin for this is the bill of sale, and proof of ownership. If a ring of Unchained can obliterate any mention of the current owner’s claim on the property, the Infrastructure will fail. The Aether receptacle is the safety deposit box that the official document is kept in. As a result, it has become something of a metaphysical open space, like a Bolthole, and many “confiscated” objects- like Gadgets -have ended up in there. Just waiting for an angel, or clever Demon, to pull them out. Beyond just the simple Defense of the building, the building itself appears to form a Linchpin for a larger piece of Infrastructure, which is quite possibly another, larger, layer of Defensive Infrastructure meant to defend something much more important than a simple Facility.

                      Angel Summoning: Of course, the most obvious angel is Castiel, but his summoning Infrastructure was in a different place and is long gone. He no longer has any direct support, and instead lures the occasional homeless person down to the basement, where he will sacrifice them to fuel himself. However, something just isn’t right with that picture. Where do his minion angels get their Essence? Why are they always in perfect health with full tanks of Essence? And why, then, does the entire building still vibrate with energy sympathetic to angel summoning? There is no obvious Linchpin, nor is there an Aether receptacle- so where the hell is it all?

                                      With the Angelic Form, it is mostly just for book-keeping purposes- I needed some way to keep track of all the potential powers the angel has, and to keep a view of its appearance fairly static. I find that if I don't write that stuff down it tends to blur and change. But yes, either they turn it all the way on, or not at all. I do agree that, with the Rank rules as written, a Rank 2 entity is more or less directly comparable with your average starting supernatural PC, but for some unusual reason I can't entirely remember, I decided to differentiate between Rank 1 angels who are purely Ephemeral beings, and those who have a "Cover", as it were. I think it was partially inspired by a mixture of how Quorum was statted in the quickstart, wondering what happens to "deleted" people from the reality-editing room in the GMC, and this idea just came out of it.

                                      Ephemeral Rank 1 angels still make an appearance, but more so as servants of a more powerful angel. I ended up discussing this with my players while I was building the setting, and they decided that with the size of the city this Chronicle is set in, fighting a Rank 1 Ephemeral angel would feel more like a "Monster of the Week" story than "Holy Crap, enemy agents are everywhere!" I think the decision also had something to do with how the different ranks of angels were described in Mr. Paraduo's Angelic Hierarchy, which has deeply inspired a lot of how I treat angels in my Demon and GMC games.
