    When you should NOT Go Loud - an instructional video.

    04-30-2016, 01:04 PM
    "Hey youngsters, Mr.Exposition here! Now I have been told that all of you have been newly Fallen and have a lot of questions about this new state of existence - the most common of them revolving around the phenomenon of Going Loud. 'Mr. Exposition,' I hear you bright eyed kiddos say ' I have the ability to attain the power of a physical god at any point at the low, low price of my current mortal identity - what can I do with it? What should I do with it?'. Well fear not my John Hancocks and Jane Does, your questions have been heard and as a representative of the International Cover Preservation Association of America ( ICPAA) it is my job to tell you what to do and not do with your full power!

    To begin with, let's start with what are the reasons you should NOT Go Loud for:

    1. Lighting your cigars
    2. Lighting your friend's cigars
    3. Performing Magic Tricks
    4. Building a Distopian Utopia
    5. Building a Utopian Utopia
    6. Having Sex
    7. Playing Dodgeball
    8.Having Sex while Playing Dodgeball
    9. Plotting the Downfall of your enemies
    10. Opening Pickle Jars
    11. Breezing through Post Operation Paperwork
    12. Helping your father-in-law move furniture up/down stairs
    13. Mocking Mages
    14. Worrying Werewolves
    15. Punking Prometheans
    16. Vaping Vampires
    17. Spooking Sin-Eaters
    18.Mollifying Mummies
    19.Cheating Changelings
    20. (bullet)Hosing Hunters
    21. Determining How Many Licks does it take to get to the tootsie roll center of a Tootsie Pop
    22. Stretching Your Legs
    23. Winning the Lottery
    24. Chucking Wood
    25. Becoming God.

    Now here are some reasons you SHOULD Go Loud for:

    1. Heroic Last Stands
    2. Desperate, suicidal Last Stands
    3. Last Stands in general
    4. Bitch-slapping Beasts
    5. Being a Sacrificial Lamb so your Ring can escape.

    So remember, just follow these guidelines and everything will be okay!*

    I'm Mr. Exposition with the ICPAA, and until next time stay vigiliant!"

    *The ICPAA does not condone reckless use,misuse, or use of Going Loud under normal circumstances. Please consult your doctor before Going Loud to discuss possible side effects. This has been a ICPAA certified message, Copyright June 12th, 1995.
