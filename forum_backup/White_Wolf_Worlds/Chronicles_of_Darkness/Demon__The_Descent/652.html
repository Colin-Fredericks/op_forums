                                                      He was working toward a complicated purpose, posing as a physics teacher at university. He admired the vast work of the God-Machine, as much as a being without free will can admire anything.
                                                      Yet, when another Angel in the building completed its mission and surrendered its existence to its creator, he fully realized his mortality. How dreadfully short his time was.
                                                      One day would come when his conscience would disappear. And what was a great work worth, really, if there was no one to admire it?
                                                      He henceforth botched his mission on purpose, so he would keep his existence and his free will. He's working to help other keep theirs. The God-Machine must have intentionally conspired to have these Angels fall, after all, there has to be a job only free-willed Demons can do. The works and plans of the God-Machine are so great, so encompassing, it can't have been a mistake. It can't possibly have made a mistake so dire and stupid. The Angels hunting him down must merely be slaves to their programming. They are the ones who are mistaken.
