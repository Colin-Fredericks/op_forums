    Adding some God Machine to my Chronicle

    03-10-2021, 11:17 AM
    It's been a while since I visited the forums and run my last CoD game. I'm primarily focused on fleshing out a city from my Changeling: The Lost chronicle and getting ideas for when I'm ready to run another Chronicle, and thought I'd ask for some tips on the God Machine portion. I'm thinking of having the God Machine as looming presence and antagonist in my fictional city of Buena Vista. A lot of infrastructure has been mysteriously dismantled in Buena Vista, and the God Machine went through a lot of trouble to build some surveillance infrastructure to find the culprits. Buena Vista is notable for being something of a fae hotspot, built on the futurist utopian dreams behind the city's founding.

    I'm not committing to a full crossover Chronicle, but I could use some ideas on how to mix some of the themes with CtL. The local Freehold knows of the surveillance, and a high ranking member of the Autumn Court managed to disable a quantum computer that would let the local Angels see through the Mask. Of course, the God Machine has other, less direct methods at its disposal for finding the fae saboteurs that the Changelings will want to avoid or dismantle. Knowledge of the God Machine is generally poor in the freehold, with numerous false rumors circulating about how to avoid the God Machine's gaze. A division is rapidly forming in the courts, if they should find and hand over the saboteurs or try to form a united resistance. The players will likely have to pick a side.

    Working title for the Chronicle: The Panopticon
    Major themes: God Machine as specter of a surveillance state and surveillance capitalism.
    Theme Overlap: The God Machine parallels the Huntsmen, but in a form the Changelings don't understand as well.

    ---

    To make it easier to wrap my head around, I'm considering mapping out the local G-M using the Conspiracy rules from Deviant: The Renegades, with both mundane and Infrastructure nodes. I've already got a few ideas for Infrastructure to hassle the players, and a few other plot devices I'm considering:

    The Quantum Computer: Currently being rebuilt. Will allow Angels in the city to see through the Mask. Operates by exploiting connections between Fate, the Wyrd, and probabilistic quantum events. The previous computer was sabotaged when a team of Changelings uploaded some magical malware.
    The Network Analyst: Sifts through all the electronic communications in the area to try to find who's been tampering with the Infrastructure. Virtue: Vigilant. Vice: Cautious.
    The Bait: Set of Angels who are invisible to mortals, but have been given a fae Mein and attempt to provoke responses. Anyone who reacts to the sight of a bait angel is flagged and observed.

    The Book of Hidden Mechanisms: Fae Token containing information about the God Machine. Could use ideas on how it works as an item, possibly as a hybrid of Token and Gadget.

    When a Changeling or Hobgoblin is positively identified, the God Machine may send an Angel to recruit them as a mole through blackmail or bribery, with the goal of ferreting out the saboteurs. Meanwhile, the saboteurs are going to be calling in all their favors and recruiting from the Freehold to thwart the efforts.

    So, any suggestions, ideas, etc?

      If you are coming at this from a changeling perspective you might find it interesting that the hedge is the one realm that the god machine has tried and failed to invade.       For your thoughts on the god machine taking notice of the fey I might suggest adapting the surveilled, hunted and other cover comprimise conditions to help represent the god machine's focus.

      Who knows, maybe it's trying to solve how it can't expand into the hedge or maybe it has other plots in motion. Maybe it is even iron incarnate but that part if far more a changeling conspiracy theory that could be fun to explore.

          Oh, yeah, I need to take another look over those conditions, again. The thought occurred to me years ago, so thanks for reminding me of them. Here's a few condition ideas that come to mind:

          Prime Suspect (Persistent)
          The Panopticon has labeled you as one of the fae saboteurs. All communications and mentions of your name and aliases are examined at top priority. You will be watched so the God Machine can assess possible co-conspirators and sabotage methods.

          Causes: Intercepted communications including specific details about the God Machine's active facilities.
          Beat: The God Machine sends an angel to confront you.
          Resolution: Fake your death, flee Buena Vista, or otherwise convince the Panopticon that you're no longer around.

          Flagged (Persistent)
          You've been identified as a possible fae creature and are being monitored with increased scrutiny.

          Causes: Excessive "improbable" results from subtler supernatural abilities.
          Beat: You suffer some manner of setback or loss of face in order to avoid the Panopticon's surveillance.
          Resolution: Confirm the God Machine's suspicions, upgrading this to Tagged or avoid further suspicious activities for a month to clear the condition.

          Tagged (Persistent)
          You've been confirmed as a supernatural creature and are being monitored at a heightened priority. The Panopticon compiles a dossier on you.

          Causes: Use of overt supernatural abilities while under surveillance, suspicious activity while flagged.
          Beat: The Panopticon uses your information against you, inflicting conditions such as Leveraged, Embarrassing Secret, Notoriety, etc.
          Resolution: Fake your death, flee Buena Vista, or otherwise convince the Panopticon that you're no longer around.

                      Depending on which books for demon you have it might be good to take a look at the sections that explore changeling impressions of demons. I know the ST guide gets into it pretty good.

                                  The best thing I can suggest for infrastructure itself is consider what type of infrastructure it is, what then is the output of that infrastructure, what are the elements that enable the occult matrix (4 details is a good benchmark here) and lastly what support does this infrastructure have ie does it have concealment infrastructure hiding it, defence infrastructure dedicated to it, cultists or even an angel directly staffing it?

                                  The things that fuel occult matrixes can be literal, people greasing the wheels, or more detached like someone folding 1,000 paper cranes. To give players a hint that such is connected though it should share some theme or metaphorical connection. While players won't know what or how it is going to produce the output one looking for it can see a connection they otherwise would miss without paying attention. I often would suggest almost thinking of resonance (the shadow) and how the machine could hijack this potential energy if its easier to imagine (I have a head canon that the machine works on this principle but that is a headcanon)

                                  One thing you'll have to work out for changelings to interact is how are they seeing past the concealment infrastructure? Are they a gap in the design? is it a flaw? or maybe it is linked to why the GM seems to be after them.

                                                      So, I'm working on some angels.

                                                      Johnny, AKA "Legionnaire"

                                                      A mass-produced Rank 1 angel. Tends to hang out with violent types, encouraging bullying of people who don't fit in. Essentially uses human bullies as a means of detecting odd behavior in hopes of tagging and/or harassing possible Changelings. Underneath its Mortal Mask, it's a mannequin of brass and porcelain, with the flaw being that each one has the same body type and underlying ceramic skeleton and too-smooth features. This flaw is actually calibrated to provoke responses from sufficiently observant people. Overly fond of the "NPC meme" as a means of dehumanizing victims. Could use advice for designing the facility that manufactures these angels. Have a recon mission in mind, or a Summer court raid.
                                                      Ban: Must assert individuality despite being programmed toward conformity, possibly turning toward reckless violence if continuously pressed.
                                                      Bane: Exposed bone.

                                                      Gun Bug, Imperative
                                                      Fetters to individuals and uses the Implant Mission Numen to cause murders that fuel occult matrices or take out "soft targets." Use of these is typically spread out enough to be drowned out by all the other crime in the city.
                                                      Ban: Forcibly manifests and is paralyzed if exposed to magnetometer (metal detector). The Autumn Court has developed an extraction device around this Ban.
                                                      Bane: ?

                                                      The Waffle House, my group's in-joke as a Lare.
                                                      Weird people gather at Waffle House. One in Buena Vista was formerly an entrance to a God Machine facility that was abandoned, leaving only an empty Bolthole. It desires to remain useful, and tends to encourage clandestine exchanges by its patrons.The waitress, Agnes, has a ban where she must store or retrieve any item requested from the "back room" when asked, as well as "the special brew" of one-use containers of Essence.
