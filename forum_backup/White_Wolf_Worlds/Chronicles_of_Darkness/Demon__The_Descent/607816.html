                                  Originally posted by Xenesis View Post
                                  More to the point, pacts need to be signed in blood.

                                  Getting blood out of a ghost may prove difficult.
                                  All Pacts require a physical medium for the terms of agreement. Paper, leather, stone, bones, bronze, etc. Once the Pact is sealed, the object containing the terms gets extra Durability to make it more resistant against deliberate harming and erosion. I am pretty sure digital Pacts are not allowed by the core rule book. Willpower Dots have to be sacrificed. You cannot use supernatural powers to force someone into signing. That would be you that's signing with their severed hand, so to say. Torture and blackmail are A-OK, as far as Demons and other spies are concerned.

                                  A Soul Pact requires all of that AND that the ink be made out of blood from both parties involved. Ghosts, as a literary creature, are not the people they were, in every way that can be interpreted. NWOD Spirits were never people to begin with, and as a rule, they don't have any unique advantages that you could steal by impersonating them. You are better off just bribing ghosts and spirits with the things you know they want the most so they'll risk non-life and Corpus to do your bidding. And if they feel like complaining about the unsatisfying resolution of the mundane deal, well...

                                  "I did as we agreed, both to the letter and *quick chuckle* spirit. If you wanted more details, you should have asked. You can Blast me if you want, but you'd be wasting your Essence. You would also look none too elegant if your Choir thinks you are becoming a Revenge magath."

                                  (I wanna make that a quote for a snarky Unchained diplomat now.)

                                      Depends on the kind of human connection they're trading; spirits can grow cults, which can then be traded away. And ghosts don't need human connections, they need anchors. A ghost driven by revenge or unfinished business could bargain away his friends at the golf course if it means avenging his death. Also, not all pacts need to involve the demon getting a Cover.

                                      Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                                      I am pretty sure digital Pacts are not allowed by the core rule book.
                                      Ish? The pact must be written on something that's fairly permanent. And digital is generally not permanent. Plus, the contract needs to be signed by their own hands, which is hard on a computer. That said, if we inscribe the pact digitally onto a CD, the ST might rule that original CD counts as the pact, even though its in a digital format.

                                      Okay, that's admittedly a stretch.

                                      Willpower Dots have to be sacrificed.
                                      Only for soul pacts. For others, its only temporary willpower points.

                                      Ghosts, as a literary creature, are not the people they were, in every way that can be interpreted. NWOD Spirits were never people to begin with, and as a rule, they don't have any unique advantages that you could steal by impersonating them.

There are some that will have something.

                                      Unless you don't want to risk said ghost or spirit being caught by the God Machine for various reasons. Or you want to keep your plans as secret as possible. Etc
