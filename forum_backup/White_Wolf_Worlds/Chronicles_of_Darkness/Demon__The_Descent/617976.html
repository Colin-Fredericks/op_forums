                              If I'll ever have the chance of ST a Demon game, my plans for the 4 Archangels is making each of them as a "prototype" for one of the God Machine's angels- Michael is a Sword, Raphael a Shield, Gabriel a Trumpet and Uriel a Wheel. Their patterns are used to make "lesser" versions of them- so if one of them is destroyed (or worse, Fall) the God Machine would lose the ability to make angels of their type.

                              Lucifer would be the first Fallen, of course, in which tried to rebel against the Machine but was struck down by Michael and literally fell to Hell (or Inferno). Now, he turned into some sort of a "radio station" which broadcast the "devil signal" in order to lure more angels to Fall and become demons. Samael, on the other hand, would be a some sort of a virus- a personality of an angel which was meant to be erased, yet managed to survive by infecting other angels and using their bodies as hosts (some sort of Agent Smith). Its goal is to infect the whole Heavenly Host until it would reach the Command and Control Infrastructure of the Machine and take over it. Metatron was the first (and perhaps, the only) Integrator to succeed and reestablish a connection with the Machine on its own terms, rising to the level of an Archangel and maintaining most of its independents, something which turned him into Michael's rival at Heaven.

                              Still thinking about ideas for Azazel and other angels/demons I am fond of.

                                                          Originally posted by malonkey1 View Post
                                                          Speaking of Inferno, could an Angel be corrupted into a Demon of the Inferno, like Ghosts and Spirits?
                                                          A full angel? Probably not - angels, by nature, try to be detached, while the Inferno really gets into the sins of humanity. An angel would Fall before they'd become corrupt enough to be infernally tainted. At which point, its harder to taint a physical being as opposed to an ephemeral one. Even if the angels did become tainted, the G-M would recall them and fix the issue.

                                                          An Exile? Now THAT is very much a strong possibility. They've very likely lost that angelic detachment, they've lost the backing of the G-M, they are without purpose. They're ripe for corruption.*
