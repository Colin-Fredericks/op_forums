    A More Traditional Demon Hack

    04-22-2016, 09:20 PM
    There is, about, a million of these I assume... Anyways, here is a google docs link.

    Anyways, I'm going to start off by saying I love Demon: the Descent, but I also loved Demon: the Fallen. That's where this unholy abomination comes from. (this is a compliment when we talk about demons!) My goal was to keep the basic chassis of Demon: the Descent, but ADD pieces of Demon: the Fallen. In the end, I think it adds a distinct flavor and new dimension to the game. One of the things I wanted to keep was the cold war, spy game, between the Elohim, and the demons. I do like the God-Machine, and have played.
    One of the things that went out the window is the Descent, and the Descent's concept of Hell. Hell is a place, it's called the Pit (known in crossoverland as the Lower Depths) and it's not a nice place to be. Demons rule there, but they constantly fight and the very nature of the place is inimical to the fallen angels, it is simply Hell for everyone involved. The Infernal Courts, and the demons who merge with human's to escape, and run from the God-Machine are trying to make a place for themselves on Earth, whether this is starting a new war with the God-Machine, or helping humanity (who they Fell for), or whatever personal goal you can think of.

    Now, the Fallen are supposed to be up-and-front better than the Unchained, but they trade that in for some more weaknesses, like a weakness to abjuration, and faith as a weapon. Torment is also a thing. I've, so far, decided against Tormented Lores and Evocations, if only because the amount of worked that goes into adding them for each Embed and Exploit.

      I have been thinking about Cover in relation to this hack. Currently, demons possess a person, subsume them into themselves, and must keep the Elohim off their trail. They can't switch Covers as easily, and can only ever hold one. They can make Soul Pacts, giving them an in-road to subsume and possess the person. (Mechanically, when Dispossessed, the Soul Pact is a beacon that speeds you onward. Then you possess them, and become them, per usual for Demon: the Descent). Unlike the Unchained, and death isn't the end. You can lose your Cover by dying, when the Host is dead, demons are in their Twilight form, and are pulled back to the Pit if they linger too long.

      This does bring up some things. What do angels do? Angels possessing people isn't totally out of genre (looking at you Supernatural) , so this is a workable option. However, when Jacob wrested an angel, the angel wasn't a possessed person. It WAS the angel in the form of a human. Classic Cover. When Abraham was visited by angels, they walked in the skin of a man. But it wasn't possession.

      This got me thinking. What if demons can have both? Generally angels are cloaked in the flesh of a human. This isn't possession (usually), but something the Metatron does. Which allows angel jacking to still be a thing. Now can demons do this? Well.. yes. Kind of. The first Cover for a demon is a person that they possess. (Newly Fallen angels possess their old Cover). However patch jobs is still a perfectly acceptable pact option. Which allows Patchwork covers to be. Bits and pieces of people assembled, and a new human form manifested, that the demonic spirit subsumes, and makes part of them. Ta da, you made a person to possess! And in a way, this may be the most ethical way to stay in the world. It sounds good in my head, but... you know, that could just be me. What happens when a Fallen is Burned though? Usually a Cover degrades in it's believably until it just... poof, gone. How does that work with possessing someone? (Well, I use the word subsume a lot because the demon and human merge on a physical and spiritual level.)

      Which brings me too... How does low cover work for a possessing demon? My thought was to give them mainly physical Taints (glitches). Now Torment ALSO gives Taints, so shunting Taints over to Torment, and having Cover rating basically dictate G-M Conditions and being Hunted is a possibility. But how would that work? Transient Taints (Glitches) could come from (optionally) succeeding on a Torment roll. And a dramatic failure on a Torment roll. Or, you could gain them the usual way, AND for Torment rolls. It means the Fallen who is careful can shred his host with Taints. This makes sense. In that case, being Burned could be, well, the ultimate Taint, where the Host is destroyed. I'm not sure I like that, because it would mean a low Torment, low Cover Fallen is still Tainted. What is the mechanism Tainting them? The god-Machine's manipulation of the world causing friction to the demon? Hm...



      Edit:

      Also, thoughts on Duty and Urge. Right now, Duty has some overlap with Incarnation, which is not a bad thing, mind, but I could see an argument for going with Virtue and Vice instead. Thoughts?

      Tangentially, what's the protocol for double posting in your own thread? Not a lot of replies, but I like to include my thoughts in case anyone finds them helpful. Should I just continually edit my post?

          Here's my latest rendition of the concept. Lemme know if you find it useful.

          Demons are things that many people come to believe exist, in one shape or form. Ancient horrors from a realm of pure chaos and torment, wicked spirits that crave forbidden acts of violence and depravity, people with black hearts and no sense of moral compass—these things and more are what people think of when they hear the word demon. But some wonder: if a creature like that of a demon can be evil, is it possible then that such a being can be drawn onto the path of enlightenment? How can there be evil demons if there are not good ones to define the former?

          Fiends of Abaddon is a shard shaped around these questions, and how even a sinner can become a saint if he walks the path of heroes. They are constantly tempted to give into their infernal nature, it having been all they’ve ever known. The Unchained are demons who have entered our world from the realm of Abaddon, the Yawning Pit, masquerading in the bodies of those with weak souls. Unlike a normal possession, however, the magic that is used to bind demons permanently to their vessel demands them to consume the corrupt soul. In doing so, they become… different. Even if the soul belonged to a morally bankrupt individual, a single soul holds the potential for something greater. Thus, the demon in question gains a semblance of morality, and the chance to change itself rather than be forced to remain as it has for perhaps entire eons.

          In this setting, demons were born in Abaddon (a place everybody wants to leave). To free themselves of the Maw, they subsume the essence of a mortal, a spirit, or a ghost, which acts like an anchor (and represents your starting Cover), allowing them to traverse the mortal realm under the guise of something that belongs. In short: you choose between having the body of a mortal, a spirit, or a ghost.

          Regarding Angels
          When a demon would normally roll "compromise", rather than warn hunter-angels of their presence, reality itself attempts to harass them. Nearby objects subtly impede them, mortal allies betray their secrets, or in the most blatant of compromises, an avatar appears to confront the interloper: an angel. They never speak of who they serve, or to what end, only that they were summoned to eliminate the Unchained.

          Rules
          1. Use DtD Template
          2. Replace Cover with Torment as your morality stat (which starts at 7). This represents how the infernal spirit has merged with the victim's soul, and thus has the capacity for sympathy and virtue.
          3. If the demon dies, he loses a permanent DOT of Torment out of 10 (which becomes 9 and so on), and must then find a new host. This represents the demon having to maintain their sanity after consuming yet another soul, each having conflicting personalities and memories. This means a given demon has a maximum of 10 "lives" before the Void claims him utterly.
          4. Agendas are replaced by Factions (Cryptics, Faustians, Raveners, Reconcilers, Unaligned).
          5. Incarnations remain the same, but with different names (see below).
          6. Embeds & Exploits remain, as does the Cipher.
          7. Primum is called Testament, but otherwise no mechanical change.
          8. Aether = soulstuff, and can be drawn from mortals via Thralls.
          9. Glitches become "Defects".
          10. Cipher (read: "Malapraxis"), is what Unchained believe holds the key to infernal apotheosis (and by extension freedom from the Pit).
          11. Hosts do no age. They have an indefinite lifespan, and are immune to mundane disease (though can become carriers). They are still susceptible to injury and death by bleeding out.
          12. Players can choose to start with a mortal, ghost, or spirit as their Host. With the latter two options, the Demon can choose the original appearance, or their Demonic Form without fear of Compromise. The visual is purely cosmetic, however, and you'll still need to partially transform in order to utilize any abilities listed under said-Demonic Form. More details under Ephemeral Cover (DtD Core pg. 164).

          --Deceptors (aka Goetics) are those who merged with the soul of a living creature.
          --Immundi are those who merged with Spirits. At first, this may seem impossible. Spirits do not have souls, as mortals understand it. However, the Essence that certain spirits acquire, specifically from the actions or presence of entities WITH souls, can bypass this restriction. Spirits of emotion, animals, even abstract philosophy all count as possible targets.
          --Larvals are those who merged directly with the souls of the dead (or what remains of them). The ghost's memories, frustrations, and desires become just enough for a diabolus to remain anchored from Abaddon, as the deceased's torment becomes their torment.

          13. Loss of Torment causes the demon to feel Abaddon's pull, the Inferno projecting itself through the Unchained (via Defects) to either corrupt the surrounding area, or otherwise endanger the demon. Should the Unchained "die", they're pulled screaming into Abaddon.


          Archaic Demon Terminology
          Agency --> Dominion
          Bolthole --> Bolgia; plural: bolge
          Cipher --> Malapraxis
          Cover --> as per normal, Host, or Visage
          Cryptids --> Cacodemons, "cacklers"

          Cryptoflora --> Mandrake; plural: mandragora
          Embeds --> Whispers
          Exploits --> Evocations

          Facility --> Bastion
          Fractals --> Nephilim
          Gadget --> Talisman
          Glitch --> Fiendish Flaw (as written in HtV: Mortal Remains.)
          Imperatives --> "Imps"

          Infrastructure --> Donarium
          Lares --> Earthbound
          Lambda --> Relic
          Occult-Matrix --> Great Work
          Offspring --> Cambion; demon-blooded

          Modifications --> Mutations (ie, modifications of the default genetics, etc).
          Technologies --> Vestments
          Processes --> Affinities
          Propulsion --> Exaltations

          Agendas (Factions)
          Inquisitors --> Cryptics
          Integrators --> Reconcilers
          Saboteurs --> Raveners (or Ravagers)
          Tempters --> Faustians

          Incarnations --> Manifestations (as written in Inferno)
          Analyst --> Nemesis
          Destroyer --> Slayer
          Guardian --> Defiler
          Messenger --> Tempter
          Psychopomp --> Subversive
