                    For those interested, this is my homebrew rules for Possession and demons without a host.
                    Dispossessed
                    When a demon's host is mortally wounded and they are unable to heal it, their spirit becomes untethered and manifests as their Apocalyptic Form above the body. The pain of death is only made worse by the Abyss, which they can feel pulling at them. They immediately lose one dot of Integrity, and gain a point of Torment as if they had failed a degeneration roll. While dispossessed, the Fallen has the traits of a ghost, with Power, Finesse, and Resistance attributes equal to the highest Attribute from their Power, Finesse or Resistance trait. Devils, Devourers, and Slayers have one higher Power, Fiends and Defilers have one higher Finesse, and Scourges and Malefactors have one higher Resistance. Regardless, they are unable to touch the material world and cannot use their Lores, but also cannot be affected by anything other than blessed items or powers specifically designed to affect spirits.
                    Obtaining a New Host
                    The demon cannot sustain themselves as pure spirits for long. Each minute they are without a host, the demon suffers a point of lethal damage. They may use what Aether they have to heal, but cannot ravage thralls or otherwise gain Aether. Once they take their Corpus in lethal or aggravated damage, the demon falls back into the Abyss. In order to avoid this fate, the demon must find a new suitable host—one that is too spiritually weak or mentally dead to struggle—and possess them quickly or fall back into the Abyss.
                    A Finesse + Resistance roll can be made to sense a suitable host within a number of miles equal to their Exousia, with whatever penalty the ST feels is appropriate to find someone suitable in the area. If successful, the demon must travel towards them and attempt possession. Upon reaching a target, the demon can claim the body with a Power + Finesse roll; anyone who might conceivably be able to resist the possession attempt cannot be targeted by the demon. If they fail, they may try again, but at a cumulative -2 penalty. If the victim is a thrall that willingly accepts the possession, no roll is necessary.
                    Possessing a new body is a traumatic experience for the Fallen. Their memories and personality are once again melded with that of the host, often leading to crises of identity as priorities and beliefs shift. Pacts may break and even knowledge of Lores forgotten. The mortal world naturally rejects any connection between the Fallen's old persona and their new one, leading to the loss of Community and other Merits. ST and player are advised to stat the Fallen as a brand new character with the same amount of experience allocated to reflect the skill set and background of the new host, but keeping the demon's (modified) Exousia, Integrity, and Torment.
                    Becoming Earthbound
                    If a suitable host cannot be found in time, the demon has one last shot: become one of the earthbound by binding their spirit to an object. No ordinary item will do, however; any would-be reliquary must resonant with humanity such that it could be considered unique or particularly special, and it must be discreet, not a part of some large object. The demon makes a Power + Finesse roll to attune the item to them, but they suffer a -3 penalty if it lacks mystical power or was not created to be a reliquary, and an additional -2 penalty if the object does not resonate with their House. Only one attempt may be made per object.
                    Once bound to an item, the Fallen is no longer pulled by the Abyss but also cannot leave their reliquary. They maintain the attributes they had as a dispossessed spirit and can manifest in their Apocalyptic Form by making a successful Power + Resistance roll, but they cannot move more than their Exousia in yards from their reliquary. Should the reliquary be destroyed the demon is freed, but they suffer the effects of having a host die and must once again seek sanctuary from the Abyss.
                    ------------

                    Exousia is what I call perm. Faith in my rules. Easy to substitute the names. Basically, they are ephermeral without any Manifestations or Numina.
