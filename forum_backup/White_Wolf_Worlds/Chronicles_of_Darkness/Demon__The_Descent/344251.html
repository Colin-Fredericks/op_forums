    Playing a Demon in nWoD Crossover

    12-18-2014, 08:32 PM

    My question is that I don't think my GM will want to bother with cover, and the God-Machine hunting me. Is is worth playing a Demon if you don't use cover, and what would you change to implement that? This would also be my first time playing one.

      What fun is it to be a spy if you can't don your mask? The ability to change your life like one changes it's clothes is the essence of cover. It can add a great depth to a demon and is their way of exploring what they have become. If anything think of it as a range of aliases that are more than just forged paperwork. You would have your main (which you could change) and a variety of burners as you grow in power to add a layer of protection. So lets say you get dragged into robbing a bank by those who know you are a demon, you don't have to risk yourself and can do the task as Burner Bob. This may side step some things but being a being of many faces is a very demonic theme if nothing more than the potential of. Also if you do not bother with cover how will you go about demon form and all the other concepts that revolve around a demons mask?


      On the fact of god machine not hunting you a lot of the powers risk compromise of cover. Sure it not actively hunting you, can be seen but what is to stop you from being far too blatant if no one will come hunting you? Even if none of the other players need to worry about the god machine a demon of the machine should always be looking over their shoulder. Not saying you have to go this way just that in my opinion it is a strong aspect of the theme of demon. (if god machine simply is not present then that raises the question of where you came from)

      If nothing else maybe take a look at infernal. Both share some demonic themes but infernal has less of the spy theme to it if that isn't what you are looking for. Either way just remember to have fun

          Cover is really important in Demon. I even would dare to say this is what Demon: The Descent is all about.

          The Cover identity is your mask in an otherwise hostile world. Hostile to Demons in particular, that is. Use your demonic powers too freely, manifest in your demonic form or otherwise risk compromises to your cover and they will come after you.

          Your storyteller doesn't need the God Machine to emphasize this. If your cover crumbles and glitches manifest, he could start with sending mortal demon hunters after you. Or some Mages who want to add a demon heart to their collection of artifacts. If your ST uses (non-god machine) Angels, those might come knocking on your door someday, too.

          Also, the ability to get covers through soul-pacts is one of the most thrilling aspects of Demon: The Descent. Provides for great scenes and some real Demon experience.

          Story-, balance- and mood-wise a lot would be lost in my humble opinion if Cover (and therefore soul-pacts, too) are not used.

                  As far as the Cover goes... alright, there's this comic from awhile back, JLA Year One. Basically detailing the first year of the Justice League of America after they first came together: Flash, Green Lantern, Aquaman, Black Canary and the Martian Manhunter. One of the subplots of the story was the team gradually getting to know and trust each other in the midst of a growing conspiracy, and at one point the rest of the team turns on the Martian Manhunter as a suspected spy and traitor after they discover that he's been using his telepathy and shapeshifting powers to assume various human guises, observing his teammates in their civilian secret identities.

                  As the Demon in the group, you're the Martian Manhunter in this scenario. You're not human, just pretending to be, and you're not sure yet if you can trust these other people or things or whatever they are. Your Cover(s) afford you an opportunity to get another perspective on your fellow PCs.

                          If you are looking to run a Demon without heavy G-M intervention you could retool Cover breach around Glitches. Do something like this:

                          Whenever the Demon fails a Cover check she either gains a minor Glitch, or upgrades an existing Glitch by one level (Storyteller's Choice). Curing Glitches is achieved by Soul Pacts. Each dot of Cover gained from a pact downgrades a Glitch by one level or removes completely a minor Glitch (Players Choice). Brand new Covers begin with no Glitches at all. Under this hack different Covers may have different glitches, at different severities.
