    Demons, Partitioning Memory and Going Native

    08-18-2014, 03:17 AM
    Hey everyone, Nyx here with an opinion question. I currently run a crossover game, (8 players at the moment) and everyone is starting out as human. This posed a problem, because two of my players really wanted to play as Demons. I gave them my approval, and they asked how I intended to make a human into a Demon. I was at first unsure of an answer, but refused to admit as much, and assuaged their worries with a knowing smile and promise to surprise them. After a lot of thought on this issue, an idea hit me. Much like President Zaphod Beeblebrox, the characters have edited their own memories, hiding their supernatural capabilities and memories from themselves while they live full time in a cover. Now, there are a myriad of reasons they might have desired to do this, but, more important than the why of it is how possible it is to do it.

    So, that brings me to the question(s) at hand: does this sound at least plausible, and, how would you feel/react, as a player, if this was written into your character?

                  Do I think turning a Demon human long term is plausible? Eh, not really.

                  That said, it's six am and I don't want to go to sleep quite yet, so let's roll with it! Rambling ahead!

                  Let's set aside the who and the why for right now and focus on the how, and when it comes to unique stuff related to the God-Machine I almost always fall back on Infrastructure. Whether implemented by the God-Machine or exiles, or suborned by the Unchained themselves, it seems pretty solidly like a very unique sort of Concealment Infrastructure. If it is Infrastructure, a few questions come to mind: what form does it take? Is it linked to any other Infrastructure? What did it take to get it going in the first place and what consequences does that action have nowadays? Does it require any maintenance now, and who does it if so? What's the Linchpin, and how can you or the players take care of it when it comes time for the humans to become Unchained once again? The Infrastructure can be personalized, like an implant, or it could be oddly detached, like a building somewhere in the same city. Maybe it's tied to a section of the city that's under construction, and as more of it's renovated the Infrastructure weakens, allowing for flashes of memory, gaining the Unseen Senses: God-Machine merit temporarily, or other such things. Perhaps once the last building is demolished the Infrastructure shatters, simultaenously releasing the quantum state of their Cover and breaking whatever tracking mechanism They had in place (somewhat mirroring the Fall, actually). The last bit is assuming that the PCs aren't the ones behind it in the first place.

                  (Side note: you could probably achieve the implant version as a rare Lambda Gadget that simultaneously keeps them human, wipes their memory and allow those who implanted it to keep track of them. Or you could keep the human and memory effects in one Gadget and the tracking in a different one, allowing the new Demons to have to figure out how They're still finding them, again assuming that the Demons themselves aren't Them, if that makes any sense. All the effects are in published Embeds, so it's not out of the realm of possibility. Remember to chip your pets and rogue demons!)

                  Speaking of Them, I think the idea that the PCs were the ones who wanted to become human and lose their memory and did so under their own power...isn't very plausible at all. A few other options that immediately come to mind.

                  1. The God-Machine or other Loyalist forces. Easiest to justify how it's done, strangely hard to explain why it was done instead of the demons being forcibly debriefed and recycled or frozen in a Facility for a rainy day.

                  2. Other Unchained. The idea that the PCs had information that was too damaging to let them roam free but too valuable to discard entirely rings fairly true, and it's not too much of a logical leap to say that an easy way to keep that information in check and completely under the God-Machine's radar is to make them a mindwiped muggle. This opens up the idea of other Unchained "handlers" lurking in the shadows, which is a bonus!

                  3. The PCs, via a deal they made with Exiles. Doesn't make much more sense than if the PCs were able to do it themselves, but marginally more interesting and with potential hooks.

                  These are, of course, only options that deal exclusively with the God-Machine. Depending on how crossover heavy your games, there could be entirely different reasons for who, how, and why these Unchained were decommissioned! Most of them aren't possible by canon but hey, this entire exercise is about playing fast and loose with those assumptions. Perhaps they were blessed (or cursed!) by a god of the Shadow, or maybe they made a bargain with one of the True Fae. Maybe the Principle has a plan and via quashilim and Pyros shenanigans the Unchained were forced through a temporary version of the Pilgrimage, or maybe someone dragged them down to that weird ocean in the Underworld and stuck them underwater until both they and reality forgot they were demons! Maybe we break the glass and reach for that always-so-easy answer of "a wizard, er, archmage did it" - why? For who? What was the Quintessence that it took to perform the spell, and what did it cost to get the favor in the first place? Whoever did it, even if it was the PCs, two questions have to be answered: what was so important that they needed to be kept alive while also being contained in a very convoluted manner? If it was the PCs, what was so heinous that their memories and their basic natures had to be changed?

                  Okay, that's all for now. Maybe more rambling later.
