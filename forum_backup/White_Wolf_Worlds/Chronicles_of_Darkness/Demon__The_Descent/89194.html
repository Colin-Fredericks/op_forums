    Exploit: Solitary Confinement - Neeeeed Help!

    03-11-2014, 11:03 PM
    A vampire in my group who has a ghoul NPC played by the Story Teller/ (Co-story teller depending on who's turn it is ) This Ghoul was sucked into confinement for 10 days and Ended the chapter, giving experience. The game is over and we had the player roll for her ghoul and he has made the meditation check to not go cuckoo bananas, but the story teller said that he would die after 3 days without water. The player then asked if she could take the True Friend Merit so that the ST couldnt kill the ghoul. ( to which the GM replied no ) Would there be other ways to keep this ghoul alive? Would he actually starve to death in this supernatural rift? Options?



    ""The demon gestures at a target and opens a rift in reality — a black pit of nothing where the person
    remains trapped. The oubliette doesn’t keep a victim incarcerated indefinitely, but it creates a place of
    perfect sensory deprivation. The victim cannot hear, see or feel anything, even sound or sensation that
    he creates. Even if the target only remains in the oubliette for a matter of minutes, the experience saps
    his will and can drive him mad.

    Success: The target falls into the oubliette. An observer sees the demon point or glare at the target, and
    then the target vanish as though pulled backward by a powerful force. While in the void, the target
    experiences no sensory input — even if the target bites his own hand, he feels nothing. Even if he
    screams, everything around him is silent. This is, of course, extremely disconcerting; the target
    experiences a breaking point (p. XX) every hour he remains confined. The Storyteller should apply a
    negative modifier to the roll of at least one die, possibly more if the character has a special reason to
    fear the dark, sensory deprivation, kidnapping, etc. The target remains trapped for a number of hours
    equal to the demon’s Primum, though the demon can cancel the effect earlier if she wishes ""

      Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
      The game is over and we had the player roll for her ghoul and he has made the meditation check to not go cuckoo bananas, but the story teller said that he would die after 3 days without water
      The Storyteller's full of shit, bluntly put:

      Originally posted by World of Darkness Core Rulebook, pg175, "Deprivation"
      Your character can go a number of days equal to his Stamina before being inhibited by lack of water. Once that threshold is exceeded, he suffers one point of bashing damage for each day that passes. He can go without food for a number of days equal to his Stamina + Resolve. After that point, he suffers another point of bashing damage a day. Denied both food and water, he suffers two bashing wounds a day.
      Unless the ghoul was already severely deprived and severely damaged, three days without water would not kill them, even before getting into whether Solitary Confinement applies any physical effects in light of how Unchained magic usually works with regard to time and biology and the like - Boltholes being the operative comparison.

      Considering that being trapped for ten days requires a) an exceptional success and b) a Primum of 10, I have to ask what the devil is going on in this game and whether the ST doesn't just want the NPC to die but is inexplicably neglecting the power of ST fiat.

          I don't quite understand how the ghoul was affected for 10 *days* by an Exploit whose effect is listed in the rules as lasting for a number of *hours* equal to the demon's Primum, but I suppose that it could have been Storyteller's fiat in the interest of telling a particular tale. There doesn't appear to be anything in the rules that would indicate that a person trapped within would be subject to hunger or thirst (which could be an oversight due to it very rarely being relevant with a power that lasts for mere hours rather than days), but I would personally feel that the need for food, water, etc would be held at bay by supernatural means. After all, there's no mention in the text of being without air, implying that there is at least SOME level of life-support in the rift.

          That said, it sounds to me like your Storyteller just wants to get rid of this particular ghoul for some reason.

                  A-hole Storyteller here to give my side...

                  a) The demon was so powerful, as some have noticed, because the group is all sitting at 30-35+ XP each, some even more than that. I had them go up against a Primum 6 demon with a number of cultists, and the demon was dead within the first move of combat. Just trying to give them a good challenge. I may have overdone it, I admit, but that is simply a flaw in my attempts to learn the balance of combat, and not anything malicious.

                  b) The reason the ghoul was trapped for 10 days was because the demon got an incredibly lucky roll and the ghoul got an incredibly unlucky one. It was a fluke, but he was pulled into solitary confinement to help with the battle, as he is really powerful and the demon was aware of this and wanted to remove him as a threat. My intention was not to trap him for a week and a half, just remove him from that combat. But hey, stuff happens, and sometimes a really unlucky roll can be the end for someone.

                  c) He is NOT a NPC. He has been a consistent, played character for 3 campaigns, with the same consideration and XP rewards that the rest of the group gets. He goes well beyond the confines of the Retainer merit he was purchased with. He's a PC. That seems pretty obvious to me.

                  d) True Friend says the ST can't kill that character as part of the "plot." A character dying due to involving himself in combat is not plot in the sense I believe they are thinking of. Plot would be me killing him as a device in the story, outside of player control. And even if it were, then giving True Friend to a player character makes them unkillable, which is absurd. As I state to you, Vitalis, then all the players could just take True Friend for each other, and there would no longer be any risk of death. I'm not saying all PCs should be easily killed, but there should be a risk.

                  e) I can see the argument that the Solitary Confinement could work like a Bolthole and negate the need for food. I had not considered that and had Vitalis brought it up when we talked about this earlier, I likely would have agreed. It's a valid point.
