                                                            Originally posted by Darinas View Post
                                                            When a Demon takes the Claws and Fangs Demonic traits, do they use it with Brawl, Weaponry, or either one and they need to pick at character creation? The description in the book is not clear about that. My initial assumption was that it was Brawl, but they do not state such and in fact refer to the claws as counting as "small weapons"
                                                            It's Brawl. Weaponry is the Skill for weapons that aren't used with direct extensions of normal violent/aggressive/suppressive motions; "Bite" is listed in the Rules Appendix as a maneuver under Unarmed Combat, Demon Core was one of the first books to use the Revised Storytelling System's assumption that weapons deal extra damage rather than adding to an attacker's dicepool, and Claws and Fangs is not unique in calling its source of lethal damage "small weapons" (Spurs does the same thing) and never actually specifying a Skill, but the implications of having literal Claws and Fangs are different than the implications of Blade Hand's "you have a weapon that you would need to be literally disarmed to be deprived of and it works with all your weapon-based Fighting Merits."
