                        Originally posted by firestar001 View Post
                        Can a demon have an interlock out of the same two embeds they have for an exploit?

                        ex: Hellfire uses check backdrop and merciless gunman. Can a demon also have an interlock using both of those? Ex: pull some X-ray trick shot assassination kinda stuff.
                        I feel like I'm not understanding the question, because an Interlock can use any two Keys as a basis, and the Embed you use as the prerequisite for an Exploit can be any Embed you can make a convincing case for allowing the character to learn that Exploit; there being two Example Prerequisite Embeds for each published Exploit and Interlocks using two Embeds as Keys is a coincidence with no mechanical relation to each other.

                        tl;dr: Yes, they can.

                        and can an interlock replicate the abilities of merits? Like Fulcrum Point + Never Here = Telekinesis?
                        With the caveats that any Interlock you devise should probably keep the design notes from the corebook and Storyteller's Guide in mind and that directly porting a Supernatural Merit as an Interlock without adjustment to fit the character may result in an awkward time fitting it into the story, sure. (Though you would probably want to not use the Telekinesis Merit wholesale anyway, because that Merit's dicepool includes Merit dots the Cipher systems do not provide immediate guidance on.)

                                            Originally posted by firestar001 View Post
                                            Can non-demons go into splinter timelines? (Like in “How an Angel Dies” from the Demon core rule book). Like can a werewolf or Changeling go into a splinter with or without a demon?
                                            It would be very weird for them not to be able to, considering the Seattle Appendix in the corebook states that people from splinter timelines can become stigmatic and leave and that nothing about the described fractures to other timelines requires demonic powers to access (and some of them directly imply to the contrary through connection to missing person incidents).

                                            The closest thing to mechanics of this type is the sidebar "Stigmatics and Splinter Timelines" on page 259 of the corebook describing how a person from a splinter timeline being turned into a stigmatic is erased on the fifth reset of their timeline after the change, and that may be more to do with the people in splinter timelines being less real than people from the dominant timeline.
