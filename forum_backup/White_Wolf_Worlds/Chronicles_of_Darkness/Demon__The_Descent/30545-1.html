                                                    What can I say, I'm a lover of the classics...

                                                    Binding Symbol- Tells

                                                    The demon has acquired a personal symbol that influences his behavior. The behavior is commonly expressed in an inability to affect the symbol or interact with anything inside the pattern or in some cases anything directly touching it (including opening a door with the symbol carved upon it or physically touching a person wearing the symbol on their neck or as a tattoo). More unique behaviors experienced by individual demons are also possible. The symbol in question is usually made up of a series of geometric figures interlocked with one another in a pattern that is both beautiful and sinister to the eyes of mortals. The dimensions almost always have to be mathematically precise and may hold so occult significance in their numbers for the highly learned or insanely paranoid. Note that a demon can find itself trapped within such a pattern on occasion, usually by unwittingly entering an incomplete symbol and finding himself entrapped as the designer inscribes the final portion of the pattern. Escape usually necessitates making a pact that heavily favors the mortals or a clever or brutal use of embeds to indirectly destroy the pattern (a rain of blood usually does the trick...as does causing the ceiling to collapse. So long as the mathematical alignments are met, the pattern can be carved, drawn, or a physical item. Only in rare cases will this glitch have more specific requirements such as being drawn in blood or carved with tools made of some precious metal.

                                                    Minor- The symbol is unique to that Demon, and unlikely to be known or discovered by outside parties unless powerful divinations are employed or the Demon himself lets the secret slip.

                                                    Major- The symbol has manifested in several Demons over the centuries, and may appear in obscure texts. Often the Demons will have something in common with the character, making it possible to "trace" or otherwise deduce the Demon's vulnerability with enough knowledge of the Demon and painstaking research on the part of those willing to exploit the weakness.

                                                    Catastrophic- The Demon's symbol, although needing to be mathematically precise in its proportions, is otherwise well known or common knowledge. Examples include religious symbols such as crosses, pentragrams, the Seal of Solomon, and so forth.
