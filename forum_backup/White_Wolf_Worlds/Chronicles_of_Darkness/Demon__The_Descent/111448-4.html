                                                Some stuff from my notes (please pardon grammar and/or formatting):

                                                81.) Plot: Hell on Earth: It started with the ringing. Days later, a massive Aetheric surge was felt across the globe. News started to filter in that massive rifts had appeared throughout the world, and strange bio-mechanical creatures - Demons or Angels, obviously - were pouring out of them. It later becomes clear that this isn’t an (obvious) God-Machine plot and that the beings are other Unchained, or near enough, come from some other dimension or timeline splinter to make war upon the Machine and conquer this world. Unfortunately, they aren’t accepting applicants. Or acting very civil towards anyone else. Pros: the God-Machine and Its Angels are largely preoccupied, and the invaders are leaving a trove of Gadgets everywhere. Cons: you are now part of a known supernatural factor, and are associated with public enemy #1, even if you are just as much at risk from them too.

                                                82.) Plot: Countdown to the Apocalypse: A ring has suborned enough Concealment Infrastructure to enact their plan - using a Lambda (see Portal Nuke in the Lambda thread, a combination Raze Infrastructure + Behind the Curtain, as an example) to tear down all veils hiding the God-Machine’s operations in the whole region/world. Now the clock is ticking as swarms of hunter angels are tearing apart every cranny and shadow looking for the source of the attack. Will the ring stop the Saboteurs before they bring down the full attention of the Machine down upon them too… to help them reveal the Machine’s influence to the world?

                                                83.) Plot: International Man of Mystery: The ring encounters a notorious “super-spy”, which turns out to be a Demon-Blooded agent loyal to an Agency masquerading as a branch of a country’s intelligence service. Equipped with all manner of Gadgets, and skilled with a range of Embeds, the mortal’s efforts are amusing… until the spy's mission leads them into indirect conflict with the Ring and their Covers.

                                                84.) Plot: The Soul Fabricator: There is a story being passed around Free Agencies of a Demon who managed to resurrect one of their Soul Pacted Covers as a whole person again. Most consider the tale mere noise, but the Ring has been contacted by another Demon who has information regarding its existence. The Demon says they feel guilty about one of the souls they’ve taken, and wants to set things right. They’d uncovered evidence that the story was accomplished using a device, and even has hints regarding its whereabouts. The Demon is willing to hire the ring to retrieve, or at least concretely locate The Soul Fabricator for them.
                                                A.) [The Soul Fabricator may be a 2nd-order Lambda (using Decoy, Alibi, Going Native, etc.) that detaches a Cover and makes it independent. This creates a whole new human, with stats based on the Demon who formed them (since those were the Cover’s stats), and memories as determined by the Demon, but this cost the Cover (obviously) and a dot of Primum to maintain existence. Allowable covers are Soul Pacts and Patch Jobs (which create a new human), but not Facades. Long term consequences may include: the person becoming Stigmatic, the person “disappearing” as Integrity degrades (similar to Cover details), the person mutating into a Cryptid, spontaneous Soul Loss, the person developing Glitches, or unwarranted G-M attention.]
                                                B.) [The Soul Fabricator might not create a “real” person, but rather an enduring, independent Cover, subject to the same Compromise checks, Conditions, and consequences that Demons face. While not being a Demon.]
                                                C.) [The Soul Fabricator may be a piece of Infrastructure that allows for the creation of new human beings. It might be active, in which case it is either a trap or very dangerous to use, or suborned, which means the “owner” may need to be reached to get it to work.]
                                                D.) [It might only “template” a person, requiring a “base” to be sacrificed to work on.]
                                                E.) [It might need to render down the “donor” Demon in order to generate a real person.]

                                                85.) Plot: The Hellbound: Rumors of men in black are popular amongst Unchained circles. Many of them were or are responsible for the tenacious spread of this modern bugaboo. In the Ring’s neighborhood, an new addition to the MiB ecology has appeared - normal, bland looking men in suits, who nonetheless radiate Aether, and look like cyborgs when their illusion is pierced. They are causing problems for some of the Ring's contacts and plans. It gets even more complicated when another of their peers contacts them with the truth behind these “hellbound” - the Demon has found out how to apply the Hellhounds Exploit to humans, turning them into potent, loyal agents (a simple Trick of the Light gadget enables them to masquerade as normal people for long enough). The Demon will share these secrets with the Ring if they help it test out its latest creation - a Lambda that will transform everyone downtown into a loyal, Aether-hungry Hellbound.
