    What do you want out of the next Demon Supplement

    01-10-2016, 11:20 AM
    (assuming there will be one at all, I've no idea if anything has been said).

    Contracts: I know they've been covered in DtD and Players guide, but I still feel like some parts aren't greatly covered. Like the sell... or some of the nitty gritty of what happens on collect. How demons deal with contracts that aren't straight forward. How they research and approach victims. Probably just because I'm not a good sales person, I don't understand outside of embedding weird shit in legalese that no one reads how they ever get anyone to sign.

    Cults: God Machine and Demon, what do they look like? how do they behave? Actually I might be happy to have an entire book on ChroD cults, seeing as how Vampires and other things have them too.

    Religious Undertones: This kind of ties into cults, but as mentioned in a different thread, DtD doesn't always feel religious, getting into it with cults might be enough, but more advice on how to give it this feel would be good. On this note describing more of how the unchained seem like devils to people would be good. To me I can't even imagine (outside of propaganda) why they'd appear evil, nor do I see their motivation to be evil. also appearance, like why would a demon end up looking big and red with horns? I know you can do all of this, I'd just like to see more of *how*.

    what do you want to see in the next supplement?

                          Back to the original post topic:

                          More blue book support for the GMC's line. As is, the G-M is tightly tied to DtD, and given the discussion that ran in the general Chronicles of Darkness, not much anywhere else. I have the feeling that the more people are interested in GMC, the more support can be justified for Demon the Descent.

                          A cryptid book (preferably a blue book for reasons above): Go whole-hog with this: Cryptoflora, albino alligators, men in black (angels in disguise, simularca, mass hallucinations?), UFO sightings, bigfoot, loch ness, etc.

                          A Deva Corporation Sourcebook. Someone floated the idea of devoting few pages to support Deva as a Hunter conspiracy, complete with reverse engineered G-M and Demon tech. I think that's an excellent idea. Also throw in new gadgets, how the Deva corp operates, what the management factions are, what the corporate culture is like, and what the security challenges are in infiltrating it. The Deva Corporation should not be a retread of Pentex. The more plausible the Deva corporation is, the more chilling. These are people committed to a new future and a new vision of humanity. If they need to vivisect demons or harvest brain tissue from fractals, so be it, that's not any worse than outsourcing to Chinese factories where children work 16 hour days making computer chips for your smart phone.

                          A Nighthorrors book for DtD: Sleepers, stigmatics, cryptids, cults, simularca, rival demons, Angels, Exiles, Slivers and Facilities. Nighthorrors is an excellent line that has knocked it out of the park every time. Let's have one for Demon.

                          A Nighthorrors Book for Tokyo: Imagine your ring chased down the back streets of Tokyo by a vengeful Inugami, bargaining for your child's life with Kuchisake-Onna, investigating a derelict school that could well be an abandoned Infrastructure. Make it a blue book for cross-over with Hurt Locker.

                          A Book of Incarnations: A book discussing incarnations more rules hacks and resources . Here's the hook: make it for both angels and demons. New embeds and exploits for demons, new numina and incepts for angels and exiles. Incarnation specific merits, etc, etc,

                          More City settings. Specifically, city books that are the equal of Splintered City Seattle. Seattle is a science-fiction-horror wonderland: Time travel reminiscent of Chrono Trigger, corporate conspiracies and cool places like the Apocalypse Vault. The cities in the seed collection were cool, and had great ideas, but were a little sparse as a result of the limited word count.

                          Travel in the GMC. think Midnight Roads, but more aimed at planes, trains and automobiles. How do you move about in world that's monitored by biomechanical horrors? Specific advice for running a Tier-3 Agency with global responsibilities. Also a generic Airport that Storytellers can incorporate into their cities a la Tales from the 13th Precinct, with locations, a dozen alphabet soup government agencies, storyteller characters, secrets and mysteries and advice for making it a convert battleground between the Unchained and the G-M's angels.

                          (meanwhile, the devs are looking at this and thinking "He does realize that we have about a dozen game lines we need to publish for, right?")

                          Edit: A Book of Splinters, may that we can mix and match for whatever city our chronicle is based in, maybe some overlap with Dark Eras (1950s Berlins where the Nazi party managed to survive via negotiated surrender perhaps? One where the Soviets took the city completely?) Future Splinters (cyberpuk ho!), past splinters, alternate timelines etc.
