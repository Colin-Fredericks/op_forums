                        Originally posted by Aiden View Post

                        Huh. See, that is directly against my reading of Cover, and that of my players. We were all under the impression you did in fact have to spend time maintaining your Cover by doing stuff the Cover would do, because not doing it would count as a Compromise because it's out of character.

                        Reference: Age and Injury, page 274
                        Maybe I was unclear: I was saying there's not a direct relationship between time and Cover, but you're right that you do have to maintain the identity some. For example: we're talking about my Cover, Sean the accountant. Sean could plausibly not show up for work for a week, then get fired, but still be Sean, because I've kept up with other Sean-related stuff like calling the mother I bought once a week. So Sean losing his job is a thing about Sean that can change and evolve without making me seem like not-Sean.

                        Now, how much Sean-related stuff do I have to do to plausibly be Sean? That's a fuzzy line, and depends a lot on how your chronicle is structured. You might be playing a game where the demons are investigating things primarily "after hours" and we don't see much of what they do in Cover. Or you might be playing a game where a big part of the story is dealing with your relationships with humans, in which case disappearing randomly and not making your commitments is "grossly out of character" and the resulting compromises are a big worry. Or you might be playing a thriller where one particular aspect of a Cover takes center stage -- say you bought a teenage son and now you have 24 hours to rescue him from kidnappers, and not doing so would be a violation of your Cover as a previously loving parent.

                        There will be more talk about nuances in the Player's Guide.

Sure, I'll give you wealth, beauty, fame, power, adoration, talent, and whatever the heck else you want. I'm going to BECOME you anyway, so I'm really just giving myself a bunch of free merits here.
