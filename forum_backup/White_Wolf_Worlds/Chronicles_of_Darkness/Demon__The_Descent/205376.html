                      The Descent Agendas map reasonably well to four of the Fallen Factions. This much works whichever way you’re translating. If translating from Fallen to Descent, the fifth Faction (the Luciferans) is too specifically tied into the cWoD metaplot to have any parallel in nWoD: to do them justice in nWoD, you’d need to port over a Lucifer-like figure for them to be searching for.

                      I’m not sure what terminology I’d use when grafting Embeds and Exploits into Fallen. “Chapter” and “Verse” are perhaps a bit too cliché. More generally, I’m not sure how well Embeds and Exploits would work in Fallen at all; conceptually, they’re a bit too tied into the “techgnostic” flavor of Descent, given their linguistic, quantum, and similar conceptual roots.

                      The one I’m most curious about, though, is how Fallen’s Houses and Descent’s Incarnations would map to the other game. Do four of the Houses map directly to the Incarnations, much like the Factions and Agendas above? Or is it messier than that? This matters when assigning Embeds and Exploits to the Fallen, or Lores to the Unchained: who gets which types? If three Houses translate as entirely new Incarnations, does that mean we need three whole new sets of Embeds and Exploits?

                      Though frankly, if I were transplanting elements of Demon: the Fallen into nWoD terms, the Unchained aren’t who I’d go to first as recipients of those grafts. Rather, I’d target the Possessed, from Inferno. They’re a much better nWoD counterpart to the Fallen than the Unchained are. In that regard, I’d probably try to draw rough parallels between Fallen’s Houses and the Seven Deadly Sins that are the closest thing the Possessed have to x-splats.


                      My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                              Working under the assumption that we're still talking about changing the technological elements of Demon: the Descent so that they are more archaic/arcane. I felt my suggestion of Chapter and Verse wasn't terribly cliche simply because more traditional angels are sometimes thought to communicate not through language (that is verbal or somatic cues ascribed meaning by the human consensus) but by intent (that is conceptual meaning). This concept seems fairly in line with the otherwise esoteric nature of Embeds and Exploits.

                              Now if we're converting Demon: the Fallen into Demon: the Descent I think that embeds and exploits are probably best placed aside in favour of conversion of the Lores from that game. My group and I did a homebrew conversion of our own using the Mage arcana as a starting point.

                                          Infrastructure could be fluffed as "Holy Ground". There are a few ways you could then define it. You could make actual consecrated ground the only infrastructure in your game world but the problem with that is that infrastructure is assumed to be more common than that. However an easy way to resolve this issue is to suggest that any sense of genuine, selfless community or territory can cause "holy ground". Anywhere people band together to make a difference - if you're assuming that your God (minus Machine) is a positive force. Or, if you're assuming that your God (minus the Machine) is a neutral (or negative) force like the gnostic demiurge then you could suggest that He works through any sense of community that He personally has a hand in creating and so consecrates those grounds simply through their existence.
