        Originally posted by csctnfire View Post
        Can someone help me understand why Ghost/Goetic/Spirit Summons are Perfecting spells instead of Ruling?
        Ephemeral entities can detect Resonant conditions on people and objects within a certain radius. The caster is making the subject emit a signal its target entity can universally detect and is drawn to, then the signal is amplified using a Perfecting. So rather than "taking the controller away from them" and walking them to the target, they are making a very compelling beacon.

                                    Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                                    About this, you seem to imply that a Resonant condition mostly originates by causes unrelated to Ley lines.

                                    Considering the first part of my first question are Resonance and Resonant the same thing?

                                    Because Prime states Resonance in its purview, but the spell Geomancy seems only capable of manipulating Ley lines Resonance

                                    Am i missing something?
                                    Parts of the world produce Resonance, and things that match the description of one or more Influences of a spirit have the Resonant Condition tagged to that spirit.

                                    Much as Anchors can only sustain ghosts who aren't spending or losing Essence at a faster rate than one point per day, the Resonant Condition can only help a spirit stay awake and active at the basic level and spirits inhabit an ecosystem where they're constantly stealing Essence from each other. In games where Resonance is a phenomenon with trackable importance, all Essence carries Resonance that affects spirits that consume it; some of that Resonance will not necessarily wholly match the Influences of that spirit, but narratively the form and nature of a spirit is affected by and adapted to the Resonance of the places it operates in and the things that it eats — the classic example being that urban and rural specimens of spirits of the same type of animal being discernibly different.

                                    On the Werewolf end of things, it's pretty much never mentioned anywhere else, but there's a relatively early 1e supplement called Territories that includes "Essence Current/Ley Line" on its list of Territory Descriptors; an Essence Current allows the pack that controls it to funnel Essence from one locus to another, and the entire list of Territory Descriptors includes appropriate Resonances for each different type of feature.

                                    It may also be worth noting that, while it's a 1e supplement, Sanctum and Sigil has a fairly extensive discussion of ley lines, their shape and direction, and the effects of their resonance.

                                            Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                                            But considering all of that, and the fact that Goetia also uses the Resonant condition, shouldn t the Resonant condition fully be under the Prime Arcanum ONLY and Essence under Spirit?
                                            The Resonant Condition as it is most conventionally encountered is a function of the existence of spirits. Resonance as a phenomenon is broader than the Resonant Condition, but the Shadow is the main reinforcing vector for the effects of Resonance on the world, and Goetia do not typically leave the Astral in normal circumstances nor use the Resonant Condition for sustenance.

                                            Prime can affect Resonance because Resonance affects Hallows, Mana, and ley lines, but Spirit is where Resonance most fully expresses itself; there's bound to be some overlap when the Watchtower of the Golden Key draws upon the part of the soul that marks a thing as living or dead, but the Pillar of the Heart that the Watchtower of the Singing Stone empowers is one's connection to and capacity for sympathy with the wider world, while the Pillar of the Essence concerns itself with the essential nature and qualities of a thing. Outside the context of magical meddling you change the Resonance of a thing or place by changing that thing or place.

                                                    Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                                                    We imagine a park, childs plays often here, a good place.

                                                    -Case 1 (no ley line intersecting the park)
                                                    Is it possible that the park has Resonant(joy) condition?
                                                    If yes, if i understand it rightly a mage with Prime wouldn t detect anything, right ?
                                                    What spell could i use to change the condition to Resonant (dogs) ?
                                                    The park has the Resonant Condition tagged for any spirit whose Influences would describe it, which ostensibly includes spirits of joy. Prime Mage Sight would not reveal its Resonance and Sacred Geometry would not show a ley line or Node in the park.

                                                    No singular published spell allows the nature of the Resonant keyword to be changed, but Erode Resonance would allow you to remove the existing Resonant Condition and a Compelling spell might allow you to stretch the presence of a dog or doglike thing into an instance of Resonant (Dogs) that covers the whole park in similar fashion to Invoke Bane. Otherwise, spells to make the park resonate more with the concept of dogs and less with the concept of joy (materially or otherwise) are the only Awakened magical methods that suggest themselves.

                                                    -Case 2 (a ley line (joy) intersect the park)
                                                    Is it correct to assume that the park MUST have the Resonant (joy) condition?
                                                    If i use the spell Geomancy to change the Ley line Resonance to (cats) the Resonant condition would slowly change to match it?
                                                    Per Sanctum and Sigil, typically only things directly on the line are affected by its resonance, but they can carry that resonance with them if they absorb it regularly and are then moved elsewhere. The ley line will affect any park structures it touches and any of the children who spend a lot of time there, and it will almost certainly give rise to spirits who will propagate more joyful resonance, such that in practice the whole park may become Resonant with joy; likewise, if the ley line's Resonance changes to a feline one the things that its flow affects will start to take on feline qualities over time, but unless the park ceases to be a source of joy through other means than the ley line it still has a Resonant condition that supports spirits of joy.
