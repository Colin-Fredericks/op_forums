    Unchained in Manch-Vegas: Session 2, 3, and 4 Recap

    03-30-2020, 04:01 PM
    So my DtD game picked up more steam with the coronavirus situation. Online weekly games definitely help bring people together at this time.

    The 2nd session wasn't as well planned out as I wanted it to be. And when it came to our last session (4) I realized I needed to re-calibrate the story from a "Investigate this New Problem" to a more community centered investigation. Where the players set their sights on problems and so they have more time to gather resources and work on their covers, which I'll delve more in session 5 if I can.
    Session 2: The Investigation
    Liberty Griswald left to meet Alan Thorne at the Crossroads tavern. Alan couldn’t make it, but sent word ahead to a friend of his Molly. The two are able to identify that all three sleeper agents who attacked Alan’s boss attended the same philosophy class. Molly goes undercover as an auditor for the philosophy class, but Prof. Draco is paranoid enough to think that Molly is onto him.
    Draco calls class early and runs to a nearby Dunkin’ Donuts. As soon as Liberty and Molly arrive they can see the gears of the God-Machine all over the Dunkin’ Donuts. The Infrastructure’s purpose is unknown to them. Inside there is a Simulacra operating the counter and Stigmatic worker. Liberty is able to ease drop a little bit before the Stigmatic recognizes him as an interloper.
    Griswald pulls out his pistol and Merciless Gunmans the Simulacra and Stigmatic. Prof. Draco falls to the floor as Molly and Liberty grab him for interrogation.
    Session 3: The Jenga Tower
    Molly opens up a doorway to her bolthole (easy access) and chucks Prof Draco into it. Inside they managed to get a better idea of what happened. Prof Draco was brought on as an ethics consultant onto a “government subliminal training” project. He believed the students they were turning into Sleeper Agents were CIA operatives. The reason he sent them to kill Alan’s boss was because he found out that his wife was having an affair with him. They also discover that two other professors were part of the project.
    Molly and Liberty think he thinks he’s telling the truth, but Molly decides to download his memory of the last hour and modify his memory (Memory Theft). In her download she finds out that Prof Draco confessed his misuse of the Sleeper Agents to the Stigmatic who responded “It was all part of God’s plan.” Hinting that the God-Machine expected the Prof to be the fall guy for this. Molly modifies Draco’s memory to believe he shot the Simulacra and Stigmatic as part of an exchange about a wrong order.
    Liberty and Molly pretended to be a father and daughter visiting the SNHU campus. With the campus on lockdown due to the shooting they managed to get in contact with the psych professor who worked with Prof Draco, Prof Roberts. They figure out that she got a piece of infrastructure from an Angel that works in tandem with a drug. After taking part in Roberts's study she admits that there are at least 30 sleeper agents among the student population.
    Molly pockes one of the drugs and takes it to a crime lab technician friend of hers, Chelsey. Chelsey is able to identify the drug as a new street drug that’s been making its way through the homeless population. The main effect being homeless people developing fugue personalities for a short time.
    Molly and Griswald manage to track a man in a business suit passing out “free samples”. It takes them seconds to identify this guy as a Reptilian. Griswald manages to knock out the Reptilian and Molly does Mind Reading. She manages to uncover that the Reptilians are working out of a Burger King, but are working for an Angel in a Hawaiian T-Shirt and Sunglasses.

    Session 4: Start of Rebellion

    Molly, Griswald and Alan get an email from another Demon, Zechariah. They meet up at an AA meeting held at the Manchester Public Library. Though there are only three other individuals. Mr Black (A tattoo’d punk rocker), Mrs. White (An Lawyer), and Sudo (A Bookish Hacker).
    Zechariah presented a report on how their Free Agency of individuals looking out for one another has lost contact with Boston based Agencies. The only conclusion is that the God-Machine has won Boston and set its eyes on New Hampshire. All of the Demons are reporting an increase in Angel activity. Mrs. White brings up the idea that the Free Agency needs to aim their sights on fighting the God-Machine. Whereas Mr. Black proposes building an arsenal of souls and gadgets.
    A vote is held and nearly unanimously Mrs. White is nominated as leader of the new Insurgent Agency. Mr. Black decided to take his leave to start his own Temporal Agency. Mrs. White lets him go and tells the newly minted agency to look for Infrastructure and disrupt the system.
    Alan decides the best course of action is to set up a shell company in a mill building to act as a front for their Demonic mission, Reasoning Incorporated. Griswald and Molly decide to dismantle the Dunkin’ Donuts infrastructure. In the closed Dunkin’ Donuts they figure out that throwing non-donuts into the frier is the lynchpin. Keeping a safe distance they watch as the Dunkin’ reverts to a gas station and two mysterious Men in Black appear, government agents under the instruction of the God-Machine.
    Griswald uses his In My Pocket ability to produce a small tracking device. The device is similar to one of those lost key devices, but they manage to hide it on the Men in Black’s car. Now they’ll have a warning if the Men in Black get too close. With the help of Alan they are able to use the tracker to locate where the Men in Black’s HQ is. Their new plan is to infiltrate their facility and take down the Infrastructure that is supporting them. But they need to up their arsenal to pull off such a feat.
