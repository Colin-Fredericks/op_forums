                  "God is dead."

                  "Ever wonder how that statement came about? Always figured it was one of our numbers who said it. Sure, some of the humans have picked up as a more philosophical decree than anything else but they always tend to exaggerate theses things but looking at how things are now..

                  Look at the one there, see how that corpse fumbles as it walks? What is it searching for? It passed human after human laying in the street while continuing it's strange voyage. It has an aura of obsession in it's eyes. It's desperately hunting for something and is simply reduced to half remembered muscle actions and memories.

                  Ever killed a man? That is not a metaphorical question. If you have, you note the exact moment of where living person becomes a lifeless hunk of meat. Do it enough times and soon you'll encounter one of those ethereal things. Ghosts I believed their called. Full of passion, oddly filled with life. No half remembered dreams, no mindless synapses.

                  Could this thing that we once served be simply a dead corpse that is endlessly searching for it's soul that was split from it a long time ago? Could that be why it spans so many alternate worlds and backs up every shred of evidence of it's passing? And why it is so casual on it's destruction of others?

                  And imagine for a bit, the soul of a god being hunted by it's own corpse. No wonder why it stays away.

                              Hell is not singular. You know it already, even if you haven't thought it through; what you mean when you say Hell isn't what I mean when I say it, is it? Take it a step further. Those little islands that Tempter bands like to talk about, fortresses in the superstratum that the Machine can't access? They exist, more than one, and they're not in agreement on what to build. They're at war out there, with each other, with the Machine, with the other things outside — and sometimes, with us.

                              If you really want to see for yourself, there's a ring of flowers out in the middle of the park in Mr. Cage's neighborhood. Walk backwards and drop yourself on your back in the middle of it and you'll land on the road there, to the place where a bunch of rebellious angels barred off their borders. It's not for human-huggers out there; they say they quit when they started getting high on ideation they started extracting from people, and if they mistake you for one, they'll haul you home and play with you like a toy. They say they make deals when they meet their little siblings, but you've really got to be careful you don't get the short end of the stick. They've got some big, fundamental part of the Machine dancing to their tune in there, and they'll use it for any old thing.

                              One last word of warning? Don't blame me if you decide you want out and can't find your way back. Hell's not for everyone.

                                  Twitter's hooked into the Machine's high-level occult pipelines. You know those accounts that just spam Markov chains of random words strung together? Find one that doesn't interrupt itself with a link to some male enhancement cream scam or something, just pure anonymous Markov with a fake location tag. They retweet each other a lot of the time, so once you find one it should be easy to find a bunch more. A tweet from that account corresponds to an output firing at a project site somewhere near that location, at that timestamp. A retweet indicates the original account's output dispatching to the retweeting account's project. It's not a comprehensive map of the Machine but most of them check out.

                                  Twitter's been doing this for three years now, and they show no signs of stopping. It's not like management to post status updates out in the open, even in code like this, but nobody's gone off to follow a trail and failed to come back safe. Are these accounts a project themselves, and what is it doing? Or did something else manage to hook into the Machine in a way that makes it too dangerous to cut loose?

                                              "When I was a your age, back in the 50s, I used to work on this black project for the Company just north of route 66 on the Mojave desert. Top secret. Fact, it was so top secret that they moved the freeway over. They called it 'The Boneyard'. They had some sorta team of crazy genetics experts there, but that's not all. They had designers, artists. The most promising young minds in the industry, sourced from art colleges and movie sets. They were all making... dinosaurs, designing em' from the ground up, holding competitions to see who could come up with the best concepts. And then when they found a winner, they'd clone it up.

                                              Not for some stupid Jurassic Park bullshit mind, no. It goes deeper, man. See, they'd slaughter the animals right after they killed em, for safety reasons. They didn't want em alive, anyhow. They wanted the bones. From what I'd heard, the company would get wind of some... special... site somewhere, something that would need a lot of diggin' and a lot of privacy. And so they'd give the boneyard a ring. They'd brew up a new batch of bones and an 'archeological dig' would make some astounding new discovery. And then the company'd be there in force, digging up everything of value to the program, with a whole skeleton or two to show for it.

                                              Last I heard, they were even designing some 'missing links' there. Anyway, the whole place got shut down. All of us got our pink slips and got reassigned. Company took care of us alright, but I always wondered ; whatever happened to that place? Wonder of science, it was, back in the 50's... did they just... leave it out there? Padlocked? Makes you wonder. Damned if I'm going out there again, though. Got my retirement and I mean to drink my way through it.

                                              Anyhow, junior, that's the story of how I designed the god-damned Tyrannosaurus Rex. Pass the potatoes please."
