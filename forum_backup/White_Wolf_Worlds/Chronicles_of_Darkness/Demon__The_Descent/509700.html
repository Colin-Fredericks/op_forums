    Can Demons innately discern Manifested Angels

    05-10-2015, 03:41 AM
    They can see Infrastructure just fine, so long as it isn't concealed:

    Originally posted by page 183, [I
    Demon: The Descent[/I]]The methods of magical occlusion the God-Machine uses to mask its works from mortal eyes do not function against the Unchained.
    For the record, I mean without resorting to Aetheric Resonance.

    Does this include Manifested Angels that don't have the Mortal Mask Numen? There aren't rules given for Angels having Covers outside of Angel-Jacking, where it's merely implied, and a few times in the book it mentions seeing angels "flying about" and such. Plus several of the angels in the book have very inhuman appearances. A couple, however, have human appearances but don't have the Mortal Mask Numen.

    Overall, I'll probably rule that some angels were lucky enough to get "Demon proofing" in the form of totally-human looking "Covers", but some didn't really need that "Cover" for one reason or another.

    Still, if I could get anything official on this I'd be super happy.

                                                      I'd say yes to demons recognising a *manifested* angel, but not one inhabiting a Cover-ed physical form.

                                                      Angels are a tricky beast and somewhat different from other spirits when it comes to the phemeral-physical divide. Ephemeral angels can manifest (with the correct abilities and conditions), and I'd suggest they appear in all the instantly recognisable divine grace of their biomechanical horror.

                                                      Angels inhabiting a cover, or even just a God-Machine granted physical form, look just like what they're supposed to be. There aren't really even rules to cover this exactly - the GM provides a body and fills in any holes in their covers. In this form, the angel shouldn't be instantly recognisable, unless their body is meant to appear obviously unnatural for some reason.
