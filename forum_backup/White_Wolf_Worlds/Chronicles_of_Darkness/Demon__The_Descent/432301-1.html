                                                Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
                                                Well - many do have "greater powers" though. Going 6+ dots to represent it isn't a necessary thing. Werewolves have Milestone Gifts. Vampires have Elder-only Devotions. Mummies get access to the cosmic-devastation versions of their Utterances when their Sekhem is high enough.

                                                I agree something new for Changelings and Demons might be appropriate too. I don't think the others need anything though.
                                                They HAD milestone gifts. I don't see their return in the new edition so far.


                                                In my opinion, I think that there's a notable difference between high level play with "elder" powers, and tier 4 games. In tier 4 vampire, that's a Primacy game, where personal powers are actually pretty meaningless. For changeling, the Game of Immortals can pretty much rewrite your reality at whim. Why would you need to buy powers when you effectively already have them using the new system?

                                                Tier 4 games are too wildly different from the other style of games that it makes the standard progression of abilities... almost meaningless. It only matters in an archmage game, because of the whole direct tie between knowledge and arcana ranking, and knowledge is everything in mage.

                                                Now, high powered games, with elders, alpha wolves, master mages, archdemons, and, uh, fae lords we'll say. Having selections of high level powers would be nice. But when it comes to tier 4 games, its not about the powers so much as a completely different mindset.



                                                For Demon, also, we have the issue that such high level powers already kinda exist. When one goes loud, you get every affinity Embed, and every Exploit. That's pretty nasty as it is.
