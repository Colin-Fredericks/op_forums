    Let's Lateral Thinking! (Exploit Qualification Thread)

    02-10-2014, 02:23 PM
    Given the flexibility of prerequisite Embeds one can use for Exploits, I thought it might be neat to set up a thread for rationales folks could use to qualify for Exploits with given Embeds.

    Format is as follows:

    [Embed Name] > [Exploit Name]
    Rationale.

    Some examples:

    Authorized > Force Relationship
    The Embed is keyed off of the relationship humans have with authority - it's easy enough to generalize that to generating reciprocal relationships.

    Alibi > Four Minutes Ago
    Name aside, "dislocate your Cover" and "I was literally never here" are connected in a straightforward fashion.

    Hesitation > Frozen in Time
    Expanding the scale of slowdown from single-turn Initiative stalling to multi-turn/multi-minute stopping.

    Sabotage > Raze Infrastructure
    Almost goes without saying; go from damaging small machines to damaging The Machine.

    If anybody feels like explaining some of the less intuitive sample prerequisites from the book, that's also more than welcome.

    Resident Lore-Hound

                  Trust No One > Allies into Gold
                  Understanding and manipulating (by disabling) social structures attached to another individual leads to proficiency in making permanent changes to one's own.

                  Fungible Knowledge > Allies into Gold
                  Similar to the above, but the Demon is experienced in manipulating (through a form of metacognition) personal variables in the form of temporary skill transfers, leading to an exploit capable of making changes of a less meta but more permanent manner.

                  Momentum > Possession (may be a bit of a stretch)
                  Learning to make use of others' actions and having it affect one's own (through effects from the other party's successes and failures) inverts at the exploit level, allowing the Demon to instead directly affect another's actions (in a rather dramatic fashion).
