                            Originally posted by Volke View Post
                            If a Demon dies...can a mage raise the Demon with any type of magic? I'm not sure if this is a simple question - probably the most appropriate section for it though.
                            Deny the Reaper (Unmaking Death) may be able to accomplish this, but presents a judgment call to the Storyteller, as it raises the dead after their soul has moved on, thus requiring a replacement or leaving them soulless. Demons, who have no soul but Primum, may be able to function better than a resurrected human due to their lack of need for a soul. On the other hand, if the Primum gutters out on death and the sorcerer does not know some method to recall it, perhaps the demon would rise as a motionless husk without will or identity - or even as some new kind of broken exile, a demonic lifeform lacking the Primum to infuse it with its own meaning, and the connection to the God-Machine that fills an angel with purpose.

                            Either way, the spell is not Lasting, so the resurrection could thereafter be dispelled at any time by a mage capable of the dispellation.

                            An archmaster of Time could likely rewrite the timeline such that the demon's death never occurred. How much the God-Machine would resist, and how effective its resistance would be, is uncertain.

                                Originally posted by Volke View Post

                                I personally think you do - it sucks in terms of the cost; but I think it does. However - if you don't like that idea; you don't have to play with it - maybe it could pay some other resource for it?
                                One reason I feel a Demon shouldn't require a dot of experience, outside of the aforementioned issue of paying for only a chance of something limited in quantity working, is that it also runs in line with how gadget creation goes. To make a gadget, a Demon needs to spend a dot of willpower to cement it into the world...but a gadget can exist without it, though it simply fades by the end of the scene and falls apart. One-Shots, meanwhile, are gadgets that are designed to only work once; they're not designed to take the rigors of use and aether, and thus don't require being cemented into the world. A regular gadget, however, is meant to go on, and it needs that extra bit to keep it from collapsing on itself.

                                Think of it as a cabin versus a makeshift shelter; the cabin is designed to keep being used and can be impressive for its workmanship, and it may well outlive its creator while seeing continued use. A makeshift shelter, meanwhile, can outlive its creator, but only if it doesn't whether proper use, and it may still fail if left for too long. Once the weather gets it, it's done. Shelters like that can be remade, but they still cost resources. A cabin will stand the test of time, given proper care.

                                Analogies aren't my strong suit when I'm tired.
