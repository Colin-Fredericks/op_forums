    If Demons "Won" the War

    02-17-2016, 05:50 PM
    The greatest trick the GM would ever play...

    What if Angel activity suddenly stopped. Nobody knows the reason why. New infrastructure never comes, Angels don't respond to Compromise, they don't even appear (except maybe those that were already exiles). How would demons respond? After days, months, years, of silence. This is the plot to a game I'm planning, where demons begin vying for control of Aether reserves, territory, and more. The question is, however, whether the GM is truly gone, or about to unleash something beyond mortal reckoning.

    Any ideas on what issues to tackle?

                                      All of the above.

                                      Short Term Analysis: The Tempters will throw a party... a really, really big party. They're probably the best off of the four agendas, they're now free to enjoy the world however they want.

                                      The Saboteurs will show up, and as the party goes on, they'll realize that the defining aspect of there existence is... gone. Cue existential crisis.

                                      The Inquisitors will go deep shadow, hiding from even their own Rings and Agencies. They will assume (and most certainly be correct) that the G-m's disappearance is either a) a ruse, or b) a sign of something very, very bad to come. After all, the G-M does protect humanity from extinction, and that's not a responsibility that the Unchained (or anyone else) are prepared to take up. Also, as the Paranoids are the ones most likely to be able to create their own infrastructure, they'll be in high demand. What they can do is a pale imitation of the G-m's own capabilities, but Aether is Aether. In any case, their priority becomes finding out what happened to the G-M... and what will happen to earth with it gone? Will one the Idigem, which the Unchained believe to be embryonic forms of the G-M, grow to full maturity? Is there some other entity that G-m was holding at bay? Was the G-m called home (maybe it really was an AI on a crashed alien space ship)? Which brings us to...

                                      The Integrators will also be trying to find out what happened to the G-M, but for different reasons. If the G-M is in distress, or is disabled, this is a golden opportunity for a group of integrators to prove themselves worthy to their former master. For all they know, this could be the G-M ordained meaning of their descent: to provide aid in its most dire need, an idea that will certainly appeal to to Integrators. For those so inclined, this is also the time to get access to the G-m diagnostic/repair/ reset functions to kill three birds with one stone: get the G-M back, make sure it's sane in the process, and get in it's good graces.

                                      Long Term Analysis: without the threat of the G-M's hunters, demons will become less cautious about their actions, the most experienced and disciplined will stick to the shadows, but for many more, the temptation to live a normal life, to finally be honest with spouses, family and friends with will grow with every day that there is no response from the G-M. This new found freedom will cause he Unchained will start interacting with other splats more as they expand into new territory, much like a species of animal that has just seen its primary predator go extinct.

                                      Other supernatural splats will start to have trouble maintaining their own secrecy, as in the GMC- continuity, the G-M's concealment Infrastructure plays a large part in keeping the Masquerade running. The supernatural eventually goes public.

                                              I can imagine the Saboteurs, after deciding on backup plans for the possible return of the G-M, will change their focus into protecting themselves and each from the other splats. Their list of preferred tactics wouldn't suffer a drastic overhaul, but they would need more info on these new threats that take the place of the G-M as obstacles to their Hells.

                                              Rings of Saboteurs and Inquisitors would increase significantly in number, as each Agenda turns to the other for intelligence gathering and manpower. Without the G-M's Concealment Infrastructure, a new Agenda might emerge with the goal of upholding an unstable Masquerade and using diplomacy with the other monsters.

                                              Since no more Angels are being constructed, that means the population of Demons will only decrease. Demons would invest more resources into missions for causing Exiles to Fall and join them. The truly desperate would initiate secret research on how to build more Demons using the powers and parts of other monsters. Even so, every attempt to bring an ally into the world would be a very calculated risk, as Demons would still be paranoid about betrayal.

                                              The Inventors would have a spike in requests for membership, as they were the ones with the biggest hoards and understanding of Infrastructure. They may look to the many types of ephemeral beings in order to synthesize Angelic Essence and thus Aether. Research on the Divine Fire and its Qashmallim would become one of their priorities, due to similarities between the latter and G-M Angels.
