                    Calling up and dismissing form abilities costs you at least two Aether that you're not getting back without risk, if you're at the level where you can burn that much Aether freely you're also at the level where losing Cover is less of a concern, and arguably the best use of that bonus is to circumvent the penalties observers apply.

                            Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                            It may be worth remembering that the net modifier for compromise rolls is explicitly capped at +/-5.
                            The total modifier for 'circumstances' explicitly shouldn't exceed +/-5. The partial transformation section then goes on to detail a roll receiving a +6 bonus. While it's reasonable to consider those bonuses 'circumstantial', and to assume the +6 modifier was before taking into account any existing penalties, etc (and hence not a final number) it's not hard to understand why many people don't interpret it that way at a glance.
