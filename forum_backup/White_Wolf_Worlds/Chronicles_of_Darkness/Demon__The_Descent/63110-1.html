        Originally posted by Helter Skelter View Post
        I was mostly curious about a straight up physical contest between the two. Let's say both meet in the ally and just start beating on one another with their fists. Vampires have quite a bump due to Vigor, not to mention spending Vitae to up their damage even further. I was mostly curious to see if Demons could throw around similarly high die pools for physical attacks without "going loud" just using their "base" abilities.
        Well, vampires also have frenzy, which adds their Blood Potency to their physical attacks. And take bashing from most every kind of damage out there. Lets also throw on the frenzy FS. Lets not forget something like the effects of Predator's Aura, which can inflict penalties in this fight (assuming its successful).

        On the other hand, there's a demon. Lets say a Destroyer, since beating people up is their thing. First of all, they get some really nice powers and effects from their Demon Form. Gun/sword/clawed arms, attribute boosts, armor, unusual movement such as flight and teleports, poison tails, acid spit, electricity, fire, shields / illusions, extra limbs, turn into an insect swarm, magnesium flares, reflexive healing. Most embeds and exploits won't work here - as we've established the vamp is using Vigor instead of Majesty or Dominate or similar, I'm going to stick with direct combat application stuff. There's two embeds dealing with healing/damage mitigation, and one embed and one exploit dealing with guns.

        There's a lot of potential power here. Exposure is the demon's bane; so I'm going to assume that the demon won't use fire, and that Cover will be ignored for the moment (both vamps and demons have ways of negating / lessening their bane, so for the sake of simplicity, I'm assuming that's true now. Otherwise, this would be a very quick battle, very likely with the vampire dead, and the demon surrounded by hunter angels.) Both have a wide range of natural abilities.

        I would have to give the starting vampire the advantage in this case, where they both start punching / stabbing each other. The sheer amount of dice they get is staggering. Vampires natural resistance to damage I think is roughly equivalent to all the armor the demon can put on. And that's before we start investing in Resilience for more.

        All in all, this style of fighting just favors vampires over demons - despite being the social game, vampires are very direct creatures, and most of their powers and advantages reflect this. Domination and control is a thing with them; they get up in your face, and beat you down. Demons, on the other hand, are spies - they have a much greater advantage over vampires when it comes to a hide-and-seek battle, or just plain mobility. They have better powers when using guns - combine with a teleport demon form ability? Or wings?

            If you want to construct a Demon that cannot not be defeated by a Vampire in physical combat, you can do so.

            E.g. if your demonic form includes the process Insect Swarm you can turn into a million tiny insects and cannot be killed by normal physical violence (if you activate only that power from your demonic form, you get a huge bonus on your compromise roll, +1 for each demonic form power not activated, so you don't really endanger your cover, even if you do it before human witnesses). Then, summon an insect swarm with the Swarm exploit and let the vampire take automatic bashing damage each turn equal to your primum (use a willpower point and you don't even roll for compromise). I have used that on my vampire players with a primum 6 demon. Vampires were fear frenzying left and right (I admit Swarm says 'living target' but why would something that lets a human take six bashing damage a turn not harm a vampire's body ? You could also just have the demon throw fire for 6 leathal at them).

            If you really want just physical stats, well, the demonic form can include for example. Swordarm (4L Weapon), superhuman strength (+2), Armored plates (3/2 Armor), huge form (+ 4size = +4 health levels), Wound Healing (heal rightmost health box every turn). You can choose to activate only a bunch of these to get bonuses to compromise as described above. Also, when turning into full demonic form, you heal damage equal to your primum.

                        See, I think 'balance' or inter-splat power comparison is not really all that important, as they are not even designed with (too much) crossover in the developers' minds. Demons are (mostly) designed to deal with GM and angels(and other demons) in many different ways, while vampires got mostly other vampires, mages got the seers/banishers/whatever, werewolves spirits and such.

                        Although I really do love Demon the Descent, I do think the game concentrates WAY too much on the 'techgnostic' side / espionage, and downplays the actual 'demonic' imagery way too much. I love VtR and Changeling because they fit the image of the traditional / mass-media-portrayed vampires and changelings pretty well, and I don't love werewolves as much because they are more spirit doctor / Native American medicine men than beastly-horrifying-halfman half wolf.

                        Just the matter of preferences, really. However, when I now think of Demon the Descent, the image that comes up is not a demon, but a cyborg spy. Not that it's a bad thing though. but yeah.

                        (And I do think powerful demons should be more powerful.
