    Originally posted by Satchel View Post
    Yeah I was hesitant to ruin people's fun, but do remember also, folks, that these are supposed to be oblique names - running &quot;the God-Machine&quot; through a thesaurus or tossing out names that would neither fit in with the corebook's matter-of-fact terms nor at least not look out of place in comic-shop discussion or a WoW board misses the point; the Unchained aren't liable to bust out flowery-mouthful titles for the thing they're trying to avoid drawing the attention of.

    I mean, if you're infiltrating a cult, that's a different matter, but let's give some consideration to tone, shall we? A little more esoteric or mundane, a little less crass — I could see a Sacred Order of the http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deus_ex_machina#Origin_of_the_expression&quot;]Ascendant Crane[/URL], for instance, but references to less archetypal technology are probably a good indicator that you've wandered into the absurd.
    'Pologies on my part. I guess I got caught up in sort-of a Lovecraftian kick. As an alternative for the last name I offered, perhaps " My old Boss, Lucy"?
