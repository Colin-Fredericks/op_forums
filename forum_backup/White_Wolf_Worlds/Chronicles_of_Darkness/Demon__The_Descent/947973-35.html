                An Angel is tasked with collecting particular items for an Infrastructure, or either to build one or perhaps simply making sure it is being repaired. It is having problems securing the proper materials for it though, due to outside interference, but it has learned through the grapevine of Demons in the area. In exchange for information, if not some assistance due to what is at stake, it gives some specific information on areas to avoid, areas of interest, or something otherwise useful but not compromising to the mission.

                Angels and Demons can approach something close to a friendship, so long as they don’t cross lines and try to pry more into each other than allowed. The Demon is allowed in the Angel’s office, to bring food that is approved of and from the office kitchen, and is not to look at the computer screen while they work, but the Angel is likewise not to lock the doors or inform anyone that the Demon is anything but human, and from there...they talk. No probing about projects beyond perhaps dangers they are trying to overcome, open ended questions that could allow the other to volunteer some information or perhaps even give some mild propoganda for their own side. Maybe they are simply discussing problems with human life in a way they can’t with humans or others of their ilk. It may all be a subtle ploy to try and pull information from a Demon, a horrible trap or somesuch, or it might be a genuine attempt at being a friend, or learning what it’s like to have one. Angels are different from each other, and have their own reasons for doing things.

                Apologies if that doesn’t help with your problem.

                                            Originally posted by Taidragon View Post
                                            So, because this was something that came up in a chat and I couldn’t support my side of the argument, I have to ask: is using an Incept bad for an Angel’s mental state? By using them, are they more likely to Fall?
                                            It's not bad for them mentally, but they are a fraught thing and using them can change a lot of things in the game, and while it's use isn't neccesarily Fall worthy, the conditions in which you use Incepts are generally bad for Angels. Let's backtrack and explain that.

                                            Incepts are a high octane-boost to the things that an angel is capable of doing, but by and large basically reveals the angel and any of the God-Machine's cards that hinge on that knowledge. Factoring that in, the God-Machine tends to try and build angels who are fit to the task It needs executed without need for the Incept. Occasionally, an incident will require an Angel instead of some other kind of occult matrix but demands the power of an Incept, but by and large, an Incept is best used by angels when they have the absolute security in knowledge of their isolation, which is rare and near impossible, or when it's clear that burning cards is no longer a meaningful penalty for the situation, either because it wins the game or at least allows for some kind of win out of the loss.

                                            The problem is that the choice to use the Incept belongs to the angel, and even if they are given the ones that move their purpose along, situations can still force them to use Incepts outside of the optimum situations, and that sort of thing can mess up an occult matrix trying to be set up. Using an Incept basically reveals parts of the hand earlier than intended and allows the potential enemies of the GOd Machine to know where and who one of the major pieces are on the board, and even probably define more of what they are for. And the Angel can, effectively, decide to when it can do that, and like there are lots of times an Exploit might seem like the right answer but could be handled better with quieter answers, an angel deals with a lot of scenarios that put them at risk and seem like it calls for an Incept but could be resolved with keeping their cards close to their chest and using other things from their toolset.

                                            So an Incept doesn't risk a Fall by itself, but they represent a temptation that can serve as a demarker for a Fall.
