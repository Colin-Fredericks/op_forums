                            01-07-2014, 12:41 PM
                            Originally posted by Xenesis View Post
                            Page 70 - Second Paragraph of the Fluff: "No few of those scouts take their orders from the God-Machine."
                            "No few" is a roundabout but grammatically correct synonym for "many." There is no issue here.

                            In the Character Creation Quick Reference Chart on pages 87-89, there are some stylistic issues.
                            • In the "Choose 4 Embeds and/or Exploits" step, the list reads: "Cacophony (Destroyer), Instrumental (Guardian), Vocal (Messenger), Psychopomp (Mundane). One of these things is not like the others.
                            • In the "Select Merits" step, consider making "5 Merit dots = a dot of Primum" "5 Merit dots = 1 dot of Primum" instead, for improved clarity.
                            • In the "Calculate Advantages" step, there's an awkward line break. "Defense is (lower of Wits and Dexteri-ty..." The word Dexterity should either not be hyphenated or be hyphenated after the first syllable (Dex-terity).
                            • At the end of the checklist, "...in order to create a Demon character." Unbolded letter in a bolded word.
                            • Under the "Incarnations" heading, more strangely-placed line breaks, in "...created by the God-Ma-chine" and "...scourge of the God-Ma-chine." The phrase, "God-Machine," is already hyphenated; inserting additional hyphens is contraindicated. Also, in the phrase, "target of their re-sponsibility," responsibility should not be hyphenated. It's only two characters, move the whole word to the next line.
                            • Under "Agendas," "...taking different ap-proaches..." has the same problem.
                            • Under "Merits," "...as they already have these abilities by de-fault by default." Badly placed line break and redundant words. Fix by omitting the bolded words.
                            • Also under "Merits," the phrase "De-mon-specific" has an extraneous line break. There's enough room on the line for the whole word "Demon."
                            • Under "Starting Experiences," in the first row of the table, "5 Experience" should be "5 Experiences."
