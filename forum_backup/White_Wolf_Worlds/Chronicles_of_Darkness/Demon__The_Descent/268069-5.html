        Session XXI

        Kilroy’s Transmissions

        T.G.I. Tuesday's

        Saturday was a day of divergent agendas. Clytemnestra spent the day with Robin Graves with plans to enlist her death-aspected senses to track down the materials we need to repair the reality damage the Abyssal has caused. Speed was planning to do some research before attempting to seduce and collect a blood sample from Miranda Asbury. Quietus wanted to meet with Crusher Creel and Rosanna to give the man his gift and cement them as allies. Vivian had plans to take Shelly to see the Bernice Burnheart and the Sisters of Chaos that evening but I decided to join him for the afternoon so I could speak to them about her entanglement with the gentry known properly as Mr Smiles, though I am still rather partial to Jack Slenderskellingtonman. I wonder, given the import of names in fairy, if it would be irksome to call him that directly? I shall have to find out when I get the opportunity, which seems likely as he did technically buy me dinner by sending me that meat and cheese basket. Though it does beg the question of how he knew to send it to the detective agency. It is possible that Michael White gave him the information if they are linked. Otherwise, it would be yet another unknown fae power. They are decidedly too unknown in their abilities and dangerously able to divine what should be secret information for my liking.

        The mass produced atmosphere was suitably unobtrusive to our purpose and the wait staff suitably scarce to give us adequate privacy. Rosanna provided us with some further information concerning the local Changeling political structure. The Autumn Court welds the lion's share of the power and numbers in this area, but not so much that it could stand against the other seasonal courts should it attempt to seize total control. Of course, their position does afford them great leeway in activities and a powerful voice in what I gather is supposed to be a council of equals. The Autumn King, known only as The Harvestman, is clearly a shrewd man who is content to be a stronger indirect power than to give into the temptations of tyranny. This bodes well, I do prefer to deal with reasonable individuals and our talents are certainly of greater impact when used with the sort of subtle and surgical precision that such a man would appreciate.

        Rosanna said that the Autumn Court was looking into the issue of Mr Smiles already, so it seems likely that we can collaborate. An excellent means to establish some mutual awareness and lay a groundwork for future dealings. She was concerned that they might wish to use me as bait to lure Mr Smiles in. I was mildly surprised at her concern, as such an approach is the rather obvious tack and dismissing such leverage without good cause would be foolhardy. Of course, she may well believe Vivian to be mortal or at the very least have no clear idea of power levels. Also, I do not believe the Changeling's fear of these gentry is entirely overblown or based on their misfortunes. Even at the reduced power that his exile causes, Mr Smiles is a formidable being. Also, these beings are apparently mystically difficult to kill, being fairy-tail monsters such as they are, they have only one, often obscure, manner of true death.

        In speaking of the Autumn Court, she did also explain that the courts operate under something akin to the medieval definition of hospitality. She advised being sure that we had such in place to keep our secrets safe from the Autumn Court when we met with them. She also explained that they do seem to have some rules of engagement that prevent their members from sowing fear too indiscriminately. The gist of it all was not pleasing. Our actions at the coffee shop seem likely to have been a violation of hospitality. Oneiromancy is a common fae talent. Given the very intensely personal content of the nightmares I experienced and the reactions of the others, I fear this Autumn Court knows entirely too much about us for comfort. For now, we must rebuild a state of hospitality to prevent further harassment. Then we must glean as much information as we can about them.

        Quietus made it clear that he was prepared to aid Rosanna and her Summer Court in reducing the power of the Autumn Court but she seemed disinclined as the current arrangement is mutually beneficial, even as unbalanced as it may be. Also, internal strife would only serve to weaken all of the Changeling courts and endanger the locals.

        I have some thoughts on repairing the breech of etiquette that we made. Hopefully, a gesture of good faith and a successful joint venture against one of their enemies will suffice. However, clearly the balance of power is delicate and could be tipped to create discord and strife which would make them all extremely vulnerable to the Gentry. As a precaution, I shall need to discover how one might go about making sure the Arcadians know of such a situation when it occurs. It seems likely they have spies of some sort watching, but relying on such could be disastrous.

        All told, it would be a difficult and dangerous undertaking and almost assuredly require the sacrifice of our current covers and the Night Train Detective Agency. Also, destroying what could otherwise be a large number of very potent allies would be a terrible waste even without the hefty expenditure of resources involved. I do hope it does not become necessary.

        Once we'd spoken about that, Quietus made his gift to Crusher. He offered the ogre a boost to his intelligence and education, with a focus on literature. It was quite well received. Mr Creel reiterated his willingness to use his powers to attempt to local Drake and once more cautioned us against entering the Hedge if it proved that he had become lost there.

        Once we departed, I shared my observations with Quietus concerning the fallout of our indiscretion toward the Autumn Court. He was not pleased but he could find no fault with my reasoning. I imagine Speed will be disappointed that it was not Micheal White's handiwork, but his list of crimes remains ample for whatever misfortunes we might see fit to visit upon him and his house.


        Once More Into the Breech

        We had finished our luncheon when Clytemnestra called us about meeting her and Robin at the Asbury house. Mrs Graves was very interested in seeing Alice after hearing about her from Clytemnestra but our Tempter was not comfortable entering the house alone as she had not personally investigated it previously. Quietus and I joined them at the house and we all entered.

        As I had not been fully prepared last time, I took the occasion to bring my kit with me and collected some much more proper samples. In particular, I was able to get some intact grubs and insects from the downstairs room for study. Quietus remained hyper vigilant as I worked and Clytemnestra and Mrs Graves waited upstairs at his direction. The aberrations of the Asbury house were unchanged from before as we made our way up to the attic. Of note were some personal effects in a previously empty and unoccupied room. There was female clothing including some rather elaborate cowboy boots. Despite the unexpected choice of foot fashion, I concurred with Quietus that it was most likely Miranda returning to her familial home. This might complicate his plans to claim the house and the identities of the deceased once we've repaired the reality damage.

        Mrs Graves was unable to ascertain whether or not Alice was tainted by the corruption or not. Her overwhelming drive to return to life concerned Robin as did her ability to possess someone and warp them into a nightmarish monster. According to Mrs Graves, this was a taxing but not damaging power for Alice and so one she was only likely to use when angry or frightened. The effect is quite harmful to the victim as their body attempts to revert back to its proper shape afterward but without the benefit of ghostly magic to aid the movement of bones and flesh. This is not a power beyond the realm of ghostly ability, but one Mrs Graves has not previously encountered. It may have been triggered by abyssal exposure or it might simply be a power Alice manifested naturally. Either way, it makes her very dangerous and her single-minded determination to find a way back to life at any cost makes her unlikely to be laid quietly to rest.

        As Clytemnestra made her intention to reveal her own nature to Mrs Graves, Quietus was open about using some partial transformations in her presence as well as making reference to the ability to 'do things'. At this point, Robin Graves admitted that she had determined that we were not mundane humans, though her theory was that we were magi. Interesting, and possibly an angle worth considering as a means of laying a false explanation for our abilities in the future. More pertinent was that she and her 'crew' had encountered angels in the past. They are the group that we have heard of that killed a group of angels using bone spurs and bleeding smoke rather than blood. Mrs Graves is an ally of value and, especially with her connections to this group of others, a very clear threat especially now that she knows more about us. I trust Clytemnestra's ability to monitor and control her. I do know she is fond of Mrs Graves, but I do not believe she would put her safety or ours at risk for such attachments. Mrs Graves account of the incident with the angels made it clear there was an occult matrix involved. A death every four days of every fourth name alphabetically of the building's residents. I should perhaps see if the location itself bears any further significance. She also made particular mention of one of her fellows, named only as the Burning Man, who she said was very hostile to, as she termed it, robots. She seems to accept that our physical similarity does not equal a like-minded dark intent but she was skeptical that there could be a peaceful meeting with this Burning Man and thus he was to be avoided. Further comments paint him as very intense and humorless with a perpetual anger. This is a mindset I can certainly fathom and have seen not infrequently among my more combative and hostile fellow Unchained. Unfortunate that such a powerful fighter is so predisposed to be hostile to us. Perhaps, with proper introduction or great subtly his rage can be directed to the God Machine's minions.


        Shelly Shocked

        Having completed the investigations at the Asbury house, we went our separate ways. Quietus and I returned to the office while Clytemnestra and Mrs Graves headed back to Clytemnestra's house for further revelations and presumably sex, as there is usually sex when Clytemnestra is involved, before going out to seek the death-marked car parts we need to produce, as Speed dubbed it, Reality Spackle at the Peterson Chemical infrastructure.

        I made ready for my date with Shelly. Vivian's typical hacker geek wardrobe isn't entirely unsuitable for a cross-dressing punk show. I selected something more anarchist than otaku for the evening and set out. Shelly had also gone with a jeans and tee casual as this wasn't her usual venue either. I did remind her that she'd asked for different.

        We started with a meal, going to a nice little family run Japanese restaurant that favors authentic quality cuisine over kitsch. Shelly seemed to enjoy the food and I was able to steer the conversation to her past without too much difficulty. She took up waitressing to pay for college but soon fell into the trap of being unable to work enough to support herself and continue her studies. She harbors a strong desire to resume her degree while it is still a viable option, but is resigned that her current circumstances do not make this a likely event. Clearly, this would make for excellent leverage to gain nearly anything she might have of interest or value to me. Her area of study is chemistry, and thus our conversation was pleasingly advanced. She is a clever woman and I do enjoy her company. Perhaps, if she proves suitably trustworthy, she could assist me in the lab. It may prove to be worthwhile to gift her the means to finish her degree simply to have her as a valuable ally.

        After eating, we made our way to the venue. Bernice and her ladies where every bit the performers as before. Even having been to the previous show, I found their talent enjoyable and while it was not Shelly's genre of choice I think the novelty of it appealed to her. I continued to be witty and charming. As the evening went on, Shelly relaxed with a few drinks and I took advantage of the rather sexual nature of the show to grow steadily more flirtatious. I do not think she'd set out with intentions of bringing me back to her place afterward but as the evening unfolded she began to flirt back and our dancing took on considerable contact. By the end, we'd dropped any real pretenses and moved into kissing and light if mostly subtle petting. I briefly considered asking her if she wanted to stop for coffee, but as she seemed quite adequately warm and stimulated already we instead headed back to her place.
