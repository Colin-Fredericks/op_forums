                                  01-07-2016, 03:52 AM
                                  p.60-1: "She chooses Discorporate and Gauntlet Breach." "Manifestation: Twilight Form, Discorporate, Gauntlet Breach." Given that the Super has a Cover (and is not a spirit), Gauntlet Breach should probably be replaced with Materialize.

                                  p.62: Descriptive text of The Burned Lot should be italicized, judging from the other Sample Missions.

                                  p.62: "Now is the time that blood will rise to prominence, ushering in new era where and cause thousands of Projects to come into fruition in one glorious moment of supremacy." Superfluous "where" or "and cause," or other error in sentence construction.

                                  p.64/72-3: "How does the Destroyer Madam X differ from other Destroyers?" Exile's character sheet refers to her as Madame X; given these are pronounced differently, a single spelling should probably be decided upon.

                                  p.66: "Abandoned Eyes are cautious around angels — if their cache contains something of use to the angels there is little the sliver can do to protect it." Described behavior is not specific to slivers and analogous subsections of other Incarnations make no reference to slivers; possibly replace with exile.

                                  p.66: "Murder is the most common, but most embrace more inventive habits." Repetitive. Suggest dropping "the most."

                                  p.68: "Their bodies are made of molten metal, or are have armored hides, or bear multiple heads." Delete are.

                                  p.68: "Once scorned, the abandoned Swords are slow to accept apologies." As this is in the Guardian section, this should be Shields.

                                  p.69: "Sometimes Messenger-Angels alter their missives every-so-slightly." Should be ever-so-slightly. Does "angel" need to be capitalized?

                                  p.72: "The exile has only a moment take or reject opportunity." Should probably be "a moment to take or reject the opportunity."

                                  p.72: "Exiles are diverse; they are angels who are not-quite demons yet lack the strings of a loyal member of the God-Machine’s agents." Superfluous hyphen; wordy phrase that could be replaced with "agent of the God-Machine."

                                  p.73-4: As Madam/e X and Samson have Covers, they should probably also have Mortal Mask on their lists of Numina; their Rank allows for it.

                                  p.74-5: Samson and Lik-a-lack's lists of Manifestations include Manifestation; this should be Materialize.

                                  p.75: "The Psychopomp responsible for matrix miscalculated and attempt the ritual with just thirty-two." Should be "for the matrix" and "attempted," respectively.

                                  p.78: The description for Echoes repeatedly uses the word Numen as a plural; Numen is the singular form of the more appropriate Numina.

                                  p.78: The other published ghosts indicate Kelly Casselman should probably have Influences more nuanced than "Anchors 4" if this is not specifically a divergence from normal ghost rules; Betrayal seems a suitable singular replacement.

                                  p.84: Some of the Incepts have no listed Essence cost for their primary effects. If "Incepts have an Essence cost to activate their primary effect" is intended to imply a minimum cost of 1, this should probably be made explicit; otherwise, the statement should probably be qualified.

                                  p.84: The Delivery Incept has multiple instances of "angel" being capitalized.

                                  p.84: "The Angel must spend twice the normal Essence cost of the Incept effect being delivere, and be able to see the target." Should be delivered.

                                  p.107: Unless Keneo's lack of unusual appearance is meant to signify a lack of need for Cover, his list of Numina should probably include Mortal Mask; his Rank allows for it.

                                  p.120: "Once integrated, the victim’s become obsessed[…]" Unnecessary apostrophe.

                                  p122-3: "The character may subtract her dots in Occult from her required number of successes, reducing the time for each roll by 15 minutes, or […]" Sould be reduce.

                                  p.124: "Inhuman Speed" does not exist; possibly substitute "Inhuman Reflexes" or "Long Limbs."

                                  p.132: "The shaman can coax the animistic sprits that inhabit the world." Should be spirits.

                                  p.153: "The further towards the city center, the less careful God-Machine is with hiding Its nature." Missing the.

                                  p.171-3: The Power and Finesse Attributes are out of order on all four angels' character sheets; Power should precede Finesse.

                                  p.172: "It restrains the Maw and centers its focus, or the lack thereof." Awkward phrasing; suggest deleting the and possibly replacing the comma with a dash.

                                  p.177: "Storefronts contain a series of menu selections; a person simply has a push a button and whatever she wanted is delivered through a small tube to her location." Should be "has to push a button."

                                  p.177: "Each district contains one (or two, in the case of the central district) angel functioning as a governmental unit." Parenthetical placement renders the singular "angel" awkward; suggest moving it between "angel" and "functioning."

                                  p.177: "The overseer angel is the only angel operatives have any access to their cell leaders." Odd sentence; suitable correction unclear.

                                  p.181: "If the first state is not restored within 24 hours, processes being to die permanently, starting with the city’s human population." Should be begin.

                                  p.183: It may be preferable to sort the Attributes available to the Wired Merit in an order other than alphabetical.

                                  p.184: "Stigmatics do not get nearly as much high -evel maintenance, but do undergo after-action reports, performance reviews, and sometimes must contend with early retirement if it seems they are on track to defection." Should be high-level.

                                  p.185: "If they do, she leads them to underground connection of rogue stigmatics." Odd sentence; phrase should probably be "to an underground collection of rogue stigmatics."
                                  Last edited by Satchel; 01-11-2016, 12:10 PM.
