See, for all you know, you are the only demon around, and the general aether in the region (if there is any aether on the region) is there because of essence use. And you are in hostile territory, with a force overwhelmingly more powerful just waiting for you to commit a mistake, any mistake.         I am thinking about running the game, and I can see the potential for players turtling up completely. The way I see, it's better to take the vampire solution: a demon knows a demon when he sees one. All others can be spoofed. And that's it.

                There's paranoia, there's complete paranoia, and then there's "Fuck this, this is stupid, let's take some shots"

                Demons aren't blind, and they aren't stupid. They can apply their own habits to others and begin looking for incongruities. Demons and Angels can't always perfectly hide-if Demons could, compromise wouldn't be an issue, and if angels could, demons would really be fucked. So a demon is going to see something sooner and later, and they can act on it-follow them, find out information, and set something up.

                Every contact is a gamble, yes. You think you found a demon who could help, turns out it's a convincing angel, or a demon who'd have no problem screwing you over. But that's the nature of the game-you have to take some chances if you're gonna do anything but sit on your ass. And even if you do sit on your ass, Demonic nature demands inevitable changes in Cover. That might be covered by regular XP, but often times you'll need to make a pact, and that's a blip of standing the hell out, just enough for people to notice.

                Sooner or later, someone is going to see you, and regardless of them being friend or foe, the situation is in their hands. You might as well be proactive, take some chances, and try and make friends at gunpoint. That way, even if you did screw it up, you have more control over the scenario.

                Rebellion is a gamble. Someone who doesn't want to play the game should never have broken the rules in the first place.

                            ERGO. If you suspect that someone is a demon, you can attempt to dig up the dirt on them. If you succeed, you can cause compromise or have enough leverage to get them to be honest with you.

                            It is also worth it to note that a demon knows when their cover has been compromised. HOWEVER the demon doesn't necessarily know the circumstances around the compromise.

                            So if a mortal for whatever reason used this system to come up with enough information to compromise the demon AND the demon fails their compromise roll the demon becomes aware that their cover was compromised and nothing else.
