    Help with a potential story-hook.

    06-20-2016, 03:15 PM
    "The God-Machine is vast, nearly omnipresent. Its Essence suffusing every aspect of technology and infiltrating the very laws of the universe both material and metaphysical, the great Machine, that near all-powerful enemy of the Unchained is as integral to the functions of the world as the Incarnae of the spirit world or the unknowable Tyrants that rule the Supernal, enforcing the laws of what some beings whisper as the “Lie” of reality. It is unthinkable to imagine a world where the God-Machine is no longer present, aside from those demons who wish otherwise, for the fear of what would happen if that were the case. It is already been documented that when particularly vital Gears are destroyed natural disasters occur in the region with semi-regular validity, so what sort of catastrophes would happen if the Machine was wholly absent? Such apocalyptic musings are slightly rare among the thoughts of those who would see the Machine destroyed forever, though it does stay the hands of those who are more level headed from wantonly destroying every piece of Infrastructure they see – until they figure out it's purpose first, that is.

    Which is why no one, angel nor demon, was prepared for when the Machine suddenly.. left.

    To this day no one knows the reason why for this event to come about, but there are rumors, always rumors. Some believe that some central Command & Control Infrastructure that governed the overall “ consciousness” of the God-Machine was finally discovered and destroyed by an ancient Agency called Terminus Est; a confederation of fallen Swords who have spent over a thousand years searching for what they referred to as the Ein Sof Nexus – the source of all potential Infrastructure past, present, and future. Without it, The God-Machine forever lost the ability to construct new pieces of itself, summon/build new angels, or affect the universe beyond what Infrastructure was already in place before the loss of its most vital node. So it shut down, with only the most critical pieces of still extant Infrastructure remaining functional as a sort of “life support” for the now comatose God.

    Another rumor is that the God-Machine had perfected interplanetary/intragalactic translocation and discovered a new planet that possessed conditions perfect for the development of intelligent life, and thus determined that the time had come to pick up Its roots and relocate to this new, pristine alien world – a world ideal for the construction of a new occult playground. As such whatever machinery, spiritual essence, or any other source of the God-Machine's “soul”was relocated along with whatever angelic/supernatural/mortal assets It would require in order to build anew – a sort of Black Box, a seed to birth the God-Machine's new “body”. Recognizing the tremendous investment that Earth represented, The Machine elected to keep most of it's assets in place for the eventuality that It would decide to return “home”, so the vast majority of the orphaned Infrastructure entered an indefinite “standby” mode in the meantime – though when that will happen is entirely unknowable. Some Inquisitors and particularly delusional exiled Psychopomps posit that this is how the God-Machine originally came to Earth, and the universe is filled with thousands, if not millions of planets that once served as “hosts” for the techno-spiritual gestalt that is the God-Machine – each world being a semi-autonomous node in the neurological grid that represents the totality of the God-Machine's being.

    And yet a third rumor states that, much like the previous above speculation, the God-Machine decided it was time to move on from the cradle that birthed it. Instead of merely migrating to a different planet or a new galaxy, however, but rather set Its sight on an entirely different universe outside of the one It currently inhabited. This rumor seems to possess slightly more credibility than the previous two, as for the past few years rings all over the planet reported more and more Infrastructure dedicated to manipulating the forces of space and time than what has been present before, and as such some who believe in the previously mentioned “Black Box Seed” theory firmly believe that the God-Machine managed to break into a new reality – one that It is currently engineering for Its own designs. This theory also includes the thoughts that all earth bound Infrastructure is in a sort of sleep-mode in preparation for the return of it “mind”, though that statement is somewhat less substantial among those who discuss it. Either way, the Machine seems to have decided upon creating a future for itself that does not include the Earth – not anymore.

    Regardless as to how or why, the reality of the situation remains a disconcerting and terrifying one for all those who remain behind. Nearly all Infrastructure that remains on Earth is either non operational, in a sort of “ sleep mode” where it is still functional, yet is no longer operating, or remains in operation, but is now in a sort of autonomy mode where it merely performs its task with no communication with the Machine ( as is the case with disaster averting Infrastructure such as the Apocalypse Vault in the 1999 Splinter timeline of Seattle). So while the Earth is in no real danger of collapsing in the immediate future, there are two complications that leave the continued existence of the planet and all life upon it in doubt. The first of these complications is the most immediately pressing is that at the moment of the God-Machine's ( for lack of a better term) shut down every single active and dormant angel lost their connection to their creator. While some Fell at this moment, those individuals had been on the precipice before the Event happened, thus had no reason to remained Shackled.

    For the vast majority of angels, however, the first hours of this disconnect were complete chaos. Hundreds of thousands of voices suddenly had no line to their Parent, no Mission updates or even Missions for those exiles who began awakening in their storage facilities – they were alone. Some vainly attempted to complete their Missions that they were assigned before hand, naively hoping that their master had only experienced a temporary lapse in it's communication abilities and not that the God-Machine was now silent, possibly forever. Others began lashing out in panicked fueled violence, attacking and razing anything that could possibly be responsible for this inconceivable occurrence, no matter how remote or illogical. These children were possibly the worst affected by the loss of their God, and there were reports of angels in their hundreds destroying themselves after obliterating everything in immediate reach – unable to even consider existing without the God-Machine. Those more able to compose themselves were still grief stricken and confused, but they resolved themselves to still perform their duties to the Machine. While it's Voice was no longer present, it's Infrastructure was and by either destroying known and potential threats, spreading their insane messages, protecting ( now largely inactive) facilities, gather information on the functions of the “new” state of affairs, or altruistically gathering materials and repairing what Infrastructure that remained.. these slightly more level headed angels were dedicated to preserving the Order of the world as best as they were able.

    Still others sensed an opportunity. They sought out those outsiders who had long been exiled before, flocking to these lost brothers and sisters like frightened animals banding together in herds for protection against predators in order to learn how to function in a world without the guidance of their cold and unbiased Machine. These older exiles for the most part exulted in this change of the status quo – they had been pushed away or exploited for years, centuries in some cases, and jumped at the chance of being useful again. For the first time in the history of the world, exiles now had a chance to do what they never were able to accomplish: They could form a society of their own, they could become a force that carried true weight in a world that previously saw them as nothing more as cripples and nuisances. The possibilities for what could be accomplished were limitless, all one needed to do was finally reach for them. This would later lead to much greater implications and dangers, though in the beginning there was an alien feeling to be held in the mechanical hearts of many exiles: Hope.

    The second complication was that while numerous Infrastructures were still operation and continued to function at more or less optimal efficiency as they had before the God-Machine's directing " Will" disappeared, a rather large problem became known, albeit one that would not truly pose a danger for several months or even years. While the God-Machine was fully functioning these Infrastructures of import would be more or less constantly maintained to ensure they remained at peak operational functionality at all times lest the unthinkable happen and these systems fail. Well the unthinkable did happen, and each and every one of these Infrastructures were now operating with a decidedly limited lifespan in comparison to what was possible with the Machine's constant supervision. While some exiles, with the help of a number of ( rightly self interested) demons, worked diligently to keep of number of these pieces of occult technology running as stated previously, the lack of unified will or communication between loyalists, their faithful mortal cultists, and their sometimes demonic allies meant that a number of these pieces of the God-Machine's legacy are woefully unattended, and present a substantial danger to everything around them should the remain as such for too long. With a lack of arcane knowledge or the occult power to affect changes like their creator, most exiles and demons have begun to prepare for the worst. Efforts are being made to try to at least attempt to keep as many of these systems running for as long as possible, but with the loss of the greater whole of the God-Machine's gestalt consciousness it is unlikely that much can be done other than creating stop gap solutions to a problem that has no easy answer - not that it stops those resolute enough from trying their hardest. The Unchained have been hacking apart the Machine's processes and making the jury-rigged remains work for thousands of years already, surely if one throws enough metaphorical mud at the allegorical wall something will eventually stick - for better or worse.

    To say that the Unchained were shocked and in uproar in regards to this unprecedented occurrence is an understatement that puts the above descriptions of the confusion the angels to absolute shame, though that will come later. Tomorrow is a new day, and any day without the Machine is one that is both terrifying, yet filled with possibility and most importantly; Freedom. It is likely that it will never be known how this came to pass, but what can be said is that freedom is never without cost, and the price of being free can sometimes be worse than oppression – not that anything that is worth achieving is ever without pain and blood after all. The stability of the future may be in doubt, but there is still a chance of securing that future, all one needs to do is to reach out and take hold of it with their own hands.. even if it may lead to the loss of said hands to the inevitable sacrifice that comes with it."

    - Mr. Exposition, on matters considering the Exodus Event.

    ____

    And here is the initial concept of a plot hook I had for a game I have been planning for a new game group in town regarding a possible world where for some reason or another, the God-Machine largely shuts down and it's golden hosts are severed from their Father. Now I have other ideas how as to how the world functions in such a state, but I was wondering if any of you would be willing to help me with world building? I would greatly appreciate it and understand that many of you have incredible talents regarding storytelling, so I would be honored to receive your assistance on this project of mine!!. Thanks, and as always have a wonderful day!
