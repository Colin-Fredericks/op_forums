    Demon Form and Compromise Checks

    04-28-2014, 01:32 PM
    So.... when one uses partial transformation to cherry pick abilities, its obvious which Cover one keeps using for Compromise checks.

    When one Goes Loud, you've already committed the ultimate breach of Cover.


    However, the regular Demon Form? You don't have a Cover up, putting it effectively at 0. But you are still rolling Compromise checks. I'm assuming from powers, revealing information about yourself, and the like, depending. You absolutely still roll compromise for having it up for more than one scene. But.... what are you rolling compromise checks with? You don't have Cover up.

    What about the Cover Conditions you pick up as a result of compromise checks while in Demon Form? I'm assuming that its applied to the same one you roll. I had thought about rolling 10-Premium for Demon Form compromises as a dice pool, but

    This in-between form is confusing to me. It doesn't feel right, game wise, to me to be making checks while in the demon form, and yet there is call for it by the rules.

                  Originally posted by MCN View Post
                  Huh.... that really feels off to me. Like its being treated as an extended partial transformation. Mm....
                  Think of it like this: Demon's are themselves a foreign and alien entity to reality itself - if they simply exist reality does its damnedest to remove them - not the god machine, reality itself. Now, a demon is a strong enough entity to force its existence through pure force of will, but the process is kind of like the cosmic version of having a shouting match with your unruly roomate - you'll live to fight another day, but everyone nearby will know too.

                  So, when a demon transforms, they normally hold on to one last thing to dodge this cosmic shouting match - they hold on to their cover's name. It's not super effective: anyone dealing with them knows a giant monstrosity is tearing apart the neighbourhood, but reality is pretty sure that the conciousness in existence is little Timmy, not a beast with pincers in the fourth dimension.
