                                shadowhowler, that's a fantastic idea. I actually have a character idea for Demon that's been lying around that would fun to try:

                                A Psychopomp/Integrator called Mr. Door who's Cover is a freelance photographer called Vance P. Adams. Before the Fall he oversaw a Logistical Infrastructure that consisted of seven blown up photographs that served as portals to the location where the picture was taken. He Fell when he grew to fascinated with the worlds that his Infrastructure led to and went through one of them and was thus disconnected from the God Machine. He's chooses the Integrator Agenda because his journey back from the other world was filled with the fear of the unknown and the humbling realization of how weak he was by himself. His want to rejoin the God Machine so that he can again have access to his former powers and influence but he loves his new found freewill to much to give it up, so he intends to find a means of become an Exile so he can have the best of both world and no longer fear the unknown.
