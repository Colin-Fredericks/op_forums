                                        “Hello Dr. Carpenter, this is Gabe Norton, again. Sorry to bother but I was wondering if the fact that I was just informed there will be a large number of vampires gathering on campus tonight possibly have anything to do with the books?” I leaned against my car.

                                        There was a pause. “Not that I know of. Hargrave is aware of part of what’s going on, but he doesn’t really interact with the books. He knows they are gone, I don't know how much of an interest he has in them though.”

                                        I tapped my fingers on my car window. “Could he be the leak? Known enough to know where they were kept but not exactly which book to pull?”

                                        “It’s possible. I hope not. Dr. Hargrave is older than I am...” He let out a sigh. “He’s focused on mind and emotion control. I don’t know his powers list, but he’s been kicking around a bit, moving from university to university, fudging records for his age. We leave him alone, and we get to ask him questions about weird things he might have come across. Also, student corpses don’t show up. It would be out of character for him to compromise the university, unless he really wanted or needed something in there.”

                                        “I getcha.” I stood up and started pacing a little. “As a question, so I don’t have to bother you every time I hear any little thing, do you mind telling me who all is involved in watching over the books?”

                                        “The only people who really know are myself, Dr. Hazard and Dr. Isaacs, the one who fled to Iowa. He's a magic using shapeshifter.” He chuckled. “Somewhat ideal to finish out our trio, though he's not the same... sort of shapeshifter as I am. Dr. Hazard is fairly certain he did not take them, however. Others know it as a very special collection, donors, etc.”

                                        I nodded. “So if someone else shows up knowing more than that, there would be a cause for concern.”

                                        He made an affirmative noise. “Dr. Hargrave only knows as part of the deal, him being left alone and kept informed, and us having an old, powerful resource for consulting.”

                                        “How does he get along with Stein?” I flipped my silver dollar in the air and caught it.

                                        “They avoid each other.” He thought a moment. “I don’t believe I’ve ever seen them together. They are rather opposites, since Stein wants to be involved with absolutely everything and Hargrave mostly wants to be left alone.”

                                        I shifted the phone to my other shoulder. “Fair enough. Well, again, sorry to bother you, it’s just the timing seemed fucking suspicious, and I realized I didn’t know who on campus should or shouldn’t know things. Thanks for the heads up.”

                                        “No problem.” He cleared his throat. “A further heads up - part of the deal is that if someone messes with him, we will back him up, and vice versa. He’s more likely to get help from me than Dr. Hazard - Hazard’s a little leery of him because he’s a vampire. Also, he’s told us about some other entity on campus, along those lines. He was cagey about what it was, but he said it didn’t think it was going to affect us. Called it a feeder.”

                                        I chuckled to myself. Not sure Clytemnestra would like that name. “Gotcha. Thanks again.” I called the rest of them and passed along everything to them, including my suspicions.

                                        “I wonder if they are who he was threatening me with. I assumed other vampires, but...” Naomi mused. “He did just say ‘other powerful people’. I’m not sure about being called a feeder. I guess it works as a taxonomy though.”

                                        “It’s more of a classification, really,” Billy Mack pointed out.


                                        There was time before sundown, so we all went to Whacko Bob’s to go memory fishing. On the way there, I got a text from Liz, ‘Ha ha, Naomi is so horny-pissed at Nick right now. It’s aggravating to be rattling around in her head, but it’s kinda hilarious from an outside perspective.’ I shook my head and rolled my eyes at Naomi when I passed her in the shop.

                                        Mary was there at the shop as expected, and Billy Mack immediately got to flirting. I started browsing, watching her through my sunglasses. There was way more resistance getting in than was usual, but I did manage to get eyes on her surface thoughts, which were pretty goddamn focused on how things were in order and that was good, and it was good that they were in order. A little obsessive for my taste, but to Billy Mack his own, for fucking sure.

                                        Going back for specific memories was difficult, like wading through molasses. There was nothing human about her thought processes - she was not and never had been human. I got there in time, finding a memory of Gabe the trench-coated sunglasses wearing guy. It didn’t help that ‘now’ for her was any time in the past decade or so. A lot of the time I spent looking was just being more precise than that.

                                        I did manage to get that the most recent time he’d come in was earlier today. He was in good spirits, and greeted Mary warmly as he started wandering around the store, whistling ‘Every Breath You Take’. I made a note all the shit he bought. It didn’t mean anything to me, but I figured that Billy Mack would be able to make something of it. I also got a credit card name and number, but the name was about a mile long and Greek. Despite its length, ‘Gabe’ couldn’t be gotten from any of it, so I didn’t have a hell of a lot of hope for it being his real name.

                                        I wasn’t really one to talk, but the man was a stickler for his sunglasses. At no point in any of the memories I got of him, Mary’s or Amy’s, did he ever show his eyes. Still, ‘doesn’t show his eyes’, ‘black’, ‘knows his way around Whacko Bob’s’ and ‘answers to Gabe’ doesn’t get you very far on an identity search. I mean, half of them apply to me most of the time, and I was working on another quarter, what with all the visits lately. I had seen him at Kiki’s before, so there was that.

                                        Because it was a day for satisfying curiosity, I went looking into her background, into that sword that Billy Mack said she was afraid of. I got a faraway memory of an Icelandic looking fucker who’d summoned away from soaring through the Shadow and bound her exceedingly powerful self into the form she had now, setting her to the task of guarding a particular chalice with Hindu symbology on it. The sword was the focus of her binding and served as a Bane for her. As long as she kept it safe, he said, fate would conspire to keep the sword away from her. If she lost it, fate would ensure it found her in the worst way. The deal was to guard it for 1001 years, then he would come and collect it and summon another guardian. A brush with her surface thoughts with the added context let me know she had 546 years, 11 months and 8 days of service left, before she could go home and be the apex predator she was supposed to be.

                                        I was losing focus, but while I was in there, and by no fucking means guaranteed to be able to get back in again, I decided to check on Whacko Bob himself. He was a raggedy looking bastard, definitely not the same Icelandic gee, with a color scheme to blind a bat. He’d left her to watch the shop while he went acquiring things, promising to be back in a year or two. He did know about her task with the chalice.

                                        Billy Mack got out of there with a pair of watermelons. I bought a few things for Miranda, things that looked like the sort of supplies she kept stocked and a couple of things that looked nice, at least as nice as my budget allows. When we got to the car, I grinned at Billy Mack. “She is way out of your league, pal.”

                                        “I don’t know about that,” he protested.

                                        I passed along what I learned. “She was far more interested in everything being in place than any part of your conversation.”

                                        He shrugged. “That just makes sense. It’s not as if she is human. Or as if I am attempting to engage her in a long term relationship after all.” I shook my head and got back to the task at hand. When I passed along the supply list, Billy Mack thought for a moment. “He is most likely a Hedge maze of the Apostle of the Dark One variety. They call upon a greater power for their magical paradigm, usually one of an odd or sinister nature. That particular set of ingredients would likely be for some sort of divination.”

                                        “This is my shocked face.” I smirked at him.


                                        Naomi took the lead on the vampire situation, watching Hargrave’s office and planning to split off into spiders and scattering herself around the meeting. The rest of us loitered vaguely nearby, trying to get eyes on who the fuck showed up. At the least, we could likely figure out which of the three factions Hargrave was with.

                                        Naomi let us know when Hargrave left, and when he arrived at the admin building, but of fucking course that’s where they were meeting. We managed to get into place in time to see Valefar’s vampire contact, and Sanderson’s pet mind fucker walk in, pegging this meeting - and Sanderson’s mind fucker - firmly in the young punks’ camp. At least, if this were a political meeting.

                                        Spiders do in fact make pretty damn good spies, so after the vampires all dispersed, and Naomi undispersed herself, she was able to give us a pretty good accounting. “Short version - he didn’t take the book. Long version - he let folks know that the books were stolen, and was wondering if a group called the Ordo Dracul have them, as they were interested in them at one point in time.”

                                        “That’s the magic ones, the ones who Langley works for,” I mused.

                                        She nodded. “If someone can determine whether they have it, he will give them a boon. If someone just finds them, he might be able to work out a boon, but it’s only guaranteed if it’s about the Ordo Dracul. There was other business I could parrot, but nothing that meant anything to me.”

                                        I walked a coin over my knuckles. “Was he the bossman?”

                                        She shook her head. “He was just hosting. They rotate meeting places, apparently, and he’d asked for it to be here, since he had the business about the books. They don’t seem to have a real leader.”

                                        “Well damn it.” I stood up for the bench.

                                        Billy Mack stood as well. “It does rule him out.”

                                        I shrugged. “Which is good, I guess. And we do have contacts with the Ordo Dracul. Not great ones, but...”

                                        “It does seem like something Langley is not likely to be privy to.” Billy Mack nodded. “We would have to actually speak to the vampires in question, who may or may not be inclined to share such information with us.”

                                        “And may be inclined to shove us in a box for asking questions about things we shouldn’t know about,” Jack pointed out.

                                        Naomi frowned. “I don’t like that.”

                                        I snorted. “We could always trade them one of the fucking Angels in my bolthole.”

                                        “As long as we’re not giving them a live one. I could use a bite.” Naomi pouted. “In the meantime, I have Capri Sun.” She matched actions to words and started sucking on the fucking straw.

                                        “Assuming we’re comfortable assisting in their studies in such a direction,” Billy Mack looked to me.

                                        I shrugged. “I was mostly kidding. The ones we have left are mostly low ranking. They might not be worth enough, but they’re probably not too big a risk to us. Anyway, it’s a direction to go. But that’s tomorrow’s shit to deal with.”

                                        I got a text from Liz. ‘Naomi’s probably gonna go strike out with Nick, then go seduce someone else to get her self esteem up. Unless you guys need me, and then I’ll have to switch to Liz to get her away from here.’

                                        “I think all our shit is tomorrow’s shit, as far as I’m concerned. I need to hit the hay. You guys do whatever the fuck you feel like.” I waved, and headed back to my car.



                                        I did get to sleep eventually. It turns out Miranda did kinda like the presents I’d bought, which were definitely nothing but a romantic gesture that also happened to back up my detective little-c cover. They certainly weren’t to prove that I’m a much better partner than any old flame who may have suddenly learned she’s still alive, who'd obviously never bothered to fucking check properly. Why would you ask?

                                        End Session LIX
