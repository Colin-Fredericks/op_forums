    Homebrewed Incepts

    06-08-2016, 11:24 AM
    Hey all, how are all of you? As you all know, I am a firm believer in making angels as formidable as possible.. so during work last night I had angels on the brain, and came up with a Incept that I really enjoyed, so I thought I'd share it with you today, and would ask if any of you have also made any custom Incepts to supplement the ( admittedly small number of) existing ones.

    It should be noted right away that going into this Incepts I had decided to make it as potent as possible in order to capture their status as Root-Access codes granted by a God, while still trying to be somewhat fair to demons or other potential enemies. It is stated that Exploits are naught but crude shadows of Incepts and as a result they should be pretty damn powerful. Sadly while canon Incepts are undeniably strong, they are not quite as good as the devs hyped them up to be - certainly not powerful or blatant enough to warrant the risk of Falling unless used in the most egregious manner possible. So I wanted to make Incepts that were a bit more frightening, the kinds of occult tools that if used by an angel during a confrontation could utterly shift the balance of power to their favor during the engagement.

    Without further ado, enjoy!
    ______

    Galvanize

    Once activated Galvanize allows the angel to spend Essence to generate a number of temporary ( "Galvanized") points of Corpus equal to Rank + 1 for the remainder of the scene( Exampiel 1.0 would gain a meager two points of temporary Corpus, Exampiel 3.0 would gain four, and Exampiel 5.0 would gain six.). Should the angel suffer damage from attack these temporary Corpus points are lost first; acting as a form of ablative shielding against damage of all types - damage inflicted by an angel's Bane bypasses the protection offered by Galvanize, however. In addition, Galvanize may be utilized multiple times per scene with an expenditure of Essence, and the angel in question may spend as many points of Essence their Rank allows each turn for the purpose of reinforcing their spiritual body with this protective "layer" ( Exampiel 3.0 has activated Galvanize earlier during a confrontation with a skilled Ring of Unchained, and over the course of the fight utilizes the Incept again just before the last of her Galvanized Corpus is burned away, spending all three points of Essence in order to gain twelve additional points of temporary corpus levels).

    The secondary effect of Galvanize allows the angel to add their Rank+1 to their Resistance Score in order to resist supernatural powers more effectively, and by spending an additional point of Willpower after activation may bestow another being with Galvanized Corpus points, though these temporary health/corpus levels cannot be additionally reinforced with replacements until the initially bestowed points expire or are expended.

    ________

    Well there you are! As always your comments are welcomed, and I would love to hear of any homebrewed Incepts you guys have come up with!

                              Rough notes so far. Probably needs better formatting and ordering.

                              Division

                              Once activated, Division allows an Angel to splinter into 4 other Angels, one Rank lower, by paying 4 Essence. These Angels are constructed using the original as a baseline (using its Attribute spread, albeit at lower values, selecting Numina and Manifestations from its list, even Incepts from those it has available) with the exception of being barred from possessing the Discorporate Manifestation. The duplicates have the same original personality, mission, Ban, and Bane (though the Ban and Bane may become more generalized as Rank decreases, if the Storyteller wishes). The original's current Essence and damage are split amongst the copies at the time they are generated. If the copies are created with the Division Incept, they can even splinter themselves further, to a minimum Rank of 1. 4 copies of the same Rank can merge to return to their original Angel as an Instant action. These copies lose 1 Corpus per day. Each copy destroyed incurs 1 extra day before the original Angel can fully reincorporate. The original Angel can only be destroyed permanently if all its copied are depleted of Essence and their Corpus filled with Aggravated damage.

                              The secondary effect of Division allows damage to be split amongst willing allied beings, with at least half of the damage inflicted (to a minimum of 1) remaining with the Angel. This damage is the same type (Bashing, Lethal, Aggravated) as would be inflicted upon the Angel, and cannot be reduced by Armor, though it can be healed as normal for its type afterwards.

                              Thoughts? For reference, a Rank 5 Angel could split into up to 256 Rank 1 shards. Each would individually be quite weak, but they can be a one-Angel flash mob. The ban on Discorporation is to prevent the Angel from immediately Discorporating one of their copies as "insurance". This is still likely overpowered, even for Incepts, but I'm not sure what exactly to do to weaken it but still allow the exponential growth factor at higher Ranks.
