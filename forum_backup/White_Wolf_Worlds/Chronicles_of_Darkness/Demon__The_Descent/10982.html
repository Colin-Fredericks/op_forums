    Issues with Embeds

    11-14-2013, 10:04 PM
    So don't know how receptive this is going to be but I currently have an issue with embeds. There are a few noticeable ones that I'll post later but my issue is both being free and reflexive. Maybe if they were all like just bruised where that has a limited number of uses in a scene but then there are others like turn blade which can be used five times a turn without a problem where things just get really silly. Then there's knockout punch which looks like it currently works on any being regardless of magical resistance. Which also brings me to the issue of why (Powerstat) doesn't apply to any of the powers that try to affect magical targets when it applies to a lot of powers across the board with other splats.

    Also, since embeds are exploiting the world naturally what happens when it's contesting something something blatantly magical. Like using Just Bruised against an astra or magical item that says it kills anything it touches or does an infinite number of successes? What happens when you try to erase the identity of someone capable of mystical forging one or unable to have theirs taken away? Do embeds just fail against these (Extremely niche and incredibly rare situations admittedly but I'd need to cross reference too many powers when I'm not feeling very well.)

          In case of conflict, you'd use the power versus power rules as normal.

          As for embeds often being reflexive actions, no where in the rule book does it say you can do an infinite amount of reflexive actions a turn.

          The book says "reflexive actions are best considered defensive or reactionary," and "are resolved immediately after the instigating action or attack."

          So I'd say they can only do one active use of embeds a turn, as opposed to using defensive or reactionary embed use.

          But while that doesn't address turn blade, it's not like it's of infinite use. First of all, using it five times a turn is five times it can botch on the player, the attacker must be in line of sight and the demon has to know it's coming, and turn blade will never remove all damage by itself. Barring exceptional successes, the demon is going to take a minimum of five points of lethal damage, or enough bashing damage to knock them out. Being beaten down or knocked out is better than dead, sure but the demon is still in serious trouble there.

                                  I think someone (can't remember the name now) in another thread mentioned the fact that a lot of Embeds have "sweeping" (there word not mine) effects that are resisted by subtracted mechanics (e.g. - Stamina, Resolve, Composure, Defense).

                                  To my understanding it's the province of resistant mechanics (e.g. vs Resolve + Blood Potency) to oppose powers that have an everything or nothing affect. like a lot of Embeds. While powers that have increments of effect (e.g. each success = a point of damage) are opposed by subtracted mechanics. In addition, its rare that subtracted mechanics include a supernatural power stat in them, instead just using an attribute or derived stat. Meanwhile resistant mechanics almost always do involve a supernatural resistance trait.

                                  I was wondering, given this detail, if that was a purposeful design choice or not? Since previously in the other game lines the standard way a power is resisted was as I stated above. All or nothing affects are resisted and incremental affects are subtracted.

                                  Is the idea here that a lot of Embeds effect the Demon rather then the target directly. So as a result the supernatural nature of the opponent simply does not come into the equation? Alternatively, is it the idea that Embeds are functions of reality and therefore are supported by the the very structure of the NWoD, allowing them to sidestep or overpower a supernatural resistance trait?

                                  Basically, given the mechanical observations, I'm curious to know if there is an in-game reason why all or nothing powers are being given subtracted defenses rather then resistant ones.

                                                  If you think a supernatural resistance roll for powers like Knockout Punch is appropriate, add one (my recommendation would be Stamina + Supernatural Tolerance). There's a section in Demon about why the Embeds work the way they do. You might not agree, but the choices aren't random.

                                                  The other thing is, I'm not interested in white-room balance. I'm interested in making compelling stories and with what rules do to the narrative. I'm not seeing what the problem is. Is the problem that a demon could hypothetically be a raging, unstoppable combat monster? Is the problem that a demon could theoretically punch out an elder vampire or something?

                                                  If the first...fine. The demon is a raging, unstoppable, combat monster. What does that mean for the story? Did the player communicate his/her desire to the Storyteller to be a RUCM, and will the Storyteller work that into the chronicle in a cool way? If not, why not? Is the player attempting to "win" the game by doing that? In that case, expectations need to be communicated more clearly.

                                                  If the second, put that into context for me. Why is the demon punching a vampire? Why is that a problem for the players and the Storyteller? Is the player just doing that because s/he can, effectively trolling the group? If so, that is not a game mechanics problem.
