    Finding Keys

    06-13-2017, 05:21 AM
    Throughout the course of the chronicle, every time the
    character gains a Beat in a scene involving angels, demons,
    stigmatics, or the God-Machine, the player makes a reflexive
    Intelligence + Wits roll. If she is following her Agenda at the
    time (and the player makes a convincing case for this to the
    Storyteller), the player can add her Primum to the roll.
    Am I missing something, or in the default format of the game (where the players are all demons) doesn't this basically mean that anytime you get a beat you get to roll to figure out your next key, since you're presumably with the other players?

    That seems...dumb. This would lead to a player fully filling out their Cipher and hitting Primum 4 within 6 sessions.

                              Originally posted by lnodiv View Post
                              All of this is irrelevant. A scene still 'involves demons' even if all of the demons are in Cover and acting totally mundane. If I have an Aspiration to crack open a cold one with the boys, and we all roll down to the bar and grab a drink, and I get a beat, if 'the boys' are demons, I get closer to finding my keys per RAW.
                              "Crack open a cold one with Mr. Molotov and the crew" is well within spec for Tempters and other socially-oriented demons and moving the game to the point where your characters have made a coordinated meeting as demons as a meaningful story beat is perfectly fine for justifying a roll to get a hint.

                              You can accomplish the same thing with an Aspiration of "Avoid angelic detection" after a local demon draws down a manhunt by not breaking character when the hunters go door-to-door asking after suspicious individuals. You can accomplish the same thing by having an Aspiration to bond with your Offspring. It's an aggregation mechanism for Things Of The Machine, not a means of solely encouraging overt risk over the lower-key risk of keeping close to the Demiurge's works.

                              The Cipher is mechanically completely incidental to most aspects of the game's subtlety-oriented half. It's one of the tempting aspects of the loud side of the game, but you can go through a whole game without pursuing it. If you want to discourage Cipher hints stemming from the quieter and less overtly supernatural means of Beat acquisition that can prompt the roll, the fact that it's a roll means you have penalties at your disposal, or flatly reducing the roll to one Attribute.

                              It's a character self-discovery mechanic that requires you to spend Experiences on supernatural powers to make proper use of and if you've bought more than one extra dot of Primum then completion promises to nudge the character into the superhuman-drawbacks segment of the chart. Characters that take a more measured approach will see more narrative slack freed up from not rushing the investigation process and characters that jump right in will have to balance a more concrete goal with less resources on every other front.

                              I didn't say the prospect of gaming Aspirations to this end wasn't dumb, I said it was no more dumb than a hypothetical game of Mage where you maintain an Obsession of "find seven new Mysteries in a week" and then spend the whole story poking around the Realms Invisible to rack up Mana and Arcane Experience. You know what you're going to do to resolve this problem that occurs from a technical reading of a mechanic in a particular playstyle that doesn't lean heavily on most of the genre-widgets to maintain pressure on the PCs. There's no need to state the obvious.
