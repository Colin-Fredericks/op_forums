                                Originally posted by Maina View Post
                                "Clearly" seems a strange word to use here: the mention of Firestorms in the "Wasteland" section explicitly says they clear away a Wasteland and may be triggered towards that end.
                                It says the reverse: that you can deliberately call down a Firestorm to clear a Wasteland, which the mechanics establish by "Firestorm" being the final category of Wasteland advancement, which may be deliberately triggered by enacting enough Festering triggers to get it to that level. Firestorms can happen without a Wasteland being involved at all.

                                I also cannot find anywhere that says that the reduced Structure/Durability are lingering?
                                They don't set in immediately — they're applied to stuff brought into the area over the course of the first day — and the destructive impact of many Aspects makes it a moot point a lot of the time.

                                And the "Firestorms" section twice says that they clear away Wastelands and one of those times explains that this is a reason Prometheans may want to trigger one on purpose. It is hardly "clear" that this is not something that happens every time. In fact, I would say it's clear that it does clear Wastelands every time; there is no place that says it does not.
                                "The Firestorm" section in Chapter Two also says that Disquiet is banished in the wake of a Firestorm despite "Disquiet is either ended or triggered" being a starting quality listed under the Azothic Element held by Wasteland Festering-induced Semnai Firestorms.

                                The description of Firestorm Catalyst Athanors, also in Chapter Two, says they can be used to set off a Firestorm with a "slightly improved" chance of cleansing a Wasteland, which wouldn't make much sense if Firestorms consistently cleansed Wastelands.

                                The Firestorm section's opening text describes them killing Pandorans, and while a Level 4 Extreme Environment can certainly accomplish that, most Firestorms last less than ten turns, most Pandorans will have at least two dots of Stamina, and "all Pandorans take aggravated damage" is a starting effect listed under Lilithian Element Firestorms.

                                The subsection on Scale provides the example of qashmallim manifesting with either centered or widespread Firestorms, which allows for the possibility of a Firestorm only clearing out part of a Wasteland.

                                Finally, the Firestorm section emphasizes the unpredictability of Firestorms — there's the breadth of Aspects they can possess along with the usual note on the list of example triggers that that list is neither exhaustive nor guaranteed — and provides mechanics that all but guarantee a Firestorm triggered by a Promethean Festering a Wasteland to the fifth stage will have enough Strength to apply at least two aftereffects from the list that includes clearing Wasteland effects, ending Disquiet, and severely damaging Pandorans.

                                It's Pyros. It's volatile and made of change, and a Firestorm is undirected change at that. You can try to stack the deck, but there are no guarantees when playing with the Divine Fire.
