    I think it would be a good idea to look at Demon's Demonic Form, considering that most of the mechanics would work similarly. Effectively, a Siren's Aquatic Form is akin to the Demon's Demonic Form in that it allows them to use all of their Alterations. However, a Siren can partially transform her body and only use some of her Alterations. For Demons, they have Modifications, Technologies, Propulsions, and Processes. They gain a fixed number when they are created and gradually develop more once they hit a certain amount of Primum. They may also lose and gain an amount of abilities when they gain Primum.

    However, most to all of the Siren's Alterations are passive with some allowing the Siren to perform special actions tied into her Alterations, like an ink pouch that allows the Siren to fire out smoky clouds of ink into the air. In this instance, the Alterations are more like Beast's Atavisms as they are something that is fundamental to the Siren. I feel that the main difference is that while supernatural creatures can see a glimpse of the Beast's true form when they activate their Atavism, the Siren's true form is only present when she becomes submerged in water. Another difference is that while a Beast's Atavisms are free action-based abilities that are free to use unless the Beast wants to enhance them using their Satiety, a Siren's Alterations are ingrained trait-based abilities that she may awaken with her Pneuma and keep awakened for a period of time.

    However, the longer a Siren has her Alteration active, the weaker the Refraction that protects the Siren from being exposed grows. A few minutes of having her Alteration up is no issue; allowing her Alteration to last for hours causes her true nature to rise up every now and then, with scales causing her skin to reflect in the sun or her teeth sharpening to that of piranhas. I would say that every scene beyond the scene the Siren activates her Alteration necessitates a roll by the Siren to keep a hold of the Refraction effect, with a penalty per scene equal to the amount of Alteration the Siren has active and allowing the penalty to be cumulative and gradually growing the longer the Siren has the Alterations active.

    As for what her Aquatic Form allows the Siren to have, the most-obvious abilities would be to have the Siren breathe underwater and swim, possibly at twice her Speed. She would also have all her Alterations active and able to be used. The Siren's vulnerability to losing limbs also disappears; while the Siren is in her Aquatic Form, her limbs do not dissolve into sea foam. If the Siren loses a body part while she is in her Aquatic Form, she may retrieve her limb and attempt to reattach it as long as the Siren and her limb are in the same body of water; if her limb is thrown onto land, the Siren may attempt to bring it back into the water. The internal logic is that, because the Siren is connected to The Deluge and the waters of time, the Aquatic Form prevents the limb from dissolving by halting time for the limb and turning time back to the moment when the limb was removed. However, if the limb dissolves into sea foam or if the Siren leaves the water and regresses into her human form, the limb will not attach without actual medical assistance. Effectively, the Siren's Aquatic Form is in-and-of-itself an Alteration that is only awakened when the Siren is submerged in water.

        ​Seconded. Also, How to enter Deluge? with the whole body submerged in water? If you're in a familiar Water, you receive bonuses?
        The Deluge is a water-based apocalypse that is going to occur in the next few years, decades, centuries, etc. This event awakens the Sirens, who are the remnants of humanity that have been swept up by The Deluge and survived by swimming up to the Surface. Realizing that this disaster was, in at least some sense, preventable if humanity itself had done differently. This allowed them to transcend time and return to what is considered to be present time and live in an alternate life. Of course, this means that Sirens were created several years before The Deluge could occur, so they are attempting to preemptively prevent it by altering events. When a Siren uses her powers in the form of Verses or Alterations, she is attempting to connect to The Deluge on a mental level, calling upon a further reincarnation of themselves and tapping into the Verses and Alterations their future selves know and have.

        When a Siren goes into her Aquatic Form by submerging herself, she is tapping into what her future incarnation appears to be. The process of submerging her body is relatively simple; as long as a reasonably large portion of her body is covered in some form of water, she may transform into her Aquatic Form. This can go anywhere from bathing in a bathtub, taking a shower, skinny-dipping in a lake, standing out during the rain, taking a mud-bath, or going into a steamy sauna. Considering that this is rather inconvenient at some times, a Siren may spend a point of Pneuma to prevent her transformation within that form of water. If the Siren spends time in a hot sauna and goes into a pool after, she would have to spend two points of Pneuma to prevent the sauna and the pool, one at each moment.

        When a Siren submerges herself within her favorite Water and transforms into her Aquatic Form, she regains a substantial amount of Pneuma back; the quality of the water reminds her of her time within The Deluge and reminds her of the fight for survival. The Siren doesn't need to spend Pneuma to prevent her transformation in her favorite Water, but she needs to allow herself to transform if she wants to regain Pneuma. If a Siren chooses to not transform immediately, she may later transform if she wants to at will. This transformation will allow the Siren to regain Pneuma if it is her favorite Water.

                                                So, Serenity and Anchors. I want to say immediately that I feel like Sirens should not have split Touchstones that Werewolves do. A Siren's Serenity is something that allows her to face the world every day, despite knowing the looming danger that approaches with every misstep and mistake. A Siren's Serenity is something that grants the Siren clear judgment as a mediator between worlds. A Siren's Serenity is a calm that lulls before a mighty storm of judgment. Serenity is similar to Integrity in that both are used to determine mental stability and the ability to cope with a situation. However, while Integrity measures a mortal's state of mind when it comes to their self and their own person, a Siren's Serenity measures her state of mind when it comes to her own actions in the world around her. A Siren knows of the dangers that hang precariously over the world. She knows of the strained relationship next door that will lead to a suicide. She knows of the hungry earth that threatens to take the lives of mischievous trespassers. She knows of the deal that will pave the land in concrete and tip the scales of balance towards humanity. When a Siren is able to cope with these events, averting them by pushing others into action or accepting them as necessary events, she demonstrates the strength of her Serenity. When a Siren is unable to understand these events, failing to avert them, directly intervening, or accepting them as inevitable endings, she demonstrates weakness in her Serenity.

                                                The three main questions that define how an event affects a Siren goes as such:

                                                Why is this event worrisome in the grand scheme? Not all events need to be investigated. While helping children look for their missing pet is something that is nice to do, what would happen if the Siren were to fail? Are the consequences of the event occurring in some way dangerous or destructive? Will it lead to catastrophe or minor nuisance? When Sirens cannot look at a situation with a calm mind and factor in what aspects would cause a domino effect, they are showing a deep disconnect between their thought pattern and the world. Preventing a child's death is a great service to the world and the well-being of their family. Preventing a child from catching a cold is just plain outrageous. Sirens need to know what is important in the world and not worry about every unnecessary aspect. This question appeals to Logos, the logic behind the situation.

                                                What is the most just way to handle this event? Whenever a Siren intervenes by directing the pieces, they must know in what way it should be handled. When a debate is raised against two opposing sides, is it correct to agree with one side or the other? Do both sides have arguments that are valid? Do both sides have arguments that are invalid? Should emotions and alliances come into play? Is it right to split a reward evenly based on individuals or based on the level of work contributed? Is the destruction of a machine a justifiable means of stopping a forceful takeover? Should murder be considered if the situation calls for drastic measures? Sirens need to understand the gravity of a situation and whether or not it allows for certain judgments. This question appeals to Pathos, the emotion surrounding the situation.

                                                What is my right to judge this event? Not all events need a Siren to step in. When a family gets into a dispute over lifestyles, is it correct for the Siren to intervene? If the situation involves the Siren herself on one side of the argument, is she able to give an unbiased judgment? Is she able to if she isn't in the argument but naturally feels a certain way? Is she the best person to be an impartial judge? Will her experiences and emotions prevent her from acting correctly? Is there someone with more right than her to judge? Should Sirens be able to judge situations that directly involve her? Sirens need to realize their place in the world and figure out what their position as a mediator means. This question appeals to Ethos, the credibility that lends itself to the situation.

                                                Granted, there are several Breaking Points that may occur for a Siren at each different level of Serenity, but they all revolve around these three questions. Failing to resolve an issue might not affect a Siren at Serenity 7 as they are willing to accept losses, but a Siren at Serenity 10 would freak out as the failure breaks the Siren's composure. Similarly, choosing to knowingly kill a mortal for the sake of a better future may shake a Siren at Serenity 4, but it would take the murder of a mortal for the sake of regaining Pneuma to shock a Siren at Serenity 2. If a Siren has her Touchstone, a person, place, or object that reminds her of her duties and her necessity to remain calm, she gains a bonus to her breaking rolls.

                                                The Siren's position as a mediator is also defined by her Sea and Shore. A Siren's Sea is the persona she takes on when she has a moment to herself. It is something that ebbs and flows in her heart, allowing her to rest and be who she wants to be. It is an archetype that she allows herself to become lost in. A Siren's Shore is the persona she takes on when she needs to interact with the world. It is the meeting point where she sees others at their best, their worst, and whatever lies in between. It is an archetype that she puts on in order to blend with society.

                                                        Here's another snippet; this time, it's about the Frenzy/Death Rage equivalent of Sirens, Dissonance.

                                                        While not everything goes as planned, there are certain expectations that are held in the world. Leaves fall from trees, the weather changes every now and then, and people live their lives every day. For a Siren, a creature who spends most of her day making sure that schemes go through without much difficulty, meeting expectations is a priority. Matchmaking is expected to go off without a hitch, meetings are civil periods of discussion and debate, and everything coexists in peace. Life is meant to be as harmonious as it can.

                                                        When life isn't harmonious, a Siren's mind cannot accept it.

                                                        Sirens can accept failure as something that occurs naturally; not everything works out. However, when something has managed to reach a point where it is virtually impossible to fail, it triggers something in a Siren. This is impossible. This cannot be. This is unacceptable. A lover suddenly rejecting the Siren's affections after months of adoration, the death of a touchstone in a freak-accident that was only due to bad fortune, these fail to meet expectations. Love is meant to be forever. People are meant to die in a peaceful manner. This mental shutdown, this momentary denial of reality is known as Dissonance; the Siren's expectations of the world do not align with the reality of the world.

                                                        Depending on the strength of a Siren's character, Dissonance may go smoothly. On the other hand, Dissonance could cause a terrible fate to fall. A Siren must make a Resolve + Composure roll to maintain her calm and accept reality as it is. The most immediate effect is an overwhelming feeling of loss and disappointment that holds the Siren down. It is not as severe as the panic and sorrow that almost claimed the Siren in The Deluge, but it is something that is as consuming. Effectively, the Siren is suffering from Soul Loss; the Siren has become detached from reality and is attempting to reaffirm her position in the world. The Siren is affected by the Shallow End Condition. Similar to the persistent Soulless Condition, the Shallow End Condition prevents the Siren from regaining Willpower naturally through sleep. However, the Shallow End Condition also causes the Siren's Sea and Shore to invert; the Siren feels that the only way to reestablish her reality is to bring an aspect of her free will into the civilized world and to incorporate the facets of her humanity into her true self. This can have disastrous effects on the Siren's life, as a Siren with, say, an outlook on the world as an Enforcer but exists in her personal time as a Wild Card, plays with the people she knows in her everyday life as some sort of mischievous spirit and becomes a stone-heart judge in her personal sanctum. Acts that benefit her Sea or Shore during this period will only restore one point of Willpower even if it comes at a great risk as the Siren cannot fully enjoy her Sea and Shore when they are complete out of the Siren's normal behavior.

                                                        If a Siren is able to understand her position by gaining a point in Serenity, losing a point in Serenity, or a achieving an exceptional success on a breaking point for Serenity, she snaps out of her stupor and resolves the Shallow End Condition, releasing herself of her Dissonance. A Siren eventually breaks out of the Shallow End Condition after a set amount of time, depending on her Akasha. Sirens with low Akasha take longer to break out of her condition as her sense of time is closer-related to the flow of time in reality. Sirens with high Akasha accelerate the process as their sense of time is compounded by the timey-wimey mess of The Deluge. However, naturally breaking out of the Shallow End Condition isn't a good thing; rather, the Siren comes to the conclusion that her moment of suffering and panic meant nothing in the grand scheme of things. When a Siren resolves the Shallow End Condition by waiting it out, she begins to hear someone calling out to her. In her mind, the Siren feels as if she is hearing the Song once again, calling her to desire life and to live without fear. The voice itself is different, however. It no longer has notes of sorrow or bitterness. The words don't feel as if they are heralding the joy of life or the sweet taste of fresh air. It sounds more perfect, as if the singer has found complete clarity. The voice is does not fail in any means of the word. It just expresses pure understanding.

                                                        It is the voice of Echidna, speaking to the Siren.

                                                        Sirens who have resolved the Shallow End Condition through waiting it out receive the Deep End Condition. Sirens with this condition are still affected by their Dissonance with reality. However, while the Shallow End Condition affected the Siren's view of herself, the Deep End Condition affects the Siren's view of others. In this moment, the Siren is filled with a bitterness that grants her a mad insight. She sees the world around her not as clay waiting to be molded but as a chipped vase that has been completely ruined. At this moment, a Siren considers her Touchstones to be worthless, effecting her breaking point rolls as if she had no Touchstones. The Siren is also filled with a corrosive sense of envy against the world. How dare humanity be so content with their failings when it is bringing the world down. Do they not know the true meaning of their actions? How can nature rest peacefully when it struggles to even survive the progress of humanity? How pointless! The voice of Echidna whispers in the Siren's mind and directs them to find something that the Siren disagrees with. The saccharine smile of the girl in the other apartment. The students that pass by the forest and carve their initials into the trees. The untouched field that blooms every spring. These idealistic fonts that exist as if to mock the Siren's attempts to ward off the dangers that will come by defiantly enjoying the peace. The Siren becomes ill with malcontent as Echidna's voice encourages the Siren to defile these naive remnants of innocence. It is something the Siren must do to regain sense of reality. When a Siren effectively destroys or defiles the target of Echidna's encouragements, she may resolve the Deep End Condition and release herself of her Dissonance. However, the moment the Siren comes to her senses, she suffers from a breaking point as she realizes in full sobriety the consequences of her actions.

                                                        By spending Willpower, a Siren may attempt to fight against Echidna's influence by rolling Composure + Resove vs. the Siren's Akasha. Sirens with a high Akasha hear Echidna's whispers in full force as it echoes in her mind for what seems to be eternity amplified by her connection to the timelessness of The Deluge. Success allows the Siren to resolve the Deep End Condition without having to suffer a breaking point. Exposure to the Siren's Touchstones also allows the Siren to fight against Echidna's influences without spending Willpower; however, the Siren may only attempt to fight the effects once per Touchstone.

                                                        Granted, Dissonance only truly affects Sirens who follow the normal Currents, as those Sirens have not been fully influenced by Echidna. When it comes to Abyssal Sirens, who have fallen for Echidna's teachings, how does Dissonance affect them?

                                                        In short, Abyssal Sirens are in a period of perpetual Dissonance. Make of that as you will.
