    Need help with some Supernatural NPCs

    09-13-2016, 04:53 PM
    So, in my vampire game, my players casted a Divination ritual to find of other supernaturals in the area. Then they used another ritual to learn about them. 16 targets. In any case, this ritual also creates a link between the vampire and the target. So now we have 16 supernaturals who feel that someone/something is doing something evil to them (some ritual/magic). They are also capable of tracking this feeling to it's source.

    So now I'm looking to the forums to help me list these people and how will they react to the situation. I'm aiming towards 3-6 major splats, and the rest being minor supernaturals or stranger things (Wicked Dead types). So please, post your supernatural (the detailed the better) and how would he react to this strange feeling. Would he ignore it? Seek out the source? Run away? Do something of his own?
