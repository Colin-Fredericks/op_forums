                                        Originally posted by LokiRavenSpeak View Post

                                        Many would argue that the Ascension tried to be a game that did it all, yet repeatedly fell well short edition after edition, up to and including M20.

                                        Usually when you try to do and be everything to all people, nothing is done particularly well.

                                        I've been a fan of both iterations of Mage since Ascension's release in the mid 1990's, but it would be foolish not to acknowledge the myriad of setting and mechanical problems with Ascension that are near legendary. Ascension was and still is a groundbreaking game, but that doesn't necessarily mean it was a well designed and easily playable game.

                                        I respect that some may not like all or part of Awakening because it's not designed to accomplish want they want in a ttrpg. However, the determination of whether a game is well designed is whether it accomplishes its intended goals and the fans enjoy it, not whether some people effectively want a different game entirely.
