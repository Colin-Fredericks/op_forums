    The last Sect- the Black Flame. These guys also got a neat and tidy overhaul. Normally, they would likely be an Antagonist faction- as would the Children of the White Temple -but the opportunity to play a character like this is far too tempting to pass up. Picture a blend of the Refinement of Cobalt and Disquietism Transmutations. Imagine an Arisen, or even, say, a Leviathan capable of actually controlling and shaping Sybaris/the Wake mixed with the power of a Daeva's Awe. That is what the Dominion of Rapture does; it manipulates Yara directly. In the same way, the Dominion of Corruption does much of what the Contamination Transmutation class does. These Announced can be devils from your worst nightmares, or some of the hardest but most rewarding teachers.

    The Black Flame

    History: The group as it exists now is without a doubt the youngest of the Sects, even compared to the Children of the White Temple- but like the Children, Announced of similar thought and purpose have existed throughout history. There are always the heretics, and there are always the Shedim- in modern nights, the Sect known as the Black Flame dedicates its time to understanding the darker side of the Principle, of understanding Flux, of embracing the dark passion of it, and understandably their ranks are filled to bursting with Shedim on the run from far less accepting Sects like the Host and Kan Fravashi. Before they were Confirmed they were outcasts and exiles that pursued their passions in secret. They recognize that by their own desire, by their own will, they called the Divine Fire down- them and no one else. No one would take that from them, no one could take that from them; they would never have to serve again.

    Except they would serve again, this time under the Principle, and they would sit under the scrutiny of judgment again. They looked upon their peers and saw hypocrites and cowards unwilling to admit their all-too-human urges. In the company of those who should have lauded them, should have accepted them, they were still rejected. Eventually, they decided that if they must serve, if they must spend time in the company of holier-than-thou fools, and upholders of the status quo who deeply misunderstand the very nature of the Divine, then they would enjoy it. People forget that the first role of Satan was as Heaven’s prosecutor.

    The Black Flame study the passion and madness of the Divine, why it affects some and not others. They indulge their own passions with a kind of iron-willed self-control that (usually) prevents them from going over the deep end, even as they tempt those without the strength to resist. They will push at the cracks in mind, body, and soul until their target either bends, breaks, or confesses in a cathartic release- and if they leave broken husks in their wake what do they care? They will serve as the barrier between the mundane and the Divine; they will assess the worth of those who seek the Divine Fire, and they will explore and acknowledge the facets of divinity that the rest of the Announced won’t.

    Modern Nights: Perhaps it says something about the world that after the Host, the Black Flame are the most common Sect in the world- and not just because they unanimously accept the Shedim. They are too individualistic and disorganized a Sect to possess factions, but they do have overarching trends. Their philosophy is ultimately one of pragmatism- the universe is amoral and what is right and wrong can only be decided by the individual. Vice is a simple subset of human nature, and should be indulged. One should exploit their ability to affect change. However, none of that precludes taking steps to ensure their freedom, or taking action that could benefit them. Members of the Black Flame tend to have surprisingly high Exousia, though they are constantly flirting at the edge of it. Every moment they aren’t ‘on the clock’ and a lot of the times they are, they move with their own utterly irreverent and potentially blasphemous flare for passion, madness, and indulgence.

    Favored Maxim: Ruach (Spirit of Vigor)

    Dominions: The Announced of the Black Flame are skilled in the Dominion of Rapture and exclusively teach the Dominion of Corruption.

    Philosophy

    Desire is the Whole of the Law: Nothing short of Metatron, or the force of another Announced, can stop one of the Announced when they want something. Human morality, human laws, no longer apply to them save for when they wish it. As such, the responsible Announced must take all things in moderation.

    Humanity is Animal: Mankind is an animal, no matter how nicely they dress themselves up. Sometimes, we are better than the rest, but usually we are far worse. Through knowledge and spirituality, we have become the most vicious animal of all. The Announced of the Black Flame learn to use this insight to tempt and torment.

    Glory is the Self: When someone strikes your cheek, make sure they know whom they did that to. Vengeance rather than forgiveness, results rather than ideals, self-knowledge rather than delusional hypocrisy. Through their own excellence, their own self-worship, their own understanding, the Announced drew down the Fire and made themselves divine. They saw the order of things and tore it down. Not gods. Not kings. Only men.

    The Fire is Change: It simply doesn’t care what you want, or what anyone wants. It cares not for morality, or choice. It makes Its efforts, It asserts Its will, and It knows only entropy or enthalpy. It only knows evolution or stagnation. Sublime, perfect, It is God and Satan both- but simply doesn’t care for either title, for those are the things of mortals. Emulate It in all things.

    The Prodigal Demiurge: It is a god, more than that, It’s a god’s god, and It still desires nothing more than to reunite with the Principle. The Divine Fire may be a cosmic force of nature that deserves respect, study, and admiration, but to lose your individuality to it is a shame. The Announced of the Black Flame look to Metatron as an example of what not to do with their power.

    Stereotypes
    • The Host: Sanctimonious hypocrites- you are everything I hate in the world
    • Sefer Raziel: You fight for what is accepted rather than what is new.
    • Children of the White Temple: Sad, delusional bastards.
    • Kan Fravashi: They are close to understanding, but they hold themselves back.
    • The Shedim: They know the lessons implicitly. For them, the abyss blinked.
    • Vampires: Oh, I like you.
    • Werewolves: Well, I knew there was a beast in everyone, but this is ridiculous.
    • Mages: You and I could do so much.
    • Demons: Sorry guys, but I’m the real deal.
    • Mortals: I remember being like you- it was awful.

        And now, the new rules on character generation. This has changed quite a bit from the original, but it will be quite a bit better. Big thank-you to Ben Quo for letting me lift parts of his Promethean homebrew, specifically ideas about Beacons and Burdens, in order to apply those concepts here- even if they aren't explained in full at this point in time, I figured it would be a good idea to acknowledge the help.

        Chapter 2: Divine Emanations

        This chapter introduces the Announced from the events leading to their Confirmation to the spectacular powers they now wield. Character creation rules are presented first, followed by the new Advantages of Glory, Maxims, Lux, and Exousia. New merits specially designed for these angelic characters and the details of the powers known as Dominions and Edicts will cap off the chapter.

        Character Creation

        Quite a few of those called by the qashmallim never really make it to the ranks of the Announced. For some reason or another, they never push themselves to the point of drawing down the Fire with the sheer force of their obsession, passion, prowess, etc. Those who go on to become Announced are fairly rare.

        In building your character, there are several aspects you need to remember. There is the human; consumed by an obsession, possessed by a passion, enamored with a cause, exalting in their skill, or exerting their authority. This is the person who achieved Glory, and no one else. There is the restrained and frustrated divinity, the angelic identity assumed by the human. Most would love to cut loose and revel in their divine prowess, but they are restrained by the dictates and whims of the Principle. How they obey, when they obey, and if they obey can define their angelic identity. And then there’s their identity within Announced society- how they seek out the limits of their freedom and push the boundaries of their Exousia. But more importantly, how they seek Communion with their own divine nature and the Pleroma as a whole. A strong Irin character should combine all of these traits.

        Step One: Concept

        Were the Announced pushed or lead to divinity? What pushed them past the limits of what was merely human? Their focus as a human was ultimately singular, as defined above. It was their core, their purpose in life. However, the Confirmation is more or less a liberating, traumatic experience because it forces them to recognize that this isn’t everything. You should recognize the same; a concept is a core, not the whole. The concept should be short, more than a sentence or two should be reduced to its bare essence. Most only comprise a few words: “Conscientious soldier”, “Impassioned activist”, “King of the heavens”. However, don’t be afraid to push it beyond the point of glory and into terror- there’s a reason angels need to say ‘do not be afraid’. “Unholy foot soldier”, “Divine retribution”, and “Jealous god” work just fine. And while a concept that inherently breaches Obedience can be interesting to work with, make sure to not make your character virtually impossible to play.

        Step Two: Select Attributes

        Before the Confirmation, the Announced excelled. What they excelled at is a question with virtually infinite answers, but ultimately they excelled due to raw, natural talent. These are things that can’t really be learned, but rather express innate traits. The massively strong weight lifter might not be a skilled combatant and vice-versa. It is possible to improve these, but it takes significant effort. With regard to rules, these are split into three categories- Physical, Mental, and Social. Physical Attributes are Strength, Dexterity, and Stamina. Mental Attributes are Intelligence, Wits, and Resolve. Social Attributes are Presence, Manipulation, and Composure. In making your character, you’ll need to prioritize these traits according to your vision of the character. You get 5 dots to distribute to the highest category, 4 to the middle category, and 3 to the lowest. Remember that every Attribute starts at *, and can generally never be reduced to zero.

        In prioritizing Attributes, remember your concept, as well as a Vocation and Sect if you have one in mind. Don’t feel obligated to play to type, however. There are Erelim who are intellectual glory hounds, and Malakim who fight for “The Cause” with divine fire and an AK-47. But more than that, they should have changed a little since the Confirmation.

        Step 3: Select Skills

        Attributes are raw talent, and Skills are hard-earned accomplishment. These are easier to gain, but also narrower in scope. The Announced might be divine, but they can’t expect to be good at everything. When creating an Announced character, consider the skills that enabled them to pursue the thing that led them to Confirmation and have come in handy since then. If an Ishim has no skill in Weaponry or Brawl, why do they think that they won’t need to fight? Are they a master of Stealth, shadowing the targets of their mission unseen? Are they masters of Empathy, only arriving after the destruction has been wreaked and cleaning up the pieces? Also bear in mind the Skills one of the Announced needs to survive in their day-to-day life, and the potential hostilities of their own people and of the World of Darkness.

        As with Attributes, Skills fall into three categories- Mental, Physical, and Social. These also must be prioritized. You receive 11 dots for the primary category, 7 for the secondary, 4 for the tertiary. Unlike Attributes, Skills don’t start with an automatic dot.

        Step 4: Select Skill Specialties

        A Skill Specialty defines a narrow application of a Skill. They can be summed up in one or two words, and lend an extra die to any roll made with the parent Skill in which the Specialty applies. A specific Specialty can only be bought once, but multiple Specialties can be bought for one Skill and some Specialties apply to multiple skills. At least one dot is needed in the Specialty’s Skill. An Irin character starts with four Specialties, one of which must apply to their method of Confirmation.

        Step 5: Apply Announced Template

        Vocation: Each Confirmation is unique, but they fall into specific patterns in which they can be grouped together by similarity. These categories, called Vocations, define the means by which one of the Announced draws down the Fire into Confirmation and becomes one of the Irin.
        • Ishim (Torches): Masters of a chosen field, consumed by an obsession that won’t let them go. The Ishim are scrutinizers of detail, perfectionists, individuals focused on the successes and failures of other individuals.
          • They are naturally adept at the Dominion of Conflagration and the Dominion of Portents.
        • Erelim (Visionaries): Ambitious lords of spectacle, full of a need for the spotlight and a passion for the attentions of others. The Erelim are proud, seemingly limitless, and adore the spotlight.
          • They are naturally adept at the Dominion of the Firmament and the Dominion of the Crucible.
        • Elohim (Powers): Modern-day regional monarchs, with the need to rule and exert control. The Elohim are entitled lords of men, natural-born leaders, who dominate with God-given power and political skill.
          • They are naturally adept at the Dominion of the Depths, and the Dominion of Divinity.
        • Malakim (Princes): Prophetic activists armed with the fires of heaven, and a cause to empower. The Malakim are charismatic, inscrutable, and impassioned, willing to fight for the greater good regardless of cost.
          • They are naturally adept at the Dominion of Humanity and the Dominion of the Luminaries.
        • Cherubim (Wardens): Obsessive protectors and guardians with a tendency to throw themselves in harm’s way. The Cherubim are sympathetic companions and fiercely loyal allies, for whom no cost is too high to protect a loved one.
          • They are naturally adept at the Dominion of Creation, and the Dominion of Flesh
        • Refer to the end of the Vocations section in Chapter 1 for Shedim Vocations and the Dominions associated with them.

        Sect: Like-minded groups of Announced united by philosophy, religion, or ideals- the Sects have been the way that the Announced attempt to unite themselves since time immemorial. While it is ideal for a character at creation to be affiliated with at least one Sect- to the point of membership -some may wish to hold off their decision until after a chronicle has started and they have a better grasp of the different factions.
        • The Host (Choristers, Altar Boys): Sincere faith mixed with corrupt power brokers. The Church of the Announced, the Host has been the status quo for well over a thousand years. They believe in a natural order to things, which includes subservience to the Principle. Known for drawing strength from their beliefs and service to others.
          • They most effectively teach the lore of the Dominion of Vitality and exclusively teach the Dominion of Hymns.
        • Sefer Raziel (Philosophers, Bluestockings): Ancient mysticism and quantum physics joined as one. The members of Sefer Raziel seek out ancient knowledge, and new secrets that can be teased from the Pleroma. In understanding the heavens, they can control its powers and the earth.
          • They most effectively teach the lore of the Dominion of Realms and exclusively teach the Dominion of Tongues.
        • Children of the White Temple (Igigi, Idols): Well-intentioned rebellion and god-like confidence. The members of the Children of the White Temple seek escape from the Principle, and the restoration of a divine empire of the Announced. In searching out ancient relics, they shall find the way back into heaven to reclaim godhood.
          • They most effectively teach the lore of the Dominion of Ephemera, and exclusively teach the Dominion of Mes.
        • Kan Fravashi (Magi, Nostalgics): A philosophy of altruism with the pride of legacy. The members of Kan Fravashi seek to improve themselves and others through their actions, and see it as their mission to guide mortals toward right action. Naturally, order shall be restored when they once again command united Announced society.
          • They most effectively teach the lore of the Dominion of Souls, and exclusively teach the Dominion of Daena.
        • The Black Flame (Daemons, Goths): Extreme individualists and pragmatists. The members of the Black Flame seek an end to hypocritical creeds, where every individual can pursue their agendas as they please. If they aren’t skilled enough to keep up, that’s their problem.
          • They most effectively teach the lore of the Dominion of Rapture, and exclusively teach the Dominion of Corruption.

        Maxims: Ways to explore the Announced soul and increase its glorious radiance, these paths to understanding and Communion are badges of accomplishment, milestones on a pilgrimage, and an observable measure of divine power. You begin play with four dots of Maxims, plus a free dot in your Vocation and Sect Maxim- if they are one and the same, double up. At character generation, only a single Maxim may remain without any dots, and you can only purchase the fifth dot of a Maxim provided you have at least one dot in every other Maxim. At character creation they may buy additional Maxims for three merit points per dot.

        Dominions and Edicts: Tied implicitly to the soul-expanding power of Maxims, you begin play with six dots of Maxims to divide up between one of their Vocation Dominions, a Common Dominion, or their Sect Dominion. Each Maxim dedicated into the Aspect of one of the Dominions. A dot of any Maxim that lines up with that favored by their Vocation or the Sect freely unlocks a new Aspect of that Dominion. If the Announced’s Sect and Vocation Maxim line up, they will have to choose whether to unlock new Aspects of one of their Vocation Dominions, or their Sect Dominion. They begin play with a single Edict, but if they have one Maxim at five, they gain another for free at character generation.

        Celestial Form: The Announced may only possess a number of Blessings with a certain Maxim as a prerequisite as they have dots in that Maxim. They can have a number of Sect Blessings equal to their Sect Status +1, and gain one freely upon joining the Sect that doesn’t count towards the tally. The Announced begins play with a number of Regalia equal to their Exousia, and may never have more Regalia than their Exousia. They begin play with one Visage, though Visages are limited more by Glory than by anything else.

        Passion and Purpose: No longer mere mortals, the Announced are not tied to their former Virtue or Vice, but instead to new sources of Willpower selected in part by their means of Confirmation and in part by the will of the Principle. This reflects the new dichotomy of their identity. Their Purpose is what the Principle desires them to be, what their Exousia pushes them to be in order to preserve it. In contrast, their Passion is what is left of their human life, the thing that pushed them to Confirmation in the first place. The Purpose is the angel they have become; the Passion is the person who earned it.

        Name and Title: Much like the Touchstones of Kindred and Uratha, the Announced also have ways of maintaining their identity. Their Celestial Name was gained during the Confirmation, thundered into their soul by the ‘voice’ of the Prodigal Demiurge. It represents their Purpose, and therefore their position as a servant of the Divine. Their Title represents something tied to their Passion, maybe a person place or thing, that reminds them of where they’ve come from, that they have earned their power and freedom, and that while they serve a greater divinity, they are themselves divine.

        Step 6: Select Merits
        • The Announced have 10 dots to spend on Merits at character creation. They can also be used to buy Glory at the rate of three Merit dots a point. For each Merit the player buys, consider whether it applies to the character’s pre-Confirmation human life, or their post-Confirmation angelic life. Sanctity of Merits applies. The Announced may not possess the following Merits: Iron Skin, Iron Stamina, Iron Stomach, Natural Immunity, and Toxin Resistance. All of these are made irrelevant by some of the innate abilities of the Announced. Naturally, any Supernatural Merits, or supernatural benefits granted by Mystery Cult Initiation, do not apply. For a list of Announced-specific Merits, refer to later in this chapter. For a list of other Merits, consult the God-Machine Chronicles.
        • Special Considerations: You begin play with five free dots to divide between Magisterium and (potentially) Cult.

        Step 7: Determine Advantages
        • Beacons: Replacing simple Aspirations are Beacons, the means by which the Announced pursue the self-exploration that leads to the production of Ichor and their ability to enhance their Maxims. Their first Beacon is tied to their current Mission, and more loosely their Purpose- through it they may discover what it is the Principle intends for them. Their second Beacon is tied to their Passion, the means through which they achieved Confirmation in the first place. The third is for them to decide, but it is likely related to a personal goal beyond their Passion. If they possess a Cult, the Cult’s goals serve as their third Beacon. Whenever the Announced resolves a Beacon, they are free to replace it with one of their own.
        • Other Advantages: Calculate as you would for a mortal character.

        Step 8: Age and Experience
        • Irin characters don’t begin play with Experience by default. However, a Storyteller may grant a more seasoned character bonus Experiences, as follows:
          • New Wings: 10 Experiences
          • Established Chorister: 15 Experiences
          • Guardian: 20 Experiences
          • Archangel: 30 Experiences
          • Divine Presence: 45 Experiences

        Step 9: Confirmation
        • At this point, the mechanics of character creation have been handled, and according to the rules the character is finished. However, your character is still probably missing some spark of life that makes them unique and vibrant. What do the Attributes and Skills say? What was his home life like? How was his childhood? Do they know they were Announced? Would they have believed it before the Confirmation? Do they see humans as equals, companions, and potential lovers? Do they see humans as beneath their notice, unable to understand what they’re privileged with? Do they have dots in Intimidation? Why? Do they use cool intellect to shake those they’re opposed to, brute force, or the sheer power of their presence? How and why did they pick up Skills? Are they still of use to them after the Confirmation? What’s changed about the way they use their Skills? What did they do before the Confirmation?
        • As the game is typically social, the faith (a group of Announced) is the core of it. Obsessions, ambitions, frustrations, powers, and limitations all simmer to the boiling point. It’s best; therefore, to make sure the group is established before the chronicle begins, so they don’t find themselves at each other’s throats. However, once everything’s been sorted out the chronicle can begin days, weeks, months, years, decades or even centuries later.
          • Hot Seat: The players each take turn being asked questions about their character by the other players. Remember that you’re answering as an author, not a character. Note that acknowledging you don’t know how to answer a certain question is perfectly valid. Getting to know the characters means that the players will be able to act as though each character already knows the others well.
          • Q&A: Each player writes two questions they would like answered, and passes them to the Storyteller, who then asks each player to answer them. Remember to make sure all questions are suitable for the group, and bear in mind that some of the answers may inspire Aspirations- or engender future plot points from the Storyteller.
        • Anecdotes: Each player picks another player’s character and makes up an anecdote about something that character was involved in. Try to keep it down to one anecdote per character. If something is grossly out of character, or highly disagreeable to the player, they retain final verdict on the anecdote. But if it is only slightly out of character, try to work it in.

            And now, new information on Glory. These days, it's looking less like Azoth and more like Sekhem, though it shares more in common metaphysically with the former than the latter.

            New Advantage: Glory

            Limitless and unknowable, the Divine Fire flares throughout all creation. Pyros permeates the entire physical world in small quantities, wherever humans (and other beings) plan, act on impulse, build, tear down, create, and destroy. Demiurges steal the Divine Fire to create Prometheans. Alchemists bottle it in their experiments. Pandorans feast upon it. The Created burn it into Azoth like messy reactors. Far from simple magical fuel, though, Pyros reacts. It forms its own agenda- its own Principle -and coalesces into its own agents and advocates.

            Unlike the qashmallim, born from the alien thoughts and needs of an unknowable intelligence, the Announced are quite understandable. With their towering passions, rages, obsessions and ambitions they serve as excellent conduits with which to usher Pyros into the world. The Azoth of the Created builds like a furnace, sublimating ideas, experiences, and other esoteric ingredients into a human soul- an exceptionally ‘pure’ one at that, immune to the maddening touch of the Divine. But the Glory of the Announced is not a furnace, but an inferno. It roars to terrifying and magnificent heights with specific intent, and disperses with time and use as the need that called it vanishes like burning fuel. Unless the Announced decide to risk their freedom of will and try to steal the Divine Fire, they generally reach the heights of their power only when they’re “on the clock”.

            Effects
            • Transhuman Potential: While the Announced can indeed cultivate superhuman Attributes and Skills, they can only enjoy the benefits while they possess Glory 6 and above- and the current Glory rating caps the Attribute or Skill rating. Unusable dots become useable again when Glory increases. Glory also limits what level of an Edict they can use, as well as the amount they can boost their Attributes by spending Lux.
            • Effortless Might: The Announced are among the most powerful beings in the World of Darkness. Filled with a maelstrom of Divine Fire, they don’t begin as supernatural weaklings, but instead begin with a Glory rating of 10 and watch as the Fire dwindles over time.
            • Burnout: After a certain period of time, the Announced uses their Glory as a dice pool during Burnout rolls.
            • Glorious Radiance: When the Announced is full of power and purpose, the towering mystic might of their Glory casts off them in waves, enabling them to sense other sources of Pyros nearby. See Chapter 3 for full rules.
            • Possession Immunity: So long as the Announced is in accord with the Principle, and still possesses a shred of Glory, they are untouchable. The Divine Fire is hot enough to melt ideas, to scorch power. Attempts to possess them fail, and the possessing entity suffers a number of points of Aggravated Damage equal to the Announced’s current Glory rating.
            • Crucible of the Will: In desperate times, the Announced may sacrifice power for fortitude. As a reflexive action once per turn, the Announced may sacrifice a dot of Glory to refill their entire Willpower pool.

            And now, the brand-new advantage of Maxims. Part Renown, part Pillars, mixed with pilgrimage and self-discovery flavour that permeates the Divine Fire as a whole, hopefully you guys appreciate the new development.

            New Advantage: Maxims

            The Divine Fire is transformative. Perhaps it destroys, perhaps it brings together, but it is always change. As such, the Announced cannot be exposed to it time and again without learning and growing. Unlike most things in the World of Darkness, Maxims are measureable indicators of the understanding of the Divine and Its relationship to themselves and the mortal world. When the Announced learns, grows, and completes goals of their own in response to lessons learned, the Divine Fire blossoms and grows in their soul, expanding into their Maxims. This process of learning about oneself and the Divine is called Affirmation, and much like Renown, each act should be a step up from the last in some way. Maxims are used as prerequisites and dice pools for various power- including Blessings and Regalia -and unlock Aspects of Dominions.

            Every time you buy a new dot in a Maxim, you immediately unlock a new Aspect of a Dominion, ‘dedicating’ that Maxim dot to the Dominion. Ultimately, as Vocation Dominions both require the Dominion Maxim, you will have to make choices based on specialization, as you can only have five dots of one Maxim.

            Maxims are collectively separated into five categories representing different kinds of understanding and dedication to the Divine. Maxims are measured in dots and have a maximum of five.

            Nefesh (Living Soul): Awareness of the physical world is a gift that is rarely appreciated, since most people observe life passively. Human senses are actually astonishingly sharp, and merely lack the total focus and ability to screen sensory information that comes as second nature to animals. With focus, a perfectly ordinary human can hear a watch ticking twenty feet away, they can smell one drop of perfume in a six-room house, they can taste a teaspoon of sugar in a gallon of water, they can feel the brush of a fly’s wing, and see a single candle alight around 30 miles away. An Announced who dedicates themselves to studying Nefesh can do all that and more. By exploring the animating force of their body- their souls -and the Pyros they’re made of, the Announced encourages their union, melding Divine and Phenomenal together to push themselves above and beyond human limits. Those who make a study of Nefesh are constantly pushing the limits of their physical experience, trying to become even more aware of their body and how it connects to their soul. In mentality, the student of Nefesh is like a rock- implacable, immovable. Their greatest happiness is a relentless pursuit of their goals, throwing all their efforts into what they believe. In the pursuit of Communion, action becomes the primary means of understanding their own soul and those of others.
            • Example Affirmations: Fight to protect your Magesterium, lead another to a revelation, reach the point of total exhaustion, best a Pandoran, protecting someone else- physically or otherwise.


            Ruach (Spirit of Vigor): The Divine Fire is always drawn to obsession, desperation, and the mad vortex of emotions that leads to spectacular acts of creation. However, the students of Ruach feel the connection between emotion and Pyros keenly. Perhaps if they can understand why the two are connected, they can understand the Principle, Metatron and Their motivations. Perhaps. You will not find so loyal a friend or an enemy so spiteful. Their emotions roil like the inferno of Glory within them- their love burns and their hatred freezes. Though the students of Nefesh define themselves by action, it is the passionate disciples of Ruach that are known for being impulsive and reacting on instinct. To explore this Maxim, those who seek it push themselves to be unwavering in their convictions and beliefs, and to explore the extremes of emotion and sensation in ways that have to do more with how they feel than the actual experience. In the pursuit of Communion, they define themselves and others by their emotions and whether others suppress or act upon their feelings.
            • Example Affirmations: Succumb to Temptation, bring the perpetrator of an inhumane act to justice without resorting to mortal law, engaging in a intimate relationship (in any sense), earn recognition for an accomplishment, maintain your honor or integrity even if your enemies do not.

            Neshama (Seat of Understanding): The Divine is unknowable and ineffable, but that certainly doesn’t stop people from trying- least of all the Announced. Neshama is an effort to understand beyond the self-exploration of Nefesh and Ruach, but instead an exploration of the Divine directly at an intellectual level. Instead of structured, quantified existence, they look at origins, at coming into being from nothingness. The Irin following Neshama chase after Azoth to understand it; examine its continuous creation, the sustenance of life and bringing things to wholeness. The Shedim, naturally, pursue Flux and seek to contemplate the ultimate dissolution of things. There is indeed overlap between the two groups studying the other aspect of the Fire, but it is not nearly as fulfilling as examining the function of the Divine Fire that is tied to their soul. The students of Neshama require a greater understanding of “why” than most, but are also in the best position to have an answer to that question. They possess both unshakeable confidence and endless questioning in equal measure. In the pursuit of Communion, they define themselves and others by their understanding- by their willingness to question or their simple acceptance.
            • Example Affirmations: Survive a Firestorm, master an Edict or Dominion (Be able to access the powers of every Edict Tier or Dominion Aspect), teach another of the Announced to use Lux more effectively, make a dramatic insight.

            Chaya (Life Divine): Perhaps, if one cannot try to define Divinity by what it is you can define it by what it is not. Those who study this Maxim seek not to understand the Divine Fire in its functions and processes, but instead by its limits. What, exactly, can Pyros do when pushed to the limit? Frankly, there isn’t a lot it can’t. These Announced gaze into the endless flames of the Divine, and the Divine gazes back. They can feel Its limitless power in a way others of the Announced can’t, they push at the limits of their power because they know they don’t actually exist. There is no limit they cannot surpass with time and effort. A dedicated student of Chaya would say that the Divine has consumed their ego, and now they exist only in union with it- but many who think that way haven’t yet reached Communion, and likely never will. Their identity rests upon their self-assurance; they judge themselves and others by how they exercise their divinity. Only the truly bold, the truly transcendent, are worthy of their respect.
            • Example Affirmations: Set an “impossible” goal and achieve it, construct a Magisterium, defeating a superior foe, fighting against overwhelming odds, creating an exceptional vision, spending Glory on a Dominion, Edict, or Celestial Form power.

            Yechida (Oneness): Those enraptured by Yechida follow a simple creed- faith without action is dead, and so is divinity. What is the point of studying the Divine Fire, or pushing the limits of your power, of merging yourself harmoniously with the Pyros in your soul and in your heart, if it is not directed to something? They pursue their own causes and goals with as much- if not even more -fervor than the missions of the Principle, and if the two come into alignment the results are spectacular. Though emotions, thoughts, or even facts will not be interpreted the same way by the Principle or the Announced, results don’t lie. Disciples of Yechida are always pushing themselves above and beyond the call of duty, often at great personal risk, in an effort to earn Communion through demonstrations of loyalty, dedication, and understanding. They judge themselves and others through their actions, and their dedication to a cause.
            • Example Affirmations: Charitable works, participation in a religious service, construct and maintain a list of wrongdoers and appropriate punishments, incite a group of mortals to action through words alone (Lux bolstered words count)

                New Advantage: Lux

                The Pleroma is described as a realm of “limitless light” so it stands to reason that the Announced would call the power of the heavens Lux or “light”. Of the same origin of the Pyros used by Prometheans, it coalesces in similar ways. If Glory is a measure of the intensity of an Announced’s access to the Pleroma, burning like a small star beneath their skin, it stands to reason that Lux is like plasma- unbound energy that radiates off of Glory like solar wind coming off of a star. Measured in points rather than dots, Lux is used to fuel the powers known as Dominions and Edicts, as well as facilitate the change into Celestial Form, and travel to the Pleroma. You are limited in how much Lux you can store by your Maxims and your Exousia, and how much you can spend per turn by your Glory- only being able to spend a number of points equal to their current Glory.
                • Powers: For Dominions and Edicts, spend Lux as written for the powers. It costs no Lux to assume Celestial Form, but at least one to change back, and potentially more to activate different abilities. If one is left without Lux while transformed, they are stuck in Celestial Form until they can get themselves more Lux. However, it can also be used to enter the Pleroma, something that will be discussed in the Appendix on that subject.

Glory means it can be extraordinarily hard to kill one of the Announced, and depending on the situation, it just gets even more difficult. They may spend a point of Lux to heal Lethal damage, but this is obviously supernatural and inflicts Fear Yara. By spending a point of Lux, the Announced using this ability gains a number of temporary Attribute dots in each trait depending on their current Glory for the rest of the scene and can add a number of Attribute dots past their current limits equal to their Exousia rating.

                Regaining Lux

                While effectively unlimited in the Pleroma- depending on the spot you can more or less pull Lux from the air or drink it like water -it is understandably much harder to come by in the physical world, but there are still some ways that the Announced can regain their power.                 Fanes

                The Divine Fire does not occur naturally in the mundane world. You can’t extract Pyros from a forest fire, or a thunderstorm (Unless you’re one of the Created, that is). Obsession, passion, and desperation don’t make Pyros; they draw it down. In a similar way, there are places in the world of sublime beauty, of perfected symmetry, that constantly evoke awe, passion, obsession and desperation. These are called Fanes. Sometimes, they’re locations or objects, other times they’re occurrences. Whatever the case may be it draws down the Divine in a way that aligns with a particular Maxim. A fane created by a desperately beautiful performance only exists as long as the performance does, but in that period of time the door to the Pleroma is open and Lux is there for the Announced to take. The Announced may take a number of points of Lux equal to the dot rating of the Fane or their dots in that Maxim, whichever is lower, in a single day. The Storyteller should determine the degree of the Fane beforehand- some guidelines will be outlined below. A Shedim must pay Willpower equal to the degree if they wish to use a Fane.
                • Nefesh: A Fane tied to the power of the animating force of the body are driven by revelations: knowledge with an impact unimpeded by the organized self. By their very nature, it is difficult to find an enduring Fane attuned to Nefesh, but they also have a tendency towards the higher degrees. They are difficult to find, except for those who really know how to look, since they are mostly impossible to articulate in spoken words. When a revelation is captured, the individual it came from loses memory of the insight, which immediately subsumes to strengthen the inner self. Announced who prefer to gather revelations are insightful self-examiners, watching bemused from the sidelines, unable to express themselves.
                • Ruach: The human spirit is rarely expressed so profoundly as through art, and some would argue that it is performances that are art’s highest form. A performance is expression made evident for the entire world to see. They look for the excellent-beyond-excellent. Repetition is preferred, but the singularly excellent, the kind of performance that will never be repeated, are always much higher in degree. Those who are, in fact, performing when the Announced harvests Lux tend to feel disconnect between their efforts and the audience. They feel exposed, embarrassed, or unappreciated; on some level they know the performance was flawless, but they are not satisfied… and it will drive them to perform again, with increasing success and less fulfillment.
                • Neshama: The tireless search for understanding is made brighter by insights. These are not found within dead texts, and empty words; they are found within the minds of humans. And while an insight can only be harvested once from the mind of an individual, when it is spread to another it can be harvested again. In the modern world, true insights are almost impossible to find amidst fields of limitless, disorganized information and so those Announced who uncovers a true insight will hold onto it tightly and spread it judiciously. Insights change degree from use to use, depending on how impressive it is to the one receiving it. When harvested, the insight seems less impressive, or the memory of it becomes hazy.
                • Chaya: The aspirants to true divinity want to erode barriers and gaze upon testaments to an enduring power. They seek out trances and visions; physical actions that offer up their self in favor of subservience to the divine, and works of art- statues, paintings, or other physical pieces -that somehow embody it. Visions are the most likely fanes to be permanent and possess a static degree- unless they’re defaced.
                • Yechida: Fighting endlessly in pursuit of their causes, the Announced beholden to this Maxim bask reverently in the light of orations and inspirations. Fierce words and fiercer passions pull at them, spontaneous explosions of artistic genius calls to them. The performance is not what calls to them, but the essential spark of delivery, the passion that precedes it. When harvested, orators feel the speeches turn to ashes in their mouth, feel their audiences fall away. Artists lose focus; lose their drive to create. Both are driven to seek it again; a good orator constantly speaks, a true artist constantly creates.

                    Their new Integrity analogue, Exousia. It's a bit more fidgety than before, with new ideas about what Chains are and what they do. More importantly, it also gives you options to get out of becoming Shackled. A big thank-you to Ben Quo for letting me use the idea of Burdens.

                    Modified Advantage: Exousia (Integrity)

                    “Did you ever notice, how in the Bible, whenever God needed to punish someone, or make an example, or when God needed a killing, he sent an angel? Did you ever wonder what a creature like that must be like? A whole existence spent praising your God, but always with one wing dipped in blood. Would you ever really want to see an angel?”
                    - Thomas Dagget, ‘The Prophecy’

                    Something should be made extremely clear here- angels are not moral beings, and neither are the Announced. They are authoritative, acting with license granted to them by the Almighty- they are punished not for doing things that are reprehensible to mortals, but for going against the will that commands them, and acting against union with the Divine. When an Assyrian army surrounded Jerusalem, and the king prayed for aid, God sent an angel that slaughtered the entire army in a night without a single man making a sound. The Assyrian general woke to find all his men butchered in the night, silently. When God wanted Gomorrah judged, he sent a pair of angels. They didn’t balk at the potential rape, or even a father abandoning his daughters to a crowd of potential rapists as substitutes for his guests, they balked at the refusal to abide by sacred hospitality.

                    The Announced are. Not. Moral.

                    They can be moral, but by their nature, they obey the will of the Principle, enforced by the being they call Metatron. If God says turn a woman mourning the loss of her city to salt for gazing on its destruction, you do it. If God says kill every firstborn child in a country, then hang around to hear the screams of their parents, you go “Yes sir, when do I start smiting?” That is the difference between a simple angel, and the Announced- the Announced, being formerly human, and ultimately separate from the Divine whole can choose what to do. They are not bound wholly to the will of God, they have free will- but the Principle certainly makes it worth their while to obey. To rebel, to be disobedient, is to lose some freedoms to Chains. Rebel often enough, and you’re Shackled- completely under the control of the Principle. Conscious, perhaps protesting, but as bound to act out Its will as any qashmal or lilitu.

                    Unlike regular Integrity, which starts at seven points out of ten, Exousia starts at three points out of ten. Instead of hitting a Breaking Point at a threshold equivalent to or below where they stand, they suffer a Breaking Point for actions undertaken equal to or above their current level of Exousia. Exousia, translated from Greek, comes across rather flatly as ‘authority’. However, a direct translation misses out on some of the nuances. Exousia isn’t just ‘authority’. It is ‘license to act’, authority that has been given down from on high, the ability or strength with which one is imbued that is either merely possessed or- more appropriately -exercised. It can sometimes even be read as ‘weight’.

                    Exousia, then, is the metaphysical union to the Principle the Announced feels- how in line the will of the Principle your own actions are. More cynically, it’s a measure of how far you can push yourself before you break. How much slack you have and weight you can throw before Metatron panics and slaps another Chain on you. Oddly enough, Exousia often has little to do with direct obedience to the Principle, and significantly more to do with maintaining a union with the Divine. Abuse of Glory, use of Dominions and Edicts to fulfill acts that go against what the Divine Fire stands for, and many other things can result in a loss of Exousia that pushes the Announced closer to becoming one of the Shackled.

                    However, unlike most Integrity meters, Exousia fluctuates wildly similarly to Clarity. To succeed at a Mission immediately awards the Announced with a dot of Exousia, while failing a Mission results in a universal Breaking Point as a Chance Roll. Beyond that, an Irin may artificially raise their Exousia by dedicating enough time to working on their relationship with the Divine Fire in order to be able to pay two experience points to increase it by a dot. As a starting character, you will need to be concerned with everything listed at three and above- because that will result in Breaking Points.

                    Chains

                    The Announced call them Chains, but to look at them with purely philosophical and metaphysical trappings, they are direct connections to the Principle. The Principle Itself- if It can truly think -sees nothing but good in this. Another piece of the Fire is coming home; It is one step closer to being whole. The Announced, however, have a very different opinion. Further union to the will of the Principle aligns them with it, prevents them from acting against it, and restricts their personal freedom. Metatron- though as far from the Announced in thought as the Principle is from It -knows that the Announced fear connection to the Divine for the same reason It craves nothing more in the universe; loss of identity, loss of separateness of persons. The Fire is one, and after untold millennia of loneliness, Metatron wants to never be lonely again. The Announced, on the other hand, have always been individuals. Loneliness to them is quite different than Metatron’s loneliness, and as an extension of the natural fear of death all living things possess; they fear the loss of their own identity, their own ability to recognize that they are, in fact, alive.

                    Chains generally come in three variations- Bans, Revelations, and Emanations. Bans are behavioral in nature, either much like the bans of ghosts, God-Machine Angels, and Hisil Spirits, or more general psychological or social tics. Revelations are changes- sometimes dramatic, to the Announced’s body that reveal their own inhuman nature. Emanations are environmental changes caused by the Irin’s direct presence, and as such often herald their arrival. The Announced roll Resolve + Composure to resist gaining a Chain, and apply any bonuses or penalties listed above for Exousia Breaking Points as the opposite for this purpose. The Announced cannot spend Willpower on this roll.

                    The sort of Chain they gain is more thematic and connected to whatever led to the situation than something that should be governed by game mechanics. Chains are always deeply personal; otherwise they wouldn’t hurt nearly as much. Some aren’t so much punishing certain behaviors, as they are encouraging others; it is entirely feasible for Chains (usually Bans) to serve the advancement of the Announced’s mission. They also last a certain period of time dependent on their Glory rating at the time of affliction unless it is a permanent Chain, and have a tendency to repeat and increase in severity.

                    Chains can be gained permanently due to an extreme decrease or increase in Exousia, as noted in the table above. If they have gained a particular Chain (even in varying severities) a number of times more than their lowest Maxim, make a note on the character sheet at the level of Exousia they gained it at. They may suppress the Chain with a Burden, but if they ever drop to that level of Exousia again the Chain comes back.

                    Roll Results
                    • Dramatic Failure: No Chain occurs, make a note to add two dice to the next roll to resist Chains the Announced has to endure.
                    • Failure: The Announced gains a minor Chain
                    • Success: The Announced gains a major Chain
                    • Exceptional Success: The Announced gains a catastrophic Chain

                    Examples of Chains
                    • Bans- The Announced applies a -3 penalty to any roll that would break the Ban. Revelations are expressions of how the Divine Fire has altered the soul of the Announced manifested on their body. Depending on the nature of the changes, they add a +2 to Social rolls if they are visible to mortals and the Announced is specifically using them as a marker of religious or supernatural power. However, some horrific changes make a -2 penalty, unless they’re used for Intimidation.

                    Hotline to God

                    Chains are, in essence, direct connections to the Principle, so shouldn’t there be some advantages to having them? Well, that’s up to you. Maybe clues to the mission of the Announced are written in the angelic script branded in their skin. Maybe another serves as a countdown to some foretold point. Ultimately, regardless of any inconvenience they lead to for the Announced, they are still direct connections to something that is almost definitely the source of souls, and could possibly be the capital-G God if your Storyteller so desires. It certainly fits the part. The connection might be applied and manipulated by Metatron, but It cannot take away everything positive about it. Chains suck, but only if you intend to follow your own agenda instead of the Principle’s mission. Keep that in mind when designing Chains. They might impede your own goals, but that same Chain will almost certainly push you towards acting in accord with the Principle.

                      • -1 per dot below 4 on all Social dice pools involving interacting with qashmallim and other inhabitants of the Pleroma. They can tell how disagreeable you are to their purpose.

                    The Shackled

                    Some unlucky Announced are so closely tied to the Principle, be it by constant testing the borders of Exousia or simple bad luck, that they lose all free will entirely. When an Announced gains a number of Chains more than their Exousia +1, they become Shackled. They are entirely bound to the will of the Principle; they exist to fulfill its Missions. They cannot deviate, they do not avoid them, and often they have no recollection of time spent ‘on the clock’. They simply regain consciousness after their Mission has been completed, and try to discern what just happened along with handling any social issues that may have arisen as a result. Of course, having completed their Mission, they are also hemorrhaging Glory and that could leave them in a very bad spot very quickly. While it is not impossible to play one of the Shackled, it does call for a very specific kind of play- one where you’re chasing after actions you can’t remember performing, and dealing with social problems you can’t remember instigating. Whether you’d prefer for a Shackled character to become a Storyteller character or to carry on bravely and keep playing is entirely up to the group.

                    There but for the Grace of God…

                    The attentive reader will notice that above in the section describing Glory, it mentions that a Glory of zero is technically possible. While the full rules for that will be described in Chapter 3, it will be mentioned here briefly that when an Announced runs out of the power of Glory, Exousia substitutes. Dots of Exousia stand in for dots of Glory for the purposes of determining regeneration, Exaltation, and the resistance of various supernatural powers.

                    Breaking Chains

                    It is possible for the Shackled to shed their Chains, or for one of the Announced terrified of becoming Shackled to avoid that fate- however it is neither easy, nor is it always preferable. Either by sacrificing a dot of Glory, or an encounter with a qashmal, the Announced may shed a Chain in favor of taking up a Burden. If taking up a Burden is suggested by the qashmal, the Announced may refuse- gaining a Willpower point (and the qashmal gains its Rank in Pyros). However, if they accept the qashmal’s Burden, or offer it up themselves, they gain 1-3 new Breaking Points somewhere on the Exousia scale from the most grievous offense they can make (the lowest is usually Exousia 8) and will never give a Breaking Point below what phases the Announced, but beyond that there’s no limit to how the Divine may impart this metaphysical weight. The Announced, invigorated by the charge, refills their entire Willpower pool, and the qashmal fills its Pyros pool. In the event of an Exousia Breaking Point, they may give up one of the Breaking Points taken up by the Burden in lieu of losing a dot and gaining a Chain, though they still suffer the appropriate Conditions. Shedding the last Burdened Breaking Point means immediately losing a dot of Exousia, but no Chain is gained as a result.

                    The Announced may use the taking up of Burdens to suppress permanent Chains until such a time as they lose Exousia again, or Chains gained by very low or very high Exousia, but they may never possess more Burdens collectively than their dots in their Vocation’s favored Maxim.

                        Magisterium (Special)
                        Effect: A Magisterium shouldn’t be an Announced’s home, but sadly more often than not it ends up being one. A Magisterium is meant to be a temple, a shrine, a place dedicated to the exploration of the relationship between the Announced and the Principle, a refuge within the Pleroma, a way to attune yourself to the Divine. An Announced without a Magisterium isn’t homeless, they’re godless; they have no way to reliably make contact with the Divine, no way to reliably harvest Lux. If they possess a Cult, to them it is a true temple; a place to connect with the Divine in a way most other mortals can barely comprehend. If they can’t find the Announced, it’s a convenient place to leave a message for them, though their enemies could always leave a calling card as well. Announced without a Magisterium lack all of these advantages, and must therefore be much more careful in when and where they choose to spend Lux and manage a Cult.

                        Mechanically, the player can put up to ten Merit dots into Magisterium, dividing them between Acoustics, Pleromic Overlay, and Endowments. If you want to stretch your investment a bit (or like helping the Storyteller heap trouble on your head) you can take Drawbacks to reduce the Merit dots cost of Magisterium. Taking five dots in Drawbacks would enable for five dots to be dedicated to every advantage, making it a powerful Magisterium indeed. (The inherent Drawback to Memory Palace is to help facilitate a semblance of balance, and does not count as a discount on the Merit) Players may conflate their Magisterium with somewhere covered by the Safe Place Merit but this is not strictly necessary.

                        Acoustics (*-*****): Perhaps better than anyone, the Announced understand the need to connect with the Principle. Until the Announced achieve Communion, using their Magisterium is the best (and only) way they have of communicating with It. Whether they actually manage to make the Principle understand or if the appeal merely fulfills the deal the Grigori made with Metatron and It is mollified by an uncomprehending attempt to intercede for It is unknown. But how does one communicate with an alien intelligence that has trouble comprehending separateness of persons? Energy, as a universal factor, is a constant. Music, song, mantras, whatever the Announced use to meditate on the Divine, they attempt to use the wavelength of the sound, and eventually the music within the Announced resonates with the music of the Principle- the Music of the Spheres.

                        However, like any engine, the more you want out of it the larger it has to be. A one-dot Magisterium is little better than a broom closet, while a five-dot one is substantially larger and therefore more potent. If the Announced chooses to enhance this effect with Relics or Icons, the Magisterium could have strange effects on the surrounding area, enhancing Yara in strange ways.

                        • **: Maybe about the size of a small office, it is likely something fairly similar to a home studio or a recording suite. Add two dice to attempts to regain Lux.
                        • ***: While it isn’t much, it was designed with consideration taken for genuine performance or worship. Add four dice to attempts to regain Lux.
                        • *****: A true work of architectural art, this is a place people would kill to be able to sing or play in. A large-scale concert hall, a genuine cathedral, that sort of thing. They might not be quite that big, but they’re close. Voices are hushed as channeled Lux and shaped Yara cause a palpable aura of true awe, and those that do raise their voices to sing would weep for the beauty of the music. Add five dice to attempts to regain Lux.

                        Pleromic Overlay (*-*****): Ultimately, in many ways this is the true purpose of a Magisterium, to be a place where the mortal realm and the worlds touched by the Divine Fire can connect and mingle. However, the exact means in which this expresses itself is different from Magisterium to Magisterium. It could be that the sanctum of one Announced is more or less a tableau of their life, with a fridge stocked with their memories of the best things they’ve ever eaten and drank. It could be another is so shrouded in the Divine Fire that it takes an effort of will for mere mortals to even enter the place. But whether it is a place that is untouchable due to the aura of sacred forbiddance that surrounds it, or a manor that twists and conforms to the will of its master, a Magisterium is always a place of beautiful madness- where the very stuff of heaven touches the earth.
                        • Memory Palace: Crafted from the memories, ambitions, and desires of the Announced who is master of it as much as it is a building. By spending a point of Lux and rolling Wits + Memory Palace, the Announced may make any change they desire to the interior of the Magisterium, even if it normally would be physically impossible. This can’t change the square footage of the Magisterium, merely how it is used. This could, theoretically, be used to remove any and all exits from the Magisterium, making it significantly more difficult to escape or break in. Depending on how stressful the situation has become, apply penalties as the Storyteller sees fit.
                          • Drawbacks: Because of how deeply connected Magisterium and Announced become due to this particular alteration, should anything happen to the Magisterium it has dire consequences for the Announced. The destruction of any part of the Magisterium is grounds for the Amnesia Condition- regarding what probably pertains to what was destroyed, but ultimately it is up to the Storyteller.
                        • Sanctum Sanctorum: The Magisterium should be a place the Announced can retreat to in order to be alone with their thoughts, and with the Principle, like the high priests of old. And for that to be true, it must be virtually impossible for others to approach. Every dot dedicated to Sanctum Sanctorum means that another 100 feet away from the Magisterium, those approaching begin to feel a profound yet sourceless distress bordering on outright terror. Those who take the hint and back off suffer no additional penalties. Those who try to approach must roll Resolve + Composure, penitentes are able to do so as a rote action, and those that succeed may approach by spending Willpower equal to the dots dedicated to Sanctum Sanctorum. Note that this works on any entity capable of using Willpower, not just humans. This particular defense for the Magisterium may be waved for those the Announced declares welcome.

                        Drawbacks: These are issues or concerns associated with the Magisterium. In character they do little except cause potential headaches for the Announced (unless they can turn them to their advantage), but out of character each dot of Drawbacks allows for extra dots to be spent on the Magisterium Merit. Each Drawback may only be taken once, and each should serve as a Story Hook given appropriate circumstances.
                        • Famous (minus **): For whatever reason, the Announced’s Magisterium is noteworthy and well known to the general public. The acoustics and the feelings of genuine reverence and worship that permeate them are well recorded. Attempting to enter the Pleroma or draw upon Lux without notice while the place is open is effectively impossible.
                        • Inhabited (minus ***): Something lives in the Magisterium, drawn to its power. Maybe it’s an old ghost, or the new ghost of a penitente. Perhaps it’s one of the jinn, or something far worse- like a Pandoran. Whatever the case, something lives there that sees the place as its own, and it won’t be easily mollified. If it is a Pandoran, build it using the ‘Minion’ level template.
                        • Ravaged (minus *): Due to some sort of defacement or spiritual defilement, the way the Magisterium attunes with the Pleroma is thrown off. Pick one Maxim. You cannot use anything using it as a prerequisite within the Magisterium. Ever.
                        • Stained History (minus *): This can only be taken by one of the Announced who possesses at least one dot of the Cult Merit.                         • Under Surveillance (minus **): A group of mortals has some deep vested interest in your Magisterium. The Storyteller has five dots to distribute between their Eminence and Ardor (Reach and Grasp). The player may have some suggestions as to who these people are and why they are interested in them, but ultimately the Storyteller must decide.

                        Endowments: Sometimes, it’s good to be a servant of the Divine. Occasionally, you catch a lucky break; the Principle smiles on you, and you can really enjoy life as a small god in a world full of mortal men. Each Endowment may only be taken once.
                        • Obscure (*): Mortals have a blind spot for your Magisterium, most of them don’t even know it exists; they must succeed a Wits + Occult roll to even notice it. (Unseen Sense or Penitente negates this) If the roll fails, they cannot notice it, and cannot roll again for another 24 hours. If the character is led there, they can see it, but if their guide abandons them, they are lost again. If you also possess the Famous Drawback, then the public parts of the Magisterium are known and celebrated, but the inner sanctum is virtually invisible. The Announced may travel to the Pleroma and meditate to gain Lux normally.
                        • Prime Location (*): The Magisterium is located somewhere particularly desirable and notable. A Parisian Announced doesn’t just have a Magisterium; they have a Magisterium within La Cathedral Saint-Chapelle.
                        • Food of Angels (***): Somewhere in your Magisterium, strange food mysteriously coalesces, seemingly out of the light streaming through the windows. Whatever shape this takes (traditionally thin wafers tasting vaguely of milk and honey) the effect is distinct. For the Announced, eating this manna grants them a number of Willpower points equal to their Exousia. If this would exceed their normal limit, the excess points disappear at the end of the scene.                                         Most effects have a target number of successes between 5 and 15, with lower end effects being equivalent to extremely long-range or persistent Tier 1 Edicts, and the higher end effects comparing to Tier 3 Edicts. When attempting to manipulate the Flower of Life, roll Exousia + Occult with each roll taking an hour of meditation and connection to the Magisterium.


                                        And now, the Announced version of Lashing Out, Judgment. I took the idea behind the Measure in Promethean and gave it some angel-themed mechanics.

                                        Judgment: When the Announced first meet, their Radiance meets and mixes, causing them to immediately become aware of the nature of the other. They know that they have met another of divinity, and they want to know where they stand. This will influence first impressions among the Choir. When one Announced meets another roll their Glory + an appropriate Power trait. A seductive or charismatic Prince might use Presence while the impressively muscled Warden uses Strength. Whatever the case, they must also choose the image they want to project, which influences the Condition they can inflict if they succeed in overwhelming the other Announced.
                                        • Avenging Angel: This is the Divine Fire as an engine of passion, of action, of human desperation. They fulfill their goals, they punish the unworthy, and they cast down those who have done wrong. If successful, the other Announced feels petty and small, perhaps selfish and guilty, in the face of this paragon. It imposes the Chastened Condition
                                        • The Almighty: This is the Divine Fire as limitless power. There is nothing that, given proper circumstances, it cannot do. They are a dynamo unbound, a walking inferno of mystic might that cannot be equaled. If successful, the other Announced feels incredibly insignificant and even fearful in the face of such an overwhelming occult force; this is the closest a god will normally get to worshipping something else. It imposes the Cowed Condition
                                        • The Messenger: This is the Divine Fire as a force in service to a higher power. The strange and occasionally esoteric ‘plans’ of the Principle can be deeply vexing, but subservience to a higher power- especially one that brings people together and supports unity -is calming and reassuring. If successful, the other Announced feels empty and arrogant, jealous and envious of the peace their better possesses. It imposes the Guilty Condition. The Shedim don’t impose this version of Judgment normally, but instead emphasize the hypocrisy and damage the Announced has experienced and been the cause of. The end result is the same, but it is a revelation of wrongdoing rather than a presentation of purity in principle.
                                        If one Announced is successful, they gain a general sense of the power of the Announced before them (a combination of both Glory and Maxims) as well as a vision of the last time their fellow divinity fell to Temptation.

                                                            Thank-you. It's been a while, but I've mostly been puttering around with some different concepts and theorizing on how things may change once Promethean 2e comes out. In the meantime, have some information about Blessings.

                                                            Blessings

                                                            The Announced emerge from the Confirmation with preternatural competence, and unfathomably good luck- this is no accident, as the touch of the Divine Fire imbues them with the tools and talents they need to fulfill their purpose. Unlike most supernatural powers, they do not require training, cannot be taught, and aren’t really learned in a conventional sense. Instead, they manifest immediately when they are purchased. The Announced do not know they have a new Blessing until it activates for the first time, at which point they intuit their new capabilities. Most Announced are as surprised by their Blessings as the rest of the world is.

                                                            Most Blessings have the appropriate prerequisites for use listed in their description, and while it is possible for an Announced to possess a Blessing they don’t have all the prerequisites for, but they remain dormant until the prerequisites are fulfilled. The type of prerequisite determines what sort of Blessing you have.
                                                            • Soul Blessings: Using the Maxims as prerequisites, the higher the prerequisite, the stronger the Blessing. Typically, Soul Blessings are subtle with no overt magic, and can often be mistaken for exceptional luck or skill. Those that are more overt typically work only when they aren’t seen. The Announced may only possess a number of Soul Blessings with a particular Maxim as a prerequisite as they have dots in that Maxim.
                                                            • Sect Blessings: These use the Sect Status Merit as a prerequisite, and draw upon the metaphysical connection between Announced and Sect. Each Sect has a specific Blessing that manifests for free upon joining the Sect, and the Announced may possess a maximum number of Sect Blessings equal to their Sect Status +1 though rumor has it that certain leaders among the Sects have ways to circumvent the limit. If they lose status, they lose the ability to use the Blessing.
                                                            • Blasphemies: Tied to the Dread Powers of the Pandorans and the horrifying corruption and decay inherent to Flux, the Shedim may purchase these sick parodies of Blessings naturally, but the Irin can only manifest them after coming into contact with one in action. These powers are always dramatic, vulgar, and horrifying- magnifying the inherent ineffable terror of the Announced. A character may possess a number of Blasphemies equal to 10-Exousia with a minimum of 2 for the Shedim.
                                                            • Miscellaneous Blessings: Using various ratings like dots in Magisterium, Cult, Repute, or Exousia as prerequisite, Miscellaneous Blessings can range from the incredibly subtle to the overt and wondrous. The character may manifest these Blessings in place of Soul or Sect Blessings, not in addition to.
                                                            Liquidation: Whenever the Announced finishes a Mission, either succeeding or failing, they may ‘liquidate’ one or more Blessings in order to use the experiences that went into them to purchase new ones, either at Storyteller discretion or your own, to represent the mercurial and ever-changing nature of the Principle. Some never liquidate their Blessings and others; when they lose the appropriate prerequisite, liquidate them in order to purchase other things.


                                                            Using Blessings: Most Blessings have a persistent effect that is constantly supporting them, and enables them to go through life untroubled by the problems that afflict mere mortal men. Others list effects that are powered by the expenditure of Willpower or Lux- generally, use the abilities as written.

                                                            Sect Blessings

                                                            Eye for the Sacred

                                                            Effect: Announced of any Sect may purchase this Blessing, as it is technically five different versions of itself with identical powers linking each Sect with relics and artifacts of the appropriate Maxims
                                                            • The Announced applies the 9-again rule on all Rapture rolls
                                                            • The Announced may use the active version of Rapture in unison with other members of their Sect. The roll uses the highest Glory amongst the group, adding +2 for all additional participants beyond the first. They apply the best target number and “X-again” abilities they possess amongst themselves, including the primary ability of this Blessing.
                                                            • While touching a relic or artifact of the appropriate Maxim, the Announced gains an automatic success for the purposes of tracking proxies who have held it before. In this way, they can backtrack the means by which it came into their possession.

                                                            Consecrated Font
                                                            Prerequisite: Sect Status (Any) *+
                                                            Effect: Announced of any Sect may purchase this Blessing, as it is technically five different versions of itself with identical powers linking each Sect with relics and artifacts of the appropriate Maxims
                                                            • The Announced gains a pool of (Sect Status) points they may use in place of Lux to activate relics or artifacts of the appropriate Maxim, or as Willpower points to subdue their individual Flux-based taints. They cannot use these points for any other reason. This pool automatically refills itself by one point every sunrise, using the nimbus of Radiance that interacts between the Announced and their Sect
                                                            • The Announced applies the 9-again rule to all rolls to uncover or study a relic or artifact of the appropriate Maxim, whether that means uncovering information in a library or interrogating witnesses. This does not assist Rapture rolls or other magical means of searching them out.

                                                            Silenced Edict Vessel
                                                            Prerequisite: Sect Status (Any) *+
                                                            Effect: Announced of any Sect may purchase this Blessing, as it is technically five different versions of itself with identical powers linking each Sect with relics and artifacts with the appropriate Maxims
                                                            • By touching an Icon that shares the same Passion as the Announced, and shares thematic similarities with the Sect Maxim, they may contain a tier of an Edict with a Maxim requirement equal to or less than their Sect Status. Additionally, the object the power is being sealed in must be an Icon with a dot rating equal to the Maxim dots needed to unleash the tier. The Announced pays all appropriate Lux in order to do so. If the object is not an Icon, then it must be an object with a Resources rating equivalent to the Maxim dots, and they must spend a point of Willpower as well as the required Lux.
                                                            • They may only empower a single vessel for each Maxim, meaning they may only have five vessels prepared at once, unless they are made in the Announced’s Magisterium, at which point they may prepare one per dot of Sect Status before tying up Maxims. Vessels prepared in the Magisterium lose all power if they are removed. Those attempting to use a vessel must spend a Willpower point to unleash the Edict tier, and use the dice pools of the Announced at the time they prepared the vessel. Even a mortal may use such a vessel, as payment in Lux and the appropriate dice pools have already been invested.
                                                            • Once an Edict tier in a vessel is expended, the object may only be ‘recharged’. Azothic memory prevents anything other than the tier enchanted within the vessel originally, and cannot be used to contain another Edict tier.
