    Making Changelings more evident (Mage game)

    12-05-2015, 03:11 AM
    I'm STing a Mage Chronicle, and I'd like to put a couple of changelings in it.
    The problem is, it's difficult to make evident that something "fairy" is going on when the Mask is on, and anything you could see is just evident supernatural effects. If I saw the application of the second dot of Contracts of Element (Fire), I would just see someone with fire. Which could be just anything. I could interpret it as a pyrokineticist, or anything else.
    How do you suggest allowing mages to get a hint that something "fairy" is going on?

      Personify, personify, personify. Describe effects as though they were simple human actors who happen to thematically mesh with the nature of the effects in a manner that suits the context of the scene and/or the fae invoking them.

      Also, allow for a couple of evident instances of catches and pledge-crafting language; weird exchanges of goods and services drive the goblin market in more ways than the weirdly literal, and everything can potentially be communicated with in the context of an offer of boon for task.

      Permit at least a modicum of off-kilter behavior — a changeling who's accustomed to their state of affairs likely has at least one or two oaths or bargains they're keeping to, and those can be bound to get weird if they're with anything other than a regular human for a minor favor.

The Obrimos isn’t looking at the world interpreted through the Aether any more, he’s looking at the Aether in the shape of the world — the walls are straining, barely holding back the power coursing through the cables, strange mandalas are visible in the heart of the sun, and the leader is decked in chains of office.
