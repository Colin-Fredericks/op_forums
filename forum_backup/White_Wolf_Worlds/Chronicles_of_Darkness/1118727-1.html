                                                        Bear in mind Conjurers of sufficient power can make nearly anything* that is needed in an Instant Action. Could an artist paint a masterpiece? In days. The same skilled Conjurer can do it in seconds. Granted painting it the old-fashioned way doesn't strain one's mind/sanity/soul, but hey, trade offs.

                                                        A Conjurer, if captured, can be deprived of all weapons and tools, stuck somewhere, then proceed to recreate all their weapons and tools. They are never really unarmed*, never without the necessary equipment*, and with Flash Conjuration, they can even generate instant cover against gunfire.

                                                        The Conjurer's problem is when they rely on their powers too much and something inevitably happens to break their toys - which correspondingly cracks their mind. But they are probably too used to their behavior, so all it does is start a downward spiral into them being a broken, powerless shell.

                                                        * = Given that the Conjurer has the appropriate skills to know what they are doing. Which they are encouraged to learn.
