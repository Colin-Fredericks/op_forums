                      When a demon takes a piece of a life through a pact it edits out the old person and writes in the demon. So that unloved 3rd cousin is, for all intents and purposes, the demon's cousin now, and to the extent that the pact rewrites things, he always was.

                      As far as the Mask, it pretty clearly spells out that when a demon takes stuff from a supernatural through pact that they get normal mortal versions of it. The example was taking a vampire's identity as a Cover through a soul pact. The demon gets all the social connections and the physical appearance of the vampire, but this Cover is a normal mortal with no vampiric traits or abilities, just like any other Cover would be. Plus, since a Mask represents the vampire's masquerade it'll likely have a lot of normal mortal interconnections. If the demon were to take a vampire's Dirge through a pact that could get interesting.

                      With regard to actual supernatural power use, it seems thematic for demonic pacts granting actual, tangible power, but the scope of such pacts could get out of hand fairly quickly. You could do something quick and dirty if you want to use it. Say, Lesser aspect granting 1 dot of a power, Medial granting 2 or increasing an existing one by 1, and Greater granting 3 or increasing an existing by 2. That's a good bit of equivalent experience and relative power level, though (5 experiences for a Greater pact granting a 5 dot merit, versus 12 experiences for a Greater pact granting a 3 dot out of clan discipline).

                                              Demons operate by modifying fundamental values of the Universe around them. The GM has set up very specific rules for how the world works: Someone (Resources 1) dies and their money is sloughed off to different places to pay off debters, fees, taxes, etc. Now imagine how many people die every day. Demons hack the underlying code of the universe so that when someone dies, their Resources are reallocated as the Demon sees fit. Remember the movie Office Space? The Demon just rewrites where the floating decimals worth of Resources go for all the people that die in the next while, funneling it towards the new recipient.

                                              That being said, there are two fundamental questions needing answers before we can say whether Demons can use this same hackery to affect supernatural values.

                                              A) Does the Demon in question understand how the value of the Supernatural trait functions?

                                              Demons are no more aware of the how and why of other races than other races are of Demon. But, different STs may allow varied levels of discovery in regards to those traits.

                                              B) Is the Supernatural Trait in question similar enough that it has a tangible value in the universe?

                                              If someone with this Supernatural Trait dies, what happens to the resulting energy? This is, of course, assuming that Death is the only way to lose values of, say, a Power Stat.

                                                      So, I think it's important to point out here that, in the book, in the section on pacts, it says more than once that Demons don't actually know how pacts WORK.
                                                      They know they DO work, and they know (instinctively) how to make them and what the rules for balancing them are, but the Demon doesn't actually MAKE the pact work, know WHERE or HOW the benefit comes from, nor do they decide any of these things, beyond simply agreeing to the pact and signing it with the mortal.

                                                      So a Demon makes a pact with a Mortal. In exchange for the persons First Born Child (Greater relationship - Cover - +3) Permanently (duration +3), the Demon grants the Mortal Wealth (Greater Asset - Resources increased by three dots - +3) Permanently (duration +3).
                                                      Mechanically - The Demon gains 3 cover XP and a free child, while the mortal's net worth jumps from middle class to Bill Gates esque.
                                                      Now, reality, undirected by the Demon, Balances the scales. Somewhere, another mortal or group of mortals LOSE resources. But the demon doesn't decide WHO. Or HOW they lose those resources.

                                                      Relevant pages: p 189, under Pacts, 3rd and 4th paragraphs; p 192 'Where does the Money Come From' sidebar
