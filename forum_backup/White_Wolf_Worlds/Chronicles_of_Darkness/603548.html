    [Beast] Horror Form Hack

    07-26-2015, 02:38 AM
    I see there are many STs like me that likes all or most of the Beast material, but they lack one, crucial thing in the Begotten as they are – ability for changing your human body in to Horror you have inside, even for a while. So here I give you mine idea on the topic – I would also gladly listen to yours takes on subject.

    Horror Form Ability
    In times of great stress and great need, the Beasts can shed a human body they have for a short while to become living embodiment of Horror they have inside themselves. But it comes with great costs to them. Not only they are clearly visible for what they are, they becoming Hunger incarnate and by this are prone to summon Heroes to kill them. That’s a cost of Horror’s true power.

    System
    Beast Character can shed her human form for one scene ( or 1 hour, whatever is shorter ) and take a form of her Horror, limited in the Size to her normal ratings – she become monstrous being Begotten is inside on the outside. For the time being she has activated all the Atavisms and Birthrights she has bought without any Satiety Expenditure. If some Atavisms or Merits made here larger or smaller they are also taken in consideration in Horror Form.

    But there is price for the unleashing the Horror inside on the outside. Beside clearly visible monstrous nature to everyone around, character lose any points of Satiety it had and is automatically in Ravenous Condition. She also summons the Heroes, on normal rules for event of Satiety 0.

    This is all I have for now. I also thought about variant with Horror Form for the Lair numbers in turn and costing only Lair number of Satiety points, but I feel the “all in for monstrous Hunger” is much better solution – akin to Death Rage of werewolves.

    Do you like it? Are there some omissions to the rules that need to be stated or cleared?

                              Ummm.....

                              What exactly does this do other than make your Horror visible to mundane people? It sounds like what I do all the time with a Starving character as is, and that's without falling into Ravenous Condition. If anything, this sounds like a disadvantage, since you're more likely to make Heroes show up, and that makes everyone else upset with you. No one likes hunters in their backyard, and that's exactly what you'd be doing.

                              Wyrdhamster, you asked if this "balanced and potent enough?" I fail to see any advantages to this at all - you're moving right into Ravenous (avoiding the chance for Beats from resolving other Conditions on your way down), have the same mechanical benefits as if you were merely Starving, drawing more heroes and hunters to you and your brood mates and kin through exposure, have no Satiety to employ the more potent powers, will have trouble regaining Satiety after the scene ends, no access to Low Satiety effects after scene ends. That's pure disadvantage.

                              At best, the only advantage I can see is using the Power/Finesse/Resilience spread, and, even then? That minor bonus pales in comparison to the Low Satiety effects of Atavisms. Plus, if you open a Primordial Pathway while Starving, you get Lair bonus to your Power when going that way.

                              Unless I'm missing something, this is neither balanced, nor potent. Its a trap.
