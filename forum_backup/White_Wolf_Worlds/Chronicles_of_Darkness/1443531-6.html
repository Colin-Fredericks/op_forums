        Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post
        Parentage: Thyrsus, Silver Ladder, Mysterium,
        Background: Background of average member.
        Appearances: Many have green stained fingers, mainly thumbs, and many wear flowers in clothes and hair
        Nickname: Gardeners, Flower Girls/Boys, Hippies
        I am now picturing Perfuma from She-Ra and the Princesses of Power.

        Doctrine

        Prerequisites: Prime 2, Life 1, Craft 3, Expression 2, Interdisciplinary Specialty (Gardening)
        Initiation: Creation of a soul seed with a senior legacy member assistance, and planting and nurturing it for a full lunar cycle past it’s sprouting
        Organization: Small Cabals that have a few links to each other, every equinox the entire legacy meets to discuss any issues concerning the legacy and problems individual members might have
        Theory: The Gardeners know that there are cracks in the lie, places where the Supernal meets the Fallen. The places can, with help, grow and with time may completely overwhelm shatter the lie.
        I feel like gardening would be more Survival than Crafts, personally, but I do like Expression for things like arrangement.

        Magic
        Ruling Arcanum: Prime
        Yantras: Flowers appropriate to the spell+1; +2 if a supernatural flower; Inside the Garden +2; success of an expression roll relating to the spell +2; in order to help the subject grow +1
        Oblations: Tending to a garden; Creating a work of art; Removing Pollution; Resolving a Potentially Hostile Situation Non-Violently


        Attainments

        First: The Garden
        Prerequisites: Initiation.
        Every initiate has a Garden, one they have tended to since it was but simple seed. After the first lunar cycle after sprouting, the soul plant rapidly grows into a young but mature specimen of its kind. This Soul Plant must grow from the Heart of the Node. This act connects binds the Node to the Flower Girl and her Legacy, transforming the Node into a Garden. The Flower Girl has a Connected Sympathetic Connection to the Garden. The area of the Garden is the area of the Node, with the Creators Prime dots acting as the number of ley lines flowing into the node. The Flower Girl senses anything that would trigger her Peripheral Mage Sight as though she was standing at every point in her Garden. She also senses anything around plants from her Garden connected by a Ley Line. As a instant action the Flower Girl may split her senses between herself and her Garden, looking as though she was standing in any space within the garden. The Flower Girl has a 360-degree field of view. She may also change her view from any plant or their seeds from her Garden, as long as they are rooted in a Ley Line originating from her Garden. The Soul Plant only grows from Nodes charged with the Life Arcanum. Mages may Attune as normal, but Sleepers that spend a lunar cycle in the Node become Attuned. Plants in the area automatically become Attuned. The resonance flowing out of the Node is determined by by the legacy, the Flower Girl’s Nimbus, and the actions of those within the Garden, usually Growth, Peace, Plant, Truth, or Wisdom.

        • Life: The Attuned may harness the Node to bring any plant put there to any point it its life cycle, as long as that point would take a year or less. These changes require a day to complete and a successful Intelligence + Crafts or Intelligence + Survival roll.

        Plants that grow within the Garden and drink of it’s resonance grow strong and healthy and beautiful, and faster than normal. This effect is limited to effects a plant would experience if it was raised in perfect conditions.

        This Garden is the heart of the Legacy, the foundation point. If a Flower Girl’s Garden is destroyed, the Legacy attainments ceases to function, although she is still a member of the Legacy. In the event of a Garden being completely destroyed, the Flower may create a new Soul Seed. This requires a seed of size 2 or below, and the expenditure of 1 mana and 1 willpower. A Flower Girl may also transfer the soul seed to another seed at any time for the same cost as making a new Soul Seed. The moment the Soul Seed is transferred, the Garden starts to slowly lose its magic with the attainments failing each week one by one starting with the highest attainment the Flower Girl has. When replanting, the garden takes one week to regain one level of the Legacy. The seed may only be Planted within the Heart of a Life Node.

        A living soul stone. I wanted to start with this because I felt the idea had interesting potential. I wanted it to generate a Node instead of a Demesne, because I felt a "free" soul stone might be a bit too much, and it would tie into ley lines and the other roots. As for why the Node gains more charges, well I felt the Node should scale like a plant grows, starting a seed, then getting stronger. Maybe I should just have this as a Life only Node, but expand what the Life charge does. or maybe I should leave it as is.
        Of the 5 effects I feel the time and space and forces effects are not in theme. Matter is an exception because if it applies to plants you can grow extremely durable plants, which I feel is in theme. If I remove all but life, I feel like the life node should be improved as the legacy continues. I would like this legacy to be able to grow Gardens in extreme locations.
        Creating a Node here is way too much, even changing the resonance is beyond the power of the first Legacy Attainment. Putting a way to find a place to grow the Garden here makes more sense, imo, Sacred Geometry with Advanced Duration. Also, it allows the Flower Girl to choose the type of Node they want, rather than being forced into just Life (this will make the next Attainment better, too).

        For the Life effect I think maybe Speak with Plants would be pretty cool and thematic.

        Attuning themself to the Node makes sense here, as well (obviously), and I feel like giving the Flower Girl a Connected Sympathy to, and the ability to cast Remotely on, their Garden makes perfect sense, it's a double-edged sword.

        Second:
        Prerequisites: Prime 2, Expression 3, Area of Expertise (Gardening)


        The stronger bond allows the Flower Girl to take some of the Power of her Garden with her on her travels. After spending a scene tending to her Garden, the Flower Girl carries part of the Node within herself, acting as if she was within the Nodes boundaries. This effect lasts for her Prime dot’s steps on the advanced duration chart. She may share this Attunement with anyone she touches for a Mana, although doing only lasts a scene. Anyone she shares the Attunement with permanently becomes Attuned to the Garden’s Node, but can only benefit while in the boundaries of the Garden.

        I wanted the legacy to have use outside the garden, but I also wanted the garden to act as the legacies home ground, where they are their strongest. The Yantras probably cover that aspect of the Legacy. I also wanted the mage to see their though soul stone like a Prime Soul Windows and the Garden like a Familiar. In retrospect this is probably not a good idea, it allows the legacy to leave their Garden and defend it from a distance.

        The next part is a Prime Ruling with advance duration to carry the effects of the node when outside the area of the node. This might be very underwhelming with only the Life charge.

        I wanted the garden to be obviously magical, and have a effect on those within it. I also thought the legacy should have some way to see resonance.
        I think carrying the Resonance is a neat Attainment, and it pushes the Gardener to find or create the best Garden they can.


        Optional: Life 2
        Root and stem listen to the Flower Girl, following her Awakened Will. While inside of the Garden, the Flower Girl can meditate in order to shape grass, bush and tree. After spending a scene meditating in her Garden, a Flower Girl may spend an instant action in which she touches the plant subject, and she determines the way it grows for her Life dots on the advanced duration chart. She may combine this Attainment with the Life effect of the Node to grow the subject any shape the plant could potentially grow in a year. She may spend a mana to rapidly grow the subject with a turn instead of a day when combining with the Node effect.

        In addition, the Garden animates to defend itself, and it's Flower Girl. The Garden becomes a Safe Place and a Sanctum to it's Flower Girl with a rating equal to her dots in Life. This comes in the form of intuitive knowledge of her Garden like she would know herself. If the Flower Girl is in a region where there are no plants, and that is still in her Garden, then she only benefits from the Initiative bonus from the Safe Place Merit. When she is within touching distance of plants, the Flower Girl benefits from the bonus spell control slots from the Sanctum Merit as the plants draw the spells into the Roots. The Flower Girl may instead of Lethal Damage chose to impart a penalty to a roll as the plants of the Garden interfere with the Intruder. this penalty is equal to the amount of Lethal damage they would normally take. If a Intruder is not within a distance that the plants of the Garden can't grow into within a year, then they do not receive any direct consequences for entering the Garden. The Flower Girl may reflexively spend a Mana to refresh the Safe Place Merit during a Invasion.

        Living Shaping-Normal Shaping requires a reach for solid objects, because it's not in the nature of solids to readily change shape like liquids and gas. I felt that a plant shaping spell would require a reach to just have the plant quickly be the shape you want. It would start by having the grow into that pattern. Plants do move, just at a nearly imperceptible rate. I then remembered that the Life node effect allowed you to quickly grow things up to a year. I figured I could substitute that for the reach need to grow plants immediately. Again I wanted the effect to be stronger in the Garden, and I figured the most common use would be to make a Safe Place merit. I probably should remove that.
        Accelerate Growth is a Patterning effect, I realize that you're harnessing the hypothetical Life node, but still. I also don't think the Merit is really appropriate at this level and the independent animation would be a Weaving (Life 3) effect.

        I'd go with something a little simpler, Web of Life.

        "The Flower Girl can detect all living things, or any category of living thing, nearby or that comes within range during the duration of the effect with an Instant Action and in an area equal to 1 + Life dots/2 on the Advanced Scale: Area chart and lasts for a number of turns equal to her Life dots. This effect is always active on her Garden, letting her know everything within or entering her garden at all times. This Attainment emulates the spell Web of Life with reach assigned to Advanced Scale and either Advanced Duration (for the Garden) or Instant Cast (when used outside the Garden)."

        It's thematic, you're detecting living things, and it lets you monitor your Garden from afar (which can help you defend it).

        Third:

        Prerequisites: Prime 3, Craft 4

        When the Lie cracks, Truth bleeds through. The Flower Girl may spend a point of Mana to cast Words of Truth with scale: area of effect and reach for instant and the reach to create the inspired or guilty conditions.

        I always wanted a Prime weaving to let a node become a nimbus. At the same time I wanted the legacy to have a social aspect. Keeping the Words of Truth.
        I would do a similar thing here as for my suggestion on the Second Alternate: give this a permanent effect for the Garden (anyone going through the Garden has whatever Truth the Flower Girl wants revealed to them, so Advanced Duration and the Condition, can be a different Truth each time it's refreshed by changing the Garden), and an out of Garden effect (Instant and the Condition). Don't forget that it's Withstood by Composure and has Potency equal to the Flower Girl's Prime dots.

        The issue here is that it's a little unclear whether the Condition is tied to the duration of the spell, I lean towards "it's not" since it's written as a Lasting effect, but that's not 100%.

        Optional: Life 3

        The Bond between the Flower Girl and her garden grows deeper, the Flower Girl may spend a Scene tending to her Garden gain the senses of any plant in her Garden and within her Life dots of Advanced Scale: Area of Effect. This effect lasts for her life steps on the Advanced Duration chart. These plants cannot see, exactly, but they can feel, and sense the resonance of anything they touch. Animals usually have the resonance of themselves, while Mortals have the resonance of the strongest emotion or action they have recently performed. Mages have their nimbus revealed as well as their resonance. The Flower Girl is considered touching the Plants for the Purposes of her Spells and Attainments.

        A Life Prime combined Unveiling with reach toward advanced scale: AoE and Advanced duration and a reach to be considered touch every plant the spell effects.
        This would be covered by the Web of Life suggestion.

        Instead I'd recommend putting your plant control here, when used outside the Garden it would have reach for Instant Cast and Advanced Scale (for Area), for the Garden it'd be an Advanced Duration and Advanced Scale.

        The spell would look like:

        Plant Bending (Life 2)
        Practice: Ruling
        Primary Factor: Duration
        Withstand: Composure (Plants usually have zero composure)
        Suggested Rote Skills: Survival, Intimidation, Expression
        Plants in the area can be moved however the Mage desires, in an area with lots of plants the Mage can impose a penalty to speed and rolls relying on movement equal to the spell's potency. The Mage can also direct large plants, such as trees, to attack using Wits + Spell Potency as the dicepool, damage dealt is bashing or lethal depending on the type of plant (vines would deal bashing, an oak would deal lethal), and the weapon modifier is equal to the plant's size.

        Fourth:
        Prerequisites: Prime 4, Expression 4.
        The Garden’s Roots destroys more of the Lie. The Garden's Node becomes a Hallow of a rating equal to the Flower Girl's dots in Prime.

        Actually not a Prime Making but a Prime Weaving with the 2 reach for Lasting or Indefinite.

        Dice modifiers are boring, even if they fit rather well. Drawing rote quality from nimbus tilts is interesting in a short term manner, but not really a good option for a Legacy.
        This is definitely a Prime Making effect. Geomancy with Advanced Duration and Advanced Scale is more appropriate.

        Optional: Life 4
        The Roots of the Garden drink deep from the resonance past the lie. Body control with advanced duration, and +2 reach toward lethal healing quicker, on both the Flower Girl and the Garden.

        I wanted the Node to scale at some point, because node effects are very limited. I am switching to body control, because that replicates pretty much everything I wanted the node to do. It's probably not as ridiculous as every node effect scaling, but it should be nice.

        I want a legacy that turns a mages body into her nimbus, soul stones, and dedicated magical tools because that's cool. This isn't the legacy for it though.


        Fifth:
        Prerequisites: Prime 5, Life 4, Craft 5, Expression 5
        The Garden finally grows to it's truest potential. Both the Garden and it's Flower Girl are connected on such intimate level that is functionally no difference between the two. And from the union of Gnosis, Root, Soul, and Stem comes a living symbol of that connection. The Soul Plant becomes a Living Soul Stone. The Soul Plant has a number of properties that separate it from a normal Soul Stone. The Soul Plant cannot be used to create Demesnes or Annulities. The Soul Plant does not reduce the maximum Gnosis of the Flower Girl. the Soul Plant is always counted as having 1 more Gnosis than The Flower Girl that created it. The Soul Plant is always dedicated to the Flower Girl as if under the effect the Profligate Dedication and Broad Dedication merits and does not count toward those Merit's maximums. The Soul Plant is very difficult to destroy, acting a both a Perfected Living Creature and gaining the Stalwart Soul Merit with a rating equal to the Flower Girls dots in Prime that stack with the regular merit. Even if every living cell of the plant is destroyed, the Roots will simply regrow the Soul Plant within one lunar cycle.

        When the Flower Girl passes away, as all must in the end, part of her will remain in the Garden. This Echo is not a Ghost, and does not respond to Death. It tends to the garden in the Flower girls absence, and defends it as she would. It has a mana pool equal to the Flower Girl at the moment of death, and can use Attainments as she would. Mages may use a Prime version of Goetic Evocation or the Echo Chamber merit to communicate with the Echo. Legacy members theorize that the Echo is a living Nimbus Knot, drawn from the Flower Girls Connection to her Garden. It may even be present before the Flower Girl's death.
        I'd just make it a Soul Stone that doesn't reduce your Gnosis, but otherwise have all the same effects.

        I do like the Echo, but I'd still use the rules for a Ghost Mage (even though it's not a Ghost) with the Garden as the Anchor, with the exception of still allowing it to use Legacy Attainments that affect the Garden.

        Optional: Life 5.
        From the Soul Plant comes special seeds. Every solar year, a number of seeds equal to the Flower Girl's dots in prime can be harvested. These seeds, when planted in a ley line that is connected to one of the ley lines from the original Garden, grows into a new Garden, with it's own Node. New Ley Lines emerge from the new Node and the process continues. For the purposes of Attainments, these new Gardens are considered extensions of the original. The new Gardens only grow to an area as if they had added another Ley Line to the intersection. Many of these new Gardens attract caretakers, many of whom become Attuned Sleepwalkers over a extended period of time.

        I wanted some way to have supernatural plants grow in the garden, then spread outward from it. I don't think I did a good job.
        I actually like it and think it's fine.

        Just an fyi, since you made this mistake a lot: "it's" is a contraction for "it is", "its" (no apostrophe) is the possessive form of the word "it".

                                                        There is also the matter of spell control, which means each imbued plant could cost a willpower dot.

                                                        Examining the mainstream theories of imbuement, passing the spell on genetically seems dubious. By the Xoristos theory, each instance requires the mage to imbue the Imago of the original spell into the item. By the Memias theory, the imbued item Imago of that spell must be imprinted in order for its continual activations to be used. Both hypothesis seem to imply a sustained effort by a being with Gnosis is indispensable, akin to custom made orders. More information can be found on page 68 of Signs of Sorcery and I believe its the reason that spell does not have such a Reach option.
