    My biggest beef is still the word though. Obligation is a fancy word for responsibility which ties strongly into Morality. Again games about Mad Science would (and should) leap into questions of scientifical ethics, but I strongly believe we need a word that would tie it better into sanity.
    I also feel like I should bring up Humility again, since as I see it, the act of humility for Mad Scientist is not turning into doormat, but understanding that non-Geniuses are not worth less than them despite not being able to engage in Mad Science. This interpritation of system would actually put Agatha higher on the scale than Baron, but also shows the flaws of most heroic characters; Sometimes it's just hard to not consider yourself better than anyone else. The moment of Transgression is the instant when Genius sees spark of disgust and confusion and fear in the eyes of the mundane-folks and snarls "Whatever! I didn't need you anyway, sheeple!!"
    Also if one likes to think of Illuminated as what Inspiration wants Inspired to turn into, one of the possible ways to blind them to their transformation and isolate them from their support network would be stoking their arrogance.

                                                    I am most geared towards Comprehension since the word gives a mental imagine of mad scientist simply not understanding people, though Ethics too has its charm.
