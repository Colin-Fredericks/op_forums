                    Practically made for werewolf.

                    Wolf-blooded and human landlubbers would be tasked to gain blood from sailors of merchant ships so the werewolves could track them easier while at sea. Totems would be of weather or the sea, greed and other things. Weather and Elemental Gifts would let them become wildly efficient, slowing down enemies and speeding themselves, creating obstructions to any pursuers. There would probably be a much sought-after rite that allowed some sort of bonding to the ship spirit so that it would become immune to the destructive impulses of raging werewolves as if it were another raging member of the pack.

                    Likely to be Lodge of the Reaver or the Drowned God or something where members would be called Seawolves and they offered up blood or treasure to the sea, where the lodge totem hoarded it.

                                            For some reason to me it seems like something the Deceived would be more likely to lean towards.

                                            Glen Cook has a sweet series of short stories about the Vengeful Dragon, a ship cursed long ago where the crew drops dead, floating eternally on fog-shrouded seas until their curse or other sorceries call upon them to act. Then they and their crew, trained to unearthly levels of ability by lifetimes of conflict and immune to any lasting harm, awaken and act as a scourge of the seas until some unknown aspect of the curse decides they're finished and puts them back to 'sleep,' where they can have fragmented dreams of being free. Sort of what I imagine a good mummy pirate crew would be like.
