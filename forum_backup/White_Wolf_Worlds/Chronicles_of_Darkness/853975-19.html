                            And here's the preliminary draft of the system for creating Domains. As anyone who's read (read: struggled through) that rulebook will recognise, it was heavily inspired by Nobilis. Hopefully this is a little more understandable.

                            I intend to add some more example Domains underneath this, so that people can just pick them if they want to. If anyone wants to make some and post them up here, feel free!

                            Domains

                            Within every demigod, there is a shard of divine light and energy. Between this light and the world, though, a demigod’s mind and soul shape that purity into changed emanations, like light refracted through prisms and coloured lenses. These are the demigod’s Domains, the aspects of reality over which they possess power and the very representations of their being. Occult-minded demigods and scholars of Luminance sometimes liken Domains to the Sephirot, the ten emanations of En Sof, the Kabbalistic Infinite.

                            Regardless of what they truly are, Domains are possessed by all demigods and allow them to work Miracles, projections of their power into the outside world. There is no definitive list of Domains, as demigods are as unique as any other person and, thus, their Domains are unique as well. Even Domains which outwardly seem the same might be understood and manifested by different demigods in different ways. An example might be a Japanese demigod, who understands Water as a negative, cold entity and manifests it in drowning floods and stagnant water, while a demigod raised as a Wiccan might understand water as being a life-giving things and work Miracles accordingly.

                            Bequeathing Power

                            When creating Domains, whether at character creation or later, there are several steps that must be followed. Indeed, a Domain is not unlike a miniature character in themselves.

                            1. First, the basic concept of a Domain must be agreed between Storyteller and player. As a rough rule of thumb, a Domain can be anything that can be said to exist, even conceptually, but should not be vastly broad. ‘Life’ is far too broad, while ‘Plants’ or ‘Animals’ is pushing it somewhat. ‘Trees’ or ‘Canines’ are well within acceptable bounds. Also, nothing kills the feeling of divine power quicker than a useless or silly Domain, so try to steer clear of ‘Earthworms’ or ‘Teddy Bears’, no matter how cool an army of animated, evil teddy bears might seem to you.Example: Joshua is creating a character who was a soldier before his Ascension and has become a kind of vigilante. He decides that he wants his primary Domain to be based around conflict and proposes that to the Storyteller. The Storyteller is a bit leery of such a broad Domain, able to be applied to all forms of conflict and asks Joshua to narrow it down a bit. After some thinking, He comes up with the Domain of War, which the Storyteller deems more appropriate.
                            ​
                            2. Once the basic concept has been established, the Domain’s Attributes should be decided upon. Domain attributes are not numerically-defined, but rather serve as a narrative description of what that Domain does. These are important, as they define your character’s understanding of that Domain and dictate what kind of things he can do with it. Attributes are also your opportunity to add conceptual elements to concrete Domains, such as saying ‘Fire purifies’. Each Domain can have up to five attributes, and these may be altered during play with the expenditure of Experience and an altered point of view.

                            Example: Having come to a decision on his Domain of War, Joshua now needs to come up with some attributes for it. He decides on
                            • War hurts people
                            • War is ugly
                            • War divides winners from losers
                            • War drives progress
                            • War makes people less than they would be
                            3. Assign your Domains a numerical value, from 1 to 5 dots. This defines the power of the Miracles which you can invoke with this Domain, from 1-dot miracles which might conjure a small flame to 5-dot miracles which call down rains of fire and sulfur.

                            Example: After assigning his Domain attributes, Joshua decides that his character’s power over War is, while not vast, significant and so decides to give him 3 dots in the Domain.

                                            The idea was less that demigods actually reincarnate and more that their returning-via-Icons thing works even if they die of old age. Basically, when a demigod dies, from whatever means (so long as they have Luminance left), they return to their primary (oldest) Icon as a bodiless, unconscious spirit, not unlike an unawakened spirit from the Shadow.

                                            If a person comes into possession of the Icon, its Halysis aura acts upon them and as they go deeper into Halysis the demigod's spirit becomes more and more cognizant. By the 3rd Threshold, the demigod has two choices: if they stay in the Icon until its bearer reaches the 5th Threshold, they can commit (Luminance) Potentia for a month to take over the body of the bearer in a way not unlike spirit-claiming, save that the mind of the host does not survive in any way, save in the leftover memories that the demigod assimilates into their own mind. On the other hand, they can also choose to sacrifice 1 Luminance to remake the Icon that they are in into a new body, at the cost of destroying the Icon.

                                            The idea was that the first option looks, from the outside, to be the Icon-bearer becoming more and more faithful to the demigod, until the demigod 'returns through them'. And as for the virgin births thing, I'm not sure about the 'virgin' part, but if the Icon-bearer is a pregnant woman, the baby would be affected by the Halysis too, and the demigod could enter it if they really wanted to.

                                            How does that sound?
