    Chaos
    The Aspect of the Trickster
    The Storm-Callers
    The Unbound

    Introduction
    Ever since the figures of order and authority have been established in different faiths, there has always existed an equal and yet opposite to them in the form of the trickster and chaos bringers. These gods were devoted to the subversion of supreme rule, the ideal of broken laws and crossing set boundaries. The other aspects know them as anarchists, malcontents (mainly by the Order Aspected), and yet also as revolutionaries who take the way that nobody else has figured to use.

    Although the Chaos Aspected don't just play their unbound role in the mortal world, but they also serve as Devil's Advocates and subverters of rule between their Demigod bretheren. Like how Loki, the Trickster God, had caused as much trouble for his fellow Aesir as he did fix it. Or the legendary god Prometheus, who brought the divine fire to mortals so they wouldn't starve or freeze and who taught them how to give scraps of offerings to the Gods and leave the bulk for themselves to survive.

    Although, the Chaos Aspected still need their trickery to be laced with caution. For without it, they risk being cast down from their positions like so many of the legendary inspirations of the Chaos Demigod. Susano'o, Loki, Set, Prometheus, even Lucifer are cautionary tales of Chaos who take it too far...or at least, got caught.

    Ascension
    Those who ascend to the Aspect of Chaos tend to be amongst the most creative, and sometimes very deceptive, beings before their Ascension. They were perhaps amongst those never satisfied with the usual way of doing things, and so sought alternatives that seemed simpler than the established system. For example, if one told them to empty a tub of water and gave them a bucket, a spoon and a cup, the potential Chaos Aspected would choose to take the cork out of the drain.

    In the days before their Ascension, the most common blockage a Storm-Caller faces is simply one standard of conduct or authority that they simply cannot subvert. They could be a criminal trying to sell an utterly illegal substance, who fails every deal and even nearly gets caught by the police a couple of times. Perhaps they're a college student trying to find a way to cheat on their finals that their professor doesn't know of. In any case, the best way that the Ascension within the Aspect of Chaos can be called is a breakthrough. The criminal behind bars shatters them in a whirlwind of lightning. The student subverts his teacher's methods in a cascade of ill fortune. The Chaos Aspected view the world in terms of unlimited opportunity, all of them just begging to be taken.

    Drive
    The Drive of the Storm Callers is uncertain. This doesn't mean that their drive is unknown, it simply means that the Unbound do not share any collective mission upon the world. Each of their perspectives is unique, different, something that is entirely their own viewpoint. And for each and every one of them, their ideal is to subvert that which would try to chain them down. But uncertainty isn't all bad, it merely gives way to infinite possibilities and opportunities, which present themselves to the Unbound who merely looks the right way.

    As such, the Storm Callers all tend to take a place where they are allowed the most flexibility for their ventures and can potentially find ways to get around the things that keep them from doing as they will. As spoken above, one can find those of the Chaos Aspect almost anywhere they care to look. Although the closest one can come to finding a common ground for the Unbound is usually within the ranks of criminal organizations, daily wanderers of countries or revolutions in places with oppressive authority.

    However, more than a few Unbound love playing the antagonist to their felllow Demigods, even those on the same side as they are. Be it for shits and giggles or usurping the authority of their fellows, it's almost never certain why they do what they do. Sometimes, it's because they can.

    Like other Demigods, the Storm Callers can be for good and for ill. One example is how the Chaos Aspected are amongst the least likely Demigods to create a Cult. Some of the Storm Callers believe it to be for the best, as they would enchain others through Halysis when their aspect is that of freedom and possibility. Some of the Unbound believe it's better to not have people who can give a name and face if someone's hunting you down.

    Followings
    The Followings a Storm Caller would follow tend to be amongst those that allow the greatest amount of flexibility and are unburdened by obligation or supremacy. The most popular followings are those of Annunaki, Embodiments, Ascendants, and most popularly, the Following of the Morning Star.

    Cults
    The Cults of the Storm Callers tend to be based upon the fringes of society. While they are usually away from public eye, the Cults dedicated to the Storm Callers are almost always made up by the common masses of people rather than people within important positions of society. Afterall, it's because of the common masses that people hold positions of power, so what better way than the unpredictable mess of the Chaos Aspected to follow?

    However, membership within the Chaos Aspected Cults tend to be as random as their Demigod up top. Seeing two people from completely different classes in the same place isn't an oddity within their Temples. The Cults tend to form in two ways. In the first version, the Cult is simply an informal gathering of people who follow the Unbound, believing that they bear unknown wisdom and beg them to show them the way. The second version is that the cult actively worships the Demigod, believing that a storm is on the horizon and that only those who are capable of calling the storm are just as capable of leading them through it with the least harm.

    Domains
    The Domains of the Unbound hold power over the uncontrollable and untamed forces of the world, those which any and all mortals believe is beyond their grasp to hold. Perhaps strangely enough, a few of the Domains that may be controlled are almost relatively simple, something most mundane people wouldn't think is possible and yet still is.

    Common Domains include Nature, Weather, Fate, Conflict, Water, Air, even Emotions, Secrecy, Travelling or Thieves. Few Unbound hold onto anything that has an absolute or utterly defined meaning.

    Concepts
    Wandering drifter, Revolutionary idealist, Usurper King, Mad God, Scam artist, Criminal

    Stereotypes
    Creation: What the great mom giveth, she also taketh away if not to her standards.
    Destruction: It's one thing to subvert, and it's another to destroy until nothing's left.
    Order: It's fun, watching how frustrated they get when their perfect organization is blasted to bits. Don't ever let them catch you though.
    Vampires: For such a self-killing governments they run, you slight one and they'll all come after you.
    Werewolves: You guys are crazy! Wanna hang out sometime?
    Mages: Be honest with them, and then tell them you're a trickster god. They'll never be sure whether they should trust you for the rest of their lives.
    Changelings: It feels like someone's toyed with them to the point where they broke. I should prolly give them a proper visit to say that that's my job.
    Princesses: Look at you, so self righteous and convinced of authority. Are you sure you have no skeletons in the closet?
    Mortals: Get ready, a storms blowing and nothing you can do can stop it!

    Quote
    "You can't stop the storm."

            To keep up with SaulottheGentle , here's the Aspect of Destruction, otherwise known as the Reapers, the grim-faced gods of death and destruction. I might have made them too grimdark in contrast to the other Aspects, but I'll leave that up to my lovely readers and helpers.

            Destruction
            The Aspect of the Adversary
            The Reapers
            The Devourers

            [e.g. Hades, Thanatos, Anubis, Cipactli, Nidhoggr, Wendigo, Uoke, Shiva, Ninurta]

            Introduction

            People say that there are only two certainties in life: birth and death. Both of these ultimate fates are well-represented in mythology, birth by the myriad mother- and fertility-deities and death by the great adversaries of the many pantheons of the world. Perhaps it goes by the name of Thanatos, Cipactli, Shiva or Azrael but there is always some kind of death-figure, the keeper of the doorway into the great beyond. Not all are so dutiful as to aid humanity in their crossing, but all are more than willing to send them on their way.

            Among demigods, those Aspected to Destruction are rarely fully trusted, except perhaps by close friends or members of the same Pantheon. As a result, they tend to stick to the edges of demigod society, taking positions in Followings which allow them to make use of their destructive talents. More than a few hunt down the monsters that can crawl from the Tree, while others become inquisitors and witchfinders, securing the territories of their Followings and Pantheons from outside threats, some far stranger than Titanspawn monsters.

            Like many of their mythical forbears, the Devourers often find a place for themselves in isolation. Like Hades in the Underworld, they closet themselves away in Gardr and stranger realms, sometimes finding beings there which comfort and aid them. Of course, there are those who swim against the current and dance while they can, taking after such merry death-gods as Baron Samedi and drinking 'til death.

            Ascension

            Those who Ascend to the Aspect of Destruction are often those who see the flaws in the world, even before that trait is exacerbated and amplified by the Ascension itself. They are most often those who have been wronged by the current state of affairs. Whether that comes in the forms of government-imposed sanctions on their race or religion, unbearable superiors or even a simple chance of birth that placed them in a less prosperous neighbourhood or a poor third-world country. Needless to say, they are often troubled before their Ascension and even more so afterwards.

            Almost without exception, a Reaper’s Ascension is brought on by an insurmountable obstacle. Perhaps this is a long-standing opposition such as an unfair teacher without whose grades the demigod will never make college, or it might be something altogether more immediate, such as an entrenched enemy position. The frustration of this situation accrues, whether quickly or slowly, and eventually erupts in a conflagration of divine fury.

            he Ascensions of the Reapers are rarely peaceful or gentle.             Often, their first Miracles are wrathful and destructive, invoked to lash out at the obstacles which barred them from the life which they believed that they should have, regardless of whether they would actually help. A young demigod incinerates the bullies which have harassed him since his first day at school. Another’s battle cries shout the fanatics who’ve got his squad pinned down right out of their bodies, creating tormented ghosts which he binds to his will, even as his aura burns out the minds of his buddies.

            Drive

            The Reapers are driven to destroy, in the name of a better tomorrow. They cannot help but see the world as cracked, flawed, stained with its vices and horrors. They see the worst and must often force themselves to see that there are things worth fighting for, that the world is not completely irredeemable. They are often the Aspect which clings most desperately to their human lives, for surrendering to the terrible divinity within them would bring only ruin, for them and those around them.

            In terms of positions in life, Devourers (or at least the less megalomaniacal and nihilistic ones) tend to make a place for themselves where they can exercise their destructive urges while helping others, or at least where they can channel them to useful ends. Among the Reapers, assassins, criminals and hitmen are common, as are policemen, special ops (or ex-special ops) and other military types. For those with a more social bent, many go into law and tear apart the defenses or prosecutions of criminals.

            Followings

            Devourers most often join Followings that allow them to either project the blame for their state onto another - like the Following of Vassals, Embodiments or the Nine Circles - or to justify it with a cause, such as the Following of the Morningstar. A relatively large amount of Devourers join or found minor Followings tailor-made to their particular issues. Such groups rarely outlast their founders or spread far, considering their inevitable focus on destruction and death as virtues or necessities of some sort, but rumours persist among demigods that there is a far-reaching and secretive Following composed solely of Reapers and dedicated to bringing about the apocalypse. It may be no more than a viral idea, similar to the Illuminati, but it is true that throughout history those who are not accepted and trusted turn to other in a similar situation...

            Cults

            The cults of the Reapers are almost universally small and tightly-knit, with most of the group having close personal connection to the demigod at their heart. Most are focused around the demigod’s representation of death or destruction, either as an inevitable end, an apotheosis or an endless cycle, depending on that particular demigod’s views on his Aspect and its connotations. Some are solemn and serious sects, gathering to study the next great adventure with tomes of old magic and strange lore. Others are convivial and lively, more like a party or a wake than a funeral.

            Reaper cults often build up complex mythologies and theories on death and what comes after, although less in the narrative manner of a Progenitor's tales and more similarly to the Orphic cults or Enochian magic. Many such cults possess supernatural power beyond that of their patron, with a relatively large number of cultists dealing with ghosts, spirits and other ephemeral beings or exhibiting minor supernatural or psychic abilities. A not-insignificant number of Reaper cults have some kind of contact with the Underworld, som having reliable access to an Avernian Gate and the means to open it while others have the means to summon spirits and ghosts from the Great Black to consult for wisdom or bind to tasks if necessary.

            Another common trend in Devourer cults is that they have a defined purpose, outside of venerating the demigod and spreading their gospel. This might be to help others through the transition into death or aiding those who have already passed over and linger as ghosts. It might be a more mundane purpose, such as acting as a personal protection or assassination agency.

            Domains

            The common Domains of the Reapers are, unsurprisingly, those which relate to death, destruction, entropy and dissolution. They hold power over the things which are inevitable in this life, the rot and the cold that creep through old houses and the hate and bitterness which steal into envious hearts. Some even have a certain dominion over the very fate that drives men to their deaths. At the same time, some rule more spectacular Domains of fire, weather, air and water, the elements which wear away and consume the works of man.

            The Miracles wielded by the Reapers are terrible to behold and earn them their names as Devourers. Common Domains include water, cold, fire, weather, death, ghosts, rot, fate, emotion and conflict.

            Concepts

            Necromantic assassin, death cult leader, Drasil explorer, monster hunter, serial killer, ruthless lawyer.

            Stereotypes
            • Creation: So arrogant, believing that their creations will last forever. I can already see the cracks.
            • Chaos: Our partners in crime.
            • Order: They think that their works will be preserved? Please. Complete order requires that all motion cease. And what is that, but death?
            • Vampires: Walking corpses, clinging to what tattered shreds of humanity remain. If I had died, we could be cousins.
            • Werewolves: Their rage is a weapon more destructive than any other in their arsenal. If only they could learn to aim it.
            • Mages: What hubris, to make Death a school of magic.
            • Sin-Eaters: What strange creatures, to escape death so easily and still dance in its aftermath. I don’t know if they’re abominations or marvels.
            • Mortals: Fear the Reaper. You’ll live longer that way. I’ve seen what comes next and it isn’t pretty.
            Quote

            “The time will come for all of us, sooner or later. Even me. You though, have a choice. So, sooner or later?”

                                Thank you, ajf115.

                                I didn't see your note until after the aspect was mostly written, so it may not have the themes you were looking for. On the other hand, there was already an element of "let me show you the right way to do this", which one might consider "education" of a sort. Feel free to change anything you like, of course.

                                Order
                                The Aspect of the Judge
                                The Binders
                                The Grid-Mappers

                                Introduction
                                Almost every culture can point to some figure who introduced its traditions. Such heroes stand at the border between civilization and the disarray that came before. They are the unifiers, the teachers, the lawgivers who ensure that everyone and everything takes their proper place in society. The Order-Aspected are the modern day inheritors of this mantle. They take the disarray they see around them and replace it with harmony. Mortals and other Demigods may not always appreciate their enforcement of the rules, but they know that the world needs a responsible hand to keep running smoothly.

                                There is also another archetype in which Grid-Mappers see themselves: that of the rulers and judges who administer law throughout the cosmos. The two roles feed into each other, for each time a Binder tries perfect the system, they find themselves having to deal with dozen trespasses against the existing order to deal with (often by their nemeses, the Chaos-Aspected) and each time they attempt to interpret and enforce the existing laws, they find places that could be streamlined.

                                Ascension
                                Mortals destined to become Grid-Mappers tend toward rigid personalities from a young age. Some may belong to the classic workaholic “Type A”, while others may be more introverted, but all share traits like perfectionism and a love of routine which lead them to demand that the world around them conform to certain standards.

                                Those about to ascend under the Aspect of Order find themselves in situations they cannot control. Often this is one in which others expect them to bring about impossible results, but at other times only their own impulses demand they latch down on the chaos around them. Whether they find themselves in a new position that involves more than the job description implied or are suddenly tasked with gathering their unruly family for a wedding or funeral, the future Grid-Mapper is confronted with a swirl of anarchy with no way to make sense of it.

                                Ascension itself often comes as a relief, not only to the new Demigods, but to those around them. The first miracles are half unconscious and many times subtle enough that onlookers might not recognize the supernatural at work. Everything simply seems to come together as it should under the Binder’s gaze. Oddly, this new order isn’t always what the Demigod would have wanted before ascending; frequently a Builder sees a hidden harmony in what had once seemed discordant, and might act in the future to spread this influence.

                                Drive
                                The Order-Aspected have a drive to control. Each has a sense of the rightness about certain things: laws that should never be broken, procedures that should always be followed, authorities which should always be respected. If others do not acknowledge these rules, then the Grid-Mapper feels compelled to make them obey. At the extreme, some Binders have a plan for the entire universe to follow, and a willingness to force it into the mold they have made for it.

                                In their mundane lives, Grid-Mappers seek positions of authority from which to punish those who violate their strictures or to promote their broader social agendas. Legal careers are thus quite common. Politics and business are even more so, for in these fields an unscrupulous Demigod can leverage Halysis and Cults to expand their influence even more quickly. Even the Binders who don’t seek to advance themselves in the halls of power tend to move in the same circles as those who do, as they always seem to have a new suggestion for how things could be run a little more efficiently, how justice could be a bit more just or the world a little more perfect.


                                Among Demigods, Binders are known for their honesty and effectiveness, at least in comparison to many who belong to other Aspects. The Grid-Mappers are often trusted, but seldom liked by their fellow Demigods, who resent their habit of interference with whatever they believe needs to be fixed.

                                Followings
                                Order entails both leadership and submission in their proper places, so the Followings of Banners and Vassals have the greatest percentage of Binders amongst their membership. Next in popularity are Ascendents and Sparks, each of which has an agenda for Grid-Mappers to embrace and impose upon the world, as well as the Embodiments, who propose a place for each Demigod in the natural order. However, all the larger Followings have members of this Aspect, even ones that seem counterintuitive like the Following of the Nine Circles. The Order-Aspected are among the most politically involved Demigods, and the more arrogant of them like to claim that a Following without one of them to lead it (or at least keep the books) is doomed to soon fall apart.

                                Cults
                                Grid-Mappers generally invest heavily in their cults, crafting them into microcosms of how each Binder believes society itself should behave. This isn’t to say that the cults of the Order-Aspected are always the largest (although they do tend to be among the higher profile, often with registered religion status or disguised as another legitimate group). Rare indeed is the Binder who would prefer a larger or more influential cult to one which is fully under control, and thus much of the effort an Order-Aspected Demigod puts into a cult is to mitigate the effect of Halysis on its actions. The most common method is to have every decision personally approved by the Grid-Mapper at the cult’s lead, but democratic mechanisms have sometimes been used to keep the most important decisions out of the hands of a Halysis-ridden leadership, and the most thoughtful Binders have designed position-rotation scheme which keep any one member of their cults from too high a threshold. Of course, each of these methods is usually backed by careful screening of potential members before they are permitted to join at all.


                                The mythology of a Grid-Mapper’s cult is often almost pedestrian, a mere chronicle the members keep of their deity’s life story, detailing mundane events together with the supernatural ones. Sometimes even the Binder’s exploits are relegated to the background, and the cultists instead recount their own histories: what brought them to the cult and what they’ve accomplished in the Demigod’s service. When deeper cosmology is included however, it can become wildly detailed, as those Grid-Mappers who care enough to explore such mysteries at all want to expose the truth of the world to their followers with as much accuracy as they can uncover.

                                Binders’ cults are the most likely of those belonging to any Aspect to survive the death or Second Ascension of the leading Demigod. Some such groups are eventually taken over by another member of the Grid-Mapper’s Following, but many go on to become purely mundane organizations, their religious origins forgotten or taken as legend.

                                Domains
                                The Binders exhibit great variety in the Domains they command, for they are unwilling to let anything slip entirely out of their grip. Oftentimes they work deliberately to hone their control over a particular force which they consider essential, although the mysterious powers of Demigods are not always as easily trained as the Grid-Mappers would like. When they work together, they frequently divide the elements of the world between them, and expect each to master an allotted domain.

                                The most common Domains are abstract concepts like Truth, Obedience, Knowledge, and Excellence. Oftentimes, the first Domain a Grid-Mapper masters reflects the thing they most felt was missing or eventually came to love in the situation which led to their Ascension, and one of the goals many Binders have is to spread the influence of their first Domain, even if it is something as specific and physical as Snow or Dogs.

                                Concepts
                                Warden of the Drasil, Lobbyist, Would-Be King, Divine Bureaucrat, Codifier of Lore, Critic

                                        Following of Sparks

                                        Members of this following tend to collect faith from members of all religions equally, seeing each as a similar means for faith to express itself in the world. They seldom belong to an organized mortal religion themselves, but some may believe that the worldwide sea of Faith is itself conscious and thus be able to reconcile it with some form of monotheism or pantheism (there are virtually no polytheists among them - the Sparks believe that polytheistic religions originally followed Demigods who have long since Ascended for the second time and thus no longer have individual consciousness). The Following of Sparks is itself more like a religion than most of the other Followings: unlike the following of Reincarnates, they believe in a higher power which should be served, and unlike the Following of Vassals, they have reached an agreement on roughly what this power is and what it wants. Simply put, it wants more of itself to be spread throughout the world. Sparks accomplish this mission by spreading new Cults and founding new Temples, which tends to make them popular with other followings even if their reasons for doing so do not, for they create far more of these resources than they use themselves and are often willing to share.
                                        Sparks believe that Second Ascension is the process of becoming one with Faith again, and they see it not only the pinnacle and goal of their own existence, but of everyone else's as well.
                                        The numinous forms in this following tend to be fiery or to radiate some other form of energy, reflecting the belief among followers that they are filled with the energy of faith.

                                        Following of Reincarnates

                                        This following believes that each Demigod is the reincarnated form of some known deity from a known religion. What pantheon they believe themselves to hail from varies from Demigod to Demigod, but they have established techniques which they claim can verify a Demigod's original divine identity. Whether this actually works or not is of course a matter of faith, but true or not, the rites involved have led to this group's explosive growth over the past few decades, now that they have been freed from the embarrassment of having dozens of "Thors" and "Dagdas" at the same time, which once opened them to all manner of mockery.
                                        Reincarnates usually establish Temples and Cults based on the original religions of the gods they believe themselves to be, although they seldom place much emphasis on doing things exactly the same way as the ancients did. They consider themselves the ultimate authority within their own cults, and typically feel free to change things not only century to century but day to day as the mood strikes them. Occasionally a group of Reincarnates who believe themselves to hail from the same pantheon will create Cults and Temples which they share amongst themselves, in which case they tend to default more to the ancient example (as they understand it) as a compromise. Even more rarely, some Reincarnates may give space in their Cults/Temples to allies of their mythical persona whose reincarnation they've never met or who are believed to still enjoy True Godhood.
                                        Reincarnates are split on the issue of Second Ascension. Many believe that Demigods who vanish in this manner have returned to their otherworldly thrones, but others believe that Demigods who supposedly ascend for the second time actually find themselves starting over in a new mortal body. The rite of identity supports both theories: some of the ascended deities are replaced almost immediately, while other names never reappear.

                                        Opinions on Second Ascension are also divided: most believe it to be the final reward for their service, with the polytheists among them believing that the True Gods promote worthy Demigods to their own ranks.                                                     Enkidu
                                                    The Imbued

                                                    Being a Demigod gives you the right to stand tall atop the world, at a height that mortals can only look up to and never reach for themselves. It can be lonely up top. Some Demigods allieviate this by the great view they have over existence. Others wish that there was someone who could understand them, that isn't a fellow Pantheon member. And the rest feel the need to have a useful and loyal companion who can act as their mouth in a way a Priest can't. However, many stories of previous Demigods always give them a mortal companion who stands by their side no matter what happens. For this reason, many Demigods seek out a mortal they can invest (emotionally or financially in terms of Potentia) into to make them into an Enkidu.

                                                    To Make a Demigod's Best Friend
                                                    Though the fabled Enkidu was created by the Gods to deal with the tyrannical king Gilgamesh, Demigods hold the potential to gain the power to create an Enkidu of their own. An Enkidu creation is almost like that of a ritual. What the Ritual entails differs with each Demigod, the exact details are that it requires anointing the Enkidu with all the symbology a Demigod holds, AKA using all of their Domains upon the potential Enkidu. Icons may be used as a tool in the ritual, and they each grant a +1 bonus per Icon used.

                                                    The Ritual is an Extended Roll. The Threshold is equal to the Demigod's Luminance + the Mortal's Resolve + Composure, and a roll is made every hour the Ritual is carried out. The reason a Demigod's Luminance is added to the Threshold is due to the brighter the Demigod shines, the more it resists letting someone else butt into their glory. The beginning Dicepool is equal to half of the Demigod's total Domains added together. The cost of the Ritual, to be paid out if successful, is a dot of Luminance and Permanent Willpower. Therefore, it's not possible to carry out this Ritual at Luminance 1. It's not possible to have someone else carry out the Ritual in the Demigod's stead either.

                                                    The dicepool can gain the following Bonuses or Penalties.

                                                    Bonus/Penalty Chart:
                                                    -3 - The Mortal has no relation to the Demigod in question or hasn't been touched by the Divine from when the Ritual began. Or the mortal is a Priest to the Demigod in question.
                                                    -2 - The mortal is an active enemy of the Demigod or is resisting the Ritual.
                                                    -1 - The mortal was manipulated or compelled to take the Ritual through magical or mundane means.
                                                    +1 - The mortal is a part of the Demigod's Cult or Temple.
                                                    +2 - The mortal is covered underneath a merit the Demigod owns, such as the Staff, Retainer or Allies merit.
                                                    +3 - The mortal has a personal connection to the Demigod, such as being their friend, lover, spouse, whatever applies.

                                                    Dramatic Failure: The ritual goes awry, and the mortal is burned by the Demigod's Luminance, becoming a monstrosity imbued by the Demigod's Domains. The Demigod loses a dot of Luminance, and the Mortal immediately becomes a Tera who's ready to rampage.
                                                    Failure: The ritual fails, the Mortal is unworthy of the Demigod's light. The Demigod may never change this mortal into a Enkidu.
                                                    Success: The Demigod makes progress. If they manage to complete the Ritual, the Demigod pays a dot of Luminance which becomes imbued into the mortal, rising him to the state of an Enkidu.
                                                    Exceptional Success: The Demigod makes exceptional progress. If his final roll was an Exceptional Success, the Demigod may immediately refill their Potentia and Willpower pools, a token gift from the Luminance they invest to their new Enkidu.

                                                    The Nature of an Imbued
                                                    Becoming an Enkidu bears it's advantages. They are considered to have Luminance 1. Though they may not raise it, they have the benefits and drawbacks of Luminance 1. They may choose up to 3 traits to apply to their Numinous Form, may hold a pool of 10 Potentia and spend one Potentia per turn. They may also benefit from Eternal Flesh by spending a point of Potentia.

                                                    Enkidus also have some of the mundane benefits available to Demigods, including immunity to Halysis, agelessness and ability to enter the Drasil. An Enkidu may even use a Demigod's Icons as if they were the Demigod they're connected to. Enkidus may also regain Potentia from their Demigod's Temple or Cult, or they may regain it from their Demigod. A Demigod may give an Enkidu Potentia as an instant action. An Enkidu may buy their Demigod's Domains at a normal price (out of affinity).

                                                    True to the original story, Enkidu's have their fair share of advantage over Demigods as well. For starters, Demigods and Enkidus may not target each other with Domains. Consider them to automatically fail if they try. The only exception is if a Domain is being used to cause widescale damage such as causing a natural disaster. Since it's not targeting anyone in particular, Enkidus and Demigods can use this to harm each other. As well, when fighting an Enkidu, a Demigod is also forced back down to human levels of power. In terms of Mechanics, they're considered to be lowered to Luminance 1 against an Enkidu. Because of this, if they have any attribute raised to the point where they're beyond human levels, they're lowered while against an Enkidu. If in Numinous Form, the Demigod is lowered down to their first 3 Numinous Form Traits.


                                                    And yes, the "Imbued" part is a shout out to Hunter the Reckoning. :3
