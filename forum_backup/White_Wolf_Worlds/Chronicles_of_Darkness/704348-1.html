            There's a few rogue ghouls that exist in the setting, most notably the founder of the Malleus Maleficarum. Mythologies has a number of 'ghouls' who are immune to the vinculum, or at least highly resistant. While most people look upon ghouls as simple slaves, its actually a concept fraught with danger for the regnant.

            Regarding vampires being coerced to Embrace, sure it can happen. There's a couple bloodlines known for having blood that cannot inflict a blood bond, and it wouldn't be hard to imagine a powerful vampire catching one and manipulating it into Embracing so that it may have a number of reliable sources for Vitae.

            In both of these situations, however, remember that characters in the World of Darkness do not have access to the Vampire the Requiem corebook and the character sheets of all the vampires in town so they know exactly how to work things out to counter all their abilities. For every vampire that knows of the bloodline that doesn't inflict a blood bond, there's likely a dozen that haven't even heard of it.
