                        Is there guidance somewhere for determining the difficulties of various common uncontested actions?
                        • Lift or move various heavy things using Strength + Stamina
                        • Run for a really long time using Stamina + Athletics
                        • Stop someone from bleeding out with Wits + Medicine
                        The only thing I can find in the rules says that the ST can apply up to a -5 dice penalty based on challenging circumstances. This... doesn't feel totally sufficient. A high-gnosis mage can boost her strength high enough to possibly lift a car over her head; I'm pretty sure that's more than a -5 penalty to the dice roll and I have no idea how large a penalty to apply. Someone who is bleeding out due to a missing hand is easier to stabilize than someone with internal bleeding due to a bullet in their liver.

                        I don't understand how to determine the difficulties of uncontested rolls in a systematic and consistent way.

                                                The mage definition of souls only include human souls and awakened souls. The two types of souls that have an Oneiros and are connected to the Temenos.
