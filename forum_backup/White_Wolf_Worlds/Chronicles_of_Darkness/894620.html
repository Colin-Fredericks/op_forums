    Want to develop a ChrofD fanfic idea

    05-20-2016, 05:18 AM
    Okay, first of all, I really don't know if this is or isn't legal here. If I've broken a rule, please delete this topic and I'll accept my punishment for it.

    So, this is something that's been bugging me a long time, and I finally decided to bite the bullet and ask it here. I've long had an idea for a Chronicles of Darkness/Black Lagoon crossover fic, but the problem is, the basic premise is that, in a once in a millennium event, a freakishly charismatic and forward-thinking Ring of Demons has managed to unite an army of other monsters behind the idea of capturing a significant chunk of land, one already rather detached from the normal laws of the world, and completely severing it from conventional reality to create a literal "Hell" where they can rule openly.

    Naturally, they've chosen Roanapur for various reasons, most of which boil down to "nobody would miss it if it went missing". So, one fateful day when the stars are right, they muster their forces, unleash occultic occlusion upon the island to temporarily sever it from all communication with the outside world, and then march on Roanapur in full-force. This is a once-in-forever, do or die sort of event, so they're either going to win or lose spectacularly.

    The reason I'm actually going public to talk about this is because, really, I'm just not sure that the twilits would actually stand a chance, and so I was hoping folks might give me their feedback.

    I mean, it's stated outright in the Demon Storyteller's Guide that demonic endgames like "escape to an alternate world/parallel dimension/alternate timeline/whatever" is perfectly legit. So, theoretically, a combination of destroyed/subverted Infrastructure and patronage from one or more Archmages should be able to "break" the island away from Earth proper and transport it Elsewhere to form the nascent seed for a new world. I think.

    What I'm worried about is that whether or not an army of twilit races working in coordination with each other could legitimately conquer even a comparatively small and anarchic human community like Roanapur, or if it'd just come off as too unbelievable that they could inflict any kind of damage to a city at all. The Chronicles of Darkness aren't the World of Darkness, after all; the twilits have had a lot of fangs pulled in comparison, so whether or not they could actually destroy the existing power structure and butcher & intimidate the mortals into bowing before them is... well, I just wanted to know if it would be believably possible.

    If it helps, the army contains a mixture of the "common" splats - vampires, mages, werewolves, changelings - with a minority of sin-eaters (the rarest) and prometheans. There's a handful - about 5 or 6 - mummies, roughly two to three times as many leviathans, and their respective cults. They vary a lot in power level, but average around the equivalent of power stat 2-4; old enough to be disillusioned with humanity/the world as it is, but young enough to still believe that there could be something better out there. And, of course, there's more exceptional types, those who've reached power stat 5 or sometimes even beyond. To say nothing of how the mummies start at power level 10 when woken up. The leadership behind it all is a Ring of three of the Unchained, with perhaps more, lesser level ones serving as "recruits", and at least one Archmage interested in seeing if this experiment in growing a "new world" could be made to pay off.

    Also, to elaborate on what I had planned as their plan of attack:
    Agents of the "Army of Darkness" (for lack of a better placeholder name) have been in Roanapur for months, scouting out its power structures and priority targets, both mundane and supernatural. When a particularly favorable occultic event occurs ("the stars are right"), the army launches its initial strike, using a combination of Leviathan ritualism, Werewolf spirit-invocations and Mage spells to turn Roanapur into a "dead zone", blocking satellite feeds and all mundane communication. Nobody can see inside, nobody can get any word out.

    At the same time, the existing agents are turned loose to go after specific targets, bolstered by specialists sent in just beforehand to destabilize the situation - assassins go after the major political figures, strike squads attack Infrastructure, loci are seized, etc.

    Then, and only then, does the bulk of the army roll in out of the night to launch its attack. It's open warfare in the streets as monsters fight with police, gangsters, mercenaries, basically anyone stupid enough to get in their way. And even then, this is just a distraction, as the most potent ritualists are deployed to seize the sites necessary to perform their "Great Work" and sunder Roanapur from the rest of the world. If it succeeds, then and only then do they give their demands: surrender, or die.

    Furthermore, the basic presumption here is of the ChrofD standard in that Humans Don't Know. There are people with some vague inkling of the occult underground beneath them, but nowhere near enough that there are people actively trained or prepared en masse to fight against the things that go bump in the night. The big bosses of Roanapur, for example, do have formidable defenses, but that's because they're criminals in a lawless hive of a city; they're expecting to defend themselves against humans, not vampires or a pack of werewolves.

    So... yeah, anyway, I'll admit it's kind of a crazy idea, but still, I'd be interested in at least seeing what happens now I posted this. It would be a pleasant surprise to see if people out there actually agree that, in numbers and united, the twilit races of the ChrofD would be a legitimate danger to humanity.


    For those of you unfamiliar with Black Lagoon and Roanapur, a basic guide to the city would be that... well, probably the nicest thing you can say about the place is that it's a shithole. A lawless island city-state in Thailand's waters, Roanapur is a nexus for every single criminal activity, illegal vice or just plain depravity you can imagine. Black marketing, smuggling, drug trafficking, prostitution, pornography, assassins, mercenaries, human trafficking... you name it, it can be found in Roanapur. It's a city inhabited almost entirely by criminals, the insane, the degenerate and the desperate. Everyone knows that the real power lies in the hand of the cartels, and even then, the true powerhouses of the city are Hotel Moscow (a squadron of Soviet VDV special forces gone renegade and turned into a criminal empire) and the Kan Yi Fan Triad, who constantly jockey for power.

    To give you some idea what kind of hellhole this place is; at one point in its home series, the head of Hotel Moscow contacts the city's premier pornography distributor and asks for a copy of every single item of child pornography or child snuff in his stock featuring Romanian twins (long story, it's to identify a pair of recently arrived psychos). She gets two hundred and fifty individual tapes, and later she and the head of the Kan Yi Fan admit to being a little disgusted at the revelation.

    Frankly, being conquered and/or razed to the ground by an army of rampaging monsters would probably be a step UP for this place.

                  ...Wow. First of all, let me thank you all for surprising me with your amazingly non-hostile reactions. I would quote those of you whom I'm replying to, but my connection absolutely hates whatever this forum is running on, so I can't do that.

                  @Gullinbursti:
                  To be honest, I don't particularly want to go as high as the "Overlords tier" - the Archmages and their ilk - because, even in my headcanon, they usually have far better things to do than to directly pay attention to the works of mortals as a whole, and even lesser supernaturals. I just didn't know if they had a hope of pulling off something like this without a "Prime Mover" covertly supporting them from the Supernal.

                  I don't think I quite follow what you're saying with that last line, but I'm going to stick with the crossover/coalition approach to this. It's the true part that excites me about this whole thing; seeing what all of monsterdom could do if they were willing to get off of their asses and work together. It does need a lot of work and a bit of handwaving to pull off, but I think it can be done. It's not like this is canonical in anyway.


                  @Vent0:
                  Thank you. As the opening post hopefully conveyed, I really want to keep the suspension of disbelief bar from being too high, so any advice or discussions about making their efforts believable would be greatly appreciated. Even with Gullinbursti's hope-inspiring comment about how monsters have a lot more "punch" when they're the ones picking the fight, I don't really want to be on the losing side of a debate like "Hotel Moscow should easily weather and defeat a surprise attack by five Blood Potency 5 vampires, each with at least mastery (Discipline value 5) of their Clan Disciplines".

                  Anyway, to reply to your questions:

                  Now that you've mentioned the supernatural presence for Roanapur, I figure that it's mostly comprised of vampires, werewolves and mages. As I covered in brief in my opening post, this is an anarchic, unstable, vice-ruled place. Not the sort of place where many of the ChrofD's twilit-folk would be attracted to living - can you see a changeling staying sane in a place with daily gun battles? Hell, if a mummy lived there, the place would probably be gone already, as a result of people being dumb enough to steal her Relics.

                  Vampires are a mixture of clans; there's a heavy Belial's Brood presence here, and it's lawless enough that they're roughly tolerated as genuine equals, though the non-converts (mostly Unaligned and perhaps a handful of Ordo Dracul and Croneites) stay away. They form the largest supernatural presence here, as Roanapur is a place where blood flows like water and there's plenty of easily-drained prey to be had. As a whole, they're onboard with the invasion; Roanapur is too anarchic a place for them to really control from the shadows, and if there was a ever a place to stir their desires of ruling openly, it's Roanapur.

                  Werewolves are... in a lot of trouble. Roanapur is either one giant Wound (which is why there's a fairly sizable amount of Uratha in the "Army"; getting rid of a spiritual sinkhole like this is a win-win for everybody), or else it's such a shithole that it's the kind of place where spirits of Murder, Rape, Drug Addiction, Prostitution, Lust, etc can thrive without the direct influence of the Maeljin (assuming those still exist in the Idigam Chronicles). Either way, there's a beleaguered minority of Uratha desperately struggling to try and make some kind of change, who're so overwhelmed that they welcome the outside help, and a presence of Bale Hounds - mostly working to undo the works of the former or to give the Maeljin more of an "in" with the local Choirs. Still, in either case, there's not a huge presence of them, mostly because Roanapur is A: such a shithole, and B: such an effectively self-sustaining shithole. It doesn't need a large amount of werewolf "coaxing" to stay a place of evil.

                  Mages are definitely the minority of native supernaturals; Roanapur isn't the sort of place conducive to Awakenings. Most of them probably leave the city, if not quite so drivenly as sin-eaters (think about it; Roanapur is the kind of place where getting shot either deliberately or by accident at your local bar is just one of the acceptable risks; do you wanna think about how bad it must be for Sin-Eater to hang out here?). Definitely not in any real position to object to some outside "cleanup".

                  Primary goal for this is, I would probably say, "platform for short(?) stories". I am curious how you think it'd work as "setting material", though?

                  Focus is definitely on the event proper. That's the big thing here, the whole "night of blood and ichor".

                      I'm really curious about the rules of CofD fanfic now. Because fanfic is generally a border-case; there's arguments that it's fair use, and arguments that it's copyright infringement. The majority of author/creator-run boards have basically a DADT policy: write what you want on your own time, but if you post it, they have to take it down and tell you not to do it, because they need to defend their copyright. It's fine if it exists, but the author/creator has to have plausible deniability.

                      Except with RPGs, the whole point of the thing is to have people creating their own stories in this world, and there's plenty of "pitch your campaign" or "share your stories" or "how does this work in your version" threads, and that sort of thing is fine. So I'd guess as long as you're not planning to sell it, it's okay?

                      That said, in terms of your "does this suspend disbelief" question, I'm a little confused as to which supernaturals are where. Initially, it sounded like you had a small group taking over a town, and I can see 10 or so supernaturals rolling a town of mortals. But then you say "army," and I've got no idea how big that is; assuming "5-6 Mummies" is one of the median numbers, that implies over a hundred in this army, and yeah, they can definitely roll a town, even with a number of other supernatural groups who would oppose them (as long as they strike first and fast, which you have them doing. Whether or not they hold the town, that's a different problem.) Honestly I think the archmaster angle throws it in the opposite direction; having someone like that involved seems more unbelievable than your army taking the town. (That and I disagree about your Mage/Sin-Eater numbers; stress and strife cause Awakenings, and more deaths = more people willing to make a deal to not be dead.)
