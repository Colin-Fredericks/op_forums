                                    Hello everyone! I am pretty new to the Genius but I've been reading development on this thread with star eyed excitement and wanted to share some of my own ideas and insight with the group. Unfortunately I have no first hand experience on the mechanics of the actual gameplay, because I haven't had a chance to play World or Chronicles of Darkness, but let's just see if any of my ideas might work?


                                    Firstly, Obligation. The mysterious Code of behavior encouraged by the Peerage and ignored by Lemuria... feels slightly out of place in a game about Weird Science and whimsical slightly amoral scientists messing with reality for fun and profit. Obligation seems to be sort of "don't be a weirdo" -morality system in a game where being a weirdo should be part of the appeal. This is on the top that the game of psychological horror and mad science seems to be completely missing an iconic Sanity system.
                                    Here is my idea how to "fix" this: Let's instead measure how much "in touch" Genius is with humanity. How much they can relate to other humans and humans can relate to them, and how much Genius cares about this ability. After mulling over the different terms that could represent it I finally had an epiphany. What if we flip the Sanity scale into Insanity scale and call it... Alienation, signifying Genius's unwanted journey from your average geek into a walking element of destructive potential of Mad Science. At Alienation 0 Genius can easily adapt to people they are trying to sympathize and empathize with (maybe even too easily?) while the higher they climb the ladder of Alienation the less they can understand them, and slowly consider them more and more just cattle. Alienation would represent how isolated Genius has become from the Consensus, how fast they make their mortal allies turn on them and how quick does it take mundane to bore them.
                                    Alienation would also make another stepping stone in "redeeming" Lemuria, as some people in this thread have suggested. While Peers were busy promoting their principles of Obligation and the Law, they might have Alienated themselves from humanity just as much as Lemuria. It takes both halves of Inspired community to realize how distant they have grown in their endless grudges and flame wars.
                                    Examples of Alienation: Bruce Wane, Alienation 2; The Doctor, Alienation 1; Doc Brown, Alienation 4, Rick Sanchez (season 1), Alienation 8; Stanford Pines, Alienation 5; True Fea, the God-Machine and Illuminated, Alienation 10.

                                    Second: This thread has brought up several times the matters of Unmada and the broken worldview of Geniuses. I recall some suggestions and some rebuttals, but my biggest complain is about how out of place "worldview" sounds in a game filled with techno babble and science-y lingo. My suggestion: In addition to Aesthetic (and possible Opus) each Genius should have a Theory. Theory is an explanation, narrative, a flawed model a genius has constructed around themselves and their wonders. Geniuses Wonders, even the ones they built on dismissed scientific theories, often fit into the larger system Genius is subconsciously iterating. For Unmada Theory and Aesthetic become distinguishable from truth as their field warps reality to confirm them. How flawed the Theory is, and how much does Genius really believe in it, should be up to the player and Storyteller. Theory could play more important role in certain foundations, baraminds, and fellowships.
                                    Examples of Theories: Dr. Vienna, Theory of Aesthetic Function; the Doctor, Timey Wimey -Theory; average Phenomenologist, Archweltanschaaung -Theory; Stanford Pines, Theory of Weird Magnetism.

                                    At the moment I haven't been able to come up with any mechanical importance for Theory, beside similar one to Aesthetic. The key difference between Obsession, Theory, and Aesthetic is that the First one is what Inspiration wants Genius to do, Second is narrative Genius spins around their Inspiration and Wonders, and Aesthetic is how they make it look.
                                    So did any of this make any sense?

                                            Data Breech ••
