    Working on a side project thanks to this, listed below. Just so we are all clear, this is a sample of a work in progress:

    Demon Translation Guide Extra: Rituals
    Inspired from: Blood Sorcery Rites, Vampire Devotions, Hunter Tactics, Awakened Thaumaturgy Spell Factors, Forsaken Rites; Wyrd Sorcery
    A new Rite costs one half of an Experience point per dot, rounded up in the Ritual. Hadrisel's Libation, which requires Awakening 3 and Storms 1 costs 2 Experiences. (I've fiddled with the mix.)
    Design Principles: (Unless others stated, assume all costs listed in the Players Guide hold true)
    • The Ankida: leader of the teamwork action, must know the Rite and the Primary Lore, selects target & spell factors.
    • The Mudu: supplemental actors, must have requisite knowledge of at least one of the Supplemental Lores and be willing to take direction from the Ankida.
    • The Khauiki: Mostly redefined; tertiary actors, they act as power batteries (spending Faith/Willpower on behalf of Mudus who are running low) for the Rite and reduce casting penalties, (rather than providing bonus dice). Still requires basic knowledge of one of the Lore Paths used in the Ritual. Think Yantras from M:tAw 2nd.
    • Restrictions: By adding resonant limitations on the performance of the Rite (such as times, places, sacrifices, specialized equipment, etc), the power increases proportionally (measured by Spell Factors). This in addition to what the success are spent on, and never include Potency, (does include size, duration, distance, etc)
    • The Sigil: (Yes, I'm still keeping it.) This includes all prep work, and at a minimum requires a (symbolically appropriate physical) circle with the Ankida & Mudu evenly spread around. Any Khauiki must form a second circle outside that one.
    • Casting Time: Limited by the lowest dicepool of all the Mudu, like all Extended Actions. Time interval between rolls is (Total Lore dots in Rite's Design multiplied Total Lore Paths in Rite) in minutes. eg; The revamped Hadrisel's Libation has each die roll every 8 minutes; needing 4 successes before the Ritual breaches threshold.

    *Includes Thralls, though they may have a smaller penalty if their knowledge of the Lores used exceeds the minimum.
    The rules for Maintaining the Ritual (pg 184) function as normal (Stamina + Occult for Ankida & Mudu), as do the rules for Losing Control (pg 184), Subverting Control & Torment. Ignore the difficulties.
    Faith Roll: Needs successes equal to Lore dots; extras spent on Spell Factors
    Mudu Dicepool: Attribute + Skill as appropriate based on non-prep Sigil
