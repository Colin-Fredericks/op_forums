    Originally posted by WHW View Post
    What is the purpose of Synthesis Embodiment of Strix? What *is* the mental make up of the...thing that emerges? What are their memories? What...is???????
    There's several purposes.
    1) Give credence to the idea that the strix are the true origin of vampires.
    2) They had the ability in Requiem for Rome, and it would be very odd if it didn't carry over.
    3) Strix are alien entities not entire concerned with self preservation as much as having fun and dragging vampires down to their level.
    4) Crazy strange things for story hooks.

    The mental makeup of the thing that emerges is no different than any other sort of fresh embrace, or a particularly low Humanity vampire. You don't suddenly absorb memories or new personality shifts beyond just having a Beast that's closer to the forefront than usual. Which is admittedly a big mental shift, but not one that can't be reproduced through rapid-fire Humanity loss in a short time.

    Synthesis effectively kills the strix. Now, you could do a whole Tainted routine and absorb some memories there, but that's not what happens by default. You get a power up shroom, and loss of Humanity. That's it.
