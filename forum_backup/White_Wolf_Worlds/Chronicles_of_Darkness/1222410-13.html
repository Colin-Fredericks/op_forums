                            Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post

So in Sleepers it causes bubbles of intense self delusion, and in everyone else should gain a mage like obsession with understanding mysteries from murders to the stranger things caused by the Wasteland. Physically, it starts empowering and intensify minor supernatural elements and drawing in monsters who feel the wasteland as beacon. It doesn’t actually empower them to make the journey, but it makes them want to. Maybe at High levels it opens a Verge or something equally scary.

                                    Torment is harder, since Tass and Mana don’t really do anything by themselves when inside someone, usually. Let’s see, how would having too much Truth juice manifest…getting extremely bogged down by the details and getting massively overwhelmed?                                                 Amirani Humour: Yellow Bile. (Remember, they were the precursors to the Frankensteins. This is also reflected by their Wasteland being identical to a Frankenstein's.) Their Torment comes in the form of anger at “the base nature of the world. Human depravity and injustice anger them, but so too do apathy and entropy. The world should be different, it should be better, and the Prophet knows this. Of course, it is the nature of prophecy to be ignored, and when it inevitably happens, many Amirani become bitter and resentful.

                                                Worse than being ignored, though, is when the Amirani is forced to confront his own inadequacy. Nothing infuriates him like seeing his own failures. The fire in his soul wants to move, to rise. If he is backed into a corner, and has no clear way to move forward, that fire consumes his own heart — or the people around him.

                                                When in Torment, an Amirani's body shows signs of its painful history. The head of a hanged Prophet might jerk or LOL in an unsettling way. A crucified one might manifest stigmata. A Prophet with less obvious injuries might smell strongly of brimstone, or a bystander might see a strange visions reflected in her eyes.”

                                                They also have the option to take a Bestowment that grants “insight into their world and the true nature of reality”. It requires them to injure themselves; and for the duration of the Injury, or 24 hours, they get a bonus to all rolls that can benefit from Willpower.
