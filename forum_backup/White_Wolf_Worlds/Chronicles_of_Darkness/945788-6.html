                    I'd love to pitch Industrial Revolution Demon/Promethean, with the focus on the God-Machine encouraging a mechanised society (with a central question of why and why now) while the economic and social ramifications, including mass migration to squalid cities, make wonderful grist for Prometheans.

                            Originally posted by amechra View Post

                            To clarify:

                            For Werewolf you have the freikorps and the (sometimes violent) politics -as one of my professors once put it, it was a democracy run by people who didn't want a democracy for people who didn't want a democracy. You have that whole aspect of Germany losing a war for the first time to play with. And I don't doubt that the Shadow would be... interesting.

                            For Changeling, you have the whole hyperliberalism of the Weimar period. Prostitution and homosexuality were legal, and women had the vote (almost 2 years before the US). There's a wild backlash in the art and nascent film movements that oppose the naturalism of their predecessors, and philosophy is all over the place. Juxtapose that with the atmosphere of poverty, shame, and anger that ultimately led to the Third Reich, and you've got an interesting backdrop for Changelings.

                            For Geist... a juxtaposition of the beginning (WWI and the Spanish Flu) and the end (Reichsmordwoche, Kristallnacht, and the rest of the rise of the Nazi party). That's all I need to say, really.
                            And then the sobering thought that any nonsense perpetrated by these splats is going to pale in comparison to a horror the world as never seen before...

                                                            First of all, Mummy is probably my favorite CofD gameline and I suggest you definitely give it a look; you'll either love it, hate it, or be like me and hate it until you figure things out and then love it.

                                                            The long and short of things is the era you're looking at is during the existence of the Nameless Empire, a predynastic Egyptian civilization centered on the lost city of Irem that was ruled by inhuman necromancer-priests called the Shan'iatu. All of the mummies would still be alive as mortal human beings in their Guilds, practicing their sacred crafts until they're ritually murdered by the Shan'iatu. Things get a little weird because they they stay dead for another 1400 years, so a Mummy setting at the time would just be a mortals game in a lost semi-mythic Bronze Age.
