        A channel that could be expressed through multiple heads is fine; a channel that requires you to have multiple heads is not. I try to be as flexible as possible about how channels work (is Besieging the Power of Will pure brain to brain telepathy of glowing swirly hypnotic eyes?). Call of the Depths is probably the only exception, but singing is strongly associated with merfolk.


        The Channel you proposed feels closer to Awareness than Sanctity. For a strategy Sanctity power I'd lean more towards a "subconcious telepathic mission control". For a multi-thought Awareness channel perhaps something like.


        Panoptic Tyrant
        • Depth 1: Birthright of Awareness
        • Depth 2: The leviathan gains the ability to run two simultanous trains of thought, allowing them to take two mental actions at a given time. They could, for instance, read a book while simultaneously solving a complex piece of arithmetic. She could not read two books at once, though, as her eyes cannot look at both at once - probably, at least.
        • Depth 3: The leviathan may take in a wholistic picture of their suroundings and analyise it. They must spend a full turn and roll Perception. On a Success they may ask one of the following questions. Every two further successes grants another question:
          • Is there anyone here I do not expect to be here?
          • Are my servants where I expect them to be?
          • Is there an unexpected source of danger here?
          • Is there anything noticably out of place here?
          • What avalable escape routes are there (including escape into the Rift)?
        • Depth 4: The leviathan's natural senses grow so varied and precise that they exceed natural laws. Whenever a supernatural power makes the distinction between natural and supernatural senses the leviathan's basic perception counts as whichever would be more advantagious. The leviathan can also see wheather something is supernatural or not supernatural, however identifying what kind of supernatural phenomina they are looking at requires occult lore and experience. Supernatural defences can block the leviathan's sight.
        • Depth 5: When using the Depth 3 itteration of this channel the following additional questions become availble:
          • Is there anything supernatural here?
          • ?
          • ?
        • Depth 6: The leviathan is constantly subconciously gathering and processing information, their mind is filled with useful information that they do not even know that they know. Once per session they may use the Depth 5 itteration of this channel reflexively.
        • Depth 7: So long as the Leviathan is in a place (or perceving a place) they know intamately they apply the Rote Action rule to all Perception rolls. Their finely tuned mind and senses detect the slightest change with contemptable ease.
