    Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
    MCN Great advice. And it involves no actual changes to the game mechanics, just presentation/rebranding.

    Something to keep in mind with Angels and "Mysterious Ways": They don't actually "talk" to God. They are given their mission and all the knowledge they need to fulfill it (as determined by the boss)... but not why. And theirs' is not the place to ask.
    First, let me point out that angels can querry the God Machine for more information. Wither or not they get it is questionable, but it is still possible.

    Well, this is actually where the comparisons to the biblical God falls apart. God isn't a discrete being like the Paternoster Exarch. You won't find a rank 10 angel in charge of everything. God is like the internet - its everywhere, and made up of millions of computers, routers, wiring, signals, servers, "clouds" and more. When you get information off the internet, you send out a general search through Google (because other search engines don't exist ), and get told where you can find said information in places. Now, put Skynet into the Internet to copy itself a million fold, and give all that information direction and a Mission. That's the God Machine. God isn't a single discrete part, its the sum of every Infrastructure, stigmatic cultist, angel and relic. This is the CofD meaning behind "God is everywhere." Because angels and Infrastructure are everywhere, and God is every one of them.

    This actually is more interesting, I find, than a discrete but all powerful being. You can actually have a Council of Angels or a pantheon of gods as a stand in for the God- they go down, the local Command / Control structure fails to work, and the local G-M ceases to operate. Hell achieved, God is dead (zones).

    The God Machine is mroe than just the Christian God. Its the Laws of Heaven as described by Confusious. Its the Hindu gods and their guiding hand over the fate of their people. When God is a machine, you can take it as a whole, or take it down bit by bit.

    For Integrators, you might change why Demon's get hauled back in - It is not so much because they are Demons, as because they break the Laws.
    Part of the reason why the God Machine "hates" demons isn't because they rejected God, or even that they're wasted resources. No, its because demons are Unchained from it. Every other person in the world is connected to God, and part of its calculations. Demons are outside those calculations, and can do whatever they want, which messes up the G-M's careful calculations. So, yes. By merely existing, demons mess up the Divine Mission. When they play the part of an average person, its easier to slip into the variables. Breaking the "divine law," however, causes major changes to the flow of Fate in the world.

                        Originally posted by Korogra View Post
                        I dunno, I wouldn't say the God-Machine hates demons.
                        The God Machine as a whole is technically incapable of the emotion of hate. That's why I put the word "hate" in quotations. But, make no mistake, the God-Machine absolutely does not want demons running around for more reasons than just seeing them as malfunctioning parts. Demons actively interfere in Its Missions, even when the Unchained try to stay away. Like I said, that's part of the reason.

                        Yes, recapture and reintegrate is another part, but that is one that everyone focuses on. I want to highlight parts of the story that people often ignore and gloss over. And "turn you back into Angels" is less stereotypical biblical demon than "Demons corrupt the world and upset the God's Plan by their mere presence."

                        Edit- Furhtermore, while the God Machine does reintegrate angels, it also has Hunter Angels that just utterly destroy demons rather than let them roam around free or reintegration. This leads me to believe that reclaiming resources is actually a side benefit rather than the main reason in many cases.
