    An idea I had:

    Escape Clause (•)

    When a Beast takes this Merit, the player designs an Escape Clause that ties into their character's Legend and Hunger. This is some action a person can take that renders them immune to feeding (for a time, at least). Returning the slab, showing the video tape to someone else, spreading goat's blood on one's front door, and apologizing sincerely are all examples of an Escape Clause. If a potential victim fulfills the requirements, the Beast can gain no Satiety from that person for the reset of the story, and also takes a Beat. To even harm said person requires spending a point of Willpower, and the roll is still penalized by the Beast's Satiety. Conversely, if the Beast does feed from someone who fails to fulfill the Escape Clause within the set parameters, increase the Satiety Potential of the meal by one.

    An Escape Clause in effect helps to solidify the narrative of a Beast. They are most often used by Nemeses, as a way out of suffering punishment, but any Hunger can find creative ways to use one, such as a Ravager that won't destroy a building marked with a certain arcane sigil, a Tyrant who respectfully withdraws from anyone who bests her in honorable combat, or a Predator who finds himself unable to kill anyone who's recently confessed their sins to a Catholic priest. An Escape Clause should be an action that an average person should have at least a chance to accomplish, though it does not have to be readily obvious or lacking in twists.

                        Mountain of Meat oooo
                        Prerequisite: Giant, advanced

                        The character isn't just towering, he's HUGE. Packed with muscles to the point even bodybuilders grow green with jealousy, the character enjoys a number of benefits:
                        - muscle mass provides cushion: reduce bashing damage by 1.
                        - although they are not actually taller, their effective Size is considered to be +1 for purposes of Health etc. due to the added musclemass
                        - add 2 dice to Strength-related rolls

                        However, being a towering bundle of meat has its drawbacks:
                        - Consider Stamina to be at -1 for purposes of enduring hostile conditions, holding your breath, staying conscious etc. Basically, whenever it says (Stamina) Rounds, subtract one.
                        - the body demands highly increased nourishment, and the character's wallet feels the effect: Resources is at -1. If the Character does not have the necessary financial means to provide for their needs, they have to steal, extort or otherwise acquire food IC or suffer from the Malnourished Condition.
                        - looking inocuous is hard, and not drawing attention is even harder: people gain an additional +2 to remember or notice you.


                        Malnourished (Condition)

                        The character severely lacks the necessary chemicals to keep themselves alive and their body functioning. Take a -3 to all physical actions. In addition, they cannot properly concentrate: mental skills lose the 10-again ability. Finally, looking intimidating is hard when you look sickly and weak - social rolls depending on physical strength or hostile authority also lose the 10-again ability. However, appealing to people's pity comes far easier when looking this pathetic: take 9-again on rolls appealing to people's empathy.

                        Resolution: Eat appropriate foods and rest for (Size) hours afterwards.

                        Beat: Fail at a task that would otherwise be easy because you are not in peak condition.

                                                Here's my attempt at a set of Amnion-style merits:

                                                Humanity's Monster (O-OOOOO)
                                                Prerequisites: Resolve 4, Silver Ladder Kinship or Mind 3 Mage Kinship
                                                Effect: To name something is to claim it. Infant Horrors squirm up out of the Mother's Land and are named and shaped by the Dark Dream of humanity. When the Beasts these Horrors bind to return to their Mother's Land, the Dark Mother attempts to embrace them once more - or devour them, if the difference means anything to Her - curtailing the explorations of the Begotten in her Land. However, there is a way to guard against the Mother's terrible love: to embrace the mythology of humanity and clothe yourself in it as armour, much like how the strange and powerful magi wrap themselves in their ego and self-image.

                                                This Merit grants the Beast an effective Amnion, which she can clothe herself in with an Instant action provided she has demonstrated her Legend this scene, granting general armor equal to her rating in Humanity's Monster against attacks that appear physical, and against the Ecstatic Wind. This pseudo-Amnion is treated as a form of equipment. It doesn’t stack with “physical” armor, but does stack with Atavisms such as Unbreakable and similar mystical powers. However, this does come with a drawback. As an embracing of her Horror's Legend and human side as opposed to its primal power, she suffers a -2 to rolls to activate Atavisms.

                                                Beneath the Monster's Shadow (OOO)
                                                Prerequisites: Humanity's Monster 3
                                                Effect: Monsters are creatures of terrible power - it is their nature - but so too are there monsters whose myths name them as protectors and guardians. A Beast can apply the same principles as imposing Lair Traits to protect their companions. Upon purchasing this Merit, the Beast gains the ability to forgo the normal effect of Mother's Kiss to instead grant the recipient the effects of Humanity's Monster for the scene. The Beast must have her pseudo-Amnion active in order to choose this alternative effect.

                                                    A very rough alternative:

                                                    Don the Dragon's Mantle (• or ••)
                                                    Prerequisites: Family Ties with a mage.
                                                    Effect: The Beast draws forth the shape of her Horror, replacing the normal Dream Form she assumes outside of her Lair. By spending a dot of Satiety, her Power, Finesse, Resistance, and Size are treated as her Horror's without any additional dots conferred by her Lair rating until she leaves the Astral or returns to her Lair.

                                                    Taking this shape allows her to spend a point of Willpower to negate any amount of damage done to her Dream Health by the effects of the Mother's Land, but she does not add her Satiety to rolls to use Nightmares or the Mother's Kiss Kinship ability while in this form.

                                                    At two dots, she gains general Armor against the effects of the Mother's Land equal to her Occult rating, and if she is "killed" by the Mother's Land, she may sacrifice a dot in this Merit rather than lose a Chamber from her Lair (Sanctity of Merits applies). Sacrificing a dot in this Merit in this way inflicts the Deprived Condition on the Beast, which resolves the next time she gains Satiety.
