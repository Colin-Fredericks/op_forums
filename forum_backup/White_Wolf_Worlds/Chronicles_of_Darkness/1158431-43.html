                            Building on this:

                            Writers have word counts to work with, and often getting across the conceptual space players will be gaming/imagining in takes the precedence. It's more important, for instance, that you comprehend that, in my project's case, the interplay between societal expectations and interpersonal relationships and the cynicism and low-key hopes and how that shapes you between these things for Paris than it was to give out a contract that embodies Black Rose's bond to Severance. With a well stocked game much as Changeling Second is, it's often enough to offer appropos Contracts at a discount. This was a couple of people's attitude.

                            I'll admit, I'm wrapping up Chapter 4 still, so I can't comment on the setting subject, but the simple fact of the matter is that if you are looking for something present in Underhill that's absent here....you'll be disappointed, because Underhill operated much the same way, even down to Seasonal Courts still having more contracts.

                            In the mean time, while there are points I have quibbles about, the simple and honest fact of the matter for me as this goes is that this version is just simply better-better written and more fully realized. Each entry makes me wish more and more that Rose would notice that I e-mailed her about wanting to write for Changeling Second, because I'm falling in love with Changeling all over again.*
