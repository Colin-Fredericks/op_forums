    [Requiem for Rome] Ancient Bloodline: Molochim

    01-02-2018, 01:09 PM
    I realize that the Requiem for Rome crazy is long past but for yours and mine entertainment I decided to write up this bloodline and post it. Mostly because I’m more interested in historical settings than in modern settings and Requiem for Rome is the best known historical setting for VtR. When or if I get time, energy and will to get through VtR 2nd edition I may update the bloodline. But we'll see what happens on that front.

    The only thing that I don’t really like the result of so far is the nickname, if anyone has an idea about it I’d be happy to listen and what to call them in singular also needs some work and perhaps also in the general bloodline name. If anyone who knows Hebrew (which should be the closest modern language to ancient Canaanite) have time and interest to offer some suggestions I would be very happy.

    But all comments are naturally welcome.

    And last thing. :P I tried to keep the text reasonably short in order to make it more reader friendly, please ask if anything's unclear.

    Molochim

    General info and history

    The Molochim claim ancestry from the eastern side of the Jordan-river and the deity who held sway there. From there, they claim, have they spread westward, into the Levant and followed the Phoenicians to Carthage were they taught the Kindred of those lands their founder Moloch’s craft.

    The being called Moloch is claimed to be the Bloodline’s founder and ultimate Sire and their legends say that he made slaves of kings and warred with anyone who would deny his divinity, while gluttonously drinking the blood of his mortal subjects. Those of a conservative pagan disposition claim that Moloch was, or is, in fact a god while the Hellenized and Christian claim that he was a powerful Kindred. But regardless of that fact they all agree it is his legacy that runs in their veins and that he was the most wonderful and good friend to anyone and the worst kind of enemy to all who affronted him.

    Given their history the Molochim traditionally takes a stand of patronage, especially for mortals, where they are both generous and greedy. Generous to offer assistance to all who needs it but greedy in demanding their tribute for assistance delivered, and all the gods of heaven help anyone in their debt who cannot pay for the aid given. Some conservative members in the Bloodline’s old lands in the east hold that only the fruits of one’s body are acceptable tribute but most Brazen Bulls are not so specific in their demands, but not seldom as demanding. Given this attitude the Molochim has slipped into Rome’s patronage system to dispense their favour to those less fortunate than themselves, but almost always with a high cost attached to their help.

    Given the mentioning of their founder in the holy texts of the Christians many pagan Molochim claim they have been warring with the followers of the Christian god since ancient times and legends are abundant of great victories and demonstrations of the foolishness of the Christian’s beliefs for anyone who wants to listen.

    Parent Clan

    Daeva

    Nickname

    Brazen Bulls

    Wing

    The Molochim are most often found within the Peregrine Collegia or to a lesser degree in the Lancea et Sanctum. Membership is often declined in the other wings due to the ties of the Bloodline with ancient Carthage which leads many Romans to suspect lacking loyalty. As such many end up in the Collegia for want of other alternatives and adopt their ways to a Roman environment. And some embrace the teachings of the Christians and point to the mentioning of their founder in the Holy Scripture and takes great pride in their history of wickedness, now enlightened to serve God’s cause. Like the graven images of old they will tempt people to make offerings onto them before putting faith in the Living God.

    Appearence

    Elder Molochim are typically of a North African or Middle Eastern appearance but younger Brazen Bulls are drawn from across the Roman world. They tend to dress in the manners of convention where they reside but sometimes with a slight nod to the iconography of their founder or local divine iconography. Bull, fire, sun, strength and rule symbolism are most prominent in how they wish to project themselves.

    Haven

    While not necessarily their haven many Molochim tend to have some kind of space to receive supplicants and that is often outfitted to impress them, often drawing their inspiration from temples.
    Many traditional pagan Brazen Bulls have some kind of shrine to Moloch and the most zealous of them make burnt sacrifices to their founder who it is said had conquered even the flame.

    Background

    The most common trait that unites the Brazen Bulls is a confidence and a patronizing attitude. A typical Brazen Bull, as the bloodlines like to imagine them, is a person of beauty and confidence whom all scramble to appease and gain the favour of and who dispense that favour and aid to its favourites in exchange for precious gifts. And when affronted the Brazen Bull brings down such horror that only the wrath of a god can compare.

    Character Creation

    Almost all Molochim favour Social Abilities highest followed by physical on a second place and many works to establish herds of devotees and retainers in the case of favourites and allies and contacts to make their unlife easier. And those who can often create temple-like havens for their celebrations and pride. In the heartlands of the bloodline a Childer can be groomed for years inside a vampire’s cult before given the Embrace, but such is not the case in Rome where many accept more local standards about the Embrace with less cultic overtones.

    Bloodline Disciplines

    Majesty, Vigor, Celerity, Nightmare

    Bloodline weakness

    Works like the Daeva weakness except that when they must always accept something that is offered to them and does not produce immediate harm, or lose a Willpower Point.

    Organization

    The bloodline’s organization varies. In the old lands east of the Levant and in North Africa the old system of favour-currying and direct worship of one’s Sire and elders still take place in a kind of local and living mythology for the mortals and younger members to marvel at while at and the elders to bear their hate for the atheists who denies the gods. In Rome, Greece and the like the Brazen Bulls have conformed more too local standards and so exist more in a more Greco-Roman Sire-Childer relationship with more individuality attached and somewhat more freedom for the Childer.

    Concepts

    Greedy Benefactor, Feared Friend, Everyone’s Best Friend, Unliving God, Cruel Mistress, Demanding Patron, Scandalizing Foreigner, Serial Diablerist, Serpent in the Congregation, Priest of his Sire
