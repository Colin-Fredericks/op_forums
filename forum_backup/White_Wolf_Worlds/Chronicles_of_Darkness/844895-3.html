    I still say that a Werewolf RPG/GTA hybrid sort of thing would be great. Especially if you had a "hunt planning" feature similar to the heist planning feature from GTA V or Payday 2 (preferably a mix of the two, where you choose the general approach and which pack mates to take (Like in GTA V) and then get to go into the fiddly details like in Payday 2's heist planning mode) for the really important hunts. The regular hunts would have something like the planning screen for the smaller hunts in Payday 2.

    And of course, there would be a drop-in multiplayer option during the hunts where your friends could play the other werewolf packmates. Human packmates would have very specific jobs to do on hunts (Getaway driver/pilot, hacker, medic, surveillance expert that sort of thing) but would give an out of hunt benefit. Not sure where to fit in wolf blooded packmates. They are just powerful enough where average team AI won't cut it, but I'm not sure if players would go for them.
