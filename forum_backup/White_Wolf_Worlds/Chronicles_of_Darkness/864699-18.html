    Is it possible for a ghost to have a whole city as their Anchor? For example: a Hunter who belongs to the Barrett Commission dies while upholding the Vigil. He was the mayor of the city, and genuinely cared about its citizens. Though the city's politics have been overtaken by vampires or what have you, his desire to protect others was so strong that he left behind a Rank 2 ghost upon dying in shady circumstances. Since the mayor was emotionaly attached to the city as a whole, could he Manifest anywhere within it? The same could happen to a vigilante who watches over their city like Batman watches over Gotham.

            Ghosts have only Influence: Anchors. When Geist 2E comes out we will probably see Rank 3+ ghosts (Geists) with different Influences, but those are changed by stuff in the Underworld.
            There's no explicit limitations to what can be an Anchor, but I would personally rule that an Anchor has to be intimate. A whole city would not be intimate enough, but places he often frequented would work. But in your game you might rule differently. As I said there are no explicit limitations to what can constitute an Anchor.

A frequent cooldown period or a mystical support structure (e.g. rituals, relics, frequent use of Avernian Gates, etc) does more to engage with how weird that kind of mobility is for a Rank 2 ghost than "the mayor is personally anchored to the whole town."

                            Originally posted by Tessie View Post
                            This suggests either that the ghost has a singular Influence over all their Anchors, or Influence dots spread over individual Anchors.
                            Or that their original Influences can collectively apply to the context of all of their original Anchors. That's a thing.

                            I assumed the former interpretation because otherwise they would never be able to new temporary Anchors as per the fourth Influence dot.
                            An angel capable of Creating instances of its Influence can always generate the Infrastructure Condition, applicable to all angels, regardless of what that Influence ordinarily governs. Creating Anchors through Influence works the same way for individual ghosts.

                            And yes, spirits kinda do have Influence: Resonance (except often spread over several types of Resonances) since the concepts that is Resonant to a particular spirit also governs the spirit's Influences. It's just impractical to write since you want to know what that concept is, and because spirits can have several (related but distinct) concepts.
                            "Whether something has the Resonant Condition for a particular is determined by whether it matches the description of one of its Influences" and "Spirits have Influence: Resonance" combine into a tautological statement that means nothing. That's more than just "impractical."

                                            Re: City Wide Anchor

                                            I had to give this some thought. Rules As Written, there is really nothing that would keep a Ghost of any rank from having a large territory as a Anchor. However, this could easily lead to relatively weak Ghost having a absurdly large mobility ( like trying to define "Texas" or "China" as an Anchor). I personally rule that the sort of connection needed to form an anchor has to be intimate. No matter how much someone cares about their City or State or what have you, no one can know that much territory well enough or deeply enough to claim the sort of connection that an Anchor is supposed to represent. Instead, the Ghost would likely have key locations within the City (possibly Several) that serve Anchors (City Hall, a particular Park,maybe even a Bar popular with local politicians). To use Batman as the ever popular example,despite his obsession with keeping Gotham safe,his theoretical ghost would be tied to specific locations that have specific significance. His ghost would haunt Wayne Manor and the caves beneath it, Crime Ally where his parents were slain, or the rooftop of the Police Precinct that hosts the bat signal. He would then use Fetter/Unfetter to manage Essence Bleed and roam more freely.
