    Jacob’s Journal

    Well, I’m not dead or locked up and I’m not exactly sure why not. I told the Motley and Luna today about my past with Rook, and they haven’t done anything but be supportive. I’m not sure I know how to handle that. I was expecting more negative reactions ending with me being shipped off to the Winter Court for questioning. Questioning may still come from the Winter court, but not as of yet. But no, my Motley didn’t really react all that much all things considered. Luna didn’t either. I was expecting a more negative reaction from Lorelei at least, considering she was lured to Arcadia in the first place by a Loyalist. Figured my past would be too close to home, but nope, she was just as understanding as the rest of them.

    So, how did this come to pass you ask? Easy, Twitch paid me a visit and then after he left I punched a door repeatedly. Yeah so, Twitch came by to talk to me. I invited him in so we could have a private chat. He was there to bring me a warning about Subject 61 (ie Rook). Apparently they caught Rook some time back and discovered that he was here to kill me or bring me back to the Lady. Darke and his people focused on the “planning to kill Jacob” bit and decided to change Rook’s mind. Not in a nice way, or through debate. No, they tortured the fuck out of him and filled him so full of drugs it will be a wonder if he’s sane. They basically used torture and drugs to break him down and try and confuse him as to what Gentry he actually served. They gave him a possible six. They used electrical shocks, strong ones, to kind of program him to be afraid of hurting me, and made it so he is afraid of buildings and the city in general. They also did something to make him afraid of the sun. Too long in the sun and his mind could break even further!

    To say I was unhappy about all this was an understatement. Twitch just kept blithely telling me about all of this and his part in it while I stood there and somehow didn’t hit him. Granted he didn’t know what Rook means to me, but even if I hated the man I would be sick at what was done to him. Yet, Twitch didn’t seem to find anything wrong with what he’d done. He did seem to realize what would happen to him if Subject 61 ever found his way back to town and to a certain jittery Winter courtier though, so there’s that. He saw what Rook did to those twelve greys when they invaded the clinic and let him go. Considering what kind of shape he had to be in at that point twelve is a damn impressive number. At some point training just kind of kicks in and you go on muscle memory. I think Twitch realizes just how much time he has left, and it’s not in years.

    So the problem is, they were planning on building Rook back up to be a respectable member of society, or at least one on the side of the freehold (and likely in Darke’s employ). But they didn’t get that far. The greys attacked and let Rook free before Darke and Twitch could “recreate” him. So, I don’t know where this is going to leave Rook. I don’t know what state of mind he’s going to be in once all the drugs wear off. I don’t know if he’s still going to want to kill me, or take me back or just keep running. He could be anywhere right now and that makes me anxious. Not really because he might still want to hurt me, but because he’s alone and hurt and has no support. I know, I know I should be hating his guts for what he did to me. For bringing me back to the Lady the first time and participating in my torture. I wish I could say that at that time I wouldn’t have done the same thing. But if I hadn’t changed, if I hadn’t seen what I used to be and been given a chance to come back to that, I probably would have done the same thing to him. The Lady’s wish was my command and I made her wishes come true.

    So, I guess a part of me really can’t completely blame Rook for what he did, and so when I heard Twitch talking and talking about all the shit they did to him I got mad. Really mad. After Twitch left to go hide I walked over to one of the interior doors and punched it until the wood cracked and my knuckles bled. I wanted to hit it some more, but the noise drew the Motley and Luna to find out what was going on. When they were all standing there looking at me I realized that I needed to come up with some excuse for destroying the door. I was about to lie and give them some other excuse for my actions. Being bothered by the blithe description of torture would probably have been it, but I stopped myself before I could even really start. I realized in that instant that they deserved the truth, not some bullshit. So, I told them the truth. I told them what Darke and Twitch and the others had done to Rook, and what condition he was in now. I told them that Rook and I used to be partners and we were both Loyalists. We served the Lady and I saw him as a teacher and a friend.

    I was expecting to be admonished for keeping this secret, or to see looks of distrust or anger in their eyes, but there was none of that. I don’t understand. Why don’t they hate me? Why did they accept what I was and what I did so easily? I don’t understand. Why? I can’t look in the mirror without seeing Rime look back. Especially lately. I see the Loyalist. I see his crimes. I see the blood on my hands and the looks in the eyes of my victims. I hear the desperate prayers on their lips as I brought them back, or abandoned them to die. They should be disgusted with me. Don’t they understand what they have in their midst?

    Calamity thinks herself a monster because she likes to fight and to hurt people. I guess from some people’s perspectives that description might fit, but not for me. She needs help and she knows it. She has admitted that she needs to talk to someone about her urges and get help to stop them. That in and of itself proves that she is not irredeemable. I know that what she did to her parents and probably some others was wrong, but I don’t think her acts were monstrous. I know what it is to be a monster and she’s not it. The monster is the one that commits heinous acts but doesn’t know that they’re wrong. They do them for one reason or another and will keep doing them because they enjoy it or because it really doesn’t bother them to do so. In my case it was both. The Lady asked me to do jobs for her and I was more than happy to do them. The tasks themselves did not bother me the way that they should. I led people to their deaths or to the Lady, which might be considered worse. I did it all knowingly and willingly. I am the monster…I just don’t know why they don’t see it.

    I’ll be honest. I wasn’t sure if I would find acceptance or denial from Calamity because she does have an inkling of what I was. She chose to accept me though, and I still don’t understand why. What I did was so much worse than anything she or the rest of them have done. So much… Why don’t they hate me? Why? I just don’t understand. A punch in face, a knife in the ribs…something. I would have deserved it, but nope. I got none of that. I don’t understand. Fuck I need a drink.

    **** **** ****

    So, yesterday we started looking for Rook. We figured he might still be close by hiding in the Hedge or nearby woods, so we started looking. I had expressed to the group that I wanted to try and help Rook and give him a second chance like I was given. They agreed and we planned out who would look where and when. Luna said that she would search the woods where her cabin was. The rest of us started our search in the Hedge. Nothing came of it so far. Not even Calamity’s “Find ‘Em” power worked…again. During the search I told the others a little bit more about what Twitch said, including something about a “Maria” that he was terrified of and running from. Apparently he’s convinced that she followed him out of the Hedge and will kill him if she finds him. I’m almost tempted to lead her to him. I’m not even sure if I’d feel bad about that or not. Yeah, probably not a healthy line of thought.

    Anyhoo…I also mentioned another thing that Twich told me. He said that they had overheard Rook talking to a hallucination, speaking in a familiar tone and asking why they changed their name to Jacob. So, yeah…he hallucinated a conversation with me as if we were out on a job. Twitch said that he wasn’t sure what that was about, but did say that at that point Rook was quite full of various drugs. In light of that I may need to have a conversation with Lowjack in the near future. No one has come to arrest me, so they don’t have any solid proof about me having been a loyalist, they just have the word of a drugged out torture victim. I may want to nip that whole thing in the bud before imaginations get the better of people.

    So, we had stopped the search for the day when Gunnar Friday and Ji Wen Ja came to the Dream House to talk to Calamity about the death of Mr. Grey. They said that there will be a trial and that she can get someone to represent her from the freehold. They said that Master Darke was interested in giving the guilty party a second chance and wanted execution taken off the table, and the Autumn Monarch was all for a non-lethal outcome as well. If execution was off the board the possible options for punishment left were banishment or a strict oath to work for someone in the Winter or Autumn court. They will also determine if Calamity was unstable at the time of the crime or not.

    As a Motley we discussed who we could ask to represent Calamity at the trial on Monday. We decided on Captain Jack. He has been a friend to the Motley and is pretty influential in his own right, so figured he might make a good advocate. Calamity gave him a call and he came over to talk. She explained what was going on and asked if he’d be willing to speak on her behalf. He agreed. In the interest of fairness he did mention that in the past he’d been in a relationship with Lowjack. It had not ended well, but they were friends again now. He said that it should not affect the trial in anyway, but thought it was only fair to mention it. We agreed it was fine, and Calamity made sure that he was still okay defending her.

    We talked for awhile after that about the coronation of the Summer King and all that entailed. Jack said that this started off with a Christian service at the church (where the Summer court is based), and after that there would be a Ren-faire type set-up with tables and vendors and food. Gawain asked about setting up a table at the faire and what was entailed. We arranged to have a table for our good Knight to display the weapons he’s made and for me to show off some of the stuff I’ve done with leather. I will admit that I was eager and a little bit nervous to be showing off my leatherworking, but I wanted to also lend my support to Gawain so we got it all arranged.

    And speaking of arranging things. Jack mentioned his plans for after the Summer coronation. It involves him dressing in a Spider-Man outfit and “stealing” bras from “unsuspecting” ladies throughout the faire with his web-shooters. De la Rocha has already offered herself as a volunteer, as did Lorelei. As far as plans go, I think this one will go about as well as the rest of them have, with flying colors.

    More importantly than plans for stealing underwear, we all planned when Calamity would go to the Winter court’s therapists for help. I mentioned earlier that my court has mortal counselors on staff to help Changelings through any difficulties that they may have. Our plan was that regardless of what was going on, I would escort Calamity to the clinic to seek help. If it looked like others, such as the Monarchs, knew about Calamity’s role in killing Grey then it would be Jack and me that went with her to the clinic. We wanted to make sure that she sought help for her problems before the trial started, but not make a big deal out of it. Calamity doesn’t need the extra scrutiny.

    After Jack left we had another serious conversation. It started somehow by Lorelei apologizing to Calamity for being hard on her the other day for killing Grey. Calamity tried to assure her that it wasn’t necessary, but Lori wouldn’t let her. She explained that she had recently remembered something that reminded her of the whole situation and took it out on Calamity. Apparently while in Arcadia Lori’s Keeper had a contest of sorts going on between the girls. The winner could ask a boon of her. Lori won her freedom this way. Though before that happened other girls were introduced periodically over the years. One girl was named Annabelle. She was very pretty and smart and nice, which made Lori nervous and a bit jealous. Overcome by that jealousy Lorelei and two others snuck up on Annabelle while the new girl slept, and killed her.

    The lot of us then tried to reassure her that she was still a good person and we didn’t blame her. I don’t think any of us said that there was nothing wrong with what she did, but we still don’t think that she is bad. I’m not sure if she believed us or not, but we tried. I can’t say that I haven’t killed out of jealousy, so I’m not one to throw stones. I was usually more jealous of the things that someone had rather than skills they possessed, but it’s a similar enough idea. I think I felt bad the first time I killed someone for that reason, but the feeling wore off. The rest were easy. Granted, I was killing for survival so I pushed a lot of things to the back of my mind while I was doing that, mostly my conscience. It could be argued that Lori was killing for her survival too. Though I’m not the best one to judge her actions against. I just don’t think it was all that bad. I mean, she feels bad about it and obviously wouldn’t do it again. If you ask me I’d say that there were extenuating circumstances. I mean, we’re not even sure what happened to those girls that did not live up to their Keeper’s expectations. It very well could have been a contest where those that failed, died.

    **** **** ****

    Today is the solstice, which means that it is officially summer. As if it wasn’t hot enough here as it was. Earlier today we did a bit more searching for Rook, but even I have to admit that he’s just not here anymore. He’s gone far away by now, and the drugs have probably started to wear off. I hope he is okay. I mean, I know that if he comes back to his senses then I very well may be in trouble, but I just can’t find it in me to worry about that. I worry more that the others might get caught up in the crossfire between us if it comes to that.

    Today is also the day that the reins of power will be transferred from Princess Jolie to Viscount Augustine. So there was the Christian service, which Lorelei and De la Rocha went to. While they were doing that, Gawain and I were setting up our table for the fair. I didn’t realize just how many blades Gawain has made in the past few weeks. The man keeps busy. He had a good selection of swords and knives. I have to give him credit. He is talented. I myself had just a few things to show off. I had made a couple of scabbards for Gawain’s longswords and a couple different styles for knives. Some are meant to be worn on the arm, others the ankle, and still more to be worn at your side. I have not yet made some of the sneakier sheaths, like the one you can keep at the small of your back, or the one to go inside your boot. Those are harder to make and would not likely be well received.

    So, we all dressed up for the Solstice party. I wore the highwayman hedgespun outfit, Gawain wore a tunic and plain pants, and the ladies wore corsets. Oh yeah, there were other articles of clothing to go with them, but there were a lot of corsets. And they did what any good corset does. Luna on the other hand also wore leather, but of the more practical variety. I will admit that though I’m sure she may look good in a corset I’m glad she didn’t wear one. They are not comfortable and I don’t like seeing her feeling so out of her element and looking so miserable. At the Spring court party she looked so unhappy to be wearing those heels, but she did for me. I don’t like it when people are uncomfortable or in pain because of me. So, she wore her leathers, looked good and seemed to feel a lot more comfortable and that’s what really mattered.

    Also at the faire Julia had a table set up and she was selling her art. Some were just sketches of people that she’d seen, but others were sketches of the Dream House and its entertainers. They were really quite good. She is a good artist, no matter the medium. I found myself hoping that she does well selling her art. I know she’d be happy to make a bit of money on the side, but having people appreciate your work is the far better reward.

    When it came time for the actual transfer of power it looked like Jolie was a little bit relieved to be done with the job. Augustine took up the mantle with good grace and humility. He made a speech about the freehold coming together to protect itself and fight off the incursion from the Mothership. I think he wanted to continue to foster that feeling of camaraderie. It seemed to go over well with the rest of the people present though. So, good feelings all around, right?

    Lemme see…what else happened. Oh yes, Juan-Carlos came by the table at one point and wanted to talk to Gawain. Apparently our resident blacksmith is interested in joining an entitlement called the Family of Silent Nights. I didn’t catch a lot of what they said. I think Juan-Carlos said he would come by the Dream House some time this week so he and Gawain can talk more in private about it. I hope that it works out for Gawain. He looked quite interested when Juan-Carlos introduced himself and what he was there to talk about. I mean, Lorelei and most of the ladies seemed quite eager to just ogle the man, but Gawain just wanted to talk to him.

    I had my own conversation about entitlements today as well. I talked to Lowjack about the entitlement that he is a part of. His is called the Knighthood of Utmost Silence. They help people that need to get away from someone or something disappear. I will admit that the idea has a great deal of appeal to me. Not just because I’d like to be able to hide myself, but the idea of keeping folks safe from people like me or Rook has a great deal of appeal. Some people may just need a second chance to start fresh. Others may need to find safety. I can understand both desires all too well. So, if I can help people feel safe, and actually be safe then I think I’d like to be a part of that. I’ve been responsible for hurting so many people, for ruining their lives. I can’t change that. Goddess knows that I wish I could, but I can’t. This may give me the opportunity to help even out the scales just a bit. I know it won’t be enough, it’ll never be enough, but maybe I can make a dent.


    Insincere Marguerite’s Whispers

    It’s been several days since I have written. Much of the time has been spent trying to find Rook. Unsuccessfully.

    Also, dealing with the Courts coming for Calamity, in the form of Gunnar Friday and Ji Wen Ja. Her death is not being sought by Darke or Lowjack, fortunately. I would not prefer to see her exiled, however.

    Lorelei also revealed her murder of her rival, Annabelle. I knew, of course, but did not say so. Naturally. She blames her Keeper. She is mistaken. She chose to do it. She is not yet ready to accept it. Unfortunately.

    Creepily, the more visceral aspects of Julia’s attraction to her were heightened by this. She was quite eager to get her hands on, and in, Lorelei after her announcement. She did not let it show too much, but I know her well. I have no idea of Lorelei picked up on it.

    Jacob also revealed that he had worked with Rook as partner loyalists, after Twitch visited him. The Winter Court were aware, it seems. Of course, he cased doing so, but still. He still sees Rook as a “partner” and “brother.” I wonder if Luna knew.

    Today has been spent preparing for the changing of the leadership to the Summer Court. The Spring Court has had scandals but lead during at least one major Gentry intervention, and that is good I think. Princess Jolie is a lovely woman and an excellent lover. I hope she remains in power. Her fondness for me not being least among the reasons, but still.

    Lorelei went and performed on stage, while De La Rocha stood by looking butch. After one especially hot number, I pointedly went over and kissed her where people could see. I am not ashamed to be involved with Lorelei, after all. While I did not flaunt my relationship with De La Rocha as much, this has a lot to do with her relative discomfort about things, and I took no pains to hide the connection. Naturally.

    As if De La Rocha’s issues were not odd enough, Julia seemed to get bent out of shape by our suggestion she is not “mostly straight.” For one thing, she is sleeping off and on with four women. For a second, her general lust for breasts exceeds any woman I know and is the equal of most men. Then she was apparently insufficiently attracted to a Fairest named Juan-Carlos Hypnos. Juan-Carlos is handsome enough to even get my attention, though I had heard of him from Flores. He wears a tie well. Julia was little attracted. Which is fine with me, but not her apparently.

    Julia has never dated a man. Kissed a boy once as a tween. She went to bed with me the first night she met me, and all but moved in in less than a week, then did move in after about a month or so. She eagerly availed herself of Lorelei and De La Rocha when I seduced them, and changed from “She is my good friend and it would be weird” to “full on lesbian” when Flores took her top off. Then Julia was not at all turned on my Juan-Carlos. Julia is not, has not ever been, and never will be “mostly straight.” So what, pardon my American, is her damn problem? She was not raised religious, her parents are (apparently) queer friendly enough she does not anticipate a problem with me meeting them if I ever decide I must go to Anaheim, but she was in tears because we don’t believe she is “mostly straight.”

    I do not understand. Unfortunately.

    I got sidetracked. Okay so Julia was selling paintings and doing sketches while keeping me nearby stealing kisses. We sold a few of some of the girls, including Lorelei, to locals, and had fun chatting. Definitely.

    Gawain set up shop with Calamity hanging around nearby chatting with Joan and Andromeda, Captain Jack (who he did a Spiderman thing with involving running around on rooftops), Juan-Carlos, and others. Everyone seemed to have a good time.

    End Session XXXI

Also his use of “High as balls.” Of course, in game only Jacob dealt with him and he was not happy, but there was a couple “Jacob is not laughing at this” or “I am not there right? Tell me I can’t hear this.”

        Session XXXII

        Gawain’s Memories

        June 22nd

        So today has been about the longest day ever. It’s been the longest weekend ever, for that matter, you know? Saturday at least had the coronation for a distraction. There was a little bit of a distraction at the show today, but it wasn’t anything nearly as positive, and has made Calamity even more mopey.

        The sparring was good, at least. Jacob and Calamity sparred. I did a solo work out alongside them, but I didn’t really feel like going up against either of them. I mean, my temper’s been under control these last few days and everything, but I dunno. I just wasn’t feeling it. We did make a good distraction for Lori's rehearsal, anyway, for her Midsummer's Night Dream thing, you know, but she kept them on task, mostly.

        Then there was the show. It went well, as a show, you know, and a lot of our regulars were there. Monica brought a new arrival by, named Dominica. She’s really pretty. We all stopped by to say hi, and you know, talk up our Courts. Not me, really, cause I figured Lucian and his bunch wouldn't take it too kindly, and besides, she was looking super overwhelmed anyway. Insincere Marguerite was being really flirty, which made Monica uncomfortable, but like I told Monica when she asked me to ask my motley-mate to stop, that would probably just make it worse. I don’t think she wound up picking her up or anything.

        Calamity left that conversation at some point to do her rounds, but then came back almost right away and pulled me aside. See, White Maria and Dark Kimberly had come in together and now were fighting kinda intensely (in Chinese). Even better, White Maria was armed again. Calamity was wondering if my Contract of Potential could help figure out, you know, what was up, since they were being all tight-lipped about it.

        I started off trying to figure out White Maria’s motivations in the argument, but came up blank. I really need to get better at that Contract. Dark Kimberly at least was easier to read - she wanted White Maria to back off on her accusation, which was that Dark Kimberly had let someone die. Not, like, killed someone, but maybe coulda stopped it, if she tried? It was kinda fuzzy. I tried White Maria again, this time about the gun, and that was super clear. She was afraid that someone (not Dark Kimberly) was out to kill her. Also, it occurred to her that if Annika blew up about her accusing Dark Kimberly, maybe the gun would get the dancer to back off.

        There was far too many someone/things in what we figured out, so Calamity decided she would talk with Dark Kimberly if they split up at some point, and I could talk with White Maria. My talk didn’t go so great. White Maria did agree to leave her gun at the bar, so, you know, not a wash, but she wouldn’t tell me anything about who was after her except that no one had ever hassled her here, that she was sticking to public, well lit spaces at night and that it had been a rough couple of weeks.

        Calamity’s went worse, cause Dark Kimberly just took off from the bathroom out the door. Calamity was following her, and White Maria got up to follow too, and I followed her. I mostly just wanted to make sure neither of them snapped and went for the other, but when I saw the shadows deepen, you know, like when the Contract of Darkness is used, I moved in close. White Maria fussed at me for it, until I mentioned about the Contract, and then she panicked. I managed to catch her before she ran blindly into the road, or off a cliff or something and carried her inside and up to the TV room.

        I got one of the kitchen crew to grab Jacob for me, and he went to look for tracks, once I filled him on what I saw. We eventually got White Maria to tell us some more of what was up. After the initial panicky bit, she definitely got her dignity back on and didn’t really wanna talk about it. But once we got her to start, she told us pretty much the whole story, I think.

        A Changeling named Sundown, a mutual friend of White Maria and Dark Kimberly, was killed in a gang hit a few years ago. White Maria hired an investigator to look into in and found out that Dark Kimberly had supposedly known about the upcoming hit and not warned her. Since White Maria had figured this out (and from before she confronted Dark Kimberly about it), she’d been attacked a couple of times, well, three times now, always hearing a whistle just before. She said she heard it tonight, but we didn’t. She gotten a glimpse of her attacker at some point and figured it was either a darkling or a humanish looking hobgoblin. It had attacked her out of her bathroom mirror, and from a car driving next to her, with long reaching arms.

        By the time we’d gotten all that, Jacob had gotten a good look around, so it seemed as safe as it could be for her to head out. We still escorted her to her car anyway. There can’t be too many people with that set of skills. Hopefully Calamity’s inquiries will turn something up.

        She did come out of the whole mess worse for wear though. She said Dark Kimberly had been acting really scared of her when she was just trying to see what was up, and you know, if she could help. I tried to convince her it was just guilt, if White Maria was right, or nervousness cause of the accusations, even if she wasn’t. Dark Kimberly had apparently had a bad couple of week too, so she was probably just stressed and scared in general. Why else would Dark Kimberly have to be scared of Calamity?

        June 23rd

        The trial is over and went well, I guess? I didn’t punch Darke, it wasn’t long and drawn out or anything, and everybody was really supportive. The sentence wasn’t just a slap on the wrist, but it wasn’t, you know, onerous or anything. She had to take a Pledge to not kill any Changeling in good standing with the freehold, not even in self-defense. She could still fight in self-defense, but since she’s so awesome with knives, they figured should should be able to non-lethally. She also has to give a glamour a month to the Winter Monarch and was drafted to work with a handler (a new to town Winter courtier named Dahlia) to defend the freehold along with Darke.

        The trial itself was pretty open and shut, like I said. We did learn that Captain Jack’s proper full name is Joaquin Martinez. He was a bit put off by the fact that darklings, and darklings only, have an advocate in Madame Spidertoes, who has to approve any trial and sentencing of darklings, so that they are treated fairly. It was kinda weird, but apparently she always approves.

        There were lots of speeches during the character witness part of things. Lori’s was really, super beautiful and Insincere Marguerite, Jacob and Julia were all really eloquent too. Norwegians are good for some things, of course, like surviving the cold but music not so much. She spoke of death metal and “Cookie Monster Vocals” but nothing that sounded remotely appealing. Certainly.

                        I have not listened to Muppets sing on purpose since I was much smaller.

                        That afternoon, Calamity and I had tea with Madam Spidertoes and Dahlia. It passed rather pleasantly. Well, mostly. Apparently Calamity and Dahlia shared a Durance, which she revealed as was intimated by Spidertoes. It was certainly not her idea. Calamity was (naturally) curious but Dahlia quieted quickly after the revelation. I was sure Madam Spidertoes presence affected that. Clearly.

                        As I felt Spidertoes asked me if De La Rocha was mine. She noted she knew of the relationship but asked the question very specifically. I confirmed she was. For one thing, she is. For another…I know she managed to escape Spidertoes web once but I would not want to risk Spidertoes trying to catch her again. Not that I am saying that is Spidertoes intention, but it certainly well crossed my mind.

                        We returned and explained to the others what had occurred. De La Rocha was…edgy…about my comments about Spidertoes. She argued angrily with I, as is her way, but seeing how she reacted only made me more sure I did the right thing saying she belonged to I. Definitely.

                        Madame Spidertoes is powerful, and keeps the Darklings in line through fear and respect. I think we all feel a bit…overpowered…by her. She does, however, brew excellent tea, has adorable flatware and cups, and throws a good tea party. Obviously.

                        Having the party in an abandoned building also reminded I that I still need to go urban exploring with Jacob. Eventually.

                        The show was fairly smooth. Desiree arrived with Princess Jolie. I spoke them (Jolie especially) briefly. She was well, relieved to not have the responsibilities of leadership, but concerned about some of the Spring Court rifts. Chalcedony was there too. Maybe or maybe not to torment Desiree. Her showing up lead Ginny Green to show. Creepy.

                        Afterwards, Gawain went with Juan-Carlos and Desiree to work on repairing her mind. Lorelei and De La Rocha went to get in a barfight. Summer Courtiers. Julia suggested having a late dinner on the roof. Calamity seemed a bit forlorn so we decided to invite her. She seemed pleased to attend. Julia ran out for Sandwiches and we had a very nice on roof picnic. Romantically.

                        It was nice, I think. Sitting under the moon, watching the Pacific Coast Highway and Ocean in the distance. It was not full, but it was close. Julia was so beautiful in the pale light. Calamity actually smiled genuinely for the first time I have seen in awhile, the stress briefly gone from her eyes. Our little Darkling family celebrated under the moon. Happily.

                        We discussed a few things…Julia was keep to discuss De La Rocha’s behavior. She liked to tease a torch may be being carried (did I write that right in English?) We also discussed doing a the show of Cannibal: The Musical with an entirely gender reversed cast.

                        Julia wears full skirts to the roof. I wonder what she would do without Contracts of Darkness to shimmy up and down. She dresses like a girl even compared to Lorelei. This pleases I. Absolutely.

                        Juan-Carlos and Desiree were at breakfast, and Captain Jack joined us. We discussed the idea of doing Cannibal, and everyone seemed to like the idea, though Lorelei had not seen it. It was suggested I should play the chief, since I am “the most racist.” Where do they even get such an idea about I? I am not racist. Anymore.

                        That said, a white person pretending to be Japanese “pretending” to be an Indian might not go over as well as just a Japanese person doing it.

                        Also, there was talk of a “Dance Off” with Gawain and Captain Jack. Which sounded fun, though perhaps outside of Gawain’s talents. Maybe Captain Jack is secretly trying to turn Gawain into a performer. I confess he had a few good ideas. Also, Julia does not like the Dirty Dancing. The rest of the Motley just does not like I comparing the Dirty Dancing Patrick Swayze to himself in Donnie Darko.

                        At least I was not the one to bring up Ghost. Ugh. I agree with Julia on that one. Obviously.

                        Regardless, spirits were high. Calamity’s therapy seemed to go well, and while we are concerned about a lot of things, it seems a lot of smiles are in the Dream House. Just the way I like it. Definitely.

                        End Session XXXIV

                                            Hi, Anteros, Lorelei's player here. Welcome to the discussion; we're glad to have you!

                                            Determining social die pools for Lori is always an adventure, and her selection of Attributes and Skills runs the gamut and presents a wide range of potential dice pools.

                                            The lowest social pool that's likely to come up for her would probably be Manipulation/Streetwise. If unmodified by any other abilities or mitigating circumstances, that pool would be a measly three dice. Lori is a surprisingly genuine and earnest person for a Fairest Spring Courtier, and she's not used to the "mean streets" of the Malibu/Santa Monica Freehold. Her instinct when such skills are needed is to leave it to someone like de la Rocha or Calamity.

                                            Her highest possible social die pool is a little more difficult to determine. Her Presence and Expression are both five at the moment, and she has several Specialties which apply to her most common rolls, such as Singing, Dancing, etc. Her pledge with her motley gives her an additional +1 to Expression rolls. If her target is attracted to her, she adds two dice due to the four-dot version of the Striking Looks Merit. She can effectively double this bonus by invoking the "Splendor of the Envoy's Protection" clause of the Contracts of Vainglory, adding another two dice. As a Fairest, she can spend Glamour to increase her die pool on Presence rolls at a one-for-one basis (up to a maximum determined by her Wyrd; currently 4 assuming that SotEP was already in effect).

                                            So that's 20 dice so far on a Presence/Expression (Singing) roll, if she's going all-out. There are other factors that might affect the roll, too. If her audience is entirely composed of Spring Courtiers, she could conceivably add her Mantle of 5, since this roll makes use of supernatural powers but is not "predicated upon" them, as stated in the Changeling rule book (I'd defer judgment on this point to the Baron if it ever came up, of course). So that takes the potential total dice pool up to 25. If she chose to hold back one point on the "Glamour for Presence" trade, she could then use her Enchanting Performance Merit (from Rites of Spring) to make this a 24 die rote action.

                                            This is, of course, an extreme example. Lorelei has never rolled so many dice on anything, and it's unlikely that all of these powers and situational modifiers would ever come into play on a single roll. Most of her Expression rolls come up to around 14-16 dice if I remember correctly, and other social pools cap a bit lower than that. While her Expression and Persuasion are maxed out, most of her other Social Skills are at more moderate levels. She is definitely the motley's social juggernaut, but this has come at a cost. She's only managed to kill one thing since the campaign began more than a year ago, and that was essentially kill-stealing by attacking something that was already on its last legs. Her entire contribution to the first fight of the campaign was to get knocked out in the surprise round and sleep through it.

                                            As a Spring Court burlesque performer with such advantages, however, Lorelei rarely wants for Glamour.
