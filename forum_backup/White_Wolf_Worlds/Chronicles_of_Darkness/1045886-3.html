                                                            Well, I'm even more terrified of Turtle now. The writeup is still incomplete, but what's been done so far certainly makes him a Pangaean that few people want to cross.

                                                            =====+=====

                                                            TURTLE

                                                            Though an unfamiliar god to most of the Vinca, this Pangaean is honored by coastal and seafaring tribes in different parts of the world. He is one of the very few denizens of the Border Marches who can pass into the Worlds of Spirit and Flesh without reprisal from Urfarah. Why this is so is a mystery for Uratha and Wise alike, and neither have yet to discover the answer despite generations of attempts.

                                                            Background: One of the greater gods of the Border Marches, it is said to rival Urfarah in both age and power. Such a claim is backed by the ability to venture out of the Border Marches unchallenged in order to roam not only the Worlds of Spirit and Flesh, but also the realms in-between.

                                                            Many among the Wise and Uratha have tales on why Turtle is given a wide berth by even the Great Predator. Some say that the two Pangaeans clashed in a great battle not unlike the one between Wolf and Bull. Most believe the battle ended at a stalemate, though a few suspect that Turtle was the victor. This belief is fueled by rumors that Turtle found some secret of the Marches which gave him an edge against his fellow gods, including the Wolf herself.

                                                            Whatever the reason may be, Turtle wanders freely as a result, and often travels to a world on either end of Pangaea in pursuit of his own agendas. Not only that, but the Wise have discovered the Pangaean spending much time in the Astral Realm, and even the Ocean Beyond Life.

                                                            Turtle is honored by the People. His animals make their homes in the River, and priests and shamans invoke his name to honor or tame the river-spirits so that their villages thrive. However, he is also called to act as a guide for the dead, so that they may reach the Underworld and make their final descent into its dark waters. Hence, his role is a dualistic one in the pantheon of the gods. Creator and Destroyer. Life and Death.

                                                            Yet despite this reverence, the People are seldom graced by Turtle's presence. The Pangaean secludes himself in the oceans of whichever world he is in. And he seldom strays far from the shore in those few times he sets foot on land. This relative isolation may be another reason why Wolf leaves him be, and it also deters even the most ambitious Wise from attempts to take its heart.

                                                            Description: Turtle's vast power is equally matched by his size, to the point that he is often mistaken for an island made wholly of black rock at a distance. The forest that grows from its shell shatters this illusion. Filled with othedworldly coral and vegetation, it provides a home to a multitude of spirits, ghosts, and goetia that Turtle has picked up over the centuries he has travelled.

                                                            Its forays have also made it widely known beyond the Land of the People. The Pangaean will become the basis of the World Turtle archetype found in future cultures around the world. China, India, and North America are but a few examples of the lands where Turtle's memory lives on to an extent.

                                                            Storytelling Hints: Turtle is revered by other tribes besides the People. Islanders and seafarers feel the Pangaean's influence more strongly in the waters of their homes, and the Wise among their number often seek him out for knowledge and other boons. These Awakened cultivate spiritual agreements with Turtle and other waterborne gods of the Marches to ensure their survival far from the shores. Such compacts are not always positive, and may demand great sacrifice on the part of the Wise. This, however, is nowhere near the hostility their counterparts in the land-based Vinca incite through their hunts for Omphalos Stones.

                                                            -+-

                                                            Turtle, Calm and Contemplative
                                                            Rank 7 Pangaean

                                                            As a Rank 7 entity, Turtle does not operate within the usual rules framework. His capabilities cannot be reduced to a few dice pools, but he possesses several characteristics that are worth noting. Overall, the Pangaean's mere presence can radically influence a scene.

                                                            Influence: Turtles 6, Earth 3, Water 3
                                                            Arcana: Death 3, Life 3, Fate 1

                                                            If a Storyteller uses the following House Rules for ephemeral beings, Turtle is treated as a Rank 5 entity with the following traits when struck by his Bane for the first time.

                                                            Attributes: Power 17, Finesse 16, Resistance 18
                                                            Numina: Awe, Blast, Drain, Essence Thief, Fate Sense, Implant Mission, Omen Trance, Pathfinder, Resurrection, Seek

                                                            Size: 100
                                                            Corpus: 218
                                                            Essence: 120
                                                            Willpower: 10
                                                            Armor: 15

                                                            Ban: Turtle must offer audience and refrain from harming anyone who summons him by sacrificing a turtle or turtle spirit through the appropriate rites. He is not bound further if the offer of audience is refused or if the visitor acts against him.

                                                            Bane: A spear made from the shell fragments of 225 turtles, with no more than fifteen pieces used for each animal.

                                                            -=-

                                                            Colossus: Turtle is immense of form, a towering god that can simply shrug off lesser attacks. The Pangaean increases his Corpus by his Size, and gains general Armor equal to its Resistance. This armor is completely ignored by attacks made with Turtle's bane. Against creatures and objects of smaller Size than him, his attacks benefit from the 8-Again rule.

                                                            Divine Bleed: The first time in a scene that he suffers Corpus damage, Turtle may reflexively use the Awe, Essence Thief, or Drain Numen for free. Whenever he takes damage, he may use the Mass Create power of Influence (Turtles) for free or create Rank 1 Turtle Spirits based on successes scored in a dice pool equal to the number of points inflicted.

                                                            High Magic: Once per scene, Turtle can spend a Willpower point to cast an improvised spell that is one dot higher than he could otherwise cast.

                                                            Island Beyond Life: In addition to being able to meditate into the Temenos and Anima Mundi, Turtle can do the same to transport into or out of the Ocean Beyond Life. Each instance of this power causes matter from one world to come rushing into the other, including any characters fortunate enough to survive being pulled in.

                                                            Overwhelming Presence: A mortal human who encounters Turtle and realizes they are in his presence suffers an Integrity breaking point. If this roll is failed, the character gains the Madness persistent condition.

                                                            World Turtle: Turtle is aware of the health and emotional state (basic information like fear, pain, hunger, etc) of all turtles and turtle spirits within 225 square miles of his location. Furthermore, he can see through their senses as per the Spirit Dominion Dread Power. If he spends a Willpower point, this power's range is doubled for a scene.
