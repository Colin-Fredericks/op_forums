                                    Small reminders:

                                    Demesnes, like Verges, apply their Paradox negation to spells that qualify for their Yantra bonus, which are limited by their semiotics. The artificial nature of a demesne is such that, as with less invested Sancta, its owner is likely to customize their ritual space to be appropriate to the types of magic they use it for, which opportunity cost means cannot be all possible types even before filtering down to the relevant Arcana.

                                    It is explicitly noted, for good reason, that most spells a demesne benefits are ritually-cast; it is implicit in the ways demesnes' boundaries and applications are described that they are established in Safe Places of sufficient privacy to practice magic without uninvited witnesses. A mage's Sanctum is a place in which one should endeavor not to fight them, because they can more easily marshall their Gnosis to maintain control of multiple spells at once in that space without risking Paradox (demesne or no demesne), but this also means an unexpected confrontation with Sleepers in your Sanctum is more likely to explode or go sideways.

                                    Demesnes can be subject to Dissonance (and have the hardest Withstand to scale against it) despite having no overt supernatural qualities of their own, and their Paradox negation only does not stop Sleeper witnesses from triggering and worsening the phenomenon — so it's probably safe to assume being seen casting in a demesne by a Sleeper is what risks Dissonance for a demesne.

                                    There are no mechanics for retroactively applying Paradox rolls.
