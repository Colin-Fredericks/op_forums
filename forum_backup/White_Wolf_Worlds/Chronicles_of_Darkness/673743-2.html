    And here's the Sect Blessings associated with the Host. I know most of these are more or less reskinned Mummy Affinities, but until Promethean 2e comes out, I'm working with the best inspiration I've got. Let me know if everything is still too much in the Mummy aesthetic or isn't differentiated enough

    Host Sect Blessings

    Faithful Soul
    Prerequisite: Sect Status (Host) *+
    Effect: The Announced are all beings connected to the Divine, but the simple and powerful belief those of the Host espouse grants them all the power they need to persevere in the face of adversity and triumph.
    • Add a +2 bonus to each of these Skills: Medicine, Athletics, and Expression. If a Willpower point is spent, they may use their Vocation Maxim instead as a bonus for a single roll.
    • The Announced are naturally fearless, but those of the Host are especially so. Any attempts to cause fear in the Announced immediately fail.
    • Add a +1 bonus to their Supernatural Power Trait for the purpose of resisting hostile powers; there is no cost to this effect. If they are resisting powers that would affect their thoughts or emotions, they may spend a Willpower point to use the higher of their Exousia or Vocation Maxim instead of their Glory.

    True Angelos
    Prerequisite: Sect Status (Host) **+
    Effect: The Host would like to see themselves as the true powers of the Announced, the mediators and judges of their peoples, truly lords of the Celestial Hosts. Technically, this is four separate Blessings, with a unique power for each Sect, and thus can be taken multiple times, but that is ill advised except for those deeply invested in diplomacy and politics among the Announced. Taking an hour on an Intelligence + Crafts roll, the Chorister makes a small artistic gift to give to their companion. They can maintain a number of these amulets equal to their Sect Status, meaning a Chorister with all four versions of this Blessing can have up to 20 amulets. So long as the amulet is worn by an Announced of the appropriate Sect, whenever they are empowered for a Mission, the Chorister is as well. Additionally, the amulets grant the Chorister additional power over their bearers, though such abilities are only suspected at this time.
    • Defy the False God: The Chorister adds a +2 bonus to their Social rolls against any Announced from the Children of the White Temple. The Igigi suffers a -3 penalty for their Social rolls against the Chorister.
    • Cutting the Reed: The Chorister may choose to take up the injuries of their Philosopher companion, or may let the Sefer Raziel scribe take the blow for them. If the Chorister or the Philosopher suffers a Lethal or Aggravated wound, the Chorister may choose to transfer the injury to the other person in the connection.
    • Power over the Unclean: The Chorister benefits from a +2 bonus to their Social rolls against one or more Daemons who invite them into their conversation- one of which must bear the amulet. They may also reflexively spend a Willpower point to silence any Daemon in their line of sight, so long as the bearer of the amulet is still present. The player of the Daemon may spend a Willpower point to speak again and prevent being silenced again. Destroying the amulet doesn’t prematurely end silencing.
    • Shepherd the Pious: Should the Chorister and one or more Magi associates- one of whom must bear the amulet -enter conflict together, whether physically or socially and as allies or enemies, they have three options. Firstly, they may allow everything to proceed normally. Secondly, they may levy a -3 penalty to any Magus in their line of sight either to Initiative or to Social rolls in order to give themselves a +3 bonus to either of those two traits. Thirdly, they may apply a -3 penalty to their own Initiative or Social rolls in order to grant a +3 bonus to an ally. They may do this to as many targets as are in their line of sight so long as the bearer of their amulet is still present and alive, and so long as they or their allies still have dice to give up.

    Crusader
    Prerequisite: Sect Status (Host) ***+
    Effect: An immensely popular Blessing among the devotees of St. Michael and the Cherubim alike, enabling them to protect and more effectively command those who fight alongside them and sit under their protection.
    • By paying a point of Willpower as a Reflexive action, and touching a target the Chorister brands them with a symbol unique to the Announced who did so (typically a sigil representing their Name) that is visible to anyone who can see or otherwise sense auras. They may need to make a successful Dexterity + Brawl roll in a stressful or combat-oriented situation. Thereafter, when the target is in a situation that causes physical harm, the Announced that placed it may roll Wits + Composure to gain a clearer understanding of the target’s condition, location, and current situation. They may brand as many targets as they please. One target may have multiple brands. The Chorister who placed it may seek their charge using Rapture at a -4 penalty.
    • By spending a point of Willpower and touching a target as a reflexive action, the Chorister may grant their companion some protection for the next 24-hours. By rolling Stamina + Composure, the Announced applies successes as temporary Armor points up to a maximum of the higher of their Stamina or Sect Status. These Armor points stack with worn Armor, but not with other magical Armors that directly affect the body.
    • Whenever the Chorister benefits from a Teamwork action as the primary actor, and forcefully commands their subordinates- as though they were some kind of military officer -their efforts are enhanced. They gain a +1 bonus to their action per companion even if their subordinates roll no successes on the teamwork action; their command of others for a righteous cause empowers them.

    Ever-Pertinent Authority
    Prerequisite: Sect Status (Host) ****+
    Effect: The truly influential members of the Host can bring the authority of their divine office to bear among mortals, as well as making their presence in the lives of their charges well and truly felt.
    • If the Chorister presents other characters with an inanimate object as though it were a badge of office, government or private identification document, or other such mark of identity/authority, the player may spend a Willpower point to initiate a contested (Manipulation + Subterfuge + Sect Status) roll. Use the highest Resolve + Composure of observers present. Announced and penitentes are immune to this trick. Using something marked with their Celestial Name and/or Title confers the 8-again quality to the roll. Repute does not apply, but Advanced Repute does. If the Announced is successful, all witnesses perceive the object as what they say it is and treats the Chorister as though they are who their “identification” says they are. Obvious evidence they are not who they claim to be causes the magic to fail, so additional props may be necessary. Implausible disguises are successful, impossible ones collapse under disbelief.
    • If the Chorister has branded someone according to the rules in Crusader (see above) they may pay an additional point of Willpower when administering the brand. From then on, they inflict Awe Yara as though they were the Announced, and may use the degree of Awe as a bonus on a Social roll that would benefit from people wanting to avoid displeasing the branded character’s patron. Awe 1 or higher confers no bonus besides that of the Awestruck Condition, and worsening Awe targets the Announced, not the branded. Use the highest Glory and the lowest Exousia for multiple empowered brands. The branded character also gives off Radiance as though they had Glory 1. While this may empower a branded penitente, it also makes them easier for other Announced, penitentes, and Pandorans to track.

    Lord Of Hosts
    Prerequisite: Sect Status (Host) *****
    Effect: The local Archangel is high priest, general, and king to the Choir. The Host moves on their word, and those among the other Sects would do well to bow to them- serving a true God-appointed king comes with benefits.
    • The Archangel synergizes the magic of up to four other Announced with Sect Status 5. So long as they are within a mile of the Chorister, and consent to have their magic merge with the character’s, then the Archangel becomes the center point for all effects that use a mile radius. Additionally, this bubble of synchronized magic extends up to a number of miles equal to the number of Sect Status 5 Announced, expanding all of their reach. If consent is ever revoked, the relevant magic ceases and the diameter shrinks.
    • By spending a point of Willpower, the Archangel creates a ward that protects an area up to their Sect Status in square miles for a number of days equal to their Exousia. Anyone trying to cross the border must bear a brand or have a Presence score greater than the Archangel’s Sect Status.

            Edicts are indeed analogous to Utterances. I was thinking of a Sodom and Gomorrah type power, but Tier 3 wouldn't be anything so simple as just mundane flames, but a full-blown Firestorm.

            I've been thinking the splat over some more, and while a lot of my changes are waiting on Promethean 2e, I was pondering maybe changing out Lux for Humors, and tying Maxims to them. I'm not really in favour of using Humors as a measurable fuel Stat, but I'll play around with it, and see how it works. Perhaps instead of relics, they'll be replaced entirely by 2e Athanors, with Seba-like gifts from the Principle standing in as things mostly encountered by the Announced.

            I've also been considering a reworking of how Vocations and Sects relate, and how the Announced grow and change. Perhaps Vocations are simply loose groupings of more or less unique beings (like 2e Lineages), while the Y-splat isn't Sect but something like Refinements, much like Prometheans. Obviously there will be some changes in how they relate to Refinements, and I'm now thinking Sects should be Z-splats, like Lodges or Legacies.

            Considering how rare the Announced are, small groups of like-minded individuals makes more sense than global organizations. This is where they would differ strongly from the Arisen, since they don't have the advantage of shared history, total immortality, and an actual government structure they have inherited. Primary antagonists (other than each other) would probably be Alchemists or Penitentes, with Pandorans taking centre stage in specific circumstances.
