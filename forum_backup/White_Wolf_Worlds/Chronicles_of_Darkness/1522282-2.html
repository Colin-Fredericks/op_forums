                    I think there's an interesting relationship between Sekhem and Pyros.
                    Sekhem is the energy of life in response to suffering; when a living thing suffers and obeys the Law of Suffering by moving away from the negative stimulus, Sekhem is generated. Sekhem is also, IIRC, related to the Law of Will that Sutekh mandated; the idea that living things can do more than just avoid suffering, and is therefore not entirely predictable.

                    So I think Pyros, a creative/destructive expression of spontaneous transformation and change, could be Sekhem that's basically exploded or caught fire in some spooky metaphysical way. I think if a Promethean told a (high-Memory) Mummy about the Principle, the Mummy would shrug and say that it lined up pretty well with Sutekh's Law.

                    If I was going to include the G-M in this idea, I'd float the idea that the God-Machine's control over reality generally tamps down on the kind of uncontrolled changes that Pyros is all about, so it gets bottled up, pressurized, and explodes out of the G-Ms cold mathematical structure as Prometheans and Qashmallim. That could explain why examples of Pyros-driven phenomena are so rare but explosive when they happen; they only occur during certain localized G-M failure modes, like steam blasting through a weak weld in a pressurized vessel. EDIT: Additional thought, that could be part of why Qashmallim look like angels, because they're manifestations of Pyros coming through angel-shaped holes in the world, as it were.

                                My favourite answer for the Principle is that it's literally a principle, a fundamental rule in creation; basically a loophole that says, in essence; "fuck entropy, sometimes you really can get a free lunch". So it's not really God but you can imagine that humans reacted to occasional free lunches as gifts and miracles, and how that could give rise to legends about Prometheus and Jesus (and Sutekh).

                                Pyros gathers in small quantities where living things plan, act on impulse, create, and destroy. That's why I think it works well as a counterpart to Sekhem: if Sekhem is generated from "acts of suffering", Pyros is generated from "acts of will". Although the mummies' own lore probably doesn't recognize much of a difference, since to them Sekhem obeys the laws of Suffering and Will both. That's why I characterized Pyros as Sekhem when it gets particularly energetic, since we know it does get energetic.

                                As far as the God-Machine goes, I personally like to keep things relatively separate, so I assume the G-M is its own thing with it's own mysterious origin.
                                It still interacts with the Principle, because if the principle is a universal rule of creation then everything should interact with it. And I like the idea that the G-M suppresses Pyros until it explodes out, because it seemed odd to me that if the Principle is, in fact, a universal rule, there should be a lot more evidence or manifestations of it but it's really just the Prometheans and their works and creators, and the Qashmallim.
                                So you could imagine in a G-M-less world there could be a lot more Pyros, a lot more uncontrolled acts of will, change and destruction at a lower level of intensity, but in the CoD the GM keeps a lid on things until the lid pops off and everything catches fire.

                                                        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                        A mix of bad actors, unfortunate real-estate placement, and the same basic reason the Begotten can no longer rely on the Astral to convey meaningful messages about their activities: there's a whole lot more people than there used to be and the overwhelming majority of them are not trained and equipped to process their trauma (nor allow their trauma to process itself), and — partially because of the aforementioned bad actors — in many cases they have been actively maladapted against doing so. Bits of the byproducts of the human soul stick around until allowed to resolve themselves, and so the inability of the disposal mechanism for those byproducts to fully break them down on its own means a horrific system of torment has been allowed to raise a rocky edifice above the drowning tides of dissolution to drag down the dead and sunder the quick.
                                                        I dig that alot!

                                                        Geist/Mummy: To me, the Lower Depths were the place where whatever entered and stayed to long was entirely annihilated, or more accurately, devoured, because the Lower Depths are Ammut.

                                                        Beast: The Dark Mother is much, much older than humanity, possibly even a pre-Fall entity, fear personified. No matter what timeline or world you go by, there was (un)life capable of fear, feeding the Dark Mother. In my headcanon, she is responsible or related to the creation of many splats, e.g. giving birth to Urfarah as well as having had a hand in twisting the Strix into their new shape.

                                                        Mage/Demon/Promethean/Mummy/Geist:
                                                        The reason the G-M seems broken and unable to progress beyond keeping a nebulous status Quo is because it literally is like a machine stuck on a process, lacking the equipment to keep going.​
                                                        You see, the G-M and the Principle used to be one cohesive unit with the Astral and Supernal. Originally, before the Fall, this complex natural system was existence: lthere was simply no real difference between the Astral, the Physical, the Spiritual, no matter what names you want to use: Duat, A'aru, Arcadia, Stygia etc. - all was one. Life before the Fall was so different, it might as well be unimaginable to us, and what we know of it, are broken faint echoes at best. There were no Watchtowers per se, but Awakening to the Principles of the Supernal was much easier due to a series of support systems, Arcana were Common, as was Knowledge about Exploits or Rites, Stepping Sideways was not limited to the Umbra not to (part-)Spirits.
                                                        To achieve this unity, energies had to flow in precise orders, be transformed at correct times and dimensions through specific events, and the system that derived from this existence was carefully calibrating to ensure the different layers of realities could mesh without breaking apart or ripping parts out of each other or tearing into each other. For a 'time' for lack of a better word, this system was incredibly stable, it could even create new forms of energie and existence, enabling the system to diversify, experiment, change, transform and grow.
                                                        However, at some point, balance was lost as energies fluctuated in a way the system could not compensate. As a result, everything broke, including the system. What was left separated into the world as we know it: the supernal Realms were ripped apart from everything and sorted, and the support systems conglomerated slowly into a guiding light, and finally under human guidance and influence into the Watchtowers.
                                                        The rest was itself separated into many spheres: some known, some unknown, some broken and dead, some so isolated and dead they might as well not exist, most cluttering together, haphazardly teethered to each other by tenuous at best connections.

                                                        The left-over pieces each continue to work in as much as they can, but are limited in functionality and input. That is why the G-M or the Principle sometimes give contradicting missions to it's agents or fail in apparently simple tasks: they are half-blind and crippled. That is why the Abyss separates the Supernal from the Fallen World, and tries to either unmake or claim the latter with seemingly little rhyme or reason.
                                                        The Principle still tries to inject forms of energy to transform them into something else, the G-M still makes sure events happen, the Abyss still seperates systems from each other for transformation, and other, smaller parts certainly also still attempt to work according to how they used to... but neither can communicate with the rest anymore, their actions influencing each other in unforeseen ways, spiraling out of control, and many are, essentially, ineffectual due to an overloaded open system incapable of supporting the amount of inhabitants sustainably.
