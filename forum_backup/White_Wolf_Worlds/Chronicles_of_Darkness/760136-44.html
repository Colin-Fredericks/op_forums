    Group Play: Conclaves
    While the imagery of a single reigning ruler lording over a single colony is appealing, the World of Darkness is designed for multiple players. This however doesn’t mean you need multiple Colonies each and real world nature and fictional material is rife with examples of large mega-colonies with multiple Queens birthing young. So as Art imitates Life, Colonies with more than one Monarch aren’t unheard of.
    Said Colonies are lead by what is known as a Conclave, a group of elites designated as a ruler.
    The Colony begins play as a normal Colony and each Monarch that joins into it adds their resources together to make the initial starting Colony more efficient than it would have been otherwise.
    Each Monarch has a vote which it may use to dictate matters involving the Colony, with a simple majority dictating terms most of the time, with the catch that Monarchs of an appropriate Caste for a given issue effectively have their votes working as 2 votes. (i.e. Marshalls for Security and War issues) Additionally, a Voice of the Conclave is elected to settle Ties, should they occur.

    Conclaves begin play with 1 dot in all Attributes. And each Monarch’s Caste adds 1 dot to a respective attribute.


    Mechs: Because even the Chronicles of Darkness needs robot fights.
    DarkFrame: Crossover setting that puts Chronicles of Darkness in the far future that is Warframe.
    Monarch: The Endless You are an alien ruler, charged with maintaining a people who you shape to suit your needs.

                                                        New Trait: Colony
                                                        All Monarchs are tended to by many lesser creatures, all various strains of the Endless made to serve her. They act as everything from a labor force, a private army, personal servants, and everything in between, all at her beck and call.
                                                        Traditionally, Colonies are located in Beneath, a place underground that skirts between realworld location and alternate reality, usually accessed through wormholes or through spatially folded physical locations. As a general rule a Monarch may access or pull forces from her Colony from any Wormholes from her own Court or via physically entering the Beneath through the realspace anchors of her Colony. Generally speaking, Wormholes are safer and more expensive, but the anchor point of a Colony is a vulnerability that anyone with the knowledge can find.

                                                        But as many servants and warriors a Monarch is able to own, there are limits to how much this force can do, its capabilities, its ability to replenish losses, its own lacks from incomplete Geneweb, and other factors.
                                                        Colonies are represented by five attributes, each representing an estimation of the Colony’s abilities at given tasks. These are:
                                                        Military
                                                        Knowledge
                                                        Industry
                                                        Espionage
                                                        Connection
                                                        All are rated from 1 to 10, with 1 dot in each at the start, plus an extra dot in the Monarch’s primary area of focus. Raising Colony Attributes is the cost single merit dot.
                                                        Colony Attributes represent the various strengths the Colony has in various fields and are rolled in situations when the Colony needs to be rolled. However, the Attributes also respent a reserve of power and resources, available to the Monarch to spend. More on this later, but these are called Resources.
                                                        Group Play: Conclaves
                                                        While the imagery of a single reigning ruler lording over a single colony is appealing, the World of Darkness is designed for multiple players. This however doesn’t mean you need multiple Colonies each and real world nature and fictional material is rife with examples of large mega-colonies with multiple Queens birthing young. So as Art imitates Life, Colonies with more than one Monarch aren’t unheard of.
                                                        Said Colonies are lead by what is known as a Conclave, a group of elites designated as a ruler.
                                                        The Colony begins play as a normal Colony and each Monarch that joins into it adds their resources together to make the initial starting Colony more efficient than it would have been otherwise.
                                                        Each Monarch has a vote which it may use to dictate matters involving the Colony, with a simple majority dictating terms most of the time, with the catch that Monarchs of an appropriate Caste for a given issue effectively have their votes working as 2 votes. (i.e. Marshalls for Security and War issues) Additionally, a Voice of the Conclave is elected to settle Ties, should they occur. It is within the rights for a given member of a Conclave to initiate a vote on any topic when all members of the Conclave are in psychic range of each other, though for courtesy’s sake do not usually “recount” votes unless a situation has drastically changed.

                                                        Conclaves begin play with 1 dot in all Attributes. And each Monarch’s Caste adds 1 dot to a respective attribute.

                                                        Colony Actions
                                                        At any point of time, the ruling Monarch or Monarchs may order their Colony to do a task. Tasks can either be automatic and are simply done, or require something done.
                                                        Similar to a Cult, a Colony is able to do a number of tasks automatically, following the list of things a Cult’s Reach and Grasp. For the purposes of this, any of the Colony’s attributes count as Reach and Grasp and determining which Attribute is used for an action or a reaction is based on context and judgment. Using the Reach and Grasp actions of a Cult are not free however. Using any of them reduces the given Attribute’s “pool”, as below.


                                                        As stated before, Colony Attributes determine the ammount of resources can be thrown at any given time. What this means is, a Colony can spend “points” of an attribute, not reducing the attribute dots, but still expending a small portion of temporary resources and using them to enact certain actions, up to the limit of the Attribute until the Colony is “refreshed”
                                                        How long it takes for a Colony to refresh is not standard. This is to allow Storytellers to tailor how often Colonies are able to act as well as to represent the fact that situations may be more active or inactive depending on danger or desperation. In any time the Storyteller wishes to refresh Colonies, he can declare it any time he desires. However, the catch is, this happens to all Colonies, including enemy holdings.
                                                        For instance, via diplomacy, a Monarch wishes to see 11 separate CEOs. She has the Connection attribute high enough to arrange meetings with powerful people, but she can’t do it for all of them in the same day. Each CEO will reduce her Connection’s pool until they are refreshed but as she is at the limit of 10, she can’t see the last one just yet. The next day, the Storyteller declares all Colonies refresh their resources, allowing her to see the last CEO.




                                                        Default Colony Actions
                                                        Colonies have more actions available to them than the standard Reach and Grasp system cults employ. Colonies possess 5 special actions that they may use at any time, simply by spending the resources to activate them. Each action takes 1 Scene to complete.
                                                        For these actions, you may convert resources from one category to the next, however doing so requires spending 2 points, in any combination of any of the other categories, making this very inefficient. Use when desperate.
                                                        There is no limit to the amount of times a Colony may use these actions, aside from the lack of resources.
                                                        Conscripts: Order a small contingent of warrior Endless to come with you, one for each point of Military spent. These warriors are smart enough to understand almost any order and obedient to you alone. Their stats:



                                                        Spy Order your Colony to Spy on a target location or creature via dispatching smaller Endless. Roll Espionage twice to determine their effectiveness at stealth and camouflage. You receive only basic details such as “target is flying away” “target notices us”.
                                                        Alternatively, this action may be used to counter an existing Spy attempt, using Espionage twice as the dice pool. 1 Espionage is spent regardless of success or failure.

                                                        Create Your swarm creates for you simple items or devices that are mundane in function, though exotic in nature. This can range to simple weapons, body armor, and electronic devices, however where such things would be made out of metal and plastic where they mundane, they are alien in appearance and nature. Each item costs 1 Industry.


                                                        Research
                                                        Influence

                                                        So I think something with the Investigation and Door systems is ideal here. Trade X resources get X clues, but the more you get, the more you need to spend to get more.



                                                        Province Actions
                                                        One other thing Monarchs may employ their Colonies for is use their Provinces. Provinces add actions and rules to them, which can benefit the Colony… provided the appropriate resources are spent to activate and maintain them.
                                                        Where as other Supernaturals employ powerful artifacts and relics, for Monarchs, this is taken on by control of the land itself.
                                                        See Provinces for details.

                                                            This is a very strange and very alien game. I like it.

                                                            Being a Nintendo fan, an example Monarch I have in mind is the bad end of the original Pikmin where Captain Olimar is dying stranded on the alien planet and the Pikmin use the onion to convert him into a permanent leader-Pik-form. Heck, when you start thinking of the 'plant -> harvest -> defeat critters -> more offspring converted from their corpses' example of strange biology that sounds like typical behavior for a ream of Endless in a monster heavy part of the Beneath.


                                                            I've been doing a lot of home-brew tweaks for the Investigative system particularly for ideas to use for Genius. It sounds like base Research would do the work of scaled Investigation, while Doors and Social Maneuvering is the realm of Diplomacy/Connection?

                                                            As a suggestion for Research, perhaps for the most basic act ordering the Colony to Research automatically creates a Clue. It has no Elements though, meaning no bonus for further actions in the Investigation, but it still counts as a step towards the conclusion. More advanced interactions with such Investigations would be higher tier options.

                                                            As it stands, what necessitates more complex uses of Colony traits? A higher cost tapping of the associated trait? Interaction with another trait? For example, I could see a combo of Espionage and Knowledge to infiltrate and deduce the intel of an opponent 'at the source' rather than getting that intel to the Colony to decrypt.

                                                            I also have a few ideas percolating in my head for Monarch: the Endless chapter fictions, if you are ever scouting about for contributions.
