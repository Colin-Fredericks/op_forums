    Short session as we spent most of the session going over some other stuff together about a new note taking website we are using now so they can more easily take and share notes with each other, and getting everyone set up for that.

    I did have everyone roll a wisdom check for destroying the ghost guarding the Killing Fields from the last chapter. Giger and Ragnar both failed and now have the Megalomaniacal condition.

    Chapter 45
    2/28/2025

    The cabal trudged off across the Killings Fields, looking for Ragnar in the Blood Gutters. As they walked Ragnar used his Death arcanum to veil the cabal, making them appear as ghosts. They knew the direction they were supposed to head but without any knowledge on how to follow that direction the cabal veered off course, coming across two groups of ghosts locked in combat. One was filled with ghosts wearing red, which outnumbered the second group of ghosts in white, led by a ghost in Ancient Greek armor. Ragnar and Giger powered up Ragnar's Wyvern familiar using Death/Matter and Time respectively and sent it to capture the Greek ghost. The rest of the ghosts, at the sight of the now enhanced ghost, cowered in shock, but then overcame their own fear and charged the Wyvern, shouting “FEALTY MUST BE SWORN!” Ragnar used Death magic to make the ghosts fight themselves again while his Wyvern captured the Greek ghost. Maxwell and Giger tried interrogating it, but the ghost was uncooperative, insisting that they were in violation of the Old Laws and must swear fealty. Even torture didn't get him to stop, as Maxwell heated the armor he was wearing to get him to talk. When Maxwell asked who they were supposed to swear fealty to, the ghost told them, “The true ruler of the Killing Fields, Clockwork,” and when the cabal learned that Clockwork was at war with a ghost named “Dominus,” Eye of God swore fealty to Dominus, causing a slight red light to envelope him. He was shocked that it worked, meaning it to be a joke to annoy the ghost. The cabal left, now using Space magic to go the correct direction.

    They came to a series of trenches dug into the ground, blood sweeping from the walls into the earth and flowing down. The trenches eventually became tunnels as they went completely underground. Giger, trying to assert his dominance in the cabal, tried to lead them through the underground maze but quickly became lost. After being lost for about half an hour underground, Giger used Fate to help guide him, Eye of God asked him if he had gotten them lost. Giger denied it of course, but Eye of God pressed the issue. As the small argument continued, they all felt a surge of magic, and the air around them burst into flames.

                Chapter 46
                3/07/2025

                The ghost mage Ragnar managed to surprise the cabal, doing massive damage, nearly knocking the living Ragnar out in the first round of combat with the help over several ghouls. Eye of God used some Space magic to make the cabal a safe place to hide out in, everyone followed, with Maxwell using Forces magic to throw all the ghosts down the tunnel of the trench, with the ghost Ragnar resisting and using his Death arcanum to keep the living Ragnar's familiar from following. Giger used his Time arcanum to alter the Living Ragnar's past, boosting his weaponry skill and also changing his past so he carried a weapon made of pine wood, the ghost’s bane. After that point the cabal quickly defeated the ghost, grabbed something that fell from him, and left the trenches.

                The thing that dropped was a milky gray stone with a nerve hanging from it, clearly an eye. Ragnar scrutinized it and saw that it was a Supernal artifact from an emanation realm, with two separate raven symbols, and an Eye symbol, although not the Exarch. Giger cast some Prime magic on it and learned it has four powers, one of which would grant a familiar if the artifact replaced someone's eye.

                Ragnar cast a spell from the grimoire he's been carrying on the death mask he had, binding it to the grimoire. The mask was absorbed by the grimoire and the sound of writing followed. The mask was being described in the back of the book now and the cabal realized that the grimoire was not a bestiary but a prison.


                Chapter 47
                3/14/2025

                Ragnar dreamed of a bronze fortress, inside he discovered it was an empty prison. Inside her found cells with their doors wide open and eventually found a small room with a stool in it, the walls covered in Supernal runes. He studied these runes and used them to reshape his gnosis, joining a legacy that would allow him to bind Abyssal monsters to the grimoire. As he was waking up someone came down the hall, an old man of Middle Eastern descent, dressed like an old Catholic monk, bounding in chains dragging on the floor. He spoke a language Ragnar didn't understand and Ragnar woke up.

                The cabal sailed down the river of blood, then the river of fire to arrive at the Junkyard. Once they heard the pounding coming from the Junkyard, the ghost of Victoria Adams that had been following Ragnar for months changed, became more machine-like, but kept her humanoid appearance. They entered the Junkyard and she went straight to the massive crushers, where they read the Old Laws of the realm; “The Design Must Be Tested, The Design Must Evolve, The Crushers are Sacrosanct, The Smith Shall Retain His Tools” and watched as broken machines were placed beneath the crushers and broken, ectoplasm being sprayed out into the river of fire while the crushed metal was taken further in. They followed the belts that left the crushers, and watched two ghosts fight. A humanoid wolf machine defeated an armored machine on legs that ended with wheels. The Wolf took a hand that could reshape itself as a blade and left. The cabal took the other hand and the armor from the defeated ghost and helped Victoria Adams arm herself with them. Maxwell found a hyena looking ghost machine, which agreed to help them find a ghost Victoria Adams could face in exchange for the wheel feet of the ghost.

                    Chapter 49
                    4/11/2025

                    As predicted by the ghost that sent them in the direction of Hel, there was no guard blocking their way. They entered and looked over the frost covered land. The ceiling was covered in roots as if the entire dominion existed beneath a large tree. Pockets of farmland existed within a forest that covered a large portion of the landscape with a massive citadel-like structure sitting at the center, a crimson light came from the highest tower, bathing the entire landscape with its light. Ragnar and Eye of God both recalled that this land was tainted by the Abyss, although neither knew in what way specifically. Ragnar, with his Death Sight, also took in the fact that the crimson light was Abyssal in some way, and that high above them in the roots was a massive cocoon that something within was writhing, pushing out against the cocoon.

                    The cabal began walking through the dead forest that greeted them when Ragnar spotted a cracked and toppled standing stone with words inscribed on it, four lines that read:
                    • “The Dead Shall Be Honored
                    • The Dead Shall Serve
                    • The Living Shall Observe
                    • The Dead Shall Be Desecrated”
                    With more words written along the spot where the stone was broken, regardless of the damage though Ragnar was able to make out what was written; “The Living Shall Be Devoured.”

                    The cabal traveled down a path through the dead woods where they came to a small farmstead with medieval Nordic appearance. They ectoplasmic crops were withered and inside the house it was completely empty, devoid of places to sleep, a fireplace, or any pleasantries aside from a table and chair. Right inside the door was a splatter of ectoplasm and some old farming tools. While searching the interior Eye of God discovered the strange rune that those in the living world have been compelled to inscribe on the bodies of the dead carved into the bottom of the table.

                    They left the farm and continued down the road until it split, one path going deeper into the woods, and one going seemingly around the woods. The cabal traveled down the path that would take them deeper into the woods and quickly the frost covered landscape became covered in an inch of soft snow. While traveling along the path they noticed fresh red snow coming from behind a thick, but dead, bush. They realized a wounded, living person was hiding in the bush, but they were preparing to run when the cabal started to approach. Ragnar used Matter patterning to change the snow to ice and trap the person, but the spell also caused a strong paradox, breaking down the rules of action and consequence following each other logically. <Three paradox that he tried and failed to contain so I used those to create an environmental tilt. I used Inconsequentiality from Nameless and Accursed page 102 if you want to see the exact rules, but I thought it would be funny…you'll see though.> Naturally the person freaked out, but Eye of God used Mind magic to calm him down and they got information from him. They learned that he and other living people were kept in a barn, and people were taking them from the barn to somewhere else one at a time. Two others were still in the barn, but he had escaped, not before being clawed by a zombie, which a small hoard of was guarding the barn. The cabal wanted him to come with him but we're unable to convince him to follow them, so instead they told him about the farm they had found and sent him there.

                    Ragnar led the cabal through the woods, following the trail of blood in the snow back to the barn. The building itself was once a two story building but was now caved in, with a hoard of about twenty zombies blocking the front door, which was sealed. Ragnar cast a powerful Death spell to shrink the zombies and they quickly stomped them all, realizing as they did that the zombies were in fact ghosts! Either way, the barn was no longer guarded and they opened the door, finding a man in his 50s and a woman in her 30s, scared but safe. Before they could glean too much information they heard someone say from the tree-lined, “Right on time!” A man in a perfectly presented black tux stepped out of the woods and approached. The man seemed put together and confident, but his eyes were replaced with analog clocks that bith told a different time and clicked at different paces. He informed them that he had already killed the cabal several times and didn't want to again, so asked them to surrender. Noting how undamaged he looked, and how Time around him seemed to be wrong somehow, Eye of God and Ragnar agreed not to fight him, Giger resisted the idea and threw a fake punch at Eye of God, who counter punched. They locked the two living back into the barn, giving them a wink, and agreed to take the man, Abraham de Moivre was his shadowname, to find the man who had escaped and pointed the cabal to the barn in the first place. Their real planned, <concealed by some lucky roles,> is to lure him back to the Abyssal Environment to try and overwhelm him there through sheer luck.

                                    Chapter 51
                                    5/16/2025

                                    The cabal had many choices to make over the unconscious body of Abraham de Moivre. They started by gathering some information. They already knew he was a Master of Time, so Maxwell dove deeper, learning he was an adept of both Matter and Death, and also that he was on the path of the Moros, but it was broken in a strange way he didn't understand. Giger looked into his past and saw him creating pipe bombs enhanced with magic, although he couldn't tell exactly how. He met with people who had clock-like eyes similar to him, and in Hel he spoke with two others, saying that a war had been started in Los Angeles, that Giger had been used to destroy the Assembly, and as he was leaving the castle they were in the face the cabal he stated “This will be the last time we see each other as I am.” Throughout looking through his past he would vanish out of Time and reappear, resulting in blank parts of his own timeline. Eye of God delved deep into his mind to learn what he was like when not acting as a mage, but learned that he spent all his time chasing his obsessions and working towards his legacy's goals. He also uncovered his memories of Giger, who Abraham de Moivre knew would play a role in his eventual death, but also “Giger” had been summoned out of the Abyss, an Abyssal manifestation that his legacy was using for their own purposes.

                                    While the others were looking over the defeated mage, Ragnar decided to do something about the Shadow That Walks Abyssal manifestation. Abraham de Moivre's nimbus was on it, and Giger had seen that he used Time/Matter Patterning to make it into a knife before turning to someone invisible to Giger and saying “Let's go retrieve it then, shall we?” Ragnar used Matter/Death Patterning to transform the knife into an ephemeral knife and then bound it to the Codex he carried. It started filling out the pages but he closed it instead of reading it.

                                    The cabal decided they couldn't allow him to live, but also didn't want to risk their own wisdom in killing him. Eye of God enhanced Giger's manipulation attribute and Giger convinced one of the two sleepers still trapped in the barn that they needed to do something about Abraham de Moivre. He trapped and tortured them, and who knew how many others before them. So it was their responsibility and right to make sure he doesn't harm anyone else. It worked, and the man agreed to do it. He shot him several times in the head and then slumped down, exhausted, and said he wanted to go home. The cabal realized that what they had just done, convince a sleeper to commit murder on their behalf, was no more than committing murder themselves using the man as the murder weapon, and all learned something from this act of hubris. Upon his death, the cabal felt a surge of magic but decided not to investigate.

                                    The cabal agreed to travel to the citadel in the center of Hel to find the other two mages and Hel herself before leaving, but first Ragnar decided he wanted to replace his eye with the artifact they had taken from the Ragnar of the Killing Fields. In order to facilitate this, Giger used Time magic on Eye of God, altering his past life to change him from a Manga store clerk into a world class surgeon who had finished college before the age of eighteen. They then put Ragnar under and Eye of God removed his right eye to replace it with the artifact. This time Ragnar did not learn from this act of hubris, but while he was unconscious he did have a dream.

                                    He found himself standing on a rocky shore, with snow and ice covering it. The sky was gray and the ocean rough. An old man with an eyepatch over his right eye and a long beard stood before him, the same man as before. This time Ragnar recognized him, realizing he was speaking with Odin. Odin was pleased that Ragnar had decided to bind himself to him. He then told him of a Scelestus named Jötunn who had almost destroyed Asgard, throwing it to the abyss, hundreds of years ago. He failed, but managed to capture Odin's Supernal sister Hel, and corrupted her with Abyssal magic before entering a sort of hibernation. Ragnar's awakening caused what was left of Hel's Supernal self to awaken, which in turn caused Jötunn to awaken. Odin also revealed that all doors to Asgard had been sealed shut by him at the end of his conflict with Jötunn, but his eye would open a door left deep in the Shadow when Ragnar was ready to learn from Odin. Odin told Ragnar not to face Jötunn until he was stronger and had spent time with Odin, but also warned him that Jötunn would seek to bring Ragnarok to Asgard once the door was opened again. Ragnar asked if there was any point of seeking out Hel but Odin admitted he did not know. Hel lost to the Abyssal manifestation that Jötunn had bound her to, so nothing left of her likely remains. Odin rode off on his eight legged horse and Ragnar awoke to see two ravens, made of both Death and Spirit ephemera, resting on his shoulders.


                                    <The artifact now in Ragnar's eye hole;>

                                    Eye of Odin (••••• •)

                                    Gnosis: 4 Death: 4 Life: 1 Spirit: 4 Mana: 12

                                    The Eye of Odin is an artifact from the Emanation Realm Asgard. A Paragon of that Realm, Odin, tore out one of his eyes during the near consumption of the realm by the Abyss and gave it to one of his mage servants, Ragnar. The Eye is a milky gray and replaces one of the eyes of the mage.

                                    Odin can look through this eye whenever he wishes, so long as the eye has replaced an eye. The artifact has a connected sympathy to Odin, and also serves as a universal key to all Irises that lead to Asgard, regardless of if someone has replaced their eye with it or not.

                                    The Eye grants the ability to speak with any ravens, and the ravens are always willing to share any information they have. This effect only works if the artifact has replaced the eye of a mortal.

                                    Likewise, if the artifact has replaced someone's eye, they are always able to see into both death and Spirit twilight at all times, passively.

                                    Lastly, if a mage has replaced their eye with this artifact, they gain a familiar. The stat block of the familiar is given below (see The Twin Ravens) but some special notes about it are provided here;
                                    • The familiar takes the form of two ravens, these ravens share a sight with each other and can exist separate from each other. Each body of the familiar has half of the corpus of the full Spirit, and both bodies must be destroyed for the familiar to die. If only one body is destroyed it will reform after a day within the artifact and can be released by the mage as they see fit. If both bodies are destroyed it takes a week for them to reform in the eye.
                                    • The familiar is both a spirit and a ghost for the purposes of which twilight it exists in, which mage sights can see it, and which arcana can interact with it.
                                    • The familiar is never detected by peripheral mage sight as if it is constantly veiled. Using manifestations or numina trigger peripheral mage sight as normal.
                                    • The mage (and Odin) can at any time see through the eye of either part of the familiar.

                                                Chapter 52
                                                5/23/2025

                                                The cabal now had the sleepers relatively safe with them but there were still several mysteries in Hel and around Hel that they were curious about, including the absence of the Lantern Keepers who they had descended with. First though, Maxwell used his Prime magic to cast a sort of a reverse Apocalypse spell, which allowed the entire cabal to view the world as Ragnar's magesight saw it. With this they saw the entire world on the edge of Ragnarok, volcanic ash filling the sky. Most importantly they all were able to see the massive, pulsing cocoon nestled into the roots protruding from the ceiling. Each of them agreed they needed to get a better look, but first they decided to find the Lantern Keepers. Eye of God used his Space magic to duplicate and borrow Ragnar's sympathetic connection to Vulcan and then used Co-Location to open a portal to her. It worked, with Eye of God feeling a subtle romantic impulse towards Vulcan upon seeing her. The Lantern Keepers were standing beside a wall of the citadel. They seemed shocked to see Eye of God. Ragnar let them know they had found the sleepers, which seemed to calm the Lantern Keepers, who stepped through the portal. They explained they believed the sleeper captives would be in the citadel and were preparing to storm it. A discussion followed about what should be done next. The Arcane Luminaries of the Expanse wanted to uncover what the cocoon Ragnar had seen with his Death sight was about, and they wanted to speak with Hel. The Lantern Keepers did not, reminding the cabal that their goal was the safe return of the sleepers, but agreed to return the sleepers to the land above and then return to investigate the cocoon. The Lantern Keepers did not want to leave a portal between Hel and the world of the living open though, for fear that some abyssal thing might slip through while they were away.

                                                They had sent one of the three remaining sleepers back towards the entrance of Hel, Ragnar, accompanied by Crypt, and went in search of him. They found him and brought him back, but Ragnar noted the body of Abraham de Moivre was missing. He alerted his cabal to this when they returned, and Eye of God reasoned he must have been pulled into the cocoon.

                                                Crown of Lead, the Mastigos of the Lantern Keepers, asked Eye of God to assist him in putting the sleepers to sleep and opening a portal. Eye of God sang a lullaby, which Crown of Lead joined in with, complimenting the use of that as a yantra, and then Crown of Lead opened a portal to their crypt sanctum in Los Angeles. Ragnar and Vulcan moved the sleeping sleepers through the portal. Before they mages could return there was a knock on the crypt door, which Vulcan opened. Outside was a guardian in pitch black robes, standing next to Chronos, one of the two Hierophants of the Mysterium. The Guardian went to Crypt and they discussed in hushed whispers, while Chronos asked where Giger was. Hearing his name Giger stepped through the portal. Chronos informed Giger that he had prepared to have him leave Los Angeles for Tokyo, revealing that a terrorist attack had happened while they cabals were in the Underworld by someone who looked like Giger. A video was shown to them where someone, clearly their companion Giger, entered a building where a large meeting was happening and blew them all up. It was revealed that this was the Free Council Assembly; one hundred mages died.
