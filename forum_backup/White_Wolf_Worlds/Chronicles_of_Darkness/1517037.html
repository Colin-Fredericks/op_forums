    Need help fleshing this Chronicle out

    07-03-2023, 03:16 AM
    So, slight spoilers for the ending of Remedy's Control ahead, but that's kind of the pitch for my game. I've told my roommate that I had a great idea for a one on one and she's willing to give it a try. But I'm definitely just at the Cool Idea™ stage of things.

    So, an example first. The finale of Control
    has Jesse failing to save the McGuffin alien entity and then the credits roll before becoming distorted. Then things go grey and dull and Jesse, who is the Director of the Federal Bureau of Control at this point, is in an early office area of the map standing in front of a peg board with people talking behind her back about how she's a loser. You go through this mission doing menial tasks over and over until you break out of it and do the name of the mission: Take Control.

    I want to run a scenario like that. Roomie makes a character who has a mundane life that she isn't satisfied with, and has dreams of a different life. She goes through her mundane life and things start feeling off and finally the character experiences something big and supernatural.

    And then I'll ask "this life you've been living, the only life you've known up until now, is false. You realize that. Do you want to keep investigating or do you want to live this life that you have now?" and since no player character is going to say no to that, I hit her with the whammy:

    "Your life crumbles away. Your family and friends don't remember you and you aren't in any of their photos anymore, because you never existed.

    "What Alembic did you calcify when you completed the New Dawn?"

    The character was a Promethean who completed the New Dawn with 10 Refinements.
    The character gains ties to the mundane world as though she had completed nine Refinements. If the character remembers everything, she can use an Alembic of one Transmutation that she fixed in her previous life, substituting Willpower for Pyros. If she remembers a mundane existence, the player can spend a further three dots on Social Merits to represent it. The first time she succeeds at a breaking point roll within the following month, she can reverse her choice instead of gaining a Condition — regaining her memories and supernatural power, or surrendering it for a normal life.

    Unfortunately I don't really have any idea where to go with things after that.
    ​Although I probably would give her 5 or 10 Pyros, instead of having her spend Willpower.

                  It's not a Promethean game, it's a Mortal+ game. How it works is that after the New Dawn, a Promethean who has completed one role from each of the ten Refinements gets two choices:
                  1. Give up your memories of your Promethean life and get a six dots of Social Merit to represent your new life. Reality warps itself around you. As far as I can tell, this is essentially the Divine Fire creating a Cover for you.
                  2. Remember your past as a Promethean and gain the use of an Alembic of your choice in your mortal life, using Willpower instead of Pyros (though for this game I'd give her a pool of five or ten points of Pyros).
                  During your first Breaking Point as a mortal, you can choose to reverse your decision. That implies those merit dots go away.

                  The idea is that the character realizes that what she wanted out of a human life is not actually what she wanted, and that she won't be satisfied with a 9-5 and a handful of decent enough friends. Just because you've completed the Pilgrimage doesn't mean your journey is over.
                  I just like the idea of playing off of that amnesia, and the way you get to choose to redo your decision.

                      The results for completing eight or nine Refinements give you two dots to spend on Social Merits whether you choose to remember or forget. You get extra dots if you choose to remember a mundane existence instead of your time as a Promethean, but going from seven Merit dots to two does not mean your place in the world has evaporated:

                      Originally posted by PtC2e p186
                      Whatever the case, the player writes his character’s human history based on her experiences on her Pilgrimage. This is the place that the Great Work has created for her in the world. Even if she remembers her time among the Created, the world has changed to reflect who she was. She may have a passport — or a criminal record — for her human identity and have to fake amnesia until she can piece it all together.​
                      Also worth pointing out that the character has to succeed at a breaking point within a month of becoming human to make this switch and the option to keep an Alembic isn't "you can calcify an Alembic when you complete the New Dawn" but "if you fixed any Alembics when you were a Promethean, you can choose to keep one of them when you become human with the best possible outcome," so this is a very narrow window of time to make a starting decision based on a character arc the player is specifically not being given the chance to go through organically in a scenario that assumes a lot more fragility to the product of a chronicle-long journey to forge a soul.

                      Likewise worth mentioning that part of the "you remember it all" package is Unseen Sense: Prometheans and no immunity to Disquiet, which is kind of important to remember if this game is going to involve Prometheans in any fashion past the setup.

                          Since part of the premise is the reversal of a decision from the New Dawn, may I ask what brought the somewhat unhappy mortal over the breaking point?
                          Because if I recall, exposure to the supernatural counts as a breaking point for mundane mortals...and if it was something that the PC encountered in their previous life, that could serve as the excuse to Remember...and then the character has to deal with part of their mortal life unrevealing while dealing with memories from both lives clashing. Throw in a mystery about the supernatural element that prompted this and you have a decent blue book/hunter story.
