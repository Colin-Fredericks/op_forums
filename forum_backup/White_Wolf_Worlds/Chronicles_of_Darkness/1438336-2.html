                                                    Patterning is required for total transformations and Weaving is used for transforming specific aspects of a subject. I think we can all agree on that.
                                                    For Matter, these specific aspects are often various material properties, which is why Alter Conductivity is a Matter spell and not a Forces spell (an equivalent Forces spell would instead simply ignore conductivity). Aggregate states are also material properties, meaning that despite water and ice being highly unlike each other and the shift between them very much looking like a complete transformation, it's still a matter (heh) of Weaving rather than Patterning. I think we can agree on this as well.
                                                    Well, allotropy is also a material property. You're just used to thinking about graphite and diamond as different substances, but they are the very same element and goes through phase transitions to each other based on pressure and temperature, just like aggregate states. Diamond happens to be metastable, but aggregate states can also be metastable. Supercooled water remains liquid below its freezing point until external force brings it out of balance and it freezes. Allotropes and aggregate states are basically the same thing.
                                                    The thing is, you're just used to thinking about the different aggregate states of water as the same material, and the different allotropes of carbon as separate materials, but anyone who recognises that allotropy (much like aggregate states, and less dramatic) is a material property should be able to change that property with a Weaving spell. Not allowing that would be the exception.
                                                    Alternatively you can look to the dramatic differences between some allotropes and consider them different materials, but then considering different aggregate states as being the same material would be the exception because then ice should be a concrete material that requires Patterning to transform into liquid water.
