Non-violent feedings are simply logical. Doing things that cause dangerous witnesses that need to be killed are not an every day part of Kindred existence. Frenzy is the wildcard here, but if we accept it to be part of "standard behavior" that's still a tiny fraction of feedings compared to Beast's standard of every feeding being harmful.

                                                            I honestly cannot speak for Morbus. 2e is my entry point into VtR (though I had a bit of experience with Masquerade before that). Morbus feeding could be considered worse than Beasts. Non-morbus plaguebearers, though, fall under the criminal negligence we've already discussed. At first taste of blood, vampires can know whether said blood carries any disease. Then deciding to knowingly spread it goes along the same lines as purposefully draining people to death or frenzying in a crowded mall. Non-standard behavior where I'll agree it can be considered as bad or worse than Beasts. But it's still non-Standard behavior. Vampire's don't run around eating Aids victims trying to spread it to all the foolish mortals.

                                                            As for blood, a few religions partake in the ritual drinking of blood, including a subculture called Sanguinarians. Hard to find much beyond that, because searches tended to go to vampire myths. That said, cultures that practice cannibalism are easier to find.
