    What would your character do ? (W:TF edition)

    08-06-2017, 12:39 AM
    I did something in the W:TA forums a couple days ago and I decided to do it here as well. The rules are simple, I give you a situation and you have to answer how your character would solve it.

    Here is the first scenario.
    ​You are currently tracking prey that is a member of a mafia family. You managed to track him down to a funeral party that is mourning the loss of his father, the Don of the family. He is currently in the funeral home shortly after the burial discussing business with his brothers. The home is heavily guarded and the goombas that surround the home won't hesitate to shoot you if you do a direct assault. What do you do?

          Fun!

          My guy hasn't been played in a while but I'll use him. Realistically, he might sneak his way in, then attempt to get the target alone and kill them, then make a hasty escape. But he does try to be cunning, and he's got the Renown to show for it. His plans often go bad, but gauru is more than enough to kill a few gunmen and a room full of gangsters. Still, try to be sneakier first.

          So. Luna's Embrace and a nice black outfit to fit in with the mourning crowd. Manage to get into one-on-one conversation with target. Lay the Hunter's Aspect on him, then Gaze of the Moon with a Delusion whammy so he runs away. Once he's outside, Command Artifice and order a car to run him over.

          My character had to do something like this at a hospital once, but I think he managed to just sneak in, get the job done, then sneak out.

                                              To the first scenario, it would depend on whether or not we were doing first edition or second edition. I've only done one werewolf character, so I'm likely to base the actions on what she'd do. Which in this case would be to infiltrate it using parkour then utilize the gift of scherezade's to cause people to sit and listen to her sing. While she was doing that, my packmates would take a more direct path, with the Warrior in our group dealing with anyone who gathers the sense to resist and getting their attention... and our irraka does what irraka do. If this were second edition, it'd be a little different because that gift isn't around anymore. Is this at night or day? If it's night, we have the advantage of vision and mobility. Parkour so that I don't have to take a direct path, find a way to the electric box, cut off the sources of their light. My entire pack then channels their inner Batman.

                                              Which, now that I think of it, should always be the answer. So change my first edition answer.

                                              In regards to being stuck in the coffin, I would lack the moldywarp facet unfortunately. Fortunately my pack has multiple pack related gifts, and I'd probably utilize them to alert the pack that something weird was going on with me. It's quite embarassing as a Storm Lord to require your pack to dig you out, but sometimes you have to have faith in the people around you to get you out of a situation you can't yourself. #Family

                                              For the third scenario... I myself think I'd need to know more about my prey to figure out the exact method I'd use. Are they aware I am hunting them? I'm assuming in this scenario that I have not tasted their blood or done anything that would seem basic for tracking..? I can't help but feel like I would lean on my Ithaeur for this one since she spends so much of her time shopping... she probably knows the center like the back of her hand and has all sorts of spirit friends there... :P
