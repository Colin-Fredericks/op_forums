                                      My thoughts:

                                      Missed Kickstarter Eras
                                      -World's Fair Vampire
                                      -Book of Judges Hunter - It missed being in DE by three votes. You have to include it
                                      -Blitz Innocents
                                      -Wild West Geist
                                      -Orient Express Vampire
                                      -Age of Piracy Geist
                                      -Mummy/Mage French Revolution

                                      Non-European/Colonial Eras
                                      -Xia Dynasty Vampire (China 1700 BCE)
                                      -Kipchack Werewolf (Kimek Khanate 1200-1220)
                                      -Mexican War of Independence Mage (Mexico 1810-1827)
                                      -Mana Musa's Hajj Promethean (West Africa to Arabia 1324-25)
                                      -1st Brazilian Republic Changeling (1889-1930)
                                      -Korean War Hunter (1950-1953)
                                      -Golden Horde Geist (1312-1341)
                                      -Rashidun Caliphate Mummy (Arabia 632-661)
                                      -Zulu Kingdom Demon (South Africa 1816-1828)
                                      -Zanzibar Sultanate Beast (Tanzania 1856-1964)

                                      Crossovers (in general, I want to see splats in crossovers actually interact, like in Mutapa and Black Death in the Companion)
                                      -Divine Comedy Geist/Promethean - Realistically Dante's Florence could host any and all game lines. I think Geist and Promethean are the closest two thematically because the Comedia keys in on the themes of Second Chances and Pilgrimage. While others may suggest focusing on Inferno (both the opening book of the Comedy and the CofD supplement) Dante is at his best if you look at all three parts of the Comedy as a whole work.
                                      -Etruscan Vampire/Mummy - Pre-Roman Vampire would be interesting, and the Etruscans practically invented Necropolises which is interesting for both Mummies and Nosferatu
                                      -Jacobite Risings Changeling/Demon - specifically on the Isle of Skye, where there are folk tales of fairies who are fallen angels.
                                      -Arthurian Mage/Changeling - Dave knows. And he knows we have. Mighty need for it.
                                      -Industrial Revolution Demon/Mummy - The God-Machine take advantage of human ingenuity while the Arisen rise to find a world that starts to resemble the one they lost lifetimes ago.

                                      Others
                                      -Timurid Werewolf - Seconded
                                      -Cuban Revolution Vampire - Seconded
                                      -Vatican Werewolf
                                      -Red River Rebellion & Northwest Rebellion Werewolf (Canada 1869 -1885) - a formative period for Western Canada and Metis culture. The importance of Louis Riel is hard to overstate.
