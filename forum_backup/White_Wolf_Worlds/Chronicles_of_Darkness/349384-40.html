        Originally posted by Corax View Post

        Kira from Part 4: Diamond Is Unbreakable is definitely a Acanthus Banisher.
        I see him starting out as a Thyrsus. His "make people explode" ability is obviously a Life Unmaking spell, and it's probably a custom Praxis. "Bites the Dust" came after that ability, and it seems to be a combination of Fate and Time magic: the ability sends people back in time, which is the Time 5 Unmaking spell "Corridors of Time," and the fact that there's a condition on when it take effect is the Fate 2 attainment "Conditional Duration." So he's a second-degree Master of Life and Time, and has at least 2 dots in Fate. So not only is he a scary-powerful Stand-user, but he's also a scary-powerful mage. Of course, at least mage!Kira would be a little hampered by Paradox, but he's so clever and subtle that he would be smart about avoiding it.

        Banisher works well for him. But Mad also appeals to me, since he's got a very obvious obsession with murdering women.

        Long story short is: some of the Stand-users in Part 4 translate to stupid-powerful Awakened.

                                                            Protagonists tend towards luck and good fortune. They are arguably fated for greatness simply by existing, in more common stories.

                                                            People seem to tend towards Mage for a lot because they have a big toolbox. One 'trick' a character pulls off, for instance Zack Morris' 'Time Out,' can be explained by a rote. Just ignore the rest of the toolbox, and assorted Mage Accessories like Wisdom, for purposes of making the character fit.

                                                            As an added benefit, mages tend to be fairly human. Spider-man's physical abilities, drastically increased strength, agility and durability, could be explained by Vampire's physical disciplines. Mayor Hundred from Ex Machina speaks to machines, effectively using the Command Artifice Gift like a werewolf. But Spider-man goes out in the sun and doesn't usually drink blood, and Mayor Hundred doesn't turn into a hulking wolfman, and these things can't be ignored. However, both of them are fairly human aside from their special abilities, so you could just stick them under Mage, though again ignoring the rest of the game.

                                                            If Deviant doesn't, I'm sure at some point there's going to be a book about having 'random' powers. We've already broached upon it with the Supernatural Merits and microtemplates, as well as Horrors and antagonist-building, but there's some sort of block for players when it comes to just hanging on a strange power to a character.

                                                            For what it's worth, Mayor Hundred would be a Wolf-Blooded of Sagrim-Ur's Blood whose Tell would be their ancestor's markings and ability to command machine spirits.
