                    Newspapers you say?


                    You should give it a try. The UK has been having a boom in neat urban fantasy (though I think America has too). And it might be worth seeing if you can detect any noticeable differences that could be used in your games.

                    Being Human and London Falling would be my go-to for example. Rivers of London is also ace, but a bit more light hearted than the WoD. You should also give Doctor Who a try, a lot of the leading lights in British urban fantasy worked on Doctor Who, and it's generally the most popular bit of British fantasy.

                    Young Dracula might also be worth a look as another contemporary British vampire story, it is aimed at kids and can't go anywhere truly dark, it's still good and if it's not worth researching it's worth watching. Wolfblood is another good kids urban fantasy, but it doesn't have any vampires.


                    I could talk in more general terms about British vs American urban fantasy; but if I'm honest it will be heavily biased by my tastes. It's not like I read dozens of books from each country to do a fair comparison. Though if you are interested in my biases just look at the Office of the Lord Stewards in Dream Catchers. Quite a few people said they didn't really fit in Hunter, which was kind of intentional - I wanted to push some boundaries so I based them on everything I perceived to be unique about British urban fantasy. I did create a hunter Conspiracy that can't carry guns and make a point of obeying that rule; that's not something I do without intent

                                            Well, I've got the 2E draft printed out here, and I'm just getting familiar with it. Will update this post with ideas in a bit.

                                            To make sure I've got things right, Atavisms are passive buffs that you get, while Channels are activated abilities that rely on Depth?

                                            Also, I'm going to be heavily critical below. I don't mean to attack you, but I do want to hammer out any problems, and that may come across aggressively. I apologise in advance.

                                            I'll leave grammatical and linguistical bugs out at this stage, unless they screw with the meaning.

                                            Current Channels:

                                            Lambent Eyes, Depth 5: Another question could be, "What is the most powerful emotion in the crowd?" (A crowd could be inspired by a speaker, but a certain section could be disgusted by it) Alternatively, "What fraction feel the strongest emotion, and what fraction feel the most common emotion?"

                                            Eye of the Watchful Diety, Prophets Adaptation: Does this adaptation forgo the Breaking Point for the avatar, since they know they are a prophet? Should it? Should it provide a bonus to the Breaking Point roll if it still inflicts one?

                                            Eye of the Watchful Diety, All-Seeing Adaptation: This requires a total of eight dots to acquire (disregarding sheol cost), is this the intention?

                                            Uncrowned Fisher King, Depth 6: The 2e core has switched to metric. You can drop the miles per hour comparison, and simply have a conversion for kilometers per hour instead of meters per turn.

                                            Uncrowned Fisher King, Depth 7: What exactly is a "reasonably sized river"? Are we talking the Danube, or Rhine? Or the Nile? It's a good abstraction, but it provides no sense of scale. How about 10 times their Apotheosis Size, as defined by Sheol, in meters? Thus a Sheol 10 Leviathan would need a 550 meter wide river, and a Sheol 2 Leviathan would need a 30m wide river. Ref: https://www.gislounge.com/river-widt...andsat-images/ for river widths in NA. A Sheol 10 Leviathan could cross America causing only serious flooding in Central USA and the Canadian border. If you mean this to significantly restrict movement, we can go even higher.

                                            Echoes of the Deluge, Depth 2, Alternate Casting: Do you mean tech with Size smaller than Int + Science + Sheol dots? or do you mean roll, and count successes? Both are a little inelegant, but I can't think of a better way right now. Will return to this.

                                            Echoes of the Deluge, Depth 6: Flavourful, but ultimately kind of weak for a Depth 6 Channel embrace. Would broadening the class to "anyone attempting to navigate in Tilts caused by this Channel" work? That way it'll (usually) hit Sailors, as well as anyone you want to hinder. Your Beloved are already immune due to Depth 5 after all.

                                            Echoes of the Deluge, Depth 7, Tribute to Tiamat: Could offering the sacrifice generate one Ichor per person if individual, or five Ichor (or ten, I'm not sure what you want the balance of economy to be like) for a group offering? Does this make it too powerful?

                                            Swallowing the Sun, Depth 5: It's never established that the Leviathan is immune to the Tilt generated by this Channel? Are Leviathans generally immune to their Channels, or should it be clarified here?

                                            Mother of a Thousand Young, Depth 3: You note "additional Ichor". Do you mean Ichor besides activation cost, or does generating a first-degree spawn cost Ichor? If it does, either the general description, or Depth 1 should be updated.

                                            I need to do a second pass, but this is what I've found so far.

                                            New Channels:

                                            A lot of these powers focus on the horrifying aspects of Lovecraftian monstrosities, but a Tranquil Leviathan knows her Beloved are people, and treats them as such. They may be a tad clinical, or prone to outbursts, but they mean well. Gods can still care for their subjects. This may be out of flavour, but I enjoy the thought of Leviathans struggling to be "good people".

                                            Nothing comes to mind immediately, but I'll mull over it. Something that empowers their Beloved maybe? Or avoids the mind-breaking aspects of being Beloved, so their cults aren't full of insane people? Maybe moves the burden to the Leviathan? It would be looked down upon as denigrating their divine nature, but it would allow their Beloved some semblance of sanity.
