              Originally posted by xenoterracide View Post
              How do Demons affect Spell Casting?
              I'm not sure what you mean. Do you mean how would they set out to interrupt or prevent spellcasting? They mostly wouldn't, although as usual with supernatural beings, they might have a few powers that are situationally useful, like freezing time with the Stop Exploit against a wizard who didn't come prepared to time-duel an Acanthus. Or do you instead mean what Arcana would you need to affect them with which spells? Pretty much the same Arcana to affect a human being with the same spells.

              Originally posted by xenoterracide View Post
              can they trace back pacts? or the stolen lives?
              Probably yes, but not as simply as casting a single Trace Pact spell. It might involve having to deduce the common factors behind many little temporal distortions witnessed under Active Time Sight, and then intelligent use of various other spells, like Postcognition and Correspondence.

              Originally posted by xenoterracide View Post
              if a mage looks at a demon with mage sight would they see anything other than a normal person?
              A demon in Cover? Normal person. While in Cover, the demon is actually physically and phenomenologically a human being with human insides. The Supernal Vision spell, with appropriate Reach, could expose the aura of the Unchained, but with a successful Spoofing roll, the demon can fool the spell.

              Originally posted by xenoterracide View Post
              How about the concealed gears of the god machine and its angels?
              Pending an official answer in the Crossover Chronicle or something, my inclination is:

              I think perceiving angels in Twilight ought to be possible with an altered Exorcist's Eye spell, similarly to how an altered Mind version can perceive goetia in Twilight. What Arcanum would govern angelic ephemera is a judgment call for the ST. I might suggest Fate or even Forces.

              Mages lack the ability of demons and stigmatics to spot the telltale signs of infrastructure instinctively, but infrastructure leaves a big footprint. The problem for mages is that it's a wide footprint, not easily contained within the purview of just one or two Arcana. All manner of Mage Sight might unfold pieces of each project as a unique puzzle: Life to sense beating hearts concealed where they should not be within machinery, Fate to trace waves of altered probability, Space to see where space has been folded inward, etc. Connecting these projects and occult matrices together into a larger, unified phenomenon is harder, and probably requires more work than spells alone. Aside from the increased likelihood of encountering demons or angels around them, or the chance of cultists or workers who can be traced to other works of the God-Machine, infrastructural projects likely come across more as independent Mysteries.

                          Originally posted by Stupid Loserman View Post
                          A demon in Cover? Normal person. While in Cover, the demon is actually physically and phenomenologically a human being with human insides. The Supernal Vision spell, with appropriate Reach, could expose the aura of the Unchained, but with a successful Spoofing roll, the demon can fool the spell.
                          I'm thinking Active Mage Sight would get a Clash of Wills vs the Spoofing effect, especially if you're using Space/Fate/Prime/[insert preferred Demon-detection Arcana here]...at least in a Mage game. Focused Mage Sight of the appropriate Arcana should probably use the higher of the Demon's Cover or Primum for Opacity, but there'd obviously need to be some reason for the Mage to be checking (like a Prime mage noticing the weird response of truth-detection magic when a Demon 'truthfully' says something false).

                          In a Demon game, I'd think about the niche you wants the Awakened to fill. If you want to emphasise Demon's abilities, make it hard for Mages to beat Spoofing (maybe a normal roll for Active Mage Sight and a Clash of Wills for Focussed). But if Mage Sight can burn straight through, Mages become a really scary, weird threat.
