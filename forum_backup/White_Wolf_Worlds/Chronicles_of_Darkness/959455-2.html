    Now here is an example hunter for the Georgia Historic Society Secret history division

    Melissa Apple, Academic: When undergrad history student Melissa Apple joined her favorite professor on an archeological expedition to the ruins of a forgotten Georgia ghost town, she had no idea it would result in a week of horror and loss that would mark her forever... nor did she realize it would get her a job offer from the Georgia Historic society to join a special division concerned with the spooky stuff.

    The expedition was supposed to be a simple thing, locate the ruins of an abandoned resort town once known for it's mineral springs, search it for any documents and relics that would explain what happened there and bring them back to Savannah. Poor professor Wilford didn't consider that what happened there might still be happening.

    When it was all over and the thing was dead, she brought the collected items back to Olgalthrope university, only to be recruited by the GHSHD in exchange for them paying off her students loans and now spends her time interviewing vampires, cataloging cursed civil war artifacts and generally having a much more peaceful relationship with the supernatural than she initially had.

    She has an on again off again relationship with George Boyce of the Bryant Family Pack, having meet him during a Historic society fundraiser gala and later helped him recover his Great grandfather/alpha's collection of rare antique (and in many cases, Fetish) firearms after a pack of iron master gang bangers stole them. She also helps the 6th Sense Savannah ghost tour company compact locate potentially haunted sites to add to their tours by going through the city death records and cross-referencing them with reports of haunting.

    In addition to her skills as an academic, she also has quite a few dots in survival and firearms, the result of training she underwent after surviving the ghost town.

    In a Savannah chronicle, she is something of a quest giver, putting together teams of hunters and/or supernaturals to acquire historic artifacts of an occult nature, many of which in are possession of an unaware mortal who can't be trusted with it, or a supernatural faction or mystery cult that plans to do something bad with it. She may also become involved in Boyce's plans for the Vinca exhibit or even the pure tribes civil war in general, thus making her a person of interest for both the werewolves and for the diamond orders helping the pure fight off the Praetorian seers.

        I suppose I ought to do mummy next

        I don't know mummy all that well, so I need some time to go over the books, but for now I will say that the Savannah nome is dominated by the Tef-Aabhi and the Su-Menent, who together are working on something that to outsiders seems similar to the project in D.C but... different and more successful. For while D.C is an attempt to build a new Irem, the Savannah nome is attempting to build not only a true necropolis, but a necrocracy one in which the arisen (and the other living dead, such as the Sin-eaters and vampires once the arisen calm them down after the initial mess happens) will rule quite openly as their various zombie servants handle the menial work. The destruction of the three lost squares was the work of the nome, boosting the necrotic energies long enough for the arisen to stabilize them (which is why the magi restoring one of the squares didn't lower the number of ghosts and moros awakening). If the plan works, the local arisen will have achieved a very different form of apotheosis, never needing to fall into henet again.

        The recent sothic turn seems to have begun the final stages of the plan, but also caused weird effects as the spike in death resonance bought the attention of others to the nome's activities, though they haven't figured out the end game yet. Sightings of odd ghosts in the underworld are circling among the sin eaters, the Mekhet are having bizarre and glorious dreams as they sleep the days away, a group of bone shadows and arisen swear they saw Anpu and Kamduis-ur on a date of all things at the Old Pink house, feeding each other fried chicken and being served by the mesmerized wait staff as they giggled like high school sweethearts and discussed their plans for "Our soon to be new hunting ground.

        Obviously anybody who likes their precious masquerade needs to the stop the arisen, but nobody has the ful picture and the age of the dead draws closer each night.

            And here are some Savannah Story seeds, all of which in some way involve the government dysfunction in Savannah.
            • The ongoing feud between judges on the Chatham county recorders court has rendered it completely dysfunctional and allowed the supernatural community to get away with pretty much any city ordinance violation they want, with the Uratha taking full advantage of the dysfunction to bring in wolf-dogs for use as packmates. Similar issues at the Savannah fire department are also impacting the vampires who make their havens outside the legally protected (and magically fireproofed) historic districts, as unknown parties set havens outside the safe zone ablaze. and all this is before you get to the embezzlement from the library system and all the other corruption...
            • maybe forcing Lovett out didn't solve all the problems, but, even in a city where pushing the limits of the masquerade to drive up tourism is the norm, do we really need some Nosferatu who thinks he's hilarious running around in a mask and costume as "the weed whacker in the garden of good and evil (as the local press have dubbed him)" and fighting crime in an over the top frank miller batman style?
            • The recent city ordinance limiting the use of roadside memorials has created an essence shortage for Savannah's many ghosts, who are acting out beyond what is safe for the tourists, looks like the local hunters and sin eaters need to team up to solve the essence crisis.
            • The Dead City Clowns are a fairly altruistic brood of beasts, a troupe of horror actors who play scary clowns on the clock, entertain sick folks for charity and do their real terror on the deserving scumbags of Savannah in their spare time. Recently they were attacked by a hero who seemed to think they were behind the recent wave of spooky clown sightings across the US, which the dead city brood swear aren't their handiwork (and to be fair, the clowns described by the panic look nothing like their costumes or their horrors). Still, the hero in question is a potential problem, one they can't get rid of easily due to her influence in the city's medical sector and they are worried she will do something really bad at the Paranoia Nights haunted house the brood are set to perform at this Halloween season. The brood are offering to set up anybody who can handle the hero with their many contacts in Savannah's nightlife and foodtruck scenes in exchange, but the hero needs to be handled quietly, while she has gone off the deep end since focusing on the brood, her reputation as a skilled doctor and the dysfunction at the local hospital she works at have kept anyone in a position of power over her from realizing how far she has gone.

                Since it's impossible to talk about Savannah's crime situation without talking about Rickey Jivens and his gang in the 80s and 90s, I am gonna put a quick summary of his criminal career, and some story seeds , here:

                Jivens, in many ways, was the ganglord that broke Savannah. Many of the most lurid urban legends about urban gangs, such as the one about the headlight murder initiation, were true in the case of his crack dealing crew and the sheer among of violence he ordered between 1989 and 1991 set the tone for the drug violence in Savannah for years to come. The fact that the violent crime rate in Savannah has just returned to the point it was at the height of "Little Rickey's" power has many of the city's residents nervous... they remember Rickey and Rickey himself has been less than quiet on the internet. An unknown friend of his has been putting managing his internet presence while he is spending life in prison and the supernatural community of Savannah waits and worries.

                Story seeds for Rickey Jivens:
                • Jivens was the Eshmaki Tyrant's Eshmaki Tyrant, one who perfectly represented the racist idea of what first lady Clinton would later call the "super-predator" when promoting her husband's crime bill. Some say that the only reason he didn't become the new apex in Savannah was that it was his life and not his legend they feared. Other say he became an Apex, just not in Savannah...
                • Jivens was tied in deep to the Carthian movement in Savannah, since it was the white flight his violence accelerated that allowed to them to finish their takeover of Savannah's city government before Lovett went down. They used their clout to have the police help him out, which may tie back to Lovett in the present...
                • Jivens would be a great NPC in a new wave requiem chronicle set in Savannah. Georgia was one of the first 3 states to get hit by the crack epidemic and Jivens was one the first crack lords, giving the PCs a chance to witness the birth of a national crisis firsthand. Depending on the PC's views, they may try to help bring him down. Of course, a less ethical PC group might try to control him and his empire.
                • Who exactly is helping Jivens post all those videos and managing his social media? All his known lieutenants went down with him, but their were many street level supernaturals that had dealings with him and so did the Night Watch and the Jagged Crescent. The Supernatural tourist promotion board wants Jivens's social media man taken out before they do something that drives off the tourists for good.

                    Savannah News Inspired adventure hook:
                    • Someone stole the Jesus statue from Savannah's blessed sacrament church. It doesn't seem like a big deal, except the Malleus and the local silver ladder have asking some pointed questions about the theft. It appears the statue was part of some sort of protective ritual cast on the city, and played a role in minimizing the damage from hurricane matthew and it's theft may be part of a larger plot.
                    • Speaking of the hurricane, Duppy Jonah has given the power he stole from the storm to his favorite huntsman, now more powerful than ever and sent it after the freehold. However, this time Jonah has instructed his huntsman to attack the Freehold's non-changeling allies in Savannah's occult community and cut off their support network before catching them.
                    • The local TFV branch is having trouble following Matthew as well. Seems that someone made off with the contents of one of their armories in the city during the hurricane, while the agents were temporarily reassigned to help with the evac. Seems there was a prototype in there that they need back right now.
                    • A coterie of kindred survivors of Katrina have come to Savannah from New Orleans to learn more about the magical defenses that minimized the hurricane damage. Archbishop Paul has given them permission to stay in the city for now, but the new guests don't quite get the political situation in Savannah and the tactless kindred are stepping on a lot of other supernaturals' toes and pissing them off as they try to find out how to prevent another Katrina in New Orleans. Not only that, but the guests are very tight lipped about the current situation in New Orleans as well, to the point that they won't say what covenant currently rules there...

                            • After the recent shootout at the City Market on 4th of July, The supernatural tourism promotion board has decided that enough is enough, the gangs have got to go, with four of the current 6 items being dedicated to fighting the gang violence problem in ways both subtle and warlike.                                     alright, first up The Court of the New South AKA ( First Tongue name to go here)

                                    ​This court, which originated in Atlanta in the late 80s and moved into Savannah two decades later, represents the emotions and desires of those in the South that want to move beyond the more hateful parts of it's legacy, including both the dreams of the historically oppressed for a better future and those of the descendants of the oppressors for an identity free of that legacy, even if that means pretending that legacy is truly over when it isn't.

                                    ​The result is a court more or less based around the idea of southern hospitality, and the related concepts of tourism, gentrification, art, gluttony and various modern day southern exports, such as the wave of cabbage patch kids based toy spirits that came flowing out of Cleaveland Georgia in the 80s and Urging moms to riot over their physical counterparts.

                                    The spirits of this court have access to a lot information about local business and cultural institutions and the mortals that run and frequent them, but their court ban can make it hard to get an honest answer out of them

                                                    This court originated in LA in the 1940s, and began slowly moving into Georgia in the 90s due to the increasing media presence in the state.                                                             and here is the next court

                                                            Court of the Grey Phoenix

                                                            Court Ban: if a monument to the confederacy is destroyed, the spirits of this court in the city lose all their will power until it is rebuilt. If a lesser relic of the confederacy is destroyed, all spirits of this court in the scene lose their willpower for the remainder of the scene.

                                                            This court, which originated during Reconstruction, represents the nostalgia for the old south, and the racial issues resulting from that nostalgia. This court, led by the Grey Phoenix spirit of the same name, is most influential in that one of it's members is currently urging Chatham county's Sheriff Wilcher, who has had multiple black men die, be raped or otherwise abused in his jail and at point blocked health monitors from inspecting the jail with the goal of claiming him and his deputies. The court also has ties to the local Seers of the Throne and some of the Ivory Claw families in the city, though the inter-generational conflicts in the tribe have been threatening that particular alliance as of late, and the younger claws actions against the seers have also caused trouble..

                                                            The court also tries, with little success so far, to discredit the BLM chapter in Savannah, which has proven resistant to such efforts by the court and it's minions due to their intentionally moderate activism, and the fact that many of the pastors that make up the local BLM's leadership are under the protection of the Pentacle and a pack of forsaken anticonfederate southerners called the Anderson Street Scalawags .

                                                            And of course, the confederate monument in forsyth park is their main locus, and their achilles heel.
