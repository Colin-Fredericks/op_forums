    IMPORTANT INFO:
    I cant add more pictures to an existing album using imgur.
    So everytime we got 5+ new wallpapers i'll create a new album replacing the older one with all the old and new wallpapers organized.

    HOW TO COLLABORATE:
    Please note if you find wallpapers on the internet with good quality and widescreen, post here.
    Make sure the wallpaper you found is not on our actual album already.
    Those who know how to work with photoshop will also be of great help.
    Im open to receive ideas for the next wallpapers! Do not exaggerate on your ideas, there are many limitations that has to be considered.
    These wallpapers mostly are montages copying the art from the book and putting together. Its really easy and there are plenty of tutorials on youtube Try yourself!
