    Your Moments of Awesome, Epic Fails, Tearful Sadness and Noodle Incidents

    08-02-2022, 04:05 AM
    The Badass Splat Boasts thread made me think of situations where those boasts would apply in-game, which in turn made me wonder: what were some of the most awesome, fun, sad or memorable moments in your game?
    This thread is meant to share retellings and for reminiscing about those crowning achievements that gave you goosebumps, those plumbing depths that made you facepalm or cry, and everything in between that stood out. Of course, it is also meant, in turn, to serve as inspiration for others.

    One of mine: a Hero was stalking the Dean, who was acting as a sort-of surrogate "Baron" for the local Beast community, and a quite new PC, who was a student, came into the scene. Due to observation stuff, to protect her from possible fallout, we needed her to appear frightened and not connected to the Dean and my PC, a local Irish mob member (which she'd never met). To support her acting scared, I had the 'brilliant' idea to support her - by scaring her with a Giant-sized Anakim with Looming Presence and an Intimidation roll, which, of course, turned out exceptional. This placed a lasting fear in the student which made cooperation that much harder, but hey, at least the scream was very convincing!

    Another: the very same Anakim had a pendant (and Specialty) in staring down people with a murderous glare. It worked really well - until the moment he met a Ventrue whose Ghoul he'd killed. Let's just say, this staring match went very much not in his favour, and he spent quite some time running errands for someone he shouldn't have liked quite that much.

                                  Inkblot guides the Rat Pack through the Dreaming Road, distancing himself from the curiously peaceful neighborhood into the seedy underbelly. The detour is uncomfortably long, and gradually inflicts a sense of creeping dread as the details of the scenery twist and deform. Detached shadows crawl around and whisper gibberish that drips with violent intent. The gas lamps' illumination takes on a lurid crimson hue and the temperature drops sharply, with the visible gusts of air from the Changelings' mouths also becoming that color. There is no more jaunty comedic music, only the steady echoing of dripping and scratching. After what seems like an eternity, they arrive at what appears to be the ruins of a hospital. The dripping and scratching noises are louder now, and the Changelings can smell a horrible wave of the ferric scent associated with blood.

                                  Ever so cautiously, they enter the hospital. There is seemingly no one there, and no massive blood stains as one would expect. But the noises, they are very loud now, as if they are one corridor away from finding whoever this entity might be. Even Inkblot seems to hesitate and fidget, as if fearing the worse for everyone involved if things go wrong. He motions for the Rat Pack to hear him whisper, his Mien all the more gruesome in the crimson light.

                                  "Whatever happens, do NOT try to fight him, I beg you," With that last warning, he knocks on the door, which slowly opens on its own. They see a humanoid figure with filth-encrusted and blade-like fingernails parting the tissues of a rotten carcass. Bloodshot, rheumy eyes fail to register any of you, swollen like overripe grapes. A red cloak, once proud, now little more than a rag stained with the viscera of past conquests. As you enter, absolute silence falls onto the room. A finger ceases its skittering movement across the body's veins.

                                  Aria's mien makes it relatively easy to hide her surprise, at least. The rim of her hollow curdles briefly, and she inclines her head. In the crimson, the whispers of her face take on a metallic edge. "Charmed. Would you prefer we conduct this business with or without names?"

                                  Cain steps next to Aria, his curiosity outweighing whatever self preservation instincts he has. “Symbolism, remember? Maybe approaching it with a gesture of good faith might work better than words.”

                                  Cain takes out his flask, and offers it to the man.

                                  Markus, for his part, busies himself with mentally cataloguing every item in the room, double-checking each one.

                                  Unfortunately for him, there aren’t many, and he’s soon forced to find other things to draw his attention away from the raw spectacle.

                                  Sanguine gives a brief and annoyed glance to the Changelings, as if they were things he would scrape off his shoes. He wordlessly makes a dismissive gesture toward them, and refocuses on the grisly and cryptic surgery.

                                  Leilani gracefully fails to grasp the severity of the scenario, though she knows something must be dead in close vicinity and their conversation partner must be particularly hard to reach.

                                  Hmh. Aria takes a step closer, circling around once she's pretty sure she's found the least in-the-way spot to stand, hands folding behind her back. A lean forward, 'looking' the surgery up and down. After a pregnant moment, she speaks a quiet, genuine, "artistry, isn't it?My studies were in the psychological rather than the physiological, but even there we learned how much of what we consider 'us' lives in those vessels." Here in the dream, her attention is palpable, a shadow that the mind can sense even if the eyes do not, 'looking' up at Sanguine. "What do you see, in this forest of rubies?" Her tone is low, attentive, drawing to mind whispers in a library or over a bottle of wine.

                                  "Artistry and science, in mystic matrimony. I appreciate that you don't recoil so easily at the sight of my handiwork. As for what I see... all of us who cleave to humanity still bleed red. My grand strategy is simply to make that perfectly apparent. A standardized genetic blueprint, with phenotypical expressions guided not by random sequencing of DNA, but by the Pneuma inherent to us all and carried within our vital fluids. Have a seat, all of you. What brings you here? You have my curiosity, do not waste it. My work is very important to me, and you're postponing universal transcendence of biological divisions if your business is trivially handled by someone else."

                                  While Leilani is sure of her ability to appeal to these bloody sensibilities, Aria seems like she has a particular handle on the situation.

                                  Aria inclines her head, having to run that through some dusty Latin picked up in med school. But she does find somewhere to sit, trusting in the motley to do likewise. "We're in need of a heart. Conjured meat--even should we be capable of that ourselves--is insufficient. We're wresting the symbolic life of it out of the hands of the Kindly Ones."

                                  A beat. "It must be more the patient's heart than his own flesh and blood."

                                  "Whatever happens, do NOT try to fight him, I beg you," Inkblot's plea rings in Cain's ears. Despite how highly he regards himself in most scenarios, avoiding fights is the one place where he will concede, just a bit. He'll let Aria handle this one.

                                  "How does that advance my own projects, pray tell?" He inquires, with his tone implying that he is far too obsessed with whatever lofty goals he just ranted about to properly conceive of making effort for anything else.

                                  It might have been that easy. Alas. A moment's consideration. "You wish universal transcendence of biological division; then your foe is not solely cells and sinew. There are beings who lay claim to the bodies of common humanity."

                                  A vague gesture to her own disfigured mien, briefly remembering the feel of her Keeper's fingers sinking in to scoop her face out. It curls the rim of her hollow into short-lived fangs. "You will have to usurp them and their claims, if you wish to free all."

                                  Perhaps due to her curse, Leilani feels a draw to appease him. Having never taken a seat -- in part because of the awkward job of fumbling for one without the attendance of Aria's helping hand, caught as she were in other dealings -- Leilani takes a step forward towards the smell she is at this point positive must be a corpse, though in the dreamland she's unsure if there is even license to take moral qualms. Can corpses come from nowhere here? It would make as much sense as anything else, she supposes.

                                  Her approach is met with a wall. Not a physical wall, but a wall of presence. A refusal of her approach, metaphysical as it were. She detects no mistake in her step, no faux pas in her mannerisms, and yet her curse doesn't do enough legwork to penetrate this surgeon's mistrust and solitude. Even the slight limp and faint dampness added to her clothes seems to have made little affect on him. The rules are different here, Leilani understands, though she's unsure how much of it is the dream and how much is him. Wordlessly, she redirects herself. Further from the table, she makes it look like she had no intention of approach anyhow. Restless legs, she states with her gait. Nothing more. Ignore me.

                                  Sanguine slowly approaches Aria, and looks her dead in the "eyes". He is unbothered by the phantasmagorical whispers that emanate from within Aria's void, and indeed seems to be eerily curious about how it works.

                                  "How do you process sensory data without the relevant organs? Do you need to eat, drink, breathe, sleep, excrete? How can you even think when a large portion of your frontal lobe is missing along with your face? Can you get pregnant and sustain a fetus if you cannot transfer nutrients and oxygen to it? If an object is inserted in that orifice, where does it go?"

                                  The blood-sorcerer speaks in a completely clinical tone, and clearly does not care in the slightest if his questions are inappropriate or invasive. "That's all quite fascinating to me, really. Relevant to my research, as well. I cannot allow humanity to end up like you. Give me a written document with detailed answers within seven days, and I'll prepare the metaphysical component of that clockwork heart."

                                  This is the gulf between Sanguine and Aria. The void of her hollow stretches for a tiny infinity to separate his vulgar dissection from the holistic approach on which she prides herself. Aria cannot afford to reduce her patients to pieces of anatomy and connective tissue; Sanguine does not--can not?--see anything but.

                                  She meets his stare in her own way, feeling the iron in her soul creeping its way up her spine, a blade threatening to unsheathe. What color do you bleed, o' sage of carmine? Is it any more vital than the liquid light which coursed through my Keeper? These thoughts rise no higher than that, however, failing to escape the darkness where her secrets live.

                                  "Provide an address. I will have the documents overnight." Conspicuous neutrality, better than feigned geniality. Show Sanguine that discomfort and hate do not control you.

                                  "Understood. I will have the item delivered to your Hollow around the same time. Now be gone from my sight, all of you. My work requires focus," He makes another dismissive gesture towards the Changelings, and then turns his back to them.

                                  Inkblot is suddenly possessed by a rage completely uncharacteristic of a Victorian alienist. He forcibly spins Sanguine around so the blood-sorcerer is looking him in his moonlight-eyes and horrid smile of barely contained violent intent.

                                  "Have you not a speck of bedside manners, sir? You might be working for the betterment of humanity, and though Aria might no longer be fully human, she is still a person! She has thoughts and feelings just like you do, even if buried beneath obsession and arrogance! You would do well to respect the boundaries of those you work with from now on, because if you don't, you will have to answer to me. And I have ways of dealing with butchers like you. Goodbye, Sanguine, and may you examine your flaws with the same dedication you have for others' flaws before it's too late."

                                  Sanguine completely ignores Inkblot and returns to his dissection.

                                  Leilani winces, inwardly and silently.

                                  Cain scoots away from Inkblot, wanting to be far away from the chaos.

                                  “Uh, well, … he doesn’t speak for all of us. Excuse his manners, please.” Cain says the last part through gritted teeth, and grabs the collar of Inkblot’s shirt. “You’re putting Aria in danger. Suck it up, or else someone’s gonna end up killed.” He whispers to the psychiatrist.

                                  Aria gets to her feet, cursing herself. I knew Inkblot was under duress-- The moment of terror passes without Inkblot erupting into a fine mist, and she hurries over to shepherd the motley out, fetching Leilani on the way.

                                  Assuming they're not avasculated or otherwise stopped, she doesn't relent until the group is back out on the cobbles and the door shut behind them. A moment to catch her breath. "L-Leilani. If you would be so kind as to... sing a song while we make our way back, please."

                                  A lingering 'look' at Inkblot. "I appreciate it, truly," an affectionate tone.
