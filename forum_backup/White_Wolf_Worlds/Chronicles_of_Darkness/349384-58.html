                        Hm... Iron Man could have a few different possible answers.

                        First, we could have an Obrimos Mage (depending on the characterization, he could be Libertine, Silver Ladder, or possibly a Seer)

                        I could also see a Hunter with a custom Endowment making up his armor.

                        He could also be a Wizened Changeling, with the Metalflesh and/or Smith Kith. His Keeper, The Ten Rings (also known as The Mandarin, Killian, and sundry other Titles), took him while he was in Afghanistan to build weapons to arm his soldiers and Huntsmen.

                        Finally, he could be a Klagen Navigator Genius, with his armors being distinct Wonders.

                        If you wanted to get a little silly, we could also use Princess for Magical Girl Iron Man, as a Champion of Diamonds, possibly dipping into Storms or Tears territory at times. Iron Man's done some stuff, so his Belief would probably be pretty low. In that case, Rhodey could reasonably be Sworn with a War Machine Bequest.

                                                    Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
                                                    I know people have attempted to stat Superman before (Beast, Princess, or Mage, was it?), but what about as a simple Horror? Obviously, he has the Blast Numen, Unbreakable, Flight, Speed (Numen?), and ludicrously high Strength (not even factoring into his other Attributes). Bane is obviously Kryptonite.
                                                    I wrote him as an epic Demon. But he also works as a Horror:

                                                    Virtue: Justice/Kindness
                                                    Vice: Pride
                                                    Aspirations: Inspire People, Be A Superhero, Do The Right Thing

                                                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 8, Resolve 10
                                                    Physical Attributes: Strength 10, Dexterity 8, Stamina 10
                                                    Social Attributes: Presence 6, Manipulation 4, Composure 8

                                                    Mental Skills: Academics (Journalism) 3, Crafts 2, Computer 1, Medicine 1, Occult 2, Politics 1, Investigation (People In Trouble) 4, Science 2
                                                    Physical Skills: Athletics (Flight, Laser Vision) 6, Brawl 5, Stealth (Plain Sight) 2, Survival 2, Weaponry 3
                                                    Social Skills: Empathy 3, Expression (Heroic) 5, Intimidation (Overwhelming Power) 3, Persuasion (Leadership) 4, Socialize 3, Streetwise 1, Subterfuge (Protect Identity) 2

                                                    Merits: Area Of Expertise 1 (People In Trouble, Heroic), Common Sense 3, Danger Sense 2 (Advanced 2), Direction Sense 1 (Advanced 2), Eidetic Memory 2, Indomitable (Epic), Interdisciplinary Specialty 1 (People In Trouble, Heroic), Professional Training 5 (Journalist: Academics, Investigation, Expression), Trained Observer 3 (Advanced 2), Virtuous (Advanced 3), Greyhound 3, Iron Stamina 3, Seizing The Edge 2, Allies (Superheroes 5), Alternate Identity 3, Closed Book 3, Contacts (Journalists, Law Enforcers), Fame 3, Inspiring 3, Iron Will 2, Resources 2, Safe Place 5 (Fortress Of Solitude), Status (Justice League 5), Striking Looks 1, Sympathetic 2, Table Turner 1, True Friend 3 (Lois Lane), Iron Skin 2 (Advanced 2), Defensive Combat (Brawl), Martial Arts 5, Unarmed Defense 5,

                                                    Defense: 14
                                                    Initiative: 16
                                                    Speed: 50 (Flight 150)
                                                    Willpower: 28
                                                    Health: 15
                                                    Potency: 10
                                                    Armor: 5/5

                                                    Bane: Kryptonite

                                                    Dread Powers: Armored Hide 3, Eye Spy, Breath Weapon*, Hideous Strength*, Juggernaut, Natural Weapons 3 (Bare Fists), Numen (Awe, Blast, Speed, Occult Sight*), Reality Stutter, Swift, Regenerate 3, Surprise Entrance, Unbreakable, Vanish*

                                                    *Breath Weapon: Horrors with this Dread Power can command the elements to destroy their enemies, often literally breathing gouts of fire or hurling bolts of lightning. Choose the type of attack when this Dread Power is selected; this is typically an element such as acid, fire, lighting or cold, but other, more exotic elements might be used depending on the nature of the creature. By spending 1 point of Willpower, the Horror can blast individual targets in range (5/10/15) that counts as a ranged attack with a thrown weapon and requires a roll of an appropriate dice pool – the opponent’s Defense. This attack has a weapon bonus of +0L. By expending 3 Willpower, the weapon bonus increases to +2L and inflicts the Burning Tilt. In Superman's case, the breath is made of ice, the Tilt inflicted is Immobilized and the Range is 100/200/400.

                                                    *Hideous Strength: Superman’s might is truly formidable; by spending a point of Willpower, he can increase his Strength by his Potency until the end of the scene. Furthermore, he adds his Potency as automatic successes on Strength + Stamina rolls made to perform a feat of strength, such as lifting a massive object, jumping or breaking free of restraints.

                                                    *Occult Sight: Superman is sensitive to supernatural phenomena. He notices the active use of supernatural abilities within the range of his senses and may make Perception rolls to approximate their distance and direction. In addition, by spending a point of Willpower, he can see through powers of concealment, illusion and deceit with a Clash Of Wills using his Wits + Potency as his dice pool. Lastly, he always knows when he is being targeted by a supernatural power and may mimic the Aura Reading Supernatural Merit, but without drawback.

                                                    *Vanish: The Horror with this Dread Power can disappear in the flash, a puff of smoke, or the blink of an eye. Once per scene, by spending 1 Willpower, the creature may instantly exit the scene. The Horrors must have an established location (at least a dot in the Safe Place Merit or equivalent) that they retreat to when using this ability.

                                                    Bye.

                                                            I might as well post the Doctor as a Horror.

                                                            Virtue: Brave/Curious
                                                            Vice: Proud
                                                            Aspirations: Explore And Have Adventures, Protect And Teach Those In Need, Solve Mysteries And Crises

                                                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 9, Wits 8, Resolve 9
                                                            Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 5, Stamina 5
                                                            Social Attributes: Presence 6, Manipulation 9, Composure 6

                                                            Mental Skills: Academics (History) 4, Crafts (Machines) 6, Computer 2, Medicine 2, Occult 3, Investigation 3, Science (Time Travel) 6
                                                            Physical Skills: Athletics 3, Drive (TARDIS) 1, Larceny (Sonic Screwdriver) 3, Stealth 2, Survival 2, Weaponry (Swords) 3
                                                            Social Skills: Empathy 2, Expression (Humor) 4, Intimidation 2, Persuasion (Leadership) 5, Subterfuge 3

                                                            Merits: Area Of Expertise 1 (Time Travel, Sonic Screwdriver), Common Sense 3, Direction Sense 1, Indomitable 2, Interdisciplinary Specialty 1 (Time Travel, Sonic Screwdriver), Investigative Aide 1 (Academics, Science), Professional Training 5 (Time Lord: Science, Expression, Persuasion), Trained Observer 3, Virtuous 2, Greyhound 1, Sleight Of Hand 2, Allies (People He Has Helped 3), Contacts (Historical Figures, Earth’s Governments), Inspiring 3, Iron Will 2, Safe Place 5 (TARDIS), Table Turner 1, Untouchable 1, Armed Defense 4, Defensive Combat 1 (Weaponry), Fighting Finesse (Sword), Light Weapons 4

                                                            Defense: 8
                                                            Initiative: 11
                                                            Speed: 13
                                                            Willpower: 25
                                                            Health: 10
                                                            Potency: 10

                                                            Ban 1: Can only use Hypnotic Gaze with Psychic Paper.

                                                            Ban 2: Can only use Gremlin, Laying On Hands, Telekinesis and Technopathy with Sonic Screwdriver.

                                                            Ban 3: Can only use Maze, Surprise Entrance and Vanish with TARDIS.

                                                            Ban 4: Can only use Regeneration once per story.

                                                            Ban 5: Cannot harm his Companions.

                                                            Dread Powers: Gremlin, Hypnotic Gaze, Influence 1 (Exploration), Know Soul, Maze, Mimir’s Wisdom*, Numen (Occult Sight*), Psychic Talent x 3* (Clairvoyance 3, Laying On Hands 3 [Advanced 3], Omen Sensitivity 3, Telekinesis 3 [Advanced 2, Epic 2], Technopathy 3, Telepathy 5, Psychometry 3), Regeneration*, Surprise Entrance, Vanish, Price Of Power x 5

                                                            *Mimir’s Wisdom: As per the Atavism (see Beast: The Primordial). The Doctor is considered to always be at Low Satiety and can mimic the Satiety Expenditure effect by spending 2 Willpower points.

                                                            *Occult Sight: The Doctor is sensitive to supernatural phenomena. He notices the active use of supernatural abilities within the range of his senses and may make Perception rolls to approximate their distance and direction. In addition, by spending a point of Willpower, he can see through powers of concealment, illusion and deceit with a Clash Of Wills using his Wits + Potency as his dice pool. Lastly, he always knows when he is being targeted by a supernatural power and may mimic the Aura Reading Supernatural Merit, but without drawback.

                                                            *Psychic Talent: Creatures with this dread power are endowed with great powers of the mind. Each dot in this Dread Power grants a creature a number of Merit dots equal to its Potency. These Merit dots may be used to purchase Supernatural Merits.

                                                            *Regeneration: The Doctor may heal all damage and Resolve all negative Conditions as an instant action without spending Willpower points. He changes appearance and often Virtue/Vice as well.

                                                            *Vanish: The Horror with this Dread Power can disappear in the flash, a puff of smoke, or the blink of an eye. Once per scene, by spending 1 Willpower, the creature may instantly exit the scene. The Horrors must have an established location (at least a dot in the Safe Place Merit or equivalent) that they retreat to when using this ability.

                                                            *Price Of Power: By taking on an additional Ban or Bane, an entity can increase its repertoire of abilities. Each additional Ban or Bane can give it one of the following.

                                                            * Two Numina, Influence dots, or Manifestations.
                                                            * An increase of its maximum Essence pool by 5.
                                                            * A Dread Power.

                                                            An entity may take this Numina a number of times equal to its Rank.
