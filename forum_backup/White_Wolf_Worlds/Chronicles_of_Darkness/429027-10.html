                        I feel the game has improved a lot more with the 2.E. That said though I feel there's still some hold over from 1E that should have been left behind such as flat bonuses, Long wait between rolls for powers. I also find the new regeneration rules a bit lacking. Considering how much more deadly 2E WoD is due to auto successes, the regeneration doesn't really strike me as a creature capable of coming back from anything.

                        Off the top of my head few things I would house rule for my games:
                        Auspice benifits will no longer be based on flat bonuses. The Cahalith Auspice ability with give +/- equal to Glory. The Elodoth will be able to use it's death rage ability equal to renown, Irraka can open doors equal to cunning and the Rahu can ignore conditions that hinder combat for a number of turns equal to Purity.

                        Any gifts that have a flat bonus would be house ruled the same.

                        I would house rule regeneration to be 1/1 with Primal Urge. so 1 bashing at PU , 2 at PU 2, 3 at PU 3, ect, ect.
