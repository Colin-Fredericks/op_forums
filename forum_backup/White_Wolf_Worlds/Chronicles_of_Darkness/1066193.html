    Examples of Patron Tools

    03-22-2017, 05:19 AM
    Patron Tool Yantras are magical tools given to the Seers of the Throne (or other people who bargain with powerful entities) that represent that Exarch's/entity's symbolism in the Fallen World. They can then use the tool as a Yantra. They are described as "strings that puppet the Fallen World like a broken marionette." Each Exarch has a specific resonance that limits what magic can be worked by the Seer using that Exarch's blessing.

    What I'm wondering is: what exactly are Patron Tools? The heading of the Tool Yantras section says that magical tools are mundane items used to help work magic, but then the Persona Yantra is listed under the Tools section, and persona is more abstract than just simple mundane items. Are Patron Tools actual physical objects with symbols of the Exarch on them? Or are they more abstract, e.g. actions or deeds symbolic of the Exarch's dominion? A possible example of such abstract "tools" could be a Prelate of the General engaging in violence as part of a spellcasting ritual (self-flagellation, torture, attacking someone, whatever), or a Prelate of the Unity using his authority over a crowd as a Patron Tool Yantra in public speaking setting.

                                                  Patron tools are the incorporation of symbolic resonance for the person's Exarchial/Abyssal/whatever patron. Physical objects are one form they can take, but they might also include a prepared environment or symbolic act.

                                                  So, for example, a monitor set to observe feeds from security cameras would definitely count as a Patron tool of the Eye. Likewise, a Seer sworn to the Paternoster might stand in a high vestibule, listening to the chanting of parishioners below, praying to a false god and murmuring useless prayers, and use that to empower a spell as well.

                                                      To my understanding,
                                                      mundane items = Minitry (Order) tools, similar to Order Tools: Mammon can use credit cards as his Order tool.
                                                      Patron Tools = Exarch's name/symbol, similar to High Speech, faster but specific: By invoking Mammon himself, seer empowers his spell

                                                      I want tools to feel important. Sure a credit card is Mammon's (mundane) tool but isn't the headquarters of MasterCard a "stronger" Yantra since it combines both the concept of "wealth" (similar to credit card) but also "control/authority of economic activity"? Shouldn't a dramatic change is the Market (Apple's stock Price) be a stronger Tool than that of a rather common US dollar?
                                                      Provided it is very thematic to the spell and situation at hand, I would rule a bonus dice of +2 (instead of normal +1) or apply a dice factor (9-again) on the dice roll.

                                                      Thus, using Prelacy Yantras incoporates an Exarch's will into the spells Imago and by some extend the nimbus flare. That must be something detectable under Active Mage sight at the time of casting or at least later under Focused Mage Sight as enemy mages try to study it.
