    Werewolf Second Edition final version changes [and possible remaining errors!]

    04-16-2015, 09:06 AM
    As some of you might have noticed, the final version of Werewolf Second Edition was recently uploaded. I compiled a list of changes (excepting spelling, grammar and stat blocks), so I though I should share it. I won't guarantee completeness, but all the important changes are in here.

    The change also introduced a new error: on page 92 "For example," should be removed under the Merits heading.

    Also Harmony is still imbalanced when considering Breaking Points. The lowest tier, 0-3 is four levels, while the highest tier, 8-10 is three levels.

    Originally posted by Final edition changes
    Clarified that not impossible to identify a werewolf in Hishu as supernatural (p.61).

    Initiative is Dexterity + Composure, not Dex + Wits (p.83).
    Speed is Species Factor + Strength + Dex, instead of Size + Strenght + Dex (p.83).

    Missing parts of Character Creation Quick Reference added (p.84).

    Clarification that a Totem cannot grant dots of Dedicated Locus or Totem, and that characters implied by social Mertis such as Allies or Retainer are not themselves part of the pack (p.92).
    [Error: under the heading Merits, "For example," should be removed.)

    Rahu Hunter's Aspect inflicts Swaggering Condition instead of Submissive Condition (p.98).

    Facets of Shadow and Wolf Gifts are unlocked at any Renown increase (p.99).

    Essence can be gathered from a Locus by physically touching the heart in the physical realm, or eating strange bits of meat or vegetation that appear around the locus in Hisil, instead of the other way around (p.100).

    When creating a werewolf, pick a set of triggers instead of choosing individual ones. Also personal triggers are suffered at most once per night, while general triggers have no limit (p.103).

    For the third level of the Favored Form Merit, specialize in a Facet instead of a whole Gift (p.106).

    The drawback of Efficient Killer Merit is that use is a common kuruth trigger, instead of sending you straight into Wasu-Im (p.108).

    Auspice Renown is used in Clash of Wills of abilities that are not Gift Facets and thus have no associated renown (p.115).

    The Pack Triumphs Together Facet is now Reflexive instead of Instant (p.127).

    The Lore of the Land Facet has a dice pool of Intelligence + Survival + Purity (p.128).

    The area of the Ward the Wolf's Den Facet keys of Glory instead of Cunning (p.135).

    While under the effect of Chain Rage, achieving five turns of lucidity during a period of Wasu-Im ends the rage (p.140).

    Jumping is Strength + Athletics (p.161).

    Using Hard Leverage is a breaking point towards Flesh. Against humans it removes one Door if the character's Harmony is 8 or more. Used on spirits remove one Door if the character's Harmony is 2 or Less. Otherwise remove two Doors (p.164).

    Environment levels now go from 0 to 4 instead of 1 to 5 (p.173).

    Stun Gun has a +1 to +3 die bonus, instead of zero (p. 177).

    Instead of Resolve + Composure, spirits roll Power + Resistance when confronted with a bane (p.185).

    Defense is lower of Power and Finesse instead of Finesse and Resistance (p.185).

    Reference about spirits having to be of the same type to be eachother's banes removed (p.185).

    References to Angels and Ghosts removed from influence rules (p.187).

    Instead of Control, Strengthen upgrades Resonant to Open (p.189).

    Rapture inflicts a Condition instead of a Derangement (p.192).

    The Fire Elemental Dread Power costs 1 Essence and has a duration of one scene (p.210).

    Toxic Bite and Venomous Ichor do not inflict Sick Tilts anymore, only Poison (p.211).

    Essence Vemon inflicts Poisoned, and not Sick (p.218).

    Under the influence of Devil Inside, a Wolfblooded stays in Dalu until she leaves the scene, instead of flees the scene (p.301).

    Under Host-Ache, Concentration roll must be Composure roll (p.302).

    Anyone who fails their roll when entering an area marked by a Marker suffers the Demorized Condition, and not Driven Away (p.302).

    With Second Skin one can change into Urhan and not Urshul (p.303).

    Shape-Shifted turns you into Urshul and not Urhan (p.303).

    Moon Taint inflicts the Poisoned Tilt and not the Sick Tilt (p.309).

    Submissive Condition replaced by Swaggering, which has a different effect (p.311).

    Poisoned description changed so that it also accounts for sickness (p.313).
    EDIT: Added the changes to choosing kuruth triggers and how often triggers can happen. Missed it on my first pass as the sidebar has a strange background color which my comparison program doesn't handle well.
