                    I'll write up more details on the other pitches later, but the basic list I have is...

                    VtR: Qin Dynasty - Covenants that rose of the Hundred Schools of Thought. The Legalist Covenant (which in addition to bureaucratic governance of vampires is also the culture's "we are monsters who are supposed to keep humanity in line through fear" faction) is beginning to enforce 'Shu' (the Masquerade) on the rest of the Kindred. The Confucian Covenant (feudal rule + serving as active "ancestors" with filial relationships with living family and necromantically with the passed) is fighting back HARD. With expansion incorporate in 2E "Immortals" with the Emperor and Taoist Alchemists seeking immortality and kindred blood as a potential key

                    WtF: Meiji Restoration

                    MtA: Classical Maya Decline

                    PtC: 17th Century England, the last gasp of the once great "Belenus" Lineage of "Wickermen" (the primary fire/choleric Lineage before the Frankenstein), whose Disquiet promotes religious fervor. The last of them die (or achieve the New Dawn) in the Fire of 1666, following decades of Civil War, theocracy, and Restoration

                    CtL: Underground Railroad (ideally expanded to allow inclusion of trods connecting similar struggles in Dahomey, Abeokuta, and Brazil)

                    HtV: Birth of the Haudenosaunee (a new Hunter Conspiracy is born under the blessing of Hiawatha and the Peacemaker themselves, to fight to uphold the Great Law against cannibal witch-cults)

                    GtSE: Dante's Italy (ideally expanded with "Inferno", the locally accessible Underworld is being corrupted by Demons/Lemures)

                    MtC: Height of the Inca - an era focusing on clashes between the Twice-Risen and indigenous undead outside the Arisen frame of reference (not that the Twice-Risen have a complete grasp on their own frame of reference)

                    DtD: Wild West (Unchained head to the frontier trying to escape the Machine, but on iron rails it chases behind them)

                    BtP: Ivan's Russia (culturally diverse groups unified as kin under the "terror" of a powerful Apex)

                    Dt???: Mauryan Empire (focusing on Visha Kanya lore)

                    Blue Line: Innocents in the Blitz



                    Other potential Eras include...


                    Werewolf: Hunting Grounds Sumer (updated to 2E and reframed as being in the window between the Sundering and the Oaths, with the Oaths on the horizon after the fall of Bau)

                    Hunter/Mummy: Book of Judges (Akhenaten's war on the cults is pushing fleeing "foreign gods" into Israel)

                    Changeling/Beast: Ulster Cycle Ireland (the Invasion Cycle plays into the LORE of this era that shapes supernatural culture)

                    Hunter/Demon: Albigensian Cathars (including a Cathar Compact which opposes the "Demiurge" [God Machine], and a pre-Malleus Church Conspiracy that hunts rebel Demons)

                    Promethean/Demon: Mansa Musa Pilgrimage (opposing omens from the God-Machine and Principle, while at the same time you have the themes of pilgrimage for the Prometheans, and building connected infrastructure for Demons)

                    *Mage/Geist: Golden Age of Piracy

                    *Mage/Beast: French Revolution

                    Vampire/Hunter: Pre-Mutiny India

                    *Vampire/Mage/Deviant: Chicago World Fair (Requiem in the White City crosses over with the experimenation of the eugenics era, as modernist Nameless and Seers of the Throne inch closer and closer together until they'll blast apart with the Great Refusal)

                    Werewolf/Geist: Great War (five beings that may be literally the Horsemen themselves walk the earth, while the Uratha hunt the unprecedented spirits of industrial warfare in the trenches)

                    Vampire/Beast: Jazz Age (Lovecraftian Racism & the Height of Post-Civil War Racial Violence clashes with the beauty and intrigue of the Harlem Renaissance)

                    Demon: Bleeding Edge (fully cyberpunk rules update for 2E; I know Demon is a little obvious BUT it provides this important opportunity - the God-Machine is maintaining different possible "near futures" simultaneously, and Demons alone have the power to move between them. So you've got your environmental disaster Earth, you're Chinese global surveillance Earth, you're warring megacorps Earth, your socio-anarchist transhumanist Earth, your Unfleshed Prometheans outnumber humanity Earth... etc etc)

                    Mummy: Infinite Macabre (full space-opera rules update for 2E; the Fifth Turn, the Arisen cults seek Starry A'aru among the literal stars on biblical pilgrimages ala Battlestar Galactica)



                    *these three eras actually have a narrative string running through their Mage elements



                    - Ok, so this is actually one era short to be the full list equal to the 26 we are getting, but I've got two ideas on that playing on very recent history... so recent they may be in bad taste. Option 1) "American Werewolves in London", the 90s, Brethren War/Reclamation rage in North America, and many Forsaken take refuge in the UK, which has its own Hisil thrown into chaos with the death of Princess Diana and the rise of a new Incarna (see Shadows of the UK); 2) Changeling in 2003 Baghdad, where Djinn lore based Courts are played off against the brutal reality the past decades of Bathist rule and the "shock and awe" destabilizing Coalition Invasion.

                    Check out my expansion to the Realm of Brass and Shadow

                                                To revive an old idea of mine: how about the Seven Years' War (focused in the American colonies moreso than elsewhere) as a Vampire/Hunter crossover Era? This is where British and French imperial, mercantile, and religious differences erupt into bloodshed, with each side not only using its armies but also hiring all sorts of former pirates and trying to win native tribes over on dubious alliances. My chronicle for this was all about Quebec and a shadow war for control of the colonies between the primarily French Sanctified and a British Invictus branch, with a unknown native covenant as a major wildcard and possible Strix involvement.

                                                Where things get really fun is when you throw in the Hunters, everything from Malleus cells an entire ocean away from their support networks and supernaturally-aware privateer crews to a possible new Native American conspiracy that may well have died off (or gone deep underground) by the modern day.

                                                The best part is it makes for a companion setting with the proposed Pirate Mage/Geist!

                                                        One of my personal favorite historical events/eras is the Taiping Rebellion (1850-1864). It's a fascinating clash of antiquity and modernity, eastern and western culture, conflicting radical ideologies. It has hallmarks of a pulpy adventure setting with colorful, larger than life characters. Also tens of millions of people died horrific deaths from war, famine, and disease while the world mostly didn't notice or didn't care.
