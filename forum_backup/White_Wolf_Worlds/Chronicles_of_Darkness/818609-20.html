    How I would approach "redeeming Lemuria"

    •Diversify Lemurians: not all Unmada, but mostly unmada creating contrast to Peerage's non-unmada majority (could be made a positive feature, since Peerage basically encourages their Unmadas to hide their true nature); Anchronism may remain but shouldn't be the core of their existence. As matter of fact even the first edition Baramins didn't really fit! What era did Phenomenologist weep over? Why are so many Machinist more exicted about market than fatalism? Didn't Atomist weep over the zeerusted future rather than actual past?
    •Make Lemurian's do right things when Peers do wrong: the Peerage was foil of Lemurian Empire, it's logical that beat up Lemuria would try to be foil of Invisible Empire. Lemurians could recognize difference between medically recognized mental disorders and disorders caused by Inspiration, making them think that Peerage's constant "You are Mad!" sounds like gaslighting tactic? Lemurians could be the guys who scavange up experiments and equipment from supernatural accidents (for their mercantuses and personal projects) to stop it from falling into mortals' hands and result in havoc? Maybe Lemurian's are The Face of Genius when interacting with other Supernaturals since Peerage's often sceptical and bitter mindset is rather unfriendly?
    •Exploring hypocricities of Peerage: We already had mentions of older Peers acting sometimes as bad as Lemurians, Peer Illumiated, mentions of Peers manipulating recruits for personal profit and Peerage's fanatical Extermination Squads? This could create just as interesting and terrifying enemies as Lemuria.
    •Creating examples of exciting cooperations and childishness of mutual pettiness.

                Guys, I want to note something: integrity isn't about how you're going to act, it's about how past events have scarred you.

                Notice how, with Dragon's Conviction, we've got Breaking Points which push towards Hubris whenever an event reinforces megalomania, and ones which push towards Guilt whenever they fail to live up to being a goddamn super-powerful Dragon. As Hubris encroaches, self-image becomes more powerful, but selfish whims and instincts become less manageable. As Guilt encroaches, self image becomes weaker while self-loathing begins to drive them to self-destruction, but determination to do just one thing right pushes them forward. The scale shows how well they're managing their newfound sense of power as it teeters between the two unhealthy extremes.

                Meanwhile, with Vampire (which I think might be the closest to this), you have Humanity. Vampires are clinging to the last shreds of their humanity, and every time it's reinforced that they are no longer human, they slip. They can have human touchstones around to keep them from losing it faster, but as the years wear on, simply outliving eras without aging is a constant reminder that they are apart from humanity. As they slip, it shows, because the supernatural afflictions which separate them from humanity get worse.

                Try and think of a system based on a series of mental-scars and psychological-reinforcements, and the benefits and consequences of those experiences.

                Dragon Rekindled: The Oroboroi Chronicles.
                The official reboot of Dragon: The Embers.

                                Lemuria don't need "redeeming". They're already redeemed. Though you're right that "fuck Lemuria" doesn't really work, and they obviously should get full sized write ups.

                                Originally posted by Weird View Post
                                What era did Phenomenologist weep over? Why are so many Machinist more exicted about market than fatalism? Didn't Atomist weep over the zeerusted future rather than actual past?
                                This is a fundamental misunderstanding of the Baramins. They don't weep over eras. They weep over ideas that were proven false.

                                Take the Atomists. They weep over the idea that "Technology will save us!". An idea that died when the power of the atom turned out to create the atom bomb instead of jetpacks, atomic powered cars and The American Dream. The zeerusted future is just the decoration, and you'll get plenty of exceptions: Nanotech Atomists who belive in post scarcity futures.                                 Originally posted by Weird View Post
                                Should we maybe include examples of Illuminated in media?
                                The thing about Illuminated is that you have to answer a very tough question. "What's the difference between Ethics 1 and Illumination?". An Ethics 1 Genius is cutting up orphan boys and for all intents and purposes unable to understand any sane thought. You can't just make an Illuminated slightly worse, they need to feel unique and different.

                                My suggestion for the Illuminated: Base them on Adolf Hitler. Not wacky mad science Nazis, the real Hitler.


                                Hitler / Nazi anti semitism is essentially insane. It requires you to believe things that are factually untrue, and yet Hitler was able to convince an entire country to believe it with absolutely horrific results.

                                That's what an Illuminated should be like. An Ethics 1 Genius is utterly alienated from normal sane people. They might do terrible things to normal sane people, but their ability to interact is mostly limited to blasting them with Katastrofi or Epikrato.

                                An Illuminated looks like they have gone through madness and come out the other side. But they haven't. They walk and talk like regular people, but what they're saying is just ever so slightly out of sync with reality. And their Mania is infectious.

                                Essentially their Unmada field affects normal people, Havoc proofing their Wonders and convincing everyone they make sense. They still need to see the wonder or speak to the Genius, but they're pre-deposed to believe. In the best case Geniuses have to work around the clock to round up and dispose of all the Jetpacks, find ways to convince people that they never owned a jetpack before they leave the Unmada field and go crazy, and censor social media to stop people outside the Unmada field going crazy.

                                In the worst case the Illuminated convinces people to commit genocide.


                                Oh. And Geniuses cannot tell an Ethics 4-6 Unmada Genius from an Illuminated. Sometimes it's obvious - if mortals are building and buying devices based on a publicly disporved scientific theory like the luminiferous aether someone's obviously Illumianted. But there's plenty of cases where some legitimate new mortal invention (lets say Graphine) catches the imaginiation of an Unmada and other Geniuses who think that Graphine is actually mad nonsense kill the "Illuminated".
