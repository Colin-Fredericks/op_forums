    Too many supernaturals?

    05-27-2016, 01:53 PM
    Greetings,

    (This might be rambling a bit, bear with me please)


    The default Chronicles of Darkness idea is that is "our world but (supernaturally) grim", and this supernatural is caused by several beings from mythology and such; my peeve is that currently there are too many supernatural beings and societies to be secret! We have: Vampires, Mages, Werewolves, Changelings, Sin-Eaters, Mummies, Beasts, low-power psychic/magicians/skinchangers, ghosts, spirits, etc.

    The secret keeping powers and effects (The lie, mascarade, Lunacy, Mask, etc) can account for people not knowing in an individual basis, the problem is aggregation, CoD is supersaturated with suernatural beings, which since have become so abundant and numerous, are no longer SUPERnatural (definition: departing from what is usual or normal especially so as to appear to transcend the laws of nature)

    In a chronicle that assumes all the gamelines are valid, isn't the world overpopulated with magical beings?

              Another thing to consider is that there is not one CofD setting. Which is to say, the cosmology for each gameline does not line up with the others neatly, which brings us to the following bit that separates CofD from the WoD:
              -A supernatural creature only exists if you want it to.

              The main consequence of that? If you're playing Vampire: the Requiem then the only supernaturals that definitely exist are Vampires and Ghouls. All the gamelines are compatible rules-wise but there's no inbuilt assumption that every type of supernatural exists in any specific world.

              I know many people like to take CofD as a kitchen sink world and assume that everything lives in the same universe, and that's a fine view, but at least the 1e gamelines were more toolkits than settings, and even in 2e there's no 'this game takes place in a setting which also includes werewolves and mages and demons and beasts' even with the tighter settings*. I know I don't like to run a game with just Vampires (I'll through in some small 'm' non-awakened mortal mages as well, and probably some type of spirits or demons), but there's no reason to assume that X exists just because they appear in another gameline.

              * Not more specific as such, but tighter thematically, I found Requiem 1e to be rather bland but Requiem 2e really highlighted the struggle for me.

              Blue is sarcasm.
