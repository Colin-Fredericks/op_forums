        Originally posted by Beriorn View Post
        I don't really see why the Axioms would require such heavy reworking. Some finetuning perhaps, and have Prostasia do something more, and you're pretty much set.
        I think it might be good to have it structured more like Werewolf 2E gifts: You have your Axiomata, rated 1-5, and then you can choose Facets from various Fields for things your Wonders can do. Every time you rank up in an Axiom, you get a new Application from one of your Fields for that Axiom for free, and you can buy access to new Fields as you wish.

        For example, you could have a Field of Biology (call it life-weaving or something if you want it to be more paradigm-neutral) that has the following Applications:
        • Mutation (Metaptropi)
        • Necrosis (Katastrofi)
        • Revivification (Exilixi)
        • Immunity (Prostasia)
        • Uplift (Automata)
        • etc.

        That way, we can have a lot of flexibility in how a Genius develops as a Genius and cut out the clutter of options for any single character, but it doesn't totally close you off from being an Omnidisciplinary Expert if you want to put in the Experiences.

                            Grimm: Wrath of a Genius

                            Grimms are defined by their fury, which comes through in their use of Katastrofi Wonders.
                            Beat: Your temper causes you to lose sight of your goals or put yourself or friends in danger.
                            Resolution: You can push yourself past normal anger into a white-hot rage. Resolve this Condition to gain a +3 bonus to attack and Intimidation rolls for the scene, but you may not use All-Out Defense.

                            Hoffnung: Give Me the Wheel

                            Visionaries are fueled by a need for control and power, sometimes as a desire to “improve” the world, otherwise just as plain power-lust.
                            Beat: You attempt to take control to the detriment of yourself or others.
                            Resolution: You stay strong when lesser people buckle. Resolve this Condition to gain a +3 bonus a Breaking Point roll, or on a roll to resist coercion or control, be it mundane, supernatural, or super-science.

                            Klagen: Cassandra Truth

                            Klagen are keenly aware of the destruction they attract, and most are devoted to desperately and pointlessly trying to warn others away.
                            Beat: You fail to prevent harm to others when you saw it coming.
                            Resolution: It's happening again, but you know what to do to defuse the situation. You can Resolve this Condition to gain a +3 on a roll to prevent violence or disaster (e.g. talking down an enemy, defusing a bomb, etc.)

                            Neid: I'll Show You All!

                            Neids are the classic bitter mad scientist with a bone to pick, and a need for approval that can drive them to extremes.
                            Beat: Your need to prove yourself drives you to harm yourself or those close to you.
                            Resolution: You will have the recognition you deserve! Resolve this Condition to gain a +3 bonus to any roll to intimidate, coerce or convince others that you're right.

                            Staunen: Neophilia

                            Your affinity for new things can easily distract you from the dangers they carry. This makes you amazing at braving the unknown, but you easily forget how bad an idea that is.
                            Beat: You endanger yourself or those close to you by pursuing a new phenomenon or obsession
                            Resolution: You can look into the Abyss longer than others, and learn more when doing so. When studying a metanormal phenomenon or advanced scientific creation, you can Resolve this condition to gain a +3 bonus on a roll to analyze or contain it.

                                Reminds me I have to think of a Condition for the Eifer Catalyst I wrote.

                                How do Facets splinter off the Werewolf Gifts? Is it similar to 2E Promethean, where each Transmutation had four sub-branches of the ability that each did different things. On one hand that would help with the organization but I don't think the Axioms should be complicated with further subdivisions. Having the 'field' in Genius be something like the Key in Geist would be ideal to minimize or eliminate Experience costs. Once you know a field it works in all your Axioms. Or maybe the Genius limits their fields based on Inspiration, and thus could advance in new fields at the cost of having their latter fields of work no longer work for them.

                                That being said, I propose another way of doing it (as to play devil's advocate):

                                Why not just pair a Skill with an Axiom?

                                When it comes to Axiom creation (at default Attribute + Skill + Axiom) expanding how Skills are applied would already create a bunch of options. The 1.1 version used five mental skills and Medicine often fit the weird biology category. Expanding the Skills used with Axioms to all categories (Physical and Social included) will also help branch Genius out of the predominate academia themes.

                                With this model your tech tree nodes/variables/infrances/what have you are collected like Skill Specialties, somewhat, only when it comes to matters of Inspiration the Genius can apply them to any other fitting Wonder, experiment or some such.

                                -----------------------------------

                                What may be best is bridging both systems for use in play. Using Skill + Axiom is the most off the cuff, useful for the innovative Geniuses to create freeform Wonders, etc. Having a grand field of work (like weird biology) could be modeled like the Fellowships. As written they focused more on individual kinds of Wonders but it would be a suitable way to map a field of the Mad Arts & Sciences. I'd wager more Geniuses working on the same stuff might bring the barest trappings of consistency, such as more easily mitigated Faults and Havoc.

                                That way several Fields/Fellowships could be written up, an overview of recent Inspired trends with favoritism amongst the Foundations. And you'd still have Skill + Axiom for more radical or alternative applications, and the options for a Genius collaborative to set up their own Field.

                                            Okay, so I've been reading some discussion in other thread, and a lot have been saying that Genius doesn't have a clear identity, and I'm inclined to agree.

                                            So we need to take a step away from the Axiomata for a second, and think about that before we do anything else.

                                            So, let's start with the theme.

                                            In the introduction, the Genius book says the theme is "Transgression". But there's not actually a lot of transgression happening. In fact, in a lot of ways, players are rewarded for not transgressing. Keeping Obligation high offers nothing but benefits, low Obligation offers nothing but problems, showing the world your creations is doomed to blow up before anything significant can even happen, and the player-aimed faction has a stated goal of "make sure we don't fuck up society." It feels like Genius is that #edgy kid that tries to say shocking stuff, but never really registers as anything but cute.

                                            So what can we do to try and make Transgression the center of Genius?
