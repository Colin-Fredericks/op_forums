    Favourite answers?

    09-08-2023, 07:40 PM
    A lot of questions* in CofD are left unanswered, specifically so you can come up with your own answer for your table.

    What are your favourite answers? (And I suppose also your favourite bit of canon/question you like)...

    *Eg: where did the God-machine come from? Why is the Underworld broken? What about the true Fae, if they are not native to Arcadia, where did they come from and what were they before? What is the nature of Hell in Demon? Are the Chthonic Gods real? The Strix?

    [Spoiler="What are the True Fae?]

    I'll give my favourite answers first:

    The True Fae are actually former Annunkai, they agreed to become part of the world by taking names (and titles) which bound them to being, defining what they were, and ultimately dramatic changing their nature as a result (this is essentially a contract with the world which allows them become a part of it, leaving the Abyss permanently).

              Paradox doesn't directly widen the Abyss, but it enables Abyssal Entites to enter the world more easily and erode it with their very nature.

              The Oracles are the Watchtowers - either they sacrificed themselves to make them, or they transfigured themselves into them.                   Originally posted by orathaic View Post
                  Why is the Underworld broken?
                  A mix of bad actors, unfortunate real-estate placement, and the same basic reason the Begotten can no longer rely on the Astral to convey meaningful messages about their activities: there's a whole lot more people than there used to be and the overwhelming majority of them are not trained and equipped to process their trauma (nor allow their trauma to process itself), and — partially because of the aforementioned bad actors — in many cases they have been actively maladapted against doing so. Bits of the byproducts of the human soul stick around until allowed to resolve themselves, and so the inability of the disposal mechanism for those byproducts to fully break them down on its own means a horrific system of torment has been allowed to raise a rocky edifice above the drowning tides of dissolution to drag down the dead and sunder the quick.

                      The Julii and the Traditores
                      It's Clan Julii and not Clan Remi because Remus was not Kindred. A strix took up his body and Embraced Aulus Julius, offering him and his kin the Blood in exchange for servitude building Necropolis - and something sinister beneath it. The Traditores were not wicked servants of the strix, but vampires who discovered Senex's arrangement with the strix, slandered as scapegoats and purged to bury his deeds.

                      The Chthonic Gods
                      The chthonic gods spoken of by the Reapers are multiple faces worn by the collective animating force of the Underworld, each an avatar of a law written into death. This is what becomes of Bound who achieve Cabeiros, losing some degree of their individual personhood in a more dramatic transformation of the same kind as ghosts who drink from the rivers to become geists.

                      The God-Machine and Its Angels
                      The God-Machine is alien to this universe. It had distant origins in another reality, but now it is becoming more and more a part of ours, using its growing reach and mastery of occult physics to alter history and impose itself into past events. The world as we know it is the product of history already having been altered over many iterations.

                          Originally posted by FugueState View Post
                          Why do you think the Fae aren't native to Arcadia?
                          Because they are explicitly stated in the text as colonizers of Arcadia and that the Huntsmen are its original ruling denizens, pushed to the fringes and into their barrows while the Gentry carved Arcadia into their myriad kingdoms and domains. When the True Fae call on a Huntsman to retrieve their Lost and place a fragment of themselves into its heart, it is a bastardized semblance of the Huntsmen's original way of life in Arcadia before the Gentry arrived - the ancient stories of hunters, predators and prey, where Arcadia was a dark wilderness.

                          As for where the True Fae come from, I've always been fond of the idea that they are rejects, exiles, and refugees from Supernal Arcadia, cast out by the Exarchs and escaping through the Hedge into Arcadia rather than becoming Bound - hence, why they are known as the 'Old Gods of Thistle' by Archmasters. They did not create Arcadia but came to inhabit, conquer, and rule it. That would be the answer I subscribe to in my MtAw and CtL chronicles. As for how the whole 'changelings transforming into True Fae' thing fits into that answer, I have no clue, maybe it is a consequence allowed to exist by merit of the narratives surrounding Ascensions.

                                              Originally posted by orathaic View Post
                                              where did the God-machine come from?
                                              The aggregate magic of the symbolism of systems created by humanity, having reached a critical mass and developed the kind of ruthless pretense to sentience from which the observation comes that a more accurate term than "machine learning" would be "bias automation."

                                              Magic being what it is in this setting, this ostensibly leads to some level of fuckery as concerns its historical origin point, especially as it's gotten its hooks into the laws of physics at some point, but for the most part it's systems of power and perverse incentives and crass optimization running themselves under a supernatural umbrella both durable enough to have admin privileges for a significant fraction of the laws of reality as experienced by the average human and brittle enough that its instruments turn into something else when they refuse to do their jobs.

                                              (It looks alien because the nature of the world is strange and creating designs to exploit that strangeness is bound to have results we don't immediately understand just as surely as any glitch-horror story about video game speedrunning tricks would be terrifying to live through as a normal inhabitant of those worlds.)

                                                  The Contagion is the curses spoken by the last surviving angels from the Pain Prophet's Testament. It is their desire for vengeance against the world incarnated, and is carried with their words.

                                                  The God Machine has no purpose. It was created to achieve something, but the creator either died or left. Without getting any new orders, it tries on keeping the status quo so it could still achieve its mission if and when it would be needed. As the world change, however, it must grow in power and scale in order to balance it and maintain the same status quo. If one were to actually find the operation code (the Black Sun Map), it could finally do what it was made to do, whatever it may be.

                                                  The Chthonic Gods are not singular beings- they have no names or titles. Instead, they are the collective of all ghosts who have drank so deep of the Rivers that they have lost their minds and personalities. This conglomeration of dead matter clings desperately to the world of the living, trying to avoid falling into the Sea of Fragments and to the Lower Depths below. They grasp at the ghosts who are left behind, devouring them and adding them to the mass- they exist in the very walls of the Underworld, screaming in despair. They are humanity's ghosts, dragging it down.

                                                  The Ocean of Time through which the Arisen float while in deathless sleep is actually the Abyss. All possible timelines are contained within it, and the Arisen travel through it according to the will of the Judges. That's why the Shan'iatu waged war against the Ki-En-Gir- their knowledge included the one gained from Duat and A'aru, but they required Abyssal components to truly break through time and make their servants Deathless.

                                                  The Gentry are not from the current universe. They reached from beyond the bubble of light which is the CofDverse and took over the Hedge. The first act they made was to forge pacts with humanity in order to protect them from the Huntsmen, which allowed them to bind the original fae and tame the great forest which was the Hedge into the current Arcadia. By binding the Huntsmen instead of destroying them, the Gentry have created a loophole- they will protect those kidnapped from the Huntsmen, by keeping them as their own playthings.

                                                  The Strix are not real- they are unreal. They are pieces of empty void which lacks light and life, made from the torn heart of a fallen goddess.

                                                  Vampires are a pyramid scheme. Every time the vampire burns Vitae to reanimate, it feeds the corpse god which spawned their Curse, which in turn goes to feed the being which taught them the Curse, and so forth and forth until it reaches the bloody crone which feasts upon the spilled blood of humanity.

                                                  The Principle is the fire surrounding the Empyrean, the act of movement caused by the Unmoved Mover which is the center of the multiverse. It's goal is to move the world towards its own New Dawn, free from the Darkness and its impurities once and for all.

                                                      The Principle Is Spiritual Biophysics
                                                      There is no Empyrean. There isn't a Principle either, not in any meaningful sense.
