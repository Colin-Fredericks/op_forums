    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

    I strongly agree with this, but also wonder if something like Death or Spirit spells with an optional inclusion of Prime and Fate and possibly some extra Reach would make sense as something to allow entering Twilight at that frequency.
    I think Matter or Forces would actually be better than Spirit or Death for interacting with G-M stuff/Twilight.

    As Satchel said, in the end "Twilight Phase" is a mechanical shorthand for different ways of becoming invisible and intangible. Thematically speaking, the God-Machine does that by using strange materials and laws of physics. It has no real connection with other Ephemera except in its capacity to use Essence as fuel, which it arguably has because it can use anything as fuel. At the end of the day, I don't see it as having much connection to Death or Spirit outside specific Projects.

    That would also make for a nice differentiation to set the mood for a Mage game involving the God Machine.

    Originally posted by Sabelforge View Post

    The reason I ask is because the source material for 2E seems to indicate you need Prime and Fate to interact with Demons/Angels/GM stuff. I don't remember seeing any source material stating the use of Death and or Spirit with the above splats.
    We simply have no hard answer.

    In fact, a Mage would probably have to first investigate enough G-M related Mysteries to even be able to find a way to use the appropriate Arcana to interact with it in a more meaningful way, or in their own Twilight. That would be more important, thematically, than whatever specific Arcanum does the job.

    Having the God-Machine operate in a way familiar to any splat, even Mages, goes against its narrative role. The whole theme is that it's based on an understanding of reality beyond what anyone can grasp. If you get it out of the G-M, then there are actually better things to work with.

        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

        It feels like one of those things obscure and/or dangerous enough to necessitate more Reach than safely possible and/or specific Yantras to use, but also the published spells for entering Death and Spirit Twilight don't have "add [Arcanum]" notes like Banishment and Exorcism do to permit consolidating all possible "enter Twilight" variants into a single spell for Rote/Praxis purposes.

        Like, setting aside angelic Twilight being mostly a personal quality in practical terms, "one or more mages have developed access to the phase of existence where the great engine that turns the world hides many of its secrets and routes several of its agents and assets through" is kind of a localized story-hook by virtue of how the Machine operates, and is thus probably better handled as a more involved Mystery beyond the scope of simply saying You Can Do This With These Arcana Ratings; if pressed for a mechanical threshold I would probably model the necessary knowledge to do so as a Merit with potential drawbacks baked in to be developed further.

Angels seem to be the exception here, but that only really matters go Mages, since no ody else if going around Changeling Essence.

                orathaic is talking about Ephemeral Cover because that power allows demons to claim Cover identities as ghosts or spirits. So they end up in ghostly Twilight (Death) or spiritual Twilight (Spirit), not angelic Twilight. This is one of very few demon powers available that can bring them into Twilight. The only other one I can think of is Decoy, which is a form of astral projection.

                Generally speaking, demons rarely enter Twilight and don't enter angelic Twilight at all, and have pretty limited means of even sensing angelic Twilight. It's part of the spy game Demon is playing: angels could be anywhere around you at a given time watching invisibly and you just don't know for sure.

                All of this is assuming God-Machine demons. There are other things called demons unrelated to Demon: the Descent and the God-Machine, like things from the Inferno or from Pandemonium. Inferno demons are pretty likely to be found in common Twilight phases (Death or Spirit, depending).

                        Yes, and sometimes the proper Arcana are beyond the scope of use for characters who aren't operating at the level of plot devices.

                        The highest confirmed Imperial Practice amounts to "For the Duration of this spell, you can do anything relating to the Arcanum it uses within sensory range except for extending the Duration of this spell or giving another iteration of this spell to someone else." It requires a unique Yantra that is as difficult for the character to get with the resources of an archmaster capable of creating Chantries and subsoul as it was for that same character to get the Yantra that allowed them to turn themselves into an archmaster. A hypothetical higher-level version of that spell that is active all the time is one speculated route to Ascension.

                                Originally posted by quelsec View Post
                                Werent mages suposed to do anything they want provided the proper Arcana?
                                Adding to what Satchel said, Mages are also supposed to have proper preparations and costs to do stuff.

                                Contrary to Ascension, in Awakening there are right answers and proper ways of doing things, and a Mage has to find out what it is they need to do what they want, even when within their power.

                                Maybe even more important, though, is that facing the God-Machine is like facing a specially powerful Archmage, just short of Ascension. You're not dealing with a static phenomenon with fixed rules and no understanding of the Supernal, you're dealing with an active entity that virtually understands every Arcanum to its fullest, at least as far as it applies to the Lie, and knew about Mages way before they knew about each other.

                                Then again, the key is to understand the tropes involved. If the God-Machine isn't like that, there are better things to use in a game than the God-Machine. And that isn't hard even if you have the Demons of Descent in play, because as a strange and arbitrary entity the G-M may be absent of an area for no discernible reason.
