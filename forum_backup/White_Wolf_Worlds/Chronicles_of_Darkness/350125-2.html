        Originally posted by ZealousChristian24 View Post
        @Reaper Verse: Neat! Seems like it'd be a fun idea. Not to pry, but did you have any ideas for their Internal and Social Axises?
        No worries. I openly welcome prying of most to any types.

        The Axes did go through a bit of finagling. The main thing is that the idea of Shadow is so vague that there aren't that many archetypes that would work. Since the Shadows were meant to represent counterparts of humanity, I started with the idea of Personae (gee, where did I get THAT word from, hint hint), archetypes that represented types of individuals such as natural leaders or walking comfort blankets and highlighting negative traits that were associated with such archetypes (leaders would be control-freaks or megalomaniacs, etc.) The naming convention was based on the Court Cards of the Tarot, going from Kings, Queens, Knights, and Pages while adding the Ace to bring the total to five (of course, I decided to flower the names up to Warlord, Consort, General, Scholar, and Herald. Why? Mainly for giggles but also to bring the idea back to an Eastern-influenced splat). Eventually, I believe I removed it because it was too...obvious to be used. That, and I didn't want a C&D hanging over my head.

        The Axis that played across it was Conceits, the manner in which Shadows responded to their unique situation and to humanity, their existential counterpart. Because this was back in the stage in which the game was based on the Jungian concept of Shadows and Personas, the Conceits were based on the 5 (technically, I used 7) Stages of Grief.

        SNIPPET TIME GO GO GO
        Conceits are means in which Shadows view the world and what aspect of humanity they are to reflect. The Unaware (Shock) are Shadows that take on the feeling of naivety, the overall complacency to the world and the disdain to change, the inability to respond or adapt to the unknown, the desire for stagnation. The Rejected (Denial) are Shadows that take on the feeling of fear, the aversion towards others, ritualistic self-isolation and self-deprecation, the belief that one is unworthy. The Indignant (Anger) are Shadows that take on the feeling of anger and justice, the righteous hand, the desire to right wrongs and punish those that do not fall into what is right. The Desperate (Bargaining) are Shadows that take on the feeling of powerlessness, the victimization of others and the helplessness that plague them, the belief that there is no way to fight against fate or change oneself. The Guilty (Guilt) are Shadows that take on the feeling of responsibility, a perceived fault, the rejection of selfish thoughts mutated into the desire to give selflessly as to apologize or make amends. The Worthless (Depression) are Shadows that take on the feeling of imperfection, the overall wrongness of oneself and the exaggeration of faults. They mock the perception of importance and follow a sort of Nihilistic belief. The Vainglorious (Acceptance) are Shadows that take on the feeling of superiority, the acceptance of faults to a degree that implies perfection, the utter perversion of pride.
        I feel like keeping this Axis, but shrinking it down to the basic five and expanding what they really are about.

        The Axis that is currently being reworked, I have yet to name; however, I find myself leaning towards Loyalty or Dominion. Basically, the Shadows originate in the feudal Labyrinth under the reign of the 8 (technically, 7) deceased Guardians. Since the Ba Gua has an associated element, organ, and animal, the Guardians were envisioned to be representations of these concepts.

                Long story short, the first idea I presented in this thread has changed quite a bit, though I think it's the better for it. Let me present...

                Viral: the Warped
                The disease in known by many names. Vitus Malus, the Twisting, Jigsaw, and many more by those few aware of it. It lurks in the shadows, spreading by tainted magic, the claws of strange, shadowy predators, and even the innocent touch of unknowing asymptomatics. Most who are infected die, the wrong part warped just the right way to be innocuously fatal. The majority of others are defiled, twisted into hateful, insane Things. The rest survive... in a fashion. Their flesh lives as it did before, even a bit better in fact, but the person who once lived in it is gone. Instead, a monster lives in their skin, a unnatural virus mutated into sapience. You are that Virus.*

                Virus: the Warped is a game of Alienation and Self-Control, as you wander through the world of the Host and attempt to define an identity from the ashes of a fallen life while struggling with a flesh that warps both with and without your whim.


                Mood
                Alienation- Though everyone around you thinks otherwise, you are not human. You are a monster, an alien creature who cannot be truly human, though with much time and effort you may come close. In order to keep control, you must turn inward, keeping yourself under a leash in order to survive. In the end, you are alone, no matter how hard you try. You're the lonely man surrounded by friends, the silent screamer in a deaf crowd.


                Theme
                Holding it together- It can be so easy to let go, to fail to maintain one's Coherency and become some Thing. The Warped must be cautious, for overuse of their greatest strengths will destroy them utterly. You have so little to gain, but so much to lose...


                INHERENT AXIS
                Vector-How you infected your flesh and your natural inclinations.

                •The Savage-Infection through beasts and other natural things.*

                •The Tainted- Infection through defiled geometries, foul food, and other things made made wrong.

                •The Given-Infection through blood, tears, and other thing of man.

                •???-

                EXTERNAL AXIS
                Treatment-How you deal with your nature.
                •"The Humanists"- Seek advantage through false humanity.

                •"The Prognosticators"- Seek to understand the disease and other strange things of the shadows.*

                •"The Contained"-Seek to master themselves and the world around them.
                •???-

                Z-Axis
                Strains- Viruses joined by common traits, both of form and of self. May be "born" into or learned.
                EX:
                •The McCarthy Syndrome- Memetic agents of discord.

                •The Romero Plague- Death-Touched masters of undeath.

                •The Techne Hubris- A Transhuman's nightmare, formed of a wicked mix of cell and circuit.*

                •The Immunization Infection- Wound-mending guardians and farmers of the Host.


                SUPERNATURAL ADVANTAGE
                Morphism- How mutable and fluid their form is, and how in tune they are with their alien nature.


                FUEL

                •Misama-The energy of disease, born of that which infects.


                INTEGRITY STAT
                Coherency- How much the Warped controls their form, how easy it is to appear human, weakened by forsaking humanity or using Vicissitudes too often. At 0, result is Chimaera, also known as Things, who twist from second to second with feral violence.

                VIRTUE/VICE
                •Benignity- Positive influence, compulsion to stability and mankind.
                •Malignancy- Negative influence, compulsion to mutation and monstrousness.


                POWERS
                Two sets of powers, one obvious and one not.
                •Transmissions- The metaphysical side of the virus, subtle influences and minor gifts. Use is harmless to Virus.
                •Vicissitudes- The physical side of the virus, blatant powers and potent mutations. Very obvious in use, and damages Coherency.

                MINOR TEMPLATE
                •Asymptomatics- Humans immune to the virus by some means. They are immune to the power of Transmissions, and may learn Transmissions that are used to try and affect them.

                                            After playing way too much Majora's Mask, it inspired me/maddened me.

                                            Shaman: the Masked
                                            Since ancient times, masks have held a certain power. They hide your identity, free your inhibitions, they assuage your fear. In many cultures, masks hold even greater power: speaking to animals and the dead, commanding the forces of nature, and preserving the memories of ancestors.

                                            Mood: Reverence for Nature: The powers of the Shaman are not their own, coming from forces that they don't understand. As a result, they must tread cautiously.

                                            Theme: Becoming the Mask; Those who constantly wear masks must be careful that they don't lose themselves. This is especially true when the mask you're wearing is overflowing with supernatural powers.

                                            Inherent Axis: Sympathy; the part of the World Soul that called out to you in the first place. Determines what type of powers your first Mask is allowed to have, as well as what kinds of Masks you can most easily make.

                                            Social Axis: Tradition; The cultural tradition your Masks descend from. You don't necessarily have to come from that culture, but you have to honestly and seriously draw inspiration from it, although putting an unusual twist on it is common in modern times.

                                            Power Stat: Instead of a singular Power Stat, Masquers have two stats: Animus, and the Power of their Masks, each of which is capped at 5. Animus represents the Shaman's connection to the World Soul, and how strongly their will is attuned to it. A Mask's Power represents the potency and variety of effects it can achieve.

                                            Fuel: Noctis; The energy of the World Soul, Noctis is found in all places, but can only be harnessed in certain by those sensitive to the World Soul, such as Shamans.

                                            Integrity: Detachment; Instead of a singular Integrity stat, each Mask has its own level of Detachment, representing how separate the Shaman is from the persona of the Mask. Low Detachment causes a Mask's Compulsions to remain for longer, but it also makes using that Mask's power easier. Conversely, a high Detachment allows you to more easily resist a Mask's Compulsion, but makes its powers less reliable.

                                            Virtue/Vice: The character retains their normal Virtue and Vice, but each Mask has its own Compulsion, which acts similarly. Each Compulsion is unique to the Mask, and can offer both Noctis and Willpower when acted upon.

                                            Powers: The powers of the Shaman come from their Masks. Each Mask falls under one of X loose groupings, which determines what type of powers available to it. Mechanically, the powers of Masks are rather narrow, being attuned to a specific subset of the wider category they fall in, but their level of control is relatively high as a result.
                                            • Beast: Masks of Beasts allow communication and influence over their subjects. (Panthers, Dogs, Sharks)
                                            • Celestial: The stars and planets help to weave fate on your behalf. (Mars as Warrior, Sirius for Wealth)
                                            • Dream: The most direct connection to the World Soul, these masks touch a part of the Astral. (Entering an Oneros, evoking emotions, warding off nightmares)
                                            • Elements: The elements of nature heed the call of Masks of Elements. (Smoke, Winds, Thunder)
                                            • Funeral: Funeral Masks commemorate the dead and call upon ghosts. (using the skills of a dead ancestor, repelling evil ghosts)
                                            Minor Template: Dreamwalkers; Individuals who are innately connected to the World Soul, but without the ability to form that connection into a Mask. They have difficulty keeping themselves distinct from their internalized "Mask", but they have an intuitive control over their powers.
