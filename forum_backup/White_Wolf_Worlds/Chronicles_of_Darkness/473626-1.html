    The metaplot gave the game a reason for going forward, and brought things to a nice little close. But nWoD is sand boxing it, and gives us the chance to write our own major story lines. :P

            I think part of the reason a lot of people like the old World of Darkness, aside from it being something they're just used to, is that it tells a story. You can see the various metaplot threads scattered around, and people like connecting the dots or developing favorite characters based on their actions. Obviously not everyone loved it, as one of the complaints I see from fans of the older game is that NPCs often overshadowed characters, so rather than being part of a story they felt like a story was being told over their characters, or did things similarly built characters would find impossible to do. But these are just things I've noticed or heard from people.

            With new World of Darkness, a lot of people seem to think it's just a bare-bones rules system. But the lack of an expansive metaplot isn't the same as a lack of a setting. We obviously have developed NPCs and cities and bloodlines, and characters or events that span into history. But it lacks the feel of a big comprehensive story, and outside of a few, NPCs rarely get the spotlight.

            I always thought they should do more with the signature characters.

                                                            PDF is a file format, they're downloadable versions you can put on your computer and move to any computer. Providing you have an account with DrivethruRPG, they'll let you download it again and again if you remove it from your computer. POD means print on demand, its how they make Onyx Path books now, outside of the Deluxe and Premium editions from Kickstarter. Essentially you buy a print book from DrivethruRPG, they use their own printer and make the book, then send it to you.

                                                            It's surprisingly quick, but apparently I'm nearby or something, delivery has never been very long for me and I understand its been a while for others.
