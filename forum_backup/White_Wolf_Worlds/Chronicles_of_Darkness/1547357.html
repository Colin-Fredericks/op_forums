    Tamers of Blood Homebrew Legacy

    12-30-2024, 11:58 PM
    Tamers of Blood
    Hemomancers

    Human cultures throughout history and across the globe know of the power of Blood. Origin stories of humanity commonly involve us springing forth from the blood of gods. Aztecs believed blood powered the sun, and European peasants would gather with cups at executions in order to get some of the criminal's blood, drinking it to gain some of the dead’s strength. Catholic doctrine says that the wine they drink during communion is the blood of Christ. Blood brothers would cut open their bodies and rub the wounds against each other in order to mix their blood, and kings and queens throughout history invoked the same symbol when they would mix, or refuse to mix, their bloodline with another family's.

    All of these examples demonstrate the Truth of Blood as the Elemental Masteries know it. Blood is Life. Blood is Strength. Blood is the Self. Blood is Connection. The Tamers of Blood demonstrate this Truth through the use of blood to strengthen, weaken, heal and harm the body. They use blood to cast sympathetic magic on their subjects, or to increase the potency of their magic.

    In the modern world the Tamers of Blood find themselves in a difficult position. Their numbers across the globe are extremely low, and the legacy going extinct is a very real fear for them. Where they do exist, their ability to control others bodies and form power sympathetic connections with others with just the subjects blood cause fear of the legacy to spread through the consillia they do exist in, threatening that they will soon be deemed left-handed. Still, the legacy members are all Space mages, so finding each other is quite possible for those Tamers who wish to. Despite their low numbers and potential adversity coming from the diamond orders, Tamers of Blood are still part of the Elemental Masters, and work to integrate themselves into sleeper society, filling roles as healers and peace brokers in order to keep and strengthen the bonds of the community.

    Background: Mages drawn to the Tamers of Blood tend to come from backgrounds where blood was important to their work. Doctors, forensic scientists, detectives who see plenty of murder, but also more dubious backgrounds, like serial killers or cannibals.

    Appearance: When looking for the dramatic flare that some mages enjoy employing, the Tamers of Blood dress in crimson or scarlet robes. However, the majority dress in a way that is appropriate for the role in sleeper society that they take.

    Organization: When the Tamers of Blood find each other they organize themselves based on the number of attainments learned, the more attainments grants the mage more authority. That said, they all understand that, because they are all blood, they are all connected. Tamers of Blood tend to view each other as siblings in their connection to Elemental Blood.

    Theory: Blood is life. Blood is the body. Blood is the self. Blood is a connective power. By mastering the attainments of the legacy and becoming Elemental Blood, the mage becomes life and community, gains control over the body, masters the self, and insights into sympathetic magic.

    Doctrine and Magic

    Parent Path: Thyrsus
    Parent Order: Any
    Prerequisites: Space 2, Life 1, Medicine 2, one of the following additional skills at 2 dots or higher: Athletics, Empathy, Expression, Occult, or Science
    Initiation: The mage enters moving water, a river or the ocean usually, and cuts themself, bleeding into it. As they bleed out they heal themselves, only to cut themselves again. As they do this they focus not on the pain, or the water, but on their blood, flowing out from them, their body, life and connection touching the shoreline, the stones in the water, and sand of the beach.
    Ruling Arcana: Space
    Yantras: Having an ounce of the subject's blood (+2); Tools used from drawing blood (+1); A sympathetic yantra in non-sympathetic spells (varies, based on the strength of the sympathetic yantra)
    Oblations: Drawing Blood; Consuming Blood; Work towards the strengthening of a sympathetic connection without the use of magic

    Attainments

    First Attainment: Seeing the Self

    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The teachings of the Elemental Masters and the shaping of a mage’s gnosis to the Truth of the legacy results in the mage becoming their chosen element, for the Tamers of Blood, this allows them to notice themselves in the world. After an hour of ritual this attainment activates, allowing the mage to see any blood in the world, similar to the “Locate Object” (Space 1, MtAw 173) with reach for advanced duration, except that it grants the mage sight of all blood that is not within a body, no matter how small the sample is.

    Optional: Life 1
    Blood carries information on its host, and the Tamer can use the blood to gain this information. The mage must spend an hour of ritual to activate this attainment, which functions as “Analyze Life” (Life 1, MtAw 148) with reach assigned to learn the specific dot ratings of each physical attribute. This attainment can be activated on a subject that is willing to participate in the ritual, however an ounce of blood will work the same, allowing the Tamer to gain information from the blood although with a lag. The attainment does not learn any changes that have happened since the blood left the body, any increases in attributes, illness caught, etc. is not discovered.

    Second Attainment: Blood is the Self

    Prerequisites: Space 2, 3 dots in Medicine
    Space mages say we are our connections, and the Tamers of Blood say that our blood is us, their first attainment demonstrates this Truth. By using blood taken from a subject, the mage can learn about other sympathetic links the subject has. After an hour of ritual, the mage may learn a number of sympathetic connections equal to their Space dots, as if casting the spell “Correspondence” (Space 1, MtAw 172) with two reach assigned to seek out specific sympathetic connections. The subject need not be present for this attainment to function, having an ounce of their blood is as good as having them right in front of the Tamer.

    Optional: Life 2
    Control over blood is control over the body. With this attainment, the Tamer of Blood fine tunes their bodily control as if under the effects of “Body Control” (Life 2, MtAw 148) with reach for advanced duration. It takes one hour of meditation to activate this attainment.

    Third Attainment: Blood is Body

    Prerequisites: Space 3, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 3 or a second option from the list must be raised to 2
    Blood carries with it sympathy to the subject it was drawn from, after all, we are our blood. This attainment allows for the mage to borrow these sympathies for themself. The attainment functions like “Borrow Threads” (Space 2, MtAw 174) with reach assigned for advanced duration, and to change the primary factor to potency. It takes an hour to activate, but unlike previous attainments, the amount of blood is not important to this attainment, instead it is the freshness of the blood that needs to be considered, the fresher the blood, the stronger the sympathy.

    Optional: Life 3
    While this attainment has no effect on supernatural entities, and therefore mages are immune to its effects, this is where fear towards the Tamers of Blood can begin. This attainment allows the mage to control the instincts of sleepers and sleepwalkers, functioning like “Control Instincts” (Life 2, MtAw 149) with reach for advanced duration and sympathetic casting. It takes an hour to activate, and costs a mana. The Tamer must use the subject's blood as their sympathetic yantra that “Sympathetic Range” (MtAw 193) calls for.

    Fourth Attainment: Blood is Life

    Prerequisites: Space 4, 4 dots in Medicine
    While Blood is a powerful sympathetic yantra, time does degrade this connection. This attainment allows for the Tamer to strengthen connections to Blood that has weakened over time, it functions like “Web-Weaver” (Space 3, MtAw 177) with reach to change the primary spell factor to potency, and for advanced duration. The ritual takes one hour to complete.

    Optional: Life 4
    Blood is the body, and with this attainment the Tamers of Blood can use their mastery of their element to alter the body. Two versions of this attainment exist. Each one takes one hour to complete and costs a mana. Each has reach for advanced duration, and to use sympathetic range in the casting, using the subject's blood as a sympathetic yantra. The difference in attainments comes down to the role the Tamer wishes to take. Those who wish to use their magic to strengthen their community learn an attainment that functions like “Honing the Form” (Life 3, MtAw 150), while those who seek personal power, or to weaken their enemies, learn an attainment that functions like “Degrading the Form” (Life 3, MtAw 150). While it is unclear which attainment is learned more, the second is much more famous, adding to the legacies poor reputation, and one of the reasons many mages wish to see it labeled as a left-handed legacy.

    Fifth Attainment: Blood is Connection

    Prerequisites: Space 5, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 4 or a second option from the list must be raised to 3 or a third option must be raised to 2
    By this point in their journey, the Tamer has fully become Blood, and as such, they are able to fully realize the Truth of Blood, they can create sympathy when there was none. This attainment functions as the spell “Create Sympathy” (Space 5, MtAw 178) with reach for advanced duration, and it takes one hour to complete.

    Optional: Life 5
    Like the previous Life attainment, this one has two options, one meant to reinforce community and connection, and one meant to harm the enemies of the Tamer. Like the previous attainment, the harmful version is more well known, and contributes greatly to arguments that the legacy should be considered left-handed. The harmful version of this spell takes one hour to activate, and costs two mana to do so. It functions as “Life-Force Assault” (Life 4, MtAw 152) with reach to cast sympathetically, and to cause aggravated damage to its subject. The Mage needs blood as a sympathetic yantra. The helpful version of this spell only costs one mana to activate, but still the hour of ritual and the subject's blood. With this attainment the mage can heal their allies no matter where they are in the world, functioning like “Knit” (Life 3, MtAw 150) with reach assigned for heal lethal damage, heal personal tilts, and to cast sympathetically.

      I have made the Tamers of Blood, a legacy I've wanted to make for a while now because I thought they were pretty cool, but as I'll be playing one in a game soon, I figured, lets just make the whole thing.

      I've done a few things here;
      1. I tried to stay consistent with what we've been told about the legacy in both Left Hand Path (page 17) and Legacies - The Ancient (page 27), which said they can create symapthy, control the body, and might soon be labled left-handed.
      2. I tried avoiding making an exact copy of Hermit's attempt at them, although in some places that couldn't be helped.
      3. I liked the idea of these rituals involving blood, things that take time, so I made no attainments that were activated instantly for this.
      4. I tried making something that was both within the rules of legacies, and that I would enjoy playing, but still had a good "viber" to it
      5. and lastely; I wanted to draw a little from the Tamers of Rivers, who I can imagine were related to the Tamers of Blood long, long ago. but still keep them distinct. You can see this in some of their Life attainments, but also I took inspiration for their initiation from the Tamers of Rivers.
      It was, honestly, exhausting to make, sympathetic range makes legacy attainments a little hard to work with, because you need to delay where you'd but them until later, so the early attainments were actually more difficult than the later.
