                Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
                It says that you can do the vitae over several turns to make it count as 1 sip.

                "This Vitae must be shared over no more than a few minutes, but is not subject to limits on Vitae per turn." Voivode 3 is not subject to vitae/turn limits. So, you can have BP 1 and use that much vitae in a single turn. Blood is a vector for the mystical force of vitae, but there's no guidelines on how much blood can contain how much vitae. A single drop could very well contain 5 vitae worth, especially when we're talking about Dragons.

                You can have a long drink that lasts a few minutes, or a short, quick gulp. It all depends on what is currently appropriate from a story perspective.

                                                    Blood bearing Vitae is slightly darker, and has an almost
                                                    syrupy quality. It has all the normal metallic smells blood
                                                    should, but also has a subtle, sweet smell, like a dessert wine.
                                                    Vitae is life. Life tastes very, very good. In fact, it tastes so
                                                    good, it’s highly addictive to both mortal and Kindred alike.
                                                    pg 98 and 99.

                                                    So it doesn't taste like blood, but it smells mostly like blood, and it looks a whole lot like blood, and no matter what it decidedly does not taste or smell or look like whatever you put it into.

                                                    And since someone brought it up:

                                                    Cold blood from a corpse or kept outside the body for
                                                    more than a few minutes (but no more than a night)
                                                    Stored Kindred Vitae is effectively cold blood.
                                                    pg 95.

                                                    I'm one of the folks that thinks you probably can't dose food or drink with it in the first place without it losing its potency. Admittedly, they don't fully define "stored" here, so it's up to ST interpretation. But there are easier ways, anyway. Obfuscate yourself and wait for the target to go to sleep. Pour it directly into their mouth. Majesty or Dominate them into drinking it. Force them if you're a physical sort of vampire. Get creative.
