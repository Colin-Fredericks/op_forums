They are a luxury, a fun pasttime. I have no right to z. I am not entitled to z. I am, however, in the lucky position that someone likes creating something that I like (and somehow, manages to get by on that). And personally? I'd rather wait for something done right, than be dissatisfied with something rushed.
                                I understand this is not the only position one can have, and I understand listening to the fans is a valid concern etc. I do not mean to come across condescending, but I needed to get this off my chest. And just to beat the dead horse, as atamajakki already said, Werewolf is in a pretty good state compared to people that wait for a 2ed Core, like us Geist fans. And we in turn are still better off than the Mummy fans...
                                I for one adore Dark Eras, and think they are a wonderful supplement. For every line.

                                I wholeheartedly agree with the statement that there should be more information on antagonists, though. Because stuff like that is always helpful, and nice, and interesting. There is some pretty good homebrew to bridge the time, though.
