                Eugh. Sorry for not being up to date on the thread. School. And also I came down with a weird cold (I don't usually get sick, but when I do, it's AWFUL).

                It makes sense that Sirens would be able to spend Pneuma to raise their Social Attributes considering that Sirens are, in and off themselves, a very social Splat.

                As for the Vaporians, I feel that the write-up is fairly poignant to what I intended Vaporians to be but the story characterizes them more towards the Discordians. The Vaporians are long-time thinkers, playing key parts in a story before disappearing to contemplate. They are the Lady of the Lake offering the sword to Arthur. What they do is calculated, always built from the bottom up but often strange and arbitrary. Why does the man throw out food into the water every Sunday morning? People may try to understand, but only he knows the reason why. He needs to train the fish to come up to the water every Sunday morning so that a fisherman may land the perfect catch in the upcoming week. That fisherman plays an important part in the overall story. Vaporians make contact in secrecy, allowing others to think that what happens is just luck and fate.

                I think Vaporians would be the "Thieves" of the Sirens, disappearing for extended times to accomplish what they need to do in secrecy. No words, no questions. They stand apart from the Tropicans who want to take the limelight and the Freshwater who want to enjoy life. The Vaporians are kinda a foil to the Gardenians; both have a "job" that they need to do but they go about their "jobs" differently, Gardenians need to stay in one place to maintain things while Vaporians move around to make sure that things get started.

                I feel that Vaporians would also look the most drab out of all the Sirens. Polarians are dark and morose, Freshwaters are bubbly and full of life, Gardenians are strong and solid, Tropicans are bright and vibrant, and Discordians are entirely kooky and clashy. The Vaporians would be drowned out and muted, grey and pastels that have gone through the wash one too many times. Something that won't really catch your eye unless you are looking right at it.

                A possible source of inspiration may be Marie from Persona 4 Golden. She's kinda super spoilery, but the main thing is that she has amnesia and feels that she should search for her past. She's cold and antisocial but has moments of profound introversion and plays a very important part in the plot. She also advises the protagonist to forget about looking for the truth and stop meddling in her affairs. For spoilers, see below.

                The main reason why I feel that she fits the Vaporians is because she is actually an avatar of Kusumi-no-Okami, a spy who watches the real world and is meant to absorb the fog if Ameno-Sagiri, a being who wants to give into humanity's desires to end their suffering and turn them into Shadows, is defeated. Once she realizes who she really is, she attempts to die by herself in order to protect humanity. She is also able to manipulate the weather.

                                I can see the Current of Conflict handling drama but I can also feel them working to break stalemates, pushing people along to work harder to accomplish what they think is right. Freshwaters of Conflict handle interpersonal conflicts as an impartial third while Tropicans of Conflict play a dangerous power game at board meetings and such. Gardenians of Conflict are forceful individuals that attempt to prevent harmful changes to the natural cycle. Polarians of Conflict encourage others to fight to the bitter end (Cold War jokes engage) until only one is left standing while Vaporians of Conflict plant seeds of doubt to make sure that people want to challenge institutions and big ideas. Freshwaters of Wisdom would definitely play the mentor or possibly even a caregiver for young children, watching over them and making sure they don't get into too much trouble while imparting what knowledge they have. Tropicans of Wisdom would be like a godfather, someone who exudes experience and knowledge behind a hardened facade; an intimidating figure due to their reputation but willing to share what they know. Gardenians of Wisdom would be custodians and librarians, archivists who wants to make sure that there is something to be passed down. Polarians of Wisdom find wisdom in death, looking to the failings of others as warnings to those still alive. Vaporians of Wisdom would be messengers and codebreakers, passing notes and letting others in on a secret. Discordians of Wisdom would be the poster child for "Manic Pixie Dream Girl", wandering into someone's life in order to deliver some ground-breaking knowledge while doing something utterly incomprehensible. The Current of Wisdom is the underlying message, the overall theme that the playwright has constructed. The Current of Dark Wisdom turns this into a harmful message that has been censored but is attempting to be unveiled.

Gardenians of Harmony maintain relationships between households and between aspects of nature, raising families or developing the cycles in nature. Polarians of Harmony, in a stark contrast, would focus more on ending toxic relationships for the better of the world. Vaporians of Harmony build and rebuild connections, mending broken ties or searching for missing persons. Discordians of Harmony ignore all social restrictions, willing to extend a helping hand out to the most unlikely individuals. The Current of Harmony would be the barkers and advertisers asking their audience to come and enjoy the show. The Current of Mankind's Victory is a perversion of this, bending down to the will of humanity in order to please them at the expense of the world.

Gardenians of Healing are intimately aware of the healing process and will allow that process to occur, even if it means meddling in someone's affairs. Polarians of Healing prefer to rip off the bandage in one stroke, reveling in pain and cathartic healing. Vaporians of Healing do not ask questions, allowing others to grieve without a word as to why. Discordians of Healing find solstace in jokes and distractions, serving as a sort of numbing agent for the wounded. The Current of Healing is a tragedy that offers a sense of closure at the end. The Current of Primordial Darkness does not find closure, calling upon the forces of nature in order to find something that allows them to heal.

Freshwaters of Buoyancy live to give and get favors, meeting up with questionable people and making connections that are best kept a secret. Tropicans of Buoyancy gladly play the villain or rival to humanity, pushing others to put their 100% through a sense of competition. Gardenians of Buoyancy play a secret game behind the scenes, maintaining the cycle by allowing others to rise to power when their time is due and lower them down when they overstay their welcome. Polarians of Buoyancy are effectively executioners in a sense, ending a relationship or making sure someone is unable to arrive on time in order to stop events from occurring.                                                     Originally posted by SaulottheGentle View Post

The only thing I have to work with at the moment is the Atlantic Ocean, their "original" version so to speak.
                                                    1. Atlantic: Ocean of Might. This ocean is associated with the "noble ocean warrior" ideal of Sirens, though many tempestuous members are just as happy with a rusty harpoon, a sawed-off shotgun and an excuse as with a golden sword. They have an affinity for the Streams of Rusted Iron and Dueling Rites.
                                                    As well, I wonder. With the X splats nearing their end of development, what shall we do next? The Y Splats? Mechanics for Alteration? Perhaps ideas for the Z Splats or what they should be like?
                                                    I was thinking that Discordian Sirens are a personification of whirlpools, tempests, and tidal waves. They are incredibly volatile, switching from one extreme to the next. One moment, they will be smiling, joyous in their life. Another, they have overturned tables and torn posters off the wall. In another, they quietly brood, sipping from their tea as they contemplate the horrors of The Deluge. In yet another, they mentally exit the world, going off on their own little journey to spread knowledge to humanity. They have been polluted, either by the blood of others filling the ocean or by swimming through seas of oil and rust. They are inconsistent, touched by the ages and maddened by revelations. However, they carry a spark of clarity that shines brightly through their antics. A mumbling madwoman torches a building down, taking the serial killer hiding within it. An eccentric socialite invests their time and money in political underdogs, hoping to overturn the corruption that is abound.

                                                    The main word would be "rebellion". Discordians are the jester of the court, a being who is considered a non-threat due to their madness but still stands close enough to the crowns to have an ear in the important discussions. They see each Deluge as a chance to try again, like a reset in a game. They are mad with possibilities, the Fool who journeys through the world in order to learn and Ascend. They are the mad oracle, the ones who hear an answer, every answer, but seek to put the answers to a question. "This man must live." But why? "Run to the forest and wait for it." What am I waiting for? "These people are your destiny. Meet with them." Who are they? Discordians may never ask, but they will know.

                                                    In terms of theatre, Discordians are the acts during the intermission. They exist somewhere outside of the grand scheme but can hint to the audience things that are coming up or give them a quick synopsis to catch up. With the other Waters, a Discordian is considered to be many things. The Tropicans think of Discordians as cute little madmen, keeping them on a short leash before unleashing them on someone they don't like; it's a sort of condescending pity. The Polarians are very much wary of the Discordians, aware of their mad insight; they give them a wide berth as they contemplate how to work their plans around a screwy little squirrel. The Vaporians like the Discordians to an extent, happy that the Discordians are willing to work for change but worried whenever there is a disaster that needs to be cleaned up. The Gardenians, in contrast, are partially terrified by the Discordians and partially appreciative; their rebellion calls for the end and rebirth of cycles. The Freshwaters are genuinely saddened by Discordians, believing their madness to be an unfortunate side-effect of the Deluge.

                                                    The Discordians themselves consider their brothers and sisters to be, in few words, too caught up in their role to truly understand their madness. The Discordians know and see with true clarity. That is why some treat the world as a comedy, laughing mad. That's why some treat the world as a tragedy, reduced to hysteria. What Discordians Know with a capital letter is that You Don't See the Depths from the Surface. That is to say, the truth is not always on the surface of things, nor is it easy to find. There are many layers to the truth, depending on perspective. For example, the Discordians are insane. That statement is true to an extent. Discordians are also intensely aware of the world. That is also a truth. It is also a lie, as with the previous statement. Discordians will let themselves sink if it means that they can see the Depths. Discordians also Know that The Gears of the World Need a Push. Discordians are not exactly subtle. Neither are rebellions. A Discordian knows that the Deluge is coming and they will see to it that it stops, even if it means stopping catastrophes at their youngest without explanation.

                                                    As for the next thing in development, we could probably go into the Y-Splat or the Verses. Something to round off character creation and playability.
