    Combat example and other beginner questions

    09-06-2016, 02:25 AM
    As I am fairly new to CoD and plan to start my first session, I wanted to ask if there is an elaborated example for combat? The rulebook lacks a couple of example to several topics.

    In addition (don't want to appear greedy) is there a cheat sheet for CoD? There are plenty for the older editions but I didn't find one for the actual one.

    Also I'd like to focus on "human" non-monster PCs with a lot of freedom in the setting. My plan is to use the MMO The Secret World as a setting for my group. Which material would you advise? Maybe a tad Mage as the heroes might utilize some magic? Can chars created with the CoD core books "awake" at a later time?
    It revolves around secret societies like the Templar or the illuminati. Would those "conspiracies" mentioned in Hunter the Vigil qualify for those secret societies?

    I would also like to include a heavy lovecraftian touch into my game. Are there sources/material helping to generate such creatures like deep ones, shoggoths and the like? Are they included in one of the setting books?

    Thanks in advance!

      I don't actually have much experience with actual play of the game so I can't be of much help with a combat example/cheat sheet. But when it comes to running a game like that, the Chronicles of Darkness book covers most of what you would need, though Second Sight can also be useful.

      Using just the core book can represent the magic players use through eventually giving them supernatural merits or allow them to "craft" magical effects using the crafting rules to make wards, trickets and such. Second Sight is a book focused around mortals with a bit of magical power through different types of traditions, though many mechanical aspects of the book have been put into the Chronicles of Darkness book in the form of the previously mentioned Supernatural merits.

      For conspiracies you can mechanically represent them as mystery cults, and you gain benefits in them as you take the mystery cult initiation merit (and you can say that players cannot take supernatural merits until they gain a supernatural merit from their mystery cult). Though some of he conspiracies mentioned in Hunter could work as secret societies that you desire, though they have more specific flavour and aren't "rule the world from the shadows" like the illuminati/templars.

      For putting in lovecraftian abominations, Chronicles of Darkness has rules for making your own monsters referred to as "Horrors", which should allow you to create Deep Ones, Shoggoths, Ghouls, Mi-Go, etc. without much trouble.

                                  As a Secret World fan myself, I can say that Templars works as London Adamantine Arrows, Illuminati as New York Mysterium ( with a bit Guardians of Veil ) and Dragons as Silver Ladder ( I guess? ) from Mage. Still, game is evenly in middle between Hunter and Mage power level, but if you want to emulate more 'low power' feel, I would go with Hunter. Not going with Mage Orders, I would work on Conspiracies as Mystery Cult Initiation groups, with special powers on their own - canon Hunter groups like Malleus Maleficarum or Cherion Group each have their own 'magic' with variety of effects, enough for 'secret society monster hunters' idea.

                                  Originally posted by Belisarion View Post
