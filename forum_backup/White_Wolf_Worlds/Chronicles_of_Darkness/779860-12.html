    Quick review of the Currents. So far, the Waters define the physical traits and a general mindset that the Sirens of that particular Water subscribe to. Polarian Sirens are colder (literally and figuratively) and are more morose and cynical as compared to the others while Freshwater Sirens are optimistic and are more willing to interact with humanity. The Waters are both a body type and a manner of reacting to the world. With Currents, they are the driving mentality and the ways in which a Siren wants to change the world.


    The Current of Conflict
    "Do you even care? If something is worth fighting for, you need to fight for it. Don't be the person standing on the outside and waiting for it to come. Be the force of change. Stand for what you believe in. If you want things to change, you must be willing to make them change." -A Gardenian of Conflict

    "No. It is not over. Get up. Take a breath. You cannot allow yourself to give up. This cannot be how this ends" -A Polarian of Conflict

    "C'mon, you're really going to leave things alone? You're just gonna let them win without a struggle? Wow, what a shame. And here I thought you were a scrapper. Guess we can't all be as great as we want to be. Oh, you're not giving up now? You're gonna make a stand? Heh, I knew I saw something in you." -A Discordian of Conflict

    The sea is a fickle being. On some days, it is calm and gentle, like a drop of dew falling into a puddle. On others, it rages and storms, battering the ships within it with crashing waves and great winds. Some ships have been taken by the oceans, overwhelmed by the depths. The ones who are willing to fight against the current, willing to go against what they know to be a great force, are allowed to continue onward and make their journey. Sirens who follow the Current of Conflict believe in the fight against idleness; they want others to take charge in what they believe in rather than waiting for someone else to take the mantle. If a Siren of Conflict isn't rallying a crowd, she is most-likely going to be in the thick of the fight, clearing a path for others to travel. Sirens of Conflict are the vanguard of the Sirens, willing to take the first step towards fighting off The Deluge.


    The Current of Harmony
    "Hold my hand. Can you feel the warmth from my body? I'm here for you and I'll always be here for you. You don't have to be afraid to reach out anymore." -A Tropican of Harmony

    "We can do whatever you want for the day. I know you're not a big fan of going out, but we've been missing you lately. You're one of us and we want you to feel like it. This is gonna be a day for you, our treat." -A Freshwater of Harmony

    "Your mother misses you. You haven't called her ever since you moved out here. I know it's not my business, but I think you miss her too. Here, I took a message from her. You can decide to call if you want." -A Vaporian of Harmony

    A young child reaches their hands into a river and partakes of the life-giving nectar that runs from one end of a country to the other. It is cold, but refreshing, like a quiet respite from the world around them. How many other children have experienced the same feeling? How many adults? How about the wild creatures that treat it as a sanctuary? Or the stretch of fertile land that allows great trees to rise up? Sirens who follow the Current of Harmony believe in the power of connections and the unity of the world around then; the strength of one is only as powerful as what it reaches out into. For change to happen, everyone must be aware, or at least on the same page. A Siren of Harmony is someone who wants to make others aware of the world around them, resonating on a single note in the world.


    The Current of Healing
    "Go ahead. Scream. Cry. Let it all out. It's killing you on the inside, so you deserve it. Don't worry. I won't judge you." -A Vaporian of Healing

    "Hey. Look, I know you're going through a bad break-up and you want some time to yourself. That's fine. However, no one has seen you in the last few weeks, so you have two options. One, you unlock the door so we can have a talk. Or two, I drop-kick this door and take you out for drinks. What'll it be?" -A Gardenian of Healing

    "Look at you, you little trooper. Don't worry, everything will be fine. You made it this far, so you'll be able to keep going on. I know you're going to be okay." -A Freshwater of Healing

    Water cleanses filth, whether it is dirt on clothes or the sins of the remorseful. Whether it is a daily ablution or a baptism, water is an agent that washes away the stains of life. Even rain, one of the most innocuous manifestations of water, represents a moment of peace and clarity, washing away rage or suffering. No stain too deep to clean. No sin too severe to forgive. No fire too hot to douse. The water will come. Sirens who follow the Current of Healing believe in rehabilitation and second chances; everyone deserves a moment to recover or a chance for redemption. To give up on something is to abandon it, believing it unable to change. Are second chances not an opportunity to change? Sirens of Healing are nothing if not patient and forgiving, willing to allow someone to feel the release of peace when the healing process is over.


    The Current of Wisdom
    "History is always cursed to repeat itself. Do not dwell on things that you cannot change. Learn from them." -A Polarian of Wisdom

    "You can't just worry about the future when you aren't experiencing it. Come with me. I think there are some things you need to see." -A Tropican of Wisdom

    "Life lessons? Me? Sure. Don't keep the lights on in your car when you leave it. Fabric sheets are a good substitute for deodorant. What else...oh! Don't think that anyone knows what's good for you other than yourself. Unless they're a professional and are talking about things that you don't know better." -A Discordian of Wisdom

    Along winding rivers that link cities, through great seas that separate countries, past the oceans that divide the continents is the unknown. How far must one travel down the current before they see a world unlike that of their own? How much can change when humans cannot communicate through the great waters? Expanding one's horizons is to travel beyond borders and seeking out the unknown. Sirens who follow the Current of Wisdom believe in expanding viewpoints and questioning the world; not everything is clear as day and not everything can be learned by simply knowing of them. A river may run for a set amount of miles and may be a set distance deep, but how far does the water actually run? What does the water mean? Sirens of Wisdom want answers from the world and want to share those answers to others, even if each answer seems to contradict one another.


    The Current of Buoyancy
    "Hey, have you ever wanted to do something crazy?" -A Discordian of Buoyancy

    "I can't really allow you to get away with this. Of course, I could always just say that I was busy with work and just happened to leave this lying around. It would also be a shame if someone were to take it and use it for their own good. But, of course, that would be just such a terrible thing to think of." -A Freshwater of Buoyancy

    "It looks like they're slacking off. Too bad, looks like there's nothing to stop us from going in and messing with their information. Are you in?" -A Vaporian of Buoyancy

    Water is not a force that will take being used so lightly. Waves will erode stone, rain will pierce through roofs. It will bend along curves and fill depressions with its presence, molding itself to each and every situation brought upon it. Water may eventually be forced off-course or pressured in a way it cannot overcome, but it will continue to flow and adapt as it exerts its own force against those that attempt to control it. Sirens who follow the Current of Buoyancy believe in pragmatism and infiltration; while it is nice to hope that everyone is willing to play nice, sometimes change can only occur with more drastic measures. Sirens have had to experience an apocalypse once; anything to stop a second disaster is more than welcome. Sirens of Buoyancy will level the playing field if they must.


    The Castaways
    "Really? You want me to stay around for the holidays? To be honest, I didn't really expect to be so important to you. Sure, I'd love to stay." -A Tropican Castaway

    "No matter how far humanity has been dragged down, they are so willing to keep hope in their heart and venture on. It's...conflicting, to say the least. I must know more." -A Polarian Castaway

    "I envy you. After all that has come down upon you, the struggle and the fear, you didn't give up. You're a hardy one. I wish I could be as strong as you are." -A Gardenian Castaway

    The land and the sea share a relationship that cannot be expressed through words alone. When the sea gives, the land takes. When the land gives, the sea takes. The waves brush against the sandy beaches and pull away grains into the water. Rainwater seeps through the soil and feeds the earth, allowing the seeds within to grow and flourish. It is not a one-sided relationship where one is superior to the other; both sides are willing to exist with one another. Sirens who do not follow a Current are Castaways, living as observers within humanity. When The Deluge took life from its world, the Sirens awoke with a different view of the world, a view that told them of the failings of humanity. Without a Current to swim along, the Castaways looked to humanity as a reflection of the world that once was. Can Sirens really guide humanity when there is still so much that the Sirens need to learn, as both their human side and their Siren side? The Castaways want answers for themselves before they take to any Current, experiencing the world as a human and as a creature of the sea.

        Phelegethon, The Current of Dark Passions

        "Did those bullies hurt you again? Did they tear up your books and make you bleed? ...Good. Use this as your inspiration to actually be successful." A Discordian of Phelegethon
        "Do you think your heart was the first one I've broken? To me, your tears are nothing more than drops in a river I have dug out from the earth." A Freshwater of Phelegethon

        Nature and Humanity, as much as everyone points out the differences, share just as many similarities to each other. When they rage, they show a fury that goes unlike any other. When they suffer, they show a level of shared pain that affects everything around them. When they show joy, it goes forth and touches others like bounding static. The Abyssal Sirens who follow Phelegethon believe that emotion is like a rock thrown into the water. The bigger and more violent it is, the more ripples it will create and affect all around it. As such, the Sirens of Dark Passions seek to stoke dark and destructive impulses to empower both themselves and others.

        (Ideas: An idea I have for Dark Passions is that these people can be a multitude of Sirens. Some want to actually better people through pain, making them suffer and leaving them to either sink or swim. Some want to escape from their own negative emotions, and as such lash them onto other people. Some want to gain Pneuma and keep it, and don't care about what means they must go to gain it. As a whole, I believe that these are the Sirens who are fine playing the "Villain" role, be it to dominate the world or to make people rise up together against a common threat)

        Cocytus, The Current of Dark Wisdom

        "I admire your resolve to figure out this much and get this far. Although you're still foolish to think that you're entitled to this knowledge. It's such a shame though. The obituary's going to have an extra name on it in tomorrow's papers." A Polarian of Cocytus
        "What are you willing to suffer for the knowledge I've acquired? I've suffered to gain this, and for something this mindbreaking, I'll expect the same payment to learn it from me." A Gardenian of Cocytus

        Perhaps one thing that is clear about the depths is that they're not visible to the surface. If you want to see what lies underneath, you'll need several things for it. An oxygen tank to survive down there, scuba gear to get down there and a flashlight and googles to even see. But there's still a danger in the depths that nobody prepares for, like bloodthirsty fish biting off your arm or water pressure beginning to cave in your skull. If you truly wish to know things, you must be prepared for what you must do to reach it and what you will suffer for learning it. The Current of Dark Wisdom believes in this, and as such they are willing to wontonly throw themselves upon any scrap of forbidden or taboo knowledge others won't tolerate.

        (Ideas: I imagine these to be the Sirens who are kind of like the Current of Wisdom, except they're willing to do ANYTHING for knowledge with a thirst the entirety of the Deluge cannot contain. I imagine these beings to be rulebreakers, adventure archaeologists to dangerous places, and even abusers with Verses and the like. I don't know, think of it as "Learn ALL The Things!" taken to an extreme)

        Lethe, The Current of Mankind's Victory

        "Who cares if a few little birds get uprooted? This housing division will make more money for us and give some nice places for people to live in." A Freshwater of Lethe
        "There's loads of money and profit to be made here. It's much better to be on the winning side." A Tropicana of Lethe

        Mankind is a creative, sapient animal, able to adapt to circumstances that nature throws at them. For the longest time, humanity has drawn their living resources and inspiration from nature, and have given in return for forms of worship and praise. This has gone on no more. Since the Industrial Revolution, Humanity had simply forgotten all about nature except for what they can take from it. They rip apart trees and dig up oils to fuel their precious machines and make more wonders from what they take. This fervor for technology and ambition for more has made nature mean nothing to them. So what if they take more than they give? Humanity's the most important, they'll keep surviving even if they must wipe out all else.

        (Ideas: I imagine these Sirens to be like the Sirens who resonate more with their human side rather than their Siren side, rejecting it as humanity (as in the people who are mostly the reason this current exists) rejects nature. Perhaps they're people who have dealt with human cruelty and feel like they don't want to be the ones suffering from them, so they point it at nature instead. And finally, there are Sirens who are so selfish and self-serving that they simply want the benefits of screwing over nature. Although regardless, this current, along with Primordial Darkness, tend to get the most visting experiments from Echidna. :3)

        Styx, The Current of Primordial Darkness

        "Who cares if some mud men are lost under the waves? They were going to ruin this beautiful river and it's ecology just to dam it up for their hyrdoelectric stations." A Gardenia of Styx
        "Why anyone would be happy to ruin nature is beyond me. Karma is a bitch though, and it's a good thing I'm one too." A Discordian of Styx

        Nature is a living and breathing ecosystem. Every animal plays a part in it, be it predator or prey, living lives together before dying to give more to the cycle of natural life. However, a strange creature rose out from the rest, a nature born of mud and land and air. They proclaimed themselves better than the cycle, and tried separating themselves from it with their higher thought and ingenuity. For a time, this was fine, for the cycle didn't suffer for their leaving. But that time has ended at the Industrial Revolution. Humanity grew ambitious and prideful, deciding to take from the cycle and give nothing but pollution and corruption in return. They hunt animals to extinction for trinkets, they deprive nature of homes to build their own, and all the while they've fought against and killed each other on the pettiest of things on the basis of "difference". No more. The moment the mud monkeys forgot about nature was the moment that their time has passed. It's time for nature to take back what belongs to it, in force.

        (Ideas: I think these Sirens are both similar and opposite to Mankind's Victory. They resonate to nature instead of humanity and reject humanity instead. They perhaps are intimate to nature's brutality and point it against Humanity instead. Perhaps in the vein that Mankind's Victory can have Sirens who are selfish to a point that it devestates other people, I would say that Primordial Darkness are hateful of people and believes that nature/animals should have the upper hand. Would it be breaking the rules if I noted their similarities to a certain group of people noted for crazy actions for nature or not? I'll just send it in a private message in that case if it is)

        Acheron, The Current of Apocalypse

        "Who even gives a shit about anything anymore? Nature is brutal, humanity is brutal. It's better to not be on either side at all." A Discordian of Acheron
        "I am the harbinger of the next world, filled with water cracked cities and drowned corpses of all! Don't fight it, just dunk your heads under and breathe! Breathe until the bubbles stop! Breathe in the Deluge!" A Vaporian of Acheron

        Nature and Humanity are similar. They're both brutal to each other and anything that threatens them, they have entire histories dedicated to killing themselves over survival. This world is full of tragedies upon tragedies and everyone and everything tries to write them off as "necessary". This current is perhaps the most radical and most distasteful out of all the Abyssal Currents, for they have come to the conclusion that neither nature nor humanity are worth upholding. They decide that the Deluge is better than to let the corruption in both survive.

        (Ideas: These Sirens are flat out batshit insane no matter how you look at it. They may have different backstories. Some were batshit from the beginning and decided to destroy it all. Some have come to the conclusion from the end of a list of tragedies that happened to them and thus are broken individuals before they even Drowned. Perhaps some decided that the works of other currents weren't worthwhile and the Deluge was truly immanent, so they decide to hasten it's coming. They're probably hated by both Styx and Lethe and Echidna and saying you're a part of this current is the equivalent of suicide. Socially and pinning a target on your face)

It is bad for Humanity and it is bad for Nature. It is also, presumably, Nature's Final Solution. The Deluge is meant to kill off all life because Humanity in one reality decided to overstep their boundaries and do their Small Bad Things against Nature. Eventually, Nature becomes fed up by Humanity and pushes the Reset Button, drowning everything in The Deluge. For Nature, The Deluge is unfortunate but it is something that can be recovered from, because Nature is nothing if not patient; The Deluge is meant to be damage control, keeping Humanity from doing something really stupid like starting a nuclear winter. For Humanity, The Deluge is the Apocalypse that means the end of their existence. Sirens are meant to calm Nature down so that it doesn't want to start The Deluge while making sure Humanity doesn't do stupid things that would cause Nature to want to get revenge. Or to keep Humanity from harming Nature to an unrecoverable state.

                                            Let's say that Nature and Humanity have set goals. For the most part, these ideas do not clash. Humanity is a part of life on Earth and Nature would allow them to grow. However, when Humanity's desire to flourish or expand comes at the expense of other life or it destroys part of the Earth, Nature feels the urge to lash back in order to put Humanity back in its place. While most Sirens are attempting to appease Nature, Sirens of Styx are attempting to direct this rage towards Humanity. Although this is going to destroy Humanity, at least it's going to stop The Deluge from wiping out all terrestrial life. In contrast, Sirens of Lethe, who are allied with Humanity and sometimes the Flensers, are attempting to effectively kill Nature in order to completely prevent The Deluge from happening in general. The world may become some sort of wasteland because of this, but at least humanity will be able to survive, right? In a second contrast, Sirens of Acheron want The Deluge because they've given up and are trying to accelerate the process.                                                             How are Breathless rogue agents? I thought they were the Subsplat for Sirens? Or am I misinterpreting things?
                                                            While the Breathless are a good way for a Siren to effectively farm out some Pneuma from sympathy and such, they aren't exactly "allied" with Sirens. Essentially, these people spend most of their lives struggling and being pitied for being so tragic. They may be dragged into some addiction that is harming them while everyone around them is smothering them with empty words of sympathy and encouragements. Nothing wrong with that, but they've spent their entire lives with that sort of falseness, which I feel would embitter them.

                                                            I think I would compare them to a mixture of Fetches and Stigmatics. If a Siren was able to Surface and Hear the Song, the Breathless were only able to Surface. They don't have that aspect of The Song within them, like Sirens or Banshees do, so they came back from The Deluge a bit off. They attempt to live out their lives, but they don't really feel...complete in a sense? They feel that their struggles are empty and are more of something that is dictated by fate. They are scarred, hateful individuals that want to feel that Breath of Life that they have lost.

                                                            In terms of mechanics, the Breathless are a source of Pneuma for the Sirens, as they are surrounded by people who want them to feel loved. It's some sort of compulsion for these people, something that makes them want to love and protect the Breathless without anything in return. The Breathless produces Pneuma around them that may be used as Unconditional Love; Sirens who spend time around the Breathless can feel the Pneuma collecting around them. However, the Breathless is unable to feel Pneuma, as they were unable to Hear The Song, like the Sirens or the Banshees did. Because of this, the Breathless are devoid of the Breath of Life and do not retain Pneuma; Pneuma just flows around them.

                                                            While the Breathless are a good feeding ground, they are also fairly unpredictable. The Song does not resonate within them, so Sirens cannot sense their presence. Sirens are also unable to Trace the Breathless or see the Breathless when they are Tracing. Some Sirens who have come across the Breathless have theorized that, because the Breathless lack the Breath of Life but managed to come back into this reality, they do not truly exist in the world. Both Sirens and Banshees were thrown back into this reality after hearing The Song, which gives them some hold in this reality. With the Breathless, their existence is more of an anomaly. Another property that the Breathless have is the ability to mute Verses that are sung in their presence. When a Siren attempts to invoke a Verse, she feels her voice disappear as soon as it leaves her mouth. The words are swallowed, the notes are diminished. Although the Breathless attracts the presence of people, it also draws the Verses into their void. While this would be a minor inconvenience when it comes to stationary Breathless, not all Breathless are docile, comatose individuals. Some of the Breathless attempt to fill their emptiness with meaning, throwing themselves into whatever they can to escape. Some take jobs as humanitarians or educators to feel some sort of importance. Others have taken the route of recreational drugs to attempt to feel something, anything. The worst-case scenario is if the Breathless decide to become killers or hunters.

                                                            The Breathless are an unknown variable that could work for or against a Siren, depending on what path the Breathless takes. They aren't loyal subjects that will bow down to the Sirens, like a Ghoul to their Vampire. They're a double-edged sword that could be a serious issue for Sirens that only care for them as an easy way to gain Pneuma.
