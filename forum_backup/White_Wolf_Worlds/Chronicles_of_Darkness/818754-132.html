                                    Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                                    1) Is there a merit to represent this or should be it just played ghouling some soldiers?
                                    Retainer works best for competent minions, but if he wants five of them it'll be costly. If you think it's too costly you could alleviate it with a home brew Merit tagged to an existing Retainer that represents having multiple retainers, but they must all share the same field.

                                    Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                                    2) What could the consequences be to go around the city with armed and armored soldiers, not anymore belonging to the city army?
                                    Unfortunately I don't know how acceptable it was for nobles to have private armies at the time. Armed guards of some sort were most likely acceptable, but uniformed soldiers could either be just showing off that you have armed guards (with whatever social implications that might have) or be interpreted as rebellious intentions (somewhat helped by the fact that there's only five of them) in case it's not socially acceptable. You'd have to ask a historian.

                                        People weren't allowed to walk around the city or village proper armored and carrying shields, swords and spears etc. Cultural norms frowned upon Roman citizens walking around carrying weapons and "farming utensils" like a barbarian and it was also against the law. (In Rome itself it was sacrilege.) Nor could you walk around with a private armed and armored retinue. However, the further away you got from major urban centers, enforcement became less rigid, it tended to revert to regional customs and the practicalities of existence.
