            Originally posted by ajf115 View Post

            I wonder, would the GM use binary or something else? I mean, it possesses the capability to warp the way mathematics and numbers work. Who's to say that it hasn't created a new Base that operates under mathematical laws that do not apply outside its mechanisms?
            I was reading the bit in Dance Macabre about the Holy Engineers, recently, and the God Machine apparently communicates with them using a numerical code that utilizes a variable base that follows the Fibonacci sequence. So... unary (tally marks, I guess), unary, binary, ternary, quinary, octal, tridecimal, unvegesimal, tetratrigesimal, pentapentagesimal, nonaoctogesimal, and so on. I would presume that it starts over at some point, but assuming that each number isn't huge, continuing to increase the base would stop being noticeable at some point.

            That being said, in this case I think the God Machine may have been deliberately creating a puzzle to be solved, and it seems unlikely that it uses a shifting base like that for its own operations.

                            The Wheel Within All Wheels.

                            I choose Wheel because it's one of the two components of a gear, the other being teeth. The name is a reference to how the G-M can be behind all sorts of mysteries and strangeness, always planning and machinating. It's also a reference to the fact that It is a Demiurge figure that keeps the laws/gears of our material reality running as best as It can, without necessarily being the true Creator.

                                                    In my head, the God-Machine is programmed in a balanced-ternary type base among the quaternions. For those of you who don't know, the quaternions are defined thusly:

                                                    {±1, ±i, ±j, ±k | i² = j² = k² = ijk = -1}

                                                    It gives a symbol-list of 9 elements, arranged in an overlapping 4-dimensional cross. The actual "software" for the Idiot Is is agent-based - the Angels are fragments of its software, emulated in our poor, poor n-dimensional world.
