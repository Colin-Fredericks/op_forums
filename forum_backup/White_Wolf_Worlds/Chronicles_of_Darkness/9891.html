    (God-Machine Rules) Condition Cards

    11-13-2013, 03:04 PM
    For those of you using the rules in the God-Machine Rules Update, it can get hectic keeping track of all the conditions necessary. That's why I have prepared these condition cards, which can be printed out onto ordinary index cards for your gaming pleasure, or just referenced as a PDF for online games.

    WHICH CARDS ARE WHICH FROM WHEN?
    Vampire: the Requiem has been updated with the full list of Blood & Smoke conditions!
    Demon: the Descent contains the full list of corebook conditions!
    Other cards are currently primarily the conditions from the God-Machine Rules Update.

    Condition Cards (with preprinted conditions and blank lines for filling out at the table)
    World of Darkness Color Black and white
    Vampire: the Requiem Color Black and white
    Werewolf: the Forsaken Color Black and white
    Mage: the Awakening Color Black and white
    Promethean: the Created Color Black and white
    Changeling: the Lost Color Black and white
    Hunter: the Vigil Color Black and white
    Geist: the Sin-Eaters Color Black and white
    Mummy: the Curse Color Black and white
    Demon: the Descent Color Black and white
    Condition Forms (without preprinted conditions, form-fillable PDFs for printing custom conditions)
    World of Darkness Color Black and white
    Vampire: the Requiem Color Black and white
    Werewolf: the Forsaken Color Black and white
    Mage: the Awakening Color Black and white
    Promethean: the Created Color Black and white
    Changeling: the Lost Color Black and white
    Hunter: the Vigil Color Black and white
    Geist: the Sin-Eaters Color Black and white
    Mummy: the Curse Color Black and white
    Demon: the Descent Color Black and white
