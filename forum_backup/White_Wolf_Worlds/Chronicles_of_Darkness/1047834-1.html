            No, he said that's what it takes. You posted the quote yourself. It must be representative of the spell, and be something the mage can use. Anyone can use an environmental yantra.
            The Environment Yantra only allows a bonus if the spell is cast at a specific location and is semiotically-linked to the spell itself, no more, no less.

            It's not a catch-all for anything symbolically linked to a spell.

            Environment
            Places and times in the Fallen World can bring about the
            Supernal if they reflect the spell a mage is using. An Acanthus
            may use the sun at noon to see through falsehoods, while a
            Mastigos might use the light of the full moon reflected in a
            pool of water to scry across vast distances. Location is just as
            important — many Obrimos seek out churches to perform spells
            of persuasion, command, and stewardship. Some wizards prepare ritual spaces that contain multiple Yantras — combining a
            favorable environment with Atlantean runes and magical tools
            into a summoning circle. Such a location combines three Yantras in one place — but inexperienced mystics must take their
            time, and a mage’s enemies will soon learn where best to strike.
            An environment has to link to the spell itself, not just the
            mage. The Obrimos in a church cannot use it as a Yantra to magically charge her cellphone. The magic must link to what Sleepers
            know about a place or time, not because their perceptions cause
            Supernal notice but because their actions unconsciously reflect
            the Supernal attributes of a place.

            Effect: Casting in an appropriate location grants a 1-die bonus.

                                            Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post
                                            The "flexibility" you're looking for is better expressed by using the Merits that expand Yantra options or borrowing from the Yantra options provided by your Order or Legacy. Etc.
                                            Like, this is one of the many reasons why Shadow Name is so appealing: you get to build in symbolism that plays to your intended areas of specialty instead of relying on chance and preparation, with the downside being if you want to venture outside of those specialties you have to either assemble materials the long way or go through the extensive process of rebuilding your magical identity.

                                            I'll freely cop that my reading of how Dedicating Tools works is not explicit and primarily based on extrapolating from the fact that it's a universal ability whose effects are also part of a universal ability whose only limitation is Size (and the fact that we don't strictly know how Order Tool categories form unless that's instead a function of the Sects' generalized equivalent of "the [Latin for 'X of the Dragon'] terms are the Diamond Orders' institutional Shadow Names built to evoke Atlantean society"), but even that conclusion is drawn under the assumption that potent bonuses are increasingly limited — you only get one Dedicated Tool at a time, soul stones notwithstanding, and the unambiguously strongest single Yantra bonuses are 1) bound to individual spells and dependent upon other stats, 2) bound to the symbolism of a defined magical actor thematically related to other defined magical actors, and 3) attached to items acquired from beyond the material realm with significant effort and consumed as part of the spell. [Honorable mention goes to 4) a chunk of your mystical superiors' magical insight, crystalized into an object.]

                                            This tends to incentivize certain activities, even if in practice the bonuses only offset enough penalties to give you a "free" spell factor or two, and for the sake of ludonarrative cohesion you're going to want that degree of extra power to be owed to something perceptible enough that it evokes the kind of Stories About Enlightened Magicians that serve as part of the basis for the template's image within the setting.
