                            It wasn't so much a setting as much as the skeleton for a small story arc. In modern times you come across bricks of a ziggurat that give you dreams that may be visions of a past that is very loosely described. Almost the entire section is made useless by the changes from the jump to second edition.

                            Some people seem to really like it, though I'm surprised considering its content. It might be worth revising for the new edition, though it would be weird justifying the god-king angle. It worked when they were 'spirit police,' basically superheroes. Now they're predators par excellence, and having a populace willing to serve under them knowing this, and that they might be the next prey, would be weird.

                            Unless they were a huge lodge.

                            It occurs to me that many of my posts have been for the most part negative about proposals than adding more of my own. I am a little tired of it, we have had these threads before and more than a few of these ideas have been repeated several times over, but there are some new ones so here's some of mine.

                            For Slasher, India in the 1830s during the suppression of the thugs, or during the time of Nimrod which was called up in the Slasher book. For vampire, the California Gold Rush, the rise of Communism in China, Morocco during World War 2, or the Roman conquest of Britain or during the Khedivate of Egypt after the French invasion of said country. Also obviously at whatever point the Pijavica began to spread. For werewolf, still Vietnam during the war, South or Central America during the colonization, as Spain enslaved but immersed with the populace, turned them from their religion, all for so much silver to ship home, Macedonia a bit past 1900. Something that would require a drastic adaptation would be the advent and proliferation of the gun, and consequently the effectiveness of silver bullets. Before you could theoretically use silver arrows but more realistically it could come down to silver knives and swords and, thus, death. So the rise of the gun would be interesting to play around with.

                            I'd really like to tackle the Crusades for everybody, but that seems very much a Classic World of Darkness setting. Overall I'd like to see more Asia, outside of Japan, and Africa. And I would really like breaks from the standard 'biomes.' More deserts and jungles. Something in Egypt after the French

                                                If Dark Ages is essentially going to be a recurring series (and it seems like it will), I'd love to see a series of books that all cover a series of history, with each "era" piggybacking off the other. This would give players and STs all the tools to play an expansive long-form game and also play with the cause-and-effect of major historical events while having thematic currents flow from one setting to the next.

                                                Someone mentioned a line-wide vision of the WoD after the fall of the Mongols, with each line focusing on a different region, and I think that's a genius idea.

                                                I'd like to see a book that, for instance, starts with the Age of Imperialism and sees those plot threads extend through the end of WWII. Off the top of my head, it might look something like this:

                                                Geist in the Belgian Congo
                                                Vampire in newly unified Germany
                                                Werewolf in the dying days of the Wild West
                                                Russian Revolution Demon
                                                Beast in the trenches and countryside of WWI Europe
                                                Mage during the Age of Spiritualism (with Edison and Tesla for added cache)
                                                Changeling in the Golden Age of Cinema
                                                Promethean Munich: 1937 - 1939
                                                Something something during the French Resistance (thoughts?)
                                                Innocents Blitz
                                                Hunter Pacific Theater
                                                Deviant Last Days of WWII (superhuman arms race)
                                                a crossover setting for Berlin just after the war, similar to what was depicted in The Third Man, with the city split up into districts controlled by the various Allies and the government in complete disarray.

                                                It would perfectly set things up for Dark Ages: Cold War, which I'd also love to see, and I think Demon: Moscow taking place shortly after the collapse of the USSR would be a fantastic capstone to that.
