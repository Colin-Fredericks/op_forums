    How Would You Use The Slender Man In The CoD?

    04-30-2016, 09:24 PM
    For a Chronicle centered around mortals or Hunters, Slendy could be a Horror or G-M Angel. Speaking conceptually, he could also be a True Fae, but the mechanics for them in 2ED haven't been revealed. I went into more detail about this on posts #24 and #29 of this thread.

    Regardless of which supernatural type you choose for him, a big part of why he became so popular is the sheer mystery. Whatever modus operandi and powers he adopts in your story, there should a sense of incomprehensibility, as this short film brilliantly demonstrates. You might say that picking a creature type for him is counter-productive and largely removes the Lovecraftian dread. I can see where you're coming from. However, I do not know how to properly address this issue in narrative terms. In mechanical terms, I believe the finished version of Amechra's Revised Ephemeral Being Rules will handle this nicely.

    Bye.

                                              Apart from a Horror, Slender Man could be a lot of things depending on the game line.

                                              Vampire - A Nosferatu with high Blood Potency and low Humanity. He loves children; they're delicious.

                                              Werewolf - A spirit of fear, specifically the fear of a stranger who comes take your children. Whenever people hear of a missing child, many can't help but think, What if that were my child? Could it happen to me?--and the Slender Man feeds on the essence.

                                              Mage - An abyssal entity. Or perhaps someone whose spell went hideously wrong.

                                              Changeling - An Exile, trying to buy its way back into Arcadia by providing captives to other Gentry.

                                              Promethean - Someone tried to create a Promethean. They failed, and instead they created something else.

                                              Deviant - THEY tried to turn you into something like that. Now they have a working prototype, and they're sending it after you. (Yeah I know we can't use it for a long time, and this version would probably be a Horror anyway. The idea just came to me.)
