            Cain
            While it is unlikely that he was the first man to slay another, murder has dogged the steps of the creature named Cain for millennia. The legends surrounding him have grown and been distorted by time. What is true is that he was once a farmer that killed his brother and was cursed for this deed to walk the earth for eternity. And he has walked; from a time thousands of years before the start of recorded history, through the rise and fall of civilizations, through periods of warfare, disaster, peace and enlightenment. In his time he has been both penitent and monster and visited every corner of the earth known to man and beyond. In in all times and all places, he leaves death in his wake.
            For the last 30 years, Cain has lived in isolation on a secluded farm in rural Kansas, finding a small measure of peace in the solitude. Cain has hard to place features, belonging to an ethnicity long extinct. He appears to be an athletic man in early middle age of average height (he was considered quite tall in his day).                 Tooth Fairy
                These gossamer-winged sloped-headed almond-eyed teal-skinned denizens of Faerie crave teeth and often exchange coins they find from sifting through place humans tend to lose or toss them into,such as in couches or within wishing fountains when interacting with humans whom believe in fairies and have left their teeth in a place for this purpose. The ugly side of the Tooth Fairies' demeanor is when they are confronted by someone who doesn't hold the proper respect or awe for them, the sprite tend to spitefully snatch that being's teeth from their face as soon as the offensive being opens their mouth, and then carry the teeth back to Faerie. Given Tooth Fairies can become a mist to enter a home, and even if struck down trying to exit they transform into a swarm still capable of carrying its prize with it, they almost always fulfill their Aspirations.

                Horror Type: Minion
                Best Dice Pool: 5 Attacking for Damage in a Swarm, Snatching all the teeth from a being's open mouth with single swipe of the hand while in full size solid form, Sneaking into and out of homes
                Worst Dice Pool: Chance Doing anything during the day, Doing anything while in contact with Cold Iron
                All Other Pools: 2
                Aspirations: Trade coins for teeth with respectful believers, Steal teeth from disrespectful unbelievers, Keep the teeth hidden and safe in lair
                Dread Powers: Discorporate (into a swarm of hornet sized Tooth Fairy, each able to carry merely a single human molar), Hunter Sense's (Teeth and any creature that currently has teeth), Mist Form
                Bane: Cold Iron
                Willpower/Scene: 2

                            Originally posted by The Festival by H.P. Lovecraft
                            The nethermost caverns...are not for the fathoming of eyes that see; for their marvels are strange and terrific. Cursed the ground where dead thoughts live new and oddly bodied, and evil the mind that is held by no head. Wisely did Ibn Schacabao say, that happy is the tomb where no wizard hath lain, and happy the town at night whose wizards are all ashes. For it is of old rumour that the soul of the devil-bought hastes not from his charnel clay, but fats and instructs the very Worms that gnaws; till out of corruption horrid life springs, and the dull scavengers of earth wax crafty to vex it and swell monstrous to plague it. Great holes secretly are digged where earth's pores ought to suffice, and things have learnt to walk that ought to crawl.
                            Worms that Walk are Horrors that originate when a Mage with Wisdom lower than 5 dies with an unresolved Abyssal Imago Paradox Condition and whose body is allowed to fester, rot, and feed loathsome wriggling invertebrates. The Worms that Walks that arises from such a Mage is a Horror with as much Potency as the Mage had Gnosis, and has the memories/identity/consciousness of the Mage which spawned it, with the normal limitation of stats of a Horror of that Potency, meaning they will likely lose Attributes, Skills and Merits. Quite frequently being reduced to a Worms that Walks greatly strips a Mage of much of their power and even their most basic abilities, and what fragments of themselves that they do retain heavily leans towards the purely mental. Their consciousness reconstitutes by sliding back from the Abyss through the Taint of their corpse being collected within the living bodies of the worms and maggots that devour their remains. Pursuing the Obsessions they had before their death becomes their only Aspirations as a Worms that Walks, and their Anchors tend to remain the same or become more extreme versions of themselves. All Worms that Walk find themselves as high ranking Members of the Cult of Yonder as their Merit points will allow, which takes priority over any other way those Merits could be used to reflect what remains of the individual the Worms that Walks once was. Worms that Walk find the Cult of Yonder instinctive and innately comforting and their alien mind copes far better than those who did not undergo a transformation of consciousness like they did making it easy for them to rise to the top. Worms that Walk come back with no Wisdom nor Integrity to moderate its most heinous curiosities to reflect how the Abyss has warped its perspective into something wholly inhuman and alien even if it has most of the memories it had in life. All Worms that Walk have the Been Yonder [Persistent] Condition.

                            Because universally Worms that Walk cause paradox as if they were a crowd of Sleeper witnesses they find reconnecting with former associates to resume studying their obsession, even if their associates wouldn't be phased by their new form. Also being driven to study Mysteries with a lack of Mage Sight or any of their Arcanum they are frequently frustrated and profoundly bitter, to say nothing of their writhing envy of still living Mages. Remembering the glory of Supernal Truth and being reduced to a being whose very nature is that of being Untrue more profoundly than the Lie is a jagged caustic pill to swallow. Worms that Walk have a single respite of their eternal life as a pile of maggots and worms and that is while they Astrally Travel or Dream they appear as they did in life, though they retain all their current Dread Powers. This has lead some to maintain former relationships through this state and others as a way to keep key allies of their pet projects in a position to assist them.

                            While the Potency and other stats are roughly derived from the Mage (or rarely other type of unlucky being) whose corpse the Worms that Walks has risen from,but the Dread Powers gained by increased Potency follow the same pattern of priority regardless of what the character was capable of in life. Potency has a minimum of 1, and increases by 1 for every Gnosis, Primal Urge, Blood Potency, Wyrd, or Synergy the Major Template has beyond one.

                            The three following Dread Powers every work that walks has is as follows:
                            • Discorporate (Worms and Bugs),
                            • Immortal (Resurrection Clause: Any organic object with a Strong temporal or spacial sympathetic connection to the dead Mage is fed to invertebrates appropriate to create a Worms that Walks)
                            • Presence of Paradox* (may not be turned off)
                            They gain the follow Dread Powers in the following Order:
                            1. Surprise Entrance (Suddenly Forming Out of Bugs and Worms to Enter the Scene)
                            2. Jump Scare
                            3. Burrow (WtF 2e Pg210)
                            4. Eye Spy (Through invertebrates that once were part of the body)
                            5. Beast Master (Except it Applies to Abyssal Horrors of animal Intelligence which are elephant size or smaller)
                            *=Abyssal Aspects and (Numina See Below)

                            Cult of the Yonder
                            Beyond all reality is the Abyss, a nonexistence that contains everything possible and impossible all constantly contradicting itself in a roiling chaos of stagnant abeyance. Members of the Cult of the Yonder universally have been in the Abyss either mentally or with their whole being. If the cult member is fortunate the way they entered the Abyss will be an event that transforms them into a creature which is no longer human in mindset(such as a being without Wisdom nor Integrity), so they don't suffer the negative affects of the Been Yonder [Persistent] Condition. This leads the Cult of the Yonder to be filled with the shaken, spooked, guilty, broken, mad, and those with alien minds. Frequently those that belong to this cult feel a strong need to make sense of their experience and this leads them look deeper into Abyss for answers, which in turn leads to more trauma that increases the desire to understand the source of their pain in a spiral going ever inward and downward.

                            Cultists: Worms that Walk, Abyssal Beings Madmen
                            Initiation Benefits
                            • All initiates learn many unspeakable secrets of the Abyss. They gain a Occult Specialty in
                            Abyssal Blasphemies.
                            •• Full members have their unconscious mind linked to horrific astral realms that reflect abyssal concepts within the Temenos which causes them to always have nightmares instead of dreams, but those nightmares become a source of terrible revelations. They gain a dot of the Dream Merit free of charge.
                            ••• As a cultist delves into the mysteries that their sleeping mind wrestles with night after night, they become metaphysically tethered to a Rank 1 Acamoth of their own Virtue in a relationship which grants them the Familiar (••) Merit free of charge.
                            •••• The crazed insane priests of the Yonder gain the Astral Adept (•••) Merit. The ceremony they must perform is to sleep un-medicated in a dark room with no one else present, and in relative silence. This will cause a horrendous night terror which will shape itself into a journey beyond their own soul in order to escape.
                            ••••• Those of the Yonder Cult which have traveled the deepest pits of raving Abyssal madness within the Astral find they able to give fellow Astral Travelers they meet in their Astral Journeys the Been Yonder [Persistent] Condition if they ask to have revelations of the Abyss revealed to them.

                            Been Yonder [Persistent]
                            You've been in the Abyss either mentally or with your whole being, experiencing in each moment as countless eternal epochs of cosmic apostatizing exposure to things minds were not mean to know. Suffer a Breaking Points simply by thinking about your experience within the Abyss, remembering it, or being reminded of it as long as you have either Wisdom or Integrity to lose. You also qualify to join the Mystery Cult of the Yonder if you explore your subconscious connection to it.
                            Causes: Experience being within formless timeless portions of the Abyss, Having Amnesia of that experience removed, Have the mental totality of that experience artificial transplanted within your mind (Which require Mind 5, or an appropriate Influence 4 by an Acamoth to Create an exact copy of an existing experience, mere modification of things that can be mundanely known does not suffice)
                            Resolution: Gain the Amnesia Condition concerning time in the Abyss

                                        The Chosen of Muoi
                                        The Chosen of Muoi are individuals chosen by a secretive cult that originates in Vietnam several hundred years ago. The cult venerates an entity named “Máu Luu Dong”, which translates roughly to “The Blood Swarm”. In return for its favor and protection from its mosquito children, the cultist offer a young women to become the entities avatar. This is considered both blessing and curse. It bestows great power, but curses the Chosen with an insatiable thirst for blood. The Chosen gains the ability to assume a “larval” form, a pale, grotesque blood-drinking creature, and an “Avatar” form – a giant, winged horror that combines the traits of a human and a mosquito. So far, the Chosen have only been seen in Vietnam, and in Vietnamese communities in France and the United States.

                                        Mystery Cult: Cult of the Blood Swarm

                                        The Cult of the Blood Swarm began almost 300 years ago in Vietnam. When a remote village was beset by vicious, plague carrying mosquitoes, the villagers made a deal with an entity they named “The Blood Swarm”. In return for good health and protection from his biting, hungry children, they must ritually set aside one of their own to be fed upon to sate the hunger of the swarm. Occasionally, one favored by the Blood Swarm would become its Avatar, to act as its strongest servant in the mortal world. Since that time, the Cult has continued on in secret, obeying the will of its patron. In recent years the cult has been making efforts to breed and smuggle species of mosquitoes into new regions, and provide sacrifices as demanded by the Blood Swarm.

                                        Cult of the Blood Swarm
                                        Purpose: To honor the demands of the Blood Swarm in exchange for health and good fortune
                                        Relic: The Idol of the Blood Swarm. A jade statue depicting a half woman, half mosquito creature.
                                        Doctrine: The cult is sworn to secrecy, and inducts only relatives of current members. The dictates of the Blood Swarm are communicated by the High Priestess, and every member routinely receives orders directly from her.

                                        Initiation Benefits
                                        ● - Cult Members become resistant to disease, gaining a dot in the Hardy Merit. Cult Members become completely immune to discomfort from and non-supernatural ailments transmitted by Mosquito bite.
                                        ●● - Cult Members are gifted a measure of a Mosquitoes speed and alertness, gaining a dot in the Fast Reflexes Merit.
                                        ●●● - Cult Members become versed in the deeper lore of the Blood Swarm and gain a dot in Occult.
                                        ●●●● - Cult Members gains access to significant resources gathered by the Cult overtime. The may divide three dots of Merits between Allies, Resources and Safe Place.
                                        ●●●●● - A High Priest or Priestess of the Cult has the favor of the Blood Swarm, and make sacrifices in its Name, gaining three dots in the Sacrificial Offering Merit (Hurt Locker, pg. 76). In addition, once a month they may call upon “The Chosen of Muoi” - a hideous, powerful creature to do their bidding.

                                                        Verminators

                                                        Background
                                                        These autonomous drones were created to solve various pest problems that infest the urban sprawl. Their small size enables them to squeeze into confined spaces after their targets, and their coordinated swarm intelligence lets them surround nests to prevent any from escaping. They've been programmed with some basic architectural information, to prevent their occasional burrowing (to access and surround entrenched vermin) from causing more damage or collapse. Despite initial tests proving successful, high start-up costs and some linger safety concerns led to the project and commercial release getting scrapped. The remaining prototypes have since been sold off to various collectors, corporations interested in niche robotics, or stolen from storage.
                                                        Description
                                                        Ironically, considering their function and primary targets, Verminators most resemble stainless steel rats, with four locomotive appendages, a barrel-like main fuselage, and leading with its primary sensor and "elimination" manipulator. There was even discussion about clothing them in faux rat coverings as a form of camouflage, before being dismissed as ghoulish.
                                                        Storytelling Hints
                                                        Verminators primarily serve as pests, such as when they've gone haywire or been reprogrammed to undermine foundations. They can serve as a more serious threat if they've been reprogrammed to target humans, and groups will have individual units grapple limbs (inflicting the respective Tilt) to bring down larger "prey". Since they are programmed and built by others, they always point to another antagonist as the responsible actor.


                                                        Best At (7 Dice): Locating targets, swarm tactics, burrowing through buildings
                                                        Worst At (1 Die): Innovative tactics, swimming
                                                        Other (3 Dice)

                                                        Aspiration: Locate and eliminate programmed targets from the area.
                                                        Initiative:
                                                        Defense: 3
                                                        Speed: 12
                                                        Size: 1
                                                        Weapons/Attacks:
                                                        Type Damage Range Dice Pool
                                                        Bite 1L (AP 1) Melee 7 (in group), 3 (alone)

                                                        Banes: Electricity
                                                        Bans: none
                                                        Dread Powers: Burrow, Hunters Senses (Programmed Target), Natural Weapons (•), Surprise Entrance
