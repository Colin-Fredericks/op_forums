                                                I think I agree with Satchel's reading, if I'm following correctly.

                                                Say we have three objects each with Durability 2 spaced out from an explosion. The explosion deals 5 damage.

                                                At ground zero the object there is subject to 5 Aggravated damage. 2 points are downgraded to Lethal which are then absorbed by Durability and the object suffers the remaining 3 points of Aggravated.

                                                In the primary blast radius the object is subject to 5 Lethal damage. 2 points are downgraded to Bashing, 2 points are cancelled. The object takes 3 damage.

                                                In the secondary radius, the object is subject to 5 Bashing damage. 2 is downgraded to nothing, a further 2 is cancelled. The object takes 1 damage.

                                                The alternative: that the downgrading happens instead of the normal cancellation, produces damages of 5/5/3.

                                                        Originally posted by ShadowcatX View Post
                                                        Items do track aggravated and lethal separately, it may be a distinction without a difference at the moment but there is nothing in the rules as far as I can see that says to track them the same.
                                                        "Each point of damage removes a point of Structure." You are not applying damage to Health boxes here, you are taking off points of Structure, and the only distinction is that attacks that deal aggravated damage do not subtract an object's Durability from the points of Structure they remove from the object. Objects don't heal over time or distinguish between falling unconscious/bleeding out/dying, and repairing objects harmed by aggravated damage is only harder in the sense that there will be more damage than there would be from an attack that was subject to Durability.

                                                        Nothing in the rules says to track damage types the same because you don't track damage types for objects after the damage is dealt — objects respond to sources of lethal and bashing damage the same way and are more susceptible to aggravated damage, but Structure is Structure.

                                                        I believe the rules for explosives supercede the normal rules for durability, it only downgrades until you're far enough out to for all the damage to be bashing. Just like with acid you wouldn't apply half the hardness then move on to the regular rules for objects and apply durability again.
                                                        That's a poor comparison, because Durability downgrading damage objects take from explosions does not involve a numerical modification of the normal function of Durability.

                                                        If the aggravated damage being downgraded to lethal isn't otherwise subject to Durability then there is no reason to say that the damage at ground zero is downgraded at all, and your reading extends that further to every bit of damage from the explosion before the secondary blast area. Is it your contention that a Molotov cocktail — an explosive that is literally just a glass bottle full of gasoline — should be able to blow an arm-width hole in a stone wall the roughly fifty percent of the time that its Force roll comes up with a success?

                                                        The system has it so that having an explosion go off within arm's reach of you is ruinous to a living body. My reading is that Durability downgrading damage — which Structure does not track differently after it's been deal — does not translate directly to objects whose Structure is less than the full damage of an explosion being vaporized by immediate proximity to the blast irrespective of their Durability, especially when Hurt Locker says that any remaining aggravated damage ignores Durability, which is what aggravated damage normally does with regards to Durability.

                                                        There are already plenty of purposes for which lethal and aggravated damage are functionally the same, and I don't think damaging objects with explosives is meaningfully different from them in this way. The function of an explosive is to deal heavy damage over a large area; being able to utterly ruin a living creature at the center of the explosion doesn't translate to the same sort of impact on things that don't have organs to disrupt. Comparing it to a punch misses the fact that the main way most sources of lethal damage distinguish themselves from unarmed attacks is by having a bonus damage from a weapon, which explosives accomplish in addition to making their "attack" roll without accounting for Defense — I don't believe the system is intended to have all varieties of explosive be more effective at damaging an object than shooting it point-blank with an equally powerful gun.

Devastating to a person of course but a steel fence for example will easily distribute the heat; shrapnel will ricochet off; expanding gases pass through the bars etc. In contrast a sufficiently strong punch is focused in one spot (and will break most mortal hands that try it).

But if that was the intent why not just say that instead of talking about downgrading at all?
