    C&C: TFV and Vampires

    11-18-2013, 01:14 AM
    I don't see why TFV even need Vampires, American Black Ops (in this case very very black) are many things, but underfunded they aren't. Even if they can't get it that way, there's plenty of accounting tricks the government could use to fund the TFV, $50000 for a light bulb for example. Last but not least, they could 'accidently' lose it, like they lost $12bn dollars in Iraq.

    It just seems like an incredibly bad thought out idea for no real reason.

                                      You can disagree with the decision, but it was a thoroughly thought-out one.

                                      Here's the origin of the decision, courtesy of the person who wrote that segment ("Tier_Three"): http://forums.white-wolf.com/default...448#post586448

                                      I saw the way the story went in Night Stalkers, when I was advised to at least hint that the vampires were running the show.

                                      I originally wrote the thing out as being a kind of a story, connecting Malleus Maleficarum and VALKYRIE through the mists of time.

                                      Welcome to the first Cold War in human history. Two mighty forces battling it out behind the shadows, wielding unthinkable powers, and the soul of the entire world is at stake.

                                      Before there even was a United States, when the only world was the Old World, the Church and the remnants of the dying Camarilla were at each other's throats.

                                      Then the Church hunters began attacking with Benedictions. And these were very effective against the Kindred.

                                      In fact, had the New World not been discovered, the Malleus might have managed to do something unthinkable - reduce a monstrous species to extinction.

                                      Nonetheless, with the opening of the new trade routes across the Atlantic, the Kindred managed to flee the worst ministrations of Mother Church, ensuring the species' survival. But the war had another effect.

                                      When Padre Ambrogio unlocked the secrets of Benedictions, and the Malleus began to score some real victories against the Kindred (they claim Milan as one of their victories; to this day The Lucifuge sits on her throne, chortling as she remembers their conceit back then - and now) the vampires realised that they could no longer feed openly against mortals.

                                      The rise of Malleus Maleficarum brought about the current Tradition of The Masquerade among the Kindred. The hunters were responsible for why vampires have to work in the shadows and behind the scenes.

                                      And then there was the New World, and the Renaissance and Enlightenment back home. Science and Reason began flexing its muscles after a long slumber wedged under the Church's glutes. A new kind of civilisation began to appear in the Americas, and a new breed of hunter conspiracy. Imagine an 1800s TF:V with electricity, vacuum tube computers and radios poached from their British equivalent - who had radio fifty years before Marconi, and RADAR in 1900.

                                      The Brits gave VALKYRIE the radios. They didn't give them RADAR.

                                      Now imagine the most astute Kindred of the time, realising that a momentous opportunity had arisen. The Cold War had not ended, but it was about to shift from one major player to the other. From the Malleus - locked away in Europe amid increasingly stifling obsolescence in a world changing faster than they could keep up - to VALKYRIE, in the forefront of that changing world. Riding the wave.

                                      How better to keep the new player under control than by getting in at its inception before it could get too strong? They wouldn't be able to stop VALKYRIE doing its thing - VALKYRIE's too big even for the Kindred to control completely - but they could run a form of damage limitation and stop the Kindred's most critical assets from getting killed, or even noticed, by the VALKYRIE war machine.

                                      Individuals may come and go, and VALKYRIE will still be able to claim major victories, but they would never be able to exterminate the Kindred.

                                      I'm going to add another pointer to help you lot out, if you're still worried about the whole "vampires running TF:V" thing.

                                      Look at my writeup in Night Stalkers. There's a whole project in there that Stearne haven't even thought of suborning. They're focused way too much on keeping Project: TWILIGHT under their thumb; and Project: ADAMSKI's too busy playing catchup with the vampires who have their own, very efficient form of Masquerade (and ADAMSKI are just finding this out now ...)

                                      So who does that leave? Oh, yes. Underfunded, ignored, chasing lights in the sky, Bigfoot sightings, cattle mutilations and black helicopters. Or is that a cover, using some cunning ADAMSKI subterfuge and misdirection?

                                      Go. Look it up. Then ask yourselves what those boys at Project: FORT could really be up to.

                                          Thanks for that sikker, I never saw the authors side of the story before.


                                          I think that when theye1 said "bad thought out idea" he meant that it was a bad conclusion, not that it hadn't been thought through. At least that's how I'm familiar with the idiom he used.


                                          Now that aside. As good as it was to see the authors point of view... I still think it's a terrible idea.

                                          First of all, nothing in there answers the key question: Why does it reinforce TF:V's core theme, government run military hunters.


                                          Secondly, it just seems poorly thought out. This secret history of the vampire kind world ignores... well the rise of Anglicism, the existence of entire Eastern Othordox Church and the subcontinent of India for starters. There's no way that the MM alone would push vampire's to extinction.


                                          Thirdly it is, at lest it gives a very strong impression of, depending on this story to be interesting. Certainly a lot of people didn't find the idea of TF:V vampire overlords interesting, and when the author tried to explain why it's interesting he went straight to this story and nothing else. This story is not in any of the books. Depending on a story that doesn't exist in your head is just bad design. This one is mostly a presentation issue, but if you don't have the word count to make the big reveal more interesting than "guess what? Vampires!", it's by far better not to do it at all.


                                          Still though. It's point #1 that's the real issue in my book.

                                              I think Task Force Valkyrie being backed by vampires is a good decision for two reasons:

                                              1) TFV is a conspiracy in the most UFO enthusiast sense of the word. They're literally the men in black. Having them be controlled by the masters of conspiracy* and undying bureaucracy reinforces their theme of being enigmatic, probably corrupt, black ops conspirators.
                                              2) Considering the emphasis on vampires being good at controlling mortals and their systems, on the ageless webs of intrigue they are supposed to spin, I for one applaud that this is, for once, actually reflected in the setting. As much as it's a cliché to say "vampires are secretly in control!" in popular media, Vampire: the Requiem has not actually been shown in control of anything of consequence throughout its run. Vampires should secretly control *something*, and TFV fits the bill better than anything else available.

                                              *=At least until Demon arrived.
