              Might as well dump my list of gadgets from my notes:

              Gadgets:
              Embedded
              • Murder Pistol (Merciless Gunman): A semi-automatic pistol capable of killing entire groups of foes in moments, or doing grievous damage to priority targets. [Might need to downgrade due to embedded gadget limitations]
              • Hat of Blending (Lost In The Crowd): As long as the wearer stays in large groups, they are nearly impossible to detect.
              • Bottomless Bandolier (In My Pocket): The armored pockets of this bandolier can produce a seeming endless number of grenades on demand.
              • Detective’s Glasses (Last Place You Look): These common sunglasses can reveal the presence things hidden with guilty intent.
              • Watchman’s Alarm (Last Place You Look) [Near-Field]: This small pocket sensor vibrates when someone who is trying to remain hidden is nearby.
              • Personal Soundtrack (Like The Movies): When this cassette is played, the user can bend reality to conform to common media tropes.
              • Getaway Paintjob (Quick Change): The driver of the vehicle bearing this paintjob can quickly switch between different appearance presets when unobserved, aiding in shaking pursuers.
              • Wolf’s Bane Rounds (Wave Function Collapse) [One Shot]: These silvered rifle rounds are capable of locking creatures in human form, preventing them from accessing their more destructive abilities.
                • Other variants might force creatures in other forms to assume their human one.
              • Trustworthy Suit (You Can Tell Me): The wearer of this impeccable 3-piece suit projects an air calm and trustworthiness. People around them feel like they can tell them anything over a drink or smoke.
              • Coat of Deflection (Check Backdrop): Bullets seem to avoid the wearer of this long coat, unless enemies take the time to aim at them directly. The coat does get in the way of the wearer’s own hasty shots, however.
              • Instruction Manuals (Download Knowledge) [One Shot]: After a few minutes perusal of this book, the reader feels knowledgeable and confident about the upcoming task.
              • Distracting Magazine (Ellipses): Whether it is the thought provoking articles, the gaudy advertisements, or the… “vivid” pictures, those who pick up and read this find time slipping away.
              • McGuyver Kit (Functional Identity): This toolbox/toolkit is able to convert nearly any other device into an impromptu weapon.
              • Performer’s Blade (Fungible Knowledge): This expertly crafted sword, disguised as a stage prop, is able to grant the wielder combat skill (Weaponry) commensurate to their acting ability (Expression).
              • Infiltration Map (Like I Built It): This odd diagram reveals all the details and blueprints of whatever structure it is unrolled inside.
              • Joy Pills (Miles Away) [One Shot, Near Field?]: This small smiley faced pills give a pleasant feeling that drives away other distracting emotions.
              • Danger Buzzer (Read Hostility): This small earbud send out a sharp buzzing sound whenever the person the wearer is looking at is preparing to attack.
              • Sacrificial Dolls (Shift Consequences) [One Shot]: These small stuff dolls are capable of absorbing the damage from a single attack directed at their wearer, provided they are “activated” beforehand.
              • Armor Vest (Turn Blade): This kevlar vest reduces all incoming damage to mere pressure and bruises (Bashing).
              • Combat Dazzler (Wasted Time): This LED sparkler makes it hard for foes to aim at the bearer, taking twice as long to draw a bead upon their target.
              • Avarice Grenades (Apple of Discord) [One Shot]: These elaborate gold-plated grenades cause those who see them to (suicidally) want to keep them close, even fighting others to do so. They can be primed and ensure potential targets cluster around the blast area, or just thrown as a distraction or to flush foes from cover.
              • Match-Light (Combustion): The target of this infrared laser becomes especially prone to spontaneous combustions, sometimes even catching fire outright.
              • Truce Flag (Cool Heads Prevail): This simple white cloth, when waived, can cause hostility to cease between combatants.
              • Crash Padding (Just Bruised): This set of padded clothing greatly protects its wearer from harm, reducing falls to a mere hard bump, and slashes to a small cut.
              • Night-Night Sap (Knockout Punch): Most anyone hit with this leather sap finds themselves taking an impromptu vacation in unconsciousness.
              • Healing Poultice (On The Mend): This foul-smelling bandage can completely restore a person from a bruised wreck to full health in a fraction of an hour.
              • Hack Drive (Password Entropy): Plugging in this unassuming USB device can greatly assist in breaching password secured systems.
              • “Suicide” Pills (Play Possum) [One Shot]: This looks like an oral cyanide capsule, but when use merely fake death. Care should be taken to make sure others don’t dispose of the “body” in an… irreversible manner.
              • Echo Rifle (Ripple): Damage inflicted with this rifle is always worse than it first seems, causing lingering wounds, or knocking the target into other hazards.
              • Door Busters (Shatter) [One Shot]: This small explosive can breach even the toughest doors, when properly placed.
              • Bleeder Scope (Victory At Any Price): When activated, this night-vision scope can guarantee a hit (1 automatic success on the next Firearms roll)… at some cost to the marksman.
              • Whisper Mike (Across a Crowded Room): This small collar microphone can transmit a spoken message in a straight line to a target with perfect clarity.
              • Basilisk Trap (The Look): When this trap is triggered, a hypnotic scene plays that the triggerer can’t look away from.
              • Auto-Pilot (Alibi): This Palm Pilot PDA can be given to the Demon’s detached Cover, and maintain it while to Demon carries out other actions. It can also be setup to activate in response to stimuli (such as someone knocking on the door).
              • Magic 8-ball (Voice of the Machine): Shaking this normal looking novelty 8-ball and speaking a question aloud will have a relevant response appear.

              Exploited
              • Hellfire Muzzle (Hellfire): A modular attachment to any gun that lets it fire blasts of incinerating flame.
              • Ring of Power (Show of Power): This clearly unnatural ring is able to replicate the supernatural powers of a Mage upon call.
              • Demonic Transmission (Demon Car): This mechanical contraption fuses with a vehicle, adding a number of modifications to its structure.
              • Vengeance Collar (Eye For An Eye): When the wearer of this choker is subjected to an attack, the damage is removed and flung back at the attacker.
              • Instant Barrier (Frozen In Time): The particles in this aerosol can can be sprayed and triggered to form a stationary and immovable cloud, blocking access points, forming cover, or turning high-speed objects into chunky salsa as they pass through the cloud.
              • Installation Tattoo Needle (Living Installation): This weird tattoo needle allows a demon to install embeds upon humans.
              • Death Note (Murder By Improbability): Writing someone’s name in this eerie notebook will often end up with their “accidental” death.
              • Guardian Gargoyle (Animate): This grotesque statuary can animate to attack intruders.
              • Introductory Video (Context Matters): This short documentary explains the nature and capabilities of a single or small group of Unchained to 5 mortals, allowing them to assist in resisting cover violations. These are often part of the entry training for mortals joining an Agency.
              • Distortion Ward (Fractured Space*): This Escheresque glyph subtly bends the space around the wearer, actually adding 20 range(?) between what appears to be the surface and then space nearby. This renders the wearer virtually immune to melee attacks, and can even affect ranged accuracy, but also means the wearer cannot physically interact with most objects.
              • Life Insurance (Raise Dead) [One Shot]: This arcanely worded life insurance document can save a favored person’s life - mostly by restoring it in the event it has been lost. On the event of the bearer’s death, starting at 4 hours later, the owner begins rolling as per Raise Dead (minus adding Primum to the roll), up to a maximum of 5 days (after which, the gadget runs out of Aether to fuel the process). If successful, they are restored to life and the document crumbles to ash (if they aren’t successful, they stay dead and the document still crumbles to ash).

              * = Homebrewed Embed/Exploit, usually.

                  Hot damn, those are cool. Might as well dump my concepts.

                  Voice of Potence (Show of Power): A tape recorder that can record any supernatural power that works by sound and play it back, retaining any supernatural effects of the original ability.

                  Eye of Potence (Show of Power): A more powerful version of the Voice of Potence. A video camera that can record supernatural powers and illusions and play them back. So, if it recorded a Nosferatu using Nightmare, the illusions created would be captured on film and would retain their power even when played back.

                  Gary's Bones (Show of Power): A set of twenty-sided dice, capable of capturing one supernatural power or effect on each face. Once a die has been "loaded", rolling it so that it lands on a "loaded" face causes the corresponding effect to occur.

                  The Binding Rod (Ultimatum): Any contract or agreement signed with this pen is magically binding, and functions mechanically as a Changeling Pledge.

                  Gourmand's Scalpel (Extispicy): A rather morbid Gadget in the shape of an ordinary kitchen knife. When it is used to cut a piece of flesh from a corpse, and the piece is consumed, the eater learns how and when the corpse perished, and certain other details: if they had a template, what template it was, and so on.

                  The Fountain of Beelzebub (Addictive Presence): A plastic pumpkin-shaped Halloween candy bowl. Any candy left in it overnight becomes magically addictive, causing anyone who consumes it to become addicted to that type of candy.
