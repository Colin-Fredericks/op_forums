            She used to be a chorus girl, languishing in obscurity, and longing for the spotlight. At night, she took singing lessons. In sleep he came to her, and in those dreams he sang. The voice called to her, spoke her name. Soon she found that she only wished to dream again, to her his song, and to learn to sing as he did. Eventually she wanted only to be his student. He appeared to her in a mirror, and took her to a land of darkness and song. Away from the garish light of day she learned his music, the music of the night. But she was too curious, and saw his true face, a face that would forever haunt her mind. When the time came for her to return to her own world, she found her song powerful, and her memories of the other place were more terrible than wonderful. When her angel of music came again to teach her, she now resisted. Having seen his face, she could only draw back in fear.

                    Not all True Fae assume forms inspired by classical fairy tale symbols such as knights, witches and dragons. Some like to go for something more modern, and even imitate pop culture. One of these True Fae fancies himself a superhero straight from comic books. He used to have a whole City filled with changeling sidekicks, hobgoblin mutants and True Fae super villains. He used to think the entire universe revolved around him and his impeccable righteousness, even as threw temper tantrums, demanded to be the center of attention and refused to be questioned. However, he started visiting Earth more frequently, saw how complex things really were and how even humans could be heroes sometimes. He began to think of others as more than just playthings, eventually abandoned his City and freed his prisoners. With two Titles left and a Beast Windwing as his trusted sidekick, he set out to protect the innocent and defenseless, wherever they may be. He calls himself The Tick.

                    Virtue: Justice
                    Vice: Childish
                    Seeming: Ogre
                    Mien Blessings: Obdurate Skin (Stonebones), Sundering Talons (Render), Improvised Mayhem (Bloodbrute)

                    Attributes: Power 7, Finesse 4, Resistance 7

                    Mental Skills: Academics 2, Crafts 2, Computer 1, Medicine 1, Occult 2, Politics 0, Investigation 3, Science 1
                    Physical Skills: Athletics (Running, Jumping) 6, Brawl 6, Drive 0, Firearms 0, Larceny 2, Stealth 2, Survival 4, Weaponry (Improvised) 6
                    Social Skills: Animal Ken 1, Empathy 3, Expression (Speeches) 2, Intimidation (Crazy) 3, Persuasion (Friendliness) 3, Socialize 2, Streetwise 1, Subterfuge 2

                    Merits: Ambidextrous, Quick Draw, Disarm, Demolisher 3, Aggressive Striking 4, Hedge Duelist 5, Heavy Weapons (All Weapons) 5, Danger Sense, Direction Sense, Striking Looks +2, Fast Reflexes 3, Fleet of Foot 3, Iron Stamina 3, Harvest (Pledges 5, Goblin Fruit 5), Hollow 7 (Size 2, Amenities 3, Garden 2), Lethal Mien, Charmed Life, Armored Mien 1/1, Acute Mien Sense (Hearing) 2, Golem’s Palate, True Friend (Arthur)

                    Wyrd: 7
                    Contracts: Stone 5, Separation 5, Forge 5, Oath And Punishment 5, Vainglory 3, Dream 2, Goblin (Delayed Harm, Diviner’s Madness, Fool’s Gold)
                    Max Glamour/Per Turn: 20/7
                    Frailties: any attack made against his antennae (major bane), cannot refuse an offer of food (minor taboo), cannot harm those he perceives as innocent (minor taboo)

                    Fae Aspects:

                    Immortal Flesh

                    Change of Heart: The True Fae are entities almost embodied by their passions and vices, with higher morality and self-denial little more than a whim to them. For them, passion is virtue, and the denial of passion an affectation. Their supernaturally amoral nature is reflected in an inverted ability to regain Willpower. They regain one Willpower point from indulging their Virtue, but refresh their whole pool when satiating their Vice.

                    The Tick, on the other hand, has spent enough time on the mortal world to absorb part of humanity’s nature. He can remain on Earth without suffering damage, regains Willpower normally, can harvest Glamour from selfless emotions, can create Hedgespun items and does not have the Skill limits of most True Fae. However, his mindset is still very much alien, and thus he has no Morality or Clarity meter, nor can his strange thought processes be represented by Derangements, as is the case with the other True Fae.

                    Titles: Guardian Of Justice (Actor), The Arthropod Aegis (Prop)

                        There is a legend among the Lost, of a great, swordsman who was born from death itself. From the age of 6 he was trained in the ways of swordsmanship and war, killing his first man before he was even 10 years old. The swordsman found comrades in arms, putting an end to a century of war and rising to the top of command among his new allies. Then it all went horribly wrong. The swordsman had a friend who longed to be king... so great was his desire that he would sacrifice his entire army to achieve his ambition. The swordsman and all his friends were dragged forcefully to Arcadia, where he saw them eaten alive by by the denizens of the that hellish place. The traitor joined the ranks of the True Fae, and though he suffered terrible injuries, the swordsman escaped.

                        Now he is out for blood, and will not rest until the man who betrayed them dies painfully. He carries a great iron sword, large enough to cleave a man and his horse in two with a single blow. Is is a member of the Summer Court, or a Changeling turned Hunter? Nobody is certain anymore; he does not go into battle with strategy in mind... he just goes berserk.

                            Most people either focus on being good or evil. But what happens if you're in between, or if one side just slightly outweighs the other? What do you think happens to those people when the meet their fate? Do they go the purgatory, to wander amidst limbo until they repent? Perhaps, but sometimes there is another option, a place for those who are only slightly more evil than righteous. Slightly more naughty, slightly more rebellious... slightly damned.

                            Technically it's not a level of hell. In fact, it's located outside the gates of hell, but within close enough proximity to be visited by those who reside there. Technically, yes, it does have demons that wander its land, but they're much less violent than the denizens of hell. In fact the only real tortures they perform includes bullying or pranks, so if your made of sterner stuff or have a broad sense of humor, they might actually be tolerable. But it's still a dull and dreary place, and the demons can get on people's nerves after a time. There is a way out, back to the world of the living... but it's located within the depths of hell itself. The choice is yours; do you assault the legions of hell in search of the portal back home, or do you stay here in this "ring of the slightly damned?"

                                    Inspired by the Fae version of Homestuck, I decided to come up with further details of the Fae-ifyng, Seemings/kiths for the Beta Kids and Trolls, plus stats for Lord English.

                                    Assume that the objects which contain the code for running Sburb are legendary Tokens. They allow changelings and mortals alike to bring pieces of the Hedge and Arcadia into Earth. Once your house is taken into the Hedge, it becomes a Hollow. From there, you explore the Hedge and earn Grist by defeating enemy hobgoblins and helping friendly hobgoblins. Grist serves as Glamour and GMC Experience Points, which you may use to create epic Hedgespun Tokens, sign Contracts and improve your Traits. When you defeat the Denizen (a powerful hobgoblin), you gain enough Grist to enter an Arcadian Realm in the form of a chess planet. There, you will temporarily have the powers of a True Fae, while an army of chess piece-like beings straight from your dreams take sides on a massive battle. The Light always loses, and its up to the players to defeat the Dark. If you win, you may return to Earth as a changeling, never being able to perfectly adjust to the mundane after what you went through. If the players lose, it's Game Over. The Arcadian Realm resets, the old players are sent to another Arcadian Realm and new players are selected. But if there is no True Fae, who is the Keeper of the players?

                                    The Wyrd itself.
