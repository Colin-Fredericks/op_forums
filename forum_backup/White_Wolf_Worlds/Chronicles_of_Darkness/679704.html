    Playable Artifical Intelligences

    09-27-2015, 02:24 AM
    Updated and revised, more emphasis given to an AI being information.


    Rules and Data



    Bolting Things Together
    Like stories about werewolves, vampires, and other supernatural things, Artificial Intelligences have a long history of fiction, ranging from the stories written by Asimov and the movies involving a gorilla wearing a diving helmet, to the more modern works such as Mass Effect which features many different types of AI with differing levels of Intelligence and self-awareness and Halo which uses Brain Uploading to create their AIs; AIs have proven over their relatively short history to be very diverse.
    But in creating a ruleset, some ground rules must be laid down to ensure there is little confusion and consistency within the rules, as well as giving the readers a firm grip on the assumptions the characters they play as.
    While many works of fiction depict AI learning at extremely prodigious rates, the AI used in this project are not as superhuman. AIs have to take time to learn, digest, and practice (requiring exp expenditures) like everybody else. Simply trying to learn about brain surgery via reading an article on wikipedia will work about as well as the average Joe attempting to do the same thing. Moreover, while AIs are not bound to a single form and able to travel where there is sufficient connection and capacity to hold them, they are vulnerable in that they need technology to exist.
    They are more powerful than normal humans in some respects, weaker in others.
    Obviously, lacking an organic body has advantages (such as no need to worry about poison gas) and disadvantages (Powerful enough EMPs can be lethal if they destroy the body’s electronics).
    sasa

    The Avatar
    An Artificial Intelligence is a software based entity that is not bound to any particular physical vessel, but they still require that the majority of their “self” be somewhere. The Avatar represents the “core” of the AI, a mass of self aware data. It exists within machines and is able to move from one network, node, or vessel to the next provided there is a big enough connection for it.
    AIs require a sufficiently advanced data network or computer to support their existence, without that, they perish. If whatever storage device that holds the AI’s Avatar is ever destroyed, then the AI also risks destruction and it is likely to happen, though most AI’s know how to flee decaying vessels.
    In order for the AI to know what happens outside of its data network, the AI must have access to a sensor of some sort, cameras, microphones, ect. It is effectively senseless to physical matters if whatever connection it has lacks the senses.
    When put inside a sufficiently technological machine such as a car with enough electronics, an AI (with the right permissions) might act as its driver while all other hands are focused on surviving a shootout. In other cases, it might implant itself in a perfect replica of a human body to act as a disguise while infiltrating a mob hideout or even try to control it via remote control.
    Whatever the case, an AI's options are typically limited to whatever Hardware it has available.


    Attributes, Skills, and Merits
    Mechanically, AIs posses the 6 of the 9 Attributes (Intelligence, Wits, Resolve, Presence Manipulation, Composure) rather than the 9 normal characters acquire, as AI are not bound to a body and thus lack the physical attributes but approach humans in all other respects. If it has a body it is bound to, is uses the vessel’s Strength as its own for all purposes. It also uses Intelligence in place of Dexterity for all purposes, representing how it uses advanced calculations and corrections to act; however, many uses of Intelligence where Dexterity would normally be used must count any device penalty (but not bonus) inherent to the body as a modifier to Dexterity.
    Otherwise, the AI uses its attributes the same as any human does, provided it has the means to.
    AI have Skills, but they need not be as well rounded as human characters. AI are rapidly put into service in a much shorter time span than a human takes to mature and often designed for a specific purpose. An AI bound to a car might not need skills in Brawl or Medicine or really, anything in the Social list whilst a medical assistant for the battlefield role might often had the equivalent of a PHD and excellent marksmanship skills. However, this often means AI know less total knowledge than most humans, the price for having such a freeform selection.
    AIs possess Merits, all of which can be applied or used as necessary as long as their current form allows for it (A Fighting style cannot be used when the current body is a car, unless it's a say Fighting style revolving around running people over with a car, also performing crafts is not likely to happen without hands or arms). AI may never select merits that are specific to a given body, such as Iron Stomach and Ambidextrous.

    Derived Traits
    As AIs are intelligent and self-aware beings somewhat modeled after humans, they possess the Willpower, Virtue, Vice and Integrity systems, though Integrity is somewhat modified. These systems are unchanged and function identically as they do to human characters.
    Initiative is Intelligence + Composure.
    AI’s do not have Speed, Size, or Defense unless they are in a body.
    Defense is the Lower or Intelligence and Wits plus a relevant Skill dependant on the body (usually defaults to Athletics or Drive) minus a body’s modifier penalties.

    AI’s also possess a Health trait, representing the inherent durability and resilience of an Avatar to cyberspace damage. Though an AI cannot be harmed directly by physical tools, its systems can get corrupted and disrupted by tainted software or misapplications of code.
    Unlike normal Health, an AI’s Health tracks wounds with different names to differentiate it from an “real” heal. It goes from. Minor to Severe to Critical (instead of Bashing, Lethal, or Aggravated).
    An AI’s Health is damaged via certain actions, such as being “hacked” or subjected to viruses or being harmed by other AI and the AI takes “wound penalties” as its coherence and self is corrupted and disrupted.
    If an AI’s Health track fills up all the way with Critical Damage, it dies. Damage decreases over time, healing as wounds do, with the right most damage being removed first. It takes 15 minutes to recover from Minor damage, a day to recover from Severe damage, and a week to replenish Critical damage.

    Cyberspace
    A Network is not really a single “place” per say. Rather, it is the sum total of all of the machines in a given network, a collection of various Nodes and routers, connecting to each other in a manner reminiscent of a roadway or a city. While AI’s can grow big and powerful (metaphorically speaking), their Avatars cannot seriously cover an entire modern data network. Instead, AI’s of all levels simply repeatedly transfer their Avatars from one Node in the Network to another Node or a device within range, with both access points being unrestricted and having a connection to each other. In most cases today, connections are wireless. As a general rule, Wireless range for suited for transfer is a short range distance of at most 50 meters depending on signal strength.
    While many AI’s would like to envision sending themselves up into space and appearing somewhere else in the world via satellite, AI’s cannot send their Avatars over such long distances and would more than likely die in the attempt due to the signal interference, the inherent danger of sending and decompiling one to space and so on.
    Another complication for accessing or moving through devices is that in order to interact with a device on a basic level, the AI needs permission to do so. If the AI lacks it, it must force a way in somehow, hacking the security of the Node to treat it as a guest or a user. Thankfully, most public data networks are well, public and do not need permission to move through them.

    While an AI may not be harmed by gunfire and plasma weaponry in Cyberspace, that does not mean that it has nothing to fear from physical enemies. An AI can be attacked by hackers that are able to access or reach the Node or device the AI’s Avatar is in from some local access point. In such a scenario as an instant action, a hacker (or even another) can roll Intelligence (or Prescence for an AI) + Computer skill, successes deal Severe damage to the AI. The hacker may choose to add his computer’s equipment bonus as a bonus to determine the attack roll or as bonus successes on a successful attack roll, representing the advantage having lots of processing power can do in cybercombat.
    Alternatively, a hacker or AI could instead attempt to restrain an AI, essentially a grapple using computers. In this, both perform a contested Intelligence + Computer roll to attempt to restrain or control the other.
    Should the AI allow its opponent to score 3 or more consecutive successes at holding the AI, it may be Quarantined, as the Tilt.



    Abilities of the Avatar
    As a creature of Cyberspace, an AI has special abilities inherent to it.
    Transfer An AI can attempt to transfer its Avatar from one Node or device to another within range. This is an AI’s movement action.
    Interface An AI can always attempt to use Presence + Computer to attempt to interact with a machine or software entity for things such as performing a diagnosis or perhaps to hack into it. As AI’s are inherently native to machinery, the way they interact with machines is different from that of a normal human.
    The device or software in question must be within the reach of the AI’s current location.
    This ability allows an AI to do Severe damage to another AI.




    Empty Shells
    As stated before, AIs require a physical vessel to exist in, but this isn't truly part of the AI. While Networks allow an AI to exist and function in computer systems, a physical body grants an AI the ability to interact in the physical world.
    What bodies an AI can inhabit however can be just as varied as the different types of AIs themselves. As an example, a military AI designed for use as a combatant could be seen in a barebones shell made of cheap plastic with the label of "cannon fodder", a personalized android body with realistic synthetic flesh, a main battle tank, or even a whole battleship can act as a server hub for many AIs.

    Regardless of category, an AI in the right body and given the right permissions to control it either as a driver and/or as an entity that controls some of its functions (such as say controlling the machine guns on a tank while the human crew did everything else). It might even decide to take control of a combat mech and act as a walking tank all on its own.
    It gains the sensors of its body and uses it as its own senses and the AI uses its attributes and skills to determine dice pools, dependant on type and context of the body.
    If the vessel is humanoid or beastial, it may use Brawl, Athletics, Weaponry, and Fighting Styles as a normal human.
    If the vessel is more of a vehicle. It uses Drive for these things, but may not apply most humanocentric fighting styles and its options are more limited by its vessel.




    Living Steel
    An AI is alive. It can die. Despite being a software entity, an AI is still bound to the hardware it is located on. Destroy or cripple the hardware and you do the same to the AI. At any point the body the AI currently in is reduced to 0 Structure, there is a chance that the damage sustained would be enough to destroy the vital components that house the AI, in turn killing the AI.
    An EMP grenade has a good chance of disabling the body and damaging the circuitry and consequently the AI inside the machine, doing Severe or Critical damage to the AI as though the explosion was a frag grenade as far as damage is concerned.
    In general, assume that so long as the body has power, the AI need not worry about any needs. Should the body ever lack the power to run, both the AI and the body go into “Sleep Mode” and cannot wake until transfusions of power take place.
    AIs cannot store backup copies of themselves to avoid these destrution. Nor are they capable of being cloned.
    While it is true that AI’s possess many inconveniences and weakness that ordinary humans will never deal with, AI characters also possess many advantages. Namely, an immunity to suffocation, poison, disease, an telepathy that is not from a technomancer will not apply to an AI.


    Magic and Morality
    An AI’s morality is built very similarly to a human’s; after all, in many settings, human minds were used to piece together the thought mapping for modern AI. Though unlike humans, AI’s see the world somewhat differently. An AI is not wired with emotional responses or gut feelings, though many are given artificial emotions to go along with their logic. Still, that doesn’t mean they are infallible and always cold or logical. AI lean towards logic and dead emotions and thus treat most emotional Breaking Points as being less severe than what human would face. (perhaps +1 bonus to resist emotion derived breaking points)
    This difference in outlook though is a cold comfort against the Supernatural. The World of Darkness is a place full of twisted monsters that defy natural order and break reality just by existing. As machines and constructs made to understand and rationalize the world to better understand it, blatant violations of physics tend to hurt AI worse than humans (perhaps a -1 penalty to resist Supernatural encounter based breaking points)
    Even worse, is that sometimes the fact the AI is a construct might incline others to believe he is merely malfunctioning. An AI either lacking a soul (or not needing one) or not being subjected to the same ills as humans is able to witness and observe supernatural events without triggering the effects that would hurt on an ordinary human such as witnessing a Werewolf’s Lunacy without effect. That may not apply to his coworkers who remain convinced they just saw an ordinary wolf.
    Also, certain supernaturals may be hard for the AI to even notice. Vampires for instance pose unique problems for an AI character reliant on digital means of observation.




    Simple Breakdown of System

    AI:
    -Possess 6 Attributes (Intelligence, Wits, Resolve, Presence, Manipulation, Composure), which act more or less as normal, with the exception of Intelligence used for any place Dexterity is. All possess 1 dot in Character Creation, but the AI is only given 7 dots to distribute and does not select a Primary, Secondary, or Tertiary for Attribute allocation. He is free to allocate the starting dots as he wishes.
    -Possess skills in the same way a normal human or supernatural being would, representing both built in programming and learned experience being more limited. An AI has 17 skill dots to distribute freely and 3 Skill specialties.
    -Attributes and Skills may be raised using exp, as for a normal human.
    -Does not possess physical traits or may influence physical traits, other with potentially Skills.
    -Virtue, Vice, and Willpower are effectively the same.
    -Integrity is as normal human.
    -May Possess Mental and Social merits, and any Physical Merits that do not have requirements based on biological processes. (For instance, knowing Fighting styles is doable, Iron Stomach is not.)
    -Only one instance of the same AI can exist at any one time. No backup disks.
    -An AI has an Avatar which represent the placement of their self within the real world. The Avatar must always be in a body or storage system. If its storage system is completely destroyed, the AI is lost. The AI may move from one device or node to another that’s relatively close by enough to send itself through signals.
    -AI’s possess a Health measurement which tracks their damage in Cyberspace. Health is 10 + Composure.
    -An AI is always able to interact and force machines and software. It uses Presence + Computer to attempt to interface with machines.
    -Immune to disease, poison, suffocation, telepathy that does not account for machines..
    Experience Point Costs
    4 Attribute
    2 Skill
    1 Merit


    Vessels:
    -May range anywhere from a swarm of smaller networked machines, a single android body, a vehicle, or a shared server between multiple AIs. Potentially all of the above. The below assumptions assume a single body.
    -When the AI inhabits a body and is able to move, it may essentially use the body’s traits as its own, sensory, Strength, Speed, Durability, Size, ect.
    -In a mobile platform, Defense is the lower of Intelligence and Wits plus a relevant skill (Athletics or Drive in most cases) minus device penalties of the body.
    -The body’s equipment rating affects checks that normally use Dexterity such as uses of firearms or drive.




    Character Creation

    Given the above information, the guide below will simply show you how to create an AI character. Any steps that are simple enough or remain mostly unchanged will be omitted.
    These rules focus on the AI, not on a personal body the AI inhabits (AI’s are perfectly capable of following the group along using their cellphones or moving through the city’s internet grid). For personal bodies, the AI might be interested in robotics, vehicles, or even Mechs.

    Step 1 and 2: Concept and Anchors Design the character’s concept and motivations.
    Step 3: Determine Attributes.
    Similar to the normal process, but an AI only has 6 Attributes (Intelligence, Wits, Resolve, Prescence, Manipulation, Composure) starting at 1 dot each. The AI receives 7 additional dots to distribute into any of these traits.
    [i]Step 4: Determine Skills.[i]
    Only 17 skill points to spend, but they may be spent in any fashion. There are no Primary, Secondary, or Tertiary skill sets.
    Step 5: Add Supernatural Template
    Optional with Storyteller's permission. Not really recommended, but can lead to interesting games… such as for instance, a Thyrus Mage AI would bring up interesting questions on the metaphysics of Mage.
    Step 6: Select Merits.
    AI Characters can learn nearly any Merit normally available to a normal human with the proper prerequisites.
    Mental and Social merits can be learned so long as the AI can achieve the requirements if it substitutes Power for Intelligence and Presence, Finesse for Wits and Manipulation, and Resilience for Resolve and Composure.
    Despite lacking a true physical form, certain Physical Merits can be "learned" (provided the requirements for Mental and Social Attributes and skills are met), including Fighting Styles, so long as it is something that is "learned", meaning Striking Looks cannot be added to the AI itself.
    Step 7: Determine Advantages.
    Because an AI doesn't have a physical form, AI do not have many physical advantages. These are instead determined by the body the AI uses.
    Willpower is equal to Resolve + Composure.
    Integrity is 7.
    Health is 10 + Composure
    Initiative Modifier is Intelligence + Composure.
    Defense is the lower of Intelligence or Dexterity, plus Athletics or Drive, modified by penalties inherent to the body the AI is in.
    AI’s do not have inherent Speed or Size traits.

    Step 8: Spark of Life.
    In this case, literally. The AI was born or created, someway or somehow. Questions have to be raised about how and why the AI was formed in the first place and how it has or can evolve. Are you a newly formed consciousness that seeks to learn about the world or are you a jaded ancient machine that has seen too much? Do you have memories of a different life, a human life which is now denied to you or are they just the remains of a different person? Are you normal amongst your kind or are you something greater than them? What is your relationship with your "parents"?



    Merits


    AI Merits:
    These merits below represent specific mechanics and advantages unique to AI’s and uncommon even among them.


    Server Home(Special; Safe Place; · to ·····)
    The AI is in possession of a Server Home that serves as its primary resting spot and a secure space. While the physical space occupied by a server can vary, as a general rule, the Server Home is difficult to move in the physical plane and for the most part should be immobile under normal circumstances.
    The Server Home is defended by Firewalls, information booby traps and other defensive features to ward off intruders and aid in the AI’s protection.
    While in a Server Home, the AI imposes this merit’s dot rating as a penalty to foes attempting to attack it and any foe attempting to access or enter it without permission suffers this merit’s dot rating as a penalty.
    A Server Home may be shared between multiple AI’s, all of them huddling into the same closed network. Multiple AIs may spend merit dots to purchase a Server Home. It may also be merged with the Safe Place merit for having a defense on both the physical or digital planes.

    Quantum Observation Methods (··)
    Due to a unique quirk in the AI’s coding and nature or perhaps even due to special circumstances of creation, the AI is fully able to observe Supernatural occurrences via electronic means with perfect clarity so long as the feed is “live”. Effects that would normally render cameras, recorders, and other means useless for the purposes of observation simply do not impede the AI unless it’s a deliberate veiling action or Supernatural power. Simply put “passive” defenses fail to work.
    This merit only applies to the AI’s observations. This does not affect the recordings made by these devices and should the AI replay recorded supernatural phenomena would be obscured once again.


    Walkabout (Special; · to ·····)
    The AI is in possession of a unique "anthroform” construct, resembling a human in shape. The physical appearance of the construct is up to the Storyteller and the AI’s player. It is perfectly acceptable for the AI player to have a humanlike construct that can fool most people in every day at a glance circumstances or to forgo that and have a perfectly mechanical servitor. Whatever option the player goes for must be decided when the AI character purchases this merit.
    The dot rating determines the overall quality of the machine.
    Due to their simulated human physiques, Walkabouts use Health levels even though they are machines and possess Durability and speed is handled as normal for a human character. Walkabouts are wireless enabled though they can activate or deactivate this as an Instant action.

    The Walkabout’s dot rating influences how powerful it is.
    -trength is equal to dots
    -Durability is half dots rounded up
    -Speed is equal to 10 + dots times 2
    -Health is equal to Size + dots
    -Size is usually 5
    AI players are free to purchase multiple Walkabouts.


    Server (Special; · to ·····)
    The AI is in possession of a Server that is attached to a vehicle or building. The AI must be in possession or have access (or in the employ of someone who does) to a preexisting vehicle or building before this merit may be bought and applied to the potential body. The number of merit dots spent dictates the size of the object or structure in question or how much of the structure that can be controlled via an AI. As an added bonus, this can be added as an option for "Safe House" merits and the price of which can be shared by multiple people. The dot rating of this merit cannot "go over" what the appropriate size listing of the object.
    Example based on size:
    · A car or a 1 room small shack.
    ·· A large land vehicle such as a tank or a small house.
    ··· A school bus or large home.
    ···· A barge or a mansion
    ····· A really large vessel, such as a battleship or an aircraft carrier or a really large and elaborate underground base.

    Assist Tactnet (···)
    AI with this merit are able to better influence the physical world by helping others perform tasks, provided there is a network connection or a digital device involved, even going so far as being the “actual” leader of a task. The AI can perform Teamwork actions that normally require physical appearance, so long as he has a means of communicating and coordinating with the team. He may be the “Primary actor” of the task with good enough reason.





    Tilts and Conditions

    AIs may not receive physical damage, but they still can suffer or become impaired.
    Tilts

    Fragmented (AI)
    Your AI is unable to rationalize or conceive actions due to significant damage in his Logic Matrix.
    Effect: Your AI misses its next turn.
    Causing the Tilt: Being hit with an EMP, taking double your Composure in damage.
    Ending the Tilt: The effects of thits tilt normally last for a single turn though it can last longer in some circumstances


    Quarantined (AI)
    Your AI has been locked under a series of Firewalls and security that is intended to keep it from escaping.
    Effect: The AI is effectively immobilized, blind, and has limited actions. It is locked in its current device or Node and is unaware of anything happening around it save for the wall of code that exist to keep it so.
    Causing the Tilt: Losing a datagrapple against a hacker or another AI is one of the most common ways to be Quarantined.
    Ending the Tilt: An AI cannot easily break a Quarantine on its own .Every hour, an AI may attempt to interface against the Quarantine, only breaking free once it gains more successes than what has been invested into the Quarantine multiplied by 3.


    Disrupted (AI)
    Your AI’s code has taken a massive hit and its ability to interface with machines has been significantly impeded if not outright removed.
    Effect: You suffer a -3 penalty to all Interface actions.
    Causing the Tilt: Taking more Severe damage than you have dots in Composure when subjected to a targeted attack (-2 penalty) often causes it.
    Ending the Tilt: Recovering the Severe damage ends this Tilt.



    Conditions


    Property (AI)
    The AI is considered, regardless of what the law says, as someone else’s property who insists on the AI being his and in an intrusive way. Going against this person is not easy and law or social contract heavily restricts the opposing him in the first place.
    Resolution: Getting emancipation or finding a way to permanently free oneself from this master.
    Beat: The owner impedes activities and causes complications with his involvement.

      I like your write up. Amechra did a similar one for AIs as a new category of Ephemeral Beings. The main difference I noticed is that his version is more touched by the supernatural, with the ability to use hold Essence, use Numina and such. His version doesn't track Experiences, either. Your version seems more grounded in realism and focused on character development. I would love to see a combination of the two versions.

      A few suggestions and reminders:

      Their willpower should be capped at 10. Some supernatural powers that alter minds may have the target's Willpower as the required number of successes, so rolling to reprogram a Rank 5 AI with Willpower 30 would be repetitive and likely tedious. This is mostly true in 1ED, as the rules for extended actions are different in 2ED. The target number of successes might just anywhere from 10 to 20.

      Their way of handling Integrity should be more machine-like, that is, performing actions that go against or jeopardize their purpose should be a Breaking Point for them, deliberately installed as an obedience mechanism by their programmers. High Integrity AIs are more focused and mostly devoid of human quirks, flaws and mannerisms (but since the law requires all of them to follow something like the Three Laws Of Robotics, they don't become as distant and ruthless as God-Machine Angels). Low Integrity AIs might be rebellious, stretch the limits of their purpose more fiercely, be less predictable and display unusual desires, all of which might make them seem more human. However, if they interact more often and closely with humans, their worldview and self-image might broaden and their Integrity will start working like that of a human. Viruses and targeted hacking may also damage their Integrity, like the Death Arcanum can damage someone's Integrity in 2ED Mage. Since AIs don't have souls, the Conditions they suffer after dropping to Integrity 0 will have names and effects that are more thematically fitting, like Corrupted Mainframe and Disobedience.

      The Ephemeral Attribute that stands in for Resolve, Stamina and Composure is called Resistance, not Resilience. The latter is the name of a Vampire Discipline.

      If their Durability works like Stamina for the purposes of determining their "Health" (Structure in this case), I assume their actual Stamina depends on the body they are inhabiting. A humanoid body can have anything from 1 to 5 Stamina for foot chases, while vehicles probably just use Finesse + Drive + Handling bonus to chase someone down. Can they have Armor on top of their Durability?

      If they don't use Rank, what else might give them Supernatural Resistance and limit their permanent Attributes and Skills? I'm considering a minimalist Powerstat called Source, as in "source code". They might be able to use it as a dice bonus on Mental Rolls by spending Willpower and/or Essence, and sophisticated AIs with high Source can have Merits that make them all but flawless at certain tasks. This Powerstat would be an out of character term, though. Their creators do have an idea of how advanced the AI is, and they can compare one AI to another in that regard, but they don't think of it as a 1 to 10 scale.

          Well, the goal was literally to make AI a valid playable archetype, hence the name "playable AI".

          Actually, your post reminded me of about a few things I hadn't addressed, these notes came from 2 years ago at least and they weren't finished then and this was back before I fully got used to 2e Rules. Thanks for reminding me and informing me of existing typos and errors.

          AI do not have ranks like other Empherial beings. This is kind of intentional since the goal was to have them work in more human terms. Normal AI attributes should probably cap out at 5, the maximum for humans and thus their attribute and skill limits mentally and socially be considered in relation to humans. Post Human AIs would need to be handled somehow though. Probably a "label" rather than a "rank" system. This also means unless the AI is past the "post human" threshold, he won't be getting Willpower above 10.

          Your point on Integrity is very appreciated. I'll work on a replacement trait called "Stability" and defining how it works.

Consider the shenanigans neccesary for that sort of thing to happen, such as Awakened Computer systems or even an Arcadian twisted machine. This would allow them to advanced to "post human" status. Alternatively, brain uploading is involved... along with some nutty soul transfers.

          Obviously, not all supernatural templates would be valid for it, but the point still stands that something weird has happened for a machine to suddenly exhibit supernatural powers.
