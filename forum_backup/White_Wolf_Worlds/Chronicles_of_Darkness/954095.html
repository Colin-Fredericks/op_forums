    Crossover Settings [Just For Fun]

    08-19-2016, 07:39 PM
    In honor for the announced Crossover Chronicles, I was thinking that making a thread about taking an existing example setting for one gameline and trying to establish in it the presence of other supernaturals could make a fun thought experiment. In order to make it a bit more interesting, I was thinking of making it into some sort of forums game- every poster would choose a certain example setting which is already given in the books (preferably 2E) and a gameline they are interest at seeing, and the next poster would write down a short paragraph about the desired setting and gameline (blue book templates are also allowed). For example, let's say the asked setting was Vampire's Montreal, and the desired gameline was Hunter-

    Montreal is a fascinating place in the eyes of the vigilantes, especially among those who specialize at hunting bloodsuckers. Usually, vampires seem to have a sophisticate culture with a well defined political system. Montreal's vampires, however, seem to live in a constant chaos- there is no reason in how they operate, as they never seem to work in large groups or uphold their twisted version of feudal system. According to the records of a local Loyalists cell, the change in behavior has started around 80 years ago, as some sort of wreck moved through the local vampires, until their society was shaped into its current state. While the cause for that change isn't well known, most of the local hunters do not care about it- instead preferring to exploit the constant paranoia and strange behavior to their advantage.

    The Cainte Heresy seem to be very active in the city, making ambushes for the small vampire coteries as they attack from the shadows and vanish just as quickly, leaving behind only their infamous sign. The Malleus Maleficarum, however, are strangely absent from the city- and some whisper rumors about that, for some reason, the blessings of their Saints simply stop working in Montreal's borders. The Lucifuge, on the other hand, show a great interest in the city, maintaining constant presence. They say that some of their members had visions about looming danger- God has forsaken Montreal, they say, and the devil took hold over it. Most of the hunter command centers and safe houses seem to be focused around Saint Paul Street, as according to the research of the Loyalists the vampires seem to avoid that place in the city for some reason.

    While most of the hunters in Montreal simply exploit the existing state, few hunter cells dedicate themselves to research the cause for the strange behavior- especially among the Loyalists and the Null Mysteriis. In fact, the later claim that the radical change in the social structure of the bloodsuckers has to mean one thing- that the vampires are no longer the apex predator in the city, and that they recognize a new alpha in the "local ecology". While that explanation is backed up by what information the Loyalists has managed to gather, it doesn't explain why the vampires simply don't leave the city- and besides, what kind of monster can be terrible enough to scare the whole vampire population?
    and that's it! And as for the next setting- what do you think about Beast in Los Angeles?

    (while the preferred state is expanding a requested setting, if someone feels inspired they may write a new setting on their own, or expand an already written setting. Just remember to place your own request for a new setting)

      The beasts of Los Angeles have found that they can, very rarely, merge with their Horror in the physical world without being an incarnate. Not all of the begotten can do it, and the few who can are all known to have inspired famous movie monsters via feeding on Hollywood's creative types, though some claim to have been shot to fame as part of a deal with a mysterious "producer". An interesting side effect of this is that the heroes in LA almost always attempt to slay their pray using the same methods the fictional movie heroes used to slay the movie version of their target, which often fails due to the Begotten having seen those movies too, and noticed all the plot holes. Their kin among the magi claim this has something to do with the borders between the Astral, of which the primordial dream is a part, and the physical world being "fuzzy" in LA.

      As per usual, the Begotten don't really understand what the Unchained of LA are up to, but they seem to have a lot on their plate.

          Los Angeles occupies a unique niche in the modern human subconsciousness, a sort of materialistic heaven where attractive people mingle and socialize and go mad in the unique way that celebrities do. The backlots of Hollywood and the blurry backdrops of tabloid photos alike do more to shape the perception of the universe more than the rest of reality put together, and that much focus on the false and the hyper-real allows the barrier between dreams and the waking world to thin. Yet for every bit of hope, every one-in-a-million breakout success, there are countless shattered dreams, taking the shape of stick-thin models, shuttered restaraunts, hungy immigrant families, and failed actors.

          Los Angeles is a hungry city, and she eats people up.

          There are no shortage of lessons to be taught in Los Angeles, and the Begotten that cling to that philosophy find especially easy targets for the all too vital expression of hubris; reach for the impossible, and you're doomed to fall short. While the Beasts that let loose and prey rampantly on whoever strikes their fancy, the smart ones among them realize there's far more Satiety to be found in one star reviews and rejected talent applications than in more brute applications of force and fear; feed smarter, not harder. Uniquely in this city is the fragile membrane beyond the physical world and the Dream, allowing easier access to Lairs and increasingly rowdy Horrors than nearly anywhere else. It's all too easy to lose control and gorge on the many desperate people of Los Angeles, leaving a trail of devastation in your wake.

          Some conspiracy theorists among the Children wonder if the Black Dahlia was slaughtered by an over-eager Beast, and whisper about Charlie Manson as a masterful Hero who gathered a rabid cult about him.
          I'd kill to see what the Arisen and their servants bring to Malta, fantasticly detailed in The Pack. If that's too much for folks, then how about the Changelings of London?

                      well, I do really like the Malta setting, so I'll give it a shot. Hope you'll like it!

                      Since Sothis called the Deathless from Duat, many among the Arisen found themselves drawn to the islands. With its role as trading hub for both mundane and supernatural agencies, many relics has found their way to the land as merchandise to be traded upon or hide beneath the waves as the ships carrying them sank to the depths of the sea. In fact, either because of Malta's occult nature or its place in maritime trade, many relics considered long lost seem to spontaneously show up on the shores, waiting to be collected by innocent collector or trader who in unaware of their curses. While the Deathless are, undoubtedly, powerful beings, it is not common for them to enter directly to Malta in search for their lost treasures- instead preferring to use their cults as agents on their behalf. There are two main reasons for that tactic- the first is the respect for the laws of the land, as Malta's shifting guardians fight against any potential threat for the delicate peace of the islands, which is a nuisance most of the Arisen would prefer to avoid. The second, and more important factor, is Malta's own weird nature- especially when connected to the dead.

                      Once a mummy gets to Malta's shore, she can feel as if something is pulling her down- the presence of the Judges becomes much closer, and the light of the stars grows distant. They find the Descent moving faster, and it becomes harder for them to awake from their deathless sleep on Malta's soil. Still, there are times where negotiations between cults go wary or events which require's the Arisen's personal touch (not to mention the interest the Shepherds of the Shell show in the ancient Neolithic structures which contain necromatic secrets from before the time of Irem)- and those are times which require some delicate work from the side of the mummy, or else all hell would break loos and they have the Moon's bastards chasing their hills out of Malta.

                      Still, there are advantages for the werewolves' constant presence- their constant hunt after the unquiet dead has pretty much cleaned any influence of the Devourer and her spawn out of the islands, and the secrets the ghosts hold could benefit not only the Uratha, but are also useful for the Deathless. The ghost lords know the greatest of those secrets- although their madness makes it almost impossible to gain no more than scraps of information from them. Because of the laws against direct conflict and neutrality, many mummies use Malta as a place to solve disputes through their cults in a more peaceful way, using trade and influence as a weapon through their cults, while they by themselves sit in the other side of the world. What is unknown for most of the Arisen is that years ago the Deceived has made Malta their fortress, operating their own cults in order to find signs of Arisen presence. Their cults control much of the relic-exchange market, and they turn one cult against the other n order to lure their masters to the land- before they bury them in it.

                              My attempt at a Mage crossover for the Bangkok setting.

                              Mage -- The Kingmakers [Bangkok, Thailand]
                              For Thailand's Awakened, Bangkok holds a greatly coveted Mystery that's tied to an ongoing game of thrones. And those who pursue it clash fiercely and frequently with each other as they work to influence not only the city but also its spiritual reflection in the Shadow. Indeed, the Mages are keenly aware of what is taking place beyond the Gauntlet. They know that the yaksha are working to restore the ancient hierarchy of spirits that once stood in the days of Ayutthaya, and that they feud among themselves over choosing which of their spirit allies should ascend to the throne. Yet their undertaking is beset by threats ranging from a savage sect of werewolves marching down from the Burmese refugee camps to an ancient and insane spirit that plots its own rise to power at Pak Kret.

                              And as the great game carries on, many Mages find themselves counted among its most unpredictable of players.

                              Bangkok's Mages keep occult records which survived Ayutthaya's fall to the Burmese. Many tell of old agreements with the yaksha which contributed to the harmony of the spirit courts in that era. With the Tribes working to usher in a new golden age, some of these old pacts have been renewed. And a number of local packs often call on the services of the Awakened (even count a few among their number) in their efforts to push the claims of their chosen contender for the throne. Others, however, limit their dealings with them out of suspicion, believing that the Mages might tear down what they have built in their hubris.

                              Such concerns are not wholly unfounded. Many among the Silver Ladder and the Seers of the Throne have come to see the throne of Bangkok's Shadow as crucial to their respective spheres of influence. And both groups are quietly building up their resources to support spirit nobles malleable to their causes. To that end, some cabals and pylons have opened negotiations with a few spirit courts. Others, though, have opted to use their magic to create their own allies among the Shadow's denizens. These Mages have managed to keep their plans for the Golden Throne a secret from their peers, but a single mistake from any of them could quickly cause strife between their Orders and the yaksha.

                                  Beast -- Rise of the Firebird [Phoenix, Arizona]
                                  For the past few years, America's Begotten have kept a low profile whenever they find themselves in Phoenix. Sheriff Brown's vendetta may be focused on their Promethean cousins, but there have been occasions when a Child of the Dark Mother has ended up on his hit list. That some of the local Heroes are counted among his deputies only worsens matters. However, one particular Beast has set out to tear down this status quo.

                                  A member of the city council has begun a smear campaign against the Bulldog. He ruthlessly criticizes the sherrif's methods, calling him out not only on his brutality, but also in his incompetence. He argues fervently over how the crime in the city has grown in the last twenty years in spite of Brown's 'tough justice', and warns that things will only get worse unless he is sacked from his position.

                                  Unknown to most people, though, the man happens to be a Beast. An Ugallu with a Hunger for Punishment, he grew to be a thorn in the sheriff's side after Brown and his deputies killed a throng whose members he considered as close kin. This Child of the Dark Mother has since placed himself as a protector for many Created whose Pilgrimages take them through Phoenix, and takes every opportunity to satisfy his Hunger on any of Brown's men.

                                  The Raptor's actions have slowly gathered supporters from the city's mundane and supernatural residents. And they have gradually set him on the path to his Inheritance. Should he succeed in his efforts, Phoenix may very well see a Beast Incarnate emerge from the ashes of Brown's disgrace. The Bulldog, however, will not go down without a fight. And others may end up standing in the Ugallu's way.

                                      Vampire -- Scholars of the Dead [Salamanca, Spain]
                                      Salamanca is an old city, and its shadows hide more than just the mystical endeavors of its Awakened residents. Some are drawn to the same Mysteries that attract Mages from other parts of the country (and, in some cases, the world). Others lie at the root of (or indirectly contribute to) the existence of such phenomena. But like their mortal counterparts, the city's supernaturals favor a (relatively) quiet lifestyle.

                                      If there is any one covenant that thrives among the city's Kindred, it would be the Ordo Dracul. The Dragons frequently deal with the Consilium over the course of their own studies, and the local Kogaion often debates with its leaders over who lays claim to a given Mystery. Unsurprisingly, the greatest disputes have been over the efimeros books and the efimera worlds. Many Defiant have expressed interest in those particular phenomena at any given point in time, and some are quite willing to circumvent their restrictions in order to conduct their own studies. A few even maintain secret ties with the handful of Seers in the city, collaborating with them in order to get what they want.

                                      Aside from the Dragons, the Sanctified also hold a strong presence. Some routinely explore the city center in search of inscriptions and patterns which hold the secrets of new Theban Miracles. Others congregate in a hidden retreat, where they refine their rites and learn to invoke them in new and more potent applications. Both groups are counted among the covenant's most proficient blood sorcerers, and their magic can potentially rival that of the Wise.

                                      The Acolytes are fewer in number compared to the Ordo Dracul and the Lancea et Sanctum. But they are no less fervent than them in their own search for knowledge. Salamanca's legends and myths draws in a quaint number of the Cirle's followers, and many are drawn to find the cave where the devil supposedly instructed seven mortals in the use of dark magic. Was this devil an avatar of the Crone? Or perhaps it was something worse? Something that flies through the night on wings of smoke?

                                          Promethean -- The Cradle of Fire [Malta]
                                          If there is one group of supernaturals that Malta's Uratha seldom tolerate for long, it is the Created. Their mere presence slowly corrupts the island and its people, and are potential catalysts to any number of unnatural calamities. Yet despite the risks they pose, they are allowed to stay so long as they follow the rules.

                                          Indeed, Prometheans frequently visit Malta. Many of the Nepri and Riven are driven by both the Elpis and their own affinity for death to investigate the local ghosts and tombs. And students of Aurum and Argentum mingle respectively with the passing humans and supernaturals in order to gain further insight into the mortal condition. Even those of other Lineages and Refinements find it worthwhile to visit the islands. A large collection of Athanors, pilgrim marks, and written Rambles of prior Created visitors lie scattered and hidden, and offer potential insights into the Great Work if found.

                                          Some Prometheans, though, regard Malta with great significance because they were born there.

                                          A Created who arrives in Malta will immediately feel the Divine Fire's presence. Pyros and Flux are in abundance, and they exist in such great amounts that they not only make Wastelands more debilitating, but the slightest fluctuation of either can quickly lead to a Firestorm. This potential hazard discourages one from channeling Pyros indiscriminately, as even a newly formed Wasteland can suddenly be consumed by the full force of the Divine Fire.

                                          Malta's reservoir of Pyros and Flux also has an effect on the generative act. Prometheans who perform it on the islands find it more likely to succeed, and those whose Azoth burns strongly do not even need to draw (much) on their reserves of Pyros to give life to their progeny. However, on the off chance that it fails, a would-be genitor can end up causing an outbreak of Pandorans. While many Prometheans have used this phenomenon to propagate their respective Lineages, a number of Extempore have been known to manifest from the Firestorms that periodically form. These Matchless are either killed or driven out by the Uratha not long after they appear, and only a fraction of those sent away ever manage to make it across the sea.

                                          It is little wonder that the Maltese packs tend to be so wary of Prometheans. Thankfully, most follow the rules of their territory and move on once their business is concluded. If a given Created lingers too long for any reason, though, the Uratha step in to remove it one way or another. And if it happens to be one of the dreaded Centimani, they will quickly move to slay it.

                                          -+-

                                          • Whenever a Wasteland in Malta forms or Festers, roll for a Firestorm with the higher of the creator's or the Wasteland's effective Azoth. This roll suffers a penalty equal to [6 - the category of the Wasteland]. If a Wasteland reaches the fifth stage, roll twice for the Firestorm's strength.
                                          • Prometheans who perform the Generative Act on Malta have an initial dice pool equal to their Azoth. Furthermore, all penalties to the roll are halved (rounded up) to a minimum of -1.
                                          • The dice pool for Pandoran Creation is twice the genitor's Azoth. Centimani who spend Willpower add +5 to the roll.
                                          • Active Pandorans on Malta absorb a point of ambient Pyros every [6 - Rank] days. Sublimati avail of this benefit at half the time.

                                              Promethean -- The Den of Freaks [Ciudad Juarez, Mexico]
                                              Prometheans find few refuges in Ciudad Juarez. Los Señores de Frontera are the foremost supernatural presence in the city, and they (and other local packs) do not take kindly to Created who wreak havoc within its borders. This has led to many Pilgrimages coming to an abrupt and violent end as Disquiet and/or the Wasteland marks them for death by Siskur-Dah, and survivors of those hunts have left behind Rambles and pilgrim marks to turn away any Promethean who is passing throug the area. Unfortunately, these warnings do not dissuade them all.

                                              Rumors among the Created claim that a number of Vitriol Founts are strewn throughout Juarez. These Athanors yield Vitriol in greater amounts than those of founts found elsewhere, and whoever stumbles upon them can perhaps harvest enough to successfully reach the New Dawn. Many Prometheans who hear these stories quickly make a beeline for the city. None are ever heard from again.

                                              Most would assume that these intrepid Created fell victim to the werewolves. However, they are not the only ones responsible. Juarez is home to an alchemist guild, and its members do possess this fabled cache of Vitriol Founts. While not as numerous as the rumors say, their collective Azothic Radiance has lured in more than a few Prometheans who ended up being harvested for materials and reagents. The guild remains a secretive group in the city, and its victims have been killed quickly and quietly to avoid drawing the werewolves' attention.

                                              Unfortunately, the rumors spread by the alchemists may very well have backfired on them. A Centimanus is making his way to the city, and he has a large pack of Pandorans at his side. The Freak plans on claiming the Athanors for himself, and intends to use them to evolve his pets. If he reaches Juarez, he will not only be hunted by every one of its resident werewolves, but also tip them off on more elusive prey in their territory.
