                    Yeah, the transformation of essence to mana from a Familiar is Lasting for the same reason most spell effects are Lasting, it’s a secondary effect rather than the point of the spell.

                    As for energy or Matter converted to mana that’s subsequently consumed, I’d say that’s gone for simplicity’s sake, but it could slowly lead to molecular build ups or fevers or something after the spell would have expired. Probably nothing immediately harmful, but requiring monitoring.
