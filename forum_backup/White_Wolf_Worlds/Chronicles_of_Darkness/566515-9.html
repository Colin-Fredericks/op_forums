        And, no, not only is Going Loud a very bad idea for a Demon (plus, lets not ignore that we have regular demon form or partial transformation!), but Demon Form does come with a series of Conditions that cause the demon in question to run out of control. Or start leaking aether. Or various other problems.

        Demon is a spy game for a reason. Doing this stuff carries a lot of risk for the Unchained with little pay out.

        Originally posted by nofather View Post

        Getting "relevant" spirits from outside your territory that you've not already established relationships with is not an easy thing.
