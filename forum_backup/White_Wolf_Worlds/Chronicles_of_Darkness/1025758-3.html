                                                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                                                            Basically, my biggest crude with those guidelines is this - Rank 2 ghosts should be more violent and low Integrity, Rank 1 ghosts should be more stable - to make the typical 'ghost stories' more probable. Because now, with those guidelines, it looks like you almost do not get any violent Rank 2 ghosts - and those that should be violent are only 'will-o-whisps' and cold spots Rank 1.
                                                            Few things to say here.

                                                            1) Not that the Chronicles world is the real world, but the vast majority of ghost stories people have are of ghosts actually being benign, generally having the worst of their experience just being the surprise of the experience. The Chronicles world does indeed darken that, but that just as often means that these benign ghosts have ways of making themselves known that just happen to be alarming. On that note,

                                                            2) A vast majority of the violence in ghost stories over the course of years(millenia, really) does not come from a violent, murderous disposition held by the figure in life, but is the result of being trapped with an unresolved fixation. These people are trapped between worlds, nailed there by some passion, a feeling that they can't let go off, a feeling that has not manifested, or has manifested but in a way that does not satisfy. That lack of catharsis, that lack of satisfaction, becomes all consuming, and the corresponding emotional reaction to that is frustration, manic-depressive mood swings, tension, anxiety-all reasons why a person would react violently. Ghosts have the problem of what happens when "If we pretend[read: be x] long enough, never giving up, it just might be who we are" is applied in a circumstances of limited space, infinite time, and a failure of resources create an emotional response that will probably be worn for as long as they fail, as those failures and emotions pile on them like blankets-or more accurately, shrouds.

                                                            We have a fixation on the murderer ghost now because it's a good story both as enjoyment and as cultural training tools, but that's particular stories against the wider backdrop. Speaking of-

                                                            3) While it's a mistake to assume that equilibrium is the natural state of any ecosystem-it's not, things ebb and flow according to minor devastations at all times with only moments of calm-there is generally a realm of acceptable chaos. Unless you are dealing with slice of life stories (Which I know for a fact you do not do), by and large this realm of "life as acceptable" is not where you tell your stories. You tell them where the equanimity breaks, when something breaches status quo for the characters. If we assume your reading is correct(It's not, though some credit must be given if for no other reason that non-underworld ghosts can have kingdoms too, even if they pale compared to the empires connected to the Below) and that by and large Rank 2 are generally more peaceful than Rank 1 ghosts and also have more power to enact their will to further that peace by subjugating the Manes, that just means it's all the more alarming when a Lemure acts out. They are a clear standing nail to deal with, which is the stuff of stories. The Equanimity is Broken, Fix It. And in relation to that-

                                                            (Also, I'm going to start calling them Manes and Lemures ala Awakening's things, because Rank 1 and Rank 2 is boring and stuffy)

                                                            4) Obsessive ghosts are well, good, and fine. Many a ghost story is satisfying dealing with a mindless, ravaging ghost. BUT that falls apart a little in longer form stories or games-you often see these more mindlessly aggressive types in what amounts to a one shot. Any longer going story tends towards more intelligent opponents, creatures that can use their power in more precise ways to facilitate designs and plots. Lemures being both more aware of themselves and more powerful while still obsessing over their lives means that the average surface dwelling ghost is, at any given point, a better antagonist for the sort of games people are playing than not. And on that mixture, let's talk about-

                                                            5) Integrity can more or less translate into "how well you keep yourself together", as many have pointed out already. That we have not had a high Integrity murderer in a book yet is sort of incidental to the fact that we just haven't had anyone interested in writing a high Integrity murderer in the places where we've had room for it, and that room is pretty sparse to begin with. Anyways, the point is that a High Integrity person keeps themselves together in the face of the traumatic by virtue of their "centeredness"-one way or another, they can keep from breaking by things they do and affirm themselves, while a Low Integrity person is much more prone to patching shit up with outside rituals and actions. These traits invariably give them less personal agency in life, and in death, when your passions and obsessions are the things that keep you from entropy, your willingness to surrender your identity to rituals and forms more or less who you are, but it also means you have little substance-you are literally a less substantial person as someone who is not self-possessive. In a game of ghosts, where substance is power, that means you're the bitch. And there are a lot of people who become the bitches of the world of the dead, because-

                                                            6) It is faulty to assume that the majority of the people in the world are Integrity 7 upon death. Sure, PC's start that way, and it is assumed to be the baselines for a lot of souls you deal with-but I personally don't find it unrealistic to assume that a lot of people Integrity 6, actually. Oh, sure, the majority aren't terrible, but I think a lot of people get broken in ways and just find small ways to handle it that has nothing to do with actual healing/self-actualizing and or -transcending. You don't have to do bad things to be low-Integrity, or even take a hit, you just have to have things you can't handle, and that happens to a lot of people. It's kind of mundane, how damage of that sort happens. And speaking of mundane-

                                                            7) There's this odd position that just because Lemures might be more populous than Manes means that the canon is violated because it posits that the terrifying spectres of lore are rare but "how can that be if the current relation between Lemures and Manes indicates that more powerful and narratively violent creatures are more populous, increasing as you go up the scale" which is not only bad statistics and failing to account that Rank 3 upwards often maintain in the Underworld and their presence is definitely an uproar-all of that ignores that Rank 2 anythings are basically the equivalent of beginning PCs. A Lemure is nowhere near the rank "Legendary and Terrifying Spectres of Lore". Don't blow it out of proportion.

                                                            So, no, things are as it should be. The only cause of disquiet is your own handling of the affair.
