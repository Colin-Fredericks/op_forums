    The Invictus Have The Best Servants

    01-06-2016, 04:14 PM
    The Invictus are the best at Ghouls. The Vassal of an Oath doesn't have to be a Vampire, and some Oaths are stupidly good for Ghouls.

    Oath of Action: Very risky... but an excellent way of boosting a Ghoul. It also lets you share any Discipline you have, so you could theoretically hand a Ghoul Blood Sorcery if you belong to the right Bloodline.

    Oath of Fealty: Requires exceptional circumstances (you do need Invictus Status •), but it's the holy grail of Blood Oaths for a Ghoul. It gives them a Vitae source that has no chance for Vitae Addiction or the Vinculum. Sure, it means their Liege automatically knows whether or not they are lying to them, but that's more-or-less fine. It's also good on the Vampire's end, since the Oath of Fealty is straight-up better than pretty much any other source of Vitae I can think of (as far as Ghouls are concerned.)

    Oath of Penance: Great for the Ghoul... terrible for you. There's a niche use with regards to dumping this on an ally's Ghoul, however. In exceptional (read: story related) circumstances, having the Ghoul be the liege and being the vassal can be heavily beneficial. Heavily.

    Oath of Serfdom: Hoo-boy. Give one of your Ghouls a "feeding territory" to take care of, and get a loyal watchdog that gets a free dot of a Physical Discipline when defending you. It does run into the issue of Ghouls not being able to use the Feeding Grounds merit, but...

    Oath of the Safe Word: Do you trust your Ghoul? I mean really trust them? Share your Invictus Status and Willpower with them. Then get them to swear an Oath of Fealty to you. This has the potential to go very wrong, but it can also go very right.

    The "dream set-up" is Oath of the Safe Word + Oath of Fealty + Oath of Action + Reversed Oath of Penance + Reversed Oath of Fealty. Assuming you have Invictus Status •••, that gives...

    For The Ghoul:
    - Invictus Status ••• (shared via Oath of the Safe Word).
    - Can draw up to three Vitae from you a week.
    - Gets one out of every ten Vitae you drink.
    - Shares your Willpower pool.
    - Gets the full benefits of one of your Disciplines.
    - Automatically knows if you're lying to them.

    For You:
    - A dot of Blood Potency.
    - Immunity to all of the Ghoul's Disciplines.
    - You can draw up to three Vitae from the Ghoul each week.
    - You share the Ghoul's Willpower pool.
    - You share one of the Ghoul's social merits.
    - You automatically know if the Ghoul is lying to you.

    Stuff That Can Go Wrong:
    - If they complete the Oath of Action, you take Aggravated damage equal to the Discipline dots you granted the Ghoul.
    - They can call off the Oath of the Safe Word, paralysing you for a turn + 1 turn per Willpower spent.

    But why should any of that be a problem? You're supplying them with their sweet, sweet blood fix and power! Sure, all the other members of the Invictus are looking at you funny, but nothing in the city charter says that Ghouls cannot receive a pre-membership in the Invictus (probably because it never came up before)!

    Also, seriously, what did the Ghoul do to earn both your fealty and an apology?

    I think the takeaway here is that some Oaths have really interesting alternate uses if you flip the words "liege" and "vassal" in your head. I can see some Domains where these "inverted" uses are more common than the standard ones (a Vampire feudal system built around vassals giving their lord Feeding Grounds in exchange for permission to use it and the Lord being better able to defend them when push comes to shove would be... interesting. I might have to give that one some thought.)

    I have decided, after some thought, that I don't really feel happy on these forums. I might decide to come back to post. Who knows - but right now, I'm gone.

    So good bye, good luck, and have a nice day.
