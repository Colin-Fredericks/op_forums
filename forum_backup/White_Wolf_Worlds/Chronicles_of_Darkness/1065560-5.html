                                        03-26-2017, 11:08 AM
                                        They're more like minor templates, but any human who performs one of the two described rituals becomes one without needing sponsorship from a major template or having to come from a bloodline tied to one either.
                                        However, the first style of Blood Bather requires Iremite symbols to be incorporated in the ritual and they become immune to Sybaris. That and their narrative ties to mummies makes them practically a minor template to MtC similar to wolf-blooded and WtF (except wolf-blooded can actually become werewolves).
                                        The other style has no explicit ties to mummies at all.
                                        There's no mention of being barred from Supernatural Merits or anything like that, and the two rituals described are just examples. Other rituals could exist with other types of benefits.
