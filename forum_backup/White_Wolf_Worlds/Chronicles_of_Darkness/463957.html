    Self-Embrace Question

    03-31-2015, 06:49 PM
    Anyway, due to a weird confluence of events, I've decided one of my living BBEGs' particular method of immortality (trading away humans to the True Fae for youth and years of luck) is slowly turning him into a vampire; every time dies and is reborn, he develops a trait more akin to one of the Kindred until eventually he'll come back without a heartbeat and a need for blood (I explicitly call it selling so much of his humanity that a Beast filled the vacuum).

    Thing is, I'm not sure what clan he'd be. He's a Civil War-era slaver with skills in voodoo (he particularly enjoys a zombification spell to create perfect servants), so Ventrue isn't unlikely (particularly given that one voodoo bloodline), but given the fact there are minor clans in Blood and Smoke, you think he could be the progenitor of a new one? What Banes and Disciplines would it have?

    Also, I think it would be fitting if he was born a Neglatu-because it'd be ironic (he is and remains the slaver, but he can never truly be safe from his slaves rebelling, because Neglatu can't form blood bonds).

      Personally, I would be tempted to give him a unique Clan or at least Bloodline. My suggestions in short:

      Unique Clan (TBN)

      Disciplines: Celerity, Dominate, Obfuscate
      Celerity feels to me to be the most "fae" of physical Disciplines. Obfuscate also feels fae (there are some parallels to a Changeling's Mask even). Dominate fits his nature and past.

      Bane (Idea #1): The ??? Curse. As he has robbed in life, so he will forever in death. Everytime he feeds from a willing vessel (this includes regular blood dolls and NPCs covered by the Herd merit, but not vessels coerced through Disciplines) he gains no nourishment from it and instead takes 10 - Humanity points of Bashing Damage. Freely given blood will never nourish him.

      Bane (Idea #2): The Fae Curse. Being attacked with an object made of Cold Iron deals an additional 10 - Humanity points of Bashing damage to him. Furthermore, he has to keep any promises he makes. When he fails to adhere to a promise or oath, he takes 10 - Humanity points of Bashing damage.

      Bane (Idea #3): The Decorous Curse. He is unable to reject a gift freely given, even if this puts him into peril. Rejecting any gift or friendly act requires a Willpower point and all his dicepools are capped by Humanity for the rest of the night.


      As a Bloodline I would make him Ventrue or Mekhet. Give him Dominate as a Mekhet or Obfuscate as a Ventrue. For Bloodline Bane I would suggest using one of the above with the following modifications:

      Bane 1: Straight 5 points of damage
      Bane 2: An additional 2 points of damage (Cold Iron)
      Bane 3: Remove the Humanity cap afterwards


      As a Clan/Bloodline Merit, maybe something that grants him Vitae for making fae deals?

                                  Somewhat paradoxically (given that they're kind of all about embracing their monstrousness), the Sanctified might not be a good fit if he doesn't feel any guilt or regret regarding the choices he made in life. The Sanctified teach that only unrepentant sinners become vampires ("The evil become Damned; God has taken those worthy of His love to His own side."), and this is presumably particularly true of one who wasn't deliberately embraced. They'd likely tell him that his condition was a consequence of his pridefulness and for consorting with what were clearly demonic beings. If that sounds like something he'd strongly disagree with, they may be a bad fit.

                                  The Ordo Dracul, on the other hand, may be quite interested in the unique circumstances of his embrace, as well as the means by which he had previously attained living immorality. They'd probably welcome him, and encourage him to keep in touch with the Fae, in order to hopefully glean some useful secrets from them. If he's hoping to in some way reverse his transformation into a one of the kindred, or at least reduce the impact of his new vulnerabilities and urges, he might well be inclined to take the Dragons up on their offer.

                                  The Circle of the Crone might work, if the local Crone cult models itself on Voodoo to some degree. That's assuming, of course, that he's a practitioner of the Voodoo religion, rather than merely someone who picked up some sorcerous tricks originating in the Voodoo tradition. There's more to being an Acolyte than just casting spells, after all, and it would be a bit odd for a southern "gentleman" to also be a blood-drenched goddess-worshiper (though the contrast might be interesting).

                                  Ultimately, the Invictus seems like the most natural fit, given the way you've characterized the man. They're likely to care far less about his unusual origin than about the resources he can contribute to the covenant. Just remember, though, that as a newcomer to kindred politics he's going to have to be willing to take orders from his elders if he wants to fit in with the First Estate.
