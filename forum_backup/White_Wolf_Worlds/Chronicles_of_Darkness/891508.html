    Project Persona

    05-15-2016, 10:07 PM
    I just saw some teasers of the upcoming Persona 5, and was wondering about how the series could be adapted to the world of CoD, or at least could use its rules.

    For the ones who don't know, Persona is a series of games, spin-offs of the Shin Megami Tensei series. In Persona, exceptional circunstances allow figments of a person's mind and emotions to manifestate in the physical world. If the person can't deal with the aspects presented, they become Shadows, and attack people (sometimes those people die, sometimes they just enter a kind of catatonic state). Since those aspects are always hard to accept, the rare person that at least partially deals with this, or at least get enough help to control it, the figment becomes the eponimous Persona, and can lend help and grant power to the "user".

    http://megamitensei.wikia.com/wiki/Persona_(Series)

    Well, in its barest form, Persona seems simply like ephemeral dwellers of the Astral Realms, from Philemon and Nyarlathotep, from the Temenos or the Anima Mundi, to the Personas and Shadows themselves, coming from the Oneiroi of people. But I'm struggling a little with the cosmology, the superimposing Realms like the Dark Hour, the Velvet Room and the relation with Tarot's Arcana. I will use this topic to add matherial as I devise it, but would also like some feedback and ideas.

                                              First, considering the whole series, the Shadows and the Personas are, actualy, the same beings, a Persona being just a "tamed" Shadow. Also, most of their manifestations are partialy independent on their humans. A Shadow can survive the human that spawned it, and though the Persona Users command their Personas, on most games they are relatively independent entities. On that, most of the actual alterations on the state of the Users could be explained even in the original games as some sort of power from the Persona instead of an aspect of being an User.

                                              So I'm prone to amechra's idea of simply use manifestations like Fetter for the Persona User herself. What's special on rules are the entities, not their "hosts".

                                              I haven't read Mage for a long time, but I remember that there where something like the embodiment of every aspect of the Mage that she despises in her Oneiros. The Shadow, both in the game and in Jungian theory, is something like that (it was probably borrowed from Jung anyway for Mage), so we could use this.

                                              My idea: the Shadow is an ephemeral entity from the Oneiros, formed by aspects of the person she have trouble accepting. Its nature is to confront the person, so she learns more about herself and accept these aspects, even if not living them. An ignored Shadow can be agressive toward people, whyle an accepted one becomes a guide for personal growth and a helper to deal with the world. This in their natural environment, in the Oneiros. Both are highly symbolic in their appearance, so they usually borrow their aspects from old and powerful archetypes from the Temenos.

                                              Under exceptional circunstances, those spirits gain access to the material and other realms outside the Oneiros, with the full might of the borrowed archetypes. In Personas 1 and 2, the entities Philemon and Nyarlathotep, both powerful gods from either the Temenos or the Anima Mundi, caused on purpose this to happen. In Personas 3 and 4, it is prompted to happen naturally by the advent respectively of the Dark Hour and the Midnight Channel, both otherworldly phenomena that allows human beings to enter (the Dark Hour actualy captures everyone, even if usualy with a protective coffin that shut senses down).

                                              While the circumstances that cause a Shadow/Persona to manifest in the world are exceptional, once done it is irreversible to some of the affected entities. Once the Shadow becomes Persona, its tie to the physical world through its User is permanent. The entity will always be there, and with effort the User can cause its Manifestation on various forms. What could be done for the User until here, so, is just the cost to summon the Persona in the everyday life (the most hard circumstance to summon the power of the Persona). Every other effect is covered by the rules for ephemeral entities, maybe with a couple of rules for those entities in particular.

                                              That leaves for later the Velvet Room, the Arcana and Social Links, the Wild Card, and some Numina characteristic of the Shin Megami Tensei series, including Persona.

                                                  The Wild Card, Igor and The Velvet Room.

                                                  Sometimes a Persona-User is able to tie herself to more than one Persona at the same time, and change between those Personae in an instant. Several extremely rare circumstances can lead to this, like the interference of Philemon and Nyarlathotep, gods of the Unconscious Mind. In Personas 1 & 2 their artificial Persona-Users were also multi-Persona.

                                                  But the best known occurrences of such phenomenon are the Wild Cards, natural multi-Persona bearers. Its unknown what causes this, though it should be noted that all the Wild Cards known until now are from the Fool Arcana, and they comprise the entirety of Fool Arcana Natural Persona-Users until now. So there is reason to think that the Fool Arcana and the Wild Card are somehow related.

                                                  It is important to note that each person have only one Shadow. The distinct Personae, in this sense, so, are not distinct entities, but distinct manifestations of the same entity, and so, of the User's inner self. The change is still a very relevant thing, since such manifestation is what defines the abilities of the entity.

                                                  Wild Cards draw power from their bonds with people, it doesn't matter how such a bond is formed or with what kind of people. Anything that presents itself to the User as a distinct personality and with what she can form meaningful relations will do the trick. Some of those relationships stand up as symbolic of certain broad archetypes (the Arcana), and are mechanicaly presented in the games as the Social Links, but every bond and its quality is a source of power for the Wild Card.

                                                  Just remember, this is an aspect of the Wild Card, not of every Persona-User. Though relationships are an integral part of what makes each person what she is, and what changes her with time, the Shadow/Persona is a manifestation of the inner self, not directly of relationships, with the sole known exception of the Wild Cards. When rules are done, the Wild Cards won't be just another z-splat, but have a set of unique rules for them.

                                                  Another indirectly related phenomena is Igor and his Velvet Room. The entity known as Igor is unique, supposedly created by Philemon as his servant from a doll, and the Keeper of the "limited" realm called The Velvet Room. Igor seems to be partial to Persona-Users and humanity as a whole, knowledgeable about the Unconscious, the Astral Realms and divination, but also removed, providing limitted but useful assistance. Until now, "he" offered his help with the management of multiple Personae to Persona-Users able to bear multi-Personae without any payment of any kind. Igor is usualy served by at least one assistant, and various entities have served this role, with at least three being alledgedly his own creations, if fully conscious and free-willed by themselves. The assistants can grant other services for the Persona-Users for any payment they want, but it seems they can't act directly. Whyle they are assistants, they have to live in the Velvet Room. At least one assistant leaved the Room and started to roam the Material world, without any effect aside not being more a resident of the Velvet Room.

                                                  Velvet Room is a limited realm located between dream and matter, if the account of its few inhabitants can be taken for granted. Its manifestations change with time, usually to accomodate the Persona-User who is expected there. Twice it was literally a room, once a giant elevator in perpetual ascension, once a eternally moving limousine, and once a prison. Always covered in blue velvet and stuffed with high-class amenities. Other properties of the room include changing the pace of time inside it, manifesting entrances in multiple spots at once, controlling who can perceive and access such entrances (usually only the one who signs a contract with Igor can do so), allowing both mental manifestations (the visitor stares at the invisible entrance for a few moments whyle her mind actualy manifests in the Velvet Room for as much time as needed) and physical manifestations (the Room was already used as refuge and as transportation), and physically changing its current state for symbolic purposes (both the Elevator and the Limousine changed their state of movement at some point as the current recurrent visitor arrived at a focus point of her journey). There is evidence that the state of the Room is tied to Igor, but is inconclusive. It was confirmed, though, that Igor can leave the Room (with alleged deleterious consequences for the realm).

                                                          I'm still considering what to do about the Personae/Shadows Manifestations. First, when I wrote the above statements, I had missed an important point, I had forgotten that being attached to an "active" Persona actually changes the User to a degree, but a very limited, if spectacular, one. The User acquires stats and weaknesses/strengths based on her Persona, but doesn't manifest any visual physical change, nor changes in personality or behavior, except what could be expected for that person acquiring this kind of power. Some Users can believe to be chosen by gods, or gods themselves, among other miriad things, but based on personal interpretations of their experience with the Persona and lack of understanding on the phenomena.

                                                          That means something similar to the Claim Manifestation, but with far more limited impact. At the same time, the distinction between User and Persona persists, and is shown here and there. An User needs effort to use any other power of the Persona aside passive efects, and even those are unclear to what degree they persist outside stressful situations. When such powers are used, the Persona manifests in visible, and often colorful, ways, and some evidence exists that such manifestations are material. The Persona just cannot actively affect the physical world without such material manifestation, or at least there are no records of such. The only place where the Persona seems to be able to exist in a non-material state is inside the User proper, what is similar, but not quite the same as Fetter.

                                                          Shadows follow even more restrictive rules, probably for being Personae without an User. They exist only in material form, or at least there is no record of a non-material Shadow. They physically wander the places they can exist, and physicaly interact with people. The nature of the circumstances in with Shadows appear, that is, anomalous dimensional phenomena, means that those beings can be perceived as existing in non-material states, but this have to do with the nature of he anomaly: people can wander the same spaces, and them be similarly perceived by others, and the Shadows still need to physicaly interact to do anything beyond scary people. Similarly, some very rare Shadow/Persona can be perceived as non-material when they're actualy not-solid. Such entities can exist in liquid, or even gaseous states, with all the expected consequences.

                                                          Also interesting to note is that such rules were tested to a degree in extreme situations when it concerned non-human Users. As said before, anything that develops an Ego, a sense of self, also develops a Shadow of its own, that can normaly becomes Persona. That happened in the games with at least a couple robots, including the playable Android Aigis in Persona 3, two entities from the Velvet Room, the systers Margareth and Elizabeth (assistants supposedly created by Igor). But the most interesting case is Teddie, playable character from Persona 4. Teddie is a Shadow that developed emotions and sense of self. It becomes a Shadow with a Shadow, and then becomes a Persona-User. As a Shadow, Teddie is always material, and as a Persona-User, it haves the same relation of any other User with its Persona. That is, Teddie's Persona lives in it, affects its stats, and visualy manifests to use any active ability it possess.

                                                          Probably two new Manifestations are under way, Shadow Manifestation and Persona Manifestation, with an added characteristicfor those entities for, besides being ephemeral, not existing in Twilight State.
