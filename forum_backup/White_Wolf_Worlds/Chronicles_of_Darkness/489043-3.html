                                    Originally posted by Illuminated Minutia View Post
                                    Yeah like I could see a fishperson being good at swimming vs a waterperson being good at escaping grapples or melding with bodies of water or something. Most of the other merges on that list make sense, though.
                                    I think we need to keep Contracts in mind here. Kith is what you were turned into; Seemings are how you took your power back. But Seemings also dictate Contract Affinities.

                                    If you were made into a being that lives in water (Aquatic Kith), your Seeming is going to influence your Contracts and further ways that expresses itself. A Beast's Contracts can focus on being more capable of physical things, while an Elemental's Contracts can be more literal about "being water."

                                    As well, this also gets around any weird things like air, sand, or other fluid Elementals that would make sense to have escaping, or melding, powers looking at the "made of water" Kith over Kiths that deal with their Elements and letting their Contracts deal with such things.
