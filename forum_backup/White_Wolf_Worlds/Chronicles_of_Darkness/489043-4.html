                                Originally posted by KismetRose View Post

                                Your question confused me at first, but I think I can answer it now. From what we've been told, your character apparently retains their human form for the majority of their durance, and finally becomes their Seeming during the escape. So, for example, your keeper might treat you like a house pet, but depending on how you escape will determine whether you become a cute kitten (Beast), or a gorgeous sex slave (Fairest), and so on. You're given your job when you come into Arcadia, and you're given your form when you leave.

                                Hope I answered that right!

                                            What Gaius said.

                                            Your Keeper abducts you, and turns you onto your Kith. Your Kith is the role you play in the Durance. When you escape, you flavour the Kith with a Seeming, based on how you took control and fled. So a Keeper might have needed a bunch of dragons. So, he abducts a bunch of Changelings and turns them into Draconic Kiths. But when the group decides to escape, they each choose a different method of escape. That means they're each still Draconic, but of different Seemings.
