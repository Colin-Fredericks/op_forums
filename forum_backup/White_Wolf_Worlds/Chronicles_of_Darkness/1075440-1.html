                Obviously, first edition has a lot of books. If you want to run a first edition game, you have or have access to every book there is, detailing bloodlines, vampires in other countries, books for each covenant and clan and pre-made adventures and alternative rule sets.

                If you're comfortable playing a more simple game without many bloodlines and stuff, or can homebrew conversions and expansions on the fly or like using the forums for that, then second edition is a plus but not everyone is good with that.

                Also some people may be used to the first edition rules, some people for one reason or another don't like learning new rules or see changes to the setting as so off-putting they refuse to play it, in which case first edition is still available to them, selling on DriveThruRPG.
