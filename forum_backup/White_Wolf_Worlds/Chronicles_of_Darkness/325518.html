    Slasher Undertaking: Sentinal/Warden

    11-29-2014, 04:29 PM
    Sentinel
    That’s the third time I saved you from a serial killer. Would you like to get a coffee sometime? You know I love you.
    A note found pinned to a dead Mask by VASCU agent John Bell.



    Most Slashers kill because they are compelled to or for the sheer horrific joy of murder. Sentinels are different, they kill to protect. It is a sheer overpowering fear of losing the one good thing in their life, their relationship with their beloved, that makes a Sentinel into a serial killer.

    A Sentinel has no restraint, the thought of losing her beloved is so horrifying that any hesitation she may have is tiny in comparison, she may not always decide murder is the best solution but when she does she strikes without hesitation or remorse. This, combined with extreme paranoia can leave as many bodies at the feet of a Sentinel as any other Undertaking.

    In person Sentinels are very good at seeming normal, the mask only exists for their love but for his sake they wear it all the time. Many make use of this talent, and lead trusting victims somewhere secluded before doing the deed so that their beloved will never find out. Others tend to extreme personality swings, they are demure and pleasant until they feel a threat, then they transform into a killer in a heartbeat.

    Background: Sentinels come from isolation. They grew up alone and unloved, if not outright abused. Then one day someone showed them kindness, it was the single ray of light in a world of darkness. The Sentinel held it close and vowed to keep it safe.

    But it was too late, the Sentinel had already decided that humanity was nothing but a source of abuse and cruelty. Kindness only made this clearer through contrast. It was too late to prevent a Slasher being born, but not too late to change her eventual Undertaking (usually from a Freak).

    When a Sentinel is created all her fear and paranoia of humanity is enhanced and redirected to a fear of losing her one connection, her beloved. Sentinels will do anything to protect their beloved, anything to keep her beloved all to herself. Even murder.

    [Picture: A Japanese schoolgirl in uniform holds a bloodstained baseball bat over her shoulder. She smiles with a terrifying grin and a thousand yard stare.]

    Sentinels favour Finesse and a wide variety of skills in order to adapt to whatever threat they may face. In defiance of their isolated origins many come to master social skills through intense study. Most Sentinels quickly learn, quite rightly, that there are more people who try to steal her beloved through social means (if only by inviting him to a night out without her) than people trying to physically harm their beloved; and they also learn that a passive aggressive campaign to destroy someone socially is far less likely to horrify her beloved than a murder. Those that kill without preparation use their Finesse and social skills to cover up their crimes rather than for committing them.

    To the outside world it often appears that her first true friend has caused the Sentinel to come out of her shell. Many people stop worrying about the Sentinel at precisely the worst time, though a skilled eye can see her behaviour for what it is: Casual manipulation that rivals a sociopath in it’s scope and breadth.

    Attribute: Wits, Dexterity or Manipulation

    Undertaking Skills: Politics, Brawl, Stealth, Weaponry, Intimidation, Socialise, Subterfuge

    Inspirations: Alice Morgan (Luther)

    Talent: Keeping What’s Mine

    Any time somebody attempts to “steal” the Sentinel’s charge she gains the Hell Hath No Fury Tilt. In this instance steal is defined as anything that the Sentinel thinks would weaken the relationship between the Warden and her beloved, and Sentinels are known to jump to the worst conclusions.

    The Hell Hath No Fury Tilt remains dormant until the Sentinel’s activates it. When she does, for the remainder of the scene, she gains 2 + Nightmare bonus dice in addition to the usual +3 when spending Willpower on any roll used to protect her relationship with her beloved. This is not limited to murder, though that’s always on the table.

    Frailty: Living Emotional Crutch

    All Sentinel’s have the Addiction Condition, they are addicted to spending time with their beloved.

    If their Beloved dies or they are decisively cut off from them, the Sentinel replaces Addiction with the Broken Condition (Wardens take Soulless instead). These Conditions can never be cured without either getting back in contact or removing the entire Undertaking.

    Warden
    I’ll always be there for you. I’m part of you my love.
    Text message from a non-existent number.


    The most important thing in a Sentinel's life is her beloved; but the only thing in a Warden’s life is her beloved.

    Warden’s just have no interest in anything that isn’t related to their beloved, they go home to an empty house and sit on a bare floor watching the phone and the clock until they can have another chance to see their love. The family she tells him about when they next meet lie dead in the basement.

    A Warden will still put effort into appearing as a normal person, but the mask is thinner. A Sentinel plans her life around her beloved, she chooses a job and a home near her love. She cares little about it, but there is something beneath her mask. A Warden rarely needs food or heat, and has no psychological need for a home. Wardens just cut out any part of their life which their Beloved is unlikely to see, they wear masks over empty nothingness.

    Much of the difference between a Warden and a Sentinel can be explained by the Scourge’s supernatural nature rather than any inherent difference in mindset. A Sentinel would cut out most of her life if she could, a Warden simply can. A Sentinel knows how hard it would be to keep her beloved locked up for his own protection, a Warden doesn't need to sleep or to worry about what's happening at home while she's getting groceries. The terrifying thing is that if a Sentinel learned what a Warden was she’d likely attempt to become one; willingly sacrificing her humanity for freedom from human suffering and to express a “purer” form of devotion.

    When Wardens’ kill they do so in much the same way as a Sentinel. They either kill the moment they feel a threat and worry about the consequences afterwards or they meticulously plan a murder that will not damage her relationship with her beloved. Warden’s just have less to worry about.

    And yet a Wardens’ killings often take on an occult slant, detached from humanity as they are Warden’s often become dispassionately aware of the supernatural world. She may ritualistically kill to ground the Resonance and prevent spirits of violence following her home, or she may even summon spirits of diseases to inflict deaths by natural causes. A Warden only has a practical interest in the supernatural, but many find it very practical.

    Background: Sentinels have connections to humanity, these connections are horrid abusive things but they serve to tie her to her humanity no matter how much she wishes they didn’t.

    A Warden does not. It is possible for a person to become a Warden directly, a feral child falls for the first human she sees and protects him in the savage ways taught to her by the jungle. As such isolation is rare most Wardens begin their careers as a Sentinel.

    After defending her beloved from a gangbanger the Sentinel realises how easy it would be to kill her abusive parents. After driving a wedge between her beloved and his childhood friend with sweet lies, she uses those same skills to get her bullies expelled.

    One by one her connections to humanity break until only her beloved is left, and one person is not enough. Untethered she drifts away from humanity. She is no longer a person, she is just part of her beloved. He has black hair, good grades, and a murderous protector.

    Wardens are often more direct than Sentinels, but they are still a subtle Undertaking who favour Finesse. A Sentinel must hide her crimes for fear of what her beloved would think if the police accused her. A Warden often develops the Talents to protect herself from discovery. Forensic science is an issue for people, not guardian spirits. Only her beloved’s skepticism at the number of accidents surrounding him may yet stay her hand.

    While few Sentinels develop any appreciation for the Occult unless they discover a supernatural being threatening their beloved, Warden’s often discover they draw the attention of protective ancestor ghosts, or guardian spirits. Creatures who feel an (unreciprocated) kinship to the Warden. They may intervene personally when the Warden’s Undertaking clashes with their own mission, or teach her secrets to better protect or possesses her beloved.

    A Warden who discovers a demon willing to make her beloved love her unconditionally, for a deal of course, is one who has no need for restraint at all.

    Attribute: Wits, Dexterity or Manipulation

    Undertaking Skills: Politics, Occult, Brawl, Stealth, Weaponry, Intimidation, Persuasion, Socialise, Subterfuge

    Picture: A Japanese schoolboy stands, a shadowy figure (the Sentinel from the previous picture) hugs him from behind, with one hand covering his eyes. Dead bodies lie at his feet.

    Talent: Your Other Half

    A Warden is not truly an independent being anymore, she is part of her beloved. No matter where her body is she is by his side in a metaphysical sense that matters more than the physical. Any attempt to communicate between the two always reaches the other. Letters always arrive quickly, texts and emails are noticed as soon as they’re sent, even if they don’t know the correct address, even if the other doesn’t have a phone (her beloved may leave the letter unopened or choose not to answer the phone, Wardens are psychologically incapable of that).

    In addition the Warden is always aware if someone is trying to “steal” her beloved (if this Talent provokes a Clash of Wills roll Wits + Nightmare). When an attempt is made or when her beloved asks she may spend a Willpower point to instantly appear by his side. She appears in the closest possible location that she could plausibly be.

    Frailty: Satellite Character

    A Warden can only gain Willpower from her Virtue or Vice through actions that revolve around the Warden’s beloved .

      As traditional, some commentry.

      This Undertaking is actually a spin-off from Princess. I was thinking of the yandere trope and realised it would work much better for Slasher than Princess.

      The Talent is different from most Undertakings. Typically Undertakings have a very narrowly focused Talent - even my revised Legends who get three dots of Dread Powers are designed to have a focused ability or at least a strong theme - think Freddy Kruger. This is intentionally the opposite, being useful in just about every situation.

      There's two reasons for this. Firstly Sentinals are a reactive Undertaking. Who knows what situation she'd be in when someone tries to "steal" her beloved - flexibility is key. That's also why they're so Finesse heavy.

      Secondly it encourages you to save your Willpower until it involves her beloved. This creates a nice monomaniacal charachter.

      I actually suspect Keeping What’s Mine will become more powerful than a Mask's Unstoppable Killing Machine once you boost it with a few dots of Nightmare.


      Fun fact - this is not the first time you've seen Sentinels in my writing. The Baker Street Irregulars has a sample Hunter who's based on John Luther and Luther's relationship with Alice Morgan. John Bell is a reference to Luthor but probably maintains a sane revulsion to the thought of romance with a Slasher.


      Now I want to see a story of a crimeboss (vampire or mortal) who intentionally creates a Sentinel in love with him, and lives to regret it enormously.
