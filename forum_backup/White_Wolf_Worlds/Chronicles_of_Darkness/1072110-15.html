                        Here's the tactic idea, could use a bit of refinement but I think it will work given the themes so far:


                        Candlelit Vigil: Some Hunters and occultists become painfully aware of how certain locations take on a life of their own under often horrific circumstances. Hauntings, Possessions, and especially Nests can be created when pain, suffering, or hate grows to a boiling point and a location becomes a corrupted creature all it's own. This is where this Tactic owes it's origins, for sometimes the only way to fight such horrific tragedy is with love, understanding, and closure. All of which is few and far between amongst those who take up the Hunter's Vigil...but humans may use another kind of Vigil to give the Hunter's just the right kind of power they need. Often used by religious or community driven Cells, this Tactic calls upon the local or regional community to gather and either pay their respects or morn a specific person or event that's connected to a spiritually poisonous location. The community puts forth it's faith, sorrow, or friendship and it weakens the tainted emotions that many haunted locations feed upon. While the Candlelit Vigil might not be enough to dispel the corruption from the location, having so many people sending forth their own positive emotions often gives the cell just the opening they need to move in and either properly purify it or at least destroy the dark-beating heat that gives foul life to the evil of the place.

                            Candlelit Vigil is a good idea. The Sauna Box is definitely over powered, and I would say its also un-fun since tracking down monsters and discovering their secret identity is part of the fun; you don't want to skip past it.

                            For TF:V might I suggest some sort of big brother related power where you file a request with the geeks back at HQ to dig through social media accounts looking for a monster. Roll Status * 2 - the monster's Anonymity merit (if they have one).

                            Exceptional Success: You're given a list of NPCs. 40% are red herrings, the remaining 60% are people of interest: ex-victims, unwitting pawns, loyal ghouls/sworn/etc, and of course the monster itself.
                            Success: As above, but none of the characters on your list is the monster. Just red herrings or people close to them.
                            Failure: Your request gets suck in a queue behind higher priority items. You get the results of a success, at the start of the next session.
                            Dramatic Failure: 80% are red herrings, 20% of the list are legitimately people of interest. If the monster has appropriate abilities (like a Princess' visionary dreams or a Genius with an AI watching for spies) they also learn about your social media scan.

                            Make it one use per session and require a target like a school or a sherrif's department, so players should do a bit of investigation first to avoid pointing it at somewhere without any monsters in the first place.

                                                Welp, in an attempt to redeem myself, one of my other endowment ideas!

                                                Infernal Archives (Castigation)

                                                Hell contains all sins and all atrocities in itself, every ugly part of humanity put in sharp relief. To gaze upon this litany of darkness would shatter the human soul. The Lurciferge, with their demonic bloodline, can do just that

                                                Cost: 1 willpowe
                                                Action: Contested
                                                Dice Pool: Presence + intimidation vs Resolve

                                                Dramatic Failure- the Luciferge sees hell, and is reminded of their own damnation. They lose the ability to spend willpower for the rest of the scene.

                                                Failure- Nothing happens except the Luciferge looks a bit silly

                                                Success- the victim sees the greatest horrors of human history in a terrifying instant. On a human target, or any monster with an integrity equivalent, the victim loses the ability to spend willpower for the scene and will attempt to leave the area as soon as possible. This isn't an overwhelming compulsion- they won't jump through windows or leave a dying friend- but they will do so as soon as an opportunity arises.
                                                A Noble has a bigger reaction. As well as the above effects, they immediately suffer a Hyper-Empathy Roll. If the roll succeeds it cannot be resolved until the end of this scene.
                                                (The ST may rule some particularly empathetic, kind or innocent humans may have a similar reaction. If so, they make the roll using their integrity as a dice poll. They cannot suffer the shadowed condition and lose 2 willpower on an exceptional success)

                                                Exceptional success- Victims also lose the ability to gain willpower. If a noble is affected, their hyper-empathy roll has a bonus equal to the hunter's castiagation rating.

                                                Drawback - Not all creatures fear sin. If used near a being that feeds on or embodies sin (creatures of the Darkness and the Inferno, definitely, others at the ST's discretion) the creature gains willpower equal to the hunter's castigation rating. If used on one of these creatures, it regains twice the hunters castigation rating.

                                                        Shield of Sins
                                                        The Lucifuge have the blood of demons in their vein, and just like them, they have power over sin. This include the ability to tempt people into committing them, but also to shield them from others influences who would try to urge them toward it. By channeling Hell’s authority, this rite allows to cast a false “shell” of sin around the innocents and the virtuous, making them appear as sinners in the eyes of forces who would want to corrupt or destroy them.
                                                        Cost: The Lucifuge must slightly cut himself, and place some of his blood on the intended subjects. This typically translates as 1 bashing damage per subjects.
                                                        Action: Extended; each turn represents one minute of preparation. The threshold is 2 successes per person.
                                                        Dice pool: Resolve + Castigation
                                                        Dramatic Failure: The hunter unwillingly corrupts the subjects with his blood. All characters who should have benefited from this rite immediately suffer a Breaking Point penalized by the hunter’s dots in Castigation.
                                                        Failure: Nothing happens.
                                                        Success: The hunter makes progress toward invoking the rite.
                                                        Exceptional Success: Extraordinary progress is made.
                                                        Once the threshold is reached, all people targeted by this rite are now wrapped in a protection made of false “sins”, causing them to appear as more corrupt than they really are and protecting against any corrupting influence. Any ability intended to jauger their Integrity Rating, such as Sense Integrity, will identify it as Castigation dots lower than it actually is. Moverover, should they visit a Nest, its Curses won’t affect them even if they behave with good intention: the Darkness will be tricked into believe they are acting with some sinister ulterior motif.
