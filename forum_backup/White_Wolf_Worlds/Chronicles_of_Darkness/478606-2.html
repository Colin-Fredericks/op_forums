                    Originally posted by falco1029 View Post

                    What made you roll that back? Seems like a good way both to cover that mystic backing (even if more anti-magic than magic) as well as adding a cost to it, given its potency.
                    I could see a reason for removing it in not wanting to cut out Sanctified characters who want to use the Covenant's Advantage, which seems fair to me. I've felt in the past that some Merits had unnecessary restrictions, and the ability to have more choices to mix together always feels like a good thing.

                    My feedback on the Theban Sorcery rituals themselves: I definitely feel some stuff should get shifted to higher dots. I also think these powers really accomplish the whole biblical feel for the most part. They feel like great set pieces for Chronicles, or as ways to kick them off. So they're an A+ for style, in my book. I could totally see myself using them in plots.

                    I think my critique is that some of them almost feel too big like Apocalypse or the one that blots out the Sun. Like they're dope as climactic Chronicle events, but as a player, they feel like rituals I wouldn't be able to/be willing to realistically use more than once in an entire Chronicle. I don't know if that's a bad thing but I will say that one of my favorite things about the great Crone rituals in the Circle's Secrets of the Covenants playtest was that these rituals felt like ones I could regularly use to achieve a signature style for a Cruac witch character.

                                                Aaron's Rod...is the intended use to be gaining a small fairly sneaky creature to go and fetch something for you? Or to just be an impressive bit of sorcery to aid in intimidation or presence style rolls? The poison suggests it;s intended for a combat role but 4 dice to attack?

                                                Could I instead milk the venom from the snake and use that venom as part of one of my own attacks (with a much higher dice pool)?

                                                For Crusade. What counts as 'dark sorceries'? Embeds and Exploits? Disciplines? Werewolf Gifts? Spirit/Ghost/Angel Numina/Manifestations/etc., Theban Sorcery?

                                                Rank 1 specifically says 'that target her' so spells/sorceries/etc. that target an area containing her are valid? So the aforementioned fireball thrown AT her would suffer the -5 penalty ('cos anyone planning on being a sorcerous knight it going to want a maxed weaponry eventually) but an exploding fireball thrown a few feet to her left would suffer no penalty?

                                                I would not want the Crusader to lose out on Theban Miracles, I like the idea of requiring another pre-req merit to purchase it and I would suggest that pre-req merit grants some level of protection against magical mental influence - and leave Crusade as defending against magical physical attacks. So you can avoid parrying mind control like some manner of Solar Exalt. There's the merit in the GMC Core that gives bonuses to resist mental effects...Indomitable or something that could work?

                                                For the Rank 2 it allows an attack to the vocal chords without penalty...what's being pictured here? because if I jam a sword through your throat that's one thing but an axe/pollaxe/chainsaw blow is fairly likely to just decapitate you.

                                                For Rank 3 the intent seems to be interrupting something going on, the extended action discipline or a ritual, does that mean that an instant cast spell (or a spell that was prepared earlier and has a trigger like some of the Theban miracles) could not be interrupted?

                                                Flock: It says the members of the Flock start any scene with a single point of Willpower, what if a member of the Flock is a fairly important NPC (or a Goul or your Retainer etc.) and could realistically have more than a single point under normal circumstances?

                                                Hmmm, could Laity would allow you to learn Crusade?

                                                I could see some fairly questionable - though legal - play with the Sanctuary Merit. Take it and have a few different 4-dot Blood Sorcerors, they may well be stuck in your Safe Place but they can cast their spells out to a very long range. You could also use them to learn the various secrets of the other Covenants. Take a Dracul and Crone criminal in and leverage them to learn the secrets of the other Covenants. Further, is there any penalty to kicking the criminals out of your Sanctuary?

                                                Does Revelatory Shroud overcome or contest Obfuscate? Clash of Wills it?
