            Better?
            (1) Spinner
            For he who would build, the first step is to acquire materials. Human builders must satisfy themselves with quarried stone, concrete, steel beams and other tricks of artifice, but a Monarch has no such restrictions. His own body can provide the resources he requires, growing organs reminiscent of a spider’s spinnerets able to secrete a substance not unlike spiderweb, except on a vastly larger scale. Even in human form a Monarch can use his psychic prowess to convert psionic energy into this material.
            Cost: 1 Psi per 5 Size of resin created or none, in Noble Form
            Dice Pool: Dexterity + Crafts + Hammers
            Action: Extended, with 3 turns per roll
            Effect: The Monarch secretes a kind of fast-solidifying resin from either his hands and fingers, in human form, or from some other organ in noble form. The resin is black and shiny, almost like a kind of plastic and has a smooth surface. However, it behaves almost like anti-slip material, being very difficult to slip against, making it an easy surface to walk on. For the first few seconds it has a consistency like treacle, adhering to anything but the Monarch’s body before swiftly hardening to a Durability of (Hammers) with Structure equal to Authority.
            The Monarch can mould the resin into any shape he desires, creating webs, restraints or more elaborate constructions, limited only by his imagination and the time at his disposal..
            Each success creates 1 Size worth of this material. An Exceptional Success doubles the amount of resin created with that roll while a Dramatic failure means that the Monarch has unwittingly made a fatal mistake, creating a flaw within the structure of the resin, effectively reducing its Durability to 1. A Monarch can only roll a number of times equal to his (Authority + Hammers + Stamina) in each scene, after which he has exhausted his reserves, which return at a rate of (Hammers) rolls per scene.
            While the resin is impervious to water, wind and other natural sources of damage, it is flammable, although not extraordinarily so, and degrades over time, losing 1 Durability for every (Authority) days. When it reaches 0 Durability, the resin collapses into a black sludgy substance before evaporating within a few hours.

                                (2) Shape
                                Once materials have been acquired, the next step in construction is to shape them into the proper form. Mortals must make use of tools such as chisels and hammers to accomplish this, but a Monarch can use his considerable psionic powers to bring his will to bear against the the world around him and thereby cause it to take on a form more pleasing to his eye.
                                Cost: 3 Psi per 5 Size of material shaped, none if used on resin created by Spinner
                                Dice Pool: Dexterity + Crafts + Hammers
                                Action: Extended, 1 minute per roll
                                Effect: The Monarch uses his psionic prowess to mould solid matter, effectively softening it to roughly the consistency of a thick treacle before telekinetically shaping it to conform to his desires. In order to do so, the Monarch first states what he is shaping and what form he desires it to have. He then spends the requisite Psi and rolls with the base dice pool. The successes required are determined by adding the target numbers of the hardness of the starting material and the complexity of the desired form, as shown in the tables below.
                                Material Target Number
                                Loose dirt, clay, resin or sand +1
                                Compacted/hardened dirt, sandstone, soft metals (lead, gold etc) +3
                                Cement, shale, slate, plastics, tough metals (bronze, copper etc) +5
                                Granite, basalt, marble, hard metals (iron, steel etc) +7
                                Diamond, titanium +10

                                Complexity Target Number
                                Simple (a rough column, a crude blade, four rough walls) +1
                                Moderate (a couple of rooms sunk into a cliffside, a simple statue, a well-made sword) +3
                                Complex (a lifelike rendering of a person, a simple firearm, a simple house) +5
                                Masterful (a perfect classical statue, a well-made gun, an ornate building) +7
                                The Monarch can roll a number of times equal to his Authority + Hammers + Stamina before having to spend the Psi again or abandon the attempt. On a Dramatic Failure, the Monarch loses all control and the material to be shaped twists into some strange, alien and - more importantly - useless shape. A Monarch may only attempt to shape Size of material equal to (5*Authority) in one go. LArger constructs require multiple separate uses of the Mandate. Constructions made using this Mandate have Durability and Structure as informed by their material.
                                Constructs created with Shape last a number of days equal to Authority before collapsing into gravel or whatever state they would most likely crumble to (a gin to scrap metal, sandstone to sand etc).
