            EDITED LAIR DESCRIPTION AND TRAITS


            the Wyrm of the Ring: Under normal circumstances, a single guardian wouldn't be enough to deter the Prince from persecuting the Circle of the Crone within their domain. But when that guardian exhales fire and has the power of flight, it's not a good idea to go poking it with a stick. The one who kindred have come to call the Wyrm of the Ring is a dangerous and tempermental creature who can swoop down from the sky at any moment and set fire to the Prince's holdings. Whether or not they'd ever actually do so is irrelevant, the point is that the Prince believes they would. the Wyrm of the Ring has no respect for the carefully orchestrated politics of vampire society. If it wants something done, it makes its intentions known, often quite loudly. The first time he came to their aid, he kidnapped the Prince's touchstone right out of her bed and threatened her life in front of the Prince's very eyes. The Wyrm of the Ring is not an enemy that the Prince can afford to antagonize, and so they have thus far backed off their mistreatment of the Pagans.

            But what could the Circle have that would move such a creature to defend them? Some binding ritual, some mystic artifact, maybe even a king's randsome? No, none of these things would sway the Beast to put their life on the line for somebody else. The truth is the Wyrm has a childhood friend within the local Circle. Someone who they've known practically their whole life and grew up together with. Now that they're both monsters, they don't have to hide anything from each other; they're free to be open about themselves. And that is worth more than any bribe you can offer!

            The Wyrm of the Ring's Lair is a raging forest fire. Gnarled roots and thick foliage grasp and scratch at anybody who tries to maneuver through without a proper travel guide. Embers dance upon the wind as flames lick the trees; choking the air with smoke and threatening to incinerate those caught in the blaze. And yet, if one wishes to meet the master of this realm, they must move towards the flames. Locating the center point of the destruction, where the Wyrm of the Ring sits within a glade of ash and charcoal.


            Family: Ugallu

            Hunger: Destruction

            Horror: A great, winged dragon with body scales as black as ink and underscales that reflect the color of nightshade. Their eyes gleam like red rubies in their sockets and wisps of black smoke leak from the corners of its maw.

            Life: Loyal - the Wyrm of the Ring takes care of those close to them and is willing to flout the rules if it means keeping them safe from harm. He's definitely the kind of guy you want on your side.

            Legend: Unexpected - when moved by aggression, the Wyrm of the Ring has all the grace of a bomber pilot. Swooping in from out of nowhere and unleashing chaos and devastation upon their targets

            Lair: Undergrowth, Thin Air, Stinging, Burning

            Atavisms: Dragonfire, Wings of the Raptor, Limb from Limb, Unbreakable

            Nightmares: Behold my True Form, Run, Fear is Contagious

                                            Countess Amber: "Alright, cocksucker. What was so important you felt the need to interrupt my meal?" the countess may hold sway among the Autumn Court, but her temperament is as curt and direct as a member of Summer. She's seen the so called 'nightmares' of Arcadia and she's got to say that she's not impressed. Somebody needs to teach these people what real fear is and the court of Autumn was practically fawning over her for proper instruction in the art of sowing terror.

                                            Countess Amber does not see anybody without an appointment; unless somebody is really insistant on speaking with her without one. When that happens, she makes it very clear that they will NOT be doing that again. She lives in a private, luxury estate bought and paid for by her Changeling allies and guarded by Autumn courtiers. In addition to comfortable living arrangements, it also grants her the privacy needed to conduct her work with as little stress as possible. Truly, Countess Amber feels like a member of the pampered nobility!

                                            Amber's Lair is like a reflection of a fairy forest, tormenting its victims with confusion and distractions. Strangling vines lined with barbed thorns crawl across the environment while a dense mist drifts between the foliage and hides the dangers from sight. There is a sweet, narcotic scent in the air that clouds the senses and makes wanderers hallucinate things that aren't there. Those who become lost within the forest rarely make it back out again, for one reason or another.

                                            Family: Makara

                                            Hunger: Power

                                            Horror: The image of a beautiful, naked woman carved into the trunk of a great tree. Or perhaps that's not quite accurate, it's more like the tree's trunk grew around an actual woman, fusing her to its bark! The wood resists attempts to cut it, as if it has petrified over time; but when successfully damaged, it seeps blood, rather than tree sap. The tree is alive, in more ways than one; it is conscious and capable of free movement. A teeming mass of writhing vines serves as its prehensile limbs with which to manipulate the world around it, and enchantingly sweet aromas disorient the minds of those exposed, drawing their focus towards the imprisoned woman and away from the dangers of the tree. Rotting corpses hang from the tree's branches, the physical remains of those who tried to oppose it in the past now meant to serve as a warning to any future adversaries.

                                            Life: Shy - Countess Amber is a private woman who prefers that others seek her out, rather than the other way around. But they'd better do her the courtesy of announcing their visit in advance!

                                            Legend: Seductive - There are many species of deadly flora who appear beautiful and alluring to those they prey upon. Countess Amber is one such species!

                                            Lair: Undergrowth, Mist, Jagged, Mirages.

                                            Atavisms: Monster from the Deep, Alien Allure, Unbreakable, Looming Presence

                                            Nightmares: You are Lost, You Must Obey, You are Alone, Everything you Do is Worthless
