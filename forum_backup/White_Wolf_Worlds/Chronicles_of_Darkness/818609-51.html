    Originally posted by -metablender View Post
    Been away for awhile but nice to see that there's been activity in this thread!

    Amazing work that's been going into Genius thus far. I find when looking at any Chronicles/2E project it helps to be able to summarize game mechanics and features down to their core points in order to be able to understand the fundamentals, and then build the lore and characters and fluff to Storyteller and players' delight around that. Cheat-sheets if you will. So after looking at some updates in the drive folder I wanted to ask for clarifications here:

    With the change to Impetus, which is condition based and tied to Inspiration values, is there no more Mania point fuel? With Impetus being more like Beast Sateity, rather than the common variations of Vitae/Glamour/Mana/Pyros etc.
    Right, there's no Mania points anymore. If you do something that would Bind Mania, then it just counts as an Impetus sink long-term. If you're doing something that involves spending Mania (e.g. Kitbashing), then you just have an extra Impetus sink for the scene.

    Point of clarification on the Axiom definitions in the Wonder creation rules. I've understood Apokalypsi to be 'information, revelation & communication' with illusion being in the purview of Metaptropi with 'transformation & transmutation'
    That was a change mostly because I feelt it made more sense to me. Apokalypsi is the Axiom of information, and I would describe an illusion as a person receiving false information. Again, you can achieve similar effects with differnt Axiomata depending on how exactly you do it, Apokalypsi is just the most direct path to that.

    Wrinkles appears to be the new name for Faults. Any reason for the name change or just personal preference? I like how succinct example Wrinkles are defined, would there be general and Axiom specific Wrinkles again?
    It was just personal preference on the naming. Genii are arrogant jerks, so I doubt many would feel comfortable referring to the problems in their Wonders as "faults".

    Do Edisons replace Beholden entirely or are they some parallel/tangential/unrelated minor template? I much like the idea of Beholden as acclimatized mortals to a Genius's Wonders and worldview, slowly losing their ambitions and grasp on reality until they are dependent on their relationship/assistance with a Genius (or teeter out in some explosive fashion). I've considered Marty McFly a prime example of a modern Beholden, with his own life but largely defined by is association with Doc, able to (barely) operate his Wonders with the same aptitude as Doc. Doctor Who companions are another example.

    (I'm curious on the use of Edison as the term as per the 'who x in history was really/not a Genius' arguments but there have been people-derived nicknames amongst the Genius tropes before)
    Beholden and Edisons are separate phenomena. A Beholden is like Morty Smith, Marty McFly, and the like. Edisons a related, but separate thing. They have a faint glimmer of Inspiration that allows them to remain their own people in the face of the Genius's Inspiration, and they can even build Wonders that they've learned to understand. As their name implies, they often have a relationship to Genii similar to Edison and Tesla. Edisons can be extremely valuable assets for communicating their worldview to the outside world, but they also have a tendency to backstab and steal from Genii.

    Thomas Edison himself is rumoured to still be kicking around under a variety of false identities, trying to run tech startups off of stolen software Wonders. I kind of want to get away from "X was a Genius/Beholden/Whatever", in favor of "There are some rumors X was a Genius/Beholden/Whatever."

    This may sound strange but if the Peerage and Lemuria have the same broad characteristics/factions, which are going to have some Field affinities, what is really the difference with a Field and a Fellowship? A more niche, cross-departmental Field? Esoteric Paradigms? If the character's knowledge of various Fields is going to be the primary means of determining function and flavor, what use from a game development perspective would there be for Fellowships as a 'z-splat' style faction? Unless it could provide something outside of the options available from character creation and the current Wonder & Field mechanics.

    There's a few other things I wanted to address but getting up to speed is more important for now.
    Fellowships are somewhere between an academic organization and a mystery cult. They have a Field Affinity, but they also have organizational traits that would distinguish them from each other. For example, you might have a Ghost-Busters-type Fellowship and a Fellowship of ecstatic mediums, and while they would both have a Field that dealt in ghosts, they'd have very different aesthetics, entry requirements, grants, and organizational perks. While the Ghostbusters might offer access to their facility and workshops, and might be willing to lend a proton pack-wielding Beholden in a pinch, while the Ecstatics would be able to provide spiritual allies and relatively reliable means of astral projection.

                Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
                Has that actually been finalized, or is it still being tested out? I'm somewhat fine either way (I think), but Mania seems like it allows for smaller units of comparison than Impetus Sinks (which really needs a better, one-word name).
                Nothing here is really finalized. If we come up with something that works better, we could certainly go with that instead. Mania is definitely more granular, which has its own advantages, but I like Impetus because it's not granular. It's flexible and approximate. If you're a Genius who maintains their psychic powers with intense daily meditation and potent psychoactive drugs, then you do that, and you can maintain X number of distinct powers at a time, instead of having one that Binds 2 Mania, another that binds 5, still another that binds 3 but also costs 1 to use. It's kind of a tradeoff between granularity and simplicity to me, and I prefer to err on the side of simplicity and ease in this case.

                I may also be looking back at Wonder creation later, as I can't say that it's objectively simpler (although I think it's at least a little more grokable) than 1E Wonders. Except for Automata. Switching to Retainer and Horror rules for Automata makes it so much easier that the whole Automation Points subsystem, to me.

                Better call. So Edisons have an Impetus/Mania pool, and can recreate (but not Invent) Wonders? But the don't suffer from Jabir?
                Pretty much, yeah. Edisons are sort of, I guess Half-Inspired? They can't make new Wonders, because they don't have that kind of spark behind them, but that means their souls are still intact enough to function in human society without too much trouble. They are the script kiddies and asset flippers to the Genius's deep-level hackers and developers. The main reason they haven't managed to totally infiltrate the Peerage and Lemuria is a mixture of Genius paranoia, and the fact that Edisons can also steal from each other, and do so quite frequently. If a Storyteller wanted to, they could add a whole layer of Inspired society made of corporate espionage and revenge with the Edisons.

                I always saw it as a matter of focus and degree - Foundations are communities of interest to entire Axioms, while Fellowships focus on specific applications or Fields.
                It's a bit of that, too. Fellowships are more specific in their interests and how they work. Some are more worried about the subject, and might have members from a variety of Foundations (or even Baramins!), others are strictly following under one specific worldview, but they all have a specific field of study that they focus on heavily. You can be a member of one of more Fellowships, provided you can maintain Dues (Fellowship Dues vary widely, from research data, to material or personnel resources, or even an Impetus Sink), but you don't want to join too many Fellowships in case of a conflict, and though you can enter and leave them freely and keep access to the Field (provided you've taken the time to learn it), you might get a reputation for disloyalty or opportunism if you leave and enter a lot of Fellowships too often.

                                                So with the change from Mania to Impetus, I wanted to address what changes that would bring to the minor templates and features converted from 1E to 2E.

                                                A Genius gained 1 Mania each day, along with other methods of gaining it. Most Manes and certain types of Wonder required 1 Mania each day, and any Inspired Manes or similar basically canceled out their daily Mania gain/spent. Thus far, easy to see how that works with Impetus.

                                                So then you get to the ways Mania was stored. At the core, Beholden were useful not only for physical assistance and servitude, they could be supplied with Mania as limited by their Intelligence + Resolve. That Mania could be used to empower other Wonders independently.

                                                So if the Genius must still transfer Impetus to a Beholden, making that character a "sink" is it always required for the Beholden to have Impetus to be a Beholden? And if Impetus is more limited in scope, like Satiety, might it instead be the Genius could extend their own Impetus with the Beholden's traits provided they maintain their loyalty? I always figured more was at work than just being Mania/Impetus batteries, as Beholden have their worldview dominated by their Genius's perspective that even their characteristics like Virtue and Vice work differently.

                                                And for the distinction with Edisons, they can generate Impetus but not use it? What is their limit of Impetus without becoming a full blown Genius (or worse)?

                                                Although in contrast, this change of system may better explain the Hollow Men/Clockstoppers, the enemies of the Inspired that become walking Banes to all Wondrous and mundane technology, innovation and development. They just suck the Impetus out of things like avatars of entropy. Which could also be used for invading Manes and stranger things to crop up.

Basically, you can place 1 Impetus into a Beholden, and that Beholden can use it as they see fit, possibly maintain a Wonder (or building one to your specifications), using certain Genius abilities like the Bat Deduction, or just holding onto it for safe keeping. A Beholden without an Impetus suffers the effects of the Soulless condition (although it doesn't progress unless they actually lose their soul).

                                                    And for the distinction with Edisons, they can generate Impetus but not use it? What is their limit of Impetus without becoming a full blown Genius (or worse)?
                                                    They can generate Impetus, and they can use it in limited fashion. Basically, they have Inspiration 0, so they need to get a Generator, sponsorship from a Genius, or go into Stressed if they want to use it. They can make Wonders they have been allowed to analyze, but they cannot produce Wonders of their own invention. I think I've previously compared them to "script kiddies," and it's still the most apt comparison I can think of.

                                                        Speaking of Impetus and Clockstoppers, I had a though about both very recently thanks to some lovely videos I've recently viewed regarding actual science!

                                                        An interesting angle to look at how Impetus is shaping up is very similar to the concept of Dark Energy, the theoretic forces that set the universe into motion and will inevitable lead to the heat death of the universe itself. In this take the Genii are able to channel that Dark Energy, the Prima Impetus, in order to accelerate physical and metaphysical phenomena into new shapes and forms that would otherwise be impossible with humanity's current level of technology. The Clockstoppers by contrast in this theory would be the unknowing avatars for the counterpart to Dark Energy: Dark Matter, the matter that binds the known universe together into something even capable of sustaining form and structure, let alone the potential for sentient life. Rather than an anti-Impetus force they would be more akin to a counter-Impetus force that act as Stasis' agents to negate strong outputs of Impetus in order to prevent reality from being irrevocably changed by the Genii's vision. But then again that's just some sci-fi musing I had. More than anything I'm just amazed that Impetus ended up sharing such similarities with an actual theoretical energy.
