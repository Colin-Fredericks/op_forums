    Spirit's a great arcanum, but it requires you to remember the presence of an entire spiritual ecosystem. That's an extra hassle on the ST sometimes. Me, personally, I love it.

    Originally posted by Newes View Post
    Going off on a tangent - it just occurs to me that based on the abilities of Spriit 1, and the description of the spirits, it seems all these numberless quiescent spirits are on the material side of the gauntlet. Are spirits actually born in the material world and then shift into shadow as they awaken?
    That's a big ol' Maybe. In all likelihood, though, probably not. Spirits of objects rarely awaken. Instead, you get concentrations of resonances in a region sustained for long enough that the essence coalescing in the Shadow forms into a spirit.

    Remember, the Shadow isn't Twilight but nor is it another world, totally disconnected from ours. The Shadow is, to our plane of existence, as the layer of dust coating everything in our world is to us; it holds a similar, recognizable shape, but is ultimately of an entirely separate nature and composition. So whatever happens to one makes echoes in the other.

            But again, I think that the Time spell isn't a spell you lose control over. You're not going to go flying into a wall because from the caster's perspective, the world will be slowing down, instead of them speeding up out of control. The character can control their own speed from their own perspective, but they cannot control how it will appear. If your potency is giving you 3x speed, you will seem to be going 3x faster than you think you are to all observers, period. That's what I think, and I think it works well.

            A space equivalent though probably definitely would send you plowing through a wall if you're going too fast to control what you're doing, but in that scenario instead of altering time, you're traveling several steps further for each step you take, so your perception will have to keep up with your actions. In Acceleration, since it is time based, your perception matches your speed so you can just take a casual walk and everyone else will see you zipping around.                                     Tamers of Rivers are just the best. I actually did a conversion for them for 2e, because my Thyrsus is trying to join that Legacy. It ended up structured as follows:

                                    All primary attainments require Matter. All secondary attainments require Life.

                                    1st Attainment: Revealing the Obstruction

                                    The Traveler's eyes are open at last, and she sees the flowing of the rivers of energies through all things. She can see their clarity with uncanny perfection; sensing any distortion in their flow as clearly as she might see a wall in front of her.

                                    This attainment functions as Discern Composition, though it only works on a liquid or predominantly liquid substance (i.e. wet sludge oozing out of a factory's run-off drain would trigger it, but a pile of mud would not). It activates reflexively when the Mage encounters any sort of liquid, and communicates its composition directly. While the Mage doesn't know the scientific terms of the substances within the solution (unless they have sufficient Science or Medicine dots to suggest that they are), they get a general sense of the "meaning" of the solution. That is, a seemingly pure stream contaminated with heavy metals will register to a Traveler as unsafe to drink, regardless of if they're aware of the dangers of heavy metal poisoning or even that such a thing as heavy metals exist.

                                    In case it isn't obvious, this attainment means it's near-impossible to poison a Tamer's drink. Even poisons in their food become instantly noticeable once the mouthful mixes with their saliva.

                                    Optional Attainment: The Unifying Truth

                                    The Rivers - the waters - are a metaphor which is also true. As the Traveler can see the flow of water in the living world, they can see the flow of live in the waters of the body.

                                    This attainment resembles Analyze Life. Unlike the previous attainment, it is not usually automatic. In most cases, to properly analyze someone's health, the Tamer must touch the subject's forehead to feel the heat of their blood or examine the colour of their urine or otherwise take a moment to get a sense of the flows of the person's body.

                                    The special exception to this is extreme illnesses and the undead. If a person carries a severe and potentially fatal illness, or one that is in any way supernatural, the Traveler's senses pick them out instantly. Something about the rasping breathing or the faint sheen of sweat tips them off. Likewise, the undead stand out because they aren't alive; like a bubble in an otherwise empty sea, they stand apart. Tamers recognize them on sight.

                                    2nd Attainment: Dowsing the Depths

                                    The Traveler's senses expand. One does not need to see the River to know its presence; one can hear it; one can smell it; one can taste it. The scenery changes before one ever sees the waters, as life draws deep from its source.

                                    This attainment resembles Detect Substance, but it may only target liquids. It has the Instant and Advanced Duration Reach effects applied. One unique characteristic of this attainment is that bodily fluids are an acceptable target of the spell, provided the Mage also has Life 1 (as just about every Traveler does). Said fluids do not need to be outside the body to be targeted, either, meaning that this spell can allow the Mage to, say, detect a hiding person through a wall.

                                    Optional Attainment: Purifying the River Within

                                    The Tamers of Rivers command the waters of life, just as they are commanded by them. This attainment starts within, and then, gradually, moves without.

                                    This attainment resembles Purge Illness with the Instant Reach effect applied. The Tamer can technically apply this effect with nothing but a touch, but many choose to perform some kind of washing ritual with it, like a baptism, to wash away the sickness.

                                    This attainment activates reflexively any time the Tamer is exposed to a contagion. For this reason, none of them ever seem to be sick.

                                    3rd Attainment: Commanding the Waters

                                    The Tamer truly begins to tame. With this level of progress, the Tamer has become one with the waters enough that her will is its will. She bends and the River bends with her.

                                    This attainment resembles Shaping, with the Instant and Sensory Range Reach effects applied. It may only effect liquids (surprise), but once waters are shaped by this attainment, they hold the shape given to them. Such shapes have a Structure of 1 (it isn't hard to break water) but a Durability equal to the Mage's dots in Matter.

                                    This attainment's Duration resets automatically every turn the Mage is in contact with the liquid in question. Yes, this means Travelers can walk on water.

                                    Optional Attainment: Flesh as Water

                                    A miraculous property of water is that it cannot be broken. Run it through with a knife and it does not notice. Scatter droplets on a sizzling rock and they shall turn to vapour, condense as clouds, fall as rain, and become the River once more. Water is an eternal and contiguous whole. By practicing this truth, the Tamers can perform their most famous miracles: The sealing of flesh, as water.

                                    This attainment resembles Knit, with the Instant Reach effect. Tamers are able to extend the effects of this spell further, applying any one of the Reach effects listed with the spell (restoring Lethal instead of Bashing; healing Personal Tilts; undoing deprivation; reproducing a full night's rest). However, doing so costs a point of Mana and inflicts a point of Resistant Bashing damage on the Traveler, as exerting the level of "force" required mangles their own flows of living energy. Multiple effects may be applied at once but the cost must be paid for each, to a maximum of 4 Mana and 4 Resistant Bashing damage.

                                    4th Attainment: Reshape the Vessel

                                    The River is all things and all things are the River. The Traveler passes through their limited understanding of what it is to be as liquid and emerges on the other side, transformed. After all, are not all things transitory? All shapes merely the latest mask worn by every piece of the cosmos through time and space? Every droplet of water has been in the sea; the sky; the earth; the body; the soul. It has been stone and ice and flesh and blood and everything else. The Traveler need only remind it of its birthright.

                                    This attainment resembles State Change, with the Instant and Advanced Duration Reach effects. The Traveler may touch any solid object and convert it into a liquid state. Once it is in its liquid state, it may be commanded with a spell, or with the Commanding the Waters attainment. As with that attainment, the Duration automatically resets so long as the Traveler is in contact with the substance in question. Once the effect wears off, it retains whatever shape it held before.

                                    This attainment may also be used to target water, turning it into one of its many solid forms or directly into vapour.

                                    Optional Attainment: Into New Wineskins
                                    Special Prerequisite: This optional attainment requires the Long-Shifting Merit.


                                    What the Traveler does for objects, she may also do for herself. Her flesh is as mutable as the world around it, and it answers her will as surely as the waters of the River.

                                    This attainment resembles Shapechanging. It may only target the Traveler herself, and has both the Instant and Advanced Duration (thanks to the Long-Shifting merit) Reach effects applied. Since this attainment requires Matter 4 anyway, the Mage has the option of their gear changing to fit her new form, or simply blending into her flesh.

                                    ---

                                    That's all of the attainments. I never made a fifth because it didn't seem likely the game would make it to Gnosis 8.

                                    The prerequisites for joining were changed to needing Empathy 2 and at least one dot in both Medicine and Occult. The third attainment requires Empathy 3 and Medicine at least at 2.

                                    Oblations are unchanged, as is the method of initiation. You die so that you can be reborn, by drowning in the river and becoming one with the source of all things.

                                    Legacy Yantras: Washing a wound or ailment mark (+1); immersion, particularly in flowing water (+1, or +2 if this inflicts a nasty Tilt); succeeding on an Empathy roll to understand the target's emotional state (+1); taking ego-suppressing drugs like LSD (+2, so long as the adverse effects aren't suppressed).

                                    Naturally, Tamers also have a lot of associated sacrament and environment yantras; things like sacred pools, water from holy springs, etc.
