                        What happens if a Cryptic drains all Aether from a demon? AFAIK:

                        If the demon is in demon form, it's stuck because it costs one Aether to return to human form.

                        If the demon is in Cover nothing happens. However, they can't use any powers that require abilities that require Aether (duh).
