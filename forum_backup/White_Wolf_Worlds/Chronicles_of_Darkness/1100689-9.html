                    Here's a bit of what I hope to see in these eras in particular:

                    One Thousand and One Nights - Really would love to see a more fleshed out version of the Islamic Covenants we heard in Ancient Mysteries. Also, this era seems strange because it covers quite a lot of time, but I'm sure they know what they're doing.

                    Rise of the Last Imperials - I wanna see how the Arisen mix their mythology with the chinese one. Also, Hunter Covenants and Conspiracies with chinese flavor.

                    Seven Wonders of the Ancient World - Perhaps an ancient lineage can show up? And the refinement of Gold might need another retouch at this era.

                    Light of the Sun: Europe - I'm curious about this one as well. Seems broad, but perhaps it will focus more on Italy for instance.

                    The Great War - The synopsis already feels fucking fun. I expect to love this one!

                    French Revolution - Rise of the Carthian Movement? Sign me in!

                    Golden Age of Piracy - After running a Geist One-shot set in this era, I really want something oficial. Hope for Sailing rules, as well.

                    Mostly, the types of things I find to be fun are tweaks in the rules to reflect the era we are seeing, as well as finding new "lore", secrets, mysteries and answers.

                                    Good questions. Matthew's pretty active on the forums about project's he's involved in, so hopefully he'll drop by and explain the reasoning. In the meanwhile, the Temple of Artemis was destroyed prior to this era, but was rebuilt shortly afterwards. It was destroyed for good in 401 AD. The Colossus was broken in the earthquake of 226 BCE, but as far as I can tell, remained relatively intact with the shins standing and the rest of the body prone on the ground until being melted the entire thing down by the Umayyad Caliph Muawiyah in 653. It would be interesting to see if the broken nature of the Colossus has a major impact, I could actually see it being an even more important pilgrimage location for Prometheans due to its flawed nature.

                                    And yes, it would likely be safe to assume that Parthia and Ptolemaic Egypt will both get some page count in this era.
