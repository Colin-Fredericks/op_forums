                Bear in mind that Deviant is probably a high-mortality game: you're fighting to keep your Scars in check, because otherwise they'll consume you and you'll die. That's what a Blowout is: because the strength of a Variation is tied to the intensity of a Scar, a Scar that goes out of control goes hand-in-glove with a swell of power that lets you achieve some impressive results before you die. Without some mechanism for healing Scars, you get a ratcheting effect where every time you power up, you're one step closer to going boom.

                Put another way: without some way to de-escalate, deviants are terminally ill; the best they can do is to postpone the inevitable. Restoring “normal human” status to one would essentially be like curing a lethal disease.

A Renegade's best hope is stability, hoping they don't get worse; there's no getting better so all they can do is fight their Conspiracy or stick close to loved ones
