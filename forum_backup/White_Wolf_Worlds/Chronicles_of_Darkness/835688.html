    Request for advice: playing future Covers

    02-23-2016, 08:35 AM
    Hi. I would like to propose a game to my gaming group. I would not tell them the name of the game. They would play normal individuals, that are slowly losing the battle against various threats, and find themselves in a position to accept soul pacts, without necessarily understanding what that entails. Then, little by little, demons would replace the poor guys/gals and help the process along for the other members of the circle. (I would take the player apart and brief him.)
    Depending on the actions of the players, I envision two outcomes: either they go full demons or they manage to pay their debts and solve their problems without losing themselves in the process.
    That is the rough idea. I am looking for advice.

      There isn't a correlation between a cover and a demon beyond the facade they represent. Once the mortals are assimilated, if you let them play the demons cashing in, they become different characters altogether.

      That is, per RAW. Maybe those specific individuals were part of some esoteric Infrastructure. Maybe something of them lingers in the demons, or /and angels. Maybe they fell before being summoned by a matrix.

      Let us know how you spin it.

          Exactly. I would take the player apart and tell him "Your old character doesn't exist anymore. Once [event from the contract] happened, the salesman/seductress/politician you signed the providential contract with was able to incorporate your whole existence to his own. Let's talk about your new demon character, wearing your old character as a Cover in a game of techgnostic espionage. Don't tell the other players at the table. Your objective is now to help your demon buddies achieve the contractual conditions required to complete their own soul pacts. Functionally, you are now an enemy of the other player characters. By the way, the clock is ticking for your demon buddies as their old Cover is very brittle."
          That is the basic idea. Looks like a way to introduce the players to a life of paranoia. I am looking for ideas on the way to proceed, like the general setup of the game. The context would be demons trying to infiltrate some place they think is valuable. A government agency? A government? A megacorporation?

              Something that may put a stick on your wheel is that all terms of the contract must be spelled out, and IIRC, it won't work if they don't know what it entails.

              Demons usually sign outright Soul Pacts by playing on sheer desperation. A deal with the devil to have my daughter's life restored, for example (there's an Exploit for that).
