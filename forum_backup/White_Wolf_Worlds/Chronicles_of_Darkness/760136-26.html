                                I'd almost being in favor of Provinces being nothing more than a collection of tags. Some may have more, some less. You might assign values to specific traits such that you can say "X is a 4-dot Province" because it possesses Bio-Diversity ••• and Metal Deposits •.                                             Ideas for tags that could be used for areas: Water-laden, Diseased, Radioactive, Abandoned Structures, Cleared (as in open land), Bugged (the spying kind), Salted (previous owner removed anything useful and inverted it), Extreme Cold, Extreme Heat, Fault-line (risk of seismic shenanigans), Flammable.


                                            Anyway, here's the new censer-ation! (that pun works better when spoken)

                                            Censers
                                            Air is most notable in its absence. A Monarch who has studied Censers can manipulate fog, smoke, and other vapors, creating a tool out of what others take for granted.

                                            Active Powers
                                            [1] Smokescreen
                                            With a hiss of breath, the Monarch can fill an area with fog, hiding anyone in the area.
                                            Cost: 1 Psi
                                            Action: Extended (1 round/roll)
                                            Dice Pool: Stamina + Authority
                                            Duration: Scene
                                            Dramatic Failure: The Monarch chokes and coughs, drawing attention to herself. She takes a -2 penalty to all Stealth attempts for the rest of the scene.
                                            Failure: The Monarch makes no progress.
                                            Success: The Monarch makes progress. She fills an area the size of Successes cubic feet with fog. Any inside the area are treated as fully concealed, and suffer from the Blinded Tilt.
                                            Exceptional Success: The Monarch can choose to blow away the fog instead.

                                            [2] Air From My Lungs
                                            A Monarch can alter his need for air, replacing it with another.
                                            Cost: 1 Psi (free in Noble Form)
                                            Action: Instant
                                            Duration: Scene
                                            Effect: A Monarch can change what gasses he needs to survive. He can replace a need for oxygen with any other gas, such as nitrogen, hydrogen, carbon dioxide, or dihydrogen oxide. He does not count as an aerobic being unless he chooses to require oxygen. He can ignore lack of air for minutes equal to his Stamina + Censers.

                                            [3] Blowhard
                                            With the strength of the her lungs, a Monarch can send an opponent flying with a gale-like wind.
                                            Cost: 1 Psi (free in Noble Form)
                                            Action: Instant, Resisted
                                            Dice Pool: Strength + Authority - Defense
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Dramatic Failure: The Monarch blows herself over. She falls Prone, and cannot rise until her next action.
                                            Failure: The Monarch creates no wind, or not enough to affect her target, and nothing happens.
                                            Success: The Monarch picks a target, and can do one of the following per successes:
                                            -Knock the target Prone
                                            -Push the target back 10 feet (can stack). If the target hits a solid, unmoving object, he takes 1 bashing, ignoring armor.
                                            -Distract the target, giving him -2 to Defense for the rest of the round
                                            -Attempt to remove something from target (can stack). Roll successes spent vs. Wits + Strength. If Monarch succeeds, the item is removed from the target.
                                            Exceptional Success: No further benefit beyond multiple successes.

                                            [4] Climate Control
                                            Whether the weather is cold, whether the weather is hot, Monarchs can alter weather altogether, whether we like it or not.
                                            Cost: 2 Psi
                                            Action: Extended (1 roll/minute)
                                            Dice Pool: Manipulation + Authority
                                            Duration: Day
                                            Dramatic Failure: The Monarch badly manipulates the ambient air, causing a bolt from the blue to hit him. He takes 3 lethal from electrocution.
                                            Failure: No successes are generated.
                                            Success: The Monarch makes progress. He can alter the weather in the next scene. The Monarch requires five successes per difference between current and goal weather, based on the chart below. For example, to go from Clear to Heavy Precipitation takes 15 successes, while to go from Breezy to Calm takes 5 successes. Other changes may be achieved at the disgretion of the GM. Only natural weather may be generated.
                                            Clear == Cloudy == Precipitation == Heavy Precipitation == Lightning storm
                                            Calm == Breezy == Windy == Heavy Winds == Gale == Tornado == Hurricane
                                            Change temperature by 5C -> +3 Successes
                                            Exceptional Success: The Monarch can choose when the effects begin, as soon as the next round or as late as next week.

                                            [5] Area Aeration
                                            By manipulating the composition of the air, a Monarch can create ambient conditions with varying effects.
                                            Cost: 2 Psi
                                            Action: Instant
                                            Dice Pool: Intelligence + Authority
                                            Duration: Scene
                                            Dramatic Failure: The gaseous mixture of the area is altered, but not in a way that benefits the Monarch. An area she wants deoxygenated becomes oxygen-enriched; a place she wants depressurized suffers sudden pressurization. Invert her intended effects based on the Success chart.
                                            Failure: The air is unmoved. The Monarch can try again at a cumulative -1 penalty.
                                            Success: The Monarch can pick a number of discrete areas equal to her successes to suffer an air-related environmental Condition. Example Conditions are below.
                                            Deoxygenated: Oxygen has been removed from the area. Aerobic beings are treated as holding their breath, and follow the rules for suffocation. Resolution: Oxygen-Enriched
                                            Oxygen-Enriched: Oxygen has been added to the area. Aerobic sentient beings feel fantastic, if a bit loopy, gaining +2 to Social checks between similarly-affected organisms, and a -2 to Composure checks. Checks to stay concious from bashing damage gain a +3. Any sparks or open flames cause this Condition to be replaced with the Inferno Condition. Resolution: Deoxygenated
                                            Pressurized: Air has been added to the area. Effective weights are doubled. Strength and Stamina checks take a -2. Resolution: Depressurized
                                            Depressurized: Air has been removed from the area. Effective weights are halved. Dexterity and Stamina checks take a -2. Resolution: Pressurized
                                            Humid: Dihydrogen oxide has been added to the area. Clouds and fog begin to form, and it may start to rain or sleet. Any checks requiring sight are at a -2, as are Stamina checks to resist diseases or infections. Extreme temperatures are counted as one value higher. Resolution: Dry
                                            Dry: Dihydrogen oxide has been removed from the area. Water is sucked from skin; every minute, roll Stamina + Composure. Dramatic: Take 1 lethal. Failure: Take 1 bashing. Success: no effect. Exceptional: Consider next roll a success. Extreme temperatures are counted as one value lower. Resolution: Humid
                                            Toxic Gasses: A poisonous gas has been added to the area, such as chorine or carbon monoxide. Treat the area as inflicting a Toxicity 3 inhaled poison. Resolution: Atmospheric
                                            Atmospheric: The air returns to the standard Earth composition. Any Environmental Tilts related to the air cease. Resolution: Any Tilt above
                                            Exceptional Success: As success, and the Monarch can extend the effects for a full day, if she wishes.

                                            Censers + Scepters: Pheromone seduction
                                            Censers + Spheres: Block scry
                                            Censers + Veils: Ninja vanish

                                                            Speaking of. We're going to want to come up with official terms for the following:

                                                            Powers, because powers seems... generic.
                                                            Cross Principalities, because we need something simpler to say.


                                                            Anyways I have some current thoughts:


                                                            Provinces are probably significanly smaller than real world provinces, probably like every city being something like 10 to 40 of the things depending on size or importance.. France having 40 Provinces in an official probably might have close to 300 to 400 Monarch Provinces all together.

                                                            However, these Provinces probably are much "bigger" in the Beneath, because real world space concerns aren't as much of an issue.


                                                            I'm thinking what Provinces have is a list of the following traits.

                                                            Name Because they might as well be memorable.
                                                            Resources Merits representing the total Wealth of an area as described my Vent0. Types of Resources are: Biomass, Minerals, Energy, and ect. All of these are a type of wealth. Resources type doesn't neccesarily matter so much, except for when tags or traits involve them.

                                                            Tags define what the conditions of the territory are. They are usually for when characters are there and need mechanical representation. Alternatively, they may have an impact on resource acquisition if the tag is bad or something.


                                                            Traits This is where things get interesting. Every Province has one... or more Traits, representing unique bonuses. When a Province is created, the Storyteller assigns it traits based on its resources. Some traits require a specific level of a Resource type, while other traits are not as strict.

                                                            Some example traits based on Vent0's suggestion.


                                                            Eden's Blessing . Biomass in this Province is highly diverse and very suited to build armies out of. When spent on Conscription, instead of the usual benefits, Biomass Wealth automatically raises the Swarm's rank by 1.
                                                            Ancestral Arms An ancient Endless bioform's corpse lies here. Massive and powerful and impossible to. However... you might be able to clone a smaller... easier to control version.
                                                            Oracle An alien ship was found here. It contains a single AI that is unable to be transfered. It might prove to be a valuable Advisor.
                                                            Evolution Pit Here is a pit, a spawning pool full of pure Endless DNA left as a result of the Kingmaker's experiments. The waters here could bring you more in line to one of the True Endless... until your genetics reassert themselves and reject it.



                                                            You may even consider offering the services of your Provinces to other Monarchs... for a price of course.
