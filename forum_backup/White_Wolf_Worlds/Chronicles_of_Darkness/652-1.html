    Well I've already posted it's demonic form in the topic related to Demonic forms so here is the idea of what I would create other than it's looks.

    Messenger/Integrator

    It did not have a name as an angel but it has chosen Wheel as it's name. The name that transcends it's covers which it never has much attachment too. As an angel it was tasked with the singular task of bringing down the the corporate structure of a fortune a local drug store. It noticed an opportunity to bring down the entire chain and recognition how it would benefit the Machine and recognized that doing this other thing would bring more power. In that moment of decided to topple the chain, It fell.

    Now somewhat angry (and in the cover of the head of the drug store chain) it finds itself with a feeling of a loss of power. It misses the connection to power it had as an angel but is angry at the machine for casting it down for being smart. An ever seeker of power, wheel looks for ways to rejoin the machine, after a few tweaks of coarse.


    Frequent Story Teller for the Circle of Five gaming group.

            For the last hundred or so years, she was part of an experimental project in which she was assigned to be the handler of one of the Deathless. When the Machine wanted a powerful, reusable asset, IT would activate her, she would wake the mummy and keep him under control. she did this by masquerading as his wife, using his broken and fragmented memory to convince him she had ALWAYs been his wife, and that he served justice, not a machine. Once, she was even tasked to ending his descent when he went awol. He was kept in his stone tomb unused since then, until Sothis woke him. He went rogue once again, after his memories began collecting. Aware that the sothic rising would be difficult to manage, she was ordered to incapacitate him so that her supervisor could make the decision of putting him in a very sealed tomb for a very long time. Somehow, she hated the idea of her false husband being trapped forever. assigned as his handler, she hoped that the sothic cycle would let them live happily for years, so she might really feel like a wife. She refuses to kill him, and instead takes her own life so that her supervisors would not activate external override and force her. In choosing to die, she chose to be free. she fell--and the impact of her fall, and the subsequent birth of her Cover, made those lies she had told him for so long REAL. now, she IS his wife.. and now, they are both free of the Machine.

                            Originally posted by xiongrey View Post
                            From what I've read, most don't get reused but some do (from the sounds of it, it's not uncommon.)
                            I imagine for the Machine, its about efficiency. It took a very long time (and many failed attempts) to whipe the mummy's memory clean enough to be taken advantage of. the "wife" had to get to know the mummy's personality and such well enough to be convincing, even when the mummy's broken memory highlighted different aspects and traits about her in each different lifetime. it would be difficult to copy all of the personality traits that he remembers onto a new copy of the wife. plus, itd be expensive. her infrastructure included all the existing details of his true wife--all of his journals, pictures of her, poetry. psychopomps gathered all of that, and it may have been destroyed in the process of this angel's genesis. its questionable if the Machine could find the right infrastructure to remake that angel--especially for every time the supervisors wanted to deploy the mummy. and the last thing they would want would be the mummy to think something is off or fishy, to go on a rampage because his knew wife was "wrong". This all added up, in the machine's logistics, to meaning it would be more efficient to keep the same angel dormant, and simply turn it on and off when needed.

                                        A Guardian, assigned a Cover as the child of a rich couple who were unknowingly helping create some vital Infrastructure. Assigned insufficient resources to defend them - seriously, his cover was six years old - they were killed by a random mugger in the street.

                                        So, having failed to destroy the threat, he trained and self-modified, using his Cover's resources as their heir. At first, he was simply going to destroy the threat; a mission that had lost its purpose, but still following orders.

                                        But then, he began to see the mugger as simply one facet of a greater threat - if it hadn't been that one mugger, well, there are a lot of muggers out there. He resolved to clean up the streets.

                                        After all, criminals are a cowardly lot. So he would teach them to fear him. To fear the legend he would create ... of a monstrous bat-man that haunted the night, dealing out justice.

                                        (Batman! Inspired by the combination of the Bolt-Hole Merit (batcave!) and the fact that grappling hooks are a Propulsion.)

                                                Mr. Lead, whose primary (and, at start of game, only) cover is a burned-out office worker named John Triton, is a Destroyer-Tempter. He Fell because he got too deep into his cover; he was supposed to "snap" and massacre the entire office in a storm of bullets. Instead, he found himself gravitating toward other unhappy workers, and instead of killing the whole office, he stood up and organized a strike, demanding better working conditions. The murders were supposed to give the God-Machine's human agents a way to have the building emptied, condemned, and destroyed... and instead, the potentially dangerous bit of Infrastructure that the building houses continues to operate (dangerous to whom would, of course, be up to the ST). I've actually begun working on a character sheet for him; apart from needing the three "unknown" Keys of his Cipher, filling out his Demonic Form, and having to decide on his final two Embeds/Exploits, I've almost got Mr. Lead down.
