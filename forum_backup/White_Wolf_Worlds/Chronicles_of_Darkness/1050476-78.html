                        Originally posted by Aeshdan View Post
                        Is there anything stopping a changeling from sealing their own words? And if so, is there any detectable sign that a changeling has done so? I'm imagining a changeling who wants to make it clear that they truly mean what they say putting a seal on their words to underline their intent.
                        AFAIU no, they can't.
                        A changeling can allow another fae creature to seal their words. I don't think they can do so by themselves. If you want to underline your intent, you'd need to find someone and request them to seal you, as a witness to your promise.

                        Oaths seem to be thematically more suited for pledges made by yourself. For instance, while it does affect an enemy, you don't need anyone to swear an hostile oath: the Wyrd is the only witness you need. (If you can't swear vengeance on your nemesis by yourself, while holding the body of a murdered loved one, possibly while it's raining, then something's wrong with these rules).
