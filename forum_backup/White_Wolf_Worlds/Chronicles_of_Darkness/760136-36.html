                                        The Geodics are a race that has lost the majority of its technology since Planetfall, being reduced to mere splinters of their true power. However, that power is mighty, and the ancient ones are able to rebuild, using the tools they have to build better and stronger tools.
                                        If given enough time, they could overrun the world. In order to slow them down, Monarchs would need to destroy their infastructure to prevent them from becoming a threat.

                                        In game turns, as the Chroncicle advances, the Geodics unlock more of their buildings which in turn unlock new capabilities.



                                        Mainframe Nexus
                                        If Geodics were Kings, these Ziggauruts would be their temples and thrones, the place where their bodies and minds lay. From here, they may splinter themselves, albefit this isn’t easy.
                                        Benefits: Allows production of Rank 1 Geodics
                                        Requires: None (free)
                                        Unlocks: Cybercore, Mass Fabricator, Neutronium Reactor



                                        Mainframe Nexus
                                        If Geodics were Kings, these Ziggauruts would be their temples and thrones, the place where their bodies and minds lay. From here, they may splinter and become legions unto themselves.

                                        Requires: None (free)
                                        Unlocks: Cybercore, Metal Extractor, Neutronium Reactor

                                        The Cybercore is the mainline computer and technical control building for the Nexus. Through it, Geodics may assemble more advanced technologies.
                                        Benefits: Predictive Computing, Analytics Scanning, and Cyber Breaching


                                        Requires: Mainframe Nexus
                                        Unlocks: Mainframe Nexus II

                                        Metal Extractor
                                        In order for the Nexus to produce robotics, it needs to acquire materials, common metals and earth to be converted into usable resources.

                                        Requires: Mainframe Nexus
                                        Unlocks: War Factory, Mainframe Nexus II

                                        Though Geodics power the most basic parts of the Nexus with their own power, that power has limits. In order to advance, the machines must become self sufficient, by draining the power of neutron dense matter.

                                        Benefits: Allows production of Rank 1 Geodics
                                        Requires: Mainframe Nexus
                                        Unlocks:, Xenoformer Ring, Mainframe Nexus II

                                        The Nexus must grow more advanced, more complex if it must become the center stage of the Geodic warmachine. More systems are required for this great temple of destruction.

                                        Requires: Cybercore, Metal Extractor, Neutronium Reactor
                                        Unlocks: Mainframe Nexus III, Figment Hall


                                        Xenoformer Ring
                                        The Geodics are trapped in the Beneath, but are destined to be its masters. They intend to make it and the surface world in their image.

                                        Benefits: Crysiformation

                                        Requires: Neutronium Reactor
                                        Unlocks: Mainframe Nexus III


                                        War Factory
                                        The Geodic’s use advanced cybernetics is but a hallow shell of what it was. Still, it is enough to build an army to stand against the tide.
                                        Benefits: Allows production of DT-Battlesuits
                                        Requires: Metal Extractor
                                        Unlocks: Mainframe Nexus III


                                        Figment Hall
                                        Though the Coreminds are trapped in the Beneath, Fragments may rise to the surface. There, they must be able to blend in, disguised in human form, for the opportunities are myriad. Many Monarchs fell thinking their enemy lay underground when in truth the surface was no safe.
                                        Benefits: Gives Fragments the “stealth” options
                                        Requires: Mainframe Nexus II
                                        Unlocks: Mainframe Nexus III

                                        Benefits: Allows production of SV-Battlesuits

                                                            Okay so AceOfAlmonds is almost back so. I've got 3 important things to discuss.

                                                            Staves:

                                                            A Monarch must be lord over even the very space that she claims for herself. She twists and turns it into a potent tool, where her edicts are received.



                                                            1. Dimensional tunneling.
                                                            1 minute casting
                                                            1 Psi
                                                            The Monarch can anchor two points of space so that they lead into one another, essentially burrowing into the very fabric of reality itself. The maximum amount of distance between the two points is equal to her staves rating times 10


                                                            2.Blink
                                                            Instant
                                                            1 Psi

                                                            The Monarch moves to any location within five times of her Staves rating, so long as the space is able to support her. She does not need direct line of sight to even do this.


                                                            3. Sabotage Spaces
                                                            Permanent Upgrade

                                                            Instead of using Dimensional Tunneling to anchor two points together, you may sabotage the connection and cause them to link up to unexpected locations.
                                                            This increase the Psi cost by 1.



                                                            4. Far Casting
                                                            Permanent
                                                            Upgrade

                                                            The Monarch’s skill with spatial warping has grown to the point that she is able to extend her range tremendously.

                                                            She may spend 2 extra Psi (4 for Tunneling) and extend the casting time of Blink to one minute and Dimensional tunneling to 10 and choose to treat her range as in Kilometers.




                                                            Set up some racial traits in the style of mirrors. I realize these are all probably gamebreakingly powerful for a player character but eh… stuff happens. And none of them can pass as human without some serious disguise checks.


                                                            Gitserai
                                                            Advanced Physiology : Due to powerful physiological advantages, Gitserai treat their Strength, Dexterity, and Presence as having attribute caps 2 higher than normal and have 2 damage claws and talons as natural weapons.
                                                            Glide : Due to naturally occuring wing membranes, Gitserai may gently glide dowards or perform
                                                            Telepathy : Gitserai do not speak any language, but have telepathy able to send complex thoughts bypassing language barriers. This telepathy is also usable as a weapon, using a contested roll of Empathy + Manipulation subtracting the lower of the target’s Composure or Resolve and dealing bashing damage equal to Successes plus the Gitserai’s Presence.
                                                            Darkvision : Gitserai ignore all penalties from darkness. However, this comes with the downside of being very sensitive to very bright lights, leaving them stunned. Gitserai must take a Stamina check every turn in bright lights. Failure leaves them stunned as per the tilt or condition for one round.
                                                            Glowing : Gitserai let out a faint luminescent glow from their wings. This provides them with illumination at all times whilst simultaneously penalizing attempts to hide. -3 penalty to stealth checks.




                                                            Modkan
                                                            Augmented Physiology : Due to cybernetic implants replacing brain and muscle tissue, Modkan treat their mental and physical maximums as being 6. This comes at the cost of having inferior social skills due to their lack as a priority. Maximum social attribute and skills maximum is 3. They must be taken as tertiary.
                                                            Blindness Assisted Senses : Modkan do not see the world around them via light due to eons of life underground. They only are able to perceive the world as a result of short range built in radar that extends for 100 meters in all directions. However, anything past 20 meters is blurry and indistinct unless it is another Modkan, resulting in a -4 to Perception for most objects past this range.

                                                            Cybernetic Organs : Modkan have replaces their digestive and immune systems with efficient machinery. They treat toxins, poisons, and disease as having half the severity minus an additional 2





                                                            Kessen
                                                            Machine Biology : Immunity to poisons and disease.


                                                            Protean Existence : As nanomachine sludge that can reform itself. Can freely redistribute attribute dots at will at the start of a turn. May also allocate “dots” into the attributes of “Armor” and “Weaponry”, granting general armor and a melee weapon with the appropriate ratings. Both can go up to 5.

                                                            Alright, so the last thing on my list is…. Something about the Endgame.
                                                            I ‘ve kept you in the dark about this, but I think it’s time to tell you. If you want to read, the below is for you.


                                                            While Vent0 brought up the possibility of ressurection of a Monarch, I have decided that such a thing is restricted, reserved for a special few. Why it is, is very important.
                                                            For you see, it ties to the reason why Monarchs exist in the first place.
                                                            The Endless are not called simply such because of their numbers, but because they truly could never die, existing in a constant circle of death and rebirth, but not just in a individual level, but a societal one as well. It is a constant cycle of Empires forming, rising, and then falling all together in cataclysmic war and disaster.

                                                            Monarchs exist in the Rebirth cycle, with the Kingmakers being part of the Death cycle. In truth they are like the Monarchs they create, but at a more advanced stage, more evolved, yet having maintained their grasp on themselves. They continue to exist to lay the foundation to the next phase and select the new rulers who should usher in a new age. The Terran Endless are the means to the end, a promise to evolve, one of many… The homeworlds of primitive species are preserved so as to maintain a proving grounds and there are many of them all through out the cosmos.


                                                            All for the goal of creating an Emperor, the final stage of life for a Monarch, becoming truly Endless and immortal. Lesser stages of development have shades of this, possessing regenerative or ressurective immortality, but an Emporer’s immortality should be above even that with his nation becoming a lasting memory even in the great and unknowable universe.
