                    One again, need to stress: this is a book about the core lore and mechanics of the game so far. Think of it as the Quickstart, or Playtesting Draft. The full book, which will take a few months, is going to have about 12 chapters filled with all sorts of extra lore, unanswered questions, antagonists, world-building, expanded mechanics, and pretty much all the story-fodder you'd need to run a Chronicle following a group of Usurpers from first discovering their Hearts all the way to the point where they retake the Dragon's former positions as Apex Predator of Pandemonium. (Much to the chagrin of any Deep Ones they don't happen to be on good terms with) I'm really happy you guys are excited about this stuff, but I'd be even happier to hear what you don't like about the core of what we've done.

                                        D̹͖͎E̫̝̳̩̘̬E̹̝̮ͅP̟̥ ̣̟͇O̭̗̣͙̖̥N̠̗̙E̲ -͔͈ ̺̱̝̳T͍͔̱h̹e͖̪͈͍ ̭Dro̫̼̹̖̣w̞͍͈̣n̮̞i̳̥̻n͚̤͓͈g̻̥ ͔̝H̝̪̙͕̤̩̰oṣ̩̣̻̱̩͙ṱ̠͚








                                        The Deep Ones are dark and mysterious creatures from Pandemonium, the place where all things eventually come to rest at the bottom of the Dreamtide. They are creatures of pure potential, who can weave new concepts into their own forms at will, but cannot travel in the waking without a Host...
                                        Deep One Sheet Creation
                                        There is currently no Deep One sheet, and won't be until they're better fleshed out. They are simple creatures, though. For now, you only need to know a few things.

                                        Attributes - Deep Ones do not have differentiated fields for Attributes, only Power, Finesse, and Resistance. Start with 0 points in each and spread 9 as desired. Remember that they have an effective Heartstring of 9, and thus, a stat maximum of 9.
                                        Skills - Deep Ones have 22 Skill Points to spend, but they do not differentiate between fields, so just spread them as needed. Once again, maximum is 9.
                                        Merits - Deep Ones do not have Merits, as these embody concepts which do not apply to them.
                                        Ablutions - Deep Ones always keep around 50 Dots of ablutions attached to their form, but can add and remove them at will. Once again, Maximum is 9.
                                        Purviews - The Deep Ones cannot harness the Will To Power, and thus, do not have Purviews.
                                        Health - 1 for every Point of Ablutions the Deep One has Manifested, meaning 50 is typical. Note that damaging a Deep One also damages the manifestation of targeted Ablution.
                                        Willpower - Deep Ones do not have Willpower, as they simply do not operate that way.
                                        Breath - The deep ones run on something equivilant to the Oroboroi's "Breath", but can have up to 50 points at a time, and can get it from breaking down anything: they just use different stats, like size, structure, of health, to determine what's there.
                                        Machination - What this Deep One is currently attempting to accomplish, and how are they planning on going about it, similar to an Oroboros's Undertaking. Make a clear and actionable goal, and divide it into a 3 to 5 step checklist. When the Deep One has achieved all the steps, they have achieved their goal and can move onto the next.
                                        Other Traits - Deep Ones are immune to Heat, Toxins, and lack of Oxygen, and downgrade any damage that is not caused by one of their weaknesses. This applies to Fire, which they do not have special immunity to. They share the Oroboroi's weakness to Jade and Dark Magic. A creature of the Waking who actively tries to will against their actions rolls Resolve + Composure, and subtracts their successes from the Deep One's.

                                        Note that Deep Ones do not have a Supernatural Power or Integrity stat, nor do they have Conditions or Experience.
                                        Common Deep One Ablutions
                                        A Deep One might have anything as an Ablution. Remember that they are able to remove the forms of things they encounter and add them to themselves, giving the ability to manifest that and causing that object to crumble into raw matter, which can be consumed for Breath. A Deep One who consumes an object can gain breath equal to its size, and they have no concept or care for whether it is considered food. In fact, it is common for them to consume things which they want new Ablutions from. Lifting a concept from an object is a 10-die roll against that object's structure. In the case of a living thing, pick the highest resistance stat.

                                        Deep Ones do NOT have access to any sort of "divinity levels", and are always considered to be in "True Form". This means they don't get any other changes for manifesting or dispelling their Ablutions, but it also means that they never lose their elemental resistances.

                                        The following are common examples of Ablutions which the Deep Ones have easy access to.
                                        • Buff:Mastery - When Manifesting this, the Deep One gains this number of dots in any Merit they want, chosen at the time of each Manifestation. If these dots represented resources, they disappear afterwards. This cannot be used to create new people, but CAN be used to temporarily alter existing ones.
                                        • Breath:Miasma - The Deep One spends a point of breath to create a room-sized cloud of Miasma, which looks, tastes, and sounds like white noise, and induces extreme confusion in anything caught in it. All creatures nearby who are not resistant to the effects of Pandemonium are caught in a space of unmeaning which lasts a number of turns equal to the number of dots in this, meaning that the number of dots the Deep One has in this are subtracted directly from their successes on every roll they make.
                                        • Weaponry:Unmaking - The Deep One has an attack in which they can simply focus on a single target and deal Lethal damage directly. This bypasses all defenses, both mundane and supernatural.
                                        • Tentacles - Creates an tentacle-like appendage, which is usually long and blunted, but can have various traits. For every point in a single Tentacle ablution, the Deep One adds 2 Meters to its length. This appendage functions like an arm with a hand, but with a -3 penalty to anything more complicated than wrapping and slapping. This Ablution can be purchased an unlimited number of times for a virtually unlimited number of tentacles.
                                        • Extra Eyes - For every point manifest, the Deep One gains another eye wherever they want it. Each eye adds 4 more dice to rolls to see things, and means they cannot be snuck up on from the angle it is pointing in.
                                        • Darklight - The Deep One creates a cloud of immaterial, pitch black darkness around themselves, in a spherical area with a diameter of 5 Meters times the number of dots in the ablution. This costs 1 breath and lasts for a scene.
                                        • [Creature] Carapace - The Deep One takes the form of the specified creature, with the number of dots in this being the number of successes needed to perceive that there is something very very wrong, assuming the Deep One is hiding it and not simply using it as a cowl to wrap around their true form, which is effectively just a writhing, formless mass with eyes and small tentacles.
                                        • Host / Human Guise - A Human who has been added as part of their form. (see "Drowning Host")

                                        Using the Deep One
                                        I say "Using" because you're not giving this to a player. Deep Ones are Tier 3 reality warpers who defy human understanding: their thought process is totally relatable to humans, but their experiences, emotions, and desires are completely alien. Deep Ones come to the surface in the course of some sort of otherworldly machination, usually either looking for fresh concepts to add to themselves, or to alter the Waking to directly effect something in the Dreamtide.

                                        Deep Ones are not inherently hostile to anyone, but their machinations may require them to kill someone, possible an Oroboroi. Machinations typically involve some greater undertaking which is of use in Pandemonium, and the Deep Ones only venture far from there when they need to get or do something for that purpose. Deep Ones are completely immortal, and aren't susceptible to the curse of the Oroboroi, so they can be extremely patient with a machination, which may take decades, or centuries to come to fruition.

                                        Examples of Machinations include...
                                        • Removing a facet of Human Civilization which is negatively affecting the Dreamtide.
                                        • Adding a facet to Human Culture which will make the Dreamtide more pleasant.
                                        • Collecting useful Ablutions, knowledge which has not reached the Dreamtide, or new emotions to experience.
                                        • Killing someone from the Waking who has been or may be a threat to another Machination.
                                        • Killing a large number of humans to lessen their influence on the Dreamtide.
                                        • Killing Dragons in revenge for Humanity's initial climb. Note that Deep Ones do not differentiate between Oroboroi and the Dragons whose Hearts they now have.

                                        The Deep Ones often act very sedate, but are absolutely resolute in whatever they do. They do not have remorse or conscience, and usually see most other creatures as tools at best and obstacles at worst. They may show some respect to an Oroboros, Beast, Leviathan, or another Deep One, as they see them as kindred, but this empathy does not translate into remorse. When they express emotions, they are not on a gradient: one who is angry will be furious, one who is pleased will be delighted, one who is sad may simply go dormant and float back down to Pandemonium.

                                        The Deep Ones were around when humans were still living in caves, and they can remember a large amount of history (both real and fictional) and science (mostly disproven theories), and are treasure-troves of Occult knowledge. After all, they've been watching our dreams for a very long time. Because of this, they have a strong enough understanding of the Ora to cross it at will: they must simply roll against the Ora's length at that time and place, and upon success, slip across.

                                        In short, they are resolute, calculative, extremely volatile, and extremely well learned.
                                        The Drowning Host
                                        A Deep One cannot travel for long in the Waking without a Host, as they need to spend a point of Breath every scene to prevent themselves from going Dormant, which dispels all Ablutions and leaves them unconscious in the form of a small, blue and gray colored, bulbous blob, though they will be sucked back into the Dreamtide when nighttime falls. Even without this, the Deep Ones do not have the ability to effectively imitate Humans, and therefore, need Humans to interact with human society for them. This leads to a very macabre deal...

                                        The Deal - In order to gain unhindered access to the Waking, the Deep Ones make deals with a Mortal Human: in exchange for her body and her life, the Human gains access to the powers of the Deep One to accomplish some goal. The Deep Ones may or may not care for what this goal is themselves, the important thing is that they have a body to take them around in the Waking. Examples of a goal a Deep One may assist with are...
                                        • Revenge against an enemy who has taken everything, possibly an Oroboros
                                        • Altering the Waking in some significant way
                                        • Going on a journey to compete some sort of "bucket-list" before dying of a terminal illness.

                                        The Union - In order to complete the deal, the human must willingly allow the core of the Deep One to consume her. This is usually done through a process where one of the two envelops the other, the human lets go of all will to remain a physical entity, and the Deep One concentrates upon breaking her down into every concept which makes up her form. The Deep One consumes the Human and when she wakes up, she has become its Host.

                                        The Symbiosis - The Human retains everything from her sheet in this new state, with the added bonus of gaining everything the Deep One's True Form has at all times. This includes resistances, the ability to manifest and dispel the Deep One's ablutions, and the power to use Breath to heal and power ablutions. The Deep One must share direct control of the body for as long as the union is going, but only needs to spend a point of Breath every day to prevent dormancy, now that it has a Host from the Waking.

                                        Note that the Deep One treats the host as a 9-dot Ablution, and that less than 9 dots would mean an incomplete form. The Deep Ones don't always need the form to be "complete", however...

                                        Length of Tenure - Depending on the goal of both the Deep One and the Host, and how well the union fits into the Deep One's machinations, the union may last for a very short or very long time. Deep Ones have been known to keep a host for mere days, while others have kept a host for Centuries, taking them back to Pandemonium when not engaged in a Machination. No matter how long the symbiosis lasts, however, the end is always the same: once the Deep One no longer has reason to keep the Host, they will discard them.

                                        The Drowning - Once a human has become a Host there is no turning back: they are no longer able to maintain their material being in the waking world without the Deep One, which now has control of their very form. If the Deep One doesn't like something about their form, they have the power to change it. Deep Ones which find the usefulness of their Host waning will begin to alter their form to make it more useful, remove parts to make it easier to maintain, or remove it all together. The Host can resist this, but cannot fight back, so eventually, the Deep One will get its way.

                                        A Host without a Deep One loses any Ablutions and Breath they didn't manage to hold onto, and loses a box of health for every scene in which they are without the Deep One,                                         as their conceptual function begins to melt away, leaving only a husk of non-descript matter. The only way for them to survive without such an entity sustaining them is to cross the Ora into the Dreamtide, where they can replenish their Health and Breath, but this is extremely perilous. A Savvy host might look for another Deep One, but this is temporary. There is only one permanent solution to being an entity without a stable Waking form, and that is to obtain an item which can sustain the forms of the Dreamtide in the Waking...
                                        Like the Heart of a Dragon.
