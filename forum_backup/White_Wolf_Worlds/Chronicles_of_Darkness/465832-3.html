        We had a short session last time, as we could not start arriving until usual start time due to family obligations of the hosts. Also, this had them tired and we had another player unusually tired. This lead to a short session. Still, we have three journals for you. No Tatiana as her player did not feel she (Tatiana) had much to add but grousing. Here are journals from the usual suspects however, enjoy.

        Session XII

        Marek’s Thoughts

        26 June 1920

        I have a moment or two before we have to leave so I will record what has happened so far. Dearbhail, Dacian and Lizette met with the Autumn courts leader, Mercutio, to discuss the necklace. Apparently the Autumn King says that the necklace sounds like a cursed item. He was not aware of anyone in their court having such a cursed object in their possession though. He also recommended that if and when we find this necklace that we quarantine it to keep its influence or curse from spreading further. In addition he offered the services of his people to see what they could do to render the necklace safe once it is found. I thought the idea had merit. I was also fully in support of just destroying the thing. There was some debate among the others whether or not we should “damage” or change the necklace enough to make the magical qualities of it fail, or if we should just have Lizette make a duplicate and use that to give to Christina’s brother.

        I am all for destroying the damn thing, like I said. Though I’m not sure if the brother plans to sell the necklace because it’s pretty or because it is a magical artifact. If it is due to the later, then the duplicate might not work. It all depends I suppose on whether or not Gunter or his buyer(s) can sense the magic on it or not. And if I recall correctly the brother did not want his sister to wear the necklace. This could mean that he did know it was cursed and didn’t want it to affect her. Or it could also meant that he didn’t want people know that he was trying to sell jewels to keep his family afloat. Regardless I doubt the others will agree to give him a functioning cursed necklace back to the man regardless of his intentions, whether they are good or not.

        Mercutio also warned our three Fae friends about interacting with the vampires in town. He is of the opinion that they are dangerous and should be avoided. I agree that they are dangerous, and could cause all manner of problems if they so desired, but I don’t think that they are an immediate threat. We have Alla on our side and she is adept at playing the vampire political game. At least so far as we’ve seen.

        Because Dearbhail was so worried about the vampires, Lizette tried to calm her down by reminding our songstress of her fully functional flame thrower. I think there was a fairly heated, no pun intended, discussion about this as they were walking back to the hotel. It was what they were discussing when they walked in the door. Lizette was assuring Dearbhail that she would not set any friends on fire unless specifically asked to by said friend, and that she had practices in mind for keeping the rest of us free of the fire. I’m assuming that she means she will make sure to aim away from the rest of us and only ignite someone if they specifically ask her to, like Wolfram has once in the past.

        After the meeting this morning and everyone had reconvened at the hotel the discussion of who was going to go to the Salon hosted by the vampires was brought up. I believe that Alla, Fenric, Tatiana, Dacian, Lizette, Wolfram and myself will be going. I am still unsure of Dearbhail. She seemed rather nervous about the whole idea. It was also mentioned that we should be appropriately attired to go to this thing. For Dacian I think this was just an excuse to get a new suit. I actually needed something nice to wear. I have a dressier suit of clothes, but it is not up to the standards that I’m sure will be expected. Lizette had made a suit for Tataina a couple of days ago, so she offered to make one for me and Dacian if we would go with her to pick out fabric and show her which style of suit we’d like. I chose a fairly simple suit and a dark grey fabric. Dacian went a little fancier than I did with his. So, we took our purchased cloth back to the hotel and in about an hour we had two very well made suits courtesy of Lizette. She is a truly gifted woman to be able to craft weapons and elegant clothing with the same amount of haste and ease. My own creations seem so simple next to hers. Though to be fair, I have only crafted charms or items to use in a ritual so far. I have a ways to go yet before I’m considered proficient.

        Once we were altogether there was some discussion about what our plan for this evening was. We debated ambushing them at the train station, at the crypt, or both places. We figured that they would have people at both places and we didn’t really want to fight the whole lot of them at once. Splitting up was seen as a good idea until it was brought up that Ilsa and Gunner would be in different areas and the whole point was to make sure that Ilsa saw how ineffective Gunnar was in this whole affair. So, I think the plan that we have settled on is to have Wolfram go incorporeal and spy on the train station to get an idea of the number of people and how many boxes or containers they have with them. While he is spying, Lizette will drive his body towards the rest of us who will be at or near the crypt. Tatiana and I will need to stay out of sense range. The others probably should too, except for maybe Dacian who can mask his presence, including smell.

        Once back together, or as soon as Wolfram was done gathering intel, he will go back to his body and tell us what to expect. We will then wait on the rest to come or even up the field a bit before they arrive and ambush the new arrivals. Honestly it would be nice to have more information now to make our planning a little easier, but we must work with what we have, and right now that’s not much. I personally think we need to solidify our plan a little more and come up with a couple of contingency plans that we can enact if things go badly. I as a matter of practice I never assume that things will go to plan. If I have a contingency in place then when or if things go wrong I can seamlessly move into the alternate plan.

        I have, since the war, never assumed that I would make it out of any particular mission alive. Even the simple ones. I have learned all too well how bad things can get in a very short period of time. In this instance we will be fighting at least two, maybe more, werewolves and who knows how many humans. I plan on taking down as many as I can and still walk away, but I do not assume that I will be walking away. Some people may call me a pessimist, but I prefer to see myself as a realist. The world is harsh and there are no second chances to make a different choice. I do not automatically assume that I will die either, I just prefer not to be surprised if I do. Of course in my case death won’t be permanent. Won’t that be a surprise?

        Perhaps I should tell them about Reznik? They are pack after all.


        Lizette’s Plans

        I have spent a good day working on getting ready for the next couple of nights. I have made a gown for Alla and suits for both Marek and Dacian. What would have taken a lesser woman hours only took me thirty minutes each. This is because I am awesome. Sure, another Lost with the Boon of the Brownie could make it with such speed, but not, dare I say, with such style!

        In the afternoon Dearbháil, Dacian and I went to town and met the Autumn King at the Autumn Court, a second theater, more traditional than Burlesque. Mercutio is some sort of darkness Elemental with a tragedy mask over his face. I’ve never seen an Elemental King before, though heard of such for the Winter Court. His bodyguard Siegfried is an Ogre, probably a Bloodbrute.

        As have the others, Mercutio made a point of being polite, finding out our business and discussing options. In the end, he could tell us little we did not already know. Messing with cursed relics is dangerous, we should not do it without care. He was more the “political” Autumn King, so while his Occult knowledge was broad he wished to consult other Courtiers. He offered to examine it or have them examine it.

        He was not interested in examining my flamethrower, nor with equipping his court (or the Changelings at large in Vienna) with such items. I suspect, being an Elemental tied to darkness, he clearly fears things that bring such light. A pity. Of course the Summer King did not want them either. Perhaps I should have brought chainsaws. I am sure Mercutio would haven found use for them.

        Mercutio also threw in a warning that Dearbháil rather latched onto about the Vampires. Namely, to be wary of them. I don’t know why everyone is not terrified of vampires. You have a human and Vampire, they go off alone, and you have two vampires. It seems like their ascendancy is inevitable. Dearbháil was more worried about Alla and a Vampire who wore a habit named Magdalena if I recall correctly. Maximillian warned she was prone to blood bonding and Dearbháil’s desire reading concurred. That and what to do about the Salon rather dominated the walk home.

        Dearbháil does not seem to understand my protocols for setting friends on fire. Setting friends on fire is not a thing done without reason and prior consultation. It is rude to simply take a flamethrower to a person, especially if that person is a friend of yours. We discussed using the flamethrower to help Alla as needed. I would, of course, apply it with care.

        En route back I let it slip I may not have been alone minding my own business when the werewolf was spotted. I know Dearbháil caught on; I need to talk to her before she constructs any sort of bizarre narrative in her head.

        We met back as a group (sans Alla) to discuss making our move tonight with the Werewolf elimination plan. After a good deal of debate I think the idea is I will drive with Wolfram’s projection to the train station and observe the size of the group moving to take the items to their car. Dacian will scout the mausoleum for the size of the group there. Wolfram will jump to his body near the rest of the team who will be near enough for Dacian to arrive quickly and we will set tasks as needed. Foremost will be the determination of whether we can handle the group together or need to work separately. I will have my star with the bullets that are silver, my chainsaw my flamethrower, and enough explosive to level the mausoleum. Everything a gal needs to feel secure.


        Dacian’s Book of Shadows

        26 July 1920 - Afternoon

        The morning was devoted to our own endeavors today. Tomorrow there is a duel and tonight there is an ambush upon the Pure. No one could find a reason to be terribly formal under those circumstances.

        I took a pear and a bit of strudel as I moved through at one point, but otherwise found that relaxing in my room, contemplating my swords and the state of Twilight were the best uses of my time.

        During Lunch, Lizette mentioned going shopping for fabrics to make a dress Alla had seen while they went window shopping. Marek and I accompanied our tiny seamstress and I find a few new fashions I quite liked as well. Glancing about the tailors’ supply, I picked up enough material for both Marek and I to have new suits made. That I paid for them was I believe somewhat of a relief to Lizette and Marek. Likely for different reasons. When we returned, it was time to visit the Autumn King. Now we shall learn what the Autumn Court of Vienna holds in store for us.

        26 July 1920 - Late Afternoon

        Lizette accompanied us to ensure that the two Spring Courtiers were properly received. We arrived at the old theatre near Gemütlichkeit at the appointed time. Dearbháil knocked and a large yellow eye peer around the slightly opened door. A deep voice rumbled that the theatre was closed. Undaunted, our Fairest expressively pouted and countered that we were invited. Also how much she loved theatres. The door opened a bit more and then we were allowed inside with a rushed apology for not realizing who we (she) were. The large grey skinned Ogre introduced himself as Siegfried and led us inside. A billowing pillar of darkness stood wearing a tragedy mask at center stage. Mercutio was a Darkness Elemental who proved very charming to us. When I described him as such, Tatiana grew terse, however I simply meant that he was willing to help us recover (and destroy if needs be) the Eye of Iblis. Additionally he was more than happy to get the word out that such a piece was in Vienna to the rest of the Autumn Court. He suggested that there may be an expert on such things but Mercutio’s faith in this person’s social graces had faltered. Lizette volunteered to be a liaison as the Changeling in question apparently has issues with the Spring Court. I can’t imagine anyone treating Dearbháil poorly simply because of her Court, but I suppose stranger things have happened.

        I went from out meeting to change and stand for Lizette to take my measurements. She truly is amazing with the creation of anything. The suit was completed in less than half an hour and was a far better quality than any article I had ever commissioned from a normal tailor. I could tell as I stood by the mirror, it would be very helpful in any persuasive endeavors I may have in the future. I can not say enough about the quality. The stitching could hold a mainsail on the Sea of Slashing Squalls and yet the fit was infinitely superior to my most expensive attire.

        Now I believe Dearbháil and I are going to make a bit of a show in the Bohemian district for Glamour. After that, we will begin our preparations for the ambush on the Pure.

        End Session XII

        Entirely an RPing session. We did not want to get into the whole thing with the heist with tired people and the late start, so decided to start with that the next time. So the session was really the Autumn Court meeting, discussion en route home, and planning the heist.

        Hopefully next session will be back to normal. Will see you in two weeks.

                                                    New Journals! Tatiana’s player had to miss due to family crisis. As she is heavily combat focused, we decided to say the shipment had been delayed to the next night, and just roleplay the intervening time. It went well, we got some good RP in, and the characters got to test their mettle against each other in friendly contests.

                                                    No Tatiana, but an extra Dearbháil this time.

                                                    Session XIII

                                                    Marek’s Thoughts

                                                    26 June 1920 (evening)

                                                    Well, that was disappointing. The shipment of goods coming into town was delayed, so in turn our ambush of said goods was delayed. The train should be coming in tomorrow, along with our quarry. So, we had free time to kill this evening. Lizette took Dearbhail aside to have a private conversation. I’m not sure what it was about but they both came back in good spirits so I’m assuming everything is okay. While the two ladies had their chat Dacian and Wolfram decided to spar with swords in preparation for our ambush tomorrow night. They both did well and fought with skill. It seems that Dacian’s lessons with the ghost are bearing fruit after a short time. That is good.

                                                    Once the ladies came back Lizette it seemed did not want to be left out of the sparring so she challenged the two men to fight. Dacian stepped back, content to watch, and Wolfram accepted. Neither one wanted to lose so they both made good use of their own particular skills and magicks to aid them in their fight. They were exceptionally well balanced and fought really well. I can only hope that their skill from tonight is just as bright tomorrow evening.

                                                    They fought twice, both times going full tilt. Wolfram won the first match and Lizette won the second by a slim margin. After they were done sparring. The slight inventor asked if anyone else wanted to have a go at it. Dacian indicated that he was fine where he was, so I said that I’d spar against her. I will admit that I am not as practiced with a sword as I am with a rifle or my claws, but I do have some skill. I knew from the onset that I would not win the fight. Lizette’s fae abilities were still partially animating her sword and I have no magicks that will aid me with a blade. The fight was fun and exhilarating though, so I did not mind her beating me. I did find it amusing that Lizette apparently found it necessary to cut several buttons off of my shirt as we sparred. It was her way of giving the others a show, Dearbhail seemed to like the view, and to prove Lizette’s superior skill in a non-lethal way. In the end I conceded the fight and gave Lizette a bow.

                                                    It seemed to be an ongoing theme for the evening and after the match with I sparred with Dacian. I did manage to best the other man, but I think that had to do more with his inexperience with a blade than my superior skill. Still it was fun and informative for both of us. He tends to leave his left side exposed more than he realizes. I pointed this out and showed him how would remedy the oversight. I suggested that he talk to Wolfram or his ghostly mentor for other suggestions as well.

                                                    Throughout this whole thing Dearbhail, Christina and Marelene seemed to be enjoying themselves just watching the rest of us fight. It was rather blatant, and a bit daring on their parts, but I don’t think anyone really minded having an audience. If men can get away with looking at women in such a way then it’s only fair that they get a chance to look at us with the same expression.

                                                    After everyone was done swinging sharp steel at one another Lizette came up with another challenge. She brought out her pistol and asked if anyone would like to have a contest shooting at targets. I smiled and said that I’d take that challenge. Wolfram also volunteered to shoot with his Luger. So, while someone was busy setting up the targets I went to get my rifle. I will admit that it was fun, if a bit quaint, to be shooting at paper targets rather than people. It is certainly a different feeling. On the plus side, the paper targets are not constantly moving this way and that as they go about doing their daily actions. Or running if they’ve caught wind that I’m after them. Moving targets are harder to hit, but infinitely more satisfying. So, while the paper targets were a bit pedestrian I did have fun. Maybe it was because I was distracted by thoughts of my usual targets, but I did not win this little contest. Wolfram actually did. Not by much, but enough to concede the point. I will give him this, Wolfram is a talented solider, and I am glad to have him on my side. Lizette was also rather skilled with her pistol. I’m not sure if she was using her fae abilities or natural talent to accomplish this. To be fair, I don’t know if Wolfram was using any of his abilities either. Regardless, as long as they are this accurate tomorrow during the fight with the Pure then I will be content.

                                                    I believe while we were sparring that Alla went out on the town looking for shows or performances that she might attend. I think she was in the mood for entertainment and to feed. I realize that it should probably bother me that she feeds off the blood of unsuspecting mortals, but it really doesn’t. Even if she took enough to kill someone I’m not sure I’d be bothered all that much. They are not pack, not family and therefore I don’t really feel overly concerned one way or another about them. This is a failing on my part, I know that. My mother tried to help me feel empathy for others, but it only ever extended so far before my capacity to care reached its limits. I will admit that the number of people that I hold in regard has increased quite a bit since becoming a part of this pack. If Wolfram has any say in the matter then Christina will be part of the pack soon enough, and by extension Marlene. My brother also got married, and so his dear wife has become part of the family. If our little group keeps growing as it has been then they may make a compassionate man out of me yet.

                                                    27 June 1920

                                                    It is a new day. Everyone slept a little later than usual due to our martial contests last night, but as we did not have anything pressing on our schedule for this morning it was fine. During breakfast conversation turned to some of our abilities and Lizette mentioned that she can increase the accuracy or efficiency of different kinds of weapons. She offered to aid some of us with our chosen weapons, but could not do it for everyone. It would take a good deal out of her to do just a couple and we need her at full strength and capacity tonight. With this in mind she used her fae magicks on Alla’s silver knuckles and my rifle. We will both be attacking with silver and we are both proven combatants. I would have suggested doing it with Tatiana’s claws, but I don’t think the power works on natural weapons like that. I do not mind gaining an advantage with my rifle though so I will not complain.

                                                    The only task left to us until tonight is to keep ourselves occupied until then. I am well familiar that waiting for the fight is usually more nerve-wracking than the actual fighting is. Battle goes by too fast to do anything but attack, defend and dodge. All we can to do now is finalize our plans and wait.


                                                    Dacian’s Book of Shadows

                                                    26 July 1920 - Night

                                                    While I am in no way racing to meet my doom, I did find myself disappointed when we learned the train from Berlin was delayed. There would be no ambush tonight.

                                                    To take the edge off and get more practice in, I moved to the inner courtyard of the Esterházy estate with my blades. Wolfram asked if I would mind an opponent. This immediately summoned the ladies. Dearbháil, Lizette, Christina, and Marlena all came to bear witness. Wolfram called our match a draw, but he was being gracious. Lizette whispered something to her blade, threw her jacket over her shoulder like a matador, and strutted like a peacock out to us. I knew the attention of the observing ladies was more the goal of any further competition just as I knew cheating would soon commence. Thus I bowed out as Lizette tossed down her jacket and crossed blades with Wolfram.

                                                    They looked majestic as their steel clashed with palatable power imbuing each thrust and parry. Each participant took one contest and looked magnificent, even in defeat. Out of respect for the proper spirit of competition, the two bowed and moved in separate directions. Lizette offered to spar with both Marek and myself. I again declined on the basis of dignity. I did smile as Marek joined the Wizened when I thought of how the playing field would be leveled when I held some command with the Contract of Blades. Dearbháil was quite attentive to this match though from my vantage, Marek was finished before he began. I had to wonder what Lizette’s skill would look like without the benefit of her Contracts? When the buttons flew from Marek’s shirt exposing his toned chest, Dearbháil barely muffled a giggle. Lizette was gracious enough to offer to repair the damage she had caused to the shirt. I again remembered how wonderful the Parisian could be.

                                                    As the Wizened left with Marlena and Wolfram with Christina, I offered to spar with Marek as I knew I could receive an honest match. The Uratha accepted and did not disappoint. I was bested but with some effect. We shook hands with warm smiles in the end. He even offered a few points for me to be aware of. I apparently leave my left side open when I advance. It is good to learn this in friendly competition rather than deadly combat.

                                                    Now I shall try to still my tense nerves. Tomorrow shall be less friendly.

                                                    27 July 1920 - Afternoon

                                                    Much of last night’s joviality burned away with the morning mists.

                                                    We joined together for a late lunch and most of us have since found ways of relaxing before our train arrives. While I still do not share in Tatiana’s seeming hunger for battle, I will be happy when it has come to an end. I will be more pleased to see all of my cabal standing when the smoke clears.


                                                    Dearbháil’s Letters

                                                    My Dear Janos,
                                                    Vienna is still a wonderful city though I do not believe the residents entirely believe the world has changed as much as it has. Despite the change in the noble class’s stature in Vienna they still believe they have the upper hand. Of course the noble’s involved are werewolves – a mad faction of the werewolves known as the Pure. I don’t entirely understand exactly what they hope to gain but they are the enemies of my dear wolves Marek and Tatiana. – I have to wonder if it would bother them that I refer to them in such a manner. It seems terribly affectionate to me, but my brothers always said I was a bit “off” in what I find to be appropriate.

                                                    I hope you get to meet them at some point in time. Oh! Perhaps you can come to the wedding! Not my wedding of course – but Wolfram’s. I feel sure they’ll be announcing a date soon. I am afraid I let the secret of his plan to ask for his lady’s hand out before he had the opportunity to talk to her. I was ever so embarrassed. Stop laughing! I am quite aware I tend to speak before I think sometimes. But Wolfram was kind and did not get upset with me. I think Lady Christina’s delight in his intentions made up for my indiscreet conversation. So, as I was saying, I’m sure a date will be announced soon. It would be a lovely time to visit our new home in France. You would like Lizette & Dacian and I think Tatiana would enjoy a drinking contest with you. She could give you a challenge I’m sure!

                                                    I should not avoid my purpose for writing any longer. I am concerned, my friend. One of the tasks we’ve undertaken in Vienna has shown me that I could be a liability to my motley and I do not want to put them at risk. A few days ago we were tracking a vampire’s whereabouts. Well, Tatiana, Marek, and Dacian were. I was along because I thought it would be nice to spend time with them and as we’ve observed with Alla, vampires are not active during the day. We didn’t believe there would be any danger but we were mistaken. The vampire was quite terrifying and somehow I seem to have alerted it to our presence & he attacked.

                                                    The others defended against him admirably but more vampires were coming so we had to leave quickly. I did attempt to help defend against the vampire – but despite the lessons in fighting my brothers gave me as a child – I was very ineffectual. The others do not see me in this way, I believe they are very gracious to acknowledge that my talents do not lie on a battlefield. But I feel as if I could be a distraction if I cannot defend myself properly. I do not believe I could, or wish to, become a warrior – but I would like to make a proper showing of it if someone were to attack me.

                                                    It is possible I will have to deal with an attack in the coming hours – I am sure it will be fine, I won’t be among the fighters but nearby just in case they are in need of healing. I am quite please to know I can assist in such a manner. But my martial skills must be improved! My dear friend, may I come to you once our time in Vienna is done for training? I will not make the trip until I hear from you. I would like to keep this from the others simply so they do not try to discourage me. I do not believe they would disapprove but I do not know if they would understand why I feel I must do this.

                                                    Last night as our plans were changed at the last minute my friends engaged in contests of skill and sparring. It was enlightening (and thrilling) to observe them. Wolfram is a soldier – his martial prowess makes sense. Marek’s abilities are of no surprise. I believe he fought in the Great War, but even if he did not, he carries himself as a warrior as does Tatiana. Dacian, with his lithe frame and quick actions also has skill – which he is improving on with the aid of a mentor or teacher. Lizette is a Wizened – she has the capability to make her weapons work even better than they should in the first place. Alla was not with us last night, but I do not worry for her safety. She is a vampire – so far I have met no vampire that cannot handle violence.

                                                    Speaking of vampires – we are going to a salon hosted by one of them in a day or two. I am not happy about this but as the rest of my motley is going I don’t feel that I can stay away. One of the vampires is interested in binding Alla to her after all and we cannot allow that to happen. It is also possible that we can find out where the necklace Christina asked us to find may be hidden or at least a clue to point us in the right direction. She’s terribly concerned about getting it back though her companion wishes it would never return as it is cursed. Christina’s brother asked her to keep it until he has a buyer for it. He’s looking to boost the family fortune and in this situation it appears he was unaware of the cursed nature of the necklace. Or he has no regard for his sister’s safety and if that is the case he should be beaten within an inch of his life. It is the brother’s duty to look out for his sister just as she looks out for him.

                                                    Did I not tell you there would be adventures when I started traveling and building my reputation once more? Are you terribly jealous that you didn’t come with me now? I only jest, dear one, I know you are meant for your blacksmith shop. You are a steady presence in my life though we are so far apart. Please say you’ll allow me to visit once more. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

                                                    Sith agus Sláinte 'bha!

                                                    Dearbháil


                                                    Lizette’ Plans

                                                    So we got all ready to go, and everything got forestalled. I am sure it will be fine; I hope the others are not too anxious. We got some extra practice sparring and exhibition matches, however.

                                                    Before then, I spoke to Dearbháil. I wanted to clarify that I was not engaged with an affair with a specific person, and did not want her looking too closely into my partners, for the sake of my partners themselves. I wanted to shield their dignity. I also dropped hints I am experimenting with women more generally, but I detected no interest. Ah well. Overall it was a success. She stressed she wanted me to be happy. She claimed she was always happy. I hope she was telling the truth.

                                                    We joined the others, and Wolfram and Dacian were doing some sparring. I noticed Christina and Marlene watching, and saw Dearbháil sit to watch and make daisy chains. I decided as much fun as the boys were having, I could add a little challenge to their evening. I enhanced the usability of a sword nearby and sashayed over and challenged Wolfram, saying I would show him how we did it in France. No half measures.

                                                    I had seen boys in Paris practice swordplay, the showy style some indulged in involved a hand on a hip and a lot of motion. I was pretty sure I could fake that. As a Wizened, I am quick, and the sword sought to serve me. I entered with my coat over my shoulder, but dropped it one handed as I moved out to spar. I am pretty sure I even got the en garde stance right. I was something to see.

                                                    Wolfram was quick to challenge. I quickly realized even as I had ensorcelled my blade, he called upon his grave-borne vigor to bring power behind his sword. Our initial exchange favored him, not by a huge margin but the victor was obvious. I pushed more power into my blade, and challenged him to a rematch. The rematch favored me as he was, I think, unprepared for how much I wanted to win. Honestly, so was I. When I saw just how intensely my sword struck under my command I…sort of took it easy. Humiliating Wolfram was never the goal, and, honestly, if he wanted to match again he would have been more prepared. It looked closer than it was, but I was the clear victor.

                                                    I challenged Dacian and Marek. Dacian demurred, but Marek stepped in. I think he was also somewhat unprepared for the magic behind my weapon. But then, claws, teeth and rifles are more his specialty. I maintained enough control to cut several buttons on his shirt and expose his chest, to Dearbháil’s delight. I felt good about the win…and quietly promised to repair the shirt.

                                                    Marek and Wolfram had a turn, which went to the German. Feeling confidant I suggested a test of skill with firearms. I glamoured my pistol well up, and Wolfram and Marek took up the challenge. It seems I was a bit overconfident, as I tied the sniper and Wolfram managed to slightly outfire us both. I did not actually want him to look bad so I suppose it was good.

                                                    Now, I had to ask myself, why did I indulge in the rowdy play with the boys? Why did I indulge in something so archaic as sword fighting? Aiming for a nonmoving target? I am a woman of the future, of the twentieth century. Swords were getting behind the times in the sixteenth century. But ultimately, it was play. It was impressing the other ladies. It was showing the boys that girl or not, I could stand side by side with them. That’s why I did it.

                                                    The rather intense carnality later that night was not such a bad reason either.

                                                    End Session XIII

                                                    The initial sparring with Dacian and Wolfram was just contested rolls, but once Lizette got involved she and Wolfram upped the ante. He used his Industrial Caul and channeled Plasm through his keystone sword, while Lizette involved Contracts of Artifice and Animation to double buff her sword and gun. While she initially only went with Artifice, when Wolfram won the first round she activated Animation as well. She used both (I think) on her gun, while Wolfram used his stored Lugar from his Caul which gave it a bonus. Not the best use of fuel before a big fight, but Lizette at least restored the 9 (!) glamour she used before the combat.

                                                    The duel with Wolfram and Lizette was both described as showing off for the ladies and flirting with each other. They have odd ways of flirting.

                                                    She used Artifice to buff Alla and Marek’s weapons the next day. She pushed for the exceptional success, and while she was able to regain glamour, she was short a couple willpower for the battle. Still the bonuses made Alla and Marek quite potent in the battle.

                                                    Alla did some hunting to top her Vitae off.

                                                    That covers it. We had the big battle tonight, and it was the whole session. One Vampire, one ghoul, six Werewolves, Five Wolf-Blooded, Three Changelings, one Sin-Eater, one Ghost, and three Spirits all were in a huge battle in the cemetery in the shadow of a Cathedral. Marek one shots a Werewolf! Lizette cuts a man in half with a chainsaw! Wolfram and Gunnar have a duel! You simply must come and read it. See you then.
