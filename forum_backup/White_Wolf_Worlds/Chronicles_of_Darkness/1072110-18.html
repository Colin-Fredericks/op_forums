    Mind if I take a crack at that:

    While the Equaliser Grenade deals with the physically shape-shifting, not all creatures need to physically change to hide. Princesses are impossible to identify thanks to the strange magic covering their identity, Changelings and Prometheans have near-perfect human disguises, and Demons steal people’s lives to wear them like masks. While TFV has yet to figure out a reliable way to pierce through these disguises, you don't need to see through a disguise to notice the ENE wearing it is acting unusually. VALKARIE operates a vast automated system called the Eye of Odin which piggybacks on government survelence over social media, financial transations, phones, and the internet in general to flag anything which might indicate ENE activity.
    Function: Once per session, a character with this Endowment can contact headquarters and ask for a report on a specific area. The area can be a school, a sheriff department, a small town or anything, similar. Upon recieving a request a team of specially trained analyists will begin combing through flagged material to identify persons of interest. Even if a Princess fusditiously avoids using social media, the Eye of Odin might still flag a suspicious spike in accademic achievements and positive sentiments on social media which when cross referenced with debit card that purchased a bulk order of socks at 11pm next to a homeless shelter suggests someone is either a Princess or inspired to follow a Noble's example.
    Once the area has been chosen, roll the character’s TVF Status * 2 to requisition a report. If the monster they are looking for has the Anonymity Merit, apply the merit’s dots as a penalty to the roll. Regardless of what you get, headquarters will present your character a list of charachters suspected to be ENEs in the area; the result merely serves to decide how accurate and useful said list will be:
    Roll Results:
    Dramatic Failure: 80% of the people on the list are red herrings, while 20% of the list are legitimately people of interest. Moreover, if the targeted monster has an appropriate ability (such as a Princess’ prophetic dreams or a mad scientist with an AI watching for spies), they become aware that you are looking for them.
    Failure:Your request gets stuck in a queue behind higher priority items. You get the results of a success, at the start of the next session.
    Success: 40% of the people on your list are red herring, while the remaining 60% are people of interest: unwitting pawns, loyal ghouls, Sworn, wolf-blooded or equivalent. None of them is the monster, however. Alternatively you learn that there is no monster present.
    Exceptional Success: Same than success, but one of the people on the list is the monster you are looking for.

            First thing I suggest? Go through the existing Dread Powers in Hunter books looking for similarities to Noble and Dark abilities.

            For instance, I'm looking at the Hunter core. "Dread Attack" is pretty close to the Kensai Charm (close enough for Hunter-only campaigns) - the only critical aspect it leaves out is the ability to deal either bashing or lethal damage. "Impress" has no single match in Princess, but several Charms do things like it.

            Mortal Remains has several more suitable Dread Powers. "Dead Flesh" should be automatic for Nobles - despite its description, its effect is very nearly that of Holy Shield. "Blast" is to Levinbolt what "Dread Attack" is to Kensai. "Cloak of Authority" represents another family of Noble social influencers. "Flicker and Flash" or "Uncanny Reflexes" can stand in for dodging and speedster Charms.

                            A few more no-core group's opinions, because hey why not?

                            Yuri's group is torn. On the one hand, they can't deny that the Nobles do good work and help a lot of the same people they do. On the other, they're intrinsically suspicious of creatures that set themselves up as moral authorities over humans. The Queens and their nobles are less openly hostile in their "lessons" then the beasts, but are they different? Yuri's group isn't sure, and keeps an eye on them for now.

                            The Cainite Heresy doesn't care. Darkness? Nobles? They're not vampires. Vampires are the problem. Let's focus on those. We can deal with the dregs of the supernatural world once the vampires are ash.

                            The Knights of Saint George are firm. Witches are witches. However good their intentions are, they pose a threat to the world and must be put down. When they get a Noble as a target (usually when she's looking into the same few businesses and organisations,oddly enough) Division Six agrees.

                            Ahl al-Jabal are suspicious. They are dedicated to preventing the monsters that stop human progress, but what about the ones that support it? Are the Nobles allies, or simply a more appealing plan to lure humanity off track? Opinions vary- for now, the group waits until more information is available before making a stance one way or another.

                            The Faithful of Shulpae... as mentioned, best not to go into details. Suffice to say, a god's essence is unchanged by its mortal shell, and eating it has the same benefits however harmless it looks. Also, they are on every courts "destroy" list and every dark creature's "make into cult" list, so there's that.
