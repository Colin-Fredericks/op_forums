    You can also check the optional rules for Beaten Down (CofD, pg 87). If Health lost to the mob represent members getting taken out, then suffering casualties could easily result in the mob breaking up.

    [Optional]
    For more resilience, have them roll Resistance (+ modifiers, like good morale, being inspired, defending themselves) - penalties (damage taken, fearsome opponents, etc.). Success = no consequence; Exceptional Success = they get fired up even more and gain 1 Willpower, or get a Condition like Inspired, or something; Failure = Spend 1 Willpower or downgrade to Dramatic Failure; Dramatic Failure = suffer Beaten Down (or just disperse);

            I think you would divide by 3. So, treating Thug #1 as the primary, with 4 equal supports, you'd get Power 2, etc. For the enforcers, 2 + (2 Power * 4 Extra Members = 8 / 3 = 2, rounded down, 3, rounded up) so 4 or 5.

            Power of 1 and 2 is still really low for the individuals. Basically Strength 1, no skill (but no untrained penalty). For average combatants, I'd say the starting point should be 3 or 4 (Average Strength/Power of 2, with 1 Skill in their attack of choice, 2 if they are more focus/active). You can increase from there as the situation warrants.

            For Willpower, you could do the same - highest Res+Com or Resistance + sum of the helpers / 3.

                Why do you need 1:1 multiplication for people power?                         Awesome suggestions, everyone! I've consolidated all the suggestions into what I think is a workable prototype. Here, take a look and tell me what you think.

                        Mob Rules, World of Darkness
                        Combining large groups into a single, simulated “character”

                        The simplest way to run mass combat is to boil the constituent characters’ stats down into three core Attributes (as per Spirits and Ghosts):
                        Power = Attack dice [equal to the average constituent character’s highest Power Attribute]
                        Finesse = Defense (only against Weaponry and Brawl attacks), Ranged attack dice [equal to the average constituent character’s highest Finesse Attribute]
                        Resistance = Health/Morale [equal to the average constituent character’s highest Resistance Attribute]
                        Health: equal to the total number of constituent characters, plus the Mob’s Resistance Attribute.
                        Willpower: a mob has one point of Willpower per member, up to a maximum of [average constituent character’s Willpower].
                        Speed: Equal to three-quarters of the average constituent character’s Speed (rounded down).
                        Initiative: Equal to the average constituent character’s Finesse. A Mob acts as a single character in combat, occupying a single Initiative slot and making one unified attack (usually Power + Finesse) against a given character (or another Mob) per turn.
                        Armor: applies only if more than half the Mob's constituent characters are wearing armor.

                        After calculating the Mob’s Attributes, then add variations or bonuses from supernatural powers or other tricks (e.g., increased Power for vampires, increased Finesse for mages, increased Resistance for werewolves, etc.)
                        Relevant training or tools can add equipment bonuses to a Mob’s dice pools.

                        Special Mob Abilities:
                        • Mayhem: In addition to this single attack per turn, a Mob deals an automatic point of Bashing damage (or Lethal damage, if armed) to every character, object, or structure at “engaged” range (i.e., standing directly next to the Mob) at the end of its turn. This damage can be overcome by damage resistance and armor.
                        • Surround: As a full-round action, a Mob may surround and absorb a single character into its mass and deal a number of points of automatic Bashing damage (or Lethal, if armed) at the end of its turn to the absorbed character; this attack deals a number of damage points equal to the Mob’s Power (the Mob still deals its normal automatic Bashing damage to all other characters who are at “engaged” range at the end of the Mob’s turn. To escape, the surrounded character may (on his or her turn) make an instant and contested Strength + Athletics/Brawl roll vs. the Mob’s Strength + Resistance roll.

                        Each point of damage which a Mob takes represents a constituent character who lost commitment to the cause, got arrested/taken down, was killed, or became too bruised to get up again. (i.e., someone spraying autofire into the Mob is likely to disperse and decimate it far more easily than trying to reason with it with rhetoric). When half of a Mob’s Health boxes are full, have the Mob roll its Resistance + morale modifiers – morale penalties (for damage taken, fearsome opponents, etc.)
                        Roll results:
                        Dramatic Failure = the Mob gains the “Beaten Down” Condition, or simply disperses (at the Storyteller’s discretion)
                        Failure = Lose 1 Willpower or downgrade to Dramatic Failure;
                        Success = no consequence;
                        Exceptional Success = they get fired up even more and gain 1 Willpower, or gain a Condition (such as Inspired, etc.)

                        NB: harming/demoralizing several people at once may count as a breaking point to most people.

                        Example Mobs:
                        5 thugs in a bar fight (Avg. Power 1, Avg. Finesse 1, Avg. Resistance 1) = Power 5, Finesse 5, Resistance 5, Speed 10, Health 10, Initiative 1
                        5 gangster enforcers (Avg. Power 2, Avg. Finesse 2, Avg. Resistance 2) = Power 10, Finesse 10, Resistance 10, Speed 12, Health 15, Initiative 2
