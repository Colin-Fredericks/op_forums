                    Yeah, I thought I was there at the end of Second Stage development.
                    I am experiencing a more and more constricted schedule as time goes on and this fan project is plummeting on my list of priorities.
                    I have at least three other projects to complete before I can give time to DtE.


                    If you want to accelerate the production of the project, you can contribute in the following ways:
                    • Do a full write-up for a philosophy. We're replacing the five in the book with four new ones:
                      • Imperators (Rule by Law)
                      • Paragons (Rule by Example)
                      • Viziers (Power behind the throne)
                      • Idols (Rule by Celebrity)


                    Philosophies need to answer the following questions:
                    • How the Dragon rationalizes her membership in the Oroboroi
                    • How the Dragon manages her Province
                    • How the Dragon manages mortals in general
                    • How the Dragon interacts with others of her kind


                    Alternatively, you can work on one of these:
                    • Write some laws for an Edict, or write up a new Edict. It is important when writing laws for Edicts that they fit together to make a more potent effect. That is: a Law should work as a two dot power independently, or as a four dot power when combined with another law from the same Edict. It helps to write out possible combinations
                    • Draw some illustrations. Illustrating the book is the most time-consuming component of making a fan project.
                    • Create some characters with rigorous origins to go into the Storytelling chapter.
                    • Develop GMC notes for the existing mechanics. Breath already has corrections to be GMC compatible, but other systems (especially Experience) are in need of corrections.


                    I'm sorry I can't work faster on this project, but I have more pressing matters to attend to.

                            I am working on the project, but slowly and between other endeavors. Regrettably I have not given it as much love as I would like, though I've recently had a surge of inspiration.

                            The biggest way to help the project currently is to provide me with information about GMC experience, since I don't use GMC myself and so I haven't purchased Blood and Smoke. I need to know the experience rates for Blood Potency and Disciplines in Blood and Smoke so that I can price Dragon's GMC experience table accordingly.

                            On another note, I have decided to give the Oroboroi a small stipend of masquerade protection. Most splats do this with memory loss (like Lunacy and Disbelief), but Embers does something a little different. A mortal who encounters a dragon will remember it with crystal clarity, but any time he tries to tell his tale no one believes him. If he attempts to reproduce the dragon with a description or illustration, the result is an ordinary reptile (usually an alligator). This protection, called the Miasma (I like the word, and since the Dreamtide is gone... well, no sense letting it go to waste) does not apply to technology. Specifically: a photograph or recording will capture the true image of an Oroboros; it is not fool-proof.


                            Glad you're on board, WarDragon. Let us know if you have any thoughts on the version as it is, and what you'd like to see changed.

                                One Experience is made out of five Beats.

                                Each time your character fulfills one of the following criteria, take a Beat. Certain criteria have limits: for example, you may only gain one Beat for a Dramatic Failure in a scene.
                                • If your character fulfills an Aspiration, take a Beat. At the end of the game session, replace the Aspiration. Choosing a new Aspiration is an excellent activity between games or to handle before the next game session. (NdJesuncolo: Aspirations are simple statements of what a character/player wants his character to happen, like "make Gabriel drink his Vitae" "Find information on the hunters" "Encounter a ghost" "Break the Masquerade". You get three Aspirations, and when you resolve one, you take a Beat and replace the Aspiration.
                                • Some rolls and powers may impart Conditions on your character. Each Condition has criteria for resolution, usually requiring a difficult choice for the character. If resolved, take a Beat. You may only take one Beat for a Condition in a given scene.
                                • Once per scene, if you fail a roll, you may opt to take a Dramatic Failure instead. If you do so, take a Beat. Certain Conditions can have you take an automatic failure as part of their resolutions; you may make these failures Dramatic and take two Beats.
                                • If your character takes damage in one of her last (rightmost) Health boxes, take a Beat.
                                • The Storyteller can choose to award a Beat for any exceptional example of roleplaying, tactics or character development. If this involves more than one character,
                                  all of them should receive the Beat.
                                • At the end of any game session, take a Beat.

                                Plus, you generally take a Beat when social influence either mundane (by simple persuasion) or supernatural (Majesty, Domination), makes your character do something he doesn't desire.

                                Experience Costs
                                Merit: 1 Experience per dot
                                Skill Specialty: 1 Experience
                                Skill: 2 Experiences per dot
                                Attribute: 4 Experiences per dot
                                Integrity: 3 Experiences per dot (It's the new Morality)
                                Willpower: 1 Experience per dot
                                Power stat:5 Experiences per dot
                                Rituals: 1 Experience per dot (though I personally use 1 Experience flat)

                                        Additionally, B&S Humanity only costs 2 Experiences/dot and vampire Disciplines cost 3 or 4 Experiences, depending on if they are Clan Disciplines or not.
                                        Admittedly this makes them very cheap, since they do more work than an Attribute dot. I see no problem with applying this cost directly to Dragon Ablutions, since they are generally weaker similar to pre GMC Vampire.

                                        If there is something to take away from B&S it is that a lot more powers have been made free-to-use with bonuses if you choose to spend Vitae on them.
                                        For example, Vigor, Resilience and Celerity are now added to Strenght, Stamina and Defense as permanent bonuses (instead of needing to be activated with Vitae for a turn/scene) - but you can spend Vitae to boost them for one action for extra benefit (Resilience gives Armor for its rating for a turn, Celerity boosts Initiative or movement speed for one action etc.)

                                        So for example some Ablutions would probably look more like this under GMC philosophy:

                                        Inferno: The dragon can belch up fire that deals Lethal damage. (no cost) If 1 Breath is spent, the damage becomes Aggravated.

                                        Wings: The dragon can soar or fly at no cost. If 1 Breath is spent, he gets some nifty effects (aerial tricks,mitigating wind penalties, mitigating aerial battle penalties (ie. can fly and fight in the air at the same time instead of flight consuming his normal action) etc.)
                                        (Or, if flying seems too powerful:
                                        Soaring at 1 dot. Full flight at 2 dots. Hovering at 3 dots...There are )many possibilities)

                                        Part of me likes that you have to spend less time managing resource pools now and only spend them when it matters.
                                        I really love the Ablutions and wouldn't dream of throwing them off balance, but this particular GMC conversion probably wouldn't mess them up too bad.

                                                    Welcome aboard, FallenEco and Pandalfino! (Sorry for the late welcome FallenEco, I spaced)

                                                    I'm ecstatic to hear that somebody's working on the new philosophies, because frankly I'm not very good at writing Y Splats! (Let's be honest, we all read my interpretation of the original Volcano model philosophies and they were awful).

                                                    I did start a little bit on the new ones, but I sort of absorbed Idols into the Paragon philosophy. Here's what I have so far, maybe it'll give you some inspiration (It's the blurbs from the first chapter).
                                                    I'm not particularly attached to any of this, so feel free to change whatever you find doesn't fit:


                                                    Imperator
                                                    An Imperator is a general and everyone she interacts with are soldiers marching to battle. She is stern with her allies, who she commands with unquestionable authority. She is ruthless with her enemies, destroying them utterly body and soul. The whole world is against an Imperator, but she will not lay down her sword at the opposition. No, she is mobilizing her forces to strike a decisive blow. Failure is not an option.

                                                    Paragon
                                                    A Paragon needs to be needed. A Paragon craves attention. A Paragon must be a beacon of respect in her community. She seeks to control the world around her with careful politics and benevolence… or at least the kind of benevolence that looks good to an audience. All the world is a stage, and she leads by example. Supporting cast receive her love, but those that upstage her disappear.

                                                    Savant
                                                    There is no better teacher on Earth than an Oroboroi Savant. Recognizing the fleeting nature of their existence and that of life itself, a Savant seeks to educate the mortal masses so that they might carry on once the Oroboroi element has vanished from the world. Ignorance is poison and must be stamped out, by force if necessary.

                                                    Vizier
                                                    There is power in puppetry. There is protection in sitting beside the throne, rather than upon it. The Viziers change the world by feeding manipulation through a paparazzi face. A Vizier has her fingers in everything; like the proverbial oil tycoon there is no business that is safe from the coils of her reach. While on the surface she may seem plain, she is the most powerful political force in a room.

                                                    Caitiff
                                                    An Oroboros that rejects her membership is a Caitiff, a coward and a criminal who wanders the world without attachment. Such is not an easy life; other Oroboroi are inherently untrusting of a dragon without borders, and rightly so. Without a province, unmarried, or hoard to sustain her a Caitiff is forced to turn to… unnatural appetites.


                                                    I already like your Imperator definition more than what I came up with!
