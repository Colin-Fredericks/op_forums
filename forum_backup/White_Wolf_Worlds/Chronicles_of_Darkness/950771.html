    Seers As Written

    08-14-2016, 06:30 PM
    So I just read back through a bunch of threads about tweaking the Seers or making alternate Seers and so forth, and felt the need to explore this in a separate thread instead of hijacking one.

    What have people done that they really enjoyed with the normal version of the Seers? Anything that's been fun to emphasize or play down?

    I'm thinking about ways to play up these shiny cryptofascists as a main set of antagonists in a game and fishing for ideas.

      As someone who's always struggled with faith, the idea of achieving Prelacy and having communication with the closest thing the Fallen World has to gods is a potent motivation.

      Beyond that, there's the sheer scale of the threat the Exarchs pose; they won so hard the timeline was forever changed, and trying to struggle against incarnate symbols of oppression is so unthinkably beyond the scope of possibility that it seems hopeless. Why not join the winning team and benefit from it? Why try to change the world if no one's managed a single measurable victory against them ever?

              So I've recently been talking about this elsewhere, and something I think is really key to emphasize about the Seers of the Throne is their view on magic - "Magic is Payment." Now contrast that with the Silver Ladder, who are said to be a large part of the driving force behind the Diamond Orders (or at the very least, like to see themselves that way). "Magic is Humanity's Birthright." It's been said before that Mages are a metaphor for the 1%. Well, if that's the case, then the diamond are the trust fund kids, and the Seers are the business tycoons. From the Seers perspective, they work for their magic. They earned everything they have by getting a job at the Pyramid and working their asses off to impress the Exarchs. What did those Diamond Mages do to deserve their power? Nothing. they were just born into it. And don't even get me started on those socialist Libertines.

                                      I always liked the idea that many Seers come from a really mundane place of just craving and greed and ignorance. Play up the wealth and temporal power aspect. Imagine a newly Awakened person who gets a private meeting, a la Mr. Anderson with Agent Smith: "Hey, congrats on Awakening. We're the guys in charge. We'll take care of all of you and your family's bills and loans and medical debt and teach you how to use magic. You start Monday."

                                      Why would they present it as a real choice between Pentacle and Seers? Why tell the truth up front? Joining the Seers should feel like "oh boy, I can get a job I love where I get to use the new magic powers I've been given!" A potential Seer recruit should be thinking about joining the Pentacle in the way that a new hire at a law firm thinks about quitting their job, selling all their possessions, shaving their head and joining a monastery on the other side of the world. I guess you COULD, but you wouldn't seriously consider it.

                                      Joining the Seers is the reasonable choice. It's what your parents would have wanted.

                                      E: along with the Matrix thing, the Stephen King short story "Everything's Eventual" would be a really good model for this aspect of the Seers.

                                              Here's one of the ways I picture the seers, though of course it's a big group with a lot of different ways of doing things.

                                              Picture an X-files type scenario where aliens (the Exarchs) exist and are interacting with the world in secret. The seers are the ones on the inside of the conspiracy who communicate with the aliens and make secret deals on behalf of themselves and mankind. Yes, they have to keep a lot of secrets from the population at large although those are secrets that they're probably not ready for. They might occasionally have to do some morally questionable activities. However they keep the aliens happy enough to let their human agents handle things for them and that lets the conspirators run things here on Earth. Some of them like the way that puts them in charge of the world, some of them like the fringe benefits and some of them like the way they can help people by running a kinder system than the aliens would if they were directly running things. Because of their work there's no alien invasion.

                                                  So I'm in the middle of running a Mage game and one of the two Mages joined the Seers. Both were brand-new Awakenings. They lived and worked in the part of the city controlled by the Seers, at one point trip their detection magicks, and a Seer Headhunter shows up at one of their doors and invited them both to dinner. Fancy place. Amazing food.

                                                  Gives them a pretty standard Seer pitch. "Hey the Exarchs blah blah basically any mortal needs - shelter, money, can be taken care of not because we are hyper-generous but because covering rent isn't exactly hard when you've got a guy on your team who can just make precious metals. You'll be asked to do things you don't want to do, just like every other job in the world." So on and so forth.

                                                  One of the Mages was a professors of physics or some such at NYU and awoke as an Obrimos, was kinda reeling from the whole "what just happened to physics oh god oh god" thing. Asked what his job would be, basically got told that if he wanted to just research magic and do some light Indiana Jones work (and all other assigned duties) that would be fine. He just wouldn't get to go check out the most interesting stuff because, hey, seniority right?

                                                  Guy jumped at the chance. Some day I'll run a Seer game and follow him down that rabbit hole and push him up against the dark side of that choice, but...let's be real. The picture in the book isn't the sales pitch unless their target has some shockingly specific tastes. That's for later.


                                                  Because it's not just the whole "everyone think they would be the hero but in reality very few people can manage it" business. Its not just the bystander complex. Study after study shows that the more money you have (once you achieve "true" wealth) the most disconnected you become from others. And this isn't just money - it's MAGIC. it's EVERYTHING. you're so far above the 1% they look like those kids on TV you get asked to support for 10 cents a day. So yeah. You'll get jaded. You'll want more. And pretty soon you too Will be wearing an opera mask while doing lines off of the dying body of a crucified guy dressed up as Jesus.

                                                      Without the Exarchs, the world in which we live cannot exist. It is -their- design, crafted to keep Humanity safe and secure.
                                                      The Seers recognise this and work to protect the world that they've always known. Some do it for power, some for gratification, others still do it for the people they love and a few even believe that what they're doing is truly right.

                                                      The Pentacle claims that the Exarchs are the symbols of tyranny, but in truth they are symbols of control. Someone needs to be in control after all, if not the Exarchs then who? What fills the power vacuum? An inscrutable machine with little regard for Humanity, monstrous Old Gods or perhaps new Exarchs to replace the old?
                                                      No matter what follows, the world as we know it is over.

                                                      The Seers -like- this world, flawed as it is. They don't want to lose their families or the lives they have made for themselves. They see the value in the status quo, which so many others seem blind to.
                                                      Sure the Exarchs sometimes ask horrible things of them, but every atrocity serves to keep their world -alive-.
                                                      ----------
                                                      These are my thoughts on what might motivate someone to become a Seer, or perhaps what they might use to justify their actions. Obviously not every Seer thinks like this and I'd say many Lesser Ministries aren't even content with the status quo of the Archgenitors.
