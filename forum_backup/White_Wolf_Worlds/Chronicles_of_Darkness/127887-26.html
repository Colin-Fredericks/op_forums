                    Some of these Ordo Dracul terms have been translated if you check the dictionary. I'll put here the ones that haven't.

                    Mirskadur-Umfisah: Order of the Dragon (same as their other name, Ordo Dracul)
                    Sahafalul: The Defiant (lit. "the one who disagrees/shakes their head"); plural is Shasahafalul]
                    An'gusdur-Hursakh: Kogaionon (lit. "the mountain of the heavenly hide-born [Zalmoxis]")
                    Mirskaglil: Mysteries of the Dragon
                    ebhik: A coil/length (of rope, muscle, snakeskin, etc.)
                    Lilebhik: A Coil of the Ordo Dracul (lit. "piece of secret knowledge")
                    Lilsuhum: A Scale of the Ordo Dracul (lit. "a knowledge-bound piece of dragon-hide"/"a knowledge-bound scale")
                    Mirskadur: Vlad Tepes (lit. "Child of the Dragon")
                    Mirska Edi: Dragon's Nest/Wyrm's Nest
                    Namus Edi: Perilous Nest (lit. "Nest of Death")
                    Lal Garum: Chapterhouse (lit. "lesser house")
                    nedhudu: Crucible, melting pot (the other term is translated in the dictionary as athi kuth, a trial or test)
                    falasuhum: Chrysalis (lit. "shell of change")
                    manlu'u: Transhuman, transhumanity, transhumanism (lit. "humans exalted to heavenly heights")
                    lu'unnakal: post-human (lit. "human-before")
                    nimmuth: Trans-vampire, trans-vampirism (lit. "rises beyond the blood")
                    muthrih-nu'ase: post-vampire (lit. "blood-eater no more"/"no longer craves hungrily for blood")
                    ka'fala: evolution (lit. "the Great Change")
                    gu'usufala: forced/guided evolution (lit. "weighted change"/"pressured change")

                    More ocean-related words will come as soon as I think of them.

                    Edit: There are some word-doubles in the dictionary that need clarifying. The writers have said that homonyms are rare in FT, so here's the new changes:

                    - Fath (hard, packed earth, etc.) becomes fadh (and by extension, fath-gus becomes fadh-gus).
                    - Nuza (unmoving, stagnant; unenergetic, lethargic; slowness, torpor) becomes nuzah
                    - Ri (                    dedication, loyalty, assistance) is now ri'i
                    - Si (                    to fill, to load, to load up) is now si'i
                    - Za (                    to close, to shut out, to block off) is now zah
