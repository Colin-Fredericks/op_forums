    Yeah, pretty much what Heavy Arms said - it's waaay too easy to overcomplicate things, and Chronicles... isn't the simplest system in the industry what with the subsystems for combat and research and chases and the like, but it's certainly less crunchy than many and I think that's part of the appeal. Plus, there's something fun about the idea of an ooold vampire who's hellbent on keeping in touch with How The Mortals Do Things even after all this time that capping Humanity via Blood Potency would kinda ruin.

    On that note, I'd actually prefer the sunlight damage thing get streamlined a little too, the current "x damage at y intervals where one variable is determined by Humanity and the other by Blood Potency" thing is just slightly weird and is one of the few base mechanics I still haven't managed to half-memorize. Buuut it's hard to do that without making it too punishing or too lenient so I get why the 2e team went with what they did, and I certainly prefer it to "dead in seconds no matter what" like 1e more-or-less was by default.

        Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
        I think we're starting to demonstrate why "bit more convoluted," easily turns into a lot more convoluted and why these sorts of things get scrapped (at least from core books).

        Wanted a quick easy optional rule for vampires to access daylight activities in the core book is a compelling thing for a 3e of VtR.

        When we're getting to the point of trying to hard code in generalities, we're starting to get away from VtR 3e and just into a different vampire game. High Humanity elders are a thing in the game, even if extremely rare because few vampires want to dedicate themselves to what it takes to maintain Humanity that high for that long. Coupling Humanity and Blood Potency starts to have all sorts of knock on effects throughout the system. Ironing out all of that is getting a bit beyond "new edition" territory.

        Its not like the changes between 1ed and 2ed were little things either, a new edition could mean anything not just a few fixes here and there, still, the title of the post is about crazy ideas, things that most likely won't be added in an eventual 3ed (if there will be one in the first place) so suggesting a little bit of radical change is not going to derail the thread. Anyway my point stand, however they want to do it, shall a 3ed ever come out, I would like a way to get around the sun problem at least for the purpose of multisplat games.

            Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
            Thing is, vampires are natural competitors. Everything they do, they weigh in how much of an advantage it is against their competitors, even if circumstantial and especially if this is an exploitable circumstance.

            If the difference between High and Low Humanity during the day is too big, they'll evaluate if any benefit from loosing Humanity is actually worth it in this competitive environment.

            Of course, you can always send your Ghouls against you opponents or to do your bidding during the day, but as they say, if wan something well done, do it yourself. If you're caught unguarded or have to flee, this is also something to consider.

            Already Humanity is a concern. Having it high is beneficial and most Kindred will at least sometimes weigh the benefits of keeping it or not. The more weight you give it, the more they'll lean towards maintaining it.

            Not to say that I really don't like the idea of making it that hard a rule about how much Humanity you can keep at each BP level. This seems just limiting on character concepts, and nothing anyone else asked for just to make high Humanity vampires more playable at day time (and keep them at low BP).

            Like I wrote above, the crux of the matter was to get a way to act during daytime in an eventual 3ed, however they want to do it, I don't care, the other things I wrote was just my immagination running wild.

                Originally posted by Sconce View Post
                the current "x damage at y intervals where one variable is determined by Humanity and the other by Blood Potency" thing
                As weird as it is, it is already simple to look for in a table and note down for a char, as it won't change that frequently. More importantly, it gives you two dials to work with.

                The reason I mentioned L5R earlier is because Health in there have exactly this proposal for a sidebox. In it your Health is defined as 2×Earth at each penalty class by default, but a sidebox explains what is expected to happen at a game with this amount and then how to adjust it to your preference, discussing how the game behaves when you change it to ×3, ×4 or ×5. It is a design dial, and the sidebox just straight out teaches the ST how to change the dial to reap an effect.

                So that's basically all we need. What's the effect of messing around with the dials? What are the designer notes about different settings? They do have this information because they have it from the very design process, just share it with the STs.

                One addition I think is worth to make is to offer the two extreme settings: no damage and insta-death. Limits on Disciplines are an option when you have no damage. The most important thing, though, is to discuss as designers what are the effects on the game for using any particular setting on the dials. It is crunch-light, but the discussion is the important part.
