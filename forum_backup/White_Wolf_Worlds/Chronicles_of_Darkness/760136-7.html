                                    Here's my first go at a Colony ability.

                                    Projection: The Monarch slaves the minds of his colony to his, halting whatever activity they are currently engaging in but allowing him to use telepathy as if he had 1 higher Authority. If the Monarch already has an Authority of 10, he can now project his mind beyond the conventional universe (Perhaps into the Void?). This puts a great deal of strain on the lesser minds however, and renders the colony unable to take any further action for a number of hours equal to the Monarch's Authority.

                                    I'm not sure what this would do with Authority 10 Monarchs. talking of which, don't you think the jump from planet-wide telepathy to infinite is a little big? I would say let the Authority 10 Monarchs be able to use it anywhere within a light-year.                                                     OK, here's my second attempt.

                                                    Supply: The Monarch commands his industrial centres to synthesize materials or commodities, either to trade with another colony, for an individual construction project or simply to supply Swarms or Servants working in the field. This can only create raw materials or form them into simple shapes. For example, this could be used to produce blocks of stone but not a statue.
                                                    Although the process used to enact this ability are a combination of biological factories and psi-chemistry that can produce most anything from most anything else the Storyteller may limit exceedingly rare materials such as precious metals or gems. Bear in mind that things produced through Supply will not necessarily be a perfect copy of their earthly counterparts. Wood will behave similarly but might not have a grain or rings.
                                                    The amount of points that must be spent to create a given resource is dependent upon the rarity, complexity and desired bulk of the final product.
