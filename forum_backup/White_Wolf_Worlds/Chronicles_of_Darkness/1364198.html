    Hypothetical scenario, your thoughts appreciated

    01-30-2020, 02:29 PM
    The year is 2035

    Over the course of the previous story, through circumstances beyond their control, my group was involved in a fight that ended up with some high level Infrastructure being destroyed in a tornado by a Demon that Went Loud. The resulting storm carried the Aether across the entire city, and was absorbed by a lot of inhabitants from that city, as well as the river leading to other areas of the Mid-west of America. As a result, Stigmatics are much more well-known, as well as some activated Latents.
    Unchained mostly still reside in the shadows, not wanting to risk death despite the new wave of Stigmatics and Cryptids.
    The event has become known as The Awakening, as so many peoples eyes were opened to the existence of the God-Machine.
    What cultural aspects do you think might come about in a world such as this?

      I think the key is to remember that the whispers of the Machine permeate the collective unconscious. There is no turning away from it, no way to be indifferent to it.

      However, that does not mean everyone is aware of it, its like the zeitgeist of the area. So in a very basic sense, we have 4 key approaches based on whether they are aware or favorable to it.

      The Runners (unaware and against): They feel the changing tide of technology, the rise of new corporations, new conspiracies, the rapturous glee of those hearings the turning of the cogs. Using any technology bought, stolen or created, they will unravel this conspiracy. This is a great place to throw some hunter groups like Null Mysteres.
      Quote: "Every run could be the big one; the one with the reaper at the other end. One day, that bastard might catch me, but he'll be breathing hard when he does."


      The Phantom (unaware and in favor): An anonymous organization that masquerades as a single "hero", taking inspiration from The Phantom comics. They believe the fire of technological innovation will fizzle out if they don't feed it. They seek inspiration in the supernatural to create new materials, like super substances via dimensional exploration (such as making perfected copper via portals to the Shadow). They focus on liberating tech for everyone in grand acts of bravado. Sometimes they run into Infrastructure, but haven't had a reason to guess at the existance of the machine yet. They are beloved by technology spirits.
      Quote: "Better to stare into the tiger's eyes, than into the cold eyes of the Phantom. For when the Phantom strikes, lightning stands still."

      The Devout (aware and in favor): The increased awareness to the Machine has spawned more cults devoted to it than can be counted. A few, like the Order of the Gear, seek to replace weak flesh with glorious machines, seeking to become post human (they are inspired by the Adeptus Mechanicus). The Order of Hammerites see the Machine as the greatest work of the Master Builder, a god that seeks to tame the chaos of supernatural nature with technology. Meanwhile The Mechanists seek to purge the world of the "imperfection of organic life, creating the Builder's Paradise" (these two are inspired by the same groups from the Thief series). The Singularity Church of the Machine God from Deus Ex:Mankind Divided is another good one, it portrays aware yet clueless cultists. I would probably also throw some technology based mages into these groups, like the Austere and Transhuman Engineers.
      Quote: "From the weakness of the mind, Omnissiah save us
      From the lies of the Antipath, circuit preserve us
      From the rage of the Beast, iron protect us
      From the temptations of the Flesh, silica cleanse us
      From the ravages of the Destroyer, anima shield us
      From this rotting cage of biomatter, Machine God set us free."

      The Cogitators (aware and against): With awareness of the occult matrix comes the possibility of exploiting resources undreamed of, uncovering rules of reality that no one suspected, repurposing technology to grant mastery of the world. These are the ones that seize, decompile and modify all God Machine tech to suit their purposes. Task Force Valkyrie and the Chiron Group would certainly be found here. I made a similar organization some time back, called Graiae Wetware, maybe it can be useful here: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...ch-cults/page2
      Quote: " You've heard shards of our voice in the phantom-radio code of a numbers station in the roar of a crowd - in the screams of your clock - in the scrape of a chalkboard - in the snow static of a TV - in the chainsaw-decibel mating of cicadas - in the urban mythos that spreads amongst children like contagion - in the silence between lies.

      White noise becomes a cadence. Words develop self-awareness. Viral. Evolving. Living poetry. Sentient language. We must seize it and shape the message"

          This is obviously a mass hysteria, made more complicated by the release of a psycho-active chemical in what was an industrial accident or a terrorist attack. There has been a suspicion that the human nervous system might be "hacked" by what amounts to a thought virus that is assisted by some sort of biological or chemical agent. All those who see some over-arching "Godly Machine" are obviously mentally ill and must be confined, perhaps quarantined and prevented from spreading the problem across the country. A national state of emergency has been declared.

          And....

          This is obviously the signal of the End of Times. The Great Beast has revealed himself to the world. We must form a Holy Army to oppose the actions of Its followers. The twisted monsters formed in its wake must be put to flame, as well as those who worship it.

          And....

          This is the Singularity - the formation of a transhuman AI. We all predicted that this would happen. The fact that we cannot understand what is going on is just our inability to comprehend an intelligence beyond our own. If we wish to participate in the next great step in evolution, we must find a way to integrate our own intelligence with the new AI.

Their philosophy is that banded together, they can avoid the prejudice of those that call them satanic. But why not go one step further? Why not eliminate prejudice altogether?                           The Completionists sound like very dangerous antagonists and not necessarily by their motivation. Some examples of the complications they could find:

                          * The God Machine is indifferent to humanity, but it likes to preserve the status quo. To generate Aether, they could work with it to setup Infrastructure, but they would be treated as expendable gears. Alternatively, they could continuously activate/steal Infrastructure to get Aether, at which point the Machine will employ a layered proportional response. Since their aims are nation wide, they would be lucky if said response merely escalated to a cataclysmic level.

                          * As mechanics like Lunacy and Quiescence from other lines point out, humanity is generally not psychologically equipped to handle the supernatural as well as the main splats. But they are militarily equipped, which is so dangerous that it made vampires create the Masquerade. So if the whole country sees, then it means everyone will trigger a flight or fight response. Sometimes it will lead to ghost towns and highly fortified bunker complexes as humans are collectively terrified. But it also means the creation of crusades against the supernatural, with the government, hunter conspiracies and supernatural puppet masters backing it.

                          * The domino effect would affect multiple supernaturals. For example, the rise of Stigmatics leads to more feelings about the Machine, leading to mechanical Resonance, causing an increase of the number and might of technology spirits. At best, it develops into a civil war in the Shadow, at worst it causes a mass exodus and a rise in Claimed. Then there are the Exarchs wanting to keep humanity blind to the supernatural, mobilizing scores of Seers to contain the mess and perhaps even ochematas. All this conflict might allow the True Fae to establish semi-sustainable bases, leading to a rise in steampunk urban legends. Imagine what happens when Stigmatic ghosts appear or when they are banished to the Underworld, techno zombies and similar horrors would abound.

                          To use a metaphor, the Completionists are akin to a person tap dancing in a minefield, next to a nuke. The only reason they are still alive is because not only did they not step on one, but they are somehow dancing around the fire from both sides. All probably agree (likely including the party) that the only way to prevent mutual annihilation is to take them out before everything goes boom.
