                        Most fun would be Spirit. While I understand where Michael Kenner is coming from, the Spirit Arcanum isn't about silly things like harmony and treating spirits tit for tat. It's about an entire world that's yours to command and build from scratch. And sure, with Death, you could make ghosts and play with souls, but there are these weird ethical things going on there. Mind, Space, Life? Same. No such thing in spirit world. You carve out a piece of unreality, build your servants ex nihilo, and do whatever the hell you want. All you need is enough power to bend it to your will.

                            Space all the way! I love the idea of being able to teleport, create portals and alter vectors. The ability to create a room out of a hole in the wall is pretty darn sweet. And then there's that whole thing you can do to recreate the magic that hid Grimmauld place in the Harry Potter story. Want to know what you're friends are up to? Scry 'em! Want to go on vacation, but can't afford the hotel bill? Teleport where you want to go, then come back in the evening to sleep. Forgot to grab your keys? No worries, you can summon them! Or better yet, you can just blink right through walls! The place you're at is too hot or too cold, but you can't leave because you need to do something there? Open a portal to someplace of a drastically different temperature, then place a fan in the middle of the portal! Trying to find something? Space is there to help you!

                                I'm a big fan of Prime. Its feel is like that of Magic at its most fundamental level.                                                     Matter.

                                                    I love the whole idea of exactly knowing how something is made and what materials it is made from, then reinforcing it do be even better at its intended purpose.
                                                    Oh and just straight up creating the things I need from nowhere or removing stuff merely by willing it to not be.
                                                    I will admit I am a big fan of the character Emiya Shirou from the Fate series and Matter lets me copy what he does to some extent.
                                                    Also Golems.

                                                    Last of all I like how Matter challenges me in achieving my goals instead of just giving me stuff, like I feel the other Arcana would do. Matter is good at giving you the right tools to achieve your goal, or improves the tools you already have, without just handing you the one solution shortcut. Then again I completely understand that particular power fantasy, but it is not what I am looking for in Mage at the moment.
