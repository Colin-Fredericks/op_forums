    It’s time to check in on the Krewe and their wily ways. Some Werewolf stuff here, that I may or may not have totally screwed up; I wrote parts of that almost three years ago.

    Session LXXVI

    Doc’s Log

    Doc’s Log: 1130 Hours, Monday, August 16, 2010

    Surprisingly, meditation went pretty well this morning. Given the madness of last night, I think centering in an hour is more than reasonable. Nightshade was up when I can down for breakfast, which was unusual but definitely a welcome change. Hearing the anguish in Rose’s voice when she screamed out Karl’s name really reminded me of what we still have to lose.

    I stopped in to check on Karl before I left. He was still as stable as I left him a few hours before. I expected as much, but just because my patient is a Brineborn doesn’t mean I can be lax in my physician’s duties. He was tired and stiff, but given that he should have been dead I think we were both satisfied with his condition.

    Work started out like a normal Monday. Quick dusting of the shelves from the weekend, people rushing in to get their loose tea for the week on the way to work, kids skipping school and hoping to find a head shop, and then more normal customers. Then I got a surprise. Zed, from Malard’s pack, stopped by. When I saw him, I knew he must want to talk about why we had some kind of throwdown with a spirit last night. I apologized immediately and assured him we weren’t responsible for that. He blinked for a minute then shook his head. That wasn’t why he was here and I felt pretty stupid. There was an issue with Ol’ Man Malard, one that could be a really problem. Deirdre's story about old werewolves going crazy immediately sprang to mind. Apparently Malard and Angelique are together now, which is not okay by Uratha law. There had been some speculation about that before but apparently it’s not a secret any more. Actually Zed said Malard was flaunting this situation, deciding that that particular edict didn’t apply to him. I asked why this was an issue by Uratha law, not really knowing more than it was frowned on. The Cahalith explained children that resulted from such a coupling could be completely normal, not even a werewolf. Sometimes they did become Uratha, but there were obviously no hard and fast rules for that. Unfortunately they could also turn out as spirit beings. This was because as Zed put it, Uratha are already half-spirit, half-flesh. But the worse case, and what all Uratha wanted to avoid is the possibility of the child being some kind of insane monster (note this is a werewolf describing something as an insane monster). Another part of this problem is that Angelique likes to be the bad girl. She is they pack mystic and calls herself a witch. The pack has worried about her darker style but until now they’ve never had any evidence she was anything more than a goth. I knew Nightshade would have issues with that part of the story. Since we were both sharing and being concerned about Malard, I asked if the information we had passed to the elder Uratha managed to filter out to the others. They all knew about Karl but Lily, Leslie, and Glenwood were all news to Zed. We agreed that this was not something the Krewe needed to get directly involved in, but he wanted us to know since we were in the immediate area. Zed said he felt a responsibility to keep us informed. I know that feeling. We traded cell numbers so we could keep in touch. He left feeling like he had given us fair warning and I felt like we had someone we could trust if things got bad.

    Happy Monday, Doc.


    Doc’s Log: 1405 Hours, Monday, August 16, 2010

    My girlfriend is so hot. Nightshade came by for lunch like normal, but she was wearing what she called her Little Black Riding Hood outfit. She had no idea about Zed coming by earlier, but the whole Big Bad Wolf idea took on a whole new meaning when I saw her. I didn’t really get into it very much over lunch since I knew it would come up during dinner. We just had a relaxing lunch. I think I’m going to have to piss Karen off and claim Nightshade for myself for a few nights very soon.


    Doc’s Log: 0345 Hours, Tuesday, August 17, 2010

    When I got back to the Bluffs after work, Gretchen was home but she was so very drunk. Snowflake was not as far gone as she was thankfully, but he was drinking with her. The Scottish Fishborn ranted about getting everyone mauled or killed or worse and how selfish she was. We talked her down a little before I got some coffee for Snowflake. She was rightfully worried about her presence putting other people in danger. We did point out we were probably better suited to deal with something like this than almost anyone else she could find. Mongler was another story. While we knew he could become a dinosaur, that wouldn’t help him anymore than being a shifter helped Karl if another of these things attacked. Eventually I got more coffee for Gretchen and convinced her she should try to sober up for dinner so we could discuss it as a group there. I’m not sure when I because the Krewe Dad, but whatever. She seemed willing to go along with it so I went to get cleaned up.

    Over dinner we had alot going on. I went over the Werewolf Update and as expected Nightshade felt like Angelique was getting a raw deal because of preconceived notions and not real evidence. Rose thought the younger Uratha was using Malard, though I’m not so sure about that one. I may just being paranoid, but after hearing a separate pack voicing concern for just this sort of thing, I’m more willing to consider the threat. Snowflake hadn’t spent his day drinking but instead researched the creature that Cupcake put down last night. It was called a Supernal Hunter and camed from an Astral Realm. I’m not sure how many realms exist in the pure Light, but we know of several in the Darkness so it’s not entirely unthinkable. This particular creature was a dimensional traveller and actually had trouble remaining in the physical state. Gretchen talked to the whole Krewe about leaving and everyone else said the same thing I had. She’s willing to try and stay if she can convince Mongler to come here while she’s with him. Rose has apparently been talking to Lily about going out with us during some of our less dangerous trips. I’m really not happy about this. I mean I don’t think Lily is intentionally looking to screw us over, but if she gets out of that doll we don’t know what she might do. Rose thinks this will strengthen her sense of responsibility and loyalty to us, which are good things. I just hope she knows what she’s doing. We ended dinner on a much lighter note with Cupcake and Chandra explaining their wedding plans. Nightshade will be officiating, Snowflake will be Chandra’s Best Man, Rose and Bright Eyes will be the Bride’s Maids, I will give Cupcake away, and Lily may even get involved. I’m going to have to let Ronin know about this and hope she can get back for it. Don’t know how that will work out.

    After dinner, Snowflake and I went to research Warding. It was a strong 20 minutes of study. Now I think we have some pretty good guidelines. We’ll know more when we have a chance to learn it and try it out.

    We had a pretty quiet night after that, but I was still keyed up from worrying about Malard so I went out on patrol. It felt good but I could tell it had been awhile. I didn’t stop anything but there were more people on the streets in West Haven tonight that normally wouldn’t be out when I came by. I’ll have to keep them guessing. But now, it’s time for some sleep.


    Jasmine’s Diary

    Monday, August 16, 2010, Noon

    Given Karl’s situation, I had to cancel my training session with Jean this morning. I had trouble with my meditation, so I went outside to the cliffs and performed some deeply meditative tai chi exercises. It was quite settling. I do routinely train in the martial form of tai chi, but it has been awhile since I’ve taken the time to perform the slower, more tranquil exercises. Perhaps, while the warm weather lasts, I should get up earlier and move my morning meditations outside. I should also ask if Doc would like to join me. Perhaps tai chi would be good for him.

    Monday, August 16, 2010, Late Afternoon

    Karl is doing much better thanks to Doc’s healing touch. He is still injured, and it will take him awhile before he’s fully recovered, but Doc shifted a great deal of his plasm into the ceremony he used to assist his medical treatment, so the worst of it is over. I should find a way to thank him.

    I called Trinh early this afternoon to see if she could put me in touch with Maria, as we believe Maria might know something about mages that could help us in dealing with the situation in Glenwood. It was nice talking to Trinh again. Unfortunately, she couldn’t put me in touch with Maria directly, but told me that she’d have Stephen (the young man known as Pilot Light) call me since he knows Maria much better. Trinh doesn’t really care for Maria, in part because of the way she bosses Stephen around. Maria also has R.L. and Jimbo wrapped around her finger. There’s something odd about those two…
    Maria has also been feuding with Barry Teasdale. I’m not even sure I recall who he is, but if our suspicions about her are true, then it’s possible Barry has some supernatural ability as well. Of course it’s not inconceivable that their rivalry is purely mundane; I’ve come to realize that my lifestyle has made me a bit detached from mortal life, so I suppose I jump to extraordinary conclusions more often than I should.

    Gretchen also returned to the mansion this afternoon. She immediately spoke with Simon, and later with the rest of the krewe, announcing her intentions to leave. The attack last night convinced her that she must not endanger us or Grin any further. Naturally, we all argued with her and insisted she stay. Gretchen can be quite stubborn, and her concerns about Grin’s safety when faced with a beast like the supernal hunter are quite valid. But I don’t think any of us are willing to let her march off to her death. We were eventually able to convince her to stay. She’s far safer in the mansion than anywhere else. Grin’s safety is a worry, but Simon and Doc suggested that Grin might move in with us until we’ve resolved this issue. Gretchen hasn’t decided exactly what she’s going to do, but at least she agreed to stay another day and think it over so that we can discuss it again once she has a clearer head.

    Monday, August 16, 2010, Evening

    I’ve been spending the evening babying Karl, and he’s dozing a bit right now, so I thought I’d take the moment to write a diary entry.

    Over dinner, Doc brought a potentially troublesome situation to our attention. Zed, one of Mr. Malard’s werewolf pack, came to speak to Doc at his store earlier in the day. He’s worried because Malard and Angelique, the fetching young woman who is currently living with Malard, are in a relationship. Malard is apparently quite a catch for his age, given his strength as a werewolf, but intimate relationships between werewolves are forbidden. There are several reasons for that, but perhaps the most compelling is that the offspring of such a relationship can become quite dangerous.

    Zed and the rest of the pack are concerned about this. Angelique is a sort of werewolf mystic (I believe Doc used the term “witch,” to which Natalie responded with a bit of cautious indignation). I gather that it is especially taboo for her to be involved in this sort of relationship. Also, if Malard is flaunting this breach of werewolf society, what other rules will he decide to break? Zed is worried this could be the first sign of a dangerous instability in a werewolf of Malard’s age.

    Zed wasn’t expecting us to do anything about it, but since Malard is our neighbor and a touchy situation may be brewing, he thought we should know. I, for one, worry that Angelique is using Malard, manipulating him to her own ends. The impression I’ve gotten of her is that she is cunning, sly and perhaps a bit arrogant, and though I am of course jumping to conclusions about her character, I wonder if she isn’t just toying with his affections to get what she wants. If so, then by all means she should be stopped. Using love to such manipulative and selfish ends cannot be allowed. I hope I’m wrong about her.

    Dinner conversation soon turned to a more pleasant topic: Alexia and Chandra’s upcoming wedding, which is scheduled for early next month. They asked us all to participate, and I became quite excited about it as we discussed it. Weddings are, after all, ideally the most romantic occasion in anyone’s life.

    Natalie will be performing the ceremony thanks to her status as a priestess of sorts, and Chandra and Alexia aren’t looking for a denominational wedding in any event. Sara and I will be Alexia’s bride’s maids. Simon is Chandra’s best man, and Doc, as the “father” of the krewe and the one who brought Alexia into it, will be giving Alexia away. There was talk of Lily being a flower girl, which would be wonderful as long as no mortals are present. Alexia has planned a magnificent rainbow-colored cake to celebrate the same-sex marriage, and the wedding is to take place on the scenic cliffs behind Dark Haven Bluffs, overlooking the sea. I think it will be lovely!


    Monday, August 16, 2010, Late Night

    Stephen called, a bit late, but well before my bed-time. He was a bit surprised for me to ask for his help, but was willing to do what he could since Trinh asked him on my behalf. He promised to have Maria contact me, and I told him to mention the mansion to her in order to pique her interest. I hope it works, and I hope Maria isn’t as cagey or antagonistic as she was the first time I tried to probe her knowledge of the supernatural.


    Footnotes

    Supernal Hunter

    Simon researched the entity that attacked the mansion in search of Gretchen last night. It is called a supernal hunter, and is an otherworldly creature that excels at hunting prey. Simon says that it has some difficulty remaining material in our world, which works to its advantage, as it can shift into Twilight when it wishes.


    Mother’s Little Helper

    I spoke to Lily about the possibility of allowing her to accompany me on some of our outings if she behaves well. She was quite excited about the possibility of coming along and helping out. We were able to make a deal: I’ll ask her to come along sometimes, when it’s not too dangerous for her, but in return she mustn’t try to sneak off with us, and must be a good little girl and stay home when I ask her to. She was a bit disappointed that she can’t try to sneak off with us (I think she enjoys the “game”), but she agreed and we made a pinky promise to that effect.

    I explained the deal to the rest of the krewe. I think that most of the krewe was satisfied with the arrangement, though as usual, Doc had his concerns. He is quite legitimately worried that if Lily accompanies us, then she is more likely to have her doll-body damaged, which would free her and possibly make her quite dangerous. I argued that it would be better for us to have more control over when she comes along so that we will be better able to make sure she’s safe, and Doc grudgingly agreed.

    I believe that she’ll be good, and I hope that her time with us gives her a better sense of responsibility so that even if she is freed from her doll body, she won’t cause too much trouble. And excluding her from everything isn’t good for her. She desperately wants to contribute, and I think that allowing her to express herself in a constructive way is better than deepening her frustrations.


    Cupid’s Arrows

    Trinh and Stephen are childhood friends, and quite close. I’ve figured out from observing them that they have serious feelings for one another, but they’ve been friends for so long that they have certain assumptions about each other that are keeping them from exploring romance. Stephen assumes that Trinh isn’t interested and Trinh seems shy about confessing her feelings for Stephen, and thinks he’s only interested in well-endowed party girls.

    Perhaps it was presumptuous of me, but I was sad to see the potential for true love go unfulfilled, so the Drowned Bride and I decided to gently nudge things in the right direction. While I was talking to Trinh, I asked about her feelings for Stephen, trying to get her to admit them to herself. I encouraged her to stop ignoring them.

    When I spoke with Stephen, I tried to casually comment that I was surprised he wasn’t dating Trinh—after all, I’d seen the way she looks at him. He honestly didn’t think she was interested since’s she’s only ever dated Blacks or other Vietnamese, but now that her grandmother’s ghost has been resolved, I’m certain she’ll be more open to a relationship with Stephen. Of course, he has no way of know about that, so I hope my gentle encouragement gets things moving.

    Of course, I was probably much more heavy handed and blunt about the whole thing than I meant to be. I seem to lack Natalie’s skill at being subtle. But I’ve come to accept that, and if it helps make Trinh and Stephen happy, then I’ll have accomplished something good.

        Doc’s Log: 0940 Hours, Tuesday, August 17, 2010

        I found a note in the Meditation Room today instead of Rose. She said she was on the Bluffs and I was welcome to come join her. When I found her, she was practicing Tai Chi. It was a scene from Karate Kid with her standing on the rocks with the ocean crashing down below. She noticed me standing there and offered to help me. That was another way she could meditate. It was different with the wind, but the sun was warm and the sounds of the sea more than made up for the lack of music. We went through different katas for about an hour. It was different but I was able to slip into a peaceful state. I then slipped into the shower again so I wasn’t soaked. I was never very good at Tai Chi. I’ve learned today that was because I was doing it wrong, and it’s hard. Now I have to look in on Karl and run to work.


        Doc’s Log: 1415 Hours, Tuesday, August 17, 2010

        I was a little surprised when Bright Eyes asked if she could come to work with me this morning, but she said she wanted to see how things worked and maybe see if she could help out. I’m in no position to turn away willful help so we rode over and I showed her around. I explained the normal routine as I went through opening.

        The morning rush went smoothly and Bright Eyes took a few questions and grabbed a few things to keep things moving. It was pretty awesome actually. After things died down again, she dropped a nuke in my lap. When she asked me, I think my brain seized at first. I went numb. All I could feel was a wall in my mind getting slammed into. I didn’t understand what was happening. The wall was getting hammered more and more, while a pressure was building like panic, but I wasn’t afraid or in danger. I had to ask her what? several times to make sure I understood everything correctly.

        Would I be upset or uncomfortable, on any level, if she slept with Nightshade and Karen?

        As the full meaning of the words seeped into my mind, I realized the wall was the blinder I built to keep me from looking too closely at the arrangement we had for a relationship. The wall that had hidden what I knew was an issue. In that moment, that wall was destroyed by the raging realization that Nightshade didn’t have any problem with the idea of sleeping with someone other than Karen or I. She never stated or inferred that this would be strictly monogamous, I just assumed that since she had both of us, she would have everything she needed. This simple and honest question had just laid bare my naive little idea that we would be all that Nightshade wanted... and shined the cold light of truth down on it like a prison spotlight.

        Bright Eyes had no real idea what to expect, though I doubt she could have known what was going through my head. I opened my mouth several times but shut it quickly as I felt the anger flowing up my throat. I clamped down, knowing abstractly that she was trying to do the right thing. She did not deserve the flames of rage that ravaged my chest and howled for blood like a pack of rabid wolves. When I finally leashed the beasts, I told her that I’d have to get back to her as I couldn’t functionally answer that question. I needed to talk with Nightshade to figure out what was going on. I turned to look at her when I could actually speak and she saw my face for the first time. She immediately retracted the question and apologized. It wasn’t her responsibility to apologize, but that made me feel bad. I knew my anger shouldn’t be directed at her and I’m pretty sure she understood my emotional state without me having to explain.

        I stayed quiet for a while before Bright Eyes started talking to me about my tattooing equipment. I knew she was trying to change the subject but I didn’t care. It let me not dwell on how stupid I was or how hurt I felt. It was easier to talk to her after a few minutes and she was seriously interested in tattoos. We talked until Nightshade got here. She brought lunch like usual, but seemed surprised to see Bright Eyes when she came in. She apologized for not bringing food for three, but Bright Eyes politely excused herself and I guess headed back to the Bluffs. I hadn’t...no I couldn’t think about what to say when I saw Nightshade so I had no clue how to go about this. She knew something was up as soon as Bright Eyes left. I tried to stay very calm because I know it bothers Nightshade when I get angry or she thinks I get angry. We had meaningless conversation without really saying anything. I don’t remember ever being this awkward with her even when I was still in the wheelchair. I could tell she was trying to help but she doesn’t really understand what’s wrong. And if I’m being honest, that is exactly what the problem is. Nightshade is not a jealous person...as in doesn’t really grasp the concept. To her, jealousy seems like greed because you want someone all to yourself. Her anti-objectification goes that deep. She doesn’t get that there are some things a couple share, that are degraded when more than just the couple share it. Yes I’m now objectifying emotions, feelings. Love.
        It was a little weird to think about sharing Nightshade with Karen at first. Then it was a little easier because Karen and I never tried to compete with each other. That made it seem more ...stable? It never seemed normal, but I never believed that it would be. I guess that’s my way of coping. I just decided that Nightshade had us both and that was all she wanted. If she was only okay with us being with her, that would have made sense. But given she pushed me and encouraged Karen to get involved with other people, …. I don’t think I’m a genius, but I must be a complete idiot or utterly delusional for this to make any sense. I need my punching bag so I don’t slam into the counter and scare anymore customers. That old lady almost knocked the loose teas off the shelf when she jumped.

        Nightshade eventually left after we had eaten. I started drawing almost as a reflex and wasn’t even thinking about it. Then when I walked back to the counter after helping a customer I realized I was drawing something that looked disturbingly like the Rose Bride. I’ve tried to get away from that image and the frightening concepts it brings up.

        I just want to beat the bag until my arms are numb. I need to think through this but thinking is so hard without realizing how stupid I’ve been. That just makes me think about things all over again. FUCK! Dinner is going to be special tonight.


        Doc’s Log: 2230 Hours, Tuesday, August 17, 2010

        When I was still in the Sand, we put our pain, loss, sadness, and whatever other emotions were in the way aside so we could finish the mission. When I came home to the Bluffs, that’s what I planned to do again.

        The first thing I noticed was that Nightshade was singing and playing piano. She was upset. My first response was to check on her to see if she was alright. Then I realized, half a second later, she was upset because of us. Some part of me thought maybe she understood. But the rational part of me, that was slowly taking back ground in my mind, shouted that idea down. Nightshade was upset because she realized she was in a real relationship and that meant she felt trapped. It wasn’t an angry thought or malicious at all. Just a statement. Likely factual. That didn’t make me feel any better so I headed upstairs until time to eat.

        Rose brought our board meeting to order with new information on Marie Smith and her apparent rival Barry Teasdale. They are both something called Reborn. Apparently Reborn are ancient beings that live in various cycles. They are not mages, though they do have some form of magic. Rose thought it might be similar to the hedge magic Snowflake and I practice. Regardless, Barry informed her that they were rivals and went out of his way to assure her they did not want to get us involved. It was blatant that Barry was afraid of us and that he at least knew what we were. Referring to us as Ghost Walkers, he said we were, in his experience, never afraid to up the ante. Also that we had buttons that should never be pushed. I laughed and immediately thought of Ronin. None of us could argue his point however. Unfortunately that again left us without any assistance in dealing with Glenwood.

        We’re not really decided on Plan A though we did consider using the DeCastro Box. I need to come up with a better name for that thing. The Frozen Onyx Dungeon maybe. Anyway, that is one option though we’ll have to work up a ritual just for that. Plus I suggested that Snowflake might need to find a ritual to bind the Outsider. He almost lost it and made me tell him I wanted him to do this out loud so there was no mistake. This was payback for the Doomponies, I’m sure. But I did specify we were looking for temporary binding until the ritual to lock them in the Onyx Prison could pull it/him in. Plan B was a controlled burn of the lodge. We’re not sure how good or bad that might be, or even if it would have any effect. Tavia suggested using fire or a bucket of lye to crack the shell to get to the Abyssal candy center. The worse part is, this is all just for learning how to deal with it. We aren’t going to have a lot of time or chances. I told them my plan for using homemade Napalm. I was terrified when it was agreed that was a good idea. Cupcake said she could get the containers, Snowflake volunteered to get the gas (Nightshade had a moment about getting gas on her hands), and the others were going to get the packing peanuts.

        With that as decided as we could make it, Cupcake stated she wanted to play Life for Game Night. Rose was the first one up from the table. We played through and Snowflake won. Nightshade was feeling a little better and tried to play with me a little to make me feel better. Unfortunately it didn’t help. Actually it just ripped open what little bit of a scab I had managed to work up. I had to get away. I didn’t want anyone else to see me as upset as I knew I was going to be.

        Now I’m back up in my room. I feel like I should go back on patrol, but I’m afraid of what might happen. I think I may hang the bag and hope I have enough tape to keep it from ripping open. I just can’t deal with people that I care about right now.


        Jasmine’s Diary

        Tuesday, August 17, 2010, Morning

        I have not been keeping up with my regimen of tai chi exercises, so this morning, instead of meditating in the meditation room, I decided to go out to the cliffs behind the house and perform several tai chi routines in order to focus and center myself. It was a good choice, and I felt quite good throughout the rest of the day.

I didn’t mess with small talk. I just held out the bottle and told her to gargle. Eventually that and time might help. But it was a small gesture to remind her there were others there if she needed us. Now I’m going to go and see if I can find Karl. He’ll need to be prepared for what’s to come.


                                    Jasmine’s Diary

                                    Sunday, August 22, 2010, Late Night

                                    I don’t really feel like writing right now, but I cannot sleep, so I think perhaps it is best that I record the events of tonight while they are still fresh in my mind

                                    I killed another person tonight.

                                    I’ve been involved in situations where other krewe members have killed people: the Ivory Coast, our fight against the werewolves. And I have killed supernatural entities before. But I have never personally killed another human being. And even though it was necessary, I’m still quite shaken.

                                    The krewe went to Glenwood to deal with the Abyssal threat there tonight, though we decided that Simon should remain at the manor in case the Gentry or his servants decided to show up again. We arrived at the mages’ lodge with Tavia and her painting. Natalie remained in the car and entered a Boneyard trance in order to muffle the perceptions of those in the lodge so that Tavia and I could sneak in to trap the pestilential beast in the cellar. Then we would deal with Louisa, the maddened mage and the Qliphoth.

                                    Tavia and I were able to slip through the house, past Louisa and her dolls, and into the cellar, though Tavia had some difficulty even with Natalie’s help. Fortunately, I was carrying the painting, which was rendered invisible by my shroud of Stillness.

                                    Simply being in that pitch dark, fetid cellar was enough to leave one sickened and weak, but we both sensed that somewhere in the lightless chamber there lurked the horrible beast we had come to trap. It was bad enough seeing Tavia’s rendition of it; I had no desire to see it in person, so I was thankful for the darkness.

                                    I stood ready to intercept the beast as Tavia began her ritual, and when it realized that she was there, it lurched from the darkness toward her, only to encounter me instead. I blocked its approach, and over the next few moments, we fought, though I was simply trying to distract it. The heightened senses granted by my Primeval caul helped me avoid most of its blows, for I couldn’t see anything, and the tattered veils of my shroud protected me when it did land a blow.

                                    I only had to distract the beast for a few moments, and then Tavia and her painting did their work. With a buzzing moan, the beast was torn from this world and imprisoned in the painting.

                                    Tavia and I moved back upstairs, and I told her to remain safe with the painting in the room above the cellar while the krewe dealt with Louisa and the Qliphoth. She agreed, and I let the others know that I was moving in to deal with Louisa so they would be ready to attack.

                                    Still invisible, I moved into the filthy room in which Louisa sat at a small writing desk, surrounded by her skin-swaddled dolls. I gave her absolutely no warning as I suddenly grabbed the back of her head and slammed her face into the desk, breaking her nose and leaving her stunned.

                                    The dolls were immediately alert, but they had no one to attack until I swept Louisa’s chair out from under her and twisted her into the floor. The bones of her arm cracked as I twisted it behind her, but in the process, I became visible again. The dolls threw themselves at me, trying to tear my flesh with their gnashing teeth, and though I tried to move in harmony with their attacks, a couple of them still hit me. My shroud protected me, but I knew I would not be able to withstand the dolls indefinitely.

                                    Fortunately Doc kicked open the lodge’s front door then and destroyed one of the dolls in an eruption of fire as he unleashed his Pyre-flame Rage upon it. Alexia and Sara followed him in. Sara quickly cleared a portion of the floor and began setting up the binding ritual, while Alexia rushed in and began hacking away at the dolls with her cleavers and knives.

                                    Natalie did something to strike Louisa blind, which might have kept her from attacking me with her magic, but it did not keep her from healing herself. I could hear the broken bones in her arm knitting, and she started to force herself to her feet, so I pulled her from the floor and smashed her against the wall next to the door to the back room. That’s when the door burst open and a corpse clawed its way into the room. It was the body slumped over the table in the back room—the one I noticed when we first investigated the place. It tried to strike me with its rotting fists, but I easily twisted out of the way as I flung Louisa against the writing desk, where she fell to her knees.

                                    She struggled to get up, but then Doc blasted her with more fire and she screamed in pain, falling again to her knees. That’s when the Qliphoth emerged. It’s all-encompassing darkness began to drain the light from the room, and as I glanced at it, I saw something in the endless void inside it that unnerved me. I felt my resolve slipping, but stubbornly steeled myself and returned my attention to Louisa. We had to end this, quickly, before the Qliphoth unhinged our minds completely.

                                    Again I grabbed Louisa’s still-healing arm and wrenched her shoulder out of joint as I slammed her head into the corner of the writing desk. This time, I heard the sickening crunch of breaking bone, and when I released her, Louisa’s body slumped limply to the ground, dead.

                                    It was not until later that the impact of that moment hit me.

                                    Doc attacked the Qliphoth to draw its attention, but it quickly realized that Sara was the real threat. The thing began screaming, and each of its metallic shrieks sent a bone-chilling wave of uncertainty through us, weakening our will. I could tell that Sara was losing her mind, and I realized that if it screamed just once more, even I might not be able to keep my wits about me. Thankfully, Natalie caught on to our distress and used her Boneyard to deafen Sara and me so that we could no longer hear the soul-shattering screams.

                                    The dolls continued attacking me, though they were largely a nuisance, and I was also easily able to avoid the zombie’s clumsy blows. Alexia came to my assistance, and together we destroyed the rest of the dolls. Doc worked with Alexia to lay the zombie to rest as well.

                                    As the ritual neared completion, the door to the cellar burst open and Tavia ran out with her painting. She was in her fishgirl form, and though I had seen her like that before, the others were surprised. She was pursued by a cloud of flies. When Tavia saw how much we were struggling against the Qliphoth, she hesitated until I shouted at her to flee. Fortunately, she listened.

                                    The cloud of flies swarmed around Sara, though, trying to fill her nose and mouth and eyes in order to stop the ritual. She was coughing and gagging, barely able to finish the binding incantation. I was scared that the flies might get the best of her, so I did the only thing I could think to do. I transformed into a flock of crows and began fluttering throughout the room, eating as many of the flies as possible. Several of my crows landed on Sara, delicately snatching the flies directly from her face and eyes. The experience was unpleasant for both of us, but it granted Sara the respite she needed to finish the ritual.

                                    The Qliphoth screamed a terrible scream that left our ears bleeding as it was drawn into the Obsidian Box. Poor Sara spent the next few minutes retching. Alexia went to comfort her, and as soon as the flies dispersed, I returned to my human form to help get Sara back on her feet and out to the car. Meanwhile, Doc set the lodge aflame both inside and out. The place was so tainted that it needed to burn. Before we left, though, Alexia and I took the writing desk and stowed it into the back of her car. It may sound cold, but the desk is now infused with death and could become a charm, largely because of how Louisa met her end. Perhaps it will help me find some deeper meaning in killing her.

                                    We returned to the mansion and spent a good, long time cleaning up, then went our separate ways to occupy ourselves for the evening. I’m certain that everyone was eager to forget the events of the day. I found my will restored by our triumph. The fact that we did not back off even when it seemed our minds were at risk gave me a confirming sense of accomplishment, and has done a bit to help me come to terms with the fact that I killed someone.

                                    Rationally, I know that Louisa had to die. She was too far gone, too tainted, and too dangerous to let live. But I am still haunted by the sound of her skull cracking, by the sight of blood oozing from the side of her head, pooling in the floor like a twisted halo. I feel cold and dead inside, or rather, I feel like a part of me has died.

                                    The worst of it, perhaps, is that I know that I could do it again. I’ve tried to steel my resolve so that if necessary, I can take measures like these. I’m fulfilling my fate as the Resolution of Death within the krewe. But that doesn’t make me feel any better. Indeed, it makes me feel even colder. I’ve lost the last vestiges of my innocence. I don’t…like this part of me.

                                    I retired to my room after cleaning up. I didn’t even go see Karl. He came by awhile ago, but I sadly told him I’d rather be alone. He seemed worried, but left me alone without any argument. I don’t mean to hurt his feelings, but I don’t want to be with anyone right now. I simply feel so wretched.
                                    Lily wanted to cheer me up, but I told her I wasn’t in the mood. Still, I hugged her tightly as I curled up on my bed and tried to sleep. Wrapping her in my arms gave me something to hold on to, something stable to keep me from going a little mad over what I’ve done and what it means for me.

                                    I suppose I should try again to get some sleep.
