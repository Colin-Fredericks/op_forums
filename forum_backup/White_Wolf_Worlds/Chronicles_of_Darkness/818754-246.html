                So, you have to read between the lines for this info, and it's scattered between the core and Half-Damned, but basically, bio ghoul families operate like this:

                They have a max natural lifespan of 150 years, and tend to age about half as fast once they hit adulthood (that last bit is based on some first edition material, but it holds true for the current edition's descriptions).

                They don't rapidly age when they lose their Vitae sources

                They can buy and use Disciplines without Kindred aid, but only the common ones (Animalism, Obfuscate, and the physical powers) unless they have special access to a clan Discipline as their benefit or have a regnant.

                They get Unseen Sense: Vampires for free.

                They have an inherent addiction to vampire blood, and if their family has a regnant, they're born with a full blood bond to her.

                Otherwise, they work like normal ghouls

                        There are two separate things that can happen to disrupt a Touchstone.

                        One is that the Touchstone can be detached. This is what happens when your Humanity drops below the Touchstone rating. It's still your Touchstone, can still restore Willpower, but no longer provides a bonus to resist Humanity detachment. This is merely the point where your relationship is not sufficient to stem your growing alienation. In your example scenario, you are at Humanity 5, but you still have a Humanity 6 Touchstone. If your Humanity increases back to 6, your Humanity 6 Touchstone reattaches and once again provides its bonus to resist breaking points.

                        The other is that the Touchstone can be lost. This is what happens when the person dies or you have a desperate argument bad enough to entirely destroy the relationship. When this occurs, the Touchstone is removed from your Humanity track. If the Touchstone was an additional one added by the Touchstone Merit, that Merit dot is refunded per Sanctity of Merits.

                        If the lost Touchstone was the last Touchstone remaining on your Humanity track, you approach a crisis as detailed on page 88 of the corebook. You may either suffer a loss of Humanity, in which case you have a month in which to develop a new relationship into a replacement Touchstone. This is separate from the Touchstone Merit, which is for having two or more Touchstones. If you refuse the loss of Humanity or can't find a new Touchstone within a month, you fall Languid and will soon enter torpor.

                                                For comparison's sake, I'll point to…

                                                • Werewolf. whose section on regaining Willpower from Touchstones doesn't use the word "attachment" at all, but whose Touchstones are much more firmly attached;
                                                • Changeling, where detached Touchstones specifically can't provide Willpower but reattaching Touchstones is a matter of spending enough scenes of meaningful interaction to unmark severe damage from the relevant Clarity box;
                                                • Hunter, which specifies defending the character's relationship to an attached Touchstone;
                                                • and Deviant's closest equivalent, affirming a Wavering Touchstone, doesn't say if it's a choice you can opt out of, but you specifically don't get Willpower from doing the sort of thing that would get you Willpower if you do affirm a Wavering Touchstone.

                                                I think it's just down to Vampire being the first line to use Touchstones and having the Predatory Aura available to farm mortals for Willpower through the Inspired Condition to supplement their Masks and Dirges.
