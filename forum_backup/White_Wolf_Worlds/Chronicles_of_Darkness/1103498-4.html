                                                    Originally posted by Michael View Post
                                                    That's very impressive. Like, I could see that in a book quality.

                                                    Two thoughts come to mind. First, the relationship between God and the Exarchs. I hesitate to draw the comparison as the real world beliefs are more complex, but as a character in a mythology, God comes across as kinda Exarch-y. As an aside there's a quick reference in Seers of the Throne to the last Father cult before Paternoster being destroyed along with the Roman destruction of Jerusalem. I get that you're probably going for God being beyond the Supernal, but it's implied that before the Diadochi, the Awakened don't really understand the Exarchs and it fits quite well.

                                                    Secondly, I've really wanted a lost era for Solomon, and this really fits.
                                                    first, thanks for your kind words! I really do enjoy writing this Era, so I'm glad others enjoy reading it

                                                    Second, yeah, I have thought about the "Exarch like" nature of God myself while writing this Era- but for obvious reasons I've decided to not go that way. After all, one of the things which really annoyed me during the Dark Eras Anthology was the story which established having some of the most important prophets of the bible as tools of the God Machine (it really "grinds my gears", so to speak)- so I've decided to totally ignore this part. In order to avoid making God identified as one of the villainous Powers that Be, no matter if as an Exarch, God Machine, True Fae or whatever, I've decided to put Him outside of the regular sphere of powers, as its own thing. Notice that I've said "outside" instead of "above"- there is only one mention of powers higher than the Supernal in the text, and it is in the Prophets section- which claim to be powered by God. While they do serve Him, the source of their powers seems to be quit different. Even according to the bible the Israelite weren't the only ones with prophets, after all- so maybe those prophets who serve God are no different than the mages who devote themselves to Him. As for the Father Cult- we still have a lot of time before it needs to show up in the setting. We are still at the First Temple, while it was destroyed in the Second. I may put hints for the future foundation of it, but it would be many years before it would be established.

                                                    In sort, I'm going to take the path of "God is different" in everything which is related to the project- not necessary "above" as much as on the "outside". I do plan on adding a sidebar about it in the finished project- something in the likes of "if you want, God could be the Father, or the God Machine, or the Principle or any other supernatural power which we already have in the setting- but I'm not going to make that choice for your own game. It's your choice to make"
