    Shapeshifter: The Ignited!

    08-18-2024, 12:34 AM
    This is my second foray into this whole thing. Shapeshifter used to be known as Nagual: The Ignited. For obvious reasons, this name wasn't well, appropriate. The original post was also done before Shapeshifter was fully ready to be shown off. I got good criticism last time that had me rework how I wanted to approach this fangame and now I present to you Shapeshifter!
    ************************************************** ************************************************** ************************************************** ************************************************** ************************************************** **********
    Shapeshifter: The Ignited!
    A Storytelling Game of Fragile Balance

    Introduction
    "You walk quietly down the street, surveying your domain. The masses of humanity walk around you, instinctively parting to let you pass. Something is wrong, off. They can’t sense it or feel it singing in their blood, urging them to find it. Destroy it. You glance at your friends, your family, they nod. The burning in their veins pushes them to act. The streets begin to clear, the people feel the coming storm, though they can’t put a face or name to it. They duck into shops, their cars, anything to move away from the violence radiating from you. They don’t know that a monster has appeared in their midst, that if left unchecked, it will leave their little worlds desolate and painted with blood as it tries to feed a hunger it cannot ever sate. Duty bound to protect your home and all who dwell in it, you pick up your pace. It is time to answer the call."


    Shapeshifter is a game about duty and family. About not losing yourself in a power that was forced upon you. About balancing your learned civility with the divine blood within you that calls you to hunt, to violence, to defend with tooth and claw. Separated from humanity and the natural world by the very power that urges you to defend it. You are a shapeshifter, a monster that turns into animalistic forms and adapts to the world around them at a rate that would make Darwin blush. That holds the will of a wild Goddess in their veins, urging them to fight, kill, and die for animals, people, and wild places that will never utter a word of thanks.


    Shapeshifter is about balancing the emotional heart and higher mind, with an animal’s instincts. To overcome the overwhelming urge to rend and tear. To not turn away from your kin, your past, and paint your future with blood and violence. Lurra-Ulaka is a demanding Goddess who does not understand the micro, Her concern is the macro. Hers is the fury of wild beasts and earth shattering natural disasters. To not give in and burn out in Her Radiant fires, and instead learn to control and wield them against the encroaching Absence is the struggle of Shapeshifter: The Ignited.


    The Ignited, so named for the Radiance that burns in their veins can be anything from the strange hermits in the woods, warning people away with their odd behaviors and large signs on their lands to the activist who rallies people to fight for better rights and legislation for humans and animals alike. Shapeshifter is a game of action. Of answering a call to duty, of fighting for a better tomorrow against a force that threatens to eat this world alive. All the while dealing with a day to day life among a Clan that may not fully understand human society, or a human society that does not understand the harm they do to the Clan’s boundaries and territories. Shapeshifter is a balancing game. Of looking around the world you live in and saying, yes. This is all important, you’ll fight tooth and claw for this. Not because it is perfect, but because you believe it can be better.
    The Darkness of the Night
    Chronicles of Darkness is a game of Modern Horror. Chronicles takes place in a world much like our own, but a little more cold. Things lurk in the darkness alongside humanity, hiding, hunting, and dealing with lives that seem at both far too distant from the mundane, and uncomfortably close. Everyone has brushed against this darkness, friends, neighbors, and authority figures have seen things they cannot explain. Few admit it in fear of drawing its attention. People go missing, no answers are given. Days later, the chill of a lingering ghost haunts their homes.


    Humans rarely speak of the darkness beyond their comfortable mundane lives. Those that do, vanish or worse, return changed. Few bring up the topic, unless its between hushed voices in the brightest of lights. Fearing what may be listening, and taking pleasure in their suffering, uncertainty, and terror.


    The Ignited were not always humans, most are not. However, many of them live seemingly human lives. Some of them were humans, changed by a stranger into something monstrous. Many of the Ignited work to protect humanity from this darkness, others take advantage of them no different from the other monsters that prowl the night. The Absence crawls out where humanity is thickest, where doors are left half-open and half-closed. In the liminal spaces of department stores no longer in use, but not yet reclaimed by the wilds. These twisted monstrosities invade and feast upon unwitting man and beast alike. Growing and feasting swiftly through their life cycles, and left unchecked will drain a place dry. Leaving nothing but a strange emptiness, a ghost town. The Ignited fight these horrific invaders, protecting man, beast, and nature from its ravages at the behest of their strange and not too distant goddess.


    The match is struck, and the call is answered. What shape will your Radiance take?
    A Lit Match, a Call from Lurra
    Shapeshifters, or collectively known as the Ignited, are therianthropes. Family lines of Lycanthropes that can pass their hereditary forms through the Radiance in their blood. Gathering in large Clans of family units, varied in shapes and techniques, but not in duty and devotion to Lurra-Ulaka. The Primal Goddess of nature, the natural cycles, and storms. Her garden is the material world, which Her Ignited work to protect from incursions from the Absence and its Invasives.


    To be Ignited is to carry a duty and the near-sentient will of Lurra-Ulaka Herself in the form of the Radiance. A small drop of Her holy blood that determines the shape of your Forms, and is passed down from generation to generation, changing and adapting to each host. It demands action in the face of danger to the Ignited, and their environments. Protection for their Wild Cousins, the normal beasts that share their form. And the cultivation of humanity, directing its potential for the good of Lurra-Ulaka.


    The Ignited gather in Clans to share this burden. Hand picked Menageries of like-minded Ignited group up, protecting small pieces of Lurra’s garden away from the Clan. Their forms may differ, and their tactics change, but they are Shapeshifters and together much more powerful than alone. They come from Broods, sharing their forms and outlooks. Picking up small quirks from the animal whose form they take when the Radiance rises. Their Blood is where they’ve been, how they grew up. Their true family origin, man, beast, or Ignited.
    Dwellers of the Liminal Spaces, the Absence
    "Something unlived slinks in between the flickering of lights in a park’s neglected bathroom. Waiting for the right moment to push between the barriers of here and there. It’s ancient and has never known the touch of grass against its inverted scales. The warmth of the sun has never touched the holes where its eyes should be. Created wrong, and locked away, the creature has instinctively followed every trace of the between until finally, it felt this chance. This moment. When the light is just about to flick back on, between that moment of darkness and brilliance, it invades.


    The Faunid inverted snake hisses as it touches the cold bathroom tiles. No tongue flicks out, instead, beneath every scale an eye opens seeing the world in different shades of motion, light, and noise. Oh the noise. The screaming and laughter of prey just outside. It slithers forward, relentless hunger driving it on. Every step of the way is polluted by its presence, twisted and warping, the light and color draining. Leaving it Barren. The world the Invasive touches never to be the same again.


    Something else also watches the bathroom. Their blood runs hot as the Radiance howls in their veins. The Shapeshifter must be careful of when and where they strike. Before or after the snake is slowed by its first prey? At the first sight of the Invasives, the humans will surely scatter. The cat waits a moment longer, but sees the giant Invasive deviate from chasing a grown man who had gone to the restroom and had run into the horror first, to a slower child that had frozen at the sight of it. Her tail lashes in frustration as she makes up her mind. Growing massive and more feral, the black cat lunges after the Invasive. The Radiance sings in her veins as she carries out her duty to Mother Lurra and the ecosystem of her small town territory."

    The Ignited are called to duty when one of these monstrosities slips through. When no other Ignited is available, Lurra turns to nature. Wild animals are sparked with a drop of blood. Tasted upon the meat of prey, or from a sweet smelling water source. The rarest, though not terribly uncommon of the Bloods, an Ashen is born. Changing forever, higher reasoning flooding them as their forms twist and change to something more capable for the task ahead.


    Most Ignited are born to Clans, starting off as Cinders. A liminal state of waiting for the Call from Lurra-Ulaka. When and where is up to Lurra, and some never hear it. Forever living as near-Shapeshifters, but no less a member of the Clan and able to wield what little of the Radiance resides in their veins.


    A few Ignited are not Called by Lurra, but instead changed by another Shapeshifter. The shedding of Ignited blood into the bloodstream of a human causes it to spark. The Ignited cannot procreate with animals or humans, but only one another. Many believe the ability to Kindle, changing a human into an Ignited if they are accepted by Lurra-Ulaka, is a patch to this in areas where the Ignited numbers run too low. If it takes hold, a Kindled is brought into the fold. Someone with a foot in both the world of humanity and the Ignited.
    Shapeshifters
    The Ignited are a broad people. No one walks, talks, or looks the same as another. The forms they take are as varied as their nationalities, skin tones, and accents. This diversity is considered a strength by the Ignited, even if it leads to conflicts and misunderstandings at times. They are all one, brought together by the Radiance into one massive family. Their duty to protect the natural, mundane world from invaders is universal.


    Individual Shapeshifters understand their duties innately. Not necessarily something that had been sat down and explained. A few of them may never encounter a Clan, but gather in tiny Menageries of likewise lost Ignited who decide to work together to accomplish goals. The Radiance pushes and pulls as they detect strange occurrences in the world around them, blazing when their senses pick up on the stirrings of the Absence. Many just blend into human society, enjoying the luxuries it brings as well as carrying out the demands of the Radiance. Balancing their lives to the best of their abilities.


    The Ignited divide themselves in two ways. Their Blood, or where they come from, and their Brood, or where their Radiance comes from. The Blood determines your base form from Human for the Kindled and Glede, and Beast for then Ashen. Their Brood determines what forms the Ignited can take, what animal statistics the five forms are based on, and what quirks may appear as their Radiance rises and falls.


    This book covers five of the Broods, but they by no means are the only ones in existence. If it is not a strict herbivore, Lurra may call upon it. Creating a Brood of that particular animal, which only dies out when all members cease to exist, or the animal goes completely extinct. These Broods are the city-dwelling raccoon shaped Azeban. The hardy, wilderness roaming, bear shaped Bruin. The curious and secretive, cat shaped Grimalkin. The slippery and collective, spider shaped Gumo. As well as the loyal and pack running, wolf shaped Vargr.


    Regardless of their shapes, the Radiance provides great advantages over normal human beings. The Ignited are faster, stronger, and tougher than their peers even before shapeshifting. Their instincts guide them to hit hard and fast, disregarding much of their morality when their blood is up. Their animal natures go in for the kill without flinching from pain or violence done back to them. It also grants them Adaptations, their Radiance learns and grows just as the Ignited does. Shaped by their environment and encounters with strange and wondrous things. Programmed as though it were a living, evolving organism to adapt and thrive in places that would otherwise be hostile to it. Strengthening the Ignited directly, or interfacing with their environments the flora and fauna found there, or bringing it to bear in environments far from their homelands. However, for all the strength of the Radiance, it brings weakness. A built in adapting one that prevents them from turning on humanity. Radiation causes the Radiance to burn out of control, and the risk of Lurra-Ulaka Herself taking the reins when things become too unstable, too damaged and the Ignited has failed.
    Ignited and Wayward
    The Call does more than enhance the Ignited’s body and mind with the Radiance. It also burns away the soul, replacing it entirely with Radiance, the Will of Lurra-Ulaka. The Ignited must seek new ways to reaffirm who and what they are, to not entirely throw away ethics and morality. This is where the Clan comes in. Giving most Ignited a uniform code of conduct and holding them responsible when they fail to uphold it.


    The Wayward, however, does no such thing. While still bound to the Radiance, and therefore still being pushed and pulled to carry out the Will of Lurra-Ulaka. The Wayward also turn their attention to other flaws in the social system of the Ignited or humanity. Some go Wayward before they even have a chance to become Ignited, their Call broken in some way. Their minds are unable to handle the stress. Others turn their attention to humanity feasting and killing indiscriminately for harm they have done to their environments, Wild Cousins, or simply because they can. Some decide to only create Menageries from servants, serial Kindling select individuals. A few turn entirely upon the Ignited, for one reason or another, devoting their new power to destroying their kin. These Shapeshifters are considered by the Ignited to be broken, reckless, and extremely dangerous threats to the Clan and their territories. Hunted down and destroyed before they can do more harm.

    This is the Introduction Chapter to Shapeshifter: The Ignited. I'm excited to hear your opinions and feedback on it. This is obviously inspired by the various Shapeshifters we've already had in both WoD and CofD, but I hope that I've done enough to also make Shapeshifter stand out from what has come before. This is its own fan-made gameline, that can be played with or without the others present.

    There is a working Core Rulebook for it here: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...it?usp=sharing

    (Note: The title on the document's art is still Nagual, I haven't been able to get it fixed yet. If you or anyone you know can, I would love to get in touch!)

    There are a couple of places that need polishing, namely the Environmental Traits (Which I'm getting to soon) but for now, what is there should work well enough. I like to take and reflavor mechanics that have already been used as those are typically tried and tested vs trying to make my own that may or may not work at all.

    A Night Horrors for Shapeshifter is also in the works on the Shapeshifter: The Ignited Discord Server as well as various other Broods ready for play outside of the 5 found in the Core Rulebook. Other supplements are also in the plans and updates are given as I work on things. So, if Shapeshifter has your fancy, the Server is: https://discord.gg/EH33vPRRTU

    Thank you for reading and giving this labor of love time out of your day! I would love to read your thoughts, suggestions, and critiques below!

          Right off the bat, I would like to say that the scale of the book is quite impressive. Good work on it.

          In terms of content, the a great deal of the book brought to mind Werewolf: The Apocalypse, particularly the focus on the Shapeshifters' connection to the natural world and the presence of states that are like breed forms. I think that the Shapeshifters' focus on immediate family and friends, coupled with their affinity to the mortal world, significantly distinguishes them from the Uratha. Funnily enough, it felt like a lot of their politics was related to a realm that they only had a partial relationship to. The Uratha are very much estranged from both sides of their heritage, while it seems like Shapeshifters have tighter social bonds with people beyond their fellow Shapeshifters.

          As an aside, I would just like to say that the inclusion of the Kindled was a nice addition. The ability to turn a normal human into a werewolf is an aspect of the werewolf mythos that was lacking in both other Werewolf games. The presence of elementals in the core rulebook was also nice to see; it seems like elemental sorts of entities are not covered very often by the sorts of ephemeral entities that are often depicted in the system. The fact that they can shift between a dormant ephemeral form and an incarnate form helps to distinguish them from Horrors.
