    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
    Blessing: Valkyrie Mot are guardians of boundaries between life and death. By this, they can see the restless dead - like ghosts – anytime they would normally see spirits in Twilight. They can also sense if particular Urged or Claimed is instead driven by dead clinging too much to living.
    I wouldn't have 'can see ghosts in Twilight' as there's a Death Facet that basically already does this and a bit more, meaning the Blessing is not very good for characters that have the Gift of Death. The second part doesn't make much sense as a standalone effect - you can't necessarily tell that a character is Urged or Claimed just by looking at them anyway, but if this *does* detect that then it's basically the Storm Lord Sacred Hunt effect except always on, which isn't a good idea as a Lodge blessing. Finally, 'driven by dead clinging too much to living' is unclear as to what it means.

    Aspiration: To find and send souls of those close to Renowns.
    What do you mean, specifically, by close to a Renown? This is pretty important, given that you then run the Ban off the same concept...

    Ban: A Lodge member must at least once per month send soul of one close to Renowns.
    Again, need to know what 'close to Renowns' means. Without having that defined, adjudicating whether an adherent has broken the Ban or not is going to be rather hard.

    SACRED HUNT
    The Lodge Sacred Hunt grants your character the ability to detect nearby dying character close to Renowns within a number of miles equal to half his Primal Urge, rounded up.
    This isn't a viable Sacred Hunt effect. It doesn't really make much sense - you go on a Valkyrie Sacred Hunt against your prey, then start pinging on 'close to Renown' characters nearby who have nothing to do with the hunt?

    I would suggest, once you've defined what close to Renown means, that the Hunt effect could work as simply 'you can tell whether the prey is (close to the Renowns/has a Renown rating of 4 or higher/whatever other tool for judging the condition is appropriate).

    TOOLS
    Valkyrie's Children may purchase the Lodge Armory, Lodge Connections and Lodge Sorcery Merits.
    Why all of these three Merits? I could see one of Armoury or Sorcery depending on the focus you want to give the Lodge, but I'd only go with one. Lodge Connections seems completely superfluous to the Lodge concept.

    Funeral Rites

    Funeral Wolf Rite (•)

    A gift of living to the dead, rite assures that souls move to afterlife and body is cleansed of other influences.
    Symbols: Death, journey, deeds
    Sample Rite: On the battlefield, Valkryie kneel beside the target of Rite, takes his head in her hands and recite his Renowns, calling Odin All Father to claim his soul. ( Presence + Expression )
    Modifiers: Renown that target earn in live and invoked in recitation +1
    Action: Instant ( 3 successes; roll represents 1 turn of recitation )
    Success: Soul of the dead is send to Afterlife. If there is Ephemeral Entity taken power over body, it’s Manifestations are ended and Entity linger in the Twilight near the body. Being rolls her Rank to contrast roll of Ritemaster. If he wins, Entity lost it’s ties to body and linger near to it in Twilight.
    First up, you need to add the specifications of how this rite can be used and targeted; if there's a limitation on how long dead the target corpse can be, etc. The mechanics don't quite make sense for the contest with punting a corpse-jacking entity out either. 'Being rolls her Rank to contrast roll of ritemaster' - which of the ritemaster's rolls? This is, functionally, a combat-time Expel for corpses only; it should probably run off the same basic mechanic as Expel rather than inventing a whole new one.

    Should have four symbols rather than three.

    It is probably worth considerably more than 1 dot. Exactly where on the scales it would fall partly depends on what happens to the soul it affects - where exactly it sends it.

    Funeral Pack Rite (•)

    This Funeral Rite is performed when there is a time to make proper funeral ceremony. This version of Rite is much more powerful, but it’s also let the target lay at least few hours, suspended between life and death. It’s also more effective in banishing bad forces from it.
    Symbols: Death, journey, deeds
    Sample Rite: Running of the Rite is local culture funeral ceremony, lead by the Ritemaster. In Norse culture, it’s typical ‘Viking funeral’ scenes. (Presence + Expression)
    Modifiers: Renown that target earn in live and invoked in recitation +1
    Action: Extended (10 successes; each roll represents 15 minutes)
    Success: Soul of the dead is send to Afterlife. If there is Ephemeral Entity taken power over body, it’s Manifestations are ended and Entity linger in the Twilight near the body. Being rolls her Rank to contrast roll of Ritemaster. If he wins, Entity lost it’s ties to body and linger near to it in Twilight. Additionally, all participants are cleansed from foreign influence of Ephemerals also – treat all Pack members taking part in ceremony as the targets of above power.
    'Like this other rite you already have, but a bit different is not a design approach I'd take. I'd suggest entirely cutting this rite.

    Needs three symbols, definitions about hows and whens, etc.

    'All participants are cleansed from foreign influence of Ephemerals' - you need to be clearer on what you mean by foreign influence here. It's also not entirely clear *why* the rite would copy the expulsion effect across the rest of the pack, given it's not their funeral and they're not dead bodies.

    Again, considerably more than 1 dot.

    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
    Pack Rite – Call the Einherjar (•••)
    This rite summons up a Einherjar from Odin’s army that was once send to Valhalla by Valkyrie, returning by their call. Bone Shadows Choosers of Slain still debate if Einherjar are really ghosts or ancestor spirits.
    Symbols: Circles, a call, deeds, the memento of Einherjar
    Sample Rite: Valkyrie prepares summoning of Einherjar by finding memento of his life and reciting his Renowns deeds. She invokes call to the Odin himself, pleading him to release one of his warriors cause he’s help in doing great deeds is needed. ( Presence + Expression)
    Modifiers: Renown that target earn in live and invoked in recitation +1
    Action: Extended (20 successes; each roll represents 1 minute); potentially contested by the Einherjar’s Resistance
    Success: This rite targets a particular Einherjar by name. The entity becomes immediately aware once the rite begins, and can try to resist or can voluntarily respond to the call. If the rite succeeds, the Einherjar immediately appears within a delineated summoning spot.
    If the summoning takes place in the material world, the area within the delineated space gains the Open Condition and the Einherjar appears in Twilight. If it has the Materialize Manifestation, then it may use this power at no cost when it appears.
    Unless I'm missing something, this rite seems redundant. It's Shadowcall, except more limited for the same cost. I would cut it.

    Fetish – Valkyrie’s Helmet (•)
    Famous winged helmets of Valkyries not only protect them from harm to head – they also convey knowledge of Renown closest deeds of seen person, as they are made of Knowledge spirits.
    Effects: Activations of helmet let’s to ‘hear’ the deeds of observed person in the head of Valkyrie for whole scene. Item also works as proper helmet that not confers typical penalty to Perception roles and having those traits: Rating 2/1, Strength 2, Defense –1, Speed 0, Covarage: Head
    The 'famous' winged helmets are, to my understanding, entirely a fabrication of artists over a thousand years later. What does 'hear the deeds' mean - does it recite a litany of the target's accomplishments, like 'And lo, Steve did stab the mighty bear with a spoon until it died, which was mightily Glorious' or is it giving a running commentary of the deeds he's undertaking right now?

    I'd probably redo it as a 3-dot Facet that lets you shift the effect of Know Thy Prey.



    Making the Lodge female-only is fairly meaningless, given every Uratha can gain the ability to alter their sex for 1 XP.

    A final note - you seem to have the Mot playing the whole Valkyrie thing pretty straight. It's a shame the Secrets of the Lodges extra material still hasn't gone up, as it'd help illuminate one of the areas of Lodges that probably needs more direct examples - they're cults, all of them. They do weird shit, pursue hidden agendas, and generally have a whole pile of skeletons in the closet. To me, it feels like the Mot are just too... normal? Too nice? Now, on the one hand, this is a bit subverted by their ban - the adherents are gonna end up murdering 'close to Renown' people on a regular basis to sate it, as there just aren't going to be enough battles to feed it anyway. However, were I looking at doing a Valkyrie Lodge, I would make it much clearer and more sinister as to what happens to the poor bastards they 'choose' (and honestly I'd probably say 'the Valkyrie spirit just eats their souls to power it up for the future Ragnarok thing; humans' glorious ideals of Valkyries are a really, really unfortunate misunderstanding of what's really going on).

    - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
