    What do Vampires want?

    05-20-2015, 03:26 PM
    This seems to come up a lot in Vampire games... PCs come to some authority figure (or even just an ally), and say they want help with something. Their potential ally says "Ahh, but in exchange for what?" And the player characters say "What do you want?"

    (Okay, often I'll turn that question around and say "What do you have to offer?", but the essence remains the same)

    So I figured I'd start brainstorming some things Vampires might want in return for a favour, from the big to the small. Or, to flip it around, what useful things can your vampire offer as payment to another?

    - A future favour (okay, this is kind of a cop-out, but probably a pretty frequent thing)
    - Inside information about someone they're close to
    - A person - whether a favored vessel or a ghoul retainer
    - Concession of territory/feeding grounds
    - Some hard to obtain physical object
    - Tutoring in a Clan-specific Discipline
    - "Drink my blood...just once...for now"
    - Taking action against another Vampire ("I need you to send Vinnie a message...")
    - Using your Disciplines on their behalf ("I've got this inconvenient mortal acquaintance...I hear you can erase memories?")

          Say nice things about me in Elysium around specific visitors.

          Pay off this beat cop. Careful he doesn't get suspicious and shoot you.

                  I'd also say that, at least during the initial stages of interaction, a lot of vampires probably give the person they're dealing with some make work. Just to see if they follow their orders, how well they follow them and the like. So it could be some minor things like,

                  Without looking inside this briefcase, deliver it to this address tomorrow at exactly midnight.
                  Drive me from here to here, take the quickest route.
                  I need you to look after this person for a week, talk to them, but never mention me.

                  However, all of these are just tests. The briefcase has nothing important inside, there is no urgency to the drive and the person is the other vampires Ghoul or retainer. The one giving the favour just wants to see how loyal and, or, intelligent the person asking for a favour is.
