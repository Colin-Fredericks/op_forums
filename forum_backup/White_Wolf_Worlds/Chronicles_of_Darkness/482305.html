    Swaggering VS Submissive conditions

    04-16-2015, 10:39 AM
    For comparison:

    Swaggering:
    Your character faced the full bore of a Rahu’s essence.
    He’s sure that he can win in the face of the Rahu’s fury. He
    can not prepare himself to face the Rahu or her pack in any
    way — buying weapons, laying traps, or changing his normal
    routine — without first spending a Willpower point and making
    an extended, reflexive Resolve + Composure roll. You may
    make one roll per turn, but each roll requires an additional
    point of Willpower. The target number of successes is equal
    to the Rahu’s Purity.
    Possible Sources: The Rahu’s Dominant hunter aspect.
    Resolution: Suffer a wound penalty from lethal or aggravated
    wounds, or suffer a lethal wound in your character’s
    last Health box.
    Beat: n/a

    Submissive:
    Your character faced the full bore of a Rahu’s essence.
    He’s cowed, and cannot bear to face the Rahu’s fury. He can
    not directly attack, insult, or defy the Rahu, or the Rahu’s
    pack, without first spending a Willpower point and making
    an extended, reflexive Resolve + Composure roll. You may
    make one roll per turn, but each roll requires an additional
    point of Willpower. The target number of successes is equal
    to the Rahu’s Purity. You don’t need to spend Willpower or
    roll if the Rahu or one of her pack has attacked your character
    this turn.
    Possible Sources: The Rahu’s Dominant hunter aspect.
    Resolution: Suffer a wound penalty from lethal or aggravated
    wounds, or suffer a lethal wound in your character’s
    last Health box.
    Beat: n/a

    I like Swaggering better. Submissive is simply boring. It ends the fight. By the time that the condition is resolved by itself, the character would be dead. Swaggering, on the other hand, allows for different outcomes, while still putting your prey right where the Rahu wants it: in a straight up brawl. Furthermore, Rahu aren't default alphas who'd apply Submissive to the first hint of dissent from their packmates.
