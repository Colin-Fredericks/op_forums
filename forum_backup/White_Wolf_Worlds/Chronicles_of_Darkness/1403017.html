    Tips for a a Legacy

    07-15-2020, 08:28 PM
    I've recently joined my first Mage 2e game, and I simply adore exploring the CofD setting in such an exciting way. My creativity has been flowing ever since I first read the Core book, and one particular thing I have been fascinated with are the Legacies. I already have an idea for one I would most certainly want to found once we reach that point in our game, so I thought I would share it here! If you guys have any feedback or criticisms, please feel free to share!

    Origins
    Parentage: Acanthus, Free Council.
    Background:
    Appearance:
    Doctrine
    Prerequisites: Life 2, Empathy 2 and one of the following additional Skills at 2 dots or higher: Persuasion, Socialize, Crafts, Medicine, or Science.
    Initiation: The would-be Whisp must give up something of great sentimental value to their Mentor, putting their wholehearted trust in them.
    Organization: Blisses follow a loose hierarchy; older, more experienced members guiding newer Initiates, until they are seen as true equals. Newer members advance as they show genuine care for their brothers and sisters, and their love for the Legacy becomes unquestionable.
    Theory: The Cloud Nine Moths see the fractures in Mage society: the Orders each vying for power as they combat the Seers, the rift between a Mage’s mundane life and their newfound Awakened world, and the rift between those who would obsess over the past, and those who immerse themselves in the promise of the future. They believe by mending these tears in the Awakened collective, all Mages can focus on the present, and work towards the Legacies’ ultimate goal: Nirvana.
    Magic
    Ruling Arcanum: Life
    Yantras: Succeeding on an Empathy roll relevant to the spell (+2); Showing affection to a loved one (+1); Putting a past problem out of your head, or refusing to worry yourself over a coming event (+1 or +2 if this causes you further complications, or stress later on); Casting to better someone else (+1 or +2 if this leaves the caster in a disadvantageous position)
    Oblations: Spending time with a dear friend, or family member; Participating in a charity event; Tending to a present obligation; An act of genuine generosity.
    Attainments
    First: Saving Grace
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    Recreational substances are often used by Blisses, especially with Sleepers, as they are an easy way to broaden one’s mind and connect with those around them. They can prove dangerous, however, and this Attainment ensures the Whisp can combat such substances that turn against the body. This emulates “Cleanse the Body”, with Reach assigned to Instant cast and potency equal to the Mage’s Life dots.

    Optional: Time 1
    The Whisp spends a scene grounding himself in the present, focusing himself in on the now. This can take the form of a meditative ritual, pep-talk, or any other form of grounding, which then emulates the effects of “Perfect Timing”, with Advanced Duration.



    Second: The Pulse of the City
    Prerequisites: Life 2, Empathy 3
    The Bliss taps into the thrum of all things living and feeling, extending her empathy to the wider world around them and becoming connected to all living beings in the area. This Attainment functions as “Web of Life”, with Reach assigned to Instant casting and Advanced Scale. It varies in that the Mage may specify a specific type of organism after the spell is cast, keeping a calm mind as they filter through the life in the area.

    Optional: Time 2
    The Whisp finds the flow of Time moving just right for them, providing them with a smooth stream to wherever they need to be. Using this Attainment, the Mage instantly gains the benefits of “Green Light/Red Light”, lasting for the next hour.





    Third: Broadened Horizons
    Prerequisites: Life 3. In addition to the Empathy 3 requirement from the previous Attainment, the Bliss must attain two dots in a second skill from the list of possible requirements.
    The Mage spends a scene revitalizing her more tangible senses, opening herself up to the physical world and all it has to offer her, so she may understand her environment on a deeper level than her peers and partake in the knowledge it has to offer her. This Attainment mimics “Heightened Senses”, with Reach allotted to Advanced Duration and allowing the Potency to be equal to their dots in Life.



    Optional: Time 3
    Some moments are far too precious to let slip away, and with this Attainment, the Bliss completely grounds themselves in the present. This Attainment acts as a “Veil of Moments'', with Reach given to Instant Cast and Advanced Duration. While this Attainment is in effect, the Whisp is granted a +1 to all Resolve or Composure rolls to act under the pressure of the present.




    Fourth: Allies Abound
    Prerequisites: Life 4, Empathy 4 and the Bliss must attain two dots in a third skill from the list of possible requirements.
    The Bliss sends out an empathetic flare over the course of a scene, subconsciously calling to those around them for aid as they take notice of the Whisps plea. This Attainment works as per “Lure and Repel”, capturing the attention of any nearby plants, animals or people to help the Bliss. Reach is assigned to Advanced Duration, shifting the Primary Spell Factor to Potency, and ensuring the attracted targets are at least marginally helpful, being compelled to offer the Bliss genial favors or otherwise help them in some small way.
    Optional: Time 4
    The Whisp uses a scene to persuade the past to send itself forward to the present, bringing the two together as one. This emulates “Temporal Summoning”, Reach assigned to Advanced Duration and to give the Attainment Temporal Sympathy, allowing the Bliss to more easily root the subject in the present.




    Fifth: Mending the Broken Heart
    Prerequisites: Life 5. In addition to the Empathy 4 requirement from the previous Attainment, the Bliss must attain three dots in a second Skill from the list of possible requirements, or two dots in a third Skill from the list.
    The Bliss spends a scene comforting their subject, mending the wounds upon both their body and heart as they embrace them with the truest expression of Supernal compassion. This Attainment functions as per “Mend”, with it’s Reach as to allow the Bliss to heal damage done by deprivation and provide the embraced with the nourishment of a full meal and plenty of water in addition to putting the subject to ease, providing them the benefits of a full night’s rest. The subject is also calmed as their pain is eased, and they focus on the present, not worrying about what may happen. This suppresses physical and emotional shock in the subject, soothing their very soul of pain.
    Optional: Time 5
    The Whisp buys back the most valuable Time of all; Time with a loved one. The Bliss spends a scene honoring someone dear to them who is now deceased, or otherwise completely removed from their life and puts pause to the world around them. This acts as a “Temporal Pocket” for the Bliss and her summoned loved one, with Advanced Duration to ensure the two have plenty of time unfettered by the march of the future. The subject is cognizant, appearing as how the Bliss last saw them at their full usage of all mental and physical faculties. The summoned retain all memory they had up to the point of death or otherwise the end of their mental capabilities and recognize the Whisp, having (at least) a good starting impression. The subject is typically considered to have come to terms with their own demise, and does their best to ease the mind of the Bliss as best they can while helping them in whatever way possible. Once the Bliss has come to terms with the summoned subject, the two part in ultimate finality, as the Whisp heals the rift within their heart. This cleanses them of any negative Conditions related to the loss of their beloved, in addition to restoring them to full Willpower. Once this Attainment is used on a specific subject, they may never be called forth again.

                  I feel the first Attainment might be there because this Legacy is inspired by the Cult of Ecstasy from Mage:The Ascension. The focus on Bliss, the use of drugs, tuning into the passions of others and using that to redefine the moment. Plus taking an Arcana from the Thyrsus, shamans on the Path of Ecstasy. However, the yantras and oblations make no reference to their use of recreational substances, which could help reinforce that aspect.

                  The theory section seems to suggest Space with the theme of rifts, whereas Nirvana would normally be associated with Mind. I think you are going for the idea that time is an illusion, that by having greater mindfulness of the present, one can organically flow down the path towards a better future. But I think that needs to be clarified and I am not sure how it correlates to their initiation. It is an interesting theme, embodying present focused generosity.

                  Regarding yantras, I think other ones could be: mementos of those you care about (+2 for something symbolizing a deep bond, like a wedding ring) and geniunely trying to save someone (+2 if it puts your life at risk)

                  For oblations, how about organizing a peacemaking event and creating a Felicity Scarf (a reference to a Tibetean myth and the moth nickname).

                  The theme you are going for is Mindful Altruism, correct ? Building on the idea, how about relating each of the Attainments to one of the Satipatthana, the Buddhist paths to mindfulness. The last one could relate to Ānanda, reflecting their role as mouthpieces and intermediaries of the bliss of nirvana.

                          Glad to help. Normally when trying to figure out an initiation, I try to check for 3 key characteristics:

                          Active: Joining a Legacy is a choice, it doesn't normally happen on accident (but there is an interestting design space to explore in accidentally obtained ones too, the Broken Bonds are a great example of this in practice). It requires an active exploration of its Mysteries, showing the mage has a basic grasp of its skillset.

                          Reflective: It demonstrates a deep commitment by the initiate. Try making it involve a small scale version of their duties or philosophy.

                          Accessible: Mages are able to join existing Legacies at a fairly low power level, so the challenge needs to be within reasonable reach. Normally you would want something they could work in during their downtime, as a sidequest or as a bonus objective during the main adventures. While a cool scene finalizing the process is awesome, a huge side plot makes it harder for the GM to work it into the campaign and is preferably avoided.

                          The initiation is one of the last things I usually write. As you flesh out the background ("what kind of situation even made it be created ?"), organization ("How do mentors and students normally behave ? How are they integrated into their Orders ? Do they have an HQ or major events ?"), appearances ("How do they commonly dress and behave in everyday life ?") and other sections, you start to refine the broad strokes of your original concept and distill the essence of the Legacy. Then it becomes a lot more intuitive to come up with an initiation.

                          In addition, I also break down my train of thought in my Legacy writeups. You can find two of them in my signature, with two more being recently posted (the Bannerets of Ameretat and the Anima Meccanica). Maybe that could prove useful in designing yours.
