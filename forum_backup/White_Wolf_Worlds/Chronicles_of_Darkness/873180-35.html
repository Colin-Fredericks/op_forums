                                            Originally posted by Morangias View Post
                                            That's an interesting interpretation that I would have never come up with. One problem I immediately see with it is that it actually contradicts the text of the power, as the Satiety effect of CM says "The Beast gains automatic successes equal to his Lair dots on all Strength related rolls for the remainder of the scene (...)" whereas treating them as a weapon bonus would mean they only apply to successful attack rolls, rather than all rolls.
                                            As Tessie says above. The phrasing of the mechanic is not optimal, but to be frank there's the simple fact that the way ChroD dice math is set up to work means there would be little reason not to have framed it as "these rolls automatically succeed, with [Lair] successes in cases where this matters."

                                            The Anakim wheelhouse is already full of stuff that effectively reduces the number of successes you would need to accomplish a Strength-related task that doesn't require the standard one success to complete; like with Fate boons in Mage 2e technically being readable as allowing a character with the right near-basic rote to automatically succeed at any spellcasting roll, the broader context of the mechanic in question makes a particular reading come across as obviously unintended.
