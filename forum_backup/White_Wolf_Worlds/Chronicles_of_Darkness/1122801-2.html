                                    There's a suburb of Houston known as the Woodlands. It's about an hour or so north of the city proper, and it's aptly named: trees are everywhere, towering over the roads, looming along running paths, ringing golf courses and dividing neighborhoods, and all those neighborhoods well-to-do or better. It's the home for the sort of people who can afford a long commute into work, or working from home, or the best sort of schools for their kids, and its east side is dominated by a mall so large that people will come in from miles around just to shop.

                                    If half the stories I've heard about it are true, as a former resident currently living on the other side of the tracks (literally), it'd make one hell of a Vampire setting. Undead thrill-seekers hurtling down the roads at night, confident that they'll survive whatever crash might come; wealthy members of the Invictus and the Lancea et Sanctum who are too self-assured and "discerning" to descend to living in the city itself; ghouls hiding out among the predominately non-white lawn care crews; and always, the necessity of keeping up with the Draculas. Gotta show that you've got just as much in your undeath as anybody else.

                                    (Admittedly, I know none of the stories I've heard are true, and the Woodlands is, by and large, a very nice place- but it's tempting to look at it and ask: what if?)
