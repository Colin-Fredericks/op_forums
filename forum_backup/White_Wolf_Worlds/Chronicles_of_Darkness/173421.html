    Homunculus-Based Lineage

    06-18-2014, 09:19 PM
    Be wary, for there be much text to follow! You've been warned!

    I tried to write-up a Homunculus based lineage for P:tC. I know it's crappy right now, but its a first draft. Much like the Tammuz and Galatea, there are many similar creatures to Homunculi in the NWoD, but also like the Tammuz and Galatea I'm trying to distance them enough from those creatures so they can be their own thing.

    I haven't really been able to decide what their Disquiet and Wasteland would be like, nor have I settled on a Lineage Bestowment (I was considering a regenerative power, but I don't know)…suggestions would be nice along with ideas or critiques in general...

    The Homuncio

    Quote: “I can’t pretend to be something I’m not.”
    Other Nicknames: Homunculi, The Misbegotten, Dippels, Little Men (derogatory)
    Humor: Choleric
    Element: The Homuncio are born from a chemical heat, personified by their formative trigger, Sunstone. Sunstone is a phosphorescent alchemical elixir that embodies the generative power of living things as well as the random imbalance that allows growth and evolution to take place.
    Bestowment: (?)
    Concepts: lone wolf, escaped mental patient, drug addict, adrenaline junkie, philosopher, tormented artist, inventor, serial murderer


    Description: The end goal of almost every alchemist was never to create life, but rather to preserve and enrich life that already existed. Some tried to find ways to transmute one element into another, some sought an elixir of immortality, while others sought the Philosopher’s Stone, or some other kind of miracle matter. Everything was done in the process of completing this Magnum Opus, this “Great Work.”

    The Homuncio Lineage knows this cost all too well.

    Homuncio or Homunculus, both mean the same thing… a “Tiny Man,” a “Poor Mortal,” a “Sorry Creature.” The Homuncio are all these things, imbalanced in both body and mind, and lacking a soul. Not hailed as failures by their creators, homunculi were nonetheless created not as an act of life, and very often not even to perform any useful function—they were but stepping stones for alchemists in pursuit of their Great Work, and to this day every Homuncio born is a stepping stone for another Promethean’s pilgrimage. The Lineage of the Homunculi knows more of its demiurge than its own progenitor.

    Homunculi come into the world thrashing about as their thoughts swirl in disarray, and unlike most Prometheans, they very often come out of their generative soup speaking words, although in totally meaningless streams of nonsense. Eventually their thoughts settle and their humors calm, but the curse of turmoil never fully goes away, and they are left unbalanced, and unwhole. To many Prometheans, the Homunculi evoke a strange combination of both pity and fear for their unstable state of being. At best Prometheans regard them as damaged, at worst they consider the entire lineage a family of demented loons and mad monsters. These are all stereotypes of course; but, then again, all stereotypes come from somewhere.

    To the Homuncio, the achievement of humanity isn’t just the end of the Saturnine Night; it is the end of insanity. It is the end of mental fragility and the healing of wounds born from being created and then being left by the wayside. It is to enter into a new life where one can be remembered amongst the masses instead of dying alone and lost in their own skulls. Prisoners inside their own minds, the Homuncio are born hating themselves, and they will do everything in their power to break free, no matter how desperate in might be…


    Torment: When one of the Misbegotten enters Torment, it can be a truly scary event indeed, mainly due to the fact that there’s absolutely no way to predict what will happen. When a Homunculus reaches her breaking point, what happens is almost entirely a roll of the dice—she could become hell-bent on vengeance as per her Choleric humor, or she could suddenly suffer a complete shift into a new humor and suffer its subsequent torment; he could suffer an array of hallucinations and delusions, or his body can suddenly start firing off transmutations at random and start tearing itself apart; she could become the embodiment of insanity, or just as easily become so super-sane that he disregards any sort of abstract understanding such as love, mercy, or murder; She could enter a catatonic sate, or enter a frenzy. Almost anything can happen when the already unbalanced Homuncio becomes even more unbalanced.


    Disquiet: (?)


    Wasteland: (?)


    Creation: A Homuncio stands unique in that it is one of the few lineages that must be “grown.” The would-be creator takes a body, or a mish-mash of body parts equivalent to an entire body (head, limbs, torso and organs) and boils it in hot water until it dissolves into a dark sludge. The bones are removed from the waste and ground into a fine powder. The slush of human remains is then placed into a container (a tub, vat, even an oil drum) along with boiling hot water and whole milk. The creator pours the powdered bone into a heated flask or large glass, which is stewed with fresh blood and hair. Sulfur is added, and after enough time has passed, the creator vomits into the brew an elixir called Sunstone, which their body had instinctually been incubating just for this event. The Sunstone glows with a bit of the creator’s own Azoth, along with a sliver of her wisdom. In the heated glass the elements bubble and collide until it appears that a tiny translucent infant made of flame grows suspended in the fluid. The creator takes the glass and pours its contents into the hot blend of dissolved dead flesh and milk, and waits. After about ten hours, the newly created Homuncio emerges.


    Progenitor: The demiurge that created them was the infamous 18th century alchemist Johann Konrad Dippel. Much like other alchemists, Dippel’s main goal was not to create new life, but rather to enhance life that already existed. The true goal of his experiments was, as he put it, to “engender life in the dead.”

    His first attempts at putting life in the lifeless entailed trying to funnel the soul of one dead body to another just as many alchemists had tried to do. This was ultimately deemed a Sisyphean task, since there ultimately was no soul or spark of life to transfer between dead bodies. Dippel then took to an alternate route, trying to isolate the vital components in dead bodies by boiling dead remains in large vats, using the resulting bile to create concoctions with which he might heal and even resurrect the dead. He moved from animal remains to boiling those of the human being.

    One fateful night, Dippel concluded that the vital spark was not something that could be harvested, but had to be created. Dippel studied and researched through all kinds of alchemical lore before he finally found fascination in the works of Paracelsus and his recipe for creating a homunculus. A created life! Though the wanted result was not to create a new life as in the traditional way of creating a homunculus, Dippel believed he could use a similar method to create the energetic reaction needed to engender life in the dead. To create a flame of life with which to spark the reaction to rebuild and regrow a lost life. He placed liquefied remains in a vat, and began the process. The creature that came into the world that night wasn’t quite a resurrection, but the experiment was deemed a success. A necessary step in moving Dippel closer to the secret of bringing the dead back to life. Sadly the resulting disquiet of the creature drove the people of Dippel’s village to run both him and his creation out of town.

    Nobody really knows what became of the creature after this. It is told the creature eventually destroyed itself after entering a mad torment, but no one knows for sure. However, there is a strange rumor that abounds, declaring that the creature moved madly about before meeting its destruction from the hands of a man named Victor Frankenstein…


    Appearance: The Homuncio bare certain features in common; all of them are pale-skinned, even when of dark complexion. They also possess next to no body fat, even when all the parts used in their creation came form the obese, and they often possess very little body hair, if any at all, with only their eyebrows and tops of their heads covered (and some not even that). Perhaps the most notable thing of any Homuncio, however, is their very large, dark eyes which people find easy to get lost in.

    Beyond these similarities the Homuncio are an extraordinarily varied lot, possessing both traits and body portions that are a mixture of the body parts used to generate them. They can be tall and thin, short and squat, large and muscular, androgynous, and on and on. You probably won’t see the mismatch of traits you might find in a Frankenstein, however, since such things as skin tones and uneven body proportions have a tendency to average out in the Homunculi’s generation process, but they can be very odd-bodied nonetheless.


    Disfigurement: When the true form of a Homuncio comes out, it is a site to behold. Their dark eyes suddenly engorge with blood and stand out from their sockets. Whatever hair they may have suddenly takes on an oily black sheen.

    Their skin possesses a grey, or even a bluish or greenish tint. It is also revealed to be thin and translucent, exposing the network of dark veins, arteries, muscles, and ligaments beneath.


    Refinements: The Homuncio practice virtually all the refinements in equal measure, the main problem is sticking with one. Homuncio have a habit of changing their refinements often, and sticking to none for more than a year or two. There does, however, seem to be a larger number of Homuncio found in the refinements of Plumbum and Cobalus, and more steadily. This could be attributed to the Homuncio’s attempts at fixing themselves; they would of course be attracted to the study of source (Plumbum), and they do have an innate connection to imbalance and impurity (Cobalus). There has also been a fair number of fervent Cuprum followers amongst the Homunculi.

    The only refinements that lack great Homuncio presence are Stannum and Centimani. This probably owes to the inordinate amount of self-loathing many Homuncio hold for themselves. It’s hard to embrace Torment or the Monster within when you truly hate both. This doesn’t stop a few from joining the ranks, however, and they often prove to be some of the most viscous psychopaths the supernatural world has to offer.


    Character Creation: Homuncio talents for manipulation are often low, but they can often have a strong presence and composure to make up for it. Physical attributes can often be a role of the dice, but mental attributes are often a focus. Though the Homunculi are mentally unbalanced, almost none of them are stupid.

    But unbalance is the name of the game in the Homuncio’s half-lives, and it often shows. Pretty much all Homuncio are ‘born’ with one or two minor derangements, and all Homunculi suffer a -1 penalty when it comes to avoid gaining new derangements.


    Stereotypes
    Frankensteins: You’re a better human being than me baby brother…
    Galatea: Come a little bit closer…please...
    Osiris: You’re funny, too funny.
    Tammuz: Do you speak?
    Ulgan: How surreal to dwell amongst things others won’t see...
    --
    Vampires: I know what it is to be born in blood.
    Werewolves: Sure you can eat me, if I can do the same to you in turn.
    Mages: Go ahead and use me, you’ll regret it one way or another.
    Mortals: Why are you so unhappy with love so available?


    Pandoran Mockery: Salaputium
    The Salaputium are the Pandoran mockery for the Homuncio lineage of Prometheans, named for the genderless word for “Manikin” or "Tiny Man."

    The Salaputium are truly bizarre creatures. Upon their genesis what emerges from the fluid of a Homuncio’s creation is not a Promethean, but a writhing sack-like egg that promptly bursts into five or six of these strange things. The creatures that emerge and crawl from this mess appear to be tiny humanoids without genitals, and possessing bizarrely proportioned extremities and heads. Their preferred method is to dissolve their prey with solvents or corrosive materials and drink in the fluids. They also have the peculiar habit of trying to crawl up inside their prey and trying to eat them from the inside out.

    While dormant, the Salaputium will often take the form of models or broken toys. Some have even been known to take forms similar to the Visible Man models and even modern Cortical Homunculus models.

    All Salaputium possess the Pandoran Transmutations Small Stature and Acid Phlegm.
