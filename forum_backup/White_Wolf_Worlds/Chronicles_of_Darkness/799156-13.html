                                            I consider how often I use wolf senses in games to be a pretty good gauge for how often a werewolf might use them in daily life.

                                            It's pretty frequent. Pretty much anytime something catches my eye.

                                            Oh, that guy is staring at me. Does he smell like another werewolf? Wolf senses.
                                            Oh that guy is isn't meeting my eyes. Wolf senses. Why's his heart beating so fast? I can smell him sweating in his shirt. Does he know me somehow? Does he know what I am?
                                            Oh, the boisterous conversation turned to hushed whispers as I came in the room? Wolf senses. Oh, they're just racist.
                                            Oh that guy bolted as soon as I turned my back. Where's he headed? Should I follow him? Wolf senses. Oh, he's just throwing up in the alley.

                                            So I'm not saying you're gonna know anytime a vampire is around, but wolf senses are pretty reflexive (literally, I mean) so it's gonna happen sometimes for sure.

                                            I also think this is a matter of two conflicting priorities. Hishu is the form that hides the werewolf, a vampire probably shouldn't be able to instantly sense a werewolf in Hishu, that doesn't gel with the role Hishu plays in Werewolf. But the story is a bit different in Vampire (which was made without any knowledge of what Werewolf would do with Hishu) It's a game where Predatory Auras play a huge role, and they're violent, instinctive and automatic.

                                            So I'd say if you're playing a Vampire game, let vampires sense werewolves the way the book describes. That is cool and terrifying and offers a lot room to play with werewolf encounters in your campaign. If you're playing Werewolf, though, then having a major splat instantly see through what is supposed to be one of your defenses would obviously not be operating as the rules intended.
