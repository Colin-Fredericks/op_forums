    Supernal Vision & Crossover

    02-15-2017, 05:04 PM
    There doesn't seem to be any specific clarification in the books, so I was hoping that the community would have some suggestions on how to handle this.
    Supernal Vision (Prime 1) can reveal which supernal realm a being is most connected to. While this is obviously meant to be a Mage's Path, could it also include general information about other supernaturals - do Vampires seem distantly related to Stygia, for example, or does it simply turn up with "not applicable"?

    I'm specifically curious about reading Sleepwalkers with this spell. It will inform if a person is a Sleeper or Sleepwalker, which implies that Sleepwalkers have a closer connection to the supernal. Would they ever have an affiliation with a particular Path (if they were going to Awaken, or revealing which Path their thoughts and actions primarily resonate with)?
    Or would you rule it instead as "Sleepers are resonant with the Abyss; Sleepwalkers with nothing"?

    I'm curious to hear peoples thoughts on this.
    Thanks,
    ~Seraph

      Supernal Vision doesn't do that.

      In fact Supernal Vision doesn't reveal degrees of connection to the Supernal at all, just that there may be a connection or the existence of a supernatural phenomena within the Mage's view (Expanded to all supernatural phenomena with the +1 Reach).

      Which Supernal Realm a target is most closely connected to would be an ST call. Most people (Sleepwalkers included) would have a stronger resonance with one Supernal realm than another. Most supernatural creatures would apparently appear as a nonsensical jumble if the Mage is unfamiliar with the kind of aura they might exude.

                                      I don't think the wording should be interpreted as 'automatically knows if they are a Mage/Sleepwalker/Proximus or if they are a Sleeper' because Proximus is a subset of Sleepwalkers. I think it makes more sense for the spell to distinguish between all four. Both Mages and Proximi have different kinds of connections to the Supernal. Sleepwalkers other than Proximi don't have any connection to the Supernal, but they also don't suffer Quiescence like Sleepers. It's definitely something a Prime Unveiling spell should be able to distinguish and there's no reason to be forced to cast a different Unveiling or Knowing spell with that kind of major overlap with this spell.
                                      In this case all major templates (aside from Mages, of course) would show up as Sleepwalkers even without the Reach option, but that should be the extent of it.
