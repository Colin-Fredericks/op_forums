    A cipher and Interlock - Input appreciated

    04-17-2016, 12:08 AM
    Hey Everyone,

    So I have finally started narrating a Demon campaign and I have three players to which I am now designing their ciphers and interlocks.

    I have just completed one of them and would like some input if you think its acceptable.

    So the First Demon's cover works in a school as psychologist, she is a guardian tempter and can be described as a "master manipulator". and heavily directed towards social maneuvering.

    So Taking into account everything the player told me about the character I decided on the following:


    1st: Fungible Knowledge. (chosen by the player)


    2nd. Embed: Heart's Desire. - I feel it goes on with her theme of getting to know people (and what makes them tick) and manipulate them.

    1st. Interlock: Fungible Desire. (FK + HD)
    Roll: Wits + Persuasion - Composure

    - The demon can swap one of her own aspirations with one aspiration of the target for a numbers of days equal to primum.
    On a exceptional success I am considering the change could be for number of weeks equal to primum.

    3rd. Embed: Don't I know you - Again makes sense to someone that is so much into social maneuvering to be able to do it more efficiently.

    2nd. Interlock: You owe me! (HD + DIKY)
    Roll: Manipulation + Subterfuge - Composure

    - The demon learns the most recent aspiration filled by the target and becomes associated to this success, in a way that the target believes now that s/he "owe one" to the demon, gaining the leveraged condition.
    * Now this was a bit problematic for I didnt want it to make redundant or a "better version" of Don't I know you. But I consider this to be a "not without risk" approach to social maneuvering.
    Sometimes circumstances make it NECESSARY for you to make someone do something. Perhaps you failed your social maneuver and you don't have the chance to go all full hard leverage on the person, you just need her do to something, and do it now.
    That's when I think You own me! should come to play, however, the target is not going to magically forget what she did and for whom or why. This may lead to a loose end that might come back to bite on the demon's ass. Also I don't think that the person would do anything that could be considered a breaking point (like not something "help me kill X"), but I see more like: ("I just want to take a look at the lab, wont take more than a minute" situation). unless of course the target's last completed aspiration was to kill his boss... which may turn to be a very interesting plot point.


    4th Embed: You can tell me - The truth will set us all free, or not. But a bit more of information wont hurt. generally.

    3rd. Interlock: Tell them you know me! (DIKY + YCTM)
    Roll : Manipulation + Subterfuge - Composure

    - The demon can say something about how he and the target met, which will be taken as a truth for a number of days equal to primum, although this will not affect cover:
    "We have met years ago at that conference" ; "We went to college together" ; "I was the first women you had sex with!" "Your secretary just introduced me to you, I am your new client" are examples of valid statements. When this embed is active you may remove one door from social maneuvering. This embed cannot be used on a target that the same demon used Don't I know you.
    * I find this embed useful for sometime you may need to reach someone but, how to start that conversation without looking like a crazy person? Or perhaps that police officer just got you but... didnt you go to school together?
    But I really would profit on someone commenting on this one!


    Now I know I had to make a bit a of a leap with don't I know you, because it says that the target does not mistake the demon for the person on his past or may not even be conscious of the connection, but I think this is somewhat limiting when thinking about interlocks (after all it would them seem to be just a mechanical embed giving more dices etc.) But i tried to stay true to a level of it of associating the demon to the target's past on a positive relation.
    I am open to all constructive criticism on how to improve what I've done .


    As soon as I finish the other 2 ciphers I'll post them here as well.

              Hello everyone,

              Here I come again, with the second cipher I came up for my second player.
              He is a destroyer saboteur, his cover is a police men, and he sees himself as a (bit twisted) gunslinger paladin.

              Taking into account everything the player told me about the character, I came up with this:

              1st. Embed: Merciless Gunnman (chosen by the player)

              2nd. Embed: Check Backdrop

              1st. Interlock: Not Today Satan, Not today. (MG + CB)
              Stamina + Firearms
              - When the demon takes a shot that would kill him, he may once a (number of primum times a day) negate the damage as if it had missed him
              --> Now this was a bit hard, for I didnt want to make it too powerfull (and ignoring that there is a just bruise embed) but also didnt want to make it useless most of the time.
              My balance to it was make it limeted to damage that would come from a firearm at the circumstance that would lead to the character death. (and hence no aether cost).
              I would really enjoy some thoughts if you feel this is weak. perhaps giving also a secondary effect?

              3rd Embed: Strike First

              2nd. Interlock: Hope it Hurts (CB + SF)
              Stamina + Firearms
              - The demon may make his first attack on the combat using a firearm, as if he had aimed for 3 turns prior to it, (giving him effectively a +3 Bonus)
              -- I thought about balancing this a bit by making: Each time this embed is used on the same day, the bonus drops by 1, to a minimum of a +1.
              I don't know if this limitation is at all necessary, since it is already restricted to his first bullet (and would also be the first damage taken by the opponent in the combat).
              please tell me what you think

              4th Embed: Shatter

              3rd. Interlock: First Bullet Heart Exploding Technique
              Wits + Firearms - Stamina
              - For the first bullet the demon shoots in the combat, it gains route quality, rerolling all failed dice.
              -- However: If the targets dies, the demon must make a compromise roll at -3.
              This was also a bit hard, and I ended up getting the example from the demon's storyteller's guide. I think that this embed combined with the other ones (and indeed I will allow them to be used in sequence) may lead to a circumstance where even a stamina 3 opponent might fall. (I know this is completely anecdotal, since I have no idea on the math behind thinking about dices and success chance, but out of 10 rolls with a 3L gun, I managed to take around 12-14 damage, when a 16 would in fact making it a killing blow, so perhaps with a bit of more luck ...).
              Now the thing about this is that choosing to use the "technique" is, as implied, at the very least assuming the risk of having a good chance of killing someone, if not purely intentional. Hence i think it should be treated the same way as a death blow, requiring the compromise roll.
              I still however think this is ok. If anyone think this may be too powerful please help me balancing.

              I hope to count with fellow forum-ers to help ^^

                      I think that Cipher is far, far too focused around gunplay. Now, I assume you as an ST and this player want to get into a lot of fights, and that's cool.

                      If they want to quickly win fights (Down and Dirty rules), they don't need Interlock 3. Merciless Gunman has them covered. And if we combine Merciless Gunman with this Cipher you've got rote-quality, 8-again shots, some with a nice aiming bonus. That's really powerful... but it's going to slow your game down a ton because you have to activate these powers. Before/during every fight, this player isn't reflexively being awesome with one power and a quick dice roll, they're using three together. That's going to eat into the pace and slow down fights hugely.... and if the fight isn't important, hey, Down and Dirty combat rules and Merciless Gunman have got you covered already.

                      There are also issues that, in a fight, this demon will dominate entirely and, presumably, put your other PCs to shame. Your other PC is currently a social powerhouse. Niche protection is good, but you need to be sure your players have similar expectations going in - you don't want one player unable to help much in a fight while the other is bored/unable to do much when socialising. Unless that's what they want, of course.

                      Based on your reasoning, the 3rd Interlock doesn't need the compromise roll - death blows don't necessarily cause compromise in DtD, it's just assumed that killing people is out of character for most Covers. Plus,the target of this Interlock may not be human, which may or may not cause compromise. If killing is going to cause a compromise (which, to be fair, it normally would), that's nothing to do with the Interlock itself.

                      The 3rd Interlock could also just kill mortal targets outright. I actually don't consider this overpowered - it's not much different to Knockout Punch or Murder by Improbability, after all. Worse, in many ways since you have to physically shoot them and, well, you're at the scene of the murder... A shot that auto-kills a mortal being and provides some sort of bonus against supernatural ones works for me - ignoring armour or Defense (since some supernatural beings can apply defense to firearms), perhaps. I'd keep a killshot power, but make it the first Interlock. Your current first Interlock is too limited - how often do you throw attacks at your players that would instantly kill them?

                      Also, I'd think about this Cipher from a character perspective. They're a paladin, you say. To me, this Interlock could evolve from powers based around being a Destroyer (what the demon was) to ones that are more about what they're becoming... Powers focused around justice, perhaps. You can still keep these a bit nasty, if twisted is a big part of the character. There's a lot of potential for him to curse/mark targets by making Interlocks that use Embeds like Everybody Knows or Unperson, for example.

                              Hello once more ^^

                              So now I am posting the cipher of my third and last player.
                              He is a Guardian Inquisitor, and has the "super servant" concept (he is a butler and takes care of his protégé. A lot like black butler but without the yaoi - unfortunately).
                              His character is quite oriented towards doing things "well" (his embeds are efficiency, momentum, right tools;right job).

                              Taking into account everything I came up with this:

                              1st. Embed: Download Knowledge (chosen by the player)

                              2nd. Embed: Cause and Effect

                              1st. Interlock: If I only Knew before (DK + CE)
                              Wits + Skill
                              - The demon gains knowledge of the result of a determined action before doing it.
                              --> The player will know if his action will fail or succeed (by rolling the action) and will be allowed to act or not.
                              --> If he decides to take no action during at that turn, focusing on his vision of what will happen, he can act on it in the next turn by: If it would have failed he can add his Primum as Bonus to the action (since knowing why it didnt work would give him more clarity in trying it now) or If the action was a success he turn it to an exceptional success.
                              * Using it to become an exceptional success more than once per scene will cause compromise roll.
                              Now as I am building this, and thinking about the exceptional success I thought I should balance it somehow, by either asking for compromise roll (since its shifting probability and causation) or asking for an Aether Cost. However, I felt that the Aether Cost would not truly bring balance especially once the characters get access to an aether source. (it would be very small price to pay 1 aether for an exceptional success, unless I asked to pay in Aether the difference between the success obtained to the exceptional one: like if he had only 1 success he would have to pay 4 Aether to make it an exceptional success).

                              3rd. Embed: Shift Consequence

                              2nd. Interlock: Try It like This (CE + SC)
                              Manipulation + Occult - Composure
                              - The demon may force the shift to the cause of action taken by another.
                              Ex: An NPC may try to attack the demon (or an ally).The demon may decide that instead of a Strength + Brawl roll, it should be taken with another skill of the same family, such as Strength + Survival. The success would apply damage as normal.
                              *Using this embed more than primum times per scene causes compromise roll.

                              4th Embed: Victory at any Price

                              3rd. Interlock: Win-Win Situation (SC + VP)
                              Wits + Occult
                              (Cost: 1 Aether)
                              -The demon may copy the number of success of an action in the scene (one time per action) to an action of his own, not needing to roll dices.
                              - On a combat situation he can copy the success of any action in the previous round.

                              I would very much like how you guys feel about balance to these interlocks. Although I confess I am please on how they turned out.

                              As I have said, here now I am posting the Final Secret of these characters:


                              1st: The Master Manipulator (The Psychologist Guardian-Tempter).
                              She believes that Hell is place and she needs to find it.
                              Considering her orientation towards social maneuvering and elements of the my Narrative (which is quite long) I came to this:

                              "Finding hell is something one can not do alone. Humans are there to help you: willingly/knowingly or not. To open the door of Hell you only need to find the Human who has the key".
                              There is indeed in my narrative a human which my lead the characters to a pocket reality which a demon could take control (and well.. making it its personal hell). Hence I think it would all fit very well.

                              2nd: The Righteous Gunslinger (Cop Destroyer-Saboteur)
                              He believes that Hell is a place where the Machine is absent, and he may create his own by thwarting the Machine plans/infrastructure in a region.
                              Considering his orientation towards combat and his gun I came to this:

                              "The Machine Created you as a Weapon of Destruction. You have now created a weapon of your own:Take it now and go and unleash Hell against the Machine before her Kingdom comes to you".

                              3rd: The Super Servant (Butler Guardian-Inquisitor)
                              The player said that Hell is not really something his character puts much thought into, being mainly focused with what he still considers to be his mission "protect his master". The Machine is nuisance (even if a major one) that may hinder him of completing this mission. I came to the conclusion that his hell is perhaps the most easily achievable of all the characters: he will be "happy" and satisfied by fully satisfying his master needs and seeing him thrive.

                              My Final secret for him them is:

                              "There is a right way of doing things and there is a better way of doing things: Do it better and Never Fail the one who needs you most, satisfaction of the job well done will be your own reward".

                              If any one would like to comment on these final secrets feel free to tell me what you think! I will love to hear, specially from players, if this would be something that they'd be glad if their ST gave to them!.

                              Thanks for all the help!
