    My opinion on nWoD stuff is entirely unoriginal: I encourage people to treat the fluff as a toolkit to be used as much or as little as you want for your individual games. I like hammering cosmologies together, which is why for my Demon game the God-Machine's origins very directly involve the Exarchs and the Principle and indirectly reference the gods of the Shadow and the Others from Arcadia, but I also have a cosmology for games that only involve Vampire/Geist/Promethean/Mummy with a heavy emphasis on the Inferno supplement. As much as cosmology wank is fun - and it's so much fun - the only time I think you're obligated to have any mention of the God-Machine around is when you're playing Demon*. Otherwise, it's all up in the air for each campaign.
    I'm the exact opposite I think an over reliance on perceived "judeo/christian" myth is boring.
    Only exception was the 7 virtues / vices and I am sad it is gone in Vampire.
    After all what most people think they know of Hell and Demons is apocryphal anyway.

    From what I know of the GMC is a fresh quasi chthonic take on the whole matter.
    Same with the Principle and the Quashmallen, very mysterious.
    If the old testament god shows up at all in my world it will be as the demiurge.

    As the ex priest cop state in prophecy states, when angels show up it is always bad news.

        Worth noting: at no point did I say you should rely on the Judeo-Christian God if you don't use the God-Machine; on the contrary, besides the role He plays in modern religion I usually leave the idea of "God" alone.

        Running oVamp or oDemon is a different story entirely, but that's not what we're talking about anyways.

        Edit: In fact, the closest I come to touching on the presence of God is in the grand unified backstory I am working on for a lot of my home games, and here it is: at the dawn of reality, many, many iterations ago, the God-Machine and the Principle were one force dedicated to protecting and enforcing causality. -Someone or something- might have needed to create such a mechanism before the dawn of time itself, but there is no other evidence of such a force existing or being able to exist before reality itself. -If- it existed, it might be God. Or not.
