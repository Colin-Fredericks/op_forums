                                The line that speaks to me the most is changeling. To deal with your entire world being changed forever while you still wear that mask and pretend that nothing has changed. To find you are not the only one with such a mask and that they too draw inspiration from the emotions of those that surround them. For your life to follow the path dictated by the laws of narrative for life is a long series of impossible coincidences. But what speaks to me more than the 'survivor' aspect is the fact that no matter the depths you have seen or fallen to you can rise to heights you never could have dreamt of if you had not been as low as you were.

                                Demon speaks to me in the same vein as changing with cover but beyond that it doesn't reach the depth that changeling resonates with me.

                                Mage resonates with how I do doubt the world's truth around me at times but I know that if I as a person were to be a mage I would descend and become one of the mad so though many things about its themes and presentation speaks to me it speaks more to my flaws that I know how to manage unlike changeling which speaks to the strengths that have allowed me to persevere.

                                                    Now that i've thought more about it there is no game in its entirety that 'speak' more to me. Instead i latch onto parts of every game. I've already spoken about mage, demon and changeling but there are elements in all the other game line that speaks to me just as much.

                                                    In vampire, its in the decay both moral and social that accompany the nights of unlife. Although there are great immediate change, its more about the weight of time and the questionnable actions you take on a nightly basis to survive.

                                                    In werewolf, i really like the clarity of purpose behing the hunt. Its almost a tautological truth in the sense that 'the wolf must hunt' because 'the wolf must hunt'. Now human minds will find way to complicate it (and thats cool too, another thing to explore). But, that simple purpose will always be there.

                                                    In prometheans, i also like the concept of purpose, but here is not an immediate purpose like in werewolf. Here we have a journey on the long term, exhausting and frustrating, but still clear in the distance.

                                                    Geist, is harder, for me its less the second chance relating with the geist and more the burden that comes with the knowledge that came with dying and the responsibilities (to be followed or avoided) that came in dealing with both the alive and the dead.

                                                    Finally, with mummy i latch onto the concept of witnessing and being part of history in a way that is more exemplify than in fellow immortals like vampire. To see history, to shape it in small ways and seeing parts of the past in the present.
