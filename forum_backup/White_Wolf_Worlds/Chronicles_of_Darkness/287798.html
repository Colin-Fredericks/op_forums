    On odd RP question

    10-20-2014, 01:24 PM
    I'm playing a mixed template game where I'm playing as a male Mage that is about to go out on a date with a female Werewolf Alpha. After having a temporary bout of Narcissistic God Complex from a spell that went horribly wrong, she grabbed him and made out with him in order to get him to stop recklessly attacking a monster of unknown power; afterwards they both admitted that they liked it and he apologized for his behavior when he sobered up. I know that the Werewolf player is partially amused, partially toying with making my character uncomfortable as payback for him being a jerk the other night.

    What social snafoos should a mage character be wary of when dating a werewolf? Is this a pairing that anyone else has had shipped before?

      Depending on their tribe, they have different vows they have to abide by. Fire-Touched don't like being lied to and for the Storm Lords, it's 'Allow no one to witness or tend to your weakness.' So getting intimate with one can be awkward depending upon their views of weakness.

      Plus there's the whole Death Rage thing. Betrayal by loved ones is something that can bring it out in werewolves of any Harmony.

      Silver's a big no no, too.

          Does the mage in question have any understanding of the Spirit Arcanum?

              Our ST isn't applying Lunacy to mages I believe but my composure is 4 and I can bring up a 4 dot mental shield. I don't have any dots in spirit but my character is also a reasonably liberal hunter in that he hangs out with other hunters that focus less on the "shoot on sight" mentality and more on the "find the monsters that are actually killing people" point of view. Through his time hunting malicious spirits on occasion, he knows more about the subject than the average non-spirit mage. I have silver bullets on me usually but I'll leave them at home...

              Especially because the date itself is a way of making up for his asshatery the other night while he was struck with narcissism, my mage is fully willing to follow her lead, although in normal circumstances they will probably butt heads. Part of the reason this whole thing started out is the Promethean in the group started teasing both of us saying our arguments and fights look awfully close to flirting. We are the two most alpha personalities in the game (me because I'm a time/mind mage that keeps the city safe and usually thinks that everyone else is way too naiive and reckless; her because she is an actual werewolf alpha). Our similar personalities seem to be what are drawing our characters together but at the same time it could lead to some terrifying fights in the future too...

              As for her tribe, I'm not sure. I know she is Forsaken and I think she is a Boneshadow of the New Moon. For death rage, I know for a fact that some of my hanging time spells when activated will let me easily outrun her and dodge like a pro. Plus Telepathic Control and Emotional Urging: Calm should help a bit. Still, I don't want to take any chances though...Gourou is scary stuff!

              Anyway, I hope the clarifying information can better help viewers to give accurate advice for the situation .
