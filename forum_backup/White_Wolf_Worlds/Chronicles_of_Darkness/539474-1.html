                No, you had it right. An appropriate insult would be throwing a drink in someone's face. If you are strictly talking about words then go with the "fighting words" standard, "those that by their very utterance inflict injury or tend to incite an immediate breach of the peace." The insult has to actually provoke the vampire, and would even if there was no beast. The beast steps in, or tries, to deal with the situation. The beast affects the reaction to stimuli, not the perception of it. The beast doesn't make a vampire get angry easier, it makes the vampire react worse, when the vampire would normally deal with stress like anger, fear, or hunger.
