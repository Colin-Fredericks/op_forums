    Any powers, abilities, skills, other, etc you think should be added?

    03-05-2014, 11:04 PM
    Just curious, what powers, abilities, skills, etc you would like to see added or changed in the books or come out in a supplement.
    Minor Changes.

    Possessions - Just would like a slight wording change to work on animals as well as people, and also allowing use of the animal traits/skills if you possess animals.

    Demon House - to work on inanimate objects (anything from jewelry to vehicles), not just buildings.

    Hellfire - this was mentioned on another thread, the name seems weird to have that tied to just projectiles/guns.

    Power I would like to see added

    Creature Cover - I think it would actually be kind of cool to have a cover that is a creature, like an animal, or a swarm (bees, birds, spiders..etc.). That is very much in theme with works. Like a whole bunch of insects coming together to to form a demon, or like the scene with Christopher Walken when he gets shot at and turns into a flock of birds on the movie 'the prophecy'. Instead of making a soul pact, maybe a demon just assimilates the creature(s) into him/her self.

    Cryptid/stigmata Cover - This is more for hellhound use, if you use hellhound on an animal and want to keep it. A power to revert/cover the animal back to its original from (cover) that it can transform into if you need or want it too. It just would be too hard to explain why your dog has cybernetic legs, venomous teeth and glowing eyes. This could also work on stigmatics and give them a reason for them to follow demons. Demons would have to spend aether every X time to cover up their glitches.

    Teleportation - Thinking of supernatural and how Cas could teleport to anywhere and take someone with them. Sure, we have behind the curtain, but it is limited to infrastructures. I like the idea of popping up behind or in front of someone and freaking them out. I would just limit that the demon/person could only teleport to places they have been and/or work embeds/exploits like tag and release where you can pinpoint a location.

    Call anyone anywhere - Ever watch the original japanese 'one missed call' or how the machine will call the characters on person of interest? This would be an awesome ability, to have the demon call anybody (the demon has made physical contact with) anywhere at any time. If a character is near any phone, that phone will ring, if they ignore it and move away, the call will jump from phone to phone they are near and ring. If they are not near a phone, then their cell phone (or just a random cell phone) will magically appear in their pocket. If they break the phone, the phone will STILL ring and follow them around. If they are near a kids toy phone, it will ring. It also works on other technology, like a computer might get email from the demon, or a message will pop up on an atm machine the user is reading, a radio will suddenly have the demon talking through it..etc. I think this would be a cool power to have.

    A power that is something between inflict stigmata and hellhound. Something that turns a person into a more powerful form of stigmata/cryptic, but binds their will to the demon. Might have a heavy cost, thinking of a demonized version of ghouls which stigmata does not come across as.

    Hear the call - people send conscious and unconscious prayers, summonings, wishes and requests to the universe for a variety of reasons. A family member silently prays over a hospital bed to god (other) for this person to be healed, a group of teens jokingly make a pentagram to summon a demon, a gambler whispers under his breath “please, please, just this once I would do anything..”, some hippies tries to send telepathic messages to the universe using their minds to touch another mind out there..etc. This power would basically be like turning on a radio and listen in to all this call outs goin on. It could allow the demon to know who is calling, where they are located, and what they want. A quick way to find people and make pacts with.

    I am the cover - this power could allow the demon to have access to his covers personality, memories and skills, however there is a risk of being possessed by the cover and have it take over or overwrite the demons personality..

                      Just thought of another one.

                      Assimilation - this is a power if someone wants a more horror aspect of the game. Basically this is inspired by the movies the thing (80’s and later remakes,) the game prototype and some anime were demons eat humans to take over their lives. A demon can absorb living or recently dead people, animals, (insects, plants?) and become them. This would not need a contract and would give the characters stats, abilities, skills to use. However, the flaw is that it removes any artificial alterations like tattoos, piercings, even repairs/regrows scars and miss limbs so it is obvious something is wrong unless the demon spends more power to keep those. There is a lot of risk, one, the cover starts to degrade with X amount of time. The only way to keep this cover is collect organic mass every so often. This does not mean killing someone, they can buy a lot of meat from the supermarket, or they can go the vampire route and drink blood from victims(make sure to have a power to erase memories)/blood bank, or if you want to get creative they can work at a hospital and collect discarded organs, or a morgue and feed off the dead (yuk). The second risk is that any natural flaws like a medical condition is still with the character, so if someone has a bad heart, they might have heart attack, anything removed with an operation like a bad organ is brought back, any disease..etc is with that cover. The finale third risk is that the character is so into the cover that the demon might get overwritten or possessed by the cover. A lot of potential.
