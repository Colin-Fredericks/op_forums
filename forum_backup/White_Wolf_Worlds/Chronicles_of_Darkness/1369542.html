      Anyways, not inviolable, but from a cosmological point of view the Powers That Be are going to have a decent argument on why A) Beast's harm is any more or less relevant than literally anything else in the world, B) how they reconcile with dealing with the harm of a Beast considering said harm is an extension of an entity that is complexly human-and-not-human, mass-humanity-while-still -an-individual, and a breathing icon in the flesh above all of that without then going on just basically saying humanity needs to be rewritten.

      So maaaaaybe? Certainly it's an easy enough comparison to ignore potential relationships, but part of Princess's point is that there is a Fundamental Good that goes above and beyond the human constructions in the Bright and Primordial Dreams (Temenos) and resides in The Mother's Land(Anima Mundi) at least, and I have trouble seeing how that fundament would end drawing a particular distinction that isn't rendered meaningless in it's articulation.

                              Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                              What fruit juice are you adding to whiskey?

                              More importantly, why didn't that occur to me.

                              Citrus juice, normally. Lemon juice of note. Sometimes orange juice. Lime juice potentially, depending on the cocktail recipe.

                              Now, there are sour mixes you can buy to make a "whiskey sour". There are recipes you can find for a whiskey sour as well that use lemon juice and have you need to make a simple syrup (very easy to do).

                              If you wanna get CRAZY with it, a traditional whiskey sour uses whiskey (2 ounces, bourbon or rye depending on your preferences), about an ounce of lemon juice, and maybe half an ounce of simple syrup (depends on how heavy you make your simple syrup (like 1 cup sugar to 1 cup or water or 2 cups sugar to 1 cup of water) and how sweet you like your drinks), and..... one egg white. You need to dry shake (no ice) the egg white (either with the other ingredients or not, your choice) in order to break up the protein of the egg white. Then shake it again with ice.

                              For some comparison, a real daiquiri is basically 2 ounces rum, 1 ounce lime juice, 1 ounce simple syrup, shaken with ice. And is absolutely delicious. You can basically play around with these ratios for your taste and with the ingredients for different flavors or different alcohols to find your preference.

                              "How To Drink" is a really good YouTube channel....
