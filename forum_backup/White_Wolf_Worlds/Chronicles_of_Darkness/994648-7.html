        Hmm, the exclusion of mages rules out my first and most preferred choice, so let's see: immortality (or at least strong potential for it), empowerment (in the sense of self-agency and mastery of my environment; mundane power interests me little, even if mind control abilities are just too useful to neglect), potential for self-development, and mystical awareness would be my main selection criteria. Their mortality rules out being a Werewolf or a Sin-Eater, even if I greatly appreciate their power and insights. Changelings and Demons have remarkable abilities and potential for immortality, but I find being eternally on the run too bothersome a lifestyle, if an adequate alternative is available. However I'd likely find the Durance or giving up my old life to a Fetch not that much of a bother, since I'm resistant to pain and abuse and you can always set up a new life and find new friends and romantic partners elsewhere. I'm selfish and callous enough not to mind a predatory lifestyle to survive, especially since I can try and minimize the damage or focus on preying on those I find worthy of victimization.

        So I'd be OK with being a Daeva Vampire with a bloodline that bestows Dominate as its specialty Discipline (perhaps the Toreador but only if I can focus their artistic obsession on something like martial arts or software development), a generalist effort to develop Blood Potency (up to the point feeding restrictions can be effectively managed with Willpower expenditure, Unnatural Affinity, Blood Price, Willful Vitae, and Sanguinary Invigoration) and most physical and mental Disciplines (espeially Celerity, Dominate, Majesty, Nightmare, Protean, Resilience, and Vigor) regardless of Clan specialization, a main membership in the Ordo Dracul (and a serious effort to master the Coils of the Ascendant, the Wyrm, Zirnitra, and Ziva, and develop a few psychic powers), and a secondary investment in the Circle of the Crone and Cruac. The focus of the Coil of the Voivode on enforcing servitude does not appeal so much to me, although its uses to resist the blood bond and bypass feeding restrictions make its pursuit worthwhile. I'd likely take as little involvement as possible in the political games of the Kindred, only bothering to establish the resources strictly necessary to sustain my Requiem, and I'd focus my immortality on exploring the supernatural, becoming a polymath, and pursuing trans-vampirism. I'd settle for a low-Humanity lifestyle until I can master the Coil of Ziva and use Banes to make myself immune to as many breaking points as possible. I'd keep the blush of life active as much as possible (and pretty much all the time once I master the Coil of the Ascendant) unless I'm starved for Vitae.

        Alternatively, I'd also be OK with being a Beast (Eshmaki, Anakim, or Ugallu are all interesting choices) with an Hunger for Prey or Punishment and a focus on developing my Lair, mastering almost all Atavisms (only Infestation really freaks me out) regardless of Family specialization, pursuing the Incarnate Inheritance, becoming a polymath, and exploring the supernatural. I'd settle for a low-Satiety lifestyle, at least until I can master the Incarnate Inheritance. I'm not sure of which Lesson(s) I'd care to teach, since several appeal to me. I'm a bit less interested in Nightmares, but they are certainly useful enough I'd strive to develop most of them for practical reasons.

        My third preferred choice would be a Purified, especially if I can develop and combine spirit and psychic powers. For the record, if the Awakened were available as an option, my first choice would be becoming an Obrimos Mage with a Legacy that provides Life or Mind as a Ruling Arcanum, a main membership in the Mysterium or the Adamantine Arrow and secondary involvement in the other Order, and a focus on developing Gnosis, Forces, Life, Mind, Fate, and Prime. Probably Death too but only insofar it gets useful to achieve immortality or protect oneself; I have zero interest in necromancy. I'd settle for a low-Wisdom lifestyle and eagerly pursue becoming a Lich, and eventually an Archmage.

        In the case I change my mind and deem being a Changeling an acceptable option despite the threat of the True Fae, I'd choose becoming some Dual-Kith hybrid of any two of Hunterheart, Earthbone, Fireheart, Gristlegrinder, or Stonebones. I'd develop ties with the Spring Court, the Summer Court, or both. I'd take a generalist approach to Contracts, since several appeal to me. I'd pursue immortality by Goblin Contracts and building up Wyrd just short of the point one is forced to enter Arcadia.

        Prometheans are Ugh, No, Just No for too many reasons (I do not mind a nomadic lifestyle, but their existence is endless punishment with the only respite being a pontless return to being a puny mortal) and mummies usually look forward to an eternity of being slaves and experiencing an endless cycle of loss. In comparison, the Coils or the Incarnate Inheritance offer a respite from most drawbacks of the vampire or beast condition I'd find meaningful.

                                Easy. Mage.

                                Yeah, I know that is against the rules. I picked a Mage because I would be a Mage and Mages famously overcome 'rules.'

                                What, you need a spell or something... fine, watch as I call upon the Supernal Arcane of Logic.

                                1: The title is 'You as a Player Character' so I am a Player Character.
                                2: Player Characters require Players or they would be Non-Player Characters. (If Player Character then Player)
                                3: I have a Player. (modus ponens of 1 and 2)
                                4: Anyone would pick a Mage if the game premise was 'What Splat would you want to be?' (If Player, select Mage)
                                5: 'My Player' would be included in the set 'Anyone'. (My Player is Player)
                                6: My Player would pick a Mage. (modus ponens of 4 and 5)
                                7: If My Player would pick a Mage, then I would be a Mage.
                                :: I am a Mage. (modus ponens hat-trick of 6 and 7)

                                Paradox inducing Objection: But the rules say you can't pick a Mage.

                                Paradox containing rebuttal: Yes, but I didn't pick; my player did.

                                So as you can see, logically I must be Mage.
