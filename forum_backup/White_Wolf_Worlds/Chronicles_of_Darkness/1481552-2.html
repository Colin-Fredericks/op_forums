    I appreciate how the Poisoner is physically weak and doesn't have any kind of naturally built poison resistance. That means that she is vulnerable to a ironic death by the same method.

    The Questing Compass being a combination of Jack Sparrow's compass and the Mao Kun map is pretty cool. Its reliable enough in seeking out the supernatural to foster a dependency on it and it can also be very useful when looking for mundane resources. Being able to push the user to be obsessive and rush into danger can be quite a powerful and subtle curse, yet seemingly manageable enough to tempt those that think they can handle it.

Being its owner can't be pleasant, its akin to having the Number Two Headband, it will endlessly draw danger to you, which means either you evolve quickly or you will be annihilated. However, I imagine those that would hunt after it would also be connoisseurs of occult relics, so it works out perfectly for this slasher.

        The Revenant
        The killer known as a Revenant was once an ordinary person; an artist that made a good living as a painter and graphic designer. That life ended with the death of his partner, murdered by a serial killer. With the authorities seemingly helpless, he took it upon himself to avenge his lover, dedicating every resource and several years to becoming a vigilante determined to enact vengeance upon all such killers, even joining a cell of hunters, and learning that monstrous killers were only the beginning of world’s horrors. With each hunt, they became more violent, more relentless, and more determined to destroy their quarry. During a disastrous mission, he was left for dead at the hands of a supernaturally potent killer. But he rose from the dead three days later, and has began his mission in earnest.
        The Revenant focuses his wrath on criminals, killers, and slashers, but has killed law enforcement agents and hunters. Anyone that has taken the life of an innocent is a fair target. He see’s himself as an avenger of the “Innocent”, defining innocence as those that have lived lives of peace and have committed no acts of violence. He has been known to “warn” people that victimize such innocents, but haven't crossed the line to murder. He is able to tell if a person is “guilty” with a glance, and can make this targets experience crippling guilt and anguish. The Revenant is also seemingly immortal, he as been reported dead multiple times, but always re-appears sometime later to continue his mission.
        The Revenant is a handsome, athletic man in his early thirties. His fair skin is as smooth and pale as porcelain and he wears his dark hair long. He dresses in rugged, dark clothing that allow movement and flexibility. He uses whatever weapons are at hand, but prefers to gun down his victims with a heavy revolver.


        Undertaking: Legend; Virtue: Focused; Vice: Merciless
        Aspiration: Vengeance against those that have spilled innocent blood
        Tell: The Revenant’s victims are all those that have taken lives, whether by deliberately or by accident

        Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 4, Resolve 3
        Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 5, Stamina 3
        Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 2, Composure 3

        Skills: Academics 2, Athletics 4 (Pursuit), Brawl 4 (Armed Opponents), Computer 2, Crafts 2, Expression 3 (Illustration), Firearms 3 (Revolver), Intimidate 3, Investigate 3 (Killers), Occult 2, Stealth 3, Streetwise 2, Weaponry 2 (Improvised)
        Merits: Damnable Certainty, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Fast Reflexes 2, Hands of a Killer, Professional Training 2 (Artist; Crafts, Expression), Style: Martial Arts 4, Style: Parkour 2, Striking Looks 1 (Handsome)

        Integrity: 0, Potency: 3, Willpower: 9
        Size: 5, Initiative: 10, Armor: 1/0 (Reinforced Clothing), Defense: 8
        Speed: 14
        Health: 8

        Weapons/Attacks
        Unarmed [+0B melee, 8 dice]
        Knife [+0L melee;-1 Initiative; 6 dice]
        Heavy Revolver [+2L ranged; -2 Initiative; 9 dice]

        Talent: Living Legend (Spilled Innocent Blood) Narrative Chains (Cannot harm an “Innocent”)
        Dread Powers: Immortal* (Three Days), Know Soul, Madness and Terror

        Living Legend: Once per scene, when someone fulfills one of the tenets of the Legend’s “rules,” the player can roll the slasher’s chosen Attribute + Occult. Successes on this roll may be applied on one of the following: restoring spent Willpower, healing damage (one success for a bashing wound, two for lethal, three for aggravated), or applying the bonuses to rolls in pursuit of their goal. After this has been activated, successes that haven’t been spent are discarded. They may not be banked to use in a following scene.

        Narrative Chains: The Legend gains a unique and specific frailty enforced by the nature of the tales surrounding them. Additionally, characters investigating the scourge’s Tell gain an exceptional success on three successes rather than five.

        Immortal: The creature isn’t bound by mortal laws of life and death. It’s not indestructible, per se, but if it’s killed by anything other than its Bane, it just comes back in a later scene like nothing happened. Even if it’s destroyed with its Bane, it’s never really gone — some accursed ritual, astrological conjunction, or freak occurrence can always bring the creature back for a sequel story.
        [Chronicles of Darkness, pg. 145]

            Rictus Grin
            The killer known as “Rictus Grin” appeared first on cryptic video clips posted on internet and dark web forums dedicated to true crime and serial killers. The videos depict a figure wearing a grinning mask, often creeping out of closets or looming over people while they sleep. The authenticity of the clips became a matter of debate and many are still certain the clips are to promote an upcoming horror film of some sort. Some of the videos leaked to the local police and FBI, who discovered that the details of the clips matched with several unsolved murder cases. Since then, new clips regularly appear every few months, each one followed by reports of murder. The person wearing the mask of Rictus Grin has left next to no clues about their true identity, baffling and frustrating law enforcement. Even more upsetting, Rictus Grin seems to have developed a fan following that collects his clips and waits for the next “content drop”.
            Rictus Grin is a tall, broad shouldered man sporting a black long coat, buttoned to the throat. He wears an apparently hand-crafted white leather mask styled with dark eyes and a macabre grin. Anyone other details about the person behind the mask are still unknown.


            Undertaking: Brute; Virtue: Methodical; Vice: Theatrical
            Aspiration: To become (in)famous.
            Tell: Can only kill as “Rictus Grin”

            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 2, Resolve 3
            Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 3, Stamina 4
            Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 2, Composure 3

            Skills: Athletics 3, Brawl 3, Computers 2, Crafts 2, Expression 2 (Mime), Firearms 2 (Silenced), Intimidate 3(Menacing), Investigate 3 (Potential Victims), Larceny 3 (Breaking and Entering), Stealth 4, Weaponry 3 (Blades)
            Merits: Allies 3 (Wealthy Patrons), Cover Up, Danger Sense, Fame 1 (Online), Iron Skin 2, Lucky Charm (Mask), Resources 2, Style: Martial Arts 3, Tell-Tale Murder, Trained Observer

            Integrity: 0, Willpower: 6
            Size: 5, Initiative: 6, Armor: 1/0 [Reinforced Clothing, 1 vs. Bashing], Defense: 5
            Speed: 13
            Health: 9

            Weapons/Attacks
            Unarmed [+0B melee, 8 dice]
            Machete [+2L melee; -2 Initiative; 9 dice]
            Silenced Light Pistol [+1L ranged, 6 dice]

            Talent: Unstoppable; Frailty: Tunnel Vision

            Unstoppable: A Brute never suffers wound penalties for any reason, and automatically passes any roll to remain conscious due to damage or pain.

            Tunnel Vision: A Brute suffers a −3 penalty to all Perception rolls, and to rolls to avoid being surprised in combat.

                    I have used Network in a few storylines as a compact of Slashers that the PCs were trying to dismantle. Several of the Slashers presented here were developed for that campaign. I especially had fun with the Gourmet as an evil cannibalistic Anthony Bourdain! I also featured Dark Father, Kill Doll and Rictus Grin as the primary antagonists.

                    The Righteous Man
                    Wyatt James was once a member of an organization called “The Church of Revelation”. He and his wife joined the organization as a way to cope with trauma he experienced while serving in the military. The Church of Revelation was an apocalyptic cult, preparing for the impending End of Days. After moving to the church’s sprawling compound, Wyatt became disenchanted (and frightened) by the increasingly radical tone of the churches sermons. When he and his wife tried to escape, they met with armed resistance, and she was killed in the attempt.
                    Over the course of a year, Wyatt murdered the leaders of the organization, and burned its compound to ashes.
                    Wyatt continues his crusade, rooting and extinguishing cults and organizations that he deems “false” or “evil”. He tries to limit the carnage to leaders and organizers, but more than a few lay members have been caught in the crossfire, which only compels Wyatt to make sure “their sacrifice was not in vain”.
                    Wyatt James is a solid, athletic man in his early thirties with dark hair and brown eyes. He crops his hair close and favors rugged practical clothing.

                    Undertaking: Avenger; Virtue: Faithful; Vice: Unforgiving
                    Aspiration: To destroy those that pervert or corrupt the Gospel
                    Tell: He targets professed “Christians” or other "People of Faith" that are hypocrites or fall short of his interpretation of the scripture.

                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 3, Resolve 5
                    Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 4, Stamina 4
                    Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 3, Composure 3

                    Skills: Academics 1(Bible), Athletics 2, Brawl 3, Crafts 2(Explosives), Drive 2, Firearms 4(Pistols), Intimidate 2, Investigate 3, Occult 1(Cults), Stealth 2(Surveillance), Subterfuge 2, Survival 2(Doomsday Prep), Weaponry 3
                    Merits: Defensive Combat (Brawl), Iron Stamina 2, Iron Will, Professional Training 3 (Soldier; Crafts, Firearms, Survival), Style: Close Quarters Combat 2, Style: Firefight 2

                    Integrity: 0, Willpower: 8
                    Size: 5, Initiative: 7, Armor: 1/3 [Kevlar Vest], Defense: 6
                    Speed: 13
                    Health: 9

                    Weapons/Attacks
                    Unarmed [+0B melee, 7 dice]
                    Knife [+0L melee;-1 Initiative; 7 dice]
                    Heavy Pistol [+2L ranged; -2 Initiative; 9 dice; 9 again]

                    Talent: Working the Room; Frailty: Nothing but the Mission

                    Working the Room: Avengers might try to kill their victims one by one, but sooner or later they wind up with a roomful of people that require their attention. The rules for Defense vs. multiple opponents do not apply to Avengers. No matter how many times in a turn an Avenger is attacked, apply his full Defense to all incoming attacks.

                    Nothing but the Mission: Most slashers are obsessive, but Avengers take it to a whole new level. The Avenger has the Obsession Condition in regards to pursuing their chosen targets. When faced with the choice between pursuing a target and taking some other course of action, the Storyteller’s must roll Resolve + Composure. If this roll succeeds, the Avenger can choose to let the target go (for now). If the roll fails, the Avenger remains focused on his mission and ignores the other situation.

                        The Road Monster
                        “The Road Monster” has been terrorizing back roads and lonely stretches of highway for years. Survivors recall being forced off the road by a large, rusted truck with blinding lights and seemingly driven by a maniac. Some get away with just a dreadful fright. Others suffer fatal collisions or are run down like dogs. The owner of the vehicle is a man named John Gibson (most locals just call him “Gib”). Always a loner and an outsider, he keeps largely to himself, rarely venturing away from the property he inherited from his deceased parents. He has turned the land into an auto-shop, impound lot, and scrap yard. He makes a good living as a mechanic and tow truck operator, and most of the locals that do business with him regard him as a strange man who does good enough work to overlook his peculiarities.
                        But on certain nights, he crawls into the cab of a custom truck he has named “Iron Dog” and goes patrolling for victims. He takes special delight in tracking down those that have slighted him in some way, and making them suffer.
                        Gib is a tall, lean Caucasian man in his late thirties. His teeth are crooked, his skin is smudged with dirt and motor oil, and his face has a five o’clock shadow no amount of shaving can banish. His brown hair is over-long and stuffed beneath a baseball cap, pulled low over his eyes. He dresses in a dirty dark blue coverall when working (and he always seems to be working). Despite his appearance and occupation, Gib is quite intelligent, and belittling him is a sure way to end up beneath the wheels of his truck.


                        Undertaking: Undesirable; Virtue: Resourceful; Vice: Resentful
                        Aspiration: To Rule the Roads
                        Tell: The Road Monster kills with his vehicles, and especially his custom truck “Iron Dog”

                        Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 5, Resolve 4
                        Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3
                        Social Attributes: Presence 2, Manipulation 3, Composure 3

                        Skills: Animal Ken 2, Athletics 2 (Strong Back), Computers 1, Crafts 4 (Automobiles, Sabotage), Brawl 2, Drive 4 (Offensive, Truck), Firearms 2, Intimidate 2, Investigation 3, Larceny 2, Persuasion 2 (Haggling), Science 1, Subterfuge 2 (Alibi), Survival 1, Weaponry 3 (Clubs)
                        Merits: Automotive Genius, Back Road Atlas, Crack Driver 2, Demolisher 2, Professional Training 3 (Mechanic; Athletics, Crafts, Drive), Resources 2, Safe Place 2 (The Junkyard), Style: Stunt Driver 4

                        Integrity: 0, Willpower: 7
                        Size: 5, Initiative: 8, Defense: 5
                        Speed: 11
                        Health: 8

                        Weapons/Attacks
                        Unarmed [+0B melee, 5 dice]
                        Heavy Pistol [+2L ranged; -2 Initiative; 5 dice]
                        Tire Iron [+1L melee; -3 Initiative; 7 dice; +1 Defense]

                        Talent: Natural Compass; Frailty: Rejection

                        Natural Compass: An Undesirable has a near-perfect sense of direction, and gains a +3 bonus to Perception, Athletics, Investigation, and Survival rolls when present in their nest.

                        Rejection: The Undesirable suffers a −4 penalty on Social rolls that do not involve slashers and other monsters.


                        Nest: The Junkyard
                        The Junkyard is several acres of land protected by a high barbed wire fence. It services as an impound lot for those unluckily enough to get their vehicles towed away, and as the final resting place for dozens of disabled vehicles. In addition to the derelict vehicles, the area is littered with heaps broken appliances and worn out furniture. A pair of large, surly dogs patrol the area, eager to take a bite out of trespassers. Near the center of the property is a garage, an office, and a small residence.


                        Safe Place Features: Arsenal, Home Security
                        Tilts: Detritus
                        Traps: Barbed Wire Fence, Deadfall, Guard Animals

                        Barbed Wire Fence: The property is surrounded by a 10ft tall fence, topped with razor sharp barbed wire. Anyone attempting to climb over the fence suffers a point of lethal damage, regardless of their success.
                        Trigger: Climbing over the fence. A successful Dexterity + Athletics OR Dexterity + Larceny roll with a -2 penalty downgrades to bashing damage. An exceptional success negates the damage entirely. Characters armed with the proper tools can cut through the fence as an extended Wits+Crafts roll.

                        Deadfall: Teetering piles of junk are primed to collapse upon the unwary. Once the trap is triggered, the trap falls and deals two levels of lethal damage and imposes the Immobilized Tilt, as the victim is pinned beneath the debris. If immobilized, escaping requires a Strength+ Athletics roll to shift aside the rubble. Dramatic failures often inflict the Arm or Leg Wrack Tilt to reflect twisted ankles and the like.
                        Trigger: Structure damage to objects in the Junkyard. A successful Dexterity + Athletics roll with a -2 penalty negates the Immobilized Tilt and downgrades to bashing damage. An exceptional success negates the damage entirely.

                        Guard Animals: The Safe Place is patrolled by animals, typical trained guardians or feral creatures that consider the Safe Place their territory, that will attack any intruders. Animals that detect intruders also make enough noise to alert the owners of the property to the presence of unwanted guests. Guard Animals make attacks and perception rolls with a dice pool of twice the dots purchased in the Merit and use Down and Dirty combat rules. All other rolls use a die pool equal to the dots purchased in the Merit.
                        Trigger: Failed rolls against the Home Security feature. Alerted Guard Animals may be avoided with a Stealth roll, scared off with Intimidate, talked-down with Animal Ken, or defeated in combat.


                        Relic: Iron Dog
                        “Iron Dog” is a beast of a truck born in a junkyard and cobbled together from the rusted hulks of dozens of other vehicles. The vehicle looks like something out of a nightmare; the cab and bed sit high upon rugged tires and an imposing cow catcher covers the grill like a maniacs mask. Its rack of gleaming headlights emit a blinding glare and it is reinforced with a roll cage and armored plates, allowing it to survive an unreal amount of punishment. It’s exhaust pipes spew black smoke and its engine roars with demonic fury.


                        The vehicle is heavily modified, with a powerful engine, improved handling, and reinforced with armor plates. It’s over-all appearance strikes fear into the heart’s of motorists and pedestrians, granting a +2 equipment bonus to rolls to harass and intimidate. Iron Dog is also equipped with a set of flood lights that emit a blinding glare. Anyone facing the vehicle must make a successful Wits + Composure roll or gain the Blinded Tilt.
                        Those who drive Iron Dog become obsessive about the truck’s maintenance, gaining the Deprived Condition if they do not spend at least an hour each day repairing or tinkering with the vehicle.

                        Dice Modifier: -1, Size: 16, Durability/Structure: 6/22, Speed: 90, Availability: 3

                        Dice Pool: Presence + (Drive or Intimidate)
                        Action: Instant
                        Success: Iron Dog is restored by bloodshed and slaughter. Whenever the vehicle deals collision damage to a living target, they may spend a point of Willpower and make a (Drive or Intimidate) roll to activate the relic. Doing so restores a point of Structure to Iron Dog for each success rolled.

                            Sawtooth Charlie
                            The man they call Sawtooth Charlie once worked as a foreman in a Canadian lumber camp. He had a reputation as a tough-but-fair man who enjoyed strong booze, but never let it get in the way of his work. He had the respect of most of his workers, but not all. After a night of drinking, he was confronted by a group of disgruntled employees, and what began as a heated argument quickly escalated to violence, with Charlie being struck in the head with an ax and seemingly falling dead. The men quickly staged an accident, hoping that by the time the body was found, it would be written off as a mishap common to such dangerous work. And yet the body was never found. Shortly thereafter, the men involved began to die. The bodies appeared dismembered with a powerful chainsaw. Witnesses remember the roar of the saw, but no one recalls seeing the perpetrator. Despite the events happening over thirty years ago, dismembered corpses still appear around logging camps and campsites in the forests of the western Canada. It’s said that Charlie developed a thirst for blood that rivals his taste for drink. So always leave shot out for Charlie, it could save your life.
                            Charlie looks like the quintessential lumberjack. He is dressed in denim and flannel, with strong arms, a broad chest and a thick, black beard. He has a deep, gruesome scar over his left eye, and smells of pine-tar, motor oil, and blood.


                            Undertaking: Legend; Virtue: Thorough; Vice: Stern
                            Aspiration: Punish those who show disrespect to Charlie or the traditions of the camp.
                            Tell: dismembers the bodies of his victims

                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 3, Resolve 4

                            Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 2, Composure 3

                            Skills: Athletics 2 (Balance, Lifting), Brawl 3, Crafts 3 (Mechanic), Drive 2, Firearms 2, Intimidation 5, Occult 1, Persuasion 2, Stealth 4 (Stalking), Survival 4 (Forest, Tracking), Weaponry 4 (Chainsaw)

                            Chainsaw [+5L melee, -6 Initiative; 9 dice, 8-again]
                            Hatchet [+1L melee, -2 Initiative, 8 dice]

                            Talent: Living Legend Frailty: Narrative Chains (Cannot refuse a toast in his honor)
                            Dread Powers: Maze, Regenerate, Unnatural Step (Unimpeded by Plants/Wooden Objects)

After this has been activated, successes that haven’t been spent are discarded. They may not be banked to use in a following scene.

                            Relic: Charlie’s Chainsaw

When revved, it unleashes a guttural roar with a rhythmic, almost musical quality. The chainsaw is battered, rusted and drips oil constantly. Despite this, it never snags, jams, starts clean with each pull, and rarely needs refueling.

                            Memento: Charlie’s Chainsaw is soaked in bloodshed and death, which has made it strangely impervious. It is never damaged by accidents or environmental hazards, has a Durability of 5 and twice its normal Structure. If using Geist: The Sin-Eaters 2nd Edition, Charlie’s Chainsaw gives access to the Key of Blood.

                                                            Vogue
                                                            Fifteen years ago, the fashion world was taken by storm by a young, ambitious German designer named Siegfried Lange (later choosing to simply go by “Siegfried”). Outspoken and often controversial, Siegfried backed it up with a rare talent for both high fashion and ready-to-wear clothing. To be their chosen “Muse” was an honor, even if only for a few months. But fashion moves quickly and time is unforgiving. While still a respected figure in the world of fashion, Siegfried has fallen out of the limelight. When a nasty fight with their current muse and paramour turned deadly, Siegfried found their artistic passions re-invigorated. They are already working on a collection that will allow them to once again rule the fashion world. They are discreet, and have thus far managed to make most their murders look like accidents, disappearances or suicides, it is only a matter of time before their deeds are uncovered. They are already plotting ways to stage their own death or disappearance. The posthumous renown will be glorious.
                                                            Siegfried is a fit and attractive non-binary person in their late thirties. They are slender, with (currently) blond hair, high cheekbones, and startling blue eyes. They dress to impress at all times, and their sense of fashion favors slim suits made of expensive fabrics. They wear a variety of stylish glasses to correct their mild nearsightedness.


                                                            Undertaking: Charmer; Virtue: Innovative; Vice: Perfectionist
                                                            Aspiration: To find the perfect Muse.
                                                            Tell: Victims are all people involved in the fashion industry, and are typically exceptionally good looking.

                                                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 4, Resolve 3
                                                            Physical Attributes: Strength 2, Dexterity 4, Stamina 3
                                                            Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 5, Composure 3

                                                            Skills: Academics 2, Crafts 3 (Tailoring), Expression 4 (Fashion, Photography), Firearms 2 (Pistols), Investigation 2, Persuasion 3 (Bribe, Seduce), Socialize 3, Stealth 3, Streetwise 2 (Drugs), Subterfuge 3 (Lying to Cops), Weaponry 3 (Improvised)
                                                            Merits: Allies 3 (Fashion Industry), Area of Expertise (Fashion), Cover-Up, Defensive Combat (Weaponry), Fame 1 (Fashion Industry), Hardy 1, Hands of a Killer, Interdisciplinary Specialty (Fashion), Language (English, French), Professional Training 4 (Fashion Designer; Crafts, Expression, Socialize), Resources 4, Striking Looks 2 (Stylish), Taste (Fashion), Untouchable

                                                            Integrity: 0, Willpower: 6
                                                            Size: 5, Initiative: 7, Defense: 7
                                                            Speed: 10
                                                            Health: 8

                                                            Weapons/Attacks
                                                            Unarmed [+0B melee, 2 dice]
                                                            Scissors [+0L melee, - 1 Initiative; 6 dice, Improvised]
                                                            Light Pistol [+1L ranged; 7 dice]
                                                            Liquor Bottle [+0L melee, - 1 Initiative; 6 dice, Improvised, Stun]

                                                            Talent: Disarming; Frailty: Thin Veneer (Suggesting they are “Out of Style”)

                                                            Disarming: The Charmer is a master manipulator and knows how to use Social Maneuvers to produce excellent results. They enjoy +2 dice when rolling to open a Door, and get a bonus die when making a first impression.

                                                            Thin Veneer: During a scene, when a Charmer fails to maintain their persona after falling prey to their unique trigger, the ripper must spend a point of Willpower or lash out at the target of their ire. Further consequences to their Social Maneuvers may result depending upon which character the Charmer has shown their “true face” to.


                                                            Nest: The Penthouse
                                                            This expensive, spacious apartment sits on the top floor of of a multi-story building. It is accessed through either a secured stairwell or an elevator that requires a key or pass code to access the floor. The apartment is comfortable, well decorated and has access to all the most modern amenities. Above all else, it is private; people with the means to live in the penthouse value discretion in all their affairs.

                                                            Safe Place Features: Home Security, Privacy

                                                            Feature-Privacy: The Safe Place is built with an eye towards discretion, even in the heart of a teaming city. The windows tint or drop a shade with the push of a button, the walls are sound-proofed, and even waste disposal is managed to prevent unwanted parties from picking through refuse looking for clues. In addition to penalties imposed by Home Security, anyone outside of the Safe Place that attempts to observe or investigate events or persons inside the Safe Place suffer an additional -2 penalty.
