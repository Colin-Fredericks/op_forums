    Re-inventing the Wheel [Hunter Endowment Hack]

    01-25-2025, 07:22 PM
    So for a while, I have worked on certain capacity on developing my Personal "Hunterverse", which divided Hunter orgs according to a certain "type" as well as rebuilding Endowments into something more flexible. The reason for the later is twofold- first, I really hate writing powers. Even as a player finding which spell I find interesting is a tenacious process which I just don't enjoy, and in general I prefer more open systems. Second, it gives that weird "anime" feeling, where the characters have access to some specific form of power which they find new ways to improvise and control it. So having something which is limited on one hand and flexible on the other is something that I found of interest, and while working on the different "gods" for my Fantasy Shard (where they are basically Endowment sources), I decided to go back to my Endowment project and start building the different orgs around them.

    So, how do "Flexible Endowments work?

    First, each Endowment is executed by using Applications- which work similarly to the open Blood Magic system from Vampire. Each Application is rated 1-5, and it defines what the Endowment attempts to achieve. For most rolls (unless the ST decides otherwise), the player needs 1 Success per Application used- as hunters, who don't really "get" the supernatural, have harder and harder time to grasp how to combine different aspects of their power. In general, Applications of Endowments lean heavily towards fighting the darkness, though there are numerous methods through which it can be achieved. Here is the current list of Applications used in the system-

    Applications
    Abjuration- disrupts supernatural powers (suppress a Bygone's power, break a spell's hold, etc)

    Affliction- inflicts negative conditions upon the target (enforce a negative Condition, Tilt or penalty)

    Booning- gives positive influence to yourself or other (gives a positive Condition or modifier)

    Divination- find information related to the target (gathers Clues, gives prophetic dream, see the unseen, etc)

    Exorcism- banish or harm spiritual entities (end Manifestation Conditions, enchant an object into a Bane, enforce a Ban, etc)

    Rejuvenation- purifies the body or the soul (heals damage, removes Conditions and Tilts of mundane origin)

    Summoning- invokes or commune with spiritual beings or worlds (speak with a ghost, summon a spirit, control an angel)

    Vanquishment- causes direct damage (throw fire ball, enchant a blade, turn water into acid)

    Warding- Protects against the supernatural (create a spiritual barrier, turn a place into a holy ground, turn a person invisible to the supernatural)

    Each Endowment have 3 favoured Application, which are bought by the price of Endowment while others cost 5 Experience per dot, as well as 3 Themes which define it- an Endowment should, ideally, act while resonating with all three Themes, and the less Themes it answers the more difficult it is to use it. Additionally, each Endowment has a set of Attributes related to it- either following the Physical/Mental/Social categories, or by following the Power/Finesse/Resistance categories. The ST choose one Attribute from that category for the roll according to the specific Application in mind.

    Additionally, Endowments work better some enemies over others- as such, each Endowment has 3 Favoured Enemies, as well as one specialty within those Enemies. Not all monsters belong to a certain Enemy category, and the ST is to decide to which category a monster belongs if any. The following is the list of Enemies available, as well as example Specialities-

    Enemies
    Vampirism (Kindred, Ghouls, Strix)

    Shamanism (Shapeshifters, Claimed, Spirits)

    Witchcraft (Mages, Hallows, Supernal Entities)

    Alchemy (Prometheans, Alchemists, Qashmallim)

    Hedge Magic (Changelings,Fetches, Hobgoblins)

    Necromancy (Bound, Ghosts, Reapers)

    Sorcery (Mummies, Sorcerers, Fiends)

    Theurgy (Angels, Demons, Infrastructures)

    Dreamwalking (Beasts, Heroes, Goetia)

    Deviance (Deviants, Manticores)

    Slashing (Slashers, Hunters, Mysterious Places)

    Infernalism (Dominions, Larvae, Immundi)

    Eldritch Magic (Acamoth, Gulmoth, Intruders)

    Contagion (Infected, Carriers, Hunger)

    Finally, there is the Cost- each Endowment roll has a Cost associated with it, generally either Lethal Damage or Willpower, but some use a more exotic approach. The roll takes 1 unit of Cost per Application used, unless the ST deems otherwise. And with that in mind, the roll for a Flexible Endowment should look something like this-


    Dice Pool: Application + Favoured Attribute + Endowment Equation Value [can be beyond +/-5]

    Modifiers: Used not in Theme [-1 for each Theme violated], +1 for a favoured Enemy, +1 for specialised target, -1 for Enemy not in the favoured list (but which can be described by it)

    Cost: see each Endowment section

    Exceptional Failure: using the supernatural is always playing with fire, and sometimes it turns against the hunter. The hunter suffers not only from a Backlash, but a complication arises of the same magnitude and in favour of a monstrous entity to the ST’s choosing.

    Failure: a Backlash, as described in the Endowment entry, appears

    Success: the hunter channels their will to control the eldritch power. The Endowment manifested as requested.

    Exceptional Success: the hunter finds the use of the Endowment as easy as breathing. They do not need to spend the required cost.

    While that describes the roll for the Endowments- it does not describe the orgs themselves. Each organization is being defined through a short writeup- most already have well defined writeups in either canon books or in fan wirteups. Some orgs in the project, however, are going to be all new- if there will be interest I will provide more information about those orgs, but currently, each org is described in the following scheme-

    Organization Template
    Concept: what the organization is about- why do they hunt and the basis of their ideology

    Sphere: the organization’s sphere of influence, geographically speaking

    Endowments: the supernatural power that the organization provide

    Key: the source behind the organization’s Endowments

    Initiation: what does it take to gain access to the organization and its Endowments

    Foundation: a basic ability bestowed through the act of initiation

    Backlash: the consequence for failing the Endowment roll

    Themes: three themes which define what the Endowment can do

    Cost: what resource is spent while activating the Endowment

    Favoured Applications: three Applications through which the Endowment more naturally manifests itself.

    Attribute: the Attribute category which is used to produce the effects of the Endowment, either Mental/Social/Physical or Power/Finesse/Resistance

    Enemy: three enemies favoured by the Endowment, with one of the subcategories having a speciality in parenthesis
    ​
    ​And that's it! Currently Compacts are done, so I am going to post them shortly after this post. Hope you will find this of interest!

      Compacts
      Compacts refer to a certain type of Hunter organizations- specifically, to organizations which are relatively small in scale and very cohesive, or wide in scale and very diffused. Because Compacts lack the same resources and authority that other orgs has, the larger the Compact, the harder it is to control its members, and the harder it is to guarantee their devotion through support and resources. Still, in this setting, each Compact has their own Endowment which they use to hunt, and their all united around the concept of the Vigil as in hunting down the darkness for a variety of reasons. If you don't see a compact you know in here, it is not because they don't exist in the setting, but because they have a better organizational category to define them- after all, Compacts, in this setting, are defined by their independence and devotion to the Vigil. Organizations which are integrated in some manner to a wider structure (such as a religion or a state), or which have less aggressive attitude towards the supernatural (such as seeking to study it or support other hunters rather than doing the work themselves), have their own categories instead.

      Ashwood Abbey (Core)
      Concept: hellfire club of hedonistic hunters who celebrate their darkest desires through hunting down monsters

      Sphere: British Isles

      Endowments: Bacchanal Projections

      Key: The Black Licence

      Initiation: after going through the Initiation Ceremony where the candidate must survive their hunt against a monster, if they manage to take down their enemy they are offered to add their name to the local member book, sanctioned by the License

      Foundation: by accepting their own dark desires, the hunter’s carnal nature becomes overwhelming. They gain another Vice, though they can only fulfill one per session for Willpower.

      Backlash: sinking deep into vice, the hunter becomes disillusioned by their own ideals. They can not gain Willpower from fulfilling their Virtue until they fulfill their Vice first, even if they can not gain Willpower from the act.

      Themes
      Carnal- Bacchanal Projections utilise the darkest desires of the target, instigating them and drawing power from them

      Transgressed- Bacchanal Projections drive the targets to break taboos, be them social or spiritual, and draw power from those acts

      Subtle- Bacchanal Projections are not obvious distortion of reality, rather working by mental manipulations and sensory distortions

      Cost: Willpower

      Favoured Applications: Abjuration, Affliction, Booning

      Attribute: Social

      Enemy: Slashing [Slashers], Infernalism, Dreamwalking

      Dies Irae (S&B)
      Concept: demon hunters who used heretical magic to transform themselves into infernal beings

      Sphere: USA

      Endowments: Art of Dying

      Key: the Ars Moriendi book

      Initiation: by invoking the First Transformation of the Ars Moriendi, the user have their physical and spiritual forms reconstructed to allow applications of the occult power

      Foundation: the First Transformation, which mutilates the flesh of the user, allowing them to interact with beings in the Twilight.

      Backlash: the demonic power takes over the hunter. They must act to fulfill their Vice in the most brutal and quick manner possible. They do not gain Willpower from the act, though it doesn’t count against the limit on fulfilling one’s Vice.

      Themes
      Mutilating- the Art of Dying transforms the flesh of the user, always causing them some sort of demonic transformation

      Vengeful- the Art of Dying is powered by pain and anger, targeting those who have harmed the hunter

      Terrifying- the Art of Dying bestows terrible presence upon the user, inflicting fear on lesser demons

      Cost: Lethal Damage

      Favoured Applications: Exorcism, Rejuvenation, Vanquishment

      Attribute: Power

      Enemy: Infernalism [Possessed], Sorcery, Eldritch Magic

      Heritage House (DE: DS, GotU)
      Concept: Blood sworn hunters who are cursed by fate and dream to maintain generational Vigil

      Sphere: USA

      Endowment: Sanguine Bonds

      Key: Accepting the generation curse which haunts the families

      Initiation: After being afflicted by their generational curse, they need to survive the curse’s Night of Haunting, which takes the form of a dream quest into their own family’s history. Those who survive gain access to the Endowment.

      Foundation: Those who are marked by the generational curse are able to sense the presence of both the enemies of the clan and those born to it with a scene, by rolling Occult + Wits.

      Backlash: the generational curse of the hunter triggers. A vampire of proper magnitude (depends on the Endowment Value) will get haunted to target them in some manner.

      Themes
      Blood- the Sanguine Bonds manipulate, control or trace blood both within and without

      Cursed- Sanguine Bonds are negative in nature, causing a corruptive influence on the world

      Psychic- Sanguine Bonds often mimic psychic phenomena, mostly empathic but also telekinetic

      Cost: Bashing Damage

      Favoured Applications: Affliction, Vanquishment, Warding

      Attribute: Finesse

      Enemies: Blood Magic [Kindred], Necromancy, Dreamwalking

      Obsidian Brotherhood (S&B)
      Concept: vengeance driven pyrokinetics who seeks to burn down the systematic control of the supernatural

      Sphere: USA

      Endowments: Pyrokinesis

      Key: Mr Schwartz’s Obsidius

      Initiation: Mr Schwartz presents himself before a victim of the supernatural, and after evaluating the candidate, if found fitting, would burn them with a scorch mark of the Obsidius representing “freedom”.

      Foundation: the members of the Brotherhood are imbued with the powers of fire, making them immune to all forms of fire damage.

      Backlash: the attempt to invoke the black fire of Pyrokinesis goes highware, setting the surrounding area on fire. If not taken care of actively, the fire would inflict the Inferno Tilt in 2 turns. Those fires can not be controlled with Pyrokinesis.

      Themes
      Flaming- Pyrokinesis, true to its name, is able to control, generate and intensify it

      Emotional- Pyrokinesis burns with the user’s emotions, becoming stronger when aligns with their aspirations, Touchstones or Anchors

      Shaped- Pyrokinesis form to the user’s imagination, forming objects, structures and entities out of the flames

      Cost: Willpower

      Favoured Applications: Affliction, Summoning, Vanquishment

      Attribute: Finesse

      Enemy: Theurgy [Demons], Blood Magic, Deviance

      Order of the Earthly Bones (HB)
      Concept: protectors of ancient megalithic structures who keep them safe from monsters who seek to abuse them.

      Sphere: Malta, France

      Endowments: Gigantism

      Key: The Gigants

      Initiation: following an occult ceremony meant to awaken the will of the buried giants within one of the sacred sites, the hunters invest themselves with the might of the megaliths themselves

      Foundation: in order to use their connection to the giants, the members of the Order gain an Essence pull of a size equal to their Stamina. They can replenish Essence by meditating in a megalithic site (gain an amount equal to their success) or exchange a Virtue channeling from Willpower to Essence.

      Backlash: the giant’s Essence rebels against the flesh which does not fit its might, inflicting an amount of Bashing Damage equal to their dots in the Application. (in case of a combination, use the Application with the highest value)

      Themes
      Transformative- Gigantism manipulates the flesh of the hunter, using it to express their supernatural power

      Geomantic- Gigantism draws power from the earth, drawing power from the flow of ley lines and sacred architecture

      Earthmelded- Gigantism mix flesh and stone together, allowing the hunter to meld with their territory

      Cost: Essence

      Favoured Applications: Booning, Summoning, Warding

      Attribute: Power

      Enemy: Shamanism [Idigam], Dreamwalking, Sorcery

      Reality Hackers (KR)
      Concept: programmers who have got access to the “source god of the universe” by an angel to “stop instabilities in the code”

      Sphere: USA

      Endowments: SERAPH Algorithm

      Key: Having their name added to the Administrator List of the SERAPH Algorithm

      Initiation: the hunter must be scanned by the Algorithm, which defines their whole existence and opens their minds to the greater truth

      Foundation: the Cherub Subsystem, a small device which, when connected to a computer, allows accessing different applications of the Algorithm, though only to authorized user, and allows scanning for nearby “anomalies” through Intelligence + Computer

      Backlash: a bug in the code starts breaking up reality. A Reality Bleed Tilt is evoked until the end of the scene.

      Themes
      Corresponded- the SERAPH Algorithm utilises mapping between the device’s commands and the entities targeted through it

      Invasive- the SERAPH Algorithm takes control over entities and aspects of reality, jamming their abilities or limiting their actions

      Informative- the SERAPH Algorithm provides and relies on information, and can’t work reliably without having analysis of the target

      Cost: Willpower

      Favoured Applications: Divination, Summoning, Affliction

      Attribute: Mental

      Enemy: Alchemy [Pometheans], Theurgy, Contagion

      Soldiers of the Forbidden Sun (DE:RotLI)
      Concept: mystical order dedicated for preparing to prophesied calamities by guarding ancient tombs

      Sphere: China

      Endowments: Shendao

      Key: Sleeping in the Hall of Ancestors

      Initiation: a meditation has to be performed in the Hall of Ancestors, where the participant needs to commune with the timeless world which lurks beyond the grave.

      Foundation: After breaching the boundary between life and death, the hunters are able to control their vitality, spending 1 Willpower point to hide all physical and spiritual signs of their life for a scene and becoming metaphysically “dead”.

      Backlash: the control that the hunter holds over their vitality is disturbed and starts glowing, making them visible to all supernatural entities.

      Themes
      Purified- Shendao are based around cleansing the soul and the world around it

      Vital- Shendao bridge the boundary between life and death and are able to manipulate both

      Internal- Shendao works within the body. While it may emanate through aura, it does not construct phenomena or manipulate reality.

      Cost: Willpower

      Favoured Applications: Abjuration, Booning, Rejuvenation

      Attribute: Resistance

      Enemy: Sorcery [Arisen], Shamanism, Necromancy

      Sons of CuChulainn (DC)
      Concept: hedge lost warriors who have reached out across time to help their descendants against the darkness

      Sphere: USA, British Isles

      Endowments: Geasa

      Key: accepting the traditional geis of paying tribute to the Hedge

      Initiation: entering one of the party’s hedge-gates and taking their geis by cutting their hands upon the sacred spear Gae Bulg

      Foundation: under the geis the hunter gains the ability to sense the hedge gate of the party and gains the support of their time displaced warrior mentors.

      Backlash: the hunter’s demands of fate are twisted, bestowing a curse upon them. They suffer from a bane equivalent to a spirit of Rank equal to the Endowment’s Value, minimum of 1.

      Themes
      Verbal- Geasa must be pronounced vocally and must be heard by the foe in question or a relevant witness

      Fair- Geasa only grant power equivalent to the risk which is taken by breaking it, and the more obscure the condition the more dangerous the risk becomes

      Warborne- Geasa are bestowed upon the holder, and act through them and their chosen weaponry

      Cost: Willpower

      Favoured Applications: Abjuration, Booning, Vanquishment

      Attribute: Physical

      Enemy: Hedge Sorcery [Huntsmen], Witchcraft, Necromancy

      Valdaermen (HB)
      Concept: heirs of norse pagan tradition who believe in proving their excellence through battle against the darkness to achieve Valhalla

      Sphere: Scandinavia

      Endowments: Runes

      Key: Root Rune

      Initiation: through careful use of divination, the crones of the company decipher the fate of the individual, and tattoo them with the Root Rune which describes it

      Foundation: once the Root Rune is inscribed, the hunter become masters of their own fate, bestowing -1 to all supernatural attempts to control or manipulate their minds.

      Backlash: the hunter’s Root Rune becomes corrupted and poisons their body, bestowing upon them the moderate version of the Sick Tilt.

      Themes
      Inscribed- Runes act upon the subject on which they are inscribed, either enhancing or enchanting it

      Language- Runes are a method of communication, and as such each Rune bestows effects appropriate to its meaning

      Warborne- Runes are the gift of Odin, the warrior god, and as such they act to fulfill success in battle

      Cost: Willpower

      Favoured Applications: Abjuration, Booning, Rejuvenation

      Attribute: Physical

      Enemy: Dreamwalking [Beasts], Shamanism, Necromancy

              Conspiracies
              Conspiracies are both similar to compacts and yet fundamentally different. Like compacts, conspiracies are entities who are devouted to the Vigil, yet lack connection to any larger entity, such as a state or a religion, making them independent organizations. However, unlike compacts, conspiracies are organized and intertwined into the fabric of human society- they exist in the shadows, holding connections to governmental agencies and religious figures while still remaining apart of them. Their signs and imprints could be found everywhere if you know where to look- yet most people lack that understanding. Still, while maintaining tremendous power, conspiracies still act in the benefit of humanity- though many do have dark secrets within their cores, which they keep far from their own members, knowing that by exposing those skeletons in the closet the whole structure might fall apart. While conspiracies have a sphere of power where they are more dominant and cohesive, even outside of it they hold a lot of diffusive influence, allowing them to operate all around the world.

              Aegis Kai Doru (Core)
              Concept: collectors of relics who seek to protect humanity’s heritage from monsters and take vengeance against those stolen it

              Sphere: Greece

              Endowments: Relics

              Key: Oath Stone

              Initiation: after being sent into a labyrinth and reaching its centre alive, the hunter has to take a sacred vow upon the Stone to fulfill their ancestral duty to fight those who have broken the world.

              Foundation: the Vow directs the hunter against their ancestral enemies, giving them an Unseen Sense towards them and gain +1 for all rolls in service of the vow

              Backlash: the relic malfunction, refusing to acknowledge the hunter as its master. The relic stops functioning until the end of the chapter.

              Themes
              Historical- Relics are tied to their historical origin, manifesting a power depending on their creation and era

              External- Relics require the use of a sacred artefact, losing hold of the object removes the ability to use it

              Objectified- Relics fill a purpose fitting their design, a spear penetrates while a shield protects

              Cost: Bygone (accessible by conspiracy)

              Favoured Applications: Exorcism, Vanquishment, Warding

              Attribute: Finesse

              Enemy: Witchcraft [Mages], Shamanism, Sorcery

              Ascending Ones (Core)
              Concept: alchemists who transform their own bodies into furnace of supernatural power in order to find the horrors of the night

              Sphere: Egypt

              Endowments: Elixirs

              Key: The Ascension Rite

              Initiation: after learning the art of alchemy, the candidate drinks a concoction of poisons, taking a leap of faith with their ability to transmute the poison into blessing. Those with appropriate faith survive, and are born again as a “phoenix”.

              Foundation: the body of the hunter has become resistant to all toxins through alchemy, making them resilient to any mundane poison of Toxicity lower than their Stamina.

              Backlash: The alchemical process is disturbed, causing an amount of bashing damage equal to the Endowment Equation Value, minimum of 1.

              Themes
              Alchemical- Elixirs act to transmute the subject, acting through the hunter’s body as a living furnace.

              Consumed- Elixirs take the form of a variety of reagents mixed with deadly poisons, which must be consumed by the hunter to activate

              Sacred- Elixirs draw their power from the themes of the Egyptian gods, allowing the hunter to harness their attributes

              Cost: Resources (replenished by conspiracy, pool corresponds to Resources merit, minimum 1)

              Favoured Applications: Affliction, Booning, Rejuvenation

              Attribute: Resistance

              Enemy: Blood Magic [Vampires], Alchemy, Sorcery

              Blood of Ahran (VII)
              Concept: descendants of Gammorah’s survivors who seek to purify the world from the terror which brought God’s ire through demonic might

              Sphere: Levant

              Endowments: Ahranaite Sorcery

              Key: Shaddad’s Pillar

              Initiation: the candidate swears an oath of loyalty to the conspiracy and allows the demon in their service to claim their heart

              Foundation: the hunter’s mind is clouded by Shaddad, stopping them from any supernatural or mundane attempt art betraying their conspiracy

              Backlash: the demonic power of Shaddad takes over the hunter, enforcing upon them the Whispers Condition until one service is made to satisfy the demon. The more failures, the more services required

              Themes
              Blood- Ahranite Sorcery draws power from the hunter’s blood, and it allows them to sense and manipulate that of others

              Demonic- Ahranite Sorcery emanate for Shaddad, a great fiend, and as such its outcomes are horrifying to behold

              Vengeful- Ahranite Sorcery draws upon an ancient oath of retribution, and as such it is designed to harm those who inflicted it

              Cost: Lethal Damage

              Favoured Applications: Affliction, Divination, Vanquishment

              Attribute: Physical

              Enemy: Sorcery [Fiends], Blood Magic, Infernalism

              Cainite Heresy (NS)
              Concept: zealot avengers who take radical actions to dismantle the abusing systems through which monsters thrive

              Sphere: Balkans

              Endowments: Rites of Denial

              Key: Sources

              Initiation: the Sources track the individual they deem as a worthy candidate due to their abuse, giving them access to the First Rites

              Foundation: the hunter is found in a constant connection with the Sources, who provide them with information and ritual materials

              Backlash: the protective rites fail, filling the hunter with a sense of suspicion for who might have disrupted the ritual, bestowing the Paranoid Condition upon them

              Themes
              Ritualistic- the Rites of Denial always demand a ritual to work- it doesn’t need faith in the ritual, only its execution

              Superstition- the Rites of Denial invoke old wives tales and common superstitions, bringing them to life

              Simple- the Rites of Denial do not require complex memorisation or exotic components. A+B=C, that’s it

              Cost: Blood Vial (the vial has number of thimbles equal to Status: Cainite Heresy, replenished by other members of the conspiracy)

              Favoured Applications: Abjuration, Affliction, Warding

              Attribute: Social

              Enemy: Blood Magic [Kindred], Necromancy, Sorcery

              Cold Guard (D&L)
              Concept: descendants of refugees of broken dreams who have abandoned all hope to keep the Darkness at bay

              Sphere: Germany

              Endowments: Penumbra

              Key: The Dreaming Ice Age

              Initiation: the candidate, filled with despair and loss, travels through a dream quest which leads them to the ruins of humanity’s hope bound by eternal winter, marking them with a sign of doom

              Foundation: due to the corruption of the darkness in their souls, members of the conspiracy gain +2 to all rolls meant to resist mental, spiritual or physical corruption of a supernatural source

              Backlash: the Darkness manages to break through their mental boundaries, bestowing a Frostbite upon them. Once they have more Frostbites than their Resolve or Composure (the lower of the two), they are bestowed with the Fugue Condition.

              Themes
              Cold- Penumbra is defined by the lacking of warmth, light and voice, be it literally or metaphorically

              Hostile- Penumbra is powered by hatred and despair, using the inner darkness within the user as a power source

              Cursed- Penumbra is a dark art, taking the form of numerous curses which inflict pain and sorrow

              Cost: Willpower

              Favoured Applications: Affliction, Booning, Warding

              Attribute: Resistance

              Enemy: Alchemy [Domemim], Slashing, Dreamwalking

              Ebony Fangs (LL)
              Concept: a collection of exiles and low class individuals who hunt down the dead to stop future calamities

              Sphere: USA, Middle East

              Endowments: Words of the Dead

              Key: the Laws of Irkalla define that only those who are considered “dead to society” or who were harmed by the dead can join the conspiracy

              Initiation: the candidate, if fitting the parameters, writes their name upon an Avernian Gate, who adds them to the conspiracy’s influence

              Foundation: due to allowing their names to die upon the gates of the Underworld, the hunters are protected from sympathetic magic who one which seeks to abuse their names

              Backlash: the powers of death lash out upon the hunter, forcing upon them a decayed image or the scent of the grave, giving -5 to all social interactions until the end of the chapter

              Themes
              Inscribed- the Words of the Dead must be written upon an object, and act through the medium on which they are inscribed

              Liminal- the Words of the Dead effect either the living or the dead, and create clear distinctions between the two

              Subtle- the Words of the Dead manipulate the behaviour of the affected entities and limit their agency, but can’t outright distort reality or physical phenomenon

              Cost: Willpower

              Favoured Applications: Affliction, Exorcism, Warding

              Attribute: Mental

              Enemy: Necromancy [Bound], Sorcery, Blood Magic

              Garde du Peuple (D&L)
              Concept: egalitarian movement who constantly seeks to rally up a revolution against the rule of the supernatural

              Sphere: France

              Endowments: Vox Populi

              Key: the Manifeste

              Initiation: going through a communal right of sharing wine and bread while taking the oath of loyalty to the conspiracy, the hunter is accepted to the Guard

              Foundation: the hunter is bound by bonds of brotherhood with his companions, letting them know the general direction to the closest conspirayc member by spending a Willpower

              Backlash: the fire of the revolution dies in the hunter’s heart, inflicting the Demoralized Condition

              Themes
              Revolution- Vox Populi ia an act of rebellion against the creatures of the night, challenging their authority
