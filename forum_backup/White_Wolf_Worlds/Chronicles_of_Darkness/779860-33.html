            In a future post I'll mark down mechanics of present Investigative or Chase merits to see if there's any new ideas to be had.

            Originally posted by SaulottheGentle View Post
            Anyways, here's my ideas so far on revamping Trace. It has five factors: Time, Range, Clarity, Audience and Verse. When a Siren invokes Trace, they must spend 1 Pneuma and a point of Willpower (if they put any suxx into Verse, it becomes a point of permanent Willpower) and roll Akashic Record + Resolve to see how many suxx they get, then they choose how many suxx they spend on each of the mechanics. The higher suxx, the higher strength the Siren may put into a mechanic. If the Siren uses Verse, then she must pay the Verses cost in the subsequent turn.

            Time allows a Siren to see forwards and backwards through time, seeing past or probable future events. The more suxx put into Time, the farther forwards or backwards she may see. Seeing forwards shows the most likely occurrance, but it's easily disrupted by things such as controlling fate or simple dumb luck. Since Time doesn't take into account for this, Sirens tend to see looking forwards with Trace as unreliable at best.

            Range allows a Siren to choose how far her Trace can pull, allowing her to see elsewhere and even exert force with more suxx invested. She may even speak telepathically to people with this. With no suxx, the Trace can only affect the Siren herself, and with one suxx, she may use the Trace on things within her line of sight. With more suxx invested to this, the Siren may communicate to those at a greater distance. With investment to Time as well, a Siren can look into the past or future of places or people far away from her.

            Clarity allows the Trace to have greater definition when Tracing things. She may read into the composition of objects and see physical and mental traits of people. With investment to Time, the Siren may get her defined answers instead of seeing things through a set timeframe. With Investment to Range, the Siren may speak more than a simple phrase and add more words to her telepathic messages.

            Audience allows the Trace to affect a group of people outside of the siren, and by itself it allows a Siren to try to "listen" into other active traces. With more suxx put into this, the group of people can grow very large. With investment to Time, the Siren shares a Time Trace's Vision with the Audience. With investment to Range, the Siren may hold a group conversation with people. With Investment to Clarity, a Siren may pre-record a message that she wants any specific audience members to hear and leaving others out.

            Verse is simply what allows a Siren to wield a Verse during her Tracing. The amount of Suxx put into this equals the dot level of the Conclusion the Siren may use through the Trace. With Investment to Time, a Siren may use Verses in the past. With Investment to Range, a Siren may use Verses at the places she sees, as if she were actually there (and thus negating the Amour modifier normally required to affect places out of sensory vision). Clarity and Audience have no effect with this.

            There's also the idea of Sirens being able to Trace other Sirens, but due to Song's nature (and thus a Siren's as well) of achronality, if the Siren doesn't wish to be traced, she may reflexively roll Akashic Record + Composure to contest the use of the trace. The Resisting Siren's suxx is subtracted from the Tracing Sirens suxx that they may spend, and if the Resisting Siren's suxx is higher, then she completely blocks the Trace from occurring.

            Any thoughts on this?
            Taking a step back to look at the previous Trace chart:

            Time looks to be the same factor, it now can peer into the past or towards the most likely future.

            Range is the modified version of Area. Would it be dependent on what target the Siren is focusing on? A person, a place, etc.

            Clarity is a fusion of Focus and Noise to some degree, for as it increases the Siren sees the fine details unobstructed.

            Audience is a new category which is how 'softly' the Siren can impact events, leaving messages and working the events of her Trace with a group of observers. Could this be used to bring other non-Sirens along with the Trace? Could she use Audience to 'tap into' the Trace of another without being noticed?

            Verse quite simply impacts how the Siren could effect the events of the Trace with her Verses.


            In regards to how a Trace occurs, does the Siren pay her costs at the start and disperse her successes (both rolled and bonus) over the parameters she wants from the start? Or while experiencing the Trace is she able to expend the pool of successes towards any parameter, enhancing the degree of her Trace on the spot. Does 'investment' mean she needs an already set expense of a parameter or spending for both Audience and Range (example) at the point to hold a group conversation.

            Since Time, Range and Clarity cover the same parameters as the previous chart, I suggest any of these are available after the Siren spends her first Pneuma to activate the Trace. Since Audience is more involved this sounds like the criteria to require a Willpower point, either spent at Trace activation or during the Trace. I do like how to use Verse it requires a Willpower dot for the high stakes, as it keeps the Siren from altering things this way too frequently. The one caveat to the price for including a Verse, I feel, would be the Sense Conclusion. As this Conclusion merely brings information to the Siren's awareness and has no tangible aspect, using Sense to enhance the Trace further should only cost a Willpower point instead of a dot.
