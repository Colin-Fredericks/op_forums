                    (While I'm still fleshing out the others in this section, decided to post up the general information on the BoV shifters)


                    Beasts of Vice: In many tribal cultures throughout the world, shapeshifters are honored and revered beings. However this is not always the case in many of the old ways. You've probably heard stories of shifters who did not alter their forms by personal choice. Stories like the Swan Princess who was cursed to the form of a swan and could only assume her human form during the full moon. Or the story of a beggar having been turned away at the door and the next day the home owners are changed into hideous monsters.

                    These stories are many, but in this particular case, we will be looking over the Beasts of Vice, mortals who's selfish urges cursed them with a form more appropriate to their behavior. In other cases, however, they can be mortals who willingly entered into a pact, sacrificing their human forms in exchange for power. To say that the Beasts of Vice are a unified culture is untrue. While they do share many similarities with one another, they are not exactly allies with one another. They ally themselves with evil spirits, and more often than not, these spirits are not looking to share their power with a potential rival, so the Beasts of Vice clash with each other just as much as they do with the Forsaken or the Pure.

                    There are 7 different types of Beasts of Vice, one for each of the 7 Vices.

                    Bunny - Lust

                    Locust - Gluttony

                    Badger - Pride

                    Wolverine - Wrath

                    Slug - Sloth

                    Vulture - Envy

                    Squirrel - Greed

                    While it is true that the Beasts of Vice are not exactly allies with one another, they do share various traits in common. Their primary goal in the physical world is to spread their particular Vice, and in doing so they increase the power of their totem. By creating weaker spirits of their associated vice, their Totem is able to consume, fuse with, or bind these spirits into servitude. This results in increasing the Totem's rise through the ranks and subsequently affords the Beasts of Vice greater power as their Totem has more to spare.

                    It is very rare for two different packs of the same breed to work together, their Totems are greedy for power and when they sense a rival of the same type as themselves, they tend to go to war with the rival Totem, rather than ally themselves with it. Despite this, there are cases of two or more packs absorbing each other following the results of a Totem war. The victorious spirit steals the power of the defeated spirit, and the losing pack joins the winning pack. Because of this, infighting is a fairly common occurrence for the Beasts of Vice.


                    Ironically while their Totems may hate working with their own kind, they have occasionally been known to form truces with other Beasts of Vice. If the spirits can find some way to share their influence in the city, they may decide to use their packs to do so. However, these truces often end with bloodshed once the greedy Totem gets the upper hand, or feels like they aren't getting their fair share of Essence from the mortals they're preying upon.


                    Because they can be created from ordinary humans who become susceptible to the Spirit's influence, Beasts of Vice can be of any ethnicity, be found in any geographical location, and can be descended from any culture. They aren't restricted to any exclusive heritage. This makes them notoriously difficult to locate for those who don't have the means to sense a person's true spiritual identity.


                    ------------------------------------------

                    Life Span: Many legends of shapeshifters peg them as being immortal creatures, but often these are exaggerations. While the Beasts of Vice are not actually immortal, they do have a reduced aging which allows them to live several times longer than ordinary humans. This can be either a blessing or a curse depending on which individual you ask. The rate at which they age varies between the breeds.


                    Squirrel, Bunny, Locust = 1/3 normal rate. Max Life Span is early 300's

                    Vulture, Badger, Wolverine = 1/5 normal rate. Max Life Span is early 500's

                    Slug = 1/10 normal rate. Max Life Span is close to 1,000 years.

                    This means that Squirrels, Bunnies, and Locusts can live 3 times longer than humans. Vultures, Badgers, and Wolverines can live 5 times longer. And Slugs can live 10 times longer!


                    -----------------------------------------------


                    Reproduction: Unlike their enemies, the Uratha, Beasts of Vice are not part of an ancient culture passed down through heredity. Everyone of them was born as a human, but through events in their lives, they were changed into shapeshifters. There are two different ways this can happen, the first and most common is by having the changed forced upon them. The second method is by the human willingly agreeing to the change, usually by entering some kind of pact or bargain in exchange for power.


                    When a Spirit of Vice reaches level 5 (minor god) rank in power, it can develop the ability to turn humans into Beasts of Vice, and usually takes on the role of acting as their Totem in the process. They seek out mortals who have a particularly strong connection to their Vice, as they are they are more susceptible to the spirit's influence and can thus be infused with the essence of the beast easier.

                    Those who agreed to the change voluntarily are loyal servants to the spirit. They unite together in the form of cults who worship the Totem as some kind of deity and offer them essence as a sacrifice. Those who had the change forced upon them are treated like slaves, forced to carry out the spirit's will as they have neither the power nor the knowledge of how to fight back.


                    Since Beasts of Vice are not part of some ancient lineage or bloodline, there is no danger when it comes to breeding among their own kind. Indeed, a Beast of Vice can mate with their own species as well as with full humans. Regardless of who they mate with, any child spawned will be born as a human, although it may exhibit a higher connection to the spirit world than normal.

                    When a Beast of Vice mates with a human, there is a 1 in 3 chance the child will exhibit a profound manifestation of the parent's Vice around the time puberty sets in. This risks increases to 60% for each child if they mate with their own species. These children attract the Totem's attention much more strongly than ordinary people, and are prime candidates to become a Beast of Vice themselves.

                    For those who agreed to the change, this is a blessing, as they indoctrinate their children from an early age in service to the Totem, in a similar way one would teach a family religion. These viceful children risk drawing the attention of Child Services (especially those who display signs of Lust, Gluttony, or Wrath), and so the parents usually prefer to home school the children until they learn how to properly control themselves around others. They can't risk drawing attention from the government and exposing their operations both physical and spiritual.

                    For those who had the change forced upon them, they usually abandon their children to what is presumably a better home, hoping that the spirits won't be able to find them, and thus spare them from the same fate as their parents. More often than not, these plans end up failing, as the slave fails to realize the true breadth of a spirit's perceptive abilities.


                    Their method of reproduction also means that it is impossible to truly wipe out a Beast of Vice's pack as long as the Totem remains. You can slaughter every member of the pack, but as long as the Totem is still active, they can simply create some new replacements. The only way to truly stamp out the threat of them resurfacing is to destroy the totem itself. Even if a packmate or two survives the battle, they are incapable of producing more of their kind, and will become a much less immediate threat.


                    ----------------------------------------

                    Gifts: Just as there is no set background to what a Beast of Vice can be, there is also no set course of Gifts they can draw upon. Traditionally, Beasts of Vice are granted a Gift that enables them to invoke, control, or draw power from the vice they represent. Werebunnies harness lust, Werevultures harness envy, and so on.

                    It is safe to say that they do not have access to gifts of Luna or Father Wolf for obvious reasons. However, they can obtain similar gifts from other malevolent spirits, such as gifts to amplify their physical attributes, manipulate the weather, and so on. Obtaining such powerful gifts is usually more difficult, but not entirely impossible. In most cases, only those who have proven themselves worthy of their Totem's favor are granted such powers. The failures and the slaves are restricted until they earn the privilege.



                    -----------------------------------------------------

                    Forms: Although they are born human, Beasts of Vice have a stronger affinity towards their animal forms than they do their original form. This is especially common for those who were forced to become a Beast of Vice against their will. Unable to come to terms with what has happened to them, they feel more comfortable remaining in their fully animal form unless they need to interact with others socially. In this manner, packs often keep them around as pets, sometimes even locking them in cages until they have a use for them.

                    Those who willingly embraced the change are able to cope with it and remain in their human form more often, but their behavior starts to reflect the beast in both mannerisms and personality. It as if the darker aspects of the person have been amplified beyond what they were prior to the First Change. Like the Uratha, they too need to shift every few days, but this has less to do with finding balance and more to do with being able to relieve the stress of pretending to be human for too long. Conversely, those forced to change feel a compulsion to remind themselves they were once human, in order to prevent themselves from going completely insane under the torment they suffer at the hands of their own pack.

                        Rites and Ceremonies: Among those who willingly agreed to the change, Beasts of Vice have been known to perform a variety of rituals in service to their Totem. The following is an explanation of the more prevalent among them.


                        Rite of Blending - This ritual is essential for a Werebeast's training in being able to blend in with human society. Unfortunately, it is also one of the most difficult to perform. The ritual takes a full day to perform, during which time the Beast of Vice is forbidden to indulge their vice. The purpose of the ritual is to teach self control and to not attract attention from their enemies. However, being ruled by their vices in such a way, it is also quite stressful. The Rite of Blending is performed annually, on the eve of the anniversary when they underwent their first change. It symbolizes the last day they were human, before they became a Beast of Vice.


                        Rite of Indulgence - This ritual is the opposite to the previous one. Where the Rite of Blending was about controlling themselves, the Rite of Indulgence is all about losing control. The ritual is a full day in dedication to satisfying their vice, and amassing power for their Totem in the process. Each breed has their own methods in which they choose to perform the Rite of Indulgence, as each vice has different methods of being satisfied. The Rite of Indulgence is performed annually on the anniversary of their First Change, and is considered a celebration of the day they became Beasts of Vice.


                        Rite of Leashing - Not everyone willingly agrees to serve their Totem master, and when that happens, submission must be forced upon them. This ritual is used to bind an unwilling Beast of Vice into service and involves a variety of profane rituals that one could only learn by worshiping an evil spirit. After the ritual's completion, the victim is incapable of bringing purposeful harm to the pack or their Totem, and is compelled to obey any order the pack gives them, so long as it does not conflict with an order given by a higher ranking pack member. Orders given by their Totem supersede all other orders. Performing the Rite of Leashing involves all members of the pack who willingly serve the Totem. They must each sacrifice 1 essence to take part in the ritual, but must also sacrifice 1 Willpower, as their collective will invades and dominates the mind of the subject. The Totem is also said to do they same, thereby binding the subject into the cult.

                        Theoretically, the Rite of Leashing can be broken if the subject has more Willpower than what is sacrificed in the rite. However, very few humans possess that strong of a will.



                        Rite of Changing - This ritual is a form of celebration and is practiced almost exclusively by those who's families have willingly joined the Totem and its pack. It is performed on the day of a subject's First Change and indoctrination into the pack. The Rite of Changing features a worship ceremony of pledging one's oath to the Spirit of Vice, and indulging one's Vice in fun and exciting ways. Such methods of indulgence can include brawls for Wrath, orgies for Lust, scavenger hunts for Greed, daring competitions for Pride, ignoring one's obligations for Sloth, grand feasts for Gluttony, and stealing from others for Envy. The Rite of Changing reaches its climax when the subject finally undergoes their First Change. If multiple newcomers are taking part, the ceremony ends when the last of them changes.



                        Rite of Cursing - This ritual is performed exclusively on those who refuse to willingly become Beasts of Vice. Although the pack itself does not have the ability to create Beasts of Vice, the Rite of Cursing does make things easier for their Totem to finish the process. The Rite of Cursing involves the subject often being abducted (after rejecting the Totem on its first visit) and brought before an altar that is imbued by essence. They are tied down upon the altar, while those who follow the path of the shaman use their gifts to crank up the desired Vice of the subject, opening their minds and bodies to the Totem's influence and allowing them to forcefully convert them into a Beast of Vice. Although the subject usually undergoes their First Change on top of the altar itself, it's infusion of essence renders the altar nearly indestructible so long as the Totem remains. This allows even Werewolverines to thrash and flail all they please without breaking free of their bonds.

                        This ritual is usually followed up by the Rite of Leashing immediately afterward.

                                Beasts of Greed (Weresquirrels): Before their First Change, Weresquirrels were hoarders, thieves, penny pinchers, and other life styles that invoke the desire to keep rather than to spend. This avarice brings them to the attention of a Spirit of Greed who initiates the first change either willingly or by forcing it upon them.


                                The First Change for a Beast of Greed is a risky one. They are filled with an insatiable urge to hoard something specific and pile it in the location in which the First Change takes place. Their urge is so powerful, they will actually go out and scour as much of the city as they can cover looking for any specimens and then hauling them back to their First Change site. By the time the First Change wears off, the Weresquirrel is likely to be surrounded by a heap of the object they were trying to collect and have little memory as to what made them think it was so important in the first place. Many of these specimens are likely stolen, and just like the Werevultures, they may need to go into hiding until the authorities lose their trail.


                                Oddly enough, many Weresquirrels willingly accepted the change and they utilize their new found powers to become peerless thieves, burglars, and heist pullers. With their new squirrel-like agility, they can slip past defenses and scale surfaces no ordinary human could ever accomplish outside of a spy movie. Without utilizing high tech equipment, their handiwork is often so precise, that officials begin to suspect it might have been an inside job!


                                Unlike most other shapeshifters, the Weresquirrels are NOT a pack based society. They embody greed, and the greedy do not like sharing what they have. When two or more Weresquirrels gather together, it is usually because they wish to breed, and then they separate afterwards. This makes the Weresquirrels vulnerable where numbers are concerned, but in terms of independence, they have more experience than other shifter breeds.


                                Weresquirrel may not be physically overwhelming in a fight, but what they lack in strength they make up for with their agility. An experienced Weresquirrel has almost full dominance over the terrain as they can leap, dodge, pivot, climb, and dance around their opponent with great speed and precision. Their fighting style often involves wearing the enemy down with hit and run tactics, or getting them so frustrated they can't think straight.

                                Although Weresquirrels do not have a society based on pack unison, they do recognize that sometimes there is safety in numbers. When multiple Weresquirrels are in the area, they have been known to form a truce in the face of a greater threat. These truces usually have a few ground rules.

                                1 - Keep away from my hoard.

                                2 - This is an alliance of convenience. I don't trust you, and you'd be stupid to trust me!

                                3 - Our enemies travel in groups, so if we're going to survive, we need to work together for now.

                                Packs of Weresquirrels usually live a sizable distance away from each other. This is because just like regular squirrels, they have a tendency to steal from one another, which leads to infighting and territorial disputes. Often at times, these chaotic packs actually work for the same Totem, and their infighting provides it with greater power. When it comes to Weresquirrel packs, the one who is most favored by their Totem is usually the one with the largest hoard, which is another reason for their constant infighting as they try to prove themselves worthy of greater power and gifts.


                                Weresquirrels are known to hoard essences like they were giant acorns! Their dens often have at least one room stacked to the ceiling with essence that is offered as a sacrifice to their Totem, or used to feed their own powers. Even when a Weresquirrel is encountered by themselves, both they and their Totem have a massive supply of spiritual energy to draw upon, which allows them to fight longer and harder than other shifters who gather essence in moderation. In fact, when there is a pack of Weresquirrels, their Totem's supply of essence can seem limitless to an unsuspecting Uratha!

                                Like all Beasts of Vice, the Weresquirrels have extended lifespans beyond normal human capacity. They age at 1/3 the normal rate, physically aging by only 1 year for every 3 that passes by. An elder Weresquirrel can approach the early 300's in age, and during that time they often build up massive fortunes, usually by ill gotten means.


                                -------------------------------------------------


                                Appearance:

                                Human: The human form of a Weresquirrel is typically the same as most other humans, but has various quirks that make them stand out. Their eyes and head are constantly shifting to observe their surroundings. As if they are either search for something, or expecting to get robbed at any moment. They move with an uncanny sense of balance or grace, and might be talented acrobats either professionally or recreational.


                                Hybrid: Like the Werebunnies, the hybrid form of a Weresquirrel is surprisingly lithe and elegant in its design. They are more acrobatic than brutish and are typically faster than other shifters while in this form. The hybrid Weresquirrel is roughly the same size as their human form, although it loses excess fat and replaces it with athletic muscle tone. They gain the ability to use a claw attack, but their teeth are blunted and not effective at biting. One of its trademark characteristics is the bushy tail.


                                Animal: In their fully animal form, the Beast of Greedy takes on the form of a squirrel. Although they are too small and weak to be much use in combat, they are incredibly fast and agile. This form is primarily used for scouting, infiltration, and making quick escapes.


                                Near Human: In this form, the Beast of Greed's body shifts to become more nimbly built. Their body slims down and muscles strengthen to allow for advanced displays of acrobatics. Their nails extending into a pair of short (roughly 1/2 inch) claws and they might develop a pair of buck teeth.


                                Near Animal: In this form, the Beast of Greed takes on the form of a giant squirrel, roughly the size of a human. Although it is not quite as fast as the fully animal form, it is much stronger and therefor better suited to combat.



                                --------------------------------------------------


                                Reproduction: Unless it is to unite against a common foe, the only other time Weresquirrels gather together is for breeding purposes. They mate, they conceive, and they seperate while the mother goes onto raise the child and the father returns to his hoard. Like all Beasts of Vice, the children are born human, but may develop a stronger affinity to the spirit world than normal.

                                When a Weresquirrel mates with a human, there is a 1 in 3 chance of the child inheriting a profound manifestation of their parent's vice. This chance increases to 60% for each child if they mate with another weresquirrel. These children are usually prime candidates to be targeted by their Totem and become another Weresquirrel when they are old enough.

                                Because romance is pretty alien between two Weresquirrels, they have a mating ritual they perform when attempting to breed with their own kind. The Beasts of Greed hate surrendering anything freely, and for a female Weresquirrel, this includes their own body. The ritual involves the male having to chase down the desired female and is only allowed to mate with her if he can actually catch her. These chases can be lengthy processes as the Weresquirrels dash across roof tops, climb over fences, duck behind alleyways, and shift between their various forms to suit the situation. If they are witnessed conducting the ritual, many would mistake it as a parkour exercise, for it often requires incredible feats of athletic prowess and life risking jumps.

                                There is no actual time limit to how long the ritual lasts, but if the male loses the female's trail, or becomes too tired to continue the chase, he is usually considered to have failed. To begin the ritual, the male simply has to declare his intentions (an open area is considered more honorable) to the desired female, and the chase begins. If he fails to catch her, he can not initiate another challenge for an entire month. Hopefully, he'll use that time to improve his poor athletic performance!

                                To those who are not aware of the nature of this ritual, male Weresquirrels can very easily be mistaken for rapists, chasing a girl down and breeding her! As such, it is usually an unspoken curtesy that the ritual is not declared in public, where witnesses may get the wrong idea.


                                --------------------------------------------------------


                                Delusion: Kleptomania - Those who witness a Beast of Greed in their hybrid, near animal, near human forms, or while shifting will be stricken with an extremely powerful urge to hoard whatever they can get their hands on. This urge is so powerful that those afflicted by it will stop paying attention to the Weresquirrel and begin scavenging the area for trinkets, baubles, or whatever junk catches their attention, piling it up into a heap. By the time their Kleptomania wares off, they will have forgotten about the Weresquirrel as they try to figure out what to do with the items they've collected.

                                Rage: Death Rage - Weresquirrels guard their hoards with their lives, and will lash out at just about anyone who would dare steal from them.

                                Primal Urge: Hoarding - Beasts of Greed are driven to hoard what they own, especially when it comes to essences. They do not like to spend, and the idea of charity is abhorrent to them. A Beast of Greed must spend 1 willpower to offer their services to others when there is no immediate reward to be gained from such assistance. Additionally, they must save against a Death Rage if they catch anyone trying to steal from them.

                                            Beasts of Gluttony (Werelocusts): Before the First Change, Werelocusts are people who can't recognize that too much of a good thing is bad for you. While they fit the traditional ideal of Gluttony in that they are voracious eaters, they also fit its more abstract definitions. Drug addicts, party animals, thrill seekers, and even pain. Their insatiable appetites attracts spirits of gluttony who initiates the First Change either willingly or forcing it upon them.


                                            The First Change for a Werelocust is a shocking and dangerous event. They develop a mind racking and insatiable craving for something like those mentioned above. During their First Change, they will indulge themselves to the point of nearly lethal intakes of such substances. And when the First Change wears off, their body needs to purge what it can not handle. They may spend hours vomiting up half digested food, or going into shock from an overdose. Their nerves may be temporarily wrecked from performing so many dangerous stunts, requiring them to regenerate before they can regain full mobility again. Even with their new regenerative powers, it takes a day for their body to recover from the experience. It is an agonizing day of healing and purging toxins from the body, and some do not survive the traumatic experience. Those who do, however, are now Werelocusts.


                                            Werelocusts are voracious creatures both in body and in spirit. Among all Shapeshifters they are one of the most cursed, for they burn through Essence at an accelerated rate. Every action the Werelocust invokes that uses Essence costs 3X the regular amount. As such, they are constantly hungry for more. If left unchecked, a plague of Werelocusts can devour all the essence in a Loci in a matter of days, leaving it as a spiritually dead zone! This makes them an extreme threat in the eyes of the Uratha and must be dealt with immediately before they turn the Shadow into a barren wasteland!


                                            Werelocust Totems use Essence in much the same way one would use a treat for taming a dangerous animal. They dangle essence in front of the Werelocust and tell them they will be rewarded for performing a service, which the shifter usually agrees to. However, they are hungry again mere hours later, and the Totem undoubtedly has yet another task for them to complete.

                                            The insatiable appetites of the Werelocusts give rise to new spirits of Gluttony which their totem can devour, merge with, or bind into service in order to increase its own power. Like all Beasts of Vice, they have extended lifespans compared to normal humans. They age at 1/3 the normal rate, physically aging by 1 year for every 3 that goes by. An elder Werelocust can reach the early 300's near the end of their lifespan.



                                            ----------------------------------------------------------------

                                            Appearance:


                                            Human: Although appearing much the same way as their original human forms, a Werelocust's body starts to show the signs of excessive indulgence. Those who possess eating disorders are typically obese, while drug addicts have bodies that are shot halfway to hell with side effects and health conditions. Thrill seekers have scars across their body from a lifetime of constant injury and hospital visits. In all cases it becomes very clear that this person just doesn't know when to stop.


                                            Animal: Like their Wereslug cousins, the Werelocust does not change into a single animal, but actually becomes a whole swarm. In their fully animal form, the Werelocust dissolves into a swarm of 10 Locusts for each rank of spirit power they have achieved (maximum of 50 Locusts at rank 5). Their attributes and health boxes count for the entire swarm, not each individual locust. When all their health boxes are destroyed, the swarm is dead. However, as long as at least one locust survives, they can reform themselves. While in this form, the Werelocust gains the power to fly, greatly improving their mobility.


                                            Hybrid: In this form, the Werelocust takes the shape of an anthropomorphic insect with pincers erupting from their cheeks, wings growing from their backs, oversized eyes, antennae, and claw like hands and feet. However their most noticeable trait is the armored plated exoskeleton which provides them much greater protection than soft flesh. While in this form, the Werelocust's hands and feet lose several of their digits, replacing the five toes with two pronged talons, and sacrificing 2 fingers for 3 pronged claws including two large fingers and a thumb.


                                            Near Animal: In this form, the Werelocust takes on the shape of a gigantic locust roughly the size of their human form. They can fly in this form, and the vibrations of their wings produce a nearly deafening hum to those close by. Their body is covered in an armor-like carapace, increasing their defensive abilities, and they gain clawed appendages on their legs.


                                            Near Human: In this form, the Werelocust retains most of their human body, but gains a number of improvements that makes them better suited to combat. Their skin becomes glossy and harder than normal, but is not as heavily armored as their hybrid form. The nails on their fingers and toes also become sharp and barbed like the claws of an insect. Their eyes increase in size by roughly 40% and their voice has a buzzing echo when they speak.


                                            ---------------------------------------------------------


                                            Reproduction: Werelocusts are born as ordinary humans that were infused with the essence of a beast when they drew the attention of a Spirit of Gluttony. In this case, they were infused with the spirit of a locust, a creature who is infamous for its voracious appetite. Werelocusts also have the smallest population of out all the Beasts of Vice, because having a large number of them is extremely dangerous even for the very Totems who they serve.

                                            When a Werelocust produces a child with a human, there is a 1 in 3 chance the child will inherit a profound manifestation of their parent's Vice. If they produce a child with another Werelocust, this risk increases to 60% for each child produced. Regardless of who they mate with, the child is born as a human, but may have a higher affinity to the spirit world than most. Children who manifest Gluttony in such a way are more vulnerable to the Totem's influence and are often targeted sometime around puberty to be granted the First Change.


                                            Werelocusts have an nontraditional sense of marriage, as they do not enjoy the idea of being restricted to one lover. As Beasts of Gluttony, they are all about brutal excess. They want as much as they are capable of getting their hands on, and to this extent, they often indulge in polygamous relationships. Often one male will claim several females as his own, and vice versa when a female decides to have several males.


                                            Werelocust females are typically rare, this causes the males to fight over her when one appears. Appeasing her with gifts and other displays in an effort to win her affections and earn the right to breed her. Because of this, female Werelocusts are typically considered to be higher in status than males, at least in the sense that they are showered with praise from sexually repressed males.


                                            Because they are born as humans, a Werelocust pack can never be truly destroyed so long as the Totem remains. Even if every living member of the pack is killed, the Totem can simply create some new replacements. The only way to truly stop the Werelocusts from creating more of themselves is to destroy the Totem itself.


                                            ---------------------------------------

                                            Delusion: Withdrawal - Those who witness a Werelocust in their Hybrid, Near Human, or Near Animal forms or witness it shapeshifting are stricken with a near crippling sense of withdrawal symptoms. Whatever activity, substance, or food is the source of their cravings, they begin to suffer physical and psychological torment as they need to satisfy their craving immediately. They will often try to flee the area in an effort to satisfy their addition, and will likely forget about the Werelocust in the process as they are too distracted by the pain to focus their attention on the giant mutant insect.

                                            Favored Renowns:

                                            Favored Gifts:

                                            Rage: Voracious Rage - Just like a true locust, the Beasts of Gluttony are prone to devouring everything in sight, leaving nothing behind but a barren wasteland. When a Werelocust gives into their rage, they become a ravenous monster who attempts to consume all organic matter including people. Should an entire pack give into their rage, they can strip a victim down to the bones in seconds!

                                            Primal Urge: Addiction - Every Beast of Gluttony feels the compulsion to indulge themselves beyond healthy levels. While this stereotypically manifests in the form of eating disorders, this does not always have to be the case. Drugs, thrill seeking, video games, anything that they could build up a physical or psychological addiction to is a possibility. A Beast of Gluttony must devote no less than 1 hour equal to 1/2 of their Primal Urge to satisfying their addition. If they fail to do so, they will lose 1 Willpower for each hour they missed, when can not be regained until they have satisfied themselves. If their willpower reaches 0 from such a method, they will immediately fly into a Voracious Rage that lasts double their appropriate quota.



                                            Form Modifications:


                                            Human: None

                                            Animal: Dexterity +3, Stamina +1, Size -2, Initiative +1, Speed +3, Perception +4, Can fly at a rate equal to their running speed.

                                            Hybrid: Size +1, Strength +2, Dexterity -1, Stamina +2, Health +4, Initiative +1, Speed +2, Perception +2, Armor +5, Claw + Bite attacks inflict lethal damage

                                            Near Animal: Size +1, Strength +2, Dexterity -2, Manipulation -3, Health +3, Initiative +2, Speed +4, Perception +2, Jump +3, Can fly at a rate equal to their running speed, Armor +3. Opponents must make a fortitude save or be rendered temporarily deaf when the Werelocust is flying.

                                            Near Human: Strength +1, Stamina +2, Health +2, Perception +1, Manipulation -1, Armor +2, Unarmed attacks inflict lethal damage.

                                                Beast of Wrath (Werewolverine): Before the first change, Werewolverines are people who are prone to violent outbursts with or without provocation. They are destructive, impulsive, and exactly what a Spirit of Wrath is looking for in a shock trooper. They decide to invoke the First Change either willingly or unwillingly and turn the mortal into a weapon against the hated Werewolves.

                                                The First Change for a Werewolverine is mockingly similar to that of a Werewolf. They are overcome with a sense of rage that drives them into a killing frenzy. By the time the First Change wears off, the entire setting around the Beast of Wrath has been demolished. Tables are snapped in half, doors are ripped off their frames, broken glass litters the floor, and blood is caked upon the walls from victims who are too mutilated to identify anymore.


                                                Indeed, it seems that the Werewolverine's entire purpose is to mock the Uratha, even their very names sound familiar. Whatever the Werewolves possess, the Werewolverine has in gross exaggeration. They are faster, stronger, and more destructive than The People have been recorded. Power, brutality, and most importantly rage are coursing through these savage shapeshifters. More than any other Beast of Vice, the Beasts of Wrath are the most prone to taking on the Werewolves directly and they actually come out victorious on many occasions!

                                                Though true Wolverines are smaller than true wolves, their savagery has allowed them to fight off creatures much larger than themselves, including wolves. The Spirit of Wrath hopes that by harnessing such an animal, they can effectively fight off the Werewolves from enforcing their tyranny upon the spirit. It is not uncommon to hear that a Werewolverine is the biggest threat out of all the Beasts of Vice, at least in terms of offensive power.


                                                Although a Werewolverine didn't exactly have the most self control when they were human, they become practically barbarians after their First Change. The slightest grievance can set them off, which makes it extordinarily difficult for them to blend in with human society anymore. Even if they do manage to retain their composure and not shift on the spot, their victims are usually left crippled from such an encounter.

Initially, the Totem has to devour, fuse with, or bind spirits that are produced from the excess rage of their own servants.                                                 As with all Beasts of Vice, the Werewolverines have extended lifespans beyond the normal human capacity. Although they are not immortal, they age at 1/5 the normal rate, physically aging by 1 year for every 5 that goes by. This allows them to retain their youth and vigor for longer periods of time and near the end of their life spans they can approach 500 years of age.

                                                Animal: In their animal form, a Beast of Wrath takes the shape of a wolverine, a vicious little animal who is prone to violence.

                                                Hybrid: In their hybrid form, the Beast of Wrath remains largely close to their normal height, but they do become more built for combat. The majority of children who possess a Werewolverine parent are bastards who were conceived during a rape.
