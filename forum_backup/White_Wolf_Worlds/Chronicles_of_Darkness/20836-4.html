    Imperators-Rule by Law

    Since the beginning of time and the great explosion that led to the creation of our universe,the cosmos has been moving silently and eternally in the flow of certain principles,seen but not understandable to the human logic.The only common truth human beings can recognize in this,is that all was created to exist.Leave out one of the great cosmic laws and the world will crumble into nothingness.Laws-unwritten rules at first,helped the first man survive,create communities and become the dominant species to rule the entire planet,where other stronger species succumbed into oblivion.If it wasn't for those guiding lights and the ones who stepped in front and have the strength of will to impose them,humanity would have surrendered itself to nothingness as well.

    Without laws,everything is chaos.And chaos,called the hidden hand of God by some,is the opposite of order-the opposite of the steadiness that nurtures life itself.In chaos,there is no control.And what we cannot control,as shown by history itself,may very well become the source of our destruction.In chaos,no civilization or province can endure and an Imperator knows that better than anybody else.Embraced with the responsibility to avoid this anarchy,Imperators strive to embody and disseminate those sacred principles,to all those in her province.To be the law one must first understand it,and thus put aside personal interest for the greater benefit.That kind of confidence makes them prudent,but also swift and sure to their judgement.Justice and order must be preserved,and many of the Imperators go to the extreme to make sure that happens.Injustice won't be tolerated,and one who disagrees with that,should do well to hide it in the presence of an imperator,lest she finds herself at the end of her wrath.Many who do not,bitterly regret the decision they made.

    As personalities,Imperators tend to reflect their philosophy tenet in everyday aspects of their life,being strict to the point of absolute in their opinions,seldom though without any solid grounds to back them up.This kind of behavior marks them as cold and cruel many of the times,and inevitable arguments rise-especially with those they hold dear,being over-protective over them,or too arbitrary with conflicts of course,rising in return.That's not to say they are devoid of emotions,or that they cannot be sensitive and tender,only that they choose wisely when to express themselves that way.They do not let rush decisions and sentiments get the better of them,and as cold as that may seem to an outsider,an Imperator knows that is the way to a just society.The strictness and the punishments they inflict are not only on others but on themselves as well,surprisingly most of the times.They do not shy away from the wrong calls they may have made.Being a ruler,one must first rule himself and discipline comes very high among Imperators.That makes them perfectionists,and many of them find themselves on the edge of obsession,with many young Imperators developing some sort of O.C.D. in their early years.That makes for perfect Finders and Challengers,though other methods may embrace the philosophy as well.A Slayer,may for example come up with more violent solutions to uphold the law,while an Inheritor learns how to impose her will,with sheer dominant looks and a single word suffising to bring almost everyone to heel by breaking their spirit.

    Imperators fear the unknown,and thus learn quickly to observe facts and then acting,becoming as squared-logic as they can be(really annoying to other philoshophies most of the time!).Their constant vigilance makes them rarely surprised to danger,but the lack of facts can be their downfall,as without them any warning is most of the times discarded as gibberish.
    Surely,being orthologistically obsessed hardly makes them the best of company,but one that follows an Imperator soon adheres to the feeling of safety when she's around her.That's what is required first and foremost by a ruler,right?

        Imperators-Rule by Law

        After the spark, Zhu Long--dragon of the south--looked upon the universe and came to understand its principles; an inescapable set of laws to which all matter was forced to obey. These laws were beacons of warmth, remnants of the spark that sought to create, balance, and destroy. Beyond their scope was nothing more than a swirling abyss of nothing. Seeing fractal beauty in this patten, Zhu Long looked to the creatures that spawned from the spark. They too seemed to follow the pattern--those with rules thrived and those without collapsed into oblivion. Zhu Long decided that the greatest gift he could share was the knowledge of this universal truth, which he witnessed as he watched the spark. The Imperators are his heirs and share his aspiration--the supply and enforcement of law.

        Without laws, everything is chaos--the very brink of abyssal oblivion. In chaos, there is no control. In what cannot be controlled waits destruction. This is the core belief of the Imperators. Imperators strive to embody and disseminate these sacred principles. To be the law one must first understand it, thus one must put aside personal interest for the greater benefit. This confidence fills them with both prudence and vengeance. Justice and order MUST be preserved, even if such involves extreme measures or violence. Injustice will not be tolerated; those who disagree will face the wrath of an Imperator and her army.

        Imperators reflect their philosophy in their everyday routine and composure, being strict and stubborn to the point of implacability. This behavior marks them as cold and cruel; though an Imperator is not incapable of emotion she is very calculated in social exchanges, carefully suppressing her mental state to maintain authoritative control. They do not let sentiments get the better of them and their decisions are rarely rash; an Imperator knows that an appropriately tuned poker face is essential to negotiation. This is a useful skill as a member of the Oroboroi, her self-discipline has taught her when using Ablutions would be frivolous and she knows to deal with mortal witnesses sooner rather than later.

        The strict expectations an Imperator holds do not only apply to her subjects but to herself as well. She requires excellence in all matters, anything less leaves cracks in her walls through which chaos might slip. After all, it was the laziness of the earliest Oroboroi that led to their downfall. Imperators fear the unknown and thus learn quickly to observe before acting. The danger of counterattack must always be assessed. Their constant vigilance leaves them rarely surprised, but an unreliable network can be their downfall.

        Imperators favor hoards as their primary means of replenishing Breath: sufficient wealth can bribe even the most principled of dignitaries and can hire an impressive force of mercenary might. Weapons are a popular choice, as they can always be plucked from the collection in a pinch. As for an Imperator’s province, it in many ways resembles a police state. Security and safety are more important than personal freedoms; the boundaries of the province are heavily monitored by an Imperator (or her trusted subordinates) and those entering and residing are subject to her forceful regime. Imperators are of the opinion they know what’s best for everyone, and being dragons they have the power to impose that opinion on others. Imperator provinces favor traditional geographic boundaries, such as city blocks.

        Being obsessed with protocol makes for unpleasant friendships; other Oroboroi are often annoyed with the seemingly unnecessary precautions that an Imperator enlists. Still, they run a tight ship and that security can be comforting, especially against the paranoia of Heart loss. An Oroboros may be driven to den with an Imperator after she suffers a serious military setback, such as loss of territorial claim or even injury from a mundane source like a mugging. An Imperator can supply the structure necessary to reduce crime in a province and has the dedication to assist in the vicious pursuit of a wrongdoer.

                        Originally posted by FallenEco View Post
                        ...And now I have a template for the Savant Philosophy. Thank you, zmajček!

                        Actually, giving my thoughts on the Teachers/Savants, it would be a good idea to catch up on the Breath restoration options...what makes a Maiden (say a TA) different from a Province, (say her/his students).
                        Okay, there are three traditional sources of Breath in the game (I call the sources Assets in the manual). A dragon spends points in an asset to draw Breath, just as if she were spending Willpower. When all the available points of an asset are depleted, she cannot draw more Breath from it until she spends some time with that asset (usually a scene), at which point all of the points affiliated are replenished. As for the assets themselves:

                        A hoard is a purely physical asset: the Oroboros draws nourishment from the economic and sentimental value of the objects. For a hoard, the dots of asset refer to the net size/net worth of the objects.

                        Example hoards: Musical Instruments (3 dots), Bottle Caps (1 dot), Medieval Weaponry (5 dots). The Musical Instruments might be on display in a single room of the Oroboros' residence, and while possibly considered eccentric the collection is not unreasonable. The bottle caps might be confined to a single container, such as a jar or box, and could be reasonably explained away as a hobby. The Medieval Weaponry hoard, however, is so tremendous and rare that it collects in piles all around the Oroboros' residence, similar to the mythical hoards of gold that might be depicted in draconic imagery. Hoards of sizes larger than five become even more ridiculous, and by 10 dots they rival Smaug's oceans of gold from the Hobbit (Note to self, list the Hobbit under sources).


                        A maiden is a purely social asset: the Oroboros draws nourishment from the subject's talent and also from the act of 'caging' the subject, be it by hindering advancement in her career or legitimately towering her away from society. For a maiden, the dots of asset refer to the extent of the Oroboros' attachment to the maiden and consequently the degree to which the maiden is isolated.

                        Example maidens: Jessica [That singer at the parlor] (3 dots), Jim [Who serves me Coffee] (1 dot), Francis [My towered princess] (5 dots). Jessica could have the potential to make a measurable career out of her talents, but the Oroboros that claims her as a maiden purposely polices talent scouts AWAY from her so that she will stay with the parlor. Jim is a friendly, but possibly only politely so, acquaintance that the Oroboros insists on interacting with every time they meet, though there is no active sabotage of his career. Francis, on the other hand, has become so dear to the Oroboros that she has been removed from society--or 'towered'--her only interactions are with the Oroboros, other maidens that have been towered, and possibly other Oroboroi in the den (the last is quite unlikely). At even higher dots the towering becomes more and more extreme, and by 10 dots the only living being the maiden is allowed to see and interact with is the Oroboros handler.
                        In the case of your TA, this might involve purposely losing paperwork related to the TA's advancement or in extreme cases even sabotaging his thesis.


                        A Province is the compromise between the two, being partly social and partly physical: the Oroboros draws nourishment from the betterment of a society or sub-culture. This often involves staking claim to the sub-culture, either openly or at least mentally, and then working to improve its stature in the world. Province asset dot ratings are a combination of size and prosperity.

                        Example provinces: The Coffee Shop (3 dots), Theater in the Park (1 dot), The City on the Bay (5 dots). 3 dots in a province represents significant emotional investment in the property; the Oroboros is determined to see it succeed. In the case of the coffee shop, the Oroboros likely makes it her business to ensure sales are always high and customers are always satisfied with their purchase and experience. 1 dots represents a passing fancy in the topic. For the theater in the park, perhaps holding a season ticket and soaking in the atmosphere of the scene is enough. 5 dots represents an extreme dedication to the region; for the city of San Francisco the Oroboros likely works diligently to ensure emergency service is never interrupted, tourism is flourishing, and the soul of the community is kept healthy and expanding. Provinces of higher dots increase in geographic scope, and at 10 dots can represent entire continents. It might seem like provinces are the hardest Breath asset to replenish, but considering their size individual refresher visits can be quite brief (stopping in for a cup of coffee and a chat would be enough to replenish the Coffee Shop asset).
                        In the case of your students, giving a lecture would probably be enough to refresh the asset, but to grow its dot rating the Savant Oroboros would really have to strive to inspire the students, to the point that they would spread her message to others.


                        There is another asset as well, which all Oroboroi are entitled to utilize. That is the asset of the Heart itself. It has a rating equal to the dragon's dots of Furnace and can be refreshed by spending time within hearing distance of its beat. As such, even if the dragon somehow has purged her other assets she always has at least one source of Breath.



                        If by chance an Oroboros cannot access her assets for some time, due to theft or geographic distance, she may still refresh them by two means: Consuming livestock will clear up a single point of Breath, and consuming human flesh will refresh an entire asset of the dragon's choice (It is best to empty the big ones with this, as it is a violation of the Mandate).



                        To spend Breath, an Oroboros reflects on the asset with which it is affiliated and draws strength (no, not "S"trength!) from the bond she shares with the asset.
                        I should also clarify on fossilization, as it relates to the Breath an Oroboros must spend each day. The furnace is strained by hiding and revealing divinities and must be stoked with sentimental fuel to compensate. Thus each day the Oroboros must spend an amount of breath equal to the HIGHEST degree of divinity she achieved the previous day (each degree of divinity represents 5 points manifest, so if she got up to 15 points manifest the previous day she must spend 3 Breath to resist fossilization). It might sound steep, but considering how quickly Breath can be recovered this is designed intentionally as a deterrent against all-out burnout.
                        So what happens if she doesn't pay? For each point of Breath the Oroboros owes but does not spend with her first meal of the day, she suffers a point of fossilization... by which I mean she loses a DOT of Health. These dots can only be recovered by the expenditure of experience; the Oroboros literally has to rip away the damaged tissue and then grow replacement tissue using the furnace.



                        I'm considering re-introducing Ideologies as an asset for Breath recovery, mainly because I think they would work well with the idea for Caitiffs I have rolling around in my head. For ideologies, the Breath is refreshed when the Oroboros refrains from action because it would conflict with her beliefs. Like a ban, these ideologies become increasingly difficult to avoid the higher their dot rating becomes (so players can't just take the ideology "what I was going to do anyway" as a quick Breath reserve; it has to be something the Oroboros would legitimately pause to consider).




                        I also have a reply for Pandalfino but I'm going to place it in a separate post.


                        EDIT: Come to think of it, given the creation myth I will be attaching to the game it is entirely appropriate to return the Ideology asset to the game and mention it as the primary source of Breath for the VIZIERS, who are highly opinionated about the world and how nations should be run, or possibly the IMPERATORS who are so dedicated to justice. That would give five types of assets total: Province, Maiden, Hoard, Ideology, Heart. It has a nice symmetry, don't you think?

                                        This is the Paragon philosophy from my pov,I hope you like it(though I may edit it later).This time however,I demand an introductory text alteration about the philosophy progenitor(Yes,this I will leave to you CJC)

                                        Paragons-Rule by Example

                                        Leadership is an attitude.It is about values and priorities measured in actions,not empty words or hollow statements.To be a Paragon is to embrace that attitude.For her,leadership comes across as a state of being,a way to approach life.Paragons act where others would consider even talking,placing themselves in the eye of the storm,thus displacing the fear and hesitation of those around them.Enacting the influence their actions make as self-leaders,they do not look for followers;they make followers look for them.It is through the uniqueness of their own dreams that Paragons become a fountain of hope and inspiration for those around them,pushing themselves to the limit to prove that only through affecting,one can face life.

                                        Paragons are well-known as passionate and warm-hearted people,with the word "over" accompanying almost every adjective they are adressed with.They also tend to be caring and tender,choosing to believe in others more than they should.Living life to its fullest,they embrace every experience like it's their last.Fearing that the time they have in front of them is not enough(quite an irony for an Oroboros),they become anxious and obsessed with time itself.Driven into frustration,they have several outbursts targetting the wrong people.Hot-headed as they can be,Paragons frequently empathise with the people in their province,grasping a hold of the situation at hand quickly and act even faster,running the danger of recklessness all too often,drawing unwanted attention so they can save time.That underlines them as risky,though in endangering themselves they find themselves in the receiving end of the trust of their social circles.

                                        Testing themselves is part of their everyday life;that is the way confidence is built and transmitted.Confidence is hope and the gravest sins indifference,and selfishness.It was those two that led Dominion to its end,claim the Paragons,and the abuse of power.Paragons now know there is difference between commanding and leading,and those that fail to comprehend it are pitied,ignored or even dealt with by force.Altruism and charity are nobleties forgotten in today's world,and Paragons know that the clock is ticking for the few remaining embers as well,so they give it all they can,in hope of humanity repaying the debt on itself.After all,most of the Paragons find comfort in themselves,for without inner strength they would not have gone far from the start.

                                        Paragons spend a lot of time among people,motivating them and nurturing enduring ideals in their hearts and minds.Needless to mention that most often they replenish Breath out of the sheer respect bred by their maidens,though objects with certain emotional value make for great hoards as well.Their provinces most often have a loose organisation,and a warm atmosphere with friendly faces popping up more often than any other place.That does not imply they lack safety;people have learned to step up after the Paragon,and idealism does not allow room for cowardice.Paragons are the first to confront anyone who does not abide or trying to disrupt peace in their close environment.Geographically,Paragons' preferences lie in suburban or even rural areas,where communities are close and people have an easier time trusting one another,resembling more of a family and the warmth Paragons revel in.

                                        Their passion and energy,bears a strong impact and most of the times gets fellow Oroboroi divided;you either love a Paragon or Hate her.In a Den however,even the latter come to the aid of the Paragon during a crisis,knowing that he may very well be the first to lay his life in the line for them,and the last to back down from a threat.Reliability is the synonym for them in Oroboroi circles,and that bears enough gravity for Paragons to have the first say in a Den.After all,True leadership comes in the form of service.

                                            Wow Pandalfino, your Paragon is perfect!

                                            Here is the story of the five original dragons. You will recognize Zhu Long's as his is unchanged.

                                            Imperators, followers of the Fire Dragon of the South
                                            After the spark, Zhu Long--dragon of the south--looked upon the universe and came to understand its principles; an inescapable set of laws to which all matter was forced to obey. These laws were beacons of warmth, remnants of the spark that sought to create, balance, and destroy. Beyond their scope was nothing more than a swirling abyss of nothing. Seeing fractal beauty in this pattern, Zhu Long looked to the creatures that spawned from the spark. They too seemed to follow the pattern--those with rules thrived and those without collapsed into oblivion. Zhu Long decided that the greatest gift he could share was the knowledge of this universal truth, which he witnessed as he watched the spark. The Imperators are his heirs and share his aspiration--the supply and enforcement of law.

                                            Paragons, followers of the Wood Dragon of the East
                                            After the spark, Quing Long--dragon of the east--looked upon the universe and was enraptured by the flash; he watched as the brilliant light permeated every object and organism, imbuing it with magnificence. Fascinated, Quing Long began to correlate the brightest lights with the greatest potential; it was the light that made life so special. Quing Long was particularly intrigued by the trees, whose sole purpose appeared to be the spread of life, light, and beauty. He saw promise in this beauty… the promise of a universe at peace, following the example of trees in bloom. Quing Long decided that the greatest gift he could share was this perspective, which he gained after witnessing the spark. The Paragons are his heirs and share his wisdom--the need to lead by example.

                                            Savants, followers of the Water Dragon of the North
                                            After the spark, Xuan Long--dragon of the north--looked upon the universe and saw beauty in its chaos; he was astounded that even the tiniest variation in circumstance could have widespread consequence. Like the waters of a stream grinding great boulders into dust, Xuan Long saw chaos as a force that could topple even the greatest of beings. To control chaos… to shape and direct it to one’s own whim… to accept it as the inescapable champion of reality… he recognized such was the ultimate expression of enlightenment. Xuan Long decided that the greatest gift he could share was the knowledge of this universal truth, which he witnessed as he watched the spark. The Savants are his heirs and share his aspiration--the breeding and acceptance of chaos.

                                            Viziers, followers of the Metal Dragon of the West
                                            After the spark, Bai Long--dragon of the west--looked upon the universe and was blinded by the flash; he was left groping in the dark and in despair over the loss. Deprived of the sense he used his snout to detect his surroundings, experiencing the universe through odor like no other dragon could. Bai Long was offended by the smell of gold, it reeked of entitlement and sloth. Conversely he was drawn to the smelting odor of iron. It was the scent of the common man. It was the scent of determination. Bai Long saw hope in these beings… the hope that he might someday witness the universe through his eyes and not his nose. For only the blind truly know what the blind need most. Bai Long decided that the greatest gift he could share was this perspective, which he gained after witnessing the spark. The Viziers are his heirs and share his wisdom--the need to relate to the common man.

                                            Caitiffs, inheritors of the Earth Dragon of the Center
                                            Huang Long--dragon of the center--looked upon the darkness and despaired at its emptiness. All that was and all that ever would be was herself and her four brothers. The others seemed content with the arrangement, but Huang Long sorrowed at the circumstance. She longed to nurture, but the extent of reality’s potential had already been spent on her and her dragon kin. The only hope of creating anything else waited in the destruction of one of her siblings. But Huang Long was honorable: she could not bring herself to harm any of them. Instead, she made the supreme sacrifice. Praying to the light within her Heart, she erupted in a cataclysm of creation: the spark, and from her formed the entirety of the universe. The earth, the stars, the trees, the rocks, mankind--all are a piece of the center dragon Huang Long. Caitiffs in particular are inheritors of her spirit; despite her absence they receive the message of Huang Long, the call to their supreme duty. It is the duty of each Caitiff to sacrifice herself for the sake of the universe. But knowing that this death is inevitable, and that is must come of their own volition, is enough to drive a Caitiff to despair. It is no wonder they flee their destiny.

                                                        https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B31...it?usp=sharing

Some of the pages may have unfortunate arrangements because of this, and the pages that have numbers are most certainly going to be the wrong numbers. Rest assured the final version I assemble will have much better presentation than this, including sub-section headings in the footers.

                                                        Also, if you go into the first and second chapter you will see my mediocre artwork has begun to work its way into the document! If you don't like them... well, you can always commission a professional for artwork and send me the information. Be sure you get their copyright release, though, I don't want to get into a tangle over something like that.

                                                        Points of interest: I've adjusted Ablutions so that the use of Breath is optional. By contrast to earlier versions of the template they may seem VERY powerful, and in fact they probably are. I'm tired of balancing dragons against other creatures, I'm only interested in balancing dragons against each other. You'll also note that the number of Edicts has gone down significantly: it is quite an endeavor making sure each law is compatible with each other law.
