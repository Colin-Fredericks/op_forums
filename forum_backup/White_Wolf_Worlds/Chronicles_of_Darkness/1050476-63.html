        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
        The Duration says "One chapter," but also I don't think anything particularly breaks for Speed/Rewind to be persistent effects, given that Changeling Hours is a Royal Contract and they're literally just affecting a limited amount of Structure damage.
        Not a bad read, though it feels a bit cheap to be able to just fix things for free/a discount (like a house or a car) if you know the name of the previous owner. I have a Wizened Changeling who has an initial character thrust of fixing up a classic Volkswagen beetle his grandparents had promised him, only for him to discover upon escaping Arcadia his Fetch had gotten the car and wrecked it, leaving it in the scrapyard for his Changeling self to find (though not intentionally of course). The Changeling manages to (barely) drive it out through Dance Of The Toys and Blessing Of Perfection, at least for my intended backstory; it feels kind of token if I could have him just take Changeling Hours, fix it up while chanting his grandparents’ names and then literally drive it out like it’s fresh off the production line.

        Granted, players are more likely to enjoy having smaller items fixed easily without having to go to the trouble of getting a replacement or specialty tools, rather than for the missed RP and story benefits of having to do the actions themselves to make something lasting. I suppose it’s something that could work differently on individual tables, with different Seemings allowing for permanence to fill in for working in perpetuity rather than just a single scene.

        Thanks for the swift reply Satchel!

            Originally posted by Taidragon View Post
            Not a bad read, though it feels a bit cheap to be able to just fix things for free/a discount (like a house or a car) if you know the name of the previous owner.
            It's one to five points of Structure damage, rising as high as ten points if you're an extremely good and extremely magical craftsman, carried out at a rate of one point per turn.

            it feels kind of token if I could have him just take Changeling Hours, fix it up while chanting his grandparents’ names and then literally drive it out like it’s fresh off the production line.
            The reading I have where these are lasting effects is also the reading where your Crafts rating is the limit of the Contract's ability to alter the object's Structure; a temporary effect would see the two clauses essentially reverse themselves at the end of the chapter.

            The same investment of resources in Royal Jewels lets you jury-rig a rocket launcher out of vaguely appropriate materials for free as long as you're wearing old glasses and using old tools. I'm not too worried about Powerful Faerie Magic making it trivial to fix completely mundane damage to precious family heirlooms; you have to learn that Contract somehow, and that knowledge affects the story by where you got it and who knows you have it, among other things.
