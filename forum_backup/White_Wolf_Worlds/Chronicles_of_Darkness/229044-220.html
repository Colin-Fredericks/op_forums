                                                        I'd like to start by saying I'm fairly new to the nWoD setting. I've read up a bit about Changeling the Lost, but that's more or less the limit of my knowledge of the setting. A friend of mine who knows a great deal more about the setting showed me the original introduction to beast and I was quite excited. After reading through the Homecoming and the various types of families and hungers I thought "Here's a chance for me to explore the setting as someone just coming in to it, both in and out of character."

                                                        With the Homecoming it seemed, to me at least, that the beasts as a whole didn't have an over-arching goal other than survival. As far as I was concerned that was great, it meant a group of players could have much more diverse goals working within the same brood. Some of the players may want to use their new found powers for personal gain, others may want to actually do some good with them, and others still might just try to survive while doing as little harm as possible, and all of these goals would be equally valid.

                                                        Now the Homecoming has been changed to the Devouring, and I feel that freedom of choice has been largely limited. Rather than coming in to your own as a beast and figuring out who you are, and what you want to do with the power, it seems more like you've been given a night job. I'll grant there's still room for choice when it comes to what type of beast you want to be, and what sort of methods you're going to use, but if you're playing by the rules the Horror has set for you, everyone is going to have the same end goal aren't they? Now rather than my character deciding what it means to be a beast and choosing their own path, they just decide whether to do a good job or not.

                                                        I know that previously some of the other people interested in this setting had been complaining that the heroes seemed too one dimensional, but I'm not sure that it's wrong if that's the case. It seems proper to me that for a story told from the Beasts perspective the hero comes about because of the beast. This isn't the Hero's story after all, history is generally written by the victors, and we have plenty of examples of the Hero's stories.

                                                        As it stands at present I'm no longer certain if the game is something I'll want to back, as the direction of the game seems to have changed fairly dramatically with the addition of a goal given to all Beasts.
