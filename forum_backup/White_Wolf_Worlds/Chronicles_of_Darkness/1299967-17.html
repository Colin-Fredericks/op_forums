    I get what the intent is. It's to replenish resources for spenditures, as you say. But Beasts have Satiety Conditions that adjust based on what value the Beast is currently at with their Satiety. And when a Beast hits Satiety 10, they get the Slumbering Condition immediately. This means that the Beast loses access to all of their Atavisms and Nightmares. The Beast is also explicitly restricted from spending Satiety for any reason.

    So, it's very bad news for a Beast. Especially for a Beast that would associate with the False.

    I don't think this power is meant to be something that screws over the wielder as hard as it does for Beasts.

    Edit: Basically, it looks like the writer just knew that Beasts spend Satiety as their resource and went &quot;Well, they get fill up with that then!&quot; without knowing what that actually means for the mechanics of the game. And okay, it's hard to know all the details about all the different gamelines. But Guest might want to double-check this as to whether it's appropriate as this is a great time to do corrections if they feel something needs to be adjusted, which I think is in their discretion.
    That does appear to be a genuine oversight, yeah, probably due to time constraints and a lapse in proof reading.

    On the other hand, this Vector does deal with the Contagion, so perhaps is was intentionally done this way, to show that one CAN get screwed over royally.

    But.. I’m not really sure this is a particularly bad thing. Then again, I am biased against Beasts, so take that with a handful of Salt!

                                                I love the vectors, a lot of fun, but a few thoughts.

                                                Evolve can lead to some seriously weird out comes. Like a Hunter Embracing someone as a Vampire, or making them their Ghoul. A Changeling casting powerful blood sorcery magic like creating a Blood God using their Glamour pool, or making Tass out of their Glamour pool. Glamour is WAY more plentiful and accessible then any other magical resource, a mage could end up with a huge mana/tass stockpile that way. A Sin Eater could lend out its ability to come back from dying to say Deviant. A magic would lend its ability to summon supernall entities to a Vampire, which I figure would be very confusing to it. It just gets weirder from there.

                                                Contagion vector would be super tempting for Ordo Dracul Vampires especially, no more worrying about Sunlight, the ability to shift away contagion, feed upon the enviroment itself, and more. Its the kind of liberty from the Vampire weaknesses they dream of.

                                                Also a True Fae False is scary, no vulneriblity to iron.

                                                Hobgoblins would also love it, as of all fae they have the hardest time gaining Glamour, just drain it out of their enviroment.

                                                I think alot of creatures are going to be attracted to the False not just out of fear of the Contagion, but because alot of the powers seemed to protect them from the weaknesses and suffering of their own template.

                                                I think a Changeling will be particularly attracted to the powers of the Jeremaid, the low level powers help with clarity/sanity, which can be problem for Changelings and those around them. And the higher level powers give them unique magics to fight the True Fae with, ones immune to iron.

                                                Also any none intergrator demon that sees Godhead abused to the point that the user gets absorbed by the Godmachine is going to concider the Jeremiad a dangerous enemy and servant of the Godmachine (which it kind of is, in a very weird way).

                                                Also Modern Miracle is unclear in it's abilities and limitations of what it can do. Like could it raise all allies in the area from the dead like a mass resurrect spell, while just automatically killing all none infected enemies? And the cost is too high, it's powerful I think, but not only do you use it only once, period, bit it cost a Willpower Dot AND a Sworn XP.

                                                Godhead is interesting, but it should be make clear that the self inflicted damage heals like normal wounds do on mortals, it's just immune to magical healing. Otherwise it sounds like permenant damage.

                                                And is it intended that you aren't just immune to wound penalties, but actual new damage (excluding the damage Godhead itself deals to you) as well. Like if while using Godhead someone one shots you at point blank range, it deals no health damage?

                                                Also two things are mentioned, but not explained, Sophia and Agora (I assume Sophia is just the characters religion/philosophy, I could be wrong).

                                                Also I could see Seekers of the Throne and Circle of the Crone as being just as attracted to the Jeremaid as Lanca Santum, they have their own religious beliefs.
