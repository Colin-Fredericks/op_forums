        What would you make Frank Underwood and Elementary's Sherlock Holmes?
        Frank made me think Ventrue Invictus more than once, what with the focus on power and power structures. And I think I wouldn't give him Majesty (or at least, very little). More than once he forcefully makes people bow to his will, which feels more Dominate-ish to me. He is less "like me" and more "you need me, now DO WHAT I SAY".

        Sherlock would make a fine Ugallu Nemesis if he went more often for the scaring routine, imo. However, the way he is, I propose something maybe a bit daring: a Wizened Changeling. Someone strangely obsessed with solving riddles, nimble and smart, he was taken by the Fae Moriarty. His Durance lasted for six months in the real world, and only through his superior perception and mind was he able to flee. There was no fetch left behind, and when he came back, he covered it up with a story about drug abuse and subsequent withdrawal.

            Virtue:             Courageous
            Vice: Wrathful

            Merits: Danger Sense 2, Air Of Menace 2, Iron Will 2, Brute Force 4, Relentless Assault 3, Defensive Combat 1 (Brawl), Unarmed Defense 5

            Health: 129 (Size 110)
            Willpower: 26
            Defense: 10 (+3 With Unarmed Defense)
            Initiative: 11
            Speed: 123
            Armor: 28
            Potency: 9
            Dread Powers: Breath Weapon*, Influence (Destruction 1). Fearless*, Fire Elemental, Madness And Terror, Numen (Awful Presence*, Weather Control* [Earthquake, Inferno]), Titanic, Toxic 2, Unnatural Healing*

            Weapons/Attacks
            Natural Weaponry [+18L Melee; 26 dice]
            Atomic Breath [+18L or +20L Ranged; 25 dice, Range 400/800/1200]

            *Awful Presence: Anyone who can directly perceive Godzilla who has less Willpower dots than his Potency suffers the effect of the Insensate Tilt for the rest of the scene, unless they succeed on a Resolve + Composure roll with a penalty equal to Godzilla's Potency/2 round up. Supernatural powers used to counter the Tilt, meanwhile, trigger a Clash of Wills, with Godzilla rolling Presence + Potency

            *Breath Weapon: Horrors with this Dread Power can command the elements to destroy their enemies, often literally breathing gouts of fire or hurling bolts of lightning. Choose the type of attack when this Dread Power is selected; this is typically an element such as acid, fire, lighting or cold, but other, more exotic elements might be used depending on the nature of the creature. By spending 1 point of Willpower, the Horror can blast individual targets in range (5/10/15) that counts as a ranged attack with a thrown weapon and requires a roll of an appropriate dice pool – the opponent’s Defense. This attack has a weapon bonus of +0L. By expending 3 Willpower, the weapon bonus increases to +2L and inflicts the Burning Tilt. In Godzilla's case, the dice pool is Stamina + Athletics + Atomic Breath Specialty, and the Range is 400/800/1200.

            *Fearless: Godzilla's mindset does not recognize fear in most situations and he is immune to any fear causing supernatural phenomena. Likewise, any method of supernatural coercion that works on humans or animals automatically fails as well.

            *Unnatural Healing: Godzilla heals all bashing and lethal damage at the start of each turn.

            *Weather Control: Typically possessed by entities of Rank 3+, this Numen allows the entity to summon a brief but powerful weather storm. The Numen costs 3 Essence to activate. If successful, the entity may impose an Environmental Tilt over an area with a radius of up to [Power x 100] yards until the end of the scene. This area is treated as an Intensity 2 Extreme Environment. An Exceptional Success creates an Intensity 3 Extreme Environment instead. The entity may use their Influence to further augment the weather storm’s size, duration and Intensity (where appropriate). This Numen only creates one type of Environmental Tilt, but may be purchased multiple times.

                                            Having been watching Jojo's Bizarre Adventure a lot recently, I can't help but imagine it as a particularly gonzo Chronicle that transitions from a Hunter game that focuses on hunting vampires to a Mage game.

                                            Basically, for the uninitiated, Jojo follows members of the Joestar family over the course of generations and the, well, bizarre adventures they have. Parts 1 and 2 revolved around their respective Joestars and their allies using solar-powered martial arts (it's anime, roll with it) to fight vampires, but Part 3 introduced Stands, which are basically (generally) anthropomorphic manifestations of a person's will that grant them supernatural powers. The best part is that Stands are invisible to all but people who possess them, which mirrors Nimbus and Sleepers' inability to process magic. Stands are also given names, which could easily translate to a Shadow Name. The abilities are pretty consistent and predictable, and probably correspond to Praxes. Alternatively, Stand-users could be Proximi, since their abilities often come with severe limitations of applicability. It would be way too long to try and list every Stand and its user, but to give people an idea, here's the first Stand and User we meet in the anime:

                                            Jotaro Kujo
                                            Shadow Name: Platinum Star
                                            Path: Thyrsus (Life 3, Time 5)
                                            Praxes: Body Control (Life 2), Heightened Senses (Life 2), Honing the Form (Life 3), Transform Life (Life 3), Acceleration (Time 3), Temporal Pocket (Time 5)

                                            Jotaro's Stand, Star Platinum, starts off with abilities that correspond well to Life magic: super-strength, super-speed, and heightened motor control and senses. His personality is dominant, but he is often cool under pressure and often displays surprising cunning, reflecting the bestial and unrelenting nature of some Thyrsus. Eventually, Jotaro discovers that Star Platinum also has the ability to stop time for the space of a few seconds, and eventually comes to rely on it as his main ability. At this point, Time magic can better represent the super-speed abilities that Star Platinum possesses. One could also make the argument that Star Platinum and all other Stands are actually Spirits, in which case Jotaro has Spirit 4, and Star Platinum is a Rank 2 Hope or Strength Spirit.

                                                            Santa Claus

                                                            ​He is a True Fae with his own snowy domain in Arcadia. He kidnaps humans en-masse to work as "Little helpers" making toys, for a purpose no one can even fathom. He sometimes travel around in a reindeer sleigh. The changelings who escape from his realm report a hellish landscape of false happiness and childish joy mixed in with horrible suffering as a Little helper (slave) in his Court. He is a rather strong fae and his visage and legend has travelled and taken root in the dreams of children everywhere and thereby rooted itself in western culture as well, but no one knows why he creates so many toys or why he is so fixated with children.
