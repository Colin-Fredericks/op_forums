    Making A Gangrel for Vampire Requiem 2E Blood and Smoke and need advice.

    04-13-2016, 08:47 PM
    We get to start off with 10 merit points and a covenant and I would like advice on how to build a powerful Gangrel and to be able to control my coterie as I chose that whole Land Lord bloodline. Like what kind of Disciplines, What Merits, and whats a great covenant. Tell me what yo uthink id appreciate it.

    More on character

    Name: Neith
    Mask: Authoritarian
    Dirge: Monster
    Concept: The huntress/The Landlord
    Clan: gangrel
    Bloodline: Annunaku though can change.
    Neith was born to her parents on December 8, 1995 in snowy Lewiston Maine. As a child she was always abrasive, a bit rebellious, but cared for her parents deeply. Her parents where well set one an accountant the other a nurse, but her father also had another love which he helped place with his daughter... Hunting. Hunting was a favorite past-time of his and to the young and often distant and abrasive Neith equaled to a fine outlet.
    Together they would bring down elk, deer, and other various animals with Neith embracing her more devious nature. She would always savor skinning the kill, always grin whilst a bullet left the rifle, and always feel warm if she only wound the animal. Something about the pain always made her feel superior, though much to the chargin of her father who recognized her issues.
    It wasn't easy, but she eventually agreed to start seeing a therapist to help talk about her issues, though she didn't seem to think she had any. Often they would chat, and often the therapist would come to the same conclusion, Neith was a sociopath. This never troubled her, and she never took any of the medications or the recommendations he would give, but she would talk to him regardless due to her parents insistence.
    Slowly, but surely years flew by as the young huntress grew, and her appetite for better game increased as animals stopped having the same effect on her. She no linger could feel the warmth she would get from the kill, the blood from the animals no longer flowed correctly, and just the feeling was off. Though, she would never tell her father that, she could never upset him that there father-daughter outings were becoming boring.
    But, the glory years were not to last. One night upon returning home from another failed job she was alarmed to see the door smashed open. She rushed in only to find her father and mother maimed and drained on the kitchen floor, a beast above them with a grin.
    What she didn't know was that her father had essentially refused a rather high rank noble of a local Kindred court when it came to finances and as a response that lord sent a hound.
    Faced to face with Ralo she steeled herself, her anger overwhelming, and her resolve against him set. he charged only for her to draw a knife and pierce through his forehead. She smirked only for it to drop as he laughed at the superficial wound. Standing up right he pulled the knife out and tossed it aside, but she did not back up as she told him to leave her home. He announced that his lord had gifted him this new territory and that the mortals inside where his to decide with, and that death was all that awaited mortals in his domain.
    She fought refusing to yield, and she got a beating for it. Breaking, and bloody she fought on refusing to yield to the beast. Every strike she inflicted only showed defiance as she kept trying to beat him, only to fail in her attempts. She started to feel her life leave her as she was finally forced to her knee's yet her gaze burned with hatred. Though as she blacked out, her wounds taking a toll she heard words that would forever haunt her "It's not your time yet Pup.''

    When she next awoke it was no longer amongst the living, she was now a member of the damned. Her sire the one who had killed her family had decided to turn her into a vampire, her defiance earning his respect. She hated him for every new moment she had.
    She was forced to accompany him on hunts which no longer had the same meaning as her father's, forced to deal with his occasional gropes which made her snarl, and forced to deal with the beast under her skin which only sought to bring her darkness out from its cage. Though she did find new solace in her new form, her anger, her lust for violence, and her rage were all brought forward and expressed as she killed now.
    Though she had to escape her sire, the few months she spent with him were intolerable, no they were insufferable. When she managed to finally get away she left quickly, remembering a phrasing of his he couldn't stand the UK and short on ideas she went to London.

    She no longer can stand playing subservient, and more however she has also embraced her change, her new self allowing her to indulge in desires long suppressed. Though, she still see's a therapist deciding to honor the memory of dear mom and dad. Though she really can't understand these peoples diagonisis of her.

    ''Is it really so wrong to want control, to be the master of your home?"

                                                  Originally posted by WHW View Post
                                                  I think he is asking from a more "what can I do with these dots and how can I use these Rituals" point of view, instead of "let's present them in different story light to make them more interesting in fluff department". Basically, how to use their stuff optimally from character sheet point of view. How?
                                                  I have no idea, I never really tried to get into Blood Magic. If you are looking for Covenant for your Gangrel Lady of The Territory, I would suggest grabbing some Ordo Dracul Mysteries instead; they have one Mystery dedicated towards being THE RULER!, and one towards being THE FRENZY IS MY TOOL; I feel that the second one, combined with Ride The Wave fighting style, might be up your alley - Frenzy like a Boss, smack up fools, and always be in control, even when mutilating people as a raging demon. It also requires relatively few merit dots to be effective, so you can spare them for something else AND be a combat monster.

                                                  Here is the basic gist of it:
                                                  - Get Animalism 2, Mystery of the Wyrm 1 as your Disciplines
                                                  - Get at least 3 dots of Riding the Wave Fighting Style (but unless you are starved for merit dots, 5 is what you want; don't bother with less than 3)

                                                  Animalism 2 gives you ability to have powerful animal minions, and Wyrm 1 gives you ability to Frenzy at Will - this seems useless, but it's actually super effective! I tried once to play a Vampire who used lighter to push herself to Frenzy, but it was very clumsy in combat situations, as it took me pretty much a whole round in which I could be pounding someone. With Wyrm 1, you can start frenzying ASAP, on a drop of hat.

                                                  Riding the Wave is fantastic, because you get the benefits even before you achieve 5 successes. It's not that optimal without the fighting style, due to the "spend a Willpower every round to...", but with the 3rd dot of Riding the Wave, your Willpower point to Ride the Wave stops being "wasted", and at pinnacle of the style, you actually get more out of it than you would without Frenzying. Only dot that is "meh" is the first dot, really.

                                                  Mysteries of the Wyrm are interesting; 1st dot is incredibly useful, 2nd one is, eh, gives you some utility for your Frenzy; 3rd dot is amazing, and 4th dot turns you into a Frenzying Legendary Super Vampire. I also think that if you really want to play up the sociopath/psychopath angle in Vampire world, relying on Riding the Wave is the way to go.
                                                  What I like the most about this "combo", is that you can start playing it from the get go, and it *does* scale if you decide to continue investing in it; you also have a lot of room for less...angry and combat related things; in combat department, all you need is about 3 dots of Brawl and some Athletics (though I prefer weaponry, to be honest), so even 4 in physical skills will, theoretically, be enough for your needs. You are free to do whatever you want with Social and Mental skills (other than investing in Animal Ken to make sure your animalism is strong and good), so you also won't be one dimensional combat monster. Riding the Wave for maximum efficiency leaves you with 5 merits to spend however you like (I would suggest Status with Ordo Dracul, obviously :P), so you can use them to become little more rounded, too - or to develop your territory.

                                                  Oh, and don't worry about your Animalism Dice Pool TOO much. Technically, even with 1 dot of relevant attribute and 1 dot of relevant skill, you are good to go as long as you spend a Willpower point - 5 dice give you a really good chance of at least one success, and Animalism doesn't really care about scoring multiple successes. It also doesn't include any penalties or resistances or whatever, so don't be afraid to take it even without heavy investment into Manipulation/Presence/Animal Ken. It's a discipline you rarely use in combat, so you probably have no excuses to *not* Willpower on it if you need to. Only time when Animalism might be reasonably contested is when someone tries to override your commands, and that's a Clash of the Will, so it's Blood Potency + Animalism, not the original Dice Pool.

                                                  If you don't like idea of Animalism and Animal Mastery, go Protean 2. Protean is reasonably good for combat (still, good weapon still is better than it), but instead of looking at it as "is this a good combat discipline", look at it's versatility - it turns you into a Teenage Mutant Vampire. Need super smell? Got it! Wall crawling? Sure! Really need to punch that sharkman? Aquatic is your friend.
                                                  While Protean 2 doesn't give you a major power boost, it gives you incredible versatility - it would be super good even if you had to choose 3 adaptations and be stuck with them forever. But as you can switch them out between nights, well...
                                                  Protean 3 is a reasonable alternative to the RIDE THE WAVE combo, but it's little clumsy in actual use - there are many times where A GODDAMN BEAR would have better stats and tools to solve your problem, but it's very inconvenient to turn into it, while you can reasonably Ride the Wave without breaking masquerade as much (as funny as it sounds).
                                                  ...nothing stops you from Riding the Wave AS A MOTHERFUCKING BEAR, actually. So if you are interested in personal power instead of the animal route, I would go Protean 2, Wyrm 1, Ride the Wave 5, aiming to get Protean 3 and EAT A BEAR asap. Remember that you can still use your disciplines in Animal Form, so you *can* become a ninja wall crawling bear, and - at Protean 4 - ninja wall crawling winged riding the wave bear. Or Wall Crawling Raging Claws of The Unholy Bear. That's probably your biggest power spike in direct combat.
                                                  ...but, as mentioned, Protean gives you a lot of non-combat utility, too. Which is why I love the way it's designed.

                                                  ...if you are interested in Protean, it would be wise to ask for Animal Stats, I think they were somewhere around here.

                                                  So yeah, either go:
                                                  A) Animalism 2 + Wyrm 1, Riding the Wave 5
                                                  B) Protean 2 + Wyrm 1, Riding the Wave 5
                                                  C) Protean 3
                                                  I would suggest A or B, as both are super fun, let you be super effective at combat without being one dimensional facesmash character, and both of the Disciplines are not Dice Pool reliant, so you have a lot of freedom to make your character a scientist socialite or something else with rest of your points, while still being a killing machine. Less killing machine than a Daeva, but imho, you get a lot of more versatility out of it.
                                                  C is risky, because you might never have a chance to get introduced into Mystery in the actual play, and Ride the Wave is really clumsy to use if you don't have a reliable frenzy trigger.

                                                  As for "Ride the Wave working asap" : http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...wave-mechanics . If your GM disagrees, ignore the whole Riding the Wave combo and go for Protean 3. Eat a Bear, a Cat, and something that can fly, and go to town.
