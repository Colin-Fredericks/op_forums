    Trenches & Pilgrims - A Promethean the Created Actual Play

    05-24-2025, 01:12 AM
    Last night (I guess technically two nights ago being I am posting this past midnight) two of my friends plus myself played the first chapter of our Promethean the Created chronicle. I have very little experience with this game but have been excited by the themes of the game for years now so I am very excited to finally get to run it. We are running this game in The Great War setting from the second Dark Eras book, starting in April of 1915 in Flanders, if you know you know.

    Both players are playing Frankienstiens, and if you follow my Mage the Awakening Chronicle you might find it interesting that Eye of God’s player is here playing Jacques Pierre, and Giger’s player is playing Lucien Steinhart. Jacques believes in the idea that someone can have a good death, and being able to obtain a good death is what makes you human. His Elpis is fear and his torment is methodical, he is starting in Phosphrum as a psychopomp. Lucien is striving to become someone who is capable of giving up power in order to be a good person. His Elpis is Pain and his torment is Paranoia. He is starting in Stannum as a savage. Jaques has taken the transmutations Morning Star and Sanctus Aspiratus, while Lucien took Arc and Redirect. I believe that is all I have to see for background information, so let's get into the prologue for each character.

    Before We get into it I’ll offer a trigger warning for all things war, especially the Great War.

    Chapter 1
    5/22/2025

    Lucien had spent the last month, his entire life, in the basement of what he assumed was a cabin, at the mercy of a Dr. Bohm. Lucien found himself on the path of Tin from the beginning, and his violent beginning to life meant that he was not only trapped in the basement, but chained, both his wrists and one around his neck. Dr. Bohm believes Lucien to be someone named “Franz” and insists on Lucien calling himself that. Every day for the past month has been the same’ Dr. Bohm brings Lucien food and asks simple, medical questions. As the conversation goes on the questions become more specific, not just to how Lucien is feeling, instead the questions are about people, places and events. When Lucien doesn’t answer in the way the Dr. wants, he is screamed at, the doctor insists he is a genius, he brought “Franz” back to life and now “Franz” is wasting that life. Recently he has begun striking Lucien before leaving.

    Tonight is different though. Lucien is no idiot and has managed to gather materials over the past month to create a simple weapon, a molotov cocktail. The desk nearby has a candle on it, likely not enough to destroy the house, but enough for a distraction. He can hear Dr. Brohm above making his way down the stairs and a short conversation happened where each insisted Lucien use their own name for him, and when Lucien refused to call himself “Franz” Dr. Bohm dropped the plate of food in frustration, but quickly apologized and went upstairs to gather another plate. When he returned Lucien pretended as if his memories had returned and that he was “Franz” and ordered Dr. Bohm to release him and explain how he had managed to bring him back to life. Dr. Bohm believed him, handed him the plate of food he had returned with, and began explaining the process of picking various parts from various corpses to reassemble a better body for him and the use of lighting. However as soon as one of the chains around his wrist was freed he grabbed Dr. Bohm by the neck, pinning him to the ground, while he struggled Dr. Bohm had no chance of overpowering Lucien.

    Lucien reminded him of how he had been treated over the past month; chained, beaten, screamed at, this was the entire existence he had known. Dr. Bohm tried explaining but was being strangled and thus, had a hard time speaking. Luckily for the Dr. in his fit of rage Lucien threw the molotov cocktail he had, which ignited over the desk when it knocked over the candle. Seeing the flame cause Lucien to experience much fear and he released Dr. Bohm, who grabbed the key to Lucien's chains and escaped. Lucien managed to escape by pulling the last two chains out of place in the wall, breaking down the door to the basement, and running into the forest, but not before making sure the cabin burned completely out of a paranoid fear that Dr. Bohm would create someone who would be more loyal to him to send after Lucien.

    Jacques Pierre had a much more simple life. He interacted with his creator much less often but was allowed to live in his house. Dr. DuPont visited some, but was perfectly content to let Jacques wander. Being on the Path of Copper and in the role of Hermit, he enjoyed this freedom and solitude. Dr. DuPont did seem to slowly be getting annoyed with Jacques, but not enough to worry him. He enjoyed roaming the streets of Paris in his freetime. He noted that he maker, a surgeon by trade, loved books and would read every night before bed. Curious about this, Jacques read the novel Dr. DuPont was working through, a story that followed two characters, a serial killer who believed he was saving his victims from purgatory by killing them, and the detective trying to stop him. Jacques didn't quite understand the fact that the killer in the book is insane, to him it seems perfectly rational to wish to save people in that way, and he decided to help his maker experience this story. He stole some money from the doctor and went out, purchasing a knife.

    The next day Jacques was giddy but refused to tell Dr. DuPont why when asked. The doctor just was glad to see his creation happy so he didn't press once he got told by Jacques “I have a surprise that someone is really going to like.” That evening, once Dr. DuPont had started reading his novel, Jacques snuck out and began knocking lightly on the windows. He snuck back into the house to turn off the lights. The entire time he was giggling with giddiness. Dr. DuPont knew it was him but not why he was doing this. Eventually he went to bed, calling out into the house that tomorrow was his birthday, something Jacques was already aware of, and he'd like to eat out for lunch tomorrow with him.

    Jacques stayed in the doctor's room until midnight and then stabbed him with the knife, shouting “surprise!” when he woke. Naturally the doctor tried to save himself but was no match for Jacques, who believed he was giving him an exciting death as a present for his birthday. The fact that the doctor fought back made it even better for him. Jacques took his maker's corpse into the catacombs and buried him with his hands over his chest as if in prayer. On his return to the surface he was almost found and had to take a different path back up than he had taken down and discovered a stone cross that was glowing. It read:
    “Here surrounded by the dead
    We who seek to become among the living
    Learn to seek the thrill of being alive.

    Here surrounded by darkness
    We who must dwell in the shadows
    Learn to bear the light.”


    By focusing on the light and the memories the cross showed him Jacques was able to join the refinement of Phosphrum, seeing the memories of a promethean who had living a long time ago, with a mask of gold welded to his face, who followed doctors into villages filled with disease and worked as a highway bandit in order to experience the feeling of flirting with death, and now Jacques would too.

    Both if the created were led to Flanders by Elpis visions, where they would meet two other Prometheans, along with a small collection of humans who would be instrumental in the completion of their current roles. They each were made aware of how the other split with their creator due to taking the measure, and both eventually landed in the French 45th Infantry Division, but were made to work with English soldiers in mining operations.

    In one such mining operation Jacques broke through into a German mine, which resulted in Lucien yelling at him for being a fool. Captain Avery, and English miner in charge here told him to be silent, but it was too late, German voices could be heard coming from down the tunnel. Jacques reacted to this but slipping into the tunnel and using transmutation to cause another English miner, Lieutenant Fleek, to follow him. He led him away from the Germans, while Lucien dropped into the tunnel and moved towards the voices.

    Jacques had been watching Fleek for a while. He was a sad, pathetic person in his eyes, sobbing all night long under artillery fire and frequently passing out in the mines due to lack of oxygen. He would die a quick death one day, not a good death, so Jacques decided to fix that and attacked him, hoping to give him a good death here in the tunnels beneath no man's land. Thankfully he did not kill him with the first stab, allowing Fleek to fight back, but missing when he fired his pistol. The sound of the pistol brought one of the fellow 45th Infantry Division soldiers down the tunnel, where Fleek shouted that Jacques was a German spy trying to kill him. A second soldier arrived and Jacques stopped fighting, instead charging a transmutation to put him in charge of the situation. Gunshots could be heard coming from back down the tunnel where the Germans had been heard, and eventually the French troops managed to get Fleek to drop his pistol. Jacques transmutation activated and he sent the men to go help the others, angrily finishing Fleek and taking his dog tags with him so he couldn't be identified.

    While this was happening, Lucien and another French soldier encountered a pair of German miners, who began firing. Lucien managed to grab and knock out one of the Germans, and after taking a few bullets, watched his friend shoot the German dead. Lucien made sure by smashing the German soldier's skulls together. Two more arrived and fired at Lucien, who used one German corpse as a body shield while charging up his transmutation and, when it was finally charged, he managed to atmozie one of the soldiers while frying the second who then died to gunfire. Captain Avery arrived with a grenade and threw it into the tunnel, collapsing it and then did the same for the tunnel they had been digging. Jacques convinced Captain Avery that Fleek had died protecting him from Germans that had come from the other direction and the surviving men made their way out of the tunnels.

              Chapter 2
              5/29/2025

              Due to the injuries he suffered in the battle, Lucien was taken away from the forward trench to a medical tent. All around him he saw the dead and dying, people missing limbs from artillery fire, and those suffering from bullet wounds, from barbed wire, or some freak accident that they had suffered from. One of his legs and both of his arms were damaged horribly in the fighting, but every nurse and doctor that came to help him took one look and decided to help someone else. He waited for hours before a doctor finally took him seriously, promising he'd be out of the trenches soon, being sent to a hospital in France to recover. Lucien did not want this, the Azothic visions had led him here, but the doctor heard none of his protests. To ensure he wouldn't be sent back Lucien snuck out of the tent to find a generator, and when no one was around he attempted to absorb the electricity. He managed to completely restore himself but fried the generator in the process.

              While he was alone, Jacques sat in a small dugout, frustrated and confused. Why had Fleek fought so hard to live when he was going to die in this war anyway? Why was he so resistant to a good, dramatic death? He sat below the ground for hours contemplating this until finally the power went out in the dugout and he rose to see what was happening. As he made his way to his officer he came across some soldiers who were putting a tarp over the body of a man who had been shot in the head until his body could be moved. Jacques did find the officer in his own dugout, but the officer was not happy to see Jacques and sent him away, uninterested in discussing the power failure with him.

              Lucien made his way back to the forward trench just in time to watch with the rest of the division out across no man's land as the sun set, just in case of an attack. Jacques participated but was distracted the entire time, still worked up over Fleek's death. When no attack came the soldiers ate and prepared for bed, where they were informed the next morning before dawn they would be attacking across no man's land to secure the German trenches. This, obviously, got mixed responses but no one could disobey the orders from high command, and the soldiers accepted their fate. When most of them had fallen asleep, Lucien found Jacques and convinced him to cross no man's land with him to the German trenches to prepare for tomorrow's attack. It took them hours to belly crawl their way to enemy lines, and as they got close they noticed a patrol of four Germans and decided to strike. Lucien engaged in hand to hand combat while Jacques decided to try out Fleek's pistol, to see if any good could come from it. He managed to hit one man, who gripped his shoulder and called out in pain, which Jacques liked, feeling like this man could have a good death, even if he wasn't close and personal. Two Germans died before reinforcements came, and Jacques nearly died himself when they fired on them. With the reinforcements, both Prometheans sensed Azoth nearby, and Jacques was able to pinpoint it within the German trenchline. Before they fell back, Lucien remembered his plan and used his Redirect transmutation on one of the soldiers before they retreated.

              While they did make it back to the French trenchline, Jacques was extremely wounded, with one leg mangled and one arm useless. The two began arguing even as they felt the presence of Azoth nearby, both blaming the other for the damage their bodies took; Jacques tonight, Lucien in the tunnels that day. Lucien struck Jacques, breaking his good leg, as a Promethean dressed as a German soldier slipped into the trenchline. It was one of the Unfleshed, clearly, and asked what they were fighting about. They tried explaining but it failed to understand, after all, “You both wear French uniforms.” The Unfleshed informed them that he was called Private Heinrich and they introduced themselves as well, with Lucien saying he was the leader of these two. Jacques responded to this by shooting Lucien in the mouth, Private Hienrich fled, and French soldiers swarmed the area expecting a night raid, but instead found Lucien strangling what, to everyone else, looked like a corpse. The soldiers threw Jacques’ body over the trench line and got Lucien to the medical tent, where he was informed he'd be leaving the front for time to relax.

              Private Heinrich remained nearby, near enough for Jacques to detect. After things calmed down he appeared, wearing a bloody and rotting French uniform, and asked if Jacques needed help. Jacques asked him to take him to electricity, and Private Heinrich carried him. As they went Jacques inquired about Private Heinrich's refinement, which was Aurum, he was in the role of companion, trying to learn how to become a true member of the German army. He then asked Jacques why he shot Lucien if they were allies, and Jacques responded “I knew it wouldn't kill him because he's like us.” Private Heinrich got Jacques to an abandoned dugout with a lightbulb which Jacques used to get mostly healed before the socket burst. <Two aggravated damage remains so one bad arm.> Private Heinrich excused himself then, saying if they met again he would shoot at them, being they wouldn't be killed.

                  Chapter 3
                  6/26/2025

                  This chapter started with Lucien having to sneak away from the medical tent again so he wouldn't be sent away from the front lines while Jacques found more electricity to heal himself before the attack on German lines. Lucien tried to find electricity as well and when he and Jacques found each other Jacques decided not to tell him where he found his unguarded electricity, meaning Lucien was unable to fully heal before the battle while Jacques was.

                  The time for the attack came and artillery picked up just before the French line stormed the German line. The rush across no man's land saw many people die to machine gun fire, Jacques took one bullet to his leg and Lucien found a French soldier who was caught in barbed wire. He tore him free, mangling his leg in the process, and carried him on his back to the German line. Despite the artillery bombardment the Germans were prepared for the French attack. Even so, Jacques watched a group of French soldiers enter a dugout and slay the Germans inside. He followed them to watch them loot chocolates and cigars and they seemed to be done in their minds, but Jacques rallied them onwards and received some chocolate as well.

                  In the meantime Lucien and his wounded companion were making their way deeper into the German trenches when they came face to face with a group of soldiers making their way to reinforce the front. He threw a bomb and wounded them, managing to kill them before he died, but his companion died in the fight. Just as the firefight ended the group of soldiers rallied by Jacques rushed past, Lucien retrieved the fallen soldier's tag and followed along. Another firefight broke out which resulted in more dead Germans and all but one of the mortal French soldiers dead. The remaining one, shocked at the loss of his friends, gathered up their tags and made his way back to French lines. Jacques and Lucien decided to push on.

                  They discovered a dugout filled with German soldiers and rats, the rats having swarmed and killed them and Lucien knew this was the man he had redirected his disquiet to earlier. They found some small treasures and continued on, getting lost in the German trenches but discovering a pile of supplies, masks with ventilation hoses on them. They each grabbed one, sensing something bad coming, and continued on.

                  They came across some four Germans leading a group of captured French soldiers to the back of the line and managed to ambush them, using bombs followed by pistols to kill them and Jacques learned he liked using the bombs up close. The night before Lucien had broken Jacques's leg during their fight and Jacques decided now was a good time for revenge, so he stabbed him with the knife he used to kill his maker. They wrestled each other to the ground and Lucien convinced the French soldiers he was a traitor. Jacques tried to charge up his Ringleader ability but was kicked to death before he could.

                  Jacques awakened near a river underground, wet as if he had been swimming in it. The only route he could take was either swimming again in the river or traveling up a tunnel against a stream of soldiers from all sides of the battle. He pushed through them, eventually meeting the soldier that had given him chocolate after he rallied him. Jacques convinced the soldier they weren't done and together they climbed up as the tunnel became steeper and steeper. Finally Jacques broke through the mouth of the tunnel and found himself standing alone. <That's one universal milestone out of the way but I had plans for this one with him and I guess that's out the window now. Either way these two continue to through me through loops I wasn't expecting.>

                          Chapter 5
                          8/21/2025

                          Lucien and Jacques felt the Azoth nearby and Jacques was able to determine it was coming from No Man's Land, five sources in all, two lingering behind the front three. They decided it must be Heinrich returning after the recent battle with more of their kind, but instead what they discovered were monsters. Pandorans that looked as if the corpses of man and dog had been sewed together, each with an alchemical symbol burned into their forehead. The beasts attacked. While Jacques kept his distances, firing with his pistol, Lucien fought with iron knuckles he had with him. While they managed to down one of the first three quickly, Lucien was still wounded greatly, and with the arrival of the second pair of these corpse hounds the throng decided to retreat towards the sounds of soldiers coming down the trench, drawn to Jacques's gunfire. Lucien didn't make it, being killed by the two he had been brawling with. Jacques did make it to the soldiers in time and while he risked disquiet with them, he stood and fought the remaining Corpse Hounds. None of the men died although all were injured and put off by what they saw, one man vomiting at the sight of the stitched corpses. Jacques remained behind to bury the corpses so no one else would see them, but the soldiers insisted on bringing one back to show their officer, believing these to be some sort of German invention to use the corpses of the dead against the British and French.

                          Lucien, like Jacques before him that morning, found himself laying with his feet in a river, surrounded by dead soldiers standing near the river's edge. He tried speaking with them but none answered. Eventually one fell face first into the river, but Lucien was able to pull him out before he was carried down the river. A German soldier, he was able to communicate with him after he had been saved and they decided to leave together once Lucien spotted a tunnel that opened into the part of the cave they were in, but before they could leave a second ghost fell into the river. Lucien saved this one, a French soldier from his own division, although he didn't recognize him. Lucien explained he believed they had all died. The German soldier seemed to accept this, but the French soldier panicked and tried to flee. Eventually Lucien caught him and calmed him down, and together the three left the cave, climbing on hand and knee to the mouth of the cave against an upward slope. When Lucien finally found himself in Flanders once again he was alone.

                          He discovered Jacques about ten yards from their trenchline where he had spent an entire day digging a hole to dump the corpses in. Lucien's corpse had swiftly rotted into nothingness a moment before, and before throwing the body into the hole with the pandorans he retrieved the medal of honor from his body. Upon looking down at the corpses together the two saw the alchemical symbol, a circle connected to an upside cross. Inside the circle were two triangles that overlapped to make a third. Seeing this triggered some of the Azothic Memory to reveal itself. They saw an old worn journal, ancient, in the hands of a promethean as if it was their own hand gripping the book. Carved into its cover was the same symbol, and they could feel a mixture of dread and excitement coming from whoever's memory this was. They could see the book was covered in slime that they recognized as congealed Flux. Somehow they knew they were in the city of Ypres; a city they could see from their trenches on a clear day.

                          They returned and slept, the next day being fed some sort of sloppy again. Jacques decided he liked the flavor of the slop, but gained recognition from the other soldiers when he complained about it with them. They were then assigned to bury the old latrine and dig a new one, a job usually many soldiers engaged in, their officer sent only them. Lucien tired from the work and the work was not completed, so they were made to return the following day. On this second day they once again sensed Azoth nearby, but this time it was Hienrich. They conversed about the battle for a moment before Hienrich warned them of the Pandorans that had attacked him the night before. They revealed to him that they had saved them two nights ago, and that Lucien had died fighting them. Hienrich warned them both that they would only be able to return once, and with Jacques confessing he had died in the attack two days ago, they both were fully aware this was their last chance. Hienrich told them he did not wish to desert the German army until he had finished his current roles, but he wished to travel to Ypres for answers to these Corpse Hounds, he would find them on his way through.

                          When they completed their work the commanding officer arrived and Lucien insisted on knowing why only two had been given this task when usually more men would be sent to do this. This caused a sort of missing contest between the two, which Lucien lost due to his rank, but he did manage to shake the officer by lying to him about rumors of his inadequacy as an officer. That evening Lucien started writing out pamphlets about the failures of their officer and placed them around the trenches, making sure he'd see them. That morning when slop was delivered he and Jacques were made to stand to and watch for an attack instead of eat, the officer insisting he knew they were behind the rumors. They afternoon replacement soldiers were brought in and the company was moved back to reserves but the order for Jacques and Lucien to shift to reserves never came through, a clear manipulation of orders from the officer to everyone who was aware. One soldier that had been pleased by Jacques vocal dislike of the slop approached him and promised to bring this up to someone in command when he could.

                              Chapter 6
                              9/18/2025

                              This chapter began with the throng meeting the new soldiers that had moved up to the front lines. Most of the French troops were part of the 45th Infantry Division, and one of these men stood out to Lucien. The man was someone he had seen in the same Azothic dream that had brought him to this part of the war and he knew he was important to the fulfillment of his current role. Jacques also saw three different men from the British line next to them that he recognized from his own dream guiding him here; a commander giving orders, a young man likely in his 20s, and an older man likely in his 30s. Lucien tried talking to his man, a Private Randall according to his uniform, but the man wanted nothing to do with him, telling him he didn’t like the way he looked. The older man approached to watch the spectacle of Randall yelling at Lucien and introduced himself as John to Jacques, speaking fluent French himself. He seemed much more relaxed than the other soldiers, mentioning he missed home like everyone else before returning to help the younger man reinforce their trenches.

                              Lucien then remembered that he had promised to find information on Olivia’s father and so began asking around for anyone from the reinforcements that was from their home town of Avignon. While none of the soldiers were, he was informed of a nurse who was, so he went to find her. When he reached the part of the line where medical aid was given he found a pair of nurses lifting stretchers into a medical van. He tried to get information on this nurse from them but they asked him to leave them alone while they were working. To speed things up he tried lifting a stretcher on his own, which caused the man to roll off and reopen his wound. The nurses rushed the man into the medical tent, leaving Lucien alone.

                              When it came lunch time Lucien and Jacques noticed that Randall was surrounded by a few men, all whispering together about something. Lucien tried to find out what, but the group of men were uninterested in including him. He insisted though, refusing to leave and calling Randall a coward. This enraged Randall, who kicked and punched at Lucien, but dealt little damage to the promethean, who continued taunting him. Finally, Randall told him if he thought he was so brave he should meet him back here after the sun went down. Lucien agreed, pleased and thinking he was making progress towards befriending Randall.

                              Lucien spoke with the new commanding officer, Sgt-Mgr Lhote about wanting to rise in the ranks into a leadership position, and he agreed he‘d find him an opportunity to show his leadership abilities. They also discussed Sgt-Mgr Allard and how he had manipulated the paperwork to ensure that both Jacques and Lucien had remained on the front when they should have been moved back to reserves with the rest of their men, and assured him that he was looking into it.

                              Finally night came and the throng went to find Randall, who was with the three other men from earlier. He challenged them to a game; enter the German lines, capture a German soldier, and bring them here before the four of them could. Lucien agreed and before anything else could be said, the four men leaped over the trench into no-man’s-land.

                                  Chapter 7
                                  10/02/2025

                                  <Before I got the write up for this started I ended up traveling and didn't get it written up, so a quick summary of the throngs competitive raid into German lines is that they managed to sneak their way all the way to the back lines of the German line and into a medical tent.
