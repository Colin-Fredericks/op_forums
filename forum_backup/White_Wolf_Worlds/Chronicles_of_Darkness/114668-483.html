            Depends on what you mean by dolls and what you want to do with them. The awesome thing about Mage is that there are always multiple Arcana combinations that can be used to approach a problem.

            Lets say you want to create an archive of magical souls and knowledge, an artificial afterlife in defiance of the Psychopomp, a lore repository and emergency source of troops akin to the Infinity Circuit of the Eldar. Prime and Death could accomplish such a thing.

            Should you prefer to make living beings into golems, you would need a Patterning of Life and the target Arcanum. For manipulating dolls and creating golems, Matter could be of aid.

            Another approach is that the mage wants everything to be in a perfect pattern, all integrating into gears as the mage makes optimal decisions with absolute certainty and builds mechanical enforcers of their perfect timeline, akin to the Vex from Destiny. Time and Matter could be used to such an end.

            In the end, it all depends on figuring out the mix that best aligns with the Mysteries they focus on.

                                                Remember that dreams still respond with dream logic, and that dreams, even nightmares, have a dampening effect on the dreamer to the point where mages can cast spells affecting the dream and its narrative without them being considered obvious magic to a dreaming Sleeper.
                                                I want to note that neither mages nor changelings (nor Sleepwalkers with the Astral Adept Merit) have any special rules for dealing with mental stress when they are lucid dreaming and travelling the Astral/Dreaming Roads/Hedge/etc. With these precedents coupled with the fact that a Sleeper in dream form outside their dream shouldn't enjoy its special protection, makes me assume that Sleepers in dream forms have no extra protection against breaking points. A horrible nightmare might technically be scarier than the Underworld, but the former comes with built-in protections that are definitely missing from the latter.

                                                then I don't think it appearing as a dream is blunting it all that much,
                                                It wouldn't appear as a dream when it actually happens (i.e. why I wouldn't grant bonus dice on breaking points for that alone), but the context matters a whole lot. Even though it's seemingly (and actually) as real as when entering the Underworld bodily, waking up after such an experience (rather than having recollection of the chain of events leading to descent and ascent, and quite possibly a noted absence in the material world during that time) makes it so much easier to rationalise it as a dream after the fact.

                                                Let's say that a character witnesses a horribly accident and calls 112/911. If the event is worthy of a breaking point for that character, it should always be rolled. However, if the responders have a professional (like a trained EMT or police officer) with them that helps ground the character and go through the shock, then that should make the event somewhat less traumatic compared to if the character was only approached to give a statement. In both cases the character experiences the same breaking point, but in the former case they should definitely have a better chance of succeeding on the breaking point roll. It's basically the same principle for experiencing the supernatural, except based in ease of denial instead of mental health support.

                                                    Would anything significant about character balance change if I altered the Inferior Arcana? For some reason, them not being all Gross or all Subtle bothers me, on a purely aesthetic level. So I was thinking...

                                                    For all Subtle, I'd change:

                                                    Mastigos - Fate (instead of Matter). My reasoning is the theme of the Mastigos is all about Will, Identity, the Self, and so forth, rather than the role it is meant to play by impersonal cosmic forces.

                                                    Acanthus - Prime (instead of Forces). My reasoning is that Arcadia is all about the role a thing plays in a story or chronology, rather than its "inherent" nature.

                                                    Obrimos would stay at Death, Thyrus at Mind, and Moros at Spirit.

                                                    For all Gross, I'd change...

                                                    Moros would go to Life. Pretty obvious Death/Life contrast, there.

                                                    Obrimos would go to Space. Universal godhead, omnipresence of the divinity, blah blah blah.

                                                    Thyrsus would go to Time because animals and nature generally are about relative time (hungry or sated, dark or light outside, etc). At least my dog only knows breakfast time, dinner time, and bed time.

                                                    I might even say PCs get a choice of inferior arcana, with the following spread:

                                                    Mastigos= Fate/Matter
                                                    Acanthus= Prime/Forces
                                                    Obrimos= Death/Space
                                                    Thyrsus= Mind/Time
                                                    Moros= Life/Spirit.

                                                    Would this break anything important?
