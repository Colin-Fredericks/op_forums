                            "The Lower Depths" is just a mage phrase that's better translated as "Someplace strange that's out there somewhere." Basically, if you're not populated by the fae, astral projections, ghosts, angels, or spirits, then its "lower depths."

                            Mages are very good at trying to draw their world according to various models that are completely wrong. Which makes sense, really. They're a bunch of scientists trying to draw models of the world, but haven't quite realized that the Earth isn't the center of the universe.

                                                    Interesting.

                                                    There's a strong "Old Gods cast out of the Supernal" vibe to the Judges of Duat, isn't there? With Sekhem taking the place of the worship most Old Gods require.

                                                    Then again, the CFae don't appear to require worship despite having been cast out of the Supernal (only they sorta kinda haven't, except they have, mostly, sometimes... I hate high-level Fate/Time magic...)

                                                    And given the strong connection between symbols and the mind (whether it's the human mind or an inhuman one makes no nevermind) I can see a similar "they've been thrown out but they kinda haven't" vibe with the Judges. I mean, in the headcanon I'm brewing, they map to Mastigos/Pandemonium in the same way that the Old Gods of Thistle map to Acanthus/SArcadia.They've been expelled from the Supernal, but as Old Gods of Mind and Space, they maintain such a strong connection to the symbols that they're simultaneously in the Lower Depths and in an Emanation.

                                                    Which is SCARY AS FUCK when you think about it.

                                                    Or maybe they're not really in the Lower Depths at all. IIRC, nobody knows what happened to the Priests of Irem, right? What if they're Mages, who've Ascended? And maybe the Judges don't "hunger" for Sekhem so much as they're demanding it because it's a form of Quintessence that the Priests (obviously Alienated) can use for a Noumenon that restores the Judges to their rightful place...
