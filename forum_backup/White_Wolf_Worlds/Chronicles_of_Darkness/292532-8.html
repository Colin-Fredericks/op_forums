                                I don't think Twilight Judge needs to be "toned down a lot" per se, I just think it needs to be noted whether there's supposed to be one per domain as is laid out in the Ordo Dracul book, or if you can have a cadre of Twilight Judges. The merit has a ton of potential, but it gets weird if you have multiple people running around using it. It doesn't automatically make every other Dragon your bitch, you really don't have any direct sway over what they do. You can leverage them if they study, or want to study, a heretical Coil, and you have access to any Coil you want to study. It's certainly very cool, but I wouldn't say it's a problem at all. It definitely should have Sworn as a prerequisite.

                                EDIT: If in fact there can be more than one Twilight Judge, maybe a note that the higher Covenant Status Judge trumps others.

                                I originally thought the Coil of Ziva was oddly formulated as well, but I've come around after a little rumination. It's totally a combination of Anoushka's Ladder and Way of the Locust, and I think that's okay. Instead of a "little goody two shoes" Golconda-lite power, you retain your Humanity through force and ultimately theft. It's a brutal contradiction, and right up the Ordo Dracul's re-defined alley. You're going to keep your Beast at bay on your own terms by any means necessary, and anyone who gives you shit about it just might be next against the wall when it's time to boost the Man another notch over the Beast. Spending Vitae to guard against degeneration is fun too. Totally Coiling the curse back against itself.

                                The problem with Scales is that you don't actually need the high prerequisite dots to develop them. A lowly 1-dot Coil Dragon who follows any Mystery at all could develop Shedding the Beast's Skin if it was a Scale. Just costs 1 more experience, that you can offset with a Crucible! That's not really okay.

                                Again, the 5 dot Scale thing for Epidermal Shielding Bath only matters to the tune of 1 experience. Additionally, Epidermal Shielding Bath can be used to protect other vampires, not just the Dragon who knows the Scale. You can make your whole coterie highly resistant to sunlight. The potential there should not be understated. Denying the Bane is broad in scope, and very cool, but it's personal.

                                One of the basic building blocks of Coils is that they give you new ways to utilize Vitae, so spending Vitae for bonuses to resist Degeneration seems good. It also ties in to the concept of coiling the curse back on itself, as I mentioned above. It also makes Blood Potency the limiting factor on how much of a bonus you get from the Coil, rather than the Coil dots itself. That last one is a matter of preference and design choice, but I like it. More powerful Blood means you can leverage it against the curse more effectively.
