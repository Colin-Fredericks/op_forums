    The description says: "Each time the Contagion emerges to threaten the fabric of our world, the only beings capable of manipulating, slowing, weaponizing, or halting it, are the unnatural denizens from across the Chronicles of Darkness. The Contagion permeates Infrastructure and warps the known antagonists and supporting blocks present in the lives of every inhuman being."

    Parsing this I am thinking this means something a bit chthulhueque ie something that "emerges to threaten the fabric of our world". "

    "Each time" suggest that it is not a permanent contagion but something that emerges periodically and taints an physical area or spiritual space from a patient zero (either an actual person or spirit or a contagion zone like radiation).

    "Contagion permeates infrastructure and warps the known antagonists..." suggests that this contagion will somehow mutate the physical and/or spiritual/supernatural nature of beings in the world that we already know shaking up the usual antagonists in a way that changes how they interact with the supernaturals against whom they usually fight or maybe drives them to fight against supernaturals they would not normally interact with.

    I also wonder whether this supernatural contagion will have a larger effect on the mortal world, infecting those that are close to where the contagion emerges into our world in a way that will have effects far beyond the hidden world of the supernatural. If that is the case i wonder whether it will also involve an element of breaking the veil of secrecy with mortals that is an essential part of most of the regular lines.
