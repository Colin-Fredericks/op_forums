        Originally posted by DArchon5 View Post
        Yes, how broad can a Field be (In specific) and probably some examples of Complexity values
        Sure. Well, a Field is, roughly, something that's specific enough you could get an advanced degree in it. So you can't have biology as a field, but you could have genetic engineering, microbiology, or biomechanics as a field. The Fields your Genius had are decent examples, although I might pare "Fae" down just slightly (maybe splitting it into Hedge Studies, Fairy Biology, and Wyrd), since there's so much stuff in that. Otherwise, the Fields you have seem to have the right breadth of scope.*

        As for Complexity, when it comes to breadth, here's a rule of thumb:

        If it's very specific in its intent ("You pass butter."), then it's a +0. Most Wonders should have a single, specific function that it doesn't deviate from.
        If it's a little vague, but it's still limited enough not to be always or nearly-always useful, then it's +1. A literally all-terrain vehicle that can do land, air and sea might fall here. It's pretty damn versatile, but it's still got one clear function.
        If it's really broad or vague, but it still has firm limits, then it's a +2. Something that could, say, detect pretty much any type of supernatural phenomenon, or is pretty much always going to come in handy, will fall in here.
        If it has nearly unlimited applications, or its uses are so varied that it's easier to list what it can't accomplish, then it's +3, and I'd be very leery of even allowing it at my tables. A device that can change anything into anything else, a machine that can arbitrarily relocate objects across the multiverse, etc.

        The Complexity increase for breadth of function is kind of there to act as a deterrent for making anything scanners and do-anything robots, which were kind of an issue in 1e Genius. I'm not sure if this is the way to go about it, though, so if anyone has any thoughts, I'd appreciate it.

        *I'd also call Lycanthropy "Lycanthropology", partly for the pun value, but that's not really relevant.

                                    First off, sorry I've been so quiet. I just got a new job at a call center (farewell Subway, I shan't miss you), and I'm getting settled into that, and things have been chaotic at my place in general.

                                    Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
                                    Should we wait until Hunter 2ED comes out so we can borrow ideas from the Endowment rules for Wonders, or do we continue brainstorming right now?
                                    It might be a good idea to look at those when they're available, yeah. I think there was an Open Dev about it. I'll see if I can find it.

                                    The Illuminated were mentioned as the possible Strix/Idigam/Huntsmen equivalent for Genius 2ED, but they are closer to Mad Mages. Is there any chance malonkey1 and others could expand on his idea for the Beyonders in the Google Doc as the Strix/Idigam/Huntsmen of 2ED?
                                    • Illuminated: These are probably the headlining "Chronicles" antagonists right now. If a Genius drops too far into Renegade, they might get Illuminated. I think it might be more interesting if they had a sort of "mad lucidity." Give them a sense that they're clearly seeing something that the rest of the Inspired can't, something they may actually want to know about. They reflect how a Genius can go too far in the search of knowledge, but it's pretty clear that nobody wants to be Illuminated, unless they're already almost there. The Illuminated should be terrifying, but have access to such a deep level of knowledge that it's tempting anyway. Maybe trick some more arrogant Genii into thinking they can dip into that well without losing control.
                                    • Edisons: The Proximus/Wolf-Blooded analog. They are as much ally as antagonist to Genii. They can still interact with normal humanity, but they also have a very limited form of Inspiration that allows them to duplicate Wonders they've been allowed to analyze. Genii might want them around, since they can help communicate his ideals and theories, but Edisons are often in it for their own gain, often stabbing Genii in the back, playing them against each other, and sometimes just outright stealing from them.
                                    • Beyonders: These are kind of a broad group. Basically, they are thing that interact with Inspiration, but aren't necessarily Genii nor Manes. You might find interdimensional monsters that get agitated upon seeing Wonders, like they know something you don't, or aliens that demand test subjects in exchange for favors (such favors including "not making you a test subject"), or transhuman invaders from a possible future where Inspiration has spread to all of humanity, and so on.

                                    I haven't really thought about the signature city, actually. It'd probably be a good idea not to share a signature city with an existing gameline for simplicity's sake.
