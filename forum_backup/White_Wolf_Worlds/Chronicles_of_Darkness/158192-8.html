        MISCELLANEOUS LEGACIES

        •Neptune's Scions(???, Forces): Created as a contrast to the Tamers of Rivers, they focus on the Sea, an aggressive, capricious force that can give and take life with equal ease. Their Attainments should focus on making them better at sailing, aquatic navigating,
        and generally living around the oceans they so revere.
        •Children of the New Dawn(Any, Prime with Mind Optional): A very rare and niche Legacy, they are open to all Paths and Orders, but only recruit Redeemed Awakened Prometheans. Their Attainments allow them to mimic some of the traits they once bore without the crippling downsides of being one of the Created. For example, one of their Attainments would allow them to flare a fake Azorothic Radiance, making them able to detect and be detected by the Created, as well as notice the "burns" which mark a body that once bore the Divine Fire(that is, the Redeemed). The optional Attainments primarily focus on resisting(or, more sinisterly, inflicting something like) Disquiet.

        • Shepherds of the Living Soul(Moros/Silver Ladder, Mind): Founded by a Silver Ladder monk, this group seeks to protect others by identifying and healing the mental and emotional problems of others.

                                            The Black Avengers: A left handed Moros legacy, these angsty mages usually face their Awakening during a young age. Recruited from the ranks of goths, emos, punks, and others who are looked down upon by society, they typically have a bleak outlook on life, if not outright disdain for it. Recognizing the inevitability of death, they have no regard for the lives of others, unless those lives are important to them in some way. Taking pleasure in the suffering of those who have wronged them, their unique form of magic focuses on the use of curses, inflicting lasting torment upon their victims.

                                                This has been bouncing around in my head for at least a year now, so I figured I'd try to write it up.

                                                Keepers of the Sacred Frequency (Left-Handed, Obrimos, Free Council)

                                                Extra Arcanum: Mind

                                                Energy is what really makes up the universe; all matter is merely condensed energy. Mages are aware of many forms of energy that Sleepers have yet to discover, from the upwellings of Supernal power at Hallows to the more mundane but equally scientifically inexplicable patterns of Ley Lines that cross the globe. The Free Council wonders if the laws of physics in the Fallen World apply in some form to supernatural forces as well as natural ones; is the ephemera of the Shadow merely a more rarefied form of energy than what's found across the Gauntlet? Is there any truth to the metaphor of the Supernal Realms being "above" the Fallen, and is there a "gravity" that draws magic down from above?
                                                The answer to these questions, actually, is no: Physics may have one set of things to say about the connection between Matter and Forces, but the Supernal World's laws say different things entirely. The Supernal is not "above" the Fallen because "direction" in the Supernal world is conceptual at best, and may not truly exist outside of Pandemonium, the realm which includes Supernal definitions of all spatial relationships.
                                                A Seattle Obrimos who used to call herself Ceres found all this out the hard way in the 1990's. A professor of conventional physics before Awakening, she joined the Free Council and set out to prove indisputably that the laws of science explored by Sleeping truthseekers mirrored the governing principles of the Supernal world. Ceres studied for four years, making constant bold claims to local mages that she was approaching "an understanding of the true laws of nature undreamt-of by the generations of magi who chased after the scraps of Atlantis," while showing little to no hard progress on proving her theories. The local Council was impressed by it, even appointing her emissary to the Consilium. Everyone else thought it was a crock of shit, and they turned out to be right. Frustrated by her lack of progress, Ceres sought out ancient lore of the sort she would've theatrically turned her nose up at in public, and reached back to the Aether, calling an Angel to the Fallen World to answer her questions. Well, she didn't like the answers she got. The Cherub of the Golden Key was compassionate; it explained as plainly as it could that her quest was futile; the laws of the Fallen were not the laws of the Supernal, and never had been. The Cherub told Ceres that her ego had backed her into a corner with the rest of the Consilium; it said it understood that after her decades of studying the laws of the world, Awakening and recognizing her own ignorance had frightened her, and it expressed sympathy for the suffering and sleepless nights she had put herself through trying to prove a theory that simply was not correct. It extended a hand past the borders of the summoning circle, and offered Ceres another glimpse of true knowledge, if she would take it.
                                                And Ceres grabbed the angel by the forearm, took her athame in hand, and slit the Angel's wrist.
                                                As divine fire poured from the wound, Ceres held the shocked and dying Cherub fast. This goddamn thing wasn't an angel, angels helped people! Telling her to give up was not helping! This being was a messenger of false prophecy, and if she had really called it down from the Aether, then the gods of the Aether were liars and frauds. She survived the outpouring of cosmic energy that came with the death of the Angel, and waited patiently at her summoning circle, leaving her conduit to the Supernal open until she felt something from the Abyss writhe its way in. Even then, she needed to call on the power of Forces to communicate with the thing, which took the form of a radio signal emitting from the point in the air where the "false" angel had appeared. It identified itself as the Sacred Frequency. It said it was drawn by Ceres' strong will and the "courage" she showed in taking the life of one who dared to deny her truth, and this, in its way, was true. It said it could show her the truth she was looking for, if she would prove it in the mortal world as an agent of the Sacred Frequency. Ceres accepted the bargain and in an instant, her mind and soul were no longer her own. The Sacred Frequency blended radio waves and her brain waves, letting her access both the signals of a station broadcasting nearby and the thoughts of everyone tuned into it. She saw visions of a great structure somewhere between a cathedral and a radio control tower, and masses of people, tuned into its signal, walking through its great doors, to...what? To unity. To add their signals to the Sacred Frequency, and to transcend. Taking a new shadow name ("AM," both for the radio frequency band and for the "new dawn" she believes she will bring to mankind), Ceres fled her Consilium and set off in search of broadcasting enthusiasts. These days, she appears to web developers, HAM radio hobbyists (the ones that are still around), and any Obrimos frustrated by conflicts between their beliefs and the Aether's black-and-white delineation of right and wrong (or true and false). As with many Left-Handed Legacies, initiates rarely know what they're getting into until they take an Attainment, at which point the Sacred Frequency hums in their minds and souls and it may be too late to ever sever the connection.

                                                With their natural power over the Forces of the universe augmented by Mind magic, Keepers of the Sacred Frequency learn first to hear radio signals in their thoughts. Then, they learn to send their thoughts to follow those radio signals, broadcasting an emotion across a large area or even implanting thoughts into a listener's mind. The ultimate goal of the Legacy is to establish a network of the broadcasting cathedrals they dream of across the world, until all of mankind is in tune with the thought of their Abyssal god.

                                                            This is the first draft of a legacy I've been toying with. First, it gives the Arrow another legacy, because really? 4 legacies, one of those Left-Handed? Second, it's a combat-focused legacy that isn't combat-exclusive like the Brotherhood of the Demon Wind or the Perfected Adepts.

                                                            The Sworn Companions

                                                            In Ancient Greece, the shield, not the sword or spear, was the symbol of war. The Sworn Companions understand this truth: The heart of warfare is to protect and defend, not for the purpose of destruction. These warriors are dedicated to the defense of a person, place, thing or group.

                                                            Arcana: Space, (optional) Life

                                                            Oblations: Standing watch over a place or person, meditation on vows, training regimins

                                                            Names: Companion, Hetairoi, Knight, Shield, Mook (Perjorative)

                                                            Special: Must possess the Oathbound merit. If they are not currently under an oath, all attainments cease to function.

                                                            1st Attainment: Semper Intenti- Space 2, (Optional Life 1), Gnosis 3
                                                            The Companion is permanently under the effects of Space 2 Scry, and Space 1 Finder towards the target of their oath. If they have life 1, they also gain the effects of Life 1 Healer's Trance toward their oaths target. However this only tells them whether the target is totally healthy or not unless the Companion takes the time to concentrate.

                                                            2nd Attainment: Semper Paratus- Space 3, (Optional Life 2), Gnosis 5
                                                            The Companion may open a portal to their charge as a reflexive action for the cost of 1 Mana. With Life 2, they receive an always-on Life 2 Organic Resilience. This armor stacks with any other mage armor, but not Organic Resilience.

                                                            3rd Attainment: Semper Armati- Space 4, (Optional Life 3), Gnosis 7
                                                            The Companion gains a Pocket Dimension, per the spell, that may only be used to store weapons and armor, including their ritual tools. They may spend an instant action and 1 mana to retrieve or stock any arms or armor to and from this pocket dimension, and if they choose to retrieve armor, they may choose whether it is summoned near them, or already worn.With Life 3, the Companion gains their Life Arcana in extra dots of physical attributes. They may redistribute these spare dots by spending one mana and an instant action. This stacks with Honing the Form.
