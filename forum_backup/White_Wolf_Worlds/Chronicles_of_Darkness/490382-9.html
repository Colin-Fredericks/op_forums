                            Pattern Scouring Clinic: You're a young Mage and don't have much money or resources yet. You just go into one of these clinics where you lay down on a nice comfortable bed, shred part of your pattern to obtain Mana and a helpful Oubrimos draws the Mana out and stores it as Tass to be sold to other Mages.                             Dream Hotel: Most Mages don't have access to a level 5 Hallow or a Demesne which makes it hard to go on an Astral Journey. A Dream Hotel is built on top of one of these locations and has full medical facilities to sustain your body on an indefinite Astral Journey. You'd need nurses, cleaning staff, receptionist, etc.

                            Matchmaking Apps: There would be so many apps for matching up a mortal to a supernatural. Humans willing to donate blood to a Vampire, invite a Changeling into their dreams, etc. would use the app to find someone looking for this service.

                            Supernatural only clubs: The ultimate in restricted venues, you need to be some sort of supernatural to get in. It's the kind of place that you can be yourself without worrying about scaring the normals.
