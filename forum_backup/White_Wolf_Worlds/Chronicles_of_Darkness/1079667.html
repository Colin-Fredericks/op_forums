    [2E] How to murder Uratha?

    04-16-2017, 11:53 PM
    My next story starts with PCs finding their Uratha friend as body in Funeral. Idea for it is he was murdered – not kill in combat, not dead of accident. As for werewolf, he was killed in wicked way by someone – marks would lead to heart attack or other humans natural causes. The problem is how to make this possible? Uratha Regeneration will resolve any toxin or non-silver wound ( and wounds by silver are very strange for coroners and other Uratha ). Any ideas how can you kill other Uratha in white gloves?

      Coroners are likely willing to accept and shrug off the appearance of supernatural things, since they'd rather not go insane or be confronted by said supernatural. Or they could be lazy or corrupt.

      I'm not sure how you're wording this. 'marks would lead to heart attack or other humans natural causes.' Are you saying they were attacked physically, and then had a heart attack? Or they just died of seemingly natural causes, like a heart attack?

      Either way, magic is probably a weapon you could use in this case. A spirit could do it with an Influence or Numina, so a totem could do it, or a fetish. Any number of the other splats have powers that cause damage without necessarily damaging a person. Werewolf lodges have strange blessings that allow them to overcome or go around usual werewolf advantages.

              Oh, then magic, or just some brute force. If, for whatever reason, a werewolf couldn't go into gauru or rely on gauru, or went into gauru and passed out coming out of it, they could simply be killed like a normal human, providing they weren't high Primal Urge or had a low Essence. Things like Efficient Killer or someone with a silver weapon and lots of skill or bad luck, or both, on behalf of the werewolf could insta-kill one. Not the kind of stuff you want to throw at a starting PC but fine for NPCs. Certainly a brutal hacking with a silver axe would leave nasty wounds, but a single stab with a silver sword into the heart might just leave one. A silver bullet, as well.

              You may want to be careful if this is going to tie into a mystery, as things like Death and Insight Gifts can get investigators answers pretty easily.
