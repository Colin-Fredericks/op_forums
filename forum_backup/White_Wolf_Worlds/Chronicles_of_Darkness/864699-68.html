            Originally posted by Scriptorian View Post
            Professional Training Merit: do the dice tricks (at 2 and 5 dots) apply to supernatural uses of the Asset Skills?

            Because I'm working on a Beast character and realized that professional training as an athlete would somewhat inexplicably improve his ability to breath fire (it's treated as a thrown weapon, Dex + Athletics). The power-gamer in me says YES, but the storyteller in me has concerns...

            Edit: this might just be a weird issue I have with the merit in general, because Firearms is explicitly a thing that can be benefited, which would probably be even more useful in combat than getting the tricks on fire-breathing. I guess it feels weird to have this potential benefit to combat skills hidden away in this merit. I could be a world-class sniper, but unless I got "professional training" in firearms, I don't get the 9-again benefit. I know 9-again is not that big a deal ... but it's just the kind of fiddly unique bonus that my inner min-maxer wants to exploit and I worry I'm making character decisions based on mechanical benefit when they make little sense in-world.
            You're getting a lot of interpretation and house rules, so for the record: there is no exemption in RAW for supernatural powers.

            The ground that many of these house rules are built on is shaky at best in my opinion - if a power includes a skill in its dice pool, that's because mundane capacity with that skill objectively improves your ability to apply the power. Restricting the benefits to mundane-only rolls makes sense only as a balance consideration, and arguments that try to anchor that decision in verisimilitude or narrative cohesion are fighting against the system itself to do so.

            If balance is an important consideration for your table, it's not a bad idea to apply similar restrictions.

                                    so, I was thinking about... crabs. Have been watching too many documentaries and now want to play them too! There are a few options for that with TheOnyxpath products. Discounting Exalted, where you can go straight Lunar Crab (it's awesome), there are a few options for Chronicles of Darkness.
                                    - One could be Brineborn splat, though they are more about fishes, not crustaceans, and have social characteristics that do not go that well with crabs anyway. Though, their system of marine based mutations could be played around for desired effect.
                                    - The other a Beast and yes, strangely enough there is some horror with crabs. A terror of unknown ocean depths. would work well with a mix of toughness powers and probably need a couple flavor modded nightmares. like substitute "you are falling" with "you are drowning" and like that.
                                    - The spirit claimed, as with those jaguar shapeshifters. though it would be in this case a more of a singular monster. Similarly, making it a PC would be junky I think.
                                    - Essentially hunter monster, the same as above but even more so. Making a crab monster with Dread Powers is easy enough, but again not very suitable for being PC in my opinion.

                                    - Original changing breed with WtF system. This could be easy and interesting enough to try. design a couple alternative forms (small crab/buff crabman) with appropriate buffs. No dexterity buff, but get sweet armor viable against bullets. No regen, but ability to 'molt' with some tie and essence healing to full health and removing physical crippling effects. No effect by silver, but allergic to pure salt (not seawater) or maybe to modern solvants that deal aggro in similar manner. No normal fetishes but the art of using something like fetish tattoos. Also, no ability to sidestep normally, but can turn sufficiently deep water into a tunnel to the other side (which one?). As for the supernatural powers... could pretty much mirror werewolves with marine twist or do as with brineborn, a mix of numina and mutation merits. Could even just dump that and make sort of 'essence technology' instead of just spells.
                                    For the fluff, probably reverse the process. Not a big fan of those ancient spirit/gods dominating the whole splat. I was thinking to make it a historically disjointed sub-splat living mostly solitary or small family units along the coasts in hotter climates. with their own varied myths, but no "one and true" origin, coming together only with the modern advent of communication and transportation.

                                    So what do you think, worth it to write up the splat or one of the other 4 choices already do it well enough? would it be interesting?
