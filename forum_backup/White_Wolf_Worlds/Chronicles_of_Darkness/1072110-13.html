    Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post
    Wait a second. Were Ammit supposed to be Stormwracked or founded by ex-Stormwracked? In interpereted it as the latter, as the Queen of Storms wouldn't convert members of Ammit into Stormwracked if they weren't going out and fighting Darkspawn personally. Also because Sworn of Storms don't gain any powers that are useful outside of combat.
    I thought of them as ex-Stormwracked, as part of the reason they aren't a generic Stormite cell is that they did "compromise", in her mind-they stopped finding collateral damage acceptable, and started worrying about the solution being worse than the problem (mostly because they realized they could generate Taint, but that's hardly new for humans to only put the breaks on when they realize they're making more problems for themselves). It's also because, as mentioned, #Ammit is mostly a cabal of hackers, community organizers, and occasionally information brokers if they really need the mystical big guns. They don't really engage in fisticuffs.

    Yes, this is a quiet lambasting of how self-destructive Storms is.

    EDIT: They are, however, a stone's throw away from the rage of the Queen herself-I wouldn't be surprised if they have quite a few members with Supernatural Merits, both because of their own Astral proximity to the living storm quietly agitating their own hidden powers and because Cults of the Dark target the kind of people exiled for their power, and #Ammit tends to recruit those victimized by Dark Cults, including ex-members. I don't think that tendency is reliable enough to figure into their Status progression (it's a side effect, not a mystic gift-they'd probably be working on turning into an Endowment if it happened reliably), but it does figure into their more open attitude towards monsters; they know how close they are to being monsters themselves, and that a lot of the time, "monsters" are just people with strange powers and strange needs. That's part of why I noted why they have a low opinion of Stormfront types who want to corrupt their community into yet another facet of the Internet Hate Machine, and why they have a neutral opinion on Beasts.

                Nevermind. From my reading, Sworn Invocations are largely mute points without their Bequests, but Invocations have one other trait you missed-they are a potential Willpower source. If your actions during a scene are in line with one of your Invocations, and have not broken the Ban, you can roll your dots-every success is a Willpower point refreshed. Admittedly, Sworn don't have many dots, but it's not completely useless for them.

                Stormwracked get the short end of the stick-because so many of them are Sworn to the Queen Herself, it's not uncommon for them to lack Bequests, since she has a hard time making those, and her Princesses can't keep up with the demand. They have their Mystery Cult Initiation, the ability to channel the Queen (at a significant hazard to their health) and Golneau patronage instead.

                Of course, that doesn't mean there isn't a market for paranoia horror caused by realizing the Lady of Cleansing Fires whispers to people who are just that angry. She doesn't tempt-she promises. All she wants you to do is continue pursuing revenge, continue to purge the Dark-and to make sure your unenlightened brethren don't get in the way of justice. Falling to her influence isn't a drop, it's a sideways step, and nobody notices-not until the clay cyborg is tearing through their flesh because the proper cell the Stormwracked is a part of has become an obstacle, and the Golneau needs a body.
