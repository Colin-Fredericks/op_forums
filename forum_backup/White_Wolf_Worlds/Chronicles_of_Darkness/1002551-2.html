                                                It really sounds like you two are arguing past each other, both saying the exact same thing but still in disagreement due to word choices.

                                                Edit: We all seem to be in agreement that Mind can't reveal their physical (or what best constitutes as physical for such creatures) with Mind (unless you're mind controlling them and telling them to willingly manifest or something), and all seem to agree that Mind can still detect the presence of their thoughts.

                                                Now where we seem to be getting hung up on is using the word 'reveal'. Again, Mind can't make their Death/Spirit based substance visible but you can can almost certainly perceive the location and presence of their minds, which resides in the same place as where their substance is. So while you can't see their form, you can detect that they are there and know their location based off of that, which is often good enough for most magical purposes.

                                                You can also cast Mind spells on them to give them some sort of physical form. Probably not theirs since you likely don't know exactly what they look like since you've never seen them, but you could cast a visible illusion on them in order to let everyone who can't sense minds or detect them in some other way use that as an 'attack/interact/notice me senpai' beacon.
