      1. Reap Memory requires you to know what memory you're trying to extract, which with ancient mummies is going to be a crapshoot. It works on the living, but mummies have a complicated relationship with whether they are living or dead. Princes of the Conquered Land's crossover rules suggest that Memory has a unique relationship to mummies compared to most sentient beings' memories, existing at a remove from the mummy. All of that adds up to "there's so many complicating factors that you might as well just conclude it won't work." You might be able to steal minor memories like things they've done in their current Descent, but not anything major enough to impact the Memory rating. (And, of course, they can apply Supernatural Tolerance to resist.)

      2. They're kind of not related at all except insofar as they are all practices that typically involve somewhat meaningful interactions. They're closer to the glamour harvested by changelings than the resonance soaked up by spirits, because memories are always interpersonal and involve some level of feeling, whereas resonance can be impersonal and symbolic. A fire can start in an abandoned building and that will generate fire resonance for spirits of fire, regardless of if anybody's still around to care about what's happening to the building. Even then, abiding memories are intrinsically personal and involve direct contact and interaction between the ghost and a specific living person. They're relational. Glamour harvesting doesn't have to be relational. The changeling has to be present at a place where emotion is being felt and expressed, but does not need to actually interact with the people expressing it or participate in the event it's focused around. They can hide and not be noticed and it will still work.

                  Whereas generating new memories (with a living person) seems closer glamour harvesting ...
                  A place that generates memories isn't going to fall under the Making Memories header but under the Finding Memories header. Making Memories covers the method by which the absent can deliberately create tradable memories, and that method requires direct contact and interaction with one of the living. Memories can naturally generate in places that have seen monumental events, and the Absent can collect those memories as per Finding Memories, but it's separate from Making Memories because it's not something you can deliberately and reliably recreate through your own actions.

                  I don't think drawing comparisons to resonance and glamour will help you understand this concept better. It's a relatively superficial comparison.

                          I mean, yes, in the sense that a place with a consistent resonance that gets a lot of visitors and produce circumstances with significant emotional context is a place that can store or generate a lot of Memories of a given type, but that's got nothing to do with resonance by itself — the only things that Resonance alone attracts are spirits and Dreamborn and the like, who are drawn there because they are sustained by that resonance and have a sense for it.

                          People go to places for personal reasons. If that happens often enough in the same sort of way, a resonance might build up, but spirits pursue the presence of their resonant phenomena, not necessarily the types of specific circumstances that form emotionally significant Memories, and conceptual spirits like those formed around emotions are explicitly rarer than the more physical choirs; the sort of locations that "generate" new Memories might have consistent enough resonance to sustain that sort of spirit such that there is an incentive to prompt visitors to feel those same sort of feelings, but that's multiple steps divorced from harvesting resonance being comparable to collecting Memories.

                          A Memory is a discrete thing. Essence and Glamour are like juice — they might have flavors that different types of spirit or changeling prefer, but the forms of those energies that are harvested through their respective mechanics are not coming from a concrete systems object with qualities other than "this provides one point of Essence/Glamour," and most of the other forms of those energies that are concrete objects (i.e. the most commonplace goblin fruits or the strange bits of meat and vegetation that appear in the Shadow near a locus) only have that quality.

                          The closest mechanical process to forming a Memory is forming a Chamber or increasing a locations degree of similarity to a Chamber in Beast, and that's likewise for reasons orthogonal to sustenance.

                              A mention of cosmological backgrounding for why the Absent can't fuck around with Arisen Memory:

                              A lot of the metaphysics around what the Rite of Return did includes what is essentially locking the individual to the individual in question. Making sense of that, the Arisen are the Arisen because their talents as themselves was inherently valuable to the grand sacrifice to Azur(PS also the Shan'iatu), and in particular that the Shan'iatu saw it as something that could make the Decree and hold out to...*checks notes* the end of time.

                              This ran into the opposite problem that the Shan'iatu really didn't want to-because while the individual set of talents were important, the actual individual, the will of the individual you might say, was not something they really wanted. They wanted....well, not quite Terminators, but they wanted automatons who could be used to subjugate civilization toward it's bronze-age ideology that couldn't challenge those parameters.

                              The catch to the matter is that they (the Judges AND the Shan'iatu, because I haven't made it clear the Judges are implicit in this) couldn't have it both ways on that point-not just because it requires the personality behind the talents to actually make it work, because civilization de/forming is not algorithmic, but because the presence of Sutekh as Will basically said you can only work with, effectively, machines or people, and the demands of the Rite of Return ultimately landed, however much no one crafting it wanted it to, on the side of people.

                              Because the Rite of Return is a hyperbolic machine of preservation, this all consequentially means that, while any given Arisen's Memory is free to wander free afield of any given Arisen, in the same way their parts of the soul can wander, it is preserved by the Judges, including Sutekh, on the grounds that basically the Arisen are allowed to be themselves, even as they develop, in regards to the perpetuity that the Rite of Return is designed to carry on.

                              Sooooooo at the best reading, even if the Absent, who are on a scale of operation lower than the Gods and Judges who are entwined with the Arisen at a wide margin, are somehow able to fuck with Memories....it's not going to be a matter that lasts in any meaningful way. More realistically, the related entities aren't even going to allow interference. Imagine if a Deathlord, hell, even a Kerebos, was able to fuck with the makeup of an Arisen, an agent of the nearest to canon creator of the cosmos as Chronicles will allow. Yeah, fuck no. Better to preserve Memories as is owed to the Arisen as something that is just.....there? for the Arisen if they can give a shit after all of the distraction the Judges can set up. But not something that can be taken up by anyone more than it needs to be-not like they could deny Sutekh on this subject, but if the line is the individual in question and not other fucking other powers, that's better than any other outcome.

                              So, why can't the Absent fuck with Arisen Memory?
