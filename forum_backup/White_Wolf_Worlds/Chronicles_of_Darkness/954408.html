    Invited to a Family Dinner...

    08-20-2016, 11:37 AM
    So, this come up as a pondering when thinking on a Beast with Family Ties, we know that the Beast can gain Satiety from a Family Dinner where they witness their chosen kin (if any) hunt and take down prey, whether for Vitae or what have you, the Beast can gain Satiety at the same time.

    What if a Beast has Family Ties with a Mage? Or a Promethean? Now, I haven't read Promethean 2nd yet, though these two splats aren't really hunter types... Prometheans can soak up Pyros though don't get it from living people like a Changeling could get Glamour from people even though they don't hunt... Mages power up with Mana and I suppose a Beast could join a particularly nebulous group of Mages in a hunt in the sticks ending in a blood sacrifice for that sweet tass though that's not the norm usually, right?

    I'm not sure how Mummies can collect Sekhem as I've rarely come across them and whilst my one friend does own the book, looking at it I found it pretty obtuse reading...

    Anyway, back to the point of the post and thread here, how does your Beast solve a Family Dinner when their Kin doesn't hunt or in any other way feed off others? Or would you rule that for example's sake if your Beast and their Promethean bro went and got the latter some new Pyros that'd boost the Beast's Satiety even though no hunt or person was involved?

          Going to illustrate the corollary to Helms's point.
          Originally posted by Family Dinner Systems
          In order to use Family Dinner, a Beast must accompany a supernatural being as he hunts, feeds, or both. It doesn’t matter whether it’s a vampire thirsting after blood, a werewolf stalking an intruder in his territory, or a changeling feeding on human emotions, so long as he is actively tracking prey and/or taking sustenance from a hunt. The Storyteller has the final say on whether or not an activity counts, but it must involve a monster’s supernatural nature or appetites directly; mundane hunting or feeding does not invoke Family Dinner.

          If the Beast witnesses a successful hunt — one that results in taking down the intended quarry, either literally or figuratively — she immediately regains 1 Satiety. If she observes a successful feeding, she also gains 1 Satiety. These benefits are cumulative, so a hunt that also results in a feeding nets 2 Satiety total. The actual number of creatures involved in the scene does not matter. The Satiety gained is based on the acts witnessed, not the number of participants.
          Mages pursuing their Obsessions to the detriment of other beings is thematically appropriate; ostensibly Tormented Prometheans regaining Willpower the only way the Condition allows them to operates similarly.

          On the more fuel-oriented side of things, there's a few example Legacy oblations that could conceivably serve the same function (to say nothing of Devouring the Slain), and the lacuna is still a thing in 2e.

          The Arisen are more likely to let their cousins feed by performing the sorts of actions that earn them a reset on their Descent counter or cannibalizing relics.

          Speaking of cannibals, though, it's important to remember that Beasts are not confined to the PC splats when it comes to sympathetic consumption; Pandorans and Shuankhsen are valid kin, as are things from the Depths and the Abyss.
