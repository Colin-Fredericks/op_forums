                                Slightly off the flow of the topic, but I was thinking about the new Julii Bane and how much I like it. I think the Strix parts are pretty obvious, but I really like the extra dagger in their backs about it applying to all ephemeral beings. Romans were obsessed with ancestor worship: they held festivals in honor of their dead family members, and made sacrifices to malevolent ghosts to keep them off their backs. Nobility publicly venerated their departed loved ones with inscriptions and statues, and Roman emperors were elevated into godhood upon death.

                                A low Humanity Julii is a superstitious monster. She knows the dead and the gods are always watching (and judging), and she makes sure she never crosses them. Even if she doesn't give a shit about the Vaticinators and their "prophecies", she goes to every damn augury because that's what you do.

                                It also plays really well into that self-deceiving invincibility the Julii see in themselves. Young Julii look at the other Kindred with their "banes" and pity them. Julii have no weaknesses; the foreign clans are corrupt and twisted. Old Julii know better: they know that this is the weight of destiny. Only a Roman could be so blessed by his righteous ancestors, and the great patron gods of Rome. It's not a burden: it's responsibility. The Julii know obedience to their patrons, nothing more.

                                Of course, a few Julii know the truth, but their havens are little but ash, filled with fragments of coal-black feathers

                                (This isn't any attempt to argue against people who don't care for it, this is just what I found neat. )

                                            Originally posted by branford View Post

                                            Imperial Mysteries for Awakening also had some suggested mechanics for attributes and skills at 6+.

                                            For those who are interested, here's the very small section in Imperial Mysteries on the subject and system for attributes and skills at 6+

                                            ***[Mods: If I posted too much material, kindly delete. Thank you.]


                                            Storytelling Transhuman Ability

                                            How do you portray someone with Intelligence 7 or
                                            Persuasion 8? Minor archmasters may possess one or two
                                            6-dot Attributes and Skills, putting them on par with the
                                            most gifted Sleepers who ever lived, but at 7 or 8 dots, traits
                                            approach levels the real world has never seen.

                                            Intellectual abilities are particularly challenging because
                                            they affect characters’ ability to predict events and uncover
                                            information. If this becomes a question, cheat — assume
                                            transhuman intellects guess and compensate for circumstances
                                            that would normally only be known to the Storyteller,
                                            or plans executed by player controlled or Storyteller
                                            characters. If you give this privilege to Storyteller characters,
                                            players’ transhumans deserve the same privileges. Furthermore,
                                            you should make it clear at the outset that these
                                            benefi ts are available, and impose reasonable limits.

                                            One approach to the challenge is to look at real situations
                                            that enhance natural abilities. A hyper-intelligent
                                            archmage possesses an internal “search engine” that allows
                                            her to exploit her memories faster and more effectively,
                                            never gets her math wrong, and can fi nd patterns in extremely
                                            large data sets. Sleepers react to a Seeker with
                                            a Presence of 7 as if she was a celebrity, with the same
                                            degree of distraction and obsession. Characters with
                                            transhuman Physical Stamina scores recover from illness
                                            and injury as if they were under skilled medical care, even
                                            if they’re alone in the wild. Characters with more than
                                            human Skills might be able to use them without normal
                                            tools, or possess an unusual level of focus and confi dence
                                            in stressful situations.

                                            It’s important to note that traits in the Storytelling
                                            System do not necessarily match casual uses of the same
                                            words. For example, we may think that high intelligence
                                            correlates with the ability to predict and shape political
                                            institutions, but much of that “social” intelligence is a
                                            better fi t for the Manipulation Attribute.

                                            Optional System: Reversal Points

                                            If you like, you might give hyper-competent characters
                                            possess “reversal points” to represent superior insight and
                                            preparation. Each reversal point allows a player to retroactively
                                            declare a basic (1 point – bodyguards were shadowing
                                            you in case of attack), moderate (2 points – your countersniper
                                            shoots the sniper), complex (3 points – “My spy
                                            network knew about this weeks ago”) preparation to deal
                                            with an event that has just been revealed in play, such as a
                                            betrayal or unexpected encounter.

                                            Your character possesses one reversal point for each dot
                                            in an Attribute or Skill above 5, and may only spend points
                                            when the situation would apply both to the linked Trait and
                                            the declared preparation. You may also spend points to nullify
                                            other characters’ reversal points on a 1 for 1 basis.

                                            Your character recovers all reversal points at the start of
                                            each game chapter.
