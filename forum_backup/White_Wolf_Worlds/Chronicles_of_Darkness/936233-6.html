    Casting Rend Lifespan on someone in the past would certainly create ripples, but it isn't able to be cast on a subject in the past with Temporal Sympathy.
    Yup.

    Rewrite History would certainly create ripples, and it can be used on a subject in the past without Temporal Sympathy as long as the subject has Unchanged Sympathy with its present self, but does require it if the Sympathy is any weaker.
    Rewrite History isn't changing common history outside the scope of its subjects' timelines, much like Temporal Summoning isn't actually yanking the subject's past self out of the past.

    Shifting Sands is actual time travel but doesn't require Temporal Sympathy.
    And rewinds you a semifixed distance from the present, either by less than a minute or in an increasingly bug-prone hour-by-hour arrangement, neither of which really engages with Temporal Sympathy in the sense of "the weight of history has built up around this point in time, so it's harder to interface with using Time magic."

    Corridors of Time is actual time travel that does require Temporal Sympathy if the subject has weaker than Unchanged Temporal Sympathy with itself at the time you send it back to.
    Yup.

    Web-Weaver isn't even a Time spell but can be used on subjects in the past with Temporal Sympathy and without being combined with a Time spell.
    You'll notice that the spell uses Temporal Sympathy to circumvent the need for current physical contact rather than to create a sympathetic connection in the past, which would create ripples on account of how spatial sympathy works.

    Maybe the quality of a spell that allows it to qualify for Temporal Sympathy is obvious to you, but you have consistently demonstrated a far better intuitive grasp of rules interactions than the average CofD fan.
    "If you want to use magic to change the past as experienced by people other than the specific targets of a spell, you cannot just shunt a spell you know into the past and must instead move yourself to a point prior to the spell's intended chronological point of origin and cast the spell you want to use" is neither complicated nor contradicted by the state of affairs implied by the set of example spells that use Temporal Sympathy.

    If you want to change The Past with magic, you yourself have to go there and do magic. Straightforward enough, yes?

                                I try to give some very boring but mechanical explanations to this:

                                What Temporal Sympathy did before errata was basically time travel with every possible spell of every arcanum. This was either never meant to be or struck the developers as too difficult to handle.

                                What Temporal Sympathy is doing now is giving you a possibility to influence the past of "further away than unchanged" subjects. In addition it is reducing the possibility of changing the past at all (no matter what connection you have to the past) to Time spells only. I think the latter one is VERY important and totally intentional. If you want to mess with the past, get good at time magic, it is not enough anymore to just get 2 dots in Time and do anything with it.

                                To when you actually NEED Temporal Sympathy? Any time you want to interact (change, bring to the present or just watch) with anything from the past that is further away than unchanged connection. If you look at the Shifting Sands spell (which does not need Temporal Sympathy), I am extremely certain that this spell is not meant to bring you to a point of your own past, where you were changed! While this might theoretically be possible with a lot of reach, this is clearly not the intention of the spell. That is what Corridors of Time is for. You may also notice, that Corridors of Time doesn't need Temporal Sympathy if you want you put yourself a couple of days back in Time if you have not changed in that time period.

                                So I suggest seeing the weaker than "unchanged" temporal connection as something as a veil, you have to pierce with Temporal Sympathy if needed.

                                                    Originally posted by Juhn View Post
                                                    I'm a little confused about Incognito Presence having a Mana cost now. Does hiding from ghosts or cameras cost Mana as well? Giving a basic stealth spell a Mana cost seems strange to me.

                                                    It's because its withstood only by the subject (tht is, if you cast it on an unwilling subject, them trying to be seen/heard anyway is how its withstood). It just makes everyone else ignore them by removing their mental "presence" in the world, the thing Mastigos call an aura.

                                                    It is therefore an automatic success on any stealth actions the subject takes, and any attempt to spot them that isn't supernaturally powered automatically fails.

                                                    Therefore, it costs Mana.
