        Cryptids in the DtD sense (animals mutated by exposure to the God-Machine) could become intelligent and also gain the ability to communicate. An Angel or Demon could also end up with an animal Cover, but that doesn't sound like what you're looking for.

        Similarly, Hedge Beasts (at least in 1e) are basically animal-form hobgoblins, so a talking dog would be perfectly reasonable. They're not mentioned in the 2e manuscript preview from the KS, but they're not explicitly disallowed, either. Although a 2e Hedge Dog would have the Hedge Denizen condition, meaning it knows three Goblin Contracts and gains Deprived whenever it leaves the Hedge, which again might not be what you're looking for. (In 1e these were handled by the Hedge Beast Companion merit, rather than Retainer.)

                Originally posted by Stralos View Post
                Is there a difference between 1 success and 4 success when an action is not contested ? (excluding exceptional success )
                Generally no, but combat is a huge exception to that rule. Outside of combat (and certain supernatural powers such as most Auspex uses in VtR) the design philosophy is that the number of successes shouldn't matter as long as it's a Success.

                Another question. Do players get minuses when trying to achieve something, they do not have a speciality in ?
                For example if I have 4 dots in drive skill, with no specialities and I try to fly a helicopter. Will I get any penalties for trying to achieve a task I am not specialised at ? I might be a pro stunt driver, but I am not a pilot.
                I don't think second edition has rules for it, but it makes sense to penalise actions when the character doesn't have a related Specialty but only for highly specialised actions. You definitely need a Helicopter spec to pilot a helicopter, but not for cars which is what the Drive Skill is primarily about.

                                                        Originally posted by Stralos View Post
                                                        Is there a difference between 1 success and 4 success when an action is not contested ? (excluding exceptional success )

                                                        For example I ask my players to roll a perception roll Wits + Composure. My players succeed with 1,2 & 3 successes. Do players who got 1 success get less information than players who got 3 success ? I could not find anything about this in the core book. It seems all it matters is if you get 1 success or 5 (when the roll is not a versus roll).
                                                        Tessie already answered this, but to be more specific: unless the roll says otherwise, explicitly no. The probability math of the Storytelling System isn't equipped to handle granular successes like, say, the World of Darkness or Exalted's systems do; it's not uncommon to roll only a single success on even quite large dice pools. If you roll successes, your success isn't partial.

                                                        This probably doesn't matter that much for your example, where multiple players in the same party are succeeding, but characters will appear to underperform in general if you restrict the results of lower-threshold successes.
