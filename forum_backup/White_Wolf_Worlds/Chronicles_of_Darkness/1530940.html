                  Elemental (Clockwork automoton) Plaything of the Autumn Court, who think they are an Angel and a Integrator Messenger Demon...
                  The two by chance end up going to the same bar, the messenger notices the Lost first, he is suspicious of anyone who has something strange, he notices the supernatural nature of his companion who is talking to someone else, normally he wouldn't care, but this case was a exception, with *Eavesdrop* he tries to evaluate what the two are talking about, most of it is useless nonsense, but he can barely hide his surprise when he realizes that the Lost mentions an angel, unfortunately his mentions are very vague and the messenger cannot draw a definitive conclusion with just this little bit of information. He watches a little more and waits for the guy leave the bar to follow him at a safe distance. It was night and already dark, ideal for what he intended to do. In front of a closed traffic signal, the messenger takes out of his pocket army knife that he kept with him and points it at the Elemental's back, with a threatening whisper he order him to follow until a vacant lot at the end of the street, the The messenger expected him to show a minimal reaction, but the guy laughs lightly and just nods in agreement, very strange, this situation was very peculiar for the demon's taste, but it was not time to hesitate, after all he still had the advantage.

                  Inside the vacant lot the messenger asks some simple questions to the changeling who doesn't seem to understand most of it, not even referring to the angel by a name or designation, just calling him "the angel". Disappointed, the messenger thinks about his next step, he is unlikely to get any valuable information from this guy and if it weren't for the obvious supernatural trait that comes from him he could easily pass for an ordinary human being, but perhaps he would still be useful, he doesn't appear to be a mere cultist and is too functional to be a simple God-Machine experiment. While rambling, the elemental lightly holds the messenger's hand who was holding the knife, he shouts a warning, but it is ignored and with a slow and agonizing movement the blade is dragged across the changeling's skin, however the sensation is not that of a cut in the flesh, or warm of blood, but metal scratching metal, this accompanied by a horrible sharp creak, then the messenger has his wrist pulled by the elemental and his body is knocked against the earth, the demon soon recovers only to witness the elemental laughing at his conquest, not a happy laugh, but a forced and almost mechanical one.

                  A thin layer of golden dust comes out of the changeling's body, then this trickle turns into a dense smoke that covers his body, showing his true appearance to the world, a large and impossibly wide body for a human, his arms and legs, were sets of pipes and pistons moved by huge gears in place of joints and covered by a layer of metal that appeared to be iron or copper, his body a crude imitation of armor that underneath hid more gears, his face was a bad joke, sets of small gears and metal plates that tried to emulate features, but were not capable of expressing any emotion, giving this work to the voice that was, more of a metallic creak with a touch of a childish voice. The messenger already realized his mistake, the mysterious golden dust already revealed everything, but even if that were the case he didn't need any evidence to know that that thing had nothing to do with angels or the God-Machine, that shape was ugly, exaggerated and grossly efficient, even if the angels were capable of a joke they would never do something so imperfect. For a brief moment the messenger is distracted and lets part of his true form escape, his eyes for a second abandon the human form giving way to two blackened visors, his skin shows several inscriptions that resemble a computer circuit instead of his veins, everything quickly returns to normal, but it is enough for the elemental to admire and try to capture the messenger for his angel, with no expectation of stopping that thing in direct combat he makes brief use of *Imagine* the Lost acts with confusion, his brain unable to process the information he has just received, taking advantage of the distraction time he takes the opportunity to escape, the elemental, still confused, tries to follow him at a smaller pace, but is stopped by a car that moves towards him, hitting him in full, it wasn't fatal, but it leaves him unconscious long enough for the messenger to escape and plan what to do next, little does he know that he just changed a life forever, but that's another story.

                  A Osiris Promethean and a Su-Menent (Mummy Necromancer)

                                      "You should have that scratch looked at, then," says the woman in cat eye glasses. She smiles with her mouth. "I have a first aid kit in my car. Would you mind coming out with me? I couldn't leave you alone like this, knowing something might happen."

                                      The woman who is not alive looks around. People are starting to stare. She pulls her broken smartphone from her pocket to cover her missing ear and stands up to leave.

                                      The woman who is not dead sets a few bills by the abandoned bottle and follows her out. She will ask her questions about the empty graves where her cultists cannot hear.

                                                  Guardian of the Veil (Mage) and Kindly Undertaker (Geist)
                                                  The old man walked into Molly's and sat down at the first table he saw in front of him, it was a particularly cold night and the bar was packed with people, but there didn't seem to be any particular event, not that he minded, but it's always good to know what people are doing. He exchanges a brief greeting with the bartender and orders a drink with some snacks. The last days weren't easy, the magician's job could be quite thankless at times, he was already used to it, but today he was considering whether he should continue with it, the worst thing was that he couldn't vent to anyone. All the people who he could talk to would call him crazy, at least he could drink, calm down and hopefully carry on for another day.

                                                  Already slightly drunk, the old man is surprised by a woman who sits next to him, she apologizes for disturbing him, the old man waves briefly and goes back to drinking. While waiting for his drink, the girl asks how he feels, the old man responds with: “Why do you ask?” something the girl responds to with “I’ve met some rookie detectives who just came from a crime scene worthy of a gore horror movie with better face than yours.” The old man takes another sip. "Seriously?" she nods “Are you a detective?” She smiles slightly. “Almost, coroner.” the old man reacts with a whistle, an old habit from his youth. "And you? What do you work with?” “I work with internal affairs” lies the mage, but not completely, as his real job is to watch over other awakened so that they don't get out of line. "Hard day?" she asks as she sips a martini. “Most are.” He responds by taking another sip. “But these last few weeks have been special.” The young woman asks what happened. “It was with a godson (apprentice) of a colleague of mine. He messed up and doesn't even know it and I guess he don't even cares about. The idiot. I promised I would keep an eye on him and now I might have to fire him (from life)” the mage sighs slightly nervously. “Does your colleague know about this?” asks the girl. “He's already left (he's missing) I don't even have a way to contact him” he takes another sip to calm down. “Sometimes I wonder why I do this.” The girl responds with a simple “I know how it is” the old man would normally think this was nothing more than empty words, but this time, call it intuition if you want, he believes there is something more in store. He passes his hand over the ring on his finger and considers reading the young woman's mind, but soon gives up on that thought, it's not right to use magic on sleepeers for a petty reason like that.

                                                  The girl then begins to tell her story, about when she started as a coroner and how it was interesting at first, but after a few cases her initial enthusiasm gave way to frustration, then depression and later apathy, each case had become just another case, just another problem to be solved, until one day things happened and she was hospitalized, she spent some time between life and death until she recovered. After that, she began to see life in a different way. “Where are you going with this story?” Asks the old man, already drunk. “Nothing really, it’s just that after almost dying I realized how important my work is and you… need to do what you think is right, otherwise you’ll look back and think you should have done something different.”

                                                  In a very strange way, the old man noticed wisdom in the girl's words, that wouldn't change tomorrow, as he had already decided what he would do, but that conversation made him feel better. He finishes his glass and pays the bartender with some shabby bills, striding out he silently heads off into the night reviewing what he would do tomorrow in his mind. Inside the bar, the woman continues to taste her martini, while a hollow, ghostly voice whispers in her ear: “Thank you”.

                                                          Atariya with 10 dots in Atariya Template (Hurt Locker) and Acantus Free Councill Mage (Mage the Awakening)
                                                          The Scarlet Princess is an upscale club where the most upscale sleepers and their guests come to drink, socialize and mostly have some exotic and sometimes morally questionable fun. Among the guests of the night was Marco, a mage from the free council, he wasn't used to this type of place, he preferred less exclusive places and preferably with more respectable people, but the job required him to be at the Scarlet Princess. A few days ago he noticed a peculiar disturbance in the city, he noticed constant changes in the motions of destiny, small variations are relatively common, especially when a supernatural is involved and even more so when a mage using the arcana of Fate is present, unfortunately this was not the case. The changes, although small, were constant and uninterrupted so much so that Marco feared that if this continued the collateral damage could be catastrophic. He traced the source of the anomaly to a guy named Garv, Marco tried to get closer, but whenever he tried was stopped in some way, he himself tried to use his own control over the Fate Arcana, but whenever he made a change it was followed by another that nullified it, finding himself forced to change strategy, the mage decided to find Garv within his own territory, the Scarlet Princess.

                                                          Marco asks the bartender for a drink and while he waited, he discreetly looked for his target. It turned out that I didn't have to look much Garv was in the gaming area, to be more direct in the Russian roulette area, the game is simple, two idiots point a gun at their own head, and it only has one bullet, the one who gets shot loses and usually dies, Garv was confident in his victory, the poor guy who was his opponent was sweating so much that you could fill a bucket with his own sweat, when it was his turn he pointed the gun at his head, but he can't pull the trigger and simply gives up, so two guys in suits carry him to another room as if he were a fainting body. Garv simply walks away with a neutral expression, some of the spectators seemed disappointed with the result and others were clearly disappointed. Marco looks for a way to meet Garv, but is soon surprised by him who invites Marco for a drink, without questioning his own luck, the awakened man accepts. Back at the bar Marco orders a glass of wine, Garv asks the barman to surprise him, so he prepares a cocktail mixing two drinks that Marco was unable to identify, then added some energy drink and finished with only a few of lemon juice, then He places the glass in front of Garv, who tastes it confidently, giving the bartender a thumbs up and also a tip. While drinking, the two talk, Garv does most of the talking, especially about the other members of the club and even giving a subtle indication of where each one was, if it were anyone else Marco would see it as mere arrogance, but in Garv's case it was a Attestation to his influence, he counted at least three dangerous supernaturals in this environment, even if Marco killed him right there, and he doesn't know if he can, he would hardly survive the retaliation and Garv certainly wouldn't die by mundane methods.

                                                          While the wizard was wandering in his thoughts, Garv takes a silver coin out of his pocket, one side of which has a man's face engraved on it and the other has a crown, he throws it into the air where it stops for a few moments and lands in his hand with its man's face side up, Garv looks at the coin for a few seconds and smiles. “You can go if you want” he says with a smile that showed all his teeth, but still didn't look friendly at all. Marco accepts the offer and slowly leaves the club, when he leaves his chest seems lighter, Garv was certainly a sleeper, but fate favored him in such a way that even the greatest mages envied him. For our awakened guy, all that was left was to believe that destiny knew what it was doing, as him had no answer.
