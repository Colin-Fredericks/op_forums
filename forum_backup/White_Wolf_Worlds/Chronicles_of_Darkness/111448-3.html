                                        67. Solomon the Wise was said to command hundreds of Angels and Demons who built his temple and palace. Historical figures like Simon Magus and Dr. Faust were said to bind demons with elaborate mystic geometric shapes. John Dee invented an entire field of magic known as Enochian sorcery, which he claimed was the language of Angels. Unchained have dismissed such stories as nonsense or perhaps methods of summoning lesser spirits. Yet what if there is more to the story? Rather than command a Demon, perhaps highly informed occultist found a way to "hack" an angel? Imagine a very different kind of Fall, one in which an Angel's will is usurped rather than awakened. What if, by precisely using seemingly mythic patterns and invoking strange equations a human could induce and control an Angel's Fall, rewriting the program to such an extent that the thoughts and shape of the resultant Demon were twisted to the desires of the mortal? Imagine a new kind of Demon, his mind and body molded by controlled Fall to the whims of a mortal master. Who knows? Maybe beneath that old house, the one rumored to be a dead witch's home, there rests a Servitor dutifully waiting the return of a stigmatic "wizard" who died long ago. Maybe that forgotten desert city is undiscovered thanks to the ceaseless vigilance of 10,000 fallen angels bound by edicts more petty yet far more compelling than the God-Machine? All I'm saying is, watch your step around self-proclaimed sorcerers. You never know what might be beholden to them.
