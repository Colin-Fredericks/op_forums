    Two Freehold Ideas I've come up with:
    The Courts of Las Vegas were once the traditional Seasonal Courts, but the Spring and Summer Courts overtook the others by far, and their Bulwark failed. Nearly overwhelmed by the True Fae and their Huntsmen, the members of the Freehold forsook tried-and-true knowledge and reinvented themselves through Wits, Skills, and Luck, redefining themselves as the Suit Courts: The Diamond Court of Want, The Spade Court of Fury, the Club Court of Apathy, and the Heart Court of Melancholy. Their Bulwark involves many games of chance and the testing of each Courts’ Champions through those games.

    The Courts of Bremen based themselves around the story of the “Musicians” of Bremen, finding kinship with the four animals who fled their masters and combined their talents to defend themselves and make their fortune. The Rooster Court of Pride, the Judges, are the sages and inquisitors who master communications and expose corruption; The Cat Court of Cunning, the Witches, are the mystics and scholars who study the secrets of the Hedge and the unknown; the Hound Court of Loyalty, the Devils, are the warriors and guards who pursue and destroy threats to the Freehold; and the Donkey Court of Resolve, the Giants, are the artisans and workers who forge and work to support the Freehold and bolster its power. The Courts submit to the authority of a council of their Monarchs who direct their efforts towards greater goals. The Bulwark consists of various processes to determine new Monarchs: The Roosters hold an election amongst themselves, the Cats seek to unveil the greatest discovery, the Hounds participate in a series of duels, and the Donkeys work to craft the most wondrous creation.

                    Courts of the Diwatas
                    Several of the indigenuous Courts in the Philippines are tied not to seasonal cycles or geographic locations. Instead, they are tied to local Dreamers, exiled True Fae who have been transformed by their interactions with humanity to a greater extent than the so-called Charlatans. These Gentry represent many of the precolonial gods of the country, and the courtiers' veneration of them forms the basis of their Bulwark. A notable example of this system are the Courts of Maria Makiling, who draw their identities from the legends attributed to her.
                    The concept of the Dreamers came from the Goblin Chronicles Fan Supplement.

                                I had the Australian Sydney Courts be based loosely around the seasons but be warped into the Celestial Courts;
                                • Court of the Moon - Which exemplified curiosity, change, liminality, potential and the seasons of autumn and spring. They make goblin vows with Full-Moons.
                                • Court of the Stars - Which exemplified greed, inevitability, endurance, self-determination, and the season of winter. They make goblin vows with Midnight.
                                • Court of the Sun - Which exemplified passion, creation, drama, fate, and the season of summer. They make goblin vows with Noon.
                                And the central coast of Australia got a court system based around the Past, Present, and hoped Future of the region:
                                • Court of Sands - Which exemplifies excitement, standing out, socializing, manipulation, entertainment, blue, blue angel, glaucus atlanticus, and the potential future of the region. As their mantle grows they become more athletic, can make goblin vows with Beaches, can get into any social event, and become one of the Illes kith. This court also grants membership to the region's kindred, parasites drifting from one event to another for easy prey, though they earn their keep my covering up the region's supernatural activity and by manipulating mortals into creating more events for the Sand Court to attend.
                                • Court of Serpents - Which exemplifies regret, reconciliation, nature, learning, memory, red, red-bellied black snake, pseudechis porphyriacus, and the past of the region. As their mantle grows they become more academic, can make goblin vows with Serpent Artwork, gain perfect memory, and become one of the Antiquarian kith. This court also grants membership to the region's awakened and bound, assisting the court in gathering knowledge on the regions history and spreading knowledge that members have discovered through more effective means.
                                • Court of Spiders - Which exemplifies contentment, togetherness, unity, safety, temperance, black, black house spider, badumna insignis, and the current state of the region. As their mantle grows they become more empathetic, can make goblin vows with Currently Used Spiderwebs, spiders start to guard and watch over your home, and become one of the Truefriend kith. This court also grants membership to the region's begotten and forsaken, where territory is carefully discussed and they work to keep the region's supernatural ecology relatively stable.

                                            I had some loose ideas for a court system for a nautical community, with the courts corresponding to proximity to the water. The half-baked version is as follows:

                                            Court of the Steady Soil - attracts those who are even in temperament, grounded in tradition, seek safety. Themes: social, mercantile, nature, safety, hearth, warmth, tradition, construction, wood and earth, stone.
                                            Court of the Shifting Sands - attracts the capricious and whimsical, exemplifies adaptiveness, very responsive but not necessarily attentive, independent. Themes: whimsy, self-improvement, leisure, adaptation, sand, driftwood, hidden treasures
                                            Court of the Unforgiving Tide - attracts the hard-headed and determined, the tide is regular and powerful, and so too are its courtiers. independent and unyielding. Themes: perseverance, combat, order, obfuscation, imminent dangers, waves, waxing and waning, cycles, ships, maritime activities
                                            Court of the Bent Horizon - attracts both the academic and the adventurous, united in curiosity and wanderlust but aware of dangers of straying too far. themes: exploration, knowledge, maritime warfare, the deep ocean, shipwrecks, the occult, hidden threats

                                            Still a lot of cooking left on the idea but given the historical and metaphysical significance of the sea there's got to be a court system somewhere set up around it...

                                                The Courts of Glasgow follow the Seasonal style, but are additionally defined by the Miracles of St Mungo. Spring is the Bird; representing rebirth after tragedy. Summer is the Tree, the fire renewed and stronger than ever. Autumn is the Fish, representing the spoils of fear and suspicion.
