    Bombadil: A mysterious, helpful being who's nature is unclear.


    Voldemort: A traitor who's betrayal nearly brought ruin to the Freehold.


    Crocker: A human that relentlessly tries to acquire proof of the existence of Fae. Possibly a Hunter with a narrow focus on prey.


    Teddy: A Beast who devotes themselves to protecting children from those who seek to harm them. The slang is sometimes followed by what kind of animal they represent (i.e Teddy Bear).


    Freakazoid: One of the Mad who still proves helpful to the Freehold... somehow.

                Keeper Of All: an alternative name for the Wyrd, as it can transform/control the lives of humans, changelings, True Fae and other monsters alike, subtly or not. May or may not be conflated with the Holy Spirit/Abrahamic God.

                Chess: a slang used by changelings and Ensorcelled to refer to the Feud of the Gentry in general. Sometimes the Gentry use this slang too.

                Chess Piece: someone manipulated in order to benefit a Feud. Sub-terms may include King and Queen for important agents and Pawns for minor ones. Who is the Gentry, then? The guy playing chess, of course.

                Sport: slang used mainly by the Gentry to refer to Feuds that are primarily physical in nature. Usually involves more pain than normal sports.

                Chat: slang used mainly by the Gentry to refer to Feuds that are primarily social in nature. Usually involves backstabbing that would make the average Kindred or Unchained proud.

                Puzzle: slang used mainly by Gentry to refer to Feuds that are primarily mental in nature. Usually involves something like a pendulum with an ax swinging ever closer to make the others think faster.

                Debate: an euphemism for when two or more Gentry are fighting over something, usually a slave or Title.

                                            Grail / Fox: The current target of Huntsmen.
