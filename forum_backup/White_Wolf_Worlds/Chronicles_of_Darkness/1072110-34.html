                                            Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post

                                            While I put a Caligo in the Vocation edition that summons spirits out of Tainted areas, because I wanted the witchcraft-themed Dark cult to call up evil spirits. Thematic overlap = potential crossover.

                                            I would say that, if you're doing a Princess game that gets heavily into the spirit world, you absolutely should have Wounds on the Shadow side of the worst Tainted areas. If the spiritual ecology isn't a big thing in your game, though, there's no reason to bring up Wounds just because Werewolf says they'd be there. Same reason you don't talk about the Hedge if you don't have fae characters, or the Underworld if you're not dealing with ghosts, or Infrastructure if the God-Machine doesn't interest the players.
