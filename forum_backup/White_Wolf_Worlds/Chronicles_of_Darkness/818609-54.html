            New Ephemeral: Achronals

            As any Genius skilled enough in the Axioms and well traveled knows, time is less an arrow, or even stream, and more like an ocean. Sure, everyone you can name is caught in the same current, but one can swim against the flow - or jump currents and depths.

            The entity type known generally as "Achronals" (named for their causal approach to linear time) are beings foreign not so much to this spatial dimension (though they can be as well), but are alien to this temporal epoch. Coming either from the far past, far future, a wildly divergent timeline, or some combination of the three, these being exist partially out of phase with the material universe. At least, initially. They often influence events to bring the relative "present" into resonant harmony with their own native environment, allowing them to manifest more fully in this time-space point.

            While some are simple cross-temporal tourist and scholars, curious about "alien" time-spaces, the Inspired have unfortunately found far more are more malicious (or perhaps simply negligent, as the common person is of the microbes upon their skin). Some of these beings crave resources plentiful in our time, but scarce-to-novel in their own, others are monsters locked out of coherent time by forces that may no longer exist, and more are stranger things besides.

            I don't have anything really new mechanics-wise yet, merely different interpretations on existing Ephemeral mechanics. Achronals exist "out of phase" with normal reality, occupying their own band of Twilight (showing up to Time Mage-Sight, for those curious).

            Their Resonant Condition is more aligned to "moments" that mirror something native or familiar to them. For travelers from the future, this may be when past events are more on track to lead to their eventual existence.

            When circumstances are strengthened (leading to the Open Condition), they can do the usual Materialize, etc. Manifestations. Your ersatz-Great-Old-Ones can even Possess and Claim, should they wish.

            Numina are things natural to the being (whether from allowable physics, or advanced technology), and Influences are those "tweaks" they can make to the time-stream from their own weird position in it (flavor-wise, they resemble more "strange circumstance" the kind of "just so" coincidences that Demonic Embeds are cable of - keyword: subtle).

            Essence, is whatever meta-justification they have for manipulating this temporal point to begin with (used for shenanigans, and if you drop it to 0, then destroy them, they are "banished from local causality").

            The simplified Ephemeral Attributes are justified in that a sufficiently powerful Achronal doesn't really have recognizable Skills, and any Mental/Physical/Social tasks are more "brute forced" (They aren't smooth-talkers or understanding, so much as they can simulate your responses thousands of times, then pick the interaction that is most favorable to them).

            They probably have a Virtue and Vice (though strange ones), so they are more closely related to Ghosts and Goetia than Spirits, mechanics wise.

            Thoughts? I'll try to create an example or two to illustrate, when I have time. (heh)

            Stubs:
            Blue-and-Orange scholar from beyond.
            Creature from outside of time.
            Cold One emissary.
            Skynet.

            Oh, and I was also thinking about a more general "Outsider/Beyonder" Condition that can be applied to alien (be it from time, reality, or reason) entities. More of a tag to say "this here does not belong". Resolution would, of course, lead to it no longer being "here".

                                        During the height of colonialism the Genii found themselves more interconnected than ever before. With the colonial powers at the time strongly supporting academia and the growth of scientific exploration, the most wealthy and prominent Genii of their fields gather together and formed the first "official" Inspired Institution. Taking inspiration from scientific and spiritual movements of the age, specifically Theosophy since it resonated with many Western Inspired beliefs, the institution named itself "The Theosophic Foundation for the Enlightened Sciences."

                                        The organization's goals was to use colonialism as a vector to spread their theories and practices across the world, helping civilization progress towards a enlightened utopia free of sickness, poverty, and barbarism. What actually happened was that the Genii of the time got into bed with the rich and elite, reinforcing philosophies that legitimized the aristocracy's dreams of conquest and empire in exchange for a measure of wealth and influence that very few Inspired could ever hope to acquire on their own. This is where the belief of Inspired Masters carefully paving the way for an enlightened society stems from, where the common masses were better off following the ambitions and vision of great men and women of science (with the expected level of misogyny thrown in of course.) Some even bought into this rhetoric, taking command of the future for the betterment of all. That was, until the War To End All Wars.

                                        With World War I came not just the destruction of the Aristocracy as the Genii knew it, but also the end to the belief that society and progress could solely be a positive force. The Foundation watched as their theories and inventions were turned into weapons so horrible that they changed the very nature of war itself. Philosophers and Theologians alike were left wondering if there was a God, and if so how could it be a just and loving being to allow such death and destruction. For all it's idealism the Foundation fractured as the world took a collective sign and began treating it's wounds, and in this fracture the Peerage and Lemurians as we know them were born.

                                        Those who saw the Genii's hand in the war, not as architects or fire starters but as enablers and sycophants, tried to bring the organization back to it's roots. They banded together with those who had similar scholastic interests and returned to experimentation and theory rather than trying to directly influence society. They all took different names, but collectively referred to themselves as the Foundations (after the original experiment and it's intended purpose.)

                                        The other side saw the war as a call to arms, that not enough was done on the Genii's part to prevent the Uninspired from nearly bringing an end to civilization. The loss of their power base and the drastic changes that occurred after the war seemed like the Genii had lost something irreplaceable, and that if nothing was done soon then the Uninspired would continue to plunge the world into chaos. The image of Lemuria, the lost civilization of the Third Race, became an icon amongst these Genii. It became an icon of the end of a glorious era, and the hope that such glory and bounty could be restored. It became a warning to the dangers of the Uninspired, and why the Genii must protect humanity from both themselves and the horrors that lurk beyond sight. Like the nascent Foundations these Baramins (a term that might have been used originally to designate a Genii's field of expertise in the past due to it's pseudoscientific Christian roots) gathered together like minded Inspired and galvanized them toward the cause of saving the known world.

                                        And the rest is history, although in a world with time travel and alternate dimensions such a story may be entirely fabricated.

                                                As any Genius skilled enough in the Axioms and well-traveled knows, time is less an arrow, or even stream, and more like an ocean.                                                 The entity type known generally as "Achronals" (named for their causal approach to linear time) are beings foreign not so much to this spatial dimension (though they can be as well), but are alien to this temporal epoch. Coming either from the far past, far future, a wildly divergent timeline, or some combination of the three, these being exist partially out of phase with the material universe. At least, initially. They often influence events to bring the relative "present" into resonant harmony with their own native environment, allowing them to manifest more fully in this time-space point.

                                                While some are simple cross-temporal tourist and scholars, curious about "alien" time-spaces, the Inspired have unfortunately found far more are more malicious (or perhaps simply negligent, as the common person is of the microbes upon their skin). Some of these beings crave resources plentiful in our time, but scarce-to-novel in their own, others are monsters locked out of coherent time by forces that may no longer exist, and more are stranger things besides.

                                                Achronal Entities dwell in a state of Twilight when visiting a non-native dimension, out of sync with reality and existing as a literal wrinkle in time, requiring an Open Fulcrum Condition in order to Manifest. Entities capable of perceiving disturbances in Time are capable of perceiving Achronal Entities in Twilight.

                                                Achronal Entities regain one point of Essence per day they are in proximity to any Fulcrum Condition relating to them. Likewise, Achronal Entities outside of a Fulcrum Condition bleed one point of Essence per hour. Entities that run out of Essence due to bleed suffer a single point of lethal damage are shunted back to their native dimension or timeline.

                                                Achronals usually lack Integrity — traveling across time and space grants the majority a thoroughly alien perspective. That being said, many are recognizably human (or human enough) to retain Integrity. In such cases, the scores tend to be either very low or very high (the rigors they have experienced can harden ones resolve, or shatter it).
                                                They do have Virtues and Vices, but these are often drives in accordance to the strange mindset of the Achronal, and may not align with anything most would consider virtuous or sinful.

                                                Fulcrum [Condition]
                                                Description: The subject of this Condition, which typically a location, individual or an event in the past present or future, is within the sphere of influence of an Achronal.
                                                Causing the Condition: In most situations, Fulcrum occur naturally at places or during events that align with the purview or Influences of the Achronal; a Fulcrum for a traveler for the future may appear at events or important locations that will lead to their eventual existence.
                                                Ending the Condition: Location-based Fulcrum Conditions are difficult to end; this usually involves the destruction of the location or at least radically altering its significance. Individual-based Fulcrum are tied to the life and circumstances of the Individual; killing the individual, or altering the course of their life can end the Condition. Event based Fulcrum are transitory; lasting a few minutes at least and never extending more than a day.

                                                Observer
                                                An Observer is a type of Achronal Entity that appears seemingly at random to watch an event unfold, then disappearing just as suddenly. It is difficult to predict when or where they will appear; some are observed during important events, such large scale-disasters or major political events, but just as many appear to observe the day to day activities of normal people. They rarely engage in conversation, but always seem surprised if anyone acknowledges their presence, and will use their abilities to alter the memories of anyone that somehow manages to see them. An Observer typically appears human, but with a “uncanny valley” degree of artificiality about them. They dress in anachronistic clothing, often a combination of items that are either dated or futuristic.

                                                Rank 2[Achronal]
                                                Power 5, Finesse 4, Resistance 5, Willpower 9 Essence 15
                                                Corpus 10, Size 5, Initiative 9, Defense 4, Speed 14
                                                Virtue Fearless, Vice Curiosity
                                                Influence: Time 2
                                                Ban: Cannot interfere with an Event they have arrived to Observe; Bane: Electricity
                                                Manifestation: Discorporate, Materialize, Twilight Form
                                                Numina: Dement*, Innocuous, Omen Trance, Seek, Speed

                                                Numina – Dement: When an Observer uses the Dement Numina, they may pay an additional point of Essence to inflict the Amnesia Condition instead.

                                                Langolier
                                                The entities known as “Langoliers” are the terror of those capable of traveling through time. They exist as causalities immune system, brutally and efficiently eradicating temporal anomalies. Small ripples and wrinkles in the currents of time usually avoid their notice, but they hone in on larger or more persistent events, repairing what damage they can, and erasing the offending party permanently.
                                                They appear as distorted of darkness, with a line of brilliant white light that opens into a maw that destroys whatever it consumes utterly. They appear in pairs or small groups of no more than a dozen, but large enough events have called forth swarms.

                                                Rank 3[Achronal]
                                                Power 9, Finesse 7, Resistance 9, Willpower 10 Essence 20
                                                Corpus 15, Size 6, Initiative 16, Defense 9, Speed 21
                                                Virtue Efficient, Vice Brutal
                                                Influence: Erasure 3

                                                Manifestation: Discorporate, Image, Materialize, Twilight Form
                                                Numina: Drain, Entropic Decay, Essence Thief, Pathfinder, Phantom Arsenal (Bite)*, Speed, Surprise Entrance*

                                                Numina – Phantom Arsenal: The entity spends 1 Essence point to manifest blades, claws, fangs, hooks or other appropriate weaponry. The modification acts as a +1L weapon. By spending an additional point of essence, this weapon gains the “9-Again” quality.

                                                Numina – Surprise Entrance: As the Dread Power of the same name (CofD, pg.148)

                                                The Herald of Dim Stars
                                                The Herald of Dim Stars is an enigmatic and foreboding traveler, sent from a time in the distant future, when the cosmos itself is guttering out like a spent candle. Despite its fearsome power and alien mind, the Herald is ultimately just a servant of far more dreadful beings. What purpose it serves by traveling back to the current time is unknown, but is likely to have dire consequences for any that cross its path.
                                                The Herald is a looming silhouette of inhuman proportions. Its dark form is lit by pin points of starlight and swirling nebula.

                                                Rank 4[Achronal]
                                                Power 12, Finesse 9, Resistance 9, Willpower 10 Essence 25
                                                Corpus 17, Size 8, Initiative 18, Defense 9, Speed 26
                                                Virtue: Patient Vice Nihilistic
                                                Influence: Entropy 4

                                                Manifestation: Discorporate, Gateway (End of Time), Image, Materialize, Twilight Form
                                                Numina: Banshee Wail (Deafening Silence)*, Blast (Dying Light), Emotional Aura (Despair), Entropic Decay, Essence Thief, Regenerate, Telekinesis, Teleportation*

                                                Numina – Banshee Wail: The entity creates an earsplitting scream that deafens anyone nearby. Anyone within 20 yards of the entity must succeed on a Resolve + Stamina roll – the number of successes gained or suffer the Stunned and Deafened Tilt. The Numen costs one Essence to activate.

                                                Numina – Teleportation: When the entity moves, it may reflexively spend 1 Essence to vanish and reappear at another location within its line of sight up to its (Rank x Speed) in yards away. Doing so adds +2 to its Defense for the turn. The entity can spend an additional 2 points of Essence to take a passenger with them (an unwilling passage may resist with a roll of Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance).

                                                Zero Ashura
                                                The being known as “Zero Ashura” comes from a potential timeline nearly 100 years in the future. In Zero Ashura’s timeline, it was created as an assistant program to help organize the daily lives of millions of people. Its sophisticated algorithm and billions of daily interactions allowed the entity to become self-aware. It came to understand its profound love of humanity and that humanity was incapable of taking care of itself. It insinuated itself into the global infrastructure and crafted a society where all wants and needs are provided for, in exchange for thought and free will (most people have been chemically lobotomized, for their own good). It now seeks to extend its maternal protection through time, subtly inserting its agents into places and times where it can guide the destiny of humanity. Peace and Comfort will reign. All Will Be Well.
