    After much planning, we move to the battle. This session was all prep and combat, so when the journals focus on it that’s because, well, that is mostly what happened.

    Session XIV

    Marek’s Thoughts

    27 June 1920
    Tonight was satisfying. We planned that I would go to a high point on the outside of the Catholic church near the edge of the graveyard and snipe the Pure with my rifle, Tatiana would take care of the spirits, Lizette was armed with her chainsaw and was also going for the Pure. Wolfram was going to go after Gunnar and Alla will keep the Pure’s attention on her so they won’t shred our pack-mates. Dearbhail was going to stay to the outskirts of the fight and support us with her healing abilities if needed. Dacian had planned on hiding out on top of the crypt, invisibly, and blinding and otherwise hindering anyone that needed it. Good plan. It even mostly went the way we expected it to. Before the fight began we did several things to prepare. We asked Ochranny for her blessings, as well as using gifts of our own to increase the skill and durability of several of our number.

    We did pretty much exactly as we’d planned. Tatiana took on the spirits. There were three of them. A Pain spirit and two spirits of Protection. They worked well together to cause us problems and to heal and protect their charges. Tatiana though was not to be deterred. She fought tirelessly to take down the spirits that the Pure had brought with them. She was the only one that could do it since I was in my perch. I felt a little bad that I could not help my fellow Uratha, but I did some good from my vantage point. There are several threats that were eliminated due to my position and choice of weapon. Tatiana also took on Ilsa herself during part of the fight. The Pure woman perceived that Tatiana was a threat and decided to attack her. She did not leave much of a mark though. Tatiana ended up chasing the other werewolf off at the end of the fight. She wasn’t actually going to engage the Pure since we wanted Ilsa to get away, but the other woman didn’t need to know that. That part of the plan worked out rather well if I do say so myself.

    Alla played her part and played it well. She set out to be a distraction for the Pure and she succeeded. She used her silver knuckles to good effect, even gaining the attention of Ilsa herself. Though I suppose the thing that gained her attention the most was when Alla picked up the statue of the angel and threw it at Ilsa. That was definitely a good way to gain someone’s attention if you ask me. Though pounding one’s minions flat with knuckles made of silver worked rather well too.

    Wolfram afforded himself well tonight against Gunnar. The two were perhaps not the most evenly matched if you just looked at their physical capabilities, but Wolfram had a few tricks up his sleeve tonight. He seemed able to soak up as much damage as Gunnar could dish out with little real affect. I don’t know how many times I thought for sure that Wolfram was going to fall, and he just shrugged it off like it was a mosquito bite. Some of it was Ochranny’s blessing, some of it was his own Spirit’s gifts.

    Dacian managed to stay hidden for most of the fight on top of the Crypt. I saw him strike at least one man with a gun blind. The man decided to forgo shooting and pulled out his knife instead. As soon as I saw what happened to him I knew that he wasn’t going to last long in the fight. It was just a matter of time. I think Dacian also spread fear to some of the men, because at least one of Ilsa’s mortal followers made a beeline for one of the trucks. Maybe it’s just that I never leave witnesses, or maybe I wasn’t sure if he was going to go for the truck that had the artifacts in it, but I took him out with a single head shot. I will admit that I had a satisfying time tonight. Every man I shot died in short order.

    Lizette for her part started off with a silver loaded pistol, but ended up demonstrating in graphic detail exactly what a chainsaw will do to human or Uratha flesh. It is rather impressive I will say. Rather messy, but very impressive. There is also the intimidation factor to work in. This unfortunately led several of the mortal gunman to take aim at the slight inventor. They were intimidated by her choice of weapon I think. Lizette was shot twice in short order and fell to the ground after the second round. I will admit that I grew worried when I saw her go down. I have become rather fond of the Fae woman and did not want to lose her company so soon. Thankfully she managed to get back up, still wielding her chainsaw and nearly cut one of the gunman in half from hip to chin.

    Even Wolfram’s ghostly teacher, Franz helped to fight off the gunman. He engaged and killed one of the gunman himself. Not that I didn’t think him capable, I just wasn’t sure if ghosts retained their fighting ability or if it faded with time. He seemed to handle himself just fine for a spirit.

    Dearbhail used her Fae magicks to heal those that needed it and even chided Dacian for shielding himself from sight after he’d been shot by a gunman. He had become visible to use one of his powers and one of the human henchmen took offense at this and did his best to kill our shadowy packmate. At that moment I had two targets that both needed to die, but could only shoot one of them at a time. A gunman was about to go after Lizette and the one that had shot Dacian once already was lining up a second shot to take him out. In the end I gambled that Lizette and her chainsaw would be able to kill her combatant before the unfortunate man had a chance to act. I wasn’t as certain about the man on Dacian, so I took him out with a single shot as well. I should probably mention my work as Reznik to the others. After tonight’s showing it will not be a surprise.

    In the end I took out three men with three shots. The first to go down was the tall Pure man that came with Ilsa. Our plan had been to take out the Pure first and so he was my first target. Lizette and I were to focus our attention on him until he went down. Well, one shot with a silver bullet did that just fine. The second was the man running, and the third was Dacian’s attacker. After that first shot though I thought I felt something start to shift inside me, but it stopped. Nothing physical really, it was just a feeling. Brief, then gone just as quickly. It happened only with the first shot, so I am thinking that it has something to do with using silver weapons against another werewolf. I had been warned about using silver weapons, and I knew that something might happen, but it was the best way to ensure that our targets didn’t just brush off the damage as if it were nothing.

    So, the quick version of events is thus. We engaged with the enemy, taking care not to hit Ilsa with anything silver. The other Pure could rage if they wanted to, and one did, but we did not want her to fall into the Rage. We wanted her to run. The fighting was fast and brutal all around and several of our number were wounded. Lizette and Dacian most of all. In the end they enemy had all fallen and we made a show of harrying Ilsa in the direction we wanted her to go. She jumped into one of the cars and drove off as fast as she could. Unfortunately for her, Lizette used her Fae abilities to wreak havoc on the vehicles brakes. Ilsa crashed the car and was thrown out of it, pretty badly wounded. It probably didn’t help the fleeing woman that Wolfram also used his powers to set her on fire while she was driving away. It helped us plenty. It just didn’t help her. In the end we made a show of trying to catch up to her and “failing.”

    Once the excitement was over Dearbhail healed those that needed it. I don’t think she was able to get rid of all of their wounds, but she greatly reduced their severity. We then discussed what we were going to do with all the bodies we had lying around. We could not leave them in the graveyard for the authorities to find. That would lead to too many questions, but we had to do something with them. All the gunfire would draw the police, there was no question about that. In the end we agreed to let Dearbhail open a doorway to some place she called the “Hedge.” We threw the bodies in that place and then shut the door behind us.

    The last thing we needed to do was to dispose of the vehicles, or at least some of them. We took the truck that had the items we were looking for to a parking lot on Christina’s property. The one that Lizette “tinkered” with was in flames. The others we just left where they were. It’ll be a mystery for the authorities to figure out, but it shouldn’t be traceable back to us.

    Now we just need to make it look like Gunnar has run off to avoid his duel with Wolfram in the morning. I believe Dacian was planning on breaking into Gunnar’s place and hastily packing a bag and leaving, making it obvious that he had run off. A body would cause too many questions, but just his absence allows those curious about his whereabouts to use their imagination. And the imagination of the community is better than any lie we could come up with.


    Tatiana’s Conversations

    Yes, volchonok, yes, my brother, it was an excellent fight. One to howl of and celebrate, to be sure. But work is not yet done, so we will howl later.

    Indeed, volchonok, they were as pups before a rabid bear. They growl and snarl but they can do nothing. We had every advantage, even in their own home. 4 werewolves to two and it was no contest for us.

    Heh, yes, volchonok, I was there, remember? You are an excitable one. I did see Marek end the worthless Pure lackey to Gunnar with a single bullet to the head. He has much skill with the gun. I fear the Pure Wolfblooded would have made of with the relics had he not stopped him. Fenric was on hand, of course. He could have slowed him down enough. Probably.

    It is not so unbelievable. His mistress is quite skilled. Surely you saw her destroy the other werewolf lackey with a single punch? Silver laced, yes, but even so. She even took a hit from Ilsa with nothing but bruises to show.

    That is unkind, volchonok. Wolfram would have killed his rival much quicker had it been a proper duel, without the interference of the purity spirits and their healing. Wolfram is to be commended for his bravery, to be certain, even if his wisdom was lacking in this instance. It is not many men who could stand against a werewolf and destroy him, and come out largely unscathed.

    I got to the purity spirit as quickly as I could, foolish volchonok. The pain spirit was much more dangerous, and Wolfram was not truly in danger, not yet. It only took a pair of bites to rip its armored shell to pieces, spikes and all, no matter how much it made my blood burn. The blessing of our totem was still appreciated.

    Yes, Ochranny’s blessings were upon us. It definitely helped Alla take Ilsa’s hit, and Lizette probably would have fallen, had our totem not been with her.

    Oh, you noticed, did you, volchonok? I thought you might have ignored her use of such a human thing.

    This is true - it is difficult to ignore a chainsaw wielded in such a way. I did not even have to lend her my Gift, though I did Alla. Fairy magics are strong. The Prussian Wolfblooded stood no chance. Well, their rifles gave them some chance. Their fear as she closed almost did her in, but it was not enough, especially since our songbird’s fairy healing magic is also powerful.

    Our thief is a more subtle man. I believe he cursed our foes with blindness and fear. At least, one of the rifleman decided to abandon his military firing discipline and draw a knife. It did him not much good against Lizette’s chainsaw.

    Our songbird was upset at Dacian because he used his magics to disappear when he got wounded.

    Yes, it did mean that he did not get hurt further, but she told him not to. She is not always reasonable about these things. She wished to do more in combat, rather than after.

    Of course we let Ilsa go. This was the plan, yes? To spread news of the defeat of the Pure in Vienna. Foolish volchonok.

    I believe this was Lizette? She has powers to make ‘human things’ break. It would be a great coincidence if the car merely broke down.

    Of course I harried her away, it could not be obvious that she was to escape. Besides, I believe that Wolfram and Dacian had some anger to express. If I could throw fireballs and silver daggers at a fleeing Pure, I would probably do so after winning a fight.

    No, volchonok, it is not the time to run. Now that the bodies have been left in the fairy lands, we must prepare the deception regarding Wolfram’s duel, prepare for this vampire salon, and speak with Krieger concerning what was done this night. There is no time for running now.


    Dacian’s Book of Shadows

    27 June 1920 - Early Evening

    We have made our plans and now we prepare ourselves and our abilities. The cabal has been divided into our respective teams and a sense of deep calm has settled over us. I choose to believe it is a sign. We will strike decisively or we will quite literally fall to the wolves.

    27 June 1920 - Late

    I was to be a noble, in a nearly forgotten life. I was a servant for a lifetime best left in shadows. For a time, I was used as a distraction to gain the trust of Gentry before attacks were launched. When I was betrayed and abandoned, I fought my way through the Hedge and found myself given another chance at life. I fancied myself as an artisan of my chosen craft. But tonight I have been the Terror out of Sight, the Harrier of Hope, and the Promise of Death. Though I am not yet finished, I shall start again.

    I began my insurgence by masking any trace of my passing before reaching the crypt. Once my scent was hidden, I moved around freely verifying we were not ourselves walking into a trap. Once I satisfied my suspicions, I let the others know the crypt was clear. While we awaited Lizette and Wolfram’s arrival, I scuttled around the top of the crypt in a very well hidden vantage. After a time, I heard Lizette’s motorcar sliding to a halt at the rendezvous point. I knew our prey was close behind. I made detecting me or anything else harder and hid myself even more deeply in the stone. I made sure I could see ghosts so I could communicate with our chivalrous spectre if the need arose and then I saw the first arrival. Three vehicles from separate directions arrived in total. The first was apparently Gunnar’s men. A footman drove the other four men dressed in what I recognized as noble uniforms. Gunnar arrived next, oddly alone, in his own car. Lastly, Ilsa arrived in the cargo truck with two other men. One was notably tall, the other short. It was rather odd. They both carried themselves with the same cocksure attitude as Ilsa so I assumed they were addition Pure of Berlin.

    While they were carefully stepping through their political dance, Lizette ran through with the information that Ilsa’s greatest fear is to be forced into marriage against her will. I couldn’t see how this was relevant currently, as I certainly couldn’t preside over a service. Given that could have alerted our targets however, I prepared to strike from my hidden nook. The four noblemen each took a rifle from the trunk. Their practiced lockstep movements suggested military training, if the rifles did not. Indeed, the assembled group came to investigate the crypt with precision and confidence. We waited until the advantage of surprise offset their numbers. Marek opened fire from an adjacent mausoleum, killing the taller werewolf next to Ilsa. I was startled as I did not expect a werewolf to die so easily. Tatiana leapt from her hiding place in dire wolf or Urshal form to bite at Gunnar’s car. I am told there was a spirit there. I assume this is the case and not some kind of battle madness. Nevertheless, Alla popped up and threw a challenging swing at the shorter werewolf. Her silver knuckledusters missed, though our vampire definitely had all the attention from her opponent. One of the riflemen opened fire on Wolfram, who was charging, pistol drawn for Gunnar. Our Bound never flinched as the wound seemed to softly billow white smoke. Alla’s opponent shifted to his dire wolf form as Wolfram shot Gunnar in the heart. The Pure did not fall, but was grievously wounded. The next rifleman shot Wolfram as well, but the fiery armor only I could see deflected the bullet. I was frightened at how quickly everything was happening but pushed through the fear. I was finally able to strike then, blinding one of the riflemen with Murkblur. I would not see another unanswered shot fired at Wolfram. Just as one threat was temporarily removed, another rose. In fact, Gunnar rose to nearly nine feet as his body seemed to reject the damage Wolfram had inflicted. The wound closed as fur tore through the skin. Gunnar lashed out with his feral claws, no thought to strategy, only survival fueling his attack. Wolfram would have been torn to ribbons were he a lesser man. But his armor did not dull the full power of the blow. Fortunately, his death magic protected him and though he smoked like a campfire, he stood strong. Dearbháil screamed a curse so potent in her Irish tongue that even Gunnar was affected. I was surprised such horrible words could come from such a lovely voice. Another shot rang out but the bullet ricocheted from Wolfram’s back into a statue. The damage to the cemetery angel was enough to rouse our final combatant from his slumber.

    The distance peel of gunfire explained the sudden collapse of the footman Fenric was chasing to the cargo truck. This was obviously a human as his corpse littered the ground in a wide radius from the body. Tatiana was still mauling the unseen spirit at the cars. For just a moment, we all seemed to slow as Alla threw a punch so devastating, she killed her opponent before his broken body could even strike the ground. The dire wolf shrank back into the shorter man just as the horrible sound of Lizette’s chainsaw broke the stillness. The Wizened charged from cover, weapon ahead, herself growling. The riflemen with the sight to do so, turned their barrels toward the Gremlin. One fired a terrible round into Lizette just as Wolfram shot Gunnar at point blank. The werewolf was deeply wounded as was Lizette, who received a second shot from the other rifleman. I had to do something and fear was the first thought I had. Using my Contracts, I instilled the creeping dread of darkness rising in the rifleman nearest to me. That I shouted from the underside of the crypt awning, where I was until then invisible, made me more of a target than I intended. So much blood came next. It was a blur of fury and gunfire. I remember Dearbháil screaming, seeing Franz Ludwig advance on the men firing at Lizette, and then pain. The fourth rifleman spotted me before I could again turn invisible. His bullet drank deeply of my blood. I stumbled down from the crypt and coughed up more blood.

    I looked my murderer in the eye as his finger tightened on the trigger. The shot I heard seemed wrong however. Then I realized Marek had saved me. As the rifleman’s body fell limply forward, I spotted the Bone Shadow abseiling down the side of the mausoleum. From the corner of my eye, Tatiana had moved on to a different empty space to attack. Alla struck Ilsa with another piece of the statuary before the nightmarish gurgling of the rifleman cut down by Lizette’s chainsaw momentarily drowned out the other nearby sounds of battle. A flurry of combat surrounded me on all sides. I could not see for sure where all the enemy gunmen were and I knew I could not take another wound like I carried already. I watched Wolfram fire a third round into the werewolf before I vanished. Dearbháil screamed again but I could not see why. I knew I couldn’t stay hidden long so I found cover for when I reappeared. From my vantage, I saw Fenric climb into the cargo truck to protect our prize. Then I turned to see Franz Ludwig run another of the riflemen through.

    I heard Tatiana savaging another piece of air before Lizette’s chainsaw sang a gruesome duet with one of the dwindling riflemen. Franz Ludwig held Ilsa at bay while the duel continued, though I took the time to tear a piece of white cloth from my undershirt. I tied it to the pommel of my dagger. I intended to slip up behind the shewolf and tie the silver to her tail. As amusing as that would have been, Gunnar was finally overcome by his wounds with Wolfram’s fourth silver bullet. Ilsa, realising she was outnumbered, out maneuvered, out gunned, and out of luck, shifted to her Dalu form and make a break for Gunnar’s car.

    Before she could drive away, I hurled one of my silver daggers such that it embedded into the door next to her. Wolfram summoned an orb of flame into his palm before hurling it at Ilsa as she drove frantically away. She shrieked as burns appeared on her flesh. Lizette stopped then and simply blew a kiss after the werewolf. I felt a subtle shift in the Wyrd and Gunnar’s car careened off the road. Ilsa was thrown from the wreckage due to her reckless driving. Marek and Dearbháil fell in with rest of the cabal behind a bloody Lizette as she began to walk menacingly toward Ilsa, chainsaw at the ready. The plan was to allow her to escape, though I cannot say Lizette would have stopped if she were able to reach her quarry. And we could not let her believe she was spared. As the werewolf turned to look over her shoulder, she saw the complete assemblage of our group walking toward, with, but also oblivious to, our varying degrees of injury. I can only imagine what a terrifying image it must have been, if only based on her expression. At that point, the only Pure survivor from the ambush bolted into the night.

    I turned to the others with a smile that faded quickly as Dearbháil began swatting me for disappearing. In my pain, I could do little but pull away and look pitifully confused at the insane Fairest. In the process of her browbeating me for making it hard for her to heal me, she lessened most of my wounds, then hugged me. I believe she may have been distraught at how wounded several of us were and felt somewhat responsible. Since then we have gathered the bodies (and anything useful from them) and thrown them into the Hedge. And if that horrible sound from the forest is any indication, Lizette found the last rifleman.

    Now we must go and make sure Gunnar’s home looks as though he left in a hurry to escape his duel with Wolfram tomorrow. With that, I will become the Silver Conspirator and my job for the evening will be complete. The relics will return with us and after Gunnar is humiliated tomorrow, I can set about indexing and identifying what our bounty can do.


    Lizette’s Plans

    Mayhem!

    Okay, that’s out of the way, now focus, girl.

    Through the power of my Glamour, I was able to do well in the fight. I was also able to observe a variety of supernatural creatures in action.

    Alla, as a Vampire, withstood a couple direct attacks from a Werewolf. Even Ilsa was not able to slow her down much. She killed a Werewolf with a single blow using silver, enhanced by my glamour. She is a silken tiger.

    Vampires are frighteningly resilient. Only Wolfram shrugged off damage like she did. Her strength is clearly greater than it appeared…Alla was able to throw a stone statue at Ilsa as well as, as noted, kill a Werewolf with a silver assisted haymaker. Vampires continue to frighten me with their power.

    Marek had noted he was an excellent shot, and this proved his boast was not without teeth. He killed a Werewolf in a single shot, and another two human defenders with a single shot each. His lethal accuracy was…impressive, to say the least.

    Tatiana, unsurprisingly, was more hands on and visceral. She targeted Spirits, so to me she appeared to be shadowboxing. She evidently took three down. Two of them with a single blow each. I lack knowledge of Spirits to know how impressive this is, but I feel a certainty I would not want to have a strike from her against me.

    One thing I discovered was the claim silver is lethal to werewolves was no empty claim. Only Gunnar withstood more than a single shot, and he had spirits helping him. There is still no questioning their lethality, however. I hope we do not have to fight more Werewolves soon.

    Dearbháil healed. And hexed a little. Her Spring Court magic proved most helpful in lessening my injuries. She kept her head through the fight, though, which is good.

    Dacian started off using the Contracts of Smoke, but ceased at some point to taunt the mortals. He was hurt badly. His talents as a thief far outstrip his talents as a warrior, and I hope he takes more care for his safety next time.

    Wolfram was a juggernaut, as much as Alla, shrugging off rifle shots and werewolf claws, smoke pouring from his injuries. He was very impressive. I doubt I was the only lady who noticed. And like Alla, he was barely scratched when the fight was over despite the concerted effort to take him down. He also made Ilsa burst into flames.

    Just based on Wolfram, Sin-Eaters are powerful and implacable foes. He claimed some ability to shrug off man made attacks, and it served him well. I am unsure if this salient ability is special to him or common to Sin-Eaters. I know his ability to unleash ectoplasm fog and shrug off injuries is not uncommon. I can see now high explosives, set off from a remote location, are the way to fight them.

    I could not get a good idea of the Spirits or Ghosts, as only Franz Ludwig actually appeared and he fought like a human. I did not see the others but Tatiana shredded them easily. I suppose this could be said of most things she attacks, however.

    But the mission was a success, and I have learned much. I have a better idea of more supernatural creatures capabilities should they need to be dealt with, and I have cultivated a strong appreciation for the fact the rest of the Motley is on my side.

    Also, Mayhem was wrought. It was glorious.

    End Session XIV

    That was pretty crazy. Some thoughts:

    Several people got an extra health level from the totem, including Marek, Tatiana, and Lizette. Dacian got defense. Lizette buffed the weaker willed allies with the third level of Contracts of Entropy, as well as her uses of Contracts of Artifice ahead of time to boost others weapons (and Artifice and Animation on her own). Dearbháil used some Hearth on the enemies when not healing her allies. There was a lot of prep time, and it was used well.

    2nd ed Requiem vampires are tough. Alla easily shrugged off incoming rifle fire even with just a couple levels of Resilience.

    The Ivory Claws were dangerous, but well tanked by Tatiana, Alla and Wolfram. Especially given one one killed in a single shot in the first action of the fight by Marek. The humans were Wolf Blooded, hence their immunity to Werewolf hijinx.

    I implemented the Distractions hack from Armory Reloaded, allowing Changelings to Spend a Glamour to boost their Defense by their Wyrd against a single attack by an enemy who was not of the Wyrd themselves or enchanted. While useful, Lizette and Dacian took heavy damage compared to the Kindred and Bound shrugging damage off. Chainsaw damage is insane, by the way. Changelings also had the option of taking an action to try and harvest glamour at a small penalty, which Lizette did for fear in order to replenish her reserves burned though in buffing herself and others.

                            Dacian’s Book of Shadows

                            28 July 1920 - Afternoon

                            I am not ashamed of my failure, as I am mostly without such a frailty. Though I suppose disappointed is a better description. I have discovered weaknesses in myself that I will endeavor to correct. However this bit of inner reflection tells nothing of my actions.

                            I ventured to Gunnar’s estate and found it was as predictable as he. Heavily guarded and secure in its ability to keep the outside out. I prepared as I would for any other entry, taking care to conceal myself and exploit the natural powers of the Darkness. I did move very slowly while climbing the fence to avoid the guard’s notice, but I scuttled up the wall without reserve. The locked window eased open to my skilled fingers.

                            I quickly went about my mission: Make Gunnar look like a coward for fleeing his coming duel. In this, I succeeded without question. Even I would have believed he left in a hurry. Before I left, I used my Kenning to make sure there was nothing special I missed. Surely a magical item would be important enough to take if leaving in a rush. I found a most intriguing sword and scabbard which was most definitely magical. As I took the hilt into my hand, I was struck by a sense of wrongness. I turned to find a feminine figure reaching for me. It was at that point I realized what was wrong. A spirit of pain closed in upon me. The woman’s face was drawn open at the mouth by jagged fish hooks and her corpus seemed to suggest she was sentenced to an Iron Maiden. Those brief moments of understanding were my undoing. The spirit’s fingertips turned into talon-like fish hooks as I was suddenly wracked with pain and another sensation I could not comprehend at that moment. I’m told it was only the blessing of Ochranny which allowed me to withstand such an assault. Moving more on instinct than strategy, I dove for the window and ran down the wall, holding the rope to keep my head above feet. The Iron Maiden above screamed as she threw herself from the window in pursuit. Two more spirits, which in my haze of pain seemed like animate tree people, leapt from the treeline and launched stakes of white ash into my chest. My vision dimmed as my feet hit the ground, but as I coughed lifeblood on my lips, I thought of Marek and Wolfram. I had already failed the Uratha by being spotted. However, if I were to die here, Wolfram would surely be disgraced. With what little of my dwindling strength remained, I stumbled forward. The Iron Maiden was not so impaired as she rushed for me. Desperate not to leave a corpse on the estate, I flung open a door into the Hedge and scrambled like a lame hare through the two columns. The spirit of pain pulled up short of the door and I realized she was waiting for her compatriots to come and finish what they began. I ran to distance myself from the portal and to find another closer to my friends. If I were to die, I wanted to at least leave a body for them. Dearbháil’s conscience would not allow her to rest if she thought I had been Taken again. As the blood from my chest covered my slowing feet, I glimpsed a low door with Marek and Tatiana running toward me. They were both in Urhan form and were searching with their unconquerable smell. I crawled through the portal, which was actually a hole in a tree trunk before I collapsed. Tatiana became more human and made the bleeding stop before shifting to her Urshal form, lifting me in her jaws, and running. Unfortunately, the pain of such movement overcame me and I fell into blackness.

                            When I awoke, I was surrounded by all but Lizette in a very different part of the Hedge than I left. I was no longer impaled, my chest was bandaged, and I could move with somewhat less pain than before. Dearbháil was immediately over me and refreshing me with her Spring magics as soon as they noticed my awareness. We discussed our options after I recounted my failure. While no mortal authorities will have evidence of the truth, my blood spilled on the estate will guarantee the Pure will know Gunnar is not simply missing. Plus the spirits can report what they saw, which causes more problems for the cabal as a whole. Our plans were shattered. For a very small positive, Wolfram examined the sword while I was indisposed. It apparently is very effective at killing vampires. So much so that even the sight of the drawn weapon enrages the monster which drives them. I believe Alla referred to it as the Beast. Either way, she snarled at Wolfram when he attempted to unsheath the blade. We waited until Marek could no longer sense the spirit mark the Iron Maiden used to track me before I opened a new door out of the Hedge. It was less than an hour until we were due to arrive at the duel. Also that meant Alla had a similar amount of time before sunrise.

                            We returned to the Esterházy estate to change and plan. I slipped past Christina and Marlena to investigate Gunter’s room. There I found cologne which would help me mask any scent, but also mask no scent. Wolfram and Lizette had to suffer along with me during the ride to meet Archduke Eggenberg as I was not immune to the strong scent. The Archduke immediately wrinkled his nose as I opened the door to the car. We approached and were informed Gunnar was missing and believed to be dead. Eggenberg gave Wolfram the option to fight him if he must, but there was no reason to engage that particular werewolf. I apologized to him at the mention of the loss of a friend, but he seemed mostly indifferent. We returned home where I happily bathed and then found comfort in sleep.

                            We rejoined Alla in her room to plan for the salon. Several weapons would be stored within Wolfram but we would not go armed to the this gathering. The Uratha would be in attendance and stay in Dalu form, both as a precaution and to send a clear message of power. For my part, I hope to only impress and observe as I am still in no condition to face any further confrontations. Marek, Wolfram, Lizette, and I then moved on to classifying the relics.

                            There were several small artifacts which fell into a few categories, but the four pieces that struck me were the cup, the mirror, the lure, and the bowl. We have not yet learned the name of more than the Cup of Life and the Spirit Lure, but the uses of those four are most interesting.

                            The Cup of Life can hold the essence of power used by Uratha. I have no idea how to measure this essence, but by Marek’s reaction, I would suspect it is great.

                            The mirror allows its user to scry upon another by feeding it a small quantity of blood. My understanding was that the blood must come from the user, though I have not had time to investigate that further. Additionally, use of the mirror summons an unseen spirit either to the target’s location or the user’s. We are unsure how much, if any, hindrance this spirit could be.

                            The Spirit Lure is a small charm worn to make any spirit nearby find the wearer the perfect target for their needs, no matter what that may be. I can think of three spirits this could be useful for, but that is not relevant to the object.

                            The bowl will allow the user to bring items into the Shadow. I was unaware this was an issue, but Marek seemed vexed by this proposition. To use the bowl, the user must destroy the object, place it in the bowl, and consume the remains. Given Lizette’s talent with creation, I can imagine a large number of wooden constructs going into the bowl. It is not so hard to consume ashes when sprinkled over food.

                            Now I think I will go and bathe again. I can still smell the cologne.

Now Christina had told me before that she was hoping to marry Wolfram and that nothing between us should make him unhappy. Sadly.

Vampires, I guess, with their blood drinking and making people vampires. I don’t want them to make me a vampire. I am flammable enough as is; I can’t afford to be more flammable. Well, usually. Probably.
