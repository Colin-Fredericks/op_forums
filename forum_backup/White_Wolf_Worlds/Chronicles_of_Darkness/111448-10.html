                                    204 - The Infant Motor
                                    Have you ever heard of John Murray Spear? He was a clergyman, an abolitionist and a spiritualist, back in the 1850s. He claimed to claimed to be in contact with a group of spirits he called the 'Association of Electrizers'. Under the direction of these spirits (mostly various dead scientific personalities, including but not limited to Benjamin Franklin and Thomas Jefferson) he and a number of followers went to a town in Massachusetts to start building what they called alternately 'the New Messiah', 'the New Motive Power' and 'the Infant Motor'; a mechanical Second Coming meant to usher in a utopian era. They built the thing over nine months, then had a 'New Mary' (we don't know who this woman was) ritualistically 'birth' a spirit for the contraption they'd put together.

                                    It's obviously a God-Machine plot; the Association of Electrizers were clearly angels or an angel, and the Infant Motor just reeks of Infrastructure. After it was 'born', though, all it seemed to do was occasionally move some of the spheres it was composed of - plenty of disappointed witnesses attested to such - and eventually it was destroyed by a Baptist mob who thought it was heresy, after it was moved to New York. Or so Spear claimed.

                                    204.a: All Grown Up
                                    Spear lied. The New Motive Power was never destroyed; it was just moved to some secret location, and it remains an important piece of Command and Control infrastructure to this day. Its exact location is hidden, and undoubtedly it's guarded by angels and stranger things besides - but perhaps its location can be traced from Spear's later writings. Something like that's got to be important - and at the very least, keeping tabs on C&C Infrastructure is never a bad thing.

                                    204.a.1: In the Beginning
                                    The Infant Motor is far more than merely C&C Infrastructure. It was the infant form of the God-Machine itself, born under the hands of spiritualists, and saved by its nascent designs. Any signs of its activity prior to this point are either trickery and Concealment Infrastructure at work to obscure the timeline of its vulnerable, embryonic stage, or the result of the Machine propagating itself through time. If there were some way to return to this point in time, however, there is nothing that could not be accomplished - or risked. Who can say what enslaving, kidnapping, destroying or altering the infant God-Machine would result in. Perhaps deliverance. Perhaps annihilation.

                                    204.b: Scapegoat
                                    The New Messiah's purpose has long since been fulfilled. It was never meant as anything more than a sacrifice; an effigy of the God-Machine, built to attract attention and to be destroyed, the news of this destruction supernaturally magnified and embedded into the human psyche, inuring mortal minds to the idea that a God-Machine remains. If its plans could be uncovered, and the Infant Motor rebuilt, though, perhaps that might undo its effects, revealing sections of the Machine's workings. Whether this is a good idea is a debate in itself, but its implications could be anything from a new crop of Stigmatics to a backlash against the Machine great enough to make a real change.

                                    204.c: There Will Come a Child
                                    The Infant Motor wasn't a creation of the God-Machine at all. Rather, it was the first, faltering attempt of a Ring, Murray's 'Association of Electrizers' at creating their own Infrastructure, using a mortal puppet to create an extra layer of deniability. Their machine failed, or was sabotaged, or destroyed - but perhaps if you could track down their designs (or maybe even the Electrizers themselves, if any still survive) the project could be attempted again. Perhaps the Unchained can make their own savior.

                                    For more info on the New Motive Power (yes, it was real), see here: https://www.lorepodcast.com/episodes/61

                                        205 - The Nail
                                        It's common knowledge that the obelisk downtown, carted in from Egypt back in the 1910s, is Infrastructure. It's got that aura of Aether, and it's got an urban mythos around its 'curse' that the Unchained have long since linked back to a guardian angel assigned to it. No new sightings or instances of 'the curse' have been reported in almost a year now, though, and all signs point to the disappearance of the angel. Fallen, maybe, recalled or something else. Whatever the case, the Infrastructure seems to be open to suborning.

                                        That's the rub, though. Obviously, it could be some kind of trap, but if that was the Infrastructure's only purpose that's a hell of a wait for a payoff. And there's news reports from around the time of the obelisk's installation of disturbances, disappearances and even sightings of some kind of monster in the area. There's been no disappearances or sightings yet, but the city has suffered multiple low-level earthquakes over the last year. The only real way to know, would be to descend into the sewers and other tunnels beneath the streets, and find out what it is, exactly, that the Nail was meant to pin in place.

                                            206 - Our Lady of Rapture
                                            We have spent a lot of time and effort tracking weather, building ships bigger and stronger and mapping the ocean, yet ships still go missing. They founder in storms, run off-course, suffer catastrophic breakdowns. And sometimes, those who make it out of such a disaster spy, far-off, a tall, dark tower with a light at the tip. Lifeboats row towards it, hoping for rescue, but when they reach the strange lighthouse they find it empty, and stairs only leading down into the craggy rock on which it stands. A strange, siren song, modulated precisely to stimulate curiosity and suppress fear, draws them downwards into the depths of this strange Facility. Space bends, and before long beyond the portholes the stranded men see spires belching black smoke into the water - somewhere between chimneys and volcanic vents. Strange creatures swim outside, lit by the red fires and the stark lighting of the Facility. Finally, they are met by the inhabitants of this domain, the machine-angels who will be the caretakers of their transformation, and by then it is too late. They will be tranquilized and modified, electrodes inserted carefully into the skull to stimulate activity and counteract the effects of soul-loss, while those extracted souls will be put to use as components in the the more esoteric products of this deep-sea factory.

                                            At the heart of all this sits the archangel her brain-numbed minions call the Principality of the Abyss, the Cold Ocean Womb, or Our Lady of Rapture. She - insofar as she has gender - presides over all the workings of this Facility, and acts as a holy medium between the God-Machine and Its undersea operations. Her children manipulate the courses of sea-storms and hurricanes, and mine resources from the sea-bed to build the Machine's great projects. She is privy to secrets of the Machine shared by few others, and her power is enormous. If she could be destroyed, or made to Fall, though, what a Hell her Facility would make.
