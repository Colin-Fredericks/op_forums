                The Darkness Demon-Blooded
                The result of unusual & unknown circumstances. What is known about the origin of this Strain, is that it was so circumstantial the odds of it being replicated are beyond microscopic.
                Despite the "name" (seriously, I do not consider "the Darkness Demon-Blooded" to be a proper name) the Darkness was not alone in the birthing this Strain. There were some people who got, let's say infected by the Darkness & by the Light, almost simultaneously.
                The Darkness provided power & the Light prevented the Darkness from making them just another group of darkspawn.
                Their Endowment is that they can use various Umbrae. One Umbrae per Endowment dot. However, their is a downside to this Endowment. The Darkness is still there, ever present, & every time a Hunter of this demon-blooded strain uses an Endowment power, they must mentally fight against the Darkness with their Willpower. Failure does not mean that the power has failed, failure means that the Hunter's morality has taken a hit. Drop to one or possibly zero dots in morality, & the Hunter is lost to become just another darkspawn, albeit one a bit more dangerous than the standard everyday darkspawn.
                Darkness Tainted places make fighting against this corruption harder, & the morality will drop more than one dot.
                Because of this eternal, internal fight that they face, the Darkness Demon-Blooded have a reputation for being very understanding, forgiving, & accepting, sometimes out doing even the Lucifuge.

                    The Light Demon-Blooded
                    In all times there are people who experiment, pushing the boundaries of what is & isn't possible.
                    In the Kingdom, one such person tried infusing the Light in its most raw form into ordinary people. What the motivation was has been lost to time, what matters is that they succeeded, at the worst possible time, the fall of the Kingdom.
                    In the Kingdom's absence, the various people who carried the Light in their bodies, they scattered to the winds.
                    Over time they bred, had descendants, & the Light was diluted, eventually only appearing in exceptional souls.
                    At the same time the world grew harsher, less a place where the Light felt at home, so those inheritors learned how to fight, & the Light was changed by this.
                    These days when the Light appears, it is not the warm, comforting glow that the Queens remember or Nobles carry, it is the harsh, blinding spear of an angel.
                    Look away for you will find no comfort here, for these are warriors with blood on their hands.
                    There's a reason the combat-focused Calling of the Nobles is called "Champion" not "Slayer." Well, these guys are the "slayers."
                    As a result of the Light in them, they have a measure of resistance against the corrupting influence of the Darkness.

                            3 more antagonists tied to the Princess the Hopeful game.

                            A low, persistent sound that is a remnant of the Kingdom from before it fell.
                            It works its way into ordinary people, gradually getting them to be better than they were. You might think that this is a good thing with nothing bad about it, but the effects is basically brainwashing & can cause many lives to be destroyed in the process.

                            It was born when some "Darkness fungi" infected an apparently normal human being. The human, as it turned out, was Gifted & the interaction between the fungus & the supernatural capability they had (whatever it was) caused the person to turn into a humanoid fungal creature. Now they sit in their lair, which slowly grows, ruling over all they surview. Their power is at least equal to that of a Dethroned, as proven when they killed one that wandered into the lair.

                            A former Mekhet which became trapped in the Dark World for a time. When they reemerged they were a thing made of shadows that drank positive "feelings" like hopes, dreams. It especially likes the taste of Inner Light, & will target Nobles, Beacons, & Sworn before others.

                                                Some more Princess: The Hopeful antagonists, these ones with a focus on the Darkness.

                                                A low-level spirit (but still high enough to be an individual spirit) of apathy that shows signs of something far darker.                                                 If the werewolf disease is removed the afflicted turns into a darkspawn.

                                                            These are currently the last Princess-specific antagonists that I've come up with at the moment. These ones I consider not really, properly attached to Dreamlanders or the Darkness. That may change if they get properly developed.

                                                            A weird affliction that effects Nobles, causing them to be driven to the unhealthy extreme of their Court's ideals/ideology/philosophy. The affliction does not have to result in Nobles going Dethroned, & I think that it doesn't need to do that.

                                                            A fallen Kingdom that does not realize its time has ended & is trying to manifest itself in the physical world.

                                                            A weapon created during the fall of the Kingdom. Designed with one purpose in mind: to fight the Darkness.
                                                            The weapon was made by a Noble of Swords & a Noble of Diamonds. It is immune to the taint of the Darkness, but that might be the only good thing about it. It isn't human, not matter how much it may look human, & cannot truly grasp human morality using the ideals of the Courts as its guiding star, & not factoring morality in its equations.
                                                            Thankfully, it is not a fan of the plans of the Twilight Courts.
                                                            Recently rumours have started to circulate that say that there are more of its kind, these ones bound to individual Court's, but again without a sense of human morality.

                                                            Something designed by TFV & chucked in a Black Box/Book(?) "archive".
                                                            It got picked out by someone, & wormed into their brain taking over.
                                                            It devours the Inner Light of Nobles, turning them back into unmagical humans for a time. The Inner Light will return thankfully.
                                                            It's motives are unknown, but suspected to be a mixture of the Darkness' agenda & TFV's agenda.
