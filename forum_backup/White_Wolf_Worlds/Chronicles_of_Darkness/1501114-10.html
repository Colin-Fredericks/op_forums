                            Originally posted by ajf115 View Post
                            This is probably a dumb question, but I'm coming back to CofD after a long time away, and one of my players is interested in playing a grappling character. Therefore, my question is, what does the rulebook mean by an 'overpower maneuver'? Is that the same thing as the moves a grapple offers?
                            Yup, the 1e corebook explicitly uses that term in the grappling rules and there's 2e Merits dating back to the God-Machine Chronicle Rules Update (meant to be used alongside the 1e corebook) that still reference that terminology and didn't get their verbiage updated to account for the 2e presentation of the rules not using that term.

                            Originally posted by nWoD 1e Core Rulebook, p38, Roll and Trait Summary
                            Grapple: Roll Strength + Brawl - opponent’s Defense for attacker to get a grip on target; roll Strength + Brawl - opponent’s Strength to perform an overpowering maneuver or to break free; instant action. (p. 157)
                            Originally posted by nWoD 1e Core Rulebook, p157, Unarmed Combat - Grapple
                            If in the next turn the attacker still has a hold, he can try to overpower his opponent. A Strength + Brawl roll is made. The target’s Strength rather than Defense is subtracted from the attacker’s dice pool. If no successes are gained, the attacker still has a hold, but accomplishes nothing more in the turn (he does not overpower his victim). If even one success is gained, one of the following overpowering maneuvers can be accomplished in the turn.
                            Originally posted by nWoD 1e Core Rulebook, 159, Sidebar: Grappling Summary
                            If the attacker’s grip on the target persists, and he is free to do so, the attacker can try to apply an overpowering maneuver to the victim. Roll the attacker’s Strength + Brawl - opponent’s Strength. Any successes allow a maneuver (see below).
                            • Possible maneuvers. Choose one:
                            Render opponent prone
                            Damage opponent
                            Immobilize opponent
                            Draw weapon
                            Attack with drawn weapon
                            Turn a drawn weapon Disarm opponent
                            Use opponent as protection from ranged attacks

                            Attempting to break free is always an option instead of performing an overpowering maneuver.​

                                Originally posted by ajf115 View Post
                                This is probably a dumb question, but I'm coming back to CofD after a long time away, and one of my players is interested in playing a grappling character. Therefore, my question is, what does the rulebook mean by an 'overpower maneuver'? Is that the same thing as the moves a grapple offers?
                                This appears to be a product of rules text that was written based on either the 1e World of Darkness Rulebook or an earlier draft of the 2e rules that didn't get revised when the final 2e rules failed to specify what overpowering is.

                                It's the same thing as the moves a grapple offers, the maneuvers on pp. 89-90 of the Chronicles of Darkness Rulebook (or wherever grappling rules are in your monster corebook). The 1e corebook referred to these as "overpowering grappling maneuvers."
