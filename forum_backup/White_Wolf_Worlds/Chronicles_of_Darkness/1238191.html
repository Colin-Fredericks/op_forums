                  07-24-2018, 04:08 PM
                  p. 181: "Per Turn Limit: Characters may only spend 1 Willpower per turn."

                  This is at odds with what's in Chronicles of Darkness (p. 73) and the other Second Edition cores: "You can only spend one point of Willpower per action."

                  It's a fiddly distinction, but it's meaningful in certain contexts (a reflexive action to bolster resistance and an instant action to activate a power in the same turn, e.g.). That said, it might be that the Changeling text is corrective and the others are wrong, or this is just a change.

                  Edit: The Fairest Blessing (p. 29) says "You may still only spend one Willpower point per action" so I think p. 181 is indeed wrong.

                                                          Changeling Character Sheet:

                                                          Touchstones do not appear on the sheet the way they're referenced in the book itself, this makes it confusing to know which line to write down the touchstone.

                                                          Health boxes are not editable, damage cannot be marked on the pdf directly. Clarity boxes are binary like Willpower boxes, they should be editable as text so that / or X can be marked in them.

                                                          Contracts are a mess. The name box is huge, while Goblin contract is an option there's no option for which Regalia or Mantle the Contract uses (should just be a text box for "source" and get rid of the Goblin checkbox. The Dice box is too small to write the actual dicepool (though it is large enough to write the dice, but it's nice to have the pool itself). There's no room to write the Contested dicepool if the action is Contested. The Loophole section is too small to write the majority of the Loopholes. There's nowhere to note the effect of the Contract.
