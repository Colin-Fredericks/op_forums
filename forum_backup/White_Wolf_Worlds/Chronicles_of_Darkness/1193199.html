    [BPG] Inguma – Family of Outsiders – and minorities

    03-08-2018, 01:43 PM
    BPG means Beast Player’s Guide – and in this book is new Family of Beasts. So called Inguma are embodying the idea of Fear of the Other in a given community. With rise of xenophobia ( and any other minorities –phobia, in reality ) in my homeland Poland – and me writing fan Dark Era Conquest of Paradise about 15th century Portugal and Spain in that were rise of violent actions against local Jews and Muslims – I wonder how much Inguma reflects those fears. As bisexual man I also looks for this Family also reflecting LGBT+ ‘minority as monsters’ in society Eye of Beholder ( pun intended ). Topic is for reflecting this kind character and try to use those Fears in not too much offensive way. But still – as embodied by monstrous Horrors the unreal fears of humanity – some grotesque views probably cannot be missed. Best would be pointing or things like Inguma Horrors could look like.

    For example, for Conquest of Paradise I was thinking about Horror reflecting antiJews views as Dybbuk, vengeful spirit from People of Torah myths. Or maybe Golem is good example of Inguma Horror? He would spread irrational fear in humans seeing him, not very different than this of Prometheans.

    I have real problem in imaging how could look like Horror reflecting fears about LGBT+ people - What, this being would look like mismatched amalgam of male and women parts? Or something like Warhammer’s Slaanesh? Because ‘man dressed as women’ is clearly not enough as true Horror image.

They're perhaps the most subtle and certainly the closest to 'civilized' of the Families, because they were born from civilization.
