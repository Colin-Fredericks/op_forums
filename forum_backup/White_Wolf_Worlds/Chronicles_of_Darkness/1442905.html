    1st edition books and and 2e: Setting, Bloodlines, Disciplines and creatures.

    04-06-2021, 07:54 PM
    Greetings,

    I have a lot of questions regarding Ancient Bloodlines and second edition:
    1. Ancient Bloodlines: Are there any plans to convert the bloodlines there to 2e?
    2. Ancient Bloodlines: Are any plans to convert Kindred Vodoun to 2e? And to update the New Orleans setting with it?
      I loved the Vodoun aspect but it was confusing when you already have been using the New Orleans setting. One of the major plot points in New Orleans is that Baron Cimitiere wishes to create a new blood magic that is vodoun-based (and a whole Covenant to go with it), and then comes Ancient Bloodlines and says: "Well, that thing you wanted to make? That thing already exists, it is well known, and established; and you being embraced in Haiti should already know it."
      It also seems appropriate for Cimitiere to try to obtain the "Eye of Ghede" from the Apollinaire bloodline.
    3. Ancient Bloodlines: Will Mérges Sorcery be updated?
      With Virtues/Vices no longer being part of the vampire condition remove the "fueled by anger" aspect (and bonuses) from it. Any plans to update this blood magic?
    4. Ancient Bloodlines: Will the Edimmu come back? And what relationship they have with the Strix, if any?
      Love those guys, and seem the perfect Strix counter, in a horrible way for Vampires.
    5. Immortal Sinners: Lithopedia and its maker are awesome, but how does it work (thematically) with Coil of the Quintessence?
      Lithopedia is fantastic, but it has strong overlap with the Coil of the Quintessence, actually, many 1st edition disciplines overlap (to a larger or lesser degree) too: Gilded Cage, Institutionalize, Tenure.


    I know those are many questions, but I really loved some things from the 1st edition, and would loathe to see them gone forever.

      Officially? No. There really aren't any plans for conversions (at least publicly), except on a case by case basis, depending on what the developer wants to do with any hypothetical book.

      However, I've got a few books up on the Storytellers Vault that convert Kindred Vodoun and the Eye of Ghede (care of falco1029, who has numerous Blood Sorcery updates in his catalogue), as well as updates of Lithopedia, Institutionalize, and Tenure (and soon, Gilded Cage). With Lithopedia, we touched on the Coil of Quintessence as an aside, but we didn't really go into how they interact.

      I do have plans to do an entire Ancient Bloodlines update book, but that's a bit of a ways down the road. That said, Merges Sorcery might end up in the Gangrel book we'll be doing. Edit: NO BAD WRONG BRAIN NO WORK.
