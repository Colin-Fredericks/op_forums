                  And truth is...I've read a lot of Xianxia stories. Webnovels, but still.

                  A straight port of morality from the Chronicles of Darkness would be significantly more morally concerned than a lot of genre fiction in Xianxia. They're infamous for having outright amoral protagonists where "I am stronger than you" is regarded as the only justification needed for really nasty behavior. Combine that with actual yaoguai and Demon Cultivators, along with Ghost Immortals, Heart Devils, Celestial Bureaucracy knows what else...there's plenty of monsters already in Xianxia that are already Mage-adjacent.

                  So, I'll start. Keeping in mind I do not have the cultural background and have no idea if "Niohuru Die" is grammatically sensible:

                  Werewolf: Way of Violent Harmony and Beast Cultivation

                  It is a fact of life that many animals, if the order of the world glitches so that they become sentient, seek to become more human, and sometimes, they succeed. They are the yaoguai, kin to human cultivators - which was not lost on several schools. In particular, the Border Balancing Sect discovered that many humans are actually descendants of the lover of the Moon Goddess, Niohuru Die, a wolf yaoguai so old and wise he had practically become a god - and slain by his human descendants when they realized he had become stuck in his spiritual progression and was increasingly victimized by the Heart Devils who call themselves the Maeljin, a word that no one knows the origin of, all that it means profound wickedness and a desire for evolution at the cost of others and the world. To that effect, the Sect developed a way to call forth their forefather's inherited ki, allowing them to assume the form of wolf yaoguai and learn the world as only demons and other spirit beasts can, as something whose very consciousness is a violation of the will of Heaven. As an orthodox sect, the Border Balancers wish to maintain the harmony between the human and spirit worlds, but in sharp contrast to others, they do so through their bloodline talent with furious and aggressive fighting styles, to the point they are infamous for losing themselves in the midst of a hunt. To cultivators who seek to learn from them, however, beware their Heretical breakaway, the Pure Forest Rage School - to them, their capacity to assume both human and yaoguai forms mean they are something more akin to gods and spirits, and as atonement for their ancestors' lack of filial piety, wish to overwhelm the mortal kingdoms with legions of angry and vengeful spirits and rule over the wreckage. And especially beware the Maeljin, and the terrible Moon Nightmares who Niohuru once imprisoned in the house of his beloved, the spirits of the world before its ordering.

                              Mandatory disclaimer: Xianxia and Cultivation stories are based on ancient Chinese cosmologies and different mystical schools (Taoism mainly, but lots of Buddhist influence along folk religion and others), while being clearly a literary fantasy genre. The closest thing I can explain to gamers it would be the same way Dungeons and Dragons is based on (but not meant to be representative of) medieval stories and history.

                              Since it copies from classical Chinese thinking, there are a lot of references to the Heaven-Man-Earth triad. Heaven is (usually) not the afterlife. When Cultivation/Xianxia says Heaven it could be several things: a higher plane of existence, the literal heavenly bodies or the spiritual part that all beings should have. Earth is the world itself, and the substance that beings are made of. And Man is literal mankind but also the rational parts that only humanity has among all Earthly beings.

                              Depending on the variations, the afterlife could be a better version of your life on earth, reincarnation, dissolution in the great Dao, ghostly existence (related to the dual souls: Hun and P'o), or a combination of all of the above.




                              That being said, based on generic stories:
                              • Regular cultivator techniques is Mage. The Arcana, Legacies and Prelacies, are the cultivation abilities, the Dao and the Heaven patronage respectively.​
                              • Beast and Vampire would be Demonic Cultivators, the Beast and the Horror could be thought as different types of Gu, spiritual contamination, demonic beings, etc.
                              • Spectral cultivators, Geist got you covered.
                              • Werewolves and other shapeshifters would be Demonic Beasts (Be aware that Demons in Xianxia are usually two different categories: mutated animals/people OR spiritual entities; shapeshifters would be the first kind).
                              • The second type of demons are of course creatures from the lower depths: Fiends from Duat, Demons from Inferno, etc.
                              • All the Ephemeral creatures are Spiritual entities, some are nature-based (Spirits), Tulpas (Goetia), and Spectres (Ghosts), Heavenly beings are obviously God-Machine Angels, and Pure Heavenly beings are the Supernal creatures.
                              • Changelings are a type of nature spirt and/or purified demonic beings.
                              • Prometheans, Deviants, and God Machine Demons I have no idea where to put.



                              Generally speaking, Mage the Awakening (with a dash of Second Sight) is what better can represent typical Cultivation and Xianxia stories. Striving for the Heavens (starting with Archmastery and finally Ascension), Mana as Qi, Legacies as Dao, Scelestus / Infernalist as Demonic cultivators, the Heavenly tribulations when growing more powerful (Exarch intervention), all fit in the genre perfectly. The basic types of cultivators also get representation: martial (Arrow), academic (Mysterium), spies/assassins/manipulators (Guardians), royal/priestly (Ladder), independent thinkers (Free Council and Nameless Orders), and can still cover other types as needed.

                                                          My experience with Xianxia is limited to a few unambitious Manhwas. Ones I ate up like the trash-eater I am. What they and this thread's suggestions have in common, is that they're optimistic and growth-oriented. Cultivators are seen to only ever rise upwards (or die). Here's where I say that Xianxia and Deviant could make for a dark and rich combination.

                                                          Consider the old teachers. They encourage their students to advance the established methods, knowing that most of them will break their souls during experimentation. The masters wait until one of their subordinates discovers a rare, safe method of progress that they can then take for themselves. Until then, they help new recruits along to that breaking point, they make sure to maintain the veneer of superiority in front of their equals, they manage the volatile Remade who made a step into the unknown and suffered for it, and they look with jealous eyes at other masters.

                                                          Who are those other masters? Manipulators who kept their souls intact. Remade, still buying into the lie. Patsies, built up to deflect suspicion from the real masterminds.

                                                          Consider the students. Some are eager in their pursuit and trust in their seniors to steer them right. Some saw through the charade, but are confident that they will succeed where others failed. Some aim at becoming the new old teacher - safe from Divergence, but joining an elite few of paranoid, backstabbing liars. Some see through the system they're tangled in. They wish to retire with their few stable powers, but with their secret knowledge and their status as a possible guinea pigs, they're in straits as dire as any Remade.

                                                          Consider the Remade themselves - Broken, but no weaker for it. Some that are certain in their superiority, competing for students, mystical resources, and jealously guarded methods. Some that realize the betrayal, and are faced with a machinery of desire that will always come back from destruction, as long as there is dangerous knowledge and people willing to use it. Both are a threat and and asset to the old teachers, to be manipulated and aimed at their opponents.

                                                          Consider a World of Cultivation where the path to ascension in yet uncharted, where treacherous guides pretend to enlightenment, where your brothers and sisters in the art twist into monsters. Where Schools of Cultivation war with inhuman powers over hidden secrets and loud deceptions. Where ascension meets decay.​
