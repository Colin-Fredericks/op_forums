    Stewards of the Celestial Orrery - 2e Legacy Update

    10-19-2022, 05:05 PM
    Hello everyone! This is my first attempt at porting over an old legacy and looking for feedback and suggestions for refinement. The Stewards of the Celestial Orrery were first published in the Silver Ladder order source book, and I was surprised when looking for 2e adaptations that this one was never documented anywhere I can find. With the canonical changes of Atlantis to the more general "Time Before" and the more ambiguous nature of the Oracles, I did some adaptation to the background and history for this re-adaptation into 2e. The original version was also very lacking on initiation details and history, so I added more 'fluff' there.

    Thematically I ran with the main concepts of Prophecy/Divination, Stars/Sky, Belief in Oracles, Timeline Integrity, and Anti-Seer in mind with designing attainments. The Prime attainments went a different direction than the 1e Legacy with some of the changes to Prime's purview between editions, as well as the expansion to 5 attainments and optional attainments from three in the first edition version.

    Stewards of the Celestial Orrery

    She spends her evening at the telescope, noting down positions and cross referencing it with a vellum tome by her side. “Based on the alignment of Jupiter and Mars, tomorrow is the best time to bring the charges forward.” She advises her Deacon the next day. He listens and is rewarded, the traitor removed from the Consilium. They always listen to what she learns in the stars.

    The Designs of the Oracles

    Awakened whisper of the Oracles, the supposed builders of the Watchtowers. Some doubt and others debate their existence, but the Stewards of the Celestial Orrery, they watch for the signs. Since antiquity, mankind has looked up at the night sky in wonder, and found that signs from the heavens could guide their path. The Awakened who did the same believe it is a reflection within the Supernal Realm that drives this fascination, a reflection of the Oracles themselves. Through careful study, they first find and study the signs in the Heavens to enact needed change in the world below.

    The Stewards are an ancient legacy, drawing from the traditions of soothsayers, seers, and diviners in mankind’s roots applied to the knowledge only the Awakened possess. They do not hold the same sway and influence that they once did, but the memory remains. Within the Orders they find a home among the Silver Ladder first and foremost. Their views on an orderly universe guided by the Oracles align with the Silver Ladder's Elemental Precepts. Stewards within the Silver Ladder act as advisors to Awakened leaders, or use their skills to monitor Cryptopolies future progress. A lesser known branch of the Legacy exists among the Guardians of the Veil. They view the deeper esoteric mysteries in alignment with the practices of their Legacy, knowing the Oracles pave the way for the Hieromagus by guiding their actions.

    Origins

    Parentage: Obrimos, Silver Ladder, Guardians of the Veil
    Nickname: Augurs, Stewards
    Background: They are often drawn from those called to leadership within spiritual or mystical hierarchies: ambitious clergy and ranking members of secret societies with metaphysical leanings, for instance.
    As society modernized, applicants began coming from astronomical studies, as the motions of the heavens are of especial interest to the Stewards. Others now come from the ranks of watchmakers, machinists, quality assurance professionals and the like, vocations that involve the establishment and maintenance of order on whatever scale.
    Appearances: For the most part, Augurs prefer to dress in stately, conservative styles when in public, often in ways that hint at civil or spiritual authority. Members of the Legacy who are part of the Guardians may forgo such style of dress in everyday life, but still have formal attire when ceremony dictates.

    Doctrine

    Prerequisites: Time 2, Occult 2, two dots in one of the following Skills; Crafts, Science, Politics, Survival, or Persuasion
    Initiation: A prospective Augur must submit themselves to potential mentors to be judged worthy of training. Most claim they felt a vocation to the legacy, often filling the void of structure or rank they left when they Awakened. Once selected an applicant studies with their mentor of the ancient methods of divination using celestial objects. They study knowledge the legacy has collected regarding the immeasurable host of Angels of Time and the mythos of the Oracles of the Watchtowers. More traditional mentors may require the actual construction of an orrery from the legacies namesake. Once the mentor believes they are ready, the applicant is instructed to use their tools and knowledge to divine the hidden designs imprinted in the Heavens by the Oracles. In the moment which shapes the soul, Augurs believe they truly glimpse the will of the Oracles in their reading and unlock the first attainment. According to those in the Legacy, the knowledge gained is Supernal in nature, and members can never recall more than the feeling of the moment, but have faith that what they learned will be applied to their future actions, making them instruments of the Oracles.
    Organization: Stewards take their view of an ordered universe and apply it to the legacy organization. Senior members are due respect of their position from their lessors and often hold positions of power within the structures of Awakened society outside the legacy which enforce this further. When multiple Stewards are within the same Consilium, this hierarchy is respected across Orders.
    Theory: Stewards of the Celestial Orrery believe the Oracles are in the Supernal, sending signs to be viewed by those who are watching into the heavens. As members of the legacy they are burdened with the duty to take those signs and use it to guide those who are Awake and Sleeping alike and to protect from the servants of Exarchs and the Lie.

    Magic

    Ruling Arcanum: Time
    Yantras: Succeed on an Occult or Science role related to the position of the stars in relation to the spell(+2), Incorporate symbols of celestial objects specific to the spell (+1), Invoking a secret symbol of an Oracle (+1), Acting in accordance to a divination of the Oracle's will (+1, +2 if it imposes a negative condition)
    Oblations: Advising others on a future endeavors without use of Magic, Making a star chart for divination purposes, studying/practicing mundane methods of divination, meditating on symbolic representation of the Oracles

    Attainments

    First: Chart the Sky
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The Augurs claim this attainment is an aspect of their momentary connection with the Oracles during initiation, guiding their actions from above. As an instant action this attainment emulates "Momentary Flux". Most commonly used to reflect on their own planned action, an Augur can also apply it to an object or person they are touching.
    Optional: Prime 1
    Augurs versed in Prime can internalize the Oracles' revelation further, as an instant action they can see an overlay of the Truth on the world. This duplicates the effects of "Pierce Deception'' with an additional effect that persons or items which are not in their current timeline also appear to be a Lie and the time dissonance is revealed to the Augur.

    Design Notes: The optional attainment extra effect isn't quite within the reach allotment, but it seems fitting for a Time focused legacy.

    Second: Record the Motion
    Prerequisites: Time 2, Occult 3
    As an Augur grows in their understanding, they know their proper place within the Oracles’ design and become resistant to the changes of other Mages. By studying the heavens and reflecting on the portents of the Oracles contained within them for a scene, this attainment mimics "Constant Presence" with reach allocated to Advanced Duration. Augurs usually enact this ritual at the end of their day or right before dawn.
    Optional: Prime 2
    Augurs with a sufficient understanding of Prime can also solidify their own pattern during their nightly meditations. This attainment emulates "Wards and Signs" with reach assigned to Advanced Duration.

    Design Notes: Nothing extra here, just 2 dot Shielding effects with Advanced Duration and Scene activation

    Third: Craft the Celestial Diagram
    Prerequisites: Time 3, two dots in a second Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills
    An Augur with this attainment has internalized the Supernal Heaven's design and can view that pattern unfolding in the Fallen World. This resembles the spell “Divination” with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range, and specific questions.
    Optional: Prime 3
    Augur's can apply this internalization to their own tools, mimicking the spell "As Above so Below" to a single tool on their person or an environmental Yantra related to celestial bodies. This attainment uses reach for Instant Action and 8-again quality but is limited to only a single Yantra at a time and no others can benefit from the attainment.

    Design Notes: Primary effect is within normal limits of Legacy design rules, the optional effect technically stretches the range rules for the environmental Yantra portion, but it's limited to the theme of the Legacy. Name adapted from the original Legacy write up in 1e.


    Fourth: Tune the Orrery
    Prerequisites: Time 4, Occult 4
    The Augurs are aware that they are not the only ones taking the measure of Time’s skien. As an additional outcome of their meditations, Augurs can protect their past from view from those without the Wisdom to understand the Oracles' design. This attainment duplicates "Shield of Chronos" with reach assigned to advanced duration and the additional effect of overlaying a false series of events.
    Optional: Prime 4
    It is an unfortunate reality that some Awakened at best let their own Hubris guide their actions, and at worst are servants of the Lie, under the Exarch's sway. Augurs suitably trained in Prime can impose the design of the Oracles on to other Awakened magics, suppressing those spells which do not fit the pattern. This effect mirrors "Dispel Magic" with reach assigned to Instant casting, Sensory Range, and making the effect Lasting (+2). This attainment costs 1 mana to use and can only affect spells under Arcana that the Augur also has knowledge of.

    Design Notes: Within the guidelines of Legacy attainments, I thought about capping the Dispel effect to only Time or Prime Arcana but by this point a Mage with Prime 4 already has access to Supernal Dispellation for non-attainment needs. Also not 100% sold on the name for this attainment.

    Fifth: Experience Supernal Clarity
    Prerequisites: Time 5, two dots in a third Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills
    In the ultimate expression of the Augur's understanding of the portents of the Oracles, in an instant the Augur can ponder any number of hypothetical questions related to a subject's future and understand what should come to pass. They can apply that wisdom within Social Maneuvering, working in alignment with their visions. This attainment emulates the spell "Prophecy" with reach assigned to instant casting and the ability to apply their dots in Time to reduce a subject's Doors where appropriate.
    Optional: Prime 5
    Augurs who master Prime have found that they can use their understanding of the Oracles Supernal designs to create a Hallow in the Fallen world. This takes a scene where the Augur must apply the location's position against the heavenly sphere and align their soul with the significance of the area and costs one mana. An Augur can only maintain one such Hallow but repeated applications of the Attainment renew the Duration. Any spells which attempt to collect Mana from the Hallow by Mage’s without the Augurs favor are withstood by the Mage’s dots rating in Prime.

    Design Notes: I didn't make sense to have them using any of the Making or Unmaking Time effects outlined in the book, so opted to return to a Divination effect and tie into the 1e Legacy notes around being advisors through the Social Maneuvering reach effect. For Prime this is the best mirror in the current rules to the 1e effect where an Augur could become their own Hallow. The control over the Hallow is an extra reach, but seems fair for a final attainment.

    1e Source:
    The Silver Ladder, pg.178 - 181

      The Augurs had such a fascinating writeup in the Silver Ladder book, I am happy you are converting them to 2ed. They are what inspired me to make my own prophecy focused Legacy. I always thought the clockwork part was very fitting thematically, but the original Prime Attainments did not build up on the Time ones.

      I think the third Attainment feels a bit less resonant with the themes of prophecy and revelation. Perhaps something like Sentinel's Ear would allow them to passively be aware of any traps, enchanted items or Supernal effects around them. That would build on their image of seeing more than others. Combined with their Prophecy Attainment, they can now walk into a room, know what is suspicious and tell you what will happen if you interact with something.

      The fifth optional Attainment is from 1ed, but it seems a bit dissonant with the theme in 2ed. Creating a Hallow means you send shockwaves through the ley lines, which creates new Mysteries. This introduces more moving parts to the existing clockwork of the area or introduces chaos if it was properly calibrated. It would also be a 2 dot Hallow due to how that spell is Withstood.

      What about Words of Truth with Reach assigned to instant use, remote viewing, advanced duration and calling the target to action, costing a mana to activate Time in a Bottle. The mage can choose who in their network they speak to with their statements, if anyone at all. This way make an annonimous call to someone about to be assassinated and compel them to do something about it, they can give their warnings over scrying windows, notify all their key contacts of something as soon as they learn it, etc. This could justify the stellar reputation they had before the creation of the Free Council catching them unawares, compeling service to the Oracles via honesty and objectivity, avoiding the Cassandra Truth situation.

          To start, I think this is a well crafted update. The Attainments are described clearly, and with an understanding of underlying rules. Having said that, here are my nitpicks:
