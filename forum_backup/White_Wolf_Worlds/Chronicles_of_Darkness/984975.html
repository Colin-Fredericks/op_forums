    [Custom Legacy] Serpents of the Garden

    10-10-2016, 02:44 PM
    Came up with this for a game set in L.A. I was rather fond of it, so I'm sharing it here.
    The last three attainments have no optional additions in an effort to strengthen (*cough* justify) the main effect.
    Has some minor inspirations from In Nomine.
    Link to original document here.


    Serpents of the Garden







    Origins

    Parentage: Mastigos

    Sobriquets: Lilim, Daughters or Sons of Lilith

    Background: The Lilim are the inheritors of a truly ancient tradition. They proudly remind outsiders that the idea of Lilith goes back well before Jewish tradition, all the way back to the first legends in the tales of Gilgamesh, to the spirit of a tree in the garden of Inanna. They do not scoff at the later traditions, either, appreciating the Jewish folkloric belief that Lilith was the first woman, a more motivated and independent being than the docile Eve. Ultimately, they believe that all human legends to be corruptions of the Truth, and that the legends encoded in the very deepest parts of the Astral Realms hold a greater answer. They say that a more accurate variation of the myth is that Lilith ate of the Tree of Knowledge, and so was the first who became more than animal becoming a Dragon; the serpent was not some third party named Samael, but Lilith herself, guiding Eve to Awaken as the first mage and instill the potential for humans to perceive Truth by seizing the fruit of Supernal knowledge. Truly arrogant Lilim may even believe that Lilith was a Dragon of Atlantis, or became one in the manner the Lilim themselves aspire to – transcendence into living legends and aspects of vice that teach humanity to transcend their ignorance and limitations.

    They also do not neglect the further folkloric traditions that say that Lilith became a demon who lay with the demons of the field – the source of the Lilims’ spiritual powers, they say – even a plague upon the Sons of Adam in vengeance for his attempted rulership, and her children likewise – never human, always lurking in stories and fears that mothers would tell their children. They believe that Lilith and her children never gave up trying to help their sisters’ progeny, but were unforgiving when they took on the foolish, blind nature of Adam. By tormenting humanity with vices and obsessions, they would lead them to question their place in the world and continue seeking enlightenment. Lilim speak of a demon that lies within each person, a part of the soul called the Persona, which may be the Daimon found in the Oneiros or it might not.

    Taking a less Judeo-Christian-centric viewpoint, many Lilim point out that, linguistically, lilitu simply means “spirit” in the Assyrian language whereas alu means demon. Indeed, their powers are no less or more evil than any spirit’s. Their ultimate goal is liberation – freedom from bad, stale, broken modes of thinking at minimum. Even among those who believe Lilith is an actual person, most usually reject the standard mythology that there was an Eve or an Adam – instead, they believe them to be metaphors for the human race as a whole, with Lilith the Dragon who guided oppressed women in dreams to Awaken, and who chastises men for holding them back.

    In order to achieve this state, Lilim undergo a progressive transformation into a spiritual being, a Goetia, reflective of the vice they most wish to emphasize, and, ultimately, ascendency to their own personal Garden. The Legacy appeals most strongly to Mastigoi and Thyrsai, but others have been known to join if they’re dedicated enough and feel as strongly called. Lilim meet in a place known as the First Garden, which lies in the Primordial Realm, a place beneath the Temenos where human artifice has been stripped away, leaving only the most raw, unfiltered emotions and archetypes.

    Appearance: Lilim tend to look like anyone when they begin their journey towards the Garden. Part of the process of Initiation is deciding upon what form their demonic persona will be, and as they grow in attainments they begin to dress and act in a manner coherent with their persona. The Fourth Attainment involves them uniting with and becoming their demon in truth, appearing as them in Twilight, while in other realms, when manifesting their Garden, or upon death when freed from the mortal coil. This demon usually resembles the mage as seen though the idealized lens of their Sin, but it’s rather common for Lilim to have demons that don’t resemble their original bodies, especially since those who are attracted to the legacy tend to come from a repressed background.


    Doctrine

    Prerequisites: Mind 2, Occult 2, one of the following skills at 2 or higher: Empathy, Expression, Persuasion, Socialize, or Subterfuge

    Initiation: A prospective Lilim who catches the eye of an existing member is first tested and coached by a mentor, and then, when the student decides they are ready, they’re led in astral travel to the Omphalos and then expected to find their way through the dark, terrible Primordial Dream. They are shadowed in this by experienced Lilim, who torment the applicant, forcing the applicant to confront his or her most tempting vices – not to make her stray, but to see if she is willing to embrace vice as a tool of self-understanding. They also ward off the natives of the realm. If the prospective Daughter reaches the gates of the First Garden, they’re tested again, this time by virtue in the form of a burning angel. There is no way to impress the angel with the user’s purity – even those who have used Amorality have the capacity to lose that spell. Passing the gates requires fearless acceptance of one’s own flaws and the conviction that they deserve what lies on the other side regardless. Once inside, they find their mentor waiting for them, and the applicant, thoroughly tested, eats of the fruit and perceives their inner demon for the first time. The other Lilim – some living, some dead – reveal themselves, then, and fete their new sister.

    Organization: Lilim array themselves in the fashion of experienced members (“mothers”) teaching their students. Often they become very close to their charges, either adopting them fully as their children or else becoming something of a third parent for those who already have strong parental bonds.

    Lilim also ally with the Beasts of the Primordial Realm. These Horrors see kindred spirits in the Lilim and treat them as Close Kin instead of distant, suggesting that their progenitor is in fact one and the same with their Dark Mother – or at least a relative. While the Lilim don’t quite know what to think of that suggestion, they too see kinship in the Horrors, the best of whom teach humanity by fear as well as they teach by vice and obsession.

    Theory: The Lilim believe that by becoming astral entities and embedding themselves in the dreams and mythologies of humans, they can help lead them to the Supernal. They see being a demon as a transcendent state, no less valuable to the consciousness of the world than an angel. By seeking union with their inner demon, they no longer fear their vices, assuming they ever did before, and indeed grow through them to become better people.


    Magic

    Ruling Arcanum: Mind

    Yantras: Fruit (+1), trees (+1), a garden (+1), an elaborately cultivated garden (+2), their own personal Garden in the Astral realm (+3), while indulging vices (+2), burning incense (+1), burning passages from restrictive religious texts (+2), breaking taboos (+1)

    Oblations: Indulging their vices; encouraging someone else to indulge their vice; gardening; encouraging someone to try new things; feasting on their Resonance.

    Attainments

    First: Born in Sin
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The Lilim knows the subject better than she knows herself. This attainment mimics the effects of the 'Know Nature' spell. At a glance, a daughter of Lilith may immediately ascertain the subject's Virtue, Vice, and any Aspirations or Obsessions they might have. They also gain a second Vice as this understanding of others leads to a deeper understanding of the self, a realization that no human being is truly so simplistic as to have one deep-seated desire. This is often, but not always a Cardinal Sin.

    Optional: Spirit 1, Empathy 2
    By invoking their mother's history with Spirit, the subject may warp the hearts of those around them. This is similar to the Invoke Bane spell, but rather than affect spirits, it affects other human beings. For the duration of a scene, a person the Lilim designates who isn’t acting in accordance with an Aspiration or Obsession requires spending a point of willpower, as does ignoring one's Vice.


    Second: The Serpent’s Embrace
    Prerequisites: Mind 2
    One may resist temptation. One may avoid it. Short of cutting it out entirely, though, one may never ignore it. Once the Lilim turns her charm on someone, she raises their Impression with her by her Mind dots. Even after the Lilim leaves their presence, some part of her lingers with them – a scent, her smile, the color of her eyes – for a long time afterward.

    Optional: Spirit 2, Empathy 3
    Just as Lilith mastered the expression of her inner spirit, so too do her daughters, who learn to bring it out in others. The Lilim gains a number of ranks in Influence equal to their Spirit, which must be one of their vices or else close to them. Instead of Essence, they use Mana to invoke its use, and roll Gnosis + Spirit when called for.


    Third: Children of the Night
    Prerequisites: Mind 3
    Perhaps the most universal feature of the Lilim in mythology was in tormenting dreams – and, perhaps not coincidentally, succubi and incubi as well – and the modern Daughters of Lilith are no exception. By touching or seeing a sleeping person, they may enter their dreams and become an active part of it, often taking on the form of their demon, though they may disguise themselves normally if they wish. They may also enter the Oneiros of a target if the conditions for astral travel are right. The Lilim are masters of this, and the effect has a Potency of their Mind dots +2, allowing them to enter even the dreams of the strong-willed.

    If the Lilim has achieved the Fourth attainment, she may enter the Oneiros bodily.


    Fourth: The Fruit of Knowledge
    Prerequisites: Mind 4, Spirit 3
    Having grown her legend among the Children of Men to a fantastic stature, Lilith became more than a woman, more than merely divine – she became a legend. Following the footsteps of their mother, the Lilim fully embrace their demon, gaining an honorary Rank equal to their dots in Mind. They may phase into or out of Mind-aspected Twilight at will, and while within become their fully-realized demonic form, shaped by their Vices and their deepest inner nature. If they meditate into the Astral Realms, they may choose to enter either bodily or mentally. They also gain a number of Manifestations in addition to Twilight Form equal to their dots in Spirit, using Mana instead of Essence to channel them; they may choose Reaching, Gauntlet Breach, Gateway (leading to a point of the Astral known by the mage), Image, or Materialize, and may use their Influence or Arcana to create Conditions relevant to themselves.

    Their identification with their demon and their use of Influence begins to change them in accord, if they do not match it – this process is slow, and never results in unnatural features, but may alter things such as eye color, skin texture, or facial structure a course of days or weeks, or height, build, and gender over a course of months.

    On death, they become a full Goetia with half their Gnosis rounded down as their Rank, lose their skills, gain spirit traits appropriate to their Rank, and Numina based on their Praxes and the Attainments of this Legacy, plus additional ones if needed for their Rank. If they have a Garden from the Fifth Attainment, that remains as well, though their material Iris may decay. They lose their Manifestations, though in places like Los Angeles they might gain new ones according to their Rank as other Goetia do.

    Finally, their second Vice becomes their Legend, similar to that of a Beast, and they no longer suffer a penalty to Degeneration checks when pursuing it, or in getting others to do so.


    Fifth: The Gates of Eden
    Prerequisites: Mind 5, Spirit 5
    On reaching the penultimate attainment, the Lilim has mastered everything it is thought that Lilith taught, though rumors persist of a secret Sixth attainment that she must teach directly. Now, she returns to the Primordial Dream and plants her own seed, the seed of her Oneiros, letting it take root and grow into a place all her own, a material haven in the Inner Worlds, sheltered from the Fallen Realm. Though the Lilim call these their “Gardens”, it can take any form appropriate to the Lilim’s own needs and desires, the main portion of which is approximately the size of a large neighborhood or small forest, which the Lilim may extensively detail, similar to the spell Spirit Manse. Beyond that, the Oneiros is its own thing – changeable, based on the mood and memories of the Lilim as normal. The Lilim may meditate directly into her Garden without intervening steps, as it is the permanent location of her Oneiros.

    She may or may not create an Iris that connects to the material world in a place that is Open to her, such as her permanent home or a Demesne she’s created or one where she’s used her Influences to inflict that quality. Creating a new Iris or changing its location costs a point of Mana, a point of Willpower, and meditation for a scene, determining a Key if desired. The Iris may take the form of a flagrant portal or a subtle pathway – for instance, if one walks a certain path in the woods or street alley and meets the requirements, they will find themselves in the Garden.

    While there may be only one material Iris, there may be similar connections to other Astral Realms in the Temenos or Primordial Dream, to other Gardens, to friendly Oneiroi (this always takes the form of a Synese and the user must project themselves through it), or become part of a Beast’s Hive, each costing a point of Mana, a point of Willpower, permission of the owner, and at least one scene of meditation.

    Seraphim of the Tree






    A curious offshoot legacy. Formed by a wayward Obrimos Daughter of Lilith, these so-called “Bright Lilim” are similar to their cousins in most respects, but instead of embracing their inner demon, they embrace what they call their inner Angel, replacing desire with duty. They still embrace a new Vice, but they elevate it, turning it into a sacred Virtue. A Seraph, in mythology, is a serpent angel, and they believe that Lilith became not a Dark Mother, but a Bright one.

    At the First attainment, they replace their optional attainment with a Pierce Deception effect.

    At the Second attainment, they replace their Influence with a Words of Truth effect.

    At the Fourth attainment, they cannot gain Manifestations without using separate Spirit magic. However, their attacks, by hand or with weapons, may benefit from Ephemeral Enchantment, including being able to spend 1 Mana to inflict aggravated damage. Further, their chosen Vice now becomes a Virtue as they internalize its lessons. Their Angel form tend towards radiant, glorious beings.
