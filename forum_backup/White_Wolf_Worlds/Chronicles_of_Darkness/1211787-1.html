                                Thank you for your suggestions, everyone! There's a lot of great ideas here, and I'm honestly not sure what to go for xD

                                For the goetia themselves, I honestly think I'll just fall back on the ol' "astral beings," unless I want to bump up the word-connection and call them "sidereals." But I'll definitely put in "dreamborn" as an alternative term, used by certain traditions and cults.

                                For the fuel, while I like the varieties on common, appropriate words ("awe" and "insight", etc.), I'm afraid it'll get confusing while speaking about it. Most other in-game terms have the benefit of being distinctly different from regular discourse ("essence," ironically, being perhaps the one word that's most likely to be used generally). So, right now, I'm leaning towards "pneuma," both for ease of use and historical context (and something magi could have used for a really long time)

                                Again, thanks to everyone for your suggestions!
