        So when I had those other Alteration ideas I didn't have one for Vaporia. I was interested if some Sirens could have a means of personal flight, besides using a Self-Wind-Travel Verse, so we have both Vaporia and Echidnal versions.

        Swan's Feathers
        A set of feathery wings appear on the Siren's body, such as her back or arms. With them she is able to glide to control her fall damage. Provided she has not be restrained or reached terminal velocity reduce all fall damage to 1 Resistant Bashing. The Siren can gain height under her own power but can only fly for Akashic Record + 1 minutes before she must rest her wings for a half hour. Her flight speed is equal to her land speed +3 (or if she spends a Willpower point, she may use her swim speed as base if it is faster).

        Dragoon's Wings
        Thick membranes form the wings of the Vodinaoi. They spend a point of Willpower to activate their flight for a scene and can fly tirelessly with a flight speed of their land speed +5. However their lithe bodies of hollowed bones are incredibly fragile. Whenever the Vodinaoi takes damage they suffer an additional point of damage of the same type. This includes fall speed if the Vodinaoi's wings were restrained.


        This also raises a question I had. Vodinaoi can only use Echidna's Malformations, correct? But Furies can use Alterations depending on what material their vessel is crafted from. Since a good third to half of the Alterations use Akashic Record in some way, do Furies have an Akashic Record? And given the disparity of Quagmires forming on Earth and Echidna sitting in the depths of the Deluge, no Fury would have a Malformation.

As for the Malformation idea, I'd say that's something Echidna might make use of only within the material realm. Otherwise, most Vodianoi who'd stay within the Celestial Ocean wouldn't use them.
                                        Forgot to write down the inspiration. Many waterfowl, such as swans, ducks, some geese and loons have water repellent feathers. They both swim, dive and fly, although it is true that the wings provide no use underwater. The webbed feet of waterfowl isn't only to paddle on the surface, it provides their main thrust to dive for prey. Quite similarly, a Siren with this alteration would use her wings out of the water and let her tail and other fins propel her as they swim.

                                        I do know the ability to fly is quite OP in a Chronicles system, which is why I tried to temper it with needed limitations. Sirens can only fly for a limited time, Vodinaoi with the Malformation version can fly indefinitely but are much more fragile. It's a delicate balance of pros and cons to give access to such an ability with a decent leash as to the frequency of use. There's also the consideration that for a Siren, given she would be in Diluvian form, would still have to manage how long she can breathe out of the water when using her wings to fly. Or is there still a system for a Siren to partially transform to only use one of their Alterations in their human guise?

                                        Plus, I saw it as a way to bring back some avian features into the Siren game, given the birdlike sirens of early myth. Whereas we now equate Sirens with mermaids and similar water nymphs/aquatic legends.

                                        All that being said, just a minute after I posted the draft I felt like the Alteration and Malformation needed to be revised. Would an extra step in the mechanics (unfurling the wings, as it were) help downplay the benefits of the ability?

                                        Swan's Feathers
                                        With them she is able to glide to control her fall damage. Provided she has not be restrained or reached terminal velocity reduce all fall damage to 1 Resistant Bashing. The Siren can gain height under her own power but can only fly for Akashic Record + 1 minutes before she must rest her wings for a half hour. Her flight speed is equal to her land speed +3. Once per scene she may spend 1 Willpower to activate one of these effects: a) double the length of time she can fly before resting; b) use her maximum swim speed to calculate flight speed.

                                        Dragoon's Wings
                                        Thick membranes form the wings of the Vodinaoi. They spend a point of Willpower to activate their flight for a scene and can fly tirelessly with a flight speed of their land speed +5. However their lithe bodies of hollowed bones are incredibly fragile. Whenever the Vodinaoi takes damage they suffer an additional point of damage of the same type. This includes fall speed if the Vodinaoi's wings were restrained. While swimming, however, the cumbersome wings remove the 10-again quality for underwater movement, unless the Vodinaoi spends 1 Willpower to restore the quality for 1 scene.
