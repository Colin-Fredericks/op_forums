    Sadly they're not up yet. There might be something in the early pages of the thread but I don't think there's more than discussion and outline and my thinkings evolved a bit since then. BTW it's 2 systems (even I'm not crazy enough to do 3 )

    They're called Phronesis & Eunoia. You risk Eroding Pheroneis when you indulge the Beastal Nature. Acts of violence, crimes of passion, etc. You risk Eroding Eunoia when you indulge the Divine Nature. Treating people like things, emotional abuse; wickedness you order your Cult to do defaults to Eunoia.

    There is also a third Trait, Tranquillity. However Tranquillity literally means "lower of Phronesis & Eunoia" and only exists so I can make mechanics that are affected by Tranquillity without having to constantly say "lower of Phronesis & Eunoia".


    Each School also has a favoured way of solving problems. If you solve a problem in the appropriate method you gain some benefit related to Phronesis & Eunoia

    That much is locked down.


    Currently the Schools doc says that the Benefit is a chance to regain Phronesis & Eunoia and that will probably stay. Originally I was thinking that if a Leviathan was going to do something evil they should do it in their School's way to reduce the risks of Erosion or turn it into more of a gamble where it could go up or down.

    But now I'm thinking that maybe I should nudge the mechanics away from that, rather than letting Schools help prevent Erosion they become exclusively a way to gain additional dots with the idea being that when a Leviathan is free to pursue their School they can rise in Phronesis & Eunoia but when a crisis hits they're likely to go down. This fits the themes of Stagnation with a Leviathan going through cycles of building up only to loose it all. (It could be worse, at least they're not Vampires who generally just go down and stay there)

    Maybe what I'll do is split the difference. School lets you roll to increase Phronesis & Eunoia when you solve a problem in the appropriate way. If you also did something worthy of Erosion you'd combine the two rolls with a success adding a dot and a failure removing a dot; but you'd use dicepool appropriate dicepool in the Erosion Chart - which is lower than the base dicepool for increasing Phronesis & Eunoia through your school.

        Good news. Barring the odd picture or stereotype the Schools are now complete! Here's a link.

        The Schools are probably the single most important section in Leviathan as they answer the two questions that every single RPG must give clear answers to: What do players do and why do players form a team?

        The seven schools and what they do are
        • Abyss - Explore the oceans, rift, or something else.
        • Clay - Try to fit into human society and study humanity.
        • Fog - Try to unify the Tribe.
        • Moon - Recreate the Tribe's lost way of life.
        • Sand - Try to solve the Tribe's problems through technology.
        • Sun - Acquire more power, wealth and influence.
        • Reef - Enhance their powers, fight monsters even worse then themselves.


        So if you've asked earlier for Leviathans to get embrace who they are (Sun) or fight evil (Reef) now is the time to see how that works.

                                                        Funny you asked that, I came up with the answer a just two days ago.

                                                        Heirlooms are basically ordinary objects once owned by a Leviathan. If you take one into the Rift you can find any memory where said object is contained. For example a Heirloom pen might lead you to memories of it's origonal owner having a conversation in his office with his pen on the desk. The pen isn't important, but it's in the memory.

                                                        Mechanically Heirlooms will probably let you trade in a dot of Heirloom for the Informed Condition (and your exp back of course) - you gain advantages to current problems by watching a Leviathan who solved a similar problem. In short, they play to the mood of stagnation. Same problems, same solutions. They'd also be a good way to learn Rituals.

                                                        The dots being traded in don't represent the Heirloom loosing power, but like a book you've already read twice there's not much more to learn from it.


                                                        From a Mummy Perspective a Heirlooms actually is a Vestige. Not just similar, they're the exact same thing. But being part-god of fecundity Leviathans produce enormous amount of life energy. If a Vestige is a Sekhem alkaline battery, a Heirloom is like a lump of plutonium. Dangerous, unstable, and ridiculously powerful. The wise Arisen sends it to the Judges quickly because they can easily and safely handle it. The Arisen who likes to take risks might try and use it themself. It helps that a Heirloom can't match a Mummy's Virtue/Vice as Leviathans obviously have no Virtue/Vice trait.

                                                        The Mummy angle was me noticing that 1E Heirlooms looked very similar to Vestiges and then going from there to obvious questions like "given the established lore, can I guess how much Sekhem would a Leviathan produce". The fact this provides a big incentive for Mummies to try and steal a Leviathan's Heirloom (or the reverse) is just the icing on the cake.
