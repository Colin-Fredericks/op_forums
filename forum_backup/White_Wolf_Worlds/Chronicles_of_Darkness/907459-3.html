A "cruel" True Fae can provide a "nice" experience (I had a ST that had a rather mean True Fae with an adamant Loyalist follower because the Durance had helped the Loyalist kick their drug addiction and find new purpose), and a "nice" True Fae can provide a "cruel" experience (a lot of the Fairest Durances are written this way in the books).

                                            The thing that gets my goat is that there are a fair number of the complaints which are based on a misunderstanding of the game. The folks that do that are often inventing a problem for themselves that isn't actually there because they're going off of their own half-remembered reading paired with the latest brilliant observation about Chronicles of Darkness out of 4chan.

                                            EDIT: I should clarify, I'm not angry because "they're getting it wrong!" but because their issue is based on a misunderstanding, I just want to help them resolve that understanding so they can decide if they're cool with things or not as a person fully informed on whatever they're thinking about. If you don't like Vampire: The Requiem because you didn't read it that closely the first time and someone on another website said something incorrect about it, I'd rather show you how that's not the case so you can decide, fully informed, whether you like it or not. At that point, do as you will.

                                            Though I feel that's a very different kind of disagreement from the one where people are saying that they, say, don't want Prometheans to become human.
