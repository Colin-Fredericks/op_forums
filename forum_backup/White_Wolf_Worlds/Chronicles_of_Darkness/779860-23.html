        Also, I had an idea for a possible Chorus.

        The Freeshooters are a gathering of Sirens in the Dark Wisdom Current who've acknowledged the usability of the Spirit world and the Denizens who live there. By using special Verses (Area-Spirit-Sense), Sirens are able to begin touching and seeing the invisible realm....but few are willing to deal with the selfish beings who live there. The Sirens in this Chorus however tend to deal with them more than any other Siren. The method of their power is deceptively simple. A Siren must first appease the spirit and get it's attention, after which she must do what she can to fill it's Essence pool for one day. If she completes this, then she gains the Fettered Condition [GMC 227] as the Spirit is now fettered to the Siren for the duration of the pact. The Siren is now free to ask any favors of the Spirit equal to her Akashic Record for whatever she desires so long as it's within the Spirit's capabilities. This includes asking it to use Numina and Influences, but they may not ask the Spirit to violate it's own Ban. However, one of these favors the Spirit may twist and use however or whenever the Spirit desires, usually when it would benefit best and almost always at a cost to the Siren who invoked the deal. Upon using up all favors, the Fettered Condition becomes automatically resolved and the Spirit is free to go on it's way.

        This Chorus is inspired ofc by the Der Freischutz opera (basically "The Freeshooter" in German) where a man made a deal with the devil for seven magical bullets that would hit wherever he desired, but the last one would be cursed by the devil and hit where he desired it to hit.

                                                    Eugh. I feel as if I haven't checked in for a while.

                                                    I like the idea of Sirens having their own item-subtype. It's also interesting that the Treasures are effectively magic wands from Dungeons & Dragons, which I don't believe has been approached yet in any splat. I do feel like there should also be a Treasure subtype known as Trinkets or something similar that offer a less substantial power. A possible Trinket could be a music box that always plays a different tune when opened and offers a point of Pneuma to the Siren that first opens it during that lunar rotation.

                                                    For the backstory, I think the main issue is that we keep coming up with good ideas that are muddling the main structure of the narrative. We had a basic frame with Ceto and Echidna being opposing sides of the same coin fighting against this cosmic force known as The Deluge. Then we added in the Furies, the Breathless, and the Banshees that operate on their own agenda, which is fine and helped create this sort of network of alliances. And then we added in the idea of Behemoth, Leviathan, and Ziz which needed to be worked into the narrative in some way, shape, or form. And then we have the Sirens, who were once human but discovered their nature due to a mystical sensation of Drowning allowing them to awaken their powers. And then there's the Vodianoi, who are once-human creations of Echidna. And then there's the Flensers who are hunting down the Sirens for their longevity-increasing flesh.

                                                    -----

                                                    I think we need to approach the main concept behind the backstory to Siren, namely Ceto, Echidna, and The Deluge. Without the names that relate to a culture, Ceto effectively becomes the Mother of Sirens while Echidna becomes the Mother of the Vodianoi. They are the Open Palm and the Closed Fist who seek to protect humanity from one great force: The Deluge.

                                                    A) Originally, The Deluge was a major "The End" force that wiped out humanity several decades in the future, which awakened Ceto and Echidna and inspired them to travel back in time to turn others into Sirens in order to prevent this Deluge from occurring.

                                                    B) This was changed to avoid comparisons to the Armageddon that was Gehenna in WoD (the original World of Darkness), so it became a reoccuring "Reset" button that reverted humanity and civilization back to its root components. Isolated cases were Floodings. We also brought up the idea of the Furies, who acted alongside the Floodings as a means to take revenge on humanity for their pride. This ended up creating an unnamed being that I'm going to call "Gaia" for brevity's sake. The Deluge was Gaia's attempt to reset the world to a point where she could recover all the damage that was done to the world and start anew, effectively giving humanity a second chance. There is no saying as to how many times The Deluge succeeded, but it is implied that this is not the first time that the world was flooded.

                                                    C) This third iteration of the backstory involves three beings: Behemoth, Leviathan, and Ziz. This one plays up the ideas of factions and war by having Leviathan rule over the sea and having Behemoth rule over the land while Ziz is the divine arbitrator who encourages the world to exist in balance. This also introduces the idea that there is a backstory to all humanity, as Leviathan created humanity while Behemoth shaped them. As well, this also introduces a reason as to why the first Sirens came to be in the world as they were humans that allied themselves to Ceto and were changed to become Sirens. The war between Behemoth and Leviathan explains The Deluge as something that is not due to "Gaia" but due to the death of Leviathan causing the first destructive waves.

                                                    The third iteration is the most developed and offers great explanations as to why and what things are in the Siren universe. However, it feels as if it steps on the toes of some of the other gamelines by establishing several ideas. It also is a bit mythical, as in it's established as being so far in the past that most of it could be embellishments and exaggerations made to fill in missing information by Sirens as the ages went by.

                                                    Of things that are clearly Things (as in Sirens are 100% positive that these things exist through experience): The Sirens that exist now were awakened from mortal humans who had no knowledge of what Sirens were. They became who they are now because of The Song, a life-changing melody that helped a Siren Surface while she was Drowning. The "Ceto" being created Sirens in order to prevent the devastation that is The Deluge and the Floodings that occur in its wake. The "Echidna" being is also attempting to prevent the devastation of The Deluge by creating Vodianoi off of the humans. The Sirens are "supposed" to be on the side of humanity, so they are to act as protectorates while figuring out ways to prevent the Floodings. There are also the Quagmires, areas that seek to harm humanity, similar to the Floodings. The Quagmires may cause harm themselves or they may alter the surrounding wildlife to become Furies, creatures that seek out humanity and desire to harm them. There are also the Banshees: Sirens that ended up "drowning" before they Surfaced as Sirens. The Banshees are starved of Pneuma and seek out humans in order to steal their lifeforce in order to fill the void that exists within themselves. The Breathless are Sirens that Surfaced without hearing The Song, preventing them from becoming Sirens. They become voids to the Sirens, preventing them from using Verses and removing themselves from a Siren's ability to sense The Song within others. And then there are the Flensers, hunters that seek to gain longevity by harvesting the body parts of Sirens and consuming them.

                                                    Of things that might be Things (as in Sirens would know this through word of mouth, possibly from Ceto or Echidna's mouth): The story of Behemoth, Leviathan, and Ziz as well as the creation of humanity and Sirens. The story behind The Deluge's existence and the possibility of there being a "Gaia" being who created Quagmires and Floodings. The possibility that The Deluge may have happened before and wiped out all of civilization once before. The possibility of Sirens becoming Ascendants and becoming one with the sky.

                                                    -----

                                                    I think that the backstory of Siren would benefit by giving Sirens just enough for them to be okay with being a Siren and knowing what a Siren's duty is; as long as we can justify why a Siren exists, that should be enough to suspend disbelief and allow us some wiggle-room in terms of actual lore. Like with Changelings. Changlings know that they were human and they know that they were changed because of their time in Arcadia with the True Fae. Or with Sin-Eaters, who know that they were supposed to die but ended up making a deal with a Geist and surviving. Or with Beasts, who had reoccurring nightmares about monsters before accepting the monster as themselves.

                                                    Sirens, when they were mortals, we bogged down by emotional baggage or terrible circumstances, feeling as if they were being held down underwater. Eventually, this process became too overwhelming and the mortal ended up feeling as if they were Drowning, dragged into the undertow by grasping hands and forced to struggle. Then, they heard The Song which filled them up with the will to live and allowed them to Surface. This has all been said before but the big change is that the mortal, when they metaphorically Surface, finds themselves brought into The Deluge (I think we renamed the Realm as something else, didn't we? Isn't it something like the Celestial Ocean now?) where their body has changed into their Diluvian Form and they are allowed a glimpse into the world of the Sirens. They may even hear the voice of Ceto or Echidna calling out to them. Afterwards, they are awakened from their vision and are brought back to the material realm. They are different, changed. They may attempt to push off that episode as just a hallucination, but they know something is different about themselves. They are even more certain when they realize that they can sense others who have gone through the same process (the ability to sense other Sirens through their Akashic Record stat, that is also still a Thing, right?).

                                                    From there, a Siren begins to learn about Ceto, Echidna, The Deluge, and their role in the world as a Siren (the things that are clearly Things). As for things that might be Things, Sirens may come across possible theories as to why and how they came to be. One such theory points to a trinity of creatures: Behemoth, Leviathan, and Ziz, that helped create the world and humanity. A war between two of the creatures ended up causing The Deluge. Another points to a sister-being to Ceto, a "Gaia" being who has become resentful of humanity and seeks to reset her once-beautiful world so that she can start again. Some Sirens may find themselves speaking to a hybrid-being like themselves who speak of Luna and a Father Wolf, comparing their Luna to Ceto. Other Sirens may find creatures of dreams and hunger talking about their Dark Mother and making comparisons to Echidna. There may be right answers; there may be wrong answers. Sirens may never know what the truth is but it doesn't keep them from trying to search for the answer.

                                                    tl;dr: Ceto and Echidna are real while everything else is purposefully vague for the purpose of storytelling.

                                                            Sweet awesomeness. We've got most of the character creation up to speed.

                                                            So, Tracing. We don't really have any concrete rules for that aside from interpretation, which is sorta a safety net during this playtesting period. As for what we have right now, Sirens can spend a point of Pneuma to ask a question and begin Tracing, which effectively creates a vision of what is being Traced and runs through it as if it were a cassette tape that can be rewound and played at any time. Each Current affects the Tracing by allowing a Siren of that Current to focus on certain details or sift through anything that is making the scene unclear.

                                                            The Dice Pool that Yads Dom came up with is Int + Investigation, so let's say that Int + Investigation is the base Dice Pool for Tracing. How about this: when a Siren asks her question and begins Tracing, she effectively creates a 3D snapshot of the scene at a moment that would offer some answer to her question. For each success, the Siren can refine this snapshot by adding a timeline to it, expanding the overall area the snapshot holds, focusing on subjects within this snapshot, or eliminating extraneous noise that clogs up the Trace. Each Current has their own specialty that effectively gives the Siren a free point in one of these refinements, so some Sirens are better at Tracing certain scenes.

                                                            Let's say that the initial parameters for a Trace goes as follows: The Time of the Trace is suspended, meaning that it is a single moment or frame in time. The Area of the Trace is roughly a room if indoors or a distinct stretch of land (such as a street's length between two other streets) if outdoors. The Focus of the Trace is blurry unless the Siren that is Tracing already knows pertinent facts about the scene such as the individuals within the scene or the rough time. The Noise of the Trace varies between scenes depending on the number of individuals, the quality of the weather, etc; it is the extra details that exist within the scene that do not serve to answer the question that the Siren asked. Sirens may spend a success on their Tracing roll to increase these parameters by an amount.

                                                            Say that Morgan, a Polarian of Wisdom, wants to find out what happened the moment before a shooting occurred. He is on the site of the shooting and asks the question "How did this all start?" He begins Tracing and envisions the scene: he sees the victim under the streetlight as a blurry figure walks out from an alleyway and points a gun at them. The vision is dark as midnight and is utterly quiet, something that has been assumed from what the police report stated. As a Polarian of Wisdom, Morgan is able to pull out the details from a scene and chooses to focus on the shooter. The blurry figure begins to take shape within the scene, becoming a woman, roughly in her late 30s, wielding a silenced pistol in her hands. She's wearing rather plain clothes, a shirt that has seen its fair share of better days and jeans that have been worn down into fraying threads. Disposable and unassuming. That is as much as Morgan's Current bonus will give him so he begins his roll. His Intelligence is 3 and his Investigation is 2, so he rolls 5 dice and ends up with 2 successes. He decides that, while he knows what the shooter looks like, he doesn't know how it began. He spends one of his successes to expand the timeline. The vision begins to animate, playing through roughly 15 minutes worth of time. Within that amount of time, he sees the victim on his phone, talking about waiting to be picked up. Morgan also sees the shooter on their phone during that time, listening intently without response. As the victim gets off their phone, they turn towards the victim and becomes shocked, as if they recognized the shooter. The gun fires and the victim goes down. The shooter leaves the scene through the alleyway and disappears into the void. Morgan decides to spend his last success to expand the area of the Trace, extending it from the one street that leads into alleyways to a rough, block-sized area. The scene plays as usual but Morgan decides to focus on the alleyway that the shooter came from. Because the vision is represented as a 3-dimensional snapshot in his mind, Morgan walks his consciousness down the alleyway and discovers a car parked on the other side of the buildings that separate the street. He hears the gunshot and sees the shooter running in his direction. The car door opens to reveal a shadowy figure at the wheel alongside a passenger. The shooter jumps in and the car speeds off. Morgan is unable to distinctly recognize the license plate or the make of the car, only recognizing the number of people and the general size and color of the car. Reviewing the scene, Morgan decides that he has as much information as he can muster and releases the Trace.

                                                            Sirens are also able to share their Traces with other Sirens. The number of extra Sirens that can view a Trace is equal to the Tracing Siren's Akashic Records. Any additional Siren in the Trace can use their Current bonus to refine the Trace but only the Tracing Siren may spend successes to refine the Trace.
