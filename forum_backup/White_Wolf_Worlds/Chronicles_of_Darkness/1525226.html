          Short answer: "The Sixties"

          Long answer: John Lennon started The Quarrymen in 1956, then Paul and George joined and Stuart Sutcliffe and Pete Best were kicked to the curb, but not before Stuart suggested changing their name to "The Beetles" as a tribute to Buddy Holly and the Crickets. They had their big breakout on The Ed Sullivan Show in '64, spent the next several years touring the world and getting unimaginably famous, stopped touring in '66 because they couldn't hear themselves over the roar of the crowds, then broke up acrimoniously in 1970 when Paul left, but the group didn't legally dissolve until 29 December 1974.

              I have two Legacies that would be into the science fiction of the time ("plausible" psionics and such). It is a rough draft since I have not worked on them in a while:

              The Geniuses is inspired by Dune lots of Free Council vibes, but would be at home in the Mysterium (Savants would LOVE the power set):


              Geniuses
              Parentage: Acanthus, Free Council or Mysterium
              Ruling Arcanum: Mind
              Yantras: Sources of knowledge (books, informants, etc.) as sacraments (Mage, p. 122); verbally explaining an occult or scientific phenomenon to an ignorant party (+1); planning out strategies and tactics to a goal (+1).
              Oblations: Engaging in unusual research; teaching or tutoring; pursuing an obsessive or antisocial habit.

              Initiate (Savantism)
              Perfect Recall - Instant Casting
              Momentary Flux - Instant Casting

              Apprentice (Multitasker)
              One Mind, Two Thoughts - Advanced Duration, "Instant Tasks"
              Constant Presence - Advanced Duration

              Disciple (Extraordinary Defense)
              Mental Shield - Advanced Duration, "Goetia Attacks"
              Shield of Chronos - Advanced Duration, "Fabricate Timeline"

              Adept (Inner Potential)
              Augment Mind - Instant Casting, Advanced Duration
              Shifting Sands - Instant Casting, "Scene Return"

              Master (External Support)
              Social Networking - Advanced Duration
              Acceleration - Advanced Duration, "Dodge Actions", "Extended Actions"; Time in a Bottle​​





              Posthumans are more into some Asimov style psionics, but still grounded in natural science.


              Posthumans
              Parentage: Mastigos, Free Council or Silver Ladder
              Ruling Arcanum: Life
              Yantras: Deliberately revealing extraordinary physical or mental capabilities (+1, +2 if superhuman abilities are shown); planning out strategies and tactics to a goal (+1).
              Oblations: Passionate sex, performing an exercise routine.​

              Initiate (Preparation)
              Analyze Life - Instant Casting
              Know Nature - Instant Casting​

              Apprentice (Full Awareness)
              Heightened Senses - Advanced Duration, "Scent Tracking"
              One Mind, Two Thoughts - Advanced Duration, "Instant Tasks"​

              Disciple (Healthy Body, Healthy Mind)
              Body Control - Advanced Duration, "1/0 Armor"
              Mental Shield - Advanced Duration, "Goetia Attacks"

              Adept (Inner Potential)
              Honing the Form - Instant Casting, Advanced Duration
              Augment Mind - Instant Casting, Advanced Duration

              Master (New Allies)
              Create Life - Advanced Duration​​
              Social Networking - Advanced Duration​
              ​



              Feel free to use, expand and/or modify.

                                          'Project Nightfall
                                          Mages move through the Fallen World with delicate steps. Most mages take pains to hide their Awakened natures from Sleepers. Guardians of the Veil are some of the greatest exponents and enforcers of this policy. The Labyrinth exists to distract the unworthy, so that Awakened truth doesn’t fall right into their laps. This keeps the Diamond Wheel pure so that the wisest souls can direct its turn toward the world’s liberation. Sometime in the late 1960s, an all-Guardian cabal in Washington, DC, decided it had learned the arts of misdirection all too well, but were tired of waiting for the Hieromagus, Atlantis’ return and all of the other idealistic, distant goals of the order. They were in the right time and place to have a much more immediate effect on the world. They had occult power and a finger on the pulses of American and Soviet espionage. In short, they wanted a piece of the action for themselves.(...)
                                          By late 1972, Project Nightfall enjoyed a salary pilfered from various “black book” discretionary funds and was thought to be one of several informal groups US intelligence turned to when the intelligence community needed to learn more about strange phenomena. At the same time, the cabal used its Arts and cunning to direct the members’ own operations. Project Nightfall became a name to mention in select Washington offi ces; cabal members spread half-truths about UFOs and remote viewing while they meddled in affairs from Southeast Asia to Moscow. Sleeper agents stole magical artifacts and harassed cabal enemies while thinking they were fighting communism or repatriating stolen property.'
