            I was thinking (with the other thread about the truth of Father Wolf), about Sumer and the city of Bau. Is it Canon? I like to think so. Perhaps not the part of the modern ziggurat, that's more of a plot that might happen in someone's game.

            After the short bit of Broken Clans and Covenants in Blood & Smoke, I would love to see something like that in Idigam Chronicles. Perhaps lost tribes? Some Forsaken societies like Bau, that rose and fell during the ages?

            Werewolf's history comes from oral tradition, I think. So names get lost in time. Forsaken might have been different (like in Bau) during the ages, but that doesn't translate in oral tradition ("We were different back then, stronger" - gives room to lots of interpretations instead of "Dude, we walked in Gauru like all the fuckin' time!"). Who knows, maybe a lot of what we know happened, happened in a loooonger time frame (The schism between Forsaken and Pure only happened a 1000 years later, for instance).

            Maybe we get hints of history in Idigam Chronicles. Specially more recently (Heck, the return of the Idigam is not a long time ago!). Perhaps a book later down the line with Forsaken in different eras, like Ancient Mythologies?

            Anyway, I would love to see more about the Forsaken's history. Something playable, something fresh.
