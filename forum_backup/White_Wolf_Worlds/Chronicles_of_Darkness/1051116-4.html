                                                Promethean was the game I was first introduced to and I think it captures both the beauty and horror of Chronicles of Darkness magnificently. Being a Promethean is simultaneously the shittiest thing to be in CofD and by far the most hopeful. It takes weird science and blends it seamlessly with classic horror and gothic fantasy.

                                                When you say "stamping it on a preexisting character" do you mean mechanically, or a mortal character becoming supernatural over the course of a story? If the latter, then yes, Promethean is not a good fit. If the former though, there is no reason you can take a character with the concept of mob flunkie, waitress, or hacker and apply the Promethean template to make a Promethean who was created to be a mob flunkie, waitress, or hacker. The only splat you can't do that for is Mummy.

                                                    Throwing my vote in for Vampire as the first "monster" line, since the mechanics are relatively easy and clear and it stands very well as a one-monster-type game. Plus, the core doesn't carry as much setting "baggage" as the others, as it focuses on the mortal world and doesn't introduce too much more outside that. Although it should be noted that its 2e core wasn't technically a "2e core" when it first came out, so some of it may or may not seem a tiny bit messy or unclear to a newcomer... but aside from that, yeah, good first pick.

                                                    Werewolf, Beast, and Promethean would be solid second pick-ups though, in all fairness. They're also mostly simple mechanically and are well-defined, but they each have their own issues. Werewolf adds a number of new creatures, leans hard on the Shadow and spirits, and the multiple forms for every PC could potentially be overwhelming to new players; Beast was explicitly designed with crossover in mind rather than just shrugging at the idea, so having at least one other monster type under your belt first could be a big help; and Promethean approaches things from the "monster into human" angle and requires very clear communication between the storyteller and players by virtue of the stories it wants to tell.

                                                            My Vote is For Demon:The Descent as your next read through for a few reasons:
                                                            1. It is by far my favorite book (love the splats, love the theses, love the antagonists, love the ST potential) and so would LOVE to see your thoughts as you read it.
                                                            2. You seem to be intrigued by The God-Machine. The Demon book might give you more to be intrigued about along those lines.
                                                            3. Other than humans, Demon seems to be the most companion to the Core 2nd edition book. I'm not fully certain but both seemed to be written with the other in mind.

                                                            As for other considerations for your next read?
                                                            - Vampire: The Requiem because it was the Flagship gameline and prolly the most simple to understand/readthrough themes wise
                                                            - Mage: The Awakening because it takes the CofD setting and adds complexity. I find this the hardest to understand but that's kind of the appeal so if that is what intrigues you, choose Mage.
                                                            - Changeling: The Lost because it is one of the bleakest of the CofD settings. I don't think it's quite out yet (the 2nd ed). Read this if you are looking to read one with a very different "flair" or atmosphere to it.
                                                            - Hunter: The Vigil because it takes a lot of the human themes from the core and adds Hunters to the mix. It's like a continuation of a Human character sheet. If that is what you're looking for go with Hunter.
