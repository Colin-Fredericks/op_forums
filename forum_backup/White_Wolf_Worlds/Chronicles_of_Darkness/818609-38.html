                    No any propositions just little bit of thinking what I would personally desire from Genius Integrity 2.0:
                    •Encourage Blue-Orange/"alien -morality. While it would be good idea to keep mad scientists from becoming just conspiracy of serial killers, I think Geniuses should be encouraged to be Heels to stay sane, not just Faces.
                    •Create paradoxical interaction with Hunters. There should be something to reward Genius for joining and supporting Hunters, but if Hunters get sniff oft their resident Genius being more than just a eccentric geek they instantly jump to treat them like a Slasher. This may not mean end of cooperation, since some Hunter cells are known to give Slashers second chances, but Genius&Hunter interaction could come in three flavors: Closeted Supernatural Ally, Stabbed in the Back with Torches and Pitchforks, and/or Not Quite Beholden and their Not Quite Slasher.
                    •Encourage Geniuses to stay away from The Lost. I really enjoy idea of Changelings being completely terrified by Inspired, while Geniuses risking breaking point from being compared to True Fae. However to add the sadistic choice Geniuses may also have a strong urge to help to protect the Lost (possibly encouraged mechanically with a stick).
                    •Make Illuminated scary not as threat to health but as extreme hazarrads to sanity. Just reading their notes or listening to babbling of their victims could mean risk of becoming like them. This could be expanded to all 0 Integrity monsters for Geniuses.

                    Meanwhile here is another possible interpritations what the number could mean: How much "life" Genius has beyond their big Idea. Integrity 10 Genius can go on for days without even thinking about their grand scheme to throw over the Government for giving their grandchild cancer, while Madcap with measly 1 spends even their time sleeping to dream big about their nearly immortality formula.
                    The difference between Illuminated and Genius becomes apparent: Genius is but a mortal obsessed (or possessed?) by an Idea, Inspired and driven by it, even if sometimes unable to express or contain it. Illuminated are Ideas wearing mortals like diving suits. Illuminated remember lives of their hosts, but are completely unable to relate to them or rest of humanity. Some may even find idea of other Illuminated completely repulsive.
