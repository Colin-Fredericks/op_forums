                                Promethean!

                                Let's compare Prometheans to spirit beasts. They both undergo alchemical transformations, gaining more understanding and power, all on the way towards an improved state of being. They intuit information they shouldn't have access to, be it through Azothic Memory or the Dao. On their path, they face Firestorms and Heavenly Tribulations, all the time being threatened by Alchemists and Cultivators wanting to exploit their bodies for their own benefit. Now, their end-state could be considerably different, but other than that, you could switch them around between their respective settings and still have both genres work. In fact, you could draft a Chinese Dark Era with both Prometheans and spirit beasts being tied through Divine fire, striving to attain humanity and maybe... maybe move beyond it. The biggest challenge would be to reconcile their differing themes. A fictional world could criticize Xianxia's disrespect towards baseline human experience, but also deconstruct Promethean's rejection of trans-humanism, and create a harmonious whole.

                                Mummy!

                                The Heavens would have a much different flavor to them, if they're replaced by Duat. It is as if Heavens and Hell were combined into a new institution. Something more hands-on, legalistic, righteous, insane and cruel. At first, it would be easier to grow in power by working with Duat, rather than challenging it - as a member of a Cult. At least until a member desires more than they can get, which leads to their sudden but inevitable betrayal. Some Judges would be inclined to open paths of Cultivation (like Nekhenku or Arem-Abfu), but also punish following them wrong (Arem-Abfu), or even embarking upon them (Usekh-Nemtet). Sorcery, intricate but mysterious metaphysics and soul-surgery are already part of the setting, just with Egyptian, rather than east-Asian flair. Consider Cultivators debating intricacies of five Pillars rather than Wuxing, and figuring out which mention of Chi refers to Ka, specifically, and which to Sekhem as a whole. You can get an eclectic fusion of the two cultures, like the Four Guardian Beasts being used as protectors of canopic jars, or Ox-Head and Horse-Head fighting with Fiends over who gets to guard Duat's gates. Also, Immortals are a valid type of PC/NPC in Mummy, and advanced Cultivators could join that "elite", looking down smugly on Blood-Bathers and Body-Snatchers they rub shoulders with.

                                    I love many of these ideas.

                                    My personal pick: Humans and Hunters!
                                    No, seriously. If I wanted Xianxia concepts with a ChroD Game, and not make a new Splat or minor Template, I'd go with Humans/Hunters (that, or Prometheans due to the already mentioned focus on philosophical understanding and insight, e.g. as Teatime mentioned above). I'd stack up on the martial fighting styles for fluff, and mecanically change compacts and conspiracies to sects and clans, and would change who can access which etc., but many concepts are already there and only need some fluffing/ tweaking like making fighting style and fighting merits half price or something like that, to have more of them thrown around (also, with Martial Arts 5 Touch of Death, Brute Force 1 Falling Pillar and Relentless Assault 2 Eye of the Tiger. you can use All-out attacks at no loss of Defense against a single target, dealing 2L damage with bare hands and 8-again - all for 8/4 xp).
                                    One reason is that humans and hunters are the ones that most capture the "starting out" feeling of these types of novels and manhua, and I personally usually prefer the first few arcs over the middle or endgame. More tension, more potential for growth, less overload of pseudophilosophical Dao-babble bullshido ("His blade is so powerful because it captures the essence of sharpness but withholds the cut!!!11eleven" - don't get me wrong, some do this well, abd I love it when they do, but then, the words mean something.) etc.
                                    But specifically, groups like Ascending Ones (slightly tweaked for this; Orthodox/'Righteous') and Cheiron (Unorthodox) already hunt down Monsters to protect people/to use parts of them to empower themselves to hunt down more to get more parts to hunt down more... like cultivators and spirit beasts /spirit beast tides. Ascending Ones then would be one of the few rare righteous sects where Cultivators actually care about people and use the monster parts in their alchemy Elixiers, while Cheiron are heretical monster bloodline cultivators with very unorthodox but effective body tempering and grafting procedures.
                                    Hunt Club and Bear Lodge already fit the 'just wanna fight the strong, it's fun' mold. Throw in some Cainite Heresy Hunters are already tainted with dark powers they use for good (or keep them seperate as a tragic demonic-blooded but otherwise orthodox clan that uses their evil blood magics for good).
                                    Just fudge a few things around, and you have the trappings of a sect of an unorthodox sect that uses a forbidden form of body tempering and blood magics to rise and fight - to borrow from Murim manwha, a Divine Demon/Blood Cult type of sect.
                                    With this, you can play up the 'ancient cultivators fighting demonic beasts and have sect wars' angle, or go for the modern variety.

                                    As the game goes on, I'd have them slowly turn into a Splat as a major breakthrough, 'fortuitous encounter' or specific cultivation method ("Drinking Blood is part of our cultivation art; we circulate the blood Qi; Side effects are being susceptible to all forms of Yin energy, so keep out of the sun", "This Cultivation Technique replaces parts of your souls with a Ghost/Spirit; here's how you circulate from now on: you punch people when they are down, literally and metaphorically"), usually at the Realm at which one would be considered a "intermediate Realm cultivator".
