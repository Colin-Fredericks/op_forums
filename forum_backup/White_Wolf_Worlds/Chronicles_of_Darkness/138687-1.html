                        I think I have an idea.

                        Fireflashes!


                        Create a bunch of Conditions and/or drawbacks that a player can voluntarily add to their power for extra dice and/or successes. These effects should be power-of-god things.

                        For example if a Promethian uses Arc, they could say that after hurting the target the lightning bolt jumps to a nearby corpse or inanimate object; bestowing life upon it for a scene. Pandora's book has a section on Firestorm aspects that could be adapted for Fireflashes. I'm sure the Bible will also have some neat possibilities.


                        Also, while I'm here. Here's a freebie.

                        Shock

                        Minor: The Promethean may charge his body with lightning, frying his opponents with a touch. By spending a point of Pyros the Promethian's next attack with his fists counts as though it were a weapon which does one damage. If the Promethean prefers he channel the electricity through a conductive weapon, increasing it's damage rating by one. All weapons enhanced by this Transmutation take (the Transmuation's damage - the weapon's Durability) point of damage to their structure each turn.

                        All damage inflicted by electricity bypasses armour, unless it has the ability to protect against electricity. (N.B, with a few word changes it should work fine for Irradiation's attack power - just don't call it Blast)

                        Major: The Promethean may now choose between a damage rating of one or three when using Shock. She may also use Shock at range, literally shooting lightning bolts from her body. This counts as a ranged weapon with a range of Azoth * 10 feet, a medium range of Azoth * 20 feet, and a long range of Azoth * 40 feet.

                        In addition the Promethian gains the ability to lace their Shock based attacks with the essence of Torment. If they elect to do so then their electricity will bypass a Promethean's natural ability to heal from electricity, but not the protection granted by Insulator. Calling upon the power of Torment causes it to build within the Promethian, if she does this more than Humanity times in a single scene it becomes unbearable; she yearns for, and must roll to prevent, Release.

                        Superlative: The Promethian may now choose between a damage rating of one, three of five when using Shock, and by spending a point of Willpower she may make the damage Aggravated. In addition, by spending an additional point of Pyros he may use the autofire rules with Shock based attacks. If he uses a conductive weapon to channel a melee attack, then the weapon's damage rating only applies to the first target.

                                                    Though I know where you're coming from, I'd remove mythological references from the masthead like that.
                                                    Reasons:
                                                    1. Those thing's King's Raven said.

                                                    2. We can go absolutely insane with Alchemical, Planetary and Scientific references, too; we can build a system around all that, but that adds a layer of complexity that I don't think it behooves us to consider until we actually have solid system (read: one that will work) down.
                                                    We can get more mileage out of Qaballah, too.

                                                    3. We would have to do this for every Transmutation set. I love mythology; I know a lot of mythology (like, a lot). The idea of shoe-horning in mythological references does not appeal to me.

                                                    4. What does this add, that simply using already-extant Transmutations does not?
                                                    Here's what I mean:
                                                    What does Chaac's Wrath (nice call-out, by the way) add to the game as a name-set for powers that simply having it say "Divine Lightning" doesn't already do?

                                                    Honestly, I'm still partial to having it say "Electrification Transmutations" and then just, naming each one after the top of the tree. Or naming each one based on what it does.



                                                    My reservations about this system still exist. They are:
                                                    1. "Blackout" isn't on there.
                                                    2. How do we work in 1-5 dot?

                                                    My solutions:
                                                    1. "Blackout" and others like it have Minor/Major Transmutations as prerequisites, from separate trees; Blackout would require "Insulator" and "Jolt" and probably be Major, for instance.
                                                    2. We have a second sub-type of Transmutation called "Alterations" that would be for stuff like Might, Perfected Reflexes, Hard Body, Alembic, and so on.

                                                    Mind you, I like these solutions, especially since they work together - you can have standard Mi --> Ma --> Su Transmutations be prerequisites alongside Alterations for those in-between or hard-to-define ones.
                                                    Also, it still makes Synergies easier to make.

                                                    Thoughts?
