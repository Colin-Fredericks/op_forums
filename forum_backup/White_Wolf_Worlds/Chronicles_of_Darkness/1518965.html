    Nagual: The Ignited
    A Storytelling Game of Duty and Community
    A Fan-Splat by Tonkers77

    ​
    Nagual!

    ​​Hello! Nagual the Ignited is a Fan-Splat created by myself for playing a Shapeshifter in the CofD. They are not of the Shadow in any way, so they can be played alongside the Uratha of Werewolf.

    Instead, they protect the material and maintain humanity through cultivating its relationship with the rest of Lurra's Creations. As far as they are concerned, Lurra-Ulaka is a the primal goddess of the cycle of life and death, predator and prey, and all living mundane things. This includes humanity. A divine spark of her power runs through their veins in the form of the Rage.

    Her Garden, the material world, is under attack. It is being invaded by a force called the Absence whose inhabitants, Invasives, crawl through the cracks between the Absence and the material to manifest and feed. They warp entire habitats and landscapes in order to better thrive. Consider them more of an Invasive Species than the crazy, evil of the Wyrm stuff.

    Nagual are born to the three Breeds, Glede the Nagual Born (They are not like the metis. No curse or twisting, not sterile, just raised among Nagual from the get-go); Scoria the Animal Born (No, Nagual do not get it on with animals. Only Lurra-Ulaka decides if an animal becomes Nagual) and finally the Kindled or Human Born. Humans who were Kindled by the Rage of another Nagual into undergoing the Call.

    Every Nagual is part of a Brood that share the same animal shape. These also cause them to pick up particular quirks of that animal as well. Raccoons will put their hands on your stuff, Cats like to watch things, and Wolves are super social.​

    Nagual also Adapt to their surroundings, their Rage honing itself to make them the perfect predators of whatever they're dealing with, or are among. This gives them plenty of Crossover options, as immersing yourself into the business of other Splats opens up particular Supernatural Adaptations.

    Adaptations come in four flavors. Brood Adaptations can only be learned by the particular Brood, like the Grimalkin being the only ones that learn Adaptation of the Cat. Environmental Adaptations for the Elemental and Garden they protect, such as one being out in an area that is snowy most of the year having the Adaptation of the Tundra. Then you have Universal Adaptations that any Nagual can learn, such as Adaptation of the Urban Predator, or the Territory. Finally, there are Supernatural Adaptations, such as Adaptation of Death which you can learn by hanging out with Sin-Eaters too long, or fighting Vampires too much.

    That's...pretty much the core stuff to it.
    ​​
    There is a Server dedicated to it. And I have posted about it here before, however, I felt that it wasn't a very good post. It didn't cover what I needed to and it has been a while.
    I am currently working on a Night Horrors Supplement for it. The Core Rulebook is finished (though a bit rough as far as editing goes).

    https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...it?usp=sharing (Core-Rulebook)
    https://discord.gg/Mzx583YSk (Server)

    Themes:

    Duty: All Nagual are tied by the Rage to Lurra-Ulaka. All of them have a duty and responsibility to use their Rage to protect their Wild Cousins and Lurra's Garden from the Invasives. To turn your back on that duty is to go Wayward.

    Family: Nagual are not only the animals they take the shape of. Even the most independent of them need others to turn to for support and comfort when things get hard. Either their real blood family, or those they choose. Family reminds the Nagual why they fight.

    Community: No Nagual is an island. Gathering in Clans of several members, along with their families and Cinder relatives. The community is the heart of the territory, even the extended human settlements they border or hide among are deeply important to the Nagual. Without a community, the Nagual could turn savage.

    Misunderstandings and Fallacies

    All Nagual are part animal

    Partially. Our lizard brain is certainly a bit more active than that of humans. We begin to take on animalistic traits and urges as our Rage rises beyond our control. We can all turn into various levels of an animal, from full beast to a pure hybrid. But completely part animal to the point of being able to reproduce with them? No, that’s not something we’re capable of. They’re our wild cousins, not our wild spouses. Even those born of animals are forever cut off from them in that way. Mother Lurra wants us to stay with one another and humanity, and so that is who we can *ahem* be with.

    Being bitten by a Nagual turns you into one.

    Partially. If we bite you, we intend to kill you, not make you one of us. Though, sometimes accidents do happen. Ingesting or injecting our blood into your veins can result in a new Nagual. However, those that we attack with our claws and teeth do not survive first contact. Certainly, not long enough for the Mother to call them. Even if they are so lucky, they must survive the burn of the Rage, and not lose their minds doing so. A tall order for even ones we select to Kindle.

    All Nagual are Cursed.

    Not all of us. Curse is not a word we would use to describe the Rage, though it certainly can be a burden. The Kindled make up enough of us that there are myths and legends associated with lycanthropes. However, they are not the typical Nagual. Most of us are born and raised as Cinders, or Nagual that have not undergone The Call. The barest embers of Rage flow through their veins, and they have small quirks based off of their animal natures that mark them as the other. Others are born and raised as animals before Lurra-Ulaka Calls to them.

    Nagual Defend Nature from Humans

    No. Humans are every bit a part of Lurra’s Garden as our Wild Cousins. We are to guide them on how to nurture the Garden, and they are to remember that they are of nature as well. Humanity are the Gardeners, and it is in their hands to see nature flourish. To make an enemy of Humanity is to move one step closer to becoming a Man-Eater or Lycanthrope-to fall Wayward. In the face of the Absence, we are all Lurra’s Children.

              The latter's probably easier, since you can excise a lot of words and even expectations (like Rage) out of your work and get wilder with the mystery behind the splat[s](particularly since you don't have to have a single unified origin for them***) and more directly in theme with a non-Werewolf rooted shapeshifter splat-for a tonal guide, I would point you at anything on the Gangrel from Requiem and towards Hunter First Edition**, both hit the particular voice of a non-spiritual take on the visceral thrill of an animalistic shapeshifter splat.

              THe former is challenging if you don't feel hyper comfortable with the ins and outs of the Forsaken universe, but one particular gem of a feature that you can take root in is the extreme ass end of things with Pangaeans, ancient entities that have explicit roles as being a viable origin for other shapeshifters and often have agendas that can (and, in fact, do) still execute into the modern day, and of course gives you tools to make shifters that also have more uniqueness to them than the usual stuff for shapeshifters. It also gives you plenty of leeway to beg, steal, and borrow from Mage: the Awakening and Beast: the Primordial. The biggest catch is that, while you can anchor things in the triage of the Flesh, Shadow, and Astral (or weirder still, the Sundering that ultimately fucked over the Pangaeans did have far reaching consequences and threw things far afield), you probably still need to have a good grasp of how these things incarnate and play out to keep it versimilitudinous with where-ever you anchor things in the Flesh, Shadow, and Astral. At a bare minimum, you'd probably at least want to have a look at how they did their own alternate shifters, most prominently in War Against the Pure.

              In any particular case, either way, you wanna scrub the Apocalypse-isms out, since it immediately stands out as odd in the text but is also not used enough as it's own language to demarcate it as a deliberate recycling to make a unique Chronicles product.

              Otherwise, it looks coherent, keep at it.
