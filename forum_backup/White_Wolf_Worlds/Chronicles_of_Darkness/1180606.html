    How can you modify the chase rules to allow players to escape angels and gods?

    01-30-2018, 12:57 AM
    Had a problem in the last chronicle I ran, the PCs, demons were being hunted by an angel. The problems I ran into were, the angel as an ephemeral, was easily able to locate them, I didn't see a way/reason/method to make the challenging for the angel. Once it had located them it had a massive dice pool to attack with, taking it head on wasn't a good option, but neither was running, because they couldn't outrun it, since as my understanding of the new chase rules is you add 2 attributes/skills for the dicepool, and being ephemeral that pool was always 15. I'd like to know of a good way to allow the characters to outrun angels and other "god like" beings. I'm thinking of scenes more like the ones with the agents in the Matrix. Agents are overwhelming, but they can escape them, cleverly perhaps even beat them (dodge this). Because this isn't really specific to demon, could be talking a true fae, or an arch mage, etc.

    The best I've come up with so far is that perhaps ephemeral and other 3 attribute beings can only apply one of their attributes to chase

    Looking for general suggestions.

                              Originally posted by xenoterracide View Post
                              I think I'ms starting to see where I was screwing up, I was doing something like Finesse + Power for the angels rolls, not even Finesse + Rank. Also I wasn't applying a cumulative -1 penalty. Would you suggest using the same mechanics for "Brief nightmares" since a brief nightmare also doesn't have Wits or Streetwise?
                              Yeah, Power + Finesse is an Attribute + Attribute task. Attribute + Attribute tasks are rare and the chase rules don't give any specific examples of them.

                              I would personally assign a suitable Attribute + Skill as if adjucating a chase between two non-ephemeral characters, and then translate the angel's dice pool into the equivalent ephemeral dice pool: whichever ephemeral Attribute corresponds to the character's Attribute, and then consider whether this particular type of chase falls within the purview and nature of this angel (if it's a hunter angel it likely is), and if so, add Rank. In situations where the terms of the chase fall outside the purview, just roll the one ephemeral Attribute by itself as the base pool. This binary Rank modifier is what ephemeral entities use instead of checking for Skill ratings.

                              So, for example, a demon flees a hunting angel with two spindly flexible legs it uses to leap long distances. The demon is caught in the open by the shore, and has to roll Strength + Athletics to outrun the angel, which the angel matches with Power + Rank. The demon thinks fast and dives over the side of a bridge, transitioning into an underwater chase. The dice pool might then become Stamina + Athletics, for argument's sake, and if the angel's configuration isn't suitable for flexible underwater movement, it may now simply be rolling Resistance as its pool. Salt to taste, but it's likely a good idea to keep that Rank modifier in mind to give your players a chance to come up with ideas to outmaneuver angels if they can infer the nature of the particular angel.

                                      Originally posted by xenoterracide View Post
                                      How does the cumulative -1 work? I don't recall that from the rules for chase at all.
                                      It's in the Turn-By-Turn section on p85 of the ChroD corebook.

                                      If the other side wishes to use other dice pools, they lose the 10-again quality on his roll, and suffer a cumulative -1 penalty. This penalty increases every time he uses a dice pool aside from the one dictated. He does not suffer it on turns where he has the Edge or otherwise uses the dictated dice pool, but it continues to amass if he decides not to on a future turn.
