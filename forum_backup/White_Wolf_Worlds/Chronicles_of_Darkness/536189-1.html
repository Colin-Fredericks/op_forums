                                    Actually what passes for blood in insects usually doesn't carry oxygen. Insects are very different animals. I don't see anything wrong with that form of "consumption" suggested. When size issues are taken out of the equation, I think most insects are fair candidates. All that is required is a resonance with the predatory Beast. Even butterflies were associated with war in some cultures. Insects can exhibit predatory behavior even if they aren't predators. They tend to be very aggressive creatures. They are certainly more like true predators than a prey animal resorting to violence for self-defense. Insectivores are predators, by the way.
