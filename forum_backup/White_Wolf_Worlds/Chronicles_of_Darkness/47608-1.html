                    Well, time to get back to this.

                    Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post
                    You start by saying that Jesus only performed miracles at tipping points, who's to say that the Church won't follow Jesus' example? I hear that the church are rather big on Jesus in general.
                    Maybe for small affairs, maybe even for something akin to the total of the people on the Mount for Jesus's sermon, with a majority of people who are likely to be swayed. But the question wasn't on small affairs with a controlled demographic, or even the same as having a revival, this is about the Catholic Church doing public miracles. That implies a large scale presentation to the uncontrolled masses. This sets the stage for some big ass problems.


                    Next you talk about a magical arms race with two advantages to the non-Catholics. Firstly that everyone else has miracles and secondly that Benediction isn't feel quite right. To the first I will counter that most magic is either:
                    1) Highly unethical.
                    2) Really really hard to learn.
                    Actually, I have a list of four problems that put it in favor of the non-Catholics:

                    1)Everyone has miracles.
                    2)Everyone else's miracles are a hell of a lot bigger.
                    3)Everyone else's doesn't require God, Jesus, Apostles, or Saints.
                    4)Benedictions feel wrong compared to everyone else's.

                    Benedictions alone aren't a dog in a fight, unless you count the chihuahua. Of course something is off about a Sin-Eaters manifestations, it's fucking fueled by death(loosely speaking). Of course you feel screwed when a Changeling uses his magic, it's clearly binding on a fated level. Of course a Mummy's power makes you want to piss yourself, it's ancient magic. Even lower end stuff, like the Les Mysteres Rites Du Cheval or the magics of Second Sight, it feels wrong to us, but it feels right given what it is. And a lot of those powers don't come with the strings of God-granted, the the Les Mysteres aren't going to do any better at recruiting, but the Ceremonial Mages and Hedge Witches are.

                    But the other people can do so much more and on much bigger scales, and that when your entire power base is on "Look what I can do", having someone who can one up you hardcore is a bad idea. In the World of Darkness, there is always some like that around. The only argument you have is that your cards come from God, and when it doesn't feel like God, it sure loses validity. Benedictions alone are bad case of magic to whip out into the open.

                    So, your two arguments.

                    1)Tell that to Ceremonial and Hedge Magic, actually. Not as big, but certainly more diverse, feels right for what it is, and is something more in your hands. THere's a lot more out there than just this.
                    2)Maybe, but most of the low hanging fruit for humans isn't necessarily any harder to learn than Benedictions

                    Sure a Changeling might be able to bring back the dead (I don't see that Contract, so lets say the Changeling can heal). Well to learn that contract you must first be taken to a horrific realm of torture in Arcadia, and then escape. That's pretty damn hard. To learn Benedictions you must take a training course in the Vatican. Mage's have to Awaken, Sin-Eaters have to die. Vampires have to be vampires and Sons of Phobos power their magic by cutting out hearts. To put it simply, in an arms race the only people in the cannon WoD who can match the MM for magic that can easily be taught are other hunter conspiracies and Vampires. I don't think I need to explain why the public will prefer the Church to Vampires.
                    Yes, no one wants to sign up for the summer camp in Arcadia(Well, maybe the extremely militant, heh)-but first of all, let's put aside learning it and just go with who's going to listen to who. Catholics raise the dead, Changeling raises the dead and then makes it snow in Florida. Catholics...scratch their head.

                    Or, with vampires, that conveniently have a God oriented covenant(Who, admittedly, will be fighting the Ordo and the Circle in this hypothetical at the same time as everything else), let's go this angle: Yes, I am a vampire, but! 1)Anything he can do, I can do better. 2) I also serve God(and I'll ignore the details of my service for now) and seek to do 'good', and 3)Just like your pop-cultural heroes! 4)Oh, by the way, these miracles are tailored to my curse, which is why it looks like/acts like/feels like this.

                    Yes, people can be xenophobic, and yes, lots of people will choose the Church over the vampires on principle-but a lot more people are going to be right there on the fence than makes either side comfortable. It's wouldn't be a landslide victory for the Catholics or the vampires-that's why neither side does it. If the Catholics thought they could end the Vigil by doing it publicly, they would(and they did, and a fair number of accounts mentioned how troublesome that was for everything),and if vampires thought they could, they would. It's not a landslide it's a war waiting to happen,which neither wants.

                    As to your second point. That Benedictions "feel wrong". What do you think the other magic feels like? Changeling magic is tinged with madness, Supernal magic triggers disbelief in your Soul, Mummies are surrounded by an aura of existential dread.
                    All of which feels appropriate to the sources these things are attributed, as opposed to conman-in-the-frock magic of Benediction.


                    So I think if it came to a maical arms race, the Church will probably win. Of all the magic that is easily taught and doesn't require hideously unethical power sources theirs is the best. The Church ideal is probably one miracle every decade or so, enough to prove the proper skeptics (rationalists, not god haters) that the big guy is real. Enough to get people's attention so they can preach about morality and faith. But if that's not an option and public miracles will inevitably cause a magical arms race; I think that a step up from arguing with Richard Dawkins.
                    The Catholics only have one advantage, and that is, as we like to discuss with humanity versus monsters in general, sheer numbers. Roman Catholicism, granted, has numbers in the millions(1.2 billion people, actually), and while most of them seem to be in the "I'm Catholic because my pappy was Catholic" camp, that's not a ringing endorsement.

                    But putting that aside, there's a reason it hasn't been done large scale in the open-because the Church wouldn't win. It'd be a very messy battle of factors rather than anything clear cut. That's why it hasn't been done yet(among a myriad of other reasons, granted-I expect the Church is somewhat aware of the things the monsters keep off the doorstep by being there as well, much like I expect many of the Conspiracies to actually know and not fuck with, but that's another subject). They don't want to push the button on a move that isn't a guarenteed win

                    Finally; you're overlooking something really really simple, obvious and important. Who says the Church has to make the first move? What's to stop the Priests of Isis building a big alter in the middle of a public place and get people to form a line for divine healing? Even if the Church doesn't want to fight a miracle war, they should be preparing their defenses.
                    Well, two reasons. One is that no one wants to be the guy who pushed the button. That entire subject is basically an the result of nuclear deterrent-the first one to make the push these days is the one who might end the world. No one wants to be that guy. The second is also the fact that many of these people have other fish to fry. Mages don't want to usher in the Abyss, changelings would really like it if no one invited the Strangers en masse, demons are quite fine with where the God Machine's focus is at, thank you very much, mummies just don't have the time, etc. Add in that humanity creates a sort of buffer zone(yes, even against the Strix, which you think wouldn't matter but it does) against these guys and now really no one wants to push the issue.

                    It's a big issue-bigger than most characters we play as can really deal with. Status quo is God(heh heh, I'm funny) for a reason.

                                            To explain this I'm going to turn to a variant of the one big lie concept. That a story can tell one big lie and everything else must follow logically from that one big lie. If you focus purely on the MM that big lie is "the church has a secret militant wing who hunt monsters with guns and magic that might or might not come from God via the saints". This interpreation is clearly is supported by the books, there are hints that Benedictions might not be as holy as the MM believes, and there's hints that they might be genuine like the morality bonus.

                                            So when you point out that Catholic doctrine contradicts the rules for Benedictions, that doesn't matter so much because that's covered by the one big lie. In the WoD, clearly catholic doctrine must have room for this because Benedictions exist and it's explicitly a possibility that they might be genuinely from god. Just like in the WoD the laws of reality must allow for vampires and magic prayers to exist.
