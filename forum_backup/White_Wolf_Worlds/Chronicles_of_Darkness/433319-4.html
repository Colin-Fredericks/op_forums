                                                    Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
                                                    As I'm assuming the Libertarians* correspond to the Republican* side of the human conflict, is there a specific reason they don't fall under the broader umbrella of the Carthians?

                                                    *US Americans, neither of these words have the meaning here you'd think they do if you're making assumptions off of US politics.
                                                    You're partially right. They are an split of Carthians. In my original idea, they were a radical Carthian faction, and I wrote originally in this way in land of the Damned: Spain. But with Requiem 2d edition, which gives more freedom in the covenant system, I converted them in a independent covenant (although split from carthians).

                                                    They are the vampire version of the internal divisions and infightings of the Republican side, in which socialists and comunists were in a inner war against anarchists and trotskists. They splintered after some internal Carthian fightings about the failed attempt of create a Carthian Republic in Spain.

                                                    And yes, they could fall under the broader Carthian Umbrella, but the hatred among both Carthian factons is so high, that thay work in practice as an independent covenant.

                                                    *Yes, in Spain, Libertarians is a term synonim of anarchist, a meaning very different than in America.
