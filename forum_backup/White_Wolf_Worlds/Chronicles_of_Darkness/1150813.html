    Home brew artifact for comments and criticism.

    10-18-2017, 06:36 PM
    Hi all, I am running a first Rd VtR game using clans and gen from VtM, I have developed an artifact to help give my players a power boost (they are all BP1 Gen 13) and to see what they would do with it. Any comments and criticisms would be great.

    Bloodstone Chalice
    A large sandstone chalice with the interior studded with Bloodstones. The exterior is polished smooth, while the interior is rough stone.
    The interior is blood stained with some blood encrusted part way up the sides, and congealed blood in the very bottom.

    System: The Bloodstone Chalice has two effects, the first is when vitae from multiple kindred is willingly put into the chalice, mixed drunk by those who put their vitae into the chalice, the drinkers Generation is reduced by One, this can only happen once per drinker. This will cause a level one vininculum to form.

    The second effect is that blood or Vitae (this will render the vitae neutral, and unable to form a blood bond) from any source no matter how old will provide sustenance to any kindred who drinks it, no matter what their feeding restrictions, this will provide on Vitae to the drinker, this is limited to one Vitae per day per kindred.

    With both of these effects there is no risk of the user forming an addiction to Vitae.

                                                      Even if we assume the Chalice won't reduce Generation below 8th, it's still a massive deal. It could be used to uplift Thin-bloods to standard Vampirehood, which has massive political and religious ramifications. In a way, the secondary effect is more notable than the intended one. Vampires have been getting blood for millenia, but decreasing Generation without Diablerie is something unique.

                                                      A level one Vinculum is not that big a deal compared to that, especially since you can use blood of someone expendable, and then kill them. Perhaps you might make the Vinculum count towards the Chalice itself, and whatever mysterious agenda an object might have. The strengthening of Generation may also depend on the continued existence of the Chalice, meaning those who benefited from it need to keep it safe.
