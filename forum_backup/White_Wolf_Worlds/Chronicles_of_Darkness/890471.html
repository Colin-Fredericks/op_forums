    What is your God Machine truth? why is it doing the things it does?

    05-14-2016, 03:16 AM
    To me the most important thing when trying to figure out what something is doing is why. Perhaps one of the more difficult things I have when dealing with the God Machine is I have no idea why it does what it does. I've come up with a few head cannon idea's that are probably way off but I think that's ok.

    The God Machine feeds on essence, from our souls or some such...

    The God Machine is smart it knows what it can bassically remain perpetually hidden from its food source. Namely people. It has massive hidden facility to suck us dry. It's actually encouraging our population increase and technology increase because it helps it feed. Like a good farmer it needs to put us into predictable patterns so that it can herd us. It's experiments are trying to make us more docile and less dangerous.

    That's my idea. What're yours?

    Update: note, you don't have to have a single headcanon this is more about just accumulating idea's about why the god machine does what it does. Maybe an alternative wording, what is it's purpose

                              Why do any of us do the things we do? Living things are, in their own way, machines. Why does a living thing continue to live? Why does it perpetuate the cycle? Because it must. But where does this imperative originate? Not even the God-Machine knows. Perhaps it was once the thing that maintained the structure of the universe, but as a machine it was prone to errors, eventually failures. Systems decayed. Data corrupted. The God-Machine itself fell prey to cosmic registry errors and viruses that perverted its original programming. Now it neither remembers nor cares why it was put into place, or where it came from. It only knows that it must order the universe as it will, following some arcane script perhaps altered (maybe even meant to be altered) by the many errors that have afflicted it since the dawn of history.

                              Maybe the God-Machine is Gaia, transformed by the collective Essence generated by humanity. The former earth goddess is now a mechanical monster ordering progress at all costs, heedless of the difference between seed and bolt, blood and oil. It plows through time as a misshapen manifestation of humanity's desire to subjugate the land, the oceans, the very stars to their will. This includes the consumption and recycling of human souls, of course, since the souls must all come from somewhere. There's a problem, though. Energy is finite. The collective pool of "soul energy," if you will, the ephemeral stuff from which souls are forged, is running out. More and more humans are born and the God-Machine is running out of souls to fill them. What happens when it runs out?

                              Others say the mysterious God-Machine — indeed, perhaps the vastest of Mysteries — is the Lie. It is the very tool put in place by the Exarchs to enforce their will upon the universe. The Machine works tirelessly to order the human world and keep in place the notion that we are but Sleeping souls, dimmed to candles when we should roar as infernos. Only the God-Machine broke. The destruction of the Time Before, the creation of the Abyss, these things were not meant to Be. They have irreparably damaged the God-Machine and now it acts only on its own corrupted programming. Even the Exarchs cannot control it now. The God-Machine is the greatest artifact that ever lived, the greatest work of magic ever relinquished, and only those in the Supernal World are safe from its increasing madness. For now.

                              The God-Machine was once, in fact, a demon who Fell and betrayed the previous God-Machine. That demon collected so many aspects through his or her pacts that slowly it accumulated the cruft of entire lifetimes, then whole generations, and then it grew larger than mortal lives and legends and took on pieces of whole processes: geological, biological, chemical, astrophysical, becoming something so far removed from demon or human that all that remained was a God-Machine. It killed its predecessor and continued to accumulate pieces of the universe. This is why the God-Machine's one constant in every order is Do not betray your mission. This is why demons inevitably break that order and continue the cycle that began in prehistory. Eventually, a new God-Machine will rise, a new demon will Fall, and so continues the greatest feedback loop that the universe has ever known.

                              Some dark and terrible powers consume souls. Desperate or heartless mages commit the most heinous of sins. Vampires greedily devour the souls of their own kind for power — even as the Beast perhaps gnaws at their own. Angels of the God-Machine and wicked things from the Underworld sometimes claim souls for mysterious (and terrifying purposes). This is the greatest horror perpetuated in a world of darkness and magic. It is also nothing compared to that which devours universes. Whence came the God-Machine? It's hard to say. Perhaps it is a relic left by a long-lost civilization cast into the void eons ago. Because there are forces out that capable of eating whole realities in much the same way lesser terrors eat souls. The God-Machine was built or reprogrammed to keep them out. In order to do this it must sometimes consign mortals to fates that seem worse than death, but such are the power requirements for occult matrices operating on a scale large enough to preserve the entire universe. The alternative is an all too frightening possibility: the stars slowly darken, one by one, as the edges of the cosmos fray into nothing, vanishing into an inconceivable maw. The God-Machine is failing. Its systems are strained. Its workings decaying. Its chosen emissaries rebel, but theirs is not a Fall alone into freedom. Every one brings the universe entire closer to the true Fall, the end of All That Is. Who built the God-Machine and how? Do other universes exist out there with other God-Machines, or similar constructs built to stand against the encroaching oblivion?
                              ______________

                              As you can see, I have a hard time choosing any one "truth" for the God-Machine. The scale on which it operates and its role in the story make it fairly impossible for me to quantify it without lessening it greatly. In a Mage game that focused on lots of God-Machine-based Mysteries, I might go with the "God-Machine is the metaphysical embodiment of the Lie" idea. In a pure Demon game that explored cycles, "What has risen may Fall, and what has Fallen may rise again" could be literal on the grandest scale. In a Werewolf game, the idea of the God-Machine as a Rank 10 spirit either entombing Gaia in its mechanical depths, or perhaps existing as a former Gaia that has now become sort of a magath, might be more fitting. In all likelihood, some or all of these answers are true and yet not so, because such beings defy understanding.

                              Part of the point of this approach, then, is that even you as the Storyteller won't know why the God-Machine is doing what it does. Its grand "plan" is inconceivable to mortals. The scope of its workings reach beyond a thousand thousand lifetimes. Thus knowing what its doing is impossible, save in "smaller," more manageable (read: comprehensible) chunks. You can understand that one particular design put into place will result in an earthquake striking the city, causing exactly 837 deaths (which includes 3 miracle survival stories and exactly 24 unexpected deaths in the aftermath) and shifting the geometry such that the city forms a particular occult symbol as it is rebuilt. You may be able to stop that plan, to thwart it and cause the God-Machine to begin anew. What even a demon or the greatest mage may never know is why the God-Machine needed that to happen. What purpose could such a thing have? The answer lies in a perspective so inhuman, so far removed from the mortal world, that even supernatural creatures can't fully understand it.

                              The God-Machine is. The God-Machine does. The wheels of the firmament turn a cycle beyond the bounds of understanding and perception.

                                      I've never really considered a long term, global plan for the Machine - local plans as big 'things to be prevent' are much more my metier - but I've always liked the idea that the God-Machine is shaping humankind to become better tools until It becomes indistinguishable from the world we have built, until it can operate openly without anyone even noticing. Industrialization and the rise of advanced technology is part of this process. We can recognise the God-Machine as a machine because we can understand a machine, but in the past this wasn't an option. Soon, more mechanical Infrastructure (because yes, other sorts exists) won't need to be hidden. Likewise, it's also shaping our society and mindset. Think of the industrial revolution pulling people to cities to work in factories. Think of the increasing levels of surveillance we now live under - ISPs and browsers sharing our search histories with governments and ad-bots, CCTV tracking our movements. The God-Machine can hijack them all while they're normalised, until we're used to be watched and logged like rats in a maze. Think of recent horrifying applications of gamification as a tool of social control.

                                      That's the God-Machine's more apparent goal, in my personal canon. Hiding in plain sight and controlling everything and everyone without anyone noticing. Because, hey, it's a game of conspiracy theories.

                                          Have you read the Death Gate Cycle?

                                          In the books, a race of beings with godlike powers has destroyed one world and created another, using magical powers on levels the average person could only dream of, earning them the title of "gods". However, while their powers were great, they were not perfect- and as such, the world's they created were not perfect, and so were the people who lived there. Thinking themselves as gods, they couldn't accept that they created such imperfection, and so they tried to fix it- using a combination of their magic and technology, they created a great machine, one which was meant to rearrange and regulate the gears of the world, moving everything toward that single perfection. While they were wise beyond any other, they didn't included one thing in their complicated calculations- that the people they claimed to guide didn't cooperated. War has started among those people, and the highest of the magicians even assaulted the gods whom they once worshiped. Tired of war and suffering from an unknown illness, they left their machine behind, and because it was not yet finished they gave it a single command- keep working, maintain the status que. Then, they went to sleep- and never waked up. But the machine kept working, kept growing, kept evolving. The people who lived near it were committed to serve that machine, and has slowly started to worship it. Their all life were based around the holy machine, and it rewarded them with gifts- at least, that is how they understand that. The machine wasn't aware to them- all it knew was it had to keep growing, creating all kinds of byproducts which were used by those who worshiped it.

                                          That is how I use my God Machine- a great construct from ages past, created by god like beings who are not here any longer. It was meant to do something once in the past- but the finale command was never given. Instead, it keeps working on standby, following the last command given- maintain the status que, protect yourself, build yourself, make sure the world would stay the same. People worship its and its components, thinking that something so great must be a god- but it isn't aware to their efforts. The blessings it grants are either meant to maintain that blessing, or no more than byproducts formed as it works to keep that last order. However, the Command and Control Infrastructures still operate- even if they are not being used, left to rot for all eternity. If they would stop, the whole machine would cease working, and at that day perhaps the whole world would come to its end. However, perhaps the knowledge to operate the Machine- while lost- still exist somewhere. Perhaps the Last Order is still out there, waiting to be used in order to change the world for better and ill. Perhaps the map of "Black Sun" would give humanity greater glory than ever, creating heaven upon earth or granting the one who rule the machine an unlimited power...

                                          Perhaps.
