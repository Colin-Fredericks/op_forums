    Mandatory Silly Questions

    04-28-2014, 11:04 AM
    Went through the forums to see if there was a Demon FAQ, but nothing came up, so guessing most folks are adapting to the game easily enough.

    I do not have that luxury, however, so I have a couple of questions regarding things that weren't too clear in the book.

    Q1: With the Embed "Just Bruised", it doesn't specify whether or not it can be used on the Demon with it, and seems to focus more on "targets". On the Mend, on the other hand, specifically states the Demon can use that Embed on itself. Is this to say Just Bruised cannot be used on self, or is it to be assumed you can use it on yourself without the same wording as On the Mend?

    Q2: With the Demonic Form Propulsion, "Tether", it states you can pull another character towards you using the tether, but doesn't seem to specify how fast the target moves towards you. Is it the same speed as the Demon approaching an object he is tethered to, or some other speed rating left unsaid? Given as I assume objects could likely also be pulled with this ability, I feel this needs to be specified, given most objects don't have speed ratings.

                  Angels do not need to be given Demonic Form abilities, as these can be accounted for easily under the Ephemeral Creature rules found in the rear GMC rules update. To answer your questions in this vein, to represent an Angel with "Rivet Arm" you would give them the "Blast Numina" or a "gun hand". It says that an Ephemeral Creature does bashing damage with its attacks unless its nature calls for lethal. If you, as the storyteller, have made the decision that the Angel has a "knife" for a hand, then he would do lethal damage with his basic attacks. If he has a gun for a hand, he would have the range of a similar weapon and do lethal on his attacks. (Though a ranged weapon might be better suited to the "Blast" Numina)
