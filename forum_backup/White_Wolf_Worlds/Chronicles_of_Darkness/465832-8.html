        The moment of truth: Do the PCs back Dana, who has refused to explain herself frequently and mislead them but never lied, and helped them with their business, or back Iris, who has used powers on them but has not lied anyone knows of and had Dana pull a gun on her every time? Lizette’s affections muddy the water, but it is time to see what the statuettes are, at least in part, and determine if the Conspiracy sides with Dana or Iris

        Session XXV

        Marek’s Thoughts

        28 April 1922

        We decided that Dana, Lizette and I would be the ones to go and meet up with Iris in her room. When we arrived and walked inside Dana surprised all of us by pulling a gun on Iris and telling her that the only way that she’s going to be taken to the Chateau is bound and blindfolded. Dana really does not trust Iris, not even a little bit. Iris for her part went along with it and allowed herself to be bound and blindfolded. We then proceeded to escort Iris to the Chateau via the “Hedge” as my fae friends have called that strange place between worlds. This would provide a faster way to get to the house than driving there. It also gives the illusion that the house is much closer to Paris than it actually is. So we arrived a short time later and untied Iris so she could freshen up and wait in one of the guest rooms in the Guest House. I believe it is called that. We were not in the main Chateau, just one of the smaller buildings behind it. Still rather nice though. I guarded the door to make sure that Iris would not leave.

        Meanwhile Dacian went to the post office to retrieve the wās discreetly. Apparently upon arriving he noticed Ewan Brandon waiting outside the post office smoking a cigarette, and inside there was another Changeling going over the packages with her hands like she was trying to sense something. The postal workers seemed to ignore her so she must have had some gift active that allowed her to blend in like she was supposed to be there. After she left Dacian was able to step up his search, but all told it still took him about an hour to find the right package. He grumbled that he had forgotten how to read French. I think that there was just a lot of packages to go through. His explanation is possible too. When you speak a number of languages sometimes it is easy to switch to different one mid thought. I myself think in different languages sometimes. I don’t know if there is a pattern to it, but it is easy to mix things up. Lizette then spoke up saying that she recognized the Changeling that Dacian described. Apparently she is an Autumn courtier named Shalice who is something of a Librarian for the Autumn Court. We’re still not sure what she was doing there.

        So then Dacian gave Dana the wās back. An interesting thing happened at that point. Dana’s eyes and the wās glowed blue for just a moment before going back to normal. She was also very keen on getting that thing back, so I’m not sure exactly what kind of connection Dana has with that device but I have a feeling it’s probably not an especially healthy one. After Dana got her wand back and Lizette had rigged the statues with explosives, just in case something went really wrong, I was given the go ahead to let Iris out of the guest room. So I did. Iris and I walk into the main room and almost immediately Dana pointed the wās at Iris and suddenly there is a blue light between Dana and Iris and then Iris was no longer there. In her place was a creature like the ones we fought on the train when we first met. She had six arms, reverse articulated legs and metallic skin. Now, apparently this creature was Iris, it talked with her voice anyway, and her human form is a way for her to blend in. I’m not thinking her natural form would be overly accepted by the public. Dearbhail confirmed that iris’s main desire at that moment was to acquire the wās.

        Iris called herself an unchained and that she is actively working against something she calls the machine god. She said that she was here to determine what the statues are and what they do. She also entreated Dana to let her turn back to her human form because she would be visible by this machine god while she was in her natural state. Dana was controlling when Iris could change with the wās, which is just interesting, and eventually agreed and shifted her nemesis back to her human shape. Around this tie Lizette comes back to say that everything is all set with the statues, so we all go to the room where they were being kept. We allowed Iris to take a look at the statues and in turn Iris asks some basic questions which seemed applicable to the devices. She asked where they had been found, which Dana was reluctant to answer. I’m not sure why she is keeping the location a secret. Maybe there are more statues like the three that she has and she doesn’t want Iris to find them. Or she could just be spiting her once lover. In any case Iris said that the three statues are part of some larger machine. They are merely a device used to store energy, much like a battery. Only the kind of energy that she describes sounds like what ghosts and spirits use to power their abilities. It is possible that these statues are responsible for the storm in Paris as well, though Dana did not knowingly create the storm in the first place.

        The thing that is troubling to me about these statues is that it is likely that there is a way to release the energy that they contain both with and without the original machine that they are a part of. Without the machine we don’t know what would happen. It might release the energy in a rapid flash and injure those of us around it, or it could release in a slow steady stream. I think we are all leaning towards the former possibility. Now the difference comes when they are plugged into the machine that they came from. Iris’s theory is that they could be used to power a weapon that would cause destruction on a global scale. Or if it’s not designed as a weapon it might be used that way by some inspired individual. I recommended that we destroy the statues. They are too dangerous to keep around. It was argued that if we try to destroy them we might release the energy that is stored in them and cause mass amounts of damage. Iris also revealed that the wās is a master control device, and that while there are other such devices out there they are rare and hard to find. The wās it seems can also override and control the statues and Iris for that matter. I guess that explains why Iris wants it so much.

        We talked for a little while longer about the devices, but in the end there wasn’t much more to say. Dearbhail offered to take Iris back to Paris and Tatiana went with her to make sure she was safe. We blindfolded and bound Iris again so she wouldn’t know where our Chateau is and let our winged companion guide her back to the hotel in Paris. As soon as they’d left Dana reiterated that Iris is not a good person and that we cannot trust her. She suspects that if Iris had the wās she would use it to control similar devices to what we had to destroy her machine god. Dana herself tried to use the was on the statues and got them to light up individually. She believed that she would be able to exert the correct amount of control to end the storm once we got back to Paris. When we met up with Dearbhail and Tatiana later they said that Iris warned them not to trust Dana and that she is not a good person. Same warning from both sides it seems. She reiterated that Dana cannot be trusted to keep her word and that she was afraid that Dana would betray us the way she did her. The warning is fair. Dana did betray her lover, so it stands to reason that she’d have no problems betraying people she just met. For my part I am keeping an eye out for any suspicious behavior from Dana or Nathan, nothing has changed in that regard.

        The topic of what we were going to do with the statues after the storm was dealt with came up, and we all agreed that it would not be safe to leave them in the museum. As much as they should be there and I think Dana really wishes they could be left there, it’s just not safe. There are too many people and groups out to get their hands on the statues. They’d be stolen within the week if we left them at the museum. And we don’t know what these different groups know about the statues and their capabilities. It’s entirely possible that others know about what they can do and want to take advantage of that knowledge and use them. It’s also possible that those different groups don’t have unethical plans for the statues, but we don’t know what they will do and can’t take the chance. These statues are too powerful to let them fall into the wrong hands. Especially if they could cause global damage. The world is just now starting to get over the Great War, it doesn’t need more trouble. In the end I think we agreed that we’d store them in the Hedge house, or Hollow as Dacian called it. It would only be accessible by other fae-kind and even then would still be protected while in the house.

        So, we went back to the museum to use the statues to stop the storm. We ran into the curator while we were there and told him that we would not be leaving the statues in his care. We were perhaps not as polite as we could have been when dealing with him, but he got the message in the end and left us to our own devices. Dana used the wās to activate the statues and concentrated on stopping the storm. It wasn’t immediate, but eventually the rain started slowing down and the clouds dispersed. After we’d done what we came to do we left the museum and headed back to the hotel. We passed Tatiana and Dearbhail on the way back and we all made our way to the hotel for dinner. Today was long and eventful and didn’t stop there.

        At dinner Dana explained about what happened with the wās that started this whole distrust between herself and Iris. We knew that Dana had been hired to acquire the wās for Iris, but then decided to keep the thing for herself. She explained that she did not trust Iris’s intentions once the artifact was turned over. Apparently Iris had hired another up and coming archaeologist, who was also her lover, to retrieve an item for her and soon after he’d delivered it he was murdered. Dana believed that it was Iris cleaning up any loose ends. So she was extra vigilant after she had gotten a hold of the wās, and discovered that there just happened to be a number of assassins in town during the time that Dana was supposed to be turning it over to Iris. Dana assumed that the assassins were there for her and so she decided to keep the wās for herself. That incident is also what prompted Dana to begin looking for the Golden Eagle in the first place. She wanted to be able to survive if Iris should send more assassins after her. Dana was sure that Iris has had other people that opposed her murdered, which is why she doesn’t trust her at all. I guess that explains why she’s always pulling a gun on the other woman and why she insisted that Iris be bound and blindfolded when we took her to the Chateau. Now, we have no absolute proof that what Dana said is the truth, but she certainly believes it.

        The last thing that we talked about over dinner was my little run in with someone hunting me in January. I had wanted to mention this earlier but we were always surrounded by strangers, Dana and Nathan usually. Over time those two learned about our unique qualities and we learned of theirs, well at least Dana’s. So, I figured it would not cause harm to mention this bit of pack business in front of them. The others were angry that someone had come after me and they asked several questions. Only some of which I had the answer to. I could tell them where the man found me and that he had silver bullets. I also mentioned the photograph. They were all very interested in finding the person responsible, even Dacian who is normally so affable, was enraged on my behalf. If we didn’t still have business in Paris I’m fairly certain that my pack would be actively trying to hunt down the person that hired the assassin. I assured them that I had people looking into it, including Jamal, and would let them know as soon as I heard anything definite.

        I will admit that I was heartened by the reaction of my pack. I know how I’d react if any of them had been hunted and their reaction mirrors my own. My reactions are not always what would be considered normal, so at least in this issue I can be assured that I would have reacted correctly. I am perhaps not as patient as I’d like to be concerning my would-be killer. I find myself having to wait on others to find information for me. I do not have the resources that Jamal and Hans do, so I have to wait until they find out something that I can work with. Once I have what I need to find the individual(s) responsible I will just have to do some hunting of my own.


        Tatiana’s Conversations

        Listen carefully, volchonok, this is very important. You must be on your guard - there may be assassins.

        Yes, volchonok, it has stopped raining. These assassins may be from the Pure in Vienna, so even you must be careful.

        No, I did not stop the rain. It was Dana, as I said before. Now, listen, they came after Marek with silver, so...

        With the was! Did you not pay attention? Dacian retrieved it, with only a little difficulty with the reading apparently. That is not important. What is important is they had a picture of Marek, and knew to use silver.

        What other threats, volchonok? The mechanical Scot that only Dacian saw? The fellow thief who stole nothing we care about? Our vampire ‘allies’? The mysterious cultists who have done nothing but linger on corners? How do these stack against a man who shot at our alpha with silver bullets?

        Well, yes, the assassin is dead.

        Bah. I do not know about this Iris. She is another of these mechanical men, but Dearbháil says her wants are different feeling. More like a real person’s. Iris says Dana broke their deal and she did. Dana could possibly say she didn’t steal the was, since she had not been paid for it, but she did break the deal. But, if what Dana says is correct, she had very good reason. And kidnap her at gunpoint. She might have warned Lizette however...

        It would not have been an option, since they needed Lizette to cheat and bring them through the Hedge.

        It is true, volchonok, the mechanical woman did seem forthcoming. I don’t believe we truly needed her help though, I believe Dana just wanted to use the was on her. With us around to protect her if it did not incapacitate her.

        We have chosen her side because Lizette likes her better.

        No, that does not mean Iris is a threat. She even promised so, as we walked her back, Dearbháil and I.

        Ppppbt. Of course not. I am not a foolish volchonok, you are. Of course she may be lying. But soon the was and statues will be out of her reach and she knows very little of us. She is not the same threat as assassins with silver bullets.

        Just because one is dead, it does not mean there are no more, volchonok.

        There is nothing to chase for now. Soon Lizette will speak with the bullets. It is simply that you must be careful that I speak with you of this.

        I would not be so sure. Marek was not in the city when they found him.

        I said no, volchonok. There will be no running this night. Soon, we will run down those who threaten the pack. But first, we must find the scent. Calm yourself, excitable volchonok, and prepare for the hunt.


        Lizette’s Plans

        29th April 1922

        It’s been an emotional rollercoaster, but I am currently riding quite high on it. I have concerns, but I have hopes as well. They say the beginning of a relationship is the most exciting part. Well Dana is exciting. She has successfully kept me off guard, and shows no sign of changing that now.

        It has come to pass that “Follow my lead” are the words I most dread hearing Dana say. In this case they preceded her taking Iris hostage at gunpoint. Of course, we were supposed to just be fetching her to take to the chateau, but apparently Dana had other ideas. We bound Iris’s hands and blindfolded her. I did not feel terribly pleased about that. We cut through the Hedge, and were careful to ensure she did not know where she was. I knew Dana had claimed Iris had tried to kill her, but even so I felt sorry for Iris.

        While we were guiding Iris back, Dacian stole the was as planned, though he saw Ewan outside, and Shalice was looking for something. She left eventually and he went in. It took him some time to find it, but he did. Wolfram, Dearbháil, and Fenric came back here with Nathan directly.

        Dana was a little scary when she got the was, to be honest. I mean she did not go mad with power, but close to it. When she acquired it, her eyes glowed momentarily. She had Marek bring in Iris, and used the was on her, which transformed Iris into a machine woman. She had reverse articulated legs, wings, several arms, and glowing amber eyes, along with red metallic skin. Sort of terrifying. But Dearbháil confirmed her concerns were more in line with a normal person than the machines we fought, which confirmed her claim she was rogue.

        She said the Statuettes were modular power sources, which could release the power in controlled ways. She could, or Dana could with the was, try and work out specific power, but they are mostly bursts of power as light or weapons. Iris said with the whole greater machine, she could fire a blast at the sun.

        If I were building a super weapon, I am thinking some sort of floating city or citadel in the sky. You know, a sort of flying city, which would rain death down anywhere needed, and fly out over whatever lands it is needed. Would need a lot of anti-aircraft cannons, and of course a ready team of gorgeous French Gremlins. We would let Wolfram come along and be all handsome and stoic of course. I mean, why have a giant flying base without Wolfram? Maybe I should go with something like a big palace shaped like my head with some sort of huge lightning rod device in my ears I can use to smite unbelievers, or failing that people who say I don’t dress femininely enough.

        Mayhem! Okay, where was I?

        And then things got ugly, and we get to the part I was dragging my feet on. Iris flat out accused Dana of taking the was and double-crossing her in Egypt. She did not hold back, telling Dana she had loved her, that she broke her heart, warning everyone, me especially, that Dana would double cross me for power. Dana angrily insisted that was not how it happened, that she had good reason to betray Iris, that she had loved Iris too, but that Iris was betraying her. Iris seized on Dana’s reluctance to explain, saying Dana was not doing so for fear Iris would show the truth, and pointing out Dana had pulled a gun (or was) on her every time they had met to prove Dana’s treachery.

        She looked right at me and called me her replacement; said Dana would betray me too.

        That was when Dana asked what we wanted to do with Iris, implying she was willing to kill her. She pointed out Iris might know too much. She did however, she admitted still have a soft spot for Iris. She would not kill her personally. None of us, myself included, wanted to kill Iris. Tatiana and Dearbháil wound up taking her back to the city. She told them she was going back to Egypt.

        By the way, just speaking as a Catholic, I wish people would stop using this “Angel” and “Demon” terminology. These are not terms to throw around lightly. I prefer “Unchained” as Iris called herself, and I really think a renaming of this “Machine God” would be nice. Just, so we don’t get blasphemous without need. I realize not everyone is Catholic here.

        I digress. The rest of us returned to the museum. I think things were a little muted. I was distracted and did not remember the conversation well, but I think there was little. I wore the apron with the Statuettes. Dana held my hand.

        Sean was upset at the museum the Statuettes had been gone. Dana ignored him and Nathan sidetracked him. Thankfully Greta did not show up to bug us. I am going to sit her down, tell her they are connected to a super weapon that can destroy the world, and if she publishes that then she may be responsible for the death of mankind. See if that shuts the little bitch up.

        Dana focused her power and dispelled the storm. It did not go away immediately, but after a few hours Paris was clear again. We met Dearbháil and Tatiana at a café (I took the Statuettes with me in my apron again) and Dana began to explain her side. She said she discovered her own predecessor as Iris’s right hand was a handsome man who, like her, was an Archeologist without formal schooling. He had helped acquire an “Old Testament Artifact,” she discovered from a guide, and then immediately after giving it to Iris was murdered by assassins. Dana said she discovered assassins had come into the city but did not know their target. She did realize if she gave Iris the was she might be next, so fled. She was unsure how much Iris knew she knew. This was clearly not their last meeting, given Iris stole the was thereafter…they clearly have a complicated relationship.

        Marek informed us at lunch that he had been hunted by a man with silver bullets. We have little to go on. Marek killed him of course, but who he was and why he was after Marek was not known. He had a picture of Marek, so probably an assassin. What skeletons in the closet does Marek have from the war? Or maybe a jealous husband hired him.

        All that done, Dana and I retired back to the chateau. She spent the night in my room, my first partner to do so with me. She spent much of the time trying to reassure me about her feelings for me, and trying to be emotionally available. It’s hard for her.

        While we had breakfast this morning, she told me she hated she could not have children by me. I was not sure what to say. I still don’t know.


        29th April 1922, Afternoon.

        Wolfram’s bachelor party is tonight. Tatiana is planning it. I am very afraid. Dana, Dearbháil, and Alla are keeping the statues, while Tatiana, the boys as I go. At least, I presume Alla is staying…she is Sapphic as well, so one never knows.

        I wonder what Tatiana is planning…

        End Session XXV

        Answers that lead to more questions

        Lizette had to make a Clarity check for taking part in the kidnapping, but I gave her an extra die as she did not know it was a kidnapping till it started. She almost got an exceptional, so she was unfazed. The other two did not, having been mostly left out of those shenanigans, and as I say I am doing fewer such checks in this game than my others.

        Dacian was given a fairly simple Investigation roll to see how quickly he could find the package. It was not fast. He did it eventually. I was gonna have him compete with Shalice, but it did not seem like it would add to the story especially when his rolls were total shit, so I changed my mind.

        Next time is a fun all IC session: Wolfram’s bachelor party! Join us then! Comments and Questions welcome.

                                    Bachelor party! While technically two nights till the wedding, Walpurgis is the play, so the Conspiracy threw Wolfram a party early. Alla’s player had to miss, so they drug it out a bit into a long IC session with lots of fun. So enjoy five journals this time, including the first from Wolfram! Also, Tatiana will fight you.

                                    Session XXVI

                                    Marek’s Thoughts

                                    29 April 1922

                                    Today picked up where yesterday left off. Lizette looked at some of the silver bullets that I had saved from the would-be assassin from January. She used a gift to “read” it and get any impressions she could off of it. The way she described it was like it had eyes and she was looking through them at it’s past. She saw that the bullets were created by a grizzled man. From there it went to a city where it was stored for a while before being collected by my attacker. It wasn’t clear if the bullets had been specifically made for him or if he was just buying them off a known seller of silver weapons. The former could be used to kill me. They got far closer to completing that goal than I would generally like to admit. If it’s the later, then it’s disturbing in and of itself that there is someone out there that makes silver bullets as a part of his livelihood. Either way he is causing problems for me, and by extension, my pack. I do not think that there are any kinds of weapons that my friends are particularly susceptible to, save Tatiana. It might be a good idea to just flat out ask them.

                                    Well, today was also Wolfram’s bachelor party. Tatiana planned it, so you know that it had plenty of drinking and girls. Wolfram’s brother Heinrich came in addition to the pack. I believe that he knows that his brother is a Claimed, a sin-eater, and probably has a good idea by now that something is odd about the rest of us as well. So, to start the bachelor party off we all went to the sauna. The lot of us wore towels and we enjoyed the hot steam. Tatiana made things more interesting by wearing her towel around her waist like the guys did. So, we all relaxed and talked about what the plans were for the rest of the evening. There was also a good deal of discussion about fire and setting people on fire and landing a plane while it was on fire. The later adventure was Lizette’s. That should come as no surprise. Apparently she jumped into a plane that was on fire, threw the pilot out and proceeded to land the plane herself. All while it was on fire. Tatiana mentioned a time that she was protecting a lady’s honor and doused a man, who was making unwanted advances to said lady, with vodka and lit him on fire.

                                    Then to lighten things up the conversation moved on to teas. Apparently Lizette has an interest in making her own flavored teas. Of course, things couldn’t stay innocent for long and before long we were talking about pulling a tea heist to emulate the British who, according to Lizette, steal things from everyone else. I will admit that this whole conversation was a little surreal for me.                                                         Time to check in with the Golden Eagle Crew! I need to head this one off with some explanation to follow the journals a bit, as a lot focuses on Ghosts, being this covers the play. So the Uratha did the Siskur da ritual to allow the whole Conspiracy to interact with ghosts physically. The thing that makes this complicated is only a few of them can see or hear ghosts; Wolfram, being a Sin-Eater, can, and can use his Keystone (a flaming sword) under the influence of the Ritual to affect ghosts as well. Dacian can through the Darkling Contract of Shade and Spirit (but is not skilled at barehanded fighting). Marek can because of Bone Shadow Gifts. However, Alla, Tatiana (by far the best barehanded fighter), Dearbháil, and Lizette are essentially blind and deaf to them. I believe Wolfram may plan to get some Ceremonies to deal with this problem for next time. Dana, not being part of the Conspiracy, did not participate in the Ritual. So the play on Walpurgis Night 1922. Begin! Session XXVII Marek’s Thoughts 30 April 1922 Today was the day of the play. We started things off by calling down a Sacred Hunt on the play and our intentions to make sure that no rituals are completed. We included most everyone in the pack, so they would have the ability to see ghosts. Much like the last ritual I conducted, Lizette represented the ritual within the play and I snuck up on her and ran my claws along her throat. I did not carry my rifle this time since my gift does not allow me to shoot ghosts, only bare handed or melee attacks will work. That was fine by me. I’ve been practicing and have gotten better since the last time we called Siskur da. We arrived at the play early so we could position ourselves. Dacian immediately went up into the rafters above the stage and hid. Dearbhail, Fenric, and Dana all went to the audience area. Tatiana stationed herself in the orchestra pit. I went backstage and hid among the shadows. Wolfram and Lizette arrived shortly after the play started. Wolfram went to the audience while Lizette snuck backstage and made her way up to the catwalk. Shortly after the play started I noticed that Greta was around. I could not see her, but I could smell her. I let Alla , who was waiting for her scene to come up, know that Greta was hiding backstage. Alla tried to get Greta’s attention to no effect. Between the first and second acts I noticed that aura that accompanies Blackstaff’s gift when he wants to spy on an area come up. It had a slightly different feel this time, but it was the same gift. I climbed up to the catwalk to let Lizette know what was going on. I tried to find Dacian, but he was too well hidden. I just hoped that he had heard me talking to Lizette. Around that time I also spotted Greta and pointed her out to our slight mechanic. I figured all of this was important for the others to know, so I made my way to the audience seating discreetly and told Wolfram what was going on. My message passed along, I made my way back to the backstage area. During the second act I noticed two ghosts walking towards the stage from the back. It was a mother/daughter pair. They looked emaciated, like they’d died of starvation or possibly exposure. I thought that they might have been refugees. They spoke Greek. I stepped in their path and tried to make myself understood though we apparently did not share a language. I tried different languages until I hit on one that they had some familiarity with, Russian. I think I managed to get the word “stop” across to them. Then suddenly the aura of the power surrounding us changed and I was suddenly struck blind, deaf and mute. It was rather disconcerting to say the least. The only reason I didn’t become more upset was because of my wolf senses. I could still smell and that gave me an idea of where everything and everyone was. I indicated my eyes, ears and throat and made a slashing motion to tell the others that I was blind, deaf and dumb. I do not know if they saw me or not, but I think they did. That I will admit is an experience that I would rather not repeat. It reminded me a little too much of when a grenade went off too close to my trench. The sound was deafening and for a while all I could hear was ringing in my ears. I could not hear it when I tried to talk, nor when someone tried to talk to me. My squad and I were brought back to the medical tent for a while until our hearing returned. I was never so happy to hear a bird’s call until that day. As soon as I was struck by that power the ghosts made a dash for the stage. I managed to catch the mother ghost and Wolfram, who’d come backstage, grabbed the little girl. He tried to tell them to stop, and that he cannot let them continue. Then just as suddenly I was able to hear and see again. Maybe Blackstaff realized that the power wasn’t restricting me the way he’d hoped it would and decided to try something different. I will admit that it was good to be able to see with my eyes again. My sense of smell gave me a good idea of what was around me, but it doesn’t take the place of my eyes. Then, before we could do anything, we were all pulled through the floor of the stage. The ghosts were pulled with us. We were not alone. The other man there asked us what we were doing, so we told him. He said that he was not a friend and then seemed to disappear. It wasn’t until later that I realized that he was likely one of the vampires charged with keeping any powers from being used on the play or the actors. I guess he assumed that we were all a threat and pulled us beneath the stage. While we were down there Wolfram continued to talk to the mother and they eventually came to an accord. They would stop trying to influence the play and Wolfram would do something for them. After that was settled we found the stairs and climbed back up to the backstage area. We were greeted by the sight of two armed ghosts wearing bone armor and carrying maces. We figured that they were part of Blackstaff’s guards. The mother and daughter ghost went back below the stage to stay out of danger. The fight with the guards didn’t go too badly. It was especially unpleasant for Dacian, but he doesn’t remember what happened. That’s for the best. It started out with me grabbing one of the guards and Wolfrman materializing a sword and going after the other one. He wounded it, if it can be said that ghosts can be injured. After a relatively short time the one that I was holding got away and Greta went after him. She used one of her powers and grappled the ghost that I lost my grip on. The power was a little disturbing to watch. Suddenly she resembled a somewhat bloated corpse. Her skin was blue-ish and distended. I think even the humans saw that. They started to react and Tatiana got them out of the backstage area to safety. We continued the fight in private. One of the ghosts then decided that possessing one of us would be his best bet and went after Dacian. Upon possessing him, Dacian’s body deformed. His ribs came out and made claws for his hands. His spine lengthened and his skull was pushed upward. It looked rather painful to me. Tatiana went ahead and grabbed Dacian to ensure that the ghost could not harm anyone on or off stage. While she was grappling that one I attacked the one that Greta was holding. I shifted to Dalu form and attacked him with my claws. I managed a couple of hits on the ghost before it finally faded. It is not permanently dead, but it will be awhile before it is possible for him to come back to the waking world. Meanwhile I believe that Alla was trying to use one of her Vampire gifts to convince the ghost to leave Dacian’s body. I believe she was successful, as the ghost fled our friend and his body returned to normal leaving wounds as it did so. As it fled I tried to attack it, but did not manage to take it down or restrain it. Dearbhail came backstage at the intermission and we caught her up on all that had happened. She immediately healed a confused Dacian. While she was doing that Wolfram, Tatiana and I discussed going after Blackstaff before he got away. We had felt his area-wide power come down shortly after the last ghost fled. The problem we ran into was the area that he could affect with that gift is awfully large and there were only three of us. And of those three only the two of us could track by scent. In the end we decided that Tatiana, Greta and I would track Blackstaff. We headed out in a South by Southeast pattern. We caught a faint scent of him, but it ended. We assume that he got into a car and fled that way. We were told, while we searched for Blackstaff, that Alla did a very good job with her altered lines in the play. The audience didn’t know that anything had been changed. I believe that Connie and Agrippa did and Agrippa at least was not happy with it. From what he heard from Dearbhail those two plus Fenric disappeared from the audience for a while and reappeared at the end. My guess is that the same guy that pulled us through the floor of the backstage did something similar to Agrippa and her two companions. Perhaps they were trying to influence the play and were removed to keep their powers from working? Fenric was likely just caught up in the wrong place at the wrong time. He was asleep when he was returned to his seat. I would have been angry if he’d been returned in worse shape than when he “left,” but he was fine. After the play we discussed Wolfram’s wedding. It was decided that Marlena will be the maid of honor, and Heinrich will be the best man. Dacian and I will be the groomsman, and Dearbhail and Lizette will be the bridesmaids. Tatiana will be the ring-bearer. I believe that something was also discussed about Lizette building a “flower cannon” to replace the need for a flower girl. That should be interesting to say the least. I will admit that I was honored to be asked to be a groomsman. I missed my brother’s wedding two years ago when I was dealing with the situation with the orphans. Sometimes I feel like I have let my brother down because I was not there for him, but I’m not sure what else I could have done. Those children needed to be freed from those monsters and I was hired to do so. I was also in a position to help them after the Deuderman’s had been dealt with. Speaking of which, I received a letter from my brother a little while ago letting me know that Denisa and Erik were able to adopt both Kvĕta and Dominik. They are both growing well and seem to be happy with their new home. I am glad that I was able to help them find them a safe and caring place to live. I have been told that both children insist on calling me “Uncle Marek.” I guess I made an impression. As for Desnisa and Erik, I only met them the one time, but they seemed like good people. Andel has confirmed this with his letter I am glad to say. That said, I am looking forward to Wolfram and Christine’s wedding. They will finally be allowed to act like the mated pair that they are, though I’m sure they would find my choice of words uncouth. I am happy for them though. They make a good couple and will make good parents. I am looking forward to it all. Tatiana’s Conversations Hush volchonok. No, I have not caught the scent of this Blackstaff. Nor has Greta seen any ghosts. It is sad. I too wanted to fight. He hurt one of our pack, this is unacceptable. Yes, volchonok, it was he who did that to Dacian. What is that even? No, volchonok, he did not decide to become a were-skeleton, whatever that is. It is true, ribs do make better weapons than human claws, but we need them in the place they are usually. He was possessed by a spirit of the dead, one of Blackstaff’s ghosts. That is what did it. Not the small girl who called Wolfram names, I don’t believe, a different one. I agree. Roach head is an excellent insult. It was good of Greta to share what the child said. I do not know. I believe they are our friends now, the small girl ghost and her mother ghost. Even if the mother could not help find Blackstaff. Anyone who tells Wolfram that he eats mudpies and kisses roaches while he has them held up off the ground should be a friend. That? That was not ghosts. It was other vampires stole the vampires. And Fenric, but they gave him back. He was probably too small. Alla says Constancia is probably alright. Because we told the Prince that someone might try something foolish. He seems to favor this direct control. I will have words with him if he has harmed Constancia. Yes, volchonok, the words would be fighting. You are learning! I could not see these ghosts, so I could not fight. I had other things to do anyway, like scare the human away and keep Dacian from attacking anyone. Yes, I bit Dacian. It was the best way to keep the ghost from using his rib bones to stab one of us. He does not even remember. I didn’t actually hurt him, and Dearbháil kissed and made him better anyway. Uh... perhaps. I do not know if Alla’s very large hat helped her control the ghost. It was for the play not for ghosts. Foolish volchonok. Of course the play was perfect! We Hunted to make it so. Yes, volchonok, that means the Hunt is over. No this does not mean we can go run. Tomorrow I will be bearing the rings for Wolfram’s wedding - it is important. There will be time and time enough for running after we see to the happiness of our pack. Lizette’s Plans 30th April 1922 We prevented the play from becoming a disaster. It soon proved out of my league. First, I escorted Wolfram to see Prince Etienne and negotiate having Vampires make sure nothing vampiric and supernatural happened at the play. This lead to the Prince sending out his goon squad of Nosferatu Vampires who thoroughly intimidated all the vampires there, well except Alla who decided they were cool. They did overstep their bounds a little with Wolfram and Marek, but that did not prove too dangerous. The real problem was the ghosts. Marek, Dacian, and Wolfram were the only ones on our team who could see ghosts, and while we could all, say, punch them, Wolfram and Dacian are not known for their fisticuffs. I suppose one could say we should have foreseen the problem, but I am not sure what we could have done about it. Greta’s arrival helped, but she is a slip of a girl and not a skilled brawler either. I need to invent some sort of ghost goggles. That would be useful. I don’t even know where I would begin to work on that, though. It will take a lot of thought. Perhaps, some sort of box trap I can store ghosts in. Possibly to release on my enemies… Mayhem! Er, where was I… Dacian took position in the catwalks and rafters above stage with me, and quickly vanished from my view. Marek came and checked on us near the end of act one, and pointed out that Greta was there as well. Soon after act two began a pair of ghosts, mother and daughter I am told, tried to get in. Marek and Dacian intercepted, but after a short conversation I could not really understand, they tried to bolt. Marek grabbed the mother, while Wolfram got the little girl, coming from the audience to back stage just in time. That was when they were grabbed by the Nosferatu and pulled beneath the stage somehow, and Dacian, Marek and Tatiana went below with Greta. This was an error, as ghosts got in I could not see. Armored ones, apparently. When they got up there was a fierce brawl, and while initially Marek and grabbed one, they escaped. Greta managed to hold onto one Wolfram tore apart, but the other briefly possessed Dacian, turning him into an awful monster. [I]That[I] I could see. Fortunately Alla, who arrived at that point, turned her Vampiric charms toward influencing that ghost to leave Dacian. He fled, since Tatiana terrified him and Wolfram had destroyed his counterpart. Alla made her change in act three. It was over. We won. But none of that matters to me now. Tomorrow, Wolfram and Christina are getting married. I am feeling morose. Dana was waiting for me when I got home. She offered her companionship, and I do not want to sleep alone tonight. She has gone to get her clothes, a few items, and pick out a bottle of Champaign and perhaps port from the cellar. I’ve got two bottles of Cognac here. Dana hopes to cheer me up. With her wit, intelligence, beauty, attitude and mystery, she is an amazing woman. One I have known for a week. A week of laughter, crying, sex, and tears, but a week. I’ve been in love with Christina for two years. And if I am being honest with myself, probably Wolfram a chunk of that. Now there is something I won’t say out loud. How can she compete with that? I mean, I guess she does not have to. She has to cheer me up for a night. But my relationship with Christina is over, even as my my, hell, I don’t even know what to call it, sort of relationship with Dana has started? I have no idea what my future will be like. It won’t be like Wolfram and Christina’s, though. Changelings wonder if they have a soul. I know I do; mine hurts. Even mayhem feels hollow right now. I should be fair to Dana. She is going to do her best to take care of me the next few days. She promised. She wants to prove herself, prove Iris a liar. That will take a long time. Even a week of taking care of me, while a mewl like a child, won’t do it. But why should she? Here Dana is opening her heart to me and I am whining about another woman and man who have been engaged to each other for nearly two years, most of the time I have known them. Why on earth should Dana even put up with or speak to me? She has shared her body with me, her weaknesses, and her private thoughts, and all I am thinking about is this other woman who never put me first. I am disgusting. I am awful. She would do better to spit on me and walk away, than have her care so roundly ignored. Okay, I washed my face. Dana would be upset if she knew I wept while she was choosing drinks. I must be the strong woman she fell in love with. Is in love with. Does she have a Wolfram like Christina does? A second partner she loves more than I? I do not know. I only know she has others, and she does not care to discuss or compare. Seems like an attitude she would only have with a main partner somewhere else. She is probably in love with Marek. I am insane. Paranoid. I mean, she is probably hot for Marek, because everyone is fucking hot for Marek. God, fucking Marek. Even Wolfram is probably hot for fucking Marek. But Marek is not the one marrying Christina, or Wolfram, tomorrow. And I can’t hate either of them, and the only one to hate is myself, the little misshapen green freak of nature. I’ve become very good at hating myself. It takes little talent or work to be able to hate a freak like me. What do I even offer anyway, but a lifetime of embarrassment at being seen in public with me, anyway? I don’t see why -- Dana dropped off the bottles, and stepped out to change into “Something a little more comfortable.” That woman is going to do everything in her power to make me feel better. I should appreciate it. I should soak my mind in alcohol and bury my face between her breasts, and maybe, just maybe, forget what is going to happen tomorrow for a while. I hope. End Session XXVII Tatiana will fight you! (wait, wrong session) At the beginning of the session Alla’s player noted Alla was going to wear the biggest, most magnificent hat they had in the theater. I am sure it helped. House Machten made their presence known this session. There was an amusing scene where Alla was lured into a room by herself and given a stern warning no breaches of the Masquerade would be tolerated. Hilariously, she found their intimidating manner amusing, and started complimenting them on how “awesomely scary” they were. They did not reply. Similarly, it was them who pulled people under the stage later, and intimidated off Agrippa and Constancia (due to Wolfram’s meeting the Prince). The child was a source of amusement as she seemed to constantly insult Wolfram, with bug or roach combined with eater, kisser, or head. Only Wolfram (and Greta) could understand her as she was yelling in Greek, but the group was amused overall. Blackstaff started with Stigmata Boneyard to empower his ghosts, but when Wolfram and Marek prevented the two from possessing anyone with muscle and social approach, he shifted to Stillness to try to stop them and back up his more martial members. He got Marek once, but Werewolves are barely inhibited by the limitations of the Stillness Boneyard, and neither he nor Tatiana were slowed down, and he could not land the effect on Wolfram.
