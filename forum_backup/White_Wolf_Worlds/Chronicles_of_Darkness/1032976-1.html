                Attention, Cover-Up and Turmoil
                Doing atrocious things get you noticed in a negative way.

                If Attention ever exceeds Cover-Up gain Surveilled Condition

                Attention = Temporary Atrocity + Permanent Atrocity

                Cover-Up is a statistic that represents the effectiveness of concealing one's monstrous nature and activities.

                It is capped by the character's Stability Advantage + local Turmoil rating. A Character's Cover-Up is the amount of Attention they can withstand before they suffer the Surveilled or Hunted Conditions.

                Cover-up = Total of Merits Dots used to conceal monstrous nature + lowest Mental or Social Attribute

                Player needs to describe how these merits contribute to their Cover-Up:

                Potentially Applicable Merits:
                Allies
                Alternate Identity
                Anonymity
                Fame
                Mentor
                Mundane Status
                Resources
                Retainers
                Safe Place

                Turmoil
                It is an unfortunate fact some places and times where Atrocities are common enough that pin pointing a specific monster committing Atrocities. This is known as Turmoil. It is rated 0-5 for the place and certain ongoing-circumstances can raise or lower Turmoil while they happen (riots, wars, and crime sprees can increase it, while gentrification, curfews, and increased law enforcement presence can lower it). Major cities with some violent crime reputation range 1 or 2 Turmoil, towns where a single murder would be shocking would be a great example of 0 Turmoil, and areas filled with genocide, thousands of brutal murders annually and warlords constantly battling each other would be examples of 5 Turmoil.

                Ephemeral Entities may use Merits of corporeal beings over which it has Manifestations condition for their Cover-up, but are still capped by Stability Advantage + local Turmoil rating.

                Example of calculation:
                Vinny, the Mortal Mafia Tough Guy
                Permanent Atrocity: 0 (as a non-template Vinny can't acquire Permanent Atrocity)

                Temporary Atrocity: 2 (Vinny does a lot of bad things but also regularly dumps his Temporary Atrocity into his Support relationship such as his battered girl friend, his long suffering mother, and other relationships that he has lost over the years. He refrains from dumping Temporary Atrocity into some relationships such as his best friend and co-knee-breaker Joe, or his mafia boss Bruno.)

                Integrity: 4 (Integrity is the number of Support the character retains. Vinny has lost several Support relationships because of his habit of dumping his Temporary Atrocity into them, as well as the Hurt caused by the breaking points he violates. His mother, girlfriend, best friend and mafia boss are his 4 remaining Supports.)

                Turmoil: 2 (Vinny works an operates in Chicago IL, and it is not currently going through either abnormally strict law enforcement or riots but is a major city known for violence)

                Intelligence: 1 (Vinny isn't particularly bright, and Intelligence is his lowest Mental or Social stat.)

                Merits: Status: Mafia 2 and Alternate Identity 1 (Vinny his standing in the Mafia and his Alias of "Mr. Gray" make sure people don't connect his work to his home life.)

                This adds up to a Cover-Up of 4 with potential maximum of 6, and an Attention of 2. This leaves Vinny vulnerable to gaining the Surveilled Condition by those looking for monstrous activity were he to commit an Atrocity that would be worth 3 or more Temporary Atrocity. Vinny would do well to drop more Atrocity before coming another Atrocity, but he risks losing more Support, thus Integrity. This is the spiral that leads to becoming a Slasher.

                What's the line between monsters, brutal hitmen or serial killers you ask? Very little when you're investigating to find them.

                Most mundane people have a Cover-Up of 2, (Having 'average' as their lowest Mental or Social Attribute, and no merits specifically applied to conceal Atrocity incurring acts) Strangely people with Mental or Social deficiency, thus a Social or Mental Attribute of 1, are more likely to dedicate merits for hypothetical or actual Atrocity incurring acts, thus raising their average Cover-Up. This is likely caused by emotionally or mentally maladjusted individuals being the most likely to resort to Atrocity. Did this just justify some players' tenancy to make min-maxxed combat-monkeys? Yes, with the caveat that it now comes with mechanical disadvantages, additional point costs, and means they cop to playing an "emotionally or mentally maladjusted individual" for a character.

                        City Name: Iron Ridge
                        Mood: Drug-Laced Energy
                        City Theme: Over Indulgence
                        Vice: Licentiousness
                        Virtue: Ingenuity
                        Population: 200,000~
                        Top Supernatural Type: Geist
                        City Merits: Striking Looks 2, Holistic Awareness, Fame 1, Pusher, Spin Doctor, Taste, Sin City*, The Beautiful People *, Surreal*

                        Architecture Styles: Spanish And Mediterranean
                        City Assets: Cultural exports and Tourism
                        City Districts: Lower Shrum, Lower West Peelrald, Lit Vale, Dellak Market, Little Yupan
                        Key Locations: Shelton Beach Front, Ivory Pinnacle Resort, Angelwing Park
                        City Description: The City is filled with Neon Hues and lights from tike torches. The beachfront and boardwalk ad the most well-kept part of the city to the point you will find many people walking around barefoot in the area with not a care in the world. Past the boardwalk near Angelwing park is where the real face of the city starts to show itself. Drug dealers, addicts, hookers and more. Even here though urban blight is given a pretty mask.
                        City Bio:/History: A city born on the west coast of the United States during the 1800's by Mexico and became a part of the United States during the 1850's. The City itself kept its Spanish/Mediterranean vibe and becoming a party city for the young seeking to let loose. The Main Attraction of the city is Shelton Beach front which Stretches 4 miles. The Beach itself has some of the best waves on the cost. Neon and Tike Torches dot the beachfront while a more postmodern look has taken the city center. All this natural beauty of lively vibes masks a serious drug problem the city has as young people are always found around the city overdosing on various new drugs that are shipped in from Mexico. The city itself has fallen on many hard times but the ingenuity of the people here always pull it out. The current Mayor of the city Sarah C. Ellison seeks to make the city an even more popular by bringing more of the rich and famous to the city by restoring even more of the natural beauty of the land.

                        Radio Stations: Laughing Lizards, WKSI-FM RADIO, WTAL Radio 1450 AM

                        Gangs: The Fallen Angels, Royal Serpents, Men Of The Night, The Soulless
                        Criminal Organizations: The Mortuus Fratrum, The Wildlings

                        *Sin City: The City itself is always encouraging you to sin. Drugs, Drinks, and Sex are all within easy reach and are even offered. People in the city gain back an extra point of willpower when giving into the sin the city encourages the most. It allows lowers the resource dot cost for items that enable the sin.

                        *The Beautiful People: The People of the city are quite strange and eclectic that it makes it hard to pick out the strange looking supernaturals in the city. When trying to spot a supernatural take a -1 per level of the merit.

                        *Surreal: Sometimes a city's vibe is so strong it can change your perception of the world. What was normal is now strange and what's strange is now normal. This merit makes it harder for people to notice the use of supernatural powers or effects at work around them. When rolling for powers being noticed the player gets a bonus to keep their powers hidden per level of this merit.
