                                                            Well, I can happily assure you that the purpose I had in mind for Chain Rage was not to patch any holes. Uratha will not be constantly Kuruthing every night and I'm not really sure why you'd think they would be.

                                                            Anyway, the purpose of Chain Rage is to offer a trade-off option. On the one hand, it offers a solid second line of protection should Kuruth occur, giving werewolves a solid chance of avoiding entering full Death Rage. However, it does this in return for two things. The first is the certainty of full-on Death Rage at the end of the month. This is supposed to be something that, of course, packs who use Chain Rage will plan around and keep an eye on; you probably want to have some sort of secure place to lock the chap into when it comes to the end of their month under the rite, then let them loose somewhere they can't destroy anything. This, er, may be problematic if they have certain Facets, of course. The second trade-off, though, is the fact you have to have the focus on you. This is a vulnerability - if that focus gets taken or destroyed, boom, death rage. Again, I'd expect packs to do everything they can to protect their rage foci as much as possible, but that doesn't mean the risk isn't there. Canny enemies and pure bad luck can both cause a serious back-fire here.
