    Carnivores

    03-08-2015, 04:30 PM
    Strictly speaking, what makes a creature carnivorous enough to be viable to sate Primal Urge restrictions? For instance, most birds will count insects as part of their diets, so could an uratha be properly nourished by a chicken parm?

      From what I've read, carnivores come in two types: mesocarnivores and hypercarnivores. Mesocarnivores have 50-70% of their diet as meat, while hypercarnviores have a diet consisting of 70%+. From what I can tell, dogs fall into the mesocarnivore group, so your typical Uratha should be aiming to eat creatures around that same amount, to sate their wolfish appetites. If I were an Uratha myself, I would go for creatures that eat 65-70% for my diet, as werewolves appear to have stronger hunting instincts than natural wolves do due to their spiritual natures.
