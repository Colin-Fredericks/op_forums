                                Parties move following agenda’s throughout the city. Alla’s old friend needs her help, and concerns continue to arise with the statuettes. Marek’s player had to miss due to holidays, so no journal for Marek and Marek is unaccounted for.

                                I kept the usual order, but you may want to read Lizette’s first to get some context for Tatiana. Marek tends to have the most complete retelling so Lizette and Tatiana both tried to step up their game a little to cover it.

                                Session XX

                                Tatiana’s Conversations

                                Well, volchonok, I imagine he asked me if I needed a hospital before he asked if I wanted to be his mate because he didn’t want to mate with someone who was injured.

                                What? No, we were not at a hospital already, we were at a den for many humans.

                                Ah, I see. They were poisoned by their own choice, and only ill of the mind.

                                I - yes, I suppose, like with the scotch and the cigars.

                                You are correct, volchonok, the woman with the knife smelled something like scotch. And... other things.

                                I would have said anger, not sadness, but I was the one spit on, not you. Her mate left her for another.

                                Yes, that is why she called me Claudette.

                                I do not believe she could hear or smell anything beyond her own noise and stink, volchonok, much less four men and a vampire.

                                Truly? No. Since this Emilie was to be Persephone in the play, once I smelled a somewhat familiar woman vampire smell, I was certain it was Agrippa. I did hope someone there might be helpful still.

                                ... Yes, the man who wanted to mate with me tried to be helpful, once I convinced him I was neither missionary nor police, and once Dearbhail sobered him up.

                                Why do you ask so many questions about this?

                                Oh you are ignoring me now? Wolfram did say it was helpful, that it pointed to the Circle of the Crone, if they were looking for not-Christians. I already knew it was Agrippa though. And it was really the other woman, the friend of the fairy, who said she was not Christian.

                                Wolfram was not there because he was looking at the statues, remember, volchonok?

                                I don’t know, I wasn’t paying attention. Something about being pieces of something bigger and missing a power source?

                                I know, boring. He did say someone removed and replaced the glass to look at them, so Dana has them in a bag.

                                You are correct volchonok, it does not make sense. If you can get to them, and want them, why leave them?

                                Perhaps. Dana did say they were cursed by the vampires, so perhaps they could see such things as well. Greta said they left someone to watch, so perhaps they want to know more about us.

                                Of course you saw Greta, you are sometimes almost as clever as she.

                                To follow the people following us. It was they who moved the glass and also they that Marek and I followed last night. So says Greta at least.

                                Well, volchonok, hopefully she will tell us.

                                We are not following with her because we are to speak with Agrippa.

                                No, silly volchonok, our songbird is not to come with. She speaks to her Queen.

                                It is like an alpha, yes, but of a time and place, not a pack.

                                Because a German fairy Queen is visiting and her house got burned down.

                                Yes, that is why Dacian made such strange faces. He was quite displeased.

                                Indeed, the fairy at the den of poisoned humans told us. Told Dearbháil at least.

                                She is friends of the actress. She was quite upset at how disturbed the room had been, what with everything having been strewn all around.

                                No, volchonok, she does not live in that den. Why is that important?

                                No, I would not want to live there.

                                Well, for one, I would prefer to go home without walking past a woman painting dead children, or a pair mating under the stairs, or a neighbor who yells about Armand.

                                Even as a human, I would rather have lived outside, even in a storm such as this, than in close quarters such as that.

                                You are a clever volchonok. No, I do not wish to run off into the night and away from such foolishness. We have a better den, and a job to do. We will run soon enough.


                                Lizette’s Plans

                                25th April 1922

                                Today has not been too eventful so far.

                                After brunch, we went to the library. Dana noted someone had been in there, but nothing major had happened. She left the glass very slightly out of place and they had moved and replaced it correctly. Wolfram, Dacian and I looked over the Statuettes and all came to the conclusion they interlocked somehow, but we need Dana’s was in order to make more sense of it. That or travel to Egypt. Perhaps they need a power source. My Contracts showed me the face of the one who entered, but not much with more information. Like with the Necklace, it was not someone I knew. We don’t have much solid to go on with, but I have a much better feel for what they may do. And with Wolfram and Dacian looking them over and backing me up, I feel pretty good about it.

                                There is a lot of potential in these devices. Just the technology to construct such a thing would be advanced, with different sorts of metals and wiring like I have never seen. We don’t know what they do, but it is likely advanced and outside our idea of what is possible; we have seen the powers of the Eagle. Imagine what could be done with this! Such advanced technology could probably build us some amazing weaponry, such as planes that have all sorts of advanced weapon system and can withstand tremendous speeds, or aereocars, or tanks the likes we have never seen! Such advanced weaponry could be a huge amount of fun to make!

                                Mayhem!

                                Of course, super weapons by such advanced technology could be why that technology is no longer around. Too much mayhem?

                                Meanwhile, Alla, Dearbháil and Tatiana got involved in a different investigation. It seems like the Persephone from Harlow’s play, a woman named Emilie Barteau, had vanished. Harlow enlisted the aid of the girls to investigate the apartments in the Bohemian part of town she was staying at, and found she was missing, her apartment tossed, though part of it may have been clutter according to Dearbháil. I am given to understand her neighbors were eccentric. Tatiana was fairly sure Agrippa had been there, though the scent was faint, which I can only assume made Dearbháil even more paranoid.

                                They were stopped by Lucille Nadeau, a girl who prowls the Salons often for ladies, and Thérèse, a local Spring Courtier. They were there to pick Emilie up for Salon. Lucille apparently dropped giant hints that Emilie was pagan, which is unfortunate, and that the people who were looking for her were asking about non-Christians, though I am given to understand that is a bit of a problem in the Bohemian districts right now so Emilie was surely not the only one. Still, Alla and Tatiana plan to speak to Agrippa later, and it will be dealt with.

                                Also, Skadi, the Winter Queen from Berlin is in town, and had the rooms she was staying at set aflame. Fire is effective. She escaped, but must have been upset. She has been relocated. I knew the Winter Queen was in town, but I don’t have time or a mind for politics so did not care. I know some good Germans, so I am less biased against her than some of my countrymen understandably are. Dearbháil and Dacian will be checking with the Spring Queen tonight. They intend to help her, which makes sense. I will back them up as I can.

                                But I am getting ahead of myself. We all met after working at the museum and investigating the apartments, and took Nathan and Dana out to eat. Dana has started doting on me, patting my head and scratching my hair behind my ear. She and I often make eye contact and smile, and that is getting more frequent. Dana was up front that ours was not romantic, but would friendly, physical, and need not end after a single encounter. That is certainly true, as Dana has been flirty and promised to continue our liaison in private tonight. But I worry that one, or both, of us may have fallen for the other. Dana is not a woman given to long-term relationships, she has made that very clear twice now. But how will we be walking away from this? As friends? Intimate friends? Am I too close to the…events…with Christina to really let myself honestly do this? Would someone like Clara be better for me? All the time in the workshop does not help me know how to handle this. I must think.

                                Oh yes, dinner. So we hashed out a general plan at Dinner. Wolfram and I would stay with Nathan and Dana to guard the statuettes. Dacian and Dearbháil will be talking to the Queen and Tatiana and Alla Agrippa as I say. Greta was lurking hidden at the restaurant, so Wolfram approached her, but she was actually lying in wait for our pursuers. She had seen the ones breaking into the museum, and ambushed the guard they left. They are operating under orders and not staying in the same place. A “master” of some sort is coming, hence their scouting. She does not know what they are up to, but plans to try to get more information. She agreed to share. Her conversation with Wolfram seemed more civil than the one in the rain I understand.

                                Back at the hotel, Dana explained to Wolfram, Tatiana and I about her plan with her was. A package from her, in a box the rectangular shape for a was, will be coming from London. This is a decoy. A package for a Ms. Boyles will be coming from Cardiff, which was square shaped and has the actual was. She expects them in 48 hours, give or take a day. Is it any wonder I hero worship her a little? I hope to be as capable as her someday. I digress; tomorrow she does research, and in the meantime is the waiting game, at least for this project. Tatiana left with Alla, so we are left here to talk.

                                The rain is continuing; the storm Dana predicted has not passed. It is good to be inside with a lovely lady. And Wolfram. Stay on task, brain…we have a lot ahead of us.

                                End Session XX

                                Man I had fun with the apartment complex. I had a wide range of encounters ready, and Tatiana mentioned most of them in her journal. The woman painting dead children droning happily on about how “all children die” eventually leading to her miscarriages having people to play with was especially disturbing, and after meeting the woman calling Tatiana Claudette and screaming about Armand I kinda think they just wanted to turn rocks over and see what kind of people they found. There was also high guitar guy, but he was less colorful.

                                Tatiana detected a vampire, but vampires have lighter harder to follow scents, so it was hard to say who, and the storm did well for obliterating scents.

                                We have hit on most plots now, the Statuettes, the Play, the visiting Queen…only took three sessions, right? I don’t expect this story to take as long as Vienna (that got extended by about 2-3 extra sessions by circumstance) but still should be fun.

                                Not covered was the meeting with the Queen of Flowers and Lights. Any idea where I can get a good picture of a woman who appears to be made of stained glass? Basically they got more info about how she had been in the chateau commonly used by the four monarchs for guests, that someone had broken into the room while she was asleep, and left accelerant there, but did not harm her. They were waiting for Court to finish so they could accompany the Queen to speak to her at the Queen’s home. With any luck we will get a Dacian next time to cover that.

                                Oddly ominous/tragic lines included the Queen of Flowers and Lights speculating that in a couple decades everyone will associate Berlin with music, theater, and cinema, and that she would not see Germany as a military power again in her lifetime.

                                I think that covers everything. Full crew for the next one, where we spend much time on the missing actress and burned room. Comments and Questions welcome.

                                                        A casual word from a Vampire and distrust begins to spread between the Conspiracy and the Archaeologists they are there to guard. Meanwhile there is an actress to find, and a queen that needs help.

                                                        I recap what is going on after the session journals in simple terms. It has session 21 spoilers, but if things are convoluted for you it may help.

                                                        Session XXI

                                                        Marek’s Thoughts

                                                        26 April 1922

                                                        Yesterday seemed to be the day that just wouldn’t end. I left the pack for a short time to talk to a messenger sent by Jamal. I had to meet him outside of Paris since he could not be seen there for various reasons. I didn’t ask. It doesn’t really matter anyway. Turns out I ran all that way just to find out that Jamal did not have any new information on my would be assassin, but he was still looking into it for me. I thanked the man for his time and we went our separate ways.

                                                        Some of us, Tatiana, Alla and I, went to talk to Agrippa to talk to her about the missing actress. There was some concern that she might have taken offense at something in the play and encouraged the young lady to leave. We did find out some information so the trip was not wasted. Agrippa also expressed her concerns about Dana and activating the artifacts. She pointed out that we know next to nothing about the archaeologist and what hints we do have just paint her in a more ambiguous light. All valid points in my opinion.

                                                        After that we came back to the hotel to talk to Wolfram, Lizette, Nathan and Dana. We mentioned Agrippa’s concern that Dana wants to activate the artifacts in a populated city without first knowing what the artifacts do. They could be destructive. We don’t know. We proposed using the Wās outside of Paris at our Chateau. It is a safe location and deliberately far away from other people. Dana wasn’t keen on that idea. She said that it would put her at a disadvantage. I would have preferred to be able to tell her to live with it, but I had to speak on the behalf of the pack, not just myself. It was then suggested that Nathan rent a cottage outside of Paris. It would be neutral ground and we would provide the security.

                                                        I believe that Dana’s Wās was being shipped to a nearby place and a another more “Wās-shaped” package was being delivered to the hotel. The actual Wās will be in the non-descript package though. Seemed like a decent security measure to take when you are being watched. Here’s hoping that it actually works.

                                                        Meanwhile Dacian and Dearbhail went to go meet with the German Winter Queen, Skadi. The purpose of their visit was to offer their assistance in promoting her message of peace and goodwill between Nations and Courts. After the war there are still hard feelings between some nations. I can understand that. Before I became a courier I was not overly fond of Germans, but after working with several over the year I lost the blinders that the war put in place and discovered that they were just people living their lives.

                                                        Dearbhail and Dacian also discussed the security for Skadi since the fire. From what they said the fire was set from inside the room while Skadi was asleep. An accelerant was used to light the fire and she was kept asleep by some sort of Fae magic while they did so. They then allowed her to wake up once the fire was lit. So, it is likely that they were not out to kill her, they just wanted to scare her. If they wanted her dead then they would have done it while in her room as she slept and used the fire to cover up the evidence of murder.

                                                        Dearbhail apparently was rather adamant that Skadi have a guard and offered Dacian’s services as a bodyguard/knight to keep watch over her. I’m sure that conversation was amusing to watch. When Dearbhail gets an idea in her head she usually finds some way to make it happen. And this time it sounded like most of what happened was her idea and Dacian got caught up in it. Not that I think he opposed the idea too strenuously, but I’ll bet it was still amusing to watch.

                                                        Now to look for our missing actress.

                                                        **** **** ****

                                                        Very early this morning we started our search for the missing actress. Unfortunately for us the weather did not cooperate and it was rainy and somewhat miserable out. That storm that Dana was talking about before seems to have found us, literally. So, Tatiana, Alla, Connie and I started looking the last place she’d been seen “getting out” of a car. More like jumping out of a moving car. It was near a Cathedral called Sacré-Coeur. As soon as we pulled over and stepped out of the car I felt like we were being watched. Tatiana and I exchanged looks and I nodded. I slipped into the shadows and crept toward the Cathedral so I could get a good vantage point and see if I could find our watcher. I climbed to the top most point, even went outside, and the whole time the feeling persisted. It followed me the whole way. Once at the top I looked around but did not see anyone. Discouraged I crept back down to the main part of the church and met up with the others. I told them what I’d discovered, or the lack thereof. While I’d been looking upstairs Tatiana had tracked Emilie sent to a certain pew, where it appears that she slept, but left again.

                                                        As we left the area Tatiana used a gift to see if what we’d sensed was supernatural or not. Turns out that it was. It was some power that affected the whole area and we could actually feel it when we left the area of influence. It was rather disconcerting if you ask me. Tatiana said that the energy felt necrotic and emotional at the same time. Necrotic makes me think of Vampires but I don’t know if something like this is within their purview. I’ll have to ask Alla and see if she knows. The only other supernatural entity that might give off death energy might be Sin-Eaters. Wolfram for example actually died and came back, joined with a spirit. The same happened to Greta. They can see and interact with any ghosts that they see as well. I will have to ask Wolfram if his kind have any powers that can affect a whole area rather than just an individual.

                                                        After the search at the Cathedral turned up nothing of import we decided to go back to the actress’s apartment and see if anything new could be discovered. As it would have it, not much. A drunk woman in the hall had not seen Emilie and her apartment had not been returned to. Her scent there was not fresh. Our next option was to talk to Lucille and Terese, two of Emilie’s friends and see if they have seen the wayward actress.

                                                        **** **** ****

                                                        After some time to rest, the pack met up with Connie, Dana and Nathan to discuss all that we’d been up to the past day and anything that we’d discovered. Wolfram confirmed that the power we described at the cathedral can be utilized by someone of his kind with the correct gifts. We also agreed that it would be a good idea for us to meet with Skadi and see if there was anything further that we could do. Dearbhail had offered our packs services to the Lady and it seemed only appropriate that we meet with her. When the idea was brought up Dana was very keen on going to the meeting. I’m not sure why. Perhaps she has not had an opportunity to socialize with a large group of Fae, or perhaps it’s the idea of meeting Fae royalty. Though Dana does not seem to be the type to be awed by the idea of meeting someone of noble birth. She seems rather accustomed to dealing with all manner of folks regardless of status. It is probably just curiosity but I will keep an eye on her.

                                                        After some time Alla received a phone call from Harlow, the man that is putting on the play that she is in and that is missing an actress. He said that the theater had been ransacked and that several scripts and such were missing. Alla’s theory was that it was the four men that had followed Emilie. Perhaps they don’t like the revisions that Harlow made to the story. He found the book that he got the story from in Greece, so it might be too close to a truth that someone wants kept quiet. So, Harlow asked Alla what he should do. Her suggestion was that he take the copy of the script and the original book to an occultist to make sure that there weren’t actually any hidden spells in the text. She recommended Wolfram and had Fenric arrange a meeting for later afternoon/early evening. Harlow also asked if Wolfram could look into the break-in at the theater.

                                                        My guess is that Harlow came across something that he shouldn’t have and decided to share what he found in the most public way possible. Someone obviously doesn’t want this to get out and is taking measures to scare off actresses and get rid of the means to perform the play altogether. So far they have elected to not use more lethal means to make sure this play is not performed, which is probably for the best. I guess we’ll see what the future holds on that front.

                                                        Later that afternoon we as a pack went to meet with Queen Skadi and the Queen of Flowers and Light. I believe she is the Spring Court monarch. Somewhat disturbingly one of the other Fae there, someone aptly named Mrs. Skitters, asked my Fae pack mates if she could have their faces after they died.
