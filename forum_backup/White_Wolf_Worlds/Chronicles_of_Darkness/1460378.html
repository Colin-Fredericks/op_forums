    Conspiracy Theories/Vigil Ascended- When Hunters Win

    09-18-2021, 06:42 PM
    One of the things I generally like in media is over complex, cosmic, occult plans with symbolic meanings where through a sequence of events and strange actions one could change the world- think about Father's plan in Full Metal Alchemist, SEELE's in Evangelion, the Headhunter's arc in Pandora Hearts or Allister's plan in A Certain Something Something. So yeah, it seems like it is a anime trope, but I am an anime fan, so it shouldn't come as a surprise, isn't it?

    Anyway, considering that fact, I've always found the concept of a Tier 4 game pretty awesome- archamsters, for example, are all about such cosmic, manipulative plans and symbolism, and Mage is the most obvious example for a Tier 4 game, even if it is controversial for it delving too deep into the mysteries of the setting, giving too much power to the players or just being a difficult experience to run. Anyway, while Mage introduced the concept, people have of course thought about how to apply it to other gamelines- and as a Hunter fan, it would make sense that I would think about a similar Hunter experience- yet unlike other gamelines, Hunter is about regular mortals who dare to fight against the darkness. In such a case, having them "evolve" into a world manipulating godlike entity would be out of theme- so how would you have a godlike reality manipulation without having a godlike reality manipulator?

    Like always- the answer is replacing power with numbers.

    Like Hillel the Elder once said, a single candle can light a thousand more without diminishing itself. Hunter is not a game about gods, but a game about lone people who band together to take back the night. In if having the world as a flawed existence is the will of the gods, it is up to humans to rise up and challenge their rule. Instead of manipulating reality through raw force, hunters who want to change the world and bring an end to the Vigil once and for all should act slowly, planning centuries ahead as they collect the necessary components, wait for the right events, build their numbers and eventually, when ready, challenge the world in a single opportunity which will never return to reach out, grasp at heaven and force the world to change. They have only one shot, so they better be careful when using it. After all, conspiracies are like they are called- conspiracies. It makes sense they would have an agenda they builds towards to.

    In here, we are going to treat the 4th Tier game for Hunter not through the lenses of an "evolved splat", but through a series of sequences which should lead towards a world changing event- so it is more like a SAS than anything else- a formulated scenario which represents the true agenda of a conspiracy, and how they may achieve their own "perfect world". I have originally presented the concept and how it applies in my AKD fan book, where I called it "Vigil Ascended", but it is a temporary name (I also consider "Conspiracy Theory", but it could be that the first term would refer to a case where the conspiracy's true intentions are "good" and the later in case those intentions are "evil). As such, I will refer to the AKD version of this scenario as an example, but I will supply other examples in order to show how other organizations may work to achieve such goals.

    Now, before we start, two notes- first, while I personally treat it as a "conspiracy territory" (naturally, as conspiracies are the big and widespread vigilante organizations), nothing stops you from applying the same formalism to compacts. Now, compacts are smaller in reach and generally have either a more localized, down to earth or abstract goals and myth, as well as being much more short lived and lack cohesivity and hierarchy. As such, them making, following and achieving such a cosmic plan makes much less sense, but it may work for certain compacts (especially those who are either on the verge of being a conspiracy, or which really look like a conspiracy yet aren't). The other note is that you may feel that using the organization rules from Geist and Deviant, or even the cult rules from Mummy, would make sense and be useful when trying to achieve those scenarios- and in that case, I totally agree with you. Personally, I think that conspiracies should have a standing of at least 6, but actually writing every conspiracy within this framework is a huge project which may be a STV product on its own right.

    So with that cleared, let's start detailing the Vigil Ascends formalism.

    Step 0- Blueprints
    In order to build such scenario, it is best to start not from the beginning but from the end. You should ask yourself "what this conspiracy tries to accomplish?". Like, if those hunters were to win, how the world without the Vigil would look like? What is their aspiration? Their vision? Their dream world? For the common hunter it would be simple- a world without monsters. but we are not talking about the "common hunter". We are talking about complex organizations, most of which are incredibly old, which have a specific vision for how the world should be. For example, the AKD follow a vendetta for being banished from Eden and losing their magic, and as such a world where they win is a world where they reclaim their Paradise Lost and unlock the lost art of artifice, creating a platonic golden age. The Lucifuge, however, seek to redeem themselves for the sins of their blood and the touch of Lucifer- and as all evil comes from the Devil (at least the evil they care about), redeeming the fallen angel should bring an end to the Inferno and cleanse their bloodline. The KoSG, however, fear magic in all of its forms, and care little for other monsters- so a world with no magic and where the Faceless Angels remain asleep for all eternity should be the goal they wish to achieve.

    Beside detailing the goal of the conspiracy, you should also detail what the themes of the scenario should be, drawing upon the mythology, symbolism and paradigm of the organization. After all, this scenario should be about having the hints all along- having the Lucifuge suddenly fighting aliens would be out of theme (unless you follow that one stupid movie where they apparently had only a bad alien doll as a prop), and the Les Mysteres building a robot army would make a little sense. One of the strongest themes you could and should draw upon are the Endowments which the conspiracy grants to tis followers, as they are a representation of the conspiracy's knowledge, resources and influence. For example, the AKD deal with relics, have a strong interest in magic, and have oaths, labyrinths and search after ancients wonders as part of their themes, not to mention severed heads and the myth of Asterion and the Mistress. As such, their scenario should take those concepts and work them together into a single story. Cheiron, on the other hand, have themes of trade and medicine, and their Endowments are about organ transplant and biological manipulation and engineering, as well as strong connection to Greece and their strange logo. TFV has vampires (which is controversial), but could be tied nicely with their themes of Germanic paganism and them as the Choosers of the Slain (with the Slain referring to bloodsuckers), which leads to elements of Ragnarok- and of course, the concept of technology and human will. Similarly, the MM would use Catholicism as a paradigm, with the KoSG will apply geotic concepts together with Anglicanism. And once you define what the conspiracy wants to do and the system they wish to use to achieve it, you can move to the first actual step in the scenario. Also, consider how the different factions within he conspiracy may play a role in the scenario, and how they contribute to achieving Sovereignty.

    Step 1- Catalyst
    The Catalyst should answer a single question- why did the conspiracy waited until now in order to achieve its plans? What took it so long to start its manipulation of heaven and Earth? What the hell they were waiting for all this time? After all, if the conspiracy could have initiated the scenario, they would have started already centuries ago- but they waited for a very specific moment, when the conditions would be just right in order to start their plans. After all, hunters are only humans, and the attempt at Sovereignty is a gamble which, if failed, could bring the end of the organization and perhaps even doom the whole world. As such, the Catalyst is generally passive in nature- it will happen when it would happen, and the conspiracy can only react for it, even though they may work to subtly move things towards that endpoint. For example, the AKD's Catalyst could be based around having many generations of hunters from the same bloodline taking the Vow and dying in the name of the Vigil, which uses their themes of oaths and the reason why the group puts emphasis on bloodline and families. The Ascending Ones, however, have a theme of prophecy, with the prophets being the links which tie the chain together- having them pronounce a prophecy which states that the time has come and which bring the end for new prophets rising should be a catalyst which would push the conspiracy towards start racing against the clock in an attempt to achieve Sovereignty. For the KoSA, it could be about rooting out a certain number of demons within a certain period of time- like 4444 demons within 4444 days and presenting them as a sacrifice to God. The Catalyst should be difficult to achieve and tied to the conspiracy in question, and not something which could be gained by accident- a Lucifuge agent killing a random priest will not be a good Catalyst, but a single Lucifuge exposing 666 corrupted priests without committing any sin may be a good one.

    Step 2- Foundation
    Where the Catalyst is an event, the Foundation is a object- either a structure or a core of some kind. It should be the base around which the rest of the scenario would focus- basically, the heart of the event around which all the other pieces will be gathered, while still having a relation to the Catalyst. In a way, the Catalyst is the event which allows the formation of the Foundation, and as such there should be a thematic connection between the two. For the AKD, considering that their Catalyst is based around generational oaths, the Foundation will be about the Oath Stone- the relic which is used to bind those vows. Gathering all the pieces of the Oath Stones and rebuilding it into the form of an Omphalos Stone works as a good Foundation for the conspiracy's Vigil, for such a case. The Council of Bones, however, may require a foundation which connects between them and Death, likely finding an Avernian Gate of some kind and summoning a messenger of Death through it, with the location and time of the summoning being incredibly important. The AO, however, may seek the help of the djinn in order to build a "furnace" to perform an act of spiritual alchemy within a sacred place, while the MM would also seek a sacred church as their Foundation, yet they may be required to sanctify it through Vitae which was purified from all taint. The Foundation should be difficult to achieve, or require a big price from the conspiracy- there are many pieces of the Oath stone, for example, and some of which are lost yet it could be possible to initiate the next step without the complete Heart, and perhaps the MM can use some Vitae which was purified for the ritual, yet such shortcuts should present a danger that would make the attempt either fail, or worse, seemingly succeed only to corrupt the whole process, Offer ways to cut corners, but be clear that there are risks by doing so.

    Step 3- Formation
    After the core of the ritual is ready, the pieces start moving on their own. Creating the Formation initiates a series of events which are still subtle, yet remind the conspiracy that time starts running out and they should act fast. This is the moment when conflict with the wider supernatural community starts, even if it is still very much "ground level". The formation requires the hunters to fight against monsters in order to start building the wider magical infrastructure which would lead to their Sovereignty. For example, in the case of the AKD, the Formation is based around the symbols of the conspiracy- three relics in the shape of a Sword, Scroll and a Temple which the hunters must find, reclaim from monsters and gather together, with those relics also being very useful to the monsters in question. Cheiron may seek the parts of very specific monsters which they need in order to produce their "perfect vessel", with those monsters may actually being produced by Cheiron itself. TFV may need to gather the pieces of the occult matrix known as the Black Sun and feeding it into their special satellite YGGDRASSIL which hides in the dark side of the moon, while the Hototogisu may need to steal very specific powers which symbolize the great gods of Shinto. The Formation should be challenging to accomplish and have the sense of hurry, but should be achievable. Also, make the parts of the Formation useful to the players- yet dangerous to use, with using them possibly risking the whole project.

    Step 4- Sacrifice
    The Vigil demands a sacrifice, that is an absolute truth- and as such the conspiracy has to make an offer in order to satisfy this zealot god. The sacrifice should be precious, but not outright evil, and should be given out of free will. It does not necessarily has to be a blood sacrifice- it can be a metaphorical or a spiritual one, but still a sacrifice, and generally, it should be offered by the higher ups of the conspiracy, instead of them just sacrificing the lives of their followers. In the case of the AKD, the Panoply would gather the heads of Johan the Baptist and sacrifice their own heads, becoming vessel for the Vigil. For the AO, the sacrifice may come with the lives of prophets, who offer their souls to ignite the fire of the furnace. The MM would probably use the symbol of an ultimate price- crucifixions, while the Lucifuge would have the Lady giving her life to open a gate to Hell- yet still living as a lingering Will within the minds of the conspiracy. The Sacrifice should be presented as a gamble, where if successful would make the conspiracy's leadership a vessel to the Vigil, and if failed will have them dead- so it is a way for the conspiracy to show that they no longer need earthly ambitions and are fully devoted to their Vigil. It is a declaration of intent that they work to grasp at heaven- or more exactly, a declaration of war against the darkness, and the darkness will answer to their call.

    Step 5- Manifestation
    After the Sacrifice is given, the first signs for an actual, world changing phenomena start to emerge. The world can no longer ignore the attempt at Sovereignty, and the masquerade outright shatters all around the globe. Mythical enemies of the conspiracy either form and awaken- yet they are not the big bad evil guy. They are great and power and on the verge of human ability to fight, but it is still not the endgame, only the beginning. The AKD, naturally, would summon their ancient Labyrinths from times unknown back to the world, each hosting a powerful relic which must be reclaimed and a monster which guards it. The Cainte Heresy would have ancient vampiric horrors awaken from their sleep, progenitors of Curses which each symbolizing a twisted form of a Roman god. The Faithful of Shulpae may summon three great beast worthy for a Feast, which must be consumed before they'll consume the earth, sea and sky, while the Merrick Institute will have to delve into the dream world in order to challenge the greatest archetypes of fear which embodied themselves within human corporation and the Web of Pain. While the hunters may have covered up the pervious instances, no one can cover up the Manifestation, which means that monsters, as well as other hunters, may try to either stop them or steal their glory, which would mess up the whole process and put an end to it. This is a competition, after all, and if one conspiracy achieves its perfect world it would mean that the others will fail theirs, leading them to sabotage the efforts or even utilize it for their own means. The enemies produced by the manifestation should resonate with the enemies which the conspiracy hunts- the Lucifuge should fight archdemons from Hell, while the KoSA would have to deal with magic wielding dragons from the Supernal which resonate with the Cardinal Kings of Ars Goetia. If the conspiracy fails the Manifestation, reality will edit itself and the conspiracy will disintegrate. If they'll win, their ambitions could be fulfilled- but how could you win such dreadful, godlike beings?

    Step 6- Vessel
    The vessel is a champion- an heroic figure which represents the wishes of the organization and into which it pour its will. The vessel is a unique individual- there is only one such person, and the Formation was created in order to allow the conspiracy to bring forth their existence. The Paragon template in the AKD book was created in order to represent such a champion, with its Tier 4 version being the one used in the scenario, but you may prefer to have their powers more vague and abstract, using Influences. The vessel is the one who must accomplish the heroic mission, even though they can be helped by other members of the conspiracy. This is a divergence from the them of "strength in numbers", but as the vessel represents the collective will of the conspiracy it still represents it, even if in a different form. For the AKD, the vessel is to be recognized by the original head of the Baptist and armed with the Aegis and Doru Talisman while sent to conquer the Labyrinths and reclaim their treasures. The AO, however, may have a dual vessel process, including a living representative of of the Phoenix and a dead representative of Set, which can only activate their abilities when working together after being purified by the alchemical fire of the furnace. Cheiron may produce the perfect hunter from the pieces of monsters, creating a vessel which can challenge the power of the gods and which could heal all plagues. The MM may have their vessel sanctified by the pope and the choirs of heaven and the saints, anointed with holy blood to fight the antichrist. The vessel has to win the war against the darkness and reclaim the pieces required for the final battle- the AKD needs to bring the relics back to the Labyrinth of the Isle, while the Faithful of Shulpae requires their vessel to devour the power of the monsters and vomit them to produce the true horror they must face.

    Step 7- Test
    This is the moment. This is the time. This is what generations upon generations of conspiracy members lived and died for. This is the final confrontation between light and darkness, between the Vigil and the monsters. The dark god of all evil rises, and it is up to the vessel, alone, fighting against it to bring an end to the night. If they'll win, they win for all eternity. If they lose, the world will fall under a catastrophe which would drown the Vigil- perhaps for all eternity. The Test is a battle, yet it is not decided by battle. There is a trick, there's always a trick. Even the vessel can't just punch Cthulhu until it dies- no, they need to figure out the loophole by following their own myths and symbolism and exploit them to bring an end for the darkness. The vessel of the AKD would have to win where their ancestors lost, reviving Asterion and the Mistress and fighting them before the Head of the Baptist only to castrate the Bull, stopping it from ever mating with the Mistress. The Lucifuge would have Satan, Lucifer Himself, rising from the pits of Hell- yet by slaying Him his evil would simply scatter across the world, and as such they must redeem him instead. The Cainte Heresy would face Remus's undead body their champion would have to embody Romulus and use the powers of Minerva in order to remove the curse of vampirism for all eternity. VASCU would have to use the sign of Cain in order to submit the embodiment of murder from ever killing again. This is a truly single, one on one event, where the conspiracy have engineered for centuries and put the whole world at risk just on the slight chance the champion will understand the trick, finish the embodiment of darkness and end the Vigil once and for all.

    Step 8- Sovereignty
    Finally, it's here. The impossible became possible. A perfect world was achieved. The darkness is no more, there are no longer monsters in the shadow. The world is not a better place, one clean from taint and guarded by the conspiracy. The AKD have rebuilt their lost Paradise, creating a world where magic flows freely- and is guarded well by the Guardians of the Labyrinth so it will never be used to break the world once more. The KoSG, however, made a world where magic is silent and dread, and the Faceless Angels sleep soundly and all of humanity follows their own strange dream worlds. Cheiron have perfected the human state, crossing the boundary between beast and man and creating a new era where as the two are the same, there is no fear from the dark. TFV have submitting the human will upon the Nine Realms, enslaving the gods and bringing a new age of Man. VASCU turned their psychic abilities into a memetic virus, removing the will to murder and kill and creating programmed rules which form a perfect human society. The Les Mysteres have rebuilt Pangaea, and there is no longer a wall between humanity and its gods as all live in harmony. The KoSA have fixed their God, stopping angels from ever falling again and allowing humans to ascend as angels.

    The Vigil has won. The darkness is gone. For better or worse, this is now the world- unless someone would shatter that dream, bringing yet another age of darkness upon the world... until the candle will be ignited once more, starting the march towards a new, shining, bright age.

    And that's it! Let me know what you are thinking, and I may add detailed scenarios for each of the conspiracies (or at least, some of them) later!

                  Well, if you find the Blueprints part useful, perhaps a deeper formalism will help you even further? :P

                  If you need help in creating Blueprints for Sovereignty, here are the sub steps I'll use to design it-

                  Endowments: obviously, the attempt at Sovereignty should utilize the Endowments of the conspiracy as a concept. The AKD would use relics, TFV would design Advanced Armory, the Lucifuge would have hell powers, etc.

                  System: the magical and/or religious paradigm which the conspiracy would use in order to achieve their goal. it could be a fictional concept, like the AKD using Labyrinths as their system or the Faithful of Shulpae using Feasts, or one based around real world concepts, like the MM having Catholicism, TVF using Germanic Paganism, the AO using Alchemy or the KoSG having Ars Goetia.

                  Mythos: where the system defines the rules, the mythos defines the goal. What is the world as the conspiracy wish it to be? What the conspiracy believes to be its origin and reason for existence? For the AKD, it is Paradise Lost. For the Lucifuge, is Redemption of the Devil. For VASCU, it would be Just Society, while the KoSA have Soldiers of God. What is the conspiracy stands for, basically.

                  Symbolism: every conspiracy has some symbols which defines it- choose a couple. Not too many, but not too few. Things which work to emphasis the themes of the conspiracy and which needs to be included in the framework and which draw upon the mythos of the conspiracy. For the AKD, the Shield and Spear are very important symbols, as well as the Scroll, Sword and Temple. To this we added the Bull and the Mistress and Severed Heads, and we have all of the symbols of the group. Notice that while it looks like a lot of symbols, groups of symbols are in fact a single symbol- the Temple would have little meaning without the Scroll and the Sword, and the Bull must come with the Mistress of Honey. Those symbols gain meaning only when they come together, and as such should be coupled as a single symbol in the storyline. Other conspiracies will have their own symbols- the MM, for example, have the Pope, Blood and the original book after which they are named. The KoSG have Dragons, Royalty, The Name Which Shall Not Be Written and the Faceless Angels. The Hototogisu has Cuckoos, thievery and contracts, the AO have the djinn, prophecy and the Cults of the Phoenix and Set and Cheiron, of course, have their logo and centaurs.

                  And that's it- combine those features together and you'll get a good basis for a Sovereignty for your chosen conspiracy!

                  Also your shulpae idea is interesting, and I'm very surprised with the seemingly "good" chieron ending. And saint George scenario is actually scary.

                  Edit: is the saint George ending based on the akatsuki goal in naruto?
                  I've always assumed that while Cheiron are scary and do terrible things, the powers behind the conspiracy are not "evil" or malicious. In my head canon, their biggest enemy is the Contagion, which they came to heal. I've detailed my version for them in my Igniting the Fire fan supplement. For a more malicious Sovereignty where a powerful cooperation takes over the supernatural world and binds all monsters through capitalism and submit the whole world for its will, I'll look for the Hototogisu, who are far darker IMO than Cheiron, even if they put a nicer facade.
