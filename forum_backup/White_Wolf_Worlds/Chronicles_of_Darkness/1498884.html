    Token Crafting and Goblin Fruit Farming For 2e

    12-05-2022, 05:18 PM
    Hello all! Here's my attempt at a Second Edition update for Changeling the Lost's Rite of Spring's Token Making! Feedback is welcome!

    Token Maker (•••)
    The Changeling may make Tokens.

    Recipes and Blueprints
    Token - Intelligence + Occult for making it from scratch (extended roll, 5 x token rating successes necessary, each roll represents a day with 6 hours of work)
    Hedgespun Item - Intelligence + Occult for making it from scratch (extended roll, 3 x token rating successes necessary, each roll represents 1 hour of work)
    • If she instead works double time (12 hours), gain a +2 on the roll.
    • She may create the effect and catch, but never the drawback.
    A good rule is for each two dots of token, one session must be dedicated to gathering materials. The base materials do not reduce the successes needed.
    Once the necessary materials are gathered, the Token Maker may begin Crafting.

    Token Crafting
    Intelligence or Wits + Crafts for making the item (extended roll, 25 x token rating successes necessary, each roll represents a day with 6 hours of work)
    • The Token Maker must spend one point of Glamour each day she rolls.
    • If she instead works double time (12 hours), gain a +2 on the roll.
    • If she leaves off in the middle of her project, accumulated successes remain but if she fails to resume work within two weeks, the successes are lost.
    • She must work inside of the Hedge.
    • Only one roll can be made per day, as any more would stress the item too much.
    Raw Materials
    She can use a part from a Token, Hobgoblin, or Hedge Ghost and integrate it into the item to expedite the process. It must make sense somehow in how you integrate it into the item, and that it makes sense in the recipe. Using a token or goblin fruit in this way permanently destroys the magic inside of it.
    • Rank 1 Hedge Ghost / Wyrd 1-2 Hobgoblin / 1 dot Token / Common Goblin Fruit = -5 Target Successes
    • Rank 2 Hedge Ghost / Wyrd 3-4 Hobgoblin / 2 dots Token / Uncommon Goblin Fruit = -10 Target Successes
    • Rank 3 Hedge Ghost / Wyrd 5-6 Hobgoblin / 3 dots Token / Rare Goblin Fruit = -15 Target Successes
    • Rank 4 Hedge Ghost / Wyrd 7-8 Hobgoblin / 4 dots Token / Very Rare Goblin Fruit = -20 Target Successes
    • Rank 5 Hedge Ghost / Wyrd 9-10 Hobgoblin / 5 dots Token / Legendary Goblin Fruit = -25 Target Successes
    Raw Glamour
    Spending Glamour beyond the first counts as an automatic success, but the Token Maker is only able to spend up to her Crafts in bonus Glamour each roll.

    Alternative Dice Pools
    At the ST's discretion, the Token Maker can use other dice pools for Crafting, such as Presence + Expression by enchanting a wind chime made of glass through the virtue of song or a Resolve + Academics by writing down hideous forbidden knowledge into a book.

    Modifiers to Dice Rolls
    • Library can be used for Recipes and Blueprints, while Workshop can be used for Crafting
    • Certain powers like Contracts (ex. Tatterdemalion's Workshop) can help reduce penalties.
    • Teamwork may be used, but all secondary actors must have the Token Maker merit.
    • Penalties might be: Recipe requires intricate design (-3), using a substitute material of lower quality (-3), distractions (-2), improper work space (-1 to -5)
    -----

    Sidebar: Hedgespun Items and Automatons

    Hedgespun Items are much easier to craft and use the same rules as crafting a Token, but no longer require the merit to create. Replace the research roll with (extended roll, 3 x token rating successes necessary, each roll represents 1 hour of work), as recipes are much easier to come by and create.

    Hedgespun Item Crafting
    Strength or Wits + Crafts for making the item (extended roll, 5 x token rating successes necessary, each roll represents 1 hour of work)
    • The creator must spend one point of Glamour for each dot of rating of the item.
    • She must work inside of the Hedge.
    Automaton Crafting
    To create an Automaton, simply replace token rating with Retainer rating in the Crafting process.
    Automatons have a battery of Glamour equal to your [1 + Skill + Specialty] and possess one field of expertise (bodyguard, transport, entertaining, etc). They need 1 Glamour every week in the Hedge and 1 Glamour every day in the Ironside.

      Goblin Fruit Farming

      Roll Wits + Crafts as a Build Equipment roll, usually sprouting in 6 - (Rushed Growing) days and giving fruit double the amount of days after that. If you fail, you may try again the next day at a cumulative -1 penalty per attempt. This entire process may be “skipped” by simply performing a Paradigm shift of 5 successes, however, the psychoactivity of doing this may alter the outcome drastically.

      Bonuses
      Tools (+1 to +5) - Using a Workshop, Hedgespinning-Made Tools, etc.
      Carcass (+1) - Give it a dead body of some kind.
      Blood (+3) - Take one lethal, and feed it to the plant.
      Attribute (+5) - Give it a dot of an Attribute, regain it after a 10 - Wyrd days.
      Skill (+3) - Give it a dot of a Skill, regain it after 10 - Wyrd days.
      Willpower (+4) - Give it a dot of Willpower, regain it after 10 - Wyrd days.
      Clarity (+4) - Give it two mild Clarity damage, or one severe Clarity damage.

      Penalties
      Improper Location (-3) - Attempting to grow a desert plant in a swamp, etc.
      Hybridization (-2) - Attempting to mix two different Goblin Fruits together
      Rushed Growing (-1 to -5) - Attempting to make the Goblin Fruit grow faster

      Alternative Dice Pools
      At the ST's discretion, the Changeling can use other dice pools for Growing Goblin Fruit, such as Dexterity + Medicine for surgically harvesting and chemically mixing Goblin "Fruits" that are actually the flesh and bones of Hobgoblins.

      Measuring Success
      If the ST needs to measure the traits of the Goblin Fruit, he may base them on the [Crafts] Skill of the Changeling. For example: a Goblin Fruit that grants invisibility might last for turns equal to his [Crafts], while a Goblin Fruit that works as a poison might have a Toxicity Rating equal to his [Crafts]. These traits do not have to display numerical values that are identical to [Crafts] every time, it just works as a benchmark.

      An Exceptional Success for Growing Goblin Fruit can make the Changeling regain a single sacrificed trait immediately, add one to his effective [Crafts] to determine benchmarks for the traits of the Goblin Fruits, or to grow an additional fruit.

      If the Changeling has a relevant Specialty in Crafts, he not only gains +1 die for the roll itself, he also increases his effective [Crafts] by +1 to determine numerical values of the Goblin Fruits' traits. He likewise increases his effective [Crafts] by +2 with a relevant Area Of Expertise.

          And a merit to go with it!

          Abstraction Crafting (•••)
          Prerequsites: Wits 3, Occult 3

          The Changeling has learned (or stolen) knowledge on how to create Abstractions from the Hobgoblins. Whenever the Changeling is witness to a special moment of intense emotion or significance in the Hedge, she may roll a perception check to notice the gathering of Glamour by following the mystical threads of the Wyrd. Within the same scene, she may pay one point of Glamour to capture it with a Subtle Shift requiring three success, creating or capturing it in an object represents the moment. Only one instance of an object can be made from a single Abstraction.

          1 - Common (embarrassment from a kiss)
          2 - Uncommon (nostalgia from hanging out with a childhood friend)
          3 - Rare (attending an amazing concert they'll never forget)
          4 - Very Rare (breaking someone's heart from a 10 year marriage)
          5 - Legendary (the last gasp of a True Fae)

          They may be used in Token Crafting to reduce the amount of successes needed based on their rank.
          They may also be sold at a Goblin Market to reduce Goblin Debt by their rating.
          They may also be used to improve a Hedgespinning or Oneiromancy roll that resonates with it, destroying it and adding successes equal to its rating.
