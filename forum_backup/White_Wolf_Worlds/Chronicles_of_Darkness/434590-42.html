                        I whipeed up a quick intro.

                        Introduction
                        Nothing beside remains. Round the decay
                        Of that colossal wreck, boundless and bare
                        The lone and level sands stretch far away.
                        ― Percy Bysshe Shelley, Ozymandias
                        Your world ended long before you were born.

                        Once the ancient gods used mankind as they pleased and basked in the worship of their slaves, but those days are past. Mankind turned upon their gods and slew them upon their thrones. Their idols were cast down, their temples looted, and their rituals were ended.

                        In their victory mankind has created wonders. Civilisation stretches across the world. Laws bring safety and unity to the masses while science and reason birth extraordinary new devices and ever greater understanding of the universe. New gods have claimed the empty thrones, their theologies are debated and interpreted by sages and philosophers questing for morality and meaning.

                        In the shadow of this great age all that remains of the world before is a few fragments of broken idols and ruined temples buried, forgotten, in the silt and muck of the sea floor.

                        And you.

                        You are a descendent of the old gods, the original gods. Their seed lies in all of humanity and it took root in you. You have inherited their divine power, an ancient primordial power to large and chaotic to fit into this modern ordered world. Your very existence shakes the foundations of morality and civilisation, your presence alone drags men back to their primordial roots, exposing the savage ape buried beneath every civilised person.

                        There is little room for you in this new world, but there are hidden places on the fringes where you can live and claim your birthright. But never for eternity. Mankind will not suffer your existence lightly, and they’ve had millennia to learn your ways and muster their forces. You can run, and fight with your own kin over the shrinking scraps of land where your kind can still hide. Or you can rise up against humanity. The odds are against you, victory may be too horrific to contemplate, but you are divine and for you nothing is impossible.

                        You could devour the world.

                        “There are no rules. Only Principles and natural laws.” - Promethius
