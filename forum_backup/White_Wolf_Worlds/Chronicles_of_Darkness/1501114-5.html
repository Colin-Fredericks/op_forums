                                                Originally posted by Horodrigo View Post
                                                How strong is the demolisher merit? Like, if a character has demolisher 3, he can completly ignore a layer of steel and try to punch a door until it breaks?
                                                When damaging an object, she ignores one point of the object’s Durability per dot with this Merit.​
                                                Durability is effectively an object's armor rating and Demolisher ••• can ignore the damage reduction of steel and iron without any reinforced layers (which add +1 Durability). So yes, given enough time and opportunity, a character could render a non-reinforced steel door unusable. As for how strong the Merit is, balance-wise? It's very useful for breaking objects and weapons and can be universally taken by any splat, but it has a three-dot Strength or Intelligence prerequisite and needs 3 XP to become as efficient as possible, so it's subjective really.
