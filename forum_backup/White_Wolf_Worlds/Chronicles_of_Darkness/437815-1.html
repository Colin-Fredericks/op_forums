                I started the write up a month ago, but didn't get around to finish it. Here is what I have so far. More to come.



                Concerning Council Territory

                Of the four territories that border Baltimore, Council Territory has turned out to be the most... colorful. Apparently, there are several packs of werewolves who make their homes nearby and one or two of them have been having skirmishes with the vampires of the Council. Over the course of the game, we have had some inkling that something like this might be happening.

                Flashback 1: Remember the Golden Ticket Hunt organized by Pilgrim? It took place in the forest south of Council Territory. That night, Elliot Jr. and his coterie captured a werewolf. Alice and I set him free before things escalated into an interspecies casus belli.

                Flashback 2: When Mandy O'Kelly came to Alice and me because she was convinced Appollonia was going to kill her, one of the reasons she gave us to suspect that the Daeva Elder was less than loyal to Mandy and to the city was that she (Appollonia) was having meetings with werewolves near council territory.

                It turns out that prior to his death, Elliot Rex (nosferatu, ancilla, former sheriff) was providing consultation and material support to the Council in their conflict with the werewolves. Since his demise, the council has been slowly losing ground against their furry foes.

                This is one of the reasons that so many vampires have been arriving in the Quarantine Zone in an attempt to gain Baltimore Citizenship.


                Bring Us Your Hungry... Yearning to be Free

                You will probably be hearing a lot of unfamiliar names in these chapters. In character there are a lot of new faces showing up in the Quarantine Zone. Out of character, even I am having trouble keeping them straight. According to the ST, this is an opportunity to fill out the ranks of the pyramid below Alice and me, so that we are not perpetually the newest citizens of Baltimore.


                Responsibilities

                When Alice and I signed up to help cousin Antony with his new responsibilities as Reeve of the City and Bailiff of Michael Seville, we made two things clear:

                1. We needed cool titles. We did not want to be compared to dogs, favorably or otherwise. This is how we got the title of Marshal.

                2. This would be a temporary assignment. Alice and I were here to get things in order, bring the level of chaos down to a dull roar, and give Antony a chance to catch his breath and select/train new marshals.

                When we reported to Michael Seville for duty (we would be serving in the Quarantine Zone, Seville's Domain), he basically said that he wasn't sure how soon he would be willing to let us resign. Well, one out of two ain't bad.

                So, Alice and I have been spending a lot of time across the bay, babysitting the newcomers and testing them for rabies.


                The Jiffy-Lube

                There is an old closed down oil change place not too far from the butcher shop where Cooper (daeva, almoner) distributes the blood ration to the newcomers. Someone has taken it upon themselves to drag in some furniture and use the place as a kindred hangout.

                One night, I am approached by Miles (daeva, carthian.) He wants me to escort him out of the Quarantine Zone so he can visit both Michael Seville, and the City Elysium. He is offering to consider the favor a boon, so I agree to take him. We stop by Seville's office and after a short wait, Miles is allowed into Seville's inner office. I am not privy to his conversation with Seville.

                From there, we travel to the City Elysium. Miles looks around and asks to speak with Madeline Ross (daeva, keeper of Elysium, ancilla.) Christina notices the three of us talking and wanders over. I casually offer my arm so Christina can come close and listen in.

                Miles: (stammering a bit) "Well... we have made this place. A place where kindred can gather and talk without fear of violence, and I was um... kinda hoping you would be willing to make that place and official Elysium.

                The Keeper invites Miles upstairs to talk more about it and I follow them. Christina pulls on my arm and I hang back long enough for her to whisper in my ear, "You can't allow this to happen." (Christina has had permission to create an official Elysium for a year or two now, but has yet to pull the trigger. She doesn't want more competition.)

                Madame Ross leads us upstairs and says, "Well, let's go see this place. But first, do you have any money on you? or valuables?" Miles indicates that he has a Rolex watch. It looks expensive.

                Madame Ross: "Good. Please place it there." She points to a plate in plain sight.
                Miles: "Please. It has sentimental value..."
                Madame Ross: "You wanted to play the game. So play."

                Reluctantly, Miles leaves his watch on the plate.


                Elysium?

                Together, we travel to the Quarantine Zone. Madame Ross wants to see the proposed site of the new Elysium before she grants it her blessing. We arrive at the Jiffy-Lube and Madame Ross spends less than thirty seconds inside before we leave. While standing in the parking lot, she takes off a rather expensive looking bracelet and turns to Miles. The bracelet is gold, and contains many precious gems.

                Madame Ross: "Mr. Miles, would you please take this bracelet and place it on the coffee table inside? Hurry back."

                Miles disappears into the Jiffy-Lube and returns shortly. We have a pleasant conversation for about five minutes before the Keeper turns to Miles again and says, "Mr. Miles, may I have my bracelet back?"

                Miles gives the keeper a slightly confused look before once again entering the Jiffy-Lube. he returns a few minutes later with a crestfallen look on his face.

                Miles: "I'm sorry Madame Ross, your bracelet is missing..."
                Madame Ross: "I see. Marshal, please see to the return of my property."

                The three of us travel once again to the city Elysium where Mile's watch is still sitting on the plate, in plain sight. Miles puts his watch back on, and I escort him back to the Quarantine Zone and his obviously not-an-Elysium Jiffy-Lube.


                The Case of the Missing Bracelet

                Grumble... stoopid newcomers making me do extra work. Well, the Keeper of Elysium is a very influential individual and I do kinda like her, so I get to work. Well, no one in the Jiffy-Lube saw anything (big surprise) so we moved on and started canvassing the local pawn shops. It took some application of Auspex and Dominate, but we did locate and recover the bracelet.

                The Bracelet was sold to a pawn shop for a lot less than its value. According to the pawn broker, he estimated he could fence it for $50,000 and he bought it for only $1,000. With Dominate, we convinced him to sell the bracelet to us for $1,000 and then left him with no memory of the bracelet or how he came into possession of it.

                From there, we started questioning the newcomers who were present at the Jiffy-Lube at the time of the theft. Near the shore of the bay, we came upon a young vamp named Piper Morgan. She was ranting to some other vamps about equality.

                Piper Morgan: "Have you noticed that women get fed on more often than men? And have you noticed that women get made into ghouls more often than men? Get turned into slaves more often than men? It's sexist!"

                We asked Piper for a moment of her time and asked her about the bracelet. She was unaware she had done anything wrong and wasn't shy about admitting that she took it. So, we dragged her into the recently vacated storage locker of the late EZE and chopped off one of her hands with a meat cleaver we had purchased from Cooper's butcher shop.

                You might be thinking WTF Felix? Why are you such a dick? Isn't that a bit harsh? Well, first I do not particularly care for most of the newcomers from Council Territory. Any of them who become citizens will take up positions that could have been filled by the childer of Baltimore Citizens. Second, I did Piper a favor. As a Marshal, my authority and the severity of punishments I can levy are limited. If I had decided to give her a slap on the wrist, there would be the very real possibility that someone could have appealed to a higher authority in favor of a harsher punishment. This potential appeal could have come months or even years in the future. The fact that I used as harsh a punishment as I was authorized to use makes a potential appeal much less likely to be brought up or even to succeed if it is. I have effectively closed the issue for Piper. Her hand will grow back in time.

                There Are Four Lights!

                                Ghouls

                                In Baltimore, blood slaves to the immortals are generally not referred to as ghouls. Instead, they have one of two designations depending upon their place (or lack thereof) in vampire society.

                                Attendants are those mortals, usually ghouls, who are told the truth (more or less) about their condition and made aware of the all night society. They are members of it, and do have some protections under the law; although they are still considered the property of their regnant.

                                Retainers are also often ghouls, but they are kept in the dark about the truth of vampire nature, society, etc, and they are forbidden from being taken to places where kindred gather. They are not considered members of society and have little protection under the law.

                                In the event that a ghoul is killed by another vampire and the matter reaches the courts, the level of restitution that may be ordered will be quite different. In the case of retainers, the restitution would be akin to replacing a broken vase. Attendants are another matter.

                                To my knowledge, there have been no cases involving the destruction of someone's ghoul since they were designated as ghouls/retainers.


                                Mouse

                                Alice's attendant was born as Cooper's mortal sister before he was embraced. For details of how she came to be Alice's ghoul check out Kiddie Pool Saga Part 1 and do a keyword search for "boy meets girl." Mouse was seventeen when she was made a ghoul about five years ago. Unlike my ghoul Sidney whose life is completely controlled by vitae and dominate, Mouse is given certain levels of freedom.

                                for example, Mouse is allowed to travel freely within Alice's and my territory, as well as the territory of my cousin Antony. She has even made friends with Tigger (Terry's ghoul) and the two of them occasionally socialize. Mouse is also permitted to visit the city Elysium, where there is an area specifically open to Attendants.

                                Mouse is insatiably curious and she does not understand the concept "none of my business." She is small of stature and easy to overlook. We often use her for daytime surveillance missions.


                                New Neighbors

                                So... one night, the doorbell rings. I don't bother to answer, I have attendants for that. Mouse comes to me and says she hasn't answered the door because she does not recognize the person outside. So, I do it. On the other side of the door is a conservatively dressed woman in her early 40s. She is holding a tray on which sits a dessert made of jell-o.

                                We have a short conversation in which she explains that she and her family (husband and two teenage sons) are new to the neighborhood. Her husband is the manager of the new manufacturing plant that is opening nearby. (believe it or not, all this new development is not wholly vampire driven. There is a new manufacturing plant in the area that has been bringing hundreds of new jobs to the area)

                                Anyway, she eventually reminds me that her arms are getting tired from holding the tray and I take it from her with a smile and a "thank you." She departs and I walk to the kitchen. Further inspection of the dessert reveals it to bear a striking resemblance to a pair of breasts.

                                I smirk and then deposit the dessert into the trash. We are far too paranoid in this household to allow our Attendants to eat desserts that may or may not be drugged/poisoned. Nevertheless, Mouse was disappointed.

                                Mouse: "Hey! I wanted to eat some of that." (pouty face.)

                                I turn to Mouse and order her to make friends with the new neighbors so we can find out if we can take their presence at face value, or if there is something else going on. We also concoct a cover story in which Mouse is my little sister and I have been taking care of her since our parents died.


                                The Case of the Dead Attendant

                                One night while visiting the Quarantine Zone, we are approached by a small group of kindred. They tell me that they have captured a lawbreaker and that I have to do something about it. Among the group are:

                                Miles (daeva, carthian)
                                Charles Wood (not much info about this guy yet.)
                                Getter (the dood who discovered the location of the OD Chapter house)

                                Getter is the plaintiff. According to him, one of his ghouls was minding his own business when he was shot in the head without provocation by a vampire named Patrick. At this point, I have not had more than an introduction with Charles Wood, and even less with Getter. So, I decide to send for Patrick and settle this dispute.

                                Once everyone is assembled, I begin asking questions. There are four things I want to know:

                                1. Most important: was the body disposed of discretely? (masquerade preservation)
                                2. Did Patrick kill the ghoul?
                                3. Why did he do it?
                                4. Was the ghoul in question an Attendant or a Retainer?

                                In the course of my questioning, I find out that Miles is here merely to observe because he has an interest in matters political and legal. As I was asking questions, I was very frustrated. No one was interested in giving me a straight answer to my questions. Getter wouldn't tell me what his ghoul was up to at the time of the shooting and while Patrick did admit that he pulled the trigger, he would not tell me why. However, I was assured the body was cut up into tiny pieces and dumped into the ocean after a boat ride. Anyway, with uncooperative witnesses, I decided to put the case on an indefinite recess unless or until new information came to light.


                                Rusty

                                Remember the ghoul who Spider found lurking outside Eve Liebsch's apartment the night EZE attacked? It turns out she is Patrick's ghoul. When we freed Patrick from a torpor encased in concrete, we did not arrange for him to woken up using potent vitae. Instead, we arranged with the almoner (Cooper) to have Patrick's blood ration delivered to his self storage container. As he is a neonate, we did not anticipate that this would take more than a few weeks.

                                In addition, we brought a cot and sleeping bag for Rusty along with a cooler full of sandwiches, several gallon jugs of drinking water, a five-gallon toilet, and some paperback novels. She would be the one feeding him through a funnel after all.


                                Confession

                                So, as I was walking away from the Jiffy-Lube where we were having our discussion, Patrick follows us.

                                Patrick: "Listen. I didn't want to say this in front of everyone in there... When I shot that guy, it was because he and another ghoul were getting ready to rape and kill one of your ghouls. The short one with dark hair. Well, when I was unable to take care of Rusty you treated her like she was one of your own. So, since I had the chance to return the favor, I did."

                                I gave Patrick a long look before I left for home.


                                Home

                                Alice has been spending a lot of time lately travelling around the city spying for the Mekhetocracy. The activities of Clan Nosferatu have her unhappy with her performance. I had to wait for her return near dawn before we could speak. The fact that I hadn't seen Mouse around the house for a past few days had not come as a surprise to me. She was after all on a mission to spy on our new neighbors.

                                Well, Alice was not best pleased with this news. We were also disappointed with Mouse. Not because this happened to her, but because she did not tell us. Anyway, Alice invaded the mind of Mouse to view the events through the lens of her memories. Here is what happened.

                                1. Mouse decided one night to visit Tigger (Terry's Attendant) and planned to visit Elysium together.
                                2. As Mouse was just outside the haven of Terry and Kat (Alice's and my former haven) a van pulled up and she was pulled inside.
                                3. She was taken across bay to the quarantine zone to a secluded place quite a distance from the places where vampires normally gather.
                                4. As the two ghouls were discussing what they were going to do with her, Patrick showed up.
                                5. Patrick warned them to let her go, and when they didn't he shot one of them in the head.
                                6. The other ghoul fled.
                                7. Patrick and Rusty brought Mouse back and dropped her off across the street from our Haven.

                                Before we went to sleep for the day, we took mouse into the bathroom and gave her a bath. Then we let her sleep with us for the day.


                                Investigation

                                With Alice back at my side, the investigation goes a bit faster from there. Here is what led to this ugly turn of events.

                                1. After the death of EZE, not all of his ghouls were found and disposed of. One of them escaped.
                                2. The surviving ghoul of EZE has been desperately trying to find a new regnant.
                                3. Charles Wood has not made any explicit offers to this survivor, but has mused many times in his presence about the poor treatment residents of the Quarantine Zone receive from the so called Citizens of Baltimore.

                                Charles Wood: "Wouldn't it be great if someone were to teach them a lesson? That there are consequences to their actions? What if someone were to just take one of their ghouls and kill it? Wouldn't that be great? I would have a lot of respect for whoever did that." (it seems to me that Mr. Wood is aware that he would be held responsible for the actions of this thralls. He was trying to manipulate the surviving ghoul without actually asking/ordering actions or offering compensation.)

                                Well all this is well and good, but now we need to teach these little fucks a lesson.


                                Witness

                                Alice and I felt that the lynchpin to this case is Getter's ghoul. He was an accomplice to the crime, but he did not get shot in the head. He wisely ran. Further, I am rather upset that the perpetrators tried to trick me into punishing the guy who rescued Mouse.

                                So, the two of us go looking for Getter's attendant. After twisting Getter's arm, we discover that Charles Wood has forced him to hire Marco and Polo (discount mercenaries) to kill his ghoul. From there, we rush to the nearby docks. We have seen Marco and Polo operate there before.

                                When we arrive, we find several bags of McDonald's hamburgers and discarded wrappers. The ghoul is eating his last meal (slowly) as Marco and Polo watch. We inform the two that we will be taking the ghoul. They are not happy with the prospect of having to give a refund, but we are able to intimidate the two and avoid violence. The ghoul spends a few days imprisoned in our basement while we make our plans.


                                The Courts

                                Charles Wood wants to manipulate the courts? We will show him how to use the courts. We are going to chainsaw fuck him with the courts. After some legal advice from Anthony (reeve, invictus, my cousin) we decided to convince everyone who has been wronged in even the smallest degree to file a grievance against Charles Wood.

                                1. First, we spoke to Terry and Kat. Mouse was kidnapped right in front of their haven. Actions like these can lead other kindred to believe their haven is unsafe and therefore refuse invitations to visit, or refrain from inviting them to other events because they are too afraid to risk sending their own ghouls as couriers to their haven.

                                Charles Wood is found guilty and sentenced to twenty-five lashes.

                                2. Soon after he received his lashes, Charles Wood received another summons to court. This time he is accused by Antony of the crime of travelling through his territory without permission and with bad intentions in his heart.

                                Charles Wood is found guilty and sentenced to kill any and all ghouls he has, and is saddled with the responsibility of disposing of their bodies.

                                3. Soon after finishing that grim business, Charles Wood is summoned to appear in court once again. This time it is Alice and I who accuse him of the crime of kidnapping Mouse with the intent to do her harm. Charles Wood is found guilty. His sentence is that he will be banned forevermore from the creation of ghouls. In all of these cases, the ghoul of Getter is the star witness who is able to establish that Charles Wood is manipulating the late-former-ghoul of EZE into taking these actions.

                                Lest you think that Alice and I have gone soft... at the conclusion of these legal proceedings we met with Antony in our official capacity as Marshals. We inform the Reeve that the vampire resident of the Quarantine Zone known as Charles Wood has failed his rabies test.

                                Well we here in Baltimore do not simply exile rabid kindred to become a problem for some other domain. We kill them. And that is what we did to Charles Wood. Oh... and we returned Getter's ghoul to Marco and Polo. They have a contract to fulfill after all.


                                Heart to Heart

                                Not long after, we found Miles (daeva, Carthian) looking sort of depressed standing by the water and decided to talk to him. Miles was feeling disappointed about the failed Jiffy-Lube-Elysium and embarrassment about the behavior of his fellow Quarantine Zone Residents. This is our home, and this is how they behave?" He was also upset about the treatment they had been receiving from the Baltimore authorities.

                                Miles: "We chose to join you. We shouldn't be treated this way. We should be able to go wherever we want. Why are we treated like spies and threats. We chose Baltimore. We want to be part of Baltimore."
                                Alice: "Miles... Fairness seems to be something that is really important to you. Fine. You chose to join us, but we didn't choose you. isn't it only fair that we get to choose too?"

                                            Katherine and Fishie

                                            Katherine and Fishie were sired by the same vampire, a nomad. Though to call them sisters would be a mistake. Katherine's sire dropped Fishie on her doorstep a few years ago. Fishie was a disobedient pain in the ass and eventually Katherine kicked her out on her ass. She was apparently something of a pain to Roy (regent of territory to the NW of Baltimore) as he banished her from his territory.

                                            From there, Fishie drifted to the NE territory which was at the time controlled by "the boss" (lancea sanctum mobsters; allies of Septimus) who were in a power struggle is Pasha. Pasha had Fishie break into my haven and kidnap Felicity (my younger sister.)

                                            Felicity was saved. Pasha was killed in a duel. Fishie was made into a special project. We released her into the Quarantine Zone and made sure that her unlife was as unpleasant as we could, without actually getting her to rebel. Over time, we broke her spirit and left her living there as a pathetic creature. She is meant to be an unliving warning to others: If you fuck with us, we will ruin you. In all honesty, I can't even remember what her name was. Everyone calls her Fishie now, and she answers to it.


                                            At Fishie's Place

                                            So, one night Alice and I are hanging out in the Quarantine Zone under cover of Obfuscate. From a nearby rooftop, we notice that a few vampires are waiting outside the haven of Fishie. Those vampires are Hope, Karma, and Trenton (new Quarantine Zone residents.)

                                            There is a narrow staircase leading to Fishie's upstairs apartment. When the door opens, Katherine emerges and begins to walk down. Hope and Karma are blocking her way and Trenton is nearby watching.

                                            Karma: "Who the fuck are you? and what are you doing in our territory?"
                                            Katherine: "Just leaving actually."
                                            Karma: "I don't think so. You're not leaving until you've introduced yourself. I checked with Cooper and he says you're neither a Citizen nor a Resident and that makes you fair game."

                                            Alice and I move into position as the vampires continue to argue. Combat starts and Katherine wins initiative. Before anyone else can act, she quick draws a water bottle and squirts Karma with a clear liquid. My nose soon informs me that Karma is covered in lighter fluid. Everyone scatters and Katherine walks.

                                            Alice and I catch up to her on the bridge and have a short conversation. I suggest we leave before Karma is finished with her bath in the bay. On the other side of the bridge we enter Alice's and my territory. Katherine is asking what the fuck was the problem with those other vampires and wondering about travelling within Baltimore.

                                            Felix (obviously joking):"Well, speaking of travel rights we are now standing on my territory."
                                            Katherine: "Oh, will this suffice?"

                                            Katherine reaches into her coat and pulls out an envelope, which she hands to me. Inside is a letter which bears her likeness in the engraved style one is accustomed to seeing on dollar bills. It reads something like this: "By order of the Seneschal in the name of the Prince, the bearer of this note is to be afforded safe travel without hindrance through the His lands."

                                            I had been planning to escort Katherine through the Quarantine Zone and Kiddie Pool before letting her go on her merry way. After reading this letter, I ask her where she is headed and offer to escort her safely there. I also advise her that in the future, she should consider showing her writ of passage before trouble starts rather than after.


                                            Big Trouble

                                            Well... Karma has really stepped in it. She has defied the orders of the Prince. The fact that she didn't know will be a mitigating factor insofar as it will prevent her from being executed. However, it is not her place to police the Quarantine Zone (as it happens that dubious honor rests with Alice and me.) I draft a summons and present it to Karma informing her that she will be standing trial in three nights. The summons is written in Latin, but that's her problem.

                                            So, three nights hence I arrive in the Quarantine Zone at the Jiffy-Lube. I have arranged for there to be extra tables and chairs for the trial I am about to conduct. Alice and I bring Aika and Aiko (daeva twins, our slaves) with us for added security.

                                            Karma has figured out that she needs to be here (there isn't really a good Latin word for Jiffy-Lube) and the place is packed. Because her offense was directly against the Prince I will not be conducting this trial in the casual way I usually do (ie: in English.)

                                            So, I call the trial to order in Latin and ask if Karma has used the three days lead time to procure a translator for herself. She looked pretty confused, but then Patrick stepped forward and asked (in Latin) if he could serve as translator. I then ask if she has procured for herself a lawyer. Miles is trying to get her to accept him as a councilor, but Karma insists that she can defend herself.

                                            One by one, I question my witnesses via the translator. Hope and Trenton both testify that Karma at no time took aggressive action toward Katherine (this trial would have been a lot more simple had Katherine not won initiative that night.) Finally, Karma herself took the stand, and swore that it was not her who started the fight that night.

                                            Next, we brought Katherine to the stand. She is our surprise witness. Katherine begins by stating that she does not know Latin. I tell her that she can testify in the vernacular, if she would like. She counters that she would be most comfortable to testify in French. I allow it. (way back at character creation I took the well travelled merit and several language merits to represent the time I spent travelling with my sire.)

                                            At the conclusion of Katherine's testimony, I announce a 15 min recess to think over the facts. When we return, I pronounce my judgment:


                                            Felix (in latin; Patrick translating):"I have weighed the testimony of all the witnesses, and I have found the testimony of Katherine to be the most convincing..."
                                            Karma: "This is bullshit! That bitch is a liar!"
                                            Karma stands and runs across the room at Katherine. Aika and Aiko intercept her in a celeritous blur. Karma spends the remainder of the trial bound and gagged.
                                            Felix (more Latin): "There is another witness to the crime in this courtroom. And that witness is me. I was watching you all on the night in question, unseen. I know which of the witnesses here stretched the truth and which one's did not. I therefore find Karma guilty acting against the wishes of the Prince. Because Karma hindered the movement of Katherine your movement will be hindered. By order of the court: the vampire known as Karma will be hobbled by having her feet removed with a butchers cleaver. Further, for disrespecting the court with her words: the vampire known as Karma will have her tongue removed. Finally, for attempting to assault a witness in a court of the City of Baltimore: the vampire known as Karma will receive 10 lashes."

                                            After the trial, Karma screams that she wants to appeal. Karma's feet are spared pending appeal, but her lashes and tongue removal are carried out and we leave.


                                            Mission: Possible

                                            About four days later, we are called to the office of Michael Seville. Once there, he begins by asking us a few questions about our competence with the discipline of Obfuscate. Specifically he wants to know if we have the ability to disguise ourselves as someone else. We do.

                                            Seville: "Now before I tell you any of the specifics of this mission, you need to be aware of the risks. This is a potentially very dangerous mission. The consequences for failing this mission would be great and felt city wide."

                                            Alice and I look at each other and decide that yes, we will help in whatever way we can.

                                            Seville tells us that there is going to be a transaction in the near future between Katherine and someone from out of town. It is our job to impersonate a pair of ghouls and join Katherine during the transaction. However, we are not there to protect Katherine, but to protect the person from out of town with whom she is doing business.

                                            Seville: "It would be better for the city if one of you were to die defending this person than for the out of towner to be harmed in any way."

                                            This sounds delightful. Well, Alice and I agree to help anyway. We are told that the transaction would be taking place "soon" and we should be prepared to leave at short notice. Further, we are told that the deal could take place:

                                            1. The Serpent of the Seas - a decommissioned cargo ship docked near Alice's and my territory.
                                            2. An alley behind "the Private Eye" - a strip club in the quarantine zone.
                                            3. An alley a few blocks away from the bus station on the NE side.

                                            In the nights that followed our meeting with Seville, we staked out each of these locations. We used our magic powers to get keys to all the nearby buildings, stashed emergency bags, and even panted bicycles around the potential sites. We also practiced our obfuscaate disguises. We didn't need to appear as any specific person, but we did need our disguises to be consistent from scene to scene for the duration of the night.


                                            Tonight's the Night

                                            Not long after our preparations were made, Spider visited us at our haven. This is not an uncommon event. When we let him in, Michael Seville was standing there instead. He apologized for entering our haven under false pretenses but explained the need for secrecy. He told us that the time had been set, and after filling us in on the details left.

                                            Alice and I put on our disguises and traveled by bicycle to a diner in downtown Baltimore. There, we met several ghouls. Some known to us, and others not.

                                            1. Rusty (Patrick's ghoul)
                                            2. Snow - Quarantine Zone ghoul (i'm not sure who her regnant is.)
                                            3. Marco or Polo's ghoul (I don't know his name)
                                            4. unknown ghoul

                                            Soon after, a minivan pulls up to the diner and we feel the pull of Majesty as Katherine invites us all into her van. (and she didn't even offer us candy or puppies. wtf?) Anyway, we drive and Katherine stops at a gas station. Once there, some of the ghouls start grumbling and asking when we are going to get paid. Katherine decides to appease us with a little cash up front. We are each given $1000. Snow, who looks emaciated, says she is going to grab something from inside the gas station. I decide it would be a good idea to follow her, but when I round the corner I don't see her inside the store. Instead, she is across the street buying drugs on the corner.

                                            Meanwhile, Katherine asks the remaining ghouls if they are armed. All but Rusty are armed with firearms. Alice speaks up: "Guns are loud. We should play it safe and use melee weapons instead." As I return to the group, Katherine is once again using her Majesty to keep the ghouls under control as she disarms them.

                                            Katherine: "Where is the other girl?"
                                            Felix: "Across the street buying drugs. I say we leave her."

                                            We all pile into the minivan and leave, minus Snow. Soon we arrive in the NE side, near the bus station. Katherine opens the back of the van and pulls out a duffel bag, which she hands to one of the ghouls. We walk toward the meeting place. As we are travelling we are approached by Ryan (gangrel, Ancilla, game warden of the NE.) The NE has been declared fallow and Ryan is there to keep it that way. However, when Katherine produces her Writ of Passage, Ryan leaves without asking any questions.

                                            Finally, we arrive at the meeting place and we wait. After about 40 minutes, two humans arrive: a boy and a girl in their early 20s. The mortals pass a duffel over to Katherine, which we soon learn is filled with cash. Katherine orders the ghoul to hand over our duffel. The girl opens the duffel and pulls out a ziplock bag filled with crystal meth. She looks at it, then lays it aside. She reaches in again and pulls out a bag filled with ecstasy pills. She looks at it, then lays it aside. This process is repeated about 15 more times before she pulls out a vial of black tar.

                                            She holds the vial up to the light and then tosses it over to her partner. He gives it a long look and then opens it and smells it. When the vial opens, the scent of vitae enters my nostrils.

                                            The boy then places a second duffel on the ground and opens it. It is filled with gold coins in plastic vacuum packs.

                                            The Girl: "Can any of you tell us where you go this? If so, you can have this bag."
                                            Katherine says she doesn't know where it's from and she isn't interested in further deals.

                                            The Boy: "If you change your mind, we will be at the Motel 6 on the corner of 5th and Main, room 3 for a few more days.

                                            Katherine brings us all back to the minivan (Majesty) and we leave. Once we are dropped off, Alice and I haul ass back to the NE side and the Motel 6. On the way there, we stop at a payphone and make a call to Michael Seville's ghoul. When we became Marshals for Seville, he put us in contact with one of his ghouls. The ghoul gave us some information about safehouses and feeding grounds we could tap if we were wounded in the line of duty. We tell him that we will need 24 hour protection of the Motel 6 for the next few days.


                                            The Vigil

                                            So, Alice and I spent a few hours patrolling the area when we noticed a group of three ghouls creeping through the small wooded area which separates the Motel 6 from the nearby highway. Alice confirms via Auspex that they are here to kill and rob the two mortals who we are here to protect. I won't bore you with the details, but Alice and I did bad things to them. One died, and the other two left badly wounded with their memories sanitized as well as a dominate compulsion to get rid of this body of their friend that they just killed

                                            Seville's agents arrive and get checked into the motel. They will watch things from here on out. We meet Seville and give him a full report of what went down. Seville wants us to find the source of the drug which smells like vitae. He wants us to find the product manufactury and report back to him. "Do not engage. Do not be seen. Just find it and report back."

                                            Before we leave, I tell Seville that I must turn myself in for punishment. I have killed without permission and in so doing transgressed the laws.

                                            Seville: "We don't really have time for this. Fine..." He leaves the room and returns with Jerome James. James Speaks in latin and accuses us of the crime to which I have just confessed.
                                            Seville: "De minimus non curat lex." (the law does not concern itself with trifles.)

                                            With that out of the way, we leave.

                                            Needle in a Stack of Needles

                                            This turned out to be a rather long and winding investigation. I'll try to distill it into the salient points:

                                            1. We spoke to Katherine to find out wtf was going on. Here is what she told us:
                                            a. She owed a boon to Trevor (we have encountered this dood a long time ago in Roy's territoy.)
                                            b. To repay the boon, Trevor wanted her to handle this deal for him. Something about these mortals spooked him and he wanted Ketherine to take the risk for him.
                                            c. Katherine visited her vampire sister Fishie to ask for contacts to get some muscle to bring with her. This is how all those crappy Quarantine Zone ghouls got hired.

                                            2. We tracked down Trevor.
                                            a. Trevor was approached by mortals who wanted drugs. They offered him an obscene amount of money to get them one sample of every drug sold in Baltimore.
                                            b. The vitae drug was hardest to get. Only Shaman knows where to get it.
                                            c. While I was talking to Trevor, Alice was prowling the neighborhood skimming the minds of bartenders and bouncers.
                                            d. She found out that in order to find him, the boy/girl mortal pair from out of town went from bar to bar asking about "drug dealers who look like vampires" and "drug dealers who tend to wind up making out with people a lot more attractive than them." Sounds like Trevor sucks at the masquerade.

                                            3. We returned to the Quarantine Zone and spoke to Shaman about the product.
                                            a. He told us that ghouls in Council Territory use the drug to take the edge off when they haven't been fed in a while.
                                            b. He tells us that he gets it from a pair of vamps called Rabbit and Gangrene who live in council territory.
                                            c. According to Shaman: Rabbit is the dangerous one. It is even rumored that he has killed a vampire in the Council Elysium without being caught.

                                            4. We spend some time staking out the Council Elysium
                                            a. As the council continues to lose ground to the werewolves their Elysium has been getting less popular.
                                            b. Eventually, we find Gangrene. He was kinda hard to miss (Nosferatu with huge size merit.)
                                            c. We follow him first to his haven. He sleeps in the basement of a preschool. he feeds on the children who wander down there by day. Sometimes he even lets himself be seen.
                                            d. Next, we follow him to an abandoned YMCA. Very slowly and very obfuscatedly we follow him to the swimming pool. It takes us about 30 minutes to catch up to him because the halls and floors are littered with broken glass and other detritus which makes it difficult to maintain obfuscate.

                                            When we finally arrive at the pool we are treated to the following tablaeu:

                                            The pool is drained. In the deep end there is a caged vampire hooked up to a drug lab. There is a blood drip into the vampire and a vitae drip out of it. The vitae is boiled on a hot plate and then mashed into powder. In the shallow end of the pool there is a wooden totem adorned with the skulls of animals. On his knees in front of the totem is a man of native american descent. He is covered in ritual scars and is wearing a respirator as he paints occult symbols on the floor of the pool with red paint (or blood.) In the corner of the room, there is an adolescent girl chained to a radiator. She appears to be about 13 years old and is nude and shaved bald. She is huddled over a small table with a scale as she fills and measures small glass vials with a rust colored powder. Alice later tells me that the man in the pool is definitely a werewolf.

                                            Werewolf: "I can't work like this. Get me a fresh one!"
                                            Gangrene leaves.

                                            So yeah... I'm pretty sure we have found the manufactury. Alice and I leave. As slowly and carefully as we had entered.


                                            Debreifing

                                            We returned to Seville and made our report.

                                            Seville: "I will answer your questions if that is what you wish, but now is your last chance to walk away with nothing by my gratitude and the gratitude of the city. You'll sleep better if you do."

                                            Alice and I decide that sleep is overrated.

                                            Seville: "The two you encountered while traveling with Katherine are human wizards from Philidelphia. The red powder is some kind of tainted version of something they need. Someone is trying to draw attention to Baltimore"

                                            Seville tells us to do nothing about the drug lab at the YMCA. It is above our pay grade and will be dealt with by those more powerful than us. Gangrene however, we can deal with. And we do.


                                            Warnings

                                            Recent events have reminded me of a scene which actually took place months ago. Alice and I were looking for her grandsire, Peter. When we arrived at his office the Primogen were meeting and the door was open. We overheard a few words from Appollonia.

                                            Appollonia: "Our city is threatened by external deadly foes. We must unite inside her or we'll crumble from within. I have told you. I have warned you. Let the squabble now begin."

                                            (yes, I know this is from Harry Potter.)


                                            Post Script

                                            Out of character, I was a bit surprised that this mission came to us via Michael Seville instead of via the Mekhetocracy. The ST told me a few meta-game things:

                                            1. He made competing rolls for the mekhetocracy vs Michael Seville's intelligence network. Seville won.
                                            2. The Prince is in fact heading toward torpor. Some nights he is more lucid than others. The Prince has certain devotions which link him to the city of Baltimore and allow him to better direct the limited resources of the Mekhetocracy. It's easy to get mekhet eyes on a problem when the Prince knows where you need to be. It's harder when the 12 remaining mekhet do not have that kind of direction.



                                            This should bring things up to date. Thanks for reading
