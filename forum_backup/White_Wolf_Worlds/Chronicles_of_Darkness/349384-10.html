    I've always been a fan of giant monsters/kaiju. The problem with including them in the NWOD is that they could easily break the masquerade, which is pretty intrinsic to the setting.

    That being said, I think the most straightforward way to include kaiju would be to make them Rank 6+ Cryptids (the ones mutated by Infrastructure). They could also be animals or objects who were Claimed by the Royal Avatars of the Gods Of The Shadow, legendary hobgoblins or especially monstrous True Fae, devastating Abyssal Incursions, ancient Pandorans, something that crawled out of the Dark Places opened by a Strix, an unusual Cthonian, or a Beast who achieved any of the three types of Inheritance. As for fan splats, I know that Geniuses with enough Inspiration, Axioms, resources and assistants can build kaiju-like Wonders. Some Conspiracies might create mecha-like Endowments to fight kaiju, Pacific Rim style.

    I believe that Godzilla himself could be the Horror of an Anakim Tyrant/Ravager who has Lair 9 or even 10. His Atavisms are Dragonfire, Cyclopean Strength, Titanic Blow, Looming Presence, Limb From Limb, Unbreakable, Relentless Hunter and Heart Of The Ocean. He probably reached the Incarnate Inheritance.

                                                Dracula

                                                When it comes to a fictional character such a Dracula, there are a staggering number of adaptions, interpretations and re-inventions. For this write up, I am presenting Vlad Dracul as I believe he would appear in Vampire the Requiem.
                                                Using the Dracula, Prince of Paris plot hook from the Chroniclers Guide, this version has ruled the City of Lights for over 100 years under an alias. His use of the coils has allowed him to transcend clan (although as the Prince of Paris he poses as a Gangrel). He is careful not to expose the true extent for his power, lest others discover who he truly is.

                                                Vlad Dracul – Dracula
                                                Mask: Master; Dirge: Philosopher
                                                Clan: Unknown
                                                Covenant: Unaligned

                                                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 6, Wits 5, Resolve 5
                                                Physical Attributes: Strength 6, Dexterity 4, Stamina 6
                                                Social Attributes: Presence 6, Manipulation 4, Composure 6
                                                Skills: Academics 5(History, Religion), Animal Ken 5 (Children of the Night), Athletics 4, Brawl 4, Crafts 2, Expression 3 (Writing), Firearms 3 (Crossbow), Intimidation 6(Terror), Investigation 5 (Supernatural Mysteries), Medicine 3, Persuasion 5, Occult 6 (Transcendence), Politics 5 (Ordo Dracul), Ride 4, Science 3 (Experimentation), Socialize 5, Survival 4(Wallachia), Stealth 5, Subterfuge 5(Occult Secrets), Warfare 4, Weaponry 5(Halberd, Sword)
                                                Merits: Acute Senses, Allies (Many), Area of Expertise (Ordo Dracul, Transcendence), Claws of the Unholy, Contacts (Many), Cutthroat, Defensive Combat (Weaponry), Eidetic Memory, Etiquette 5, Encyclopedic Knowledge (Occult), Eye for the Strange, Fame 3, Haven 5, Herd 5, Indomitable, Inspiring, Iron Will, Language (Many), Meditative Mind 4, Professional Training 4 (Voivode; Persuasion, Politics, Warfare), Resources 5, Retainer 5,Safe Place 5, Status 5 (Prince of Paris), Striking Looks 1, Style: Armed Defense 3, Style: Armored Combat 1*, Style: Heavy Weapons 4, Style: Kindred Dueling 4, Style: Mounted Combat 2*, Style: Riding the Wave 5, Style: Weapon and Shield 2*, Swarm Form, Taste, Tolerance for Biology, Trained Observer 1
                                                Merits marked with an asterisk (*) are from the Hurt Locker playtest document.
                                                Disciplines: Animalism 5, Auspex 6, Celerity 3, Dominate 4, Majesty 5, Nightmare 4, Obfuscate 4, Protean 6, Resilience 4, Vigor 3
                                                Blood Magic: Cruac 3 (Cheval, Deflection of Wooden Doom, Drops of Destiny, Touch of the Morrigan), Theban 3 (Damned Radiance, Malediction of Despair, Sanctity, Vitae Reliquary)
                                                Devotions: Force of Nature, Quicken Sight, Shared Sight, Summoning, Vermin Flood
                                                Coils: Ascendant 6, Voivode 5, Wyrm 5, and likely more

                                                            The Wolfman

                                                            For this write up, I am presenting Lawrence Talbot, the titular character from the 1941 version of “The Wolfman” as I believe he would appear in Werewolf the Forsaken. This version of Lawrence Talbot was a talented and popular student, gifted in both athletics and having a keen scientific mind, destined for a productive and successful life until he inherited the “family curse”. At least one member of the Talbot family is born wolf-blooded every few generations. Lawrence is the first full Uratha of his family line for over 100 years. The legend passed to him from his family lore is that he is fated to die at the hands of someone who loves him, so he walks the path of a lone wolf. After years on the road learning about his abilities, he has returned home to investigate the mysterious death of his older brother.

                                                            Lawrence Talbot – The Wolfman
                                                            Blood: Destroyer; Bone: Rationalist
                                                            Auspice: Rahu
                                                            Tribe: Ghost Wolf

                                                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 4, Wits 3, Resolve 3

                                                            Merits: Allies 3 (Talbot Family), Anonymity 2, Blood Affinity, Common Sense, Danger Sense, Favored Form 2(Gauru),Fortified Form 4 (Gauru), Fleet of Foot 1, Hardy 1, Mentor 3, Patient, Striking Looks 1, Style: Relentless Assault 3, Style: Street Fighting 2
                                                            Gifts: Change (Purity), Evasion (Purity), Full Moon 2, Knowledge (Wisdom), Rage (Purity), Strength (Honor, Purity)
