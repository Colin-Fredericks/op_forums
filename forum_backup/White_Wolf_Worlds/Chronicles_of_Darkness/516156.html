    Slasher made prometheans?

    05-16-2015, 10:05 AM
    So between a discussion in the general section, the fact that human creators will be much more common in 2d edition, and the movie May, I had a thought: If serial murder can create a promethean, then slashers, who are by definition serial killers, are a solid candidate for a promethean's demiurge.

    So the simple way to do it, of course, is to give a promethean, probably of the frankenstein or galateid lineage, a particularly traumatic backstory.

    On the more ambitious level, one can always come up with a new lineage.

    So has anyone come up with what a promethean made by such a twisted soul might be like?

          Obviously, the Demiurge's presence and involvement in the Prommie's life is going to play a huge part, and I think the slasher's motivations will as well. A slasher who is trying to build a perfect mate, for example, will possibly come up with a Galateid-like creature; as he searches for parts that meet an aesthetic ideal. Or, he might be driven by the rejection of a previously idealized mate, or even using their their body, and end up with a Wretched from all the bile he's saved up.

          Problem is, the slasher won't get what he wants. In a matter of days, this supreme predator will come to realize all the flaws in his work, and serial killers are nothing if not exacting. He might try to kill the Created and start again, or he might try to make the most of the situation and train the creature to be a lure for new prospects. All of this will be formative for the Promethean. I think Abigail from Hannibal would be an instructive character to look at. One thing I don't think will happen is the slasher letting the Promethean go out on its own.

          LFP: Foreign Bodies (Deviant: the Renegades)

                              Looking at it through the Ripper Undertakings...

                              Avenger: I can't see an Avenger making a Promethean, even as a way of punishing their victims. The only way I could see it working was as a "someone must carry on my work" thing for an Avenger who thinks the end is drawing nigh - in this case, I'd expect to see something like the Wretched emerge from the process.

                              Brute: Brutes can't assemble sentences, let alone bodies - only the hands-on manipulation of a Qashmal would let them do it. Unless you had a Brute assembling parts of victims through mindless instinct, and a freak Fortean phenomenon animate it as an Extemporae.

                              Charmer: Again, I can't see a Charmer doing it. People are disposable tools - if you want one, you go get one, you don't go the trouble of putting one together. Unless someone's become the subject of the Obsessive Frailty and dies before the game can be complete, then the Charmer might try to "fix" them so they game can be played out - in that case, I'd expect a Tammuz (built for a purpose they're expected to "naturally" fulfil.

                              Freak: Now we're getting somewhere. A Freak trying to make a companion is a perfect match for a Galateid: their origin is suited, their Disquiet makes people feel judged on their looks, their Wasteland makes territory feel off kilter and easy to get lost in (and the Freak has a built-in Talent for getting round that problem), and their Torment can make them stalkers - it's a scarily good match. A place where a Freak has made a Muse is not a good place to find yourself.

                              Genius: A Genius might also have a "someone must continue my work vibe," though in their case I'd expect an Osirian (or maybe an Unfleshed living deathtrap). They might also want a hunting partner or flunky (which could also be an Osirian, or maybe a Tammuz) - or tiring of a world where no one understands their brilliance, they may want to make a mind that can keep up with them (Osirian, or maybe Galateid or Unfleshed). A Genius is probably the Undertaking with the most potential reasons for making a Promethean.
