                        Spell Control (•• or ••••)
                        Prerequisites: Proximus, Resolve 3
                        Effect: You are better able to manage control of your Blessings, and can keep more than one active at once. At 2 dots, you can increase your Spell Control by 1, and increase it by 1 again at 4 dots.
                        [Comments: A little something to give Proximi some breathing room when it comes to Spell Control.]

                        Mystic Tools (•, •••, or •••••)
                        Prerequisites: Proximus, Wits 3
                        Effect: You have learned how to utilize various mystically important devices, conditions, and methods to improve your Blessings. You can utilize 1 Yantra at 1 dot, 2 Yantras at 3 dots, and 3 Yantras at 5 dots.
                        [Comments: Now Proximi can get in on the wand-waving, and bolstering their spell casting dice pools.]

                        Attaining Insight (Style, • to •••••)
                        Prerequisites: Proximus, Occult 3
                        Effect: In depth studies have led to new insights into the nature of your gifts, granting you Attainments within a select Arcana.
                        The Sight (•): You now have access to Passive and Active Mage Sight within the selected Arcana.
                        The Rebuke (••): You can now have the Counterspell Attainment for the selected Arcana, except all uses require a point of Mana.
                        The Shield (•••): You now have access to the selected Arcana's Mage Armor Attainment, fixed as if they had 2 dots in the relevant Arcana.
                        The Scroll (••••): You now have access to the Lesser Utility Attainment for the selected Arcana.
                        The Call (•••••): You can now summon a Supernal Entity (as per the Summoning rules on page 95), except the summoned Supernal Entity only has the selected Arcana. Roll Willpower for the summoning, and treat Gnosis as 1 for relevant rolls and effects (such as Abyssal interference).
                        Note: A Proximus can select this merit once for each of their Blessing Arcana - each instance applies separately.
                        [Comments: Access to Attainments, now in Proximus flavor. Helps cover up a few gaps in their Mage- skill set.]

                        Rote Sorcery (•••••)
                        Prerequisites: Sleepwalker, Occult 1, Occult Specialty in Awakened lore
                        Effect: Your studies have enabled you to replicate, if only barely, the effects of pre-encoded Supernal magic. You can now use and learn Rotes, despite not being Awakened or possessing the ability to cast the spells improvised. Spells have the default ritual casting time of 5 hours. Additionally, you can utilize one Yantra per dot in Occult for the casting of the Rote, and spend a point of Reach to add +2 dice to the spell casting pool.
                        Note: Lacking Gnosis and Arcana ratings, your dice pool for casting any Rotes is limited only to what Yantras you can incorporate. While you benefit from the free Reach of the Rote, your lack of Arcana means all spell factors start at their default value of 1. Any additional Mana costs still have to be paid normally (such as through Cup Bearer, being a Proximus, using Tass or a Sacrament, etc.). You incur 1 Paradox die per exceeded Reach.
                        [Comments: Now non-Mages can use Awakened magic... provided they've read the manual, and have enough modifiers to even make the attempt. Seriously, no free spell factor increases and a piddling dice pool means this merit won't be outshining real Mages any time soon. Nevermind having to get one's hands on Rotes to begin with.]

                        Note, the Proximus ones above largely are (or make) redundant the Dynastic Heir (original recipe) merit chain, and Arcana-Strewn Nature and/or Illumination. These would be for those who prefer more a la carte enhancements. Or just different options.

                            Related Idea to Rote Sorcery

                            Abyssal Sorcery (•••••)
                            Prerequisites: Fitful Slumber, Occult 1, Occult Specialty in Abyssal lore
                            Effects: The trauma left by the Abyss has scarred your soul... and opened your mind. You can learn Rotes (and antinomian Rotes), and can force them into reality despite not being Awakened yourself. The base ritual casting time is 5 hours, you can utilize one Yantra per dot in Occult for the casting of the Rote, and each Reach adds 1 Paradox die. Unlike with normal Rotes, you don't get free Reach - instead, all castings roll Paradox, but you can control as many Paradox successes as you would have had free Reach. If you score less successes on the Paradox roll, you only have that much Reach to use. Any Reach over your free allotment is handled normally.
                            Note: Lacking Gnosis and Arcana ratings, your dice pool for casting any Rotes is limited only to what Yantras you can incorporate. Any additional Mana costs still have to be paid normally (such as through Cup Bearer, using Tass, etc.).
                            [Comments: For Bargain Bin Abyssal Sorcerers to throw at your Mages. Note the interplay with Reach - you can use the Rote's "free" Reach Safely, but the total amount is determined by the Paradox roll. Too few dice, and you don't have much to play with, while adding in too many risks the spell escaping from you on a good roll.

                            I probably need to add in more advantages to justify the massive gamble like nature of its use. Maybe rate the Merit 1 to 5, with each dot offering another "controlled" Reach?]

                                Shifter (••, ••• or ••••)
                                Prerequisites: Life 1, Stamina 3
                                Effect: Some of those who signed their names on the Watchtower did this not with a hand but with a paw. When taking this Merit choose an animal that the mage can change into(him and only him). At 2 dots you gain access to spells that grant features of this particular animal one dot earlier - you still use your normal dice pool and stats, though. Example: Choosing a carp lets you change into this fish at 3rd dot of Life (with Shapechanging) or give yourself gills at 2nd dot. In both options you don’t get the free reach since you are using practice higher then your own.

                                With the 3 dot version the mage is particularly comfortable in the animal forms he assumes, and his magic lasts longer than other mages who lack his mastery of shapeshifting. A mage with this Merit can worry less about awkwardly timed transformations back to human form (while the mage is flying as an eagle, for example), and he need not worry about the strain of repeated casting under circumstances where recasting a spell might be difficult. Add 2 steps on your Duration ladder for the purpose of those spells.

                                At 4th dot of this Merit understanding of his inner animal is even greater and mage can now assume his chosen animal form 2 dots earlier. In this case you must pay 1 Reach just to be able to cast those spells.

                                Note: It's a nod to those poor bastards who want to change forms but can't because of the need to wait for 4th dot. It's obvious and vulgar magic that is hard to use and you often have other, more useful spells to cast.
