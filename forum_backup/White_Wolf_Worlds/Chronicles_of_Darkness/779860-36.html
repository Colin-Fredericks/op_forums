                        Ascended and Descended forms for Sirens are sounding top notch. It seems like Siren consistently finds the cream of the crop amongst similar mechanics and systems to bring the best all into one game.


                        When it gets to the Crossover conversation, there was one point I wanted to address first. Siren presents a particular platform to make or break a crossover and storytellers will want to think twice about what game lines they chose to include, whether Siren is the sole focus or part of the ancillary background.

                        Let me quote a few sections on the Deluge and Celestial Oceans to build upon:

                        In truth, the Deluge is the culminations of all apocalypses, past and future. It is to this extent that the event that causes the Deluge to occur is ultimately unclear because in today’s day and age it’s possible for anything and everything to have a hand in causing the Deluge. Afterall, an apocalypse caused by hands of man is just as plausible as a freak backlash caused by nature or the cosmos.
                        Perhaps the one thing that it lacks is the existence of any other realities that would normally be connected to the Earth. There is no Shadow, Underworld, Hedge, nor is there any Twilight for that matter. This calms some Sirens, as it means they have less they’re required to deal with. To others, it simply freaks them out because the Deluge isn’t just a danger to a single reality, but to all realities connected to the Earth when it happens.
                        In short, when working with Siren in a crossover setting it is the ultimate exercise of conversation of detail. Consider that the sister venue paired with Siren will play a part in the Deluge. If Sirens are the muses, oracles, and stand-takers of the conflict between Civilization and Nature, another society of supernatural figures will have their own impact on both and thus a massive impact on the Deluge.

                        Although the Delgue has no Twilight and no connection to other realms, its cataclysms in a crossover venture should hint to where these realms originally were. The cataclysm is decidedly supernatural in origin but should still be shaped by how that venue interacts with both Civilization and Nature. Entering the Deluge of a Geist game reveals a dead sea where the dead of the Underworld have been spat out. The Deluge from a Promethean game is the result of of the Firestorm to end all firestorms. In a Changeling game the Deluge contains the last of the Hedge's thorns when Arcadia cut all ties with Earth. With a Leviathan crossover the Rift and the Deluge have become one and the same, only as the ultimate end instead of the origin of primordial waters. And Princess could have the Second Fall, with Darkness having taken all of the world and not just the former kingdoms.

                        Dealing with other Supernatural figures will be harder than dealing with humanity. As they are no longer mortal they don't fit as easily in the conflict of Nature and Civilization. But they have their own influence on the conflict, however realized, much as Sirens can be both preventers and instigators to changing trends.

                        This is decidedly more of a thematic problem, one to shape the entire scope of your Chronicle. Thankfully when it comes to the mechanics of Crossovers that becomes very easy with the applications of the Opuses.

                                Feel free to use anything of mine for your Crossover appendix! I'm sure that's where a lot of my future commentary will go since Crossover as a use of compare/contrast for game themes is a favorite of mine, and a part for my own crossover heavy Princess game.

                                The main thing when having another venue with your Siren game is the consideration of how other supernatural forces will play a part in the 'End of the World As We Know It.' How they compare to Sirens isn't the only focus, it's how the other Supernaturals might/do bring the world to its doom. How this occurs fits the importance of the mixed setting on your Chronicle. Other Supernaturals don't have to be at fault directly, a cataclysm could come from either Civilization ("humanity's full uprising against their leaching from Vampires, or forced history by Geniuses") or Nature ("out-evolving the need of Werewolves, or erasing the long geographies of Mummies or Leviathan").

                                I'm sure how another kind of Supernatural views their mythos would shade their perspective of a passenger visit into the Celestial Sea. It brings a mix of potential interactions from Sirens trying to gain allies into incomprehensible matters, trying to convince big shakers and movers of the consequences of their folly, or as outright antagonists if a group is extremist/self-justified enough it causes catastrophic ripples in the Deluge.


                                In the Crossover section (which may end up like a small-upcoming-Chronicles book) there could be a template of how another game's themes compare to Siren, suggestions for how their presence would influence the Deluge, and a reciprocal look as how that venue would view Sirens. This would be bridged with what kinds of Waters and Currents associate with both the good and the bad of that venue, although need not be as specific as direct faction to faction equation.

                                There are game lines I haven't perused as greatly, so there will still be a need for various contributors. Those write ups could get lengthy so since I just found a scribbled list I wanted to switch to Opus crossover.

                                ------------------------------------

                                Much like Mage Arcana, the Opuses offer direct integration for Sirens into the abilities of various other venues. Ideally you would have 1 Opus represent each venue, although there is some overlap and some may require the joint of 2 Opuses, but no more. A few were already suggested in the Opus list and some come naturally:
                                • Opus of Blood for Vampire
                                • Opus of Spirit for Werewolf and Spirits
                                • Opus of Fate for Changeling
                                • Opus of Human for Hunter/Mortal+
                                • Opus of Death for Sin-Eaters and Ghosts

                                Now these are some official game lines that are difficult to peg into one Opus:
                                • Mage is a bit of a sticking point. The Arcana and Opus have their counterparts easily enough, but what single Opus represents the 'identity' of a Mage, an awakened being and the perception of Supernal Truth?
                                • Promethean shares an aspect of Death but with 2E's broadened emphasis even never formerly living Created are available. The character struggles of Promethean suggest Growth or Humanity as well.
                                • Demons and Angels could play into the Technology of the God-Machine, to really play up the machine aspects, yet its occult matrixes and bizarre classification of infrastructure at times bleeds into other Opuses, although I can't put my finger on which.
                                • Mummy is quite tricky, although Death is a likely aspect for the Arisen's condition. The rites and legacies of the Nameless Empire, the timeless aspects of this early culture that persist to this day, do not readily identify amongst the current Opuses.
                                • For Beast I am split on a mix of Passion and Darkness, for the Hungers/Horrors and self explanatory reference to primordial nightmares. There isn't exactly a 'Dreaming' Opus which could have been another thread of rationale
                                • We don't know enough about Deviant to give it a good Opus range, as the types of suggested Renegades are quite varied. Deviants are caused, most consistently, by humans preying upon and experimenting on others, the Remade aspect fitting both Flenser and Echidna's themes to some degree.

                                And these are some fan lines I favor, with some easy and some harder to place due to early development or broad aspects:
                                • Opus of Technology for Genius
                                • Opus of Light for Princess. Their opposite, the Outer Darkness, works with both Darkness and Taint
                                • Opus of Pressure for Leviathan. Runner up, Chaos.
                                • Janus: the Persona is a favorite of mine from RPG.net, an alpha-build about people whose personalities become so broken they split into their Shadow, the psychological 'other you' that acts on worse impulses. Your own enemy is yourself. However it is a very insular focused game which doesn't suggest much on the Crossover crisis spectrum. Possible aspects include Human, Darkness and Chaos
                                • Demigod: the Empyrean shares a bit of the Mage problem, as its Domains as as counterpart varied as the Opuses that their single identity is hard to grasp. You could argue Light as well, since a 'Divine' Opus doesn't exist.
                                • Monarch: the Endless has the PCs as (semi)reluctant leaders of an alien hive mind. It's current trend is towards an interesting nuance of both Nature and Civilization as the Endless are the epitome of the problems you get from evasive species. A possible unifying aspect could be Growth.

                                        For the Crossovers, here's my early draft for the Princess: the Hopeful article;

                                        Princess: the Hopeful

                                        Overview: Perhaps no other Supernatural being resonates as strongly with Sirens as the Princesses and Princes of the Light (the Nobility) for there are many similarities between the two. Both experience a pivotal stress in their life that brings them awareness of a great universal conflict beyond themselves. Where Sirens must find balance amongst Nature and Civilization after their Drowning, Princesses find themselves on the side of the Light against the entropic Darkness that degrades humanity. Being the paragons of humanity's best qualities, Princesses can become staunch allies for matters relating to Civilization. Although followers of the Queen of Clubs are as attuned to Nature as many Sirens. Princesses draw their power from within and their relationships with others, much like Sirens.

                                        Yet for their common bonds this does not mean Princesses and Sirens are automatic BFF 'besties.' Their respective conflicts run perpendicular to each other. For the majority of Nature minded Sirens they can find gripes with the reciprocal lack of conservational minded Nobility. Sirens often intuit their way through issues with little concern for ancillary matters, for the Deluge is a sign of what little time remains to prevent a disaster. Although they can be just as impassioned Princesses are more social and nostalgic agents, needing to confer with their Queens, fellow Nobility and others to determine the best course of action. As Princesses naturally cling to their humanity this can cause discord with Sirens who have lessened this emphasis in their mission.

                                        Princess Perspective: For those that study the countries from before the Fall, Princess scholars wonder if Ceto is herself a former Queen who went on to embody the aspect of the Song. This simple theory is complicated by the twisty bends of Siren 'history.' Ceto is both an actor in the creation myth and the 'first' great cataclysm that brought the Deluge and the need to send Sirens to warn the world of impending doom.

                                        Regardless, a Princess who befriends a Siren finds a complimentary partner to tackle both of their campaigns. Although originally an Embassy for exploration and world travel, the Embassy to the Ocean makes a Princess as close to a Siren as possible. The signature 'mermaid form' that is added to the Princess's Magical Self is all but a Diluvian form in name. The Embassy offers no further synergy with Siren: the Drowning, although it's the easiest for helping the Princess keep up with a Siren in an aquatic environment.

                                        Antagonists: Sirens that learn of the differences between the Radiant and Twilight Queens see a mirror with the Celestial and Abyssal Currents. All are sides in the same conflict with very different methodologies. There is nothing that suggests Twilight Princesses and Abyssal Sirens will find common ground, same as other pairings, as the Fallen Nobility are set against the Darkness primarily. Uneasy alliances may crop up from time to time, likely brief, between the agents of the Twilight Queens and the Sirens foes.

                                        Waters and Currents: many Siren splats find their counterpart in Princess. Of note Sirens from Freshwater are the most attuned to humanity and are the first to note the Blossomings and activities of a Princess. Tropicana empathizes with the ruling energies of emotions and self belief Princesses draw from. For Currents, Healing and Harmony find common ground with many Princesses, while Buoyancy connects with the the freeing and inspired aspects of Light's Nobility.

                                        Suggested Opuses: Light, Passion, Dream

                                        Mechanics: When a Princess would gain a Wisp from a significant relationship, it can be offered to a Siren as a point of Pneuma if they are in the same scene. This requires the Princess's consent, otherwise the Siren would have to devise a Verse to leech off the emotive speck of power. Alternatively, Princesses in the Embassy to the Ocean may use the Mermaid's Kiss benefit to offer the spent Wisp as a Pneuma.

                                        Cataclysm: The Outer Darkness

                                        The Darkness inflicts wounds on the world and its peoples just as any other Quagmire. Indeed a Tainted Place is much like a Quagmire, although it only connects to the Dark World and its abominations. As the Creatures of Darkness destroy both Nature and Civilization alike Sirens sometimes come into conflict with these areas.

                                        In the Deluge marked by the stain of the Outer Darkness it is the aftermath of the second Fall. The world has been devastated from the attack of dark forces, leaving lightless ruins and freezing void as large wounds on the Earth. Between flow that is more ink than water are survivors that are even more depraved that accustomed, given into vices and lashing out at each other as well as outsiders. The very light of the world, it's last hope, has been snuffed out.

                                        What's worse is the great roving storm, a radioactive cloud front of searing green fire from the heavens that will still blight the remains of the Darkness. Unlike the apotheosis Queen of Storms, her Princesses would find no sentience in this Deluge cataclysm, no swirl of ever fighting souls. Unlike Echidna, no sapience of the Queen's form appears to have survived entry into the drowned planet.

                                        In the Deluge, these environments pertain to Polaria and Discordia Waters respectively.

                                        Suggested Opuses: Darkness, Taint, Chaos

                                        Antagonists: Abyssal Currents may find poaching a Creature of Darkness for their particular goals to be a useful strategy. Echidna sometimes gains curiosity towards the Darkened, Darkspawn, Mnemosyne and Cataphractoi to transfer their Umbrae and Calignes as adaptations for her Vodinaoi. However, there is little human or natural about the latter three Creatures of Darkness. Flensers find the Darkness objectionable towards the progression of humanity.

                                        Most tellingly, Darkspawn and their Taint are some of the worse sins against Nature in the mind of a Quagmire, as much of the Darkness is formed from humanity's worst traits.

                                        Mechanics: When a Quagmire and a Tainted Place would butt boundaries, compare the Resentment and Severity of each. Roll these numbers as a dice pool and whichever side gets more successes begins eating away at the other, taking over its space. The transfer of Resentment or Severity occurs every 12 hours, this time is halved for each further transgression committed in the area. The dominating side gains access to the other's curses. Furies treat a source of Tainted Darkness as Pollution when crafting their Vessels. Otherwise Darkspawn and Furies do not gain access to each other's abilities.

                                        Cataclysm: The Searing Light

                                        They say the road to hell is paved with good intentions. Within the chronicles of a world filled with darkness, aren't the paragons of the Light good for changing the course of this wretched world?

                                        That's what one would think. Although the Deluge filled to the brim with searing light raises many uncomfortable questions. It is a light that blinds. It is a light that burns. It is a light that has shown every ugly truth of the past world yet has not been able to better it. When not in a blinding patch the waters of the Deluge are crystal clear. Survivors are friendly and pleasant, not always for the benefits from lending a helping hand. There's a palpable fear, a sense of being judged. Anything deemed dark and wrong is eaten away, scorched from the inside out. In the battle of Light and Darkness the light has upended it's lifelong foe. Yet this has not brought peace, prosperity or contentment.

                                        For Sirens privy to this warning from the Deluge it paints a degree of questionableness to the Nobility. And the origin of this cataclysm is not obvious. Is this the true result of the Outer Darkness's final defeat? Has the hidden Queen of Mirrors unleashed her ultimate agenda with the True Heir? Or has something happened to the Radiant Queens, worse than their complacency that gave rise to the first Fall enacted by the Darkness? May-haps the Twilight Queens can count more amongst their Fallen number.

                                        In the Deluge, this searing environment pertains to the Tropicana Water. Clearer, more populated areas correspond with Freshwater.

                                                Sidebar: Self-Time-Travel?
                                                It would be inevitable that a question would be asked along the lines of "Can a Verse using the Time Opus and Travel Conclusion be able to travel through time?".
                                                The simple answer is "No". Mostly to avoid the problems that come with Time Travel and that Sirens already can mess with temporal events without it through their Traces. Instead, Using a Time-Travel Conclusion allows for a Siren to achieve extra actions upon her rolls. For each dot in the Travel Conclusion in a Time-Travel Verse, the Siren may have a number of extra actions equals to the dots. For example, a Siren who uses Self-Time-Travel 3 can have three extra actions through a turn.
