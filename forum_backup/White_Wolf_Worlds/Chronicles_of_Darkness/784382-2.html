    Which Ventriloquist? There have been 3:
    ​1. Arnold Wesker, who beat a man to death to take the original Scarface* dummy from him. He was convinced Scarface told him to.
    2. "Sugar", a former mob wife who found Scarface after Wesker's death. Her crimes were largely about getting revenge after her husband shot her in the face and left her for dead. She was in the habit of rigging her dummies to explode.
    3. I can't remember her real name, but a third Ventriloquist appeared in Gail Simone's recent Batgirl run. This disturbingly pale and emaciated young woman was a killer from childhood. She possesses telekinetic abilities, and her dummy, which looks like the puppet from the "Saw" movies, has built-in weapons. I can't remember her dummies name either, but she killed the performer at her birthday party, then modified his dummy.

    *The original Scarface was carved from the wood of the gallows tree at Blackgate Penitentiary, from which 666 men had been hanged. Cursed/haunted object?

                    The video game version is way different, it makes him seem more like a strung-out addict of some sort. In the comics he's like a serial killer who's always going through his phases. He sees everyone as zombies or corpses, wastes of flesh serving no purpose, not understanding or even really living. He sees himself as a predator, liberating them from their shallow existences. If you had to put him into one of the undertakings, he'd probably be a Brute. But a non-standard one, as he is actually pretty intelligent and very cunning. And physically, though he doesn't appear strong in many of his appearances, he's usually a tough combatant even for Batman. Surprise attacks really seem to be the best way to take him out. Part of the problem is that he isn't as 'showy' as a lot of the other villains, he doesn't want to make a statement or usually have a big show. He just sees someone, stalks them, kills them, and moves on to the next person.

                                I really need to check the forums more often. I was actually set to run a Werewolf game set in Gotham before my group fell apart. I'm actually tempted to set my Mage game in Gotham (the core Mystery is that the Primordial Dream is closer to reality, sort of like in LA), though I don't think my players want that. Still, I wonder how long I could get away with using not-quite Batman villains. Hunter would probably be the best splat, whether or not you directly want Batman.
                                As for what I was going to do, well, I wasn't going to have actual Batman, but Bruce Wayne's return to Gotham was going to be a subplot, along with a Year One style vigilante. Players would obviously be able to put two and two together, but what to do about him? Especially when their Totem, "The Detective on Leather Wings" takes an interest in this vigilante? I tend to give my players some hints and suggestions for what they'll deal with in a game, but one thing I kept under wraps is that the Detective was a spirit created by a young Bruce Wayne obsessing over his parent's death. So he was likely going to become a Wolfblooded.

                                Other things include:
                                • The tainted Wound that formed in Crime Alley as everyone's depression pooled there.
                                • The undead monster (Solomon Grundy) stalking the underground (who can only die on a Saturday and must be buried on a Sunday)
                                • The pack's Wolfblooded:
                                  • Awkward and quirky forensic analyst Edward Nigma (based heavily on the Gotham version) who just wanted to be helpful to the Pack, and probably saw his detective Werewolf as the coolest guy ever
                                  • Rich and popular high school Mean Girl Barbara Kean (based slightly less on the Gotham version) who felt she owed her life to the Werewolf that bit her for saving her from a monster
                                  • Jason Todd, the standoffish homeless kid with the Anger Issues Tell
                                • The Scarecrow, a powerful Fear Spirit that attacked the high school and now has its sights set on Barbara Kean... but first, it needs to Claim a host, and Johnathan Crane will do just fine
                                • The Falcone Crime Family, a group of Ivory Claws loyal to Steel Spire Falcon and headed by Carmine Falcone, though his Uratha son Alberto is jealous that his much more intelligent Wolfblooded father is running the show.
                                • A secret group of wealthy mortals with ties to the Strix. They provide the Strix with bodies to inhabit, and have secretly pulled the strings in Gotham.
                                • A no talent greasy and perpetually wet looking Nosferatu with a limp and a penchant for wearing three piece suits muscling in on the Falcone territory, because Gotham had a great version of Penguin.
