                                                Also, the Odense, Denmark preview is up.

                                                Characteristically of the setting of Hamlet, the Contagion here is effectively viral catatonic depression. It's fatal, because its sufferers can't work up the will to finish crossing the street in a busy intersection. Most supernaturals infected are asymptomatic carriers, unless they're vampires, since Patient Zero here is King Knud the Holy, who became a unique Kindred at some point; then the depression not only hits but inflames the Beast (ironically, Beasts themselves are the ones who are most involved in treatment along with Lost, because mortal infected can't feel hope or fear).

                                                Uniquely, everyone also knows the cure; it's Knud's Touchstone, the pendant he was given at birth. Unfortunately, the King is also the leader of the local Machiavelli Gambit, and they've decided that ruling over mortal shells of people is good governance. Thus, Theseans are playing Van Helsing, desperately trying to find the Pendant and mutate the Contagion into something life-affirming.
