                        In the eternal arms race of spam vs moderation, there are spam bots out there that are designed to make normal-ish looking posts for a bit, and then go back and edit in spam if they aren't caught fast enough. So edit spam is actually a thing moderator tools have to address. From what I've heard from moderators elsewhere this wasn't a big problem for a while because the spam bots just weren't good enough to get around basic automated moderation, but the uptick in AI writing has made it much harder for automated systems to spot these things. Basically, spam bots have gotten a lot better in the past few years at getting around the first layers of "I am not a robot" defenses.

                        It's not as big of a problem on forums, but a lot of people that make anti-spam tech for moderators aren't so platform specific.
