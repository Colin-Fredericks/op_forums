                Rather than the, frankly ludicrous, suggestion that the "One World of Darkness" will be a merger of the new and classic WoD surely it's more likely that Paradox want to consolidate the two (soon to be three) post Revised cWoD timelines into a coherent ongoing canon? Currently V20, the upcoming V4 and BNS MET line have different takes on what happened since 2003 and, unlike the split between the two Worlds of Darkness, that could be confusing and detrimental to the "transmedia" plan Paradox appear to have.

                The new White Wolf Head Storyteller will most likely be in charge of taking and consolidating the best of the OP and BNS V:tM versions and then keeping BNS, OP and the producers of the expected video games (most likely Obsidian and maybe Paradox Development Studio) and potential TV/movies/comics/novels etc. on the same page for new WoD products. Such a development, while it will cause some initial consternation and may lead to V4 being pushed back to ensure the work on it isn't wasted, will no doubt prove beneficial to cWoD in the medium to long term, combine the BNS and OP fanbases (as the initial press release stated) and, so long as the various licensees are still able to put their ideas to the Head Storyteller, expand the pool of talented writers contributing to it.
                The big concerns are that design by committee sets in and ideas that would be fantastic in one medium get nixed due to issues in another and that the various gamelines are pushed together too hard, sacrificing elements of one line to avoid messing with another's lore (ideally the current 'unified world if you cross your eyes and squint a bit' approach will be maintained with maybe a touch of fixing up the glaring inconsistencies where possible).

                I've not touched upon nWoD much because I suspect that as BNS don't currently publish any nWoD books and it isn't as attractive as cWoD for video games it's likely to remain solely in OP's lap for the time being.

                    Originally posted by Second Chances View Post
                    More percisely, 5 in the top 50, 9 in the top 100. Five of those are nWoD, four are cWoD. You look at risk and reward, and I can't see a solid financial reason to cancel or merge either line.
                    I can.

                    You're looking at the ultra-small-picture there. Good sales on RPG-specific PDF/PoD sites are not a great deal of money, especially not to a company like Paradox.

                    Importantly, Paradox have explicitly stated that they believe that the WoD, as an IP, has huge cross-media potential. The '90s suggest they are right. Most of what was popular then is, if anything, more popular now (urban/modern supernatural stuff, existential and conspiracy horror, etc.).

                    The issue is that whilst the WoD has a ton of powerful IP, the best bits are split among nWoD and oWoD, and some bits are looking decidedly outdated (I would suggest), so could perhaps be entirely redone. So merely picking one and using that as the "flagship" doesn't make sense. Further, you have an audience who could be powerful fans, but are currently lapsed entirely or split among these two sub-IPs.

                    You have to not piss them off - you have to get them excited. Remember that many don't play at all now, but they remember the oWoD, particularly. So you don't cancel the lines in the sense of preventing sales. You just politely wind them down, leave them PDF/PoD only, and create a new WoD brand which contains the best features of both, a vastly updated and superior system, and has high production quality, as well as actual "books in shops" and so on.

                    You also attempt to leverage that new IP into computer games (probably licensed with Paradox publishing but other studios developing), comics, movies/TV if you can, and so on. Your best friends in all this are the casual/lapsed WoD fans, not the ones with really strong opinions.

                    Hopefully OPP can remain involved in this. I see no fundamental reason why they couldn't. Paradox want to manage the entire multi-media deal, the brand, not necessarily to micro-manage the products. So they'd be heavily involved in conceptualizing and producing the core books for this "Modern WoD" (let's call it), but are unlikely to be focused on supplements/splats etc.

                    It could go tremendously well.
