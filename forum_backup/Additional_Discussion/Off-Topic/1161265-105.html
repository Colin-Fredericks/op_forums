                            Originally posted by nofather View Post

                            They seem to be taking a Blizzard method to development,
                            Oh, about that...

                            http://dmdavid.com/tag/why-fourth-ed...ragons-needed/

                            There is a huge thread about this on EnWorld, which I didn't have the fortitude to wade through, but I read the article. I know it'll likely spark arguments, but I want to make one remark: as much as the article's writer makes the comparison to WoW as the main driving factor and inspiration, one thing I noticed immediately about 4e back in the day, that it looked like WoW. Honestly, I generally liked the 4e artworks, much more than the 5e ones, but the whole general direction felt very WoW-ish (just as Heroes of Might and Magic 5 felt the same, after the 3rd and 4th iteration, but that's another story... I guess, at that time, everyone wanted to copy WoW). Oversized pauldrons, everything glowing, over-the-top action scenes, very bright colors, etc. Even if not for the rules, it felt very different than the older editions.

                            Edit: oh, yeah, and Essentials, again, was the best, curbing it back, but still looking vibrant and modern.

                            Edit 2: http://dmdavid.com/tag/how-the-end-o...e-of-dd-books/ I wanted to comment on this last part of the series and on Mearls's and the FATE developer's quotes. Namely, that this business strategy might make the game a success, financially, it also made the game into one I practically have no interest in. Because you know, I'm precisely the kind of person, who never found MMOs entertaining, or found joy in watching other people doing something I'd rather do myself, be it sports, or playing games. On the other hand I very much like to immerse myself in these imaginary worlds, through setting books and novels and such, when I can't play and it gave me many hours of joy and entertainment.

                            So, I'm just happy there's still Paizo (and other publishers and games, of course) for me and those like me.
