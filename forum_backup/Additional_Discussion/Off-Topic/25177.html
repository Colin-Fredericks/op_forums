                                      Honestly? I'd suggest medicaid/SSI. It's a not-great social safety net, that I feel is underfunded but it's there for a reason. Because it is, at times, needed and people in general are terrible at planning. You pay into when you can and take out from it when you can't. It's not like it's theft or something wrong.


                                      Apart from that... not a hell of a lot I can do. Well, if you're not used to basement living I can offer a few suggestions that might help.

                                      The concrete is going to be sucking up heat making it cold. So if you're sleeping on an airbed, one of the best things I can recommend is seeing if there's a way to raise it up. Some storage boxes or even milk crates spread out and then putting the mattress on top? a heck of a lot better than on the ground. plus if you can wiggle things around a bit you can make a space in the middle of it if you happen to have a space heater. This can take your airbed from something that saps heat from you all night long into something that keeps warming you all night. Just be careful to set it up so that there is space around the heater and so that even if the air mattress deflates it doesn't contact the heater.

                                      Watch the amount of sunlight you get. I know from my days living in a basement it's an issue. When you don't get enough people tend to get into a depressive spiral, which just causes problems. Somehow I doubt you have much influence over what the place looks like, but bright colours can also help a bit.

                                      You could also partially block some of the heating ducts to direct more heat into the room you are in, at the cost of less heat going to the rest of the house (mostly upstairs)... which would cause someone to raise the temperature. (oh noes).
