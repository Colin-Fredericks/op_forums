    I'm not sure what the much hated One More Day story has to do with the MCU. Though to be fair to the justifiably least popular Spider-man arc ever, Peter and MJ mutually agreed to let the devil take their marriage to save Aunt May's life... that's not exactly "divorced his wife in the name of Satan." And hilariously the whole point was to open up the comics for better development of the universe by taking away what had become the new calcified status quo for Spider-man.

    The MCU has, so far, done what Marvel and DC have both utterly refused to do in the comics: let main character die off for good. It helps that actors don't want to be stuck in the same massive high budget movies forever. They're developing the MCU more deeply, and letting different characters take center stage while phasing older ones out. It's the opposite of the comic's and the incessant and unending resets, cross-over events, and refusal to try to build up new characters while clinging to failing sales of the old standbys.

    Like... why should the MCU stop when the comics are the medium that they can't figure out (despite starting as comics...) in the current cultural climate? It's not like comics are dead as a medium, Marvel and DC both guilty of poisoning their own well with insipidly dumb business practices that have repeatedly undercut the art and artists responsible for their profits, and turned fans off time and time again. Meanwhile the MCU is a cultural juggernaut.
