    I would take strong issue with the idea that role playing games are inherently liberal or leftist. There is nothing about role playing as a hobby that has any politics leanings. The World of Darkness is leftist in many regards, though not completely nor to the degree that conservatives can't enjoy it. In fact, I have lots of very conservative friends who love playing WW games in the WoD and have for many years. Along the same lines, there are probably just as many right wing messages in the fantasy genre as there are left wing messages in the WoD but there is nothing contradictory about about leftists playing fantasy games.

    Many times when the bad guys in the WoD are conservative or the good guys are liberal, it is in ways which are completely nonpartisan in regard to modern political divides. For example, the original V:tM setting made an assumption that the players would probably side with the Anarchs against the oppressive elders. Well, in that regard, the players would be liberal from the perspective of vampire politics, but it's not as though many modern conservatives favor immortal feudalism, so the game is effectively non-partisan. Though I suppose that I have noticed that right wing gamers I know are more sympathetic to the Tremere and Ventrue, as examples. They like Gangrel too - rugged outdoorsmen, hunters, rural, and/or libertarian types.

    Werewolf was much more overtly leftist, especially on environmentalism (if one considers that leftist) and often gets very heavy handed inserting other left wing messages into the game. This has always bee something that troubled me about W:tA. It actually wouldn't take many slight tweaks in interpretation to make things in that game more friendly to conservatives, save for environmentalism which is pretty central to the setting.

    Mage: the Ascension... This is the most right wing game ever made. It is accidentally right wing, the left wing game designers didn't know they were making the most conservative game ever, but they did. How so?

    Well, the conservative gamers don't see the Technocracy as conservative and in fact, it isn't consvative at all. It is progressive, that is what it is at its very essence. In fact, it is like a right win conspiracy theory made manifest. A world spanning globalist group of progressives who want to force modern values on everyone, want to eliminate freedom of speech and expression, want to eliminate religion, want to establish a "New World Order" - they invented Info Wars the RPG. When I was young, I didn't know how left leaning the M:tA creators were and I was amazed at how right wing the game seemed to be. Even when I looked at the setting from the perspective of Technocractic good guys, it was a matter of thinking that progressives have done a lot of good in the past few hundred years.

    Subsequent WW games were relatively non-partisan save for a few minor biases on the part of authors that found their way in.
