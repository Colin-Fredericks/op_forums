                                So at the risk of starting a conversation no one wants-

                                I hate how the current state of affairs has rendered Star Wars completely unfun to talk about.

                                It doesn't matter if I disagree or kind of agree with someone about something in regards to Star Wars, click bait hot topic issue or not, the vitriol that's entered into Star Wars since the Phantom Menace and has gotten exacerbated up to now means that I can't a conversation about any facet of it without that vitriol seeping in, and it's been at that point now that even in private and secure places, I don't like talking about Star Wars, because just thinking about it brings up memories of completely frothing-at-the-mouth hatred and that's not fun to think about. I kind of don't care that I've enjoyed Mandalorian or Visions or gained new appreciation for all the movies recently , because that bile has so thoroughly come define the emotional backdrop of thinking about Star Wars.

                                Like, I was never really that hardcore a Star Wars fan. It's a thing I used to enjoy whenever it came up, and I was glad to have a shared cultural touchstone, but even before this feeling set it, I kind of knew if I never got into anything Star Wars again, I'd be okay with that.

                                But now I'm in the same place, but because it's become polluted this way, not because of my own feelings on the subject.

                                ANd I hate that that's happened.
