            Remember this lady from the Simpsons?



            The one that goes "see children, there's hope for everyone." and when Bart asks "even for me?" she replies with that chilling "no"?

                            I love my cousin's kids. The last couple of years, we've not had the time together that I'd have wanted, but today they were attending a barbecue that my folks were holding while I was visiting. I played a bit of football with the sporty one, listened to their stories, introduced them to Nintendo Switch via Mario Kart and especially Super Mario Odyssey (which they were rather taken with, and did pretty well at), and sat down with one to help her with her reading*, before we settled down to watch Kiki's Delivery Service (I introduced those kids to Miyazaki a few years back as well).

                            ​It was a good day, the kind of thing that I generally want with them. I think they enjoyed themselves

                            ​* I didn't actually know about her dyslexia before today, but when her younger sister asked me to read something and she started trying to go along with it, it was quite apparent to me, so I felt obligated to do my best to help her work her way through the words, before her mother explicitly confirmed her difficulty to me.
